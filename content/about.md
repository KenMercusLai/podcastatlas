Podcast Atlas is **a living knowledge atlas synthesized from podcasts**. It turns individual episodes into navigable episode guides, source notes, concepts, entities, and an evolving synthesis of ideas across shows.

## What Podcast Atlas is

The atlas is designed for readers who want to move beyond a single episode: follow an idea across conversations, see which people and organizations recur, return to the underlying source, and discover questions that remain unresolved.

Podcast Atlas is not a podcast directory, listening service, or substitute for the original recording. Its primary purpose is knowledge discovery and synthesis. Episode pages always preserve a route back to the original audio when a public source URL is available.

## How to read the atlas

- Start with an **Episode Guide** for a structured path through one conversation.
- Open its **Source Note** to see the canonical source relationship used by the Wiki.
- Follow **Concepts** and **Entities** to move across episodes and shows.
- Use **Current Synthesis** for the atlas's present knowledge state and **Open Questions** for what remains unsettled.
- Consult **Update History** to see when the living knowledge layer changed.
