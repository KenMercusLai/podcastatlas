# Esther Duflo

Esther Duflo is named in [[piles-of-cash-and-a-town-of-solutions-in-kenya-nigeria-summer-school]] as one of the economists who won Nobel recognition alongside [[MichaelKremer]] and [[AbhijitBanerjee]] for randomized approaches to global poverty. The source uses that recognition to explain how [[RandomizedControlledTrials]] became central to modern [[DevelopmentEconomics]].

The episode does not make Duflo a field-story character, but her mention places the Busia work inside a wider evidence movement. That link matters because the source repeatedly asks whether field experiments lead to [[EvidenceBasedDevelopmentPolicy]] and local benefits, not only academic prestige.

## Connections
- [[MichaelKremer]] and [[AbhijitBanerjee]] - co-recognized economists in the source's Nobel context.
- [[Kenya]] and [[BusiaKenya]] - field setting behind the episode's research story.
- [[DevelopmentResearchEthics]] - ethical frame around experiments and local benefit.
