# DAZN

DAZN appears in [[e237-yangshi-he-fifa-tanpan-fenzheng-beihou-tiyu-saishi-zhuanboquan-de-boyi-yu-shengyi-c3607c08-6eb6-48bd-8250-b41b5b8272e6]] as the international comparison for sports-rights bubbles and streaming-platform patience. [[ZhuXiaodongSportsRights|Zhu Xiaodong]] says DAZN bought J.League rights in Japan at a much higher annual price than before, then spent years increasing users and pricing before approaching break-even.

The source contrasts DAZN with China's more destructive rights bubble. Overseas sports-rights markets can also overheat, but the episode suggests longer operating cycles, more stable competition, and more mature paid habits can give a high-priced rights strategy more time to recover.
