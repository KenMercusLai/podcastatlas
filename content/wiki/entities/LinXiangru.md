# 蔺相如 / Lin Xiangru

蔺相如 / Lin Xiangru enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-74-huanyuan-wanbi-guizhao-zhenxiang-lg1irvbbc1wp8oph2kmpatfoxebk]] as the [[ZhaoState|赵国]] envoy sent to [[QinState|秦国]] with [[HeShiBi|和氏璧]]. The episode presents him as solving [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]]'s dilemma over [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]]'s promise to exchange fifteen cities for the jade.

His strategy is to accept the exchange so Zhao does not appear to reject Qin's formal offer, then make Qin's failure to name and transfer the promised cities visible. When Lin Xiangru sees Qin enjoying the jade without discussing the cities, he retrieves it through a ruse, sends it back to Zhao by a hidden route, and remains in Qin to face the consequences.

The source therefore makes him the human operator of [[WanbiGuizhaoDiplomacy|完璧归赵式外交]]. He protects the state asset while accepting personal danger, and Qin ultimately releases him because killing the envoy would not recover the jade and would worsen Qin-Zhao relations.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-76-shui-geile-chuqingxiangwang-de-zixin-lsut4cfic7xbbrmbmimrruf7n6y0]] uses Lin Xiangru only as recent background. The host suggests that Qin's later attack on [[ZhaoState|赵国]] may have been shaped not only by Zhao's refusal to join an attack on [[QiState|齐国]], but also by lingering irritation after Lin's successful return of the jade. The claim is framed as plausible inference, not as a directly recorded motive.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-77-lianpo-yu-linxiangru-shishang-zui-zhuming-de-yidui-hao-jiyou-lmelnhhtfuzezvmmsuw4yf10plsb]] makes Lin Xiangru central again at [[Mianchi|渑池]]. When [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] makes [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] play the se at a banquet, Lin forces a matching Qin performance by demanding that Qin strike a fou for Zhao. The episode reads the move as diplomatic parity under threat: Lin prevents the meeting from becoming a one-sided humiliation.

The same source then turns Lin from daring envoy into restrained minister. After Zhao He promotes him above [[LianPo|廉颇]], Lin avoids Lian Po rather than escalating the rank dispute. His explanation creates [[JiangXiangHeInternalUnity|将相和式内部团结]]: Qin hesitates because Zhao has both Lin and Lian Po, so private rivalry between them would weaken the state's deterrent.
