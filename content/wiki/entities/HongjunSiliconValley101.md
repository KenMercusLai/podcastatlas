# 红君 / Hongjun (硅谷101)

## Overview
红君 / Hongjun is the [[SiliconValley101|硅谷101]] host and moderator of the source's [[BundConference|外滩大会]] roundtable on agent transactions.

## Current Profile
In this bounded source, Hongjun organizes the discussion around a practical trust test: whether panelists have actually let an agent pay, what infrastructure would make that safe, and how much they might delegate one year later. Her prompts connect payment networks, platforms, smartphones, and model systems into one transaction chain.

## Key Characteristics
- Moderates technology and business discussions for 硅谷101.
- Uses concrete transaction examples to expose ambiguous intent, unsafe execution, recommendation opacity, and delegated-spend limits.
- Frames agent commerce as both human-to-agent delegation and agent-to-agent exchange.
