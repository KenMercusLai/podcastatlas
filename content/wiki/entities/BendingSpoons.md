# Bending Spoons

Bending Spoons appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-googles-ai-brain-drain-spacexs-huge-quarter-airtables-90-collapse-us-data-fuels-china-ai-42362555]] as the Milan-based software acquirer of [[Airtable]]. The hosts describe the company as a buyer of challenged but still interesting software assets and treat its Airtable deal as a disciplined operator's response to a reset SaaS valuation.

The episode's Bending Spoons case sits between ordinary strategic acquisition and [[PrivateEquityAITransformation]]. [[DavidSacks|David Sacks]] argues the likely opportunity is to cut cost, refocus on [[ProductLedGrowth|product-led growth]], and convert Airtable's remaining user base into a higher-margin product rather than keep the old venture-backed sales motion intact.
