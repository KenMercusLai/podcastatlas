# 《红楼梦的两个世界》 / Two Worlds of Dream of the Red Chamber

[[ritan-chuantai-hongloumeng-haishi-keyi-du-yidu-de-587167638]] recommends [[YuYingshi|余英时]]'s 《红楼梦的两个世界》 near the end of its discussion of editions, sequels, and Redology schools. The source uses the recommendation as a way to keep scholarship useful while still returning to [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]] as a literary work.

The page is source-scoped: the episode does not summarize the book's argument in detail. Within this wiki, the recommendation belongs to [[RedChamberVersionAndSequelReading]], [[SuoYinReading]], and [[InterpretationAndOverinterpretation]] because it appears beside the warning against treating the novel only as a code to solve.
