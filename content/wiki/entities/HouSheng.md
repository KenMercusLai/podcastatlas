# 后胜 / Hou Sheng

后胜 / Hou Sheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-2-kongzi-zai-luguo-de-shenshi-zhimi-lphy12exg4u7f-30qkir96gv634r]] as the [[QiState|齐国]] chancellor under [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]]. The episode says Qin repeatedly bribes him, and that he advises Qi Wang Jian not to concern himself with other states' survival.

His source role is less a full biography than a mechanism for late Qi isolation. Qi is the nearest major state to [[LuState|鲁国]] and could plausibly have coveted Lu, but the episode argues that post-[[LeYi|乐毅]] weakness and Hou Sheng's Qin-funded advice leave Qi unwilling to act. Hou Sheng therefore helps explain why [[ChuState|楚国]], not Qi, becomes the final destroyer of Lu.

The page is source-scoped. Later sources may need to distinguish Hou Sheng's personal agency from broader [[CoalitionSelfPreservationFailure|联盟自保失灵]], [[ShieldStateRescueFailure|屏障国救援失灵]], and Qi's long post-restoration decline.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-3-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhenzheng-yiyishang-chuilian-tingzheng-taihou-liq9d3itmbar00ij43zzed-mulul]] moves Hou Sheng from background isolation mechanism to immediate adviser. After [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]] continues [[JunWangHou|君王后]]'s pro-Qin line, Hou Sheng advises him to go personally to Qin, setting up [[YongmenSima|雍门司马]]'s remonstrance.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-120-1-shui-shi-huangdi-zhong-de-mabaonan-lhw8vsj6ta6rjdkugcvfohaqqxc0]] records Hou Sheng from Qin's post-unification accusation layer. The episode says Qin's legitimacy story blamed [[QiState|齐国]] on Qi Wang Jian's trust in Hou Sheng, alleged rupture of Qin-Qi relations, and supposed rebellion plotting. This keeps Hou Sheng as a negative explanatory figure, while source-scoping the charge as part of [[ConquestSelfDefenseNarrative|兼并自卫叙事]].
