# 后胜 / Hou Sheng

## Overview
后胜 / Hou Sheng is the late-[[QiState|齐国]] chancellor portrayed as a Qin-funded adviser whose influence helps keep [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]] outside anti-Qin action and nearly draws him into a personal visit to Qin.

## Current Profile
Hou Sheng operates less as a standalone biographical subject than as the center of a captured advice network. Qin rewards him and Qi guests who travel there; their favorable reports make continued peace look like evidence of durable friendship. His advice contributes to non-intervention around Lu, discourages war preparation, and culminates in a proposal that Qi Wang Jian personally congratulate Qin at Xianyang. [[YongmenSima|雍门司马]] blocks the journey, but the broader information distortion remains.

## Key Characteristics
- Receives Qin benefits in the sources and advises Qi not to intervene for other states.
- Reinforces isolation through guests whose rewarded Qin visits return favorable assessments.
- Treats decades of quiet Qin-Qi relations as grounds for avoiding renewed military preparation.
- Recommends a personal royal journey to Qin at the terminal stage of unification.
- Becomes part of both later blame for Qi's fall and Qin's own post-conquest justification narrative.

## Evidence
- **Isolation and nearby non-action:** [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-2-kongzi-zai-luguo-de-shenshi-zhimi-lphy12exg4u7f-30qkir96gv634r]] links Qin bribery of Hou Sheng to Qi's unwillingness to intervene around nearby Lu.
- **Personal-visit advice:** [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-3-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhenzheng-yiyishang-chuilian-tingzheng-taihou-liq9d3itmbar00ij43zzed-mulul]] places his recommendation at the transition from long peace to immediate terminal danger.
- **Captured information channel and readiness advice:** [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-4-shishang-neng-cheng-juying-de-junwang-qiwangjian-livcg5uz60ne-6oje-qzjfpghhph]] says Hou Sheng and Qin-rewarded guests repeatedly return pro-Qin judgments and advise against strengthening war preparation.
- **Later accusation layer:** [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-120-1-shui-shi-huangdi-zhong-de-mabaonan-lhw8vsj6ta6rjdkugcvfohaqqxc0]] records Qin's post-unification story blaming Qi Wang Jian's trust in Hou Sheng while presenting the charge as part of Qin's legitimacy construction.

## Qualifications
The sources are hostile to Hou Sheng and do not provide his own defense, the precise form of payments, or an independent account of his motives. Qin's later accusation against Qi is preserved as [[ConquestSelfDefenseNarrative|兼并自卫叙事]], not accepted uncritically as neutral fact.

## What Changed
- His influence is now understood as a recurring guest-and-reward information channel, not only one minister's advice.
- His policy role now explicitly includes discouraging military readiness.
- The proposed Qin visit is completed as a failed recommendation after Yongmen Sima's intervention.

## Relationships
- [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]] - ruler whose threat perception and travel decision he influences.
- [[QinState|秦国]] - external power said to reward him and associated guests.
- [[YongmenSima|雍门司马]] - remonstrant who defeats his personal-visit recommendation.
- [[DistantStateAppeasementTrap|远国安抚误读]] - strategic pattern sustained by his advice.
- [[LongPeaceReadinessAtrophy|长期和平战备退化]] - military consequence worsened by his no-preparation recommendation.
- [[ConquestSelfDefenseNarrative|兼并自卫叙事]] - later Qin narrative in which he becomes an explanatory villain.
