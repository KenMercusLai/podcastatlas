# Robert Peel

## Overview

Robert Peel was a British Conservative prime minister remembered in this source through two exceptionally durable associations: repeal of the Corn Laws and creation of the Metropolitan Police.

## Current Profile

[[35-the-prime-ministers-world-cup-glt6124252189]] presents Peel as a politician whose public reputation benefits from clear, teachable institutional associations. Corn Law repeal and the Metropolitan Police give voters concise reasons to recognize him, helping him defeat the seeded [[RobertWalpole|Robert Walpole]] with 64%.

The hosts regard Walpole's foundational role and longevity as strong reasons to rank him higher, so Peel's victory becomes evidence that accessible schoolbook memory can outweigh diffuse constitutional importance. The result does not establish that Peel's premiership was more consequential overall.
