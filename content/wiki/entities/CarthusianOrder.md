# Carthusian Order

## Overview

The Carthusian Order is the monastic community that guards the formula for [[Chartreuse]] and supplies [[the-formula-the-formula|The Formula]] with its contrasting model of secrecy.

## Current Profile

Former Carthusian Father Michael Halloran says Chartreuse supports the community's contemplative life rather than wealth or fame as ends in themselves. After leaving with knowledge of the formula, he reports that the monks neither threatened nor legally restrained him and instead trusted him not to disclose it.

The episode uses that account to develop [[SecrecyAsCommunityBond]], but the evidence is one former member's reported testimony rather than an institutional history, governance record, or independent account of recipe custody.

## Key Characteristics

- Contemplative religious order represented as steward of a secret liqueur formula.
- Connects commercial production to support for communal religious life.
- Relies, in Halloran's account, on trust and shared obligation rather than post-exit legal threat.
- Supplies a normative contrast to corporate trade-secret enforcement.

## Evidence

### Purpose of production

- [[the-formula-the-formula]] attributes to Halloran the claim that Chartreuse supports contemplative life rather than a pursuit of wealth or fame.

### Trust after departure

- [[the-formula-the-formula]] reports that Halloran left with formula knowledge without being threatened or legally restrained.

## Qualifications

This page is based on one structured episode summary and a former member's reported account. It does not independently verify the recipe, number of custodians, legal arrangements, institutional history, or whether every departure has been handled similarly.
