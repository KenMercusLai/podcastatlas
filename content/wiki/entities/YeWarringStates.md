# 邺城 / Ye (Warring States)

邺城 / Ye enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-2-ltlobdujy-hdipimfdupvwod2ech]] as the place where [[JinBi|晋鄙]]'s [[WeiState|魏国]] rescue army stops during the [[Handan|邯郸]] crisis. [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] initially sends Jin Bi with 100,000 troops to aid [[ZhaoState|赵国]], but Qin's threat causes him to halt the army at Ye and watch events.

In this source, Ye is not developed as a full urban or administrative history. Its role is operational: it marks the gap between nominal rescue and effective relief. The army has moved, but it has not reached Zhao, so Ye becomes the visible location of [[CoalitionSelfPreservationFailure|coalition self-preservation failure]] and [[CoalitionTransitDependency|transit-dependent rescue]].

## Connections
- [[JinBi|晋鄙]] and [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] - commander and ruler connected to the stopped army.
- [[WeiState|魏国]], [[ZhaoState|赵国]], and [[Handan|邯郸]] - sending state, endangered state, and intended rescue target.
- [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] and [[QinState|秦国]] - threat source causing the halt.
- [[CoalitionTransitDependency|联盟救援借道依赖]] and [[CoalitionSelfPreservationFailure|联盟自保失灵]] - concepts made visible by the halted position.
