# Alexander Farnese / Duke of Parma

[[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] adds Parma before Antwerp's fall, as the battle-hardened Spanish commander strengthening [[PhilipII|Philip II]]'s position in the [[LowCountries|Low Countries]] while [[FrancisDrake]]'s return forces [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]] to judge the risk of angering [[Spain]].

Alexander Farnese, Duke of Parma, appears in [[695-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-the-shadow-war-part-5-glt5637968455]] as [[PhilipII|Philip II]]'s most effective commander in the [[LowCountries|Low Countries]]. The episode identifies him as governor general of the Netherlands and captain general of the Army of Flanders from 1578.

Parma's role combines war and diplomacy. By 1584, he has reversed earlier Spanish weakness, recovered much of the south for [[Spain]], offered surrender terms that restore Catholicism, and put enough pressure on the [[DutchRevolt]] that English leaders fear the rebellion could fail.

His siege of [[Antwerp]] is the episode's decisive demonstration. Forts, a pontoon bridge across the Scheldt, and failed rebel fire-ship attacks make the city's surrender in August 1585 the crisis that sends [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]] toward the [[TreatyOfNonsuch]].
