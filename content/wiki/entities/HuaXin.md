# 华歆 / Hua Xin

华歆 / Hua Xin enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-995-ta-zuishang-cibei-weihe-duoci-cansha-enren-llicukoezccd5iycin4uq7qun51k|Hanji 995]] as the later court-appointed administrator of [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]] after the deaths and contests around [[ZhuHaoLateHan|朱皓]], [[ZhugeXuan|诸葛玄]], and [[ZeRong|笮融]].

The source does not give Hua Xin an independent character study. His role is administrative closure: after Ze Rong kills Zhu Hao, occupies Yuzhang, loses to [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]], flees into the mountains, and is killed by locals, the court appoints Hua Xin to the post.
