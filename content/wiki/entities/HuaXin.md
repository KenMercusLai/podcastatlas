# 华歆 / Hua Xin

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-941-shenchu-guanchang-ta-jing-xiang-ganfan-huangdi-liu-fqiz9pi7u6cltrn-cjmjz-r|Hanji 941]] backfills Hua Xin's earlier risk judgment before his later Yuzhang administrative branch. When [[WangFenLateHan|王芬]] seeks support for the plan to seize [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] and install [[HefeiMarquisLateHan|合肥侯]], Hua Xin warns [[TaoqiuHongLateHan|陶丘洪]] that Wang Fen lacks the capacity for such a dangerous action.

The source pairs Hua Xin with [[CaoCao|曹操]] as a refusal figure. Both cite the difficulty of imperial deposition, but Hua Xin's direct source role is practical dissuasion: he converts Taoqiu Hong's initial eagerness into withdrawal before the plot collapses.

华歆 / Hua Xin enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-995-ta-zuishang-cibei-weihe-duoci-cansha-enren-llicukoezccd5iycin4uq7qun51k|Hanji 995]] as the later court-appointed administrator of [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]] after the deaths and contests around [[ZhuHaoLateHan|朱皓]], [[ZhugeXuan|诸葛玄]], and [[ZeRong|笮融]].

The source does not give Hua Xin an independent character study. His role is administrative closure: after Ze Rong kills Zhu Hao, occupies Yuzhang, loses to [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]], flees into the mountains, and is killed by locals, the court appoints Hua Xin to the post.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1029-guoshi-wushuang-taishici-yinuo-qianjin-yi-dangxian-lvckwlhz5g-oappy89thye9oyrto|Hanji 1029]] gives that office a sharper limit. [[TaishiCi|太史慈]] reports to [[SunCe|孙策]] that Hua Xin has good conduct but lacks strategy and cannot control [[TongZhiLateHan|僮芝]] in [[LulingLateHan|庐陵]] or [[PanyangLocalForces|番阳民帅]] near [[HaihunLateHan|海昏]]. Hua Xin therefore becomes a morally respectable but weak-control administrator in Sun Ce's Yuzhang assessment.
