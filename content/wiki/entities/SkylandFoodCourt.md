# 食通天 Skyland Food Court

食通天 Skyland Food Court appears in [[kafeidou-chuantong-meishi-guangchang-jielian-bidian-dashidaimen-yudao-naxie-fazhan-zuai-1007530222]] as a newer food-court operator still expanding in high-end malls such as MixC and Hang Lung properties. The source contrasts Skyland with older [[FoodRepublic|大食代 / Food Republic]] decline by stressing branded tenant resources, including [[LuckinCoffee|Luckin Coffee]] and snack-chain relationships, and some investment in small merchants.

Skyland's role in the wiki is to show that [[MallFoodCourtDecline]] does not mean food-court-like demand disappeared. The old stall-rental format weakens, but operators that can bring stronger brands, better mall fit, and more active tenant support may still find room inside [[ShoppingCenterRestaurantization|restaurantized shopping centers]].

## Connections
- [[FoodRepublic]] - older model the source contrasts against.
- [[SceneLedFoodMarket]] and [[CityMart]] - neighboring successor formats.
- [[FoodCourtSecondLandlord]] - underlying model Skyland partially upgrades.
- [[ShoppingCenterRestaurantization]], [[RestaurantExperienceDesign]], and [[ChainRestaurantStandardization]] - mall and brand capabilities that make the newer format viable.
