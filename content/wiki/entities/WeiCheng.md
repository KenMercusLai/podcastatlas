# 魏成 / Wei Cheng

魏成 / Wei Cheng appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-1-lk-6ejgmjhvukqgtlz2lowu8qx-v]] as one of [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]]'s two chancellor candidates, opposite [[DiHuang|翟黄]]. The source says Wei Wenhou ultimately chooses Wei Cheng after hearing [[LiKe|李克]]'s character-testing criteria.

Wei Cheng's advantage is not described as administrative detail. The episode says he uses most of his generous income outside his household and thereby attracts [[ZiXia|子夏]], [[TianZifang|田子方]], and [[DuanGanmu|段干木]], people Wei Wenhou treats as teachers. That makes Wei Cheng a case for [[TalentReferralQuality|talent referral quality]] and extends [[TalentAttractionThroughRespect|respect-based talent attraction]] beyond the ruler alone.
