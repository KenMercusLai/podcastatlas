# 吕布 / Lü Bu

吕布 / Lü Bu is developed across the Hanji 1018-1019 late-Han branch as a militarily dangerous but politically unstable powerholder. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1018-ta-weihe-rang-caocao-jidan-sunce-weiju-liubei-wanxi-lqccdhrjmero-gt7vsaq2ejuhhw8|Hanji 1018]] shows him expecting [[ChenDeng|陈登]] to secure the [[Xuzhou|徐州]] governor title from [[CaoCao|曹操]], then raging when Chen Deng and [[ChenGui|陈珪]] benefit while he does not.

Chen Deng's answer turns Cao Cao's mistrust into [[JiFuBaoYang|饥附饱扬]], a diagnosis that Lü Bu's attachment lasts only while he remains dependent. The same episode gives Lü Bu a tactical success: after [[ChenGui|陈珪]] identifies [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s coalition as brittle, Lü Bu persuades [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]] and [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] to reverse sides against [[ZhangXunYuanShu|张勋]].

The later half of Hanji 1018 also shows the cost of Lü Bu's short horizon. He ignores [[GaoShun|高顺]]'s warning not to demand supplies from [[ZhangBaLateHan|张霸]] in person, fails to get them, and loses face. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1019-jiemi-chenminwang-liuchong-de-chuanqi-yisheng-ll2fma7wupp5gczudysfett5lhio|Hanji 1019]] then keeps the personnel-judgment branch compact: Lü Bu trusts [[WeiXuLateHan|魏续]] more than Gao Shun even though the source treats Gao Shun as the loyal one.
