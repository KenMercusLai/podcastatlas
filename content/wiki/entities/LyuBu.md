# 吕布 / Lü Bu

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-947-dongzhuo-shoufu-lvbu-kaiqi-chengba-zhilu-lvr-24l6j8f6zvpfcmqgknmgywa1|Hanji 947]] supplies Lü Bu's entry into Dong Zhuo's command before the Yangren and assassination branches. The source presents him as [[DingYuanLateHan|丁原]]'s trusted subordinate and clerk, then as the actor Dong Zhuo reads as ambitious enough to be turned.

Lü Bu kills Ding Yuan and joins [[DongZhuo|董卓]], bringing Ding Yuan's troops into Dong Zhuo's expanding Luoyang control. This backfills the later page's pattern: Lü Bu is dangerous because he can convert close access and personal calculation into decisive violence, long before [[PersonalGuardAttackSurface|贴身侍卫反噬]] culminates in Dong Zhuo's assassination.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-955-guanyu-zhan-huaxiong-qishi-shi-wei-lishi-lndkrj-fnepye8iu-ifgyh-vzysf|Hanji 955]] adds a pre-assassination battlefield sabotage branch before the later Dong Zhuo guard-reversal sequence. [[DongZhuo|董卓]] sends [[HuZhenLateHan|胡轸]] with Lü Bu against [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]], but the episode emphasizes Bingzhou-Liangzhou tension and Lü Bu's resentment at serving under Hu Zhen.

The practical result is factional disruption inside Dong Zhuo's force. Lü Bu spreads false reports, throws Hu Zhen's camp into disorder, and gives Sun Jian the opening to win at [[YangrenBattleLateHan|阳人之战]] and kill [[HuaXiongLateHan|华雄]]. The same episode later says Sun Jian defeats Lü Bu again after entering [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], keeping Lü Bu dangerous but operationally unreliable before his later anti-Dong-Zhuo assassination role.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-962-dongzhuo-yu-lvbu-daodi-weishenme-juelie-lgp3kxfqnhokibf6kpli6p4j8iti|Hanji 962]] supplies Lü Bu's pre-assassination break with [[DongZhuo|董卓]]. The episode presents Lü Bu as Dong Zhuo's trusted close guard because of his riding, archery, and strength, but the father-son language cannot absorb Dong Zhuo's violent temper, the thrown-hand-halberd incident, or Lü Bu's fear that an affair near Dong Zhuo will be exposed.

This source therefore gives the psychological and physical precondition for Hanji 963. [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] can persuade Lü Bu only after the guard relationship has become a [[PersonalGuardAttackSurface|贴身侍卫反噬]]: Lü Bu already has access, and Wang Yun's argument makes that access usable against Dong Zhuo.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-963-shishang-bei-diantian-deng-de-diyiren-shi-ta-lkpkhlpkv3xpw-hur4b7sh-3druk|Hanji 963]] supplies Lü Bu's direct role in killing [[DongZhuo|董卓]]. Under [[WangYunLateHan|王允]]'s plan, Lü Bu controls the guard channel, places [[LiSuLateHan|李肃]] and other armed men at [[WeiyangPalace|未央宫]], announces that there is an imperial edict against a traitorous minister, and then kills Dong Zhuo himself.

The episode makes Lü Bu's merit real before later sources make it politically corrosive. After Dong Zhuo's death, [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] rewards him with general rank, `假节`, and the Wen marquis title, and he joins Wang Yun in the new court arrangement. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-964-bie-jiaoao-bie-jiaoao-bie-jiaoao-lo2-edzffu0v9ixoabc06rx-oqoq|Hanji 964]] then supplies the immediate merit-claim layer: Lü Bu proposes killing Dong Zhuo's private soldiers and distributing Dong Zhuo's wealth, but Wang Yun refuses both. The episode emphasizes Lü Bu's public boasting about his role in killing Dong Zhuo, making his real merit a case of [[PostSuccessArrogance|得势后的骄傲]] and the setup for Hanji 965's warning about publicized help.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-965-bangguo-bieren-de-mang-weishenme-yao-shao-ti-ltjd-cv59hnm-n8ar9jxdogp9q0q|Hanji 965]] backfills Lü Bu's attempted cleanup after [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s death. He sends [[LiSuLateHan|李肃]] against [[NiuFuLateHan|牛辅]], but Li Su is defeated near Shan County and flees back toward Hongnong; Lü Bu executes him for the failed mission. The episode therefore shows Lü Bu's anti-Dong-Zhuo side still unable to neutralize the armed remnant field before Li Jue and Guo Si move on Chang'an.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-966-meiyou-yexin-budong-quanmou-jiu-bie-dang-lingdao-lldn3hi1gl5v3axvklwakgf6vx3u|Hanji 966]] backfills Lü Bu's final Chang'an moment before the Hanji 967 flight sequence. When [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] take [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]], Lü Bu's Shu soldiers open the city from within, he fails to stop the plunder, and he escapes with several hundred riders while carrying [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s head. He urges [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] to flee with him, but Wang Yun refuses to abandon [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-967-ta-canyu-mousha-dongzhuo-jingneng-taoguo-sishen-weilie-lv24biyswj9ljdld5htynhauhph3|Hanji 967]] backfills Lü Bu's immediate post-[[DongZhuo|董卓]] movement before the Yuan Shao alliance in Hanji 970. After leaving [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] through Wuguan toward Nanyang, he first attaches to [[YuanShu|袁术]], who honors him for killing Dong Zhuo. Lü Bu then overclaims that merit, allows his troops to plunder, loses Yuan Shu's confidence, shifts to [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]], and finally moves to [[YuanShao|袁绍]] under [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]]'s bounty pressure.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-970-yuanshao-ansha-lvbu-weihe-hui-shibai-ln2pnvit-qgpnhn-pne7o-p1irrs|Hanji 970]] backfills Lü Bu's departure from [[YuanShao|袁绍]] before the later Yanzhou-entry branch. Lü Bu first joins Yuan Shao against [[ZhangYanLateHan|张燕]] and the [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]], but the alliance deteriorates because Yuan Shao dislikes the conduct of Lü Bu's soldiers and Lü Bu reads the hostility.

The episode adds one of Lü Bu's most important survival episodes before his Xuzhou and Yanzhou reversals. In the [[YuanShaoLyuBuAssassinationAttempt|袁绍刺吕布未遂]], Yuan Shao uses appointment and escort language as a killing cover, but Lü Bu detects the danger, leaves zither music sounding in his tent, escapes through a prepared passage, and then returns toward [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]]. This supports the later page's picture of Lü Bu as politically unstable, but not simply gullible or tactically unaware.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-973-changpingguan-zhizhan-shengsi-zhidi-zhanhou-jing-chonggui-yuhao-lgazc9dewfayb8qnkncfkfa12yet|Hanji 973]] gives Lü Bu's Yanzhou entry as the sudden reversal that interrupts [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s renewed [[Xuzhou|徐州]] offensive. [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] and [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] welcome Lü Bu into [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]], forcing Cao Cao back from the Tao Qian campaign.

This source is not yet interested in Lü Bu's motives or Zhang Miao's prior oath with him. It records the operational effect: Lü Bu becomes the outside force that turns Yanzhou disaffection into a province-level military crisis.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-974-caocao-xiang-ta-tuo-qi-xian-zi-weihe-zhuanshen-ai-daozi-lhfabab7xv2vfsgnhr9ozwqhmhd|Hanji 974]] adds the alliance prelude to Lü Bu's [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] entry. After leaving [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and passing through [[Chenliu|陈留]] on the way toward [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]], Lü Bu visits [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]], takes his hand, and forms a sworn connection that later makes Zhang Miao both more suspect to Yuan Shao and more available as an ally.

This source makes Lü Bu an opportunity as much as an aggressor. [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] can persuade Zhang Miao to act because Lü Bu supplies the military force and prestige that local Yanzhou plotters lack, while [[BianRangLateHan|边让]]'s killing supplies the fear that makes inviting an outsider plausible.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-975-yanzhou-zhiluan-xunyu-ruhe-bang-caocao-shou-dabenying-ln0hh4jrokkwtqsmxmqhdjzcosdr|Hanji 975]] adds Lü Bu's entry into [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] before the later Puyang and Xuzhou branches. [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] and [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] welcome him as Yanzhou governor while [[CaoCao|曹操]] is away attacking [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]], and [[XunYu|荀彧]] detects the move because Lü Bu's route does not fit the cover story that he is coming to help attack [[Xuzhou|徐州]].

The episode therefore introduces Lü Bu as both a military threat and a test of enemy route-reading. His later occupation of [[PuyangLateHan|濮阳]] follows [[XiahouDun|夏侯惇]]'s recall to [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]], while the old-woman and street-rhyme detail is kept source-scoped as a [[BattlefieldInformationControl|战场信息控制]] story rather than a settled independent fact.

吕布 / Lü Bu is developed across the Hanji 999-1000, Hanji 1008-1009, and Hanji 1015-1019 late-Han branch as a militarily dangerous but politically unstable powerholder. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu|Hanji 999]] gives the first Xuzhou status inversion. [[YuanShu|袁术]] offers grain if Lü Bu attacks [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s [[Xiapi|下邳]] rear, and after [[ZhangFei|张飞]] kills [[CaoBaoLateHan|曹豹]], [[XuDanLateHan|许耽]] opens the city. Lü Bu captures Liu Bei's family and turns from Liu Bei's guest into the holder of Xuzhou, making the episode a case of [[GrainLeverageAllianceBetrayal|粮食诱导式同盟背叛]] before his later threat-driven attack on Liu Bei.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-978-gushen-quantui-shuwan-dijun-ta-zenme-zuodao-de-lhgd8vsvuaf5oi9aefh0wxabmzwv|Hanji 978]] adds the earlier Yanzhou pressure layer through rumor, hostage leverage, and local agents. Guo Gong's arrival frightens [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]] partly because he may cooperate with Lü Bu; Lü Bu's side also seizes [[JinYunLateHan|靳允]]'s family and sends [[FanYiLateHan|范仪]] toward [[FanCountyLateHan|范县]]. The same sequence shows limits in that pressure because [[XunYu|荀彧]] deters Guo Gong and Jin Yun kills Fan Yi after [[ChengYu|程昱]]'s persuasion.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-979-zaoyu-tongliao-beici-ta-ruhe-wei-caocao-liwan-kuanglan-luu58a2ww11g-radh-5g4l7fcis|Hanji 979]] adds an earlier Yanzhou missed-opportunity layer. [[CaoCao|曹操]] says Lü Bu would have been far more dangerous had he occupied [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]], blocked the Taishan-Kangfu route, and cut the grain road; instead Lü Bu returns toward [[PuyangLateHan|濮阳]]. The episode therefore frames Lü Bu's limitation as strategic assessment, not only later factional instability.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-980-lvbu-xiansha-caocao-kankan-yi-ju-hua-ruhe-niuzhuan-lishi-lgggw3vmonu-3wvzabvg943v5qcg|Hanji 980]] backfills Lü Bu's earlier [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] pressure around [[PuyangBattleLateHan|濮阳之战]]. His side uses [[PuyangTianClan|濮阳田氏]]'s false inside-response signal to draw [[CaoCao|曹操]] into [[PuyangLateHan|濮阳]], and a Lü Bu cavalryman reportedly catches Cao Cao during the escape but fails to recognize him.

The same source makes Lü Bu's danger and limitation coexist. He can nearly destroy Cao Cao in the city fight, but the episode's later attrition branch shows grain exhaustion, locust pressure, and defeat by [[LiJinLateHan|李进]] at [[ChengshiCountyLateHan|乘氏县]], reinforcing the host's source-scoped reading that Lü Bu's bravery does not reliably convert into sustained campaign control.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-986-tianyu-qiwang-bixian-lingqi-kuang-lvlvu057-tnz3hal6oodhydvzvbd|Hanji 986]] adds the compact Yanzhou defeat immediately before the Dingtao-focused source. [[CaoCao|曹操]] kills Lü Bu's Juye commanders [[XueLanLateHan|薛兰]] and [[LiFengLateHan|李封]], then defeats Lü Bu after Lü Bu comes to rescue them. The episode preserves Lü Bu here as a reactive battlefield actor whose rescue move gives Cao Cao an opening.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-987-dingtao-zhizhan-caocao-he-lvbu-dianfeng-duijue-lofq0pq8n9rrfvfttunahisy0ave|Hanji 987]] backfills the prior defeat that sends Lü Bu toward Liu Bei. Lü Bu and [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] attack [[CaoCao|曹操]] from Dongming with more than ten thousand troops, but Cao Cao uses dike-and-forest terrain at [[DingtaoBattleLateHan|定陶之战]] to ambush the larger force, capture [[Dingtao|定陶]], and recover surrounding [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] counties.

The same source adds Lü Bu's first Xuzhou reception problem. After fleeing east, Lü Bu speaks to Liu Bei as a fellow frontier man and complains that the Guandong powers wanted him dead after he killed [[DongZhuo|董卓]]. Yet his hospitality misfires: seating Liu Bei on [[LadyYanLyuBu|严氏]]'s bed, having her salute Liu Bei, and calling Liu Bei "old brother" produces a [[PowerEtiquetteReading|权力礼仪细读]] problem before the later Xuzhou alliance reversals.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1000-hao-meng-panluan-jingbi-lvbu-fanqiang-taopao-lluthd9hv-0nqbot4fuctjcuka68|Hanji 1000]] backfills an internal crisis at [[Xiapi|下邳]] after that status inversion: [[HaoMeng|郝萌]]'s night revolt reaches his headquarters, forces him to flee to [[GaoShun|高顺]], and leaves him deciding what to do after [[CaoXingLateHan|曹性]] accuses [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]]. The episode reads his non-punishment of Chen Gong through [[LyuBuFactionalBalance|吕布集团派系平衡]] rather than only as gullibility.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1008-ruhe-pingjia-caocao-de-tuntianzhi-ls8wvh9ox-pcjmt0z8w82opbha-k|Hanji 1008]] first gives him a successful mediation moment: after [[YuanShu|袁术]] sends [[JiLing|纪灵]] against [[LiuBei|刘备]], Lü Bu judges that Liu Bei's fall would expose himself to Yuan Shu's wider encirclement and uses辕门射戟 to force both sides to withdraw. The page treats this as [[CredibleForceDisplayDeterrence|可信武力展示威慑]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1009-liubei-touben-caocao-xianxie-langru-hukou-llm0ds4krkptjdtb71hbusnpzbdc|Hanji 1009]] then shows him turning on Liu Bei after Liu Bei's following grows beyond ten thousand. The episode reads this as [[ThreatDrivenAllianceReversal|威胁感驱动的同盟反转]]: Lü Bu removes an immediate rival but pushes Liu Bei toward [[CaoCao|曹操]].

The same source adds [[YuanHuanLateHan|袁涣]] as a moral check on Lü Bu. Lü Bu wants Yuan Huan to write an abusive letter against Liu Bei, but Yuan Huan refuses, making Lü Bu's demand a case of [[MoralDisengagementRationalization|道德推脱式自我合理化]] rather than useful strategy.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] frames Lü Bu as [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s eastern flank problem. [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] and [[XunYu|荀彧]] argue that if Cao Cao waits until [[YuanShao|袁绍]] is free from the [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] war, Lü Bu could combine with Yuan Shao and make the northern confrontation much more dangerous.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1016-sanguo-luanshi-li-chengui-jiazu-de-mingyun-jueze-ltuckdcxpyxk-xmzp8rt-2gjfzcs|Hanji 1016]] shows the pre-1017 marriage-alliance reversal: Lü Bu first accepts [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s proposal through [[HanYinLateHan|韩胤]], sends his daughter toward Yuan Shu, then follows [[ChenGui|陈珪]]'s warning that the marriage would expose [[Xuzhou|徐州]] and stain Lü Bu with Yuan Shu's illegitimacy.

The result is both tactical and character-revealing. Lü Bu recalls his daughter, sends Han Yin to [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s court, and opens the route for [[ChenDeng|陈登]] to travel toward Cao Cao. The source therefore makes Lü Bu available to Chen Gui's persuasion before the later pages show him angry, suspicious, and dependent.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1017-sanguo-yincang-yingxiong-chendeng-de-chuanqi-wangshi-lhcvtm8fobuzjfyo9uirdcgx2plq|Hanji 1017]] gives the compact diagnosis [[QingYuQuJiu|轻于去就]]: Lü Bu is brave, but his quick changes of affiliation make durable trust nearly impossible.

That source treats Lü Bu's instability as a strategic problem for both [[ChenDeng|陈登]] and [[CaoCao|曹操]]. Chen Deng urges early action, and Cao Cao's "诚难久养" answer marks Lü Bu as a force that can be used only with caution, not safely maintained as a loyal partner.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1018-ta-weihe-rang-caocao-jidan-sunce-weiju-liubei-wanxi-lqccdhrjmero-gt7vsaq2ejuhhw8|Hanji 1018]] shows him expecting Chen Deng to secure the [[Xuzhou|徐州]] governor title from Cao Cao, then raging when Chen Deng and [[ChenGui|陈珪]] benefit while he does not.

Chen Deng's answer turns Cao Cao's mistrust into [[JiFuBaoYang|饥附饱扬]], a diagnosis that Lü Bu's attachment lasts only while he remains dependent. The same episode gives Lü Bu a tactical success: after [[ChenGui|陈珪]] identifies [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s coalition as brittle, Lü Bu persuades [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]] and [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] to reverse sides against [[ZhangXunYuanShu|张勋]].

The later half of Hanji 1018 also shows the cost of Lü Bu's short horizon. He ignores [[GaoShun|高顺]]'s warning not to demand supplies from [[ZhangBaLateHan|张霸]] in person, fails to get them, and loses face. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1019-jiemi-chenminwang-liuchong-de-chuanqi-yisheng-ll2fma7wupp5gczudysfett5lhio|Hanji 1019]] then keeps the personnel-judgment branch compact: Lü Bu trusts [[WeiXuLateHan|魏续]] more than Gao Shun even though the source treats Gao Shun as the loyal one.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1026-jiemi-sanguo-di-yi-mengjiang-lvbu-de-luomu-lhjf-agujuliglqchumtff3tftxg1|Hanji 1026]] closes Lü Bu's arc at [[Xiapi|下邳]]. [[CaoCao|曹操]] besieges and floods the city until Lü Bu's subordinates turn, after which Lü Bu surrenders and offers to serve as Cao Cao's cavalry commander. [[LiuBei|刘备]] reminds Cao Cao of [[DingYuanLateHan|丁原]] and [[DongZhuo|董卓]], so Cao Cao treats Lü Bu's talent as too dangerous to incorporate and orders him strangled. The episode's use of [[ChenShou|陈寿]] and [[WangFuzhi|王夫之]] reinforces the final judgment: Lü Bu's martial force never became durable political trust.
