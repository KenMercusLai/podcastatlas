# 吕布 / Lü Bu

吕布 / Lü Bu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1019-jiemi-chenminwang-liuchong-de-chuanqi-yisheng-ll2fma7wupp5gczudysfett5lhio|Hanji 1019]] in the episode's opening personnel-judgment branch. The source says he speaks and acts erratically, turns quickly against people near him, and fails to reward [[GaoShun|高顺]] despite Gao Shun's loyalty.

The contrast case is [[WeiXuLateHan|魏续]]. Lü Bu trusts Wei Xu more than Gao Shun, but the episode says Wei Xu later participates in the turn against Gao Shun. The branch is not developed into a full Lü Bu biography; it functions as a leadership warning before the episode moves into [[CaoCao|曹操]], [[SunCe|孙策]], and [[LiuChongChenKing|刘宠]].
