# Mike Lazaridis

## Overview

Mike Lazaridis is the engineer and BlackBerry co-CEO whom [[JimBalsillie]] joined at Research in Motion before the company became a major smartphone manufacturer.

## Current Profile

In the bounded source, Lazaridis represents both the engineering partnership behind [[BlackBerry]]'s rise and the hardware commitment opposed to Balsillie's later services strategy. The episode says Lazaridis and new CEO Thorsten Heins believed opening [[BlackBerryMessenger|BBM]] across rival platforms would destroy the hardware business, while Balsillie believed that business was already dead.

Neither source provides Lazaridis's own extended reasoning, so his position should not be reduced to simple blindness. In the later discussion, [[MalcolmGladwell]] uses Lazaridis's extraordinary achievement and the southern Ontario technology ecosystem to complicate a simple failure-to-rethink verdict, while [[AdamGrant]] points to employee losses and discontinued technological possibilities. The dispute shows that personal accomplishment, company outcome, stakeholder harm, and regional spillover are different evaluative units.

## Key Characteristics

- Engineer associated with the founding and technical side of Research in Motion.
- Co-CEO partner in BlackBerry's rise alongside [[JimBalsillie]].
- Advocate for retaining the company's hardware business during the 2011 strategic fork.
- Internal counterparty to the proposal for an open, cross-platform BBM service.
- Counterexample used to test whether persistence should be judged by personal achievement, company survival, stakeholder effects, or wider ecosystem spillovers.

They establish the strategic disagreement and competing evaluation frames, but not every technical, financial, governance, or execution reason behind his position or a causal measure of BlackBerry's regional spillovers.
