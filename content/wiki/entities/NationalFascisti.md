# National Fascisti

## Overview

The National Fascisti was a more militant splinter from the early [[BritishFascisti]] milieu, emerging by 1924 and illustrating the pull from Conservative-adjacent patriotic organization toward harder paramilitary politics.

## Current Profile

The episode introduces the National Fascisti as a splinter rather than a mass movement. Its significance lies in showing that the first British fascist wave contained disputes about militancy and political violence even before Mosley's Blackshirts.

[[VictorBarker|Victor Barker]], also known in the source as Valerie Arkell Smith, Colonel Barker, and Captain Barker, joined the organization. Barker's presence gives the group a distinctive biographical connection, but the episode treats it largely as a teaser and does not establish that identity history as an explanation of the movement.

## Key Characteristics

- Militant offshoot of the early British fascist movement.
- Organization emerging by 1924.
- Evidence of internal division over tactics and intensity.
- Precursor within the wider pre-Mosley fascist field.
- Organization associated in the episode with Victor Barker.
