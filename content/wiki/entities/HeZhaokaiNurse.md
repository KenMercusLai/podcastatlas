# 何昭凯 / He Zhaokai

## Overview
何昭凯 / He Zhaokai is presented as an intensive-care nurse at [[PekingUnionMedicalCollegeHospital|北京协和医院]] and the hospice-care guest in a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] Nurses Day episode.

## Current Profile
Within the source, He connects bedside nursing with [[HospiceCare|安宁疗护]] across symptom relief, comfort, communication, family support, and [[WholePersonEndOfLifeCare|physical, psychological, social, and spiritual care]]. Her account treats nursing as clinical judgment and relational work rather than only execution of orders. She also describes prior study of dance therapy and a Korean hospice placement, using those experiences to discuss embodied support, familiar environments, faith, sensory contact, and farewell.

## Key Characteristics
- Intensive-care nurse whose practice includes medical and surgical patients and repeated exposure to dying.
- Explains hospice as active comfort-focused care rather than abandonment or euthanasia.
- Centers pain, breathlessness, nutrition, elimination, hygiene, dignity, and practical symptom response.
- Treats goals-of-care and DNR communication as continuing, revisable work with patients and families.
- Extends care into psychological, social, spiritual, sensory, relational, and environmental support.
