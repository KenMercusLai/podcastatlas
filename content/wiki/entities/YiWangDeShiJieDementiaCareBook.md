# 《遗忘的世界：失智老人的照护图景》

## Overview
《遗忘的世界：失智老人的照护图景》 is [[TangYongSociologist|唐勇]]'s non-fiction book on dementia care in Shenzhen families.

## Current Profile
In the ingested episode, the book functions as a fieldwork anchor rather than only a publication notice. It gives the conversation concrete cases for dementia variation, remaining memory, family caregiver exhaustion, cross-city care, institutional mismatch, and the difficulty of building a [[DementiaCareSocialSupportSystem]] when professional labor, payment, and community services are incomplete.

## Key Characteristics
- Focuses on dementia older adults and their families in [[Shenzhen]].
- Uses family stories to show both the person with dementia and the caregiver's changing role.
- Supports a public-care argument that family sacrifice cannot be the default long-term solution.
