# 《以美之名》 / In the Name of Beauty

## Overview
《以美之名》 is the medical drama used in a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode to open a discussion of plastic surgery, medical aesthetics, patient motivation, institutional differences, and procedural safety.

## Current Profile
The source does not treat the series as a documentary or technical lesson. It describes the drama as professionally informed enough to support public discussion while using extreme cases, compressed decisions, and heightened conflict for narrative effect. Its value in this wiki is therefore educational and interpretive: plot cases become prompts for [[BotulinumToxinInjectionSafety]], [[MedicalAestheticProviderVerification]], and [[CosmeticProcedureExpectationManagement]], not evidence that viewers can diagnose or choose treatment from fiction.

## Key Characteristics
- Plastic-surgery and medical-aesthetics drama discussed through its early episodes.
- Uses rare or extreme cases to establish stakes and attract attention.
- Depicts both public-hospital and private medical-aesthetic settings.
- Includes clinical environments, props, suturing, poisoning, surgery, and patient-choice conflicts.
- Serves as a public-literacy hook rather than a clinical protocol.

## Evidence
- Professional credibility: [[vol-159-cong-yi-mei-zhi-ming-dao-xianshi-zhengxing-yisheng-jiedu-juzhong-mei-xishuo-de-yimei-neimu-lksohygmb6epma5qgaf76pddx2fk]] says the department atmosphere, medical scenes, props, suturing, and institutional contrasts capture recognizable features.
- Dramatic compression: [[vol-159-cong-yi-mei-zhi-ming-dao-xianshi-zhengxing-yisheng-jiedu-juzhong-mei-xishuo-de-yimei-neimu-lksohygmb6epma5qgaf76pddx2fk]] treats face-replacement, poisoning, and high-risk excision plots as heightened cases whose educational value does not make them typical.
- Public-literacy use: [[vol-159-cong-yi-mei-zhi-ming-dao-xianshi-zhengxing-yisheng-jiedu-juzhong-mei-xishuo-de-yimei-neimu-lksohygmb6epma5qgaf76pddx2fk]] repeatedly moves from a plot point to real-world questions about products, qualifications, symptoms, history disclosure, and surgeon judgment.

## Qualifications
The page reflects discussion of only the early episodes described in one podcast note. It does not independently verify the drama's full plot, production credits, medical consultants, later episodes, or clinical accuracy.
