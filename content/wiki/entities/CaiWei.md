# 蔡伟 / Cai Wei

## Overview
蔡伟 / Cai Wei is the [[AntLingGuang|蚂蚁灵光]] leader interviewed by [[ZhangXiaojunCommercialInterviews|张小珺Jùn｜商业访谈录]] about personal-agent product history, service ecosystems, safety, business models, and future interfaces.

## Current Profile
The source presents Cai as a product and technology operator who spent about ten years in the United States, worked on computer vision at [[Google]], then returned to China for entrepreneurship and investing before joining [[AntGroup|蚂蚁集团]] in 2024. His current thesis is that personal agents become useful only when model intelligence, callable service supply, and trustworthy execution mature together.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses “what could a real human assistant do?” as the first-principles direction for LingGuang.
- Treats initiative as selective attention and timing rather than frequent notification.
- Evaluates products through completed, continuing user matters as well as DAU and retention.
- Expects model capability and product use to shape one another through evaluations, data, and pretraining priorities.
- Distinguishes a focused initial wedge from the much broader long-term assistant vision.
