# 蔡伟 / Cai Wei

## Overview
蔡伟 / Cai Wei is the [[AntLingGuang|蚂蚁灵光]] leader interviewed by [[ZhangXiaojunCommercialInterviews|张小珺Jùn｜商业访谈录]] and [[LateTalk|晚点聊 LateTalk]] about personal-agent product history, service ecosystems, safety, business models, and future interfaces.

## Current Profile
The sources present Cai as a product and technology operator who began a visual-search company in 2011, later worked on Google Photos and Google Lens, returned to China for entrepreneurship and investing, and joined [[AntGroup|蚂蚁集团]] in 2024. Cai's current thesis is that personal agents become useful only when model intelligence, callable service supply, and trustworthy execution mature together, and that product timing depends on recognizing when continuous technical progress crosses a discontinuous user-experience threshold.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses “what could a real human assistant do?” as the first-principles direction for LingGuang.
- Treats initiative as selective attention and timing rather than frequent notification.
- Evaluates products through managed and completed continuing matters as well as DAU and retention.
- Expects model capability and product use to shape one another through evaluations, data, and pretraining priorities.
- Distinguishes a focused initial wedge from the much broader long-term assistant vision.
- Uses the gap between a working demo and end-to-end production delivery as a product-readiness test.
