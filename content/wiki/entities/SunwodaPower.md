# Sunwoda Power / 欣旺达动力

## Overview
Sunwoda Power / 欣旺达动力 appears in the source as one of the battery suppliers receiving more attention from Chinese automakers looking beyond [[CATL|宁德时代]].

## Current Profile
The episode says [[LiAuto|理想汽车]] increased investment in Sunwoda Power and became its second-largest shareholder, while [[Xiaomi]] also announced cooperation with the company. Sunwoda Power therefore functions as a second-line supplier in [[BatterySupplierDiversification]].

## Key Characteristics
- Battery supplier positioned as an alternative or complement to CATL.
- Receives strategic investment from Li Auto in the source account.
- Named by Xiaomi as a cooperation partner.
- Source-scoped case for automaker bargaining and cost control.
