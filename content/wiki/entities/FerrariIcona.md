# Ferrari Icona

Ferrari Icona is the limited-model product category discussed in [[ferrari-1]] as part of modern [[Ferrari]]'s product pyramid. The source groups Icona cars with special series and halo supercars as high-priced, scarce products that can generate disproportionate profit while preserving rarity.

For the wiki, Icona is an explicit product-line example of [[HaloProductStrategy]]. It lets Ferrari sell heritage, scarcity, and collector access without turning every customer car into the same ultra-limited object.
