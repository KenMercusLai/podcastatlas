## Overview

韩安国 / Han Anguo is presented as a senior Western Han official whose steadiness, political calculation, and ability to balance interests had value in an earlier governing environment but did not protect him from a serious frontier intelligence failure under [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]. His page is a bounded account of the episode's final-career portrait, not a complete biography.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-275-dapo-xiongnu-bubai-shenhua-weiqing-ruhe-zuodao-de-2-lpn6t8p8d0iumsd7gabohtevxxqo|Hanji 275]] rejects a simple incompetence story. It characterizes Han Anguo as calm, shrewd, politically flexible, and able to balance competing forces, while also describing him as money-seeking and willing to attach himself to powerful people. Those traits are presented as compatible with the cautious statecraft associated with the Wen-Jing period but increasingly out of step with Wudi's emphasis on frontier initiative and military merit.

The decisive failure is more specific than the era judgment. A captured Xiongnu soldier reportedly tells Han Anguo that the opposing force has already been driven away by [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]]. Han accepts the report and asks to release his soldiers for agricultural work. When the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] return after the force has dispersed, he is left with a reported seven hundred men and cannot prevent plunder.

Wudi's response makes operational failure political. Han Anguo is transferred to [[YoubeipingCommandery|右北平]] after already losing court influence, while younger commanders rise through campaign success. The episode treats his shame, desire to return to the center, illness, and death soon afterward as a combined story of error, marginalization, and [[PoliticalMilitaryEraFit|political-military era mismatch]]. The durable conclusion is narrower: previously demonstrated ability does not substitute for verifying current intelligence or matching the present office's objectives and risk conditions.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior Western Han official portrayed as steady, calculating, politically flexible, and capable of balancing interests.
- Morally mixed court actor whom the episode also describes as money-seeking and willing to cultivate powerful patrons.
- Frontier commander whose acceptance of a captive's report leads to premature demobilization and renewed Xiongnu exposure.
- Older political-military figure contrasted with Wei Qing's rising merit and stronger fit with Wudi's frontier priorities.
- Reassignment and death case used to distinguish durable ability from continued suitability under changed objectives and risk tolerance.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one short podcast summary whose ending is explicitly incomplete. It does not reconstruct Han Anguo's earlier career, the full “死灰复燃” story, his court alliances, every office he held, the complete campaign sequence, or the classical wording behind Sima Qian's evaluation. The deception, demobilization mechanics, roughly seven-hundred-man remnant, losses, emotions, reassignment purpose, and death timing remain episode-attributed. “Being discarded by the era” is an interpretive frame, not proof that cautious administration or political balancing had ceased to be useful throughout Wudi's reign.

## What Changed

- Established a canonical Han Anguo profile centered on the distinction between prior ability and present role fit.
- Preserved the frontier failure as a specific intelligence-verification and demobilization error rather than reducing it to generational symbolism.
- Marked the episode's era-mismatch explanation and detailed chronology as source-scoped.
