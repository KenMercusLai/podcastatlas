## Overview

北匈奴 / Northern Xiongnu enters this page as the late Emperor-He-era frontier field destabilized by [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]]'s fall and transformed by [[Xianbei|Xianbei]] occupation of former territory. Hanji 767 records surrender reversal, chanyu flight, [[YuChuNorthernXiongnu|Yu Chu]]'s destruction, and mass remnant self-identification as Xianbei.

## Current Profile

The source presents Northern Xiongnu power after a severe defeat and leadership breakdown. Some groups that had agreed to submit rebel after they see Dou Xian removed, validating the source's earlier warning that frontier actors are watching Han court stability. The Northern Xiongnu chanyu flees, and Xianbei occupy the abandoned lands.

Yu Chu is the remaining concrete leader in this source. Dou Xian had installed him over remnant forces and planned to escort him back to the royal court, but Yu Chu abandons the arrangement after Dou's dismissal. Emperor He responds by sending Wang Fu and Ren Shang with cavalry; Yu Chu is killed and his force destroyed. The source then marks the Xianbei aftereffect: many former Xiongnu households begin calling themselves Xianbei.

## Key Characteristics

- Steppe-frontier field whose surrender arrangements are sensitive to Han court stability.
- Remnant polity weakened by chanyu flight after prior Northern Xiongnu defeat.
- Contains Yu Chu as a Dou-backed remnant leader who rebels after Dou's fall.
- Loses territory to Xianbei occupation in the source's transition narrative.
- Supplies an early background layer for later Northern Xiongnu and Xianbei frontier pressure.

## Qualifications

This is a narrow late-Han remnant page, not a complete Xiongnu history. The source's large household figure, the exact mechanism of self-identification, and the earlier campaign background remain source-scoped pending additional evidence.
