## Overview

丁夫人 / Lady Ding is [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s original wife and [[CaoAng|曹昂]]'s foster mother in the current source set. Her refusal to reconcile after Cao Ang's death makes her the household figure through whom Wancheng's military cost becomes a durable moral and emotional consequence.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1013 and Hanji 1014 establish the rupture: Cao Ang dies after giving his horse to Cao Cao, Lady Ding blames Cao Cao, and the marriage ends. Hanji 1105 and Hanji 1158 repeat a reconciliation attempt in which Cao Cao visits her natal home and leaves after she does not respond; the later episode adds [[LadyBianCaoCao|卞夫人]]'s reported deference and funeral care. Together the sources give Lady Ding continuing agency through refusal rather than treating her only as a name in Cao Cao's household list.

Hanji 1158 also alleges that Lady Ding caused Cao Ang's birth mother to die by tampering with her food. Because the supplied summary cites no underlying text and the earlier bounded sources do not establish this, the allegation is preserved as source-scoped rather than incorporated into Lady Ding's current profile.

## Key Characteristics

- Original wife of Cao Cao in the episode tradition.
- Foster mother of Cao Ang.
- Makes Cao Ang's death a non-negotiable household rupture.
- Refuses the reported reconciliation attempt at her natal home.
- Remains central to Cao Cao's reported deathbed guilt.
- Retains senior household dignity in Lady Bian's reported treatment of her after separation.

## Evidence

- Foster motherhood and rupture: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1014-caocao-de-15-ge-laopo-dou-you-shui-lv-hyvprg73k8w8jspxjosqwmc69|Hanji 1014]] identifies Lady Ding as Cao Ang's foster mother and links his Wancheng death to her break with Cao Cao.
- Causal sharpening: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] places the rupture after the command-security failure and says she never forgives Cao Cao.
- Refusal and final memory: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1105-guanyu-zhisi-maizang-le-caocao-zenyang-de-mengxiang-lgmk_dlknjpzz7fvzasno6tu7fs3|Hanji 1105]] narrates Cao Cao's failed apology visit and reconnects Lady Ding to his reported final words about Cao Ang.
- Refusal, later dignity, and disputed backstory: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1158-shui-shi-rang-caocao-kuijiu-yisheng-de-nvren-lqokptbwh95sxivohr4la7ims1ch|Hanji 1158]] repeats the failed reconciliation, adds Lady Bian's reported deference and funeral care, and supplies the unsupported allegation about Lady Liu.

## Qualifications

The exact private dialogue, gestures, visit sequence, deathbed wording, meal seating, and funeral arrangements are source-scoped and need comparison with the underlying historical texts. Hanji 1158's allegation that Lady Ding caused Lady Liu's death is not corroborated by the other bounded sources and is not adopted as fact. The current evidence supports a durable rupture and remembered regret, but it does not supply Lady Ding's complete life after separation.
