# 丁夫人 / Lady Ding (Cao Cao)

丁夫人 / Lady Ding enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1014-caocao-de-15-ge-laopo-dou-you-shui-lv-hyvprg73k8w8jspxjosqwmc69|Hanji 1014]] as [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s original wife and [[CaoAng|曹昂]]'s foster mother. The source uses her position to connect the household list back to the Wancheng disaster already present in the wiki's Cao Cao branch.

After Cao Ang dies in the Wancheng aftermath, the episode says Lady Ding breaks with Cao Cao. Her role is therefore not just genealogical. She makes the cost of Cao Cao's earlier security failure visible inside the family, where Cao Ang's death changes marriage, succession, and household memory.
