## Overview

霍显 / Huo Xian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-424-huoguang-qushi-hou-huojia-shi-ruhe-fumie-de-ltisydawuqdhqpffdjv_xkwts90p|Hanji 424]] as [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]]'s wife and the household actor most directly tied to the Huo family's private excess, palace overreach, succession threat, and eventual destruction.

## Current Profile

The source presents Huo Xian less as an officeholder than as a court-family accelerant. After Huo Guang's death, she is said to continue an affair with Feng Zidu, expand Huo Guang's tomb beyond proper scale, enter the palace freely with her daughter [[HuoChengjunWesternHan|霍成君]], and attempt to harm crown prince [[HanYuandi|刘奭]]. These actions make Huo household power look not merely inherited but uncontrolled.

Her role also reaches backward. The episode closes by invoking Ban Gu's criticism that Huo Guang concealed his wife's earlier evil plot and installed his daughter as empress. In this reading, Huo Xian's misconduct matters because Huo Guang's failure to expose or restrain it lets private household crime become dynastic risk.

## Key Characteristics

- Huo Guang's wife whose posthumous household conduct helps explain the family's rapid fall.
- Palace-risk figure because her access to Huo Chengjun and Liu Shi turns harem politics into succession danger.
- Overreach signal because funerary expansion and palace familiarity blur ministerial household and imperial privilege.
- Conspiracy participant who is arrested after the Huo plan against ministers and Xuan is exposed.
- Retrospective blame focus because Ban Gu's criticism makes Huo Guang's concealment of her crime a root of family disaster.
