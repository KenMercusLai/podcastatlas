# 耿鄙 / Geng Bi (late Han)

耿鄙 / Geng Bi (late Han) enters through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-937-zhanshen-zhisi-haoren-zongshi-duozai-duonan-lp7przsmag9ookq7eoisvsmnm5aw|Hanji 937]] as the Liangzhou inspector whose command collapses before the 187 CE western rebellion expands. He trusts the corrupt [[ChengQiu|程球]] and ignores [[FuXie|傅燮]]'s advice to slow down, discipline the army, and wait for [[HanSui|韩遂]]'s rebel coalition to fracture.

When the army reaches [[DiDaoLateHan|狄道]], mutineers kill Cheng Qiu and then Geng Bi. Hanji 937 therefore makes Geng Bi a direct source case for [[FrontierCommandCollapse|边郡军政崩盘]]: personnel corruption and timing failure break the force before a normal campaign can unfold.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-938-lishi-de-jingxing-ruanruo-zhihui-dailai-quru-lnlnrketgfbadhfjbzdjjtrmqfsh|Hanji 938]] continues the consequence layer. The transcript renders him as "耿彼"; this page keeps the late-Han inspector identity but records the correction as source-scoped. His subordinate [[MaTeng|马腾]] rises from low resources through military service, but after Geng Bi is betrayed and killed, Ma Teng continues into rebellion with Han Sui and [[WangGuoLateHan|王国]]. The source ties that move to frontier governance failure: the local official order has become intolerable for both Qiang-Hu groups and ordinary people.
