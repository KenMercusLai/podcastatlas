# Battles of Lexington and Concord

## Overview

The Battles of Lexington and Concord were the April 1775 clashes that turned an imperial constitutional crisis into open armed conflict.

## Current Profile

British regulars moved to seize colonial weapons after Massachusetts had been declared in rebellion. A confused confrontation at Lexington produced the first firing, while militia then fired at British troops at Concord and harried their retreat toward Boston. The episode treats the day as both an armed threshold and evidence that restoring royal authority would require more than a limited raid.

## Key Characteristics

- British expedition directed against colonial military stores.
- Confused opening confrontation at Lexington.
- Rebel fire and organized militia resistance at Concord.
- Costly British retreat toward Boston.
- Transition from preparation for violence to sustained war.
