# 费达生 / Fei Dasheng

## Overview

[[FeiDasheng|费达生]] is presented as a silk-industry reformer who combined technical instruction, farmer organization, and a distributive commitment to improving rural livelihoods.

## Current Profile

Fei Dasheng's work supplied practical substance to the rural-industry questions later associated with her brother [[FeiXiaotong|费孝通]] and [[CocoonFeiXiaotong|《茧》]]. She participated in technical guidance and pursued forms of silk reform intended to let farming households share the benefits rather than merely secure raw material and profit for a factory.

Her career also demonstrates why women in social reform should be treated as practitioners rather than as supporting biography. She continued silk-related work through wartime displacement, and later correspondence indicates that other women social workers recognized her professional standing. The source's discussion of her private relationships is much less secure than its account of her public work.

## Key Characteristics

- Joined technical silk production to rural livelihood improvement.
- Favored farmer participation and benefit sharing over purely enterprise-centered control.
- Contributed practical knowledge to [[FeiXiaotong|费孝通]]'s rural reform thinking.
- Continued professional activity amid wartime disruption.
- Has often been interpreted through kinship or possible literary prototypes despite an independent reform career.

## Qualifications

The source summary does not provide organizational records or a full biography, and it cannot independently measure the outcomes received by farmers. A remembered claim about a romance with Zou Jinheng is supported only indirectly by a descendant's recollection, envelopes, and photographs; contact is better established than romance.

## What Changed

- Created an independent professional profile centered on technical and distributive reform.
- Separated evidence for public work from speculation about romance and fictional prototypes.

## Relationships

- [[FeiXiaotong|费孝通]] - brother whose writing and reform thought drew on her practice.
- [[CocoonFeiXiaotong|《茧》]] - novel partly informed by the industrial and social world in which she worked.
- [[RuralIndustryLedImprovement]] - practical reform model she pursued through sericulture.
- [[RegionalVariationInRuralChina]] - explains the Jiangnan specificity of her silk-based approach.
