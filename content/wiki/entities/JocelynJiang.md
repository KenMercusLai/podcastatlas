# Jocelyn Jiang

## Overview
Jocelyn Jiang is presented in [[ep-19-navigating-the-future-of-workplace-health-and-benefits-with-ai]] as an actuary and vice president of employer and consulting solutions in [[MultiPlan]]'s data and decision science service line.

## Current Profile
Her source-scoped perspective joins actuarial practice, healthcare analytics, and employee-benefit design. She supports predictive and optimization uses of AI while insisting that employer access to health information, discriminatory benefit design, and final decision authority remain bounded by privacy, regulation, and human review.

## Key Characteristics
- Brings an actuarial and consulting perspective to healthcare cost, risk, and benefit-plan decisions.
- Distinguishes population-level employer insight from identifiable member outreach under [[DeidentifiedEmployerHealthAnalytics]].
- Treats AI as an assistant for prediction and scenario testing rather than a replacement for accountable professionals.
- Connects employee trust to motivation, transparency, and voluntary participation in health-data programs.
