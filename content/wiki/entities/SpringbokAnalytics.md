# Springbok Analytics

## Overview

Springbok Analytics is the company presented as combining MRI and artificial intelligence to create three-dimensional, muscle-specific representations for elite sport and rehabilitation.

## Current Profile

[[pushkin-goes-to-the-olympics-pushkin-goes-to-the-olympics]] says the company serves Olympic and professional-sport clients. A roughly ten-minute MRI scan is processed into a “digital twin” showing muscles, bones, balance, development, and muscle health, allowing staff to inspect imbalances that broad strength tests can miss.

In the featured soccer case, the analysis reportedly located weaknesses in the quadriceps, calf, and deep hip muscles after injury. Coaches could then target those areas and repeat scans to check change. The source illustrates a workflow and a commercial claim; it does not supply independent validation or a controlled outcome comparison.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines MRI with AI-assisted anatomical processing.
- Produces three-dimensional muscle and bone representations.
- Identifies individual-muscle and side-to-side differences.
- Connects assessment to targeted training and longitudinal rescanning.
- Serves elite-sport clients according to the episode.
