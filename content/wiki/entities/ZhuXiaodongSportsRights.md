# 朱晓东 / Zhu Xiaodong

Zhu Xiaodong is introduced in [[e237-yangshi-he-fifa-tanpan-fenzheng-beihou-tiyu-saishi-zhuanboquan-de-boyi-yu-shengyi-c3607c08-6eb6-48bd-8250-b41b5b8272e6]] as a sports-event rights specialist and CEO of [[LizanSports]]. The episode says he had participated in Japan-side sales work for the current [[FIFAWorldCup]] cycle, making him the guest who explains pricing, packaging, buyer capacity, and rights-sale timing from inside the rights business.

His role in the source is to pull the [[ChinaCentralTelevision]]-[[FIFA]] dispute away from simple headline prices. He emphasizes that exact prices are rarely public, that multi-event packages can distort per-tournament comparisons, that sponsors can apply pressure without directly determining rights fees, and that healthy rights buying starts from what a broadcaster or platform can realistically monetize.
