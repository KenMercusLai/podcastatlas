# Roger Seheult

## Overview
Roger Seheult is presented in the source as a board-certified pulmonology and sleep-medicine physician, intensive-care clinician at Loma Linda University, and medical educator associated with MedCram.

## Current Profile
In the Huberman Lab interview, Seheult connects respiratory-infection care with sleep, circadian timing, light exposure, metabolic health, air quality, hydration, and patient communication. His style combines clinical experience with mechanistic explanations and a wide evidence range, from trials and observational studies to historical reports and anecdotes.

The profile therefore has two parts that must remain together: he offers a broad systems view of immune resilience, and he repeatedly acknowledges that plausible mechanisms or striking clinical stories are not proof. His sunlight, heat, supplement, and long-COVID discussions are best treated as source-bounded education rather than individualized medical direction.

## Key Characteristics
- Pulmonology and sleep-medicine physician working in intensive-care settings.
- Medical educator who explains immunity through respiratory, metabolic, circadian, mitochondrial, and behavioral connections.
- Uses the NEW START mnemonic to organize foundational health behaviors.
- Differentiates randomized, observational, historical, mechanistic, and anecdotal evidence, though the episode ranges across claims of uneven strength.
- Supports proportionate prevention and individualized medical risk-benefit decisions rather than a single universal intervention.
- Includes faith, gratitude, forgiveness, and community as optional support domains that require patient permission and cultural sensitivity.
