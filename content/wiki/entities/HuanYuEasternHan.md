## Overview

桓虞 / Huan Yu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-755-zhege-chaodai-huangshang-jiu-kaishi-guli-shengwa-le-lj7bdkxkdtjhpo469yxhskjyfdco|Hanji 755]] as the situ who supports returning [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]] captives and livestock seized by [[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]]. His role is narrow but important because the disagreement with [[ZhengHongEasternHan|Zheng Hong]] sets up Hanji 756's court-speech and restitution resolution.

## Current Profile

The source places Huan Yu on the pro-restitution side with [[YuanAnEasternHan|Yuan An]]. Against [[ZhengHongEasternHan|Zheng Hong]] and [[FifthLunEasternHan|Fifth Lun]], Huan Yu treats Meng Yun's credibility warning as strong enough to justify returning the Southern Xiongnu gains.

The episode ends as the court conflict intensifies, with Zheng Hong and Huan Yu clashing over the decision. Huan Yu's current page role is therefore as a ministerial position inside a divided frontier policy debate, not a broader career profile.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han situ in the 85 CE Xiongnu restitution debate.
- Pro-restitution minister who shares Yuan An's side of the policy dispute.
- Debate counterpart to Zheng Hong and Fifth Lun.
- Setup figure for Zhangdi's Hanji 756 insistence that ministers keep arguing rather than resign.
