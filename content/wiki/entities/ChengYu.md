# 程昱 / Cheng Yu

## Overview
程昱 / Cheng Yu is a [[CaoCao|曹操]] adviser whose current profile emphasizes condition-sensitive judgment under pressure. Across the bounded evidence he moves from pre-Cao neutrality advice, to Yanzhou base preservation, to resisting [[YuanShao|袁绍]] dependency, to protecting [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]] before [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] by reading what Yuan Shao will or will not bother to attack.

## Current Profile
The current synthesis treats Cheng Yu as a hard-edged operator whose value lies in turning actor psychology and local conditions into concrete action. In the early Yanzhou material he judges that distant [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] is not reliable protection for [[LiuDaiLateHan|刘岱]], then enters Cao Cao's camp through [[XunYu|荀彧]]'s recommendation and his own Dong'e local standing. During the Yanzhou crisis, his local prestige and route-control work help hold Fan County, Dong'e, and Cangting Ford when [[LyuBu|吕布]] and [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] threaten Cao's remaining base.

The later crisis cases sharpen the pattern. In Hanji 981, Cheng Yu refuses Yuan Shao's family-hostage offer by converting Cao Cao's fear into an inventory of remaining capacity. In the White Horse source, he applies the same opponent-reading logic defensively: seven hundred troops in Juancheng are too few to interest Yuan Shao, but a 2,700-person reinforced garrison would make the city worth attacking. His judgment works because he reads target selection, not because the city is objectively strong.

## Key Characteristics
- Cao-side adviser rooted in Dong Commandery and Yanzhou local knowledge.
- Converts opponent and ally psychology into concrete decisions under pressure.
- Preserves bases, crossings, and city positions rather than only giving abstract strategic advice.
- Blunt anti-dependency voice who stops Cao Cao from entering Yuan Shao's household-pledge orbit.
- Source-scoped dark-adviser figure whose willingness to absorb moral stain is treated as thematic commentary.
- Pre-Guandu small-force defense case at Juancheng, where apparent weakness lowers Yuan Shao's incentive to attack.

## Qualifications
The transcript's "陈玉" in Hanji 976 remains a likely Cheng Yu cluster reference rather than a standalone name proof. Hanji 977's dark-adviser reading is thematic and should not override the more concrete route-control, local-mobilization, hostage-dependency, and Juancheng-defense evidence. Cheng Yu's Juancheng judgment works in this source because of Yuan Shao's target-selection psychology; it should not be generalized into a rule that fewer troops are always safer.
