# 董义 / Dong Yi (Qin)

董义 / Dong Yi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-3-chensheng-zhisi-weishenme-dele-minxin-hanle-renxin-lnx4zs-vsi3zeoixnqskkpexmr3|Qinji 129-3]] as one of the Qin figures [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] sends to assist [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] against the rebels. The supplied transcript writes the name as 董义; this page preserves the source form.

Like [[SimaXin|司马欣]], Dong Yi is not developed as an independent decision-maker in this episode. He matters because his appointment shows Qin adding named support around Zhang Han while [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] and [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]] are running out of reliable forces near Chen County.
