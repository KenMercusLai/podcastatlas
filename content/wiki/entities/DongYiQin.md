# 董义 / 董翳 / Dong Yi (Qin)

董义 / 董翳 / Dong Yi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-3-chensheng-zhisi-weishenme-dele-minxin-hanle-renxin-lnx4zs-vsi3zeoixnqskkpexmr3|Qinji 129-3]] as one of the Qin figures [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] sends to assist [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] against the rebels. The supplied Qinji 129-3 transcript writes the name as 董义; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-139-ta-zaici-yingxiang-hanchao-lp1zujuq0o3ohxc79xqi1phpb5zo|Hanji 139]] uses 董翳. This page keeps both source forms on one canonical page rather than splitting the person.

Like [[SimaXin|司马欣]], Dong Yi is not developed as an independent decision-maker in this episode. He matters because his appointment shows Qin adding named support around Zhang Han while [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] and [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]] are running out of reliable forces near Chen County.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-139-ta-zaici-yingxiang-hanchao-lp1zujuq0o3ohxc79xqi1phpb5zo|Hanji 139]] gives Dong Yi / 董翳 a post-surrender endpoint. When [[XiangYu|项羽]] decides to kill the surrendered Qin soldiers at [[XinanMassacre|新安坑降]], Dong Yi is named with [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] and Sima Xin as one of the senior surrendered Qin figures spared from the massacre.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-143-xiangyu-shi-ruhe-feijin-xinji-nongsi-chuhuaiwang-mixin-lstfzfvbd2h9i39f7y5dxrbxxwv2|Hanji 143]] gives Dong Yi / 董翳 his settlement title. Because he had persuaded [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] to surrender, [[XiangYu|项羽]] makes him Di king over the Shang commandery area. He therefore joins Zhang Han and [[SimaXin|司马欣]] in [[ThreeQinContainment|三秦压制]], converting a surrendered Qin official into a territorial blocker against [[LiuBang|刘邦]].
