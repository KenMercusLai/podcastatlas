# 尤三姐 / You Sanjie

[[ritan-chuantai-hongloumeng-haishi-keyi-du-yidu-de-587167638]] presents 尤三姐 as active, clear-sighted, and difficult to fit into a passive-victim reading of [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]]. The episode says she sees through Jia-family men and has her own will, but her rejected marriage leaves her with no recoverable path, leading to suicide.

The source also uses the discussion around You Sanjie to complicate [[JiaBaoyu|贾宝玉]]. Even a relatively clean figure inside [[GrandViewGarden|大观园]] can slip into male talk when speaking from outside that protected space, which keeps [[TruthfulTemperamentInRedChamber|truthfulness]] and gendered constraint in tension rather than making Baoyu a perfect moral observer.
