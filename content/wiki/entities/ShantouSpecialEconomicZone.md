# Shantou Special Economic Zone / 汕头经济特区

Shantou Special Economic Zone is the reform-era institution at the center of [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046]]'s comparison between [[Shantou]] and [[Shenzhen]]. The episode emphasizes that Shantou was included among the first four special economic zones, but its initial area and functional design were far smaller and narrower than Shenzhen's.

The source treats the special-zone label as insufficient by itself. Shantou had an overseas Chinese base and return-capital story, but [[ChinaSpecialEconomicZoneAsymmetry]], distance from [[HongKong]], [[RegionalAdministrativeFragmentation]], and later [[ExportTaxFraudCreditCrisis]] all limited its route from port-commercial city to industrial city.
