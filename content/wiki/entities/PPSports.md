# PP体育 / PP Sports

PP Sports appears in [[e237-yangshi-he-fifa-tanpan-fenzheng-beihou-tiyu-saishi-zhuanboquan-de-boyi-yu-shengyi-c3607c08-6eb6-48bd-8250-b41b5b8272e6]] as a China sports-streaming case tied to rights-price volatility after the first copyright boom. The episode says PP Sports once bought [[PremierLeague]] rights at a very high price, while later China-market Premier League rights fell sharply and [[Migu]] acquired a newer cycle at a much lower level.

The source uses PP Sports to show that a sports-rights bubble should be judged against local monetization ability. A price can look plausible during a competitive platform race but become unsustainable if advertising, membership, sublicensing, and ecosystem conversion do not cover the rights cost.
