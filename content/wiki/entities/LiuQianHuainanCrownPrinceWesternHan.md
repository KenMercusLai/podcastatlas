## Overview

淮南太子刘迁 / Liu Qian is the son and crown prince of [[LiuAn|淮南王刘安]] whose threatened arrest precipitates a failed Huainan mobilization, a proposal to assume responsibility for his father, and [[WuBeiWesternHan|伍被]]'s late confession; the current sources then report his execution after Liu An's death.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-303-1-huainanwang-ruhe-bei-jiujiexin-bisi-de-ljtqn-nrxyf8yqx-yowsainkzmjc|Hanji 303-1]] places Liu Qian at the crisis point. After the court learns of his intent and sends officers to Huainan to arrest him, father and son reportedly plan to summon and kill the kingdom's senior officials before rising. Almost no one answers. Liu Qian then judges that the usable officials have already been removed, offers to go to court and take responsibility for Liu An, and attempts suicide but survives within the excerpt.

The adjacent sources supply disclosure and outcome rather than a fuller biography. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-304-1-gudai-ruhe-zuo-shuangmian-jiandie-lu05uaxp5b_buvl5go2bn7iy9q_x|Hanji 304-1]] says [[LeiBeiWesternHan|雷被]] had earlier reported Liu An and Liu Qian and that Liu Qian's vulnerability to arrest made Wu Bei fear losing the value of first disclosure. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-303-2-saiweng-shima-de-zuozhe-shi-zenme-si-de-lp9u6yu1la2w6sfqwqfikcvgq9pt|Hanji 303-2]] places Liu Qian and the queen in the post-confession arrest sweep and says they are killed with the alleged co-conspirators after Liu An's suicide.

## Key Characteristics

- Crown prince and son of Liu An in the Huainan royal house.
- Alleged co-subject of Lei Bei's early denunciation.
- Direct target of the court mission that forces the conspiracy from planning into crisis.
- Participant in a proposed roundup of senior kingdom officials that fails for lack of attendance.
- Heir who reportedly offers to assume responsibility for his father and attempts suicide.
- Arrested figure whose possible testimony changes Wu Bei's incentive to confess.
- Royal heir reported killed with the queen and alleged co-conspirators after Liu An's death.

## Evidence

Crisis and failed mobilization:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-303-1-huainanwang-ruhe-bei-jiujiexin-bisi-de-ljtqn-nrxyf8yqx-yowsainkzmjc|Hanji 303-1]] says the arrest mission targets Liu Qian, father and son discuss killing summoned officials, the summons fails, and Liu Qian responds with a self-sacrificial proposal and unsuccessful suicide attempt.

Denunciation and disclosure pressure:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-304-1-gudai-ruhe-zuo-shuangmian-jiandie-lu05uaxp5b_buvl5go2bn7iy9q_x|Hanji 304-1]] places Liu Qian in Lei Bei's report and says his arrest exposure prompts Wu Bei to disclose before another insider can implicate him.

Final punishment:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-303-2-saiweng-shima-de-zuozhe-shi-zenme-si-de-lp9u6yu1la2w6sfqwqfikcvgq9pt|Hanji 303-2]] places Liu Qian in the immediate post-confession sweep and reports his later execution with the queen and alleged co-conspirators.

## Qualifications

The current sources do not reconstruct Liu Qian's life, the prior conduct that prompted the arrest mission, his specific role in earlier planning, independent evidence, or exact legal disposition. Hanji 303-1's dialogue, official-attendance sequence, offer to take responsibility, and suicide attempt remain episode-attributed. The notes establish a crisis and punishment role, not an independent adjudication of every allegation.

## What Changed

- Liu Qian now has an active crisis role before arrest rather than appearing only as leverage in Wu Bei's disclosure calculus.
- His proposal to bear responsibility is preserved without treating it as proof that he alone, or not at all, caused the conspiracy.
- The failed official summons adds evidence that the Huainan royal house lacked reliable institutional support.

## Relationships

- [[LiuAn|淮南王刘安]] - father whose failed mobilization and exposure define Liu Qian's crisis role.
- [[WuBeiWesternHan|伍被]] - adviser whose confession allegedly accelerates when Liu Qian becomes vulnerable to arrest.
- [[LeiBeiWesternHan|雷被]] - insider said to have denounced father and son earlier.
- [[PlanningWithoutDecisionTimingLoss|谋而不断式时机丧失]] - failure pattern visible when arrest overtakes unresolved plans.
- [[TimingDependentDefectionLeniency|倒戈宽免的时机依赖]] - concept for the declining bargaining value of disclosure after the case becomes easier to expose.
