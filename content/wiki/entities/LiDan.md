# 李诞

李诞 is the guest in [[fanwai-14-gen-lidan-liaoliao-boke-chuangzuo-ai-yu-zhongnian-972693552]], where he discusses [[Banlatte|半拿铁]], podcasting, comedy, AI learning, and middle-age memory with [[XiaoLei|肖磊]] and [[LiuFei|刘飞]]. In the episode he approaches podcasts partly as a listener: he says long-form audio lets public figures become more complete and familiar than their variety-show, livestream, or social-media labels.

His AI comments are practical rather than purely alarmist. He describes using AI to study philosophy and then checking his understanding with experts, which makes him a source case for [[AIAsTutor]] and [[HumanJudgmentUnderAI]]. On creativity, he thinks AI comedy generation has no principled impossibility, but he keeps disclosure and the human value of flaws, digressions, and wrong turns in view.
