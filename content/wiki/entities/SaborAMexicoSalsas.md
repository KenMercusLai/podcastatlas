# Sabor a Mexico Salsas

## Overview
Sabor a Mexico Salsas is the Chicago family salsa company co-founded by [[ChristopherNavarro]] with his parents and discussed as a farmers-market-to-retail growth case in the Daymond John Advice Line episode.

## Current Profile
The company sells authentic family-recipe salsas rooted in Christopher Navarro's grandmother's taqueria in Mexico. In the episode, it has about eight salsas plus one pickled product, began officially in 2024, sells mainly through farmers markets, and expects about $340,000 in annual sales after roughly doubling the previous year's revenue.

The advice is to avoid treating large grocery retail as the immediate next milestone. [[DaymondJohn]] and [[GuyRaz]] recommend testing a small number of Chicago mom-and-pop food stores, sampling in-store, and learning whether the family story, shelf presentation, pricing, and reorder behavior transfer beyond farmers markets.

## Key Characteristics
- Family-recipe salsa brand with a Chicago market base and Mexican taqueria origin story.
- Current traction comes primarily from farmers markets, where direct customer feedback and margin are strong.
- Faces a channel-timing question: whether it has outgrown farmers markets enough to enter retail.
- Receives advice to test small local stores before regional or larger grocery accounts.
- Needs in-store sampling and customer conversation to learn how the product sells off a shelf.
