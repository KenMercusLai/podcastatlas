# Federal Bureau of Investigation

## Overview
The Federal Bureau of Investigation appears in [[tech-20260831-0831-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260831-0831-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the federal law-enforcement institution connected to [[CODIS]], the national DNA database receiving profiles from DHS detainee sampling.

## Current Profile
This page's current role is narrow: the FBI is the destination and database context for DNA profiles collected by [[USDepartmentOfHomelandSecurity|DHS]] and [[USImmigrationAndCustomsEnforcement|ICE]]. The episode treats the FBI-linked database as a policing infrastructure layer that can be searched in future criminal investigations.

## Key Characteristics
- Federal law-enforcement agency associated with CODIS in the episode.
- Recipient infrastructure for DNA profiles created from DHS detainee samples.
- Part of the policing-search pathway that makes immigration DNA collection durable beyond detention.
- Institutional actor not independently represented in the episode by a detailed defense or response.
