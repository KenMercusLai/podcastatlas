# Federal Bureau of Investigation

## Overview
The Federal Bureau of Investigation appears in the bounded sources in two distinct federal roles: operating the [[CODIS]] criminal-policing database and investigating organized interstate cargo theft.

## Current Profile
[[tech-20260831-0831-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260831-0831-mp-tech-pod-128]] presents the FBI as the database destination for DNA profiles collected from DHS detainees, making immigration sampling durable and searchable beyond detention. [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356]] presents the bureau through [[WayneJacobs]] as an investigator of cyber-enabled, interstate, and internationally directed cargo crime.

## Key Characteristics
- Federal law-enforcement agency associated with CODIS.
- Recipient infrastructure for DNA profiles produced from DHS detainee samples.
- Investigative authority for interstate cargo crime, including alcohol shipments.
- Source of an organized-syndicate account of strategic cargo theft.
- Constrained when suspected organizers operate in less cooperative foreign jurisdictions.

- Database role - [[tech-20260831-0831-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260831-0831-mp-tech-pod-128]] says DHS detainee DNA profiles enter the FBI's CODIS database for future policing searches.
- Cargo investigation - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356]] says A21 reported the interstate bourbon theft to the FBI and presents the bureau's cybercrime assessment.
- Syndicate and jurisdiction account - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356]] attributes international organization, mixed domestic participation, threats, and arrest difficulty to the FBI.

## Qualifications
The DNA source does not include a detailed FBI or DOJ defense of retention and search policy. The cargo source supplies investigative assessments without named indictments or final adjudications for the described syndicates. These roles should not be generalized into a complete institutional profile.

## What Changed
- Added the FBI's interstate cargo-theft and cybercrime role.
- Expanded the profile from database infrastructure to active supply-chain investigation.
- Preserved the distinction between established agency function and source-reported investigative assessment.
