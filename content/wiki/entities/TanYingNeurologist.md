# 谭颖 / Tan Ying

## Overview
谭颖 / Tan Ying is presented as a neurologist at [[PekingUnionMedicalCollegeHospital|北京协和医院]] and a clinical guest in a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode about [[MyastheniaGravis|重症肌无力]].

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents Tan as the principal explainer of neuromuscular-junction failure, fluctuating weakness, acute-versus-maintenance treatment horizons, pregnancy planning, long-term follow-up, and the efficacy-and-safety logic of [[MyastheniaGravisDoubleTargetManagement|“双达标”管理]]. Tan also treats communication as part of care: patients and families can preserve longitudinal evidence through diaries, videos, comparisons with personal best and worst periods, and focused questions, while clinicians retain responsibility for interpretation and treatment choice.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains myasthenia gravis through impaired neuromuscular transmission and autoimmune targeting.
- Separates rapid control during acute worsening from longer-term maintenance and de-escalation.
- Frames efficacy and safety as simultaneous rather than sequential treatment goals.
- Integrates pregnancy planning, adverse-effect management, patient constraints, and long-term follow-up.
- Encourages structured symptom evidence and family participation in clinical communication.
