# Daniel Ellsberg

## Overview

Daniel Ellsberg appears as the former defense analyst who disclosed the [[PentagonPapers]] in order to expose official deception about the Vietnam War.

## Current Profile

The episode treats Ellsberg's leak as a catalytic event in the prehistory of the [[WatergateScandal]]. Although the documents concerned decisions made before [[RichardNixon|Richard Nixon]] became president, the administration responded as if uncontrolled disclosure threatened its authority. That reaction produced the [[WhiteHousePlumbers]] and an illegal break-in at Ellsberg's psychiatrist's office in search of discrediting material.

## Key Characteristics

- Former defense analyst with access to a classified history of U.S. Vietnam decision-making.
- Disclosed the papers to demonstrate that successive administrations had misled the public.
- Became a target of White House efforts to find damaging personal information.
- Connected a national-security leak to the illegal methods later associated with Watergate.
