# Airwallex

Airwallex is the fintech company discussed in [[11-nian-110-yi-meijin-ranhou-ne-duihua-airwallex-wu-kai-ai-shidai-xiayizhan-1000-yi-lr4tvdrq25by7fugoqkqojw6vwdk]]. In the source, [[WuKai]] describes the company as a global finance and payment platform for businesses, with roots in a 2015 Melbourne cross-border payment problem and a later expansion into accounts, FX, payouts, cards, acquiring, yield, credit, APIs, and finance workflows.

The source's Airwallex story has two layers. The first is [[GlobalFinancialNetwork]]: local licenses, bank partners, clearing-network access, and APIs make money movement usable across markets. The second is [[IntelligentFinance]]: products such as [[KaiAirwallex]], [[AirwallexAgentOS]], [[T0Finance]], and [[ARID]] try to turn that transaction layer into a broader AI finance entry point.

Airwallex is also a globalization case. Wu argues that a global network matters because large payment corridors are often cross-continental, and that local general managers are needed because compliance, marketing, culture, and customer experience are local rather than purely headquarters-driven.

## Key Points
- Founded in Melbourne in 2015, according to the source's company history.
- Initially motivated by slow and expensive cross-border payments in a cafe import scenario.
- Built product, local licenses, banking partners, and clearing access before revenue became meaningful.
- Expanded from money transfer into transaction-banking-like products for global businesses.
- Positions APIs and finance-system integration as part of the customer experience, not only back-office plumbing.
- Uses AI products and acquisitions to pursue a broader intelligent-finance strategy.
