# Mitch Zuckoff

## Overview
Mitch Zuckoff is the journalist and author whose research anchors [[PlanetMoney]]'s reconstruction of [[CharlesPonzi]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Zuckoff as a documentary interpreter rather than merely a commentator. His use of Italian birth records, court files, victim records, private correspondence, and photographs supports a portrait that joins Ponzi's fraud to his migration, unstable employment, appetite for risk, persuasive ability, and documented act of physical generosity.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses multiple archival record types to reconstruct a financial criminal's life and operation.
- Distinguishes a complex personal history from an excuse for later fraud.
- Connects biography, business mechanics, investor psychology, and regulatory context.
