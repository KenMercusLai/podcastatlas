# 六和塔 / Liuhe Pagoda

## Overview
六和塔 / Liuhe Pagoda appears in the Hangzhou travel segment of [[246-hangzhou-bowuguan-yiwei-hangzhi-haojiu-mei-kan-guo-zheme-tuijian-de-zhanlan-le-8217911423-973793]].

## Current Profile
The source treats Liuhe Pagoda as both an impressive Song-era heritage site and a live example of tourism attention changing how visitors use old monuments. The hosts admire its climbability, large windows, views, brick-carving remains, and relation to the Qiantang River bridge, while also noting the safety pressure created by social-media photo behavior.

## Key Characteristics
- Presented as a Song-era pagoda with preserved brick-and-stone core and later rebuilt external wooden corridors.
- Visually reads as thirteen levels while the source says visitors can climb seven internal levels.
- Offers large-window views, including toward the Qiantang River bridge associated with [[MaoYisheng|茅以升]].
- Contains heritage details such as Song brick carvings and a Zhenwu statue.
- Exposes tension between heritage visitation and social-media photo traffic.
