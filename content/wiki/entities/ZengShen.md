# 曾参 / Zeng Shen

曾参 / Zeng Shen appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-57-gudai-fa-dushi-haiyou-zhe-zuoyong-lj6wi61osaytowgmdp8dtkrwb-si]] inside the parable [[GanMao|甘茂]] tells [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] before the [[YiyangWarringStates|宜阳]] campaign. In the story, Zeng Shen's mother initially refuses to believe accusations that her son killed someone, but repeated reports eventually panic her.

The episode uses Zeng Shen less as a biographical figure than as a trust benchmark. Gan Mao says his own virtue is not equal to Zeng Shen's and Qin Wuwang's trust is not necessarily equal to Zeng Shen's mother's trust; therefore repeated accusations by more than two or three opponents could plausibly overturn the king's confidence.
