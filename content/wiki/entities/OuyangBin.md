# 欧阳斌 / Ouyang Bin

In [[176-niuyue-yinian-yige-beiguanzhuyizhe-de-huofa-725-shalong-shilu-lte-k8lw8e1txxw3nfxvo3wal8ah]], Ouyang Bin appears as the [[NiuyueMantanlu|纽约漫谈录]] host and [[725Salon|725沙龙]] convening voice for [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]]'s New York live event. His questions move the conversation from city impressions into U.S.-Japan-China comparison, overseas Chinese opportunity, creator-audience relations, "乱纪元", asset allocation, exchange-rate risk, and learning under information flows.

欧阳斌 appears in [[149-baiwu-teji-he-liangwei-laomeitiren-mantan-boke-meijie-yiji-shengyin-shengtai-de-weilai-lihbq8vujsqxvb7h2-pz2xm-eqga]] as the convening voice for a crossover among [[QizhulouYanBinke|起朱楼宴宾客]], [[QuXianchang|去现场]], and [[725Salon|725沙龙]]. He frames the conversation from the listener and media-observer side, noting that [[HuzuoHuyou|忽左忽右]] and 起朱楼宴宾客 were among his own leading listening choices.

His role is to keep the episode broad: he asks why Chinese podcasting grew, how it differs from professional media, whether "活人感" can be produced by institutions, whether podcasting could become MCN-like, and how creators should think about exposing themselves to public scrutiny. That makes him a bridge between personal listening, cultural programming, and [[ChinesePodcastEcosystem]] analysis.
