# Ryan Abbott

## Overview
Ryan Abbott is the legal and technology expert cited by Marketplace Tech for the argument that AI can receive a payroll-tax advantage over human workers.

## Current Profile
Abbott's role in the wiki is narrow but important: he supplies the tax-neutrality frame for AI labor substitution. In the episode, [[DavidBrancaccio]] credits Abbott with explaining that a business may save on payroll taxes when software replaces a worker, because employers owe Social Security and Medicare contributions for human employees but not for AI systems.

Abbott's proposal is to remove payroll taxes from human labor so the tax system becomes neutral between work done by people and work done by machines. The episode treats this as an "edgy" idea that would require replacement revenue rather than as a complete enacted tax plan.

## Key Characteristics
- Abbott connects AI labor substitution to tax incentives rather than only wages or productivity.
- He frames payroll-tax asymmetry as a policy design problem.
- His proposed reform aims for neutrality between human and machine work.
- His contribution sits inside the broader AI public-finance debate over replacing labor-tax revenue.

## Evidence
- Tax-incentive evidence: [[tech-20260914-tech-pod-128-tech-20260914-tech-pod-128]] says Abbott helped Brancaccio understand that replacing a worker with AI can save employer-side payroll taxes.
- Payroll-tax evidence: [[tech-20260914-tech-pod-128-tech-20260914-tech-pod-128]] gives Social Security and Medicare employer contributions as the concrete mechanism.
- Reform evidence: [[tech-20260914-tech-pod-128-tech-20260914-tech-pod-128]] presents Abbott's idea of eliminating payroll taxes on humans to create tax neutrality.
