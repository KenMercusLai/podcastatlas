# 高尔基 / Gao Erji

## Overview
高尔基 / Gao Erji is the [[CaixinMedia|财新传媒]] executive president who speaks in the [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] episode [[ep278-ai-shidai-bu-juan-zhuanye-juan-shenme-renzai-zhongliu-tebie-cehua-02-lhvsbropwclouwshe-2-fczwnwzz]] on AI, education, major choice, and professional formation.

## Current Profile
In the source, Gao Erji uses a finance and media-operations background to argue that AI changes work by compressing routine execution rather than by making any single discipline useless. His profile in this wiki is therefore tied to AI-era career judgment: students should build foundations, use AI to explore interests, and become distinctive enough that tools amplify rather than replace them.

## Key Characteristics
- Gao frames AI disruption as cross-disciplinary task substitution, not a simple humanities-versus-sciences problem.
- He treats university study as a tool-box-building period where disciplinary background can later become differentiated capability.
- He emphasizes exploratory AI use by young people, including using tools to prototype ideas and test how interests might connect to future work.
- He argues that original content, upstream question-setting, and grassroots reporting can remain valuable because they create material AI has not already absorbed.
- He ends the episode with a person-centered answer: the durable competition is to become a better, more distinctive self while treating AI as a tool.
