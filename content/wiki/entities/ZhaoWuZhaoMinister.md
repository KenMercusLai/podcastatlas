# 赵午 / Zhao Wu (Zhao minister)

赵午 / Zhao Wu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-178-maodun-weihe-yao-fangguo-liubang-zhe-cai-shi-zhenxiang-2-loluh53iyrzo4ucf3ttzmn3-otkv|Hanji 178 part 2]] as one of [[ZhangAo|张敖]]'s Zhao ministers. He is grouped with [[GuanGao|贯高]] in the reaction against [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s rough treatment of Zhang Ao at [[Handan|邯郸]].

The source does not develop Zhao Wu separately from Guan Gao, so this page keeps his role narrow. He matters as part of the ministerial group that wants to avenge their king's humiliation while trying not to implicate Zhang Ao in the assassination plan.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-179-lishishang-heqin-zenme-laide-lslks-ovbomneornk4j-q6-ns0x1|Hanji 179]] keeps Zhao Wu paired with [[GuanGao|贯高]] in the failed 百人县 ambush. The plot places killers inside the lodging walls, but [[LiuBang|刘邦]] leaves before staying there. Zhao Wu remains a narrow figure, but the new source confirms that the Handan grievance becomes an attempted attack rather than only a plan.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-180-xihan-diyi-yinghan-guangao-de-gushi-1-li5u-dmsmb1lp4ny3rk02kaiyu4k|Hanji 180]] gives Zhao Wu's end only as part of the exposed-conspirator group. When Liu Bang orders the arrests, Zhao Wu and several others kill themselves, leaving Guan Gao as the surviving conspirator who can still testify for Zhang Ao.

The suffix "Zhao minister" distinguishes him from other Zhao-Wu name routes, including [[ZhaoWuhou|赵武侯]].
