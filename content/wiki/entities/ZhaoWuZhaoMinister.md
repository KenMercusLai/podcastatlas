# 赵午 / Zhao Wu (Zhao minister)

赵午 / Zhao Wu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-178-maodun-weihe-yao-fangguo-liubang-zhe-cai-shi-zhenxiang-2-loluh53iyrzo4ucf3ttzmn3-otkv|Hanji 178 part 2]] as one of [[ZhangAo|张敖]]'s Zhao ministers. He is grouped with [[GuanGao|贯高]] in the reaction against [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s rough treatment of Zhang Ao at [[Handan|邯郸]].

The source does not develop Zhao Wu separately from Guan Gao, so this page keeps his role narrow. He matters as part of the ministerial group that wants to avenge their king's humiliation while trying not to implicate Zhang Ao in the assassination plan.

The suffix "Zhao minister" distinguishes him from other Zhao-Wu name routes, including [[ZhaoWuhou|赵武侯]].
