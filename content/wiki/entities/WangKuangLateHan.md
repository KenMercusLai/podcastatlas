# 王匡 / Wang Kuang (late Han)

王匡 / Wang Kuang (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-950-budong-quanli-beihou-de-renxing-bi-bai-wuyi-lu8-u78yqvvwwarsz8dlevvzchxr|Hanji 950]] as the Henei administrator stationed with [[YuanShao|袁绍]] in [[HeneiCommandery|河内]] during the first anti-[[DongZhuo|董卓]] deployment. The source uses him mainly as part of the coalition map rather than as a developed actor.

His placement matters because Henei becomes one of the coalition's visible fronts before later sources show the alliance failing to convert distributed positions into unified action.
