# Jorge Durand

## Overview

Jorge Durand is an anthropologist presented in [[general-chapman-s-last-stand-general-chapman-s-last-stand|General Chapman’s Last Stand]] as a co-founder with [[DouglasMassey]] of the [[MexicanMigrationProject]].

## Current Profile

The episode places Durand within a decades-long effort to interview migrant families in Mexico and reconstruct individual histories of first, last, and total trips to the United States. This design made repeated departures and returns visible and supported the project’s conclusion that much twentieth-century Mexican labor movement was [[CircularMigration|circular]] rather than a one-way transition to permanent residence.

Durand is also named as a coauthor, with Massey and Karen Pren, of a 2016 counterfactual study estimating that holding enforcement resources at 1986 levels would have produced a smaller undocumented Mexican population. The estimate is central to the episode’s account of [[BorderEnforcementSettlementBackfire]] but remains source-scoped here.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses household and life-history evidence to reconstruct repeated cross-border movement.
- Co-developed a long-running Mexico–United States migration dataset.
- Contributed to the circular-migration interpretation at the center of the episode.
- Coauthored the counterfactual enforcement estimate cited by the episode.
