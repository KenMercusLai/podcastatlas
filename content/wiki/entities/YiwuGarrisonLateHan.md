## Overview

伊吾屯田 / Yiwu garrison farming is the late-Han Western Regions garrison-agriculture installation whose current profile now begins with 77 CE withdrawal and [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]] reoccupation, then later records restoration under [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] in Hanji 810. The route is deliberately disambiguated from the modern [[Yiwu]] page because this page refers to Western Regions garrison agriculture and the office of Yiwu sima.

## Current Profile

Hanji 747 supplies the earliest current layer. In 77 CE, [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Zhangdi]] withdraws the Yiwu Lu tuntian troops, and Northern Xiongnu occupies the position again. The notice makes Yiwu a compact example of [[StrategicFrontierAbandonment|strategic frontier abandonment]]: a removed garrison becomes an enemy-held route node almost immediately.

Hanji 810 supplies the later restoration layer. Shun restores Yiwu tuntian in 131 CE and establishes a Yiwu sima, following an earlier model from the Yongyuan reign. The episode frames the move as route security: garrison farming supports Han presence on the Western Regions corridor.

Yiwu's current profile therefore joins agriculture, military logistics, withdrawal risk, and frontier diplomacy. It is a fixed support node rather than a general city page, and its role contrasts with the separate Huangzhong case where moving tuntian too close to Qiang groups produces fear and alliance behavior.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Regions route-security installation rather than a modern city route.
- Tuntian site where agriculture and garrison logistics are combined.
- Withdrawal case where abandoned position is reoccupied by Northern Xiongnu.
- Restored by Shun as a deliberate revival of an earlier frontier model.
- Given a named office, Yiwu sima, to administer the restored position.
- Source-bounded place page linked to one 77 CE withdrawal notice and one 131 CE restoration notice.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 747 and Hanji 810. It does not reconstruct the full history of Yiwu, the Yongyuan precedent, the exact Yiwu Lu garrison structure, or every later Western Regions garrison. The modern Yiwu page remains separate because the route collision would otherwise mix unrelated subjects.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 747 as the earlier withdrawal and Northern Xiongnu reoccupation layer.
- Reframed the 131 CE Hanji 810 restoration as a later repair of an older route-security position.
- Preserved the late-Han Yiwu garrison farming page as a disambiguated route for tuntian, not the modern city.
