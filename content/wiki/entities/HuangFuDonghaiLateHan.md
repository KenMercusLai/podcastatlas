## Overview

黄浮 / Huang Fu is the East Sea official in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] who executes [[XuXuanLateHan|徐宣]] after the Xiapi killing case and is then punished when eunuch complaint reaches [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]].

## Current Profile

Huang Fu's current profile is built around risky enforcement. After Xu Xuan, nephew of [[XuHuangLateHan|徐璜]], kills [[LiGaoRunanLateHan|李暠]]'s daughter, Huang Fu orders Xu Xuan arrested, interrogated, executed publicly, and displayed. His subordinates warn that this is dangerous because Xu Xuan is protected by eunuch kinship.

The source's moral focus is Huang Fu's answer: he treats Xu Xuan as a state criminal and accepts the possibility that he may die for punishing him. The court result confirms the risk. Xu Huang's complaint leads Huan to punish Huang Fu, making the page a core example of enforcement rebounding under palace-protected power.

## Key Characteristics

- East Sea official who treats Xu Xuan's violence as a public crime rather than a private family dispute.
- Risk-aware enforcer who acts despite subordinate warnings about eunuch retaliation.
- Official punished after palace complaint converts enforcement into offense.
- Parallel case to Zhai Chao, another official punished in the same source field.
- Source-scoped example of local justice failing to survive captured court access.

## Qualifications

The source transcript renders this actor as "皇甫"; the page normalizes him to 黄浮 / Huang Fu because the Xu Huang, Xu Xuan, Xiapi, Li Gao, and East Sea sequence matches that identity. The page is not about the separate 皇甫 surname figures already present in the wiki.

## What Changed

- Hanji 872 creates Huang Fu's canonical page as the Donghai official who executes Xu Xuan and is punished under eunuch pressure.
