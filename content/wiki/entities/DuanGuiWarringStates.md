# 段规 / Duan Gui (Warring States)

## Overview
Duan Gui is Han Hu's steward and adviser who responds to [[ZhiYao|Zhi Yao]]'s humiliation and territorial demand with calculated concession rather than immediate resistance.

## Current Profile
Duan Gui argues that refusing Zhi Yao would trigger an isolated war that Han is poorly placed to win. Granting a large city instead will reward Zhi Yao's appetite, encourage another demand, and shift the coercive conflict toward other houses. The strategy accepts a real near-term loss in order to change the future coalition structure.

## Key Characteristics
- Distinguishes emotional refusal from survivable timing.
- Treats concession as an instrument rather than proof of submission.
- Predicts that repeated predation can align previously separate victims.
- Converts personal humiliation into a delayed house-survival strategy.

## Evidence
- Land-demand advice: the source has Duan Gui persuade Han Hu that refusal means immediate attack, while compliance induces Zhi Yao to seek the same gain elsewhere and provoke broader war. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-02-jiangyu-baizhi-bi-gufuzhi-lglk2_eu1uvegdac8sj6bfuwv1dw|Zhouji 02]]

## Qualifications
This is a single-source profile. The exact speech, city size, emotional state, and degree of deliberate control over later events remain source-scoped. The eventual anti-Zhi coalition does not make territorial sacrifice costless or make the strategy safe in general.
