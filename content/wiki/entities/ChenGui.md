# 陈珪 / Chen Gui

陈珪 / Chen Gui enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1018-ta-weihe-rang-caocao-jidan-sunce-weiju-liubei-wanxi-lqccdhrjmero-gt7vsaq2ejuhhw8|Hanji 1018]] as [[ChenDeng|陈登]]'s father and the adviser whom [[LyuBu|吕布]] blames after [[YuanShu|袁术]] attacks. The episode says Chen Gui had earlier advised against Yuan Shu's marriage alliance, making him the convenient target when Lü Bu sees Yuan Shu's seven-direction campaign forming.

Chen Gui's answer is an alliance diagnosis. He argues that [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]], [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], and Yuan Shu do not have a durable interest bond, so their joint attack can be split. The page therefore treats Chen Gui less as a biographical subject than as the voice of [[InterestStructuredAllianceFragility|利益结构式联盟脆弱性]] in this source.
