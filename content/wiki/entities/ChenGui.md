# 陈珪 / Chen Gui

陈珪 / Chen Gui enters the wiki through the Hanji 1016-1018 late-Han branch as [[ChenDeng|陈登]]'s father and political partner. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1016-sanguo-luanshi-li-chengui-jiazu-de-mingyun-jueze-ltuckdcxpyxk-xmzp8rt-2gjfzcs|Hanji 1016]] makes him the pivotal family strategist before Chen Deng's direct [[CaoCao|曹操]] channel opens: [[YuanShu|袁术]] invokes old friendship and hostage pressure through Chen Deng, but Chen Gui refuses to treat that private tie as stronger than the Han-court legitimacy field.

The same source gives Chen Gui the marriage-alliance blocking move. He persuades [[LyuBu|吕布]] that marrying into Yuan Shu's side would expose [[Xuzhou|徐州]] and attach Lü Bu to an illegitimate claimant, then pushes Lü Bu to recall his daughter and send [[HanYinLateHan|韩胤]] to Cao Cao's court. This makes Chen Gui a source case for both [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] and [[MarriageAllianceWarExposure|联姻同盟战场暴露]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1017-sanguo-yincang-yingxiong-chendeng-de-chuanqi-wangshi-lhcvtm8fobuzjfyo9uirdcgx2plq|Hanji 1017]] reads him as a deeper family-survival strategist rather than only a tactical adviser: short term, he uses Lü Bu to balance Yuan Shu's pressure on Xuzhou; middle term, the Chen family uses offices and local force to build its own capacity; long term, the family keeps a channel to Cao Cao for the post-Lü-Bu order.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1018-ta-weihe-rang-caocao-jidan-sunce-weiju-liubei-wanxi-lqccdhrjmero-gt7vsaq2ejuhhw8|Hanji 1018]] then shows Chen Gui as the adviser whom Lü Bu blames after [[YuanShu|袁术]] attacks. The episode says Chen Gui had earlier advised against Yuan Shu's marriage alliance, making him the convenient target when Lü Bu sees Yuan Shu's seven-direction campaign forming.

Chen Gui's answer is an alliance diagnosis. He argues that [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]], [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], and Yuan Shu do not have a durable interest bond, so their joint attack can be split. The page therefore treats Chen Gui less as a biographical subject than as the voice of [[InterestStructuredAllianceFragility|利益结构式联盟脆弱性]] in this source.
