# Lambda Labs

## Overview
Lambda Labs is an AI GPU cloud company used in the wiki as a smaller Nvidia-backed customer and leaseback example within the AI infrastructure financing debate.

## Current Profile
The Nvidia financing episode presents Lambda Labs as another case where Nvidia is not merely selling chips. Nvidia is described as investing in Lambda Labs and then leasing back GPU capacity, which turns the relationship into a mix of supplier, investor, customer, and ecosystem backstop. The case matters because it shows the CoreWeave pattern is not isolated: smaller AI clouds can receive capital and demand support that makes GPU capacity financeable while raising questions about independent demand and utilization.

## Key Characteristics
- AI GPU cloud provider positioned around access to Nvidia GPU capacity.
- Nvidia-backed customer whose relationship includes both investment and capacity leasing.
- Example of supplier-supported demand outside the larger CoreWeave case.
- Risk profile depends on utilization, lease pricing, GPU useful life, and customers beyond Nvidia-linked demand.

## Evidence
- Investment relationship: [[vol-273-yingweida-ze-jianji-tianxia-1010956114]] says Nvidia invested in Lambda Labs.
- Leaseback mechanism: [[vol-273-yingweida-ze-jianji-tianxia-1010956114]] says Nvidia spent about $1.5 billion leasing GPU capacity from Lambda Labs.
- Pattern extension: [[vol-273-yingweida-ze-jianji-tianxia-1010956114]] uses Lambda Labs to show Nvidia's support model extends beyond CoreWeave.
- Finance risk: [[vol-273-yingweida-ze-jianji-tianxia-1010956114]] ties this kind of support to the wider question of whether AI cloud demand is independent and durable.

## Qualifications
The page reflects one episode's description and does not independently verify Lambda Labs' financing terms. The relationship can be strategic market development without being fraudulent, but it still matters for circular-demand analysis.
