# 武平君畔 / Wu Ping Jun Pan

武平君畔 / Wu Ping Jun Pan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-3-chensheng-zhisi-weishenme-dele-minxin-hanle-renxin-lnx4zs-vsi3zeoixnqskkpexmr3|Qinji 129-3]] as the person [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] sends to monitor the Tan-area rebel force raised by [[QinJiaRebel|秦嘉]] and [[ZhuJishi|朱吉石]]. His title makes him a proxy for [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]]'s central authority rather than a fully developed independent biography in this episode.

Qin Jia refuses to accept him, tells subordinates that Wu Ping Jun Pan is only a young appointee, and then falsely claims a Chen Sheng order to kill him. The episode uses the event to show that Chen Sheng's authority has become easy to borrow rhetorically and difficult to enforce operationally.
