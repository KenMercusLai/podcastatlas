# Jamaican Women's National Team

The Jamaican Women's National Team appears in [[ep-12-insightful-conversation-with-a-football-analytics-professional]] as one of [[AnnaDSouza|Anna D'Souza]]'s current football analytics contexts. The source says Anna works with the team on scouting and insights, including metrics and KPIs aligned with a team's game model.

The team is used to explain why national-team [[DataDrivenFootballScouting]] differs from club scouting. Anna says Jamaica depends heavily on diaspora recruitment, so eligible player identification, geography, federation identity, and fit with the team's model become part of analytics work rather than background administration.

In the wiki, the team connects sports analytics to [[OpenFootballTalentMarkets]]: national-team quality can depend on whether a federation can find, evaluate, and integrate a wider player pool instead of only analyzing players already inside a local club system.
