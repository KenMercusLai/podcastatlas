# 《别认输，惠子》

《别认输，惠子》 is the autobiographical source as presented in [[88-bie-renshu-huizi-zai-ting-budao-hecai-de-shijie-li-huiquan-708510803]]. The episode says the original account is by [[KeikoBoxer|惠子]] herself and stresses its plainness: many experiences that could be dramatized into easy pathos are narrated in a flat, matter-of-fact style.

For the wiki, the book matters because its style keeps the disability and boxing story grounded. Instead of making 惠子's life a single upward curve, the account preserves ordinary-school isolation, envy of her sister's deaf-school environment, adolescent conflict, work instability, repeated boxing refusals, the first professional win, and the later work of repair.

## Key Claims
- The book's value in the episode comes from understatement rather than literary polish.
- The source supports [[CommunicationAccessAndMisrecognition]] by showing how daily hearing-world friction accumulates before it becomes visible crisis.
- It supports [[BoxingAsEmbodiedAgency]] because boxing appears as practice, pain, repetition, and permission, not only as symbolic victory.
