## Overview

Hou Ba / 侯霸 enters the wiki as the Guangwu-era administrator who links local preservation to institutional reconstruction. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-690-jiaoyou-bushen-you-duo-kepa-kankan-tianrong-de-jingli-lpbms8pebzdo2ldiwho1cba81njk|Hanji 690]], he preserves Linhuai during the Wang Mang disorder, is summoned by [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] at Shouchun, becomes Shangshu Ling, and helps the court recover usable rules by collecting scattered documents and earlier policy precedents.

## Current Profile

The source gives Hou Ba two linked functions. First, he is a local-order survival case: while the Wang Mang endgame creates wider disorder, Hou Ba is said to keep Linhuai stable. Second, he becomes a central institutional actor precisely because the new court lacks both old statutes and enough old officials.

His value is documentary and procedural, not just moral. Hou Ba knows earlier institutions, gathers dispersed records, lists useful prior policies, and submits them to Liu Xiu. The episode frames Liu Xiu's acceptance of those proposals as part of Eastern Han stabilization beyond battlefield victory.

## Key Characteristics

- Linhuai administrator who preserves a commandery through late Xin/Eastern Han transition disorder.
- Shangshu Ling appointed by Liu Xiu during the Shouchun branch.
- Institutional-memory actor in a court short of old laws and experienced officials.
- Document collector who turns scattered records into usable governing precedent.
- Evidence point for state capacity as administrative reconstruction after war.

## Qualifications

This one-source page does not establish Hou Ba's full biography or all details of the documents he recovered. The source supports an institutional-memory claim, but the exact relationship between each recovered policy and later Eastern Han law remains outside current evidence.
