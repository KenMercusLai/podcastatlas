# French Restoration / 法国王政复辟

The French Restoration appears in [[21-hong-yu-hei-ta-si-yu-zhencheng-569042001]] as the historical pressure behind [[Stendhal|Stendhal]]'s [[RedAndBlackNovel|《红与黑》]]. The source treats it as a world where the heroic upward route associated with [[NapoleonBonaparte|Napoleon]] has narrowed, while aristocratic rank, church patronage, and social performance again organize ambition.

For [[JulienSorel|Julien Sorel]], this setting makes the clerical path less a sincere vocation than one of the few visible mobility routes. That is why the source uses the black robe not only as a religious symbol but as a sign of self-control, strategic disguise, and institutional adaptation.

The page is source-scoped: the episode is not a comprehensive political history of the Restoration. Its role is to explain why class resentment, Napoleonic nostalgia, religious hypocrisy, and aristocratic gatekeeping matter so much to the novel's emotional logic.
