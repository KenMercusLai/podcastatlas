# 项伯 / Xiang Bo

项伯 / Xiang Bo enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-140-xiangyu-yu-liubang-shi-zhen-youqing-haishi-xianghu-liyong-1-lmmhoq2wo3bs6b1johpc0ixuf662|Hanji 140 part 1]] as [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s uncle and an older Xiang-family figure after [[XiangLiang|项梁]]'s death. The source says he had helped Xiang Liang in the army, held the Chu office of lingyin, and remained a trusted elder whom Xiang Yu respected.

The episode introduces Xiang Bo at the moment when Xiang Yu decides to attack [[LiuBang|刘邦]] before [[HongmenYan|鸿门宴]]. Xiang Bo is shocked by the plan, and the available transcript immediately turns toward [[ZhangLiang|张良]] notifying Liu Bang. Because the transcript segment is incomplete, this page keeps the episode's Xiang Bo role as a pre-banquet channel rather than a fully narrated intervention.

## Key Claims
- Xiang Bo's authority in this source comes from kinship, age, and prior service in the Xiang camp.
- His entry makes the Hongmen crisis depend on personal channels inside a formal military confrontation.
- The source has not yet completed the full Xiang Bo-Zhang Liang-Liu Bang sequence, so later claims about his motives should be grounded in subsequent sources before being expanded here.
