# 始祖鸟山地课堂 / Arcteryx Mountain Classroom

始祖鸟山地课堂 / Arcteryx Mountain Classroom is the outdoor training context named in [[ep250-zoujin-shanye-shunliu-ershang-xunzhao-huwai-yundong-zhong-de-xinliu-shike-lpv4egumbxcjrxk3jypehyxcqfuo]]. [[ZhangShi|张时]] and [[Amon|阿蒙]] describe learning about flow, goal setting, mountain safety, clothing layers, equipment details, and women trail runners through this classroom setting.

The page matters to the wiki because it makes outdoor participation institutional rather than purely personal. In the episode, modern beginners no longer need to learn only through dangerous trial and error; courses, mountain classrooms, climbing classes, and trail-running instruction can transmit [[OutdoorSafetyPreparation]] and make [[OutdoorFlowState]] more likely without turning it into a reckless chase.
