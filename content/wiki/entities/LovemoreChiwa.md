# Lovemore Chiwa

## Overview

Lovemore Chiwa is a Zimbabwean smallholder whose farm illustrates diversified production supported in part by tobacco contract finance.

## Current Profile

Chiwa farms roughly five to six hectares near Mvurwi, north of Harare. The source describes tobacco, maize, citrus, chickens, and tilapia on his farm, using his case to show how tobacco-company advances can supply cash and inputs that ordinary agricultural lending often does not.

## Key Characteristics

- Operates a small farm near Mvurwi.
- Combines crops, poultry, and fish rather than relying on one output alone.
- Uses the tobacco value chain's contract-finance mechanism.
- Illustrates a partial recovery pathway rather than the condition of all Zimbabwean farmers.
