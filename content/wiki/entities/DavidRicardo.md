# David Ricardo / 大卫·李嘉图

David Ricardo / 大卫·李嘉图 is one of the classical economists cited in [[162-caifu-de-benzhi-yiji-ziyou-de-zhenzheng-hanyi-chuantai-shifen-xiyin-lofyxfhiprud7zvekufexsjhtust]] to separate wealth from money or precious metal. The episode groups him with [[AdamSmith|Adam Smith]] and [[IrvingFisher|Irving Fisher]] as part of the move from monetary signs toward real goods, services, property rights, and future value.

The source uses Ricardo only as a supporting reference, so this page should keep the claim source-scoped. His role in the episode is to reinforce [[WealthAsCapabilityAndRights]], not to summarize his full economic theory.
