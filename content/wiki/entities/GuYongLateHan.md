# 谷永 / Gu Yong (late Han)

谷永 / Gu Yong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-897-zhichang-bidu-shenme-cai-jiao-lingdaoli-lvrrmksykazlsb7w8abbdmnqfzqq|Hanji 897]] as a frontier administrator who pacifies a large population through trust and grace rather than through the failed force model represented by [[MengTuoLateHan|孟陀]].

The episode says Gu Yong induces more than 100,000 frontier people to submit, after which the court grants hat-and-belt civilizing markers and establishes seven counties. The page keeps the transcript's place and group labels source-scoped, because the source appears to render some names phonetically, but the administrative mechanism is clear enough to connect to [[PoliticalSurrenderCascade|招降示范级联]].
