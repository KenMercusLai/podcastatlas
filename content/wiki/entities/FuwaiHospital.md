# 中国医学科学院阜外医院 / Fuwai Hospital

## Overview
中国医学科学院阜外医院 is the hospital affiliation given for cardiologist [[ZhouQiong|周琼]] in VOL.134 of [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] and the likely institution meant by VOL.58's statement that [[DongXintong|董心彤]] was studying cardiac anesthesia at “北京福外医院.”

## Current Profile
Within the current bounded evidence, Fuwai Hospital supplies the institutional cardiology context for a public explanation of [[HeartFailure|heart failure]]. The episode also describes the hospital as participating in continuing education, medical alliances, and quality-control work intended to support guideline-based heart-failure care beyond one large-city center.

VOL.58 adds only a source-scoped training context: Dong is said to be studying cardiac anesthesia there. Because the supplied summary spells the institution “福外医院,” this mapping to Fuwai Hospital is treated as a likely correction rather than independently verified placement history.
