# Oedipus / 俄狄浦斯

[[Oedipus|Oedipus / 俄狄浦斯]] appears in the title and psychological section of [[29-qianmian-yingxiong-edipusi-zhengzai-xiaban-ji-ditie-590112891]]. The episode uses him less as a full tragedy case than as a shorthand for how myth, dream, family desire, taboo, and psychoanalytic interpretation can enter ordinary modern experience.

In this source, Oedipus belongs to the same caution as [[SigmundFreud|Freud]] and [[MythAsPublicDream]]: mythic figures can make hidden fears and desires readable, but the reading should stay source-scoped rather than becoming a total explanation for every story or person.
