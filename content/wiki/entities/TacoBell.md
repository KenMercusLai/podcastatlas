# Taco Bell

Taco Bell is the fast-food company involved in the Chihuahua advertising dispute described by [[TomRinks]] in [[sun-bum-tom-rinks-the-secrets-of-a-master-brand-builder-2023-77ab41eb-99a8-4dd2-ae39-d066818afc21]]. The source says Tom and [[JoeShields]] worked with Taco Bell for about 18 months on a Chihuahua-based campaign before Taco Bell released its own Chihuahua ads.

In the episode's account, the later lawsuit was about implied contract, recognition, and fair treatment as much as money. Tom says the campaign also damaged [[PsychoChihuahua]] sales because consumers now wanted the Taco Bell Chihuahua instead.

[[big-lettuce-meets-small-intestine]] adds Taco Bell in a different role: a fast-food chain connected to many reported illness cases in a July 2026 [[Cyclospora]] outbreak. The [[PlanetMoney]] source says investigators traced the outbreak potentially to shredded lettuce from [[TaylorFarms]], making Taco Bell part of a [[ProduceContaminationAmplification]] case rather than an advertising-rights dispute.

[[caracas-under-pressure-democracy-in-venezuela-6a7d8bbe7d85cbdf96e00822]] adds Taco Bell to the broader U.S. [[FastFoodDemandReset]]. The source says the chain has tried to incorporate social-media menu hacks, placing it among fast-food companies using attention and customization to offset traffic pressure.
