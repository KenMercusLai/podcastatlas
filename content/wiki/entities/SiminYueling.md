## Overview

《四民月令》 / *Simin Yueling* is an Eastern Han seasonal text that enters the wiki through two [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episodes: one on New Year food and ritual, and one on historical pepper-poisoning claims.

## Current Profile

The New Year episode cites the text for pepper-cypress wine, using pepper fragrance and cypress longevity or protective meaning to place drink, plant symbolism, health hopes, and seasonal ritual together. Hanji 331-4 attributes to a note on the text a different pepper claim: pepper is pungent, very heating, and toxic, with closed fruit described as especially dangerous.

Together the episodes show one ingredient moving across culinary, ritual, medicinal, and hazardous registers. They do not establish the exact transmitted wording, commentary layer, botanical species, or historical practice, so the text is an evidence anchor to verify rather than proof that everyday pepper use was lethal.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han seasonal text used in the wiki as a record of calendrical and household practice.
- Cited for pepper-cypress wine as an early New Year custom.
- Connects fragrant plants, longevity wishes, protective belief, and material festival practice.
- Cited in a separate episode for a note describing pepper, especially closed fruit, as toxic.
- Requires edition- and commentary-sensitive reading before its wording is used as botanical or toxicological proof.

## Qualifications

Both inputs are short podcast summaries rather than textual studies. They do not identify the edition, commentator, manuscript history, plant species, or relationship between the base text and later annotation. The pepper-cypress custom and toxicity wording should remain source-scoped until checked against the transmitted text and specialist scholarship.
