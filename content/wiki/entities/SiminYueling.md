# 《四民月令》 / Simin Yueling

《四民月令》 / Simin Yueling enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-150-plus-zaiye-bujian-2022-qing-duo-guanzhao-2023-fjhgtqac9r-1rpjh6oubmspqywue|Hanji 150 PLUS New Year special]] as the Eastern Han text the episode cites for pepper-cypress wine. The source uses it to show that the drink was not only a later literary image but had an early seasonal-custom record.

In the episode's explanation, "椒" is pepper and "柏" is cypress leaf. Their fragrance, longevity, and protection meanings make the drink part of [[YuanDanFestivalLayering|元旦节日层累]] and [[FestivalFoodMaterialHistory]], where plants, health hopes, and ritual action sit together.
