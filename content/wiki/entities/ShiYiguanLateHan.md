# 师宜官 / Shi Yiguan

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-911-gudai-gongwuyuan-ye-neng-bao-fenpei-ls3pxu9xlpuulsdailx8tcoylv9e|Hanji 911]] introduces 师宜官 / Shi Yiguan as one of the best-known figures associated with [[HongduMenxue|鸿都门学]]. The episode uses his restaurant-wall calligraphy anecdote to show that the Hongdu channel, despite士大夫 contempt, could attract people with visible artistic skill.

His role in the wiki is therefore not only biographical. Together with [[LiangHuLateHan|梁鹄]], Shi Yiguan complicates a simple anti-Hongdu reading: the appointment channel may damage public selection, but the artistic field it promotes is not automatically talentless.
