## Overview

Wang Lin / 王林 is the Xin crown-prince figure in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-658-erzi-queshi-buyinggai-wangmang-nusha-taizi-ln4tu6ly8eie6jspvrurzn2rjjp|Hanji 658]], where his fear after involvement with [[YuanBiXinPalaceAttendant|元璧]] escalates into a failed plot against [[WangMang|王莽]]. His death turns palace scandal into succession collapse because Wang Mang's public sons are all gone by the end of the episode.

## Current Profile

The source presents Wang Lin as trapped between crown-prince status and household terror. He had been allowed to live near [[EmpressXiaomuXin|王皇后]] to care for her after Wang Mang had already killed other sons. After Wang Lin's connection with Yuan Bi and his fear that Wang Mang would discover it, he and Yuan Bi plot to kill Wang Mang.

Wang Lin's wife [[LiuYingWangLinWife|刘英]] adds an omen layer by predicting a white-clothed mourning scene. Wang Lin reads this as support for his plan, but after Wang Mang sees Wang Lin's fearful letter to his mother and Yuan Bi confesses under interrogation, Wang Lin is denied the funeral, ordered to drink poison, and dies by suicide in another way.

## Key Characteristics

- Xin crown-prince figure whose position does not protect him from Wang Mang's suspicion.
- Son shaped by the precedent of earlier brother deaths and by fear of repeating them.
- Palace-scandal actor connected to Yuan Bi and Liu Ying's omen reading.
- Failed patricide conspirator whose exposure ends in coerced suicide.
- Succession-collapse trigger because his death leaves Wang Mang without public sons.

- Plot and confession: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-658-erzi-queshi-buyinggai-wangmang-nusha-taizi-ln4tu6ly8eie6jspvrurzn2rjjp|Hanji 658]] says Yuan Bi confesses the murder plot after arrest and torture.
- Death and succession effect: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-658-erzi-queshi-buyinggai-wangmang-nusha-taizi-ln4tu6ly8eie6jspvrurzn2rjjp|Hanji 658]] says Wang Mang gives Wang Lin poison, Wang Lin kills himself by another method, and Wang Mang's public sons are gone after Wang An also dies.
