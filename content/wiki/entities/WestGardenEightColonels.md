# 西园八校尉 / West Garden Eight Colonels

西园八校尉 / West Garden Eight Colonels enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-941-shenchu-guanchang-ta-jing-xiang-ganfan-huangdi-liu-fqiz9pi7u6cltrn-cjmjz-r|Hanji 941]] as the 188 CE capital military restructuring under [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]. The episode treats the institution as a major disturbance in [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] military command because it places armed authority in a new palace-centered arrangement.

The source's key appointments are [[JianShuoLateHan|蹇硕]] as shangjun xiaowei, [[YuanShao|袁绍]] as zhongjun xiaowei, and [[CaoCao|曹操]] as dianjun xiaowei. Bao Hong, Zhao Rong, Feng Fang, and others are also named, but the episode's durable wiki contribution is the command structure: a trusted eunuch receives the top military channel, while future warlord actors gain early official military footholds.

This backfills the setup for [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-942-yue-youxiu-de-ren-huode-yue-lei-heli-ma-lkwlrcgnt-kxxc2qqz6z6wkg7e5z|Hanji 942]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-943-quanshi-kaoyan-renxing-beihou-cangzhe-juda-xiongxian-lg7ia5xok-swdmhml35yv9qqkvt|Hanji 943]]. Jian Shuo's later ability to move threats and attempt succession violence is easier to understand after the source shows him as a palace-trusted military office-holder.
