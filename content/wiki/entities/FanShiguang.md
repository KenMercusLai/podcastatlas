# 范世广 / Fan Shiguang

## Overview
范世广 / Fan Shiguang is the documentary director interviewed by [[BuHeShiYi|不合时宜]] about [[FrontWave2Documentary|《前浪2》]] and the representation of aging in China.

## Current Profile
The source presents Fan as a Shanghai Broadcasting Television documentary director associated with 《前浪2》 and earlier documentary work including 《人间世》. In the interview, he explains why the team filmed aging in Shanghai, why many shot storylines were abandoned, and why the series tries to keep older adults specific rather than turning them into types. His strongest profile in the wiki is methodological: documentary work should be restrained, fact-centered, and willing to leave a case unused when the team cannot responsibly know the person's inner life.

## Key Characteristics
- Documentary director using aging stories to examine care, family change, intimacy, freedom, sacrifice, and institutional friction.
- Emphasizes [[DocumentaryEthicalRestraint]] over opinion-first narration or easy judgment.
- Treats Shanghai as a concrete aging sample while arguing that many old-age themes generalize beyond one city.
- Reflects personally on guilt toward his own parents after filming elder-life stories.
- Rejects generalized "elderly" stereotypes and argues for seeing each older person as specific.
