# 笮融 / Ze Rong

笮融 / Ze Rong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-995-ta-zuishang-cibei-weihe-duoci-cansha-enren-llicukoezccd5iycin4uq7qun51k|Hanji 995]] as the former subordinate whom [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] appoints to supervise grain transport across [[GuanglingCommandery|广陵]], [[Xiapi|下邳]], and Pengcheng. Instead of preserving that logistics assignment, Ze Rong diverts grain to build Buddhist temples, orders sutra recitation, offers tax exemptions to Buddhist households, and stages large bathing-Buddha feasts for [[ShakyamuniBuddha|释迦牟尼佛]]'s birthday.

The episode does not treat this as evidence against Buddhist belief. It reads Ze Rong as a violent actor using religious infrastructure as a power base: after [[CaoCao|曹操]] defeats Tao Qian, Ze Rong flees with more than ten thousand followers, is received by [[ZhaoYuLateHan|赵昱]] in Guangling, murders him, plunders the commandery, then repeats the pattern against [[XueLiLateHan|薛礼]] and later [[ZhuHaoLateHan|朱皓]] in [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]].

Ze Rong is therefore the episode's concentrated case for [[InstrumentalizedReligiousPower|工具化宗教权力]] and [[HospitalityPredation|接纳者掠杀]]. The host stresses that ordinary bad actors still calculate costs and reciprocity, while Ze Rong behaves as a no-bottom-line actor whose moral language does not constrain violence.
