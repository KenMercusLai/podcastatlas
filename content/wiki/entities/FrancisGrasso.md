## Overview

Francis Grasso was a New York DJ presented as a foundational [[Disco]] figure through obscure-record selection, beat mixing, and slip cueing at the Sanctuary.

## Current Profile

In [[439-disco-sex-and-race-in-seventies-america-glt8206137792|the source]], Grasso works in the Sanctuary, a club in a disused Hell's Kitchen church with a largely gay clientele. He selects Motown, psychedelic rock, percussion-heavy, and unfamiliar records for room effect. Beat mixing and slip cueing reduce silence and sustain an immersive set.

## Key Characteristics

- DJed at the Sanctuary in Hell's Kitchen.
- Chose records for dance-floor effect rather than chart ranking.
- Used beat mixing and slip cueing for continuity.
- Helped make the DJ the organizer of the night's musical arc.
