# 垣雍 / Yuanyong

垣雍 / Yuanyong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-1-lq4wvkd8qx3q7zji6fnnkxi3qcl1]] as the [[HanState|韩国]] land ceded to [[QinState|秦国]] when Qin stops its post-[[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] offensive. The episode pairs Han's cession of Yuanyong with [[ZhaoState|赵国]]'s cession of six cities.

The place matters as a settlement marker. [[SuDai|苏代]]'s persuasion of [[FanJu|范雎]] does not produce mercy without price; it redirects Qin from a potentially high-risk destruction of Zhao into immediate territorial gains and campaign rest.
