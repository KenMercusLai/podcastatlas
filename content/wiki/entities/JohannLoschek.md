# Johann Loschek

## Overview

Johann Loschek is [[CrownPrinceRudolf]]'s valet in the Mayerling episode and one of the people who discovers the locked-room crisis.

## Current Profile

Loschek matters as the household-service witness who turns a private bedroom into a court emergency. Rudolf asks him to return for breakfast; when no answer comes, Loschek and others break the locked door and find [[MaryVetsera]] and Rudolf dead.

## Key Characteristics

- Loschek appears as Rudolf's close service attendant at Mayerling.
- His breakfast call creates the immediate discovery timeline.
- Breaking the locked door converts private death into reported fact.
- Rudolf leaves him a letter, keeping him inside both service and aftermath evidence.

## Evidence

- Discovery sequence: [[690-a-murderous-affair-death-at-mayerling-part-2-glt7203783068]] says Rudolf wakes Loschek early, asks to be called for breakfast, and later receives no answer.
- Locked-door entry: [[690-a-murderous-affair-death-at-mayerling-part-2-glt7203783068]] says Loschek and others break open the locked bedroom door and find the bodies.
- Farewell evidence: [[690-a-murderous-affair-death-at-mayerling-part-2-glt7203783068]] lists Loschek among the recipients of Rudolf's letters.
