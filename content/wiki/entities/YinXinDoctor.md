# 银欣 / Yin Xin

## Overview
银欣 is the imaging-side doctor in the VOL.218 [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode on missed diagnosis, report limits, and clinical information on imaging requests.

## Current Profile
The source presents 银欣 as the radiology and imaging voice who explains why tests cannot replace clinical questioning. Patients who bypass history and physical examination by directly seeking imaging or lab tests can miss the clinical problem that the test is supposed to answer.

Her key contribution is the request-quality point: imaging departments read better when the ordering clinician writes the symptoms and suspected issue clearly. A generic lumbar MRI, for example, may not cover the exact region needed for a transverse-process fracture unless the clinical question directs attention there.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains imaging as an answer to a clinical question rather than a standalone diagnostic shortcut.
- Warns against bypassing history-taking and examination by going directly to tests.
- Emphasizes clear symptoms and suspected risks on imaging orders.
- Keeps negative or incomplete imaging bounded by coverage, modality, and clinical context.
