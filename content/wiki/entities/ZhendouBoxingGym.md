# 真斗拳馆

真斗拳馆 is the boxing gym in [[88-bie-renshu-huizi-zai-ting-budao-hecai-de-shijie-li-huiquan-708510803]] that accepts [[KeikoBoxer|惠子]] after other routes have treated deafness as disqualifying. The episode stresses the symbolic force of the gym leader's blindness: he does not frame disability as automatic exclusion from boxing instruction.

The gym is not presented as a soft exception to sport. It becomes important because permission is joined to serious training, medical examination, professional testing, and a willingness to advocate with boxing authorities. That makes the gym a concrete case for [[DisabilityAccessInCombatSports]] rather than a sentimental refuge.
