## Overview

郎顗 / Lang Yi enters the wiki through Hanji 813 as a Shun-era remonstrant and recommender. The source transcript writes 郎毅, but the drought-remonstrance and recommendation context supports normalizing him to 郎顗 while preserving the spelling caution.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-813-gudai-de-gaoguan-jing-ye-hui-bei-ouda-ln-dw3xohnmpavkpu-bdvasvietv|Hanji 813]] places Lang Yi in 133 CE, when drought and unseasonable west winds let him speak about political failure through a Heaven-and-human-affairs frame. He argues that sincere self-correction matters more than repeated sacrificial performance, and he recommends [[HuangQiongLateHan|黄琼]] and [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] as people [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] should bring into use.

Lang Yi is not presented as a durable office-seeker in this source. Shun appoints him langzhong after receiving the memorial, but Lang Yi feigns illness and does not take up the post. His wiki significance is therefore concentrated in two roles: opening the Shun-era disaster-remonstrance sequence and creating the recommendation path through which Li Gu and Huang Qiong enter the 133 CE court-repair field.

## Key Characteristics

- Disaster-remonstrance speaker who translates drought and wind anomalies into political criticism.
- Talent recommender who helps bring Huang Qiong and Li Gu into Shun's attention.
- Critic of empty ritual performance when it is not joined to real self-correction.
- Reluctant office-holder who avoids the langzhong post after being appointed.
- Source-bounded figure whose current wiki identity depends on Hanji 813.
