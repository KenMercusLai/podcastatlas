# Chris Comparato

Chris Comparato is the executive [[Toast]] brought in as CEO in early 2015 in [[toast-aman-narang-how-a-long-wait-for-the-dinner-check-launched-a-2-billion-business-6eca81bc-b0e1-47e2-a87d-f3b243e76a97]]. [[AmanNarang]] says the company had grown to a few hundred customers but was struggling with returns, outages, downtime, and support quality.

The source makes Comparato a [[StageAppropriateHiring]] case. Toast had customer demand, but its restaurant customers needed reliability and support systems that founder-led hustle could not keep absorbing. Comparato's arrival marks the shift from proving a wedge toward building the operating discipline needed for [[MissionCriticalRestaurantSoftware]].
