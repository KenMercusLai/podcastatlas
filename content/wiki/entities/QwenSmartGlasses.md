# 千问眼镜 / Qwen Smart Glasses

## Overview
千问眼镜 / Qwen Smart Glasses are Alibaba wearable devices intended to place the [[Qwen]] assistant in first-person visual, voice, shopping, payment, and health contexts.

## Current Profile
The earlier source uses the glasses as an example of location-plus-image context improving place recognition within the broader [[AIGlassesProductFit]] debate. The newer roundup reports two new models priced at RMB 2,999 and RMB 3,499, with shopping and payment integration and a Pro version adding eye interaction and health monitoring.

Together the sources move the profile from contextual recognition toward a broader assistant and commerce surface. That breadth does not settle product fit: weight, battery, prescription support, privacy, accidental interaction, payment confirmation, and real-world reliability remain decisive.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines first-person image and contextual information for recognition tasks.
- Connects the Qwen assistant to a body-proximate hardware surface.
- Extends reported capability into shopping and payment.
- Uses eye interaction and health monitoring as Pro-model differentiators.
- Remains subject to smart-glasses comfort, privacy, trust, and utility constraints.
