# Brandon Young

## Overview

Brandon Young was a 17-year-old whom [[JamesGundlach]] sheltered while Brandon was trying to leave gang life. His 1994 shooting death became the personal dedication hidden in a footnote to “Damned Upon Arrival.”

## Current Profile

The episode remembers Brandon as intelligent, protective, eager to learn, and capable of building a different life. He passed the GED examination, read regularly, expanded his vocabulary, and was being encouraged toward college while living with other vulnerable teenagers at the Gundlachs' Macon County cabin.

On August 23, 1994, Brandon reportedly directed three other teenagers upstairs and under a bed when he heard a disturbance, then was shot through a window. The episode says an ambulance took more than an hour to travel six miles and that he bled to death; his case embodies the claim that emergency response can determine whether an assault becomes a homicide.

## Key Characteristics

- Was trying to leave gang life and build an educational path.
- Is remembered by Gundlach as intelligent, likable, and committed to reading and vocabulary growth.
- Protected more vulnerable young people in Gundlach's household.
- Reportedly moved three teenagers out of danger during the attack that killed him.
- Became the human case through which a statistical paper's medical-survival argument was dedicated and remembered.

### Protection of others

- [[guns-part-5-the-footnote]] recounts Gundlach's view that Brandon defended disadvantaged young people and says his actions during the attack saved three lives.

### Death and dedication

- [[guns-part-5-the-footnote]] reports the shooting, delayed ambulance arrival, death from blood loss, and Gundlach's use of the paper's footnote as a dedication.

## Qualifications

The source is a structured podcast summary rather than a transcript, police file, medical record, dispatch log, or independent biography. Brandon's motives, gang history, academic prospects, actions during the attack, ambulance timeline, cause of death, and the driver's statement remain bounded to Gundlach's recollection and the episode. The episode corrects the death date to August 23, 1994.
