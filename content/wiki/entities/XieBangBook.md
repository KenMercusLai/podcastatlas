# 《鞋邦：中国运动品牌四十年》

## Overview
《鞋邦：中国运动品牌四十年》 is [[HuangZiyiManufacturingReporter|黄子毅 / Huang Ziyi]]'s book discussed in [[500-jinjiang-chendaizhen-wangshi-huangziyi-tan-gaikai-jincheng-zhong-de-zhongguo-yundong-pinpai-lswtplovdbo4pl52yoxu6rgzyal2]]. The episode uses it to reconstruct [[Jinjiang]], [[ChendaiTown|陈埭镇]], and Chinese sportswear brands across reform-era manufacturing, branding, retail, and portfolio strategy.

## Current Profile
The book is represented in the wiki as a business-history source object, not as a product-review guide. Its value is to place [[AntaSports|Anta]], [[LiNing|Li-Ning]], [[Xtep]], [[361Degrees]], [[Nike]], and [[Adidas]] inside regional industrial soil and global sportswear competition.

## Key Characteristics
- Centers Chinese sportswear history on Jinjiang and Chendai's industrial formation.
- Connects overseas Chinese resources, clan society, informal finance, and local policy tolerance to manufacturing startup conditions.
- Treats Nike and Li-Ning as learning coordinates for process capability and domestic-brand imagination.
- Compares Anta and Li-Ning without reducing their divergence to family business versus professional managers.
- Follows Anta into DTC reform, Fila operation, Amer Sports acquisition, and niche sports segmentation.

## Qualifications
The episode says Huang did not interview Nike, Adidas, Anta, Li-Ning, or their founders directly, relying instead on existing materials and early manager interviews. The book's full argument should not be inferred solely from this episode summary.
