# Tether

Tether appears in [[155-ruhe-lijie-huangjin-de-shishiji-bodong-lp3lcda5zskiv-dcezcugf2q93vi]] as a crypto-sector actor used to explain why gold demand may no longer be separate from crypto-market capital. The source says Tether held about 140 tonnes of gold, and [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] uses that example to argue that some crypto wealth may have rotated toward gold during a period when crypto assets themselves were performing poorly.

The page keeps that claim source-scoped. Its wiki role is not a full Tether profile; it is a bridge between [[DigitalGold]], [[BitcoinSafeHavenBehavior]], crypto liquidity, and [[GoldMonetaryAnchor|gold's monetary-anchor]] narrative.
