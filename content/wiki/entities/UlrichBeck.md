# Ulrich Beck / 乌尔里希·贝克

## Overview
Ulrich Beck / 乌尔里希·贝克 appears in [[episode-227-ershiyishiji-qingchun-wuyu-8211230332-278487]] as the sociologist behind [[RiskSocietyBook]], which Ren Ning uses to connect structural risk with personal responsibility.

## Current Profile
The source uses Beck to explain why modern insecurity is often experienced as an individual burden even when its causes are systemic. In the episode, unemployment, unaffordable housing, career choice, and family planning become examples of risks that social institutions distribute but individuals are expected to absorb and justify.

## Key Characteristics
- Supplies the risk-society framework for the episode.
- Helps shift the youth-conservatism claim from temperament to social structure.
- Connects institutional risk distribution with individual blame and planning pressure.
- Supports the episode's account of caution as adaptation to structural insecurity.

## Evidence
- Risk-society role: [[episode-227-ershiyishiji-qingchun-wuyu-8211230332-278487]] cites Beck's [[RiskSocietyBook]] to describe modern society as increasingly organized around distributing bads rather than only goods.
- Individualization: [[episode-227-ershiyishiji-qingchun-wuyu-8211230332-278487]] uses Beck to argue that systemic problems are translated into personal failure or poor planning.
- Youth caution: [[episode-227-ershiyishiji-qingchun-wuyu-8211230332-278487]] places youth risk aversion inside this individualized-risk setting.
