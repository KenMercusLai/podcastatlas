# 李杰 / Li Jie (Respiratory Physician)

## Overview
李杰 / Li Jie is the respiratory physician in [[vol-155-meiye-zhixi-51miao-yisheng-jiangshu-nianqing-nvhuanzhe-de-chongsheng-gushi-luct7ka8jrmtlsmbwlk9mwow3sxv]], a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode about obstructive sleep apnea, polysomnography, treatment selection, and home positive-airway-pressure use.

## Current Profile
The source presents Li as a clinical explainer who turns snoring from a folk sign of deep sleep into a possible airway-obstruction signal. She describes upper-airway anatomy, nocturnal and daytime symptoms, risk groups, diagnostic monitoring, complications, and treatment options while repeatedly directing severity and treatment choice back to clinical assessment.

Her practical contribution is device literacy. She distinguishes home noninvasive positive-airway-pressure support from invasive ICU ventilation, then explains single- versus bilevel devices, pressure titration, masks, humidification, leak compensation, ramp settings, noise, portability, data review, and after-sales support as parts of adherence rather than product decoration.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains obstructive sleep apnea through upper-airway narrowing, apnea or hypopnea, and intermittent hypoxia.
- Uses symptom patterns and polysomnography to separate clinical evaluation from informal listening or consumer screening alone.
- Presents lifestyle change, positive airway pressure, oral appliances, and surgery as indication-dependent options.
- Treats mask fit, pressure, comfort, data, and follow-up as determinants of long-term device use.
- Uses patient cases to illustrate possible benefit while leaving diagnosis and outcomes individualized.
