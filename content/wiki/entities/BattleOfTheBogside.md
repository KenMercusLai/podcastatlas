# Battle of the Bogside

The Battle of the Bogside appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] as the August 1969 street battle in [[Derry]] that helped make British intervention unavoidable. The episode says Catholics in Derry had prepared barricades and petrol bombs through the [[DerryCitizensDefenceAssociation]], and that clashes around the Apprentice Boys parade escalated into a battle zone in the [[Bogside]].

The source presents the battle as an interpretive split: Catholics saw the [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]] as hostile and the [[BritishArmy]] as a needed buffer, while many Protestants saw barricades, petrol bombs, and [[JackLynch]]'s comments as signs of insurrection or Irish-state pressure.
