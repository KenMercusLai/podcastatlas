# Qian Cheng / 钱程 (Ecovacs)

## Overview
Qian Cheng / 钱程 is a second-generation executive and CEO of [[EcovacsRobotics|Ecovacs]], associated with the company's overseas expansion, multi-category household-robot strategy, and "80% technology reuse, 20% scenario migration" product model.

## Current Profile
The bounded sources show Qian Cheng learning through increasingly broad operating roles rather than following a fixed succession plan. After early e-commerce work, he took responsibility for overseas markets and describes Germany as his first clear operating success. His later CEO retrospective is more self-critical: a leader can identify plausible directions yet fail to transmit them through an organization shaped around the founder's relationships, strengths, and decision style.

His current profile joins ambition to constraint. He continues to support internationalization, premiumization, multiple product categories, and a long-run household “butler robot,” but treats pace and sequencing as questions of [[StrategicAmbitionOrganizationalCapacityFit|organizational carrying capacity]]. At the product level, his 80/20 model explains how a reusable robotics core still needs scenario-specific engineering and local market knowledge.

## Key Characteristics
- Built early legitimacy through e-commerce and overseas operating assignments rather than title inheritance alone.
- Distinguishes strategic direction from the organizational capacity required to execute it.
- Treats founder-era routines as mixtures of constraints and functioning strengths that cannot be removed casually.
- Articulates reusable technology and scenario-specific adaptation as complementary approaches.
- Recognizes local teams, channels, and customer behavior as necessary inputs to overseas product strategy.
- Supports long-horizon embodied-AI investment while rejecting demonstrations or market heat as proof of near-term household value.

## Qualifications
One source is a sponsored short episode and the other is a long first-person executive interview; neither independently audits Qian Cheng's biography, performance, market-share figures, overseas-income expectations, strategic diagnosis, or robot timelines. His titles, results, ratios, and forecasts therefore remain source-scoped.

## What Changed
- Expanded the profile from product strategy into operating apprenticeship, succession, and CEO self-critique.
- Added the distinction between strategic direction and organizational carrying capacity.
- Added his deliberately staged view of embodied AI and general household robots.
