# 孙河 / Sun He (late Han)

## Overview
孙河 / Sun He is a Sun-family follower whose wiki role now spans early [[SunCe|孙策]] recruitment, the later [[DanyangCoupLateHan|丹阳郡变]], and the posthumous troop-inheritance branch through his son [[SunShaoLateHan|孙韶]]. Hanji 983 uses him as part of the early Danyang recruitment network, Hanji 1067 presents him as the Sun-side investigator killed after [[SunYiLateHan|孙翊]]'s assassination, and Hanji 1068 shows his remaining troops becoming Sun Shao's first command base.

## Current Profile
Hanji 983 introduces Sun He as one of the companions Sun Ce brings toward [[QuALateHan|曲阿]] after [[YuanShu|袁术]] sends Sun Ce to recruit through [[WuJingLateHan|吴景]] and [[SunBenLateHan|孙贲]]. In that source, Sun He's main function is network evidence: Sun Ce's early capacity depends on kinship, followers, and [[DanyangCommandery|丹阳郡]] access rather than personal charisma alone.

Hanji 1067 adds a later and more dangerous role. After Sun Yi is killed, Sun He comes to investigate, but [[GuiLanLateHan|妫览]] and [[DaiYuanLateHan|戴员]] also kill him. The episode uses his death to show that the conspirators are not merely hiding an isolated murder; they are willing to attack Sun-family follow-up authority.

Hanji 1068 adds the succession-of-command aftermath. Sun He's seventeen-year-old son Sun Shao gathers the remaining troops, repairs and guards [[JingCountyLateHan|泾县]], passes Sun Quan's readiness test, and receives formal command over the force Sun He left behind.

## Key Characteristics
- Belongs to Sun Ce's early follower and kinship-adjacent recruitment network.
- Appears in Danyang-linked mobilization before Sun Ce's larger Jiangdong consolidation.
- Functions as an investigator after Sun Yi's murder in Hanji 1067.
- Becomes a second Sun-side victim, escalating the Danyang conspiracy.
- Leaves a troop group that Sun Shao can reorganize into a recognized local command.

## Qualifications
The current page remains narrow. Hanji 983 does not develop Sun He's independent action, Hanji 1067's transcript context may compress exact office and location details, and Hanji 1068 develops Sun He mostly through Sun Shao's inheritance of his remaining troops. The profile is therefore limited to early Sun Ce accompaniment, later investigative death, and posthumous command continuity.
