# 孙河 / Sun He (late Han)

孙河 / Sun He (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] as one of the early companions [[SunCe|孙策]] brings back toward [[QuALateHan|曲阿]] after [[YuanShu|袁术]] sends him to recruit through [[WuJingLateHan|吴景]] and [[SunBenLateHan|孙贲]].
