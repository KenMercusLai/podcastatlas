# Vishal (Data Science With Sam)

Vishal is the [[DataScienceWithSam]] guest in [[ep-16-data-decoded-navigating-the-ai-revolution]]. The source identifies him by first name only and describes him as an analytics and AI entrepreneurship practitioner with more than 20 years of experience across data science, analytics, machine learning, BI, SaaS, healthcare, IT, sales, marketing, product, and finance contexts.

His central contribution is a business-facing view of AI analytics. He argues that GPT-like tools and [[NaturalLanguageAnalytics]] can make data work faster and more accessible, but only when companies begin from a real business problem, prepare data through [[AIDataReadiness]], and keep human judgment responsible for strategy, interpretation, privacy, and bias control.

Vishal's most concrete example is [[CustomerChurnPrediction]] for a B2B SaaS company. The source says his team used prior-customer behavior, logistic regression, [[PredictiveModelValidation]], and [[ExplainableAIBusinessDecisions|explainable AI]] inside [[Salesforce]] so sales, marketing, and customer-success teams could see churn likelihood and reasons before contacting customers.
