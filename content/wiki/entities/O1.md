# O1

O1 is the [[OpenAI]] reasoning-model release used in [[zhengliu-fengbao-yichang-wuren-gongkai-tanlun-de-jishu-jingsai-1-179-1]] as a turning point for the [[ModelDistillation]] debate. The source says O1's September 2024 release made large-scale post-training and test-time reasoning more visible, especially the idea that more inference compute and longer reasoning traces could improve results.

For the wiki, O1 matters less as a standalone product page than as a distillation catalyst. The episode argues that richer reasoning behavior creates more valuable teacher data for capability-seeking distillation, while also increasing the sensitivity of closed-model user agreements, [[AIModelDistillationGovernance]], and [[ModelDistillationEvidence]].
