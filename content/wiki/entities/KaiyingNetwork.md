# Kaiying Network / 凯英网络

Kaiying Network / 凯英网络 appears in [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]] through the later mobile and licensed-product phase of [[LegendOfMir|《传奇》]]. The episode says Kaiying signed with [[WeMade]] in 2016 to develop an authorized Legend mobile game, prompting Shanda-side objections and later litigation.

The 2026 payment to WeMade in the episode's account makes Kaiying a late-stage case for [[OnlineGameIPRightsDisputes]]. It shows how [[GameIPLongTail]] can make an early-2000s MMORPG commercially relevant long after the original operator's corporate structure has changed.
