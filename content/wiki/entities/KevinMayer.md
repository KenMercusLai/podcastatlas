# Kevin Mayer / 凯文·梅耶尔

## Overview
Kevin Mayer is the former Disney executive whom the source identifies as [[TikTok]]'s global CEO during its 2020 U.S. political crisis.

## Current Profile
[[ep137-cong-tiktok-shijian-liaoliao-qiye-chuhai-gongguan-you-duo-nan-wkg5jv9ddtotvckwbgkes6bhu2q322]] treats Mayer's appointment as part of [[ByteDance]]'s effort to present TikTok as a globally managed company with U.S.-legible leadership. His departure after only a few months is interpreted as a negative confidence signal and evidence that executive localization had little power over a conflict driven by governments, export rules, ownership pressure, and national-security claims.

The episode does not establish Mayer's private reasons, the internal decision process, or whether the departure was primarily personal, negotiated, or forced by events. His significance in this wiki is therefore narrower than a full career biography: he is a case showing both the value and the limit of executive localization during a geopolitical crisis.

## Key Characteristics
- Former Disney executive recruited to lead TikTok globally in 2020.
- U.S.-legible leadership figure in TikTok's global-company positioning.
- Short-tenure executive whose departure became a confidence signal during the crisis.
- Example of localized leadership operating under constraints set above the company level.
