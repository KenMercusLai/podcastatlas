# 其至鞬 / Qizhijian (Xianbei)

## Overview

Qizhijian is the [[Xianbei|Xianbei]] leader in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-815-kebei-gudai-zhongchen-de-jiafeng-zhishang-llkhbxo-63pjw-gk9pihnbfr94v|Hanji 815]] whose death is associated with a major reduction in raids into Han interior territory.

## Current Profile

The source presents Qizhijian through a frontier outcome, not a biography. Xianbei forces raid Ma Cheng again, and the Daijun taishou leads commandery troops but cannot defeat them. After Qizhijian dies, raids into the interior become much rarer.

This makes Qizhijian a leadership-continuity marker: the Han military response in the notice is ineffective, while the enemy leader's death changes raid frequency.

## Key Characteristics

- Identified as a Xianbei leader active during the 133 CE Shun-era frontier notice.
- Associated with raids on Ma Cheng and pressure on the Dai commandery frontier.
- His death is linked to a decline in interior raids, suggesting leadership mattered to Xianbei operational intensity.
