# AppLovin

## Overview
AppLovin is an advertising-technology company described by chief executive [[AdamForoughi|Adam Foroughi]] as helping mobile-game developers monetize attention and acquire users through algorithmic recommendations.

## Current Profile
In the source, AppLovin is moving from game-to-game recommendations toward a broader [[DiscoveryAdvertising]] channel that can introduce products to consumers who did not begin with purchase intent. Its operating case rests on a measurable model-performance loop, mobile-game inventory, differentiated data, automation, and a lean specialist organization. Its public-market case rests on surviving a severe drawdown through cash generation, share repurchases, employee alignment, and a claimed April 2023 deep-learning inflection.

## Key Characteristics
- A performance-advertising platform rooted in mobile-game publishers and rewarded ad inventory.
- A recommendation business that seeks to create new purchase intent rather than only capture existing search intent.
- A model-driven platform where advertiser returns can create a rapid spend-and-data feedback loop.
- A company that temporarily owned game studios to solve its initial training-data cold start.
- A highly automated, lean operation that Foroughi says produces approximately 84% EBITDA margins.
- A public company that used cash flow, repurchases, and performance equity during a roughly 92% drawdown.

## Qualifications
The profile is based on one friendly interview with the company's CEO. Market size, margins, share-price figures, repurchase value, model causality, user scale, and moat durability are not independently verified in the episode. High margins and rapid recovery may reflect genuine software leverage, but the source gives limited attention to regulation, attribution, competition, customer concentration, platform dependence, or downside scenarios.
