# 刘繇 / Liu Yao (late Han)

刘繇 / Liu Yao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-995-ta-zuishang-cibei-weihe-duoci-cansha-enren-llicukoezccd5iycin4uq7qun51k|Hanji 995]] as the Han-recognized official trying to choose a survival route after moving from Dantu. He initially considers [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]], but [[XuShao|许劭]] argues that Kuaiji is rich, exposed to the sea, and wanted by [[SunCe|孙策]], making it dangerous as a refuge.

Xu Shao advises [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]] instead because it keeps Liu Yao connected north toward Yu and west toward Jing, gives him room to settle officials and people, and preserves the chance that [[CaoCao|曹操]] or [[LiuBiao|刘表]] may later support him as a legitimate Han刺史. Liu Yao then stations at Pengze and sends [[ZeRong|笮融]] to support [[ZhuHaoLateHan|朱皓]] against [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s appointee [[ZhugeXuan|诸葛玄]], but Ze Rong kills Zhu Hao and occupies Yuzhang.

The page keeps Liu Yao source-scoped as a route-and-personnel judgment case. His plan has a coherent [[PoliticalSurvivalFirst|安全第一政治生存]] logic, but his use of Ze Rong shows how a correct geographic assessment can still fail when actor character is misread.
