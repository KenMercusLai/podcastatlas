# Memphis / 孟菲斯

## Overview
Memphis / 孟菲斯 is the U.S. logistics-hub city used in the Shengdong Jixi aviation roundtable to show how air freight can extend older transport advantages.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Memphis through FedEx and multimodal geography. Ma Xiao describes it as FedEx's headquarters city and as a convergence point for the U.S. mainland, the Mississippi River system, rail, interstate highways, and aviation.

Memphis therefore functions in the wiki as a logistics-network case: aviation does not replace older route systems so much as amplify cities already sitting at multiple transport crossings.

## Key Characteristics
- Freight-oriented aviation city case.
- Multimodal logistics node combining air, river, rail, and road advantages.
- Example of aviation inheriting and amplifying pre-existing transport geography.
