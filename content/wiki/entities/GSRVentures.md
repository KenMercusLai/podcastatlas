# 金沙江创投 / GSR Ventures

## Overview
金沙江创投 / GSR Ventures is the venture-capital firm through which the episode introduces [[ZhuXiaohu|朱啸虎]]'s O2O investment path.

## Current Profile
The source uses GSR less as a full institutional history than as a capital-positioning node. Through Zhu's investments in Baixing, Lashou, Ele.me, Didi, Xiaohongshu, Inke, and [[Ofo|OFO]], the firm becomes part of the episode's account of how investors read speed, category timing, and network effects during the mobile-internet O2O boom.

## Key Characteristics
- It is presented as Zhu Xiaohu's venture platform in the O2O narrative.
- Its source role is tied to fast growth, category choice, and expansion logic.
- It connects the shared-bike story to earlier group-buying, food-delivery, ride-hailing, and community-platform investments.
- In the OFO branch, it helps represent capital pressure behind rapid shared-bike scaling.
