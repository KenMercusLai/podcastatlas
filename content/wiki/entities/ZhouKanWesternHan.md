## Overview

周堪 / Zhou Kan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-483-1-lishi-jiemi-hun-zhichang-de-jianghu-guiju-lgn4p7pgqa8euxgrspcwdgsusce7|Hanji 483-1]] as a Western Han official previously blamed with [[ZhangMengWesternHan|张萌]] for disaster omens, then recalled to court by [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] after worse disasters weaken that accusation.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Zhou Kan less as an autonomous policy actor than as a case of title without channel power. Yuan recalls him and appoints him guanglu dafu with responsibility for memorials, but [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]] still supervises the memorial channel and fills the surrounding offices with allies. Zhou Kan can hold a formally relevant post yet still depend on hostile intermediaries to reach the ruler.

The source therefore makes Zhou Kan a compact example of failed reentry under an information monopoly. His restoration corrects part of the earlier injustice, but it does not restore effective voice. The episode says he soon suffers loss of voice and dies, making his fate symbolically aligned with the loss of court speech.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official attacked through disaster-omen personnel politics.
- Recalled after later disasters weaken the case against him and Zhang Meng.
- Given a memorial-handling role that should provide access to the ruler.
- Neutralized because Shi Xian supervises the same channel and surrounding personnel.
- Used by the source as a workplace and court-politics example of formal position without effective communication access.
