## Overview

周堪 / Zhou Kan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-472-cong-qizhi-zhuya-dao-zairu-bantu-jiemi-hainan-lishi-lhvp37w_0z8rebkka6squgfgledp|Hanji 472]], [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-478-chushi-zhihui-zhezhong-ren-tai-jiduan-yudao-qing-yuanli-lq3hhg58lohdi-tbpmcuhwajjo7h|Hanji 478]], and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-483-1-lishi-jiemi-hun-zhichang-de-jianghu-guiju-lgn4p7pgqa8euxgrspcwdgsusce7|Hanji 483-1]] as a Western Han official whose early promotion under [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] precedes later exposure to disaster-omen blame, opportunistic denunciation, and [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]]'s control of access to Yuan.

## Current Profile

Hanji 472 supplies the pre-conflict status layer: Yuan promotes Zhou Kan to Guanglu Xun and also advances Zhou Kan's student Zhang Meng. Hanji 478 shows Zhou Kan's vulnerability before the later hollow-recall sequence. Yuan appears to probe whether the disaster blame against Zhou Kan and Zhang Meng should stand, but Yang Xing answers by attacking them and proposing a title-without-power settlement. Zhuge Feng then reverses from earlier praise to denunciation, and Yuan's mixed punishment leaves Zhou Kan demoted even while Zhuge Feng is removed.

Hanji 483-1 presents Zhou Kan less as an autonomous policy actor than as a case of title without channel power. Yuan recalls him and appoints him guanglu dafu with responsibility for memorials, but Shi Xian still supervises the memorial channel and fills the surrounding offices with allies. Zhou Kan can hold a formally relevant post yet still depend on hostile intermediaries to reach the ruler.

The source therefore makes Zhou Kan a compact example of failed reentry under an information monopoly. His restoration corrects part of the earlier injustice, but it does not restore effective voice. The episode says he soon suffers loss of voice and dies, making his fate symbolically aligned with the loss of court speech.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official attacked through disaster-omen personnel politics.
- Earlier promoted by Han Yuandi to Guanglu Xun before the later disaster-blame sequence.
- Teacher-side or anti-Shi-Xian official whose reputation becomes vulnerable to opportunistic advisers and accusers.
- Recalled after later disasters weaken the case against him and Zhang Meng.
- Given a memorial-handling role that should provide access to the ruler.
- Neutralized because Shi Xian supervises the same channel and surrounding personnel.
- Used by the source as a workplace and court-politics example of formal position without effective communication access.

## Qualifications

This profile is source-scoped to Hanji 472, Hanji 478, and Hanji 483-1. It does not settle Zhou Kan's full biography, exact offices beyond these episodes, original conflict with Shi Xian's group, medical condition, or the full historical record behind his death.
