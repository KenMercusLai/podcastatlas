# 少梁 / Shaoliang

少梁 / Shaoliang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] as land [[WeiState|魏国]] yields to [[QinState|秦国]] after Qin surrounds [[Jiaoyi|焦邑]] and [[QuwoWarringStates|曲沃]] in 330 BCE.

The source makes Shaoliang part of a cumulative pressure pattern. Qin's earlier and later attacks are not isolated raids; each settlement leaves Wei weaker and Qin stronger, and the Shaoliang cession sits beside [[HexiWarringStates|河西]] in that sequence.
