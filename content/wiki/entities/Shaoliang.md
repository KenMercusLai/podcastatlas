# 少梁 / Shaoliang

少梁 / Shaoliang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-11-shijie-fengqi-yunyong-2-llzzalowv5vmbvgd6l-jujltkbyf]] as the 362 BCE battlefield where [[QinState|秦国]] defeats [[WeiState|魏国]] and captures a Wei figure rendered as [[GongshuWeiChancellor|公孙措 / 公孙痤 / 公叔痤]]. The episode connects it to [[QinEastwardPressure|秦国东进压力]] and to the immediate succession from [[QinXianGong|秦献公]] to [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-15-jiu-bu-chun-yinfa-de-shijie-dazhan-li-iojxthq8komhlhyumho3yvtem]] adds a 354 BCE sequel: after the failed or inconclusive Duping meeting between [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]] and [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]], Qin defeats Wei at Yuanli and occupies Shaoliang. The episode emphasizes that Shaoliang is a repeatedly contested Qin-Wei city rather than a one-off battlefield.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] later records Shaoliang as land Wei yields to Qin after Qin surrounds [[Jiaoyi|焦邑]] and [[QuwoWarringStates|曲沃]] in 330 BCE. Taken together, the sources make Shaoliang part of a cumulative pressure pattern: Qin's earlier and later attacks are not isolated raids, and each settlement leaves Wei weaker and Qin stronger.
