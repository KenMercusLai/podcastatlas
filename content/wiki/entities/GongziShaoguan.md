# 公子少官 / Gongzi Shaoguan

公子少官 / Gongzi Shaoguan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-18-zhongguo-lishishang-di-yi-li-rentoushui-lgqnequk0rxejqgihwdqxqyqnsvq]] as the Qin prince sent by [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]] to [[Fengze|逢泽]]. He leads troops, joins the assembled lords, and proceeds with them to audience with the Zhou king.

The page is source-scoped. Gongzi Shaoguan matters because [[QinState|秦国]]'s formal elevation happens even though Qin Xiao Gong does not personally attend, and because the military escort makes [[ZhouHegemonRecognition|Zhou hegemon recognition]] look like symbolic rank backed by visible force.
