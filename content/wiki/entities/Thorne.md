# Thorne

Thorne appears in [[fuzhuang-pinpai-a-f-xunzhao-zhongguo-hezuo-huoban-fufei-tiqian-kan-telangpu-tiewen-fuwu-shangxian-1004810677]] as the premium nutrition and supplement brand [[ProcterGamble|Procter & Gamble]] agreed to acquire for $3.8 billion. The source says Thorne was founded in 1984 and sells products such as folate, vitamins, and other nutritional supplements.

The source frames Thorne as evidence that large consumer-goods companies see supplement demand as a premium, recurring category. It also highlights valuation acceleration: the episode says Thorne had been acquired in 2023 for $680 million and was expected to reach about $650 million in annual revenue.
