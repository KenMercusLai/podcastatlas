# JJ Goode

## Overview

JJ Goode is a writer whose prosopagnosia anchors [[face-value-face-value|Face Value]]'s account of how an invisible recognition difference can be mistaken for interpersonal rejection.

## Current Profile

Goode became friends with [[MissyKurzweil]] at a coffee shop but failed to identify her when she moved away from their usual table. Kurzweil reasonably read two apparent walk-bys as deliberate avoidance until his later warmth and another recognition failure revealed the mismatch between his behavior and intent.

He identifies people through context and changeable cues such as hair, glasses, or a distinctive chin. His broad accommodation is social rather than technical: he greets everyone entering the coffee shop as though they may already know one another. The episode presents that habit as a way to protect relationships without pretending that compensatory cues are reliable.

## Key Characteristics

- Experiences prosopagnosia while retaining ordinary perception of facial features and emotion as described by the episode.
- Recognized the pattern through repeated failures with acquaintances and film characters whose appearance changed.
- Uses context and distinctive nonidentity cues to compensate.
- Treats uncertain recognition as a reason for inclusive greeting rather than withdrawal.
- Makes the social cost of face blindness visible through a friendship that nearly ended before explanation.

## Qualifications

The page is bounded to Goode's account in one structured episode summary. It does not provide a diagnostic record, test results, developmental history, or a complete account of his functioning. Prosopagnosia varies across people, and his strategies should not be treated as universal or foolproof.
