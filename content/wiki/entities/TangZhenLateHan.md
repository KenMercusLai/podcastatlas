# 唐珍 / Tang Zhen (late Han)

唐珍 / Tang Zhen enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-904-juezhao-neiguan-huode-xinling-da-ziyou-de-mijue-lopn-okbzr4ljvqmgzcsb7qav0n2|Hanji 904]] as the official appointed sikong after [[YangCiLateHan|杨赐]] is removed in 173 CE. The episode's interpretive point is not a full biography but the appointment channel: Tang Zhen is identified as [[TangHengLateHan|唐衡]]'s younger brother, so his rise shows eunuch-family connection entering the Three Excellencies field.

His appointment sits beside epidemic, earthquake, amnesty, frontier pressure, and solar-eclipse notices. The page therefore treats him as an early office-churn marker under [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]], before later episodes develop Hongdu selection, office sale, and armed capture as more elaborate versions of appointment disorder.
