# 《自深深处》 / De Profundis

[[DeProfundis|《自深深处》 / De Profundis]] appears in [[10-wang-erde-qiyi-gushiji-yong-zui-hui-sahuang-de-zuichun-qinwen-tianshi-545332195]] as the prison-letter context for [[OscarWilde|Oscar Wilde / 王尔德]]'s relationship with [[AlfredDouglas|Lord Alfred Douglas / 波西]]. The episode stresses the letter's mixed emotional logic: Wilde condemns Douglas's behavior and the damage to his money, health, and creative life while still preserving attachment and love.

For the wiki, the page matters less as a full biography than as a bridge between [[LiteraryGossipAsContext]] and the story discussion. The source uses the letter to show that Wilde's aesthetic pose did not float above law, illness, debt, dependency, and prison; his later life puts pressure on any simple reading of his wit as effortless brilliance.
