# 许汜 / Xu Si (Late Han)

许汜 / Xu Si (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-974-caocao-xiang-ta-tuo-qi-xian-zi-weihe-zhuanshen-ai-daozi-lhfabab7xv2vfsgnhr9ozwqhmhd|Hanji 974]] as one of the officials who secretly discuss betraying [[CaoCao|曹操]] while Cao Cao is away attacking [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] in [[Xuzhou|徐州]]. The episode names him with [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]], [[WangKaiLateHan|王楷]], and [[ZhangChaoLateHan|张超]] in the group that moves the plan toward [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]].

The source does not give Xu Si an independent biography. His wiki role is as evidence that the Yanzhou rupture was not only Zhang Miao's private decision; it involved a local official-adviser circle willing to invite [[LyuBu|吕布]] into [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]].
