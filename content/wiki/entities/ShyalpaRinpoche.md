# Shyalpa Rinpoche

## Overview
Shyalpa Rinpoche is the Tibetan Buddhist teacher interviewed by [[ShekharNatarajan]] in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-97450677c47f502770248abb02f35793-163628914-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-97450677c47f502770248abb02f35793-163628914]]. The episode presents him through exile family history, childhood recognition as a reincarnate child, rigorous Buddhist education, training under [[ChhatralRinpoche]], teaching across cultures, and leadership of the [[UniversalPeaceSanctuary]] vision.

## Current Profile
The source frames Shyalpa Rinpoche less as a biographical celebrity than as a lineage-based teacher translating Tibetan Buddhist practice into a global peace and technology ethics conversation. His account joins trauma, discipline, compassion, karma, [[Dzogchen]], breathing, stillness, death, and institutional peace-building into one profile: inner clarity is treated as the basis for both personal conduct and public peace.

## Key Characteristics
- Tibetan Buddhist teacher whose family displacement from Tibet grounds his emphasis on cultural resilience and compassion.
- Recognized in childhood as a reincarnate child and trained in philosophy, ritual, memory, discipline, and lineage responsibility.
- Student of [[ChhatralRinpoche]] and a wider group of masters, with [[Dzogchen]] presented as a central path of awareness and completeness.
- Public peace-builder through the [[UniversalPeaceSanctuary]] project in Lumbini.
- Cross-cultural teacher who argues that dharma, compassion, and wisdom can be communicated through reasoning, honesty, skillful means, and shared human mind.
- Technology interlocutor who connects [[AngelicIntelligence]] to absolute intelligence, compassion, meaning, and AI serving human development.
