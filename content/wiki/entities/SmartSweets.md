## Overview
SmartSweets is the lower-sugar candy company founded by [[TaraBosch]] and used as the main operating reference in [[advice-line-with-tara-bosch-of-smartsweets-8aab80a1-b0c5-4103-b875-f5d4823d07ce]].

## Current Profile
In the episode, SmartSweets functions as a consumer-brand case for combining taste with a better-for-you promise, using wholesale and DTC for different purchase occasions, learning through natural retail, and matching national ambition to manufacturing and distribution readiness.

## Key Characteristics
- Built around reducing sugar without abandoning the expected candy experience.
- Reportedly used a wholesale-heavy mix alongside a meaningful DTC channel.
- Serves as Bosch's evidence that physical retail and online sales can be complementary.
- Provides the prior operating experience behind Bosch's advice on production, positioning, and retailer sequencing.

## Evidence
### Product promise
- [[advice-line-with-tara-bosch-of-smartsweets-8aab80a1-b0c5-4103-b875-f5d4823d07ce]] identifies SmartSweets as Bosch's low-sugar candy company and uses its experience to frame better-for-you indulgence.

### Channel and scale lessons
- [[advice-line-with-tara-bosch-of-smartsweets-8aab80a1-b0c5-4103-b875-f5d4823d07ce]] reports an approximately 70% wholesale and 30% DTC historical mix and connects early natural-retail access to learning before mass expansion.
