## Overview

Dong Xian / 董贤 is synthesized as [[HanAidi|Emperor Ai of Han]]'s highly visible favorite and a weak political operator at the late [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] succession hinge. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-602-tanxun-guoxue-zhihui-pojie-2024nian-fantaisui-zhi-mi-lvrwnpanicgjpf43cbmveigtxqj|Hanji 602]] shows him already prominent enough for a Xiongnu chanyu to ask about him in court, while [[126-wangmang-zhisi-shensheng-diguo-de-huixuanbiao-he-huixuanbiao-de-huixuanbiao-786255780]] uses his later failure to show that imperial proximity and symbols do not automatically become durable power. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-603-aidi-sihou-wangmang-ruhe-miaosu-duoquan-lvp-ywlvxiwrqdoj-4b-sx9486ol|Hanji 603]] adds the missing crisis scene: after Aidi dies, Dong Xian cannot answer Wang Zhengjun's funeral-procedure questions, loses palace access through Wang Mang's impeachment move, has his Da Sima seal and ribbon recovered, and dies with his wife the same day. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-605-dongxian-sihou-tamen-liuxiale-eyu-de-yanlei-lladjfbixqzhxjurjs1z8ztsa1st|Hanji 605]] adds the aftermath: once Aidi is dead and Dong Xian is gone, the same favorite status exposes his family, appointees, and property to rapid Wang Mang cleanup.

## Current Profile

Dong Xian's visibility layer comes from the Yuanshou 2 chanyu visit. The foreign ruler notices a young, handsome high official among the Han court ministers and asks who he is. Aidi has him identified as the grand marshal Dong Xian and explains his high office through youth paired with worthiness. The scene does not prove Dong Xian's competence, but it does show his conspicuous elevation within Aidi's court.

Dong Xian's succession-failure layer turns on symbolic access without organizational control. Emperor Ai entrusted him with the imperial seal before death, but Dong Xian blocked information without taking effective political action. Hanji 603 makes that failure operational: when Wang Zhengjun asks him how to arrange Aidi's funeral, he panics, removes his cap, kowtows, and cannot provide a plan. He had palace and military proximity but lacked the judgment needed to convert possession into rule, allowing [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] and [[WangMang|王莽]] to recover control.

Hanji 605 supplies the posthumous exposure layer. Wang Mang prosecutes the Dong family after first handling the Fu and Ding households: Dong-family wealth is confiscated, officials advanced through Dong Xian are removed, his father Dong Gong, brother Dong Kuanxin, and relatives are relocated, and the episode treats the reported forty-three hundred million cash from property sale as evidence of a spectacular but fragile favorite-dependent fortune. Dong Xian's wiki role is therefore diagnostic rather than biographical. He marks the difference between intimacy, status, authority, and safety: a favorite can be visible enough to impress foreign observers and still fail when the court's real succession machinery starts moving, then leave his network exposed after his patron dies.

## Key Characteristics

- Highly visible favorite of Han Aidi whose youth and appearance draw notice in a diplomatic court scene.
- Holder of very high office in Aidi's reign, presented by the court as worthy despite youth.
- Possessor of palace and symbolic access at Aidi's death.
- Failed succession operator whose possession of office and proximity did not become funeral, palace, or command control.
- Immediate purge target whose loss of palace access, seal, ribbon, and life lets Wang Mang reenter the center.
- Posthumous cleanup target whose family, appointees, and property are punished after Aidi's death.
- Foil for Wang Zhengjun and Wang Mang, whose institutional and seniority advantages beat Dong Xian's proximity.

## Qualifications

The current page is not a full biography of Dong Xian. Hanji 602 supports his public visibility and court rank in one diplomatic scene, while the Xiangrui source supports his late succession failure. Hanji 603 supports his immediate incapacity, access removal, office removal, death, and coffin inspection, but Aidi's exact cause of death, Dong Xian's inner state, the full legal micro-procedure, and Wang Mang's private psychology remain source-scoped. Hanji 605 supports the episode's account of family confiscation, appointee removal, relocation, and fragile favorite-dependent wealth, but the complete Dong-family kin list, the legal basis for each punishment, and crowd motives outside the Dong household remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 603's immediate crisis layer: funeral-procedure incapacity, palace-access loss, office removal, death, and coffin verification.
- Preserved Hanji 605's posthumous cleanup layer: property confiscation, appointee removal, family relocation, and fragile patronage wealth.
