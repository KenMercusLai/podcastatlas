# Catherine Judge

## Overview
Catherine Judge is the Columbia law professor quoted in the Marketplace Tech episode on AI use in banking.

## Current Profile
Judge contributes the regulator-side third-party software argument. In the source, she says bank regulators should be able to understand the services third-party providers supply and monitor their risks more directly, rather than leaving all model-risk visibility inside individual banks.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames banking AI risk as a vendor-oversight problem as well as a bank-compliance problem.
- Emphasizes regulator access to understanding third-party services.
- Shifts some attention from individual-bank responsibility toward direct software-provider monitoring.
