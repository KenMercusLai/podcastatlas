# Felicette / 菲利切特

Felicette / 菲利切特 is the French space cat mentioned in [[34-maomi-mishi-he-yuchun-you-keai-de-renlei-601590607]]. The episode places her near the end of a long cat-history arc, after granaries, temples, ships, wars, and polar expeditions, to show that [[Cat|猫]] also entered modern state science and space experimentation.

In the source, Felicette's flight is described as a short 1960s rocket mission that survived the trip. The page records the episode's role for her rather than making a full independent space-history claim: Felicette is the "space age" endpoint of [[MaomiMishiBook|《猫咪秘史：从史前时期到太空时代》]] and a reminder that animal usefulness can become scientific exposure as well as companionship.

## Key Claims
- Felicette turns the cat-history narrative from premodern practical labor into modern experimental science.
- Her role sits uneasily between contribution, state prestige, and [[AnimalExperimentEthics]].
- The episode uses her as evidence that cats were not culturally marginal even in the technological imagination.
