# 长平观之战 / Changpingguan Battle (late Han)

长平观之战 / Changpingguan Battle (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-973-changpingguan-zhizhan-shengsi-zhidi-zhanhou-jing-chonggui-yuhao-lgazc9dewfayb8qnkncfkfa12yet|Hanji 973]] as the failed 194 CE western intervention against [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]]. The page uses `ChangpingguanBattleLateHan` to avoid collision with the Warring States [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]].

In the source, [[MaTeng|马腾]] attacks after clashing with Li Jue, while [[HanSui|韩遂]] arrives as a mediator and then shifts to Ma Teng's side. Court officials [[ZhongShaoLateHan|种劭]], [[MaYuLateHan|马宇]], and [[LiuFanLateHan|刘范]] also prepare an internal response from [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]], but the plot leaks before it can work.

Li Jue sends [[FanChouLateHan|樊稠]], [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]], and [[LiLiLateHan|李利]] against Ma Teng and Han Sui. They defeat the attackers, force Ma Teng back toward Liangzhou, pursue Han Sui, and attack Huaili, where the inside-plot actors are killed.

The event's long-term importance is indirect. The episode says the battle does not immediately transform the map, but Han Sui's later conversation and drinking with Fan Chou show how wartime enemy status can coexist with hometown ties and private courtesy. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-984-liangzhouxi-junfa-neidou-ta-weihe-di-yige-daoxia-lurb2qfzsa7e-owh-dl-m-sccze5|Hanji 984]] later makes that field contact usable as suspicion against Fan Chou inside [[MilitaryPrestigeThreatPurge|军功声望威胁清洗]].
