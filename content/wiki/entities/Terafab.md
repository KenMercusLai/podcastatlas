# Terafab

## Overview
Terafab is the source-scoped [[Tesla]] and [[SpaceX]] chip-manufacturing initiative discussed by [[ElonMusk]] and [[GwenShotwell|Gwynne Shotwell]] in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-elon-musk-gwynne-shotwell-on-ai-risks-and-peer-review-starship-terafab-spacex-tesla-merger-42906702]].

## Current Profile
The episode frames Terafab as both a geopolitical hedge and an AI-scaling dependency. Musk says future chip supply could be constrained by Taiwan risk, fab capacity, packaging capacity, and rising demand from AI servers, humanoid robots, and cars. Shotwell describes execution as staged rather than immediate scale manufacturing, while Musk says an R&D fab is being built in Austin at the Giga Texas campus.

## Key Characteristics
- Proposed chip-manufacturing effort linked to Tesla and SpaceX rather than a standalone public semiconductor company.
- Motivated by secure future supply for AI servers, humanoid robots, and cars.
- Explicitly framed as a hedge against possible [[Taiwan]] chip-supply disruption.
- Begins with R&D and packaging capability before attempting large-scale production.
- Depends on logic, memory, packaging, equipment delivery, and manufacturing learning rather than only chip-design intent.

## Qualifications
The page records Terafab as described in one interview. It does not independently verify equipment orders, fab construction status, production capability, chip-node ambitions, Taiwan-risk timing, or whether Tesla and SpaceX will formalize the project structurally.
