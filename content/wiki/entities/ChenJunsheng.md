# 陈俊生 / Chen Junsheng

## Overview
陈俊生 / Chen Junsheng enters the wiki as Luo Zijun's ex-husband in [[MyFirstHalfLife|《我的前半生》]], read in EP385 as a more complex weak man than a flat villain.

## Current Profile
The source treats Chen Junsheng as responsible for marital betrayal while still dramatically vivid because he remains tired, avoidant, soft, and socially recognizable. The hosts also compare his TV name to the novel's "涓生," arguing that the literary allusion to Lu Xun's [[LuXun|《伤逝》]] character carries more cultural weight than the drama's more generic name.

## Key Characteristics
- Marriage-betrayal trigger for Luo Zijun's divorce and rebuilding arc.
- Weak and avoidant man rather than a single-note villain.
- Workplace-marriage fatigue figure whose second household does not solve his exhaustion.
- Character whose ordinary vulnerability makes the affair feel socially plausible.
- Adaptation-loss case because the name change weakens the novel's literary allusion.
