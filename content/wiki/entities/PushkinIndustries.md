# Pushkin Industries

## Overview

Pushkin Industries is the podcast company behind [[RevisionistHistory]] and the workplace setting for this teaser's detergent dispute.

## Current Profile

The source depicts Pushkin as willing to package internal criticism as bonus audio: employees challenge [[MalcolmGladwell]]'s earlier framing, the host answers with mockery and praise, and the resulting exchange promotes Pushkin Plus. That presentation suggests editorial tolerance and self-aware workplace humor, but a company-produced teaser cannot independently establish the breadth of dissent or employment safety inside the organization.

## Key Characteristics

- Produces [[RevisionistHistory]] and uses its staff disagreement as follow-up material.
- Markets a paid subscription through ad-free listening, behind-the-scenes material, extended interviews, outtakes, and bonus content.
- Presents internal criticism as compatible with continued employment and creative contribution.
- Turns editorial self-critique into both audience value and subscription promotion.

### Workplace culture claim

- [[teaser-pushkin-s-dirty-laundry-teaser-pushkin-s-dirty-laundry]] has Gladwell say that employees can hold their theories so long as they make good work, within an exchange framed as joking rather than a formal policy statement.

## Qualifications

- The source is produced by the company and functions partly as an advertisement for its subscription product.
- A staged, humorous exchange with senior employees does not demonstrate how criticism is handled throughout the organization.
- The price, naming, benefits, and catalog described are dated to the 2021 teaser and should not be treated as current.

## What Changed

- Initial profile captures Pushkin's use of internal editorial criticism as both follow-up journalism and subscription marketing.
