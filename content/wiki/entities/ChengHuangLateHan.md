# 程璜 / Cheng Huang (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-912-mimi-daodi-gai-bugai-shuo-chukou-lhsgipxwc17pahc-r6jl7xdyrs2a|Hanji 912]] supplies the pre-Hanji-913 retaliation setup. After [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] leaks [[CaiYong|蔡邕]]'s confidential memorial, the source says Cheng Huang secretly directs an anonymous accusation against Cai Yong and Cai's uncle Cai Zhi, using old grievances with [[LiuHeSituLateHan|刘郃]] and [[YangQiuLateHan|阳球]] as the attack surface. The transcript's exact kinship wording between Cheng Huang and Yang Qiu is kept source-scoped, while the stable point is a household or affinal access path.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-913-kan-dong-saiweng-shima-wu-tou-rensheng-fuhuo-lkfbrwtgsjma85bzugqonzk3hy4g|Hanji 913]] adds Cheng Huang's earlier role before the Hanji 918 plot leak. The source says he secretly instigates an accusation against [[CaiYong|蔡邕]] and Cai's uncle, turning private resentment claims into a case that nearly kills Cai.

[[ChengHuangLateHan|程璜]] was the eunuch intermediary through whom the 179 CE anti-[[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] plot in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-918-shiqing-bu-shunli-shi-weishenme-yao-bizui-lh-tbohgo96qw8bjqu3x1yp6czv5|Hanji 918]] leaks.

Because Cheng Huang can be reached through [[YangQiuLateHan|阳球]]'s household, he turns private relational access into political surveillance. Once pressured by gifts and threats, he tells Cao Jie, who accuses Liu He and the others before [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]].
