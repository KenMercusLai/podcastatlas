# 程璜 / Cheng Huang (late Han)

[[ChengHuangLateHan|程璜]] was the eunuch intermediary through whom the 179 CE anti-[[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] plot in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-918-shiqing-bu-shunli-shi-weishenme-yao-bizui-lh-tbohgo96qw8bjqu3x1yp6czv5|Hanji 918]] leaks.

Because Cheng Huang can be reached through [[YangQiuLateHan|阳球]]'s household, he turns private relational access into political surveillance. Once pressured by gifts and threats, he tells Cao Jie, who accuses Liu He and the others before [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]].
