# Mike Bauer

## Overview

Mike Bauer is a retired [[LosAngelesCountySheriffsDepartment|LASD]] captain and former supervisor of [[JohnAjay]] who disputes the department's reported suicide explanation for Ajay's 1998 disappearance.

## Current Profile

[[from-valley-of-shadows-the-devil-s-punchbowl-from-valley-of-shadows-the-devil-s-punchbowl|The Devil's Punchbowl]] uses Bauer as its principal insider critic. He recalls speaking with Ajay about an assignment on the day he vanished, says an official promoted suicide during search briefings by the third day, and argues that responsibility for the case was never made clear.

After retiring in 2002, Bauer reportedly devoted substantial time to investigating the disappearance. He alleges that homicide personnel excluded and lied to him and warns the reporters that further inquiry may be dangerous. His rank, supervisory relationship, and persistence make his account important, but the episode does not independently verify all his allegations.

## Key Characteristics

- Retired LASD captain with intelligence and supervisory experience.
- Former supervisor of John Ajay's unit.
- Early skeptic of the reported suicide explanation.
- Persistent post-retirement investigator of Ajay's disappearance.
- Insider source making serious but incompletely corroborated allegations.

## Qualifications

Bauer is both an informed insider and an advocate for reopening scrutiny, which gives him access and a strong interpretive position. The supplied summary does not include the case file, the accused personnel's responses, or independent corroboration for every claim. His allegations should therefore be preserved as allegations rather than adopted as proof of a cover-up.
