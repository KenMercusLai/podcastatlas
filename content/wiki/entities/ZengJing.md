# 曾静 / Zeng Jing

曾静 / Zeng Jing is the rural scholar at the center of the case in [[11-yongzheng-wangchao-zhi-dayi-juemi-shijingqian-bi-xia-de-qiangu-qi-an-547265513]]. The episode presents him as the teacher who sent [[ZhangXi|张熙]] to persuade [[YueZhongqi|岳钟琪]] to rebel, but not as the head of a mature revolutionary organization.

Zeng Jing's political imagination, as the source summarizes it, combined civil-service frustration, local distress, omen reading, idealized Confucian restoration, and [[LuLiuliang|吕留良]]'s anti-Qing Hua-Yi arguments. Once arrested, he admitted primary responsibility and became the object of [[YongzhengEmperor|雍正]]'s forced pedagogical project: repeated confession, imperial correction, and eventual publication in [[DaiYiJueMiLu|《大义觉迷录》]].

The episode treats his fate as a measure of imperial strategy. Yongzheng spared him as a deluded commoner and propaganda convert, while [[QianlongEmperor|乾隆]] later executed him and Zhang Xi after Yongzheng's death. That reversal makes Zeng Jing a case for [[ImperialPublicRebuttal]], [[PoliticalPropagandaBackfire]], and the instability of pardon under personal rule.
