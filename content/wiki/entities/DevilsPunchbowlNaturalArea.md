# Devil's Punchbowl Natural Area

## Overview

Devil's Punchbowl is the rugged Antelope Valley park where [[JohnAjay]] began his final known run and where the initial search for him was centered.

## Current Profile

[[from-valley-of-shadows-the-devil-s-punchbowl-from-valley-of-shadows-the-devil-s-punchbowl|The Devil's Punchbowl]] describes a landscape joining desert, forest, mountains, steep rock formations, earthquake faults, gullies, mines, and routes toward Mount Baden-Powell. Ajay reportedly visited regularly and parked near the main trailhead, where his truck was found after he failed to return.

The terrain makes accident, injury, concealment, and a very large search radius plausible without resolving the disappearance. Bloodhound interest, a reported gunshot, footprints, a rugged canyon, and an examination of the sealed Big Horn Mine produced no confirmed breakthrough in the account.

## Key Characteristics

- Antelope Valley wilderness area near Pearblossom in Los Angeles County.
- Mixes desert, forest, mountain, fault, canyon, and rock terrain.
- Familiar training landscape for Ajay.
- Starting point and principal search area in the 1998 disappearance.
- Terrain whose scale and complexity widened the search problem.
