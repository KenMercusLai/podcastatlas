# Elizabeth Laird

Elizabeth Laird appears in [[tech-20260409-0409-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260409-0409-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the [[CenterForDemocracyAndTechnology|Center for Democracy and Technology]] speaker explaining nationally representative polling on federal government data practices. Her main contribution is to connect broad privacy concern to concrete behavior: people may avoid public benefits if they do not trust how their data will be used.

For the wiki, Laird turns data privacy from a private-sector or data-broker problem into a public-services trust problem. She links [[FederalDataPracticeTrust]] to [[PublicBenefitsDataChillingEffect]], [[EnforcementAgencyDataSharing]], and [[GovernmentDataAccountability]] rather than treating concern as only generalized anxiety about data collection.
