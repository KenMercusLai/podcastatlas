# 燕易王 / King Yi of Yan

燕易王 / King Yi of Yan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-29-suqin-lizhi-shuangwen-zhishi-gaibian-mingyun-lrf5cqvoriyep2lkbozju-lyjaxv]] as the successor after [[YanWengong|燕文公]] dies in the episode's 333 BCE succession cluster.

The source does not develop his later rule. Here he anchors Yan's place in the broader [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]] annalistic frame before the episode shifts to [[SuQin|苏秦]].
