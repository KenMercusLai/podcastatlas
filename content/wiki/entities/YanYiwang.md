# 燕易王 / King Yi of Yan

燕易王 / King Yi of Yan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-29-suqin-lizhi-shuangwen-zhishi-gaibian-mingyun-lrf5cqvoriyep2lkbozju-lyjaxv]] as the successor after [[YanWengong|燕文公]] dies in the episode's 333 BCE succession cluster.

The source does not develop his later rule. Here he anchors Yan's place in the broader [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]] annalistic frame before the episode shifts to [[SuQin|苏秦]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-37-suqin-liuguo-jituan-ceo-de-bukan-qingshi-fj2kdqlaxzyy2nqtcutktuwrq5fo]] develops him through the Su Qin scandal. The episode says Yan Yi Wang learns that Su Qin had been involved with [[YanWengong|燕文公]]'s wife. Su Qin then argues that he can serve [[YanState|燕国]] better by pretending to flee to [[QiState|齐国]], and Yan Yi Wang agrees. His role is therefore not only succession: he authorizes the covert Yan-facing use of Su Qin in Qi.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-40-5-yue-chusheng-de-haizi-re-shui-la-ll9e-thidxl5cqoacfrbz-ygw8-t]] gives the endpoint of that Yan reign notice. In 321 BCE, Yan Yi Wang dies and [[YanWangKuai|燕王哙]] succeeds him. The episode does not develop the transition; it uses the notice as one of the compact annalistic handoffs in [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]]'s final year.
