# Satya Nadella

Satya Nadella is [[Microsoft]]'s CEO and the guest in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-microsoft-ceo-satya-nadella-on-ais-business-revolution-what-happens-to-saas-openai-and-microsoft-live-from-davos-39818140]]. In the source, he explains Microsoft's AI strategy as a platform stack: [[MicrosoftCopilot|Copilot]] and [[GitHubCopilot|GitHub Copilot]] for work surfaces, [[Agent365|Agent 365]] for governed agent identity, [[Azure]] as a token-serving infrastructure layer, [[MicrosoftFoundry|Microsoft Foundry]] for model and agent orchestration, and [[Windows]] plus [[PhiSilica|Phi Silica]] for local AI.

His main strategic contribution to the wiki is the diffusion frame. Nadella argues that AI value depends on broad use across companies, sectors, countries, and public services rather than only frontier model ownership. He also frames workers as people who will increasingly delegate to many AI systems while retaining steering, review, and judgment.
