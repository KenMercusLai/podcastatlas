# 鲍叔牙 / Bao Shuya

鲍叔牙 / Bao Shuya enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-75-2-qiongshe-jichi-de-zushiye-guanzhong-lo8uei2u1qwj9tszftel1b4lswoa]], where he is both [[QiHuanGong|姜小白 / 齐桓公]]'s protector and [[GuanZhong|管仲]]'s decisive recommender. Before [[QiXiangGong|齐襄公]]'s disorder fully becomes regime crisis, Bao Shuya anticipates danger and takes Jiang Xiaobai to Ju as a safer external base.

His more important wiki role is talent mediation after civil conflict. Guan Zhong had supported [[GongziJiu|公子纠]] and even shot at Jiang Xiaobai during the succession race, but Bao Shuya persuades the new Qi Huan Gong to use Guan Zhong rather than settle a private score. The episode therefore makes Bao Shuya a strong [[TalentReferralQuality|荐才层级质量]] case: the best recommendation can require asking a ruler to look past injury, faction, and personal resentment.

The page should stay source-scoped. The episode is not a full biography of Bao Shuya; it uses him to explain how [[QiState|齐国]] gets access to the minister whose reforms make [[ConcessionBasedHegemony|hegemonic diplomacy]] possible.
