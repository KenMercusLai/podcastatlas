# 番茄小说 / Fanqie Novel

## Overview
Fanqie Novel is ByteDance's free-reading product and, in the bounded evidence, both a growth-system case and an upstream story, rights, and audience substrate for [[Hongguo|红果]] and [[AIShortDrama|AI漫剧]].

## Current Profile
The growth source places Fanqie inside a ByteDance system suited to free or ad-supported content with broad supply, measurable retention, recommendation, acquisition, and internal traffic. It also connects Fanqie's advertising and creative-material lineage to later short-drama growth.

The creator sources make that relationship operational. [[AnhuiXiaoMujiang|《安徽小木匠》]] began with a Fanqie story licensed through a copyright center, then moved through production, distribution, and revenue sharing. The follow-up says creators can discover stories by listening nightly, apply for rights, adapt in segments, and feed audiences back toward originals when viewers want to continue before new video episodes arrive. Fanqie is therefore not merely an IP warehouse: it is part of a cross-format loop joining reading, listening, video, rights, recommendation, and settlement.

## Key Characteristics
- Free-reading product suited to ByteDance's recommendation and acquisition system.
- Upstream story-discovery and adaptation-rights layer for short drama.
- Cross-format audience loop connecting novels, audio, and video.
- Content and growth lineage feeding Hongguo's supply and distribution ecosystem.
- Rights channel whose availability and terms can materially constrain creators.

## Evidence
- Growth-system role: [[quanmian-yazhi-buliu-kongdang-zijie-tiaodong-ruhe-zuo-zengzhang-zijie-tiaodong-di-7-ji-lqszvmur6jv8b9xt8rjgmwfdqtec]] places Fanqie among ByteDance products where free content, recommendation, paid acquisition, creative supply, and retention work together.
- Rights and distribution case: [[267-3000-kuai-chengben-3-5-yi-ci-bofang-ai-duanju-zenme-zai-douyin-zhengqian-lrvmi-ruxz81afnqxdfk-dldixhx]] traces 《安徽小木匠》 from Fanqie authorization into the Hongguo/Douyin revenue chain and says that particular adaptation route later closed.
- Creator discovery and cross-format demand: [[lhhrwpwmn7e-unjt7wpxdixllxgs-lhhrwpwmn7e-unjt7wpxdixllxgs]] describes listening-based story selection, rights applications, segmented adaptation, and viewers returning to originals while waiting for drama updates.

## Qualifications
Rights routes, readership figures, audience transfer, and commercial mechanisms are episode- or participant-reported. The sources do not provide current contract terms, representative conversion rates, or proof that video-to-novel transfer occurs for every adaptation.
