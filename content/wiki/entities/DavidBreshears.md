# David Breshears

## Overview
David Breshears is the filmmaker and Everest expedition leader named in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-842831df1049b1024b0dfd8bf11ec723-108598757-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-842831df1049b1024b0dfd8bf11ec723-108598757]]. ## Current Profile
Within the source, Breshears matters less as a full biography than as a team designer. Segarra recalls that he did not want only the best climbers, but people able to work together at the highest level, and she links the expedition's gender equity, international diversity, and trust to that selection logic.

## Key Characteristics
- Filmmaker associated in the source with the Everest IMAX documentary expedition.
- Opportunity connector for [[AraceliSegarra]], whom he met at Everest base camp after her earlier north-side Everest attempt.
- Expedition leader whose selection criteria emphasized collaboration, not only individual climbing ability.
- Team-design example for [[ExpeditionTeamTrust]] because his crew combined high-altitude skill, filming constraints, and rescue responsibility.
