# Omai

## Overview

Omai was a Polynesian traveler who became a public celebrity in Britain and whose return home supplied the declared purpose of [[JamesCook]]'s third Pacific voyage.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Omai at the intersection of Indigenous mobility, European celebrity, elite patronage, and imperial expedition. In Britain he met [[GeorgeIII]] and was painted by Joshua Reynolds. On returning to the Society Islands, he carried furniture, weapons, armor, a globe, and other British goods, while the king supplied livestock intended to introduce European farming.

His return made the voyage legible as benevolent repatriation even though the Admiralty's secret objective was a Pacific search for the Northwest Passage. The supplied source gives little access to Omai's own interpretation of his celebrity, possessions, or return, and later reports that he died about three years afterward remain secondhand within the episode.

## Key Characteristics

- Polynesian traveler whose movement to Britain complicates a one-directional European discovery story.
- Celebrity received by George III and represented in elite British portraiture.
- Publicly stated reason for Cook's third voyage to the Pacific.
- Carrier of European prestige goods, weapons, domestic objects, and scientific material on his return.
- Participant whose own judgments and later life are only thinly recoverable in the supplied account.

## Qualifications

This is a one-source profile filtered through a popular-history episode and European records. It does not establish Omai's own motives, the meaning he assigned to British celebrity and goods, the effects of the livestock and weapons, or the exact date and cause of his death.
