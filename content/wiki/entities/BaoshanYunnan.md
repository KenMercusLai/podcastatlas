# 保山 / Baoshan, Yunnan

## Overview
Baoshan / 保山 is a Yunnan coffee-producing area discussed in [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]].

## Current Profile
Baoshan now has two wiki roles. In the Yunnan coffee interview, it is an earlier specialty-coffee comparison point with a high-altitude Catimor 7963 example. In the Nujiang travel episode, it is also a practical gateway between Kunming and [[NujiangGorge|Nujiang Gorge]], and a place where travelers can encounter coffee production, local snacks, old-city sites, and western Yunnan wartime memory before returning to larger transit systems.

## Key Characteristics
- Treated as an earlier Yunnan specialty-coffee region.
- Used to show that traditional local varieties can produce high-quality cups under suitable conditions.
- Provides one of the episode's concrete high-altitude quality examples.
- Functions as a gateway city for organized Nujiang travel.
- Makes Yunnan coffee processing, cupping, and estate management visible to tourists.
- Adds old-city and wartime-history context beyond coffee.

## Evidence
- Specialty history: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] contrasts Baoshan with Pu'er and Dehong by saying Baoshan moved toward specialty earlier.
- Traditional variety example: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] reports Yu Wenjie's praise for a Baoshan 1900-meter Catimor 7963 lot.
- High-altitude boundary: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] keeps altitude tied to frost, microclimate, variety, tree age, and processing rather than treating elevation as a single quality guarantee.
- Nujiang gateway: [[245-nujiang-chunri-mijing-chutan-8221838951-646453]] has the hosts take the train from Kunming to Baoshan, then enter Nujiang by car before returning through Baoshan.
- Tourist-legible coffee production: [[245-nujiang-chunri-mijing-chutan-8221838951-646453]] describes a Baoshan coffee estate visit with cupping, hand-brew learning, ripe-cherry picking messages, anaerobic tanks, honey processing, natural drying, coffee trees, and Yunnan arabica management.
- City and history layer: [[245-nujiang-chunri-mijing-chutan-8221838951-646453]] adds Yuhuang Pavilion, Yiluo Pond, Guangzun Temple, Qinglong Old Street, student deaths during Japanese bombing, western Yunnan's wartime role, rice-skin rolls, and roasted-pork rice noodles.

## Qualifications
The Catimor 7963 evaluation and altitude detail are Yu Wenjie's source-scoped tasting account. The city-walk and estate details are travel observations from one Songtsam itinerary, not a full Baoshan city or coffee-industry survey.

- Added Baoshan's role as a Nujiang gateway and travel-facing coffee-production site.
