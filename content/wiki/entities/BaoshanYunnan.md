# 保山 / Baoshan, Yunnan

## Overview
Baoshan / 保山 is a Yunnan coffee-producing area discussed in [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]].

## Current Profile
The source uses Baoshan as the earlier specialty-coffee comparison inside the Yunnan map. Its most concrete example is a high-altitude Catimor 7963 lot that Yu Wenjie describes as balanced, clean, sweet, and identifiable.

## Key Characteristics
- Treated as an earlier Yunnan specialty-coffee region.
- Used to show that traditional local varieties can produce high-quality cups under suitable conditions.
- Provides one of the episode's concrete high-altitude quality examples.

## Evidence
- Specialty history: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] contrasts Baoshan with Pu'er and Dehong by saying Baoshan moved toward specialty earlier.
- Traditional variety example: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] reports Yu Wenjie's praise for a Baoshan 1900-meter Catimor 7963 lot.
- High-altitude boundary: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] keeps altitude tied to frost, microclimate, variety, tree age, and processing rather than treating elevation as a single quality guarantee.

## Qualifications
The Catimor 7963 evaluation and altitude detail are Yu Wenjie's source-scoped tasting account.
