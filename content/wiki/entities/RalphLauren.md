# Ralph Lauren

Ralph Lauren is the fashion and lifestyle brand discussed in [[zhongguo-xiaofeizhe-daidong-lafu-laolun-zengzhang-donghang-youhua-jipiao-tuigaiqian-zhengce-1005631805]]. The [[ShengdongZaokafei|声动早咖啡]] episode says the company reported quarterly revenue near $2 billion and China sales growth above 40%, helping it beat analyst expectations.

The source treats Ralph Lauren as a [[MultiPriceBandLuxuryBranding]] case. Its brand can sell across multiple price points and a broad style range, which may make it more resilient when consumers cut non-essential spending. At the same time, the episode says Ralph Lauren plans to reduce supply to discount retailers and leave some lower-positioned full-price stores from the second half of fiscal 2027, turning growth into a [[LuxuryRetailChannelControl]] and [[LuxuryScarcityDiscipline]] problem.
