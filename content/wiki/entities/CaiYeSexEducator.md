# 蔡夜 / Cai Ye (sex educator)

## Overview
蔡夜 is presented in VOL.84 of [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] as a sex-health counselor and sex educator discussing comprehensive education, bodily boundaries, contraception, sexual pain, clinical shame, child safeguarding, and inclusion.

## Current Profile
Cai's educational frame begins from the gap between people's real questions and the trustworthy help available to them. She treats sex education as wider than anatomy or intercourse: it includes relationships, bodies, gender and identity, consent, preparation, contraception, infection prevention, clinical communication, and the ability to ask for help.

Her practical contributions connect felt discomfort with refusal rights, accurate information with lower anxiety, and inclusive education with recognition of people often omitted from public sexual-health discussion. The source also presents her as planning further child sex-education work centered on how parents understand and discuss sexuality.

## Key Characteristics
- Defines comprehensive sex education across bodies, relationships, identity, consent, health, and communication.
- Uses bodily discomfort as an accessible starting point for recognizing and stating boundaries.
- Connects condoms and lubrication with preparation, comfort, contraception, and infection-risk reduction.
- Explains intimate clinical questions and examinations to reduce shame and avoidance.
- Treats child education as parent learning and ordinary conversation, not only a warning lecture.
- Calls for older and disabled people's sexual needs to be recognized with dignity.
