# Flexcoat

Flexcoat is the fishing-rod epoxy business developed by [[RogerSeiders]], discussed in [[yeti-ron-and-ryan-seiders-how-two-brothers-turned-a-400-cooler-into-a-2-billion-brand-d7f8e0e8-51a5-4444-8c1d-f5c43c353285]] as the family business behind [[RoySeiders]] and [[RyanSeiders]]. The episode treats it as practical background for the brothers' later ability to understand serious outdoor users and physical-product quality.

## Key Points
- The product was a clear flexible epoxy for fishing rods.
- Flexcoat supported the Seiders family and exposed the brothers to a user community that cared about gear performance.
- The business helped Ryan's later [[WaterlooRods]] work feel natural and gave both brothers a small-manufacturing reference point before [[YETI]].
- Roger sold the business only after decades of operation.
