# Mariupol / 马里乌波尔

Mariupol / 马里乌波尔 enters the wiki through [[05-ta-laizi-maliwuboer-ruguo-ni-kanjian-guo-wo-ceng-jiandao-de-533375925]], where its current news visibility becomes the doorway into [[SheCameFromMariupol|《她来自马里乌波尔》]]. The episode describes the city as a formerly prosperous, multiethnic [[Ukraine|Ukrainian]] port with Greek, Italian, and other cultural traces, not only as a symbol of present war.

In the source, Mariupol matters because it is the place whose lost local world [[NataschaWodin|纳塔莎·沃丁]] tries to reconstruct. [[KonstantinMariupolGenealogist|康斯坦丁]]'s forum-based genealogical work turns the city from a public-news location into a family-memory archive linking [[YevgeniaSheCameFromMariupol|叶弗根尼亚]], [[LidiyaSheCameFromMariupol|莉蒂娅]], and their relatives to broader twentieth-century violence.
