# 陈球 / Chen Qiu (late Han)

[[ChenQiuLateHan|陈球]] was Yongle shaofu in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-918-shiqing-bu-shunli-shi-weishenme-yao-bizui-lh-tbohgo96qw8bjqu3x1yp6czv5|Hanji 918]], where he helps push [[LiuHeSituLateHan|刘郃]] into an anti-[[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] plot.

His argument frames inaction as a betrayal of imperial-clan duty and state responsibility. The episode treats Chen Qiu's pressure as morally understandable but operationally weak because it does not solve the secrecy problem that Liu He explicitly raises.
