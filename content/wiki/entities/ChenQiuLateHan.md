# 陈球 / Chen Qiu (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-901-jixingzi-de-ren-hui-shi-shenme-xiachang-lh1yfyjzqptxvtnv9taiqms9yjud|Hanji 901]] backfills Chen Qiu's earlier 172 CE court role. The source transcript writes him as 陈求, but the role matches the existing Chen Qiu branch: as tingwei, he is the first senior official to argue that [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦皇太后]] should be buried with [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] and continue to share the proper sacrificial status.

His argument says Dou helped [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] inherit the throne, should not be treated as the author of every Dou-family offense, and has a stronger ritual claim than [[FengGuirenLateHan|冯贵人]]. The source therefore gives Chen Qiu an early [[ImperialBurialLegitimacy|帝后合葬名分]] and [[LiAsPoliticalOrder|礼制政治秩序]] role before his later anti-eunuch plot in Hanji 918.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-913-kan-dong-saiweng-shima-wu-tou-rensheng-fuhuo-lkfbrwtgsjma85bzugqonzk3hy4g|Hanji 913]] gives Chen Qiu an earlier senior-office marker. The source says he is appointed taiwei from taichang in 178 CE and removed in November, before Qiao Xuan succeeds him the next month.

[[ChenQiuLateHan|陈球]] was Yongle shaofu in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-918-shiqing-bu-shunli-shi-weishenme-yao-bizui-lh-tbohgo96qw8bjqu3x1yp6czv5|Hanji 918]], where he helps push [[LiuHeSituLateHan|刘郃]] into an anti-[[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] plot.

His argument frames inaction as a betrayal of imperial-clan duty and state responsibility. The episode treats Chen Qiu's pressure as morally understandable but operationally weak because it does not solve the secrecy problem that Liu He explicitly raises.
