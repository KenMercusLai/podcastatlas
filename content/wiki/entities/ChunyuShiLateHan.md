## Overview

[[ChunyuShiLateHan|淳于式]] is the Kuaiji administrator who reports that Lu Xun's military levies burden local people and is then recommended by Lu Xun for his public concern.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1161 portrays Chunyu Shi as complaining to [[SunQuan|孙权]] that [[LuXunDongWu|陆逊]]'s requisitions harm local welfare. When Sun Quan raises the complaint, Lu Xun does not treat it solely as a personal attack: he acknowledges operational disruption, credits Chunyu Shi's contribution to mobilization, and argues that an official attentive to the population should be used rather than discarded.

## Key Characteristics

- Local administrator concerned with the civilian cost of military mobilization.
- Critic whose complaint may combine institutional responsibility with personal rivalry.
- Official whose useful public-welfare signal survives the interpersonal conflict.
- Beneficiary of an opponent's non-retaliatory personnel judgment.

## Evidence

Complaint and public-welfare concern:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1161-caocao-wei-shenme-yao-cisi-erxifu-lhv36g01hxpxaabh77bbyosdjzfr|Hanji 1161]] says Chunyu Shi reports that Lu Xun's levies burden Kuaiji residents.

Recognition by the criticized commander:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1161-caocao-wei-shenme-yao-cisi-erxifu-lhv36g01hxpxaabh77bbyosdjzfr|Hanji 1161]] says Lu Xun credits Chunyu Shi's assistance and recommends that Sun Quan promote him.

## Qualifications

The source does not establish Chunyu Shi's complete career, exact office, complaint text, personal motives, the measured civilian burden, or whether promotion followed. Treating him either as a jealous accuser or an unambiguous public-interest whistleblower would exceed the supplied evidence.

## What Changed

- Created a profile that preserves both the complaint's institutional value and uncertainty about motive.
- Made the civilian-cost side of Lu Xun's military expansion visible.

## Relationships

- [[LuXunDongWu|陆逊]] - criticized commander who nevertheless validates and recommends him.
- [[SunQuan|孙权]] - ruler who receives the complaint and asks Lu Xun to answer it.
- [[NonRetaliatoryAdversityConduct|逆境中的不迁怒]] - conduct pattern that lets criticism retain informational value despite conflict.
