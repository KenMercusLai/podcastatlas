# 王十朋 / Wang Shipeng

王十朋 / Wang Shipeng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-65-wo-chuanyue-qu-gei-langman-de-chuwang-nian-shou-shi-lvyd7pve-tfu4ixsqrbdvhm6g64j]] as the Song-dynasty poet whose seven-character quatrain about [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] closes the episode. The host uses the poem to shift from chronicle summary into elegiac reception of a ruler who died in Qin custody.

The source does not analyze Wang Shipeng as an independent literary figure. His role is receptional: later poetry helps frame Chu Huaiwang as tragic and remembered, not only as a ruler who was deceived by [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] and manipulated through [[ZhengXiu|郑袖]].
