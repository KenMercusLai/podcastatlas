# 韩桓惠王 / King Huanhui of Han

韩桓惠王 / King Huanhui of Han enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-83-zhuhou-bailan-jing-chengle-jingdian-ji-zhong-ji-ljp7nbqnrctr5mw6i9u982abmx-1]] as the ruler who succeeds [[HanXiwang|韩釐王]] after the [[HuayangBattle|华阳之战]] crisis. The episode keeps the succession notice brief, but its placement matters: Han has just survived a Wei-Zhao attack only because [[QinState|秦国]] chose to intervene.

The episode says Han Huanhui Wang's first major direction is to mobilize with [[WeiState|魏国]] under the shadow of [[BaiQi|白起]]'s coming attack on [[ChuState|楚国]]. That puts him inside [[WarringStatesAllianceSwitching|战国同盟转向]] immediately: the same Han that had just asked Qin to rescue it now stands in a Qin-led order where Han and Wei both behave like subordinate partners.
