# Ayman Khoury

## Overview

Ayman Khoury is the father prosecuted in [[USVKhoury|U.S. v. Khoury]] after paying Georgetown tennis coach [[GordonErnst]] in connection with his daughter [[CatherineKhoury]]'s admission.

## Current Profile

Part 1 reports that Khoury rejected a plea because he viewed the payment as later generosity rather than an advance bribe, while [[TimothyDonovan]] described an agreed $200,000 exchange and $180,000 transfer. Part 2 reports that Catherine's testimony and messages undercut the alleged false-injury plan, and the jury acquitted him on every count.

## Key Characteristics

- Made the admitted payment at the center of the prosecution; the summaries distinguish a $200,000 alleged agreement from $180,000 delivered.
- Rejected a plea and chose trial while maintaining that the payment followed the relevant events.
- Had taught Catherine tennis from childhood and served as her primary coach.
- Became estranged from Catherine after the breakup of her parents' marriage.
- Was acquitted on every count after a defense closing centered on Catherine's messages.

## Qualifications

The acquittal does not make the payment undisputedly proper or establish every motive. The bounded sources reflect a defense-centered episode summary rather than a complete trial record or independent biography; Part 1 calls him Amon, while Part 2 and the canonical page use Ayman.
