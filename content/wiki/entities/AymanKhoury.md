# Ayman Khoury

## Overview

Ayman Khoury is the father prosecuted in [[USVKhoury|U.S. v. Khoury]] after paying Georgetown tennis coach Gordie Ernst $180,000 in connection with his daughter [[CatherineKhoury]]'s admission.

## Current Profile

The episode reports that Khoury admitted the payment but denied the government's criminal theory. Catherine's testimony and messages undercut the alleged plan to use a false injury as an excuse for not playing, and the jury acquitted him on every count.

## Key Characteristics

- Made the admitted $180,000 payment at the center of the prosecution.
- Had taught Catherine tennis from childhood and served as her primary coach.
- Became estranged from Catherine after the breakup of her parents' marriage.
- Was acquitted on every count after a defense closing centered on Catherine's messages.
