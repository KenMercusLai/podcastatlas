# 齐襄王 / King Xiang of Qi

齐襄王 / King Xiang of Qi is the ruler title taken by [[TianFazhang|田法章]] in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-73-lishishang-ziyou-lianai-de-kaichuangzhe-shi-shui-lkumfsfialqgdl6qpb3c-hnz7h9u]]. After [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]] is killed and [[QiState|齐国]] is mostly occupied, Qi ministers in [[JuWarringStates|莒城]] search for the surviving royal son and install Tian Fazhang there.

The page separates the regnal identity from the existing Tian Fazhang page. In this source, Qi Xiang Wang matters because the same person moves from hidden servant to restored ruler, turning personal survival into a public claim that Qi is not fully extinguished.

His first kingly act in the episode is also domestic and symbolic: he brings [[JunWangHou|君王后]] from [[TaishiJiao|太史角]]'s household and makes her queen, preserving the crisis relationship formed before restoration.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-79-shishang-yong-gengniu-da-shengzhang-diyi-ren-lsrl0posblloqwugox160xsicp2p]] moves his restoration from declaration to capital return. After [[TianDan|田单]] defeats [[QiJie|骑劫]] and recovers the lost cities, Tian Dan goes to Ju, escorts Qi Xiang Wang back to [[Linzi|临淄]], and is rewarded as Anping Jun.

The same source also revisits Qi Xiang Wang's household by saying [[JunWangHou|君王后]] later gives birth to crown prince Tian Jian. The wiki keeps that as a source-scoped succession note until a later source develops Tian Jian's reign.
