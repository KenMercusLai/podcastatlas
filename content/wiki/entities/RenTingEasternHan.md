## Overview

任廷 / Ren Ting is synthesized as an Eastern Han official whose profile rests on one sharp appointment exchange with [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-714-shi-shui-di-yi-ge-han-chu-nan-er-dang-ma-ge-guo-shi-lpkqxrl3dsok4ui0lij4c3x8zfpi|Hanji 714]] says Liu Xiu appoints him Wuwei governor and urges him to serve superiors carefully, while Ren Ting answers that true integrity lies in keeping public duty and law rather than maintaining surface harmony.

## Current Profile

Ren Ting appears in the Hexi and Liangzhou personnel branch after Dou Rong's Luoyang entry. Liu Xiu warns him not to lose popular support and to serve senior officials well. Ren Ting's answer refuses to equate official virtue with compliant harmony: loyal ministers may not always be agreeable, and people who are too agreeable may not be loyal.

His positive formula is "奉公守法" - serving public duty and observing law. The episode's significance is not that Ren Ting wins a policy debate at scale, but that Liu Xiu can accept a newly appointed official's blunt correction of the ruler's own framing.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han frontier-region official appointed as Wuwei governor.
- Blunt speaker who challenges the idea that harmony with superiors is the core of official virtue.
- Law-bound integrity model summarized by "奉公守法."
- Evidence for Liu Xiu's ability to accept uncomfortable but principled advice in this episode.

## Qualifications

The page rests on one appointment exchange. The source does not provide Ren Ting's earlier biography, later Wuwei administration, factional position, or whether his principle produced later conflict. The durable current claim is narrower: Ren Ting functions as a compact model of law-bound official integrity accepted by Liu Xiu.
