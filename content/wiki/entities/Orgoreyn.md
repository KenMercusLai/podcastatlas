# 欧格瑞恩 / Orgoreyn

欧格瑞恩 / Orgoreyn is the centralized, bureaucratic, and publicly orderly state contrasted with [[Karhide|卡海德 / Karhide]] in [[UrsulaKLeGuin|Le Guin]]'s [[TheLeftHandOfDarkness|《黑暗的左手》]]. In [[36-heian-de-zuoshou-liufang-yu-ai-de-weilai-wangshi-605254522]], it first appears attractive to [[GenlyAi|金利·艾 / Genly Ai]] because it offers order, comfort, and official welcome.

The episode then reverses that first impression. Orgoreyn censors information, stages political hospitality, uses secret-police pressure, and sends Genly to a voluntary farm where forced labor and drugs nearly kill him. [[Estraven|伊斯特拉凡 / Estraven]]'s rescue exposes the coercive structure beneath the surface of rational administration.
