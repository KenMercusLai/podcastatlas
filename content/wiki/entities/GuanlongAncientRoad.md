## Overview
关陇古道 / Guanlong Ancient Road enters the wiki as the old route system through which the [[LongshanGansuShaanxi|陇山 / 龙山]] crossing becomes politically and materially legible. In the source, it is both a fieldwork object and a state-infrastructure problem.

## Current Profile
The episode presents the Guanlong route as plural and changing rather than as one fixed road. Different periods used different passes and paths across the Longshan system, but the larger mountain-and-river structure made the corridor durable. [[YanHaijun|严海军 / 阎海军]]'s field account includes local fear of the route, searching near Malu for stone paving and old-road traces, and feeling the connection between physical ascent and historical movement.

The route's historical status changes with political scale. The source suggests ordinary interregional movement and archaeological connections preceded the Qin, but large organized crossings become more visible with Qin military movement. After Qin unification, the route becomes a state road carrying transport, documents, troop movement, and grain supply.

## Key Characteristics
- Old route system linking Guanzhong and Longyou across or around Longshan.
- Historically variable path rather than a single immutable line.
- Fieldwork object where stone road traces, passes, and local memory help ground historical geography.
- Route whose role expands from local passage to imperial infrastructure under Qin and later states.
- Material frame for understanding how ancient campaigns depended on walking, pack movement, and difficult mountain terrain.

## Qualifications
The source does not produce a definitive map of every Guanlong route or settle which path dominated in each dynasty. Zhangmianyi, Zhang Qian-related place memory, Malu traces, and village anecdotes remain source-scoped until corroborated by additional notes.
