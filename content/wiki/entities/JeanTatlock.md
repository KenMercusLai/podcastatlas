# Jean Tatlock

## Overview

Jean Tatlock was a medical student, Communist Party member, and major romantic partner of [[JRobertOppenheimer|J. Robert Oppenheimer]] whose political commitments and continued contact with him became part of his wartime security vulnerability.

## Current Profile

The episode treats Tatlock as more than a name in a security file. She is presented as a formative intellectual and emotional influence who helped connect Oppenheimer to communist circles, antifascist causes, and political engagement after an unusually sheltered early life.

That personal significance later collided with state secrecy. Oppenheimer continued seeing her while under surveillance during the [[ManhattanProject|Manhattan Project]], although the episode is uncertain whether the relationship remained romantic at that stage.

## Key Characteristics

- Medical student and committed Communist Party member.
- Central romantic and intellectual relationship in Oppenheimer's adult life.
- Influence on his movement toward left-wing politics and humanitarian causes.
- Security-relevant contact whose later relationship with Oppenheimer remains partly uncertain.

## Evidence

- Political and personal influence: [[343-oppenheimer-the-father-of-the-atom-bomb-part-1-glt9202576882]] describes Tatlock as Oppenheimer's great love and a major influence on his political awakening.
- Communist affiliation: [[343-oppenheimer-the-father-of-the-atom-bomb-part-1-glt9202576882]] identifies her as a regular party participant rather than merely a fellow traveler.
- Wartime contact: [[343-oppenheimer-the-father-of-the-atom-bomb-part-1-glt9202576882]] notes that monitored meetings continued during the project while qualifying their romantic status.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded by an Oppenheimer-centered narrative and does not provide a complete independent biography of Tatlock, her medical work, political reasoning, or mental health. Her importance should not be reduced to the later use of her association against Oppenheimer.
