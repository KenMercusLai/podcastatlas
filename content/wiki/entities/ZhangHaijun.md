# Zhang Haijun / 张海军

Zhang Haijun / 张海军 is the guest in [[huawei-de-tao-dinglv-shi-chuangxin-haishi-xuetou-bonus-e471f937-616b-4f49-a7ae-49137d32dbe5]], where he is introduced as a former senior engineer at [[Synopsys]] and the author behind the public account "福利业的猫." In the episode, he explains [[Huawei]]'s [[TauLaw]] from a semiconductor-engineering and [[ElectronicDesignAutomation|EDA]] perspective.

His key contribution is the distinction between ordinary die-to-die [[Semiconductor3DStacking]] and the more ambitious [[CellToCellLogicStacking]] implied by Huawei's logic-folding language. Zhang treats Tau Law as meaningful but not proven: the metric shift toward system time is reasonable, while the cell-level implementation still needs new tools, packaging capability, and product evidence.
