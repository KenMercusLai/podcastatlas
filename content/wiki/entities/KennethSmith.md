# Kenneth Smith

## Overview

Kenneth “Kenny” Smith is presented as one of two men convicted of killing Elizabeth Sennett and as a central subject of [[TheAlabamaMurders]].

## Current Profile

The source uses Smith's case to expose the gap between controlled execution protocols and lived procedure. Alabama's unsuccessful attempt to establish intravenous access occurred outside ordinary clinical conditions and largely outside witness view; his case later became central to the state's use of nitrogen hypoxia. The page records that institutional role without attempting a complete biography or case adjudication.
