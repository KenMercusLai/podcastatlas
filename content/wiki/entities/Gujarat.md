# Gujarat

Gujarat appears in [[settler-bet-a-west-bank-flashpoint-6a842bb539e73ea8f3c13a2d]] as one of the [[BharatiyaJanataParty|BJP]]-ruled states that has passed a version of [[UniformCivilCodeIndia|uniform civil-code]] legislation. The source groups it with [[Assam]] and [[MadhyaPradesh]] as evidence that the model is moving beyond [[Uttarakhand]].

The episode does not use Gujarat as a detailed legal case. Its significance is that the civil-code push is becoming a state-by-state political project that tests equality rhetoric, minority family law, and [[MajoritarianLegalUniformity]].
