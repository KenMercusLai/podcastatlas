# Mr. Game Show Florida

Mr. Game Show Florida is the live game-show events business founded by [[AndyJeremiah]] and [[TracyJeremiah]] in [[advice-line-with-curt-richardson-of-otterbox-e94bf774-de11-4434-843b-a551309a6773]]. The company runs events such as music bingo and game-show formats for gated communities, with the source reporting about 10 events per month, just over $50,000 in recent annual revenue, and a starting price of $350 for a 90-minute event.

The Advice Line question is whether the business can scale nationally by providing content, kits, training, or subscriptions to other local hosts. [[GuyRaz]] and [[CurtRichardson]] treat the first validation step as a [[FounderLedServiceReplication]] test: train a nearby host, observe the event quality, and find out whether customers are equally happy when Andy and Tracy are not personally leading the show.
