# Fujian / 福建

[[ep260-yikou-feimei-zhifang-cangzhe-zhongguo-yangzhi-de-xin-gushi-linjq2fkzhqvzjcgrmhudq12vs4j]] adds Fujian's eel-buying-advice branch. The episode says consumers comparing ready-to-eat grilled eel should notice that [[Fujian|福建]] is more associated with American eel while [[Guangdong|广东]] is more associated with Japanese eel, making origin a practical clue inside [[PreparedHighValueFoodFormats|prepared high-value food formats]].

[[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw]] adds Fujian's footwear-manufacturing and succession branch through [[Putian|莆田]] and [[WoteShoes|沃特鞋企]]. The episode uses a Putian shoe-family case to show how a successor can inherit a business shaped by OEM production, failed brand scaling, inventory complexity, e-commerce competition, and renewed overseas-channel experiments.

[[ep249-minnan-gucheng-youshenji-chuantong-yishi-beihou-de-xiangtu-wangluo-lpk1vb9-zmgmuous1nyzefgapgww]] adds Fujian's Minnan ritual and folk-religion branch through [[YongningAncientCity|永宁古城]]. The episode connects deity processions, ancestor worship, lineage halls, [[JitongSpiritMedium|鸡童/乩童]], [[Chenghuang|城隍]] temple politics, and tourism pressure into [[MinnanRitualNetwork|闽南仪式网络]].

[[ep245-yinian-yidu-xunwei-zhilv-jinnian-qu-chi-chuangyi-cai-li9wtavemokcckialbbj7unpmqsz]] adds Fujian as a regional-cuisine contrast case. The source says Fujian cuisine carries historical prestige and burden through dishes and settings such as 佛跳墙, 红蟳米糕, 官府菜, and 国宴菜, so its innovation context differs from less codified regional cuisines. It also notes a local bistro path from 烧酒配 and cold dishes toward hotter food and stronger drink pairing.

Fujian / 福建 enters the wiki through [[150-zhongguo-ruhe-tiaochu-pinkun-xianjing-xiandaihua-zhi-lu-you-hui-zouxiang-hefang-lgpvp04rngagkpubte2ac6i5ji3g]] as one of the coastal regional cases in [[YuenYuenAng|洪源远]]'s development frame. The episode treats Fujian as a place where clan ties, hometown trust, and overseas Chinese relationships helped attract early investment before formal property-rights institutions were fully mature.

The province matters as an example of [[InformalPropertyRightsProtection]]. The source's point is not that informal networks are ideal, but that they can sometimes create enough trust to begin market formation before stronger professional bureaucracy and formal rules catch up.
