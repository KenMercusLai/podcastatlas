# Fujian / 福建

Fujian / 福建 enters the wiki through [[150-zhongguo-ruhe-tiaochu-pinkun-xianjing-xiandaihua-zhi-lu-you-hui-zouxiang-hefang-lgpvp04rngagkpubte2ac6i5ji3g]] as one of the coastal regional cases in [[YuenYuenAng|洪源远]]'s development frame. The episode treats Fujian as a place where clan ties, hometown trust, and overseas Chinese relationships helped attract early investment before formal property-rights institutions were fully mature.

The province matters as an example of [[InformalPropertyRightsProtection]]. The source's point is not that informal networks are ideal, but that they can sometimes create enough trust to begin market formation before stronger professional bureaucracy and formal rules catch up.
