## Overview

王闳 / Wang Hong is a source-bounded Eastern Han transition official whose current wiki role is the Langya confrontation in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-682-jiemi-gudai-dihuang-de-yongrenzhidao-lk1eq46vftaul1hazk2ggptrtvdv|Hanji 682]]. Appointed Langya governor by Liu Xuan but blocked by [[ZhangBuEasternHan|张步]], Wang Hong turns a losing military contest into a mandate-backed personal confrontation that makes Zhang Bu apologize and hand over commandery affairs.

## Current Profile

Hanji 682 gives Wang Hong a compact but high-signal profile. Liu Xuan appoints him Langya governor, but Zhang Bu occupies Langya first and refuses to let him govern. Wang Hong persuades or attracts Gan-yu and five other counties, then loses in direct fighting against Zhang Bu.

Unable to match Zhang Bu by force, Wang Hong goes to Zhang Bu in person. Zhang Bu receives him with troops arrayed and angrily asks why Wang Hong attacks him. Wang Hong answers while holding his sword hilt: he came by court order, Zhang Bu resists with troops, and therefore Wang Hong is attacking bandits. Zhang Bu recognizes the loss of normative position, kneels to apologize, entertains Wang Hong as an honored guest, and gives him commandery affairs.

## Key Characteristics

- Liu Xuan-appointed Langya governor whose formal mandate initially lacks military control.
- Local official who gains six counties but cannot defeat Zhang Bu through force alone.
- High-risk negotiator who personally enters an adversary's camp after military weakness becomes clear.
- Mandate-backed speaker who converts Zhang Bu's armed refusal into a legitimacy problem.
- Temporary Langya settlement actor whose success does not yet prove durable Zhang Bu submission.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 682. It does not reconstruct Wang Hong's full biography, exact office history, or later fate. The Langya settlement should not be overread as lasting submission by Zhang Bu, because later Zhang Bu pages show renewed title politics, battlefield defeat, controlled surrender, and eventual execution. This 王闳 is kept distinct from the later [[WangHongLateHan|王宏 / Wang Hong]] page.
