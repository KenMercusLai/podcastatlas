# Alabama

## Overview

Alabama is a U.S. state represented here through two distinct institutional branches: attorney-general litigation over [[Roblox]] child safety and [[TheAlabamaSolution|a documentary investigation]] of its prison system.

## Current Profile

The platform branch places Alabama with [[Nevada]] and [[WestVirginia|West Virginia]] in a reported $36 million Roblox settlement cluster over accusations that the service failed to protect minors from predatory behavior. It connects state enforcement to age estimation, communication limits, privacy, and speech-access tradeoffs.

The prison branch is substantially more severe and contested. [[AndrewJarecki]] describes a system marked by managed access, violence, deaths, low parole, compelled labor, retaliation, and official denial of systemic failure. Incarcerated participants' prohibited-phone recordings become the main alternative to institutional visibility, while the labor account raises a possible conflict between community-work trust and release.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses state attorney-general authority in technology-platform child-safety litigation.
- Operates a prison system the documentary source describes as unusually closed and violent.
- Combines very low parole with economically valuable incarcerated labor in the episode's account.
- Is represented through sharply different evidence types: a short technology-news report and a filmmaker interview.
