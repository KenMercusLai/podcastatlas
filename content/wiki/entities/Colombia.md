# Colombia

[[afghanistan-five-years-on-our-correspondent-visits-6a7ee547049c692a6b3ee0a9]] adds Colombia as an earthquake-response and [[DisasterInequality]] case. The source says a 7.4-magnitude earthquake struck four days earlier, killing hundreds and leaving many missing, with response conditions sharply worse in poorer, more remote Choco and Quibdo than in better-served areas such as Cali and the coffee region.

The earthquake gives [[AbelardoDeLaEspriella]] an immediate governing test. Having been sworn in three days before the disaster, he is presented as personally commanding the response, while geography, landslides, weak hospitals, volunteer rescue gaps and armed-group pressure make the event a [[DisasterResponseStateCapacity]] problem rather than only a natural shock.

Colombia is the country case in [[starmergeddon-british-pm-resigns-6a390b674a8189f2c3b4e6f1]], where [[TheIntelligence]] presents [[AbelardoDeLaEspriella]]'s narrow presidential runoff victory as a reaction against worsening security under [[GustavoPetro]].

The source frames Colombia through [[SecurityBacklashPolitics]]. Voters punish Petro's "Total Peace" strategy because crime, gang control, cocaine production, and armed-group territorial power are described as worsening. The episode says de la Espriella can promise a tougher line, but legally protected spending, weak congressional support, and rule-of-law risks limit what a hardline turn can safely accomplish.
