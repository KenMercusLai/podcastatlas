# 赵靖侯 / Marquis Jing of Zhao

赵靖侯 / Marquis Jing of Zhao appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-2-lvaqlgyccrugfo0l-wuwf1mt1kcm]] as [[ZhaoLiehou|赵烈侯]]'s son Zhao Zhang, whom Zhao people install after [[ZhaoWuhou|赵武侯]] dies in 387 BCE. The episode notes that [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] gives him little direct biography.

The host brings him back through [[HanFei|韩非]]. In that reading, Zhao Jinghou is indulgent, violent, and personally ugly, yet Han Fei uses him to argue that a ruler who can use people and detect truth or falsehood in ministers' words can still keep a state standing. That makes Zhao Jinghou the source's concrete case for [[LegalistRulerTechnique]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl]] names Zhao Jinghou in the new-generation ruler setting around the early Warring States melee. Because the transcript later varies Zhao's ruler name in the war narrative, the war events are recorded mainly on [[ZhaoState|赵国]] rather than treated as new Zhao Jinghou biography.
