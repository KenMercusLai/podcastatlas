## Overview

Zhang Yu / 张瑜 enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-549-ruguo-youren-ma-bifu-ni-zhidao-sha-yisi-bu-lskfkw_udnitdfzzg1lw8b1ch3si|Hanji 549]] as [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s teacher and the named target of [[ZhuYunWesternHan|朱云]]'s public accusation. The episode uses him less as a full biography than as the protected figure whose status makes Zhu Yun's direct speech explosive.

## Current Profile

The current profile is deliberately narrow. Zhang Yu is presented as Chengdi's teacher and as the person Zhu Yun identifies after being pressed to name the `佞臣` he wants to execute with the imperial sword. Because Zhang Yu is tied to Chengdi personally, Zhu Yun's accusation touches both court hierarchy and imperial emotion.

The episode does not independently establish Zhang Yu's broader policy role or guilt. Its narrative function is to show how direct criticism becomes dangerous when it names a figure protected by personal trust, rank, and teacher-student intimacy.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han court figure identified as Han Chengdi's teacher.
- Protected target whose naming turns Zhu Yun's moral critique into a palace crisis.
- Embodies the episode's risk boundary between abstract criticism of bad ministers and direct accusation against a ruler's intimate authority figure.
