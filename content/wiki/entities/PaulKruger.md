# Paul Kruger

## Overview

Paul Kruger was the Boer president whose Transvaal government confronted British pressure and declared war at the opening of the [[BoerWar]].

## Current Profile

[[241-young-churchill-prisoner-and-fugitive-part-3-glt9087091726]] presents Kruger as the political leader of a republic under growing military and imperial pressure. His government moved first in the formal sequence by declaring war, but the episode embeds that act in a longer struggle over outlander rights, gold, British troop concentration, the Jameson Raid, and the survival of Boer independence.

## Key Characteristics

- President and public representative of the Boer Transvaal.
- Defender of an independent Boer republic against British expansion.
- Led a government criticized for excluding English-speaking outlanders politically.
- Declared war after Britain concentrated forces near the Transvaal.

## Evidence

- Political grievance: [[241-young-churchill-prisoner-and-fugitive-part-3-glt9087091726]] names outlander exclusion as one British complaint against Kruger's government.
- Escalation sequence: [[241-young-churchill-prisoner-and-fugitive-part-3-glt9087091726]] says British forces massed near the Transvaal before Kruger and the Boers declared war.
- Independence context: [[241-young-churchill-prisoner-and-fugitive-part-3-glt9087091726]] places Kruger's republic against Rhodes's territorial ambition and British interest in the Rand.
