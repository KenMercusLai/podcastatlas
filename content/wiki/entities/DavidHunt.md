# David Hunt

## Overview

David Hunt is an Australian historian, broadcaster, and podcaster who appears on [[TheRestIsHistory]] to discuss Australia before British colonization.

## Current Profile

Hunt uses comedy and evidentiary caution to replace a Cook-centered national origin story with a longer account of [[AboriginalAustralians|Aboriginal Australian]] deep history, environmental debate, oral traditions, trade, and pre-1770 maritime contact. He treats [[JamesCook]] as an exceptional navigator without accepting the claim that Cook simply “discovered” Australia.

His account repeatedly separates secure evidence from attractive speculation. Archaeological and genetic ranges remain distinct from creation traditions; megafauna extinction remains multicausal and contested; Makassan exchange is better supported than Chinese-fleet or Portuguese east-coast claims; and British settlement appears contingent on penal and imperial circumstances after the American Revolution.

## Key Characteristics

- Australian public historian working across broadcasting and podcasting.
- Comic storyteller who uses disputed monuments and anecdotes to open source-critical questions.
- Advocate of a deep-time frame for Australian history before European arrival.
- Careful separator of navigational achievement from discovery mythology.
- Interpreter who keeps uncertainty visible in archaeology, oral memory, extinction, and early-contact claims.

## Qualifications

This profile derives from one popular-history appearance rather than Hunt's full body of work. Dates, population estimates, migration models, oral-memory interpretations, artifact claims, and colonization counterfactuals retain the source's stated uncertainty and have not been independently adjudicated here.
