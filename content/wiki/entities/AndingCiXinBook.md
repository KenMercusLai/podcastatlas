# 《安定此心》

## Overview
《安定此心》 is [[JiangTaoPsychiatrist|姜涛]]'s book discussed in [[shi-di-fu-shuo-466-jiangtao-5wan-jingshenke-yisheng-dui-1yi-jingshen-jibing-huanzhe-lpdsk96akpwtccxyesjdgtw8skbn]]. In this source, the book anchors a public conversation about psychiatric illness, clinical experience, adolescent distress, treatment boundaries, patient-family support, and a calmer attitude toward mental-health problems.

## Current Profile
The episode presents the book less as a literary object than as a vehicle for destigmatizing psychiatric care. Jiang Tao says he wants readers to see real illness and recovery processes, to avoid panic, contempt, overinterpretation, and excessive intervention, and to treat psychological and psychiatric problems with understanding, patience, and regular help-seeking.

The book also supplies the episode's tone. "安定" points both to [[BeijingAndingHospital|北京市安定医院]] and to a posture of steadiness: mental illness should be faced directly, but neither mystified nor used as a fashionable label.

## Key Characteristics
- Psychiatry-facing book by [[JiangTaoPsychiatrist|姜涛]] discussed through his long clinical experience.
- Uses clinical stories to reduce fear and mystery around mental illness.
- Frames mental-health response as understanding, acceptance, patience, and regular treatment.
- Opposes both stigma and casual overdiagnosis.
- Links the institutional name "安定" to a broader posture of steadiness toward illness and care.
