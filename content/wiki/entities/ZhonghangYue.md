## Overview

中行说 / Zhonghang Yue is the probable historical identity behind the transcript form “钟杭越” in Hanji 217: a Han eunuch reportedly compelled to accompany a wengzhu marriage mission, who defects to [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] and becomes an adviser on material dependence, administration, diplomatic form, and pressure against Han.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-217-kan-fuhei-hanwendi-ruhe-wansi-yimudi-liuchang-2-ljmhwnxkjcegqfevo8hmvoblrcye|Hanji 217]] presents coercion as the immediate background to defection. The envoy resists the dangerous assignment, warns that he will harm Han if forced to go, and then reportedly enters the chanyu's service. That sequence can explain grievance inside the episode's narrative, but it does not independently establish the full motive, chronology, or voluntariness of every later act.

His advice has two connected layers. First, he treats taste and consumption as strategic: if Xiongnu elites become dependent on Han silk and food, a larger agrarian state may gain influence at low cost. Demonstrations comparing silk with felt and fur, and Han food with milk products, therefore defend ecological fit, autonomy, and military readiness rather than rejecting exchange in the abstract. Second, he transfers institutional knowledge by encouraging records, counts of people and livestock, larger tablets and seals, stronger document formulas, and calibrated threats toward Han envoys.

The episode's harsh “汉奸,” “帝师,” and “国师” labels are judgments rather than offices established by the supplied evidence. The durable profile is an insider defector who converts familiarity with Han goods, diplomatic conventions, and political vulnerabilities into an opponent's strategic capacity.

## Key Characteristics

- Han eunuch assigned against his wishes to accompany a wengzhu marriage mission.
- Probable normalization of the transcript form “钟杭越,” with the identity kept explicitly qualified.
- Defector whose grievance is presented as arising from a coerced high-risk assignment.
- Adviser who links imported consumption to dependence, weakened custom, and reduced fighting capacity.
- Administrative knowledge-transfer figure associated with record keeping and population-livestock counts.
- Diplomatic strategist who uses document size, seals, headings, and threats to project parity or superiority toward Han.

## Qualifications

The supplied document is a structured episode summary rather than a transcript or primary-text edition. Its identification of “钟杭越” as Zhonghang Yue is contextual inference; exact name forms, status, mission role, dialogue, demonstrations, administrative innovations, influence across later chanyu reigns, and official standing require comparison with transmitted histories. Coercion is relevant context but does not erase agency in the reported defection or prove a single sufficient motive. The episode's moral label “汉奸” and grand titles should not be mistaken for neutral institutional classifications.
