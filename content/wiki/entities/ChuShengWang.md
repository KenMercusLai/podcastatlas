# 楚声王 / King Sheng of Chu

楚声王 / King Sheng of Chu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-2-lmfzo4sl9nd5kz3mrxdl1dl9efei]] as a briefly recorded [[ChuState|楚国]] ruler killed by "bandits." The episode says he is not developed much in the surviving record, then uses his death to ask whether "bandits" sometimes covers political manipulation by people near power.

The source does not assert a settled alternative culprit for Chu Sheng Wang's death. It uses the case to motivate [[BanditAssassinationAttribution]] and to compare other examples such as [[ZhengWenGong|郑文公]] and [[JinYouGong|晋幽公]].
