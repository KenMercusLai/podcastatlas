## Overview

臧儿 / Zang Er is [[EmpressDowagerWangWesternHan|王娡]]'s mother and [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s maternal grandmother. The current wiki evidence presents her narrowly as the family strategist who responds to a status prediction by removing Wang from a first marriage and obtaining entry to Crown Prince Liu Qi's household.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-238-qiguo-zhiluan-huainanwang-liuan-ruhe-zhuanweiweian-2-lpik9exsirvyrppu8c9i89iqjncr|Hanji 238 part 2]] says Zang Er descends from a fallen elite family, first marries Wang Zhong and later remarries into the Tian family. After a physiognomist predicts high status for her two daughters and an imperial son for Wang Zhi, she repeatedly invokes ancestral standing, removes Wang from Jin Wangsun's household, and uses personal connections to place her in Crown Prince Liu Qi's residence.

The episode makes Zang Er's conduct the initiating family gamble behind Wang Zhi's ascent and Liu Che's later birth. It does not establish her exact lineage, private beliefs, legal authority over the first marriage, the means of palace access, or whether Wang freely chose the transition.

## Key Characteristics

- Maternal strategist linking a declining household's status memory to a risky palace-entry plan.
- Treats physiognomic prediction as actionable information rather than passive destiny.
- Uses family persuasion and social connections to redirect Wang Zhi's marriage path.
- Extends the household strategy across both daughters when Wang Zhi's sister also enters the crown prince's residence.

## Qualifications

The profile rests on one short popular-history summary whose transcript writes 张儿 rather than 臧儿 and gives an uncertain form for her claimed royal ancestor. It does not independently establish the genealogy, marriages, children's full identities, physiognomist, prediction, dialogue, legal process, consent, palace-selection channel, or Zang Er's inner motive. Later outcomes cannot by themselves validate the omen or prove that one maternal plan caused Liu Che's accession.

## What Changed

- Created a bounded profile separating Zang Er's initiating family strategy from Wang Zhi's later court conduct.
