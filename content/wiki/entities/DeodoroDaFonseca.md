# Deodoro da Fonseca

## Overview

Deodoro da Fonseca appears in the episode as the marshal who led Brazil's republican transition and chose to keep the old anthem tune.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Deodoro less as a full political subject than as the republic's symbolic decision point. After the monarchy fell, the new regime held a contest for a replacement anthem, but Deodoro preferred [[FranciscoManuelDaSilva]]'s older tune. That choice shows how republican legitimacy could reuse inherited national emotion rather than discard every imperial-era symbol.

## Key Characteristics

- Military leader associated with Brazil's transition from empire to republic.
- Represents the army's role in the late-1880s turn against [[DomPedroII]].
- Presided over a new-anthem contest in the episode's account.
- Preferred the older anthem tune, helping preserve it as the national melody.
- Makes [[RegimeSymbolContinuity]] visible at the republic's founding moment.

## Evidence

- Republican transition: [[681-brazil-the-emperors-anthem-part-5-glt5596929385]] says public opinion turned against Pedro II and Brazil became a republic under Marshal Deodoro da Fonseca.
- Anthem decision: [[681-brazil-the-emperors-anthem-part-5-glt5596929385]] says the new regime held a contest for a new anthem, but Deodoro preferred the old one.
