# E. B. Hirschberg

## Overview

E. B. Hirschberg was an experimental chemist and engineer presented as Louis Fieser's principal collaborator in formulating napalm and turning it into a deployable incendiary device.

## Current Profile

[[may-the-best-firebomb-win-may-the-best-firebomb-win]] describes Hirschberg as experienced with explosives, fuses, poison gas, and incendiaries. Working with [[LouisFieser]], he tested gels and fuels until the pair found a gasoline mixture whose burning globules could adhere, disperse, and transfer heat effectively.

Hirschberg also designed the prototype delivery mechanism: TNT and white phosphorus inside a napalm-filled canister so an explosion would ignite and scatter the gel. His role joins experimental iteration to weapon engineering, although the source does not fully partition credit between him, Fieser, Hottel's program, industrial suppliers, and later military production teams.

## Key Characteristics

- Experimental chemist and engineer with prior weapons-related experience.
- Fieser's central collaborator in comparing gels, fuels, and consistency.
- Helped identify the napalm formulation based on naphthenate and palmitate thickeners.
- Designed an explosive mechanism to ignite and disperse the gel.
- Used recovered globule size and distribution as feedback for further refinement.
