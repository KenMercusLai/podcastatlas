# Leapmotor T03 / 零跑 T03

## Overview
Leapmotor T03 / 零跑 T03 is presented as the small EV that helped [[Leapmotor|零跑汽车]] move out of the S01 failure period and into a healthier sales loop.

## Current Profile
The source gives T03 a survival-and-recovery role. It began deliveries in 2020 and grew from hundreds to thousands of units per month, with the source saying it sold more than 40,000 units in 2021. In the company story, T03 mattered because it bought time, showed that the company could define a more saleable product, and created the conditions for the higher-impact C11 launch.

## Key Characteristics
- Post-S01 small EV that reopened Leapmotor's sales momentum.
- Began delivery in 2020 according to the source.
- Grew gradually from about 700 monthly units toward higher monthly volumes.
- Source-scoped 2021 sales exceeded 40,000 vehicles.
- Served as a bridge from survival to the later C11 turning point.
