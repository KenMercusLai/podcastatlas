# 《论语》 / Analects

## Overview
The Analects is a classical text associated with [[Confucius]]. These six podcast notes use selected sayings and comparisons, not a critical edition or a comprehensive summary of the canon.

## Current Profile
Short sayings become interpretive tools across appointment, political authority, moral character, humility, and self-command. The apparent common thread is disciplined conduct, but the modern historical applications belong to the hosts rather than to the original text's stated subject matter.

## Key Characteristics
- Its brief, often fragmentary form is contrasted with the longer connected arguments of the Mencius text.
- The North Star image supplies a metaphor for centered political authority.
- Trustworthiness and courage require moral direction rather than mere determination.
- Minor skills have value without necessarily qualifying as a central path to office or learning.
- Not boasting of virtue or effort offers an interpersonal self-cultivation standard.
- Not being ruled by drink extends restraint to ordinary social practice.

## Evidence
- **Form and comparison:** A host contrasts the compact, fragmentary Analects with the more continuous [[MenciusText]] while discussing [[Mengzi]]'s meeting with Wei Hui Wang. This is a reading impression, not a formal measure of textual difficulty. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-26-mengzi-laile-ln4twwp2frmr-xpumu0rqg-2gpyn]]
- **Centered rule:** The North Star passage is invoked to explain why a ruler remaining at the political center mattered in a podcast reading of [[QinShiHuang]]'s first post-unification journey from [[Xianyang]] through western territory. The proposed [[HighTempleRite]] context for that trip is the episode's inference; the Analects did not discuss Qin's itinerary. [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-121-yingzheng-zao-shijie-zuizao-gaosu-gonglu-2200nian-huangcao-busheng-ls1u8dmuvs5bbrzadf2dtd00js-p]]
- **Direction of 信 and 勇:** The line on “言必信，行必果” is brought into [[YeGong]]'s warning that [[BaiGongSheng]]'s resolve may become dangerous without ren and yi. The episode reads his subsequent rebellion through that ethical lens, without making the ancient saying a direct contemporary record of Bai. [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-2-zanmen-dou-yuanwang-le-beifu-2000-duonian-heiguo-de-yegong-haolong-lh-h-gezpqtvnyq40zlqac8s-5e]]
- **Small ways and appointments:** [[ZiXia]]'s “虽小道必有可观者焉” is applied to [[CaiYong]]'s criticism of Emperor Ling favoring calligraphy, painting, and literary fu as routes into serious office. The episode distinguishes appreciation of skill from treating it as the criterion for weighty appointments, amid competition with classical/ritual learning. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-908-nijing-chu-qiangzhe-xialu-chu-yongzhe-gudu-chu-zhizhe-lqih1dradhzi0mipxsaiedd52dlj]]
- **Virtue without self-advertisement:** [[YanHui]]'s “无伐善，无施劳” becomes the host's explanation for neither advertising virtue nor imposing one's labor on others. The episode connects this to [[ZengGuofan]] and to leaders avoiding jealousy when able subordinates receive credit; those management analogies are later uses of the line. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-994-genzhe-dongwu-zhongchen-lvfan-xuexi-zhichang-zhihui-lgqp2vqmh1mss96gcwmflp-ccpfu]]
- **Self-command around wine:** A cross-period alcohol episode invokes “不为酒困” alongside public service, family duty, and mourning to commend enjoyment without losing judgment. Its Li Qingzhao and Genghis Khan discussions provide wider context, not additional Analects passages. [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-3-liqingzhao-zhongnian-fuqi-libie-hejiu-jie-xiangsi-lnzxhrj2iulahccy2-pkm-wphdei]]

## Qualifications
All quotations and applications here are mediated by podcast summaries, not independently collated against editions of the Analects. The North Star/Qin, appointment/Cai Yong, and management/Sun Ce analogies must not be recast as original historical claims by Confucius.

## What Changed
- Distinct textual uses are compared without treating six episode references as a unified original argument.
- Historical and workplace analogies remain attributed to their presenters.
