# Ma Jianrong / 马建荣

## Overview

Ma Jianrong / 马建荣 is the Shenzhou successor and expansion leader in [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]]. The episode uses him to show how a second-generation operator can deepen an OEM business through environmental investment, major-customer trust, and vertical process integration.

## Current Profile

Ma Jianrong's key source moment is the 1997 management buyout and immediate decision to invest that year's profit into wastewater treatment. The episode frames that decision as early preparation for international brand compliance rather than a short-term cost.

He is also tied to Shenzhou's Uniqlo relationship. The source says Shenzhou fulfilled a demanding 20-day order for 350,000 shirts for the then less-famous Japanese customer, helping position the company for later growth and listing.

## Key Characteristics

- Ma represents the successor who turned Shenzhou from early export factory into a scaled international OEM supplier.
- He prioritized environmental compliance before it was widely understood internally as a customer-selection threshold.
- He used fast, reliable delivery to build trust with Uniqlo and other global customers.
- His operating route deepened OEM capabilities rather than shifting directly into consumer branding.
- The source presents Shenzhou under Ma as highly scaled but also dependent on a concentrated customer base.
