# 养生堂 / Yangshengtang

## Overview
养生堂 / Yangshengtang is the consumer-health company co-founded by [[ZhongShanshan|钟睒睒]] in 1993 and later part of the ownership structure connecting [[NongfuSpring|农夫山泉]] and [[WantaiBiological|万泰生物]].

## Current Profile
The episode treats Yangshengtang as both Zhong's first durable commercial success and a later control vehicle. Early products used simple benefit claims, emotional advertising, and event marketing to generate the cash and marketing capability that preceded Nongfu Spring; the broader group later held beverage and biotech assets.

## Key Characteristics
- Began as a health-products business after Zhong's earlier ventures failed.
- Used memorable advertising and event-led promotion to make unfamiliar products legible.
- Supplied capital and marketing experience for the move into packaged beverages.
- Became part of a concentrated cross-company ownership structure.

- Capability transfer: [[fengwei-chihe-nongfu-shanquan-zhongshanshan-ruhe-sandu-wending-zhongguo-shoufu-gkwrijiotjlral0wegtwycfd]] connects health-product marketing and cash generation to the later bottled-water entry.

## Qualifications
The episode does not independently verify early product efficacy, historical earnings, the full current group structure, or exact ownership percentages. Health-product claims should not be read as medical evidence.
