## Overview

叔孙无忌 / Shusun Wuji is a Taishan-area bandit leader whose current wiki role now has two bounded layers. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-851-hanhuandi-weihe-zhuding-yichou-wannian-ltgm1ix6cgqb-kqo9bztfrzjgeun|Hanji 851]] uses his raids into Xu and Yan territory as the disorder pretext by which [[ShanChaoLateHan|单超]] frames [[FifthZhongLateHan|第五种]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-854-lishi-gaosu-ni-xuanguan-yongguan-hen-zhongyao-lhin-iwk6y1xr8vvzdmnjch3s2um|Hanji 854]] then makes him the local violence trigger whose killing of Hou Zhang brings a central response under [[ZongZiLateHan|宗资]] and [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 851 presents Shusun Wuji as opportunistic local disorder spilling into Xu and Yan. The offices there are too weak to suppress him, and Shan Chao converts the disorder into a vague accusation against Fifth Zhong after Fifth Zhong's report against Shan Chao's family network. In this layer, Shusun Wuji matters less as an independent insurgent than as the pretext that lets protected power punish an inspector.

Hanji 854 gives the more direct suppression layer. Shusun Wuji attacks Taishan, kills commandery duwei Hou Zhang, and is defeated after the court sends Zong Zi. The episode's larger interest is state response quality: first suppression, then Huangfu Gui as a fitting pacification appointment.

## Key Characteristics

- Taishan-area bandit leader whose disorder spills into Xu and Yan in Hanji 851.
- Pretext figure used by Shan Chao to frame Fifth Zhong after an anti-corruption report.
- Violence trigger whose later attack kills a commandery military official.
- Source-bounded antagonist whose importance lies in testing state and court response.

## Qualifications

- Hanji 854 remains the direct Taishan attack, Hou Zhang death, Zong Zi suppression, and Huangfu Gui appointment layer.
