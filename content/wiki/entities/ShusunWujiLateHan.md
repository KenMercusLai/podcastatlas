## Overview
叔孙无忌 / Shusun Wuji is a Taishan bandit leader in Hanji 854. His role is narratively brief but structurally important: his attack and killing of the commandery duwei Hou Zhang trigger a central military response under Zong Zi and then Huangfu Gui's appointment to pacify Taishan.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-854-lishi-gaosu-ni-xuanguan-yongguan-hen-zhongyao-lhin-iwk6y1xr8vvzdmnjch3s2um|Hanji 854]] presents Shusun Wuji as a local violence trigger rather than a developed political actor. He attacks Taishan, kills Hou Zhang, and is defeated after the court sends Zong Zi. The episode's larger interest is how the state responds: first with suppression, then with Huangfu Gui as a fitting pacification appointment.

## Key Characteristics
- Taishan bandit leader whose violence escalates enough to kill a commandery military official.
- Trigger figure for a two-stage state response: Zong Zi's suppression and Huangfu Gui's pacification.
- Source-bounded antagonist with no current evidence for broader aims or organization.
