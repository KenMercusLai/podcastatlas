## Overview

郭芙 / Guo Fu is a [[ReturnOfTheCondorHeroes|《神雕侠侣》]] character whose pride, antagonism, recurring pleasure at seeing [[YangGuoJinYong|杨过]], and concern for both him and 耶律齐 make her romantic feeling real but difficult to rank or isolate.

## Current Profile

The episode treats “郭芙是否爱杨过” as an open textual problem rather than a binary answer. Her shyness at the proposed marriage, happiness at reunions, concern for his history, willingness to imagine dying for him, and confused fear during his rescue of 耶律齐 support attachment. Yet she also cares for her husband and has previously responded to the 武 brothers. The hosts therefore reject the inference that more than one attachment makes each false.

Her hostility is tied to upbringing, pride, poor expression, and repeated mutual misreading, though none of these erase the severe harms she causes. A second disputed reading concerns Yang Guo: his repeated restraint after his amputation and Xiaolongnü's further injury may indicate buried affection. More broadly, Guo Fu embodies wealth, beauty, family standing, and the social recognition denied to Yang Guo in childhood, so his need for her acknowledgment may exceed romance alone.

## Key Characteristics

- Moves repeatedly from pleasure at reunion to pride, competition, insult, and conflict.
- Shows evidence of attachment to Yang Guo without making him her sole emotional concern.
- Cares genuinely for 耶律齐 and acts decisively when his survival is threatened.
- Benefits from abundant familial and social love rather than treating one man as her only rescue.
- Represents status and recognition to the marginalized young Yang Guo.
- Causes grave harm whose explanation through upbringing or immaturity does not erase responsibility.

## Qualifications

The profile derives from one interpretive episode rather than an edition-specific textual collation. Whether Guo Fu loves Yang Guo, how that feeling ranks beside her marriage, whether Yang Guo loves her, and how much their hostility conceals attachment remain disputed. Multiple attachments need not be false, but the episode's “排序而无排他” proposition is not adopted as a universal law. Context does not excuse amputation or other harms.

## What Changed

- Established a profile that treats attachment as compatible with, but not superior to, other bonds.
- Added social recognition and class injury to the romance-versus-hostility reading.
- Preserved responsibility for severe harm alongside contextual explanation.
