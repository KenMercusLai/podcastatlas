## Overview

Hou Lan is a powerful late-Han eunuch whose wiki profile now starts with an earlier complaint-channel role against [[ChengJinLateHan|成瑨]] and [[LiuZhiTaiyuanLateHan|刘质]], continues through [[ZhangJianLateHan|张俭]]'s enforcement against his local abuses, appears in [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]]'s 168 CE anti-eunuch warning, drives a grievance-backed accusation chain against Zhang Jian in the 169 CE party-prohibition disaster, and ends with his later fall after office removal in 172 CE.

## Current Profile

The current evidence makes Hou Lan a two-sided example of eunuch power: he can turn local enforcement into danger for officials, and he is himself later exposed when court protection weakens. Hanji 871 supplies the earliest current enforcement-rebound layer. After [[ZhangFanWanLateHan|张汎]]'s wife petitions over the killing of Zhang Fan and his network, Hou Lan uses that grievance channel to accuse Cheng Jin and Liu Zhi. The point is not that Hou Lan personally witnessed the local cases; it is that palace-linked complaint access makes the enforcers vulnerable after they punish protected actors.

Hanji 872 gives the local-abuse and access-blocking layer around Hou Lan himself: Zhang Jian memorializes against Hou Lan's property seizure, lavish buildings, tomb construction, and household abuses, but Hou Lan intercepts the memorials before they reach [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-885-shengsi-guantou-ta-qingjia-xiuxi-luydnh9oqdyyfpbeztooi2e7lvca|Hanji 885]], Chen Fan names Hou Lan with [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] as part of the court danger that [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] and Chen Fan want [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦妙 / 窦皇太后]] to confront. The source does not make Hou Lan the operative countercoup actor; it places him in the broader eunuch-linked target field.

In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-893-rongyu-bang-ruhe-biancheng-siwang-mingdan-lmxzqoauf79-swwnk-0cevav0gq|Hanji 893]], Hou Lan's resentment against Zhang Jian moves through [[ZhuBiLateHan|朱璧]], who accuses Zhang and twenty-four others of forming a dangerous party. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-894-yi-chang-taowang-yinfa-de-da-tusha-lhulp9hqu07uayyfl3eg-xwrtkeu|Hanji 894]], that accusation pushes Zhang Jian into a flight that destroys many host households. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-900-zhichang-quanshu-dou-budong-gai-zenyang-shengcun-lmhjwosy4eswyhfppdghfsmfycqr|Hanji 900]], Hou Lan is stripped of office and seals and dies by suicide after charges of arrogance and extravagance. The combined evidence keeps him inside the same court ecology: eunuch-side accusation can generate terror for others, but palace access does not guarantee durable protection for the eunuch himself.

## Key Characteristics

- Powerful late-Han eunuch whose complaint channel helps punish officials who act against protected local offenders.
- Local-abuse figure whose own household and property practices are attacked by Zhang Jian before later retaliation.
- Access-controlling figure who can intercept memorials before they reach the ruler.
- Eunuch whose resentment against Zhang Jian triggers a lethal accusation and flight chain.
- Earlier named member of the eunuch-linked danger field in Chen Fan's anti-eunuch memorial.
- Patron-side figure whose intent is carried through Zhu Bi's local memorial.
- Negative figure in the party-prohibition field, where denunciation reaches beyond the accused person.

## Qualifications

Hanji 871 gives Hou Lan's complaint-channel role in the Cheng Jin and Liu Zhi case, while Hanji 872 gives the Zhang Jian enforcement and intercepted-memorial prelude. Hanji 893 supplies resentment and Zhu Bi's accusation, and Hanji 894 shows the flight consequence. The source chronology is kept bounded: Hanji 871-872 sit in the 166 CE Emperor Huan field, while the later accusation and flight pages are 169 CE under Emperor Ling. Hanji 885 names Hou Lan in the broad anti-eunuch target field but does not give him the operational role that Cao Jie, Wang Fu, and Zhu Yu receive in the adjacent coup pages. Hanji 900 is careful that the chronicle does not spell out the exact reporting path behind Hou Lan's fall. The host's internal-faction or scapegoating interpretation remains source-scoped.

## What Changed

- The current profile now starts with Hou Lan's complaint-channel role in the Cheng Jin and Liu Zhi case.
- The page separates two 166 CE layers: accusation against local enforcers and Zhang Jian's direct action against Hou Lan's own abuses.
- The later 169 CE Zhang Jian accusation now reads as retaliation after an earlier enforcement conflict, not Hou Lan's first appearance.
