# 侯览 / Hou Lan (late Han)

侯览 / Hou Lan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-900-zhichang-quanshu-dou-budong-gai-zenyang-shengcun-lmhjwosy4eswyhfppdghfsmfycqr|Hanji 900]] as a powerful late-Han eunuch whose fall interrupts the episode's Hu Guang survival frame. The source says he is stripped of office and seals after being charged with arrogance and extravagance, then dies by suicide.

The episode is careful that the chronicle does not spell out the exact reporting path. The host infers that Hou Lan may have crossed other eunuchs' interests or become costly enough to sacrifice, making his fall a source-scoped example of internal faction danger rather than a fully reconstructed case.

Hou Lan's role is therefore a negative counterpart to [[HuGuangLateHan|胡广]]. Where Hu Guang survives by remaining useful, cautious, and institutionally knowledgeable, Hou Lan shows how palace power can fail when [[PalaceAccessMonopoly|宫廷接触垄断]] no longer protects the actor. His page connects eunuch access to [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]] and [[PoliticalSurvivalFirst|安全第一政治生存]] without treating the host's motive reading as settled fact.
