## Overview

Hou Lan is a powerful late-Han eunuch whose wiki profile now includes [[ZhangJianLateHan|张俭]]'s earlier enforcement against his local abuses, his appearance in [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]]'s 168 CE anti-eunuch warning, his grievance-backed accusation chain against Zhang Jian in the 169 CE party-prohibition disaster, and his later fall after office removal in 172 CE.

## Current Profile

The current evidence makes Hou Lan a two-sided example of eunuch power. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] gives the local-abuse and access-blocking layer: Zhang Jian memorializes against Hou Lan's property seizure, lavish buildings, tomb construction, and household abuses, but Hou Lan intercepts the memorials before they reach [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-885-shengsi-guantou-ta-qingjia-xiuxi-luydnh9oqdyyfpbeztooi2e7lvca|Hanji 885]], Chen Fan names Hou Lan with [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] as part of the court danger that [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] and Chen Fan want [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦妙 / 窦皇太后]] to confront. The source does not make Hou Lan the operative countercoup actor; it places him in the broader eunuch-linked target field.

In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-893-rongyu-bang-ruhe-biancheng-siwang-mingdan-lmxzqoauf79-swwnk-0cevav0gq|Hanji 893]], Hou Lan's resentment against Zhang Jian moves through [[ZhuBiLateHan|朱璧]], who accuses Zhang and twenty-four others of forming a dangerous party. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-894-yi-chang-taowang-yinfa-de-da-tusha-lhulp9hqu07uayyfl3eg-xwrtkeu|Hanji 894]], that accusation pushes Zhang Jian into a flight that destroys many host households. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-900-zhichang-quanshu-dou-budong-gai-zenyang-shengcun-lmhjwosy4eswyhfppdghfsmfycqr|Hanji 900]], Hou Lan is stripped of office and seals and dies by suicide after charges of arrogance and extravagance. The combined evidence keeps him inside the same court ecology: eunuch-side accusation can generate terror for others, but palace access does not guarantee durable protection for the eunuch himself.

## Key Characteristics

- Powerful late-Han eunuch whose local abuses are attacked by Zhang Jian before later retaliation.
- Access-controlling figure who can intercept memorials before they reach the ruler.
- Eunuch whose resentment against Zhang Jian triggers a lethal accusation and flight chain.
- Earlier named member of the eunuch-linked danger field in Chen Fan's anti-eunuch memorial.
- Patron-side figure whose intent is carried through Zhu Bi's local memorial.
- Negative figure in the party-prohibition field, where denunciation reaches beyond the accused person.
- Later fallen court actor stripped of office and seals before suicide.

## Evidence

- Local-abuse prelude: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] says Zhang Jian memorializes against Hou Lan's seized houses and land, overbuilt mansions, tomb construction, and household abuses.
- Blocked memorials: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] says Hou Lan intercepts Zhang Jian's reports so they do not reach Emperor Huan.
- Earlier anti-eunuch target field: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-885-shengsi-guantou-ta-qingjia-xiuxi-luydnh9oqdyyfpbeztooi2e7lvca|Hanji 885]] says Chen Fan names Hou Lan and Cao Jie as dangerous eunuch-linked actors while pressing for action.
- Accusation construction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-893-rongyu-bang-ruhe-biancheng-siwang-mingdan-lmxzqoauf79-swwnk-0cevav0gq|Hanji 893]] says Hou Lan resents Zhang Jian and Zhu Bi accuses Zhang and twenty-four others under Hou Lan's influence.
- Accusation consequence: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-894-yi-chang-taowang-yinfa-de-da-tusha-lhulp9hqu07uayyfl3eg-xwrtkeu|Hanji 894]] says Zhang Jian is forced to flee after Hou Lan's accusation.
- Collateral consequence: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-894-yi-chang-taowang-yinfa-de-da-tusha-lhulp9hqu07uayyfl3eg-xwrtkeu|Hanji 894]] links Zhang Jian's resulting flight to the punishment of many sheltering households.
- Later fall: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-900-zhichang-quanshu-dou-budong-gai-zenyang-shengcun-lmhjwosy4eswyhfppdghfsmfycqr|Hanji 900]] says Hou Lan is stripped of office and seals after charges of arrogance and extravagance, then dies by suicide.
- Survival contrast: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-900-zhichang-quanshu-dou-budong-gai-zenyang-shengcun-lmhjwosy4eswyhfppdghfsmfycqr|Hanji 900]] contrasts Hou Lan with [[HuGuangLateHan|胡广]], whose usefulness, caution, and institutional knowledge preserve him longer.

## Qualifications

Hanji 872 gives the enforcement and intercepted-memorial prelude, while Hanji 893 supplies resentment and Zhu Bi's accusation and Hanji 894 shows the flight consequence. The source chronology is kept bounded: Hanji 872's supplied note dates its case to 166 CE, while the later accusation and flight pages are 169 CE. Hanji 885 names Hou Lan in the broad anti-eunuch target field but does not give him the operational role that Cao Jie, Wang Fu, and Zhu Yu receive in the adjacent coup pages. Hanji 900 is careful that the chronicle does not spell out the exact reporting path behind Hou Lan's fall. The host's internal-faction or scapegoating interpretation remains source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Hanji 872 adds Hou Lan's direct local-abuse and blocked-memorial prelude before the later Zhang Jian accusation chain.
- Hanji 885 still marks Hou Lan's appearance in Chen Fan's pre-coup warning field without making him a direct countercoup operator.
- Hanji 893 adds Zhu Bi as Hou Lan's local intermediary in the Zhang Jian accusation.
- Hanji 894 adds the collateral consequences of Hou Lan's accusation after Zhang Jian flees.
