# 侯览 / Hou Lan (late Han)

## Overview

Hou Lan is a powerful late-Han eunuch whose wiki profile now includes his grievance-backed accusation chain against [[ZhangJianLateHan|张俭]] in the 169 CE party-prohibition disaster and his later fall after office removal in 172 CE.

## Current Profile

The current evidence makes Hou Lan a two-sided example of eunuch power. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-893-rongyu-bang-ruhe-biancheng-siwang-mingdan-lmxzqoauf79-swwnk-0cevav0gq|Hanji 893]], Hou Lan's resentment against Zhang Jian moves through [[ZhuBiLateHan|朱璧]], who accuses Zhang and twenty-four others of forming a dangerous party. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-894-yi-chang-taowang-yinfa-de-da-tusha-lhulp9hqu07uayyfl3eg-xwrtkeu|Hanji 894]], that accusation pushes Zhang Jian into a flight that destroys many host households. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-900-zhichang-quanshu-dou-budong-gai-zenyang-shengcun-lmhjwosy4eswyhfppdghfsmfycqr|Hanji 900]], Hou Lan is stripped of office and seals and dies by suicide after charges of arrogance and extravagance. The combined evidence keeps him inside the same court ecology: eunuch-side accusation can generate terror for others, but palace access does not guarantee durable protection for the eunuch himself.

## Key Characteristics

- Powerful late-Han eunuch whose resentment against Zhang Jian triggers a lethal accusation and flight chain.
- Patron-side figure whose intent is carried through Zhu Bi's local memorial.
- Negative figure in the party-prohibition field, where denunciation reaches beyond the accused person.
- Later fallen court actor stripped of office and seals before suicide.
- Example of palace power's instability when protection channels or factional interests shift.

## Evidence

- Accusation construction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-893-rongyu-bang-ruhe-biancheng-siwang-mingdan-lmxzqoauf79-swwnk-0cevav0gq|Hanji 893]] says Hou Lan resents Zhang Jian and Zhu Bi accuses Zhang and twenty-four others under Hou Lan's influence.
- Accusation consequence: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-894-yi-chang-taowang-yinfa-de-da-tusha-lhulp9hqu07uayyfl3eg-xwrtkeu|Hanji 894]] says Zhang Jian is forced to flee after Hou Lan's accusation.
- Collateral consequence: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-894-yi-chang-taowang-yinfa-de-da-tusha-lhulp9hqu07uayyfl3eg-xwrtkeu|Hanji 894]] links Zhang Jian's resulting flight to the punishment of many sheltering households.
- Later fall: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-900-zhichang-quanshu-dou-budong-gai-zenyang-shengcun-lmhjwosy4eswyhfppdghfsmfycqr|Hanji 900]] says Hou Lan is stripped of office and seals after charges of arrogance and extravagance, then dies by suicide.
- Survival contrast: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-900-zhichang-quanshu-dou-budong-gai-zenyang-shengcun-lmhjwosy4eswyhfppdghfsmfycqr|Hanji 900]] contrasts Hou Lan with [[HuGuangLateHan|胡广]], whose usefulness, caution, and institutional knowledge preserve him longer.

## Qualifications

Hanji 900 is careful that the chronicle does not spell out the exact reporting path behind Hou Lan's fall. The host's internal-faction or scapegoating interpretation remains source-scoped. Hanji 893 supplies resentment and Zhu Bi's accusation as the Zhang Jian prelude, while Hanji 894 shows the flight consequence; neither source reconstructs Hou Lan's full factional network.

## What Changed

- Hanji 893 adds Zhu Bi as Hou Lan's local intermediary in the Zhang Jian accusation.
- Hanji 894 adds the collateral consequences of Hou Lan's accusation after Zhang Jian flees.
