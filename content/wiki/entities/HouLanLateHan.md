# 侯览 / Hou Lan (late Han)

## Overview

Hou Lan is a powerful late-Han eunuch whose wiki profile now includes both his role as [[ZhangJianLateHan|张俭]]'s accuser in the 169 CE party-prohibition flight and his later fall after office removal in 172 CE.

## Current Profile

The current evidence makes Hou Lan a two-sided example of eunuch power. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-894-yi-chang-taowang-yinfa-de-da-tusha-lhulp9hqu07uayyfl3eg-xwrtkeu|Hanji 894]], his accusation pushes Zhang Jian into a flight that destroys many host households. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-900-zhichang-quanshu-dou-budong-gai-zenyang-shengcun-lmhjwosy4eswyhfppdghfsmfycqr|Hanji 900]], he is stripped of office and seals and dies by suicide after charges of arrogance and extravagance. The paired evidence keeps him inside the same court ecology: eunuch-side accusation can generate terror for others, but palace access does not guarantee durable protection for the eunuch himself.

## Key Characteristics

- Powerful late-Han eunuch whose accusation against Zhang Jian triggers a lethal flight chain.
- Negative figure in the party-prohibition field, where denunciation reaches beyond the accused person.
- Later fallen court actor stripped of office and seals before suicide.
- Example of palace power's instability when protection channels or factional interests shift.

## Evidence

- Accusation power: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-894-yi-chang-taowang-yinfa-de-da-tusha-lhulp9hqu07uayyfl3eg-xwrtkeu|Hanji 894]] says Zhang Jian is forced to flee after Hou Lan's accusation.
- Collateral consequence: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-894-yi-chang-taowang-yinfa-de-da-tusha-lhulp9hqu07uayyfl3eg-xwrtkeu|Hanji 894]] links Zhang Jian's resulting flight to the punishment of many sheltering households.
- Later fall: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-900-zhichang-quanshu-dou-budong-gai-zenyang-shengcun-lmhjwosy4eswyhfppdghfsmfycqr|Hanji 900]] says Hou Lan is stripped of office and seals after charges of arrogance and extravagance, then dies by suicide.
- Survival contrast: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-900-zhichang-quanshu-dou-budong-gai-zenyang-shengcun-lmhjwosy4eswyhfppdghfsmfycqr|Hanji 900]] contrasts Hou Lan with [[HuGuangLateHan|胡广]], whose usefulness, caution, and institutional knowledge preserve him longer.

## Qualifications

Hanji 900 is careful that the chronicle does not spell out the exact reporting path behind Hou Lan's fall. The host's internal-faction or scapegoating interpretation remains source-scoped. Hanji 894 supplies the Zhang Jian accusation branch but does not by itself reconstruct Hou Lan's full motive or procedure.
