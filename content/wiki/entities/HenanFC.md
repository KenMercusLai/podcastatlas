## Overview
河南足球俱乐部 / Henan FC is referenced in the source as a club where financial difficulty, wage arrears, equity reform, and stadium constraints complicate the case for Chinese top-flight expansion.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Henan FC as a reminder that market recovery does not erase club-level fragility. Expansion is presented as plausible only if clubs with unpaid wages, tax issues, or weak self-financing are governed through stricter entry and finance rules rather than allowed to carry unresolved risk into a larger league.

## Key Characteristics
- Financial-fragility example in the expansion discussion.
- Equity-reform case mentioned as ongoing.
- Stadium-capacity and infrastructure example where a more suitable professional stadium is argued to matter.
