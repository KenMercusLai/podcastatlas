# 郭威 / Guo Wei

Guo Wei / 郭威 is the founder of [[LaterZhou|后周]] in [[169-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-shang-957052909]]. The episode introduces him as the northern military patron whom the young [[ZhaoKuangyin|赵匡胤]] eventually joins after early frustration and wandering.

Guo Wei matters less as an individual protagonist than as part of the Five Dynasties succession pattern. His rise by military force and his adoption-family succession field help the hosts explain why power transfer in this period often moved through soldiers, kinship substitutes, and command credibility rather than stable hereditary routine.

## Connections
