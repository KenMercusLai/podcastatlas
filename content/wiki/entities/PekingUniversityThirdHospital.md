# Peking University Third Hospital / 北医三院

## Overview
Peking University Third Hospital / 北医三院 appears in ingested [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episodes as the institutional context for sports-medicine, orthopedic, and emergency-care doctors explaining safe exercise, injury prevention, first-aid judgment, and trauma escalation.

## Current Profile
The current sources use 北医三院 narrowly as a clinical authority signal rather than a full hospital profile. VOL.212 uses doctors from its sports-medicine department, including [[LiuZimingSportsMedicine|刘子明]], to translate clinical experience into practical advice for sedentary workers, beginner exercisers, weekend athletes, and older adults.

VOL.222 adds [[ZhuTengjiao|祝腾娇]] and [[LiuChenEmergencyDoctor|刘臣]] as orthopedic and emergency-care guests in a World First Aid Day episode. The hospital's wiki role now anchors the show's sports-medicine and first-aid claims while remaining source-scoped.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as the show's source-scoped institutional context for sports medicine, orthopedics, and emergency-care discussion.
- Connects public medical literacy to lower-limb, knee, hip, training, rehabilitation, and injury-prevention questions.
- Connects first-aid literacy to chest-pain escalation, CPR/AED boundaries, seizures, wounds, burns, medication risk, and emergency calling through named doctors in VOL.222.
- Supports an ordinary-listener framing of exercise safety rather than elite sports performance.
- Helps bind the episode's advice to professional boundaries around pain, swelling, acute injury, braces, tape, and rehabilitation devices.

## Qualifications
Only two ingested sources currently support this page. It should not be read as a complete profile of Peking University Third Hospital or all of its departments.
