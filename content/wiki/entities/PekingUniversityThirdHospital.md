# Peking University Third Hospital / 北医三院

## Overview
Peking University Third Hospital / 北医三院 appears in the VOL.212 [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode as the institutional context for sports-medicine doctors explaining safe exercise and injury prevention.

## Current Profile
The current source uses 北医三院 narrowly as a sports-medicine authority signal. The episode does not develop the hospital's full institutional history; it uses doctors from its sports-medicine department, including [[LiuZimingSportsMedicine|刘子明]], to translate clinical experience into practical advice for sedentary workers, beginner exercisers, weekend athletes, and older adults.

The page is therefore intentionally source-scoped. Its wiki role is to anchor the episode's sports-medicine claims without turning the hospital into a general medical profile.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as the source's institutional sports-medicine context.
- Connects public medical literacy to lower-limb, knee, hip, training, rehabilitation, and injury-prevention questions.
- Supports an ordinary-listener framing of exercise safety rather than elite sports performance.
- Helps bind the episode's advice to professional boundaries around pain, swelling, acute injury, braces, tape, and rehabilitation devices.
