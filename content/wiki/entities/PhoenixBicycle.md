# Phoenix Bicycle / 凤凰自行车

Phoenix Bicycle is one of the "凤永飞" Chinese bicycle brands in [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]]. The episode traces its lineage to Tongchang-related Shanghai bicycle production and describes how quality problems and later brand renewal made Phoenix part of the state-era bicycle memory.

Phoenix matters in two distinct branches. In [[ChinaBicycleShortageEconomy]], it is a scarce branded durable good tied to tickets, wages, and household status; in [[SharedBikeManufacturingShock]], its later strategic agreement and dispute with [[Ofo|OFO]] show how a legacy manufacturer could be exposed to platform-financed order risk.
