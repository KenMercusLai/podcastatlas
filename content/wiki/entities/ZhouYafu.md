# 周亚夫 / Zhou Yafu

周亚夫 / Zhou Yafu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-135-fanwaipian-2-nvxiangshi-yin-kanming-nengli-bei-liubang-fenghou-lgu1fr1zrzmc0o72wtbpxmajb7s|Qinji 135 番外篇2]] as one of [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负]]'s later prediction cases. He asks her to read his face after hearing of her reputation.

The episode says Xu Fu predicts a staged career arc: Zhou Yafu will become a marquis after three years, then after another eight years become a major military and political figure, and nine years later die of hunger. Zhou Yafu initially rejects the prediction because his brother is the heir to the title and because his aristocratic status makes starvation seem implausible.

The source then fulfills the first two stages when Zhou Yafu's brother loses the title and Zhou Yafu inherits it, then rises through service around the Seven States crisis. The final stage comes after his son buys burial armor and refuses to pay the workers; a grievance becomes a report of suspected rebellion, Zhou Yafu is imprisoned, and the episode says he dies by refusing food.
