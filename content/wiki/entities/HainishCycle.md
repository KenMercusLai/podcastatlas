# 海恩宇宙 / Hainish Cycle

海恩宇宙 / Hainish Cycle is the larger [[UrsulaKLeGuin|Le Guin]] science-fiction setting invoked in [[36-heian-de-zuoshou-liufang-yu-ai-de-weilai-wangshi-605254522]]. The episode describes [[TheLeftHandOfDarkness|《黑暗的左手》]] as belonging to a line of works that often feel like anthropological reports, with planets, cultures, myths, and institutions presented through documents and situated observers.

In this source, the Hainish setting matters because it makes interstellar contact a problem of recognition rather than conquest. [[Ekumen|艾库曼 / Ekumen]] sends a single mobile envoy to [[Gethen|格森星 / Gethen]], so the drama depends on trust, translation, local politics, evidence, and the envoy's ability to ask better questions.
