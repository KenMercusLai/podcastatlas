# Steve Papa

Steve Papa appears in [[toast-aman-narang-how-a-long-wait-for-the-dinner-check-launched-a-2-billion-business-6eca81bc-b0e1-47e2-a87d-f3b243e76a97]] as an important [[Endeca]] figure and early [[Toast]] supporter. The source says he encouraged [[AmanNarang]] and [[SteveFredette]] to build Endeca's mobile business internally before the [[Oracle]] acquisition.

After the Toast team pivoted from the payment app toward point-of-sale, Steve Papa invested about $500,000. In the source, his role connects platform-timing belief to early capital: the founders had seen mobile and Android-like shifts open old markets, but still needed patient seed support before institutional investors accepted restaurants as a software market.
