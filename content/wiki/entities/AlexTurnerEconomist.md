# Alex Turner (Economist defence editor)

## Overview
Alex Turner is identified by [[drone-truth-russia-has-the-upper-hand-6aad03f98a1508074de8337e]] as The Economist's defence editor and the reporter explaining Russia's temporary long-range strike advantage over [[Ukraine]].

## Current Profile
Turner's analysis separates a broadly balanced front line from a more asymmetric contest behind it. He connects faster [[Shahed136|Shahed-style drones]], ballistic missiles, civilian-infrastructure attacks, and scarce interceptors to Ukraine's economic and social endurance rather than treating the problem as a weapon-specification race alone.

## Key Characteristics
- Distinguishes frontline balance from long-range strike advantage.
- Connects technical adaptation to civilian, economic, and political consequences.
- Treats production scale and interceptor scarcity as central constraints.
- Reports from [[Kyiv]] and from inside the [[NemesisDroneUnit]].

### Strategic interpretation
