# DJ Shipley

## Overview
DJ Shipley is a retired Navy SEAL and former Tier 1 operator whose [[HubermanLab]] interview connects combat service, severe injury, medical retirement, family strain, training, and supervised psychedelic treatment.

## Current Profile
Shipley's profile is organized around standards after identity disruption. He describes elite military culture as meaningful and addictive, but also recounts loss, helplessness, accumulated physical damage, medication dependence, and the relational cost of blocking off distress. Recovery begins in his account with whatever action remains possible: walking, wrist curls, protected training, morning preparation, therapy, family rituals, and removal of harmful social inputs.

His public framework is [[MentalPosture]], not invulnerability. Physical preparation, attention control, and repeated transitions can make strain more manageable, but the same source shows that discipline did not prevent trauma or family harm. Shipley also reports major benefit from medically supervised ibogaine and [[5MeODMT|5-MeO-DMT]], while insisting that the experience required later [[PsychedelicIntegration|integration]] and life restructuring.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats physical training as a durable baseline for mood, capability, family usefulness, and identity after service.
- Uses evening setup and ordered morning actions to reduce decision load and establish early control.
- Protects attention through delayed calls, social-media limits, and a non-negotiable preparation block.
- Uses explicit transition rituals to move from work intensity into reciprocal family presence.
- Frames recovery as beginning with the smallest safe action still available after injury or collapse.
- Discusses trauma, guilt, medication dependence, marriage strain, and psychedelic treatment through retrospective personal testimony.

## Evidence
- Service and loss - [[how-to-make-yourself-unbreakable-dj-shipley-scim4854936060]] traces Shipley's SEAL career, combat exposure, deaths of friends, survivor helplessness, and refusal to process traumatic events at the time.
- Injury and rebuilding - [[how-to-make-yourself-unbreakable-dj-shipley-scim4854936060]] describes accumulated orthopedic injury, medical retirement, medication washout, electrocution, and progressive return through walking and adaptable strength work.
- Daily standards - [[how-to-make-yourself-unbreakable-dj-shipley-scim4854936060]] describes evening preparation, morning micro-wins, protected movement, input control, and a structured evening walk with his wife.
- Treatment and integration - [[how-to-make-yourself-unbreakable-dj-shipley-scim4854936060]] reports supervised ibogaine and 5-MeO-DMT treatment followed by social pruning, relationship repair, advocacy, and continued routines.

## Qualifications
This profile is bounded to one long-form interview and Shipley's retrospective account. It does not independently verify operational history, medical events, medication use, other people's motives, treatment effects, fitness benchmarks, or causal explanations. His experience does not establish that routine prevents psychiatric illness, that psychedelic treatment is suitable or effective for others, or that medication should be changed without qualified supervision.
