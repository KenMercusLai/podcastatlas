# James Earl Ray

## Overview

James Earl Ray was the escaped convict who pleaded guilty to murdering [[MartinLutherKingJr|Martin Luther King Jr.]] in Memphis on April 4, 1968.

## Current Profile

The source reconstructs Ray through the boarding-house room rented as “John Willard,” a Remington rifle recovered near the Lorraine Motel, aliases including Eric Stavro Galt, and a flight through Atlanta and Toronto to London. British authorities arrested him at Heathrow while he was attempting to travel onward to Rhodesia. He received a 99-year sentence after pleading guilty, then recanted and said he had been framed.

The episode regards Ray as the clear assassin and describes him as a racist who had stalked King. That judgment is still bounded by the source: the page records the podcast's synthesis rather than independently resolving every later forensic or conspiracy claim.

## Key Characteristics

- Escaped convict with a racist background.
- Used multiple names while renting accommodation and fleeing authorities.
- Linked in the episode to the boarding-house firing position and recovered rifle.
- Fled internationally before arrest at Heathrow Airport.
- Pleaded guilty and received a 99-year sentence.
- Later recanted and claimed he had been framed.
