# 曹谦 / Cao Qian (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-922-ruhe-zai-fanchen-zhong-xiulian-qingshang-lsxiqneyyeod6aeeqxuhs517pn1x|Hanji 922]] presents Cao Qian as the official sent to administer [[BaCommanderyLateHan|巴郡]] after [[ChenBaoLateHan|陈鲍]] explains the [[BandunMan|板楯蛮]] rebellion as a consequence of official abuse. [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] pairs Cao Qian's appointment with amnesty, and the source says the Bandun people surrender quickly.

Cao Qian's significance in the wiki is mostly functional: he marks the personnel-change side of [[RootCausePacification|查因安抚式治理]]. The episode uses his appointment to show that removing extractive local governance can settle a revolt more effectively than an expeditionary campaign.
