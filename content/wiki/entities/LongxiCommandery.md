# 陇西郡 / Longxi Commandery

陇西郡 / Longxi Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-85-xuantaihou-weihe-sitong-30-nian-hou-ansha-le-laoqingren-lg7zy6vqtdjcufou4-mox5qkg2ct5]] as one of the Qin northern frontier commanderies that become strategically meaningful after [[QinState|秦国]] destroys [[YiquState|义渠]]. The episode says both Longxi and the later [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]] came from former Yiqu territory.

Its role in the source is connective. Once Beidi is added, Longxi, Beidi, and [[Shangjun|上郡]] form a linked Qin defensive belt against northern Hu groups, turning a long-standing rear-frontier threat into administered border depth.

The page is source-scoped until additional episodes develop Longxi independently. Here it supports the [[QinFrontierBufferManagement|秦国后方边患管理]] branch and the episode's explanation of why eliminating Yiqu mattered to Qin's wider expansion.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-121-yingzheng-zao-shijie-zuizao-gaosu-gonglu-2200nian-huangcao-busheng-ls1u8dmuvs5bbrzadf2dtd00js-p]] adds Longxi as part of Qin Shi Huang's first post-unification journey in 220 BCE. The episode reads the route through Longxi and [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]] as a westward movement into Qin old lands, likely connected to [[HighTempleRite|高庙礼]] at [[YongchengQin|雍城]] rather than to a generic inspection tour.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-149-xichu-bawang-xiangyu-weihe-shijun-2-lpohbb3prsgvokpjipfd7i1ntiwn|Hanji 149 part 2]] adds the Chu-Han reuse of Longxi after Liu Bang recovers Guanzhong. The episode says Han forces take Longxi after Liu Bang moves his capital to [[LiyangQinHan|栎阳]], making the old Qin frontier commandery part of Liu Bang's rear-base consolidation before the eastward contest.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-155-1-lishi-jizai-shenme-qingkuang-hui-fasheng-ren-chi-ren-canju-lrwz-prnxd8hzchagyrbywkux7lh|Hanji 155-1]] returns to Longxi after [[Feiqiu|废丘]] falls. The episode says Han fully pacifies former Yong territory and sets [[ZhongdiCommandery|中地郡]], [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]], and Longxi Commandery, making Longxi part of the completed administrative conversion after [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]]'s last position collapses.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-937-zhanshen-zhisi-haoren-zongshi-duozai-duonan-lp7przsmag9ookq7eoisvsmnm5aw|Hanji 937]] adds a late-Han crisis layer. [[HanSui|韩遂]] attacks Longxi after absorbing other Liangzhou rebel forces, and Longxi administrator [[LiXiangruLateHan|李相如]] joins the rebellion. In this source, Longxi is a frontier commandery where formal Han administration folds under rebel pressure.
