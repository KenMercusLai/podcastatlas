# Okta

## Overview
Okta is an identity-security company used in the bounded source as a test of whether genuine AI-related security demand is already supported by mature products and defensible public-market valuation.

## Current Profile
The source presents Okta's AI identity product as an attempt to verify that the right person reaches the right employee database at the right time. That function places the company inside the practical identity problem created by AI-enabled attacks and delegated systems.

The same discussion warns against inferring company value from category demand alone. Okta is described as early in its AI product rollout and as trading at a materially higher valuation multiple than a similarly growing software comparison, so product maturity, scaled testing, customer outcomes, and durable differentiation remain open evidence requirements.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates in identity security, where access decisions connect users, accounts, employees, and protected systems.
- Presents AI identity controls as a way to match the correct person, resource, and time of access.
- May benefit from growing concern about AI-enabled fraud, rogue agents, and identity attacks.
- Is treated as distinct from endpoint, incident-response, and vulnerability-discovery vendors rather than as a generic cybersecurity beneficiary.
- Remains subject to product-maturity, scaled-testing, competitive, and valuation scrutiny.
