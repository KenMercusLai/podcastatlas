# 杨武 / Yang Wu (Han)

杨武 / Yang Wu (Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-168-bawangbieji-qian-fashengle-shenme-2-liqgict85wh-bf4fn2bytygmk-ca|Hanji 168 part 2]] as one of the named Han-side men associated with the division and confirmation of [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s body after the final fight at [[WujiangChuHan|乌江]].

The source does not develop Yang Wu independently. His page records the narrow role because the episode uses the named reward group to show how Xiang Yu's heroic body becomes a contested object under [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s bounty.
