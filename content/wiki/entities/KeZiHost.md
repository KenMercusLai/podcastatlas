# 柯子

柯子 is the host who introduces [[chuantai-ni-chi-xiangcai-ma-nvshengban-ni-yuanyi-he-xingzhuan-zhihou-de-ziji-zai-yiqi-ma-555771723]] and one of the speakers who initially says she would date a gender-swapped self. Her stated reason is risk control: a partner who shares background, taste, worldview, and visible defects feels more predictable than someone whose unacceptable problems might appear later.

The episode repeatedly jokes that she is easily persuaded by both sides, then gives her the "墙头草奖" in the closing awards. In wiki terms, that flexibility is useful because it shows the source's argumentative method: [[GenderSwapSelfThoughtExperiment]] works by forcing speakers to test both [[SelfSimilarityInIntimacy]] and [[ComplementaryRelationshipSupport]] rather than treating either answer as settled.
