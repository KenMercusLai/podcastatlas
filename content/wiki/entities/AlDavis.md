# Al Davis

Al Davis appears in [[the-nfl-1]] as the aggressive [[AmericanFootballLeague|AFL]] commissioner who intensified the talent war with the [[NationalFootballLeague|NFL]]. The episode says Davis targeted NFL quarterbacks after the Giants signed an AFL kicker, increasing pressure on owners to settle the interleague conflict.

In the source's business-history frame, Davis improved the AFL's leverage by making competition costly enough that merger became more attractive. He therefore sits on the path from fragmented football leagues to the modern [[LeagueFirstOperatingModel]].
