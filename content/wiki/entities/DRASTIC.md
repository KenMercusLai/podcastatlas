## Overview

DRASTIC is presented in the bounded source as a decentralized research collective investigating COVID-19's origins through shared scientific information and document discovery.

## Current Profile

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-gurley-searching-for-feynman-42956628|Bill Gurley's presentation]] casts DRASTIC as an independent "searcher" that kept a possible laboratory accident open when larger institutions and newsrooms were, in his account, reluctant to do so. The source especially credits the collective with surfacing the DEFUSE proposal, while treating that document as grounds for further inquiry rather than conclusive origin proof.

## Key Characteristics

- Operates outside the major institutions Gurley says had conflicts or failed to investigate.
- Uses collaborative scientific and document-based inquiry.
- Expands the evidentiary record around research proposals and possible laboratory pathways.
- Is praised by the speaker but not independently evaluated by the episode.

## Evidence

- Independent searcher role: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-gurley-searching-for-feynman-42956628|Bill Gurley's presentation]] groups DRASTIC with researchers and journalists who continued investigating under criticism.
- Document contribution: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-gurley-searching-for-feynman-42956628|Bill Gurley's presentation]] credits DRASTIC with uncovering the DEFUSE proposal years after the pandemic began.
- Evidentiary boundary: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-gurley-searching-for-feynman-42956628|Bill Gurley's presentation]] treats DEFUSE as evidence requiring scrutiny rather than proof of a laboratory origin.

## Qualifications

The source is openly favorable to DRASTIC and does not audit the collective's methods, membership, errors, or full publication record. The page therefore records its role in Gurley's argument, not an independent endorsement of every claim associated with the group.
