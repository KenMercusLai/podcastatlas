# Khartoum

Khartoum is the Sudanese capital described in [[outsider-chance-ending-sudans-proxy-war-6a75b3695d765781a2b1b132]] after the [[SudaneseArmedForces|SAF]] retook it from the [[RapidSupportForces|RSF]]. [[TomGardner]] says the city feels quiet, empty, pitch-black at night, and devastated around the presidential palace and airport.

The episode uses Khartoum to qualify battlefield-win narratives. Retaking the capital does not mean [[Sudan]] is near peace: richer neighborhoods remain largely empty, services and safety are uncertain, many buildings have been looted, and fighting elsewhere can still threaten the capital if [[ElObeid]] falls.
