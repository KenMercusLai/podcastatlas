# Danone / 达能

## Overview
Danone / 达能 is the foreign shareholder and conflict counterpart in the current wiki's Wahaha story.

## Current Profile
The 大小电波 source gives the fuller business-history account: Danone entered Wahaha through the 1996 joint venture with Hong Kong Baifu Le, eventually controlled the foreign 51% side, and later challenged non-joint-venture companies that used the Wahaha brand and channel. The 不熄灯 source emphasizes the dispute's later narrative effect: a foreign-capital versus national-brand frame helped [[ZongQinghou|宗庆后]] and [[WahahaGroup|娃哈哈]] win public sympathy, but that same public-emotion field can later produce [[NationalBrandNarrativeBackfire]].

## Key Characteristics
- Foreign consumer-products group entering China's beverage market through a Wahaha joint venture.
- Conflict centered on equity control, trademark licensing, non-joint-venture entities, and brand use.
- Served as the external opponent in Wahaha's national-brand narrative.
- Its exit through settlement strengthened the founder-victory story while costing Wahaha expansion time.

## Evidence
- Joint-venture structure: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] describes Danone, Baifu Le, foreign-side 51% control, Wahaha's 49% stake, and Zong Qinghou's retained operating role.
- Trademark issue: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] says the original trademark transfer was rejected and later replaced by a trademark-use license.
- Non-joint-venture conflict: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] details Danone's challenge to 69 non-joint-venture companies and its proposed acquisition of their majority stake.
- Narrative afterlife: [[bu-xi-deng-e02-bi-quan-shan-beng-mei-guo-zheng-fu-guan-men-first-brands-po-chan-wa-ha-ha-feng-bo-lt-ghzgmiroo9a-odaih4xkhsulu]] uses Danone mainly to explain how national-brand rhetoric later shaped Wahaha public opinion.
