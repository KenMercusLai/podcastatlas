# 刁伯 / Diao Bo

刁伯 / Diao Bo enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-81-lishi-zaoqi-pua-jishi-lu-1-ljxbn4gznuohegzyee0-tudi9z-y]] as a capable but initially withdrawn [[QiState|齐国]] figure recommended by [[TianDan|田单]]. The episode says he had defended [[JuWarringStates|莒城]] for six years against [[YanState|燕国]] and had the wisdom and nerve to handle a [[ChuState|楚国]] envoy at the Wu Tong palace.

His source role is less an independent biography than a political trigger. [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]]'s nine favored ministers first recommend him for a mission to Chu, then use [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]]'s warm reception of him as material for suspicion against Tian Dan. Their argument is that Diao Bo could not receive such treatment on his own status and must be relying on Tian Dan's power.

The page therefore tracks Diao Bo as an example of how a minister's talent network can become evidence in a [[TreasonAccusationTrap|treason accusation trap]]. In this source, recommending a worthy man no longer reads only as public service; in a suspicious court, it can be framed as building an independent faction.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-81-lishi-zaoqi-pua-jishi-lu-2-lpksu7523ut-skpmxxh9e-tcbz0k]] gives Diao Bo the corrective role after the slander. Returning from Chu, he directly tells [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]] that calling [[TianDan|田单]] in as though he were an ordinary minister ignores the precedent of rulers honoring their greatest helpers. He compares Tian Dan's achievement to the honored-minister relationships around [[JiangTaigong|姜太公]] and [[GuanZhong|管仲]], then reminds the king that Tian Dan restored Qi without self-coronation. His remonstrance leads to the execution of the nine favored ministers and a larger grant for Tian Dan.
