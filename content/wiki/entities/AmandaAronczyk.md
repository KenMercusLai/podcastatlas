# Amanda Aronczyk

Amanda Aronczyk appears in [[currency-chaos-in-argentina-summer-school]] as one of the [[PlanetMoney]] reporters who traveled to [[Argentina]] in 2023 during the presidential election year. The episode uses her reporting with [[ErikaBeras]] to show worsening inflation through tango travel, dollar timing, worker pay adjustments, and shoe-store repricing.

Her role in the source is field-reporting rather than expert commentary. The reporting makes [[MenuCosts]], [[MultipleExchangeRateRegime]], and [[CurrencyRisk]] visible through ordinary purchase decisions and business operations.
