# Henoko

## Overview
Henoko is the [[Okinawa]] site of a contested U.S.-base relocation project backed by Japan's central government.

## Current Profile
The project is the episode's clearest institutional test of Okinawan resistance. [[TamakiDenny]] and his predecessors failed to stop it through political and legal action, while governor-elect [[KojaGentar]] supports it, making implementation more likely to face less prefectural opposition.

## Key Characteristics
- Concentrates the dispute between national alliance policy and local consent.
- Survived repeated prefectural legal challenges.
- Became a symbol of political fatigue when resistance did not stop construction.
- Connects local base burdens to Japan's broader southwest defense strategy.

## Evidence
### Political conflict
- Source grounding - [[rise-and-shine-warshs-fed-rate-test-6aabb60b964bc344092c48cd]] contrasts Tamaki's opposition with Koja's support and links the change to the 2026 election.

## Qualifications
- The source does not provide the project's engineering status, legal chronology, or full environmental record.
- Reduced gubernatorial resistance does not imply complete local consent.

## What Changed
- Okinawa elected a governor who supports the project after two terms under an opponent.
