## Overview
Peak State Coffee is a mission-driven functional-coffee company founded by [[DannyWalsh]] that infuses organic roasted coffee with adaptogenic mushroom extracts.

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents Peak State as a mostly direct-to-consumer business with early revenue, subscriptions, and repeat purchase, but without a settled need for outside funding. Its central question is how to grow while preserving organic sourcing, conservation giving, and B Corp commitments. The advice is to define true non-negotiables, make mission legible as customer value, and prove stronger retention and acquisition economics before financing expansion.

## Key Characteristics
- Seeks to preserve familiar coffee taste and ritual while adding mushroom extracts.
- Reportedly produced about $500,000 in trailing twelve-month revenue.
- Generates 95% of sales direct to consumer, including roughly 20% from farmers markets, with 5% from retail.
- Uses subscriptions for most online revenue and reports strong repeat purchase among non-subscribers.
- Commits 1% of sales to environmental conservation and maintains B Corp status.
