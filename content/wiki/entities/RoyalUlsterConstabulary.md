# Royal Ulster Constabulary

The Royal Ulster Constabulary appears in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as the overwhelmingly Protestant police force of [[NorthernIreland]], with wide powers over search, arrest, internment, curfews, marches, and publications. Part 1 uses this structure to explain why many Catholics saw policing as part of [[InstitutionalSectarianDiscrimination]] rather than neutral order.

[[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] then presents RUC actions at Caledon, Derry, Burntollet, the [[BattleOfTheBogside]], and Belfast as moments when many Catholics concluded that the state either would not or could not protect them fairly. The RUC's use of batons, water cannon, armoured cars, tear gas, and heavy machine-gun fire made policing part of the conflict rather than a neutral solution in the source's account. The later Hunt Report recommendation that the [[BritishArmy]] take over military policing and that the [[BSpecials|B Specials]] be disbanded helped trigger loyalist riots.
