# 成言梁 / Cheng Yanliang

## Overview
成言梁 appears as the [[HuzuoHuyou|忽左忽右]] host guiding the episode on [[LaiAnArchitect|赉安 / 赖安]] and modern Shanghai architecture.

## Current Profile
In this source, Cheng Yanliang works as an interviewer and framing voice. He introduces [[WuFeipeng|吴飞鹏]]'s research on Lai An, compares Lai An with [[HudecShanghaiArchitect|乌达克]], asks for explanations of [[ShanghaiArtDecoArchitecture|Art Deco]], modernism, and neoclassicism, and repeatedly turns building examples back toward Shanghai geography, public memory, and preservation.

## Key Characteristics
- Cheng frames Lai An as an underrecognized architect beside better-known Hudec.
- He uses concrete building and street examples to make the architecture discussion navigable.
- He pushes the interview from biography into style, city development, and memory.
