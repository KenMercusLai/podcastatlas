# 成言梁 / Cheng Yanliang

## Overview
成言梁 appears as a [[HuzuoHuyou|忽左忽右]] host who guides long-form cultural and historical interviews, including episodes on [[LaiAnArchitect|赉安 / 赖安]], [[Ceuta]], and [[WilliamShakespeare|Shakespeare]].

## Current Profile
Across the bounded sources, Cheng Yanliang works as an interviewer and framing voice. In the Lai An episode, he introduces [[WuFeipeng|吴飞鹏]]'s research, compares Lai An with [[HudecShanghaiArchitect|乌达克]], asks for explanations of [[ShanghaiArtDecoArchitecture|Art Deco]], modernism, and neoclassicism, and turns building examples back toward Shanghai geography, public memory, and preservation. In the Ceuta episode, he frames [[LuDapeng|路大鹏]]'s historical-geography account by moving from current migration news into the [[StraitOfGibraltar]], [[IberianReconquista]], [[PortugueseMaritimeExpansion]], and Spain-Morocco border politics. In the Shakespeare episode, he uses a British stage-arts event to open a conversation about Shakespeare's literary value, history plays, the [[GlobeTheatre]], global adaptation, and live theatre.

## Key Characteristics
- Cheng frames Lai An as an underrecognized architect beside better-known Hudec.
- He uses concrete building and street examples to make the architecture discussion navigable.
- He pushes the interview from biography into style, city development, and memory.
- He can also use a current international-news event as the entry point into long historical geography.
- He can use a cultural event as the entry point into literary history, English monarchy, and performance traditions.
- His hosting role is source-bounded to explanation and framing rather than independent factual authority.
