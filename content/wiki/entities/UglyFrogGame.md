# 丑蛙 / Ugly Frog game

丑蛙 / Ugly Frog is the casual independent game named by [[WuJunIndieGameCreator|巫君]] in [[ep242-duli-youxi-shi-yitiao-gaoqian-hao-saidao-ma-lrqbzrceumbuggzuero5qb4jv3zu]] as one of his team's three complete [[Steam]] works. The source gives it less detail than [[DragonSpiritGameSeries|龙魂]], but it matters as evidence that the team's model is not a single one-off release.

In the episode's business logic, multiple small games increase the chance of recovering cost because no one type can guarantee revenue. Ugly Frog therefore supports the source's cautious account of [[IndieGameCommercialization|indie game commercialization]] as portfolio-like small-team work rather than a simple hit-making formula.
