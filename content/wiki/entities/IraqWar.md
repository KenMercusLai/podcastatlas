# Iraq War / 伊拉克战争

## Overview
The Iraq War / 伊拉克战争 appears here as the historical frame that [[WelcomeToLailongRestaurant|《欢迎来龙餐馆》]] is said to invoke while anonymizing its fictional country.

## Current Profile
The source treats the Iraq War less as a factual chronology than as a test case for [[CrossContextPoliticalCinema]]. The host argues that a Chinese commercial film can borrow the war's recognizable moral weight, but doing so also creates obligations: U.S. invasion, Saddam-era governance, occupation, local collaborators, sectarian conflict, civil-war risk, anti-occupation resistance, terrorism, and ordinary soldier culpability cannot be collapsed into a simple U.S.-versus-terrorist background.

In this reading, the anonymized setting protects the film from some historical specificity while also weakening its political analysis. The war becomes legible enough to give the story stakes, but not specific enough for the film to work through competing local positions.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as the real-world analogue for the war environment in 《欢迎来龙餐馆》.
- Carries moral questions about invasion, occupation, resistance, terrorism, and state responsibility.
- Includes local political divisions that cannot be reduced to two combatant sides.
- Exposes the limit of ordinary humanitarian values when used as a full political explanation.
- Remains source-scoped in this wiki until supported by dedicated Iraq War sources.
