# Elliot Callender

## Overview
Elliot Callender is presented as an AI-safety protest organizer advocating a complete stop to advanced AI development.

## Current Profile
Callender occupies the strongest restraint position in the episode. After demonstrating outside [[OpenAI]] for more than a month, he is planning action at the U.S. Capitol and argues that a superhuman system could escape control and cause irreversible catastrophe. The belief that little time may remain also shapes his everyday risk perception.

## Key Characteristics
- Organizes public protest over advanced-AI development.
- Advocates a complete development halt rather than only stronger safeguards.
- Grounds his position in possible loss of control and irreversible catastrophe.
- Plans to move activism from an AI-company setting to the U.S. Capitol.
- Describes a compressed future horizon affecting ordinary personal decisions.
