## Overview

建章宫 / Jianzhang Palace is a Western Han palace complex that the current sources connect to [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s post-fire building program, court display, and materialized search for immortality.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-259-hanwudi-wo-ba-ni-dang-dashi-ni-ba-wo-dang-shazi-2-lo4brmkuzpoozgszshrqz4hbgrgt|Hanji 259 part 2]] supplies the origin-and-landscape branch: after the 柏梁台 fire and advice favoring a larger replacement, Wudi orders a monumental complex whose Taiye Pool reproduces immortal islands and whose 神明台 bronze figure catches dew for longevity. The source presents the palace as both spectacular architecture and a costly attempt to make sacred geography operational.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-371-2-shangdi-shuyu-zhongguo-zhexia-shichui-le-ltllclatwddfxlawrdpgziqlp3xq|Hanji 371-2]] places the palace in the later Taichu-era field of Mingtang sacrifice, coastal searches, Houtu worship, Penglai imagery, calendar reform, and frontier policy. Together the sources support continuity of palace symbolism across episodes, but not the exact building chronology or a claim that every later use remained exclusively religious.

## Key Characteristics

- Monumental Western Han palace associated with post-fire expansion.
- Taiye Pool landscape reproducing distant immortal-island geography near the imperial center.
- Site of a dew-catching immortal figure in the episode's longevity architecture.
- Court space in which ritual, spectacle, administration, and immortality seeking overlap without becoming identical.

## Evidence

- **Origin, scale, and landscape:** [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-259-hanwudi-wo-ba-ni-dang-dashi-ni-ba-wo-dang-shazi-2-lo4brmkuzpoozgszshrqz4hbgrgt|Hanji 259 part 2]] links the post-fire building decision to monumental gates, Taiye Pool, artificial immortal islands, 神明台, and the bronze dew collector.
- **Later ritual field:** [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-371-2-shangdi-shuyu-zhongguo-zhexia-shichui-le-ltllclatwddfxlawrdpgziqlp3xq|Hanji 371-2]] places Jianzhang Palace and Penglai imagery beside Wudi's state sacrifice, continued immortal search, and Taichu ordering program.

## Qualifications

Both inputs are short structured podcast summaries. The current evidence does not settle the palace's precise plan, dimensions, construction phases, labor bill, relationship to every named platform, archaeological identification, or destruction history. “千门万户,” the twenty-plus-zhang gate, the modeled islands, the bronze figure's dimensions and medical function, and the claim that the site pioneered later palace gardens remain source-attributed.

## What Changed

- Created a focused palace profile joining the post-fire origin account to its later Taichu ritual setting.
- Distinguished materialized sacred geography from proof that the promised longevity effects existed.
