# U.S. Green Building Council of California

The U.S. Green Building Council of California appears in [[tech-20260420-0420-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260420-0420-mp-tech-pod-128]] through CEO [[BenStapleton]]. The organization supplies the episode's market-development explanation for [[California]]'s [[EmbodiedCarbonBuildingCode]].

Stapleton's comments frame the mandate as a demand signal. If designers, builders, and owners have to look for sustainable options, manufacturers have clearer evidence that [[LowCarbonConstructionMaterials]] are worth producing and distributing. The organization therefore connects code compliance to [[EconomicClimateTechAdoption]] rather than only to environmental messaging.
