# Flying Pigeon / 飞鸽自行车

Flying Pigeon is the Tianjin-rooted Chinese bicycle brand discussed in [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]] as part of the "凤永飞" trio. The episode links it to New China's bicycle industry, everyday mobility, and the symbolic gift of Flying Pigeon bicycles during George H. W. Bush's 1989 presidential visit to China.

The brand anchors the symbolic side of [[ChinaBicycleShortageEconomy]]. Like [[PhoenixBicycle]] and [[ForeverBicycle]], it was not only transport equipment; it became a durable-goods sign of industrial capacity, household status, and China-as-bicycle-country memory.
