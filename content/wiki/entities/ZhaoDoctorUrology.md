## Current Profile
The current source presents 赵医生 as a clinician-educator who turns everyday urology anxieties into context-dependent clinical questions. His explanations repeatedly separate a symptom or test result from a diagnosis: PSA can rise for reasons other than cancer, urinary frequency can come from several lower-urinary-tract or physiological causes, yellow urine is often concentrated urine, and renal ultrasound findings may need CT or observation rather than immediate surgery.

His practical style is conservative in both directions. He discourages panic and unnecessary surgery for small asymptomatic stones, benign-leaning renal findings, or cleanly managed long foreskin, while also setting clear escalation thresholds for obstruction, infection, impaired kidney function, suspected malignancy, urinary or rectal foreign bodies, recurrent inflammation, phimosis, and persistent symptoms.

- Explains urology through symptom pattern, organ system, function, imaging, and follow-up rather than single-marker judgment.
- Treats prostate, urinary frequency, kidney-stone, renal-mass, foreskin, and infection questions as context-sensitive clinical problems.
