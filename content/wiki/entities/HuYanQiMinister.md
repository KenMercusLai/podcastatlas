# 胡衍 / Hu Yan (Qi Minister)

胡衍 / Hu Yan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-69-bikan-gudai-diwang-qipa-sifa-da-pandian-lukvjsktg-pgfjhizvypwmdmw-13]] as a Qi remonstrant who challenges [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]] after Qi's conquest of [[SongState|宋国]]. The source says Qi Min Wang is preparing to attack [[ChuState|楚国]], pressure the Three Jin, absorb the East and West Zhou polities, and make himself Son of Heaven; Hu Yan calls the plan absurd and is executed in the marketplace.

His role is source-scoped but important. Hu Yan marks the point where Qi's external overreach and internal advice failure become the same problem: a ruler who needs correction most has made truthful speech lethal.
