# BrainHQ

## Overview
BrainHQ is the brain-training platform discussed in [[accelerate-learning-increase-cognitive-capacity-dr-tommy-wood-scim4201272551]] as a processing-speed-oriented cognitive training tool influenced by Michael Merzenich's work.

## Current Profile
The source uses BrainHQ as the main named example of targeted cognitive training rather than as a general replacement for rich real-world learning. Its importance is bounded: processing-speed training with boosters is described as having a long-term dementia-risk signal in ACTIVE trial follow-up, while Wood still favors broader activities such as dance, sport, music, language, and martial arts when the goal is transferable adult capacity.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents targeted cognitive training focused partly on processing speed.
- Is linked in the episode to Michael Merzenich's neuroplasticity work.
- Functions as one component inside broader multidomain dementia-prevention programs and cognitive-training evidence.
- Remains narrower than embodied, social, multimodal skill learning in Wood's practical hierarchy.

## Evidence
- Platform description - [[accelerate-learning-increase-cognitive-capacity-dr-tommy-wood-scim4201272551]] describes BrainHQ as a platform focused partly on processing speed.
- Evidence context - [[accelerate-learning-increase-cognitive-capacity-dr-tommy-wood-scim4201272551]] relates processing-speed training with booster sessions to lower long-term dementia risk in an ACTIVE-trial secondary analysis.
- Scope contrast - [[accelerate-learning-increase-cognitive-capacity-dr-tommy-wood-scim4201272551]] contrasts narrow computer-based training with broader activities that recruit movement, senses, social interaction, and decisions.

## Qualifications
The episode does not independently evaluate BrainHQ's full product claims, pricing, or suitability for particular users. The strongest claim is a source-scoped link between processing-speed training and long-term dementia-risk signals, not proof that every brain-training program transfers broadly.
