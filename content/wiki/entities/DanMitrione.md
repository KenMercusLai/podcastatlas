# Dan Mitrione

## Overview

Dan Mitrione was an American police adviser kidnapped and killed by the [[Tupamaros]] in 1970; [[261-uruguay-the-tupamaros-glt1539968794]] treats allegations that he taught torture in [[Uruguay]] as important but unresolved.

## Current Profile

The episode follows Mitrione from municipal policing in Indiana into a US overseas police-assistance program, with assignments in Brazil and Uruguay. Former Uruguayan police and CIA-linked testimony is presented as support for torture-training allegations, while people who worked with him deny them and describe counterinsurgency and riot-control advising instead. His kidnapping, the government's refusal to exchange prisoners, and his killing intensified the conflict without settling the historical dispute around his work.

## Key Characteristics

- Moved from local US policing into overseas police assistance.
- Served in Brazil and then Uruguay.
- Became the subject of contested torture-training allegations.
- Was kidnapped on 31 July 1970.
- Was killed after the government refused a prisoner-release demand.
- Became a Cold War symbol whose public meaning exceeds the evidence settled in the episode.

## Evidence

- Career and allegations: [[261-uruguay-the-tupamaros-glt1539968794]] sets testimony alleging torture instruction against colleagues' denials.
- Kidnapping: [[261-uruguay-the-tupamaros-glt1539968794]] describes his seizure, Tupamaro leadership arrests, the demand to release 150 prisoners, government refusal, and his death.
- Cultural afterlife: [[261-uruguay-the-tupamaros-glt1539968794]] connects the case to Costa-Gavras's film *State of Siege*.

## Qualifications

The page intentionally does not adjudicate the torture allegation. The episode says the surviving claims conflict and that certainty is unavailable. It also does not provide a complete account of US assistance programs, Uruguayan police abuse, CIA involvement, or the operational decision to kill Mitrione.
