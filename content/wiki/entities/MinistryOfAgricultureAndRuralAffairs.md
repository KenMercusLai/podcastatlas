# Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs / 农业农村部

## Overview
The Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs / 农业农村部 appears in the source as the official context for China's pork-price monitoring, pig-industry analysis, and policy attention to live-hog capacity.

## Current Profile
In this wiki entry, the ministry is not analyzed institutionally in full. Its current role is evidentiary: the source uses ministry-linked monitoring data and industry-analysis commentary to describe the altered [[PorkCycle|猪周期]], especially the long low-price phase, scale-farming threshold, and historical cycle count since 2002.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides monitored pork-market data used in the source's price discussion.
- Supplies or anchors industry-analysis commentary about historical pork cycles.
- Defines large-scale pig farms in the source as operations above a stated annual slaughter threshold.
- Appears in relation to live-hog capacity regulation meetings and market stabilization concerns.
- Functions as a source-side authority rather than as the sole causal actor behind pork prices.

## Evidence
Price monitoring:
- [[zhurou-jiage-jingli-man-chang-xiadie-weishenme-zhuzhouqi-buzai-zunxun-laojiezou-1012895922]] cites ministry monitoring of 500 market points for late-August pork prices.

Industry cycle framing:
- [[zhurou-jiage-jingli-man-chang-xiadie-weishenme-zhuzhouqi-buzai-zunxun-laojiezou-1012895922]] attributes the historical five-complete-cycle account since 2002 to a ministry-linked pork-industry analyst.

Scale-farming threshold:
- [[zhurou-jiage-jingli-man-chang-xiadie-weishenme-zhuzhouqi-buzai-zunxun-laojiezou-1012895922]] says the ministry defines farms with annual slaughter above 500 pigs as scaled operations.
