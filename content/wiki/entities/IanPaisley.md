# Ian Paisley

Ian Paisley appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] as the hardline unionist challenger who turns Catholic civil rights activism into a warning about republican subversion. The episode says his "IRA front" argument was misleading as a broad claim, but drew force from real overlap between some republican figures and civil-rights organizing.

Paisley nearly defeated [[TerenceONeill|Terence O'Neill]] in Bannside in the February 1969 Stormont election, exposing the fracture inside unionism. The episode also says loyalist infrastructure bombings were carried out by the [[UlsterVolunteerForce|UVF]] working with Paisley-linked Ulster Protestant Volunteers, helping destroy O'Neill's authority while publicly blaming the [[IrishRepublicanArmy|IRA]].
