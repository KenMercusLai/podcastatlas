# Ian Paisley

Ian Paisley appears in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as a hardline Protestant preacher whose anti-Catholic theology, public performance, and publicity tactics helped turn [[UnionistSiegeMentality|unionist siege fear]] into street and electoral pressure. The episode links him to the [[FreePresbyterianChurchOfUlster|Free Presbyterian Church of Ulster]], [[UlsterProtestantAction]], attacks on [[TerenceONeill|Terence O'Neill]] over [[SeanLemass]], and the 1964 tricolor riot involving [[LiamMcMillen]].

In [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]], Paisley becomes the hardline unionist challenger who turns Catholic civil rights activism into a warning about republican subversion. He nearly defeated O'Neill in Bannside in the February 1969 Stormont election, exposing the fracture inside unionism. The episode also says loyalist infrastructure bombings were carried out by the [[UlsterVolunteerForce|UVF]] working with Paisley-linked [[UlsterProtestantVolunteers]], helping destroy O'Neill's authority while publicly blaming the [[IrishRepublicanArmy|IRA]].
