# 东城 / Dongcheng (Chu-Han)

东城 / Dongcheng (Chu-Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-168-bawangbieji-qian-fashengle-shenme-2-liqgict85wh-bf4fn2bytygmk-ca|Hanji 168 part 2]] as the place where [[XiangYu|项羽]] tries to prove, after the [[GaixiaBattle|垓下之战]], that his defeat is not a failure of battlefield courage or tactical ability. By the time he reaches Dongcheng, the source says he has only twenty-eight riders left while Han pursuers number in the thousands.

The episode's interest is not Dongcheng as a long-term base. It is a stage for the final self-proof. Xiang Yu divides his riders into four groups, charges, kills Han officers, confuses the surrounding force, and regroups with only two losses. The host accepts the tactical brilliance, but reads the scene as part of [[TacticalSelfVindicationTrap|战术自证陷阱]]: Xiang Yu demonstrates what everyone already knows while avoiding the more important question of why his strategic position has become hopeless.
