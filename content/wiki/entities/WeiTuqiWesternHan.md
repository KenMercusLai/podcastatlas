## Overview

魏屠耆 / Wei Tuqi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-403-2-dujian-zhan-loulan-bugai-bei-yiwang-de-chaoji-waijiaoguan-fujiezi-lssxm3z-fkdblz8nfoy1pojnetja|Hanji 403-2]] as the Loulan hostage in Chang'an whom [[FuJieziWesternHan|傅介子]] announces as king after killing [[LoulanKingAnguiWesternHan|Angui]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Wei Tuqi as the hinge between punitive assassination and post-assassination settlement. Because he has been kept in Chang'an as a hostage, Han can use him as a prepared successor after Angui's death. Fu Jiezi's announcement makes the succession a Han-backed reordering of [[LoulanKingdomWesternHan|Loulan]], paired with the renaming of Loulan as Shanshan.

Wei Tuqi's own agency appears in the return arrangement. Before leaving Chang'an, he requests a Han military-agricultural presence at Yixun so that Han force can help stabilize his new position. The court answers with a small sima-led group of forty men, making the succession depend on light but visible Han support rather than on the assassination alone.

## Key Characteristics

- Loulan hostage in Chang'an used as Han's replacement ruler after Angui's killing.
- Installed in the episode as king when Loulan is renamed Shanshan.
- Receives Han ritual and material support before returning west.
- Requests a small Yixun屯田 presence to secure his authority.
- Source case for hostage kingship as a low-cost frontier proxy mechanism.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 403-2. It does not reconstruct Wei Tuqi's full reign, Shanshan's later institutional history, the precise location and administration of Yixun, or the durability of his local acceptance after returning from Chang'an.
