# Neon (film distributor)

## Overview

Neon is a film distributor described in the episode as oriented toward art-house exhibition, festivals, awards, and auteur work that can overlap with horror.

## Current Profile

Within the source's horror-industry map, Neon occupies a prestige and festival position distinct from [[BlumhouseProductions]]' low-budget production portfolio and [[A24]]'s wider cultural-brand model. Its relevance is not that it distributes only horror, but that the broader acceptance of unsettling genre-adjacent auteur films gives horror creators access to criticism, festivals, and awards circuits.

## Key Characteristics

- Distributor associated with art-house and festival positioning.
- Connects genre-adjacent auteur films to prestige and awards pathways.
- Part of a wider ecosystem in which horror has gained institutional legitimacy.
- Distinct in the episode from low-budget slate production and lifestyle-brand marketing.
