# Toussaint Louverture

## Overview

Toussaint Louverture was a central military and political leader of the [[HaitianRevolution]] who defended emancipation during the multi-sided war in [[SaintDomingue]].

## Current Profile

The episode places Toussaint's leadership after French representatives and the revolutionary government had moved toward emancipation under military pressure. He emerged inside a conflict of changing alliances and incompatible goals rather than a simple two-sided liberation war, and his opposition to restored slavery made him central to the revolutionary settlement.

The account resists a flawless-hero profile. Long war, institutional collapse, foreign intervention, and continual alliance shifts forced severe political and military choices. Napoleon's expedition removed Toussaint but did not resolve the problem France faced: people who had secured freedom through arms were unlikely to accept re-enslavement.

## Key Characteristics

- Major leader of the Haitian Revolution.
- Military and political defender of emancipation.
- Actor operating amid shifting domestic and international alliances.
- Opponent of restored slavery in Saint-Domingue.
- Leader removed during Napoleon's effort to reassert French control.
- Emancipatory figure whose exercise of power remains subject to moral qualification.
