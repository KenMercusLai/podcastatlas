# Henry the Quarrelsome

## Overview

Henry the Quarrelsome was duke of Bavaria and a dynastic challenger to [[OttoII]] whose ambitions also threatened the minority succession of [[OttoIII]].

## Current Profile

The episode introduces Henry as Otto II's uncle and an internal rival during the consolidation of the younger emperor's rule. Otto II eventually imprisoned him, but the dynasty's vulnerability returned after the southern defeat and the emperor's death.

Henry's presence helps explain why Otto III's coronation and [[Theophano]]'s regency were urgent political acts rather than ceremonial formalities.

## Key Characteristics

- Bavarian duke inside the Ottonian dynasty.
- Challenger to Otto II's authority.
- Recurrent succession risk around a child heir.
- Example of the internal opposition contained by Theophano's regency.
