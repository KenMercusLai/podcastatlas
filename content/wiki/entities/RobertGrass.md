# Robert Grass

## Overview

Robert Grass is [[KennethSmith]]'s long-term lawyer, friend, and an eyewitness to his nitrogen-hypoxia execution in [[TheAlabamaMurders]].

## Current Profile

Grass began representing Smith pro bono in 2005 and repeatedly traveled from New York to Alabama over nearly two decades, developing a friendship as well as an attorney-client relationship. He describes Smith as religious, gracious, decent, and constructive inside prison. At the nitrogen execution, Grass observes seizure-like movement, straining, and apparent gasping lasting minutes rather than the predicted seconds, while stopping short of claiming knowledge of Smith's subjective pain. His account shows both the legal work before the failed first attempt and the grief carried by counsel after the second execution.

## Key Characteristics

- Began representing Smith pro bono in 2005 and continued for approximately eighteen years.
- Developed an unusual friendship with Smith through repeated visits and long representation.
- Was selected as an execution witness.
