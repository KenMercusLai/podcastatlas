# Robert Grass

## Overview

Robert Grass is [[KennethSmith]]'s long-term lawyer and an eyewitness to his nitrogen-hypoxia execution in the final episode of [[TheAlabamaMurders]].

## Current Profile

Grass represents Smith for roughly eighteen years in the episode's account. At the execution, he observes seizure-like movement, straining against restraints, and apparent gasping lasting minutes rather than the predicted seconds. He does not claim to know whether Smith felt pain, preserving a boundary between observation and subjective experience. His grief after the execution shows how a capital case can shape counsel as well as the condemned person and family.

## Key Characteristics

- Represented Smith for approximately eighteen years.
- Was selected as an execution witness.
- Described movement and apparent gasping that challenged the rapid, painless public model.
- Explicitly stopped short of claiming knowledge of Smith's subjective pain.
- Remembered the execution as the failed endpoint of long legal representation.
