# John Stubbs

John Stubbs appears in [[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] as the Protestant pamphleteer who attacked [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]]'s proposed marriage to [[FrancoisDukeOfAnjou|Francois, Duke of Anjou]]. The episode says Stubbs had his right hand cut off for sedition after opposing the match.

Stubbs matters less as an individual political strategist than as a sign of domestic limits on [[MarriageDiplomacyAsStrategicOption]]. Elizabeth could use Anjou as a French counterweight to [[Spain]], but many English Protestants saw a French Catholic husband as a religious and succession danger.

The source pairs Stubbs's Protestant objection with [[WilliamAllen]]'s Catholic criticism, showing that the Anjou option was diplomatically useful precisely because it was politically explosive at home.
