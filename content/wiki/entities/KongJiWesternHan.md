## Overview

孔吉 / Kong Ji is a Western Han Confucius-family figure who appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-553-zhichang-zhong-ruhe-shibie-mingsheng-anjiang-lrxip_degagyo5vhek5pdxnubpsi|Hanji 553]] as the person [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] enfeoffs after officials treat the Confucius line as the usable ritual successor to the Shang-Tang line.

## Current Profile

The source says Chengdi had ordered a search for Shang descendants, but officials found the old lineage dispersed into many surnames and hard to identify through a direct heir. [[KuangHengWesternHan|匡衡]] and Mei Fu therefore argue that the Confucius family can be honored as a Shang-Tang successor line, and Chengdi accepts the recommendation by making Kong Ji Yin Shaojia Hou. In Suihe 1 / 8 BCE, the source says Kong Ji and the Zhou successor marquis are both elevated to ducal rank, each with a hundred-li domain.

Kong Ji's role in this wiki is therefore narrow. He is not treated as a political actor in the Three Excellencies reform; he anchors the episode's opening ritual-lineage material before the source turns to [[HeWuWesternHan|何武]], [[WangGenWesternHan|王根]], and top-office balance.

## Key Characteristics

- Confucius-family figure used in this source as a ritual successor to the Shang-Tang line.
- Enfeoffed by Chengdi as Yin Shaojia Hou after officials cannot identify a straightforward Shang direct descendant.
- Elevated to ducal rank in the same Suihe 1 / 8 BCE notice that also raises the Zhou successor line.

## Qualifications
