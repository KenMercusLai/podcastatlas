## Overview
皇甫棱 / Huangfu Leng was an Eastern Han Duliao general whose current wiki profile centers on protecting [[ShiziChanyu|Shizi]] from [[AnGuoSouthernXiongnu|An Guo's]] assassination risk. His role is narrow but important in the prehistory of the Southern Xiongnu crisis.

## Current Profile
Hanji 768 presents Huangfu Leng as the commander who recognized that An Guo intended to kill Shizi at court meetings. By not sending Shizi to those meetings, Huangfu Leng blocked An Guo's access to him. Emperor He's court later dismissed Huangfu Leng and appointed [[ZhuHuiHeEraLateHan|Zhu Hui]] as acting Duliao general.

## Key Characteristics
- Frontier official who understood the internal danger inside Southern Xiongnu politics.
- Protected Shizi through exposure management rather than open confrontation.
- Temporarily frustrated An Guo's plot by controlling meeting attendance.
- Was removed before the crisis fully broke open.
- Serves as an example of preventive frontier administration.

- Replacement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-768-lishi-gaosu-ni-jidu-shi-shaxin-lgk8qoxvs5ax8oikgecrxqvispna|Hanji 768]] reports the court's decision to dismiss Huangfu Leng and appoint Zhu Hui as acting Duliao general.
- Crisis context: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-768-lishi-gaosu-ni-jidu-shi-shaxin-lgk8qoxvs5ax8oikgecrxqvispna|Hanji 768]] places Huangfu Leng's protection before An Guo's complaint, flight, failed attack, and death.

## Qualifications
The source does not explain why Huangfu Leng was dismissed, so the page should not infer fault or success beyond his protective handling of Shizi. His later career is not covered here.
