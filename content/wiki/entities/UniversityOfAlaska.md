# University of Alaska

The University of Alaska appears in [[tech-20251224-1224-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251224-1224-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the research setting for work on [[MyceliumInsulation]]. The episode follows researchers combining wood fiber, cardboard, or pulp with mushroom mycelium to make an insulation board that could substitute for polystyrene foam.

The source makes the university a local materials-research node rather than a generic academic affiliation. Its [[Alaska]] setting matters because [[FomesFomentarius]] is described as common across Arctic and subarctic regions, and because nearby seafood shipping gives [[SeafoodColdChainPackaging]] an immediate testing context.
