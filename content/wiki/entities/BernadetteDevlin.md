# Bernadette Devlin

Bernadette Devlin appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] as a radical student activist associated with [[PeoplesDemocracyNorthernIreland|People's Democracy]]. She helped lead the New Year's Day 1969 march from Belfast to Derry that culminated in the [[BurntolletBridgeAttack|Burntollet]] ambush.

The episode also highlights Devlin's rapid entry into Westminster after she won the Mid Ulster by-election at age 21. Her maiden speech condemned unionist rule, pressed [[HaroldWilson]] to abolish [[Stormont]], and warned that sending British troops could be dangerous.
