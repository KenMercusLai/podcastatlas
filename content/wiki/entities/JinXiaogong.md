# 晋孝公 / Duke Xiao of Jin

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-3-lsbx8l7wxeioae2lwuwkkkutpdwn]] records Jin Xiao Gong's accession after [[JinLiegong|晋烈公]] dies in 393 BCE. The source immediately qualifies the notice: succession within [[JinState|晋国]] no longer changes much because the ruler is already dependent on [[HanState|韩国]], [[ZhaoState|赵国]], and [[WeiState|魏国]].

晋孝公 / Duke Xiao of Jin appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl|Zhouji 09]] through his death in 378 BCE. The source says his son succeeds as [[JinJinggong|晋静公]].

The page is a source-scoped succession node. Its importance comes from what follows: the next Jin ruler becomes the last vestige of [[JinState|晋国]] before Han, Zhao, and Wei remove him and divide the remaining territory.
