# BN Builders

BN Builders is the construction firm represented by [[EmilyEnglish]] in [[tech-20260420-0420-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260420-0420-mp-tech-pod-128]]. The episode uses the firm's perspective to test whether [[California]]'s [[EmbodiedCarbonBuildingCode]] is a practical construction burden.

English's comments make [[ConstructionCarbonCompliance]] look achievable when requirements are clear and actionable. The source says the embodied-carbon hurdle was not large enough to make her projects cost more or take longer, which keeps the episode's emphasis on documentation, review, and supplier awareness rather than on unavoidable construction disruption.
