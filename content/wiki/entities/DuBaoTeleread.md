# 读报teleread / 独报

## Overview
读报teleread / 独报 is the Chinese podcast show represented in the wiki by a fifth-anniversary special on AI, creator workflow, podcast form, and long-term creative sustainability.

## Current Profile
The current source presents the show as a highly scripted, voice-centered podcast whose creator treats research, article selection, show notes, and precise spoken scripts as distinct production layers. AI is welcomed as an assistant for recommendations, search, organization, and operational details, but the show resists handing over script voice, argument progression, and speaker reaction to AI because those choices define the program's identity.

The source also frames the show as a small, long-running creator project. Its fifth-anniversary reflection links [[PodcastProductionWorkflow]] and [[PodcastAuthenticityBoundary]] to a practical sustainability problem: repeated "correct but expensive" choices can preserve quality and principle while also creating creator exhaustion.

## Key Characteristics
- It is a Chinese podcast centered on reading, interpreting, and discussing articles through a recognizable host voice.
- Its workflow treats scripts as final spoken expression rather than only background notes.
- It uses AI selectively for support tasks such as article recommendation, research breadth, and show notes.
- It places high value on accuracy, source checking, and listener correction.
- It treats podcasting as a voice medium even while acknowledging the growth of video podcasts.
- It frames long-term creation as a balance between principle, cost, recognition, and reduced friction.
