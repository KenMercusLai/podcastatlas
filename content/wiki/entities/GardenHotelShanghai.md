# 花园饭店 / Garden Hotel Shanghai

## Overview
花园饭店 appears in the episode as a major [[LaiAnArchitect|Lai An]] building and as the former French Club, combining neoclassical exterior authority with a more modern interior.

## Current Profile
The source treats Garden Hotel as one of Lai An's strongest durability and style examples. Wu Feipeng says the building opened on 1926-01-01 after a 1923 design process and used extensive fir-pile foundations. Its exterior needed formal, government-adjacent classicism, while its interior spaces, including the second-floor hall and east-building lobby, are interpreted as modernist and Art Deco in spirit.

## Key Characteristics
- Garden Hotel is used as evidence of Lai An's construction-quality standards.
- It combines exterior neoclassicism with interior modernist and Art Deco design.
- Its site history includes the French Club and earlier tennis courts.
- Its lost swimming pool becomes an example of modernist heritage vulnerability.
