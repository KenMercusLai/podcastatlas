# 静子 / Shizuko

静子 / Shizuko is the woman journalist in [[NoLongerHuman|《人间失格》]] with whom [[ObaYozo|大庭叶藏]] briefly lives in [[33-renjian-shige-danxiaogui-de-zhuanglie-xianji-599924624]]. The episode frames this period as one of Yozo's temporary shelters after interrogation and lodging with the merchant figure called 比目鱼.

With Shizuko and her daughter, Yozo appears to approach a small household life while earning money from cheap comics. That possibility collapses when the daughter says she wants a real father; Yozo decides he will damage them and leaves instead of taking up the role.

## Key Claims
- Shizuko's household shows that Yozo can temporarily receive care and usefulness, but cannot trust his ability to remain inside it.
- The daughter's father wish makes ordinary family normality feel like an accusation.
- This episode connects Yozo's shame to [[CoercedNormality|normality coercion]]: even kindness becomes unbearable when it implies a role he cannot occupy.
