# Sandals Resorts

## Overview
Sandals Resorts is the all-inclusive resort operator named as the target of [[RoyalCaribbean|Royal Caribbean]]'s source-reported majority-stake acquisition plan.

## Current Profile
The episode values Sandals above $6 billion in the reported transaction and treats its land-based Caribbean resort network as a way for Royal Caribbean to extend customer spending beyond cruise ships and ports. Sandals contributes resort operating capability and destination inventory, while the buyer contributes cruise customers and a broader vacation ecosystem.

## Key Characteristics
- All-inclusive resort operator.
- Caribbean destination exposure in the source's strategic frame.
- Reported valuation above $6 billion.
- Potential land-based extension of Royal Caribbean's vacation ecosystem.
