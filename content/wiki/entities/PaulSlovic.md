# Paul Slovic

Paul Slovic appears in [[vol-110-touzi-jiushi-dui-shijieguan-de-toupiao-maichu-zichan-peizhi-di-yi-bu-wanjiepian-lk5jkgnzvzerymcixylsdqdtn7f8]] through the host's discussion of [[RiskPerception]]. The episode uses Slovic's work to explain why ordinary people often judge risk through vivid details, feelings, and broad danger categories rather than through fine probability distinctions.

His role in the source is a bridge from psychology to investing. The host uses Slovic, via [[DanielKahneman]], to explain why rational personal-finance implementation can feel boring, difficult, and emotionally mismatched even when the need to save or allocate assets is obvious.
