# Bjorn Adek Olsen

Bjorn Adek Olsen appears in [[how-to-beat-the-resource-curse-in-norway-summer-school]] as the Norwegian fish marketer hired to sell Norwegian seafood into [[Japan]]. The episode describes him as a former fisherman who spoke Japanese and understood that salmon sold for sushi could command more value than salmon sold only for cooking.

His work turns the salmon segment into a [[SalmonSushiMarketCreation]] case. After initial rejection by Japanese industry executives, Olsen's team tried branding, imagery, and relationship-building, then used a large discounted sale to [[Nishire]] on the condition that the fish be sold as sushi.
