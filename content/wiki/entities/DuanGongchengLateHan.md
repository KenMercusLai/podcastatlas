# 段恭诚 / Duan Gongcheng (late Han)

## Overview

Duan Gongcheng is the Guanghan commandery reporting official in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-815-kebei-gudai-zhongchen-de-jiafeng-zhishang-llkhbxo-63pjw-gk9pihnbfr94v|Hanji 815]] who defends [[PangCanLateHan|Pang Can]] against slander by presenting popular testimony and moral warning to [[EmperorShunOfHan|Emperor Shun]].

## Current Profile

Duan's profile is that of a lower-channel truth carrier. He is not shown controlling court decisions directly, but he uses a memorial to tell the emperor that Pang Can's loyalty is publicly recognized and that the court's flattering environment has made a good minister isolated and vulnerable.

His intervention produces a short-term imperial response: Shun sends a small yellow gate attendant and an imperial doctor with sheep and wine to ask about Pang's illness. That comfort does not save Pang after the later household murder scandal, but Duan's memorial preserves a record of Pang's reputation and marks the court's danger in harming loyal ministers.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses local reporting status to bring non-capital public judgment into court.
- Defends a high minister by combining popular testimony with ruler-centered moral warning.
- Treats slander against upright officials as a legitimacy risk for the throne, not only a private injustice.
- Produces a limited response: imperial comfort and inquiry, not durable institutional protection.

## Evidence

- Public testimony as defense: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-815-kebei-gudai-zhongchen-de-jiafeng-zhishang-llkhbxo-63pjw-gk9pihnbfr94v|Hanji 815]] has Duan report what farmers and common people say about Pang Can's loyalty and exhaustion.
- Moral warning to the ruler: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-815-kebei-gudai-zhongchen-de-jiafeng-zhishang-llkhbxo-63pjw-gk9pihnbfr94v|Hanji 815]] presents Duan's warning that harming loyal good people is a major taboo.
- Limited effect: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-815-kebei-gudai-zhongchen-de-jiafeng-zhishang-llkhbxo-63pjw-gk9pihnbfr94v|Hanji 815]] says Shun sends envoys and gifts after the memorial, but Pang is later dismissed.

## Qualifications

Duan appears here in relation to Pang Can; the source does not provide an independent career profile. His influence is visible as a short feedback opening, not as control over the final personnel decision.
