# Pine Drama

## Overview
Pine Drama is a ByteDance-linked overseas short-drama application used in the episode as a distribution case for Chinese production capacity.

## Current Profile
[[dafeixin-guonei-hangsi-tiaozheng-hangxian-buju-jianfeiyao-liuxing-huo-yingxiang-pijiuye-gkwriaiotragi7matv3jls]] says most producers on the platform are Chinese and that its operating team buys TikTok advertising to acquire viewers. The case links outbound content supply to platform-controlled acquisition rather than assuming overseas distribution is organic or free.

## Key Characteristics
- Overseas-facing short-drama application.
- Production base described as predominantly Chinese.
- Uses TikTok advertising as a user-acquisition channel.
