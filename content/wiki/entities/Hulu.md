# Hulu

Hulu is part of Disney's streaming bundle context in [[disney-the-renaissance-and-the-empire-1]]. The source treats Hulu less as a standalone origin story than as one piece of the post-cable aggregation problem alongside [[DisneyPlus|Disney+]] and [[ESPN]].

In the episode's framing, Disney needs enough breadth to compete in streaming without losing the premium scarcity that made its older [[EntertainmentIPFlywheel]] work. Hulu therefore belongs to the [[StreamingPlatformBundling]] and [[StreamingConsolidation]] branch: bundling can reduce churn, but it also makes Disney behave more like a general entertainment aggregator.
