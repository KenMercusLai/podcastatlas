# OvaLabs

## Overview

OvaLabs is a European fertility-biotechnology startup described in the episode as developing Embryo Protect One, a molecule inspired by shugoshin-1's chromosome-protection role in developing eggs.

## Current Profile

The company is presented as pursuing direct reinforcement of the molecular machinery that keeps paired chromosomes together during oocyte maturation. Its goal is not to increase ovarian reserve but to reduce chromosome-segregation errors before embryo formation and thereby increase viable, chromosomally normal eggs during IVF.

The profile remains preclinical. The supplied summary says the startup had not reached clinical trials and provides no study identifiers, financing, leadership, regulatory status, or human outcome data.
