# Angel Raymer

## Overview
Angel Raymer is the founder of [[SacredPawsSoapCompany]] and the third caller in [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]], calling from Bristol, Indiana about translating offline emotion into online sales.

## Current Profile
Raymer started the business in December 2025 while raising five children and working a full-time factory job. She sells self-care kits built around soap with an embedded gemstone, an affirmation tied to that stone, and an exfoliating bag that catches the stone as the soap dissolves. She works one market a month, and because soap needs six weeks to cure, production has to be planned well ahead. In person, at metaphysical expos, oddity shows, music festivals, and witch dances, customers react strongly; online, she says, the emotion and story do not carry.

## Key Characteristics
- Solo founder building a product business around limited time and a full-time job.
- Story-driven product where the gemstone, affirmation, and personal history supply the meaning.
- Offline-strong, online-weak distribution: live events produce emotional reactions that the website does not reproduce.
- Production planning constraint: six-week cure time forces long lead times and a monthly market cadence.
- Capital constraint: she can only save toward one larger project at a time.
