# The Skatepark Project

## Overview
The Skatepark Project appears in the wiki as [[TonyHawk]]’s nonprofit supporting public skatepark development in underserved communities.

## Current Profile
The current evidence presents the organization as an access and community-building project. It assists local efforts with advice, funding, and the credibility needed to develop public skateparks. Hawk links the mission to his own experience of skateparks as places where young people—especially outsiders and misfits—can gain identity, peers, purpose, and a dependable home away from home.

## Key Characteristics
- Focuses on public skateparks in underserved areas.
- Supports communities through advice, funding, and institutional credibility.
- Treats skate infrastructure as social infrastructure rather than only athletic equipment.
- Extends Hawk’s personal experience of belonging into philanthropy.
- Is described by Hawk as his proudest work.

## Evidence
- Operating role - [[harnessing-passion-drive-persistence-for-lifelong-success-tony-hawk-scim8571002701]] describes advice, grants, and credibility support for local skatepark efforts.
- Scale claim - [[harnessing-passion-drive-persistence-for-lifelong-success-tony-hawk-scim8571002701]] reports support for almost 1,000 parks, with roughly 700 or 800 open.
- Mission - [[harnessing-passion-drive-persistence-for-lifelong-success-tony-hawk-scim8571002701]] links public skateparks to community, identity, purpose, and a home away from home.
