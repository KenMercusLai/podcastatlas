# 陈举 / Chen Ju (Qi Minister)

陈举 / Chen Ju enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-69-bikan-gudai-diwang-qipa-sifa-da-pandian-lukvjsktg-pgfjhizvypwmdmw-13]] as a second Qi minister who remonstrates with [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]] after [[HuYanQiMinister|胡衍]] has already been killed. The source says Chen Ju continues to speak against Qi Min Wang's overreach and is executed at the east gate.

Chen Ju's significance is cumulative. His death shows that Hu Yan's execution was not a single angry episode but a court rule: direct correction now carries death risk, and Qi Min Wang's state loses the internal mechanism that might have restrained its external expansion.
