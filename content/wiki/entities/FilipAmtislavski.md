# Filip Amtislavski

Filip Amtislavski is the [[UniversityOfAlaska]] professor quoted in [[tech-20251224-1224-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251224-1224-mp-tech-pod-128]] about fungus-based insulation. He identifies [[FomesFomentarius]] as a fungus common across Arctic and subarctic environments and presents its foam-like qualities as a clue for [[MyceliumInsulation]].

The episode also uses Amtislavski to connect the research to place. His comment that mushrooms have long been used in the Arctic frames the material as locally legible in [[Alaska]], not only as a new lab invention.
