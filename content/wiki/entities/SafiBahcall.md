# Safi Bahcall

## Overview

Safi Bahcall is the drug-development expert whom [[MalcolmGladwell]] asks to interpret the [[RotaTeq]] package insert.

## Current Profile

In [[the-rfk-jr-problem-the-rfk-jr-problem|The RFK Jr. Problem]], Bahcall identifies the vaccine's one-versus-51 diarrhea or gastroenteritis result as extraordinary and reads the serious-adverse-event table as showing no meaningful difference from placebo. He rejects [[RobertFKennedyJr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]]'s harm claim as inconsistent with the data presented and doubts that Kennedy personally examined the primary source.

## Key Characteristics

- Drug-development veteran used as an independent reader of the package insert.
- Centers the placebo comparison when interpreting efficacy and safety.
- Distinguishes events after treatment from evidence that treatment caused them.
- Treats Kennedy's conclusion as inconsistent with the cited trial data.
