# 吴浩天 / Wu Haotian (Anesthesiologist)

## Overview
吴浩天 is introduced in the source as a deputy chief anesthesiologist and founder of [[LuffyMedicalChannel|Luffy医学频道]] who explains anesthesia and airway safety during painless gastrointestinal endoscopy.

## Current Profile
His contribution is to make the anesthesiologist's work visible inside a short outpatient procedure. He describes preoxygenation, intravenous medication, electrocardiographic, blood-pressure, and oxygen-saturation monitoring, continuous attendance, airway maneuvers, recovery assessment, and discharge safety. He also separates non-intubated endoscopy anesthesia from major-operation general anesthesia involving muscle relaxation, intubation, and ventilatory support.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains painless endoscopy through continuous anesthesia monitoring rather than a single injection.
- Centers respiratory depression, oxygenation, airway obstruction, and shared airway access as practical safety concerns.
- Distinguishes non-intubated endoscopy anesthesia from intubated major-surgery anesthesia.
- Uses accessible analogies to address fears about anesthetic drugs, memory, and recovery.
