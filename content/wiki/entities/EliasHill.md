# Elias Hill

## Overview

Elias Hill is the disabled Black Baptist preacher, Union League leader, and educator whose assault and migration from York County show the first Klan attacking community institutions as well as elections.

## Current Profile

Hill led a local Union League and opened a school for Black children in South Carolina. The source recounts that Klansmen attacked and beat him through the night in May 1871; he later joined migration to Liberia and died after contracting malaria, making his case an example of terror producing displacement even where federal force later suppressed the perpetrators' organization.

## Key Characteristics

- Black Baptist preacher and York County community leader.
- Led a Union League and supported Black education.
- Targeted despite severe physical disability, underscoring the political rather than defensive purpose of the attack.
- Left the United States for Liberia after the violence.
