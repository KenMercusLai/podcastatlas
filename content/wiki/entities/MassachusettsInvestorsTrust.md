# Massachusetts Investors Trust

Massachusetts Investors Trust appears in [[vol-126-gongmu-jijin-hai-zhide-mai-ma-lojrz1qctaqzkc2rg0lqokos-o6v]] as the first mutual-fund example. The source says it was established in Boston on March 21, 1924, still exists under the ticker MIT, and illustrates how a long-lived mutual fund can turn modest annual compounding into large lifetime wealth effects.

The episode uses the fund as a benchmark story rather than as a current recommendation. Its role is to show what the [[PublicMutualFundEcosystem|public mutual fund ecosystem]] can look like when a product survives long enough for households to use compounding, fee discipline, and institutional continuity.
