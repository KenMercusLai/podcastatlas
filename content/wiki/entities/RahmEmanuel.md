# Rahm Emanuel

[[RahmEmanuel|Rahm Emanuel]] is the guest in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-rahm-emanuel-trumps-foreign-policy-china-europes-decline-immigration-dsa-vs-democrats-42416310]], where [[AllIn|All-In]] presents him as a former [[BillClinton|Clinton]] adviser, [[BarackObama|Obama]] chief of staff, Chicago mayor, and Biden ambassador to [[Japan]].

The source frames Emanuel as a pragmatic [[DemocraticParty|Democratic Party]] operator. He defends government action but stresses trust, accountability, and long-run execution; he supports immigration but insists on border and workplace enforcement; and he treats education, research, immigration, alliances, and industrial capacity as the real base of American power.

His strongest foreign-policy contribution is [[AlliedEconomicBlocAgainstChina]]. Emanuel argues that the [[UnitedStates|United States]] should use allies, institutions, economic statecraft, and research capacity to counter [[China]] rather than rely on unilateral tariffs or isolation. Domestically, he connects [[PragmaticImmigrationCompromise]], [[EducationWorkforcePipeline]], and tax reform into a growth agenda, while sharply criticizing the [[DemocraticSocialistsOfAmerica|Democratic Socialists of America]] as a separate party that should run separately.
