# 濮阳 / Puyang (Late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] adds two earlier Puyang uses before the Yanzhou-rebellion branch. [[CaoCao|曹操]] defeats [[BaiRaoLateHan|白绕]] around Puyang while entering [[DongCommandery|东郡]], and [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]] is carried there as a hostage by a [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] chanyu after conflict with [[YuanShao|袁绍]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-975-yanzhou-zhiluan-xunyu-ruhe-bang-caocao-shou-dabenying-ln0hh4jrokkwtqsmxmqhdjzcosdr|Hanji 975]] adds Puyang as the city exposed by the first emergency response to the [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] rebellion. [[XunYu|荀彧]] recalls [[XiahouDun|夏侯惇]] from Puyang to [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]], and [[LyuBu|吕布]] then occupies Puyang while [[CaoCao|曹操]] is still away against [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]].

The episode also attaches a source-scoped information-operation story to Puyang: an old-woman informant and a later street rhyme are presented as Xun Yu's way of unsettling Lü Bu's newly occupied city. The wiki keeps this as the episode's dramatic [[BattlefieldInformationControl|战场信息控制]] claim, separate from the later [[PuyangBattleLateHan|濮阳之战]] mechanics in Hanji 980.

濮阳 / Puyang (Late Han) is the [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] city where [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-980-lvbu-xiansha-caocao-kankan-yi-ju-hua-ruhe-niuzhuan-lishi-lgggw3vmonu-3wvzabvg943v5qcg|Hanji 980]] places [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s near-fatal confrontation with [[LyuBu|吕布]]. The episode describes Cao Cao entering after believing [[PuyangTianClan|濮阳田氏]]'s inside-response signal and then burning the east gate, which makes the later withdrawal more dangerous.

This page is distinct from [[PuyangWarringStates|蒲阳]], the Warring States place used in an earlier Qin diplomatic maneuver. The late-Han Puyang page is tied to the Cao-Lü Yanzhou branch and to [[PuyangBattleLateHan|濮阳之战]] rather than to the Qin-Wei concession story.
