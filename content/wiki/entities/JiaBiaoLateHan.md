## Overview

Jia Biao is a late-Han official, Taixue reputation leader, and party-prohibition rescuer. His current wiki role now spans early public-opinion leadership with [[GuoTaiLateHan|郭泰]], refusal to shelter [[CenZhiLateHan|岑晊]] when doing so would transfer political liability to helpers, Xinxi enforcement against local infanticide, and targeted rescue of imprisoned士人 through [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]].

## Current Profile

Jia Biao's earliest current layer belongs to [[QingyiReputationPressure|清议声誉压力]]. Hanji 871 places him and Guo Tai at the head of a large Taixue student-scholarly field that praises Li Ying, Chen Fan, and Wang Chang while making officials fear criticism. Jia Biao is therefore already a reputation actor before the source sequence turns to prison rescue.

Hanji 874 gives him a two-sided timing profile. In the fugitive-shelter scene, he refuses to take in Cen Zhi after [[ChengJinLateHan|成瑨]] and [[LiuZhiTaiyuanLateHan|刘质]] die in prison, because Cen Zhi had helped create the danger by pressing his superior and should not now implicate others. The formula "相时而动，无累后人" makes Jia Biao a judge of both moment and collateral risk: action is not virtue if it merely passes the cost onto people who did not choose the danger.

The same source also gives Jia Biao a local-governance branch. As Xinxi magistrate, he confronts a custom of killing infants, treats child-killing as homicide, and prioritizes a mother-child killing case over an ordinary bandit killing because the former violates the most basic moral relation. The source says bandits surrender and thousands of children later grow up under the changed custom, remembered as "Jia father's sons."

Hanji 876 supplies the later rescue branch. After [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]]'s removal frightens officials away from pleading for [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]], [[FanPangLateHan|范滂]], and the other prisoners, Jia Biao does not submit a direct memorial or stage public protest. He finds Dou Wu, whose family status and court access can actually move [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]'s decision field. Together, the two episodes make Jia Biao a source case for both [[InfanticideCustomCorrection|杀婴风俗纠偏]] and ethically constrained [[PowerStructureTargeting|权力结构定向求援]].

## Key Characteristics

- Taixue reputation leader whose public judgment helps make officials fear scholar criticism.
- Timing judge who distinguishes necessary action from action that merely transfers danger to helpers.
- Collateral-risk thinker whose refusal to shelter Cen Zhi is grounded in not implicating later people.
- Local magistrate who uses law to reverse normalized infant-killing rather than accepting custom as excuse.
- Moral-order enforcer who prioritizes the mother-child killing case as the deeper social rupture.
- Party-prisoner rescuer who later chooses targeted intercession through Dou Wu instead of expressive protest.
- Low-visibility actor whose value lies in knowing when not to act directly and when to activate the right channel.

## Qualifications

The current page remains source-bounded to Hanji 871, Hanji 874, and Hanji 876 rather than a complete Jia Biao biography. Hanji 871 clarifies the Taixue reputation layer and supports normalizing the earlier transcript's Chen Zhi to [[CenZhiLateHan|岑晊]]. Zhang Mu, Cai Yan, Liu Yu, Chen Xiang, and the exact Xinxi place remain source-scoped pending stronger disambiguation. The transcript form "假标" is normalized to Jia Biao / 贾彪 because the sequence and later Hanji 876 route match the established page.

## What Changed
