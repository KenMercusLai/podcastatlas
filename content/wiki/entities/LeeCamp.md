# Lee Camp

## Overview

Lee Camp is the theologian and Church of Christ insider whose account in Part 1 of [[TheAlabamaMurders]] explains both the tradition's formative gifts and its capacity to produce fear and shame.

## Current Profile

Camp describes a religious world that gave him love, intellectual formation, and belonging while teaching many members that theirs was the restored and therefore true church. He argues that certainty, exclusion, and the threat of disfellowshipping could become harmful when overwhelming shame was not met by a constructive understanding of grace. The episode uses that analysis to interpret the pressures around [[CharlesSennett]], not to establish Sennett's motive or condemn every congregation.

## Key Characteristics

- Theologian presented as an insider to the Church of Christ tradition.
- Explains the historical claim that the church restored the New Testament model.
- Credits the tradition with love, intellectual seriousness, formation, and belonging.
- Criticizes fear, exclusion, and disfellowshipping as sources of harm.
- Connects overwhelming shame without constructive grace to dangerous psychological consequences.
