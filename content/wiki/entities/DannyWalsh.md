## Overview
Danny Walsh is the founder of [[PeakStateCoffee]], a functional-coffee company combining conventional coffee ritual with adaptogenic mushroom extracts.

## Current Profile
Walsh is presented as a founder trying to finance growth without weakening product and environmental commitments. His company has early direct-to-consumer traction, but the episode treats the financing decision as a sequencing problem: clarify non-negotiables, strengthen repeatable economics, and determine whether outside capital is necessary before accepting its constraints.

## Key Characteristics
- Centers the company on organic sourcing, conservation giving, and B Corp status.
- Wants functional ingredients without abandoning conventional coffee taste and ritual.
- Has built a primarily direct-to-consumer sales base with subscriptions and farmers-market exposure.
- Is considering debt, equity, and continued organic growth.
- Frames growth as a mission-protection problem rather than only a capital-access problem.
