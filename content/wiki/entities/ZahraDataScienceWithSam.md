# Dr. Zahra (Data Science With Sam)

## Overview
Dr. Zahra is the first-name-only guest in [[ep-52-the-ai-ceo-leadership-governance-and-the-ai-gold-rush|Data Science With Sam EP52]]. The source introduces her as co-founder and CEO of [[IGenticAI|iGentic AI]], author of [[TheAICEOBook|The AI CEO]], and a practitioner whose background spans biochemistry, strategy, regulated industries, AI transformation, policy, governance, and executive leadership.

## Current Profile
Zahra's leadership frame is augmentation with retained responsibility. Leaders can use AI to research, prepare, communicate, generate options, and accelerate execution, but they remain responsible for defining the problem, challenging output, calibrating risk, and making the final decision. She extends this into an ownership argument: time saved by AI matters strategically when it becomes intellectual property, market access, visibility, negotiating power, income, or influence rather than only more throughput.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames the “AI CEO” as an AI-enabled human leader rather than an artificial replacement executive.
- Treats good questions, output challenge, judgment, courage, empathy, and accountability as core leadership capabilities.
- Advocates starting adoption from a real problem, measurable outcome, and selective deployment.
- Presents AI as a possible ownership, visibility, and capital-access tool for women, not only a workplace productivity assistant.
- Connects regulated-industry adoption to governance, compliance, trust, and identifiable human decision ownership.
