# 屈侯鲋 / Qu Houfu

屈侯鲋 / Qu Houfu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-1-lk-6ejgmjhvukqgtlz2lowu8qx-v]] as one of [[DiHuang|翟黄]]'s recommended people. When [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] needs a teacher for his son, Di Huang says he recommended Qu Houfu.

The episode gives Qu Houfu little independent biography, so this page stays source-scoped. His role is structural: he helps define Di Huang's claim to merit through referrals, while [[LiKe|李克]]'s reply distinguishes useful recommendations like Qu Houfu from the higher-status scholar attraction associated with [[WeiCheng|魏成]], [[ZiXia|子夏]], [[TianZifang|田子方]], and [[DuanGanmu|段干木]].
