# Liberia

Liberia is the central country case in [[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]]'s [[WestAfricaCocaineTransit]] segment. The episode describes a raid on a bungalow between the main airport and [[Monrovia]] that found suitcase-sized cocaine packages reportedly worth more than $300 million.

The source uses Liberia to show why major seizures do not automatically prove control. [[GregoryColeman]] says public trust is low because officers and state actors have been identified or charged, while [[OriOgunbiyi]] argues that low official pay, weak port and maritime technology, and high European demand make cartels hard to stop.
