# Bacha Coffee

## Overview
Bacha Coffee is the luxury-positioned coffee retailer used in the source as a cautionary example about inferring product quality from story, packaging, location, and price.

## Current Profile
The episode describes a Beijing China World Mall visit, premium prices, orange luxury styling, historical-origin messaging, retail coffee goods, and a long printed shelf life. [[JenniferCoffee|珍妮花]] interprets the aroma sample and product dating as evidence that luxury signals did not establish freshness, but the wiki retains this as one speaker's store-level observation rather than a general verdict on the brand.

## Key Characteristics
- Is presented through luxury retail design and high-end mall placement.
- Uses historical storytelling and premium packaging as prominent brand signals.
- Combines cafe service with packaged coffee retail.
- Serves as the episode's example of a possible gap between symbolic premium and perceived freshness.

## Evidence
- Retail positioning: [[kafei-xiaoketang-kouliangdou-zenme-mai-zui-huasuan-yifen-gei-putongren-de-kafei-bikeng-zhinan-gkwrijiodf2uarzatatuffjr]] describes high-end location, orange visual design, premium prices, and cafe-plus-retail format.
- Narrative signal: [[kafei-xiaoketang-kouliangdou-zenme-mai-zui-huasuan-yifen-gei-putongren-de-kafei-bikeng-zhinan-gkwrijiodf2uarzatatuffjr]] reports the brand's 1910 Marrakech framing and the speaker's skeptical interpretation of it.
- Freshness criticism: [[kafei-xiaoketang-kouliangdou-zenme-mai-zui-huasuan-yifen-gei-putongren-de-kafei-bikeng-zhinan-gkwrijiodf2uarzatatuffjr]] records the speaker's negative aroma judgment and concern about production dates and a three-year shelf life.

## Qualifications
The source does not independently verify the brand's corporate history, registration, ownership, or freshness across products and stores. Prices, dates, aroma, and condition describe one reported visit and should not be generalized without further evidence.
