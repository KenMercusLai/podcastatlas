# Audemars Piguet

Audemars Piguet appears in [[rolex-1]] as a high-end Swiss watch comparison for [[Rolex]]. The hosts use it with [[PatekPhilippe]] and [[VacheronConstantin]] to distinguish low-volume high horology from Rolex's more industrial, higher-volume luxury model.

The source-scoped point is that luxury has more than one operating shape. Audemars Piguet reinforces the contrast behind [[HighVolumeLuxuryOperator]] because Rolex can produce far more watches while still managing status, access, and brand trust.
