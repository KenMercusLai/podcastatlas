# 田巿 / Tian Shi

田巿 / Tian Shi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-143-xiangyu-shi-ruhe-feijin-xinji-nongsi-chuhuaiwang-mixin-lstfzfvbd2h9i39f7y5dxrbxxwv2|Hanji 143]] as the existing Qi king who is moved in [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s post-Qin settlement. The episode says Xiang Yu changes him into Jiaodong king while giving the central Qi kingship to [[TianDu|田都]] and creating a Jibei kingship for [[TianAn|田安]].

Tian Shi's role is mainly distributive. His reassignment shows Xiang Yu's willingness to break existing restored-state titles and reorder Qi around who cooperated with his westward campaign and who did not.
