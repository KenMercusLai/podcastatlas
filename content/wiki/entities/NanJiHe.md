# 南基贺 / Nan Jihe

## Overview
南基贺 is a Seoul-based psychiatrist and mental-health guest in [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episodes on school and workplace power, intimacy, Korean youth pressure, and Chinese residents' mental-health experience. VOL.198's title renders his name as 南基赫; its body and the earlier sources use 南基贺, so the wiki preserves one stable identity while recording the spelling variation.

## Current Profile
Across the source set, 南基贺 works as a clinical interpreter of distress within relationships and social structure. VOL.214 uses [[TrueEducationKDrama|《铁拳教育》/《真教育》]] to connect parent pressure, student violence, teacher collapse, workplace coercion, and victim self-blame while rejecting retaliatory violence. Its practical response combines self-worth separation, low emotional feedback, evidence preservation, complaint routes, expression, and professional help.

VOL.202 adds intimacy. Using [[LoveTranslationKDrama|《爱情怎么翻译》]], he reads push-pull behavior through shame and attachment: a person may reject first to avoid being rejected. He connects reduced capacity for relationships to source-scoped Korean “three-give-up” and “five-give-up” pressure, while treating relationships as mirrors that can reveal behavior and attachment patterns.

VOL.198 moves into practice observation. It distinguishes pressure after employment from simple joblessness by joining workplace recognition, bullying, Seoul housing prices, marriage costs, and uncertainty. For Chinese patients, it emphasizes mother-tongue disclosure, language isolation, family expectations, age-specific student context, bodily presentations, delayed help-seeking, and the limits of generalizing from a Xiaohongshu-recruited patient sample.

## Key Characteristics
- Interprets distress through psychiatric, developmental, relational, and social-structure lenses.
- Keeps popular labels such as workplace PUA and ADHD close to concrete behavior, history, context, and function.
- Treats bullying as a power-relationship problem rather than evidence of personal weakness.
- Recommends nonviolent coping through self-worth separation, low emotional feedback, records, complaint routes, disclosure, and professional help.
- Reads intimacy difficulties through shame, attachment, indirect communication, work pressure, and tolerance of difference.
- Treats language, migration setting, family relations, education stage, and healthcare access as part of psychiatric context.
- Keeps Korean social, legal, medical, and cross-cultural claims source-scoped rather than universal.

## Qualifications
The wiki has no independent biographical or credential evidence for 南基贺 beyond these episode notes. His practice observations are affected by Chinese-language access, Xiaohongshu recruitment, and a young student-heavy patient pool. Korean youth pressure, complaint routes, suicide and emergency observations, consultation fees, insurance, public-private hospital roles, referral behavior, medication attitudes, and group differences remain source-scoped. The episodes are public discussion, not individualized psychiatric, medical, legal, workplace, crisis, insurance, or relationship advice.
