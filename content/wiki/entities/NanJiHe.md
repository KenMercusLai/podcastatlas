# 南基贺 / Nan Jihe

## Overview
南基贺 is a Seoul-based psychiatrist and mental-health guest in a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode on school authority conflict, workplace bullying, and psychological boundaries.

## Current Profile
The source presents 南基贺 as a clinical interpreter rather than a media critic. He uses [[TrueEducationKDrama|《铁拳教育》/《真教育》]] to explain why scenes of parent pressure, student violence, teacher collapse, and workplace coercion feel psychologically recognizable, while repeatedly warning that real-world response cannot rely on retaliatory violence.

His practical frame connects individual distress to social structure. He reads "for your good" control as objectification, boss pressure as partly rooted in organizational interests and transferred accountability, and victim self-blame as something to interrupt through [[PsychologicalBoundaryProtection|psychological boundary protection]].

## Key Characteristics
- Interprets drama scenes through psychiatric, developmental, and social-structure lenses.
- Keeps popular labels such as workplace PUA close to concrete behavior rather than casual diagnosis.
- Treats bullying as a power-relationship problem, not only as a personal weakness or personality flaw.
- Recommends practical coping through self-worth separation, low emotional feedback, evidence preservation, complaint routes, expression, and professional help.
- Keeps Korean legal and workplace complaint examples source-scoped rather than universal.
