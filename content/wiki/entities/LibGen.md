# LibGen

## Overview
LibGen appears in the wiki as the pirated-book repository named in the All-In discussion of [[Anthropic]]'s source-reported copyright settlement.

## Current Profile
The current source-scoped profile is narrow: LibGen is not being analyzed as a full institution here; it is the acquisition-path marker that separates a training-data fair-use argument from a piracy-based settlement posture. The episode uses LibGen to argue that downloading pirated books is legally and reputationally different from buying books and then litigating whether model training is fair use.

## Key Characteristics
- Pirated-book repository in the source's account of Anthropic's training-data dispute.
- Acquisition-path marker separating piracy claims from broader fair-use training claims.
- Legitimacy risk amplifier because it changes the story from learning from texts to obtaining texts unlawfully.
- Evidence node connecting AI copyright litigation to output-training hypocrisy arguments.
