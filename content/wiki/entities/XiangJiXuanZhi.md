# 《项脊轩志》

《项脊轩志》 is [[GuiYouguang|归有光]]'s prose work read closely in [[164-guwen-de-liliang-qing-shou-xia-zhe-fen-qiannian-wei-bian-de-shenqing-dongjian-he-yongqi-940236576]]. The episode says the piece was written in two layers, with an early account of the repaired small room and family memories later joined to a supplement about his deceased wife.

The source's reading turns the room into an emotional archive. Its leaking roof, partition walls, grandmother's hopes, deleted allusions to ambition, wife's casual visits, and the final loquat tree all make [[ClassicalProseReading]] a practice of noticing small details before extracting a theme.

## Key Claims
- The piece is not only sad nostalgia; it also contains youthful aspiration and examination pressure.
- The wife's casual entry into the study is read as evidence of concrete intimacy rather than abstract marital virtue.
- The final tree image is powerful because the grief is transferred to an object and a span of time.
