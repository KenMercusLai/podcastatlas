# 袭肃 / Xi Su (Late Han)

## Overview
袭肃 / Xi Su enters the wiki as a commander who surrenders to [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s side after Red Cliffs and retains his troops because [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] argues for trust-based incorporation.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1109 says Xi Su reads his position as a gateway whose rear can be cut off and judges Wu's recent victories credible enough to justify early surrender. When [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] proposes adding Xi Su's force to Lyu Meng's command, Lyu Meng instead argues that a capable, sincere defector should keep and strengthen his own troops; Sun Quan agrees.

## Key Characteristics
- Makes a survival and opportunity judgment before isolation becomes complete.
- Brings an intact military unit rather than only personal allegiance.
- Becomes a test case for whether a receiving regime converts surrender into trust or disarms it into dependence.

## Qualifications
The current profile is bounded to one surrender-and-incorporation episode. The source's inferred motives—preserving troops and seeking opportunity—are plausible readings rather than a complete biography.
