# Success Academies

Success Academies appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-dan-loeb-the-lost-art-of-short-selling-and-why-stock-picking-is-back-41552605]] as the New York charter-school network that [[DanLoeb|Dan Loeb]] says drew him into education reform and then criminal-justice reform. In his account, education matters because children without strong intellectual tools face structural barriers to opportunity.

The source uses Success Academies less as a full institutional case and more as the bridge from school reform to [[CriminalJusticeClemencyFramework]]. Loeb's argument is that opportunity, schooling, criminal justice, and individual rehabilitation are connected, but the source does not independently evaluate charter-school outcomes.
