# 刘恒 / Emperor Wen of Han

刘恒 / Emperor Wen of Han enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-117-1-papa-dalian-lishishang-shuangwen-de-kaishan-bizu-lr0h-kfeguvatattnj-mz8fgvnc]] as the later fulfillment of [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负]]'s prediction about [[BaoJi|薄姬]]. The episode says Wei Bao hears that Bao Ji will bear a future royal or imperial son, but the child who matters is Liu Heng, son of [[LiuBang|刘邦]], not a Wei heir.

The source places Liu Heng's accession in 180 BCE and uses it to explain why Xu Fu's physiognomy becomes memorable. In this wiki branch he is not yet a full Western Han ruler biography; he is the historical payoff that turns [[WeiBao|魏豹]]'s self-interested interpretation into an ironic misreading.
