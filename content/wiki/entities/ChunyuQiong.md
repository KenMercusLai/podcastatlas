# 淳于琼 / Chunyu Qiong

淳于琼 / Chunyu Qiong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] as a [[YuanShao|袁绍]] adviser who joins [[GuoTu|郭图]] in opposing [[JuShou|沮授]]'s recommendation to bring [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] to Yuan Shao's base. The objection is practical and reputational: once the emperor is nearby, Yuan Shao may be trapped between carrying out unwanted commands and looking disloyal if he refuses.

The source uses Chunyu Qiong to make Yuan Shao's choice intelligible without endorsing it. His warning identifies a real control problem in hosting a sovereign, but the host's interpretation is that Yuan Shao still misses the larger [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] and [[LegitimacyWindowSeizure|合法性窗口抢占]] upside.
