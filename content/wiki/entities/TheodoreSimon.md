# Theodore Simon / 西奥多·西蒙

Theodore Simon / 西奥多·西蒙 appears in [[37-zhishang-ceshi-qingwen-ni-shi-zhili-poluomen-ma-608020679]] as [[AlfredBinet|Alfred Binet / 阿尔弗雷德·宾内]]'s collaborator in the early child intelligence-testing work. The episode places their test in the context of public schooling: the initial purpose was to notice developmental mismatch and provide educational support, not to create a permanent human hierarchy.

For the wiki, Simon mainly anchors the collaborative and institutional nature of [[IntelligenceTesting|intelligence testing]]. The Binet-Simon branch shows that a measurement device can begin as diagnosis for help and later become [[PsychometricSocialSorting|psychometric social sorting]] when other institutions use the score for placement, exclusion, or status.
