# Bharatiya Janata Party

The Bharatiya Janata Party appears in [[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]] as the ruling-party context around [[NarendraModi]] and the youth-led [[CockroachMovement]]. The source says protesters deliberately used patriotic symbols because BJP politics often frames critics as anti-national.

The episode also keeps the party's strength in view. Even after [[DharmendraPradhan]]'s resignation, the source describes the BJP as politically strong and the opposition as weak and divided, so the protest victory is treated as a breach in invulnerability rather than a full political realignment.

[[settler-bet-a-west-bank-flashpoint-6a842bb539e73ea8f3c13a2d]] adds the BJP through state-level [[UniformCivilCodeIndia|uniform civil-code]] politics. [[KiraHuyu|Kira Huju]] says BJP-ruled states present the codes as universal rights, while the practical effect often pressures [[IndianMuslims]] to conform to majority-rooted family-law norms, the pattern captured by [[MajoritarianLegalUniformity]].
