# Bharatiya Janata Party

## Overview

The Bharatiya Janata Party is India's ruling-party context across the wiki's youth-protest, uniform-civil-code, and Bangladesh-border enforcement branches.

## Current Profile

The protest source presents a strong national party whose patriotic framing can cast critics as anti-national, but which was still forced into a rare ministerial resignation by youth pressure. The civil-code source shows BJP states presenting equal-law reform while imposing much of the practical change on [[IndianMuslims]]. The border source adds aggressive migration and security enforcement in [[WestBengal]], where religious identity and citizenship suspicion can overlap.

## Key Characteristics

- Remains politically strong despite episodic public-pressure defeats.
- Uses patriotic and national-security framing across dissent and migration politics.
- Advances uniform-law reforms with both women's-rights gains and majoritarian risks.
- Is associated in the newer source with expanded border intelligence and informal pushbacks.
- Faces a recurring legitimacy test over whether formally national policy burdens minorities unevenly.
