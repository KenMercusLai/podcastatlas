# Euploid Biotechnologies

## Overview

Euploid Biotechnologies is a European fertility-biotechnology startup described in the episode as developing Liventa, an experimental molecule intended to protect chromosome cohesion during egg maturation.

## Current Profile

The reported approach is indirect. Rather than imitate shugoshin-1, Liventa is said to occupy the enzyme that would otherwise degrade this chromosome-protecting molecule. The intended downstream result is fewer segregation errors and more chromosomally normal eggs available during IVF.

The profile remains preclinical. The supplied summary says the startup had not reached clinical trials and does not provide primary research, leadership, regulatory, financing, or outcome records.

## Key Characteristics

- European startup focused on chromosomally normal eggs for IVF.
- Developer of the reported Liventa molecule.
- Uses an enzyme-diversion rather than direct-protection strategy.
- Targets the preservation of shugoshin-1 activity during egg maturation.
- Remains preclinical in the supplied episode summary.
