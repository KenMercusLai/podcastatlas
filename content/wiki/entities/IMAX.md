# IMAX

IMAX appears in [[aodesai-ba-baomihua-zhuangjin-teluoyi-muma-haolaiwu-dapian-weihe-zhadui-dingzhi-baomihuatong-1004599628]] through the special [[TheOdyssey|《奥德赛》]] popcorn bucket shaped like an IMAX film camera. The source says this version was sold through a domestic IMAX mini-program, making the premium format itself part of the collectible object.

In this source, IMAX is not only a projection or screen technology reference. It becomes a brand surface for [[CustomMoviePopcornBuckets|custom movie popcorn buckets]], where the camera shape signals premium theatrical spectacle and gives fans a physical reminder of the viewing format.
