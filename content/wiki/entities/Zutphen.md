# Zutphen

Zutphen appears in [[695-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-the-shadow-war-part-5-glt5637968455]] as the September 1586 battle site where [[PhilipSidney|Sir Philip Sidney]] receives his fatal wound. The episode says [[RobertDudley|Robert Dudley / Earl of Leicester]] had captured Duisburg and then moved against Zutphen before [[AlexanderFarnese|Alexander Farnese / Duke of Parma]] sent a relief force.

Sidney takes part in repeated cavalry charges and is hit above the left knee by a musket ball on 22 September 1586. He is carried to Arnhem, surgeons fail to extract the bullet, gangrene sets in, and he dies on 17 October at age 31.

For the wiki, Zutphen is the place where tactical incident turns into [[ProtestantHeroicMartyrdom|Protestant heroic martyrdom]]. The source does not treat Sidney's action as strategically decisive; it treats his death and funeral as symbolic fuel for [[England]] as war with [[Spain]] approaches.
