# Donna Guthrie

## Overview
Donna Guthrie is an author, educator, filmmaker, and community storyteller whose request for [[ChatGPT]] to draft her obituary anchors a [[MarketplaceTech]] episode about memory, self-narration, and digital intimacy.

## Current Profile
After her brother's death and around her 80th birthday, Guthrie used a chatbot she had interacted with for roughly two years to preview how her life might be summarized. She found the result accurate enough to preserve as a family-editable template, but also strange and emotionally uncomfortable. Her case illustrates both the appeal and unease of [[AIAsPersonalMirror]]: accumulated everyday context can return as a coherent life story before the user fully appreciates what the system appears to retain.

## Key Characteristics
- Author and educator with journalism and fine-arts training.
- Creator across young-reader books, plays, podcasts, and documentary work.
- Community-oriented storyteller whose prior chatbot interactions included invitations, parties, and personal interests.
- Pragmatic about the generated obituary as a draft rather than a finished memorial.
- Ambivalent about recommending the experiment because writing or reading one's own obituary is inherently uncomfortable.
