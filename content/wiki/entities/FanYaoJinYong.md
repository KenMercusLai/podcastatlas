## Overview

范遥（金庸小说） / Fan Yao is a Mingjiao leader in [[HeavenSwordDragonSaber|《倚天屠龙记》]] whose long disguise as 苦头陀 produces an ambiguous record of silence, delayed activation, decisive rescue, self-punishment, and cross-camp attachment.

## Current Profile

The episode refuses to equate duration undercover with effectiveness. Fan Yao says he watched 成昆 for Mingjiao, yet the source identifies no regular handler or intelligence channel and emphasizes that he did not warn the fractured organization before its near destruction at 光明顶. This supports readings ranging from failed practice to disillusioned waiting, but it does not establish that his original motives were false.

His record changes at 万安寺. Internal access, help obtaining the antidote, and cooperation with 张无忌 make the rescue of the six sects plausible and politically valuable. Cutting off two fingers then functions as visible accountability and a loyalty signal, although its complete meaning remains interpretive. His later rapport with 赵敏 also resists a clean double-agent stereotype: deception, service to Mingjiao, and genuine interpersonal regard coexist.

## Key Characteristics

- Sustains a demanding deaf-mute disguise and preserves long-term access around 赵敏.
- Provides little visible reporting or warning before Mingjiao's existential crisis.
- Becomes operationally decisive during the 万安寺 rescue.
- Uses bodily self-punishment as a public signal of accountability and renewed loyalty.
- Maintains relationships across the line of deception rather than leaving only hatred.
- Combines patience, calculation, political tact, and unresolved private motive.

## Qualifications

The source does not prove why Fan Yao first left Mingjiao, whether rejection by 黛绮丝 affected the decision, whether he poisoned 韩千叶, or whether waiting for a worthy leader explains his silence. Modern espionage criteria clarify the debate but are not the novel's own explicit institutional standard. His 万安寺 success does not retroactively supply missing reports, while earlier failure does not erase later service.
