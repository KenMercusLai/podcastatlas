## Overview

Lillie Langtry, known as the Jersey Lily, was a celebrated beauty, royal mistress, artists' model, and actress who converted metropolitan notoriety into a transatlantic career.

## Current Profile

[[97-top-ten-mistresses-glt8855582591|Top Ten Mistresses]] follows Langtry from Jersey to London, where elite attention, painted images, and a relationship with the Prince of Wales made her famous. Unlike figures whose security depended entirely on one patron, she used scandal and recognizability to build an acting career that extended to the United States. The episode therefore treats her as a prototype of modern celebrity produced through the interaction of beauty, media, elite access, and commercial performance.

## Key Characteristics

- Jersey-born social figure known publicly as the Jersey Lily.
- Artists' model and celebrated beauty whose image circulated before her stage career.
- Mistress of the Prince of Wales, later Edward VII.
- Actress who converted social notoriety into commercial and transatlantic celebrity.
