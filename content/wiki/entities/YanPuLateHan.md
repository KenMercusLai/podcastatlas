# 阎圃 / Yan Pu (late Han)

## Overview
阎圃 / Yan Pu is [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]]'s adviser in the Hanji 1055 Hanzhong branch. His importance is not a separate power base, but the strategic counsel that turns Zhang Lu away from claiming a Hanning kingship when the name would add danger without adding real control.

## Current Profile
Yan Pu appears after a jade-seal omen raises pressure for Zhang Lu to claim kingship. He acknowledges that [[Hanzhong|汉中]] has large population, fertile land, production, and defensible terrain, but argues that Zhang Lu can already preserve autonomy or wait for a chance to support the Han emperor. In that setting, a royal title would make Zhang Lu more exposed while adding little to the substance he already holds. Zhang Lu accepts the counsel and does not become king.

## Key Characteristics
- Reads title adoption through risk and timing rather than prestige alone.
- Treats real territorial capacity as more important than immediate status inflation.
- Keeps Zhang Lu's autonomy compatible with nominal Han accommodation.
- Functions as a restraint adviser inside a religious-administrative Hanzhong regime.
