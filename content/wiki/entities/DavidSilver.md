# David Silver

## Overview
David Silver is a DeepMind-linked reinforcement-learning researcher whose wiki profile connects the AlphaGo-era route to later debates over Google DeepMind talent movement and language-model competition.

## Current Profile
The earlier source places Silver in the DeepMind story as a central reinforcement-learning collaborator around [[DemisHassabis]], games, and the path from game environments to broader AI ambition. His profile therefore anchors the older DeepMind belief that agents could learn powerful behavior through experience, search, and reinforcement rather than only through language-model scaling.

The newest source uses Silver differently. Zhou Jiangong treats his departure from Google DeepMind as one early signal that the post-ChatGPT organization and route choice were creating pressure inside the lab. That claim is source-scoped, but it makes Silver part of the broader [[GoogleDeepMindOrganizationalFriction]] pattern.

## Key Characteristics
- Reinforcement-learning researcher associated with DeepMind's game and AlphaGo-era trajectory.
- Evidence point for the older experience/search path toward AGI.
- Contrast case for later language-model scaling and Gemini-era commercialization pressure.
- Source-scoped talent signal in the newer Google DeepMind reshuffle narrative.
