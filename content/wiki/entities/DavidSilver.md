# David Silver

David Silver appears in [[e226-liaoliao-deepmind-chuangshiren-hasabisi-yige-kexuejia-yu-shikong-de-ai-jingsai-7abda28b-99c6-4ebc-8c0d-37bcc77f6a73]] as an early collaborator of [[DemisHassabis]] in games and later as a key figure in [[DeepMind]]'s reinforcement-learning route. The source says Silver aligned with [[RichardSutton]]'s reinforcement-learning theory and studied at the University of Alberta.

In the episode's narrative, Silver helps connect game intelligence to [[AlphaGo]] and the broader [[ReinforcementLearningAGIPath]]. That makes him part of the contrast with [[LanguageModelScalingBet]]: DeepMind's early conviction was that agents learning from environments and rewards could generalize toward AGI, while language models later shifted the competitive center.
