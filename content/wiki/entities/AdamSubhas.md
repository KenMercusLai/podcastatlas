# Adam Subhas

Adam Subhas is the [[WoodsHoleOceanographicInstitution]] researcher in [[tech-20260728-0728-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260728-0728-mp-tech-pod-128]] who studies [[OceanAlkalinityEnhancement]]. He describes alkaline materials such as olivine as acting like an "antacid" for the ocean by neutralizing CO2.

Subhas also gives the episode its cautionary boundary. He frames the method as an acceleration of natural geological processes, but says research still needs to test whether it can scale, happen fast enough for climate needs, and avoid unacceptable consequences or tradeoffs.
