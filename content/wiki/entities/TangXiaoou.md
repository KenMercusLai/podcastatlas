# 汤晓鸥 / Tang Xiao'ou

Tang Xiao'ou / 汤晓鸥 appears in [[147-he-mayi-lingbo-shenyujun-liao-jiqiren-yuansheng-jichu-moxing-danao-he-benti-de-guanxi-yuxunlian-yu-shuju-scale-up-laoshi-tangxiaoou-luxtyuafi-2onim15fw6lpypo2ga]] as [[ShenYujun|沈宇军]]'s teacher and the leader of the [[ChineseUniversityOfHongKong|香港中文大学]] research team Shen joined after earlier exposure to [[SenseTime|商汤]]. Shen recalls Tang as humorous and unusually good at finding the main contradiction in chaotic technical situations.

In this source, Tang's role is lineage rather than a detailed technical argument. The episode uses him to locate Shen inside the Chinese computer-vision and generation-model tradition that later feeds into [[EmbodiedAI]] and robot-brain work.
