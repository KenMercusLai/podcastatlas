# 窦瑰 / Dou Gui (Eastern Han)

## Overview

窦瑰 / Dou Gui appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-766-lishi-jiaogei-ni-weishenme-yao-guyi-fa-piqi-lrs-mguyfmnxe970hdwjvmevl8oc|Hanji 766]] as a Dou-family member caught in the anti-[[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] purge but saved by [[ZhangPuEasternHan|Zhang Pu]]'s evidence-specific memorial. His importance is that the purge is not portrayed as blind revenge against everyone with the Dou name.

## Current Profile

Dou Gui's profile is a narrow exception inside the Dou family collapse. Zhang Pu argues that Dou Gui kept his retainers disciplined, stayed loyal, and should not be destroyed simply because courtiers who flattered Dou Xian before the purge now find it convenient to condemn every Dou relative.

That profile makes Dou Gui useful for distinguishing regime self-protection from indiscriminate factional vengeance. He survives because Zhang Pu persuades Emperor He that evidence should be individualized even in a high-risk cleanup.

## Key Characteristics

- Dou-family member threatened by the post-purge punishment wave.
- Presented as more disciplined than the household's main conspiratorial or abusive actors.
- Saved through Zhang Pu's willingness to criticize opportunistic post-purge denunciations.
- Marks Emperor He's ability, at least in this case, to combine severity against the bloc with selective mercy.

## Evidence

- Purge context: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-766-lishi-jiaogei-ni-weishenme-yao-guyi-fa-piqi-lrs-mguyfmnxe970hdwjvmevl8oc|Hanji 766]] sends Dou Xian and his brothers to their fiefs after stripping Dou Xian's general-in-chief seal.
- Zhang Pu's defense: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-766-lishi-jiaogei-ni-weishenme-yao-guyi-fa-piqi-lrs-mguyfmnxe970hdwjvmevl8oc|Hanji 766]] has Zhang Pu argue that Dou Gui was loyal and restrained, while other courtiers had shifted from flattering the Dou house to piling on accusations.
- Mercy outcome: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-766-lishi-jiaogei-ni-weishenme-yao-guyi-fa-piqi-lrs-mguyfmnxe970hdwjvmevl8oc|Hanji 766]] says Emperor He is moved and Dou Gui alone survives.

## Qualifications

The episode contains a compressed statement that the Dou brothers were secretly forced to die after reaching their fiefs, followed by the more specific Dou Gui survival account. The page follows the specific survival account and treats the compressed death wording as source-scoped tension.

## What Changed

- Hanji 766 creates Dou Gui as a standalone Eastern Han entity.
- The current profile frames him as the evidence-specific mercy case inside the anti-Dou cleanup.
