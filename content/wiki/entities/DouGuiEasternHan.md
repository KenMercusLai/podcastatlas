# 窦瑰 / Dou Gui (Eastern Han)

## Overview

窦瑰 / Dou Gui appears as the restrained Dou-family counterexample inside the anti-[[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] sequence. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-762-lishi-gaosu-ni-nanren-youquan-neng-you-duo-huangtang-limdo2hfd8epcfkwwsrmzz9no0dj|Hanji 762]] has [[YuanAnEasternHan|Yuan An]] recommend consulting him because he is scholarly, frugal, self-cultivating, and power-averse. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-766-lishi-jiaogei-ni-weishenme-yao-guyi-fa-piqi-lrs-mguyfmnxe970hdwjvmevl8oc|Hanji 766]] later shows him caught in the Dou purge but saved by [[ZhangPuEasternHan|Zhang Pu]]'s evidence-specific memorial.

## Current Profile

Hanji 762 makes Dou Gui a living internal contrast before the purge. Yuan An's memorial positions him as a Dou relative who resists the aggressive power style of Dou Xian and [[DouJingEasternHan|Dou Jing]], so the state has a family member worth consulting rather than only external critics.

Hanji 766 then makes Dou Gui a narrow exception inside the Dou family collapse. Zhang Pu argues that Dou Gui kept his retainers disciplined, stayed loyal, and should not be destroyed simply because courtiers who flattered Dou Xian before the purge now find it convenient to condemn every Dou relative.

That profile makes Dou Gui useful for distinguishing regime self-protection from indiscriminate factional vengeance. He survives because Zhang Pu persuades Emperor He that evidence should be individualized even in a high-risk cleanup.

## Key Characteristics

- Dou-family member threatened by the post-purge punishment wave.
- Internal Dou-family contrast presented as scholarly, frugal, self-cultivating, and power-averse.
- Consultative alternative to aggressive relatives such as Dou Xian and Dou Jing.
- Presented as more disciplined than the household's main conspiratorial or abusive actors.
- Saved through Zhang Pu's willingness to criticize opportunistic post-purge denunciations.
- Marks Emperor He's ability, at least in this case, to combine severity against the bloc with selective mercy.

## Qualifications

Hanji 762 presents Dou Gui through Yuan An's evaluative memorial, so the page does not infer a full independent political program for him. Hanji 766 contains a compressed statement that the Dou brothers were secretly forced to die after reaching their fiefs, followed by the more specific Dou Gui survival account. The page follows the specific survival account and treats the compressed death wording as source-scoped tension.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 762 as Dou Gui's internal-family contrast and consultation layer.
