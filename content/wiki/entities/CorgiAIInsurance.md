## Overview
[[CorgiAIInsurance|Corgi]] is an early-stage insurer serving technology companies and offering a policy framed specifically around AI-caused liability.

## Current Profile
The company combines insurance underwriting with an unusual physical presence: a San Francisco financial-district coffee shop that also functions as a visible gathering and branding point for founders. Its substantive wiki relevance is the attempt to make AI failures explicit insured events rather than leaving customers to infer coverage from legacy policies.

## Key Characteristics
- Offers insurance to technology companies, including a dedicated [[AILiabilityInsurance|AI liability product]].
- Describes covered AI failures as including improper statements, incorrect calculations, and service interruption.
- Uses a coffee shop as a founder-facing community and brand touchpoint, although the episode does not establish its commercial effect.
