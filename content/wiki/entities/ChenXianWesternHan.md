## Overview

陈贤 / Chen Xian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-542-zhichang-xinlixue-shengzhi-hou-weihe-geng-rongyi-zao-paiji-lua_jsiusjojpwlwlroehw7jebgm|Hanji 542]] as a Western Han official distinct from the later Xin-era [[ChenXianXinOfficial|陈贤]]. In this source, he is an older-ranking friend and rival of [[ZhaiFangjinWesternHan|翟方进]], loses the Yushi Dafu competition to Zhai, presses Zhai during the rushed-funeral inquiry, and is later removed after Zhai links him to [[ChenTangWesternHan|陈汤]]'s recommendation network. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-546-haoyou-bianwei-choudi-neng-you-duo-kepa-lrtypxhdqvzkme_rgesxwoiv1asv|Hanji 546]] adds his attempted return: [[WangLiWesternHan|王立]] recommends him as 方正, Chengdi appoints him Guanglu Dafu and Jishi Zhong, and Zhai immediately blocks the restoration.

## Current Profile

Chen Xian is presented less as an independent administrator than as a pressure case for friendship under promotion. The source says he had a genuinely close relationship with Zhai Fangjin when Zhai was Jingzhao Yin, but the friendship becomes unstable after the Yushi Dafu post opens and Zhai wins despite Chen and [[FengXinWesternHan|冯信]] being senior.

The episode reads Chen's later inquiry posture as a mixture of practical ambition and psychological compensation. When Zhai is implicated in the rushed funeral of [[QiongchengTaihouWesternHan|邛成太后]], Chen has a procedural chance to push him down and perhaps reopen the office field. After Zhai survives, Chen's earlier pressure creates a self-protection problem: he can expect retaliation, so hostility becomes easier to justify as necessity.

Hanji 546 shows that the rupture does not end with Chen's first removal. Wang Li's recommendation gives Chen a route back into the emperor's near service, but that very restoration revives Zhai's old injury and political fear. Zhai's renewed memorial recasts Chen not as a rehabilitated talent but as a formerly corrupt official whose return would put a dangerous rival back inside Chengdi's court.

## Key Characteristics

- Chen Xian is a Western Han official who had seniority over Zhai Fangjin but lost the Yushi Dafu competition to him.
- His former friendship with Zhai becomes a sharper comparison field precisely because the two men know each other well.
- During the funeral inquiry, Chen is portrayed as pressing Zhai rather than protecting him.
- His motive is source-scoped as a combination of renewed office opportunity, resentment after lost promotion, and psychological release.
- After Zhai survives, Chen becomes vulnerable to counterattack because his prior conduct gives Zhai reason to treat him as an enemy.
- His removal is tied to Zhai's later claim that Chen used Chen Tang's favor to gain office improperly.
- His attempted reappointment through Wang Li's 方正 recommendation becomes a second flashpoint, and Zhai blocks it by reviving accusations of harming subordinates and people, corruption, and unfitness for close imperial service.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 542 and Hanji 546. It does not reconstruct Chen Xian's full career, exact offices beyond the sources' frame, or independent legal merits of the accusations. The motive account is the episode's psychological interpretation, not a separately verified interior record.
