# Yoko Ono

## Overview

Yoko Ono is an artist represented here through her relationship and collaboration with [[JohnLennon]] during [[TheBeatles|the Beatles']] final years, with the source explicitly rejecting her as a sufficient cause of the breakup.

## Current Profile

The episode places Ono beside Lennon's disenchantment with being a Beatle, heroin use, and growing political direction. Her presence in recording sessions is described as a real source of strain, but not as an explanation that can absorb managerial loss, money disputes, drugs, artistic divergence, and the other members' agency.

Her later profile is tied to Lennon through marriage, the Gibraltar period, peace campaigning, and work associated with the Plastic Ono Band. The episode's useful contribution is therefore causal restraint: relational change mattered, but the familiar blame narrative converts a multi-causal institutional breakdown into a story about one woman.

## Key Characteristics

- Artist and partner whose relationship with Lennon overlapped with the Beatles' final years.
- Was present in recording contexts that the source says created interpersonal strain.
- Became connected with Lennon's post-Beatles artistic and political direction.
- Is treated as one factor in a wider network rather than the cause of the breakup.
- Exposes how simplified cultural memory can personalize institutional failure.
