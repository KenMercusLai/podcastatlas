# Committee of General Security

## Overview

The Committee of General Security was the policing and internal-security body that operated beside the [[CommitteeOfPublicSafety|Committee of Public Safety]] during the Terror.

## Current Profile

By late 1793, the source assigns the committee responsibility for policing and links it to the [[RevolutionaryTribunal]]. In March 1794 it joined the Committee of Public Safety in approving [[GeorgesDanton|Danton's]] arrest, although two members refused to sign. Its role shows that revolutionary repression was distributed across overlapping bodies rather than commanded by one individual or one undifferentiated committee.

## Key Characteristics

- It handled policing and internal security within revolutionary government.
- It operated alongside rather than inside the Committee of Public Safety.
- It was institutionally connected to the Revolutionary Tribunal.
- It participated in the decision to arrest Danton and his allies.
- Internal refusal to sign shows that committee action was not unanimous.

## Evidence

### Police and tribunal role

- [[710-the-terror-death-at-the-guillotine-part-4-glt5038102951]] distinguishes the committee's policing and tribunal responsibilities from the policy direction of the Committee of Public Safety.

### Danton arrest

- [[710-the-terror-death-at-the-guillotine-part-4-glt5038102951]] says the two governing committees approved Danton's arrest on 29 March while recording two refusals to sign.

## Qualifications

- The source supplies a focused institutional role, not a complete history of membership, jurisdiction, or rivalry between committees.
- Participation in the arrest decision does not establish identical motives or equal responsibility for every member.
- The exact allocation of control over the tribunal remains source-scoped.

## What Changed

- A canonical profile now separates internal policing and arrest authority from the public-safety committee's wider policy role.

## Relationships

- [[CommitteeOfPublicSafety]] - parallel governing body that joined it in approving Danton's arrest.
- [[RevolutionaryTribunal]] - political court linked to its internal-security function.
- [[GeorgesDanton]] - former revolutionary leader whose arrest it approved.
