# Microsoft Excel

Microsoft Excel appears in [[betty-boop-excel-olympics-penny-isms-our-2026-valentines]] through [[JessJang]]'s Valentine to [[JesseDoherty]]'s [[WashingtonPost|Washington Post]] article about the Microsoft Excel Collegiate Competition.

The episode treats Excel as more than generic productivity software. Contestants solve spreadsheet puzzles under spectator conditions, making Excel a medium for [[CompetitiveSpreadsheeting]] and a compact example of how ordinary workplace tools can become technical performance.

[[ep-11-growing-technology-footprints-in-insurance-sector]] adds the insurance-infrastructure version. [[NickBlamer]] calls Excel a major development platform and describes [[CoherentSpark]] as a way to turn spreadsheet inputs and outputs into version-controlled, auditable APIs, making Excel a starting point for [[SpreadsheetToAPIGovernance]] rather than only an office tool.
