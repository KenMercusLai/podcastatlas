## Overview

翟义起义 / Zhai Yi Rebellion enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-652-wangmang-de-waike-shoushu-ltdiqldskazy-cb6igdmrv5huxm9|Hanji 652]] as a major anti-[[WangMang|王莽]] uprising before the later late-[[XinDynasty|新朝]] collapse sequence.

## Current Profile

The episode uses the rebellion mainly as background for [[WangSunqingXinRebel|王孙卿]]'s dissection. It says the uprising was one of the largest challenges produced by Wang Mang's reform overreach and political damage to different social strata, with participation reaching more than 100,000 people in the host's account.

Its current wiki role is therefore chronological and explanatory. The rebellion predates later Chimei and Lulin endgame material, but it already shows that Wang Mang's rule faced large organized resistance. Wang Sunqing's nine-year escape and eventual capture let Wang Mang convert an old rebel enemy into a public terror object.

## Key Characteristics

- Major anti-Wang-Mang uprising named as one of the largest early challenges to Xin power.
- Framed by the source as a reaction to Wang Mang's radical and damaging rule.
- Important here because it explains Wang Sunqing's long fugitive status and punitive dissection.
- Prefigures later late-Xin rebellion pressure without being identical to Chimei or Lulin.
- Current evidence is background-level rather than a full military history.

## Evidence

- Scale claim: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-652-wangmang-de-waike-shoushu-ltdiqldskazy-cb6igdmrv5huxm9|Hanji 652]] describes the Zhai Yi uprising as involving more than 100,000 people and nearly threatening Wang Mang's regime.
- Reform-overreach context: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-652-wangmang-de-waike-shoushu-ltdiqldskazy-cb6igdmrv5huxm9|Hanji 652]] frames the uprising against Wang Mang's radical and socially damaging reform style.
- Wang Sunqing connection: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-652-wangmang-de-waike-shoushu-ltdiqldskazy-cb6igdmrv5huxm9|Hanji 652]] says Wang Sunqing was a rebellion leader who escaped for nine years after failure.
