# Amazon Prime

## Overview
Amazon Prime is [[Amazon]]'s membership program and the bundle through which the source says [[AlexaPlus|Alexa+]] is offered without an additional charge.

## Current Profile
In this source, Prime functions as the business-model container for Alexa+. Amazon can treat the assistant as a membership benefit whose costs may be justified indirectly if it improves subscription acquisition, retention, usage, and shopping activity.

Amazon reports a 25% lift in Prime sign-ups among people who try Alexa+ and 40% larger baskets for Alexa+-assisted shopping. These figures illustrate the proposed [[PrimeMembershipFlywheel|membership flywheel]], but the episode supplies no independent verification, baseline definition, selection controls, or methodology.

## Key Characteristics
- Bundles Alexa+ as a membership benefit rather than a separate incremental charge in the source's account.
- Links assistant adoption to subscription acquisition and retention.
- Connects conversational task execution to Amazon shopping activity.
- Lets Amazon evaluate assistant value indirectly through ecosystem behavior rather than stand-alone software revenue.

## Evidence
- Bundle evidence: [[tech-20260928-0928-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260928-0928-mp-tech-pod-128]] says Alexa+ is available to Prime members without an additional charge.
- Acquisition evidence: [[tech-20260928-0928-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260928-0928-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports Amazon's claim of a 25% lift in Prime sign-ups among users who try Alexa+.
- Commerce evidence: [[tech-20260928-0928-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260928-0928-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports Amazon's claim of 40% larger Alexa+-assisted shopping baskets.

## Qualifications
This page captures Prime only through one Alexa+-focused source, not the membership program's full history or benefits. The reported commercial lifts are company claims without independent methodology and do not establish causation, profitability, retention, or general adoption.
