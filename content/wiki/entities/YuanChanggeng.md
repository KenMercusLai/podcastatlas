# 袁长庚 / Yuan Changgeng

袁长庚 / Yuan Changgeng is the anthropologist named near the end of [[ep244-jizhe-yanzhong-de-hao-gongzuo-shenme-yang-lk8iz9cllzx2jou5-sw9yt2ogpyb]] for a time-scale view of work and life. The source uses his idea to move the episode from finding a ready-made [[GoodWorkNewStandard|good job]] toward [[LongHorizonWorkEvaluation|long-horizon work evaluation]].

The page is source-scoped. In this episode, Yuan matters less as a full biographical subject than as the thinker whose frame helps [[WeiQianSanlian|魏茜]] ask whether a person should spend a third or more of life on a job that does not fit what they need or want.
