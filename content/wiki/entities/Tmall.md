# Tmall / 天猫

## Overview

Tmall / 天猫 is an official-brand ecommerce platform represented here as both a market-entry testing channel and digital flagship infrastructure for premium fashion.

## Current Profile

The earlier source presents Tmall as [[AloYoga|Alo Yoga]]'s first official mainland China sales channel, allowing the brand to test whether social and daigou attention converted into local demand before a full offline rollout. [[ep151-ep151]] broadens that role: official flagship stores can carry imagery, styling, lookbooks, video, livestreams, active brand search, customer operations, and premium presentation rather than functioning only as transaction or clearance outlets.

Episode 151 also cites platform growth, rankings, and more than 64 million 88VIP members, with high-value members contributing about half of GMV for some leading brands. These figures and rankings are source-scoped, and platform payment GMV must be reconciled with returns, promotions, and channel shifts.

## Key Characteristics

- Official ecommerce channel for mainland market entry and demand testing.
- Flagship-store infrastructure for premium brand presentation and customer operation.
- Search behavior can reveal intentional brand demand.
- High-value membership can concentrate purchasing power for leading brands.
- Platform sales and rankings require return, promotion, and scope context.

## Evidence

- Market-entry test - [[bali-shui-he-shengpeilu-hai-neng-zhuanqian-quechao-weihe-yao-boli-shui-yewu-1005452453]] says Alo planned a Tmall flagship before a full mainland offline presence.
- Brand-showroom role - [[ep151-ep151]] describes flagship stores using high-resolution imagery, lookbooks, scenes, styling, and livestreams to express lifestyle.
- Demand signals - [[ep151-ep151]] treats active brand search, full-price conversion, repeat purchase, and new-product contribution as more informative than GMV alone.
- Member economics - [[ep151-ep151]] reports more than 64 million 88VIP members and about half of GMV from them for some leading brands.

## Qualifications

The sources do not provide platform datasets or consistent measurement definitions. A first-minute launch, ranking, member count, active search, or payment GMV does not establish retained revenue, profitability, or durable brand preference. Tmall's role is represented only through the listed sources, not a complete platform history.
