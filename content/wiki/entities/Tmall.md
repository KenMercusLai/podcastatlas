# Tmall / 天猫

Tmall / 天猫 appears in [[bali-shui-he-shengpeilu-hai-neng-zhuanqian-quechao-weihe-yao-boli-shui-yewu-1005452453]] as [[AloYoga|Alo Yoga]]'s first official mainland China sales channel. The source says Alo planned to open a Tmall flagship store before building a full offline retail presence, using the platform to test product, price, fit, fabric, and brand conversion among mainland consumers.

The page should be kept near [[Taobao]] and [[Alibaba]], but its role here is more premium-brand entry than broad marketplace history. Tmall functions as a lower-commitment official channel where a foreign brand can separate social-media heat and daigou demand from actual local purchase behavior.
