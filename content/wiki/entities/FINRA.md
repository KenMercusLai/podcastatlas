# FINRA

## Overview
FINRA is the U.S. securities self-regulatory organization used in the latest All-In source as the analogy for Demis Hassabis's proposed AI standards body.

## Current Profile
FINRA's role in this wiki is analogical, not a full financial-regulation profile. The All-In source describes a proposed AI standards organization modeled on FINRA: industry-funded, federally overseen, staffed by independent technical experts, and focused on evaluating frontier-model risks before release.

The profile matters because the analogy defines the tradeoff. A FINRA-like AI body could bring technical expertise and industry funding into governance, but it also raises the same concerns the wiki tracks in [[AIRegulatoryCaptureRisk]]: who controls the rules, which actors can afford compliance, and whether a standards body becomes de facto permissioning.

## Key Characteristics
- FINRA is used as a model for industry-funded, government-overseen self-regulation.
- The source imports the analogy into AI frontier-model release governance rather than securities-market enforcement itself.
- The analogy makes independence, expert staffing, and federal oversight central to the proposed AI body.
- The same analogy also exposes capture and permissioning risk if incumbents dominate the organization.
