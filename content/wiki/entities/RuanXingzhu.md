## Overview

阮星竹 / Ruan Xingzhu is a lover of [[DuanZhengchun|段正淳]] in [[TianLongBaBu|《天龙八部》]] whose low-pressure attachment, preserved affection, and limited resentment make her the bounded episode's most internally stable comparison case.

## Current Profile

Strict family expectations lead Ruan Xingzhu to send away the two daughters born outside marriage. She later lives at 小镜湖 and keeps Duan Zhengchun's love poem visible, treating the relationship as a source of warmth rather than something that must be denied or avenged.

The episode emphasizes her gentleness toward rivals and her limited pressure on Duan to remain. Lower expectations may help the relationship last longer and prevent loss from governing her life. The same accommodation remains morally and socially qualified: her stability does not erase separation from her daughters or establish that reduced expectation is equal partnership.

## Key Characteristics

- Preserves affectionate memory without organizing life around revenge.
- Places relatively little pressure on Duan Zhengchun for exclusivity or continued presence.
- Responds to a rival with verbal de-escalation rather than reciprocal hostility.
- Retains warmth and daily stability while bearing the cost of family rules and separated children.

Emotional stability does not cancel the consequences for her daughters, and limited resentment is not a universal standard for healthy response to betrayal or absence.
