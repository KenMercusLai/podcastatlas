# Lynx / 猞猁

Lynx / 猞猁 enters the wiki through [[163-jiao-yi-zhi-sheli-wangji-dongwuyuan-xiye-de-gushi-bi-fengkuang-dongwucheng-2-geng-jingcai-939887179]], where [[YuanZhang|袁掌]] uses animated lynx characters as an entry point into real cat taxonomy, morphology, habitat, and public recognition. The source explains that "猞猁" can refer narrowly to the Eurasian lynx or more broadly to the lynx genus.

The episode's focal individual, [[TianxianBaobaoLynx|天线宝宝]], is discussed as a Eurasian lynx. The film comparison lets the source distinguish that animal from a Canada lynx-like screen image with smaller body size, longer colder-colored fur, and large snow-adapted paws.

## Key Claims
- Lynx are recognizable through features such as ear tufts and large paws, but public familiarity may still remain low without a memorable story.
- The episode marks a taxonomy boundary: a pleasing animated lynx image should not be directly substituted for the Chinese Eurasian lynx case.
- In China, the source identifies lynx as a national second-class protected animal.
- The animal becomes a useful conservation-communication case because it sits between charismatic big cats and internet-cute small cats.
