# Revisionist History

## Overview

Revisionist History is the podcast presenting “From The Big Dig: Mitt & Ted,” an episode about [[TedKennedy]]'s lost 1974 health-reform opportunity and the later Massachusetts-to-ACA lineage.

## Current Profile

[[from-the-big-dig-mitt-ted]] introduces a season-long investigation into where the [[AffordableCareAct]]'s ideas came from, how they reached national politics, and why they became divisive. The opening episode combines family experience, archival political history, working-level negotiation, and counterfactual judgment to make the failed Nixon-Kennedy talks the emotional and institutional foundation for the later [[MittRomney]] partnership.

## Key Characteristics

- Narrative history podcast connecting personal experience to institutional and policy change.
- Uses the 1974 Nixon-Kennedy negotiations as a lost-opportunity case.
- Frames later Massachusetts reform as part of the Affordable Care Act's political genealogy.

## Evidence

### Season framing and method

- [[from-the-big-dig-mitt-ted]] moves from Ted Jr.'s cancer treatment through secret negotiations and retrospective regret to the promised Romney-Kennedy story.

## Qualifications

Only one episode currently informs this page. Its “greatest lost chance” framing is narratively powerful but does not itself prove that the 1974 compromise could have passed Congress.
