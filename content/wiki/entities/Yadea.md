# Yadea / 雅迪

## Overview
Yadea / 雅迪 is a major traditional Chinese electric two-wheeler manufacturer used in the source set as the scale-and-value incumbent against which [[Ninebot|九号]] and [[NiuTechnologies|小牛]] built premium challenger positions.

## Current Profile
Yadea remains associated with broad market coverage and an ex-factory average near the lower end of the compared brands, while retaining strength below the highest price tier. Its recent revenue and profit pressure reflects both category weakness and the cost of adapting to new standards; the sources also show that it is following challengers into higher-end products rather than remaining fixed in a low-price strategy.

## Key Characteristics
- Yadea is positioned as a traditional scale manufacturer with strong coverage below the highest price bands.
- Its historical competitive context emphasized low prices, volume, and broad-market reach.
- Recent first-half revenue and profit are described as declining amid weak demand and regulatory adjustment.
- The company is investing in higher-end products as challengers expand the category's premium segment.
- New safety requirements and material costs create production and margin pressure across its portfolio.

## Evidence
- Scale and price position: [[yiliang-xiaodianlv-maidao-san-siqian-yuan-xiaoniu-jiuhao-ruhe-shixian-diandong-lianglunche-gaoduanhua-1017641937]] places Yadea near a roughly 2,000-yuan ex-factory average and says it remains dominant below 4,000 yuan.
- Performance pressure: [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] groups Yadea with [[AimaTechnology|爱玛]] as a first-half revenue-and-profit decliner.
- Premium response: [[yiliang-xiaodianlv-maidao-san-siqian-yuan-xiaoniu-jiuhao-ruhe-shixian-diandong-lianglunche-gaoduanhua-1017641937]] says traditional manufacturers increased R&D and marketing attention after challengers opened the high-end segment.
- Regulation and cost: [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] and [[yiliang-xiaodianlv-maidao-san-siqian-yuan-xiaoniu-jiuhao-ruhe-shixian-diandong-lianglunche-gaoduanhua-1017641937]] connect new standards, demand timing, and higher production inputs with a tougher category environment.

## Qualifications
The sources do not provide a complete product-level margin, dealer, export, or profitability comparison. Yadea's presence in the above-4,000-yuan segment also means the legacy-versus-premium distinction is relative, not absolute.

## What Changed
- Yadea's profile now includes its continued sub-4,000-yuan strength and its response to premium challengers.
- The synthesis distinguishes traditional scale positioning from an inability to sell higher-priced products.
