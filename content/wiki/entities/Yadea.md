## Current Profile
The source uses Yadea as part of the legacy-manufacturer side of [[ElectricTwoWheelerMarketDivergence]]. Demand pulled forward by trade-in policy, new national standards, and material-cost pressure made the category harder, while smart-feature brands gained relative momentum.

## Key Characteristics
- Yadea is treated as a traditional electric two-wheeler maker rather than a smart-feature challenger.
- Its first-half performance is described as declining in both revenue and profit.
- The company is exposed to industrywide demand, regulation, and input-cost pressure.
