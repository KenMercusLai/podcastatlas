# 胡武 / Hu Wu (Zhang Chu)

胡武 / Hu Wu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-3-chensheng-zhisi-weishenme-dele-minxin-hanle-renxin-lnx4zs-vsi3zeoixnqskkpexmr3|Qinji 129-3]] as the si guo inspection official [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] appoints alongside [[ZhuFangZhangChu|诸坊]]. His job is to inspect faults among Zhang Chu officials and commanders.

The source says the pair's enforcement is extremely harsh: returning commanders are criticized, people they or Chen Sheng dislike are handled without normal judicial review, and severe treatment of commoners is treated as loyalty. Hu Wu therefore helps turn [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]] from a revolt with broad symbolic appeal into a fearful court around Chen Sheng.
