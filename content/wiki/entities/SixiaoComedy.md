# 四孝喜剧 / Sixiao Comedy

## Overview
四孝喜剧 / Sixiao Comedy appears in the wiki as the live-comedy partner for [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] panel episodes about borrowing, debt, money, snobbery, moral dilemmas, workplace power, and consumption symbols.

## Current Profile
In the money-and-debt source, 四孝喜剧 supplies the live-show context and several participant voices. Its role is not simply venue branding: the episode's loose, interruptive, joke-heavy structure lets sensitive money topics become speakable through teasing, audience interaction, and personal stories from [[XianyuStandup|咸鱼]], [[WangJiye|王季叶]], [[XiaoSiye|小四爷]], and [[CaoWeiLuoyonghaoColleague|草薇]].

The "势利" episode extends the same live format into moral and status discomfort. [[KuaigeStandup|快哥]], [[HulanStandup|呼兰]], [[WangJiye|王继业 / 王季叶]], and [[XuanZongStandup|选总]] use comedy-industry hierarchy, transactional warmth, workplace status, club management, diamonds, and luxury goods to make [[SnobberyAsValueRanking|snobbery]] concrete.

## Key Characteristics
- Live-comedy collaborator for a Luo Yonghao podcast panel.
- Provides a setting where advertising, audience response, and debt stories are folded into the same performance.
- Provides a setting where moral discomfort and status comparison can be handled through jokes, audience reaction, and personal stories.
- Source-scoped comedy entity currently represented by two ingested Luo Yonghao panel episodes.
