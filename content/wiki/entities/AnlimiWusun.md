## Overview

安犁靡 / Anlimi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-550-sha-diguo-taizi-xiatui-jiqian-zhuibing-ta-zenme-zuodao-de-lvzhcjutqnu4or4elds_qg4s1gto|Hanji 550]] as the [[Wusun|乌孙]] small kunmi installed after [[MozhenjiangWusun|末振将]]'s death and as the ruler whose riders surround [[DuanHuizongWesternHan|段会宗]] after [[FanqiuWusun|番丘]] is killed.

## Current Profile

The source presents Anlimi as both Han-backed successor and local kinship actor. After the large-kunmi side and Nanqi kill Mozhenjiang, they install Anlimi, son of the earlier small kunmi Anri, as the new small kunmi. This settles the office but not the emotional and political aftermath of Han punishment.

When Duan kills Fanqiu, Anlimi reacts with force and surrounds Duan with thousands of riders. Yet he also accepts a rhetorical exit. He says Mozhenjiang wronged Han and Fanqiu's killing was deserved, but asks why Duan did not notify him so he could send Fanqiu off. Duan replies that notice would either create liability for letting Fanqiu flee or force Anlimi to violate kin feeling. Anlimi withdraws, making him the counterpart whose retreat proves Duan's combined threat-and-face strategy worked.

## Key Characteristics

- Wusun small kunmi installed after Mozhenjiang's death.
- Represents the successor branch that Han must keep from turning punitive action into open clash.
- Responds to Fanqiu's killing by surrounding Duan Huizong with thousands of riders.
- Accepts a face-saving explanation and withdraws without killing Duan.
