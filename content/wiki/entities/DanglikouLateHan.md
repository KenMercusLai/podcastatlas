# 当利口 / Danglikou (late Han)

当利口 / Danglikou (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] as a defensive point held by [[ZhangYingLateHan|张英]] for [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]]. It appears beside [[HengjiangLateHan|横江]], where [[FanNengLateHan|樊能]] and [[YuMiLateHan|于糜]] are stationed.

The place matters because it shows Liu Yao turning an uncertain appointment into a guarded line against [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s camp. In the source's sequence, control of crossings and mouths such as Hengjiang and Danglikou is the practical shape of the contest before Sun Ce's later Jiangdong expansion.
