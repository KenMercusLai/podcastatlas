# 背包十年 / Backpack Ten Years

## Overview
背包十年 is a Chinese hostel chain presented in [[gkwriueoruikahg1awtsiv4y-gkwriueoruikahg1awtsiv4y]] as a local example of the move from inexpensive lodging toward accommodation, social space, and ancillary consumption.

## Current Profile
The episode says the company completed a new financing round in late September with investors including New Oriental. Its public areas reportedly occupy 25% to 30% of floor area, compared with roughly 15% in traditional hostels, making shared space part of the product rather than leftover circulation area.

## Key Characteristics
- A Chinese chain-hostel operator rather than a single independent property.
- Larger common-area allocation than the traditional-hostel comparison cited by the episode.
- A social and consumption layer added to basic accommodation.
- Recent outside financing used as evidence of investor interest in the model.

## Evidence
- Space design - [[gkwriueoruikahg1awtsiv4y-gkwriueoruikahg1awtsiv4y]] cites an industry report placing common areas at 25% to 30% of the chain's floor area.
- Capital interest - [[gkwriueoruikahg1awtsiv4y-gkwriueoruikahg1awtsiv4y]] reports a late-September financing round that included New Oriental.
- Market analogy - [[gkwriueoruikahg1awtsiv4y-gkwriueoruikahg1awtsiv4y]] uses the company to ask whether the European lodging-plus-social-consumption logic can travel to China.

## Qualifications
The source does not provide financing size, store economics, occupancy, ancillary-revenue share, or evidence that the European chain model transfers broadly across Chinese cities. Floor-area allocation alone does not establish demand or profitability.
