# Moku

Moku is the bistro operator interviewed in [[ep246-rang-nvsheng-anxin-de-yuehui-xiaojiuguan-shenme-yang-lpd5zw9ryd3kplmnaywsoqokvakm]]. The episode presents her through the experience of running [[Wanjiu|万九]], a Beijing small bistro that became known as a place where women could come alone, eat, drink, sit at the bar, and feel looked after without being pressured.

Her source role is practical rather than theoretical. Moku explains how portions, bar seats, corner seats, staff chat, eye contact, and owner presence create [[FemaleFriendlyBistro|女性友好小酒馆]] conditions. She also describes a hospitality boundary: when men bring women dates, the staff may help the woman feel safe and included; when women bring men dates, the staff may stay more discreet so the woman is not socially judged for being a regular night drinker.
