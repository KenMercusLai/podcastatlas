# Greg Wallace

## Overview

Greg Wallace is the law professor, competitive shooter, and gun enthusiast who gives [[MalcolmGladwell]] a practical firearm lesson in [[guns-part-3-a-shooting-lesson]].

## Current Profile

Wallace serves as the episode's technically informed critic of assault-weapon bans. At a shooting range, he explains the difference between civilian semi-automatic and military selective-fire rifles, demonstrates AR-15 modularity, teaches safe handling, and compares firing and reloading behavior.

His demonstrations make firearm mechanics concrete, but they do not independently evaluate the population-level effect of regulation. His opposition to assault-weapon bans is therefore relevant context for interpreting which technical distinctions and practical workarounds the episode emphasizes.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines legal scholarship with practical competitive-shooting experience.
- Explains semi-automatic operation, selective fire, modularity, and recoil to a novice.
- Demonstrates rapid semi-automatic fire and magazine changes as tests of policy assumptions.
- Argues that feature-based bans leave functionally similar firearms available.
- Supplies technical criticism from an explicitly anti-ban position.
