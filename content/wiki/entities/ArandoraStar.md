# Arandora Star

## Overview

The *Arandora Star* appears in the wiki as a wartime transport whose sinking exposed the lethal consequences of Britain's mass internment of Italians and Germans.

## Current Profile

[[73-england-v-italy-glt3184835237]] places the ship inside the backlash against Italians in Britain after Mussolini entered the Second World War. Shops were attacked, employment was lost, and the “collar the lot” policy swept Italians into internment without the episode distinguishing individual risk in detail. The *Arandora Star*, carrying mostly Italians and some Germans toward Canada, was then torpedoed by a German U-boat.

The event qualifies the episode's humorous treatment of national stereotypes. Anglo-Italian hostility was not only rhetorical: policy, crowd violence, detention, transport, and submarine warfare combined in a mass-casualty disaster.

## Key Characteristics

- British wartime transport used to carry civilian internees overseas.
- Ship associated particularly with Italian internees living in Britain.
- Torpedoed by a German U-boat during the Second World War.
- Maritime disaster produced by the intersection of internment policy and naval war.
- Corrective to nostalgic or purely comic accounts of Anglo-Italian relations.
