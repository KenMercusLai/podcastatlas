# Robert Curtis

Robert Curtis appears in [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] as the 20-year-old gunner killed by [[BillyReid]] in February 1971, becoming the first British soldier to die in [[NorthernIreland]] during [[TheTroubles]]. The episode notes that Curtis left a pregnant wife of the same age.

Curtis functions in the wiki as a casualty marker for the [[BritishArmy]]'s transition from temporary peacekeeping buffer to direct combatant target. The source links his death to British public desire to withdraw and to cabinet fears that withdrawal would produce international condemnation and a sectarian bloodbath.
