# Redwood Materials

Redwood Materials appears in [[tech-20260129-0129-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260129-0129-mp-tech-pod-128]] as an electric vehicle battery recycling startup that has expanded into grid energy storage for data centers. The [[MarketplaceTech]] episode uses Redwood to show how the AI infrastructure boom can create demand for [[SecondLifeEVBatteryStorage]] as a faster route around slow grid interconnection or new natural-gas turbine construction.

The source's concrete example is Redwood's [[Nevada]] project: a 60 megawatt-hour, 12 megawatt system of reused EV batteries powering a local data center while disconnected from the grid. [[ColinCampbell]], Redwood's chief technology officer, says the project took four months to build and that Redwood is engineering a possible 10 gigawatt-per-year manufacturing line.
