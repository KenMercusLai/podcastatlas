# 城市集市 / City Mart

City Mart, called 城市集市 in the source, appears in [[vol-270-da-shi-dai-liu-zai-le-ta-de-shi-dai-1006063835]] as a newer Shanghai food-market operator. The episode says it resembles [[FoodRepublic|大食代]] on the surface because it organizes restaurants and stalls, but it competes through themed design, creative layout, visual circulation, and social-media-friendly space rather than only cheap variety and shared seating.

The 2021 North Bund Raffles old-Shanghai lane-themed food market is used as the episode's example of how a food court can become [[SceneLedFoodMarket|scene-led]]. In that model, the operator invests in decor, blank space, small-program interfaces, new retail, and mixed shop content, making the business harder to copy across cities without local adaptation.

[[kafeidou-chuantong-meishi-guangchang-jielian-bidian-dashidaimen-yudao-naxie-fazhan-zuai-1007530222]] reinforces the City Mart reading by describing the team as a commercial-space operator, not only a restaurant aggregator. In this account, City Mart handles scene design and marketing and mixes food with non-food formats so customers can eat, browse, photograph, and check in.
