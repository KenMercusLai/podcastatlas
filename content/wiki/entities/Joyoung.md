# Joyoung / 九阳

## Overview

Joyoung / 九阳 is a Chinese small-appliance company used in the source both as an example of domestic profit pressure and as an engineering-capability contributor to [[SharkNinja]]'s international product development.

## Current Profile

The source creates a two-sided profile. Joyoung participates in a Chinese market where sales can grow while intense price competition compresses leading firms' profits. At the same time, its technical experience and China-based development resources are presented as useful inputs to SharkNinja's U.S. product expansion, especially in kitchen appliances.

The episode therefore separates production and engineering capability from market capture. Strong product-development resources do not by themselves determine profit when local competition, price, brand position, and channel structure differ.

## Key Characteristics

- Chinese small-appliance producer exposed to intense domestic price competition.
- Source-reported technical strength in heating and air-circulation problems for kitchen appliances.
- Engineering contributor to a cross-border product-development system.
- Company whose domestic profit outcome contrasts with the overseas growth attributed partly to related capabilities.

## Evidence

- **Domestic pressure:** [[jiuyang-jinglirun-dadie-jin-75-ta-de-meiguo-qinqi-zenme-yue-zhuan-yue-duo-gkwriueoyniragzajwt5lgbb]] reports a near-75% year-on-year net-profit decline despite category sales growth.
- **Cross-border engineering:** [[jiuyang-jinglirun-dadie-jin-75-ta-de-meiguo-qinqi-zenme-yue-zhuan-yue-duo-gkwriueoyniragzajwt5lgbb]] says a multifunction cooker combined U.S. demand research with a China team using Joyoung technology to solve heating and airflow problems.
- **Ownership history:** [[jiuyang-jinglirun-dadie-jin-75-ta-de-meiguo-qinqi-zenme-yue-zhuan-yue-duo-gkwriueoyniragzajwt5lgbb]] reports that Joyoung's parent Shanghai Lihong and CDH obtained 70% of SharkNinja's parent in 2017.

## Qualifications

The source does not provide Joyoung's filing, segment economics, ownership records, or a complete comparison with SharkNinja. The profit decline, transaction history, engineering attribution, and causal role of price competition remain episode-reported. The source does not establish that capability transfer alone explains either company's financial result.

## What Changed

- Created a profile separating engineering capability from the market and channel conditions that determine profit capture.
