## Overview

Wang Tan / 王谭 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-533-zhichang-zui-wujie-de-siju-zhende-wujie-ma-lv64fxua_avw1toxw_e88ty1xru7|Hanji 533]] as Ping'a Hou, a Wang-family figure whose death makes [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] regret an earlier personnel choice and renew his use of [[WangShangWesternHan|王商]] as a counterweight inside Wang-family power.

## Current Profile

Wang Tan is not the episode's active mediator or main officeholder. His role is structural: Chengdi had earlier imagined using Wang Tan to expose or complicate Wang-family dominance, but Wang Tan's death closes that option. The episode therefore uses Wang Tan as the missing counterweight whose absence helps explain why Chengdi turns again to Wang Shang.

The source also compares Wang Tan and Wang Shang as similar in temperament once favored: both are described as less rigorous or more prone to self-inflation than the very cautious Wang Yin. That comparison matters because Chengdi's balancing logic depends not only on formal office but on personality differences that could become political friction.

## Key Characteristics

- Ping'a Hou and Wang-family figure in Chengdi-era court politics.
- Dead by the episode's opening moment in 17 BCE.
- Remembered by Chengdi as a missed counterweight option.
- Functions as a structural absence that leads to Wang Shang's renewed empowerment.
- Used in the source as a temperament comparison for Wang Shang.
