# 滇吾 / Dian Wu (Qiang leader)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-930-ruhe-pochu-tanxin-he-buganxin-lkyysh5jbgxwjq8ayrfar4gsjdcd|Hanji 930]] presents Dian Wu as a Qiang leader in the battle where [[GaiXunLateHan|盖勋]] is defeated and wounded while trying to rescue the protector of the Qiang. When other fighters want to kill Gai Xun, Dian Wu stops them, praises Gai Xun as worthy, and tries to give him a horse.

The source uses Dian Wu mainly to make Gai Xun's reputation visible through an enemy's response. Dian Wu is therefore a source case for hostile respect rather than a developed Qiang biography.
