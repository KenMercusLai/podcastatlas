## Overview

刘棁 / Liu Zhuo is the elder half-brother of [[LiuBoChangshanKingWesternHan|刘勃]] whose exclusion from paternal favor, inheritance, and post-succession support precedes his denunciation of Liu Bo and [[QueenXiuChangshanWesternHan|王后脩]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-342-2-gudai-fuqi-tongfang-jingyou-zhezhong-falv-xianzhi-lsnhz4q-oxjpefgkdfxkcal-37iv|Hanji 342-2]] presents Liu Zhuo as [[LiuShunChangshanKingWesternHan|刘舜]]'s elder son by a disfavored concubine. His mother's low standing transfers to him: Liu Shun does not favor him or allocate property to him, and Queen Xiu and Liu Bo reject advice to give him a share.

When a Han envoy attends Liu Shun's funeral, Liu Zhuo reports failures of sickbed service and mourning conduct. The denunciation is both an information channel and a product of household conflict. His grievance gives him a motive to retaliate, but it does not by itself establish that the allegations were false; Liu Bo's later obstruction gives the investigation a separate institutional basis.

## Key Characteristics

- Elder son of Liu Shun by an unfavored concubine.
- Excluded from paternal affection and inheritance.
- Receives no support from Liu Bo after the succession.
- Reports Queen Xiu's and Liu Bo's conduct to a visiting Han envoy.
- Accusation joins filial duty, sexual and entertainment conduct, and public impropriety.
- Interested witness whose grievance and factual claims must be assessed separately.

## Evidence

Household exclusion:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-342-2-gudai-fuqi-tongfang-jingyou-zhezhong-falv-xianzhi-lsnhz4q-oxjpefgkdfxkcal-37iv|Hanji 342-2]] says Liu Zhuo and his mother are disfavored, he receives no property, and requests for a share are refused.

Denunciation:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-342-2-gudai-fuqi-tongfang-jingyou-zhezhong-falv-xianzhi-lsnhz4q-oxjpefgkdfxkcal-37iv|Hanji 342-2]] says he reports nonattendance during illness, early departure from mourning quarters, and Liu Bo's prohibited conduct.

Evidentiary boundary:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-342-2-gudai-fuqi-tongfang-jingyou-zhezhong-falv-xianzhi-lsnhz4q-oxjpefgkdfxkcal-37iv|Hanji 342-2]] also reports later witness concealment and official coercion by Liu Bo, so the case does not rest only on Liu Zhuo's motive.

## Qualifications

The source does not provide Liu Zhuo's dates, later life, formal status, the estate rules governing his claim, or the full evidence behind his accusations. “Revenge” is a plausible source interpretation, not proof that every allegation was invented. The transcript's “刘卓” is normalized to the received form 刘棁; the romanization follows the common reading zhuo.

## What Changed

- Created a bounded profile that holds exclusion, retaliatory motive, and possible evidentiary value together without collapsing one into another.
