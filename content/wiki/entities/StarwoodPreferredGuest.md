# Starwood Preferred Guest

Starwood Preferred Guest is the [[StarwoodHotelsAndResorts]] loyalty program discussed in [[140-jiudian-jituan-weishenme-dou-rezhongyu-dazao-huiyuan-he-jifen-tixi-chuantai-yuanxingzhe-yu-suibingjiang-lj-xa3wxzjog6-8zm9nfakh6ulew]]. The source highlights SPG as a program with strong fan attachment, including no-blackout and no-capacity-control features in its earlier form.

The episode uses SPG as a benchmark for [[HotelLoyaltyPrograms]] before later programs moved toward more dynamic redemption and tighter linkage between points and cash room prices.
