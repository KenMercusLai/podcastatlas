# Walter Raleigh

## Overview

Walter Raleigh is the Elizabethan courtier and colonial-project figure whom the source connects to [[JohnDee]]'s cartographic, Atlantic, and imperial ideas.

## Current Profile

The current evidence is narrow. Raleigh appears as a downstream connection between Dee's intellectual program and English projects around Virginia and Roanoke. The episode uses that relationship to show how maps, navigation, antiquarian claims, and providential imperial language could move from a scholar's library toward practical expansion.

Raleigh should therefore be treated here as an influence case within [[ImperialCartographicMythmaking]], not as proof that Dee designed Raleigh's ventures or that ideas alone caused colonization.

## Key Characteristics

- Elizabethan court figure associated by the source with Atlantic expansion.
- Colonial-project actor linked to Virginia and Roanoke.
- Downstream recipient or user of ideas associated with Dee's navigation and imperial program.
- Source-bounded connector between intellectual legitimation and practical expansion.

## Evidence

- Intellectual connection: [[542-elizabeth-is-sorcerer-angels-and-demons-in-renaissance-europe-glt4512754016]] says Dee's ideas influenced Raleigh's thinking around overseas expansion.
- Colonial setting: [[542-elizabeth-is-sorcerer-angels-and-demons-in-renaissance-europe-glt4512754016]] associates that influence with Virginia and the Roanoke colony.
- Qualified causation: [[542-elizabeth-is-sorcerer-angels-and-demons-in-renaissance-europe-glt4512754016]] provides a biographical narrative of Dee rather than a full reconstruction of Raleigh's projects.

## Qualifications

This source does not supply a full Raleigh biography or enough evidence to measure Dee's influence against finance, court competition, maritime capacity, privateering, settler decisions, Indigenous resistance, and other causes of English colonization. The relationship remains an episode-level interpretive claim.
