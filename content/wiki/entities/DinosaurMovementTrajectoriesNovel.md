# 《恐龙的移动轨迹》

## Overview
《恐龙的移动轨迹》 is a novel by 金和珍 discussed by [[ChaoGe|超哥]] as a multi-perspective story of friendship, grief, desire, misunderstanding, and the movement of love.

## Current Profile
The episode follows a three-woman, one-man writing group that becomes intimate and then dissolves. A small dinosaur tattoo leaves a body and observes the group, turning movement into a metaphor for love changing location and form. After 志元 loses a friend and withdraws, 帅雅 wants connection but fears direct inquiry and rejection. The novel becomes the episode's clearest evidence for [[RelationalSilenceProjection]].

## Key Characteristics
- Uses multiple interior viewpoints to reveal incompatible assumptions.
- Gives a moving tattoo an observing perspective and symbolic role.
- Connects bereavement-driven withdrawal to friendship dissolution.
- Shows longing for recognition coexisting with performed self-sufficiency.

## Evidence
- Group dissolution: [[vol-353-qiuri-teji-pingguo-de-xiangwei-zhong-wo-jianjian-hunhun-yushui-gkwriaiohmv_a7ndcatastow]] describes the writing group forming intimacy and gradually separating.
- Silence after grief: [[vol-353-qiuri-teji-pingguo-de-xiangwei-zhong-wo-jianjian-hunhun-yushui-gkwriaiohmv_a7ndcatastow]] recounts 志元 withdrawing after loss while 帅雅 avoids asking directly.
- Projection mechanism: [[vol-353-qiuri-teji-pingguo-de-xiangwei-zhong-wo-jianjian-hunhun-yushui-gkwriaiohmv_a7ndcatastow]] emphasizes people imagining one another and waiting for the other to act.
