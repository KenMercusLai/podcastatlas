# 淳于髡 / Chunyu Kun

淳于髡 / Chunyu Kun appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-14-qiweiwang-buming-zeyi-yiming-jingren-lrs6rbifyiqrojxg0mccpg7sf9rv]] as the [[QiState|齐国]] speaker who reaches [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] when direct remonstrance has become politically risky. The episode says he is physically unimpressive and had suffered the punishment associated with his name, but that his speech and diplomatic skill let him serve Qi effectively among other rulers.

His source role is to turn [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|feedback collapse]] into a repairable problem. Since Qi Wei Wang likes hidden speech, Chunyu Kun uses [[AllusiveRemonstrance|隐语进谏]]: the silent bird in the capital is really the ruler, and the riddle lets the king acknowledge criticism without being publicly cornered.
