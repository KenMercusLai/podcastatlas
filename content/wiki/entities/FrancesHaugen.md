# Frances Haugen

## Overview
Frances Haugen is represented in the wiki as a Facebook whistleblower whose disclosed internal documents inform public, legal, and cultural arguments about youth social-media harms and platform responsibility.

## Current Profile
The sources use Haugen as part of the evidentiary turn in social-media litigation, regulation, and dramatization. The Chinese-language policy discussion frames her disclosed Facebook materials as evidence that [[Meta]]/Facebook knew product design could create youth addiction and mental-health risks while continuing to optimize engagement. [[TheSocialReckoning]] then turns the whistleblower-journalist exchange into a film about platform harms and responsibility.

Her page's wiki role is therefore not a full biography. It is an evidence-node profile: internal documents can shift debate from abstract correlation studies toward what platforms knew, measured, and chose to build, while dramatization shows how that evidence enters popular culture and can simplify unresolved governance questions.
