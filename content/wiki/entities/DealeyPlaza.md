# Dealey Plaza

## Overview

Dealey Plaza is the compact Dallas urban space where [[JohnFKennedy|John F. Kennedy]] was assassinated on 22 November 1963.

## Current Profile

The episode treats the plaza's ordinary scale and street geometry as essential to understanding both the shooting and its afterlife. The motorcade left Main Street, turned onto Houston Street, then onto Elm Street beside the Texas School Book Depository before approaching the underpass. Security attention focused partly on the bridges ahead, while witnesses observed the Depository's sixth-floor windows and later interpreted sounds and movements across the plaza.

Because buildings, the grassy knoll, slow vehicles, dense crowds, engines, echoes, and multiple viewing angles were compressed into a small space, the plaza became both the crime scene and a continuing interpretive problem. Its geography conditions claims about visibility, distance, sound direction, witness perception, and possible firing positions without resolving those claims by itself.

## Key Characteristics

- Small downtown Dallas plaza traversed by the presidential motorcade.
- Road geometry requiring turns from Main to Houston to Elm Street.
- Site adjacent to the Texas School Book Depository and grassy knoll.
- Dense witness environment shaped by crowds, traffic, buildings, and elevated windows.
- Acoustic and visual setting central to later disputes over shot direction and sequence.

Geography constrains plausible interpretations but does not by itself prove a particular shooter, shot count, direction, or conspiracy theory.
