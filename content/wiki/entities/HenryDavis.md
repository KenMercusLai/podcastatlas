# Henry Davis

Henry Davis is the Charlotte founder who introduces [[TannenOralCare]] in [[advice-line-with-bobbi-brown-of-jones-road-beauty-september-2025-427590c2-11bc-43fa-9c92-d1eccf05842a]]. He says the product began from noticing the post-coffee mouthfeel problem, learning that brushing immediately after coffee can affect softened enamel, and experimenting with ingredients alongside his co-founder and high school chemistry teacher.

The Advice Line diagnosis is that Davis has a promising origin story but needs clearer category language. [[BobbiBrown]] and [[GuyRaz]] suggest broadening beyond coffee into natural mouth freshness, oral microbiome education, ingredient clarity, taste, and specific social-wellness tribes such as gyms, dentist offices, saunas, or local wellness communities.
