## Overview

[[CuiShiCaoZhiWife|崔氏]] is presented as [[CaoZhi|曹植]]'s wife and a member of the Qinghe Cui clan whose forced separation and death become entangled with household rules, succession discipline, and great-clan politics.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1161 says Cui Shi accompanies the despondent Cao Zhi in a garden while the couple wear ornate clothing. [[CaoCao|曹操]] treats the display as a breach of household austerity, orders Cao Zhi to send her back to her natal family, and later has poison delivered after the couple allegedly continue meeting. The episode also connects her vulnerability to Cao Zhi's lost succession position and to the political weight of her clan after the death of [[CuiYanLateHan|崔琰]].

## Key Characteristics

- Cao Zhi's wife in an elite-clan marriage.
- Target of coercive separation and lethal household authority in the episode's account.
- Person whose own motives and voice are largely absent from the supplied source.
- Link between intimate family discipline and wider succession or clan politics.

## Qualifications

The source does not supply Cui Shi's personal name, independent testimony, exact kinship within the Qinghe Cui clan, legal marital procedure, clothing details, meeting evidence, execution mechanism, or a cited primary text. Its account of Cao Cao's motives is interpretive, and Cui Shi should not be reduced to an instrument in a conflict among men.

## What Changed

- Created a distinct identity so this Cui Shi is not conflated with [[CuiShiZhuMaichenWife|朱买臣之妻崔氏]].
- Preserved the difference between reported events and inferred political motives.
