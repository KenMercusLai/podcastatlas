# Oman

Oman appears in [[omission-accomplished-why-the-iran-war-cycle-spins-on-6a6c7475aa1642a30f17f1cb]] as the mediator proposing a temporary [[StraitOfHormuz]] truce. [[GregCarlstrom]] says Oman proposed roughly ten days of truce and a joint regional consortium to manage Hormuz without mandatory tolls.

In this source, Oman is useful because even a narrow compromise over shipping control fails. [[Iran]] rejects the proposal and demands complete control over inbound traffic into the Persian Gulf, turning Oman into a test case for [[ThinDiplomaticFrameworks]] and [[ChokepointShippingConfidence]].
