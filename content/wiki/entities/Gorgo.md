# Gorgo

## Overview

Gorgo was the daughter of Spartan king [[CleomenesI|Cleomenes I]], remembered in the episode as a young royal woman whose warning shaped a decision about whether Sparta should support revolt against Persia.

## Current Profile

During an Ionian appeal for Spartan aid, the visitor reportedly increased his financial offer as Cleomenes resisted. Gorgo warned her father that the stranger would corrupt him if not sent away. The anecdote presents her as direct, politically alert, and able to speak inside a royal decision.

The story also supports the episode's broader claim that elite Spartan women could be educated and unusually forthright by comparison with women elsewhere in Greece. It does not establish representative political power for Spartan women or make later claims about gender equality reliable.

## Key Characteristics

- Daughter of Cleomenes I within the Spartan royal household.
- Remembered for recognizing and naming an attempted escalation of bribery.
- Participated through counsel rather than formal civic office.
- Used as evidence for an ideal of forthright elite Spartan womanhood.

## Evidence

- Political warning: [[82-sparta-glt1755395266]] recounts Gorgo advising Cleomenes to reject the Ionian visitor and his increasing offers.
- Gender interpretation: [[82-sparta-glt1755395266]] places the anecdote within a discussion of education and outspoken behavior among Spartan girls and women.

## Qualifications

The page is bounded to one anecdote in a conversational episode. Gorgo's exact age, wording, influence, and representativeness depend on literary tradition, while elite visibility should not be generalized into equal rights or a complete account of Spartan women's lives.
