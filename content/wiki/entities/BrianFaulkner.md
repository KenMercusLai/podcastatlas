# Brian Faulkner

Brian Faulkner appears in [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] as the Northern Ireland prime minister who succeeded [[JamesChichesterClark]] and pushed for [[InternmentWithoutTrial]]. The episode portrays him as a skilled and ambitious law-and-order politician facing sharply rising shootings, explosions, hospitalizations, and deaths by mid-1971.

Faulkner offered limited concessions to the SDLP while asking [[EdwardHeath]] to approve internment. The source says the [[BritishArmy]], the [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]], and [[JackLynch]] warned that the policy would likely fail and drive Catholics toward the IRA, but Heath approved it to support Faulkner and avoid direct rule.
