# Wright Brothers / 莱特兄弟

The Wright brothers enter [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]] through their bicycle shop, repair, rental, sales, and high-end bicycle-making business. The episode says this business helped fund their flight experiments and gave them practical experience with balance, light structures, mechanical parts, and iterative engineering.

Their page makes the aviation side of [[BicycleIndustrialSpillovers]] explicit. In the source's account, the bicycle was not only a historical predecessor to cars; it also trained operators who could think about dynamic balance and control before powered flight became practical.
