# 文丑 / Wen Chou (late Han)

## Overview
文丑 / Wen Chou (late Han) enters the wiki through Hanji 1034 as a [[YuanShao|袁绍]] general paired with [[YanLiangLateHan|颜良]] in the pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] debate. His current function is to show how a large force can look strong through famous names while still being downgraded by organizational assessment.

## Current Profile
In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]], [[KongRong|孔融]] worries that Yuan Shao has Wen Chou, Yan Liang, advisers, territory, and troops. [[XunYu|荀彧]] replies that Wen Chou and Yan Liang are brave but not enough to change the strategic balance. The source therefore uses Wen Chou as part of the Cao-side distinction between visible assets and effective command.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan Shao-side general named as an apparent pre-Guandu asset.
- Paired with Yan Liang as a brave but strategically limited commander in Xun Yu's reply.
- Source-scoped marker of why organizational analysis can outweigh roster reputation.
