# 文丑 / Wen Chou (late Han)

## Overview
文丑 / Wen Chou (late Han) enters the wiki as a [[YuanShao|袁绍]] general paired with [[YanLiangLateHan|颜良]] in the pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] debate and then killed in [[YanjinBattleLateHan|延津之战]]. His current function is to show how a large force can look strong through famous names while still being defeated by organizational assessment, baiting, and timing.

## Current Profile
In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]], [[KongRong|孔融]] worries that Yuan Shao has Wen Chou, Yan Liang, advisers, territory, and troops. [[XunYu|荀彧]] replies that Wen Chou and Yan Liang are brave but not enough to change the strategic balance. Hanji 1044 supplies the payoff: Wen Chou advances with [[LiuBei|刘备]] and a larger cavalry force, but [[CaoCao|曹操]] and [[XunYou|荀攸]] let Yuan cavalry scatter around exposed baggage before striking with fewer than six hundred riders and killing him.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan Shao-side general named as an apparent pre-Guandu asset.
- Paired with Yan Liang as a brave but strategically limited commander in Xun Yu's reply.
- Source-scoped marker of why organizational analysis can outweigh roster reputation.
- Killed at Yanjin after Cao Cao and Xun You bait the larger cavalry force into disorder.

## Evidence
- Apparent strength: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] includes Wen Chou in Kong Rong's list of Yuan Shao's advantages.
- Cao-side downgrade: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] has Xun Yu judge Wen Chou and Yan Liang as capturable despite their courage.
- Guandu context: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] places the assessment inside Cao Cao's pre-campaign morale and strategy discussion.
- Yanjin death: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1044-guanyu-qianli-zou-danqi-xu-ganxie-caocao-busha-enqing-lpptlnc-e4zu4lcjr49bquxdw0pm|Hanji 1044]] says Wen Chou and Liu Bei lead Yuan cavalry, become disordered while seizing baggage, and are defeated when Cao attacks.

## Qualifications
This page is not a full biography. It records Wen Chou's source-bounded role in the Hanji 1034 assessment debate and the Hanji 1044 Yanjin defeat. Liu Bei is named with Wen Chou in the cavalry movement, but the current evidence does not make Liu Bei the Yuan-side tactical designer.
