# Amy Webb

Amy Webb is the futurist interviewed in [[tech-20260106-0106-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260106-0106-mp-tech-pod-128]] about why the defining technology trend for 2026 may be [[AIConvergence]] rather than AI alone. The source uses her as a forecasting voice, not as a full biography.

Her argument connects three layers. First, [[PostSearchInternet]] behavior could shift discovery from browsing and links toward direct AI answers. Second, [[PhysicalAI]] could move robots from research demonstrations toward everyday infrastructure and labor. Third, [[ProgrammableMatter]] and [[GenerativeBiology]] show AI-driven design moving into materials, medicine, packaging, and biological systems.
