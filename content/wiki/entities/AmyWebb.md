## Overview
Amy Webb is a futurist who appears in [[MarketplaceTech]] as both a technology forecaster and an AI-governance critic. The bounded sources cover her 2026 convergence forecast and her argument that AI safety requires accountable action, broadly accessible participation, and aligned incentives rather than manifestos alone.

## Current Profile
Webb's work in these sources connects technological possibility to institutional capacity. Her convergence forecast treats AI as an enabling layer across search, robotics, materials, and biology. Her governance argument applies the same systems lens to safety: rules and review processes should be judged not only by intent, but by whether they endure, operate across borders, and avoid turning compliance resources into an incumbent advantage.

## Key Characteristics
- Forecasts technology through convergence across AI, physical systems, materials, biology, and internet behavior.
- Treats governance as an incentive-and-accountability design problem rather than a choice between manifestos and regulation alone.
- Tests safety proposals for unequal compliance burdens on startups and frontier incumbents.
- Uses cross-domain analogies, including biotechnology regulation and corporate oversight bodies, to evaluate institutional design.
- Preserves human agency by framing technology's consequences as dependent on collective choices.

## Qualifications
The sources use Webb as an expert interviewee, not as a complete biography or an independent evaluation of the Future Today Strategy Group. Her biotech and Facebook analogies are compressed, and the September episode does not specify a complete cross-border incentive mechanism or independently establish the motives of frontier AI leaders.
