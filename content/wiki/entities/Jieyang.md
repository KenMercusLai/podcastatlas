# Jieyang / 揭阳

Jieyang is one of the three modern cities commonly used to define [[Chaoshan]] in [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046]]. The episode names it as part of the post-1991 regional structure after the old Shantou area was split into [[Shantou]], [[Chaozhou]], and Jieyang.

The page matters because the source treats administrative division as part of the region's development problem. The issue is not that separate cities cannot grow, but that duplicated infrastructure, local competition, and weaker coordination can make [[MerchantToIndustrialCityUpgrade]] harder when a region already lacks [[Shenzhen]]-level policy scale and external proximity.
