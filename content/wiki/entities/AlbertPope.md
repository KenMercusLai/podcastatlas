# Albert Pope / 阿尔伯特·波普

Albert Pope is the U.S. bicycle entrepreneur in [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]] who turns the bicycle from a mechanical novelty into a patent, marketing, credit, and road-infrastructure business. The source says he saw the high-wheel bicycle opportunity around the 1876 Philadelphia Centennial Exhibition, founded [[PopeManufacturingCompany]], and acquired key [[PierreLallement]] patent rights.

Pope's source role is deliberately ambivalent. His litigation and licensing look like a coercive patent strategy, while his advertising, installment payments, trade-in practices, and support for the [[GoodRoadsMovement]] helped expand the market that later bicycle and automobile users inherited.
