# Lewis Merrill

## Overview

Lewis Merrill is the U.S. Army major whose York County investigation supplied evidence for the federal suppression of the first Ku Klux Klan in South Carolina.

## Current Profile

Merrill arrived skeptical of reports about Klan violence but changed his judgment through investigation. The source presents him as discovering a captured local justice system in which the sheriff, judge, jurors, and suspected attackers overlapped, then working with Attorney General [[AmosTAkerman|Amos T. Akerman]] to build cases rather than treating troop presence alone as protection.

## Key Characteristics

- Federal Army officer deployed to York County during Reconstruction.
- Investigator whose conclusions changed in response to local evidence.
- Documented the overlap between Klan organization and formal local authority.
- Helped connect military presence to arrest, evidence, and federal prosecution.
