# Julie Rochman

## Overview
Julie Rochman is a former insurance executive and former chief executive of the [[InstituteForBusinessAndHomeSafety|Institute for Business and Home Safety]]. In [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-9de180c7bff8bba31ae5f11fce51f1e1-34185790-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-9de180c7bff8bba31ae5f11fce51f1e1-34185790]], she explains both the economic role of insurance and the case for insurer-funded building research.

## Current Profile
Rochman is presented as the organizer who converted a cross-industry research proposal into a $40 million laboratory. Her argument joined public-safety value to insurer incentives: rigorous evidence could identify hazardous materials, reward safer construction, improve risk decisions, and reduce repeated claims without requiring competing insurers to coordinate prices or markets.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames property and casualty insurance as infrastructure that lets households and businesses transfer otherwise unmanageable risks.
- Raised the laboratory budget from insurers with different geographic exposures.
- Tailored the research case to hurricanes, hail, wind, tornadoes, wildfire, and winter weather.
- Defends shared safety research while preserving antitrust boundaries between competitors.
- Separates credible structural science from problems that engineering alone cannot solve.
