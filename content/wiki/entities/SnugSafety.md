# Snug Safety

Snug Safety is the daily check-in service founded by [[PreetAnand]] and discussed in [[advice-line-with-chris-riccobono-of-untuckit-c087247f-1f85-4c15-b4aa-4ffea0368c4c]]. Users choose a daily check-in time; if they do not respond, Snug sends reminders and can alert emergency contacts. The paid plan can also call the user, call contacts, and arrange an emergency-services visit if safety is not confirmed.

The Advice Line question is awareness on a limited budget. The source says Snug has about $400,000 in annual revenue, 70% or greater growth, strong word of mouth, some Google traffic, and some success with senior YouTubers. [[ChrisRiccobono]] recommends testable radio/audio channels, while [[GuyRaz]] emphasizes [[TrustBasedDistribution]] through earned media, local publications, doctors, churches, and other trusted intermediaries.
