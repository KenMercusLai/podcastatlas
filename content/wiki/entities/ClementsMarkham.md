# Clements Markham

## Overview

Sir Clements Markham was an influential Royal Geographical Society patron presented in the bounded episode as a formative authority over [[RobertFalconScott]] and an opponent of dogs and skis in Antarctic travel.

## Current Profile

Markham represents the institutional side of Scott's expedition design. He helped establish Scott's position and carried enough prestige to shape decisions, yet the episode says he had never visited Antarctica and resisted methods with demonstrated polar value. His role illustrates how support can create obligation and how distant authority can displace field evidence.

The source does not establish that Markham alone determined the expedition's transport failures. His importance is diagnostic: a patron's confidence, status, and prior preferences can narrow an operator's choices even when the patron is not present in the operating environment.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior geographical patron who helped advance Scott's expedition leadership.
- Exercised influence through status and Scott's sense of obligation.
- Opposed dogs and skis despite their established polar usefulness.
- Had no direct Antarctic experience according to the episode.
- Embodies the tension between resource sponsorship and operational autonomy.
