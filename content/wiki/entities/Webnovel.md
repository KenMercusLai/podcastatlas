# Webnovel / 起点国际版

Webnovel / 起点国际版 appears in [[ep240-ba-zong-tian-chong-zai-haiwai-duanju-chuhai-de-chanye-mima-ltvxvfj5ko6djhsjatiphdy-dzek]] as the international platform through which Chinese web-fiction systems trained and attracted overseas local authors. The episode links it to [[Qidian|起点中文网]] and the broader [[YuewenGroup|阅文集团]] platform lineage.

The source's strongest Webnovel example is a Nigerian female author whose werewolf romance drew on Chinese romance-web-fiction techniques while using local and global fantasy material. That case shows [[ChineseWebFictionMethodology|中国网文方法论]] moving from translated Chinese novels toward overseas writers learning genre selection, editor feedback, pacing, and commercial storytelling.
