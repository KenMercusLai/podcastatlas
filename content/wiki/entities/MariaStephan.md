# Maria Stephan

## Overview

Maria Stephan is the scholar who challenged [[EricaChenoweth|Erica Chenoweth]] to compare violent and nonviolent campaigns systematically rather than reasoning from selected historical examples.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Stephan's decisive contribution as an evidentiary challenge. When Chenoweth cited successful armed revolutions and failed nonviolent uprisings, Stephan asked her to test the alternatives across comparable maximalist campaigns. Their collaboration then joined strict outcome coding to a mechanism-based account of participation, institutional defection, tactical adaptation, and discipline.

## Key Characteristics

- Scholar associated with comparative research on violent and nonviolent campaigns.
- Prompted a move from anecdotal rebuttal toward systematic historical comparison.
- Co-developed the episode's demanding definition of campaign success.
- Collaborator in the research underlying the reported effectiveness gap.
