# Graham Steele

Graham Steele appears in [[tsr-s5-ronconway-v5-tsr-s5-ronconway-v5]] as the [[USTreasury|U.S. Treasury]] point person [[WallyAdeyemo]] directed [[RonConway]] to during the [[SiliconValleyBank]] crisis. Conway says his pitch to Steele was that the government needed two words: "guarantee deposits."

The page is source-scoped because the episode gives Steele a process role rather than a long biography. His main wiki relevance is the way the SVB weekend moved from startup panic into Treasury decision-making around [[DepositGuaranteeCrisisResponse]], [[MoralHazardContagionTradeoff]], and the [[SystemicRiskException]].
