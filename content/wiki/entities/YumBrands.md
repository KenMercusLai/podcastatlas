# Yum Brands / 百胜美国

Yum Brands / 百胜美国 appears in [[145-gaijia-zhongzi-de-canyin-yangpinpai-feng-tou-quan-1-145-1]] as the U.S. brand owner selling [[PizzaHutChina]] brand ownership to [[YumChina]]. The source says the China deal was 1.2 billion USD, while global Pizza Hut business outside China was sold separately for 1.5 billion USD to a U.S. private-equity buyer.

The source uses Yum Brands to highlight how valuable the China operating region became relative to the rest of the brand. The case extends [[ForeignRestaurantBrandLocalControl]] because the China operator is not merely a licensee in the episode's frame; it becomes owner of the brand rights for its market.
