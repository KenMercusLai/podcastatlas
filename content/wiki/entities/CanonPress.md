# Canon Press

## Overview
Canon Press is the for-profit publisher and media company presented in the source as a commercial and distribution engine for [[DougWilson]]'s Christian social and political project.

## Current Profile
The company sells books, curricula, audiobooks, and podcasts and operates event and production facilities in Moscow, Idaho. The episode reports that Wilson describes it as a multimillion-dollar business and shows staff producing professional media across several formats. Its role is therefore both economic and ideological: it packages a local church-centered worldview for national audiences while reducing dependence on outside publishers or broadcasters.

Provocative campaigns such as “No Quarter November” connect culture-war attention to product promotion. That does not prove every controversy is planned or commercially successful, but it makes Canon Press a concrete mechanism through which attention can become audience, revenue, and recruitment.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates as a for-profit seller of religious books, curricula, audio, podcasts, and events.
- Gives Wilson's movement an owned production and distribution system.
- Links ideological mission to employee alignment and commercial output.
- Uses provocative online material to attract attention to products and arguments.
- Extends a local institutional ecosystem into a national media audience.
