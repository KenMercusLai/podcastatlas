# 张楚政权 / Zhang Chu Regime

张楚政权 / Zhang Chu Regime enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-2-qilai-bei-yapo-de-renmen-lpx9-iicwog4nj-nudaj-i4ajb25|Qinji 128-2]] as the rebel regime implied by [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] and [[WuGuang|吴广]]'s uprising. The episode does not narrate the regime's full creation or institutional form; it uses Zhang Chu mainly as the destination of the [[DazexiangUprising|大泽乡起义]] and as the setting for later internal fragility.

The source's most concrete Zhang Chu claim is negative. It says [[GeYing|葛婴]]'s death matters because it removes a major general and worsens suspicion between Chen Sheng and his subordinates. For now, this page keeps Zhang Chu source-scoped as an emerging anti-Qin regime whose legitimacy begins with [[RebelMandateManufacture|起义天命制造]] but whose durability is threatened by mistrust.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-3-chensheng-wuguang-zudui-chenggong-locnaq-fo1fztyud5nghwkteams|Qinji 128-3]] directly narrates Zhang Chu's creation. After Chen Sheng takes Chen County, local elders ask him to become Chu king; [[ZhangEr|张耳]] and [[ChenYu|陈馀]] advise against it and propose [[SixStateRestorationAntiQinStrategy|六国复国式反秦策略]] instead. Chen Sheng rejects that advice, declares himself Chu king, and names the regime Zhang Chu. The host glosses the name as "expanding Chu," making Zhang Chu a claim to larger succession of the unified realm, not just a local rebel label.

The same episode gives Zhang Chu an initial command move: [[WuGuang|吴广]] is made acting Zhang Chu king and sent to supervise generals moving west against [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]]. That makes the regime's first recorded direction both territorial and legitimacy-driven.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-5-wo-you-zhengju-zanmen-buneng-shuo-qinershi-sha-ye-bushi-ljuome-xea6sjv4hhzmrr0b0-1hg|Qinji 128-5]] shows Zhang Chu's momentum and fragility together. [[ZhouWenRebel|周文]] advances toward [[Xianyang|咸阳]] with a large gathered army, but [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] defeats him after Qin uses [[CapitalDefenseMobilization|都城防卫动员]] and [[LishanConvictMobilization|骊山刑徒应急动员]]. The defeat weakens the sense that Zhang Chu can simply roll west into Qin.

The same source makes the regime's internal authority problem sharper. [[WuChen|武臣]] declares himself Zhao king, [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] only recognizes him after [[CaiCi|蔡赐]] warns against creating another enemy, and [[ZhangEr|张耳]] plus [[ChenYu|陈馀]] then advise Zhao not to attack Qin but to expand itself. Zhang Chu therefore becomes the first center of anti-Qin revolt and the first center to lose strategic coordination under [[AntiQinCoalitionFragmentation|反秦阵营诸侯化分裂]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-1-jiemi-wuguang-bei-miekou-de-lishi-zhenxiang-lk1bkhqkwf9upbbj08pjkn8cstyp|Qinji 129-1]] shows Zhang Chu's military and internal crises converging. [[ZhouWenRebel|周文 / 周章]] is defeated repeatedly by [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] and kills himself, while [[WuGuang|吴广]] cannot take [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]] from [[LiYou|李由]]. [[TianZang|田臧]] then uses the crisis to justify killing Wu Guang, and Chen Sheng's promotion of Tian Zang turns battlefield difficulty into a regime-authority problem.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-2-lishishang-hejiu-neng-wu-duo-da-shi-zhuan-gei-jiugui-laogong-kan-zhe-ji-lvzxpoxclwdrr66bwejd-bga8dp|Qinji 129-2]] gives the sequel to that command crisis. Tian Zang leaves Li Gui and others around Yingyang, leads elite troops west, and is killed by [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] at Ao granary. Zhang Han then defeats Li Gui, Deng Shuang, and Wu Feng in sequence, while [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] executes Deng Shuang after retreat. Zhang Chu therefore loses both military initiative and internal trust: failed commanders can be killed by enemies, by peers, or by their own ruler.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-3-chensheng-zhisi-weishenme-dele-minxin-hanle-renxin-lnx4zs-vsi3zeoixnqskkpexmr3|Qinji 129-3]] turns that deterioration into terminal collapse. [[QinJiaRebel|秦嘉]] and [[ZhuJishi|朱吉石]] raise troops but reject the central monitor [[WuPingJunPan|武平君畔]], killing him under a false Chen Sheng order. [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] reaches the Chen County field, [[ZhangHeZhangChu|张鹤]] is defeated, [[ZhuangGu|庄谷]] kills Chen Sheng, and [[LuChenQinRebel|吕臣]] only partially repairs the symbolic damage by killing Zhuang Gu and reburying Chen Sheng. The regime now carries both the initiating glory of [[DazexiangUprising|大泽乡起义]] and the internal fear pattern of [[PunitiveInspectionLoyaltyTrap|苛察忠诚陷阱]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-4-jiuquangui-zhangliang-ruhe-zai-liubang-shouxia-anshen-lp6qwg3ma5hdy15g4box7pe23jav|Qinji 129-4]] shows the post-Chen-Sheng field splintering rather than disappearing. [[SongLiu|宋刘]]'s surrender at [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]] is punished by Qin, [[QinJiaRebel|秦嘉]] backs [[JingJu|景居 / 景驹]] as a new Chu claimant, and [[LuChenQinRebel|吕臣]] joins with [[YingBu|英布 / 黥布]] to defeat Qin forces and retake Chen County. Zhang Chu's central regime is gone, but the Chu banner persists through competing restorers, local commanders, and armed outsiders.
