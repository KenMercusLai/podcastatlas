# Wikipedia

## Overview
Wikipedia is the openly editable encyclopedia launched in 2001 as a wiki-based companion to [[Nupedia]] and later sustained through volunteer governance, nonprofit stewardship, and small donations.

## Current Profile
Wikipedia's wiki role joins a founding model with a current sustainability problem. The founder interview explains why immediate, partial contribution outpaced Nupedia's expert gatekeeping and how volunteers developed correction, moderation, neutrality, and sourcing rules as scale increased. The later [[MarketplaceTech]] source shows that this public knowledge can be consumed through search panels and AI summaries without equivalent visits, making future contributor motivation and institutional funding part of the same commons problem.

## Key Characteristics
- Open contribution system in which a small accurate edit can become the starting point for later collective improvement.
- Volunteer-governed knowledge community whose policies and enforcement developed through use, conflict, and visible failure.
- Reference layer intended to orient readers toward questions, citations, original sources, and unresolved discussion rather than replace verification.
- Nonprofit public-knowledge project designed to resist advertising-driven editorial priorities.
- Globally distributed community able to monitor and repair content across time zones.
- Knowledge commons exposed to answer-layer substitution when search or AI products satisfy users without sending visits back.

## Evidence
- Founding architecture - [[vmp5702291377-vmp5702291377]] says Wikipedia surpassed Nupedia's first-year article total in roughly two weeks after lowering the contribution threshold.
- Community correction - [[vmp5702291377-vmp5702291377]] describes volunteer responses to vandalism, disputes, neutrality, sourcing, and biographies of living people.
- Funding and stewardship - [[vmp5702291377-vmp5702291377]] links nonprofit status, advertising refusal, and small-donor fundraising to global free-knowledge priorities.
- Contemporary access pressure - [[tech-20261111-1111-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261111-1111-mp-tech-pod-128]] uses Wikipedia as an example of a public knowledge source whose direct visits can be displaced by search and AI answer layers.

## Qualifications
Open contribution does not make every revision reliable or every governance conflict self-resolving. Wales explicitly treats Wikipedia as a starting point rather than unquestioned authority, and the founder interview does not independently settle its full founding history, representation gaps, labor distribution, bias, policy disputes, or institutional controversies. The later source identifies referral risk without measuring its effect on Wikipedia contributions or finances.

## What Changed
- Established Wikipedia as both a low-barrier collaboration case and a living knowledge commons.
- Connected the founding funding model to later AI and search referral pressure.
