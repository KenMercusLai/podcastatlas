# 芒市 / Mangshi

## Overview
Mangshi / 芒市 is the Yunnan city and coffee-origin base discussed in [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Mangshi as both a coffee work base and a changing border-city destination. For [[YuWenjie|余文杰]], living there made small farmers, plots, processing batches, and local coffee culture more visible; for the hosts, it also carries food, religious architecture, ethnic culture, and a slower city scale.

## Key Characteristics
- Functions as an origin-work base for discovering small lots and farmer relationships.
- Sits inside the [[Dehong|德宏]] coffee map rather than only a tourism map.
- Has become more popular after 2023 while retaining a smaller-city daily rhythm in the source's account.
- Contains a growing local cafe scene that serves locals as well as visitors.

## Evidence
- Origin-work base: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] says Yu Wenjie stayed in Mangshi for about seven months in 2022 and used long residence to contact small farmers, plots, and small processing batches.
- Dehong coffee map: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] places Mangshi within Dehong's coffee resources and Babbitt Coffee's origin relationship.
- Tourism and daily life: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] says Mangshi became hotter as a destination after 2023, while still being small enough for daily movement by electric scooter.
- Cafe growth: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] reports Yu Wenjie's rough estimate that local cafes grew sharply, but marks the count as not officially checked.

## Qualifications
Cafe counts, tourism intensity, and local-market scale remain oral and source-scoped. The page should not treat Mangshi only as coffee; the current evidence is coffee-centered because this is the source's frame.
