# Streltsy / 射击军

## Overview

The Streltsy were an old Russian infantry institution and political force whose unrest became, in this episode, the immediate trigger for [[PeterTheGreat|Peter the Great]]'s return from the [[GreatEmbassy|Great Embassy]] and a campaign of torture, public execution, and institutional destruction.

## Current Profile

Peter had secured Streltsy loyalty during the 1689 defeat of Sophia, yet suspicion of the corps persisted. Complaints before the embassy led to exemplary executions, and the 1698 march on Moscow convinced Peter that the Streltsy represented both armed disobedience and a wider conservative conspiracy.

After loyal forces defeated the revolt, Peter reopened the case through dedicated torture chambers. Extracted confessions were used to implicate Sophia and justify executions staged across Moscow and outside her convent. The corps was then disbanded, removing an autonomous military-political constituency and strengthening Peter's personal command.

## Key Characteristics

- The corps combined military function with recurring political leverage in Moscow.
- Its relationship with Peter shifted from tactical loyalty to suspected existential threat.
- The 1698 revolt was defeated before Peter returned, but he expanded punishment afterward.
- Torture converted uncertain conspiracy claims into confessions and public spectacle.
- Institutional abolition was part of consolidating autocratic control, not only punishing individual mutineers.

- Repression and abolition: [[563-peter-the-great-bloodbath-in-the-kremlin-part-2-glt4388388819]] describes torture chambers, coerced confessions, mass executions, Sophia's confinement, and disbandment.

## Qualifications

The source says Sophia almost certainly did not know of the revolt, so torture-derived claims of a coordinated restoration plot should not be treated as settled fact. Exact numbers, execution details, Peter's personal participation, and the institutional history of the Streltsy beyond this episode remain source-scoped.
