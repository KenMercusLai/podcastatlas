# NetEase

[[qishui-yinyue-chongji-tengxun-wangyi-tesila-deng-cheqi-zai-guonei-kaizhan-daguimo-zhaohui-1008396395]] adds NetEase through [[NetEaseCloudMusic|NetEase Cloud Music]]. The source says NetEase Cloud Music's first-half net profit fell by nearly 60%, while [[QishuiMusic|汽水音乐]]'s cited June 2026 monthly active users surpassed it, making music another area where NetEase faces platform-entry pressure.

NetEase appears in [[2026-ai-youxi-quanjing-saomiao-si-ceng-tujing-san-da-wuqu-yi-ge-gongshi-quekou-duitan-405-youju-xiaoning-lgk71gytqtsvkc-wipz0hkzkemne]] as one of the large Chinese game companies changing its posture toward AI. [[Xiaoning]] says NetEase had not seemed as active in AI application earlier, but public signals in 2026 showed more active AI adoption.

[[no-200-dianshang-sanguo-zhi-qunxiong-zhulu-yaogua-gongzhang-chijian-juqian-yiji-108-zhong-sifa-974601500]] adds NetEase's ecommerce experiments through [[NetEaseYanxuan]] and [[NetEaseKaola]]. Yanxuan represents selected-goods and ODM ecommerce, while Kaola represents the cross-border import window that NetEase later sold to [[Alibaba]].
