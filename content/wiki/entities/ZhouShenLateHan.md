# 周慎 / Zhou Shen (late Han)

周慎 / Zhou Shen enters through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-935-ren-huo-yi-kou-qi-nan-cheng-de-shi-ziji-lmy8tyrttv-gjvke7sdi6dx-oopw|Hanji 935]] as the late-Han commander appointed under [[ZhangWenLateHan|张温]] during the 185 CE campaign against [[BianZhangLateHan|边章]] and [[HanSui|韩遂]]. After the rebels withdraw toward Yuzhong, Zhang Wen sends Zhou Shen with 30,000 troops in pursuit.

The episode's main use of Zhou Shen is a logistics failure. [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] advises him to cut the rebels' grain road while the main force supports from behind, arguing that the city force cannot hold if external supply is severed. Zhou Shen instead besieges the city directly, and the rebels cut the Han army's own grain road at Kuiyuan Gorge, forcing a retreat.

This page is source-scoped. Zhou Shen matters here as the foil that reveals Sun Jian's early operational judgment and as a late-Han example of how ignoring supply-line logic can turn numerical strength into vulnerability.
