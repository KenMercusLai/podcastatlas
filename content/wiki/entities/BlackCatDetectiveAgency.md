# 黑猫侦探社 / Black Cat Detective Agency

## Overview
黑猫侦探社 / Black Cat Detective Agency is the crime podcast founded by [[MiziCrimePodcaster|咪仔]] during her pandemic-era stay in Switzerland. The source uses the show to examine self-taught audio production, nonfiction responsibility, recommendation-driven growth, hostile feedback, paid-content design, and the difficulty of supporting a small team through Chinese podcast revenue.

## Current Profile
The show began as a low-resource experiment after Mizi could not find suitable work or an initial co-host. Early episodes were recorded on a phone and edited through newly learned tools; the first serial-killer subject was retrospectively judged too broad. Platform and short-video recommendations helped the audience grow, but discovery appears episodic and externally dependent rather than predictable.

Commercially, 黑猫侦探社 sits inside a difficult category. Crime subject matter can deter advertisers, annual subscription feels more expensive than low-priced single episodes, and production conscience can turn payment into escalating labor. The source nevertheless treats the program as a professional project whose journalism-derived ethics, narrative craft, and small team deserve sustainable compensation.

## Key Characteristics
- It is a nonfiction crime show organized around researched, sound-designed narrative.
- Its early production was bootstrapped through phone recording, self-taught editing, and iterative topic selection.
- External platform and creator recommendations played a significant role in discovery.
- The show preserves complete free public-interest context while using paid material for additional depth.
- Crime-category brand concerns, limited market scale, and annual-payment friction constrain revenue.
- A roughly four-person team remains only partly supported by show income according to the source.

## Qualifications
The source is an interview with the founder, not an audience, revenue, or editorial audit. Team size, subscriber changes, income, recommendation effects, and the completeness of particular case series remain source-reported. The page does not assess the accuracy or sensitivity of individual crime episodes.
