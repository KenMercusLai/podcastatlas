# 庞淯 / Pang Yu (late Han)

## Overview
庞淯 / Pang Yu is a Jiuquan official whose public mourning for the murdered inspector [[HandanShangLateHan|邯郸商]] turns loyalty into an action taken under an explicit death threat.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1076 places Pang Yu as county chief at Poqiang when [[ZhangMengLateHan|张猛]] kills Handan Shang and forbids mourning. Pang Yu resigns, travels to Wuwei without delay, mourns openly, and carries a dagger intending to kill Zhang Meng. Zhang Meng does not punish him and instead permits him to assist with the funeral, making Pang Yu's conduct a reputation constraint even on the man he opposes.

## Key Characteristics
- Jiuquan man serving as chief of Poqiang in the episode's account.
- Resigns office rather than let official position block his obligation to Handan Shang.
- Publicly mourns despite Zhang Meng's threat to kill mourners.
- Prepares personal revenge by carrying a dagger to Zhang Meng's residence.
- Survives because Zhang Meng reads his loyalty as conduct that should be honored rather than suppressed.
