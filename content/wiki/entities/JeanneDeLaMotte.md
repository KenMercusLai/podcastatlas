# Jeanne de la Motte

## Overview

Jeanne de la Motte was the principal confidence trickster in the [[DiamondNecklaceAffair|Diamond Necklace Affair]], using claimed Valois ancestry, forged royal access, theatrical impersonation, and [[CardinalDeRohan|Cardinal de Rohan]]'s ambition to obtain a vast diamond necklace.

## Current Profile

The source presents de la Motte as an effective social engineer who turned several parties' desires into mutual confirmation. She gained Rohan's support by presenting herself as an impoverished Valois descendant, claimed intimacy with [[MarieAntoinette|Marie Antoinette]], arranged a staged garden encounter with a lookalike, and supplied forged correspondence that Rohan accepted as royal. After the necklace passed through Rohan, her associates dismantled it and attempted to sell its diamonds. Conviction and severe public punishment did not end her political effect: after escaping to London, she recast herself as a persecuted insider and published sexual slanders that matched existing hostility toward the queen.

## Key Characteristics

- Claimed Valois descent and converted aristocratic genealogy into credibility and access.
- Identified Rohan's desire for royal favor as the main vulnerability in the fraud.
- Used staged embodiment, forged letters, secrecy, and intermediaries as mutually reinforcing evidence.
- Coordinated the necklace's transfer and dispersal through lovers and her husband.
- Reframed conviction and punishment as evidence of royal persecution after escaping to London.
- Converted private fraud into public political damage through allegations audiences were already prepared to accept.

## Qualifications

The episode identifies de la Motte as the central fraudster, but many intimate details come through partisan legal memoirs and later self-justification. Its claim that she and Rohan probably had an affair, the exact choreography of the garden scene, and private motives remain source-scoped. Her severe punishment can be condemned without accepting her claims about Marie Antoinette as true.
