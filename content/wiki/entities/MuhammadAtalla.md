# Muhammad Atalla

Muhammad Atalla is named in [[zhenzheng-gaibian-shijie-de-jishu-weishenme-yikaishi-dou-bu-bei-kanhao-s10e16-8c95b3dc-d75a-4bdd-84d4-2c06fd2d85b1]] as one of the [[BellLabs]] researchers associated with the early [[MOSFET|MOS transistor]]. The source uses his work with [[DawonKahng]] to show how a technology can be technically important before an incumbent organization sees a matching business need.

In the episode's interpretation, the MOS route was not immediately attractive because it was unstable and slow compared with [[BipolarJunctionTransistor|BJT]] devices. Its later importance came from being cheaper, smaller, and better suited to integration.
