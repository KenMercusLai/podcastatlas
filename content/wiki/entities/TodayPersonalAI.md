# Today Personal AI

## Overview
Today is a personal-agent product presented as a named, continuous digital assistant that learns a user over time, initiates useful work, and progresses from reminders toward task completion.

## Current Profile
[[TodayPersonalAI|Today]] combines [[PersonalAIMemory|long-term personal memory]] with [[ProactiveAgents|proactive service]]. Users can connect sources such as email, calendars, Slack, Notion, documents, and recordings; the product is described as preparing daily priorities, meeting material, research, messages, scheduling, and follow-up while learning preferences, relationships, and recurring goals.

Its intended interface is one familiar front agent rather than a visible team the user must coordinate. The product's claimed advantage is not raw connector breadth but a [[PersonalAgentUnderstandingLayer|context-interpretation layer]] that decides whose information a fact concerns, when it was true, whether it remains relevant, and whether the next step should be a quiet update, suggestion, confirmation request, or action. The team frames success as returning time and attention to the user, but the source does not establish broad reliability, retention, payment willingness, or product-market fit.

## Key Characteristics
- Centers the product on continually revised memory and calibrated initiative.
- Uses connected personal and work services to prepare and coordinate recurring tasks.
- Presents one named front agent while allowing specialist execution behind the interface.
- Tries to lower repeated explanation through retained preferences, relationships, and task context.
- Gives users visibility into recorded memory plus deletion and account-exit controls.
- Seeks to progress from information and reminders toward low-risk execution with graduated confirmation.
- Favors a general personal context layer while prioritizing deeper recurring workflows.

## Qualifications
The profile rests on one launch-period podcast summary featuring the team, not an independent product audit. Task success, privacy implementation, model evaluation, user growth, retention, product-market fit, cost, and willingness to pay are not independently established. Episode 275's Town/Today naming ambiguity does not prove that Town and Today are the same product, so this page keeps them distinct.

## What Changed
- Created the product entity from its launch-period episode.
- Separated Today from the earlier Town/Today transcript ambiguity.
- Recorded identity-aware memory, low-interruption initiative, and reduced delegation friction as the product's central thesis.
