## Overview

傅太后 / Fu Taihou enters the wiki as a late [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] consort-side power figure whose death and posthumous honors remain politically active. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-595-wangmang-shazi-jielu-quanli-de-canku-lh9nr18cllq1rrl0nz4fzipai4eq|Hanji 595]] supplies the earlier death-and-burial layer: after Fu Taihou dies, she is buried with [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] at Weiling and styled Xiaoyuan Fu Huanghou, creating an awkward ritual-political position while [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] is still alive. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-626-waren-fenmu-wangmang-jing-ruci-sangxinbingkuang-lotwk6rbtl5pvbrnlfrysu5ol6rz|Hanji 626]] then shows [[WangMang|王莽]] later reopening that status as a 5 CE retrospective prosecution.

## Current Profile

Hanji 595 says Fu Taihou's death does not end her political effects. Her burial with Han Yuandi and posthumous title as Xiaoyuan Fu Huanghou elevate a former consort-side figure into a ritual field that embarrasses the still-living Wang Zhengjun and gives Wang Mang later leverage against the Fu family.

Hanji 626 says Wang Mang accuses Fu Taihou of having exceeded the rank proper to a vassal ruler's consort-side household. Her tomb, seals, ribbons, coffin, jeweled garments, and temple memory are treated as evidence of above-rank status. Wang Zhengjun initially refuses to approve the grave-opening request, but Wang Mang presses until her tomb is opened, its honors are stripped, and the burial landscape is leveled.

Fu Taihou's wiki role is therefore a posthumous-status case. The episode does not reconstruct her lifetime politics in detail; it shows how a dead woman's title and tomb can become a live instrument for [[LiAsPoliticalOrder|礼制政治秩序]], factional sorting, and [[TombDesecrationPoliticalViolence|陵墓亵渎式政治暴力]].

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han consort-side figure remembered through death, burial, posthumous rank conflict, and later tomb prosecution.
- Mother of the Dingtao king and connected to the earlier Ai-era title settlement.
- Dead political target whose tomb goods and ritual honors are recoded as improper privilege.
- Case through which Wang Mang converts ritual correction into grave violation and public punishment.

## Qualifications

Fu Taihou is source-bounded here. Hanji 595 supports her death, burial with Han Yuandi, posthumous styling, and later vulnerability, while Hanji 626 supports the later tomb prosecution. The page does not settle the full earlier court dispute over her title, the exact received-text form of her posthumous title, her lifetime agency, or Wang Mang's inner motive.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 595's death-and-burial layer before Hanji 626's posthumous prosecution and tomb-destruction case.
