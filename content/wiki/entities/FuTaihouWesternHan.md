## Overview

傅太后 / Fu Taihou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-626-waren-fenmu-wangmang-jing-ruci-sangxinbingkuang-lotwk6rbtl5pvbrnlfrysu5ol6rz|Hanji 626]] as the mother of the Dingtao king whose posthumous rank and burial treatment become targets of [[WangMang|王莽]]'s 5 CE retrospective prosecution. The episode treats her less as an active character than as a dead political target through whom Wang Mang reopens the earlier [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]-era consort-rank dispute.

## Current Profile

The source says Wang Mang accuses Fu Taihou of having exceeded the rank proper to a vassal ruler's consort-side household. Her tomb, seals, ribbons, coffin, jeweled garments, and temple memory are treated as evidence of above-rank status. [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] initially refuses to approve the grave-opening request, but Wang Mang presses until her tomb is opened, its honors are stripped, and the burial landscape is leveled.

Fu Taihou's wiki role is therefore a posthumous-status case. The episode does not reconstruct her lifetime politics in detail; it shows how a dead woman's title and tomb can become a live instrument for [[LiAsPoliticalOrder|礼制政治秩序]], factional sorting, and [[TombDesecrationPoliticalViolence|陵墓亵渎式政治暴力]].

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han consort-side figure remembered in this source through posthumous rank conflict.
- Mother of the Dingtao king and connected to the earlier Ai-era title settlement.
- Dead political target whose tomb goods and ritual honors are recoded as improper privilege.
- Case through which Wang Mang converts ritual correction into grave violation and public punishment.

## Qualifications

Fu Taihou is source-bounded here. The page does not settle the full earlier court dispute over her title, the exact received-text form of her posthumous title, her lifetime agency, or Wang Mang's inner motive. Those should remain source-scoped until fuller Ai-era notes are ingested.
