## Overview

国渊 / Guo Yuan is a late-Han official represented through agricultural administration, restrained reporting of domestic revolt, an anonymous-letter investigation, promotion under [[CaoCao|曹操]], and a source-scoped portrait of austere personal conduct. The semantic suffix in this page key distinguishes him from [[GuoYuanLateHan|郭援 / 郭元]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1127-caocao-cuanwei-sanjian-ta-zhide-shi-shenme-livrh3obobkcfhj_ngnhe-kgv2ls|Hanji 1127]] supplies the administrative prequel. It credits Guo Yuan with productive屯田 work, says he supports clemency for surrenderers after the Hejian revolt, and emphasizes that he reports the domestic victory without the customary multiplication of killed and captured. His distinction is reputational as well as factual: inflated figures may deter an external enemy, but boasting about subjects in rebellion advertises a failure of internal order. The episode then introduces his inference that an anonymous accusation quoting [[ZhangHengLateHan|张衡]]'s *Er Jing Fu* came from someone familiar with the text.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1128-meiyou-heikeji-guren-ruhe-diaocha-nimingxin-lqdm57jzw9wqo_umlasz2j3vlzqh|Hanji 1128]] completes that investigation. Guo Yuan sends three people to find someone capable of explaining the text; they pose as students and ask the target to write a letter. He then compares that sample with the accusation, identifies a suspect, and orders arrest and interrogation.

The episode says Cao Cao promotes him to taipu after the case. It also portrays him as wearing plain cloth, eating simple food, distributing salary and rewards to relatives and old associates, and dying in office. These biographical details add a frugality-and-service profile but remain bounded to this popular-history source.

## Key Characteristics

- Investigator who begins with what the document reveals about its author's knowledge.
- Uses a pretext to obtain a comparison sample before coercive action.
- Treats handwriting resemblance as corroboration within a narrowed candidate field.
- Administrator who distinguishes external victory signaling from honest reporting of domestic disorder.
- Source-scoped agricultural official associated with increased stores and clemency toward controlled surrenderers.
- Cao-side official rewarded with promotion after solving the case.

## Qualifications

The source summaries do not supply the anonymous letter's full text, the number of plausible candidates, comparison criteria, independent corroboration, false-positive controls, or interrogation record. The case should not be projected backward as validated modern forensic handwriting analysis. Harvest claims, reporting custom, captive totals, office chronology, and the virtue portrait also require direct historical-text corroboration.
