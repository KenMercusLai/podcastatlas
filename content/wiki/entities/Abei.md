# 阿北 / Abei

阿北 / Abei is the [[Douban|豆瓣]] founder figure in [[no-212-cengjing-de-jingshen-jiaoluo-douban-zhihu-tieba-hupu-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-25-1003510980]]. The episode describes him as a reader, science-fiction fan, programmer, Tsinghua physics student, U.S. PhD student, IBM employee, traveler, and early returnee entrepreneur before Douban.

The source uses Abei to explain Douban's product temperament. His earlier travel-guide startup failed, but Douban's 2005 launch combined book taste, recommendations, groups, and a restrained interface into a platform whose slogan was "萝卜青菜各有所爱." The same temperament also shaped what Douban refused: copied growth tactics, heavy ads, points-driven spam, content-farm behavior, and a full turn toward algorithmic media.

Abei therefore sits at the center of the wiki's [[MobileCommunityFragmentationRisk]] and [[OnlineCommunityCommercializationFriction]] branch. The source treats his ideals as both a protective force and a strategic constraint: they preserved a rare cultural mood, but also contributed to missed mobile, ticketing, ecommerce, and community-product opportunities.
