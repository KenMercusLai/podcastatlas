# 李渊 / Li Yuan (Tang)

李渊 / Li Yuan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-2-pandian-huangdimen-jiuhou-xiaopihao-livpg3zh7lsqffgca2pomiqnlvyd|Qinji 131-2]] as the future Tang founder in the Jinyang-palace drinking story. The page uses the `LiYuanTang` slug to avoid collision with [[LiYuanWarringStates|李源 / 李元]], the Warring States Chu succession actor already in the wiki.

In the source's account, [[PeiJi|裴寂]] hosts Li Yuan, keeps pressing drink, and arranges palace women in a way that leaves Li Yuan exposed to punishment under [[YangGuangSui|杨广]]. [[LiShimin|李世民]] and Pei Ji then turn that danger into an argument for rebellion: if exposure means death anyway, Li Yuan may as well act first.

The source treats this as the episode's "drinking can accomplish things" counterexample, but with a strong qualification. Alcohol creates the irreversible situation; it does not supply Li Yuan's later ability, family support, or political capacity.
