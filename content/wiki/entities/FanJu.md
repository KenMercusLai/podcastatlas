# 范雎 / Fan Ju

范雎 / Fan Ju appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-67-zhanguo-mieba-baiqi-de-junshi-caineng-jiujing-ruhe-ljw0lltowxd4qz75v-wcnlkwqskv]] through a [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] dialogue about [[YiqueBattle|伊阙之战]]. In the episode's use of the passage, Fan Ju praises [[BaiQi|白起]] for breaking the Han-Wei coalition and killing 240,000 troops, which gives Bai Qi the occasion to explain the victory.

The page is source-scoped because this episode does not yet develop Fan Ju's later political career. His function here is interpretive: by asking or praising, he lets the source frame Bai Qi's victory as an analytical reading of terrain, coalition incentives, and enemy disunity rather than a lucky or purely brutal outcome.
