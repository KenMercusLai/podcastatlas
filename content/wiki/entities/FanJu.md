# 范雎 / Fan Ju

范雎 / Fan Ju appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-67-zhanguo-mieba-baiqi-de-junshi-caineng-jiujing-ruhe-ljw0lltowxd4qz75v-wcnlkwqskv]] through a [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] dialogue about [[YiqueBattle|伊阙之战]]. In the episode's use of the passage, Fan Ju praises [[BaiQi|白起]] for breaking the Han-Wei coalition and killing 240,000 troops, which gives Bai Qi the occasion to explain the victory.

The page is source-scoped because this episode does not yet develop Fan Ju's later political career. His function here is interpretive: by asking or praising, he lets the source frame Bai Qi's victory as an analytical reading of terrain, coalition incentives, and enemy disunity rather than a lucky or purely brutal outcome.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-87-xialu-xiangfeng-yongzhe-sheng-zhongyu-zhaodao-chuchu-le-loppr83sdq5hhesmqihckwmhpl2h]] returns to Fan Ju only as a teaser for the next branch. The episode says Fan Ju accompanies a Wei envoy to [[QiState|齐国]], receives recognition from [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]], and is later punished by Wei Qi after returning to Wei. The transcript renders the name as 范珠/范居, so this page treats the notice as a source-scoped normalization rather than a full career account.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-88-ni-zenme-kandai-fenghuangnan-1-lpuxkdq-rvnifuoneq212g53w5z5]] develops that teaser into Fan Ju's origin crisis. The episode presents him as a poor but ambitious Wei man who cannot reach the Wei ruler directly and therefore serves under [[XuJia|须贾 / 徐谷]]. When [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]] rewards him during the Qi mission, Xu Jia reports the gift as possible betrayal, and [[WeiQiWarringStates|魏齐]] has Fan Ju beaten and publicly humiliated without real investigation.

The same source turns Fan Ju's near death into a career pivot. [[ZhengAnping|郑安平]] helps him escape, Fan Ju hides under the alias 张禄, and [[WangJiQin|王稽 / 王继]] later brings him into [[QinState|秦国]] as a talent for [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]]. His biography therefore now links [[PrivatePunishmentWithoutProcedure|无程序私刑]] in Wei to [[WarringStatesCareeristMobility|interstate career mobility]] and to the coming Qin court struggle against [[WeiRan|魏冉]].
