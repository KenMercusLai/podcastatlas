# Kondrati Selivanov

## Overview

Kondrati Selivanov was an eighteenth-century Russian sectarian leader associated with the Skoptsy, claims to be both the Messiah and Peter III, and organized bodily removal as a path away from sexual sin.

## Current Profile

[[45-top-ten-eunuchs-glt5221212176]] presents Selivanov as the episode's most developed collective case of [[ReligiousSelfCastration]]. Unlike disputed stories about Origen or Boston Corbett's individual act, Selivanov built communal authority around a doctrine that treated sexual organs as marks of original sin.

The Skoptsy reportedly practiced forms of male castration and female breast removal, survived into the modern period, and were later suppressed by the Soviet state. Selivanov's claims to be both the Messiah and the former tsar Peter III joined spiritual purification to charismatic and political identity.

## Key Characteristics

- Russian peasant and sectarian leader active from the eighteenth century.
- Charismatic claimant to messianic and imperial identities.
- Leader associated with organized male and female bodily-removal practices.
- Figure who turned sexual renunciation into communal discipline and identity.

## Evidence

- Doctrine and leadership - [[45-top-ten-eunuchs-glt5221212176]] connects Selivanov to the Skoptsy and the belief that sexual organs represented original sin.
- Charismatic claims - [[45-top-ten-eunuchs-glt5221212176]] reports his identification with both the Messiah and Peter III.
- Movement scale and suppression - [[45-top-ten-eunuchs-glt5221212176]] describes a large following continuing into the twentieth century before Soviet repression.

## Qualifications

Membership estimates, the uniformity of Skoptsy belief and practice, Selivanov's motives, and the details of suppression remain episode-attributed. The source's labels should not imply that every participant had the same degree of agency or that female and male procedures were equivalent.
