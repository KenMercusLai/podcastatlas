## Overview

薛泽 / Xue Ze enters the wiki as the Western Han official appointed chancellor after [[HanAnguoWesternHan|韩安国]] breaks a leg while serving in an acting capacity. The episode presents his long but low-visibility tenure as a political-survival case rather than as proof of distinguished administration.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-266-sihui-furan-yu-yi-pao-niao-de-gushi-2-ltzr_gqioz9ftrqzbs7gyced90fm|Hanji 266 part 2]] identifies Xue Ze as a grandson of Gaozu-era meritorious official Xue Ou and says [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] elevates him after Han Anguo's riding injury blocks the expected permanent appointment. The host gives his tenure as roughly seven years, from Yuanguang 4 to Yuanshuo 5, and says he leaves little recorded achievement or personal biography.

The episode interprets this thin record as cautious survival under an autocratic emperor: Xue Ze reportedly commits no major fault and therefore preserves his life. The bounded conclusion is narrower than “doing nothing is good government.” In this source, low visibility is associated with personal safety, but the episode supplies too little policy evidence to evaluate administrative quality or the full reason for his survival.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han chancellor appointed after Han Anguo's injury changes the succession to the office.
- Descendant of the Gaozu-era meritorious official Xue Ou, according to the episode.
- Long-serving but thinly documented officeholder portrayed as having neither conspicuous achievement nor major fault.
- Political-survival case used by the host to illustrate the danger of high office under Han Wudi.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on one short podcast episode. The precise offices, dates, kinship, duration, reasons for appointment and removal, absence of a biography, policy record, and claim that caution preserved Xue Ze's life require comparison with the underlying historical records. A sparse narrative record does not by itself prove inactivity, and survival does not establish administrative success.
