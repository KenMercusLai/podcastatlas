# Yuvika Sharma

## Overview
Yuvika Sharma is a data and AI practitioner and mentor interviewed in [[ep-22-governing-ai-with-purpose-and-inclusion|Data Science With Sam EP22]] and presented as a co-founder of [[AsianWomenAdvancingAI]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Sharma as bringing more than two decades of cross-sector data and AI experience to a governance view centered on enforceable rules, continuous bias control, proportional oversight, and public trust. She also connects responsible AI to leadership representation, sponsorship, mentorship, and safer professional communities for Asian women.

## Key Characteristics
- Compares governance to traffic rules and enforcement for a digital highway.
- Locates bias across data, labels, algorithms, deployment, and user interaction rather than in one technical stage.
- Advocates audits, testing, red teaming, human review, domain expertise, and affected-community participation.
- Supports [[RiskTieredAIOversight]] that scales with potential harm.
- Treats irresponsible deployment as a threat to innovation because harm and backlash erode trust.
- Co-founded [[AsianWomenAdvancingAI]] around mentorship, learning, visibility, and representation.

## Evidence
- Governance and bias position: [[ep-22-governing-ai-with-purpose-and-inclusion]] attributes the traffic-law analogy and multi-stage bias account to Sharma.
- Risk position: [[ep-22-governing-ai-with-purpose-and-inclusion]] has her distinguish low-stakes photo organization from medical and criminal-justice uses.
- Trust position: [[ep-22-governing-ai-with-purpose-and-inclusion]] attributes to her examples of public backlash after data misuse, vehicle accidents, and facial-recognition harms.
- Representation position: [[ep-22-governing-ai-with-purpose-and-inclusion]] records her experience as the only woman in technical rooms and her argument for inclusive leadership pipelines, role models, mentorship, sponsorship, and equitable spaces.
