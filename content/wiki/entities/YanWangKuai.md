# 燕王哙 / King Kuai of Yan

燕王哙 / King Kuai of Yan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-40-5-yue-chusheng-de-haizi-re-shui-la-ll9e-thidxl5cqoacfrbz-ygw8-t]] as the successor after [[YanYiwang|燕易王]] dies in the 321 BCE cluster under [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]].

The source gives only the succession notice. Its immediate function is chronological: Yan's ruler handoff sits beside the Zhou royal handoff while the episode's narrative weight moves to [[TianYing|田婴]] and [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]] in [[QiState|齐国]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-47-yanwangkuai-de-lixiangzhuyi-lhgl6rqkv9nonzxc9xqquzzlz5pr]] gives the main explanation of his collapse. After the failed anti-Qin coalition, Yan Wang Kuai knows he is not strong enough to make Yan powerful, and [[ZiZhi|子之]], [[SuDai|苏代]], and [[LuMaoshou|鹿毛寿]] exploit that self-doubt plus his desire for moral reputation.

The episode does not deny Yan Wang Kuai's personal benevolence. It says he can be frugal, caring, and uninterested in sensual or material excess, but still politically incompetent because he treats ancient abdication ideals as usable court procedure. By giving Zi Zhi appointment power over senior officials, he turns trust into [[RuleBreakingAbdication|破坏规则式禅让]] and breaks the succession boundary that would normally pass rule to his son.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-48-junwang-weisha-buneng-chuanwei-zaixiang-lnwhljnn888mzlj7let2ptn1pcdm]] gives the immediate consequence. After [[ZiZhi|子之]] has held power for three years, Yan is in chaos, [[ShiBei|市被]] and Yan Wang Kuai's crown prince attack Zi Zhi and fail, and [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]] uses the disorder to send [[ZhangZiQiGeneral|张子]] into [[YanState|燕国]]. Qi then kills both Zi Zhi and Yan Wang Kuai, making his failed abdication a family, state, and personal destruction case under [[LiAsPoliticalOrder|礼制政治秩序]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-49-mengzi-yue-wo-jiao-ni-zhiguo-ni-que-ba-wo-dang-huaping-lhy14pbf9thcfdiaulcmf8dwcef8]] adds the aftermath context around Yan's vulnerability. The episode begins after [[QiState|齐国]] has conquered [[YanState|燕国]] and uses [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] to argue that occupation can be legitimate only if Yan's people experience it as rescue rather than added suffering.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2]] uses Yan Wang Kuai as the negative comparison for later Yan recovery. The source says he mistook [[ZiZhi|子之]] for a worthy person and helped produce Yan's collapse, while [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]]'s later use of [[LeYi|乐毅]] and [[JuXin|剧辛]] shows the opposite personnel judgment.
