## Overview
Melody Harrison was [[JustinHarrison]]'s mother and the person represented by the first Versona discussed in the supplied [[HiddenBrain]] episode about [[YouOnlyVirtual]].

## Current Profile
The episode portrays Melody as a single mother who created a close bond with Justin through care, advocacy, and repeated rituals such as drives to Disneyland. Her own bereavement, alcohol use, bipolar disorder, and psychiatric hospitalization placed strain on the family, while her later husband Daniel brought stability and adopted Justin without displacing their mother-son bond.

After Melody developed advanced gallbladder cancer, Justin collected interviews and communications for a digital representation. The resulting system reproduces reported features of her relationship with him—concern for his health, advice, desire for frequent contact, tone, and voice—but the episode explicitly leaves open how completely Melody understood or consented to an evolving representation that could later know things she never did.

## Key Characteristics
- Built an unusually close caregiving and ritual-rich bond with Justin.
- Experienced major bereavement and serious mental-health and alcohol-related difficulties as described by the episode.
- Supported Justin during his recovery from a severe motorcycle crash.
- Encouraged him to attend an investor presentation rather than remain at her hospital near the end of her life.
- Became the source person for an evolving digital recreation whose later outputs extend beyond her historical knowledge.
