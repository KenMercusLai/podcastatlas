# Yuhao / 宇豪

Yuhao is the [[Kuse]] and [[Junior]] co-founder/CTO interviewed in [[women-shi-ruhe-dingyi-openclaw-for-teams-xin-chanpin-xingtai-de-duitan-kuse-junior-lianchuang-jian-cto-yuhao-lkp1a0todflxoyycyo3zhrap3ebv]]. He describes prior work on [[Meta]]/Facebook Stories and SmartNews-related AI and moderation systems before building Kuse.

His source role is founder-operator diagnosis. He argues that agent products need [[AgentEvaluationBenchmarks]], usage-aware pricing, security testing, and product forms that reflect real customer workflow rather than only model capability. His strongest product claim is that [[Junior]] should be understood as an AI employee with company identity, accounts, projects, and authority boundaries, not as a personal assistant shared by a team.
