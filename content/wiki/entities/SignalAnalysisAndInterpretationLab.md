# Signal Analysis and Interpretation Lab

The Signal Analysis and Interpretation Lab, or SAIL, is [[SriNarayanan]]'s [[UniversityOfSouthernCalifornia|USC]] lab in [[tech-20260730-0730-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260730-0730-mp-tech-pod-128]]. The source uses the lab's machine-learning work on real-time MRI videos of beatboxers to show how AI can analyze detailed physical behavior rather than only text or generated media.

SAIL matters in the wiki as an example of [[BehavioralSignalProcessing]] with health and identity stakes. The episode says the lab studies human physical patterns and behavioral signals, including work related to neurodevelopment, autism, and early depression biomarkers, while keeping privacy, bias, and human agency visible as design concerns.
