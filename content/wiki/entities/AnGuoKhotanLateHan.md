## Overview

安国 / Anguo is the son of [[KhotanKingJianLateHan|于阗王建]] installed as Khotan king after Wang Jing's banquet killing and Shubo's failed self-enthronement in Hanji 838. His importance is succession repair: Khotanese actors use Jian's line to stabilize the polity after two violent disruptions.

## Current Profile

Anguo appears only at the end of the Hanji 838 incident. After Wang Jing kills King Jian and Shubo kills Wang Jing, Shubo tries to make himself ruler. Khotanese people reject Shubo and kill him, then install Anguo as the original king's son.

The source does not develop Anguo's reign. His current wiki role is therefore narrow: he is the legitimate or acceptable succession endpoint after rumor-driven intervention and a failed internal seizure.

## Key Characteristics

- Son of King Jian and successor chosen after Jian's violent death.
- Succession endpoint accepted after Khotanese people reject Shubo's self-enthronement.
- Restores the royal line rather than rewarding the general who killed Wang Jing.
- Source-scoped figure whose role is stabilization, not a developed reign profile.
