# 诸葛玄 / Zhuge Xuan

诸葛玄 / Zhuge Xuan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-995-ta-zuishang-cibei-weihe-duoci-cansha-enren-llicukoezccd5iycin4uq7qun51k|Hanji 995]] as [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s appointed administrator for [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]]. [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]] sends [[ZhuHaoLateHan|朱皓]] as a competing administrator to attack him, and Zhuge Xuan retreats to Xicheng.

The episode does not develop Zhuge Xuan beyond that administrative contest. His source role is to show that Yuzhang is already a contested office before [[ZeRong|笮融]] murders Zhu Hao and seizes the commandery for himself.
