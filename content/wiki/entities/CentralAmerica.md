# Central America

Central America appears in [[tech-20260820-tech-pod-128-tech-20260820-tech-pod-128]] as one target region for the Trump administration's planned public funding to replace subsea cable infrastructure. The episode treats the region as a place where commercial incentives, connectivity improvement, and [[TrustedNetworkGeopolitics]] can overlap.

In this source, Central America is not analyzed country by country. Its wiki role is regional: cable funding could strengthen network redundancy, create more trusted routes, and reduce exposure to suppliers or systems viewed as risky in the [[UnitedStates]]-[[China]] infrastructure contest.
