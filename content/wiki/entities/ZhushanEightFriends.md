# Zhushan Eight Friends / 珠山八友

Zhushan Eight Friends / 珠山八友 are the loose Jingdezhen porcelain-painting group discussed in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode says the label formed around artists such as Wang Qi and Wang Dafan after a porcelain-panel commission and later became a name for a wider artistic route in modern Jingdezhen.

The group matters because it shifts porcelain value from anonymous workshop output toward named artistic style. In the source, poetry, signatures, seals, literati painting language, and personal reputation begin to affect porcelain prices, making [[ArtCommerceIntegration]] part of [[Jingdezhen]]'s modern transition.
