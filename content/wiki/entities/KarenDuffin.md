# Karen Duffin

[[KarenDuffin]] is named in [[sand-heists-and-property-rights-in-the-caribbean-summer-school]] as one of the hosts of the original 2018 [[PlanetMoney]] stolen-sand story, alongside [[SarahGonzalez]]. Her source-scoped role is reporting context for the Jamaican beach-theft segment rather than expert testimony.

The episode uses that earlier reporting to connect a local [[Jamaica]] theft case to [[SandScarcity]], resort demand, and [[SandTheftExternality]].
