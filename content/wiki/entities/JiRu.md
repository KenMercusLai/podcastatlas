## Overview

Ji Ru / 籍孺 is presented as a young male favorite of [[LiuBang|刘邦]] whose intimate access becomes visible when [[FanKuai|樊哙]] forces his way into the emperor's quarters during the [[YingBu|英布 / 黥布]] crisis.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-372-3-xihan-huangdi-dou-you-naxie-nanpengyou-lkfxzzsl2dc-vnaxupjlfn14j6kt|Hanji 372-3]] portrays Ji Ru as favored for beauty, pliancy, and personal attendance rather than demonstrated administrative ability. His significance is institutional as well as intimate: the ruler's withdrawal behind a favorite can obstruct ministers, while the favorite's clothing and cosmetics can become models for court imitation.

The episode calls him “季如,” but the historical name normalized here is 籍孺. Its “boyfriend” wording is retained as the host's interpretation rather than treated as proof of a modern sexual identity.

## Key Characteristics

- He is described as Liu Bang's young, beautiful, and highly favored attendant.
- His proximity is visible during a ruler-access crisis in which ministers wait outside while Liu Bang remains secluded.
- The episode treats his advancement as favor-based rather than grounded in demonstrated office competence.
- His dress, feathered headwear, shell-decorated belt, and cosmetics are said to influence palace fashion.
- His intimate role is historically suggestive but does not by itself establish a modern orientation category.

## Qualifications

The supplied source is a compressed podcast summary, not a primary text or verbatim transcript. It normalizes poorly in several places and does not establish the precise sexual content of the relationship, Ji Ru's formal office, the extent of his political influence, or the chronology of the fashion claims. An earlier episode calls the lap attendant a eunuch without naming Ji Ru, leaving a source tension unresolved.
