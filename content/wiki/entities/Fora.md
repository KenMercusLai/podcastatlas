# Fora

Fora appears in [[its-not-easy-being-green-zack-polanski-6a82d1a0ff328abd84724cf0]] as a modern travel agency that built a billion-dollar business by giving advisers an AI-powered platform. In [[CaitlinTalbot]]'s segment, Fora is not evidence that AI simply replaces agents; it is evidence that AI can become adviser infrastructure.

The company connects [[AITravelPlanning]] to [[TravelAgentResilience]]. If AI handles repetitive administration, itinerary drafting, and information retrieval, human advisers can spend more attention on taste, destination judgment, hotel networks, client fit, and support when plans fail.
