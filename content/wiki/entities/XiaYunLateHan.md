# 夏韵 / Xia Yun (late Han)

夏韵 / Xia Yun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-924-1-de-cuowu-100-de-shibai-lmeo7vufm6ib5lni6p49rponazut|Hanji 924]] as a source-named eunuch favorite in [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s court. The page preserves the episode's spelling; later sources may require a variant-normalization note if the same figure appears under another character.

In this source, Xia Yun is important because he joins [[ZhaoZhongLateHan|赵忠]] in eliminating [[LyuQiangLateHan|吕强]]. After Lyu Qiang supports [[PartyBanCrisisAmnesty|party-ban crisis amnesty]] and attacks corrupt palace attendants, Xia Yun and Zhao Zhong accuse him of party ties and family corruption, leading to Lyu Qiang's suicide and family punishment.
