## Overview

《雨血》 is [[LiangQiwei|梁其伟]]'s early independent dark-wuxia game project and the creative origin of the later [[SGame|灵游坊]] and *Phantom Blade* lineage.

## Current Profile

The source treats 《雨血》 as both a work and a capability seed. Its online collaborators helped form the founding team, while its revenge, wrongful-accusation, dark-organization, and damaged-character tone established an audience expectation that later promotional moralizing could not simply overwrite.

## Key Characteristics

- Began as a creator-led independent project before the studio existed.
- Joined writing, drawing, atmosphere, and action design in a dark wuxia setting.
- Supplied collaborators who later joined the founding company.
- Established thematic continuity around revenge, injustice, and concealed organizations.
- Became the fictional and aesthetic lineage behind 《影之刃》 and 《影之刃零》.
