# 《盲山》 / Blind Mountain

## Overview
[[BlindMountainFilm|《盲山》]] enters the wiki through a short [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode on trafficking. The film is used as a modern narrative example of a woman deceived, trafficked into marriage, repeatedly blocked from escape, and trapped by local coercion.

## Current Profile
In the episode, 《盲山》 is not discussed primarily as cinema history. It operates as a public-imagination bridge between ancient punishment, modern trafficking news, and the difficulty of recognizing coercion that is hidden inside family, village, and local-protection structures.

## Key Characteristics
- It is presented as a film about a 1990s trafficking and forced-marriage scenario.
- The episode emphasizes deception, forced marriage, escape attempts, beatings, and chains.
- The film illustrates rescue difficulty even when outside authority becomes aware.
- It supports the episode's claim that trafficking is not merely an ancient crime.
- It functions as a symbol of social blindness and the need for public attention.
