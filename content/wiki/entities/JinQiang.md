# 金强 / Jin Qiang

金强 / Jin Qiang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-141-huanyuan-lishi-zhong-de-hongmenyan-lpmb-bcoocngtqjvtnpj0elm-siy|Hanji 141]] under the transcript form "金强." The episode presents him as one of [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s loyal old subordinates in the group that accompanies Liu Bang from [[Bashang|霸上]] toward [[HongmenYan|鸿门宴]].

In this source, Jin Qiang is paired with [[JiXin|纪信]] as part of the mounted entourage management rather than as a separately developed character. The important point is the composition of Liu Bang's crisis team: the most trusted advisers and guards come close, but only Liu Bang and [[ZhangLiang|张良]] enter [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s tent.
