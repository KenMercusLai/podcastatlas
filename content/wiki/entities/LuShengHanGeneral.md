# 吕胜 / Lu Sheng (Han)

吕胜 / Lu Sheng (Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-168-bawangbieji-qian-fashengle-shenme-2-liqgict85wh-bf4fn2bytygmk-ca|Hanji 168 part 2]] as one of the Han-side men associated with the division and confirmation of [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s body after the final fight at [[WujiangChuHan|乌江]].

This page is deliberately disambiguated from [[LuSheng|卢生 / Lu Sheng]], the Qin fangshi in the immortality-search branch. The Hanji 168 source gives this Lu Sheng only a narrow role in the reward group after Xiang Yu's death.
