## Overview

Ziwudao / 子午道 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-627-1-nvxing-biting-dute-shijiao-jingqi-yinyu-beihou-de-lishi-zhihui-lqomjfejmglm9hylkomktx6rtbtt|Hanji 627-1]] as the road [[WangMang|王莽]] announces in 5 CE after invoking [[EmpressWangPingdi|汉平帝王皇后]]'s "子孙瑞."

## Current Profile

The source presents Ziwudao as a route from Duling county through the Zhongnan mountains toward Hanzhong commandery. Its wiki significance is less engineering detail than political framing: Wang Mang attaches the road to a reproductive auspicious sign from the young empress, converting infrastructure into legitimacy performance.

The page remains narrow until more sources cover the route's construction, military value, terrain, or later use.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han route associated in the episode with Duling, the Zhongnan mountains, and Hanzhong.
- Infrastructure project publicly announced by Wang Mang in 5 CE.
- Road whose justification is attached to the empress's "子孙瑞" sign.
- Source-scoped example of infrastructure receiving auspicious political framing.
