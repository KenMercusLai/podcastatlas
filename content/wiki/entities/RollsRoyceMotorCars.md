# Rolls-Royce Motor Cars

Rolls-Royce Motor Cars appears in [[vol-268-liang-ge-lao-si-lai-si-1003563933]] as the luxury-car branch separated from the strategically protected aero-engine assets after the 1971 [[RollsRoyceRB211|RB211]] crisis. The episode uses this split to explain why aircraft engines and luxury cars can both carry related double-R brand marks while belonging to different corporate histories.

The car business is not the source's main financial driver. The episode says the aviation business had become more important to the original company, while the cars carried symbolic luxury value. After the crisis, the car branch went through later ownership changes, including Vickers and the late-1990s split that left Rolls-Royce cars with BMW and Bentley with Volkswagen.
