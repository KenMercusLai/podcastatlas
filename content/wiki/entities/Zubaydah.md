# Zubaydah

## Overview

Zubaydah was a cousin and favored wife of [[HarunAlRashid]], mother of al-Amin, and an Abbasid court figure remembered for luxury, influence, piety, charity, and support for the pilgrimage route to Mecca.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Zubaydah at the intersection of dynastic politics and public benefaction. As al-Amin's mother, she belonged to the family structure that shaped the contested succession after Harun; as a patron, she became associated with hostels and infrastructure on the route later called the Zubaydah road or trail.

Her literary portrait may combine conventional opposites: sensual court luxury and exemplary pious generosity. The current profile therefore preserves her remembered patronage while treating ornamental biographical details as shaped by genre.

## Key Characteristics

- Abbasid princess, Harun's cousin and favored wife.
- Mother of the caliph al-Amin.
- Influential court figure associated with wealth and luxury.
- Remembered donor to the poor and patron of pilgrimage infrastructure.
- Subject of a literary portrait combining magnificence with piety.

## Evidence

- **Dynastic position:** [[378-baghdad-the-golden-age-part-3-glt9689814482]] identifies Zubaydah as Harun's cousin, wife, and the mother of al-Amin.
- **Public patronage:** [[378-baghdad-the-golden-age-part-3-glt9689814482]] associates her with charitable giving and hostels on the Mecca pilgrimage route.
- **Literary construction:** [[378-baghdad-the-golden-age-part-3-glt9689814482]] cautions that the combination of beauty, luxury, piety, and charity may follow a conventional formula.

## Qualifications

This account is not a complete biography or an independent assessment of the surviving infrastructure. The exact scale, chronology, and attribution of her patronage, along with descriptions of beauty and extravagance, remain source-scoped.
