# 贾元春 / Jia Yuanchun

[[ritan-chuantai-hongloumeng-haishi-keyi-du-yidu-de-587167638]] treats 贾元春 as the figure who authorizes and protects [[GrandViewGarden|大观园]]. Her status makes the garden possible as a temporary gathering place for [[JiaBaoyu|贾宝玉]], his sisters, cousins, and the young women whose lives make [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]]'s beauty and grief visible.

The episode's closing argument is that Yuan Chun's departure removes the garden's protection. Once that shelter fails, [[GrandViewGardenYouthUtopia|the youth world]] gives way to household decline, marriage pressure, status hierarchy, and the wider ethical society that cannot preserve figures such as [[LinDaiyu|林黛玉]], [[Qingwen|晴雯]], and [[Lingguan|龄官]].
