# Bola Tinubu

## Overview
Bola Tinubu is Nigeria's president and the incumbent candidate whose re-election confidence is examined against widespread insecurity, hunger, and economic hardship.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Tinubu as pursuing reforms with a defensible macroeconomic logic but an unequal and delayed social payoff. Removing the fuel subsidy and reducing exchange-rate uncertainty are credited with lower inflation, higher government revenue, and greater stability, while currency devaluation, food inflation, weak harvests, energy costs, and inadequate relief continue to hurt poorer households.

His electoral position does not rest on popularity alone. Incumbency, ruling-party organization, campaign funding and visibility, religious and ethnic affiliation, selective credit for basic services, opposition weakness, insecurity, and low participation may insulate him from dissatisfaction.

## Key Characteristics
- Incumbent Nigerian president seeking re-election amid severe insecurity and household hardship.
- Sponsor of subsidy and exchange-rate reforms credited with macroeconomic stabilization.
- Politically exposed because reform costs arrive before benefits for many poorer Nigerians.
- Beneficiary of ruling-party organization, funding, visibility, and identity-based loyalty.
- Potential beneficiary of low turnout where danger and cynicism keep dissatisfied citizens home.

## Evidence
- Reform record and distributional cost: [[songs-of-disenchantment-the-sorrow-of-nigeria-6275426999307c5f9d5c7073a5b16e0d]] credits subsidy removal and exchange-rate action with improved national indicators while describing food inflation, malnutrition, and energy pressure.
- Electoral organization: [[songs-of-disenchantment-the-sorrow-of-nigeria-6275426999307c5f9d5c7073a5b16e0d]] emphasizes incumbency, party machinery, campaign visibility, religious solidarity, and Yoruba identification.
- Participation mechanism: [[songs-of-disenchantment-the-sorrow-of-nigeria-6275426999307c5f9d5c7073a5b16e0d]] reports very low Borno and national turnout in 2023 and argues that abstention can help a well-organized incumbent.

## Qualifications
The profile comes from one pre-election episode and does not establish the election result, current vote intention, reform causality, or the relative weight of identity, coercion, service provision, opposition fragmentation, and organization. National aggregates do not describe every Nigerian household or region.

## What Changed
- Created a profile separating Tinubu's macroeconomic reform case from its uneven lived effects.
- Identified party machinery, identity, insecurity, and low turnout as distinct sources of electoral resilience.
