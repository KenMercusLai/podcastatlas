# 田角 / Tian Jiao

田角 / Tian Jiao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-3-weihe-li-mutong-wei-chuwang-limian-dayou-wenzhang-lmcuvrxer-cou6gmlppqwhb3n4vz|Qinji 130-3]] as the chancellor appointed when Qi officials install [[TianJia|田假]] as king after [[TianDanQinRebel|田儋]] dies.

After [[TianRong|田荣]] returns to [[QiState|齐国]] and drives out Tian Jia, Tian Jiao flees to [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. The source pairs him with [[TianJianQiGeneral|田间]], who is also under Zhao's protection.

Tian Jiao's main significance is diplomatic rather than biographical. Tian Rong makes Qi participation in [[XiangLiang|项梁]]'s planned joint attack on [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] conditional on Zhao killing Tian Jiao and Tian Jian. Zhao refuses, so the anti-Qin coalition fails to concentrate.
