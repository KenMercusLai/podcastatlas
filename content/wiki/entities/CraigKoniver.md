# Craig Koniver

## Overview
Craig Koniver is the physician guest in a [[HubermanLab]] episode on peptides, hormone-related therapies, NAD, mitochondrial support, recovery, sleep, and performance medicine.

## Current Profile
The source presents Koniver as a clinician whose practice uses supervised, frequently off-label or compounded interventions alongside lifestyle foundations. His contribution is primarily practitioner observation: he compares compounds, describes clinic protocols and patient responses, and argues for individualized assessment, trusted clinical guidance, legal access, and product-quality controls. Those observations can identify hypotheses and practical monitoring questions, but they do not substitute for controlled human evidence.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses a performance-medicine frame aimed at health, function, recovery, and vitality rather than acute disease care alone.
- Describes extensive clinical use of peptides, NAD, methylene blue, supplements, and hormone-adjacent interventions.
- Emphasizes physician oversight, individualized dosing, medication and condition review, sourcing, sterility, and endotoxin control.
- Treats off-label prescribing and compounding as useful clinical tools while acknowledging regulatory and legal constraints.
- Places trust, positive framing, purpose, and foundational health behaviors alongside biomedical interventions.
