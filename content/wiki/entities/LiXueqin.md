# 李雪琴 / Li Xueqin

[[LiXueqin|李雪琴]] is a performer and [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] guest in [[ep243-jiang-qiming-x-li-xueqin-x-shuang-xuetao-xiang-zai-dongbei-shangtian-zhe-shi-er-haishi-tai-fuza-le-lkqbo0zg5y7p1fo4x90n8yq394t6]], where she discusses why she joined [[FeixingjiaFilm|《飞行家》]], Northeast language memory, family life, and women in Northeast households. The source presents her as both a film participant and a speaker whose own Northeast background makes the regional discussion concrete.

Her strongest contribution is to connect the film's dream story to lived family structures. She notes that many pursuit stories make family and lovers into obstacles, while [[FeixingjiaFilm|《飞行家》]] surrounds Li Mingqi with people who support him. Her remarks on grandparents, aunts, "family" versus "relatives," and Northeast women extend [[FamilySupportedAspiration]] and [[NortheastWomenFamilyAgency]].
