## Overview

Empress Dowager Wei / 卫太后 is synthesized as [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]]'s birth mother, whose honored exclusion from Chang'an begins in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-609-xihan-wang-yu-wangzhengjun-ni-tongyi-ma-lu0kkjoqgj_n4hn4pxmo5wetpets|Hanji 609]] and later becomes the emotional and political trigger for the [[DogBloodCase|狗血案]] in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-you-xinji-de-ren-you-duo-kepa-lnhkk70905eqg4lqwh3mp2sxzamc|Hanji 616]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 609 presents the first stage as honor without access. Wang Mang invokes Ding and Fu outer-relative precedent, then has Pingdi's mother honored while keeping her and the wider Wei family in Zhongshan rather than near the child emperor.

Hanji 616 then presents Wei Taihou as a mother blocked from reaching her son because [[WangMang|王莽]] fears the return of Pingdi's maternal Wei clan to court politics. Her first memorial is conciliatory: she praises Wang Mang, criticizes earlier Ding and Fu consort-kin conduct, and says the Wei family has no power ambition. Wang Mang responds by increasing her material honors but refusing the core request to enter Chang'an.

Her role is therefore less active conspiracy leader than excluded maternal center. Wang Yu, Wu Zhang, and Lü Kuan act around her blocked access, while the failed dog-blood tactic gives Wang Mang a reason to punish the wider Wei network. Wei Taihou survives because she is the emperor's birth mother, but the episode treats the Wei clan as politically broken.

## Key Characteristics

- Birth mother of Han Pingdi and focal point of the blocked maternal-clan access problem.
- Honored outside Chang'an before the later memorial effort, making status compensation separate from access.
- Memorial writer who uses deference and ritual language to seek a route back to Chang'an.
- Recipient of material compensation that does not solve her political and maternal exclusion.
- Survivor of the purge because of her emperor-mother status.
- Figure through whom Wang Mang weakens the older Western Han pattern of rotating consort-kin influence.

- Excluded mother: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-you-xinji-de-ren-you-duo-kepa-lnhkk70905eqg4lqwh3mp2sxzamc|Hanji 616]] says Wang Yu wants to help Wei Taihou reach Pingdi after Wang Mang blocks Wei-clan access to Chang'an.
- Conciliatory memorial: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-you-xinji-de-ren-you-duo-kepa-lnhkk70905eqg4lqwh3mp2sxzamc|Hanji 616]] says Wei Taihou praises Wang Mang, criticizes Ding and Fu precedents, and denies Wei power ambition.
- Material but not political response: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-you-xinji-de-ren-you-duo-kepa-lnhkk70905eqg4lqwh3mp2sxzamc|Hanji 616]] says Wang Mang increases her fief and gives gold, but still prevents her from going to Chang'an.
- Purge survival: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-you-xinji-de-ren-you-duo-kepa-lnhkk70905eqg4lqwh3mp2sxzamc|Hanji 616]] says Wei Taihou is spared because she is Pingdi's mother while much of the Wei clan is destroyed.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 609 and Hanji 616. It does not reconstruct Wei Taihou's complete biography, formal title history, independent political network, or later fate outside these episodes.
