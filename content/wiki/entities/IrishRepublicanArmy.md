# Irish Republican Army

The Irish Republican Army appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] as both a remembered republican threat and a limited armed presence during the 1968-1969 escalation. The episode links the IRA to the earlier border campaign, notes republican presence around some civil-rights organizing, and says the Belfast IRA had very few weapons during the August 1969 violence.

The source also stresses that some attacks blamed on the IRA were not IRA attacks. Infrastructure bombings that helped bring down [[TerenceONeill|Terence O'Neill]] are attributed to the [[UlsterVolunteerForce|UVF]] and aligned loyalist networks, making the IRA label part of [[ParamilitarySpoilerViolence]] and propaganda as well as an armed organization.
