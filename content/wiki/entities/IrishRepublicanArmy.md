# Irish Republican Army

The Irish Republican Army appears in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as an organization that never fully disappeared after the Irish civil war, even when reduced to a few hundred active members. The episode cites the 1939 S-Plan bombing campaign, wartime crackdowns by London and Dublin, the failed 1956 border campaign, and [[CathalGoulding]]'s later Marxist turn as the pre-1968 republican background.

[[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] then presents the IRA as both a remembered republican threat and a limited armed presence during the 1968-1969 escalation. The source also stresses that some attacks blamed on the IRA were not IRA attacks. Infrastructure bombings that helped bring down [[TerenceONeill|Terence O'Neill]] are attributed to the [[UlsterVolunteerForce|UVF]] and aligned loyalist networks, making the IRA label part of [[ParamilitarySpoilerViolence]] and propaganda as well as an armed organization.
