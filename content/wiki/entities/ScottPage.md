# Scott Page

Scott Page appears in [[you-bet-your-life-insurance]] as the person whose private AIDS-crisis financing arrangement helped become the early [[ViaticalSettlement|viatical settlement]] market. After his partner [[GregScottPagePartner|Greg]] became seriously ill and could not keep a $100,000 life insurance policy active, Page accepted support from a wealthy person in the HIV support-group community who paid premiums and living expenses in exchange for repayment from the future death benefit.

The episode says Page later adapted that rescue into a repeatable policy-sale model for other people with AIDS, taking a 3% commission and using doctor-estimated life expectancies to make the deal legible to investors. His later discomfort with the industry's profit-driven abstraction makes him the source's moral bridge between emergency liquidity and the modern [[LifeInsuranceSecondaryMarket|life insurance secondary market]].

## Key Claims
- Page's original problem was practical and urgent: keep Greg's policy alive while paying for basic care and living needs.
- His market innovation was to change the structure from a private loan into a sale of the life insurance policy and beneficiary position.
- He became uncomfortable as the market moved from helping desperate people into a large [[DeathBenefitPortfolio|portfolio]] business.
