## Overview

梁兴 / Liang Xing is a Guanzhong armed leader whose wiki profile currently covers his post-[[WeinanBattleLateHan|渭南之战]] survival, predation in the Zuopingyi region, progressive loss of followers under [[ZhengHunLateHan|郑浑]]'s policy, and death during the later Cao-side suppression.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1128-meiyou-heikeji-guren-ruhe-diaocha-nimingxin-lqdm57jzw9wqo_umlasz2j3vlzqh|Hanji 1128]] presents Liang Xing as one of the remaining actors from the [[MaChao|马超]] and Han Sui coalition field. He withdraws to Fu County and raids across Zuopingyi, creating enough fear that county officials gather at the commandery seat and propose moving to more defensible terrain.

His weakness is social rather than only military. The source says many followers are coerced, making the group vulnerable when Zheng Hun maintains visible authority and supplies rewards, family-recovery mechanisms, persuasion, and reception for defectors. Liang Xing retreats with the remainder into Fu city; [[XiahouYuan|夏侯渊]] later helps kill him and suppress the remaining group.

## Key Characteristics

- Residual Guanzhong coalition actor after the Weinan defeat.
- Mobile raider who exploits weakened local administration.
- Leader whose following includes coerced rather than uniformly committed participants.
- Vulnerable to a policy that creates credible individual and family exit routes.
- Ultimately defeated through political erosion followed by military suppression.
