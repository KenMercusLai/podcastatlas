## Overview

夜郎王兴 / Ye Lang King Xing enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-512-xihan-chuzheng-yelang-wo-budang-dage-haoduonian-lufpmcdwb20dhx2qxfjws7-qdpkb|Hanji 512]] as the southwestern ruler whose conflict with neighboring polities and rejection of Han mediation make him the immediate focus of a Western Han frontier response.

## Current Profile

The episode frames Xing through arrogance and escalation. He first appears as a local ruler fighting Gouding and Louwo around [[ZhangkeCommanderyWesternHan|牂柯郡]], but his political meaning changes when he refuses Zhang Kuang's mediation and turns Han official images into archery targets.

For [[DuQingWesternHan|杜清]], Xing is dangerous less because he has already attacked the commandery than because delay may let him use distance, poor communications, and local geography to annex neighboring groups, gather forces, and strike Zhangke before Han can respond effectively.

## Key Characteristics

- Ruler of [[YelangStateWesternHan|夜郎]] during the Heping 2 / 27 BCE episode.
- Presented by the host as overconfident and morally inflated.
- Fights nearby Gouding and Louwo before the conflict threatens Han territory.
- Rejects Han mediation sent through Zhang Kuang.
- Symbolically insults Han authority by using official-shaped wooden images as archery targets.
- Serves as Du Qing's example of a frontier threat that may grow if conciliation continues too long.
