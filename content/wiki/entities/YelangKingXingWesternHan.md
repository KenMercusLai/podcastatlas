## Overview

夜郎王兴 / Ye Lang King Xing enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-512-xihan-chuzheng-yelang-wo-budang-dage-haoduonian-lufpmcdwb20dhx2qxfjws7-qdpkb|Hanji 512]] as the southwestern ruler whose conflict with neighboring polities and rejection of Han mediation make him the immediate focus of a Western Han frontier response. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-513-yongchuang-duomingdao-hanchen-chenli-zhusha-yelang-guowang-lvfqkcbuzts119gtpzfipzo5wbo|Hanji 513]] records his death at the hands of [[ChenLiWesternHan|陈立]] and the short deterrent shock that follows.

## Current Profile

The episode frames Xing through arrogance and escalation. He first appears as a local ruler fighting Gouding and Louwo around [[ZhangkeCommanderyWesternHan|牂柯郡]], but his political meaning changes when he refuses Zhang Kuang's mediation and turns Han official images into archery targets.

For [[DuQingWesternHan|杜清]], Xing is dangerous less because he has already attacked the commandery than because delay may let him use distance, poor communications, and local geography to annex neighboring groups, gather forces, and strike Zhangke before Han can respond effectively.

Hanji 513 completes that arc by showing Xing misreading the local balance in the opposite direction. He trusts his own territory, thousands of troops, and allied rulers when Chen Li summons him to Qietong Pavilion, but Chen Li has already arranged an ambush. Xing's execution breaks the immediate coalition display, yet his father-in-law Wengzhi and son Yewu keep the Ye Lang resistance alive until Chen Li destroys its supply and water access.

## Key Characteristics

- Ruler of [[YelangStateWesternHan|夜郎]] during the Heping 2 / 27 BCE episode.
- Presented by the host as overconfident and morally inflated.
- Fights nearby Gouding and Louwo before the conflict threatens Han territory.
- Rejects Han mediation sent through Zhang Kuang.
- Symbolically insults Han authority by using official-shaped wooden images as archery targets.
- Serves as Du Qing's example of a frontier threat that may grow if conciliation continues too long.
- Killed by Chen Li during a staged meeting at Qietong Pavilion, creating immediate surrender pressure but not fully ending kin-based Ye Lang resistance.
