## Overview

长杨宫 / Changyang Palace enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-551-jiemi-hanchengdi-de-huangwei-chuancheng-zhimi-lkefo-pkg_n8ll57kdsofcrpad_v|Hanji 551]] as the site of [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s shooting-bear spectacle and the immediate setting behind [[YangXiong|扬雄]]'s 《长杨赋》.

## Current Profile

The current evidence is narrow. Hanji 551 says Chengdi has captured animals brought to the palace's shooting-bear enclosure, uses nets to confine them, and watches Hu men fight wild beasts for reward. The episode treats the spectacle as late-Western-Han display power built on burdensome mobilization of commoners and as the event that later becomes literary material for Yang Xiong.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han palace setting tied to Chengdi's animal spectacle.
- Site where captured beasts and human combat become imperial entertainment.
- Literary occasion for Yang Xiong's 《长杨赋》 in the current source set.

## Evidence

- Hunting spectacle: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-551-jiemi-hanchengdi-de-huangwei-chuancheng-zhimi-lkefo-pkg_n8ll57kdsofcrpad_v|Hanji 551]] says Chengdi sends captured animals to Changyang Palace's shooting-bear enclosure and watches combat there.
- Literary occasion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-551-jiemi-hanchengdi-de-huangwei-chuancheng-zhimi-lkefo-pkg_n8ll57kdsofcrpad_v|Hanji 551]] says Yang Xiong later writes 《长杨赋》 after accompanying Chengdi to the site.

## Qualifications

This page does not reconstruct Changyang Palace's architecture, location history, full institutional role, or all literary references. It only records the Hanji 551 spectacle-and-fu context.
