# Dreame / 追觅

Dreame appears in [[qishui-yinyue-chongji-tengxun-wangyi-tesila-deng-cheqi-zai-guonei-kaizhan-daguimo-zhaohui-1008396395]] through its sharply reduced car project. The episode says Dreame announced a vehicle plan in August 2025, expanded rapidly toward a near-thousand-person team, released concept vehicles, then faced rounds of layoffs from June 2026 after factory, contract-manufacturing, and financing plans failed to reach substantive progress.

For the wiki, Dreame is a cautionary [[EfficiencyOverScaleExpansion]] and [[ChineseHardwareGlobalization]] case: crossing from consumer appliances into cars can look plausible while capital is abundant, but automotive manufacturing, delivery, compliance, and funding gates are much harder than concept announcements or fast hiring suggest.
