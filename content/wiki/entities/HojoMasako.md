# Hojo Masako

## Overview
Hojo Masako appears in this source as the Izu marriage link that helps [[MinamotoNoYoritomo]] turn exile into a samurai network.

## Current Profile
[[659-dawn-of-the-samurai-bloodbath-at-the-bridge-part-2-glt3420393571]] mentions Masako during Yoritomo's Izu exile. The episode's focus is not her full later career, but it uses the marriage to show that Yoritomo was not an isolated prisoner: he was building local ties in Izu and Kanto before the anti-Taira revolt became open war.

Within this source, Masako's importance is structural. She marks the family and regional alliance layer underneath Yoritomo's later ability to raise the Minamoto banner.

## Key Characteristics
- Masako is introduced as part of Yoritomo's Izu network formation.
- Her marriage to Yoritomo signals that exile can become alliance-building rather than political disappearance.
- The source treats her as a precondition for Yoritomo's local base, not yet as a full independent political actor.

## Evidence
- Izu alliance: [[659-dawn-of-the-samurai-bloodbath-at-the-bridge-part-2-glt3420393571]] says Yoritomo builds links with samurai in Izu and Kanto and marries Hojo Masako.
- Revolt setup: [[659-dawn-of-the-samurai-bloodbath-at-the-bridge-part-2-glt3420393571]] places that marriage before Yoritomo raises the white Minamoto banner.
- Source boundary: [[659-dawn-of-the-samurai-bloodbath-at-the-bridge-part-2-glt3420393571]] does not yet develop Masako's later Kamakura political role.
