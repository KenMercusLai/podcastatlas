## Overview

王平 / Wang Ping enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-402-1-sanghongyang-ceng-bang-hanwudi-zhifu-weihe-zuihou-canzao-miezu-ltpcos20d5mafcoywmfcb6iifwsv|Hanji 402-1]] as the Tingwei who, with [[XuRenWesternHan|徐仁]], initially applies a narrower amnesty reading to [[HouShiWuWesternHan|侯史吴]] and is later punished after [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] rejects that judgment.

## Current Profile

Wang Ping's profile is a legal-risk profile. As Tingwei, he participates in judging Hou Shiwu's sheltering of [[SangQianWesternHan|桑迁]] and concludes that short-term protection of a fugitive does not make Hou a rebel and should fall under the amnesty. Huo Guang's review turns this into "indulging rebels." Wang Ping is imprisoned and then waist-cut, showing how a lenient judicial interpretation can become politically fatal when rebellion classification is controlled by the regent.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Tingwei involved in the Hou Shiwu harboring case.
- Reads the amnesty narrowly enough to spare Hou Shiwu because Hou was not personally a rebel.
- Punished after Huo Guang forces a stricter review of Sang Qian and Hou Shiwu's status.
- Example of judicial office becoming vulnerable when legal interpretation conflicts with regent political will.
- Executed by waist-cutting along with other implicated officials in the episode's account.
