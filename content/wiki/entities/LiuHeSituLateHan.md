# 刘郃 / Liu He (late-Han situ)

[[LiuHeSituLateHan|刘郃]] was a late-Han situ and imperial-clan figure in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-918-shiqing-bu-shunli-shi-weishenme-yao-bizui-lh-tbohgo96qw8bjqu3x1yp6czv5|Hanji 918]]. He is distinct from [[LiuHeLateHan|刘和]], the later son of [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]].

In the episode, [[ChenQiuLateHan|陈球]] and [[LiuNaLateHan|刘纳]] press Liu He to act against [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] by invoking the state crisis, Liu He's royal-clan identity, his office, and the death of his brother [[LiuTiaoLateHan|刘鯈]] in the earlier [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] anti-eunuch attempt. Liu He recognizes the danger of eunuch surveillance but still joins the plot with [[YangQiuLateHan|阳球]], and is killed after the plan leaks through [[ChengHuangLateHan|程璜]].
