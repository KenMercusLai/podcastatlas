# 李娇杨 / Li Jiaoyang

## Overview
Li Jiaoyang is a writer, artist, translator, and former student of [[AnneCarson|Anne Carson]] who appears in [[ep289-zai-anni-kasen-de-maxituan-ketang-chaidiao-wenxue-de-tiaotiaokuangkuang-lhqupidadktnqhxizmxzja9hkjhp|EP289]].

## Current Profile
Li's contribution joins readerly attraction to Carson's work with direct classroom and translation experience. She describes moving from London to New York partly to study with Carson, then finding a classroom whose games and cross-media collaborations matched her own creative inclinations. Her testimony stresses Carson's warmth and attention without claiming authority to explain Carson's works or private intentions.

## Key Characteristics
- Approaches literature through writing, translation, visual art, performance, and collaborative experiment.
- Values teaching that triggers work without prescribing style or supplying one correct interpretation.
- Treats translation as a creative practice rather than mechanical equivalence.
- Uses personal memory carefully, distinguishing lived acquaintance from authoritative literary explanation.
