## Overview
《上下半场》 / Shang Xia Banchang is the podcast/project Zhao Lina names in the [[FuyouTiandi]] interview as part of her post-retirement shift from athlete being watched to interviewer, listener, and public encourager.

## Current Profile
The current wiki evidence treats 《上下半场》 as a transition platform rather than a catalogued show with a full episode archive. Zhao Lina links it to receiving many interviews after retirement, discovering that different questioners helped her read different parts of herself, and wanting to use interviews to understand other athletes and people. The project sits beside her football charity and public-facing content work, making it part of her effort to turn athletic experience into expression, connection, and encouragement.

## Key Characteristics
- The project is associated with Zhao Lina's move from being a sports subject to asking questions of others.
- It uses interview practice as a way to "read people" when book reading is difficult for Zhao in the source's account.
- It is framed as encouragement for athletes and women who may fear expression or new roles after the sport field changes.
- It belongs to a broader post-retirement toolkit that includes public labels, content work, and football charity rather than a single media identity.
