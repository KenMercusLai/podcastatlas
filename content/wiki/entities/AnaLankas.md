# Ana Lankas

Ana Lankas appears in [[pump-and-circumstance-is-china-the-new-opec-6a7c37287cf3c58886773a66]] as the analyst for the [[Brazil]] segment on racist-speech law. She argues that tougher prosecution can raise awareness of racist language, but the source presents her as skeptical that criminal penalties directly reduce the education, earnings, and policing inequalities that structure racism in Brazil.

The page's role is to connect the Brazil case to [[HateSpeechCriminalizationTradeoff]]: the episode treats her comments as a warning that symbolic legal severity can outrun institutional repair.
