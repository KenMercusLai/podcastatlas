# Tang Yong / 唐勇

## Overview
Tang Yong / 唐勇 is a Shenzhen University sociology professor and dementia-care researcher discussed in the [[BuHeShiYi|不合时宜]] episode on失智症, caregivers, and aging.

## Current Profile
The source presents Tang as a scholar of elder care, family caregivers, and social-work education whose research direction was shaped by personal family-care experience and later fieldwork with dementia households in [[Shenzhen]]. His book [[YiWangDeShiJieDementiaCareBook|《遗忘的世界：失智老人的照护图景》]] becomes the episode's main evidence base for connecting [[DementiaPersonhoodMemory]], [[DementiaCaregiverBurden]], and [[DementiaCareSocialSupportSystem]].

## Key Characteristics
- Researcher focused on older-person care, family caregivers, and social-work education.
- Uses field interviews with dementia families to resist stigma, simplification, and patient-only framing.
- Connects personal care experience, academic fieldwork, and policy analysis rather than treating them as separate domains.
- Argues that love and family duty are insufficient without external resources, trained carers, community support, and care financing.
