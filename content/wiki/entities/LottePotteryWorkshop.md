# Lotte Pottery Workshop / 乐天陶社

Lotte Pottery Workshop / 乐天陶社 is the contemporary ceramic-community node in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode says a Hong Kong ceramic artist founded it in the old sculpture-porcelain factory area around 2005 and invited overseas ceramic artists to work in Jingdezhen.

The workshop matters because it helped make [[JingpiaoCreativeCeramicEcology|景漂]] culture visible. The source connects its cafe-front Saturday stalls to the growth of creative markets, residency-style exchange, and the low entry cost created by [[Jingdezhen]]'s complete ceramic supply chain.
