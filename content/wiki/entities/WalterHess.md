# Walter Hess

## Overview
Walter Hess is the neuroscientist cited in the [[HubermanLab]] aggression Essentials episode for brain-stimulation experiments that could evoke rage-like behavior in cats.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Hess as an early experimental bridge between broad aggression behavior and specific brain regions. His cat-stimulation work is presented as showing that activating a hypothalamic region could rapidly switch a calm animal into a rage-like state and that stopping stimulation could return the animal toward calm within seconds.

The source uses Hess mainly to prepare the later modern VMH and optogenetic evidence. It does not present a full account of his career or the historical ethics of the experiments.

## Key Characteristics
- Early brain-stimulation reference in the episode's aggression-circuit story.
- Linked to cat experiments that produced rapid state changes.
- Used to introduce the [[VentromedialHypothalamus]] as a candidate aggression-related structure.
- Functions as a historical antecedent to later mouse and human evidence.

## Evidence
