# Ropet

Ropet is the AI toy product named in [[ep253-baohuo-de-ai-haowu-daodi-shi-zhenxiang-haishi-zhishangshui-lgt0cdkotgnzjl0mu2tx41p9fw-4]] as a higher-priced companion toy example, around 1800 RMB in the episode's account. [[WangYiranSanlian|王依然]] uses it to contrast with speech-centered AI toys: Ropet reportedly chooses not to speak because a pet-like object may preserve emotional immersion better when it avoids saying the wrong thing.
