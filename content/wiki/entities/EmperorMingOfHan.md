# 汉明帝 / Emperor Ming of Han

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-899-hao-qing-dou-tanguan-ljhic1plzmv8k6lu73j5terihanx|Hanji 899]] introduces Emperor Ming of Han as the precedent behind the Eastern Han tomb-sacrifice practice that [[CaiYong|蔡邕]] defends. When [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] visits [[LiuXiu|光武帝]]'s Yuanling in 172 CE, Cai Yong says direct experience of the ceremony helps him understand Mingdi's hidden filial intention.

The page's role is narrow. The source does not give a full biography of Mingdi; it uses him to explain why a seemingly cumbersome ritual can preserve dynastic memory, filial hierarchy, and [[LiAsPoliticalOrder|礼制政治秩序]]. Mingdi therefore enters the wiki as a ritual-precedent figure rather than as a broad reign profile.
