# Niconico

Niconico appears in [[no-214-xunzhao-tonglei-xiaohongshu-bilibili-yiji-wuhuabamen-de-naxie-shequ-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-26-1005977305]] as the Japanese video site whose time-axis comment system influenced Chinese bullet-comment communities. The episode places it as a Japanese answer to [[YouTube]] that made comments visible inside video playback rather than only below the video.

The wiki relevance is not Niconico's full company history, but its product affordance: comment placement turned viewing into a shared, time-coded experience and shaped the later [[AcFun]] and [[Bilibili]] branches.

## Connections
- [[AcFun]] and [[Bilibili]] - Chinese platforms that adopted or extended the bullet-comment model.
- [[BulletCommentCommunity]] - concept grounded partly in Niconico's interface.
- [[YouTube]] - comparison platform named in the source.
