# 燕国 / Yan State

燕国 / Yan State appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-2-lmfzo4sl9nd5kz3mrxdl1dl9efei]] through a brief succession notice: [[YanMinGong|燕闵公]] dies and [[YanXiGong|燕僖公]] succeeds him. The episode also notes that Yan's capital was Ji, associated with present-day Beijing.

The page is currently source-scoped. Its role is to anchor the annalistic chronology branch of the episode rather than to provide a complete Yan history.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl]] adds Yan as the state attacked by [[QiState|齐国]] at Sangqiu. [[YanJiangong|燕简公]]'s vulnerability draws in Han, Zhao, and Wei, whose rescue attack helps the episode build its [[EarlyWarringStatesInterstateWar|early Warring States interstate-war]] frame.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] adds a 373 BCE reversal: Yan defeats Qi at Linghu. The same year [[YanXiGong|燕僖公]] dies and [[YanHuanGong|燕桓公]] succeeds.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-11-shijie-fengqi-yunyong-2-llzzalowv5vmbvgd6l-jujltkbyf]] adds the next compact Yan succession notice in 362 BCE: [[YanHuanGong|燕桓公]] dies and [[YanWengong|燕文公]] succeeds. The episode uses it as one of several ruler handoffs around the Qin Xiao Gong accession.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-14-qiweiwang-buming-zeyi-yiming-jingren-lrs6rbifyiqrojxg0mccpg7sf9rv]] adds Yan to the 356 BCE diplomatic meeting cluster: [[ZhaoState|赵国]] and Yan meet at Ajuan while Zhao, Qi, and Song meet at Pinglu in the same year.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-29-suqin-lizhi-shuangwen-zhishi-gaibian-mingyun-lrf5cqvoriyep2lkbozju-lyjaxv]] adds the 333 BCE Yan succession notice. [[YanWengong|燕文公]] dies, and [[YanYiwang|燕易王]] succeeds in the same cluster that later turns toward [[SuQin|苏秦]] and [[ZonghengjiaDiplomacy|纵横家外交]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-30-suqin-de-kaigua-rensheng-lkmn-fruukdxgkddx7zb4lc3o9bw]] adds Yan as Su Qin's first successful patronage base. The episode describes Yan as the weakest of the Seven Warring States, and Su Qin persuades [[YanWengong|燕文公]] that Yan's security depends on [[ZhaoState|赵国]] blocking [[QinState|秦国]]. Yan funding then turns Su Qin's return to Zhao into credentialed diplomacy rather than another unsupported pitch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-31-hezong-lianheng-weiou-qin-lncpgmxrojc0y1meg9a3ul-o9nih]] uses that Yan-Zhao line to explain the geography of [[HezongAlliance|合纵]]. Yan belongs to the north-south anti-Qin alignment that Su Qin is trying to turn from bilateral patronage into a larger coalition.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] returns Yan as Su Qin's fallback direction after the alliance starts breaking. When [[ZhaoSuhou|赵肃侯]] blames [[SuQin|苏秦]] for failing to hold the Qi-Wei-Zhao alignment, Su Qin asks to go to Yan and says he will retaliate against [[QiState|齐国]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-37-suqin-liuguo-jituan-ceo-de-bukan-qingshi-fj2kdqlaxzyy2nqtcutktuwrq5fo]] explains how that retaliation branch becomes Su Qin's entry into Qi. [[YanYiwang|燕易王]] learns of Su Qin's affair with [[YanWengong|燕文公]]'s wife, and Su Qin proposes leaving under the appearance of criminal flight so he can serve Yan from [[QiState|齐国]]. The source reads his later advice to [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]] as [[StrategicLuxurySabotage|奢侈诱导式削弱]], a way to drain Qi's resources and reduce pressure on Yan.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-40-5-yue-chusheng-de-haizi-re-shui-la-ll9e-thidxl5cqoacfrbz-ygw8-t]] adds the next Yan succession notice. In 321 BCE, [[YanYiwang|燕易王]] dies and [[YanWangKuai|燕王哙]] succeeds, in the same compact cluster as [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]]'s death.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-45-yi-shi-wei-jian-junwang-daodi-gai-bu-gai-sharen-chengxing-lo3cok7cfwzrxesp-i8miz1fc1db]] adds Yan as one of the five eastern states in the 318 BCE attack on [[QinState|秦国]]. Yan reaches [[HanguPass|函谷关]] with Chu, Zhao, Wei, and Han, but the coalition fails because each state preserves its own forces, making Yan part of the episode's [[CoalitionSelfPreservationFailure|联盟自保失灵]] case.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-49-mengzi-yue-wo-jiao-ni-zhiguo-ni-que-ba-wo-dang-huaping-lhy14pbf9thcfdiaulcmf8dwcef8]] moves Yan from alliance participant to conquered population. [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]] has already taken Yan, and [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] says the only justification would be that Yan's people welcome Qi as rescue from suffering. The episode then says Yan's people rebel after Qi fails that standard, making Yan a case for [[MencianPeopleBasedLegitimacy|孟子的民本合法性]] and [[PeopleBasedPoliticalSecurity|民心型政治安全]] after conquest.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-51-chuhuaiwang-qin-xiao-er-kan-jiujiu-ruhe-mie-le-ni-ltfkdlivomnvux5xodh2rlfqf6ac]] only touches Yan as a closing hook. After finishing the Chu Huaiwang war sequel, the episode notes that Yan prince Ji Ping had escaped after [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]] killed his father, setting up the next Yan-politics branch without yet developing it.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2]] develops that hook through Ji Ping's return as [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]]. Yan becomes a recovery case: the new ruler mourns the dead, shares hardship with survivors, asks [[GuoWeiWarringStates|郭隗]] how to attract talent, and then draws [[LeYi|乐毅]] and [[JuXin|剧辛]] from Zhao. The source contrasts this recovery with [[YanWangKuai|燕王哙]]'s earlier misjudgment of [[ZiZhi|子之]], making Yan a test case for whether ruler respect can turn defeat into a credible talent market.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-54-wo-zun-ni-shi-wang-ni-dang-wo-shi-pi-lvhcz0ban9frlmwshd9vch-q7-ie]] adds Yan as the last stop in [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s lobbying tour, though the transcript does not develop the Yan speech. The state-level importance is the transition that follows: after leaving Yan and returning toward [[Xianyang|咸阳]], Zhang Yi learns that [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] has died and [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] has succeeded.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-55-zhangyi-tongshi-guiren-qinhuiwang-lsclfbwn06fgzooipf5up9sdkedy]] supplies the missing Yan speech. Zhang Yi tells [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]] that [[ZhaoState|赵国]] has already moved toward Qin, ceded Hejian, and could be used to attack Yan if Yan refuses. Yan's five-city concession therefore becomes the final example of [[EncirclementThreatPersuasion|包围威胁式说服]] in the tour.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-70-xihuan-huayang-zuosi-de-qiguo-junzhu-luuyasp5-nhnikdcozzaueosb-md]] completes Yan's revenge branch. [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]] appoints [[LeYi|乐毅]] as coalition commander, Yan drives beyond the first victory despite [[JuXin|剧辛]]'s caution, and the capture of [[Linzi|临淄]] lets Yan send Qi treasure and ritual objects home. Yan therefore becomes the clearest beneficiary of [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]]'s overreach and the most durable participant in the anti-Qi [[CommonEnemyAlliance|共同敌人联盟]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-72-shenme-yang-de-ren-cai-shi-zhongzhen-zhishi-logxegnqexgc7z-lodw6tr6-8f28]] turns that victory into an occupation-governance case. Yan's forces try to stabilize captured Qi by forbidding plunder, honoring recluses, reducing taxes, restoring older good policies, and converting captured cities into Yan administrative units. The same source also records the coercive edge of occupation when Yan soldiers threaten [[WangZhu|王蠋]]'s city if he refuses service.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-73-lishishang-ziyou-lianai-de-kaichuangzhe-shi-shui-lkumfsfialqgdl6qpb3c-hnz7h9u]] adds Yan's post-victory allocation problem. [[LeYi|乐毅]] keeps Yan deep in Qi while other coalition members receive wealth or nearer gains; when Qin attacks Wei afterward, Yan is named with [[ZhaoState|赵国]] as returning to help [[WeiState|魏国]]. Yan therefore remains both beneficiary of the Qi campaign and a participant in stabilizing the post-campaign balance.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-78-qiguo-mingyun-jing-bei-chengguan-gei-jiule-lpt3reyr6ok8g2lhcxrnd5qctyug]] turns Yan's advantage into a succession and command fragility case. Under [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]], slander against [[LeYi|乐毅]] is publicly rejected, and Yan's occupation of Qi remains tied to patient [[OccupationPacificationStrategy|pacification]]. After [[YanHuiwang|燕惠王]] succeeds, [[TianDan|田单]]'s [[CounterintelligenceRumorWedge|rumor wedge]] leads Yan to replace Le Yi, leaving the army angry, divided, and exposed before Qi's counterattack.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-79-shishang-yong-gengniu-da-shengzhang-diyi-ren-lsrl0posblloqwugox160xsicp2p]] shows that exposure becoming defeat. [[QiJie|骑劫]] responds to Tian Dan's bait by mutilating prisoners and digging graves, reversing Le Yi's pacification logic and creating [[AtrocityBackfireMobilization|atrocity backfire mobilization]]. When Tian Dan's [[DeceptiveSurrenderLull|false surrender]] leads into the [[FireOxNightRaid|fire-ox night raid]], Yan loses Qi Jie and its occupied Qi cities return to [[QiState|齐国]].
