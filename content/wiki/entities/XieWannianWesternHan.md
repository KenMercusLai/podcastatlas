## Overview

谢万年 / Xie Wannian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-541-ta-shi-minzu-yingxiong-zuihou-weihe-yin-tan-ruyu-llcktocpmxxib1pjckboldlzgxdu|Hanji 541]] as the Jiangzuo Dajiang connected to [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s Changling works and punished after the court reverses course. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-537-ta-weihe-yongyou-liangzuo-lingmu-ruiqi-daini-jielu-zhenxiang-lsfaatftrg7jay3mxyskl7k0u1at|Hanji 537]] supplies the prior decision layer: Xie recommends abandoning Yanling for a grander Changling project at Xinfeng, promises completion in three years, and becomes the official face of an overambitious mausoleum plan that remains unfinished after five years.

## Current Profile

Hanji 537 presents Xie Wannian earlier in the policy chain. His Changling proposal appeals to Chengdi's desire for a more imposing tomb and to Xie's own reputation interest, but the promised three-year completion proves unrealistic. The project consumes money, labor, and transport capacity, including costly earth haulage from distant locations, until officials argue for stopping Changling and returning to the already advanced Yanling works.

Hanji 541 then presents Xie as part of the same punishment field as [[ChenTangWesternHan|陈汤]]. After Chengdi accepts arguments against continuing Changling palace-city works and rewards the proposal to stop them, Xie is blamed for having overseen works that harmed the people and is ordered to be moved to Dunhuang with Chen Tang.

His current profile is therefore administrative rather than biographical. The source does not give his broader career or inner motive; it records that when a public-works project becomes politically toxic, the official attached to implementation can be recoded as disloyal or harmful even while other actors are rewarded for cancellation.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han project official tied to the Changling works.
- Proposes replacing the already-started Yanling project with Changling and promises rapid completion.
- Identified in Hanji 541 as Jiangzuo Dajiang.
- Punished after the same Changling policy field produces rewards for cancellation advocates.
- Moved toward Dunhuang with Chen Tang.
- Functions as a narrow example of proposal and implementation responsibility after a public-works reversal.
