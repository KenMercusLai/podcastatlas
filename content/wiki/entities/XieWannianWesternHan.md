## Overview

谢万年 / Xie Wannian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-541-ta-shi-minzu-yingxiong-zuihou-weihe-yin-tan-ruyu-llcktocpmxxib1pjckboldlzgxdu|Hanji 541]] as the Jiangzuo Dajiang connected to [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s Changling works. The episode uses him narrowly as a project-responsibility figure punished after the court reverses course on the Changling construction burden.

## Current Profile

Hanji 541 presents Xie Wannian as part of the same punishment field as [[ChenTangWesternHan|陈汤]]. After Chengdi accepts arguments against continuing Changling palace-city works and rewards the proposal to stop them, Xie is blamed for having overseen works that harmed the people and is ordered to be moved to Dunhuang with Chen Tang.

His current profile is therefore administrative rather than biographical. The source does not give his broader career or inner motive; it records that when a public-works project becomes politically toxic, the official attached to implementation can be recoded as disloyal or harmful even while other actors are rewarded for cancellation.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han project official tied to the Changling works.
- Identified in the source as Jiangzuo Dajiang.
- Punished after the same Changling policy field produces rewards for cancellation advocates.
- Moved toward Dunhuang with Chen Tang.
- Functions as a narrow example of implementation responsibility after a public-works reversal.
