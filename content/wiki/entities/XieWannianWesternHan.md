## Overview

谢万年 / Xie Wannian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-541-ta-shi-minzu-yingxiong-zuihou-weihe-yin-tan-ruyu-llcktocpmxxib1pjckboldlzgxdu|Hanji 541]] as the Jiangzuo Dajiang connected to [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s Changling works and punished after the court reverses course. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-537-ta-weihe-yongyou-liangzuo-lingmu-ruiqi-daini-jielu-zhenxiang-lsfaatftrg7jay3mxyskl7k0u1at|Hanji 537]] supplies the prior decision layer: Xie recommends abandoning Yanling for a grander Changling project at Xinfeng, promises completion in three years, and becomes the official face of an overambitious mausoleum plan that remains unfinished after five years. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-526-hanchao-qipa-zhidu-huangdi-si-le-jiu-neng-qianhukou-lkht_jo0z7je4gbyk10ckuxv4cub|Hanji 526]] adds that Xie also encourages [[ChenTangWesternHan|陈汤]] to revive a mausoleum county at Changling, explicitly noting the family residence, land, and status benefits such a county could bring them both.

## Current Profile

Hanji 537 presents Xie Wannian earlier in the policy chain. His Changling proposal appeals to Chengdi's desire for a more imposing tomb and to Xie's own reputation interest, but the promised three-year completion proves unrealistic. Hanji 526 adds a related political entrepreneurship layer: Xie sees a new mausoleum county as a route to accomplishment and as a concrete household benefit for his and Chen Tang's families. The project consumes money, labor, and transport capacity, including costly earth haulage from distant locations, until officials argue for stopping Changling and returning to the already advanced Yanling works.

Hanji 541 then presents Xie as part of the same punishment field as [[ChenTangWesternHan|陈汤]]. After Chengdi accepts arguments against continuing Changling palace-city works and rewards the proposal to stop them, Xie is blamed for having overseen works that harmed the people and is ordered to be moved to Dunhuang with Chen Tang.

His current profile is therefore administrative rather than biographical. The source does not give his broader career or inner motive; it records that when a public-works project becomes politically toxic, the official attached to implementation can be recoded as disloyal or harmful even while other actors are rewarded for cancellation.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han project official tied to the Changling works.
- Proposes replacing the already-started Yanling project with Changling and promises rapid completion.
- Encourages Chen Tang to support a renewed Changling mausoleum county, mixing project credit with family settlement advantage.
- Identified in Hanji 541 as Jiangzuo Dajiang.
- Punished after the same Changling policy field produces rewards for cancellation advocates.
- Moved toward Dunhuang with Chen Tang.
- Functions as a narrow example of proposal and implementation responsibility after a public-works reversal.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 526, Hanji 537, and Hanji 541. It does not reconstruct Xie Wannian's complete office history, exact office title at the moment of the Changling proposal, technical responsibility for every construction failure, legal charge wording, or later fate. Hanji 526 adds motive and prompting around the mausoleum-county idea, but not a full private correspondence record. Wang Hong's identity in Hanji 541 remains source-scoped rather than merged into an existing Wang Hong page.
