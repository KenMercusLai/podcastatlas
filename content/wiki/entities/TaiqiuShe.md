# 宋太丘社 / Taiqiu She

宋太丘社 / Taiqiu She appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-26-mengzi-laile-ln4twwp2frmr-xpumu0rqg-2gpyn]] as the object or site whose disappearance is recorded by [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] in 336 BCE. The episode explains it as probably connected to a 社 god marker, perhaps a shrine-like stone or altar associated with soil, community, and state ritual.

The source is careful about uncertainty. It says later readers may interpret the disappearance as a sign of transferred Heaven's mandate, but the concrete form and meaning of Taiqiu She remain unresolved. The page therefore links it to omen-reading frames without turning the notice into a settled prophecy.
