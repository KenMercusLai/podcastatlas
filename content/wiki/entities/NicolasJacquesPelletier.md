# Nicolas-Jacques Pelletier

## Overview

Nicolas-Jacques Pelletier, convicted of armed robbery, was the first person officially executed by guillotine, at Paris's Place de la Grève on 25 April 1792.

## Current Profile

Pelletier's execution tested the new method in public. [[CharlesHenriSanson|Charles-Henri Sanson]] prepared and secured him before releasing the blade; the event ended so quickly that a crowd familiar with longer punitive theater reportedly reacted with bewilderment and disappointment. The episode uses that reaction to show both the intended reduction of suffering and the survival of spectacle around the machine.

## Key Characteristics

- He was sentenced for armed robbery rather than a famous political crime.
- His death marked the transition from tested mechanism to official execution practice.
- The crowd's reported disappointment exposed the entertainment expectations surrounding public punishment.
