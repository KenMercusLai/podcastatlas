# 吕蒙正 / Lyu Mengzheng

## Overview

吕蒙正 / Lyu Mengzheng enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-3-juju-jieshi-qiangu-mingyan-qijie-quan-ni-yiding-yao-du-lkck6rsfd-xtzwipdy-stdhwebey|Hanji 733-3]] as the Northern Song official to whom the episode attributes [[HanyaoFu|《寒窑赋》]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Lyu as a Luoyang man, a 977 Dingchou jinshi first-place graduate, and a later high minister. Its use of him is not a full biography. He matters because the text's narrated contrast between earlier poverty in Luoyang and later court rank gives the source's fate-and-timing lesson a personal witness structure.

The page keeps the profile source-scoped. The source renders his name as 吕蒙政, while the wiki normalizes the canonical route to 吕蒙正 because the same episode identifies the received Northern Song chancellor and the text traditionally linked to him. The source does not independently prove the text's authorship.

## Key Characteristics

- He is presented as the attributed authorial figure behind [[HanyaoFu|《寒窑赋》]].
- The episode uses his poverty-to-high-office contrast to support a claim about 时, 运, and 命 rather than only personal merit.
- His biography in this source is compact: Luoyang origin, 977 Dingchou first-place examination success, and later senior office.
- His role is literary and exemplary in this source; the episode does not reconstruct his broader Song political career.
- The name normalization and attributed authorship remain source-scoped.

## Qualifications

The page records only the episode's use of Lyu Mengzheng. It does not settle the authorship of 《寒窑赋》, the exact title history, or details of Lyu's Song career. The transcript form 吕蒙政 is treated as a likely name variant or ASR error, not as a separate entity.
