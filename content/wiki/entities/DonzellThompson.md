# Donzell Thompson

## Overview

Donzell Thompson, known as “Sea Dog,” is presented in [[the-tipping-point-revisited-an-excerpt|The Tipping Point Revisited: An Excerpt]] as [[RobertSheldonBrown|Robert Sheldon Brown (“Casper”)]]'s partner in organizing a large Los Angeles bank-robbery operation.

## Current Profile

The episode places Thompson inside an operating model that separated organizers from the recruited crews entering banks. Target choice, vehicles, training, hideouts, and distribution of proceeds made repeated takeover robberies possible without requiring the organizers to appear at each scene.

## Key Characteristics

- Partnered in an organization that coordinated robbery planning and logistics.
- Remained removed from direct execution, complicating investigation and prosecution.
- Helped turn takeover robbery into a repeatable crew-based practice in the episode's account.

## Evidence

### Coordinated operating model

- [[the-tipping-point-revisited-an-excerpt]] identifies Thompson and Brown as the planners behind recruited crews and reports 175 robberies over four years.

### Removal and decline

- [[the-tipping-point-revisited-an-excerpt]] says fifty FBI agents worked on the case and that Los Angeles robberies fell substantially within roughly a year of the organizers' arrests.
