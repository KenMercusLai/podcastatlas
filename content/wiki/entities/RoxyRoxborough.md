# Roxy Roxborough

## Overview

Michael “Roxy” Roxborough is the veteran bettor and odds-maker whose career in [[gambling-with-michael-lewis-gambling-with-michael-lewis|Gambling with Michael Lewis]] connects data-driven betting, Las Vegas bookmaking, and the transition from market-making through sharp wagers to mobile customer selection.

## Current Profile

The source describes Roxborough developing a baseball-totals advantage from weather, atmospheric conditions, and ballpark dimensions that contemporary bookmakers had not fully priced. Once those signals became common knowledge, the advantage disappeared, illustrating how an informational edge can be competed away.

After describing gambling's effects on his mood, personality, and relationships, he stopped betting and founded Las Vegas Sports Consultants in 1982. The firm became a major odds supplier. His older-market account supplies the episode's normative baseline: bookmakers accepted informed action, adjusted lines, and passed risk elsewhere instead of excluding a customer solely for being likely to win.

## Key Characteristics

- Used statistics and contextual data rather than visual intuition to price baseball totals.
- Treats profitable information as temporary because markets absorb widely known signals.
- Moved from betting into centralized odds supply through Las Vegas Sports Consultants.
- Describes professional gambling as personally and relationally costly despite apparent opportunity.
- Defends an older bookmaker norm of accepting sharp action and using it to improve prices.
