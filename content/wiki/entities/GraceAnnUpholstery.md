# Grace Ann Upholstery

## Overview
Grace Ann Upholstery is a custom upholstery and furniture-refinishing workshop in Nashville, Tennessee, founded by a person named Grace in [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]].

## Current Profile
The workshop serves interior designers and residential customers, working on everything from inherited antique chairs to new furniture that only needs new fabric. Grace came into the trade by accident, moving from an art-history and decorative-arts background into an upholsterer's workshop in early 2020, then training with a British upholsterer before starting in her own garage in 2021. Demand arrived faster than her process: she was working it as a side business alongside a full-time job and a young family, and early communication failures disappointed designers.

## Key Characteristics
- Craft service business with both designer and direct residential customers.
- Skill and training come from hands-on apprenticeship rather than formal trade education.
- Demand exceeded capacity from the start, making reliability rather than awareness the binding constraint.
- Reported annual revenue around $50,000, with expansion and hiring as the stated ambition.
- Structural constraint: finding skilled upholsterers is itself difficult.
- Relationship-repair case after early service failures with interior designers.
