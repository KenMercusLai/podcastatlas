# Ironman / 铁人三项

Ironman appears in [[shangye-xiaoyang-47-jianshen-zuotijia-de-xinhuan-hyrox-daodi-you-shenme-huchenghe-1008179335]] as the closest comparison for [[HYROX]]. The episode uses Ironman to show how an event brand can bundle existing sports into an official format, distance, title, and participant identity even though swimming, cycling, and running are not themselves owned by the organizer.

For the wiki, Ironman is mainly a precedent inside [[MassParticipationSportsEventMoat]]. The official name and event system can matter more than the raw movements because participants want recognized completion, comparable results, and the status attached to the sanctioned race.
