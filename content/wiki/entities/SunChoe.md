# Sun Choe

Sun Choe appears in [[vans-kuangwei-fengguang-buzai-jingdian-fanbuxie-weishenme-maibudongle-1006742982]] as the former [[Lululemon]] chief product officer brought in to lead [[Vans]] as global brand president. The source uses her appointment to show Vans trying to repair [[HeritageProductInnovationDebt]] with product, store, and marketing changes rather than relying only on old shoe shapes.

The episode also says Choe restarted [[VansWarpedTour|Vans Warped Tour]] as part of the brand's attempt to reconnect with youth, skate, punk, and street-culture surfaces. The result is framed cautiously: Vans' latest decline narrowed to 8%, but the source does not treat that as a completed turnaround.
