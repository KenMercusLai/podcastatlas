# 荥泽 / Xingze

荥泽 / Xingze enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-103-1-simaqian-kanzhong-de-xinlingjun-suan-shi-xiaoren-ma-lvty1pdnibvqpk0m16kkxzw4t-ck]] as the water-control point southeast of [[GuanchengWarringStates|管城]]. The episode says [[QinState|秦国]] already controls Xingze when [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] considers the next step after defeating [[MengAo|蒙敖]].

The source uses Xingze to explain why [[Daliang|大梁]] remains in danger even after the coalition victory. Control of water near the Wei capital could let Qin threaten flooding or hydraulic pressure, and the host compares this danger with the earlier [[ZhiYao|智瑶]] attempt to flood Jinyang.
