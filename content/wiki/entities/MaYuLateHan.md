# 马宇 / Ma Yu (late Han)

马宇 / Ma Yu (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-973-changpingguan-zhizhan-shengsi-zhidi-zhanhou-jing-chonggui-yuhao-lgazc9dewfayb8qnkncfkfa12yet|Hanji 973]] as a Chang'an court attendant who joins [[ZhongShaoLateHan|种劭]] and [[LiuFanLateHan|刘范]] in planning an inside response against [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]]. The conspirators hope [[MaTeng|马腾]]'s attack can combine with internal action to remove Li Jue.

The episode associates Ma Yu with the plan's courier layer: his household servant is disguised as a hu-cake seller to transmit information. Once the plan leaks, Ma Yu flees to Huaili and is killed after Li Jue's forces win the [[ChangpingguanBattleLateHan|长平观之战]] branch and attack the refuge point.
