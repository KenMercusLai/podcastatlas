# Orange House

The Orange House appears in [[the-elon-game-musks-vision-of-the-future-6a633594d19896314260e5c4]] as the family beach house in southern [[Lebanon]] that [[MonaKhalil]] and [[HabibaFayed]] restored after Khalil returned in 1999. The episode describes it as becoming a B&B and a center for turtle-conservation training, education, and volunteer work on Mansouri Beach.

The house also becomes the source's concrete image of [[ConservationUnderWar]]. It was hit during the 2006 war, later sat beside a checkpoint, and remained the place Khalil refused to abandon during renewed Israeli attacks.
