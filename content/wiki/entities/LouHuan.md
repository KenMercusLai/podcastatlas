# 楼缓 / Lou Huan

楼缓 / Lou Huan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-56-qiaoqiao-liangqian-duo-nianqian-sha-shi-da-zhangfu-ltokbutqgsnrno-y29xmzwa3da41]] as one of the additional persuaders named in the same breath as [[SuDai|苏代]], [[SuLi|苏厉]], and [[ZhouZui|周最]]. The episode does not give him an independent story; it uses the list to show that the fame of [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]], [[SuQin|苏秦]], and [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]] belonged to a broader persuader wave.

In the source's frame, Lou Huan matters because the number of such figures makes [[ZonghengjiaDiplomacy|纵横家外交]] a political profession rather than a handful of isolated anecdotes. His page is intentionally source-scoped until later sources add more detail.
