# Gao Honghao / 高宏昊

Gao Honghao, or 高宏昊, appears in [[yao-shunyu-laidao-tengxun-300tian-1-176-1]] as the LatePost reporter whose reporting grounds the [[LateTalk]] discussion of [[YaoShunyu]] and [[TencentHunyuan]]. The source says he has long reported on [[Tencent]] and [[ByteDance]], which is why the episode uses his view to compare Hunyuan, WeChat, and Tencent's older platform organization.

His role in the wiki is source provenance: the episode's claims about Hunyuan morale, hiring, leadership changes, WeChat VLM, and Tencent's AI application map are reported-observation claims from Gao and the hosts rather than independently verified facts.
