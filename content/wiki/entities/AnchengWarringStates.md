# 安城 / Ancheng

安城 / Ancheng appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-73-lishishang-ziyou-lianai-de-kaichuangzhe-shi-shui-lkumfsfialqgdl6qpb3c-hnz7h9u]] as a [[WeiState|魏国]] city captured by [[QinState|秦国]] after the anti-Qi coalition victory. The episode says Qin then advanced toward [[Daliang|大梁]] before withdrawing.

The source uses Ancheng as evidence that Qin's post-coalition behavior remains territorial and balancing rather than satisfied by the wealth sent home after [[LeYi|乐毅]]'s anti-Qi campaign. Ancheng therefore belongs to [[QinEastwardPressure|秦国东进压力]] and the episode's [[PostCoalitionSpoilsImbalance|联军战后利益失衡]] frame.
