# Eumaeus

## Overview

Eumaeus is the loyal swineherd who shelters the disguised [[Odysseus]] in [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]].

## Current Profile

The episode uses Eumaeus as the lower-status hospitality and loyalty hinge of the Ithacan return. He welcomes Odysseus as a beggar, remains faithful to Odysseus' household, becomes the site of father-son reunion, and later helps execute the plan against the suitors.

## Key Characteristics

- Eumaeus shows that hospitality in [[TheOdyssey]] is not limited to kings or ideal societies.
- His loyalty protects Odysseus before public recognition is possible.
- His hut becomes the first secure space where Odysseus and [[Telemachus]] can plan.
- He participates practically in the revenge by managing the bow transfer and helping bind Melanthius.

## Evidence

- Welcoming the stranger: [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] describes Eumaeus receiving the disguised Odysseus with hospitality.
- Loyalty under uncertainty: [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] says Eumaeus believes the false parts of Odysseus' story but not the true claim that Odysseus is returning.
- Reunion site: [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] has Athena bring Telemachus back so father and son reunite at Eumaeus' hut.
- Revenge support: [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] has Eumaeus give Odysseus the bow after Telemachus' signal and help capture Melanthius.
