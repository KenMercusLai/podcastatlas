# 谬刘 / Miu Liu

谬刘 / Miu Liu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-44-yi-ge-gongsi-weishenme-buneng-you-liang-wei-ceo-lsz0xk6-0wwznkc62k-xkoxgz-r]] as the adviser [[HanXuanhuiwang|韩宣惠王]] consults when considering whether to appoint [[GongzhongHan|公众]] and [[GongshuHan|公叔]] as joint chief ministers of [[HanState|韩国]].

His advice is a warning about [[DualExecutivePowerRisk|双首长权力风险]]. Miu Liu argues that two chief ministers will not stay balanced: the stronger will build a domestic faction and approach monopoly power, while the weaker will look outside the state for backing and may sacrifice territory or interests.

The episode treats his reasoning as serious but not complete. The host notes that a single powerful minister can also become a usurping or destructive figure, and speculates that Miu Liu may have had private factional motives if he was already aligned with one of the two brothers. The page therefore keeps him as both a source of useful governance analysis and a possible example of self-interested remonstrance.
