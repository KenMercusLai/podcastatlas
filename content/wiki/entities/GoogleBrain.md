# Google Brain

Google Brain is the AI research and infrastructure organization that [[JiaYangqing|Jia Yangqing]] joined after [[UCBerkeley|UC Berkeley]]. In [[jia-yangqing-wo-suo-jingli-de-rengongzhineng-yisi-dao-ai-dianfu-shijie-de-shunian-jubian-chuantai-shengdongjixi-s10e24-a3884ade-4669-4d5c-ab2e-f98aa580f429]], it represents the moment when deep learning moved from academic breakthrough into large-company platforms, products, and specialized compute.

Jia's account connects Google Brain to [[TensorFlow]], [[TPU|TPUs]], AlphaGo, Google Photos, and Google Translate. The source uses that arc to show why AI progress depended on research, software frameworks, hardware strategy, and product channels developing together.

## Source Position
- Google Brain is framed as a place where model research and infrastructure engineering met.
- [[GPU|GPUs]] and later [[TPU|TPUs]] are treated as key enabling resources rather than incidental hardware.
- [[TensorFlow]] becomes the software expression of the organization's desire to share and scale deep-learning work.
- Consumer products such as Google Photos and Google Translate make the AI shift visible outside research.
