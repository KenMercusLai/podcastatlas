# 宋悼公 / Duke Dao of Song

宋悼公 / Duke Dao of Song appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]] through a 396 BCE succession notice. The episode says he dies and his son [[SongXiugong|宋休公]] succeeds him.

The source uses the notice mostly as an entry into [[SongState|宋国]]'s special status as a [[ShangRemnantStateLegitimacy|Shang-remnant state]], rather than as a detailed biography of Duke Dao.
