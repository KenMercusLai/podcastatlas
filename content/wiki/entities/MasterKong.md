# Master Kong / 康师傅

Master Kong / 康师傅 appears in [[xingbake-huiying-mixue-bingcheng-daigong-deng-chuanwen-li-ning-fouren-yu-mubapei-qianyue-1006054195]] as a bottled-beverage incumbent under demand pressure. The episode says its first-half beverage revenue still formed its largest income source but grew less than 1%, with tea the only growing beverage category while packaged water, juice, and carbonated drinks declined.

The source makes Master Kong part of [[BottledBeverageDemandPressure]]. The key pressure is not a single weak product line but a category shift: fresh tea drinks and coffee price declines can pull away consumption occasions that packaged beverages once served.
