# 无人知晓

## Overview
无人知晓 is a podcast/show context in the wiki for long-form conversations and essays around uncertainty, investing, engineering, AI, human agency, and ordinary-life practice. Its recurring pattern is to begin from a concrete case, then move toward a wider question about how a person should see, decide, and live.

## Current Profile
The show now has two strong branches. One branch uses investing and organizations to ask how people relate to risk, incentives, trust, and the desire for a better life. The other uses AI, language, attention, and embodied presence to ask what remains human when tools compress work and expression. E46 extends the second branch by making "seeing another person" the central problem: not only whether AI or markets distort attention, but whether the mind's own images, labels, and projections keep people from seeing each other directly.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats finance, AI, and relationships as practical philosophy rather than separate specialist domains.
- Uses conversation and essay form to translate abstract systems into ordinary-person judgment.
- Keeps human attention, body, friendship, and value choice visible when discussing technology or markets.
- Connects uncertainty and agency: portfolios, companies, feeds, and relationships all become tests of what a person can see and own.
- Presents AI as a pressure that raises the value of sincere, embodied, non-generic human expression.
