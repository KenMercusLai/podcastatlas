# AFTRA / American Federation of Television and Radio Artists

AFTRA / American Federation of Television and Radio Artists appears in [[77-ta-shi-ziyou-de-yegou-ta-shi-falaqi-685710187]] through [[OrianaFallaci|Oriana Fallaci]]'s Tonight Show anecdote. The episode says Fallaci expected a television appearance fee, but most of the amount was redirected into tax and compulsory AFTRA membership or dues, after which she was threatened with further consequences if she did not pay more.

The source uses the story as comic evidence for a serious Fallaci pattern. A union that names worker protection can still look, from the worker's perspective, like a compulsory authority extracting money and speaking in threats. The point is not an anti-union policy argument in general; it is another case in the episode's suspicion that protective institutions can become [[ProtectionAsControl]].
