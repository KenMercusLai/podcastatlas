# Sylvia Beach

## Overview
Sylvia Beach is the earlier Paris bookseller whose Shakespeare and Company bookshop supplied the inherited name and literary mythology for the current [[ShakespeareAndCompany]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Beach as the modernist literary predecessor rather than as the current shop's founder. Her earlier shop was founded in 1919, published Ulysses when others considered it obscene, and was associated with writers including [[ErnestHemingway]]. The current shop's name and aura partly depend on the episode's account that George Whitman received permission to adopt the name after Beach's shop closed.

## Key Characteristics
- She founded the earlier Shakespeare and Company in Paris in 1919.
- She is associated with high-risk literary publishing through Ulysses.
- Her circle included major modernist writers, giving the name literary inheritance.
- Her earlier shop provides the symbolic lineage for George Whitman's later bookshop.
