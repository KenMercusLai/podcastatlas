# 西双版纳植物园 / Xishuangbanna Botanical Garden

西双版纳植物园 / Xishuangbanna Botanical Garden appears in [[ep261-mifeng-weiji-ruguo-shiqu-mifeng-shijie-jianghui-zenyang-lqp-cwcdxx1ziixb35ijmmvd6cv]] as the local research setting where systematic bee investigation was only beginning in 2024. The source uses that detail to show how underdeveloped wild-bee knowledge can be even in a high-biodiversity area such as [[Xishuangbanna|西双版纳]].

The institution matters in the wiki as a concrete node for [[WildPollinatorCrisis|wild-pollinator crisis]] knowledge gaps. Without species surveys, local agriculture and conservation cannot easily know which bees support which plants, which habitats are critical, or which losses are already underway.
