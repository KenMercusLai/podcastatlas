## Overview

刘刚 / Liu Gang enters the wiki as the falsely accused younger brother in the murder case of [[LiuChangDuxiangHouEasternHan|Duxiang marquis Liu Chang]]. Hanji 760 uses him to show how a powerful outer-relative can redirect suspicion toward a convenient royal-family target until officials challenge the investigative direction.

## Current Profile

Hanji 760 says [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] kills Liu Chang and frames Liu Gang. Court investigators interrogate Liu Gang, but [[HanLengEasternHan|Han Leng]] argues that the killer should be sought in the capital rather than far away. [[HeChangEasternHan|He Chang]] then criticizes the blind pursuit of an unnamed culprit and supports a broader review that quickly clears the real direction of the case.

Liu Gang's profile is therefore evidence of procedural distortion under court power, not a biography. He matters because the accusation against him lets the source show how suspicion is redirected, challenged, and overturned.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han royal kinsman tied to the Liu Chang murder case.
- Younger brother framed by Dou Xian after Liu Chang's killing.
- Interrogated by officials before the investigation is redirected.
- Procedural evidence for scapegoating risk under outer-relative pressure.
- Source-bounded figure with no independent policy profile in the current wiki.
