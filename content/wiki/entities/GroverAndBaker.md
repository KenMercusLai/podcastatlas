# Grover and Baker

Grover and Baker appears in [[the-invention-invention]] as one of the sewing-machine companies involved in the nineteenth-century patent war. The source names the company among the firms that sued or were sued over overlapping sewing-machine patents.

Grover and Baker later joined the [[SewingMachineCombination]], making it part of the source's first [[PatentPool]] case.
