# 衡复风貌区 / Hengfu Historic District

## Overview
衡复风貌区 is the present-day Shanghai historic district through which the episode reads [[LaiAnArchitect|Lai An]]'s former French Concession apartments, villas, balconies, and street-level memory.

## Current Profile
The source treats Hengfu as a dense field of modern Shanghai architectural heritage. Lai An's works around Gao'an Road, Anting Road, Hengshan Road, and nearby streets make the area a practical walking map of [[ShanghaiFrenchConcessionArchitecture]] and [[ShanghaiArtDecoArchitecture]], while also showing how research, plaques, and citizen attention turn buildings into public memory.

## Key Characteristics
- Hengfu concentrates many of the Lai An works discussed in the episode.
- The district combines villas, apartments, leisure facilities, and street forms.
- Its memory depends on both preserved buildings and ongoing interpretation.
- It is not only French in style; its meaning is shaped by mixed Shanghai residents and researchers.

## Qualifications
