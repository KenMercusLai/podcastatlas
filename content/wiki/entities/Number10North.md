# Number 10 North

Number 10 North is [[AndyBurnham]]'s proposed [[Manchester]] office for splitting the Downing Street operation in [[a-firm-andy-what-are-new-british-pms-plans-6a5deeca76f8ee25bb4bbf9c]]. The episode treats it as the clearest symbol of Burnham's commitment to [[DevolutionAsGoverningProject|devolution]].

The source also flags the project's political risk. Because Burnham was mayor of [[GreaterManchester]], the Manchester office can look like local favoritism unless it is tied to a wider [[UnitedKingdom]] governing strategy for the North, South, East, West, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland.
