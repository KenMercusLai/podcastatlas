# 黄石公 / Huangshi Gong

黄石公 / Huangshi Gong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-123-2-gudai-shiqi-de-daochamen-daodi-you-duo-weixian-lgcknwubwm3uzqmzgbgdlxlvcwxy]] as the legendary old man who tests [[ZhangLiang|张良]] after the failed [[Bolangsha|博浪沙]] attack. The episode also calls him 圯上老人, tying him to the bridge encounter where Zhang Liang retrieves and puts on the old man's shoe.

The source treats the episode as a transformation scene. Zhang Liang's patience and forbearance convince Huangshi Gong to give him [[TaigongBingfa|《太公兵法》]], with the promise that ten years later the world will be in disorder and Zhang Liang can become a teacher of kings. This shifts Zhang Liang's wiki role from remnant-Han assassin toward strategist and future adviser.
