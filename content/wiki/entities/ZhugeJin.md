# 诸葛瑾 / Zhuge Jin

## Overview
诸葛瑾 / Zhuge Jin is [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]]'s elder brother and a Jiangdong official whose friendship with [[LuSu|鲁肃]] gives the proposed Sun-Liu alliance an interpersonal trust channel.

## Current Profile
The current source introduces Zhuge Jin indirectly. It says he fled disorder for Jiangdong, served [[SunQuan|孙权]] as a senior staff official, and was Lu Su's colleague and friend. Lu Su invokes that friendship immediately after proposing an alliance to [[LiuBei|刘备]], making Zhuge Jin's relationship network politically useful even though he does not appear in the scene.

## Key Characteristics
- Elder brother of Zhuge Liang.
- Jiangdong official connected to Sun Quan's administration.
- Friend and colleague of Lu Su.
- Cross-camp relationship node invoked during the opening of the Sun-Liu alliance.
