# Thomas Philippon

## Overview

Thomas Philippon is an economist presented by [[PlanetMoney|Planet Money]] as a warning voice on France's deteriorating fiscal trajectory and the value of gradual adjustment before markets force harsher choices.

## Current Profile

Philippon argues that advanced economies have not collectively carried debt this high for this long since World War II. In 2024 he and other economists reportedly proposed that France balance its budget over seven years without immediately reducing the existing debt stock. The source says the government did not adopt the recommendation, borrowing costs rose, and investors increasingly focused on France's worsening direction rather than its absolute debt ratio.

## Key Characteristics

- Emphasizes debt trajectory and plan credibility over a single headline ratio.
- Advocates gradual budget balancing rather than immediate debt repayment.
- Connects political inaction to higher market risk and less control over later adjustment.
- Uses France and Greece to show why improving direction can outweigh starting debt level.
- Warns that delayed emergency cuts may harm vulnerable people most.
