# 郭贡 / Guo Gong (Late Han)

郭贡 / Guo Gong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-978-gushen-quantui-shuwan-dijun-ta-zenme-zuodao-de-lhgd8vsvuaf5oi9aefh0wxabmzwv|Hanji 978]] as the uncertain military actor whose arrival threatens [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]] during [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] crisis. The source says he brings several tens of thousands of soldiers, while rumor claims he may cooperate with [[LyuBu|吕布]] and [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]].

His source role is not a long biography but a test of [[XunYu|荀彧]]'s judgment. Xun Yu reads Guo Gong as still undecided rather than already committed to Cao Cao's enemies, meets him calmly outside the city, and causes him to suspect that Juancheng has more strength or preparation than it appears to have.
