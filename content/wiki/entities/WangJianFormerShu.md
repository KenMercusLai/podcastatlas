# 王建 / Wang Jian (Former Shu)

Wang Jian / 王建 is the [[FormerShu|前蜀]] founder discussed in [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]]. The episode uses his family background, described as connected to pancake-making, to show how [[WudaiSocialMobility]] could push low-status families into rulership when military power displaced older aristocratic barriers.

The source also uses Wang Jian's worries about heirs to explain [[WudaiSuccessionCrisis]]. Founder competence did not automatically convert into stable second-generation rule, especially when military and civil expectations shifted after conquest.
