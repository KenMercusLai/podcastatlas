# 靳歙 / Jin Xi (Han general)

靳歙 / Jin Xi (Han general) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-182-weishenme-shuo-chengye-xiaohe-baiye-xiaohe-2-lqk-jifupm9lbghfzaf09igfufgb|Hanji 182 part 2]] as one of the Han commanders assigned to pursue the rebel branch around [[HouChang|侯昶]] during [[ChenXiRebellion|陈豨之乱]]. The semantic suffix keeps the page distinct from unrelated names with similar romanization.

The episode pairs Jin Xi with [[GuanYing|灌婴]] in the Quni pursuit track. Guan Ying is the figure highlighted for defeating and killing Hou Chang at [[QuniCounty|曲逆]], while Jin Xi helps mark the operation as a coordinated Han pursuit rather than a single-command anecdote.
