# 王季叶 / Wang Jiye

## Overview
王季叶 / Wang Jiye appears as a panel participant in the [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] and [[SixiaoComedy|四孝喜剧]] live episode on borrowing, money, and debt pressure.

## Current Profile
Wang Jiye's source role is to make borrowing risk concrete through operational mistakes and peer credit. He describes a dormitory campus-loan chain, a mistaken 3-million-yuan payout during [[Zhihu]] operations work, a smaller bank-transfer error, and the social difficulty of asking people to repay directly.

## Key Characteristics
- Panel voice connecting personal borrowing to campus-loan and platform-payment examples.
- Uses large operational mistakes to describe the psychological ceiling of imagined debt.
- Represents indirect collection psychology, where repayment requests are softened through excuses.
