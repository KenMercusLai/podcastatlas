## Overview

高伦 / Gao Lun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-832-wei-lingdao-beiguo-ta-shi-sha-haishi-zhi-lmfpuixmi4odrf6lu31rf6-66-lq|Hanji 832]] as the local superior pressured by [[HouLanLateHan|侯览]] into recommending or appointing an unsuitable person. His profile matters because [[ChenShiLateHan|陈寔]] absorbs responsibility for the appointment, allowing Gao's public reputation to remain clean until Gao later explains the truth.

## Current Profile

Gao Lun is not presented here as a fully developed administrator. The source uses him as the middle layer between palace pressure and county-level responsibility. When Hou Lan's request arrives, Gao fears the eunuch's power and complies. Chen Shi judges the candidate unfit but also recognizes that Hou Lan cannot simply be offended, so he privately takes on the signature responsibility.

Gao's later public statement is the source's second important move. After being summoned to serve as shangshu, he tells the farewell audience that Chen Shi returned the paperwork privately and bore the fault, giving the good name to Gao and taking the blame himself. Gao therefore functions as both beneficiary and witness of Chen Shi's responsibility.

## Key Characteristics

- Local superior whose decision is distorted by Hou Lan's court-side pressure.
- Beneficiary of Chen Shi's voluntary assumption of blame in the appointment case.
- Later public witness who restores the moral meaning of Chen Shi's action.
- Example of how local officeholders can face reputation and compliance traps under eunuch power.
