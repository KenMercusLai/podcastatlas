## Overview
天津津门虎 / Tianjin Jinmen Tiger is the [[ChineseSuperLeague|Chinese Super League]] club used in the source as a case of surviving a ten-point deduction through stability, tactical adaptation, player development, and local trust under constrained spending.

## Current Profile
The episode portrays Tianjin Jinmen Tiger as competitive beyond what its budget would predict. Its operating model joins low-cost foreign-player selection, second-chance domestic recruitment, patience with young players, and a close relationship among coaches, players, staff, and supporters. Midseason renewals stabilized expectations during the relegation fight, while two four-match winning streaks converted that trust into survival.

The club's institutional profile is less settled. It operates under a multi-party custodial arrangement with financial oversight and no single clear parent company. That can support timely pay, lean operations, and civic proximity, but the next arrangement is unresolved and the current system depends heavily on [[YuGenwei]]'s combined sporting and managerial authority.

## Key Characteristics
- Budget-constrained club that emphasizes player fit and development over transfer-market reputation.
- Relegation survivor after beginning the source-described season with a ten-point deduction.
- Team whose midseason contract renewals reduced uncertainty when new spending was limited.
- Lean organization where trust, care, and belonging partly compensate for weaker salary leverage.
- Custodially governed club supported by multiple parties and subject to public-sector oversight.
- Locally rooted matchday institution with strong attendance, merchandise interest, and close supporter contact.
- Person-dependent operation whose sporting and cultural model is concentrated around [[YuGenwei]].

## Qualifications
