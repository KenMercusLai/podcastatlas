# Yuan Ming / 袁鸣

Yuan Ming is the founder associated with [[Mashie]] in [[reai-yige-hangye-15-nian-de-liyou-shi-shenme-duitan-wang-tianfan-woyao-tou-zhenzheng-de-kuaile-tou-zui-chun-de-yuanjing-tou-renxing-de-guanghui-gonglu-boke-lu98aa1byafbbljyjrn8oquiezk]]. [[WangTianfan]] describes him as someone who had been building for years before the current AI wave, including earlier work on Taiji language and Taiji engine.

The source uses Yuan Ming as an example of founder vision and persistence. Wang's point is not only that Mashie belongs to an AI category, but that its founder carries a long-standing [[AIForFun]] direction and has already experienced the difficult move from lab-style building toward productization and user-facing AI.

## Key Claims
- Yuan Ming is presented as an early and persistent AI/product founder rather than a short-term market entrant.
- His stated "AI for fun" vision makes him a case for judging founders by motivation, taste, and desired user experience.
- The source links his path to the broader investor question of whether a founder can carry a vision through category changes, model changes, and productization.
