# 水母阴姬 / Shui Mu Yin Ji

## Overview

水母阴姬 / Shui Mu Yin Ji is the ruler of 神水宫 and the culminating opponent in [[PaintingThrushGuLong|《画眉鸟》]]. The bounded episode presents her as an overwhelmingly powerful fighter whose authority, hidden attachments, loss, and final mercy complicate a purely monstrous reading.

## Current Profile

水母阴姬 enters through water, ritualized space, and the apparent force of an entire palace. Her demand that 柳无眉 exchange [[ChuLiuxiang|楚留香]]'s head for treatment drives the earlier murder plot, yet she later insists that 柳无眉 was never poisoned. That statement may coexist with 柳无眉's sincere suffering if the latter's causal belief is false.

Her relationships with 宫南燕 and the remembered 熊娘子 give emotional exposure to a character otherwise defined through invulnerability. In the underwater fight, she is constrained by breath rather than defeated in ordinary martial exchange; both combatants release a chance to kill, so mercy interrupts the encounter twice. The source nevertheless finds the mechanics of this resolution physically and narratively weak.

## Key Characteristics

- Rules 神水宫 through concentrated martial, spatial, and symbolic authority.
- Functions as the apparent source of both a cure bargain and the claim that no poison exists.
- Combines extreme combat superiority with grief, attachment, and susceptibility to mercy.
- Is defeated provisionally through a change of environment and constraint rather than direct force parity.
- Participates in a reciprocal refusal to kill that prevents a simple victor-villain closure.
- Carries gender and intimacy reversals that the hosts regard as striking for the work's period.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one podcast's account of one novel. Intimate relationships, motive, dialogue, martial ranking, the cure bargain, and the truth known by 水母阴姬 remain source-scoped within a fictional narrative. The underwater sequence's breathing claims should not be treated as human physiology.

## What Changed

- Created a profile separating overwhelming fictional power from emotional vulnerability, epistemic authority, and the weak mechanics of the final duel.
