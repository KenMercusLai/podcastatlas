# Beck Weathers

## Overview
Beck Weathers appears in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-842831df1049b1024b0dfd8bf11ec723-108598757-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-842831df1049b1024b0dfd8bf11ec723-108598757]] as a climber badly injured during the 1996 Everest disaster. [[AraceliSegarra]] describes her [[EverestIMAXExpedition]] team helping him after he survived exposure in the storm.

## Current Profile
In this source, Weathers functions as the concrete rescue case that shifts the Everest story from summit pursuit to human obligation. Segarra says the IMAX team used tents, food, oxygen, and filming resources to help distressed climbers, then needed a helicopter rescue because Weathers could not be carried through the icefall.

## Key Characteristics
- Injured 1996 Everest climber whose survival and rescue anchor the episode's crisis account.
- Evidence case for expedition resources being redirected from filming and summit goals toward humanity.
- Figure tied to the helicopter rescue performed by [[ColonelMadanEverestPilot]] in Segarra's account.
- Boundary case for [[HighAltitudeClimbingEthics]] because rescue obligation becomes more important than achievement.
