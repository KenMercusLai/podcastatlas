## Overview

电影工作室 / Film Workshop is the creator-centered Hong Kong production company associated in the source with [[TsuiHark|徐克]]'s ability to finance, produce, and shape films across formal credit boundaries.

## Current Profile

The episode treats Film Workshop as an organizational expression of Tsui's authorship: it connects a director-producer to investment, casting, writing, art design, and production decisions rather than limiting creative identity to a directing credit. This supports a model of project control without establishing that Tsui personally directed every scene in films he produced.

## Key Characteristics

- Organizes production around a strongly involved creator-producer.
- Links financing and production control to a recognizable visual and thematic house style.
- Complicates simple equations between authorship and the director line in screen credits.

The current profile rests on one retrospective podcast rather than corporate records, contracts, production archives, or verified oral history. Founding chronology, legal structure, financing arrangements, individual credits, and claims of industrial priority remain source-scoped.
