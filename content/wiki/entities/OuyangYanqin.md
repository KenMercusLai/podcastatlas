# 欧阳艳琴 / Ouyang Yanqin

## Overview

欧阳艳琴 is a former Caixin investigative journalist and the founder of [[FoodSchool|食物学堂]], represented in the source through a decade of work on education and occupational transition for children of migrant workers.

## Current Profile

Ouyang's practice developed through revision rather than a fixed educational doctrine. She moved from legal, political, and social reporting into a Dongguan maker space, later worked as a class adviser in a Beijing school for migrant workers' children, and founded Food School in 2018 after seeing how easily students could fall out of both academic and vocational pathways.

Her initial response joined whole-person education to fixed specialties. Graduate employment experience, student rejection of offered roles, and changes in programming and design work led her to abandon the implied promise that teaching a specialty would produce a matching job. Her current model prioritizes vitality, occupational reality, agency, relative strengths, self-support, and a continuing community that permits failure without abandonment.

## Key Characteristics

- Connects educational practice to direct familiarity with migrant families and factory communities.
- Treats local material, social, and industrial environments as learning resources.
- Revises programs when graduate experience contradicts institutional assumptions.
- Rejects credentials, specialties, and employer introductions as automatic employment guarantees.
- Places student agency beneath communication, collaboration, time management, and execution skills.
- Combines freedom to choose with rules, consequences, help-seeking, and responsibility.
- Accepts a small experimental institution's limits rather than claiming a universal scalable solution.
