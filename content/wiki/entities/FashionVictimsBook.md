# 《时尚受害者》 / Fashion Victims

[[FashionVictimsBook|《时尚受害者》]] is the book discussed in [[16-shishang-shouhaizhe-jing-luse-huo-wei-zui-du-yanse-559889282]]. The episode presents it as a heavily visual fashion-history work whose beautiful garments, accessories, cosmetics, and advertisements carry "death information": disease, poison, fire, workplace injury, and ecological damage are part of the history of dress.

The book matters to the wiki because it turns clothing into [[MaterialHistoryNarrative]]. A hat, green flower, white powder, purple shoe, ballet skirt, scarf, sneaker, dry-cleaned suit, or imitation-ivory comb can reveal chemistry, class performance, gender restriction, labor exposure, consumer desire, and environmental substitution at the same time.

## Key Claims
- The source frames the book as a corrective to fashion history that treats beauty and novelty separately from health and production.
- The book's cases make [[ToxicFashionMaterialRisk]] concrete through mercury, arsenic, lead, aniline dyes, radioactive metal, dry-cleaning residue, and artificial-fiber chemistry.
- The episode's reading emphasizes that the "victim" may be a wearer, but may also be a worker, child laborer, performer, animal, or downstream community.
- Fashion risk is historical, but not only historical: new materials and global supply chains can move the danger rather than remove it.
