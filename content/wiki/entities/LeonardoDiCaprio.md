# Leonardo DiCaprio

## Overview

Leonardo DiCaprio is an actor represented here through his early interest in [[BlinkFilm|Blink]], which helped convert [[StephenGaghan]]'s response to [[MalcolmGladwell]]'s book into a highly sought Hollywood project.

## Current Profile

The source presents DiCaprio as an initiating and commercially catalytic collaborator. He responded immediately to Gaghan's proposal despite the book's lack of an obvious conventional plot, and his attachment helped make the later studio pitch unusually attractive.

His eventual displacement was not narrated as a formal rejection or public conflict. Gaghan's extended rewrite made Teddy roughly ten years younger, and Gaghan increasingly imagined [[HeathLedger]] in the role. DiCaprio's joking response, as remembered by Gaghan, signals the relational awkwardness of a creative change without establishing contractual status or motive.

## Key Characteristics

- Early listener whose enthusiasm helped move an abstract adaptation idea into development.
- Star attachment that materially increased studio demand for the project.
- Participant willing to consider a book without an obvious narrative adaptation route.
- Intended lead displaced as revision changed the protagonist's age and the writer's casting imagination.
