## Overview

卫斯理 is [[NiKuang|倪匡]]'s recurring adventure and science-fiction protagonist, represented in the bounded episodes as a curiosity-driven investigator who becomes physically or emotionally implicated in the mysteries of [[BlueBloodManNiKuangNovel|《蓝血人》]], [[BuSiYaoNovel|《不死药》]], [[HairNiKuangNovel|《头发》]], and [[XunMengNiKuangNovel|《寻梦》]].

## Current Profile

《蓝血人》 supplies the earliest bounded version of his adventure grammar. Wesley moves from a former classmate's blue blood and mental influence into diplomatic infiltration, secret-society captivity, international pursuit, alien machinery, and a mission to return [[FangTianNiKuang|方天]] to Saturn. Physical skill, disguise, improvised action, miraculous healing, and repeated escape make him a modern-wuxia hero inside a science-fiction plot. Curiosity again creates both insight and intrusion: it uncovers an extraterrestrial history, but Fang Tian explicitly challenges the assumption that another person's secret life exists to satisfy Wesley's need to know.

In this story, 卫斯理 changes from reluctant outsider to prison-break planner because he cannot tolerate an unexplained execution. His ingenuity enables 骆之逊's escape, and his pursuit carries him through concealment, smuggling networks, a ship, and a South Pacific island. Curiosity, haste, self-confidence, loyalty, and interventionist impulse operate together rather than forming a purely altruistic rescue motive.

The same traits generate the episode's strongest craft criticism. He repeatedly accepts partial explanations, fails to infer the drug's dependence despite visible evidence, inadequately searches and restrains 骆之谦, and is shot after releasing him. Forced ingestion then makes him dependent on the resource he was trying to protect from monopoly. The character therefore carries both the narrative's momentum and its strained transitions.

《头发》 broadens that pattern from a bounded drug mystery to an origin-and-salvation system. Wesley begins as a skeptic investigating death, shared dreams, sacred objects, and a Nepalese installation, then is accidentally transported to the advanced world behind those clues. He identifies the story's sharpest contradiction—that the supposed sinners were manufactured by creators seeking immortality—but the hosts argue that he compromises this insight by quickly accepting those creators as partners in eliminating earthly evil and tolerating mass sacrifice as a possible means.

《寻梦》 makes his boundary-crossing role interpretive rather than bodily. He recognizes that Yang Liqun and Liu Liling report the same earlier murder from opposed viewpoints and treats dreams, birthmarks, dialect, pain, place recognition, and historical correspondence as a cumulative case for past-life memory. His attempt to keep the pair apart shows caution about inherited hatred, but their accidental meeting, love, concealment, and final repetition exceed his control. The episode therefore uses him primarily as a comparator of testimony and a witness to the limits of explanation and intervention.

Across the four episodes, his strongest stable function is therefore not flawless judgment but boundary crossing. Curiosity carries him into systems of knowledge and power; bodily exposure or comparative testimony makes abstract questions personal; and his errors or limits give the hosts grounds to test whether a provocative ending has preserved the moral force of its own discovery.

## Key Characteristics

- Pursues hidden explanations even when the legal and personal risks are extreme.
- Uses disguise, deception, coercion, and underworld contacts as practical investigative tools.
- Acts from a mixture of curiosity, intervention, pride, and concern rather than one stable heroic motive.
- Converts observation into action quickly, sometimes before testing an explanation.
- Becomes vulnerable when confidence and plot urgency override basic control of a dangerous captive.
- Encounters alien displacement, immortality, salvation, and reincarnation through dangerous alliance, forced ingestion, accidental transport, and comparative testimony.
- Can expose an authority's foundational contradiction without maintaining that skepticism through the resolution.

## Qualifications

This is a four-novel, four-episode profile rather than a synthesis of the full 卫斯理 series. Labels such as “强行降智,” reconstructions of motive, judgments about indirect responsibility, and assessments of what a competent investigator should notice are interpretive craft claims. The clues in 《寻梦》 support a fictional hypothesis but do not independently establish reincarnation or memory transfer. These sources do not prove that every version of the character has the same traits or that any retelling preserves every plot detail, edition difference, or authorial intention.

## What Changed

- Added 《蓝血人》's early modern-wuxia adventure grammar and extraterrestrial branch.
- Qualified curiosity by adding Fang Tian's exposure-and-intrusion objection.
- Extended boundary crossing from bodily and mnemonic systems to alliance with a displaced nonhuman scientist and refusal of a life-shortening journey.
