## Overview

卫斯理 is [[NiKuang|倪匡]]'s recurring adventure and science-fiction protagonist, represented in the bounded episodes as a curiosity-driven investigator who becomes physically implicated in the mysteries of [[BuSiYaoNovel|《不死药》]] and [[HairNiKuangNovel|《头发》]].

## Current Profile

In this story, 卫斯理 changes from reluctant outsider to prison-break planner because he cannot tolerate an unexplained execution. His ingenuity enables 骆之逊's escape, and his pursuit carries him through concealment, smuggling networks, a ship, and a South Pacific island. Curiosity, haste, self-confidence, loyalty, and interventionist impulse operate together rather than forming a purely altruistic rescue motive.

The same traits generate the episode's strongest craft criticism. He repeatedly accepts partial explanations, fails to infer the drug's dependence despite visible evidence, inadequately searches and restrains 骆之谦, and is shot after releasing him. Forced ingestion then makes him dependent on the resource he was trying to protect from monopoly. The character therefore carries both the narrative's momentum and its strained transitions.

《头发》 broadens that pattern from a bounded drug mystery to an origin-and-salvation system. Wesley begins as a skeptic investigating death, shared dreams, sacred objects, and a Nepalese installation, then is accidentally transported to the advanced world behind those clues. He identifies the story's sharpest contradiction—that the supposed sinners were manufactured by creators seeking immortality—but the hosts argue that he compromises this insight by quickly accepting those creators as partners in eliminating earthly evil and tolerating mass sacrifice as a possible means.

Across both episodes, his strongest stable function is therefore not flawless judgment but boundary crossing. Curiosity carries him into systems of knowledge and power; bodily exposure makes abstract questions personal; and his errors or reversals give the hosts grounds to test whether a provocative ending has preserved the moral force of its own discovery.

## Key Characteristics

- Pursues hidden explanations even when the legal and personal risks are extreme.
- Uses disguise, deception, coercion, and underworld contacts as practical investigative tools.
- Acts from a mixture of curiosity, intervention, pride, and concern rather than one stable heroic motive.
- Converts observation into action quickly, sometimes before testing an explanation.
- Becomes vulnerable when confidence and plot urgency override basic control of a dangerous captive.
- Experiences immortality and salvation problems bodily through forced ingestion and accidental transport.
- Can expose an authority's foundational contradiction without maintaining that skepticism through the resolution.

## Qualifications

This is a two-novel, two-episode profile rather than a synthesis of the full 卫斯理 series. Labels such as “强行降智,” reconstructions of motive, judgments about indirect responsibility, and assessments of what a competent investigator should notice are interpretive craft claims. They do not establish that every version of the character has the same traits or that either retelling preserves every plot detail, edition difference, or authorial intention.
