## Overview
张五常 / Zhang Wuchang appears as an economist associated with empirical contract analysis and with the translation of institutional economics into Chinese public commentary.

## Current Profile
The bee-economics source emphasizes Zhang's method. He checked [[JamesMeade|James Meade]]'s positive-externality example against beekeeper and grower contracts, finding crop-, season-, location-, risk-, and output-specific pricing arrangements that could internalize pollination value.

Episode 504 adds a public-writing role. After returning from the United States, Zhang was invited by [[LinXingzhi|林行止]] to write for the [[HongKongEconomicJournal|《信报》]]. The source says Lin edited and encouraged his initially inexperienced Chinese prose, and that the resulting columns and books helped circulate property-rights and institutional-economics arguments in the Chinese-speaking world.

## Key Characteristics
- Tests textbook propositions against observed contracts and industry practice.
- Uses property rights and institutional arrangements to explain market coordination.
- Shows how externalities may be priced without claiming that every market is already complete.
- Became a Chinese-language public economist through sustained editorial collaboration.
- Links specialist research with broad institutional and reform debate.

## Evidence
- Empirical method: [[jiufan-zhongting-mifeng-jingjixue-956460448]] describes investigation of U.S. beekeeper-grower contracts rather than acceptance of a teaching example as settled industry fact.
- Market qualification: [[jiufan-zhongting-mifeng-jingjixue-956460448]] preserves pesticide, disease, biological, standards, and immature-market limits alongside contract pricing.
- Editorial collaboration: [[504-lin-xingzhi-xinbao-yu-xianggang-jingji-huangjin-niandai-lsqh0b2fi11uc5udp330ibwuyih]] says Lin invited, edited, and encouraged Zhang's Chinese column writing.
- Public reach: [[504-lin-xingzhi-xinbao-yu-xianggang-jingji-huangjin-niandai-lsqh0b2fi11uc5udp330ibwuyih]] connects those columns to later collections and wider Chinese-language reform discussion.

## Qualifications
The bee source cautions that observed contracts do not erase biological risk or the possible role of policy where markets are immature. Episode 504's assessment of readership, influence, and the Lin-Zhang collaboration is retrospective and source-scoped.
