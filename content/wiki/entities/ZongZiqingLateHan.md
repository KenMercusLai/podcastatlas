## Overview

宗子卿 / Zong Ziqing is an adviser in [[HouYinLateHan|侯音]]'s Nanyang revolt who uses a seemingly supportive release proposal to rescue [[DongliGunLateHan|东里衮]] and escape.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1163 says Zong Ziqing judges Hou Yin's detention of an administrator to be open resistance to Cao Cao but fears saying so directly. He instead argues that freeing Dongli Gun will build popular support, obtains Hou Yin's written authorization, prepares horses, releases the captive, and rides with him to [[CaoRen|曹仁]]. The source portrays this as survival through deception inside a dangerous and unstable command relationship.

## Key Characteristics

- Adviser who recognizes that Hou Yin's action has become rebellion.
- Conceals opposition because direct disagreement appears unsafe.
- Frames escape as a popular-support strategy Hou Yin wants to hear.
- Prepares transport and converts written authorization into a successful rescue.

## Evidence

Deceptive counsel and escape:

## Qualifications

The source does not establish Zong Ziqing's formal office, prior allegiance, exact words, or whether rescue, self-preservation, opposition to revolt, and loyalty to Dongli Gun carried equal weight. The psychological reconstruction remains episode-scoped.
