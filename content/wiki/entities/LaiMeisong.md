# 赖梅松

## Overview
赖梅松 is the founder of [[ZTOExpress|中通快递]] and a key figure in building carrier-controlled trunk transport.

## Current Profile
The episode describes Lai leaving a profitable timber business after encouragement from classmates and relatives already in express delivery. His decisive operating thesis was “先有车再养单”: scheduled interprovincial routes could create predictable service before existing parcel volume appeared sufficient, strengthening franchise confidence and later economics.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder recruited through the Tonglu classmate and relative network.
- Early investor in scheduled self-controlled trunk routes.
- Associated with cost discipline and infrastructure-led scale.

## Evidence
- Founding: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] reports a small first-day parcel count and several difficult early years.
- Infrastructure: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] says the first trunk routes lifted volume and profit, prompting rivals to follow.
- Current position: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] portrays 中通 as the largest carrier by parcel volume and a high-margin franchise operator.
