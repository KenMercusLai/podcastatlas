# Haiti

## Overview

Haiti is the independent Caribbean state created by the defeat of French colonial restoration in [[SaintDomingue]] and the success of the [[HaitianRevolution]].

## Current Profile

In the episode, Haiti's founding demonstrates that enslaved people could overturn a plantation colony, defeat attempts at renewed subjugation, and create an independent state. This achievement gave hope to enslaved populations elsewhere and terrified slaveholding societies, making Haiti both a freedom symbol and a warning invoked for reform or repression.

The founding is not rendered morally pure. Independence under [[JeanJacquesDessalines]] followed years of enslavement, civil conflict, international war, shifting alliances, and French efforts to restore control; afterward, Dessalines's government organized the killing of some remaining white inhabitants. The episode contextualizes that massacre without denying its horror.

## Key Characteristics

- Independent state born from a successful slave revolution.
- Successor to French colonial rule in Saint-Domingue.
- Demonstration that experienced armed freedom could resist re-enslavement.
- Symbol of emancipation for enslaved people beyond the island.
- Strategic warning for slaveholders elsewhere in the Atlantic world.
- Founding marked by both liberation and retaliatory mass violence.

## Qualifications

This source supplies a revolution-centered founding account rather than a general history of Haiti. Chronology, casualty totals, the scope and targets of post-independence killings, individual motives, international effects, and later Haitian political development remain outside or source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Established Haiti as both an emancipatory precedent and a source of slaveholder fear.
- Preserved the moral tension between revolutionary liberation and retaliatory massacre.
