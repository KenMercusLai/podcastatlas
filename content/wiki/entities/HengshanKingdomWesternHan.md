## Overview

衡山国 / Hengshan Kingdom is the Western Han vassal kingdom ruled in Hanji 305-2 by [[LiuCiHengshanKingWesternHan|刘赐]]. The episode treats its abolition as the institutional endpoint of a royal-house crisis in which succession conflict exposes alleged recruitment and weapons preparations to the central court.

## Current Profile

The kingdom appears not as a complete administrative history but as the jurisdiction within which family politics and military risk become inseparable. Liu Ci confines crown prince [[LiuShuangHengshanCrownPrince|刘爽]], entrusts [[LiuXiaoHengshanPrince|刘孝]] with weapons preparation, and asks the court to approve a change of heir. Rival reports then give the emperor grounds to investigate; the episode says the royal principals are ordered to die and the kingdom is converted into directly administered territory.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han vassal kingdom dependent on imperial approval for a formal heir change.
- Royal jurisdiction in which household succession conflict intersects with alleged military preparation.
- Polity exposed to central investigation through reciprocal denunciation by its ruling family.
- Abolished kingdom whose territory the source says becomes a commandery under direct court control.

## Qualifications

This page is intentionally disambiguated from [[HengshanState|恒山国]], the pre-Qin polity in Guan Zhong's economic-war story. The present profile rests on one short narrative episode and does not establish the kingdom's full chronology, geography, offices, population, fiscal organization, precise investigative procedure, or the administrative sequence and boundaries behind the stated conversion to 衡山郡.

## What Changed

- Established the Western Han 衡山国 as a distinct canonical polity rather than merging it with pre-Qin 恒山国.
- Recorded royal-house denunciation and imperial investigation as the source's mechanism for abolition.
