# Meghna Nanu

Meghna Nanu appears in [[the-elon-game-musks-vision-of-the-future-6a633594d19896314260e5c4]] as the reporter for the segment on [[Sweden]]'s new [[ChildPrisonUnits|child prison units]]. She visits Roshersberg prison north of Stockholm and uses that site to explain the country's turn toward imprisoning children convicted of serious crimes.

Her segment broadens the Swedish policy into a comparative [[YouthCriminalResponsibility]] debate. It places Sweden alongside [[Argentina]], the [[Philippines]], [[SouthKorea|South Korea]], and [[UnitedKingdom|Britain]], while stressing that lower age thresholds may not deter gangs and may worsen long-term reoffending.
