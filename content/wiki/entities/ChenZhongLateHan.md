## Overview

陈忠 / Chen Zhong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-799-donghan-mingchen-yangzhen-ruhe-jujue-zou-houmen-lmufb7lvb-kzsvjtn6iu-zrpiptf|Hanji 799]] as a court official who links frontier strategy and talent recommendation. He opposes abandoning the Western Regions after [[ZhangDangLateHan|张珰]]'s memorial and later recommends [[ZhouXieLateHan|周燮]] and [[FengLiangLateHan|冯良]] as learned, upright scholars.

## Current Profile

In the Western Regions debate, Chen Zhong argues that long-submitted states are actively probing Han intentions because they dislike Northern Xiongnu control and still look toward Han. If the court abandons Shanshan and the other states, they will attach to Northern Xiongnu; Xiongnu wealth and courage will grow, and pressure could connect toward Qiang regions, making Hexi more dangerous.

Chen Zhong therefore recommends retaining the frontier rather than closing the passes. He urges a Dunhuang command structure and strengthened [[HexiFourCommanderiesLateHan|河西四郡]] defenses, and [[EmperorAnOfHan|Emperor An]] accepts his advice by appointing [[BanYongLateHan|班勇]] as Western Regions chief clerk with five hundred troops at Liuzhong.

The same source later shows Chen Zhong as a recommender of high-reputation talent. He praises Zhou Xie of Runan and Feng Liang of Nanyang for learning and upright conduct, leading Emperor An to summon them with formal gifts. Their refusal makes Chen Zhong's recommendation successful in identifying prestige but unsuccessful in converting that prestige into service.

## Key Characteristics

- Court policy evaluator who rejects Western Regions abandonment as strategically self-defeating.
- Reads small-state alignment as a live signal of Han prestige and Xiongnu pressure.
- Links frontier withdrawal to larger Hexi and Qiang security risk.
- Recommender of respected scholars whose refusal exposes court-confidence limits.
- Source-bounded figure currently known through advice and recommendation rather than an independent biography.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 799. It does not settle Chen Zhong's full office history, family background, later career, or whether his recommendation of Zhou Xie and Feng Liang was politically coordinated with his frontier advice. The page treats him as a policy-and-recommendation actor because those are the actions recorded in the source.
