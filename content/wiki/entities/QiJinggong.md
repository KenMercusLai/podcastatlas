# 齐景公 / Duke Jing of Qi

齐景公 / Duke Jing of Qi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-2-lmfzo4sl9nd5kz3mrxdl1dl9efei]] as the [[QiState|齐国]] ruler who employs [[SimaRangju|司马穰苴]] after [[YanYing|晏婴]] recommends him. The source keeps Qi Jinggong in a supporting role, using him to show how recommendation can overcome a commander's low-status origin.
