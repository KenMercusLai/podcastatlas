# Leda Meritz

## Overview

Leda Meritz is identified in [[the-economics-of-podcasting-and-npr-plus|The economics of podcasting and NPR+]] as [[NPR]]'s senior director of subscriptions growth and the guest explaining [[NPRPlus|NPR+]] to [[AlexGoldmark]].

## Current Profile

Meritz presents subscription growth as a system problem spanning product design, station allocation, platform fees, customer data, churn, benefits, and listener feedback. Her account favors a network bundle over separate show subscriptions because the bundle can offer more value while reducing duplicated administration.

She also describes direct enrollment as strategically important: it preserves more revenue and gives NPR the contact information needed to maintain a supporter relationship. Her stated growth benchmark is to move podcast support toward the broadcast contribution rate of roughly 10%, without treating that level as a final ceiling.

## Key Characteristics

- Leads subscription-growth explanation from an NPR institutional perspective.
- Frames bundling as both listener value and operational simplification.
- Connects subscription revenue to the wider public-radio system.
- Prioritizes direct supporter data and relationships alongside net revenue.
- Treats failed-payment recovery and subscriber feedback as growth work.
