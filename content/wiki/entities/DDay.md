# D-Day

D-Day appears in [[socialist-mobility-a-telling-michigan-vote-6a74600ec3e1347e8b5bf216]] through the postcard segment. The source says postcards and photos sent after [[JohnGodfrey]]'s 1942 BBC appeal were sorted under landing-place names including Omaha, Sword, and Juno as planning began.

In this wiki, the D-Day detail is mainly a case for [[PostcardIntelligenceInfrastructure]]: everyday travel images could become military knowledge when aggregated and categorized at scale.
