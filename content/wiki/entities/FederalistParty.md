# Federalist Party

## Overview
The Federalist Party appears as the founding-era political rival to [[ThomasJefferson|Thomas Jefferson]] and the [[DemocraticRepublicanParty|Democratic-Republicans]] in the wiki's [[FirstPartySystem|first party system]] account.

## Current Profile
The Jefferson episode presents the Federalists through conflict with Jefferson: too close, in Democratic-Republican eyes, to monarchy, Britain, banks, debt, and centralized power. The Hamilton episode supplies the more affirmative Hamiltonian program behind that attack: strong central government, national banking, manufacturing, and British alignment. The party won the 1796 presidency under John Adams, lost the 1800 transfer, and in 1804 became a possible vehicle for [[AaronBurr|Aaron Burr]]'s New York governorship bid, alarming Hamilton because of rumored separatist schemes.

## Key Characteristics
- Founding-era party opposed to Jefferson's Democratic-Republicans.
- Associated in the episode with Hamiltonian federal power, banking, debt, and British alignment.
- Held the presidency under John Adams before the 1800 transfer.
- Attacked Jefferson as atheist and Jacobin during the 1800 campaign.
- Contained factions willing to consider Burr's 1804 New York campaign despite his Democratic-Republican label.
- Declined as a coherent force after Jefferson's presidency in the Jefferson episode's account.

## Evidence
- Rival program: [[686-jefferson-the-betrayal-of-liberty-part-4-glt6535571769]] describes Jefferson's clash with Hamilton over federal power, banks, debt, Britain, France, farmers, and tyranny.
- Hamiltonian program: [[685-hamilton-duel-to-the-death-part-3-glt9231431046]] says Hamilton wanted strong central government, a national bank, manufacturing support, and closer relations with Britain.
- 1796 outcome: [[686-jefferson-the-betrayal-of-liberty-part-4-glt6535571769]] says Jefferson lost to John Adams by 71 electoral votes to 68.
- 1800 rhetoric: [[686-jefferson-the-betrayal-of-liberty-part-4-glt6535571769]] says Jefferson was called atheist and Jacobin, while Adams was painted as a monarchist and British stooge.
- 1800 House role: [[685-hamilton-duel-to-the-death-part-3-glt9231431046]] says Federalists controlled the House when the Jefferson-Burr tie was resolved and Hamilton urged them to prefer Jefferson.
- 1804 Burr opening: [[685-hamilton-duel-to-the-death-part-3-glt9231431046]] says Burr ran for New York governor with Federalist support despite technically being a Democratic-Republican.
- Decline: [[686-jefferson-the-betrayal-of-liberty-part-4-glt6535571769]] says Jefferson helped destroy the Federalists as a coherent force.

## Qualifications
The sources give a Jefferson- and Hamilton-centered view rather than a full Federalist institutional history. The page therefore records the party's program and crisis behavior only where the episodes use them.
