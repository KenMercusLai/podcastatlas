# Federalist Party

## Overview
The Federalist Party appears as the founding-era political rival to [[ThomasJefferson|Thomas Jefferson]] and the [[DemocraticRepublicanParty|Democratic-Republicans]] in the episode's [[FirstPartySystem|first party system]] account.

## Current Profile
The episode presents the Federalists mainly through conflict with Jefferson. They are associated with [[AlexanderHamilton|Alexander Hamilton]]'s program and with a politics that Jefferson and his allies attacked as too close to monarchy, Britain, banks, debt, and centralized power. The party won the 1796 presidency with John Adams but lost the bitter 1800 rematch, after which the episode says Jefferson helped destroy the Federalists as a coherent force.

## Key Characteristics
- Founding-era party opposed to Jefferson's Democratic-Republicans.
- Associated in the episode with Hamiltonian federal power, banking, debt, and British alignment.
- Held the presidency under John Adams before the 1800 transfer.
- Attacked Jefferson as atheist and Jacobin during the 1800 campaign.
- Declined as a coherent force after Jefferson's presidency in the episode's account.

## Evidence
- Rival program: [[686-jefferson-the-betrayal-of-liberty-part-4-glt6535571769]] describes Jefferson's clash with Hamilton over federal power, banks, debt, Britain, France, farmers, and tyranny.
- 1796 outcome: [[686-jefferson-the-betrayal-of-liberty-part-4-glt6535571769]] says Jefferson lost to John Adams by 71 electoral votes to 68.
- 1800 rhetoric: [[686-jefferson-the-betrayal-of-liberty-part-4-glt6535571769]] says Jefferson was called atheist and Jacobin, while Adams was painted as a monarchist and British stooge.
- Decline: [[686-jefferson-the-betrayal-of-liberty-part-4-glt6535571769]] says Jefferson helped destroy the Federalists as a coherent force.

## Qualifications
The source gives a Jefferson-centered view of the Federalists. The page preserves their role as party rival without reconstructing Federalist ideology beyond the episode's contrasts.
