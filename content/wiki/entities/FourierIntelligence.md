# Fourier Intelligence / 傅利叶智能

## Overview

Fourier Intelligence / 傅利叶智能 is a Chinese robotics company represented by the bounded source as moving from rehabilitation robots into humanoid products for social interaction, companionship, assistance, and possible household service.

## Current Profile

The source's central case is a reported collaboration with [[Meta]] on a roughly 1.5-meter humanoid intended for domestic tasks and as a possible physical carrier for the [[MetaMuseModels|Muse]] consumer agent. The prototype is described as using [[Nvidia]] chips, imitating facial expressions, and grasping small objects with five-finger hands.

The current judgment is narrower than the demo description: Fourier contributes rehabilitation and humanoid hardware experience to a household-robot experiment, but U.S. restrictions on importing newer Chinese humanoids create testing and parts-access friction before repeatable home service or commercial scale is established.

## Key Characteristics

- Chinese robotics company with a rehabilitation-robot starting point.
- Developer extending into humanoid social, companionship, and assistance scenarios.
- Reported hardware partner for Meta's attempt to embody a consumer AI agent.
- Builder combining expressive interaction and five-finger manipulation in a human-scale prototype.
- Cross-border collaborator exposed to import restrictions and supply-chain access risk.
