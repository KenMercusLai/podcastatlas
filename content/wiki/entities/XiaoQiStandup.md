# 小齐 / Xiao Qi (Stand-Up Comedian)

## Overview
小齐 / Xiao Qi is a stand-up comedian featured in the [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] episode on "装逼" alongside [[LuoYonghao|罗永浩]], [[ZhangJunStandup|张俊]], and [[ShiLifen|史礼芬]].

## Current Profile
In the source, Xiao Qi is presented as the "implicit" or less obvious half of the status-display contrast. He repeatedly uses low-education identity, self-mockery, local-background examples, and "没文化" posture to win audience warmth, while the conversation also argues that this stance can itself become a form of performance.

The episode does not flatten him into anti-intellectual comedy. Xiao Qi says vanity pushed him to read, gather concepts from short videos, and try to surprise people who expect him to lack culture. His material therefore makes [[ZhuangbiAsSocialSignaling|装逼]] visible from below: self-deprecation can be honest, strategic, defensive, and status-seeking at the same time.
