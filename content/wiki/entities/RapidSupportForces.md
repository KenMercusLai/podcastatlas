# Rapid Support Forces

The Rapid Support Forces, or RSF, are one of the two main armed camps in [[Sudan]]'s war as described by [[outsider-chance-ending-sudans-proxy-war-6a75b3695d765781a2b1b132]]. The source says the RSF lost [[Khartoum]] to the [[SudaneseArmedForces|SAF]] but still controls much of western Sudan, especially [[Darfur]].

The source keeps foreign-backing claims source-scoped: [[TomGardner]] says evidence points to significant [[UnitedArabEmirates|UAE]] support for the RSF, while the UAE denies this. The RSF is also tied to [[LowCostDroneWarfare]] through sustained aerial assault around [[ElObeid]].

## Connections
- [[Sudan]], [[Darfur]], [[Khartoum]], and [[ElObeid]] - places central to the RSF account.
- [[SudaneseArmedForces]] - opposing armed camp.
- [[UnitedArabEmirates]] - alleged sponsor in the source, with denial noted.
- [[ForeignBackedCivilWar]], [[LowCostDroneWarfare]], and [[FragmentedCeasefireEnforcement]] - concepts linked to the RSF branch.
