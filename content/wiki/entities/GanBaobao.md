## Overview

甘宝宝 / Gan Baobao is a former lover of [[DuanZhengchun|段正淳]] in [[TianLongBaBu|《天龙八部》]] who builds a second household and maintains behavioral boundaries despite unresolved attachment.

## Current Profile

Pregnant with Duan Zhengchun's daughter, Gan Baobao marries 钟万仇 and values his exclusive devotion despite differences in appearance, martial ability, and status. The bounded episode credits her with loyalty to husband and household and treats 钟灵's comparatively stable upbringing as evidence that reconstruction after romantic injury can be incomplete yet meaningful.

Seeing Duan again still moves her, so the new marriage is not presented as emotional erasure. Her distinguishing choice is conduct: she continues to identify herself as married and does not simply resume the old relationship. The profile therefore separates lingering feeling from the capacity to maintain a chosen boundary.

## Key Characteristics

- Rebuilds family life after separation from Duan Zhengchun.
- Values exclusive care over superior appearance, power, or rank.
- Retains affection for a former partner without treating feeling as authorization to return.
- Protects household continuity and gives her daughter a comparatively stable environment.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on one podcast interpretation. The quality of her marriage, degree of love for either man, motives, family dynamics, and comparative judgment about 钟灵's upbringing are literary readings rather than settled facts. Maintaining a household does not itself prove emotional resolution or relational equality.
