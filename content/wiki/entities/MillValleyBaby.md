# Mill Valley Baby

Mill Valley Baby is the independent baby and nursery store opened by [[LilyKanter]] and her husband Mark in July 2002, discussed in [[serena-lily-serena-dugan-and-lily-kanter-they-built-a-20m-brand-then-one-investor-almost-destroyed-it-31a4ddcd-1cc1-48d0-9369-fd85e8714e66]]. The store sold nursery furniture, bedding, accessories, gliders, rugs, and some apparel, and quickly moved into a nearly 3,000-square-foot space.

The store matters because it was both a market sensor and the meeting place for the future [[SerenaAndLily|Serena & Lily]] founders. Lily saw customer demand for more curated nurseries, and [[SerenaDugan]] left her design portfolio there hoping for referrals. Lily then asked Serena to paint room environments in the store so customers could imagine a complete nursery or kids' room.

Lily sold Mill Valley Baby in December 2005 after Serena & Lily reached about $750,000 in annual sales and became too demanding to run alongside the store and family obligations.

## Key Claims
- The store gave Lily direct visibility into nursery-category gaps and specialty-retail economics.
- Its quick growth showed demand for curated baby and children's rooms, but Lily did not want to scale it as an owner-dependent independent retail chain.
- Serena's portfolio at the store created the connection that led to Serena & Lily.
- The store became an early example of using room environments to sell a complete design system rather than individual products.
