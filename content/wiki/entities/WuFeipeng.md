# 吴飞鹏 / Wu Feipeng

## Overview
吴飞鹏 is the architectural culture researcher interviewed by [[ChengYanliang|成言梁]] about [[LaiAnArchitect|赉安 / 赖安]], Shanghai's former French Concession, and modern architectural heritage.

## Current Profile
The source presents Wu Feipeng as a researcher who moves between archival work, old newspapers, personal contacts, on-site building walks, and public-facing writing. His book-search for Lai An begins with a 2015 encounter, then develops through a building list from Lai An's grandson, Shanghai archival confirmation, Paris travel, and continued correction of uncertain details.

## Key Characteristics
- Wu grounds architectural claims in archives, newspapers, field visits, and bounded uncertainty.
- He argues that Lai An's role in shaping the former French Concession has been underestimated.
- He links architectural style to construction quality, urban development, immigration, war, and city memory.
- He publicly corrects his own earlier mistake about Anna, showing the provisional nature of this research.
