# 刘嚣 / Liu Xiao (late Han)

## Overview

Liu Xiao is the Changsha man in Hanji 891 who becomes sikong after being presented as skilled at flattering palace eunuchs.

## Current Profile

Current evidence gives Liu Xiao a compact court-politics role rather than a full biography. His appointment to one of the Three Excellencies is interpreted by the host as an example of high office moving through eunuch favor and palace access, not through demonstrated public merit in the episode itself.

## Key Characteristics

- Changsha-origin late-Han official named in the 169 CE Three Excellencies turnover.
- Appointed sikong after Wenren Xi and Xu Xun are removed in the previous office-churn sequence.
- Presented as adept at flattering zhongchangshi / palace eunuchs.
- Evidence marker for high office being shaped by inner-court access.

- Patronage interpretation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-891-gao-duanwei-de-muqin-shi-jia-de-dinghai-shenzhen-lmqkzsnhtiwc9wzcee869kuu57m5|Hanji 891]] says he is promoted because he is especially good at flattering the zhongchangshi.

## Qualifications
