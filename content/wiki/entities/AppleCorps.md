# Apple Corps

## Overview

Apple Corps was [[TheBeatles|the Beatles']] late-1960s company, represented in this source as a creatively permissive and tax-conscious experiment whose weak controls intensified the band's financial and managerial problems.

## Current Profile

The episode describes Apple as a “groovy” attempt to organize creative freedom while also responding to tax concerns. Its openness attracted people and proposals that the band was poorly equipped to evaluate, allowing exploitation and administrative disorder to join existing musical, drug, and relationship tensions.

The company therefore appears less as a standalone cause of breakup than as an institutional amplifier. Conflict over whether Linda McCartney's father or Allen Klein should manage the band's finances made trust, family loyalty, bargaining power, and artistic partnership part of the same dispute. The rooftop concert on the Savile Row building nevertheless shows Apple remaining a site of memorable creative performance amid business disorder.

## Key Characteristics

- Combined creative idealism with tax and business purposes.
- Operated with controls the source characterizes as naive and vulnerable to exploitation.
- Turned financial administration into a conflict over trust and authority.
- Amplified rather than independently caused the band's wider fragmentation.
- Served as the setting for the final rooftop performance.

## Evidence

- Business model and vulnerability: [[the-beatles-the-british-invasion-with-conan-obrien-part-2-glt4240205398]] describes Apple's permissive ethos, tax context, opportunistic claimants, and money problems.
- Adviser conflict: [[the-beatles-the-british-invasion-with-conan-obrien-part-2-glt4240205398]] contrasts McCartney's preferred family adviser with the other members' support for Allen Klein.
- Creative coexistence: [[the-beatles-the-british-invasion-with-conan-obrien-part-2-glt4240205398]] places the Get Back rooftop concert on Apple's Savile Row building.

## Qualifications

This profile relies on one compressed podcast account rather than company records, contracts, financial statements, or a complete corporate history. Tax motives, losses, exploitation, adviser quality, member intentions, and the causal relationship between company disorder and breakup remain source-scoped. Creative openness does not by itself imply administrative incompetence, and later hindsight may over-organize a contested period.

## What Changed

- Created a profile treating Apple as an institutional amplifier of creative freedom and financial conflict.
- Separated business disorder from monocausal explanations of the breakup.
