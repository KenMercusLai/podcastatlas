# 任新 / Ren Xin (Imaging Guest)

## Overview
任新 is identified in one [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode as an imaging-department guest contributing to a multidisciplinary discussion of constipation.

## Current Profile
The available source gives Ren Xin a narrow, source-scoped role. Ren adds an imaging perspective to [[ChronicConstipationRecognitionAndManagement]] by noting that bowel symptoms can arise from compression outside the bowel, including uterine or ovarian masses, and that imaging can support differential diagnosis.
