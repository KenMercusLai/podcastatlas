# 庐陵 / Luling (late Han)

庐陵 / Luling (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1029-guoshi-wushuang-taishici-yinuo-qianjin-yi-dangxian-lvckwlhz5g-oappy89thye9oyrto|Hanji 1029]] as a local area inside the [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]] branch. [[TaishiCi|太史慈]] reports that [[TongZhiLateHan|僮芝]] controls Luling, making it one of the signs that [[HuaXin|华歆]]'s formal authority is weaker than the office title suggests.

The page keeps the place source-scoped because the episode uses Luling as an intelligence marker rather than a developed administrative history. Its importance is comparative: together with [[HaihunLateHan|海昏]] and [[PanyangLocalForces|番阳民帅]], Luling helps [[SunCe|孙策]] see that Yuzhang can be approached through local fragmentation.
