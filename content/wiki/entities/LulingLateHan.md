# 庐陵 / Luling (late Han)

## Overview
庐陵 / Luling is a Yuzhang-area locality that first appears as a marker of fragmented control and then becomes a separate commandery under Sun Ce's post-surrender administration.

## Current Profile
The current evidence gives Luling a narrow but important structural role. It shows that [[HuaXin|华歆]]'s formal authority in [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]] did not reach every local actor, and Hanji 1039 then shows [[SunCe|孙策]] converting that fragmentation into a new administrative unit assigned to [[SunFuLateHan|孙辅]].

## Key Characteristics
- Signals Yuzhang's internal fragmentation because Tong Zhi controls it despite Hua Xin's formal office.
- Becomes a newly separated commandery after Sun Ce receives Hua Xin's surrender.
- Is assigned to Sun Fu, who advances toward Da Luling when Tong Zhi is sick.
- Remains a place node rather than a developed local-society page under current evidence.

## Evidence
- Fragmentation marker: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1029-guoshi-wushuang-taishici-yinuo-qianjin-yi-dangxian-lvckwlhz5g-oappy89thye9oyrto|Hanji 1029]] says Taishi Ci reports Tong Zhi's control of Luling to Sun Ce.
- Administrative split: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] says Sun Ce separates Luling from Yuzhang and creates a Luling commandery.
- Sun Fu appointment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] assigns Sun Fu to Luling and describes his movement when Tong Zhi is ill.
- Source scope: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1029-guoshi-wushuang-taishici-yinuo-qianjin-yi-dangxian-lvckwlhz5g-oappy89thye9oyrto|Hanji 1029]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] use Luling as a control and administration signal, not a full geography.
