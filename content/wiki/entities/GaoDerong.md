## Overview

高德荣 / Gao Derong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-496-qiong-pa-le-jiemi-tanguan-zouxiang-tanfu-de-xinli-youyin-lnskbcqvjhtpfi4ikq44kpbbfnol|Hanji 496]] as the positive counterexample to "穷怕了" corruption rhetoric. The episode says he also knew poverty, but converted that memory into concern that Dulongjiang residents would continue living in hardship.

## Current Profile

The source uses Gao Derong as a moral and governance contrast rather than as a complete biography. His role is to show that poverty experience does not have a single psychological destiny. In the episode's frame, the same memory that a corrupt official may invoke for self-compensation can become empathy, public duty, and practical poverty-alleviation work.

## Key Characteristics

- Presented as someone who had also experienced childhood poverty.
- Used as a positive contrast to Gao Fubo and the Zhao Dehan-style "穷怕了" excuse.
- Associated in the source with concern for Dulongjiang residents' continued poverty.
- Functions as evidence that hardship memory can become public responsibility.
- Anchors the constructive half of [[PovertyMemoryCorruptionExcuse|贫穷记忆贪腐借口]].
