# Sutherland Springs Church Shooting

## Overview

The Sutherland Springs church shooting was a November 2017 mass shooting in Texas in which 26 people were killed. In the bounded source, the tragedy exposed the [[UnitedStatesAirForce|U.S. Air Force]]'s failure to report the gunman's prior violent conviction to the FBI.

## Current Profile

The episode uses the shooting as a case of preventable institutional failure and leadership response. The gunman had served in the Air Force, had been convicted of a violent offense, and should have been barred from legally buying a firearm, but the required report was not made. An earlier audit had already identified deficiencies in military reporting procedures.

The source focuses primarily on [[HeatherWilson]] and [[DavidGoldfein]], not on a complete history of the attack or the experiences of victims and survivors. Goldfein's funeral attendance and the community's request that he appear in uniform make the institutional relationship visible, while Pastor Frank Pomeroy's reported prayer for the shooter and his family is presented as an act of forgiveness after the death of his daughter.

## Key Characteristics

- November 2017 mass shooting at a church in Sutherland Springs, Texas.
- Killed 26 people, according to the bounded source.
- Exposed an unreported military conviction that should have disqualified the gunman from firearm purchase.
- Connected local grief to institutional accountability by the Air Force.
- Included a community response that the episode frames through welcome, mourning, and forgiveness.
