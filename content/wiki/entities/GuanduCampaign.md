# 官渡之战 / Guandu Campaign

## Overview
官渡之战 / Guandu Campaign is currently represented in the wiki as a looming [[CaoCao|曹操]]-[[YuanShao|袁绍]] confrontation rather than as a fully narrated battle. The available sources map the preconditions: Cao Cao's eastern settlement after Lü Bu, southern constraint against [[LiuBiao|刘表]], Yuan Shao's flawed war-council process, Cao-side analysis of Yuan's organization, forward deployment through [[LiyangYellowRiverLateHan|黎阳]], Zhang Xiu's renewed submission, Yang Fu's Cao/Yuan comparison, and Wei Ji's Guanzhong rear-governance plan.

## Current Profile
The campaign's current synthesis is that Guandu is prepared before it is fought. Cao Cao's position improves through territorial consolidation, selective incorporation of defeated and defected forces, pressure on potential Yuan Shao allies, practical attention to grain and population control, and conversion of analysis into deployment. Yuan Shao remains the larger northern rival, but the sources increasingly frame Cao Cao's advantage as organizational: decisive command, talent use, adviser compatibility, disciplined law, and capacity to turn surrender, salt revenue, refugees, and field placement into usable military and logistical strength.

## Key Characteristics
- Strategic horizon for Cao Cao's transition from regional survival to direct contest with Yuan Shao.
- Multi-front preparation problem involving Xuzhou/Qingzhou, Jingzhou, Zhang Xiu's Nanyang force, Guanzhong, the Yellow River line, and Guandu.
- Adviser-system contrast in which Yuan Shao has capable names but Cao Cao and Xun Yu produce clearer shared judgment.
- Defector-incorporation arena where former enemies can become manpower, legitimacy signals, and rear-security assets.
- Logistics-and-governance problem in which refugee settlement, agriculture, salt revenue, grain supply, and postwar exhaustion matter before battlefield contact.
- Deployment threshold where Cao secures Qingzhou through Zang Ba, places Yu Jin on the Yellow River bank, returns to Xudu, and arrays troops at Guandu.

- Zhang Xiu incorporation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1035-zhansha-caocao-qinzi-he-aijiang-ta-jing-zaidu-guijiang-caocao-lh10wwkwcqw5hnap3bodw6oxpt7v|Hanji 1035]] presents Zhang Xiu's renewed submission as both the removal of a potential spoiler and the gain of a strong force before Guandu.
- Guanzhong rear base: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1035-zhansha-caocao-qinzi-he-aijiang-ta-jing-zaidu-guijiang-caocao-lh10wwkwcqw5hnap3bodw6oxpt7v|Hanji 1035]] connects Yang Fu's Cao/Yuan comparison and Wei Ji's salt-funded agricultural recovery to rear order and grain support.

## Qualifications
The battle itself has not yet been narrated by the current bounded sources, so tactical battlefield claims should remain out of this page. Cao Cao and Xun Yu's assessment of Yuan Shao is source-bounded and should not be treated as a complete neutral audit. Hanji 1034 also qualifies the simple "Yuan ignored Ju Shou" explanation by making advice framing and factional meeting dynamics part of the causal field.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1034's prewar council, Cao-side assessment, and initial deployment chain to the Guandu setup.
- Reframed the campaign profile to include adviser compatibility and analysis-to-deployment conversion, not only defector incorporation and rear logistics.
- Clarified that Yuan Shao's weakness is currently shown as organizational and communicative, not merely numerical disadvantage or a single ignored proposal.
