# 官渡之战 / Guandu Campaign

## Overview
官渡之战 / Guandu Campaign is currently represented in the wiki as a looming Cao Cao-Yuan Shao confrontation rather than as a fully narrated battle. The available sources map the preconditions: Cao Cao's eastern settlement after Lü Bu, southern constraint against Liu Biao, Zhang Xiu's renewed submission, Yang Fu's Cao/Yuan comparison, and Wei Ji's Guanzhong rear-governance plan.

## Current Profile
The campaign's current synthesis is that Guandu is prepared before it is fought. Cao Cao's position improves through territorial consolidation, selective incorporation of defeated and defected forces, pressure on potential Yuan Shao allies, and practical attention to grain and population control. Yuan Shao remains the larger northern rival, but the sources increasingly frame Cao Cao's advantage as organizational: decisive command, talent use, disciplined law, and capacity to turn surrender, salt revenue, and refugees into usable military and logistical strength.

## Key Characteristics
- Strategic horizon for Cao Cao's transition from regional survival to direct contest with Yuan Shao.
- Multi-front preparation problem involving Xuzhou/Qingzhou, Jingzhou, Zhang Xiu's Nanyang force, and Guanzhong.
- Defector-incorporation arena where former enemies can become manpower, legitimacy signals, and rear-security assets.
- Logistics-and-governance problem in which refugee settlement, agriculture, salt revenue, and grain supply matter before battlefield contact.
- Organizational comparison point that makes Cao Cao's decisiveness and talent policy more important than simple size.

## Qualifications
The battle itself has not yet been narrated by the current bounded sources, so tactical battlefield claims should remain out of this page. Yang Fu's comparison of Cao Cao and Yuan Shao is a source-bounded judgment rather than a complete institutional audit. Zhang Xiu's later Guandu contribution is recorded through the episode's summary, but the page should not infer details not yet sourced.

## What Changed
- Expanded the campaign profile from eastern and southern setup to include Zhang Xiu's renewed submission and Guanzhong rear governance.
- Added agriculture, salt revenue, refugee settlement, and local-general containment as pre-battle Guandu factors.
- Clarified that the current wiki evidence treats Guandu as an organizational and logistical threshold before it becomes a battlefield story.
