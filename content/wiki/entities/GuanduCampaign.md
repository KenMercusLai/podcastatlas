# 官渡之战 / Guandu Campaign

官渡之战 / Guandu Campaign enters this wiki branch through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1027-jingzhou-zhizhu-liubiao-weihe-nan-cheng-bazhu-ll-ebguqllsf-5ycvpdogihzn3lj|Hanji 1027]] as the looming [[CaoCao|曹操]]-[[YuanShao|袁绍]] confrontation that gives [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s [[Jingzhou|荆州]] position its strategic stakes. The episode does not narrate the battle itself; it uses the pre-Guandu moment to show why [[ZhangXianLateHan|张献]] and [[HuanJieLateHan|桓阶]] attaching southern commanderies to Cao Cao matters.

In this source, the campaign is a timing horizon. If Liu Biao can aid Yuan Shao, Cao Cao faces pressure from the south while contesting the north; if [[SouthernJingzhouLateHan|southern Jingzhou]] defects toward Cao Cao, Liu Biao is constrained before he can become Yuan Shao's effective support.
