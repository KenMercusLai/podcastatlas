# Derek Abbott

## Overview

Derek Abbott is a University of Adelaide physicist presented by the source as a long-running investigator of the [[SomertonManCase]]. He developed a paternity theory involving [[JessicaThompson]]'s family, married Thompson's granddaughter Rachel, and later worked with genealogist Colleen Fitzpatrick on the hair-DNA research that reportedly identified [[CarlWebb]].

## Current Profile

Abbott argued that genetic evidence was needed both from the unidentified man and from descendants of Jessica's son Robin. Robin had died, but Abbott met his daughter Rachel; the two became engaged within a day, later married, and had three children. The episode treats this personal turn humorously while recognizing that Rachel's family connection made the proposed paternity test possible.

After authorities initially refused exhumation, Abbott obtained hairs embedded in the Somerton Man's death mask. He and Fitzpatrick used DNA and genealogical reconstruction to announce Webb's identification. The reported results also excluded Robin as the deceased man's son, requiring Abbott's earlier theory to be revised.

## Key Characteristics

- Physicist who became a persistent historical-case investigator.
- Advocate for using DNA to test identity and family hypotheses.
- Researcher whose personal life became intertwined with the Thompson family.
- Collaborator in the reported Carl Webb genetic-genealogy identification.
- Investigator whose evidence overturned part of his own earlier theory.

## Qualifications

This profile reflects a short podcast account rather than Abbott's full publications or the underlying laboratory and genealogical record. The source does not independently establish the chain of custody, analytical method, statistical confidence, or official acceptance of the identification. Abbott's willingness to revise paternity does not validate every other interpretation he offers, including the betting-note hypothesis.
