# Meng Yan / 孟岩

## Overview
Meng Yan / 孟岩 is a speaker and public explainer in the wiki's investing, AI, and life-practice branches. Across the ingested sources, he translates abstract systems into ordinary questions: what should an investor do, what should a company refuse, what should a person delegate to AI, and what does it mean to see another person clearly?

## Current Profile
Meng Yan's recurring role is not only host, investor, or entrepreneur. He is a translator between technical frames and lived practice. In the investing sources, that means turning tail risk, fund advisory, platform incentives, and sufficiency into ordinary investor behavior. In the AI sources, it means asking how people keep intention, body, attention, and connection when models can do more cognitive work. E46 adds a more solitary essay mode: he uses film, neuroscience, Buddhism, internet criticism, and Krishnamurti to argue that the person one sees may be a projection built from one's own fear and labels.

## Key Characteristics
- Translates technical or abstract systems into ordinary-person decisions and practices.
- Uses investing as a route to discuss risk, sufficiency, incentives, and life rather than only returns.
- Treats AI as an existential and attentional pressure, not only a productivity tool.
- Keeps body, friendship, human flavor, and human connection visible in technology conversations.
- Uses first-person reflection and cultural material to turn cognition into relationship practice.
