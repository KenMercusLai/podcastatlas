# 陆逊 / Lu Xun (Eastern Wu)

陆逊 / Lu Xun (Eastern Wu) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-994-genzhe-dongwu-zhongchen-lvfan-xuexi-zhichang-zhihui-lgqp2vqmh1mss96gcwmflp-ccpfu|Hanji 994]] only as a title-history comparator. The host explains that [[LuFan|吕范]]'s early `都督` role under [[SunCe|孙策]] was a lower-status military-discipline and enforcement office, not the later high command associated with Eastern Wu grand-command figures such as Lu Xun.

This page exists to keep the Eastern Wu figure distinct from [[LuXun|鲁迅]], the modern writer, because both normalize to `LuXun`. Hanji 994 does not give Lu Xun's biography or battle record; it only uses him to prevent listeners from retrojecting the later `大都督` idea onto Sun Ce's early camp.
