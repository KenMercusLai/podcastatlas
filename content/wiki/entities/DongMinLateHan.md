# 董旻 / Dong Min (late Han)

董旻 / Dong Min (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-962-dongzhuo-yu-lvbu-daodi-weishenme-juelie-lgp3kxfqnhokibf6kpli6p4j8iti|Hanji 962]] as [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s younger brother. The episode says Dong Zhuo appoints him left general while Dong Zhuo's broader kin network captures military and court authority around [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]].

This page uses the semantic suffix `LateHan` because [[DongMin|董敏]] already names a different modern writer in the wiki. In this source, Dong Min's role is narrow but structurally useful: he marks how Dong Zhuo's order turns from public Han office into family-held coercive power alongside [[DongHuangLateHan|董璜]].
