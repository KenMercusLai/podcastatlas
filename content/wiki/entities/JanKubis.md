# Jan Kubiš / 扬·库比什

## Overview

Jan Kubiš was the Czech member of the [[OperationAnthropoid|Operation Anthropoid]] strike team whose bomb fatally wounded [[ReinhardHeydrich]].

## Current Profile

Kubiš entered occupied Czechoslovakia with [[JozefGabcik|Jozef Gabčík]] and prepared the attack through months of clandestine support. When Gabčík's gun jammed, Kubiš threw the bomb that exploded beside Heydrich's car. He was wounded but escaped, then died from injuries sustained during the later German assault on the Prague church sheltering the resistance group.

## Key Characteristics

- Czech soldier selected for the exile government's assassination mission.
- Supplied the successful improvised action after the primary weapon failed.
- Was wounded by the roadside blast but escaped the initial scene.
- Died after the siege that destroyed the team's final refuge.
- Became a national resistance hero within the episode's memory account.
