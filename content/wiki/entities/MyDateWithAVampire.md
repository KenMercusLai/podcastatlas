## Overview

《我和僵尸有个约会》 is a Hong Kong supernatural television series represented here through [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]]'s discussion of the 佳佳大厦 “百鬼夜行” arc.

## Current Profile

The bounded arc joins apartment-block neighbors, adult family dependence, romance, murder investigation, ghosts, vampires, exorcism, and reincarnation. [[PingMaMyDateWithAVampire|平妈]]'s return as a living corpse externalizes qualities already present in life—suspicion, possessiveness, and punitive moral judgment—while [[APingMyDateWithAVampire|阿平]]'s apparent gentleness collapses into complicity and revenge once obedience is threatened.

Its distinctive craft lies in making the supernatural domestic and urban. [[MaXiaoling|马小玲]] carries ritual tools like cosmetics and work equipment; elevators can route ghosts toward rebirth; underworld officials resemble ordinary office workers; and an apartment building becomes the site of both neighborhood intimacy and mass haunting. The hosts find the emotional causality stronger than the folklore research or combat logic: the final crisis ends through confession and apology rather than force alone.

## Key Characteristics

- Places supernatural danger inside recognizable apartment, family, work, and neighbor relationships.
- Uses ghosts and living corpses to intensify traits and conflicts established before death.
- Joins horror, melodrama, humor, romance, and moral discussion within one serial arc.
- Modernizes folk-occult imagery through portable tools, cosmetics, elevators, and urban institutions.
- Makes the parent-child tragedy depend on autonomy, complicity, acknowledgment, and repair.
- Builds suspense from explicit ritual rules even when some rules or power levels remain inconsistent.
