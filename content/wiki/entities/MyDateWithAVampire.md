## Overview

《我和僵尸有个约会》 is a 1998 Hong Kong supernatural television series represented here through [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]]'s discussion of its opening and the following 佳佳大厦 “百鬼夜行” arc.

## Current Profile

The opening begins in the violence of 1938 红溪村, transforms 况国华, [[FushengMyDateWithAVampire|复生]], and [[YamamotoKazuoMyDateWithAVampire|山本一夫]] into vampires, and then carries their unequal uses of immortality into 1998. Renamed [[KuangTianyou|况天佑]], 况国华 lives as a police officer, avoids killing, and repeatedly changes identity; Fusheng remains inside a child's body; Yamamoto converts the same condition into wealth, organization, and domination. Their contrast makes immortality a social and ethical condition rather than a power fantasy alone.

The series' distinctive form comes from mixing wartime history, vampire rules, ghost cases, investigation, urban commerce, apartment neighbors, friendship, comedy, and romantic melodrama. [[MaXiaoling|马小玲]] crystallizes that modernization: family exorcist duty becomes a paid “cleaning company,” ritual objects become portable professional equipment, and a short-skirted urban woman replaces the conventional image of a robed master. The Japan 初春 case brings those elements together while establishing the Kuang–Ma–王珍珍 romantic triangle and Yamamoto's concealed survival.

The subsequent apartment arc makes the supernatural domestic. [[PingMaMyDateWithAVampire|平妈]]'s return as a living corpse externalizes qualities already present in life—suspicion, possessiveness, and punitive moral judgment—while [[APingMyDateWithAVampire|阿平]]'s apparent gentleness collapses into complicity and revenge once obedience is threatened. Portable ritual tools, elevators to rebirth, office-like underworld agents, and a high-rise haunting make occult procedure part of ordinary urban infrastructure. Across both episodes, the hosts find the emotional causality and genre invention stronger than some folklore claims, power levels, secrecy rules, or tactical logic.

## Key Characteristics

- Hybridizes supernatural horror, romance, investigation, wartime memory, comedy, and neighborhood melodrama.
- Treats immortality through feeding ethics, arrested aging, unstable identity, bereavement, wealth, and domination.
- Modernizes exorcism as female professional work with clients, fees, portable tools, and urban style.
- Places supernatural danger inside recognizable hotels, companies, police work, apartments, families, and friendships.
- Uses ghosts and living corpses to intensify traits and conflicts established before death.
- Builds suspense from explicit ritual and vampire rules even when continuity and power levels remain inconsistent.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to two consecutive podcast reconstructions rather than the complete series or an independent production history. The supplied opening source reports that 梁立仁's script moved from TVB to ATV, that 陈十三 extensively revised it, and that 林正英 was considered before illness prevented participation, but motives, casting logic, and Jin Yong's reported praise are not independently verified here. Supernatural mechanisms and ritual powers are story-world rules; cultural-transmission histories, character motives, romantic judgments, and writing-quality assessments remain source-scoped reception evidence.

## What Changed

- Added the 1938 origin and the contrasting ways Kuang, Fusheng, and Yamamoto live with immortality.
- Reframed the series as an intentional hybrid of wartime history, investigation, romance, horror, comedy, and urban life.
- Extended Ma Xiaoling's profile backward from apartment-arc coordinator to the modern professional-exorcist design established in the Japan case.
- Added transformation consent and the right to a finite life as core ethical tensions.
- Joined the opening's power-rule questions to the continuation's folklore and tactical inconsistencies.
