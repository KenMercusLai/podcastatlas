# The Interview (film)

## Overview

*The Interview* is the Sony Pictures comedy in which two journalists are recruited to assassinate [[KimJongUn]]. In [[the-sony-hack-the-mistakes-series-the-sony-hack-the-mistakes-series]], the film is the decision object through which [[MichaelLynton]] examines the [[SonyPicturesHack2014|2014 Sony Pictures hack]].

## Current Profile

The episode does not treat the film's artistic quality as the central issue. Its importance is organizational: an enthusiastic table read led Lynton to promise immediate approval rather than submit the project to [[SonyPictures]]' normal greenlight meeting. He believes routine legal, public-policy, and parent-company review would have surfaced the geopolitical sensitivity and likely stopped the project despite its plausible financial viability.

After threats and the breach, major theater chains declined to screen the film. Online release support and independent cinemas preserved distribution, turning the project into a dispute over whether cancellation would reward intimidation.

## Key Characteristics

- Political comedy built around a plot to assassinate North Korea's leader.
- Approved after a table read without Sony Pictures' normal cross-functional review.
- Offered limited upside in Lynton's account because it was a modest-budget, R-rated comedy with a constrained audience.
- Became the focus of warnings, attempted content softening, and distribution withdrawal.
- Was ultimately released through online channels and independent cinemas.
