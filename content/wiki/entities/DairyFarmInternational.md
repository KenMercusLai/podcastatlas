# Dairy Farm International / 牛奶公司 / DFI

Dairy Farm International / 牛奶公司 / DFI is the target company in [[vol-269-xiao-lishi-bu-yao-pa-shi-jishu-xing-tiaozheng-1004793119]]. The episode traces it from an 1886 Hong Kong fresh-milk venture into cold storage, ice, imported meat, airline catering, supermarkets, and everyday food retail before [[HongkongLand|香港置地]] launched the [[HongkongLandDairyFarmTakeover|置地饮牛奶]] takeover.

The source treats Dairy Farm as more than a consumer brand. Its milk supply, cold-chain infrastructure, supermarket footprint, and land resources made it a strategic asset inside [[HongKong|Hong Kong]]'s oligopolistic commercial system. Its vulnerability came from dispersed shareholders, high dividend expectations, a cross-directorate board, and weaker ability to mobilize retail holders during a paper-share tender.

After the takeover, the source says the old local milk business declined as mainland supply changed Hong Kong's dairy market, while Dairy Farm became a retail and restaurant management platform inside the broader Jardine/Hongkong Land orbit.
