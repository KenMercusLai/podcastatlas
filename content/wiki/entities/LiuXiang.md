# 刘向 / Liu Xiang

刘向 / Liu Xiang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-2-zanmen-dou-yuanwang-le-beifu-2000-duonian-heiguo-de-yegong-haolong-lh-h-gezpqtvnyq40zlqac8s-5e]] as the late Western Han compiler associated with [[XinXu|《新序》]]. The episode says the familiar [[YeGongHaolong|叶公好龙]] story comes from the "杂事五" section of that text.

The source does not make Liu Xiang the villain of the story in a simple authorial sense. Its point is reception-historical: a compact didactic anecdote compiled for moral persuasion can outlive the original historical actor's fuller record and turn [[YeGong|叶公]] into a symbol of hypocrisy. Liu Xiang therefore becomes a node for how edited classical story collections shape later idiom memory.
