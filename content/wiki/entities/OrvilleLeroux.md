# Orville Leroux

## Overview

Orville Leroux was the superintendent in Darby, Montana, whose family and school district became the episode's central case of conspiratorial accusation escalating into intimidation, violence, institutional damage, and forced exit.

## Current Profile

Leroux burned worn-out school Bibles after a clergyman advised that burning was a respectful disposal method. [[john-birch-vs-the-pta-john-birch-vs-the-pta]] reports that local [[JohnBirchSociety]] members turned the incident into a campaign against renewal of his contract. When the school board refused, he and his family experienced obscene calls, gestures, surveillance, threats, and harassment.

The pressure spread beyond Leroux personally. His fifth-grade son was beaten by classmates who identified him as the son of a "Bible burner," and the family reported an attempt to force their car off the road. Leroux eventually left Darby; only seven of the district's 23 teachers reportedly returned the next fall. His daughter later described a lasting loss of trust in neighbors within the family.

## Key Characteristics

- School superintendent targeted after a routine disposal decision was reframed as religious hostility.
- Retained the school board's support despite demands that his contract not be renewed.
- Tried to document surveillance by recording following vehicles' licence numbers.
- Saw political hostility extend to his children and family travel.
- Left a community to which he felt strongly attached after cumulative threats.
- Represents institutional and relational damage beyond the original political dispute.
