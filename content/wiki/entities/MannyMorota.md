# Manny Morota

## Overview

Manny Morota is a content creator and nonprofit law clerk who publishes manually researched historical news feeds on [[Twitter|X]], recounting events from 25, 50, and 100 years earlier as though they were happening in the present.

## Current Profile

[[tech-20260929-0929-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260929-0929-mp-tech-pod-128]] presents Morota as a platform-native public historian rather than an automated archival account. He researches newspapers, wire reports, and archived websites, selects a mix of major and ordinary news, and publishes it in real time or day by day. His stated aims are to restore the uncertainty of unfolding events, connect generations through memory, and create a more educational space on a platform he regards as vulnerable to toxicity and conspiracy claims.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses [[RealTimeHistoricalReenactment]] to turn archival research into an unfolding news experience.
- Keeps research and posting manual rather than relying on scheduling or automation.
- Mixes major events with international, religious, and popular-culture stories to reconstruct a period's broader texture.
- Treats contemporary confusion and misinformation as part of the historical experience, while creating an unresolved need for clear context and correction.
- Frames the project as intergenerational education for audiences with and without firsthand memory.
- Draws an ethical boundary around monetizing traumatic history by pledging the September 11 proceeds described in the episode to charity.

## Evidence

### Manual archival and editorial practice

- [[tech-20260929-0929-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260929-0929-mp-tech-pod-128]] says Morota researches historical newspapers, the [[WaybackMachine|Wayback Machine]], and wire or broadcast reporting, then manually publishes roughly 10–20 stories on an ordinary day.

### Chronology and public memory

- [[tech-20260929-0929-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260929-0929-mp-tech-pod-128]] describes his September 2001 recreation, audience responses across generations, and future plans to cover Charles Lindbergh's 1927 flight, the buildup to the [[IraqWar]], and the 2002 Beltway sniper attacks.

### Community and monetization boundaries

- [[tech-20260929-0929-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260929-0929-mp-tech-pod-128]] records Morota's wish to cultivate accurate educational discussion, limit toxic participation, and donate revenue associated with the September 11 feed.

## Qualifications

The profile rests on one short interview and its structured summary, not an independent audit of Morota's accounts, source selection, accuracy, follower growth, revenue, or audience effects. Including historical misinformation may communicate period uncertainty but can also recirculate falsehoods when posts lose context. The episode does not specify Morota's correction policy, moderation practice, citation format, or method for handling archival disagreement and traumatic material.
