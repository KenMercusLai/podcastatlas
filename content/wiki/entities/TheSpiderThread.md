# 《蛛丝》 / The Spider's Thread

《蛛丝》 / The Spider's Thread is an [[AkutagawaRyunosuke|芥川龙之介]] story used in [[17-diyubian-zhongsheng-jie-ku-yi-nian-ru-mo-561780565]] to name the author's repeated question of whether anyone can be saved. The episode summarizes it as a salvation chance that fails when self-preservation and selfishness break the thread.

The page's wiki role is mainly conceptual. In the episode, 《蛛丝》 helps connect [[HellScreen|《地狱变》]] to [[AkutagawaSalvationProblem]]: Akutagawa's dark scenes are not only displays of cruelty, but tests of whether pity, rescue, or grace can survive human fear and desire.
