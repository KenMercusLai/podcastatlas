# Bruce Schneier

## Overview
Bruce Schneier is the technology-policy voice in the Marketplace Tech episode who questions both token-tax practicality and the profitability assumptions behind public AI-company equity funds.

## Current Profile
Schneier's source role is skeptical and implementation-focused. He says he had favored a token tax earlier, but now doubts it will work if AI models run locally on phones or other devices rather than through taxable cloud tokens. He also challenges the premise that AI-company stock will reliably fund public programs, because current AI companies may lack sustainable business models and AI may behave like a commodity.

## Key Characteristics
- Schneier treats tax design as dependent on technical architecture, especially cloud versus local AI execution.
- He shifts from support for token taxation toward skepticism as AI deployment paths change.
- He challenges public equity-fund proposals by asking whether AI companies will become durably profitable.
- His episode role is a policy-practicality check rather than a rejection of planning for AI disruption.
