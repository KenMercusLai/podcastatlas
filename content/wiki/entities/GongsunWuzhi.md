# 公孙无知 / Gongsun Wuzhi

公孙无知 / Gongsun Wuzhi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-75-2-qiongshe-jichi-de-zushiye-guanzhong-lo8uei2u1qwj9tszftel1b4lswoa]] as the figure who kills [[QiXiangGong|齐襄公]] and briefly seizes power in [[QiState|齐国]]. His own killing by the people creates the power vacuum that brings [[QiHuanGong|姜小白 / 齐桓公]] and [[GongziJiu|公子纠]] back from exile-like safety bases to contest the throne.

The episode uses him as a transition device: Qi's court scandal does not end with the ruler's death, but turns into a succession crisis that determines whether [[GuanZhong|管仲]] remains a defeated partisan or becomes the architect of Qi reform.
