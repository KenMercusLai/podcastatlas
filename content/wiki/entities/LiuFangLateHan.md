# 刘放 / Liu Fang (late Han)

## Overview
刘放 / Liu Fang (late Han) enters the wiki through Hanji 1070 as the Yuyang man who persuades [[WangSongLateHan|王嵩]] to bring Zhuo commandery under [[CaoCao|曹操]]. The source uses him as an early personnel and persuasion gain in the post-Ye northern settlement.

## Current Profile
In Hanji 1070, Liu Fang appears before the Jiao Chu surrender sequence. Wang Song holds Zhuo commandery, and Liu Fang advises him to submit to Cao Cao. Cao Cao then judges Liu Fang as talented and later brings him into the司空府 military-affairs apparatus. Liu Fang's current role is therefore a local persuasion channel that turns a commandery's uncertain position into Cao-side attachment.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuyang-origin adviser in the Youzhou-side settlement field.
- Persuades Wang Song to submit Zhuo commandery to Cao Cao.
- Gains Cao Cao's attention as a useful talent after the surrender advice.
- Connects local commandery attachment with Cao-side personnel absorption.
