# Arin Dube / Arindrajit Dube

Arin Dube is the labor economist in [[the-real-horror-of-alien-and-how-it-explains-why-were-not-paid-enough]]. The episode identifies him with [[UniversityOfMassachusettsAmherst|UMass Amherst]] and uses his reading of [[AlienFilm|Alien]] to explain how [[LaborMonopsony]] can lower wages and weaken working conditions even when workers are not literally trapped on a spaceship.

His book [[TheWageStandard]] supplies the episode's policy and empirical frame. Through Dube, the episode connects [[WeylandYutani]] to [[NegativeJobAmenities]], [[CompensatingDifferentials]], [[ShroudedJobAttributes]], [[LaborSearchFrictions]], [[NonCompeteAgreements]], and [[LaborMarketCounterforces]].
