# Myanmar Military Junta

The Myanmar military junta is the military government discussed in [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]]. The episode says it overthrew [[AungSanSuuKyi]]'s government in 2021, jailed her, and later sentenced her to decades in prison before reducing the sentence.

The junta's central tension in the source is diplomatic versus domestic. It wants greater acceptance in [[ASEAN]] and at the [[UnitedNations]], so even proof-of-life access to Aung San Suu Kyi could lower opposition to normalizing relations. Yet the episode suggests the junta may fear her [[NonviolentSymbolicPower]] enough to withhold credible access despite those possible gains.
