# 日谈

[[ritan-chuantai-hongloumeng-haishi-keyi-du-yidu-de-587167638]] adds a second [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] and 日谈 crossover, this time centered on [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]]. In contrast to the Women's Day restart-life episode, this source makes 日谈 part of a public classic-reading setup: the conversation starts from hosts who have not fully read the novel and tests how a beloved classic can be made inviting without flattening it.

日谈 is the crossover context for [[ritan-chuantai-jiemei-liaotianhui-chongqi-rensheng-yeshi-wo-zui-yaojiao-616116425]]. The episode is presented as a Women's Day special responding to listeners' desire for more female voices, with [[Hookie]], [[YinJie|殷姐]], and [[QinZong|秦总]] meeting online for the first time.

In this wiki, the source makes 日谈 a bridge into personal narrative, [[FemaleSelfPossession]], and the use of popular culture as a prompt for [[LifeRestartThoughtExperiment]] rather than only entertainment discussion.
