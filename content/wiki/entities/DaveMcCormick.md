# Dave McCormick

## Overview
Dave McCormick appears in the wiki as the Republican [[UnitedStatesSenate|U.S. senator]] from [[Pennsylvania]] who joins [[JohnFetterman|John Fetterman]] on [[AllIn|All-In]] to discuss bipartisan Senate work, AI and energy investment, blue-collar opportunity, data-center politics, and campaign spending.

## Current Profile
The episode presents McCormick as a conservative senator who treats Pennsylvania's divided electorate as a mandate to find practical overlap without hiding ideological disagreement. His strongest source-specific role is the AI-infrastructure and political-economy voice: he argues that Pennsylvania's gas, power, manufacturing, life-sciences, and trade-labor base can turn AI data centers into durable regional opportunity if communities receive clear commitments and government does not slow the buildout through broad moratoriums.

## Key Characteristics
- Republican senator using Pennsylvania's mixed electorate to justify bipartisan cooperation.
- Conservative vote posture paired with willingness to work on selected legislation.
- AI and energy infrastructure advocate tying data centers to manufacturing, life sciences, defense, and society.
- Wealth-concentration critic who says capitalism must expand opportunity to retain legitimacy.
- Blue-collar growth advocate emphasizing welders, electricians, energy projects, and industrial sites.
- Campaign-finance realist who describes Senate races as extremely expensive but not his top reform priority.
