# 薄姬 / Lady Bo

薄姬 / Lady Bo enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-117-1-papa-dalian-lishishang-shuangwen-de-kaishan-bizu-lr0h-kfeguvatattnj-mz8fgvnc]] in the post-Wei afterlife branch. The episode first places her with [[WeiBao|魏豹]], who asks [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负]] to read her face and hears that she will bear a future royal or imperial son.

The episode's main point is that Bao Ji's significance is displaced across regimes. Wei Bao thinks the prediction points to his own possible future, but Bao Ji later becomes connected to [[LiuBang|刘邦]] and bears [[LiuHeng|刘恒]]. When Liu Heng becomes Emperor Wen of Han in 180 BCE, the prediction is fulfilled through Han, not through restored Wei.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-135-fanwaipian-2-nvxiangshi-yin-kanming-nengli-bei-liubang-fenghou-lgu1fr1zrzmc0o72wtbpxmajb7s|Qinji 135 番外篇2]] repeats this branch while centering [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负]]. It keeps Bao Ji as the hinge between Wei Bao's mistaken ambition and Western Han succession: the prophecy is accurate inside the story only because Bao Ji's future is rerouted from Wei Bao to Liu Bang.
