## Overview

薄姬 / Lady Bo, later the dowager mother of [[LiuHeng|汉文帝刘恒]], connects the wiki's displaced-prophecy account of Western Han succession to a later conflict in which her only brother [[BoZhaoWesternHan|薄昭]] is compelled to die for killing an imperial envoy.

## Current Profile

The two Qinji sources place Bao Ji first with [[WeiBao|魏豹]], who hears [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负]] predict that she will bear a future ruler and mistakes the promise for his own dynastic future. Bao Ji later becomes connected to [[LiuBang|刘邦]] and bears Liu Heng, so the narrative fulfills the prediction through Han rather than restored Wei. This is a story of displaced fulfillment, not evidence that physiognomy predicts succession.

Her later dynastic role appears through her status as a living dowager and Bo Zhao's only sister. [[LiDeyu|李德裕]] uses that relationship to criticize Liu Heng's compelled-suicide method on filial and ritual grounds: enforcing law against the emperor's uncle also imposes grief on the emperor's mother. The episode gives Bao Ji no direct speech or intervention. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-220-minjian-geyao-fengci-wendi-jia-renyi-liuheng-fanying-guguai-2-lplkv_tu9yxh-ys565ebs4unt7nj|Hanji 220 part 2]]

## Key Characteristics

- Woman whose predicted imperial child is fulfilled through a different man and dynasty than Wei Bao expects.
- Mother of Liu Heng and later senior dowager in the Western Han court.
- Sister whose family bond becomes part of the moral argument over Bo Zhao's punishment.
- Largely mediated by others' predictions and evaluations in the current sources rather than by preserved speech or action of her own.

## Evidence

Succession and prophecy narrative:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-117-1-papa-dalian-lishishang-shuangwen-de-kaishan-bizu-lr0h-kfeguvatattnj-mz8fgvnc|Qinji 117-1]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-135-fanwaipian-2-nvxiangshi-yin-kanming-nengli-bei-liubang-fenghou-lgu1fr1zrzmc0o72wtbpxmajb7s|Qinji 135 番外篇2]] route Xu Fu's prediction from Wei Bao's mistaken ambition to Bao Ji's son Liu Heng.

Dowager kinship and punishment cost:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-220-minjian-geyao-fengci-wendi-jia-renyi-liuheng-fanying-guguai-2-lplkv_tu9yxh-ys565ebs4unt7nj|Hanji 220 part 2]] identifies Bo Zhao as her only brother and attributes to Li Deyu the argument that Liu Heng's decision failed to console her as his mother.

## Qualifications

The prophecy sources narrate fulfillment but do not validate physiognomy, and they do not fully reconstruct Bao Ji's movement from Wei Bao's household to Liu Bang's. Hanji 220 part 2 uses her as the object of Li Deyu's filial argument but supplies no direct evidence of her response, political intervention, or relationship with Bo Zhao beyond the asserted sibling tie.
