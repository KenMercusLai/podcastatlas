## Overview

尹忠 / Yin Zhong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-507-ta-zhansha-xiongnu-chanyu-weihe-fanbei-xiayu-lhfkej7pqtikx5st73sls-kwo_ze|Hanji 507]] as the Yushi Dafu blamed after the Yellow River flood. The episode says [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] sharply criticizes his rescue plan as rough and impractical, after which Yin Zhong kills himself.

## Current Profile

Yin Zhong's page records a disaster-accountability role rather than a full career. Hanji 507 contrasts the earlier failure to invest in prevention with harsh scrutiny after the breach. Yin Zhong stands at that second stage: once flooding has already destroyed offices, stations, housing, and fields, the court demands usable rescue planning, and an official whose plan is judged inadequate becomes the visible liability point.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Yushi Dafu in the Hanji 507 flood aftermath.
- Blamed by Chengdi for a rescue plan judged crude and impractical.
- Kills himself after imperial reprimand.
- Illustrates the office-liability side of disaster response after prevention has failed.
