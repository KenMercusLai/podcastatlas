# analytics.js

analytics.js is the small open-source routing library that becomes the decisive pivot point in [[peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted-peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted]]. [[PeterReinhardt]] says it was originally created to send [[ClassMetric]] data to tools such as [[Mixpanel]], [[GoogleAnalytics|Google Analytics]], and [[Kissmetrics]].

The library matters because it was more valuable than the larger analytics product the team had spent a year building. A [[HackerNews]] launch produced strong attention, stars, and signups, and users then asked for a hosted version where destinations could be changed with buttons. That turns analytics.js into the source's cleanest [[OpenSourceWedge]] and [[InternalToolProductization]] example: a small internal tool became [[Segment]] because outside users recognized the integration pain.
