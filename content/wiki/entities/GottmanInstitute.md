# Gottman Institute / 哥特曼研究所

## Overview
[[GottmanInstitute|Gottman Institute / 哥特曼研究所]] appears in the wiki as the institution attached to [[GottmanMethod]] in the Culture Limited Vol.349 episode.

## Current Profile
The source uses the institute as a shorthand for the couple-therapy framework associated with [[JohnGottman|John Gottman]]. Its wiki role is therefore institutional context: the episode does not profile the organization, but it places the method's love-map, repair, shared-meaning, and four-horsemen language under this named relationship-therapy tradition.

## Key Characteristics
- It is the named institutional context for the Gottman relationship framework in this source.
- It anchors the episode's claim that couple interaction can be observed and modeled.
- Its practical language enters the wiki through love maps, repair, and shared meaning.
- Its risk language enters through criticism, contempt, defensiveness, and stonewalling.
