# 曹豹 / Cao Bao (late Han)

曹豹 / Cao Bao (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu|Hanji 999]] as the Xiapi official whose conflict with [[ZhangFei|张飞]] opens the rear of [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s [[Xuzhou|徐州]] position. The episode says Cao Bao had served [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]], did not get along with Zhang Fei, and was killed after Zhang Fei found a pretext.

The source treats his death as the proximate trigger for disorder in [[Xiapi|下邳]]. That disorder makes [[XuDanLateHan|许耽]]'s gate-opening to [[LyuBu|吕布]] possible and turns [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s grain promise into a successful attack route against Liu Bei.
