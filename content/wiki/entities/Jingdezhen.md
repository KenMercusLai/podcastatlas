# Jingdezhen / 景德镇

Jingdezhen / 景德镇 is the [[Jiangxi]] city at the center of [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode treats it as a long-running porcelain production system whose value came from raw materials, water transport, specialized labor, official and export orders, kiln know-how, market intermediaries, and city ecology rather than from a single object or isolated technique.

The source follows Jingdezhen from early [[QingbaiPorcelain|青白瓷]] and [[YuanBlueAndWhitePorcelain|元青花]] through the [[JingdezhenImperialKilnFactory|御窑厂]], late Qing and Republican industrialization attempts, post-1949 state-owned factories, the 1990s market transition, and the contemporary mix of [[JingpiaoCreativeCeramicEcology|景漂]] creators, [[Taoxichuan|陶溪川]], livestream ecommerce, tourism, and [[AdvancedCeramicsIndustrialUpgrade|advanced ceramics]].

## Key Claims
- Jingdezhen's durable advantage is systemic: [[JingdezhenPorcelainProductionSystem]] joins clay, porcelain stone, kilns, fuel, river transport, artisans, merchants, and orders.
- The city is historically connected to [[Fuliang]], [[HutianKilnSite]], [[GaolingVillage]], and [[ChanglingPorcelainStoneMine]] rather than only to finished museum objects.
- Modern Jingdezhen has cultural visibility and tourist pull that exceed its provincial GDP ranking, making it a case for [[LongTermPlaceOperation]] as well as manufacturing history.
