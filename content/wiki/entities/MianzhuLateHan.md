# 绵竹 / Mianzhu (late Han)

绵竹 / Mianzhu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-940-de-minxin-zhe-de-tianxia-ni-xin-bu-xin-loipf7b0p5a-yh0rj2njnsml8a1j|Hanji 940]] as the [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] location where [[MaXiangLateHan|马相]] and [[ZhaoZhiLateHan|赵祗]] begin their uprising. The episode also says [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]] later moves the provincial seat there after entering Yizhou.

Mianzhu therefore functions as both crisis site and attempted settlement site. It is where official extraction backlash becomes open revolt, and then where Liu Yan tries to stabilize the province through [[YizhouPacificationByLeniency|益州宽惠安抚]].
