# Nicole Nguyen

Nicole Nguyen is the [[WallStreetJournal|Wall Street Journal]] personal technology columnist whose AI fitness testing anchors [[tech-20260204-0204-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260204-0204-mp-tech-pod-128]]. In the [[MarketplaceTech]] episode, she compares [[AppleWorkoutBuddy]], [[FitbitAIHealthCoach]], [[Peloton]]'s camera-based feedback, and free chatbot workout planning.

Her role in the wiki is as a consumer-technology evaluator. The source uses her product testing to separate useful [[AIFitnessCoaching]] from unresolved limits: hallucinated workout steps, missed rep counts, expensive hardware and subscriptions, and weak accountability.
