# Sydney Diet Heart Study

## Overview

The Sydney Diet Heart Study was a 458-participant dietary trial whose surviving magnetic tape enabled [[ChristopherRamsden]] to reanalyze saturated-fat replacement with linoleic-acid-rich oil.

## Current Profile

In the episode, the Sydney study is the first recovered trial in Ramsden's search for older randomized evidence about vegetable-oil replacement. A former research assistant had preserved its data on nine-track tape. Ramsden's reanalysis reportedly found a similar pattern to the later Minnesota analysis: lower cholesterol did not translate into demonstrated lower mortality.

## Key Characteristics

- Older 458-participant dietary fat-replacement trial.
- Survived through individual preservation of a nine-track magnetic tape.
- Reanalyzed as evidence about linoleic-acid-rich oil and mortality.
- Used as a parallel to the larger [[MinnesotaCoronaryExperiment]].

## Evidence

### Preservation and reanalysis

- [[the-basement-tapes-the-basement-tapes]] reports the surviving tape, Ramsden's recovery, and the similar cholesterol-versus-mortality conclusion.

## Qualifications

The source does not provide the original protocol, intervention composition, population details, endpoints, effect estimates, publication record, or later critiques. The study is evidence in a contested nutrition literature, not a complete dietary recommendation.
