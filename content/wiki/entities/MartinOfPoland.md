# Martin of Poland

## Overview

Martin of Poland was a medieval chronicler whose expanded account became the canonical version of the [[PopeJoan]] legend.

## Current Profile

[[403-the-mystery-of-the-pregnant-pope-glt1981561492]] says Martin placed Joan in the ninth century and added English origin, Mainz birth, a lover, male disguise, study, Roman advancement, election as John, pregnancy, a procession route, and public childbirth. Specificity gave the tale historical texture but did not solve its late-source and chronology problems.

## Key Characteristics

- Systematized a prior popess story into a biography and papal sequence.
- Connected the legend to named places, office, procession, and Roman ritual memory.
- Made accumulated narrative detail substitute rhetorically for contemporary evidence.

## Evidence

- Biographical elaboration: [[403-the-mystery-of-the-pregnant-pope-glt1981561492]] lists the origin, lover, education, disguise, court rise, and papal name in Martin's version.
- Ceremonial anchoring: [[403-the-mystery-of-the-pregnant-pope-glt1981561492]] ties public childbirth to the route between St Peter's and the Lateran.

## Qualifications

Martin wrote centuries after the period he described. The episode treats his details as stages in legend formation, not corroboration of a female pontificate.

## What Changed

- Created a profile of Martin's role in making the legend appear chronologically and ritually grounded.
