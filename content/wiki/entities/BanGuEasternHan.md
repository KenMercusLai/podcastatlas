## Overview

班固 / Ban Gu enters the wiki as the Eastern Han historian whose unfinished `Han Shu` becomes a court problem after he dies in prison during the [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] cleanup. Hanji 767 makes his death a compact case of private grievance being folded into public purge conditions.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Ban Gu through the end of his life rather than through his full literary career. His servant had once drunkenly insulted a Luoyang magistrate; when the court later arrests Dou-family guests, that old grievance becomes an opportunity to take Ban Gu down. Ban Gu dies in prison while `Han Shu` is still unfinished.

The immediate institutional consequence is that [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]] orders Ban Gu's younger sister [[BanZhaoLateHan|Ban Zhao]], identified as the wife of Cao Shishu, to continue the work. Ban Gu's current profile is therefore tied to historiographical continuity, but the causal event in this source is political exposure during an outer-relative purge.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han historian attached in this source to the unfinished `Han Shu`.
- Court casualty whose death occurs in prison during the Dou-family cleanup.
- Example of how an old private grievance can become lethal once a broader purge opens.
- Brother whose unfinished work creates Ban Zhao's continuation role.
- Source-bounded figure whose broader scholarship is not yet synthesized on this page.
