## Overview

Barbara Carvalho is identified in the supplied [[PlanetMoney]] episode as director of the [[MaristPoll|Marist Poll]], responsible for survey questions, methodology, and statistical analysis.

## Current Profile

Carvalho presents polling as an adaptive measurement practice: historical errors motivate random selection, contemporary nonresponse motivates weighting and new contact modes, and disappointing experimental results remain useful when they clarify what a question does and does not measure.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses historical polling failures to distinguish sample size from sample representativeness.
- Oversees question design, survey methodology, and statistical work at Marist.
- Treats weighting as a response to identified sample imbalance rather than a universal cure for nonresponse.
- Frames failed forecasting experiments as evidence that can redirect research toward turnout and voter expectations.
