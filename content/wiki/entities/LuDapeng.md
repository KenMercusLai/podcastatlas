# 陆大鹏 / 路大鹏 / Lu Dapeng

## Overview
陆大鹏 / 路大鹏 / Lu Dapeng appears as a historical explainer across [[HuzuoHuyou|忽左忽右]] and [[WeirdHistoryPodcast|怪东西 Weird History]], moving from [[Ceuta]] and Mediterranean borderlands through [[WilliamShakespeare|Shakespeare]] and English drama to comparative disaster history.

## Current Profile
Across the bounded sources, Lu works as a historical explainer with strong contextual range. The Ceuta episode moves from border news into Mediterranean and Atlantic history; the Shakespeare episode joins literary ambiguity, stage practice, dynastic history, and political memory. In the earthquake episode, personal experience opens a comparison of [[HuaxianEarthquake1556|Huaxian]] and [[LisbonEarthquake1755|Lisbon]] through buildings, public health, communications, relief, legitimacy, archives, and philosophy. His recurring posture is comparative and evidentiary: legends, literary memory, famous totals, and causal stories are useful only when their limits remain visible.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses a single geographic site to organize long-span world history.
- Uses a canonical literary figure to connect text, stage, language, monarchy, and historical memory.
- Connects ancient, medieval, early-modern, and present-day border politics without treating any one layer as sufficient.
- Separates documented history from legends and later national memory.
- Treats theatre and literature as historical evidence for reception, memory, and public imagination rather than as transparent records of events.
- Uses comparative catastrophe to connect environmental triggers with institutions, knowledge, belief, and secondary mortality.

## Qualifications
This page is bounded to three podcast appearances and does not profile Lu Dapeng's broader writing, translation, or media work. The source notes disagree on Chinese surname spelling: the Ceuta source uses 路大鹏, while the other two use 陆大鹏; the wiki keeps one `LuDapeng` entity and records the variant here. Quantitative earthquake claims and priority claims remain source-scoped.
