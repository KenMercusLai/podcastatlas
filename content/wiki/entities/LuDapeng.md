# 路大鹏 / Lu Dapeng

## Overview
路大鹏 / Lu Dapeng appears in the Ceuta episode as the [[HuzuoHuyou|忽左忽右]] guest explaining how [[Ceuta]] connects Mediterranean antiquity, Islamic Iberia, Portuguese expansion, Spanish rule, and modern migration politics.

## Current Profile
In this source, Lu works as a historical-geography explainer. He moves from the immediate news of Moroccan swimmers reaching Ceuta into a long chronological account of the [[StraitOfGibraltar]], the [[IberianPeninsula]], North Africa, [[IberianReconquista]], and [[PortugueseMaritimeExpansion]]. His interpretive posture is cautious: he uses classic origin claims, legends, and literary memory, but marks several of them as uncertain or simplified.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses a single geographic site to organize long-span world history.
- Connects ancient, medieval, early-modern, and present-day border politics without treating any one layer as sufficient.
- Separates documented history from legends and later national memory.
- Frames Ceuta as a gateway into wider stories about Mediterranean exchange and Atlantic expansion.
