# 陆大鹏 / 路大鹏 / Lu Dapeng

## Overview
陆大鹏 / 路大鹏 / Lu Dapeng appears in [[HuzuoHuyou|忽左忽右]] episodes as a guest who turns a specific cultural object or place into a long historical explanation, from [[Ceuta]] and Mediterranean borderlands to [[WilliamShakespeare|Shakespeare]] and English drama.

## Current Profile
Across the bounded sources, Lu works as a historical explainer with strong contextual range. In the Ceuta episode, he moves from Moroccan swimmers and border news into the [[StraitOfGibraltar]], the [[IberianPeninsula]], North Africa, [[IberianReconquista]], and [[PortugueseMaritimeExpansion]]. In the Shakespeare episode, he moves from literary value and English idiom into mixed early-modern theatre audiences, [[GlobeTheatre|Globe Theatre]] performance practice, English dynastic history, and [[ShakespeareHistoryPlayPoliticalMemory]]. His interpretive posture is cautious: he uses legends, literary memory, and canonical works, but separates them from direct historical proof.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses a single geographic site to organize long-span world history.
- Uses a canonical literary figure to connect text, stage, language, monarchy, and historical memory.
- Connects ancient, medieval, early-modern, and present-day border politics without treating any one layer as sufficient.
- Separates documented history from legends and later national memory.
- Treats theatre and literature as historical evidence for reception, memory, and public imagination rather than as transparent records of events.

## Qualifications
This page is bounded to two 忽左忽右 appearances. It does not profile Lu Dapeng's broader writing, translation, or media work. The source notes disagree on Chinese surname spelling: the Ceuta source uses 路大鹏, while the Shakespeare source uses 陆大鹏; the wiki keeps one `LuDapeng` entity and records the variant here.
