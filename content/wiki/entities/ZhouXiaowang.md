# 周孝王 / King Xiao of Zhou

周孝王 / King Xiao of Zhou appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] in the [[QinState|秦国]] origin digression. The source says he gives [[QinFeizi|非子]] land at Qin after valuing his horse-raising skill and lets him continue the Ying family sacrifices.

The page is source-scoped. Zhou Xiao Wang matters here because the later [[TaishiDan|太史旦]] speech relies on the idea that Zhou and Qin have an older political and genealogical relationship.
