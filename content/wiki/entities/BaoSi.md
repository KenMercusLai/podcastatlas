# 褒姒 / Bao Si

## Overview
褒姒 / Bao Si enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-601-2-fenghuo-xi-zhuhou-yixiao-tianxia-wang-baosi-shi-hefang-yaonie-lkirbrvonyiyaousoblcd|Hanji 601-2]] as the Western Zhou consort whose later memory is dominated by the "一笑亡国" and "烽火戏诸侯" stories around [[ZhouYouwang|周幽王]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Bao Si less as a recoverable court actor than as a blurred figure produced by legend, moral blame, and dynastic-collapse storytelling. It retells a supernatural birth account from the [[LienuZhuan|《列女传》]] tradition, then explicitly warns that this material is myth-like and should not be treated as firm history.

Bao Si's profile therefore remains intentionally qualified. The episode records her being offered to Zhou after Bao's defeat in 779 BCE, becoming tied to Zhou Youwang's efforts to make her smile, and disappearing after the 771 BCE attack that killed the king and ended Western Zhou. It does not establish that she personally caused the dynasty's fall.

## Key Characteristics
- Legendary consort whose public memory is attached to the fall of Western Zhou.
- Figure filtered through mythic birth narrative, abandonment, adoption, and ominous signs.
- Target of a gendered "妖姬" blame frame that the episode repeats but does not fully endorse.
- Historically obscure person whose moral responsibility remains source-scoped and disputed.

## Evidence
Legendary consort:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-601-2-fenghuo-xi-zhuhou-yixiao-tianxia-wang-baosi-shi-hefang-yaonie-lkirbrvonyiyaousoblcd]] says Bao Si is remembered through Zhou Youwang's attempt to win her smile and the later collapse at Lishan.

Mythic birth narrative:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-601-2-fenghuo-xi-zhuhou-yixiao-tianxia-wang-baosi-shi-hefang-yaonie-lkirbrvonyiyaousoblcd]] retells the dragon-saliva box, black-lizard, miraculous pregnancy, abandonment, and Bao adoption story while marking it as myth-like.

Gendered blame frame:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-601-2-fenghuo-xi-zhuhou-yixiao-tianxia-wang-baosi-shi-hefang-yaonie-lkirbrvonyiyaousoblcd]] groups Bao Si with Mei Xi, Da Ji, and Li Ji as "四大妖姬," but its closing qualification keeps the label from becoming a settled causal claim.

Historical obscurity:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-601-2-fenghuo-xi-zhuhou-yixiao-tianxia-wang-baosi-shi-hefang-yaonie-lkirbrvonyiyaousoblcd]] closes by describing Bao Si as complex and hard to resolve rather than as a transparent villain.

## Qualifications
The source is a short popular-history episode and itself says the supernatural biography "当不得真." Bao Si's precise origin, agency at court, role in beacon-fire stories, and fate after the Quanrong attack remain source-scoped.

## What Changed
- Added Bao Si as a canonical entity for the Hanji 601-2 myth-and-reception episode.
- Separated her legendary afterlife from any settled causal responsibility for Western Zhou's fall.
