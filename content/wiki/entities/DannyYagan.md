# Danny Yagan

## Overview
Danny Yagan is an economist identified by [[middlegarchs-are-the-new-oligarchs-32587514]] as a coauthor of research on private-business owners, taxation, and top incomes.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Yagan as part of the [[EricZwick]]-[[OwenZidar]] research team that used linked tax records to reveal a large, previously less visible private-business contribution to income concentration. The work underlies the episode's distinction between celebrity billionaires and a much broader wealthy-owner class.

## Key Characteristics
- Researcher using administrative tax data to study private business and inequality.
- Coauthor of the episode's foundational pass-through-business analysis.
- Contributor to a view of top-income growth that reaches beyond public corporations and finance.

## Evidence
Research role:
- [[middlegarchs-are-the-new-oligarchs-32587514]] names Yagan alongside Zwick and Zidar in the Treasury-linked project connecting private businesses to owners.

Distributional contribution:
- [[middlegarchs-are-the-new-oligarchs-32587514]] uses the team's results to argue that pass-through businesses explain a large share of analyzed top-income growth.
