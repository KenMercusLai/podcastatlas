# 刘备 / Liu Bei

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-958-jiemi-shishang-zui-duanming-de-tongchoudikai-lve41q9gyijbop8wk-b7e6shogju|Hanji 958]] adds Liu Bei's early personal setup before the later Xuzhou and Cao Cao branches. The episode presents him as a Zhongshan Jing Wang descendant from Zhuo commandery whose Han-clan label still carries symbolic value, but not enough inherited material power to explain his later ascent by itself.

The source instead emphasizes reputation and conduct. Liu Bei is described as poor in youth, supported by relatives for schooling, quiet, difficult to read emotionally, and able to avoid unnecessary hostility through the host's "three no" summary: not making enemies, not haggling over every slight, and not fearing small losses. Hanji 958 therefore backfills why later pages can treat Liu Bei as a plausible [[LocalEliteProxySelection|地方豪强代理人选择]] candidate: before he has a stable base, he has a social style that lowers threat perception while preserving ambition.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-973-changpingguan-zhizhan-shengsi-zhidi-zhanhou-jing-chonggui-yuhao-lgazc9dewfayb8qnkncfkfa12yet|Hanji 973]] supplies Liu Bei's first major [[Xuzhou|徐州]] foothold before the later succession pages. [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] asks [[TianKaiLateHan|田楷]] and Liu Bei for help against [[CaoCao|曹操]], then gives Liu Bei four thousand Danyang soldiers, recommends him as Yuzhou inspector, and stations him at [[PeiCounty|小沛]].

The source frames this as a career step rather than a completed takeover. Liu Bei loses his first clash with Cao Cao east of Tan, but Cao Cao's [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] crisis forces withdrawal, allowing Liu Bei to survive long enough for the Tao Qian succession branch later developed in Hanji 981 and Hanji 982.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] adds a later thematic reading of Liu Bei's regime formation. The episode says Liu Bei's move from roving warlord to Hanzhong-king scale depends on adviser-enabled system upgrade: [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] turns survival politics into a state operating system, while [[FaZheng|法正]] represents sharp campaign-level planning in the Dingjun Mountain example.

The source does not narrate the full Shu-Han chronology. It uses Liu Bei as the positive contrast to [[YuanShao|袁绍]] inside [[AdviserStateArchitecture|谋士政权架构]]: advice matters because it becomes institutions, legitimacy, and executable strategy rather than remaining impressive but unused counsel.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]] supplies the first Xuzhou acceptance scene. After [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] dies, [[MiZhu|糜竺]] brings officials and people to welcome Liu Bei, but Liu Bei initially refuses and suggests [[YuanShu|袁术]] because his own force is thin and [[Xuzhou|徐州]] is politically exposed. [[ChenDeng|陈登]] and [[KongRong|孔融]] then persuade him that Yuan Shu is the wrong alternative and that refusing a locally supported opening would leave future regret.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-982-ba-yi-ba-liubei-ru-xu-beihou-de-liyi-zhi-zheng-ljajdc95xtylhkn5teefz26yyyz1|Hanji 982]] adds the pre-986 Xuzhou acceptance logic. The episode says [[MiZhu|糜竺]], [[ChenDeng|陈登]], and [[KongRong|孔融]] support Liu Bei because he has reputation and Han-clan legitimacy but not enough force to swallow the local order. Liu Bei therefore becomes the source case for [[LocalEliteProxySelection|地方豪强代理人选择]]: a useful proxy for Xuzhou elites who want protection and legitimacy without accepting [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s Huainan absorption risk.

刘备 / Liu Bei enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-2-pandian-huangdimen-jiuhou-xiaopihao-livpg3zh7lsqffgca2pomiqnlvyd|Qinji 131-2]] as the person harmed by [[ZhangFei|张飞]]'s drunken loss of Xuzhou. The episode uses Liu Bei's resulting lack of a base to make the Zhang Fei story political rather than merely comic.

His role here is secondary but structurally important. In the source's alcohol frame, the drinker's lapse matters because it transfers cost to allies, dependents, and a wider military project.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-986-tianyu-qiwang-bixian-lingqi-kuang-lvlvu057-tnz3hal6oodhydvzvbd|Hanji 986]] adds the immediate Xuzhou succession opening before his first reception of [[LyuBu|吕布]]. After [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] dies, Liu Bei takes over [[Xuzhou|徐州]], and [[CaoCao|曹操]] sees a chance to attack before Liu Bei has fully rooted himself. The episode stops before the adviser warning, so Liu Bei's role here is the newly installed holder whose position creates Cao Cao's temptation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-987-dingtao-zhizhan-caocao-he-lvbu-dianfeng-duijue-lofq0pq8n9rrfvfttunahisy0ave|Hanji 987]] adds the immediately earlier reception scene before the later [[Xuzhou|徐州]] status inversion. After [[LyuBu|吕布]] loses [[DingtaoBattleLateHan|定陶之战]] and flees east, Liu Bei receives him. The source says Lü Bu seats Liu Bei on [[LadyYanLyuBu|严氏]]'s bed, has her salute him, and calls Liu Bei "old brother"; the host reads Liu Bei's polite but unhappy response through [[PowerEtiquetteReading|权力礼仪细读]] and a clash of public/private ritual boundaries.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu|Hanji 999]] supplies the detailed late-Han Xuzhou loss behind that compact memory. Liu Bei is fighting [[YuanShu|袁术]] around Xuyi and [[Huaiyin|淮阴]] while [[ZhangFei|张飞]] guards [[Xiapi|下邳]]. After Zhang Fei kills [[CaoBaoLateHan|曹豹]], [[XuDanLateHan|许耽]] opens Xiapi to [[LyuBu|吕布]], Liu Bei's family is captured, and his force collapses into hunger before [[MiZhu|糜竺]] funds the remnant.

The same source makes Liu Bei's surrender to Lü Bu a survival-first move rather than a voluntary alliance. He has lost Xiapi, been pushed toward Haixi, and faces an army so hungry that the source reports cannibalism. Submitting to Lü Bu and later stationing at [[PeiCounty|小沛]] keeps a damaged organization alive inside a new hierarchy created by [[GrainLeverageAllianceBetrayal|粮食诱导式同盟背叛]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1008-ruhe-pingjia-caocao-de-tuntianzhi-ls8wvh9ox-pcjmt0z8w82opbha-k|Hanji 1008]] adds Liu Bei's immediate pre-Hanji-1009 vulnerability. [[YuanShu|袁术]] sends [[JiLing|纪灵]] against him after trying to bind [[LyuBu|吕布]] through marriage, and Liu Bei asks Lü Bu for help. Lü Bu's辕门射戟 mediation saves Liu Bei for the moment, but the next episode shows how quickly that temporary balance collapses.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1009-liubei-touben-caocao-xianxie-langru-hukou-llm0ds4krkptjdtb71hbusnpzbdc|Hanji 1009]] gives Liu Bei a direct late-Han branch. After [[LyuBu|吕布]] attacks him, Liu Bei flees to [[CaoCao|曹操]], even though that shelter is dangerous. The episode emphasizes his ability to endure reversal and accept a hard host relationship rather than cling to pride.

The same source makes Liu Bei the test case for [[ReputationConstrainedTalentProtection|名声约束下的人才保护]]. [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] warns Cao Cao that killing Liu Bei after he arrives in desperation would frighten other capable people away. Cao Cao therefore protects and uses him, sending him toward [[PeiCounty|小沛]] with troops and supplies against Lü Bu.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] adds Liu Bei as one of the actors pressured by [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s self-coronation. The episode says Liu Bei's仁义 reputation would be damaged if he ignored an open imperial usurpation against [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], making him part of the [[AntiUsurpationCoalitionPressure|讨逆同盟压力]] around Yuan Shu.
