# Delamu / 《德拉姆》

## Overview
Delamu / 《德拉姆》 is the documentary invoked in [[245-nujiang-chunri-mijing-chutan-8221838951-646453]] to compare early-2000s Nujiang life and transport with the hosts' contemporary road trip.

## Current Profile
Within this source, Delamu functions as memory infrastructure. The hosts use it to imagine the Nujiang Gorge before current highway access, when Tea Horse Road-style movement, mules, short mountain horses, rockfall, and isolated settlements still shaped everyday life more strongly.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a visual record of Nujiang mobility before current roads became dominant.
- Connects Nujiang travel to [[TeaHorseRoad|Tea Horse Road]] labor rather than only scenery.
- Shows hardship without reducing local people to misery.
- Helps the hosts question simple assumptions about why residents would or would not leave remote places.

## Evidence
- Transport contrast: [[245-nujiang-chunri-mijing-chutan-8221838951-646453]] says the film shows early-2000s local reliance on Tea Horse Road paths, mules, and short-legged horses.
- Hardship marker: [[245-nujiang-chunri-mijing-chutan-8221838951-646453]] recalls a mule being killed by falling rocks, making older transport danger concrete.
- Ethical interpretation: [[245-nujiang-chunri-mijing-chutan-8221838951-646453]] says Momo noticed strong vitality rather than resentment in the film's people, while the hosts question their own reflex to ask why residents do not simply leave.
