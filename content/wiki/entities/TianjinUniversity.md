# Tianjin University / 天津大学

Tianjin University / 天津大学 appears in [[ep266-dang-ai-zhonggou-daxue-women-gai-ruhe-dingyi-hao-zhuanye-llbzcgsefktcvkq-f37hoan79dd]] as an AI-and-chemistry education example. [[LiXiaojie|李小杰]] says some chemistry teaching there began adding AI content around 2020, initially creating friction around programming and environment setup, but later becoming more legible as AI entered research and employment.

The source also names Tianjin University in the resource-inequality branch. Alongside [[ZhejiangUniversity|浙江大学]], it represents schools with stronger spaces, projects, and front-line talent for AI-era learning, while the episode worries that students in less resourced regions may need stronger self-directed learning and AI literacy.
