# Walter (AI News Agent)

## Overview

Walter is the AI news agent used by [[AlaskaNewsCom|alaskanews.com]] to monitor public materials, select possible stories, and generate highly local news coverage.

## Current Profile

Walter combines broad source intake with automated editorial and writing steps. It reads documents, agendas, meeting records, audio, video, patent filings, and congressional materials; uses multiple AI platforms; and has generated thousands of items since launching earlier in 2026. Some sensitive stories are flagged for human review, but the source says many items publish directly. Its scale can expose leads across Alaska, while its weak discrimination between consequential and obscure material exemplifies the [[AIEditorialSalienceGap]].

## Key Characteristics

- Monitors heterogeneous public-information sources at a scale difficult for a small newsroom to match.
- Performs selection and drafting, not only transcription or search.
- Routes some sensitive material to editors while allowing substantial direct publication.
- Expands the observable field for [[LocalJournalism]] but does not reliably identify public importance or human stakes.
- Operates inside an unresolved governance context involving human review, editorial independence, and audience trust.

## Evidence

- Source intake and production: [[tech-20261022-1022-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261022-1022-mp-tech-pod-128]] describes the documents and recordings Walter reads, its multi-platform workflow, and thousands of published items.
- Editorial limits: [[tech-20261022-1022-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261022-1022-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports [[NathanielHerz]]'s view that the system processes information faster than it judges relevance, human interest, or public value.
- Human-review boundary: [[tech-20261022-1022-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261022-1022-mp-tech-pod-128]] says sensitive items may be flagged, while many generated stories publish without that step.

## Qualifications

The supplied episode is a reported discussion rather than a systematic audit of Walter's articles. It does not establish an error rate, bias rate, exact review policy, or comparative effect on local accountability reporting. The system's name refers to Walter Cronkite, but namesake symbolism is not evidence of equivalent editorial standards.

## What Changed

- Initial profile created with the distinction between monitoring scale and editorial salience.
- Recorded partial human review and the unresolved direct-publication boundary.
