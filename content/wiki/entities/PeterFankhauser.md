# Peter Fankhauser

## Overview
Peter Fankhauser is the Anybotics CEO voice in the All-In robotics special. He explains why quadruped inspection robots are a nearer commercial path than broad humanoids for many hazardous industrial sites.

## Current Profile
Fankhauser's profile in the wiki is source-scoped to this interview: he argues for task-fit form factors, autonomy plus cloud analysis, sensor-rich inspection, infrastructure-data trust, and a cautious boundary around weaponized robots. His role is to translate robotics from demos into uptime, safety, and workflow value for industrial customers.

## Key Characteristics
- He frames four-legged robots as a practical mobility answer for stairs, rough ground, balance, and facility conditions.
- He emphasizes superhuman inspection: thermal, acoustic, gas, visual, and AI sensor fusion can produce better operational insight than routine human rounds.
- He treats reliability and data usefulness as more important to customers than fascination with the robot body itself.
- He separates current inspection autonomy from harder manipulation and repair tasks that still need more reliability.
- He treats non-China sourcing, cybersecurity, and sensitive data trust as central to infrastructure robotics.
- He rejects weaponized robots in the episode while leaving broader European defense responsibility as a separate issue.
