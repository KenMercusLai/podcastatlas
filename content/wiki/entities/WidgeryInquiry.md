# Widgery Inquiry

## Overview
The Widgery Inquiry appears in the episode as the early official inquiry into [[BloodySunday]] that largely exonerated the paratroopers and was later contrasted with the [[SavilleInquiry]].

## Current Profile
The episode uses Widgery mainly as an example of contested official memory. Its protective framing helped leave nationalist distrust unresolved for decades, because the later Saville account found no support for the soldiers' claim that they had been answering armed attack. Widgery therefore matters in the wiki as part of the legitimacy problem after the killings, not simply as an administrative inquiry.

## Key Characteristics
- Early official inquiry into Bloody Sunday.
- Presented by the episode as largely exonerating the paratroopers.
- Later displaced by the Saville findings.
- Part of the nationalist grievance over state killing followed by official denial.
- Institutional memory marker for how public truth can lag behind events.

## Evidence

- Bloody Sunday context: [[700-the-troubles-bloody-sunday-part-4-glt3909829334]] says the later inquiry found no evidence for the soldiers' sniper-fire account.
