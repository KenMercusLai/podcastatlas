## Current Profile
Steinberg's wiki role is narrow and source-scoped. He appears as a tech worker, startup founder, former cybersecurity CTO, licensed bounty hunter, and organizer of strange comedic technology events. In this episode, he explicitly says he is not using [[Palantir]] data or software, but spends roughly 50 hours using publicly reachable, brokered, professional, facial, court, and leaked-data sources to build Palantir-inspired dating profiles.
