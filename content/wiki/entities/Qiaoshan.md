## Overview

桥山 / Qiaoshan is the episode pair's location for [[Huangdi|黄帝]]'s tomb, [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s sacrifice, and the question of how an immortalized ancestor can leave a grave.

## Current Profile

Qiaoshan matters here as the point where imperial movement becomes ancestral ritual and a supernatural claim meets visible material evidence. Han Wudi's sacrifice links a current ruler to Huangdi and provides the narrative bridge into [[SimaQian|司马迁]]'s historical treatment of the culture ancestor. When Wudi asks why an ascended immortal has a tomb, [[GongsunQingFangshi|公孙卿]] answers that grieving ministers buried Huangdi's clothing; the explanation lets Wudi preserve the ascent story and imagine the same posthumous treatment for himself. The supplied episodes do not provide enough geographical or archaeological evidence for a broader site profile.

## Key Characteristics

- Mountain site identified with Huangdi's tomb in the episode.
- Ritual stop on Han Wudi's 110 BCE tour.
- Bridge between imperial travel, ancestral memory, and legitimacy narrative.
- Site where a clothing-burial explanation reconciles a visible tomb with Huangdi's reported immortality.

## Qualifications

This page records only the two episodes' use of Qiaoshan. It does not independently verify the tomb identification, exact ancient route, archaeological status, relationship to other places bearing the same name, Huangdi's ascent, or the grave's contents.
