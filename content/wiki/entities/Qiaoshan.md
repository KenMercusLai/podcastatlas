## Overview

桥山 / Qiaoshan is the episode's location for [[Huangdi|黄帝]]'s tomb and [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s sacrifice during the 110 BCE imperial tour.

## Current Profile

Qiaoshan matters here as the point where imperial movement becomes ancestral ritual. Han Wudi's sacrifice links a current ruler to Huangdi and provides the narrative bridge into [[SimaQian|司马迁]]'s historical treatment of the culture ancestor. The supplied episode does not provide enough geographical or archaeological evidence for a broader site profile.

## Key Characteristics

- Mountain site identified with Huangdi's tomb in the episode.
- Ritual stop on Han Wudi's 110 BCE tour.
- Bridge between imperial travel, ancestral memory, and legitimacy narrative.
