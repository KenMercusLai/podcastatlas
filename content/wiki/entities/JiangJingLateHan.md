## Overview
江京 / Jiang Jing now appears as a palace actor whose current profile begins with anti-Deng slander before moving into post-Deng reward. ## Current Profile
Hanji 794 makes Jiang Jing part of the slander-and-security layer before Deng's death. The source does not give his independent motive, but it places him in the palace circle that turns An's fear of being deposed into attacks on Deng. This makes Jiang more than a later beneficiary; he is already present when succession anxiety is being shaped inside the palace.

Hanji 795 then frames Jiang as a service-to-access beneficiary. His role in escorting Emperor An from Qinghe to the throne is converted into enfeoffment as Duxiang Hou, promotion to Zhongchangshi, and appointment as Da Changqiu, placing him close to imperial household channels just as the Deng regency network collapses.

Jiang Jing's importance remains structural. The sources establish slander, access, reward, and network position more clearly than a detailed independent policy agenda. He belongs beside Li Run, Wang Sheng, Bo Rong, and Fan Feng as part of the early An-era field in which palace proximity becomes public rank and political leverage.

## Key Characteristics
- Palace-intimate actor named in anti-Deng slander before Empress Dowager Deng's death.
- Accession-service beneficiary rewarded for helping bring Emperor An from Qinghe.
- Enfeoffed as Duxiang Hou and promoted to Zhongchangshi after the Deng family's fall.
- Also identified as Da Changqiu, giving him a powerful household-office position near palace administration.
- Paired with Li Run and grouped with Fan Feng, Wang Sheng, and Bo Rong in a wider post-Deng network.
- Evidence currently shows access and network role more than a detailed personal policy agenda.

## Qualifications
Jiang Jing's current profile is source-bounded. Hanji 794 establishes his place in anti-Deng slander but not the exact wording, initiative, or personal motive; Hanji 795 establishes reward and access but not a complete later career or precise faction leadership.
