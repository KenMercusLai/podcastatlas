# 扬州 / Yangzhou (late Han province)

扬州 / Yangzhou (late Han province) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-969-yuanshi-xiongdi-neidou-shui-neng-jianlou-lsgj6sewo35jpsgfm-koivijtr6r|Hanji 969]] as the province whose office and territory expose the gap between appointment and control. After [[ChenWenLateHan|陈温]] dies, [[YuanShao|袁绍]] sends [[YuanYiLateHan|袁遗]] to lead Yangzhou, but [[YuanShu|袁术]] defeats him. Yuan Shu then uses [[ChenYuWuCommandery|陈瑀]] in the Yangzhou office field, while later fighting his way toward [[Shouchun|寿春]] after [[CaoCao|曹操]] drives him south.

The source also makes Yangzhou a strategic-base problem, not only an office title. The host compares Yangzhou with [[Jingzhou|荆州]] and [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] as the three Yangtze-line provinces and treats Yangzhou as the richest eventual base: the eastern commanderies, including [[DanyangCommandery|丹阳郡]], [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]], and [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]], become the later foundation for [[SunCe|孙策]] and the Jiangdong/Sun-family branch.

In this page, Yangzhou is source-scoped to late-Han province politics. It should not be collapsed into later city-focused Yangzhou uses; the episode's point is that formal provincial title, river geography, Huai-River access, and local commandery control split apart during the Yuan-family struggle.
