# 黻服 / Fu Robe

黻服 / Fu robe appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-11-shijie-fengqi-yunyong-1-lt60b2gddiosf5kade35uopwohi5]] as the high-grade ritual garment [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]] gives to [[QinXianGong|秦献公]] after Qin's victory at [[ShimenBattle|石门之战]].

The episode uses the robe to explain how clothing could carry political meaning in the pre-Qin order. The host says upper garments and lower skirts bore symbolic patterns later grouped as [[TwelveInsigniaPoliticalSymbolism|十二华章]], and explains 黻 as a pattern of two opposed bows.

Its wiki role is not fashion history alone. The robe makes a military victory legible through Zhou ritual language: Qin's conquest receives a form of [[RitualRecognitionOfMilitaryPower|recognition]] from the still-symbolic [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]].
