# 李寄斩蛇 / Li Ji Slays the Snake

李寄斩蛇 / Li Ji Slays the Snake is the action story in [[06-soushenji-ershisi-xiao-zhe-kou-guo-ganbao-bu-bei-536821044]] where Li Ji volunteers to face a giant snake that has been consuming sacrificed girls. She brings a dog, sword, and bait, kills the snake, and sees the remains of earlier victims.

The episode uses the tale to pull [[SouShenJi|《搜神记》]] away from a purely obedient or moralizing image. Li Ji's courage exposes the social failure around her: the village and authorities have accepted sacrifice as routine, while one girl treats the monster as something that can be fought. That makes the story a classical strange-tale extension of [[OrdinaryPeopleResistance]] and [[FemaleSelfPossession]].
