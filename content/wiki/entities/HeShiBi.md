# 和氏璧 / He Shi Bi

和氏璧 / He Shi Bi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-74-huanyuan-wanbi-guizhao-zhenxiang-lg1irvbbc1wp8oph2kmpatfoxebk]] as the treasure at the center of the "完璧归赵" story. The episode first traces it to [[BianHe|卞和]] in [[ChuState|楚国]], where raw jade hidden in stone is misidentified until the stone is cut open.

The jade then becomes a political object. The source says it later reaches [[ZhaoState|赵国]], appears in [[Handan|邯郸]], is bought by [[MiuXian|缪贤]], and is forced into [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]]'s possession. Once [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] offers fifteen cities for it, the object becomes a test of interstate credibility rather than only royal desire.

In the episode, [[LinXiangru|蔺相如]] preserves the jade by sending it back to Zhao while remaining in Qin himself. That makes He Shi Bi the material core of [[WanbiGuizhaoDiplomacy|完璧归赵式外交]]: the object is protected, Qin's failure to supply the promised cities becomes visible, and the envoy carries the personal danger left by the exchange.
