# Charles D. Ellis / 查尔斯·埃利斯

## Overview
[[CharlesEllis|Charles D. Ellis / 查尔斯·埃利斯]] is the author of the Capital Group history discussed in [[lossad1trayjdp11pbjiywrxy43o|the episode]] and newly translated into Chinese by [[FanJie|番杰]] as 《长线至胜》.

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents Ellis as a long-term observer of [[CapitalGroup|Capital Group]] who built the book from interviews and documentary research. The episode also attributes to him a strong expectation that indexation will continue, with active opportunities potentially becoming more concentrated among the smaller number of investors still willing and able to perform price discovery.

## Key Characteristics
- Wrote an institutional history from long-term proximity, interviews, and documentary research.
- Uses Capital Group to examine how investment skill can become organizational rather than remain attached to one star.
- Frames value discovery partly as clarifying what money is for and how utility is defined.
- Is represented as expecting continued indexation while retaining a conditional role for active price discovery.
