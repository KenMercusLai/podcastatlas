# 周昕 / Zhou Xin (late Han)

周昕 / Zhou Xin (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] as the figure whose alignment helps explain why [[YuanShu|袁术]] pushes [[WuJingLateHan|吴景]] and [[SunBenLateHan|孙贲]] into [[DanyangCommandery|丹阳郡]]. The source presents Zhou Xin as leaning toward [[YuanShao|袁绍]], while Yuan Shu and Yuan Shao are hostile.

The page is source-scoped. Zhou Xin is not developed as a biography here; he represents the factional background that turns a commandery appointment into part of the Yuan brothers' wider competition.
