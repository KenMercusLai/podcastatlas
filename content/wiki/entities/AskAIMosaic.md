# Ask AI (Mosaic predecessor)

## Overview

Ask AI was [[AlanTalmor]]'s general-purpose enterprise assistant and the predecessor to [[MosaicAI]]. The semantic suffix distinguishes it from other products that may use the generic name “Ask AI.”

## Current Profile

The product connected enterprise systems, modeled company terminology and information, and offered a conversational assistant across that context. It reportedly approached $1 million in ARR, but ChatGPT's arrival led the team to conclude that general enterprise assistants would become harder to defend, prompting gradual specialization in technical support.

## Key Characteristics

- Connected many enterprise data sources into a company-specific context layer.
- Served customer-facing and support teams before the technical-support specialization.
- Reached meaningful early commercial traction without establishing a durable generic-assistant position.
- Supplied connectors, context, customers, and workflow experience that made an incremental pivot possible.

## Evidence

Product and traction:
- [[chatgpt-should-have-killed-his-ai-saas-instead-hes-nearing-10m]] reports roughly 100 connectors, customers including monday.com, and ARR approaching $1 million.

Strategic transition:
- [[chatgpt-should-have-killed-his-ai-saas-instead-hes-nearing-10m]] says the team progressively added support-specific tools before rebranding and ending investment in the general assistant.

## Qualifications

Connector count, customer relationships, and ARR are founder-reported. The source does not establish that ChatGPT alone caused the pivot or that the predecessor could not have remained viable; it records the team's strategic judgment under changing platform conditions.
