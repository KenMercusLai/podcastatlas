## Overview

杜冶芬 / Du Yefen was [[JinYong|金庸]]'s first wife, whose short marriage and disputed separation survive mainly through later recollection rather than a balanced documentary record.

## Current Profile

The episode places their meeting around 1947, after Jin Yong befriended her brother 杜冶秋 through a newspaper advice column and began visiting the family. It describes mutual courtship, marriage, and an initially happy period that continued after the couple moved to Hong Kong.

Their marriage ended around 1953. Jin Yong later said Du betrayed him and dated another person, while her brother described a relationship in which love may already have been insufficient. Because Du's own account is absent, “betrayal” cannot establish the conduct, timing, or condition of the marriage by itself. The episode likewise treats attempts to identify her directly inside Jin Yong's fiction as suggestive but unproven.

## Key Characteristics

- First wife of Jin Yong and participant in his early Hong Kong years.
- Entered the relationship through a family and newspaper-social connection.
- Became the subject of a later one-sided “betrayal” narrative.
- Remains difficult to reconstruct because her own testimony is not represented.

## Qualifications

The current profile derives from one podcast's synthesis of biographies, interviews, and family recollection. Du Yefen's own account is not supplied, dates are approximate, and the meaning of “betrayal” is unresolved. The proposed origin of Jin Yong's pen name “林欢” is attributed to her brother and was not confirmed by Jin Yong.
