# Olivia Carville

Olivia Carville is the [[BloombergBusinessweek]] reporter interviewed in [[tech-20260812-0812-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260812-0812-mp-tech-pod-128]] about [[TikTok]]'s reported exclusion of about 15 million U.S. users from a 2021 filter-bubble safety update. Her reporting brings internal platform documents into the wiki's child-safety and product-liability branch.

The episode uses Carville's account to connect [[ChaseNasca]], [[PlatformSafetyABTesting]], [[InternalSafetyResearchExposure]], and [[SocialMediaProductLiability]]. Her most important contribution to the wiki is not a general claim about all algorithms, but the source-specific evidence that a TikTok safeguard did not apply to one vulnerable user's account "by design."
