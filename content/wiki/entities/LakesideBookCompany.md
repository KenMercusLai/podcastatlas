# Lakeside Book Company

Lakeside Book Company appears in [[our-book-vs-the-global-supply-chain]] as the domestic printer that manufactures the [[PlanetMoneyBook]] at its Crawfordsville, Indiana plant. The episode uses the plant to show [[BookManufacturingSupplyChain]] in physical sequence: digital files, aluminum plates, CMYK printing, folded signatures, stacking, gluing, binding, sealing, boxing, and quality control.

The company matters in the source because it makes [[DomesticBookPrintingFlexibility]] concrete. Printing in the [[UnitedStates|United States]] costs more than some overseas options, but it reduces transit uncertainty, lets [[JuliaDruskin]] inspect the press run directly, and gives [[WWNorton|W. W. Norton]] faster reprint capacity if demand exceeds the first run.

## Key Claims
- Lakeside's plant is presented as a high-volume bookmaking facility rather than a boutique printer.
- The episode's press-floor details ground [[PrintingSignatureConstraint]]: pages are imposed into multi-page sections, then folded and bound as a physical object.
- Domestic printing became more attractive after overseas compliance and schedule risks rose and expected demand increased.
- The Lakeside example extends [[BookPrintRunStrategy]] because printer location affects not only cost but also stockout recovery time.
