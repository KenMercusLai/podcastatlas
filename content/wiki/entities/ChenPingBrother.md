# 陈平兄 / Elder Brother of Chen Ping

陈平兄 / Elder brother of Chen Ping is the unnamed brother whose household support helps explain [[ChenPing|陈平]]'s early formation. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-150-chenping-weihe-bei-cheng-zhiwei-chenggong-zhengzhijia-yangban-1-llale6fron0tkye7n0e-aanojqar|Hanji 150]] says he absorbs farm labor and lets Chen Ping read, socialize, and develop the local ties that later become [[LocalSocialCapitalAccumulation|乡里社会资本积累]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-154-diangu-chenping-daosao-lingyou-yinqing-li-c6ezwfpejcvbrpzojg-xv2ge8|Hanji 154]] makes him important as evidence in the "陈平盗嫂" discussion. The source cites [[Shiji|《史记》]]: when [[ChenPingSisterInLaw|his wife]] complains that Chen Ping is not productive, he expels and divorces her. The host treats this as a family-trust signal that weakens the later accusation that Chen Ping had an illicit relationship with his sister-in-law.

His page is kept source-scoped because he is unnamed and appears only through Chen Ping's biography. The significance is structural: his support gives Chen Ping the room to become socially useful, and his later action turns family evidence into part of [[MoralSlanderReframing|道德诬陷焦点转移]].
