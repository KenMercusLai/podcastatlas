# China Construction Bank / 建设银行

China Construction Bank / 建设银行 appears in [[136-yinhang-licai-hai-neng-zenme-mai-llc7n0f3g2-jrz4xkoefz1nui5lt]] as one of the first two banks approved by [[ChinaBankingAndInsuranceRegulatoryCommission|银保监会]] on 2018-12-26 to establish a bank wealth-management subsidiary. The source also says its wealth-management subsidiary became the first to open in 2019.

The episode does not evaluate China Construction Bank as an equity investment. It uses the bank as an institutional milestone in the move from old [[ChineseBankWealthManagement|bank wealth-management]] departments and implicit guarantees toward separately incorporated [[BankWealthManagementSubsidiary|wealth-management subsidiaries]].

## Source Position
- CCB is a regulatory-transition example rather than a product recommendation.
- Its subsidiary launch marks the formal start of the post-资管新规 bank wealth-management subsidiary era.
- The case helps show how state-bank scale and regulation shaped the new license system.
