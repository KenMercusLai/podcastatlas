# 芈完 / 楚考烈王 / Mi Wan

芈完 / Mi Wan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-92-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhuming-tiangou-shi-shui-1-lmwf0q0-3hxb6d4hro8fdmc3zdxp]] as the [[ChuState|楚国]] crown prince kept in [[QinState|秦国]] as hostage after the Qin-Chu repair arranged in the previous Huang Xie branch. The source presents him as the son and heir of [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]], with [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] having accompanied him in Qin for nine years.

His importance in the episode is succession leverage. When Mi Heng becomes gravely ill, Mi Wan's status changes from ordinary diplomatic collateral into the person who may determine whether the Chu throne remains on the current crown-prince line. Huang Xie therefore argues to [[FanJu|范雎]] that letting Mi Wan return would produce a grateful pro-Qin Chu ruler, while keeping him could make him worthless if another prince succeeds.

The first Zhouji 92 part stops before the outcome. It records Qin's suspicion that Chu may be using the illness as a pretext, Qin's plan to send Mi Wan's teacher back to verify the situation, and Huang Xie's counterplan: Mi Wan should disguise himself as a Chu envoy's driver and flee, while Huang Xie stays behind in Xianyang and pretends the prince is ill.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-92-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhuming-tiangou-shi-shui-2-lt1nwraiuuvpy2tzo-zwaoqsajzx]] completes the succession arc. Mi Wan reaches Chu, Mi Heng dies three months later, and Mi Wan succeeds as Chu Kaolie Wang. His first important wiki action is to reward [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] with the chancellorship, a large fief, and the Chunshenjun title, confirming that the hostage-court companionship has become governing trust.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-1-ljshdryvf9qgabtn-siontthzsok]] uses his newly established reign as the transition into 262 BCE. The episode says Chu Kaolie Wang's first year brings no battle but still sees [[ChuState|楚国]] cede large territory to [[QinState|秦国]], so Mi Wan's successful return does not reverse Chu's broader decline under Qin pressure.
