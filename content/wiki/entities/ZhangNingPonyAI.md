# 张宁 / Zhang Ning (Pony.ai)

Zhang Ning is the [[PonyAI|Pony.ai]] speaker in [[acc532947b65-acc532947b65]]. He explains Pony.ai's Robotaxi business as part of a broader unmanned-transportation portfolio that also includes logistics, port trucks, bulk-commodity trucking, last-mile delivery, and compute-platform work.

His strongest contribution is the operating definition of Robotaxi. In Zhang's account, L4 is not just a more advanced L2 feature set; it means the system owns the driving task and the operator must absorb the service responsibilities formerly handled by drivers. That links him to [[AutonomousDrivingResponsibilityBoundary]], [[RobotaxiFleetOperations]], and [[RobotaxiEconomics]].

The episode also uses Zhang to connect [[WorldModels]] and [[AutonomousDrivingSimulation]] to real driving. He argues that L4 has to reason about what other road users may do after the vehicle acts, so useful simulation cannot be only replay; it must support counterfactual world evolution and continuous Sim2Real improvement.
