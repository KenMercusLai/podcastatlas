# Abu Dhabi United Group

## Overview
Abu Dhabi United Group is the ownership group that took over [[ManchesterCity]] in 2008 and financed the club's transformation into a dominant English football team.

## Current Profile
In the bounded evidence, the group represents foreign owner capital operating inside revenue-linked football regulation. Its majority owner, Mansour bin Zayed Al Nahyan, is described as a senior United Arab Emirates political figure, which gives the ownership both commercial and state-linked significance.

The source alleges that inflated sponsorship agreements allowed more owner-backed spending than [[PremierLeague]] rules permitted. That claim remains contested by Manchester City and subject to appeal.

## Key Characteristics
- Owner of [[ManchesterCity]] since the 2008 takeover described by the source.
- Investment vehicle associated with rapid player recruitment and sporting success.
- Majority-owned by Mansour bin Zayed Al Nahyan, a senior UAE political figure.
- Central actor in a disputed boundary between sponsorship revenue and direct owner funding.
