# 上官金虹（古龙人物） / Shangguan Jinhong (Gu Long character)

## Overview

上官金虹 is the second-ranked master on [[WeaponsRankingGuLong|兵器谱]] and a central antagonist in [[SentimentalSwordsmanRuthlessSword|《多情剑客无情剑》]]. The bounded episode presents him as disciplined, strategically patient, power-oriented, and ultimately vulnerable to the symbolic prestige he claims not to need.

## Current Profile

He continues training while 天机老人 ages under the burden of first place, eventually defeating the nominal leader and becoming the practical strongest figure. His “手中无环，心中有环” exchange with [[LiXunhuan|李寻欢]] turns combat into psychological and philosophical positioning rather than an inventory of moves.

His death exposes the limit of his self-image. After reaching the summit, he deliberately permits Li Xunhuan an opening because catching or surviving 小李飞刀 would destroy its legend. The hosts disagree over whether this contradicts his caution or reveals the pride produced by success; either way, he loses through a chosen symbolic contest rather than simple technical inferiority.

## Key Characteristics

- Joins extreme self-discipline to expansionist ambition and organizational power.
- Treats wealth, pleasure, and relationships primarily as instruments.
- Improves beyond his published rank while an older rival declines.
- Uses psychological presence and restraint as part of martial dominance.
- Retains attachment to his son 上官飞 despite a generally instrumental orientation.
- Abandons [[JingWumingGuLong|荆无命]] after disability, exposing the conditionality of their bond.
- Becomes vulnerable to the prestige of defeating an apparently infallible technique.
