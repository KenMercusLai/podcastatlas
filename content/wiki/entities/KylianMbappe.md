# Kylian Mbappe / 姆巴佩

## Overview
Kylian Mbappe is a football star whose sponsorship shift is used in the bounded sources to distinguish an unconfirmed Li-Ning rumor from a later reported long-term agreement with [[OnRunning|On Running]].

## Current Profile
The earlier source records [[LiNing|Li-Ning]] denying that it had signed or planned cooperation with Mbappe. The later sources report that Mbappe left a long [[Nike]] relationship for an approximately ten-year agreement with On that may include equity and performance bonuses, plus participation in football-product development and testing. In this evidence set, he moves from sponsorship speculation to the anchor of On's first football expansion, while also illustrating why celebrity reach is an input rather than proof of product adoption.

## Key Characteristics
- Elite footballer with enough visibility to move sportswear sponsorship expectations before confirmation.
- Maintained a long reported relationship with Nike before the On agreement.
- Was linked by rumor to Li-Ning, which the company denied.
- Is reported to have signed a ten-year partnership with On Running.
- Is expected to participate in football product development and testing, not only promotion.
- Gives On global reach, but cannot by himself establish product quality, football distribution, or sales conversion.

## Evidence
- Rumor and denial: [[xingbake-huiying-mixue-bingcheng-daigong-deng-chuanwen-li-ning-fouren-yu-mubapei-qianyue-1006054195]] records Li-Ning's denial and treats Nike-relationship uncertainty as a reason the rumor seemed plausible.
- Reported new sponsor: [[canban-anaya-bianshen-remen-jingdian-yidu-xiaoshou-nan-de-dichan-xiangmu-ruhe-baituo-kunjing-1017012362]] reports the end of the Nike relationship and a ten-year On agreement tied to future football products.
- Partnership depth and limit: [[angpao-qianxia-mubapei-zhihou-paoxie-pinpai-zuo-zuqiu-shengyi-haiyou-naxie-nanti-1018738205]] reports equity and performance-linked incentives, development and testing work, and global ambassador duties, while stressing that attention may not become boot demand.

## Qualifications
The agreement terms, equity share, bonuses, product role, and 2027 launch schedule remain source-relayed rather than independently verified here. One athlete's visibility cannot establish a new brand's product credibility or commercial return, and the earlier Li-Ning denial is not contradicted by a later deal with a different company.

## What Changed
- Added the reported equity and performance-linked incentive structure.
- Clarified that Mbappe's development role and attention support entry but do not guarantee football-shoe adoption.
