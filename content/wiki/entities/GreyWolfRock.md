# 大灰狼罗克 / Grey Wolf Rock

大灰狼罗克 / Grey Wolf Rock is a [[ZhengYuanjie|郑渊洁]] character discussed in [[23-feima-pai-qiche-anquan-shi-di-yi-wei-de-572699943]] as a favorite of [[Beimin|北明]]. The episode uses him to show Zheng's willingness to make a traditionally "bad" animal into the protagonist and then move that protagonist through ordinary adult social roles.

The source says the Rock stories became increasingly realist, including jobs, licenses, popular language, and Zheng's own social experience. In the wiki, this supports [[ChildrensLiteratureComplexity]] and the broader claim that children's stories can carry adult social observation without giving up play.
