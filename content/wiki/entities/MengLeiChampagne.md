# 孟磊 / Meng Lei (Champagne practitioner)

## Overview

孟磊 / Meng Lei is a Champagne practitioner and China-France cultural intermediary whose [[FuyouTiandi|蜉蝣天地]] interview connects wine enjoyment, professional education, origin governance, long-term producer trust, and a winery partnership in Cizhong, [[Yunnan|云南]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Meng as someone who entered an unfashionable field from personal interest, then added sales capacity because cultural work without a route to market remained constrained. His Champagne approach separates consumer welcome from professional rigor: ordinary drinkers may begin with happiness, balance, and familiar memories such as mung-bean cake or hawthorn, while sommeliers and trade workers need disciplined knowledge of grapes, production, vintages, cuisine, and service.

His cross-cultural method tries to retain a French producer's character while finding genuine Chinese points of recognition. The same long horizon appears in his reported winery partnership in Cizhong village, Deqin, where agricultural potential is inseparable from remote roads, fragmented parcels, high altitude, weather, and multi-year production decisions.

## Key Characteristics

- Treats Champagne first as pleasure and human connection rather than a status examination.
- Defends professional rigor for the trade while rejecting condescension toward consumers.
- Uses Chinese sensory memories to make flavor language locally intelligible.
- Prefers patient art, lifestyle, and cultural exchange over celebrity-led volume growth.
- Connects origin, regulation, brand trust, and family reputation to long-term value.
- Participates in a Cizhong/Deqin wine project and hopes eventually to build a Champagne brand through aligned grower partnerships.
