# 范鑫宇 / Fan Xinyu

范鑫宇 / Fan Xinyu is the [[ChangjiangBusinessSchool|长江商学院]] observer cited in [[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw]] to explain [[FactorySecondGeneration|厂二代]] succession patterns. In the episode, [[LiXiaojie|李小杰]] draws on his work with programs such as [[JieliChangjiang|接力长江]], 基业长青, MBA, and EMBA to frame how second-generation family-business successors learn.

His key contribution is the phrase [[TacitManagementKnowledge|默会知识]]. He argues that founders often go to business school to systematize past experience, while second-generation successors already have formal education but need practice-based wisdom: how to judge people, manage relations, read situations, and turn knowledge into operating decisions.

## Source Position
- Fan Xinyu separates founder and successor learning needs: one generation codifies experience, the next lacks lived operating friction.
- He identifies new business, digitalization/intelligentization, and foreign trade or overseas expansion as easier entry paths for successors.
- He frames the founder's relation to the company as almost parental, while successors may treat the company more like a job or vehicle for self-realization.
