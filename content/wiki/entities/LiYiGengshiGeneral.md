## Overview

李轶 / Li Yi is a Gengshi-side figure in the Guangwu accession sequence whose current wiki profile now begins with [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-665-ta-busi-liuxiu-ye-nandang-huangdi-liauc3k3omnzqu09dqicxifjxy1t|Hanji 665]] and continues through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-676-hanguangwudi-liuxiu-jiedao-sharen-shijian-shimo-luebqlivstmkjaqqfdlcukxtnmii|Hanji 676]]. Hanji 665 makes him a court realignment actor and adviser for Liu Yan's killing; Hanji 676 then shows how that old exposure makes his later Feng Yi correspondence dangerous. The sources call him 李轶/五阳王李毅 in transcript-sensitive forms; the wiki normalizes him as 李轶 in the Liu Xiu-Gengshi conflict and keeps him distinct from [[LiYiEasternHan|李邑 / Li Yi]], the later Western Regions envoy.

## Current Profile

Hanji 665 supplies Li Yi's earliest current exposure layer. The source says Li Yi had previously been close to Liu Yan and Liu Xiu, but after Liu Xuan becomes emperor he moves toward the New Market and Pinglin power circle. He joins Zhu Wei in warning Liu Xuan that Liu Yan must be bound and killed quickly, making him part of the court mechanism that turns Liu Yan's restoration merit into a death sentence.

Hanji 676 presents Li Yi as exposed because he had participated with [[ZhuWeiEasternHan|朱鲔]] in the killing of Liu Xiu's elder brother. When [[FengYiEasternHan|冯异]] writes to him and analyzes the collapsing Gengshi field, Li Yi answers with pro-Han-restoration language and begins corresponding with Feng Yi rather than fighting him.

That ambiguous cooperation helps Feng Yi expand, but it does not make Li Yi safe. Liu Xiu judges him crafty, circulates Li Yi's letters to commandery officials, and turns the correspondence into a warning signal. Once Zhu Wei hears of the letters, he suspects Li Yi and sends assassins against him, producing fear in [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] and pushing more people toward surrender.

Li Yi therefore functions as a pivotal but unstable intermediary. His private letter channel is useful to Feng Yi, threatening to Zhu Wei, and politically usable by Liu Xiu. The page treats him as a source-scoped Gengshi general rather than a full Eastern Han official biography.

## Key Characteristics

- Gengshi-aligned figure whose past role in Liu Yan's death makes reconciliation with Liu Xiu insecure.
- Court realignment actor who shifts from Liu Yan/Liu Xiu proximity toward the New Market and Pinglin Gengshi center.
- Wavering correspondence partner who stops fighting Feng Yi and gives Liu Xiu's side operational space.
- Author of letters that become political evidence after Liu Xiu circulates them outside their original channel.
- Suspicion target whose exposure destabilizes Zhu Wei's Luoyang camp.
- Source-scoped identity kept separate from the later Western Regions Li Yi / 李邑.

## Qualifications

The current page is bounded to two podcast source notes and should not reconstruct Li Yi's full biography, title history, or exact death record. Hanji 665 supports the court-realignment and Liu Yan killing-advice layer, but the exact mechanics of New Market/Pinglin affiliation and Liu Xuan's deliberation remain source-scoped. The transcript form 李毅 and title 五阳王 are treated as ASR-sensitive; the Guangwu-Gengshi context supports normalization to 李轶, while [[LiYiEasternHan|李邑 / Li Yi]] remains a distinct later figure.
