# Templafy

Templafy is an enterprise document automation company discussed in [[enterprise-sales-with-no-product-landing-a-big-four-customer]]. [[ChristianLund]] presents it as a cloud-era rebuild from the older [[Omnidocs]] document-workflow business, created because enterprise technology buying and usage expectations shifted around 2012-2014.

The episode's Templafy case is less about a new category name than about enterprise selling discipline. The company sold early through [[FounderLedSales]], thought leadership, domain expertise, and targeted access to sophisticated buyers before the product was mature, then used demanding enterprise implementations to harden the product.

Templafy also becomes an AI-era reset case. Lund says the company later spent roughly two and a half years redoing its technology foundation for AI, but initially communicated too far ahead of customer readiness. That puts the company at the intersection of [[TechnologyResetRebuild]], [[SaaSTrustMoat]], [[AIGovernanceAndCompliance]], and [[CategoryCreation]].
