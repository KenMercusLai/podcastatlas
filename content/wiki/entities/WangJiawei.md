# 王家伟 / Wang Jiawei

## Overview
[[WangJiawei|王家伟]] is the chief scientist of [[ShenpuIntelligence|深普智能]] and the guest of one [[ShizilukouCrossing]] interview recorded as he was finishing his PhD. The episode traces his route from a USTC youth-class program and a joint doctoral program with [[MicrosoftResearchAsia]], through document-intelligence research and internships at [[DeepSeek]] and ByteDance Seed, into a full-time embodied-intelligence role.

## Current Profile
The source presents him as 24 years old at recording, turning 25 the following month, and as a first-job researcher who chose an unconverged field over a stable one. He describes his doctoral work as document intelligence, including OCR, layout analysis, and form understanding, with algorithms applied inside Microsoft APIs and infrastructure. At [[DeepSeek]] he worked on multimodal models, agentic RL, and commercialization between V2 and R1; at ByteDance Seed he worked on agents inside a large, finely divided organization. In the episode he frames his own management style around asking good questions, naming his weaknesses, sharing context, and giving a research team autonomy rather than long hours.

## Key Characteristics
- Youth-class background: a cohort of roughly 30-plus students selected through a gaokao threshold plus same-day mathematics and physics exams and a one-on-one interview, with generalist coursework before specialization and early research participation.
- He defines being "smart" less by raw speed than by asking good questions and knowing and confronting one's own gaps; he names hardware, soldering, and circuit building as his weaknesses and says he asks the hardware team for help.
- He links his document-intelligence PhD to deployment, because the work reached Microsoft APIs and infrastructure, which shaped his preference for research that can serve downstream applications.
- His [[DeepSeek]] internship left an impression of clear project planning, access to low-level and inference code, and strong output without extreme hours, while his ByteDance Seed internship showed a large, finely specialized organization with ready-made data and training infrastructure that was harder to see beyond.
- He chose embodied intelligence over continuing in large language models because the language-model field had stabilized enough that a newcomer mostly executes existing plans, while embodied work is still open enough to define one's own problem; he names time, not money, as the main risk and says he gave up salary and compute access for a preference for small teams, high autonomy, and problem-definition ownership.
- His stated three-year goal is a home robot that removes the roughly 20-minute plate-scraping chore before dishes go into a dishwasher, which he puts at about an 80% probability.
- He treats research output as nonlinear in hours and emphasizes problem definition, context sharing, and AI tool leverage, with [[Codex]] used widely at the company.
