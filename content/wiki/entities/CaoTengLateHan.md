## Overview
曹腾 / Cao Teng is tracked as a senior eunuch power broker, a participant in the 146 CE succession choice, the adoptive father of [[CaoSong|曹嵩]], and the ancestor whose court position gives [[CaoCao|曹操]] both early access and a lasting reputation problem.

## Current Profile
After [[EmperorZhiOfHan|汉质帝]]'s death, Cao Teng opposes [[LiuSuanLateHan|刘蒜]] first through a personal grievance over failed deference and then through a strategic argument: Liu Suan's strictness would threaten [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]]'s clients and eunuch fortunes. Cao Teng therefore supports Liu Zhi, the future [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]], as the safer accession choice for the incumbent bloc.

Later sources shift from Cao Teng's active court role to his family afterlife. His adoption of Cao Song places Cao Cao inside a powerful palace-connected lineage, enabling an unusually early official start. The same connection exposes Cao Cao to elite suspicion and helps explain why visible enforcement against [[JianShuoLateHan|蹇硕]]'s uncle can function as reputation recoding.

## Key Characteristics
- Senior eunuch whose palace access carries succession influence.
- Personal-grievance actor whose opposition to Liu Suan begins with perceived disrespect.
- Factional-security strategist who evaluates a ruler through risk to incumbent interests.
- Enabler of Liu Zhi's accession as Emperor Huan in the current evidence set.
- Adoptive father of Cao Song and source of Cao Cao's early access.
- Ancestral figure whose eunuch status becomes a later elite stigma as well as an advantage.

## Evidence
- Personal grievance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-827-yi-ju-hua-yinfa-de-diwang-zhisi-lnf6rz1i2vmgsclgiejbh0qevzc2|Hanji 827]] says Cao Teng rejects Liu Suan after an earlier failure of deference.
- Succession self-protection: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-828-da-jiangjun-liangji-shanquan-feili-de-donghan-jianchen-llqnqijagtmdlgitulddxspncu0e|Hanji 828]] records his warning that Liu Suan would threaten Liang Ji's circle and eunuch wealth.
- Family access and stigma: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-904-juezhao-neiguan-huode-xinling-da-ziyou-de-mijue-lopn-okbzr4ljvqmgzcsb7qav0n2|Hanji 904]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-926-zhenzheng-de-qiangzhe-dou-dongde-zaoshi-lssmabzoku4ue28irdmfn0-d-0dc|Hanji 926]] connect Cao Teng to Cao Cao's entry and reputation problem.
- Early-career consolidation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1103-caocao-liangci-hui-laojia-zhen-shi-yinwei-guanchang-shiyi-ma-lq_e-te21mwhczyomeyxcem1brkz|Hanji 1103]] identifies Cao Teng's office and political standing as the family resource behind Cao Cao's unusually early access.

## Qualifications
The current profile is not a complete biography. The succession episodes develop Cao Teng's grievance and factional calculation, while the Cao-family episodes use him chiefly to explain later access and stigma. Hanji 1103's compressed office descriptions and broad claims about family advantage remain source-scoped where they exceed the narrower evidence.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1103 as a consolidating source for Cao Teng's role in Cao Cao's early access.
- Clarified that the same inherited connection operates as both political capital and reputational liability.
