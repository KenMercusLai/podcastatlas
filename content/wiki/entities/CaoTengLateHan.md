## Overview
曹腾 / Cao Teng is now tracked in two bounded roles: first as the eunuch adviser who helps [[LiangJiLateHan]] choose [[EmperorHuanOfHan]] in the 146 CE succession crisis, and later as the eunuch-line ancestor whose adoption of [[CaoSong]] shapes [[CaoCao]]'s inherited reputation problem. The profile therefore connects palace access, succession self-protection, and later elite stigma around eunuch family background.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-828-da-jiangjun-liangji-shanquan-feili-de-donghan-jianchen-llqnqijagtmdlgitulddxspncu0e|Hanji 828]] gives Cao Teng his earliest current wiki role. After [[EmperorZhiOfHan]] dies, Cao Teng warns Liang Ji that [[LiuSuanLateHan]] is strict and clear enough to threaten Liang Ji's guests and eunuch fortunes; he advises installing Liwu Hou Liu Zhi instead, because that choice would better preserve the incumbent bloc's wealth and status.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-904-juezhao-neiguan-huode-xinling-da-ziyou-de-mijue-lopn-okbzr4ljvqmgzcsb7qav0n2|Hanji 904]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-926-zhenzheng-de-qiangzhe-dou-dongde-zaoshi-lssmabzoku4ue28irdmfn0-d-0dc|Hanji 926]] supply the later Cao-family memory layer. They present Cao Teng as the adoptive father of Cao Song, making him the eunuch-connected ancestor through whom Cao Cao's early reputation is judged and partially recoded by later anti-eunuch signaling.

## Key Characteristics
- Eunuch power broker whose palace position gives him decisive advisory access during a succession vacuum.
- Self-preservation strategist who frames ruler choice around the security of Liang Ji's clients and eunuch fortunes.
- Enabler of Liu Zhi's accession as Emperor Huan in the current evidence base.
- Adoptive father of Cao Song and ancestral link in Cao Cao's eunuch-family reputation problem.
- Figure whose legacy shows how eunuch status can matter both as immediate court power and as later elite stigma.

- Eunuch interest alignment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-828-da-jiangjun-liangji-shanquan-feili-de-donghan-jianchen-llqnqijagtmdlgitulddxspncu0e|Hanji 828]] explicitly links Cao Teng's argument to the eunuch bloc's desire to preserve wealth and status.
- Cao family genealogy: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-926-zhenzheng-de-qiangzhe-dou-dongde-zaoshi-lssmabzoku4ue28irdmfn0-d-0dc|Hanji 926]] treats Cao Teng as the eunuch ancestor whose adoption of Cao Song makes Cao Cao vulnerable to reputational attack.
- Career-entry setup: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-904-juezhao-neiguan-huode-xinling-da-ziyou-de-mijue-lopn-okbzr4ljvqmgzcsb7qav0n2|Hanji 904]] adds the earlier Xiaolian and first-entry context in which Cao Cao benefits from the Cao family channel while also carrying its stigma.

## Qualifications
The current profile is not a full biography of Cao Teng. Hanji 828 captures one decisive succession intervention, while Hanji 904 and Hanji 926 use Cao Teng primarily to explain Cao Song and Cao Cao's reputation pathway. The source set does not yet establish every office, patronage tie, or later evaluation attached to Cao Teng in broader historical tradition.
