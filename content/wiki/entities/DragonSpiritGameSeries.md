# 龙魂 / Dragon Spirit game series

龙魂 / Dragon Spirit is the independent game series discussed by [[WuJunIndieGameCreator|巫君]] in [[ep242-duli-youxi-shi-yitiao-gaoqian-hao-saidao-ma-lrqbzrceumbuggzuero5qb4jv3zu]]. The episode describes it as a buyout-style RPG about collecting dragon-like creatures and battling with them, similar in broad player fantasy to creature-collection games.

The series is the source's strongest example of [[VerticalFandomCrowdfunding|vertical fandom crowdfunding]]. Dragon enthusiasts could support the project, appear in staff credits, or in earlier phases have their own dragon designs adapted into the game. Later, higher animation and art standards made that contribution path more expensive and harder to scale.
