# Roy Seiders

Roy Seiders is the co-founder of [[YETI]] with [[RyanSeiders]], as discussed in [[yeti-ron-and-ryan-seiders-how-two-brothers-turned-a-400-cooler-into-a-2-billion-brand-d7f8e0e8-51a5-4444-8c1d-f5c43c353285]]. The episode presents Roy as the brother who moved from shallow-water boat work and [[IcyTech]] distribution into product design, operations, and early CEO responsibility for YETI.

## Key Points
- Roy grew up around [[RogerSeiders]]'s [[Flexcoat]] business, giving him practical exposure to outdoor enthusiasts, fishing rods, and small-product entrepreneurship.
- His shallow-water fishing-boat work made ordinary cooler failures visible because customers used coolers as gear, seating, and casting platforms.
- Roy built the initial Texas retailer relationships through [[IcyTech]] before he and Ryan created their own YETI-branded cooler.
- He held a 50.5% founding stake after outside advice warned the brothers against an exact 50-50 split.
- Roy's operating role included product improvement, factory coordination, and the post-[[IvanBrown]] manufacturing recovery that shaped [[SupplierConcentrationCrisis]].
- He stepped down as CEO in 2015 while remaining involved with the company.
