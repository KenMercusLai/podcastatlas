# 田英齐 / Tian Yingqi

田英齐 / Tian Yingqi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl|Zhouji 09]] as the son and successor of [[TianQiHuanGong|Tian-family Qi Huan Gong]]. The page preserves the name as the source gives it rather than using the episode ingest to settle variant historical naming.

His wiki role is to mark the continuity of Tian-family rule in [[QiState|齐国]] after [[QiKangGong|齐康公]]'s death ends the older Jiang line. That makes him part of the episode's broader movement from Spring-Autumn lineage order toward [[WarringStatesPeriod|战国时期]] power politics.
