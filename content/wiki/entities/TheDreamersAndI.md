# The Dreamers and I

## Overview
The Dreamers and I is [[KenjiYamamoto]]'s PBS documentary about Silicon Valley hacker houses and the people living inside them while pursuing startup ambitions.

## Current Profile
Within the wiki, the documentary is a source-scoped project that turns [[HackerHouseStartupInfrastructure]] into a human-interest case. It focuses on [[StartupEmbassy]], [[CarlosStartupEmbassy|Carlos]], and residents from places such as Istanbul and Seville to show how the Silicon Valley dream is experienced through crowded housing, founder persistence, and immigrant opportunity.

## Key Characteristics
- Documentary case on hacker houses as live-work startup gateways in the [[SanFranciscoBayArea]].
- Centers [[StartupEmbassy]] as a community with an explicit startup-dreamer mission.
- Uses resident stories to show personal tragedy, family strain, long development cycles, and entrepreneurial persistence.
- Connects hacker-house life to neighborhood complaints, housing pressure, and debates over who these houses should serve.
