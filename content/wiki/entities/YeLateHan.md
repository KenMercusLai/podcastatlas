# 邺城 / Ye (Late Han)

## Overview
邺城 / Ye (Late Han) is [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s Jizhou headquarters city in the wiki, first appearing as the seat transferred from [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]], later threatened by Wei Commandery and Black Mountain violence, and in Hanji 1061-1063 becoming [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s siege target and final breach point during the Yuan-family succession collapse.

## Current Profile
Ye's current profile has three linked functions. It is first Yuan Shao's Jizhou seat, acquired after Han Fu sends the seal ribbon and Yuan Shao's troops reach the city. That handover is not frictionless: clearer Han Fu subordinates leave, and [[GengWuLateHan|耿武]] with [[MinChunLateHan|闵纯]] die trying to resist it.

Ye is also a headquarters whose vulnerability includes households and officials' families. During the [[WeiCommanderyLateHan|魏郡]] revolt, Wei commandery soldiers and [[YuDuLateHan|余毒]]'s [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]] attack the city, and Yuan Shao's followers panic because many families are inside. [[TaoShengLateHan|陶生]] prevents a household disaster by entering from the western side, shutting the gate, protecting Yuan Shao's family and state officials' families, and escorting them to Chiqiu.

The latest layer makes Ye the focal point of Cao Cao's intervention in the Yuan succession collapse. [[YuanShang|袁尚]] retreats toward Ye when Cao advances, later leaves [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] and [[SuYouLateHan|苏由]] to defend it while he attacks [[YuanTan|袁谭]] again, and Cao surrounds the city with earth hills and tunnels. The surrounding campaign also clears grain-route threats and repairs water transport, making Ye a siege target tied to logistics, internal response, and perimeter control.

Hanji 1062 extends the siege from initial works into full isolation. Cao Cao abandons the earth-hill and tunnel approach, opens a long moat, lets the first shallow appearance lull Shen Pei, and then deepens the trench overnight. He diverts Zhang River water into it, cutting the city off from news and food. [[LiFuLateHan|李孚]] briefly breaks that information barrier by posing as a Cao-side command inspector, reaching the wall, and delivering Yuan Shang's message before escaping among old and weak people sent out under white flags.

Hanji 1063 completes the Ye arc. Yuan Shang approaches from the western mountain route and coordinates by torch signal, but Cao Cao prevents Shen Pei's internal response and breaks the outside relief force. Cao then shows Yuan Shang's captured official and personal goods to Ye's defenders, turning the rescue failure into visible morale evidence. Shen Pei keeps resisting, even nearly killing Cao with hidden crossbowmen, but the city falls after [[ShenRongLateHan|审荣]] opens the east gate at night.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan Shao's late-Han Jizhou seat, acquired through Han Fu's handover.
- Headquarters whose political meaning includes family and official-household security, not only walls.
- Repeatedly appears when Yuan power is vulnerable to internal or nearby revolt.
- Becomes the Yuan-family capital target that Cao Cao can attack once Yuan Tan and Yuan Shang split.
- In Hanji 1061-1063, its siege depends on internal response attempts, siege works, river logistics, moat-and-water isolation, communication-breakthrough attempts, morale proof, and insider gate control.

## Qualifications
This page is a late-Han Ye route, kept separate from [[YeWarringStates|邺城 / Ye (Warring States)]]. The Hanji 1061-1063 campaign details are drawn from podcast transcripts and should be treated as source-scoped where minor local names, waterworks, troop counts, or exact engineering details depend on transcript accuracy. Hanji 1062 renders the city as "夜城"; the wiki normalizes to 邺城 based on the surrounding Yuan-family siege sequence.

## What Changed
- Extended Ye's siege profile from water isolation into final relief failure, defender morale pressure, Shen Rong's east-gate opening, and post-breach capture of Shen Pei.
- Reframed Ye as a campaign setting where physical isolation, information control, and insider gate control converge.
- Added the Xin Ping family-killing detail as a household-security consequence of Ye's fall.
