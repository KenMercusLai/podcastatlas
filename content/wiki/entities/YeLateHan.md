# 邺城 / Ye (Late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] gives Ye its earlier entry moment as [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s new Jizhou seat. After [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] sends the seal ribbon and Yuan Shao's troops reach Ye, clearer Han Fu subordinates leave and [[GengWuLateHan|耿武]] with [[MinChunLateHan|闵纯]] die trying to resist the handover.

邺城 / Ye (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-970-yuanshao-ansha-lvbu-weihe-hui-shibai-ln2pnvit-qgpnhn-pne7o-p1irrs|Hanji 970]] as [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s vulnerable headquarters during the [[WeiCommanderyLateHan|魏郡]] revolt. The episode distinguishes this late-Han crisis use from the earlier [[YeWarringStates|Warring States Ye]] page by placing Ye inside Yuan Shao's Jizhou-family and official-household security problem.

When Wei commandery soldiers and [[YuDuLateHan|余毒]]'s [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]] attack the city, Yuan Shao's followers panic because many families are inside. [[TaoShengLateHan|陶生]] prevents the crisis from becoming a household disaster by entering from the western side, shutting the gate, protecting Yuan Shao's family and state officials' families, and escorting them to Chiqiu.
