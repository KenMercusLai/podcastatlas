# 公众 / Gongzhong of Han

公众 / Gongzhong of Han appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-44-yi-ge-gongsi-weishenme-buneng-you-liang-wei-ceo-lsz0xk6-0wwznkc62k-xkoxgz-r]] as one of the two men [[HanXuanhuiwang|韩宣惠王]] considers appointing as joint chief minister of [[HanState|韩国]].

The page is source-scoped because the episode says the name is probably not a personal name. The host reads "公" as a marker of ruling-house kinship and "仲" as a rank/order term, making Gongzhong likely one of Han Xuanhui Wang's brothers rather than a fully developed biography in the source.

Gongzhong's importance is structural. Alongside [[GongshuHan|公叔]], he makes the proposed dual appointment concrete and gives [[MiuLiu|谬刘]] a reason to warn about [[DualExecutivePowerRisk|双首长权力风险]].
