# 董卓 / Dong Zhuo

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-947-dongzhuo-shoufu-lvbu-kaiqi-chengba-zhilu-lvr-24l6j8f6zvpfcmqgknmgywa1|Hanji 947]] adds Dong Zhuo's first Luoyang consolidation before the forced-deposition branch. He reaches the escaped [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]] and Liu Xie / [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], ignores officials asking him to pull back troops, and reads Liu Xie's composure as a reason to contemplate replacing Liu Bian.

The same source shows his initial control as partly manufactured and partly real. Starting with only about three thousand troops, he stages repeated visible entries to create [[MilitaryPresenceTheater|军力声势表演]], then absorbs He-family troops, turns [[LyuBu|吕布]] against [[DingYuanLateHan|丁原]], takes sikong after Liu Hong is removed, and threatens [[CaiYong|蔡邕]] into service through [[CoercedTalentLegitimacy|胁迫征士合法性]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-948-dongzhuo-feidi-quanchen-ruhe-gaibian-huangquan-youxi-ltekg1a-5b5u37aeekjm3dm2m6kk|Hanji 948]] backfills Dong Zhuo's first decisive court takeover before the Luoyang terror and coalition branches. He tests [[YuanShao|袁绍]] on deposing [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]], intimidates the officials, suppresses [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]]'s objection, forces [[YuanWeiLateHan|袁隗]] through the seal-transfer procedure, and installs [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] as the ruler he can more easily control.

The same source shows Dong Zhuo pairing [[CoercedImperialReplacement|胁迫式废立]] with legitimacy packaging. He removes [[EmpressDowagerHe|何太后]] and punishes [[HeMiaoLateHan|何苗]] plus [[WuyangJun|武阳君]], but also appoints prestige figures such as [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]], [[YangBiao|杨彪]], and [[HuangWanLateHan|黄琬]], courts [[CaiYong|蔡邕]], and rehabilitates [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] and [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] through [[PartyProhibitionRehabilitation|党人翻案合法性]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-949-lishishang-de-dongzhuo-daodi-duo-canbao-lk6idogwmc54nk1sdfc5wonyvgbb|Hanji 949]] backfills Dong Zhuo before the first anti-Dong coalition branch in Hanji 950. The source presents his rule in [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] as direct civilian terror: soldiers loot households, seize people, and turn control of the court into daily fear for the capital's population.

The same source puts Dong Zhuo at a formal high point and a legitimacy low point. He receives xiangguo rank and exceptional court privileges, but the host stresses that he lacks durable territory outside Luoyang and therefore tries to borrow scholar prestige through [[XunShuangLateHan|荀爽]], [[ChenJiLateHan|陈纪]], and [[HanRongLateHan|韩融]]. [[ShentuPanLateHan|申屠蟠]]'s refusal makes this a source case for [[CoercedTalentLegitimacy|胁迫征士合法性]] rather than ordinary talent attraction.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-950-budong-quanli-beihou-de-renxing-bi-bai-wuyi-lu8-u78yqvvwwarsz8dlevvzchxr|Hanji 950]] backfills Dong Zhuo immediately before the forced-relocation branch. His violence produces the first [[AntiDongZhuoCoalition|反董卓联盟]] formation, while he has [[LiRuLateHan|李儒]] poison [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]] and then considers either attacking the Guandong armies or moving the court west.

The same source makes Dong Zhuo's control look politically nervous rather than secure. [[ZhengTaiLateHan|郑泰]] talks him out of a broad attack by diagnosing coalition weakness and warning that excessive mobilization will damage authority, while Dong Zhuo later invokes [[ChenweiPolitics|谶纬政治]] to justify moving from [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] to [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]]. [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]]'s refusal to cooperate shows that old-Han officials can still see the damage even when fear keeps most ministers silent.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-951-tianjiang-hengcai-jiujing-shi-haoshi-haishi-huaishi-lovwgng2wrtfno-s9ttpybbhksvh|Hanji 951]] backfills Dong Zhuo's relocation decision before the [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] destruction in Hanji 952. He argues that [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] and [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] are more defensible than Luoyang, closer to his western support base, and protected by passes such as [[HanguPass|函谷关]], but he treats consent as irrelevant and says civilians can be driven west by soldiers.

The same source makes Dong Zhuo's move a case of [[CoerciveCapitalRelocation|强制迁都]]. He removes [[YangBiao|杨彪]] and [[HuangWanLateHan|黄琬]], kills [[WuQiongLateHan|伍琼]] and [[ZhouBiLateHan|周毖]], contains [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]] by summons, confiscates wealthy Luoyang households, and forces a mass westward migration that turns strategic geography into terror administration.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-952-danxing-haoshi-weishenme-mowen-qiancheng-li9vvaeaues9-fxy4e4yp-f4pugj|Hanji 952]] supplies the devastation stage immediately after Dong Zhuo forces [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] west. He remains around [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], burns palaces, offices, homes, and the surrounding field, and turns the former capital into a living ruin before later sources return to its damaged aftermath.

The same source adds grave and captive violence. Dong Zhuo loots imperial mausoleums and elite tombs, burns captured Shandong soldiers, and kills more than fifty members of [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s clan. The page therefore now treats his pre-assassination rule as [[TombDesecrationPoliticalViolence|陵墓亵渎式政治暴力]] and terror administration before the later guard-reversal and corpse-humiliation arc.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-954-renxingbenshan-haishi-renxingbene-lkurz9dbb25jrjyl5c-rrahda39h|Hanji 954]] adds the captured-court precondition behind the anti-Dong-Zhuo field. Dong Zhuo's control over [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] is strong enough that [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] consider installing [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] as an alternative emperor.

The same source makes Dong Zhuo's coercion work indirectly through family risk. Liu Yu's son [[LiuHeLateHan|刘和]] is in Chang'an, and the host recalls Dong Zhuo's killing of Yuan-family members as evidence that exposed households could become retaliation targets. Dong Zhuo therefore creates not only military opposition but also the [[AlternativeSovereignLegitimacyTrap|另立君主合法性陷阱]] his enemies cannot easily solve.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-955-guanyu-zhan-huaxiong-qishi-shi-wei-lishi-lndkrj-fnepye8iu-ifgyh-vzysf|Hanji 955]] adds the 191 CE anti-coalition pressure before the later court-terror and assassination branches. Dong Zhuo receives the tai shi title, faces [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] as the most effective front-line enemy, and sends [[HuZhenLateHan|胡轸]] with [[LyuBu|吕布]] against Sun Jian at [[YangrenBattleLateHan|阳人之战]], where internal factional disruption helps produce defeat and [[HuaXiongLateHan|华雄]]'s death.

The same source shows Dong Zhuo trying inducement after force fails. He sends [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] to offer Sun Jian marriage alliance and appointment discretion, but Sun Jian rejects the offer. Dong Zhuo then loses ground toward [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], while the closing scene at [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] sets up his old resentment against [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] supplies a pre-assassination coercive-court branch. Dong Zhuo tests [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]] with a fear question, solicits [[CaiYong|蔡邕]] on an inflated honorific, and then expands terror by ordering officials and people labeled disloyal, unfilial, corrupt, or disrespectful to be executed and confiscated. The episode therefore makes Dong Zhuo's rule a setting where officials rely on [[DefensiveSpeechUnderTyranny|暴政下的防御性言说]] while public moral categories become tools of fear and private revenge.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] adds an earlier pre-assassination terror-and-narrative branch before the later Dong Zhuo-Lü Bu rupture. After a court astrologer predicts that a minister will die, Dong Zhuo accuses [[ZhangWenLateHan|张温]] of secret contact with [[YuanShao|袁绍]], has him beaten to death publicly, and then treats the death as omen fulfillment.

The source frames this as [[OmenLaunderedPurge|天象包装式清洗]]. Dong Zhuo is not only violent; he uses [[ChineseAstrologicalPolitics|中国星占政治]] and the audience's self-protective fear to make a personal or factional killing look like unavoidable celestial timing.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-958-jiemi-shishang-zui-duanming-de-tongchoudikai-lve41q9gyijbop8wk-b7e6shogju|Hanji 958]] supplies the pre-assassination coalition background before the later Dong Zhuo-Lü Bu rupture pages. Dong Zhuo controls the court strongly enough that [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] looks for rescue through [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]], while the regional response nominally gathers in the [[AntiDongZhuoCoalition|反董卓联盟]].

The source makes Dong Zhuo's threat real but not sufficient to bind his enemies. [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] fights Dong Zhuo's and [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s forces, yet [[YuanShao|袁绍]], [[YuanShu|袁术]], and [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] are already fighting over offices, rear bases, and troop flows. Hanji 958 therefore frames the later post-Dong-Zhuo instability as visible even before Dong Zhuo is removed.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-962-dongzhuo-yu-lvbu-daodi-weishenme-juelie-lgp3kxfqnhokibf6kpli6p4j8iti|Hanji 962]] supplies the pre-assassination structure behind Dong Zhuo's fall. The episode presents him in 192 CE as converting court power into a family-centered command system through [[DongMinLateHan|董旻]] and [[DongHuangLateHan|董璜]], hollowing out [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s authority, and building [[MeiwuFortress|郿坞]] as a fortified grain-backed fallback.

The same source makes Dong Zhuo's personal security self-defeating. He relies on [[LyuBu|吕布]] as a close guard while also damaging that relationship through violent temper and the thrown-hand-halberd incident. This gives [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] a route to turn the guard layer into [[PersonalGuardAttackSurface|贴身侍卫反噬]] before Hanji 963 narrates the actual killing.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-963-shishang-bei-diantian-deng-de-diyiren-shi-ta-lkpkhlpkv3xpw-hur4b7sh-3druk|Hanji 963]] supplies Dong Zhuo's direct death scene. [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] and [[LyuBu|吕布]] use [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s court meeting at [[WeiyangPalace|未央宫]] as the access window; [[ShiSunRuiLateHan|士孙瑞]] writes the edict, [[LiSuLateHan|李肃]] opens the attack, and Lü Bu announces imperial authority before killing Dong Zhuo.

The same episode turns Dong Zhuo's body and hoard into the first aftermath layer. [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] celebrates, Dong Zhuo's family is killed, [[MeiwuFortress|郿坞]] is confiscated, and the corpse is displayed as a source-scoped case of [[PublicCorpseHumiliation|公开尸体羞辱]]. The page therefore now treats his death as both a tyrant-removal scene and the opening of an unstable post-Dong-Zhuo order.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-964-bie-jiaoao-bie-jiaoao-bie-jiaoao-lo2-edzffu0v9ixoabc06rx-oqoq|Hanji 964]] adds the follow-on death-and-credit setup before the remnant-force crisis in Hanji 965. After Dong Zhuo is killed, Wang Yun executes [[CaiYong|蔡邕]] and rejects Lü Bu's proposals for disposing of Dong Zhuo's private soldiers and property.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-965-bangguo-bieren-de-mang-weishenme-yao-shao-ti-ltjd-cv59hnm-n8ar9jxdogp9q0q|Hanji 965]] adds the pre-counterattack remnant layer after Dong Zhuo's death. The episode focuses on Wang Yun's failure to give Dong Zhuo's Liangzhou soldiers a credible safety path, Niu Fu's fearful end, and the way Jia Xu redirects Li Jue and Guo Si from dispersal into revenge attack on Chang'an.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-966-meiyou-yexin-budong-quanmou-jiu-bie-dang-lingdao-lldn3hi1gl5v3axvklwakgf6vx3u|Hanji 966]] backfills the immediate danger after Dong Zhuo's death. The episode is less about Dong Zhuo's own rule than about the surviving military ecology: [[JiaXu|贾诩]] advises [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and other remnant commanders to attack [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]], [[LyuBu|吕布]] flees with Dong Zhuo's head after the city falls, and [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] is killed as the public anti-Dong-Zhuo official.

董卓 / Dong Zhuo enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] as the comparison case for [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]]'s late fortress strategy. The episode compares Gongsun Zan's [[YijingFortress|易京]] to Dong Zhuo's [[MeiwuFortress|郿坞]], arguing that both men tried to convert hoarded food and fortified enclosure into security.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-967-ta-canyu-mousha-dongzhuo-jingneng-taoguo-sishen-weilie-lv24biyswj9ljdld5htynhauhph3|Hanji 967]] adds the immediate post-assassination aftermath. [[WangYunLateHan|王允]], [[LyuBu|吕布]], and officials including [[ShiSunRuiLateHan|士孙瑞]] are tied to the anti-Dong-Zhuo action, but Dong Zhuo's death does not restore stable court order. His remnant commanders, especially [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]], remain armed enough to return against [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] after [[JiaXu|贾诩]]'s survival advice.

This page remains source-scoped because the wiki has not yet ingested a full Dong Zhuo career sequence. The new branch makes his death a transition from personal dictatorship to remnant-military revenge rather than a clean liberation of the Han court.
