# 雍门司马 / Yongmen Sima

## Overview
雍门司马 / Yongmen Sima is a late-[[QiState|齐国]] remonstrance figure who stops [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]] from personally entering [[QinState|秦国]] at [[HouSheng|后胜]]'s urging.

## Current Profile
His intervention works by defining kingship as a duty to the state altars rather than a ruler's private freedom of movement. Once Qi Wang Jian agrees that the king exists for the state, the proposed journey becomes self-contradictory. The king turns back, but the episode does not show Yongmen Sima converting that success into rearmament or a durable anti-Qin policy.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses a short governing-principle question to expose the danger of personal travel to Qin.
- Represents surviving corrective advice inside an otherwise Qin-influenced late-Qi information system.
- Achieves a narrow, immediate reversal without resolving Qi's larger strategic paralysis.
- Is associated in an old *Zhanguoce* version with physically blocking the royal carriage with a halberd.

## Evidence
- **State-purpose remonstrance:** [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-3-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhenzheng-yiyishang-chuilian-tingzheng-taihou-liq9d3itmbar00ij43zzed-mulul]] introduces his question about whether the king exists for the state or the state for the king.
- **Successful intervention:** [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-4-shishang-neng-cheng-juying-de-junwang-qiwangjian-livcg5uz60ne-6oje-qzjfpghhph]] completes the exchange: Qi Wang Jian accepts the premise, turns his carriage around, and abandons the Qin visit.
- **Forceful textual variant:** [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-4-shishang-neng-cheng-juying-de-junwang-qiwangjian-livcg5uz60ne-6oje-qzjfpghhph]] reports an old *Zhanguoce* account in which he stands across the road with a halberd.

## Qualifications
The sources provide no broader biography, office history, or evidence that Yongmen Sima designed an alternative national strategy. The halberd scene is a reported textual variant, not independently established here.

## What Changed
- The unfinished remonstrance now has an outcome: Qi Wang Jian turns back.
- His role is clarified as tactically effective but strategically bounded.
- The old carriage-blocking variant is now recorded with source caution.
