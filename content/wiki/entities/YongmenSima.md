# 雍门司马 / Yongmen Sima

雍门司马 / Yongmen Sima enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-3-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhenzheng-yiyishang-chuilian-tingzheng-taihou-liq9d3itmbar00ij43zzed-mulul]] at the moment when [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]] is considering [[HouSheng|后胜]]'s advice to go personally to [[QinState|秦国]]. The transcript stops at the opening of his remonstrance, so this page is intentionally narrow.

His first move is to reframe the issue from diplomatic convenience to ruler purpose. By asking whether the king exists for the state altars or the state exists for the king, he turns Qi Wang Jian's Qin visit into a question about responsibility to [[QiState|齐国]] rather than personal safety or short-term protocol.
