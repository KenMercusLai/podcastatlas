# Yokohama / 横滨

Yokohama is the city where [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] buys a self-use new-build condo in [[131-wo-zai-riben-mai-le-yi-tao-zizhu-fang-llofkgaj-hyajksrpkoylakuocxz]]. The source presents the choice as a daily-life fit rather than a maximized investment thesis: Yokohama sits between quieter coastal or suburban living and Tokyo-scale density, making transport, nature, population pressure, and ordinary convenience the decisive factors.

The city is useful to the wiki because it turns [[Japan]] from a macro or healthcare case into a residence-choice case. In the episode, the buyer chooses an ordinary mansion-style condo in Yokohama because location, property management, and reduced coordination burden matter more than tower amenities or a detached-house ideal.

[[177-riben-yibao-jijin-weihe-yao-zhichi-mianfei-de-guomin-tijian-lh06qonrzgjprqdwl8dygn4hoxpk]] adds Yokohama as a municipal preventive-health case. The city sends checkup materials and a voucher to eligible National Health Insurance residents, allowing [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] to use a nearby small clinic for a nearly free exam that becomes the episode's entry into [[JapaneseSpecificHealthCheckups]].
