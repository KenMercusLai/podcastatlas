# 武大郎 / Wu Dalang

[[fanwai-15-banlatte-jiang-jinpingmei-la-erqie-haishi-luse-de-991508433]] introduces 武大郎 through vulnerability rather than spectacle. He has fled famine, sells炊饼 in Qinghe, raises his daughter 迎儿, and depends on rented shopfront space and local tolerance. His small body, weak social position, and habit of appeasing stronger people make him an easy target for ridicule.

His marriage to [[PanJinlian|潘金莲]] is presented as an unequal bargain created by 张大户's coercion and household conflict. The arrival of [[WuSong|武松]] briefly gives Wu Dalang protection, prestige, and neighbor-facing confidence, but Wu Song's departure leaves him exposed again. That makes Wu Dalang central to the source's [[HouseholdAsSocialWeb]] reading: family, shop, street, gossip, and male protection all decide how fragile ordinary life can be.
