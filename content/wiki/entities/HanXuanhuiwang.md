# 韩宣惠王 / King Xuanhui of Han

韩宣惠王 / King Xuanhui of Han appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-29-suqin-lizhi-shuangwen-zhishi-gaibian-mingyun-lrf5cqvoriyep2lkbozju-lyjaxv]] as the successor after [[HanZhaohou|韩昭侯]] dies in the episode's 333 BCE succession cluster.

The page is source-scoped. The episode does not develop his reign; it uses the notice to show how [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] moves from compact state successions toward the fuller [[SuQin|苏秦]] and [[ZonghengjiaDiplomacy|纵横家外交]] story.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-32-shishang-shouwei-lianheguo-mishuzhang-suqin-lgfkk45yq1c0yblequ-d1t7mol3]] gives him a developed Su Qin scene. Su Qin argues that Han's limited land cannot satisfy [[QinState|秦国]]'s open-ended demands, then uses the "宁为鸡口，无为牛后" proverb to make submission feel dishonorable. The episode reads Han Xuanhui Wang's agreement as one step in completing [[HezongAlliance|合纵]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-44-yi-ge-gongsi-weishenme-buneng-you-liang-wei-ceo-lsz0xk6-0wwznkc62k-xkoxgz-r]] makes him the ruler facing a ministerial-structure choice. He considers appointing [[GongzhongHan|公众]] and [[GongshuHan|公叔]], likely his brothers, as joint chief ministers. [[MiuLiu|谬刘]] warns that the arrangement could create rival power centers, and the episode turns the scene into [[DualExecutivePowerRisk|双首长权力风险]] while also noting that a single unchecked minister can be dangerous too.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2]] closes his source-scoped reign through a compact succession notice. The episode renders him as Han Hui/Xuanhui Wang, says he dies, and records his son Cang's accession as [[HanXiangwang|韩襄王]].
