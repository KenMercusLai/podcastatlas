# 和嵩 / He Song (Eastern Han)

## Overview

和嵩 / He Song appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-762-lishi-gaosu-ni-nanren-youquan-neng-you-duo-huangtang-limdo2hfd8epcfkwwsrmzz9no0dj|Hanji 762]] as an upright Eastern Han official sent to Jinan as tutor after [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] learns of [[YuanAnEasternHan|Yuan An]]'s sealed memorial before the emperor sees it. His role links court-message leakage, punitive reassignment, and local moral governance.

## Current Profile

He Song's profile is defined by integrity under displacement. The source says he is the son of He Chong, enters court in 86 CE after Taiwei Song You recommends him, and is known for generosity and honesty. When sent to Jinan, he improves local customs and gains popular affection.

His tutorship is difficult because Jinan King Liu Kang is arrogant and indulgent. He Song repeatedly remonstrates, and although Liu Kang does not fully change, he still respects He Song. The episode uses him as a model of principled official conduct in a compromised assignment.

## Key Characteristics

- Upright official whose family and recommendation background mark him as a moral-political actor.
- Sent away from court after Dou Xian learns of Yuan An's sealed memorial.
- Governs Jinan with generosity and honesty, improving local custom.
- Remonstrates with an arrogant royal household without fully transforming it.
- Gains respect even when his advice is only partially effective.
