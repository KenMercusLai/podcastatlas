# 蔡泽 / Cai Ze

蔡泽 / Cai Ze enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-1-lishishang-diyi-chounan-nixi-zuo-qinxiang-lvmdrgbhvpbiyngm-nviunour0sk]] as the first main figure in the show's 秦纪 sequence. The episode presents him as a [[YanState|燕国]] man without distinguished background, famous instead for being physically ugly, repeatedly mocked, and unsuccessful in earlier attempts to lobby rulers.

His importance is timing and rhetorical aggression. When [[FanJu|范雎]] is politically exposed by [[BaiQi|白起]]'s death, [[ZhengAnping|郑安平]]'s surrender, and [[WangJiQin|王稽]]'s execution, Cai Ze comes to [[QinState|秦国]] and sends Fan Ju a deliberately insulting message: if he gets to meet the Qin king, he will make Fan Ju lose his position. The provocation works because Fan Ju's insecurity makes him summon the challenger.

The episode ties Cai Ze's confidence to [[TangJu|唐举]], who says his ugly features do not prevent future high rank. That forecast is not treated as proof by the wiki; it explains the episode's narrative psychology. Cai Ze's actual strategic move is to turn Fan Ju's anxiety into a [[PowerExitTrap|功成身退]] warning: powerful ministers who do not retreat after completing their work risk becoming the next [[ShangYang|商鞅]], [[WuQi|吴起]], or [[WenZhongYue|文种]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-2-mingming-yilian-baixiang-de-caize-ping-shenme-baixiang-lhfyb6hzcqkpbzdrwsxajb7hhmhe]] completes that move. Cai Ze does not only warn Fan Ju; he turns the warning into a direct succession proposal, telling Fan Ju to hand over the chancellor's seal and retreat while reputation and safety can still be preserved. Fan Ju accepts, recommends him to Qin Zhaoxiang Wang, and Cai Ze becomes keqing and then chancellor, though the episode notes that he holds the chancellorship only for a few months.

The same source adds Cai Ze's failed wandering background. Before Qin, he travels through Zhao, Han, and Wey looking for office, is expelled or ignored, and even loses his carriage and horses to robbers. His rise therefore extends [[WarringStatesCareeristMobility|乱世职业经理人式流动]]: an unsuccessful mobile persuader can still win briefly if he reads a court's risk better than the incumbent does.
