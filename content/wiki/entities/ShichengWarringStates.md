# 石城 / Shicheng

石城 / Shicheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-76-shui-geile-chuqingxiangwang-de-zixin-lsut4cfic7xbbrmbmimrruf7n6y0]] as the [[ZhaoState|赵国]] city [[QinState|秦国]] takes in 281 BCE after the prior year's attack on Zhao. The episode locates it near modern southwest Shijiazhuang.

Its role is source-scoped. Shicheng marks the continuation of [[QinEastwardPressure|Qin eastward pressure]] after [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] rejects Qin's proposal to attack [[QiState|齐国]] and after the unresolved tension around [[WanbiGuizhaoDiplomacy|完璧归赵式外交]].
