# William Craig

William Craig appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] as the Northern Ireland home affairs minister who banned the planned Derry civil-rights march of 5 October 1968. Protesters still turned up, the [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]] used batons and water cannon, and the resulting images helped turn the crisis international.

The episode also uses Craig to show [[TerenceONeill|Terence O'Neill]]'s reform dilemma. Craig warned that concessions to the minority could provoke a loyalist uprising, making [[ReformTimingTrap|late reform]] politically dangerous inside [[Stormont]].
