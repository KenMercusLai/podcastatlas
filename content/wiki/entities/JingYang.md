# 景阳 / Jing Yang

景阳 / Jing Yang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-6-lteqe-0ykjtv01cmxv1fhx6z0vdi]] as the [[ChuState|楚国]] general already leading a rescue force during the [[Handan|邯郸]] crisis.

His role is brief but structurally useful. The episode says Chu and Zhao do not directly border each other, so Jing Yang's army cannot simply move to Handan without the Wei route problem being solved. Once [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] seizes [[JinBi|晋鄙]]'s army and moves north, he sends word to the Chu force, and the Wei-Chu-Zhao side can finally cooperate against [[QinState|秦国]].

Jing Yang therefore functions as the Chu-side proof that [[MaoSui|毛遂]]'s earlier hezong success was real but incomplete. Chu commitment existed; it needed [[WeiState|魏国]]'s forced participation to become battlefield relief.
