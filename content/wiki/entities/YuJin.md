# 于禁 / Yu Jin

## Overview
于禁 / Yu Jin is a Cao-side commander whose current profile joins battlefield recovery after Wancheng with strict enforcement against the repeatedly rebellious [[ChangXiLateHan|昌豨]].

## Current Profile
Hanji 1013 and 1014 establish Yu Jin's crisis discipline. After [[CaoCao|曹操]] escapes Wancheng, Yu Jin defeats [[ZhangXiu|张绣]]'s continued pursuit, keeps his own troops controlled, suppresses plundering by Qingzhou soldiers, and builds defenses before answering their accusations. Cao Cao rewards him as Yishou tinghou because he protects operational order and civilians while trusting that evidence can clear his name.

Hanji 1076 adds a second kind of rule-bound conduct. When Chang Xi surrenders only after encirclement, other officers favor sending him to Cao Cao. Yu Jin instead invokes the stated non-pardon rule, rejects friendship as grounds for exception, bids farewell to Chang Xi, and executes him. Together the cases present discipline as priority ordering: immediate defense over self-vindication at Wancheng, and command law over private relationship in the Chang Xi case.

## Key Characteristics
- Stabilizes retreat after Wancheng and helps prevent a local disaster from becoming total collapse.
- Keeps his own command ordered while suppressing looting by allied Qingzhou soldiers.
- Fortifies against continued pursuit before defending his personal reputation.
- Trusts the command system to distinguish evidence from an earlier accusation.
- Enforces the stated rule against post-encirclement surrender even against an old acquaintance.
- Combines visible emotion with refusal to let emotion change the command decision.
