# Matt Ashby

Matt Ashby is the [[UniversityCollegeLondon|University College London]] researcher discussed in [[spring-then-fall-a-weakened-muslim-brotherhood-6a7aef1b616564bf00d9b354]] for a dataset comparing crime across large global cities. The episode says Ashby and colleagues selected cities with at least 2.5 million people, adjusted boundaries to make population densities more comparable, and harmonized crime definitions using Britain as the benchmark.

Ashby's phrase "least worst" captures the source's central methodological caution. The dataset is useful because it makes cross-city comparison less arbitrary, but the episode still warns that reporting behavior, definitions, density, vehicle ownership, and local administrative boundaries can distort crime narratives.
