# Nigel Barley / 奈杰尔·巴利

Nigel Barley is the British anthropologist whose [[Cameroon]] fieldwork among the [[DowayoPeople|Dowayo people]] anchors [[35-tianzhende-renleixuejia-zuo-tianye-hui-shangyin-de-603418624]]. The episode centers him through [[TheInnocentAnthropologist|《天真的人类学家》 / The Innocent Anthropologist]], especially his 1977-1979 trips, failed expectations, illness, bureaucracy, dependence on helpers, and comic self-exposure.

The page should stay source-scoped. In this wiki, Barley matters less as a full intellectual biography than as a case of [[FieldworkRomanceDeflation]]: the fieldworker becomes knowledgeable precisely because he is not a clean instrument. He gets sick, misunderstands people, is classified by local categories, relies on translators and missionaries, and cannot avoid participating in the social world he is trying to study.

## Key Claims
- Barley's self-mocking narrative turns fieldwork logistics into evidence about how anthropology is actually made.
- His local position is neither invisible nor neutral; he is a conspicuous white outsider with special constraints and permissions.
- His reliance on translators, missionaries, local officials, and hosts makes [[ParticipantObserverEntanglement]] concrete.
- The episode uses his failures to resist both fieldwork hero worship and armchair dismissal of fieldwork.
