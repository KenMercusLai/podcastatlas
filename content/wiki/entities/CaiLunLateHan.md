## Overview

蔡伦 / Cai Lun enters the wiki as an Eastern Han palace official whose current profile is built around paper-making improvement, court workshop authority, and later proximity to Deng-regency politics. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-776-hanshi-tanmi-cailun-shi-zenyang-faming-zaozhishu-de-ljqhhcywxr-yggzmcgzbbtg-q-hb|Hanji 776]] makes him the central figure in [[CourtDemandDrivenPapermaking|court-demand-driven papermaking]], while Hanji 781 and Hanji 794 keep him visible as a palace assistant whose earlier conduct still matters in post-Deng politics.

## Current Profile

Hanji 776 places Cai Lun inside the palace production system. The episode says he rises from xiaohuangmen to zhongchangshi and shangfang ling, supervising the making of court implements such as weapons and crafted goods. Because [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Deng]] favors books, calligraphy, painting, pens, ink, and frugality over rare treasures, Cai Lun looks for a lighter and cheaper writing material that fits the court's needs.

The paper-making account is practical rather than purely heroic. Cai Lun is said to use low-cost fiber materials such as old hemp goods and bark, along with repeated processing, to create soft, light paper. He presents it to [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]], receives praise, and the process spreads. The later phrase "Cai Hou paper" is tied to his 114 CE Longting marquisate after Deng continues to value him.

Hanji 781 and Hanji 794 add political afterlife without fully explaining it. Zhou Zhang's anti-regency plot treats Cai Lun, with Zheng Zhong, as one of the palace assistants helping Deng's government. Hanji 794 later mentions Cai Lun's old accusation case during the post-Deng transition, showing that his name remains politically consequential beyond the paper-making story.

## Key Characteristics

- Palace production official whose workshop role gives him access to both imperial demand and material experimentation.
- Paper-making improver in the current source, associated with cheap fiber inputs, lighter writing material, court presentation, and later spread.
- Trusted by Deng's court environment before and during the regency sequence.
- Politically exposed palace assistant in later Deng-regency conflict, not only a neutral inventor figure.
- Source-bounded figure whose "invention" language is narrowed to improvement, presentation, and promotion in the current wiki.

## Qualifications

The current evidence is a podcast-source synthesis, not a full technical history of paper before Cai Lun. Because Hanji 776's body emphasizes material improvement, court presentation, and process spread, this page avoids the stronger claim that Cai Lun invented paper from nothing. Hanji 781 and Hanji 794 show political proximity and later exposure, but they do not yet provide a complete biography, legal record, or death narrative.

## What Changed

- Created Cai Lun as a canonical late-Han entity from Hanji 776's papermaking account.
- Added Hanji 781 and Hanji 794 as bounded evidence that Cai Lun also belongs to the Deng-regency palace-politics field.
- Narrowed the source's invention framing into court-backed improvement, presentation, and diffusion of fiber paper.
