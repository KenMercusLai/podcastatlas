## Overview

蔡伦 / Cai Lun now spans an early Zhangdi-era palace-politics exposure, a later paper-making contribution, and Deng-regency afterlife. Hanji 751 places him as the xiaohuangmen who examines the Song sisters during [[SongGuirenZhangHeLateHan|Song Guiren]]'s accusation case. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-776-hanshi-tanmi-cailun-shi-zenyang-faming-zaozhishu-de-ljqhhcywxr-yggzmcgzbbtg-q-hb|Hanji 776]] then makes him central to [[CourtDemandDrivenPapermaking|court-demand-driven papermaking]], while Hanji 781 and Hanji 794 keep him visible as a palace assistant whose earlier conduct still matters in post-Deng politics.

## Current Profile

Hanji 751 gives the earliest current layer. During [[EmpressDowagerDouHeLateHan|Empress Dou]]'s accusation against [[SongGuirenZhangHeLateHan|Song Guiren]] and the Song sisters, Cai Lun appears as a xiaohuangmen assigned to examine them. The source does not make him the mastermind; it presents him as a palace executor operating inside a high-stakes succession struggle. Still, the episode explicitly treats this case as one root of the later danger around his death.

Hanji 776 places Cai Lun inside the palace production system. The episode says he rises from xiaohuangmen to zhongchangshi and shangfang ling, supervising the making of court implements such as weapons and crafted goods. Because [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Deng]] favors books, calligraphy, painting, pens, ink, and frugality over rare treasures, Cai Lun looks for a lighter and cheaper writing material that fits the court's needs.

The paper-making account is practical rather than purely heroic. Cai Lun is said to use low-cost fiber materials such as old hemp goods and bark, along with repeated processing, to create soft, light paper. He presents it to [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]], receives praise, and the process spreads. The later phrase "Cai Hou paper" is tied to his 114 CE Longting marquisate after Deng continues to value him.

Hanji 781 and Hanji 794 add political afterlife. Zhou Zhang's anti-regency plot treats Cai Lun, with Zheng Zhong, as one of the palace assistants helping Deng's government. Hanji 794 later mentions Cai Lun's old accusation case during the post-Deng transition; after Hanji 751, that reference can be read as the Song Guiren examination case rather than an undefined blemish.

## Key Characteristics

- Palace executor exposed early through the Song Guiren interrogation during a succession struggle.
- Palace production official whose workshop role gives him access to both imperial demand and material experimentation.
- Paper-making improver in the current source, associated with cheap fiber inputs, lighter writing material, court presentation, and later spread.
- Trusted by Deng's court environment before and during the regency sequence.
- Politically exposed palace assistant in later Deng-regency conflict, not only a neutral inventor figure.
- Source-bounded figure whose "invention" language is narrowed to improvement, presentation, and promotion in the current wiki.

## Qualifications

The current evidence is a podcast-source synthesis, not a full technical history of paper before Cai Lun or a complete legal biography. Because Hanji 776's body emphasizes material improvement, court presentation, and process spread, this page avoids the stronger claim that Cai Lun invented paper from nothing. Hanji 751 identifies the early Song Guiren examination case, but it does not prove Cai Lun's independent intent beyond his role as palace examiner under orders.
