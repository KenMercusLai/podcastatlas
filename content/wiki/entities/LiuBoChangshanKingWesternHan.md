## Overview

常山王刘勃 / Liu Bo is the son and brief successor of [[LiuShunChangshanKingWesternHan|常山宪王刘舜]] whose failure of sickbed service and mourning restraint, followed by interference with a central investigation, ends the Western Han Changshan kingship.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-342-1-waihao-bie-luan-jiao-you-de-mingzi-hui-bei-duzhou-yisheng-lpehgfgtkbed4vg3z7yegsqalll1|Hanji 342-1]] supplies the outcome: Liu Bo inherits [[ChangshanKingdomWesternHan|Changshan]] but is removed after only several months. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-342-2-gudai-fuqi-tongfang-jingyou-zhezhong-falv-xianzhi-lsnhz4q-oxjpefgkdfxkcal-37iv|Hanji 342-2]] supplies the mechanism. It presents Liu Bo as the son of [[QueenXiuChangshanWesternHan|王后脩]], an inattentive heir during his father's terminal illness, and a new king whose conduct during mourning becomes evidence of unfitness.

The case is more than private misconduct. [[LiuZhuoChangshanWesternHan|刘棁]]'s accusation triggers an investigation by [[ZhangQianWesternHan|张骞]], and Liu Bo allegedly hides witnesses, orders violence against officials, and releases suspects. Han Wudi declines to execute him but accepts deposition and household exile to Fangling, combining kinship lenience with removal from royal authority.

## Key Characteristics

- Crown prince and son of Liu Shun and Queen Xiu.
- Accused of failing to taste medicine or remain with his dying father.
- Succeeds as King of Changshan but reigns only several months.
- Accused of leaving mourning quarters early and pursuing alcohol, gaming, music, sex, public carriage display, and prison visits.
- Escalates the case by allegedly hiding witnesses, beating investigating officials, and releasing suspects.
- Spared execution but deposed and moved with his household to Fangling.

## Qualifications

The allegations reach the wiki through a popular-history summary of an interested denunciation. Liu Zhuo had been excluded from affection, inheritance, and support, while Liu Bo then obstructed the investigation; motive and evidentiary weight must therefore remain separate. The episode does not reconstruct the complete legal record, testimony, exact offenses, Fangling conditions, or Liu Bo's later life. Its transcript form “刘伯” is normalized to 刘勃.

## What Changed

- Created the first canonical profile by joining Hanji 342-1's deposition outcome to Hanji 342-2's family and investigative mechanism.
- Separated the mourning accusations from the later obstruction that independently worsened Liu Bo's position.
