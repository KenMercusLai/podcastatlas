# 田斑 / Tian Ban

田斑 / Tian Ban appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-29-suqin-lizhi-shuangwen-zhishi-gaibian-mingyun-lrf5cqvoriyep2lkbozju-lyjaxv]] inside [[ZhangChou|张丑]]'s argument to [[ChuWeiwang|楚威王]]. Zhang Chou warns that if [[TianYing|田婴]] is expelled from [[QiState|齐国]], Tian Ban or a similar replacement may regain influence and strengthen Qi's military response against Chu.

The page is source-scoped. Tian Ban's role is not biographical here; he is the counterfactual replacement who makes keeping Tian Ying in office look better from Chu's perspective.
