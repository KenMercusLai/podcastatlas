## Overview

张释之 / Zhang Shizhi is a Western Han official whose current profile joins slow early advancement, concrete historical counsel, status-neutral palace protocol, skepticism toward eloquence as a promotion signal, and root-cause advice against lavish burial.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-214-tiemian-zhangshizhi-dui-huangdi-lan-taizi-1-lqlbul2drksmqgmligaelawflsvq8|Hanji 214 part 1]] introduces Zhang as a Nanyang man who reportedly obtained a costly 骑郎 position through family resources, remained without promotion for more than a decade, and considered leaving. [[YuanAngWesternHan|袁盎]]'s recommendation makes him a 谒者, but proximity alone does not explain the later rise: [[LiuHeng|汉文帝刘恒]] asks for concrete, currently usable discussion, and Zhang answers through lessons drawn from Qin, Chu, and Han.

Three episodes define his present governing profile. At the imperial animal park, he refuses to let one fluent answer stand in for a full appointment judgment and uses [[ZhouBo|周勃]] and Zhang Xiangru as counterexamples to the equation of verbal fluency with worth. At Sima Gate, he stops Crown Prince [[HanJingdi|刘启]] and [[LiuWuLiangKingWesternHan|梁王刘武]], accuses them of disrespect, and requires formal pardon before allowing entry. At Baling, he tells Liu Heng that desirable grave goods, rather than insufficient stone sealing, create the basic theft incentive.

The relationship is reciprocal: Zhang constrains the ruler's convenience and family preference, while Liu Heng accepts correction and promotes him. A much later episode shows the burial advice becoming precedent. [[LiuXiang|刘向]] reportedly invokes Zhang's Wendi-era warning while criticizing [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s Changling project, so the Baling exchange survives in this evidence set not only as biography but as reusable [[MausoleumFrugalityRemonstrance|陵墓节俭式进谏]].

## Key Characteristics

- Late-recognized palace official whose advancement begins through recommendation but continues through usable historical and institutional judgment.
- Personnel skeptic who distinguishes verbal fluency from administrative fitness and moral reliability.
- Protocol enforcer willing to apply a public gate rule to the crown prince and a powerful king.
- Root-cause remonstrant who shifts tomb security from harder barriers to reduced burial temptation.
- Adviser whose effectiveness depends on a ruler willing to tolerate constraint and convert criticism into personnel decisions.

## Qualifications

This remains a bounded profile rather than a complete biography or reconstruction of Zhang's later work as 廷尉. Hanji 214 part 1's entry mechanism, family financing, tenure, offices, promotion chronology, dialogue, official titles, Sima Gate procedure, royal and dowager actions, and Baling design require comparison with transmitted histories. The host's modern “向上管理” reading may illuminate adviser strategy but does not prove Zhang's private intent, and the suggested Shanglin interest-group background is speculative. Hanji 536-1 establishes later reuse of the burial precedent within that episode's account, not a complete reception history.

## What Changed

- Created Zhang's profile from the career-access, personnel-judgment, palace-gate, and Baling episodes.
- Connected his Baling advice to its later reuse by Liu Xiang as anti-extravagance precedent.
- Distinguished the episode's institutional evidence from its speculative “向上管理” and hidden-interest readings.
