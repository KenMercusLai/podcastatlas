# Antoine Louis

## Overview

Antoine Louis was the physician who translated the National Assembly's commitment to uniform beheading into the technical requirements for a reliable mechanical execution device.

## Current Profile

After [[JosephIgnaceGuillotin|Guillotin]] withdrew from implementation, Louis remained responsible for the project. He accepted [[CharlesHenriSanson|Sanson]]'s warning that manual beheading depended on skill and could fail, worked with [[TobiasSchmidt|Tobias Schmidt]] on a machine, and evaluated tests on animals and human corpses before its first official use.

## Key Characteristics

- He supplied technical and medical authority after the political proposal had been accepted.
- He treated instant, reliable decapitation as a mechanical-design problem.
- His role is distinct from Guillotin's advocacy and Schmidt's construction.
