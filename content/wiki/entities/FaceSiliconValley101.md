# Face (硅谷101)

Face is the [[SiliconValley101]] host/narrator in [[e245-cangzai-damoxing-beihoude-xinwenren-gptmen-de-huifu-shi-zheyang-xie-chulaide-5aeaeb64-9165-4271-9884-23329b511e11]]. She frames the episode from her own [[ChatGPT]] use: late-night personal reflection, startup ideas, relationships, writers she likes, and thoughts that are hard to say to other people. That personal entry point lets the episode connect the feeling of being understood by AI to the hidden labor of [[ContentEngineering]].

The source also gives Face a media-labor role. She discusses graduating from [[ColumbiaJournalismSchool]] into a difficult journalism job market and receiving an [[AITrainerLabor|AI trainer]] data-labeling invitation. Her questions keep the episode from becoming only a new-role success story: she pushes on creator replacement anxiety, sycophantic AI, information bubbles, fact-checking responsibility, and what it means to form a relationship with an AI system.
