# Eugene Chia

Eugene Chia is the founder of [[FeatherlessAI|Featherless AI]] and the guest in [[featherless-ai-when-your-weekend-experiment-makes-more-than-your-startup]]. In the episode, he explains the company's path from [[Recursor]] and [[RWKV]] toward a hosted inference platform for many [[OpenSourceAIModels|open-source AI models]].

The source presents Eugene as a founder willing to separate mission from attachment to an original technical bet. He says the mission was always to make AI accessible across languages and compute constraints, but the product changed after customers showed stronger pull for instant access to models such as [[Llama]] and [[MistralAI|Mistral]].

## Key Points
- Says Featherless recently closed a 20 million dollar Series A and has roughly 30 globally distributed team members.
- Treats the weekend experiment as decisive [[CustomerPull]] because it generated more revenue than the original platform.
- Explains [[GPUHotSwapping]] as an internal constraint that became the company's market differentiation.
- Argues that [[FlatRateAIInferencePricing]] helps buyers who cannot predict usage-based AI bills.
- Recommends "Start With Why" as a business-advice frame, matching his emphasis on mission continuity through the pivot.
