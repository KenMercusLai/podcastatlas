# 麹义 / Qu Yi (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] backfills Qu Yi before Jieqiao. He is introduced as [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]]'s rebelling subordinate: Han Fu fails to suppress him, and [[YuanShao|袁绍]] uses the opportunity to pull him into his own camp. In this source Qu Yi is not yet the formation commander of Hanji 960; he is a pressure point in Yuan Shao's Jizhou acquisition.

麹义 / Qu Yi (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-960-caowei-dingji-zhinang-xunyu-weihe-qi-yuan-tou-cao-lmor1txqpm88itt4incalusconjr|Hanji 960]] as [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s tactical commander at [[JieqiaoBattleLateHan|界桥之战]]. The source says Yuan Shao places Qu Yi in front with a small infantry force while arranging strong crossbows on the sides.

Qu Yi's value in this episode is tactical discipline. His soldiers do not break when [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]]'s cavalry charges; they hold behind shields until the mounted force is close enough for the crossbows to matter. The result kills [[YanGangLateHan|严纲]], disrupts Gongsun Zan's army, and lets Qu Yi pursue to the enemy camp gate.

The page is source-scoped. Qu Yi matters here as the named operator of [[ShieldCrossbowAntiCavalry|盾弩反骑兵战术]], showing that Yuan Shao's camp can produce battlefield competence even while the episode criticizes Yuan Shao's broader talent-use judgment through Xun Yu.
