# 邓教授 / Professor Deng (cardiology guest)

## Overview
邓教授 / Professor Deng is the cardiovascular-medicine guest in [[vol-211-yue-yundong-yue-kualian-zhengxing-yisheng-yu-xinxueguan-yisheng-de-jianshen-qumei-zhinan-lsp1xlzq1iapc-qale-mzutigv0u]], a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode about exercise, appearance, heart safety, overtraining, sleep, supplements, and emergency response.

## Current Profile
The source records 邓教授 as the cardiology-side voice who turns exercise enthusiasm into [[CardiovascularExerciseRiskBoundary]] and expanded [[ExerciseLoadManagement]]. She accepts exercise as generally beneficial, but repeatedly asks listeners to account for baseline heart disease, blood pressure, adaptation, sleep, fatigue, supplement use, and the difference between ordinary hobby exercise and performance-oriented training.

Her safety frame extends from prevention to rescue. She discusses rhabdomyolysis-like muscle breakdown, creatine kinase, kidney burden, electrolyte problems, cardiopulmonary exercise evaluation, BLS, CPR, AED use, and public training needs, while keeping these as public education rather than personal prescriptions.

## Key Characteristics
- Source-scoped cardiovascular-medicine guest explaining exercise safety for ordinary listeners.
- Uses heart rate as an accessible but incomplete signal because blood pressure, disease status, and cardiopulmonary reserve change risk.
- Warns that poor adaptation and excessive training can produce severe muscle breakdown, kidney burden, and electrolyte problems.
- Treats sleep deprivation, late intense exercise, caffeine, and sympathetic arousal as recovery and safety issues.
- Recommends professional assessment and exercise prescription for people with underlying disease, high training ambition, or near-professional exercise goals.
- Extends exercise safety into BLS/CPR/AED literacy and public emergency response.
