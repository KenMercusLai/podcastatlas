# TrustedPrenup

TrustedPrenup is a prenup service discussed by [[JamesSexton]] in one [[HubermanLab]] source.

The bounded episode presents TrustedPrenup as an effort to make prenuptial agreements less expensive and more accessible while preserving the need for each partner to receive separate legal counsel. Its role in the source is narrow: it supports Sexton's broader claim that couples should be able to replace an unexamined [[MarriageDefaultContract|legal default]] with deliberate, informed planning.
