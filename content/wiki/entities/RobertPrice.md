# Robert Price

Robert Price is [[SolPrice]]'s son and partner in the [[costco-1]] account of [[FedMart]] and [[PriceClub]]. After the conflict with [[HugoMann]] ended the Price family's control of FedMart, Robert and Sol Price redirected the centralized-warehouse idea into Price Club.

The episode uses Robert Price mainly as a continuity figure: he helps carry FedMart's operating learning into Price Club, the direct predecessor that [[Costco]] later merged with through [[PriceCostco]].
