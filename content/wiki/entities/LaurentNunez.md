# Laurent Nuñez

## Overview

Laurent Nuñez is France's interior minister in the source's account of the government's failure to anticipate and contain widespread urban disorder.

## Current Profile

The episode says authorities underestimated both turnout and the potential for violence. Nuñez acknowledged that police lost control and offered to resign, but the resignation was not accepted. His role makes him the clearest individual accountability point in a wider dispute over staffing, intelligence, protester violence, and political blame.

## Key Characteristics

- Holds ministerial responsibility for internal security in the reported crisis.
- Acknowledged a loss of police control.
- Offered a resignation that was not accepted.
- Sits at the center of competing claims about preparation, force levels, and incitement.
