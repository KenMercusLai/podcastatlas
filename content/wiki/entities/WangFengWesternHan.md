## Overview

王凤 / Wang Feng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-544-yuangong-yue-zhongcheng-laoban-yue-huangkong-lk52zm46585z2noqxmtfn9kpwek2|Hanji 544]] as the Western Han Wang-family outer relative whose power makes criticism dangerous. The episode centers on the aftermath of [[WangZhangWesternHan|王章]]'s criticism and death, then uses [[MeiFuWesternHan|梅福]]'s memorial to frame Wang Feng as part of a larger outer-relative warning under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Wang Feng less through policy and more through the court atmosphere around him. Wang Zhang's criticism of Wang Feng becomes lethal, and that outcome leaves the court unwilling to speak openly. Mei Fu then names the resulting silence as a danger to the state, because powerful relatives can continue accumulating authority when correction channels are blocked.

Wang Feng also anchors the episode's precedent logic. Mei Fu invokes earlier Lu, Huo, and Shangguan outer-relative dangers not merely to attack a person, but to argue that favored kin must be protected from their own overreach through instruction, restraint, and balanced power.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han outer-relative powerholder in Chengdi's court.
- Target of Wang Zhang's criticism.
- Associated in the source with Wang Zhang's death and the resulting silence.
- Serves as the immediate Wang-family example behind Mei Fu's broader outer-relative warning.
