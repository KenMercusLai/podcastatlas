# 李傕 / Li Jue (late Han)

李傕 / Li Jue (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu|Hanji 999]] as one of the armed actors whose struggle over [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] leaves the late-Han court exposed. The episode presents his conflict with [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] as a power fight that makes Chang'an unlivable for officials and pushes the emperor toward the dangerous eastward return.

The page keeps Li Jue's role source-scoped to this branch. He matters here less as a full biography than as the immediate pressure behind the emperor-escort problem later handled by [[DongChengLateHan|董承]], [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]], and [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]].
