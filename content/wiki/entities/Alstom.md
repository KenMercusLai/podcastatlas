# Alstom / 阿尔斯通

Alstom is the French railway supplier named in [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]] as one of the foreign firms competing for China's early EMU orders. The episode connects Alstom's technology lineage to [[Hexiehao]] CRH5 and says its financial pressure at the time made the Chinese order strategically important.

Within the wiki, Alstom is a concrete case inside [[ProcurementLedTechnologyTransfer]]. The source emphasizes that China's buyer leverage came from concentrated orders, joint-bid requirements, and a unified railway channel rather than from an ordinary open consumer market.
