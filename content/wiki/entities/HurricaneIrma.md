# Hurricane Irma

[[HurricaneIrma]] is the 2017 disaster event that triggers the Barbuda land-policy conflict in [[sand-heists-and-property-rights-in-the-caribbean-summer-school]]. The episode describes seawater, wind, broken windows, damaged doors, roofless buildings, exposed residents, and a mandatory evacuation after forecasts suggested another hurricane might arrive.

The event matters because it turns land tenure from background institution into urgent recovery policy. [[GastonBrowne]] presents title conversion as a way to finance rebuilding, while Barbudan speakers read the same moment as [[DisasterRecoveryPrivatization]] that threatens [[CommunalLandTenure]].
