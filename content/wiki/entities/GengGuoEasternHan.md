## Overview
Geng Guo / 耿国 appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-2-gengyan-youzhizhe-shijingcheng-jiangmen-huzi-yongguan-sanjun-lnlfumtdzrigby9ox4v6pzbeogeh|Hanji 733-2]] as a Geng-family member whose court service helps make family loyalty credible to [[LiuXiu|刘秀 / 光武帝]]. His significance in this wiki is primarily political: he embodies a trust signal during the [[PengChongEasternHan|彭宠]] rebellion.

## Current Profile
The episode identifies Geng Guo as [[GengYanEasternHan|耿弇]]'s brother and [[GengKuangEasternHan|耿况]]'s son. When Peng Chong attempts to draw the Geng family into rebellion, Geng Kuang sends Geng Guo to Luoyang to serve Liu Xiu. The host frames this as similar in function to a hostage signal: not necessarily a formal hostage arrangement, but a way to show that the family will not use its local military base against the court.

## Key Characteristics
- Geng-family court-service figure used to reduce central suspicion.
- Brother of Geng Yan whose role is political rather than campaign-centered in the episode.
- Evidence of how family members could become credibility instruments in founding-era trust management.

## Qualifications
Geng Guo is a supporting figure in the current source. The "hostage-like" interpretation is the episode's political reading and should not be upgraded into a formal institutional claim without another source.
