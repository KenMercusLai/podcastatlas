## Overview
Geng Guo / 耿国 now has two current wiki functions. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-2-gengyan-youzhizhe-shijingcheng-jiangmen-huzi-yongguan-sanjun-lnlfumtdzrigby9ox4v6pzbeogeh|Hanji 733-2]] presents him as a Geng-family member whose court service helps make family loyalty credible to [[LiuXiu|刘秀 / 光武帝]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-724-xiongnu-neiluan-donghan-weishenme-bu-chenji-yao-ta-ming-lpqksa0j1y0tskwv6fpflhovg9b8|Hanji 724]] then presents him as the official who argues for accepting [[HuhanyeChanyuBi|日逐王比]] and the emerging [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] as a useful frontier buffer.

## Current Profile
Hanji 733-2 identifies Geng Guo as [[GengYanEasternHan|耿弇]]'s brother and [[GengKuangEasternHan|耿况]]'s son. When [[PengChongEasternHan|Peng Chong]] attempts to draw the Geng family into rebellion, Geng Kuang sends Geng Guo to Luoyang to serve Liu Xiu. The host frames this as similar in function to a hostage signal: not necessarily a formal hostage arrangement, but a way to show that the family will not use its local military base against the court.

Hanji 724 adds a later frontier-policy role. When Bi's southern Xiongnu bloc asks to become a Han vassal, most officials warn that the realm is newly settled, Han capacity is weak, and Xiongnu intentions are uncertain. Geng Guo argues for acceptance on the Han Xuan precedent: Southern Xiongnu can help resist [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] to the east, pressure [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|北匈奴]] to the north, demonstrate Han attractiveness to other frontier groups, and support [[FrontierCommanderyRestoration|边郡恢复治理]]. Liu Xiu follows this advice.

## Key Characteristics
- Geng-family court-service figure used to reduce central suspicion.
- Brother of Geng Yan whose role is political rather than campaign-centered in the episode.
- Evidence of how family members could become credibility instruments in founding-era trust management.
- Frontier-policy adviser who favors accepting a submitted Xiongnu bloc as a buffer rather than rejecting it out of suspicion.
- Figure whose significance expands from family loyalty signaling to state-level frontier sequencing.

## Qualifications
Geng Guo remains a bounded figure rather than a full biography. Hanji 733-2 supplies the family loyalty-signal layer; Hanji 724 supplies the frontier-policy layer. The "hostage-like" interpretation in Hanji 733-2 is the episode's political reading and should not be upgraded into a formal institutional claim without another source. Hanji 724 does not show Geng Guo designing the later detailed Southern Xiongnu settlement; it shows the acceptance argument that precedes Hanji 727's implementation.
