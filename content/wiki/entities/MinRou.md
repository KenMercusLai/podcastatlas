## Overview

闵柔 is a [[XiakeXingNovel|《侠客行》]] character whose compassion, shared values with [[ShiQingXiakeXing|石清]], and protective response to [[ShiZhongyu|石中玉]] make her central to the episode's account of durable partnership and parental partiality.

## Current Profile

The episode describes Min Rou as gentle and compassionate without reducing her to passive obedience. She and Shi Qing endure the loss of a child without converting grief into accusation, and their agreement on justice, family responsibility, and basic conduct lets complementary temperaments function as partnership rather than hierarchy.

Her protection of Shi Zhongyu creates the major qualification. Prior bereavement may have intensified compensatory indulgence, and she initially leans more strongly than Shi Qing toward shielding the remaining son. Yet the couple do not remain fixed at opposite poles: they move toward a combined response involving acknowledgment, accompaniment, and attempted restitution.

## Key Characteristics

- Gentle and empathic, including toward Mei Fanggu after catastrophic harm.
- Partner whose value alignment and mutual support survive bereavement.
- Active participant in household judgment rather than a figure of automatic obedience.
- Protective mother whose grief may have weakened limits around the surviving child.
- Capable of moving from pure protection toward shared accountability without abandoning attachment.

## Qualifications

The episode's causal chain from child loss to indulgence and later wrongdoing is speculative and must not become mother-blame. Shi Zhongyu retains agency, Shi Qing shares parental responsibility, and school, peers, opportunity, temperament, and other factors remain outside the source's full analysis.
