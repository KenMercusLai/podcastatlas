## Overview

Emperor Ping of Han / 汉平帝 is synthesized as the child [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] emperor whose reign and death let [[WangMang|王莽]] move from regent toward acting emperor and eventual founder of the [[XinDynasty|新朝]]. [[126-wangmang-zhisi-shensheng-diguo-de-huixuanbiao-he-huixuanbiao-de-huixuanbiao-786255780]] treats Ping as the young ruler Wang Mang elevated and married to his favored daughter, while cautioning that Wang Mang may still have imagined himself at first as a Huo Guang-style regent. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-627-1-nvxing-biting-dute-shijiao-jingqi-yinyu-beihou-de-lishi-zhihui-lqomjfejmglm9hylkomktx6rtbtt|Hanji 627-1]] adds the illness-and-death narrative before succession: Ping resents Wang Mang after separation from his mother and Wei relatives, then the episode follows [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]]'s pepper-wine poisoning account while contrasting [[HanShu|《汉书》]]'s illness notices. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-628-ruguo-chuanyue-jiao-ni-ruhe-dang-huangdi-lljocpgp247g7frbrkta39zrtlc|Hanji 628]] adds the succession aftermath: Ping's death in 5 CE opens the heir-selection field that Wang Mang narrows toward controllable children and then uses to justify "摄皇帝" authority.

## Current Profile

Ping's minority made court management decisive. In the Xiangrui source, Wang Mang uses kinship marriage, regent reputation, and Zhougong-like moral performance around Ping's illness to strengthen his position without the wiki treating the poisoning accusation as settled fact.

Hanji 627-1 makes the source tension more explicit. It narrates Ping's resentment after he cannot see his mother and after Wei-family relatives are killed, then follows the poisoning story in which Wang Mang offers pepper wine and writes a prayer text asking to die in Ping's place. The same source keeps the textual difference visible by saying 《汉书》 records illness and prayer performance more cautiously than 《资治通鉴》.

Hanji 628 makes Ping's death a succession mechanism. Once Ping is gone, Wang Mang can argue against adult Liu-line successors, summon younger Emperor Xuan descendants, and let the court describe an interim imperial-power package as necessary guardianship for a future child heir. Ping's role is therefore not only personal vulnerability but the institutional vacancy that lets regency become near-sovereignty.

## Key Characteristics

- Child emperor whose minority made regency and court management decisive.
- Marriage-alliance figure whose household link to Wang Mang tightened Wang-family access to imperial status.
- Illness-and-death boundary case through which Wang Mang performed Zhougong-like loyalty and faces a source-scoped poisoning accusation.
- Succession trigger whose death opened a broad Liu-line heir field.
- Political absence whose replacement problem let Wang Mang prefer a controllable young successor and claim acting-emperor authority.

## Evidence

- Child-regency role: [[126-wangmang-zhisi-shensheng-diguo-de-huixuanbiao-he-huixuanbiao-de-huixuanbiao-786255780]] says Wang Mang elevated Ping and operated through regent-like authority.
- Marriage tie: [[126-wangmang-zhisi-shensheng-diguo-de-huixuanbiao-he-huixuanbiao-de-huixuanbiao-786255780]] says Wang Mang married his favored daughter to Ping.
- Illness performance: [[126-wangmang-zhisi-shensheng-diguo-de-huixuanbiao-he-huixuanbiao-de-huixuanbiao-786255780]] says Wang Mang staged a Zhougong-like prayer to die in Ping's place and hid the prayer text; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-627-1-nvxing-biting-dute-shijiao-jingqi-yinyu-beihou-de-lishi-zhihui-lqomjfejmglm9hylkomktx6rtbtt|Hanji 627-1]] gives the prayer text its Zhougong金縢 model.
- Death-cause source tension: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-627-1-nvxing-biting-dute-shijiao-jingqi-yinyu-beihou-de-lishi-zhihui-lqomjfejmglm9hylkomktx6rtbtt|Hanji 627-1]] says 《资治通鉴》 adopts a pepper-wine poisoning narrative while 《汉书》 gives illness-centered records and annotation detail.
- Succession crisis: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-628-ruguo-chuanyue-jiao-ni-ruhe-dang-huangdi-lljocpgp247g7frbrkta39zrtlc|Hanji 628]] says Ping's 5 CE death forces Wang Zhengjun and the court to choose a successor from Liu-line descendants.
- Acting-emperor aftermath: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-628-ruguo-chuanyue-jiao-ni-ruhe-dang-huangdi-lljocpgp247g7frbrkta39zrtlc|Hanji 628]] says the ministers use the youth of the candidate pool and the white-stone fuming to justify Wang Mang's "摄皇帝" status.

## Qualifications

The source set does not prove that Wang Mang poisoned Ping. The Xiangrui source explicitly cautions that the poisoning claim lacks firm evidence and that Ping's survival would not obviously have ended Wang Mang's regency. Hanji 627-1 strengthens the caution by recording the 《资治通鉴》 and 《汉书》 difference, while still presenting the poisoning version as the episode's main narrative. Hanji 628 supports the post-death succession and acting-emperor sequence, but the exact adult Liu candidates, Wang Mang's inner motive, and the legal validity of the succession argument remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 627-1's Pingdi resentment, pepper-wine poisoning narrative, Zhougong金縢 prayer performance, and 《汉书》/《资治通鉴》 death-cause contrast.
- Preserved Hanji 628 as the post-death succession crisis and "摄皇帝" transition layer.
- Preserved the caution against treating Ping's death as proven murder.

## Relationships

- [[WangMang|王莽]] - regent and later usurper whose authority grows through Ping's minority, marriage tie, illness performance, and death aftermath.
- [[EmpressWangPingdi|汉平帝王皇后]] - Wang Mang's daughter and Ping's empress, whose "子孙瑞" is used in Hanji 627-1.
- [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] - senior dowager who must manage the succession crisis after Ping dies.
- [[RuziYing|孺子婴 / 刘婴]] - later child figure selected from the young Liu-line field opened by Ping's death.
- [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]] - dynasty whose succession vulnerability Ping's death exposes.
- [[XinDynasty|新朝]] - successor regime that grows from the acting-emperor and usurpation sequence after Ping.
- [[ControllableChildAccession|可控幼主继位]] - succession mechanism sharpened by the search for a manageable post-Ping heir.
- [[AuspiciousOmenPolitics|祥瑞政治]] - legitimacy technology used to convert the succession crisis into Wang Mang's acting-emperor title.
- [[HanShu|《汉书》]] - comparison text for Pingdi's illness and death record.
