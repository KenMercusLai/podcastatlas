# James Sexton

## Overview
James Sexton is a family-law and divorce attorney presented in this [[HubermanLab]] source as having more than twenty-five years of experience with divorce and prenuptial agreements.

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents Sexton as a legal practitioner who translates recurring divorce conflicts into preventive relationship questions. He argues that marriage always carries legal and economic rules, frames prenup negotiation as a test of how partners handle fear and disagreement, and connects formal planning to everyday honesty, intimacy, generosity, and maintenance. He also discusses [[TrustedPrenup]] as an effort to reduce the cost and friction of obtaining a prenup.

## Key Characteristics
- Reframes prenups as safety, disclosure, and future-design conversations rather than predictions of failure.
- Uses divorce practice to identify recurring disputes over money, caregiving, sex, fidelity, custody, pets, and competing definitions of fairness.
- Distinguishes observational experience from causation when discussing infidelity, gender patterns, and marriage durability.
- Treats small daily signs of attention and regular feedback as more useful than relying only on romantic ideals.
- Prefers explicit couple-created rules while recognizing that separate counsel and legal enforceability matter.
