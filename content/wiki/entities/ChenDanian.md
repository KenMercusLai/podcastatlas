# Chen Danian / 陈大年

Chen Danian / 陈大年 appears in [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]] as [[ChenTianqiao]]'s younger brother and an important early [[Shanda|盛大]] cofounder. The episode credits him with stronger early software exposure and later places him at the center of [[ShandaInnovationInstitute|盛大创新院]].

The source uses Chen Danian to show that Shanda was not only a sales-and-operations story around [[LegendOfMir|《传奇》]]. It also had a technical and experimental strand that later produced projects such as Bambook, cloud services, and [[WiFiMasterKey|Wi-Fi 万能钥匙]], even when the parent organization struggled to turn every early idea into a durable platform.
