# Hope College

## Overview

Hope College is a Christian undergraduate institution in Michigan presented in the source as the home of [[HopeForward]], an experiment that removes tuition for participating students in exchange for a voluntary post-graduation giving covenant.

## Current Profile

The college functions as both beneficiary and risk bearer. It promises education and preparation for a life of impact, while students promise serious participation and later annual giving without a required amount. That design changes the institution's relationship with graduates from collection under contract to continued persuasion through educational value, gratitude, and belonging.

The source reports that Hope receives about $55 million in tuition annually and would require roughly $1 billion to extend the program across the institution. Its church-shaped culture may make voluntary giving more legible to students, but also limits claims that the same design would work under different institutional conditions.

## Key Characteristics

- Christian liberal-arts setting whose communal culture supports the program's gift vocabulary.
- Carries the financing and transition risk that conventional tuition and debt place more directly on students.
- Uses a covenant with no prescribed donation amount rather than an enforceable repayment obligation.
- Began with a small cohort and an interim ambition of 100 first-year participants annually.
- Links future financial support to educational value, belonging, and alumni gratitude.

## Qualifications

The source supplies a sympathetic program portrait rather than audited finances, longitudinal alumni-giving data, a controlled academic evaluation, or a comparative institutional study. The college's location, cohort size, transition estimate, current program scale, student outcomes, and cultural mechanisms remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Established Hope College as the institutional setting and financial risk bearer for Hope Forward.
- Distinguished early academic promise from evidence of long-term financial sustainability.
