# 陈老师 / Chen (primary-school teacher)

## Overview
陈老师 is the pseudonym-level identification used for a primary-school teacher and homeroom teacher in [[ep180-feidianxing-beijing-mama-de-foxi-jiwa-women-jiujing-xiang-peiyang-zenyang-de-haizi-ckwridoewbb3abaaaadgpbgf|EP180]]. The semantic filename distinguishes this source-scoped educator from other people called 陈老师 in the wiki.

## Current Profile
With roughly seven years of student-facing experience, 陈老师 interprets “鸡娃” through classroom attention, fatigue, teacher workload, staffing, family communication, and student safety. She argues that advance learning and intensive tutoring can help or harm depending on the child, while institutional quality depends on teaching teams and stable staffing rather than school reputation alone.

Her educational ideal is deliberately broader than examination rank: kindness, curiosity, experience, safety, and the capacity to face setbacks remain valuable even when aptitude and academic performance differ.

## Key Characteristics
- Primary-school teacher and homeroom teacher with about seven years of student-facing experience.
- Classroom observer of advance learning, crowded extracurricular schedules, fatigue, and uneven attention.
- Systems-oriented commentator on teacher shortages, substitute turnover, school teams, and district capacity.
- Student-safety advocate who treats teachers as ordinary workers with professional and personal limits.
- Supporter of kindness, curiosity, experience, and resilience as educational outcomes beyond scores.
