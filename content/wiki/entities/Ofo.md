# OFO / 小黄车

OFO is the shared-bicycle company in [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]] that anchors the episode's boom-and-bust manufacturing section. The source says OFO's 2017 strategic agreement with [[PhoenixBicycle]] promised very large bicycle purchases, but later financing stress and under-fulfilled orders transmitted pressure back to suppliers.

The page's main concept is [[SharedBikeManufacturingShock]]. Platform growth appeared as industrial demand while financing was abundant, but once cash, orders, and collection weakened, manufacturers faced [[ReceivablesRisk]] and custom or excess-bike exposure.
