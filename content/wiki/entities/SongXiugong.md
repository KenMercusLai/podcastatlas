# 宋休公 / Duke Xiu of Song

宋休公 / Duke Xiu of Song appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]] as [[SongDaogong|宋悼公]]'s son and successor. The episode gives his personal name as Song Tian, then uses the succession notice to explain the broader status of [[SongState|宋国]].

The page is source-scoped and records the episode's role for him as a succession node.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] records the other end of the succession line: Song Xiugong, named Song Tian in the source, dies in 373 BCE and [[SongPigong|宋辟公]] succeeds.
