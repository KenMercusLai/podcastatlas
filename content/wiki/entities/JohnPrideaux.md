# John Prideaux

[[socialist-mobility-a-telling-michigan-vote-6a74600ec3e1347e8b5bf216]] adds Prideaux as the political analyst for the [[Michigan]] Democratic Senate primary. He treats [[AbdulElSayed]]'s win over [[HaleyStevens]] as a stronger left-wing signal than a progressive victory in a safely liberal city, while also warning that [[PrimaryElectorateDistortion]] can make a primary electorate misread national opinion.

John Prideaux appears in [[trailer-tocqueville-road-trip-6a31522c9b6fd8dcaba7b494]], [[trailer-tocqueville-road-trip-6a315642780d0f7579af216c]], [[trailer-tocqueville-road-trip-6a31598d780d0f7579b04fb7]], [[trailer-tocqueville-road-trip-6a315920101389aa3d42d134]], and [[trailer-tocqueville-road-trip-6a315813780d0f7579afb892]] as the host of [[TocquevilleRoadTrip]]. He frames [[AlexisDeTocqueville]]'s 1831 journey through the [[UnitedStates]] as a route for asking whether Tocqueville's democratic analysis still fits the country.

In the trailer, Prideaux's reporting plan is social cross-section rather than abstract commentary. He says the series will speak with people from high society, prisons, presidential-supporter circles, and communities hurt by government power, making [[AmericanDemocraticResilience]] a lived question.
