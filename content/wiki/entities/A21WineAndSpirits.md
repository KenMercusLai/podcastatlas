# A21 Wine and Spirits

## Overview
A21 Wine and Spirits is the company whose stolen [[NobleOak]] bourbon shipment anchors the cyber-enabled freight case in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents A21 as both a direct theft victim and an example of why cargo loss exceeds invoice value. Replacing the bourbon required bottles, corks, caps, labels, production time, and inventory replanning after criminals allegedly used a compromised dispatch identity to collect the original load.

## Key Characteristics
- Producer and distributor associated with Noble Oak bourbon.
- Lost 10,800 bottles across 1,800 cases and 18 pallets.
- Faced an estimated direct cargo value of about $500,000.
- Reported the interstate alcohol theft to the FBI.
- Used a concealed pallet tracker that thieves found and removed.
