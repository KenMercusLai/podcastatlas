# 祭肜 / Ji Tong (Eastern Han)

## Overview

Ji Tong appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-1-lishi-suo-yiwang-de-diwang-zhishi-mingjun-liuzhuang-lor7vhzzpwz1rv9tijksogukjqvr|Hanji 733-1]] as Liaodong taishou, whose frontier management uses Xianbei leader Pian He against Chishan Wuhuan and helps produce a northeastern security result under [[EmperorMingOfHan|Mingdi]].

## Current Profile

The source gives Ji Tong a compact frontier-administration profile. He sends Xianbei leader Pian He to attack Chishan Wuhuan, and the success leads outside-frontier groups to submit to Han authority. The Han state then withdraws the Wuyuan border garrison because the northeast is described as calm.

Ji Tong's significance is not a pitched Han expedition but mediated frontier leverage: a local commander uses one non-Han military actor to discipline another, turning local action into an empire-level border result.

## Key Characteristics

- Liaodong official with operational influence over northeastern frontier politics.
- Uses Xianbei force rather than relying only on direct Han deployment.
- Associated with Chishan Wuhuan defeat and broader submission outside the frontier.
- His action contributes to Han withdrawal of a defensive border garrison.
