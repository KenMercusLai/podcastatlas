# 何同学工作室 / He Tongxue Studio

## Overview
何同学工作室 / He Tongxue Studio is the creator-led video organization built around [[HeTongxue|何同学]], represented in the current source as a roughly 32-person entertainment studio capable of producing costly, engineering-heavy DIY videos.

## Current Profile
The studio combines editorial, filming, post-production, structural engineering, electronics, software, and project work inside a large former printing-factory space. Its engineered objects serve video outcomes: originality, comedy, surprise, and visual legibility matter more than whether each object becomes a manufactured product.

Its scale creates a three-way constraint. Complex work needs specialists, time, and capital; sponsorship must fit the story without displacing it; and delegation reduces the founder's frame-by-frame control. Overseas dubbing and localization extend distribution, but large short-form view counts do not yet substitute for long-form fan conversion or a sustainable production model.

## Key Characteristics
- Entertainment-video company whose hardware builds are production assets rather than its primary products.
- Multidisciplinary studio joining creative, editorial, filming, post-production, mechanical, electrical, and software work.
- High-cost, low-cadence operation whose flagship builds can consume many months and substantial cash.
- Founder-dependent quality system seeking stronger project and organizational management.
- Selective advertising business constrained by topic-product fit and client expectations for hit performance.
- Internationalizing channel that uses English translation, dubbing, and highly visual premises.

- Internationalization: [[liprfske7d5elpqdhiorn-ztvxzh-liprfske7d5elpqdhiorn-ztvxzh]] describes translated and dubbed overseas publishing, strong short-form reach, modest platform income, and stronger fan conversion from long videos.

## Qualifications
Headcount, floor area, costs, losses, staffing history, project duration, overseas followers, views, and revenue are self-reported snapshots from one interview. The source does not provide audited accounts, a formal organization chart, retention data, or evidence that the desired monthly DIY cadence is achievable.

## What Changed
- Established the studio as an entertainment-video organization rather than a hardware startup.
- Identified founder dependence, sponsor fit, project economics, and scarce engineering talent as linked scaling constraints.
- Added overseas localization as a distribution branch with a long-form conversion advantage but weak near-term revenue.
