# Eddie Espinosa

Eddie Espinosa is the [[Equinix]] customer operations manager who guides the Palo Alto data-center tour in [[tech-20260127-0127-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260127-0127-mp-tech-pod-128]]. His role is to make the site's infrastructure history visible: a 1929 [[PacificTelephoneAndTelegraph]] building, server cages, fiber, [[AltaVista]], and [[PaloAltoInternetExchange|PAIX]] all become part of one communications-infrastructure story.

For the wiki, Espinosa is a source-specific guide rather than a standalone theorist. His practical role helps the episode connect [[ColocationDataCenter]] and [[NeutralInternetExchange]] to the physical rooms where cloud and AI traffic are routed.
