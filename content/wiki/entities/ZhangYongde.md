# 张永德 / Zhang Yongde

Zhang Yongde / 张永德 is a powerful [[LaterZhou|后周]] general in [[169-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-shang-957052909]]. The episode presents him as [[GuoWei|郭威]]'s son-in-law, a senior military figure above [[ZhaoKuangyin|赵匡胤]] for part of the story, and one of the figures [[ChaiRong|柴荣]] had to balance.

His main narrative role is the "点检做天子" incident. Because Zhang Yongde held the 点检 office when the prophecy-like message appeared, the rumor threatened him directly; Chai Rong removed him and put Zhao Kuangyin in the position instead. The hosts leave the author of the maneuver unresolved, making Zhang Yongde a case in how rumor can change military appointment outcomes.
