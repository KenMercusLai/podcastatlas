# 曾焱 / Zeng Yan

## Overview
曾焱 / Zeng Yan is identified by the episode as a former deputy editor of [[SanlianLifeWeekly|《三联生活周刊》]] and the host of this [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] conversation.

## Current Profile
Zeng structures the discussion from digital-life unease toward concrete bodily practice, professional dance, public art, and cross-cultural connection. Her closing synthesis gives the body a form of “democracy”: attention should not treat it as a silent tool subordinate to thought, but as a participant in how people perceive themselves and the world.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames AI, algorithms, screens, and virtual recognition as the episode's opening challenge to bodily attention.
- Moves between ordinary bodily experience and the standards of professional artistic creation.
- Invites guests to make abstract dance ideas practical through guided breath and touch.
- Synthesizes dance as a route to connection, perception, and public life rather than only performance.
