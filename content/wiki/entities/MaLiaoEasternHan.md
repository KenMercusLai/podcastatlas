## Overview

马廖 / Ma Liao is the cautious senior figure of the [[MaFamilyZhangEraEasternHan|Zhang-era Ma family]] whose profile now starts as an object of [[EmpressMaMingdeEasternHan|Empress Ma]]'s household restraint before moving to court-culture remonstrance, title restraint, and later household failure. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-748-laozuzong-de-zhonggao-qinqi-bugongcai-gongcai-liangwulai-luqvnld4wbdgc3nrdumyhfhscs4z|Hanji 748]] has Empress Ma use him to correct a Ma-family burial-mound violation, [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-749-guanliren-bikan-fa-jiangjin-shi-guanli-de-zuijia-shouduan-ma-lnhhoh0oxlstuwakl3rsqvcss-cf|Hanji 749]] makes him a remonstrant to Empress Ma on top-down example, [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-750-jiemi-donghan-wanmei-nvxing-beihou-manshi-xisheng-lu-96lbdh7r0tsmiopbqqrj0q1gt|Hanji 750]] shows him accepting noble honor while leaving office, and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-752-shiqu-kaoshan-de-ren-gai-ruhe-chushi-lt0qb7alyh0ipf6mk8hvz92cs297|Hanji 752]] treats him as personally restrained but politically unsuccessful because he cannot discipline younger relatives.

## Current Profile

Hanji 748 gives Ma Liao his earliest current role as a senior Ma-family brother under preventive restraint. Empress Ma refuses [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Zhangdi]]'s plan to enfeoff Ma-family maternal uncles and later acts against household privilege directly. When a Ma-family burial mound at Lingshi exceeds the rule, she orders Ma Liao to level it, making him the channel through which family status is subordinated to public standard.

Hanji 749 then gives Ma Liao his court-culture role. When frugality edicts risk becoming empty moral language, he writes to Empress Ma that lasting change must start with the palace, royal relatives, capital officials, and elite households. His ruler-preference examples argue that lower levels copy visible status signals. Empress Ma accepts the memorial, so Ma Liao's profile becomes that of a court-culture diagnostician as well as a restrained relative.

Hanji 750 gives Ma Liao the title-restraint layer. Zhangdi grants him the Shunyang marquisate as part of a broader reward to Empress Ma's brothers. After Empress Ma worries about the reputational danger of family reward, Ma Liao and his brothers try to decline the titles. When the refusal is not accepted, they retain the titles but resign their offices and leave the court center.

Hanji 752 gives Ma Liao the later mixed profile. He is described as cautious, sincere, and self-guarding, so he is not the main embodiment of Ma-family extravagance. His failure is household governance: he cannot restrain younger Ma relatives, especially Ma Fang and Ma Guang, whose mansions, guests, horses, and Qiang-Hu exactions draw imperial displeasure after Empress Ma dies.

[[YangZhongEasternHan|Yang Zhong]]'s warning makes the failure sharper. Yang tells Ma Liao that high status makes the household visible to the empire and that young, impulsive relatives with reckless guests can destroy the family if they are not taught restraint. Ma Liao does not take the warning far enough, and Zhangdi eventually accepts official impeachment that sends the Ma brothers back to their fiefs.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior Ma-family relative who is himself subject to Empress Ma's preventive household discipline.
- Tomb-restraint channel ordered to correct a Ma-family burial-mound violation.
- Court-culture remonstrant who tells Empress Ma that palace and capital example must lead frugality.
- Senior Ma-family outer relative who accepts noble honor only after trying to decline it.
- Official who resigns office after receiving a marquisate, separating rank honor from active power.
- Personally cautious man whose restraint does not become effective household discipline.
- Recipient of Yang Zhong's written warning about younger relatives and dangerous guests.

## Qualifications

The current page does not reconstruct Ma Liao's full offices, family genealogy, or earlier service. Hanji 748 proves that Empress Ma used him to enforce family standard, but it does not make him the only brother subject to restraint. Hanji 749 shows his public-culture diagnosis, Hanji 750 shows real restraint in the title-and-office episode, and Hanji 752 should be read as a later household-governance failure rather than as simple personal corruption. The source's 马玉 appears to align with Ma Yu / Ma Yu, but the page keeps that son source-scoped until further notes warrant a distinct route.
