## Overview

马廖 / Ma Liao is the cautious senior figure of the [[MaFamilyZhangEraEasternHan|Zhang-era Ma family]] whose profile now spans both early title restraint and later household failure. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-750-jiemi-donghan-wanmei-nvxing-beihou-manshi-xisheng-lu-96lbdh7r0tsmiopbqqrj0q1gt|Hanji 750]] shows him accepting noble honor while leaving office under [[EmpressMaMingdeEasternHan|Empress Ma]]'s restraint logic, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-752-shiqu-kaoshan-de-ren-gai-ruhe-chushi-lt0qb7alyh0ipf6mk8hvz92cs297|Hanji 752]] treats him as personally restrained but politically unsuccessful because he cannot discipline younger relatives.

## Current Profile

Hanji 750 gives Ma Liao the first current layer. [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Zhangdi]] grants him the Shunyang marquisate as part of a broader reward to Empress Ma's brothers. After Empress Ma worries about the reputational danger of family reward, Ma Liao and his brothers try to decline the titles. When the refusal is not accepted, they retain the titles but resign their offices and leave the court center.

Hanji 752 gives Ma Liao the later mixed profile. He is described as cautious, sincere, and self-guarding, so he is not the main embodiment of Ma-family extravagance. His failure is household governance: he cannot restrain younger Ma relatives, especially Ma Fang and Ma Guang, whose mansions, guests, horses, and Qiang-Hu exactions draw imperial displeasure after Empress Ma dies.

[[YangZhongEasternHan|Yang Zhong]]'s warning makes the profile sharper. Yang tells Ma Liao that high status makes the household visible to the empire and that young, impulsive relatives with reckless guests can destroy the family if they are not taught restraint. Ma Liao does not take the warning far enough, and Zhangdi eventually accepts official impeachment that sends the Ma brothers back to their fiefs.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior Ma-family outer relative who accepts noble honor only after trying to decline it.
- Official who resigns office after receiving a marquisate, separating rank honor from active power.
- Personally cautious man whose restraint does not become effective household discipline.
- Recipient of Yang Zhong's written warning about younger relatives and dangerous guests.
- Figure through whom lost patronage becomes a family-management problem after Empress Ma's death.
- Household head connected to later Ma-family demotion and dispersal from the capital.
- Negative contrast to active self-protection because he recognizes danger insufficiently late or insufficiently strongly.

## Qualifications

The current page does not reconstruct Ma Liao's full offices, family genealogy, or earlier service. Hanji 750 shows real restraint in the title-and-office episode, so Hanji 752 should be read as a later household-governance failure rather than as simple personal corruption. The source's 马玉 appears to align with Ma Yu / Ma Yu, but the page keeps that son source-scoped until further notes warrant a distinct route.
