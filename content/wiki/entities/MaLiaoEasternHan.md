## Overview

马廖 / Ma Liao enters the wiki as the cautious senior figure of the [[MaFamilyZhangEraEasternHan|Zhang-era Ma family]] after [[EmpressMaMingdeEasternHan|Empress Ma]] dies. Hanji 752 treats him as personally restrained but politically unsuccessful because he does not discipline the younger relatives whose wealth, guests, and exactions make the whole household exposed under [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Emperor Zhang]].

## Current Profile

The source gives Ma Liao a mixed profile. He is described as cautious, sincere, and self-guarding, so he is not the main embodiment of Ma-family extravagance. His failure is household governance: he cannot restrain the younger Ma relatives, especially the brothers Ma Fang and Ma Guang, whose mansions, guests, horses, and Qiang-Hu exactions draw imperial displeasure.

[[YangZhongEasternHan|Yang Zhong]]'s warning makes the profile sharper. Yang tells Ma Liao that high status makes the household visible to the empire and that young, impulsive relatives with reckless guests can destroy the family if they are not taught restraint. Ma Liao does not take the warning far enough, and Zhangdi eventually accepts official impeachment that sends the Ma brothers back to their fiefs.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior Ma-family outer relative whose personal caution does not become effective household discipline.
- Recipient of Yang Zhong's written warning about younger relatives and dangerous guests.
- Figure through whom lost patronage becomes a family-management problem after Empress Ma's death.
- Household head connected to later Ma-family demotion and dispersal from the capital.
- Negative contrast to active self-protection because he recognizes danger insufficiently late or insufficiently strongly.

## Qualifications

The current page is source-bounded to Hanji 752 and does not reconstruct Ma Liao's full offices, family genealogy, or earlier service. The source's 马玉 appears to align with Ma Yu / Ma Yu, but the page keeps that son source-scoped until further notes warrant a distinct route.
