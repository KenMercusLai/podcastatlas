## Overview

马廖 / Ma Liao is the cautious senior figure of the [[MaFamilyZhangEraEasternHan|Zhang-era Ma family]] whose profile now starts with a court-culture memorial before moving to title restraint and later household failure. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-749-guanliren-bikan-fa-jiangjin-shi-guanli-de-zuijia-shouduan-ma-lnhhoh0oxlstuwakl3rsqvcss-cf|Hanji 749]] makes him a remonstrant to [[EmpressMaMingdeEasternHan|Empress Ma]] on top-down example, [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-750-jiemi-donghan-wanmei-nvxing-beihou-manshi-xisheng-lu-96lbdh7r0tsmiopbqqrj0q1gt|Hanji 750]] shows him accepting noble honor while leaving office, and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-752-shiqu-kaoshan-de-ren-gai-ruhe-chushi-lt0qb7alyh0ipf6mk8hvz92cs297|Hanji 752]] treats him as personally restrained but politically unsuccessful because he cannot discipline younger relatives.

## Current Profile

Hanji 749 gives Ma Liao his earliest current role. When frugality edicts risk becoming empty moral language, he writes to Empress Ma that lasting change must start with the palace, royal relatives, capital officials, and elite households. His ruler-preference examples argue that lower levels copy visible status signals. Empress Ma accepts the memorial, so Ma Liao's profile begins as a court-culture diagnostician rather than only as a cautious relative.

Hanji 750 gives Ma Liao the title-restraint layer. Zhangdi grants him the Shunyang marquisate as part of a broader reward to Empress Ma's brothers. After Empress Ma worries about the reputational danger of family reward, Ma Liao and his brothers try to decline the titles. When the refusal is not accepted, they retain the titles but resign their offices and leave the court center.

Hanji 752 gives Ma Liao the later mixed profile. He is described as cautious, sincere, and self-guarding, so he is not the main embodiment of Ma-family extravagance. His failure is household governance: he cannot restrain younger Ma relatives, especially Ma Fang and Ma Guang, whose mansions, guests, horses, and Qiang-Hu exactions draw imperial displeasure after Empress Ma dies.

[[YangZhongEasternHan|Yang Zhong]]'s warning makes the failure sharper. Yang tells Ma Liao that high status makes the household visible to the empire and that young, impulsive relatives with reckless guests can destroy the family if they are not taught restraint. Ma Liao does not take the warning far enough, and Zhangdi eventually accepts official impeachment that sends the Ma brothers back to their fiefs.

## Key Characteristics

- Court-culture remonstrant who tells Empress Ma that palace and capital example must lead frugality.
- Senior Ma-family outer relative who accepts noble honor only after trying to decline it.
- Official who resigns office after receiving a marquisate, separating rank honor from active power.
- Personally cautious man whose restraint does not become effective household discipline.
- Recipient of Yang Zhong's written warning about younger relatives and dangerous guests.
- Figure through whom lost patronage becomes a family-management problem after Empress Ma's death.
- Negative contrast to active self-protection because he recognizes danger insufficiently late or insufficiently strongly.

## Qualifications

The current page does not reconstruct Ma Liao's full offices, family genealogy, or earlier service. Hanji 749 shows his public-culture diagnosis, Hanji 750 shows real restraint in the title-and-office episode, and Hanji 752 should be read as a later household-governance failure rather than as simple personal corruption. The source's 马玉 appears to align with Ma Yu / Ma Yu, but the page keeps that son source-scoped until further notes warrant a distinct route.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 749 as Ma Liao's earliest remonstrance layer on palace and capital example.
- Reframed Hanji 750 as title-and-office restraint after an already visible exemplar-governance stance.
- Reframed Hanji 752 failure as inability to extend personal caution and earlier advice into family discipline.
