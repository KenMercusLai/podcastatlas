# British Columbia / 加拿大BC省

[[the-skyscrapers-that-nimbys-and-zoning-couldnt-stop]] adds a land-return and housing-policy branch. The episode says British Columbia officials forced Squamish residents from [[SinakVillageSite|the village site]] in 1913, making the later [[SquamishNation|Squamish Nation]] high-rise development a case where provincial dispossession history and modern [[SovereignHousingDevelopment]] meet.

British Columbia is one of the main [[Canada]] travel regions in [[ep265-dang-qing3xiu13-chongshang-resou-women-ruhe-gei-rensheng-fang-yige-changjia-lsloczlnkmcrrfpn9coh8rrqo-wb]]. The episode uses [[Vancouver]], [[Whistler]], Sea to Sky Highway, Vancouver Island, ferries, parks, food routes, and mountain access to show how a long holiday can move between city life and natural immersion.

The province's source role is practical and symbolic. It gives [[LeiTao|雷涛 / 无二旅人]] a first-entry route for Canada, while giving [[YanFeiSociologist|颜飞]] examples of bodily relaxation after arrival and of a former student becoming calmer on Vancouver Island.
