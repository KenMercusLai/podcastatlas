# 汤鹏 / Tang Peng (Qing thinker)

## Overview
汤鹏 appears as the most severe pessimist in the episode's comparison of Qing mid-dynasty diagnoses.

## Current Profile
The source associates Tang with “审几,” decomposing the logic and configuration of accumulated problems, and “审萌,” tracing where they first emerged. His diagnosis is presented as darker than those that still expected institutional adjustment to produce revival.

## Key Characteristics
- Offers the source's harshest judgment of the dynasty's accumulated problems.
- Emphasizes analytical decomposition before response.
- Traces mature institutional trouble back to early emergence.
- Provides a contrast to reformist hopes for self-correction.
