# CameraJet

## Overview
CameraJet is the [[Dyson]] electric toothbrush introduced in [[daisen-jinru-diandong-yashua-lingyu-chuantong-jindian-jiameng-chixu-shousuo-1010976825]] as a premium oral-care product that combines brushing, water flossing, camera-assisted inspection, and app-based feedback.

## Current Profile
The product's distinctive claim is not only better brushing but a shift toward instrumented oral care: a camera helps users see teeth and gaps in real time, while water-flossing functions target cleaning between teeth. The episode presents CameraJet as an attempt to make oral care feel like a high-end Dyson personal-care category, but it leaves open whether consumers want or trust camera-equipped toothbrushes at a USD 499 price point.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines electric brushing and water-flossing functions in one consumer device.
- Uses an onboard camera to detect tooth gaps and send live images to an app.
- Makes oral hygiene more observable, visual, and feedback-driven than ordinary toothbrushes.
- Carries premium-price adoption risk because the source reports a USD 499 price.
- Depends on consumers accepting sensor-rich bathroom hardware as useful rather than excessive.
