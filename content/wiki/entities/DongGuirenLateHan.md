## Overview

董贵人 / Consort Dong is [[DongChengLateHan|董承]]'s daughter and a pregnant consort of [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] who is executed after the failed clothes-belt edict plot in 200 CE.

## Current Profile

Her death makes the cost of court resistance household-wide. The emperor's title cannot protect a consort or unborn child once [[CaoCao|曹操]] controls coercive enforcement; the podcast further interprets the pregnancy as a possible succession symbol around which opposition could organize.

## Key Characteristics

- Daughter of Dong Cheng and consort of Emperor Xian.
- Pregnant when the Dong Cheng conspiracy is punished.
- Subject of an unsuccessful plea by the emperor.
- Killed as the purge extends from conspirators into the imperial household.

## Evidence

Household punishment:

## Qualifications

The podcast's claim that Cao Cao specifically feared the unborn child becoming an anti-Cao political focus is a plausible interpretation, not independently demonstrated motive evidence. The exact pregnancy stage, dialogue, and execution procedure remain source-scoped.
