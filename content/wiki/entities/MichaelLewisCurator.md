# Michael Lewis (Curator)

## Overview
Michael Lewis appears in the wiki as the curator-side speaker who explains the [[BayeuxTapestry|Bayeux Tapestry]]'s visual immediacy and reflects on its scenes of violence.

## Current Profile
His source-bounded role is interpretive. He compares the tapestry's figures and sequential storytelling to cartoon books or graphic novels and later helps the episode frame the makers' experience of stitching violent scenes as a way to think through conquest and suffering.

## Key Characteristics
- Curatorial interpreter of the Bayeux Tapestry's visual readability.
- Connects medieval composition to modern graphic narrative habits.
- Helps shift the tapestry from a conquest record toward an object of violence memory.

- Visual comparison - [[ire-and-ice-the-toll-of-americas-deportations-6a993426809ae7c2f4293df9]] attributes the cartoon-book or graphic-novel comparison to Lewis.
