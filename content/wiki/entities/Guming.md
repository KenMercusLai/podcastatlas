# Guming / 古茗

## Overview

Guming / 古茗 is a Chinese tea-drink chain used to show both beverage-category convergence in stores and supply-chain-led differentiation in bottled retail products.

## Current Profile

The coffee source presents Guming's tests as evidence that tea chains can move into standardized coffee by reusing sites, franchise operations, fresh-drink preparation, and mass-market traffic. The bottled source shows the reverse direction of capability transfer: Guming uses its existing cold chain to support high-pressure-processed refrigerated juice whose taste is described as closer to fresh juice.

This gives Guming a more specific bottled proposition than a shared shelf-stable formula, but it also binds distribution to refrigeration. The reported five-year exploration horizon and limited present earnings effect make the initiative a patient capability test rather than a proven second engine.

## Key Characteristics

- A scaled tea chain testing adjacent coffee and packaged-juice categories.
- Reuses store, franchise, sourcing, and preparation knowledge across beverages.
- Uses an existing cold chain to support refrigerated high-pressure-processed juice.
- Seeks process and logistics differentiation rather than relying only on brand or flavor.
- Treats bottled drinks as a long-cycle experiment with limited current performance contribution.

- **Coffee crossover:** [[141-kafei-zhanzheng-2026-jigouhua-yu-bentuhua-feng-tou-quan-1-141-1]] says Guming coffee tests reached roughly 20% of order volume and uses the case to explain [[BeverageCategoryConvergence]].
- **Cold-chain bottled product:** [[kafeidou-xicha-ruixing-baishang-shangchao-huojia-xianzhiyinpin-qijia-de-pinpaimen-weishenme-douzai-fali-pingzhuangyinliao-gkwrijiowiswahe8wgt2vl4q]] describes high-pressure-processed juice supported by Guming's existing refrigerated network.
- **Strategic horizon:** [[kafeidou-xicha-ruixing-baishang-shangchao-huojia-xianzhiyinpin-qijia-de-pinpaimen-weishenme-douzai-fali-pingzhuangyinliao-gkwrijiowiswahe8wgt2vl4q]] reports the CEO's five-year exploration frame and limited present effect on company performance.

## Qualifications

The roughly 20% coffee-order share, process advantages, price position, and strategic horizon are episode-reported. Neither source provides audited sales, margins, repeat purchase, spoilage, cold-chain cost, or packaged-juice distribution coverage. Refrigerated differentiation may reduce direct sameness while limiting geographic reach and raising inventory risk.

## What Changed

- Migrated the profile to synthesis-v1 while preserving the coffee-category evidence.
- Added cold-chain-backed packaged juice as a distinct capability-transfer case.
- Reframed bottled drinks as a long-cycle experiment rather than established earnings growth.
