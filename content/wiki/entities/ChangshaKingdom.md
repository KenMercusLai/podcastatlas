# 长沙国 / Changsha Kingdom

长沙国 / Changsha Kingdom enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-169-pandian-liubang-dengjiqian-buweirenzhi-de-gushi-2-lqq8qhramfzii5o5icesd-uagwkc|Hanji 169 part 2]] as the southern kingdom [[LiuBang|刘邦]] grants to [[WuRui|吴芮]] after the Han accession. The source says Wu Rui had previously been Hengshan king and had led [[Baiyue|百越]] forces in the anti-Qin struggle, so the grant converts southern coalition service into a place inside the new [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]] order.

This page is intentionally distinct from [[Changsha|长沙]], the modern city page already present in the wiki. The Hanji 169 part 2 use is a founding-era royal polity, not the later fieldwork or modern urban entity.

The kingdom is part of a paired southern settlement. Wu Rui's Changsha kingship recognizes one Baiyue-linked military ally, while [[WuzhuMinyueKing|无诸 / Wuzhu]]'s [[Minyue|闽越]] kingship recognizes a Yue-descended southeastern ally who had supported Liu Bang against [[XiangYu|项羽]] after being left unrewarded by Xiang Yu.

## Key Claims
- Changsha Kingdom is introduced as a Han founding settlement grant, not as a generic geographic reference to the modern city.
- Wu Rui's title links southern military assistance to Liu Bang's post-accession title distribution.
- The grant expands [[BattlefieldVictoryToFoundingOrder|战场胜利到建国秩序]] beyond the northern Chu-Han contributors into southern and Yue/Baiyue-linked actors.
