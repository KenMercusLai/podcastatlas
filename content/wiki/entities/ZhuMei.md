## Overview

朱玫 / Zhu Mei was a journalist, an early worker in [[MingPao|《明报》]], the mother of [[JinYong|金庸]]'s four children, and his second wife, whose contribution, divorce, and later life are often compressed into conflicting moral narratives.

## Current Profile

The episode describes Zhu as capable, strong-willed, and professionally active. After marrying Jin Yong in 1956, she worked in the early Ming Pao operation across reporting, management, and related publications. The popular claim that she sold jewelry or dowry assets to sustain the paper remains plausible within the story but unconfirmed; Jin Yong's own description instead emphasized his fiction income and suggested that he did not fully share the founding decision with her.

Jin Yong's long affair with [[LinLeyi|林乐怡]] was a major cause of the marriage's eventual end in 1976, and he later acknowledged that he had wronged Zhu. The episode refuses to convert possible prior marital conflict into an excuse for infidelity. It also separates Zhu's reported loneliness and lasting injury from unsupported claims of destitution: accounts of transferred shares and property and an estate left to children complicate, without erasing, the harm of divorce.

## Key Characteristics

- Journalist and early operational contributor to Ming Pao.
- Strong-willed partner whose work and family life overlapped with Jin Yong's publishing career.
- Mother of four children with Jin Yong.
- Injured party in a marriage broken amid Jin Yong's long extramarital relationship.
- Subject of disputed claims about startup sacrifice, divorce terms, wealth, and later-life deprivation.

## Qualifications

The page rests on one episode's comparison of biographies, interviews, and third-party recollection. It does not verify the jewelry sale, precise ownership transfer, motives for rejecting later help, the date at which Zhu learned of the affair, or the alleged sterilization condition. Her daughter's comparison between Zhu and [[HuoQingtong|霍青桐]] is family interpretation, not proof of a complete fictional prototype.

## What Changed

- Created a profile holding Zhu Mei's professional contribution, marital injury, and economic uncertainty together.
- Distinguished evidence of loneliness from the stronger and disputed claim that she died in poverty.
- Preserved Jin Yong's responsibility without pretending the earlier state of the marriage is fully documented.
