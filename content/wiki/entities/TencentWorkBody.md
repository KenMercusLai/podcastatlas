# Tencent WorkBody

Tencent WorkBody is the Tencent AI office product named in [[270-da-chang-yazhu-ai-bangong-feishu-he-dingding-que-xian-chengle-peijue-lmb4dgcgov3mr4cn7cikbghpfro4]] as an attempt to build an "AI-era Office." The source frames WorkBody as a product for non-programmers who want to use natural language to handle office work, information organization, files, materials, and task execution.

The episode treats WorkBody as a fast-moving reference point in Chinese [[AIOfficeAgent|AI office agent]] competition. It cites a relatively credible source for million-level DAU, but that metric remains source-scoped in the wiki. The source also compares WorkBody with Feishu-like enterprise collaboration assets: [[Tencent]] may not own a Feishu-equivalent enterprise office graph, so WorkBody is described as leaning more toward personal or local work data.

[[kafeidou-liangci-zaoyu-pingguo-chongji-yundong-shoubiao-jiaming-weihe-hai-neng-zengzhang-1006272684]] adds a quarterly-resource signal. The source says WorkBody grew quickly in Tencent's second quarter, received more internal resources, and helped justify higher AI spending for both model inference across multiple models and training a larger next-generation model planned for the year.
