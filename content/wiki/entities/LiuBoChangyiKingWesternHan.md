## Overview

昌邑王刘髆 / Liu Bo, King of Changyi is a son of [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] and [[LadyLiWesternHan|李夫人]] whose 97 BCE enfeoffment establishes his royal position before his later importance as a succession candidate. His sources present him mainly as a favored younger son and as the passive object around whom maternal-family political hopes form.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-380-2-liling-sandai-zhongliang-weihe-yidai-bi-yidai-can-lmtnhwjsyzlsfwpzcw18lubbauu0|Hanji 380-2]] says Wudi enfeoffs Liu Bo as King of Changyi in 97 BCE. As Lady Li's son and Wudi's younger son before [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|刘弗陵]] is born, he is presented as receiving particular affection, but the source does not show him exercising policy or factional agency.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-388-1-shaqi-shazi-zhihou-hanwudi-weihe-zumie-dajiuzi-liguangli-lkkwpivpwqpeg5kd7azu-wmdqmgb|Hanji 388-1]] supplies the later succession-risk layer. Liu Bo is Lady Li's son and therefore [[LiGuangliWesternHan|李广利]]'s nephew. Because [[LiuQuliWesternHan|刘屈氂]] is tied to Li Guangli by marriage, both men have family and interest-based reasons to favor Liu Bo's elevation as crown prince.

Liu Bo is not shown acting directly in the episode. His importance is structural: the hope of making him heir is what turns Li Guangli's front-line expedition and Liu Quli's chancellorship into a dangerous succession faction. Once curse-and-prayer charges are raised, Liu Bo's possible accession becomes the alleged objective around which treason is defined.

## Key Characteristics

- Son of Lady Li and royal son of Han Wudi in the episode's succession branch.
- Enfeoffed as King of Changyi in 97 BCE and presented as a favored younger son before Liu Fuling's birth.
- Nephew of Li Guangli, whose military and family fortunes are tied to his potential elevation.
- Preferred crown-prince candidate for Li Guangli and Liu Quli.
- Mostly a passive succession object rather than an active decision-maker in this source.
- His potential accession gives the curse-and-prayer accusation its political meaning.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 380-2 and Hanji 388-1. It does not reconstruct Liu Bo's complete biography, Changyi kingdom administration, the degree or duration of Wudi's favor, mother-line politics beyond these episodes, or later succession implications. His current role remains favored younger prince, succession object, and family-interest anchor rather than an independently acting faction leader.
