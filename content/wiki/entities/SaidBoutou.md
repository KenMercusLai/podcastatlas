# Saïd Boutou

## Overview

Saïd Boutou was a 14-year-old Algerian shepherd whom French reservist [[StanislasUta]] found being tortured with electricity during the Algerian War.

## Current Profile

The source gives Boutou little independent biography, but it preserves his name, age, occupation, and portrait. Uta photographed him after the torture, and the portrait later appeared on covers of critical histories and became a recognizable image of the conflict. Boutou therefore appears both as an individual victim and as a warning against allowing iconic imagery to erase the person it depicts.

## Key Characteristics

- Fourteen-year-old Algerian shepherd at the time of the reported abuse.
- Victim of electrical torture by French soldiers in north-eastern Algeria.
- Identifiable person preserved in Stanislas Uta's testimony and photography.
- Portrait subject whose image entered later histories of the Algerian War.

## Evidence

- Identification evidence: [[coffer-stop-ai-may-shrink-tax-base-9d736d6116fe084db141fce2778cebf6]] names Boutou, gives his age and occupation, and places the event in north-eastern Algeria.
- Abuse evidence: [[coffer-stop-ai-may-shrink-tax-base-9d736d6116fe084db141fce2778cebf6]] says Uta discovered him being shocked from a field generator.
- Image-afterlife evidence: [[coffer-stop-ai-may-shrink-tax-base-9d736d6116fe084db141fce2778cebf6]] says Uta's portrait of Boutou later appeared on historical book covers and became recognizable.

## Qualifications

The episode does not provide Boutou's later life, family account, own testimony, or a full chain of custody for the photograph. His image should not be treated as a substitute for his agency or as proof of abuses beyond the event Uta described.
