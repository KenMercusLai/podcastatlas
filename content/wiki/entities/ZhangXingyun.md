# 张星云 / Zhang Xingyun

张星云 / Zhang Xingyun is the [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] speaker in [[ep246-rang-nvsheng-anxin-de-yuehui-xiaojiuguan-shenme-yang-lpd5zw9ryd3kplmnaywsoqokvakm]], where she frames the annual love issue through [[SpatialLoveStudies|空间爱情学]]. Her source role is to move love reporting away from only relationship psychology and into the places where encounters happen: coffee shops, bistros, libraries, offices, travel, games, phones, and neighborhood routines.

The episode presents Zhang's method as close to lifestyle fieldwork. She interviews not only people dating but also third-party observers such as [[Moku]], coffee shop staff, and wedding hosts, treating operators and service workers as witnesses to how city intimacy becomes visible in seating choices, arrival order, drinks, awkwardness, makeup, eye contact, and repeat visits.
