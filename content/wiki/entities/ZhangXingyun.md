## Overview
张星云 / Zhang Xingyun is a [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] journalist and [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] speaker whose source profile spans frontier literary geography, urban relationship space, and evidence-poor historical-route fieldwork.

## Current Profile
Zhang Xingyun's stable method is to treat space as an active organizer of behavior and narrative. Frontier posts and routes shape Cen Shen's poetry; seating, staff, noise, and familiarity shape dating encounters; bridges, farmed-over alignments, market-road memory, and local legends shape what can be known about the [[LitchiRoad|荔枝道]].

The Shu-road episode adds her strongest evidence-boundary case. Because physical remains are sparse, she must work across gazetteers, maps, waterways, bridges, oral history, climate arguments, trade evidence, and disputed inscriptions while resisting the appeal of a simple Yang Guifei story.

## Key Characteristics
- Spatial reporter who explains how routes and places structure experience.
- Uses observers, operators, residents, experts, documents, and material traces as complementary witnesses.
- Preserves ambiguity when route, date, motive, or provenance cannot be settled.
- Connects intimate urban space and large historical geography through close attention to how people move.

## Qualifications
The sources establish a reporting method rather than a complete biography. The Litchi Road reconstruction remains especially provisional because surviving evidence is discontinuous and local tourism incentives can distort provenance.
