# 雍州 / Yongzhou (Late Han)

雍州 / Yongzhou (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-979-zaoyu-tongliao-beici-ta-ruhe-wei-caocao-liwan-kuanglan-luu58a2ww11g-radh-5g4l7fcis|Hanji 979]] as a provincial structure created when the [[HexiFourCommanderiesLateHan|河西四郡]] request separation from Liangzhou administration.

The source frames the creation of Yongzhou as administrative realism inside wider breakdown. Distance from the Liangzhou office and road disruption mean normal command channels no longer work, so the court responds by creating a nearer, separate provincial layer and appointing [[HandanShangLateHan|邯郸商]] as inspector. The act shows residual court administration even while Chang'an offices and appointments are being distorted by armed commanders.
