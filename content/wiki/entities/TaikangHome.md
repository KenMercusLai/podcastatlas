# 太康之家 / Taikang Home

Taikang Home / 太康之家 is the elder-care operator case in [[ep241-xiaoqi-hezuo-shi-xinyidai-de-tiefanwan-ma-lnmpsbvoqbexeardxyx88hywufig]]. [[LiXiaojie|李小杰]] discusses Taikang Home Yanyuan as part of the episode's [[SchoolEnterpriseCooperation|校企合作]] examples around training and recruiting younger elder-care workers.

The source presents Taikang as a higher-fee, more attractive wage-setting case than many ordinary elder-care institutions. Its "青春养老" framing tries to make [[ElderCareWorkforce|elder-care work]] more recognizable to students, while the episode still stresses that student retention depends on dignity of work, salary, progression, and willingness to handle intimate frontline care.
