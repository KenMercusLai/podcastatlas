# Eliza Hamilton

## Overview

Eliza Hamilton appears in the episode as [[AlexanderHamilton|Alexander Hamilton]]'s wife, the addressee of his farewell letter, and the long-lived keeper of his posthumous memory.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Eliza to keep the [[HamiltonBurrDuel|Hamilton-Burr duel]] from becoming only a story about male rivalry. Hamilton's farewell letter frames her as the person most directly burdened by his anticipation of death, his family debts, and the emotional aftermath. After Hamilton died, friends helped her keep the Grange, and she preserved his memory while also co-founding the Orphan Asylum Society of New York.

## Key Characteristics

- Wife and surviving family anchor in Hamilton's final letters.
- Faced Hamilton's debts and household vulnerability after his death.
- Preserved Hamilton's reputation and documentary memory.
- Co-founded the Orphan Asylum Society of New York in the episode's account.
- Lived long enough to shape Hamilton's nineteenth-century afterlife.
