# Joseph Strader

Joseph Strader is the educator and [[GoCyberAcademy]] founder interviewed by [[SamDataScienceWithSam|Sam]] in [[ep-9-chatgpt-and-education-systems]]. The source identifies him as having more than 10 years of education experience across roughly second through eighth grade, with some ninth-grade experience.

Joseph's role in the wiki is to ground early [[ChatGPT]] education debates in K-12 teaching practice. He treats AI as both alarming and useful: it can accelerate plagiarism or online-exam shortcuts, but it can also help teachers create differentiated material, lesson-plan drafts, and alternative explanations if educators preserve human judgment and classroom relationships.

## Key Claims
- Joseph frames [[ChatGPT]] as a tool whose school impact depends on timing, task, and teacher guidance.
- He argues against pushing education too far into AI-generated instruction because students still need the human element of teaching.
- He treats [[TeacherAILiteracy]] as a broad requirement for educators, not only for computer-science teachers.
- His GoCyber Academy work connects the episode's AI-policy discussion to [[K12ComputerScienceAccess]] and student opportunity.
