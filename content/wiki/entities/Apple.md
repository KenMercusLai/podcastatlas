# Apple

[[vans-kuangwei-fengguang-buzai-jingdian-fanbuxie-weishenme-maibudongle-1006742982]] adds a China-market AI update. The source says Reuters reported on 2026-08-14 that Apple was working with [[Alibaba]] to train a China-market large language model, after earlier reporting that mainland [[AppleIntelligence]] would integrate [[Qwen]] and [[Baidu]] technology. The source frames this as Apple seeking more control over local AI experience while competing in China smartphones.

[[zhongguo-xiaofeizhe-daidong-lafu-laolun-zengzhang-donghang-youhua-jipiao-tuigaiqian-zhengce-1005631805]] adds two hardware-strategy updates. The source says Apple's design team had studied new [[AppleWatch|Apple Watch]] forms including a screenless band, different screen sizes, and a round face, making Apple part of [[WearableFormFactorPressure]]. It also says Apple was testing [[ChangXinMemory|ChangXin Memory]] chips for products such as iPhone and MacBook, but that ChangXin's 2026 capacity was already full and could not quickly cover Apple's supply needs.

[[fuzhuang-pinpai-a-f-xunzhao-zhongguo-hezuo-huoban-fufei-tiqian-kan-telangpu-tiewen-fuwu-shangxian-1004810677]] adds the next turn in the [[OpenAI]] trade-secret dispute. The episode says OpenAI publicly responded that it did not possess or need Apple's commercial secrets, while the source interprets the deteriorating relationship through ChatGPT's Siri integration, OpenAI's [[JonyIve]]-linked device push, employee movement, and overlapping hardware ambitions.

[[tech-20260727-0727-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260727-0727-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds Apple through app-store accountability for AI nudify apps. The [[MarketplaceTech]] episode says San Francisco's city attorney sent cease-and-desist letters to Apple and [[Google]] under [[California]] law, arguing that app stores should stop profiting from nudify apps; this extends Apple's [[AppStore]] role into [[NudifyAppEcosystem]] enforcement.

[[tech-20260724-0724-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260724-0724-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds Apple through [[Bloomberg]]-reported [[AppleDeviceLeasing]] with [[Klarna]]. The [[MarketplaceTech]] Bytes episode treats lease-like access to Apple watches and Mac computers as a growth strategy for a premium device company, then links expected device-price pressure to [[MemoryChipShortage]] and [[AIHardwareSupplyChainPressure]] as memory makers prioritize AI data centers.

[[zhizhuxia-xinpian-naxia-jinban-guonei-piaofang-ai-moxing-baofa-jiagezhan-1004403588]] adds another device-price-pressure note. The [[ShengdongZaokafei|声动早咖啡]] episode says Bloomberg reported Apple was facing chip and memory supply constraints and that iPhone prices might rise by $100-$200, while [[TimCook]] compared the memory-price environment to an unusually severe flood.

[[we-almost-had-a-smartphone-in-the-90s-why-did-it-fail]] adds an earlier product-discipline branch for Apple through [[GeneralMagic]], [[TonyFadell]], [[SteveJobs]], the [[IPod]], and the later [[IPhone]]. The episode says [[Apple]] was a major General Magic investor, then uses Fadell's later Apple work to show the opposite operating model: a clear customer desire, constrained budget, deadline pressure, borrowed components, and repeated iteration rather than one huge all-or-nothing device launch.

[[146-meiguo-jingji-zheme-cha-meigu-hai-neng-jixu-zhang-ma-chuantai-meilun-meihuan-lrshkxgofprel-e-7lkiqu-en8gu]] adds Apple as the cautious-AI-capex and shareholder-return example in a U.S. equity-market discussion. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] contrasts Apple with more aggressive AI spenders: slower or more selective AI investment can disappoint product futurists, but in the source it supports the stock when paired with large buybacks and durable earnings.

[[tech-20260424-0424-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260424-0424-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds Apple through an announced leadership transition: [[TimCook]] is described as stepping down as CEO after about 15 years and moving to executive chairman, while [[JohnTernus]] is named as the next CEO. [[AnitaRamaswamy]] reads Ternus's hardware-engineering background as a signal that Apple may put more strategic weight behind devices, product innovation, and the search for a post-[[IPhone|iPhone]] interface after tepid response to [[AppleIntelligence]] and mixed reaction to [[VisionPro]].

[[tech-20260417-0417-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260417-0417-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds Apple through [[Globalstar]]'s existing satellite-service partnership for some iPhones and Apple Watches, including emergency texting when terrestrial service is unavailable. The source makes Apple part of [[DirectToDeviceSatelliteConnectivity]] as a consumer-device endpoint rather than only an AI, App Store, or hardware-supply-chain actor.

[[tech-20260717-0717-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260717-0717-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds Apple as the plaintiff in a trade-secret lawsuit against [[OpenAI]]. The source links Apple to [[AIHardwareTradeSecretDispute]] and qualifies the [[AIPlusTerminals]] branch: AI device competition can carry old hardware-industry risks around designs, suppliers, and employee movement.

[[tech-20260227-0227-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260227-0227-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds Apple as a [[TechManufacturingReshoring]] case. The source also qualifies the announcement: the Mac Mini is not one of Apple's most popular products, most Apple manufacturing remains overseas, and high-skilled labor shortages may shape who fills the advanced-manufacturing jobs.

[[264-kuke-de-daode-maodian-guoqu-15-nian-kuke-gei-pingguo-liuxiale-shenme-lg3t77r6ajm31wcsdv1ze6c8hhj0]] adds Apple as a governance and values institution under [[TimCook]].
