# Arsenal One

## Overview

Arsenal One is [[Anduril]]'s Columbus, Ohio factory campus in the All-In defense technology episode. The source presents it as a concrete manufacturing project for [[DefenseIndustrialBaseReindustrialization]] and [[FactoryBasedDeterrence]].

## Current Profile

In the episode, [[TreyStephens|Trey Stephens]] describes Arsenal One as a five-million-square-foot campus designed for modular defense production. Its strategic role is not only to make a single product. It is meant to show how a new defense prime can pair software-defined products, private capital, and flexible factory design with the ability to replenish systems during crisis.

Arsenal One also functions as a contrast case. The episode says early Ukraine-war needs exposed how hard it can be to restart production knowledge for systems such as Stingers and Javelins once supply chains and factory memory have thinned.

## Key Characteristics

- Anduril factory campus located in Columbus, Ohio in the source's account.
- Presented as a five-million-square-foot production project.
- Designed around modular production rather than a single locked product line.
- Serves the episode's claim that factories and replenishment capacity are deterrence assets.
- Used as evidence for a private-capital defense-prime model with physical manufacturing depth.
