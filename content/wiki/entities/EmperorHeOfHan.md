## Overview

汉和帝 / Emperor He of Han enters the current wiki through Hanji 777 as the Eastern Han emperor whose death creates the immediate accession crisis handled by [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|和熹邓皇后]]. His source role is primarily structural: the episode uses his death, dead sons, and secretly fostered later princes to explain why infant succession and regency begin under unusual uncertainty.

## Current Profile

Hanji 777 records Emperor He's death in Zhangde qian dian and then looks backward to the succession problem created during his reign. Because many sons had died young, later-born princes were quietly sent out to be raised outside the palace, leaving ministers without full knowledge of the available heirs.

After his death, Deng brings the princes back into the palace. Liu Sheng is set aside because the source says he has chronic illness, while the infant Liu Long is made crown prince and then emperor that same night as [[EmperorShangOfHan|Emperor Shang]]. Emperor He's profile in this ingest is therefore not an independent reign synthesis; it is the posthumous condition that makes Deng's first regency decision necessary.

## Key Characteristics

- Deceased Eastern Han ruler whose death opens the Deng-regency sequence.
- Source of an opaque succession field because multiple sons had died young and later-born princes were secretly fostered outside the palace.
- Father of Liu Sheng and Liu Long in this source's accession account.
- Posthumous link between palace mourning, infant accession, and adult regency authority.
- Structural predecessor to [[EmperorShangOfHan|Emperor Shang]] rather than an independently analyzed ruler in the current evidence.

## Qualifications

This page is intentionally narrow. Hanji 777 does not reconstruct Emperor He's reign, policies, court factions, or full family chronology. It supplies the succession context for Deng's regency and should be expanded only when later source notes provide bounded evidence.

## What Changed

- Created Emperor He as the canonical page for the death-and-succession bridge that opens Hanji 777.
- Bound the current profile to succession opacity and infant accession rather than a full reign biography.
