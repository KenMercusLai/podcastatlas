# Charles François Dumouriez

## Overview

Charles François Dumouriez was a French general whose 1792 campaigns helped stop the Prussians at [[BattleOfValmy|Valmy]] and then carried the revolutionary war into Belgium.

## Current Profile

The source presents Dumouriez as an operationally ambitious commander redirected from Belgium to the immediate defence of France. His position helped deter Brunswick by threatening Prussian supply lines; after Valmy, victory at Jemappes and the advance into Belgium made him central to the shift from defensive survival toward [[RevolutionaryIdeologicalWar|ideological expansion]].

## Key Characteristics

- He initially preferred a campaign in Belgium but was ordered to block the Prussian invasion.
- His maneuvering created a threat to Brunswick's supply and retreat routes.
- Kellermann's reinforcement enabled the French stand at Valmy.
- He resumed the offensive after the Prussian withdrawal.
- His victory at Jemappes opened Brussels and further Belgian advances to French forces.
