# 赫德 / Robert Hart

## Overview

赫德 / Robert Hart is the British official whom the bounded source treats as a modern, broad-form guest official because he spent most of his career directing the [[ImperialMaritimeCustoms|Chinese Imperial Maritime Customs Service]] for the [[QingDynasty|Qing dynasty]].

## Current Profile

Hart's source profile combines technical administrator, institutional broker, and politically ambiguous outsider. He moved from British consular service into Qing customs work, became inspector general after the [[LayOsbornFlotilla|Lay-Osborn Flotilla]] crisis displaced Horatio Nelson Lay, and helped extend standardized customs administration, training, education, and diplomatic services. These contributions increased Qing governing capacity, but the same career sat inside treaty-port and British imperial power, so usefulness to Qing rulers does not settle whose interests the institution ultimately served.

## Key Characteristics

- Long-serving foreign leader inside a Qing revenue institution.
- Associated with administrative standardization, training, efficiency, and anti-corruption norms.
- Broker between Qing officials, foreign governments, education, and diplomacy.
- Example of imported expertise whose value and sovereignty costs must be assessed together.
