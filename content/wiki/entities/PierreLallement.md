# Pierre Lallement / 皮埃尔·拉勒芒

Pierre Lallement appears in [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]] through the front-wheel-pedal bicycle lineage and the later U.S. patent story. The episode presents his patent rights as commercially important because [[AlbertPope]] acquired key U.S. interests and used them to structure licensing and litigation around bicycle manufacturing.

Lallement's wiki role is therefore both technical and legal. His design belongs to [[BicycleProductEvolution]], while the patent afterlife belongs to the episode's account of bicycle commercialization, [[PopeManufacturingCompany]], and the early use of patents as market power.
