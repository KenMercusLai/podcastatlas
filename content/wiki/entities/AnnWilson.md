# Ann Wilson

## Overview

Ann Wilson is a singer and member of Heart represented as a musical co-creator of [[BlueSeattle]] with [[NancyWilson]] and [[CameronCrowe]].

## Current Profile

Wilson joined Crowe and her sister during their 1986 honeymoon at her cabin in Cannon Beach, Oregon. The cabin became a short-lived workshop in which the trio wrote and recorded ten songs designed to satisfy the conventions of an [[ElvisPresley]] musical.

Her participation helped convert Crowe's research interest and screenplay idea into a surviving audio artifact. The episode emphasizes the group's enthusiasm and relative lack of irony, placing Wilson within an affectionate reconstruction rather than a detached spoof.
