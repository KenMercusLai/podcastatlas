# Jonathan Haidt

## Overview
Jonathan Haidt is the psychologist and public intellectual whose argument in [[TheAnxiousGeneration|The Anxious Generation]] anchors this wiki's current Haidt-specific branch on youth social-media restrictions.

## Current Profile
In [[351-xifang-zhengfu-jinzhi-qingshaonian-shiyong-shejiao-meiti-zoudao-nabu-le-luvc8m2kuqkyv2hddfcse-6ogxym]], Haidt is presented less as a simple anti-phone campaigner than as a policy translator trying to separate child protection from internet censorship. The episode summarizes his agenda as delaying smartphones and social-media accounts, making schools phone-free, and restoring real-world independence for children.

His role in the source is to define institutional boundaries: social-media account creation is treated as a data contract with a platform, age checks should disclose only the minimum needed eligibility signal, and regulation should focus on platform design rather than government judgment about lawful speech.

## Key Characteristics
- Public advocate connecting youth mental health, smartphones, social media, and childhood independence.
- Proposes age and institutional defaults rather than relying only on individual family discipline.
- Distinguishes restrictions on social-media accounts from restrictions on public internet access.
- Favors privacy-preserving age assurance over broad identity disclosure.
- Frames platform design as a regulatory target while warning against content censorship.
