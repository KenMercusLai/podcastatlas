# 孟佳 / Meng Jia

## Overview
孟佳 is a singer and dancer whose participation in [[SistersWhoMakeWaves|《乘风破浪的姐姐》]] is used to examine professional persistence after a group career and a period of reduced recognition.

## Current Profile
The source says Meng continued singing-and-dancing practice through limited work, lost stage access, group dissolution, and a return to China. Program exposure helped audiences—and, in her own reported framing, her parents—understand what kind of performer she was. The hosts also emphasize her team contribution after elimination, making her a case of professional continuity joined to non-zero-sum support.

## Key Characteristics
- Maintained specialist performance skills during a career low point.
- Re-entered public attention through a concentrated competition format.
- Is portrayed as especially effective in team-based singing and dancing.
- Continued helping teammates even when her individual competition outcome worsened.
