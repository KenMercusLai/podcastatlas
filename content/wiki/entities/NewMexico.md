# New Mexico

New Mexico appears in [[tech-20260401-0401-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260401-0401-mp-tech-pod-128]] through a jury verdict finding [[Meta]] violated state law and misled consumers about child-safety guardrails in its apps. [[EricGoldman]] distinguishes the case from the Los Angeles verdict because the plaintiff was the state attorney general and the statutory issues were different.

Earlier Marketplace Tech coverage had already placed the New Mexico attorney-general case inside the broader [[SocialMediaProductLiability]] wave. [[tech-20260205-0205-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260205-0205-mp-tech-pod-128]] mentions it as part of the wider litigation landscape, while [[tech-20260327-0327-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260327-0327-mp-tech-pod-128]] says it raised product-change questions such as encrypted messages and real age verification.

[[kyiv-brawl-ukraines-top-general-fired-6a609aec4fac21e67f1be165]] adds a policing branch through [[Albuquerque]]. [[DanielKnowles]] uses the state as a recurring high-rate case for police killings, with [[DanielWilliamsACLU|Daniel Williams]] of [[ACLUOfNewMexico|ACLU of New Mexico]] linking the city case to a broader state-wide pattern.
