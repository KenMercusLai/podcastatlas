# Abraham Colby

## Overview

Abraham Colby is a formerly enslaved minister, organizer, and Georgia legislator whose testimony illustrates the first Klan's use of torture against Black voting and Republican organization.

## Current Profile

The source presents Colby as a target because political citizenship had become practical power. Klansmen dragged him from his home and whipped him for hours in front of his family; his later testimony identified prominent local attackers and linked their violence directly to his vote for Grant and organization of Black voters.

## Key Characteristics

- Born enslaved and later became a minister and political organizer.
- Elected to the Georgia House of Representatives during Reconstruction.
- Targeted for Republican voting and Black political mobilization.
- Survived prolonged violence but reported lasting physical and family consequences.
