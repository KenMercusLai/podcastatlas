# China Railway Corporation / 中国铁路总公司

China Railway Corporation is the post-2013 operating successor named in [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]]. After the [[MinistryOfRailwaysChina]] was dismantled, the episode says administrative supervision moved to a separate regulator while enterprise operation went to this railway company structure.

In the source, China Railway Corporation matters less as a company profile than as the institutional endpoint after [[LiuZhijun]]'s fall and the [[WenzhouTrainCollision]]. It later leads the [[ChineseStandardEMU]] effort that produces [[Fuxinghao]], turning the earlier mixed [[Hexiehao]] platforms into a more unified standard, component, and operating system.
