# 余英时 / Yu Yingshi

[[ritan-chuantai-hongloumeng-haishi-keyi-du-yidu-de-587167638]] mentions 余英时 as a recommended Redology scholar, especially through [[TwoWorldsOfRedChamberDream|《红楼梦的两个世界》]]. The recommendation appears in the episode's closing move from versions, sequels, and Redology schools back toward [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]] as literature.

In this source, Yu Yingshi is not used for a detailed scholarly summary. He marks a route for readers who want to think about [[RedChamberVersionAndSequelReading]], [[SuoYinReading|索隐式阅读]], and interpretation without reducing the novel to historical code or family archive.
