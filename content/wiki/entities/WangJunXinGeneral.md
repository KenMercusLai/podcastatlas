## Overview

王骏 / Wang Jun is a Xin-era frontier official whose current profile now spans two failed pressure points under [[WangMang|王莽]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-640-qipa-wangmang-ruhe-chedi-bifan-xiongnu-lhxbx6keibmfe8z6bcsmlpnr6eht|Hanji 640]] shows him in the Xiongnu seal mission where Xin symbolic downgrading angers the chanyu. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-652-wangmang-de-waike-shoushu-ltdiqldskazy-cb6igdmrv5huxm9|Hanji 652]] later shows him as the Wuwei general whose Western Regions mission collapses after [[YanqiKingdomXin|焉耆国]] ambushes him and allied troops defect.

## Current Profile

Hanji 640 adds Wang Jun's earlier Xiongnu diplomacy layer. After the chanyu finds that the old Xiongnu seal's "玺" has become "章," he demands the old seal back from Wang Jun and the Xin envoys. They can only show that the old seal has already been smashed and explain Wang Mang's mandate logic. The episode uses the exchange to show how Wang Jun's mission transmits [[SymbolicStatusDowngradeBackfire|symbolic status downgrade]] rather than repairing relations.

Hanji 652 then places Wang Jun inside [[XinDynasty|新朝]] Western Regions failure. Wang Mang sends him west together with [[LiChongXinProtector|李崇]] and [[GuoQingXinGeneral|郭清]]. Western Regions states initially come out to welcome the mission and supply labor and grain, but Wang Jun tries to attack them instead of sustaining the diplomatic setting.

The later result is a compact command-failure case. Yanqi pretends to submit while preparing troops; Wang Jun marches with forces from Shache and [[QiuciKingdom|龟兹]], but after he enters Yanqi territory he is ambushed and killed while troops from other Western Regions states defect.

## Key Characteristics

- Xin general sent into the Western Regions under Wang Mang.
- Earlier Xiongnu-mission figure who carries Wang Mang's seal downgrade into direct chanyu anger.
- Represents coercive frontier posture after initial local welcome and supply.
- Fails when Yanqi uses false submission and ambush.
- His death exposes the fragility of Xin authority beyond inner commanderies.
- Current profile is bounded to Xiongnu seal diplomacy and one failed Western Regions expedition.

## Qualifications

The sources give Wang Jun as a frontier failure figure, not a full career biography. Hanji 640 supports the Xiongnu seal-mission role but keeps the full envoy hierarchy, Chen Rao's exact relationship to Wang Jun, and Wang Jun's own inner judgment source-scoped. Hanji 652 supports the Western Regions expedition but keeps exact office history, troop numbers, Shache participation, and responsibility relative to Wang Mang's orders source-scoped.
