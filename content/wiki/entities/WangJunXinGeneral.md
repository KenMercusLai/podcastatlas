## Overview

王骏 / Wang Jun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-652-wangmang-de-waike-shoushu-ltdiqldskazy-cb6igdmrv5huxm9|Hanji 652]] as the Xin-era Wuwei general whose Western Regions mission collapses after [[YanqiKingdomXin|焉耆国]] ambushes him and allied troops defect.

## Current Profile

Hanji 652 places Wang Jun inside [[XinDynasty|新朝]] Western Regions failure. Wang Mang sends him west together with [[LiChongXinProtector|李崇]] and [[GuoQingXinGeneral|郭清]]. Western Regions states initially come out to welcome the mission and supply labor and grain, but Wang Jun tries to attack them instead of sustaining the diplomatic setting.

The result is a compact command-failure case. Yanqi pretends to submit while preparing troops; Wang Jun marches with forces from Shache and [[QiuciKingdom|龟兹]], but after he enters Yanqi territory he is ambushed and killed while troops from other Western Regions states defect.

## Key Characteristics

- Xin general sent into the Western Regions under Wang Mang.
- Represents coercive frontier posture after initial local welcome and supply.
- Fails when Yanqi uses false submission and ambush.
- His death exposes the fragility of Xin authority beyond inner commanderies.
- Current profile is bounded to one failed expedition.
