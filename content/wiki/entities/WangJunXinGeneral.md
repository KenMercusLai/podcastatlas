## Overview

王骏 / Wang Jun is a Xin-era frontier official whose current profile spans two failed pressure points under [[WangMang|王莽]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-639-zhengshou-suodeshui-di-yi-ren-shi-zhewei-zuzong-ljwyj6k7u0zpegj74rbwbdgkzrcq|Hanji 639]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-640-qipa-wangmang-ruhe-chedi-bifan-xiongnu-lhxbx6keibmfe8z6bcsmlpnr6eht|Hanji 640]] show him in the Xiongnu seal mission where concealment and destruction turn Xin symbolic downgrading into chanyu anger. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-652-wangmang-de-waike-shoushu-ltdiqldskazy-cb6igdmrv5huxm9|Hanji 652]] later shows him as the Wuwei general whose Western Regions mission collapses after [[YanqiKingdomXin|焉耆国]] ambushes him and allied troops defect.

## Current Profile

Hanji 639 adds the first Xiongnu diplomacy layer. Wang Jun and the Xin envoys give the chanyu valuables, announce the dynastic transfer, and obtain the old “匈奴单于玺” without explaining that the replacement says “新匈奴单于章.” After concern that the wording will be discovered, [[ChenRaoXinEnvoy|陈饶]] smashes the old seal. Hanji 640 supplies the reaction: the chanyu demands it back, but the envoys can only show the fragments and explain Wang Mang's mandate logic. The sequence shows Wang Jun's mission transmitting [[SymbolicStatusDowngradeBackfire|symbolic status downgrade]] rather than repairing relations.

Hanji 652 then places Wang Jun inside [[XinDynasty|新朝]] Western Regions failure. Wang Mang sends him west together with [[LiChongXinProtector|李崇]] and [[GuoQingXinGeneral|郭清]]. Western Regions states initially come out to welcome the mission and supply labor and grain, but Wang Jun tries to attack them instead of sustaining the diplomatic setting.

The later result is a compact command-failure case. Yanqi pretends to submit while preparing troops; Wang Jun marches with forces from Shache and [[QiuciKingdom|龟兹]], but after he enters Yanqi territory he is ambushed and killed while troops from other Western Regions states defect.

## Key Characteristics

- Xin general sent into the Western Regions under Wang Mang.
- Earlier Xiongnu-mission figure who obtains the old seal through incomplete disclosure before Chen Rao destroys it.
- Represents coercive frontier posture after initial local welcome and supply.
- Fails when Yanqi uses false submission and ambush.
- His death exposes the fragility of Xin authority beyond inner commanderies.
- Current profile is bounded to Xiongnu seal diplomacy and one failed Western Regions expedition.

## Qualifications

The sources give Wang Jun as a frontier failure figure, not a full career biography. Hanji 639 calls him the son of [[WangJiWesternHan|王吉]] and connects him with the Western Han “three Wangs,” but the wiki does not merge him with [[WangJunWesternHan|王吉's Western Han official son]] because that figure belongs to an earlier chronology. Hanji 639-640 keep the full envoy hierarchy, Chen Rao's exact relationship to Wang Jun, and Wang Jun's own inner judgment source-scoped. Hanji 652 supports the Western Regions expedition but keeps exact office history, troop numbers, Shache participation, and responsibility relative to Wang Mang's orders source-scoped.
