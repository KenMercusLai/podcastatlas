# 王陵母亲 / Mother of Wang Ling

王陵母亲 / Mother of Wang Ling enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-148-2-peng-le-wangling-muqin-xiangyu-sharen-zhuxin-ljyv8bow4rj1l5ths3znx2evgvtm|Hanji 148-2]] as the unnamed mother of [[WangLingHanMinister|王陵]]. [[XiangYu|项羽]] captures her and places her in camp as a way to pull Wang Ling away from [[LiuBang|刘邦]].

The source's key scene is deliberately staged. When Wang Ling's envoy arrives, Xiang Yu gives her an honored east-facing seat, making the captivity look like respect and signaling that Wang Ling could still join Chu without family disgrace. The formal courtesy is therefore part of the pressure mechanism, not merely kindness.

She breaks that mechanism by acting before the hostage bargain can mature. In private, she tells the envoy to have Wang Ling serve the Han king well, calls Liu Bang a broad and generous elder who will gain the world, and asks Wang Ling not to hold two hearts because of her. She then kills herself with a sword, removing herself as usable leverage.

Xiang Yu's reaction completes the backfire in the episode's reading. By cooking her corpse after the suicide, he turns a failed coercion attempt into a visible cruelty marker. The source says this pushes Wang Ling completely toward Liu Bang and makes her death part of the larger pattern where Xiang Yu's violence creates enemies rather than stable obedience.
