# 向兰欣 / Xiang Lanxin

## Overview

向兰欣 / Xiang Lanxin is an international-relations scholar whose episode profile combines transatlantic training, multi-country archival history, encounters with senior strategists, and a realist interpretation of U.S.-China-European relations.

## Current Profile

Xiang presents his refusal of a conventional U.S. China-studies track as methodologically formative. European languages and transatlantic history let him compare national archives, question the smooth image of the Anglo-American “special relationship,” and reconstruct the Boxer War through [[MultiArchiveConflictHistory]] rather than a single national narrative.

His contemporary analysis favors balance, responsibility transfer, material capability, and mutual security perception over ideological regime-change frames. That produces useful concepts—[[PerformanceBasedCoerciveBargaining]], [[GreatPowerTriangularBalance]], and [[PerceptionDrivenAccidentalWar]]—but the episode also contains forecasts and personal recollections that require independent corroboration.

## Key Characteristics

- Chose European and transatlantic history instead of an assigned China-studies path.
- Uses multiple national archives to compare state narratives and conflict perceptions.
- Interprets current politics through realism, strategic balance, capability, and bargaining.
- Connects personal encounters with [[RobertSkidelsky]], [[HenryKissinger]], [[AndrewMarshall]], and [[MichaelPillsbury]] to intellectual history.
- Distinguishes “not intending war” from having institutions and signals capable of preventing it.
- Treats Chinese institutional learning as selective rather than simple Western copying or rejection.

## Qualifications

This profile is based on one long autobiographical interview. Career details, private conversations, policy influence, simulations, forecasts, and causal claims about governments or wars are episode-reported rather than independently verified. The episode title uses “相蓝欣,” but the body consistently identifies the guest as 向兰欣.
