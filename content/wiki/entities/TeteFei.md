# 特特飞

特特飞 appears in [[vol-267-ruhe-xianchang-kan-shijiebei-bing-da-kui-yi-bi-feat-yingyan-shijian-1002986145]] as the [[YingyanShijian|鹰眼时间]] speaker who visited multiple 2026 [[FIFAWorldCup|FIFA World Cup]] host cities in the [[UnitedStates]] and [[Mexico]]. His first-person examples give the episode its fan-side evidence: expensive tickets, paid fan activities, parking and beverage prices, official merchandise, and the feeling that every part of the live-event journey can become a charged surface.

His most concrete contribution is the ticket-resale loss story. The source says he bought a Mexico group-stage ticket for about $2,400, then faced falling demand after Mexico secured first place early; after listing it at about $1,750 and paying the 15% seller fee, he lost more than $900. The episode uses that case to make [[OfficialSportsTicketResale]] a market-structure problem rather than only an anecdote about one fan's bad timing.
