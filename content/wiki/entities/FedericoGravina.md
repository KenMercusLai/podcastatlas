# Federico Gravina

## Overview

Federico Gravina is presented as the Sicilian-born Spanish admiral who led Spain's contingent in the combined French-Spanish fleet during the closing phase of the [[TrafalgarCampaign|Trafalgar campaign]].

## Current Profile

Gravina commanded within an alliance whose governments and officers did not share the same incentives. Spanish commanders had been warned against following the French toward the Channel, while disease, manpower shortages, British pressure, and French demands constrained their choices. The episode argues that [[HoratioNelson|Nelson]]'s concealment of British strength also activated honor: remaining in Cadiz against an apparently weaker enemy would have been difficult for Gravina and his officers to defend.

## Key Characteristics

- Sicilian-born aristocrat serving as a senior Spanish naval commander.
- Leader of the Spanish contingent in Villeneuve's combined fleet.
- Operated under conflicting French demands and Spanish government preferences.
- Faced manpower, disease, supply, and coalition-command constraints at Cadiz.
- Participated in a decision to sail shaped partly by honor and the apparent balance of forces.

## Qualifications

The episode provides only a campaign-level sketch of Gravina. His own reasoning, the internal Spanish command debate, exact government orders, and the relative weight of honor, logistics, and French pressure require additional evidence.
