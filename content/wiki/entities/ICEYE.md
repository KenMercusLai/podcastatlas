# ICEYE

## Overview
ICEYE is a Finnish commercial operator of small radar satellites and an example of Europe closing selected surveillance gaps through faster commercial procurement.

## Current Profile
The source says ICEYE operates roughly 80 small radar satellites able to observe Earth and serves customers in Europe and the United States. Sweden's accelerated military-satellite procurement illustrates how buying commercial technology can shorten timelines that traditional national or multinational programs might stretch toward 2030.

## Key Characteristics
- Finnish commercial space company focused on radar Earth observation.
- Operator of an episode-reported fleet of roughly 80 small satellites.
- Supplier to European and American customers.
- Example of commercial procurement accelerating selected sovereign surveillance capability.

## Evidence
- Fleet and service: [[songs-of-disenchantment-the-sorrow-of-nigeria-6275426999307c5f9d5c7073a5b16e0d]] describes ICEYE's radar constellation and customer base.
- Procurement model: [[songs-of-disenchantment-the-sorrow-of-nigeria-6275426999307c5f9d5c7073a5b16e0d]] says Sweden accelerated military satellite plans by purchasing technology from ICEYE and other providers.

## Qualifications
The episode does not independently audit fleet availability, sensor performance, revisit rates, contracts, wartime survivability, or the degree of Swedish control. Commercial purchasing can close a narrow gap without providing complete space autonomy.
