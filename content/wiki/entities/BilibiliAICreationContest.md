# B站 AI 创作大赛 / Bilibili AI Creation Contest

## Overview
B站 AI 创作大赛 is the [[Bilibili|B站]] creator contest that frames the No.227 三五环 discussion of AI filmmaking.

## Current Profile
The source presents the contest as a visible platform signal for AI-generated video creation in China. It reports a 3 million RMB prize pool, 77 awards, and broad participation, then uses the experience of [[JiaoshouYiXiaoxing|叫兽易小星]], [[LiChenFenmo|李晨]], [[FenmoTeam|粉墨]], and [[NvwaZhiSi|《女娲之死》]] to ask what current AI-video tools can and cannot do.

For the wiki, the contest matters less as a complete event history than as a forcing function. It concentrates practitioner evidence about visual spectacle, story quality, AI-video workflow, audience expectations, tool choice, and industry anxiety. The episode suggests that contests can make AI-video adoption legible while also exposing the difference between technical novelty and durable creative work.

## Key Characteristics
- Platform-hosted AI creator contest associated in the source with [[Bilibili|B站]].
- Reported as having a 3 million RMB prize pool and 77 awards.
- Provides the contest setting for [[NvwaZhiSi|《女娲之死》]] winning third prize.
- Reveals a current gap between visual AI spectacle and consistently strong story or emotional expression.
- Acts as a platform-level signal that AI video is moving from demo novelty into creator workflows.
