# Juan Pablo Gospino-Gomez

Juan Pablo Gospino-Gomez appears in [[currency-chaos-in-argentina-summer-school]] as an [[Argentina]] worker managing inflation through multiple jobs and recurring pay adjustments. The episode says he drives Uber on weekends, sells Natura products, and works customer service for brands including Oreo and Milka.

His story connects inflation to labor routines. Regular raises help preserve living standards when monthly inflation is high, but the episode also warns that nominal adjustments can feed the wider inflation process when government spending is financed by money printing.
