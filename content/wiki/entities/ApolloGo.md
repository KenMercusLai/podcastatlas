# Apollo Go / 萝卜快跑

## Overview
Apollo Go is [[Baidu]]'s robotaxi service, presented in the source through its first reported European testing program.

## Current Profile
The episode says Apollo Go supplies vehicles and autonomous-driving technology for a London test with Lyft, while the local passenger and fleet interface would run through FreeNow. The structure separates the technology layer from local market operations and begins with safety drivers before a planned driverless commercial phase.

## Key Characteristics
- Baidu-owned robotaxi service and technology platform.
- Uses a local ride-hailing partnership for foreign passenger and fleet operations.
- Enters a market where other autonomous-driving developers are already active.
