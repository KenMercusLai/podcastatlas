# Ever Victorious Army

## Overview

The Ever Victorious Army was a small, mixed, foreign-officered force raised by European commercial interests around Shanghai to fight the [[TaipingRebellion|Taiping Rebellion]] and later commanded by [[CharlesGeorgeGordon|General Gordon]].

## Current Profile

Despite its grand name, the episode describes the force as relatively small and ragtag. Merchants organized it because Taiping advances threatened Shanghai; after earlier commanders failed or died, British authorities supplied Gordon even though he had never commanded an army in battle.

Under Gordon, engineering knowledge and map-making suited the canal-and-river terrain. Strict discipline, a ban on hard liquor, reduced personal pay, gifts to troops, and an unarmed public style helped create authority. The force contributed to a rapid sequence of victories and Gordon's fame, but the episode cautions that its role may have been magnified relative to the much larger Qing effort.

## Key Characteristics

- Merchant-backed force formed to defend Shanghai-area commercial and political interests.
- Small and mixed formation whose reputation exceeded its scale.
- Dependent under Gordon on engineering, mapping, waterways, and strict discipline.
- Vehicle for Gordon's austere, fearless, and theatrical command style.
- Significant participant whose share of the Qing victory remains open to qualification.
