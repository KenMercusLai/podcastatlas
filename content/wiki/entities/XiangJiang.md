## Overview

襄疆 / Xiang Jiang is presented by Qinji 128-4 as a claimed descendant of the Chu royal house whom [[GeYing|葛婴]] installs as king during the early spread of the anti-Qin revolt.

## Current Profile

Xiang Jiang's importance in the current evidence is positional rather than biographical. His enthronement gives Ge Ying's detached force a Chu claimant before news of [[ChenSheng|陈胜]]'s own kingship reaches it. Ge Ying then kills Xiang Jiang in an attempt to remove the rival title, but the act does not save Ge Ying from execution.

## Key Characteristics

- Claimed Chu royal descendant in a rapidly fragmenting anti-Qin field.
- Rival kingship focus created by a commander operating away from the rebel center.
- Victim of Ge Ying's failed attempt to reconcile competing authority claims.
