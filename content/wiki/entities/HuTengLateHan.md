## Overview

胡腾 / Hu Teng is a late-Han figure introduced by [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-888-luanshi-zhizhong-zhijing-bupasi-de-ren-ll7s9e5bh8reuy4xzzjwq0psowvq|Hanji 888]] as an old subordinate in [[DouWuLateHan|Dou Wu]]'s great-general office. After Dou Wu's death, Hu Teng buries and mourns him, then hides Dou Wu's young grandson.

## Current Profile

Hu Teng's profile centers on retainer loyalty after political collapse. He does not simply avoid the defeated patron's name; he gives Dou Wu burial and public mourning, accepting that this destroys his eligibility for office. The source then makes the loyalty more concrete by having him protect Dou Wu's two-year-old grandson as if the child were his own son.

The hiding place is [[LinglingCommanderyLateHan|Lingling Commandery]], where Zhang Chang helps keep the child safe. Because the grandson and Zhang Chang remain thinly identified in the source note, this page keeps them source-scoped while making Hu Teng a canonical route for the episode's orphan-protection branch.

## Key Characteristics

- Hu Teng is a former subordinate of Dou Wu in the great-general office.
- His first defining act is burial and public mourning for a politically defeated patron.
- He accepts loss of official eligibility as the price of honoring Dou Wu.
- He later hides Dou Wu's two-year-old grandson as his own son.
- His loyalty depends on long-term concealment and helper trust rather than public argument.
