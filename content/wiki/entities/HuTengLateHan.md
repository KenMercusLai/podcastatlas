# 胡腾 / Hu Teng (late Han)

## Overview

胡腾 / Hu Teng is a late-Han figure whose wiki profile now has an earlier administrative layer and a later loyalty layer. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-863-yi-ba-huo-shaochu-de-zhandouli-ljpfmj8pphunruioce64ywjk7yhb|Hanji 863]] presents him as a Guiyang man and escort official during [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]'s southern tour, where he strengthens discipline over the traveling entourage. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-888-luanshi-zhizhong-zhijing-bupasi-de-ren-ll7s9e5bh8reuy4xzzjwq0psowvq|Hanji 888]] later presents him as an old subordinate in [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]]'s great-general office who buries Dou Wu and hides Dou Wu's young grandson.

## Current Profile

Hu Teng's earlier profile is practical rather than martyr-like. Hanji 863 places him in the imperial-tour field, where Huan's entourage creates heavy local burden and discipline problems. Hu Teng proposes raising the Jingzhou inspector's treatment to the level of the Sili xiaowei and taking a supervisory role himself, so that the moving court can be controlled more strictly. Huan approves, and discipline becomes firmer.

Hanji 888 centers retainer loyalty after political collapse. Hu Teng does not simply avoid the defeated patron's name; he gives Dou Wu burial and public mourning, accepting that this destroys his eligibility for office. The source then makes the loyalty more concrete by having him protect Dou Wu's two-year-old grandson as if the child were his own son.

The hiding place is [[LinglingCommanderyLateHan|Lingling Commandery]], where Zhang Chang helps keep the child safe. Because the grandson and Zhang Chang remain thinly identified in the source note, this page keeps them source-scoped while making Hu Teng a canonical route for the episode's orphan-protection branch.

## Key Characteristics

- Hu Teng is introduced as a Guiyang man and escort official during Huan's southern tour.
- His administrative role is to strengthen discipline over a burdensome imperial retinue by clarifying inspection authority.
- He is later a former subordinate of Dou Wu in the great-general office.
- His loyalty includes burial and public mourning for a politically defeated patron despite the loss of official eligibility.
- He later hides Dou Wu's two-year-old grandson as his own son through long-term concealment and helper trust.

## Qualifications

- The page now combines Hanji 863's earlier escort-official role with Hanji 888's later Dou Wu loyalty branch, but it does not reconstruct Hu Teng's full career between those moments.
- Hanji 863 reports improved discipline after Hu Teng's proposal without measuring duration or downstream effects.
- Zhang Chang is kept source-scoped because the episode gives too little evidence for a stable canonical route.
- Dou Wu's grandson is not named in the source note, so the page records the protected child without inventing an identity.

## What Changed

- Created the page from Hanji 888's account of Hu Teng burying Dou Wu and protecting Dou Wu's grandson.
- Added Hanji 863 as Hu Teng's earlier imperial-tour discipline and Jingzhou inspection-authority layer.
