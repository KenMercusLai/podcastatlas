## Overview

胡三省 / Hu Sanxing enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-103-1-simaqian-kanzhong-de-xinlingjun-suan-shi-xiaoren-ma-lvty1pdnibvqpk0m16kkxzw4t-ck|Qinji 103-1]] as the later commentator on [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] who criticizes the [[GuanchengWarringStates|管城]] episode. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-521-guanchang-li-de-zhaogu-shi-ruhe-toutou-jinxing-de-lmxkflbc1avst9k2zh06n02noonr|Hanji 521]] adds him as the annotator whose short caution about [[XueXuanWesternHan|薛宣]] keeps a seemingly upright official statement from being read only at face value.

## Current Profile

Hu's current wiki role is not as a full Song biography, but as an interpretive pressure point inside Ruiqi's Zizhi Tongjian readings. Qinji 103-1 uses him to prevent the Guancheng episode from resolving into sentimental praise of suicide or simple condemnation of coercion. He criticizes [[SuGao|苏高]]'s son for defending a Qin-held city against Wei, criticizes Su Gao and [[AnlingJun|安陵君]] for treating family and local duty as if [[WeiState|魏国]]'s survival were not at stake, and criticizes [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] for apologizing when a commander was doing what the state emergency required.

Hanji 521 uses him differently. His note on Xue Xuan's "law as teacher" answer does not prove a hidden action, but it marks the gap between accepted public rhetoric and possible political reality. In this source, Hu functions as a guide to reading silence, praise, and non-action as evidence that may require caution.

## Key Characteristics

- Later commentator on [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] used by the wiki as an interpretive authority.
- Pushes beyond face-value moral readings toward institutional and state-responsibility analysis.
- In Guancheng, criticizes multiple sides rather than choosing a simple hero-villain frame.
- In Xue Xuan's case, flags that public agreement with an official's words may not settle what actually happened.

## Qualifications
