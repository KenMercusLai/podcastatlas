# Nevada Bristlecone Preserve

## Overview
The Nevada Bristlecone Preserve is the [[LongNow]]-stewarded site in [[02026-heggli-keats-csank-podcast-v2]] where ancient bristlecone pines, environmental instruments, scientists, artists, and visitors meet.

## Current Profile
The preserve functions as both a protected living archive and an observatory across timescales. Bristlecone rings retain millennial environmental responses, [[NevCAN]] records present conditions every ten minutes, and [[JonathanKeats]]'s calendar project asks people to treat variable living growth as timekeeping rather than noise around a uniform clock.

## Key Characteristics
- Long Now has stewarded the preserve since 2001 after considering the area for a monumental 10,000-year clock.
- Its bristlecone pines are among Earth's oldest living organisms and preserve thousands of years of environmental evidence.
- The site joins conservation, direct monitoring, paleoclimate interpretation, public art, and long-horizon institutional responsibility.
- It provides elevation contrasts through which current warmer sites can inform questions about future conditions at higher sites.
- Its records are fragile: a tree or an unbroken measurement series can be lost far faster than it can be recreated.

## Evidence
### Living Archive
- [[02026-heggli-keats-csank-podcast-v2]] presents bristlecone rings as records of temperature, moisture, frost, volcanic effects, and solar events across millennia.

### Instrumented Observatory
- [[02026-heggli-keats-csank-podcast-v2]] places the NEVCAN transect and bristlecone physiology measurements in a shared program for linking current weather to tree growth.

### Public Deep-Time Site
- [[02026-heggli-keats-csank-podcast-v2]] connects the preserve to [[CenturiesOfTheBristlecone]] and a public municipal clock intended to carry the trees' variable time into Reno.

## Qualifications
The episode does not provide detailed preserve boundaries, legal status, visitor management, conservation protocol, or a durable multigenerational funding and governance plan. The site's long-term scientific and artistic role therefore depends on arrangements that the source describes as necessary but not settled.

## What Changed
- Established the preserve as a combined living archive, instrumented observatory, and public deep-time setting.
- Added fragility and stewardship continuity as core features of the site rather than background logistics.
