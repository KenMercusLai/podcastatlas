# Anatoly Dyatlov / 加特洛夫

[[AnatolyDyatlov|Anatoly Dyatlov / 加特洛夫]] is the deputy chief engineer figure emphasized in [[04-qieernuobeili-de-wuye-woyao-jin-jianyu-le-530594843]]. The episode describes him as experienced, stubborn, and resistant to contradiction during the pre-maintenance test that was delayed into the night.

The source uses Dyatlov to hold two ideas together. His command style and test decisions mattered, but the accident also exposed [[HumanDependentSafetySystem|a safety system too dependent on operators]] and design knowledge they did not fully control. His later insistence that design flaws were central becomes part of [[PostDisasterTruthAndResponsibility]] rather than a simple excuse.

Dyatlov is therefore a responsibility node, not just an antagonist. The episode's account leaves room for both operational blame and [[TechnologicalDisasterAsInstitutionalFailure]].
