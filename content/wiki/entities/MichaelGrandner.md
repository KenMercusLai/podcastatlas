# Michael Grandner

Michael Grandner is the University of Arizona sleep expert quoted in [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]]. The episode uses him to warn listeners not to obsess over one ideal sleep number.

Grandner's main contribution is practical interpretation. He says different body systems may need different amounts of sleep to function optimally, and that duration is only one dimension beside quality, regularity, and continuity. His advice supports [[PopulationLevelHealthAdvice]] and [[SleepAnxietyLoop]] caution more than precise optimization.
