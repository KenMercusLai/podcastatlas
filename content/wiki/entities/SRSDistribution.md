# SRS Distribution

## Overview

SRS Distribution is the pro-contractor distribution company that [[HomeDepot|Home Depot]] bought in 2024, according to [[home-depot-1]], as the largest acquisition in Home Depot's history.

## Current Profile

The episode uses SRS as evidence that Home Depot's modern growth is not only about stores. SRS extends the company toward roofers, landscapers, pool contractors, and other trades through bulk job-site delivery outside the traditional retail aisle.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves professional trade customers rather than casual DIY shoppers.
- Supports bulk and job-site delivery use cases.
- Represents the later, more focused version of Home Depot's pro-distribution ambitions.

## Evidence
- [[home-depot-1]] says Home Depot bought SRS in 2024 for $18.25 billion.
- [[home-depot-1]] says SRS serves roofers, landscapers, pool contractors, and other professional trades.
- [[home-depot-1]] distinguishes the SRS-era pro strategy from [[BobNardelli]]'s earlier adjacency push by emphasizing timing, focus, and execution.
