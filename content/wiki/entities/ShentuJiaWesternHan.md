## Overview

申屠嘉 / Shentu Jia is a Western Han founding-era veteran and chancellor whose profile begins with selection by [[LiuHeng|汉文帝刘恒]] as a probity-coded alternative to an outer-relative candidate and ends with an unsuccessful attempt to prosecute [[ChaoCuoWesternHan|晁错]] early in [[HanJingdi|汉景帝]]'s reign.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-227-he-rennai-xuming-baisui-yang-qiqie-bairen-ta-shi-shui-liu5ugz2bds9e0rgl9xytk0a4pvj|Hanji 227]] gives the appointment prequel. After [[ZhangCang|张苍]] leaves office, Liu Heng reportedly considers [[DouGuangguoWesternHan|窦广国]] but rejects the visible favoritism risk of appointing the empress's brother. He chooses Shentu Jia instead. The episode frames Shentu Jia through service under Liu Bang, military achievement,廉洁耿直 conduct, and refusal to receive private visitors at home; these details explain the selection logic but remain source-attributed.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-230-shentujia-yu-sha-chaocuo-weihe-fan-bei-qisi-ltwm5zfpdau5p-lmsnnr8wynfzqj|Hanji 230]] places Shentu Jia among the officials who establish Gaozu's and Emperor Wen's ancestral titles at the start of Jingdi's reign. His conflict with Chao grows from unequal access as well as policy disagreement: Chao repeatedly receives private audiences, changes rules with the emperor's support, and discusses reducing the royal kingdoms, while the chancellor's advice is rejected.

Shentu Jia then tries to use Chao's opening of a southern passage through a wall associated with the Former Emperor's temple precinct as a capital case. Chao learns of the plan and reaches Jingdi overnight. At court, Jingdi says the wall enclosed unused outer ground and that he authorized the work, leaving Shentu Jia unable to carry the impeachment forward.

The episode reports that Shentu Jia regrets not killing Chao before presenting the memorial, returns home, and dies after vomiting blood in anger. The durable judgment is political rather than medical: his office and legal charge cannot overcome prior access, admission, and ruler sponsorship.

## Key Characteristics

- Founding-era veteran selected after Liu Heng declines an outer-relative candidate.
- Chancellor presented as personally upright and closed to private household lobbying.
- Senior official whose advice loses ground as Chao Cuo gains repeated private access.
- Rival who converts a temple-precinct wall breach into a proposed capital prosecution.
- Prosecutor who loses the timing contest when Chao reaches the emperor first.
- Minister whose formal authority is defeated by the ruler's sponsorship of the accused.

## Qualifications

This is a two-source profile, not a complete biography. Hanji 227 does not independently establish Shentu Jia's archery role, campaigns, rewards, character, household practice, appointment date, or Liu Heng's private comparison of candidates. Hanji 230 leaves the ancestral-title process, broader advice, hostility toward Chao, wall and temple geography, applicable law, authority to execute before memorializing, leaked information, court speeches, emotional state, and medical cause of death episode-attributed. The page supports an access-and-patronage explanation for the failed impeachment, not a general rule that formal office is always powerless against favorites.
