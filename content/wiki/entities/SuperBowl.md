# Super Bowl

The Super Bowl is the championship event in [[the-nfl-1]] that turned the NFL-AFL merger into a national media spectacle. The episode presents the early championship game as both a competitive test between the [[NationalFootballLeague|NFL]] and [[AmericanFootballLeague|AFL]] and a deliberately packaged event for broadcasters, sponsors, reporters, and fans.

[[PeteRozelle]] built media week, partner events, press conferences, and promotion around the game. [[JoeNamath]]'s Super Bowl III guarantee and the Jets' upset then gave the merged league a stronger story because the AFL was no longer merely a weak rival.
