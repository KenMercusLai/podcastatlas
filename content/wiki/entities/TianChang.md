# 田常 / Tian Chang

田常 / Tian Chang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-57-gudai-fa-dushi-haiyou-zhe-zuoyong-lj6wi61osaytowgmdp8dtkrwb-si]] as the [[QiState|齐国]] chief minister whom [[Zigong|子贡 / 端木赐]] persuades during the effort to save [[LuState|鲁国]]. The episode presents him as the first lever in Zigong's chain: if Tian Chang can be moved away from attacking weak Lu, the immediate danger to Lu can be displaced into a larger strategic field.

The page is source-scoped. Tian Chang's function in this telling is to show that successful rescue diplomacy begins by reading the decision-maker's incentives, not by pleading for mercy in the abstract.
