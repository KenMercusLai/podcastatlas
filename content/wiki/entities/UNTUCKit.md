# UNTUCKit

UNTUCKit is the apparel brand co-founded by [[ChrisRiccobono]] and [[AaronSanandres]] around a shorter button-down shirt designed to be worn untucked. In [[advice-line-with-chris-riccobono-of-untuckit-c087247f-1f85-4c15-b4aa-4ffea0368c4c]], [[GuyRaz]] uses the original story as the background for Riccobono's Advice Line role.

The source's update presents UNTUCKit as a mature direct-to-consumer apparel brand facing COVID-related debt, high interest rates, tariff pressure, and a source-scoped roughly $9 million cash impact from tariff and de minimis rule changes. The company is entering wholesale for the first time, making the case an extension of [[DirectToConsumerBrandControl]] into a stage where owned stores, e-commerce, and department stores have to coexist.
