# 周口店

周口店 enters the wiki through [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] as the site of the Peking Man discoveries. The episode narrates how earlier animal fossils, disputed teeth, international funding, and Chinese field persistence converged before [[PeiWenzhong|裴文中]] found the first skull.

The source makes 周口店 a test case for [[FieldworkAsKnowledgeMethod]] and [[ScholarshipInWartime]]. Discovery depends on repeated excavation and local presence, while later loss of the skulls during wartime shows how fragile physical evidence can be even after scientific achievement.

## Key Claims
- 周口店 links geology, paleontology, medicine, and archaeology in the episode.
- The site shows how field research can remain uncertain until the decisive find appears.
- The lost skulls make preservation and war risk part of the knowledge story.
