# Hal Hodson

Hal Hodson appears in [[elbows-up-again-a-us-canada-trade-flare-up-6a8c0fa3a89d26398ee7a5db]] as the [[TheIntelligence]] correspondent explaining the breakdown in [[UnitedStates]]-[[Canada]] trade talks. His segment frames [[MarkCarney]]'s position as a strategic trap: Canada needs to diversify away from the American market, but a tariff war with [[DonaldTrump]] makes the necessary investment harder to attract.

Hodson also supplies the domestic Canadian constraint map. In the source, [[Quebec]] has high tariff exposure and politically sensitive French-language protections, while [[Alberta]] has low tariff exposure but major oil leverage over the United States, making energy retaliation economically powerful and politically risky.
