# 阿里原初引力波探测站 / Ali Primordial Gravitational Wave Observatory

The Ali primordial gravitational-wave observatory is one of the main [[BigScienceInfrastructure|大科学基础设施]] cases in [[ep248-zai-gaoyuan-yu-dixia-tanxun-zhongguo-da-kexue-xiangmu-lidn7xxkcw4-66fw-y50fqqacwo]]. [[MiaoQian|苗千]] describes visiting the site at roughly 5,250 meters above sea level, where the dry high-altitude atmosphere helps observations of cosmic microwave background radiation.

The source frames the station as a [[WeakSignalDetection|weak-signal detection]] project. Its target is not a short burst like a black-hole merger; it searches for B-mode polarization patterns in the cosmic microwave background that may carry traces of primordial gravitational waves. That requires long data accumulation, statistical analysis, and careful separation of foreground contamination from possible cosmological signal.

The project also shows how [[ExtremeEnvironmentPhysics|extreme-environment physics]] becomes engineering. The detectors need cryogenic operation near 0.3K, the site must support oxygen, data rooms, clean spaces, and telescope structures at high altitude, and upgrades depend partly on semiconductor-device work discussed through [[ChinaSpallationNeutronSource|Dongguan]] and cleanroom fabrication.
