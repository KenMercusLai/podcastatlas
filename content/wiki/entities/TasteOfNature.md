# Taste of Nature

Taste of Nature is the family-built snack brand and business that preceded [[MadeGood]] in [[madegood-salma-and-nima-fotovat-lost-their-first-business-they-grew-their-next-one-into-a-snack-giant-cf5715bf-e9e0-45e0-a8a9-1256a7a4ed0d]]. The episode presents it as the first branded product chapter after the Fotovat family moved from nougat and co-packing into fruit-and-nut bars.

## Key Points
- The family relaunched Taste of Nature in 2008 after learning from co-packing work for other brands.
- Advisors and a design agency helped sharpen positioning, packaging, and shelf presence.
- By 2012, the business had more than $10 million in revenue and around 100 employees.
- Tensions with the father's partner over reinvestment, dividends, next-generation roles, values, and direction ended in a sudden buyout.
- Losing Taste of Nature supplied the negative governance lesson that pushed the family to avoid outside partners when creating [[RiversideNaturalFoods]].
