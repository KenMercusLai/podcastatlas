# 曲星 / Qu Xing (late Han)

曲星 / Qu Xing (late Han) enters through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-938-lishi-de-jingxing-ruanruo-zhihui-dailai-quru-lnlnrketgfbadhfjbzdjjtrmqfsh|Hanji 938]] as the Changsha rebel who proclaims himself general in winter 187 and commands more than ten thousand people in the episode's account. [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] responds by appointing [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] as Changsha governor.

The source's main interest in Qu Xing is the Sun Jian prelude. Sun Jian suppresses the uprising and receives the Wu Cheng marquis title, giving him an early local military-and-office branch before later episodes develop his anti-[[DongZhuo|董卓]] campaign, alliance with [[YuanShu|袁术]], and inherited title value for [[SunCe|孙策]].
