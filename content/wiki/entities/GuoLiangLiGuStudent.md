## Overview

郭亮 / Guo Liang is the young [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] student in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-830-donghan-ligu-wo-sui-si-yourong-lt-j7n9gotiw-negqxojoacf6ji|Hanji 830]] who first asks to recover Li Gu's body and then joins [[DongBanNanyangLateHan|董班]] in defying [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]]'s mourning ban.

## Current Profile

Hanji 830 presents Guo Liang after petition channels have already failed to save Li Gu. He carries a petition and punishment gear to the palace gate, asking to collect his teacher's body. The court refuses.

Guo Liang then goes with Dong Ban to the displayed body north of Luoyang. When threatened by the Xia Gate亭长, they say Li Gu's righteousness has moved them and they no longer count life and death. [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan|梁妠 / 梁太后]] pardons them after hearing of the act, and Guo Liang later withdraws from office with the other mourners.

## Key Characteristics

- Young student whose loyalty to Li Gu continues after prison death.
- First seeks formal permission to collect the body.
- Defies Liang Ji's public mourning ban after permission is denied.
- Accepts death risk alongside Dong Ban at the displayed corpse.
- Withdraws from official life after the burial sequence.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 830. It does not reconstruct Guo Liang's later life beyond the source's claim that he withdraws from official service.
