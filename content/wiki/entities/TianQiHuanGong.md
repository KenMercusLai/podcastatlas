# 田氏齐桓公 / Tian-family Qi Huan Gong

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-3-lsbx8l7wxeioae2lwuwkkkutpdwn]] records the immediate successor problem after [[TianHe|田和]] dies, saying Tian He's son succeeds. This page keeps that successor branch source-scoped under the existing Tian-family Qi Huan Gong node rather than creating a second page from the transcript's brief name notice.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-3-ltca-ikuzo9ej7a0lfrgwv2-wulb]] identifies this ruler more explicitly as 田武 / Tian Wu and treats the succession as violent. In the episode's account, Tian Wu kills his brother [[TianYan|田衍]], first installs Tian Yan's son [[GongziXiQi|公子喜]] as a puppet, then kills Gongzi Xi and rules directly. The same source gives Tian Wu a mixed legacy: he gains power through murder, but is also credited with stabilizing [[QiState|齐国]], supporting [[JixiaAcademy|稷下学宫]], and leaving the foundation that later helps [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] and [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]].

The episode also attaches the [[BianQue|扁鹊]] illness story to Tian Wu. In the wiki, that story is kept as a ruler-judgment caution rather than as a settled medical-history biography.

田氏齐桓公 / Tian-family Qi Huan Gong appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl|Zhouji 09]] as the Tian-family ruler of [[QiState|齐国]] who dies in the same broad period when [[QiKangGong|齐康公]]'s death ends Jiang-line Qi. The source says his son [[TianYingqi|田英齐]] succeeds him.

This page deliberately keeps the Tian-family title separate from the older [[QiHuanGong|齐桓公]] page. The matching posthumous title can otherwise collapse two different Qi branches: the Spring-Autumn Jiang-line hegemon and the later Tian-family ruler named in this episode.
