# 江城 / Jiang Cheng (Fund Manager)

Jiang Cheng appears in [[175-gongmu-jijin-erjibao-jizhi-de-baotuan-yu-gelie-zhihou-lv20uprxrcw0tde5ugohu29tmlg6]] through a Zhongtai Asset Management report that [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] reads as a coherent investment-framework example. The source uses him as a counterweight to both hot AI optimism and short-term performance chasing.

His framework emphasizes that investors cannot change the market and can only adjust their own process. He defines a framework as a combination of philosophy, market view, and strategy: what to do, why to do it, and where to do it.

## Key Claims
- Jiang Cheng's view prioritizes survival, long-term weighing, and enterprise durability over short-term prosperity.
- He prefers win rate over payoff odds, where certainty means being able to withstand forecast error rather than forecasting precisely.
- The source frames his fund positioning as cycle-crossing and adequate-return oriented rather than always-leading.
- For ordinary investors, his example connects [[PortfolioSuitability]] to temperament: if one cannot hold a framework calmly, speculative trades should stay in a smaller account.
