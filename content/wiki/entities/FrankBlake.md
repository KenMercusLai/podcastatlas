# Frank Blake

## Overview

Frank Blake is the [[HomeDepot|Home Depot]] CEO in [[home-depot-1]] credited with repairing the company's culture after [[BobNardelli]] while shifting growth toward productivity, capital allocation, and logistics.

## Current Profile

Blake's profile is the culturally sensitive turnaround leader. The episode emphasizes humility, reconnection with [[BernieMarcus]], compensation alignment, slower store growth, store productivity, distribution investment, and buybacks.

## Key Characteristics
- Reconnected Home Depot leadership with founder culture.
- Aligned most of his compensation with stock options.
- Stopped aggressive store expansion and emphasized same-store productivity.
- Invested in distribution centers and omnichannel fulfillment.
- Used the HD Supply sale and buybacks as a concentrated bet on the core business.

## Evidence
- [[home-depot-1]] says Blake became CEO after Nardelli despite skepticism because he also had GE experience.
- [[home-depot-1]] says his first moves included reconnecting with Marcus, reviving the inverted pyramid, and aligning compensation with stock.
- [[home-depot-1]] says he slowed store growth, sold HD Supply in 2007, bought back stock, and invested in rapid deployment centers.

## Qualifications

The source treats Blake favorably, but it also frames his success as partly dependent on the underlying strength of the core retail business and the timing of buybacks.
