# Alien: Romulus

Alien: Romulus is the later franchise case in [[the-real-horror-of-alien-and-how-it-explains-why-were-not-paid-enough]]. [[PlanetMoney]] highlights that its first spoken words are a workplace announcement and that [[FedeAlvarez]] intentionally puts labor dynamics at the center of the film.

The episode reads the film's opening colony as a [[CompanyTownLaborControl]] case. [[WeylandYutani]] controls mining and farming work, raises quotas, and prevents the protagonist Rain from leaving, turning [[LaborMonopsony]] from an abstract market structure into an administrative trap.
