# War of the Three Henrys

The War of the Three Henrys appears in [[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] as the renewed French civil-war phase after [[FrancoisDukeOfAnjou|Francois, Duke of Anjou]] died on 10 June 1584. His death left [[HenryIIIOfFrance|Henry III]] without an heir and made the Protestant [[HenryOfNavarre]] next in line.

The episode frames the conflict around three rival Henrys: Henry III as king, Henry of Navarre as Huguenot claimant, and [[HenryDukeOfGuise|Henry, Duke of Guise]] as the Catholic League leader. That structure matters to England because French instability weakens the French alternative to Spanish pressure.

For [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]], the war means [[France]] becomes less useful as a stable counterweight to [[Spain]] and less reliable as either ally or immediate invasion platform. The Anglo-Spanish path toward [[ConfessionalWarEscalation]] therefore becomes more direct after the [[ThrockmortonPlot]] and Mendoza's expulsion.
