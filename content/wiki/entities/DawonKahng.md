# Dawon Kahng / 姜大元

Dawon Kahng / 姜大元 is named in [[zhenzheng-gaibian-shijie-de-jishu-weishenme-yikaishi-dou-bu-bei-kanhao-s10e16-8c95b3dc-d75a-4bdd-84d4-2c06fd2d85b1]] as one of the [[BellLabs]] researchers associated with the early [[MOSFET|MOS transistor]]. The source pairs him with [[MuhammadAtalla]] in the story of a technology that was invented before its best industrial use case was obvious.

The episode says the early MOS transistor looked unattractive against [[BipolarJunctionTransistor|BJT]] devices on stability and speed. Its later advantage came from low cost, small size, and better fit with dense integration.
