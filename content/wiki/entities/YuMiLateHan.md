# 于糜 / Yu Mi (late Han)

于糜 / Yu Mi (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] as one of [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]]'s officers guarding [[HengjiangLateHan|横江]]. He is paired with [[FanNengLateHan|樊能]] after Liu Yao expels [[WuJingLateHan|吴景]] and [[SunBenLateHan|孙贲]] from [[QuALateHan|曲阿]].

The source uses Yu Mi as part of Liu Yao's border defense. His page is source-scoped: the episode does not develop his independent biography, but his placement helps show how Liu Yao converts fear of [[YuanShu|袁术]] and [[SunCe|孙策]] into a guarded river-crossing line.
