# Publius Sulpicius Rufus / 普布利乌斯·苏尔皮基乌斯·鲁弗斯

## Overview

Publius Sulpicius Rufus / 普布利乌斯·苏尔皮基乌斯·鲁弗斯 is the tribune whose citizenship bill, street following, and alliance with [[GaiusMarius]] turn Italian enfranchisement politics into the [[RepublicanCommandTransferCrisis]] against [[LuciusCorneliusSulla]].

## Current Profile

Episode 702 presents Sulpicius as a tribune with strong street support and a program for full citizenship for the Italian allies. His alliance with Marius is transactional: Marius supports Sulpicius's citizenship policy, while Sulpicius supports transferring the Mithridatic command from Sulla to Marius.

That bargain transforms a rights question into a coercive command dispute. Sulpicius reintroduces his bill, violence breaks out, Pompeius Rufus's son is killed, Sulla takes refuge in Marius's house, and a plebiscite strips Sulla of the eastern command.

## Key Characteristics

- Sulpicius uses tribunate authority and street organization around Italian citizenship.
- His alliance with Marius joins enfranchisement policy to military command ambition.
- His supporters help create a coercive urban environment in which Sulla is forced to yield.
- The plebiscite associated with his move transfers command from Sulla to Marius.
- He makes [[PopularisPolitics]] appear less like ordinary assembly politics and more like organized street power.

## Evidence

- Citizenship program: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] says Sulpicius supported full citizenship for the Italians.
- Street support: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] describes Sulpicius as having strong street support when he reintroduced his bill.
- Marius bargain: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] says Marius would support Sulpicius's citizenship policy while Sulpicius supported giving Marius the eastern command.
- Coercive escalation: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] describes rioting, the breaking up of Sulla and Pompeius Rufus's meeting, a murder, and Sulla's refuge in Marius's house.
- Command transfer: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] says the plebiscite removed Sulla from the Mithridatic command and transferred it to Marius.

## Qualifications

The source presents Sulpicius through the final command-crisis setup. It does not yet provide a full independent biography or settle how much of the violence should be attributed to his direction, his supporters, rival groups, or the wider Roman street-political field.

## What Changed

- Created Sulpicius as the tribune who links Italian citizenship politics to the transfer of Sulla's command.
