# Santiago Abascal

## Overview
Santiago Abascal is presented as the leader of Spain's populist-right Vox party and a potential coalition power broker after the source-dated general election.

## Current Profile
The episode places Abascal at the point where electoral arithmetic becomes policy bargaining. Vox's support could help produce a right-wing government, but its anti-immigration demands and emphasis on conflicts involving feminism and gay rights could also mobilize late opposition to its entry into office.

## Key Characteristics
- Leader of the populist-right Vox party.
- Potential coalition partner or parliamentary constraint on [[AlbertoNunezFeijoo]].
- Associated with tougher anti-immigration policy.
- Associated with culture-war conflict around feminism and gay rights.
- Source of both right-wing governing leverage and possible anti-Vox electoral mobilization.
