# 辛评 / Xin Ping (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] introduces Xin Ping as a Han Fu-side insider or old associate used in [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s mission to persuade [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] to yield [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]]. His placement matters because the surrender argument is not delivered only by visible outsiders.

Xin Ping's source role is therefore structural. He belongs to the insider channel that makes external pressure from Yuan Shao and [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] sound actionable inside Han Fu's own circle, alongside [[GaoGanLateHan|高干]], [[XunChenLateHan|荀谌]], and [[GuoTu|郭图]].

## Connections

- [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] - target whose trust network is used against him.
- [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] - beneficiary and province at stake.
- [[GaoGanLateHan|高干]], [[XunChenLateHan|荀谌]], and [[GuoTu|郭图]] - paired persuaders in the source.
- [[PsychologicalSurrenderTrap|心理迫降陷阱]] and [[InterestReframingPersuasion|利益重框式说服]] - persuasion frames sharpened by Xin Ping's role.
