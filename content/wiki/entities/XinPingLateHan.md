# 辛评 / Xin Ping (late Han)

## Overview
辛评 / Xin Ping (late Han) is a Yuan-side adviser whose bounded wiki role moves from insider persuasion in the Jizhou transfer to factional provocation in the [[YuanTan|袁谭]]-[[YuanShang|袁尚]] succession split. Hanji 1058 also makes him the elder brother of [[XinPiLateHan|辛毗]], Yuan Tan's later envoy to [[CaoCao|曹操]].

## Current Profile
Hanji 956 introduces Xin Ping as a [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]]-side insider or old associate used in [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s mission to persuade Han Fu to yield [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]]. His placement matters because the surrender argument is not delivered only by visible outsiders: Xin Ping helps make external pressure from Yuan Shao and [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] sound actionable inside Han Fu's own circle.

Hanji 1058 adds a later Yuan-family role. After Yuan Shao's non-designation leaves Yuan Tan and Yuan Shang in rivalry, Xin Ping appears with [[GuoTu|郭图]] telling Yuan Tan that [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] caused his succession disadvantage by arranging his adoption out to an elder line. The episode then identifies Xin Ping's younger brother Xin Pi as the envoy Yuan Tan sends to Cao Cao for aid after Yuan Shang besieges him.

## Key Characteristics
- Insider-channel persuader in Yuan Shao's acquisition of Jizhou.
- Yuan-side adviser whose role depends on access to a target's existing trust network.
- Factional grievance amplifier in Yuan Tan's post-Yuan Shao break with Yuan Shang.
- Kinship link to Xin Pi, who later carries Yuan Tan's request for Cao Cao's aid.
