# 辛评 / Xin Ping (late Han)

## Overview
辛评 / Xin Ping (late Han) is a Yuan-side adviser whose bounded wiki role moves from insider persuasion in the Jizhou transfer to factional provocation in the [[YuanTan|袁谭]]-[[YuanShang|袁尚]] succession split, then to family-victim grievance when [[YeLateHan|邺城]] falls. Hanji 1058 also makes him the elder brother of [[XinPiLateHan|辛毗]], Yuan Tan's later envoy to [[CaoCao|曹操]].

## Current Profile
Hanji 956 introduces Xin Ping as a [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]]-side insider or old associate used in [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s mission to persuade Han Fu to yield [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]]. His placement matters because the surrender argument is not delivered only by visible outsiders: Xin Ping helps make external pressure from Yuan Shao and [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] sound actionable inside Han Fu's own circle.

Hanji 1058 adds a later Yuan-family role. After Yuan Shao's non-designation leaves Yuan Tan and Yuan Shang in rivalry, Xin Ping appears with [[GuoTu|郭图]] telling Yuan Tan that [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] caused his succession disadvantage by arranging his adoption out to an elder line. The episode then identifies Xin Ping's younger brother Xin Pi as the envoy Yuan Tan sends to Cao Cao for aid after Yuan Shang besieges him.

Hanji 1063 adds the cost of factional violence at Ye's fall. Xin Ping's family has been imprisoned inside Ye, and when the city breaks he rushes in expecting to rescue them. He instead learns that Shen Pei has already ordered them killed, then confronts and whips the captured Shen Pei while Shen Pei blames Xin Ping's side for bringing Cao's devastation onto Jizhou.

## Key Characteristics
- Insider-channel persuader in Yuan Shao's acquisition of Jizhou.
- Yuan-side adviser whose role depends on access to a target's existing trust network.
- Factional grievance amplifier in Yuan Tan's post-Yuan Shao break with Yuan Shang.
- Kinship link to Xin Pi, who later carries Yuan Tan's request for Cao Cao's aid.
- Family-victim figure at Ye's fall, where Shen Pei's prison killing turns factional conflict into personal grief and execution pressure.

## Qualifications
This page remains bounded to three source scenes. It does not yet establish a full biography of Xin Ping or determine how much independent initiative he has beyond the adviser clusters in which the sources place him. Hanji 1063 renders the name as 新萍; the wiki normalizes to 辛评 because the sequence matches the existing Yuan-family and Xin Pi route, while keeping the family-killing detail source-scoped.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1063's Ye-fall family grievance: Xin Ping's imprisoned family is killed under Shen Pei's order before Xin Ping confronts him.
- Reframed Xin Ping from adviser-cluster figure alone into a later victim of Yuan-camp factional coercion.
