## Overview

赵敬肃王刘彭祖 / Liu Pengzu, Zhao King enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-382-1-bazhan-shumu-hanwudi-zhizi-liudan-you-duo-huangtang-loi-6-s1z2okmskqvbfwryz-qjto|Hanji 382-1]] as [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s elder half-brother, king of Zhao, and father of [[LiuDanZhaoPrinceWesternHan|赵太子刘丹]]. His role in the episode is narrow: he first benefits [[JiangChongWesternHan|江充]] as a Zhao-court guest through Jiang's family connection, then tries to save Liu Dan after Jiang reports Liu Dan's scandal to the central court.

## Current Profile

The source presents Liu Pengzu as a Zhao royal father caught between a damaging heir and Han Wudi's central authority. Jiang Chong, then Jiang Qi, becomes a guest in Liu Pengzu's circle because Jiang's sister marries Liu Dan. When Jiang later accuses Liu Dan in Chang'an, Liu Pengzu tries to discredit Jiang as a fugitive whose testimony should not be trusted.

Han Wudi does not accept that defense. Liu Pengzu then offers to personally serve on the frontier to save Liu Dan, and the episode says this plea changes the outcome from death to demotion. Liu Pengzu therefore functions less as an independent policy actor than as the royal-household context for Jiang Chong's first major denunciation.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Zhao king and elder half-brother of Han Wudi in the episode's family frame.
- Father of Liu Dan, whose misconduct threatens Zhao succession.
- Early patron-context figure for Jiang Chong/Jiang Qi before Jiang flees Zhao.
- Attempts to defend Liu Dan by arguing that Jiang Chong is an unreliable fugitive.
- Offers frontier service as a plea to save Liu Dan from execution.
- Functions as a bounded royal-kin context for Jiang Chong's rise, not as a full biographical subject.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 382-1. It does not reconstruct Liu Pengzu's full reign as Zhao king, his complete family, the exact legal language of his plea, or the later status of the Zhao kingdom.
