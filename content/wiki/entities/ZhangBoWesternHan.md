## Overview

张伯 / Zhang Bo enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-488-yixue-dashi-jingfang-ruhe-shenxian-bisi-ju-llf_4yvk8urtkgidnj2qhvca3vah|Hanji 488]] as [[LiuQingHuaiyangKingWesternHan|淮阳王刘清]]'s maternal uncle and [[JingFang|京房]]'s father-in-law. The episode makes him the private recorder and possible leak path through which Jing Fang's post-audience discussions become prosecutable political material.

## Current Profile

Zhang Bo is presented as a wind-reading court broker rather than a doctrinal figure. He studies the Yi with Jing Fang while Jing Fang is favored, marries his daughter to Jing Fang, and listens when Jing Fang reports conversations with [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] at home.

His decisive role is documentation. Hanji 488 says he secretly records Jing Fang's words and then encourages a plan in which Liu Qing would petition to enter court and help push Jing Fang's [[KaogongfaReformBacklash|kaogong]] agenda. Once the plan is exposed, those notes and the princely petition route make Jing Fang vulnerable to [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]]'s charge of conspiracy, slander, and improper involvement of a regional king.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han court-adjacent broker tied to Liu Qing and Jing Fang by kinship.
- Learns from Jing Fang when Jing Fang is politically favored.
- Records private political conversations that Jing Fang assumes are safe.
- Helps route Jing Fang's reform hopes through Liu Qing's petition channel.
- Becomes co-punished with Jing Fang after the case is exposed.

## Qualifications
