# Kaylee Robbins

## Overview
Kaylee Robbins is the parent and emerging content creator interviewed by [[MarketplaceTech]] about how an unexpected [[MetaAI|Meta AI]] exchange changed her approach to family privacy online.

## Current Profile
Robbins says she had 348 followers when her original post drew viral attention and rejects the idea that she began as an established influencer. She had intended to create a supportive space for mothers after postpartum struggles, but the privacy incident placed her in a much larger public debate.

Her current position is selective rather than anti-technology. She continues to use AI and share family material, but avoids identifiable details about her children, restricts app permissions, and uses [[Tinybeans]] for more private family sharing under a disclosed paid partnership.

## Key Characteristics
- Parent-centered creator whose audience expanded unexpectedly after a Meta AI privacy incident.
- Accepts some responsibility for earlier family posting without accepting the harshest public blame.
- Treats names, schools, addresses, landmarks, home images, and permissions as practical privacy boundaries.
- Continues using AI while reducing the information and device access available to it.
- Balances child privacy against relatives' emotional attachment to online family archives.
