# SOM

SOM is the sommelier-training documentary recommended in [[sanlian-chuantai-shen-ci-gei-ren-putao-shijie-weixun-le-585125156]]. In the episode, [[Heimai|黑麦]] uses it to make [[TasteTraining]] concrete: professional blind tasting requires repeated practice with typical regions, grape varieties, aromas, acidity, tannin, structure, soil, climate, and vintage clues.

The source also keeps the training claim bounded. Blind tasting can be trained because some wines carry relatively typical signs, but it remains difficult when new and old worlds converge in technique or when human choices alter a region's expected style. The documentary therefore connects directly to [[WineTerroir]] as a practice of educated inference rather than magic certainty.
