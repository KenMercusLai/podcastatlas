# 刘琮 / Liu Cong (late Han)

## Overview
刘琮 / Liu Cong (late Han) is [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s successor in [[Jingzhou|荆州]] whose surrender to [[CaoCao|曹操]] transfers the province and its personnel network into Cao's court-centered order.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1095 begins after Liu Cong has submitted. Cao Cao appoints him Qingzhou inspector and grants him marquis rank, making the surrender legible as incorporation rather than simple dispossession. The episode focuses less on Liu Cong's decision process than on what follows: Han Song is freed, former Jingzhou officials receive appointments, and Wen Ping's delayed arrival is judged through loyalty rather than automatic rebellion.

## Key Characteristics
- Successor to Liu Biao in Jingzhou.
- Surrenders the province to Cao Cao in the episode's 208 CE setting.
- Receives a court title and noble rank after submission.
- Becomes the formal hinge through which Jingzhou elites enter Cao's personnel order.

- Regime transfer: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1095-liubei-shujin-yiqie-hou-ping-shenme-jianxin-neng-fanshen-lmflbj9kshnmgsztdghnqj2foav3|Hanji 1095]] places the release and appointment of Jingzhou officials immediately after Liu Cong's submission.

## Qualifications
The current evidence begins after the surrender and does not establish the full succession dispute, advisers' roles, legal mechanics, or Liu Cong's later career. The Qingzhou appointment and marquis rank remain episode-presented details.
