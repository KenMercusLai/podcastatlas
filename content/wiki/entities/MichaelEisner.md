# Michael Eisner

Michael Eisner is the Disney CEO brought in after the 1984 boardroom crisis in [[disney-the-renaissance-and-the-empire-1]]. The source frames him as an outsider from Paramount whose story-first, cost-disciplined live-action playbook unexpectedly fit [[TheWaltDisneyCompany]]'s need to regain momentum.

Eisner's early era raises park prices, improves cash flow, builds a profitable live-action slate, and supports the animation reset that becomes the [[DisneyRenaissance]]. The source also treats his later period as a succession and governance caution: after [[FrankWells]] dies and [[JeffreyKatzenberg]] leaves, strategic confusion, animation decline, the failed Michael Ovitz hire, and shareholder pressure lead toward Eisner's exit and [[BobIger]]'s rise.
