# Peter Steinberger

## Overview

Peter Steinberger is an Austrian developer and founder whom [[ep-30-openclaw-the-open-source-ai-agent-that-got-its-creator-hired-by-openai]] identifies as the creator of [[OpenClaw]] and a former founder of PSPDFKit.

## Current Profile

Within the available evidence, Steinberger is a source-scoped example of an individual developer turning a personal assistant experiment into a widely noticed open-source agent project. The episode says the project began in November 2025, passed through two names, grew rapidly, and led to Steinberger joining [[OpenAI]] while stewardship moved to an open-source foundation. The source does not provide primary records for that chronology, employment arrangement, or governance transfer.

## Key Characteristics

- Built the personal-agent project that the episode identifies as OpenClaw.
- Is presented as an experienced software founder rather than a first-time hobbyist.
- Used open-source distribution and community extension as part of the project's growth.
- Is reported to have joined OpenAI after the project attracted substantial attention.
