## Overview

窦笃 / Dou Du is one of the Dou-family brothers whose profile now starts under [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Emperor Zhang]] and extends into the early He-era regency. Hanji 752 shows him as an empress-side relative with palace access, favor, and enough protected status that his night travel can trigger a backlash against [[ZhouYuLuoyangEasternHan|Zhou Yu]]'s Luoyang enforcement. Hanji 761 later makes him a mansion beneficiary and palace-guard commander beside [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] and [[DouJingEasternHan|Dou Jing]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 752 gives Dou Du's early access layer. He appears with Dou Xian in the Zhangdi-era Dou household that receives favor after Empress Dou's rise, gathers guests, and worries officials such as [[FifthLunEasternHan|Fifth Lun]]. The same source's night-travel episode shows how protected kinship converts routine enforcement into political exposure: station chief Huo Yan stops Dou Du, Dou reports upward, Zhou Yu is arrested, and Zhangdi releases Zhou after several days.

Hanji 761 gives the later regency privilege layer. While officials are protesting Dou Xian's Northern Xiongnu expedition, [[EmpressDowagerDouHeLateHan|Empress Dowager Dou]] orders mansions built for Dou Du and Dou Jing. [[HeChangEasternHan|He Chang]] criticizes those residences as connected luxury projects imposed on commoners while the army is mobilizing and public finance is empty.

After the campaign victory, Dou Xian controls military power while Dou Du and Dou Jing command palace guard forces. Dou Du therefore marks the family layer of Dou power: he is not developed as an independent policy actor, but he makes outer-relative access, mansion privilege, enforcement backlash, and palace coercive proximity visible.

## Key Characteristics

- Dou-family brother whose early profile includes palace access, guest networks, and protected night travel.
- Trigger figure in the Zhou Yu/Huo Yan enforcement backlash under Zhangdi.
- Mansion beneficiary under Empress Dowager Dou's ignored-remonstrance order.
- Named target in He Chang's criticism of public burden and elite excess.
- Palace-guard commander after Dou Xian's Northern Xiongnu victory.
- Evidence node for family-wide outer-relative overreach rather than an independently developed statesman.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 752 and Hanji 761. It should not be used as a full biography of Dou Du, and it does not identify his later fate beyond the source set's Dou-family power expansion and later cleanup context. Huo Yan remains source-scoped because Hanji 752 uses him mainly to expose the risk of enforcing against Dou Du.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 752 as Dou Du's Zhangdi-era access and night-travel enforcement-backlash layer.
- Preserved Hanji 761's mansion, public-burden, and palace-guard command profile.
- Reframed Dou Du as a bridge between early protected kinship and later family-wide regency privilege.
