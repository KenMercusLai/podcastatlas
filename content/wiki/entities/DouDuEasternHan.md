## Overview

窦笃 / Dou Du enters the wiki as one of the Dou-family brothers whose mansion construction and palace-guard command make [[OuterRelativeCourtPower|outer-relative court power]] visible beside [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] and [[DouJingEasternHan|Dou Jing]]. Hanji 761 does not give him an independent policy program; it uses him as a concrete sign of dowager-backed household privilege.

## Current Profile

Dou Du's first bounded role is as a beneficiary of [[EmpressDowagerDouHeLateHan|Empress Dowager Dou]]'s construction order. While officials are protesting Dou Xian's Northern Xiongnu expedition, the dowager orders mansions built for Dou Du and Dou Jing. [[HeChangEasternHan|He Chang]] criticizes those residences as connected luxury projects imposed on commoners while the army is mobilizing and public finance is empty.

His second role appears after the campaign victory. The source says Dou Xian controls military power while Dou Du and Dou Jing command palace guard forces. Dou Du therefore marks the household layer of Dou power: the family does not only enjoy rank and wealth, but also stands close to coercive palace force after the Yanran victory.

## Key Characteristics

- Dou-family brother whose profile is source-bounded to luxury construction and palace guards.
- Mansion beneficiary under Empress Dowager Dou's ignored-remonstrance order.
- Named target in He Chang's criticism of public burden and elite excess.
- Palace-guard commander after Dou Xian's Northern Xiongnu victory.
- Evidence node for family-wide overreach rather than an independently developed statesman.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 761. It should not be used as a full biography of Dou Du, and it does not identify his later fate beyond the source's claim that Dou-family power expands after the Northern Xiongnu victory.
