## Overview

钩弋夫人 / Lady Gouyi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-396-1-12-sui-huangdi-yu-6-sui-huanghou-shui-ganchu-de-huangtang-shier-lnq-puxt0zdxhfm7pwilxellccdr|Hanji 396-1]] as [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]]'s mother, whose death before Han Zhao's accession helps explain why [[GaiChanggongzhuWesternHan|鄂邑 / 盖长公主]] is brought into the palace to care for the young ruler.

## Current Profile

The source uses Lady Gouyi as background for Han Zhao's household vulnerability rather than as a full biography. Her absence creates the care gap around an eight-year-old emperor and makes the older princess's palace role politically consequential. That household arrangement later intersects with consort selection, [[DingWairenWesternHan|丁外人]]'s access to the princess, and six-year-old [[ShangguanTaihouWesternHan|上官氏]]'s entry.

## Key Characteristics

- Mother of Han Zhao.
- Deceased palace figure whose absence creates a care gap around the child emperor.
- Background figure connecting succession, harem politics, and princess household authority.
