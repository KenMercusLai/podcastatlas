# Mary Pat Campbell

Mary Pat Campbell appears in [[data-risk-and-actuarial-science-in-insurance]] as the guest explaining [[ActuarialScience]] to a data-science audience. The source identifies her as a life actuary working in insurance research, a fellow of the [[SocietyOfActuaries]], and a member of the [[AmericanAcademyOfActuaries]], with a background in physics and applied mathematics.

Her role in the wiki is to ground insurance data science in professional practice. She uses examples from mortality tables, COVID mortality, underwriting selection, electronic health records, policy flags, reporting lags, and regulatory limits to show why [[ActuarialDataQuality]] and [[InsuranceModelRegulatoryConstraint]] matter before AI or machine learning can be useful.
