## Overview

唐太宗 / Emperor Taizong of Tang enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-467-guanchang-li-jiang-zhenhua-shuo-shihua-you-duo-nan-ls_zlyqolvpfttspah8wery4e3fs|Hanji 467]] as the ruler-side condition behind [[WeiZhengTang|魏征]]'s remembered remonstrance. The episode uses Taizong to show that direct speech depends not only on an official's courage but also on a ruler's ability to receive warning.

## Current Profile

The source does not build a full Taizong profile. It invokes him through the mirror metaphor after Wei Zheng's death, where the ruler remembers a direct adviser as a lost instrument of self-correction. Taizong therefore functions here as the contrasting receiver of advice beside [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]]'s weaker court-feedback environment.
