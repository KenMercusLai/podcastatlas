# Matt Scogin

## Overview

Matt Scogin is the president of [[HopeCollege]] and the architect of [[HopeForward]], a tuition-free covenant intended to replace upfront student payment with later voluntary giving.

## Current Profile

The source presents Scogin as a former Wall Street professional whose parents' deaths prompted a reassessment of purpose and led him back to his undergraduate institution despite having no prior academic career. His institutional argument combines moral and financial claims: student debt can narrow meaningful choice, while a voluntary covenant can turn education from a transaction into a continuing relationship.

Scogin does not claim that the financial outcome is known. He acknowledges that some graduates may give less than the cost of their education, others may give more, and the college must raise substantial transition capital before the model can scale. His case is therefore one for an experiment with openly transferred risk rather than a demonstrated replacement for tuition.

## Key Characteristics

- Moved from finance into higher-education leadership after a personal and spiritual reassessment.
- Frames student debt as a constraint on educational purpose, service, and career choice.
- Rejects a legally enforceable repayment amount in order to preserve voluntary giving.
- Treats institutional exposure to alumni choice as an incentive to improve educational value and student outcomes.
- Uses Christian gift language and communal reciprocity to explain the model while recognizing financial uncertainty.
