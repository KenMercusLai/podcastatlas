# Sam Walton

Sam Walton is the [[Walmart]] founder who appears in [[costco-1]] as a close observer of [[SolPrice]] and [[PriceClub]]. The episode says Walton visited Price, learned about the warehouse-club model, and launched [[SamsClub|Sam's Club]] within about a year.

The source uses Walton to show that the Price Club model was legible to strong retailers, but also that copying the surface format did not automatically reproduce [[Costco]]'s full combination of culture, markups, membership trust, and operating discipline.
