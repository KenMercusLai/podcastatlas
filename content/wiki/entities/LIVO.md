# LIVO

## Overview
LIVO is [[KuaikanManhua]]'s AI-native content product described by [[ChenAnni]] in [[ta-beipian-guo-beijiu-guo-beipeng-shang-shentan-guo-beitengtong-jidao-guo-jueding-zai-ai-shidai-jixu-maoxian-duitan-chen-anni-lomvn15fklk80bhkreied1iotuof]]. Chen calls it a real-time living AI world and frames it as a possible new storytelling carrier for the AI era.

## Current Profile
The source presents LIVO as a product hypothesis rather than a validated market result. Users enter different authored worlds, watch a living map of character activity, join main or side stories, interact with agent characters, and change events that keep unfolding even when the user is away. Its differentiation from chatbots is shared experience and narrative consequence; its differentiation from observer simulations is the user's role inside the story world.

## Key Characteristics
- Living world structure: LIVO worlds continue running with agent activity, system-recorded events, and visible character trajectories.
- User-influenced fate: the product centers on story arcs that users can affect, such as persuading a character away from a destructive choice.
- Agentic character design: characters are agents with jobs, goals, dreams, boundaries, and evolving choices rather than human players or static NPCs.
- Authored premium worlds: Chen says LIVO still depends on strong content ability, layered IP design, and stories that create joy and emotional movement.
- Cost-sensitive AI product: persistent worlds consume tokens, making inference cost and product willingness to pay part of the design problem.

- Authored premium worlds: [[ta-beipian-guo-beijiu-guo-beipeng-shang-shentan-guo-beitengtong-jidao-guo-jueding-zai-ai-shidai-jixu-maoxian-duitan-chen-anni-lomvn15fklk80bhkreied1iotuof]] says high operating cost makes premium IP quality and layered emotional content necessary for the business to work.
- Cost-sensitive AI product: [[ta-beipian-guo-beijiu-guo-beipeng-shang-shentan-guo-beitengtong-jidao-guo-jueding-zai-ai-shidai-jixu-maoxian-duitan-chen-anni-lomvn15fklk80bhkreied1iotuof]] explicitly notes token consumption and the need to achieve good effects at lower cost.

## Qualifications
The source does not provide LIVO user metrics, pricing, launch status, technical architecture, safety controls, or external validation. Claims that LIVO could become an "AI-era movie" remain Chen's product vision.
