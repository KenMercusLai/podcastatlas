# 扣子妈妈 / Kouzi's mother

## Overview
扣子妈妈 is the parent guest in [[ep180-feidianxing-beijing-mama-de-foxi-jiwa-women-jiujing-xiang-peiyang-zenyang-de-haizi-ckwridoewbb3abaaaadgpbgf|EP180]], presented as a busy and self-described atypical Beijing mother navigating school and extracurricular choices.

## Current Profile
扣子妈妈 rejects intensive preschool academics even though her child's initially weaker first-grade preparation caused anxiety. She reports that the child later caught up and says she would make the same preschool choice again.

Her “佛系” position is selective rather than hands-off: dance, fencing, English, and later online mathematics remain part of family life, but she distinguishes experience, physical development, resilience, and intellectual breadth from a certificate-maximizing race. She also frames parental consistency and calm participation as more useful than argument or panic-driven purchasing.

## Key Characteristics
- Beijing parent offering a family-side account of early schooling and extracurricular choice.
- Skeptic of heavy preschool academic preparation despite experiencing first-grade comparison anxiety.
- Selective investor in sport, art, language, and later mathematics rather than a no-activity parent.
- Advocate for resilience, broader experience, health, and eventual child autonomy.
- Supporter of clear adult responsibility and consistent parental boundaries.
