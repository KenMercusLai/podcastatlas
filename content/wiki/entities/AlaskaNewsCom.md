# alaskanews.com

## Overview

alaskanews.com is the Alaska-focused news site in the source that publishes coverage generated by [[WalterAINewsAgent|Walter]].

## Current Profile

The site is presented as an automation-first response to gaps in [[LocalJournalism]]. It publishes about 20 highly local stories per day on subjects including tax rates and wildlife violations. Walter supplies much of that volume by monitoring public materials and generating articles, with human review reserved for at least some sensitive items.

## Key Characteristics

- Focuses on highly local Alaska information that may otherwise receive little coverage.
- Uses an AI agent for source monitoring, story selection, drafting, and publication.
- Produces enough material that readers must distinguish consequential reporting from obscure informational items.
- Faces unresolved questions about review standards, political independence, errors, and audience trust.

## Evidence

- Coverage model: [[tech-20261022-1022-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261022-1022-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports the approximate daily volume and examples of local topics.
- Workflow: [[tech-20261022-1022-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261022-1022-mp-tech-pod-128]] describes Walter's broad input collection, automated selection, drafting, and partial escalation to editors.
- Governance: [[tech-20261022-1022-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261022-1022-mp-tech-pod-128]] records newsroom concern about the political backgrounds of some team members without demonstrating biased articles.

## Qualifications

The source does not audit the site's accuracy, corrections, readership, ownership, financial model, or editorial-review rate. Political affiliations within the team warrant scrutiny but are not themselves proof that the site lacks independence.

## What Changed

- Initial profile created around its AI-generated local-news model.
- Separated coverage expansion from demonstrated editorial quality.
