# 袭人 / Xiren

In [[ritan-chuantai-hongloumeng-haishi-keyi-du-yidu-de-587167638]], 袭人 is discussed beside [[XueBaochai|薛宝钗]] as a figure more able to survive ordinary worldly order than [[LinDaiyu|林黛玉]] or [[Qingwen|晴雯]]. The episode does not make that adaptability a simple virtue or vice; it uses her to show that [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]] compares different strategies for living under hierarchy.

Xiren therefore helps define the boundary of [[GrandViewGardenYouthUtopia|the Grand View Garden youth space]]. Figures who understand social expectations may survive better when the dream dissolves, while figures of [[TruthfulTemperamentInRedChamber|真]] remain more beautiful, more exposed, and less compatible with marriage and household discipline.
