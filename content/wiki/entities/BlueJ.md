# BlueJ

BlueJ is the [[Amazon]] robotics example named by [[AmyWebb]] in [[tech-20260106-0106-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260106-0106-mp-tech-pod-128]]. The source describes it as overhead robotic arms that move packages faster and at lower cost than a human workforce.

The wiki role is source-scoped: BlueJ is not analyzed as a full product line here, but as a concrete case for [[PhysicalAI]] becoming operational infrastructure. It connects warehouse automation to [[AutomationDisplacementEffect]] because productivity gains can also raise concern about a worker cliff.
