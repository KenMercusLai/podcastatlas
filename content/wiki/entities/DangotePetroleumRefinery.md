# Dangote Petroleum Refinery

## Overview

Dangote Petroleum Refinery is the Nigerian refinery presented in the episode as preparing a potentially record-setting African public listing led by [[AlikoDangote]].

## Current Profile

The proposed listing is described as beginning trading in November at a possible $50bn valuation, which would make the refinery Africa's most valuable listed company if achieved. Its significance in the episode is not only size: distribution through banks, apps, point-of-sale terminals, and diaspora platforms makes it a test of whether a flagship African asset can broaden domestic and continental ownership.

That market-deepening potential coexists with concentration and volatility risk. Heavy retail demand before listing does not establish fair pricing, liquid secondary trading, or positive investor returns, and large institutions remain important to the depth of African exchanges.

## Key Characteristics

- Nigerian energy asset preparing a domestic public listing in the source.
- Prospective $50bn company and possible largest listed company in Africa.
- Flagship offering intended to attract ordinary African as well as institutional investors.
- Potential catalyst for local market depth and financial participation.
- Candidate for a later secondary New York listing.

## Evidence
