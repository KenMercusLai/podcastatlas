# Rick Knudtson

## Overview
Rick Knudtson is a SaaS founder associated with [[FlywheelWordPressHosting|Flywheel]] and [[WorkshopInternalComms|Workshop]]. In the available source, he is presented through a [[TheSaaSPodcast]] interview as a repeat founder who used a prior exit to fund a second company but still had to work through a difficult product-market-fit search.

## Current Profile
Rick's current profile in the wiki centers on disciplined recovery from a mistaken product thesis. His Workshop story shows a founder moving from a preferred intranet idea toward a less glamorous but stronger customer signal: internal email infrastructure for large organizations. The episode also positions him as a founder who treats marketing, values, and pricing as operational levers rather than late-stage wrappers around the product.

## Key Characteristics
- Repeat SaaS founder with a prior infrastructure company, [[FlywheelWordPressHosting|Flywheel]], that scaled to a major customer and employee base before its sale.
- Founder of [[WorkshopInternalComms|Workshop]], a platform for enterprise internal communications across email, SMS, Slack, Microsoft Teams, SharePoint, and related channels.
- Uses customer discovery as a corrective against [[FounderEgo]], particularly after initially dismissing email as too simple or too familiar.
- Advocates [[AudienceBeforeProductMarketing]] through early investment in [[HappyMondayClub]] before Workshop had a finished product to sell.
- Treats pricing as part of product design by avoiding seat-based friction and tying Workshop packages to audience size and communication channels.

## Evidence
- Repeat founder and prior operating context: [[rick-knudtson-workshop-the-email-signal-he-ignored-for-9-months]] ties Rick to Flywheel's scale, sale, and internal communications pain.
- Customer-discovery posture: [[rick-knudtson-workshop-the-email-signal-he-ignored-for-9-months]] describes six to nine months of ignored email feedback before Workshop pivoted from intranet to email.
- Marketing and growth approach: [[rick-knudtson-workshop-the-email-signal-he-ignored-for-9-months]] connects Rick's Happy Monday Club advice to pre-product audience building and community-led resources.
- Pricing judgment: [[rick-knudtson-workshop-the-email-signal-he-ignored-for-9-months]] reports Workshop's audience-and-channel model and Rick's rejection of per-seat expansion friction.

The transcript includes some spelling inconsistency between "Knudtson" and "Knudsen"; the wiki uses "Knudtson" because the episode metadata and title use that spelling.
