# 吴庆瑞 / Goh Keng Swee

## Overview

Goh Keng Swee appears in the bounded source as a senior [[PeoplesActionParty|People's Action Party]] leader, finance minister, and principal Singapore negotiator whose oral history provides the “Albatross File” frame for the 1965 separation from Malaysia.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Goh as an unusually consequential but not solitary agent. He shared a Malayan political world and a personal link with Tun Abdul Razak, judged the federation increasingly unworkable, obtained Lee Kuan Yew's written authority to negotiate a constitutional rearrangement, and pushed that authority toward separation. His account complicates Singapore's passive-expulsion narrative without eliminating federal coercion, Lee's grief, or the structural conflicts already making union unstable.

## Key Characteristics

- Combined technical-government authority with high-stakes political negotiation.
- Used personal trust with a Malaysian counterpart to create a confidential channel.
- Interpreted merger as an “albatross” after expected market and political benefits failed to materialize.
- Exercised agency beyond Lee's fully informed instructions while remaining within a written negotiating mandate.
- Left oral-history evidence that later became part of Singapore's public founding narrative.
