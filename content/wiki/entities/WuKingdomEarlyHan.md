# 吴国 / Wu Kingdom (early Han)

吴国 / Wu Kingdom enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-189-liubang-siqian-zuihou-yibo-lllydj2xyk4cr7b9d2qx0hvfw-dz|Hanji 189]] as the post-[[YingBu|英布 / 黥布]] replacement form of [[JingKingdomEarlyHan|荆国]]. After the rebellion is settled, [[LiuBang|刘邦]] changes Jing into Wu and appoints [[LiuBiWuKing|刘濞]] as king.

The source gives the kingdom a scale of three commanderies and fifty-three counties. Its role is not developed administratively in this episode; it mainly shows [[SameSurnameKingEnfeoffment|同姓王分封]] continuing after yet another vassal-king crisis.
