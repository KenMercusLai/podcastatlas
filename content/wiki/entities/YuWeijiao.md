# 喻渭蛟

## Overview
喻渭蛟 is the founder of [[YTOExpress|圆通速递]] and a member of the wider [[TongluExpressCluster|桐庐快递创业网络]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Yu as a former renovation contractor pushed toward express delivery after a large unpaid receivable trapped his earlier business. With advice from his wife 张小娟, who had worked in 申通 finance, he borrowed startup capital and founded 圆通 in Shanghai in 2000.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder who entered logistics after construction receivables failure.
- Early adopter of the Taobao volume-for-lower-price relationship.
- Advocate against restrictive reserved-letter weight rules.
