# 李白 / Li Bai

李白 / Li Bai enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-4-lphaverx7gyxntncak-lb5hsrqa]] as a later poetic admirer of [[YuQing|虞卿]]'s friendship toward [[WeiQiWarringStates|魏齐]]. The episode uses him not for a full Tang biography but as a reception lens: Li Bai is the reader who sees abandoned office, sudden trust, and life-risking loyalty as heroic.

That lens matters because it contrasts with [[XunYue|荀悦]] and [[WangAnshi|王安石]]. Xun Yue worries that private loyalty erodes office duty; Wang Anshi sees the danger of high political position and sudden disaster; Li Bai preserves the affective glamour of [[AristocraticFriendshipStateConflict|贵族义气与国家秩序冲突]].

The page is source-scoped until later materials develop Li Bai independently. Here he is mainly a marker for how Warring States friendship stories could be re-aestheticized as noble spirit in later literature.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-101-2-ruhe-kandai-xingbenshan-yu-xingbene-lmawsqkttjmitwkczhyfs7f7-mqt]] adds a second Warring States reception case. The episode says Li Bai admired [[LuZhonglian|鲁仲连]] for independent personality, free will, chivalric spirit, and wisdom, including through the poem cycle 《古风》. Li Bai therefore now marks not only friendship loyalty around [[YuQing|虞卿]], but also later poetic attraction to high-minded independence.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-3-liqingzhao-zhongnian-fuqi-libie-hejiu-jie-xiangsi-lnzxhrj2iulahccy2-pkm-wphdei|Qinji 131-3]] adds the more familiar wine-poet image. The episode uses Li Bai as the emblem of [[PoeticWineCulture|诗酒文化]]: drink can feed poetic momentum, public persona, and the later memory of inspired spontaneity. This source also places him inside [[DuFu|杜甫]]'s 《饮中八仙歌》, where Du Fu's admiration helps turn Li Bai's drinking into a literary portrait rather than a flat anecdote.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-176-shusuntong-ruhe-wei-dahan-zhiding-liyi-zhidu-loeoqccrfwbmtr634ukyau9xykqy|Hanji 176]] uses Li Bai's frontier-poetry mood differently. The episode opens from 《关山月》 to frame [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] pressure and the coming [[BaidengEncirclement|白登之围]], then pivots from the frontier threat back to [[ShusunTong|叔孙通]]'s court ritual work. Li Bai is therefore a scene-setting literary lens rather than a main historical actor in this source.
