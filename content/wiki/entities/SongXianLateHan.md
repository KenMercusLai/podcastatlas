# 宋宪 / Song Xian (late Han)

宋宪 / Song Xian (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1025-bai-yu-caocao-lvbu-cuo-zai-jinjiuling-ljjt1t2mk77si6re-wew-hpl8qz|Hanji 1025]] as one of [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s officers at [[Xiapi|下邳]]. The source does not give him an independent backstory; his importance is collective. After [[HouChengLateHan|侯成]] is frightened and angered by Lü Bu's response to the wine-meat offering, Song Xian joins Hou Cheng and [[WeiXuLateHan|魏续]] in the internal surrender.

The group captures [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] and [[GaoShun|高顺]] and surrenders troops to [[CaoCao|曹操]]. Song Xian therefore marks the point at which Hou Cheng's individual grievance becomes organized command breakage rather than a private dispute.
