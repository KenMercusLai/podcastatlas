# 《江湖儿女》 / Ash Is Purest White

[[JiangHuErNv|《江湖儿女》]] appears in [[ep276-duihua-jiazhangke-shang-yige-shidai-zouyuan-yiding-shi-yinwei-lingyige-shidai-zai-kaojin-ni-lqzsj-ujv-gpem-796cvncekdewc]] as an example of how [[JiaZhangke|贾樟柯]]'s film emotion forms before shooting. He recalls imagining a character leaving without farewell and returning to the jianghu, then becoming emotionally overwhelmed before the scene was filmed.

The source uses this memory to show that Jia's films do not treat departure as only plot motion. Departure carries lived memory, imagined fate, and the broader [[MobilityFarewellGeneration]] problem of people repeatedly separating from places and companions.
