# Mansur Yavas

Mansur Yavas is the Ankara mayor mentioned in [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]] as the opposition's backup presidential candidate. The source says rumors suggest he could become the next target after arrests of [[RepublicanPeoplesParty|CHP]] mayors and local officials.

His role is narrow but important: the episode uses Yavas to show that pressure on the Turkish opposition is not limited to [[EkremImamoglu]]. Even alternative candidates can be pulled into [[JudicializedOppositionSuppression]] before an early election.
