## Overview

沐剑屏 / Mu Jianping is a young woman in [[LuDingJi|《鹿鼎记》]] whose relationship with [[WeiXiaobao|韦小宝]] is read as the most innocent and youthfully affectionate of his seven marriages.

## Current Profile

The bounded episode treats Wei's early demand that Mu become his “little wife” as childish play more than a mature proposal. Her youth, directness, and relative lack of calculation distinguish the relationship from the fear, strategy, erotic struggle, or household management surrounding several other partners.

She is not merely passive: the episode remembers her help and warnings during the Divine Dragon Cult plot. Even so, the available evidence supports companionship and early affection more strongly than an account of mature mutual commitment.

## Key Characteristics

- Young, direct, and comparatively free of strategic calculation.
- Associated with Wei's earliest playful language of marriage.
- Offers help and warning during danger rather than functioning only as an object of affection.
- Represents youthful companionship more clearly than mature romantic commitment.

## Qualifications

This profile derives from one conversational summary rather than a complete study of the novel or its editions. “First love,” future marital durability, motives, and emotional maturity remain interpretive, and the age and power asymmetries should not be romanticized.
