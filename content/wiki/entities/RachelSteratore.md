# Rachel Steratore

## Overview

Rachel Steratore is presented by [[MarketplaceTech]] as a senior researcher at [[RAND]] who studies the development and governance implications of AI systems that can contribute to later AI systems.

## Current Profile

In [[tech-20261124-1124-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261124-1124-mp-tech-pod-128]], Steratore supplies a three-stage framework for [[RecursiveSelfImprovement]]: automation of known research work, AI-generated research ideas incorporated by people, and direct construction of a successor system. Her policy position is precautionary but not timeline-certain. Governments should build testing and reporting capacity before Type 3 exists, while distinguishing observed research automation from an undisclosed autonomous takeoff.

## Key Characteristics

- Separates current research assistance from direct successor construction.
- Treats declining human visibility as a safety and governance problem.
- Connects internal lab capability to government information asymmetry.
- Favors useful-across-scenarios “no regrets” capacity building.
- Proposes combining model benchmarks with confidential operational reporting.
