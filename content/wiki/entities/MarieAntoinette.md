# Marie Antoinette

## Overview

Marie Antoinette was the imprisoned French queen whose court relationship with [[PrincesseDeLamballe|the Princesse de Lamballe]] became a target of revolutionary sexual and political propaganda.

## Current Profile

This episode presents Marie Antoinette only through the September 1792 crisis. She and [[LouisXVI|Louis XVI]] were held in the Temple after the monarchy's collapse, while Lamballe's loyalty to them became dangerous at La Force. Pamphlet claims about a sexual relationship between the queen and Lamballe supplied an existing gendered vocabulary that later atrocity stories reused after Lamballe's death.

## Key Characteristics

- She was imprisoned with the royal family in the Temple after the storming of the Tuileries.
- Lamballe remained personally loyal to her despite revolutionary danger.
- Revolutionary pamphlets sexualized the relationship between the two women.
- Later stories about Lamballe's remains were shaped partly by this pre-existing pornographic demonology.
