## Overview

Jibin / 罽宾 is a distant Western Regions polity in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-516-gudai-zhongguo-weihe-hui-yu-zhege-guojia-duanjiao-llfmuseg49szpxvypvfsp4naimiy|Hanji 516]]. The episode routes it through the transcript form `季宾` and uses its conflict with Western Han envoys to show how nominal recognition can exceed enforceable diplomatic reach.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Jibin as confident that distance protects it from Han military punishment. Its rulers repeatedly attack envoys despite Han intervention in a local succession and the granting of a royal seal to [[YinmofuJibinKing|阴末赴 / 殷墨傅]]. When the recognized ruler later attacks another mission, the court chooses rupture and exclusion of the apology envoy rather than a distant punitive expedition.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Regions polity treated by the episode as too distant for reliable Han military enforcement.
- Diplomatic actor whose rulers repeatedly attack Han envoys.
- Site of a Han-backed local regime change carried out through an envoy and a subordinate prince.
- Example of recognition and investiture failing to create durable compliance.

## Qualifications

The supplied source is a short podcast summary, not a full reconstruction of Jibin's geography, dynastic sequence, relations with neighboring polities, or the original `Hanshu` text. Its transcript uses `季宾`; the canonical page uses 罽宾 / Jibin to avoid treating a likely transcription form as a separate polity. The exact identity of Rongqu, the sequence of attacks, and the court's legal treatment of Jibin's apology envoy remain source-scoped.
