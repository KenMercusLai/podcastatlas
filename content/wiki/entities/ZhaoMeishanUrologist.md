## Current Profile
The current source presents Zhao as a public medical educator who separates ordinary variation from symptoms needing assessment. She distinguishes urinary frequency from repeated low-volume voiding, hesitation from a full bladder that cannot empty, and occasional delay from habitual overdistension. Her practical frame connects [[HabitualUrineRetentionRisk]] and [[UrologicalSymptomTriage]] to everyday work, travel, sleep, hydration, and public-restroom conditions.

The episode names her but does not supply an institution, title beyond urologist, or professional biography. Although earlier episodes feature [[ZhaoDoctorUrology|a urology guest called 赵医生]], the available notes do not establish that they are the same person, so the identities remain separate.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains urinary physiology in accessible steps from kidney filtration to bladder storage and emptying.
- Distinguishes symptoms by volume, timing, pain, obstruction, residual urine, associated disease, and functional impact.
- Rejects deliberate urine holding as bladder strengthening and emphasizes timely emptying when access permits.
- Frames hydration as distributed and individualized rather than as late catch-up drinking or a universal quota.
- Connects health behavior to occupational, travel, and gendered public-restroom access constraints.
