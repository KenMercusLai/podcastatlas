## Overview

Wu Zhang / 吴章 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-you-xinji-de-ren-you-duo-kepa-lnhkk70905eqg4lqwh3mp2sxzamc|Hanji 616]] as [[WangYuXinPrince|王宇]]'s teacher, a Taixue scholar-official whose advice turns Wei-clan access lobbying into the staged [[DogBloodCase|狗血案]].

## Current Profile

The source makes Wu Zhang both learned adviser and failed risk reader. His first plan is cautious: have [[EmpressDowagerWeiPingdi|卫太后]] use a deferential memorial to show that the Wei clan understands adoptive-heir ethics and has no desire to seize power. After that fails, Wu Zhang proposes exploiting [[WangMang|王莽]]'s fear of spirits and anomalies through a "变怪" tactic.

That second plan destroys him. Lü Kuan's dog-blood act is discovered, the case reaches Wang Yu, and Wu Zhang is taken to Chang'an's east market, executed brutally, and burned. His student network is禁锢, making him a case where scholarly prestige offers no protection once a palace-political scheme is coded as dangerous ritual manipulation.

## Key Characteristics

- Wang Yu's teacher and a learned Taixue-linked figure.
- Adviser behind both the conciliatory memorial strategy and the later staged-anomaly strategy.
- Scholar whose risk judgment fails inside Wang Mang's symbolic-politics environment.
- Executed and posthumously humiliated after the case is exposed.
- Teacher whose students become collateral targets, with Yun Chang as a loyalty counterexample.
