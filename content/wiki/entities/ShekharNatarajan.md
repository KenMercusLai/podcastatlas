# Shekhar Natarajan

## Overview
Shekhar Natarajan is the host of [[TomorrowToday]] across the wiki's current sources. His interviews use guest biography to explore branding, resilience, spirituality, survival, elite sport, public institutions, creative work, inequality, leadership, and the human and governance questions raised by AI.

## Current Profile
The bounded sources present Shekhar as an interviewer who turns life stories into operating questions. With [[AndyStalman]], he pushes branding toward values, process, privacy, and [[AngelicIntelligence]]. With [[AraceliSegarra]], he connects Everest, uncertainty, safe teams, and the human “why” under AI. With [[ShyalpaRinpoche]], he tests spiritual claims against democracy, war, meaning scarcity, and peace infrastructure. With [[NataliaOlson]], he brings logistics experience into duplicated delivery networks, open government, sovereign infrastructure, and governed autonomy. With [[AdahSharma]], he links acting, rejection, public backlash, and AI's double edge for access and labor. With [[ChetanBhagat]], he connects education, platform inequality, intent-sensitive AI safety, authorship, cognition, and life after conventional success. With [[AlvaroVizcaino|Álvaro Vizcaíno]], he examines fear, acceptance, loss, and action. With [[AndreaFuentes]], he moves from Olympic biography and family grief into athlete autonomy, shared purpose, pain, safety, and whether a coach succeeds by making athletes less dependent on the coach.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames long-form guest stories as questions about identity, purpose, leadership, institutions, and technology.
- Translates abstract values into organizational design, process, collaboration, and decision-making.
- Uses concrete operating examples from logistics and business to test institutional claims.
- Treats AI as a question of intent, human purpose, moral direction, cognition, and accountable autonomy, not only productivity.
- Connects strategy to lived experience, including fear, vulnerability, trust, inequality, meaning, and public legitimacy.
- Invites disagreement and cross-domain analogy to expose assumptions and implementation gaps.
- Uses relevant personal experience to deepen a guest's account while marking interpretation as interpretation.
