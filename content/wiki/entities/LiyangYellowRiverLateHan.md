# 黎阳 / Liyang (Yellow River, late Han)

## Overview
黎阳 / Liyang (Yellow River, late Han) enters the wiki through Hanji 1034 as a Yellow River forward point in [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] deployment. It is deliberately separated from [[LiyangLateHan|历阳 / Liyang (late Han)]] and [[LiyangQinHan|栎阳 / Liyang (Qin-Han)]], which are different places.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] says Cao Cao advances to Liyang in the eighth month of 199 CE, sends [[ZangBaLateHan|臧霸]] with elite troops to [[QingzhouLateHan|青州]], leaves [[YuJin|于禁]] on the Yellow River bank, returns to [[Xudu|许都]] in the ninth month, and places troops at Guandu. Liyang's current role is therefore a staging and sequencing marker before the main campaign.

## Key Characteristics
- Yellow River forward point in Cao Cao's 199 CE pre-Guandu preparation.
- Source-scoped place marker, not a general geographic survey.
- Disambiguation node preventing 黎阳 from being folded into unrelated Liyang pages.
