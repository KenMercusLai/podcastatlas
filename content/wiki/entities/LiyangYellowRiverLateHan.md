# 黎阳 / Liyang (Yellow River, late Han)

## Overview
黎阳 / Liyang (Yellow River, late Han) is a Yellow River campaign point in Cao Cao's conflict with the Yuan camp. It is deliberately separated from [[LiyangLateHan|历阳 / Liyang (late Han)]] and [[LiyangQinHan|栎阳 / Liyang (Qin-Han)]], which are different places.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1034 introduces Liyang as a forward point in [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] deployment. Cao Cao advances there in the eighth month of 199 CE, sends [[ZangBaLateHan|臧霸]] with elite troops to [[QingzhouLateHan|青州]], leaves [[YuJin|于禁]] on the Yellow River bank, returns to [[Xudu|许都]] in the ninth month, and places troops at Guandu. In that source, Liyang is a staging and sequencing marker before the main campaign.

Hanji 1058 adds a later 203 CE layer. Cao Cao attacks Liyang, defeats [[YuanTan|袁谭]] and [[YuanShang|袁尚]], pursues them toward [[YeLateHan|邺城]], and later returns toward Xudu while leaving Jia Xin at Liyang. The place therefore shifts from prewar forward deployment to a post-Guandu pressure point that sets up [[GuoJia|郭嘉]]'s advice to let Yuan-family infighting ripen.

## Key Characteristics
- Yellow River forward point in Cao Cao's 199 CE pre-Guandu preparation.
- Later 203 CE pressure point where Cao Cao defeats Yuan Tan and Yuan Shang before the succession split hardens.
- Sequencing marker connecting direct military pressure to Guo Jia's later pressure-release advice.
- Disambiguation node preventing 黎阳 from being folded into unrelated Liyang pages.
