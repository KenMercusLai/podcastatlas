# HummingAgent

## Overview
HummingAgent is an AI software company presented in [[ep-51-building-ai-agents-that-actually-work-in-production]] as building private, customized agent workflows and full-stack applications for small and medium-sized businesses.

## Current Profile
The source positions HummingAgent as a service-led custom-software company rather than a single standardized agent product. Its claimed differentiation is discovery of client operations, connection to proprietary data and legacy systems, private deployment, model portability, and progressive permissioning. The company also describes Humcrew as a command-center product spanning voice, text messaging, proposal generation, and other operating functions, but the source does not provide technical or commercial validation.

## Key Characteristics
- Builds customized workflows around client-specific data, systems, APIs, and rules.
- Uses paid, fixed-scope discovery and copies of real client data before implementation.
- Presents private deployment and non-training use of customer data as product assurances.
- Keeps models replaceable and treats model changes as quality-assurance events.
- Offers private search and a source-described Humcrew operational command center.

## Evidence
- Service model: [[ep-51-building-ai-agents-that-actually-work-in-production]] says HummingAgent studies time-consuming work and client systems before designing a solution.
- Data and discovery: [[ep-51-building-ai-agents-that-actually-work-in-production]] says development begins against copied real data to expose missing fields, exceptions, and tacit rules.
- Product and architecture: [[ep-51-building-ai-agents-that-actually-work-in-production]] describes private GPT-style search, model replacement, and Humcrew's voice, messaging, and proposal functions.
- Claimed outcome: [[ep-51-building-ai-agents-that-actually-work-in-production]] reports that search over 44 years of PDFs reduced research from hours to minutes for one client.

## Qualifications
All capability, privacy, client, productivity, and workforce claims come from one company-leader interview summarized by the episode publisher. The note gives no system architecture, evaluation protocol, error rate, deployment cost, security audit, customer identity, or independent outcome evidence.
