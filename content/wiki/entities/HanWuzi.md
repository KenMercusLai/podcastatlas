# 韩武子 / Han Wuzi

韩武子 / Han Wuzi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] as the Han ruler who turns [[HanKangzi|韩康子]]'s starting position into more direct pressure on [[ZhengState|郑国]]. The source says he attacks Zheng early in his rule, kills Zheng Yougong, and later moves the capital to Yiyang.

The episode uses Han Wuzi as evidence that [[HanState|韩国]]'s conquest of Zheng was a multi-generation project. His war and relocation become the first explicit signs of [[StrategicCapitalRelocation|strategic capital relocation]] toward Zheng.
