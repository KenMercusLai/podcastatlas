# Okomfo Anokye

## Overview

Okomfo Anokye is the priestly and strategic founder whom the episode pairs with [[OseiTutu]] in the creation of a unified Asante polity.

## Current Profile

The episode treats Okomfo Anokye's sacred authority as political capacity. He recognizes that dispersed groups need a shared identity, is credited with superseding local stools through the [[GoldenStool]], and appears in the traditional account of coalition-building and victory over [[Denkyira]]. The magical language is preserved as founding tradition rather than converted into literal causal proof.

## Key Characteristics

- Priest and sacred authority in the Asante founding narrative.
- Close partner of [[OseiTutu]].
- Architect of a shared political identity in the episode's interpretation.
- Figure associated with the descent of the [[GoldenStool]].
- Traditional strategist credited with defections and supernatural intervention against Denkyira.

## Evidence

- Founding partnership: [[269-ghana-the-ashanti-empire-glt3638102749]] pairs Okomfo Anokye with Osei Tutu as sacred and political founders.
- Integrating symbol: [[269-ghana-the-ashanti-empire-glt3638102749]] credits him with replacing multiple stools with one supreme Golden Stool.
- War tradition: [[269-ghana-the-ashanti-empire-glt3638102749]] says traditional accounts credit him with securing defections and magical help during the Denkyira conflict.

## Qualifications

The source compares Okomfo Anokye to Merlin and openly narrates magical traditions. Those stories are evidence of remembered legitimacy and political imagination, not independently verified supernatural events or a complete biography.

## What Changed

- Created Okomfo Anokye as the sacred-political founder behind the episode's integration narrative.

## Relationships

- [[OseiTutu]] - ruler with whom he forms the episode's founding partnership.
- [[GoldenStool]] - integrating sacred object attributed to his intervention.
- [[Asante]] - polity whose collective identity his authority helps construct.
- [[Denkyira]] - rival defeated in traditions that assign him strategic and magical agency.
