# 望夷宫 / Wangyi Palace

望夷宫 / Wangyi Palace enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-138-wudu-qiannian-de-lishi-zhenxiang-zhaogao-bushi-taijian-2-lvtievjrclb1lxxxxr6k-q7kryto|Qinji 138 part 2]] as the palace where [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] is isolated and forced to suicide. It is the setting where [[ZhaoGao|赵高]]'s [[CrisisInformationBlockade|危机信息封锁]] and [[PalaceAccessMonopoly|宫廷接触垄断]] become direct armed action.

The coup enters the palace through a policing story. [[YanLe|阎乐]] claims thieves have entered the palace and kidnapped his mother, while she is actually hidden at Zhao Gao's house. Yan Le arrives with more than a thousand trusted soldiers, kills the gate official after accusing him of letting thieves in, attacks palace personnel, and reaches Hu Hai's inner quarters.

## Key Claims
- Wangyi Palace is the concrete place where Hu Hai discovers his isolation after the crisis has been hidden from him.
- Yan Le's entry shows [[PalaceCoupUnderPolicingPretext|治安名义政变]]: a palace assault is packaged as pursuit of thieves.
- The palace scene completes the feedback-failure arc because Hu Hai's last remaining eunuch says silence is what kept him alive.
