# Gaza

[[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]] turns Gaza from a brief example of vague diplomacy into the main implementation case. The episode says around 2.1 million Gazans are clustered in about one third of the territory, about three quarters are displaced, the Israeli army occupies about two thirds, [[Hamas]] controls much of the rest, and small militia enclaves further complicate any withdrawal map.

Gaza appears in [[omission-accomplished-why-the-iran-war-cycle-spins-on-6a6c7475aa1642a30f17f1cb]] as one of the unresolved Middle East files [[GregCarlstrom]] compares with the U.S.-[[Iran]] memorandum. The episode says the Gaza agreement leaves hard questions unanswered, including [[Hamas]] disarmament and [[Israel|Israeli]] withdrawal.

The source-scoped role of Gaza is to show how a diplomatic formula can avoid the issues that make settlement enforceable. In that sense it belongs to [[ThinDiplomaticFrameworks]] and [[ProxyConflictSpoilerRisk]] rather than to a full reconstruction or humanitarian account.
