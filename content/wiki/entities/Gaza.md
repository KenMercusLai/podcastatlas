# Gaza

## Overview
Gaza is a densely populated Palestinian territory whose wiki profile combines refugee history, blockade, war, displacement, Hamas rule, Israeli territorial control, and an unresolved postwar implementation problem.

## Current Profile
The 2021 episode places Gaza within the Nakba and says many residents descend from families displaced from places now inside Israel. It frames blockade and economic restriction as conditions that sustain desperation and armed resistance. Later sources add a severely fragmented wartime map and show why declarations of peace remain incomplete without agreed sequencing for Israeli withdrawal, Hamas disarmament, civilian return, verification, and Palestinian administration.

## Key Characteristics
- Territory whose population and politics are deeply connected to 1948 displacement.
- Subject to Israeli blockade and extensive control over goods and movement in the source account.
- Governed in part by Hamas and affected by Palestinian political division.
- War-damaged and heavily displaced, with territory divided among Israeli forces, Hamas, and smaller armed groups in later sources.
- Central test of whether diplomatic agreements can become enforceable governance.

- Territorial and humanitarian implementation: [[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]] reports mass displacement and divided control while mapping withdrawal, disarmament, verification, and administration gaps.
- Diplomatic omission: [[omission-accomplished-why-the-iran-war-cycle-spins-on-6a6c7475aa1642a30f17f1cb]] cites Gaza as a case where agreements leave core enforcement questions unanswered.

## Qualifications
The source set is not a full humanitarian, demographic, legal, or military history. The 2021 episode strongly emphasizes Israeli responsibility and gives limited treatment to Hamas attacks on civilians; later figures and territorial estimates are time-bound source claims.

## What Changed
- Added the Nakba and refugee origin of much of Gaza's population to the existing deal-implementation profile.
- Added blockade, economic restriction, and resistance-support dynamics.
- Preserved the later distinction between headline agreement and workable territorial governance.
