# 刘作虎 / Pete Lau

## Overview
刘作虎 / Pete Lau is identified by the source as senior vice president and chief product officer of [[OPPO]], speaking from the smartphone and personal-agent perspective.

## Current Profile
Lau argues that the smartphone can shift from connecting people to apps toward connecting intent to outcomes because it holds personal memory, real-time context, devices, and services. He divides agent trust into capability trust and risk trust, and treats a trustworthy Agent OS as a system with explicit authorization, traceability, third-party services, and A2A behavior rules.

## Key Characteristics
- Positions the phone as a long-term personal-agent and intent entry point.
- Distinguishes confidence that an agent can perform from confidence that its autonomy is bounded.
- Connects personalization to on-device context, memory, devices, and partner services.
- Advocates ecosystem-level behavior norms for interactions among agents.
