# Jane Fonda

## Overview
Jane Fonda is represented here as the actor-activist who helped turn the clerical-worker organization [[NineToFiveOrganization|9to5]] into the 1980 workplace comedy [[NineToFiveFilm|9 to 5]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Fonda's role as a translation of activism into mass entertainment. She knew [[KarenNussbaum]] through anti-war activism, wanted to support 9to5 with a major motion picture, and treated office workers' own stories as the basis for a Hollywood project.

Fonda's significance in this source is not only celebrity endorsement. She gathered worker material, asked Cleveland workers about boss-revenge fantasies, and helped create the conditions for [[PatriciaResnick]] to build a script from real office experiences. After release, Fonda and Nussbaum used the film's attention to support screenings, rallies, and organizing.

## Key Characteristics
- Actor-activist linking anti-war networks, women's workplace politics, and Hollywood production.
- Cultural producer who treated clerical workers as an underrepresented mass audience.
- Listener and collector of worker stories used to shape the [[NineToFiveFilm|9 to 5]] script.
- Public amplifier who helped connect the film's release to 9to5 screenings and rallies.
- Collaborator whose support turned [[CultureLedLaborOrganizing]] from local organizing into national pop-culture visibility.
