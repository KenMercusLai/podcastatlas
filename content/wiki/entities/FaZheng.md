# 法正 / Fa Zheng

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] adds a positive tactical-planning use of Fa Zheng before the darker Hanji 977 branch. In the episode's Liu Bei comparison, Fa Zheng appears at Dingjun Mountain as the adviser who sees how a specific battlefield position can be turned against Xiahou Yuan.

The wiki keeps this as a source-scoped summary rather than a full campaign account. Its function is to pair Fa Zheng's tactical precision with [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]]'s broader state-system role inside [[AdviserStateArchitecture|谋士政权架构]].

法正 / Fa Zheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-977-yi-zheng-yi-xie-luanshi-dushi-de-guijue-fengyun-lgtpgxl5anxial8ym2nt8dmuc3ee|Hanji 977]] as the episode's example of an adviser who turns formal tools into private revenge. The source cites the familiar judgment that he remembered small favors and small resentments, then frames his use of law and defamation cases as a way to remove enemies without looking openly lawless.

For the wiki, Fa Zheng is therefore a source-scoped case inside [[DarkAdviserCraft|毒士灰度谋略]]. The episode does not provide a full biography or independent legal record; it uses him to show how an adviser can make revenge look administrative, public, and orderly. That connects him to [[LegalistRulerTechnique|法家君术]] as a danger case: law-like instruments can stabilize rule, but they can also launder private resentment when office power and personal grievance overlap.
