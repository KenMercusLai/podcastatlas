# 法正 / Fa Zheng

## Overview

法正 / Fa Zheng is a Fufeng-born adviser whose current profile moves from long underuse under [[LiuZhang|刘璋]], through a covert alignment with [[ZhangSongLateHan|张松]] and [[LiuBei|刘备]], to later tactical precision and morally troubling use of administrative power.

## Current Profile

Fa Zheng's profile begins with frustrated ability rather than immediate influence. He enters Shu during disorder and famine, remains in minor posts under Liu Zhang, and is reportedly mocked despite his abilities. His friend Zhang Song recommends him as envoy to Liu Bei; Fa Zheng returns persuaded that Liu Bei can become a ruler worth serving, after which the two men begin planning to receive Liu Bei into Yizhou. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1123-ta-bu-shang-zhanchang-ruhe-li-xia-xianhe-zhangong-losloy1puogq4o9azo2x_ombrbyz|Hanji 1123]] supplies this early layer. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1124-liuzhang-qing-liubei-ru-shu-shi-qiusheng-haishi-songsi-lkzau4jsd4ocgkgw8cg9jjwshhlm|Hanji 1124]] then places him inside the executed invitation as Liu Zhang's envoy to welcome Liu Bei, while preserving Huang Quan and Wang Lei's opposition. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1125-bing-bu-xueren-zhuo-liuzhang-liubei-weihe-bu-yuanyi-lsaakh0scmfvorjhhtjv5j4m0kcb|Hanji 1125]] gives Fa Zheng's explicit argument that Liu Zhang's weakness and Zhang Song's internal support make Yizhou attainable, and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1126-maizhu-qiurong-de-zhangsong-zenme-si-de-lkfazjqcokpijxykqxagybgewhrfx|Hanji 1126]] carries the alignment through Zhang Song's death, Liu Bei's conquest, and Fa Zheng's later Hanzhong importance.

His later profile joins precise tactical value to morally troubling power use. At Dingjun Mountain, he is presented as recognizing how a particular position can be turned against [[XiahouYuan|夏侯渊]], placing him inside [[AdviserStateArchitecture|谋士政权架构]] as a campaign planner alongside, but not identical to, Zhuge Liang's broader state-system role. He is also portrayed as remembering small favors and resentments and using law-like or administrative instruments against private enemies. The current synthesis therefore retains both strategic ability and retaliatory danger rather than converting effectiveness into moral innocence. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-977-yi-zheng-yi-xie-luanshi-dushi-de-guijue-fengyun-lgtpgxl5anxial8ym2nt8dmuc3ee|Hanji 977]] ground these later layers.

## Key Characteristics

- Talented but underused Liu Zhang official whose frustration becomes part of an alignment shift.
- Close Zhang Song associate and envoy who turns insider access into an explicit argument for Liu Bei to take Yizhou.
- Survives the exposure of that project and converts alignment choice into strategic service during Liu Bei's Yizhou and Hanzhong campaigns.
- Tactical planner associated with exploiting terrain and timing at Dingjun Mountain.
- Adviser whose strengths are compared in the source tradition with Cao-side figures such as Cheng Yu and Guo Jia.
- Morally ambiguous officeholder whose formal tools can serve private resentment as well as public strategy.

- Invitation role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1124-liuzhang-qing-liubei-ru-shu-shi-qiusheng-haishi-songsi-lkzau4jsd4ocgkgw8cg9jjwshhlm|Hanji 1124]] says Liu Zhang sends Fa Zheng with a welcoming force after accepting Zhang Song's recommendation.
- Entry advice: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1125-bing-bu-xueren-zhuo-liuzhang-liubei-weihe-bu-yuanyi-lsaakh0scmfvorjhhtjv5j4m0kcb|Hanji 1125]] presents Fa Zheng as arguing that Liu Zhang's weakness plus Zhang Song's internal help makes the province vulnerable to takeover.
- Consequence of realignment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1126-maizhu-qiurong-de-zhangsong-zenme-si-de-lkfazjqcokpijxykqxagybgewhrfx|Hanji 1126]] presents Fa Zheng as helping Liu Bei take Yizhou and later contributing important judgment in the Hanzhong campaign.
- Tactical precision: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] presents Fa Zheng as identifying a usable position against Xiahou Yuan at Dingjun Mountain.
- Administrative revenge: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-977-yi-zheng-yi-xie-luanshi-dushi-de-guijue-fengyun-lgtpgxl5anxial8ym2nt8dmuc3ee|Hanji 977]] uses Fa Zheng as a case where legal or bureaucratic form can conceal retaliatory purpose.

## Qualifications

The six podcast notes are interpretive summaries rather than a complete biography. Hanji 1123's genealogy, migration motive, degree of underuse, hometown ridicule, feigned reluctance, private conversation, Liu Bei charisma, and Chen Shou comparison require direct textual corroboration. Hanji 1124's escort figure, exact role, and motive reconstruction remain source-scoped, as do Hanji 1125's confidence, wording, and private strategic intent; Hanji 1126's account of Fa Zheng's later contributions is compressed. Hanji 976 compresses the Dingjun campaign, while Hanji 977's revenge examples and legal mechanics are metaphor-heavy. The evidence does not establish that grievance alone caused Fa Zheng's realignment or that later tactical skill validates every method attributed to him.

## What Changed

- Added Fa Zheng's operational role in the invitation as Liu Zhang's welcoming envoy.
- Connected the covert alignment, executed entry channel, later argument for taking Yizhou, and eventual strategic service.
- Preserved the distinction between demonstrated strategic value and moral approval of Fa Zheng's methods.
