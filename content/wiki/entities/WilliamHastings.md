# William Hastings

## Overview

William Hastings is Edward IV's loyal court ally whose refusal to support Richard of Gloucester's likely move toward kingship made his sudden execution the crisis's clearest turn from managed succession to usurpation.

## Current Profile

Hastings began by seeking a working accommodation with Elizabeth Woodville despite factional tension. The episode suggests Richard later sounded him out about taking the crown; his continued loyalty to Edward V then made him an obstacle. His execution without ordinary process removed a central loyalist and signaled that council politics had become coercive regime change.

## Key Characteristics

- Longstanding loyalist of [[EdwardIV]].
- Participant in attempted compromise with [[ElizabethWoodville]].
- Opponent of replacing [[EdwardV]] despite hostility toward the Woodvilles.
- Summarily executed after Richard accused Elizabeth of witchcraft and Hastings of complicity.
- Threshold figure whose death marks the episode's shift into overt usurpation.

## Evidence

- Factional compromise: [[139-the-princes-in-the-tower-part-2-glt4685691538]] describes Hastings and Elizabeth trying to stabilize the transition.
- Loyalty and removal: [[139-the-princes-in-the-tower-part-2-glt4685691538]] infers that Richard tested Hastings's support and presents the execution as the removal of a loyalist obstacle.
- Escalation: [[139-the-princes-in-the-tower-part-2-glt4685691538]] places his death immediately before the second prince's transfer and the legitimacy campaign.

## Qualifications

Richard's private approach to Hastings is the hosts' reconstruction rather than directly demonstrated fact. The episode treats the witchcraft and treason accusations as politically convenient rather than independently substantiated.

## What Changed

- Created a profile of Hastings as the loyalist whose removal marks the coercive threshold of the crisis.

## Relationships

- [[EdwardIV]] - king he had loyally served.
- [[EdwardV]] - heir whose title he continued to support.
- [[ElizabethWoodville]] - factional rival with whom he attempted compromise.
- [[RichardIII]] - protector who ordered his execution.
