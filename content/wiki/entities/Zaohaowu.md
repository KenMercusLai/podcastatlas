# 造好物 / Zaohaowu

## Overview

造好物 / Zaohaowu is a [[ShumeiWanwu|Shumei Wanwu]] consumer product described in the source as turning user ideas, images, and personalized concepts into manufactured physical goods.

## Current Profile

Zaohaowu is the source's clearest demand-and-delivery validation surface. Ren presents it as a way to let ordinary users experience "what you see is what you get" physical creation, while the company uses high-concurrency personalized orders to test whether AI 3D output is close enough to production-ready to avoid heavy manual rework.

The product also supplies a feedback loop: users receiving physical goods may post again, submit more works, and help reveal what model, design, fulfillment, and quality standards must improve.

## Key Characteristics

- Consumer-facing path from concept or image to personalized physical object.
- Common use cases include figures, pets, toys, family/friend likenesses, and creative characters.
- Reported submission rate is above 20%, defined as daily work-posting users divided by daily active users.
- Personalized printing is described with a target gross margin above 50%.
- Delivery discipline is measured through a T+7 shipping-information goal and reported 90%-95% on-time rate.
- Functions as proof that production-grade direct output matters because excessive manual model repair would break order economics and timing.
