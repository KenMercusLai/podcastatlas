# 赵冲 / Zhao Chong (late Han)

## Overview

Zhao Chong is a source-bounded late Han frontier officer who appears in the 143-144 CE Qiang campaigns under [[EmperorShunOfHan|Emperor Shun of Han]]. His profile is defined by tactical success against the Shaodang Qiang followed by death in an overextended pursuit after surrendered Hu auxiliaries defect.

## Current Profile

In the 143 CE layer, Zhao Chong works with Zhang Gong against the Shaodang Qiang at 参丝 and later attacks again at 阿阳. The episode presents these actions as materially weakening the Qiang even before his final pursuit fails.

In 144 CE, Zhao Chong pursues rebel Qiang after hundreds of surrendered Hu defect from his command. He dies in ambush or defeat, but the court still recognizes his accumulated frontier work by enfeoffing his son as a亭侯.

## Key Characteristics

- Frontier commander whose remembered value rests on cumulative campaigning rather than survival.
- Qiang-war figure whose victories reduce enemy momentum before his death.
- Example of the late Han court rewarding service even when the final operation fails.
- Source-bounded case of operational risk: pursuit after auxiliary defection turns prior advantage into personal catastrophe.
