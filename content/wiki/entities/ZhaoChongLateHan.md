# 赵冲 / Zhao Chong (late Han)

## Overview

Zhao Chong is a source-bounded late Han frontier officer whose current profile now begins in the 141 CE Western Qiang aftermath and continues through the 143-144 CE Qiang campaigns under [[EmperorShunOfHan|Emperor Shun of Han]]. He is defined by repeated tactical usefulness on a failing frontier: pursuit and surrender success after [[MaXianLateHan|Ma Xian]]'s defeat, later victories with Zhang Gong, and final death in an overextended pursuit after surrendered Hu auxiliaries defect.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-821-budong-laoban-xinsi-haixiang-jinsheng-meimen-lmjxr8fdxgw-colrlinmujba74fh|Hanji 821]] supplies Zhao Chong's earliest current layer. After Ma Xian's defeat worsens the Western Qiang situation, Zhao Chong pursues rebel forces, kills or captures several hundred people, and induces more than two thousand surrenders. Shun then appoints him to command the Hexi four-commandery local troops.

In the 143 CE layer from [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-824-weishenme-women-yao-xue-shouzhuo-lpssy8ltykugdpvmgrzwcpf6-9v9|Hanji 824]], Zhao Chong works with Zhang Gong against the Shaodang Qiang at 参丝 and later attacks again at 阿阳. The episode presents these actions as materially weakening the Qiang even before his final pursuit fails.

In 144 CE, Zhao Chong pursues rebel Qiang after hundreds of surrendered Hu defect from his command. He dies in ambush or defeat, but the court still recognizes his accumulated frontier work by enfeoffing his son as a亭侯.

## Key Characteristics

- Frontier commander whose remembered value rests on cumulative campaigning rather than survival.
- Qiang-war figure whose victories and surrender work reduce enemy momentum after earlier command failure.
- Officer promoted into Hexi command responsibility after partial success in 141.
- Example of the late Han court rewarding service even when the final operation fails.
- Source-bounded case of operational risk: pursuit after auxiliary defection turns prior advantage into personal catastrophe.

## Qualifications

The page is limited to Hanji 821 and Hanji 824 and does not reconstruct Zhao Chong's full military career. Hanji 821 presents his 141 actions as useful but not decisive, because wider Qiang pressure continues. The podcast transcript form 赵充 is normalized to the received Tongjian form 赵冲 because the surrounding events match the received text.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 821 as Zhao Chong's earlier 141 CE pursuit, surrender, and Hexi command layer.
- Reframed Hanji 824's 143-144 operations as a continuation of earlier frontier responsibility rather than a standalone appearance.
