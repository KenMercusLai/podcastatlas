# Operation Demetrius

Operation Demetrius appears in [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] as the internment operation launched before dawn on 9 August 1971. British troops raided homes across [[NorthernIreland]] using outdated [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]] intelligence files that did not properly distinguish the [[OfficialIRA]] from the [[ProvisionalIRA]].

The episode says the operation arrested 342 people, all Catholics and no Protestants, then triggered riots, barricades, burned houses, mass displacement, and 17 deaths in gun battles with the [[BritishArmy]]. It is the source's sharpest case of [[IntelligenceFailureMassArrest]] and [[CounterinsurgencyLegitimacyTrap]].
