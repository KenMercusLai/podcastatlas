# 郭闳 / Guo Hong (late Han)

## Overview

Guo Hong is a Huan-era frontier official introduced in Hanji 855 as the Liangzhou inspector whose desire to share military credit obstructs [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]] during a Qiang campaign.

## Current Profile

The current evidence is narrow but consequential. Guo Hong appears when [[XianlingQiang|先零羌]], [[ShendiQiang|沈氐羌]], and other Qiang groups attack Bingzhou and [[LiangzhouLateHan|Liangzhou]]. Instead of enabling Duan Jiong's campaign, he tries to capture part of the merit, blocks military movement, and delays the opportunity.

The episode then turns Guo Hong's obstruction into a command-failure case. Hu soldiers serving far from home desert during the delay, but Guo Hong shifts the blame onto Duan Jiong. The court recalls Duan, imprisons him, and sends him to Left School hard labor, making Guo Hong a source-bounded example of merit-seeking interference becoming wrongful punishment.

## Key Characteristics

- Liangzhou inspector in a Huan-era Qiang-front crisis.
- Seeks a share of campaign credit rather than preserving unified command movement.
- Obstructs Duan Jiong's advance, causing operational delay.
- Shifts responsibility for the failed campaign outcome onto Duan Jiong.
- Functions in the wiki as a negative contrast to root-cause, public-duty frontier governance.
