# 裴文中

裴文中 enters the wiki through [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] as the fieldworker who remained at [[Zhoukoudian|周口店]] when confidence was low and discovered the first Peking Man skull near the end of a dig. The episode presents him as a concrete example of discovery depending on patient local presence, not only on famous institutions or foreign funding.

His story sits inside the transition from geology and mining-related survey to paleontology and human-origins research. The source also notes the later loss of the skulls during wartime and the symbolic importance of later bone fragments that could fit earlier models.

## Key Claims
- Pei Wenzhong's discovery shows how [[FieldworkAsKnowledgeMethod]] can depend on staying with an uncertain site.
- The source links him to the institutional shift from geological survey to paleontology.
- The lost Peking Man skulls make [[ScholarshipInWartime]] a problem of physical evidence, not only people and schools.
