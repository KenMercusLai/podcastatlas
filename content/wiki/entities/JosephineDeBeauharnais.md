# Josephine de Beauharnais

## Overview

Josephine de Beauharnais was a Martinique-born French social figure, survivor of revolutionary imprisonment, mother of Eugène and Hortense, and the woman Napoleon married before taking command of the Army of Italy.

## Current Profile

The source presents Josephine as socially experienced and politically connected after an abusive marriage to Alexandre de Beauharnais, his execution, and her own imprisonment during the Terror. Relationships with Lazare Hoche and [[PaulBarras|Paul Barras]] placed her within post-Thermidorian networks. [[NapoleonBonaparte|Napoleon]] became intensely attached to her even though her initial response was ambivalent; their marriage connected his emotional life and emerging family role to his military ascent.

## Key Characteristics

- She survived the Terror after imprisonment in Les Carmes and the execution of her first husband.
- She was socially sophisticated and well connected in post-Thermidorian Paris.
- She was mother to Eugène and Hortense, toward whom Napoleon behaved warmly.
- Her initial ambivalence contrasted with Napoleon's obsessive attachment.
- Her marriage to Napoleon immediately preceded his departure to command the Army of Italy.
