# 谭思维 / Tan Siwei

## Overview
谭思维 is the anorectal clinician featured in a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode about hemorrhoids, fissure, fistula, rectal bleeding, procedure selection, and postoperative care.

## Current Profile
In the available source, 谭思维 translates anorectal surgery into symptom-led, function-preserving decisions. The profile distinguishes common hemorrhoidal tissue from symptomatic disease, explains why recurrent bleeding still needs assessment, and frames surgery as a tradeoff among symptom relief, continence preservation, wound burden, pain, recovery time, work, appearance preferences, and recurrence rather than as guaranteed eradication.

The same practical approach continues after a procedure. Cleaning, stool consistency, rest, pain control, and communication with the treating team are treated as parts of the intervention, while persistent bleeding or infection signs require escalation rather than improvised home management.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains common anorectal conditions in non-stigmatizing language for lay listeners.
- Separates the presence of hemorrhoidal tissue from symptoms that justify treatment.
- Uses anatomy, symptom burden, function, preferences, and recovery cost to frame procedure choice.
- Emphasizes preservation of continence structures during fistula and hemorrhoid treatment.
- Treats postoperative hygiene, bowel management, rest, and warning signs as integral to outcomes.
