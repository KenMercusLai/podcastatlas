## Overview

耿夔 / Geng Kui is now a two-front Eastern Han commander in the wiki's bounded evidence. Hanji 763 places him with [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]] in the 91 CE pursuit and defeat of the [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]] chanyu at Jinwei Mountain, while Hanji 776 later records him as the Liaodong administrator who counterattacks [[GoguryeoLateHan|Goguryeo]] after king Gong raids Han border counties.

## Current Profile

Hanji 763 supplies the earliest current layer. In 91 CE, after [[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]] attacks badly weaken the northern chanyu's camp, Geng Kui and Ren Shang lead troops out through Juyan, defeat Northern Xiongnu forces at Jinwei Mountain, and force the chanyu to flee. The page therefore now treats Geng as part of [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]]'s wider Northern Xiongnu campaign field, even though Hanji 763's main commemorative focus falls on [[JiluoshanBattleLateHan|Jiluoshan]] and [[YanranInscription|Yanran]].

Hanji 776 adds the later northeastern layer. Goguryeo king Gong enters the Liaodong frontier and plunders six subordinate counties. In autumn of the same year, Geng Kui attacks Goguryeo and defeats its forces. The source does not provide more tactical detail, but it gives Geng a second clear place in the [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]] frontier field before later An-era Goguryeo material.

## Key Characteristics

- Commander in the Jinwei Mountain pursuit against the Northern Xiongnu chanyu.
- Actor inside Dou Xian's wider Northern Xiongnu campaign field.
- Liaodong administrator in the later bounded source.
- Frontier commander associated with the 105 CE response to Goguryeo raiding.
- Source-bounded actor whose current profile remains narrower than a full Geng-family or frontier-command biography.

## Qualifications

This page should not be used as a full biography of Geng Kui, a complete reconstruction of the Northern Xiongnu campaign, or a complete history of Han-Goguryeo conflict. The wiki currently treats the Jinwei and Liaodong notices as belonging to the same canonical Geng Kui page, but the detailed office sequence, troop numbers, routes, and command hierarchy remain source-scoped unless later bounded notes require a split or expansion.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 763 as Geng Kui's earliest current layer: the Jinwei Mountain defeat of the Northern Xiongnu chanyu.
- Reframed the older Hanji 776 Goguryeo material as a later northeastern frontier notice rather than the whole current profile.
