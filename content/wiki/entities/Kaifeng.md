# 开封 / Kaifeng

Kaifeng / 开封 is the capital city foregrounded in [[169-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-shang-957052909]]. The episode treats it as both the practical center of the [[ChenQiaoMutiny|陈桥兵变]] and a long-term strategic compromise for the [[NorthernSong|北宋]].

In the coup story, control of Kaifeng's gates helps [[ZhaoKuangyin|赵匡胤]] return from Chenqiao and complete the transfer of power. In the capital-choice discussion, the source argues that Kaifeng was easier to supply by canal and southern grain transport than Luoyang or Chang'an, but also geographically exposed and therefore dependent on heavy defense.

The city therefore anchors [[KaifengCapitalTradeoff|开封定都取舍]]: economic integration and transport convenience came at the price of military vulnerability and a defensive posture the episode summarizes as "守内虚外."
