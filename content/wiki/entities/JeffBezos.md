# Jeff Bezos

Jeff Bezos appears in [[tsr-s4-ronconway-part3-v2-tsr-s4-ronconway-part3-v2]] as one of the early angel investors [[RonConway]] names in [[Google]]. The source does not expand on Bezos's decision-making, but his presence reinforces that Google reached a credible network of internet-era operators before the [[KleinerPerkins]] and [[SequoiaCapital]] venture round.

In this wiki branch, Bezos is therefore a supporting node in Google's early investor network rather than a full [[Amazon]] founder biography. The page should be expanded when a source covers Amazon or Bezos directly.

[[omission-accomplished-why-the-iran-war-cycle-spins-on-6a6c7475aa1642a30f17f1cb]] adds Bezos through [[BlueOrigin]] and [[WallyFunk]]. The episode says Bezos invited Funk to fly as his guest on a Blue Origin rocket when she was 82, turning Bezos from a Google angel-investor node into a supporting figure in the wiki's private-spaceflight and [[GenderBarrierSpaceflight]] branch.
