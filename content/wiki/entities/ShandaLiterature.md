# Shanda Literature / 盛大文学

Shanda Literature / 盛大文学 is the web-fiction platform group discussed in [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]]. The episode presents it as the most durable result of [[Shanda|盛大]]'s post-game content strategy: starting from assets such as [[Qidian|起点中文网]], it gathered multiple Chinese web-fiction sites and at one point held a dominant market share.

The source treats Shanda Literature as the part of [[FamilyInternetPortalStrategy]] that survived the failure of [[ShandaBox|盛大盒子]]. Its later merger with Tencent Literature into [[YuewenGroup|阅文集团]] moved Shanda's web-fiction legacy into a different platform ecosystem.
