# 《我许可》 / Wo Xuke

《我许可》 is the film at the center of [[ep254-you-xiaoying-x-wenqi-x-du-sujuan-nvxing-zhe-yisheng-dou-zai-fanfu-xuexi-xuke-ziji-lm-w7ryj4zay6vldsrkadohj997d]]. The episode does not treat it mainly as plot recap; it uses the film's three-generation structure around 黄薇, 许可, and 胡春荣 to discuss how women learn [[SelfPermission|permission]] through body experience, refusal, friendship, and mother-daughter recognition.

For the wiki, the film matters as a cultural object that concentrates several existing women-and-body branches. Menarche, menstruation concealment, harassment, gynecological exams, beauty anxiety, "I want", female friendship, and maternal sacrifice all become connected cases inside [[FemaleSelfPossession]] rather than separate topics.
