# Kanye West

Kanye West is the artist and fashion collaborator discussed in [[vol-271-a-di-da-si-ruhe-chudi-fantan-1007333155]] through [[Adidas]]' [[Yeezy]] partnership. The source frames him as a celebrity-collaboration risk: the partnership created major revenue and profit contribution, but the 2022 break after his public controversy turned remaining Yeezy goods into a sudden inventory and brand-governance problem.

## Key Claims
- The source says the first Adidas Yeezy shoe appeared in 2015.
- The episode cites an outside estimate that Yeezy contributed large revenue and disproportionate profit to Adidas in 2020 and 2021; the wiki records that as source-scoped.
- The 2022 controversy and termination show how a celebrity-linked product line can create a sharper shock than an ordinary style decline.
