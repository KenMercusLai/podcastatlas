# 宋皇后 / Empress Song

Song Empress / 宋皇后 is [[ZhaoKuangyin|赵匡胤 / 宋太祖]]'s empress in [[170-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-xia-958158115]]. She appears at the decisive moment after Taizu's death, when she reportedly sends [[WangJien|王继恩]] to summon [[ZhaoDefang|赵德芳]].

The source emphasizes that Wang Jien does not follow that instruction and instead brings [[ZhaoGuangyi|赵光义 / 宋太宗]]. Song Empress then asks whether Defang has arrived, loses color when told the Jin王 has come, and quickly addresses Zhao Guangyi as the new ruler.

Her role shows how succession can become irreversible before formal deliberation. The episode reads her response as a forced adjustment to power already in motion rather than as free endorsement of Taizong's claim.
