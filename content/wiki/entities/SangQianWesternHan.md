## Overview

桑迁 / Sang Qian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-402-1-sanghongyang-ceng-bang-hanwudi-zhifu-weihe-zuihou-canzao-miezu-ltpcos20d5mafcoywmfcb6iifwsv|Hanji 402-1]] as [[SangHongyangWesternHan|桑弘羊]]'s son, whose flight after the Sang purge triggers the Hou Shiwu harboring case and the later punishment of officials who read the amnesty narrowly.

## Current Profile

Sang Qian is not developed as an independent political actor in the source. His importance is legal and relational: he is a fugitive son of a condemned minister, hidden by [[HouShiWuWesternHan|侯史吴]], later captured and sentenced to death. Huo Guang's reviewers treat his knowledge of his father's rebellion and failure to prevent it as enough to make him rebellion-equivalent, which turns those who sheltered or pardoned around him into suspect actors.

## Key Characteristics

- Son of Sang Hongyang in the Han Zhao-era purge field.
- Fugitive sheltered by Hou Shiwu after Sang Hongyang's destruction.
- Death-sentenced figure whose case tests whether an amnesty covers related but previously undiscovered conduct.
- Treated by Huo Guang's stricter review as rebellion-equivalent because he knew and did not stop his father's plot.
- Legal hinge connecting Sang Hongyang's execution to the punishment of Hou Shiwu, Wang Ping, and Xu Ren.
