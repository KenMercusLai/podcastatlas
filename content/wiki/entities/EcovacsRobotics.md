# Ecovacs Robotics / 科沃斯

## Overview
Ecovacs Robotics / 科沃斯 is the Chinese household-robot company used in [[shangye-xiaoyang-50-dou-zai-taolun-jiawu-jiqiren-buru-guanxin-daodi-you-shenme-jiawu-1017031596|商业小样50]] to show how a mature floor-cleaning capability stack can expand into adjacent chores.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Ecovacs less as a single-category vacuum maker than as a repeatable product-development system. Capabilities built around sensing, positioning, mapping, path planning, and motion control transfer from DEEBOT into WINBOT window cleaning, GOAT lawn mowing, and ULTRAMARINE pool cleaning, but each new environment requires specific engineering and market learning. Its strategic claim is therefore not simple reuse: value comes from combining a reusable core with the difficult remainder that makes a real chore safe and reliable.

## Key Characteristics
- Reuses a mature mobile-robot stack across multiple household-cleaning environments.
- Treats vertical glass, outdoor lawns, and underwater pools as distinct engineering systems rather than cosmetic product variants.
- Uses long product iteration to wait for category demand, with WINBOT as the episode's main patient-development case.
- Builds overseas fit through local dealers, teams, channels, and housing-pattern knowledge.
- Frames competitive advantage as an organizational ability to discover and satisfy needs repeatedly, not as one protected feature.

## Qualifications
This profile rests on a sponsored short episode centered on Ecovacs. The source provides little competitor comparison, does not examine failed projects, and does not establish the profitability or return on investment of each new category. Sales, penetration, and growth figures remain source-scoped.

- Recorded localization and long iteration as parts of the moat, not merely supporting execution.
