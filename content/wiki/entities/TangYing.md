# Tang Ying / 唐英

Tang Ying / 唐英 is the Qing official and porcelain manager in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode says he was sent to assist the imperial kiln under the Yongzheng reign, learned the craft by living and working closely with artisans, and later became a major figure in Jingdezhen imperial porcelain.

Tang matters because he is an administrative example of [[ProcessKnowledge]]. His work at the [[JingdezhenImperialKilnFactory]] joined management, technical trial, imitation of famous wares, new glaze and vessel experiments, and written documentation of the ceramic workflow.
