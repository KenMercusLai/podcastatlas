# Michael Gove

## Overview

Michael Gove is a British Conservative politician presented in [[205-the-last-days-of-boris-johnson-glt2329301983]] as a former [[BorisJohnson|Boris Johnson]] ally, a prominent [[Brexit]] campaigner, and the levelling-up secretary dismissed during Johnson's July 2022 government collapse.

## Current Profile

The episode treats Gove less as a full biographical subject than as a diagnostic figure in Johnson's fall. He had worked with Johnson during the Brexit campaign, then opposed Johnson's first leadership bid, leaving a history of alliance and grievance. By July 2022 the hosts regarded him as one of the government's more effective ministers and his department as central to Johnson's levelling-up programme. Johnson's decision to dismiss him while ministers were already departing is therefore presented as a sign that personal retaliation had displaced administrative continuity.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior Conservative politician associated with the Brexit campaign.
- Former Johnson ally whose later leadership intervention damaged their relationship.
- Levelling-up secretary during the final crisis of Johnson's premiership.
- Dismissal used by the episode as evidence of personal grievance overriding government capacity.
