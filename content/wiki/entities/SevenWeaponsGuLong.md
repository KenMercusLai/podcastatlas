# 《七种武器》 / Seven Weapons

## Overview

《七种武器》 / Seven Weapons is a [[GuLong|古龙]] wuxia story cycle in which each nominal weapon gives way to a human capacity or moral claim. The bounded sources discuss [[LongevitySwordGuLong|《长生剑》]] and [[PeacockFeatherGuLong|《孔雀翎》]] while treating six stories, rather than a completed set of seven, as the generally accepted core.

## Current Profile

Across the two [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episodes, the cycle combines independent plots with a shared Jianghu background and recurring Green Dragon Society pressure. Its formal promise is allegorical: spectacular weapons attract pursuit and establish stakes, but the ending identifies some less tangible force—“smile” in 《长生剑》 and confidence in 《孔雀翎》—as the true weapon.

The sources also expose a recurring critical tension. The symbolic conclusion may remain memorable even when the chain of borrowing, theft, sale, disguise, betrayal, or final revelation looks under-motivated. The cycle is therefore represented here as both a successful machine for psychological reframing and a case where aphoristic endings invite scrutiny of the plots that support them.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses a shared Jianghu and the Green Dragon Society without requiring one continuous plot.
- Replaces physical weapons with psychological, relational, or moral capacities.
- Builds suspense through concealed identities, false objects, betrayal, and delayed explanation.
- Treats belief about a weapon as causally important alongside the weapon's actual capability.
- Produces compact thematic endings whose clarity can exceed their causal preparation.

## Qualifications

The two bounded sources are conversational readings of only two installments, not a publication history or survey of the complete cycle. They report six stories as the generally accepted set and note disputed attempts to count other works, but this page does not independently resolve the bibliography. The Green Dragon Society's continuity and the relation among the stories are therefore described only at the level established by these episodes.

## What Changed

- Created a cycle-level profile connecting 《长生剑》 and 《孔雀翎》.
- Distinguished the weapon-to-virtue design from the causal credibility of individual plots.
