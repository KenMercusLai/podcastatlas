# Kamala Harris

Kamala Harris appears in [[tsr-s5-ronconway-v5-tsr-s5-ronconway-v5]] as Vice President during the [[SiliconValleyBank]] weekend. [[RonConway]] says he had known Harris since her time as San Francisco district attorney and that her office called him on Sunday as the decision deadline approached.

Conway says Harris asked for the situation straight, heard that California small businesses could miss payroll and that bank contagion could spread, then called [[JanetYellen]], President Biden, Jeff Zients, and [[JeromePowell]] with urgency. The page keeps that sequence source-scoped because the episode is Conway's participant account.
