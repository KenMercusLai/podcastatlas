# 3AM Innovations

## Overview

3AM Innovations is a public-safety technology company presented by co-founder and COO [[RyanLitt]] as building situational-awareness tools for firefighters and incident commanders.

## Current Profile

The company combines AI-assisted radio monitoring, phones, wearables, network data, and three-dimensional personnel location. Its intended role is to surface distress signals and responder location without requiring firefighters to manage another screen or procedure during an incident.

The profile comes from a sponsored episode and does not independently establish product accuracy, deployment scale, safety effects, security, or comparative performance.

## Key Characteristics

- Builds technology around fire-service observation and responder workflow.
- Uses AI to prioritize concerning radio traffic rather than issue autonomous commands.
- Combines multiple sensor and network inputs to estimate responder location, including floor level.
- Treats unobtrusive operation as a safety and adoption requirement.
- Records incidents for later reconstruction and organizational learning.
