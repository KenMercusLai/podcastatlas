# Neil Johnston

## Overview

Neil Johnston is the [[NationalArchivesUK|National Archives]] guide and co-author who interprets the exhibition *Treason: People, Power and Plot* in episode 249 of [[TheRestIsHistory|The Rest Is History]].

## Current Profile

Johnston uses statutes, trial records, proclamations, notebooks, and court-martial papers to explain treason as an adaptable relationship among law, government power, public spectacle, and disputed sovereignty. His examples range from the trial of [[CharlesIOfEngland|Charles I]] to [[WilliamJoyce|William Joyce]], with particular attention to how evidence and procedure become politically meaningful when allegiance is contested.

## Key Characteristics

- Archival interpreter of British treason law and political history.
- Guide to the exhibition *Treason: People, Power and Plot*.
- Emphasizes the theatrical and political dimensions of treason trials.
- Connects legal change to shifting definitions of monarch, state, nation, and people.
- Co-author of the exhibition's accompanying book, *A History of Treason*.
