# 侯嬴 / Hou Ying

侯嬴 / Hou Ying enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-3-lsrmq1-rjqnsja8g07bhaqvhxhpi]] as the [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] adviser who judges whether [[YuQing|虞卿]] and [[WeiQiWarringStates|魏齐]] should be received after fleeing [[ZhaoState|赵国]].

His role is brief but structurally important. When Xinlingjun hesitates because receiving Wei Qi risks offending [[QinState|秦国]], Hou Ying answers by stressing how hard it is to know others and be known by others. He points to Yu Qing's history: repeated recognition by [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]], rise to high office, and willingness to abandon wealth and rank for a friend.

The episode uses Hou Ying as a gatekeeper of aristocratic reputation. His argument turns hospitality from a private courtesy into a public test of character: if Xinlingjun refuses a person who has already sacrificed office for friendship, the refusal would damage his own standing as one of the [[WarringStatesFourLords|战国四公子]].
