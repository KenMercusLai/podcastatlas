# 荆南 / Jingnan

Jingnan / 荆南 is the small regime that [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]] uses as the main "参与型" example in [[TenKingdomsRelationshipTypes]]. The source presents it as a power surviving among stronger neighbors by participating, bargaining, and maneuvering in multiple directions.

Its importance is analytical rather than territorial size. Jingnan shows that [[FiveDynastiesTenKingdoms|五代十国]] cannot be understood only through large states; small regimes also reveal how diplomacy and position could become survival strategy.
