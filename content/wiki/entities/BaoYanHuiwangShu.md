# 《报燕惠王书》 / Letter in Reply to King Hui of Yan

《报燕惠王书》 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-80-zhugeliang-de-ouxiang-daodi-you-duo-lihai-lse6qmqqhoyqdzqjl4qfuvioeiw0]] as [[LeYi|乐毅]]'s answer to [[YanHuiwang|燕惠王]] after Le Yi has fled to [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. The episode presents Yan Huiwang's letter as a defensive accusation: he regrets the lost Qi occupation but asks why Le Yi abandoned Yan and how he can face [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]]'s former favor.

Le Yi's reply is both refusal and restraint. He uses [[WuZixu|伍子胥]] to explain why a minister must read the ruler in front of him rather than rely on earlier merit, says he will not use [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]]'s favor to attack [[YanState|燕国]], and invokes the ethic that a gentleman does not speak evil after relations end. The source says [[CaoCao|曹操]] was moved to tears when reading the letter, making it part of Le Yi's later reception as a model of exposed-command exit.
