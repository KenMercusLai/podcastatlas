# Red Hat

## Overview
Red Hat is presented in the bounded source as [[IBM]]'s open-source enterprise-software partner in [[ProjectLightwell]].

## Current Profile
The episode positions Red Hat between open-source communities and large institutional users. That intermediary experience is offered as a reason it could help coordinate vulnerability intake, repair, and upstream contribution at a scale individual companies or volunteer maintainers cannot reach alone. The source does not independently evaluate Red Hat's authority across communities, operating capacity, or results under the new initiative.

## Key Characteristics
- Bridges open-source software and enterprise users in the source's account.
- Co-sponsors Project Lightwell with IBM.
- Contributes community and enterprise coordination to the proposed repair clearinghouse.
- Is represented in the episode by [[GunnarHellekson|Gunnar Hellekson]].
