# 南汉 / Southern Han

Southern Han / 南汉 is the Lingnan regime used in [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]] as the main "偏远型" case in [[TenKingdomsRelationshipTypes]]. Its distance from the central plains gave it more room to adjust posture as northern power rose or weakened.

The episode also names Guangzhou-area Southern Han tombs as part of the material record that has made Ten Kingdoms study more locally grounded in recent decades.
