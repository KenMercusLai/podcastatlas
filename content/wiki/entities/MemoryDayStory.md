# 《记忆日》

[[MemoryDayStory|《记忆日》]] is a [[SarahPinsker|Sarah Pinsker / 莎拉·平斯克]] story discussed in [[166-youyou-changfeng-wenrou-de-huanxiang-zhi-ge-fuping-jiaolv-de-gouhe-948043059]]. In the episode's account, it is set after a large war and follows a narrator, grandmother, and veteran mother through public commemoration, family loss, and the use of a memory-shielding tool called "沙漠".

The story gives the source its sharpest ethical branch. The "沙漠" tool can reduce veteran suffering, but it can also erase the narrator's dead father from the mother's accessible memory and convert private pain into a collective annual vote. The episode's reading of [[MemoryDeletionEthics]] therefore links trauma relief to agency, responsibility, and historical reckoning rather than treating forgetting as a clean medical fix.
