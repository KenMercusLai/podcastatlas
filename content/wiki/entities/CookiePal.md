# Cookie Pal

Cookie Pal is the human-grade pet treat brand launched by [[RiversideNaturalFoods]] and discussed in [[madegood-salma-and-nima-fotovat-lost-their-first-business-they-grew-their-next-one-into-a-snack-giant-cf5715bf-e9e0-45e0-a8a9-1256a7a4ed0d]]. The source contrasts it with [[GoodToGo]]: Cookie Pal remained active because it had its own team and was less distracting from [[MadeGood]].

## Key Points
- The opportunity came from a [[Costco]] buyer who moved to the pet department and saw demand for human-grade pet treats.
- The episode presents Cookie Pal as an adjacent brand that could work because it was structurally separated enough from MadeGood's core operating focus.
- Its path reinforces the idea that retailer pull can create product opportunities, but only if the company can resource them without weakening the core brand.
