# Horizon Robotics / 地平线

## Overview
Horizon Robotics is presented in the source as a physical-AI platform company supplying chips, algorithms, and system capabilities for intelligent vehicles and robots rather than manufacturing complete robot bodies.

## Current Profile
The episode frames Horizon's automotive concentration as the first commercial stage of a longer robotics strategy. Mass-produced cars provide a mature supply chain, large deployment volume, engineering feedback, data, and cash flow; the company then aims to reuse chip design, perception, decision, control, and data-loop experience in robot platforms while acknowledging that multi-joint bodies, balance, contact, and manipulation require new control data and hardware capabilities.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines algorithm and chip development to bridge fast model change and multi-year silicon cycles.
- Treats vehicles as an early, scaled physical-AI product and robots as a broader later platform opportunity.
- Supplies “brain,” “small-brain,” development-tool, and system capabilities rather than complete robot bodies.
- Positions itself as a horizontal platform serving multiple automakers and robot developers.
- Uses developer tools, sample projects, university collaboration, and competitions as an ecosystem-seeding strategy.
- Emphasizes long-cycle product definition and advance trend judgment over reacting to current hype.

- [[tefan-cong-hui-kaiche-dao-hui-zuoshi-ai-ruhe-jinru-zhenshi-shijie-gkwrijeopcilaoo4hwtn2dns]] says the robot offering covers high-level decision, low-level control, feedback, tools, examples, and support for varied bodies, with universities and young developers treated as early ecosystem participants.

## Qualifications
- The profile is based on a sponsored or company-centered live podcast without automaker, regulator, customer, or independent researcher cross-examination.
- The claimed platform advantages, future market split, transfer from cars to robots, and commercial timing are strategic statements rather than independently validated outcomes.
- Vehicle intelligence does not automatically solve robot manipulation, balance, body reliability, action-data, or use-case economics.
