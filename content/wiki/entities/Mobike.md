# Mobike / 摩拜

## Overview
Mobike / 摩拜 is the shared-bike platform the wiki uses to represent the custom-hardware, smart-lock, and high-asset-cost side of China's shared-bike boom.

## Current Profile
[[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]] first introduced Mobike as a custom-bike inventory-risk case: platform-specific bikes could become hard to resell if shared-bike demand collapsed. The No.218 O2O episode adds the broader company arc through [[HuWeiwei|胡玮炜]], early backing from [[LiBinNio|李斌]], later operations under Wang Xiaofeng, dense financing, rivalry with [[Ofo|OFO]], Tencent-linked payment data arguments, and the 2018 sale to [[Meituan]].

The current synthesis is that Mobike's strength and weakness were the same thing. Smart locks, positioning, durable frames, solid tires, shaft drive, integrated wheels, and riding-generated power gave it better operating control and design credibility, but also produced high unit cost, heavy assets, and visible losses even after acquisition.

## Key Characteristics
- Mobike emphasized dockless shared-bike reliability through smart locks, positioning, and durable custom hardware.
- Its first-generation bike represented a much more expensive and engineered route than OFO's early low-cost model.
- Its product design became a defensible user and operations story, but also created [[InventoryWriteDownRisk]] if demand collapsed.
- Tencent-backed data arguments and smart-lock debates made Mobike a platform-data and hardware-control case.
- Its acquisition by Meituan was a consolidation outcome rather than proof that the standalone economics had already worked.

## Evidence
- Custom-asset risk - [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]] says Mobike-style bikes used solid tires, reinforced frames, and electronic locks that were less useful in ordinary resale channels.
- Founder and backing - [[no-218-o2o-yu-gongxiangjingji-shuyu-danche-chongdianbao-shangmenmeijia-he-anmo-de-mohuan-saqian-niandai-1013073318]] says Hu Weiwei prepared Mobike and Li Bin provided early funds and backing.
- Product route - [[no-218-o2o-yu-gongxiangjingji-shuyu-danche-chongdianbao-shangmenmeijia-he-anmo-de-mohuan-saqian-niandai-1013073318]] describes shaft drive, solid tires, aluminum body, integrated wheels, riding-generated power, and an approximate 3000-yuan first-generation unit cost.
- Capital and rivalry - [[no-218-o2o-yu-gongxiangjingji-shuyu-danche-chongdianbao-shangmenmeijia-he-anmo-de-mohuan-saqian-niandai-1013073318]] traces rapid financing, Beijing and Shanghai deployment, the Ma Huateng/Zhu Xiaohu smart-lock debate, and the subsidy war with OFO.
- Acquisition economics - [[no-218-o2o-yu-gongxiangjingji-shuyu-danche-chongdianbao-shangmenmeijia-he-anmo-de-mohuan-saqian-niandai-1013073318]] cites Meituan's acquisition price, assumed debt, ride volume, revenue, and post-close loss figures as source-scoped evidence that scale still carried heavy losses.

## Qualifications
The wiki treats acquisition price, debt, ride volume, revenue, loss, and market-share figures as source-scoped. Mobike's product reputation does not by itself establish durable standalone profitability.
