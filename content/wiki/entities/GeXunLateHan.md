# 葛勋 / Ge Xun (late Han)

葛勋 / Ge Xun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-951-tianjiang-hengcai-jiujing-shi-haoshi-haishi-huaishi-lovwgng2wrtfno-s9ttpybbhksvh|Hanji 951]] as the Jingzhao official summoned to court while [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]] holds troops at Fufeng. He privately discusses an anti-[[DongZhuo|董卓]] opening with Huangfu Song, but lacks enough force to act once Huangfu Song refuses.

The same source also gives Ge Xun a remonstrance scene. When Dong Zhuo threatens [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]] during a military discussion, Ge Xun invokes Wu Ding's willingness to listen and persuades Dong Zhuo to apologize. The episode therefore makes Ge Xun a rare example of effective but fragile [[DefensiveSpeechUnderTyranny|defensive speech]] inside a court where other critics are being removed or killed.
