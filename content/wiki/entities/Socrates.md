# Socrates / 苏格拉底

## Overview

Socrates / 苏格拉底 appears as a philosophical mentor, a symbol of principled death, a critic of manufactured desire, and now a source-bounded example of bodily endurance joined to intellectual discipline.

## Current Profile

The workplace-satire source casts Socrates as [[Plato|Plato]]'s onboarding guide at [[ThinkReasonLtd|思考理性有限公司]], rebuilding familiar biography as office procedure and accident. The Caesar episode uses his death as one possible symbolic frame through which political violence is remembered, while the wealth episode uses the market anecdote to argue that freedom can increase by clarifying desire rather than only accumulating money.

The fitness episode adds endurance without turning Socrates into a modern exercise authority. It presents his ability to withstand hunger, cold, and military hardship as context for Plato's view that bodily training and cultural education can jointly shape the soul. Endurance matters, but the episode's standard remains judgment about what difficulty is worth facing.

## Key Characteristics

- Mentor figure connecting Plato to philosophical inquiry in both serious and comic frames.
- Symbolic death case through which later narratives organize sacrifice, tyranny, and political memory.
- Market anecdote supplies a desire-reduction counterpoint to accumulation-centered wealth.
- Bodily endurance is presented as compatible with reflection rather than opposed to intellectual life.
- The current wiki uses Socrates as an interpretive figure and does not provide a complete historical biography.

## Qualifications

The page combines four source-specific uses rather than establishing a unified historical Socrates. The endurance claims, war anecdotes, market story, and philosophical interpretations are episode-attributed. The fitness source does not supply a Socratic exercise program or show that endurance by itself constitutes courage or virtue.

## What Changed

- Added bodily endurance as context for joining physical and philosophical education.
- Distinguished endurance from a complete account of courage or good judgment.
