# 杜太白 / Du Taibai

## Overview
杜太白 / Du Taibai is the central character of [[XianDeWanXiao|《闲得玩笑》]], presented as a county-town Chinese teacher whose learning, dignity, family life, and existential questions are repeatedly unsettled by ordinary circumstances.

## Current Profile
Du knows canonical thinkers and writers and is treated locally as a learned person, yet the knowledge that supports his occupation also becomes the material through which life mocks him. The resulting comedy is not ignorance comedy; it emerges from misalignment among education, status, vulnerability, and a world that does not obey inherited intellectual categories.

His family context intensifies that mismatch. The source describes a father with limited external capability who rules the household by finding the weaknesses of his wife and children, leaving the courtyard marked by crying. Du's question about why he should live therefore joins philosophy to domestic experience rather than appearing as an abstract classroom exercise.

## Key Characteristics
- County middle-school Chinese teacher publicly associated with learning.
- Character whose education cannot insulate dignity from everyday humiliation.
- Son living within a household structured by asymmetrical domestic power.
- Existential questioner placed beside canonical literary and historical figures without leaving ordinary life.
- Participant in an ending where a speaking pig and an untied knot introduce imaginative possibility.
- Source-scoped fictional profile based on author interview rather than the complete novel.

## Qualifications
