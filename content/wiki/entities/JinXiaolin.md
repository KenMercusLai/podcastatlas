# 金晓霖 / Jin Xiaolin

## Overview
金晓霖 / Jin Xiaolin is identified by the episode as an [[AWPlanet]] co-founder, choreographer, and content creator.

## Current Profile
Jin frames choreographic work as observation translated into a bodily world that audiences can enter without being assigned one correct conclusion. Her practical contribution is an eyes-closed touch exercise that moves from fingers and joints across the face, neck, shoulders, ribs, and other body regions so participants can recover awareness of bodily shape, texture, and signals after prolonged screen attention.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats artistic creation as a response to social life, personal experience, and observation of the world.
- Compares choreography to writing: both create a world, but choreography invites entry through bodies.
- Rejects a single mandatory audience interpretation.
- Uses simple touch and joint-awareness exercises to restore bodily sensitivity.
- Treats criticism and audience response as possible feedback into later creation.
