# 巫郡 / Wu Commandery

巫郡 / Wu Commandery appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-62-ji-er-kuang-ni-jiujiu-de-tui-a-lumch-ibj8m94abjh5nvr3qhiqff]] as one of the two [[ChuState|楚国]] regions [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] demands after detaining [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] in [[Xianyang|咸阳]]. It is paired with [[Qianzhong|黔中]] as territory Qin wants to extract from Chu by holding the Chu king.

The source stresses strategic consequence rather than geography for its own sake. If Chu yields Wu Commandery and Qianzhong, the episode says Chu's capital region would become dangerously exposed, which helps explain why Chu Huaiwang refuses even while captive.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-82-daqin-de-hewuqi-baiqi-laile-lo0w8mifkijhzmx7fdwcimmv7ade]] later records Wu Commandery as actually captured by [[BaiQi|白起]] in 277 BCE, paired again with [[Qianzhong|黔中]]. The page therefore tracks a progression from Qin's hostage-backed demand in Zhouji 62 to Qin's later battlefield seizure and administrative consolidation in the southern campaign sequence.
