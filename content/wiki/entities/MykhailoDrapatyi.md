# Mykhailo Drapatyi / Drapati

Mykhailo Drapatyi is the Ukrainian general whom [[kyiv-brawl-ukraines-top-general-fired-6a609aec4fac21e67f1be165]] names as [[OleksandrSyrskyi|Oleksandr Sersky]]'s successor. The source spelling is "Drapati"; the page uses Drapatyi as the canonical filename while preserving the source spelling through aliases.

The episode says Drapatyi had been responsible for Ukrainian defenses in the northeast and was likely to be received well inside the army. [[ShashankJoshi]] interprets him as part of a newer generation whose public statements about technology, protecting people, and commander responsibility push against Syrskyi's higher-casualty approach.

In the wiki, Drapatyi is less a full biographical subject than a marker for [[UkraineMilitaryCommandReform]]. His appointment becomes the source's test of whether command change can turn Fedorov-style modernization into battlefield practice without solving every constraint around [[AirDefenseSaturation]] or [[PatriotMissileSystem|Patriot]] scarcity.
