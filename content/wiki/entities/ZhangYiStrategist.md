# 张仪 / Zhang Yi (Warring States strategist)

张仪 / Zhang Yi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-28-hanzhaohou-de-kuaile-ni-xiangxiang-budao-ln2tbxniz4kvwogbwp32exmdokz8]] as one of the major Warring States persuaders whose story is about to receive sustained treatment after compact 333 BCE notices. This page uses the filename `ZhangYiStrategist.md` to avoid conflating him with the existing modern robotics-founder page [[ZhangYi|Zhang Yi]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-29-suqin-lizhi-shuangwen-zhishi-gaibian-mingyun-lrf5cqvoriyep2lkbozju-lyjaxv]] begins developing him through his relationship to [[SuQin|苏秦]]. The source repeats the tradition that Su Qin and Zhang Yi both studied under [[Guiguzi|鬼谷子]], but says Zhang Yi is still obscure when Su Qin is already famous.

The episode previews their later strategic contrast: Su Qin works the [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] line, while Zhang Yi becomes the opposing counterpart in [[ZonghengjiaDiplomacy|纵横家外交]]. It also says Su Qin later uses provocation to push Zhang Yi toward [[QinState|秦国]], making their rivalry both competitive and mutually enabling.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-31-hezong-lianheng-weiou-qin-lncpgmxrojc0y1meg9a3ul-o9nih]] narrates that provocation. Zhang Yi is still frustrated in [[ChuState|楚国]] when Su Qin draws him to Zhao, humiliates him, and lets his anger point him toward Qin as the only power strong enough to strike Zhao. Su Qin then secretly funds the journey, and Zhang Yi wins [[YingSi|秦惠文王]]'s trust. Once he learns the truth, Zhang Yi accepts that Su Qin has used him to stabilize Qin while he builds [[HezongAlliance|合纵]], and he promises not to trouble Su Qin while Su Qin lives.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] turns Zhang Yi from a placed court figure into an active Qin strategist. After [[QinState|秦国]] captures [[PuyangWarringStates|蒲阳]] from [[WeiState|魏国]], Zhang Yi advises [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] to return the city and send a Qin prince as hostage, then uses that apparent generosity to push [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]] into yielding [[Shangjun|上郡]]'s fifteen counties. The episode reads this as [[StrategicConcessionDiplomacy|欲擒故纵式让利外交]] and makes Zhang Yi's reward the Qin chief-minister post.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-35-lishishang-diyi-qibing-jiaofu-shi-ta-lnxgnu1czkl-ftfb3gq9kzxwnxdk]] keeps Zhang Yi in that active Qin role. The episode explains Qin's return of [[Jiaoyi|焦邑]] and [[QuwoWarringStates|曲沃]] to Wei as a continuation of Zhang Yi's flexible method: half pulling, half hitting, and using apparent generosity to improve Qin's diplomatic environment. It contrasts Zhang Yi with [[ShangYang|商鞅]]: Shang Yang is tied to Qin's force-centered state-building, while Zhang Yi survives by not being fully bound to one state or one personal feud.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-37-suqin-liuguo-jituan-ceo-de-bukan-qingshi-fj2kdqlaxzyy2nqtcutktuwrq5fo]] continues Zhang Yi through 324-323 BCE. He attacks [[WeiState|魏国]] for [[QinState|秦国]], takes [[ShanchengWarringStates|陕城]], and the episode summarizes the method as taking land while returning people. The next year he meets Qi and Chu ministers at [[Niesang|啮桑]], possibly to improve Qin's relations with those states or to counter [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]]. After the meeting, Zhang Yi himself leaves Qin for Wei as chief minister, making his mobility part of the new Qin-Wei-anti-Qin puzzle rather than a simple Qin-loyalty story.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-38-gao-jianzhi-de-zhangyi-bu-yiban-lobhcpz679psy54k0iduccqkfcrn]] makes that mobility the main problem. Zhang Yi goes to [[WeiState|魏国]] as chief minister but frames the move to [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] as still useful to [[QinState|秦国]]: because [[QiState|齐国]] hates him, his presence in Wei can provoke Qi-Wei conflict and let Qin exploit the disorder. After Qi attacks Wei, he sends [[FengXiWarringStates|冯喜]] to reveal the plan, causing Qi to withdraw. The episode reads this as [[YinmouYangmouConversion|阴谋阳谋转换]] and treats Zhang Yi as the clearest case of [[WarringStatesCareeristMobility|乱世职业经理人式流动]]: a strategist whose court position, personal survival, and state alignment cannot be reduced to a single loyalist category.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-39-lishishang-zhangyi-shi-zenyang-nudui-qiangdi-de-lv2gc2wtcxuhugfgixk7rbbhpa6x]] follows the same ambiguity into Wei court politics. [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] keeps rewarding Zhang Yi despite his failure to make Wei serve Qin, and the episode reads that favor as [[GraceDebtLoyaltyPressure|恩赏负债式忠诚压力]]. Inside Wei, Zhang Yi defeats [[HuiShi|惠施 / 惠师]] over the Qin-Han-Wei versus Qi-Chu line, helps push Hui Shi out of Wei, and then blocks [[ChenZhenWarringStates|陈枕]] before Chen can establish himself. This extends Zhang Yi from a mobile strategist into a court operator who can use favor, policy framing, and preemptive accusation together.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-44-yi-ge-gongsi-weishenme-buneng-you-liang-wei-ceo-lsz0xk6-0wwznkc62k-xkoxgz-r]] cites Zhang Yi retrospectively through [[MiuLiu|谬刘]]'s warning to [[HanXuanhuiwang|韩宣惠王]]. Zhang Yi's paired presence with [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]] in Wei becomes an example of [[DualExecutivePowerRisk|双首长权力风险]], because competing high ministers can turn strategy into factional power and territorial loss.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-47-yanwangkuai-de-lixiangzhuyi-lhgl6rqkv9nonzxc9xqquzzlz5pr]] shows Zhang Yi losing a strategic debate inside Qin. He urges [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] to attack [[HanState|韩国]], approach the Zhou royal center, and use the [[Jiuding|九鼎]] and map symbolism to command the world. [[SimaCuo|司马错]]'s reply reframes that as a risky prestige route compared with the concrete gains of [[ShuStateWarringStates|蜀国]], so Zhang Yi's plan becomes the rejected alternative that clarifies Qin's resource-first choice.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-50-zhangyi-shi-zenyang-ba-chuhuaiwang-huyou-que-de-loxg3ylhmskvdm-axtco5y-acege]] makes Zhang Yi's anti-[[HezongAlliance|合纵]] role explicit in the [[ChuState|楚国]] court. Acting for [[YingSi|秦惠文王]], he promises [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] six hundred li of [[Shangyu|商於]] if Chu cuts ties with [[QiState|齐国]], then delays after returning to Qin and reduces the delivery to six li once Chu has offended Qi. The episode therefore adds [[UnverifiedTerritorialPromiseTrap|未验地先断交陷阱]] to Zhang Yi's repertoire: he wins not by giving a real small concession, but by making the target destroy its own alliance leverage before verifying the promise.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-51-chuhuaiwang-qin-xiao-er-kan-jiujiu-ruhe-mie-le-ni-ltfkdlivomnvux5xodh2rlfqf6ac]] adds the preparation and outcome of that repertoire. The source says Zhang Yi had already used gifts and court influence inside Chu and had helped slander [[QuYuan|屈原]], leaving [[ChenZhenWarringStates|陈枕]] isolated when he warned against the Shangyu promise. After the promise collapses, Zhang Yi's deception succeeds beyond the diplomatic break: Chu's angry campaign loses at [[DanyangBattle|丹阳之战]], gives [[QinState|秦国]] [[Hanzhong|汉中]], and ends in another defeat near [[LantianBattle|蓝田之战]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2]] keeps Zhang Yi's post-deception danger unresolved. The episode closes by returning from the Yan and Qin-Shu branches to [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]'s desire for revenge, with Zhang Yi entering Chu as the next test of his court-survival skill.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-53-qinhuiwang-yong-zhangyi-huan-dipi-lvmo2cwbafcg2gnxliuo18fewimk]] resolves that test. When [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] considers exchanging Qin land for [[Qianzhong|黔中]], Chu Huaiwang asks for Zhang Yi instead, and Zhang Yi voluntarily enters Chu despite the risk. He is imprisoned, but survives through [[CourtBackchannelSurvival|宫廷后门自救]] involving [[ZhengXiu|郑袖]], then resumes persuasion by warning that Qin can use Han, Wei, Ba-Shu, and Wuguan pressure against Chu. The episode adds a darker layer to his career: Zhang Yi remains highly useful, but Qin can still treat him as a [[MinisterAsExchangeableAsset|可交换功臣]] whose survival, use, or death may all serve Qin.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-54-wo-zun-ni-shi-wang-ni-dang-wo-shi-pi-lvhcz0ban9frlmwshd9vch-q7-ie]] follows Zhang Yi after that release. He tours [[HanState|韩国]], [[QiState|齐国]], [[ZhaoState|赵国]], and [[YanState|燕国]], using [[EncirclementThreatPersuasion|包围威胁式说服]] to argue that each target is already surrounded by Qin-aligned states or exposed to Qin attack routes. The campaign extends his [[LianhengStrategy|连横]] role from the Chu trap into a serial persuasion circuit, but the closing succession turn weakens his personal position: [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] dies, [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] succeeds, and Zhang Yi faces [[PatronSuccessionExposure|靠山更替暴露风险]] because the new ruler already disliked him.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-55-zhangyi-tongshi-guiren-qinhuiwang-lsclfbwn06fgzooipf5up9sdkedy]] completes that risk branch. It supplies the fuller Yan stop, then shows Zhang Yi returning toward [[Xianyang|咸阳]] when [[YingSi|秦惠文王]]'s death lets Qin courtiers attack him and lets the lords abandon [[LianhengStrategy|连横]] for renewed [[HezongAlliance|合纵]]. Zhang Yi survives by presenting retreat as service: he asks [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] to send him to [[WeiState|魏国]], predicts [[QiState|齐国]] will attack because it hates him, and frames the resulting disorder as Qin's chance to attack [[HanState|韩国]] and enter [[SanchuanWarringStates|三川]]. When Qi attacks, he has the design disclosed to [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]], causing Qi to withdraw; the episode reads his later year as Wei chief minister and death as [[SelfPreservationAsStateStrategy|以自保包装国策]] completed through [[YinmouYangmouConversion|阴谋阳谋转换]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-56-qiaoqiao-liangqian-duo-nianqian-sha-shi-da-zhangfu-ltokbutqgsnrno-y29xmzwa3da41]] steps back from Zhang Yi's last maneuver to the moral evaluation of his whole type. [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] rejects the claim that Zhang Yi and [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]] are "great men" merely because rulers fear them, while the host notes that Zhang Yi's long adherence to [[LianhengStrategy|连横]] and his endurance under humiliation could still look like part of the [[MencianGreatManCriterion|大丈夫标准]]. The later [[YangXiong|扬雄]] / [[Fayan|《法言》]] critique sharpens the negative side: Zhang Yi's talent remains morally suspect if it serves wealth, rank, and deceptive technique rather than public rescue.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-65-wo-chuanyue-qu-gei-langman-de-chuwang-nian-shou-shi-lvyd7pve-tfu4ixsqrbdvhm6g64j]] uses Zhang Yi retrospectively while reassessing [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]. The episode argues that Chu Huaiwang's tragedy cannot be reduced to stupidity: Zhang Yi's earlier praise, gifts, and "sweet net" exploited vanity and court access, turning the ruler's appetite for validation into a durable vulnerability. Zhang Yi therefore remains an active cause in the Chu Huaiwang branch even after his own career has already been morally evaluated.
