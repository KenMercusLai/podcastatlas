# 张仪 / Zhang Yi (Warring States strategist)

张仪 / Zhang Yi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-28-hanzhaohou-de-kuaile-ni-xiangxiang-budao-ln2tbxniz4kvwogbwp32exmdokz8]] as one of the major Warring States persuaders whose story is about to receive sustained treatment after compact 333 BCE notices. This page uses the filename `ZhangYiStrategist.md` to avoid conflating him with the existing modern robotics-founder page [[ZhangYi|Zhang Yi]].

In this source, Zhang Yi is a signpost rather than a developed biography. The episode uses him with [[SuQin|苏秦]] to mark a coming turn toward [[ZonghengjiaDiplomacy|纵横家外交]], where arguments, alliance design, and ruler persuasion become major instruments of interstate competition.
