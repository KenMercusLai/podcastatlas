## Overview
Miles Williams is the Alex Theatre artistic director interviewed by [[MarketplaceTech]] about the theater's 70mm retrofit for [[TheOdyssey]] and the renewed appeal of analog projection.

## Current Profile
Williams's role in the source is explanatory and operational. He translates the difference between IMAX 70mm, digital IMAX, standard digital, standard 70mm, and 35mm into public terms, then links those format differences to the real work of rebuilding a historic theater for film projection.

The source also uses Williams as a cultural observer. He argues that film projection makes moviegoing feel like an event, describes historic theaters as living museums, and connects younger interest in projection and physical media to a desire for tangible connection.

## Key Characteristics
- Artistic director of the [[AlexTheatre]] in the Marketplace Tech source.
- Public explainer of [[SeventyMillimeterFilmProjection]] and the differences among IMAX 70mm, digital IMAX, standard digital, standard 70mm, and 35mm.
- Operator-facing witness to the theater's projection, sound, booth, budget, and schedule constraints.
- Advocate for [[AnalogMoviegoingExperience]], emphasizing event value, historic theaters, and closeness to projected film.
- Observer of projectionist apprenticeship and younger analog-media interest, especially around visible booth labor.
