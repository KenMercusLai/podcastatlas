# Forever Bicycle / 永久自行车

Forever Bicycle is one of the major Chinese bicycle brands in [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]]. The source connects it to Shanghai manufacturing history, the "永久" brand name, planned-economy consumer scarcity, and later public-market decline after Shanghai Forever's 1993 listing and late-1990s losses.

The brand's wiki role is to make [[ChinaBicycleShortageEconomy]] and market transition concrete. A bicycle could be a household durable, a status object, and a rationed purchase during scarcity, then become exposed to [[GiantBicycles]]-style consumer competition, low-end private factories, and changing urban mobility.
