# Fauda

## Overview
*Fauda* is an Israeli thriller co-created by and starring [[LiorRaz]], following undercover Israeli commandos operating in Gaza and the West Bank.

## Current Profile
[[home-disadvantage-risks-in-housing-markets-6ab4e209079b46c819b12f5e]] treats the show's fifth season as a deliberately partial work shaped by the aftermath of October 7th. Earlier seasons are described as giving Israeli and Palestinian characters families, grief, grievances, and internal conflict; the new season narrows that empathy and centers Israeli bereavement, rage, guilt, revenge, and institutional failure.

The review therefore reads the season as [[FictionAsTraumaWitness]], not as a comprehensive account of the conflict. Its immediacy can communicate one society's emotional reality to audiences who avoid news, while its exclusions make other sources and Palestinian perspectives indispensable.

## Key Characteristics
- Israeli espionage and counterterrorism thriller centered on undercover operations.
- Co-created by and starring Lior Raz, whose military background parallels the fictional unit.
- Earlier seasons are described as distributing grief and motivation across Israeli and Palestinian characters.
- Season five reenacts October 7th and its aftermath from a predominantly Israeli perspective.
- The season's emotional force and representational limitation arise from the same narrowed viewpoint.
