# David Thompson (explorer)

## Overview

David Thompson was a surveyor, mapmaker, and fur-trade employee used by the episode to connect commercial expansion with the geographic knowledge from which colonial Canada was mapped.

## Current Profile

Thompson mapped Canadian territory for the [[HudsonsBayCompany|Hudson's Bay Company]] and a rival company. The episode presents his 1814 map as exceptionally durable and his writing as unusually alert to the beaver's landscape-making importance, even though his work also belonged to the commercial system driving beaver depletion.

## Key Characteristics

- Surveyor and cartographer in the North American fur economy.
- Worked for the Hudson's Bay Company and a competitor.
- Produced an influential map in 1814.
- Joined route knowledge to trade and imperial expansion.
- Described pre-European Canada through the paired agency of humans and beavers.

## Evidence

- Corporate setting: [[285-canada-beaver-wars-glt6084067740]] places Thompson's mapping work inside competing fur companies.
- Cartographic durability: [[285-canada-beaver-wars-glt6084067740]] says Canadian government maps still drew on his work a century later.
- Environmental observation: [[285-canada-beaver-wars-glt6084067740]] attributes to him the description of Canada as belonging to humans and beavers.

## Qualifications

The episode offers Thompson as a revealing figure rather than a full biography. The map's exact institutional use, his relations with Indigenous guides and communities, and the quoted wording remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Established Thompson as a bridge among fur commerce, cartography, colonial reach, and recognition of animal agency.
