# Razor USA

Razor USA is [[CarltonCalvin]]'s consumer-products company in [[advice-line-with-carlton-calvin-of-razor-c0de1a84-3d35-45f3-921c-6144693ad86e]]. The episode uses Razor as mature-company context for trend spotting, brand defense, retail distribution, and delegated sales.

The source says Razor's original collapsible aluminum scooter remains its largest-selling item by volume, at close to a million units a year. Razor also supplies the episode's copycat and patent-litigation lesson: litigation can clean up a market, but Carlton argues that brand strength, speed, quality, and distribution may matter more than damages recovery when copycats have limited assets.
