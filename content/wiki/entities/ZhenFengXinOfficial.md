## Overview

甄丰 / Zhen Feng is synthesized as an early [[WangMang|王莽]] ally whose pre-accession office, accusation work, and post-accession family disaster show both the rewards and risks of Wang Mang's legitimacy machine. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-625-jiemi-renxing-anmian-weihe-pangren-chenggong-zong-ling-women-xinsheng-jihen-li64ngkv8nujwegbix6iororjmbo|Hanji 625]] places him before formal usurpation as a Wang Mang-aligned censorial actor who targets [[BanZhiWesternHan|班至]] and [[GongsunHongLangyaWesternHan|公孙洪]] for failing to join the praise and auspicious-reporting environment. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-629-liujiaren-shui-kangqi-fankang-wangmang-de-daqi-lmhws7-jptdh9tf45bqtlfvzdw55|Hanji 629]] then places him in 6 CE as ta'a and right assistant when [[RuziYing|孺子婴 / 刘婴]] is made crown prince. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-642-kao-xinji-shangwei-de-ren-zui-taoyan-shenme-lnc6e3dgxxnrylkfqzgl2ro9gpbj|Hanji 642]] later presents his family as caught in fuming backfire after Wang Mang's accession.

## Current Profile

The source set first places Zhen Feng as an enforcer of Wang Mang's praise environment. In Hanji 625, he dislikes Ban Zhi and Gongsun Hong because they do not supply auspicious signs or praise material and because Gongsun Hong discusses disasters. Rather than acting only by direct command, he sends subordinates to encourage local officials and people to accuse them, making him an early case of [[CoercedPraiseConformityRisk|颂圣不合群风险]] before Wang Mang's formal accession.

Hanji 629 then places Zhen Feng among the rewarded officeholders around Wang Mang's regency. His appointment as ta'a and right assistant belongs to an archaizing office package around Liu Ying's crown-prince installation. Hanji 642 later places him among the people who helped praise Wang Mang's virtue and support his rise through title and office expansion. That makes him part of the rewarded inner circle around the transition from Western Han to [[XinDynasty|新朝]].

After accession, the same environment turns dangerous. Wang Mang uses fuming language to move Zhen Feng into a new military title and away from the center. Zhen Feng's son [[ZhenXunXinOfficial|甄寻]] then submits fuming texts, first winning Zhen Feng a right-bo office and then provoking disaster by proposing marriage to Wang Mang's daughter. Zhen Feng's suicide makes him the family-cost side of the fuming purge rather than the main operator of the final claim.

## Key Characteristics

- Early Wang Mang ally associated with pre-accession praise and reward.
- Accusation-channel operator who targets officials whose silence or disaster talk disrupts the praise environment.
- Pre-accession ta'a and right assistant in the 6 CE regency office package.
- Xin court actor whose rank and relocation are mediated through fuming language.
- Father of Zhen Xun, whose fuming claim triggers the later purge.
- Suicide case showing how a rewarded ally network can become politically disposable.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 625, Hanji 629, and Hanji 642. It does not settle Zhen Feng's full earlier career, exact title chronology, legal authority over the local accusation process, or private attitude toward Wang Mang beyond the episodes' account.
