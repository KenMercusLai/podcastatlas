## Overview

甄丰 / Zhen Feng is synthesized as an early [[WangMang|王莽]] ally whose pre-accession office and post-accession family disaster show both the rewards and risks of Wang Mang's legitimacy machine. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-629-liujiaren-shui-kangqi-fankang-wangmang-de-daqi-lmhws7-jptdh9tf45bqtlfvzdw55|Hanji 629]] places him in 6 CE as ta'a and right assistant when [[RuziYing|孺子婴 / 刘婴]] is made crown prince. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-642-kao-xinji-shangwei-de-ren-zui-taoyan-shenme-lnc6e3dgxxnrylkfqzgl2ro9gpbj|Hanji 642]] later presents his family as caught in fuming backfire after Wang Mang's accession.

## Current Profile

The source set first places Zhen Feng among the rewarded officeholders around Wang Mang's regency. In Hanji 629, the appointment of Zhen Feng as ta'a and right assistant belongs to an archaizing office package around Liu Ying's crown-prince installation. Hanji 642 later places him among the people who helped praise Wang Mang's virtue and support his rise through title and office expansion. That makes him part of the rewarded inner circle around the transition from Western Han to [[XinDynasty|新朝]].

After accession, the same environment turns dangerous. Wang Mang uses fuming language to move Zhen Feng into a new military title and away from the center. Zhen Feng's son [[ZhenXunXinOfficial|甄寻]] then submits fuming texts, first winning Zhen Feng a right-bo office and then provoking disaster by proposing marriage to Wang Mang's daughter. Zhen Feng's suicide makes him the family-cost side of the fuming purge rather than the main operator of the final claim.

## Key Characteristics

- Early Wang Mang ally associated with pre-accession praise and reward.
- Pre-accession ta'a and right assistant in the 6 CE regency office package.
- Xin court actor whose rank and relocation are mediated through fuming language.
- Father of Zhen Xun, whose fuming claim triggers the later purge.
- Suicide case showing how a rewarded ally network can become politically disposable.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 629 and Hanji 642. It does not settle Zhen Feng's full earlier career, exact title chronology, or private attitude toward Wang Mang beyond the episodes' account.
