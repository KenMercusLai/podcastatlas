## Overview

马邑 / Mayi is a northern Western Han frontier city whose current evidence joins two separated Han-Xiongnu crises. Under [[LiuBang|刘邦]], it moves from defensive capital to siege, surrender, invasion breach, and punitive reconquest; under [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], its frontier location and wealth make it the bait and intended killing ground of the [[MayiAmbushWesternHan|马邑之谋]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-174-liubang-weihe-dafeng-zijide-chouren-1-lnzzvpieqtgatmvgogz3msyhs60u|Hanji 174]] introduces Mayi as the frontier capital requested by [[HanWangXin|韩王信]]. After Liu Bang moves him from the old Han interior to the Taiyuan-area kingdom, Han Wang Xin argues that [[Jinyang|晋阳]] is too far from the border to answer [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] attacks. Mayi therefore embodies the double edge of [[FrontierVassalContainment|边境迁封式牵制]]: a capable non-Liu king is positioned against an external threat but also moved away from the strategic heartland.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-175-bikan-liubang-de-huangjia-liyi-ke-los9hsomndnhyqpxafstgyquomzf|Hanji 175]] turns the capital into a siege and surrender point. Xiongnu forces surround the city, Han Wang Xin negotiates while asking Liu Bang for help, imperial suspicion grows, and he finally hands Mayi over. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-177-beikun-baidengshan-liubang-mingxuan-yixian-ljdmxf6bej8ju0gmvm9cp48gy0on|Hanji 177]] makes that surrender the breach opening the northern campaign that culminates at the [[BaidengEncirclement|白登之围]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-182-weishenme-shuo-chengye-xiaohe-baiye-xiaohe-2-lqk-jifupm9lbghfzaf09igfufgb|Hanji 182 part 2]] returns to Mayi during the final Han Wang Xin branch. [[ZhouBo|周勃]] and [[ChaiWu|柴武]] attack around it; after Han forces take the city, the episode says it is slaughtered. The place remains a hard frontier hinge where surrender, Xiongnu connection, and punitive reconquest overlap.

In the later [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-260-mayi-zhimou-hanwudi-fanji-xiongnu-diyizhan-luji7sarjbc2y4l8ljsnilld8xjb|Hanji 260]] setting, [[NieWengyiWesternHan|聂翁壹]] offers to feign local betrayal and promise Mayi's wealth and people to [[JunchanChanyu|军臣单于]], while a massed Han force waits nearby. A city previously lost through siege and mistrust is now used as apparent insider-access bait intended to bring Xiongnu cavalry onto Han-selected ground. The episode stops before the plan's outcome.

## Key Characteristics

- Northern frontier city whose proximity to Xiongnu pressure drives repeated strategic use.
- Capital selected to improve frontier response while containing a powerful different-surname king.
- Siege and surrender point where external coercion interacts with imperial mistrust.
- Campaign breach connecting Han Wang Xin's defection to Liu Bang's Baideng crisis.
- Site of punitive Han reconquest in the closing rebel branch.
- Wealthy apparent target used as bait for Wudi's planned border ambush.

## Qualifications

These podcast notes do not supply a continuous urban history or prove that every reference denotes an unchanged settlement, population, or administrative unit. The motives behind Han Wang Xin's relocation and surrender remain mixed between defense, containment, coercion, fear, and mistrust. Hanji 182's slaughter claim, Hanji 260's wealth and access assumptions, the displayed-head ruse, force totals, and exact ambush geography remain source-attributed. Hanji 260 ends before the Mayi plot's outcome, so the city should not be described here as the site of a completed Han victory.
