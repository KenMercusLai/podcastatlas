# Steve Hilton

## Overview
Steve Hilton is tracked as a California Republican political figure whose wiki role has shifted from a brief alternative-governance mention to a full 2026 gubernatorial candidate profile.

## Current Profile
Hilton's current profile is a change-candidate campaign against California's governing status quo. The Carolla source only names him as one possible alternative to business-as-usual governance, while the later All-In interview gives his own case: California should cut taxes for working households, reduce spending growth, audit waste, weaken housing-cost veto points, permit more in-state energy production, enforce crime and encampment laws, and rebuild addiction and mental-health treatment.

## Key Characteristics
- California Republican gubernatorial candidate using affordability as the central campaign issue.
- British-born Thatcher-influenced political actor who frames California through 1970s Britain analogies.
- Anti-capture reformer in his own telling, targeting unions, trial lawyers, environmental litigation, spending growth, and one-party governance.
- Platform candidate linking tax relief, spending audits, housing reform, energy permitting, school accountability, public safety, and homelessness enforcement.
- Electoral strategist relying on California's top-two system, Republican consolidation, and a change-versus-continuity general-election frame.
