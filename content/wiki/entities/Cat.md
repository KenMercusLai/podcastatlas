# Cat / 猫

[[Cat|猫]] is the central animal of [[34-maomi-mishi-he-yuchun-you-keai-de-renlei-601590607]], where [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] uses [[MaomiMishiBook|《猫咪秘史：从史前时期到太空时代》]] to reread human civilization from a cat-centered angle. The episode treats cats as more than household pets: they enter the wiki as pest-control partners, divine or magical beings, literary agents, ship workers, battlefield companions, and experimental animals in modern science.

The source's cat is powerful because it crosses practical and symbolic registers. In [[CatDomesticationHistory]], cats become useful around stored grain and rodents. In [[CatAsCivilizationalInfrastructure]], they protect food, books, ships, and military logistics. In [[CatReligiousMythology]] and [[CatCulturalAmbivalence]], the same animal becomes a goddess sign, household guardian, soul vessel, witch companion, bad omen, comic philosopher, or emblem of freedom.

## Key Claims
- Cats become culturally durable because their real usefulness in rodent control could be translated into protection, magic, and household intimacy.
- Human attitudes toward cats swing sharply: dependence and worship can coexist with fear, persecution, and resentment of feline independence.
- Cat stories require evidence discipline because the source moves among natural history, recorded custom, literary anecdote, folklore, and open legend.
