# Coolit

## Overview

Coolit was an automated growth agent intended to handle SEO, content marketing, advertising, and customer development for existing businesses.

## Current Profile

The product is presented as a discontinued experiment rather than the team's current direction. It reportedly obtained some prepaid customers before full readiness, but sustained growth delivery proved much harder than generating websites or tools. Its internal data and API routing layer was later separated into [[Trigg]].

## Key Characteristics

- Targeted multiple growth functions through one agent system.
- Tested demand through pre-sales before full product readiness.
- Exposed the difficulty of automating dynamic channel and keyword judgment.
- Produced infrastructure reused in the later Trigg marketplace.
