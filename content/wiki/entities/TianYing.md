# 田婴 / Tian Ying

田婴 / Tian Ying appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-19-maling-zhi-zhan-lhbapt09x2ptdqx0uopkp8apbada]] as one of the [[QiState|齐国]] commanders sent after [[HanState|韩国]] has been worn down by [[WeiState|魏国]]. The episode identifies him as [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]]'s younger son and notes that his son later becomes the famous Mengchang Jun.

In this source, Tian Ying is less developed than [[TianJi|田忌]] or [[SunBin|孙膑]]. His wiki role is to mark that Qi's intervention is a formal state campaign with multiple commanders, while Sun Bin remains the military adviser shaping the plan.
