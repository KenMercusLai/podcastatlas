# 圆通速递

## Overview
圆通速递 is a major Chinese franchise express carrier founded by [[YuWeijiao|喻渭蛟]] in 2000.

## Current Profile
The episode presents 圆通 as a later Tonglu entrant that made an early decisive bargain with [[Taobao]]: lower parcel prices in exchange for ecommerce volume. It subsequently invested in sorting, transport, information systems, and its own aviation capacity, becoming a central case in both platform-enabled scale and the ensuing price war.

## Key Characteristics
- Tonglu-linked franchise carrier.
- Early Taobao partner and price-volume adopter.
- Public company with logistics infrastructure investment.
- Carrier with a self-operated aviation branch.
