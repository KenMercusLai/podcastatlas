## Overview

冯野王 / Feng Yewang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-520-daji-duishou-weishenme-yao-xian-faqi-yulunzhan-lh1ibhc9xsihtrh6mhzznqq9ioso|Hanji 520]] as an official caught in the political aftermath of [[WangZhangWesternHan|王章]]'s death. His sick leave and return to Duling become the basis for an impeachment encouraged by [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]], while [[DuQingWesternHan|杜清]] argues that the case should not be punished under unclear or retroactive rules.

## Current Profile

The source presents Feng Yewang less as a full biography than as a disciplinary test case. After Wang Zhang is killed and Wang Feng's authority dominates the court, Feng becomes ill, receives approved leave, extends that leave, and returns with his wife to Duling for treatment while still on salary. Wang Feng then has the Yushi Zhongcheng impeach him for taking a tiger tally beyond the commandery boundary and allegedly disrespecting the imperial order.

Feng's durable wiki role is therefore procedural. His case shows how a technical violation can become politically useful after a factional shock. Du Qing's defense does not prove Feng's conduct perfect; it argues that a state damages credibility when it punishes under a rule that was unclear or only created after the fact.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official described as a two-thousand-stone officeholder in Hanji 520.
- Takes extended sick leave with Han Chengdi's approval.
- Returns to Duling for treatment while still officially on leave and salary.
- Impeached after Wang Zhang's death in a Wang Feng-dominated court environment.
- Serves as a procedural test for non-retroactivity and cautious punishment.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 520 and does not reconstruct Feng Yewang's full career, family background, political alignment, or whether Wang Feng considered him a direct factional threat. The episode reads the impeachment as part of Wang Zhang's aftershock, but the exact degree of personal opposition between Feng and Wang Feng remains source-scoped.
