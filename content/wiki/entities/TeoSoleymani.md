# Teo Soleymani

## Overview
Teo Soleymani is the dermatologist and dermatologic surgeon interviewed by [[AndrewHuberman]] in a [[HubermanLab]] episode on skin health, appearance, inflammatory disease, ultraviolet exposure, skin cancer, and evidence-based treatment.

## Current Profile
The source presents Soleymani as a clinician who joins cosmetic goals to barrier biology and cancer surveillance. He favors simple, tolerable daily care and physical sun protection, then reserves prescription retinoids, immune-directed treatment, and procedural tools for appropriate indications and supervision. His account resists two extremes: expensive product complexity is not evidence of effectiveness, and ultraviolet exposure is neither harmless nor a complete explanation of every skin cancer.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains visible skin through renewal, barrier function, blood flow, connective tissue, inflammation, immune activity, and environmental exposure.
- Prioritizes low-complexity cleansing and moisturizing over price, fragrance, or long ingredient lists.
- Uses a dose-and-context model for sunlight that emphasizes avoiding burns and combining physical barriers with sunscreen.
- Distinguishes weak cosmetic actives from prescription retinoids and clinician-selected procedures with meaningful effects and risks.
- Treats skin-cancer risk as multifactorial, joining ultraviolet damage to genetics, immune surveillance, HPV, lesion location, and family history.
- Separates broad education from individualized diagnosis, prescribing, procedures, and cancer surveillance.

## Evidence
- Barrier-first care: [[how-to-improve-protect-your-skin-health-appearance-dr-teo-soleymani-scim2145176738]] has Soleymani favor mild fragrance-free cleansers, skin-type-matched moisturizers, and avoidance of over-cleansing.
- Exposure judgment: [[how-to-improve-protect-your-skin-health-appearance-dr-teo-soleymani-scim2145176738]] combines limited sunlight, burn avoidance, physical barriers, and broad-spectrum mineral sunscreen preference.
- Treatment hierarchy: [[how-to-improve-protect-your-skin-health-appearance-dr-teo-soleymani-scim2145176738]] distinguishes prescription retinoids and laser resurfacing from weaker over-the-counter actives and variable red-light devices.
- Disease mechanisms: [[how-to-improve-protect-your-skin-health-appearance-dr-teo-soleymani-scim2145176738]] explains psoriasis, vitiligo, acne, rosacea, and eczema through different barrier, immune, vascular, microbial, and genetic mechanisms.
- Cancer surveillance: [[how-to-improve-protect-your-skin-health-appearance-dr-teo-soleymani-scim2145176738]] links full-body examination and family history to UV damage, HPV, immune recognition, and cancers outside routinely sun-exposed skin.

The source introduces him as double board certified, Stanford-trained, and formerly on UCLA's clinical faculty, but those details are not independently verified here.
