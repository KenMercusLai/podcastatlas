# Frost & Sullivan / 弗若斯特沙利文

## Overview
Frost & Sullivan / 弗若斯特沙利文 appears in the wiki as a third-party consulting and market-research firm whose data is cited in [[LaoPuGold|老铺黄金]]'s investor-facing disclosure.

## Current Profile
In [[shangye-xiaoyang-48-buyao-zai-caibao-zhong-chuangzao-zhibiao-1009836716]], Frost & Sullivan is not analyzed as a standalone business. Its role is evidentiary: the episode uses its presence in Lao Pu Gold's report to ask whether third-party rankings and productivity metrics are still useful when investors cannot trace definitions across periods or reproduce the calculation from basic disclosures.

## Key Characteristics
- Frost & Sullivan functions in the source as an external provider of market and ranking data for company disclosure.
- Its name can make a company-defined metric look more authoritative, but the episode argues that authority is not a substitute for transparent definitions and base data.
- The source does not accuse Frost & Sullivan of false work; the critique is about how issuer reports use third-party conclusions.
- The firm is therefore tied to [[FinancialReportOperatingMetricIntegrity|financial-report operating metric integrity]] rather than to a settled judgment about consulting quality.

## Evidence
- External data provider role: [[shangye-xiaoyang-48-buyao-zai-caibao-zhong-chuangzao-zhibiao-1009836716]] says Lao Pu Gold's special metrics and rankings are largely supplied or concluded by third-party consultancies including Frost & Sullivan.
- Authority-versus-verifiability: [[shangye-xiaoyang-48-buyao-zai-caibao-zhong-chuangzao-zhibiao-1009836716]] argues that even third-party-sourced metrics remain weak if the report does not disclose enough consistent inputs for outside verification.
- Source boundary: [[shangye-xiaoyang-48-buyao-zai-caibao-zhong-chuangzao-zhibiao-1009836716]] critiques the reporting use of these metrics, not Frost & Sullivan's broader business or methodology.
