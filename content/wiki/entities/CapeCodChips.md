# Cape Cod Chips

## Overview
Cape Cod Chips is the potato-chip company founded by [[SteveBernard]] and presented in [[late-july-snacks-nicole-bernard-dawes-crackers-and-cookies-were-failing-tortilla-chips-saved-them-97049a31-9144-42f5-856c-9590c5e6beb4]] as the family and product-world context behind [[NicoleBernardDawes]] and [[LateJulySnacks]].

## Current Profile
The episode describes Cape Cod Chips as a small Hyannis storefront experiment inspired by old-fashioned potato chips. It nearly failed, received accidental publicity and insurance money after a car crash into the store, sold to Anheuser-Busch in 1985, was later bought back by Steve Bernard, and was sold again to Lance. For the wiki, its main role is not a standalone corporate history but the taste-first natural-snack proof case that Nicole later adapted.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder-led potato-chip brand rooted in Cape Cod summer place identity.
- Early proof that a better-tasting snack could bridge natural-food sensibility and mainstream appeal.
- Family-business precedent that exposed Nicole to product development, marketing, and owner/investor tradeoffs.
- Sale-and-buyback story that shaped Nicole's later emotional response to Late July ownership changes.
