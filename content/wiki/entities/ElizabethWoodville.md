# Elizabeth Woodville

## Overview

Elizabeth Woodville is Edward IV's widow and mother of the princes in the Tower, whose attempt to protect her family through affinity, council negotiation, and sanctuary failed against Richard of Gloucester's control of force and access.

## Current Profile

The episode does not reduce Elizabeth to the hostile image used against her. She and Hastings initially sought accommodation in London, and she entered sanctuary after Richard arrested her son and relatives. Her eventual surrender of Richard of Shrewsbury shows the constrained choices of a queen whose formal status could not overcome control of the council, king, and armed retainers.

## Key Characteristics

- Queen dowager and mother of [[EdwardV]] and [[RichardOfShrewsbury]].
- Center of a Woodville affinity that Richard represented as dangerous to his own position.
- Participant in an attempted compromise with [[WilliamHastings]] before the arrests.
- Used Westminster sanctuary to protect her younger son and household.
- Target of witchcraft accusation and marriage-invalidity claims used during Richard's escalation.

## Evidence

- Compromise and threat perception: [[139-the-princes-in-the-tower-part-2-glt4685691538]] describes negotiations over retinue size and factional cooperation after Edward IV's death.
- Sanctuary: [[139-the-princes-in-the-tower-part-2-glt4685691538]] follows Elizabeth's move into Westminster and the transfer of her younger son.
- Delegitimation campaign: [[139-the-princes-in-the-tower-part-2-glt4685691538]] records the witchcraft accusation and attack on the validity of her marriage.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to the 1483 crisis. The episode does not independently establish the witchcraft allegation or invalid marriage; it treats both as politically useful claims in Richard's consolidation.

## What Changed

- Created a profile emphasizing constrained protective strategy rather than the regime's hostile allegations.

- [[WilliamHastings]] - uneasy ally in the attempted post-Edward compromise.
- [[RichardIII]] - brother-in-law whose actions dismantled her family's control and her sons' claims.
