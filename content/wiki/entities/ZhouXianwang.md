# 周显王 / King Xian of Zhou

周显王 / King Xian of Zhou appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-3-lmhj4le2mwu1yhhl-qmuoqkfmhjf]] as the younger brother and successor of [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]] after Zhou Lie Wang dies in 369 BCE.

The page is source-scoped. Zhou Xian Wang matters here as the annalistic frame for the episode's real political action: the [[WeiState|魏国]] succession war and the failed [[HanState|韩国]]-[[ZhaoState|赵国]] settlement after [[ZhuozeBattle|浊泽之战]].
