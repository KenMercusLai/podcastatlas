# 周显王 / King Xian of Zhou

周显王 / King Xian of Zhou appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-3-lmhj4le2mwu1yhhl-qmuoqkfmhjf]] as the younger brother and successor of [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]] after Zhou Lie Wang dies in 369 BCE.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-11-shijie-fengqi-yunyong-1-lt60b2gddiosf5kade35uopwohi5]] makes Zhou Xian Wang the main annalistic frame. The episode says the second Zhou-annals volume covers his full forty-eight-year reign from 368 BCE to 321 BCE and opens with Qi, Zhao, Wei, Han, Song, and Qin fighting across several fronts.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-11-shijie-fengqi-yunyong-2-llzzalowv5vmbvgd6l-jujltkbyf]] continues that frame into his seventh year, 362 BCE. The year combines [[WeiState|魏国]]'s defeat of [[HanState|韩国]] and [[ZhaoState|赵国]], Qin's victory over Wei at [[Shaoliang|少梁]], [[WeyState|卫国]] and [[YanState|燕国]] successions, and the accession of [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]].

The page is source-scoped. Zhou Xian Wang matters here as the annalistic frame for the episode's real political action: the [[WeiState|魏国]] succession war and the failed [[HanState|韩国]]-[[ZhaoState|赵国]] settlement after [[ZhuozeBattle|浊泽之战]].

In the Zhouji 11 source, he also performs a symbolic action: after [[QinXianGong|秦献公]] wins the [[ShimenBattle|Battle of Shimen]], Zhou Xian Wang grants [[FufuRobe|黻服]], which the host reads as [[RitualRecognitionOfMilitaryPower|ritual recognition of military power]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-13-gongsunyang-keke-bianfa-re-shashen-1-lgmjapc2eecefbdshnpenzfcv6ga]] keeps him as the annalistic frame for the next Qin turn. The episode notes that 360 BCE has no chronicle entry, then moves to 359 BCE as [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]] backs [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] and [[ShangYangReforms|商鞅变法]] begins to reshape Qin.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-14-qiweiwang-buming-zeyi-yiming-jingren-lrs6rbifyiqrojxg0mccpg7sf9rv]] continues his frame through 358-356 BCE. The episode records Qin-Han war, Wei wall-building, Qin-Chu marriage diplomacy, Wei-Han and multi-state meetings, and a [[QiChronologyDispute|Qi Wei Wang chronology dispute]] in which a received Zhou-court visit cannot be to [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]] if that ruler is already dead.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]] continues his annalistic frame into 353 BCE. The year combines [[GuilingBattle|桂陵之战]] and [[EncirclingWeiToRescueZhao|围魏救赵]], [[HanState|韩国]]'s attack on [[EasternZhouPolity|东周国]], and [[ChuState|楚国]]'s appointment of [[ZhaoXixu|昭奚恤]] as chancellor.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] continues the frame through 352-350 BCE. The cluster links Qin's attacks on Wei, Wei's reconciliation with Zhao and Qi, [[ShenBuhai|申不害]]'s Han service under [[HanZhaohou|韩昭侯]], [[ShangYangReforms|商鞅变法]] moving into a second round, and [[ZhaoChenghou|赵成侯]]'s death followed by Zhao succession conflict.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-18-zhongguo-lishishang-di-yi-li-rentoushui-lgqnequk0rxejqgihwdqxqyqnsvq]] continues the frame through 349-343 BCE. The episode records [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s likely [[PollTax|人头税]] reform, [[ZhaoFan|赵范]]'s failed surprise attack on [[Handan|邯郸]], [[LuKanggong|鲁康公]]'s death and [[LuJinggong|鲁景公]]'s accession, [[WeyState|卫国]]'s demotion, and the paired royal-status signals of [[AudienceVsMeetingDiplomacy|诸侯会于京师]] and [[ZhouHegemonRecognition|王致伯于秦]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-19-maling-zhi-zhan-lhbapt09x2ptdqx0uopkp8apbada]] continues the frame into 341 BCE after a blank 342 BCE notice. The year brings [[MalingBattle|马陵之战]] through [[WeiState|魏国]]'s attack on [[HanState|韩国]], [[QiState|齐国]]'s delayed rescue decision, and [[SunBin|孙膑]]'s plan to exploit [[PangJuan|庞涓]] through [[ForcedMarchOperationalRisk|急行军作战风险]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-20-pangjuan-si-ci-shu-xia-ls-8-3jhz5q1c5ca11mhdzmz4wg4]] completes that 341 BCE Maling notice through outcome rather than setup. [[SunBin|孙膑]] uses [[DecreasingStovesDeception|减灶诱敌]], Pang Juan dies in the ambush, [[QiState|齐国]] breaks [[WeiState|魏国]], and [[PrinceShenWei|太子申]] is captured.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-22-gongsunyang-zha-wei-gongzi-ang-lss1l7lojfwgwdtcje7m5ijodpxi]] continues his frame into 340 BCE, Zhou Xian Wang's twenty-ninth year. The episode records [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] attacking Wei, Qi and Zhao also attacking Wei, and [[ChuXuanwang|楚宣王]]'s death, but its substantive reading focuses on Qin exploiting Wei's weakness and capturing [[GongziAng|公子卬 / 公子昂]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-23-weihuiwang-hui-buyong-gongshu-zhi-yan-lveaqklo7oncmqudgmdk7l03qhsf]] carries that frame through 340-338 BCE. The episode completes the Qin-Wei outcome, notes 339 BCE as blank, and uses 338 BCE for [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]]'s death, [[YingSi|嬴驷]]'s succession, and Shang Yang's fall.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-27-weiguan-weihuiwang-he-qiweiwang-shi-za-shangye-hupeng-lminlxhrm5bethub9kyy-8blimrb]] continues the frame into Zhou Xian Wang's thirty-fourth and thirty-fifth years, 335-334 BCE. The episode records [[QinState|秦国]] taking [[YiyangWarringStates|宜阳]] from [[HanState|韩国]], then centers [[XuzhouMutualKingship|徐州相王]] between [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] and [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王 / 梁惠王]] as a post-Maling diplomatic reordering.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-28-hanzhaohou-de-kuaile-ni-xiangxiang-budao-ln2tbxniz4kvwogbwp32exmdokz8]] extends the same thirty-fifth-year and thirty-sixth-year frame. The source develops [[HanZhaohou|韩昭侯]]'s high gate, [[QuYijiu|屈宜臼]]'s [[UntimelyExtravagance|时绌举赢]] warning, [[YueWangWujiang|越王无疆]]'s failed anti-Qi move, and the post-Xuzhou attack on Qi by [[ChuWeiwang|楚威王]]. It also says the chronicle's 333 BCE narrative weight is about to shift toward [[SuQin|苏秦]] and [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-29-suqin-lizhi-shuangwen-zhishi-gaibian-mingyun-lrf5cqvoriyep2lkbozju-lyjaxv]] develops that thirty-sixth-year frame through succession notices and source criticism. The episode records Han, Qi, Yan, and Wei successions, but treats the Qi notice and Su Qin's sudden six-state [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] success as doubtful chronology, linking them to [[ChronicleChronologyDrift|编年错位]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-30-suqin-de-kaigua-rensheng-lkmn-fruukdxgkddx7zb4lc3o9bw]] keeps Zhou Xian Wang in the Su Qin biography as a failed first target after renewed study. The episode says familiar local observers around the Zhou ruler do not take [[SuQin|苏秦]] seriously, using the case to show that [[ChuaimoTechnique|揣摩]] still needs a receptive audience and enough distance from old reputation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-31-hezong-lianheng-weiou-qin-lncpgmxrojc0y1meg9a3ul-o9nih]] continues the same thirty-sixth-year Su Qin branch. The episode shifts from failed local persuasion and Yan backing to Zhao authorization for [[HezongAlliance|合纵]], Qin's attack on Wei, and the setup that sends [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] into Qin.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-32-shishang-shouwei-lianheguo-mishuzhang-suqin-lgfkk45yq1c0yblequ-d1t7mol3]] keeps the same Zhou Xian Wang thirty-sixth-year frame while completing Su Qin's hezong tour. The episode also uses [[Shiji|《史记》]] for a later homecoming scene in which Zhou Xian Wang receives Su Qin at [[Luoyi|洛阳 / 洛邑]] after his six-state success.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-33-lishi-diyi-shuangwen-nanzhujiao-suqin-lvebjyhvzctjgaevwwoddpvg9nr0]] expands that reception scene. Zhou Xian Wang's road-clearing and ceremonial welcome make Su Qin's rank publicly legible in Luoyang, which is why the episode can read his family's later deference as [[QianjuHougongReversal|前倨后恭]] rather than only private reconciliation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] continues his annalistic frame through 332-328 BCE. The episode records Qin's use of [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]] against the hezong arrangement, Wei's repeated territorial concessions, [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]'s accession after [[ChuWeiwang|楚威王]], [[SongJunYan|宋君偃]]'s coup against [[SongTicheng|宋剔成]], and [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s rise after the [[PuyangWarringStates|蒲阳]] and [[Shangjun|上郡]] exchange.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-35-lishishang-diyi-qibing-jiaofu-shi-ta-lnxgnu1czkl-ftfb3gq9kzxwnxdk]] continues the frame through Zhou Xian Wang's forty-second and forty-third years. The episode records [[QinState|秦国]]'s partial victory over [[YiquState|义渠]], Qin's return of [[Jiaoyi|焦邑]] and [[QuwoWarringStates|曲沃]] to [[WeiState|魏国]], [[ZhaoSuhou|赵肃侯]]'s death in 326 BCE, and [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]]'s accession gestures.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-36-weisijun-de-diwang-zhishu-lt5cpdm9yqdj4erwykd0kubfpucz]] continues the frame into Zhou Xian Wang's forty-fourth year, 324 BCE. The episode records [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] claiming kingly title and the death of [[WeyPinghou|卫平侯]], then develops [[WeySijun|卫嗣君]]'s escaped-prisoner story and a [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] supplement around [[WuxiaWarringStates|武夏]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-37-suqin-liuguo-jituan-ceo-de-bukan-qingshi-fj2kdqlaxzyy2nqtcutktuwrq5fo]] continues the frame across his forty-fifth and forty-sixth years, 324-323 BCE. The episode records [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] attacking Wei and taking [[ShanchengWarringStates|陕城]], [[SuQin|苏秦]] entering Qi after leaving Yan, Zhang Yi's meeting with Qi and Chu ministers at [[Niesang|啮桑]], Han and Yan rulers claiming kingship, [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] refusing royal title, and [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]] beginning anti-Qin alliance work from Wei.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-38-gao-jianzhi-de-zhangyi-bu-yiban-lobhcpz679psy54k0iduccqkfcrn]] continues the late-reign branch into the forty-seventh-year frame, around 322 BCE. It centers [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] as chief minister in [[WeiState|魏国]], Qin's attack on [[QuwoWarringStates|曲沃]] and [[PingzhouWarringStates|平州]], and a [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] supplement involving [[QinWuwang|秦武王]], [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]], and [[FengXiWarringStates|冯喜]]. The episode's contribution is both strategic and source-critical: Zhang Yi's [[YinmouYangmouConversion|阴谋阳谋转换]] sits inside an unstable source-layer problem.
