# Hidden Brain

## Overview
Hidden Brain is a psychology and human-behavior podcast represented here by an episode on explanation, bias correction, and depression reframing.

## Current Profile
The current bounded evidence shows the program combining research explanation, everyday examples, listener accounts, and expert qualification. [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361|The Story of Stories]] follows [[TaniaLombrozo]] from ordinary sense-making into political, legal, and conspiratorial failure modes, then uses [[JonathanRottenberg]]'s mood-system account to reframe depression without denying severe illness or treatment.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses concrete stories to make cognitive and psychological models legible.
- Connects research ideas to relationships, politics, justice, and mental health.
- Pairs reframing with qualifications about evidence, severity, and individual variation.
