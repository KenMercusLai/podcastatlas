# Hidden Brain

## Overview
Hidden Brain is a psychology and human-behavior podcast represented here through episodes on explanation, depression reframing, arts and health, child development, learning, theory of mind, relationship understanding, regret, moral conflict, whistleblowing, preference falsification, incivility, forgiveness, false-confession research, difficult conversations, chance-sensitive causality, sustainable crisis engagement, personally fitting goals, and AI-mediated grief.

## Current Profile
The current bounded evidence shows the program combining research explanation, concrete scenes, listener experience, and explicit qualification. [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361|The Story of Stories]] follows [[TaniaLombrozo]] from ordinary sense-making into political, legal, and conspiratorial failure modes, then uses [[JonathanRottenberg]]'s mood-system account to reframe depression without denying severe illness or treatment.

[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-6aaa4c79fff3c55a5053383f6c9c253b-51902403-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-6aaa4c79fff3c55a5053383f6c9c253b-51902403|What Art Does For Your Brain]] follows [[DaisyFancourt]] from hospital-arts observations into reward, emotion, social, cohort, and cognitive-aging research. Its practical branch presents attentive, varied arts participation as an accessible health-supporting routine while keeping observational associations, biomarkers, brain-age estimates, dementia claims, and individual treatment outside what one public episode can establish.

[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-e107f1ec59fe4e6311a12befafe07923-88839568-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-e107f1ec59fe4e6311a12befafe07923-88839568|Relationships 2.0: Why Did You Do That?]] joins [[LianeYoung]]'s intention-sensitive account of moral judgment to [[FredLuskin]]'s boundary-preserving account of forgiveness. It treats mind-reading as necessary but vulnerable to outcomes, identity, and group loyalty, then separates emotional release from reconciliation and requires acknowledgment and corrective action before self-forgiveness.

[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-7b40091055d1394cc6a3435ffecc95c8-53960014-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-7b40091055d1394cc6a3435ffecc95c8-53960014|Did I Really Do That?]] has [[SaulKassin]] show how authority, isolation, duration, misinformation, immediate relief, and memory doubt can produce voluntary, compliant, or internalized false confessions. It then follows the confession into [[PostConfessionEvidenceContamination]], where jurors, witnesses, alibi providers, and forensic interpretation may cease to function as independent checks.

[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-741c785acf100f951118c170a6512dc3-52645116-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-741c785acf100f951118c170a6512dc3-52645116|The Conversations that Bring Us Closer]] has [[AlisonWoodBrooks]] treat difficult interaction as a problem of arousal, goals, perspective discovery, visible openness, and repair. [[ConversationalReceptiveness]] combines questions, acknowledgment, validation without agreement, qualified language, and common ground; apology and emotional reframing remain useful only within the episode's good-faith and safety boundaries.

[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-0a3c8347ab08972a4dce8dba455358cf-53039697-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-0a3c8347ab08972a4dce8dba455358cf-53039697|Fluke: How Chance and Chaos Shape Our Lives]] has [[BrianKlaas]] connect personal survival, atomic targeting, coup failure, evolutionary history, and chaos theory through [[ChanceSensitiveCausality]]. [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b88be0f610dc041ad6ff6e655ae4dc57-64189981-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b88be0f610dc041ad6ff6e655ae4dc57-64189981|Wellness 2.0: When It's All Too Much]] has [[SarahJaquetteRay|Sarah Jaquette Ray]] connect climate education, fear-based communication, guilt, paralysis, and burnout through [[ClimateDistressEngagement]], then develops [[CollectiveEfficacySustainableAction]] as a nonheroic model of shared action, bounded contribution, pleasure, and rest. [[PicoIyer|Pico Iyer]] adds [[RestorativeWithdrawalAndReturn]], where temporary silence is judged by whether it improves the return to care and responsibility.

[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-db5f9d45fb650a533486e6eb38780ebf-49695184-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-db5f9d45fb650a533486e6eb38780ebf-49695184|Wellness 2.0: Who Do You Want To Be?]] has [[KenSheldon]] move from goal execution to goal selection. [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-ea5bbec6d5f8dceb54214fa00e91a356-48702281-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-ea5bbec6d5f8dceb54214fa00e91a356-48702281|What Makes Relationships Thrive]] has [[HarryReis]] separate accurate interpersonal knowledge from [[PerceivedUnderstandingInRelationships|the felt experience of being understood]]. [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-e3af8fff4431a2e0de997f372a273cac-28609793-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-e3af8fff4431a2e0de997f372a273cac-28609793|You 2.0: Regrets, I Have a Few...]] has [[AmySomerville]] treat regret as comparison with an imagined alternative that can clarify values or future action but can also become intrusive rumination and exaggerated self-blame. [[PsychologicalCounterfactualThinking]] separates improvement-oriented upward comparison from relief-oriented downward comparison, [[RegretResponsibilityAttribution]] restores situational and multi-actor causes, and [[SatisficingDecisionStrategy]] limits endless search for an unknowable best option.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses concrete stories to make cognitive and psychological models legible.
- Connects research ideas to relationships, politics, justice, moral conflict, organizations, mental health, history, and systems design.
- Pairs reframing with qualifications about evidence, severity, and individual variation.
- Preserves safety, accountability, and evidential limits when translating charitable interpretation or forgiveness into advice.
- Uses wrongful-conviction cases and experiments to challenge intuitive confidence in confession evidence and to motivate transparent investigation reform.
- Uses sustained personal cases to expose tradeoffs among technological possibility, emotional meaning, consent, health, and living relationships.
- Connects interpersonal and public behavior to feedback loops that can spread incivility, stabilize conformity, or produce rapid social and political change.
