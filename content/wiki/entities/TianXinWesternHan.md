## Overview

田信 / Tian Xin enters the wiki as the merchant whom [[ZhuMaichenWesternHan|朱买臣]] selects as the bridge for accusing [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]] of leaking state information and enabling commercial profit.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-339-1-sanrenchenghu-ruhe-bisi-xihan-diyi-kuli-lowphplbvgugdyfag-sczpgggyrv|Hanji 339-1]] describes Tian as a commercially agile figure who profits around state controls and has a friendly relationship with Zhang Tang. Zhu infers that such profit requires protection by a senior official and decides Zhang must be that protector. He then begins framing Tian's gains as evidence of policy leakage, official-commercial collusion, and private benefit.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han merchant in the episode's accusation narrative.
- Described as exploiting openings around state economic controls.
- Presented as friendly with Zhang Tang.
- Selected by Zhu Maichen as the evidentiary bridge in the counterattack.
- Alleged to benefit from leaked policy information and official protection.

## Qualifications

The supplied transcript ends before the allegation is fully stated. It does not independently establish Tian's business methods, source of information, profits, payments, relationship with Zhang, or adjudicated guilt. This page records Tian's role in the accusation rather than treating the accusation as a verified commercial history.
