# Drew Harris

## Overview
Drew Harris is presented in the bounded source as an Air Force veteran, AI scientist, instructional designer, software engineer, and representative of [[ApexReplicate]]. He links the platform's origin to repeated organizational failures to transfer practical judgment before experienced people become unavailable or retire.

## Current Profile
Harris frames expert knowledge as more than factual recall. His instructional-design background leads him toward interviews, active listening, targeted follow-up, and continued learning from exceptions. He also describes using AI coding systems like junior engineers: productivity depends on a human who can architect the work, supply context, identify mistakes, and redirect execution.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects instructional design with AI-based expert-judgment capture.
- Treats contextual interviewing as the starting point for representing expertise.
- Keeps experts involved when a protégé reaches a knowledge boundary.
- Presents AI as leverage for people able to supervise and correct it.
- Accepts that automation can erode skills and argues for education centered on critical thinking.
