## Overview

公孙敬声 / Gongsun Jingsheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-383-2-chengxiang-gongsunhe-shi-ruhe-bei-erzi-kengsi-de-lt1yhocymjtgwjb7537yotbwoikz|Hanji 383-2]] as [[GongsunHeWesternHan|公孙贺]]'s son and the immediate trigger of the Gongsun family disaster before the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]] escalates toward [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]].

## Current Profile

The page is currently source-scoped to Hanji 383-2. The episode says Han Wudi gives Gongsun Jingsheng the taipu post after Gongsun He becomes chancellor, but Gongsun Jingsheng then embezzles a large North Army military fund. Gongsun He tries to save him by capturing [[ZhuAnshiWesternHan|朱安世]], only for Zhu to retaliate with accusations of adultery with Yangshi Princess and wugu cursing against [[HanWudi|汉武帝]].

The source's synthesis is not that Gongsun Jingsheng alone causes the Wugu crisis. It is narrower: his office misuse creates the opening for a desperate rescue bargain, and the retaliatory wugu accusation against him becomes a pre-open-conflict branch of the larger Wugu disaster.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han high-office son whose taipu appointment comes through his father's chancellor-era favor.
- Accused embezzler of North Army funds, making his offense military-political rather than only private corruption.
- Object of Gongsun He's attempted rescue bargain with Han Wudi.
- Target of Zhu Anshi's retaliatory accusations involving Yangshi Princess and wugu cursing.
- Early Wugu-case defendant whose fall destroys his father and clan before the later Liu Ju crisis.
