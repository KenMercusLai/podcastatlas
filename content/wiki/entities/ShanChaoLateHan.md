## Overview

单超 / Shan Chao is a Huan-era eunuch marquis in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-853-jiemi-hanchao-ban-de-zhaoshi-gu-er-lvsa2pby8iyfevfoobobpik52mij|Hanji 853]]. His death matters less as a biography than as a signal of [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]'s favor and as the trigger for the episode's account of the remaining [[FiveMarquisesHuanEra|Five Marquises]] becoming more indulgent.

## Current Profile

Hanji 853 says Shan Chao dies in the same 160 CE layer as Huan's amnesty and the Li-family survival story. Huan grants him imperial coffin and clothing gifts, calls out Five Camps cavalry, and has the construction office lead his tomb work. The episode treats this as a public display of court favor.

After Shan Chao's death, the source says the remaining four marquises become still more arrogant and indulgent. Shan Chao therefore functions as a hinge: his funeral shows how high the eunuch-favorite reward could rise, while his death frames the group's intensified local predation.

## Key Characteristics

- Huan-era eunuch marquis whose death receives exceptional imperial honor.
- Burial-status signal of court favor after the Liang Ji crisis.
- Narrative hinge for the remaining marquises' indulgence and arrogance.
- Source-bounded node in the wider eunuch patronage and local violence field.
