## Overview

单超 / Shan Chao is a Huan-era eunuch marquis whose profile now runs from 159 CE reward politics to active family protection and then 160 CE burial honor. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-850-huandi-weihe-zhiyi-sha-zhongchen-lokxtceq7vv-giec6iufwwk9fwk7|Hanji 850]] makes him one of the large post-[[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] eunuch recipients criticized by [[LiYunLateHan|李云]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-851-hanhuandi-weihe-zhuding-yichou-wannian-ltgm1ix6cgqb-kqo9bztfrzjgeun|Hanji 851]] then shows him still being elevated while sick and protecting a kinship corruption field. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-853-jiemi-hanchao-ban-de-zhaoshi-gu-er-lvsa2pby8iyfevfoobobpik52mij|Hanji 853]] later records his death and lavish burial treatment.

## Current Profile

Hanji 850 says Shan Chao is one of the eunuchs rewarded after Liang Ji's fall. His grant is presented as especially large, and Li Yun folds the reward scale of the [[FiveMarquisesHuanEra|Five Marquises]] into a broader warning that court favor has outrun military and institutional balance.

Hanji 851 adds the active-power layer before his death. Although sick, Shan Chao is appointed Chariot-and-Cavalry General. His nephew [[ShanKuangLateHan|单匡]] becomes a corrupt Jiyin administrator; [[FifthZhongLateHan|第五种]] and [[WeiYuLateHan|魏宇]] expose the case; Shan Kuang hires [[RenFangLateHan|任芳]] to kill Wei Yu; and [[YangBingLateHan|杨秉]] is punished after asking to bring Shan Kuang to Luoyang. Shan Chao later uses [[ShusunWujiLateHan|叔孙无忌]]'s disorder to frame Fifth Zhong, while his maternal grandson [[DongYuanLateHan|董元]] makes Fifth Zhong's exile dangerous.

Hanji 853 then records Shan Chao's death and burial. [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] grants imperial coffin and clothing gifts, calls out Five Camps cavalry, and uses the construction office for the tomb. The same source says the remaining marquises become still more indulgent after his death, making Shan Chao a hinge between reward, protected-family power, and local predation.

## Key Characteristics

- Huan-era eunuch marquis whose large post-Liang reward becomes part of Li Yun's critique.
- Sick but elevated court favorite whose rank continues to rise in Hanji 851.
- Patronage source for Shan Kuang's corruption, witness-suppression attempt, and failed confrontation.
- Retaliation actor who uses Shusun Wuji's disorder to frame Fifth Zhong.
- Burial-status signal of continuing court favor after the Liang Ji crisis.
- Narrative hinge for the remaining marquises' indulgence and arrogance.

## Qualifications

This page remains bounded to Hanji 850, Hanji 851, and Hanji 853. Hanji 851's sick but active Shan Chao is not a contradiction of Hanji 853's later death and burial. Shan Chao's page should not absorb the entire Five Marquises chronology, but it now includes the Shan Kuang branch because the protection and retaliation flow directly through his family network.

## What Changed

- Hanji 851 adds Shan Chao's sickbed elevation, Shan Kuang protection, Fifth Zhong retaliation, and Dong Yuan exile-threat branch between Hanji 850 and Hanji 853.
- The page now treats Shan Chao as an active patronage shield before his burial becomes a status signal.
