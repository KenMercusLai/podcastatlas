# 须贾 / Xu Jia

须贾 / Xu Jia appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-88-ni-zenme-kandai-fenghuangnan-1-lpuxkdq-rvnifuoneq212g53w5z5]] as the [[WeiState|魏国]] official under whom [[FanJu|范雎]] serves. The source summary renders his name as 徐谷, which this page treats as a transcript variant of the standard 须贾.

In the episode, Fan Ju lacks the money and access needed to reach the Wei ruler directly, so he attaches himself to Xu Jia's mission to [[QiState|齐国]]. When [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]] privately rewards Fan Ju, Xu Jia reads the gift as possible evidence that Fan Ju has betrayed Wei secrets and reports the matter to [[WeiQiWarringStates|魏齐]].

Xu Jia therefore represents the gatekeeping and suspicion side of [[WarringStatesCareeristMobility|Warring States careerist mobility]]. A talented subordinate's cross-state recognition can look like betrayal when hierarchy, jealousy, and interstate espionage fears override verification.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-90-jiemi-xianqin-zui-wanmei-de-fuchou-liu7gbl-bffknkw6dx5udqcq4tcm]] returns Xu Jia as the exposed envoy. [[WeiState|魏国]] sends him to [[QinState|秦国]] after [[FanJu|范雎]]'s near-attack pressure makes peace necessary, and he mistakes Fan Ju for a poor refugee rather than the Qin chancellor using the alias 张禄. Xu Jia's robe gift saves his life, but Fan Ju still forces him through apology, public status loss, and a banquet humiliation that makes the old accusation return as visible disgrace.
