# 徐霞客

徐霞客 appears in [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] as the historical ancestor whom [[DingWenjiang|丁文江]] reclaims for modern field inquiry. The episode contrasts older praise of Xu as a "奇人" with Ding's dissatisfaction that such praise made him eccentric rather than intellectually serious.

For ZhangQuan's reading, Xu matters because he traveled to know, not merely to perform endurance or collect scenery. Ding's use and republication of 《徐霞客游记》 therefore becomes part of the source's broader argument: modern Chinese scholarship could adopt scientific methods while also finding earlier Chinese precedents for direct observation and difficult travel.

## Key Claims
- Xu Xiake is framed as a knowledge seeker, not only a legendary traveler.
- Ding Wenjiang's rereading of Xu creates continuity between older travel records and modern [[FieldworkAsKnowledgeMethod]].
- The episode uses Xu to show that fieldwork can challenge book-centered scholarly prestige.
