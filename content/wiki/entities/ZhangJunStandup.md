# 张俊 / Zhang Jun (Stand-Up Comedian)

## Overview
张俊 / Zhang Jun is a stand-up comedian featured in the [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] episode on "装逼" as the explicit elite-style contrast to [[XiaoQiStandup|小齐]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Zhang Jun as someone whose education, overseas experience, accent, body language, vocabulary, and polished self-description make him easy to read as "显性装逼". He discusses Columbia University, overseas Chinese student hierarchies, Wall Street-adjacent professional circles, and the way achievement-list culture can become a form of self-display.

His role is not only to be teased. Zhang Jun offers several of the episode's strongest analytic claims: display is judged against audience expectation; status behavior exists at every class level; and successful signaling requires enough cultural overlap for the audience to recognize what is being displayed.

## Key Characteristics
- Stand-up comedian positioned as the source's explicit elite-signaling case.
- Associated in the episode with Columbia University, overseas Chinese student stratification, finance-world polish, and written or academic-style language.
- Provides analytic claims about expectation matching, cultural distance, and class-specific status display.
- Uses stand-up to expose and weaken the daily-life advantages of elite fluency.
- Source-scoped profile limited to one panel episode rather than a complete biography or comedy-career account.
