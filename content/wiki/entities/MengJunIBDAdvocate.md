# Meng Jun / 孟君 (IBD Advocate)

## Overview
Meng Jun / 孟君 is presented as a person living with inflammatory bowel disease and a full-time public-communication worker at [[CCCFIBDFoundation|CCCF 爱在延长炎症性肠病基金会]].

## Current Profile
Meng's account connects a severe onset immediately before the gaokao with stepwise treatment, clinical remission, maintenance medicine, and later nonprofit work. She treats remission as an active condition to sustain rather than the disappearance of disease, while using her experience to support public education and information exchange between patients and clinicians.

## Key Characteristics
- Describes onset at age eighteen with abdominal pain, bleeding, fever, appetite loss, insomnia, and repeated urgent toilet use.
- Reports moving through mesalazine, corticosteroids, immunomodulation, a biologic clinical trial, and later small-molecule maintenance treatment.
- Presents remission as compatible with ordinary eating, work, and preferred activities while still requiring continuing care.
- Works in external communication for an IBD nonprofit whose staff includes people living with the disease.
