# Su-Lin Wong

Su-Lin Wong is the [[TheIntelligence]] participant in [[for-bucks-sake-the-rise-of-self-made-billionaires-6a61e076562e3ad886d54c95]] who explains [[Cambodia]]'s scam-compound crackdown. Her segment frames online fraud as an organized transnational industry rather than as scattered individual scammers.

The source uses Wong to connect victim-facing [[SocialEngineeringFraud]] to upstream sites, casinos, sanctions, extradition, tourism damage, and political protection. Her core contribution is [[ScamCompoundPoliticalProtection]]: raids and financial sanctions can disrupt some compounds while leaving the enabling system intact if protected elites remain untouched.
