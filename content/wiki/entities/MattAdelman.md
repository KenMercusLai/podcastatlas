# Matt Adelman

Matt Adelman appears in [[who-decides-what-big-box-sells-our-game-got-us-answers]] as a former [[Target]] buyer interviewed by [[PlanetMoney]] to explain how big-box retail decisions work. Although his own buying category was deli and prepared foods rather than board games, the source uses him to generalize the [[RetailLineReview]] process across categories.

His contribution is the buyer-side constraint. He says a large funnel of submissions turns into far fewer meetings and only a small number of shelf wins, and he explains that a buyer must think about [[PlanogramGatekeeping]], [[SalesVelocity]], and whether a new item will perform better than the one it replaces.
