# Alfa Romeo

Alfa Romeo is the Italian automaker and racing organization that gave [[EnzoFerrari]] his early racing and commercial platform in [[ferrari-1]]. Enzo raced for Alfa, became a dealer, and then proposed running races with his own organization when Alfa reduced its direct racing activity.

That arrangement produced [[ScuderiaFerrari]], making Alfa Romeo the institutional bridge between Enzo's racing ambition and the later [[Ferrari]] company. The source also uses Alfa to show that Ferrari's identity began inside a larger Italian racing ecosystem before becoming independent.
