# 《穿透估值》

## Overview
《穿透估值》 is presented in the wiki as [[ZouPeixuan|邹佩轩]]'s valuation book preceding [[ChuantouXushi|《穿透叙事》]]. The episode summarizes its core role as restoring DCF to first-principles status while treating relative valuation ratios as simplified cases under specific assumptions.

## Current Profile
The book's podcast-described function is connective. It sits between financial-report analysis and narrative judgment: financial reports supply the verified business facts, while valuation translates those facts and future assumptions into a price language.

The episode links the book to DCF, CAPM, investment-portfolio theory, PE, PB, PEG, and PS. The durable wiki takeaway is not that one formula is sufficient, but that multiples should be read as compressed assumptions about cash flows, risk, and terminal state.

## Key Characteristics
- It makes DCF the first-principles anchor for valuation work.
- It treats PE, PB, PEG, and PS as simplified formulas under specific assumptions rather than valuation origins.
- It connects risk and discount-rate thinking to CAPM and portfolio theory.
- It prepares the move from financial-report verification to narrative judgment.
- It warns against treating relative multiples as substitutes for explicit business assumptions.

## Evidence
- **DCF-first profile:** [[caibao-de-gen-guzhi-de-jing-xushi-de-guoshi-lolxytqwzzxq9kzjbtkmfolafavb]] summarizes 《穿透估值》 around DCF as the valuation system's first principle.
- **Relative-valuation compression:** [[caibao-de-gen-guzhi-de-jing-xushi-de-guoshi-lolxytqwzzxq9kzjbtkmfolafavb]] states that PE, PB, PEG, and related ratios are simplified DCF forms under particular conditions.
- **Trilogy connection:** [[caibao-de-gen-guzhi-de-jing-xushi-de-guoshi-lolxytqwzzxq9kzjbtkmfolafavb]] places valuation between financial reports as foundation and narrative as final investment judgment.

## Qualifications
- This page is based on the episode's summary, not the book's full argument.
- CAPM and portfolio theory are mentioned as context in the source but are not explained in detail.
- The page should not be read as endorsing mechanical DCF precision; the same episode emphasizes reverse use, source-scoped discount-rate assumptions, and model sensitivity.
