# Padmini Soni

## Overview
Padmini Soni is an AI literacy, adoption, business-transformation, and responsible-growth practitioner interviewed in [[ep-22-governing-ai-with-purpose-and-inclusion|Data Science With Sam EP22]] and presented as a co-founder of [[AsianWomenAdvancingAI]].

## Current Profile
Soni frames governance as both an operating framework and a people-centered mindset spanning an AI system's full lifecycle. Her position joins early risk assessment, inclusive participation, cultural context, practical tool use, and continuous monitoring, with the claim that well-designed governance can support innovation by reducing avoidable harm and rework.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats [[LifecycleAIGovernance]] as work that begins during design rather than after deployment.
- Connects technical controls with accountability, ethics, transparency, safety, and regulation.
- Advocates [[InclusiveAIDesign]] involving people affected by a system.
- Uses cultural and everyday design examples to show how narrow teams can miss requirements.
- Frames governance education through education, exposure, and hands-on experience.
- Co-founded [[AsianWomenAdvancingAI]] to support visibility, mentorship, learning, and belonging.
