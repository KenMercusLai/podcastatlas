# 徐秋 / Xu Qiu (Guangling late Han)

## Overview
徐秋 / Xu Qiu (Guangling late Han) enters the wiki as the former Guangling administrator who receives the [[HeirloomImperialSeal|传国玉玺]] after [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s death and sends it back to the court.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] gives Xu Qiu a narrow symbolic role. The seal had helped Yuan Shu imagine an imperial claim, but after Yuan Shu dies it does not anchor a successor regime. Xu Qiu's act of sending the object to the court closes the source's seal branch by returning a dynastic object to the recognized title order.

## Key Characteristics
- Former Guangling administrator named in the Yuan Shu collapse source.
- Receives the Heirloom Imperial Seal after Yuan Shu's death.
- Sends the seal to the court rather than using it for a new claimant.
- Functions as an endpoint for Yuan Shu's failed symbolic legitimacy project.

## Evidence
- Seal return: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] says Xu Qiu obtains the Heirloom Imperial Seal and soon presents it to the court.
- Symbolic endpoint: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] places the seal return immediately after Yuan Shu's death and Yuan Yin's flight to Liu Xun, making it part of the dismantling of Yuan Shu's title project.
