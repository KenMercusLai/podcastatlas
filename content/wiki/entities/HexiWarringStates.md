# 河西 / Hexi (Warring States)

河西 / Hexi appears in the Zhouji Qin-Wei branch as contested western Wei territory that [[QinState|秦国]] keeps pulling away from [[WeiState|魏国]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-23-weihuiwang-hui-buyong-gongshu-zhi-yan-lveaqklo7oncmqudgmdk7l03qhsf]] records Wei ceding Hexi after [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] defeats Wei and captures [[GongziAng|公子卬 / 公子昂]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] shows the pressure continuing in the later 330 BCE sequence. After Qin surrounds [[Jiaoyi|焦邑]] and [[QuwoWarringStates|曲沃]], Wei yields [[Shaoliang|少梁]] and Hexi land, so Hexi should be read as a continuing frontier-loss branch rather than a single isolated transaction.
