# Javier Milei

Javier Milei appears in [[currency-chaos-in-argentina-summer-school]] as the Argentine presidential candidate and later president whose campaign promised drastic economic change after years of inflation and exchange-rate instability. The [[PlanetMoney]] episode says he proposed sharp budget cuts, used a chainsaw as a public symbol of those cuts, and discussed replacing the peso with the U.S. dollar.

The source treats Milei's early presidency as a stabilization tradeoff rather than a settled success story. It says inflation had slowed by 2026, but also notes layoffs, business closures, recession pressure, and still-high annual price increases. [[SebastianGaliani]] presents the deficit cuts and reduced money printing as central, while rejecting full dollarization because [[ExchangeRateFlexibility]] can help a country respond to real shocks.
