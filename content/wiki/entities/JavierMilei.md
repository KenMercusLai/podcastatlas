# Javier Milei

## Overview
Javier Milei is the Argentine president whose chainsaw-branded reform program anchors the wiki's current [[Argentina]] stabilization branch.

## Current Profile
[[currency-chaos-in-argentina-summer-school]] introduces Milei as the candidate and later president promising drastic change after years of inflation and exchange-rate instability. [[chainsaw-sputtering-mileis-experiment-falters-6aa1299999455f9a93f7de9b]] updates him as a still-confident but strained incumbent: inflation, poverty, debt service, and market confidence have improved, while jobs, wages, growth breadth, household debt, and campaign pressure now test his program.

The current profile is a stabilization tradeoff, not a settled success or failure. Milei's symbolic chainsaw still communicates [[Austerity]], deregulation, and ideological commitment, but the newer source says voters are increasingly judging material recovery rather than only inflation relief.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses the chainsaw as a public symbol of cutting spending and taxes.
- Frames his project as radical free-market reform and deregulation after long monetary instability.
- Has delivered lower monthly inflation in the source account, with poverty and market confidence also improving.
- Faces weak job and wage conditions, slower-than-planned growth, and household debt stress.
- Aligns foreign policy closely with the [[UnitedStates]], [[DonaldTrump]], and [[Israel]], while becoming more pragmatic toward [[China]] after swap-line support.
- Uses combative rhetoric toward journalists and the Falklands/Malvinas dispute as part of his political style.

## Evidence
- Early program: [[currency-chaos-in-argentina-summer-school]] says Milei proposed sharp budget cuts, used a chainsaw symbol, discussed dollarization, reduced money printing, and loosened some dollar restrictions.
- Stabilization update: [[chainsaw-sputtering-mileis-experiment-falters-6aa1299999455f9a93f7de9b]] says inflation fell sharply from the inherited monthly rate, poverty fell by about a third, debt payments pleased markets, and the [[InternationalMonetaryFund]] was positive.
- Political vulnerability: [[chainsaw-sputtering-mileis-experiment-falters-6aa1299999455f9a93f7de9b]] says private-sector employment is below 2023, public-sector jobs were cut, manufacturing and tourism struggle, and many households are more than 90 days behind on debt.

## Qualifications
The sources evaluate Milei through podcast reporting, not through a full macroeconomic model. [[chainsaw-sputtering-mileis-experiment-falters-6aa1299999455f9a93f7de9b]] also says he remains narrowly favored because opposition alternatives are divided or associated with earlier excesses.
