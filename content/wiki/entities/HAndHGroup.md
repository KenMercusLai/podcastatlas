# H&H Group / 合生元·健合集团

## Overview
H&H Group, formed around the Chinese infant-formula and consumer-health company 合生元 and now known as 健合集团, is the owner that acquired Swisse and, in the source's reading, bought origin-country brand value more than production capacity.

## Current Profile
The episode says 合生元 took control of Swisse in September 2015 and completed full ownership about two years later, with the transaction reported near AUD 1.7 billion, or roughly RMB 8.7 billion. It judges the price as not especially generous for Swisse but favourable for the buyer, because the acquisition added a large block of revenue and a growth story rather than a defensible technology.

The source's key observation is that ownership does not change the marketing identity: Swisse can still describe itself as an Australian supplement brand, and Chinese consumers can keep applying their general impression of Australian quality to it. That makes [[ConstructedCategoryTrust|belief built around origin]] the asset being transferred, which the episode calls an unusually good outcome for a category it sees as having no strong moat.

## Key Characteristics
- Chinese consumer-health acquirer of a well-known Australian supplement brand.
- Transaction framed as buying brand equity and category exposure rather than manufacturing or scientific capability.
- Ownership change did not force a change of national brand story, because origin-country framing remains commercially useful.
- Deal economics are reported as low premium for the seller and high strategic value for the buyer.
- Illustrates how supplement-industry value concentrates in brand goodwill when production is externalised.
