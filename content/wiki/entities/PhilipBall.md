# Philip Ball

Philip Ball is named in [[ep256-ai-shidai-ziyou-yizhi-hai-cunzai-ma-lk9aci8oqnwerk26jy683nbdddcx]] as the author of [[HowLifeWorks|How Life Works]], one of the prompts behind [[TuMotuo|土摩托]]'s turn from abstract [[FreeWill|free will]] toward biology and life.

In this source, Ball matters because his life-science framing helps make [[MeaningAsEvolvedFunction|meaning]] and [[BiologicalAgency|biological agency]] discussable without relying only on theology or pure introspection. The episode uses that route to ask why a living organism's action feels different from a stone rolling under gravity.

- [[HowLifeWorks|How Life Works]] - book named as an inspiration for the episode.
- [[TuMotuo]] - speaker who cites Ball's work.
