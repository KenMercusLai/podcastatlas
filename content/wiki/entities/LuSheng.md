# 卢生 / Lu Sheng

卢生 / Lu Sheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-123-1-shen-zhi-yuyan-wangqin-zhe-hu-jing-shi-zhi-lg7m3vfdbcweb4joxe4jvhxjx6y8]] as the Yan-region searcher sent by [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] to look for ancient immortals around the 渤海 and [[Jieshi|碣石]] route. The source says he does not return with immortals, but he does return from the sea with a 图书 carrying the phrase "亡秦者胡."

His role is therefore different from [[XuFu|徐福]]'s in the previous branch. Xu Fu converts failed access to [[Penglai|蓬莱]] into renewed material demands, while Lu Sheng converts failed immortal contact into a warning text that can shape imperial action. The episode treats that warning as the immediate frame for Qin Shi Huang's decision to send [[MengTian|蒙恬]] and 300,000 troops north against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-125-1-qinshihuang-xiujian-shishang-zuida-lanweilou-afanggong-lhqc6jw4jzoyrg7jzc2mbxdebj4|Qinji 125-1]] adds Lu Sheng's next-stage advice. Instead of returning with immortals, he tells Qin Shi Huang that a ruler seeking immortality should avoid fixed residence and keep his lodging secret so ghosts cannot obstruct him and immortals can appear. The result is [[ImperialItinerarySecrecy|帝王行踪保密]] and a growing [[FangshiAccountabilityTrap|方士问责陷阱]]: if the emperor obeys and still receives no immortal medicine, the advisers who sold the method become dangerously exposed.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-125-2-lishishang-qinshihuang-weihe-yao-fenshu-kengru-lulaivzp1xqs71nbaltr-ygpjihw|Qinji 125-2]] turns that exposure into flight and backlash. Lu Sheng and [[HouShengFangshi|侯生]] criticize Qin Shi Huang's self-willed, punitive, overcentralized rule and then run away. Qin Shi Huang's anger at their criticism and flight becomes the source's immediate trigger for interrogating Xianyang scholars and for the [[QinKengruIncident|秦代坑儒事件]].

The page keeps Lu Sheng source-scoped. The episode's interest is not a full biography of a fangshi, but the mechanism by which a court specialist can turn unverifiable sacred access into [[ProphecyTriggeredPolicy|预言触发政策]].
