# Stendhal / 斯汤达

Stendhal is the French writer at the center of [[21-hong-yu-hei-ta-si-yu-zhencheng-569042001]], where [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] presents [[RedAndBlackNovel|《红与黑》]] as a key 19th-century novel of ambition, love, class, and inner contradiction. The episode stresses that Stendhal personally experienced the [[NapoleonBonaparte|Napoleonic]] era rather than merely inheriting it as legend, making Julien's military dream and Restoration-era blockage feel historically charged.

The source frames Stendhal as underrecognized in his own time, criticized by figures such as [[SainteBeuve|Sainte-Beuve / 圣伯夫]], and later vindicated through readers and writers including [[LeoTolstoy|Tolstoy]]. It uses that reception history to support [[ClassicReadingComplexity]]: a novel can look like scandalous social news in outline while carrying lasting force through structure, style, and psychology.

Stendhal's most important contribution in this source is [[PsychologicalRealism]]. The episode argues that he turns plot through inner movement: shame, pride, ambition, desire, religious performance, jealousy, and sudden sincerity do the work that a more external plot machine might otherwise do.
