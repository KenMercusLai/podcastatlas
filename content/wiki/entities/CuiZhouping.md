# 崔州平 / Cui Zhouping

## Overview

崔州平 / Cui Zhouping is a late-Han recluse and associate of [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] whose wiki profile now separates his historical friendship network from his later [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]] reception image. He is also presented as a son of [[CuiLieLateHan|崔烈]], creating a family contrast between the father's bought-office disgrace and the son's reclusive reputation.

## Current Profile

Hanji 934 introduces Cui Zhouping as a literary gateway into the late-Han Liangzhou crisis. Its opening evokes the plain robe and 逍遥巾 associated with the *Romance of the Three Kingdoms* scene in which [[LiuBei|刘备]] briefly mistakes him for Zhuge Liang. That source does not make him an actor in the subsequent frontier-policy dispute; his function there is reception and narrative transition.

Hanji 1081 adds a narrower historical-social role. It places Cui Zhouping with [[XuShuLateHan|徐庶]] among the people who recognized Zhuge Liang's talent during the latter's farming and reclusion years, and identifies Cui Zhouping as Cui Lie's son. The episode interprets the two generations as disgrace followed by redemption, but that moral closure remains the host's framing rather than a demonstrated family program.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Han recluse associated with Zhuge Liang's Jingzhou friendship network.
- Early recognizer of Zhuge Liang's ability alongside Xu Shu in Hanji 1081's account.
- Son of Cui Lie in the episode's family genealogy.
- Literary reception figure recognizable from Liu Bei's Longzhong-visit scenes.
- Bridge between popular Three Kingdoms memory and narrower late-Han political history.

## Evidence

- Literary reception: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-934-sha-yiren-ze-tianxia-an-ni-sha-busha-ltar8hvdkqct8oizm3k22z38b1v8|Hanji 934]] uses Cui Zhouping's novelistic appearance to open a source mainly concerned with Cui Lie, Fu Xie, Liangzhou, and Huangfu Song.
- Friendship and talent recognition: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1081-aizai-caocao-tongshi-zui-fengli-de-moulue-zhi-ren-lj1svjc8nuh7vdawypetjqa1h88u|Hanji 1081]] places him with Xu Shu among Zhuge Liang's friends and early appreciators.
- Family connection: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1081-aizai-caocao-tongshi-zui-fengli-de-moulue-zhi-ren-lj1svjc8nuh7vdawypetjqa1h88u|Hanji 1081]] identifies Cui Zhouping as Cui Lie's son and uses their reputations as a two-generation contrast.

## Qualifications

The bounded sources do not supply a continuous biography, offices, dates, writings, or independent evidence for Cui Zhouping's motives. Hanji 934 is explicitly a literary-reception opening, while Hanji 1081 gives only a compact friendship and genealogy claim. The latter's “two-generation redemption” language is interpretive and should not be treated as Cui Zhouping's stated project.
