# 崔州平 / Cui Zhouping

崔州平 / Cui Zhouping enters through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-934-sha-yiren-ze-tianxia-an-ni-sha-busha-ltar8hvdkqct8oizm3k22z38b1v8|Hanji 934]] as the episode's literary gateway into the 185 CE late-Han crisis. The source presents him as a young man who associated with notable figures such as [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]], and notes that [[LiuBei|刘备]] briefly mistakes him for Zhuge Liang during the Longzhong visit scene in [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]].

The page is intentionally narrow. In this source, Cui Zhouping is not the driver of the Liangzhou policy dispute; he is a reception marker whose plain robe, xiaoyao headscarf, and reclusive-literati image make a familiar Three Kingdoms entry point before the episode turns to [[CuiLieLateHan|崔烈]], [[FuXie|傅燮]], and [[LiangzhouLateHan|凉州]].
