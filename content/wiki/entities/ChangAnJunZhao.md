# 长安君 / Lord Chang'an of Zhao

长安君 / Lord Chang'an of Zhao appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-91-chulong-ruhe-qiaokai-shougua-zhaotaihou-de-men-1-lmgd7kdl1nrfwcrtkqttumtlpqok]] as [[ZhaoWeihou|赵威后]]'s youngest and most beloved son. When [[QinState|秦国]] attacks [[ZhaoState|赵国]], [[QiState|齐国]] conditions aid on Zhao sending Chang'an Jun as a hostage.

In this first half of the story, Chang'an Jun is more political object than active agent. His value comes from what others do around him: Zhao Weihou refuses to expose him to hostage risk, ministers cannot move her, and [[ChuLong|触龙]] has to reframe protection as a long-range future rather than immediate physical closeness. The episode stops before the final outcome, so the page keeps his hostage status unresolved.
