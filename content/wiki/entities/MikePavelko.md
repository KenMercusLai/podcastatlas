# Mike Pavelko

Mike Pavelko appears in [[a-pro-worker-experiment-in-private-equity]] as a [[GSI]] worker who joined the company in 2018, the same year [[KKR]] bought it. He describes GSI's work as hands-on, intense, and sometimes dangerous.

Mike's story is the episode's strongest example of [[PrivateEquityWorkerOwnership]] working as a visible incentive. Because GSI workers were told about their equity from the start, he says employees felt more involved and cared more about hitting company targets.

When KKR announced GSI's sale in October 2024, Mike received an initial $195,000 and could receive another $25,000 for each of the next two years if he stayed, totaling about $250,000. He used the money to buy his first home in Tennessee.
