# 《谏逐客书》 / Jian Zhu Ke Shu

《谏逐客书》 / Jian Zhu Ke Shu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-109-2-chunshenjun-bei-huangdi-dajiuge-quanzu-miemen-locma2plu7v-vsssm5dr5frc3szw]] as the text previewed for the next episode. The source says [[QinState|秦国]] orders foreign scholars expelled, and [[LiSi|李斯]] writes this memorial while packing to leave.

That episode's only claim about the memorial is its narrative function: the host asks what it says that can make [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] wake up and stop the expulsion order.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-110-1-lisi-micang-laoshu-he-xiashuidao-laoshu-ni-zenme-xuan-lmv-fw-kgsuokonssq8bkbfvdsb]] supplies the crisis setup but still stops before the memorial's full argument. It identifies the policy pressure behind the text: after [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] loses central office, Qin royal-clan ministers push the [[GuestExpulsionOrder|逐客令]] by claiming foreign guests may serve their original rulers and by pointing to cases such as [[ZhengGuoHydraulicEngineer|郑国]]. The page therefore remains compact on the text's content while expanding the political reason Li Si must write.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-110-2-qinshihuang-de-cp-weihe-bei-quzhu-chuguo-lj0222vw8ijbvj25iwfzh-qhvzr9]] gives the memorial's argument. Li Si first cites Qin precedent: [[QinMugong|秦穆公]], [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]], [[YingSi|秦惠王 / 嬴驷]], and [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] all used guest talent to build Qin power. He then contrasts Qin's willingness to enjoy non-Qin luxury goods with its proposed rejection of non-Qin people, and finally warns that expelling guests is equivalent to sending useful labor, advice, and weapons to enemy states. The episode says this persuades Ying Zheng to recall Li Si, restore his office, and revoke the guest-expulsion order.
