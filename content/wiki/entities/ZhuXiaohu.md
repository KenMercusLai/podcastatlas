# 朱啸虎 / Zhu Xiaohu

## Overview
朱啸虎 / Zhu Xiaohu is the venture investor used by the episode as a representative O2O-era capital figure.

## Current Profile
The source presents Zhu through his [[GSRVentures|金沙江创投]] path and earlier O2O bets. His experience with Baixing, Lashou, Ele.me, Didi, Xiaohongshu, Inke, and especially [[Ofo|OFO]] makes him a useful lens on speed, scale, and category timing in the 2010s China internet market.

In the shared-bike section, Zhu is not just an investor. He becomes part of the public narrative battle over whether OFO's lower-cost model or [[Mobike]]'s smart-lock and hardware model had the better future, and later pushes for an OFO-Mobike merger when the subsidy war looks unsustainable.

## Key Characteristics
- He is framed as a high-profile China internet venture capitalist associated with O2O-era speed and expansion thinking.
- His prior investments make him a bridge between classifieds, group buying, food delivery, ride hailing, community platforms, and shared bikes.
- In the OFO case, he is shown backing rapid scaling from campus economics into city-level bike deployment.
- His public exchange with [[PonyMa|马化腾]] over OFO and Mobike makes investor narratives part of the shared-bike war.
- He later publicly supports a Mobike-OFO merger because the duopoly subsidy burn looked economically unsustainable.
