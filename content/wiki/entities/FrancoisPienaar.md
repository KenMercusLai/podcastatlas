# Francois Pienaar

## Overview

Francois Pienaar is presented as the [[Springboks]] captain whose relationship with [[NelsonMandela]] helped make the 1995 Rugby World Cup final a public reconciliation ritual.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Pienaar as the team-side anchor of Mandela's rugby strategy. Mandela reaches out to him, puts public weight behind the Springboks, wears Pienaar's number-six shirt at [[EllisPark]], and later lets the captain stand as part of the "one team, one country" image. Pienaar's reported emotional difficulty during the anthem reinforces the episode's view that the event worked because it was charged, not because symbolism was easy.

## Key Characteristics

- Captain of the [[Springboks]] during the [[RugbyWorldCup1995|1995 Rugby World Cup]].
- Public partner in Mandela's "one team, one country" reconciliation strategy.
- Number-six jersey wearer whose shirt Mandela adopted for the final.
- Figure tied to the team's attempt to sing [[NkosiSikelelIAfrika|Nkosi Sikelel' iAfrika]].
- Rugby actor through whom Mandela addressed [[Afrikaners|Afrikaner]] pride without endorsing apartheid nostalgia.
