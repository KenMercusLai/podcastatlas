# 阎忠 / Yan Zhong (late Han)

[[YanZhongLateHan|阎忠]] appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-942-yue-youxiu-de-ren-huode-yue-lei-heli-ma-lkwlrcgnt-kxxc2qqz6z6wkg7e5z|Hanji 942]] as a former Xindu official from Hanyang whom [[HanSui|韩遂]] coerces into leading the rebel coalition after [[WangGuoLateHan|王国]] is removed. His role is nominal and unstable rather than a sign of consolidated leadership.

Yan Zhong soon dies of illness, after which Han Sui's side turns inward and weakens through mutual attacks. The episode uses this as a concise illustration of [[InterestStructuredAllianceFragility|interest-structured alliance fragility]]: once Wang Guo's campaign fails, the coalition's shared front gives way to disputes over command and survival.
