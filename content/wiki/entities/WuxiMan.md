# 五溪蛮 / Wuxi Man

五溪蛮 / Wuxi Man enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1027-jingzhou-zhizhu-liubiao-weihe-nan-cheng-bazhu-ll-ebguqllsf-5ycvpdogihzn3lj|Hanji 1027]] as the southwestern tribal-force field that [[LiuBiao|刘表]] tries to court after [[ZhangXianLateHan|张献]] and [[HuanJieLateHan|桓阶]] bring [[SouthernJingzhouLateHan|southern Jingzhou]] pressure into [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s orbit.

The source names [[ShaMoke|沙摩柯]] as the leader Liu Biao tries to draw in. In this episode, the Wuxi Man are not developed ethnographically; they are a military and frontier-response option that shows Liu Biao reacting to strategic pressure after his more central [[Jingzhou|荆州]] control has been strained.
