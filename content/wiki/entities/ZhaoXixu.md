# 昭奚恤 / Zhao Xixu

昭奚恤 / Zhao Xixu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]] when the [[ChuState|楚国]] ruler appoints him as chancellor. The episode uses the notice to explain [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s recurring interest in chief ministers and to introduce [[ChancellorCenteredGovernance|chancellor-centered governance]] as a Song literati reading habit.

The second half of the source makes Zhao Xixu the target of [[JiangYi|江乙]]'s rhetoric. Jiang Yi tells stories that portray Zhao Xixu as someone blocking useful speech from reaching the ruler, turning the chancellor into a case in [[PoliticalParableWeaponization|political parable weaponization]] and [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|court feedback]] politics.
