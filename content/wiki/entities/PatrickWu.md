# Patrick Wu

Patrick Wu is the [[Clink]] founder and CEO interviewed in [[keyi-gei-nide-agent-fa-yidian-linghuaqian-le-s10e22-9a652c19-ceb3-46c2-87b4-bca36e684311]]. The source presents him as a payments operator whose background includes Amazon retail payments, AWS B2B payments, Chinese third-party payments, and a senior payment role at a cross-border e-commerce platform.

Patrick's main wiki contribution is a payment-infrastructure reading of the agent boom. He argues that [[AgenticCommerce]] is not solved by letting an agent click checkout: user intent, budget, merchant data, one-time payment capability, dispute handling, and liability all have to be represented in the transaction.

He also frames [[Stablecoins]] and fiat rails differently. Stablecoins may be technically straightforward for small transfers or B2B settlement, while fiat card and bank networks remain stronger for ordinary consumer trust because dispute and protection mechanisms already exist.
