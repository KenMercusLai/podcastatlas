# Aweil / 阿维尔

## Overview
Aweil / 阿维尔 is the [[SouthSudan|南苏丹]] city area used in [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] as the field-office gateway to [[WedweilRefugeeCamp|维德维尔难民营]] and nearby Sudanese refugee flows.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Aweil as a logistical hinge between the capital-based UN compound world and the refugee-camp field. Wang Hongrui reaches Aweil by UN aircraft after a long multi-stop trip, then travels by road to Wedweil the next day.

Aweil's significance in the source is geographic and humanitarian. Its proximity to the Sudan border makes it a plausible entry area for refugees fleeing [[Sudan]], while the nearby camp becomes a site where WFP, UNHCR, and displaced families encounter each other.

## Key Characteristics
- South Sudan field-office area in the episode's WFP travel sequence.
- Gateway to Wedweil refugee camp.
- Region close enough to Sudan to receive refugee arrivals.
- Logistical contrast with the capital, requiring UN flight access and road movement to camp.

- Camp access: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] says Wedweil is about a one-hour drive from Aweil.
