# 《教一只猞猁忘记动物园》

《教一只猞猁忘记动物园》 is the new book by [[YuanZhang|袁掌]] discussed in [[163-jiao-yi-zhi-sheli-wangji-dongwuyuan-xiye-de-gushi-bi-fengkuang-dongwucheng-2-geng-jingcai-939887179]]. The source presents it as a first-person nonfiction account of [[TianxianBaobaoLynx|天线宝宝]]'s rescue, rehabilitation, release, and post-release monitoring through [[XiningWildlifePark|西宁野生动物园]]'s work.

The book matters in the wiki because it makes [[WildAnimalRescueResponsibility]] legible as a sequence of practical decisions rather than a sentimental slogan. The source repeatedly returns to the title's paradox: to help a wild animal survive, caretakers may have to make the zoo less visible, less comfortable, and less emotionally available.

## Key Claims
- The book uses one [[Lynx|lynx]] individual to explain broader rescue, rehabilitation, release, and public-communication problems.
- First-person narration lets readers follow decisions about treatment, feeding, visitor exposure, release timing, satellite monitoring, livestock risk, and emotional attachment.
- The title does not mean erasing care; it means reducing human habituation so the animal can keep enough distance from people after release.
- The book format carries background and context that short online posts or viral clips can lose, especially around decisions that look harsh without full explanation.
