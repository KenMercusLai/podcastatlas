# 范滂 / Fan Pang (late Han)

## Overview

Fan Pang is a late-Han party-prohibition figure whose current profile runs from honor-list visibility in Hanji 892 to voluntary imprisonment, non-implication of helpers, and family farewell in Hanji 893.

## Current Profile

Hanji 892 supplies Fan Pang's pre-crackdown reputation setting by listing him among the士人 honor categories that make public praise visible. Hanji 893 then places Fan Pang among the party figures named in [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]]'s accusation chain. When [[WuDaoLateHan|吴导]] receives the order to arrest him and grieves at the post station, Fan Pang recognizes the situation and goes to prison himself. [[GuoYiLateHan|郭揖]] tries to free him and even prepares to abandon office, but Fan Pang refuses to drag Guo Yi into the disaster.

The episode uses Fan Pang's death-facing choice as a high-intensity名节 case. His mother accepts that a person who shares fame with [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]] and Du Mi cannot also expect long life, while Fan Pang's words to his young son expose the court's moral collapse: neither doing evil nor doing good provides a coherent survival rule when the state has reversed praise and punishment.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Han士人 whose honor-list visibility precedes his later party-prohibition arrest.
- Party figure whose name appears in the arrest field created by Cao Jie's framing.
- Voluntary prisoner who recognizes the arrest order and goes to prison instead of hiding.
- Non-implicating actor who rejects Guo Yi's escape plan to avoid transferring danger to a helper.
- Death-facing son whose farewell with his mother turns reputation into accepted sacrifice.
- Moral witness whose final words accuse the court of making ordinary good and evil unreadable.

## Qualifications

This page is grounded in Hanji 892 and Hanji 893 and does not reconstruct Fan Pang's full career before the second party-prohibition disaster. Du Mi is preserved in prose rather than promoted to a canonical page from this source alone because the episode uses him mainly as a reputation peer. Transcript variants such as 范胖 are normalized to 范滂.

## What Changed

- Hanji 892 adds the pre-crackdown honor-list visibility layer before Hanji 893's arrest and farewell account.
- The current profile now treats Fan Pang's名节 as already publicly legible before the formal party accusation reaches him.
