# 封谞 / Feng Xu (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-923-chengnianrende-dingji-henjin-guzi-lide-juejue-lifzh-gohpqnmdznnfv72tndqf8h|Hanji 923]] backfills Feng Xu before his exposure in Hanji 924. In this source, [[MaYuanyiLateHan|马元义]] repeatedly enters [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] and links the [[TaipingDao|太平道]] plan to palace contacts including Feng Xu and [[XuFengLateHan|徐奉]].

封谞 / Feng Xu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-924-1-de-cuowu-100-de-shibai-lmeo7vufm6ib5lni6p49rponazut|Hanji 924]] as one of the palace eunuchs exposed as an inside contact for [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]] during the [[YellowTurbansLateHan|Yellow Turban]] emergency.

His case matters because it gives [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] direct evidence against the court narrative that party figures were the main rebellion danger. The emperor can scold the attendants by contrasting loyal party figures with Feng Xu and [[XuFengLateHan|徐奉]], but the source shows that this recognition does not break the broader protection around the [[TenAttendantsLateHan|十常侍]].
