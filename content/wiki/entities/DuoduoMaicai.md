# 多多买菜 / Duoduo Maicai

## Overview
多多买菜 / Duoduo Maicai is [[Pinduoduo]]'s platform-backed community-group-buying business.

## Current Profile
The sources position Duoduo Maicai as a large-platform version of preorder and neighborhood pickup. Pinduoduo's existing consumer demand and merchandising capacity could reduce dependence on group-head acquisition, while regional orders could inform local procurement, warehouse capacity, vehicle allocation, and promotion.

That demand visibility is an operating advantage, not a guarantee of attractive economics. The business still shares the category's cold-chain, sorting, pickup, refund, subsidy, and regulatory risks, and the later historical account places it among the clearer surviving platform-backed routes only after substantial industry retrenchment.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses Pinduoduo's consumer entrance and demand signals in community grocery.
- Can coordinate local supply and routes around aggregated orders and promotion.
- Competes on next-day pickup rather than individual instant delivery.
- Remains constrained by thin margins, execution detail, subsidy pressure, and regulation.

## Qualifications
The sources do not provide audited profitability or a current market assessment. Promotion-led demand shaping can improve asset use, but the episode does not establish its net welfare, supplier, or long-term margin effects.
