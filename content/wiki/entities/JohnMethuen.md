# John Methuen

## Overview

John Methuen is represented as the English diplomat who negotiated the 1703 [[MethuenTreaty]] with Portugal.

## Current Profile

[[12-days-port-wine-and-darwin-sets-sail-glt6423200478]] places Methuen at the junction of war, alliance, and trade. As ambassador to Portugal, he helped secure Portuguese realignment during the War of the Spanish Succession and a commercial exchange favoring English cloth and Portuguese wine. The episode also identifies him as Lord Chancellor of Ireland and notes Jonathan Swift's hostile judgment of his character and ability.

## Key Characteristics

- English ambassador associated with negotiating the Methuen Treaty.
- Diplomatic actor linking wartime alignment to commercial terms.
- Officeholder also presented as Lord Chancellor of Ireland.
- Historically remembered in the episode through both treaty consequences and hostile contemporary characterization.

## Evidence

- Diplomatic role - [[12-days-port-wine-and-darwin-sets-sail-glt6423200478]] identifies Methuen as Britain's ambassador to Portugal and treaty negotiator.

- Reputation - [[12-days-port-wine-and-darwin-sets-sail-glt6423200478]] reports Swift's strongly negative description.
