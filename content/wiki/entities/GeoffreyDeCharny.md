# Geoffrey de Charny

## Overview

Geoffrey de Charny is presented as an exemplary French knight who joined tournament reputation, crusading experience, chivalric writing, and practical concern for France's military effectiveness.

## Current Profile

The episode describes Charny as a younger Burgundian son who rose through martial achievement and became the first known owner of the Turin Shroud. His failed 1349 attempt to retake Calais by bribing Almerich of Pavia shows that chivalric standing did not exclude deception; Almerich informed [[EdwardIIIOfEngland|Edward III]], and Charny was ambushed, captured, and later ransomed.

Charny then helps [[JohnIIOfFrance|John II]] establish the Order of the Star, whose battle-centered obligations are meant to answer French military failure. At [[BattleOfPoitiers|Poitiers]], he carries the Oriflamme beside John and dies holding it, making his end both an emblem of fidelity and a warning that an oath against retreat can turn honor into tactical rigidity.

## Key Characteristics

- Burgundian younger son who rises through martial reputation.
- Tournament participant, crusader, and writer on chivalry.
- First known owner of the Turin Shroud in the episode's account.
- Planner willing to use bribery to recover Calais.
- Organizer of the Order of the Star's battle-centered chivalry.
- Oriflamme bearer killed at Poitiers.
