# Nepal

Nepal enters the wiki through [[afghanistan-five-years-on-our-correspondent-visits-6a7ee547049c692a6b3ee0a9]] as the national and mountain context for [[NirmalPurja]]. The source says Purja was born within sight of Dhaulagiri, left the mountains as a child, trained through a Gurkha and military route, and later returned to Himalayan climbing through [[ProjectPossible]].

The page is source-scoped. The episode uses Nepal less as a general country profile than as a way to connect birthplace, military training, Sherpa recognition, high-altitude labor and elite mountaineering records.
