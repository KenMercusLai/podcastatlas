# Gusty Spence

Gusty Spence appears in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as a key early figure in the revived [[UlsterVolunteerForce|UVF]]. The episode describes him as a former British Army soldier and Harland and Wolff scaffolder from the Protestant Shankill milieu, shaped by family memory of [[EdwardCarson|Carson]]'s volunteers and the First World War.

Spence and associates are tied in the source to petrol bombings, the mistaken attack that killed [[MatildaGould]], the attack on [[JohnPatrickScullion]], and the [[MalvernArms]] shooting that killed [[PeterWard]]. He therefore grounds [[ParamilitarySpoilerViolence]] before the later 1969 infrastructure-bombing branch.
