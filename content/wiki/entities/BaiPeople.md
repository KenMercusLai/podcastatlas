# 白族 / Bai People

白族 appears in [[144-nvxia-duan-sihe-he-tade-jiaxiang-mapingguan-women-ceng-yiwei-you-dian-you-lu-zhishi-meng-linmjqt-n-sa5sarpqgdxgtoeejt]] through [[DuanSihe|段四合]]'s identity and through the source's discussion of [[MamaHui|妈妈会]] as a village women's organization common in Bai communities. The page is source-scoped and does not attempt to summarize Bai history beyond this episode.

The episode uses Bai context to make village organization concrete. Ethnicity is not presented as decoration; it appears through language, local ritual, women's groups, performance, and the social forms through which residents organize care, prayer, expression, and public work.

## Key Claims
- The source says 段四合 is Bai and links her early Mandarin difficulty partly to local language environment.
- [[MamaHui|妈妈会]] gives the episode a Bai-associated institution through which women can express needs and join public affairs.
- Bai cultural context is connected to [[RuralWomenPublicParticipation]], [[VillageSelfGovernance]], and [[SlowRuralTourism]] because tourism and development operate through local social forms, not only through scenery or products.
