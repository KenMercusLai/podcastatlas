## Overview

侯胤 / Hou Yin enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-493-xiongdi-shi-hehuo-weisha-douhui-chouren-shi-sanhuo-lishi-gaosu-ni-llquknhihdms-6jyjbtfdfhvwv4g|Hanji 493]] as the Western Han official who opposes withdrawing frontier defenses after [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]] offers to guard the border for [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Hou Yin as a frontier-literate remonstrator. He does not reject Huhanye's current submission in simple bad faith; instead he argues that strategic infrastructure must be judged against future incentive change, population movement, local disorder, sunk construction, and bargaining leverage.

His importance is methodological as much as historical. While most officials accept the apparent peace dividend, Hou Yin breaks the issue into geography, history, military capacity, border administration, civilian flight, fugitive control, and future cost. The episode uses him as a model of structured risk counsel under hierarchy.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official familiar with frontier affairs.
- Opposes removing border defenses after Huhanye Chanyu's pledge of protection.
- Treats current submission as reversible because weakness, gratitude, and strength can change.
- Values Yinshan and accumulated forts, roads, garrisons, and beacon systems as strategic screens.
- Links frontier defense to internal governance, fugitive control, and population stability.
- Warns that delegated protection can become future reward-demand leverage.
