# Emmanuel Mignot

Emmanuel Mignot appears in [[wake-up-haul-an-ozempic-moment-for-the-brain-6a85798d1ad8716bb6078744]] as the [[StanfordUniversity|Stanford]] researcher whose late-1990s dog work helped connect [[NarcolepsyWakeSleepBoundary|narcolepsy]] to orexin biology. The episode says he studied narcolepsy in breeds such as Dobermans and Labradors and linked the condition to a mutation that made the dogs fail to respond to orexin.

The source uses Mignot as the discovery bridge between observable sleep-wake disruption and the later drug-development story around [[OrexinAgonists]]. Human trials are described as supporting the idea that narcolepsy patients lack enough orexin molecules in the brain.
