# 伍子胥 / Wu Zixu

伍子胥 / Wu Zixu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-80-zhugeliang-de-ouxiang-daodi-you-duo-lihai-lse6qmqqhoyqdzqjl4qfuvioeiw0]] as the historical warning [[LeYi|乐毅]] uses in [[BaoYanHuiwangShu|《报燕惠王书》]]. The episode says Wu Zixu helped King Helu of Wu build major achievements, but later [[Fuchai|吴王夫差]] ignored his advice, gave him a sword, forced him to die, and threw his body into the river.

The source does not develop Wu Zixu as a full biography. His role is comparative: he shows why a minister cannot assume that service under one ruler remains safe under a successor. For Le Yi, the lesson is that honoring [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]] does not require returning to [[YanHuiwang|燕惠王]] after suspicion and removal have already made the court dangerous.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-1-chaijie-jingke-ciqin-bi-bai-de-mimi-lnonhw9tlsu5dudu8750zvgeaz1p]] adds Wu Zixu to the [[BaiGongSheng|白公胜]] comparison chain. The source says Wu Zixu carries [[TaiziJianChu|太子建]]'s orphan through exile into Wu and later avenges his father and brother through the Wu attack on Chu, before dying under [[Fuchai|吴王夫差]]. Here he is background for the episode's argument that revenge, loyalty, and courage need [[RenyiGroundedVirtue|仁义为本的信勇]] rather than becoming self-justifying.
