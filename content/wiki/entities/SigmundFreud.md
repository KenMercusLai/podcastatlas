# Sigmund Freud / 西格蒙德·弗洛伊德

[[SigmundFreud|Sigmund Freud / 西格蒙德·弗洛伊德]] appears in [[29-qianmian-yingxiong-edipusi-zhengzai-xiaban-ji-ditie-590112891]] as one of the psychoanalytic backgrounds for [[JosephCampbell|Joseph Campbell / 约瑟夫·坎贝尔]]'s myth reading. The episode links Campbell's treatment of dreams, desire, childhood, sexuality, Oedipal material, and symbolic journeys to Freudian interpretation, especially when it discusses [[Oedipus|俄狄浦斯]] and Hamlet-like desire problems.

The source does not turn Freud into settled scientific authority. It treats Freudian reading as one layer of [[MythAsPublicDream|myth as public dream]]: useful for noticing why stories of mothers, fathers, taboo, desire, and death feel charged, but risky when psychological resonance is promoted into proof.
