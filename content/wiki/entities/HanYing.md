# 韩婴 / Han Ying

韩婴 / Han Ying enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-60-zhongguo-di-yi-wei-bachi-chaozheng-de-taihou-jingshi-ta-ltazis9qo0dwvwmzxikk8mmpvosc]] as the [[HanState|韩国]] crown prince who meets [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] and [[WeiXiangwang|魏襄王]] at [[LinjinWarringStates|临晋]] in 302 BCE. The episode then has Han Ying visit [[Xianyang|咸阳]] before returning home.

His source role is diplomatic rather than biographical. The meeting and Xianyang visit mark Qin's effort to repair relations with Han and Wei after the [[MiHeng|芈横]] hostage incident breaks Qin's [[ChuState|楚国]] alignment and makes a future attack on Chu more likely.
