## Overview

诸葛丰 / Zhuge Feng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-478-chushi-zhihui-zhezhong-ren-tai-jiduan-yudao-qing-yuanli-lq3hhg58lohdi-tbpmcuhwajjo7h|Hanji 478]] as a Western Han official whose reversal from praising [[ZhouKanWesternHan|周堪]] and [[ZhangMengWesternHan|张萌]] to denouncing them becomes a case of unreliable political speech under [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Zhuge Feng as a once-forceful censorial figure whose conduct becomes politically suspect after demotion. He had served as Sili Xiaowei, offended imperial relatives, and was reduced to Chengmen Xiaowei. When he later submits a memorial attacking Zhou Kan and Zhang Meng, Yuan treats the reversal itself as evidence that Zhuge Feng is not acting from stable public principle.

Zhuge Feng's importance in the source is diagnostic. His speech is not evaluated only for content but for trajectory: a person who once praised officials and later destroys their reputation with hard-to-verify language may be pursuing factional access rather than truth.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official formerly serving as Sili Xiaowei, later demoted to Chengmen Xiaowei.
- Known in the episode for forceful conduct that offends imperial relatives.
- Previously praises Zhou Kan and Zhang Meng before later denouncing them.
- Accuses with claims the episode describes as difficult to verify.
- Used by Sima Guang and the host as an example of speech that may serve factional advancement rather than public correction.
