## Overview

安众侯刘崇 / Liu Chong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-629-liujiaren-shui-kangqi-fankang-wangmang-de-daqi-lmhws7-jptdh9tf45bqtlfvzdw55|Hanji 629]] as the Liu royal relative whom the episode presents as the first person to raise an armed banner against [[WangMang|王莽]] during the 6 CE regency.

## Current Profile

Liu Chong is important less for battlefield effect than for political signal. With [[ZhangShaoFengguoChancellor|张绍]], he concludes that Wang Mang's conduct will endanger the Liu house and that someone from the imperial clan must lead before wider opposition can gather.

The actual action is militarily fragile. Liu Chong and Zhang Shao bring only about one hundred followers against Wan in Nanyang, fail to enter the city, and are captured quickly. The source still treats the event as the symbolic first shot of anti-Wang Mang resistance, because it shows a Liu-family actor choosing active opposition while others remain afraid or compliant.

## Key Characteristics

- Liu royal relative and Anzhong marquis positioned against Wang Mang's regency.
- Acts from a dynastic-protection judgment that Wang Mang threatens the Liu house.
- Expects a Liu-led action to unlock wider anti-Wang Mang support.
- Commands only a small and poorly prepared following.
- Fails immediately at Wan and is captured.
- Becomes a symbolic first-resistance figure rather than a successful rebel commander.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 629. It does not reconstruct Liu Chong's full genealogy, title history, sentence after capture, or the detailed composition of his group. The page keeps him distinct from [[LiuChongChenKing|刘宠 / Chen king]] and [[LiuChongLateHanMinister|刘宠 / late Han minister]].
