# 大小电波

## Overview
大小电波 is the podcast show hosting the Yunnan coffee conversation in [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]].

## Current Profile
The show appears here as a coffee-focused interview format. It uses [[YuWenjie|余文杰]]'s sourcing work to move from consumer impressions of [[Coffee|coffee]] into producing-region realities in [[Yunnan|云南]], especially [[Mangshi|芒市]] and [[Dehong|德宏]].

## Key Characteristics
- Hosts a coffee-mainline episode rather than a generic travel or lifestyle discussion.
- Uses a practitioner interview to connect sensory judgment with agriculture, trade, and regional development.
- Frames [[YunnanCoffeeOriginDevelopment]] as a topic for listeners interested in Chinese coffee identity.
