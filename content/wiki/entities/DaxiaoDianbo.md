# 大小电波

## Overview
大小电波 is a Chinese podcast represented in the wiki by practitioner interviews, consumer education, beverage-business history, and place-based food culture spanning coffee origins, drink design, purchasing, company strategy, and Tianjin chocolate.

## Current Profile
The available episodes show a program that moves between field expertise, business history, local memory, and accessible explanation. [[YuWenjie|余文杰]] connects tasting to Yunnan varieties, processing, sourcing, and farmer income; [[LiuZhanhong|柳展鸿 Andy]] connects competition creativity to multisensory and commercially feasible drink design; the Wahaha history connects remembered products to channels, litigation, diversification, and succession; and [[JenniferCoffee|珍妮花]] turns coffee operations into practical advice on freshness, cafes, and home equipment.

The Tianjin episode broadens “风味吃喝” from company history into urban food history. It begins with viral chocolate snacks, reconstructs childhood fillings and legacy brands, links them cautiously to port-city exchange, and then asks why broad flavor familiarity has not yet become [[BeanToBarConsumerLiteracy|Bean-to-Bar literacy]]. This adds a recurring editorial move: use everyday taste memory as an entry point, then separate strong lived evidence from historical mechanisms and market forecasts that remain uncertain.

## Key Characteristics
- Centers coffee, beverages, and adjacent consumer-business questions.
- Uses practitioners to connect sensory experience with agriculture, product development, and operations.
- Supports both long-form business history and compact consumer-education formats.
- Moves from specialist vocabulary toward decisions listeners can apply in buying or tasting.
- Preserves operational constraints such as repeatability, supply, maintenance, channel structure, and farmer income.
- Uses regional food memory to examine how imported products become ordinary local culture.

- Origin and sourcing practice: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] uses Yu Wenjie's fieldwork to connect cupping with variety, microclimate, processing, and producer economics.
- Creative beverage practice: [[kafei-shijian-he-zhongguo-shouwei-shijie-kafei-yu-liejiu-dasai-guanjun-andy-liaoliao-chuangyi-tediao-gkwrijiohqdbargqkqtalwpn]] turns a championship drink into a discussion of theme, flavor balance, multisensory service, feasibility, and team infrastructure.
- Beverage-business history: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] moves from Wahaha product memories to distribution, the Danone conflict, diversification, ecommerce delay, and succession.
- Consumer education: [[kafei-xiaoketang-kouliangdou-zenme-mai-zui-huasuan-yifen-gei-putongren-de-kafei-bikeng-zhinan-gkwrijiodf2uarzatatuffjr]] launches “咖啡小课堂” with practical guidance on product freshness, retail observation, and equipment fit.
- Place-based food culture: [[fengwei-chihe-tianjin-weishenme-shi-qiaokeli-tongcheng-gkwriueoo7cnayah8atndka]] moves from Tianjin chocolate-filled snacks and legacy memory into regional localization and craft-category education.

## Qualifications
The current evidence covers five episodes and does not establish the show's ownership, full host roster, complete catalog, audience, or release pattern. Competition, business-history, urban-history, product, price, and brand details remain participant- or episode-reported unless supported elsewhere.

- Extended the show's represented consumer education from coffee purchasing into Bean-to-Bar category literacy.
