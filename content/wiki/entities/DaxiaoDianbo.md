# 大小电波

## Overview
大小电波 is a Chinese podcast show represented in the wiki by episodes on Yunnan coffee sourcing and Wahaha business history.

## Current Profile
The show can host practitioner and business-history formats. The Yunnan coffee episode uses [[YuWenjie|余文杰]]'s sourcing work to move from consumer impressions of [[Coffee|coffee]] into producing-region realities in [[Yunnan|云南]]. The Wahaha episode opens a "中国饮料风云录" series by turning [[WahahaGroup|娃哈哈]] and [[ZongQinghou|宗庆后]] into a long-form case on founder judgment, consumer products, channels, and succession.

## Key Characteristics
- Hosts coffee, food-and-drink, and consumer-business episodes rather than only generic lifestyle discussion.
- Uses a practitioner interview to connect sensory judgment with agriculture, trade, and regional development.
- Uses business history to connect product memories with company strategy, channels, and governance.
- Frames Chinese beverage and coffee topics through both nostalgia and operating detail.
