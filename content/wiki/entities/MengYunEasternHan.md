## Overview

孟云 / Meng Yun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-755-zhege-chaodai-huangshang-jiu-kaishi-guli-shengwa-le-lj7bdkxkdtjhpo469yxhskjyfdco|Hanji 755]] as the Wuwei administrator who frames a [[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]] raid on [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]] as a Han credibility problem. His memorial supplies the policy setup that Hanji 756 resolves through compensated restitution.

## Current Profile

The source places Meng Yun at the frontier policy hinge. Southern Xiongnu forces defeat a Northern Xiongnu group at Zhuoye Mountain and seize people and livestock. Meng Yun warns the court that Northern Xiongnu had already reached a reconciled posture with Han, so letting Southern Xiongnu retain the gains could make Northern Xiongnu think Han had deceived them.

His recommendation is restitution: Southern Xiongnu should return the seized captives and livestock to quiet Northern Xiongnu suspicion. The page therefore treats Meng Yun as a one-source frontier credibility adviser, not as a general biography.

## Key Characteristics

- Wuwei administrator in the 85 CE Xiongnu restitution dispute.
- Frontier adviser who reads allied success as a possible credibility liability for Han.
- Proponent of returning Southern Xiongnu gains to prevent Northern Xiongnu retaliation.
- Setup figure for Hanji 756's strategic forbearance decision.
