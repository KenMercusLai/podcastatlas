# Pizza Hut China / 必胜客中国

Pizza Hut China / 必胜客中国 appears in [[bali-shui-he-shengpeilu-hai-neng-zhuanqian-quechao-weihe-yao-boli-shui-yewu-1005452453]] as a [[YumChina|百胜中国]] brand-ownership and margin case. The source says Yum China completed the purchase of Pizza Hut brand ownership in mainland China on August 7, removing the need to pay ongoing franchise fees to Yum Brands in the United States.

The episode treats the move as store-economics improvement rather than only legal housekeeping. If roughly 3% of sales previously went to brand fees, recapturing that expense can improve margin and make new-store economics more attractive for franchisees.

[[145-gaijia-zhongzi-de-canyin-yangpinpai-feng-tou-quan-1-145-1]] adds the valuation comparison. It says Yum China bought Pizza Hut China brand ownership from [[YumBrands]] for 1.2 billion USD, while Pizza Hut outside China sold for 1.5 billion USD. The source uses that comparison to argue that the China region's local operation and scale gave it unusually high weight in the global brand.

[[zhongguo-yinglai-hanbao-kaidianchao-guochan-huangningmeng-jiage-huiluo-1006920543]] adds a concrete burger-rollout target. The episode says Pizza Hut China had added burgers in more than 200 stores through a store-within-store format and planned to reach 500-600 stores by year-end, about 10% of its China network.
