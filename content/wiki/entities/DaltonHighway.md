# Dalton Highway / 道尔顿公路

The Dalton Highway / 道尔顿公路 is the [[Alaska]] road at the center of [[84-binglu-kuanghua-hao-guniang-ni-zhishi-wangle-yonggan-699443676]]. In the episode, [[JoyWiebe|Joy "Joey" Wiebe / Joey]] drives this route toward [[PrudhoeBay|Prudhoe Bay]], and [[AmyButcher|Amy Butcher / 艾米]] experiences it as both a literal dangerous road and a symbolic route out of a life organized by fear.

The road's practical danger matters. The episode emphasizes extreme weather, steep passes, landslides, isolation, limited visibility, death markers, and the need for supplies, radio communication, and judgment under pressure. That makes the highway a concrete case for [[ExtremeEnvironmentRiskManagement]].

The road is also a labor community. Meal-ticket walls, memorial plaques, rescues, and Joey's habit of carrying illegal extra blankets and food show that survival depends on [[HighRiskLaborMutualAid]], not only on individual toughness.
