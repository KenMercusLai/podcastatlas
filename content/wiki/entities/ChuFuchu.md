# 熊负刍 / 楚王负刍 / Xiong Fuchu

熊负刍 / 楚王负刍 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-1-jintian-liaoliao-yingzheng-dou-you-naxie-canren-shouduan-lmqky6g3qi9nklpdnye4lk5rrbz6]] as the older half-brother who replaces [[ChuAiwang|熊昊 / 熊犹 / 楚哀王]] after [[ChuYouwang|楚幽王]] dies. The episode says Fuchu's faction launches the coup before the newly installed ruler can stabilize power.

The source does not yet develop Fuchu's reign. His wiki role here is the coup endpoint of a late Chu succession chain: after the [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] and [[LiYuanSister|李源妹妹]] branch already compromised the throne's legitimacy, another royal relative can use factional violence to redirect the line.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-118-1-wangjian-lv-liushiwan-dajun-duizhan-xiangyan-haoxi-kaichang-le-lsf2puinwg0m3bgyi25znof7aznu]] gives Fuchu's endpoint in the conquest sequence. After [[WangJianQin|王翦]] and [[MengWu|蒙武]] defeat [[XiangYan|项燕]]'s main force, the source says they capture Fuchu in 223 BCE while [[ChangpingJun|昌平君]] dies, making Fuchu the captured ruler at Chu's terminal defeat.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-118-2-chi-gua-le-wangshi-de-gua-bao-tian-lns7jt-kbv3qunm66hzltdgugbco|Qinji 118-2]] adds a genealogy role. The host cites traditions that make Fuchu another son of [[MiWan|楚考烈王]], using him alongside Xiong Qi / [[ChangpingJun|昌平君]] to challenge the claim that Mi Wan had no sons. The same episode narrates Wang Jian's sequence as Chenying first, then [[ShouchunWarringStates|寿春]] and Fuchu's capture, before the last Changping Jun and Xiang Yan resistance is crushed.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-120-1-shui-shi-huangdi-zhong-de-mabaonan-lhw8vsj6ta6rjdkugcvfohaqqxc0]] reuses Chu's final defeat in Qin's post-unification narrative. Qin's story presents Chu as breaking covenant and attacking [[NanCommandery|南郡]], so the campaign against Chu can be framed as response rather than expansion. This page records the claim as [[ConquestSelfDefenseNarrative|兼并自卫叙事]], separate from the campaign and genealogy notes above.
