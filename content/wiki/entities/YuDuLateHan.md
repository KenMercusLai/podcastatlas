# 于毒 / Yu Du (Late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] adds Yu Du to the earlier [[DongCommandery|东郡]] attack before Hanji 961's [[DongWuyangLateHan|东武阳]] branch. The source names him with [[BaiRaoLateHan|白绕]] and [[SuiGuLateHan|眭固]] as forces [[WangGongLateHan|王肱]] cannot resist, creating the opening for [[CaoCao|曹操]] to enter Dong Commandery.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-961-neihuang-zhizhan-caocao-chulu-fengmang-ljdlt6kiu8tg0qws-jlyej-yhgxr|Hanji 961]] normalizes this page's display name to 于毒 / Yu Du while preserving the existing stable route. In this earlier episode, Yu Du attacks [[DongWuyangLateHan|东武阳]] while [[CaoCao|曹操]] is stationed at [[DunqiuLateHan|顿丘]], but Cao Cao avoids direct relief and marches toward Yu Du's base instead.

The result is the first visible Yu Du branch in the wiki's chronology. Yu Du abandons the Dong Wuyang siege to return, after which Cao Cao advances to [[NeihuangBattleLateHan|内黄之战]] and defeats [[SuiGuLateHan|眭固]] and [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]]. The source uses Yu Du as the pressure point that lets Cao Cao demonstrate [[EncirclingWeiToRescueZhao|围魏救赵]] logic.

Yu Du enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-970-yuanshao-ansha-lvbu-weihe-hui-shibai-ln2pnvit-qgpnhn-pne7o-p1irrs|Hanji 970]] under the earlier transcript label "余毒" as the [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]] figure who connects with the [[WeiCommanderyLateHan|魏郡]] soldier revolt and attacks [[YeLateHan|邺城]], then becomes [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s first major retaliation target.

The source says Yuan Shao attacks Yu Du around Zhaoge's Luchang Mountain, fights for five days, breaks his position, and kills Yu Du along with many followers. Yu Du's page therefore marks the transition from Yuan Shao's exposed rear crisis to a punitive mountain campaign.
