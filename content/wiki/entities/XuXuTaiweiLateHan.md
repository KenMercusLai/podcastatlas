# 许訏 / Xu Xu (late-Han taiwei)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-922-ruhe-zai-fanchen-zhong-xiulian-qingshang-lsxiqneyyeod6aeeqxuhs517pn1x|Hanji 922]] presents Xu Xu as the taiwei assigned with [[ZhangJiSikongLateHan|张济]] to answer [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s 182 CE order to identify officials harming the people. The source says both are close to powerful eunuchs and accept benefits, so the review protects eunuch-linked corrupt officials while falsely reporting twenty-six clean administrators from remote areas.

Xu Xu matters as an early late-Han [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]] case. The ruler asks for information after hearing popular criticism, but the officials who filter the answer turn the inquiry into private-party protection. [[ChenDanLateHan|陈耽]] later exposes the inversion, and the wronged officials are promoted, but Xu Xu's consequence is only a scolding in the episode.
