# Dan Wang

[[150-zhongguo-ruhe-tiaochu-pinkun-xianjing-xiandaihua-zhi-lu-you-hui-zouxiang-hefang-lgpvp04rngagkpubte2ac6i5ji3g]] extends Dan Wang's wiki role from a Planet Money guest to one of three book authors in a Chinese-modernization synthesis. The episode uses [[Breakneck]] to contrast [[EngineeringState]] and [[LawyerState]], with [[ProcessKnowledge]], [[Shenzhen]], and high-speed-rail comparison making the frame less about speed alone and more about retained practice communities.

Dan Wang enters the wiki through [[building-things-and-breaking-things-in-china-summer-school-world-tour]] as the [[PlanetMoney]] Summer School guest professor. The episode identifies him as a fellow at the [[HooverInstitution]] and author of [[Breakneck]], using his [[EngineeringState]] concept to connect Chinese infrastructure, real estate, youth pressure, fertility policy, and welfare design.

His role in the source is analytic rather than biographical. Wang argues that [[China]] has unusual state capacity to build physical systems, but that the same literal-minded build culture can produce [[InfrastructureMalinvestment]], coercive social planning, and weak attention to services that do not look like concrete or steel. His U.S. advice is [[BuildBuildRegulate]], a call to expand building capacity while preserving corrective feedback.
