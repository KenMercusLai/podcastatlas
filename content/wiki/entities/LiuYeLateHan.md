# 刘晔 / Liu Ye (late Han)

## Overview
刘晔 / Liu Ye is the Lujiang-side adviser whose warning in Hanji 1038 identifies the real danger in [[SunCe|孙策]]'s invitation to attack Shangliao.

## Current Profile
The current source presents Liu Ye as the accurate but ignored strategist around [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]]. He sees that Shangliao will be hard to take quickly, that Liu Xun's main force leaving [[LujiangCommandery|庐江郡]] will expose the rear, and that Sun Ce's gifts and deferential language should be read as bait rather than support.

## Key Characteristics
- Reads Sun Ce's apparent deference as a strategic lure rather than a sincere subordinate request.
- Identifies Shangliao as a fortified target that can consume Liu Xun's campaign time without delivering the promised grain.
- Connects the outward expedition to rear-base vulnerability in Lujiang.
- Fails institutionally because Liu Xun does not accept the warning.

## Evidence
- Strategic diagnosis: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1038-sanshiliu-ji-zhi-diaohu-lishan-sunce-zhiqu-wancheng-lhomk8hfdidl61degwbqtj2xkj9z|Hanji 1038]] has Liu Ye warn that Shangliao is hard to capture and that Sun Ce may attack Liu Xun's rear once the main army leaves.
- Deception recognition: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1038-sanshiliu-ji-zhi-diaohu-lishan-sunce-zhiqu-wancheng-lhomk8hfdidl61degwbqtj2xkj9z|Hanji 1038]] makes Liu Ye the figure who sees through the gift-and-humility performance at the center of the [[LureEnemyOutOfPosition|调虎离山]] operation.
- Advisory failure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1038-sanshiliu-ji-zhi-diaohu-lishan-sunce-zhiqu-wancheng-lhomk8hfdidl61degwbqtj2xkj9z|Hanji 1038]] shows Liu Xun ignoring Liu Ye, reaching Haihun, and then losing Wan City after Shangliao is already emptied.
