# LOFTER

LOFTER appears in [[no-214-xunzhao-tonglei-xiaohongshu-bilibili-yiji-wuhuabamen-de-naxie-shequ-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-26-1005977305]] as [[NetEase]]'s Chinese Tumblr-like light-blog product that later became important for anime, fan fiction, and fan creation. The source says China's broader light-blog wave did not become a mainstream category, while LOFTER survived by leaning into fan and subculture use.

The episode uses LOFTER to show how tags, CP/ship communities, works, and shared interests help fans find one another. It also notes that fan-culture reporting and takedown pressure around the 2020 Xiao Zhan/AO3 incident affected LOFTER, making the platform a [[PlatformCommunityGovernance]] case.
