# 黄祖 / Huang Zu

## Overview
黄祖 / Huang Zu is [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s Jiangxia commander whose local coercive and naval capacity repeatedly checks Sun-family expansion before his 208 CE defeat removes Jingzhou's eastern river shield.

## Current Profile
The evidence presents Huang Zu as a regional military instrument with real blocking power but limited independent strategic initiative. He is the endpoint of the [[BorrowedKnifeKilling|借刀杀人]] chain against [[MiHengLateHan|祢衡]], supplies Liu Biao's naval response to southern Jingzhou pressure, aids [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]], and suffers major losses to [[SunCe|孙策]].

[[SunQuan|孙权]]'s first recorded westward attack defeats Huang Zu on the water but cannot take the city before [[Shanyue|山越]] rebellion forces withdrawal. Hanji 1085 completes that pressure arc in 208 CE. Huang Zu turns [[MianKouLateHan|沔口]] into a fixed river barrier with anchored warships and archers, but [[DongXiLateHan|董袭]] and [[LingTong|凌统]] break the obstruction, [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] kills naval commander [[ChenJiuLateHan|陈就]], and Wu takes Xiakou and kills Huang Zu. Hanji 1091 then records the political consequence: the vacant [[JiangxiaCommanderyLateHan|江夏郡]] becomes [[LiuQiLateHan|刘琦]]'s route out of the succession center.

## Key Characteristics
- Completes the delegated-violence chain against Mi Heng after Liu Biao transfers the disruptive scholar to Jiangxia.
- Serves as Liu Biao's naval and eastern-commandery instrument on the Yangtze line.
- Provides aid to Liu Xun but fails to reverse Sun Ce's advance and loses people, ships, and family members.
- Survives Sun Quan's earlier city assault because Shanyue rebellion disrupts Wu's rear.
- Defends Mian Kou through a fixed warship-and-archery barrier in the 208 CE campaign.
- Loses his naval response and Xiakou after Wu breaches the barrier, and is killed during the collapse.
- Leaves a command vacancy that Liu Qi turns into an external survival base.

## Evidence
- Delegated killing: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1011-miheng-zhisi-zuiju-beiju-secai-de-jiedao-sharen-ltrzpchwdfcyp4ntmum9giu-5mva|Hanji 1011]] has Liu Biao send Mi Heng onward to Huang Zu, whose anger supplies the final act of violence.
- Liu Biao military instrument: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1027-jingzhou-zhizhu-liubiao-weihe-nan-cheng-bazhu-ll-ebguqllsf-5ycvpdogihzn3lj|Hanji 1027]] places Huang Zu's navy in Liu Biao's attempt to block the Yangtze after southern commanderies lean toward Cao Cao.
- Aid and earlier Sun-family defeat: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1038-sanshiliu-ji-zhi-diaohu-lishan-sunce-zhiqu-wancheng-lhomk8hfdidl61degwbqtj2xkj9z|Hanji 1038]] shows Huang She aiding Liu Xun, then follows Sun Ce's victory with a further attack that takes Huang Zu's ships and family members.
- Interrupted Sun Quan attack: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1061-yuanshao-zhizi-he-shafu-chouren-jiecheng-qinjia-lnsnj6ipiyscw-vopnjjgoxpnt5r|Hanji 1061]] says Sun Quan wins on the water but withdraws from the city assault because Shanyue rebellion breaks out behind him.
- Final river defense and death: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1085-dongwu-naxia-jiangxia-dengyu-wan-le-liubiao-de-xin-lrnhjns-qu0d6tsit3j_re96zk2u|Hanji 1085]] describes the Mian Kou barrier, its breach, Chen Jiu's death, Xiakou's fall, and Huang Zu's killing.
- Succession consequence: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1091-zhugeliang-dianhua-liuqi-beihou-ancang-shenme-zhanlue-fubi-lj2tfbi8qd3dxta8ehh8a0kt7qpq|Hanji 1091]] says Huang Zu's death creates the Jiangxia vacancy sought by Liu Qi.

## Qualifications
Huang Zu appears mainly through hostile or opponent-centered popular-history narratives. Exact force and captive totals, warship construction, the scale of slaughter, the claim that he was Liu Biao's only independently capable external commander, Sun-family revenge motives, and the degree to which Jiangxia contained a mature shipyard and naval-training system remain source-scoped. Hanji 1061 should not be read as proof of Huang Zu's battlefield superiority, and Hanji 1085's defeat should not erase the earlier defensive capacity that delayed Wu.

## What Changed
- Added the 208 CE Mian Kou barrier, Wu breach, Chen Jiu's death, and Huang Zu's final defeat.
- Reframed the earlier interrupted attack and final campaign as one sustained Sun-family pressure arc.
- Connected Huang Zu's death to both Jiangxia's military-economic transfer and Liu Qi's later command vacancy.

- [[MiHengLateHan|祢衡]] - transferred guest whose killing makes Huang Zu the endpoint of delegated violence.
- [[SunCe|孙策]] - opponent who defeats Huang Zu after the Lujiang campaign.
- [[SunQuan|孙权]] - later Sun-family opponent whose first assault is interrupted and whose 208 campaign kills Huang Zu.
- [[MianKouLateHan|沔口]] - river chokepoint used for Huang Zu's final fixed defense.
- [[ChenJiuLateHan|陈就]] - naval commander killed after the barrier is breached.
- [[JiangxiaCommanderyLateHan|江夏郡]] - command and resource base lost after Huang Zu's death.
