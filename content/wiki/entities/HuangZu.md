# 黄祖 / Huang Zu

黄祖 / Huang Zu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1011-miheng-zhisi-zuiju-beiju-secai-de-jiedao-sharen-ltrzpchwdfcyp4ntmum9giu-5mva|Hanji 1011]] as the Jiangxia official to whom [[LiuBiao|刘表]] sends [[MiHengLateHan|祢衡]]. The transcript renders him as "黄族"; the wiki normalizes the late-Han figure to Huang Zu.

The source makes Huang Zu the final actor in a delegated-killing chain. He initially treats Mi Heng well because the guest comes through Liu Biao's recommendation, but his direct and fiery temperament makes him the person most likely to answer Mi Heng's public insult with violence. When he kills Mi Heng, the higher-status actors have already protected their distance from the act.

In this reading, Huang Zu is less the mastermind than the endpoint of [[BorrowedKnifeKilling|借刀杀人]]. [[CaoCao|曹操]] and Liu Biao preserve reputation and plausible distance, while Huang Zu supplies the immediate anger and local authority that complete the removal.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1027-jingzhou-zhizhu-liubiao-weihe-nan-cheng-bazhu-ll-ebguqllsf-5ycvpdogihzn3lj|Hanji 1027]] adds Huang Zu's military role inside Liu Biao's response to southern pressure. After [[ZhangXianLateHan|张献]] and [[HuanJieLateHan|桓阶]] attach [[SouthernJingzhouLateHan|southern Jingzhou]] commanderies to Cao Cao, Liu Biao uses Huang Zu's navy to block the Yangtze line. The episode frames this as a costly reaction after [[FenceSittingStrategicExposure|骑墙式战略暴露]] has already reduced Liu Biao's initiative.
