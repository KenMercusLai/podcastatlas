# Democratic-Republican Party

## Overview
The Democratic-Republican Party appears as [[ThomasJefferson|Thomas Jefferson]]'s political vehicle in the [[FirstPartySystem|first party system]], opposed to [[AlexanderHamilton|Alexander Hamilton]] and the [[FederalistParty|Federalists]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents the Democratic-Republicans through Jefferson's public role and James Madison's organizing work. Jefferson stands as the enlightened philosopher above the fray, while Madison is credited as the practical party organizer. The party's conflict with the Federalists over banks, debt, Britain, France, farmers, federal power, and tyranny structures the elections of 1796 and 1800.

## Key Characteristics
- Jefferson-led founding-era party faction.
- Organized practically by James Madison in the episode's account.
- Opposed Hamiltonian and Federalist arguments for stronger federal power and British-leaning policy.
- Associated with suspicion of banks, debt, centralized power, and tyranny.
- Winner of the 1800 electoral crisis that produced a peaceful transfer of power.
