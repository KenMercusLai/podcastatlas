# Haier Group / 海尔

Haier Group appears in [[79-gewei-lingdao-danfan-zan-xuedian-boyilun-690004262]] through its [[RenDanHeYi|人单合一]] management model. The episode says the model attracted attention and even entered Harvard Business School classroom discussion, but it uses [[ContractTheory]] to question whether turning internal colleagues into customer-like market actors should be generalized.

The source treats Haier as a boundary case for management experimentation. Market mechanisms can sometimes activate initiative, but firms exist partly because hierarchical coordination can reduce transaction costs where contracts are incomplete, knowledge is proprietary, or repeated negotiation is too expensive.
