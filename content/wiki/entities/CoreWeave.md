# CoreWeave

## Overview
CoreWeave is a specialized AI cloud and neocloud provider that turns GPU fleets, data-center capacity, operating software, and long-term customer contracts into training and inference infrastructure.

## Current Profile
Across the sources, CoreWeave is both an operating-cloud company and a financing test case. It developed from GPU-based crypto mining into rendering, scientific compute, and neural-network workloads, then specialized in integrating and operating Nvidia clusters for model companies and hyperscalers. The newest source adds the company's own account of a contract-backed financing box: customer payments support data-center, power, interest, and principal obligations before residual cash returns to CoreWeave.

That structure can make scarce GPU capacity financeable, but it does not remove dependency. CoreWeave remains exposed to Nvidia supply and support, concentrated counterparties, power and component bottlenecks, compute rental prices, GPU useful life, refinancing, and whether demand remains independent after related ecosystem commitments.

## Key Characteristics
- Specialized AI cloud built around purpose-operated GPU clusters rather than generic cloud breadth.
- Originated in crypto mining and reused GPU-operating knowledge across rendering, research, training, and inference.
- Uses long customer contracts, equipment, and data-center obligations as a project-like financing package.
- Depends strategically on Nvidia supply, ecosystem support, and successive accelerator ramps.
- Fleet economics depend on utilization, contract durability, power availability, rental pricing, and residual value across GPU generations.
- Inference is treated as the recurring monetization layer that can extend demand beyond one-time training runs.

## Qualifications
CoreWeave's account is a CEO presentation, and its reported contract duration, depreciation, financing, capital-raising, debt-paydown, cost-of-capital, demand, and allocation claims are not independently audited here. Continuing A100 demand argues against universal near-term obsolescence, but it does not prove every fleet will remain profitable for six years. Nvidia support does not prove artificial demand, yet it makes customer independence, utilization, residual value, and refinancing especially important.
