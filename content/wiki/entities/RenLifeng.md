# 任立峰 / 卷卷

## Overview

任立峰 / 卷卷 is the Shumei Wanwu founder and CEO interviewed in the source. The episode presents him as a former [[ByteDance]] product and operations executive who led early [[Douyin]] work, then moved into AI-generated 3D for physical manufacturing through [[ShumeiWanwu|树美万物]].

## Current Profile

Ren's profile is built around transfer and discontinuity. He reuses a Douyin-era habit of looking for large infrastructure and behavior changes, but the source stresses that manufacturing requires different learning: materials, equipment, process, suppliers, delivery, and company operations cannot be solved by internet-product iteration alone.

As a founder, he describes himself as feeling-driven but not intuition-only. Inspiration points to a direction, then he tries to verify whether the underlying variable is large enough, whether users have real demand, and whether the company can turn a product into repeatable physical outcomes.

## Key Characteristics

- Former ByteDance executive and early Douyin product leader.
- Founder/CEO trying to turn AI-generated 3D into manufacturable goods through Shumei Wanwu.
- Uses large-variable judgment to compare the mobile-short-video wave with the AI-plus-3D-plus-manufacturing wave.
- Treats entrepreneurship as a long-cycle operating role that requires health, emotional control, user feedback, fundraising, hiring, BD, and delivery accountability.
- Resists romanticizing entrepreneurship, but supports founders who still pursue incremental value after being challenged.
- Frames personal growth through humility, learning, and shortening the gap between high goals and current capability.
