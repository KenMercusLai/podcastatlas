# Federica Fornaciari

## Overview
Federica Fornaciari is presented in the bounded [[TomorrowToday]] interview as a consultant, agency executive, educator, entrepreneur, and investor whose work across countries and sectors informs her approach to leadership and transformation.

## Current Profile
The source connects Fornaciari's economics and marketing education, work at [[BainAndCompany]] and [[Havas]], teaching, board education, and international experience to a single operating thesis: strategic thought matters only when leaders accept responsibility for execution. She treats transformation as a willingness to question assumptions, align organizational layers, and change behavior, while retaining human judgment and accountability as AI accelerates analysis.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines economic, marketing, consulting, agency, teaching, and governance perspectives.
- Treats execution as an executive responsibility rather than a downstream implementation detail.
- Uses cross-cultural listening and local trust patterns to shape working relationships.
- Connects crisis communication, investor psychology, culture, and operations to enterprise outcomes.
- Frames AI as an amplifier whose value depends on human criteria, verification, ethics, and alignment.
- Defines success through character, service, teaching, and positive effects on others as well as professional achievement.
