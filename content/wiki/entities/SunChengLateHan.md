## Overview

孙程 / Sun Cheng is a Shun-era eunuch and restoration merit-holder. Hanji 805 supplies his accession role in restoring [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] and destroying the Yan outer-relative network; Hanji 806 then shows him as the palace-access ally who helps save [[YuXuEasternHan|虞诩]] from [[ZhangFangEunuchLateHan|张防]]'s retaliation before his own merit group becomes politically uncomfortable.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-805-tongtou-renxing-de-nvren-jing-neng-yuce-jixiong-ll3bzoyrkbtnvt-aqu9nqelqonew|Hanji 805]] supplies Sun Cheng's accession layer. The source says Sun Cheng and the core eunuch group restore the deposed Liu Bao as Emperor Shun, after which the Yan outer relatives are thoroughly destroyed. That makes Sun Cheng part of the restoration coalition whose victory creates the immediate need to manage [[EmpressDowagerYanLateHan|Yan Taihou]] and clean out hostile Yan-side networks.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-806-xiangyao-chengshi-zhaodui-ren-hen-guanjian-luswh-p-oyutysxue6shweqcwqxs|Hanji 806]] then supplies Sun Cheng's positive role in Yu Xu's case. Yu Xu's friends seek him out because he is close to the emperor and opposed to Zhang Fang. Sun Cheng confronts Shun with the inconsistency between the accession struggle against bad ministers and the new court's tolerance of Zhang Fang, directly names Yu Xu as a loyal official, rebukes Zhang Fang in court, and asks for Zhang Fang's arrest.

The same source then makes Sun Cheng's position unstable. Later in the year, Sun Cheng and other marquises bring memorials to court to press their merit claims, angering Shun and giving officials a chance to accuse them of interfering with government, disobeying orders, and factional arrogance. Shun removes them from office, changes their marquisates to remote territories, and orders them out of Luoyang. Zhou Ju's pressure on Zhu Chang wins a partial repair, and Shun later restores Sun Cheng's original title and estate after Sun Cheng returns in resentment, but still sends him back to his fief.

## Key Characteristics

- Accession-restoration actor in the eunuch group that brings Shun back to the throne.
- Palace-access ally who can move a blocked accusation back before the emperor.
- Eunuch-side actor opposed to Zhang Fang rather than a simple member of one undifferentiated eunuch bloc.
- Direct remonstrant who frames Yu Xu's case as a contradiction in Shun's own accession legitimacy.
- Restoration merit-holder whose service to Shun becomes dangerous once expressed as public credit.
- Politically contained figure: Shun restores title and rewards but keeps him away from Luoyang.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 805 and Hanji 806. Hanji 805 establishes Sun Cheng's restoration role but does not reconstruct the full coup operation or all nineteen core eunuchs. Hanji 806 establishes the Yu Xu rescue and merit-backlash layers but does not settle the exact legal basis for the later accusations. The source's evaluation of "飞鸟尽，良弓藏" remains an interpretive frame rather than proof of Shun's inner motive.
