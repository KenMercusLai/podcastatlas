## Overview

孙程 / Sun Cheng is a Shun-era eunuch and restoration merit-holder. Hanji 803 now supplies the 125 CE planning and execution layer: Sun Cheng reads [[LiuYiBeixiangHouLateHan|北乡侯刘裔]]'s illness as the opening to restore [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝 / Liu Bao]], leads the West Bell Tower oath group, kills Yan-side gate guards, coerces [[LiRunLateHan|李润]], and cuts [[EmpressDowagerYanLateHan|阎太后]] off from outside communication. Hanji 804 then supplies the post-victory reward-allocation layer, Hanji 805 supplies the broader restoration settlement, and Hanji 806 shows Sun Cheng as the palace-access ally who helps save [[YuXuEasternHan|虞诩]] from [[ZhangFangEunuchLateHan|张防]]'s retaliation before his own merit group becomes politically uncomfortable.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-803-han-shundi-ruhe-zai-xuefengxueyu-zhong-dengshang-huangwei-lqvno7t-gcgxtbs3phuflr4ybe2s|Hanji 803]] supplies Sun Cheng's operational accession layer. As Liu Yi lies gravely ill, Sun Cheng tells Changxing Xu that Liu Bao is the prior emperor's legitimate son and has no true offense, so a failed recovery should become the moment to eliminate Jiang Jin and [[YanXianLateHan|阎显]]. After Liu Yi dies and Yan Taihou conceals the death, Sun Cheng joins [[WangKangLateHan|Wang Kang]], [[WangGuoEunuchLateHan|Wang Guo]], Huang Long, Peng Kai, Meng Shu, Li Jian, and other eunuchs in an oath at the West Bell Tower.

The same source shows how Sun Cheng turns the plan into irreversible action. At Zhangtai Gate, Sun Cheng and Wang Kang kill Jiang Jin, Liu An, and Chen Da, then force Li Run to join. Sun Cheng brings Liu Bao to the West Bell, enthrones him, summons shangshu officials, keeps the forbidden gates, cuts off Yan Taihou's communication, and transmits the order that lets [[GuoZhenLateHan|郭镇]] arrest Yan Jin.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-804-gei-ni-tang-de-ren-bu-yiding-shi-guiren-lqongdqg-hgetoegpylv-os86buh|Hanji 804]] supplies the post-victory reward layer. After restoration, Sun Cheng receives the highest reward among the nineteen eunuchs and is later made qiduwei.

The same source makes Sun Cheng a manager of post-coup credit. He delegates the merit list to [[WangKangLateHan|Wang Kang]], whose quiet inclusion of [[MiaoGuangLateHan|Miao Guang]] exposes hidden hesitation once Miao Guang fails to receive a certificate and confesses. Sun Cheng does not punish Wang Kang or Miao Guang; the episode reads this as a calculated choice that wins Wang Kang, reassures other eunuchs, keeps Wang Kang as a balance against [[WangGuoEunuchLateHan|Wang Guo]], and leaves Sun Cheng with a handle.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-805-tongtou-renxing-de-nvren-jing-neng-yuce-jixiong-ll3bzoyrkbtnvt-aqu9nqelqonew|Hanji 805]] supplies the broader settlement layer. The source says Sun Cheng and the core eunuch group restore Shun, after which the Yan outer relatives are thoroughly destroyed. That makes Sun Cheng part of the restoration coalition whose victory creates the immediate need to manage [[EmpressDowagerYanLateHan|Yan Taihou]] and clean out hostile Yan-side networks.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-806-xiangyao-chengshi-zhaodui-ren-hen-guanjian-luswh-p-oyutysxue6shweqcwqxs|Hanji 806]] then supplies Sun Cheng's positive role in Yu Xu's case. Yu Xu's friends seek him out because he is close to the emperor and opposed to Zhang Fang. Sun Cheng confronts Shun with the inconsistency between the accession struggle against bad ministers and the new court's tolerance of Zhang Fang, directly names Yu Xu as a loyal official, rebukes Zhang Fang in court, and asks for Zhang Fang's arrest.

The same source then makes Sun Cheng's position unstable. Later in the year, Sun Cheng and other marquises bring memorials to court to press their merit claims, angering Shun and giving officials a chance to accuse them of interfering with government, disobeying orders, and factional arrogance. Shun removes them from office, changes their marquisates to remote territories, and orders them out of Luoyang. Zhou Ju's pressure on Zhu Chang wins a partial repair, and Shun later restores Sun Cheng's original title and estate after Sun Cheng returns in resentment, but still sends him back to his fief.

## Key Characteristics

- Accession-restoration planner who moves from Liu Yi's illness to a concrete anti-Yan coup plan.
- Gate-side executor who turns the West Bell Tower oath into Zhangtai Gate killing, Li Run's coerced alignment, and Liu Bao's enthronement.
- Top-ranked nineteen-eunuch merit-holder who manages reward-list exposure after victory.
- Palace-access ally who can move a blocked accusation back before the emperor.
- Eunuch-side actor opposed to Zhang Fang rather than a simple member of one undifferentiated eunuch bloc.
- Direct remonstrant who frames Yu Xu's case as a contradiction in Shun's own accession legitimacy.
- Restoration merit-holder whose service to Shun becomes dangerous once expressed as public credit and later requires political containment.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 803, Hanji 804, Hanji 805, and Hanji 806. Hanji 803 establishes the illness-triggered plan, oath, gate killing, Li Run coercion, palace-gate control, and anti-Yan enforcement layer. Hanji 804 establishes the reward and Wang Kang / Miao Guang list-management layer, but it does not settle the later biographies of all nineteen eunuchs. Hanji 805 establishes the broader restoration settlement, and Hanji 806 establishes the Yu Xu rescue and merit-backlash layers but does not settle the exact legal basis for the later accusations. The source's evaluation of "飞鸟尽，良弓藏" remains an interpretive frame rather than proof of Shun's inner motive.
