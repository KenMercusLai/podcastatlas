# 三岛由纪夫 / Yukio Mishima

三岛由纪夫 / Yukio Mishima appears in [[33-renjian-shige-danxiaogui-de-zhuanglie-xianji-599924624]] as a contrast figure for [[DazaiOsamu|太宰治]]. The episode presents the familiar opposition cautiously: Dazai is associated with self-exposure, fear, left-leaning youth politics, and failure, while Mishima is associated with right-leaning politics, masculine display, and more theatrical self-construction.

The source's sharper point is that the contrast should not become a clean strong-versus-weak binary. The hosts suggest that Mishima also had fear and performance inside him, but chose an extreme aesthetic and political language to cover or transform those elements. This makes him a source-scoped case for [[AuthorMythDeflation]] rather than a full Mishima study.

## Key Claims
- Mishima is used as a contrast device, not as the episode's main subject.
- The source resists treating masculine performance as the obvious opposite of cowardice.
- His comparison with Dazai helps define [[CowardlySacrificeWitness]]: confessing weakness may be harder than staging strength.
