# 鬼谷子 / Guiguzi

鬼谷子 / Guiguzi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]] as the teacher under whom [[SunBin|孙膑]] and [[PangJuan|庞涓]] study military methods. The source does not develop a broad biography; it uses Guiguzi to explain the shared training background that makes Pang Juan's later fear and betrayal more pointed.

The page is deliberately source-scoped. In this wiki branch, Guiguzi matters because his teaching setting links two divergent outcomes: Pang Juan converts talent anxiety into court violence, while Sun Bin later converts the same strategic learning into [[TianJiHorseRacing|resource reallocation]] and [[EncirclingWeiToRescueZhao|indirect military pressure]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-29-suqin-lizhi-shuangwen-zhishi-gaibian-mingyun-lrf5cqvoriyep2lkbozju-lyjaxv]] adds another student pair: [[SuQin|苏秦]] and [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]. The episode uses Guiguzi differently here. Su Qin's initial post-study failure weakens a simple "名师出高徒" story and supports [[FailureDrivenSelfStudy|失败后的自学翻身]]: the later breakthrough comes after Su Qin returns home humiliated and studies again.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-30-suqin-de-kaigua-rensheng-lkmn-fruukdxgkddx7zb4lc3o9bw]] links Guiguzi to technique rather than only teacher lineage. The episode notes that the received [[Guiguzi|鬼谷子]] text contains "揣" and "摩" chapters, using that vocabulary to explain [[SuQin|苏秦]]'s [[ChuaimoTechnique|揣摩]] after deep reading [[YinfuJing|《阴符》]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-31-hezong-lianheng-weiou-qin-lncpgmxrojc0y1meg9a3ul-o9nih]] keeps Guiguzi in the background by making Su Qin and [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] classmates whose shared training does not make them allies. The episode uses that relationship to stage [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] and [[LianhengStrategy|连横]] as rival but intertwined professional paths.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-56-qiaoqiao-liangqian-duo-nianqian-sha-shi-da-zhangfu-ltokbutqgsnrno-y29xmzwa3da41]] cites Guiguzi through [[YangXiong|扬雄]]'s [[Fayan|《法言》]] critique. The passage, as presented by the episode, says Zhang Yi and Su Qin learned from Guiguzi and used hezong/lianheng arts, but Yang Xiong denies that effectiveness or temporary order makes those arts morally acceptable.
