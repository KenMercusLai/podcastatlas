# 鬼谷子 / Guiguzi

鬼谷子 / Guiguzi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]] as the teacher under whom [[SunBin|孙膑]] and [[PangJuan|庞涓]] study military methods. The source does not develop a broad biography; it uses Guiguzi to explain the shared training background that makes Pang Juan's later fear and betrayal more pointed.

The page is deliberately source-scoped. In this wiki branch, Guiguzi matters because his teaching setting links two divergent outcomes: Pang Juan converts talent anxiety into court violence, while Sun Bin later converts the same strategic learning into [[TianJiHorseRacing|resource reallocation]] and [[EncirclingWeiToRescueZhao|indirect military pressure]].
