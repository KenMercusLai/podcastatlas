# 赤松子 / Chisongzi

赤松子 / Chisongzi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-171-zhangliang-jiao-ni-ruhe-mingli-shuangshou-1-lptgy7wxwsve8ljt79asetubin41|Hanji 171 part 1]] as the immortal figure invoked by [[ZhangLiang|张良]] after [[LiuBang|刘邦]] has founded the early [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]] order. The episode introduces him as an ancient mythic immortal, also called 赤宋子, associated with rain mastery, healing and longevity lore, and stories preserved in [[SouShenJi|《搜神记》]].

The page's main role is political rather than hagiographic. Zhang Liang's stated wish to follow Chisongzi lets him turn retreat from court life into a transcendent aspiration. [[SimaGuang|司马光]] reads this as intelligent self-protection: if Zhang Liang knows immortal claims are unreliable, the Chisongzi language becomes a way to make non-participation in power respectable and non-threatening.

## Key Claims
- Chisongzi functions in this source as a named destination for Zhang Liang's post-founding withdrawal.
- The episode treats the mythology as part of Chinese immortal and fangshu lore, not as verified supernatural fact.
- Zhang Liang's Chisongzi reference links [[DaoistNourishingLifeTechniques|道教养生术]] to [[TranscendentExitPoliticalCamouflage|假托神仙式权力退场]]: spiritual vocabulary helps make political retreat durable.
