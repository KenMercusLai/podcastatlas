# Lindsay Haven

## Overview
Lindsay Haven is a former video game industry worker whose [[MarketplaceTech]] interview connects career exit with profit pressure, subtle gender discrimination, education, family caregiving, and community life outside paid employment.

## Current Profile
Haven initially treated leaving the game industry and returning to school as a break rather than a permanent workforce exit. The pandemic, a change in family structure, and full-time caregiving altered that plan. Her account makes unpaid care and volunteering visible while showing how social expectations can still make a person feel required to justify not holding a paid job.

## Key Characteristics
- Enjoyed work in video games but objected to a model centered on maximizing profit rather than delivering a good product at a set price.
- Describes subtle, unspoken gender discrimination in promotion decisions.
- Returned to school after leaving the industry and later became a full-time caregiver for a family member.
- Questions whether a desire to return to paid work is personally held or partly produced by admired peers' expectations.
- Builds purpose through raising her child, volunteering, and participation in a local permaculture group.
