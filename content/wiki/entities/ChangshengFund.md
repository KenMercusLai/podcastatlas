# 长盛基金 / Changsheng Fund

长盛基金 appears in [[vol-121-cong-tanhua-yixian-de-fenji-jijin-dao-fengtou-zhengjing-de-ganggan-etf-yongyuan-buyao-digu-renxing-de-fengkuang-lusagcitdozvzm8wausvecvi-qmb]] through 长盛同庆. The source uses 同庆 A and 同庆 B to show the second stage of Chinese [[ChineseStructuredFund|structured funds]], where investors could buy a mother fund and then hold or sell separated priority and leveraged shares.

The episode treats this stage as the beginning of more visible discounts, premiums, and arbitrage behavior before [[StructuredFundPairConversion|pair conversion]] made the third-generation products more flexible.
