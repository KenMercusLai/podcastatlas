# Lens Technology

Lens Technology is the glass and touch-cover supplier associated with [[ZhouQunfei]] in [[no-210-zhongguo-shouji-jianghu-xia-cong-meizu-xiaomi-chuizi-dao-ov-huawei-de-xin-shinian-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-24-1000932027]]. The episode links the company to the transition from feature-phone industrial design to touchscreen smartphones, with Motorola V3 glass work and later Apple/Samsung supplier roles showing how component capability could become strategically important.

[[vol-127-nianbaoji-zhong-de-zhenshi-zhongguo-2025-lqjy2pnfy09zyewj0ljxjuc3gxqv]] extends Lens into the smart-car supply-chain branch. The episode notes that Lens moved from consumer electronics and the Apple chain into intelligent vehicle and cockpit business, making it another example of Chinese suppliers reusing precision-manufacturing capability across adjacent hardware ecosystems.
