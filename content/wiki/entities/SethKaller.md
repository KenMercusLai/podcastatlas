# Seth Kaller

## Overview

Seth Kaller is a historical-document dealer and researcher whose work provides the principal rebuttal to the Livingston attribution of [[AVisitFromStNicholas|“A Visit from St. Nicholas”]].

## Current Profile

[[twas-the-night-before-christmas-twas-the-night-before-christmas]] says Kaller entered the dispute after a reporter asked about a handwritten [[ClementClarkeMoore]] copy of the poem that he owned. Beginning without a declared preference, he and colleagues compared anti-Moore claims with Moore's other writings and found relevant parallels in words, images, rhymes, phrases, meter, Christmas subject matter, and writing for children.

Kaller also challenged dates in Livingston family stories and restored context to quotations used to portray Moore as humorless. His contribution shifts the case from candidate personality toward comparative documents and falsifiable chronology.

## Key Characteristics

- Dealer in historical documents who owned a handwritten Moore copy of the poem.
- Entered the dispute through a request for documentary evaluation.
- Compared attribution claims with Moore's broader surviving writing.
- Identified chronological problems in Livingston family narratives.
- Criticized decontextualized quotations and media treatment of the dispute as evenly balanced.
