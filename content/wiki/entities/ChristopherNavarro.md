# Christopher Navarro

## Overview
Christopher Navarro is the Chicago founder-caller behind [[SaborAMexicoSalsas]], asking how to move a farmers-market salsa business toward retail without losing the advantages of direct customer contact.

## Current Profile
In the episode, Christopher says he co-founded Sabor a Mexico Salsas with his parents, Marlene and Sergio. His role is to translate a family taqueria recipe story into a scalable local food brand while deciding when the business should move beyond farmers markets.

The advice he receives is deliberately staged. [[DaymondJohn]] and [[GuyRaz]] steer him toward small local stores, sampling, and direct customer observation before larger retail accounts, because his current channel still supplies margin, feedback, and story-rich selling.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents the family-founder side of Sabor a Mexico's brand story.
- Has early traction but is still testing whether retail can improve on farmers-market economics.
- Brings a clear local and family origin story that small stores can retell.
- Needs customer-behavior proof from a limited retail test before scaling channel commitments.
