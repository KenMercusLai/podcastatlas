# Samson Rowley / Hassan Aga

## Overview

Samson Rowley, later known as Hassan Aga, was a sixteenth-century English captive who was castrated, converted to Islam, and rose to become treasurer in Ottoman-linked Algiers.

## Current Profile

[[45-top-ten-eunuchs-glt5221212176]] presents Rowley as a Norfolk merchant's son captured from the *Swallow* and forcibly moved into a North African imperial system. Castration and conversion marked a violent rupture rather than a freely chosen career strategy.

His later rise to treasurer makes him a strong case for [[CastrationCoercionAndMobility]]: exceptional authority could follow enslavement and bodily coercion, but office does not cancel the conditions of entry. The episode also places him within Elizabethan-Ottoman contact and the much larger movement of enslaved Europeans into the Islamic Mediterranean.

## Key Characteristics

- English merchant-family captive seized from the ship *Swallow*.
- Person castrated and converted after violent displacement.
- Ottoman-Algerian official known as Hassan Aga.
- Treasurer whose advancement followed, but did not erase, captivity.

## Evidence

- Capture and transformation - [[45-top-ten-eunuchs-glt5221212176]] identifies Rowley's Norfolk origin, maritime capture, castration, conversion, and new name.
- Administrative rise - [[45-top-ten-eunuchs-glt5221212176]] places Hassan Aga as treasurer of Algiers.
- Wider system - [[45-top-ten-eunuchs-glt5221212176]] connects his life to Elizabethan-Ottoman relations and European enslavement in the Mediterranean.

## Qualifications

The source offers a compressed biography and does not establish the details of Rowley's capture, conversion, office, agency, or private accommodation to Algiers. Advancement should not be treated as evidence that the initial violence became voluntary.
