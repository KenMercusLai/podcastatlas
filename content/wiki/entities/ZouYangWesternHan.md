## Overview

邹阳 / Zou Yang is presented as a Liang court advocate who helps [[LiuWuLiangKingWesternHan|梁孝王刘武]] seek imperial leniency after the investigation of [[YuanAngWesternHan|袁盎]]'s assassination reaches the royal household.

## Current Profile

In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-241-liangxiaowang-liuwu-kan-wo-ruhe-huayang-zuosi-lqbllfh9nph-vvu9emzhsxdfj1le|Hanji 241]], Zou Yang approaches 王信, brother of Empress Wang, after [[HanJingdi|汉景帝]] turns against Liu Wu. His first move is interest alignment: if the emperor executes his brother, [[EmpressDowagerDouWesternHan|窦太后]] may blame influential people who failed to save him, exposing the Wang family rather than strengthening it.

His second move is narrative reframing. The story of Shun's tolerance of his brother Xiang gives Wang Xin a family precedent with which to ask Jingdi not to carry the Liang case further. The episode says Jingdi's anger eases, but this is advocacy for clemency, not exoneration; the advisers are already dead and the later destruction of evidence is handled separately by [[TianShuHan|田叔]].

## Key Characteristics

- Liang-aligned advocate acting after a political murder investigation reaches the prince.
- Persuader who begins with the intermediary's self-interest rather than an abstract plea for mercy.
- User of historical family analogy to shift an emperor from criminal anger toward sibling attachment.
- Participant in de-escalation whose argument does not prove Liu Wu innocent.

## Evidence

Interest-based intercession:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-241-liangxiaowang-liuwu-kan-wo-ruhe-huayang-zuosi-lqbllfh9nph-vvu9emzhsxdfj1le|Hanji 241]] says Zou warns 王信 that Dowager Dou's grief may endanger the Wang family if Liu Wu is executed without their intervention.

Family analogy:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-241-liangxiaowang-liuwu-kan-wo-ruhe-huayang-zuosi-lqbllfh9nph-vvu9emzhsxdfj1le|Hanji 241]] says Zou supplies the Shun-Xiang story, Wang Xin carries the plea to Jingdi, and the emperor's anger softens.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on one compact podcast account. Zou's office, access to 王信, exact speech, the source and fit of the Shun-Xiang analogy, Wang Xin's motive, Jingdi's response, and the intervention's causal weight require comparison. The episode supports successful advocacy for reduced pursuit, not a finding that Liu Wu lacked responsibility.

## What Changed

- Created a focused profile of interest alignment followed by kinship-based historical analogy.
- Kept political clemency separate from factual or legal exoneration.

## Relationships

- [[LiuWuLiangKingWesternHan|梁孝王刘武]] - prince for whom Zou builds an indirect clemency channel.
- [[HanJingdi|汉景帝]] - ruler whose anger is the ultimate target of the intervention.
- [[EmpressDowagerDouWesternHan|窦太后]] - anticipated source of political pressure if her favored son is executed.
- [[TianShuHan|田叔]] - complementary mediator who later handles the evidentiary side of the same settlement.
