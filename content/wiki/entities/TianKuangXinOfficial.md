## Overview

田邝 / Tian Kuang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-659-youxie-hexin-rencai-buyao-qingyi-dong-lg1nzal53ihdw-krici2thek-ij|Hanji 659]] as the late-[[XinDynasty|新朝]] Yiping commander who briefly makes a collapsing local field governable. His current profile is narrow but important: he mobilizes local men without prior authorization, deters [[FanChongChimei|樊崇]]'s group from entering Yiping, wins repeated actions, diagnoses why rebellion scaled, and is then removed by [[WangMang|王莽]] because his success becomes politically threatening.

## Current Profile

Hanji 659 presents Tian Kuang as an emergency field actor rather than a normal court official. When local order is threatened, he calls up adult men, distributes stored weapons, and publishes military rules openly. The action violates central control, but it works: armed groups avoid Yiping, and Wang Mang temporarily tolerates the offense because Tian Kuang promises results.

His memorial gives the page's most durable content. Tian Kuang argues that small rebel groups could initially have been handled by local order if counties had reported honestly and responded early. Instead, counties deceived commanderies, commanderies deceived the court, commanders arrived late and ineffectively, officials spent their time answering envoys, and hungry civilians lost trust in surrender offers.

Wang Mang's later replacement of Tian Kuang turns competence into danger. The source says Wang Mang fears Tian Kuang's ability and merit, secretly sends an imperial edict to replace him, and recalls him to Chang'an as palace staff. The result is renewed disorder in Qi, making Tian Kuang the episode's key case of [[KeyOperatorRemovalRisk|关键执行者移除风险]].

## Key Characteristics

- Emergency local commander who acts before permission when the formal command system is too slow.
- Practical stabilizer whose mobilization and public rules deter rebel entry into Yiping.
- Field diagnostician who identifies underreporting, official delay, failed command, and surrender distrust as systemic causes.
- Planner who favors local defense, food storage, movement of vulnerable civilians, clear rewards and punishments, and fewer disruptive envoys.
- Politically vulnerable competent actor removed because his merit threatens Wang Mang.
