## Overview

钱小豪 / Chin Siu-ho is the performer of [[DongTianbao|董天宝]] in [[TaiChiMaster1993|《太极张三丰》]], praised by the bounded episode for giving the antagonist co-lead dramatic weight.

## Current Profile

The hosts argue that Chin's gaze, hesitation, physical force, and quieter dramatic choices preserve ambition, anger, affection, and conflict inside Tianbao. His performance helps the character survive the screenplay's accelerated late turn toward open cruelty: the audience can still see a brotherly attachment that does not cancel chosen harm. This is a single-film profile, not a general career judgment.

## Key Characteristics

- Gives Tianbao the presence of a tragic co-lead rather than a disposable villain.
- Holds genuine attachment and escalating cruelty in the same performance.
- Uses expression and dramatic timing as well as martial skill.
- Preserves complexity where the screenplay's later moral transition becomes compressed.
