# Alexandra Ravello

Alexandra Ravello appears in [[tech-20251224-1224-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251224-1224-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the researcher explaining how the mycelium board is made. She describes mixing wood fiber such as pulp or cardboard, using a foaming apparatus, inoculating the foam with mushroom, and incubating it until the mixture becomes a solid board.

Ravello's contribution is the process boundary around [[MyceliumInsulation]]. In the source's account, the mushroom binds the board without added chemicals or binding material, which makes the material relevant to [[BiodegradableFoamPackaging]] and [[LowCarbonConstructionMaterials]].
