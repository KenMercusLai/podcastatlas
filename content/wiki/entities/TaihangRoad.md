# 太行道 / Taihang Road

太行道 / Taihang Road enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-92-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhuming-tiangou-shi-shui-1-lmwf0q0-3hxb6d4hro8fdmc3zdxp]] as the route [[BaiQi|白起]] attacks after taking [[HanNanyangWarringStates|韩国南阳]]. In the episode's reading, controlling or cutting this road severs the connection between [[Shangdang|上党]] and Han's capital region around Xinzheng.

The road matters because the campaign is about movement and communication as much as land. Once the Taihang route is cut, Shangdang becomes difficult for [[HanState|韩国]] to support or command, and a local decision not to surrender to [[QinState|秦国]] can become the trigger for a larger Qin-Zhao conflict.
