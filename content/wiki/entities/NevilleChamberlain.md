# Neville Chamberlain / 张伯伦

Neville Chamberlain / 张伯伦 appears in [[jifa-dongwu-jingshen-chuangzao-gengduo-jihui-lndyfdmtgo-9l5cr8oy82t1xnlfe]] as a bounded example of economic and industrial policy rather than as a full political biography. [[ZhouLuohua|周洛华]] says Chamberlain's political judgment had major problems, but uses him to discuss equipment replacement and aircraft manufacturing before the Second World War.

The source's point is that policy can create opportunity without preserving old firms. In Zhou's example, replacement incentives let capable and risk-taking entrepreneurs adopt advanced equipment while less willing incumbents were forced out, making the case part of [[OpportunityGeneratingDecentralization]] and [[RiskCostSeparation]].
