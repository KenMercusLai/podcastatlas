# AI Impact Summit India

## Overview
The AI Impact Summit in India is the event used by the bounded source to discuss India's potential role in AI governance, science, infrastructure, and equitable access.

## Current Profile
The episode presents the summit's people, planet, and progress framing as distinct from a narrow frontier-model race. Google's announcements and a speaker lineup including [[NandanNilekani]], [[YoshuaBengio]], and [[YannLeCun]] make the event a signal of ambition, but the source does not document formal agreements, participant representation, or subsequent outcomes.

## Key Characteristics
- Places India and Global South participation inside the international AI-policy conversation.
- Joins corporate commitments, national strategy, research, climate technology, and connectivity.
- Emphasizes distribution of AI benefits as well as capability competition.
