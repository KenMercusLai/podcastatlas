# 梁衍 / Liang Yan (late Han)

梁衍 / Liang Yan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-951-tianjiang-hengcai-jiujing-shi-haoshi-haishi-huaishi-lovwgng2wrtfno-s9ttpybbhksvh|Hanji 951]] as [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]]'s changshi. When [[DongZhuo|董卓]] is in [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] and [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] is being moved west, Liang Yan proposes that Huangfu Song use his troops to welcome the emperor and then attack Dong Zhuo under imperial authority, while coordinating with [[YuanShao|袁绍]] from the east.

The source uses Liang Yan as the adviser who sees the legitimacy-window logic that Huangfu Song rejects. His plan would convert the emperor's name into an anti-Dong-Zhuo command channel, but Huangfu Song treats the risk to family, followers, and formal position as too high.
