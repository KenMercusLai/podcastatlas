## Overview
中国足球甲级联赛 / China League One is the second tier in the Chinese professional-football system discussed as the necessary counterpart to [[ChineseSuperLeague]] expansion.

## Current Profile
The episode frames China League One as a pressure point in [[ChineseFootballPyramidDesign]]. If the top flight expands without a stronger second tier, relegation can remain financially destructive and promotion can remain too abrupt; if China League One expands and improves together with the top flight, the system may absorb stronger competition and possible B-team participation more fairly.

## Key Characteristics
- Second-tier league whose scale and quality shape top-flight expansion feasibility.
- Candidate for synchronized expansion to 18 clubs alongside [[ChineseSuperLeague]].
- Promotion channel where playoff design can keep both promotion hopefuls and relegation-threatened top-flight clubs under pressure.
- Possible venue affected by any future decision to allow top-flight B teams into higher competition.
