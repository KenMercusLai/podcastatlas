# Five Guys

Five Guys appears in [[zhongguo-yinglai-hanbao-kaidianchao-guochan-huangningmeng-jiage-huiluo-1006920543]] as the foreign burger entrant that made the China burger opening wave visible. The source says its first Beijing store drew lines that at one point exceeded two hours.

The wiki relevance is not a full Five Guys company history. The case anchors [[ChinaBurgerExpansion]] by showing that burger demand can attract both established foreign chains and adjacent Chinese restaurant brands when consumers want a filling, relatively affordable meal.
