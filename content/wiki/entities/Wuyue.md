# 吴越 / Wuyue

Wuyue / 吴越 is the southern regime foregrounded by [[Taipingnian|《太平年》]] and discussed in [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]]. The source treats Wuyue as a "归附型" kingdom: it accepted titles and performed submission while retaining substantial local control.

The episode also uses Wuyue to shift viewpoint. Instead of looking only from the central plains outward, it asks what [[FiveDynastiesTenKingdoms|五代十国]] looks like when seen from a southern state whose stability, economy, and material remains help explain [[EconomicCulturalCenterSouthwardShift]].
