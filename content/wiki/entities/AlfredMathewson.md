# Alfred Mathewson

Alfred Mathewson appears in [[kyiv-brawl-ukraines-top-general-fired-6a609aec4fac21e67f1be165]] as a legal and reform voice in the [[AlbuquerquePoliceDepartment]] segment. The source spells the name "Matthewsons"; this page uses Mathewson as the normalized name while preserving the source's role.

His key claim is that Albuquerque's federal consent decree did not accomplish its primary purpose if it failed to reduce shootings. In the wiki, that makes Mathewson central to [[PoliceConsentDecreeCultureGap]]: the gap between formal policy compliance and actual fatal-force reduction.
