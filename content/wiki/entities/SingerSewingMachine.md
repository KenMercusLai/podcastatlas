# Singer Sewing Machine

The Singer sewing machine is [[the-invention-invention]]'s bridge from invention romance into patent conflict. The source says [[IsaacMerrittSinger]] improved a rudimentary Boston sewing machine and received an 1851 patent, after which his company rapidly began producing machines and winning commercial recognition.

The product also created legal conflict because it used pieces claimed by [[EliasHoweJr]] and operated inside a wider [[PatentThicket]] involving other sewing-machine makers. That thicket made the [[SewingMachineCombination]] the source's first major example of a [[PatentPool]].
