# 王萌 / Wang Meng (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-915-shishang-meiyou-zhenzheng-de-kouwu-zhehua-dui-ma-lgxcodjxnaozvyegxqurgzji5gus|Hanji 915]] presents 王萌 / Wang Meng as [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]]'s son, arrested when [[YangQiuLateHan|阳球]] moves against Wang Fu, [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]], and Wang Fu's family network. His role is brief but emotionally sharp: he pleads that his father not be tortured, then curses Yang Qiu when the interrogation turns brutal.

The episode uses Wang Meng to show that Yang Qiu's anti-eunuch prosecution is not a clean legality tale. The target is a corrupt and violent network, but the method includes torture, mouth-stopping with mud, beating father and son to death, public corpse display, confiscation, and family exile.

The Hanji 915 transcript renders the name as 王猛; this page normalizes the late-Han Wang Fu son as 王萌.
