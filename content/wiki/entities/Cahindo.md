# Cahindo

## Overview

Cahindo is a luxury special-occasion fashion brand rooted in its founder's Congolese heritage and broader African upbringing, with garments handcrafted by female artisans in Africa.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Cahindo as a rebuilding brand after the loss of major wholesale accounts reduced reported annual sales from $268,000 in 2023 to about $50,000. Its strongest path is not broad digital acquisition but focused proof: concentrate on a small set of bestselling occasion pieces, learn who buys them and why, let customers encounter the garments and artisan story in person, and use the resulting sales, returns, reviews, and occasion data to approach smaller boutiques.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses African colors, prints, names, history, and artisan production as substantive brand inputs.
- Focuses on special-occasion clothing sold directly and through selected retail relationships.
- Has identifiable bestselling products already worn by wedding guests and mothers of the bride.
- Faces limited founder time and capital after losing important wholesale accounts.
- Is testing pop-ups, trunk shows, focused influencer work, and smaller boutiques as rebuilding channels.

## Evidence

- Brand and production: [[advice-line-with-seth-berkowitz-of-insomnia-cookies-vmp8144262595]] describes the founder's heritage, African artisan work, and historically grounded design language.
- Revenue and channel reset: [[advice-line-with-seth-berkowitz-of-insomnia-cookies-vmp8144262595]] reports the loss of Anthropologie, Rent the Runway, and Nuuly accounts alongside the sales decline.
- Focus strategy: [[advice-line-with-seth-berkowitz-of-insomnia-cookies-vmp8144262595]] recommends three to five leading products, customer-and-occasion data, mission-led presentation, and smaller-boutique outreach.

## Qualifications

Revenue, account history, product performance, and planned channel tests are founder-reported snapshots. The episode does not provide SKU margins, inventory exposure, attribution data, customer-acquisition costs, or evidence that the proposed experiments have succeeded.

## What Changed

- Initial profile created around the wholesale loss and focused special-occasion rebuild.
- Identified bestseller and occasion evidence as the bridge to future boutique distribution.
