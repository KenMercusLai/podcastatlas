## Overview

张萌 / Zhang Meng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-478-chushi-zhihui-zhezhong-ren-tai-jiduan-yudao-qing-yuanli-lq3hhg58lohdi-tbpmcuhwajjo7h|Hanji 478]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-483-1-lishi-jiemi-hun-zhichang-de-jianghu-guiju-lgn4p7pgqa8euxgrspcwdgsusce7|Hanji 483-1]] as [[ZhouKanWesternHan|周堪]]'s paired Western Han ally in the struggle against [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]]'s court group.

## Current Profile

Hanji 478 shows the earlier damage to Zhang Meng's position. He is paired with Zhou Kan as a target of Shi Xian and Shi Gao's disaster-omen blame, then of Yang Xing's over-eager attack and Zhuge Feng's reversal from praise to denunciation. Hanji 483-1 then presents Zhang Meng as part of the same failed return sequence as Zhou Kan. Earlier disaster blame sends them out of the center; later disasters make that blame unsustainable; [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] recalls Zhang Meng as taizhong dafu and jishi zhong. But the return does not give the pair effective power because Shi Xian's allies dominate the court's information and personnel environment.

Zhang Meng's fate is more explicitly coercive than Zhou Kan's. After Zhou Kan's death, the episode says Shi Xian begins falsely accusing Zhang Meng, who ultimately kills himself while in office. The source uses this outcome to show that restored rank is fragile when the hostile faction still controls access and accusation channels.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official paired with Zhou Kan in an anti-Shi-Xian court struggle.
- Earlier target of disaster-omen blame later weakened by subsequent disasters.
- Target of Yang Xing's and Zhuge Feng's damaging speech in the earlier Hanji 478 layer.
- Recalled to court as taizhong dafu and jishi zhong.
- Remains vulnerable because Shi Xian's group dominates the surrounding power field.
- Dies by suicide after Shi Xian's false accusations, according to the episode.
