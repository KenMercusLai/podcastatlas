## Overview

## Current Profile

Hanji 472 supplies the pre-conflict status layer: Zhang Meng is Zhou Kan's student, promoted by Yuan to Guanglu Dafu and given the jishi zhong access title. Hanji 475 then shows the constructive side of Zhang Meng's court speech. When Xue Guangde turns a safety warning into a public threat, Zhang Meng preserves the substance but changes the path of acceptance: he praises Yuan's willingness to hear direct speech, acknowledges Xue's concern, and then gives practical reasons that autumn wind and river conditions make the bridge safer than the boat. The source makes him a case for [[FaceSavingRemonstrance|留面子式进谏包装]] rather than for evasive flattery.

Hanji 478 shows later damage to Zhang Meng's position. He is paired with Zhou Kan as a target of Shi Xian and Shi Gao's disaster-omen blame, then of Yang Xing's over-eager attack and Zhuge Feng's reversal from praise to denunciation. Hanji 483-1 then presents Zhang Meng as part of the same failed return sequence as Zhou Kan. Earlier disaster blame sends them out of the center; later disasters make that blame unsustainable; [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] recalls Zhang Meng as taizhong dafu and jishi zhong. But the return does not give the pair effective power because Shi Xian's allies dominate the court's information and personnel environment.

Zhang Meng's fate is more explicitly coercive than Zhou Kan's. After Zhou Kan's death, the episode says Shi Xian begins falsely accusing Zhang Meng, who ultimately kills himself while in office. The source uses this outcome to show that restored rank is fragile when the hostile faction still controls access and accusation channels.

## Key Characteristics

- Zhou Kan's student and Western Han official later paired with him in an anti-Shi-Xian court struggle.
- Earlier target of disaster-omen blame later weakened by subsequent disasters.
- Target of Yang Xing's and Zhuge Feng's damaging speech in the earlier Hanji 478 layer.
- Guanglu Dafu who successfully restates Xue Guangde's safety advice in a face-saving form.
- Recalled to court as taizhong dafu and jishi zhong.
- Remains vulnerable because Shi Xian's group dominates the surrounding power field.
- Dies by suicide after Shi Xian's false accusations, according to the episode.

## Qualifications

This profile is source-scoped to Hanji 472, Hanji 475, Hanji 478, and Hanji 483-1. It does not settle Zhang Meng's full biography, exact relationship to Zhou Kan, the full accusation record, or the legal and personal circumstances of his suicide. The character variant 张猛/张萌 is preserved as a source-scoped naming issue rather than split into separate pages.
