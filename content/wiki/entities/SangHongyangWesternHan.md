## Overview

桑弘羊 / Sang Hongyang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-402-1-sanghongyang-ceng-bang-hanwudi-zhifu-weihe-zuihou-canzao-miezu-ltpcos20d5mafcoywmfcb6iifwsv|Hanji 402-1]] as a major [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]-era fiscal minister whose late-life conflict with [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] ends in execution and family destruction.

## Current Profile

The episode gives Sang Hongyang a deliberately compressed profile. His earlier achievement is financial: he helped Han Wudi enrich the state and manage fiscal pressure. His final political profile is darker: he asks unsuccessfully for offices for family members, falls into deep policy conflict with Huo Guang, and is described as participating in Liu Dan's anti-Huo rebellion. By age seventy-four, old fiscal service no longer protects him; Huo Guang has him executed and his kin group destroyed.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han fiscal official remembered in the episode as an important contributor to Han Wudi's state finance.
- Political opponent of Huo Guang during the Han Zhao regency field.
- Late-life participant, in the episode's account, in Liu Dan's anti-Huo rebellion.
- Merit-holder whose old service does not prevent execution once factional conflict and rebellion charges combine.
- Father of Sang Qian, whose flight and concealment open a second legal-political case after Sang Hongyang's death.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 402-1. It does not reconstruct Sang Hongyang's full fiscal career, salt-and-iron policy role, debate history, relationship with Han Wudi, or the complete legal record of the Liu Dan rebellion. The episode emphasizes his end more than his fiscal program.
