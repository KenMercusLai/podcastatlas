# Gaspard Coligny

Gaspard Coligny appears in [[692-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-massacre-in-paris-part-2-glt4665799025]] as the leading Huguenot whose failed assassination and later killing by men tied to [[HenryDukeOfGuise|Henry, Duke of Guise]] help ignite the [[StBartholomewsDayMassacre]]. The episode presents his presence in Paris as part of a royal wedding meant to reconcile Catholics and Protestants, making the violence especially destructive as a collapse of settlement politics.

Coligny's body is abused in the street after his murder, and the episode treats that spectacle as part of the massacre's evidence of extreme confessional violence. His death therefore becomes a key node in [[ConfessionalTraumaMemory]]: for English Protestants, Paris could be remembered as what might happen if Catholic power, claimant politics, and mob violence aligned in [[England]].
