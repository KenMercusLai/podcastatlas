# Stephen Kosslyn

## Overview
Stephen Kosslyn is a cognitive scientist credited in the episode for mental-imagery experiments in which imagined travel time across a learned map scaled with distance.

## Current Profile
Within the bounded source, Kosslyn's work supplies evidence that imagined perception retains spatial organization. The episode uses that result to argue that visualization can recruit structured representations relevant to practice while remaining weaker and less complete than real perception or action.

## Key Characteristics
- Cognitive scientist associated with experimental study of mental imagery.
- Provides imagined-navigation evidence linking mental travel time to represented distance.
- Supports a structured but non-identical relationship between imagery and perception.

## Evidence
- Imagined navigation - [[science-based-mental-training-visualization-for-improved-learning-scim9944841947]] credits Kosslyn's map experiments for travel times that scale with actual spatial distance.
- Interpretive role - [[science-based-mental-training-visualization-for-improved-learning-scim9944841947]] uses the result as one basis for task-matched, simple visualization.
