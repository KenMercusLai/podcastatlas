# 陈玄奘（《西游降魔篇》）

## Overview

陈玄奘 is the [[JourneyToTheWestConqueringDemons|《西游降魔篇》]] protagonist discussed in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-4-zhoumo-ruiqi-pei-ni-lingyi-shijiao-zai-kan-yibian-xiyou-xiangmo-lolxgqbkq2owmzzay91dy4fanwn3|Hanji 456-4]]. He is related to but distinct from the historical [[Xuanzang|玄奘]] page.

## Current Profile

The episode reads Chen Xuanzang as a practitioner whose declared compassion is incomplete because he has split universal love from human love. His refusal of [[DuanMissConqueringDemons|段小姐]], accidental release of [[SunWukong|孙悟空]], confession before loss, and final transformation make his arc a movement from slogan-like mercy into compassion grounded in pain.

## Key Characteristics

- He begins with a universal-love posture that avoids admitting romantic attachment.
- His relation to Duan Miss exposes the difference between rejecting desire and evading love.
- His encounter with Sun Wukong externalizes the danger of an ideal that has become heart-demon.
- His awakening depends on loss, confession, pain, and the reassembly of broken understanding.
