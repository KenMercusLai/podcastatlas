# 陈玄奘（《西游降魔篇》）

## Overview

陈玄奘 is the [[JourneyToTheWestConqueringDemons|《西游降魔篇》]] protagonist discussed in the paired Hanji 456-4 and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-5-xiuxingren-shi-ruhe-kandai-xiyou-de-lurr8m74eut6qcemzruhn3ia62oq|Hanji 456-5]] readings. He is related to but distinct from the historical [[Xuanzang|玄奘]] page.

## Current Profile

The episodes read Chen Xuanzang as a practitioner whose declared compassion is incomplete because he has split universal love from human love and has not yet transformed insight into mastery. He sees through false appearances in the fishing village and 高老庄, but his compassion, desire, and affection remain dependent on painful tests through [[ShaWujing|沙和尚]], [[ZhuBajie|猪八戒]], [[DuanMissConqueringDemons|段小姐]], and [[SunWukong|孙悟空]]. His refusal, accidental release of Sun Wukong, confession before loss, and final transformation make his arc a movement from slogan-like mercy into compassion grounded in pain.

## Key Characteristics

- He begins with a universal-love posture that avoids admitting romantic attachment.
- His relation to Duan Miss exposes the difference between rejecting desire and evading love.
- His encounter with Sun Wukong externalizes the danger of an ideal that has become heart-demon.
- His awakening depends on loss, confession, pain, and the reassembly of broken understanding.
- He can identify illusion before he can subdue the desire or attachment behind it.
- His practice is criticized when moral purity becomes a posture rather than inner non-attachment.

## Evidence

- Universal-love avoidance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-4-zhoumo-ruiqi-pei-ni-lingyi-shijiao-zai-kan-yibian-xiyou-xiangmo-lolxgqbkq2owmzzay91dy4fanwn3]] says Chen Xuanzang frames love as compassion while refusing to recognize his own romantic love.
- Duan Miss relation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-4-zhoumo-ruiqi-pei-ni-lingyi-shijiao-zai-kan-yibian-xiyou-xiangmo-lolxgqbkq2owmzzay91dy4fanwn3]] treats the ring, marriage demand, finger-breaking refusal, and final confession as the core emotional test.
- Sun Wukong as heart-demon: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-4-zhoumo-ruiqi-pei-ni-lingyi-shijiao-zai-kan-yibian-xiyou-xiangmo-lolxgqbkq2owmzzay91dy4fanwn3]] says Sun Wukong manipulates Chen's emotional disorder into breaking the white-lotus seal.
- Awakening through loss: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-4-zhoumo-ruiqi-pei-ni-lingyi-shijiao-zai-kan-yibian-xiyou-xiangmo-lolxgqbkq2owmzzay91dy4fanwn3]] links Duan Miss's death, the restored children's songs, and the emergence of scripture to Chen's realization.
- Insight before mastery: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-5-xiuxingren-shi-ruhe-kandai-xiyou-de-lurr8m74eut6qcemzruhn3ia62oq]] says Chen sees 高老庄's illusion clearly but still cannot subdue Zhu Ganglie without Duan Miss's intervention.
- Posture versus inner release: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-5-xiuxingren-shi-ruhe-kandai-xiyou-de-lurr8m74eut6qcemzruhn3ia62oq]] uses the master's goose-leg teaching to show Chen is still attached to purity signs and gender/food distinctions.
