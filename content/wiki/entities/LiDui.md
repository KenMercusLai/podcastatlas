# 李兑 / Li Dui

李兑 / Li Dui appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-66-zhongguo-lishishang-naxie-diwang-shi-bei-huohuo-e-si-de-1-lvl1uxjadiedqwad-skxrwf-usnq]] as a Zhao taifu who sees the danger in [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]]'s post-abdication succession arrangement. The earlier source summary rendered the name as 李队; [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-66-zhongguo-lishishang-naxie-diwang-shi-bei-huohuo-e-si-de-2-ltsw2eilkegs5yjtrp6gkhrmsxsg]] uses 李兑, so this page uses 李兑 as the canonical display while preserving the transcript variant as source-scoped.

His warning is structural as well as personal. He tells [[FeiYi|肥义]] that [[ZhaoZhang|赵章]] is strong, arrogant, followed by many people, and dissatisfied with [[ZhaoHe|赵何]], while [[TianBuli|田不李]] is violent and overbearing. Together, they look less like an ordinary elder-son grievance and more like a ready-made coup faction.

Li Dui advises Fei Yi to claim illness and hand government to [[ZhaoChengWarringStates|赵成]], but Fei Yi refuses because Zhao Wuling Wang entrusted Zhao He to him. Li Dui's grief after that answer marks the episode's fatalism: he recognizes the coming danger, but cannot make the loyal minister step out of it.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-66-zhongguo-lishishang-naxie-diwang-shi-bei-huohuo-e-si-de-2-ltsw2eilkegs5yjtrp6gkhrmsxsg]] turns Li Dui from warning voice into crisis actor. He and Zhao Cheng bring troops from Handan, defeat Zhao Zhang, and enter Zhao Wuling Wang's quarters to kill Zhao Zhang after the prince flees there. Li Dui initially wants to ask Zhao He how to proceed, but Zhao Cheng blocks the request so the young ruler is not forced to order the fate of his brother and father.

After Zhao Zhang is killed, Li Dui and Zhao Cheng fear that withdrawing will invite later punishment. They keep Zhao Wuling Wang surrounded, empty the palace, and leave him without food until he dies. The episode then places Li Dui in the new order as sikou, making him one of the people whose survival depends on completing the [[ShaqiuCrisis|Shaqiu]] settlement.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-68-jingtan-songkangwang-huangyinwudu-de-gouxue-lishi-1-lss3e9g45jyndnvmsexwibwce5oo]] adds Li Dui as Fengyangjun and the holder of Zhao government while [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] is young. The episode places him on the pro-Qi anti-Qin side of Zhao's policy split and says he, like [[WeiRan|魏冉]], covets [[SongState|宋国]]'s wealth and especially [[Dingtao|陶 / 定陶]] as a fief target. This turns Li Dui from only a Shaqiu crisis actor into one example of how ministerial private interest can shape interstate alignment.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-89-qinwang-jing-bei-ta-dui-mabaonan-ljugedx8qxhtuzd1ja-xiwxgse19]] reuses Li Dui as one of [[FanJu|范雎]]'s danger precedents. In Fan Ju's argument to [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]], Li Dui's role in the Zhao crisis shows that ministers who control the court and military environment can convert ruler weakness into lethal confinement.
