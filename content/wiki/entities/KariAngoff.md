# Kari Angoff

## Overview
Kari Angoff is the founder of [[ElectiveStudyAbroad]] and the first caller in [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]], calling from Paris about converting interest into enrolment.

## Current Profile
Angoff describes a business roughly eighteen months old that runs week-long, seminar-style courses for adults, built around a single theme tied to a specific place and taught by local instructors connected to both. The audience is mostly but not exclusively retired, and class size is deliberately capped at ten because the mix of backgrounds makes the discussion nearly as valuable as the curriculum. Her reported problem is a funnel gap: strong click-through, specific email questions, and steady call bookings, but few enrolments.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder of an adult study-abroad business built on place-linked, discussion-heavy seminars.
- Deliberate small-group design, capped at ten participants per course.
- Conversion-constrained rather than demand-constrained: interest signals are strong but enrolment is not.
- Has tried deadlines and discounts and found them ineffective.
- Resistant to tourist clichés in marketing because she wants to differentiate education from sightseeing.
- Working through the advice by adding a sample-syllabus request path.

## Evidence
- Business model: [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]] describes week-long courses such as architecture and food in southern Spain and world religions in Istanbul, taught by locally rooted instructors.
- Scale and pricing: [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]] says five courses and twenty-six students are planned by year end and that the average course price is about $6,200.
- Conversion problem: [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]] records her question about creating urgency after deadlines and discounts failed.
- Positioning tension: [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]] has her rejecting Eiffel Tower imagery to avoid a sightseeing frame while worrying about losing comparison appeal.
- Advice received: [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]] records the screening questions, peer-group framing, testimonials, follow-up email, channel partnerships, and print-ad suggestions.

## Qualifications
All business figures are self-reported in the episode. The source does not include the actual conversion rate, cost per lead, course margin, or whether any of the suggestions were tested.
