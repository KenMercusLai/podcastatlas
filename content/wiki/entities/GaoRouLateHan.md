# 高柔 / Gao Rou (Late Han)

高柔 / Gao Rou (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-974-caocao-xiang-ta-tuo-qi-xian-zi-weihe-zhuanshen-ai-daozi-lhfabab7xv2vfsgnhr9ozwqhmhd|Hanji 974]] as the [[Chenliu|陈留]] observer who predicts that [[CaoCao|曹操]] and [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] will eventually diverge despite their visible friendship. The episode gives him a diagnostic role: Cao Cao's ambition reaches beyond one province, while Zhang Miao has Chenliu as a local base and therefore has independent room to calculate.

The source says people around Gao Rou initially dismiss the warning because Cao Cao and Zhang Miao seem close. Gao Rou then moves his clan toward Hebei to join his cousin Gao Gan, making his judgment a practical exit decision rather than just a clever comment.
