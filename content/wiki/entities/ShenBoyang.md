# 沈伯洋 / Shen Po-yang

## Overview
[[ShenBoyang|沈伯洋]] is the challenger to incumbent Taipei mayor [[JiangWanan|蒋万安]] in the election analysis supplied by [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]].

## Current Profile
The episode portrays Shen as a candidate who improved his standing by building a municipal, responsive, and pragmatic public image rather than centering rebuttals to hostile labels. Polling cited in the source reportedly shows the share with no view of him falling while favorable opinion rose and unfavorable opinion remained broadly stable.

His opportunity and vulnerability are the same: voters who previously backed [[HuangShanshan|黄珊珊]] appear more favorable toward him but do not carry stable attachment to his party. He therefore needs not only preference but confidence and turnout intensity, making his campaign an example of [[SoftSupportTurnoutCompetition]].

## Key Characteristics
- Challenges an incumbent in a city the episode describes as structurally favorable to the incumbent's party.
- Builds recognition through municipal discussion and willingness to answer questions.
- Avoids allowing hostile labels around recall politics to dominate his self-definition.
- Benefits from growing favorability among previously uncommitted respondents in the cited polling.
- Depends on converting weakly attached independent support into actual turnout.

## Evidence
- Image formation: [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]] says Shen emphasized serious municipal discussion and responsiveness rather than repeated rebuttal of the “recall chief culprit” label.
- Poll movement: [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]] reports a cited poll in which favorable opinion rose as no-opinion responses fell, without a comparable increase in dislike.
- Turnout dependency: [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]] identifies former Huang supporters as favorable but weakly attached voters whose participation cannot be assumed.

## Qualifications
The source is campaign commentary based on selected polling and does not provide full questionnaires, sampling methods, official results, or a complete policy comparison. Its claim that Shen's image strategy caused the polling movement is an interpretation rather than an identified causal effect.

## What Changed
- Established a profile centered on favorable first-impression formation and turnout-dependent support.
- Preserved the difference between improving favorability and securing committed votes.
