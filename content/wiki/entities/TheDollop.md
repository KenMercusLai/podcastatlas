# The Dollop

## Overview

The Dollop is a comedy-history podcast represented here by a [[RevisionistHistory]] crossover in which Dave Anthony and Gareth Reynolds explore Staten Island Loyalism and Christopher Billopp during the [[AmericanRevolution|American Revolution]].

## Current Profile

The episode uses a loose, joke-heavy conversational format to make an obscure local history accessible. [[BenNaddaffHafrey]] supplies reporting developed for a Staten Island series, while the hosts follow Billopp's kidnappings, British occupation, Patriot raids, and the collapse of local Loyalist militia support.

The comedy does not remove evidentiary boundaries. The source distinguishes facts from inference and leaves incomplete records unresolved, although its conversational pace sometimes interrupts chronology and gives some claims less documentary context than a formal history would.

## Key Characteristics

- Comedy-history podcast using conversational narration and improvisation.
- Crossover venue for reporting omitted from another series.
- Uses an individual biography to enter a larger political and military conflict.
- Makes local and morally complicated history accessible through humor.
- Retains visible uncertainty despite an informal format.
