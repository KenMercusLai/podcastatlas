# Sapna Maheshwari

Sapna Maheshwari appears in [[tech-20260811-0811-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260811-0811-mp-tech-pod-128]] as a [[NewYorkTimes|New York Times]] reporter interviewed by [[MarketplaceTech]] about AI-powered nursery and parenting products. The source is grounded partly in her own use of [[Nanit]] with both of her children, which lets the episode move between reporting on companies and the parent-side experience of sleep recommendations, paid upgrades, and monitoring data.

Her role in the wiki is to connect consumer technology reporting to [[AIParentingAdvice]], [[ParentalJudgmentOutsourcing]], and [[ChildBedroomDataPrivacy]]. The source does not treat her as simply pro- or anti-baby-tech; it uses her experience to explain why parents may want reassurance while still worrying about dependence, privacy, and anxiety.
