# William Landay

William Landay is the author of [[DefendingJacob|《捍卫雅各布》 / Defending Jacob]], discussed in [[24-hanwei-yagebu-xiangxin-ni-shi-wo-weiyi-de-zhinian-576218441]]. The episode emphasizes his legal education and prosecutor background to explain why the novel can make courtroom procedure, grand-jury questioning, evidence strategy, and legal psychology carry the suspense.

For the wiki, Landay matters less as a general author biography than as the source of a fiction case where professional criminal-law knowledge becomes a family-ethics test. His novel lets [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] connect crime fiction to [[LegalTruthEvidenceGap]], [[ParentalDefenseConflict]], and [[GeneticCulpabilityProblem]].
