# 万象更新

万象更新 is the podcast and listener-community context represented by [[NanxiWanxiang|南希]] in [[ep274-ruhe-zhaodao-shihe-ziji-de-gengnianqi-daan-li2bp1rtitdnvc04rmizyfg1txfh]]. In this source, it functions less as a media brand profile than as the experience base behind a sustained public discussion of menopause and women's health.

The episode uses 万象更新 to bring listener stories into [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]]'s reporting frame. Those stories make clinical advice more concrete: some people cannot follow ideal sleep advice because of children, some need language for body changes, and some need a group where desire, partners, sex, and mother-daughter relations can be discussed without overburdening family ties.
