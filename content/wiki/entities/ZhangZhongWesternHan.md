## Overview

张忠 / Zhang Zhong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-511-taihou-tianhuaban-ta-ruhe-yitian-feng-wuge-didi-wei-hou-lhoz_ubecvc8-mgfoad2pya72uht|Hanji 511]] as the Yushi Dafu who impeaches [[WangZunWesternHan|王尊]] for cruelty and arrogance.

## Current Profile

Zhang Zhong's role in the episode is institutional rather than biographical. His memorial has enough force to remove Wang Zun, but the source immediately complicates it through [[GongchengXingWesternHan|公乘兴]]'s counter-memorial, which says the accusation rested on [[YangFuWesternHan|杨辅]]'s private resentment.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Yushi Dafu in Hanji 511.
- Impeaches Wang Zun as cruel and arrogant.
- Becomes the formal official channel through which a possibly private accusation enters state judgment.

## Evidence

- Impeachment role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-511-taihou-tianhuaban-ta-ruhe-yitian-feng-wuge-didi-wei-hou-lhoz_ubecvc8-mgfoad2pya72uht|Hanji 511]] says Zhang Zhong submits the impeachment that causes Wang Zun to lose office.
- Procedural ambiguity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-511-taihou-tianhuaban-ta-ruhe-yitian-feng-wuge-didi-wei-hou-lhoz_ubecvc8-mgfoad2pya72uht|Hanji 511]] says Gongcheng Xing later argues that the charges came from Yang Fu's private grievance.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 511 and does not settle whether Zhang Zhong knowingly carried a false accusation, whether the charges had partial merit, or what happened to him after the review request.
