# Fotheringhay Castle

Fotheringhay Castle appears in [[696-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-killing-the-queen-of-scots-part-6-glt2334035496]] as the secure inland site chosen for the trial and execution of [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]]. The episode says its fortifications, difficult surrounding ground, and large ceremonial spaces made it suitable both for imprisonment and for judgment.

Mary's trial opens there on 14 October 1586, with [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]] absent but represented by an empty throne. The room, commissioners, evidence, and lack of defense counsel make Fotheringhay central to [[PoliticalTrialTheatre]].

On 8 February 1587, Fotheringhay becomes the scene of Mary's scaffold performance and execution. The source treats this as a defeat for Mary in state-security terms but a symbolic success in [[MartyrdomStaging]].
