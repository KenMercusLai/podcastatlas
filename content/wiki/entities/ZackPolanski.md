# Zack Polanski

Zack Polanski is the [[GreenPartyOfEnglandAndWales]] leader profiled in [[its-not-easy-being-green-zack-polanski-6a82d1a0ff328abd84724cf0]]. [[AndrewMiller]] presents him as a politician who has made the Greens more visible to younger voters by combining left-populist rhetoric, theatrical performance instincts, and a policy platform that moves environmental politics toward [[EcoPopulism]].

The source uses Polanski's biography as part of his political appeal and vulnerability. It describes him as gay, Jewish, raised near [[Manchester]], bullied as a teenager, later trained in drama and sociology, and shaped by precarious work as an actor, nightclub worker, waiter, and hypnotherapist. The episode also treats old embarrassments, including a hypnotherapy claim and a misleading Red Cross spokesperson claim, as material his critics use to attack his trustworthiness.

The most serious tension in the profile is around [[Gaza]] and [[Israel]]. The source says Gaza has become a Green Party touchstone under Polanski and that many British Jews view his anti-Israel stance and comments about Jewish safety as hostile despite his own Jewish background.
