# 刘怡 / Liu Yi (Space and Military History)

## Overview

刘怡 appears in the bounded source as the guest in a [[HuzuoHuyou|忽左忽右]] live discussion of the Challenger disaster and the institutional history of U.S. human spaceflight.

## Current Profile

In [[502-yuzhou-chaoji-gongcheng-shi-ruhe-kua-diao-de-liuyi-tan-tiaozhanzhehao-shigu-sishi-zhounian-loylfmnxyc-cuajgcn12-v7-ybd]], Liu uses a nonfiction work on Challenger to move from the O-ring failure into the political, budgetary, contractor, engineering, and public-relations system around the shuttle. His role is historical interpretation and synthesis rather than primary accident investigation.

## Key Characteristics

- Connects a technical accident to Cold War and post-Apollo institutional history.
- Uses the Apollo-shuttle contrast to identify political, financial, and objective-setting conditions for megaprojects.
- Treats engineering design, contracting, maintenance, schedules, and public communication as one coupled system.
- Uses “go fever” to explain how accumulated commitment can suppress stopping decisions.
