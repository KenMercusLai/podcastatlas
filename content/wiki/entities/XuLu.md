# 许路 / Xu Lu

许路 / Xu Lu is the sailing-focused interview subject discussed by [[SunRuoxi|孙若曦]] in [[ep238-2025-nian-ni-wan-guo-zenyang-de-xiaozhong-yundong-lpaw-kela5vk6avl-s11h-yexwlg]]. The source presents him as an [[Xiamen|厦门]] native whose childhood around docks and boats shaped both his early contact with [[LeisureSailing|休闲帆船]] and his later interest in ancient wooden sailing ships.

His wiki role is to connect a niche sport to maritime history. In the episode, Xu Lu looks for traditional wooden vessels and craftspeople in [[Fujian|福建]], studies boat eyes, mast proportions, keel materials, hull imagery, and Mazu-related details, and wants to test old sailing records through reconstructed voyages. That makes his case a bridge between [[UrbanNicheSports|城市小众运动]], field reporting, craft continuity, and experimental archaeology.
