# 《存韩》 / Cun Han

《存韩》 / Cun Han enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-112-1-yaojia-shi-ruhe-ping-yizhangzui-po-siguo-hezong-lthw0fvvinmf6ghxpqut60agsueg]] as [[HanFei|韩非]]'s written attempt to protect [[HanState|韩国]] after [[LiSi|李斯]] urges [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] to attack Han first. The episode says Han Fei's speech difficulty makes writing the practical channel for persuasion.

The memorial's strategy is indirect. Han Fei writes as if he is reasoning from [[QinState|秦国]]'s interest, arguing that Han has not been Qin's primary enemy while [[ZhaoState|赵国]] has been central to [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] pressure. He warns that if Qin attacks Han first, Han will resist desperately and Zhao will gain an opportunity to attack Qin.

Li Si's answer, as reported by the episode, is that this argument hides the danger of Han's own uncertainty. If Qin attacks Zhao and [[QiState|齐国]] joins the war, Han could reverse position and expose Qin to a second front. The text therefore becomes a case of defensive redirection: a weak state's thinker tries to move the stronger state's first blow elsewhere, while a rival adviser exposes the residual risk.
