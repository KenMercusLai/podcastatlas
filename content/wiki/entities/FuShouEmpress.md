# 伏皇后 / Empress Fu Shou

伏皇后 / Empress Fu Shou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-991-zhengduo-xiandi-zhizhan-shui-neng-zuizhong-qusheng-lkbsl5uzqxouzm6mlewrnuns0x6p|Hanji 991]] as the empress accompanying [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] during the night escape and Yellow River crossing. The source says her brother Fu De supports her while carrying silk, and that one of her attendants is killed in the scramble, splashing blood onto the empress's clothing.

The episode does not build a full biography for Fu Shou. Her role is to make the imperial flight concrete: even the empress's immediate space is pierced by panic, killing, and bodily danger, so court status cannot shield the household from the same collapse that consumes officials, palace women, and followers left behind.
