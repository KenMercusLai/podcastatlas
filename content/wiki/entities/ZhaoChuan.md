# 赵传 / Zhao Chuan

## Overview
赵传 is a singer represented in the wiki through one [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] interview about a multi-decade music career, physical conditioning, voice maintenance, walking, touring, and later-life renewal. The episode identifies 《我是一只小小鸟》 and 《我很丑但我很温柔》 as signature songs and uses the album 《老不休》 to frame his current life stage.

## Current Profile
The source presents Zhao Chuan as a working performer who learned to treat physical condition as professional infrastructure after feeling unwell during a heavily scheduled period. Because his singing and stage style are physically demanding, he describes an evolving mix of basketball, resistance training, swimming, plank work, walking, food restraint, and later voice-care study. These are personal practices shaped by age, injury, enjoyment, appearance, and performance requirements rather than a universal training prescription.

His artistic outlook joins continuity with renewal. 《我是一只小小鸟》 has accumulated meanings over decades that he could not have anticipated at the start of his career, while 《老不休》 expresses a wish to practice, mature, and begin again in later life. Walking through tour cities and revisiting Beijing venues connect bodily movement, memory, observation, and creative input.

## Key Characteristics
- Veteran singer whose classic songs continue to carry multi-generational popular memory.
- Treats health and physical capacity as prerequisites for sustaining a demanding stage career.
- Combines enjoyable cardio, gradual strength work, low-impact substitution, core training, and walking.
- Balances vocal force, body weight, stage appearance, recovery, and present physical tolerance.
- Uses city walking, travel, and venue revisiting as sources of memory and creative observation.
- Frames later life as continued practice and renewed participation rather than automatic retirement.
