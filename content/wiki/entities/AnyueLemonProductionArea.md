# Anyue Lemon Production Area / 安岳柠檬产区

Anyue Lemon Production Area / 安岳柠檬产区 appears in [[zhongguo-yinglai-hanbao-kaidianchao-guochan-huangningmeng-jiage-huiluo-1006920543]] as the supply-side anchor for domestic yellow lemons. The source describes Sichuan Anyue as China's largest lemon production area.

The episode says the 2025-2026 production season returned to more normal weather, fruiting improved, and output rose about 30% from the prior season. That stronger supply met weaker exports and softer B-end tea-drink procurement, turning Anyue into the concrete place behind the source's [[YellowLemonPriceCycle]].
