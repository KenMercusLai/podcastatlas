# 楚留香 / Chu Liuxiang

## Overview

楚留香 / Chu Liuxiang is [[GuLong|古龙]]'s wuxia investigator and the central problem-solver in [[BorrowedCorpseReturnsSoulGuLong|《借尸还魂》]] and [[PaintingThrushGuLong|《画眉鸟》]]. The bounded episodes present him less as an overpowering fighter than as a reader of clues, motives, reactions, habits, system composition, and negotiable success conditions.

## Current Profile

楚留香 moves between two cases. He dismantles the apparent possession of 左明珠 by tracing the substituted body, 施茵 and 叶盛兰's escape, shared information, learned technique, and medical collusion. Once the social purpose is clear, he does not expose the lovers mechanically; he treats investigation as compatible with mercy and offers to preserve their route out of family-controlled marriage.

His confrontation with [[XueYirenGuLong|薛衣人]] shows the same preference for reframing over direct domination. Knowing that he is unlikely to win a conventional weapons contest, he defines “not losing” as victory, uses speech to disturb the swordsman's certainty, relies on evasion, and refuses to attack when his opponent becomes vulnerable. His bluff about fingerprints later elicits the incriminating response through which 薛笑人 reveals knowledge he should not possess.

In 《画眉鸟》, 楚留香 treats a six-person sword formation as a coordination problem. Before combat he disables 柳无眉's intended participation, leaves the weaker 李玉函 inside the formation, and sends 胡铁花 to release the hostages. He then offers an extra sword to a fighter accustomed to using two, turning a practiced response into an incompatible seventh weapon and exploiting the resulting pause. His later decision to help the couple who tried to kill him extends discretionary mercy, while the underwater encounter with [[ShuiMuYinJi|水母阴姬]] makes reciprocal restraint clearer than its disputed breathing mechanics.

## Key Characteristics

- Investigates by combining physical clues, social motives, concealed alliances, and involuntary reactions.
- Rejects the supernatural explanation without dismissing why its layered evidence initially persuades others.
- Uses discretion after solving the lovers' deception rather than equating truth with compulsory exposure.
- Changes the terms of unequal conflict instead of pretending to possess superior raw force.
- Uses verbal pressure and bluffing as extensions of investigation and combat.
- Alters team composition and exploits practiced habit to disrupt coordinated opponents.
- Preserves dignity by declining an opportunistic strike after gaining advantage.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to two novels as discussed by two podcast episodes; it is not a comprehensive account of 楚留香 across the wider series. His motives, exact investigative sequences, martial limits, and decisions to conceal or forgive wrongdoing are represented through the supplied summaries. Praise for the duel and formation strategies is critical interpretation rather than an independently established ranking of fictional combat ability, and 《画眉鸟》's skin-breathing claim is not credible human physiology.

## What Changed

- Extended the profile from individual bluffing and asymmetric dueling to coordinated-system disruption.
- Added hostage-task separation and mercy toward prior attackers.
- Qualified the underwater victory as thematically legible but mechanically implausible.
