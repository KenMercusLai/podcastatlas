# 楚留香 / Chu Liuxiang

## Overview

楚留香 / Chu Liuxiang is [[GuLong|古龙]]'s wuxia investigator and the central problem-solver in [[BorrowedCorpseReturnsSoulGuLong|《借尸还魂》]]. The bounded episode presents him less as an overpowering fighter than as a reader of clues, motives, reactions, and negotiable success conditions.

## Current Profile

楚留香 moves between two cases. He dismantles the apparent possession of 左明珠 by tracing the substituted body, 施茵 and 叶盛兰's escape, shared information, learned technique, and medical collusion. Once the social purpose is clear, he does not expose the lovers mechanically; he treats investigation as compatible with mercy and offers to preserve their route out of family-controlled marriage.

His confrontation with [[XueYirenGuLong|薛衣人]] shows the same preference for reframing over direct domination. Knowing that he is unlikely to win a conventional weapons contest, he defines “not losing” as victory, uses speech to disturb the swordsman's certainty, relies on evasion, and refuses to attack when his opponent becomes vulnerable. His bluff about fingerprints later elicits the incriminating response through which 薛笑人 reveals knowledge he should not possess.

## Key Characteristics

- Investigates by combining physical clues, social motives, concealed alliances, and involuntary reactions.
- Rejects the supernatural explanation without dismissing why its layered evidence initially persuades others.
- Uses discretion after solving the lovers' deception rather than equating truth with compulsory exposure.
- Changes the terms of unequal conflict instead of pretending to possess superior raw force.
- Uses verbal pressure and bluffing as extensions of investigation and combat.
- Preserves dignity by declining an opportunistic strike after gaining advantage.

## Qualifications

His motives, exact investigative sequence, martial limits, and decision to conceal the lovers' deception are represented through the supplied summary. The episode's praise of his duel strategy is a critical reading rather than an independently established ranking of fictional combat ability.
