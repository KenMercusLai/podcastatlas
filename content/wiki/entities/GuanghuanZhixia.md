# 光环之下 / Guanghuan Zhixia

## Overview
《光环之下》 is a short AI comic drama by [[XiaoGuoGege|小果哥哥]], adapted from a little-read short story and used to show that original-reader popularity does not mechanically predict adaptation reach.

## Current Profile
The creator reports making the roughly 40-minute work in two or three days because he liked the story and had unused generation credits. Initial performance was weak, then the drama reportedly gained about six million plays in one hour and eventually exceeded 300 million. The creator could not reduce the result to one cause, naming story, adaptation, opening material, regional-language resonance, distribution timing, and luck as possible contributors.

## Key Characteristics
- Fast, interest-led experiment rather than a direct attempt to reproduce the creator's prior hit.
- Adaptation of a roughly ten-thousand-character story with only hundreds of readers at the time.
- Delayed breakout whose causal mechanism remains uncertain.

## Evidence
- Production choice: [[lhhrwpwmn7e-unjt7wpxdixllxgs-lhhrwpwmn7e-unjt7wpxdixllxgs]] reports a two-to-three-day production cycle motivated by personal interest and spare compute credits.
- Reach and uncertainty: [[lhhrwpwmn7e-unjt7wpxdixllxgs-lhhrwpwmn7e-unjt7wpxdixllxgs]] reports more than 300 million cumulative plays after a slow start and explicitly avoids a single-cause explanation.

## Qualifications
Length, readership, playback, production time, and causal explanations are creator-reported and not independently verified. A single nonlinear breakout cannot show which factor was necessary or repeatable.
