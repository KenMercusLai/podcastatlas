# 长沙郡 / Changsha Commandery (late Han)

长沙郡 / Changsha Commandery enters the late-Han wiki branch through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1027-jingzhou-zhizhu-liubiao-weihe-nan-cheng-bazhu-ll-ebguqllsf-5ycvpdogihzn3lj|Hanji 1027]] as the southern [[Jingzhou|荆州]] commandery governed by [[ZhangXianLateHan|张献]]. This page is disambiguated from the modern [[Changsha|Changsha city]] page and from [[ChangshaKingdom|长沙国]] in the early Western Han branch.

In the source, Changsha matters as a local-power base rather than as a city profile. Higher taxation and tension with [[LiuBiao|刘表]] give [[HuanJieLateHan|桓阶]] room to advise Zhang Xian to combine Changsha with [[LinglingCommanderyLateHan|零陵郡]] and [[GuiyangCommanderyLateHan|桂阳郡]], then attach to [[CaoCao|曹操]]. The commandery therefore becomes the lead node in [[SouthernJingzhouLateHan|southern Jingzhou]]'s breakaway.
