## Overview

朱晖 / Zhu Hui now appears in the wiki as a He-era official whose bounded profile begins with anti-[[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] remonstrance and later moves into Southern Xiongnu frontier command. Hanji 761 places him among officials who object to Dou's Northern Xiongnu expedition, Hanji 768 makes him an acting Duliao general in the An Guo crisis, and Hanji 769 records his execution after false reporting in the next Southern Xiongnu phase.

## Current Profile

Hanji 761 adds the earlier court layer. Zhu Hui joins [[HanLengEasternHan|Han Leng]] and [[LeHuiEasternHan|Le Hui]] in memorializing against the Northern Xiongnu campaign before [[EmpressDowagerDouHeLateHan|Empress Dowager Dou]] ignores the advice. This makes him part of the first visible resistance field before Dou's victory increases family power.

Hanji 768 then places Zhu Hui on the frontier as the acting Duliao general appointed after [[HuangfuLengLateHan|Huangfu Leng]] is dismissed. He joins [[DuChongProtectorOfXiongnu|Du Chong]] in countercharging [[AnGuoSouthernXiongnu|An Guo]], enters the royal court area, attempts mediation, and participates in the attack after An Guo's failed move against [[ShiziChanyu|Shizi]].

Hanji 769 complicates that command profile. Zhu Hui and Du Chong are executed after the court discovers false reporting in the next Southern Xiongnu phase. The current profile therefore holds together three source-bounded roles: remonstrant, crisis intervener, and later false-report accountability target.

## Key Characteristics

- Early anti-expedition remonstrant in the Dou Xian Northern Xiongnu debate.
- Replacement frontier commander inserted into a volatile Southern Xiongnu succession and security crisis.
- Worked with Du Chong against An Guo after An Guo's complaint against Du Chong was intercepted.
- Combined mediation attempts with military action.
- Became part of the later false-report accountability chain.
- Shows how frontier intervention could shift from necessary response to punishable misreporting across adjacent episodes.

## Qualifications

Zhu Hui's current page should not collapse the Hanji 768 intervention into the Hanji 769 punishment as a single moral judgment. The first source layer presents campaign objection, the second presents crisis intervention, and the third presents false reporting and accountability after the crisis evolves. Hanji 761's transcript renders the name as 朱徽; this page normalizes it to the established Zhu Hui route because the He-era office and sequence fit the same actor.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 761 as Zhu Hui's earlier anti-expedition remonstrance layer.
- Reframed his profile as a sequence from court objection to frontier intervention and later accountability.
