## Overview
朱晖 / Zhu Hui was an Eastern Han frontier commander in the Southern Xiongnu crisis spanning Hanji 768 and Hanji 769. His current profile links legitimate intervention against [[AnGuoSouthernXiongnu|An Guo]] with later accountability for false reporting alongside [[DuChongProtectorOfXiongnu|Du Chong]].

## Current Profile
Hanji 768 places Zhu Hui as the acting Duliao general appointed after [[HuangfuLengLateHan|Huangfu Leng's]] dismissal. He joined Du Chong in countercharging An Guo, entered the royal court area, attempted mediation, and then participated in the attack after An Guo's failed move against [[ShiziChanyu|Shizi]]. Hanji 769 later shows Zhu Hui and Du Chong executed after the court discovered false reporting in the next Southern Xiongnu phase.

## Key Characteristics
- Replacement frontier commander inserted into a volatile succession and security crisis.
- Worked with Du Chong against An Guo after An Guo's complaint against Du Chong was intercepted.
- Combined mediation attempts with military action.
- Became part of the later false-report accountability chain.
- Shows how frontier intervention could shift from necessary response to punishable misreporting across adjacent episodes.
