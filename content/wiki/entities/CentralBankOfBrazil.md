# Central Bank of Brazil

The Central Bank of Brazil appears in [[working-memory-the-surprising-decline-of-dementia-6a6b0eeca585d3be78cd9d1b]] as the creator and operator context for [[Pix]]. The episode says Pix's rapid adoption depended on a trusted and powerful central bank, mandatory bank participation, and a large cash-reliant domestic market.

The source treats the institution as a reason Pix is hard to replicate. A similar system may need not only software, but regulatory authority, banking-sector coordination, consumer trust, and enough cash displacement opportunity to make instant payments socially normal.
