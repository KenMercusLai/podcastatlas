# Nick Blamer

Nick Blamer appears in [[ep-11-growing-technology-footprints-in-insurance-sector]] as Senior Technology Director at [[Coherent]]. The episode presents him as an insurance-technology practitioner whose career began in the late 1990s and moved through software development, consulting companies, large insurance and reinsurance companies, [[Microsoft]], and Coherent.

His core contribution is a business-user view of [[InsuranceTechnologyModernization]]. Nick frames insurance technology as a repeated shift from centralized, slow systems toward faster and more reusable tools: mainframe workloads moved to desktop calculations, cloud made workloads more scalable, and [[BusinessLogicAPIs]] can now expose spreadsheet logic to enterprise systems.

Nick also supplies the episode's governance boundary. He sees generative AI as already embedded in everyday tools and useful for chatbots, reports, formulas, and BI-style pattern discovery, but he warns that insurance use still needs legal guardrails, bias checks, and understanding of when correlation does not imply causation.
