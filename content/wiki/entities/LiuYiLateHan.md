# 刘翊 / Liu Yi (late Han)

刘翊 / Liu Yi (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-989-ningwei-taipingquan-buzuo-luanshi-ren-lkoycygmjnltuvpq-6wjv-ls61s6|Hanji 989]] as the man who kills [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] before Zhang Miao can leave for [[YuanShu|袁术]]. The episode identifies him as a close guard around Zhang Miao and presents his action as a calculation that letting Zhang Miao join Yuan Shu would strengthen [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s enemy.

The page is source-scoped. The host says Liu Yi came from the Yingchuan adviser world and may have been an earlier Cao Cao hidden asset, but the durable wiki claim is narrower: in this episode, Liu Yi converts Zhang Miao's attempted exit from [[YongqiuLateHan|雍丘]] into a lethal internal betrayal, helping close the Yanzhou branch as Cao Cao receives formal recognition as Yanzhou governor.

This page uses the `LiuYiLateHan` key to avoid colliding with [[LiuYiSanlian|刘仪 / Liu Yi]], the modern Talk Sanlian guest.
