## Overview

赵半山 / Zhao Banshan is a senior martial figure whose role in [[FeihuWaizhuan|《飞狐外传》]] combines instruction, moral example, and chosen kinship for [[HuFei|胡斐]]. The bounded episode presents him as a mentor who joins compassion to a firm refusal to let friendship excuse wrongdoing.

## Current Profile

Zhao Banshan travels to seek justice for a wrong against his community and refuses to abandon that standard when friends intervene. His conduct gives Hu Fei a usable model: loyalty and affection matter, but they do not automatically cancel responsibility to victims.

His mentorship is not confined to technique. He improves Hu Fei's martial understanding, explicitly warns him that acquired skill must follow the right path, and becomes his sworn brother. The episode interprets this combination of care, authority, and ethical formation as partly father-like without collapsing the relationship into literal parenthood.

Hu Fei's later refusal to release Feng Tiannan at Yuan Ziyi's request echoes Zhao's earlier stance. The connection makes侠义 transmissible through witnessed conduct and relational trust, not merely maxims.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines benevolence and compassion with a firm standard of accountability.
- Refuses to let friendship erase a serious wrong.
- Teaches martial skill together with an explicit demand for right conduct.
- Offers Hu Fei trust, kinship, and moral formation beyond technical instruction.
- Serves as a behavioral precedent for Hu Fei's later choice under romantic pressure.

## Qualifications

This profile comes from a focused podcast discussion rather than a complete comparison of Zhao Banshan across editions of 《飞狐外传》 and 《书剑恩仇录》. Describing him as a father-like mentor and treating Hu Fei's choice as direct imitation are interpretive judgments, not explicit causal proof.

- Identified principled conduct under friendship pressure as the model Hu Fei later echoes.
