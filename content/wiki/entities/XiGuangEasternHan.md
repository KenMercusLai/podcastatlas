## Overview
锡光 / Xi Guang is an early Eastern Han southern-frontier official whose current wiki profile comes from [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-699-gan-yu-liuxiu-zhengxiong-de-ta-weihe-candan-shouchang-lth-8mndudj3khf9cnsouj86c1-z|Hanji 699]]. The episode places him in [[JiaozhiCommandery|交趾郡]] and credits him, together with [[RenYanEasternHan|任延]], with teaching Han ritual and everyday practices in Lingnan.

## Current Profile
Hanji 699 presents Xi Guang as part of the southern consolidation layer around Jiaozhi. After Cen Peng's correspondence and Liu Xiu's proclamations draw southern tribute and enfeoffment, the episode shifts from elite submission to local instruction.

Xi Guang's role is described through cultural and administrative transmission. In Jiaozhi, he teaches ritual norms and social customs associated with the central plains, including marriage practice. The episode treats this as one starting point for Lingnan's adoption of Zhongyuan cultural forms.

His wiki significance is not military command but durable governance. Xi Guang shows how frontier integration can proceed through norms, teaching, and local social practice after formal allegiance is made visible.

## Key Characteristics
- Jiaozhi official associated with early Eastern Han southern consolidation.
- Paired with Ren Yan as a transmitter of Han ritual and social practice.
- Represents local instruction after political submission rather than conquest alone.
- Evidence point for ritual and marriage-custom reform in frontier governance.
- Southern-frontier case for integration through administrative presence and teaching.

## Qualifications
This page is source-bounded to Hanji 699. It does not reconstruct Xi Guang's full office title, chronology, local reception, or the degree of coercion or adaptation involved in cultural change. The transcript form 西光 is normalized to 锡光.
