## Overview

郅恽 / Zhi Yun is synthesized as an Eastern Han adviser who appears at two linked succession moments. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-719-guangwudi-liuxiu-suanbusuan-zhanan-lk8eme3wwyk5qkjjfxkp0zsc77ix|Hanji 719]] has him warn [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] that deposing [[GuoShengtong|郭圣通]] is a state-foundation issue, not only household management; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-720-donghan-kaiguo-taizi-weihe-fanfu-da-cizhi-baogao-lslizspo8rywnf3cebyhincdhvz0|Hanji 720]] has him advise [[LiuQiangDonghaiKing|刘强]] to leave the crown-prince position after that maternal-status field changes.

## Current Profile

Hanji 719 supplies Zhi Yun's first current role. As Liu Xiu prepares to depose Guo Shengtong and make [[YinLihua|阴丽华]] empress, Liu Xiu initially treats the matter as private family affairs. Zhi Yun counters that an empress's elevation or deposition concerns the foundation of the state and must be handled carefully.

Hanji 720 supplies the downstream succession role. After Guo's deposition, Liu Qiang becomes uneasy because his mother's status had supported his crown-prince position. Zhi Yun advises him that remaining crown prince is dangerous and does not help him serve his mother; retiring to a fief is safer and more filial.

Across the two episodes, Zhi Yun functions as a boundary adviser. He tells the ruler that palace change is public politics, then tells the endangered heir that moral language and withdrawal may preserve life and kinship under the changed order.

## Key Characteristics

- Adviser who reframes palace-household decisions as state-foundation matters.
- Succession-risk diagnostician after Guo Shengtong's deposition.
- Links empress status, crown-prince safety, and filial service as one political field.
- Offers cautionary advice without becoming an independent power center in the current evidence.

- Careful-handling response: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-719-guangwudi-liuxiu-suanbusuan-zhanan-lk8eme3wwyk5qkjjfxkp0zsc77ix|Hanji 719]] says Liu Xiu accepts the need to handle the matter carefully before issuing the no-congratulation deposition settlement.
- Withdrawal advice: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-720-donghan-kaiguo-taizi-weihe-fanfu-da-cizhi-baogao-lslizspo8rywnf3cebyhincdhvz0|Hanji 720]] says Zhi Yun advises Liu Qiang that staying crown prince has become dangerous after Guo's demotion.
- Filial reframing: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-720-donghan-kaiguo-taizi-weihe-fanfu-da-cizhi-baogao-lslizspo8rywnf3cebyhincdhvz0|Hanji 720]] says Zhi Yun frames fief retirement as a better way for Liu Qiang to serve his mother.

## Qualifications

The page is source-bounded to two succession-advice scenes. It does not reconstruct Zhi Yun's full official biography, offices, family line, or later reputation. The Hanji 719 and Hanji 720 scenes should be read together because the second is the direct consequence field of the first, but the sources do not prove Zhi Yun controlled the final imperial decision.
