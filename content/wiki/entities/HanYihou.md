# 韩懿侯 / Marquis Yi of Han

韩懿侯 / Marquis Yi of Han appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-2-lmnqfjmydygqnfec6xhzpixralvi]] as the son and successor of [[HanAihou|韩哀侯]] after Han Aihou is killed during the [[HanGui|韩廆]] and [[YanZhongzi|严遂]] assassination crisis.

The page is source-scoped. Han Yihou matters here because the episode places his accession immediately after a court murder, preserving the Han succession sequence while also showing how ruler death could come from factional violence rather than orderly transfer.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-3-lmhj4le2mwu1yhhl-qmuoqkfmhjf]] gives him an active interstate role. After [[WangChuo|王绰]] flees from Wei and [[GongsunQi|公孙齐]] urges action, Han Yihou joins [[ZhaoChenghou|赵成侯]] against [[WeiState|魏国]]. The source contrasts Han's preferred settlement, splitting Wei to remove a long-term threat, with Zhao's plan to install [[GongzhongHuan|公中缓]] and take land.
