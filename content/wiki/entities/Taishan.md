# 泰山 / Taishan

[[Taishan|泰山]] enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-122-1-jiemi-qinshihuang-fengchan-weishenme-xuan-taishan-lkcyayuqrtsgy-k4bhdvy-l-lapo|Qinji 122-1]] as the sacred mountain chosen by [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] for the "封" half of the fengshan ceremony during his 219 BCE eastern tour. The episode treats the site as a political and ritual choice: Taishan sits in the eastern cultural world Qin had just conquered, so sacrificing and engraving there helped make imperial possession visible.

The source does not treat Taishan as merely scenic geography. It is the place where [[FengshanRitualLegitimacy|封禅礼制合法性]] lets military unification claim heavenly approval, where [[ImperialTourPoliticalTheater|imperial tour political theater]] turns travel into rule, and where Qin's own ritual practice collides with Qi-Lu scholarly uncertainty.

## Key Claims
- Taishan is the main mountain for Qin Shi Huang's "封" ceremony before he descends toward [[LiangfuMountain|梁父山]] for "禅."
- The episode links Taishan to post-conquest assertion over former six-state territory, especially the eastern lands.
- The road-clearing work before ascent shows that sacred performance also depended on practical infrastructure.
- Taishan's meaning in the source is source-scoped to Qin Shi Huang's 219 BCE ceremony rather than a general history of all later fengshan rites.
