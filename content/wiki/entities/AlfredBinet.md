# Alfred Binet / 阿尔弗雷德·宾内

Alfred Binet / 阿尔弗雷德·宾内 appears in [[37-zhishang-ceshi-qingwen-ni-shi-zhili-poluomen-ma-608020679]] as the French psychologist who helped create a modern child intelligence test with [[TheodoreSimon|Theodore Simon / 西奥多·西蒙]]. The episode presents Binet's starting problem as educational support: after France expanded schooling, teachers and administrators needed a way to identify children who were not keeping up with classroom instruction.

The source uses Binet as a contrast with later sorting regimes. His age-sensitive testing and "mental age" frame helped make [[IntelligenceTesting|intelligence testing]] practical, but the episode's broader warning is that a support tool can become [[EducationalTrackingByTesting|educational tracking by testing]] or exclusion when institutions attach rank, destiny, or resource allocation to the score.
