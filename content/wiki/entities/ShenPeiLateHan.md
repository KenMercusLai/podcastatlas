# 审配 / Shen Pei (late Han)

## Overview
审配 / Shen Pei (late Han) is a [[YuanShao|袁绍]] adviser-administrator whose bounded wiki profile currently moves from inherited Jizhou personnel to pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] factional counsel, the arrest of [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]]'s family that helps trigger defection, and post-Guandu suspicion over Shen Pei's own captured sons. The sources use him as evidence that Yuan Shao could gather capable or consequential people after taking [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]], but that the later adviser environment could still reward flattery, factional accusation, and fragile trust.

## Current Profile
Shen Pei enters the bounded profile through Yuan Shao's takeover of [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]]: he had served under [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] and becomes part of Yuan's adviser-administrative circle after Han Fu loses power. In the Guandu-preparation evidence, he and [[GuoTu|郭图]] argue for immediate war against Cao Cao and attack [[JuShou|沮授]]'s concentrated command authority, after which Yuan Shao divides Ju Shou's troops. Cao-side assessment through [[XunYu|荀彧]] reads Shen Pei as authoritarian and arrogant inside an adviser mix whose members do not combine well.

Before Wuchao, Shen Pei becomes an internal trigger inside Yuan Shao's collapse sequence. While guarding [[YeLateHan|邺城]], he arrests Xu You's family after they violate the law. The episode reads the arrest as factional pressure that enrages Xu You and pushes him to Cao Cao, where his knowledge of Yuan Shao's supply position becomes decisive.

After Guandu, Shen Pei becomes a post-defeat trust test. After his two sons are captured by Cao Cao, Meng Dai argues that Shen Pei's high status, power, and family force make betrayal likely, while Guo Tu and [[XinPingLateHan|辛评]] echo the suspicion. Yuan Shao briefly shifts Ye defense supervision to Meng Dai, then asks [[FengJiLateHan|逢纪]], who has private hostility toward Shen Pei. Feng Ji says the private quarrel should not override public judgment and defends Shen Pei as rigid, upright, and unlikely to betray for two captured sons. The page therefore now holds two sides of Shen Pei's bounded profile: he participates in damaging prewar factional advice, yet later receives an integrity defense from a rival in a captured-family suspicion scene.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan Shao camp figure inherited through the Jizhou takeover.
- Adviser-administrator whose current evidence is source-scoped rather than biographical.
- Pro-war/flattery voice in the pre-Guandu council.
- Participant in the factional move that weakens Ju Shou's command authority.
- Part of the adviser environment that turns Yuan Shao's talent pool into factional competition.
- Internal-law actor whose arrest of Xu You's family helps turn factional pressure into enemy defection.
- Post-Guandu trust-test subject whose captured sons make loyalty suspicious but whose integrity is defended by a private enemy.

## Qualifications
The page remains narrow. Hanji 957 only marks Shen Pei's entry into Yuan Shao's personnel field, and the Guandu-preparation sources reflect hostile Cao-side assessment as well as the host's reading of Yuan-camp dysfunction. Hanji 1048 does not prove that Shen Pei acted only from factional malice; it says Xu You's family broke the law while the episode reads the arrest as politically consequential. Hanji 1050 gives a loyalty-defense scene, not a full exoneration of every prior Shen Pei judgment.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1048's arrest of Xu You's family at Ye and its role in triggering Xu You's defection.
- Reframed Shen Pei's profile around captured-family politics on both sides of Guandu: his arrest of Xu You's family before Wuchao and suspicion of his own sons after defeat.
- Qualified the arrest as source-scoped law enforcement whose factional effect, not proven motive, is the current wiki claim.
