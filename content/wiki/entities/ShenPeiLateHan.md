# 审配 / Shen Pei (late Han)

## Overview
审配 / Shen Pei (late Han) is a [[YuanShao|袁绍]] adviser-administrator whose bounded wiki profile currently moves from inherited Jizhou personnel to pre-Guandu factional counsel. The sources use him as evidence that Yuan Shao could gather capable or consequential people after taking [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]], but that the later adviser environment could still reward flattery and internal consumption.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] introduces Shen Pei as one of the people absorbed into Yuan Shao's adviser and administrative circle after [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] loses Jizhou. He is not developed there beyond the talent-transfer point. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] gives him a sharper role: paired with [[GuoTu|郭图]], he praises Yuan Shao's martial prestige and Hebei's strength against [[JuShou|沮授]]'s recovery-first advice, while [[XunYu|荀彧]] later identifies Shen Pei as authoritarian and arrogant inside Yuan's adviser mix.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan Shao camp figure inherited through the Jizhou takeover.
- Adviser-administrator whose current evidence is source-scoped rather than biographical.
- Pro-war/flattery voice in Hanji 1034's pre-Guandu council.
- Part of the factional Yuan-camp environment that weakens advice conversion.
- Named by Xun Yu as one of the advisers whose personal style prevents a unified Yuan Shao decision system.

## Evidence
- Personnel absorption: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] says Shen Pei had served under Han Fu but becomes part of Yuan Shao's wider Jizhou adviser circle after the transfer of power.
- Pro-war meeting role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] pairs Shen Pei with Guo Tu in telling Yuan Shao that Cao Cao can be defeated easily and that Hebei's strength makes immediate attack attractive.
- Organizational diagnosis: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] has Xun Yu identify Shen Pei and other Yuan advisers as mutually incompatible, making their talent less useful than their names suggest.

## Qualifications
The page remains narrow. Hanji 957 only marks Shen Pei's entry into Yuan Shao's personnel field, and Hanji 1034 reflects hostile Cao-side assessment as well as the host's reading of Yuan-camp dysfunction. Further sources are needed before making broader claims about his whole career.
