# 审配 / Shen Pei (late Han)

## Overview
审配 / Shen Pei (late Han) is a [[YuanShao|袁绍]] adviser-administrator whose bounded wiki profile currently moves from inherited Jizhou personnel to pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] factional counsel and then to post-Guandu suspicion over captured family members. The sources use him as evidence that Yuan Shao could gather capable or consequential people after taking [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]], but that the later adviser environment could still reward flattery, factional accusation, and fragile trust.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] introduces Shen Pei as one of the people absorbed into Yuan Shao's adviser and administrative circle after [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] loses Jizhou. He is not developed there beyond the talent-transfer point. The Guandu-preparation sources give him a sharper role. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] pairs him with [[GuoTu|郭图]] in rejecting [[JuShou|沮授]]'s recovery-first plan and then attacking Ju Shou's concentrated authority, leading Yuan Shao to divide command. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] emphasizes the flattering side of that role and later has [[XunYu|荀彧]] identify Shen Pei as authoritarian and arrogant inside Yuan's adviser mix.

Hanji 1050 adds a post-defeat trust test. After Shen Pei's two sons are captured by Cao Cao, Meng Dai argues that Shen Pei's high status, power, and family force make betrayal likely, while Guo Tu and [[XinPingLateHan|辛评]] echo the suspicion. Yuan Shao briefly shifts Ye defense supervision to Meng Dai, then asks [[FengJiLateHan|逢纪]], who has private hostility toward Shen Pei. Feng Ji says the private quarrel should not override public judgment and defends Shen Pei as rigid, upright, and unlikely to betray for two captured sons. The page therefore now holds two sides of Shen Pei's bounded profile: he participates in damaging prewar factional advice, yet later receives an integrity defense from a rival in a captured-family suspicion scene.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan Shao camp figure inherited through the Jizhou takeover.
- Adviser-administrator whose current evidence is source-scoped rather than biographical.
- Pro-war/flattery voice in the pre-Guandu council.
- Participant in the factional move that weakens Ju Shou's command authority.
- Part of the adviser environment that turns Yuan Shao's talent pool into factional competition.
- Named by Xun Yu as one of the advisers whose personal style prevents a unified Yuan Shao decision system.
- Post-Guandu trust-test subject whose captured sons make loyalty suspicious but whose integrity is defended by a private enemy.

## Evidence
- Personnel absorption: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] says Shen Pei had served under Han Fu but becomes part of Yuan Shao's wider Jizhou adviser circle after the transfer of power.
- Pro-war meeting role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] pair Shen Pei with Guo Tu in telling Yuan Shao that Cao Cao can be defeated and that Hebei's strength makes immediate attack attractive.
- Authority attack: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] says Guo Tu's side argues that Ju Shou's military and administrative authority is too concentrated, after which Yuan Shao divides Ju Shou's troops.
- Organizational diagnosis: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] has Xun Yu identify Shen Pei and other Yuan advisers as mutually incompatible, making their talent less useful than their names suggest.
- Captured-sons suspicion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1050-cong-caocao-fenxin-xuexi-baogui-de-lingdao-zhihui-lsqg-qaxozjpaxyukrvxbtf-mcse|Hanji 1050]] says Shen Pei's two sons being captured by Cao Cao makes Meng Dai, Guo Tu, and Xin Ping suspect him of future betrayal.
- Rival's public defense: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1050-cong-caocao-fenxin-xuexi-baogui-de-lingdao-zhihui-lsqg-qaxozjpaxyukrvxbtf-mcse|Hanji 1050]] records Feng Ji separating private animosity from public affairs and defending Shen Pei's uprightness to Yuan Shao.

## Qualifications
The page remains narrow. Hanji 957 only marks Shen Pei's entry into Yuan Shao's personnel field, and the Guandu-preparation sources reflect hostile Cao-side assessment as well as the host's reading of Yuan-camp dysfunction. Hanji 1050 gives a loyalty-defense scene, not a full exoneration of every prior Shen Pei judgment; it shows his reputation for uprightness under one pressure point while leaving the broader career unresolved. Further sources are needed before making broader claims about his whole career.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1050's captured-sons suspicion after the Guandu defeat.
- Added Feng Ji's public defense of Shen Pei despite private hostility.
- Reframed Shen Pei from only a pre-Guandu pro-war/flattery figure into a more mixed Yuan-camp adviser whose integrity can be defended under a later trust test.

- [[GuoTu|郭图]] - paired adviser in the flattering immediate-war line and later echoer of suspicion against Shen Pei.
- [[XinPingLateHan|辛评]] - Yuan-camp figure who echoes the captured-sons suspicion against Shen Pei.
- [[FengJiLateHan|逢纪]] - private rival who defends Shen Pei's integrity when Yuan Shao asks for judgment.
- [[JuShou|沮授]] - opposing adviser whose rest-and-harassment strategy Shen Pei's side displaces.
- [[XunYu|荀彧]] - Cao-side analyst who treats Shen Pei's personal style as evidence of Yuan-camp incompatibility.
- [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] - strategic threshold where Shen Pei's source-bounded role becomes visible before and after the defeat.
