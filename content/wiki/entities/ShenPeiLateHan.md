# 审配 / Shen Pei (late Han)

## Overview
审配 / Shen Pei (late Han) is a [[YuanShao|袁绍]] adviser-administrator whose bounded wiki profile now runs from inherited Jizhou personnel and pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] pro-war factional counsel to four post-Guandu crises: [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]]'s family arrest before Wuchao, the Yuan-family succession settlement after Yuan Shao's death, the isolated defense of [[YeLateHan|邺城]] under [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s siege, and execution after Ye falls. The sources present him as capable and consequential, but also as a hard-edged factional actor whose institutional power can convert ambiguity into coercive outcomes.

## Current Profile
Shen Pei enters through Yuan Shao's takeover of [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]]: he had served under [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] and becomes part of Yuan's adviser-administrative circle after Han Fu loses power. In the Guandu-preparation evidence, he and [[GuoTu|郭图]] argue for immediate war against [[CaoCao|曹操]] and attack [[JuShou|沮授]]'s concentrated command authority, after which Yuan Shao divides Ju Shou's troops. Cao-side assessment through [[XunYu|荀彧]] reads Shen Pei as authoritarian and arrogant inside an adviser mix whose members do not combine well.

Before Wuchao, Shen Pei becomes an internal trigger inside Yuan Shao's collapse sequence. While guarding [[YeLateHan|邺城]], he arrests Xu You's family after they violate the law. The episode reads the arrest as factional pressure that enrages Xu You and pushes him to Cao Cao, where his knowledge of Yuan Shao's supply position becomes decisive.

After Guandu, Shen Pei first appears as a trust-test subject: after his two sons are captured by Cao Cao, Meng Dai, Guo Tu, and [[XinPingLateHan|辛评]] argue that his high status and captured family make betrayal likely, while [[FengJiLateHan|逢纪]] defends him despite private hostility. Hanji 1056 then makes Shen Pei an active succession actor. Because he and Feng Ji fear that [[YuanTan|袁谭]] would punish them if he inherited, they help install [[YuanShang|袁尚]] by claiming Yuan Shao left a final order before Yuan Tan arrives for mourning. Shen Pei remains at Ye when Yuan Shang goes to rescue Yuan Tan at [[LiyangYellowRiverLateHan|黎阳]], and Yuan Tan later kills Feng Ji after Shen Pei's side refuses to release more troops.

Hanji 1062 moves Shen Pei from succession-side Ye power into besieged-city defense. Cao Cao abandons earlier earth hills and tunnels, opens a moat, and lets it look harmless before deepening it overnight; the episode says Shen Pei laughs from the city wall and does not disrupt the work. Once Cao diverts Zhang River water into the moat, Ye is cut off from supply and news. [[LiFuLateHan|李孚]] breaks through that isolation as Yuan Shang's messenger, reports the outside situation to Shen Pei, and then uses Shen Pei's release of old and weak civilians as cover to escape.

Hanji 1063 closes the Ye-defense arc. Shen Pei coordinates with Yuan Shang by torch signal and plans an inner-outer attack, but Cao Cao personally blocks him before he can leave the city. After Yuan Shang is beaten, Shen Pei tries to keep morale alive despite Cao's display of Yuan Shang's captured goods, argues that Cao is tired and Yuan Xi will soon arrive from Youzhou, and later nearly kills Cao with hidden crossbowmen during a siege inspection. The city falls when [[ShenRongLateHan|审荣]], Shen Pei's nephew and east-gate commandant, opens the gate to Cao's army. Captured afterward, Shen Pei refuses to soften his answers, confronts [[XinPingLateHan|辛评]] after Xin Ping's family is found dead in Ye, and asks to face north at execution because his ruler is in the north.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan Shao camp figure inherited through the Jizhou takeover.
- Adviser-administrator whose current evidence is source-scoped rather than biographical.
- Pro-war and anti-Ju-Shou voice in the pre-Guandu council.
- Internal-law actor whose arrest of Xu You's family helps turn factional pressure into enemy defection.
- Post-Guandu trust-test subject whose captured sons invite suspicion but whose integrity is defended by a private enemy.
- Succession faction actor who helps turn Yuan Shao's private preference for Yuan Shang into a claimed final-order installation.
- Ye-based power holder whose troop control deepens Yuan Tan's resentment and whose later siege defense is tested by moat, water isolation, Li Fu's messenger breakthrough, Yuan Shang's failed relief, Shen Rong's gate opening, and his own execution.

## Qualifications
The page remains bounded by episodic evidence. Hanji 1048 does not prove that Shen Pei acted only from factional malice; it says Xu You's family broke the law while the episode reads the arrest as politically consequential. Hanji 1050 gives a loyalty-defense scene, not a full exoneration of every prior Shen Pei judgment. Hanji 1056 makes the claimed final order a succession-faction action, but the page keeps the precise authenticity of Yuan Shao's deathbed order source-scoped. Hanji 1062-1063 render the name as 沈佩/沈沛; the wiki normalizes to Shen Pei / 审配 because the Ye siege sequence matches the existing route.

## What Changed
- Extended Shen Pei's Ye-defense profile from water isolation into the failed Yuan Shang relief attempt, morale maintenance, crossbow ambush, east-gate betrayal, and execution.
- Reframed Shen Pei's final role as both loyalist resistance and coercive factional liability after Xin Ping's family is killed in Ye.
- Added Shen Rong as the insider whose gate opening ends Shen Pei's defensive control of Ye.
