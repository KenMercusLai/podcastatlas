# 审配 / Shen Pei (late Han)

审配 / Shen Pei (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] as one of the people who become part of [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s adviser and administrative circle after Yuan Shao takes [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] from [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]]. The source says Shen Pei had served under Han Fu but was not properly valued before the transfer of power.

This page is source-scoped to the Jizhou talent-transfer moment. Shen Pei's later career is not developed here; his value in this ingest is as evidence that Yuan Shao inherits and concentrates people whom Han Fu had failed to use decisively.
