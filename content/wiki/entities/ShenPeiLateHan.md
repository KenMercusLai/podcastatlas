# 审配 / Shen Pei (late Han)

## Overview
审配 / Shen Pei (late Han) is a [[YuanShao|袁绍]] adviser-administrator whose bounded wiki profile currently moves from inherited Jizhou personnel to pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] factional counsel. The sources use him as evidence that Yuan Shao could gather capable or consequential people after taking [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]], but that the later adviser environment could still reward flattery and internal consumption.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] introduces Shen Pei as one of the people absorbed into Yuan Shao's adviser and administrative circle after [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] loses Jizhou. He is not developed there beyond the talent-transfer point. The Guandu-preparation sources give him a sharper role. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] pairs him with [[GuoTu|郭图]] in rejecting [[JuShou|沮授]]'s recovery-first plan and then attacking Ju Shou's concentrated authority, leading Yuan Shao to divide command. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] emphasizes the flattering side of that role and later has [[XunYu|荀彧]] identify Shen Pei as authoritarian and arrogant inside Yuan's adviser mix.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan Shao camp figure inherited through the Jizhou takeover.
- Adviser-administrator whose current evidence is source-scoped rather than biographical.
- Pro-war/flattery voice in the pre-Guandu council.
- Participant in the factional move that weakens Ju Shou's command authority.
- Part of the adviser environment that turns Yuan Shao's talent pool into factional competition.
- Named by Xun Yu as one of the advisers whose personal style prevents a unified Yuan Shao decision system.

## Evidence
- Personnel absorption: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] says Shen Pei had served under Han Fu but becomes part of Yuan Shao's wider Jizhou adviser circle after the transfer of power.
- Pro-war meeting role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] pair Shen Pei with Guo Tu in telling Yuan Shao that Cao Cao can be defeated and that Hebei's strength makes immediate attack attractive.
- Authority attack: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] says Guo Tu's side argues that Ju Shou's military and administrative authority is too concentrated, after which Yuan Shao divides Ju Shou's troops.
- Organizational diagnosis: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] has Xun Yu identify Shen Pei and other Yuan advisers as mutually incompatible, making their talent less useful than their names suggest.

## Qualifications
The page remains narrow. Hanji 957 only marks Shen Pei's entry into Yuan Shao's personnel field, and the Guandu-preparation sources reflect hostile Cao-side assessment as well as the host's reading of Yuan-camp dysfunction. Further sources are needed before making broader claims about his whole career.

## What Changed
- Hanji 1033 adds Shen Pei's role in the factional authority attack that weakens Ju Shou before Guandu.
- Hanji 1034 remains the source for Xun Yu's bounded criticism of Shen Pei's personal style within Yuan Shao's adviser system.

## Relationships
- [[YuanShao|袁绍]] - leader whose Jizhou camp absorbs Shen Pei and later hears his pro-war counsel.
- [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] - former Jizhou holder under whom Shen Pei had served before Yuan Shao's takeover.
- [[GuoTu|郭图]] - paired adviser in the flattering immediate-war line.
- [[JuShou|沮授]] - opposing adviser whose rest-and-harassment strategy Shen Pei's side displaces.
- [[XunYu|荀彧]] - Cao-side analyst who treats Shen Pei's personal style as evidence of Yuan-camp incompatibility.
- [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] - strategic threshold where Shen Pei's source-bounded role becomes visible.
- [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]] - concept sharpened by the gap between adviser presence and useful decision quality.
