# 司马防 / Sima Fang (late Han)

司马防 / Sima Fang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-904-juezhao-neiguan-huode-xinling-da-ziyou-de-mijue-lopn-okbzr4ljvqmgzcsb7qav0n2|Hanji 904]] as the shangshu youcheng who recommends nineteen-year-old [[CaoCao|曹操]] for the north commandant post in [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]]. The source says he had previously served as Luoyang magistrate, so he understands the capital's difficult enforcement environment and judges Cao Cao's forceful temperament as a fit for it.

His function is narrower than a full Sima-family biography. In this episode, Sima Fang turns reputation and family access into concrete office placement: after [[QiaoXuanLateHan|桥玄]] sends Cao Cao to [[XuShao|许劭]], Sima Fang makes Cao Cao's early reputation actionable through appointment.
