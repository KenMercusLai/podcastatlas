# 豫让 / Yu Rang

豫让 / Yu Rang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-04-yurang-wei-zhiyao-fuchou-ln34tydha6iqwlrgmw7iugqfgfik]] as [[ZhiYao|智瑶]]'s retainer after the fall of the Zhi family. With no realistic political recovery available, he turns revenge against [[ZhaoXiangzi|赵无恤 / 赵襄子]] into a public performance of loyal service.

The episode gives two assassination attempts. First, Yu Rang disguises himself as a punished man and enters Zhao Wuxu's palace with a dagger while cleaning the toilet; Zhao Wuxu releases him because revenge for a destroyed lord can still look like loyal conduct. Second, after ruining his skin and voice to evade recognition, Yu Rang waits near a bridge and is captured again, then killed.

Yu Rang's answer in the [[Shiji|《史记》]] frame anchors [[RetainerReciprocityEthic|patron-recognition reciprocity]]: Fan and Zhonghang treated him as an ordinary person, so he repaid them ordinarily, while Zhi Yao treated him as a "国士," so he repaid Zhi Yao as one. The [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] version supplies the associated "士为知己者死" formula.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-4-lphaverx7gyxntncak-lb5hsrqa]] reuses Yu Rang as an analogy for [[YuQing|虞卿]] and [[WeiQiWarringStates|魏齐]]. The point is not that Wei Qi equals Zhi Yao morally; it is that the act of being trusted or treated as a person of worth can create a repayment obligation strong enough to override ordinary calculation.
