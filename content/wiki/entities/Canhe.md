# 参合 / Canhe

参合 / Canhe enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-182-weishenme-shuo-chengye-xiaohe-baiye-xiaohe-2-lqk-jifupm9lbghfzaf09igfufgb|Hanji 182 part 2]] as the northern place where [[ChenXi|陈豨]] and [[HanWangXin|韩王信]] converge after battlefield setbacks in the [[ChenXiRebellion|陈豨之乱]]. The source locates it near the southwest of modern Yanggao County, Shanxi.

The episode stresses Canhe's route value: it sits at a transport hinge among Shanxi, Inner Mongolia, and Hebei. That geography makes sense of Han Wang Xin and Chen Xi's reliance on [[Xiongnu|匈奴]]-side possibilities, because Canhe can function as both a defensive rally point and an escape-support corridor.

Canhe also marks the rebellion's hard coercive endpoint. [[ChaiWu|柴武]] first writes to Han Wang Xin urging surrender, but Han Wang Xin refuses; Han forces then defeat and kill him there, take the city, and slaughter it. Chen Xi survives temporarily before later being defeated by [[FanKuai|樊哙]].
