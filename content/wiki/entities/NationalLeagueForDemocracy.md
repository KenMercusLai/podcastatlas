# National League for Democracy

The National League for Democracy is [[AungSanSuuKyi]]'s political party in [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]]. The episode says she led it to an election win in 1990 and later became state counsellor after the 2015 election.

The party's role in the source is historical mandate rather than current operational control. The episode uses the NLD to explain why Aung San Suu Kyi became an international democratic icon, then complicates that image through the [[Rohingya]] crisis, jailed journalists, and the post-2021 opposition's turn toward armed resistance.
