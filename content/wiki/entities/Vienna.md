## Overview
Vienna / 维也纳 is a recurring wiki city that now carries three distinct source roles: symbolic military memory, elite European cultural life, and large-scale housing policy.

## Current Profile
The current synthesis treats Vienna as a city where institutions make abstract historical and economic claims concrete. In one source, the 1683 siege stands for totemic memory in [[SiegeTypology]]. In another, the city is [[StefanZweig|Stefan Zweig / 茨威格]]'s cultural world in [[TheWorldOfYesterday|《昨日的世界》]]. In the Planet Money Summer School finale, Vienna becomes a housing-market example through [[ViennaSocialHousingScale]].

## Key Characteristics
- Vienna can function as a totemic siege reference, where later symbolic meaning matters alongside battlefield history.
- It is a cultural setting for [[BelleEpoque]] memory, elite mobility, salons, literature, language, and the fragility of cosmopolitan Europe.
- It is also a contemporary policy case where social housing reaches enough scale to affect the wider rental market.
- Its housing model combines stability and supply with fairness concerns when higher-income residents keep very low-rent apartments.

## Evidence
- Symbolic military memory: [[elbows-up-again-a-us-canada-trade-flare-up-6a8c0fa3a89d26398ee7a5db]] uses Vienna in 1683 as one of [[JeremyBlack]]'s totemic siege examples.
- Cultural world: [[139-cong-zuo-ri-de-shi-jie-dao-ru-jin-de-ou-zhou-mei-hao-nian-dai-belle-epoque-ta-hai-neng-hui-lai-ma-ltvsgov8ht9-l7yp9qamdgjmyrf]] presents Vienna as the city where Zweig encountered a literary, artistic, multilingual elite world.
- Housing policy: [[the-continent-nobody-owns-everyone-benefits-from-summer-school]] says around half of Vienna residents live in government-supported, cooperative, or rent-controlled housing, while a cited study links nonprofit supply to lower for-profit rents.

## Qualifications
Each source is narrow. The siege source is classificatory, not a full military history; the Zweig source is a literary-cultural frame, not a complete city history; and the housing example is a brief listener-selected policy case, not a complete fiscal or eligibility study.
