# Severus Snape / 西弗勒斯·斯内普

Severus Snape appears in [[26-niudun-yu-weibi-zhizaozhe-keneng-shi-sineipu-de-yuanxing-580065317]] as an analogy for reading [[IsaacNewton|Isaac Newton / 牛顿]], not as a confirmed source claim about literary influence. The episode's title asks whether Newton might be a Snape prototype, but the source summary keeps the connection at the level of mood and character pattern: childhood injury, isolation, brilliance, severity, resentment, and a dark teacher-like aura.

This page exists mainly to keep the analogy bounded. It should not be used as evidence that Newton directly inspired Snape unless a later source supplies actual authorial or textual evidence.
