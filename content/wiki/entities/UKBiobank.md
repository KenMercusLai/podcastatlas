# UK Biobank

UK Biobank is the dataset named in [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]]'s sleep segment. The episode says the May Nature study used data from around half a million British people to study sleep duration and biological aging signals across 23 body parts.

In this source, UK Biobank matters because it enables population-scale health analysis while also requiring caution in individual use. The data supports a [[SleepDurationUShape]] pattern and [[OrganSpecificAgingClock|organ-specific aging clock]] estimates, but the episode stresses that causality and personal prescription remain difficult.
