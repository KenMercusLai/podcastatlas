## Overview

阎显 / Yan Xian is the defeated Yan outer-relative leader in the 125 CE restoration-coup layer. Hanji 803 now shows his side trying to preserve succession control after [[LiuYiBeixiangHouLateHan|北乡侯刘裔]] dies, while Hanji 804 shows the post-victory liquidation that makes [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝 / Liu Bao]]'s restoration irreversible.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-803-han-shundi-ruhe-zai-xuefengxueyu-zhong-dengshang-huangwei-lqvno7t-gcgxtbs3phuflr4ybe2s|Hanji 803]] shows Yan Xian before defeat. After Liu Yi becomes gravely ill, [[SunChengLateHan|孙程]] names Yan Xian as one of the figures who would need to be removed for Liu Bao's restoration to succeed. Once Liu Yi dies, [[EmpressDowagerYanLateHan|Yan Taihou]] conceals the death and summons royal descendants; after Sun Cheng's group enthrones Liu Bao, Yan Xian tries to counter through Yan Taihou's authority, [[FengShiLateHan|冯师]], Yan Chong, and bounty promises for anyone who captures Liu Bao or [[LiRunLateHan|李润]].

The same source shows why Yan Xian's counterattack collapses. Feng Shi exits and refuses commitment, [[GuoZhenLateHan|郭镇]] captures Yan Xian's brother Yan Jin at Shengde Gate, and the Yan side loses the armed recovery path before dawn.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-804-gei-ni-tang-de-ren-bu-yiding-shi-guiren-lqongdqg-hgetoegpylv-os86buh|Hanji 804]] presents the post-victory liquidation. After Liu Bao is installed at Jiade Hall, Yan Xian and his brothers are arrested and executed, while the wider family is exiled and [[EmpressDowagerYanLateHan|Yan Taihou]] is moved to a detached palace.

The source's main use for Yan Xian is structural rather than biographical. He is the visible enemy whose destruction makes the restoration irreversible, but his defeat also exposes the next problem: once the Yan external enemy disappears, the eunuch merit group must manage reward rank, hidden hesitation, and internal competition.

## Key Characteristics

- Defeated outer-relative leader in the 125 CE Shun restoration.
- Yan-family power center whose side tries to preserve succession control through concealed death, summoned royal candidates, troops, and bounties.
- Counterattack organizer whose military options fail when Feng Shi refuses and Guo Zhen captures Yan Jin.
- Defeated target whose collapse follows the seizure of palace symbols and Liu Bao's installation.
- Purge target whose execution helps make the new succession order irreversible.
- Structural counterpart to the eunuch merit group that becomes politically problematic after victory.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 803 and Hanji 804. It does not reconstruct Yan Xian's full prior career, Yan Chong's role, Yan Jin's biography, his brothers' individual biographies, or the legal procedure behind the executions. Hanji 803 supplies the attempted counter-mobilization, and Hanji 804 supplies the post-victory liquidation. The exact scope of later mitigation for more distant Yan relatives remains source-scoped here.
