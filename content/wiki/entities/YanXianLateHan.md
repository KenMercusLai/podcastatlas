## Overview

阎显 / Yan Xian is the defeated Yan outer-relative leader in Hanji 804's 125 CE restoration-coup layer. His fall marks the moment when [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝 / Liu Bao]]'s supporters convert palace control into a purge of the Yan family and the removal of the succession order that had excluded Liu Bao.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-804-gei-ni-tang-de-ren-bu-yiding-shi-guiren-lqongdqg-hgetoegpylv-os86buh|Hanji 804]] presents Yan Xian as the outer-relative powerholder whose family loses when the eunuch-backed restoration succeeds. After Liu Bao is installed at Jiade Hall, Yan Xian and his brothers are arrested and executed, while the wider family is exiled and [[EmpressDowagerYanLateHan|Yan Taihou]] is moved to a detached palace.

The source's main use for Yan Xian is structural rather than biographical. He is the visible enemy whose destruction makes the restoration irreversible, but his defeat also exposes the next problem: once the Yan external enemy disappears, the eunuch merit group must manage reward rank, hidden hesitation, and internal competition.

## Key Characteristics

- Defeated outer-relative leader in the 125 CE Shun restoration.
- Yan-family power center whose collapse follows the seizure of palace symbols and Liu Bao's installation.
- Purge target whose execution helps make the new succession order irreversible.
- Structural counterpart to the eunuch merit group that becomes politically problematic after victory.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 804. It does not reconstruct Yan Xian's full prior career, his brothers' individual biographies, or the legal procedure behind the executions. The source distinguishes Yan Xian's close-kin destruction from later mitigation for more distant Yan relatives, but the exact scope of that later narrowing remains source-scoped here.

## What Changed

- Created Yan Xian as the defeated outer-relative target of the 125 CE Shun restoration.
- Disambiguated the Yan-family purge layer before the later Hanji 805 settlement and Yan Taihou ritual-management layer.
