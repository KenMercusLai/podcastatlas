# 项飙 / Xiang Biao

## Overview
项飙 / Xiang Biao is referenced in EP280 as the source of a distinction between "经验" and "体验" used to interpret university education, fieldwork, and young people's contact with the world.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki source, Xiang Biao functions as a theoretical reference rather than an interview participant. Wei Qian uses his distinction to argue that not every event or activity becomes formative experience. A managed, frictionless, user-experience-like activity may pass over the body, while an encounter that creates reflection, discomfort, memory, and changed self-understanding can become experience in the stronger sense.

## Key Characteristics
- He is cited as a conceptual reference for distinguishing formative "经验" from passing "体验."
- His frame lets the episode criticize education that packages activities without letting students convert them into self-shaping experience.
- The reference helps connect fieldwork, conversation, and social friction to personality formation rather than only assignment completion.
