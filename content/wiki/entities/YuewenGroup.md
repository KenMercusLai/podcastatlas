# Yuewen Group / 阅文集团

Yuewen Group / 阅文集团 appears in [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]] as the later company formed when [[ShandaLiterature|盛大文学]] merged with Tencent Literature. The source presents Yuewen as one of the surviving institutional forms of Shanda's content-platform push.

For the episode's Shanda narrative, Yuewen is evidence that some assets outlived the failed [[ShandaBox|盛大盒子]] and the decline of [[ShandaGames|盛大游戏]] inside Shanda proper. It connects early web-fiction aggregation around [[Qidian|起点中文网]] to a mature IP platform under a different corporate ecosystem.

[[cong-yangshi-jilupian-dao-baokuan-ai-duanju-di-yi-pi-zhuanshen-de-daoyan-s10e11-3c05e3d5-d8f6-44c1-97ca-698261d7b2bc]] adds Yuewen as a platform-side counterpart in [[Taitai]]'s AI comic-drama business map. The source says some AI manju companies earn revenue by making platform-native dramas for [[Hongguo]] and Yuewen under guarantee plus traffic-share arrangements, while remaining exposed to platform price and policy changes.

[[ep240-ba-zong-tian-chong-zai-haiwai-duanju-chuhai-de-chanye-mima-ltvxvfj5ko6djhsjatiphdy-dzek]] adds the overseas author-cultivation branch through [[Webnovel|起点国际版]]. The episode says Chinese web-fiction platforms exported not only translated stories but also editor systems, genre guidance, and commercial writing practices that helped overseas local authors produce Chinese-method web fiction in local fantasy shells.
