# 张梁 / Zhang Liang (Yellow Turban)

张梁 / Zhang Liang (Yellow Turban) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] as [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]]'s younger brother and one of the late-184 [[YellowTurbansLateHan|黄巾军]] leaders. At Guangzong, [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]] first fails to defeat him directly, then closes camp, watches Zhang Liang's side relax, and launches a dawn attack after the Yellow Turban camp discipline loosens.

The episode presents Zhang Liang's death as one of the decisive steps in the collapse of the main Yellow Turban force. He is beheaded in the battle, after which Huangfu Song turns to [[ZhangBaoYellowTurban|张宝]] at Quyang.

This page is deliberately disambiguated from [[ZhangLiang|张良]], the early-Han strategist. The two names share the same common romanization but refer to different people and different historical periods.
