# Gaston Browne

[[GastonBrowne]] is the prime minister of [[AntiguaAndBarbuda]] in [[sand-heists-and-property-rights-in-the-caribbean-summer-school]]. After [[HurricaneIrma]] damaged [[Barbuda]], he proposed giving residents individual title to the plots they occupied for one Eastern Caribbean dollar.

The source presents Browne's plan as a [[PropertyRightsAsInvestmentIncentive]] argument: title would make land usable as collateral for rebuilding loans. It also makes him the central political actor in [[DisasterRecoveryPrivatization]], because Barbudan residents saw the proposal as weakening [[CommunalLandTenure]] and opening most of the island to outside development.
