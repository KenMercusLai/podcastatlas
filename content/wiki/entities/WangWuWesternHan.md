## Overview

王乌 / Wang Wu is a Western Han envoy presented by [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-368-1-ewu-zhizhan-beihou-de-tanpan-boyi-lm1l-z62lcewag-x6kxwgyusukh|Hanji 368-1]] as a northern-border native chosen by [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] to test whether repeated [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] overtures could produce a real political settlement.

## Current Profile

Wang Wu's distinctive capability in the current evidence is cultural and procedural fluency. He understands that access to the chanyu requires compliance with Xiongnu audience rules, so he leaves the Han tally outside, changes clothing, marks his face, and enters alone. The episode interprets those choices as tactical concessions rather than abandonment of office.

Once admitted, Wang Wu separates form from substance. He preserves the counterpart's ritual dignity but still demands submission on Han's behalf, pairing that condition with broad material inducement. The chanyu's reported willingness to send his son to Chang'an creates an apparent breakthrough, although the episode ends before implementation and cannot establish a completed settlement.

## Key Characteristics

- Frontier-raised envoy selected for familiarity with Xiongnu culture and audience practice.
- Willing to relax symbolic protocol when doing so preserves access to the negotiation.
- Keeps Han's substantive demand distinct from the ritual concessions used to deliver it.
- Uses material inducement together with political pressure rather than relying on threat alone.
- Produces a reported promise of a princely mission whose fulfillment remains outside the source.

## Qualifications

The supplied note calls the envoy “王巫”; this page normalizes the name to 王乌 and does not treat the two forms as separate people. The source is a short structured summary rather than a primary-text study. It does not settle Wang Wu's wider biography, the exact legal or ritual meaning of leaving the tally, whether the face marking was permanent, the full terms of the exchange, the chanyu's intent, or whether the proposed princely mission occurred.

## What Changed

- Created the canonical 王乌 entity and normalized the supplied note's “王巫” transcript form.
- Established protocol accommodation plus substantive firmness as his current evidence-backed profile.
