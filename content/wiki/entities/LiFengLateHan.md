# 李封 / Li Feng (late Han)

李封 / Li Feng (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-986-tianyu-qiwang-bixian-lingqi-kuang-lvlvu057-tnz3hal6oodhydvzvbd|Hanji 986]] as a [[LyuBu|吕布]] subordinate stationed with [[XueLanLateHan|薛兰]] around Juye. [[CaoCao|曹操]] kills both men in the [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] campaign, after which Lü Bu moves to rescue them and is defeated by ambush.

The page is source-scoped. Li Feng is not developed as an independent biography in the episode; he marks the local commander layer whose loss helps Cao Cao regain initiative before the Yanzhou branch continues in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-987-dingtao-zhizhan-caocao-he-lvbu-dianfeng-duijue-lofq0pq8n9rrfvfttunahisy0ave|Hanji 987]].
