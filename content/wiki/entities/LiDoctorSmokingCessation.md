# 黎医生 / Li Doctor (respiratory and smoking cessation)

## Overview
黎医生 is the respiratory-clinical guest in a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] World No Tobacco Day episode on smoking, vaping, environmental exposure, and cessation support.

## Current Profile
The available source presents 黎医生 as a clinician who encounters chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, asthma, lung lesions, nodules, and airway complaints and asks about smoking history as part of clinical assessment. Her public-education role is to correct harmless-vaping claims, explain why smoke and aerosol exposure affect more than the lungs, and route difficult quit attempts toward qualified cessation clinics, medication assessment, follow-up, and psychological support.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses respiratory cases to make tobacco and vaping risk concrete for lay listeners.
- Treats electronic and combustible cigarettes as different exposure profiles without presenting either as safe.
- Extends exposure assessment from the smoker to household members, bystanders, clothing, and indoor environments.
- Frames cessation as a supported process involving behavior, medication boundaries, follow-up, and relapse recovery.
