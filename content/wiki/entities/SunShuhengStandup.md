## Overview
孙舒恒 / Sun Shuheng appears as a panel participant in a [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] and [[SixiaoComedy|四孝喜剧]] live episode on relationship thought experiments involving loyalty drugs, perfect AI partners, pain-sharing, infidelity-memory deletion, and romantic compatibility scores.

## Current Profile
Sun Shuheng's current wiki role is the strongest anti-overdefinition voice in the panel. Across the thought experiments, he repeatedly resists technologies that make intimacy too certain, too transparent, too painless, or too score-driven.

His answers are not simply anti-technology. They form a consistent stance about life narrative and emotional opacity: romance needs ambiguous possibility, painful experience cannot be cleanly erased without generating other pain, and an external system should not tell him that an existing partner is only a 70% match. He is also one of the clearest voices for rejecting an AI romantic partner because knowing the partner is artificial blocks the experience for him.

## Key Characteristics
- Panel voice resisting technological certainty inside intimate relationships.
- Treats ambiguity and unscored possibility as part of what makes romance livable.
- Rejects pain-sharing and memory-deletion mechanisms because they can alter life logic rather than only reduce suffering.
- Uses the AI partner and compatibility-score examples to defend human authenticity and narrative self-determination.
