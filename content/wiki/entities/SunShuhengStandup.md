## Overview
孙舒恒 / Sun Shuheng appears as a panel participant in [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] live episodes on relationship thought experiments and emotional stability. The current sources show him resisting overdetermined intimacy in one panel and preferring immediate emotional settlement in another.

## Current Profile
Sun Shuheng's current wiki role is the strongest anti-overdefinition voice in the panel. Across the thought experiments, he repeatedly resists technologies that make intimacy too certain, too transparent, too painless, or too score-driven.

His answers are not simply anti-technology. They form a consistent stance about life narrative and emotional opacity: romance needs ambiguous possibility, painful experience cannot be cleanly erased without generating other pain, and an external system should not tell him that an existing partner is only a 70% match. He is also one of the clearest voices for rejecting an AI romantic partner because knowing the partner is artificial blocks the experience for him.

His emotional-stability role is less abstract. Sun says that in work he prefers to respond on the spot rather than leave emotional residue to build up. He also appears in the creator-misreading branch, where comedians discuss how public posts or routines can be read in a way that completely misses the intended joke.

The “折腾” conversation adds an experimentation and environmental-reset branch. Sun describes operating a small braised-food stall whose better ingredients did not create a differentiated product, attempting music without presenting it as mastered craft, and moving fourteen times in twelve years. Folding furniture and unfurnished rentals turn relocation into [[MovingAsLifeReset|a repeatable sorting and restart system]], while the failed stall keeps [[CostlyLifeExperimentation]] separate from guaranteed success.

## Key Characteristics
- Panel voice resisting technological certainty inside intimate relationships.
- Treats ambiguity and unscored possibility as part of what makes romance livable.
- Rejects pain-sharing and memory-deletion mechanisms because they can alter life logic rather than only reduce suffering.
- Uses the AI partner and compatibility-score examples to defend human authenticity and narrative self-determination.
- Prefers immediate response when he feels wronged at work, treating delayed resentment as its own emotional cost.
- Treats small ventures, awkward creative attempts, and repeated relocation as experiments rather than proof of mastery.
