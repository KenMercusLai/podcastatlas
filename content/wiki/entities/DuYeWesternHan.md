## Overview

杜邺 / Du Ye is synthesized as a [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] adviser who appears in two different registers. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-533-zhichang-zui-wujie-de-siju-zhende-wujie-ma-lv64fxua_avw1toxw_e88ty1xru7|Hanji 533]] shows him earlier as a palace gentleman and private friend who mediates between [[WangYinWesternHan|王音]] and [[WangShangWesternHan|王商]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-593-zhenzheng-lihai-de-ren-zheyang-shuohua-luigzfyq6rtua0neci9jqi8ybxqq|Hanji 593]] later previews him as another critic in the Dong Xian favor crisis; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-594-ni-shenbian-you-yi-ziwo-wei-zhongxin-de-ren-ma-lo27wnfpchgxpzvstea4azmxsga2|Hanji 594]] supplies his actual memorial, where he uses omens, ritual hierarchy, filial precedent, and historical examples to criticize dowager-linked and outer-relative power under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]].

## Current Profile

Du Ye's current profile is no longer only a late-Aidi remonstrance preview. Hanji 533 backfills an earlier interpersonal-political skill: when Chengdi's empowerment of Wang Shang threatens to divide Wang-family authority from Wang Yin, Du Ye speaks privately to Wang Yin. He uses kinship, reputation, and historical precedent to argue that resentment between relatives would damage both political order and family standing. His practical advice is simple but demanding: consult Wang Shang sincerely on public affairs.

Hanji 594 presents a later and more formal version of the same ability to speak into hierarchy. Du Ye answers Aidi's invitation for ministers and worthies to state imperial faults after disasters. His argument accepts the court's omen vocabulary: solar eclipses and earthquakes are read as Heaven warning that yin is overpowering yang and that political hierarchy is out of balance.

The memorial's operative target is not abstract cosmology alone. Du Ye uses Zheng Bo, King Xiang of Zhou, Empress Lu, Zeng Shen, Min Ziqian, and filial-obedience stories to tell Aidi that respect for mother-side authority cannot justify blind political compliance. He then points to relatives holding palace, guard, and military posts without regard to merit, and treats the appointment of two Da Sima generals on an eclipse day as a sign that kinship favor has become an office-design and command-risk problem.

Hanji 594 also makes Du Ye a case of failed or uncertain advice conversion. The source does not record Aidi's direct response, and the host reads Aidi's later consultation with [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] as a search for a more acceptable answer. Du Ye therefore sits beside [[WangJiaWesternHan|王嘉]] as a late-Aidi critic whose diagnosis reaches the throne but does not visibly repair the favorite and outer-relative structure. Across the two sources, Du Ye's successful private mediation contrasts with his uncertain public remonstrance: advice can convert when the listener can still protect face and relationship, but fail when it threatens the ruler's deeper attachments and power arrangements.

## Key Characteristics

- Palace gentleman and Wang Yin friend who appears earlier as a Wang-family relationship mediator.
- Former Liangzhou inspector who appears later as a late-Aidi remonstrant.
- Uses [[OmenCodedRemonstrance|天象灾异包装式进谏]] to turn eclipses and earthquakes into political warning.
- Frames filial obedience as bounded by right order, not as blind compliance with dowager or parental wishes.
- Criticizes outer-relative and consort-side concentration in palace, guard, and military posts.
- Treats two simultaneous Da Sima appointments on an eclipse day as a dangerous office-design signal.
- Shows both successful advice conversion in private mediation and nonconversion when advice reaches Aidi without clear correction.

## Qualifications

Hanji 533 and Hanji 594 supply two slices of Du Ye rather than a full biography. Hanji 533 does not settle his complete early career, office history, or long-term relationship with Wang Yin and Wang Shang. Hanji 594 supplies Du Ye's memorial argument but still does not settle his exact later treatment or the original text beyond the source's paraphrase. The source's yin-yang and gender hierarchy are recorded as the memorial's period vocabulary for criticizing court power; they should not be flattened into a modern gender theory claim. Aidi's inner response and Kong Guang's actual answer remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 533's earlier private-mediator role between Wang Yin and Wang Shang.
- Reframed Du Ye as an adviser whose private relationship repair converts more clearly than his later Aidi-era remonstrance.
