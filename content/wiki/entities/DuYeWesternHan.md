## Overview

杜邺 / Du Ye is synthesized as a late [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] former Liangzhou inspector and remonstrant under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-593-zhenzheng-lihai-de-ren-zheyang-shuohua-luigzfyq6rtua0neci9jqi8ybxqq|Hanji 593]] previewed him as another critic in the Dong Xian favor crisis; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-594-ni-shenbian-you-yi-ziwo-wei-zhongxin-de-ren-ma-lo27wnfpchgxpzvstea4azmxsga2|Hanji 594]] supplies his actual memorial, where he uses omens, ritual hierarchy, filial precedent, and historical examples to criticize dowager-linked and outer-relative power.

## Current Profile

Du Ye's current profile is no longer only a preview. Hanji 594 presents him answering Aidi's invitation for ministers and worthies to state imperial faults after disasters. His argument accepts the court's omen vocabulary: solar eclipses and earthquakes are read as Heaven warning that yin is overpowering yang and that political hierarchy is out of balance.

The memorial's operative target is not abstract cosmology alone. Du Ye uses Zheng Bo, King Xiang of Zhou, Empress Lu, Zeng Shen, Min Ziqian, and filial-obedience stories to tell Aidi that respect for mother-side authority cannot justify blind political compliance. He then points to relatives holding palace, guard, and military posts without regard to merit, and treats the appointment of two Da Sima generals on an eclipse day as a sign that kinship favor has become an office-design and command-risk problem.

Hanji 594 also makes Du Ye a case of failed or uncertain advice conversion. The source does not record Aidi's direct response, and the host reads Aidi's later consultation with [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] as a search for a more acceptable answer. Du Ye therefore sits beside [[WangJiaWesternHan|王嘉]] as a late-Aidi critic whose diagnosis reaches the throne but does not visibly repair the favorite and outer-relative structure.

## Key Characteristics

- Former Liangzhou inspector who appears as a late-Aidi remonstrant.
- Uses [[OmenCodedRemonstrance|天象灾异包装式进谏]] to turn eclipses and earthquakes into political warning.
- Frames filial obedience as bounded by right order, not as blind compliance with dowager or parental wishes.
- Criticizes outer-relative and consort-side concentration in palace, guard, and military posts.
- Treats two simultaneous Da Sima appointments on an eclipse day as a dangerous office-design signal.
- Functions as a nonconversion case because his advice reaches Aidi without clear evidence of correction.

## Qualifications

Hanji 594 supplies Du Ye's memorial argument but still does not settle his full career, exact later treatment, or the original text beyond the source's paraphrase. The source's yin-yang and gender hierarchy are recorded as the memorial's period vocabulary for criticizing court power; they should not be flattened into a modern gender theory claim. Aidi's inner response and Kong Guang's actual answer remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Replaced the Hanji 593 preview-only profile with Hanji 594's full memorial role.
- Added Du Ye's omen, filial-precedent, and outer-relative office critique.
- Reframed Du Ye as a corrective-advice nonconversion case under Aidi.
