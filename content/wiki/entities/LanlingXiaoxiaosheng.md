# 兰陵笑笑生 / Lanling Xiaoxiaosheng

兰陵笑笑生 is the unresolved authorial name attached to [[JinPingMei|《金瓶梅》]]. [[fanwai-15-banlatte-jiang-jinpingmei-la-erqie-haishi-luse-de-991508433]] treats the identity question as still open, naming competing attributions such as 王世贞, 屠隆, 徐渭, and 李开先 without settling them.

The source's more durable claim is contextual rather than biographical. The author was likely a Wanli-era literatus who knew Shandong local life, officialdom, household operations, and ordinary urban detail well enough to turn them into [[UrbanEverydayFiction]] and [[FictionAsHistoricalEvidence]]. That makes the pseudonym part of [[ClassicReadingComplexity]]: uncertain authorship should sharpen attention to textual texture rather than invite overconfident decoding.
