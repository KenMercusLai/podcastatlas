# Century Huatong / 世纪华通

Century Huatong / 世纪华通 appears in [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]] as the later A-share vehicle that took over [[ShandaGames|盛大游戏]]. The episode describes a complex acquisition and restructuring path that eventually made the old Shanda game business part of Century Huatong's public-company structure.

The source uses Century Huatong to show the capital-market afterlife of [[Shanda|盛大]]'s game assets. By this point, the story is no longer founder-led internet expansion but a combination of [[GameIPLongTail]], listed-company restructuring, and continued Legend nostalgia products.
