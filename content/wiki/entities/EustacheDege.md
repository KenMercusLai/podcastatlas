# Eustache Degé

## Overview

Eustache Degé is the valet whom [[411-the-man-in-the-iron-mask-glt3787137343]] identifies as the most likely historical [[ManInTheIronMask]].

## Current Profile

Letters from [[LouisXIV]] and Louvois to [[SaintMars]], together with a 1669 lettre de cachet, name Degé and order his strict isolation after arrest at Calais. The episode tracks him through Pignerol, Exilés, Sainte-Marguerite, and the Bastille until the masked prisoner's death in 1703. His status as a valet and his service to Nicolas Fouquet in prison make royal or aristocratic identities implausible, but the reason he was considered dangerous remains unknown.

## Key Characteristics

- Valet rather than prince, noble, or acknowledged political leader.
- Arrested at Calais in 1669 under an order requiring unusually strict isolation.
- Chronologically traceable through the same prison sequence as the masked prisoner.
- Defined more securely by archival continuity than by any proven offense or secret.
