# Joe Namath

Joe Namath appears in [[the-nfl-1]] as the celebrity quarterback who helped broaden the [[AmericanFootballLeague|AFL]]'s appeal and validate the merged professional-football product. The episode says his signing escalated football's talent competition, while his public persona made football players visible as mass-media figures.

Namath's guarantee and the Jets' upset in [[SuperBowl|Super Bowl III]] matter in the source because they changed perception. The AFL no longer looked like an inferior league, making the merger more compelling and strengthening the NFL's combined [[SportsEntertainmentFlywheel]].
