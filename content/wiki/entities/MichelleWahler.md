# Michelle Wahler

## Overview
Michelle Wahler is the co-founder and former CEO of [[BeyondYoga]] and the [[HowIBuiltThis]] Advice Line guest in [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]]. The episode presents her as a bootstrapped apparel operator who spent roughly a decade building a women's yoga brand through wholesalers, studios, and department stores before an acquisition, and who now advises founders rather than operating a company.

## Current Profile
Wahler's advice is built from the pre-acquisition build rather than from the exit. She tells founders they do not have to raise capital, that they should not start with an exit plan, and that a small business which sustains the founder is a complete outcome; in her framing, outside investors make the exit the focus and redirect the company. She now spends most of her time advising founders, listening to their problems, and connecting them with people who understand web development, marketing, or inventory — work she describes as matchmaking. Her current activity sits in investing, mentorship, and teaching rather than apparel operations.

## Key Characteristics
- Bootstrapped consumer-brand founder: built [[BeyondYoga]] slowly through wholesale, yoga studios, and department stores rather than through venture funding.
- Explicitly anti-default fundraising: rejects "you must raise" advice and treats external capital as a choice that changes the company's objective.
- Focus discipline: describes chasing adjacent ideas as a founder weakness and says what you refuse matters more than what you accept.
- Relationship repair and service reliability: pushes founders to fix operations before asking clients for a second chance.
- Post-exit connector: uses [[QuinceHill]] and personal advice to match founders with specialists rather than to run their businesses.
- Educator: teaches a culture-building course at the [[UniversityOfSouthernCalifornia|University of Southern California]].
