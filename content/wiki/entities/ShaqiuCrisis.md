# 沙丘之变 / Shaqiu Crisis

沙丘之变 / Shaqiu Crisis is the Zhao succession disaster foreshadowed in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-66-zhongguo-lishishang-naxie-diwang-shi-bei-huohuo-e-si-de-1-lvl1uxjadiedqwad-skxrwf-usnq]]. This episode stops at the setup: [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] brings [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] and [[ZhaoZhang|赵章]] to Shaqiu after deciding that Handan is too controlled by Zhao He's side for his power-recovery plan.

The event page should remain source-scoped for now. The episode title points toward the later starvation death associated with Zhao Wuling Wang, but this source does not yet narrate the death itself. Its contribution is the prelude: rival sons, a retained-authority father, officials guarding against forged summons, and an elder branch made dangerous by territory and advisers.

In the wiki, Shaqiu Crisis links the earlier [[AffectionDrivenSuccessionRisk|favored-son succession risk]] to the more specific [[PartialAbdicationPowerSplit|partial-abdication power split]]. The crisis is not framed as a sudden accident; it is prepared by the failure to cleanly separate family status, formal kingship, military authority, and ministerial loyalty.
