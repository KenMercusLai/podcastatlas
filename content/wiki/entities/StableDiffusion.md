# Stable Diffusion

Stable Diffusion appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-open-source-wins-agi-is-here-and-scorseses-ai-toolkit-with-ceos-of-cerebras-black-forest-labs-42029880]] as the prior model lineage behind [[BlackForestLabs|Black Forest Labs]]. [[RobinRombach|Robin Rombach]] says he and his co-founders worked on Stable Diffusion and invented [[LatentDiffusion|latent diffusion]], which compressed natural data into a more efficient space before model training.

In this wiki, Stable Diffusion matters as a predecessor to newer visual and multimodal model work. The episode links it to [[Flux]], generative-image control layers, video, audio, action prediction, and future [[WorldModels|world-model]] directions.
