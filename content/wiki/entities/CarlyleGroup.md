# Carlyle Group / 凯雷资本

Carlyle Group / 凯雷资本 appears in [[145-gaijia-zhongzi-de-canyin-yangpinpai-feng-tou-quan-1-145-1]] as the minority private-equity investor in the 2017 [[McDonalds]] China transaction alongside [[CITICCapital]]. The source says Carlyle later exited when McDonald's global increased its China stake, and the hosts infer that the exit return implied McDonald's global may have underestimated the China business in 2017.

The page is source-scoped to Carlyle's role in this restaurant-control case. It helps the wiki distinguish local operating control from ordinary financial participation: Carlyle appears as a financial co-investor, while CITIC remains the controlling local shareholder in the source's telling.
