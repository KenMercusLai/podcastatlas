# Marshall McLuhan / 麦克卢汉

Marshall McLuhan / 麦克卢汉 appears in [[159-suanfa-de-liufu-miankong-ta-shi-ruhe-cong-chuli-shuju-biancheng-dingyi-women-shi-shui-de-lh5ng5mbvkworfxlq9l-coscepc7]] as the media-theory bridge for understanding algorithms. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] invokes the "medium is the message" line to argue that algorithms are not merely channels for content; they reshape the scale, speed, pattern, and social meaning of connection.

In the source, McLuhan helps move algorithms away from the narrow question of software performance. If the medium itself changes perception and social organization, then [[PublicRelevanceAlgorithms]] should be analyzed like media forms: they alter what people can see, how quickly they react, and which publics can become legible.
