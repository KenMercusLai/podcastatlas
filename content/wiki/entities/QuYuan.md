# 屈原 / Qu Yuan

屈原 / Qu Yuan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-51-chuhuaiwang-qin-xiao-er-kan-jiujiu-ruhe-mie-le-ni-ltfkdlivomnvux5xodh2rlfqf6ac]]. The source presents him as a firm anti-Qin voice in the [[ChuState|楚国]] court whom [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] and aligned Chu elites help slander before the [[Shangyu|商於]] deception reaches its military sequel.

The episode uses Qu Yuan to explain why [[ChenZhenWarringStates|陈枕]]'s warnings fail to carry enough political weight. If Qu Yuan is pushed out of court and Zhang Yi has already influenced ministers and favored palace figures, then [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] hears correct advice from a weakened minority position. The source also notes that Qu Yuan later writes 《离骚》, but does not analyze the work.
