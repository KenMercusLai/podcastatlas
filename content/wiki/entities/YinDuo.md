# 尹铎 / Yin Duo

## Overview
Yin Duo is the Zhao administrator credited with turning [[Jinyang|Jinyang]] from a revenue source into the prepared fallback that later shelters [[ZhaoXiangzi|Zhao Wuxu]].

## Current Profile
Before taking office, Yin Duo asks [[ZhaoJianzi|Zhao Jianzi]] whether he should extract wealth or build a refuge. Given the second mandate, he reduces burdens and strengthens defenses. The later siege narrative treats those ordinary administrative choices as accumulated resilience: Jinyang remains the viable destination when Zhao's other bases have exhausted or alienated their populations.

## Key Characteristics
- Clarifies the strategic purpose of an office before choosing policy.
- Trades immediate extraction for long-term loyalty and defensibility.
- Builds resilience before the timing and form of crisis are known.
- Connects local administration to lineage-level survival planning.

## Evidence
- Mandate and implementation: the episode has Yin Duo ask whether Jinyang is to be harvested or preserved, then lower burdens and repair defenses after Zhao Jianzi chooses refuge. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-02-jiangyu-baizhi-bi-gufuzhi-lglk2_eu1uvegdac8sj6bfuwv1dw|Zhouji 02]]
