# 董扶 / Dong Fu (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-939-donghan-luanju-de-fengkuang-sanguo-xuqu-lthmvw8ihk96fuez9vamlryzcngy|Hanji 939]] introduces 董扶 / Dong Fu as the shizhong who privately redirects [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s provincial plan. Liu Yan first wants the remote Jiaozhi post to escape central disorder, but Dong Fu says the capital will become chaotic and that [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] has imperial qi, making Yizhou the desired destination.

The episode then says Dong Fu follows Liu Yan into Yizhou, along with [[ZhaoWeiLateHan|赵韪]]. Dong Fu is therefore not only a prophecy voice; he is part of the early entourage that makes Liu Yan's provincial move look like a deliberate political relocation under [[ProvincialShepherdAutonomy|州牧集权与地方割据]].
