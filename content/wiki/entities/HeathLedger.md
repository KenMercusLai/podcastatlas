# Heath Ledger

## Overview

Heath Ledger is an actor represented here through [[StephenGaghan]]'s account of imagining him as Teddy in [[BlinkFilm|Blink]] and of losing the emotional capacity to continue the project after Ledger's death.

## Current Profile

Gaghan describes an immediate personal and creative connection with Ledger and expected that they would make several films together. As revisions made Teddy younger, Ledger became the actor through whom Gaghan imagined the role, while the studio reportedly became more receptive as Ledger's commercial standing rose.

The source does not establish a formal commitment. Its strongest evidence is relational: a copy of the screenplay bearing Gaghan's number and Gladwell's book were reportedly found near Ledger's bed, after which Ledger's father and a professional associate called Gaghan. The call and death made *Blink* profoundly upsetting for Gaghan and transformed a developed screenplay into work he could not then continue.

## Key Characteristics

- Actor who became central to Gaghan's revised imaginative conception of Teddy.
- Prospective collaborator associated with a hoped-for series of films rather than a single transaction.
- Rising commercial stature reportedly changed studio resistance to the casting shift.
- Death became a major emotional discontinuity in the project's development.

## Qualifications

This profile is based on Gaghan's retrospective account as summarized in a non-verbatim source. It does not include Ledger's testimony, a signed agreement, family records, studio documents, or independent confirmation of the objects and call. Interest should not be converted into formal attachment, and Ledger's death should not be treated as the sole cause of nonproduction.
