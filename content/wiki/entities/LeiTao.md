# 雷涛 / 无二旅人 / Lei Tao

雷涛, also known on social media as 无二旅人, is the travel columnist and long-term traveler in [[ep265-dang-qing3xiu13-chongshang-resou-women-ruhe-gei-rensheng-fang-yige-changjia-lsloczlnkmcrrfpn9coh8rrqo-wb]]. Because travel is also part of his work, he does not experience [[VacationShame|vacation shame]] in the same way as people who must separate ordinary employment from leave.

His main contribution is to make the long-holiday argument concrete through [[Canada]]. He describes routes and scenes around [[BritishColumbia|British Columbia]], [[Vancouver]], [[Whistler]], [[BanffNationalPark|Banff]], Revelstoke, northern lights, seafood, Indigenous-guided walking, cycling food tours, and small-town festivals.

The wiki relevance is not only itinerary advice. Lei Tao's examples show [[TravelSensoryRecovery|travel sensory recovery]] and [[LocalLifeDensityTravel|local-life density travel]]: a useful vacation can come from walking, eating, waiting for sunset, entering public art spaces, or following a local friend to an ordinary childhood place.
