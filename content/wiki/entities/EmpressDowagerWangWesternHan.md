## Overview

王太后（王娡） / Empress Dowager Wang is [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s mother and the maternal authority whose support for her brother [[TianFenWesternHan|田蚡]] shapes the emperor's room for maneuver in the Dou Ying-Guan Fu case.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-265-dongchaotingbian-ruhe-yanbian-cheng-jieduandazhan-2-lvu23osgv46nn3rbgque1mbhmpgw|Hanji 265]] represents Empress Dowager Wang primarily through political backing rather than direct speech or office. Tian Fen's access to her support makes intervention for [[DouYingWesternHan|窦婴]] costly for a Han Wudi whom the episode describes as not yet fully controlling court politics.

The same source says Tian Fen's later death removes her principal political helper and leads her to withdraw completely from politics. That consequence is useful for the episode's centralization narrative, but the source does not document the precise institutional form, timing, or completeness of her political retreat.

## Key Characteristics

- Mother of Han Wudi and senior maternal authority at court.
- Sister and political protector of Tian Fen in the episode's account.
- Her support changes the relative vulnerability of Tian Fen and Dou Ying.
- Reportedly loses her principal political helper when Tian Fen dies.

## Evidence

- Maternal leverage: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-265-dongchaotingbian-ruhe-yanbian-cheng-jieduandazhan-2-lvu23osgv46nn3rbgque1mbhmpgw|Hanji 265]] says Han Wudi would risk conflict with both his mother and uncle by defending Dou Ying.
- Tian Fen protection: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-265-dongchaotingbian-ruhe-yanbian-cheng-jieduandazhan-2-lvu23osgv46nn3rbgque1mbhmpgw|Hanji 265]] contrasts Tian Fen's backing from Empress Dowager Wang with Dou Ying's loss of Empress Dowager Dou.
- Political retreat: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-265-dongchaotingbian-ruhe-yanbian-cheng-jieduandazhan-2-lvu23osgv46nn3rbgque1mbhmpgw|Hanji 265]] links Tian Fen's death to Wang's withdrawal from political life.

## Qualifications

The page is grounded in one compact episode summary and does not supply Wang Zhi's wider biography, her own stated view of the case, a direct record of pressure on Han Wudi, or an institutional account of her influence. “Complete withdrawal” is therefore kept as the episode's characterization rather than an independently established endpoint.
