# 韩信 / Han Xin

韩信 / Han Xin enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-144-xiangyu-jituan-fumie-de-yuanyin-2-lut3jiatchla3pfxpkznsrs7wkzu|Hanji 144 part 2]] as one representative of the disappointed people who follow [[LiuBang|刘邦]] into [[Hanzhong|汉中]]. The episode has not yet developed his later command career; it uses his appearance to show that Xiang Yu's settlement leaves capable but unrewarded or under-placed people searching for another patron.

His source role therefore belongs to the transition from forced retreat to latent opportunity. Liu Bang's move toward Hanzhong looks like confinement under [[ThreeQinContainment|三秦压制]], but the migrating personnel field means the same retreat can absorb future talent while [[XiangYu|项羽]] is pulled east by [[TianRong|田荣]], [[PengYue|彭越]], and [[ChenYu|陈馀]].
