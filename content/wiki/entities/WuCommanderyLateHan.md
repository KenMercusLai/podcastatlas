# 吴郡 / Wu Commandery (late Han)

## Overview
吴郡 / Wu Commandery is a late-Han Jiangdong commandery that Hanji 1045 treats as both part of [[SunCe|孙策]]'s territorial base and the place where [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] remains after Sun Ce's death. It is a transition setting for early Sun-family rule, not a generic page for the older state of Wu.

## Current Profile
Wu Commandery appears as a central node in the post-Sun Ce handoff. The source places Zhou Yu there as central guard alongside [[ZhangZhaoLateHan|张昭]], making the commandery a site where mourning, military custody, and political continuity overlap.

## Key Characteristics
- Wu Commandery is named as one of the six commanderies Sun Ce holds at death.
- It functions as a practical center for the early [[SunQuan|孙权]] transition.
- Zhou Yu remains in Wu Commandery rather than simply returning home after mourning Sun Ce.
- The page is scoped to late-Han Jiangdong and remains distinct from broader [[Wuyue|吴越]] geography.

## Evidence
- Territorial base: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1045-ta-shi-jiangdong-baye-de-kaichuangzhe-kexi-si-de-tai-wonang-lgjjdmcfw1qkuba2xblgvvlfpy9d|Hanji 1045]] lists Wu Commandery among the commanderies Sun Ce controls before the succession handoff.
- Transition center: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1045-ta-shi-jiangdong-baye-de-kaichuangzhe-kexi-si-de-tai-wonang-lgjjdmcfw1qkuba2xblgvvlfpy9d|Hanji 1045]] has Zhou Yu return from Baqiu with troops and remain in Wu Commandery as central guard with Zhang Zhao.

## Qualifications
The current page is source-scoped to the Sun Ce-Sun Quan transition. It does not yet synthesize administrative history, borders, or later Eastern Wu developments beyond what Hanji 1045 contributes.

## What Changed
