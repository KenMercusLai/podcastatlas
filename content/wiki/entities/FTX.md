# FTX

## Overview

FTX was a cryptocurrency exchange whose withdrawal crisis, bankruptcy, and founder's fraud conviction made it a central case in custody, reserve, governance, and credibility failure.

## Current Profile

[[socialradarspod-brianarmstrong-final]] presents FTX as a reputational shock to the crypto industry and a trust contrast with [[Coinbase]]. In [[BrianArmstrong]]'s account, a fully reserved exchange should not face a traditional bank-run dynamic; FTX therefore illustrates why public status and apparent growth cannot replace evidence about customer assets, related-party exposure, and financial controls.

The collapse sequence centers on a leaked Alameda Research balance sheet showing heavy dependence on illiquid tokens created by [[SamBankmanFried]], which triggered withdrawals FTX could not meet, failed fundraising, and bankruptcy. Bankman-Fried's continuing claim that more time could have saved the company is retained as a contested defense rather than the current profile's conclusion. [[dunes-and-gloom-mbss-annus-horribilis-0a44ecf1bba6689b3b1e02af9ca13b2c]]

## Key Characteristics

- Crypto exchange whose rapid growth and elite credibility concealed severe trust and governance risk.
- Closely exposed to related trading firm Alameda Research and illiquid affiliated tokens.
- Unable to meet a rush of customer withdrawals after balance-sheet concerns became public.
- Failure that damaged confidence in the wider cryptocurrency industry.
- Subject of a continuing founder narrative that liquidity and time, rather than fraud, caused the collapse.

## Evidence

- **Industry and trust effect:** [[socialradarspod-brianarmstrong-final]] describes FTX as giving crypto a reputational "black eye" and contrasts its failure with one-to-one custody.
- **Warning signals:** [[socialradarspod-brianarmstrong-final]] records rapid founder status and spending that appeared difficult to reconcile with the apparent business.
- **Collapse mechanism:** [[dunes-and-gloom-mbss-annus-horribilis-0a44ecf1bba6689b3b1e02af9ca13b2c]] links the leaked Alameda balance sheet to withdrawals, failed fundraising, and bankruptcy.
- **Legal outcome and dispute:** [[dunes-and-gloom-mbss-annus-horribilis-0a44ecf1bba6689b3b1e02af9ca13b2c]] reports Bankman-Fried's fraud conviction and his continuing denial.

## Qualifications

The sources summarize rather than audit FTX's books, bankruptcy estate, customer recoveries, or trial evidence. Armstrong supplies a competitor's participant perspective, and Bankman-Fried's survival claim is a convicted founder's defense. The distinction between liquidity, insolvency, misuse of customer assets, and eventual recovery value should not be collapsed into a single balance-sheet claim.
