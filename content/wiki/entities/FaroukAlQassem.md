# Farouk Al Qassem

Farouk Al Qassem appears in [[how-to-beat-the-resource-curse-in-norway-summer-school]] as the Iraqi geologist whose expertise helped [[Norway]] recognize and prepare for offshore oil. The episode says he moved to Norway in the 1960s for family medical reasons, then reviewed oil-company drilling data for the Ministry of Industry.

His source role is pivotal because he identified signs that Norway had already found oil and helped develop recommendations before the [[EkofiskOilField|Ekofisk]] discovery made the boom undeniable. The episode uses him to show how expert knowledge can matter most when a state is able to absorb it early.
