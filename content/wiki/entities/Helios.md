# Helios

## Overview

Helios is the Sun god whose sacred cattle become the final taboo that Odysseus' crew violates before the ship is destroyed.

## Current Profile

In [[687-the-odyssey-hero-of-the-trojan-horse-part-1-glt2393125423]], [[Tiresias]] and Circe both warn [[Odysseus]] not to harm Helios' cattle on Thrinacia. After a divine storm traps the crew and food runs out, Odysseus sleeps while the men slaughter the cattle. Helios complains to Zeus, and Zeus destroys the ship, leaving Odysseus the only survivor.

## Key Characteristics

- Helios' cattle make divine property and cosmic order part of the voyage's survival terms.
- The cattle taboo is repeatedly warned about, so the violation is framed as disobedience under hunger rather than ignorance.
- Odysseus fails to prevent the violation because he is absent and asleep when the crew acts.
- The punishment completes the loss pattern by destroying the ship and every remaining crew member except Odysseus.
