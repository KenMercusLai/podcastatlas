# Mayerling Incident

## Overview

The Mayerling Incident is the January 1889 death of [[CrownPrinceRudolf]] and [[MaryVetsera]] at Rudolf's hunting lodge.

## Current Profile

The episode treats Mayerling as a private catastrophe that immediately becomes a dynastic, Catholic, media, and succession crisis. The event begins in clandestine meetings and death-pact language, passes through a locked-room discovery, and then becomes a struggle over burial, public reporting, Mary's erasure, and Habsburg succession.

## Key Characteristics

- Mayerling is framed as both gothic melodrama and court-political crisis.
- The immediate event is reconstructed through hunting routines, hidden presence, valet discovery, and reported gunshot wounds.
- The aftermath centers on Catholic burial for Rudolf and suppression of Mary.
- The episode treats conspiracy theories as evidence of unsettled memory rather than as established explanation.
- The succession consequences push the imperial line toward [[KarlLudwigOfAustria]] and [[FranzFerdinand]].

## Qualifications

The episode reconstructs a famous scandal from letters, testimony, newspapers, funeral ritual, and retrospective accounts. It explicitly keeps coercion, consent, embellishment, and conspiracy claims uncertain.
