# Longhua Airport / 龙华机场

## Overview
Longhua Airport / 龙华机场 is the former Shanghai airport site used as the physical setting and historical cue for the Shengdong Jixi aviation roundtable.

## Current Profile
The source presents Longhua Airport as a place where aviation history and urban redevelopment overlap. Xu Tao identifies the live-event location as Shanghai West Bund, the former Longhua Airport area, and ties the airport name to nearby Longhua Temple.

Longhua's function in the episode is not only archival. It lets the speakers contrast early aircraft assembly, repair, storage, and multi-stop international routes with today's routine long-haul travel, making the airport a local reminder that modern aviation depends on decades of infrastructure, technology, and city-space change.

## Key Characteristics
- Former Shanghai aviation site used as the roundtable's place-based opening.
- Historical cue for early aircraft operations before contemporary airport systems.
- Example of airport infrastructure later becoming urban memory and redevelopment context.
