# Meta Muse Glimmer / Muse Spark

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-more-trillion-dollar-ipos-anthropic-3t-zucks-price-war-china-ends-open-source-trump-accounts-42041390]] adds the All-In price-war reading. The hosts refer to "MuSpark 1.1" pricing and use it as evidence that [[Meta]] can pressure [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]] by making capable model access much cheaper, even if exact naming and release details remain source-scoped.

Meta Muse Glimmer and Muse Spark appear in [[xingbake-huiying-mixue-bingcheng-daigong-deng-chuanwen-li-ning-fouren-yu-mubapei-qianyue-1006054195]] as [[Meta]] model releases used to signal a renewed open-weight posture. The episode says Meta released Muse Glimmer weights on 2026-08-10 and said Muse Spark 1.2 weights would follow in the coming weeks.

The source frames the releases against Meta's earlier [[Llama]] reputation and the disappointing reception of Llama 4. Muse therefore becomes less a standalone model story than a sign of how large AI labs use open weights to repair ecosystem confidence, lower adoption friction, and argue against concentrated AI control.
