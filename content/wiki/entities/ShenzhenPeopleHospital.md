# Shenzhen People's Hospital / 深圳市人民医院

## Overview
Shenzhen People's Hospital / 深圳市人民医院 is the hospital affiliation named for [[LvDoctorENT|耳鼻喉吕博士]] in the VOL.208 [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode on headphone-heavy use and ear health, and for [[DongXintong|董心彤]] in the VOL.32 and VOL.33 episodes on anesthesia and perioperative safety.

## Current Profile
The current wiki profile is narrow and source-scoped. The sources do not provide an institutional history, department structure, or independent hospital profile. They use the hospital mainly to ground specialist guest authority: 吕博士 is introduced from its ENT context and explains earphone-related external ear-canal problems, hearing risk, tinnitus, sudden hearing-loss warning signs, and practical care boundaries, while Dong Xintong is introduced from its anesthesiology context and explains perioperative safety, drug misconceptions, operating-room physiology, and postoperative recovery boundaries.

The new preoperative anesthesia source keeps the same boundary. It names the hospital as Dong's affiliation while the substantive evidence concerns [[PreoperativeAnesthesiaAssessment]], anesthesia clinics, dental and airway preparation, pulse-oximetry reliability, medication review, fasting, infection testing, and blood-pressure judgment. For the wiki, the hospital matters as a source anchor for public medical-literacy episodes, not as a broad healthcare-system node. Future ingests would be needed before making claims about its services, rankings, research, leadership, patient volume, department capacity, or institutional policies.
