# 老钱 / Lao Qian (知行小酒馆)

## Overview
老钱 is a [[ZhixingXiaojiuguan|知行小酒馆]] speaker who interprets household-finance choices through both investment opportunity cost and changing housing-market structure.

## Current Profile
In the bounded evidence, 老钱 retains the practical principle “应取尽取、应用尽用” for housing provident funds, but makes it conditional on a genuine permitted use, local rules, and a comparison of cash-flow and financing consequences. He argues that exceeding the source-reported account yield may be feasible while also accepting that forced saving can be valuable for people who would otherwise spend the withdrawal.

He places the policy changes inside a broader transition from property expansion toward existing-home use, maintenance, and buyer protection. That interpretation does not become a prediction of nationwide price recovery; the source stresses variation across cities, neighborhoods, properties, and households.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects personal-finance decisions to policy and market structure.
- Uses opportunity cost without ignoring behavioral self-control.
- Treats low-cost credit as one input rather than a sufficient reason to buy.
- Favors explicit local-rule checks and scenario calculations before action.
- Avoids a single nationwide answer for differentiated housing markets.
