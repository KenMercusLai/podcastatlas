# 卫龙 / Weilong

## Overview

卫龙 / Weilong is the Luohe-based snack company used in [[no-221-zhongguo-latiao-jianghu-1020016798]] to explain how a low-price regional flour snack became a standardized, marketed, listed consumer brand.

## Current Profile

The episode traces Weilong from Liu Weiping and Liu Fuping's Pingjiang-linked migration into Henan's wheat and food-manufacturing base. Its advantage is presented as more than production scale: standardized naming and packaging created recognition; factory imagery and internet-native campaigns recoded a category associated with dirty workshops; and later product expansion shifted growth toward konjac and other vegetable snacks.

By 2024-2025, the source says vegetable products had overtaken seasoned flour products in revenue share. The current profile is therefore a broader spicy-snack platform whose historic辣条 category remains important but is no longer its main reported growth engine.

## Key Characteristics

- Weilong moved辣条 production into Luohe's stronger wheat, transport, and food-industry ecosystem.
- Early packaging and brand consistency separated it from many undifferentiated small workshops.
- Clean-factory imagery and contrast-driven internet marketing helped challenge the category's low-end sanitation stigma.
- Regulatory conflict exposed Weilong to cross-province additive enforcement even when it said it followed the Henan standard.
- Product mix increasingly favors higher-priced, higher-margin konjac and vegetable snacks over traditional flour-based辣条.
- External financing and a Hong Kong listing made the firm the episode's main capital-market case.

## Evidence

- **Location and brand building:** [[no-221-zhongguo-latiao-jianghu-1020016798]] connects the move to Luohe with wheat supply, transport, food-industry infrastructure, standardized packaging, and trademark registration.
- **Trust repair through media:** [[no-221-zhongguo-latiao-jianghu-1020016798]] describes the 2014 clean-factory publicity and later Apple-style, meme-based, and staged-event marketing campaigns.
- **Regulatory exposure:** [[no-221-zhongguo-latiao-jianghu-1020016798]] uses the 2018 Hubei inspection dispute to show how provincial classifications produced conflicting additive judgments.
- **Portfolio transition:** [[no-221-zhongguo-latiao-jianghu-1020016798]] reports vegetable-product revenue share rising above seasoned flour products by 2024 and further in 2025.
