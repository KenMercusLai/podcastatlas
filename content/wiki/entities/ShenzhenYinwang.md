# Shenzhen Yinwang / 深圳引望

Shenzhen Yinwang / 深圳引望 is the intelligent-vehicle technology company that [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] says was established in January 2024 to receive Huawei intelligent-automotive solution assets and personnel. The source presents it as the supplier-platform counterpart to [[HongmengZhixing|鸿蒙智行]].

Yinwang matters because it clarifies [[HuaweiNoCarManufacturingBoundary|Huawei's no-car-manufacturing boundary]]. Huawei can keep providing assisted driving, intelligent cockpit, vehicle control, cloud, and other systems to a wider set of automakers through Yinwang, while automakers such as [[Seres]] and [[Avatr]] can buy equity stakes and become both customers and strategic participants.

## Source Position
- Yinwang is framed as a way to make Huawei's automotive technology more broadly available beyond the deepest 鸿蒙智行 cooperation.
- The source links Yinwang to Huawei's first annual profitability in intelligent automotive solutions and to fast revenue growth in 2025.
- Its long-term significance is supplier scale: more installations can support engineering amortization, data learning, and bargaining power.
