## Overview

田胜 / Tian Sheng enters the wiki as the late-[[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] favorite whose personal favor does not become durable formal protection. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-868-ying-le-ye-shi-shu-jia-donghan-hougong-zhengyan-ji-lmmjgx23nlkeqr3mkzliij-zwe2v|Hanji 868]] says Huan wanted to make the source's 田圣 empress, but [[YingFengLateHan|应奉]] and [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] blocked the move on status grounds and [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦妙]] became empress instead. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-879-si-zai-nvren-shenshang-de-hunyong-nanren-lqz5-mpi0waa-xmuhjhwkaecjjrc|Hanji 879]] then shows Tian Sheng's continued favor and post-Huan execution by Dou Miao.

## Current Profile

Tian Sheng's profile is a source-scoped palace-favorite case rather than a full biography. Her importance in the wiki is structural: she shows the difference between being loved by the ruler and holding an institutionally protected status. Hanji 868 gives the empress-selection conflict: Huan's preference is strong, but Ying Feng and Chen Fan argue that her background and rank cannot support the empress title. Hanji 879 then presents her as powerful while Huan lives, exposed because she lacks elite lineage and formal rank, and fatally vulnerable once Dou Miao becomes empress dowager.

The source also gives her agency a moralized harem-politics frame, saying she tried to deepen Huan's attachment by bringing in eight other palace women. That detail is useful for the episode's desire-and-rivalry interpretation, but it should not be expanded into a complete account of her motives without further sources.

## Key Characteristics

- Tian Sheng is favored strongly enough that Emperor Huan considers making her empress.
- Ying Feng and Chen Fan block her elevation by turning private favor into a formal-status problem.
- Her low origin prevents favor from becoming secure formal status.
- Her continued favor after Dou Miao's appointment creates a split between affection and rank inside the palace.
- Her protection depends on Huan personally and collapses when he dies childless.
- Her death becomes the source's clearest case of harem rivalry turning into post-patron vulnerability.

## Qualifications

The current evidence comes from two podcast-summary sources. Hanji 868 renders the name as 田圣 while Hanji 879 and the existing route use 田胜; the wiki normalizes them together because the role, ruler, empress-selection conflict, and later outcome match. The page should not infer Tian Sheng's full family background, factional connections, or private motives beyond those source-bounded claims.

## What Changed

- Hanji 868 adds the earlier formal-status objection that blocks Tian Sheng's empress title before Hanji 879's post-Huan collapse.
- Hanji 879 creates Tian Sheng as a canonical late-Han palace-favorite page.
- The page anchors a new distinction between personal favor and formal status protection.
