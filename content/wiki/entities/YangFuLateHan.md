# 杨阜 / Yang Fu (late Han)

## Overview
杨阜 / Yang Fu is a Liangzhou-Guanzhong official figure introduced through the pre-Guandu branch of the wiki. His role is evidentiary rather than biographical: after being sent to [[Xudu|许都]] by Wei Duan, he returns as an observer whose judgment helps Guanzhong commanders read the Cao Cao-Yuan Shao contest.

## Current Profile
Within the current bounded evidence, Yang Fu functions as a strategic assessor. He does not predict victory by counting territory or prestige alone; he compares organizational quality, command tempo, legal discipline, army strength, talent policy, and subordinate commitment. That makes his assessment a Guanzhong-facing version of [[XingshiqingStrategicAssessment|行势情战略评估]] before the [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]].

## Key Characteristics
- Cross-regional observer moving between Guanzhong/Liangzhou politics and Cao Cao's Xudu court.
- Evaluates Yuan Shao as lenient but indecisive, with a core team that lacks determination and misses opportunities.
- Evaluates Cao Cao as decisive, law-disciplined, militarily strong, open in talent use, and able to secure full subordinate effort.
- Makes Cao-Yuan rivalry legible to Guanzhong commanders who are waiting to see which side will prevail.

## Qualifications
The current wiki evidence is limited to this episode's pre-Guandu use of Yang Fu. It does not yet establish his full official career, later military activity, or independent writings. Wei Duan is named as the sender in the source, but the wiki does not yet require a separate Wei Duan page from this bounded input alone.

## What Changed
- Established Yang Fu as a source-bounded late-Han evaluator of Cao Cao and Yuan Shao.
- Connected his judgment to Guanzhong's waiting posture before Guandu rather than treating it as a detached character assessment.
