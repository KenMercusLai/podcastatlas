# Carla Sugirana

Carla Sugirana is the [[TheIntelligence]] speaker in [[working-memory-the-surprising-decline-of-dementia-6a6b0eeca585d3be78cd9d1b]] who explains [[Pix]] as a Brazilian public-payment success and as a target of [[DonaldTrump]]'s tariff pressure. Her segment argues that Pix expanded formal financial access while the trade-discrimination case against it is weak.

The source uses her analysis to separate several issues that can otherwise blur together: lower merchant fees, competition with cash, payment-network business pressure, central-bank governance, data concentration, and whether foreign firms face discriminatory rules.
