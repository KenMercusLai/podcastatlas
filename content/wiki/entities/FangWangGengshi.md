## Overview

方望 / Fang Wang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-675-luanshi-fenzheng-zhizhong-liuxiu-ruhe-jueqi-lrvxu1q4-ysg4kfxkf25hswygfyo|Hanji 675]] as a Jianwu 1 claimant organizer in the unsettled space around [[LiuXuanGengshiEmperor|更始帝刘玄]] and [[LiuXiu|刘秀]]. The source says Fang Wang, formerly a military adviser, works with Anling man Gong Lin to install [[RuziYing|孺子婴 / 刘婴]] as emperor, gathers several thousand followers, occupies Linjing, and is then defeated and killed by Li Song under Liu Xuan's order.

## Current Profile

Fang Wang's profile is currently narrow but useful: he shows that Han restoration legitimacy was not monopolized by one claimant in 25 CE. The source presents his move as a dynastic-symbol play rather than a developed state-building program. Ruzi Ying's lineage gives the group a banner, but the episode provides no evidence that Fang Wang can convert that banner into durable institutions or enough force to survive.

His destruction also clarifies Liu Xuan's still-active coercive capacity before the later Chimei collapse. Even while larger pressures are growing, Liu Xuan can send Li Song to remove a small rival restoration claim. That local success sits beside the same source's Chimei defeats of Su Mao and Li Song, showing how quickly the balance shifts from suppressing fringe claimants to losing against larger armies.

## Key Characteristics

- Jianwu 1 claimant organizer operating in the fragmented post-Xin field.
- Former military adviser whose source profile is defined by political use of a royal descendant.
- Organizer of a Linjing restoration claim around Ruzi Ying.
- Limited-force actor whose several-thousand-person base cannot survive Liu Xuan's suppression.
- Evidence that Han legitimacy remained divisible and contestable before Guangwu consolidation.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 675. Gong Lin, Li Song, Linjing, exact office titles, and the internal decision process remain source-scoped. The page does not infer Fang Wang's earlier career, motives, military organization, or relationship to other Fang Wang figures beyond this source.
