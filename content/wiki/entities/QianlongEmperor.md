# 乾隆 / Qianlong Emperor

乾隆 / Qianlong Emperor enters [[11-yongzheng-wangchao-zhi-dayi-juemi-shijingqian-bi-xia-de-qiangu-qi-an-547265513]] as the ruler who reversed [[YongzhengEmperor|雍正]]'s handling of the [[ZengJing|曾静]] case. Soon after Yongzheng's death, the episode says Qianlong had Zeng Jing and [[ZhangXi|张熙]] arrested again and executed, while also suppressing [[DaiYiJueMiLu|《大义觉迷录》]].

The episode uses Qianlong as a contrast in ruler style. Yongzheng argued with and tried to convert a rural scholar; Qianlong preferred erasure, punishment, and restored control. The source does not present either strategy as finally successful: suppression could make the banned book seem more dangerous, just as publication had made rumors easier to remember.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-920-mingwu-zhege-daoli-caineng-zhuan-daqian-lqmabg3bpyz6ct2v4arx-iun57ws|Hanji 920]] uses Qianlong in a different, comparative register. While explaining [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s palace shops, the episode cites Qianlong's Tongleyuan New Year buy/sell street as a later palace-market amusement staffed by outer-city shop clerks and allegedly stopped after Qianlong's death in 1799.

This comparison adds Qianlong to [[PalaceMarketTheater|宫市游乐式权力错位]] without changing the earlier Zeng Jing branch. In this source, he is a later example of royal market play rather than the punitive ruler of the Yongzheng succession-rumor case.
