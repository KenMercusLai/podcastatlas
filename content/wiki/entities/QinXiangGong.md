# 秦襄公 / Duke Xiang of Qin

秦襄公 / Duke Xiang of Qin appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] as the Qin leader whose service during the [[ZhouYouwang|周幽王]] crisis and [[ZhouPingwang|周平王]]'s eastward move changes Qin's status. The source says Zhou Ping Wang then recognizes Qin as a vassal and grants land west of Qishan.

His source role is institutional. He marks the move from [[QinFeizi|非子]]'s dependent Qin fief to [[QinState|秦国]] as a recognized polity that still has to win its granted territory from the Quanrong.
