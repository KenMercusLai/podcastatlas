# Five Stars Out of the East Brocade / 五星出东方织锦

## Overview
The Five Stars Out of the East brocade is the textile artifact used by the Hanji 689-3 episode to join material archaeology with Han omen language and Western Regions frontier history. In this source, it is an excavated brocade or armguard from the Niya site whose phrase, fragmentary continuation, and tomb context make it a bridge between [[ChineseAstrologicalPolitics]] and the [[WesternRegionsProtectorate]] world.

## Current Profile
The source profiles the brocade as a high-status, technically sophisticated object from a likely late-Eastern-Han tomb at [[NiyaSite]], associated with [[JingjueKingdom]]. Its reported inscription "五星出东方，立中国" and fragment "讨南羌" allow the episode to connect the artifact to Western Han frontier campaigning under [[EmperorXuanOfHan]] and [[ZhaoChongguoWesternHan]], while keeping the gift, warning, and armguard-use readings as plausible but not proven.

## Key Characteristics
- The artifact is presented as a 1995 Niya excavation find from a royal or elite joint tomb context.
- Its reported text links planetary omen language to China-centered military and diplomatic messaging.
- The "讨南羌" fragment is the hinge between the artifact and a Zhao Chongguo frontier-campaign interpretation.
- Craft details make it a rare prestige textile rather than merely a written slogan.
- Its armguard placement creates a function question that the episode treats cautiously.
- Its modern status as a restricted national treasure turns an ancient omen object into a contemporary memory object.

## Evidence
- Royal or elite tomb context: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-689-3-wuxing-chu-dongfang-li-zhongguo-yige-gulao-yuyan-de-xiandai-jiedu-lqccxzbvg-jziurfxm5tr5k4jlvq|Hanji 689-3]] says the brocade was found in 1995 at Niya in a two-person tomb whose pottery marked "王" and whose male occupant had the textile wrapped on the right arm.
- Omen and campaign language: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-689-3-wuxing-chu-dongfang-li-zhongguo-yige-gulao-yuyan-de-xiandai-jiedu-lqccxzbvg-jziurfxm5tr5k4jlvq|Hanji 689-3]] reports the core phrase and broken "讨南羌" fragment, then connects the expanded formula to Emperor Xuan's Zhao Chongguo campaign.
- Craft and prestige: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-689-3-wuxing-chu-dongfang-li-zhongguo-yige-gulao-yuyan-de-xiandai-jiedu-lqccxzbvg-jziurfxm5tr5k4jlvq|Hanji 689-3]] describes a small but dense five-color brocade with cloud, animal, white-tiger, and auspicious patterns.
- Function uncertainty: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-689-3-wuxing-chu-dongfang-li-zhongguo-yige-gulao-yuyan-de-xiandai-jiedu-lqccxzbvg-jziurfxm5tr5k4jlvq|Hanji 689-3]] notes that an armguard would normally protect the left arm, so the right-arm placement may imply left-handed use or a talismanic function.
- Contemporary memory: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-689-3-wuxing-chu-dongfang-li-zhongguo-yige-gulao-yuyan-de-xiandai-jiedu-lqccxzbvg-jziurfxm5tr5k4jlvq|Hanji 689-3]] places the object in Xinjiang Museum custody and names it among relics prohibited from overseas exhibition.

## Qualifications
The episode's gift or warning reconstruction is source-scoped, not a settled provenance claim. The right-arm interpretation is also uncertain, and the modern 2040-09-09 planetary comparison is an explanatory analogy rather than proof of the artifact's ancient meaning.
