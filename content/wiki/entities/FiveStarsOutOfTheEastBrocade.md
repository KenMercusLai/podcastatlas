# Five Stars Out of the East Brocade / 五星出东方织锦

## Overview
The Five Stars Out of the East brocade is the textile artifact through which the Hanji 689 sequence connects [[NiyaSite]] archaeology, [[JingjueKingdom]] memory, Han omen language, and Western Regions frontier history. Hanji 689-2 introduces it as the cliffhanger object from the 1995 Niya tomb excavation; Hanji 689-3 develops its inscription, function, and political interpretations.

## Current Profile
The brocade is currently profiled as a high-status, technically sophisticated object from a likely late-Eastern-Han tomb at [[NiyaSite]], associated with [[JingjueKingdom]]. Hanji 689-2 supplies the discovery setup: a male tomb occupant has the vivid textile on his right arm, and the eight-character formula "五星出东方利中国" draws the episode into the next installment. Hanji 689-3 supplies the main interpretation but records the phrase as "五星出东方，立中国"; that source-note discrepancy stays visible while the "讨南羌" fragment allows a connection to Western Han frontier campaigning under [[EmperorXuanOfHan]] and [[ZhaoChongguoWesternHan]]. Gift, warning, and armguard-use readings remain plausible but not proven.

## Key Characteristics
- The artifact is presented as a 1995 Niya excavation find from a royal or elite joint tomb context.
- Its reported text links planetary omen language to China-centered military and diplomatic messaging.
- The "讨南羌" fragment is the hinge between the artifact and a Zhao Chongguo frontier-campaign interpretation.
- Craft details make it a rare prestige textile rather than merely a written slogan.
- Its armguard placement creates a function question that the episode treats cautiously.
- Its modern status and narrative placement turn an ancient omen object into a contemporary memory object.

## Qualifications
Hanji 689-2 mainly previews the brocade and should not be overread as an independent interpretation of its meaning. The current source notes differ on "利中国" versus "立中国," so the exact character is unresolved here. Hanji 689-3's gift or warning reconstruction is source-scoped, not a settled provenance claim; the right-arm interpretation is also uncertain, and the modern 2040-09-09 planetary comparison is an explanatory analogy rather than proof of the artifact's ancient meaning.
