# Federal Communications Commission

The Federal Communications Commission appears in [[vol-265-kuayue-50-nian-de-meiguo-banben-zhizi-1001004591]] as the U.S. regulator relevant to the [[Paramount]] transaction. The source emphasizes that FCC leadership is appointed politically, so media ownership approval can become part of [[PoliticalRegulatoryLeverage]] when the buyer has visible ties to the administration.

The episode does not present the FCC as acting illegally. Its point is structural: when broadcast licenses, media mergers, litigation pressure, and political relationships overlap, even formally lawful approvals can create [[MediaOwnershipIndependenceRisk]].

[[tech-20260128-0128-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260128-0128-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds a telecommunications-capacity use of the agency. [[MarketplaceTech]] cites an FCC estimate that by 2007 roughly two-thirds of 45 million miles of fiber were still [[DarkFiber|dark]], making the agency a source of evidence for the scale of unused post-dot-com network capacity.
