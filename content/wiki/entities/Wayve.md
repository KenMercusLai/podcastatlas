# Wayve

Wayve appears in [[tech-20251229-1229-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251229-1229-mp-tech-pod-128]] as a UK-based autonomous-driving company that [[KirstenKorosek]] identifies as worth watching. The source frames Wayve differently from fleet-first robotaxi operators: its hoped-for route is licensing technology rather than necessarily operating its own consumer ride network.

That makes Wayve part of the wiki's broader distinction between autonomous-driving capability and [[RobotaxiEconomics]]. A company can pursue value through model or software licensing, but the eventual buyer still has to solve deployment, regulation, vehicle integration, safety evidence, and local operating acceptance.
