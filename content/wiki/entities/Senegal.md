# Senegal

Senegal appears in [[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]] as one of the countries trialing DAB radio. The episode groups Senegal with [[Kenya]] and [[Ghana]] as places testing a way to compress radio waves into digital data and create room for more stations.

In the source, Senegal is part of [[DABRadioCapacityExpansion]] rather than a broad country case. The practical point is that radio's resilience can create capacity pressure: where FM bands are crowded, digital broadcast formats can expand supply without making listeners depend entirely on mobile data.
