# Julia Druskin

Julia Druskin appears in [[our-book-vs-the-global-supply-chain]] as [[WWNorton|W. W. Norton]]'s director of trade production for the [[PlanetMoneyBook]]. The source uses her to make production judgment visible: she evaluates scratch-and-sniff feasibility, overseas printer options, [[EuropeanDeforestationRegulationSupplyChain|EU deforestation compliance]], paper sourcing, schedule risk, and final press quality.

Her role matters because she turns editorial desire into manufacturing reality. A scent idea, a paper choice, a printer country, or a late size change is not only aesthetic; it changes equipment risk, shipping time, compliance documentation, per-copy cost, and whether a publication date can hold.

## Key Claims
- Druskin investigated scratch-and-sniff cover production and helped decide that the supplier samples and printing-equipment risk did not fit the book.
- She reassessed Malaysia, Turkey, and U.S. printing options as the schedule tightened and forest-compliance risk became clearer.
- Her on-site press checks at [[LakesideBookCompany]] show that automated printing still requires human quality control over color, registration, smears, spots, and other defects.
- In the source, trade production is a strategic publishing role, not a back-office afterthought.
