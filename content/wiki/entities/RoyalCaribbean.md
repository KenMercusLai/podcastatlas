# Royal Caribbean

## Overview
Royal Caribbean is the cruise company in the source-reported plan to acquire a majority stake in [[SandalsResorts|Sandals Resorts]].

## Current Profile
The episode frames Royal Caribbean as extending from ship-based travel into controlled land destinations. Its renovated private-island facilities already keep more passenger spending within the company ecosystem; the reported Sandals deal would add resort inventory and diversify exposure when regional conflict affects some cruise itineraries.

## Key Characteristics
- Cruise operator expanding its control of destination spending.
- Has invested in private-island beach and water-park facilities in the source's account.
- Reportedly pursuing a majority stake in Sandals Resorts.
- Uses land expansion as both customer-lifetime extension and geographic diversification.

## Evidence
- Destination control: [[yuebing-shichang-chixu-jiangwen-doubao-suojian-duihua-yewu-tuandui-1017879242]] describes Royal Caribbean's private-island renovation and retained guest spending.
- Resort expansion: [[yuebing-shichang-chixu-jiangwen-doubao-suojian-duihua-yewu-tuandui-1017879242]] reports the planned Sandals majority acquisition.
- Demand context: [[yuebing-shichang-chixu-jiangwen-doubao-suojian-duihua-yewu-tuandui-1017879242]] links the move to uneven itinerary demand under regional conflict.

## Qualifications
The proposed transaction, valuation, route-demand effects, and strategic interpretation are source-reported. Cruise operations and resort operations have different capital, staffing, and integration requirements.
