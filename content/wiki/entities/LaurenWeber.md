# Lauren Weber

Lauren Weber is the [[WallStreetJournal|Wall Street Journal]] reporter whose work anchors [[tech-20260416-0416-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260416-0416-mp-tech-pod-128]], a [[MarketplaceTech]] episode on older workers, AI adoption, and retirement. In the episode, she treats AI as one factor among several pushing some older workers out of the labor force, alongside the pandemic and financial ability to retire.

Weber's contribution to the wiki is the worker-and-organization boundary around AI adoption. She distinguishes inability to use AI from a late-career choice not to undergo another employer-led transition, and she warns that companies lose knowledge that is not captured online when experienced employees leave without handoff.
