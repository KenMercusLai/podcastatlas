# Daria Shepolova

## Overview
Daria Shepolova was a 28-year-old Russian researcher whose unexplained death after plague work at the [[IrkutskAntiPlagueResearchInstitute]] became a case of public uncertainty and [[HighConsequenceBiologicalResearchTransparency]].

## Current Profile
The episode reports that Shepolova died after working on plague. Russia's health watchdog said microorganisms connected with her professional work were not responsible and attributed the death broadly to pneumonia, while also saying that the pneumonia's origin could not be confirmed.

Reported quarantines, movement restrictions, disinfection of her flat, and an unconfirmed rumor of a second death intensified public concern. The available account does not establish occupational infection, an outbreak, or weapons research; its central finding is that incomplete disclosure prevents confident independent assessment.

## Key Characteristics
- Young researcher working with plague at a specialized Russian institute.
- Subject of an official denial of work-related microbial causation.
- Death attributed broadly to pneumonia of unconfirmed origin.
- Became the focal point of uncertainty amplified by precautionary measures and institutional opacity.
