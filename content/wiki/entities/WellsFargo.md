# Wells Fargo

## Overview
Wells Fargo is the financial institution represented by CISO [[ManishSharmaWellsFargo|Manish Sharma]] in the source's case for collaborative open-source security and AI-assisted remediation.

## Current Profile
The source presents Wells Fargo as both highly dependent on shared software and exposed to concentrated cyber risk. Sharma argues that the bank uses automated AI capabilities to remediate findings and that institutions should pool capacity through [[ProjectLightwell]] instead of maintaining private forks across tens of thousands of packages. The supplied summary does not audit the bank's controls, performance, affected systems, or participation terms.

## Key Characteristics
- Early financial-sector participant in the source's Project Lightwell account.
- Treats AI-accelerated vulnerability discovery as an urgent software-lifecycle problem.
- Uses source-reported automated AI remediation workflows.
- Favors pooled open-source repair over large private dependency forks.
