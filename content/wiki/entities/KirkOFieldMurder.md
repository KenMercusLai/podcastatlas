# Kirk o' Field Murder

## Overview

The Kirk o' Field murder is the 10 February 1567 killing of [[HenryStuartLordDarnley|Henry Stuart / Lord Darnley]], reconstructed by the episode as a failed gunpowder assassination followed by strangulation and evidence suppression.

## Current Profile

Darnley was lodged outside Edinburgh and expected soon to return to [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary]]. On 9 February, Mary, nobles, and attendants visited him before she left for a wedding masque. Gunpowder had been placed beneath the lodging, but the chair, rope, cloak, uninjured bodies, and testimony support the episode's conclusion that Darnley and his valet escaped before the blast, were intercepted outside, strangled, and placed in the garden.

The physical mystery cannot be separated from the political one. The episode identifies [[JamesHepburnEarlOfBothwell|Bothwell]], [[JamesDouglasEarlOfMorton|Morton]], [[WilliamMaitlandOfLethington|Maitland]], and allied nobles as likely organizers. Because implicated men sat on the Privy Council, the official investigation lacked independence; missing or suppressed evidence let public suspicion center on Bothwell and then spread to Mary when she failed to act decisively against him.

## Key Characteristics

- Two-stage killing in which the explosion failed to kill the intended victim.
- Crime scene whose chair, rope, cloak, and unmarked bodies record an attempted escape.
- Elite conspiracy combining personal revenge and perceived state security.
- Investigation compromised by the governing council's likely participation.
- Scandal that damaged Mary even if she lacked advance knowledge.

## Evidence

- Conspirator network: [[588-mary-queen-of-scots-the-mystery-of-the-exploding-mansion-part-5-glt2383249995]] uses Drury's intelligence, confessions, and later evidence to identify Bothwell, Morton, Maitland, and allied retainers.

## Qualifications

This profile records the episode's reconstruction, not a final judicial finding. Exact responsibility, the timing and quantity of gunpowder, individual actions outside the lodging, Moray's knowledge, and Mary's private awareness remain disputed or inferential.

## What Changed

- Established the two-stage murder reconstruction and the connection between captured investigation and reputational damage.
