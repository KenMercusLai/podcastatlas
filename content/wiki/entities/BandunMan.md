# 板楯蛮 / Bandun Man

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-922-ruhe-zai-fanchen-zhong-xiulian-qingshang-lsxiqneyyeod6aeeqxuhs517pn1x|Hanji 922]] treats the Bandun Man as a Ba-region group whose rebellion is produced by official extraction rather than inherent anti-Han hostility. In [[ChenBaoLateHan|陈鲍]]'s explanation, their tax exemptions come from earlier military service, and their aid against rebellions and Qiang incursions once made them known as "divine soldiers."

The rebellion begins after local officials impose illegal taxes, treat the group harshly, force desperate sales of wives and children, ignore complaints, and block their path to the capital. The court's repair combines amnesty with the appointment of [[CaoQianLateHan|曹谦]] as [[BaCommanderyLateHan|巴郡]] administrator, creating an early case for [[RootCausePacification|查因安抚式治理]] and [[PeopleBasedPoliticalSecurity|民心型政治安全]].
