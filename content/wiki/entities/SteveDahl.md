## Overview

Steve Dahl is a Chicago radio personality associated with the "Disco Sucks" campaign and 1979 Disco Demolition Derby, a defining symbol of the [[DiscoBacklash]].

## Current Profile

[[439-disco-sex-and-race-in-seventies-america-glt8206137792|The source]] connects Dahl's campaign to his firing after a station switched from rock to disco. He converted a labor and format grievance into an on-air identity that culminated at a Chicago White Sox double-header on 12 July 1979. The explosion, field invasion, fires, police response, and forfeited game concentrated musical resentment and wider reaction into a visible spectacle.

## Key Characteristics

- Built an anti-disco campaign after losing a job during a format switch.
- Used the "Disco Sucks" slogan and record-destruction stunts.
- Fronted Disco Demolition Derby on 12 July 1979.
- Became a symbol of mixed backlash motives rather than a sole cause of decline.

## Qualifications

The source does not establish Dahl's private motives or show that every participant shared racist or homophobic intent. The derby symbolized but did not by itself cause disco's decline. Counts and crowd motives remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Established Dahl through radio-format grievance, anti-disco mobilization, and the demolition event.
