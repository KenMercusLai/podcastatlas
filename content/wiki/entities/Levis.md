# Levi's

## Overview
Levi's is the apparel company named in [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]] as the acquirer of [[BeyondYoga]] in 2021.

## Current Profile
Within this source, Levi's functions as the strategic buyer that ends the bootstrapped phase of a women's yoga apparel brand. The episode uses the roughly $400 million acquisition to mark the point at which [[MichelleWahler]]'s operating role began to close, and Wahler's later advice is framed by her having gone through the sale and stayed for a period afterwards.

## Key Characteristics
- Strategic apparel acquirer of [[BeyondYoga]] in 2021 in the episode's account.
- Provides the exit context that shapes Wahler's post-acquisition perspective.
- Appears in the wiki only through this source, so its profile here is acquisition-side rather than company-wide.

## Evidence
- Acquisition: [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]] says Levi's acquired Beyond Yoga for about $400 million in 2021.
- Post-deal timing: [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]] says Wahler left the company in 2024, several years after the sale.

## Qualifications
The deal price and timing are episode claims. The source does not discuss Levi's overall strategy, integration choices, or the brand's performance under its ownership.
