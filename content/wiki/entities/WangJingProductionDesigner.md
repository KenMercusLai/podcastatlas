# 王靖 / Wang Jing (production designer)

## Overview
王靖 is the production designer interviewed in VOL.88 about the fictional hospital and domestic environments of [[AskTheHeartTVDrama|《问心》]]. The semantic suffix distinguishes him from other people named Wang Jing in the wiki.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Wang Jing as a designer of contemporary Chinese television whose realism is built through spatial function, historical accumulation, operational detail, and character circumstance. For 《问心》, he rejects copying the polished visual language of foreign medical dramas and instead targets the recognizable density and mixed-age fabric of a large Chinese public tertiary hospital.

His governing idea is that production design creates people and situations rather than attractive rooms. Hospital notices, added beds, crowds, mismatched household objects, room assignments, furniture positions, warm or cold palettes, and sightlines are therefore narrative evidence. He also treats clinical consultation as a corrective input without claiming that a drama becomes a documentary or medical lesson.

## Key Characteristics
- Builds contemporary realism from observation of Chinese institutions rather than direct imitation of foreign genre imagery.
- Uses connected architecture and reusable sets to support camera movement, scene transitions, and an impression of greater scale.
- Treats operational clutter, informal notices, crowding, and later additions as evidence of institutional life rather than visual defects.
- Designs homes through care obligations, privacy, exclusion, income, family history, and present dramatic pressure.
- Prefers lived and functional plausibility to stylistic uniformity when the two conflict.
- Accepts consultant criticism and acknowledged imperfections while keeping the boundary between drama and documentary explicit.
