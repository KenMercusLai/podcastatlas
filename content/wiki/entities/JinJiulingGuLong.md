## Overview

金九龄（古龙人物） / Jin Jiuling is a senior law-enforcement figure who invites [[LuXiaofeng|陆小凤]] to investigate the embroidered-bandit crimes in [[EmbroideredBanditGuLong|《绣花大盗》]] and is ultimately exposed as their perpetrator.

## Current Profile

Jin's narrative power comes from institutional and relational proximity: he does not merely evade the investigation but helps frame it. His expensive tastes without matching wealth operate as motive clues, while a desire to build a perfect crime gives the scheme a competitive dimension. The bounded episode finds the reveal effective but the money motive and chain from clue to conclusion insufficiently developed; it prefers adaptations that emphasize rivalry with Lu.

The final fight converts Jin's eighty-seven-jin iron hammer into a light, quiet, controlled weapon intended to defeat Lu's finger technique. As fatigue accumulates, that control breaks down. The scene makes his refinement and strain visible through weapon handling, although the hosts question the plausibility of the final projectile kill between near-equal fighters.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses official authority and participation in the case to direct suspicion away from himself.
- Turns a trusted relationship into a structural resource for concealment.
- Joins expensive consumption to a reported shortage of wealth.
- Seeks not only money but the prestige of a perfect crime in the episode's stronger motive reading.
- Uses an extremely heavy weapon with deceptive quietness and agility.
- Produces a powerful culprit reveal whose motivational and inferential support remains disputed.
