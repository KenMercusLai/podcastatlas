# PCAST

[[PCAST]] is the President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology as discussed in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-anthropics-generational-run-openai-panics-ai-moats-meta-loses-lawsuits-40647420]]. [[DavidSacks|David Sacks]] says [[DonaldTrump]] appointed him to the council and that he co-chairs it with [[MichaelKratsios|Michael Kratsios]], expanding his advisory context from AI and crypto into a wider science-and-technology remit.

The source frames PCAST as a policy bridge between [[AmericanAIStackStrategy]], [[AIForScience]], [[StrategicIndustrialPolicy]], and [[ResearchInstitutionsAsStrategicCapacity]]. [[DavidFriedberg|David Friedberg]] connects the council to a broader science race in which AI, nuclear power, quantum computing, semiconductors, biotech, and [[China]] competition all become linked questions of national capacity.
