# Mito Habe Evans

Mito Habe Evans appears in [[our-book-vs-the-global-supply-chain]] as the [[NPR]] creative director who helped the [[PlanetMoneyBook]] team find illustrators and build the book's visual language. The source places his work inside a [[PhysicalBookDesignTradeoff]]: after postcards, posters, special paper, and scratch-and-sniff ideas became too costly or impractical, four-color illustration became the way to keep the book visually playful without breaking the production model.

His page matters because the episode treats book design as production-constrained creative work. Visual identity has to support the reader experience, the retail price, the printer's capabilities, and the schedule at the same time.

## Key Claims
- Habe Evans helped route the book's playful concept into internal color illustrations rather than removable or specialty inserts.
- The illustration plan preserved some of [[PlanetMoney]]'s show voice while fitting [[BookManufacturingSupplyChain|book manufacturing]] constraints.
- His role links [[BookCreatorWork]] to physical design, not only writing or promotion.
