# 波才 / Bo Cai (late Han)

波才 / Bo Cai enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-925-tuoxie-bushi-rensong-xuehui-tuoxie-lu-geng-changliang-limafqskgblrwetestyh39w-hzm4|Hanji 925]] as the [[YellowTurbansLateHan|Yellow Turban]] commander who first defeats [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]] and forces the Han side into a vulnerable posture. The episode stresses that the Yellow Turbans are brave and resilient despite weak formal training, so Bo Cai is not treated as a trivial opponent.

The same source then makes Bo Cai the target of [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]]'s [[ChangsheBattleLateHan|长社之战]] design. Huangfu Song retreats into Changshe, has Zhu Jun hidden nearby, harasses and exhausts Bo Cai's force, then attacks at night when wind and camp vegetation make fire effective. [[CaoCao|曹操]] arrives as a cavalry commander after the main disruption and joins the pursuit.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-928-zhichang-buke-mingshuo-song-dui-li-banshi-geng-shunli-lgup21lsa-u1ykaiyabcko2vhp0o|Hanji 928]] continues Bo Cai's trajectory source-scopingly: Huangfu Song and Zhu Jun defeat Yellow Turban forces under Bo Cai around Yangdi before turning toward the Runan-Chen field.
