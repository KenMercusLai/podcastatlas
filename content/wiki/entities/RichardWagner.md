# Richard Wagner

Richard Wagner appears in [[caracas-under-pressure-democracy-in-venezuela-6a7d8bbe7d85cbdf96e00822]] through the 150th anniversary of the first complete performance of [[DerRingDesNibelungen]]. The episode presents him as an artistic revolutionary whose music dramas used [[Leitmotif|leitmotifs]], uninterrupted melody, and [[Gesamtkunstwerk]] to join poetry, music, staging, and spectacle.

The source also keeps Wagner politically difficult. It links the Ring's origins to the 1849 European uprisings and Wagner's exile, then contrasts the work's anti-royal and anti-capitalist readings with Wagner's antisemitism and later Nazi enthusiasm. In the wiki, he becomes a compact case in [[IdeologicallyCompromisedArt]]: a creator's politics can damage reception without exhausting the work's interpretive possibilities.
