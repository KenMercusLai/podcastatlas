# 荆南 / Southern Jingzhou (late Han)

荆南 / Southern Jingzhou (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1027-jingzhou-zhizhu-liubiao-weihe-nan-cheng-bazhu-ll-ebguqllsf-5ycvpdogihzn3lj|Hanji 1027]] as the southern commandery zone inside [[Jingzhou|荆州]]. This page is disambiguated from [[Jingnan|荆南]], the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms regime.

The source defines the region through [[ChangshaCommanderyLateHan|长沙郡]], [[LinglingCommanderyLateHan|零陵郡]], and [[GuiyangCommanderyLateHan|桂阳郡]], all geographically distant from [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s [[XiangyangLateHan|襄阳]] center. That distance, combined with local gentry networks and economic-cultural difference, lets [[ZhangXianLateHan|张献]] and [[HuanJieLateHan|桓阶]] turn southern discontent into an external alignment with [[CaoCao|曹操]].
