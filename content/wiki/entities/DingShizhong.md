# 丁世忠 / Ding Shizhong

## Overview
丁世忠 / Ding Shizhong appears in [[500-jinjiang-chendaizhen-wangshi-huangziyi-tan-gaikai-jincheng-zhong-de-zhongguo-yundong-pinpai-lswtplovdbo4pl52yoxu6rgzyal2]] as the [[AntaSports|Anta]] founder whose early sales experience, family business ethic, and later channel reforms anchor the episode's Anta case.

## Current Profile
The source presents Ding less as a lone heroic founder than as an operator formed by the [[Jinjiang]] footwear cluster. His teenage Beijing selling, department-store negotiation, family "49/51" letting-profit rule, store visits during the inventory crisis, digital retail systems, and willingness to run acquired brands separately all become evidence for Anta's channel-sensitive and portfolio-oriented growth.

## Key Characteristics
- Learned the market through direct shoe selling in Beijing before Anta became a mature brand.
- Inherited a family ethic of leaving partners more benefit than oneself.
- Kept strong founder control while gradually professionalizing management.
- Responded to inventory crisis by visiting stores and improving retail data visibility.
- Helped push Anta from a mass domestic brand toward multi-brand group operation.
