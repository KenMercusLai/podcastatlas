# 赵高 / Zhao Gao

## Overview
赵高 / Zhao Gao is a legally trained Qin court insider whose offices place him near the ruler, imperial vehicles, seals, documents, and succession credentials. Across this source set he moves from keeper of an undelivered letter, through the received account's accession manipulation and Meng-family purge, to palace access monopoly, coerced confession, public truth tests, crisis concealment, and the killing of [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]].

## Current Profile
The current profile is institutional as much as personal. Qin Shi Huang makes Zhao Gao 中车府令 and entrusts him with seal-related duties, putting him at a critical access point before the emperor dies. Qinji 127-1 now shows that the letter summoning [[FuSu|扶苏]] remains with Zhao Gao; Qinji 127-2 then presents him converting document custody, the Meng family's threat, Hu Hai's availability, and [[LiSi|李斯]]'s status anxiety into a forged-edict succession. The [[ZhaoZhengShu|《赵正书》]] alternative narrows this originator role by allowing Hu Hai to have been selected before the emperor's death, but the later sources consistently show Zhao Gao enlarging power through purge, isolation, legal process capture, information control, and palace violence. His inherited identification as a castrated eunuch is disputed by the later terminology-focused source.

## Key Characteristics
- Court proximity, legal literacy, carriage-office command, and seal access give Zhao Gao leverage greater than his formal rank alone suggests.
- His earlier death sentence under [[MengYiQin|蒙毅]] makes a Fusu-Meng succession a personal survival threat.
- He repeatedly converts other actors' vulnerabilities—Hu Hai's dependence, Li Si's rank fear, and the ruler's desire for seclusion—into institutional control.
- His methods escalate from credential control to purge, access monopoly, coerced confession, enforced falsehood, and crisis-news suppression.
- His role in Hu Hai's accession remains source-dependent, but his later domination and purge activity are common to the cluster.
- The source set supports a palace-service and hidden-office background while disputing automatic identification as a castrated eunuch.

## Qualifications
Zhao Gao's role at the origin of Hu Hai's accession is not settled. The received account makes him the initiating strategist, while the Zhao Zheng Shu branch can make him a downstream beneficiary. The claim that he was not castrated rests on the episode's lexical and family reconstruction and should remain source-scoped. The source inventory ends before the separate Qinji 138 part 2 account of Zhao Gao's own death, so this page does not treat that later note as evidence in the canonical profile.

## What Changed
- Added the undelivered Fusu letter as Zhao Gao's earliest concrete succession credential and access bottleneck.
- Added his 中车府令 and seal-related duties as the institutional basis for later action.
- Clarified that Qinji 127-1 ends at the start of Li Si's status-pressure sequence rather than already completing the coup.
- Compressed the prior chronological narrative into a current profile organized by mechanisms of control.

## Relationships
- [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] - ruler who promotes and pardons Zhao Gao and leaves the final letter within his access sphere.
- [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] - pupil, succession vehicle, dependent ruler, and eventual target.
- [[LiSi|李斯]] - chancellor recruited through status anxiety and later destroyed through access and legal-process capture.
- [[FuSu|扶苏]] - rival heir named in the undelivered summons and removed in the received account.
- [[MengTian|蒙恬]] - military rival whose relationship with Fusu supplies Zhao Gao's leverage over Li Si.
- [[MengYiQin|蒙毅]] - former judge and personal threat whose absence helps narrow the deathbed circle.
- [[PalaceAccessMonopoly|宫廷接触垄断]] - mature form of Zhao Gao's control over ruler, documents, timing, and interpretation.
