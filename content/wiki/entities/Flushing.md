# Flushing

Flushing appears in [[695-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-the-shadow-war-part-5-glt5637968455]] as one of the cautionary towns handed to [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]] under the [[TreatyOfNonsuch]]. [[PhilipSidney|Sir Philip Sidney]] is appointed to command it, briefly loses the appointment, tries to sail with [[FrancisDrake]], and is then recalled into the Dutch post.

The source uses Flushing to show the practical strain of intervention in the [[LowCountries|Low Countries]]. Sidney arrives in muddy misery, struggles with inadequate funds, warns that unpaid soldiers could cost the queen her garrisons, and operates inside friction among English commanders, Dutch allies, Elizabeth, and [[RobertDudley|Robert Dudley / Earl of Leicester]].

For the wiki, Flushing is less a general port page than a concrete site where the [[StrategicInterventionThreshold|strategic decision]] becomes logistical burden.
