# Francis Galton / 弗朗西斯·高尔顿

Francis Galton / 弗朗西斯·高尔顿 is the nineteenth-century British polymath introduced in [[37-zhishang-ceshi-qingwen-ni-shi-zhili-poluomen-ma-608020679]] as a crucial origin figure for modern [[IntelligenceTesting|intelligence testing]] and [[EugenicGovernance|eugenic governance]]. The episode connects him to statistics, regression, fingerprinting, anthropometry, the invention of the term "eugenics," and a deep belief that ability, character, class position, and racial hierarchy could be read through heredity.

The source keeps Galton double-edged. It acknowledges real measurement talent and statistical influence, but treats his project as a warning about scientific method mixed with social prejudice. His fascination with bodies, families, scores, and distributions helped make [[PsychometricSocialSorting|psychometric social sorting]] imaginable as public policy.
