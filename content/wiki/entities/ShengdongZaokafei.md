The announced starting cadence is Monday, Wednesday, and Friday mornings, with a stated hope that the show can eventually become a daily morning companion.

## Source Position
- 声动早咖啡 is a show-launch case for [[ShortFormBusinessTechAudio]].
- The Garmin episode shows the format's Friday question-answer mode: listener curiosity becomes a compact business-history explainer after a short news scan.
- The Vans/Converse episode shows the format using a consumer-brand decline case to connect history, culture, product architecture, and current business metrics.
- The Kao episode shows the format's mix of quick daily business updates and one deeper business-history explanation.
- The Spider-Man/AI-price-war episode shows the show's compact multi-industry roundup mode.
- The popcorn-bucket episode shows how a playful consumer object can become an entry point into media economics, licensing, retail margin, and fandom behavior.
- The Food Republic episode shows how the listener-question format can reuse an existing business-history topic while adding margin, channel, and successor-operator details.
- The Unitree/LandSpace roundup shows the format's compact market-scan mode: technology milestones, earnings pressure, retail expansion, food, aviation, and city services are connected through capital, user behavior, and service experience.
- The Whatnot episode shows the format using a Western ecommerce success case to qualify live-shopping transfer, while still carrying compact Chinese business updates.
- The burger/lemon episode shows the format moving between portfolio focus, consumer-brand channel strategy, restaurant category expansion, agricultural supply-demand, and discount-retail operating models.
- It extends [[MorningAudioRitual]] by making news listening part of waking, coffee, commuting, and planning the day.
- Its coffee metaphor links the show to [[Coffee|coffee / 咖啡]] and [[CaffeinatedModernity]], but the trailer uses coffee as a media-positioning device rather than a food or retail topic.
