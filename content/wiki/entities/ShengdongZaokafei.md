[[trailer-ding-xiazhouyi-zaochen-lai-bei-shengdong-zaokafei-ba-431629439]] introduces 声动早咖啡 as a [[ShengdongHuopo|声动活泼]] workday-morning audio show hosted by [[MengYi|孟依]]. The trailer positions the show as a concentrated, under-15-minute business-and-technology news companion for listeners before work or on the way out the door.

The show's launch frame is deliberately ritualized. Instead of advertising only speed or information density, the trailer compares the program to an espresso-like morning habit: short, fresh, energizing, and repeatable. The announced starting cadence is Monday, Wednesday, and Friday mornings, with a stated hope that the show can eventually become a daily morning companion.

## Source Position
- 声动早咖啡 is a show-launch case for [[ShortFormBusinessTechAudio]].
- The Kao episode shows the format's mix of quick daily business updates and one deeper business-history explanation.
- The Spider-Man/AI-price-war episode shows the show's compact multi-industry roundup mode.
- The popcorn-bucket episode shows how a playful consumer object can become an entry point into media economics, licensing, retail margin, and fandom behavior.
- It extends [[MorningAudioRitual]] by making news listening part of waking, coffee, commuting, and planning the day.
- Its coffee metaphor links the show to [[Coffee|coffee / 咖啡]] and [[CaffeinatedModernity]], but the trailer uses coffee as a media-positioning device rather than a food or retail topic.
