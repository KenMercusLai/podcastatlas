# Matt Krop

Matt Krop is a managing director and senior partner at [[BCG]] interviewed in [[tech-20260331-0331-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260331-0331-mp-tech-pod-128]] about a BCG study on [[AIBrainFry|AI brain fry]]. He frames the problem as cognitive exhaustion that appears when workers must supervise fast AI workflows, especially when many agents or outputs demand attention at once.

Krop's workplace recommendation is not to avoid AI, but to redesign work around task fit. AI can improve morale when it handles mundane, repetitive toil; it can worsen morale when it multiplies high-cognitive oversight, constant switching, and the feeling that workers must always be on.
