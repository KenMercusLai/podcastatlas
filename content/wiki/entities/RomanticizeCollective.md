## Overview
Romanticize Collective is Ali Aguilar's subscription service for making creativity, exploration, reflection, and play more accessible in everyday life.

## Current Profile
The company has an early recurring-member base but has not yet established whether its durable product is a monthly box, a program of offline experiences, a local community, or a wider partner network. Its next learning loop should examine completion, retention, recommendation, and friend conversion while testing small real-world gatherings.

## Key Characteristics
- Sends a monthly box with guides and selected items supporting four varied experiences.
- Reported 30 recurring members and just under $20,000 in total sales after launching in January.
- Organizes experiences across creation, exploration, reflection, and whimsy.
- Holds a broader mission of making experiences as easy to access as physical products.
- Is testing local collaboration and event formats without yet abandoning the subscription box.

## Qualifications
Subscriber count and cumulative sales do not establish retention, engagement, margin, or product-market fit. Referral behavior is a diagnostic signal rather than a sufficient definition of value, and events may add operational complexity.

## What Changed
- Established Romanticize Collective as an early subscription-to-community validation case.
