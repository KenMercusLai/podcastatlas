# Ferrari Purosangue

The Ferrari Purosangue is the utility vehicle in Ferrari's modern product pyramid in [[ferrari-1]]. The source emphasizes that it has a naturally aspirated V12 and is capped at 20% of total production.

The Purosangue is important because it tests [[LuxuryScarcityDiscipline]]. It gives [[Ferrari]] access to broader utility-vehicle demand, but the cap and engine choice are presented as ways to keep it inside Ferrari's brand logic rather than making Ferrari an ordinary luxury SUV maker.
