# 尹鑫 / Yin Xin (nutrition guest)

## Overview
尹鑫 / Yin Xin is the nutrition-department guest in VOL.119 of [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]], contributing personal experience of congenital red-green [[ColorVisionDeficiency|color-vision deficiency]].

## Current Profile
The source uses 尹鑫's account to show why color-vision diagnoses should not be equated with an inability to function. He describes altered red and green perception, discovery around primary-school graduation, and the ability to distinguish traffic lights and work in medical imaging despite the condition.

His contribution also exposes a social misconception: after disclosure, people may turn a diagnosis into a guessing game about colors or even confuse color vision with visual acuity. The episode instead treats capability as type-, degree-, task-, and context-dependent.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides a first-person account of congenital red-green color-vision difference.
- Separates altered perception from total inability to distinguish useful signals.
- Uses work and traffic-light examples to keep functional impact task-specific.
- Challenges casual social tests and misconceptions after disability disclosure.
