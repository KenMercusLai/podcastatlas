# Pliny the Elder

## Overview

Pliny the Elder is the Roman author and fleet commander whose observation of the [[EruptionOfVesuvius79|79 CE eruption]] at Misenum became an attempted naval rescue and ended with his death at Stabiae.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Pliny first as a curious reader and observer, then as an admiral responding to a plea for evacuation. When pumice prevented entry to Pompeii's harbor, he continued toward his friend Pomponianus at Stabiae, where he tried to project calm through bathing, dining, and sleep. Unable to escape by sea and physically struggling as ash and sulfur arrived, he collapsed and was found dead two days later. The portrait depends heavily on his nephew's admiring literary account.

## Key Characteristics

- Roman intellectual whose first response to the unusual cloud was observation.
- Admiral at Misenum with ships and command capacity available during the disaster.
- Responder who shifted from investigation to evacuation after receiving a request for help.
- Figure of performed Roman composure at Stabiae under worsening conditions.
- Victim whose exact cause of death remains uncertain in the source.

## Evidence

- Observation and command: [[355-roman-apocalypse-pompeii-79-ad-glt6664993493]] places Pliny at Misenum and identifies him as fleet admiral.
- Rescue attempt: [[355-roman-apocalypse-pompeii-79-ad-glt6664993493]] connects a woman's letter to his decision to sail toward the threatened coast.
- Stabiae conduct: [[355-roman-apocalypse-pompeii-79-ad-glt6664993493]] describes his calming performance, bath, meal, rest, and shore-side wait.
- Death: [[355-roman-apocalypse-pompeii-79-ad-glt6664993493]] reports physical exhaustion or breathing difficulty, collapse, and later recovery of his body.

## Qualifications

The source does not settle whether Pliny died from asthma, toxic exposure, cardiac or respiratory failure, exhaustion, or another cause. His calmness, words, motives, and final posture come through Pliny the Younger's shaped family testimony rather than neutral observation.

## What Changed

- Created a profile joining scientific curiosity, public command, rescue action, literary commemoration, and uncertain death.
