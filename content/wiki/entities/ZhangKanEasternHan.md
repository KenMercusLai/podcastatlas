## Overview

张堪 / Zhang Kan is currently synthesized as an Eastern Han official whose profile combines Chengdu-campaign judgment, military success against Xiongnu pressure, and stable Yuyang local governance. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-712-liuxiu-weihe-bei-yuwei-zui-hui-da-zhang-de-huangdi-lt7rx7tblodjfngqfdq33ikrcmyn|Hanji 712]] places him at the Chengdu front, where he persuades Wu Han not to retreat under food pressure and supports a show-weakness lure. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-717-13-ge-zi-yinfa-de-diwang-zhinu-liz3jsbvw3snfapzsrqw917fqv7v|Hanji 717]] later places him at Gaoliu, where he uses [[DuMaoEasternHan|Du Mao]]'s former troops to defeat Xiongnu, then serves as Yuyang taishou and is remembered for agriculture, security, and popular prosperity.

## Current Profile

Hanji 712 supplies the Chengdu-campaign judgment layer. When [[WuHanEasternHan|Wu Han]] has only seven days of grain and secretly prepares ships for withdrawal, Zhang Kan argues that retreat is the wrong read: [[GongsunShuEasternHan|Gongsun Shu]] is already exhausted, he has no surviving strategic margin, and withdrawing would squander Han's pressure. Wu Han follows Zhang Kan's advice and turns toward a show-weakness lure instead.

Zhang Kan's current significance is not only battlefield success. The source makes his frontier tenure valuable because military stabilization is followed by local economic recovery: for eight years, Xiongnu do not invade, farming is encouraged, and people become wealthy enough for local praise.

This makes Zhang Kan a useful node for the wiki's frontier-administration branch. His page connects Guangwu-era border defense to appointment quality, local tenure stability, and the idea that commandery restoration requires both armed deterrence and productive settlement.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han official whose Chengdu advice prevents a food-pressure retreat.
- Campaign interpreter who reads enemy exhaustion and strategic closure before the final Shu collapse.
- Eastern Han official whose Gaoliu action turns reassigned troops into a successful anti-Xiongnu defense.
- Frontier administrator appointed as Yuyang taishou after military success.
- Local-governance exemplar associated with agriculture, security, and prosperity during an eight-year tenure.
- Link between battlefield judgment, border defense, and civilian commandery restoration rather than a purely military figure.

## Qualifications

The current sources do not give Zhang Kan's full career, family background, later offices, or exact administrative measures in Yuyang. Hanji 712 ends before the final outcome of the show-weakness plan, so the durable claim is that Zhang Kan prevents immediate withdrawal and identifies the enemy-exhaustion logic. The praise song and later poetic memory in Hanji 717 show reputation but should not be used as independent statistical proof of prosperity.
