## Overview

张堪 / Zhang Kan is currently synthesized as an Eastern Han frontier official whose profile combines military success against Xiongnu pressure with stable Yuyang local governance. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-717-13-ge-zi-yinfa-de-diwang-zhinu-liz3jsbvw3snfapzsrqw917fqv7v|Hanji 717]] places him at Gaoliu, where he uses [[DuMaoEasternHan|Du Mao]]'s former troops to defeat Xiongnu, then serves as Yuyang taishou and is remembered for agriculture, security, and popular prosperity.

## Current Profile

Zhang Kan's current significance is not only battlefield success. The source makes his frontier tenure valuable because military stabilization is followed by local economic recovery: for eight years, Xiongnu do not invade, farming is encouraged, and people become wealthy enough for local praise.

This makes Zhang Kan a useful node for the wiki's frontier-administration branch. His page connects Guangwu-era border defense to appointment quality, local tenure stability, and the idea that commandery restoration requires both armed deterrence and productive settlement.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han official whose Gaoliu action turns reassigned troops into a successful anti-Xiongnu defense.
- Frontier administrator appointed as Yuyang taishou after military success.
- Local-governance exemplar associated with agriculture, security, and prosperity during an eight-year tenure.
- Link between border defense and civilian commandery restoration rather than a purely military figure.

## Evidence

- Gaoliu victory: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-717-13-ge-zi-yinfa-de-diwang-zhinu-liz3jsbvw3snfapzsrqw917fqv7v|Hanji 717]] says Liu Xiu sends Zhang Kan with Du Mao's troops and Zhang Kan defeats Xiongnu at Gaoliu.
- Yuyang tenure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-717-13-ge-zi-yinfa-de-diwang-zhinu-liz3jsbvw3snfapzsrqw917fqv7v|Hanji 717]] says Zhang Kan becomes Yuyang taishou after the victory.
- Frontier stability and agriculture: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-717-13-ge-zi-yinfa-de-diwang-zhinu-liz3jsbvw3snfapzsrqw917fqv7v|Hanji 717]] says his eight-year tenure prevents Xiongnu invasion, encourages farming, and leaves people wealthy and stable.

## Qualifications

The current source does not give Zhang Kan's full career, family background, later offices, or exact administrative measures in Yuyang. The praise song and later poetic memory show reputation but should not be used as independent statistical proof of prosperity.

## What Changed

- Connected his profile to both Gaoliu defense and Yuyang agricultural administration.
- Kept exact song wording, later poetic reception, and non-Yuyang biography source-scoped.
