# DeePhi Tech / 深鉴科技

DeePhi Tech, or 深鉴科技, is [[YaoSong]]'s first startup in [[173-duihua-yao-song-shenjian-dongfang-kongjian-zaichufa-tiancai-shaonian-shinian-hou-1-173-1]]. The source describes its origin as a [[TsinghuaUniversity]] AI-chip demo built from complementary work in algorithms, architecture, software systems, and compiler-like tooling, with [[WangYu]] and [[HanSong]] as important technical anchors.

The company is a [[HardTechFundraising]] case because early investors did not know how to underwrite AI-chip risk. Yao says the team met roughly 50 institutions before learning to explain the company through market size, product need, and technical landing rather than only through technical possibility. Its later sale to [[Xilinx]] becomes a [[StrategicAcquirerFit]] case: the acquirer needed software, algorithm, and solution capability to move from devices toward platform strategy.

## Key Claims
- DeePhi's startup timing depended on deep learning moving toward commercial application and CPU/GPU alternatives becoming more interesting.
- The source connects [[AIChipSpecialization]] to productization: a hardware acceleration demo had to become a platform, solution, and customer story.
- The company cut its smart-drone direction after Yao judged that [[DJI]] controlled too much of the market, then moved attention toward smart security and assisted driving.
- The acquisition decision combined valuation, strategic fit, IPO uncertainty, future risk, and team endurance rather than a single founder-liquidity motive.
