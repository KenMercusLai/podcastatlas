# Clorox

Clorox appears in [[tech-20260203-0203-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260203-0203-mp-tech-pod-128]] as [[ChristopherMims]]' example of a company that is not usually treated as a technology leader but is using AI productively. The episode says Clorox used generative image tools to make simple web ad variants for [[HiddenValleyRanch]] and used [[MicrosoftCopilot|Microsoft Copilot]] with [[ChatGPT]] underneath as a brainstorming participant.

The case matters because it grounds [[BusinessLedAITransformation]] outside software companies. AI is useful here not as a general replacement for workers, but as a way to speed ad-variant generation and add an unusual voice to product ideation when the workflow is bounded.
