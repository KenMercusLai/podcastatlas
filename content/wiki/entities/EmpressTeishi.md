# Empress Teishi

## Overview

Empress Teishi is the imperial patron served by [[SeiShonagon]] and the social center of the court world preserved in [[ThePillowBook]].

## Current Profile

Teishi's household joined intimacy, play, literature, and factional prestige. The episode's snow-mountain anecdote and paper-gift origin story present a close relationship between empress and attendants, while Sei Shonagon's brilliance enhanced Teishi's cultural standing.

That world depended on Teishi's father, Fujiwara no Michitaka. After his death, [[FujiwaraNoMichinaga]]'s rise and the elevation of [[EmpressShoshi]] created a rival empress of equal rank. Teishi's death in childbirth ended Sei Shonagon's court service and turned the book's preserved delight into a memorial to a lost household.

## Key Characteristics

- She appears as Sei Shonagon's patron and the center of a playful literary circle.
- Cultural brilliance contributes to her household's prestige.
- Her political security depended on her natal Fujiwara faction.
- Shoshi's elevation transformed court patronage into explicit dynastic competition.
- Her death in childbirth closes the social world celebrated by *The Pillow Book*.

## Evidence

- Patronage and intimacy: [[561-the-golden-age-of-japan-secrets-of-the-imperial-court-part-2-glt9428275194]] recounts the paper gift and snow-mountain wager as scenes of empress-attendant closeness.
- Cultural prestige: [[561-the-golden-age-of-japan-secrets-of-the-imperial-court-part-2-glt9428275194]] says Sei Shonagon's fame increased Teishi's standing.
- Factional decline: [[561-the-golden-age-of-japan-secrets-of-the-imperial-court-part-2-glt9428275194]] links Teishi's vulnerability to Michitaka's death and Michinaga's ascent.
- End of household: [[561-the-golden-age-of-japan-secrets-of-the-imperial-court-part-2-glt9428275194]] says Teishi died in childbirth after Shoshi's elevation.

## Qualifications

The episode focuses on Teishi through Sei Shonagon's literary memory and factional politics, not as a complete biography. Private emotions, the exact political meaning of anecdotes, and causal claims about literary composition remain interpretive.

## What Changed

- Created Teishi as the patronage and loss node behind *The Pillow Book*.

## Relationships

- [[SeiShonagon]] - lady-in-waiting whose literary fame strengthened Teishi's court.
- [[ThePillowBook]] - work that preserves the pleasures of her household.
- [[EmpressShoshi]] - rival empress elevated through the competing Michinaga branch.
- [[FujiwaraNoMichinaga]] - political rival whose rise weakened Teishi's faction.
- [[FujiwaraMarriagePolitics]] - dynastic system that made her marriage and childbirth politically consequential.
