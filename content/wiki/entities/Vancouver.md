# Vancouver / 温哥华

[[the-skyscrapers-that-nimbys-and-zoning-couldnt-stop]] adds Vancouver as a housing-policy and sovereignty setting. The city is adjacent to [[SinakVillageSite|the returned Squamish site]], and the episode contrasts ordinary Vancouver zoning limits with the [[SquamishNation|Squamish Nation]]'s sovereign authority to build much denser rental housing.

In that source, Vancouver is not simply the municipal government being bypassed. It still matters for infrastructure: the episode says the city supported road, sewage, power-grid, and related connections while the Squamish agreed to fund traffic and transit-related upgrades. That makes Vancouver part of [[SovereignHousingDevelopment]] as coordination, not only as zoning constraint.

Vancouver is the central city example in [[ep265-dang-qing3xiu13-chongshang-resou-women-ruhe-gei-rensheng-fang-yige-changjia-lsloczlnkmcrrfpn9coh8rrqo-wb]]. [[LeiTao|雷涛 / 无二旅人]] uses it as a first-entry point for [[Canada]] travel and as a case where city, sea, parks, cycling, food, and wildlife can sit close together.

The episode's Vancouver is less a city profile than a rest-design example. Stanley Park, seaside sunset waiting, bike routes, food tours, crab-catching rules, ferries, and easy links toward [[Whistler]] and Vancouver Island all make [[NatureCityProximity|nature-city proximity]] concrete.
