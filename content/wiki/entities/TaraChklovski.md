# Tara Chklovski

## Overview
Tara Chklovski is represented in the current source as the CEO and founder of [[Technovation]], an education nonprofit using entrepreneurship and technology projects to develop agency among young people, especially girls.

## Current Profile
Chklovski's episode profile joins engineering training, nonprofit leadership, gender inclusion, and AI-era education. She describes moving from aerospace engineering toward education because birthplace and circumstance strongly shape opportunity, then building a program where students practice uncertain problem solving rather than merely receive technical instruction.

Her central judgment is developmental: the app is an instrument, while the learner's identity, courage, judgment, and capacity to act are the intended product. She extends that logic to AI safety and inclusion, arguing that people at risk of exclusion or harm need enough access and practical experience to shape technology while remaining alert to its dangers.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats education as a lever for changing opportunity and self-conception.
- Uses entrepreneurial projects to train courage, independent learning, systems thinking, and tolerance for uncertainty.
- Centers girls while supporting a guarded expansion to boys that does not displace female participation.
- Frames women's AI participation as an access, time, confidence, and power problem rather than only a skills deficit.
- Combines concern about AI alignment and harm with a preference for informed participation and alternative-building.
- Pursues institutional scale through mentors, corporate funding, educator training, and nonprofit-government-industry partnerships.
