# Newlane

## Overview
Newlane is [[DomCotton]]'s company making a patented, safety-certified bicycle helmet that folds into a flatter form.

## Current Profile
Newlane addresses the carrying inconvenience that can make helmet ownership fail to become helmet use, especially for commuters and shared-bike riders. Its distinct form can support convenience positioning and offline conversation, but the same product must also carry the economics of UK manufacturing, paid acquisition, and wholesale margins. The episode therefore treats positioning and buyer influence as immediate constraints, not only production scale.

## Key Characteristics
- Produces a folding helmet intended to be easier to carry.
- Manufactures in the UK for source-reported quality control.
- Launched in 2024 after six years of development and certification.
- Reportedly earned about $650,000 in its first year and approached $1 million annual revenue.
- Serves both users and gift-buying or safety-concerned family members.

## Evidence

## Qualifications
The source does not independently evaluate safety, comfort, durability, patent scope, return rates, margin, customer acquisition cost, or repeat/referral behavior. A distinctive folded shape may attract attention without changing consistent helmet use.
