# Fuliang / 浮梁

Fuliang / 浮梁 is the older administrative and regional context around [[Jingdezhen]] in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode notes that the area was earlier known for tea, while later porcelain production made Jingdezhen the stronger name.

Fuliang matters institutionally because the Yuan court created the Fuliang porcelain office in 1278. The source uses that office to explain [[OfficialSupervisionCivilianKilns|官监民烧]], where state demand and supervision shaped private kiln production without all production becoming directly official.
