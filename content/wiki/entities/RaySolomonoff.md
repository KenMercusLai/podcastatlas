# Ray Solomonoff / 芮

## Overview
Ray Solomonoff appears in the source as the Dartmouth participant whose probabilistic reasoning path was less dominant at the time but later became easier to recognize.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Ray as a counterweight to symbolic deduction. He is described as disagreeing with a pure deductive-reasoning route, keeping detailed notes during the workshop, and later working as an individual contributor whose papers were better appreciated decades afterward.

Yang Bin's source-scoped interpretation is that Ray can be seen as one precursor to probability-based next-token prediction. That claim is useful for the wiki's AI-history branch, but it should remain qualified until corroborated by more technical sources.

## Key Characteristics
- Dartmouth participant associated by the source with probabilistic reasoning.
- Detailed note-taker whose records helped the Rockefeller Foundation report.
- Later individual contributor and consultant rather than a mainstream institution-builder in the episode's telling.
- Used as an example of delayed recognition for non-mainstream technical routes.
