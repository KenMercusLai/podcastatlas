# Coze / 扣子

## Overview

Coze / 扣子 is the ByteDance agent/workflow product discussed in [[270-da-chang-yazhu-ai-bangong-feishu-he-dingding-que-xian-chengle-peijue-lmb4dgcgov3mr4cn7cikbghpfro4]] and [[272-cong-feishu-jizuo-dao-agent-youxian-doubao-gongzuo-all-in-one-jinzhui-workbuddy-lqmfcnfkkhoxt440qy26vwpnvswp]] as part of ByteDance's developer and work-agent evolution.

## Current Profile

The bounded sources describe Coze as a product that was visible before the newer Doubao Work push but initially closer to developer, workflow, or agent-building audiences. Episode 272 says Coze and [[Trae]] were part of the Stone system that later merged into Flow, making Coze one of the components ByteDance has to integrate as it turns scattered AI applications into a work-oriented line.

The page treats Coze as adjacent to [[DoubaoWork|Doubao Work]] rather than identical to it. Its main wiki role is to mark the agent-building and workflow side of ByteDance's AI product stack as office-agent products converge.

## Key Characteristics

- ByteDance agent/workflow product named in the office-agent consolidation discussion.
- Initially framed as more developer-oriented than broad enterprise office adoption.
- Part of the Stone-to-Flow integration story with [[Trae]].
- Adjacent to Doubao Work as ByteDance consolidates AI products around work scenarios.
