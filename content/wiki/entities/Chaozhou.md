# Chaozhou / 潮州

## Overview

Chaozhou is a historical administrative place-name and a modern city-level anchor of [[Chaoshan]]. The current sources use it in two separated settings: [[HanYu|Han Yu]]'s Tang demotion and the modern regional economy formed around Shantou, Chaozhou, and Jieyang.

## Current Profile

In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-341-3-chuangzuo-331-ge-chengyu-de-dalao-you-duo-lihai-lpxxovwptomp2mmt64o5armp8xrk|Hanji 341-3]], Chaozhou is the distant southern posting to which Han Yu is demoted after the 819 Buddha-relic memorial. The episode uses his practical work and *Sacrificial Text to the Crocodiles* to make the place a test of whether a punished official continues to serve local people.

In [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046|No.205]], Chaozhou is one of three modern city-level anchors of Chaoshan and part of the 1991 division of the old Shantou region. That modern layer emphasizes administrative scale: separate governance for [[Shantou]], Chaozhou, and [[Jieyang]] can coexist with duplicated infrastructure, local competition, and weaker region-wide coordination.

## Key Characteristics

- Historical southern administrative setting for Han Yu's demotion and local-service memory.
- Modern city-level component of the wider Chaoshan cultural and economic region.
- Part of a three-city comparison with Shantou and Jieyang after the 1991 administrative split.
- Place where historical memory and modern regional coordination questions attach to different territorial arrangements.

## Evidence

- Tang local-service memory: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-341-3-chuangzuo-331-ge-chengyu-de-dalao-you-duo-lihai-lpxxovwptomp2mmt64o5armp8xrk|Hanji 341-3]] locates Han Yu's continued administration and crocodile narrative in Chaozhou after imperial punishment.
- Modern regional role: [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046|No.205]] places Chaozhou in the contemporary Shantou–Chaozhou–Jieyang frame and links the 1991 split to regional coordination problems.
- Maritime-commercial context: [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046|No.205]] situates the wider region within overseas migration, qiaopi remittances, and reform-era industrialization.

## Qualifications

The Tang administrative unit and the modern prefecture-level city should not be assumed to share identical boundaries or governing functions. Hanji 341-3 is a brief admiring narrative and does not verify the crocodile story's causal or ecological details; No.205 uses Chaozhou mainly as one component of a larger regional argument rather than as a standalone urban history.

## What Changed

- Added the Tang layer of Han Yu's demotion and continued local service.
- Reframed the page to distinguish historical administrative memory from the modern city and regional economy.
- Preserved the 1991 fragmentation account while qualifying territorial continuity across periods.
