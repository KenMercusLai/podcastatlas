# Chaozhou / 潮州

Chaozhou is one of the three modern city-level anchors of [[Chaoshan]] in [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046]]. The episode uses it less as a standalone city story than as part of the historical Chaozhou prefecture and the 1991 division of the old Shantou region.

Its role in the source is to clarify scale. Before the split, the larger Shantou/Chaoshan area could be discussed as one regional economy; after the split, [[Shantou]], Chaozhou, and [[Jieyang]] gained separate administration but also faced [[RegionalAdministrativeFragmentation]] and weaker coordinated infrastructure planning.
