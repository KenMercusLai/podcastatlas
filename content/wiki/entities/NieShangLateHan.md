## Overview

聂尚 / Nie Shang enters the wiki as the Shu commandery administrator who succeeds [[DengXunEasternHan|Deng Xun]] as Protector of the Qiang and fails quickly in a conciliation attempt toward [[MiTangQiangLeader|Mi Tang]]. Hanji 767 uses him as the negative contrast to Deng's accumulated frontier legitimacy.

## Current Profile

Nie Shang's policy is initially conciliatory. He sends an interpreter to bring Mi Tang back to the large and small Yu valleys; Mi Tang responds by sending his grandmother Bi Que to meet him. Nie then personally escorts her beyond the frontier barrier and sends the interpreter Tian Si with five people to escort her back to Qiang territory.

The apparent opening collapses. Mi Tang rebels again, kills Tian Si and the escorts with allied groups, and attacks Jincheng fort. Nie is punished and dismissed. The host describes the mistake as one-sided wishful thinking, compounded by unwillingness to accept Deng Xun's standard and by a desire for quick credit.

## Key Characteristics

- Shu commandery administrator appointed after Deng Xun as Protector of the Qiang.
- Conciliation-minded frontier official whose gesture toward Mi Tang lacks adequate trust testing.
- Sends Bi Que back under escort, creating a vulnerable party that Mi Tang later kills.
- Failure case where goodwill, speed, and credit seeking substitute for grounded frontier judgment.
- Negative foil to Deng Xun's slower and deeper legitimacy with frontier communities.

## Qualifications
