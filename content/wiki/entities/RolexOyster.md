# Rolex Oyster

Rolex Oyster is the waterproof wristwatch line registered by [[Rolex]] in 1926 and discussed in [[rolex-1]] as a major technical and branding milestone. The source says [[HansWilsdorf]] bought an exclusive patent for a moisture-proof winding stem and crown system, then used [[MercedesGleitze]]'s swim publicity to make the waterproof claim memorable.

The Oyster matters because it turned waterproofing from an engineering feature into [[ProfessionalToolBranding]]. The product showed that Rolex could combine acquired technology, naming, public proof, and mass advertising into a durable [[ConsumerBrandMoat]].
