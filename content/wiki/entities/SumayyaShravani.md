# Sumayya Shravani

## Overview
Sumayya Shravani is the [[DataScienceWithSam]] guest in [[ep-43-the-outsiders-advantage-how-diverse-perspectives-unlock-enterprise-ai-success]]. The episode identifies her as a technical program manager and data infrastructure lead at [[UniversityOfColoradoDenver]], where she manages a large analytics environment, and as the builder of [[DataReady]].

## Current Profile
Her profile in the source is an enterprise AI adoption practitioner who connects data readiness with institutional trust and user behavior. She argues that organizations often ask whether a model, budget, or dataset is ready while missing whether people trust the institution enough to change daily work.

The episode also makes her outsider experience part of the professional profile. Sumayya says she came to the United States from Bangladesh at 19 and learned institutional systems as an adult from the outside, which trained her to notice unstated rules, hidden decision structures, and situations where users may avoid asking questions because they fear looking like they do not belong.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines data infrastructure work with enterprise AI adoption and readiness thinking.
- Treats [[InstitutionalTrustAIAdoption]] as a core adoption condition rather than a soft side issue.
- Uses outsider experience as a practical diagnostic lens for hidden workflow and belonging barriers.
- Built [[DataReady]] to audit semantic layers, documentation, model quality, and governance posture before copilot testing.
- Recommends adoption measurement through frontline user interviews, [[AIOverwriteRate]], and [[QuietAIAdoptionDeparture]] rather than launch activity alone.

## Qualifications
The profile is based on one podcast source. The source does not independently verify the scale, architecture, or outcomes of [[DataReady]], nor does it provide before-and-after adoption data from Sumayya's own enterprise rollouts. Its strongest claim is a practitioner interpretation of adoption failure, not a controlled study.
