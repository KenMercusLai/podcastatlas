# Nevada

Nevada appears in [[tech-20260424-0424-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260424-0424-mp-tech-pod-128]] as one of the states whose attorney general reached settlements with [[Roblox]] over child-safety concerns. The episode groups Nevada with [[Alabama]] and [[WestVirginia|West Virginia]] in a $36 million settlement package.

[[tech-20260129-0129-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260129-0129-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds Nevada as the location of [[RedwoodMaterials]]' reused EV battery project for a local data center. [[ColinCampbell]] says the 60 megawatt-hour, 12 megawatt system was built in four months and operates disconnected from the grid, making Nevada a concrete case for [[SecondLifeEVBatteryStorage]] and [[DataCenterPowerBottleneck]].
