# Talking Heads Toastmasters Club

Talking Heads Toastmasters Club is the Houston Toastmasters club named in [[ep-17-ais-impact-on-creativity-a-consumers-perspective]], where [[MarkDataScienceWithSam|Mark]] serves as president. The club supplies the source's most concrete public-speaking example: Mark uses [[ChatGPT]] to generate draft speeches, then edits, fact-checks, rehearses, and personalizes them before delivery.

In the wiki, the club is a source-scoped community setting for [[AIFirstDraftGeneration]] and [[AICreativeCollaboration]]. It shows AI entering an ordinary volunteer and self-development context rather than a high-stakes enterprise, research, or classroom setting.
