# Gnaeus Octavius / 格奈乌斯·屋大维

## Overview

Gnaeus Octavius / 格奈乌斯·屋大维 is the 87 BC consul whom episode 703 uses as a conservative counterpart to [[LuciusCorneliusCinna]] during the first aftermath of [[LuciusCorneliusSulla]]'s seizure of Rome.

## Current Profile

The source presents Octavius as the new consul more likely to honor Sulla's settlement after Sulla departs for the east. His major role is the conflict with Cinna over the enfranchisement of Italians after the [[SocialWar]]. Octavius expels Cinna, and the Senate then strips Cinna of office and declares him a public enemy.

Octavius's profile turns tragic when the logic he helps use against Cinna returns to Rome as armed revenge. Cinna and [[GaiusMarius]] besiege the city; after [[GnaeusPompeiusStrabo]] dies and the attackers enter Rome, Octavius refuses to flee and is killed by Marius's men while seated in his chair of office.

## Key Characteristics

- Octavius appears as the more conservative consul after Sulla's first march on Rome.
- His dispute with Cinna centers on how far Italian citizenship settlement should go.
- He helps convert a consular disagreement into expulsion, office stripping, and public-enemy politics.
- He remains committed to his office posture even when flight might save him.
- His death shows how formal office dignity no longer protects a Roman magistrate once armed revenge enters the city.
