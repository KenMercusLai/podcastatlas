# 张曼成 / Zhang Mancheng (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-930-ruhe-pochu-tanxin-he-buganxin-lkyysh5jbgxwjq8ayrfar4gsjdcd|Hanji 930]] names Zhang Mancheng as the earlier [[YellowTurbansLateHan|Yellow Turban]] leader whose death does not end the Nanyang rebellion. After he is eliminated, the remaining troops elect [[ZhaoHongLateHan|赵弘]], and the force grows again to more than 100,000 people.

The source uses Zhang Mancheng mainly as the precondition for the next command chain. His importance here is organizational: removing a leader does not dissolve the movement when the underlying armed network can select a successor and resume operations.
