# 无诸 / Wuzhu (Minyue king)

无诸 / Wuzhu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-169-pandian-liubang-dengjiqian-buweirenzhi-de-gushi-2-lqq8qhramfzii5o5icesd-uagwkc|Hanji 169 part 2]] as the former Yue ruler whom [[LiuBang|刘邦]] makes king of [[Minyue|闽越]] over Minzhong after the Han accession. The available transcript writes the name as "吴珠"; the page keeps that spelling source-scoped while using the disambiguated Minyue Wuzhu identity implied by the episode's Yue and Minzhong context.

The source presents him as a descendant of [[Goujian|勾践]]. After [[ChuState|楚国]] destroyed [[YueState|越国]], Yue royal descendants scattered along the Jiangnan coast. During the Qin rebellion, the episode says Wuzhu followed [[WuRui|吴芮]] and joined the anti-Qin coalition, but [[XiangYu|项羽]] did not make him a king after Qin's fall.

That omission becomes a Chu-Han alignment signal. In the episode's reading, Wuzhu has reason to support Liu Bang during the Chu-Han struggle because Liu Bang can supply the recognition Xiang Yu withheld. His Minyue kingship therefore joins [[ChangshaKingdom|长沙国]] as a southern branch of [[BattlefieldVictoryToFoundingOrder|战场胜利到建国秩序]].

## Key Claims
- The transcript's "吴珠" is treated as a source spelling for the Minyue Wuzhu context, not as a separate canonical page.
- Wuzhu's legitimacy in the source rests on Yue royal descent and on service in the anti-Qin and anti-Xiang fields.
- Liu Bang's grant makes southeastern recognition part of Western Han's founding settlement.
