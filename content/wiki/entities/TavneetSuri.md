# Tavneet Suri

Tavneet Suri is the MIT economist who guides [[PlanetMoney]]'s Kenya and Nigeria Summer School episode in [[piles-of-cash-and-a-town-of-solutions-in-kenya-nigeria-summer-school]]. The source identifies her as Kenyan and uses her to connect visible African entrepreneurship with the policy questions of jobs, [[HumanCapitalDevelopment]], [[MissingMiddle]], and [[RandomizedControlledTrials]].

Suri's role in the episode is interpretive. She explains why African demographic growth creates a job-creation challenge, why entrepreneurs need both skills and capital, why informal records make bank lending hard, and why education and health investments can raise long-run productivity.
