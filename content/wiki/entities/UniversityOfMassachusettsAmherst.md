# University of Massachusetts Amherst

The University of Massachusetts Amherst appears in [[the-real-horror-of-alien-and-how-it-explains-why-were-not-paid-enough]] as [[ArinDube|Arin Dube]]'s institution. The episode uses his UMass Amherst labor-economics role to ground a public explanation of [[LaborMonopsony]] through [[AlienFilm|Alien]].

In this wiki, the page anchors a labor-economics research connection rather than a broad institutional profile. Its main relevance is that Dube's minimum-wage and wage-standard work becomes part of the wiki's [[LaborMarketCounterforces]] and worker-bargaining branch.
