# Ningbo Zhoushan Port / 宁波舟山港

## Overview

Ningbo Zhoushan Port / 宁波舟山港 is the port infrastructure node in [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]]. The episode uses it to connect Ningbo's manufacturing base with foreign trade, logistics scale, and global market access.

## Current Profile

The source presents the port as more than background scenery. Its cargo throughput, container routes, and links to hundreds of global ports help explain why Ningbo manufacturers learned export markets early and could keep tying local industrial specialization to international customers.

## Key Characteristics

- The port is Ningbo's major logistics and foreign-trade infrastructure.
- The episode reports more than 1.4 billion tons of cargo throughput in 2025 and a seventeenth consecutive year at global number one by cargo throughput.
- It also reports 309 container routes by the end of 2025, connecting more than 700 ports across more than 200 countries and regions.
- Its strategic role is to support a [[PortEnabledManufacturingCity|port-enabled manufacturing city]] rather than only port operations.
