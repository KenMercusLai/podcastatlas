# 郭泰 / Guo Tai (late Han)

郭泰 / Guo Tai (late Han) enters through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-931-rensheng-jiannan-chu-bianshi-xiuxin-shi-lpzmvxop8lmlwvxhoojzj5ok9qd6|Hanji 931]] as the reputation witness for [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] before Wang Yun's later anti-[[DongZhuo|董卓]] role. The episode says Guo Tai praised Wang Yun as having the capacity to assist a ruler.

The page is intentionally narrow. Guo Tai matters here because the host uses his praise to turn Wang Yun's ambition, classical study, and riding-archery discipline into early [[MoralReputationPoliticalCapital|道德名望政治资本]], not because the source gives a fuller biography of Guo Tai.
