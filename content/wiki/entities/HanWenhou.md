# 韩文侯 / Marquis Wen of Han

韩文侯 / Marquis Wen of Han appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-2-lvaqlgyccrugfo0l-wuwf1mt1kcm]] as [[HanLiehou|韩烈侯]]'s son and successor after Han Lie Hou dies in 387 BCE. The episode includes him in a wider fifteenth-year succession cluster across Qin, Zhao, and Han rather than giving him a separate biography.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl]] adds the other end of the reign: Han Wen Hou dies in 377 BCE, and his son [[HanAihou|韩哀侯]] succeeds. This makes his page a bridge between the previous succession cluster and the later Zhouji 09 succession notice.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] retroactively places Han Wenhou in the family-scale strategy that ends with Han's destruction of [[ZhengState|郑国]]. The source uses him as one generation in the continuity from [[HanKangzi|韩康子]] to [[HanAihou|韩哀侯]].
