# 鲁康公 / Duke Kang of Lu

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-15-jiu-bu-chun-yinfa-de-shijie-dazhan-li-iojxthq8komhlhyumho3yvtem]] adds Lu Kang Gong's accession in 355 BCE after [[LuGonggong|鲁共公]] dies. The episode also notes a textual detail: [[Shiji|《史记》]] gives his personal name as 屯, while [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] writes 毛, which the host treats as a likely character-copying error rather than a separate ruler.

鲁康公 / Duke Kang of Lu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-18-zhongguo-lishishang-di-yi-li-rentoushui-lgqnequk0rxejqgihwdqxqyqnsvq]] through a 346 BCE succession notice: he dies, and his son succeeds as [[LuJinggong|鲁景公]]. The episode does not expand his reign, so this page keeps the claim tied to that notice.

The source places Lu Kang Gong's death beside [[QiState|齐国]]'s killing of [[MouXin|牟辛]] and [[WeyState|卫国]]'s demotion. That makes him part of the episode's compressed [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]] chronology rather than a developed Lu biography.
