# Henry Keswick / 亨利·凯瑟克

Henry Keswick / 亨利·凯瑟克 appears in [[vol-269-xiao-lishi-bu-yao-pa-shi-jishu-xing-tiaozheng-1004793119]] as the young [[JardineMatheson|怡和]] and [[HongkongLand|香港置地]] leader associated with the 1972 takeover of [[DairyFarmInternational|牛奶公司]]. The episode emphasizes that he had stronger internal control and clearer execution authority than Dairy Farm's more dispersed governance system.

For the wiki, Keswick is important as a person-node for [[ConglomerateControlInColonialHongKong]]. The source does not make him merely a charismatic dealmaker; it uses him to show how family control, board alignment, Chinese business ties, advertising resources, and a share-exchange offer could operate together.
