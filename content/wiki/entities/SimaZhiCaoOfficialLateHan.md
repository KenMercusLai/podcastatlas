# 司马芝 / Sima Zhi (Cao official, late Han)

## Overview
司马芝 / Sima Zhi is a late-Han official mentioned in Hanji 1096 among the personnel appointed after [[CaoCao|曹操]] takes [[Jingzhou|荆州]].

## Current Profile
The episode gives Sima Zhi only a brief appointment notice in its account of post-conquest personnel movement. His current wiki significance is therefore narrow: he helps show that Cao Cao's takeover reorganizes administrative talent as well as territory. This page is deliberately distinct from [[SimaZhiLateHan|司马直]], the earlier Han official whose Chinese name and biography are different despite a colliding romanization.

## Key Characteristics
- Appears in the post-Jingzhou-conquest Cao-side appointment cluster.
- Represents administrative incorporation after territorial change.
- Requires explicit disambiguation from 司马直.
