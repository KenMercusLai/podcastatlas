## Current Profile
In the supplied episode summary, Chen Jia presents hematology as broader than leukemia and repeatedly resists diagnosis from one symptom, search result, or blood-count value. The discussion links leukemia classification and confirmatory testing to the lived realities of remission, transplantation, fertility, infection precautions, rare-blood-type transfusion support, cost, and donor recruitment.

## Key Characteristics

- Includes supportive, reproductive, financial, and family burdens in treatment discussions.
- Presents stem-cell donation as a medically screened registry and matching process.
