# Globe Theatre / 环球剧院

## Overview
Globe Theatre / 环球剧院 is the Shakespearean theatre institution used in the source to reconnect [[WilliamShakespeare|Shakespeare]] with live performance, commercial theatre, audience mixture, and actor training.

## Current Profile
The episode presents the Globe in two layers. The historical Globe, built in 1599 on the south bank of the Thames, belonged to the theatre world in which Shakespeare was playwright, actor, manager, and shareholder. The modern Globe, opened in 1997 near the original site, is treated as an educational reconstruction that makes early-modern staging conditions visible: open-air space, close audience contact, limited modern equipment, live music, and demanding speech-centered acting.

## Key Characteristics
- It anchors Shakespeare in a theatre business rather than only in literary authorship.
- Its historical audience mixed social ranks and supported a style that could combine high poetry with popular entertainment.
- Its performance conditions make voice, speed, gesture, and audience contact central.
- Its modern reconstruction serves as a bridge between historical theatre practice and contemporary spectatorship.
