# German Swiss International School / 香港德瑞国际学校

German Swiss International School / 香港德瑞国际学校 is the [[HongKong|Hong Kong]] school context for [[SuTinghao|Su Tinghao / 苏廷昊]] in [[151-17sui-bei-2026-nian-icml-shoulu-lunwen-de-xiao-shaonian-wo-bet-kaixin-kaixin-kaixin-lgs-qedm2hdrxrfkgnsphg4i5h5u]]. The source says Su was about to enter his final high-school year there when interviewed.

The school's role in the episode is contextual rather than institutional. Su contrasts a strong Shanghai primary-school foundation with an international-school environment that gave him more room to explore math competitions, programming, chess, and AI research.
