# Axel von Fersen

## Overview

Axel von Fersen was a Swedish count and friend of [[MarieAntoinette|Marie Antoinette]] who planned and personally assisted the royal family's attempted escape from Paris in June 1791.

## Current Profile

The source presents Fersen as the operational planner of [[FlightToVarennes|the Flight to Varennes]]. He entered the Tuileries in disguise, favored separating the travelers to reduce attention, arranged transport, and drove one of the cabs himself while disguised as a Parisian coachman. His more discreet design was displaced by the royal couple's insistence on traveling with their children, [[LouisXVI|Louis XVI]]'s sister, attendants, and guards, producing a larger party and conspicuous coach whose timetable proved fragile.

## Key Characteristics

- Swedish aristocrat close to Marie Antoinette.
- Planner and active participant in the royal escape attempt.
- Preferred smaller, separated travel parties as a security measure.
- Used disguise and personally drove a cab during the departure from Paris.
- Saw his operational plan altered by royal family and household requirements.
