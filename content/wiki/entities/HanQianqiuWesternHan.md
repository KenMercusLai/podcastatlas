## Overview

韩千秋 / Han Qianqiu is the Western Han commander whose small force enters [[Nanyue|南越]], wins several early engagements, and is then annihilated by [[LyuJiaNanyue|吕嘉]] near Panyu. His posthumous treatment makes him the source's central example of courage being honored without battlefield success.

## Current Profile

Hanji 352-2 presents Han Qianqiu as the leader of the failed first military thrust against Lü Jia. The advance initially appears successful but ends with the entire group killed about forty li from Panyu. [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] then distinguishes outcome from conduct: the force has not completed meritorious work, yet is still called the bravest vanguard.

The court compensates the dead soldiers' families and makes Han Qianqiu's son [[HanYannianWesternHan|韩延年]] marquis of Cheng'an. Han Qianqiu's place in the wiki is therefore not that of a victorious conqueror, but of a dead commander whose service becomes a signal for [[FallenVanguardHeirReward|阵亡先锋遗属封赏]] before a much larger campaign.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han commander sent into Nanyue before the large conquest mobilization.
- Leader of a force that moves from initial success to complete destruction near Panyu.
- Vanguard remembered for courage even though the mission does not produce victory.
- Father of Han Yannian, whom the episode says is made marquis of Cheng'an.

- Advance and defeat: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-352-2-hanwudi-weihe-fengshang-baijun-zhi-jiang-li16pfjb6p0owys5htecscfomhch|Hanji 352-2]] says Han Qianqiu takes several cities before Lü Jia's force kills the entire unit near Panyu.
- Court judgment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-352-2-hanwudi-weihe-fengshang-baijun-zhi-jiang-li16pfjb6p0owys5htecscfomhch|Hanji 352-2]] says the group has not completed merit but is still the army's courageous vanguard.
- Family reward: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-352-2-hanwudi-weihe-fengshang-baijun-zhi-jiang-li16pfjb6p0owys5htecscfomhch|Hanji 352-2]] says Han Yannian receives the Cheng'an marquisate after his father's death.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one short popular-history episode. It does not establish Han Qianqiu's earlier career, exact force size, detailed route, tactical responsibility for the defeat, or whether every reported city changed durable control. The reward records the court's stated evaluation; it does not turn the failed expedition into a victory.

## What Changed

- Created a source-bounded profile separating Han Qianqiu's operational defeat from the court's positive judgment of vanguard courage.
- Connected his death and his son's title to the later mobilization signal without claiming a proven private motive for Han Wudi.
