## Overview

韩千秋 / Han Qianqiu is the former Jibei chancellor and volunteer commander who argues that royal support inside [[Nanyue|南越]] will let a small brave force kill [[LyuJiaNanyue|吕嘉]]. His two-thousand-person mission wins several early engagements and is then annihilated near Panyu; his posthumous treatment makes him the sources' central example of courage being honored without battlefield success.

## Current Profile

Hanji 352-1 supplies the decision before the march. After [[ZhuangCanWesternHan|庄参]] refuses a two-thousand-person mission as too large for friendly persuasion but too small for coercion, Han Qianqiu volunteers. He argues that Nanyue is small and that [[ZhaoXingNanyue|赵兴]] and [[QueenDowagerJiuNanyue|樛太后]] will respond from inside, so three hundred brave men can kill Lü Jia. Wudi instead sends him and the queen dowager's brother [[JiuLeNanyue|樛乐]] with two thousand.

Hanji 352-2 presents the operational outcome. The advance initially appears successful but ends with the entire group killed about forty li from Panyu. [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] then distinguishes outcome from conduct: the force has not completed meritorious work, yet is still called the bravest vanguard.

The court compensates the dead soldiers' families and makes Han Qianqiu's son [[HanYannianWesternHan|韩延年]] marquis of Cheng'an. Han Qianqiu's place in the wiki is therefore not that of a victorious conqueror, but of a dead commander whose service becomes a signal for [[FallenVanguardHeirReward|阵亡先锋遗属封赏]] before a much larger campaign.

## Key Characteristics

- Former Jibei chancellor who volunteers after Zhuang Can refuses the assignment.
- Commander who expects royal support to make a small strike against Lü Jia sufficient.
- Co-leader with Jiu Le of the mission sent into Nanyue before the large conquest mobilization.
- Leader of a force that moves from initial success to complete destruction near Panyu.
- Vanguard remembered for courage even though the mission does not produce victory.
- Father of Han Yannian, whom the episode says is made marquis of Cheng'an.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to two short popular-history summaries. “Former Jibei chancellor” adds one office but not a full earlier career. The sources do not settle his command relationship with Jiu Le, whether the royal party could actually coordinate with him after entry, his tactical responsibility for defeat, or whether every reported city changed durable control. His confidence and the exact three-hundred/two-thousand figures remain episode-attributed. The reward records the court's stated evaluation; it does not turn the failed expedition into a victory.
