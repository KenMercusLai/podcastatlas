# Gloria Steinem

## Overview
Gloria Steinem was an American journalist and feminist organizer whose career in [[drone-truth-russia-has-the-upper-hand-6aad03f98a1508074de8337e]] runs from a 1963 undercover Playboy Club exposé to co-founding [[MsMagazine|Ms. magazine]] and organizing for women's rights.

## Current Profile
The source presents Steinem as an institution builder shaped by gendered newsroom exclusion and shared testimony. Her own illegal abortion, her reporting on women speaking openly about abortion, and her experience of being confined to “women's subjects” helped turn individual experience into public journalism, fundraising, and discussion networks.

## Key Characteristics
- Used undercover reporting to expose women's workplace exploitation.
- Challenged gendered assignment boundaries in American journalism.
- Co-founded a national feminist magazine controlled by women.
- Converted shared personal testimony into organizing practice.
- Lived through both major legal gains and reversals for abortion rights.
