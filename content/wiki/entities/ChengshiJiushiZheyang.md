# 城市就是这样

## Overview
城市就是这样 is a city-observation series associated with [[ShangyeJiushiZheyang|商业就是这样]], using grounded urban cases to connect industry, business, administrative structure, local history, and residents' choices.

## Current Profile
Across its bounded wiki sources, the series has two complementary modes. The Shiyan episode treats one industrial city as a system made by [[SecondAutomobileWorks|二汽]], [[DongfengMotor|东风汽车]], [[ThirdFrontConstruction|三线建设]], headquarters relocation, supplier retention, and diversification. The [[DavidFishman|David Fishman]] episode turns the choice of cities itself into a research problem, showing why rankings need [[CityTypeSampling|city types]], district-level attention, repeated observation, and [[UrbanFieldInterviewing|interviews]] before they support broader claims.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses one or several cities to make abstract business and development systems concrete.
- Connects industrial history and corporate decisions to urban form, local opportunity, and resident mobility.
- Treats field visits as tests capable of revising the framing established before travel.
- Combines statistics and administrative research with interviews and situated observation.
- Preserves variation across regions, city centers, counties, industries, and time rather than forcing one national model.

## Evidence
- Industrial-city system: [[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-21-shiyan-yizuo-yin-qiche-erqi-you-bugan-bei-qiche-paoxia-de-chengshi-1001770066|城市就是这样21]] follows Shiyan from auto-led creation through Dongfeng's headquarters move and the retained commercial-vehicle base.
- Research-method correction: [[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-22-zhongguo-de-zhongweishu-chengshi-shi-nazuo-1021674577|城市就是这样22]] follows the failed search for one median city and the move toward regional types and person-centered field evidence.
- Individual choice as development evidence: [[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-22-zhongguo-de-zhongweishu-chengshi-shi-nazuo-1021674577|城市就是这样22]] uses Zixing and Shishi to show how tourism and clothing specialization become legible through residents' work and migration decisions.

## Qualifications
The wiki currently contains only two episodes from the series, so this profile should not be read as a complete account of its catalog. Both episodes use reported figures and short field encounters whose exact claims remain source-scoped. Grounded cases can expose mechanisms and counterexamples, but they do not automatically establish prevalence or causal effects.

## What Changed
- Added research design and sampling as explicit subjects of the series, beyond the earlier industrial-city case method.
- Replaced any implied one-city national proxy with plural city types and internal variation.
- Added resident choices and longitudinal revisits as checks on ranking- or image-based judgments.
