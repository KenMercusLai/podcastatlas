# 城市就是这样

城市就是这样 is the city-observation series context for [[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-21-shiyan-yizuo-yin-qiche-erqi-you-bugan-bei-qiche-paoxia-de-chengshi-1001770066]], connected in the source metadata to [[ShangyeJiushiZheyang|商业就是这样]]. In this episode, the series method is to use one city, [[Shiyan]], to connect industrial policy, corporate relocation, urban form, local memory, and contemporary business strategy.

The Shiyan episode extends the wiki's [[CityCommercialObservation]] branch from consumer-facing urban scenes into heavier industrial urbanism. Instead of asking how shops, platforms, tourism flows, or sports events work in cities, it asks how a single industrial system can make a city and then leave that city with both assets and constraints.
