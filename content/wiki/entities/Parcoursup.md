# Parcoursup

## Overview

Parcoursup is the French higher-education application and admissions platform that the source presents as a central target of the 2026 student protests.

## Current Profile

The platform combines academic records, subject choices, motivation statements, extracurricular experience, and program-specific review. Unlike a single examination threshold, it makes decisions taken from early secondary school consequential across a longer application process.

The source's main criticism is not automation alone but opacity plus unequal support. Programs can use locally defined criteria and processing methods that applicants may not understand, while affluent or highly educated families can obtain better subject-choice advice, application coaching, and paid assistance. Students in under-resourced schools may therefore face both weaker instruction and weaker navigation of the admissions system.

## Key Characteristics

- Centralizes applications to French higher-education programs.
- Evaluates a broader dossier than final examination performance alone.
- Makes earlier subject and grade choices consequential for later admission.
- Allows program-level variation in review criteria and processing.
- Gives applicants limited visibility into how some decisions are produced.
- Can amplify family-resource differences in information, counseling, and paid application support.

## Qualifications

The source summarizes a parliamentary assessment and protester criticism but does not provide the underlying report, platform documentation, code, program-level rules, applicant outcome data, or a causal estimate of inequality. Technical operation, political legitimacy, transparency, and distributional fairness are distinct questions; the episode's criticism does not establish that every program, criterion, or admission decision is arbitrary.
