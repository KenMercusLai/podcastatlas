## Overview

温青青 / Wen Qingqing is a central character in [[BixueJian|《碧血剑》]] whose courage, competence, generosity, insecurity, jealousy, and impulsiveness make her relationship with [[YuanChengzhi|袁承志]] both durable and unstable.

## Current Profile

The episode resists reducing Wen Qingqing to either a lovable heroine or a jealous “妖女.” Her marginal status as [[WenYiBixueJian|温仪]]'s daughter in a violent family helps explain why she expects abandonment and fights for attention, while beauty, ability, and partial family favor also give her the confidence to act forcefully.

Her jealousy changes with the perceived threat. She quarrels over minor rivals and nearly compromises a rescue through suspicion, yet withdraws privately when she concludes that Yuan may genuinely love [[AjiuBixueJian|阿九]]. That retreat joins fear, accurate perception, pride, and a last defense of dignity.

## Key Characteristics

- Capable, brave, romantically active, and willing to place inherited treasure at public service.
- Formed by insecure family status, missing paternal protection, and a coercive household culture.
- Uses anger, departure, and confrontation to manage perceived threats to attachment.
- Can respond to correction and change course, distinguishing her from characters fixed in cruelty.
- Shows greater restraint when a rival appears genuinely loved rather than merely imagined.

## Qualifications

Childhood insecurity is an explanatory literary frame, not a diagnosis or excuse for endangering others. The episode's “妖女” category and claims about why Jin Yong revised her jealousy are comparative interpretations rather than settled authorial facts.

## What Changed

- Created a profile balancing capability and generosity with threat sensitivity, harmful impulsivity, and capacity for change.
