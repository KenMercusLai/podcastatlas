# Guildford Dudley

## Overview

Guildford Dudley is [[LadyJaneGrey|Lady Jane Grey]]'s husband and the son of [[JohnDudleyDukeOfNorthumberland|John Dudley, Duke of Northumberland]]. His disputed status exposes one of the few moments in which the episode shows Jane exercising authority after accepting the crown.

## Current Profile

Jane refuses to make Guildford king and offers the lesser title Duke of Clarence, provoking conflict with his family. The dispute matters less for Guildford's independent program than for what it reveals about consort power, Dudley expectations, and Jane's capacity to resist them.

After Mary's victory, Guildford is tried with Jane and sentenced to death. The sentence initially appears likely to remain suspended, but the [[WyattRebellion|Wyatt Rebellion]] ends that prospect. He is executed shortly before Jane, who reportedly witnesses part of his final passage and the return of his body.

## Key Characteristics

- Guildford's marriage places the Dudley family inside the proposed royal settlement.
- Jane refuses to convert his marital position into kingship.
- His family treats his title and access to Jane as politically consequential.
- His fate remains joined to Jane's even after their regime collapses.
- Wyatt's rebellion turns a suspended sentence into execution.

## Evidence

- Consort dispute: [[294-lady-jane-grey-the-axe-falls-part-2-glt7286066638]] records Jane's refusal to crown Guildford and the angry Dudley family response.
- Trial and conditional sentence: [[294-lady-jane-grey-the-axe-falls-part-2-glt7286066638]] says Guildford and Jane were convicted in November but initially expected to survive.
- Execution: [[294-lady-jane-grey-the-axe-falls-part-2-glt7286066638]] places Guildford's death immediately before Jane's after Wyatt's rebellion.

## Qualifications

The source supplies little independent evidence about Guildford's political ideas, consent, or conduct beyond the kingship dispute, imprisonment, and execution. He should not be assigned his father's strategy simply because of kinship.

## What Changed

- Established Guildford's role as husband, proposed consort, and Dudley dynastic link.
- Used Jane's refusal of his kingship to clarify her bounded agency.
- Connected his eventual execution to the changed security environment after Wyatt's rebellion.
