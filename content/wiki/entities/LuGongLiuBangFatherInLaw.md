# 吕公 / Lu Gong

吕公 / Lu Gong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-6-dingji-tianshilun-touziren-lvgong-lhqqja27zxdiypgf6wuv1abgkw3j|Qinji 128-6]] as the man who recognizes [[LiuBang|刘邦]] before Liu Bang has real wealth or power. The source says Lu Gong comes from Shanfu, has an old friendship with the Pei County magistrate, and moves to [[PeiCounty|沛县]] after making enemies elsewhere.

His banquet creates the episode's central test. [[XiaoHe|萧何]] sorts guests by gift size, but Liu Bang arrives without invitation or money and publicly claims a gift of ten thousand cash. Lu Gong studies Liu Bang's face and bearing, sees him acting like a high guest despite the false pledge, and treats the performance as evidence of unusual future potential.

The episode's title calls Lu Gong a "top angel-round investor" because he makes a family-level bet before the external evidence exists. He keeps Liu Bang after the banquet and offers his daughter [[LuZhi|吕雉]] in marriage. The source also names Lu family members whose later links matter to the Han founding circle: Lu Ze and the transcript's "吕士之" follow Liu Bang, while Lu Xu marries [[FanKuai|樊哙]].
