# Jamal Khashoggi / 贾迈勒·卡舒吉

## Overview

Jamal Khashoggi was a Saudi journalist whose 2018 killing in the Saudi consulate in Istanbul is used by the current source to expose the coercive boundary of [[MohammedBinSalman|MBS]]’s reform image.

## Current Profile

The episode says Khashoggi entered the consulate on October 2, 2018 and did not emerge, after which Turkish disclosures and changing Saudi statements established an international crisis. It cites the UN investigation’s characterization of the killing as a premeditated extrajudicial execution.

Within the episode’s argument, Khashoggi is not an isolated biographical subject but the event that forced many outside observers to separate Saudi social and economic modernization from democratization or protection of political dissent.

## Key Characteristics

- Saudi journalist and Washington Post contributor in the source account.
- Killed after entering the Saudi consulate in Istanbul in October 2018.
- Central reputational rupture in international views of [[MohammedBinSalman|MBS]].
- Demonstrates that reformist presentation did not remove coercive authoritarian power.
