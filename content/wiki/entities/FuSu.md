# 扶苏 / Fu Su

## Overview
扶苏 / Fu Su is [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]]'s eldest son, a remonstrating prince sent to the northern frontier with [[MengTian|蒙恬]], and the strongest displaced alternative to [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] in this source cluster. His political afterlife outlasts him: later rebels use his reputation and unjust death to make anti-Qin mobilization legible.

## Current Profile
Fusu's profile joins three stages. He first warns that extreme punishment in the [[QinKengruIncident|秦代坑儒事件]] may destabilize the realm, after which Qin Shi Huang sends him to [[Shangjun|上郡]]. During the final tour, Qin Shi Huang eventually orders a letter summoning Fusu to the funeral at [[Xianyang|咸阳]] and, in the episode's reading, to succeed; the letter remains with [[ZhaoGao|赵高]] instead of being dispatched. The received account then has Zhao Gao, Li Si, and Hu Hai replace that opening with forged commands, while the [[ZhaoZhengShu|《赵正书》]] variant makes Fusu's distance a reason to choose nearby Hu Hai. After Fusu's death, Chen Sheng and Wu Guang reuse his name as a legitimacy resource.

## Key Characteristics
- Fusu is willing to remonstrate when imperial punishment threatens political stability.
- His frontier posting gives him a strong alliance with Meng Tian but leaves him physically absent from the deathbed transition.
- Qin Shi Huang's late summons makes Fusu the intended returnee in the received account, while the undelivered document leaves that intention vulnerable.
- His deference to a purported paternal command prevents him from verifying the forged order before suicide.
- His reputation as a wronged and comparatively acceptable Qin heir becomes usable by later rebels.

## Evidence
- Remonstrating prince: [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-125-2-lishishang-qinshihuang-weihe-yao-fenshu-kengru-lulaivzp1xqs71nbaltr-ygpjihw|Qinji 125-2]] records his objection to extreme punishment and the resulting Shangjun assignment.
- Late summons and documentary vulnerability: [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-127-1-jiemi-qinshihuang-weili-chujun-shi-shenme-yuanyin-lniz32t2r2d3thgqnbfarrqeq2a2|Qinji 127-1]] says Qin Shi Huang ordered Fusu to return for the funeral, but the letter remained with Zhao Gao.
- Competing succession accounts: [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-127-2-lisi-weisha-bu-bang-yingming-de-fusu-lq-vny5duwwzfubpeijr21oazxbg|Qinji 127-2]] presents the forged-order sequence and Fusu's suicide; [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-127-3-haiwai-huigui-yipi-zhujian-jiekai-huhai-lidi-zhi-mi-lja0pankcx4ugtylbxxqkwuavdnw|Qinji 127-3]] preserves the alternate nearby-heir account.
- Posthumous legitimacy: [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-2-qilai-bei-yapo-de-renmen-lpx9-iicwog4nj-nudaj-i4ajb25|Qinji 128-2]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-3-chensheng-wuguang-zudui-chenggong-locnaq-fo1fztyud5nghwkteams|Qinji 128-3]] show Chen Sheng and Wu Guang using Fusu's name and interpret it within a broader argument over who should inherit Qin's unified order.

## Qualifications
The evidence does not settle one uncontested accession sequence. Qinji 127-1 and 127-2 follow the received letter-and-forgery narrative, while Qinji 127-3 preserves a version in which Hu Hai may have been selected before Qin Shi Huang died. Fusu's title as an "英明" heir also exceeds what these sources can prove: they establish remonstrance, reputation, and political usefulness, but not how he would have governed.

## What Changed
- Added Qin Shi Huang's late order summoning Fusu to Xianyang for the funeral and succession.
- Identified the letter's non-dispatch and retention by Zhao Gao as the documentary gap before the later coup account.
- Clarified that Fusu's frontier distance was simultaneously a power base and a transition vulnerability.

## Relationships
- [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] - father whose punishment sends Fusu north and whose late summons points back toward him.
- [[MengTian|蒙恬]] - frontier commander, political ally, and co-target of the forged order.
- [[ZhaoGao|赵高]] - keeper of the undelivered letter and principal operator in the received coup account.
- [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] - nearby rival who succeeds instead of Fusu.
- [[SuccessionNonDesignationRisk|未定继承人风险]] - broader mechanism that makes Fusu's distance and the letter's status consequential.
- [[RebelMandateManufacture|起义天命制造]] - later mechanism through which Fusu's remembered legitimacy is reused.
