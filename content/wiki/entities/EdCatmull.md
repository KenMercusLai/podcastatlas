# Ed Catmull

Ed Catmull is the computer graphics and animation-organization figure in [[disney-the-renaissance-and-the-empire-1]]. The source traces his path from the University of Utah through New York Tech and [[GeorgeLucas]]'s Lucasfilm computer graphics group before that group becomes [[Pixar]] under [[SteveJobs]].

Catmull matters in the episode because Pixar's advantage is not treated as software alone. Its story reels, production process, animation tools, rendering discipline, and leadership culture become the system [[BobIger]] wants to preserve when [[TheWaltDisneyCompany]] buys Pixar.
