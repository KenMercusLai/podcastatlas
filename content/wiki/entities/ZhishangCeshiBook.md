# 《智商测试》

《智商测试》 is the book discussed in [[37-zhishang-ceshi-qingwen-ni-shi-zhili-poluomen-ma-608020679]]. The episode uses it as a history of [[IntelligenceTesting|intelligence testing]] from nineteenth-century measurement culture through [[FrancisGalton|Francis Galton / 弗朗西斯·高尔顿]], [[EugenicGovernance|eugenic governance]], [[AlfredBinet|Alfred Binet / 阿尔弗雷德·宾内]], immigration screening, army testing, school tracking, forced sterilization, and Nazi-era disability persecution.

For the wiki, the book matters less as a single factual authority than as the episode's organizing source for a moral history of measurement. It lets the hosts ask when a test is useful as self-knowledge or educational support, and when it becomes [[PsychometricSocialSorting|psychometric social sorting]] that allocates dignity, opportunity, mobility, or bodily autonomy.
