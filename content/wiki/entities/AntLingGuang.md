# 蚂蚁灵光 / Ant LingGuang

## Overview
蚂蚁灵光 / Ant LingGuang is an [[AntGroup|Ant Group]] consumer AI product led by [[CaiWei|蔡伟]], described as evolving from fast research and generated interactive applications toward a [[PersonalLifeAgent|personal agent]] for managing continuing life matters.

## Current Profile
LingGuang's reported direction combines research, multimodal expression, interactive generated applications, memory, initiative, long-horizon execution, and access to authorized services. Its current wedge is “help me manage things,” while its longer-term ambition is to act across apps, service ecosystems, household devices, vehicles, and potentially robotic hardware.

## Key Characteristics
- Started as Fast Research in March 2025 before broadening into interactive generated applications and personal assistance.
- Prioritizes completion and ongoing matter management over exposing tool traces to ordinary users.
- Uses the Alipay ecosystem as an early source of callable services and partner relationships.
- Favors restrained, personalized initiative that can work in the background without unnecessary interruption.
- Develops product and model capabilities together around task decomposition, exception recovery, and tool use.
- Treats privacy, authorization, compliance, and safety as prerequisites for shopping, payment, personal data, and cross-device action.
