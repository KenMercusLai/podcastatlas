## Overview

张厂 / Zhang Chang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-430-chigua-qunzhong-qianren-yu-xianren-de-gongdou-daxi-lkfcjewfhmhfgw39vqfqkpa9umzq|Hanji 430]] as the Shanyang governor ordered by [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] to secretly monitor [[LiuHeHaihunHou|刘贺]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Zhang Chang as a surveillance and reporting official rather than as an independent policy-maker. He receives a confidential order, keeps it hidden, observes Liu He's contacts and daily condition, probes him through conversation, and periodically reports the former emperor's body, speech, family, and risk profile to Xuan.

## Key Characteristics

- Shanyang governor trusted with a confidential imperial monitoring task.
- Turns local administration into intelligence gathering around a deposed emperor.
- Reports bodily details, conversation, family count, and behavioral judgment.
- Distinct from [[ZhangChangWesternHan|张敞]], the Jingzhao official in other Xuan-era pages.

## Qualifications
