# 梅鋗 / Mei Juan

梅鋗 / Mei Juan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-143-xiangyu-shi-ruhe-feijin-xinji-nongsi-chuhuaiwang-mixin-lstfzfvbd2h9i39f7y5dxrbxxwv2|Hanji 143]] as a subordinate of [[WuRui|吴芮]] who receives a large marquisate in [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s settlement. The episode says he had much merit and was made a ten-thousand-household marquis.

His page records a lower-rank reward path inside the same system that makes kings. Mei Juan is not elevated to one of the major kingdoms in this source, but his marquisate shows that Xiang Yu's distribution reaches below the top lords to reward important sub-commanders.
