# Yongkang / 永康

## Overview
Yongkang / 永康 is presented in this source as a Zhejiang manufacturing cluster whose hardware and cup-making base helped titanium cups move from niche gear into broader consumer production.

## Current Profile
The episode describes Yongkang as a hardware-manufacturing city with a strong thermos and cup industry. Its relevance is not only that it produces many cups, but that local firms had already been experimenting with titanium in cups and cookware before the recent consumer boom. As raw titanium became cheaper and processing know-how improved, Yongkang's small and midsized manufacturers could produce more shapes, colors, and price points.

Yongkang therefore functions as the supply-side counterpart to outdoor lifestyle demand: consumer interest made titanium cups attractive, but cluster capability made the category easier to copy, scale, and eventually over-supply.

## Key Characteristics
- Yongkang is framed as a major Chinese cup and hardware manufacturing base.
- The source says close to 60% of China's thermos cups are produced there.
- Local producers had studied titanium cups and cookware before the recent boom.
- Early titanium-cup production faced immature processing, higher scrap rates, and supply shortages.
- Over time, more small and midsized firms learned to produce varied titanium-cup forms and colors.
- The same cluster capability that enabled scale also intensified homogeneity and inventory clearing.
