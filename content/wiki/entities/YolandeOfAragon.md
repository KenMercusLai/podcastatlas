# Yolande of Aragon

## Overview

Yolande of Aragon was [[CharlesVIIOfFrance|Charles VII's]] mother-in-law and a principal dynastic supporter of the Valois cause during its weakest phase in the [[HundredYearsWar|Hundred Years' War]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Yolande as an active political backer rather than a merely familial connection. Her daughter Marie married Charles, and the birth of their son Louis in 1423 strengthened her investment in preserving a Valois succession after the [[TreatyOfTroyes|Treaty of Troyes]] had disinherited Charles.

Her support mattered inside a coalition whose resources and legitimacy were precarious. Charles remained uncrowned, English and Burgundian power dominated much of northern France, and defeat at Verneuil preceded the crisis at Orléans. The source identifies Yolande's commitment as one element sustaining the alternative dynasty until [[JoanOfArc|Joan of Arc's]] mission changed its prospects.

## Key Characteristics

- Angevin dynastic actor supporting the disinherited Dauphin Charles.
- Mother-in-law whose daughter's marriage tied her family to the Valois succession.
- Grandmother whose grandson Louis gave the threatened line a future heir.
- Political supporter operating during English-Burgundian military predominance.
