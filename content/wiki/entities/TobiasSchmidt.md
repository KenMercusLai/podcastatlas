# Tobias Schmidt

## Overview

Tobias Schmidt was the German piano maker who built the mechanical decapitation device developed under [[AntoineLouis|Antoine Louis]]'s supervision.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Schmidt as the machine's fabricator rather than a political theorist of punishment. His craft work turned the Assembly's egalitarian execution principle and Louis's technical direction into a tested device, first tried on animals and corpses and then used on [[NicolasJacquesPelletier|Nicolas-Jacques Pelletier]] in April 1792.

## Key Characteristics

- He brought maker expertise from piano construction to penal machinery.
- He built and tested the device under Louis's direction.
- His role is often obscured by the machine's attachment to Guillotin's name.

## Evidence

### Fabrication and testing

- [[505-the-french-revolution-the-shadow-of-the-guillotine-part-3-glt1620539569]] identifies Schmidt as the builder and describes trials on a sheep, a calf, and three corpses.

## Qualifications

- The source does not give a complete workshop record or settle attribution for each mechanical feature.
- Building the machine does not by itself establish Schmidt's view of capital punishment or later revolutionary uses.

## What Changed

- The wiki now separates the guillotine's physical construction from Guillotin's reform advocacy and Louis's supervision.

## Relationships

- [[AntoineLouis]] - medical and technical supervisor of Schmidt's construction work.
- [[JosephIgnaceGuillotin]] - reformer whose name displaced both builder and designer in public memory.
- [[NicolasJacquesPelletier]] - first person executed with the completed device.
- [[GuillotineReformSpectacleParadox]] - historical frame for the machine's humane claim and scalable use.
