# Margaret Thatcher / 玛格丽特·撒切尔

Margaret Thatcher / 玛格丽特·撒切尔 enters the wiki through [[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] as the [[UnitedKingdom|British]] prime minister who read and acted on intelligence from [[OlegGordievsky|Oleg Gordievsky]]. The episode says Gordievsky's reporting shaped her understanding of Soviet fear and helped push Britain toward contact with [[MikhailGorbachev|Mikhail Gorbachev]].

Her second role is operational. When [[OperationPimlico]] became active and some British officials worried about damaging detente with the [[SovietUnion]], Thatcher approved the extraction. The episode frames that approval as [[ExtractionPromiseEthics]]: a state that asks a source to take mortal risk must be willing to honor the rescue promise.
