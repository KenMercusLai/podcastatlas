# Multidisciplinary Association for Psychedelic Studies

## Overview
The Multidisciplinary Association for Psychedelic Studies (MAPS) is presented in the source as the organization that built an authorized clinical-research program around MDMA-assisted psychotherapy for severe PTSD.

## Current Profile
In the episode's June 2023 frame, MAPS coordinates or supports clinical groups in the United States, Canada, and elsewhere to compare psychotherapy plus [[MDMA]] with psychotherapy plus placebo. The source emphasizes preparation, paired therapists, monitored drug sessions, and follow-up integration rather than recreational access or stand-alone dosing. It also portrays MAPS as seeking legal clinical pathways while avoiding a general recreational-legalization message.

## Key Characteristics
- Develops authorized clinical research on MDMA-assisted psychotherapy.
- Uses structured preparation, monitored sessions, and integration around drug administration.
- Focuses the discussed trials on severe PTSD and associated functional burden.
- Operates through a multi-site clinical network described as international.
- Is portrayed as pursuing bounded clinical use rather than recreational legalization.

## Evidence
- Research program - [[the-science-of-mdma-its-therapeutic-uses-benefits-risks-scim6952643449]] describes MAPS obtaining authorization for controlled PTSD trials.
- Protocol structure - [[the-science-of-mdma-its-therapeutic-uses-benefits-risks-scim6952643449]] describes preparation, MDMA-or-placebo sessions, paired therapists, and follow-up therapy.
- Institutional stance - [[the-science-of-mdma-its-therapeutic-uses-benefits-risks-scim6952643449]] says MAPS tried to keep the work within legal and clinical boundaries.

## Qualifications
This page records the organization's role as described in a June 2023 episode. It does not establish current organizational structure, approval status, legal access, protocol availability, or the final regulatory interpretation of the trials.
