# Claude-Methos Preview

## Overview
Claude-Methos Preview is the [[Anthropic]] cyber-capable model name preserved from the Marketplace Tech source; the new [[AllIn|All-In]] source discusses a closely related or possibly renamed Anthropic model as "Mythos." The wiki should keep the names source-scoped until a later source confirms whether Claude-Methos Preview, Mythos, [[MythosAISecurityTest]], and [[ProjectGlassfin]] refer to the same system, adjacent tests, or transcription variants.

## Current Profile
Across the bounded sources, the model matters less as a benchmark result than as a release-governance case. It is described as unusually strong at finding security vulnerabilities, valuable for defenders, and too risky for broad public release because the same capability can support exploit discovery or exploit chaining.

## Key Characteristics
- It is treated as a cyber-capable frontier system rather than a general consumer assistant.
- Access is routed through [[ProjectGlasswing]] to trusted organizations instead of broad public release.
- Its capability is dual-use: the same vulnerability-discovery strength can help defenders patch old weaknesses or help attackers find exploitable paths.
- The All-In discussion adds disagreement over whether the rollout is prudent self-restraint, safety theater, or both.
- The sources do not provide architecture, benchmark details, audited vulnerability counts, or independent validation.

## Evidence
- Restricted-access evidence: [[tech-20260410-0410-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260410-0410-mp-tech-pod-128]] says Anthropic did not release Claude-Methos Preview publicly and instead gave access through [[ProjectGlasswing]] to more than 40 companies and technology organizations.
- Dual-use evidence: [[tech-20260410-0410-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260410-0410-mp-tech-pod-128]] and [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-anthropics-30b-ramp-mythos-doomsday-openclaw-ankled-iran-war-ceasefire-israels-influence-40808300]] both frame vulnerability discovery as useful for defense and dangerous if turned toward exploitation.
- Release-governance evidence: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-anthropics-30b-ramp-mythos-doomsday-openclaw-ankled-iran-war-ceasefire-israels-influence-40808300]] records Sacks granting the cyber risk more legitimacy than generic AI fear marketing, while Chamath questions whether the rollout and 100-day hardening window are partly theatrical.

## Qualifications
The page preserves name uncertainty rather than merging all Anthropic cyber-model names. Neither bounded source proves the model's technical architecture, exploit capability, or real-world remediation results. The All-In discussion is also opinion-driven and partly based on host descriptions of company claims.
