# 汝南郡 / Runan Commandery (late Han)

## Overview
汝南郡 / Runan Commandery is a late-Han local theater inside the pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] Cao-Yuan struggle. Hanji 1045 first frames it through [[LiuPiRunanLateHan|刘辟]] and other Yellow Turban remnants answering [[YuanShao|袁绍]], while Hanji 1047 makes it the place where [[LiuBei|刘备]] rebuilds local pressure after leaving Yuan Shao.

## Current Profile
Runan functions as an allegiance-testing and pressure-projection field rather than a settled rear area. Hanji 1045 uses it to show that Cao Cao's contest with Yuan Shao is fought through local rebels, envoy recruitment, and commandery responses as much as through the main armies.

Hanji 1047 deepens that role. Liu Bei first pressures the southern zone from Yuan Shao's side and is defeated by [[CaoRen|曹仁]], but Runan remains usable after Liu Bei leaves Yuan Shao by proposing a Liu Biao alliance. Liu Bei returns to Runan, joins [[GongDuLateHan|龚都]], gathers more than a thousand troops, and kills [[CaiYangLateHan|蔡阳]], showing that the commandery stays active even after Cao Ren's counterattack.

## Key Characteristics
- Runan is the setting for Yellow Turban remnant rebellion against Cao Cao.
- Yuan Shao's support gives the local revolt wider Cao-Yuan significance.
- Neighboring commanderies are described as responding to the Yuan-backed movement.
- The Runan branch creates a loyalty test for Li Tong in nearby Yang'an.
- Liu Bei uses Runan as a post-Yuan-Shao local foothold after Cao Ren defeats his first southern movement.
- Gong Du and Cai Yang make Runan a recurring armed-pressure theater before the main Guandu camp standoff.

## Qualifications
This page covers Runan only as used in the Hanji 1045 and Hanji 1047 episodes. It does not yet synthesize the commandery's broader administrative or social history.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1047's Liu Bei-Gong Du-Cai Yang branch, shifting Runan from a single allegiance test into a recurring Guandu-prelude pressure theater.
