# 汝南郡 / Runan Commandery (late Han)

## Overview
汝南郡 / Runan Commandery is a late-Han local theater that Hanji 1045 places inside the pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] Cao-Yuan struggle. [[LiuPiRunanLateHan|刘辟]] and other Yellow Turban remnants answer [[YuanShao|袁绍]], creating pressure on [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s local allies such as [[LiTongLateHan|李通]].

## Current Profile
Runan functions as an allegiance-testing field rather than a settled rear area. The episode uses it to show that Cao Cao's contest with Yuan Shao is fought through local rebels, envoy recruitment, and commandery responses as much as through the main armies.

## Key Characteristics
- Runan is the setting for Yellow Turban remnant rebellion against Cao Cao.
- Yuan Shao's support gives the local revolt wider Cao-Yuan significance.
- Neighboring commanderies are described as responding to the Yuan-backed movement.
- The Runan branch creates a loyalty test for Li Tong in nearby Yang'an.
