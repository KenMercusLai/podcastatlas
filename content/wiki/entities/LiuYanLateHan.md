# 刘焉 / Liu Yan (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-939-donghan-luanju-de-fengkuang-sanguo-xuqu-lthmvw8ihk96fuez9vamlryzcngy|Hanji 939]] backfills Liu Yan's institutional move before his entry into [[YizhouLateHan|益州]]. Serving as taichang, he argues that weak provincial inspectors can no longer control local violence and proposes replacing inspectors with stronger provincial shepherds, opening the wiki's earliest explicit [[ProvincialShepherdAutonomy|州牧集权与地方割据]] branch.

The episode makes his destination choice self-interested as well as institutional. Liu Yan first wants remote Jiaozhi as a survival exit, then [[DongFuLateHan|董扶]] tells him the capital will become chaotic and Yizhou has imperial qi. Liu Yan therefore turns toward Yizhou, where [[XiJianLateHan|郗俭 / 郤俭]] is already criticized as extractive; [[ZhaoWeiLateHan|赵韪]] later follows him into the province.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-940-de-minxin-zhe-de-tianxia-ni-xin-bu-xin-loipf7b0p5a-yh0rj2njnsml8a1j|Hanji 940]] backfills Liu Yan's entry into [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] before his later autonomy is developed. After [[JiaLongLateHan|贾龙]] defeats [[MaXiangLateHan|马相]] and [[ZhaoZhiLateHan|赵祗]], Jia Long invites Liu Yan into the province. The source says Liu Yan moves the seat to [[MianzhuLateHan|绵竹]] and uses [[YizhouPacificationByLeniency|益州宽惠安抚]] to calm rebels and unstable people, making people-support the first visible layer of his Yizhou base.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-959-ruguo-ni-chuanyue-dao-sanguo-gai-ruhe-cunhuo-lroaihmpnrelmfcugym9zx2h2dhq|Hanji 959]] backfills Liu Yan's Yizhou autonomy before the later succession crisis. The episode says Liu Yan had advocated replacing provincial inspectors with stronger provincial shepherds, then uses his own [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] post to kill local strongmen, send [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]] and [[ZhangXiuFivePecks|张修]] into [[Hanzhong|汉中]], block the [[XieguRoadLateHan|斜谷道]], and cut practical communication with the court under a "rice-bandit" explanation.

The same source makes his autonomy ritually visible. Liu Yan constructs emperor-level carriage equipment, which [[LiuBiao|刘表]] reports as imperial imitation; when [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] sends [[LiuZhang|刘璋]] to persuade him, Liu Yan keeps Liu Zhang in Yizhou rather than allowing him to return to Chang'an.

刘焉 / Liu Yan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]] as the [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] powerholder who becomes involved in the 194 CE [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] struggle. The episode says he secretly sends five thousand Shu troops to support [[MaTeng|马腾]] against [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]], while his sons [[LiuFanLateHan|刘范]] and [[LiuDanLateHan|刘诞]] act as insiders in Chang'an.

The plan fails when Li Jue's intelligence network uncovers the plot. Liu Fan and Liu Dan are killed while fleeing, leaving Liu Yan's family position damaged before his own death. The source then narrates a lightning-triggered fire at Mianzhu, the move of the provincial seat to [[Chengdu|成都]], and Liu Yan's death from a back abscess.

Liu Yan's legacy in this episode is structural. His order in Yi Province rests partly on Dongzhou troops from Nanyang and Sanfu migrant backgrounds, which creates tension with older local elite families. That tension becomes the setting for [[LiuZhang|刘璋]]'s succession and for [[ImportedTroopsLocalEliteCleavage|外来武装与本土豪族裂缝]].
