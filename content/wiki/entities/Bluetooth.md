# Bluetooth

Bluetooth appears in [[the-invention-invention]] as one of the later standards whose patent-pool structure followed the path reopened by the [[MPEGPatentPool]]. The source uses Bluetooth as a downstream example of [[StandardsBasedInnovation]]: devices work together because firms coordinate around shared technical rules and licensing institutions.
