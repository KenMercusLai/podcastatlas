## Overview

郅都 / Zhi Du enters the wiki as a Western Han harsh official whom Hanji 250 presents as both coercively effective and personally restrained, making him the principal contrast against [[NingChengWesternHan|宁成]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-250-ningcheng-pingshenme-chengwei-zuizha-de-kuli-lj9gnsyyvta1g0fa1asp0vr5xivs|Hanji 250]] gives Zhi Du a compact comparative role rather than a full biography. It credits him with frontier ability against the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], local cooperation with Ning against powerful Jinan families, and forceful capital enforcement after appointment as zhongwei. The host also describes him as personally clean and without substantial private wealth at death.

The episode's larger distinction is between severity as a governing instrument and unbounded predation. Zhi Du can still be coercive, but competence, restraint, and an asserted public purpose keep him from being equated with Ning. His involvement around Liu Rong's death then shows the danger of zealous enforcement near the imperial family, although the supplied account is too compressed to settle responsibility or the exact manner of Zhi Du's own death.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han harsh official presented as militarily capable on the northern frontier.
- Jinan official who cooperates with Ning Cheng in suppressing powerful local families.
- Zhongwei credited with intimidating capital elites through aggressive enforcement.
- Comparative case in which severity is paired with personal restraint and asserted public purpose.
- Official whose involvement around Liu Rong's death exposes the political risk of coercion near the ruling house.

- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-250-ningcheng-pingshenme-chengwei-zuizha-de-kuli-lj9gnsyyvta1g0fa1asp0vr5xivs|Hanji 250]] links excessive zeal and Liu Rong's death to Zhi Du's removal from the capital sequence.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on one short popular-history comparison. The source does not establish Zhi Du's full biography, exact offices and dates, frontier campaigns, Jinan division of responsibility, case record, property, relationship to Liu Rong's death, or the process and manner of his own death. The host's favorable moral contrast with Ning Cheng should not be read as proof that Zhi Du's coercion was lawful, proportionate, or harmless.

## What Changed

- Created a bounded profile separating the episode's claims about coercive effectiveness from its favorable moral judgment.
