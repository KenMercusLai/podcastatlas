# 玉真记事 / Yuzhen Jishi

## Overview

玉真记事 is an independent rural documentary team and media account created by [[ZhaoYushun|赵玉顺]] and [[YuanZhengzheng|袁征征]] to make overlooked people and places visible.

## Current Profile

The project began with weekend travel beyond the Pearl River Delta and became full-time field reporting across Chinese villages and towns. Its work centers on farmers' livelihoods, cash crops, elderly people, children, public services, land consolidation, and ordinary memories that may otherwise disappear.

Its operating model is both enabled and constrained by internet distribution: direct audience access reduces dependence on institutional credentials, while algorithmic reach, account safety, travel costs, and debt create editorial and financial pressure.

## Key Characteristics

- Rural and town reporting focused on subjects outside metropolitan news attention.
- Open-ended field method based on crops, work, household experience, and conversation rather than tourist attractions.
- Repeated visits designed to reveal agricultural and social change over multiple years.
- Long-horizon archival aim centered on the last generation of traditional farmers and other aging witnesses.
- Independent distribution that expands access while exposing the project to platform and income volatility.
