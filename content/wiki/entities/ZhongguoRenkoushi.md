## Overview

《中国人口史》 / Zhongguo Renkoushi enters the wiki through Hanji 388-3 as the population-history work edited by [[GeJianxiong|葛剑雄]] that the host uses to interpret ancient Chinese population numbers. Its role in the episode is methodological: raw official household and population figures are not treated as direct death counts.

## Current Profile

The book is cited for a correction logic around the Three Kingdoms population trough. Hanji 388-3 says official records can understate actual population because of hidden households, powerful-family concealment,屯田 producers outside county registration, and uncounted minority populations. On that basis, the source gives a corrected trough around 23 million rather than the official 7.67 million.

## Key Characteristics

- Population-history reference used to correct literal readings of official demographic records.
- Linked in this source to the difference between state registration and actual population.
- Provides the episode's main support for treating massive dynastic population drops as real but not mechanically identical to recorded death totals.
