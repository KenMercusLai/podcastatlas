# Voyager Golden Record

The Voyager golden record appears in [[sp-01-yuzhou-kaer-sagen-de-xingchen-dahai-he-renlei-mengxiang-543901074]] as one of [[CarlSagan|Carl Sagan]]'s most concrete cosmic-time-capsule projects. The episode links the record, the Pioneer plaques, and Sagan's childhood encounter with the idea of a time capsule: a human message can be preserved for audiences radically distant in space, time, or species.

In the source, the golden record's importance is symbolic as much as technical. It shows Sagan's ability to make public science become a civilization self-portrait: humanity does not only study the cosmos from outside; it chooses what image, sound, knowledge, and hope it would send into the unknown.

## Key Claims
- The record turns cosmic curiosity into an artifact rather than only a metaphor.
- Its time-capsule logic links Sagan's childhood imagination to actual space missions.
- The project belongs to [[HumanisticScienceCommunication]] because it asks what humanity wants to say about itself, not only what instrumentation can measure.
- The record complements [[SETI]] by moving from listening for extraterrestrial signals toward outbound representation.
