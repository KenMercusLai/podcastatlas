# 朴朴厨房 / Pupu Kitchen

## Overview
朴朴厨房 is [[PupuSupermarket|朴朴超市]]'s source-reported self-operated prepared-meal delivery experiment in Fuzhou and Xiamen.

## Current Profile
The experiment combines central-kitchen production, standardized dishes, front-warehouse fulfillment, and instant delivery. It is positioned as “clean takeaway” and, internally, as a traffic product: frequent meal orders may bring customers into Pupu's broader grocery business. That cross-selling logic also makes standalone meal profitability uncertain.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates from more than ten source-reported front warehouses across Fuzhou and Xiamen.
- Uses a self-built central kitchen and internally developed standardized dishes.
- Reuses instant-retail fulfillment infrastructure for prepared food.
- Treats high-frequency meal demand as an acquisition channel for the parent grocery business.

## Qualifications
The source supplies no order volume, repeat rate, food-safety record, cross-purchase measurement, unit economics, or audited profitability. Reusing warehouses may lower some acquisition and delivery costs while adding kitchen utilization, spoilage, labor, and meal-quality complexity.
