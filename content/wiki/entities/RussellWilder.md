# Russell Wilder

## Overview
Russell Wilder is the Mayo Clinic physician named in [[essentials-diet-nutrition-for-mental-health-dr-chris-palmer-scim2465421786]] as the developer of the ketogenic diet for epilepsy in 1921.

## Current Profile
The source uses Wilder as a historical anchor rather than as a biographical subject. His wiki role is to connect fasting observations, epilepsy treatment, and ketogenic diet design before the episode borrows that neurological history for mental-health plausibility.

## Key Characteristics
- Wilder is credited in the source with developing ketogenic diet as a long-term mimic of fasting for epilepsy.
- His work is framed as clinical neurology history, not weight-loss culture or a universal diet claim.
- The episode uses his 1921 intervention as a bridge from seizure control to later claims about diet affecting the brain.

## Evidence
- Historical origin - [[essentials-diet-nutrition-for-mental-health-dr-chris-palmer-scim2465421786]] says Wilder developed the ketogenic diet at [[MayoClinic]] in 1921 to mimic fasting for epilepsy.
- Clinical purpose - [[essentials-diet-nutrition-for-mental-health-dr-chris-palmer-scim2465421786]] distinguishes the original epilepsy use from weight-loss framing or universal-diet claims.
- Downstream relevance - [[essentials-diet-nutrition-for-mental-health-dr-chris-palmer-scim2465421786]] uses this origin story to support the plausibility of nutritional effects on the brain.
