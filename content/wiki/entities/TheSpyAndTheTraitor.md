# 《间谍与叛徒》 / The Spy and the Traitor

[[TheSpyAndTheTraitor|《间谍与叛徒》 / The Spy and the Traitor]] is [[BenMacintyre|Ben Macintyre]]'s 2018 nonfiction account of [[OlegGordievsky|Oleg Gordievsky]], the [[KGB]] officer who became one of [[MI6]]'s most valuable Cold War sources. [[80-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-shang-691866649]] uses the book to narrate the first half of Gordievsky's life: family background, Soviet institutional pressure, Denmark postings, British contact, and the setup of [[OperationPimlico]].

The book matters in the wiki because the episode treats it as a narrative answer to spy fiction. Its suspense comes less from gadgets than from [[TotalitarianDoubleLife|double life]], slow [[ColdWarSpycraft|source handling]], ideological disgust, and the operational problem of keeping a human source alive long enough for his intelligence to matter.

[[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] completes that answer to spy fiction by showing how Gordievsky's intelligence affected Cold War decision-making before the famous escape. The book's second-half branch adds [[OperationRYAN]], [[ColdWarNuclearMisperception]], [[AldrichAmes]], [[CIA]], [[MargaretThatcher]], [[MikhailGorbachev]], and [[Finland]], while keeping [[OperationPimlico]] grounded in mundane objects, diplomatic routine, and family cost.
