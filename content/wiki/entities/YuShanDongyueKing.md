## Overview

余善 / Yu Shan is the Dongyue ruler whose killing of Han officials and self-proclamation as “武帝” trigger a multi-route campaign ordered by [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] in Hanji 356-1.

## Current Profile

The episode introduces Yu Shan only as the rebel target after the Nanyue campaign. It says he raises the banner of revolt, kills court officials, and assumes an imperial title, after which Han Wudi deploys several forces, including [[YangPuWesternHan|楼船将军杨仆]], against Dongyue.

Yu Shan's importance in this bounded source is therefore strategic rather than biographical: his revolt gives Han Wudi a new mission through which to discipline and continue using Yang Pu. The transcript promises Yu Shan's later end but stops before the fighting and outcome.

## Key Characteristics

- Dongyue ruler in open rebellion against Western Han authority.
- Challenger who is said to kill Han officials and adopt the title “武帝.”
- Campaign target of a multi-route Han mobilization that includes Yang Pu.

- Rebellion and title claim: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-356-1-budong-yuren-ni-ping-sha-dang-lingdao-lsaa6j1jskivfhyvvpbpmnorq-jm|Hanji 356-1]] says Yu Shan kills court officials, rebels, and calls himself Wudi.
- Campaign response: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-356-1-budong-yuren-ni-ping-sha-dang-lingdao-lsaa6j1jskivfhyvvpbpmnorq-jm|Hanji 356-1]] places him against forces dispatched by Han Wudi from several routes.
- Yang Pu connection: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-356-1-budong-yuren-ni-ping-sha-dang-lingdao-lsaa6j1jskivfhyvvpbpmnorq-jm|Hanji 356-1]] makes the Dongyue campaign Yang Pu's opportunity to redeem alleged faults after Nanyue.

## Qualifications

The supplied transcript writes the name as “于善”; this page normalizes it to the received form 余善. The source does not provide Yu Shan's accession, family background, territorial administration, campaign outcome, or death, and its contemptuous prediction that he is “找死” is the host's framing rather than biographical evidence.
