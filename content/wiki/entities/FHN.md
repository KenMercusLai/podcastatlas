# FHN

FHN is the collaborator named by [[WuJunIndieGameCreator|巫君]] in [[ep242-duli-youxi-shi-yitiao-gaoqian-hao-saidao-ma-lrqbzrceumbuggzuero5qb4jv3zu]]. The source presents FHN as the original force behind the [[DragonSpiritGameSeries|龙魂]] project and as someone connected to the dragon-lover community that later became important for crowdfunding, feedback, and early distribution.

The FHN case matters because it keeps [[IndieGameCommercialization|indie game commercialization]] tied to a specific creator-community fit. The episode does not say the project worked because games are generally easy to monetize; it says a game about collecting and battling dragon-like creatures had a natural audience inside a vertical fandom.
