# Amir Rashidi

Amir Rashidi is the [[MianGroup]] expert interviewed in [[tech-20260402-0402-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260402-0402-mp-tech-pod-128]] about [[Iran]]'s wartime internet blackout. He explains that the blackout leaves the [[NationalInformationNetwork|National Information Network]] available while cutting Iranians off from global internet services, international news, and social media.

His role in the wiki is to make the technical distinction concrete: blackout does not mean every networked service disappears. In Rashidi's account, domestic apps can still work under state control, but [[DomesticServiceCensorship]] and [[InternetBlackoutPublicSafetyRisk]] make that controlled access a political and public-safety problem.
