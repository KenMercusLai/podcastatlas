# 游凯超 / Kaichao Yu

游凯超 is the [[Infract]] co-founder and chief scientist interviewed in [[148-dui-you-kaichao-3-xiaoshi-fangtan-kaiyuan-infra-he-moxing-co-design-ruguo-vllm-shibai-women-hui-houhui-yibeizi-lg-fhgpmq4r-8l-5-yrimxgkims]]. The source presents him as a major maintainer of [[VLLM|vLLM]] and as a researcher whose path moved from ML algorithms toward machine-learning systems, open-source infrastructure, and inference-engine co-design.

His background in the episode runs through [[TsinghuaUniversity|清华大学]], a Berkeley research visit, and later work around vLLM. The source says he became drawn to systems after seeing that paper-level algorithmic improvements could be dominated by scale, data, hardware, and software implementation quality.

For the wiki, Yu matters because he ties together three branches that are often discussed separately: open-source governance, AI inference economics, and [[ModelInfraCoDesign]]. His account treats [[PagedAttention]] as vLLM's origin, but argues that the durable work is maintaining an engine that keeps up with changing model architectures, hardware features, Chinese open-model releases, and agent workloads.
