# Adrin Nazarian

## Overview

Adrin Nazarian is a Los Angeles City Council member who, in the bounded [[RevisionistHistory]] source, converts a podcast argument about private golf courses into a parcel-tax proposal and negotiations over public value.

## Current Profile

After a staff member introduces him to [[MalcolmGladwell]]'s earlier episode, Nazarian treats the gap between club land value and reported tax payments as a policy problem. His team develops an approximately four-dollar-per-square-foot parcel tax intended to approach taxation based on highest and best use.

The proposal is deliberately severe: the source estimates an annual charge of roughly $55 million for [[LosAngelesCountryClub]]. Nazarian also entertains compromise, including converting one of the club's two large parcels into a public park. His role is therefore both agenda-setting and bargaining rather than simple course abolition.

## Key Characteristics

- Converts a media argument into a municipal tax proposal.
- Uses a high-cost proposal to create bargaining leverage and public debate.
- Connects tax fairness to land access and tangible public benefit.
- Remains open to negotiated compromise rather than insisting on eliminating every private course.
