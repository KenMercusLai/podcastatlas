## Overview

无盐县 / Wuyan County is synthesized as a place that becomes politically charged in two different historical settings. [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-133-songyi-zhan-xiangyu-weihe-bei-fansha-lms7noyrm5omedv77gqfhjeu53zx|Qinji 133]] uses it as the Chu-Qi border location where [[SongYi|宋义]]'s private celebratory sendoff contrasts with army hardship. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-584-xihan-hushan-lishi-moufan-shijian-shimo-lmpoeruya1v3qmixnvi8ewaffqfo|Hanji 584]] later uses it as the Dongping-kingdom county where road and Hu Mountain stone anomalies become material for [[DisasterOmenTreasonAccusation|灾异谋反指控]].

## Current Profile

In Qinji 133, Wuyan matters less as geography than as contrast. At [[AnyangQinRebellion|安阳]], Chu soldiers are hungry, cold, wet, and undersupplied while the relief army waits. At Wuyan, Song Yi is associated with family advancement, wine, ceremony, and visible comfort. [[XiangYu|项羽]] uses that appearance to accuse Song Yi of ignoring soldiers and state survival, helping prepare the ground for killing him after he returns to camp.

In Hanji 584, Wuyan belongs to [[LiuYunDongpingKingWesternHan|东平王刘云]]'s Dongping kingdom. The episode says soil on a local mountain moves into a road-like form and a large stone at Hu Mountain stands upright. Because Aidi is ill and the court is highly sensitive to sacrifice, Heaven, and omens, Liu Yun and [[LiuYeDongpingQueenWesternHan|刘烨]]'s worship of the stone becomes politically dangerous and eventually feeds a treason prosecution.

## Key Characteristics

- Place associated with visible elite behavior under crisis pressure.
- In Qinji 133, marks Song Yi's comfort and family advancement while the Chu army suffers.
- In Hanji 584, belongs to Dongping kingdom under Liu Yun.
- Site of reported road and standing-stone anomalies.
- Becomes politically dangerous when local omen worship is attached to royal-kin suspicion.
- Functions as a place where private or ritual action becomes public legitimacy evidence.

- Command-legitimacy damage: [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-133-songyi-zhan-xiangyu-weihe-bei-fansha-lms7noyrm5omedv77gqfhjeu53zx|Qinji 133]] shows Xiang Yu using the contrast to attack Song Yi's command credibility.
- Dongping setting: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-584-xihan-hushan-lishi-moufan-shijian-shimo-lmpoeruya1v3qmixnvi8ewaffqfo|Hanji 584]] says Wuyan is in Liu Yun's Dongping kingdom.

## Qualifications

This page does not reconstruct Wuyan's full administrative geography. Qinji 133 and Hanji 584 use the place for different historical episodes separated by dynasty and political context. Hanji 584's physical anomalies remain source-scoped; the page records their role in political interpretation, not their natural cause.
