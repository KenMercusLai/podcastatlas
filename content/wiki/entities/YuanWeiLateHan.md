# 袁隗 / Yuan Wei (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-907-suoyou-de-bing-doushi-ni-de-xinbing-ltqzyohcauduwuanlvqph9l81d79|Hanji 907]] adds an earlier 176 CE office marker before Yuan Wei's later Wang Yun rescue and succession-crisis roles. The transcript renders him as "袁伟"; this page keeps the canonical Yuan Wei route because the senior-office role matches the later late-Han official. In the source, Yuan Wei is removed as situ before [[YangCiLateHan|杨赐]] is appointed in his place.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-931-rensheng-jiannan-chu-bianshi-xiuxin-shi-lpzmvxop8lmlwvxhoojzj5ok9qd6|Hanji 931]] backfills Yuan Wei before his 185 situ removal and later succession-crisis roles. The source transcript renders him as "袁伟"; this page keeps the canonical [[YuanWeiLateHan|袁隗]] route because the late-Han senior-official role and rescue context match the existing Yuan Wei identity.

In this source, Yuan Wei joins [[HeJin|何进]] and [[YangCiLateHan|杨赐]] in rescuing [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] after the Zhang Rang prison retaliation case. His significance is protective and procedural rather than coercive: before later sources show him handling unstable titles and seal transfer, Hanji 931 shows him helping preserve an upright official from quiet destruction.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-933-weiguan-zhidao-yousuo-wei-yousuo-buwei-lopulnufsagoqyx7nngvcqi2nhrj|Hanji 933]] backfills Yuan Wei before his succession-crisis roles. The episode says he is removed as situ before [[CuiLieLateHan|崔烈]]'s March appointment, making him part of high-office turnover already visible in 185 CE before later false-edict and coerced seal-transfer episodes.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-943-quanshi-kaoyan-renxing-beihou-cangzhe-juda-xiongxian-lg7ia5xok-swdmhml35yv9qqkvt|Hanji 943]] adds Yuan Wei at the moment [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]] becomes emperor. The source says Yuan Wei is appointed taifu and shares memorial-handling authority with [[HeJin|何进]], giving the new accession a senior Yuan-family procedural support before the later false-edict and forced seal-transfer episodes.

This makes Yuan Wei's later roles less abrupt. In Hanji 943 he helps stabilize the He-backed succession through ordinary office; in Hanji 946 he uses false procedure during palace retaliation; in Hanji 948 [[DongZhuo|董卓]] forces him into formalizing a coerced emperor replacement.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-946-zhichang-douzheng-shi-yourouguaduan-you-duo-zhiming-lpyyweaajonzg7kxuhwm9z7ae7p5|Hanji 946]] adds Yuan Wei before his later seal-transfer role under [[DongZhuo|董卓]]. After [[HeJin|何进]] is killed, [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and Yuan Wei use a false edict to summon and kill [[FanLingLateHan|樊陵]] and [[XuXiangLateHan|许相]], whose appointments had been forged by the eunuch side.

This makes Yuan Wei part of the same unstable title-and-office field that Hanji 948 later develops. In Hanji 946 he participates in retaliatory pseudo-procedure against eunuch-backed appointees; in Hanji 948 he is forced into real court procedure for Dong Zhuo's deposition.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-948-dongzhuo-feidi-quanchen-ruhe-gaibian-huangquan-youxi-ltekg1a-5b5u37aeekjm3dm2m6kk|Hanji 948]] later presents Yuan Wei as the Yuan-family elder and grand tutor whom [[DongZhuo|董卓]] uses to formalize the deposition of [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]]. After [[YuanShao|袁绍]] refuses Dong Zhuo's proposal and leaves Luoyang, Yuan Wei lacks the same room to resist and is made to participate in the ceremony.

His role is procedural rather than ideational. The source has Yuan Wei remove the imperial seal from Liu Bian and hand the position to [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], making him the visible court official through whom armed coercion receives ritual form. This makes him a source case for [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] under duress: the seal and office still matter, but the will behind them belongs to Dong Zhuo's military control.
