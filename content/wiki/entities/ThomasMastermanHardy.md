# Thomas Masterman Hardy

## Overview

Thomas Masterman Hardy was a Royal Navy lieutenant whose work, courage, and reciprocal rescue experiences during the 1796 Mediterranean withdrawal began his enduring professional bond with [[HoratioNelson|Horatio Nelson]].

## Current Profile

The episode introduces Hardy while Nelson operated aboard *Minerve* during the evacuation from Elba. Hardy was portrayed as physically imposing, deliberate, hardworking, brave, and loyal. After Spanish frigates captured him in circumstances that helped Nelson escape with prizes, Nelson recovered him; Nelson later saved Hardy again after he fell overboard. These source-scoped episodes establish the trust that would make Hardy one of Nelson's closest officers, although this source stops before their later service together.

## Key Characteristics

- Royal Navy lieutenant encountered during Britain's 1796 Mediterranean withdrawal.
- Hardworking and courageous officer whose steadiness earned Nelson's trust.
- Participant in reciprocal rescue episodes that formed an unusually durable command relationship.
