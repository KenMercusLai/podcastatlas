# Akbar Khan

## Overview
Akbar Khan was the son of [[DostMuhammad|Dost Muhammad]] and a leading Afghan resistance figure during the [[FirstAngloAfghanWar|First Anglo-Afghan War]].

## Current Profile
[[88-the-first-anglo-afghan-war-glt5156371854]] associates Akbar Khan with the resistance that transformed a vulnerable occupation into military catastrophe. He is described as helping plan actions against the retreating column, including the Jigdallik ambush, while his father's eventual restoration converted resistance into political success.

The source uses him primarily as an operational and dynastic actor. It does not provide a rounded biography or resolve his precise responsibility for every attack and negotiation during the retreat.

## Key Characteristics
- Son of the displaced Afghan ruler Dost Muhammad.
- Leading figure in resistance to the Company occupation.
- Connected to planning against the retreating Kabul column.
- Associated with the Jigdallik ambush and the collapse of withdrawal arrangements.
- Part of the military success that enabled his father's return.

## Evidence
- Resistance leadership: [[88-the-first-anglo-afghan-war-glt5156371854]] names Akbar Khan as a leader of opposition to the occupation.

- Political consequence: [[88-the-first-anglo-afghan-war-glt5156371854]] connects the resistance victory to Dost Muhammad's strengthened restoration.
