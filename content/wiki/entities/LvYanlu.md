# 吕颜璐 / Lü Yanlu

## Overview
吕颜璐 / Lü Yanlu is the source-scoped Shenzhen People's Hospital otolaryngologist interviewed in VOL.184 of [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] about motion sickness, sensory conflict, habituation, and everyday travel management.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Lü as a clinician-educator who translates vestibular function into ordinary travel situations. She explains motion sickness through the brain's integration of inner-ear motion signals, vision, and proprioception, then uses phones, acceleration, regenerative braking, seating direction, posture, odor, ventilation, sound, and prediction to show why susceptibility changes across people and settings.

Her practical frame is layered rather than curative: reduce avoidable sensory conflict, prepare before symptoms build, stabilize and orient the body, and use gradual tolerable exposure when appropriate. Medication and vestibular exercises are mentioned as options, but the source does not supply a personalized assessment, a validated protocol, or enough safety detail to make the episode a substitute for clinical care.

## Key Characteristics
- Source-scoped otolaryngologist affiliated by the episode with Shenzhen People's Hospital.
- Explains motion sickness as multisensory conflict involving vestibular, visual, and proprioceptive information.
- Uses common transport and screen-use examples to make vestibular mechanisms legible to a general audience.
- Treats prediction, control, posture, visual direction, environmental triggers, and habituation as interacting modifiers rather than universal causes or cures.
- Keeps persistent symptoms, medication, ear-pressure problems, and training within a qualified-care boundary.
