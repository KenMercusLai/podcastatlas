# Striding AI / 正行创新

Striding AI, or 正行创新, is [[YaoSong]]'s third startup in [[173-duihua-yao-song-shenjian-dongfang-kongjian-zaichufa-tiancai-shaonian-shinian-hou-1-173-1]]. The source describes it as a physical-intelligence company, not merely a model company or robot-body company.

Yao's Striding thesis is [[PhysicalIntelligenceSystemStack]]: data, compute, models, algorithms, software, hardware, scene access, solutions, and remote systems have to be designed together. The company starts from retail and 3C manufacturing scenes through [[CPGroup]] and an ODM partner, but Yao presents the long-term goal as general physical intelligence rather than narrow integration.

## Key Claims
- The company was founded after Yao judged that the timing, resources, partners, and model-route uncertainty were converging.
- Yao says [[VisionLanguageActionModels|VLA]] progress was approaching a bottleneck by late 2025, making [[RobotReinforcementLearning]], [[WorldModels]], and [[WorldActionModels]] important to watch.
- Striding's early robot form favors [[WheelBasedDualArmRobots]] because wheel bases are more stable and lower-cost than bipedal humanoids for near-term operation.
- The company also explores humanoid forms because the long-term target remains broader physical intelligence.
- Yao says every technical milestone should produce some commercial value instead of waiting for a final general robot.
- The episode presents demo truthfulness as a company boundary: speed and autonomy status should be labeled rather than hidden.
