## Overview

令胡茂 / Linghu Mao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-387-1-taizi-liuju-taowang-hou-can-can-can-lodlsf5wtqtvoejjhe4apec7trku|Hanji 387-1]] as the low-ranking Hu County elder who submits a memorial for [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]] after the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]] has made higher officials afraid to speak.

## Current Profile

The source gives Linghu Mao a narrow but important role. He is presented as a local,基层 figure rather than a high minister, which makes the memorial's risk more visible: while court officials reportedly believe Liu Ju has been wronged, they stay silent under [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s anger. Linghu Mao argues that [[JiangChongWesternHan|江充]] used imperial authority to persecute the crown prince, that Liu Ju killed Jiang Chong after being unable to see the emperor, and that the crown prince's mobilization was fear-driven self-protection rather than deliberate rebellion.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han low-ranking elder associated with Hu County.
- Remonstrator who speaks when higher officials remain silent.
- Defender of Liu Ju's grievance after the Wugu crisis.
- Memorialist who reframes Liu Ju's armed action as fear and self-preservation.
- Catalyst for Han Wudi's partial emotional awakening before any public pardon.
