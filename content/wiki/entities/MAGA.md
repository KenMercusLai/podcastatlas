# MAGA / Make America Great Again

MAGA / Make America Great Again appears in [[sp-05-liuqing-linyao-qiangsha-yibao-jutou-an-beihou-de-sikao-xia-fenlie-de-meiguo-hui-you-xianzheng-weiji-ma-790241978]] as the [[DonaldTrump]]-aligned movement whose meaning is contested after the 2024 election. [[LinYao|林垚]] argues that Trump's victory should not be inflated into proof that MAGA has become an overwhelming national majority.

The source treats MAGA as a coalition rather than a single ideology. It includes anti-establishment resentment, white-national belonging language, conservative party machinery, Silicon Valley capital, and grassroots suspicion toward immigration. The H-1B fight with [[ElonMusk]] is used to show why [[MAGACoalitionFracture]] can appear even before governing begins.

[[tech-20251223-1223-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251223-1223-mp-tech-pod-128]] reinforces the same fault line through [[SuyashPasi]]'s contrast between the [[SteveBannon]] faction and technology leaders seeking skilled-worker visas. The episode shows that [[TechGovernmentAccommodation]] can coexist with internal MAGA conflict: tech leaders may align with the [[DonaldTrump]] administration on AI, crypto, or defense while clashing with protectionist immigration politics.
