# 负黍 / Fushu

负黍 / Fushu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]] as a city that repeatedly changes hands among [[HanState|韩国]], [[ZhengState|郑国]], and [[ChuState|楚国]]. The episode says it was originally a Han city, was seized by Zheng in 407 BCE, defected back to Han in 394 BCE, and was soon taken from Han by Chu.

The page anchors [[TerritorialControlChurn]]. Fushu is not treated as important only because of its own size; it is a compact example of how border cities, defections, and fast follow-on campaigns make the early [[WarringStatesPeriod|战国时期]] feel less stable than the older aristocratic order.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-98-lishishang-shouge-laolai-dengchang-le-luxsxepdedsxd-zprezfg3u3bsnu]] brings Fushu back in the late Warring States endpoint. In 256 BCE, a Qin offensive against [[HanState|韩国]] takes Yangcheng and Fushu, showing that the same place once used to illustrate early territorial churn now sits inside [[QinState|秦国]]'s final pressure on the eastern states and the closing of the Zhou-annals frame.
