## Overview

樊弘 / Fan Hong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-728-weihe-mingchen-nan-shanzhong-beihou-de-yuanyin-ni-gai-dong-ltjl2bgpyyz1bmwe-4mfcyd9e6cf|Hanji 728]] as [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]'s uncle and the Shouzhang Marquis whose high status is paired with unusual caution, household discipline, and thin-burial instructions.

## Current Profile

Hanji 728 uses Fan Hong as the episode's positive answer to the problem of why prominent ministers often fail to end well. He is described as humble and cautious: he arrives early before court, destroys draft memorials himself, and avoids casual direct answers to the emperor in court.

That caution extends beyond personality into family governance. The source says his style affects relatives serving at court, who do not violate the law, and this becomes part of why Liu Xiu respects him. Near death, Fan Hong leaves instructions for a thin burial with no grave goods and gives specific directions for joint burial with his wife.

The strongest profile claim is not that Fan Hong lacks status. It is that he preserves status by refusing excess display. Liu Xiu reads the will, praises it before officials, and says his own death should be handled in the same spirit.

## Key Characteristics

- High-status Eastern Han relative of Liu Xiu presented as a caution-and-restraint model.
- Uses procedural discretion in court by arriving early, controlling draft memorials, and avoiding rash answers.
- Shapes household behavior so relatives serving in government avoid legal violations.
- Turns death arrangements into a final act of anti-display through thin burial and no grave goods.
- Becomes an explicit example Liu Xiu publicly praises and says he wants to follow.
