## Overview
李进 / Li Jin (Wuling, late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-818-guanchang-weishenme-yao-duo-jiaoyou-neng-jiuming-liyziudpezaoa4l6pbsq0wk2qdjdr|Hanji 818]] as the Wuling administrator sent in 137 CE to suppress and settle the [[WulingManLateHan|武陵蛮]] revolt. He is disambiguated from the existing [[LiJinLateHan|李进]] in the Lu Bu branch.

## Current Profile
Li Jin's current profile is bounded to one source note. After [[YuXuEasternHan|虞诩]] warns against increasing Wuling Man burdens and [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] rejects the warning, tax and tribute pressure contributes to revolt. More than twenty thousand rebels besiege Chongcheng, and another force of eight thousand attacks a strategic point.

Shun sends Li Jin as Wuling administrator. The episode says Li Jin defeats the rebels, then follows suppression with appointments of good local officials and soothing measures for the population. His role therefore pairs military pacification with administrative repair after a tax-stability failure.

## Key Characteristics
- Wuling administrator whose current evidence is the 137 CE Wuling Man revolt.
- Military suppressor who defeats rebel forces after tax and tribute pressure erupts.
- Administrative repair actor who selects better local officials and calms the locality after fighting.
- Disambiguated Li Jin route kept separate from the later Yanzhou figure in the Lu Bu branch.
