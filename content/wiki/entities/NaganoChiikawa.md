# Nagano (Chiikawa creator)

## Overview
Nagano is the pseudonymous Japanese artist identified by the source as the creator of [[Chiikawa]].

## Current Profile
The bounded evidence describes Nagano narrowly as an amateur artist who began posting the Chiikawa online comic in 2020. That work grew into a major character franchise while retaining a distinctive combination of visual softness, insecurity, grotesque transformation, and friendship.

## Key Characteristics
- Pseudonymous Japanese creator represented only as "Nagano" in the source.
- Originator of the Chiikawa online comic in 2020.
- Creator whose character world combines cuteness with work, anxiety, loneliness, and body horror.
