# Bubbles and Crashes

*Bubbles and Crashes* is the book associated with [[DavidKirsch]] in [[tech-20260130-0130-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260130-0130-mp-tech-pod-128]]. The source uses the book as a historical frame for technological bubbles rather than as a text under detailed review.

For the wiki, the book page anchors Kirsch's [[TechBubbleConditions]] framework: uncertainty, novice investors, investable access, and powerful narratives. The Marketplace Tech episode applies that frame to [[AIEquityValuationRisk]], [[TechnologyInstallationCycle]], and [[AGINarrative]], asking whether AI investment expectations have outrun the time needed for broad economic value to become visible.
