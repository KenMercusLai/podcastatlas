# Blake Lemoine

Blake Lemoine appears in [[i-robot-ai-and-consciousness-6a880840b0d5e24ea40c7015]] as the 2022 [[Google]] engineer who became convinced that a chatbot had feelings and did not want to be turned off. The episode uses his case as a modern version of [[ChatbotMindProjection]]: a fluent conversational interface can invite users to infer an inner life even when consciousness remains unproven.

In the source, Lemoine is not treated as proof that AI systems are conscious. His role is evidentiary caution: human response to a chatbot is important social data, but it is not the same thing as a direct [[ConsciousnessMeasurement|measurement of consciousness]].
