# Peng Lei / 彭磊

## Overview

Peng Lei / 彭磊 is the Huawei terminal and [[HongmengZhixing|鸿蒙智行]] product speaker in [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]]. The episode presents him as a product leader explaining assisted-driving rollout, active safety, the no-map route, G9 product definition, and the way owner feedback reaches engineering decisions.

## Current Profile

The current wiki view of Peng Lei is source-scoped to EP279. His role is not a general Huawei biography; he appears as the person translating [[HuaweiSmartSelectionCarModel|Huawei's product influence]] into user-facing vehicle choices. He repeatedly connects technical ambition to restraint, safety, real-road coverage, outdoor scenarios, and customer feedback.

## Key Characteristics

- Acts as the product-side explainer for Hongmeng Zhixing's assisted-driving and vehicle-experience logic.
- Frames safety and active safety as the precondition for consumer acceptance of assisted driving.
- Describes the shift from high-definition-map dependence toward no-map perception and judgment as a usability route.
- Presents G9 product definition through outdoor, comfort, oxygen, physical-control, and scenario-specific needs.
- Treats user feedback, NPS, store reports, and owner interviews as inputs to product iteration.

- **Assisted-driving route:** [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]] says Peng Lei described Huawei's car BU as founded in 2019 with autonomous driving as an early department, then emphasized restrained market release, 2023 delivery, no-map routing, nationwide usability, and active safety.
