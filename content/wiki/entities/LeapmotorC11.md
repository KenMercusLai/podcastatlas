# Leapmotor C11 / 零跑 C11

## Overview
Leapmotor C11 / 零跑 C11 is presented as [[Leapmotor|零跑汽车]]'s key turning-point model, pushed forward during a funding crisis and defined as a high-specification SUV below RMB 200,000.

## Current Profile
The source frames C11 as the product decision that changed Leapmotor's trajectory. Zhu Jiangming says the team considered slowing the project during tight cash conditions in early 2020, but he chose to continue. The model was defined near Mercedes GLC size/spec ambition while selling below RMB 200,000 with features such as three screens, an 815 chip, and 500-km range in the source description.

## Key Characteristics
- Mid-price SUV turning point in Leapmotor's company story.
- Continued despite a funding squeeze around 2020.
- Defined against a higher-class vehicle target while priced below RMB 200,000.
- Used visible configuration to make Leapmotor's value-for-money promise concrete.
- Pre-order discounting and early reservations helped create demand before delivery.
