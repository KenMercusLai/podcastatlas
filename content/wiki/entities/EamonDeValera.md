# Éamon de Valera

## Overview

Éamon de Valera was an Irish republican political leader represented here through his treaty alternative, anti-treaty alignment, wartime marginalization, and later constitutional transformation of the Irish Free State.

## Current Profile

The episode presents de Valera as politically central to rejecting the Anglo-Irish Treaty but less influential over the anti-treaty military campaign once armed leaders took control. His proposed “Document No. 2” sought external association with the Commonwealth without recognizing the British monarch as head of state or swearing allegiance. After military defeat, he entered parliament under the disputed oath in 1927, won power in 1932, removed the oath, and secured the Treaty Ports in 1938, shifting part of the republican program from insurrection to constitutional statecraft.

## Key Characteristics

- He proposed external association as an alternative to the treaty's crown relationship and oath.
- He aligned with the anti-treaty cause but was increasingly sidelined by military leaders during the civil war.
- His later willingness to swear the oath as a procedural requirement marked a transition into parliamentary opposition.
- In government he removed the oath and recovered the Treaty Ports while operating through the institutions created by the treaty settlement.
- His eventual dominance makes comparisons with Collins tempting, but the episode cannot establish what either man's survival or choices would have changed.

## Qualifications

This is a source-bounded profile of one political sequence, not a full biography or assessment of de Valera's long rule. The episode's account of his influence, later attitude to the oath, relationship with [[MichaelCollinsIrishRevolutionary|Collins]], and marginalization by militarists remains attributed to its speakers. It does not settle the feasibility of “Document No. 2,” responsibility for the war, or counterfactual claims about partition, church influence, economics, and party competition.

## What Changed

- Established de Valera as a case of defeated armed opposition re-entering and revising the treaty state through parliamentary government.
