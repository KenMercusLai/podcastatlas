# 陈老师 / Chen (sports-rehabilitation guest)

## Overview
陈老师 is the name used in VOL.89 for a sports-rehabilitation practitioner, founder of 瑞波康复, and health-education creator discussing chronic pain, posture, workstation habits, and individualized rehabilitation.

## Current Profile
The source presents 陈老师 as having worked in CBA 北控, a sports-administration setting, a hospital, and national-team synchronized-swimming rehabilitation before combining clinical rehabilitation with public education. His episode role is to shift listeners from body-part self-diagnosis toward history, movement, function, occupation, habits, and symptom-sensitive exercise selection.

His identity and career claims remain source-scoped because the supplied episode note gives no full name or independent biographical documentation. The semantic route distinguishes him from other people identified only as 陈老师.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames rehabilitation as relevant to chronic pain and functional limitation as well as postoperative recovery.
- Uses a detective metaphor to explain why the painful site may not be the only relevant source of symptoms.
- Connects desk setup and posture to mobility, muscular capacity, loading, and repeated movement rather than one rigid pose.
- Warns that generic stretching or internet exercises can aggravate mismatched pain mechanisms.
- Treats occupation, hobbies, habits, symptoms, and appropriate clinical information as inputs to individualized assessment.
