# Bill Cunningham

Bill Cunningham is the Illinois state senator quoted in [[tech-20260417-0417-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260417-0417-mp-tech-pod-128|the April 17 Marketplace Tech source]] on the [[IllinoisAILiabilityBill|Illinois AI liability bill]]. The source says Cunningham told the host it is highly unlikely that final AI law would include sweeping liability relief for AI developers.

His role in the wiki is to anchor the legislative caution inside [[StateAILiabilityShield]]. The source presents company lobbying and state constituent pressure as real, but Cunningham's comment keeps the bill from being recorded as a settled developer immunity regime.
