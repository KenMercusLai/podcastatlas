# 太平道 / Taiping Dao

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-923-chengnianrende-dingji-henjin-guzi-lide-juejue-lifzh-gohpqnmdznnfv72tndqf8h|Hanji 923]] supplies the movement's pre-uprising layer. [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]] first gains trust through healing during epidemic and poverty pressure, then uses Taiping Dao to turn relief, sacred authority, and belonging into organized followership. The episode says the movement is divided into thirty-six `fang`, with [[MaYuanyiLateHan|马元义]] coordinating believers and palace contacts before [[TangZhouLateHan|唐周]] exposes the plan.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-995-ta-zuishang-cibei-weihe-duoci-cansha-enren-llicukoezccd5iycin4uq7qun51k|Hanji 995]] later uses 太平道 / Taiping Dao as [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]]'s late-Han religious movement and as one comparator for how religious language and organization can mobilize large numbers in a collapsing political order.

The page remains source-scoped. Hanji 923 narrates the pre-uprising organization, while Hanji 995 does not retell the Yellow Turban story in detail; it uses Taiping Dao to contextualize [[ZeRong|笮融]]'s use of Buddhist ritual as social organization, while keeping Ze Rong's repeated murders distinct from the broader comparator.
