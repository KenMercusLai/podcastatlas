# 太平道 / Taiping Dao

太平道 / Taiping Dao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-995-ta-zuishang-cibei-weihe-duoci-cansha-enren-llicukoezccd5iycin4uq7qun51k|Hanji 995]] as [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]]'s late-Han religious movement. The host invokes it as one comparator for how religious language and organization can mobilize large numbers in a collapsing political order.

The page is intentionally source-scoped. Hanji 995 does not narrate the Yellow Turban story in detail; it uses Taiping Dao to contextualize [[ZeRong|笮融]]'s use of Buddhist ritual as social organization, while keeping Ze Rong's repeated murders distinct from the broader comparator.
