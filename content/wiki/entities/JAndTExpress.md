# 极兔速递

## Overview
极兔速递 is an Indonesia-founded express carrier that entered China through license acquisition, regional agents, subsidies, financing, and network consolidation.

## Current Profile
The episode presents J&T as proof that a mature express market can still admit a fast entrant when idle infrastructure, struggling outlets, capital, and acquisition targets are available. Its rapid growth intensified the price war, drew regulatory intervention in Yiwu, and later absorbed Best Express's domestic parcel business and SF's economy network.

## Key Characteristics
- Southeast Asian origin with OPPO-linked operating talent.
- Agent-network expansion supported by above-market incentives.
- Acquisition-led consolidation of licenses and networks.
- Major catalyst in the 2020-2021 price war.
