## Overview

征贰 / Zheng Er is synthesized as [[ZhengCeJiaozhi|征侧]]'s sister and co-leader in the 40 CE Jiaozhi revolt. The current evidence gives less independent biography for Zheng Er than for Zheng Ce, but it consistently places her inside the same female-led revolt and the same 43 CE defeat under [[MaYuanEasternHan|马援]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 718 introduces Zheng Er through her joint action with Zheng Ce. After [[SuDingJiaozhi|苏定]] attempts to punish Zheng Ce, the sisters revolt together in spring 40 CE. The revolt spreads beyond Miling as Jiuzhen, Rinan, Hepu, and other groups respond, and Zheng Ce turns the revolt into a sovereign claim.

Zheng Er's current profile is therefore relational but important. She is not merely a family detail; the source frames the revolt as a sister-led political event, and [[FemaleRebelSovereignty|女性起义称王]] depends on the shared action of the two women even though Zheng Ce is the ruler named as declaring herself king.

Hanji 718 and Hanji 720 both give the end of the branch. In 43 CE, Ma Yuan suppresses the Jiaozhi revolt, defeats and kills Zheng Ce and Zheng Er, and the campaign becomes part of the Eastern Han southern-frontier restoration sequence.

## Key Characteristics

- Sister and co-leader whose identity is inseparable from Zheng Ce's Jiaozhi revolt.
- Female rebel actor in the same sovereignty branch, even though Zheng Ce is the named king.
- Participant in a coalition revolt drawing Jiuzhen, Rinan, Hepu, and other southern groups.
- Defeated and killed with Zheng Ce during Ma Yuan's 43 CE Jiaozhi campaign.

## Qualifications

The current wiki has no independent Zheng Er biography beyond her role with Zheng Ce. The page should not infer separate command structure, title, family background, or posthumous cult from the current source notes. Her durable role is co-leadership in the revolt and shared death in the suppression.
