## Overview

段会宗 / Duan Huizong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-550-sha-diguo-taizi-xiatui-jiqian-zhuibing-ta-zenme-zuodao-de-lvzhcjutqnu4or4elds_qg4s1gto|Hanji 550]] as the elderly Western Han official repeatedly sent to stabilize [[Wusun|乌孙]] after its large and small kunmi arrangement breaks into violence. The episode highlights him as an envoy-commander whose credibility rests on both personal composure and the remembered punitive power of Han.

## Current Profile

The source first presents Duan as the court's trusted repair actor after Wusun's small kunmi Anri is killed. Han Chengdi summons him from former commandery service, appoints him to court posts, and sends him west to restore order. Duan installs [[MozhenjiangWusun|末振将]] as small kunmi, then returns after the immediate situation is calmed.

His second visible role is more dangerous. After Mozhenjiang kills the large kunmi Cilim and Han cannot immediately mount a direct campaign, Duan helps install Cilim's successor. When the court later wants Mozhenjiang's son [[FanqiuWusun|番丘]] punished, Duan avoids a large, noisy army, chooses about thirty armed specialists, summons Fanqiu, lists the offenses, and has him killed.

The decisive profile layer is crisis speech under encirclement. Surrounded by [[AnlimiWusun|安犁靡]]'s thousands of riders, Duan says killing him would barely register as a Han loss, then recalls earlier Han punishments of a Dayuan king and [[ZhizhiChanyu|郅支单于]]. He follows the threat with a face-saving explanation for Anlimi: prior notice would either cause Fanqiu's escape or force Anlimi into a kinship betrayal. The episode therefore casts Duan as a practitioner of small-force coercion, deterrent memory, and exit-ramp diplomacy.

## Key Characteristics

- Elderly but still trusted Western Han envoy-commander for Western Regions crisis repair.
- Uses small, quiet force when a large army would warn the target and ruin the mission.
- Turns formal accusation into immediate punitive action against Fanqiu.
- Survives encirclement by invoking Han's remembered capacity for distant punishment.
- Couples threat with face-saving reasoning so the surrounding Wusun force can withdraw without total humiliation.
