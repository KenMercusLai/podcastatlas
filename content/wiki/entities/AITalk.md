# AI Talk

## Overview
AI Talk is [[HanqingUrey|汗青]]'s early AI-content project in the Yuri source, built around synthetic virtual conversations before the team shifted toward original IP.

## Current Profile
The source presents AI Talk as a 2023-era experiment in combining generated scripts, digital-human tools, lip sync, and TTS into watchable dialogue videos. Its breakout examples used public figures such as [[SteveJobs|Steve Jobs]] and [[ElonMusk|Elon Musk]], with one Jobs-Musk video reportedly nearing 10 million WeChat Channels plays in one evening.

The project matters in the wiki less as a durable franchise than as a training ground. It taught the team how timing, novelty, topic packaging, and creator editing turn model output into media, while also exposing copyright and authorization boundaries that pushed Hanqing toward [[UreyVirtualIdol|Yuri / 尤栗 / 优瑞]] as original IP.

## Key Characteristics
- AI Talk combined multiple early AIGC components into virtual conversation videos.
- Its early success depended on novelty, public-figure relevance, and creator packaging rather than a stable repeatable formula.
- Hanqing still manually adjusted generated scripts for topic transitions, tone, and conversation structure.
- The project later narrowed public-figure use toward authorized figures or public-domain historical subjects.
- It served as a method and team-training precursor to [[UreyVirtualIdol|Yuri / 尤栗 / 优瑞]].
