# 叶国富 / Ye Guofu

## Overview
叶国富 / Ye Guofu is represented as the founder of [[Miniso|名创优品]] and the principal shareholder driving the discussed reform of [[YonghuiSuperstores|永辉超市]].

## Current Profile
The interview presents Ye as a store-centered retailer who treats repeated mall visits, product-selection meetings, store inspections, and customer feedback as direct management work. His method combines standardized execution with subjective product judgment: use suppliers, data, systems, and repeatable store routines, but keep senior attention on what customers encounter and which products deserve more space.

His strategic arc moves from low-price general merchandise to licensed IP, original-character experiments, and quality grocery. Across those categories, the recurring claim is that retail advantage comes from product, supply chain, service, and the reasons people choose to visit—not from an abstract choice between online and offline. The source also shows a high-risk capital allocator: he accepts expensive stores, uncertain original-IP investment, and a difficult supermarket turnaround while arguing for patience and controlled experimentation.

## Key Characteristics
- Store-centered operator who uses browsing, weekly selection, inspections, and frontline feedback as management inputs.
- Standardizer who separates local capital provision from headquarters control of store operations.
- Product-and-supply-chain thinker who treats channel choice as secondary to customer value and execution.
- IP strategist moving from international licenses toward a portfolio of original characters.
- Long-horizon risk taker willing to fund uncertain creative experiments and multi-year supply-chain reform.
- Source-scoped narrator whose performance figures and market forecasts require independent outcome evidence.

## Qualifications
The profile derives from one long founder interview and therefore reflects Ye's retrospective causal story, preferred metrics, and strategic confidence. Store economics, supplier counts, IP targets, overseas growth, market concentration, turnaround timing, and willingness-to-spend claims remain source-scoped. The source does not establish that Miniso's original-IP strategy or Yonghui's transformation will reach the stated goals.
