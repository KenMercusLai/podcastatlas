# Operation Forrest Gump

Operation Forrest Gump is the anti-corruption operation named in [[bombs-and-bombshells-zelensky-under-pressure-6a86d3561ad8716bb64646f0]]. The episode says detectives alleged that people near [[VolodymyrZelensky]]'s circle helped launder money to provide bail for officials charged in earlier investigations.

The page is source-scoped: the wiki records the operation as part of the episode's account of [[WartimeLeadershipAccountability]], not as an independent legal finding. Its importance is that corruption allegations become politically sharper when [[Ukraine]] is also under military, export, and election-pressure constraints.
