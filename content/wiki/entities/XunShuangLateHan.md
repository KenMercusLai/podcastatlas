## Overview

荀爽 / Xun Shuang is a late-Han Yingchuan scholar whose wiki profile now spans an early Huan-era moral critique and later Dong Zhuo-era coerced service. Hanji 870 shows him using a post-eclipse recommendation exam to criticize harem scale and palace burden under [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]], while Hanji 949 and Hanji 951 show his prestige being pulled into [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s legitimacy and relocation crisis.

## Current Profile

Xun Shuang's current profile is not a full biography of the Yingchuan Xun lineage. Hanji 870 gives the earliest bounded role: after a solar eclipse, [[ZhaoDianLateHan|赵典]] recommends him as a filial and capable candidate. Xun uses the examination answer to argue from ritual, marriage, yin-yang order, and harem excess, saying thousands of palace women burden commoners and disturb the political-cosmic order. Huan responds by appointing him langzhong.

Later sources shift the pressure field. Hanji 949 shows Dong Zhuo rapidly elevating Xun through guangluxun to sikong within 93 days, using Yingchuan scholar prestige to decorate coercive rule. Hanji 951 then places Xun beside [[HuangWanLateHan|黄琬]] as a damage-control figure after [[YangBiao|杨彪]] confronts Dong Zhuo over forced relocation from [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] to [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]]. Across the bounded sources, Xun's stable role is high-prestige speech under regimes that can use or tolerate him without becoming genuinely corrected by him.

## Key Characteristics

- Yingchuan scholar with enough reputation to be recommended after an eclipse and later used by Dong Zhuo for legitimacy.
- Huan-era critic of harem excess, palace burden, and disorder framed through ritual and cosmic harmony.
- Official whose frank answer produces appointment rather than immediate punishment in Hanji 870.
- Dong Zhuo-era coerced talent whose rapid elevation borrows scholar prestige under violent rule.
- Defensive court speaker who can soften a confrontation without stopping forced relocation.
- Source-bounded figure whose current page does not reconstruct the full Xun lineage or intellectual career.

## Qualifications

Hanji 870 gives a source-scoped harem and ritual critique, not an independent census of palace women or a complete Xun Shuang policy program. Hanji 949 and Hanji 951 frame his Dong Zhuo-era role through coercive recruitment and defensive speech, not voluntary endorsement of Dong Zhuo. The current page keeps Xun Shuang distinct from [[XunYu|荀彧]] and does not expand into the whole Yingchuan Xun family.
