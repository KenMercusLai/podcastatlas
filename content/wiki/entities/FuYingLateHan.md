# 傅婴 / Fu Ying (late Han)

## Overview
傅婴 / Fu Ying is one of the loyal followers mobilized by [[LadyXuSunYi|徐夫人]] in the Hanji 1067 Danyang revenge episode. The supplied transcript writes the name as 傅英; the wiki normalizes the clear event to Fu Ying / 傅婴 while preserving the variant as source-scoped.

## Current Profile
Fu Ying appears alongside [[SunGaoLateHan|孙高]] as a former subordinate trusted by [[SunYiLateHan|孙翊]]. Lady Xu turns to them after seeing through [[GuiLanLateHan|妫览]] and [[DaiYuanLateHan|戴员]]'s conspiracy. Fu Ying helps gather the small loyalist force, hides in Lady Xu's room, and kills Gui Lan when Lady Xu gives the signal.

After the conspiracy is destroyed, [[SunQuan|孙权]] rewards Fu Ying and Sun Gao with promotion to yamen general posts.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a loyal Sun Yi follower rather than a neutral local actor.
- Helps organize the revenge force behind Lady Xu's staged compliance.
- Kills Gui Lan in the decisive room ambush with Sun Gao.
- Receives Sun Quan's formal reward after the conspirators are eliminated.
