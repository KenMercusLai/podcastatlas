# 汪一鸣 / Wang Yiming (reproductive-medicine doctor)

## Overview
汪一鸣 is presented in VOL.84 of [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] as a Tongji Hospital reproductive-medicine andrology attending physician discussing sex education, consent, contraception, sexual pain, infection screening, pregnancy care, and inclusion.

## Current Profile
The episode positions Wang as a clinical voice arguing that many adult anxieties and apparent sexual-health problems are intensified by missing or inaccurate education. He connects prevention with care navigation: people should understand boundaries and contraception before crisis, seek qualified assessment for pain or symptoms, and use formal medical institutions for unintended-pregnancy care.

He also broadens clinical discussion beyond able-bodied younger adults. Children need protected refusal rights, while older and disabled people's sexual needs should be visible without voyeurism, shame, or automatic denial of agency.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats sex education as preventive health literacy rather than only biological instruction.
- Connects consent and harassment prevention with responsibility placed on potential perpetrators and institutions as well as individuals.
- Explains sexual pain and infection testing through cause-specific assessment rather than embarrassment or self-diagnosis.
- Directs unintended-pregnancy care toward qualified institutions and warns about misleading low-price advertising.
- Includes children, men, women, older adults, and disabled people in sexual-health discussion.
