# 太子申 / Prince Shen of Wei

太子申 / Prince Shen of Wei appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-19-maling-zhi-zhan-lhbapt09x2ptdqx0uopkp8apbada]] when [[WeiState|魏国]] responds to [[QiState|齐国]]'s advance toward [[Daliang|大梁]]. [[PangJuan|庞涓]] is recalled from the Han front, and Wei raises another army under Prince Shen.

The episode uses this appointment to explain [[CrownPrinceCommandRisk|太子挂帅风险]]. A crown prince is politically valuable as the stabilizing heir, but dangerous as a field commander because military reputation, officer networks, and inheritance legitimacy can converge in one person.
