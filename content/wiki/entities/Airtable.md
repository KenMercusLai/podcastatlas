# Airtable

Airtable appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-googles-ai-brain-drain-spacexs-huge-quarter-airtables-90-collapse-us-data-fuels-china-ai-42362555]] as the episode's clearest post-ZIRP software reset case. [[JasonCalacanis|Jason Calacanis]] says [[BendingSpoons|Bending Spoons]] acquired Airtable for far below its 2021 peak valuation, while the hosts still describe the product as having revenue, loyal users, and a recognizable no-code niche.

For the wiki, Airtable is not treated as a failed company so much as a capital-structure and category-fit case. The episode argues that high peak valuation, slowing growth, sales inefficiency, and [[NoCodeAIDisruption]] can make a once-hot software company better suited to cost discipline and [[PrivateEquityAITransformation|private-equity-style]] operation, especially after the AI-agent work is spun into [[HyperAgent]].
