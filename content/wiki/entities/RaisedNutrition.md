# Raised Nutrition

## Overview
Raised Nutrition is the Australian whole-food protein bar company co-founded by [[DarcyRaisedNutrition]] and Jess, used in the Daymond John Advice Line episode to show how a niche athletic origin can support broader wellness positioning.

## Current Profile
The company makes protein bars without artificial flavors, sweeteners, preservatives, fillers, or natural flavors. Darcy says the brand was inspired partly by RXBar and by his and Jess's Brazilian jiu-jitsu training. Seven months after launch, the company reports about $18,000 in sales over the previous 30 days, mostly direct-to-consumer.

The positioning tension is that many customers are not from jiu-jitsu; they are health and wellness consumers who read ingredient labels. [[DaymondJohn]] and [[GuyRaz]] advise keeping the jiu-jitsu origin story because it explains why the product exists, while giving more emphasis to collagen, recovery, clean-label ingredients, and Australian sourcing.

## Key Characteristics
- Whole-food protein bar brand with a jiu-jitsu origin story and broader wellness uptake.
- Uses Australian grass-fed collagen, Australian-grown fruit where possible, pumpkin seed protein, and organic sprouted brown rice protein.
- Reports early direct-to-consumer traction rather than retail-led scale.
- Faces a positioning decision about whether a niche athletic story limits or strengthens broader appeal.
- Receives advice to keep the origin story while making collagen and Australian sourcing more visible.

## Qualifications
The source does not include margin, repeat-purchase, customer-acquisition cost, ingredient-supply, regulatory, or retail-channel data. The jiu-jitsu story is a positioning recommendation, not proof that the niche can supply enough growth on its own.
