# Gaoling Village / 高岭村

Gaoling Village / 高岭村 is the clay-source place in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]] that gives [[Kaolin]] its international technical name. The episode treats this etymology as more reliable than the local story that "China" comes from Jingdezhen's older name Changnan.

The village matters because clay from Gaoling helped Jingdezhen potters move beyond porcelain stone alone. In the source, that shift into [[PorcelainStoneClayBinaryFormula]] increased firing stability and made larger, more demanding forms possible inside the [[JingdezhenPorcelainProductionSystem]].
