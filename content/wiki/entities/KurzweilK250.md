# Kurzweil K-250

The Kurzweil K-250 is the digital instrument that becomes the turning point in [[ChuckSurack]]'s [[Sweetwater]] story in [[sweetwater-chuck-surack-how-a-customer-service-strategy-built-a-billion-dollar-online-pro-audio-and-music-company-50c2b5e3-3bc3-4039-a92d-26228b8ac908]]. Chuck saw a prototype at NAMM in 1984, sold his Yamaha grand piano to help buy one, and used it to add piano, bass, choir, strings, brass, and other sounds to recordings.

The source treats the K-250 as more than a piece of gear. It created [[CustomerPull]] for rare sounds, pulled Chuck into a community of other users, and led from free sound disks to paid sound-disk orders and dealer status. In that sense it links [[OperationalPainFounderInsight]] and [[RelationshipLedSales]]: specialized technical help became the bridge from recording services into product sales.
