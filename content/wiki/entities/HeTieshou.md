## Overview

何铁手 / He Tieshou is a [[BixueJian|《碧血剑》]] character whose older-edition mistaken love for the disguised [[WenQingqing|温青青]] leads to crisis, recovery, and a new direction.

## Current Profile

The hosts value He Tieshou's capacity to survive disillusionment. In the older version, discovering Wen Qingqing's sex and breaking with the Five Poisons Cult precipitate a suicide attempt, but rescue and conversation with [[YuanChengzhi|袁承志]] are followed by rapid reorientation rather than lifelong organization around the lost bond.

Revision deletes the mistaken-love line and recasts her interest in Yuan as a wish to become his student. The episode argues that this avoids the earlier same-sex misrecognition plot but also weakens the emotional cause of her institutional break and repeated pursuit.

## Key Characteristics

- Mistakes Wen Qingqing's disguise for a male romantic object in the older edition.
- Experiences acute collapse when the relationship and institutional identity fail together.
- Recovers sufficiently to pursue another life rather than converting rejection into permanent control.
- Provides a direct contrast with [[HeHongyao|何红药]]'s inability to release an unavailable attachment.
- Loses some motive coherence when revision removes the original romantic engine.

## Qualifications

The account depends on the hosts' memory of edition differences and does not exhaust He Tieshou's later characterization. Recovery is not evidence that the crisis was trivial, and the episode's preference for the older motive does not settle the broader representational merits of either version.
