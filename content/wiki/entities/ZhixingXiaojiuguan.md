# 知行小酒馆 / Zhixing Xiaojiuguan

## Overview
知行小酒馆 / Zhixing Xiaojiuguan is a podcast connecting economics and money to work, relationships, culture, and practical life design.

## Current Profile
In the bounded wiki evidence, the show uses conversational interpretation, expert interviews, personal stories, and practical decision lists rather than market prediction or a standardized success formula. E255 moves from weak prices, property, population, production incentives, exports, and AI to work dignity and practical life design. E254 uses five intimate windfall stories to show how family systems, agency, loss, and trust shape the meaning of money. E253 returns to AI through rural health, education, employment, and giving. E252 turns housing provident funds into a conditional household choice across policy, cash flow, behavior, mobility, and future borrowing. E251 subjects value-investing success stories to the same test by restoring omitted capital, cash-flow, business-knowledge, and lifestyle conditions.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects macroeconomic structure to household feeling and individual life choices.
- Treats work, wealth, consumption, and technology as moral and cultural questions as well as economic ones.
- Uses historical, institutional, and popular-culture examples to build an explanatory map rather than a point forecast.
- Uses varied listener stories to compare how the same broad theme changes across family, career, legal, and care contexts.
- Tests abstract economic and technology claims against frontline delivery, trust, and distribution.
- Turns financial slogans into decision sequences that include local rules and behavioral implementation.
- Uses practitioner self-critique to separate profitable outcomes from reproducible skill and hidden prerequisites, then ends with plural forms of agency rather than a standardized success formula.
