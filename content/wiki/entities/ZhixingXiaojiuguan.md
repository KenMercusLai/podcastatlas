# 知行小酒馆 / Zhixing Xiaojiuguan

## Overview
知行小酒馆 / Zhixing Xiaojiuguan is a podcast connecting economics and money to work, relationships, culture, and practical life design.

## Current Profile
In the bounded wiki evidence, the show uses conversational interpretation and personal stories rather than market prediction or a standardized success formula. Episode E255 moves from weak prices, property, population, production incentives, exports, and AI to work dignity and practical life design. E254 moves in the opposite direction, using five intimate windfall stories to show how family systems, agency, loss, and trust shape the meaning of money.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects macroeconomic structure to household feeling and individual life choices.
- Treats work, wealth, consumption, and technology as moral and cultural questions as well as economic ones.
- Uses historical, institutional, and popular-culture examples to build an explanatory map rather than a point forecast.
- Uses varied listener stories to compare how the same broad theme changes across family, career, legal, and care contexts.
- Ends with plural, present-tense forms of agency instead of a standardized success formula.
