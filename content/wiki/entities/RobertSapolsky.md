# Robert Sapolsky / 萨波斯基

Robert Sapolsky appears in [[ep256-ai-shidai-ziyou-yizhi-hai-cunzai-ma-lk9aci8oqnwerk26jy683nbdddcx]] as the strongest no-free-will voice discussed by [[TuMotuo|土摩托]]. The episode presents his argument as a causal-chain view: criminal behavior, addiction, temperament, and opportunity are shaped by birth, genes, family, education, poverty, nutrition, social unfairness, and many other factors outside a person's choosing.

The source does not fully accept Sapolsky's conclusion that there is absolutely no [[FreeWill|free will]]. It uses his position to sharpen [[MoralResponsibilityUnderDeterminism]]: punishment, justice, and social policy should take causes seriously rather than treating every harmful act as proof of an isolated bad will.
