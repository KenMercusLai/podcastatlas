## Overview

曹节 / Cao Jie is a senior late-Han eunuch whose profile now begins with the prelude to the 168 CE anti-[[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] crisis. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-885-shengsi-guantou-ta-qingjia-xiuxi-luydnh9oqdyyfpbeztooi2e7lvca|Hanji 885]] first places him inside the target field: Dou Wu asks [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦妙 / 窦皇太后]] to remove him, [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] names him in a dangerous eunuch-linked network, and [[ZhengSaLateHan|郑飒 / 郑萨]]'s interrogation names him with [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-886-shenchu-zhichang-zuoge-chengfu-henshen-de-ren-lo6dgcdbshjgto-qsfcouwjersdi|Hanji 886]] then places him in the emergency response: he moves [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] into a controlled palace setting, helps secure seals and gates, and creates the ruler-facing authority environment that later lets the eunuch side reverse Dou Wu and Chen Fan.

## Current Profile

Cao Jie's profile is best read as palace access converted into durable political leverage. In Hanji 885, his danger to Dou Wu's side is visible before the countercoup: he remains close enough to Dou Miao to make her hesitate, and the anti-eunuch side has to move through memorials, arrests, and confession rather than immediately control the palace. In Hanji 886, the eunuch side does not merely complain about Dou Wu's memorial; Cao Jie helps seize the practical sources of court authority: the emperor's person, the palace doors, the seal apparatus, and the forced production of edict language. Hanji 887 then shows that access becoming an operational countercoup through forged authority, military messaging, Zhang Huan's uninformed force, and Wang Fu's accusation.

The later sources show Cao Jie repeatedly operating through access and interpretation. He resists [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦妙 / 窦皇太后]]'s posthumous recognition because it would reopen Dou Wu and Chen Fan's grievance. He appears in an anonymous Zhuque Gate accusation with [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]], leaks [[CaiYong|蔡邕]]'s confidential memorial, recovers influence after Wang Fu's corpse humiliation, gains shangshu ling access, is criticized by [[LyuQiangLateHan|吕强]] for title privilege, survives [[YangQiuLateHan|阳球]]'s anti-eunuch plot after household leakage, and then dies in 181 CE with [[ZhaoZhongLateHan|赵忠]] inheriting the favorite channel rather than the system ending.

## Key Characteristics

- Cao Jie is presented as a direct crisis actor in the 168 CE counteraction against Dou Wu and Chen Fan.
- Before the counteraction, he is already named as a removal target whose proximity to Dou Miao slows authorization.
- His power depends on palace access, document visibility, seal and gate control, and ruler-facing interpretation.
- He defends eunuch-side memory by resisting actions that would make Dou Wu, Chen Fan, or Dou Miao publicly honorable.
- His network is resilient: accusation, punishment, corpse humiliation, and remonstrance pressure do not remove the broader eunuch channel.
- He becomes a recurring target for officials who read eunuch privilege as a source of court failure, but those attacks often expose the attackers first.
- His death functions as a succession point inside eunuch power, with Zhao Zhong carrying forward the favorite channel.

## Evidence

- Pre-coup target field: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-885-shengsi-guantou-ta-qingjia-xiuxi-luydnh9oqdyyfpbeztooi2e7lvca|Hanji 885]] says Dou Wu seeks authorization to remove Cao Jie, Chen Fan names him in a dangerous network, and Zheng Sa's interrogation links him with Wang Fu.
- Emergency palace control: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-886-shenchu-zhichang-zuoge-chengfu-henshen-de-ren-lo6dgcdbshjgto-qsfcouwjersdi|Hanji 886]] says Cao Jie moves Emperor Ling to the front hall, urges self-protection, participates in gate and seal control, and helps force Shangshu officials to write edicts.
- Countercoup mechanics: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-887-dang-zhuduizhang-pengshang-zhuduiyou-jieguo-taican-lvyh9a-kvsdqaokmylgg139bvhfk|Hanji 887]] says Cao Jie and the eunuch side use information asymmetry, forged orders, Zhang Huan's return, and troop coordination to reverse Dou Wu's position.
- Anti-Dou memory defense: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-900-zhichang-quanshu-dou-budong-gai-zenyang-shengcun-lmhjwosy4eswyhfppdghfsmfycqr|Hanji 900]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-901-jixingzi-de-ren-hui-shi-shenme-xiachang-lh1yfyjzqptxvtnv9taiqms9yjud|Hanji 901]] show Cao Jie opposing Dou Miao's full funeral and burial recognition with Wang Fu.
- Accusation and speech control: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-902-kongzhi-qingxu-shi-rensheng-bixiuke-ljynylvdlsdtjf4es9jmilav863b|Hanji 902]] places Cao Jie in the Zhuque Gate accusation and the search for the anonymous speaker.
- Confidential document access: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-912-mimi-daodi-gai-bugai-shuo-chukou-lhsgipxwc17pahc-r6jl7xdyrs2a|Hanji 912]] says Cao Jie reads and leaks Cai Yong's confidential memorial after watching Emperor Ling's reaction.
- Power peak and rebound: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-915-shishang-meiyou-zhenzheng-de-kouwu-zhehua-dui-ma-lgxcodjxnaozvyegxqurgzji5gus|Hanji 915]] places Cao Jie beside Wang Fu at a pre-destruction eunuch-power peak, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-916-ming-hao-he-yun-hao-nage-geng-zhongyao-lmzudjq5zfkfsukmhdeiumesm9qu|Hanji 916]] shows him using Wang Fu's public corpse humiliation to regroup the eunuch side and regain access.
- Title criticism, plot survival, and succession: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-917-ruhe-xunsu-zhaodao-shengzhi-jiaxin-de-pojudian-lk2zt5c3onvui9a4ppbowshy66tg|Hanji 917]] names Cao Jie in Lyu Qiang's critique of no-merit marquisates and family privilege; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-918-shiqing-bu-shunli-shi-weishenme-yao-bizui-lh-tbohgo96qw8bjqu3x1yp6czv5|Hanji 918]] makes him the target and beneficiary of a leaked anti-eunuch plot; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-921-jinqian-ru-shui-que-ze-ke-si-tan-ze-yan-si-lj1dk4qdgcago9elr7ctuvre3zic|Hanji 921]] records his death and Zhao Zhong's succession.

## Qualifications

- Hanji 885, Hanji 886, and Hanji 887 place Cao Jie inside the eunuch-side target and action field, but they do not separately reconstruct every decision he personally made.
- This page preserves Cao Jie's agency without making him the only cause of late-Han eunuch politics. Wang Fu, Zhao Zhong, Zhu Yu, Zhang Rang, Cheng Huang, and other actors carry adjacent branches.
- Some source notes outside the preserved frontmatter inventory also mention Cao Jie; this structured page uses the canonical source list plus the newly appended Hanji 886 source.

## What Changed

- The profile now starts with the pre-coup target field before 168 CE emergency palace control and the Hanji 887 battlefield reversal.
- Cao Jie's access pattern now includes seal, gate, edict, and emperor-position control, not only later document leakage and regrouping.
- The page keeps the later Dou memory, confidential memorial, title-privilege, plot-leak, and succession branches in one current profile.
