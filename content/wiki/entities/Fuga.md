# Fuga

## Overview
Fuga is the trail-running brand or product line that the source treats as [[Kailas|Kailas / 凯乐石]]'s main vehicle for entering overseas outdoor markets.

## Current Profile
In the current evidence, Fuga is not analyzed as a standalone company. It is the technical and category-specific face of Kailas's European expansion. The source uses it to explain why trail running gives a Chinese outdoor brand a plausible opening: the sport grew quickly, traditional road-running giants were slower to occupy it, and specialist credibility could be built through events, athletes, and mountain-town retail.

## Key Characteristics
- Trail-running identity tied to Kailas's overseas strategy.
- Used as a category wedge into Europe rather than as a broad lifestyle line in the source.
- Benefits from trail running's boom and the prior absence of some traditional running giants.
- Depends on practitioner credibility through races, professional athletes, and mountain retail contexts.

## Evidence
- Overseas-entry role: [[kaile-shi-zai-ouzhou-kaidian-guanggao-shouci-chengwei-b-zhan-diyi-da-yewu-1010144081]] states that Kailas is entering overseas markets through Fuga.
- Category timing: [[kaile-shi-zai-ouzhou-kaidian-guanggao-shouci-chengwei-b-zhan-diyi-da-yewu-1010144081]] says trail running's growth and incumbent absence opened space for brands such as Kailas.
- Credibility-building route: [[kaile-shi-zai-ouzhou-kaidian-guanggao-shouci-chengwei-b-zhan-diyi-da-yewu-1010144081]] links Kailas and Fuga to sponsored trail races and professional runners.
