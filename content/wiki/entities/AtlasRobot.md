# Atlas Robot

## Overview
Atlas is Boston Dynamics' humanoid robot platform discussed in the All-In robotics special. The episode treats Atlas as a future commercial platform rather than the already-scaled deployment case represented by Spot.

## Current Profile
Atlas appears as Boston Dynamics' humanoid bridge from research reputation toward robot-as-a-service deployment. The source says Atlas will likely use a service model, has swappable batteries, and depends on the same two-brain architecture: local physical control for movement and manipulation, with higher-level reasoning potentially handled through cloud partners.

## Key Characteristics
- Atlas is discussed as a humanoid platform whose business model will likely differ from Spot's CapEx history.
- Robot-as-a-service is presented as a probable commercial model for Atlas.
- Swappable batteries are described as a way to keep one battery available while another is replaced.
- Atlas sits inside Boston Dynamics' two-brain architecture: physical control on the robot and reasoning through higher-level AI systems.
- The episode leaves Atlas's exact first scaled commercial workflow less defined than Spot's inspection market.

## Qualifications
Atlas's service model, battery architecture, first workflow, and commercial timing are not independently verified by the episode. The source gives Atlas less customer evidence than Spot, so the wiki keeps it as a future Boston Dynamics platform rather than a proven deployment category.
