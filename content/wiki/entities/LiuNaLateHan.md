# 刘纳 / Liu Na (late Han)

[[LiuNaLateHan|刘纳]] was a shangshu in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-918-shiqing-bu-shunli-shi-weishenme-yao-bizui-lh-tbohgo96qw8bjqu3x1yp6czv5|Hanji 918]] and one of the officials who urge [[LiuHeSituLateHan|刘郃]] to act against [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]].

The episode gives Liu Na the hard version of the responsibility argument: if a senior minister sees a collapsing state and does not support it, his rank is empty. That rhetoric helps overcome Liu He's caution but cannot prevent the fatal leak through [[YangQiuLateHan|阳球]] and [[ChengHuangLateHan|程璜]].
