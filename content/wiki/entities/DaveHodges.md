# Dave Hodges

Dave Hodges is the founder and leader of [[ZydorChurch]] in [[a-trip-to-the-magic-mushroom-megachurch]]. The source presents him as a movement builder who describes [[PsilocybinMushrooms]] as a direct route to the divine and argues that people may have religious experiences through mushrooms whether or not they initially name them that way.

Dave's origin story gives Zydor its internal religious narrative. The episode says the broader [[ChurchOfAmbrosia]] began as a marijuana church in 2019, then Dave recentered it around mushrooms after a claimed mushroom vision in July 2019 convinced him that he needed to protect the psychedelic movement.
