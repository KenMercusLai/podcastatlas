# Didi

## Overview
Didi is a Chinese mobility platform represented in the wiki through operating updates, Robotaxi ecosystem positioning, and now a detailed ride-hailing safety-operations account.

## Current Profile
The earlier sources used Didi as a mobility platform and business-update reference. In the [[Momenta]] interview, it appears as one of the Chinese platform partners [[CaoXudong]] names for a supplier-led Robotaxi route alongside [[AutoNavi]] and [[T3Chuxing]]. In the 声动早咖啡 roundup, Didi is described as returning to profitability while still investing overseas and in Robotaxi.

The 三五环 safety episode adds Didi's most operationally detailed profile so far. [[XuXiaonan|徐晓楠 / 曲晓楠]] describes the ride-hailing business as a [[RideHailingSafetyOperations]] system rebuilt after 2018: driver admission, verification, safety education, safety dispatch, in-trip AI and human warning, post-trip responsibility handling, and user-side behavior all shape the safety envelope. This makes Didi important not only as distribution for autonomous mobility, but also as a case in [[OfflinePlatformSafetyBoundary]] and [[PublicSafetyPrivacyTradeoff]].

## Key Characteristics
- Didi is a mobility platform with strategic relevance to China Robotaxi distribution and fleet operations.
- It is named as a potential platform partner rather than a self-operated autonomous-driving supplier in [[Momenta]]'s China Robotaxi route.
- A business-news source says Didi returned to profitability after two loss-making quarters while overseas losses remained a priority-market cost.
- Its ride-hailing safety account treats safety as an operating system extending from order matching to safe driver-passenger separation.
- Its safety model favors [[HighRecallSafetyIntervention]], accepting false alarms to reduce missed severe incidents.
- Its offline-service risks depend on user behavior, recording settings, minors, drunk passengers, and off-platform transactions.

## Qualifications

The safety account is from a Didi safety leader in an interview. The Didi page remains distinct from broader legal, regulatory, labor, and consumer-experience assessments not yet represented by bounded sources.
