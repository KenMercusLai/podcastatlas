# Taksin / 郑信

Taksin, also discussed as 郑信 or 达信大帝, appears in [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046]] as a heroic Siam/Thai figure with [[Chaoshan]] ancestry. The episode uses his story to show why [[Thailand]] and Siamese trade had symbolic as well as economic force in Chaozhou migration memory.

The source does not treat Taksin as the sole cause of migration. His role is narrative infrastructure: alongside rice trade, [[RedHeadShips]], and [[ZhanglinPort]], he made the southward route feel imaginable and prestigious for later migrants.
