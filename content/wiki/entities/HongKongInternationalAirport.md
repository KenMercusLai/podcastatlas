# Hong Kong International Airport / 香港国际机场 / 赤鱲角机场

## Overview
Hong Kong International Airport / 赤鱲角机场 is the current Hong Kong airport in the Shengdong Jixi aviation roundtable's account of airport migration, cargo strength, and hub capacity.

## Current Profile
The source contrasts Hong Kong's former Kai Tak Airport with the move to Chek Lap Kok. The migration is framed as a response to growing traffic and constrained approach conditions at Kai Tak, while Chek Lap Kok represents the infrastructure layer that supports Hong Kong's current aviation-hub and cargo role.

The page is currently limited to the episode's city-network frame: Hong Kong airport capacity matters because it supports Cathay Pacific, international transfer traffic, high-value cargo, and Greater Bay Area connectivity.

## Key Characteristics
- Current Hong Kong aviation hub named through the Chek Lap Kok airport move.
- Infrastructure response to traffic growth and Kai Tak approach constraints.
- Airport layer behind Hong Kong's cargo, transfer, and Greater Bay Area role.
