# 宋佳 / 小花老师 / Song Jia

## Overview
宋佳 / 小花老师 / Song Jia appears in the wiki as the returning guest in a [[TianzhenButianzhen|天真不天真]] conversation about pausing, acting, speech, relationships, humility, and self-cultivation.

## Current Profile
The source presents Song Jia less through external biography than through a current life posture. She says she has not been in a production crew for a while and is learning that she can be busy or not busy without turning the pause into panic. Her career language still includes strong desire and certainty, including the wish to be an excellent actress, but the emphasis has shifted from self-display toward what a story or role can transmit.

Her strongest profile in the episode is relational and reflective. She repeatedly redirects the conversation from "how others treat me" to "what intention I carry and how I respond." She accepts that first reactions such as jealousy, anger, or hurt can arise, but treats the second thought, apology, and choice not to control others as the actual practice.

## Key Characteristics
- She frames the current career pause as a stage and process rather than a reason for anxiety.
- She treats desire as something to face honestly, including ambition and money, without pretending to be above it.
- She links speech to intention: words about other people's looks, luck, or ability reveal the speaker's inner movement.
- She treats acting as transmission through story and role, not only a chance to display technical performance.
- She uses scent as an entry into character imagination, including examples tied to 林慧 and 张桂梅.
- She emphasizes relationship freedom: needs can be expressed, but another person cannot be forced into an imagined ideal.
- She treats timely apology and humility as practices for interrupting old emotional inertia.
