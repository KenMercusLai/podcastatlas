# 卫平侯 / Marquis Ping of Wey

卫平侯 / Marquis Ping of Wey appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-36-weisijun-de-diwang-zhishu-lt5cpdm9yqdj4erwykd0kubfpucz]] through a compact 324 BCE succession notice: he dies and [[WeySijun|卫嗣君]] succeeds him. The episode uses the notice to show how [[WeyState|卫国]] sits on the weak side of the Warring States title order.

This page uses Wey for 卫, following [[WeyState|卫国 / Wey State]], to avoid collision with [[WeiState|魏国 / Wei State]]. It should not be confused with [[WeiPinghou|魏平侯 / Marquis Ping of Wei]], a different ruler from a different state in the Zhouji 29 succession cluster.
