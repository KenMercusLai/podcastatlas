# 刘彬晏 / Liu Binyan

刘彬晏 / Liu Binyan is a guest in [[e239-spacex-yao-rang-taikong-suanli-cong-kehuan-zouxiang-xianshi-dan-ta-huasuan-ma-259291f5-2715-4dde-bcfe-b5beb4df5793]], where he plays the physics-and-economics skeptic beside [[LouisHong]]. The episode presents him as a rocket enthusiast who accepts that orbital compute is physically possible while repeatedly asking whether it beats terrestrial data centers on cost, demand, and operating constraints.

His most important contribution is the engineering decomposition. He explains why vacuum makes [[OrbitalDataCenterThermalManagement]] harder than the intuitive phrase "cold space" suggests, why radiation can be mitigated but not ignored, why low-earth-orbit compute may fit inference better than training, and why the [[Moon]] may have nearer-term economic logic than [[Mars]] under chemical-rocket constraints.
