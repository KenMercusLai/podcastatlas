# Leonardo (MPEG)

Leonardo is the source-scoped Italian inventor in [[the-invention-invention]] who imagined portable, shareable video before digital video was easy to store or transmit. The local source identifies him only as Leonardo, so this page keeps the page title narrow rather than expanding the name beyond the source.

In the episode, Leonardo's role is to make the [[MPEG]] story less about one solitary breakthrough and more about gathering many technical pieces into a standard. His early vision of video that could move across devices becomes the practical problem later solved through [[StandardsBasedInnovation]] and the [[MPEGPatentPool]].
