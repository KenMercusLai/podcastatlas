# 沈忠 / Shen Zhong (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-916-ming-hao-he-yun-hao-nage-geng-zhongyao-lmzudjq5zfkfsukmhdeiumesm9qu|Hanji 916]] introduces Shen Zhong as a Liangguo-born langzhong who submits a high-risk memorial to [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] after [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] and [[ZhuYuLateHan|朱瑀]] regain room against [[YangQiuLateHan|阳球]]. His memorial looks backward to [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] and [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]], then accuses Zhu Yu and the eunuch network of fabricating rebellion charges, disturbing palace order, monopolizing favor for relatives, and corrupting provincial talent selection.

The episode makes Shen Zhong a late-Han witness for [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]]. He ties locusts, frontier rebellion, solar eclipse, earthquake, and other signs to political responsibility, invokes older collapse precedents such as [[ZhaoGao|赵高]] under Qin, and offers to confront Zhu Yu directly. His willingness to be boiled and have his family exiled if false turns the memorial into [[DeathDefyingRemonstrance|赴死式直谏]], but the court shelves it without response.
