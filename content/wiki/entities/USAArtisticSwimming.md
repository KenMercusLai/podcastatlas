# USA Artistic Swimming

## Overview
USA Artistic Swimming is the American national artistic-swimming program described by head coach [[AndreaFuentes]] as moving from organizational weakness and a roughly fifteenth-place world position toward world medals in 2023 and an Olympic medal in 2024.

## Current Profile
Fuentes says she accepted the coaching role after learning the program trained in California, where she had already imagined relocating. Her account depicts a resource-constrained organization recovering from corruption and relying heavily on an older volunteer who wanted to rebuild it.

The team's most important change in the episode is cultural rather than simply technical. Athletes rejected an Olympic-level workload that conflicted with education and said they wanted happiness rather than an imposed Olympian identity. A long discussion produced “grow together” as the shared purpose; Fuentes removed Olympic imagery and rebuilt training around explanation, discussion, shared learning, and collaborative challenges.

## Key Characteristics
- National artistic-swimming program led in the source by [[AndreaFuentes]].
- Rebuilt from a weak competitive and resource position according to the coach's account.
- Balances elite training with athletes' education and stated well-being priorities.
- Uses “grow together” as a shared purpose rather than making medals the daily objective.
- Encourages transparent conflict resolution, learning, and distributed problem-solving.
- Includes [[AnitaAlvarez]], whose blackout and return make safety part of the program's profile.

## Qualifications
The page represents one coach's retrospective account rather than an independent institutional history. Corruption, resource levels, ranking, workload, decision process, and causal connection between culture change and medals remain source-scoped. Athlete-centered methods do not remove selection power, safeguarding duties, or sport-specific medical oversight.
