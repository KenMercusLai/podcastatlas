# The Farming Game

## Overview

The Farming Game is a board game created by [[GeorgeRohrbacher]] and developed with [[AnneRohrbacher]] that models agricultural income, debt, operating costs, harvest variability, and bankruptcy.

## Current Profile

The game begins players with inherited acreage, debt, a line of credit, and an off-farm job, then asks them to earn enough from farming to leave that job. [[board-game-season-board-game-season]] says many of its misfortunes came from the Rohrbachers' experience or that of nearby farmers, while repeated testing made the system punishing without eliminating the possibility of success.

Its significance exceeds product sales in the episode's account. Farm families used the game to laugh at recognizable adversity, classrooms used it to teach agricultural economics, mental-health programs used it to open discussion, and advocates sent copies to members of Congress. The game helped fund Breaks Ranch and remained in print, but the source does not independently evaluate learning, therapeutic, advocacy, or sales outcomes.

## Key Characteristics

- Models inherited land, debt, credit, crops, harvest uncertainty, expenses, and bankruptcy.
- Defines success as earning enough from the farm to leave off-farm employment.
- Uses adverse events grounded in reported farming experience.
- Balances severe risk with a designed possibility of recovery and success.
- Serves as a commercial product, educational simulation, family activity, and discussion tool.
