## Overview

郭贺 / Guo He is an Eastern Han Jingzhou inspector whose current wiki profile comes from Mingdi's public reward for his local administrative merit. The source makes him a case where good regional governance becomes an imperial performance for the people to see.

## Current Profile

Guo He is singled out during Mingdi and the empress dowager's visit to Zhangling. Mingdi rewards him with a robe associated with the Three Excellencies and has him remove the carriage curtain so commoners can see the honor. The point is not only personal promotion; it is a public display that makes administrative merit visible.

The timing also qualifies the praise. The same year records floods in Luoyang and seven commanderies or kingdoms, so Guo He's reward belongs to a broader Yongping 3 scene where the court alternates between rewarding good order and responding to disasters.

## Key Characteristics

- Regional official known for local administrative merit in Jingzhou.
- Publicly rewarded by Mingdi with clothing associated with the Three Excellencies.
- Becomes an exemplar through a staged visible honor rather than a private commendation.
- Sits beside disaster records that keep the episode from becoming simple triumphal praise.
