# Project Nix

Project Nix appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-the-future-of-everything-what-ceos-of-circle-crowdstrike-more-see-coming-in-2026-39870920]] as the defense partnership between [[ArcherAviation|Archer Aviation]] and [[Anduril]]. The source describes it as an autonomous collaborative attack helicopter drone meant to provide attack-helicopter-like capability at lower cost and without putting pilots at risk.

The project matters to the wiki because it turns eVTOL technology into a [[DualUseDefenseTechnology]] case. The same aircraft, battery, autonomy, and control-stack learning that supports urban air mobility can be redirected toward [[DefenseAIProcurement]] and military aviation.
