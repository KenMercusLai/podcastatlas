# Henry of Navarre

Henry of Navarre appears in [[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] as the Huguenot leader among the episode's "three Henrys" of late-sixteenth-century [[France]]. He had escaped court in 1576 and returned to Protestantism after the violence and pressure that followed the [[StBartholomewsDayMassacre]].

Navarre becomes strategically important after [[FrancoisDukeOfAnjou|Francois, Duke of Anjou]] dies on 10 June 1584. With [[HenryIIIOfFrance|Henry III]] childless, Navarre's position as next heir makes the French succession crisis sharper and helps drive the [[WarOfTheThreeHenrys]].

For [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]] and [[England]], Navarre matters indirectly. French instability weakens France as a stable counterweight to [[Spain]], leaving the [[LowCountries|Low Countries]], [[ThrockmortonPlot]], and Anglo-Spanish crisis more exposed.
