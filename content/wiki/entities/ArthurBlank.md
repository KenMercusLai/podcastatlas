# Arthur Blank

## Overview

Arthur Blank is a [[HomeDepot|Home Depot]] cofounder in [[home-depot-1]], first appearing as [[BernieMarcus]]'s Handy Dan colleague and later as Home Depot's CFO, operating partner, and eventual CEO.

## Current Profile

Blank's role is the finance-and-operations complement to Marcus's retail vision. The episode presents him as part of the original founding quartet, then as the internal successor who led after Marcus before the board turned to [[BobNardelli]].

## Key Characteristics
- Finance and operations partner in the founding team.
- Helped convert the warehouse home-improvement idea into a scalable public company.
- Represented internal leadership continuity before Home Depot's outside-CEO succession problem.

## Evidence
- [[home-depot-1]] says Marcus recruited Blank as CFO at Handy Dan and that both were fired before founding Home Depot.
- [[home-depot-1]] places Blank among the four original founders with [[BernieMarcus]], [[KenLangone]], and [[PatFarah]].
- [[home-depot-1]] says Blank became CEO after Marcus and that the board later lacked an obvious internal successor after him.

## Qualifications

The source gives Blank less narrative detail than Marcus, Langone, or the later Nardelli/Blake contrast, so his day-to-day operating contributions remain summarized rather than deeply evidenced here.
