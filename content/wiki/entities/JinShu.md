# 《晋书》 / Jinshu

《晋书》 / Jinshu enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-150-plus-zaiye-bujian-2022-qing-duo-guanzhao-2023-fjhgtqac9r-1rpjh6oubmspqywue|Hanji 150 PLUS New Year special]] as the text the source names for the earliest appearance of the term "元旦." The episode does not develop a detailed textual history, so this page records the claim as source-scoped.

Its wiki role is narrow: it anchors the textual-memory layer inside [[YuanDanFestivalLayering|元旦节日层累]], beside the mythic [[YaoShunYuLegend|尧舜禹传说]], [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s calendar standardization, and later custom records such as [[JingChuSuiShiJi|《荆楚岁时记》]].
