# 赵廷美 / Zhao Tingmei

Zhao Tingmei / 赵廷美 is the younger brother of [[ZhaoKuangyin|赵匡胤 / 宋太祖]] and [[ZhaoGuangyi|赵光义 / 宋太宗]] in [[170-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-xia-958158115]]. He becomes important because the brother-to-brother logic of the [[GoldenCabinetPledge|金匮之盟]] could make him Taizong's next heir.

The episode says Taizong initially elevated Tingmei by giving him major titles and making him Kaifeng prefect, but that his position became dangerous after [[ZhaoDezhao|赵德昭]] and [[ZhaoDefang|赵德芳]] died. Tingmei tried to retreat politically, yet was soon accused of plotting rebellion.

The Tingmei case is presented as the clearest political clean-up after Taizong's accession. [[ZhaoPu|赵普]]'s return to the chancellorship and the use of the Golden Cabinet story make the case look less like ordinary criminal prosecution than an effort to close the succession question.
