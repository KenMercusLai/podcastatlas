# John Kenneth Galbraith

John Kenneth Galbraith is the economist and author referenced in [[157-ruhe-daizou-niushi-de-shengli-guoshi-lory40ilowkjfe-lt-hiwjdsdbq2]] through [[TheGreatCrash1929]]. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] uses Galbraith's "bezzle" idea to describe the period in a bull market when investors feel enriched by wealth that has not yet been revealed as fragile, overstated, or impossible to realize.

In this wiki branch, Galbraith matters less as a full intellectual biography than as the source of a vocabulary for [[BullMarketBezzleTrap]]. The episode translates that vocabulary into Chinese market examples such as [[LeEco]] and [[BaofengYingyin]], where strong dreams and clean narratives can become dangerous before investors recognize the difference between vision and cash-generating proof.
