# 贾从 / Jia Cong (late Han)

Jia Cong enters through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-927-zuo-shengyi-de-zuigao-jingjie-shi-shenme-lngfbjn6xui9k-9lp-ihdqkhpbpr|Hanji 927]] as the official selected to handle a rebellion in [[JiaozhiCommandery|交趾郡]]. The source identifies him as a man from [[DongCommandery|东郡]] who had served as [[JingCountyLateHan|京县]] magistrate before being appointed Jiaozhi inspector.

His method is the page's significance. Jia Cong does not treat Jiaozhi as merely a distant bandit problem. He first investigates why people rebelled, finds that earlier inspectors extracted wealth and blocked complaints, and then issues a reassurance proclamation, recalls refugees and hungry people, exempts corvee labor, kills major violent leaders, and appoints clean capable acting county heads.

Within one year, the source says Jia Cong pacifies Jiaozhi and wins popular praise for clean rule. The wiki treats him as the source case for [[RootCausePacification|查因安抚式治理]] and as a late-Han example of [[PeopleBasedPoliticalSecurity|民心型政治安全]] working through administrative repair rather than conquest.
