# Exigent Solutions

Exigent Solutions is the organization represented by [[EdFerron]] in [[ep-14-what-is-observability]]. The source presents the company as helping organizations with [[Observability]] and [[OpenTelemetry]] journeys, especially where existing monitoring and APM tools have not yet been tied to end-to-end business activity.

The wiki treats Exigent Solutions as source-scoped observability consulting context. The episode does not give a product catalog or customer list, but it does use the company to frame [[FullStackObservability]] as a practice involving architecture, telemetry ingestion, business transaction visibility, security signals, AI-assisted analysis, and operational decision support.
