# Laura Modi

Laura Modi is the founder of [[Bobbie]] and the guest in [[bobbie-laura-modi-how-a-baby-formula-startup-took-market-share-from-two-industry-giants-4488842b-74d3-40ed-8776-5831c5934e69]]. The episode traces her path from Westport, Ireland, through product operations at [[GoogleFinance]] and operating roles at [[Airbnb]], then into infant formula after her own breastfeeding difficulty and shame around buying formula in 2016.

Her wiki role is a founder-product-fit case. Modi was not a scientist, but she recruited regulatory, medical, and formula expertise around a clear lived insight: parents needed better options and less shame around [[InfantFormula]]. The episode presents her strongest operating choices as handling the [[FoodAndDrugAdministration|FDA]] recall with compliance, choosing [[SarahHardy]] as culture-first co-founder, protecting existing subscribers during shortages, and moving toward owned manufacturing after seeing contract-manufacturer dependence.
