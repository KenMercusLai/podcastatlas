# 蒋奇明 / Jiang Qiming

[[JiangQiming|蒋奇明]] is an actor and [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] guest in [[ep243-jiang-qiming-x-li-xueqin-x-shuang-xuetao-xiang-zai-dongbei-shangtian-zhe-shi-er-haishi-tai-fuza-le-lkqbo0zg5y7p1fo4x90n8yq394t6]], where he discusses entering the Northeast world of [[FeixingjiaFilm|《飞行家》]]. The source presents his casting as a performance problem: he had not previously played a Northeast person, so the role required more than copying pronunciation.

His main wiki contribution is [[RegionalLanguageAsCharacterAccess]]. Jiang says the language challenge includes habits, local cognition, and thinking style, which makes dialect a route into a character's inner life. The episode also uses his remarks on Li Mingqi's withheld interiority to show how Northeast emotional expression can be both intense and indirect.
