# 李通 / Li Tong (late Han)

## Overview
李通 / Li Tong is a Cao-aligned late-Han military actor whose Hanji 1022 role centers on accepting legal enforcement against his wife's kin rather than seeking exemption through private access.

## Current Profile
Li Tong appears through a family-pressure scene. When the uncle of his wife violates the law and Zhao Yan sentences him to death, Li Tong's wife asks him to intervene. Li Tong refuses, saying his service with Cao Cao cannot be made subordinate to private family interest. The source then presents Li Tong as praising Zhao Yan's impartial enforcement and forming a friendship with him, turning Li Tong from a possible pressure source into a witness for Cao Cao's law-backed camp order.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as the household-pressure side of Zhao Yan's legal case.
- Refuses to use Cao-side access to rescue a wife-side relative from punishment.
- Converts a potential kinship exemption into public support for the magistrate's enforcement.
- Represents a Cao-aligned military actor who strengthens organizational trust by accepting law over private pleading.
- Carries a source-scoped later military profile, including appointment in the Runan field and service against Guan Yu.
