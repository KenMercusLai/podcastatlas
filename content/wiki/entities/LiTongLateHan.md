# 李通 / Li Tong (late Han)

## Overview
李通 / Li Tong is a Cao-aligned late-Han military actor whose current wiki role combines household-level law enforcement with pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] allegiance choice. He first exposes how legal discipline can reach inside elite households; Hanji 1045 then places him under [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and [[LiuPiRunanLateHan|刘辟]] recruitment pressure in the Runan branch.

## Current Profile
Li Tong appears through two pressure scenes. When the uncle of his wife violates the law, he refuses to use service with Cao Cao as a private rescue channel. When Yuan Shao offers a commission and Liu Pi adds local Runan pressure, he rejects the defection path, kills the envoy, and sends the seal and ribbon to Cao Cao because he judges Cao Cao as the more durable political-military center.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as the household-pressure side of Zhao Yan's legal case.
- Refuses to use Cao-side access to rescue a wife-side relative from punishment.
- Converts a potential kinship exemption into public support for the magistrate's enforcement.
- Represents a Cao-aligned military actor who strengthens organizational trust by accepting law over private pleading.
- Faces direct recruitment by Yuan Shao and Liu Pi in the Runan branch rather than only household pressure.
- Rejects Yuan Shao's commission by killing the envoy and sending the seal and ribbon to Cao Cao.
- Treats Cao Cao's future victory over Yuan Shao as a matter of political quality, not visible force alone.
