# The SaaS Podcast

The SaaS Podcast is the show context for [[featherless-ai-when-your-weekend-experiment-makes-more-than-your-startup]], [[community-led-saas-growth-how-ninety-hit-44m-arr]], [[bootstrapped-saas-12m-arr-across-5-products-with-a-team-of-10]], [[eric-ries-on-how-founders-quietly-lose-their-company]], [[ai-startup-hits-8-6m-arr-with-v0-mvp-and-eur85-pricing]], [[finding-product-market-fit-after-3-years-of-failed-ideas]], [[how-danny-jenkins-bootstrapped-threatlocker-from-150k-debt-to-200m]], [[he-demoted-his-saas-to-sell-a-service-and-4xd-revenue-in-12-months]], [[50-cents-a-pool-the-pricing-model-behind-a-saas-exit]], [[stuck-at-50k-arr-for-5-years-now-1-5m-with-ai-agents]], and [[enterprise-sales-with-no-product-landing-a-big-four-customer]]. In these episodes, [[OmerKhan]] interviews SaaS founders and startup thinkers about growth, product validation, distribution, funding choices, hiring lessons, AI-era strategy, founder governance, AI-search category formation, compliance automation, cybersecurity category creation, SaaS-to-service pivots, bootstrapped vertical SaaS execution, AI-enabled solo operation, open-source AI inference infrastructure, and enterprise pre-product sales.

The Skimmer episode adds [[RonHash]] on [[Skimmer]] and [[QuickFax]]. It extends the show's SaaS branch into pool-service workflow software, field-first mobile UX, offline technician use, serviced-customer pricing, churn reduction through onboarding, and the founder's decision to sell once company-building needs exceeded his preferred operating role.

The Happierleads episode adds [[GeorgeGeorgiadis]] and [[Happierleads]] as a more extreme bootstrapped case: a technical solo founder moved from a five-year $50K ARR plateau to about $1.5M ARR with no employees by pairing deeper [[OutboundEmailGrowthEngine|outbound email]] execution with an internally built [[AIInternalOperatingSystem]]. The episode qualifies the show's AI-era SaaS branch because George now treats hiring as necessary for durability even after proving high solo leverage.

The Featherless AI episode adds [[EugeneChia]] and [[FeatherlessAI|Featherless AI]] as an infrastructure-startup pivot case. It connects a weekend validation spike, [[Recursor]]'s [[RWKV]] origin, [[GPUHotSwapping]], [[LongTailModelHosting]], [[FlatRateAIInferencePricing]], and simpler website messaging into one example of customer pull overriding the founders' original technical attachment.

The Templafy episode adds [[ChristianLund]], [[Templafy]], and [[Omnidocs]] as an enterprise SaaS case. It extends the show's validation branch from LOIs, payment, and product experiments into [[EnterprisePOCDiscipline]]: large-company pilots should prove concrete criteria and trigger a real rollout path rather than absorb startup resources as vague exploration.
