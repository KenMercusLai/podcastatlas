# Mustafa Suleyman

Mustafa Suleyman is one of the [[DeepMind]] cofounders in [[e226-liaoliao-deepmind-chuangshiren-hasabisi-yige-kexuejia-yu-shikong-de-ai-jingsai-7abda28b-99c6-4ebc-8c0d-37bcc77f6a73]]. The source describes him as coming from an immigrant-family background, leaving Oxford theology, building trust with [[DemisHassabis]], and playing a major role in financing and operating the company.

The episode uses Suleyman to show the application and institution-building side of DeepMind. His London NHS medical project becomes the source's main reputation controversy because patient data use triggered privacy criticism. After internal tension and marginalization, the source says Suleyman left DeepMind, founded [[InflectionAI]], and later became head of [[Microsoft]] AI, placing him in direct strategic contrast with Hassabis at [[GoogleDeepMind]].
