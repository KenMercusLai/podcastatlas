# ACE Studio

## Overview
ACE Studio is an AI-music company led by [[GuoJingACEStudio|郭靖]] that builds its own music-generation models and professional creation software.

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents ACE Studio as a remote company whose growth and product strategy changed through [[HF0]]. During the residency it systematized influencer outreach, pricing, conversion, and paid acquisition; afterward it concluded that those mechanisms could not substitute for stronger model capability and product experience.

The company then adapted the cohort model into temporary [[InternalStartupAccelerator|internal accelerators]]. Guo attributes one accelerator to development of the first self-built music foundation model and another to the ACE Studio 2.0 product integration. Its proposed moat joins professional workflow, authorized editing traces, and feedback from expert users through a [[ProfessionalUserFeedbackFlywheel|professional-user feedback flywheel]].

## Key Characteristics
- Develops AI music models and professional music-creation software.
- Operates as a fully remote company with periodic temporary co-location.
- Treats acquisition, pricing, and conversion as systems that can be measured and iterated.
- Places model quality and product experience above growth tactics as the basis of durable growth.
- Seeks authorized professional workflow traces and expert preference signals for model improvement.
