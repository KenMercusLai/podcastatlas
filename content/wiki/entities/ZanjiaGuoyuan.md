# Zanjia Guoyuan / 咱家果园

## Overview
Zanjia Guoyuan / 咱家果园 is the replacement juice brand introduced by [[HuiyuanGroup|汇源集团]] after the group announced that it would no longer use the Huiyuan trademark.

## Current Profile
The episode presents 咱家果园 as a rebranding vehicle intended to carry Huiyuan Group's distributor and industrial resources into a legally distinct consumer identity. Marketing reportedly accelerated from July, followed by national distributor recruitment at the 2026 autumn food-and-drink fair. The unresolved task is whether channel inheritance can overcome the loss of the old mark's recognition.

## Key Characteristics
- New juice brand created under trademark-litigation pressure.
- Backed by an existing operating and distribution organization rather than a greenfield startup.
- Subject of increased marketing and nationwide distributor recruitment.
- Brand whose consumer awareness, sell-through, and long-term differentiation are not yet established.
