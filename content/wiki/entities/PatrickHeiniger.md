# Patrick Heiniger

Patrick Heiniger is the Rolex CEO in [[rolex-1]] associated with the company's major modern vertical-integration push. The source says he became CEO in 1992, oversaw consolidation into major production sites, and led the period in which [[Rolex]] bought [[Aegler]] in 2004.

His role matters because Rolex's luxury position depends on industrial execution as well as brand. Under this reading, [[VerticalIntegrationForQualityControl]] gives Rolex the manufacturing control needed to operate as a [[HighVolumeLuxuryOperator]] without weakening quality trust.
