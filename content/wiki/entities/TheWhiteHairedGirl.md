# The White-Haired Girl

## Overview
The White-Haired Girl is the revolutionary opera that anchors the source's account of [[GuoLanying]]'s political and artistic self-understanding.

## Current Profile
[[out-numbered-ais-contentious-maths-milestone-6aa3ce622e8bb8424c920cfb]] describes the opera as the story of a peasant woman abused by a landlord, forced toward concubinage, driven into hiding, and eventually rescued by Chinese Communist Party troops. Guo's attachment to the role makes the opera a source-scoped example of revolutionary art presenting women's repression and liberation through performance.

## Key Characteristics
- Revolutionary opera centered on peasant suffering, gendered oppression, and Communist rescue.
- Key role in Guo Lanying's artistic identity.
- Used by the source to connect performance with participation in revolution.

## Evidence
- Plot and political frame - [[out-numbered-ais-contentious-maths-milestone-6aa3ce622e8bb8424c920cfb]] summarizes the heroine's abuse, flight, cave hiding, and rescue by CCP troops.
- Guo's attachment - [[out-numbered-ais-contentious-maths-milestone-6aa3ce622e8bb8424c920cfb]] says Guo fell in love with the opera at age 15 and saw singing it as revolutionary participation.
