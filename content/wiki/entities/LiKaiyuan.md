# 李开元 / Li Kaiyuan

李开元 / Li Kaiyuan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-118-1-wangjian-lv-liushiwan-dajun-duizhan-xiangyan-haoxi-kaichang-le-lsf2puinwg0m3bgyi25znof7aznu]] as the modern historian whose [[QinMi|《秦谜》]] is cited for a reconstruction of the late Qin-Chu conflict around [[ChangpingJun|昌平君]].

The episode uses Li Kaiyuan after noting that [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] gives a very compact version of the 224-223 BCE Chu campaign. In the host's summary, Li Kaiyuan starts from source tensions and reconstructs Changping Jun as Xiong Qi, a son of [[MiWan|楚考烈王]] born from a Qin royal woman while Mi Wan was hostage in Qin.

This page records Li Kaiyuan's role only as the source-cited historian behind that reconstruction. It does not independently verify the whole Qin Mi argument.
