# 谭嗣同 / Tan Sitong

## Overview

Tan Sitong enters this wiki through Hanji 895 as the later Qing reformer whose prison poem rereads [[ZhangJianLateHan|张俭]] and [[DuGenLateHan|杜根]] as paired examples of flight and steadfastness under persecution.

## Current Profile

The current source uses Tan Sitong as reception rather than as a full biography. His quoted prison poem connects late-Han party-prohibition stories to later political suffering, making Zhang Jian's desperate refuge and Du Gen's hidden endurance part of a broader language about whether to leave, stay, live, or die.

## Key Characteristics

- Later political-persecution reference point used to interpret late-Han stories.
- Poet whose prison verse links Zhang Jian and Du Gen as contrasting ethical choices.
- Reception bridge that turns an ancient party-prohibition episode into a reusable moral vocabulary.
- Source-scoped figure whose broader reform career is not yet developed in this wiki.
