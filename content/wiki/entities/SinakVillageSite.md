# Sinak Village Site and High-Rise Development

The source names Sinak as the village site near downtown [[Vancouver]] that anchors [[the-skyscrapers-that-nimbys-and-zoning-couldnt-stop]]. In the episode's account, British Columbia officials forced Squamish residents out in 1913; the [[SquamishNation|Squamish Nation]] later sued the Canadian government in 1977 and regained about 10.5 acres in 2003.

The site's current role is a large rental-housing development: 11 towers, roughly 6,000 apartments, commercial space, parks, and Squamish design elements. Because the land is sovereign Squamish territory, the project becomes a concrete [[SovereignHousingDevelopment]] case rather than a normal municipal rezoning story.
