## Overview
中国女足 / China Women's National Football Team appears in the Zhao Lina interview as the elite team context where goalkeeper competition, coach trust, major-tournament atmosphere, public visibility, and gendered comparison pressure become concrete.

## Current Profile
The current wiki evidence sees China women's national football through Zhao Lina's first-person account. It is the setting where a player who had trained since childhood could first feel football as chosen and emotionally charged, especially around the 2015 Canada World Cup and Olympic qualification. It is also the public object that Chinese media and fans often notice most intensely after wins, sometimes praising women athletes by using them to criticize men's football rather than watching women's football as its own sporting field.

## Key Characteristics
- The national team functions as a visibility threshold: the source's turning point comes when Zhao warms up before a large World Cup crowd despite being a reserve goalkeeper.
- It is a high-pressure selection environment where goalkeeper hierarchy, coach trust, and rare playing opportunities shape confidence.
- Its public reception is gendered because praise after victories can still make the team a comparison instrument rather than an autonomous sports object.
- Local fan scenes, including the Shanghai Jinshan example in the source, suggest women's football can develop spectatorship on its own terms.
- The team is connected to youth-access debates because Zhao wants more girls to encounter football by choice before elite filtering begins.
