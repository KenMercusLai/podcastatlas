# Hamish Clayton

Hamish Clayton is the [[TheIntelligence]] correspondent in [[spring-then-fall-a-weakened-muslim-brotherhood-6a7aef1b616564bf00d9b354]] who visits the London leg of the [[PremierPadel]] tournament near Wimbledon. His segment explains why [[Padel]] is spreading quickly while asking whether participation growth can become lasting spectator demand.

The report ties on-site tournament atmosphere to the sport's broader economics: small courts, friendly doubles play, a lower beginner threshold, professional prize money, streaming on YouTube and Red Bull TV, and the high cost of British courts all shape the source's [[PadelParticipationBoom]] and [[PadelCourtEconomics]] branches.
