# Louis of Orléans

## Overview

Louis of Orléans is the French royal prince whose rivalry with [[JohnTheFearless|John the Fearless]] and murder in 1407 helped turn competition around [[CharlesVIOfFrance|Charles VI]] into the [[ArmagnacBurgundianCivilWar]].

## Current Profile

The episode portrays Louis as the king's brother, a sophisticated and forceful French patriot, and a bitter opponent of [[HenryIVOfEngland|Henry IV's]] regime. Charles VI's incapacity made control of royal government and the Dauphin especially valuable, bringing Louis into direct competition with Burgundy.

After Louis removed Burgundian sympathizers from the royal council and acquired Luxembourg, he and John staged a reconciliation. Masked attackers killed him in Paris three days later. John's admission and defense of tyrannicide made the murder a founding grievance for the coalition that became known as the Armagnacs.

## Key Characteristics

- Brother of Charles VI and major prince competing for influence over royal government.
- Anti-Lancastrian French political figure in the episode's Anglo-French setting.
- Rival of John the Fearless over council access, territory, and the Dauphin.
- Murder victim whose death transformed factional competition into durable civil conflict.
