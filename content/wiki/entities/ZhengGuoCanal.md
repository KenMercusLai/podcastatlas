# 郑国渠 / Zhengguo Canal

郑国渠 / Zhengguo Canal enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-104-yinmou-shisuan-rang-qinguo-bantu-you-you-you-pengzhang-le-lgnyhbduqutegtksrjwcvhmn8-t6]] as the canal that [[HanState|韩国]] wants [[QinState|秦国]] to build in order to exhaust Qin resources. The episode describes the project as drawing Jing River water eastward through the Guanzhong region toward the Luo River system, with the practical aim of improving irrigation.

The canal becomes the episode's main historical irony. Although [[ZhengGuoHydraulicEngineer|郑国]] is sent with a delaying motive, [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] already wants Guanzhong waterworks as a foundation for Qin's future wars. Once completed in 236 BCE in the source's chronology, the project enriches Qin agriculture and is named after Zheng Guo.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-110-1-lisi-micang-laoshu-he-xiashuidao-laoshu-ni-zenme-xuan-lmv-fw-kgsuokonssq8bkbfvdsb]] adds the canal's court-politics afterlife. Its exposed hostile origin helps make foreign technical expertise look dangerous, even though the earlier source emphasizes that Qin keeps and benefits from the project.
