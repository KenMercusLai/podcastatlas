# Zhang Ziqiang / 张子强

## Overview
Zhang Ziqiang / 张子强 is discussed here as the real-crime and Hong Kong genre-film reference behind [[KongqiangFilm|《空枪》]]'s "century thief king" material.

## Current Profile
The source does not build a full biographical page for Zhang; it uses him as a comparison point for how [[KongqiangFilm|《空枪》]] rewrites Hong Kong crime memory. Earlier Hong Kong films had already used Zhang-like material inside gangster, crime, and heroic-criminal traditions. The episode argues that 《空枪》 shifts the meaning by giving its fictionalized figure a mainland-readable desire for dignity, recognition, and class mobility.

The source sharply distinguishes the historical Zhang from the film's Wan Ziqiang figure. It says the historical Zhang is closer to unbounded personality inflation and crime addiction in a speculative 1990s Hong Kong environment, while the film's character is framed through contemporary mainland language about inferiority, class hardening, real-estate resentment, and capital domination.

## Key Characteristics
- Functions as a real-crime source for repeated Hong Kong gangster and crime-film adaptation.
- Associated in the source with 1990s Hong Kong's speculative, risk-taking atmosphere.
- Used as a contrast to the fictionalized, dignity-seeking Wan Ziqiang figure in 《空枪》.
- Helps reveal how mainland political language can overwrite older Hong Kong crime-film motivations.
- Remains source-scoped here rather than a complete criminal biography.
