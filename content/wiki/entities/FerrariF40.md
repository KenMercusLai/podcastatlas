# Ferrari F40

The Ferrari F40 is the halo supercar that [[ferrari-1]] treats as the last car [[EnzoFerrari]] made before his death in 1988. The source describes it as raw, stripped down, dangerous, and different from a modern comfort-oriented luxury supercar.

The F40 matters because it made difficulty and danger part of the product's attraction. It is therefore an early source case for [[HaloProductStrategy]] and [[FunctionalAlibi]] inside the Ferrari branch.
