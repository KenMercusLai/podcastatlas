# Hypen Vice

## Overview
Hypen Vice appears in the source as a women's collegiate apparel brand that combines university marks with women's fashion and streetwear.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Hypen Vice to show that collegiate licensing is not limited to unisex basics or campus-shop souvenirs. A brand can target a more specific consumer segment by translating school marks into fashion-coded apparel while still operating inside formal university licensing.

## Key Characteristics
- Focuses on women's college apparel rather than generic all-campus basics.
- Mixes university marks with fashion and streetwear codes.
- Uses multi-school licensing coverage to address a broad collegiate audience.
- Demonstrates that school identity can become a style system, not only a team-support signal.
- Still depends on licensing approval and demand concentration around known schools.
