## Overview

Wei Boyang / 魏伯阳 is represented as an early anchor of Chinese external alchemy, associated with [[ZhouyiCantongqi|《周易参同契》]] and with a later elixir-test legend preserved through [[GeHong|葛洪]].

## Current Profile

The two sources give Wei Boyang both a textual and legendary profile. The textual branch joins Yi learning, yin-yang, five phases, coded mercury-sulfur vocabulary, and material transformation, making [[DaoistAlchemy]] an interpretive cosmology as well as furnace practice. The legendary branch has Wei, disciples, and a dog apparently die after taking an elixir before revival and transcendence. That story shows how toxicity and failure can be narratively converted into a test of faith; it is evidence about alchemical authority and storytelling, not verified resurrection.

## Key Characteristics

- Anchors an early genealogy of Chinese external alchemy in the current sources.
- Connects coded material language to cosmology and textual interpretation.
- Is associated with 《周易参同契》 as a major later alchemical authority.
- Becomes the subject of a death, loyalty-test, revival, and transcendence legend.

## Evidence

- **Textual and technical role:** [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]] links Wei Boyang and 《周易参同契》 to coded material vocabulary, yin-yang and five-phase theory, and immortality-oriented transformation.
- **Legendary authority:** [[46-guren-ruhe-changsheng-bulao-dongxifang-de-lianjinshu-liandanshu-yu-changshengyao-liporj2ydsu1pwehpgflapgm6zpx]] recounts the dog-and-disciples elixir story through Ge Hong's attributed *Shenxian Zhuan* tradition.

## Qualifications

The sources are broad podcast surveys, not a textual-critical biography. Authorship, dating, historical identity, recipe vocabulary, and the relation between Wei Boyang and the received 《周易参同契》 require specialist verification. The revival story is a religious legend and does not demonstrate safe elixirs, resurrection, or immortality.
