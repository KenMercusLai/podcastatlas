# Wei Boyang / 魏伯阳

Wei Boyang / 魏伯阳 appears in [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]] as a foundational figure for Chinese external alchemy. The episode links him to [[ZhouyiCantongqi|《周易参同契》]], presented there as a major alchemical classic that joins Yi learning, yin-yang, five phases, and coded material practice.

The source treats Wei Boyang's importance as textual and technical. Alchemical language hides materials and processes behind symbolic names, making [[DaoistAlchemy]] both a material practice and an interpretive tradition.

## Key Claims
- Wei Boyang anchors the episode's early Daoist-alchemy genealogy.
- Coded language makes alchemy hard to separate from cosmology and interpretation.
- His branch connects [[FangshuSystematization]] to later [[GeHong|葛洪]] and [[Baopuzi|《抱朴子》]].
