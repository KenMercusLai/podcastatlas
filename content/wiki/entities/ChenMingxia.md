# 陈明霞 / Chen Mingxia

陈明霞 / Chen Mingxia is the main [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] guest in [[ep275-token-tongzhang-shidai-shui-hai-neng-bu-ke-tidai-renzai-zhongliu-tebie-cehua-01-lgnagvell4jyvv7eejrcgno2xjsr]]. She pushes the episode away from generic AI adoption advice and toward the question of who benefits when token use, AI tools, and AI anxiety become workplace narratives.

Her key contribution is the episode's human-scale AI standard. She argues that people should judge AI by whether it improves their work quality, life quality, value creation, and agency, not by whether they satisfy token KPIs or big-company FOMO. She also develops the contrast between algorithmic skills and [[NonAlgorithmicCapabilities]], naming judgment, connection, decision, aesthetics, and self-definition as more important when standard execution becomes automatable.
