# Meghan Markle

## Overview

Meghan Markle appears as an American actor and royal spouse whose interview with Prince Harry is interpreted as a collision among celebrity self-narration, royal ritual, outsider status, race, and inherited institutional rules.

## Current Profile

[[30-a-royal-row-glt5120507213]] places Meghan within a series of imperfect comparisons to American and commoner entrants into royal families. The hosts argue that entertainment-industry expectations of self-expression and personal storytelling collided with a monarchy structured around lineage, ceremony, discretion, and duty. Meghan's race and Americanness make the episode's treatment of this structure inseparable from contested questions of belonging.
