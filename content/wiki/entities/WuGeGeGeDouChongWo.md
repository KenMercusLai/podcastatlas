# 五个哥哥都宠我

《五个哥哥都宠我》 is the early overseas [[AIShortDrama]] project discussed in [[cong-yangshi-jilupian-dao-baokuan-ai-duanju-di-yi-pi-zhuanshen-de-daoyan-s10e11-3c05e3d5-d8f6-44c1-97ca-698261d7b2bc]]. [[Chouxiangzai]] says he served as total director and released it on [[RodeoAI]], using domestic hit short-drama logic but localizing scenes, character appearance, and status-signaling details for overseas audiences.

The source uses the work to show both the promise and the early pain of [[AIVideoProductionWorkflow]]. Its first episode was under two minutes, but reportedly took almost ten days because the team needed roughly 70 images, then video generation for those shots, then editing and shot-language checking.

The project also illustrates the "budget imagination" side of AI short drama. The source contrasts live-action constraints such as an ordinary car or simple stage with AI-generated fantasy settings such as an Empire State Building roof, helicopter arrival, or Oscar-like award stage, making [[AIShortDramaOverseasLocalization]] a question of spectacle and cultural legibility as well as language.

## Key Points
- It is presented as an early outbound AI short-drama case, not as a mature scalable template.
- Its production timeline shows why early AI short drama still depended on large amounts of manual selection and editing.
- Its localization strategy changed visible status symbols and settings, not only dialogue.
