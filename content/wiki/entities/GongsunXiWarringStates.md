# 公孙喜 / Gongsun Xi

公孙喜 / Gongsun Xi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-67-zhanguo-mieba-baiqi-de-junshi-caineng-jiujing-ruhe-ljw0lltowxd4qz75v-wcnlkwqskv]] as the commander associated with the Han-Wei coalition at [[YiqueBattle|伊阙之战]]. The source first identifies him as a Korean general leading the allied force, then later calls him a Wei general when describing his capture; this page keeps that as source wording texture rather than forcing a settled state affiliation from this episode alone.

His function in the episode is mostly structural. Gongsun Xi represents the coalition command that [[BaiQi|白起]] defeats by exploiting weak coordination between [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]]. His capture makes the scale of Qin's victory concrete and marks the coalition's failure as both military and organizational.
