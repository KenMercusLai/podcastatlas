# Adam Foroughi

## Overview
Adam Foroughi is the co-founder and chief executive of [[AppLovin]], an advertising-technology company built around mobile-game inventory, recommendation models, and performance measurement.

## Current Profile
The interview presents Foroughi as a founder-operator who interprets advertising as applied machine learning, treats measurable advertiser returns as the test of model quality, and prefers narrow focus and organizational speed over broad platform scope. His account of AppLovin's drawdown emphasizes conviction-backed repurchases, employee alignment, deep-learning deployment, and renewed investor communication, while his comments on privacy and market size reflect the perspective and incentives of an advertising-platform CEO.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames advertising as an early and economically measurable application of machine learning.
- Distinguishes search intent capture from discovery systems that attempt to create new demand.
- Uses ownership and capital allocation as signals of conviction during public-market stress.
- Attributes competitive advantage to focus, speed, differentiated data, automation, and specialist talent.
- Expects agents to automate routine purchasing without eliminating discovery-oriented shopping.

## Qualifications
This profile derives from a single CEO interview and largely reflects Foroughi's retrospective explanation of company outcomes. The episode does not independently test his causal claims about the deep-learning launch, privacy changes, model superiority, buyback value, or market size. His relevance-versus-privacy framing underweights collection, consent, and surveillance concerns emphasized elsewhere in the wiki.
