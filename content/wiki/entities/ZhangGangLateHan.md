## Overview

张纲 / Zhang Gang enters the wiki through Hanji 822 as the late-Han official who turns an imperial inspection mission away from routine local review and toward the protected center of corruption. His buried-wheel gesture in Luoyang and direct impeachment of [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] and [[LiangBuyiLateHan|梁不疑]] make him an early source case for high-status accountability under [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-822-chailang-dangdao-ta-jiang-maotou-zhizhi-huangdi-yuefu-lqhpmayvd3p07byr-zoiv-jyhdv|Hanji 822]] places Zhang Gang among the eight inspectors sent after a 142 CE amnesty to praise good officials and investigate corrupt ones. Instead of departing for a provincial circuit like the others, he buries his carriage wheel at Luoyang's duting and says that wolves are blocking the road, so it is pointless to ask only about foxes.

The action reframes the inspection problem. Zhang Gang submits a memorial against Liang Ji and Liang Buyi with fifteen major accusations, producing shock in Luoyang. The source says Emperor Shun knows the memorial is correct but cannot immediately punish the Liang brothers because Liang-family harem favor and court networks are too strong.

Zhang Gang's immediate impact is therefore exposure rather than complete enforcement. His memorial helps make central corruption publicly legible, but it also makes him personally unsafe because Liang Ji resents him and waits for a chance to retaliate.

## Key Characteristics

- Inspector who refuses to treat local officials as the only proper target when the central powerholder is the root problem.
- Remonstrant whose "wolves before foxes" framing turns hierarchy into an accountability argument.
- Early anti-Liang voice whose memorial shocks Luoyang but cannot by itself overcome protected outer-relative power.
- Politically exposed official whose truthful accusation creates later retaliation risk.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 822. It does not reconstruct Zhang Gang's full career, later appointment history, family background, or the text of all fifteen charges. The source establishes his inspection refusal, direct anti-Liang memorial, immediate court effect, and retaliation exposure.
