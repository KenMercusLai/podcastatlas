## Overview

张纲 / Zhang Gang enters the wiki through Hanji 822 as the late-Han official who turns an imperial inspection mission away from routine local review and toward the protected center of corruption. His buried-wheel gesture in Luoyang and direct impeachment of [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] and [[LiangBuyiLateHan|梁不疑]] make him an early source case for high-status accountability under [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]]. His local-order aftereffect appears in Hanji 825: after Zhang Gang dies, [[ZhangYingGuanglingLateHan|张婴]] rises again at [[GuanglingCommandery|广陵郡]], showing that Zhang Gang's pacification authority did not automatically survive him.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-822-chailang-dangdao-ta-jiang-maotou-zhizhi-huangdi-yuefu-lqhpmayvd3p07byr-zoiv-jyhdv|Hanji 822]] places Zhang Gang among the eight inspectors sent after a 142 CE amnesty to praise good officials and investigate corrupt ones. Instead of departing for a provincial circuit like the others, he buries his carriage wheel at Luoyang's duting and says that wolves are blocking the road, so it is pointless to ask only about foxes.

The action reframes the inspection problem. Zhang Gang submits a memorial against Liang Ji and Liang Buyi with fifteen major accusations, producing shock in Luoyang. The source says Emperor Shun knows the memorial is correct but cannot immediately punish the Liang brothers because Liang-family harem favor and court networks are too strong.

Zhang Gang's immediate impact is therefore exposure rather than complete enforcement. His memorial helps make central corruption publicly legible, but it also makes him personally unsafe because Liang Ji resents him and waits for a chance to retaliate.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-825-lishi-gaosu-ni-dang-ni-meiyong-shi-renxing-zui-zisi-luv-8z9axuejnfhlkgsuxee3uked|Hanji 825]] supplies a later notice about Zhang Gang's death and local aftereffect. The episode says Zhang Gang had earlier induced Zhang Ying to submit, but after Zhang Gang dies, Zhang Ying again gathers several thousand people and occupies Guangling. This does not replace the buried-wheel profile; it adds that Zhang Gang's authority could stabilize a local rebel relationship only while he remained present.

## Key Characteristics

- Inspector who refuses to treat local officials as the only proper target when the central powerholder is the root problem.
- Remonstrant whose "wolves before foxes" framing turns hierarchy into an accountability argument.
- Early anti-Liang voice whose memorial shocks Luoyang but cannot by itself overcome protected outer-relative power.
- Politically exposed official whose truthful accusation creates later retaliation risk.
- Pacification figure whose death is followed by Zhang Ying's renewed Guangling revolt.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 822 and Hanji 825. It does not reconstruct Zhang Gang's full career, later appointment history, family background, the text of all fifteen charges, his appointment around Zhang Ying, or the precise terms of Zhang Ying's earlier submission. The sources establish his inspection refusal, direct anti-Liang memorial, immediate court effect, retaliation exposure, and a local pacification aftereffect visible only after his death.
