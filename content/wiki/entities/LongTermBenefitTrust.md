# Long-Term Benefit Trust

## Overview
The Long-Term Benefit Trust is an Anthropic governance structure intended to protect long-term public-benefit goals under financial and competitive pressure.

## Current Profile
[[EricRies]] presents the trust as a promising response to [[FinancialGravity]] and says it has held up “so far.” The new episode adds the public-authority boundary: a trust may preserve a mission without making its selectors, succession arrangements, or decisions democratically representative of humanity. Durability, transparency, accountability, and legitimacy are separate tests.

## Key Characteristics
- Attempts to preserve long-term mission in a capital-intensive AI company.
- Can influence board composition and organizational direction.
- Extends alignment from models into organizational governance.
- Faces capital, competition, and founder-control pressure.
- Does not by itself resolve who may act for the public.

## Evidence
- **Mission structure:** [[eric-ries-incorruptible-by-design-wrgromn5peq]] uses the trust as an alternative to shareholder-first governance and connects it to [[AIAlignmentGovernance]].
- **Pressure:** [[eric-ries-incorruptible-by-design-wrgromn5peq]] says AI capital needs test purpose-protecting structures.
- **Legitimacy:** [[e257-duihua-vitalik-ai-anquan-beihoude-zhexue-sixiang-youxiao-lita-zhuyi-ruhe-yingxiang-guigu-7a1e61d3-f02c-498b-ac84-cf68f59b4ca9]] reports founder voting control, trust board-selection power, and opaque succession details as reasons to ask who represents humanity.

## Qualifications
Neither source supplies the trust instrument, full voting schedule, selection records, or longitudinal outcomes. The episode's claims are not an independent legal audit. A non-democratic corporate trust can still reduce mission drift; it answers a different question from public legitimacy.

## What Changed
- Added transparency and legitimacy limits to the mission-preservation account.
- Separated structural durability from authority to represent the public.
- Migrated the profile to synthesis-v1.
