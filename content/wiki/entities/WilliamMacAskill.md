# William MacAskill

## Overview
William MacAskill is presented as a co-founder of effective altruism and [[EightyThousandHours|80,000 Hours]].

## Current Profile
The episode associates MacAskill with turning finite money and career time into comparative-impact questions. His earning-to-give argument illustrates both leverage and moral hazard: high earnings and later donations may increase beneficial resources, but future good can rationalize present conduct.

## Key Characteristics
- Applies analytical comparison to charitable and career choices.
- Helped institutionalize effective altruism and impact-oriented career advice.
- Is associated with earning to give as an indirect route to impact.
- Represents movement expansion toward longer-term and higher-leverage causes.

## Qualifications
This is not a complete intellectual biography. The episode's movement history and earning-to-give consequences are interpretive and do not make MacAskill responsible for every later action.
