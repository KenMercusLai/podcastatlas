# 雍城 / Yongcheng (Qin)

雍城 / Yongcheng (Qin) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-108-1-lishi-gan-dangmian-dingzhuang-qinshihuang-diyi-ren-lg0dssyicp387foiraohkv1x5aij]] as the Qin ancestral-temple setting where [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] stays for his crown ceremony in 238 BCE. The episode explains the rite as the transition into adulthood and personal rule.

Yongcheng also becomes the crisis geography for the [[LaoAi|嫪毐]] rebellion and its aftermath. Lao Ai aims at the palace setting there, and after the rebellion is suppressed, Ying Zheng moves [[ZhaoJi|赵姬]] to Yongcheng's Fuyang Palace before [[MaoJiao|茅焦]] persuades him to bring her back to [[Xianyang|咸阳]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-121-yingzheng-zao-shijie-zuizao-gaosu-gonglu-2200nian-huangcao-busheng-ls1u8dmuvs5bbrzadf2dtd00js-p]] adds a post-unification ritual role. The episode reads Qin Shi Huang's first westward departure from [[Xianyang|咸阳]] through [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]] and [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]] as likely movement toward Yongcheng for [[HighTempleRite|高庙礼]], a report of imperial achievement to Qin ancestors in the old capital region.
