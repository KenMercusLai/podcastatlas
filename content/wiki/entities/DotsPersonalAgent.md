# Dots Personal Agent

## Overview
Dots is the source's name for an [[OpenAI]] personal assistant that remains available inside [[ChatGPT]], operates through a cloud computer and browser, and can delegate work to other tools.

## Current Profile
The bounded source positions [[DotsPersonalAgent|Dots]] as the work-and-coding-oriented branch of the emerging [[PersonalLifeAgent|personal-agent]] market. Its persistent cloud environment can preserve execution state and run tasks in the background, but the reported paid entry point, downstream tool usage, compute demand, privacy exposure, and authority boundaries narrow its near-term reach.

Dots is not yet established here as a mass-market winner or independently documented product. The episode compares its reported minimum paid tier unfavorably with Muse's free entry point and interprets the difference partly through OpenAI's constrained compute supply.

## Key Characteristics
- Resides inside ChatGPT as a persistent assistant rather than a separate messaging-only product.
- Uses a cloud computer and browser to perform background work.
- Can hand tasks to work or coding tools while retaining a single front-door relationship.
- Is reported as rolling out through paid Pro tiers, with execution tools consuming their own allowance.
- Leans toward work and coding continuity more than Muse's travel, shopping, and household positioning.
- Inherits sensitive-account, permission, reliability, and compute-cost constraints from persistent cloud agents.

## Evidence
- Product form and execution: [[ai-jibao-26q3-muse-yinbao-geren-zhuli-astra-jinru-jiqiren-openai-shouru-mengzeng-1-183-1]] describes a ChatGPT-resident assistant with a cloud computer, browser, background execution, and tool delegation.
- Commercial access: [[ai-jibao-26q3-muse-yinbao-geren-zhuli-astra-jinru-jiqiren-openai-shouru-mengzeng-1-183-1]] reports phased access through $100, $200, and $500 monthly Pro tiers and says execution-tool use still consumes allowance.
- Competitive positioning: [[ai-jibao-26q3-muse-yinbao-geren-zhuli-astra-jinru-jiqiren-openai-shouru-mengzeng-1-183-1]] contrasts Dots's work and coding path with Muse's everyday consumer tasks and Instinct's messaging interface.

## Qualifications
The page rests on one podcast summary and does not independently verify the Dots name, availability, pricing, architecture, reliability, security, usage, or retention. The claim that compute scarcity explains pricing is the guest's interpretation. Persistent background access can improve continuity while increasing the consequences of credential, privacy, or action errors.
