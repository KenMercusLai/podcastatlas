# HP Inc.

[[fuzhuang-pinpai-a-f-xunzhao-zhongguo-hezuo-huoban-fufei-tiqian-kan-telangpu-tiewen-fuwu-shangxian-1004810677]] adds HP to a reported [[ChangXinMemory|长鑫存储]] DRAM trial. The source says HP, [[Asus|ASUS]], and [[Acer]] have begun using ChangXin chips in small numbers of entry-level notebooks, turning HP from only an allocation-advantaged large buyer into a cautious alternative-supplier test case.

HP appears in [[tech-20260113-0113-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260113-0113-mp-tech-pod-128]] as one of the large PC vendors that [[TomMinelli]] expects to fare better than smaller vendors during the [[MemoryChipShortage]].
