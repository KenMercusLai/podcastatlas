# 巨鹿郡 / Julu Commandery (late Han)

巨鹿郡 / Julu Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-932-weishenme-ren-yue-hao-yue-nan-dang-daguan-lvwzel782wcxs1lpt3rg2vmkdy29|Hanji 932]] as the commandery to which [[SimaZhiLateHan|司马直]] is appointed under [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]. The episode does not develop local Julu politics; the commandery matters because its administrator post exposes the entry fee that clean officials were expected to repay through local extraction.

This page is separate from [[Julu|巨鹿 / Julu]], the Qin-era siege setting, because Hanji 932 uses Julu as a late-Han administrative office rather than as the Chu-Han battlefield. Its current role is to anchor Sima Zhi's appointed post inside [[PalaceConstructionFeeExtraction|修宫钱转嫁盘剥]].
