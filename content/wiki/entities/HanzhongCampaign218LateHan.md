## Overview

The [[HanzhongCampaign218LateHan|218 CE Hanzhong campaign]] is presented as the shift from a failed Liu Bei probe into a prolonged contest sustained by Yi-region mobilization before Cao Cao's westward arrival.

## Current Profile

The first branch ends when Cao Hong attacks Wu Lan after [[CaoXiuLateHan|曹休]] discounts Zhang Fei's public rear-cutting threat. [[LiuBei|刘备]] renews the offensive against commanders including [[XiahouYuan|夏侯渊]], [[ZhangHeLateHan|张郃]], and [[XuHuangCaoGeneral|徐晃]], but road-cutting and fortress attacks fail. The resulting need for replacement troops and supplies turns the campaign into a rear-governance test answered by [[YangHongLateHan|杨洪]] and [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]].

## Key Characteristics

- Multi-stage campaign rather than one decisive engagement.
- Early Cao-side exploitation of a weaker opposing wing.
- Liu-side tactical setbacks followed by renewed commitment.
- Growing dependence on troops, grain, roads, and stable local administration.
- Time pressure created by Cao Cao's movement toward Chang'an and simultaneous Nanyang unrest.

## Qualifications

The page covers only the phase represented in Hanji 1162 and does not claim to summarize the full Hanzhong war or its later decisive events. Dates, route names, battle sequence, force scale, and the relation between Nanyang unrest and Cao Cao's choices remain source-scoped.
