## Overview

The [[HanzhongCampaign218LateHan|218 CE Hanzhong campaign]] is presented as a Fa Zheng-backed opportunity probe that suffers an early local defeat before becoming a prolonged contest sustained by Yi-region mobilization.

## Current Profile

The campaign opens when [[FaZheng|法正]] reads [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s departure from Hanzhong and failure to attack Yi as an opportunity, and [[LiuBei|刘备]] authorizes a probe. Separate deployments by [[ZhangFei|张飞]], [[MaChao|马超]], and [[WuLanLateHan|吴兰]] let [[CaoHong|曹洪]] concentrate against the weaker Wu Lan wing.

[[CaoXiuLateHan|曹休]] interprets Zhang Fei's public rear-cutting threat as a feint. Liu Bei later renews the offensive against commanders including [[XiahouYuan|夏侯渊]], [[ZhangHeLateHan|张郃]], and [[XuHuangCaoGeneral|徐晃]], but road-cutting and fortress attacks fail. The resulting need for replacement troops and supplies turns the campaign into a rear-governance test answered by [[YangHongLateHan|杨洪]] and [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]].

## Key Characteristics

- Multi-stage campaign rather than one decisive engagement.
- Opportunity assessment driven by Cao Cao's departure and Fa Zheng's recommendation.
- Early Cao-side exploitation of a weaker opposing wing.
- Liu-side tactical setbacks followed by renewed commitment.
- Growing dependence on troops, grain, roads, and stable local administration.

## Qualifications

The page covers only the phases represented in Hanji 1161-1162 and does not claim to summarize the full Hanzhong war or its later decisive events. Dates, route names, battle sequence, force scale, Fa Zheng's knowledge of Cao Cao's motives, and the relation between Nanyang unrest and Cao Cao's choices remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Backfilled Fa Zheng's opportunity assessment and the separated Liu-side opening deployment.
- Clarified that Wu Lan's local defeat precedes the renewed logistics-and-governance phase.
