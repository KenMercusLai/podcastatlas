# 《捍卫雅各布》 / Defending Jacob

[[DefendingJacob|《捍卫雅各布》 / Defending Jacob]] is the [[WilliamLanday|William Landay]] legal thriller discussed in [[24-hanwei-yagebu-xiangxin-ni-shi-wo-weiyi-de-zhinian-576218441]]. The episode treats it as a courtroom and family-ethics story rather than only a puzzle about who killed Ben Rifkin.

The work's pressure comes from making [[AndyBarber|Andy Barber]] both prosecutor and father. Once [[JacobBarber|Jacob]] becomes a suspect, legal procedure, family love, public suspicion, and private fear stop being separable. [[LaurieBarber|Laurie Barber]] and [[BillyBarber|Billy Barber]] widen the problem by adding maternal fear, inherited violence, and the possibility that family protection can become coercion or cover-up.

## Key Claims
- The novel makes [[LegalTruthEvidenceGap]] dramatic by refusing to let the reader replace legal proof with simple omniscience.
- Its family plot is a strong [[ParentalDefenseConflict]] case: protection of a child can become obstruction, denial, or preemptive violence.
- The "murder gene" material matters less as settled science than as a [[GeneticCulpabilityProblem]] that tests criminal law's action-based premise.
- Andy's first-person voice creates [[FirstPersonMoralAlignment]], letting readers feel the father's belief before judging its costs.
