# 田法章 / Tian Fazhang

田法章 / Tian Fazhang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-72-shenme-yang-de-ren-cai-shi-zhongzhen-zhishi-logxegnqexgc7z-lodw6tr6-8f28]] as the son of [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]] after Qi Min Wang has been killed and [[QiState|齐国]] has been shattered by [[YanState|燕国]]'s campaign. The episode presents him at the start of a survival story rather than as a developed ruler: he hides his identity and flees to avoid being killed.

His page is source-scoped for now. In this episode, Tian Fazhang matters because the fall of Qi has moved from the ruler's death into the next generation's fear, concealment, and possible restoration path.
