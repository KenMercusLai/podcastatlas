# 田法章 / Tian Fazhang

田法章 / Tian Fazhang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-72-shenme-yang-de-ren-cai-shi-zhongzhen-zhishi-logxegnqexgc7z-lodw6tr6-8f28]] as the son of [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]] after Qi Min Wang has been killed and [[QiState|齐国]] has been shattered by [[YanState|燕国]]'s campaign. The episode presents him at the start of a survival story rather than as a developed ruler: he hides his identity and flees to avoid being killed.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-73-lishishang-ziyou-lianai-de-kaichuangzhe-shi-shui-lkumfsfialqgdl6qpb3c-hnz7h9u]] resolves that setup. Tian Fazhang hides in [[JuWarringStates|莒城]] at [[TaishiJiao|太史角]]'s household, accepts servant labor to avoid discovery, and reveals his identity only after Qi ministers search for the royal heir. He is then installed as [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]], making his flight a restoration path rather than only a survival episode.

The same source gives his restoration a personal branch through [[JunWangHou|君王后]]. Before he becomes king again, Taishi Jiao's daughter notices him, treats him kindly, learns his identity, and forms a private relationship with him. After restoration, Tian Fazhang brings her out and makes her queen, turning the episode title's [[CrisisAutonomousMarriage|患难自主婚姻]] theme into part of Qi's restored court.
