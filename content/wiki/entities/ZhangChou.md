# 张丑 / Zhang Chou

张丑 / Zhang Chou appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-28-hanzhaohou-de-kuaile-ni-xiangxiang-budao-ln2tbxniz4kvwogbwp32exmdokz8]] as a [[QiState|齐国]] minister who warns [[TianYing|田婴]] not to accept [[WeiState|魏国]]'s proposed "crown" in the [[XuzhouMutualKingship|徐州相王]] sequence. The source presents him as a clear-eyed speaker with tactical sense and strong anticipation of other powers' reactions.

His warning is that title elevation does not only affect Qi and Wei. If Qi accepts the status gift too eagerly, it appears to rely on Qi strength while slighting powers such as [[QinState|秦国]] and [[ChuState|楚国]]. The later attack by [[ChuWeiwang|楚威王]], pressure from Zhao and Yan, and siege of [[Xuzhou|徐州]] make Zhang Chou the episode's anticipatory voice for [[StatusOrderBacklash|名分秩序反弹]].
