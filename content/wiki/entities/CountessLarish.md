# Countess Larish

## Overview

Countess Larish is Rudolf's cousin and the court intermediary who brings [[MaryVetsera]] into contact with [[CrownPrinceRudolf]].

## Current Profile

The Mayerling sources make Larish structurally important because clandestine romance depends on access. Part 1 shows her turning Mary's distant fascination into direct meetings by telling Rudolf about Mary's admiration, arranging a Prater coach transfer, and bringing Mary to a Hofburg back entrance. Part 2 follows the same access-broker role through shopping, photography, theatre covers, coach logistics, and later alarm once the Mayerling plan moves beyond controlled secrecy.

## Key Characteristics

- Larish functions as the key access broker between Mary and Rudolf.
- She is both Rudolf's cousin and Mary's mentor, giving her access to both sides of the affair.
- She uses respectable social routines as cover for risky court movement.
- Her arrangements escalate from signalling admiration to coach meetings and Hofburg apartment access.
- Her role shows that the affair is not simply private emotion but depends on helpers, coaches, schedules, and spaces.
- Her later alarm marks the point where clandestine logistics become a family and police problem.

## Qualifications

The sources treat Larish primarily as a functional intermediary. They do not settle her full motive, later self-defense, or responsibility beyond enabling access.
