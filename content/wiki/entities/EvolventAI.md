# Evolvent AI

Evolvent AI is the startup co-founded by [[MengFanqing|孟繁青]] in [[cong-zhengliu-dao-hecheng-shuju-dao-rsi-moxing-jingzheng-de-xiayige-jiaodian-shi-shenme-duitan-evolvent-ai-lianchuang-mengfanqing-lq1xnhp4muc3ividqhvd0ul77qmi]]. The source says the company is roughly half a year old, mostly staffed by post-2000 researchers, and focused on [[RecursiveSelfImprovement|RSI]].

The company is described as having two near-term workstreams: [[SyntheticAgentData]] collaboration and commercial data projects that often require [[EnvironmentBasedAgentBenchmarks]], and a longer-term [[RSIData]] direction. Meng says Evolvent AI does not plan to train an RSI-specific base model or only build an RSI harness; its intended position is a channel between model labs and application companies.

## Key Claims
- Evolvent AI treats data, environments, benchmarks, and training traces as the practical near-term route into RSI.
- The company's [[RSIBenchData|RSIbench-data]] work is presented as an early exploration of data-level self-improvement inside its platform.
- Its recruiting bar favors hands-on engineering and research execution over paper-count or citation-count proxies.
- The source positions the company as infrastructure between foundation-model labs and vertical applications, not as another frontier-model race entrant.
