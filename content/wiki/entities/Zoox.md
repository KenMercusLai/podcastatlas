# Zoox

Zoox is an autonomous-vehicle company discussed in [[tech-20251229-1229-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251229-1229-mp-tech-pod-128]] as one of the robotaxi companies that survived industry consolidation. The episode notes that [[Amazon]] acquired Zoox, making it part of a larger company with deep operations, capital, and logistics experience.

Zoox matters in the source because its vehicle design creates a specific regulatory problem. [[KirstenKorosek]] says Zoox still faces a federal issue because its vehicles do not include traditional mechanisms covered by existing federal law; it has an exemption to demonstrate vehicles and hopes to move into commercial operation rather than free rides only.
