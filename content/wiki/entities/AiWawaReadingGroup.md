# 爱娃娃亲子阅读小组 / Ai Wawa Reading Group

爱娃娃亲子阅读小组 is the parent-child reading project described in [[144-nvxia-duan-sihe-he-tade-jiaxiang-mapingguan-women-ceng-yiwei-you-dian-you-lu-zhishi-meng-linmjqt-n-sa5sarpqgdxgtoeejt]]. In the source, [[DuanSihe|段四合]] links it to [[Mapingguan|马坪关]] children's difficulties after school consolidation: no preschool preparation, Mandarin gaps, distance from families, and shame about being from the mountains.

The group matters because it treats reading as social confidence, not only literacy. Parents gather with children around picture books, activities, and visiting young people, giving children repeated low-stakes contact with outsiders and with peers from different backgrounds.

## Key Claims
- The project is a local response to a school-access and confidence problem rather than a generic reading promotion.
- It connects [[RuralParentChildReading]] to [[FamilyReadingEcology]] because parents, not only teachers, become part of children's language and belonging environment.
- The source says children moved from fear of strangers toward more natural interaction with city children, making reading a bridge into social ease.
- The project also supports [[RuralWomenPublicParticipation]] because the adults organizing and attending it expand their own public role.
