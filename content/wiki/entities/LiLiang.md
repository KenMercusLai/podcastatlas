# 李亮 / Li Liang

## Overview

李亮 / Li Liang is represented in the wiki as a [[ByteDance]] public-relations executive involved in public disputes over platform competition, measurement, motive, and reputation.

## Current Profile

The bounded sources place Li Liang in two related conflict settings. A retrospective on the [[ToutengWar]] uses his PR history to show how regulatory anxiety, reputational memory, lawsuits, and mutual suspicion intensified ByteDance-Tencent competition. A later [[KejiLuandun|科技乱炖]] episode says he challenged the methodology and interpretation behind the “ByteDance time” claim and then became a defendant in Qin Shuo's reported reputation-rights action after the exchange escalated. These accounts establish a public-conflict role, not the merits or outcomes of the underlying claims.

## Key Characteristics

- ByteDance public-relations executive in the sources' accounts.
- Participant in public disputes where product competition becomes reputational and legal conflict.
- Critic of the reported methodology and inference behind the “ByteDance time” framing.
- Example of how platform communications can amplify regulatory and governance debates.
