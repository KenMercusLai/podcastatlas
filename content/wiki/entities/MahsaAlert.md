# Mahsa Alert

Mahsa Alert is the app [[AmirRashidi]] says the internet freedom community outside [[Iran]] developed to help with alerting during the wartime blackout discussed in [[tech-20260402-0402-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260402-0402-mp-tech-pod-128]]. The episode treats it as a useful but constrained workaround because it still requires internet access.

The source uses Mahsa Alert to show the limits of tool-level responses to state-level network partitioning. If the [[NationalInformationNetwork|National Information Network]] blocks global connectivity, an outside-developed alerting app can exist but still fail to scale as civilian infrastructure for a country of roughly 90 million people.
