# 孙老师 / Doctor Sun (Foreign-Aid TCM Adviser)

## Overview
孙老师 / Doctor Sun is the source-scoped TCM physician and medical adviser in [[vol-95-wu-shi-mailecun-yizhen-zhapo-ni-dui-zhuxuanlv-ju-de-keban-yinxiang-he-yangguang-duitan-bianju-shouxi-yiliao-guwen-lj1pk4rns8hrpqls6jrmkdzin2u|VOL.95]], discussing foreign-aid medical service and advising [[WelcomeToMileleVillage|《欢迎来到麦乐村》]].

## Current Profile
The episode attributes three Africa trips to Sun: a 2020 public-health mission to Zimbabwe and Equatorial Guinea, a 498-day posting as a TCM physician with China's 18th medical team to Zimbabwe, and travel with the television production in Tanzania and Zanzibar. These experiences support his descriptions of interrupted utilities, water scarcity, illness, language barriers, resource-limited practice, team interdependence, and patient relationships.

His central professional claim is contextual rather than triumphalist. Local doctors may share internationally grounded training, while material limits change what can be done; Chinese teams therefore adapt, improvise, communicate, and build trust. He also describes prolonged service as an experience that can slow clinicians down and renew attention to patients and professional meaning, without denying family, health, and living-condition burdens.

## Key Characteristics
- Brings public-health mission, long-term medical-team, and production-adviser experience to the episode.
- Distinguishes local clinical capability from shortages of equipment and material resources.
- Treats language as everyday and cultural interpretation, not only translation of technical terms.
- Describes improvisation, optimism, team support, and trust as practical parts of foreign-aid medicine.
- Presents overseas service as potentially renewing clinical attention and professional meaning.
- Advises prospective participants to expect incomplete preparation and solve problems in context.
