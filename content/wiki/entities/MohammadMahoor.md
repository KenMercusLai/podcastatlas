# Mohammad H. Mahoor

## Overview
Mohammad H. Mahoor is the [[DataScienceWithSam]] EP42 guest, referred to in the local source as Professor Mohammed. The episode identifies him with [[UniversityOfDenver]] lab research and [[DreamFaceTechnologies]], and discusses his work on [[RyanSocialRobot|Ryan]], [[ArtificialEmotionalIntelligence]], computer vision, affective computing, and [[SocialRoboticsElderCare]].

## Current Profile
In the source, Mahoor is a computer-science and social-robotics researcher whose work moved from face recognition into human-centered AI. His postdoctoral exposure to autism research, facial expressions, gaze, and emotion becomes the bridge from technical computer vision into robots designed for older adults with loneliness, depression, cognitive impairment, and dementia.

His profile is not framed as a pure model-builder profile. The episode makes him an example of applied AI leadership where perception, LLM conversation, hardware reliability, ethics review, privacy, and user experience have to be designed together because the users are vulnerable and the robot operates in a care setting.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects face recognition, affective computing, social robotics, and elder-care use cases.
- Treats [[ArtificialEmotionalIntelligence]] as cue sensing and appropriate response, not proof that machines understand emotion like humans.
- Uses [[RyanSocialRobot|Ryan]] to test how embodied AI behaves with older adults in clinical-pilot settings.
- Frames LLMs as a major interaction upgrade while keeping hallucination, overconfidence, compute, and cloud dependence visible.
- Emphasizes dignity, consent, transparency, privacy, and trust as design requirements from the beginning.
- Presents care robots as augmenting caregivers and families rather than replacing human care.

## Evidence
- Career path: [[ep-42-when-ai-meets-robotics-building-machines-that-care]] says Mahoor moved from PhD face-recognition work and psychology-department autism research into social robotics after joining the University of Denver in 2008.
- Technical frame: [[ep-42-when-ai-meets-robotics-building-machines-that-care]] defines artificial emotional intelligence through facial expression, gaze, head pose, body language, voice, and sentiment cues.
- Ryan deployment: [[ep-42-when-ai-meets-robotics-building-machines-that-care]] describes Ryan as a robot for older adults that can converse, read expressions, play cognitive games, and has logged nearly 900 hours in clinical pilots.
- Ethics and trust: [[ep-42-when-ai-meets-robotics-building-machines-that-care]] emphasizes IRB approval, consent, guardian consent for some residents, coercion checks, privacy, transparency, and trust.
- Entrepreneurship: [[ep-42-when-ai-meets-robotics-building-machines-that-care]] says [[DreamFaceTechnologies]] grew from University of Denver lab research and that Mahoor's entrepreneurship path began with NSF I-Corps-style customer discovery.
