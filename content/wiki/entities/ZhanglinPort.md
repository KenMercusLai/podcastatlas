# Zhanglin Port / 张林港

Zhanglin Port was the early [[Chaoshan]] outlet highlighted in [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046]]. Located around today's Chenghai area of [[Shantou]], it connected river transport, Chaozhou prefectural markets, and maritime trade before steamships and siltation shifted more activity toward Shantou port.

The source uses Zhanglin Port to show that Chaoshan's overseas orientation did not begin with modern treaty-port Shantou. It was already a trading node tied to [[RedHeadShips]], Siam rice, [[Thailand]], and the early formation of [[ChaoshanMaritimeCommercialCulture]].
