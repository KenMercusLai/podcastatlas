# 晏婴 / Yan Ying

晏婴 / Yan Ying appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-2-lmfzo4sl9nd5kz3mrxdl1dl9efei]] as the official who recommends [[SimaRangju|司马穰苴]] to [[QiJinggong|齐景公]]. The source uses him less as an independent biographical subject than as the talent-recognition link that lets a low-status but capable military figure enter command.
