# Airey Neave

## Overview
Airey Neave appears as the campaign organizer who made [[MargaretThatcher|Margaret Thatcher]]'s seemingly hopeless challenge to [[EdwardHeath|Ted Heath]] tactically viable in 1975.

## Current Profile
[[662-britain-in-the-70s-the-rise-of-thatcher-part-1-glt1785737730]] presents Neave as the practical operator behind Thatcher's underdog campaign. He arranged meetings with Conservative MPs, encouraged first-ballot protest lending, and helped turn anti-Heath frustration into votes for Thatcher before the contest moved beyond symbolic challenge.

## Key Characteristics
- Internal parliamentary campaign manager for Thatcher's 1975 leadership bid.
- Used Thatcher's underdog status to attract protest votes against Heath.
- Helped convert diffuse Conservative dissatisfaction into an organized first-ballot shock.
- Serves as the episode's reminder that ideological turns still require tactical organization.
