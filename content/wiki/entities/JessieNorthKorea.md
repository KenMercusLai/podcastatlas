# Jessie (North Korea entrepreneur)

Jessie is the North Korean woman in [[getting-entrepreneurial-in-korea-summer-school]] whose childhood and adult trading story makes ordinary entrepreneurship visible under repression. The episode says she began selling wild raspberries as a child, later sold purer alcohol, and then expanded into smuggling goods across the Yalu River.

Her story is also the episode's clearest [[PropertyRightsAsInvestmentIncentive]] counterexample. After her uncle reported her business activity, the state took her money and possessions; she escaped using the same trade routes she had once used for business. The source uses Jessie to show that [[DonjuMarketClass|donju]] talent can exist without secure ownership, courts, or reliable protection.
