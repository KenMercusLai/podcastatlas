# WeMade

WeMade is the Korean game company whose search for a China partner helped bring [[LegendOfMir|《传奇》]] to [[Shanda|盛大]] in [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]]. The episode says the connection happened through a partly accidental Shanghai animation-association route, which made Legend look like a lucky meeting between Korean game production and Shanda's interactive-entertainment orientation.

WeMade remains important because the rights relationship did not end with the original agency contract. Later disputes and settlements involving [[KaiyingNetwork]] show how [[OnlineGameIPRightsDisputes]] can persist as long as a game has enough [[GameIPLongTail]] value to justify new products and litigation.
