# Southern Transitional Council / 南方过渡委员会

## Overview

The Southern Transitional Council is a Yemeni separatist movement presented as UAE-backed, anti-Houthi, and central to the immediate Saudi-UAE dispute over southern Yemen.

## Current Profile

[[304-shate-alabo-guoqu-shinian-zai-jinxing-yichang-zenyang-de-zhengzhi-gaige-lsm0nzvie2-h_sgbmhavg7xbv74t|episode 304]] describes the council as pro-UAE, anti-[[Houthis|Houthi]], anti-Iranian, and influential along Yemen’s Gulf-facing coast. UAE support fits the episode’s [[CorridorStateStrategy]] because southern ports and passages have network value.

The same support creates a Saudi security problem because separatist military activity occurs near the Saudi border and fragments the nominal anti-Houthi side. The council therefore connects local territorial aims to wider Gulf rivalry.

## Key Characteristics

- Southern Yemeni separatist movement in the source account.
- Supported by the [[UnitedArabEmirates]].
- Opposed to the [[Houthis]] and Iran.
- Connected to coastal, port, and passage control.
- Source of strategic pressure and distrust between the UAE and [[SaudiArabia]].
