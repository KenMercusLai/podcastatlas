## Overview

韦康 / Wei Kang is the Liangzhou inspector who, in the current source, opens Ji to [[MaChao|马超]] and is killed after surrender. His death becomes the immediate grievance around which [[YangFuLateHan|杨阜]] and other nominal subordinates organize resistance.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1137 presents Wei Kang mainly through the consequences of his death. Surrender does not preserve him, and Ma Chao's reported killing converts officials who outwardly accept the new regime into covert opponents. The durable profile is therefore narrower than a full biography: Wei Kang is a surrendered local superior whose treatment damages the incoming ruler's credibility and supplies a common revenge claim for a resistance network.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Han inspector associated with Liangzhou and Ji.
- Opens Ji to Ma Chao and surrenders in the episode's account.
- Is killed after surrender, making his fate a case of failed surrender protection.
- Becomes the named superior whose death unifies Yang Fu and other local officials in resistance.

## Qualifications

The current profile rests on one structured popular-history summary. Wei Kang's exact office, authority to surrender, surrender terms, chronology, dialogue, and the immediate circumstances of his death require comparison with transmitted histories and specialist scholarship. His killing is a credible contributor to the revolt in this source, but it should not be treated as a complete explanation of every participant's motive.
