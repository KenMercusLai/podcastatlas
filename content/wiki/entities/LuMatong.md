# 吕马童 / Lu Matong

吕马童 / Lu Matong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-168-bawangbieji-qian-fashengle-shenme-2-liqgict85wh-bf4fn2bytygmk-ca|Hanji 168 part 2]] as the Han cavalry sima whom [[XiangYu|项羽]] recognizes during the final fight at [[WujiangChuHan|乌江]]. The source says Lu Matong also points Xiang Yu out to [[WangYiHanGeneral|王翳]].

Xiang Yu's response gives Lu Matong a distinctive place in the episode. After hearing that [[LiuBang|刘邦]] has offered gold and a marquisate for his head, Xiang Yu says he will give Lu Matong a favor and then kills himself. In the source's final image, Lu Matong is one of the Han-side men attached to the reward distribution around Xiang Yu's body.
