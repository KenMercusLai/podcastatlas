# 孙权 / Sun Quan

## Overview
孙权 / Sun Quan is the younger Sun-family heir who turns from background family marker into a working Jiangdong successor after [[SunCe|孙策]]'s fatal wound. The current wiki profile now covers his family base, his initial public stabilization under Zhang Zhao and Zhou Yu, and his first active consolidation through talent recognition, advice intake, and punishment of internal threats.

## Current Profile
Sun Quan enters the source base as part of the [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] and [[LadyWuSunJian|吴夫人]] family trajectory, then becomes explicit successor when Sun Ce transfers the seal. Hanji 1045 shows the dangerous first moment: Sun Quan is grieving and afraid, and [[ZhangZhaoLateHan|张昭]] plus [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] must make his authority visible to the camps.

Hanji 1053 adds the next layer of independent rulership. Sun Quan preserves room for his own personnel judgment when Zhang Zhao criticizes [[LuSu|鲁肃]], inspects inherited commanders, promotes [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] after seeing disciplined troops, and accepts [[LuoTong|骆统]]'s advice on honoring and observing worthies. The same episode also shows the hard side of consolidation: [[SunFuLateHan|孙辅]] secretly asks [[CaoCao|曹操]] to intervene, and [[LiShuLateHan|李术]] turns [[LujiangCommandery|庐江]] into a resistance and defector-shelter point. Sun Quan answers by isolating Sun Fu and by attacking Li Shu after first cutting off Cao-side rescue.

## Key Characteristics
- Belongs to the Sun Jian-Lady Wu family trajectory that links Qiantang Wu resources to later Jiangdong rule.
- Receives Sun Ce's seal after the assassination wound and initially needs senior advisers to turn grief into visible public authority.
- Keeps independent personnel judgment even when a senior adviser gives a negative view of an important talent such as Lu Su.
- Tests inherited commanders by inspecting actual unit condition, with Lyu Meng's disciplined troops becoming a promotion signal.
- Accepts Luo Tong's advice that respect, personal meetings, questions, and speech observation are tools for finding usable people.
- Treats kinship and Sun Ce-era appointment as insufficient guarantees of loyalty, as shown by Sun Fu and Li Shu.
- Uses legal-political sequencing and force to establish authority, while the Li Shu campaign carries a severe civilian-cost qualification.

- Successor designation and first stabilization: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1045-ta-shi-jiangdong-baye-de-kaichuangzhe-kexi-si-de-tai-wonang-lgjjdmcfw1qkuba2xblgvvlfpy9d|Hanji 1045]] has Sun Ce hand the seal and ribbon to Sun Quan, compare their respective strengths, and then shows Zhang Zhao and Zhou Yu stabilizing the transition.
- Personnel recognition and advice intake: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1053-genzhe-sunquan-xuexi-ruhe-zhengdun-jituan-gaoguan-lq9hy25xonjzhj1sfisvauu3bwmi|Hanji 1053]] shows Sun Quan valuing Lu Su despite Zhang Zhao's criticism, promoting Lyu Meng after troop inspection, and accepting Luo Tong's advice on observing and honoring talent.
- Internal threat suppression: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1053-genzhe-sunquan-xuexi-ruhe-zhengdun-jituan-gaoguan-lq9hy25xonjzhj1sfisvauu3bwmi|Hanji 1053]] records Sun Fu's secret appeal to Cao Cao, Sun Quan's punishment of Sun Fu's intimates, the breakup of Sun Fu's army, and Sun Fu's eastward relocation.
- Lujiang authority test: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1053-genzhe-sunquan-xuexi-ruhe-zhengdun-jituan-gaoguan-lq9hy25xonjzhj1sfisvauu3bwmi|Hanji 1053]] has Sun Quan write Cao Cao before attacking Li Shu, then captures Wan City, kills Li Shu, slaughters civilians, and relocates more than twenty thousand people.

## Qualifications
Sun Quan's early strength should not be flattened into a mature-ruler profile. Hanji 1045 still shows him dependent on Zhang Zhao and Zhou Yu during the first succession shock. Hanji 1053 strengthens the judgment that he can select people and act decisively, but it also keeps coercion and civilian harm visible, especially in the Li Shu campaign.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1053 as the first source for Sun Quan's active post-succession personnel and internal-discipline program.
- Reframed Sun Quan from a successor stabilized by seniors into a young ruler who begins testing commanders, using advice, and suppressing internal threats.
- Added the qualification that effective authority consolidation in the Li Shu case includes civilian slaughter and forced relocation.
