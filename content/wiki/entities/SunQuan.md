# 孙权 / Sun Quan

## Overview
孙权 / Sun Quan is the younger Sun-family heir who moves from background family marker to explicit successor after [[SunCe|孙策]]'s fatal wound. The current wiki profile covers his origin in [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] and [[LadyWuSunJian|吴夫人]]'s household, his generation's exposure to Cao-side recognition politics, and his first public stabilization under Zhang Zhao and Zhou Yu.

## Current Profile
Sun Quan is still thinly developed as an independent ruler in the current source base, but Hanji 1045 gives his succession a clear institutional frame. Sun Ce judges him better suited to selecting worthy people and preserving Jiangdong, while [[ZhangZhaoLateHan|张昭]] and [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] convert his initial grief into visible authority before the camps.

## Key Characteristics
- Sun Quan belongs to the Sun Jian-Lady Wu family trajectory that links Qiantang Wu resources to later Jiangdong rule.
- Before succession, his generation is part of the political environment in which Cao Cao recognizes Sun Ce while keeping Sun-family heirs under leverage.
- Sun Ce designates Sun Quan as successor through seal transfer after the assassination wound.
- His first weakness is visible grief and fear, not yet strategic incompetence or mature rulership.
- Zhang Zhao's intervention makes Sun Quan perform office publicly by changing clothes, mounting him, and touring the camps.
- Zhou Yu's return and decision to stay gives Sun Quan's transition a military partner alongside Zhang Zhao's civil authority.

## Evidence
- Family origin: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-982-ba-yi-ba-liubei-ru-xu-beihou-de-liyi-zhi-zheng-ljajdc95xtylhkn5teefz26yyyz1|Hanji 982]] introduces Sun Quan as one of Sun Jian and Lady Wu's sons, tying him to the Qiantang Wu-clan support base.
- Recognition and leverage environment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1028-xiaobawang-sunce-liwei-chengba-jiangdong-lgur-3fmpr55wy3aji-0rm38y7x|Hanji 1028]] places Sun Quan's generation in the context of Cao Cao's recognition of Sun Ce and related hostage-like family politics through Sun Yi.
- Successor designation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1045-ta-shi-jiangdong-baye-de-kaichuangzhe-kexi-si-de-tai-wonang-lgjjdmcfw1qkuba2xblgvvlfpy9d|Hanji 1045]] has Sun Ce hand the seal and ribbon to Sun Quan and compare their respective strengths.
- Public stabilization: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1045-ta-shi-jiangdong-baye-de-kaichuangzhe-kexi-si-de-tai-wonang-lgjjdmcfw1qkuba2xblgvvlfpy9d|Hanji 1045]] shows Zhang Zhao rebuking the grieving Sun Quan, dressing him in official clothes, and taking him through the camps while Zhou Yu stays in Wu Commandery.

## Qualifications
The page still depends on early succession evidence and should not project later Eastern Wu rulership backward too strongly. Sun Ce's favorable comparison of Sun Quan's talent-selection capacity is important, but Hanji 1045 simultaneously shows the young successor needing urgent support from senior advisers.
