# 孙权 / Sun Quan

孙权 / Sun Quan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-982-ba-yi-ba-liubei-ru-xu-beihou-de-liyi-zhi-zheng-ljajdc95xtylhkn5teefz26yyyz1|Hanji 982]] as a son of [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] and [[LadyWuSunJian|吴夫人]]. The source uses him less as an active actor in the 194 CE scene than as the later payoff of the Sun family's early marriage-resource network.

The episode says Sun Quan later relies on Qiantang's water-route knowledge and the old standing of [[QiantangWuClan|钱塘吴氏]], then honors Lady Wu after becoming emperor. This makes Sun Quan's source role a downstream marker for [[KinshipResourceStateBuilding|姻亲资源建构]]: the family and locality resources assembled before [[SunCe|孙策]]'s rise remain politically useful in the later Jiangdong regime.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1028-xiaobawang-sunce-liwei-chengba-jiangdong-lgur-3fmpr55wy3aji-0rm38y7x|Hanji 1028]] mentions Sun Quan only as part of [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s leverage over [[SunCe|孙策]]. After recognizing Sun Ce with office and marriage ties, Cao Cao asks that Sun Quan and [[SunYiLateHan|孙翊 / Sun Yi]] come to the capital as officeholders; the host reads this as carrying a hostage function, not simply as honor.
