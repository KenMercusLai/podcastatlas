# Harry Houdini

## Overview
Harry Houdini was an escape artist, magician, researcher, and promoter whose name remained culturally active a century after his death in 1926.

## Current Profile
The source presents Houdini's fame as an integrated operating system rather than a publicity trick. He trained his body, studied magic history, researched illusion origins, refined performance, engineered public challenges into news, adopted new media, and confronted imitators. His posthumous myth was also shaped by a controlled test: his widow Bess waited for a prearranged secret code before rejecting claims that he had returned through spiritualism.

## Key Characteristics
- Athletic and technical discipline supported the credibility and repeatability of his escapes.
- Historical research helped him refine tricks, challenge false origin stories, and invent within a tradition.
- Public challenges and visually legible danger converted local performances into news events.
- Early use of radio and film extended promotion beyond conventional advertising.
- Aggressive name and secret protection treated public identity as an asset, though patents could expose methods.
