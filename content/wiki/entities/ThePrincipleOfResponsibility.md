# The Principle of Responsibility / 《责任原理》

## Overview
The Principle of Responsibility / 《责任原理》 is the [[HansJonas]] book used in [[episode-227-ershiyishiji-qingchun-wuyu-8211230332-278487]] to explain precaution under technologies and decisions with irreversible consequences.

## Current Profile
Within the wiki, the book functions as an ethics node rather than a full bibliographic entry. Ren Ning presents it as a response to modern technical capacity: when nuclear weapons, genetic technologies, and other large systems can create irreversible outcomes, action must be judged by possible future harm as well as present intention.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames responsibility as expanding with human technical power.
- Treats some choices as too irreversible for simple trial and error.
- Grounds the source's move from state and technology ethics into everyday [[YouthPrecautionaryCulture]].
- Helps distinguish harm prevention from traditional order-protecting conservatism.

## Evidence
- Irreversible-technology frame: [[episode-227-ershiyishiji-qingchun-wuyu-8211230332-278487]] uses the book to discuss nuclear and genetic technologies as cases where consequences cannot be casually tested.
- Precautionary extension: [[episode-227-ershiyishiji-qingchun-wuyu-8211230332-278487]] connects the book to the broader precautionary principle and then to personal life.
- Youth-risk distinction: [[episode-227-ershiyishiji-qingchun-wuyu-8211230332-278487]] uses the book to show that fear of harm is not the same as nostalgia for old hierarchy.
