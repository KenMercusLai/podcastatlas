# John McCain

## Overview

John McCain was a U.S. naval aviator, prisoner of war, senator, and presidential nominee whom the episode presents as an honor-centered foil to [[DonaldTrump|Donald Trump]]'s anti-establishment political style.

## Current Profile

The episode emphasizes McCain's captivity, torture, and refusal to yield as a Roman-coded war-hero profile inside American political culture. Trump's public attack on him for having been captured becomes a direct rejection of that honor system, while McCain's funeral—with bipartisan establishment attendance and Trump excluded—is interpreted as political theatre defending inherited norms.

## Key Characteristics

- Combined military captivity and endurance with a public reputation for honor.
- Represented an older bipartisan and establishment ideal of presidential character.
- Acquired posthumous symbolic force through a funeral staged as a defense of political norms.
