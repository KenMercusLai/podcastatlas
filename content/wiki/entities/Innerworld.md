# Innerworld

## Overview
Innerworld is the boxing-and-psychology community described by [[SteveShiDiFuShuoHost|Steve]] in [[shi-di-fu-shuo-467-joanjie-kanqing-ziwo-hou-jiu-meiren-neng-zudang-ni-huochu-ziwo-le-ltoecvltslyfjflqanu2lvo7yqqg]]. The source says Steve and his wife first built it from their own experience that boxing improved mental state, then it grew into a space where members train, spar, share feelings, and receive psychological and relational support.

## Current Profile
The current evidence frames Innerworld less as a conventional gym than as a growth environment. Boxing matters because real-time sparring forces attention into body, fear, anger, apology, aggression, restraint, and instinct. The community layer matters because practice is followed by reflection, witnessing, and mutual support, especially for people in transition, burnout, depression, unemployment, leave, gap periods, or other states where ordinary identity and energy are unstable.

Innerworld's distinct claim is that self-development needs soil, not only information. The source describes a combination of bodily challenge, ritual, professional psychological guidance, and nonjudgmental peer presence. It also stresses operational difficulty: the form can be imitated, but the harder work is maintaining relationships, atmosphere, safety, and actual attention to specific people.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines boxing, sparring, emotional sharing, and psychological support rather than treating boxing as isolated fitness.
- Uses embodied challenge to move members from overthinking into sensation, balance, instinct, and direct self-contact.
- Treats vulnerability and strength as connected: members are not trying to become invulnerable but to find power inside vulnerability.
- Builds rituals and group witnessing to make emotional support felt rather than only explained.
- Serves many people in life-transition states who need a nonjudgmental place to be seen and rebuild agency.
- Presents community atmosphere and relationship operation as harder to copy than the boxing-plus-psychology format.

## Qualifications
The current profile is based on Steve's own interview account. It does not independently evaluate Innerworld's safety protocols, clinical boundaries, coaching standards, business model, or member outcomes. Because boxing and group vulnerability can carry physical and emotional risk, the source's claims should remain bounded to this account.
