# Ghost of Tsushima

## Overview
Ghost of Tsushima is the video game used by [[TheRestIsHistory]] to introduce the modern global image of the samurai before the episode turns back to early medieval origins.

## Current Profile
[[658-dawn-of-the-samurai-the-shadow-of-the-sword-part-1-glt7378881498]] uses Ghost of Tsushima as a doorway into the gap between samurai myth and samurai history. The source describes the game as set during the Mongol invasion of 1274 and centered on Jin Sakai, a nobleman trained in samurai ideals who is pushed toward assassination and irregular warfare.

For the wiki, the game's significance is not game-history detail but framing. It shows how samurai now circulate as a global visual and moral icon: Japanese, medieval, cinematic, honorable, and conflict-ridden. That image makes the later distinction between modern [[BushidoInventedTradition|Bushido]] and older warrior formation more important.

## Key Characteristics
- The game is used as a modern gateway into samurai history.
- Its Jin Sakai framing dramatizes the tension between honorable-warrior ideals and irregular violence.
- It belongs to a broader media chain that includes film, museums, manga, television, and [[StarWars]]-adjacent samurai imagery.
- The source uses it as an image of samurai myth rather than as evidence for 10th-century origins.
