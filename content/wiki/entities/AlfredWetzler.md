# Alfred Wetzler

## Overview

Alfred Wetzler, called Fred Wetzler in the source, was an Auschwitz-Birkenau escapee who co-authored the [[VrbaWetzlerReport]] with [[RudolfVrba]].

## Current Profile

[[292-the-shadow-of-the-holocaust-glt7756076339]] places Wetzler beside Vrba at the origin of one of the most consequential wartime accounts of Auschwitz-Birkenau. Their escape enabled them to describe gas chambers, crematoria, deportation patterns, and the camp's mass-murder system to people outside Nazi control.

The episode concentrates on the report's reception and on Vrba's later life, so Wetzler's independent biography is not developed. His canonical importance here is collaborative: the evidence and warning were produced by two escapees even when later public memory foregrounded Vrba.

## Key Characteristics

- Escaped Auschwitz-Birkenau with Rudolf Vrba.
- Co-produced a detailed account of the camp's killing system.
- Supplied direct witness evidence that circulated internationally in 1944.
- Remains essential to the report's identity despite the episode's stronger biographical focus on Vrba.

## Evidence

- Joint authorship: [[292-the-shadow-of-the-holocaust-glt7756076339]] identifies Vrba and Wetzler's escape and report as the source of revelations reaching Allied and Hungarian decision-makers.
- Historical consequence: [[292-the-shadow-of-the-holocaust-glt7756076339]] connects the report's publication to pressure preceding the halt in Budapest deportations.

## Qualifications

This source does not provide a full Wetzler biography or disentangle his individual contribution from Vrba's. Claims about authorship, circulation, effect, and rescue remain attached to the joint report; the precise causal contribution to Horthy's decision remains contested.
