## Overview

谢烟客 is a [[XiakeXingNovel|《侠客行》]] character whose promise attached to the 玄铁令 turns power into self-imposed constraint when [[ShiPotian|石破天]] refuses to ask him for anything.

## Current Profile

The bounded episode presents 谢烟客 as extremely capable, calculating, and morally mixed. He values reputation and the letter of his promise enough that he will not simply ignore the final 玄铁令, yet he tries to escape its obligation by manipulating its accidental holder into making a request or learning dangerous martial practice.

石破天's learned refusal to ask others for things defeats that strategy without a counter-strategy. 谢烟客 expects desire, bargaining, and dependence; literal self-sufficiency leaves him unable to complete the promise on his own terms. The comic reversal therefore exposes both a real commitment to keeping one's word and the narrowness of honoring form while trying to defeat the beneficiary.

## Key Characteristics

- Possesses exceptional martial power and a reputation strong enough to make the 玄铁令 credible.
- Treats promises and public standing as binding even while seeking manipulative escape routes.
- Relies on anticipating ordinary desire, calculation, and requests from others.
- Becomes constrained when 石破天 does not participate in that exchange logic.
- Combines a genuine code with selfishness, scheming, and limited moral perspective.

## Qualifications

This profile comes from one conversational literary reading rather than a complete comparison of editions or all of 谢烟客's conduct. Respect for promises does not neutralize attempted harm, and the episode's judgment that his central defect is limited perspective rather than a simple good-evil category remains interpretive.

## What Changed

- Added nonparticipation in ordinary exchange logic as the limit of strategic manipulation.
