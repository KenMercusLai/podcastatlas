# Zinc (popcorn-bucket design)

Zinc is the Australian popcorn-bucket design company discussed in [[aodesai-ba-baomihua-zhuangjin-teluoyi-muma-haolaiwu-dapian-weihe-zhadui-dingzhi-baomihuatong-1004599628]]. The episode says the company had long experience designing themed buckets and tried to expand the business into U.S. cinemas in 2016.

Its key source case is the 2019 [[StarWars|Star Wars]] R2-D2 bucket. The episode says [[AMCTheatres|AMC Theatres]] bought a large order, priced the bucket at about $50, and sold through quickly, helping convince theaters that audiences would pay for a plastic container when it acted like a collectible.
