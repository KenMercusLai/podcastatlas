# Orienspace / 东方空间

Orienspace, or 东方空间, is [[YaoSong]]'s second hard-tech company in [[173-duihua-yao-song-shenjian-dongfang-kongjian-zaichufa-tiancai-shaonian-shinian-hou-1-173-1]]. The source frames it as a China-specific commercial-space bet: if satellite internet and future communications need large constellations, launch capacity becomes part of [[SpaceEconomyInfrastructure]].

Yao describes the company's launch priority as "好多快省": high reliability, enough lift capacity, market-entry speed, and then cost. That ordering becomes [[CommercialRocketLaunchPriority]] because it differs from a pure low-cost launch story. The episode says Orienspace moved from solid rockets toward liquid recoverable rockets, with [[Gravity1Rocket|引力一号]] as the most visible early milestone.

## Key Claims
- The source separates "China," "commercial," and "space" as the three parts of the thesis.
- Satellite internet and possible 6G orbital infrastructure are treated as demand drivers, not only as space symbolism.
- Yao's role is described as complementary to rocket engineers: strategy, capital, market, local-government, and organization-building capacity.
- The source says the company had entered a more stable phase, with large contracts signed, before Yao left amicably.
