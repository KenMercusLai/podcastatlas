# 伊能静 / Yi Nengjing

## Overview
伊能静 is a performer and participant in the first season of [[SistersWhoMakeWaves|《乘风破浪的姐姐》]]. The source uses her as a case of established experience that can support others but can also harden into authority when a new environment requires adaptation.

## Current Profile
The hosts present a mixed judgment. One recalls a brief professional encounter in which Yi was considerate and courteous, and both credit her persistence, care for other participants, and continued public expression under heavy criticism. They also argue that she initially instructed teammates with more authority than her current performance justified. Her case is framed as a senior professional learning that prior success does not automatically transfer into a new competitive setting.

## Key Characteristics
- Brings a long career and strong confidence in prior experience.
- Is described as courteous, hardworking, and attentive to colleagues.
- Continued expressing views despite intense negative public reaction.
- Sometimes adopted an instructional authority that the hosts believed exceeded demonstrated fit.
- Appeared to adjust her conduct after feedback and experience in the program.
