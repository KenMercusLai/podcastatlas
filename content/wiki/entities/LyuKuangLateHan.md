## Overview
[[LyuKuangLateHan]] is a former [[YuanShang]] subordinate whose defection to [[CaoCao]] becomes the setting for [[YuanTan]]'s credibility-costing seal maneuver.

## Current Profile
In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1061-yuanshao-zhizi-he-shafu-chouren-jiecheng-qinjia-lnsnj6ipiyscw-vopnjjgoxpnt5r|Hanji 1061]], Lyu Kuang and [[GaoXiangLateHan]] leave Yuan Shang for Cao Cao after Yuan Shang withdraws from Pingyuan toward Ye. Yuan Tan then secretly gives them general seals in an attempt to draw them toward himself, revealing to Cao Cao that Yuan Tan is still maneuvering for private advantage.

## Key Characteristics
- Acts as a defector from Yuan Shang to Cao Cao during the Yuan brothers' split.
- Becomes an object of Yuan Tan's private recruitment attempt.
- Exposes the distrust inside Cao Cao's temporary support for Yuan Tan.

## Evidence
- Defection and seal-lure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1061-yuanshao-zhizi-he-shafu-chouren-jiecheng-qinjia-lnsnj6ipiyscw-vopnjjgoxpnt5r|Hanji 1061]] pairs Lyu Kuang with Gao Xiang as Yuan Shang subordinates who defect to Cao and then receive secret seals from Yuan Tan.

## Qualifications
The current evidence treats Lyu Kuang as part of a paired defection episode. It does not yet distinguish his independent conduct from Gao Xiang's.
