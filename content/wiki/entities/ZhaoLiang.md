# 赵良 / Zhao Liang

赵良 / Zhao Liang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-24-shangyang-yue-wo-za-lie-le-lt4yd6pwlezqia5yljg-gankyhut]] as the figure whose advice to [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] is placed in flashback after Shang Yang's death. The episode says [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] moves this exchange out of simple chronology so the reader sees it as a possible warning that went unheeded.

This episode mostly sets up Zhao Liang rather than completing his argument. Shang Yang asks him to compare Shang Yang's own Qin service with [[BailiXi|百里奚]], expecting praise. The host treats that setup as a test of whether a successful minister can still hear a warning about danger, resentment, and exit before the political trap closes.
