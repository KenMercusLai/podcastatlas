# 地质研究所 / 地质调查所

The 地质研究所 and 地质调查所 appear in [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] as one of the four institutional peaks in [[HuangyeShangDeDashi|《荒野上的大师》]]. The episode associates them most strongly with [[DingWenjiang|丁文江]] and the early formation of modern Chinese geology.

The source presents the geology branch as both scientific and national. Field survey, mining sites, rock and mineral analysis, microscopes, maps, and foreign-trained instructors make geology a way of knowing China's territory and resources. The later movement toward paleontology and the [[Zhoukoudian|周口店]] discoveries shows the institution's research range expanding beyond immediate industrial use.

## Key Claims
- The geology institutions embody [[FieldworkAsKnowledgeMethod]] because researchers must walk, sample, measure, and compare.
- They train a new generation of Chinese geologists while mixing traditional scholarly forms with modern scientific tools.
- The episode treats their openness to international expertise as part of [[CosmopolitanChineseScholarship]], not as a loss of Chinese purpose.
- Their paleontology branch leads toward the [[Zhoukoudian|周口店]] Peking Man story.
