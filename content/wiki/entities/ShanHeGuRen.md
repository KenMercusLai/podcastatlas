# 《山河故人》 / Mountains May Depart

[[ShanHeGuRen|《山河故人》]] is a central film reference in [[ep276-duihua-jiazhangke-shang-yige-shidai-zouyuan-yiding-shi-yinwei-lingyige-shidai-zai-kaojin-ni-lqzsj-ujv-gpem-796cvncekdewc]]. [[JiaZhangke|贾樟柯]] discusses both its closing dance by [[ZhaoTao|赵涛]] and the opening disco scene, saying the opening brought the crew to tears because it recalled a familiar youth setting and a song that had been absent for a long time.

The episode frames the closing dance as a case of [[FilmClipRemixAfterlife]]. Jia does not fully explain why viewers love it, but he points to a tension between the music's free feeling, a character who ages without leaving her hometown, and the desire for freedom that never fully arrives.
