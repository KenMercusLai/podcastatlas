## Overview

迷雾 / Mi Wu enters the wiki as the Qiang leader whose conflict with [[FuYuEasternHan|Fu Yu]] and [[ZhangYuEasternHan|Zhang Yu]] creates the immediate prehistory of [[MiTangQiangLeader|Mi Tang]]'s later Shaodang Qiang arc. Hanji 757 shows him defeating Fu Yu at Sandou Valley, later seeking surrender after defeat, and being killed by Zhang Yu at a poisoned banquet.

## Current Profile

Hanji 757 first names Mi Wu as the destination for Qiang and Hu groups after they recognize Fu Yu's attempt to provoke conflict between them. Fu Yu then pursues Mi Wu before the requested commandery troops have assembled. Mi Wu withdraws, waits until Fu Yu's force relaxes at night in Sandou Valley, and attacks, killing Fu Yu and more than eight hundred subordinates.

Mi Wu's second layer is reversal under Zhang Yu. He gathers Qiang forces to attack Jincheng defenses, is defeated in Mucheng Valley, and sends an interpreter as surrender envoy. When he comes to Linqiang with his leaders, Zhang Yu poisons the banquet and kills him along with a reported eight hundred-plus Qiang leaders.

The source makes Mi Wu important less as a complete biography than as a hinge. His death does not end the conflict. His son Mi Tang reconciles former enemies, makes marriage and hostage ties, and gathers a stronger rebel force in the large and small Yu valleys.

## Key Characteristics

- Qiang leader who becomes the rally point after Fu Yu's failed Qiang-Hu manipulation.
- Battlefield victor over Fu Yu through withdrawal, pursuit bait, and night attack.
- Later defeated by Zhang Yu's forces and drawn into surrender contact.
- Killed through banquet poisoning and ambush after arriving with his leaders.
- Father of Mi Tang in the source's rebellion-continuity chain.
