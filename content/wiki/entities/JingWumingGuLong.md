# 荆无命（古龙人物） / Jing Wuming (Gu Long character)

## Overview

荆无命 is [[ShangguanJinhongGuLong|上官金虹]]'s dependent killer in [[SentimentalSwordsmanRuthlessSword|《多情剑客无情剑》]]. Although absent from [[WeaponsRankingGuLong|兵器谱]], the bounded episode places him among its elite through extreme speed, concealed technique, and victory over 郭嵩阳.

## Current Profile

His visible left-handed sword conceals a right-handed capacity, making incomplete observation central to his threat. The hosts generally place him above the fourth-ranked 郭嵩阳 and below [[LiXunhuan|李寻欢]], while treating any exact position as speculative.

His deepest feature is his attachment to Shangguan Jinhong. The relationship combines obedience, worship, rivalry, jealousy, dependence, and possible desire without settling into one family, command, or romantic category. Being unable to match Shangguan's pace becomes a repeated sign of hierarchy, and abandonment after disability reveals how little security the bond provides.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses an exceptionally fast sword style optimized for killing rather than display.
- Conceals right-hand ability behind a known left-hand technique.
- Sits outside the ranking despite defeating a highly ranked opponent.
- Minimizes ordinary attachment and distraction without becoming psychologically independent.
- Organizes identity around Shangguan Jinhong's pace, judgment, and attention.
- Becomes expendable when injury reduces instrumental usefulness.
