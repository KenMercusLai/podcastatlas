# 谷利 / Gu Li (late Han)

## Overview
谷利 / Gu Li enters the wiki as the Wu officer whose practical riding instruction helps [[SunQuan|孙权]] escape across a damaged bridge during the withdrawal from the 215 [[HefeiBattle215|合肥之战]].

## Current Profile
As [[ZhangLiao|张辽]] attacks the withdrawing Wu force near Xiaoyao Ford, Sun Quan reaches a bridge with a gap that his horse cannot cross normally. Gu Li tells him to hold the saddle and loosen the reins, then strikes the horse so it clears the break. The episode gives Gu Li a narrow but consequential role: he converts immediate physical judgment into the ruler's escape before [[HeQiLateHan|贺齐]] receives him on the far side.

## Key Characteristics
- Close-protection figure operating during a collapsing withdrawal.
- Uses specific horsemanship instruction rather than general encouragement.
- Helps preserve command continuity by enabling Sun Quan's escape.

## Evidence
