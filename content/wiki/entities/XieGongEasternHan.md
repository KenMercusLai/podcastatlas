## Overview

谢躬 / Xie Gong (Eastern Han) is a Gengshi-side commander in the Hebei-Yecheng theater whose current wiki role is narrow but now has a pre-removal setup. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-673-diwang-quanmou-shienshu-rang-xiashu-dui-ni-sixintadi-lrmv-gchamvgmui8keducozwshghd|Hanji 673]] shows Xie Gong and [[LiuXiu|刘秀]] already estranged after the Wang Lang campaign, with Liu Xiu using envoys and gifts to relax Xie Gong's suspicion. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-674-ruoguo-wuwaijiao-gengshi-huangdi-liuxuan-you-duo-biequ-lu59astedsljoefnl6yr9d4ilirh|Hanji 674]] then shows him returning to Yecheng after a setback, only for Liu Xiu to send [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]] and [[CenPengEasternHan|岑彭]] to seize the city while Xie Gong is away. He is captured and killed, and his army submits to Liu Xiu. This page is disambiguated from [[XieGong|谢公 / Xie Gong]], the Western Han envoy.

## Current Profile

Hanji 673 places Xie Gong in the unsettled Hebei-Gengshi field before Liu Xiu's formal imperial claim. Xie Gong had fought beside Liu Xiu against Wang Lang, but after the campaign the two hold separate cities around Handan and no longer interact directly. Xie Gong wants to attack Liu Xiu more than once but hesitates because he fears Liu Xiu's strength.

The same source gives the pre-removal lull. Liu Xiu repeatedly sends envoys and gifts to Xie Gong's army, causing Xie Gong to relax and trust the gesture. Xie Gong's wife warns that Liu Xiu and Xie Gong have deep enmity and that the apparent affection should not be trusted.

Hanji 674 then places Xie Gong at Yecheng after a setback. Xie Gong has withdrawn after defeat around Longlü and returns to Yecheng, where his presence still represents a Gengshi-linked military problem inside Liu Xiu's consolidation field.

Liu Xiu's response is delegated and timed. The source says Liu Xiu sends Wu Han and Cen Peng to attack Yecheng while Xie Gong is away; they take the city, and Xie Gong's army is left without a secure base. Xie Gong is later captured and executed, after which the troops submit to Liu Xiu.

The profile therefore centers on command displacement rather than biography. Xie Gong is important because Liu Xiu removes a Gengshi-side foothold by striking the base and absorbing the remaining force.

## Key Characteristics

- Gengshi-side commander tied to Yecheng before Liu Xiu's accession.
- Former Wang Lang campaign ally whose relationship with Liu Xiu has already turned hostile in Hebei.
- Suspicious but vulnerable actor lulled by Liu Xiu's envoys and gifts before the later Yecheng strike.
- Armed presence whose base becomes vulnerable when he is away from the city.
- Target of Liu Xiu's delegated surprise action through Wu Han and Cen Peng.
- Defeated commander whose death clears troops for submission to Liu Xiu.

## Qualifications

This is a two-source bounded profile. Hanji 673 adds the prelude but keeps the exact cause of the Liu Xiu-Xie Gong enmity, the specific city positions around Handan, the number of attempted attacks, and Xie Gong's wife's identity source-scoped. Longlü, Youlai, the exact route back to Yecheng, the full Gengshi command structure, and Xie Gong's earlier career remain source-scoped. The name 谢躬 is not merged with [[XieGong|谢公 / Xie Gong]], because that page refers to a Western Han envoy in a different period and role.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 673 as the pre-removal relationship layer: Xie Gong is already hostile to Liu Xiu, but Liu Xiu's envoys and gifts relax his guard.
- Preserved Hanji 674 as the Yecheng seizure, execution, and army-submission layer.
