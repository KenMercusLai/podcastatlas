## Overview

谢躬 / Xie Gong (Eastern Han) is a Gengshi-side commander in the Yecheng theater whose current wiki role is narrow: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-674-ruoguo-wuwaijiao-gengshi-huangdi-liuxuan-you-duo-biequ-lu59astedsljoefnl6yr9d4ilirh|Hanji 674]] shows him returning to Yecheng after a setback, only for [[LiuXiu|刘秀]] to send [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]] and [[CenPengEasternHan|岑彭]] to seize the city while Xie Gong is away. He is captured and killed, and his army submits to Liu Xiu. This page is disambiguated from [[XieGong|谢公 / Xie Gong]], the Western Han envoy.

## Current Profile

Hanji 674 places Xie Gong in the unsettled Hebei-Gengshi field before Liu Xiu's formal imperial claim. Xie Gong has withdrawn after defeat around Longlü and returns to Yecheng, where his presence still represents a Gengshi-linked military problem inside Liu Xiu's consolidation field.

Liu Xiu's response is delegated and timed. The source says Liu Xiu sends Wu Han and Cen Peng to attack Yecheng while Xie Gong is away; they take the city, and Xie Gong's army is left without a secure base. Xie Gong is later captured and executed, after which the troops submit to Liu Xiu.

The profile therefore centers on command displacement rather than biography. Xie Gong is important because Liu Xiu removes a Gengshi-side foothold by striking the base and absorbing the remaining force.

## Key Characteristics

- Gengshi-side commander tied to Yecheng before Liu Xiu's accession.
- Armed presence whose base becomes vulnerable when he is away from the city.
- Target of Liu Xiu's delegated surprise action through Wu Han and Cen Peng.
- Defeated commander whose death clears troops for submission to Liu Xiu.
