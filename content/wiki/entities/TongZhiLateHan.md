# 僮芝 / Tong Zhi (late Han)

## Overview
僮芝 / Tong Zhi is a Yuzhang-area local actor whose control of Luling exposes the gap between Hua Xin's formal office and actual territorial command.

## Current Profile
The current evidence keeps Tong Zhi source-scoped. He matters because [[TaishiCi|太史慈]] reports him as a control problem for [[HuaXin|华歆]], and Hanji 1039 later shows [[SunFuLateHan|孙辅]] moving into the newly created Luling commandery while Tong Zhi is ill.

## Key Characteristics
- Controls Luling in Taishi Ci's Yuzhang report, marking fragmented authority.
- Exposes Hua Xin's limited practical control despite Hua Xin's commandery title.
- Appears in transcript variants, including the earlier "同志" form normalized here to 僮芝.
- Reappears in Hanji 1039 as an ill local actor during Sun Fu's move toward Da Luling.
