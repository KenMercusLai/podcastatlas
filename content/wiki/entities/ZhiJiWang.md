# 主机网

## Overview
主机网 is the Palm and webOS-centered BBS community that [[LaoMaiSspai|老麦 / Michael]] describes as the predecessor community experience behind [[Sspai|少数派]].

## Current Profile
In the source account, 主机网 begins when readers of 老麦's Palm-related blog posts outgrow a QQ group and want a place where knowledge can persist. A technically capable member builds the BBS overnight, and the name, domain, and early logo emerge from group discussion.

The community later grows beyond Palm into BlackBerry, Android, and iPhone sections, but its strongest identity remains tied to Palm and [[WebOS|webOS]]. When HP abandons webOS hardware, the community loses its platform future, yet it briefly sustains developer energy through donations and a forum-based software store.

## Key Characteristics
- It grew from blog-reader and QQ-group activity rather than a top-down company launch.
- It used BBS persistence to solve the memory problem of chat-based technical support.
- It centered on Palm and webOS while adding boards for other smartphone ecosystems.
- It briefly turned community currency and software distribution into developer support.
- It shows how an enthusiast community can be highly alive while still depending on a fragile upstream platform.
