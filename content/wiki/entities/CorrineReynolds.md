# Corrine Reynolds

Corrine Reynolds appears in [[burning-questions-a-more-fire-prone-world-6a687ea64207b2a60119df8b]] as [[Boston]]'s director of the nighttime economy. The episode presents her role as part mediator, policymaker, regulator, and troubleshooter for late-night activity.

The concrete details make [[NightlifeMayor]] work operational: Reynolds may observe crowd management at 2 a.m., measure noise when residents complain, and advocate for policies that help nighttime businesses survive. The source therefore treats her as a working example of [[NighttimeEconomyGovernance]] rather than only as a symbolic appointment.
