## Overview

马公 / Ma Gong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-626-waren-fenmu-wangmang-jing-ruci-sangxinbingkuang-lotwk6rbtl5pvbrnlfrysu5ol6rz|Hanji 626]] as a Western Han official whose earlier participation in [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]]'s title settlement becomes dangerous after [[WangMang|王莽]] reopens the case. His survival shows a constrained purge outcome shaped by personal ties and public office logic.

## Current Profile

The episode says Ma Gong had served as Guanglu xun in the [[HanAidi|Han Aidi]] period and joined the chancellor and censor in settling Fu Taihou's posthumous title. When Wang Mang later prosecutes people tied to that decision, Ma Gong is exposed. Because he has a close private relationship with Wang Mang, he avoids death or severe ruin, but he becomes fearful and ashamed enough to request surrender of title, income, and seals.

Wang Mang's reply keeps Ma Gong's marquis rank and income while requiring him to return the seals and ribbons of Taishi and Da Situ and go home as a marquis. The profile is therefore neither exoneration nor full purge: Ma Gong loses high office but not noble survival.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han high official implicated by an earlier Fu Taihou title decision.
- Protected in part by personal closeness to Wang Mang.
- Self-accusing petitioner who tries to surrender title, income, and high-office symbols.
- Case of removal from top office without loss of marquis status.

## Qualifications

The source does not settle Ma Gong's exact responsibility for the earlier title decision, the full list of other officials punished, or whether his self-accusation was voluntary conscience, survival strategy, or both. The wiki records the limited outcome as source-backed and keeps inner motive source-scoped.
