# OneWeb

## Overview
OneWeb is the satellite-internet constellation owned by [[Eutelsat]] and presented as Europe's most immediate but incomplete alternative to [[Starlink]].

## Current Profile
OneWeb gives Europe an already deployed communications base that could be expanded for resilience and military use. The episode nevertheless describes a major scale and architecture gap: roughly 600 satellites compared with more than 10,000 for Starlink, with Starlink's lower orbits supporting faster links through smaller, cheaper terminals.

## Key Characteristics
- Existing European-controlled low-earth-orbit satellite-internet system.
- Owned by Eutelsat and considered during concerns about Starlink access in Ukraine.
- Useful foundation for expansion rather than a current one-for-one Starlink replacement.
- Constrained by constellation scale, latency, terminal size, and terminal cost.

## Evidence
- Ownership and strategic role: [[songs-of-disenchantment-the-sorrow-of-nigeria-6275426999307c5f9d5c7073a5b16e0d]] identifies Eutelsat as owner and OneWeb as a possible European alternative.
- Scale and architecture gap: [[songs-of-disenchantment-the-sorrow-of-nigeria-6275426999307c5f9d5c7073a5b16e0d]] compares approximately 600 OneWeb satellites with more than 10,000 Starlink satellites and describes terminal and latency disadvantages.

## Qualifications
Satellite counts, service characteristics, terminal costs, and military suitability are source-dated and can change. Constellation size alone does not establish coverage quality, resilience, capacity, procurement readiness, or operational control.

## What Changed
- Created OneWeb as Europe's existing but incomplete Starlink alternative.
