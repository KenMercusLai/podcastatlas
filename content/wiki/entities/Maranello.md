# Maranello

Maranello is the Ferrari manufacturing home in [[ferrari-1]]. The source says [[EnzoFerrari]] moved operations there during World War II and that modern [[Ferrari]] remains unusually vertically integrated and make-to-order in Maranello.

For the wiki, Maranello is not just a location. It is part of [[MakeToOrderLuxuryManufacturing]] because buyers believe they are purchasing a car tied to a specific factory, craft system, racing history, and place-based identity.
