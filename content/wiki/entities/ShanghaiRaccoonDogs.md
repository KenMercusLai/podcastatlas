## Overview
Shanghai raccoon dogs / 上海貉 are the residential urban wildlife case in [[ep282-bu-wanmei-de-gongcun-dang-yesheng-dongwu-chengwei-chengshi-xin-jumin-lg31-8lajmed4k4mhg5iz7hh8r1a]], where nocturnal animals use old neighborhood structures, garbage, and cat food while residents negotiate concern, curiosity, pet safety, and disease anxiety.

## Current Profile
The source presents Shanghai raccoon dogs as a quieter and more hidden form of urban coexistence than macaques or boars. They live around settlement gaps, artificial rockwork, old residential compounds, household waste, and cat-feeding sites. Their visibility rises when residents see unhealthy fur, skin disease, or signs that cat food is attracting animals beyond its intended target.

## Key Characteristics
- They are nocturnal and therefore difficult to observe directly during ordinary daytime reporting.
- Old residential structures and artificial landscape features can function as shelter.
- Human food systems matter because garbage and cat food become raccoon dog food.
- Cat food is treated as nutritionally inappropriate for raccoon dogs, not a harmless supplement.
- Health signs such as skin disease or matted fur convert curiosity into public-health and pet-safety concern.

## Evidence
- Hidden residential habitat: [[ep282-bu-wanmei-de-gongcun-dang-yesheng-dongwu-chengwei-chengshi-xin-jumin-lg31-8lajmed4k4mhg5iz7hh8r1a]] says raccoon dogs use settlement gaps and artificial rockwork in old neighborhoods.
- Food access: [[ep282-bu-wanmei-de-gongcun-dang-yesheng-dongwu-chengwei-chengshi-xin-jumin-lg31-8lajmed4k4mhg5iz7hh8r1a]] describes them eating garbage and cat food, with cat food framed as unhealthy relative to a more balanced omnivorous diet.
- Health concern: [[ep282-bu-wanmei-de-gongcun-dang-yesheng-dongwu-chengwei-chengshi-xin-jumin-lg31-8lajmed4k4mhg5iz7hh8r1a]] reports observations of skin disease and matted fur that raise resident concerns about transmission to humans or pets.
- Governance complication: [[ep282-bu-wanmei-de-gongcun-dang-yesheng-dongwu-chengwei-chengshi-xin-jumin-lg31-8lajmed4k4mhg5iz7hh8r1a]] notes that bans on feeding wildlife are hard to enforce when the stated action is feeding stray cats.

## Qualifications
The source does not present raccoon dogs as uniformly dangerous. The conflict is lower-key than macaque bites or boar crop damage, and the main evidence is residential observation and expert/reporter interpretation rather than a full epidemiological assessment.
