# Dave Ross

Dave Ross is a New York City-based comedian who appears in [[tech-20260714-tech-pod-128-tech-20260714-tech-pod-128]] as one of the creators of [[FakeAISubwayAds]]. In the [[MarketplaceTech]] episode, he explains the project's method: photograph real AI subway ads, list their recurring phrases and visual habits, then recombine them into fake companies and slogans.

Ross gives the source its most explicit language critique. He calls the repeated AI-ad style "slop voice," while his wife calls it "jargonian," making the episode a concrete example of [[AIMarketingJargon]] and a cultural extension of [[AISlop]].
