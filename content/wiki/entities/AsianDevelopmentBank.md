# Asian Development Bank / 亚洲开发银行

## Overview

The Asian Development Bank is a multilateral development-finance institution represented in this source through former president [[TakehikoNakao|Takehiko Nakao]].

## Current Profile

The episode uses Nakao's ADB background to frame infrastructure lending as both an economic-development tool and a debt-sustainability problem. Transport, electricity, and other infrastructure can prepare conditions for trade and private investment, but projects must be productive enough and borrowers capable enough to service their obligations.

The source does not provide a general institutional history or evaluate the ADB's portfolio. Its role is narrower: it anchors Nakao's experience in cross-border development and his insistence that overseas economic expansion account for government, business, community, and repayment interests.

## Key Characteristics

- Multilateral institution associated with development finance across Asia.
- Connects infrastructure investment with later trade and private commercial activity in the episode's argument.
- Makes project productivity and borrower repayment capacity explicit constraints.
- Supplies development-policy context for Nakao's host-country-benefit framework.
