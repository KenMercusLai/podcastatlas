# 齐宣王 / King Xuan of Qi

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-919-xiang-huode-hao-xian-tikai-shanliang-de-ziji-lrqxfzir4bqxt0v3ccvfef8faade|Hanji 919]] adds Qi Xuan Wang as the negative side of a Mencian garden comparison. His smaller park becomes politically oppressive because ordinary people are excluded and punished for entering, making him a warning case for the [[SharedPleasureGovernanceTest|shared pleasure governance test]] and for [[ClassicCitationLegitimationCapture|classic citation legitimation capture]] when later courtiers cite the passage without the public-access condition.

齐宣王 / King Xuan of Qi is the ruler on whom [[30-mengzi-zhanguo-fenqing-de-chizi-zhixin-592494138]] says [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] placed unusually strong political hope. The episode presents him as vivid and contradictory: ambitious enough to ask about hegemonic models, candid enough to admit personal weaknesses, capable of compassion in the "以羊易牛" story, yet ultimately unable to embody Mencius's political program.

The source uses Qi Xuan Wang to develop [[WangdaoVsBadao|王道 versus 霸道]], [[MencianBenevolentGovernment]], and [[MencianPeopleBasedLegitimacy]]. His failed Yan campaign is also the episode's central case for [[MencianIdealismLimits]]: Mencius's belief that a righteous ruler could improve conquered people's lives runs into army discipline, interstate pressure, and the lack of reliable institutional guarantees.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-29-suqin-lizhi-shuangwen-zhishi-gaibian-mingyun-lrf5cqvoriyep2lkbozju-lyjaxv]] adds him as a chronology-correction node rather than as a developed ruler. The episode says [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] places [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]]'s death and Qi Xuan Wang's accession in 333 BCE, but the host argues this is wrong and that Qi Xuan Wang's first year should be 319 BCE. The source also mentions the chronicle's claim that he recalls [[TianJi|田忌]], while keeping the larger notice under [[QiChronologyDispute|齐威王编年争议]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-37-suqin-liuguo-jituan-ceo-de-bukan-qingshi-fj2kdqlaxzyy2nqtcutktuwrq5fo]] adds another source-scoped Qi Xuan Wang notice but also intensifies the chronology problem. In the episode's 324 BCE frame, Qi Xuan Wang appoints [[SuQin|苏秦]] as guest minister after Su Qin's staged flight from [[YanState|燕国]]. Su Qin then advises palace and garden expansion as [[StrategicLuxurySabotage|奢侈诱导式削弱]]. Because the wiki already records the host's 319 BCE correction, this should remain a [[ChronicleChronologyDrift|编年错位]] example rather than a clean biographical date.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-45-yi-shi-wei-jian-junwang-daodi-gai-bu-gai-sharen-chengxing-lo3cok7cfwzrxesp-i8miz1fc1db]] places the practical succession endpoint in 320 BCE: [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] dies and Tian Bijiang succeeds as Qi Xuan Wang. The source is best read with the existing correction that his first regnal year belongs around 319 BCE, so it reduces the 333 BCE problem rather than creating a new contradiction.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-48-junwang-weisha-buneng-chuanwei-zaixiang-lnwhljnn888mzlj7let2ptn1pcdm]] supplies the pre-Mencius Yan intervention. Qi Xuan Wang tells [[ShiBei|市被]] and the Yan crown prince that restoring ruler-minister and father-son order is justified, then sends [[ZhangZiQiGeneral|张子]] into [[YanState|燕国]] after their attack on [[ZiZhi|子之]] fails. The episode makes his moral language politically double-edged: Qi can present itself as correcting broken [[LiAsPoliticalOrder|礼制政治秩序]], but its killing of [[YanWangKuai|燕王哙]] and Zi Zhi leaves the later occupation needing the people-based test developed in Zhouji 49.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-49-mengzi-yue-wo-jiao-ni-zhiguo-ni-que-ba-wo-dang-huaping-lhy14pbf9thcfdiaulcmf8dwcef8]] develops the Yan-campaign failure directly. Qi Xuan Wang treats the quick conquest of [[YanState|燕国]] as possible heavenly endorsement, but [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] says the test is whether Yan's people are saved from suffering. The episode makes Qi Xuan Wang a negative case for [[CorrectableLeadershipError|犯错能改的领导力]]: after Yan rebels, he is ashamed that he ignored Mencius, but the source emphasizes that shame without corrective action is not enough.

The same episode adds two source-scoped closing notes. It says the chronicle's notice of Qi Xuan Wang's death and [[QiMinWang|田地 / 齐湣王]]'s succession is chronologically wrong by about twelve years, and it briefly places [[ZhongliChun|钟离春 / 钟无艳]] and [[JixiaAcademy|稷下学宫]] in Qi Xuan Wang's wider cultural evaluation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-61-shui-shi-lishishang-daner-zui-fei-de-diwang-lvpoaszanascnlrmu2atinlxzmk1]] supplies the corrected endpoint flagged there. In its 301 BCE frame, Qi Xuan Wang dies and Tian Di succeeds as [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]]. The episode explicitly says the earlier Zhouji 49 death notice should belong more than ten years later, making this page's death branch a clear case of [[ChronicleChronologyDrift|编年错位]] being clarified by a later source.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-50-zhangyi-shi-zenyang-ba-chuhuaiwang-huyou-que-de-loxg3ylhmskvdm-axtco5y-acege]] keeps Qi Xuan Wang alive and politically active in 313 BCE, reinforcing the prior chronology correction. After [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] delays Qin's promised transfer to [[ChuState|楚国]], [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] sends Song Yi to insult Qi Xuan Wang in order to prove the Qi-Chu break is complete. Qi Xuan Wang's anger then pushes [[QiState|齐国]] toward [[QinState|秦国]], making him the offended ally whose reaction completes the trap.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-54-wo-zun-ni-shi-wang-ni-dang-wo-shi-pi-lvhcz0ban9frlmwshd9vch-q7-ie]] continues the same corrected chronology in 311 BCE. Zhang Yi comes east to persuade Qi Xuan Wang after receiving reward from [[YingSi|秦惠文王]], and argues that the confidence behind [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] is obsolete because Qin-Chu ties, Han submission, Wei concessions, and Zhao's Qin-facing move have left Qi exposed. Qi Xuan Wang accepts the proposal, making him a case where [[EncirclementThreatPersuasion|包围威胁式说服]] builds on the prior Qi-Chu rupture.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-55-zhangyi-tongshi-guiren-qinhuiwang-lsclfbwn06fgzooipf5up9sdkedy]] gives Qi Xuan Wang the withdrawal decision in Zhang Yi's final maneuver. Qi attacks [[WeiState|魏国]] after Zhang Yi arrives there, but Zhang Yi has the plan disclosed through a Chu diplomatic channel: the attack is exactly what Zhang Yi and [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] wanted. Qi Xuan Wang withdraws once continuing would validate the trap.
