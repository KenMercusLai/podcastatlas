# 齐宣王 / King Xuan of Qi

齐宣王 / King Xuan of Qi is the ruler on whom [[30-mengzi-zhanguo-fenqing-de-chizi-zhixin-592494138]] says [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] placed unusually strong political hope. The episode presents him as vivid and contradictory: ambitious enough to ask about hegemonic models, candid enough to admit personal weaknesses, capable of compassion in the "以羊易牛" story, yet ultimately unable to embody Mencius's political program.

The source uses Qi Xuan Wang to develop [[WangdaoVsBadao|王道 versus 霸道]], [[MencianBenevolentGovernment]], and [[MencianPeopleBasedLegitimacy]]. His failed Yan campaign is also the episode's central case for [[MencianIdealismLimits]]: Mencius's belief that a righteous ruler could improve conquered people's lives runs into army discipline, interstate pressure, and the lack of reliable institutional guarantees.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-29-suqin-lizhi-shuangwen-zhishi-gaibian-mingyun-lrf5cqvoriyep2lkbozju-lyjaxv]] adds him as a chronology-correction node rather than as a developed ruler. The episode says [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] places [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]]'s death and Qi Xuan Wang's accession in 333 BCE, but the host argues this is wrong and that Qi Xuan Wang's first year should be 319 BCE. The source also mentions the chronicle's claim that he recalls [[TianJi|田忌]], while keeping the larger notice under [[QiChronologyDispute|齐威王编年争议]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-37-suqin-liuguo-jituan-ceo-de-bukan-qingshi-fj2kdqlaxzyy2nqtcutktuwrq5fo]] adds another source-scoped Qi Xuan Wang notice but also intensifies the chronology problem. In the episode's 324 BCE frame, Qi Xuan Wang appoints [[SuQin|苏秦]] as guest minister after Su Qin's staged flight from [[YanState|燕国]]. Su Qin then advises palace and garden expansion as [[StrategicLuxurySabotage|奢侈诱导式削弱]]. Because the wiki already records the host's 319 BCE correction, this should remain a [[ChronicleChronologyDrift|编年错位]] example rather than a clean biographical date.
