# 郑萨 / Zheng Sa (late Han)

[[ZhengSaLateHan|郑萨]] appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-903-weishenme-quan-ni-yuanyuan-xiangbao-heshi-le-lokpt93muc8kasvn44vgsjki8bom|Hanji 903]] as a monitored associate in [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]]'s case against [[LiuKuiBohaiKing|刘亏]]. Wang watches Zheng's contacts with Liu and [[DongTengLateHan|董腾]], then arrests Zheng into the Beisi prison as the case shifts from an unpaid access bargain into a treason accusation.

The episode says [[LianZhongLateHan|廉中]] forges evidence claiming Zheng and others plotted to welcome Liu Kui as emperor. Zheng's page therefore belongs less to ordinary factional networking than to the mechanics of a [[TreasonAccusationTrap|treason accusation trap]], where monitored association becomes accusation material.
