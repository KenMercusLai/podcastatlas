# 年羹尧 / Nian Gengyao

年羹尧 / Nian Gengyao appears in [[11-yongzheng-wangchao-zhi-dayi-juemi-shijingqian-bi-xia-de-qiangu-qi-an-547265513]] as the recently fallen official whose case made [[YueZhongqi|岳钟琪]] especially anxious. Yue had served under him and then occupied a sensitive Sichuan-Shaanxi post after the purge, so [[ZhangXi|张熙]]'s anti-Qing letter could look like a trap or chain of implication.

The episode also links Nian's old case to anti-Qing books and court sensitivity around "逆书." This makes him part of the background through which [[YongzhengEmperor|雍正]] read the Zeng Jing affair: not as an isolated letter, but as another possible sign of literary, official, and regional disloyalty.
