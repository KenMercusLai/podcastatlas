# Olivier Blanchard

## Overview

Olivier Blanchard is an economist presented by [[PlanetMoney|Planet Money]] as an influential analyst of when public debt can remain manageable and when changing rates and confidence create danger.

## Current Profile

The episode revisits Blanchard's 2019 argument that debt rollover can be sustainable when economic growth exceeds the interest rate paid by government. He does not treat that claim as a timeless guarantee: COVID-19, war, inflation, and higher rates changed the relevant conditions. In the French case he describes a danger zone in which weakening investor faith can raise yields and make the feared fiscal deterioration more likely.

## Key Characteristics

- Rejects a universal debt-to-GDP crisis threshold.
- Analyzes debt sustainability through the relationship between growth and interest costs.
- Revises the practical judgment when inflation, rates, and shocks change.
- Treats investor confidence as capable of producing self-reinforcing financing stress.
- Preserves uncertainty about timing rather than predicting an inevitable French default.

## Qualifications

This profile is based on one reported episode rather than Blanchard's full scholarship. The source does not supply a complete French fiscal model, a crisis probability, or a precise intervention threshold, and his decision not to publish a vivid bank-run analogy illustrates the reflexive risk of public crisis forecasts.
