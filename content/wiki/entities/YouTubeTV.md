# YouTube TV

YouTube TV appears in [[the-nfl-1]] as the streaming television service that took over NFL Sunday Ticket distribution. The episode uses the move to show that the [[NationalFootballLeague|NFL]] could carry premium live rights from satellite and cable-era bundles into digital distribution.

In the source's frame, YouTube TV is part of a broader [[LiveSportsStreamingTransition]] alongside [[Amazon]], [[Netflix]], and [[ESPN]]. The NFL does not need to become a streaming platform itself when it can sell scarce live attention to competing distributors.
