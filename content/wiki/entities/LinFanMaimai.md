# 林凡 / Lin Fan

Lin Fan / 林凡 appears in [[no-214-xunzhao-tonglei-xiaohongshu-bilibili-yiji-wuhuabamen-de-naxie-shequ-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-26-1005977305]] as the founder of [[Maimai|脉脉]].
