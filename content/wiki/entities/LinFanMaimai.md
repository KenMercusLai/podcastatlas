# 林凡 / Lin Fan

## Overview
Lin Fan / 林凡 is the founder and CEO of [[Maimai|脉脉]], with earlier experience at Sogou and Dajie in the wiki's source-bounded profile.

## Current Profile
The earlier source establishes Lin as a founder working at the intersection of professional identity, relationship discovery, and workplace community. The newer interview adds his current product and labor-market thesis: Maimai is not merely an “anonymous forum,” but a recruiting, community, and social-network platform whose nickname-facing speech is backed by employer and occupation verification.

Lin interprets workplace anxiety as a signal of real economic and technological pressure rather than a platform-created condition. His AI argument is similarly conditional rather than absolute: specific tasks are automated before whole jobs, and practical substitution depends on an AI system's worst-case reliability, the cost of errors, how finely work can be decomposed, the availability of service data, and the quality of human review.

His career advice joins two forms of capital. Workers should maintain relationships and visible professional reputation, while also learning to use AI and AI-assisted coding. Experience remains valuable only when it becomes judgment, rapid learning, problem diagnosis, and feedback rather than tenure by itself.

## Key Characteristics
- Builds Maimai around professional identity, workplace information, recruiting, and relationship maintenance.
- Frames the platform as a workplace thermometer and pressure-release valve, not as proof that all negative posts are accurate.
- Distinguishes public nickname use from backend identity and employment verification.
- Analyzes AI displacement at the task level through reliability, error tolerance, decomposition, workflow engineering, and data.
- Treats experienced judgment as the ability to select, correct, and give feedback on AI output.
- Predicts that AI-assisted coding and practical AI use will become baseline work capabilities, while keeping that timing source-scoped.

## Qualifications
Lin's claims are founder and practitioner testimony. Maimai metrics, occupational demand changes, executive-role decline, intern compensation, U.S. graduate-employment figures, workflow-accuracy examples, and replacement timelines are not independently established by the source. His positive view of professional networking also aligns with Maimai's business interests, and his claim that AI benefits some experienced workers does not establish protection for all older workers.
