# Dahua Technology / 大华

## Overview
Dahua Technology / 大华 appears in the source as [[ZhuJiangming|朱江明]]'s first major entrepreneurial platform before [[Leapmotor|零跑汽车]], moving from dispatch communications into remote image monitoring and embedded digital video recorders.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Dahua as an origin story for Zhu's later EV method. Dahua is presented as a cash-flow disciplined hardware company that grew from modest dispatch-communications products into security-video equipment, with an embedded DVR breakthrough moving it from engineering projects toward scalable devices. It matters here because it trained Zhu in product creation, technical support, sales contact, chip-related organization, and later management, all of which shaped Leapmotor's full-stack orientation.

## Key Characteristics
- Early Zhu Jiangming venture starting from dispatch communications in the early 1990s.
- Shifted toward remote image monitoring when unmanned electrical substations created video-monitoring demand.
- Built an embedded DVR all-in-one product rather than a PC-based patched-together recorder.
- Grew through rolling profits and source-described limited borrowing rather than heavy early financing.
- Formed Zhu's confidence in hardware systems, chip work, and self-owned technical capability.
