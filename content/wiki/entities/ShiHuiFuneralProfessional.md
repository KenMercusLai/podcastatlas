# 石慧 / Shi Hui (funeral professional)

## Overview
石慧 / Shi Hui is the founder of [[BaidurenBDR|摆渡人 BDR]] and the guest in [[shi-di-fu-shuo-476-shihui-women-rensheng-zuihou-de-yishi-pubian-caoshuai-cangcu-mei-zunyan-lo2evzu5zgam7jjevwovlwxr37ah|史蒂夫说476期]]. She describes moving from executive search into funeral work after encountering rushed, impersonal services and then spending five years learning conventional practice before founding her own organization.

## Current Profile
The source presents Shi as a practitioner joining ceremony design, family inquiry, bereavement support, staff development, death education, and mission-driven business. Her method resists both standardized throughput and novelty for its own sake: the practical question is what the deceased and their relationship network need the farewell to recognize. She also treats emotional responsiveness as a professional capability that requires boundaries and organizational support rather than limitless personal sacrifice.

## Key Characteristics
- Builds funeral practice around the deceased's biography, preferences, and relationships.
- Combines traditional funeral knowledge with selective personalization rather than rejecting tradition wholesale.
- Extends service attention beyond bodily disposition into family grief and recurring remembrance.
- Treats empathy as required professional capacity while acknowledging burnout and turnover.
- Advocates concrete death discussion as a way to clarify values and reduce taboo.
- Links transparent payment to organizational continuity and occupational dignity.

## Qualifications
The profile is based on one interview and has no independent verification of career dates, staffing, turnover, prices, case outcomes, or regulatory history. Her claims about families, psychology, suicide, and the funeral industry are practitioner testimony and should not be generalized into clinical, population-level, or legal conclusions.
