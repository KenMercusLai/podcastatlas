# 永宁古城 / Yongning Ancient City

永宁古城 / Yongning Ancient City enters the wiki through [[ep249-minnan-gucheng-youshenji-chuantong-yishi-beihou-de-xiangtu-wangluo-lpk1vb9-zmgmuous1nyzefgapgww]], a [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] episode on Minnan ritual practice. [[ZhangCongzhi|张从志]] presents Yongning as a Ming-era military guard settlement that became a multi-surname immigrant community, making it different from single-surname or few-surname villages often used to discuss southern lineage society.

The source makes Yongning useful because many layers are compressed into a limited space: temples, ancestral halls, ancestral homes, public ancestral rooms, deity processions, ancestor worship, lineage associations, and local territorial units called "角落." The episode cites claims of more than one hundred surnames and more than forty ancestral halls as source-scoped signs of social density rather than as independently verified demographic totals.

Yongning's [[Chenghuang|城隍]] temple anchors the episode's [[LocalDeityGovernance]] case. As a main city-protection deity with incense money, heritage status, engineering needs, events, and committee seats, the temple becomes both sacred infrastructure and a contested public resource inside the [[MinnanRitualNetwork|Minnan ritual network]].
