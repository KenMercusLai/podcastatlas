# 英姐 / Yingjie (Qingtian migrant entrepreneur)

## Overview
英姐 / Yingjie is the Qingtian-born migrant worker and Spanish neighborhood-bar entrepreneur whose life provides an older-generation parallel to [[XiaoRuiQingtianMigrant|小瑞]] in a [[DuanwenNewsPodcast|端闻]] report.

## Current Profile
The episode says Yingjie experienced parental absence as a child, left primary school at thirteen to work, and later migrated alone to Spain. Over fifteen years in a pork factory she learned Spanish, obtained a driver's licence, repaid debt, and financed a car and home. In 2018 she used €5,000 of her own money plus kin loans to take over a bar, learning operations from customers and later expanding to several venues.

Her success depended on long hours, local social knowledge, and family finance rather than a frictionless entrepreneurial path. She also sent her own daughter back to China after a failed attempt at European schooling and still postpones return while accumulating retirement security. In Xiaorui's transition she acts as employer, business teacher, and practical caregiver, including taking her to hospital when her parents dismissed her illness.

## Key Characteristics
- Carries a multigenerational history of parental migration and childhood separation.
- Built economic mobility through factory work, language acquisition, debt repayment, kin finance, and hospitality entrepreneurship.
- Treats a neighborhood bar as a social business requiring differentiated attention to price, respect, and companionship.
- Offers Xiaorui paid work, operating knowledge, and practical protection outside the immediate parental household.
- Embodies both the asset gains and continuing family costs of the migration strategy.
- Remains represented through one reported family case rather than independently verified business records.
