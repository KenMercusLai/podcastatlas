# 1930 FIFA World Cup

## Overview

The 1930 FIFA World Cup was the first official World Cup, hosted and won by [[Uruguay]] after its Olympic football successes.

## Current Profile

The episode presents the tournament as both the realization of [[JulesRimet]]'s internationalist plan and a South American claim to football status. Uruguay combined sporting credibility, its independence centenary, the new Centenario Stadium, and financial support for visiting teams to secure hosting. European reluctance and difficult sea travel limited participation, while Uruguay's 4-2 final win over [[Argentina]] intensified an already established rivalry and gave a small country global recognition.

## Key Characteristics

- First official edition of the [[FIFAWorldCup|FIFA World Cup]].
- Hosted by Uruguay after its 1924 and 1928 Olympic football victories.
- Tied to Uruguay's independence centenary and construction of the Centenario Stadium.
- Constrained by Atlantic travel time, cost, and weak European participation.
- Included teams transported together on the Conte Verde while Egypt missed the event after travel delays.
- Ended with Uruguay defeating Argentina 4-2 amid celebration and cross-river hostility.
