## Overview
赵丽娜 / Zhao Lina is a former [[ChinaWomensNationalFootballTeam|Chinese women's national team]] goalkeeper whose [[FuyouTiandi]] interview frames football as participation, responsibility, embodied intuition, confidence, and post-retirement public work.

## Current Profile
The current wiki evidence presents Zhao Lina less as a complete career biography than as a reflective athlete narrator. Her path begins with age-seven entry into a football training system, long periods of obedience and limited choice, and a difficult early senior-team stage as a fifth goalkeeper. The turning point in the source is not a single trophy but the feeling of being seen: warming up before a large crowd at the [[FIFAWomensWorldCup|2015 Canada Women's World Cup]] and receiving coach trust before Olympic qualification let her convert football from assigned work into personal desire.

After retirement, Zhao's profile broadens from goalkeeper to public communicator. She accepts labels such as former women footballer, content creator, podcast host, and charity initiator, while trying to help children and athletes find routes into football and expression that are not limited to national-team selection.

## Key Characteristics
- Zhao Lina's football identity is late-claimed: she trained from childhood but describes the national-team and World Cup visibility moment as when she truly felt football's charm.
- Her goalkeeper self-understanding centers on responsibility, rhythm, safe choices, and accepting a less visibly celebrated but stabilizing role.
- She treats confidence as relational and practical, built by coach trust, match feedback, crowd attention, and action after the whistle rather than by private affirmation alone.
- Her post-retirement work turns athletic experience into public communication through [[ShangXiaBanchang|《上下半场》]], charity, and willingness to carry multiple public labels.
- Her youth-football view shifts away from elite extraction: children need football in ordinary school and community life even if they never become professional players.
