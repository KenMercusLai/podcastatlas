# 非常可乐 / Future Cola

## Overview
非常可乐 is [[WahahaGroup|娃哈哈]]'s cola product used by the source to show how channel depth can challenge global brands in lower-tier markets.

## Current Profile
The product launched in 1998 with a national-brand message and a low-price, lower-tier-city/rural route. The episode presents it as a partial victory: it found demand where Coca-Cola and Pepsi were weaker, but its rural image, packaging perception, carbonated-drink category pressure, and delayed sugar-free response limited its long-term ceiling.

## Key Characteristics
- Low-priced cola positioned as a domestic alternative to global cola brands.
- Built around [[LianxiaotiDistribution|联销体]] access to county, township, and rural stores.
- Demonstrates the ceiling of channel-led success when brand upgrade and category trends move on.

## Evidence
- Launch positioning: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] says 非常可乐 launched in 1998 with "中国人自己的可乐" positioning and a 2-yuan price.
- Channel advantage: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] credits the product's lower-tier reach to Wahaha's distributor network.
- Long-term limits: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] says rural-market association, packaging criticism, carbonated-drink weakness, and late sugar-free entry constrained the product.

## Qualifications
Market-share and sales figures in the source are source-scoped. The page should not infer that the product defeated Coca-Cola or Pepsi across all of China.

## What Changed
- Established 非常可乐 as a channel-led domestic cola case.

## Relationships
- [[WahahaGroup|娃哈哈]] - parent company and distributor network.
- [[LianxiaotiDistribution|联销体模式]] - operating system behind its lower-tier reach.
- [[NationalGoodsBranding]] - domestic-cola positioning used against global brands.
- [[ProductLedBrandLongevity]] - long-term ceiling depends on product and category renewal after initial channel success.
