## Overview

Wei Bao / 卫宝 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-you-xinji-de-ren-you-duo-kepa-lnhkk70905eqg4lqwh3mp2sxzamc|Hanji 616]] as a Wei-clan relative of [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]] whom [[WangYuXinPrince|王宇]] contacts during the failed effort to help [[EmpressDowagerWeiPingdi|卫太后]] reach Chang'an.

## Current Profile

The source gives Wei Bao a narrow connector role. Wang Yu uses him to communicate with Wei Taihou before the memorial strategy. After the dog-blood case is exposed, Wei Bao is named among the Wei relatives caught in Wang Mang's punishment, and his daughter survives only because she is married to Liu Cheng, king of Zhongshan, before being demoted and exiled to Hepu.

## Key Characteristics

- Pingdi maternal-clan relative contacted by Wang Yu.
- Messenger or channel into Wei Taihou's side of the case.
- Punished member of the Wei family after the case expands.
- Father of a daughter spared by marriage status but still demoted and exiled.
