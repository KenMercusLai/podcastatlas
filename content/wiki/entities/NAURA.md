# NAURA / 北方华创

NAURA / 北方华创 appears in [[vol-111-guanyu-2025-nian-de-si-ge-caixiang-lgaira5qw1fhic4qwihrimed6q9g]] as the episode's concrete example of Chinese semiconductor-equipment localization. The host says Chinese semiconductor equipment localization rose in 2024 and cites NAURA entering the global top-ten semiconductor-equipment-company conversation as a sign that [[ChinaDivergentTechnologyRoute]] is not only an abstract policy slogan.

The page records the source's investment and industrial-policy framing. In that framing, NAURA matters because equipment capability sits inside the wider [[SemiconductorSupplyChain]]: domestic design or chip demand alone does not solve tooling, materials, process control, yield, packaging, or system-level performance constraints.

## Key Claims
- The episode uses NAURA as evidence that sanctions can push domestic firms to build capability in blocked upstream layers.
- The host does not claim the alternative route is cheaper or higher performing; the point is that it may be more controllable under external restriction.
- NAURA's importance in the source is strategic as much as financial, linking shareholder logic to [[SupplyChainSovereignty]] and industrial security.
