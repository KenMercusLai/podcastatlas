# 陈简 / Chen Jian (Yuan Shu retainer)

## Overview
陈简 / Chen Jian (Yuan Shu retainer) enters the wiki as one of the old subordinates who refuses to shelter [[YuanShu|袁术]] during Yuan Shu's final collapse.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] uses Chen Jian narrowly. After Yuan Shu burns his palace and tries to fall back on old retainers, Chen Jian and [[LeiBoYuanShuRetainer|雷波]] refuse him. The source does not develop Chen Jian as an independent actor; his role is to show that Yuan Shu's former network no longer provides credible refuge.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan Shu-side old subordinate named only in the final-collapse episode.
- Holds or represents a possible refuge after Yuan Shu abandons the palace.
- Refuses Yuan Shu rather than joining a last stand or renewed title project.
- Functions as evidence of Yuan Shu's relationship and authority collapse.

## Evidence
- Refuge refusal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] says Yuan Shu plans to rely on Chen Jian and Lei Bo, but both refuse to receive him.
- Collapse signal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] places the refusal beside soldier desertion and food exhaustion, making it part of the wider breakdown of Yuan Shu's position.
