# 公子喜 / Gongzi Xi

公子喜 / Gongzi Xi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-3-ltca-ikuzo9ej7a0lfrgwv2-wulb]] as [[TianYan|田衍]]'s son. After [[TianQiHuanGong|田武]] kills Tian Yan, the episode says Tian Wu first installs Gongzi Xi as a puppet ruler of [[QiState|齐国]] rather than immediately taking the title himself.

The arrangement does not last. Tian Wu later kills Gongzi Xi and rules directly as the Tian-family Qi Huan Gong, making Gongzi Xi a source-scoped marker for the violent succession layer inside [[TianFamilyReplacementOfQi|田氏代齐]].
