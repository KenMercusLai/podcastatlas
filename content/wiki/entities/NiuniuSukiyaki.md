# Niuniu Sukiyaki / 牛牛寿喜烧

Niuniu Sukiyaki is the restaurant-chain case in [[kafeidou-he-niu-ziyou-cheng-zizhu-canting-maidian-guijia-guanghuan-cong-he-er-lai-1004978054]]. The source describes it as a sukiyaki hot-pot buffet brand that uses tiered套餐 and repeated [[Wagyu|和牛]] naming to make "premium ingredients freely available" feel like a clear consumer proposition.

The episode's business point is that Niuniu is not only selling beef. It is selling [[BuffetBudgetLockIn]] and [[QualityLowPriceReasonedPremium]] at the same time: customers can set the meal's upper price before entering, while higher-priced tiers promise rarer or more expensive ingredients. That makes "expensive but controlled" the center of the experience.

The source also flags an operating constraint. Niuniu's long-term supply chain is described as concentrated in the Yangtze River Delta, so expansion into Beijing can raise cold-chain and long-distance logistics costs. The Beijing queue therefore becomes a test of [[RestaurantSupplyChainLocalization]] and [[ChainRestaurantStandardization]], not only of short-term social-media curiosity.
