# 北京市安定医院 / Beijing Anding Hospital

## Overview
北京市安定医院 / Beijing Anding Hospital is the psychiatric hospital affiliation named for [[JiangTaoPsychiatrist|姜涛]] in [[shidai-zhenghou-yu-anding-cixin-lphaw0qek8ibr0jt44yygou6akql]] and [[shi-di-fu-shuo-466-jiangtao-5wan-jingshenke-yisheng-dui-1yi-jingshen-jibing-huanzhe-lpdsk96akpwtccxyesjdgtw8skbn]]. In this wiki entry, the hospital functions as the institutional context for a clinician discussing psychiatric diagnosis, inpatient care, outpatient pressure, psychotherapy boundaries, adolescent treatment difficulty, and family-facing treatment decisions.

## Current Profile
The sources do not profile Beijing Anding Hospital as an institution in depth. Its durable relevance is as a high-salience Chinese psychiatric-care context that lets the episodes speak from clinical practice rather than only from personal experience.

Through Jiang Tao's comments, the hospital branch connects outpatient assessment, inpatient routines, medication side effects, rehabilitation needs, rural and community outreach, and the missing bridge between hospital and family. The 史蒂夫说 source adds the hospital's career-context role: Jiang Tao describes entering Anding in the 1990s, ward work, senior outpatient work, and the way psychiatric practice functions as a window onto family and social strain.
