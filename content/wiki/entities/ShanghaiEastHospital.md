# 上海市东方医院 / Shanghai East Hospital

## Overview
上海市东方医院 / Shanghai East Hospital is the hospital affiliation given for digestive-endoscopy guest [[LianJingjing|练晶晶]], plastic-surgery guest [[LuoLuPlasticSurgeon|罗路]], and trauma-surgery guest [[ZhangYueTraumaSurgeon|张悦]] in the supplied podcast sources.

## Current Profile
Within the bounded evidence, the hospital is represented through four public-education episodes in three specialties. The digestive-endoscopy episode covers gastroscopy, colonoscopy, other modalities, anesthesia assessment, bowel preparation, biopsy, and follow-up. Two plastic-surgery episodes cover breast conditions and reconstruction, body contouring, wounds, scars, surface tumors, closure, and ear reconstruction. The trauma episode adds emergency shifts, high-energy injury, life-before-limb decisions, staged surgery, consent, conservative first aid, and clinician vulnerability. Together they show cross-specialty medical-literacy work without establishing a complete or current catalog of hospital services.

## Key Characteristics
- Source-stated hospital affiliation for digestive-endoscopy, plastic-surgery, and trauma-surgery clinicians.
- Institutional context for public education about gastrointestinal screening, procedural safety, plastic surgery, reconstruction, trauma decisions, and first aid.
- Associated in the sources with diagnostic and interventional endoscopy and with indication-sensitive reconstructive and appearance care.
- The plastic-surgery sources specifically associate a breast reconstruction group with post-cancer timing, implant, fat, and autologous-tissue decisions.
