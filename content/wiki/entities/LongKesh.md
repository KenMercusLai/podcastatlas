# Long Kesh

Long Kesh appears in [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] as the detention site where internees were held after [[OperationDemetrius]]. The episode describes corrugated iron huts and reports that detainees were beaten or mistreated, allegations initially denied by the army and [[Stormont]].

The source connects Long Kesh to the [[FiveTechniques]] and to the wider legitimacy damage caused by [[InternmentWithoutTrial]]. In the wiki, the site marks how detention infrastructure became part of [[CounterinsurgencyLegitimacyTrap]] rather than a neutral administrative backend.
