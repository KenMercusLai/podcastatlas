## Overview
[[YuanShang|袁尚]] is presented in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1058-shaonian-sunquan-chuzhang-jiangdong-ruhe-yingdui-caocao-fanan-lgqs4-a9qgf21woeh-ppinw4z9jg|汉纪1058]] as one of [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s posthumous successor claimants. His rivalry with [[YuanTan|袁谭]] turns from latent succession competition into open conflict once [[CaoCao|曹操]] eases pressure after the Liyang-Ye campaign sequence.

## Current Profile
The episode frames Yuan Shang through the instability left by Yuan Shao's non-designation. [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] tells Cao Cao that Yuan Tan and Yuan Shang still have enough comparable backing to cooperate when pressured by an outside enemy, but that they will fight if that pressure relaxes.

In the narrated sequence, Yuan Shang and Yuan Tan are defeated around [[LiyangYellowRiverLateHan|黎阳]] and flee toward [[YeLateHan|邺城]]. When Yuan Tan asks Yuan Shang for armor so that he can pursue Cao Cao before Cao crosses the Yellow River, Yuan Shang suspects the request will be turned against him and refuses. The dispute escalates into Yuan Tan attacking Yuan Shang near Ye, losing, and later being besieged after Yuan Shang renews the attack.

## Key Characteristics
- Successor claimant whose position depends on Yuan Shao's unsettled inheritance rather than a clear public settlement.
- Rival of Yuan Tan, with enough force and support to make the conflict strategically meaningful for Cao Cao.
- Suspicious of Yuan Tan's military requests, treating an anti-Cao pursuit proposal as a possible threat to himself.
- Participant in the fraternal conflict that lets Cao Cao exploit Yuan-family fragmentation without immediate direct assault.
