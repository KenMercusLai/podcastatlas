# Zalando

## Overview
Zalando appears in this wiki as the European fashion marketplace where [[FelixHoffman]] saw predictive retail decision-making before founding [[SevenLearnings]].

## Current Profile
The source uses Zalando as a practical exposure point rather than as the main subject. Felix's time there showed him that predictive decision-making could shape retail operations, and that many other companies lacked comparable systems.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as the retail-technology environment that shaped Felix Hoffman's product thesis.
- Demonstrates, in the source's account, that predictive retail decisions were already possible inside advanced operators.
- Connects marketplace operating experience to later SaaS productization for external retailers.
