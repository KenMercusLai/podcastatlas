# 忽左忽右 / Huzuo Huyou

忽左忽右 is [[YangYi|杨一]]'s podcast context in [[149-baiwu-teji-he-liangwei-laomeitiren-mantan-boke-meijie-yiji-shengyin-shengtai-de-weilai-lihbq8vujsqxvb7h2-pz2xm-eqga]]. The source says the show launched in February 2018, at a time when Chinese podcasting still required basic market education and few listeners clearly understood the medium.

In this episode, the show matters less as a content catalog than as early ecosystem evidence. 杨一 uses the show's path to explain how Chinese podcast practitioners moved from high-production public-radio ideals toward a market where personal voice, creator community, enterprise podcasts, brand-podcast services, and offline events all became part of the [[ChinesePodcastEcosystem]].
