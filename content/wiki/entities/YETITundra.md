# YETI Tundra

YETI Tundra is the hard-cooler line that became [[YETI]]'s defining early product, as discussed in [[yeti-ron-and-ryan-seiders-how-two-brothers-turned-a-400-cooler-into-a-2-billion-brand-d7f8e0e8-51a5-4444-8c1d-f5c43c353285]]. The episode presents Tundra as the product where [[RoySeiders]], [[RyanSeiders]], [[IvanBrown]], and mold makers turned lessons from [[IcyTech]] into a more controlled, rugged, premium cooler system.

## Key Points
- Tundra was designed in 2007 and launched in 2008 in multiple sizes.
- Its product details included integrated hinges, anchor points, rubber feet, rope handles, and tougher construction.
- The price range, from the mid-hundreds to several hundred dollars, depended on users believing the durability and ice-retention claims.
- YouTube abuse tests and bear-resistant certification made its physical strength legible beyond the specialty-retail counter.
- The line anchors [[RotomoldedCoolerCategory]] and [[DemonstrationLedProductProof]] inside the YETI story.
