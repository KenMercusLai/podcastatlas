# 《烟雨濛濛》 / Misty Rain

## Overview

[[YanYuMengMeng|《烟雨濛濛》 / Misty Rain]] is an early [[QiongYao|琼瑶]] novel represented here through a retrospective that reads its romance as a family tragedy structured by revenge, paternal authority, injury, and failed attempts to escape inherited grievance.

## Current Profile

Yiping begins from poverty, exclusion, and humiliation after she and her mother are driven from Lu Zhenhua's household. Her plan to use Shuhuan against Rǔping becomes genuine love, but the concealed retaliatory motive later lets Shuhuan answer injury with another injury: he turns to Rǔping, offers marriage, and then withdraws it. Rǔping's death emerges from that reversal together with family collapse, maternal abandonment, and longstanding inferiority.

The episode treats Yiping and Lu Zhenhua as the novel's deeper emotional line. The commanding father first appears as the object and source of revenge, then aging, illness, loneliness, awkward gifts, and small scenes of care reveal vulnerability beneath authority. His lost-love history can explain his repetition of Pingping's features across later wives, but it cannot settle whether the narrative has deepened his violence or partially excused it.

## Key Characteristics

- A revenge plot in which retaliation migrates through romantic and family relationships.
- Melodramatic events supported by accumulated humiliation, expectation, concealment, and reversal.
- A father-daughter arc moving from hatred toward care without erasing prior domination.
- Characters whose moral differences arise partly from unequal safety, injury, and social experience.
- A tragic ending in which no retaliating character receives the restoration they sought.

## Qualifications

The current profile rests on one podcast's retelling and interpretation rather than a fresh textual collation across editions. Plot detail, autobiographical parallels, character responsibility, and the claim that the father-daughter bond is the central relationship remain source-scoped. Explanation of Lu Zhenhua's loss, aging, or vulnerability does not excuse coercion, serial possession, or violence; describing Rǔping as weak does not imply that only forceful women merit narrative attention.

## What Changed

- Established revenge and counter-retaliation as the novel's organizing causal structure.
- Recentered the emotional profile on Yiping and Lu Zhenhua's father-daughter transformation.
- Preserved disagreement over whether tragic backstory produces complexity or mitigation.
