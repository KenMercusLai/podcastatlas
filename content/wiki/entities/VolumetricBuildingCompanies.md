# Volumetric Building Companies

## Overview
Volumetric Building Companies is the modular-construction firm used in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-76efcd195d1b26a4faab0a64b31c1972-52966951-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-76efcd195d1b26a4faab0a64b31c1972-52966951]] to illustrate factory-built apartments and hotels at multi-story scale.

## Current Profile
The company presents volumetric modular construction as a parallel-production system: factories complete rooms while foundations and site work proceed, then deliver substantially finished modules for assembly. Its Croydon towers are offered as evidence that modular production can support tall, architecturally conventional housing when developers, designers, planners, engineers, factories, and site teams align early.

## Key Characteristics
- Manufactures three-dimensional apartment and hotel modules in controlled factories.
- Attributes schedule compression to simultaneous factory and site work.
- Uses digital tools, robotics, and controlled workplaces to change labor conditions and reduce waste.
- Operates within a scaling problem that depends on demand continuity, regulation, logistics, and manufacturing capacity.

## Qualifications
Schedule, completion, emissions, and workforce claims come from one episode summary and partly from an interested company leader. They do not establish lifecycle cost, defect, maintenance, financing, or quality advantages across all project types.
