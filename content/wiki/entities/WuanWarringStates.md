# 武安 / Wuan (Warring States)

武安 / Wuan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-1-lq4wvkd8qx3q7zji6fnnkxi3qcl1]] as a [[ZhaoState|赵国]] target after the [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]]. The episode says [[BaiQi|白起]] orders [[WangHe|王龁]] to attack Wuan and [[Pilao|皮牢]] while [[SimaGeng|司马梗]] operates toward [[TaiyuanWarringStates|太原]] and [[Shangdang|上党郡]].

The source treats Wuan as part of the pressure belt before a possible move on [[Handan|邯郸]]. The place is kept source-scoped here because the episode does not give a separate local history.

- [[WangHe|王龁]] and [[BaiQi|白起]] - attacking Qin commander and higher campaign planner.
- [[Pilao|皮牢]], [[TaiyuanWarringStates|太原]], and [[Shangdang|上党]] - paired post-Changping targets.
- [[QinState|秦国]], [[ZhaoState|赵国]], [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]], and [[Handan|邯郸]] - strategic setting.
