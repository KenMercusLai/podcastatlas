# 郑袖 / Zheng Xiu

郑袖 / Zheng Xiu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-53-qinhuiwang-yong-zhangyi-huan-dipi-lvmo2cwbafcg2gnxliuo18fewimk]] as a favored woman of [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]. She becomes decisive after [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] is imprisoned in [[ChuState|楚国]] and threatened with death.

The source says a Chu favorite connected to Zhang Yi persuades Zheng Xiu that [[QinState|秦国]] may redeem Zhang Yi with [[Shangyong|上庸]]'s six counties and a beautiful woman. The argument turns Zhang Yi's state-level danger into Zheng Xiu's personal status risk: if a Qin woman enters Chu with high honor, Zheng Xiu's position may weaken.

Zheng Xiu then pleads with Chu Huaiwang day and night. She reframes Zhang Yi's earlier deception as service to his own state, warns that killing him could anger Qin, and says she and her children should be moved south to avoid the danger. In the episode, this is the turning point in [[CourtBackchannelSurvival|宫廷后门自救]]: private palace fear becomes the route by which Zhang Yi survives.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-65-wo-chuanyue-qu-gei-langman-de-chuwang-nian-shou-shi-lvyd7pve-tfu4ixsqrbdvhm6g64j]] uses Zheng Xiu retrospectively in the reassessment of [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]. The episode retells the Wei beauty nose story as an earlier example of palace manipulation: Zheng Xiu turns the newcomer's attempt to please the king into evidence of contempt, and Chu Huaiwang responds with mutilating punishment. The episode treats the story as part of the court environment that made Chu Huaiwang vulnerable to filtered perception, not merely as a private jealousy anecdote.
