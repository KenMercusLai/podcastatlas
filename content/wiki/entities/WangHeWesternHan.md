## Overview

王贺 / Wang He enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-463-caicaikan-zai-hanxuandi-yanzhong-shui-shi-zuidade-gongchen-lqblzdg7sdkzln4vxhtnnye92292|Hanji 463]] as [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s grandfather and a former embroidered-uniform censor under [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-379-2-jiemi-jinyiwei-zuzong-shenmi-de-hanchao-xiuyi-shizhe-lj7naddqh_zcdvolio3u7bjvvotf|Hanji 379-2]] backfills the concrete enforcement story behind that family memory: Wang He, rendered there as 王赫, pacifies Wei Commandery disorder without killing, is condemned by [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] for not using punitive terror, and survives only after court pleas.

## Current Profile

The source profile is genealogical, moral, and administrative. Hanji 379-2 supplies the action layer: Wang He is sent as an embroidered-uniform censor to Wei Commandery, but instead of deploying troops, arresting bandits, or punishing local officials, he releases implicated officials and lets local gratitude pull rioters toward surrender. The commandery becomes peaceful without bloodshed, yet Wudi treats the restraint as disobedience to a mandate to "establish威 by killing." Wang He is nearly executed, spared after many ministers plead, and dismissed.

Hanji 463 later remembers that same leniency as Wang-family prehistory. Wang He was ordered to supervise local officials and catch bandits, but unlike harsher officials such as [[BaoShengzhiWesternHan|鲍圣之]], he spared people when he could. He later says he saved more than ten thousand lives and therefore expects his descendants to flourish. The host treats that dismissal as paradoxically protective: losing office removes him from harsher killing politics while leaving a family memory of accumulated merit.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han embroidered-uniform censor in the episode's Wang-family backstory.
- Grandfather of Wang Zhengjun.
- Described as lenient toward people caught in military or public-order enforcement.
- Pacifies Wei Commandery through release, persuasion, and voluntary surrender rather than arrests or executions.
- Dismissed for not carrying out Han Wudi's punitive mandate harshly enough.
- Remembered by the host as a hidden condition for later Wang-family survival and rise.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 463's brief retrospective and Hanji 379-2's Wei Commandery story. It does not reconstruct Wang He's full career, exact title history, the complete legal case, or whether the claimed number of saved lives is literal, conventional, or family-memory rhetoric. Hanji 379-2 renders his name as 王赫; the wiki preserves the existing 王贺 normalization. He is distinct from [[WangHe|王龁]], the Warring States Qin general.
