## Overview

王贺 / Wang He enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-463-caicaikan-zai-hanxuandi-yanzhong-shui-shi-zuidade-gongchen-lqblzdg7sdkzln4vxhtnnye92292|Hanji 463]] as [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s grandfather and a former embroidered-uniform censor under [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]]. The episode uses him as Wang-family prehistory: his lenient handling of people accused of fleeing battle costs him office but is remembered as a life-saving act that he thinks should bless his descendants.

## Current Profile

The source profile is genealogical and moral rather than administrative. Hanji 463 says Wang He was ordered to supervise local officials and catch bandits, but unlike harsher officials such as Bao Shengzhi, he spared people when he could. He is dismissed by [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] for failing to fulfill the mission properly, yet he later says he saved more than ten thousand lives and therefore expects his descendants to flourish. The host treats that dismissal as paradoxically protective: losing office removes him from harsher killing politics while leaving a family memory of accumulated merit.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han embroidered-uniform censor in the episode's Wang-family backstory.
- Grandfather of Wang Zhengjun.
- Described as lenient toward people caught in military or public-order enforcement.
- Dismissed for not carrying out the mission harshly enough.
- Remembered by the host as a hidden condition for later Wang-family survival and rise.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 463's brief retrospective. It does not reconstruct Wang He's full career, exact title history, the full Bao Shengzhi comparison, or whether the claimed number of saved lives is literal, conventional, or family-memory rhetoric. He is distinct from [[WangHe|王龁]], the Warring States Qin general.
