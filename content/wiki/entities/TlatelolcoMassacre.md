# Tlatelolco Massacre / 三文化广场枪击

The Tlatelolco massacre / 三文化广场枪击 appears in [[77-ta-shi-ziyou-de-yegou-ta-shi-falaqi-685710187]] as the 1968 Mexico City student-protest crackdown that [[OrianaFallaci|Oriana Fallaci]] witnessed before the Mexico Olympics. The episode says Fallaci saw children killed, was beaten, shot, and left among bodies because security forces thought she had died.

The source uses the massacre to anchor Fallaci's hatred of organized violence. It is not treated as battlefield fog but as a peacetime killing that appeared to her unusually shameless, cold, and planned. That makes the event a journalism case for [[PoliticalTruthTelling]] and a political case for [[PowerViolenceDistinction]].
