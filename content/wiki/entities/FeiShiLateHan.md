## Overview

费诗 / Fei Shi is a Liu Bei-side envoy represented in the current wiki by his delivery of Guan Yu's appointment and his response to Guan Yu's angry refusal.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1166-ruhe-yu-piqi-da-nengli-qiang-de-ren-xiangchu-lhm17dmeln7dmj1czmeq7omxgtmh|Hanji 1166]] says [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] recommends Fei Shi to deliver [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s appointment of [[GuanYu|关羽]] as 前将军. When Guan Yu objects to ranking alongside [[HuangZhong|黄忠]], Fei Shi reportedly waits until the outburst subsides, restores the ruler-minister and collective-interest frame, and makes clear that he can report the refusal and that Guan Yu may regret it. Guan Yu then accepts.

## Key Characteristics

- Liu Bei-side official selected for a sensitive appointment mission.
- Does not answer Guan Yu's first emotional surge with immediate confrontation.
- Reframes the dispute from personal comparison to political relationship and group responsibility.
- Preserves a consequence boundary by treating refusal as a reportable choice.
- Is presented as succeeding without humiliating the recipient.
