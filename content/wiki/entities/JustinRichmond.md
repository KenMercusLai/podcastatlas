# Justin Richmond

## Overview

Justin Richmond is a journalist, producer, and podcast creator who helped build *Broken Record* and created [[StartedFromTheBottomPodcast]], a show about how successful people of color began their careers and navigated obstacles.

## Current Profile

Richmond describes a nonlinear educational path shaped by explicit racism, low expectations, unequal discipline, and falling confidence. After leaving high school at fourteen, he credits reading *The Autobiography of Malcolm X* with restoring his ambition; community college and transfer to [[UCBerkeley]] then returned him to formal education.

His career account joins journalism to a search for practical guidance. Work at [[NPR]], family financial pressure, a chance meeting with Quincy Jones, and an unsuccessful podcast proposal showed him that talent and access did not automatically supply business knowledge. Black scholars, friends, and professional advisers helped him interpret consequential choices, and he built *Started From the Bottom* to make similar guidance more public.

## Key Characteristics

- Presents progress as repeated loss and recovery of confidence rather than uninterrupted upward mobility.
- Connects racial treatment and low institutional expectations to educational disengagement without treating them as permanent destiny.
- Credits books, community college, Black scholars, friends, and advisers with reopening paths that had become difficult to imagine.
- Uses audio journalism to translate private access to successful people into public learning.
- Treats failed projects as evidence of missing business knowledge and networks, not only insufficient talent or effort.
- Advocates authentic self-presentation while acknowledging impostor feelings and environments that penalize difference.
