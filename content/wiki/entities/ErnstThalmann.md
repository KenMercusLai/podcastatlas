# Ernst Thälmann

## Overview

Ernst Thälmann was the German Communist leader whose Stalinist party expanded during the Depression while treating the Social Democrats as enemies rather than democratic allies against Nazism.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Thälmann as a Hamburg manual laborer, First World War veteran, revolutionary, and loyal follower of Moscow. Communist membership and parliamentary strength grew alongside Nazi mobilization as unemployment, political violence, and distrust of the Weimar order intensified.

The party's hostility toward the Social Democrats drew on the repression of post-1918 revolution and the deadly Bloody May policing of 1929. That history helps explain the split without removing its consequence: communists and Social Democrats did not form a common front even as extremist representation made parliamentary government increasingly unworkable and communist growth deepened middle-class fear.

## Key Characteristics

- Working-class communist leader and presidential candidate.
- Revolutionary Stalinist aligned with Moscow.
- Beneficiary, like the Nazis, of Depression-era political radicalization.
- Participant in a divided left shaped by remembered state and Social Democratic repression.
- Source of bourgeois fear that conservative elites used to rationalize accommodation with Nazism.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to the episode's explanation of Weimar collapse and does not cover Thälmann's full career or later imprisonment and death. Explaining communist hostility to Social Democrats does not make left division the sole cause of Hitler's appointment or equate communist and Nazi objectives and violence.
