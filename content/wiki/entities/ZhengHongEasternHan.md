## Overview

郑洪 / Zheng Hong now has a paired 85-86 CE source profile. Hanji 755 places him as the taiwei on the no-restitution side of the [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]] captive-and-livestock dispute, opposing [[HuanYuEasternHan|Huan Yu]] and [[YuanAnEasternHan|Yuan An]]. Hanji 756 then shows the same dispute escalating into seal-and-ribbon resignation gestures before [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Emperor Zhang]] restores discussion, and it makes Zheng Hong the early anti-[[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] warning voice before the later Dou-power sequence.

## Current Profile

Zheng Hong's first current layer is frontier policy disagreement. After [[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]] forces seize captives and livestock from Northern Xiongnu, [[MengYunEasternHan|Meng Yun]] recommends restitution to protect Han credibility. Zheng Hong and [[FifthLunEasternHan|Fifth Lun]] oppose returning the gains, while Huan Yu and Yuan An support the return. Hanji 755 ends at the point where Zheng Hong and Huan Yu's clash has become intense.

Hanji 756 supplies the resolution of the same conflict. Zheng Hong, Fifth Lun, and Wei Biao surrender seals and ribbons in anger, but Emperor Zhang refuses to let disagreement become official collapse. He orders the ministers back into discussion and later chooses compensated restitution.

The second layer is anti-Dou remonstrance. Zheng Hong repeatedly submits memorials saying that the palace attendant Dou Xian's power is too great. The source treats this as politically risky because Dou is the empress's brother and a descendant of Dou Rong's line, giving him outer-relative status before the later dowager-regency crisis.

The final layer is retaliation and death-facing testimony. Zheng Hong impeaches Dou-linked Zhang Ling and Luoyang magistrate Yang Guang for corruption and brutality, but Yang Guang hears of the memorial through a friendly clerk and reports it to Dou. Dou counteraccuses Zheng Hong of leaking court secrets. Zhangdi strips Zheng Hong's seal and ribbon, then releases him after he submits himself to the tingwei. Zheng Hong asks to retire; when badly ill, he sends a last memorial warning that Dou's evil threatens state survival, and dies before the imperial physician arrives.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior minister whose first layer is a no-restitution stance in the Xiongnu captive-and-livestock dispute.
- Participant in open but heated court conflict that Zhangdi refuses to turn into official rupture.
- Early anti-Dou warning voice before the later He-era Dou Xian crisis.
- Remonstrant willing to attack an outer-relative powerholder despite high retaliation risk.
- Victim of counteraccusation after confidential impeachment material leaks to the target network.
- Death-facing memorialist whose final warning frames Dou Xian as a state-survival danger.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 755 and Hanji 756 and is not a complete Zheng Hong biography. Hanji 755 clarifies his initial no-restitution side of the Xiongnu debate; Hanji 756 supplies the court-speech resolution and the anti-Dou career-defining material. The page treats Zheng Hong as an early warning and remonstrance actor in the Dou Xian field, not as evidence by itself for every later charge against Dou.
