## Overview

郑洪 / Zheng Hong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-756-weishenme-quan-ni-de-rao-ren-chu-qie-rao-ren-ltn4tqzy1d-sodhjidncv8dyqxpj|Hanji 756]] as the Eastern Han taiwei whose anti-[[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] warnings precede the later Dou-power sequence. The episode first places him in a court argument over Northern Xiongnu captives, then makes him the official who repeatedly warns [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Emperor Zhang]] that Dou Xian's influence is already excessive.

## Current Profile

Zheng Hong's first layer is participation in court conflict. He argues with Huan Yu over whether the [[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]] side should return captives and livestock seized from [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]]. The dispute escalates until Zheng Hong, [[FifthLunEasternHan|Fifth Lun]], and Wei Biao surrender seals and ribbons in anger. Emperor Zhang refuses to let disagreement become official collapse and orders the ministers back into discussion.

The second layer is anti-Dou remonstrance. Zheng Hong repeatedly submits memorials saying that the palace attendant Dou Xian's power is too great. The source treats this as politically risky because Dou is the empress's brother and a descendant of Dou Rong's line, giving him outer-relative status before the later dowager-regency crisis.

The final layer is retaliation and death-facing testimony. Zheng Hong impeaches Dou-linked Zhang Ling and Luoyang magistrate Yang Guang for corruption and brutality, but Yang Guang hears of the memorial through a friendly clerk and reports it to Dou. Dou counteraccuses Zheng Hong of leaking court secrets. Zhangdi strips Zheng Hong's seal and ribbon, then releases him after he submits himself to the tingwei. Zheng Hong asks to retire; when badly ill, he sends a last memorial warning that Dou's evil threatens state survival, and dies before the imperial physician arrives.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior minister whose first source layer combines policy dispute, anger, and restored court participation.
- Early anti-Dou warning voice before the later He-era Dou Xian crisis.
- Remonstrant willing to attack an outer-relative powerholder despite high retaliation risk.
- Victim of counteraccusation after confidential impeachment material leaks to the target network.
- Death-facing memorialist whose final warning frames Dou Xian as a state-survival danger.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to one source note and is not a complete Zheng Hong biography. The source's exact official names around Huan Yu, Wei Biao, Zhang Ling, Yang Guang, and the handling clerk remain source-scoped. The page treats Zheng Hong as an early warning and remonstrance actor in the Dou Xian field, not as evidence by itself for every later charge against Dou.
