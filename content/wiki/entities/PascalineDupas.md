# Pascaline Dupas

Pascaline Dupas appears in [[piles-of-cash-and-a-town-of-solutions-in-kenya-nigeria-summer-school]] as the economist who developed a school curriculum about HIV risk after [[CarolNkesa]] described girls' relationships with older men in [[BusiaKenya|Busia]]. The source says the curriculum taught that older men had higher HIV rates and was tested across dozens of schools.

The episode reports that schools receiving the teaching had fewer pregnancies, suggesting less unprotected sex. Dupas's example ties [[RandomizedControlledTrials]] to locally surfaced knowledge, adolescent health, and the policy question of whether [[EvidenceBasedDevelopmentPolicy]] actually changes what governments and schools do.
