## Overview

刘封 / Liu Feng enters the current wiki as the commander Liu Bei sends to assume Meng Da's force and continue the northern advance into Shangyong.

## Current Profile

In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1165-zhangji-zhuan-didiao-caishi-dingji-shengcun-zhihui-lr7x5qltfi_ikktqabowt1garphl|Hanji 1165]], Liu Feng takes control of [[MengDaLateHan|孟达]]'s troops after the Fangling attack. Their combined pressure leads [[ShenDanLateHan|申耽]] to submit at Shangyong, after which Shen Dan retains his office and Liu Feng continues toward the Guanzhong direction.

## Key Characteristics

- Liu Bei-side commander inserted into an ongoing northern operation.
- Receives authority over an existing field force rather than building a separate campaign from the episode's account.
- Helps convert military pressure into Shen Dan's negotiated submission and local continuity.
