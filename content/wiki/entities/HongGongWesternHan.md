## Overview

弘恭 / Hong Gong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-470-xiao-wangzhi-shasi-wode-chule-taijian-haiyou-wode-yufu-lof1c5nctali_zsy0hotqyocjgoe|Hanji 470]] as an early [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]]-era eunuch favorite aligned with [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]] against [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]], [[ZhouKanWesternHan|周堪]], and [[LiuXiang|刘向 / 刘更生]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 470 presents Hong Gong as part of the inner-court group that resists Xiao Wangzhi's restoration and blocks the return of Xiao-aligned officials. When Yuan wants to reuse Zhou Kan and Liu Gengsheng, Hong Gong and Shi Xian press the appointments down to lower posts. When Liu Gengsheng uses disasters to urge that Hong Gong and Shi Xian be removed from court, they push Yuan toward investigation and Liu is again reduced to commoner status.

Hong Gong also participates in the Xiao Wangzhi death case. The source says Hong Gong and Shi Xian understand Xiao's pride and persuade Yuan that arresting Xiao will only teach him a lesson, not cause death. That reading lowers Yuan's resistance to a public arrest operation that Xiao experiences as intolerable humiliation.

## Key Characteristics

- Early Yuan-era eunuch favorite paired with Shi Xian in the Xiao Wangzhi conflict.
- Blocks or downgrades the return of Xiao-aligned officials.
- Treats disaster-framed criticism as a prosecutable factional move.
- Helps turn Xiao Wangzhi's reputation vulnerability into a coercive trap.
