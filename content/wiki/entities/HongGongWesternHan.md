## Overview

弘恭 / Hong Gong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-470-xiao-wangzhi-shasi-wode-chule-taijian-haiyou-wode-yufu-lof1c5nctali_zsy0hotqyocjgoe|Hanji 470]] as an early [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]]-era eunuch favorite aligned with [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]] against [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]], [[ZhouKanWesternHan|周堪]], and [[LiuXiang|刘向 / 刘更生]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-468-yan-duo-bi-shi-xiao-wangzhi-ruhe-yin-zui-chi-da-kui-losjg52ruc7ov6k3uvmxvhxlzvjw|Hanji 468]] adds the initial institutional target: Hong Gong is Zhongshu ling, Shi Xian is puye, and Xiao's memorial argues that Zhongshu should not be run by eunuchs. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-469-weishenme-junzi-dou-buguo-xiaoren-kankan-xiaowangzhi-de-xiachang-lj8iji-c3scj6vznntavm6awolho|Hanji 469]] then adds the procedural trap in which Hong Gong and Shi Xian turn accusations from [[ZhengPengWesternHan|郑朋]] and [[HuaLongWesternHan|华龙]] into Tingwei custody and then a face-saving pardon.

## Current Profile

Hanji 468, Hanji 469, and Hanji 470 present Hong Gong as part of the inner-court group that is first institutionally challenged and then counterattacks Xiao Wangzhi's side. In the setup stage, Hong Gong's Zhongshu role is exactly what Xiao targets. In the first procedural stage, Hong Gong and Shi Xian direct Zheng Peng and Hua Long's accusations, propose Tingwei custody, and later reframe release as imperial pardon. In the second stage, when Yuan wants to reuse Zhou Kan and Liu Gengsheng, Hong Gong and Shi Xian press the appointments down to lower posts. When Liu Gengsheng uses disasters to urge that Hong Gong and Shi Xian be removed from court, they push Yuan toward investigation and Liu is again reduced to commoner status.

Hong Gong also participates in the Xiao Wangzhi death case. The source says Hong Gong and Shi Xian understand Xiao's pride and persuade Yuan that arresting Xiao will only teach him a lesson, not cause death. That reading lowers Yuan's resistance to a public arrest operation that Xiao experiences as intolerable humiliation.

## Key Characteristics

- Early Yuan-era eunuch favorite paired with Shi Xian in the Xiao Wangzhi conflict.
- Holds the Zhongshu ling position targeted by Xiao Wangzhi's anti-eunuch memorial.
- Uses legal-procedure language and pardon framing to make Xiao Wangzhi's release preserve Yuan's face while damaging Xiao.
- Blocks or downgrades the return of Xiao-aligned officials.
- Treats disaster-framed criticism as a prosecutable factional move.
- Helps turn Xiao Wangzhi's reputation vulnerability into a coercive trap.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 470, Hanji 469, and Hanji 468. It does not reconstruct Hong Gong's full offices, biography, relationship to Shi Xian before or after this case, or the precise legal wording of the accusations.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 468's institutional setup: Hong Gong's Zhongshu role is the target of Xiao Wangzhi's anti-eunuch memorial before the later Tingwei and suicide sequences.
