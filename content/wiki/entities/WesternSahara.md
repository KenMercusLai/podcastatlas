# Western Sahara

[[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]] adds Western Sahara as a maritime seizure location in the [[WestAfricaCocaineTransit]] segment. [[OriOgunbiyi]] says a ship off Western Sahara was intercepted carrying nearly 31 tons of cocaine, more than all cocaine seized in West Africa in 2025.

Western Sahara enters the wiki through [[taken-littorally-spains-sudden-crisis-in-ceuta-6a70712c034f16a52ebfaed7]] as the territorial dispute underneath the [[Ceuta]] crisis. The episode says [[Spain]] previously supported a UN-backed referendum, then [[PedroSanchez]] shifted toward [[Morocco]]'s autonomy plan under Moroccan sovereignty.

The source treats the shift as diplomatically costly. It improved Spain's position with Morocco but angered [[Algeria]] and Spanish critics, leaving Spain exposed when it later tried to restore Algerian relations.
