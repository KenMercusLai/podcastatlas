# Eric Zwick

## Overview
Eric Zwick is an economist featured by [[middlegarchs-are-the-new-oligarchs-32587514]] for research linking U.S. private businesses to their owners through tax records.

## Current Profile
In the episode, Zwick is the principal interpreter of a dispersed wealthy-owner class he variously calls “everywhere millionaires,” “Main Street millionaires,” the “stealthy wealthy,” and the “middle-garchy.” His account joins distributional tax research to a qualified political-economy claim: many owners create real business value, but favorable tax rules and local political influence can reinforce their position.

## Key Characteristics
- Tax and inequality researcher using linked administrative data.
- Co-researcher with [[OwenZidar]] and [[DannyYagan]] on wealthy private-business owners.
- Advocate of distinguishing productive entrepreneurship from tax preference and market protection.
- Source for the episode's geographically dispersed business-elite framing.

## Evidence
Research role:
- [[middlegarchs-are-the-new-oligarchs-32587514]] says Zwick, Zidar, Yagan, and Treasury collaborators linked private-business and owner tax records to identify where top incomes came from.

Interpretive role:
- [[middlegarchs-are-the-new-oligarchs-32587514]] attributes the episode's “everywhere millionaires” and “middle-garchy” framing to Zwick and reports his distinction between owner-created value and political advantage.
