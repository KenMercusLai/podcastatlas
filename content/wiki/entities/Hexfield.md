# Hexfield

Hexfield is the image/video AI product discussed by [[Albert]] in [[youhua-shenglv-erfei-peilv-ba-yi-jian-shi-zuodao-lilun-shang-gaiyou-de-yangzi-duitan-lianxu-chuangyezhe-albert-lu0vamaawctwva3qblnsf99esar2]]. The source treats it as a practical example of [[ModelCapabilityPackaging]] rather than dismissing it as a simple model wrapper.

Albert's account emphasizes that users face a large loss between language intent and imagined visuals. Hexfield's value, in the source, is to package model strengths through model aggregation, templates, role consistency, Drag to Video, lighting controls, and presentation choices that make the result feel usable and impressive.

## Key Points
- Hexfield is presented as benefiting from uneven capability across first-tier image and video models: different models are better for different tasks and stages.
- The product opportunity is not only access to models, but helping users express taste, reduce trial cost, and see concrete deliverables.
- Albert says strong content sense matters because demonstrations, materials, and workflow framing can expose model strengths while avoiding weak cases.
