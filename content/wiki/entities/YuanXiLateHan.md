# 袁熙 / Yuan Xi (late Han)

## Overview
袁熙 / Yuan Xi is the Yuan-family figure whose Youzhou base becomes [[YuanShang|袁尚]]'s refuge after the loss of Ye, then collapses into Wuhuan and Liaodong dependence before his death at [[GongsunKangLateHan|公孙康]]'s hands.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1070 presents Yuan Xi as the Youzhou-side Yuan heir whom Yuan Shang reaches after fleeing from [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s pressure. That refuge quickly fails. Jiao Chu and Zhang Nan counterattack Yuan Xi, forcing both Yuan Xi and Yuan Shang to flee toward Liaoxi Wuhuan protection. The episode therefore treats Yuan Xi as part of the Yuan-family retreat from provincial bases into frontier dependence.

Hanji 1079 supplies the endpoint. After Wuhuan is defeated at [[BailangMountainLateHan|White Wolf Mountain]], Yuan Xi and Yuan Shang flee with [[SupuyanLateHan|Supuyan]] to Liaodong. Cao Cao deliberately declines immediate pursuit, and Gongsun Kang kills the refugees once their presence becomes a greater threat to his autonomy than Cao-side accommodation.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan-family heir associated with Youzhou after the loss of Ye.
- Refuge point for Yuan Shang before local subordinates reverse against the Yuan family.
- Politically weak in the current source because his own side can be overturned by Jiao Chu and Zhang Nan.
- Bridge from Yuan-family provincial power to Wuhuan-dependent exile.
- Survivor of the Wuhuan refuge until the White Wolf Mountain defeat removes that protection.
- Killed in Liaodong when the host-refugee relationship loses its common-enemy basis.

## Qualifications
This remains a narrow two-source profile rather than a complete biography. Hanji 1070 uses Yuan Xi mainly as a collapsing refuge position, while Hanji 1079 gives his endpoint through Cao Cao's strategic reading. The latter episode's ambush scene and reported dialogue remain literaryized and source-scoped.

## What Changed
- Added Yuan Xi's flight from White Wolf Mountain to Liaodong and his death under Gongsun Kang.
- Completed the current trajectory from Youzhou base-holder to frontier-dependent refugee.
