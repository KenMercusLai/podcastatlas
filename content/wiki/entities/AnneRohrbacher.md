# Anne Rohrbacher

## Overview

Anne Rohrbacher is an American farmer and co-founder of [[WeekendFarmerCompany]] who helped turn [[TheFarmingGame]] from [[GeorgeRohrbacher]]'s crisis-born idea into a family business.

## Current Profile

[[board-game-season-board-game-season]] presents Anne as the more cautious and pragmatic half of a long partnership. She joins George in the back-to-the-land move, farm expansion, drought and debt crisis, game testing, home assembly, customer service, and the blending of household, farm, and company work.

Her initial doubt about the game does not become simple opposition. She helps adjust its economics, undertake the production gamble, fulfill orders around the birth of their daughter Laura, and sustain a business whose revenue protects Breaks Ranch. The episode uses the couple's contrasting play styles to argue that their partnership, rather than either temperament alone, made persistence possible.

## Key Characteristics

- Shared the move from conventional life into farming and ranch operation.
- Brought caution and operational judgment to a high-risk partnership.
- Helped test the game's difficulty and possibility of success.
- Participated in household assembly, order fulfillment, and company operations.
- Joined farm, family, and business continuity rather than treating them as separate projects.
