## Overview
《信报》 / Hong Kong Economic Journal is the Hong Kong financial newspaper founded in 1973 by [[LinXingzhi|林行止]] and [[LuoYoumei|骆友梅]].

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents 《信报》 as a small, specialist newspaper that grew with Hong Kong's financial and service economy. Its value lay in joining market information to economics, political institutions, public policy, culture, and international affairs in readable Chinese. That made it both an investor publication and a public-knowledge platform for professionals, business operators, and an expanding middle class.

The source also frames the paper as an institutional test of independence. It reportedly withstood a large advertiser-withdrawal threat after criticizing [[LiKaShing|李嘉诚]], while Lin distinguished such pressure from factual error that required apology. The later sale to [[LiTzarKai|李泽楷]] marks a change from founder control to group ownership, though the episode argues that elements of the early editorial style persisted.

## Key Characteristics
- Specialized in financial commentary rather than price and market data alone.
- Linked economics and investment to policy, politics, institutions, and international events.
- Addressed investors, professionals, bankers, and business operators through accessible Chinese.
- Relied on founder vision plus operational, editorial, and contributor collaboration.
- Built public credibility around resistance to political and advertiser alignment.
- Transitioned from founder-led ownership to control by a larger commercial group.

## Qualifications
This profile comes from one retrospective episode, not a complete institutional history. Circulation, advertising value, public-trust rankings, internal disputes, and the continuity of editorial independence after the ownership transfer remain source-scoped claims.
