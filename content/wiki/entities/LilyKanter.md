# Lily Kanter

Lily Kanter is the retail and operations co-founder of [[SerenaAndLily|Serena & Lily]] in [[serena-lily-serena-dugan-and-lily-kanter-they-built-a-20m-brand-then-one-investor-almost-destroyed-it-31a4ddcd-1cc1-48d0-9369-fd85e8714e66]]. The source traces her from accounting and technology roles, including [[Microsoft]], into independent retail through [[MillValleyBaby]] and then into a premium home-brand partnership with [[SerenaDugan]].

Lily's role in the story is cash discipline under growth pressure. She formed the LLC, kept the founders' ownership equal by treating some of her additional contributions as loans, asked retailers for deposits when early orders arrived before inventory, raised a $1.5 million friends-and-family round in 17 days, and later resisted financing terms she believed would block future fundraising.

The episode makes her a source case for [[FounderCashFlowConstraint]], [[StartupGovernance]], and [[BadMoney]]. Lily understood that the company had customer demand, but also that inventory-heavy growth and weak investor fit could turn success into loss of control.

## Key Claims
- Lily's accounting and retail background made her the operational counterweight to Serena's design strengths.
- [[MillValleyBaby]] gave Lily category knowledge about nurseries, kids' rooms, customer taste, and specialty retail limits.
- She sold Mill Valley Baby at the end of 2005 to focus on Serena & Lily's growth.
- She pushed back on both the early private-equity term sheet and the later 2x participating preferred structure.
- She left Serena & Lily at the end of 2015 after beginning a CEO search, citing exhaustion and a desire for more time with her children.
