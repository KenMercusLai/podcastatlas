# Dharmendra Pradhan

Dharmendra Pradhan appears in [[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]] as the Indian education minister whose resignation becomes the concrete win claimed by the [[CockroachMovement]]. The source treats his exit as politically unusual because it is described as a cabinet resignation forced by public pressure during [[NarendraModi]]'s rule.

His role in the wiki is less about personal biography than about accountability in an exam-and-education system. The protest demand for his resignation connects [[NationalEligibilityCumEntranceTest|NEET]] leaks, student pressure, unemployment, and [[EducationAccountabilityProtest]] into one visible target.
