# 王绰 / Wang Chuo

王绰 / Wang Chuo appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-3-lmhj4le2mwu1yhhl-qmuoqkfmhjf]] as a [[WeiState|魏国]] official who flees to [[HanState|韩国]] during the succession conflict between [[LiangHuiWang|魏罃]] and [[GongzhongHuan|公中缓]].

His wiki role is source-scoped. The episode uses his flight less as biography than as an information trigger: a defector can make a neighboring state more confident that civil conflict has made a great power vulnerable.
