# Prince Mochihito

## Overview
Prince Mochihito is the blocked imperial claimant whose anti-Taira appeal helps open the armed revolt that becomes the [[GenpeiWar]].

## Current Profile
[[659-dawn-of-the-samurai-bloodbath-at-the-bridge-part-2-glt3420393571]] presents Mochihito as a prince excluded by [[TairaNoKiyomori]]'s dynastic politics. With [[MinamotoNoYorimasa]], he sends a secret appeal condemning Kiyomori and asking Minamoto lords and Buddhist monasteries to rise against the Taira.

The plan fails quickly. Kiyomori's spies discover the plot, Mochihito flees Kyoto disguised as a woman, gains limited monastic support at Miidera, and is killed after the [[UjiBridgeBattle]].

## Key Characteristics
- Mochihito turns imperial succession grievance into anti-Taira rebellion.
- His appeal matters because it links court legitimacy, Minamoto survivors, and monastery power.
- His coalition fails to assemble enough support before Taira forces catch it.
- His death makes the uprising a defeat, but the signal helps embolden Yoritomo.

## Evidence
- Appeal network: [[659-dawn-of-the-samurai-bloodbath-at-the-bridge-part-2-glt3420393571]] says Mochihito and Yorimasa appeal to Minamoto lords and monasteries, with Yoritomo as a major hope.
- Flight and failure: [[659-dawn-of-the-samurai-bloodbath-at-the-bridge-part-2-glt3420393571]] describes his disguise, Miidera support, movement toward Nara, defeat at Uji, and death.
- Political consequence: [[659-dawn-of-the-samurai-bloodbath-at-the-bridge-part-2-glt3420393571]] links news of Uji to Yoritomo raising the white Minamoto banner.
