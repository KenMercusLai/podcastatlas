# 田单 / Tian Dan

田单 / Tian Dan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-78-qiguo-mingyun-jing-bei-chengguan-gei-jiule-lpt3reyr6ok8g2lhcxrnd5qctyug]] as a [[Linzi|临淄]] market administrator caught in the aftermath of [[YanState|燕国]]'s conquest of [[QiState|齐国]]. The episode uses his low office to explain the title's "城管" joke: Qi's later recovery begins with someone whose authority was administrative and local before it became military.

His first important act is practical. During the flight from Anping, he orders his clan to wrap the ends of cart axles in iron, so their vehicles survive when others break down. When [[JimoWarringStates|即墨]] loses its defending official, residents remember that judgment and choose Tian Dan as commander.

The episode does not yet cover Tian Dan's later fire-ox counterattack. It focuses on the conditions that make that counterattack possible: [[LeYi|乐毅]] is removed, Yan's army loses morale, and [[YanHuiwang|燕惠王]] has been pushed into a decision by Tian Dan's [[CounterintelligenceRumorWedge|counterintelligence rumor wedge]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-79-shishang-yong-gengniu-da-shengzhang-diyi-ren-lsrl0posblloqwugox160xsicp2p]] completes that setup. Tian Dan first stages divine-sign theater through food offerings, birds, and a "spirit teacher," then uses rumor to make [[QiJie|骑劫]] mutilate prisoners and dig ancestral graves. Those acts create [[AtrocityBackfireMobilization|atrocity backfire mobilization]] inside Jimo because surrender now looks humiliating and unsafe.

He then moves from morale to deception. Tian Dan makes Jimo appear ready to surrender, sends wealthy households to bribe Yan officers, and uses the resulting [[DeceptiveSurrenderLull|deceptive surrender lull]] to prepare the [[FireOxNightRaid|fire-ox night raid]]. The attack kills Qi Jie, routs Yan, and turns nearby captured cities back toward [[QiState|齐国]].

After the victory, Tian Dan escorts [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]] from [[JuWarringStates|莒城]] back to [[Linzi|临淄]] and is enfeoffed as Anping Jun. The page therefore now tracks him from low-status local official, to information operator, to restoration general.
