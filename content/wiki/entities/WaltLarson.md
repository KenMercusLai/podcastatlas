# Walt Larson

Walt Larson is discussed in [[yeti-ron-and-ryan-seiders-how-two-brothers-turned-a-400-cooler-into-a-2-billion-brand-d7f8e0e8-51a5-4444-8c1d-f5c43c353285]] as an ICAST contact whose feedback helped [[YETI]] sharpen its customer-education message. His suggested direction, summarized as "wildly stronger, keep ice longer," made the brand's durability and ice-retention promise easier to sell.

## Key Points
- Larson liked the product but warned that customers needed a clearer explanation for why it mattered.
- His feedback helped the brothers move from product heft alone toward a memorable proof and tagline frame.
- The episode treats this as an important step in [[ProductLedWillingnessToPay]] because a premium cooler needed a repeatable explanation at retail.
