# Egypt

[[outsider-chance-ending-sudans-proxy-war-6a75b3695d765781a2b1b132]] adds Egypt to the [[Sudan]] conflict branch. The episode describes Egypt as one of the outside backers of the [[SudaneseArmedForces|SAF]] and also names it as part of the Sudan "quad" with the [[UnitedStates]], [[SaudiArabia]], and the [[UnitedArabEmirates]].

Egypt appears in [[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]] as one of the regional actors pressuring [[Hamas]] to cooperate with the Trump administration's Gaza plan. The source groups Egypt with [[Qatar]] and [[Turkey]] as countries important to Hamas's regional operations.

[[spring-then-fall-a-weakened-muslim-brotherhood-6a7aef1b616564bf00d9b354]] adds Egypt as the central [[MuslimBrotherhood]] reversal case. The episode says [[MohamedMorsi]] became Egypt's first democratically elected president, then [[AbdelFattahAlSisi]]'s coup and repression became the sharpest example of [[PostArabSpringIslamistDecline]].

The page is source-scoped for now. Egypt's role here is not a full regional profile; it is a pressure point in [[GazaDealImplementationGap]], where outside actors can influence Hamas but cannot by themselves solve disarmament, withdrawal, verification, and governance.
