# Egypt

Egypt appears in [[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]] as one of the regional actors pressuring [[Hamas]] to cooperate with the Trump administration's Gaza plan. The source groups Egypt with [[Qatar]] and [[Turkey]] as countries important to Hamas's regional operations.

The page is source-scoped for now. Egypt's role here is not a full regional profile; it is a pressure point in [[GazaDealImplementationGap]], where outside actors can influence Hamas but cannot by themselves solve disarmament, withdrawal, verification, and governance.
