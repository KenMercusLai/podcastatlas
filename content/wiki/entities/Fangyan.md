## Overview

《方言》 / Fangyan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-654-jiemi-xihan-yangxiong-de-chuanqi-rensheng-lq3o-13h2in89qwh0dvchczlvrqk|Hanji 654]] as [[YangXiong|扬雄]]'s long-horizon language work. The episode presents it as China's first work devoted to dialect study, explaining regional word differences, older and newer forms, and language change.

## Current Profile

The source's use of Fangyan is biographical and scholarly rather than textual. It does not analyze many entries from the book; instead, it uses the twenty-seven-year writing process and Yang Xiong's solitary completion of the work to show disciplined, underrecognized scholarship.

Within the episode, Fangyan supports the larger Yang Xiong profile. Yang Xiong is not only a fu writer compared with [[SimaXiangru|司马相如]], but also a scholar whose patient documentation gives him a cultural-history afterlife stronger than his official career.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han language work attributed to Yang Xiong.
- Presented by the episode as China's first dialectological text.
- Explains regional vocabulary differences, historical word variation, and linguistic change.
- Represents twenty-seven years of individual scholarly labor.
- Functions as evidence for Yang Xiong's delayed cultural importance beyond court rank.
