# 山越 / Shanyue

山越 / Shanyue enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1028-xiaobawang-sunce-liwei-chengba-jiangdong-lgur-3fmpr55wy3aji-0rm38y7x|Hanji 1028]] as the mountain-based local population and armed support field inside [[DanyangCommandery|丹阳郡]]. The episode says [[YuanShu|袁术]] tries to use [[ZuLangLateHan|祖郎]] to stir Shanyue resistance against [[SunCe|孙策]], while [[TaishiCi|太史慈]] also gains Shanyue support after moving into the Wuhu and [[JingCountyLateHan|泾县]] mountain zone.

The page keeps Shanyue source-scoped. Hanji 1028 does not give an ethnic or social history of the group; it uses Shanyue as a local-force indicator. For Sun Ce, Jiangdong consolidation requires more than defeating named rivals: he must make mountain and county-level actors believe that resistance is less useful than attachment to his regime.
