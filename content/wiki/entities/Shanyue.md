# 山越 / Shanyue

## Overview
山越 / Shanyue is the wiki's Jiangdong local-force category for mountain-based resistance and support networks that complicate Sun-family consolidation. Hanji 1028 presents Shanyue as a field that [[YuanShu|袁术]], [[ZuLangLateHan|祖郎]], and [[TaishiCi|太史慈]] can draw on; Hanji 1061 shows Shanyue rebellion forcing [[SunQuan|孙权]] to interrupt his attack on [[HuangZu|黄祖]] and consolidate counties directly.

## Current Profile
In the current synthesis, Shanyue functions as a local-force field inside Jiangdong rather than as one court faction. In the Sun Ce branch, Yuan Shu tries to use Zu Lang to stir resistance against [[SunCe|孙策]], while Taishi Ci also gains Shanyue support after moving into the Wuhu and [[JingCountyLateHan|泾县]] mountain zone. The group functions there as an indicator of local armed capacity rather than a fully explained ethnic or social history.

In the Sun Quan branch, Shanyue pressure becomes a direct consolidation problem. Rebellion behind Sun Quan forces him to withdraw from the Huang Zu attack after a water victory. Sun Quan responds by assigning officers into vulnerable counties and by sending [[HeQiLateHan|贺齐]] against larger county rebellions, turning Shanyue pressure into a county-administration and troop-extraction problem.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents mountain and county-level armed populations in Jiangdong rather than a single named court faction.
- Can be activated by outside or rival actors, as Yuan Shu's Zu Lang channel shows.
- Can support individual commanders outside stable Sun-family control, as with Taishi Ci before incorporation.
- Forces Sun Quan to prioritize rear pacification over offensive momentum against Huang Zu.
- Makes Jiangdong consolidation depend on local administration, county officials, reconstruction, and troop selection.

## Qualifications
The wiki evidence uses Shanyue as a local-force and pacification category. It does not yet provide a social, ethnic, or geographic history of Shanyue communities beyond the specific Jiangdong campaign contexts.
