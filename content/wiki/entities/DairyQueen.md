# Dairy Queen

Dairy Queen appears in [[132-xuegao-jianghu-fengtouquan-1-132-1]] as a steadier ice-cream chain comparison to [[HaagenDazs]]. The episode frames DQ's China model around smaller stores, a roughly 20-30 RMB price band, and a buy-and-go format rather than a large premium dessert-shop experience.

[[145-gaijia-zhongzi-de-canyin-yangpinpai-feng-tou-quan-1-145-1]] adds DQ to the [[CFBGroup]] multi-brand restaurant-platform branch. The source treats DQ and [[MeetFresh]] as dessert and happiness assets that can sit under a private-equity-backed operating group rather than only as standalone brands.

## Source Position
- The episode says Dairy Queen has close to 2000 China stores and plans to add about 800 more over the following three years.
- The hosts contrast DQ's relatively stable mid-price position with Haagen-Dazs's more expensive and weaker premium store model.
- DQ is described as globally tied to the [[BerkshireHathaway]] system, while China operation is handled by an authorized operator.
- The source treats DQ as a store-based ice-cream chain, but not the same kind of "fresh made" gelato story as [[YerenXiansheng]].
