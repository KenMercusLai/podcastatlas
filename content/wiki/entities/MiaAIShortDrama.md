# Mia / AI漫剧制作人

## Overview
Mia is the founder of [[JuwubaAIStudio|剧无霸]] and an operator of Mia文化 and 小尾巴影视 in the supplied [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]] episode. Her account connects earlier live-action short-drama and platform experience to roughly one year of AI comic-drama production.

## Current Profile
Mia's operating stance is selective rather than volume-first. She reports that a small share of boutique works creates most company revenue, that domestic one-click experiments mostly lost money, and that falling platform payouts make quality, story, production judgment, and channel diversification more important. She still treats overseas one-click work, advertising, animation, interactive content, and film as experiments rather than settled revenue engines.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines live-action short-drama, rights-operation, platform, and AI-production experience.
- Favors boutique domestic production over competing on the lowest labor cost.
- Uses small specialized teams, reusable visual assets, and multiple models selected by final effect.
- Treats sustainable work rhythm and team stability as production capabilities.
- Describes industry figures and outlooks as practitioner observations rather than universal rules.
