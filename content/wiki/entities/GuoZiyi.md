## Overview
郭子仪 / Guo Ziyi enters the wiki through Hanji 1065 as a comparative example of a high-merit commander who survives by lowering ruler suspicion after major military achievement.

## Current Profile
The source uses Guo Ziyi as a mature model inside [[HighMeritThreatManagement|high merit threat management]]. After the An Lushan Rebellion frame, the episode says he hands over military authority and remains careful about perceived threat in old age. His value in this wiki is source-scoped: he is not yet a full Tang biography page, but a named example for how command surrender, humility, and self-abasement can keep great service from becoming lethal overexposure.

## Key Characteristics
- High-merit military figure used as an example of post-crisis survival.
- Commander whose safety is tied in the source to surrendering active military authority after achievement.
- Comparative model for keeping reputation useful while reducing ruler suspicion.
