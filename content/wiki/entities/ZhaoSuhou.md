# 赵肃侯 / Marquis Su of Zhao

赵肃侯 / Marquis Su of Zhao appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] as the crown prince who succeeds [[ZhaoChenghou|赵成侯]] after the 350 BCE Zhao succession conflict. The source names him after [[GongziXie|公子泄]] fails in the succession struggle and flees to [[HanState|韩国]].

The page is source-scoped. Zhao Suhou matters here because the episode places succession instability beside Qin-Wei diplomacy and Qin's second reform round, keeping [[ZhaoState|赵国]] inside the same unstable early Warring States field.
