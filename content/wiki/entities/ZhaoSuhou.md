# 赵肃侯 / Marquis Su of Zhao

赵肃侯 / Marquis Su of Zhao appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] as the crown prince who succeeds [[ZhaoChenghou|赵成侯]] after the 350 BCE Zhao succession conflict. The source names him after [[GongziXie|公子泄]] fails in the succession struggle and flees to [[HanState|韩国]].

The page is source-scoped. Zhao Suhou matters here because the episode places succession instability beside Qin-Wei diplomacy and Qin's second reform round, keeping [[ZhaoState|赵国]] inside the same unstable early Warring States field.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-18-zhongguo-lishishang-di-yi-li-rentoushui-lgqnequk0rxejqgihwdqxqyqnsvq]] adds an early challenge to Zhao Suhou's rule. [[ZhaoFan|赵范]] attacks [[Handan|邯郸]] in 347 BCE, but the surprise attack fails and Zhao Fan is killed.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-31-hezong-lianheng-weiou-qin-lncpgmxrojc0y1meg9a3ul-o9nih]] makes Zhao Suhou the ruler who gives [[SuQin|苏秦]] practical backing for the [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] project. Su Qin persuades him that [[ZhaoState|赵国]] cannot rely only on its own strength because [[QinState|秦国]] can first break [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]], then turn against Zhao without rear pressure.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-32-shishang-shouwei-lianheguo-mishuzhang-suqin-lgfkk45yq1c0yblequ-d1t7mol3]] brings Su Qin back toward Zhao after the remaining states have accepted. The episode says Su Qin returns north to report to Zhao Suhou with a procession and gifts on a scale comparable to a king's, making Zhao Suhou the ruler to whom the completed six-state work is formally presented.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] reverses that moment quickly. After [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]] helps pull [[QiState|齐国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]] into pressure against [[ZhaoState|赵国]], Zhao Suhou blames Su Qin for failing to hold the [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] arrangement together. Su Qin's departure toward [[YanState|燕国]] makes Zhao Suhou a witness to the alliance's fragility as well as its earlier recipient.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-35-lishishang-diyi-qibing-jiaofu-shi-ta-lnxgnu1czkl-ftfb3gq9kzxwnxdk]] closes Zhao Suhou's reign. In 326 BCE he dies, and the episode says his funeral draws large elite detachments from other major states except Han, suggesting that Zhao's position remains weighty even after hezong's fragility is exposed. His son [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] succeeds him.
