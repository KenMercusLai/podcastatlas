## Overview

张延寿 / Zhang Yanshou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-431-zhichang-bidu-kuli-zhizi-zhanganshi-de-zhichang-zhihui-lmxey8uoliwynimxcnh1khjefenl|Hanji 431]] as [[ZhangAnshiWesternHan|张安世]]'s son, whose appointment becomes part of Zhang Anshi's family-risk management under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]].

## Current Profile

The source does not make Zhang Yanshou an independent protagonist. His significance is relational: Zhang Anshi sees father and son becoming too prominent at court and asks that Yanshou be sent out to local office. Xuan appoints him Beidi taishou, then later recalls him as left cao taipu because Zhang Anshi is old. Yanshou therefore marks a family-positioning device inside the episode's larger lesson about not letting household honor become excessive court visibility.

## Key Characteristics

- Son of [[ZhangAnshiWesternHan|张安世]] in the Western Han court setting.
- Sent outward to Beidi as taishou when Zhang Anshi fears family prominence.
- Later recalled to the capital as left cao taipu because Xuan considers Zhang Anshi's old age.
- Functions mainly as evidence of family de-concentration rather than as an independently profiled official.
