# Odoacer

## Overview

Odoacer was the military ruler who deposed [[RomulusAugustulus|Romulus Augustulus]] in 476, governed Italy without installing another western emperor, and sought legitimacy from the emperor in [[Constantinople]].

## Current Profile

The episode places Odoacer at the center of a qualified political ending. After defeating Orestes, he removed Romulus but did not claim that Roman authority had disappeared. He sent the western imperial insignia east and grounded his position in recognition from Constantinople.

Italy under Odoacer remained Roman in important respects: consuls continued, the Senate sat, and public institutions and titles retained prestige. His rule therefore shows how a western imperial office could end while administrative practice, elite identity, and Roman legitimacy survived under a different political arrangement.

## Key Characteristics

- Military ruler who defeated Orestes and deposed Romulus Augustulus.
- Chose not to maintain a separate western emperor.
- Sent the western imperial insignia to Constantinople.
- Sought legitimacy through continuing eastern Roman authority.
- Governed an Italy whose Roman institutions and prestige did not immediately disappear.

## Qualifications

This is a source-bounded profile rather than a full account of Odoacer's origins, titles, administration, diplomacy, wars, or death. Continued Roman institutions do not mean the western imperial system was unchanged, and Constantinopolitan recognition did not erase Odoacer's independent military control of Italy.
