## Overview
BNB is the token launched with [[Binance]] in 2017 and described by [[ChangpengZhao|CZ]] as both an immediate exchange-utility instrument and a longer-term ecosystem asset.

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents BNB as part financing mechanism, part customer incentive, and part strategic roadmap. Binance sold 60% of the supply in an ICO for roughly $15 million without selling company equity, while the initial holder utility was a 50% trading-fee discount. The white paper also described a future chain and decentralized ecosystem, but this source does not independently evaluate later delivery, token economics, governance, or regulatory status.

## Key Characteristics
- BNB helped finance Binance's launch through a token sale rather than an equity round.
- Its immediate exchange utility was a large trading-fee discount.
- Its founding narrative included a future native chain and decentralized ecosystem.
- Its early buyer base was described by CZ as predominantly Chinese.

## Evidence
- Financing and allocation: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-czs-untold-story-the-rise-fall-and-redemption-of-binances-founder-40056770]] says Binance sold 60% of BNB supply for roughly $15 million to about 20,000 buyers.
- Product utility: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-czs-untold-story-the-rise-fall-and-redemption-of-binances-founder-40056770]] identifies a 50% trading-fee discount as BNB's main immediate use.
- Ecosystem roadmap: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-czs-untold-story-the-rise-fall-and-redemption-of-binances-founder-40056770]] says the white paper also proposed a BNB chain and decentralized ecosystem.

## Qualifications
The allocation, buyer composition, fundraising, and roadmap claims come from CZ's retrospective. The source does not independently audit the ICO, assess later token distribution, or settle whether token financing aligned investor, user, and regulatory interests.
