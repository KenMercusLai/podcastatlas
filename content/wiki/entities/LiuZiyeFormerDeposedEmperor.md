## Overview

宋前废帝刘子业 / Liu Ziye enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-plus-pan-dian-shi-shang-de-hao-se-nv-ren-men-lgsf8_asfrictdmsptsyfizykpon|Hanji 616 plus]] as a teenage Liu-Song ruler whose compressed reign is used to illustrate palace coercion, sexual privilege, and political disorder.

## Current Profile

The episode says Liu Ziye became emperor at fifteen, compelled or invited his sister [[ShanyinPrincessLiuChuyu|刘楚玉]] into a sexual relationship, later granted her thirty male attendants, and was killed before completing two years on the throne. The page preserves the intimate narrative as disputed while retaining the stronger structural point: imperial authority could convert private appetite, sibling hierarchy, and court appointments into coercive palace arrangements.

## Key Characteristics

- Teenage Liu-Song emperor in the episode's account.
- Brother of Shanyin Princess Liu Chuyu.
- Ruler associated with a disputed incest narrative.
- Grants thirty male attendants to Liu Chuyu in the source account.
- Violent short-reign figure killed before two full years in power.

## Qualifications
