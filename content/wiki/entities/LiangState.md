# 梁国 / Liang State

梁国 / Liang State enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-75-3-gudai-de-jingji-zhanzheng-neng-you-duo-kepa-liybgoz8lspypax6dhuke2js0pxf]] as one of the targets in [[GuanZhong|管仲]]'s textile-demand strategy. The episode pairs Liang with [[LuState|鲁国]]: once [[QiState|齐国]] creates strong demand for Lu-style gauze and suppresses its own Qi silk, Lu and Liang rulers encourage people to produce and sell cloth rather than stay with ordinary grain production.

The source does not develop Liang's internal politics or chronology. Its wiki role is therefore source-scoped: Liang is the secondary target that helps show how [[DemandInducedAgrarianDisruption|demand-induced agrarian disruption]] can spread across more than one neighboring state. It should not be conflated with later Liang/Wei usages unless a future source makes that identification explicit.
