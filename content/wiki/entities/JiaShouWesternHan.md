## Overview

贾寿 / Jia Shou is presented as a Western Han palace official whose return from Qi brings [[LuChanWesternHan|吕产]] urgent intelligence about the outside coalition against the Lü family.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-206-zhu-lv-an-liu-shishang-diyi-miemen-an-2-lpsj8exoquanifh7njkrhqszrhkh0|Hanji 206 part 2]] identifies Jia Shou as a langzhongling returning from a mission to Qi. He reportedly rebukes Lü Chan for not withdrawing earlier, reports that [[GuanYing|灌婴]] has aligned with Qi and Chu against the Lü household, and urges immediate action. Because [[CaoKuWesternHan|曹窋]] hears the warning and relays it to [[ChenPing|陈平]] and [[ZhouBo|周勃]], Jia Shou's report also accelerates the opposing coalition's seizure of the Northern Army.

## Key Characteristics

- Returning envoy whose report links the Qi front to the Chang'an crisis.
- Adviser who urges Lü Chan to act after warning that outside forces have aligned against the Lü family.
- Unintentional source of intelligence for the anti-Lü ministers because Cao Ku overhears his warning.

This one-source profile does not establish Jia Shou's wider biography, mission instructions, exact office, independent knowledge, loyalty, dialogue, timing, or later fate. The coalition report and its causal effect remain episode-attributed.
