# Niels Bohr

## Overview

Niels Bohr appears as the Danish physicist who escaped occupation, advocated Swedish refuge for Danish Jews, joined the Allied atomic effort, warned that Germany was pursuing a bomb, and argued that atomic knowledge ultimately required international openness rather than permanent national monopoly.

## Current Profile

Across the episodes, Bohr joins scientific stature to cross-border political intervention. After escaping occupied [[Denmark]], he is associated with efforts to secure [[Sweden]]'s public willingness to receive Danish Jewish refugees, making him one link in the [[RescueOfTheDanishJews]].

His information about [[NaziGermany|Nazi Germany]] also reinforces the wartime atomic race, while his internationalism anticipates the postwar problem: a secret powerful enough to reorder world politics is unlikely to remain contained within one state.

[[JRobertOppenheimer|Oppenheimer]] was receptive to that vision, whereas Churchill is described as hostile to it. Bohr therefore functions as an early bridge between the [[ManhattanProject|Manhattan Project]]'s military necessity and [[AtomicKnowledgeInternationalControl|international control of atomic knowledge]].

## Key Characteristics

- Refugee physicist whose escape from occupied Denmark preceded both rescue advocacy and Allied scientific work.
- Advocate associated with securing Swedish admission for Danish Jewish refugees.
- Source of warning that Heisenberg and Germany were working on an atomic bomb.
- Advocate of eventual openness and international governance for atomic knowledge.
- Influence on Oppenheimer's belief that scientific power required a wider political settlement.

## Evidence

- Danish rescue branch: [[284-denmark-the-great-escape-glt4809968647]] says Bohr escaped to Sweden and pressed for an assurance that Danish Jews would be admitted.
- Wartime movement: [[343-oppenheimer-the-father-of-the-atom-bomb-part-1-glt9202576882]] traces Bohr from Denmark through Britain to Los Alamos.
- Threat information: [[343-oppenheimer-the-father-of-the-atom-bomb-part-1-glt9202576882]] attributes to him the warning that Heisenberg was working on a Nazi bomb.
- Internationalist proposal: [[343-oppenheimer-the-father-of-the-atom-bomb-part-1-glt9202576882]] presents him arguing that atomic knowledge should ultimately be shared to support international control.

## Qualifications

The episodes compress Bohr's scientific work, escape, rescue advocacy, wartime diplomacy, and atomic proposals into short narratives. His exact role in Sweden's admission decision, the Greta Garbo and King Gustav V contact chain, his recommendations to political leaders, and his influence on atomic policy require documentary treatment beyond these sources.
