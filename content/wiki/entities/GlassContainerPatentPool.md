# Glass-Container Patent Pool

The glass-container patent pool is [[the-invention-invention]]'s abuse case for [[PatentPoolCartelRisk]]. The source says a pool organized around glass-container machinery, including a machine called a gob feeder, involved four companies and controlled more than 94% of U.S. glass containers.

The U.S. government sued, arguing that the pool restricted output, fixed prices, and blocked competitors. The episode says the 1945 [[SupremeCourt|Supreme Court]] decision helped establish that patent-pool licensing needed to be fair, reasonable, and nondiscriminatory, later summarized as [[FRANDLicensing|FRAND]].
