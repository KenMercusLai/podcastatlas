# 阴丽华 / Yin Lihua

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-915-shishang-meiyou-zhenzheng-de-kouwu-zhehua-dui-ma-lgxcodjxnaozvyegxqurgzji5gus|Hanji 915]] mentions 阴丽华 / Yin Lihua through a 33 CE hostage precedent. The episode says her mother and brother are taken by hostage-takers during [[LiuXiu|刘秀]]'s reign, and the government still attacks rather than paying or negotiating, after which both hostages are killed.

The page is source-scoped because this ingest uses Yin Lihua mainly as part of the [[HostageRansomDeterrence|hostage-ransom deterrence]] comparison with [[QiaoXuanLateHan|桥玄]]. Later sources can expand her broader Eastern Han role.
