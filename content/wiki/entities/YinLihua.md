## Overview

阴丽华 / Yin Lihua is now synthesized from two source-bounded roles. Hanji 915 preserves her family as a Guangwu-era hostage precedent, while Hanji 737 gives her broader Eastern Han imperial-memory profile: [[LiuXiu|刘秀]]'s original consort, a long-restrained palace figure, [[EmperorMingOfHan|汉明帝]]'s mother, and the empress whose 光烈 title becomes the source's founding case for [[ImperialConsortPosthumousTitleSystem|皇后谥号制度]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 737 gives the main current profile. The episode presents Yin as Liu Xiu's beloved original wife who yields the empress position, lives through seventeen years of restraint, and is later remembered as the mother of the empire. In 64 CE / Yongping 7, Empress Dowager Yin dies and is buried; her posthumous title 光烈 is explained as joining Liu Xiu's 光武 "光" with her own evaluative "烈." The source treats this as a major precedent for later empress posthumous titles.

Hanji 915 supplies a narrower earlier-chronology reference. It says that in 33 CE, Yin Lihua's mother and brother are seized by hostage-takers during Liu Xiu's reign. The government attacks rather than paying or negotiating, and both hostages are killed. That branch matters mainly as evidence for a harsh [[HostageRansomDeterrence|hostage-ransom deterrence]] policy.

The combined profile therefore separates three layers: Yin as consort and empress dowager in dynastic memory, Yin's family as a hostage-deterrence precedent, and the host's interpretive lesson about restraint and "showing weakness," which remains source-scoped commentary rather than settled institutional analysis.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han empress dowager remembered in the current sources through both dynastic ritual and hostage-precedent contexts.
- Liu Xiu's original consort and Mingdi's mother in Hanji 737's narrative of restrained palace survival.
- 光烈皇后 figure whose posthumous title is treated as a precedent for later empress title formation.
- Family member in Hanji 915's 33 CE hostage case, where state deterrence overrides ransom or negotiation.
- Source-scoped example of the host's gendered interpretation of restraint, not a page for a full independent biography.

## Qualifications

The page remains bounded to the two listed source notes. Hanji 737's claim that Yin "showed weakness" as a form of strength is preserved as host interpretation, not as a settled historical theory of her agency. Hanji 915's hostage branch concerns her family and Liu Xiu's policy precedent more than Yin's own conduct.
