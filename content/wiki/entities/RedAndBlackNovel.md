# 《红与黑》 / The Red and the Black

《红与黑》 is the [[Stendhal|Stendhal / 斯汤达]] novel discussed in [[21-hong-yu-hei-ta-si-yu-zhencheng-569042001]]. The episode follows [[JulienSorel|Julien Sorel / 于连]] from a sawmill family through tutoring, seminary life, Paris service under a marquis, prison, trial, and execution, while treating the plot as a carrier for [[PsychologicalRealism]] rather than as the main achievement.

The source's central reading is that the novel should not be reduced to a scandal story about seduction and ambition. Julien's movement through [[FrenchRestoration|Restoration France]] shows [[ClassMobilityPerformance]] under a world where military glory has receded, clerical performance becomes an available route, and aristocratic circles reward masks they also despise.

The title's red and black are read through several layers: military uniform and clerical robe, blood and control, passion and death, fantasy and restraint, and the emotional contrast between [[MadameDeRenal|Madame de Renal]] and [[MathildeDeLaMole|Mathilde de La Mole]]. The episode's final emphasis is that Julien dies not only from failed ambition but from the inability to make himself entirely false.
