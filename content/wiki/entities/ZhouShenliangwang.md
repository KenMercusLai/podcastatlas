# 周慎靓王 / Zhou Shenliang Wang

周慎靓王 / Zhou Shenliang Wang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-40-5-yue-chusheng-de-haizi-re-shui-la-ll9e-thidxl5cqoacfrbz-ygw8-t]] as the son and successor of [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]] after Zhou Xian Wang dies in 321 BCE. The source file renders the title as 周慎亮王; this page keeps that spelling visible while normalizing the page title to the common 周慎靓王 form.

The episode does not develop his reign. His wiki role is to mark the closing handoff after Zhou Xian Wang's forty-eight-year annalistic frame, before the source turns to Yan succession and the Qi/Tian-family story.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-45-yi-shi-wei-jian-junwang-daodi-gai-bu-gai-sharen-chengxing-lo3cok7cfwzrxesp-i8miz1fc1db]] makes his reign the first half of the 周纪三 frame. The episode says 周纪三 covers 320-298 BCE, combining Zhou Shenliang Wang's six years with the first seventeen years of [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-48-junwang-weisha-buneng-chuanwei-zaixiang-lnwhljnn888mzlj7let2ptn1pcdm]] gives his endpoint. In 315 BCE Zhou Shenliang Wang dies, and [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]] succeeds, moving the chronicle into the last Zhou Son of Heaven's long annalistic frame.
