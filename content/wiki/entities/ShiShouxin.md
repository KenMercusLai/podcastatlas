# 石守信 / Shi Shouxin

Shi Shouxin / 石守信 is one of [[ZhaoKuangyin|赵匡胤]]'s close military associates in [[169-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-shang-957052909]]. The episode places him among the禁军 commanders whose relationship with Zhao Kuangyin made [[ChenQiaoMutiny|陈桥兵变]] operationally possible.

His role has two sides. During Chenqiao, the source says Shi Shouxin already controlled the capital gate, helping Zhao Kuangyin's army enter [[Kaifeng|开封]]. After the founding, he becomes one of the generals associated with [[CupWineDisarmsGenerals|杯酒释兵权]], where former coup partners are converted from military threats into wealthy, honored, and kinship-linked elites.
