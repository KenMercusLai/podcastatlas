## Overview

吴阳 / Wu Yang is the Yue marquis whom Hanji 356-2 presents as defecting from [[YuShanDongyueKing|余善]] to the Han side with seven hundred followers.

## Current Profile

Wu Yang's durable role is that of an early internal defector during the [[DongyueWesternHan|东越]] campaign. His move does not by itself end the war, but it demonstrates that Han pressure has begun to separate local elites and followers from Yu Shan before Ao and Jugu carry out the final killing and surrender.

## Key Characteristics

- Yue local elite or marquis in the Dongyue campaign.
- Leader said to bring seven hundred followers to the Han side.
- Early signal of political fracture around Yu Shan.
