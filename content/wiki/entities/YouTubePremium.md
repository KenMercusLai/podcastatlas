# YouTube Premium

YouTube Premium appears in [[tech-20260731-0731-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260731-0731-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the subscription surface where [[NBCUniversal]]'s [[PeacockStreaming|Peacock]] content will be available. The episode says subscribers will be able to watch franchises, films, and sports content inside [[YouTube]] rather than leaving for a separate Peacock app.

The page's wiki role is product-context rather than a full YouTube business history. It makes [[StreamingPlatformBundling]] concrete: YouTube Premium can absorb legacy media content into the platform's existing subscription and viewing interface.
