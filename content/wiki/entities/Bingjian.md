# 《冰鉴》 / Bingjian

《冰鉴》 / Bingjian is the conventional page for the physiognomy/person-reading text discussed in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-135-fanwaipian-3-jiao-ni-xiangmian-kanren-xiaojiqiao-ruhe-yi-mao-kantou-mingyun-lpupsu8fduwdwbdg8xq8gjxegztu|Qinji 135 番外篇3]]. The source transcript renders the title as 《兵剑》, so this page preserves the uncertainty while using the recognized Bingjian key for the attributed Zeng Guofan person-reading tradition.

In the episode, the book matters less as a verified authored work than as a container for 相人术. The host says it is not necessarily written by [[ZengGuofan|曾国藩]], but Zeng's reputation for judging people made the attribution commercially and culturally useful. The text's method is presented through exterior signs: spirit, bones, facial features, affect, bearing, and speech.

The wiki records Bingjian as a source-scoped method object, not as proof that physiognomy works. Its value in this ingest is to connect older fate-reading stories around [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负]] with a later personnel-evaluation frame: [[ShenGuPhysiognomy|神骨相法]], language-order judgment, and the caution preserved by [[PhysiognomyMoralAppearanceFallacy|以貌定德谬误]].
