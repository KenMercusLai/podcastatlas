# 赖靖平 / Lai Jingping

赖靖平 / Lai Jingping is the Chinese American founder of [[Wuxiaworld|武侠世界]] named in [[ep240-ba-zong-tian-chong-zai-haiwai-duanju-chuhai-de-chanye-mima-ltvxvfj5ko6djhsjatiphdy-dzek]]. The episode says he started the site in 2014 as a translation outlet for Chinese web fiction, which later became a broader translator community.

His source role is to anchor the fan-translation stage of Chinese web-fiction globalization. EP240 treats that stage as imperfect and often unauthorized, but important because it proved overseas reader demand before formal platforms such as [[Webnovel|起点国际版]] scaled author cultivation and commercialization.
