# Carlos (Startup Embassy)

## Overview
Carlos is the Startup Embassy operator in [[KenjiYamamoto]]'s account of Silicon Valley hacker houses.

## Current Profile
Carlos functions in the wiki as the operator example for mission-driven hacker-house gatekeeping. He is presented as welcoming Yamamoto into [[StartupEmbassy]], trying to build hacker houses into a startup, and protecting the house's startup-dreamer identity even when a more conventional rental strategy might have made the business easier.

## Key Characteristics
- Welcoming house operator who offered Yamamoto food and lodging access.
- Treated Startup Embassy as a community with a startup mission rather than only a bed-rental business.
- Tried repeatedly to open new hacker houses, including a hacker-hotel idea in San Francisco.
- Resisted renting to [[Facebook]] and [[Google]] employees because they did not match his intended founder-resident profile.
- Embodies the episode's "purity to a fault" tension: mission clarity created identity but may have weakened financial resilience.
