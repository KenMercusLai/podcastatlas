# 李暹 / Li Xian (late Han)

李暹 / Li Xian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-984-liangzhouxi-junfa-neidou-ta-weihe-di-yige-daoxia-lurb2qfzsa7e-owh-dl-m-sccze5|Hanji 984]] as [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]]'s nephew and the operative who physically moves [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] from the palace to Li Jue's camp. The page uses `LiXianLateHan` to keep the public route distinct from other romanized Li Xian names.

In the source, [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] plans to abduct the emperor to his own camp. After someone tells Li Jue, Li Jue preempts on 195-03-25 by sending Li Xian with several thousand soldiers to surround the palace. Li Xian places the emperor in three carts and moves him to Li Jue's camp.

[[YangBiao|杨彪]] objects that the emperor should not live in a subject's house, but Li Xian answers that the general has already made the decision. Ministers follow on foot. The episode uses the scene to show [[ProtectorCaptorAmbiguity|护驾与挟持模糊性]] before Hanji 985's later hostage symmetry: Li Jue's side can present the move as preempting Guo Si, but the practical result is forced imperial relocation.
