# 范县 / Fan County (Late Han)

范县 / Fan County enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-978-gushen-quantui-shuwan-dijun-ta-zenme-zuodao-de-lhgd8vsvuaf5oi9aefh0wxabmzwv|Hanji 978]] as one of the three remaining Cao-side strongholds in [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]], alongside [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]] and [[DongALateHan|东阿]]. The source places it under immediate pressure because [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s side has seized [[JinYunLateHan|靳允]]'s family and sends [[FanYiLateHan|范仪]] into the county.

Its source role is the county-level counterpart to Juancheng's morale defense. [[ChengYu|程昱]] passes through Fan County, persuades Jin Yun to hold for [[CaoCao|曹操]], and Jin Yun kills Fan Yi by ambush after Fan Yi enters. Fan County therefore becomes a small but necessary piece of Cao Cao's remaining base before the Hanji 979 Dong'e and Cangting defense.
