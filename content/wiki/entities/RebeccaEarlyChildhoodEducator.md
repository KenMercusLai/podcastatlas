## Current Profile

The episodes describe Rebecca as a sociology graduate who worked at a Hong Kong welfare organization serving migrant domestic workers and their children, then studied early-childhood education and special education at Columbia University Teachers College. Her examples span developmental support, preschool adjustment, emotion recognition, curriculum design, safety, play, feedback, and communication among teachers, parents, and grandparents.

Her recurring educational position combines respect and structure: adults should explain what a child can do, acknowledge emotion, keep safety boundaries legible, observe individual response, and avoid treating one outcome or one imperfect adult choice as destiny.
