# Jamaica

[[Jamaica]] is the source's sand-theft and expert-context case. [[DamianKing]] of the [[CaribbeanPolicyResearchInstitute]] explains [[SmallOpenEconomyVulnerability]], property rights, and tourism dependence from Jamaica, while the second half of [[sand-heists-and-property-rights-in-the-caribbean-summer-school]] revisits a stolen-beach case in northern Jamaica.

The episode uses Jamaica to make [[SandScarcity]] visible. [[MichaelHilton]] investigated a 2008 case in which an entire beach was allegedly removed by truck; the source then links that local theft to resort demand, forensic uncertainty, witness intimidation, and the broader [[IllegalSandMining]] problem.
