# Irving Fisher / 欧文·费雪

Irving Fisher / 欧文·费雪 is cited in [[162-caifu-de-benzhi-yiji-ziyou-de-zhenzheng-hanyi-chuantai-shifen-xiyin-lofyxfhiprud7zvekufexsjhtust]] for a modern finance-adjacent view of wealth as rights and future cash flows rather than only physical stockpiles or money. The episode groups Fisher with [[AdamSmith|Adam Smith]] and [[DavidRicardo|David Ricardo]] to show how economic definitions of wealth move toward welfare, property claims, and income-producing capacity.

The source uses Fisher as a bridge into investing. If wealth includes future cash flow and recognized rights, then [[ProfitAndCashFlowQuality]], [[AssetAllocation]], and [[ClientCenteredWealthManagement]] become more important than simply naming an asset class.
