# William Maitland of Lethington

## Overview

William Maitland of Lethington is the Scottish secretary and diplomat whom the episode places in both the political discussion of removing [[HenryStuartLordDarnley|Darnley]] and the later murder conspiracy.

## Current Profile

Maitland helped blacken [[DavidRizzio|Rizzio]]'s reputation and joined the conspiracy linking [[HenryStuartLordDarnley|Darnley]] to the exiles defeated in the [[ChaseaboutRaid]]. After the murder and Mary's escape, he was dismissed and placed under house arrest, but [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary]] later restored him because she needed his diplomatic ability.

His reconciliation with [[JamesHepburnEarlOfBothwell|Bothwell]] joined previously opposed nobles around the Darnley problem. At Craigmillar, Maitland promoted divorce while using language that suggested the lords could find a way to free Mary without dishonoring her.

The episode then places Maitland with Bothwell, [[JamesDouglasEarlOfMorton|Morton]], and others in the January 1567 commitment to murder Darnley. His role illustrates how administrative indispensability and elite conspiracy could coexist inside Mary's government, weakening any later official investigation.

## Key Characteristics

- Skilled secretary whose diplomatic value enabled restoration after rebellion.
- Rizzio propagandist and conspirator later restored for his diplomatic value.
- Advocate of removing Darnley through an ambiguously described solution.
- Likely murder conspirator whose government position compromised accountability.

## Qualifications

The Rizzio source identifies Maitland's political role but leaves the authorship and reach of propaganda, exact plot commitments, and private motive source-scoped. The later episode treats his participation in Darnley's murder as the best reconstruction from intelligence and later evidence, not a verdict produced by an independent Scottish proceeding. The precise words and commitments at Craigmillar and Whittingham remain uncertain.

## What Changed

- Established Maitland as an indispensable diplomat whose reintegration also helped assemble the anti-Darnley coalition.
- Added his role in constructing the case against Rizzio and the punishment preceding his politically necessary restoration.
