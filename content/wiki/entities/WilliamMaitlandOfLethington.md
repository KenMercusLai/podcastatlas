# William Maitland of Lethington

## Overview

William Maitland of Lethington is the Scottish secretary and diplomat whom the episode places in both the political discussion of removing [[HenryStuartLordDarnley|Darnley]] and the later murder conspiracy.

## Current Profile

Maitland had participated in the Rizzio conspiracy and fallen from favor, but [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary]] restored him because she needed his diplomatic ability. His reconciliation with [[JamesHepburnEarlOfBothwell|Bothwell]] joined previously opposed nobles around the Darnley problem. At Craigmillar, Maitland promoted divorce while using language that suggested the lords could find a way to free Mary without dishonoring her.

The episode then places Maitland with Bothwell, [[JamesDouglasEarlOfMorton|Morton]], and others in the January 1567 commitment to murder Darnley. His role illustrates how administrative indispensability and elite conspiracy could coexist inside Mary's government, weakening any later official investigation.

## Key Characteristics

- Skilled secretary whose diplomatic value enabled restoration after rebellion.
- Former Rizzio conspirator required to reconcile with Bothwell.
- Advocate of removing Darnley through an ambiguously described solution.
- Likely murder conspirator whose government position compromised accountability.

## Evidence

- Restoration and reconciliation: [[588-mary-queen-of-scots-the-mystery-of-the-exploding-mansion-part-5-glt2383249995]] grounds Mary's need for Maitland and his accommodation with Bothwell.
- Craigmillar discussions: [[588-mary-queen-of-scots-the-mystery-of-the-exploding-mansion-part-5-glt2383249995]] connects Maitland to divorce proposals and hints of a permanent solution.
- Murder conspiracy: [[588-mary-queen-of-scots-the-mystery-of-the-exploding-mansion-part-5-glt2383249995]] places him in the Whittingham alignment against Darnley.

## Qualifications

The episode treats Maitland's participation as the best reconstruction from intelligence and later evidence, not a verdict produced by an independent Scottish proceeding. The precise words and commitments at Craigmillar and Whittingham remain uncertain.

## What Changed

- Established Maitland as an indispensable diplomat whose reintegration also helped assemble the anti-Darnley coalition.

- [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]] - sovereign who restored him for diplomatic service.
- [[JamesHepburnEarlOfBothwell|James Hepburn / Earl of Bothwell]] - former rival and likely co-conspirator.
- [[JamesDouglasEarlOfMorton|James Douglas / Earl of Morton]] - fellow Rizzio conspirator and likely partner in Darnley's murder.
- [[HenryStuartLordDarnley|Henry Stuart / Lord Darnley]] - political obstacle the episode says he helped remove.
- [[KirkOFieldMurder|Kirk o' Field murder]] - conspiracy whose investigation was compromised by elite participation.
