# Jim Dunnam

## Overview

Jim Dunnam was the Texas Democratic legislative leader who organized a 2003 quorum break described in [[divide-and-conquer-divide-and-conquer|Divide and Conquer]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Dunnam as the organizer of 51 state representatives who left [[Texas]] for Ardmore, Oklahoma, denying the 150-member House the 100 members needed for a quorum. The action temporarily blocked a Republican redistricting effort and triggered arrest warrants, extensive media attention, and an attempt involving Homeland Security resources to locate a legislator's plane.

His role supplies the political prehistory for the episode's larger argument: boundary and representation disputes already produce extraordinary procedural tactics even before the more radical possibility of state subdivision enters the discussion.

## Key Characteristics

- Democratic leader in the 2003 Texas House redistricting conflict.
- Organized a collective interstate departure to deny a legislative quorum.
- Used jurisdictional limits on Texas arrest authority as part of the tactic.
- Illustrates procedural resistance within a high-stakes representation dispute.
