# 陈群 / Chen Qun

陈群 / Chen Qun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1026-jiemi-sanguo-di-yi-mengjiang-lvbu-de-luomu-lhjf-agujuliglqchumtff3tftxg1|Hanji 1026]] as [[ChenJiLateHan|陈纪]]'s son, present in [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s army at the fall of [[Xiapi|下邳]]. The episode does not build an independent biography; it uses Chen Qun with Chen Ji to show how [[CaoCao|曹操]] treats surrendering elite families after executing Lü Bu, [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]], and [[GaoShun|高顺]].

That placement makes Chen Qun part of Cao Cao's post-siege distinction between personal danger and usable social capacity. Where Lü Bu's record blocks trust, the Chen family channel can be incorporated through courtesy and office into [[StrategicDefectorIncorporation|纳叛安抚式整合]].
