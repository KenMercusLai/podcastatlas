## Overview

郑吉 / Zheng Ji is synthesized as a Western Han Western Regions official who turns force, escort, and local knowledge into durable authority. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-443-huoqubing-mei-gaoding-de-xiongnu-zhaochongguo-zenme-gaoding-de-lofu-y4fuwvoyvra7l9mpe9hombz|Hanji 443]] adds his earlier institutional founding layer: he receives Rizhu Wang Xianxianshan's defection, escorts him safely to Chang'an, and is made the first protector over the Western Regions. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-461-lishi-shouwei-nvwaijiaojia-fengliao-ruhe-woxuan-xiyu-weiji-lpxkawzol3526yztjyxxmz26phcq|Hanji 461]] later shows that same office style in the Wusun crisis, where Zheng relieves Chigu City and sends [[FengLiaoWesternHan|冯嫽]] to persuade Wujiutu before full war begins.

## Current Profile

The current evidence has two phases. Hanji 443 shows Zheng Ji at the founding edge of the protectorate. After Xiongnu succession conflict pushes Rizhu Wang Xianxianshan toward Han, Zheng mobilizes Quli and nearby groups, reportedly gathers fifty thousand people for the reception mission, and prevents desertion during the escort. Xuan rewards the defector as Gui De Hou and Zheng as Anyuan Hou, while the court makes Zheng responsible for the Western Regions north route and recognizes the protectorate office from this point.

Hanji 461 shows the office after it is already an active regional instrument. Zheng Ji appears when Xishenshou surrounds Chigu City after the failed assassination against Nimi. The episode says Zheng gathers cavalry from smaller Western Regions states to rescue [[XieyouPrincessWesternHan|解忧公主]] and the Han envoys. Xishenshou retreats after seeing the strength of Han-aligned forces.

His second role is more strategic. When Wujiutu claims the Wusun title and faces Han deployment, Zheng Ji chooses Feng Liao as the intermediary because she understands Han documents and Western Regions affairs, is respected by local states, and is connected by marriage to the Wusun right general. Zheng Ji therefore becomes the official who turns military readiness into a negotiated route before the crisis becomes open war.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han frontier official operating through the [[WesternRegionsProtectorate|Western Regions protectorate]] field.
- Turns a major Xiongnu defection into escorted submission and institutional promotion.
- Coordinates regional cavalry to relieve the Chigu City siege after the failed Nimi assassination.
- Treats Wusun as a strategic hinge in Han-Xiongnu competition, not only as a local succession dispute.
- Uses Feng Liao's personal authority and Wusun network as a diplomatic instrument.
- Combines coercive pressure with intermediary-based negotiation.

## Qualifications

This page does not reconstruct Zheng Ji's complete office history, appointment chronology, wider protectorate administration, or later career. Hanji 443 supplies the defection-and-founding layer but keeps Rizhu Wang's broader biography and the protectorate's exact bureaucratic mechanics source-scoped. Hanji 461 uses Zheng chiefly as the official who coordinates rescue and mediation in the Wusun crisis.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 443's founding layer: Zheng's reception of Rizhu Wang, escort discipline, Anyuan Hou reward, and first protectorate authority.
- Preserved Hanji 461's Chigu relief and Feng Liao mediation branch as the later operational layer.
