## Overview

郑吉 / Zheng Ji is synthesized as a Western Han Western Regions official who turns屯田, allied local forces, garrisoning, escort, and local knowledge into durable authority. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-422-1-weihe-xihan-huiyou-name-duo-de-guanbi-minfan-lnj1etibzsz_poqkoazcyprjc1k|Hanji 422-1]] adds the earliest current Cheshi layer: Zheng and Sima Xi use [[QuliWesternRegions|渠犁]] stores, pardoned convicts, and Western Regions troops to defeat [[CheshiKingdomWesternHan|车师国]], then leave a small屯田 administration after Wugui flees. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-429-jiaobingbibai-de-gushi-zenme-laide-lokha4dmrik4ez4vtpunb9rwhzav|Hanji 429]] adds the later Cheshi rescue crisis, where Zheng leaves a small garrison after Cheshi's submission and is surrounded when he leads Quli屯田 troops back to rescue it. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-443-huoqubing-mei-gaoding-de-xiongnu-zhaochongguo-zenme-gaoding-de-lofu-y4fuwvoyvra7l9mpe9hombz|Hanji 443]] adds his later institutional founding layer: he receives Rizhu Wang Xianxianshan's defection, escorts him safely to Chang'an, and is made the first protector over the Western Regions. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-461-lishi-shouwei-nvwaijiaojia-fengliao-ruhe-woxuan-xiyu-weiji-lpxkawzol3526yztjyxxmz26phcq|Hanji 461]] later shows that same office style in the Wusun crisis, where Zheng relieves Chigu City and sends [[FengLiaoWesternHan|冯嫽]] to persuade Wujiutu before full war begins.

## Current Profile

The current evidence has four phases. Hanji 422-1 shows Zheng before the later "骄兵必败" crisis. Cheshi's Wugui has aligned with [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] and threatens the Han-Wusun route, so Zheng and Sima Xi attack from the Quli屯田 base. The victory demonstrates Zheng's method: use stored grain, pardoned convicts, local Western Regions forces, and a small continuing屯田 presence rather than relying only on a large central army. It also shows the method's limits, because Wugui flees to [[Wusun|乌孙]], Xiongnu installs Dumo, and Han's holding force remains thin.

Hanji 429 shows the same frontier problem returning. Cheshi's submission gives Han a forward foothold, but Zheng's three-hundred-man garrison is too small to hold against Xiongnu counterattack. When he brings seven or eight thousand Quli屯田 troops to rescue it, distance and enemy numbers leave him surrounded. Xuan's limited response extracts Zheng and his men, and Zheng is then made Wei Sima responsible for Shanshan and the southwest-route security field.

Hanji 443 shows Zheng Ji at the founding edge of the protectorate. After Xiongnu succession conflict pushes Rizhu Wang Xianxianshan toward Han, Zheng mobilizes Quli and nearby groups, reportedly gathers fifty thousand people for the reception mission, and prevents desertion during the escort. Xuan rewards the defector as Gui De Hou and Zheng as Anyuan Hou, while the court makes Zheng responsible for the Western Regions north route and recognizes the protectorate office from this point.

Hanji 461 shows the office after it is already an active regional instrument. Zheng Ji appears when Xishenshou surrounds Chigu City after the failed assassination against Nimi. The episode says Zheng gathers cavalry from smaller Western Regions states to rescue [[XieyouPrincessWesternHan|解忧公主]] and the Han envoys. Xishenshou retreats after seeing the strength of Han-aligned forces.

His second role is more strategic. When Wujiutu claims the Wusun title and faces Han deployment, Zheng Ji chooses Feng Liao as the intermediary because she understands Han documents and Western Regions affairs, is respected by local states, and is connected by marriage to the Wusun right general. Zheng Ji therefore becomes the official who turns military readiness into a negotiated route before the crisis becomes open war.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han frontier official operating through the [[WesternRegionsProtectorate|Western Regions protectorate]] field.
- Cheshi crisis officer whose Quli-based attack, small forward garrison, and later Quli rescue force expose both the utility and cost of holding contested oasis positions.
- Turns a major Xiongnu defection into escorted submission and institutional promotion.
- Coordinates regional cavalry to relieve the Chigu City siege after the failed Nimi assassination.
- Treats Wusun as a strategic hinge in Han-Xiongnu competition, not only as a local succession dispute.
- Uses Feng Liao's personal authority and Wusun network as a diplomatic instrument.
- Combines coercive pressure with intermediary-based negotiation.

## Qualifications

This page does not reconstruct Zheng Ji's complete office history, appointment chronology, wider protectorate administration, or later career. Hanji 422-1 and Hanji 429 supply two Cheshi layers but do not settle the full Cheshi geography, Wugui/Dumo chronology, Sima Xi's career, or the later front/rear Cheshi terminology. Hanji 443 supplies the defection-and-founding layer but keeps Rizhu Wang's broader biography and the protectorate's exact bureaucratic mechanics source-scoped. Hanji 461 uses Zheng chiefly as the official who coordinates rescue and mediation in the Wusun crisis.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 422-1 as Zheng Ji's earlier Cheshi layer: Quli屯田 stores, allied Western Regions troops, Wugui's surrender and flight, hostage handling, and small屯田 administration.
- Preserved Hanji 429's later Cheshi crisis layer: small garrison, Quli rescue force, Xiongnu encirclement, limited extraction, and Wei Sima appointment.
- Preserved Hanji 443's founding layer and Hanji 461's Chigu relief plus Feng Liao mediation branch as later operational layers.
