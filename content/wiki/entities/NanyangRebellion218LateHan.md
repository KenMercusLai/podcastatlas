## Overview

The 218 CE Nanyang revolt is presented as a levy-driven local seizure that becomes open rebellion without a viable completion plan and ends in suppression and collective civilian punishment.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1163 says [[DongliGunLateHan|东里衮]] arrives to collect further resources after earlier grain extraction. [[HouYinLateHan|侯音]] refuses, surrounds the administrator, and recaptures him after an escape attempt. Once this becomes rebellion, Hou Yin lacks a stable next step and accepts [[ZongZiqingLateHan|宗子卿]]'s deceptive proposal to release the captive in the name of popular support.

Zong and Dongli Gun escape to [[CaoRen|曹仁]]. Cao Ren surrounds the city, Hou Yin is killed during a failed breakout, and the episode says Cao Ren then orders a massacre that frightens nearby counties away from armed resistance. The event therefore combines extraction pressure, decision failure, deception, coercive suppression, and civilian harm.

## Key Characteristics

- Begins in conflict over wartime levy and subsistence pressure.
- Escalates from detention of an administrator into rebellion.
- Lacks a credible plan for coalition, defense, or negotiated exit.
- Collapses after an insider uses authorized release to escape.
- Ends with exemplary violence extending beyond the rebel leader.

## Qualifications

The episode compresses the event and supplies no independent administrative or military record. The year, levy scale, popular support, city, force totals, decision authority, massacre, nearby-county response, and relationship to the wider Jingzhou front require verification. The moral frame should not convert structural extraction and collective punishment into personality alone.
