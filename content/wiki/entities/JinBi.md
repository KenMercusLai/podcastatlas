# 晋鄙 / Jin Bi

晋鄙 / Jin Bi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-2-ltlobdujy-hdipimfdupvwod2ech]] as the [[WeiState|魏国]] commander whom [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] sends north with 100,000 troops to rescue [[ZhaoState|赵国]] during the [[Handan|邯郸]] crisis. His dispatch shows that Wei has not completely ignored Zhao's danger after [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]].

The episode's key point is that dispatch is not the same as effective rescue. After [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] warns Wei that Qin will attack Wei next if Zhao is destroyed, Wei Anxi Wang orders Jin Bi to stop at [[YeWarringStates|邺城]] and observe. Jin Bi therefore becomes the military face of a stalled coalition: troops exist, but political fear prevents them from reaching the decisive battlefield.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-6-lteqe-0ykjtv01cmxv1fhx6z0vdi]] completes Jin Bi's role in the crisis. [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] arrives with the matching half of the [[TigerTallyCommandSystem|tiger tally]], but Jin Bi remains suspicious because a single noble abruptly replacing the commander of 100,000 troops looks irregular even if the credential matches. [[ZhuHaiWeiRetainer|朱亥]] then kills him with a hidden iron hammer.

This episode treats Jin Bi as the ethical center of the story's discomfort. He is not depicted as a Qin collaborator or coward in the killing scene; he is a field commander doubting a dangerous command transfer. His death makes [[StealingTigerTalliesToRescueZhao|窃符救赵]] an effective rescue with a serious [[MoralCostOfEffectiveRescue|moral cost]].
