# Marie-Louise von Franz / 玛丽-路易丝·冯·弗兰兹

[[MarieLouiseVonFranz|Marie-Louise von Franz / 玛丽-路易丝·冯·弗兰兹]] is the Jungian analyst whose [[TheEternalYouth|《永恒少年：我们为何拒绝长大》]] provides the main framework for [[07-yong-rongge-lilun-fenxi-xiaowangzi-yongheng-shaonian-ji-qita-539091165]]. The episode refers to her as "法兰兹博士" and uses her account of the eternal-youth type to reread [[TheLittlePrince|《小王子》]].

Her role in the source is conceptual rather than biographical. The hosts use her ideas to distinguish [[PuerAeternus|永恒少年]] from ordinary immaturity, to connect that pattern with [[MotherComplex|mother complex]], and to argue that maturity often arrives through suffering, daily work, and grounded responsibility rather than through preserving fantasy untouched.

## Key Claims
- Von Franz supplies the episode's explicit Jungian vocabulary for refusing adulthood.
- The source uses her work to preserve both sides of the eternal-youth figure: creativity and spiritual possibility, but also avoidance, laziness, dependency, and self-destruction.
- Her phrase about the "statistical" poisoning of the soul becomes the episode's route from literary symbol into modern-life critique.
