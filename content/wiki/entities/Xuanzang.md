# 玄奘

## Overview

玄奘 is the historical pilgrimage figure behind the取经 tradition and the source horizon for later [[JourneyToTheWest|《西游记》]] and film-adaptation protagonists.

## Current Profile

The wiki distinguishes historical 玄奘 from later literary and film figures. [[43-xiyouji-duo-ni-shi-shenme-yaojing-618154182]] uses him to mark the distance between real pilgrimage history and the accumulated novel world. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-4-zhoumo-ruiqi-pei-ni-lingyi-shijiao-zai-kan-yibian-xiyou-xiangmo-lolxgqbkq2owmzzay91dy4fanwn3]] adds [[ChenXuanzangConqueringDemons|陈玄奘]], a [[JourneyToTheWestConqueringDemons|《西游降魔篇》]] protagonist whose name and role derive from the same tradition but whose arc is interpreted as inner cultivation.

## Key Characteristics

- He anchors the historical pilgrimage layer behind the取经 story.
- His later companions and ordeals show how literary tradition can grow away from direct biography.
- Adaptations can use Xuanzang-derived protagonists to stage new moral and psychological arcs.

## Evidence

- Historical pilgrimage layer: [[43-xiyouji-duo-ni-shi-shenme-yaojing-618154182]] separates the real travel background from the later novel-world team.
- Literary growth away from biography: [[43-xiyouji-duo-ni-shi-shenme-yaojing-618154182]] connects [[SunWukong|孙悟空]], [[ShaWujing|沙和尚]], and [[ZhuBajie|猪八戒]] to different source histories around the pilgrimage.
- Adaptation into inner cultivation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-4-zhoumo-ruiqi-pei-ni-lingyi-shijiao-zai-kan-yibian-xiyou-xiangmo-lolxgqbkq2owmzzay91dy4fanwn3]] reads the film's Chen Xuanzang as moving from abstract compassion through love and loss into awakened restraint.

## Qualifications

The page covers the historical and source-tradition anchor. Film-specific claims should remain on [[ChenXuanzangConqueringDemons|陈玄奘]] rather than being treated as direct biography of historical Xuanzang.
