# 郭汜 / Guo Si (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-989-ningwei-taipingquan-buzuo-luanshi-ren-lkoycygmjnltuvpq-6wjv-ls61s6|Hanji 989]] adds Guo Si's obstruction immediately before the Huayin fight. After receiving title recognition, Guo Si still refuses the emperor's move toward Hongnong, preferring a route that would keep [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] nearer his own control. His later plan at Xinfeng to force the emperor back west fails after Zhong Ji alerts [[YangDingLateHan|杨定]], [[DongChengLateHan|董承]], and [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], turning Guo Si's control project into flight toward Zhongnan.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-990-lijue-guosi-shengsi-yuanjia-weihe-turan-lianshou-lmop5mcrsjuq-ixuv0sfho5mvc-f|Hanji 990]] backfills the temporary reunion with [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] before the pursuit sequence in Hanji 991. After [[YangDingLateHan|杨定]] attacks [[DuanWeiLateHan|段煨]], Guo Si and Li Jue recognize that the real prize is [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], not victory in their private feud; Guo Si then blocks Yang Ding's attempted retreat toward Lantian.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-991-zhengduo-xiandi-zhizhan-shui-neng-zuizhong-qusheng-lkbsl5uzqxouzm6mlewrnuns0x6p|Hanji 991]] backfills Guo Si's pursuit role before the Anyi and Luoyang-return branches. He appears with [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]] in the renewed chase after [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], turning the imperial flight into repeated battle, captured officials, scattered court records, and emergency reliance on [[BaiboArmy|白波军]] and [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] reinforcements.

郭汜 / Guo Si (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu|Hanji 999]] as [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]]'s rival in the contest over [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]. Hanji 992 keeps him inside the peace-and-release backdrop around the emperor's flight, while the ruined [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] branch shows that the Li-Guo crisis has already destroyed ordinary urban order.

Hanji 999 then uses the Li Jue-Guo Si conflict to show why the emperor's physical escape does not restore sovereignty: each armed "protector" can also become a captor.

In this source, Guo Si is part of the setting that makes later court politics legible. Once the emperor leaves the immediate Li-Guo danger, he still falls among [[DongChengLateHan|董承]], [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]], and [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]], so the problem shifts from one military pair to a broader field of interested escorts.
