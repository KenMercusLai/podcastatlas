# Delhi

Delhi appears in [[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]] as the protest setting where the [[CockroachMovement]] escalated after [[Sonamji]] was removed from a hunger-strike site. The source says the resulting mobilization became Delhi's biggest protest in more than half a decade.

The episode also uses Delhi as a state-response case: central internet shutdowns, metro closures, tear gas, pellet-gun use, and police beatings make the city part of the wiki's [[DissentSpaceCompression]] branch rather than merely a location.
