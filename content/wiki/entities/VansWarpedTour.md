# Vans Warped Tour

Vans Warped Tour appears in [[working-memory-the-surprising-decline-of-dementia-6a6b0eeca585d3be78cd9d1b]] as part of the wider emo and punk-adjacent revival. The episode says the tour returned for its 30th anniversary with dates across the United States, Canada, and Mexico.

The source uses the tour as revival infrastructure. Alongside [[WhenWeWereYoungFestival]], it shows that emo demand is not only one band's comeback but a wider live-event market around memory, genre identity, and intergenerational participation.
