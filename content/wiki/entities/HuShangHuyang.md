# 胡伤 / 胡阳 / Hu Shang or Hu Yang

胡伤 / 胡阳 / Hu Shang or Hu Yang is a source-normalized Qin commander entry. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-83-zhuhou-bailan-jing-chengle-jingdian-ji-zhong-ji-ljp7nbqnrctr5mw6i9u982abmx-1]] mentions Hu Yang as a Qin guest minister moving with [[WeiRan|魏冉]] and [[BaiQi|白起]] during the [[HuayangBattle|华阳之战]] rescue.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-89-qinwang-jing-bei-ta-dui-mabaonan-ljugedx8qxhtuzd1ja-xiwxgse19]] then records a Qin commander written 胡伤 leading an attack on Zhao's Yan Yu / Eyu area in 269 BCE. The host argues that 胡伤 is likely a textual or transcriptional confusion for 胡阳 because the characters are graphically close.

The wiki keeps both names on one page but treats the identification as source-scoped. In Zhouji 89, his failed attack also supports the episode's broader source-critical point that Qin and Zhao records can describe the same military event with very different degrees of embarrassment or triumph.
