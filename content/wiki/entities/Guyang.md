# 固阳 / Guyang

固阳 / Guyang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] as a [[WeiState|魏国]] place surrounded by [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] for [[QinState|秦国]] in 351 BCE. The source says Guyang's defenders surrender, making it one of the concrete markers of [[QinEastwardPressure|秦国东进压力]] after Qin has already attacked or taken [[Anyi|安邑]].

The page is source-scoped. Guyang matters less as a fully developed local history than as evidence that Qin pressure has crossed into Wei's strategic depth, helping explain why Wei seeks to repair relations with [[ZhaoState|赵国]] and [[QiState|齐国]].
