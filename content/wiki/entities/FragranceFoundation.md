# Fragrance Foundation

## Overview
The Fragrance Foundation is the industry organization through which [[AnnetteGreen]] organized interdisciplinary education about scent, its production, and its effects.

## Current Profile
The source presents the foundation as more than a marketing body. Under Green's influence, its lectures brought neuropathologists, clinical psychologists, anthropologists, city planners, perfumers, and beauty specialists into conversation, helping establish fragrance as a subject for scientific, cultural, and professional inquiry.

## Key Characteristics
- Served as an education and convening platform for the fragrance industry.
- Connected perfumery with health, psychology, anthropology, planning, and beauty expertise.
- Supported Green's effort to make fragrance legible as emotion, identity, and everyday practice.

## Evidence
- Interdisciplinary education: [[social-insecurity-numbers-the-money-behind-the-manosphere-7eb4966be1685ee4f19adde69e240aaa]] says Green organized foundation lectures involving neuropathologists, clinical psychologists, anthropologists, city planners, perfumers, and beauty specialists.
- Category development: [[social-insecurity-numbers-the-money-behind-the-manosphere-7eb4966be1685ee4f19adde69e240aaa]] places those lectures alongside Green's laboratory and advocacy for everyday fragrance use.
