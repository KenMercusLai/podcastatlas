# George Crook

## Overview

George Crook was a U.S. Army commander whose winter attack displaced Cheyenne people toward the growing Native coalition and whose defeat at the [[BattleOfRosebud]] silently removed one column from the campaign preceding the [[BattleOfLittleBighorn]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Crook as the only one of [[PhilipSheridan]]'s three commanders to achieve even limited action during the failed winter campaign. His force attacked a Cheyenne village, but the result was strategically perverse: survivors moved toward [[CrazyHorse]] and then [[SittingBull]], enlarging the coalition that U.S. commanders underestimated.

In the summer campaign Crook moved north from Fort Fetterman. On 17 June 1876, he discounted warnings from Crow scouts before Lakota and Cheyenne forces attacked at the Rosebud. After roughly six hours of fighting, he stopped advancing and did not inform [[AlfredTerry]], leaving Terry and Custer to act as though the converging plan remained intact.

## Key Characteristics

- His winter action produced displacement without the intended strategic suppression.
- He underestimated warnings from scouts before the Rosebud attack.
- The Rosebud checked his advance and damaged his force's morale.
- His failure to communicate withdrawal created a consequential information gap.
- His campaign role illustrates how local action can undermine a wider operational design.
