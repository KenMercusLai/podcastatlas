# Mandubracius

## Overview

Mandubracius was an exiled prince of the [[Trinovantes]] whose appeal to [[JuliusCaesar]] helped turn the 54 BC expedition into an intervention in British dynastic politics.

## Current Profile

After [[Cassivellaunus]] overthrew and killed his father, Mandubracius sought Caesar's support. Caesar returned to Britain as the protector of a displaced prince, while the Trinovantes' submission gave the Roman army local allies and intelligence against Cassivellaunus.

The negotiated settlement restored Mandubracius and required Cassivellaunus not to attack him. His restoration gave Caesar a client relationship and a claim of political success but did not create direct Roman administration.

## Key Characteristics

- Exiled Trinovantian prince with a dynastic claim.
- British ally who supplied Caesar with an intervention rationale and local political access.
- Beneficiary of a settlement limiting Cassivellaunus.
- Example of how internal rivalry could redirect foreign military power.

## Evidence

- Exile and appeal: [[499-the-roman-conquest-of-britain-julius-caesars-invasion-part-1-glt9770560726]] links his father's overthrow to his request for Caesar's help.
- Alliance utility: [[499-the-roman-conquest-of-britain-julius-caesars-invasion-part-1-glt9770560726]] presents the Trinovantes and allied submissions as part of Caesar's advance beyond Kent.
- Restoration: [[499-the-roman-conquest-of-britain-julius-caesars-invasion-part-1-glt9770560726]] makes his reinstatement a term of the settlement with Cassivellaunus.

## Qualifications

Mandubracius appears through Caesar's account, which benefits from portraying intervention as protection of an ally. His father's position, the exact sequence of exile and alliance, local support for his restoration, and the durability of his rule are not independently established by this source.

## What Changed

- Established Mandubracius as the client claimant who connected British dynastic conflict to Caesar's second expedition.

## Relationships

- [[Trinovantes]] - polity whose dynastic claim he represented.
- [[Cassivellaunus]] - rival whose earlier victory drove him into exile.
- [[JuliusCaesar]] - Roman patron who restored him.
- [[Commius]] - intermediary in the settlement securing his restoration.
- [[CaesarsBritishExpeditions]] - intervention through which he regained power.
