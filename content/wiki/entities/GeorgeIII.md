# George III

## Overview

George III appears in the Franklin episode as the British king Franklin admired before his political confidence in Britain collapsed.

## Current Profile

The source uses George III as a marker of Franklin's original imperial identity. Franklin is not portrayed as anti-British from birth; he spent much of his life in Britain, considered himself a loyal Englishman, and admired the king before the revolutionary crisis changed his position. George III therefore matters here as the symbol of the allegiance Franklin left behind.

## Key Characteristics

- British monarch associated with Franklin's pre-revolutionary loyalty.
- Figure Franklin admired before turning against British rule.
- Symbolic reference point for [[ColonialLoyaltyRupture|colonial loyalty rupture]].
