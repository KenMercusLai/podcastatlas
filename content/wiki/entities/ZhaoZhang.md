# 赵章 / Zhao Zhang

赵章 / Zhao Zhang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-57-gudai-fa-dushi-haiyou-zhe-zuoyong-lj6wi61osaytowgmdp8dtkrwb-si]] as [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]]'s earlier legitimate eldest son by a Han princess. The episode introduces him only briefly, but the mention is loaded: Zhao Wuling Wang's later affection for [[WuWa|吴娃]] and her son [[ZhaoHe|赵何]] creates the succession fault line the host says will eventually lead to Zhao Wuling Wang's starvation death.

Zhao Zhang's page therefore anchors the displaced-elder-son side of the future Zhao conflict. The source is not yet the full story of that conflict, so this page keeps the claim narrow.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-66-zhongguo-lishishang-naxie-diwang-shi-bei-huohuo-e-si-de-1-lvl1uxjadiedqwad-skxrwf-usnq]] turns that displaced position into an organized base. Zhao Wuling Wang makes Zhao Zhang Anyang Jun in the Dai region and gives him [[TianBuli|田不李]] as a chancellor-like helper, while [[LiDui|李队]] warns that Zhao Zhang's strength, arrogance, followers, and resentment make him dangerous. The episode also shows Zhao Wuling Wang pained by watching the elder brother bow to [[ZhaoHe|赵何]], which pushes the father toward a rejected plan to split Zhao and toward the Shaqiu setup.
