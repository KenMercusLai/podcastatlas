# 扁鹊 / Bian Que

扁鹊 / Bian Que appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-3-ltca-ikuzo9ej7a0lfrgwv2-wulb]] through the story of repeatedly warning [[TianQiHuanGong|田氏齐桓公 / 田武]] that his illness is worsening. The ruler refuses the diagnosis until the disease can no longer be treated.

The episode uses the story less as medical biography than as political judgment: a ruler who dismisses unwelcome expertise can turn a manageable problem into a fatal one. Bian Que therefore links the Tian-family Qi branch to the wiki's broader concern with evidence, counsel, and the limits of ruler self-certainty.
