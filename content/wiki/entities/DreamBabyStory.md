# 《梦宝宝》

[[DreamBabyStory|《梦宝宝》]] is a [[SarahPinsker|Sarah Pinsker / 莎拉·平斯克]] story discussed in [[166-youyou-changfeng-wenrou-de-huanxiang-zhi-ge-fuping-jiaolv-de-gouhe-948043059]]. The story follows a woman in a same-sex partnership who has given up on expensive assisted reproduction, then repeatedly dreams of a child so vividly that the dream begins to affect her body and later seems to become a collective public event.

The episode reads the story through [[DreamParenthoodAmbiguity]]. The dream child may be desire, grief, guilt, physiological response, alien intrusion, or a real child entering the world through human longing; the force of the story is that none of those explanations fully cancels the others. The hosts compare it with [[StoryOfYourLife|《你一生的故事》]]: [[TedChiang|Ted Chiang]] is treated as more logically clean, while Pinsker is treated as more dreamlike and unresolved.
