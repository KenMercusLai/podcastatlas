# Rebuild Paradise Foundation

## Overview
The Rebuild Paradise Foundation is a recovery organization connecting post-Camp-Fire rebuilding with insurance availability, household mitigation, and the wider economic recovery of [[ParadiseCalifornia|Paradise]].

## Current Profile
Through director [[JennyGoodland]], [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-043ffe0bc2c7516bac64132229755e93-43026861-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-043ffe0bc2c7516bac64132229755e93-43026861]] presents the foundation as a local coordinating institution. It recognized that technically rebuilt homes still needed financeable insurance and used resident organization and insurer engagement to make mitigation visible.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats insurance as infrastructure for housing and local economic recovery.
- Connects individual rebuilding with neighborhood-scale risk reduction.
- Helps translate resident practices into evidence insurers can inspect.
- Uses direct engagement with carriers to challenge remote or overly broad risk classifications.
