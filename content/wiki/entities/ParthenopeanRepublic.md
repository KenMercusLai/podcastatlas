# Parthenopean Republic

## Overview

The Parthenopean Republic was the French-backed republican regime established in Naples in 1799 and destroyed by royalist counterrevolution within months.

## Current Profile

The republic emerged after French forces occupied Naples, defeated the monarchist Lazzaroni, and abolished royal government. Its leadership drew from intellectuals, reformers, Freemasons, merchants, and other elites influenced by revolutionary ideals, but the episode portrays it as socially narrow in a city and countryside where Catholic and monarchist allegiance remained strong. [[CardinalRuffo|Cardinal Ruffo]]'s Sanfedisti reconquered the mainland, after which republican defenders accepted a capitulation promising passage to Toulon. Their disarmament, detention, trials, and executions made the republic's end a case of [[SurrenderGuaranteeIntegrity|surrender-guarantee failure]] as well as military defeat.

## Key Characteristics

- French-backed revolutionary polity created after royal flight.
- Led by reformist and commercial elites with limited mass support.
- Opposed by popular Catholic, monarchist, and rural mobilization.
- Militarily dependent on a French garrison and fortified positions.
- Ended through negotiated surrender followed by disputed detention and punishment.

## Qualifications

The episode offers a compressed British narrative rather than a full account of republican institutions, policy, factions, popular support, French control, or regional variation. Numbers imprisoned and executed vary, and the legal status of the capitulation remains contested.

## What Changed

- Created a profile joining elite reform, weak social reach, popular counterrevolution, and a disputed surrender aftermath.
