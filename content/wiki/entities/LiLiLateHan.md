# 李利 / Li Li (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-973-changpingguan-zhizhan-shengsi-zhidi-zhanhou-jing-chonggui-yuhao-lgazc9dewfayb8qnkncfkfa12yet|Hanji 973]] adds Li Li to the initial 194 CE campaign force. [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] sends him with [[FanChouLateHan|樊稠]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] against [[MaTeng|马腾]] and [[HanSui|韩遂]], so his later reporting role in Hanji 984 sits after an already shared victory at [[ChangpingguanBattleLateHan|长平观之战]].

李利 / Li Li enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-984-liangzhouxi-junfa-neidou-ta-weihe-di-yige-daoxia-lurb2qfzsa7e-owh-dl-m-sccze5|Hanji 984]] as [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]]'s nephew attached to [[FanChouLateHan|樊稠]]'s campaign against [[MaTeng|马腾]] and [[HanSui|韩遂]]. The page uses `LiLiLateHan` to avoid collisions with other Li-named figures.

In the source, Li Li's role is small but consequential. He follows Fan Chou but fights lazily, prompting Fan Chou to scold him and threaten punishment. Later he sees Fan Chou and Han Sui hold hands and talk for a long time during the pursuit toward [[ChenCang|陈仓]]. He cannot hear their words, but he reports their closeness to Li Jue.

Li Li is therefore a transmission channel rather than the full cause of Fan Chou's death. His report matters because Li Jue is already jealous of Fan Chou's bravery, military prestige, and army support; the report converts ambiguous field courtesy into a usable suspicion.
