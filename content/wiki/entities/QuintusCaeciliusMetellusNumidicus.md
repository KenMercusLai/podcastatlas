# Quintus Caecilius Metellus Numidicus / 梅特鲁斯

## Overview

Quintus Caecilius Metellus Numidicus / 梅特鲁斯 is the aristocratic Roman commander and patron whose conflict with [[GaiusMarius]] over the Jugurthine War makes elite patronage and outsider ambition visible inside the late [[RomanRepublic|Roman Republic]].

## Current Profile

Episode 701 presents Metellus as part of the powerful Metelli network that helps Marius after Scipio Aemilianus's death. As consul for 109 BC, he receives the war against [[Jugurtha]] in Africa and takes Marius as deputy because Marius is experienced, capable, and a long-term client. Metellus is not portrayed as incompetent: he restores discipline, defeats Jugurtha in battle, destroys the elephants, and wages a harsh campaign.

The crisis comes from ambition and status. In 108 BC, Marius asks to return to Rome and run for consul. Metellus treats the ambition with contempt and delays him, while Marius's agents present Metellus in Rome as arrogant and out of touch. Marius then wins the 107 BC consulship and receives Metellus's African command.

## Key Characteristics

- Metellus represents elite aristocratic patronage that could sponsor a talented outsider but still resist his highest ambitions.
- His African command is effective enough that Marius's rise cannot be reduced to replacing incompetence.
- His contempt for Marius's consulship ambition turns clientage into rivalry.
- His loss of command shows popular electoral legitimacy breaking into senatorial command allocation.

## Evidence

- Patronage frame: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] says Marius relied on the Caecilii Metelli after Scipio's death and later rebuilt relations after his difficult post-tribunate years.
- Military competence: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] credits Metellus with restoring discipline, defeating Jugurtha, destroying elephants, and pursuing a harsh countryside campaign.
- Status conflict: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] describes Metellus's contempt for Marius's consulship ambition and his delay of Marius's departure.
- Command displacement: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] says Marius's agents campaigned against Metellus in Rome before Marius won the consulship and African command.

## Qualifications

The source gives Metellus mainly through his relationship to Marius. It does not attempt a full Metellan family history or complete biography, and it keeps the moral balance mixed: Metellus is aristocratic and patronal, but also a serious commander.

## What Changed

- Created Metellus as the aristocratic-patron and command-displacement counterpart to Marius's rise.

## Relationships

- [[GaiusMarius]] - former client and deputy who challenges and replaces Metellus in Africa.
- [[Jugurtha]] - Numidian opponent against whom Metellus first conducts the Roman campaign.
- [[RomanRepublic]] - political system where patronage, election, and command assignment collide.
- [[CursusHonorum]] - office ladder whose elite assumptions Metellus expects Marius to respect.
- [[PopularisPolitics]] - public-political mode Marius uses against Metellus's aristocratic image.
- [[LateRomanRepublicCrisis]] - crisis frame in which elite command and popular legitimacy become unstable.
