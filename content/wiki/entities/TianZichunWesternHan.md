## Overview

田子春 / Tian Zichun is presented as a Qi political broker who takes payment from [[LiuZeLangyeKingWesternHan|刘泽]] to seek a royal title, delays for two years, and then builds an indirect Chang'an access route through elite display and his son's service under a favored chief eunuch.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-202-lishishang-yin-lengbaoli-bei-esi-de-ren-shi-ta-2-lrs7h5hhj0m0oixd6gw8flqpi0nd|Hanji 202 part 2]] introduces Tian as a Qi itinerant persuader who claims he can plan Liu Ze's advancement from marquis to king. Liu Ze gives him two hundred jin of gold, but Tian returns home and does nothing visible for two years. The episode does not establish whether this is fraud, deliberate patience, or later narrative dramatization.

After Liu Ze sends a letter implying their relationship cannot simply end, Tian goes to Chang'an. Rather than approach Liu Ze directly, he rents an impressive residence, entertains guests and powerful people, and makes himself known. He then places his son under a chief eunuch trusted by Lu Zhi, allowing the son to enter the eunuch's household and build confidence. The episode ends at this setup stage, before Tian's decisive maneuver or its result.

## Key Characteristics

- Qi itinerant persuader who offers a strategy for Liu Ze's promotion to king.
- Paid broker who accepts a reported two hundred jin of gold before delaying for two years.
- Reluctant or pressured agent who acts only after Liu Ze's warning letter.
- Reputation builder who uses conspicuous housing and elite hospitality in Chang'an.
- Indirect access strategist who places his son inside a favored eunuch's trust network.
