# 张大白 / Zhang Dabai

## Overview

张大白 appears as a co-host of [[WeirdHistoryPodcast|怪东西 Weird History]] in comparisons of major earthquakes, outsider officials, chivalric traditions, and culturally consequential meals across Chinese and wider world history.

## Current Profile

In the earthquake episode, Zhang opens from personal experience of distant shaking near Beijing and helps move the discussion from immediate fear into historical comparison. In the guest-official episode, Zhang introduces a separate history program and helps frame [[LiSi|李斯]] and [[RobertHart|赫德]] through political opportunity, trust, and ruling interests. Episode 33 adds an intellectual-biographical entry: he recalls resisting the idea of a universal “literati dream of chivalry” after reading Chen Pingyuan, then later recognizing youxia as a historical social phenomenon distinct from fictional martial prowess. Episode 24 has him survey Chinese political and literary banquets before selecting the [[LantingGathering|Lanting Gathering]] as a thematic counterpart to the [[LastSupper]], then move from remembered school recitation toward adult reflection on aging and limited time. The co-hosting profile therefore spans disaster explanation, comparative institutions, historical-literary interpretation, and mortality-centered cultural comparison.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses personal earthquake experience as an entry into historical analysis.
- Participates in a comparative rather than nationally isolated disaster narrative.
- Keeps attention on both institutional response and the cultural need to explain suffering.
- Uses paired lives to ask how political systems recruit, trust, and control outside expertise.
- Distinguishes historical social roles from later literary archetypes and tests cross-cultural resemblance against institutional difference.
- Uses Chinese banquet traditions and changing life experience to connect literary interpretation with mortality and present responsibility.
