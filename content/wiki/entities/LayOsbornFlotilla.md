# 阿思本舰队事件 / Lay-Osborn Flotilla

## Overview

The Lay-Osborn Flotilla was a Qing-funded purchase of British warships that collapsed over who would command the force.

## Current Profile

The bounded source presents the flotilla as a principal-agent and sovereignty failure inside otherwise useful foreign expertise. Ships intended for the campaign against the [[TaipingRebellion|Taiping Rebellion]] were financed through Qing customs revenue, but Horatio Nelson Lay and Sherard Osborn sought a command structure that did not place the fleet under ordinary Qing official control. Qing opposition ended the arrangement, removed Lay, and opened the inspector-generalship to [[RobertHart|Robert Hart]].

## Key Characteristics

- Foreign procurement intended to add naval capacity against domestic rebellion.
- Qing financing without an accepted Qing command chain.
- Conflict between professional autonomy and sovereign authority.
- Personnel crisis that changed leadership of the customs service.
