# Cambricon / 寒武纪

Cambricon, also referred to as 寒武纪, appears in [[ep270-yi-mei-xinpian-de-manchang-zhengtu-women-li-suanli-ziyou-haiyou-duoyuan-lm7lxlmcnjwnawtq-9typc-fnrci]] as a prominent Chinese AI-chip company. The episode uses it as part of a broader domestic accelerator wave that includes earlier AI-chip startups and later GPU-oriented teams founded around 2018 to 2020.

The source does not give a detailed Cambricon product analysis. Its main point is ecosystemic: domestic AI-chip firms are judged not only by chip design, but by access to advanced processes, manufacturing yield, cost, software stacks, application adaptation, and whether users can substitute them for [[Nvidia]] systems in real workloads.

[[guochan-ai-suanli-neng-ping-chaojiedian-wandao-chaoche-ma-waic-shendu-guancha-s10e23-a6c6ab3e-72b2-470b-aefd-04b19679d37f]] adds Cambricon to the source's near-term winner filter. [[ZhangHaijun]] says he is relatively more optimistic about [[Huawei]], Cambricon, and large-platform-linked teams such as [[Pingtouge]] and [[Kunlunxin]], while warning that domestic chip firms need major internet-company orders before the market sorts out.
