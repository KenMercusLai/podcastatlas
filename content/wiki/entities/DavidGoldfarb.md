# David Goldfarb

## Overview

David Goldfarb is the Staten Island attorney who organized against secession and argued that independence threatened both public services and the borough's internal political cohesion.

## Current Profile

Goldfarb preferred remaining part of New York City and helped build [[StatenIslandersForUnifiedNewYork|Staten Islanders for Unified New York]]. His opposition combined practical and democratic concerns: an independent city would need to finance police, schools, a jail, water, and other institutions, while residents of the diverse North Shore could become peripheral inside the new city.

In the episode's debates, Goldfarb's detailed warnings struggled against the secessionists' optimistic language of trust, autonomy, and local control. The source treats that asymmetry as evidence that accurate fiscal information does not automatically defeat an identity-based political project.

## Key Characteristics

- Staten Island attorney and anti-secession organizer.
- Defended continued membership in New York City.
- Emphasized the fiscal and administrative costs of creating a new city.
- Warned that North Shore communities could lose power inside an independent Staten Island.
- Represented an evidence-heavy campaign facing a more emotionally resonant autonomy message.
