# Marissa Valenzuela

Marissa Valenzuela is the founder of [[GildedCoachTeas]] and a caller in [[advice-line-with-curt-richardson-of-otterbox-e94bf774-de11-4434-843b-a551309a6773]]. She asks how to regain momentum after taking a one-year hiatus following the birth of her first child.

The advice from [[CurtRichardson]] and [[GuyRaz]] treats her problem as a restart and retention case. Rather than chasing broad retail immediately, Marissa is encouraged to use consistent weekly action, direct-to-consumer storytelling, repeat-customer outreach, and possible tea-party bundles to prove which parts of the brand still create [[CustomerPull]].
