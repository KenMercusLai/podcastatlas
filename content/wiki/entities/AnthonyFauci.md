# Anthony Fauci

## Overview
Anthony Fauci appears in the All-In Kratsios interview as the episode's main symbol of COVID-era damage to public trust in science. The source records [[MichaelKratsios|Michael Kratsios]] saying Fauci did severe damage to science and using school masking, protest restrictions, hospital-visit restrictions, and anti-science labeling as examples of institutional credibility loss.

## Current Profile
This page is not a full biography of Fauci. Its current wiki profile is source-scoped: Fauci functions as the named figure through whom the episode discusses [[MedicalDogmaTrustRepair|medical dogma trust repair]] and the broader [[ScienceTrustPoliticization|politicization of science trust]].

## Key Characteristics
- Fauci is used as a contested public-health authority figure rather than as a neutral institutional actor in this source.
- The episode associates him with COVID-era certainty, school masking, and perceived inconsistency in public-health restrictions.
- Kratsios's criticism makes Fauci a symbol of why the administration says scientific institutions must protect questioning and dissent.
- The page keeps the source's negative judgment bounded because the transcript does not independently adjudicate pandemic-policy evidence.
