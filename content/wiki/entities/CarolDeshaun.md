# Carol Deshaun

## Overview

Carol Deshaun is the founder of [[Desden]], a licensed collegiate-accessories company focused on clear stadium bags and game-day products for female fans.

## Current Profile

Deshaun anticipated that clear-bag rules would spread from professional football to college campuses and built Desden around that constraint. Her company now combines licensing, in-house work, imported production, ambassadors, pop-ups, and direct sales. The next growth question is less about adding opportunity in the abstract than identifying which end users, licenses, teams, sports, and collaborations already show enough performance to justify added complexity.

## Key Characteristics

- Identified a policy-driven product opportunity before it spread broadly through college venues.
- Built a mixed production system with in-house manufacturing and printing capability.
- Uses campus ambassadors to place products inside student game-day culture.
- Recognizes that the payer and wearer often belong to different generations.
- Is weighing license expansion against the operating burden of the current portfolio.
