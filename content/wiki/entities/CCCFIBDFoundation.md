# CCCF IBD Foundation / CCCF 爱在延长炎症性肠病基金会

## Overview
CCCF 爱在延长炎症性肠病基金会 is presented as a China-based nonprofit serving people with inflammatory bowel disease through training, support, advocacy, and research.

## Current Profile
In the episode, the foundation bridges clinic and daily life. Its work includes professional training, patient education, health kits for newly diagnosed patients, camps for children and adolescents, World IBD Day advocacy, research, fundraising, and a staff that includes people living with IBD. Its stated approach—“education is the best medicine” and “help people help themselves”—frames knowledge and agency as scalable complements to direct financial aid.

## Key Characteristics
- Founded in Hangzhou in 2016 according to the source, initially through donations and volunteer labor.
- Organizes its work around training, patient support, advocacy, and research.
- Includes patients among its full-time staff and uses lived experience in public communication.
- Runs health-kit and youth-camp programs intended to improve information, confidence, nutrition, psychological support, and connection.
- Relies on clinicians, patients, families, donors, medical students, and volunteers rather than treating IBD support as a clinic-only task.

## Qualifications
The foundation's founding date, funding, staff count, project reach, cost comparison, mission, vision, and outcomes come from one 2024 episode and are not independently audited here. The page does not imply that education or peer support replaces qualified medical, nutritional, psychological, or social care.
