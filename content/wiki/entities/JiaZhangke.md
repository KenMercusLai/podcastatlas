# 贾樟柯 / Jia Zhangke

Jia Zhangke is the director interviewed in [[ep277-duihua-jiazhangke-xia-wo-meiyou-beipan-zhenshi-shijie-wo-zhishi-zai-xunzhao-dianying-de-xin-keneng-lqprbtgi7pkch3hj3wxa1q8wovox]]. The episode centers on his defense of AI experimentation: a director associated in the source with attention to the real world can still test AI because responding to reality is not identical to photographing reality in a realist style.

His contribution to the wiki is [[FilmRealityBeyondRealism]]. Jia treats AI as a possible transformation of film production methods, not only a new carrier like digital video. He imagines a future where AI cinema may develop its own world and aesthetics, but he also keeps his own creative boundary clear: his primary interest remains [[LiveActionFilmUnderAI|live-action film]] grounded in locations, real people, camera presence, and captured feeling.
