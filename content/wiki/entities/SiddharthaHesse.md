# 《悉达多》 / Siddhartha

[[SiddharthaHesse|《悉达多》 / Siddhartha]] appears in [[20-demian-gaiyin-de-etou-shang-na-shenmi-de-yinji-567302734]] as the [[HermannHesse|Hesse]] work the hosts compare most directly with [[DemianNovel|《德米安》]]. The episode says 《悉达多》 mixes Western philosophy and Buddhist material around a young man's search for life's truth.

The source uses the book mainly as a contrastive shorthand. If 《悉达多》 is associated with the image of the river, [[DemianNovel|《德米安》]] is associated with the mirror: other people and symbols keep reflecting [[EmilSinclair|辛克莱尔]] back to himself.

## Key Claims
- The source treats 《悉达多》 as part of Hesse's larger spiritual-search branch rather than as the episode's main close-reading object.
- Its river image helps clarify why 《德米安》 works differently: the latter relies on doubled figures, inner mirrors, and symbolic confrontations.
- The comparison links Hesse's Eastern-philosophy interest to [[SelfDiscoveryAgainstMassConformity]] without collapsing the two novels into one lesson.
