# 张范 / Zhang Fan (late Han)

张范 / Zhang Fan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1001-weishenme-quan-ni-buyao-fanzhongnu-lolurkcjpie14nnczk0tu7iqerke|Hanji 1001]] as the recluse Yuan Shu tries to recruit while preparing an imperial claim. Zhang Fan does not personally accept service under [[YuanShu|袁术]], and instead sends his younger brother [[ZhangChengLateHan|张承]] to meet him.

The source does not build a full biography for Zhang Fan. His function is political: refusal to appear keeps a respected scholar from validating Yuan Shu's title project, while Zhang Cheng's later answer makes the family's stance explicit. In that sense, Zhang Fan belongs to the source's [[PoliticalSurvivalFirst|安全第一政治生存]] and [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] field: accepting proximity to an unsupported claimant can itself become endorsement.
