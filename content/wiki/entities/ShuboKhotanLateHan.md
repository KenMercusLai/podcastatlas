## Overview

输僰 / Shubo is the Khotan noble and general in Hanji 838 who kills [[WangJingXiyuLateHan|王敬]] after the killing of [[KhotanKingJianLateHan|于阗王建]], then briefly self-enthrones before Khotanese people kill him. The route uses the Khotan suffix because [[Shubo]] already names an unrelated modern AI-founder page.

## Current Profile

Shubo first appears as the Khotanese military responder after King Jian is beheaded at Wang Jing's banquet. He leads troops against Wang Jing, rejects Wang's claim that the killing was ordered by the Han emperor, goes upstairs, kills Wang, and displays Wang's head.

His own power claim fails immediately. Shubo makes himself king after killing Wang Jing, but the Khotanese people do not accept him. They kill him and install Jian's son [[AnGuoKhotanLateHan|安国]], turning Shubo's counterattack into a short-lived interruption rather than a durable succession.

## Key Characteristics

- Khotan noble and general who reacts militarily to King Jian's killing.
- Rejects Wang Jing's imperial-command claim and kills the Han envoy.
- Publicly displays Wang Jing's head as retaliation and warning.
- Attempts to convert anti-Wang action into personal kingship.
- Fails because Khotanese people reject his self-enthronement and restore Jian's line through Anguo.
