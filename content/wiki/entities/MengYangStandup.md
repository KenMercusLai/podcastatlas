# 梦阳 / 孟阳

## Overview
梦阳 / 孟阳 is a Chinese stand-up comedian, short-video creator, and former auditor and internet-content operator interviewed by [[LiuFei|刘飞]] on [[SanWuHuan|三五环]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Mengyang through a nonlinear career rather than a planned conversion story. She studied accounting at the University of Macau, worked in audit at [[KPMG]], taught IELTS reading, edited and operated finance content at Phoenix Finance, then spent six years at [[Tencent]] across finance content, mid-length video, and [[TencentWeishi|腾讯微视]]. After a short education-business attempt, she moved toward stand-up and short video.

Her current creative identity rests on direct feedback and sustained practice. Stand-up lets her perform her own stories and viewpoints, while laughter supplies an immediate signal that she found difficult to locate in large-platform work. Short video began as a way to direct people toward live shows but became a second creative form, with compact premise-setting feeding deeper stage material.

The profile also preserves mixed motives. Mengyang accepts that she likes creation and stand-up while also wanting recognition and a respectable form of visibility; the source treats that admission as more stable than a purified artist identity. Her commitment is practical rather than absolute: she imagines lowering living costs to remain in comedy if income falls.

## Key Characteristics
- She combines audit, newsroom-style content operations, large-platform management, short video, and live comedy experience.
- She describes herself as low-energy and introverted despite being able to perform publicly with high expressive intensity.
- She treats direct audience laughter as a clearer occupational signal than diffuse contribution inside a large platform.
- She links sustained output to creative discovery because prediction cannot reliably identify which idea will work.
- She recognizes that corporate risk-review and ROI habits can become self-defeating when applied to every personal experiment.
- She accepts mixed motives around art, recognition, income, and “体面地红” without treating impurity as disqualification.
- She frames long-term commitment partly as willingness to redesign living costs around the work.
