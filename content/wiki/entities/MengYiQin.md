# 蒙毅 / Meng Yi (Qin)

## Overview
蒙毅 / Meng Yi is [[MengTian|蒙恬]]'s brother and a trusted central official under [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]]. In this source cluster, his importance lies less in action during the succession crisis than in forced absence from it: Qin Shi Huang sends him on a ritual mission while gravely ill, leaving the final-tour court without a Meng-family counterweight.

## Current Profile
Meng Yi combines central trust, legal authority, and factional danger. He had earlier sentenced [[ZhaoGao|赵高]] to death before Qin Shi Huang pardoned Zhao Gao, giving Zhao Gao a direct reason to fear a Fusu-Meng succession. During the final illness, Qin Shi Huang sends Meng Yi to conduct a sacrifice for relief from illness. Later sources treat that absence as one enabling condition for death concealment and succession manipulation, and they follow Zhao Gao's revenge through Meng Yi's arrest and killing under Hu Hai.

## Key Characteristics
- Meng Yi is a trusted Qin central official with influence complementary to Meng Tian's frontier command.
- His earlier judgment against Zhao Gao creates a personal survival conflict inside the succession struggle.
- His ritual mission removes him from the immediate deathbed and document-control circle.
- He functions as a missing institutional counterweight rather than an active coup participant.
- His later killing shows the Hu Hai-Zhao Gao regime eliminating both central and military sides of the Meng family.

## Evidence
- Ritual removal from the deathbed circle: [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-127-1-jiemi-qinshihuang-weili-chujun-shi-shenme-yuanyin-lniz32t2r2d3thgqnbfarrqeq2a2|Qinji 127-1]] says Qin Shi Huang sends Meng Yi away to conduct a distant sacrifice after becoming seriously ill.
- Absence and Zhao Gao's motive: [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-127-2-lisi-weisha-bu-bang-yingming-de-fusu-lq-vny5duwwzfubpeijr21oazxbg|Qinji 127-2]] connects Meng Yi's absence and earlier death sentence against Zhao Gao to the coup's opportunity and threat structure.
- Variant-sensitive aftermath: [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-127-3-haiwai-huigui-yipi-zhujian-jiekai-huhai-lidi-zhi-mi-lja0pankcx4ugtylbxxqkwuavdnw|Qinji 127-3]] preserves Meng Yi as Zhao Gao's target even while reweighting the initial accession under the Zhao Zheng Shu version.
- Final purge: [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-127-4-qinshihuangling-de-jingtian-mimi-lhgwr2of7zcmg2ggbcdzragwstyi|Qinji 127-4]] records Ziying's failed warning and the deaths of both Meng brothers.

## Qualifications
The sources make Meng Yi's absence consequential, but they do not prove that his presence would have prevented manipulation. The exact nature and location of the ritual mission are filtered through the episode's interpretation, and the Zhao Zheng Shu variant changes the initial accession story without removing the later revenge and purge.

## What Changed
- Added the final-illness context in which Qin Shi Huang personally sends Meng Yi away for a healing sacrifice.
- Refined Meng Yi's absence from a background fact into a timed loss of counterweight before the letter and seal bottleneck forms.
- Kept the counterfactual boundary explicit: absence enabled action but does not prove Meng Yi could have stopped it.

## Relationships
- [[MengTian|蒙恬]] - brother and frontier half of the Meng family's combined influence.
- [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] - ruler who trusts Meng Yi, pardons Zhao Gao, and dispatches the ritual mission.
- [[ZhaoGao|赵高]] - former defendant whose fear and resentment make Meng Yi a target.
- [[FuSu|扶苏]] - heir alternative whose accession would likely strengthen the Meng family.
- [[ImperialDeathConcealment|秘不发丧式过渡控制]] - later transition mechanism made easier by Meng Yi's absence.
