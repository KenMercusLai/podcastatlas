# Seema Sanghavi

## Overview
Seema Sanghavi is the founder-caller behind [[CooksWhoFeed]], asking how a mission-led kitchen-linen company can persuade B2B customers to switch from existing vendors.

## Current Profile
In the episode, Seema calls from the Niagara region outside Toronto and describes Cooks Who Feed as a for-profit social enterprise that combines ethically made linens, customization, meal give-back, and jobs for women in villages near its production facility north of Delhi. Her B2B pitch has already reached restaurant groups, Celebrity Cruises, culinary schools, and a Relais & Châteaux location in Canada.

Her strongest sales insight is that the client should be the hero. The Celebrity Cruises example works because Cooks Who Feed helps the buyer create aprons from recycled plastic bottles with a meal give-back story, making the buyer's own sustainability and impact narrative more credible.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder of a social enterprise linking kitchen linens, meals, and fair-work production.
- Focused on B2B growth through restaurants, hospitality, cruise lines, culinary schools, and aligned organizations.
- Frames customization and impact as part of the customer's own brand story.
- Needs stronger referral and pilot mechanisms to reduce vendor-switching risk.
