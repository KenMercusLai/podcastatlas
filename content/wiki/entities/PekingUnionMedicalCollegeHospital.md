# 北京协和医院 / Peking Union Medical College Hospital

## Overview
北京协和医院 / Peking Union Medical College Hospital is the clinical affiliation stated for gastroenterologist [[LiJingnan|李景南]] and intensive-care nurse [[HeZhaokaiNurse|何昭凯]] in two [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episodes.

## Current Profile
The bounded sources present the hospital through clinicians rather than as a complete institutional history. One episode locates specialist inflammatory-bowel-disease education and shared decisions there; the other locates intensive-care nursing and an effort, described by the guest, to connect hospital, community, and home hospice services. Together they show a tertiary clinical setting contributing both disease-directed specialist care and comfort-focused end-of-life discussion, without establishing the current scope or availability of any institutional program.
