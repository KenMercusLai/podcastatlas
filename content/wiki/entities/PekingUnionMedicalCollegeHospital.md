# 北京协和医院 / Peking Union Medical College Hospital

## Overview
北京协和医院 / Peking Union Medical College Hospital is the clinical affiliation stated for gastroenterologist [[LiJingnan|李景南]], intensive-care nurse [[HeZhaokaiNurse|何昭凯]], and neurologist [[TanYingNeurologist|谭颖]] in three [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episodes.

## Current Profile
The bounded sources present the hospital through clinicians rather than as a complete institutional history. One episode locates specialist inflammatory-bowel-disease education and shared decisions there; another locates intensive-care nursing and a source-described effort to connect hospital, community, and home hospice services. The myasthenia-gravis episode adds neurologic and neuroimmune public education about disease mechanism, treatment horizons, pregnancy planning, longitudinal monitoring, and efficacy-safety tradeoffs. Together they show a tertiary clinical setting represented across disease-directed specialist care, chronic management, nursing, and comfort-focused end-of-life discussion without establishing the current scope or availability of any institutional program.

## Key Characteristics
- Tertiary hospital affiliation for specialists appearing in public medical-education episodes.
- Clinical context for gastroenterology and inflammatory-bowel-disease management.
- Clinical context for neurology, autoimmune neuromuscular disease, and long-term treatment management.
- Clinical context for intensive-care nursing and repeated end-of-life encounters.
- Source-described participant in hospital-community-home hospice coordination.

## Qualifications

The hospice-network description remains source-scoped to the 2023 discussion, while the myasthenia-gravis statements remain source-scoped public education from a 2025 episode rather than institutional guidance.
