## Overview

宋弘 / Song Hong is an Eastern Han official used in Hanji 878 as the positive comparison to Yang Qiao's fatal coerced-marriage case.

## Current Profile

The current page is source-bounded to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-878-bu-shou-yueshu-de-quanli-hairen-buqian-lvevnahcxshgbz5zyqbaf36pa7nk|Hanji 878]]. The episode says [[LiuXiu|刘秀 / 光武帝]] once considered a princess marriage involving Song Hong and indirectly tested his position. Song answered with the moral premise that old companions and the wife of hardship should not be abandoned, and Liu Xiu withdrew rather than forcing the match.

Song's profile therefore functions mainly as a restraint benchmark. He is not the central late-Han actor in the episode, but his answer helps the source separate imperial suggestion from imperial coercion: a ruler may raise or test a marriage possibility, but the minister's existing obligation remains a real boundary.

## Key Characteristics

- Song Hong is presented as a loyal existing-husband figure in the "糟糠之妻" moral frame.
- His answer gives Liu Xiu a clear reason to withdraw from a possible princess marriage.
- His case contrasts with Yang Qiao by showing that imperial marriage interest need not become coercion.

## Evidence

- Restraint comparison: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-878-bu-shou-yueshu-de-quanli-hairen-buqian-lvevnahcxshgbz5zyqbaf36pa7nk|Hanji 878]] uses Song Hong's case to contrast Liu Xiu's restraint with Emperor Huan's pressure on Yang Qiao.
- Existing obligation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-878-bu-shou-yueshu-de-quanli-hairen-buqian-lvevnahcxshgbz5zyqbaf36pa7nk|Hanji 878]] frames Song's answer around not abandoning the wife who shared hardship.
- Non-coercive outcome: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-878-bu-shou-yueshu-de-quanli-hairen-buqian-lvevnahcxshgbz5zyqbaf36pa7nk|Hanji 878]] says Liu Xiu does not press the marriage after Song's answer.

## Qualifications

The current page should not treat Song Hong as a full biography or as a broader Eastern Han policy actor. His bounded role here is a comparison case for imperial restraint around marriage.
