# 归有光 / Gui Youguang

Gui Youguang is the author of [[XiangJiXuanZhi|《项脊轩志》]] in [[164-guwen-de-liliang-qing-shou-xia-zhe-fen-qiannian-wei-bian-de-shenqing-dongjian-he-yongqi-940236576]]. The episode presents him as a writer whose apparently small domestic details preserve family rupture, examination pressure, youthful ambition, and devastating marital grief.

The source resists turning Gui Youguang into either a boring textbook writer or an internet caricature. His small room, grandmother's hopes, late success in the examination system, and memory of his wife are treated as one life-world where [[RestrainedEmotionInClassicalWriting]] carries more force than explicit confession.
