## Overview

霍去病 / Huo Qubing is synthesized here through two downstream effects of his Western Han campaigns: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-402-2-shudiwushu-de-kuli-duzhou-weihe-zuizhong-deyi-shanzhong-lqrunv51se0w7_2fi2ifs_f7zub4|Hanji 402-2]] links his attack on Xiongnu's left territory to later Wuhuan relocation and protector-office arrangements, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-376-2-cong-liulanger-nixi-cheng-jiangjun-jiemi-hanjiang-zhaoponu-lszmnylwk8zxpjhelkd1gd6jsfsd|Hanji 376-2]] places [[ZhaoPonuWesternHan|赵破奴]]'s rise and early merit inside Huo's victorious military era.

## Current Profile

The current page remains narrow, but it now shows both institutional and personnel legacy. Hanji 402-2 uses Huo's campaign as the hinge between Han Wudi-era anti-[[Xiongnu|匈奴]] warfare and northeastern frontier administration: after Xiongnu's left territory is broken, Wuhuan groups are moved near five commanderies and tasked with watching Xiongnu under Han supervision. Hanji 376-2 adds a career-memory layer by presenting Zhao Ponu as a Huo-aligned officer whose early victories belong to that high-success phase and whose later reversals mirror the changed conditions after it.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han general associated here with a successful campaign against Xiongnu's left territory.
- Upstream actor in the Wuhuan relocation and protector-office origin story.
- Patronage and campaign context for Zhao Ponu's rise in the episode's reconstruction.
- Source-scoped figure whose wider military biography remains to be built from fuller notes.

## Qualifications

This page is not a full biography. It preserves only two podcast-source uses of Huo Qubing: campaign background for Wuhuan frontier administration and the military lineage through which Zhao Ponu's rise is interpreted. Hanji 376-2 does not document a complete formal patron-client relationship or Huo's wider career.
