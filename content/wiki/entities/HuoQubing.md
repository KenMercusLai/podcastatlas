## Overview

霍去病 / Huo Qubing enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-402-2-shudiwushu-de-kuli-duzhou-weihe-zuizhong-deyi-shanzhong-lqrunv51se0w7_2fi2ifs_f7zub4|Hanji 402-2]] as the Western Han general whose attack on Xiongnu's left territory creates the background for Han's later Wuhuan relocation and protector-office arrangement.

## Current Profile

The current page is intentionally narrow. Hanji 402-2 does not reconstruct Huo Qubing's career, but it uses his campaign as the hinge between Han Wudi-era anti-[[Xiongnu|匈奴]] warfare and northeastern frontier administration: after Xiongnu's left territory is broken, Wuhuan groups are moved near five commanderies and tasked with watching Xiongnu under Han supervision.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han general associated here with a successful campaign against Xiongnu's left territory.
- Upstream actor in the Wuhuan relocation and protector-office origin story.
- Source-scoped figure whose wider military biography remains to be built from later or earlier notes.
