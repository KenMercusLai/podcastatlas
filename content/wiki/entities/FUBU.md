# FUBU

## Overview
FUBU is the consumer brand founded by [[DaymondJohn]] and used in the Daymond John Advice Line episode as operating context for founder story, small-retailer support, and customer-facing product proof.

## Current Profile
The episode does not retell FUBU's full company history. It uses FUBU in two narrower ways: as Daymond John's founder credential and as an example of how small stores can help build a brand by explaining the founders and product quality directly to customers. In that role, FUBU becomes evidence for a staged, relationship-heavy retail approach rather than merely a famous-name reference.

## Key Characteristics
- Functions as [[DaymondJohn]]'s founder-history credential in the Advice Line format.
- Serves as a small-retailer brand-building example for [[SaborAMexicoSalsas]].
- Connects founder story to retail explanation, not only media or packaging.
- Anchors Daymond's advice that early distribution should preserve learning and customer trust.

- Small-store proof: [[advice-line-with-daymond-john-of-fubu-0961848f-0f5a-4fb7-932c-3a04f08bc8d4]] says small stores helped FUBU by telling customers about the founders and product quality.

## Qualifications
