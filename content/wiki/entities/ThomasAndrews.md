# Thomas Andrews

## Overview

Thomas Andrews was the [[RMSTitanic|Titanic]] designer who inspected its service environment before disaster and diagnosed the collision damage that made sinking inevitable.

## Current Profile

The two episodes present Andrews on both sides of Titanic's transformation from prestige product to terminal emergency. Before the collision, he and [[BruceIsmay]] inspected and refined design and service standards within the ship's hotel-like environment. After inspecting the flooding, Andrews judged that six forward compartments had been breached and that water would pass over bulkheads that did not extend high enough to contain it. His estimate of perhaps two hours transformed an apparently modest collision into a terminal emergency, although that knowledge could not eliminate disbelief, communication delay, limited boat capacity, or inconsistent evacuation practice.

## Key Characteristics

- Ship designer with detailed knowledge of Titanic's compartment system.
- Pre-disaster inspector concerned with design and service standards aboard the new liner.
- Early technical interpreter of damage that many aboard did not initially perceive as catastrophic.
- Concluded that flooding exceeded the vessel's survivable compartment limit.
- Estimated that the ship had at most roughly two hours remaining.
- Figure whose diagnosis exposed the gap between expert knowledge and coordinated emergency action.

## Evidence

- Pre-disaster inspection: [[429-titanic-countdown-to-disaster-part-3-glt1273657377]] describes Andrews and Ismay inspecting and refining Titanic's design and service.
- Damage diagnosis: [[431-titanic-nightmare-at-midnight-part-5-glt2941711597]] says Andrews identified six breached forward compartments and progressive overflow across insufficiently high bulkheads.
- Time horizon: [[431-titanic-nightmare-at-midnight-part-5-glt2941711597]] attributes to Andrews an estimate of no more than about two hours before sinking.
- Operational significance: [[431-titanic-nightmare-at-midnight-part-5-glt2941711597]] contrasts his diagnosis with continued reassurance, passenger disbelief, and delayed evacuation.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to two retrospective episodes rather than a full biography or naval-architecture assessment. The pre-voyage division of responsibility, exact inspection sequence, damage geometry, reported wording, and time estimate are presented through the episodes and are not independently established here.
