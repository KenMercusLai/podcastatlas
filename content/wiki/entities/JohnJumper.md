# John Jumper

## Overview
John Jumper is an AlphaFold researcher whose wiki profile links DeepMind's AI-for-science achievement to newer questions about whether top scientific-AI talent remains inside Google DeepMind.

## Current Profile
The earlier source presents Jumper through [[AlphaFold]], protein-structure prediction, and DeepMind's claim that AI can change scientific discovery. In that frame, he is evidence that DeepMind's culture could produce practical scientific breakthroughs rather than only game-playing demonstrations.

The newest source uses Jumper as part of a talent-movement signal. It says he later left Google DeepMind and treats that exit alongside [[DavidSilver]] and [[JeffDean]] as evidence that Google DeepMind's scientist-led culture, Gemini pressure, and large-company operating model may have become misaligned. The motive and exact causality remain source-scoped.
