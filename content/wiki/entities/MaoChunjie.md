# 毛春杰 / Mao Chunjie

## Overview
毛春杰 / Mao Chunjie is the ophthalmology educator in four [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] sources, covering common eye care, systemic and fundus disease, myopia boundaries, refractive-surgery selection, inherited macular disease, medical blindness, color vision, and new-onset floaters after infection.

## Current Profile
VOL.36 describes Mao as a deputy chief physician in ophthalmology at [[TianjinGeneralHospital|天津总医院]], with more than ten years of ophthalmology work. He connects common eye symptoms to whole-body disease, screening, surgical limits, and careful medication use: diabetes and vascular risk can damage the fundus, dry eye needs mechanism-aware chronic management, clearer vision after myopia surgery does not erase high-myopia retinal risk, and red eye should not trigger indiscriminate drops.

VOL.119 extends the profile into inherited retinal disease and functional vision. Mao distinguishes total absence of sight from acuity- or field-based medical blindness, explains retinal pigment epithelium, cone, and rod roles, describes juvenile inherited macular degeneration and research directions cautiously, and treats color-vision deficiency as a varied congenital or acquired category rather than one uniform incapacity.

VOL.56 adds a graduation-season refractive-surgery decision branch. Mao frames surgery as elective improvement after adulthood, relative prescription stability, examination, and a real personal need; he distinguishes full- and half-femtosecond approaches through incision, flap, dry-eye, trauma, prescription, and occupational context while keeping the final choice individualized.

VOL.51 adds a narrow post-infection symptom branch. Mao distinguishes pre-existing or myopia-associated floaters from a first new episode that could reflect vitreous change or intraocular inflammation and therefore merits examination rather than reassurance from the symptom label alone.

## Key Characteristics
- Ophthalmology clinician voice tied in VOL.36 to [[TianjinGeneralHospital|天津总医院]].
- Connects eye symptoms and retinal findings to systemic disease, screening, and cross-specialty assessment.
- Separates optical correction and symptom relief from elimination of underlying disease risk.
- Explains inherited macular disease through retinal structure, function, progression, and research boundaries.
- Distinguishes acuity, visual field, central vision, light sensitivity, and color discrimination as different dimensions of function.
- Keeps eye drops, surgery, supplements, protective behavior, first-onset floaters, and experimental treatment inside qualified-care boundaries.
- Treats refractive-surgery timing and method as candidacy decisions rather than a simple degree or technology ranking.

## Qualifications
The wiki has no independent biography, credential record, guideline, or publication review beyond the podcast-derived sources. All four episodes are public education rather than individualized ophthalmology advice. VOL.119 renders the name as 毛春洁, while VOL.36, VOL.51, VOL.56, and the established canonical page use 毛春杰; shared show and specialty context support treating this as an orthographic discrepancy, but the exact character is not independently verified here. Age, prescription-change, anisometropia, astigmatism, recovery, surgical-method, prevalence, inheritance, screening, post-infection inflammation, and treatment-research claims remain source-scoped.
