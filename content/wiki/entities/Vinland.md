## Overview

Vinland is the name used in Norse saga tradition for a North American expedition zone reached around the year 1000.

## Current Profile

[[103-the-norse-sagas-glt8280648151]] situates Vinland south of saga regions called Helluland and Markland and links its voyages mainly to Eric the Red's children. Archaeology at [[LAnseAuxMeadows|L'Anse aux Meadows]] confirms a Norse presence in Newfoundland and supports the broad westward-travel outline, but it does not fix every saga place-name, route, encounter, or southern extent.

## Key Characteristics

- Belongs to the saga geography of Norse travel west of Greenland.
- Associated with expeditions around the year 1000 rather than lasting colonization.
- Has a material anchor in the temporary Norse site at L'Anse aux Meadows.
- Remains geographically broader and less certain than the archaeological site alone.

## Evidence

- Saga geography: [[103-the-norse-sagas-glt8280648151]] distinguishes Helluland, Markland, and Vinland as rocky, forested, and more southerly zones.
- Material corroboration: [[103-the-norse-sagas-glt8280648151]] uses L'Anse aux Meadows to support Norse overwintering, ship repair, and possible onward travel.
- Limits of settlement: [[103-the-norse-sagas-glt8280648151]] describes the Newfoundland presence as temporary and reports saga conflict with Indigenous peoples as one reason voyages ended.

## Qualifications

The episode does not settle the full location or boundaries of Vinland. Saga place correspondence, motives, encounters, and the reach of further journeys remain uncertain or source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Initial profile created by joining saga geography to bounded archaeological corroboration.
