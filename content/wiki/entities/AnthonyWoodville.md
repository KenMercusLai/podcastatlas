# Anthony Woodville

## Overview

Anthony Woodville, Earl Rivers, was Elizabeth Woodville's brother and Edward V's governor, combining learning, chivalric reputation, and royal trust with exposure as a leading member of the Woodville affinity.

## Current Profile

The episode resists treating Anthony as merely a grasping royal relative. It presents him as athletic, learned, connected to early English printing, and trusted with Edward V's disciplined Ludlow household. Those strengths could not be separated from faction: custody of the future king made Anthony useful to Edward IV and threatening to magnates who feared durable Woodville control after the king's death.

## Key Characteristics

- Brother of [[ElizabethWoodville]] and a leading beneficiary of her marriage to Edward IV.
- Chivalric figure associated with a celebrated tournament against the Bastard of Burgundy.
- Learned patron connected with William Caxton and early English print culture.
- Governor of [[EdwardV]] at Ludlow and therefore a central holder of access to the heir.
- Accomplished courtier whose family identity made his power factionally vulnerable.

## Qualifications

The source offers a favorable compressed portrait rather than a full biography. It does not independently measure Anthony's influence, establish that Edward IV intended him as a deliberate counterweight to the king's brothers, or cover his arrest and execution in the depth supplied by Part 2.
