# 《几何原本》 / Chinese Euclid's Elements

《几何原本》 enters the wiki through [[ep259-yu-miao-shifu-mantan-xu-guangqi-sibainian-qian-ruhe-rangren-biancongming-lkirczxnx8skbbfpmz5-m-mivs4e]] as the translated mathematical work that makes [[XuGuangqi|徐光启]]'s intellectual transformation concrete. The source emphasizes the collaboration with [[MatteoRicci|利玛窦]] and says Xu helped translate terms such as point, line, plane, geometry, and parallel.

The episode's main point is not that the early volumes were unreadable. Instead, it says the difficulty lay in a system of definitions, postulates, proofs, and consequences. That makes the work the anchor for [[EuclideanProofTraining|欧几里得式证明训练]] and a contrast with a Chinese mathematical tradition described in the episode as more focused on executable algorithms and problem solving.

The source also treats the translation history as evidence of discontinuity. The first six volumes were translated early, while later volumes waited about 250 years before completion by Li Shanlan, making the work a marker of both brilliant encounter and fragile institutional uptake.
