# Claire Clairmont / 克莱尔·克莱尔蒙特

## Overview

Claire Clairmont was [[MaryShelley|Mary Shelley / 玛丽·雪莱]]'s stepsister, a participant in the Shelley travel network, a crucial connector to [[LordByron|Lord Byron / 拜伦]], and the mother of [[AllegraByron|Allegra Byron]].

## Current Profile

The Frankenstein source presents Clairmont as the practical link that drew Mary and [[PercyShelley|Percy Shelley / 珀西·雪莱]] toward Byron and the [[VillaDiodati|Villa Diodati / 迪奥达蒂别墅]] circle. Her role shows how literary history arose from travel, desire, family arrangements, weather, and unequal social relationships rather than isolated genius.

The Byron episode makes the cost of that link more explicit. Claire arrived at Geneva pregnant by Byron; he kept her at emotional distance, while their daughter was first called Alba by the Shelleys and renamed Allegra by Byron. The compressed account establishes parentage and separation but does not fully represent Claire's own view of the decisions governing her daughter's life.

## Key Characteristics

- Stepsister and travel companion within Mary Shelley's family network.
- Intimate connection who helped bring the Shelley circle into contact with Byron.
- Pregnant participant in the socially tense Geneva gathering.
- Mother of Allegra Byron.
- Person whose connective literary-history role came with unequal emotional and parental consequences.

## Evidence

- Travel and Geneva connection: [[141-zhizao-fulankensitan-kehuan-zhimu-he-tade-shenqi-shidai-857923931]] links Clairmont to the Shelleys' flight and the Villa Diodati gathering.
- Pregnancy and Byron's distance: [[443-lord-byron-death-of-a-vampire-part-4-glt3247503776]] describes her pregnancy at Lake Geneva and Byron's refusal of emotional intimacy.
- Allegra: [[443-lord-byron-death-of-a-vampire-part-4-glt3247503776]] identifies Claire as Allegra's mother and records the child's initial and later names.

## Qualifications

Both sources approach Clairmont primarily through more famous writers. Her own voice, later life, and the full custody and care history of Allegra require dedicated evidence.
