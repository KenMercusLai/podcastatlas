# 霍峻 / Huo Jun

## Overview
霍峻 / Huo Jun is a [[LiuBei|刘备]] officer whom Hanji 1147 presents as the defender of Jiameng, a northern Yizhou strongpoint between Hanzhong and the Sichuan basin.

## Current Profile
The current evidence is a single popular-history episode. It portrays Huo Jun as an entrusted garrison commander who refuses an inducement associated with [[ZhangLu|张鲁]], holds Jiameng with several hundred soldiers against a much larger force for nearly a year, and converts defense into a successful counterattack. Liu Bei's later appointment of him as Zitong administrator is presented as recognition that holding the city protected the northern approach during the conquest of Yizhou.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats control of the entrusted city as non-negotiable even when his own life is threatened.
- Sustains a small garrison through a prolonged siege by a substantially larger force.
- Waits for an opportunity to counterattack rather than reducing defense to passive endurance.
- Preserves a strategic link between Hanzhong and Liu Bei's developing position in Yizhou.
- Moves from wartime strongpoint command to post-conquest territorial administration.

## Evidence
- Refusal of inducement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1147-zhongyong-wushuang-hunshen-shidan-zhaozilong-zhuan-lpesotvhdf7zmkqtuv90rdny1kgz|Hanji 1147]] reports that Huo Jun answers Yang Ang's approach with “小人头可得，城不可得.”
- Siege command: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1147-zhongyong-wushuang-hunshen-shidan-zhaozilong-zhuan-lpesotvhdf7zmkqtuv90rdny1kgz|Hanji 1147]] says several hundred defenders hold Jiameng against Fu Jin and Xiang Cun's force for nearly a year.
- Counterattack: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1147-zhongyong-wushuang-hunshen-shidan-zhaozilong-zhuan-lpesotvhdf7zmkqtuv90rdny1kgz|Hanji 1147]] credits Huo Jun with exploiting an opening and killing Xiang Cun.
- Strategic and administrative value: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1147-zhongyong-wushuang-hunshen-shidan-zhaozilong-zhuan-lpesotvhdf7zmkqtuv90rdny1kgz|Hanji 1147]] describes Jiameng as a northern support point and says Liu Bei later appoints Huo Jun administrator of the newly established Zitong Commandery.

## Qualifications
The supplied source is a structured summary, not a verified transcript or primary text. The force totals, siege duration, inducement dialogue, identities and roles of Fu Jin, Xiang Cun, and Yang Ang, exact geography, office sequence, funeral conduct, and account of Huo Jun's son require broader textual verification. Tactical success does not by itself establish every moral or psychological inference attached to it.

## What Changed
- Established the first canonical profile for Huo Jun.
- Identified the combination of inducement refusal, siege endurance, counterattack timing, and later administration as the current evidence-backed profile.

## Relationships
- [[LiuBei|刘备]] - ruler who entrusts Jiameng to Huo Jun and later appoints him to Zitong.
- [[ZhangLu|张鲁]] - Hanzhong ruler whose subordinate reportedly tries to obtain the city through inducement.
- [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] - conquest and governance field in which Jiameng has strategic value.
- [[ZhaoYun|赵云]] - fellow Liu Bei officer paired by the episode as a model of active loyalty and judgment.
