# Darfur

Darfur is the western Sudanese region that [[outsider-chance-ending-sudans-proxy-war-6a75b3695d765781a2b1b132]] identifies as the strongest remaining territorial base for the [[RapidSupportForces|RSF]]. The episode contrasts RSF control in much of the west with the [[SudaneseArmedForces|SAF]]'s control of [[Khartoum]] and much of northern and eastern [[Sudan]].

In the source, Darfur helps explain why the war is not over after the capital's recapture. Control of the west, displaced populations, and the possible reconnection of fronts through [[ElObeid]] keep Sudan inside a continuing [[ForeignBackedCivilWar]].
