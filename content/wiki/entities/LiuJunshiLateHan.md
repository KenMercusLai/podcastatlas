## Overview
刘君世 / Liu Junshi is the late-Han Yongchang administrator whose gold patterned snake gift becomes the corruption signal in Hanji 826. His action matters less as an isolated bribe than as the trigger for [[ZhongHaoLateHan|种暠]]'s report and [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]]'s later resentment.

## Current Profile
Liu Junshi's current profile is bounded to the 145 CE account. He casts a gold snake with patterned scales and presents it to Liang Ji, turning provincial office into a private access transaction.

Zhong Hao, then Yizhou inspector, reports the gift upward. The report threatens Liang Ji's prestige and links Liu Junshi to the wider pattern where corruption exposure becomes retaliation risk for the official who reports it.

## Key Characteristics
- Yongchang administrator using a precious object as a Liang Ji access gift.
- Corruption signal rather than a developed biographical actor in the current wiki.
- Trigger for Zhong Hao's anti-corruption report as Yizhou inspector.
- Early example of Liang Ji's court resentment being generated by exposure of private tribute.
