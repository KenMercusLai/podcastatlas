# 西周国 / Western Zhou Polity

西周国 / Western Zhou polity appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]] as the counterpart to [[EasternZhouPolity|东周国]] after the diminished [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]] domain splits internally. The source mentions it to explain why a 353 BCE attack on "东周" refers to a small Zhou polity rather than the entire Eastern Zhou era.

Its wiki role is contextual. The episode's main action lies with [[QiState|齐国]], [[WeiState|魏国]], and [[ZhaoState|赵国]], but the East-West Zhou split shows the same Warring States pressure from another angle: the royal house still structures the chronology while its own practical territory has become narrow and divided.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-76-shui-geile-chuqingxiangwang-de-zixin-lsut4cfic7xbbrmbmimrruf7n6y0]] adds the late-Zhou side of the distinction. The episode says [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]] is in Western Zhou and that the Son of Heaven's ritual vessels are also there, while [[DongzhouWugong|东周武公]] acts as envoy to [[ChuState|楚国]]. The point is to keep Western Zhou polity separate from the earlier Western Zhou dynasty period.
