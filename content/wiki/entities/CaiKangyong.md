# 蔡康永 / Cai Kangyong

## Overview
蔡康永 / Cai Kangyong is represented here through a long [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] interview as a host, writer, and public figure whose calm is an achieved stance rather than simple temperamental softness. The source presents him through [[KangxiLaile|《康熙来了》]], emotional-advice writing, written-art exhibitions, AI-era creative planning, and unusually direct reflection on aging, grief, wills, funerals, and death.

## Current Profile
Cai's public profile in the current evidence is organized around gentle precision. He does not treat visibility as an unquestioned good: after leaving regular programs, he describes a willingness not to disturb audiences and not to be disturbed by constant public response. This retreat does not read as disappearance so much as a chosen lowering of public demand.

His hosting profile centers on curiosity and service. He values interviews when guests say something that surprises even themselves, and the episode treats [[KangxiLaile|《康熙来了》]] as evidence that a durable program can emerge from talent, pairing, era, permissive scale, and accidental fit rather than from a reproducible formula.

His current creative profile is format-shifting. He is less interested in writing another advice book and more interested in a long fantasy story, written-art works, and possible AI-assisted routes toward comics or film. AI is therefore not only a threat to authorship; in this source it also lowers the burden of moving a world across media.

His death-facing profile is the strongest new contribution. Cai speaks about wills, last wishes, social-account closure, burial choices, funerals, gravestones, long-lived loneliness, and the word "死" without turning the discussion into either gloom or inspirational avoidance. His position is that meaning happens while living, and that farewell is a basic human action worth practicing before the final one.

## Key Characteristics
- Host whose interview ideal is helping guests discover and articulate something they did not know they would say.
- Public figure willing to withdraw from constant visibility without treating reduced exposure as failure.
- Creator shifting from advice-book authority toward fantasy, text-based visual art, and AI-assisted media possibilities.
- Interpreter of grief who favors personally created ritual over generic consolation.
- Death-facing thinker who normalizes wills, last wishes, funerals, gravestones, and direct language around death.
- Key [[KangxiLaile|《康熙来了》]] figure whose partnership with [[XiaoS|小S]] is treated as talented, era-bound, and difficult to reproduce.
- Source-scoped profile; this page does not yet cover Cai's full publishing, film, television, or public-speaking career.
