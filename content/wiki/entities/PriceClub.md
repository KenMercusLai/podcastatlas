# Price Club

Price Club is the warehouse-club retailer founded by [[SolPrice]] and [[RobertPrice]] after [[FedMart]] in [[costco-1]]. It originally targeted small businesses that could buy inventory directly from a warehouse, then expanded consumer demand through a credit-union group membership arrangement.

The episode treats Price Club as the direct prototype for [[Costco]]. Its limited SKU count, pallet handling, supplier-direct delivery, customer pickup, membership economics, hot-dog traffic, and [[NegativeCashConversionCycle]] created the operating system [[JimSinegal]] later scaled.

Price Club merged with Costco in 1993 to form [[PriceCostco]], helping the combined company compete with [[SamsClub|Sam's Club]] in the warehouse-club market.
