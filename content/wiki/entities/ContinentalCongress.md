# Continental Congress

## Overview

The Continental Congress appears in the Franklin episode as the revolutionary institution Benjamin Franklin joined after returning from Britain in 1775.

## Current Profile

The source treats the Continental Congress as the working body that converted Franklin's break with Britain into institutional service. Franklin becomes a Pennsylvania delegate, serves on committees for postal and military supply work, helps with the Olive Branch Petition, and joins the committee that produced the [[DeclarationOfIndependence|Declaration of Independence]]. The Congress is therefore where Franklin's private conclusion about independence becomes public founding labor.

## Key Characteristics

- Revolutionary institution that received Franklin after his return from Britain.
- Committee-driven body handling practical wartime needs as well as political texts.
- Setting for Franklin's Declaration committee work.
- Bridge between [[ColonialLoyaltyRupture|colonial loyalty rupture]] and organized independence.

## Evidence

- Delegate role: [[684-franklin-revenge-of-the-american-genius-part-2-glt6013835340]] says Franklin was chosen as a Pennsylvania delegate soon after reaching Philadelphia in May 1775.
- Practical committees: [[684-franklin-revenge-of-the-american-genius-part-2-glt6013835340]] says Franklin served on many committees, including postal and military-supply work.
- Declaration work: [[684-franklin-revenge-of-the-american-genius-part-2-glt6013835340]] says Franklin was on the committee that drafted the Declaration.
