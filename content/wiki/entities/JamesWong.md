## Overview

黄霑 / James Wong is the songwriter-composer associated with 《倩女幽魂》, 《黎明不要来》, and 《道道道》 in the film series context.

## Current Profile

The source treats Wong's music as structural. The songs align with Ning Caichen, Nie Xiaoqian, and Yan Chixia, giving wandering, forbidden longing, and philosophical struggle distinct voices. His account of ghost stories as tragic beauty rather than mere fear defines the film's emotional priority.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses song to differentiate character perspectives.
- Helps turn supernatural romance into cultural memory.
- Connects genre entertainment with a philosophical voice.
