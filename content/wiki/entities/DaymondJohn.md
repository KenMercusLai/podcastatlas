# Daymond John

## Overview
Daymond John is the founder of [[FUBU]] and a business-advice guest on [[HowIBuiltThis]], where he evaluates early-stage founders through story, customer value, numbers, and operating discipline.

## Current Profile
In the Daymond John Advice Line episode, Daymond's advice style is practical rather than abstract. He begins with whether he can reduce costs or increase sales, then asks whether the founder understands the customer, the market, and the numbers. His operator background appears most clearly in the [[SaborAMexicoSalsas]] call, where he warns that big retail can add requirements, pricing pressure, shipping, and buyer complexity before a small food brand is ready.

His brand lens also comes from [[FUBU]]. Daymond treats a founder story as a trust and sales asset when it helps retailers and customers explain why the product exists. Across the calls, he favors staged proof: small retailers before major grocery accounts, a first tribe before broad repositioning, low-risk B2B trials before large vendor displacement, and direct feedback before scaling away from customers.

## Key Characteristics
- Applies an investor-operator filter: can he help reduce costs, increase sales, or clarify customer value?
- Treats founder story as commercially useful only when paired with market knowledge, numbers, and resilience.
- Uses [[FUBU]]'s early small-store experience as evidence that local retailers can carry founder story and product quality.
- Favors staged expansion over premature scale in retail, positioning, and B2B selling.
- Frames financial intelligence and patient wealth-building as lessons he would give his younger self.

## Qualifications
The episode gives Daymond's advice in a conversational format, not a full audit of the companies' financials, margins, or channel economics. His advice should be treated as source-scoped founder guidance rather than a complete retail or B2B operating plan.
