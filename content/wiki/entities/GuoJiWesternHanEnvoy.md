## Overview

郭吉 / Guo Ji is the Western Han envoy whom [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] sends to challenge the Xiongnu chanyu during the 110 BCE northern tour.

## Current Profile

Guo Ji's role is a concentrated case of coercive diplomacy. He carries an either-fight-or-submit message to the chanyu, making the emperor's frontier presence operational through speech. The episode says the chanyu kills the official who admitted him, detains Guo Ji, and sends him toward the Beihai region; it does not supply a broader biography.

## Key Characteristics

- Imperial envoy in the 110 BCE Han-Xiongnu confrontation.
- Bearer of an explicit fight-or-submit ultimatum.
- Detainee whose reception exposes the personal risk of coercive diplomatic speech.

## Evidence

- Mission and message - [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-360-1-simaqian-taishigong-chenghao-jing-you-ci-er-lai-lq6ulwhl_jscf6ipkjxev6zvwbrf|Hanji 360-1]] says Han Wudi sends Guo Ji to tell the chanyu that the emperor awaits battle at the frontier or will accept submission.

## Qualifications

The current page rests on one short source summary. The exact wording, route, detention location, later career, and claim that Xiongnu forces had lost the will to fight remain episode-attributed.
