# Project Glasswing

Project Glasswing is the [[Anthropic]] collaboration named in [[tech-20260410-0410-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260410-0410-mp-tech-pod-128]]. In the episode, it is the mechanism by which Anthropic gives restricted access to [[ClaudeMethosPreview|Claude-Methos Preview]] to more than 40 companies and technology organizations rather than releasing the model to the general public.

The source frames Project Glasswing as a controlled defensive-security rollout. [[JoannaStern]] says the model could help trusted users find long-existing vulnerabilities, but the same capability would be dangerous if it helped offensive actors identify or exploit weaknesses.

## Source Position
- The episode names [[Google]], [[JPMorganChase|JPMorgan Chase]], and [[Cisco]] as example participants or access recipients.
- The source does not describe the contractual, monitoring, evaluation, or disclosure procedures behind the collaboration.
- The project connects model capability to [[FrontierModelAccessRestrictions]]: access depends on trusted-user status, not only on payment or public availability.
