# Project Glasswing

## Overview
Project Glasswing is the [[Anthropic]] restricted-access collaboration attached to a cyber-capable model described as [[ClaudeMethosPreview|Claude-Methos Preview]] in Marketplace Tech and as Mythos in the new [[AllIn|All-In]] source. Its public meaning in the wiki is controlled defensive access: give trusted organizations time to find, fix, and harden vulnerabilities before similar capability spreads.

## Current Profile
The collaboration is framed as a practical test of voluntary frontier-model governance. Rather than releasing a powerful vulnerability-discovery model broadly, Anthropic routes capability to a bounded set of companies and technology organizations, while supporters argue that the arrangement gives defenders a patching window and skeptics question whether the timeline can materially harden the internet.

## Key Characteristics
- It is an access-control and coordination mechanism, not merely a model announcement.
- It links [[FrontierModelReleaseGovernance]] to concrete remediation work by trusted organizations.
- The bounded sources describe participants or targets as major technology and financial institutions, including [[Google]], [[JPMorganChase|JPMorgan Chase]], and [[Cisco]] in the Marketplace Tech source.
- The All-In source adds a claimed 100-day hardening window and a broader debate over whether voluntary governance can substitute for top-down regulation.
- The sources do not describe the contractual, monitoring, evaluation, or disclosure procedures behind the collaboration.

## Evidence
- Access-program evidence: [[tech-20260410-0410-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260410-0410-mp-tech-pod-128]] says Anthropic gave restricted access to more than 40 companies and technology organizations through Project Glasswing rather than releasing the model to the general public.
- Defensive-window evidence: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-anthropics-30b-ramp-mythos-doomsday-openclaw-ankled-iran-war-ceasefire-israels-influence-40808300]] records a 100-day effort to use advanced AI to find, fix, and harden software vulnerabilities before attackers exploit them.
- Governance evidence: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-anthropics-30b-ramp-mythos-doomsday-openclaw-ankled-iran-war-ceasefire-israels-influence-40808300]] presents Project Glasswing as evidence for market and industry coordination with government, while also recording skepticism that systemic internet patching can happen so quickly.
