## Overview

[[XingYongLateHan|刑颙]] is a strict late-Han official assigned to Cao Zhi's household, presented as the corrective counselor whose practical value survives his principal's dislike and political decline.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1149 describes Xing Yong as ceremonially strict, serious, and unwilling to flatter [[CaoZhi|曹植]]. [[LiuZhenLateHan|刘桢]] compares literary ornament with seasonal flowers and Xing Yong's useful counsel with fruit, arguing that Cao Zhi is neglecting the more durable contribution.

After Cao Zhi loses standing, Xing Yong's career continues because the episode grounds it in rectitude and clean administration rather than factional dependence on the prince. He is reported to reach the office of taichang and die without sharing Cao Zhi's political fall.

## Key Characteristics

- Provides strict rather than pleasing counsel.
- Represents useful correction over ornamental admiration.
- Maintains an independent reputation for ritual rectitude and clean conduct.
- Survives the decline of the principal he was assigned to advise.
