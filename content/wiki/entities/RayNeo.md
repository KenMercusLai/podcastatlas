# 雷鸟创新 / RayNeo

## Overview
雷鸟创新 / RayNeo is the smart-glasses company represented by CEO [[LiHongwei|李鸿伟]] in the No.221 [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] interview on AI glasses, AR display, and next-computing-platform strategy.

## Current Profile
The source presents RayNeo as an AI-glasses company using staged products to approach a possible all-day computing platform. Its X series is described as binocular full-color optical-waveguide glasses, its V series as no-display AI camera and audio glasses, and its R/GT series as head-worn viewing and portable-screen products.

RayNeo's differentiated claim in the episode is not only hardware sales. Li says the company has a large-model team, software staffing greater than hardware staffing, optical-display investment, on-glasses AI work, and spatial-computing understanding. The current evidence treats those as RayNeo's own practitioner framing, not independently verified competitive ranking.

## Key Characteristics
- It is positioned around AI glasses and AR-style real-world augmentation rather than only VR immersion.
- Its product strategy separates display exploration, first-person capture, and portable-screen use before a unified device matures.
- Its technical thesis combines optical display, low-power chips, on-device sensing, AI models, spatial computing, and product-scene judgment.
- It treats lower friction, daily wearability, and stage-specific user feedback as preconditions for a platform transition.
- Its leading-position, partnership, and market-share claims remain source-scoped to the CEO interview.
