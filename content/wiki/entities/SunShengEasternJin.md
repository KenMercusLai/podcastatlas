# 孙胜 / Sun Sheng (Eastern Jin)

## Overview
孙胜 / Sun Sheng enters the wiki through Hanji 1039 as an Eastern Jin historian/commentator whose criticism frames Hua Xin's surrender as a problem of loyalty and ministerial integrity.

## Current Profile
The current evidence makes Sun Sheng a historiographical countervoice. He condemns [[HuaXin|华歆]], [[YuFan|虞翻]], and [[SunCe|孙策]] for the Yuzhang surrender episode, while the host contextualizes his stance through Jin/Wei legitimacy and keeps a competing civilian-protection reading in view.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides the orthodox condemnation of Hua Xin's surrender.
- Extends blame beyond Hua Xin to Yu Fan's persuasion and Sun Ce's coercive conquest.
- Evaluates the episode through loyalty to Han office and later legitimacy concerns.
- Functions as counterevidence against treating surrender as automatically prudent or humane.
