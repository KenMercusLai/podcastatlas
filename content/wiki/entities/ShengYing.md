# 盛颖 / Sheng Ying

[[e247-duihua-shengying-xai-infra-de-langman-sglang-kaiyuan-pingquan-yu-zhenhuanchuan-6c9d13b1-ac9a-4a7a-a35b-99bfb8374668]] presents Sheng Ying as co-founder and CEO of [[RadixARC|Redix ARK]], the researcher behind [[SGLang]], and a former [[XAI|xAI]] engineer who worked on inference infrastructure around [[Grok]]. Her story connects [[FormalVerification]], [[SMTSolver|SMT solver]] research, large-model serving, open-source community work, and founder responsibility.

The episode emphasizes motivation and fit. Sheng says she performs badly on work she cannot care about, but can enter intense flow when a problem feels real. That self-knowledge becomes part of her [[ResearchTaste]]: she leaves a mathematically elegant but narrow verification route for AI infrastructure because inference systems feel closer to real deployment pressure.

Sheng's public position in the source is not only technical. She links [[OpenSourceAIDemocratization]] to her own learning path, argues that infrastructure should be treated as product craft through [[AIInfrastructureAsProduct]], and frames [[LMSYS|LM-SYS]] as a long-term institution for giving non-established builders and researchers access to high-leverage AI projects.
