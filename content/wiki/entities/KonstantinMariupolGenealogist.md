# 康斯坦丁 / Konstantin (Mariupol genealogist)

康斯坦丁 / Konstantin is the genealogy-minded helper in [[05-ta-laizi-maliwuboer-ruguo-ni-kanjian-guo-wo-ceng-jiandao-de-533375925]]. After [[NataschaWodin|纳塔莎·沃丁]] leaves a message on a [[Mariupol]]-related forum, he responds and gradually finds names, relatives, photographs, and family-history traces that had been inaccessible to her for decades.

The episode gives him a strong [[PersonalArchiveAsHistory]] role. His own earlier search for the truth about a misjudged pilot uncle explains why he understands archival recovery as more than hobby work: it can restore dignity to people flattened by state categories, rumor, war, or disappearance.
