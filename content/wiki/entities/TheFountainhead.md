# The Fountainhead / 《源泉》

The Fountainhead / 《源泉》 enters the wiki through [[09-atelasi-songsongjian-anlande-zhishi-shuangwen-zuojia-ma-543277898]] as the first major [[AynRand|Ayn Rand / 安·兰德]] novel the episode uses to explain Rand's appeal and limits. The source presents it less as realist fiction than as a myth or allegory about architectural creation, social approval, moral rhetoric, and the right to preserve one's work from compromise.

The episode's reading centers on Howard Roark as the creator figure: he refuses academic convention, client alteration, imitation, and popularity when those things would corrupt the work. Peter Keating represents second-hand success built from social recognition, while Ellsworth Toohey represents a more dangerous enemy: the critic or moralist who uses public virtue, sacrifice, and anti-talent language to gain power over creators.

The source treats the novel's controversial and unrealistic elements as part of its symbolic mode rather than as ordinary behavioral advice. It explicitly cautions that Roark's perfect competence and absolute refusal should not be copied as a real-life template, which ties the page to [[ClassicReadingComplexity]], [[AuthorCharacterSeparation]], and [[PureRationalityTrap]].
