## Overview

赵彦 / Zhao Yan is the yilang who, in this source, advises [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] on current government and is killed by [[CaoCao|曹操]] in Jian'an 19 / 214 CE.

## Current Profile

Zhao Yan's importance is institutional rather than biographical. His access gives a materially weak emperor an independent policy channel; Cao Cao's decision to kill him demonstrates that advice itself can be treated as a threat when an overmighty minister controls force but cannot erase the sovereign's residual legitimacy.

## Key Characteristics

- Serves as yilang in Emperor Xian's court.
- Provides the emperor with policy criticism or proposals.
- Is interpreted by Cao Cao as preparing the emperor to resist him.
- Is killed, turning adviser access into a warning to the remaining court.
