# 杭州博物馆 / Hangzhou Museum

## Overview
杭州博物馆 / Hangzhou Museum enters the wiki through the [[BowuzhiPodcast|博物志]] episode on [[YiweiHangzhiExhibition|“意味杭之：馆藏明清古籍展”]].

## Current Profile
In this source, the museum is valued less as a container for rare holdings than as an institution that can explain difficult material culture. The episode presents the North Hall second-floor exhibition as compact, object-rich, and unusually effective at making ancient books, formats, editions, and Hangzhou publishing history accessible to non-specialists.

## Key Characteristics
- Hosts the source episode's central [[YiweiHangzhiExhibition|ancient-books exhibition]].
- Uses exhibition design to make specialist book terms and object details legible.
- Connects rare holdings to [[Hangzhou|杭州]] local cultural history rather than displaying them as isolated treasures.
- Serves as a positive example of [[MuseumInterpretationLegibility]].

## Evidence
- Exhibition venue - [[246-hangzhou-bowuguan-yiwei-hangzhi-haojiu-mei-kan-guo-zheme-tuijian-de-zhanlan-le-8217911423-973793]] says the exhibition was at Hangzhou Museum's North Hall second floor.
- Rare holdings - [[246-hangzhou-bowuguan-yiwei-hangzhi-haojiu-mei-kan-guo-zheme-tuijian-de-zhanlan-le-8217911423-973793]] reports that six of the museum's seven National Rare Ancient Books Catalogue items were shown publicly for the first time.
- Interpretive role - [[246-hangzhou-bowuguan-yiwei-hangzhi-haojiu-mei-kan-guo-zheme-tuijian-de-zhanlan-le-8217911423-973793]] praises the museum's labels, red-line highlights, glass annotations, short explanatory phrases, and term screens.

## Qualifications
This page is source-bounded to one exhibition episode. It does not assess the museum's full collection, institutional history, or other exhibitions.
