## Overview

李文秀 / Li Wenxiu is the protagonist of [[WhiteHorseNeighsInWesternWind|《白马啸西风》]], represented here as a Han orphan raised near a Kazakh community whose response to unreturned love makes kindness and restraint more decisive than martial superiority.

## Current Profile

After her parents are killed over the 高昌 map, Li is raised by 马家骏 under his 计老人 identity and grows up with 苏普. She distances herself when 苏普's father opposes their bond, then gives 阿曼 the wolf skin that helps the new couple form. Even after learning martial arts and imagining that power might let her recover 苏普, Li rescues both lovers and accepts that their willingness to die for each other cannot be displaced by her skill or sacrifice.

The source calls kindness her strongest “martial art.” This is not passivity: Li acts under danger, protects people whose happiness hurts her, and changes 苏鲁克's collective judgment of Han people through sustained conduct. Her ending remains open. Returning toward the Central Plains after losing love and two father-teacher figures can signify isolation, new capacity, or both; the bounded source does not settle what follows.

## Key Characteristics

- Survives childhood violence and grows up between Han origin and Kazakh social life.
- Loves 苏普 without gaining a claim over his choice.
- Moves from fantasies of recovery through power to recognition of another couple's mutual devotion.
- Repeatedly protects 苏普, 阿曼, and 苏鲁克 despite personal loss.
- Uses conduct rather than identity argument to challenge ethnic prejudice.
- Combines first-rate martial competence with an unresolved, solitary future.

## Qualifications

The current profile rests on one podcast's structured summary rather than direct textual collation. Li's motives, the degree to which she consciously “gives” 苏普 to 阿曼, the meaning of her tests, and her future after departure remain interpretive. Kindness changes one fictional character's prejudice but should not be generalized into a sufficient remedy for systemic violence or entrenched hatred.

## What Changed

- Created a profile separating love, grief, action, and possession.
- Framed kindness as active protection under cost rather than harmless temperament.
- Preserved both loneliness and future capacity in the open ending.
