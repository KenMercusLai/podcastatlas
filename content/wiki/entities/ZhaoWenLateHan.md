## Overview

赵温 / Zhao Wen is a late-Han situ whose career in the current evidence spans dangerous remonstrance under [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and dismissal after recruiting [[CaoPi|曹丕]] under [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s dominant political order.

## Current Profile

Hanji 986 shows Zhao Wen using moral and institutional language against Li Jue's assault, plunder, rejection of imperial mediation, and coercive control of [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]. The office can still articulate correction, but the armed power holder can answer it with a threat to kill the remonstrant.

Hanji 1084 adds a later appointment conflict. Zhao Wen recruits Cao Pi into his staff under the situ's formal personnel authority; Cao Cao accuses him of improper selection, and Emperor Xian dismisses him. The host reads the act as a challenge to Cao Cao's control of personnel channels, but that motive and the claim of total monopoly remain interpretations rather than settled institutional fact.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses senior office to remonstrate against armed coercion of the emperor.
- Retains formal institutional voice despite weak enforcement capacity.
- Exercises a recruitment power attached to the situ's office.
- Becomes vulnerable when formal office authority collides with dominant extra-imperial power.
- Shows that procedure can remain legally legible while becoming politically dangerous.

## Qualifications

The current record contains two popular-history windows rather than a complete career. Exact letters, appointment mechanics, the legal basis of recruitment, Cao Cao's motive, the role of elite factional networks, and the host's “personnel monopoly” interpretation require verification against underlying texts.
