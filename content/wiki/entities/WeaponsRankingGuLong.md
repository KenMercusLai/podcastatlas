# 兵器谱（古龙） / Weapons Ranking (Gu Long)

## Overview

兵器谱 is [[BaiXiaoshengGuLong|百晓生]]'s ranking of weapons and martial artists in [[SentimentalSwordsmanRuthlessSword|《多情剑客无情剑》]]. The bounded episode treats it as both a useful map of Jianghu danger and a fallible institution whose apparent precision exceeds its evidence.

## Current Profile

The list establishes a shared order: 天机老人 stands first, [[ShangguanJinhongGuLong|上官金虹]] second, [[LiXunhuan|李寻欢]] third, 郭嵩阳 fourth, and 吕凤先 fifth. That order is credible enough to guide reputation and fear, but it captures ability only at one moment. Aging, continued practice, injury, emotional state, hidden technique, matchup, and the difference between testing and mortal combat can all change the result.

Its omissions are equally important. [[JingWumingGuLong|荆无命]] and [[AFeiGuLong|阿飞]] remain outside the list despite elite ability, while the episode argues that women such as 大欢喜女菩萨 and 蓝蝎子 could plausibly enter its top tier. The ranking therefore creates narrative certainty while its gaps preserve danger, imagination, and criticism of authority.

## Key Characteristics

- Converts dispersed martial reputation into a legible shared hierarchy.
- Functions as a historical snapshot rather than a continuously updated measurement.
- Ranks reputations and combat ability without fully modeling opportunity, matchup, psychology, or hidden skill.
- Gains social power because Jianghu participants use it to assess status and risk.
- Excludes or misses capable fighters, including women and unpublicized specialists.
- Remains narratively useful precisely because later outcomes can challenge it without making it meaningless.

## Qualifications

This profile reflects one podcast's reading of a fictional ranking rather than an independently calibrated combat system. Exact placements, hypothetical matchups, the compiler's motives, the strength of omitted fighters, and authorial intention remain source-scoped or interpretive. The episode identifies gender exclusion but does not establish a complete alternative ranking.
