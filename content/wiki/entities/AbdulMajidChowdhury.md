# Abdul Majid Chowdhury

Abdul Majid Chowdhury is the Bangladeshi businessman in [[getting-entrepreneurial-in-korea-summer-school]] who wanted to create employment and earn export dollars through clothing production. The source says he looked to [[SouthKorea|South Korea]] because it had also rebuilt after war and used garment exports as an early development step.

His meeting with [[Daewoo]] led to training for 128 Bangladeshis in Korea and helped create [[DeshGarments]] in Chittagong. In the wiki, Chowdhury anchors the human and entrepreneurial side of [[TextileKnowledgeTransfer]] rather than only the trade-policy side of the [[MultiFiberArrangement]].
