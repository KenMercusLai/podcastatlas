## Overview

淳于衍 / Chunyu Yan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-413-2-shi-shui-dusha-le-hanxuandi-liubingyi-de-laopo-lguo142uuouzmhqjvewjpjkgqxa|Hanji 413-2]] as the female palace physician who poisons [[XuPingjunWesternHan|许平君]] during childbirth under pressure from [[HuoXianWesternHan|霍显]]. The source presents her as medically skilled but morally collapsed: a healer whose access to childbirth medication becomes the operational channel for murder.

## Current Profile

The episode gives Chunyu Yan a narrow but important role in the Huo-family arc. She is close enough to Xu Pingjun to attend her pregnancy and delivery, and her husband works in a low-status night gatekeeping role in the palace. Her request to improve that husband's position gives Huo Xian leverage. Once Huo Xian tells her the murder plan, Chunyu Yan understands that refusal is dangerous because she now knows the secret; she therefore agrees to try.

Her significance is not independent factional power but access conversion. Huo Xian has motive and family authority, but Chunyu Yan has the professional route into the empress's body. The episode makes the breach especially stark because palace medicines are supposed to be reviewed and tasted, yet Chunyu Yan uses her presence beside the approved medication to introduce toxic附子 and deny Xu Pingjun's immediate suspicion.

## Key Characteristics

- Palace physician with direct access to Xu Pingjun during pregnancy and childbirth.
- Job-seeking petitioner whose husband's position gives Huo Xian an opening for pressure and reward.
- Operational agent of Xu Pingjun's poisoning, according to the episode.
- Medical-ethics negative example because healing knowledge and medicine access are turned into murder.
- Dependent actor rather than independent planner; Huo Xian supplies motive, target, and political pressure.
