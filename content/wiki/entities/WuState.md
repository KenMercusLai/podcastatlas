# 吴国 / Wu State

吴国 / Wu State enters this wiki branch through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-57-gudai-fa-dushi-haiyou-zhe-zuoyong-lj6wi61osaytowgmdp8dtkrwb-si]], where [[Zigong|子贡 / 端木赐]] redirects the threat to [[LuState|鲁国]] into a wider interstate chain. After persuading [[TianChang|田常]] not to attack Lu, Zigong persuades [[Fuchai|吴王夫差]] to attack [[QiState|齐国]].

Wu's role in the episode is not simply military success. Fuchai defeats Qi, then presses onward toward [[JinState|晋国]] and is defeated there. That exhaustion gives [[Goujian|越王勾践]] and [[YueState|越国]] the opening to attack Wu near its capital, leading eventually to Fuchai's suicide and Wu's destruction.
