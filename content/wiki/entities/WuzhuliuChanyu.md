## Overview

乌珠留单于 / Wuzhuliu Chanyu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-554-chengnianren-de-dingji-zilv-kezhi-yuwang-lo3brdufkiaks3mfsqsruxprqdcn|Hanji 554]] as the newly succeeded Xiongnu ruler who handles a Western Han land demand by first testing responsibility and delaying, then openly rejecting the request and reporting the matter to [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 554 presents Wuzhuliu Chanyu as politically careful rather than merely defiant. When [[XiahouFanWesternHan|夏侯藩]] asks that Xiongnu offer a border tract to Han, the chanyu immediately asks whether this is Xiahou Fan's personal view or the emperor's command. That question forces responsibility clarity and gives the envoy a chance to keep the issue deniable.

After Xiahou Fan claims imperial backing, the chanyu still avoids immediate rupture by saying the land belongs to the Wenuotu king's residence zone and that he must investigate terrain and resources. When Xiahou Fan later presses again, the chanyu shifts to open refusal: the land has belonged to Xiongnu for generations, supplies western kings with wood, and cannot be casually surrendered. He then sends an envoy to Chengdi, turning Han's deniable probe into an imperial embarrassment.

## Key Characteristics

- Newly succeeded Xiongnu chanyu in the Suihe 1 / 8 BCE setting.
- Uses a responsibility question to distinguish personal probe from imperial demand.
- Initially preserves face by delaying rather than rejecting the request outright.
- Grounds rejection in ancestry, land continuity, and material resource needs.
- Uses reverse reporting to force Chengdi to answer for the envoy's conduct.
