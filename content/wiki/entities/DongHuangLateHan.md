# 董璜 / Dong Huang (late Han)

董璜 / Dong Huang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-962-dongzhuo-yu-lvbu-daodi-weishenme-juelie-lgp3kxfqnhokibf6kpli6p4j8iti|Hanji 962]] as [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s nephew and central-army figure. The source says Dong Zhuo appoints him 中军校尉 while other relatives such as [[DongMinLateHan|董旻]] also receive high military office.

His page is source-scoped. Dong Huang matters here less as an independent biography than as evidence that Dong Zhuo's 192 CE power is organized through kinship offices, troop control, and private loyalty rather than ordinary Han court balance around [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]].
