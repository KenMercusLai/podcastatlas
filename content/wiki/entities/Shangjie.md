# Shangjie / 尚界

Shangjie / 尚界 is the [[SAICMotor|上汽]] and [[Huawei]] branch of [[HongmengZhixing]]. [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] says SAIC actively sought deep cooperation with Huawei and framed its value as helping put Huawei intelligent-vehicle capability into lower-price vehicles through manufacturing experience and cost control.

The source treats 尚界 H5 as the portfolio's mass-market gap filler. Its listed price range of RMB 159,800 to 199,800 puts it below the high-end [[AITO|问界]], [[Stelato|享界]], and [[Maextro|尊界]] cases, making it the clearest [[SmartDrivingDemocratization|smart-driving democratization]] branch in the episode.

## Source Position
- Shangjie is the source's lower-price 鸿蒙智行 branch.
- SAIC's claimed contribution is manufacturing, vehicle experience, and cost control rather than only brand access.
- The H5 small-order figure is treated as source-scoped demand signal, not as settled delivery proof.
