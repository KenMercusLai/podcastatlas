# James V of Scotland

## Overview

James V is the Scottish king whose death days after [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]]' birth turned military defeat and the absence of a male heir into an infant royal succession.

## Current Profile

The episode places James's final days after the English defeat at Solway Moss. He had no surviving legitimate son, was absent from Mary's birth at Linlithgow, and died about a week later, leaving the crown to a newborn daughter and government to competing regency interests.

His marriage to [[MaryOfGuise|Mary of Guise]] also made Mary's succession part of the [[FrancoScottishAlliance|Franco-Scottish alliance]]. The vulnerability created by his death did not dissolve the monarchy, but it opened the struggle over Mary's custody, marriage, religion, and international alignment.

## Key Characteristics

- Stuart king defeated by England shortly before his death.
- Father whose lack of a surviving legitimate son made Mary the heir.
- Husband in a French dynastic marriage reinforcing the Franco-Scottish alignment.
- Deceased ruler whose infant successor created a regency rather than an immediate collapse.

## Qualifications

The episode gives only James's final crisis, not a complete reign. His reported disappointment at Mary's sex, final words, illness, mental state, and the causal role of Solway Moss in his death remain episode-attributed.

## What Changed

- Established James V as the immediate dynastic and military prehistory to Mary's infant accession.

## Relationships

- [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]] - daughter and newborn successor.
- [[MaryOfGuise|Mary of Guise]] - wife whose French connections shaped the minority government.
- [[Scotland]] - kingdom left to a regency after his death.
- [[HenryVIII|Henry VIII]] - English opponent whose marriage policy followed the Scottish succession.
