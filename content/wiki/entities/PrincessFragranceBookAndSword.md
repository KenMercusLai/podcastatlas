## Overview

香香公主 / Princess Fragrance is [[HuoQingtong|霍青桐]]'s sister and [[ChenJialuo|陈家洛]]'s beloved in [[BookAndSword|《书剑恩仇录》]], represented here through trust, admiration, dependence, emotional refuge, and the identity these give Chen as protector.

## Current Profile

The episode argues that Chen loves Princess Fragrance more than Huo Qingtong, citing his spontaneous association of flowers with her and the emotional position she offers him. She trusts that danger will recede when he is present, depends on him, and provides acceptance and warmth after a life substantially directed by family, teacher, and organization.

This profile is relational rather than comprehensive. Her admiration can make Chen feel capable and necessary, but describing that function should not reduce her to a passive device or prove that dependence exhausts her character. The episode's emphasis is on why the relationship feels safe to Chen, not on a complete account of her agency or political meaning.

## Key Characteristics

- Expresses trust, admiration, and emotional dependence toward Chen.
- Gives Chen a strong protector role and sense of personal efficacy.
- Is associated with acceptance, warmth, and relief from political struggle.
- Serves as the episode's contrast to Huo Qingtong's equality and strategic competence.
- Makes romantic choice legible as a choice of self-position as well as of partner.

## Qualifications

The supplied episode foregrounds how Chen experiences her and therefore risks reproducing a male-centered view. Beauty, dependence, admiration, and emotional comfort do not supply a complete profile of her motives, agency, cultural position, or sacrifice. The claim that she fills Chen's developmental or emotional deficit is interpretive.

## What Changed

- Established trust, admiration, and refuge as the episode's central relational functions.
- Linked Chen's attraction to the protector identity the relationship supports.
- Qualified the account against reducing her to beauty or dependence alone.
