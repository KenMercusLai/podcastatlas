# 郭胜 / Guo Sheng (late Han)

郭胜 / Guo Sheng (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-943-quanshi-kaoyan-renxing-beihou-cangzhe-juda-xiongxian-lg7ia5xok-swdmhml35yv9qqkvt|Hanji 943]] as the regular palace attendant whose personal tie to the He family defeats [[JianShuoLateHan|蹇硕]]'s emergency appeal. The source says Guo Sheng is from [[HeJin|何进]]'s home region and had helped [[EmpressDowagerHe|何太后]] and He Jin become powerful.

When Jian Shuo writes to [[ZhaoZhongLateHan|赵忠]] and others urging them to close the palace gates and kill He Jin, Guo Sheng rejects the plan and turns the letter over to He Jin. That choice makes him a small but decisive access broker: the anti-He message moves through the eunuch channel, but Guo Sheng routes it to the He side.

The result is Jian Shuo's arrest and death. Guo Sheng therefore marks the pre-Hanji-944 condition in which the eunuch world is not unified; personal debt and factional benefit can fracture an apparently shared palace interest.
