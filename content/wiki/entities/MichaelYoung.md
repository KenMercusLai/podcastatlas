# Michael Young

## Overview

Michael Young is the social critic invoked in the episode for treating meritocracy as a warning rather than an uncomplicated ideal.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Young to question what examination success does to social morality. When winners interpret measured achievement as proof that they deserve their rank, institutional selection can harden into a dignity hierarchy and make inherited advantage or limited opportunity disappear from view.

## Key Characteristics

- Associated with a critical rather than celebratory use of “meritocracy.”
- Focuses attention on the moral self-understanding of selected winners.
- Provides a bridge from exam design to class closure and status legitimacy.

## Evidence

- Meritocracy warning: [[84-exams-glt6507641896]] presents Young's idea as a warning about educated winners treating success as evidence of moral deservingness.
- Institutional context: [[84-exams-glt6507641896]] places that warning beside examination mobility, elite scarcity, and social closure.
