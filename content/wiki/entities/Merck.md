# Merck / 默沙东

Merck / 默沙东 appears in [[vol-117-shengwu-yiyao-de-2025-chaodi-zhongguo-yanfa-jiaolv-he-xinwang-jiwei-lmhral0rmq6tohiqdwsgmfapnyn7]] as the central [[MultinationalPharmaPatentCliffAnxiety|patent-cliff anxiety]] example. The episode says [[Keytruda|K药]] became a larger PD-1 success than earlier rivals partly through clinical-development strategy, which makes its eventual patent expiration a major pipeline-replacement problem.

The source also uses Merck to show defensive BD behavior. In the episode's telling, Merck publicly asked to see stronger overall-survival evidence around the [[PD1VEGFBispecific|PD-1/VEGF bispecific]] mechanism, but later moved to acquire a Chinese PD-1/VEGF asset.
