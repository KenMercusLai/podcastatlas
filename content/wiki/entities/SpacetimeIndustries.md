# Spacetime Industries

## Overview
Spacetime Industries is an early-stage robotics company represented as building deployable monitoring outposts for remote and hazardous critical-infrastructure sites.

## Current Profile
The company presents Sentinel, its orchestration software, as the main product and potential moat. Sentinel is intended to coordinate commercially available drones and ground vehicles, connect to existing site sensors, and run through containerized local infrastructure even when external connectivity fails. The represented product is observation-first: it gathers security and inspection data for customers but does not currently repair equipment or control facility operations.

## Key Characteristics
- Focuses on remote energy, cable-landing, port, coastline, and military monitoring environments.
- Uses partner hardware and fabrication rather than building every robotic component internally.
- Treats cross-platform orchestration software as its primary technical asset.
- Separates rapid device communication from slower production testing and certification.
- Aims for persistent, low-intervention deployment while currently offering inspection and observation.

## Qualifications
The wiki has one structured interview summary rather than technical documentation, contracts, customer metrics, audited security controls, regulatory approvals, or long-duration field results. Cyber resilience, calibration responsibility, data liability, unit economics, deployment reliability, and the durability of the claimed moat remain unresolved. The Alaska example demonstrates reported monitoring activity but does not establish that damage was imminent or prevented.

## What Changed
- Created the company profile with an observation-first critical-infrastructure robotics scope.
- Preserved the gap between fast integration, production readiness, and demonstrated persistent autonomy.
