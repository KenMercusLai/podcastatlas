## Overview

郭淮 / Guo Huai appears in the current wiki as a Cao-side officer who helps restore command after [[XiahouYuan|夏侯渊]]'s death and designs a set-back river defense against [[LiuBei|刘备]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1163-gaogu-ziji-shibushi-zui-da-de-yuchun-lokzo7kcz69kf_lhgskx8kie-9_c|Hanji 1163]] places Guo Huai with [[DuXiLateHan|杜袭]] during the post-Dingjun crisis. They gather the soldiers and support [[ZhangHeLateHan|张郃]] as commander, after which Zhang He inspects the positions and morale stabilizes.

Guo Huai then rejects defending directly on the riverbank. He proposes forming farther back so that Liu Bei's troops can begin crossing and be struck while divided by the river. The episode says Liu Bei recognizes the danger and does not cross, leaving Cao-side forces able to hold while requesting reinforcement.

## Key Characteristics

- Officer active in emergency command succession.
- Helps convert Zhang He's battlefield standing into recognized authority.
- Prioritizes restoration of morale after commander loss.
- Uses depth and crossing vulnerability rather than a rigid bank defense.

River defense:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1163-gaogu-ziji-shibushi-zui-da-de-yuchun-lokzo7kcz69kf_lhgskx8kie-9_c|Hanji 1163]] attributes the set-back formation and mid-crossing strike logic to Guo Huai.

## Qualifications

One structured podcast summary supplies the profile. Guo Huai's office, authority in Zhang He's appointment, exact formation, river identity, force balance, Liu Bei's reasoning, and the causal effect of the plan require textual verification. Deterrence is inferred from non-crossing and does not prove the formation would have succeeded in combat.

## What Changed

- Created a profile centered on command recovery and defense-in-depth after Dingjun Mountain.
