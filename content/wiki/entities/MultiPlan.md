# MultiPlan

## Overview
MultiPlan is presented in [[ep-19-navigating-the-future-of-workplace-health-and-benefits-with-ai]] as a healthcare analytics organization serving employers and brokers through cost-driver analysis, population-risk prediction, recommended interventions, and benefit-plan scenario optimization.

## Current Profile
The episode's account positions MultiPlan between employer budgeting and member health support. Its analytics are described as giving employers de-identified population insight while directing identifiable engagement through authorized partners, and its optimizer is framed as searching benefit and contribution combinations within employer-set budgets.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses healthcare insurance data to identify cost drivers and predict selected population health risks.
- Separates employer-facing aggregate insight from member-level PHI handling.
- Supports care-navigation workflows intended to connect higher-risk members with education and providers.
- Applies large scenario search to employee-benefit plan design under budget and contribution constraints.

## Evidence
### Population analytics and navigation
- [[ep-19-navigating-the-future-of-workplace-health-and-benefits-with-ai]] attributes cost-driver analysis, risk prediction, de-identification, and partner-led member engagement to MultiPlan's employer and consulting work.

### Plan-design optimization
- [[ep-19-navigating-the-future-of-workplace-health-and-benefits-with-ai]] describes a MultiPlan optimizer that evaluates many plan and contribution combinations against an employer budget.

### Governance posture
- [[ep-19-navigating-the-future-of-workplace-health-and-benefits-with-ai]] presents legal guardrails, fairness, transparency, and human review as necessary constraints on the organization's AI uses.

## Qualifications
- This profile is based on one employee interview and does not independently verify MultiPlan's products, legal controls, model performance, optimization objective, or health and cost outcomes.
- The episode does not detail which entities act as HIPAA covered entities or business associates in each workflow.
