# 唐文斌 / Tang Wenbin

## Overview
唐文斌 / Tang Wenbin is presented as [[Gaode]]'s embodied-business lead and the main guest in [[dang-jiqiren-xuehui-renlu-wuli-shijie-cai-zhenzheng-jieshangle-ai-658f592c4a52]]. The episode uses his account to explain why a map company would enter robotics through navigation, mobility, and real-world task execution.

## Current Profile
Tang's profile in the wiki is source-scoped to this episode. He acts as an interpreter of both robotics history and Gaode strategy: robot progress is explained as the co-evolution of structure and computation, while Gaode's own route is framed around movement through physical space before general humanoid manipulation.

## Key Characteristics
- Tang treats current robotics progress as an accumulated systems shift rather than a sudden exhibition trend.
- He separates robot capability into low-level motion control and higher-level perception, planning, memory, and decision-making.
- He argues that Gaode's spatiotemporal data and navigation models can become useful robot infrastructure.
- He presents [[GuideRobotDogs]] as the first core application for Gaode's embodied route.
- He keeps broader robot commercialization tied to deployable scenes, road rights, safety, and data feedback.

It does not provide independent cost, safety-certification, accessibility-user, or regulator evidence.
