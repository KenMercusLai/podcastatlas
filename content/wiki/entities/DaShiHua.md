# 大食话 / Da Shi Hua

## Overview
大食话 is the food-media affiliation named in five [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode titles, represented in the wiki through guest explanations of weight management, balanced eating, household cooking oil, food systems, processing, health-coded foods, sugar, and food labels.

## Current Profile
The current wiki evidence remains narrow: 大食话 appears as the title-level source context for [[YueWanrou|岳宛柔]] in VOL.160, VOL.178, VOL.192, and VOL.215 and for a VOL.161 guest called 岳老师. The page therefore treats it as a source-scoped media or project label rather than a fully profiled organization, and treats the nickname-only guest reference as likely but not explicitly proven to be the same person.

Across the five episodes, the 大食话 branch adds everyday food literacy to the medical-literacy show context. Its contribution is not an independent brand history; it is the food-culture and nutrition voice that turns carbohydrate and fat restriction, portions, food form, high-protein, low-GI, zero-fat, dairy, prepared food, meal replacements, additives, cooking-oil types, heat stability, oxidation, storage, low-GI honey, zero-sucrose products, juice, sweeteners, staple foods, and dessert habits into contextual distinctions.

## Key Characteristics
- Appears as a source-scoped food-media affiliation in five public nutrition-literacy episodes.
- Is represented through a guest explanation rather than through independent institutional evidence.
- Connects food culture, consumer labels, and practical health decisions.
- Helps broaden the show context from clinical medicine toward everyday diet interpretation.
- Repeats a dose-, context-, and whole-product approach across health-food and sugar-control discussions.
- Connects individual food literacy with processing, delivery convenience, marketing, and food-system defaults.

## Qualifications

None of the sources supplies an independent institutional profile, and VOL.161 names only 岳老师, so the project identity and that episode's speaker match remain source-scoped.
