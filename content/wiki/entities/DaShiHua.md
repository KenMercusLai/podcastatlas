# 大食话 / Da Shi Hua

## Overview
大食话 is the food-media affiliation named in two [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode titles, represented in the wiki through a guest's explanations of health-coded foods, sugar, and food labels.

## Current Profile
The current wiki evidence remains narrow: 大食话 appears as the title-level source context for a VOL.161 guest called 岳老师 and for [[YueWanrou|岳宛柔]] in VOL.215. The page therefore treats it as a source-scoped media or project label rather than a fully profiled organization, and treats the two guest references as likely but not explicitly proven to be the same person.

Across the two episodes, the 大食话 branch adds everyday food literacy to the medical-literacy show context. Its contribution is not an independent brand history; it is the food-culture and nutrition voice that turns high-protein, low-GI, zero-fat, dairy, prepared food, meal replacements, additives, low-GI honey, zero-sucrose products, juice, sweeteners, staple foods, and dessert habits into contextual distinctions.

## Key Characteristics
- Appears as a source-scoped food-media affiliation in two public nutrition-literacy episodes.
- Is represented through a guest explanation rather than through independent institutional evidence.
- Connects food culture, consumer labels, and practical health decisions.
- Helps broaden the show context from clinical medicine toward everyday diet interpretation.
- Repeats a dose-, context-, and whole-product approach across health-food and sugar-control discussions.
