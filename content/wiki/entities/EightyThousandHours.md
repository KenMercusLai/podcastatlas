# 80,000 Hours

*80,000 Hours* is [[BenjaminTodd]]'s career-advice book in [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]]. The episode treats it as the strongest of the three reviewed books because it rejects the idea that young people should simply follow existing passions.

The source highlights several claims: many 21-year-olds do not yet know what they want, many passions do not correspond to available jobs, and useful skill-building can create motivation over time. The book's [[EffectiveAltruism]] influence also turns career choice into [[ImpactCareerChoice]], though the reviewer warns that impact maximization can become demoralizing if treated too rigidly.
