## Current Profile

The episode says Li Xian was deeply attached to Shangguan Wan'er, but their relationship was interrupted by [[WuZetian|武则天]] and reduced to friendship. In the episode's argument, this reinforces the idea that Shangguan Wan'er's early relationships could not be separated from dynastic and palace power.
