## Overview

常惠 / Chang Hui is synthesized as a Western Han frontier envoy-commander who repeatedly turns court decisions into Western Regions field action. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-413-1-yiren-jia-sanwang-tade-gushi-yuanbi-zhaojun-chusai-geng-chuanqi-ltmt4y60eusm8a_mwthnfg5cys6p|Hanji 413-1]] introduces him as the envoy sent to [[Wusun|乌孙]] after [[XieyouPrincessWesternHan|解忧公主]] and Wengguimi request aid. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-414-xihan-diyi-quanchen-huoguang-weihe-zongrong-qizi-haisi-huanghou-lnaev5r0uxo0xnwhquoyl7f_tf8q|Hanji 414]] shows the military result: Wusun cavalry coordinated through Chang Hui strike [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] more effectively than the main Han armies. Later, [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-429-jiaobingbibai-de-gushi-zenme-laide-lokha4dmrik4ez4vtpunb9rwhzav|Hanji 429]] uses him for limited extraction at Cheshi, and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-444-xihan-chehui-le-heqin-gongzhu-xiongnu-lqvcsgep3fsjtru13s694vqkr08r|Hanji 444]] shows him reporting a Wusun succession change that causes Han to recall [[LiuXiangfuPrincessWesternHan|刘相夫]].

## Current Profile

Chang Hui's current profile is not a full biography but a repeated frontier-function profile. In the Wusun-Xiongnu branch, he appears after Xieyou's letter and Xuan's decision to respond. Hanji 413-1 presents his mission as the bridge between Wusun's appeal and the Han-Wusun counterstrike. Hanji 414 then gives the result: Wusun cavalry, coordinated through Chang Hui, attack the Xiongnu right-side royal court, capture important people and livestock, and contribute to a cascading weakening sequence when Xiongnu's revenge against Wusun fails.

In the Cheshi branch, Chang Hui appears as a controlled alternative to broader war. After [[ZhengJiWesternHan|郑吉]] is surrounded and [[EmperorXuanOfHan|Xuan]] considers attacking Xiongnu's western side, [[WeiXiangWesternHan|魏相]] persuades him to avoid proud or opportunistic war. Xuan sends Chang Hui with Zhangye and Jiuquan cavalry to recover Zheng Ji, install Junxiu, relocate Cheshi's people to Quli, and concede the old Cheshi land to Xiongnu.

In the Liu Xiangfu branch, Chang Hui acts as a reporting and challenge channel. Hanji 444 says he reports that Wengguimi has died and [[NimiWusun|泥靡 / 狂王]] has taken the Wusun throne, making the planned marriage to Yuanguimi unsafe. That report lets Xuan accept Xiao Wangzhi's renewed caution and recall Liu Xiangfu from Dunhuang before she becomes trapped inside the changed Wusun succession order.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han envoy-commander active in Wusun and Cheshi frontier episodes.
- Converts Xieyou's Wusun appeal into a field mission that supports Han-Wusun military coordination.
- Helps allied Wusun force produce major anti-Xiongnu results when main Han armies are less decisive.
- Serves as a limited-action instrument when Xuan chooses extraction and settlement at Cheshi over broad attack.
- Reports Wusun succession change in time for Han to reverse Liu Xiangfu's marriage mission.
- Functions as a connective officer rather than an independently synthesized strategist in the current evidence.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to compact podcast source notes. It does not reconstruct Chang Hui's full offices, biography, relationship to Huo Guang, complete Wusun command role, Qiuci action, or all Western Regions missions. Hanji 414 notes Huo Guang's private instruction to Chang Hui about Qiuci, but the current page keeps Qiuci details source-scoped because the available source note treats them as secondary to the Huo-family and Wusun-Xiongnu branches.
