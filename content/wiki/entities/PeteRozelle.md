# Pete Rozelle

Pete Rozelle is the NFL commissioner presented in [[the-nfl-1]] as the central architect of the league's national media era. The episode says owners selected him after a long deadlock, and that his public-relations background shaped the NFL as a media product rather than only a sports competition.

Rozelle moved league headquarters to New York, cultivated press and advertising relationships, pooled television rights, pushed for the [[SportsBroadcastingAct]], helped build the [[SuperBowl]] into a spectacle, and worked with [[RooneArledge]] on [[MondayNightFootball]]. In the wiki, he anchors the move from local team economics to [[LeagueFirstOperatingModel]] and [[SportsMediaRights]].
