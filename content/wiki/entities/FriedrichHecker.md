# Friedrich Hecker

## Overview

Friedrich Hecker was a German radical presented in [[326-the-year-of-revolutions-1848-glt9746323554]] as the armed-insurrectionary contrast to [[RobertBlum]]'s parliamentary strategy during the [[RevolutionsOf1848]].

## Current Profile

Hecker rejected slow parliamentary politics and led an armed rising in Baden. The episode judges the revolt a severe failure that wasted lives and did not displace the existing states. He later went to Switzerland and the United States, where he served in the Union Army.

His role in the source is strategic rather than comprehensive: he shows how impatience with institutional deliberation could produce direct action without the military capacity needed to succeed.

## Key Characteristics

- German radical committed to direct armed action.
- Leader of a failed Baden insurrection.
- Strategic counterpoint to Blum's parliamentary persistence.
- Revolutionary exile who later moved to the United States.
- Later Union Army participant.
