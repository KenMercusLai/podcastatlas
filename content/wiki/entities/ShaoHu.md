# 召忽 / Shao Hu

召忽 / Shao Hu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-75-2-qiongshe-jichi-de-zushiye-guanzhong-lo8uei2u1qwj9tszftel1b4lswoa]] as the defeated [[GongziJiu|公子纠]] loyalist who dies after the succession struggle. The episode contrasts him with [[GuanZhong|管仲]], who had also served Gongzi Jiu but lives and accepts appointment from [[QiHuanGong|齐桓公]].

The source does not turn Shao Hu into a simple foil for disloyalty. Its point is that different choices produce different historical visibility: Shao Hu becomes a brief example of old-master loyalty, while Guan Zhong's survival lets his capacity reshape [[QiState|齐国]].
