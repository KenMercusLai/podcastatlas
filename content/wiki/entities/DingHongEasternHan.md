# 丁鸿 / Ding Hong (Eastern Han)

## Overview

丁鸿 / Ding Hong appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-766-lishi-jiaogei-ni-weishenme-yao-guyi-fa-piqi-lrs-mguyfmnxe970hdwjvmevl8oc|Hanji 766]] as the minister who uses the 92 CE solar eclipse to warn [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]] against [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]]'s outer-relative monopoly. The transcript writes 丁弘, but the wiki normalizes the figure to Ding Hong because the office, He-era anti-Dou setting, and solar-eclipse memorial match the received Eastern Han actor.

## Current Profile

Ding Hong enters the anti-Dou sequence after [[YuanAnEasternHan|Yuan An]] dies. His warning uses the language of Heaven, disaster, and dynastic precedent to make a direct political point: maternal relatives may begin close to the throne, but without virtue and institutional restraint they can become existential threats.

His profile is therefore an omen-coded remonstrance profile. He does not command the palace operation, but he gives Emperor He a court-legible diagnosis of why Dou Xian's power cannot be treated as normal kinship privilege.

## Key Characteristics

- Becomes situ after Yuan An's death in the 92 CE anti-Dou crisis.
- Uses a solar eclipse as the formal occasion for warning against outer-relative monopoly.
- Cites Lu-clan and Ai-Ping-era precedents to show how kinship power can endanger the dynasty.
- Converts a concrete administrative sign, officials visiting Dou's household before office, into evidence of court capture.
- Functions as a bridge between visible ministerial dissent and Emperor He's palace operation.

## Evidence

- Omen-coded warning: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-766-lishi-jiaogei-ni-weishenme-yao-guyi-fa-piqi-lrs-mguyfmnxe970hdwjvmevl8oc|Hanji 766]] places Ding Hong's memorial after a sixth-month solar eclipse and frames it as criticism of powerful maternal relatives.
- Historical precedents: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-766-lishi-jiaogei-ni-weishenme-yao-guyi-fa-piqi-lrs-mguyfmnxe970hdwjvmevl8oc|Hanji 766]] has Ding invoke earlier Lu-clan and Ai-Ping failures to argue that relatives without virtue produce dynastic danger.
- Administrative capture: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-766-lishi-jiaogei-ni-weishenme-yao-guyi-fa-piqi-lrs-mguyfmnxe970hdwjvmevl8oc|Hanji 766]] says new governors and two-thousand-shi officials visit Dou Xian's household before taking office, showing where real authorization has shifted.

## Qualifications

Ding Hong's memorial is part of the warning and legitimation environment, not the secret execution channel. The source assigns the operational channel to Emperor He, Zheng Zhong, Liu Qing, palace guards, and northern army command.

## What Changed

- Hanji 766 creates Ding Hong as a standalone Eastern Han entity.
- The current profile normalizes transcript 丁弘 to 丁鸿 and records the reason.
- His page now anchors the early He-era branch of omen-coded remonstrance against outer-relative power.
