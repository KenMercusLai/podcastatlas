# 高一丁 / Gao Yiding

[[ep242-duli-youxi-shi-yitiao-gaoqian-hao-saidao-ma-lrqbzrceumbuggzuero5qb4jv3zu]] adds Gao's host role in a young-side-income episode. He frames [[IndieGameCommercialization|independent games]] as a concrete way to test whether "搞钱" paths are real businesses, then keeps the conversation tied to [[WuJunIndieGameCreator|巫君]]'s operating details and [[WangYiranSanlian|王依然]]'s broader reporting on creator communities.

Gao Yiding is the [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] host in [[ep270-yi-mei-xinpian-de-manchang-zhengtu-women-li-suanli-ziyou-haiyou-duoyuan-lm7lxlmcnjwnawtq-9typc-fnrci]]. He frames the episode around why chips became newly visible through AI, electric vehicles, car-chip shortages, capital-market attention, and global technology competition.

His role in the source is to keep a broad technical subject anchored in ordinary questions: why chips are everywhere, why [[GPU|GPUs]] fit AI, what fabs and packaging plants do, and how the price of compute eventually reaches users through token costs and application design.
