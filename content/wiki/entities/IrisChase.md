# Iris Chase / 艾丽丝

## Overview
Iris Chase is the elderly narrator of [[TheBlindAssassinNovel|《盲刺客》]], the sister of [[LauraChase|Laura]], and the concealed lover and author disclosed by the novel's nested structure.

## Current Profile
Iris is first legible through roles assigned by family and public life: dutiful elder sister, economically strategic wife, and survivor of a respectable household. Her late manuscript and the embedded novel disclose a second history of desire, secrecy, resistance, failure, and guilt. This does not replace the passive image with a simple heroic one; both constraint and agency remain true.

Her narration is belated. Only after Laura's death does Iris understand more of the abuse her sister endured, including Richard's coercion and Laura's pregnancy, and her own disclosure about Alex helps precipitate the final departure. The manuscript cannot restore Laura or obtain her forgiveness. Its remaining work is to transmit a hidden family history, accept responsibility, and assert that Iris once lived beyond the identities others assigned her.

## Key Characteristics
- Elderly first-person narrator reconstructing family history near the end of life.
- Sister whose love and assigned responsibility coexist with failure to see Laura clearly.
- Wife constrained by an economically strategic and abusive marriage.
- Secret lover whose relationship with Alex preserves desire and limited resistance.
- Concealed author of the novel publicly attributed to Laura.
- Retrospective witness seeking neither simple exoneration nor complete closure.
