## Overview

司马相如 / Sima Xiangru enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-654-jiemi-xihan-yangxiong-de-chuanqi-rensheng-lq3o-13h2in89qwh0dvchczlvrqk|Hanji 654]] as the Western Han literary model whom [[YangXiong|扬雄]] admired and imitated. The episode uses him as the comparison point that situates Yang Xiong inside the fu tradition.

## Current Profile

The source does not present a full Sima Xiangru biography. Its focused claim is that Yang Xiong admired Sima Xiangru's talent, often imitated his fu, and later came to be paired with him by later readers.

Sima Xiangru therefore functions here as literary lineage rather than political actor. His importance is to make Yang Xiong's writing legible: the episode treats Yang Xiong's 《蜀都赋》 as part of a broader Han-dynasty fu culture and as a precursor to later capital fu.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han writer used as Yang Xiong's admired predecessor.
- Represents the fu tradition against which Yang Xiong's writing is measured.
- Appears in the source as comparison rather than as independent biographical subject.
- Helps frame 《蜀都赋》 as a significant literary development.
