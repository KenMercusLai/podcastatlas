# TVB / Television Broadcasts Limited / 无线电视

## Overview
TVB / Television Broadcasts Limited / 无线电视 appears in the wiki through 王晶's account as the television institution that trained writers, actors, comedy timing, production discipline, and future film workers inside [[HongKongFilmIndustry]].

## Current Profile
In this source, TVB matters less as a company history than as a practical training machine. Wang says his own comedy craft came from writing for variety programming under deadline and acceptance pressure, and he also describes TVB as a place where older actors and production people could pass technique to younger workers.

The source also records Wang's criticism that later TVB became short-termist and missed a stronger mainland-market move. That claim remains a practitioner judgment from one interview, but it gives the wiki a useful contrast between TVB as earlier talent engine and TVB as later constrained incumbent.

## Key Characteristics
- Television workplace that trained fast writing, comedy rhythm, and production reliability.
- Talent feeder into Hong Kong film through writers, actors, and production workers.
- Apprenticeship setting where learning came from seniors, live production, and deadline pressure more than formal curriculum.
- Symbol of 1970s Hong Kong upward mobility and work intensity.
- Later criticized in the source as short-termist and slow to plan for mainland expansion.
