# Princess Stephanie of Belgium

## Overview

Princess Stephanie of Belgium is [[CrownPrinceRudolf]]'s arranged wife and the mother of [[ElisabethMarieOfAustria]] in the Mayerling prehistory episode.

## Current Profile

The source uses Stephanie to show how dynastic marriage worsens Rudolf's role entrapment rather than stabilizing him. [[FranzJosephI]] arranges the marriage, Stephanie later describes the wedding night as terrifying and disillusioning, and the relationship deteriorates after the birth of their daughter. The episode also says Rudolf likely transmitted gonorrhea to Stephanie, preventing more children and compounding the succession problem around the [[HabsburgDynasty]].

## Key Characteristics

- Stephanie's marriage to Rudolf is presented as a dynastic arrangement by [[FranzJosephI]].
- Her recollection of the wedding night makes the marriage a site of fear and disillusionment rather than romantic repair.
- She and Rudolf have one daughter, [[ElisabethMarieOfAustria]], but the marriage does not produce another direct male heir.
- The source links the marriage's deterioration to Rudolf's sexuality, illness, and violent fantasies while keeping the psychological interpretation source-scoped.

- Dynastic arrangement: [[689-a-murderous-affair-the-habsburgs-greatest-scandal-part-1-glt6194086702]] says [[FranzJosephI]] arranges Rudolf's marriage to Stephanie, daughter of King Leopold II of Belgium.
- Wedding-night fear: [[689-a-murderous-affair-the-habsburgs-greatest-scandal-part-1-glt6194086702]] records Stephanie's later description of their wedding night as terrifying and disillusioning.
- Child and succession context: [[689-a-murderous-affair-the-habsburgs-greatest-scandal-part-1-glt6194086702]] says Stephanie and Rudolf have [[ElisabethMarieOfAustria]], later called the Red Archduchess.
- Illness and deterioration: [[689-a-murderous-affair-the-habsburgs-greatest-scandal-part-1-glt6194086702]] says Rudolf's likely gonorrhea is said to have been passed to Stephanie and prevented further children.
