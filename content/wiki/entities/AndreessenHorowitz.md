## Overview
Andreessen Horowitz, also known as a16z, is a venture-capital firm appearing in the bounded sources as an investor-network actor, an AI-infrastructure backer, a subject of a source-scoped antitrust inquiry, and the institutional context for [[MarkAndreessen|Marc Andreessen]]'s founder and technology worldview.

## Current Profile
The sources do not provide a complete firm history. They show four narrower roles: relationship leverage in [[ParkerConrad]]'s account of the Zenefits aftermath; reported investment in [[Runta]] and its agent-runtime thesis; a Bloomberg-reported interlocking-directorate inquiry involving [[Databricks]] and [[Fivetran]]; and the venture platform behind Andreessen's arguments about founder selection, institutional resistance, AI development, and technological optimism. Together these make a16z a capital, network, and ideas institution, while leaving firm-wide policy and conduct questions source-scoped.

## Key Characteristics
- Venture platform associated with founder selection and non-consensus technology investment.
- Relationship network capable of changing incentives around startup and accelerator conflicts.
- AI-infrastructure investor connected in the sources to long-running agent execution and governance.
- Public ideas institution through its co-founder's arguments about innovation, AI, and regulation.
- Governance subject where board relationships and market influence can attract antitrust scrutiny.
