# 《一块滚石》 / A Drift in the South

## Overview
《一块滚石》 is [[XiaoHaiPoet|小海]]'s Chinese memoir; A Drift in the South is its English version translated by [[TonyXiaoHaiTranslator|Tony]]. The project grew from the nonfiction essay 《深圳南流记》 after translation and publication in Granta.

## Current Profile
The episode presents the memoir as an artist-development narrative grounded in factory labor, migration, poetry, rock music, friendship, and changing ideas of freedom. Chinese editing and English translation proceeded in parallel but did not produce identical books: the represented account gives the editions different chapter counts, arrangements, and amounts of retained poetry. The English structure also makes the relation between consumer devices and factory labor more explicit.

The “rolling stone” metaphor joins movement to obstruction. It describes repeated jobs, cities, failed settlements, and continued action rather than effortless escape or completed upward mobility. International publication broadened the work's readership and created personal encounters, but the episode preserves disagreement over whether the book is primarily social history, worker testimony, or an artist's formation.

## Key Characteristics
- Memoir expanded from Xiaohai's earlier nonfiction 《深圳南流记》.
- Combines labor history with poetry, music, migration, friendship, and artistic development.
- Exists in related but non-identical Chinese and English editorial forms.
- Uses a rolling-stone metaphor for obstructed but continuing movement.
- Invites both production-chain awareness and literary reading beyond hardship spectacle.
