# Andy Stalman

## Overview
Andy Stalman is the branding strategist and entrepreneur interviewed in [[how-modern-brands-build-loyalty-purpose-and-identity-andy-stalman-shekhar-natarajan-podcast]]. [[ShekharNatarajan]] introduces him as "Mr. Branding," and the episode uses his biography and business practice to connect brand identity, culture, AI, and human meaning.

## Current Profile
The source presents Stalman as a practitioner who treats branding as an organizational discipline rather than a marketing department output. His personal path from [[Argentina]] to [[Spain]], his work at [[Lacoste]] and [[AeropuertosArgentina2000]], and his later company-building through [[TotemBranding]] all support a view of brands as identity, purpose, values, behavior, and sensory experience.

## Key Characteristics
- Branding strategist identified in the source as "Mr. Branding."
- Argentine-born operator whose crisis background informs a resilience and creativity frame.
- Former media, [[Lacoste]], and airport executive who moved from employment into entrepreneurship in Spain.
- Co-founder associated with [[TotemBranding]] and a methodology around why an organization exists and who it is.
- Advocate of [[BrandDoing]]: brand value depends on repeated behavior rather than only communication.
- AI-era brand thinker who argues that AI magnifies the organization's real alignment, privacy discipline, and culture.
