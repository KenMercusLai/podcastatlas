# Star Wars

[[29-qianmian-yingxiong-edipusi-zhengzai-xiaban-ji-ditie-590112891]] adds [[StarWars|Star Wars]] as the clearest modern influence case for [[JosephCampbell|Joseph Campbell / 约瑟夫·坎贝尔]]. The episode says [[GeorgeLucas|George Lucas]] drew on [[TheHeroWithAThousandFaces|《千面英雄》]] and [[HeroJourneyNarrative]], making the franchise a standard example of how [[Monomyth|单一神话]] moved from comparative mythology into popular storytelling.

[[StarWars|Star Wars]] is the franchise background for [[42-anduo-fengqi-yu-qingping-zhi-mo-616391638]]. The episode sketches the familiar frame of a republic becoming [[GalacticEmpire]], the Empire oppressing the galaxy, and resistance developing through the [[RebelAlliance]].

The source argues that [[Andor|《安多》 / Andor]] is special inside this setting because it leaves the Skywalker-family and heroic-bloodline frame for ordinary people, local communities, prison workers, bureaucrats, and morally compromised organizers. That makes the franchise useful here as a contrast case for [[AntiHeroicResistanceNarrative]].
