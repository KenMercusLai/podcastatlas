# Lingxi Interactive Entertainment / 灵犀互娱

Lingxi Interactive Entertainment / 灵犀互娱 appears in [[zhongguo-yinglai-hanbao-kaidianchao-guochan-huangningmeng-jiage-huiluo-1006920543]] as the [[Alibaba]] gaming unit that Bloomberg reportedly said would be sold to 星宸资本. The source says the transaction may value Lingxi at more than $1.5 billion, and describes the Guangzhou-based unit as having more than 1,000 employees and games such as 《三国志·战略版》.

The page's role is to anchor Alibaba's portfolio-focus move. In the episode, Lingxi is not analyzed mainly as a game studio; it is evidence that Alibaba may keep shedding non-core assets while treating [[Qwen]], AI, and cloud computing as more strategic.
