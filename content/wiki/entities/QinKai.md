# 秦开 / Qin Kai

秦开 / Qin Kai enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-3-jingke-ciqin-weihe-jiujiu-bu-xingdong-zai-deng-shui-liihe-kgwigu3bggxmyofgvotged]] as the ancestor used to explain [[QinWuyang|秦武阳]]'s prestige. The episode says Qin Kai had once been a hostage among northern Hu peoples, returned to [[YanState|燕国]], and then defeated [[Donghu|东胡]], pushing them back more than a thousand li.

In this ingest, Qin Kai is not the main narrative actor. His function is reputational: the memory of a successful Yan frontier commander helps explain why Qin Wuyang looks like a plausible assistant for a high-risk mission.
