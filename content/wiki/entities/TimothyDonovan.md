# Timothy Donovan

## Overview

Timothy Donovan was the prosecution witness who said he acted as intermediary in the payment at the center of [[USVKhoury|U.S. v. Khoury]].

## Current Profile

Donovan testified that [[AymanKhoury]] agreed to pay $200,000 for a Georgetown tennis recruiting slot, gave him $180,000 in cash, and understood that [[CatherineKhoury]] would not play. His account made the prosecution initially appear strong, but later evidence disputed parts of its account of Catherine's ability, intent, and reasons for leaving tennis.

## Key Characteristics

- Described himself as the intermediary between Khoury and [[GordonErnst]].
- Said he retained $20,000 and delivered the remaining cash to Ernst's wife.
- Testified that Catherine was not qualified for Georgetown-level tennis and did not intend to compete.
- Supplied the prosecution's direct account of an agreed exchange.

## Qualifications

The source is a structured defense-centered summary rather than Donovan's complete testimony, cooperation agreement, cross-examination, or corroborating financial record. Part 2 reports that he testified with immunity and presents Catherine's conflicting account; neither summary independently resolves credibility.

## What Changed

- Established Donovan as the source of the prosecution's direct exchange narrative and a focal point of later evidentiary conflict.

## Relationships

- [[AymanKhoury]] - defendant whom Donovan said supplied the cash.
- [[GordonErnst]] - coach to whose wife Donovan said he delivered the money.
- [[CatherineKhoury]] - prospective recruit whose ability and intent he characterized.
- [[USVKhoury|U.S. v. Khoury]] - trial in which his account supported the prosecution.
- [[TrialNarrativeReframing]] - later defense evidence challenged the causal story built partly from his testimony.
