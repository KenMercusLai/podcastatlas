# On Running

## Overview
On Running is a Swiss premium footwear and sports brand represented in the wiki through running technology, China direct retail, lifestyle positioning, and its reported long-term signing of footballer [[KylianMbappe|Kylian Mbappe]] to enter football.

## Current Profile
The bounded sources show On trying to widen from premium running-and-lifestyle appeal into a broader sports brand without abandoning price or experience control. Product experimentation includes robotic spray construction for uppers; the China strategy emphasizes owned stores and apparel attachment; and the reported Mbappe agreement gives On an athlete-development partner and global attention for football footwear. The move responds to slower growth and a footwear-heavy sales mix, but remains unproven because football demands specialist products, sustained ecosystem spending, and conversion from star awareness into a comparatively narrow on-pitch purchase.

## Key Characteristics
- Premium positioning across running performance, comfort, commute, fashion, and lifestyle use.
- Recognizable sole design functioning as both product feature and brand signal.
- Product-process experimentation beyond foam and carbon plates.
- China emphasis on [[DirectToConsumerBrandControl|direct-to-consumer brand control]] through owned stores.
- Use of apparel as a possible second growth curve beside footwear.
- Reported long-term Mbappe partnership as the brand's formal entry into football shoes, apparel, and lifestyle extensions.
- Preference for category expansion and controlled inventory over discount-led growth.

- Product and market position: [[e241-paoxie-jishu-diedaishi-malasong-paojin-2-xiaoshi-kao-ren-haishi-kao-xie]] places On among performance-footwear challengers and notes robotic-arm spray printing for uppers.
- Direct retail and apparel: [[138-angpao-zhongguo-zhong-zhiying-chaoji-xingxing-bu-banka-feng-tou-quan-1-138-1]] links owned China stores to price control, experience, lower legacy distributor burden, and apparel attachment.
- Football commitment: [[canban-anaya-bianshen-remen-jingdian-yidu-xiaoshou-nan-de-dichan-xiangmu-ruhe-baituo-kunjing-1017012362]] reports a ten-year Mbappe signing, athlete participation in development and testing, and a planned 2027 football-shoe launch.
- Entry logic and barriers: [[angpao-qianxia-mubapei-zhihou-paoxie-pinpai-zuo-zuqiu-shengyi-haiyou-naxie-nanti-1018738205]] links the move to slowing growth, price discipline, a footwear-heavy mix, LightSpray transfer, jerseys and lifestyle shoes, while qualifying it with incumbent sponsorship moats and uncertain attention-to-sales conversion.

## Qualifications
The agreement's duration, compensation, equity, bonuses, development role, and launch timing are relayed through reporting rather than contract documents. Entry through one global athlete does not establish product quality, distribution, club or team adoption, cultural legitimacy, or share in a category still led by [[Adidas]], [[Nike]], and [[Puma]]. LightSpray's running-shoe relevance does not itself prove football-boot performance.

## What Changed
- Reframed the Mbappe deal as a response to slower growth and a footwear-heavy revenue mix.
- Added the reported equity and performance-bonus structure, LightSpray transfer, and apparel/lifestyle extension plan.
- Sharpened the distinction between athlete attention and sustainable football-category demand.
