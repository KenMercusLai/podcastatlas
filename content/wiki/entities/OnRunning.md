# On Running

## Overview
On Running is a Swiss premium footwear and sports brand represented in the wiki through running technology, China direct retail, lifestyle positioning, and its reported long-term signing of footballer [[KylianMbappe|Kylian Mbappe]].

## Current Profile
The bounded sources show On trying to widen from premium running-and-lifestyle appeal into a broader sports brand without abandoning controlled experience. Product experimentation includes robotic spray construction for uppers; the China strategy emphasizes owned stores, price discipline, and apparel attachment; and the reported Mbappe agreement provides a route into football footwear. The expansion is strategically legible but not yet proven: football is dominated by entrenched brands, and the source says the first football products are expected only in 2027.

## Key Characteristics
- Premium positioning across running performance, comfort, commute, fashion, and lifestyle use.
- Recognizable sole design functioning as both product feature and brand signal.
- Product-process experimentation beyond foam and carbon plates.
- China emphasis on [[DirectToConsumerBrandControl|direct-to-consumer brand control]] through owned stores.
- Use of apparel as a possible second growth curve beside footwear.
- Reported ten-year Mbappe partnership as the brand's formal entry into football shoes.

## Evidence
- Product and market position: [[e241-paoxie-jishu-diedaishi-malasong-paojin-2-xiaoshi-kao-ren-haishi-kao-xie]] places On among performance-footwear challengers and notes robotic-arm spray printing for uppers.
- Direct retail and apparel: [[138-angpao-zhongguo-zhong-zhiying-chaoji-xingxing-bu-banka-feng-tou-quan-1-138-1]] links owned China stores to price control, experience, lower legacy distributor burden, and apparel attachment.
- Football expansion: [[canban-anaya-bianshen-remen-jingdian-yidu-xiaoshou-nan-de-dichan-xiangmu-ruhe-baituo-kunjing-1017012362]] reports a ten-year Mbappe signing, athlete participation in development and testing, and a planned 2027 football-shoe launch.

## Qualifications
The football agreement, duration, development role, and launch timing are relayed through the source's reporting rather than contract documents. Entry through one global athlete does not establish product quality, distribution, team adoption, or share in a category still led by [[Adidas]], [[Nike]], and [[Puma]].
