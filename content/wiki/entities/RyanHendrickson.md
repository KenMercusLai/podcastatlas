# Ryan Hendrickson

Ryan Hendrickson is the engineer whose story opens [[older-workers-arent-retiring-should-they-be-forced-to]]. The source says he worked on [[NASA]] missions involving rovers and spacecraft, had been promoted several times, and then lost a supervisor opportunity to someone with roughly 40 to 45 years at the organization.

Hendrickson matters in the wiki because his story makes [[CareerMobilityBottleneck]] concrete. He acknowledges the older colleague had stronger credentials in some respects, but the experience still made delayed retirement feel like a blocked path rather than ordinary competition.
