# Dominican Republic

The Dominican Republic appears in [[tech-20260818-tech-pod-128-tech-20260818-tech-pod-128]] as one of the places exploring spaceport capacity as commercial launches grow globally. The episode does not analyze a specific Dominican project in detail; it uses the country as evidence that [[SpaceLaunchCapacityBottleneck]] is becoming an international infrastructure opportunity.

In the wiki, the Dominican Republic is therefore a source-scoped example inside [[SpaceportSitingConstraint]] and [[SpaceEconomyInfrastructure]]. Its mention sits beside [[China]] and countries on Africa's east coast as signs that launch geography may become more geographically distributed if U.S. sites remain constrained.
