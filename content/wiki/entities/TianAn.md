# 田安 / Tian An

田安 / Tian An enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-143-xiangyu-shi-ruhe-feijin-xinji-nongsi-chuhuaiwang-mixin-lstfzfvbd2h9i39f7y5dxrbxxwv2|Hanji 143]] as the Qi-side figure made Jibei king in [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s post-Qin settlement. The appointment forms one part of Xiang Yu's division of the Qi field alongside [[TianShi|田巿]] and [[TianDu|田都]].

The page records Tian An as a settlement node rather than a developed independent actor in this source. His significance is that Xiang Yu fragments Qi into multiple kingships while excluding [[TianRong|田荣]], whose earlier refusal to follow Xiang Yu's orders leaves him outside the reward structure.
