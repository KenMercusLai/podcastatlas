# 田不李 / Tian Buli

田不李 / Tian Buli appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-66-zhongguo-lishishang-naxie-diwang-shi-bei-huohuo-e-si-de-1-lvl1uxjadiedqwad-skxrwf-usnq]] as the adviser [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] assigns to [[ZhaoZhang|赵章]] after making Zhao Zhang Anyang Jun in the Dai region. The source calls his role a chancellor-like position, which the host treats as abnormal for an ordinary fief.

His importance is institutional. By giving Zhao Zhang both territory and Tian Buli as a governing partner, Zhao Wuling Wang turns consolation into a rival power base inside [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. The episode therefore makes Tian Buli one of the concrete mechanisms by which [[AffectionDrivenSuccessionRisk|宠爱驱动的立储风险]] becomes organized factional danger.

[[LiDui|李队]] describes Tian Buli as violent, proud, and hard to control. In that diagnosis, Tian Buli's personal temperament matters because it joins Zhao Zhang's resentment against [[ZhaoHe|赵何]] and makes a palace or field crisis more likely.
