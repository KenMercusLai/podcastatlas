# Napoleon Bonaparte / 拿破仑

Napoleon Bonaparte appears in [[21-hong-yu-hei-ta-si-yu-zhencheng-569042001]] mainly as the symbolic horizon for [[JulienSorel|Julien Sorel]] and [[Stendhal|Stendhal]]. The episode stresses that Stendhal had lived through the Napoleonic age, while many later writers inherited it more as myth or aspiration.

For Julien, Napoleon represents an imagined route where talent, will, and military achievement might break birth hierarchy. The tragedy is that [[FrenchRestoration|Restoration France]] has made that route largely unavailable, pushing Julien toward clerical performance and aristocratic service instead.

## Connections
- [[Stendhal]] - writer whose historical experience makes the Napoleonic background concrete in the episode.
- [[RedAndBlackNovel|《红与黑》]] and [[JulienSorel]] - novel and character shaped by Napoleonic aspiration.
- [[FrenchRestoration]] - later order that narrows the military route.
- [[ClassMobilityPerformance]] - Julien's substitute strategy after the heroic path closes.
