# Richard Sutton

Richard Sutton appears in [[e226-liaoliao-deepmind-chuangshiren-hasabisi-yige-kexuejia-yu-shikong-de-ai-jingsai-7abda28b-99c6-4ebc-8c0d-37bcc77f6a73]] as the reinforcement-learning theorist whose school influenced [[DavidSilver]] and the early [[DeepMind]] route. The episode contrasts Sutton's reinforcement-learning lineage with [[GeoffHinton]]'s deep-learning lineage before describing how DeepMind later fused the two in practice.

In this wiki, Sutton anchors the older agent-and-reward view of AGI. The source uses that lineage to explain why [[ReinforcementLearningAGIPath]] was compelling before [[ChatGPT]] and [[LanguageModelScalingBet]] shifted frontier-model strategy.
