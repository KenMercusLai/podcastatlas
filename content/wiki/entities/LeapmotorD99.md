# Leapmotor D99 / 零跑 D99

## Overview
Leapmotor D99 / 零跑 D99 appears in the source as [[Leapmotor|零跑汽车]]'s upper-price-band test for proving the company can compete beyond entry and mid-price value vehicles.

## Current Profile
The source places D99 inside the D-platform plan, described as a roughly RMB 300,000 luxury-vehicle platform planned several years earlier. D99's role is less settled than S01, T03, or C11 because the episode says it had just begun delivery and had source-scoped initial orders above 10,000. The current profile is therefore an early upmarket proof point rather than a confirmed success.

## Key Characteristics
- D-platform vehicle tied to Leapmotor's attempt to reach around RMB 300,000.
- Used to test whether a value-for-money automaker can gain broader brand trust.
- Mentioned alongside D19 as part of a higher-price platform direction.
- Source-scoped early order signal is positive but not yet durable performance evidence.
- Connects Leapmotor's price-band coverage goal from roughly RMB 60,000 to RMB 300,000.
