## Overview

Zhang Fan of Wan is a late-Han wealthy merchant whose source role is to show how harem ties, luxury gifts, and palace access can shield local dominance until enforcement against him rebounds on the enforcers. This page is deliberately distinct from [[ZhangFanLateHan|张范 / Zhang Fan]], the later recluse connected to Yuan Shu.

## Current Profile

Hanji 871 presents Zhang Fan as a protected local actor rather than a developed biographical subject. He lives at [[WanCounty|宛]], has ties to the rear palace, provides carved luxuries and gifts into the inner court, and uses the resulting status to act forcefully in the locality.

His importance is structural. [[CenZhiLateHan|岑晊]] and [[ZhangMuLateHan|张牧]] persuade [[ChengJinLateHan|成瑨]] to arrest him, and Cheng Jin kills Zhang Fan along with a large network after an amnesty. The later complaint by Zhang Fan's wife gives [[HouLanLateHan|侯览]] and allied eunuch channels the opening to accuse Cheng Jin and [[LiuZhiTaiyuanLateHan|刘质]]. Zhang Fan therefore anchors the merchant-and-harem branch of [[EunuchPatronageViolenceShield|宦官庇护下的地方暴行]].

## Key Characteristics

- Wealthy Wan merchant with harem-linked status.
- Supplier of luxury goods and gifts into the palace.
- Local dominant actor whose influence depends on access as well as wealth.
- Enforcement target whose post-amnesty execution gives opponents a procedural attack point.
- Distinct identity from the later recluse Zhang Fan connected to Yuan Shu.
