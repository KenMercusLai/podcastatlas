# Austin Currie

Austin Currie appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] as the nationalist MP whose June 1968 occupation of a council house in Caledon made housing allocation a symbol of Catholic second-class citizenship in [[NorthernIreland]]. The house had been assigned to Emily Beattie while hundreds of Catholic families were waiting, and the [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]] removed Currie and the others from the property.

The episode groups Currie with [[GerryFitt]] and [[JohnHume]] as part of a younger Catholic political generation shaped by civil rights language and focused on housing, local voting, electoral boundaries, and policing reform.
