# 孙承昊 / Sun Chenghao

## Overview
Sun Chenghao appears in the wiki as the [[ShengdongJixi|声东击西]] guest explaining the 2026 [[NATO]] summit, transatlantic responsibility rebalancing, European strategic autonomy, and China-facing implications.

## Current Profile
Sun is represented as an analyst of U.S.-Europe relations and alliance politics. He treats the summit less as a slogan event than as a shift toward defense spending, industrial capacity, operational capabilities, Ukraine aid, and technology security.

His most important contribution is the distinction between burden sharing and [[TransatlanticBurdenShifting]]. The United States still wants [[NATO]] as a platform for keeping Europe stable and connected to broader strategy, but it also wants European members to assume more regional security responsibility so U.S. resources can move toward global and Indo-Pacific priorities.

Sun's China-facing reading is issue-specific. He argues that [[UnitedStates|U.S.]] and [[EuropeanUnion|European]] policy may converge around economic security, AI, investment screening, and critical minerals, but that Europe is not simply following a total confrontation line and still leaves cooperation space in multilateral governance.

## Key Characteristics
- Guest analyst focused on U.S.-Europe relations, NATO, and alliance politics.
- Interprets the 2026 NATO summit through implementation, spending, industrial capacity, and military capability rather than only diplomatic language.
- Distinguishes ordinary burden sharing from U.S.-led [[TransatlanticBurdenShifting]].
- Frames [[EuropeanDefenseAutonomy]] as dependence reduction rather than anti-American military separation.
- Separates European China policy from a simple U.S.-China competition template.
