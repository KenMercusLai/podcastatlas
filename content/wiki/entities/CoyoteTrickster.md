# Coyote / 凯奥蒂

[[CoyoteTrickster|Coyote / 凯奥蒂]] is the North American culture hero and trickster foregrounded in [[32-yindianren-jiang-le-yi-bai-wan-ci-de-gushi-shi-maerkesi-de-laoshi-596386714]]. The episode presents him as a mixed figure: part creator, part Prometheus-like helper, part Loki-like deceiver, part practical teacher, and part comic troublemaker.

In the source's retellings, Coyote receives transformative power after sleeping through a divine naming order, defeats river monsters, restores salmon movement, teaches fishing rules, changes rivers, kills dangerous beings, creates or explains mineral mountains, and steals fire through coordinated animal help. He also boasts, sulks, tricks others, pursues marriage, and accepts rules that remain morally odd. This makes him a strong extension of [[TricksterAmbivalence]] beyond the wiki's existing [[Anansi]] branch.
