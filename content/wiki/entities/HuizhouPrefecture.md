# Huizhou Prefecture / 徽州

Huizhou Prefecture / 徽州 is the historical region at the center of [[no-211-huishang-wangshi-cong-qianshi-buxiu-dao-zuiniu-fengtou-1002275189]]. The episode emphasizes that old "徽商" refers to merchants from this region, not all modern [[Anhui]] merchants. It should not be confused with [[Huizhou|惠州]] in Guangdong, which is a separate place already represented in the wiki through the Su Shi exile branch.

The source describes old Huizhou as a one-prefecture, six-county region including Shexian, Xiuning, Wuyuan, Qimen, Yixian, and Jixi. Its mountainous terrain, limited farmland, and population pressure made outward migration normal, while nearby Jiangnan demand made commerce feasible.
