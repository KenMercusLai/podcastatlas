## Overview

李郃 / Li He enters the wiki as the Hanzhong household official who turns a dangerous gift mission to [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] into a timing solution. In Hanji 767, he first warns against contact with Dou, then volunteers to carry the gift himself and delays the trip until Dou's fall makes the [[HanZhongLateHan|Hanzhong]] administrator safe from the purge.

## Current Profile

Li He's episode begins with judgment before tactics. He tells the Hanzhong administrator that Dou Xian is arrogant, monopolizes power, and is near disaster, so a gift connection will be dangerous. When the administrator still feels unable to refuse the social pressure around Dou's concubine-taking, Li He asks to be the envoy.

The mission then becomes controlled delay. Li He travels slowly and watches the political situation; by the time he reaches Fufeng, the Dou family has fallen and the court is removing officials who had interacted with Dou. Because the Hanzhong gift was not delivered, the administrator escapes the cleanup. The source uses Li He as a model of "outer round, inner square" conduct: he does not stage open refusal, but he preserves the substantive judgment through timing.

## Key Characteristics

- Hanzhong official whose political reading of Dou Xian is sharper than his superior's fear-driven compliance.
- Tactical actor who accepts the outward mission while changing its timing and risk profile.
- Uses delay as a defensible administrative act rather than as empty procrastination.
- Protects his superior by preventing completed association with a falling outer-relative powerholder.
- Embodies the source's distinction between principled adaptability and mere smoothness.
