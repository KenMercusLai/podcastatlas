# Andrew Nelson

Andrew Nelson appears in [[tech-20260107-0107-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260107-0107-mp-tech-pod-128]] as a fifth-generation farmer and software engineer in Farmington, Washington. He grew up on the farm, studied computer science and business, and returned to agriculture with a builder's interest in how software can fit real field work.

Nelson matters in the wiki because his workflow makes [[DigitalAgriculture]] concrete. He uses GPS-guided equipment, [[DroneCropMapping]], [[CropWizard]], and [[ChatGPT]]-style voice tools, but he keeps [[HumanJudgmentUnderAI]] visible by treating AI as a secondary sounding board while still consulting human agronomists.
