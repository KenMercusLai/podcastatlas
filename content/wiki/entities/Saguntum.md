# Saguntum / 萨贡托

## Overview

Saguntum / 萨贡托 is the Iberian city south of the Ebro whose relationship with Rome and siege by [[HannibalBarca]] make it the immediate diplomatic trigger for the Second Punic War.

## Current Profile

The city sits inside the broad area that the Ebro agreement appears to leave to Carthaginian influence, yet the [[RomanRepublic]] develops friendly relations with it. That overlap lets Rome warn Hannibal away while Hannibal argues that Saguntum lies inside the recognized Carthaginian sphere and claims allies within the city need protection.

Hannibal besieges Saguntum in 219 BC. Rome does not intervene before the city falls in early 218 BC; afterward, anger and reputational pressure harden its response into a demand that [[Carthage]] surrender Hannibal.

## Key Characteristics

- Located south of the Ebro inside a disputed sphere of influence.
- Connected to Rome without the episode fully resolving its treaty status.
- Targeted after Hannibal consolidates control farther inland.
- Becomes a symbol of Roman delay, Carthaginian aggression, and incompatible claims.

## Qualifications

The episode does not settle Saguntum's exact legal status, the content of every agreement, Hannibal's expectation of Roman action, or the reported scale and form of the sack. Those details remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Established Saguntum as the immediate trigger rather than the sole cause of the war.
- Preserved the territorial and legal ambiguity behind the competing claims.

## Relationships

- [[HannibalBarca]] - besieger who frames intervention as protection within the Carthaginian sphere.
- [[RomanRepublic]] - outside partner whose warning and later surrender demand raise the stakes.
- [[Carthage]] - polity held responsible for Hannibal's action.
- [[SaguntumWarTrigger]] - escalation mechanism centered on the city's ambiguous position.
