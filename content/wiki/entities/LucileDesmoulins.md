# Lucile Desmoulins

## Overview

Lucile Desmoulins was the wife of [[CamilleDesmoulins|Camille Desmoulins]], arrested during the Dantonist trial and guillotined one week after her husband.

## Current Profile

The source presents Lucile as both a family member appealing to former friendship and a politically useful alleged conspirator. Her requests that [[MaximilienRobespierre|Robespierre]] spare Camille were ignored. [[LouisAntoineSaintJust|Saint-Just]] then falsely linked her to a supposed mass prison escape, helping justify the decree used to silence the Dantonist defendants. She was imprisoned before Camille could send his final letter and was executed after writing a farewell to her mother.

## Key Characteristics

- Her relationship to Camille placed family intimacy inside revolutionary factional conflict.
- She appealed to Robespierre through his former friendship with her husband.
- She was falsely implicated in an alleged prison-escape conspiracy.
- Her arrest helped justify procedural coercion during the Dantonist trial.
- She was executed one week after Camille.
