# Roone Arledge

Roone Arledge is the ABC sports executive in [[the-nfl-1]] who saw the potential for nationally televised football and later helped create [[MondayNightFootball]] with [[PeteRozelle]]. The source credits Arledge with understanding that production, story, camera placement, announcers, music, and highlights could make football into prime-time entertainment.

Arledge's role connects [[ABC]] to the NFL's broader [[SportsMediaRights]] strategy. He made televised football more than a relay of the game; it became a packaged weekly media event.
