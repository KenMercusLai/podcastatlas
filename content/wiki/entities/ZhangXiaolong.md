# Zhang Xiaolong / 张小龙

Zhang Xiaolong, or 张小龙, appears in [[yao-shunyu-laidao-tengxun-300tian-1-176-1]] as the WeChat-side product leader whose priorities help explain why [[WeChatVLM]] remains separate from [[TencentHunyuan]]. The source says he is heavily involved in WeChat's AI work and cares strongly about privacy, user data, and control inside [[WeChat]].

The episode also uses a familiar WeChat origin story: Zhang is quoted indirectly as thinking that WeChat benefited from being born in Guangzhou, away from Shenzhen headquarters interference. In this source, that memory becomes part of the explanation for Tencent's AI tension: edge autonomy can preserve product judgment, but AI compute scarcity pushes [[Tencent]] toward more centralized coordination.
