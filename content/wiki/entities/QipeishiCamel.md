## Overview

齐佩石 is the camel that carried equipment during [[QilinPhotographer|麒麟]] and [[MartinKenyaGuide|马丁]]'s long-distance journey across [[Kenya]].

## Current Profile

Initially experienced as stubborn and difficult, Qipeishi became the animal Qilin regarded as right for the trip. The journey required time for forage, rest, parasite checks, and nighttime protection rather than treating the camel as inert equipment. Being taken twice by armed people and escaping twice made Qilin recognize an attachment that had developed through shared travel; Qipeishi was later placed in a private conservation area in central Kenya.

## Key Characteristics

- Carries expedition equipment while the two people walk.
- Requires active forage, rest, health checks, and camp protection.
- Displays behavior that resists complete human control.
- Becomes a companion rather than only transport infrastructure.
- Drives the journey's emotional recognition and part of its ending.
