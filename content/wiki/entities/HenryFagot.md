# Henry Fagot

Henry Fagot appears in [[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] as the code name for one of [[FrancisWalsingham]]'s moles inside the French embassy at Salisbury Court. The mole helped expose correspondence around [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]] and the [[ThrockmortonPlot]].

The episode treats Henry Fagot's identity as disputed. It notes John Bossy's 1991 suggestion that the mole may have been Giordano Bruno, but also says many historians reject or doubt the claim because the evidence is circumstantial.

For the wiki, Henry Fagot is therefore an intelligence uncertainty node. The important grounded claim is not the person's exact identity, but that Walsingham had penetrated [[MichelDeCastelnau]]'s embassy before the later [[BabingtonPlot]] operation.
