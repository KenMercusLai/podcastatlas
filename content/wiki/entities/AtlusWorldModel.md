# Atlus World Model

## Overview
Atlus is the [[WorldLabs]] model described in [[daisen-jinru-diandong-yashua-lingyu-chuantong-jindian-jiameng-chixu-shousuo-1010976825]] as a multimodal world model that accepts text, image, video, and 3D inputs and infers spatial context and alternate viewpoints.

## Current Profile
The source presents Atlus as a world-model product that turns ordinary media into richer spatial understanding. Its most important wiki role is to connect [[WorldModels]] with practical capture workflows: location inference from photos, inferred views from a single scene, and bullet-time-style video made from a handful of ordinary phones or action cameras.

## Key Characteristics
- Is framed by the source as a multimodal world model handling text, images, video, and 3D inputs.
- Infers where a photo may have been taken and what other viewpoints might look like.
- Extends world-model work from generated clips toward scene understanding and spatial reconstruction.
- Can support bullet-time-like capture from three to five consumer devices, according to the episode.
- Strengthens World Labs' positioning around spatial intelligence rather than only text or image generation.
