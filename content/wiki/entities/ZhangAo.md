# 张敖 / Zhang Ao

张敖 / Zhang Ao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-169-pandian-liubang-dengjiqian-buweirenzhi-de-gushi-1-liwkaygjtyafxxpdwzq9pdhuzroi|Hanji 169]] as the Zhao king named among the regional rulers urging [[LiuBang|刘邦]] to accept the imperial title after [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s death.

The source does not develop Zhang Ao's biography. His narrow function is political: the accession petition presents Liu Bang's new title as supported by several kings whose wartime or settlement positions now have to be folded into the emerging [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]] order.

This page also keeps Zhang Ao separate from the earlier [[ZhangEr|张耳]] page. Hanji 169 only needs him as a Zhao-side participant in the postwar recognition of Liu Bang's kingship-to-emperorship transition.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-174-liubang-weihe-dafeng-zijide-chouren-2-luet7gqkako8idt9o3ccqq7cgpbt|Hanji 174 part 2]] gives Zhang Ao another narrow founding-order mention. In the first-class merit ranking, he is named third after [[XiaoHe|萧何]] and [[CaoShen|曹参]]. The episode does not expand his biography, but it places him inside the same public hierarchy that turns battlefield, rear, and political settlement contributions into ranked early Han status.
