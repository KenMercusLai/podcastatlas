# Chio Reichman

## Overview

Chio Reichman was a [[TheHolocaust|Holocaust]] survivor whose oral history, as presented by [[MalcolmGladwell]], recounts escape from Treblinka during the 1943 uprising and survival in Nazi-occupied Poland through a chain of limited help.

## Current Profile

The bounded source uses Reichman's experience to show that survival need not depend on one complete rescue. Farmers, a sheltering family, a frightened acquaintance, and other helpers supplied food, clothing, papers, transport, concealment, or work. Their actions differed in courage and duration, but their combined effect kept Reichman alive until liberation.

## Key Characteristics

- Escaped Treblinka during the 1943 uprising.
- Survived in Nazi-occupied Poland through multiple temporary forms of assistance.
- Provides the episode's strongest historical case for [[DistributedKindness]].
- His account preserves helpers' fear and limits rather than recasting every helper as fearless.
