# Geoff Hinton

Geoff Hinton appears in [[e226-liaoliao-deepmind-chuangshiren-hasabisi-yige-kexuejia-yu-shikong-de-ai-jingsai-7abda28b-99c6-4ebc-8c0d-37bcc77f6a73]] in two roles. First, the source uses him as the deep-learning counterpart to [[RichardSutton]]'s reinforcement-learning lineage. Second, it says [[Google]]'s earlier acquisition pricing for Hinton's team influenced how it valued [[DeepMind]] researchers.

The episode later names Hinton alongside [[YoshuaBengio]] as a scientist publicly worried about AI risk. That makes this page a bridge between [[ReinforcementLearningAGIPath]], the deep-learning route that eventually helped [[AlphaFold]], and [[AIAlignmentGovernance]].
