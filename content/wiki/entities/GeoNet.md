# GeoNet

## Overview
GeoNet is tracked here as the decentralized high-precision location-network project pitched in the All-In investment competition. Its wiki role is a crypto-linked physical-infrastructure case: the claimed network utility is real-time kinematics location correction, while the proposed investment exposure is the GeoD token rather than company equity.

## Current Profile
The source presents GeoNet as a decentralized RTK network that can localize position to roughly two centimeters compared with roughly two meters for ordinary GPS. Kyle argues that individuals deploy rooftop base stations and earn Geo tokens, allowing the network to scale across 150 countries and more than 11,000 cities while keeping infrastructure costs low. The investment case depends on customer usage, token buybacks funded by revenue, limited float, and future robotics or AI demand; the main objections are liquidity, securities-law risk, and possible satellite competition.

## Key Characteristics
- Decentralized RTK location network claiming centimeter-level positioning accuracy.
- Physical deployment model uses individually owned rooftop base stations compensated with Geo tokens.
- Use cases in the pitch include agriculture, robotic mules, autonomous-vehicle maps, robotic lawn mowers, and drones.
- Revenue model is tied to token value accrual because the source says about 80% of revenue buys Geo tokens in the open market.
- Investment exposure is the GeoD token in the source's account, not company equity.
- Risks include liquidity, fully diluted market-cap interpretation, securities law, and satellite-based alternatives.

## Qualifications
GeoNet's customer list, revenue run rate, coverage, token ticker, and legal structure are source claims from a pitch format. The page does not verify whether the token is a security, whether buybacks are enforceable, whether customers are recurring, or whether satellites can eventually match the cost and accuracy profile.
