# Sarah Godfrey

## Overview
Sarah Godfrey is the owner of [[SarahsGlutenFreeGoods]], the fresh allergy-friendly bakery featured as a caller case in [[advice-line-with-ben-goodwin-of-olipop-f9d3fe17-eb2c-440d-b223-990fed970727]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Godfrey as a founder whose personal dietary constraint gives the bakery credibility but also keeps the business tightly dependent on her own labor. Her question is how to keep a fresh, safe, local promise while moving beyond one-person production.

## Key Characteristics
- Owner and operator of [[SarahsGlutenFreeGoods]] in Westfield, Massachusetts.
- Personally connected to the gluten-free and allergy-safe eating problem.
- Runs baking, marketing, operations, farmers markets, and wholesale drop-offs herself in the source account.
- Has standardized recipes, making future staff training possible in principle.
- Is debt-averse but has used a small local grant and is considering larger capacity options.
