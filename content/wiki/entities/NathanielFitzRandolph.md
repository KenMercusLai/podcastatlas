# Nathaniel Fitz Randolph

## Overview

Nathaniel “Fitz” Randolph was a Patriot captain who led nighttime raids from New Jersey to [[StatenIsland|Staten Island]], captured Loyalists, and became the principal personal antagonist of [[ChristopherBillopp|Christopher Billopp]].

## Current Profile

Randolph used the narrow water frontier to seize soldiers and prominent civilians while, according to even Loyalist accounts cited by the episode, treating captives decently and discouraging household looting. Billopp placed a £100 bounty on him; Randolph matched it and later abducted Billopp after identifying him at a ball.

Loyalist forces captured Randolph at his New Jersey residence in February 1779 and imprisoned him in New York in irons. Patriot forces then captured Billopp again as leverage, making both men participants and bargaining assets in a cycle of raid, retaliation, coercive confinement, and exchange.

## Key Characteristics

- New Jersey-based Patriot raiding captain.
- Specialist in nighttime crossings and targeted capture on Staten Island.
- Adversary who turned Billopp's bounty into a reciprocal personal contest.
- Captor remembered by hostile accounts as restraining looting and treating prisoners decently.
- Prisoner whose treatment drove retaliatory pressure on Billopp.
