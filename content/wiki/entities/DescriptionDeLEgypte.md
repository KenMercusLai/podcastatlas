# Description de l'Egypte

## Overview

The *Description de l'Egypte* was the monumental published record derived from the scholars who accompanied Napoleon's Egyptian expedition.

## Current Profile

The work catalogued ancient monuments as well as dress, instruments, and other features of Egypt. It gave the failed campaign a durable scholarly afterlife and helped build European [[Egyptology]], while its encyclopedic gaze also exemplifies how survey, classification, and imperial presence could reinforce one another.

## Key Characteristics

- Emerged from the expedition's organized corps of savants.
- Recorded both antiquities and aspects of contemporary Egyptian life.
- Converted field observation into a large reproducible knowledge artifact.
- Helped France retain cultural influence after military defeat.
- Joined preservation and curiosity to the classificatory power of occupation.
