# 《她来自马里乌波尔》 / She Came From Mariupol

[[SheCameFromMariupol|《她来自马里乌波尔》]] is the nonfiction book discussed in [[05-ta-laizi-maliwuboer-ruguo-ni-kanjian-guo-wo-ceng-jiandao-de-533375925]]. The [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] episode presents it as [[NataschaWodin|纳塔莎·沃丁]]'s late-life search for her mother [[YevgeniaSheCameFromMariupol|叶弗根尼亚]] and the family world around [[Mariupol]] that had been cut off by war, exile, and silence.

The book's role in the source is to turn macrohistory into recoverable personal traces. Forum posts, photographs, family names, state victim lists, relatives, and [[LidiyaSheCameFromMariupol|莉蒂娅]]'s notebooks connect [[PersonalArchiveAsHistory]] to [[FamilyMemoryNonfiction]]: the point is not only to know what happened in the twentieth century, but to restore people who had become rumors, absences, and inherited pain.
