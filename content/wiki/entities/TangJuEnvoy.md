# 唐雎 / Tang Ju (Anling envoy)

唐雎 / Tang Ju enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-117-1-papa-dalian-lishishang-shuangwen-de-kaishan-bizu-lr0h-kfeguvatattnj-mz8fgvnc]] as the envoy [[AnlingJun|安陵君]] sends to answer [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] after Anling refuses a proposed land exchange. He is the central figure in the [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] story commonly remembered as 《唐雎不辱使命》.

The episode's Tang Ju is a weak-side speaker who survives by refusing the ruler's frame. When Ying Zheng threatens "天子之怒," Tang Ju answers with [[ClothCladAngerPersuasion|布衣之怒]], implying that a commoner or unarmed envoy can still turn imperial-scale violence into immediate five-step bodily danger. That reversal is why the host calls the scene a [[FaceSlappingReversalNarrative|face-slapping reversal narrative]].

The page keeps him separate from [[TangJu|唐举]], the physiognomist who appears in the Cai Ze branch. It also keeps the story source-scoped: the episode stresses that Tang Ju's court access, sword-carrying, reported age, and Anling chronology all make the anecdote historically doubtful even though it remains rhetorically powerful.
