# Waterloo Rods

Waterloo Rods is the fishing-rod company [[RyanSeiders]] built before co-founding [[YETI]], as discussed in [[yeti-ron-and-ryan-seiders-how-two-brothers-turned-a-400-cooler-into-a-2-billion-brand-d7f8e0e8-51a5-4444-8c1d-f5c43c353285]]. The episode uses Waterloo as evidence that Ryan already understood serious anglers, specialty outdoor retail, and physical-product selling before joining [[RoySeiders]].

## Key Points
- Ryan built and sold fishing rods, including a durable rod associated with grass-fishing use and Terry Oldham.
- The business reached roughly 1,000-1,500 rods per year at about $175 per rod.
- Ryan sold Waterloo and used proceeds from the sale to help fund YETI's first container purchases.
- Waterloo strengthened the brothers' [[FounderProductFit]] because YETI's first customers overlapped with the same fishing and outdoor-performance world.
