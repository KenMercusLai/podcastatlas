# Arnold Schwarzenegger

## Overview

Arnold Schwarzenegger is an actor and later California governor represented here through his unexpected direction of [[ChristmasInConnecticut1992|Christmas in Connecticut]], a low-budget television remake undertaken after *Terminator 2*.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Schwarzenegger's directing detour as a bounded experiment in a new role. He volunteered through agent Lou Pitt, accepted a reported $100,000 fee, and chose a family-friendly television movie whose small scale limited his exposure while allowing him to direct a feature-length production.

His influence was decisive rather than ceremonial. He requested more humor and jeopardy, including an opening rescue; encouraged rewrites; consulted filmmakers including Ivan Reitman and Steven Spielberg; and stayed closely engaged through a 20-day shoot even as *Terminator 2* was becoming a major commercial success.

The result did not establish him as a director. Crew recollections identify awkward staging and selected takes that failed their scenes, while participants also remember his enthusiasm and the production's joyful improbability. His continuing friendship with producer [[StanBrooks]] outlasted their only film together and later extended into public service when Governor Schwarzenegger appointed Brooks to the California Film Commission.

## Key Characteristics

- Used a small television movie as a lower-risk experiment in feature directing.
- Exercised substantial creative influence through action, comedy, and rewrite requests.
- Sought advice from experienced filmmakers while learning a new role.
- Remained intensely engaged despite the project's modest scale and visible production limits.
- Produced a flawed result that collaborators could remember fondly without treating it as a directing success.
