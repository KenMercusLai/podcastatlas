# Meng Fanqing / 孟繁青

Meng Fanqing / 孟繁青 is the [[EvolventAI|Evolvent AI]] co-founder interviewed in [[cong-zhengliu-dao-hecheng-shuju-dao-rsi-moxing-jingzheng-de-xiayige-jiaodian-shi-shenme-duitan-evolvent-ai-lianchuang-mengfanqing-lq1xnhp4muc3ividqhvd0ul77qmi]]. In the source, he describes himself as an NUS PhD student who previously interned at [[Kimi]] on agent and infrastructure work and participated in Kimi model releases.

His main contribution to the wiki is a data-and-environment view of [[RecursiveSelfImprovement|RSI]]. Meng argues that post-training advantage increasingly depends on [[SyntheticAgentData]], [[EnvironmentBasedAgentBenchmarks]], [[RSIData]], and fast validation loops rather than only on novel algorithms or raw compute.

## Key Claims
- Model-lab post-training has become service-like enough that data construction, environment design, benchmark integrity, and iteration speed can differentiate researchers.
- [[ModelDistillation]] is a practical shortcut, but the source does not treat it as the decisive cause of Chinese model progress.
- [[EvolventAI]] is positioned between model labs and application companies, not as a foundation-model lab or a pure harness company.
- Good researchers for this work need hands-on engineering ability: building environments, running training, cleaning traces, and validating whether data improves models.
