## Overview
Vivi's Vacation is a small sustainable swimwear label shaped by its founder Vivi's Hawaii upbringing, Brazilian family background, apparel training, and long experience making bikinis.

## Current Profile
The brand has authentic raw material for differentiation but has not yet compressed fabric quality, construction, Brazilian manufacturing, and founder story into a fast, consistent reason to buy. Its immediate task is not broad scale but learning which emotional and functional themes its few current customers actually value.

## Key Characteristics
- Manufactures inventory in Brazil using sustainable fabrics.
- Connects Hawaii surf culture, Brazilian heritage, and professional apparel-design experience.
- Sells DTC and reported about 15 sets sold after its recent relaunch.
- Needs consistent positioning across its website, packaging, email, imagery, and social media.
- Has a plausible narrow-community entry point but little behavioral evidence of repeatable demand.
