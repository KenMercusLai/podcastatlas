## Overview
[[CaoZhengLateHan]] appears in the wiki as the son of [[CaoCao]] used to bind [[YuanTan]] through marriage after Cao detects Yuan Tan's secret maneuver around defectors.

## Current Profile
In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1061-yuanshao-zhizi-he-shafu-chouren-jiecheng-qinjia-lnsnj6ipiyscw-vopnjjgoxpnt5r|Hanji 1061]], Cao Cao responds to Yuan Tan's suspect behavior by marrying Cao Zheng to Yuan Tan's daughter. The page records Cao Zheng as a political instrument inside Cao Cao's temporary stabilization of Yuan Tan rather than as an independently developed actor.

## Key Characteristics
- Functions as a Cao-family marriage bond in a volatile Yuan-family campaign environment.
- Links Cao Cao and Yuan Tan after Yuan Tan has already signaled unreliability.
- Illustrates how kinship can stabilize a tactical alliance without creating genuine trust.

## Evidence
- Marriage stabilization: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1061-yuanshao-zhizi-he-shafu-chouren-jiecheng-qinjia-lnsnj6ipiyscw-vopnjjgoxpnt5r|Hanji 1061]] says Cao Cao marries Cao Zheng to Yuan Tan's daughter after discovering Yuan Tan's private seal-lure for [[LyuKuangLateHan]] and [[GaoXiangLateHan]].

## Qualifications
Cao Zheng's broader biography is not developed by the current evidence. His role here is source-scoped to Cao Cao's management of Yuan Tan.
