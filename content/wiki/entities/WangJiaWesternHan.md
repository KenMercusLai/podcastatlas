## Overview

王嘉 / Wang Jia is synthesized as a late [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] chancellor whose case shows how direct court correction could become lethal under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-598-gudai-chaotang-de-douzheng-you-duo-canku-luof-1bxomtcsrkcvmuplpugaw4d|Hanji 598]] presents him as a senior official trying to preserve capable ministers after the [[LiangXiangWesternHan|梁相]] review dispute, then as a target once Aidi links that recommendation to his anger over [[DongXian|董贤]].

## Current Profile

Wang Jia's profile is built around high-risk remonstrance inside a suspicious court. After Liang Xiang is removed for asking to reexamine the Dongping king Liu Yun case, Wang Jia recommends Liang's group again and argues that the ruler should remember service and ability rather than only past fault. The episode treats this as talent-preservation logic, but Aidi reads it as challenge.

The danger sharpens after Wang Jia refuses to smoothly accommodate Dong Xian's fief-enlargement edict. Aidi does not attack only that incident; he reopens the older Liang Xiang recommendation and asks why Wang Jia first joined criticism and later praised the same officials. When [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] and others recommend sending Wang Jia to the Tingwei prison, Wang Jia's staff understands the summons as an expected suicide signal. Wang Jia refuses poison, breaks the cup, dresses formally, receives the edict, and submits to custody.

## Key Characteristics

- Late Western Han chancellor and New Fu marquis exposed under Han Aidi.
- Senior official who tries to re-legitimate Liang Xiang's group after their dismissal.
- Remonstrant whose talent-preservation argument is interpreted as imperial defiance.
- Official whose Dong Xian-related resistance becomes part of Aidi's anger.
- Target of a Tingwei summons understood by his household as a death signal.
- Refuses private poison-suicide and insists that a Three Excellencies minister should face public legal death if death is required.

## Qualifications

The current wiki evidence is source-scoped to Hanji 598. The page does not yet cover Wang Jia's full career, the complete Dong Xian edict dispute, the later outcome after this arrest, or the full legal record behind Aidi's accusation. The episode frames Wang Jia sympathetically, but his prior participation in criticizing Liang Xiang's group remains a qualification on any simple loyal-versus-disloyal reading.
