# David Garrick

## Overview

David Garrick was [[SamuelJohnson]]’s pupil, traveling companion, friend, and later celebrated actor, whose rapid theatrical success contrasted with Johnson’s longer literary precarity.

## Current Profile

Garrick entered the story as one of only three pupils at the failed school Johnson opened with [[ElizabethPorter|Tetty Porter’s]] money. He and Johnson then traveled to London together with one horse, alternating between riding and walking. Their shared arrival became the start of sharply different but connected careers.

Garrick rose through Shakespearean performance while Johnson remained dependent on hack writing. The episode allows for Johnson’s envy but also emphasizes continuing friendship: Garrick eventually helped stage Johnson’s *Irene*. He therefore connects Johnson’s failed teaching experiment, London migration, theatrical celebrity, and eventual access to the stage.

## Key Characteristics

- Pupil whose relationship with Johnson outlasted the failure of their school.
- Migration companion on Johnson’s formative journey to London.
- Celebrated Shakespearean actor whose success contrasted with Johnson’s slow literary rise.
- Friend and theatrical intermediary who helped bring Johnson’s play to the stage.

## Qualifications

This is a Johnson-centered account rather than a full profile of Garrick’s acting, theatre management, Shakespearean influence, finances, or public reputation. Johnson’s envy, the pair’s private dynamics, the causes of Garrick’s success, and the details of *Irene*’s production remain episode-attributed.
