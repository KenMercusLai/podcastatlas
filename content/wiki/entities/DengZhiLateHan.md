## Overview
Deng Zhi is a senior Deng-family actor whose profile combines visible household discipline during Empress Dowager Deng's regency with later vulnerability when the Deng family loses protection after her death.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-790-lishi-gaosu-ni-ruhe-he-lingdao-da-jiaodao-lulf0r1sdkod3luzwru0-kkrijxk|Hanji 790]] shows Deng Zhi managing household risk after his son Deng Feng accepts a horse from [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]]. He punishes both his wife and son with hair-removal sanctions and apologizes to the court, signaling an attempt to keep Deng-family privilege from appearing openly corrupt. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-794-yidai-nvjun-dengsui-qushi-hou-jing-quanjia-zaoyang-llmlhabdd09opca6-pr80q4mfoxi|Hanji 794]] later records his collapse under the post-Deng purge environment, where former privilege turns into lethal exposure.

## Key Characteristics
- Senior Deng-family figure operating under the political shadow of [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]].
- Uses household punishment and court apology to contain gift-related reputational risk.
- Serves as a counterweight to a crude reading of Deng-family power as pure impunity.
- Later becomes vulnerable once the regency patronage structure disappears.
- Connects Ren Shang's command-credit dispute to Deng-family internal discipline.

## Evidence
- Household discipline: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-790-lishi-gaosu-ni-ruhe-he-lingdao-da-jiaodao-lulf0r1sdkod3luzwru0-kkrijxk|Hanji 790]] records Deng Feng accepting Ren Shang's horse and Deng Zhi punishing his wife and son while apologizing to the court.
- Reputational containment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-790-lishi-gaosu-ni-ruhe-he-lingdao-da-jiaodao-lulf0r1sdkod3luzwru0-kkrijxk|Hanji 790]] places that punishment inside the same episode as Ren Shang's execution and Deng Zun's reward, making the gift politically sensitive.
- Post-regency collapse: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-794-yidai-nvjun-dengsui-qushi-hou-jing-quanjia-zaoyang-llmlhabdd09opca6-pr80q4mfoxi|Hanji 794]] records Deng Zhi's later fatal exposure in the Deng-family purge.

## Qualifications
The current page does not treat Deng Zhi's household discipline as proof that the Deng family lacked structural advantage. It shows that advantage required active reputation management and could become dangerous once court protection changed.
