# 《贵妃醉酒》 / Guifei Zuijiu

《贵妃醉酒》 / Guifei Zuijiu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-2-pandian-huangdimen-jiuhou-xiaopihao-livpg3zh7lsqffgca2pomiqnlvyd|Qinji 131-2]] as the performance branch of the episode's alcohol theme. The source presents [[YangGuifei|杨贵妃]] drinking after Tang Minghuang fails to arrive at Baihua Pavilion and instead goes elsewhere.

The episode treats the work as a staged version of drunken desire, abandonment, and palace humiliation. It also says older versions included more explicitly erotic material, while [[MeiLanfang|梅兰芳]] later reshaped the piece by removing or softening material that the source calls unsuitable for broader audiences.
