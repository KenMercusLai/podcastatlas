# Shake Shack

## Overview
Shake Shack is a U.S. burger chain associated with [[DannyMeyer]] and used in the coffee-bean episode as the closest comparator for [[FiveGuys]] in China. Its role in the wiki combines a hospitality-founder lineage with evidence that premium casual-fast-food launch queues can be real while still leaving unresolved questions about rollout pace and repeat demand.

## Current Profile
The China-market source places Shake Shack and Five Guys in the casual-fast-food category that offers more service, richer flavor, and more made-to-order experience than ordinary fast food. Shake Shack's China precedent is double-edged: its 2019 Shanghai opening produced long waiting lines, but the source cites CBN Data as saying its expansion slowed from 2024 and some underperforming stores closed.

The Danny Meyer episode adds origin context but does not evaluate Shake Shack's current operations. It places the company among the restaurant businesses behind Meyer's authority on [[ExpectedBehaviorCulture]], hospitality, and scaling, while the China evidence keeps the commercial judgment qualified: founder reputation and opening heat do not remove the need to defend price and repeat purchase after novelty falls.

## Key Characteristics
- U.S. premium burger chain used as the main positioning comparator for Five Guys in China.
- Company associated with Danny Meyer's restaurant-building and hospitality career.
- Casual-fast-food category marker: more service and richer product experience than standard quick-service restaurants.
- Early China launch heat was strong, with the source describing hours-long waits after the 2019 Shanghai opening.
- Later expansion caution makes the brand a warning that opening queues do not automatically become durable unit economics.
