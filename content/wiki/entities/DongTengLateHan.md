# 董腾 / Dong Teng (late Han)

[[DongTengLateHan|董腾]] appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-903-weishenme-quan-ni-yuanyuan-xiangbao-heshi-le-lokpt93muc8kasvn44vgsjki8bom|Hanji 903]] as a Yellow Gate figure whose wide contacts with [[LiuKuiBohaiKing|刘亏]] and [[ZhengSaLateHan|郑萨]] become politically dangerous. The source does not present Dong as the primary accuser or defendant; instead, he functions as part of the social network that [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]] can reinterpret after Liu refuses payment.

Dong's role is important because it shows how court access and private relationship webs can become state-security evidence. In Wang Fu's hands, association with a resentful imperial kinsman helps make the forged treason story plausible to [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]].
