# Robert Beale

Robert Beale appears in [[692-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-massacre-in-paris-part-2-glt4665799025]] as [[FrancisWalsingham]]'s brother-in-law and as a witness to the fear produced by the [[StBartholomewsDayMassacre]]. The episode says Beale warned that [[England]] needed to wake up before similar violence reached it.

Beale's second source role is informational. The episode cites him for Walsingham's later recruitment of agents in about forty overseas locations, using both royal money and Walsingham's own funds. For the wiki, Beale helps bridge [[ConfessionalTraumaMemory]] to practical intelligence-building before later [[SpyNetworkEntrapment]] cases.
