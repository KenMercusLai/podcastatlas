# Maintain Your Brain Trial

## Overview
Maintain Your Brain is the Australian dementia-prevention intervention named in [[accelerate-learning-increase-cognitive-capacity-dr-tommy-wood-scim4201272551]] as an example of multidomain risk-factor work.

## Current Profile
In the source, Maintain Your Brain helps ground dementia prevention as more than one behavior or one supplement. Wood describes it as using individualized risk factors to route participants into diet, physical activity, behavioral therapy, and online brain-training components, making it a practical example of combined cognitive, lifestyle, and clinical-risk management.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents a multidomain dementia-prevention intervention rather than a single-tool protocol.
- Uses individual risk-factor profiles to select diet, activity, therapy, and brain-training components.
- Sits beside the U.S. POINTER trial as evidence that dementia-prevention research is moving toward bundled interventions.

## Evidence
- Multidomain design - [[accelerate-learning-increase-cognitive-capacity-dr-tommy-wood-scim4201272551]] says Maintain Your Brain included diet, physical activity, behavioral therapy, and online brain training based on individual risk factors.
- Prevention context - [[accelerate-learning-increase-cognitive-capacity-dr-tommy-wood-scim4201272551]] places the trial in a discussion of cognitively engaging hobbies, processing speed, executive function, and dementia-risk reduction.
- Comparative role - [[accelerate-learning-increase-cognitive-capacity-dr-tommy-wood-scim4201272551]] discusses Maintain Your Brain alongside POINTER as a large intervention testing combined dementia-prevention levers.

## Qualifications
The source summarizes the trial as an example and does not provide full methods, effect sizes, enrollment criteria, or publication status. Claims remain bounded to the episode's description.
