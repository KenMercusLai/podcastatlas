# Hasdrubal (Third Punic War) / 哈斯德鲁巴（第三次布匿战争）

## Overview

Hasdrubal was the Carthaginian commander who led resistance during the [[ThirdPunicWar]], imposed military rule inside the besieged city, and surrendered to [[ScipioAemilianus]] during its final destruction.

## Current Profile

Carthage initially condemned Hasdrubal while trying to appease Rome after the unauthorized war against [[Massinissa]]. Once Rome demanded evacuation and demolition, he was reprieved and returned to command a city mobilized for survival. Guerrilla attacks, sallies, and pressure on Roman supply lines helped exploit disease and early Roman failures, allowing Carthage to resist longer than expected.

After Scipio Aemilianus took command, Hasdrubal withdrew inside Carthage. During the blockade he imposed a military dictatorship, reserved food for soldiers while civilians starved, and publicly tortured Roman prisoners. The episode interprets that atrocity as making negotiation politically harder, but its causal force remains inferential. At the end Hasdrubal surrendered and was spared for Scipio's triumph, while his wife rejected his submission and died with their children in the flames.

## Key Characteristics

- Commander restored when Roman terms made resistance unavoidable.
- Effective user of guerrilla attacks, sallies, and supply-line pressure during the early siege.
- Military dictator who privileged soldiers over starving civilians.
- Participant in atrocity escalation that narrowed space for negotiation.
- Final surrenderer whose survival contrasts with his family's literary death scene.

## Qualifications

This source-bounded profile distinguishes this Hasdrubal from [[HasdrubalTheHandsome]]. Roman and Greek narrative dominance requires caution around his motives, formal authority, battlefield effectiveness, dictatorship, atrocities, dialogue, surrender behavior, and the dramatic death of his family.

## What Changed

- Established the final Carthaginian commander as both an adaptive defender and a coercive ruler implicated in siege escalation.
