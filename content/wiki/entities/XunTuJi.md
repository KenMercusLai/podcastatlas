# 《训兔记》

《训兔记》 is the [[ZhengYuanjie|郑渊洁]] story that [[23-feima-pai-qiche-anquan-shi-di-yi-wei-de-572699943]] uses to explain Zheng's critique of obedience education. In the episode's summary, children who become excessively compliant are transformed into rabbits, while school authority treats rabbit-like obedience as a model to be promoted.

The story strengthens the wiki's [[AntiAuthoritarianEducation]] branch because it makes obedience itself uncanny. [[Luxixi]] is said to enter the rabbit track quickly, while [[Pipilu]] resists before being pressured into the same system, turning "good behavior" into social training rather than moral growth.
