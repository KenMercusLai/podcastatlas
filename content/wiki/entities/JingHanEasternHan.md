## Overview

Jing Han is a Gongsun Shu-side strategist in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-700-lishi-gaosu-ni-bie-rang-renzhi-piancha-zuai-nide-chenggong-lhgt6dyzgc4s-ost2nbzdhfyzofv|Hanji 700]]. The episode identifies him as a Pingling commandant under [[GongsunShuEasternHan|Gongsun Shu]] who argues that Shu must attack before [[LiuXiu|Liu Xiu]] can finish consolidating the realm.

## Current Profile

The source presents Jing Han as the clearest strategic voice inside Gongsun Shu's regime. He reads the post-eastern-war moment as a narrowing window: Liu Xiu has pacified the east but not yet settled every western rival, so waiting will give Liu Xiu time to concentrate against Shu.

Jing Han proposes a two-line offensive. Tian Rong should occupy Jiangling and use the Wushan barrier while sending documents into Wu and Chu, and [[YanCenEasternHan|Yan Cen]] should advance through Hanzhong toward Sanfu, Tianshui, and Longxi. The proposal aims to pull Liu Xiu's attention across theaters before the Han side can make Shu the single remaining target.

The episode also makes Jing Han an argumentative adviser. When Wu Zhu warns against a long-distance expedition without allied support, Jing Han rebuts by invoking Liu Xiu's own rise from weak beginnings and by treating Wei Xiao's western waiting posture as a negative example. Gongsun Shu initially accepts the plan, but opposition from Shu generals and Gongsun Guang makes him pull back and ignore later requests for command.

## Key Characteristics

- Shu-side adviser who sees delay as the central strategic danger after Liu Xiu pacifies the east.
- Offensive planner whose proposal splits pressure between Jiangling/Wu-Chu and Hanzhong/Sanfu-Tianshui-Longxi lines.
- Persuader who uses historical comparison, Liu Xiu's rise, and Wei Xiao's hesitation as argumentative evidence.
- Adviser whose plan gains initial approval but fails under internal opposition from Shu generals and Gongsun Guang.
- Source-bounded figure whose known wiki profile is strategic counsel rather than independent command.

## Qualifications
