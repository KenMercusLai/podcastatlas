# DALL-E

DALL-E appears in [[ep-17-ais-impact-on-creativity-a-consumers-perspective]] as the image-generation tool [[MarkDataScienceWithSam|Mark]] accesses through [[ChatGPT]] to create event-specific University of Illinois alumni imagery. The source contrasts this with copying generic clip art, making DALL-E part of [[AICreativeCollaboration]] for volunteer communications rather than only a model-company or synthetic-media case.

The episode keeps the use case practical: Mark prompts for images tailored to alumni events, such as diverse Illinois alumni gathered around a game-watch location. The generated image still sits inside [[HumanJudgmentUnderAI]], because the user decides whether the image fits the event, audience, brand context, and communication need.
