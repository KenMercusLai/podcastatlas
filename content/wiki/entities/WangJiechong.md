# 王杰宠 / Wang Jiechong

## Overview
王杰宠 / Wang Jiechong is identified in VOL.87 as a pediatric emergency-surgery resident at [[BeijingChildrensHospital|北京儿童医院]] and a guest on [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Wang as a public medical educator translating pediatric emergency-surgery cases into caregiver decisions. His central frame combines deliberation with speed: identify airway, circulation, injury, neurological, or abdominal warning signs; use only fitted first aid; and avoid delaying qualified care because of infection anxiety.

His examples connect immediate household actions with downstream clinical consequences. A retained airway foreign body explains persistent pneumonia and atelectasis, while a rapidly deteriorating midgut-volvulus case shows how resuscitation, urgent surgery, and focused family-clinician communication can converge under time pressure.

## Key Characteristics
- Pediatric emergency-surgery resident affiliated in the source with Beijing Children's Hospital.
- Describes emergency thinking as both careful and fast rather than reflexive or slow.
- Uses clinical cases to explain why delayed escalation can convert a manageable event into severe illness.
- Encourages caregivers to learn assessment-first prehospital skills for burns, choking, convulsions, and bleeding.
- Treats calm, structured family questions as useful inputs to urgent shared decisions.
