# Cosmos Lab

Cosmos Lab is the [[Nvidia]] team led by [[LiuMingyu|Liu Ming-Yu / 刘洺堉]] in [[150-dui-yingweida-yanjiu-fuzongcai-liu-mingyu-de-4-xiaoshi-fangtan-cosmos-3-shijie-moxing-wushu-huangrenxun-yingxiang-wode-he-ni-bu-xuyao-jibai-suoyou-duishou-lghqbpi7ehexavjv1gjrfv-24k8y]]. The source says Liu's direct team is about 80-90 people, while the broader Cosmos project involves about 200-300 people across Nvidia.

The lab's role is to build Cosmos as a [[WorldFoundationModels|World Foundation Model]] stack for [[PhysicalAI]]. In the source, that means helping developers with better data, better starting points, and better environments rather than only releasing a media-generation model. [[Cosmos3]] is the episode's current focal point: it consolidates earlier predict, transfer, reason, and policy directions into a single Omni-Model with language, video, audio, and action.

## Source Position
- Cosmos Lab is presented as an ecosystem-enabling internal team, not as a standalone startup or customer-facing application company.
- The source connects the lab to [[LargeCompanyOpenSourceStrategy]] because open releases can reduce developer burden while also helping Nvidia learn future infrastructure requirements.
- The lab's operating challenge is [[AIOrganizationDesign]] at large-model scale: shared vision, high compute cost, many contributors, and enough autonomy for teams to make local decisions.
