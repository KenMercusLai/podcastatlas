# Pacific Telephone and Telegraph

Pacific Telephone and Telegraph appears in [[tech-20260127-0127-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260127-0127-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the company that built the Palo Alto site in 1929 for telephone switchboards. The episode uses this origin to show continuity between voice networks, early internet exchange points, and today's cloud and AI traffic.

The company's role in this wiki entry is historical and site-specific. The building's later use by [[Equinix]], [[AltaVista]], and [[PaloAltoInternetExchange|PAIX]] makes it a compact example of how communications infrastructure gets reused as technical regimes change.
