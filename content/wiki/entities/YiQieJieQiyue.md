# 《一切皆契约》

《一切皆契约》 is the [[NieHuihua|聂辉华]] book used in [[79-gewei-lingdao-danfan-zan-xuedian-boyilun-690004262]] to translate [[ContractTheory]] and [[GameTheory]] into workplace, education, publishing, compensation, and organizational-design cases. The episode gives the book the subtitle frame of game and decision analysis in real life, workplace, and markets.

The book's wiki contribution is not one new formula but a portable diagnosis habit: when a situation looks like stupidity, laziness, betrayal, or greed, ask what information is hidden, what incentives are misaligned, what signal is being sent, and what contract is incomplete.
