# Ed Ferron

Ed Ferron is the [[DataScienceWithSam]] guest in [[ep-14-what-is-observability]], where he explains [[Observability]] from the perspective of software engineering, architecture, and application performance work. The source identifies him as director of full stack observability practices at [[ExigentSolutions]], with prior experience at [[Microsoft]] and [[Cisco]] and more than 25 years in software engineering and architecture.

His role in the wiki is to ground observability as an operating practice rather than a tool buzzword. He connects technical telemetry to customer-visible business transactions, security interruptions, AI-assisted anomaly detection, and real-time data use by developers, architects, operators, executives, and data scientists.
