# 皇甫郦 / Huangfu Li (late Han)

皇甫郦 / Huangfu Li (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-986-tianyu-qiwang-bixian-lingqi-kuang-lvlvu057-tnz3hal6oodhydvzvbd|Hanji 986]] as the envoy [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] sends to mediate between [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]]. Guo Si accepts the settlement attempt, but Li Jue refuses and argues that Guo Si is beneath him.

Huangfu Li then turns mediation into direct remonstrance. He invokes [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s death at [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s hands, says Li Jue's seizure of the emperor is worse than Guo Si's seizure of ministers, and warns that [[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]], [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], and White Wave-linked actors already see Li Jue's conduct as wrong. Li Jue expels him and sends [[WangChangLateHan|王昌]] after him, making Huangfu Li a case where accurate counsel increases personal danger.
