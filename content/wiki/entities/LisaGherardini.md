# Lisa Gherardini

## Overview
Lisa Gherardini is identified in [[667-the-mystery-of-the-mona-lisa-glt1735270133]] as the sitter for [[MonaLisa|the Mona Lisa]]. The episode treats her as the ordinary Florentine woman whose portrait later became an extraordinary global icon.

## Current Profile
The source presents Lisa as a woman from an old Tuscan family who married [[FrancescoDelGiocondo]] at fifteen when he was thirty. She became his second wife, had six children, raised his son from a previous marriage, and was described in Francesco's 1537 will as beloved, noble-spirited, and faithful.

## Key Characteristics
- Lisa is treated as the most credible sitter for the Mona Lisa.
- Her social world was upwardly mobile Florentine business rather than courtly celebrity.
- Her marriage to Francesco del Giocondo supplies the "La Gioconda" naming context.
- The 1503 Heidelberg evidence strongly supports her connection to Leonardo's painting.
- Her later fame is radically disproportionate to her documented social role.

## Evidence
- Sitter identification - [[667-the-mystery-of-the-mona-lisa-glt1735270133]] cites [[GiorgioVasari]] and the 1503 Agostino Vespucci note mentioning Leonardo and Lisa del Giocondo.
- Family and marriage - [[667-the-mystery-of-the-mona-lisa-glt1735270133]] describes Lisa's Tuscan family background, young marriage, children, stepchild care, and Francesco's affectionate will.
- Naming context - [[667-the-mystery-of-the-mona-lisa-glt1735270133]] links Lisa's married name to La Gioconda and to the Giocondo family.

## Qualifications
The source treats Lisa's sitter identification as strongly supported, but it does not claim full knowledge of her inner life, the commission's exact terms, or Leonardo's reason for keeping the painting.

## What Changed
