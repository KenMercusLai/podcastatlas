# 颜良 / Yan Liang (late Han)

## Overview
颜良 / Yan Liang (late Han) enters the wiki through Hanji 1034 as a [[YuanShao|袁绍]] general named in the pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] debate. The page is intentionally narrow: he functions as a test of whether impressive military names equal usable strategic strength.

## Current Profile
[[KongRong|孔融]] names Yan Liang and [[WenChouLateHan|文丑]] when worrying that Yuan Shao has strong generals as well as territory, troops, and advisers. [[XunYu|荀彧]] answers by separating reputation from battlefield reliability: in the source's framing, Yan Liang and Wen Chou have brute courage but can be captured in a single fight. Yan Liang therefore enters the wiki as part of Cao-side assessment before Guandu, not as a complete biography.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan Shao-side general invoked as evidence of apparent military strength.
- Paired with Wen Chou in Kong Rong's fear and Xun Yu's rebuttal.
- Source-scoped example of reputation or courage not automatically becoming strategic advantage.

## Evidence
- Apparent strength: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] has Kong Rong list Yan Liang among Yuan Shao's formidable assets.
- Cao-side downgrade: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] has Xun Yu judge Yan Liang and Wen Chou as brave but capturable rather than as strategic commanders.
- Guandu context: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] places the assessment immediately before Cao Cao's Guandu preparations.

## Qualifications
This page should not infer Yan Liang's wider biography from one source note. Hanji 1034 presents him through Cao-side strategic assessment, so the current profile records how he is used in that debate rather than treating the judgment as exhaustive.
