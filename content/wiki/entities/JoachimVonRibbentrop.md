# Joachim von Ribbentrop

## Overview

Joachim von Ribbentrop was [[NaziGermany|Nazi Germany]]'s foreign minister and the principal German negotiator of the [[MolotovRibbentropPact|Molotov-Ribbentrop Pact]] immediately before the invasion of Poland.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Ribbentrop as an instrument and amplifier of [[AdolfHitler]]'s expansion strategy. His diplomacy joined non-aggression agreements and economic influence in eastern and southeastern Europe to deception of allies: he reassured [[BenitoMussolini]] that Germany did not expect war for years while Berlin was preparing to attack Poland.

His most consequential role was turning an ideologically improbable Nazi-Soviet accommodation into an operational diplomatic agreement. After a German-Soviet trade deal, he flew to Moscow, negotiated with [[JosephStalin]] and Vyacheslav Molotov, accepted the secret division of eastern European spheres, and returned to acclaim in Berlin. The apparent triumph depended on the mistaken conclusion that Britain and France would not act without Soviet support.

## Key Characteristics

- Foreign minister who treated agreements as instruments for isolating the next target rather than stable restraints on German expansion.
- Negotiator who helped convert German-Soviet economic contact into a non-aggression pact and secret territorial settlement.
- Diplomat whose assurances to Italy conflicted with Germany's immediate war preparations.
- Participant in the forecast that the Soviet bargain would neutralize British and French commitments to Poland.
