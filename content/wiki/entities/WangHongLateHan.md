# 王宏 / Wang Hong (late Han)

王宏 / Wang Hong (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-966-meiyou-yexin-budong-quanmou-jiu-bie-dang-lingdao-lldn3hi1gl5v3axvklwakgf6vx3u|Hanji 966]] as the official who correctly reads the trap after [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] use [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s court to summon him and [[SongYiLateHan|宋翼]].

Wang Hong's judgment is that he and Song Yi remain [[WangYunLateHan|王允]]'s outside shield: Li Jue and Guo Si hesitate because the two men hold troops outside the immediate captured court. He therefore proposes not answering the summons and instead joining with eastern anti-Dong-Zhuo forces. The episode treats this as the last plausible move to turn danger into counterattack.

The plan fails because Song Yi refuses to disobey. Wang Hong then goes with him, is killed, and reportedly rebukes Song Yi before death for being unfit to discuss state affairs. The page frames Wang Hong as the episode's positive but defeated case of [[XingshiqingStrategicAssessment|行势情战略评估]] inside a collapsing court.
