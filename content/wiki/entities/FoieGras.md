# 鹅肝 / Foie Gras

Foie gras / 鹅肝 is the fatty luxury-food and animal-welfare case in [[ep260-yikou-feimei-zhifang-cangzhe-zhongguo-yangzhi-de-xin-gushi-linjq2fkzhqvzjcgrmhudq12vs4j]]. The episode places it between French banquet prestige, Chinese export history, Cantonese and Chaozhou restaurant use, baby-food marketing, flavored gold-bar products, and local income for goose-raising regions.

The source does not remove the ethical problem. Instead, it makes foie gras a concrete [[FoieGrasWelfareTradeoff]] case: breeding, brooding, farmer grow-out, force-feeding, feed texture, mortality, disease, liver extraction, worker skill, farmer income, and domestic/export demand all shape what "animal welfare" can mean in this production system.
