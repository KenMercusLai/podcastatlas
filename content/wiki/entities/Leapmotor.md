# Leapmotor / 零跑汽车

## Overview
Leapmotor / 零跑汽车 is presented as a Chinese new-energy-vehicle company founded by [[ZhuJiangming|朱江明]], built around full-stack self-research, manufacturing cost discipline, and a product-line recovery from early S01 failure to broader price-band coverage.

## Current Profile
The source frames Leapmotor as an engineering-led automaker rather than a story-first consumer brand. Its core claim is that cars are low-margin industrial products, so the company must use self-research, platformization, core-component manufacturing, and logistics clustering to offer high configuration at lower prices. The company is also in a transition: it has moved from survival and low-price credibility toward broader price bands, overseas expansion through [[LeapmotorInternational|零跑国际]], and a second-brand idea around vehicle cabins as "mobile space."

## Key Characteristics
- Engineering-led EV company whose founder background comes from electronics and Dahua-style embedded hardware.
- Uses full-stack self-research and self-manufacturing as a cost and quality thesis.
- Learned from S01's poor two-door coupe definition before relying on T03 and C11 for recovery.
- Positions itself as a value-for-money brand, summarized in the source as "车界的优衣库."
- Attempts to stretch from roughly RMB 60,000 entry products toward RMB 300,000 models and a second-brand space concept.
- Pursues overseas expansion through Stellantis-linked channels rather than wholly independent market entry.
