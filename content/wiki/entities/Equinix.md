# Equinix

Equinix appears in [[tech-20260127-0127-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260127-0127-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the operator of a downtown Palo Alto data center inside a historic 1929 [[PacificTelephoneAndTelegraph]] building. The episode describes Equinix as a [[ColocationDataCenter|colocation]] company: customers lease data-center space so their servers, networks, cloud providers, internet service providers, and enterprise systems can exchange traffic.

The source uses Equinix less as a financial case than as an infrastructure-history case. Its Palo Alto building ties older telephone switching to [[AltaVista]], [[PaloAltoInternetExchange|PAIX]], cloud infrastructure, and today's AI boom, showing that virtual services depend on physical places and dense interconnection.
