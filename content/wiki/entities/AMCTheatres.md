# AMC Theatres

AMC Theatres appears in [[aodesai-ba-baomihua-zhuangjin-teluoyi-muma-haolaiwu-dapian-weihe-zhadui-dingzhi-baomihuatong-1004599628]] as the cinema chain that helped validate high-priced novelty popcorn buckets. The episode says AMC bought tens of thousands of [[StarWars|Star Wars]] R2-D2 buckets designed by [[ZincPopcornBuckets|Zinc]] in 2019, priced them around $50, and sold through in a few days.

The source frames AMC's case as a theater-economics lesson. Cinemas had initially worried that odd-shaped buckets might not fit seats or concession holders, but the R2-D2 sellout suggested that fans could treat the bucket as [[CustomMoviePopcornBuckets|movie merchandise]], not just food packaging.
