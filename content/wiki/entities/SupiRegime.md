# Supi Regime / 苏毗政权

## Overview
Supi Regime / 苏毗政权 enters the wiki through Hanji 689-2 as the polity the episode identifies behind the Kharosthi enemy name "Surpiece." It is used to explain why [[JingjueKingdom]] may have disappeared through external pressure rather than through a simple local ecological-collapse story.

## Current Profile
The current source presents Supi as a powerful West Qiang polity with far greater demographic and military potential than Jingjue. It is also associated with an Eastern Women's Kingdom tradition: elite women and queens are foregrounded in the source's account, and [[Xuanzang]] is used as a later witness connecting that tradition to the cultural memory behind [[JourneyToTheWest|《西游记》]].

## Key Characteristics
- Supi is identified as the Chinese-source counterpart to the Kharosthi enemy name "Surpiece."
- It is presented as a much larger and stronger polity than Jingjue.
- The source associates Supi with West Qiang origins and with later Eastern Women's Kingdom traditions.
- Supi's wealth is linked to gold, copper, spices, yak resources, and regional strength.
- Supi attack reports are used as evidence for a military-pressure explanation of Jingjue's disappearance.

- Resource base: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-689-2-gujin-duihua-jiesuo-gui-chuideng-zhong-jingjue-guguo-de-shixue-zhi-mi-loznfitfv-dwsbgelk2wmdnk-kvu|Hanji 689-2]] says Supi's region produced gold, copper, spices, and yaks, giving it wealth and power.
- Pressure on Jingjue: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-689-2-gujin-duihua-jiesuo-gui-chuideng-zhong-jingjue-guguo-de-shixue-zhi-mi-loznfitfv-dwsbgelk2wmdnk-kvu|Hanji 689-2]] cites Kharosthi letters reporting expected attacks and later seizures of people and horses.

## Qualifications
The current page is source-bounded to Hanji 689-2. Supi's identification with "Surpiece," its exact territorial reach, and its causal role in Jingjue's final disappearance remain source-scoped inferences rather than independently settled conclusions inside this wiki.
