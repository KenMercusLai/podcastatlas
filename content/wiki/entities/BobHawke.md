# Bob Hawke

## Overview

Bob Hawke was an Australian Labor prime minister whose trade-union authority, popular larrikin image, social provision, and market-opening reforms anchor the episode's account of modern Australian politics.

## Current Profile

[[189-australian-prime-ministers-bob-hawke-scott-morrison-part-3-glt2222829202]] presents Hawke as a figure who joined elite education and popular performance: a Rhodes scholar and former union leader remembered for beer-drinking bravado, but also a disciplined, patriotic, pro-American, anti-communist Labor politician. His government strengthened universal healthcare while floating the Australian dollar, lowering tariffs, deregulating the economy, and opening [[Australia]] further to globalization. The partnership with treasurer [[PaulKeating]] created the [[HawkeKeatingReformSettlement]], but their broken succession agreement ended in Keating's successful 1991 challenge.

## Key Characteristics

- Former trade-union leader who entered parliament shortly before becoming prime minister.
- Combined a popular Australian persona with Rhodes scholarship and an intensely religious upbringing.
- Retained a moral sense of mission after losing Christian faith.
- Joined universal healthcare to currency, tariff, and regulatory reform.
- Led a pro-Western Labor government rather than an anti-American left.
- Lost the premiership when Keating's second 1991 leadership challenge succeeded.
