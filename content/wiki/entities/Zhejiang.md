# Zhejiang / 浙江

Zhejiang / 浙江 enters the wiki through [[150-zhongguo-ruhe-tiaochu-pinkun-xianjing-xiandaihua-zhi-lu-you-hui-zouxiang-hefang-lgpvp04rngagkpubte2ac6i5ji3g]] as a coastal case in the episode's account of Chinese regional development. The source highlights early township-enterprise restructuring, privatization, and private-economy formation as part of a locally specific path into market growth.

In the episode, Zhejiang helps show that [[China]]'s modernization was internally uneven. Coastal regions could use dense private networks, local initiative, and earlier marketization differently from inland regions such as [[Hubei]], making [[DevelopmentAsVariationSystem]] more useful than a one-model account.
