# William of Wykeham

## Overview

William of Wykeham was a medieval English bishop and lord chancellor who founded New College, Oxford, and [[WinchesterCollege|Winchester College]].

## Current Profile

[[368-the-history-behind-hogwarts-ancient-schools-and-revolting-students-glt6783238013]] presents Wykeham as a non-aristocratic climber who responded to the Black Death's depletion of educated clergy and administrators by building an educational pipeline. Winchester prepared poor scholars in Latin and religious discipline for New College and clerical or administrative careers, while constitutional access for sons of noble and influential families embedded elite patronage inside the charitable design.

## Key Characteristics

- Rose from outside the aristocracy to become Bishop of Winchester and Lord Chancellor.
- Treated Latin schooling as preparation for clerical, university, and administrative work.
- Linked Winchester and New College in a feeder-school model.
- Combined provision for poor scholars with limited access for socially powerful families.

- Career and context: [[368-the-history-behind-hogwarts-ancient-schools-and-revolting-students-glt6783238013]] connects Wykeham's rise and foundations to post-plague demand for trained clergy and officials.
- Institutional design: [[368-the-history-behind-hogwarts-ancient-schools-and-revolting-students-glt6783238013]] describes Winchester's seventy poor scholars, Latin discipline, religious organization, and pathway to New College.
- Mixed access: [[368-the-history-behind-hogwarts-ancient-schools-and-revolting-students-glt6783238013]] says Winchester's constitution also admitted a limited number of noble and influential families' sons.

## Qualifications

The profile comes from one narrative podcast summary rather than Wykeham's statutes or a full biography. The episode's labor-shortage explanation, Gospel symbolism for the number seventy, social-background description, and interpretation of elite access remain source-scoped.
