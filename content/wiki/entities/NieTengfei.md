# 聂腾飞

## Overview
聂腾飞 was an early private express entrepreneur from [[Tonglu|桐庐]] and a founder of the business that became [[STOExpress|申通]].

## Current Profile
The episode places Nie at the origin of the Hangzhou-Shanghai document-delivery route in the early 1990s. The service exploited the gap between time-sensitive export paperwork and slow postal delivery, then expanded through a family and hometown network before Nie died in a 1998 road accident at age twenty-five.

## Key Characteristics
- Early finder or operator of the time-sensitive customs-document niche.
- Cofounder of 盛彤, the predecessor to 申通.
- Early user of contracted local operations.
- Foundational but short-lived figure in the Tonglu carrier genealogy.

## Evidence
- Market opening: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] describes overnight train delivery between Hangzhou and Shanghai for export documents.
