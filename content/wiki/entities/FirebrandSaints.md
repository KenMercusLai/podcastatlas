# Firebrand Saints

Firebrand Saints is the bar near [[AmanNarang]] and [[SteveFredette]]'s workplace in Kendall Square where the original [[Toast]] idea began in [[toast-aman-narang-how-a-long-wait-for-the-dinner-check-launched-a-2-billion-business-6eca81bc-b0e1-47e2-a87d-f3b243e76a97]]. The source says customers could wait more than ten minutes for the check, prompting the founders to ask whether diners could pay from their phones.

The restaurant's owner, [[GaryStark]], allowed the first experiment. In the source, Firebrand Saints is not important because the first app worked; it matters because visible consumer frustration exposed a deeper operational layer and turned the story toward [[OperationalPainFounderInsight]] and [[RestaurantOperatingSystem]].
