# 《喜乐会》

## Overview
《喜乐会》 is a novel by 周宏翔 recommended by [[DaYiCultureLimited|大一]] as a multigenerational family story about bereavement, reproductive choice, hidden injury, and compulsory harmony.

## Current Profile
The episode centers three women: widowed 黄子彤, her continuing relation with mother-in-law 叶永珍, and 叶敏, whose hidden family pain becomes visible only after celebration has obscured it. The ironic title names a household that performs joy while members swallow unspoken suffering. The novel extends [[RelationalSilenceProjection]] from friendship into family attention and asks who decides when grief should end or whether a woman may begin again through childbirth.

## Key Characteristics
- Spans multiple generations of women and family obligation.
- Joins murder or intrusion suspense to buried domestic history.
- Contrasts birthday celebration with unnoticed wounds and distress.
- Questions social authority over grief, remarshaling of life, and reproductive choice.

## Evidence
- Grief and continuity: [[vol-353-qiuri-teji-pingguo-de-xiangwei-zhong-wo-jianjian-hunhun-yushui-gkwriaiohmv_a7ndcatastow]] describes 黄子彤 remaining connected to her husband's family six years after his death.
- Reproductive agency: [[vol-353-qiuri-teji-pingguo-de-xiangwei-zhong-wo-jianjian-hunhun-yushui-gkwriaiohmv_a7ndcatastow]] identifies 叶永珍's decision to have another child as a major conflict.
- Hidden injury: [[vol-353-qiuri-teji-pingguo-de-xiangwei-zhong-wo-jianjian-hunhun-yushui-gkwriaiohmv_a7ndcatastow]] notes that celebration prevents relatives from noticing 叶敏's wounds and abnormal emotional state.
