# West Bengal

West Bengal appears in [[settler-bet-a-west-bank-flashpoint-6a842bb539e73ea8f3c13a2d]] as the larger prospective test for [[UniformCivilCodeIndia|uniform civil-code]] politics. [[KiraHuyu|Kira Huju]] says the state is about 30% Muslim, roughly twice the national average, and has a reputation for bouts of political violence.

The source uses West Bengal to shift the civil-code story from [[Uttarakhand]] implementation into escalation risk. If the [[BharatiyaJanataParty|BJP]] pushes the same legal model into a more politically contested state with a larger [[IndianMuslims|Muslim]] population, the episode suggests the debate could produce civil unrest.
