# West Bengal

## Overview

West Bengal is the Indian state where the source set brings together minority-law politics, BJP control, the Bangladesh border, and the civilian costs of intensified migration enforcement.

## Current Profile

The earlier source presents West Bengal as a prospective high-stakes test for [[UniformCivilCodeIndia]] because of its large Muslim population and history of political violence. The newer episode moves from prospect to governing pressure: after a BJP state-election victory, expanded intelligence and pushback operations expose border villagers and Bengali-speaking Muslims to movement restrictions, citizenship checks, and disputed removals.

## Key Characteristics

- Has a larger Muslim population share than India overall in the source account.
- Is politically contested and vulnerable to communal-policy escalation.
- Shares a historically porous, Bengali-speaking border region with [[Bangladesh]].
- Contains Indian villages inside a controlled border strip with severe daily mobility burdens.
- Is a setting where security enforcement and religious identity can become entangled.
