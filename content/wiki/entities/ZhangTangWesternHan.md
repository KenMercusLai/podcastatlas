## Overview

张汤 / Zhang Tang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-431-zhichang-bidu-kuli-zhizi-zhanganshi-de-zhichang-zhihui-lmxey8uoliwynimxcnh1khjefenl|Hanji 431]] as the Western Han cruel official whose reputation and death form the family background for [[ZhangAnshiWesternHan|张安世]]'s later caution.

## Current Profile

The source does not profile Zhang Tang independently. It uses him as a remembered negative inheritance: a capable but harsh official close to imperial power, resented and pressured by other ministers, and finally dead by suicide. In the episode's reading, Zhang Anshi learns from that family memory that proximity to the ruler and visible influence can create jealousy even when one is useful.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official remembered in the episode as a famous cruel official.
- Father of [[ZhangAnshiWesternHan|张安世]].
- Associated with harsh, cold, and poisonous methods in the host's summary.
- Used as a cautionary family precedent for court jealousy and overexposure near the ruler.
