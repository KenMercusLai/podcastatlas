## Overview

陈元 / Chen Yuan is a source-scoped late-Han resident in Hanji 862 whose mother accuses him of unfilial conduct. His wiki role is to show how [[QiuXiangLateHan|仇香]] turns a punitive family complaint into a case of teaching, repair, and eventual filial reputation.

## Current Profile

The episode does not present Chen Yuan as a public official. He matters because his case tests how a local officer reads disorder. His mother brings a charge of unfiliality, but Qiu Xiang notices that Chen Yuan's dwelling is orderly and his farming timely, so he treats the problem as incomplete moral education rather than proof that Chen Yuan is evil.

After Qiu Xiang persuades the mother and then teaches mother and son at home, Chen Yuan is said to become a known filial son. Chen Yuan's profile is therefore not a standalone biography but an evidence node for humane local governance: a person who might have been fixed in public shame is instead redirected through instruction.

## Key Characteristics

- Ordinary local resident rather than high official or scholar.
- Subject of a mother-filed unfiliality complaint.
- Assessed through concrete household and farming evidence rather than accusation alone.
- Reformed through Qiu Xiang's moral instruction.
- Later remembered in the source as a filial son.
- Source case for non-punitive local correction.
