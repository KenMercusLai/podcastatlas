## Overview

孔奋 / Kong Fen is synthesized as a clean Eastern Han frontier official whose value is defined by contrast with officials who used the wealthy Hexi environment to enrich themselves. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-714-shi-shui-di-yi-ge-han-chu-nan-er-dang-ma-ge-guo-shi-lpkqxrl3dsok4ui0lij4c3x8zfpi|Hanji 714]] presents his four years at Guzang as a conspicuous refusal to convert office into private goods, after which [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] praises him and appoints him Wudu commandery assistant.

## Current Profile

Kong Fen appears in the Hexi incorporation branch after [[DouRongEasternHan|Dou Rong]] and five commandery governors are called to Luoyang. The episode says Guzang is rich, and many officials in the disorderly period use office there to accumulate goods and money. Kong Fen instead serves as county magistrate for four years without building private wealth.

The visible proof comes during the Luoyang journey. Other officials travel with loaded carts and goods, while Kong Fen has only one cart and no stockpile. Liu Xiu's praise turns personal clean conduct into a court-recognized personnel signal, and Kong Fen is appointed Wudu commandery assistant.

## Key Characteristics

- Frontier local official whose distinction comes from clean conduct in a wealthy administrative setting.
- Counterexample to wartime and postwar officeholders who treat local prosperity as an extraction opportunity.
- Personnel signal made visible through poverty in movement: one cart and no accumulated goods.
- Court-recognized official whom Liu Xiu praises and reuses in Wudu.

## Qualifications

The source does not reconstruct Kong Fen's whole career, family background, later Wudu performance, or the exact institutional path from county magistrate to commandery assistant. The current page should be read as a bounded clean-official profile inside the Hexi incorporation episode.
