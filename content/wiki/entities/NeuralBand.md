# Neural Band

Neural Band is the wristband control accessory named in [[tech-20260109-0109-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260109-0109-mp-tech-pod-128]] as part of [[Meta]]'s updated [[RayBanSmartGlasses|Ray-Ban smart glasses]] launch. The episode says the new version launched in the United States in September and was later put on hold in international markets because demand exceeded supply.

In the source, Neural Band matters less as a standalone gadget than as an adoption feature for [[WearableAIAssistant|wearable AI assistants]]. Gesture control can make glasses feel more useful and less phone-dependent, while the broader product still faces [[SmartGlassesBystanderPrivacy]] and [[ConsumerCameraSurveillance]] concerns.
