# Similac

Similac is the infant-formula brand used with [[Enfamil]] as the U.S. incumbent backdrop in [[bobbie-laura-modi-how-a-baby-formula-startup-took-market-share-from-two-industry-giants-4488842b-74d3-40ed-8776-5831c5934e69]]. The episode also links the 2022 national formula shortage to an [[AbbottNutrition]] Similac recall and facility shutdown.

The source makes Similac important less as a brand story than as infrastructure context. When an incumbent recall and shutdown stressed supply, [[Bobbie]]'s inventory decisions, subscriber protection, and later move toward owned manufacturing became clearer examples of [[FormulaSupplyChainResilience]].
