## Overview

卓王孙 / Zhuo Wangsun appears as a wealthy Linqiong resident, [[ZhuoWenjun|卓文君]]'s father, and a family gatekeeper whose banquet enables her encounter with [[SimaXiangru|司马相如]] before his refusal and later material support shape the couple's post-elopement life.

## Current Profile

Hanji 358-2 gives Zhuo Wangsun a structural rather than biographical role. After [[WangJiLinqiongWesternHan|王吉]]'s repeated visits make Sima look socially important, Zhuo invites him to a large banquet. The host interprets Zhuo's motive as elite display, while emphasizing that he does not know Sima has a courtship purpose.

Hanji 359-1 turns him from access provider into an opposing father and eventual material patron. It says he publicly disowns Zhuo Wenjun and withholds money after the elopement, then feels humiliated when the couple opens a tavern nearby. Relatives persuade him to relent, after which the episode reports a large transfer of property and servants. The causal role of shame and the quantities remain part of the retelling rather than independently verified biography.

## Key Characteristics

- Wealthy local household head in Linqiong.
- Father of Zhuo Wenjun.
- Banquet host who converts staged reputation into social access.
- Presented by the episode as unaware of Sima's intended courtship.
- Initially withholds support after the elopement, then provides major material backing after family mediation.

## Qualifications

The sources do not provide a full business, family, or political biography. Zhuo Wangsun's motives, the banquet and tavern scenes, his knowledge of Sima's intentions, the role of shame, the relatives' intervention, and the scale of the eventual transfer are preserved as claims within the episodes' dramatic retelling.

## What Changed

- Extended the profile from banquet mediation into post-elopement refusal, family pressure, and eventual material support.
- Separated the reported transfer from the host's interpretation that public embarrassment caused it.
