## Overview

卓王孙 / Zhuo Wangsun appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-358-2-simaxiangru-diao-fengliu-guafu-lphrip8prodrc46x__y6elbrkigz|Hanji 358-2]] as a wealthy Linqiong resident, [[ZhuoWenjun|卓文君]]'s father, and the host whose banquet brings her into hearing range of [[SimaXiangru|司马相如]].

## Current Profile

The episode gives Zhuo Wangsun a structural rather than biographical role. After [[WangJiLinqiongWesternHan|王吉]]'s repeated visits make Sima look socially important, Zhuo invites him to a large banquet. The host interprets Zhuo's motive as elite display, while emphasizing that he does not know Sima has a courtship purpose.

## Key Characteristics

- Wealthy local household head in Linqiong.
- Father of Zhuo Wenjun.
- Banquet host who converts staged reputation into social access.
- Presented by the episode as unaware of Sima's intended courtship.

- Wealth and family: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-358-2-simaxiangru-diao-fengliu-guafu-lphrip8prodrc46x__y6elbrkigz|Hanji 358-2]] identifies Zhuo Wangsun as a rich local man and Zhuo Wenjun's father.
- Banquet role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-358-2-simaxiangru-diao-fengliu-guafu-lphrip8prodrc46x__y6elbrkigz|Hanji 358-2]] says he invites Sima after Wang Ji's deference makes the visitor appear exceptional.
- Scale and interpretation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-358-2-simaxiangru-diao-fengliu-guafu-lphrip8prodrc46x__y6elbrkigz|Hanji 358-2]] describes a large household celebration and reads the banquet as a chance for Zhuo to display status.

## Qualifications

The source does not provide a full business, family, or political biography. Zhuo Wangsun's motive, the banquet's scale, and his knowledge of Sima's intentions are preserved as claims within the episode's dramatic retelling.
