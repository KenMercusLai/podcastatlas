# Laos / 老挝

[[pump-and-circumstance-is-china-the-new-opec-6a7c37287cf3c58886773a66]] adds Laos as an economic concentration case through [[BeerLao]]. The episode says the beer accounts for nearly 90% of domestic beer consumption, that [[LaoBreweryCompany]]'s 2024 sales equaled about 3.5% of GDP, and that the company was the country's largest taxpayer that year. The source treats [[Carlsberg]]'s joint venture with the Lao state as a possible investment model, but also as evidence of [[SingleProductNationalChampionDependence]] because few other industries have reached similar scale.

Laos / 老挝 is the main travel setting in the first half of [[ep267-tafang-meigonghe-yuji-hunfeiyi-yu-shuili-diguo-lkq2vxaatn4wxue7y5q1fc3-8yfk]]. [[XingHaiyang|邢海洋]] uses Luang Prabang, Vientiane, temples, morning almsgiving, local app friction, relay-style transport, and a village engagement ritual to make everyday society visible before the episode widens into regional history.

The source positions Laos between older river-valley culture and newer infrastructure. The [[ChinaLaosRailway|中老铁路]] makes movement much faster, but local ticketing and transfer systems remain dependent on agents and face-to-face coordination. The episode also uses [[LanXangKingdom|澜沧王国]], spirit-house practices, and rainy-season Buddhism to place Laos inside [[MonsoonSeasonalSocialOrder]] rather than only modern tourism.
