# Hubei Humanoid Innovation Center

## Overview
The Hubei Humanoid Innovation Center is the Wuhan robot training center described in the episode as a concrete example of China's humanoid robot data infrastructure.

## Current Profile
The source presents the center as a staged physical environment for collecting real-machine robot data. Its coffee station, grocery store, pharmacy, and factory line allow human-robot pairs to practice tasks such as pouring coffee, stocking shelves, and picking up boxes. The center matters because it turns data collection into industrial infrastructure rather than isolated lab demonstration.

## Key Characteristics
- It is located in [[Wuhan]] in [[Hubei]], making it part of China's regional robotics buildout.
- It contains staged service, retail, healthcare-retail, and factory environments for repeatable robot practice.
- It uses human-robot pairs to train robots on concrete manipulation and logistics tasks.
- It can reportedly fit about 100 humanoids, making it smaller than a planned Shanghai center but still substantial.
