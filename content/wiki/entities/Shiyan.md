# Shiyan / 十堰

Shiyan / 十堰 is the [[Hubei]] city at the center of [[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-21-shiyan-yizuo-yin-qiche-erqi-you-bugan-bei-qiche-paoxia-de-chengshi-1001770066]]. The source presents it as an unusually clear [[AutomobileCityPathDependence|automobile-city]] case because [[SecondAutomobileWorks|二汽]] and later [[DongfengMotor|东风汽车]] did not merely become local employers; they helped create the city itself.

The episode says Shiyan was formally created in 1969 during [[ThirdFrontConstruction|三线建设]], when strategic factories were placed in inland mountainous areas. Its valley geography shaped factory layout, worker communities, transport friction, and the later difficulty of hosting the land-hungry logistics and passenger-car operations that pulled Dongfeng toward [[Wuhan]].

After Dongfeng's headquarters moved to Wuhan in 2003, Shiyan lost administrative and passenger-car centrality but kept a [[CommercialVehicleIndustrialBase|commercial-vehicle industrial base]]. The source says the city still has many automotive workers, thousands of suppliers, and production lines for trucks, special vehicles, and related components.

The episode's transition picture is mixed rather than purely declinist. Shiyan tries to reuse its auto base through smart mining trucks, [[CommercialVehicleEnergyTransition|commercial-vehicle energy transition]], [[EnvisionAESC]] batteries, [[WanrunNewEnergy]] materials, [[DanjiangkouReservoir]] water resources, Chinese medicine, turquoise, and [[WudangMountain]] tourism.
