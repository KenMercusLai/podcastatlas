# 张家齐 / Zhang Jiaqi (diver)

## Overview

张家齐 / Zhang Jiaqi is the elite diver whose appearance in [[MyDaughterRealityShow|《我家那闺女》]] is discussed by [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]] as a case of early athletic independence, achievement-centered parental recognition, and adult boundary setting.

## Current Profile

[[ep389-wo-jia-na-guinu-zhang-jiaqi-dai-luwa-dongya-qinzi-guanxi-weihe-zong-chongman-shangtong-he-yihan-gkwridoopvw0aeyu2wtodgpk]] describes Zhang entering diving training at four, joining a team at six, and winning at high level by twelve. The episode treats that institutional training as demanding rather than as a substitute family: a young athlete still needs parental interest and support while navigating competition, group life, body change, and Olympic-cycle uncertainty.

In the program material as recounted, Zhang disputes claims of frequent parental visits, says she continued transferring money to her mother and that her mother knew her bank-card password, and distinguishes gifts for winning from concern for her as a person. At the family meal, she answers specific claims, resists emotional redirection, and declines her mother's proposal to start again as her mother. The episode reads this as strong subjectivity and a refusal to let a televised reconciliation overwrite two decades of experience.

## Key Characteristics

- Elite diver whose childhood training required early separation and intensive collective discipline.
- Adult daughter who distinguishes performance recognition from unconditional personal care.
- Clear, fact-focused speaker in the family confrontation presented by the episode.
- Boundary setter who refuses an immediate symbolic reset while leaving room for accountability.
- Public figure whose documented achievement and familiar growth history increase audience sympathy.

## Qualifications

This profile is limited to one podcast's synthesis of selected reality-TV scenes and reported public background. It does not establish the complete family history, the amount or present status of Zhang's money, either parent's private motives, the father's role, or what occurred off camera. The claim that her earnings were spent is explicitly treated as unverified. Audience sympathy and “strong subjectivity” are interpretations, not clinical or representative findings.
