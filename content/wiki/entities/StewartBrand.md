# Stewart Brand

## Overview
Stewart Brand appears in the wiki as a counterculture and media figure whose influence links alternative culture, whole-Earth imagery, environmental consciousness, tools, and technology creativity.

## Current Profile
The first source uses Brand briefly but strategically. After discussing climbing culture, North Beach, City Lights, and anti-mainstream scenes, Yang Bin points to Brand's influence on Steve Jobs to show how non-mainstream cultural fields can shape technology founders and product imagination.

Episode 228 adds the Whole Earth image branch. Brand is presented as asking why humans had not yet seen a complete Earth image, helping put a whole-Earth color photograph on [[WholeEarthCatalog|Whole Earth Catalog]], and supplying the closing line that humans are already godlike and should learn to handle that power. In the wiki, Brand is now a bridge node between counterculture, planetary imagery, tool culture, and [[TechnologicalResponsibilityRadius]], not a comprehensive media or technology biography.

## Key Characteristics
- Counterculture figure named in the source's innovation-environment discussion.
- Used to connect alternative culture with technology founder imagination.
- Associated by the episode with influence on [[SteveJobs|Steve Jobs]].
- Helps broaden the Dartmouth analogy from academia into cultural ecology.
- Associated with whole-Earth imagery as a way to change human scale perception.
- Supplies an "as gods" responsibility frame for expanded technological power.
