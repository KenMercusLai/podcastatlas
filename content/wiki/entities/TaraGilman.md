# Tara Gilman

## Overview

Tara Gilman is the founder of [[SaunaSociete|Sauna Société]], a brand developing stylish sauna hats for modern wellness customers.

## Current Profile

Gilman's founder insight comes from repeated sauna use, perceived hair damage, and dissatisfaction with the aesthetics of existing hats. Her early selling experience already points toward the central go-to-market constraint: the product converts through explanation and physical exposure more readily than through passive influencer visibility. Her current task is to turn that observation into repeatable venue, sampling, feedback, and gifting experiments.

## Key Characteristics

- Builds from a personally experienced wellness problem.
- Combines functional product claims with fashion and category modernization.
- Has learned most effectively through direct in-person selling.
- Is exploring corporate gifting and venue partnerships as distribution paths.
- Holds a broader sauna-apparel vision that remains dependent on entry-product proof.
