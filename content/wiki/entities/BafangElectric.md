# Bafang Electric / 八方股份

## Overview
Bafang Electric / 八方股份 is a Chinese e-bike motor and electric-assist systems company used in the wiki to ground the shift from mechanical bicycle components toward electromechanical bicycle value.

## Current Profile
The bicycle-history source introduces Bafang as a company that makes the [[EBikeValueChainShift]] concrete. Its motor business is used to show why electric assistance can move value away from drivetrain incumbents such as [[Shimano]] and toward motors, batteries, sensors, control systems, tuning, safety, service, overseas channels, and brand trust.

The downstream operator view comes through [[ChengHongningYuzhouEBike|程泓宁 / 陈鸿宁]] and [[SanWuHuan|三五环]]. He says he once heavily invested in Bafang and that Bafang later became a supplier to [[YuzhouEBike|宇宙 E-Bike]], making the company not only a value-chain example but also a bridge between investor research and hands-on premium e-bike product building.

## Key Characteristics
- It represents the motor and electric-assist side of the bicycle value chain.
- It is contrasted with [[Shimano]]'s older mechanical drivetrain-centered supplier power.
- It helps show why motors, batteries, sensors, software, tuning, and service can become new bicycle value pools.
- It is connected to [[ChengHongningYuzhouEBike|程泓宁 / 陈鸿宁]] as an earlier investment focus and later supplier in the No.222 source.
- Its wiki role remains source-scoped to e-bike value-chain discussion rather than a full company history.
