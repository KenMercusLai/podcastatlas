# Henry Leach

## Overview

Sir Henry Leach was First Sea Lord when Argentina invaded the [[FalklandIslands]] and the senior naval adviser who argued that Britain could and should send a task force.

## Current Profile

The episode makes Leach the link between [[MargaretThatcher|Margaret Thatcher]]'s political determination and an executable military option. At a moment when John Nott and other ministers doubted that the Islands could be recovered, Leach said a task force could be ready within three days, although the voyage south would take about three weeks. He also framed non-response as a long-term loss of international standing rather than merely a local territorial defeat.

His intervention did not determine the whole campaign: Cabinet authorization, merchant and naval shipping, troops, aircraft, Ascension Island access, allied support, and later operational decisions were all required. The source nevertheless treats credible professional advice at the decision point as decisive in making action imaginable.

## Key Characteristics

- First Sea Lord during the opening Falklands crisis.
- Supplied a concrete readiness estimate when political leaders doubted feasibility.
- Framed military response as a test of Britain's future international credibility.
- Connected political intent to available naval, amphibious, and logistical capacity.
- Favored prompt mobilization despite the long voyage and uncertain diplomacy.

## Evidence

- Feasibility judgment: [[170-the-falklands-war-the-task-force-sails-part-2-glt1917394924]] attributes the three-day preparation estimate and roughly three-week voyage to Leach.
- Strategic argument: [[170-the-falklands-war-the-task-force-sails-part-2-glt1917394924]] presents him as warning that failure to act would leave Britain diminished.

## Qualifications

The source offers a compressed decision narrative rather than a full naval planning or civil-military history. Leach's personal motives, the influence of his family background, exact dialogue, force readiness, and relative weight beside other advisers remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Created a profile of military advice converting political resolve into a credible expeditionary option.
