# Sydney McLaughlin

## Overview

Sydney McLaughlin is an elite American runner whose chance appearance during a UCLA reporting trip became [[MalcolmGladwell]]'s favored example of an unscripted documentary discovery.

## Current Profile

Gladwell and [[BenNaddaffHafrey]] were at UCLA for an unrelated interview when Gladwell noticed a woman running intervals and identified the quality of her movement before recognizing her as McLaughlin. In the retrospective, the encounter matters less as a career profile than as an observational test: an unplanned athlete supplied the visual and narrative proof for his attempt to communicate the beauty of running.

The source calls her a multiple world-record holder and Olympic champion, but this page retains those as episode-reported descriptors rather than a current statistical record.
