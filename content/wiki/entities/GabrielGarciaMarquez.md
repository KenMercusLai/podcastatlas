# Gabriel Garcia Marquez / 加西亚·马尔克斯

Gabriel Garcia Marquez / 加西亚·马尔克斯 appears in [[32-yindianren-jiang-le-yi-bai-wan-ci-de-gushi-shi-maerkesi-de-laoshi-596386714]] as the literary endpoint for the episode's comparison between [[NativeAmericanOralLiterature|美洲原住民口头文学]] and Latin American magical realism. The host does not make a strict influence claim; Marquez is used to name the feeling that abrupt causal leaps, living nature, family storytelling, colonial history, and the everyday marvelous can coexist without explanation.

In the source, Marquez's own remarks about grandmotherly storytelling help bridge written literature and oral narrative. This makes him part of [[OralMythMagicalRealismSource]]: magical realism becomes easier to read when Indigenous, colonial, local, and family story traditions are treated as cultural soil rather than as exotic decoration.
