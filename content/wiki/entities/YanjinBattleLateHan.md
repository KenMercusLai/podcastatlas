# 延津之战 / Yanjin Battle (late Han)

## Overview
延津之战 / Yanjin Battle is a pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] encounter in which [[CaoCao|曹操]] uses exposed baggage and controlled timing to break a larger [[YuanShao|袁绍]] cavalry force and kill [[WenChouLateHan|文丑]]. In the current wiki it functions as the concrete battlefield confirmation that Yuan Shao's visible strength could be turned into vulnerability by decision and formation control.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1044 places the battle after [[YanLiangLateHan|颜良]]'s death and Yuan Shao's angry decision to cross the Yellow River. [[JuShou|沮授]] advises caution around Yan Ford and Guandu, but Yuan Shao presses south and weakens Ju Shou's command instead. Cao Cao positions below the southern slope, lets the enemy see baggage from White Horse, and waits while Yuan cavalry gather and scatter toward the goods.

[[XunYou|荀攸]] reads the same scene as active bait rather than defensive negligence. When Cao's officers want to return to camp, Xun You warns that the lure should not be abandoned. Cao then launches fewer than six hundred riders after the Yuan force has lost cohesion, defeating the larger cavalry group and killing Wen Chou.

## Key Characteristics
- Preliminary Guandu encounter rather than the decisive Guandu battle itself.
- Takes shape after Yuan Shao converts Yan Liang's death into an angry pursuit and Yellow River crossing.
- Depends on visible baggage and relaxed horses as a lure that makes Yuan cavalry disperse.
- Shows Cao Cao and Xun You sharing timing judgment while other officers read the scene defensively.
- Turns Wen Chou from a famous Yuan-side asset into another morale shock before Guandu.

- Lure setup: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1044-guanyu-qianli-zou-danqi-xu-ganxie-caocao-busha-enqing-lpptlnc-e4zu4lcjr49bquxdw0pm|Hanji 1044]] has Cao Cao tell observers to stop reporting the growing enemy count, rest the horses, and leave the White Horse baggage visible.
- Adviser timing: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1044-guanyu-qianli-zou-danqi-xu-ganxie-caocao-busha-enqing-lpptlnc-e4zu4lcjr49bquxdw0pm|Hanji 1044]] records Xun You identifying the moment as baiting the enemy when other officers want to retreat to camp.

## Qualifications
The current evidence comes from one source note and should not be expanded into a complete military reconstruction. Hanji 1044 identifies Liu Bei as present with Wen Chou's cavalry but does not make him the tactical designer of the Yuan-side move. The battle should also stay distinct from the earlier White Horse/Yan Liang episode and from the later decisive Guandu engagement.
