## Overview

The Pazzi family was an old Florentine banking family and rival of the [[HouseOfMedici|House of Medici]] whose papal financial alliance and members' participation in the 1478 [[PazziConspiracy|Pazzi conspiracy]] ended in collective exile, confiscation, and symbolic erasure.

## Current Profile

The episode describes the Pazzi as older and more prestigious than the Medici, with banking offices across European commercial centers. Their bank financed Pope Sixtus IV's acquisition of Imola after Lorenzo refused and then displaced the Medici as papal banker.

Francesco de' Pazzi joined Girolamo Riario and Archbishop Francesco Salviati in planning Lorenzo's removal, while Jacopo de' Pazzi reluctantly accepted the plot after being told it had papal support. Francesco helped murder [[GiulianoDeMedici|Giuliano]], but the coup failed when Lorenzo survived and Florentine institutions resisted seizure.

Punishment extended beyond individual plotters: the family was banished, property was confiscated, its dolphin emblem was erased, and its name was subjected to civic suppression. The episode therefore presents both conspiracy and reprisal as family-level politics.

## Key Characteristics

- Prestigious multi-city Florentine banking family.
- Papal financier that displaced the Medici account.
- Family network whose leading members joined an assassination and coup plot.
- Rival whose commercial, ecclesiastical, and political interests converged.
- Subject of collective legal, material, and symbolic punishment after failure.

## Qualifications

This profile is organized around one hostile crisis narrative and does not provide a complete family or banking history. Individual responsibility should not be inferred from kinship alone; the episode itself says some people killed in the aftermath were innocent. The scope and duration of collective penalties require specialist verification.

## What Changed

- Created a profile distinguishing the family's banking position from individual conspirators' responsibility.
- Added collective punishment and symbolic erasure as part of the conspiracy's aftermath.
