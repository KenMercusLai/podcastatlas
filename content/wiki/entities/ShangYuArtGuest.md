## Current Profile
The episode uses Shang Yu's path from finance-oriented study and color-vision anxiety through life in Denmark, London, and New York to explain how art can include food, scent, wine, institutions, markets, and everyday perception. His contribution is strongest where audience access meets institutional structure: visitors may trust their first response, yet provenance, exhibition history, collection records, curatorial sequence, museum economics, and publicity still shape how art is understood and priced.
