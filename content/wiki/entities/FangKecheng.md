# 方可诚

## Overview
方可诚 is represented in the wiki as a Chinese-language media and communication scholar discussing youth social-media regulation, platform responsibility, and the public-health analogy between social-media companies and tobacco companies.

## Current Profile
In the current source, Fang Kecheng appears as a Hong Kong Chinese University journalism and communication associate professor and as Xu Tao's guest on [[ShengdongJixi|声东击西]]. His role is to organize the regulatory debate: he distinguishes social-media account restrictions from an internet ban, weighs objections about enforcement and evidence, and argues that platform companies should bear responsibility for harmful design rather than shifting impossible monitoring work to parents and schools.

His strongest wiki contribution is the institutional reading of the [[BigTobaccoPlatformAnalogy]]. He treats uncertainty, lobbying, research influence, responsibility shifting, and internal company knowledge as the important parallels, while keeping the social-media/tobacco comparison from becoming a claim that the products are identical.

## Key Characteristics
- Academic voice on journalism, communication, social media, and platform governance.
- Guest on a [[ShengdongJixi|声东击西]] episode about youth social-media regulation.
- Explains why imperfect enforcement does not by itself invalidate child-safety law.
- Uses the tobacco analogy to analyze uncertainty manufacture and delayed regulation.
- Shifts responsibility from parents and schools toward platforms that design the product environment.
