# 李克 / Li Ke (Li Kui)

李克 / Li Ke appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-1-lk-6ejgmjhvukqgtlz2lowu8qx-v]] as [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]]'s adviser on chancellor selection. When asked to choose between [[WeiCheng|魏成]] and [[DiHuang|翟黄]], he avoids naming a winner directly and instead gives [[SituationalCharacterTests|five situational character tests]] for judging people.

The episode also makes Li Ke a mediator between two talent standards. In the chancellor case, he helps Wei Wenhou compare referral quality rather than only visible service. In the [[WuQi|吴起]] case, he acknowledges Wu Qi's troubling desires and reputation but argues that his military ability is too valuable for [[WeiState|魏国]] to ignore, placing Li Ke beside [[UseStrengthsOverFaults]] and in tension with [[TalentVirtueDistinction]].

The source notes that Li Ke is also known as Li Kui / 李悝 and links him to early Warring States reform and legal thought. It treats his legal tradition as part of the background later associated with [[ShangYang|商鞅]] and [[LegalistRulerTechnique]], while keeping that larger intellectual genealogy compact and source-scoped.
