# Sam's Club

Sam's Club is [[Walmart]]'s warehouse-club chain in [[costco-1]], launched after [[SamWalton]] studied [[PriceClub]]. The episode treats it as Costco's obvious format competitor, but argues that the average Sam's Club produces far less revenue than the average [[Costco]] warehouse.

The comparison matters because it separates the visible warehouse-club format from Costco's fuller operating system. Membership, low prices, limited selection, supplier scale, employee retention, and culture all have to reinforce one another for [[WarehouseClubModel]] economics to compound.
