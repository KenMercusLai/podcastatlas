# Black Forest Labs

Black Forest Labs appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-open-source-wins-agi-is-here-and-scorseses-ai-toolkit-with-ceos-of-cerebras-black-forest-labs-42029880]] through CEO [[RobinRombach|Robin Rombach]]. Rombach describes the company as based in Freiburg in the Black Forest and San Francisco, built by people who worked on [[StableDiffusion|Stable Diffusion]] and [[LatentDiffusion|latent diffusion]].

The source presents Black Forest Labs as more than an image-model company. Its known open-source model family is [[Flux]], but Rombach frames the broader direction as multimodal visual models trained on images, video, and audio, then extended toward action prediction, robotics, and [[WorldModels|world models]].

The episode also makes Black Forest Labs part of the creative-workflow and rights branch. [[MartinScorsese|Martin Scorsese]] used the company's tools for visual ideation, and Rombach says the company works with some IP holders on models that can enable protected or licensed creative work.
