# Abdullah Pratt

## Overview

Abdullah Pratt is an emergency physician from Chicago's South Side whose clinical work, personal losses, trauma-center advocacy, and youth training place him at the intersection of gun violence and community health.

## Current Profile

Pratt grew up in Calumet Heights and spent time in Woodlawn, pursued science and medicine, and returned to serve communities marked by violence and health inequity. The episode reports that gun violence killed his older brother and 15 close friends, and that some people he knew arrived in his own trauma bay.

His practical response extends beyond hospital treatment. After seeing emergency personnel wait for police to secure a shooting scene, Pratt and his team taught young people how to call for help, find bleeding, compress wounds, use tourniquets, and understand legal protections for bystander assistance.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines emergency-medicine expertise with firsthand experience of bereavement and neighborhood violence.
- Chose to work in the community where he grew up rather than treating its health inequities as an external problem.
- Links current retaliation to unresolved childhood trauma, grief, status, and powerlessness.
- Treats community members as capable first responders during the interval before professional help arrives.
- Balances service with his late brother Rashad's warning that depleted self-sacrifice can undermine sustained care.
