# Callum Williams

Callum Williams is the [[TheIntelligence]] participant in [[for-bucks-sake-the-rise-of-self-made-billionaires-6a61e076562e3ad886d54c95]] who explains the episode's billionaire-wealth taxonomy. His role is to separate a moral verdict on billionaires from a narrower question: whether more billionaire wealth now comes from competitive company-building rather than inheritance, natural-resource rents, casinos, or political favor.

The source uses Williams to make [[CompetitiveBillionaireWealth]] and [[WealthTaxLegitimacy]] more precise. He argues that self-made wealth has risen across technology, entertainment, sports, finance, restaurants, and retail, but that political and justice arguments for wealth taxation should still be analyzed separately.
