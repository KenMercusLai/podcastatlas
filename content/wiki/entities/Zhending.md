# 真定 / Zhending

真定 / Zhending enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-182-weishenme-shuo-chengye-xiaohe-baiye-xiaohe-2-lqk-jifupm9lbghfzaf09igfufgb|Hanji 182 part 2]] as the name [[LiuBang|刘邦]] gives [[Dongyuan|东垣]] after taking it during the [[ChenXiRebellion|陈豨之乱]]. The source treats the name as a political marker, not just a geographic label.

The renaming matters because it follows a hard siege, punishment of prisoners who had cursed Liu Bang, and broader relief measures for counties in the Dai/Zhao theater. Zhending therefore records how early Han pacification could pair coercive conquest with a declarative claim that order had now been stabilized.
