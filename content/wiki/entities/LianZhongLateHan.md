# 廉中 / Lian Zhong (late Han)

[[LianZhongLateHan|廉中]] appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-903-weishenme-quan-ni-yuanyuan-xiangbao-heshi-le-lokpt93muc8kasvn44vgsjki8bom|Hanji 903]] as the shangshu ling who, at [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]]'s direction, forges evidence against [[ZhengSaLateHan|郑萨]] and the circle around [[LiuKuiBohaiKing|刘亏]]. The forged claim says Zheng and others planned to install Liu as emperor.

Lian's role makes the Liu Kui case a procedural failure rather than just a private quarrel. A private debt dispute passes through an inner-court actor, gains bureaucratic paperwork, reaches [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]], and produces punishment across the Bohai royal household and official staff.
