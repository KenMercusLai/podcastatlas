## Overview

隆虑公主 / Longlü Princess enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-392-1-hanwudi-shoule-meimei-de-qian-weihe-reng-yao-shasi-qinwaisheng-lhey4utgx3njqea-suiqohjybrpa|Hanji 392-1]] as [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s sister and the mother whose protective request creates the episode's law-versus-kinship dilemma.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Longlü Princess as a late-life mother deeply worried about her only son, [[ZhaoPingjunWesternHan|赵平君]]. Before death, she pays a large sum into the state treasury and asks Han Wudi to spare Zhao Pingjun if he later commits a capital offense.

Her profile is therefore not one of direct political office. She matters as the source's example of [[ParentalIndulgenceBlindness|溺爱不明]]: care becomes dangerous when it tries to preempt consequences rather than forming judgment and restraint. Wudi's grief after hearing the plea shows that her request still has emotional force, but it fails as a legal claim.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han princess and Han Wudi's close sister in the episode's account.
- Mother of Zhao Pingjun, whom she tries to protect through a prepaid plea for future pardon.
- Source case for parental love becoming a shield that may deepen a child's sense of exemption.
- Her request forces the ruler to choose between sibling affection and public law.
