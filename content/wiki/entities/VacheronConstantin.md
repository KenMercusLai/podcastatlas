# Vacheron Constantin

Vacheron Constantin appears in [[rolex-1]] as one of the high-horology brands used to contrast with [[Rolex]]. The hosts frame Rolex as less artisanal and lower-priced on average than brands such as Vacheron Constantin, [[PatekPhilippe]], and [[AudemarsPiguet]], while still behaving like a luxury company.

For the wiki, the comparison helps clarify [[HighVolumeLuxuryOperator]]. Rolex's moat depends on reliable scale, brand memory, and access control rather than only extreme rarity or handcraft.
