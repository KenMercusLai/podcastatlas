# 宋方金 / Song Fangjin

宋方金 / Song Fangjin appears in [[271-tangchao-douyao-bucunzai-le-weishenme-weishilun-hui-zai-jinnian-da-baofa-lg7-wdrdw1b9h7lwa4vwkvmyb4un]] as the screenwriter who joins the live discussion after its first half. His source-specific role is to move [[PseudoHistoryConspiracy|伪史论]] from a debunking topic into a knowledge-society topic: people often want simple cause-and-effect stories, but reality is usually multi-causal and requires trust in professional communities.

He also supplies the episode's "editor world, search world, algorithm world" frame. That connects [[ProfessionalCommunityTrust|professional community trust]] to [[PublicRelevanceAlgorithms]], because what people believe depends not only on evidence but on which media environment makes evidence visible, searchable, or repeatedly pushed.
