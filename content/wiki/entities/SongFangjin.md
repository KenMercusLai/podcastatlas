## Overview
宋方金 / Song Fangjin is represented as a screenwriter and public conversationalist who links complex-cause reasoning, media environments, long-form dialogue, screenplay authority, and film-industry change.

## Current Profile
In the pseudohistory discussion, Song moves [[PseudoHistoryConspiracy|伪史论]] from isolated debunking into a knowledge-society problem. His “editor world, search world, algorithm world” frame explains that belief depends not only on available evidence but also on which media system selects, retrieves, or repeatedly pushes it. His emphasis on multi-causal reality supports [[ProfessionalCommunityTrust]] without making experts infallible.

The “折腾” conversation adds a media-form and production branch. Song describes podcasts through [[LongFormConversationEmergence|long-information collision and emergence]], where sustained talk can deviate from its initial route and generate unplanned connections. As a screenwriter, he also distinguishes film's heavier audiovisual and scheduling demands from television while emphasizing that production is often dominated by waiting even when many crew roles remain continuously busy.

The long interview with Luo Yonghao supplies the missing creative and institutional center. Song grounds his method in character relationships, theme, local experience, and stories that newly recognize their time. He advocates [[ScreenplayCenteredProduction|剧作中心制]], criticizes [[OriginalStoryDataLegibilityTrap|data pressure that privileges controllable IP]], and treats [[MicroDramaFormBoundary|micro-drama]] as an immature but durable form. His AI position remains double-sided: he cannot see the long-run human-machine settlement, but [[ScreenwriterAIDirectingPath|direct script-to-image production]] could give writers more expressive control if they update their methods.

## Key Characteristics
- Screenwriter who interprets public claims through multi-causal rather than single-cause explanation.
- Media observer distinguishing editor-selected, search-retrieved, and algorithmically pushed information worlds.
- Advocate for long-form conversation as a site of unplanned informational emergence.
- Story theorist centering character relations, theme, novelty, and contact with current social feeling.
- Advocate for screenplay-centered coordination and stronger credit, authorship, and anti-plagiarism norms.
- Qualified optimist about micro-drama maturation, cinema's future, and AI-enabled screenwriter direction.
