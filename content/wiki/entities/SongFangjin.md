## Overview
宋方金 / Song Fangjin is represented as a screenwriter and public conversationalist who links complex-cause reasoning, media environments, long-form dialogue, and the practical labor of film production.

## Current Profile
In the pseudohistory discussion, Song moves [[PseudoHistoryConspiracy|伪史论]] from isolated debunking into a knowledge-society problem. His “editor world, search world, algorithm world” frame explains that belief depends not only on available evidence but also on which media system selects, retrieves, or repeatedly pushes it. His emphasis on multi-causal reality supports [[ProfessionalCommunityTrust]] without making experts infallible.

The “折腾” conversation adds a media-form and production branch. Song describes podcasts through [[LongFormConversationEmergence|long-information collision and emergence]], where sustained talk can deviate from its initial route and generate unplanned connections. As a screenwriter, he also distinguishes film's heavier audiovisual and scheduling demands from television while emphasizing that production is often dominated by waiting even when many crew roles remain continuously busy.

## Key Characteristics
- Screenwriter who interprets public claims through multi-causal rather than single-cause explanation.
- Media observer distinguishing editor-selected, search-retrieved, and algorithmically pushed information worlds.
- Advocate for long-form conversation as a site of unplanned informational emergence.
- Production-aware participant who keeps film craft connected to waiting, coordination, and invisible labor.
