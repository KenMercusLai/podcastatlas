# 晋烈公 / Duke Lie of Jin

晋烈公 / Duke Lie of Jin appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-3-lsbx8l7wxeioae2lwuwkkkutpdwn]] through his death in 393 BCE. His son [[JinXiaogong|晋孝公]] succeeds him, but the source treats the succession as politically thin because [[JinState|晋国]] has already become a puppet polity behind [[HanState|韩国]], [[ZhaoState|赵国]], and [[WeiState|魏国]].

His wiki role is to mark Jin's hollow middle stage between [[ThreeJinVassalRecognition|三晋受封]] and the final extinction of Jin. The ruler line continues, but the source says the office no longer has much independent strategic meaning.
