# Gilbert Gifford

Gilbert Gifford appears in [[696-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-killing-the-queen-of-scots-part-6-glt2334035496]] as the Catholic courier who arranges the beer-barrel route into [[ChartleyManor]] and as a double agent turned by [[FrancisWalsingham]]. His role is the practical hinge of the episode's [[SpyNetworkEntrapment]] account.

The apparent security of Gifford's channel encourages correspondence between [[AnthonyBabington]] and [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]]. In reality, the messages first pass through Walsingham's network and [[ThomasPhillips]] before reaching their intended recipients.
