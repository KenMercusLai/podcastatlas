# 前蜀 / Former Shu

Former Shu / 前蜀 is one of the southern/local regimes discussed in [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]]. The source presents it as a relatively strong and parallel regime in [[TenKingdomsRelationshipTypes]], rather than as a merely passive fragment outside central history.

The episode also uses [[WangJianFormerShu|王建]] and his family background to show [[WudaiSocialMobility]]: in a violent age, people outside old elite lines could build regimes through military power and local control, even as their successors faced serious inheritance problems.
