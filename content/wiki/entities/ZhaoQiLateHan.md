# 赵岐 / Zhao Qi (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-969-yuanshi-xiongdi-neidou-shui-neng-jianlou-lsgj6sewo35jpsgfm-koivijtr6r|Hanji 969]] adds Zhao Qi as an active mediator before the Shouchun and Luoyang-support branches. The court sends the elderly envoy to settle eastern conflicts; he first pressures [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] with [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s edict, then sends [[YuanShao|袁绍]] a box tied to Yuan Shao's old anti-Dong-Zhuo writing, creating leverage for a temporary settlement in [[QingzhouLateHan|青州]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-982-ba-yi-ba-liubei-ru-xu-beihou-de-liyi-zhi-zheng-ljajdc95xtylhkn5teefz26yyyz1|Hanji 982]] adds an earlier Shouchun branch. Zhao Qi is sent to [[Shouchun|寿春]] with [[MaRidiLateHan|马日磾]], but the episode contrasts him with Ma Ridi: Zhao Qi refuses to bend to [[YuanShu|袁术]], and Yuan Shu becomes wary of him rather than successfully using him.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu|Hanji 999]] later uses 赵岐 / Zhao Qi (late Han) as the official who persuades [[LiuBiao|刘表]] to support [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s return toward [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]]. The episode says Liu Biao then sends troops, supplies, and grain to the damaged court.

The source uses Zhao Qi as a small but important repair actor. He does not control an army like [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] or [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]], but his persuasion converts a regional power's resources into court support at a moment when the emperor's title has more symbolic force than material capacity.
