## Overview

赵岐 / Zhao Qi is a late-Han official whose current profile now begins with flight and concealment in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-853-jiemi-hanchao-ban-de-zhaoshi-gu-er-lvsa2pby8iyfevfoobobpik52mij|Hanji 853]] before moving into later envoy and court-support roles in Hanji 969, Hanji 982, and Hanji 999. The combined evidence makes him a survivor who later becomes a small but effective repair actor under [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 853 supplies the early survival layer. Zhao Qi resigns rather than serve under [[ZuoShengLateHan|左胜]], brother of [[ZuoGuanLateHan|左悺]], and then becomes the target of [[TangXuanLateHan|唐玹]], who destroys Zhao Qi's family and relatives. Zhao Qi escapes, changes his name, sells cakes in the [[BeihaiCommandery|Beihai]] market, and survives because [[SunSongLateHan|孙嵩]] recognizes and shelters him.

Hanji 969 and Hanji 982 show a later official who can use stature without an army. In Qingzhou, Zhao Qi pressures [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] through an imperial edict and then uses Yuan Shao's old anti-Dong-Zhuo writing to create leverage for settlement. In [[Shouchun|寿春]], the episode contrasts him with [[MaRidiLateHan|马日磾]]: Zhao Qi refuses to bend to [[YuanShu|袁术]], and Yuan Shu becomes wary rather than successful in using him.

Hanji 999 then uses Zhao Qi as the official who persuades [[LiuBiao|刘表]] to support Emperor Xian's return toward [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]]. Liu Biao sends troops, supplies, and grain to the damaged court. Zhao Qi does not control force like [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] or [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]], but his persuasion converts regional resources into court support.

## Key Characteristics

- Early survivor who refuses shameful service under a eunuch-family superior and suffers family-destruction retaliation.
- Fugitive who survives through name change, market labor, and Sun Song's hidden shelter.
- Envoy-mediator who uses imperial edict pressure and personal leverage to settle Qingzhou fighting.
- Principled court official whose conduct at Shouchun contrasts with Ma Ridi's vulnerability before Yuan Shu.
- Persuader who helps turn Liu Biao's regional resources toward Emperor Xian's damaged court.
- Repair actor whose authority is rhetorical and institutional rather than military.

## Qualifications

Hanji 853 writes 赵齐, while the existing route follows Zhao Qi / 赵岐 from the later source notes. The wiki treats the early fugitive and later envoy branches as one normalized route because the name, late-Han sequence, and received figure align, but it does not reconstruct every intervening office. The source notes do not make Zhao Qi an army holder; his impact works through edicts, reputation, and persuasion.

## What Changed

- The profile now starts with Zhao Qi's 160 CE refusal, family disaster, disguise, and Sun Song shelter.
- The later envoy material now reads as post-persecution repair activity rather than Zhao Qi's first wiki appearance.
