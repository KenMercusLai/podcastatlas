# 北海郡 / Beihai Commandery

北海郡 / Beihai Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1007-cong-shentong-dao-fuwang-kongrong-zhenshi-mianmu-puguang-lkiljj4yvl5fhslrc9gcxudxxklt|Hanji 1007]] as [[KongRong|孔融]]'s late-Han jurisdiction and failure site. The episode presents Beihai as an exposed local base: Kong Rong has ambition and cultural prestige, but the commandery lacks enough troops and grain when Yellow Turban pressure and neighboring great-power competition make isolation dangerous.

Beihai's role is strategic rather than decorative. [[ZuoChengzu|左承祖]] advises Kong Rong to seek protection from a larger force because nearby powers such as [[YuanShao|袁绍]], [[CaoCao|曹操]], and [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] already shape the regional field. Kong Rong refuses and kills him; later [[YuanTan|袁谭]] attacks from spring into summer, takes the city at night, and captures Kong Rong's wife and children after Kong Rong flees.
