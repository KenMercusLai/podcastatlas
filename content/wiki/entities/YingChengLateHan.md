## Overview
应承 / Ying Cheng is the Ba Commandery administrator paired with [[ZhongHaoLateHan|种暠]] in the failed suppression of [[FuZhiLateHan|服直]]. His case shows how a failed anti-rebellion assignment can become punishment material when Liang Ji already wants revenge against Zhong Hao.

## Current Profile
Ying Cheng appears when Fu Zhi gathers hundreds of followers in Ba Commandery and claims the title Heavenly King. Zhong Hao and Ying Cheng are responsible for suppressing the revolt but fail to finish it.

Liang Ji then uses the failure to imprison both men. [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] memorializes that killing officials for a failed but real service attempt would make future officials stop exerting themselves, so [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan|梁妠]] orders their sentence reduced and Ying Cheng loses office rather than dying.

## Key Characteristics
- Ba Commandery administrator responsible for suppressing Fu Zhi's revolt.
- Failed-service case rather than a proven traitor or corrupt actor in the current evidence.
- Co-target of Liang Ji's retaliation against Zhong Hao.
- Saved from execution by Li Gu's institutional-risk argument but removed from office.
