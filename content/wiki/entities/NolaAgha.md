# Nola Agha

Nola Agha appears in [[tech-20260330-0330-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260330-0330-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the [[UniversityOfSanFrancisco]] sports management professor interviewed by [[StephanieHughes]] about [[MajorLeagueBaseball]]'s [[AutomatedBallStrikeChallengeSystem|Automated Ball Strike Challenge System]].

Her contribution is to frame ABS as a practical governance and trust problem, not only a camera-tracking story. She explains strike-zone calibration, [[Hawkeye]]'s ball-tracking role, why fans are accustomed to digital tracking, and why human umpires remain part of the system when technology misses or misrecords a pitch.
