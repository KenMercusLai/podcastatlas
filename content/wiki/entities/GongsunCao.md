# 公孙操 / Gongsun Cao

公孙操 / Gongsun Cao appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-85-xuantaihou-weihe-sitong-30-nian-hou-ansha-le-laoqingren-lg7zy6vqtdjcufou4-mox5qkg2ct5]] as the [[YanState|燕国]] chief minister who kills [[YanHuiwang|燕惠王]] and installs [[YanWuchengwang|燕武成王]]. The episode links his coup to Yan Huiwang's lost prestige after the Le Yi replacement and Tian Dan's recovery of Qi.

The page is source-scoped because the episode does not develop his biography. His role is to make Yan's weakness visible internally: failed command succession and lost occupation gains become a palace-level power break that other states can exploit.
