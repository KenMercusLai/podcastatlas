## Overview

卫氏朝鲜 / Wiman Joseon is the polity defeated by Western Han after a prolonged siege of [[WangxianCity|王险城]], divided Han command, intensified attack, elite defection, the killing of [[UgeoWimanJoseon|卫右渠]], and final internal capitulation.

## Current Profile

The episode enters only at the campaign's final stage. Wiman Joseon exploits or is protected by the split between [[XunZhiWesternHan|荀彘]] and [[YangPuWesternHan|杨仆]], preferring Yang Pu's surrender channel while resisting Xun Zhi. After Yang Pu's arrest creates unified Han command, senior officials defect, Ugeo is killed, and remaining resistance under Seong Gi is overcome through persuasion from Ugeo's son and the son of a defector. Han then converts the conquered space into the [[HanFourCommanderies|汉四郡]].

## Key Characteristics

- Regional polity represented through the final phase of war with Western Han.
- Defender able to navigate competing Han military and diplomatic channels.
- Court whose senior officials divide when unified Han pressure increases.
- Regime whose king's death precedes rather than instantly completes political collapse.
- Conquered territory subsequently reorganized through commandery administration.

- Divided siege: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-366-2-hanwudi-henxin-zhansha-gongsunsui-ls1izjhow_j4ubgmm0lm8ruaurox|Hanji 366-2]] says Joseon prefers surrender to Yang Pu and resists Xun Zhi while the Han commanders remain divided.
- Elite fracture: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-366-2-hanwudi-henxin-zhansha-gongsunsui-ls1izjhow_j4ubgmm0lm8ruaurox|Hanji 366-2]] says senior officials defect and Sam arranges Ugeo's death after the combined Han army increases pressure.
- Final settlement and reorganization: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-366-2-hanwudi-henxin-zhansha-gongsunsui-ls1izjhow_j4ubgmm0lm8ruaurox|Hanji 366-2]] says remaining resistance is removed and four commanderies are established.

## Qualifications

This page does not adopt the episode's broad claim that the region was already Yan territory in the Spring and Autumn period. The source is also insufficient for a full account of Wiman Joseon's origins, boundaries, population, political offices, or the later history and exact geography of the commanderies. The closing Jizi passage is moral historiography rather than a direct description of this final regime.

## What Changed

- Established a bounded final-war profile centered on divided enemy command, court fracture, and administrative conquest.

## Relationships

- [[UgeoWimanJoseon|卫右渠]] - final king killed during the regime's collapse.
- [[WangxianCity|王险城]] - capital and final siege center in the episode.
- [[XunZhiWesternHan|荀彘]] - Han land commander who completes the intensified assault.
- [[YangPuWesternHan|杨仆]] - Han commander whose separate surrender channel Joseon initially prefers.
- [[HanFourCommanderies|汉四郡]] - successor administrative structure imposed after conquest.
