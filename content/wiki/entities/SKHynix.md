# SK Hynix

[[tech-20260731-0731-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260731-0731-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds a market-volatility update. [[AnitaRamaswamy]] says SK Hynix and [[Samsung]] mainly manufacture memory chips that support AI model training and inference, but the discussion also emphasizes [[StorageIndustryCyclicality]]: strong AI demand can still collide with investor fear that capacity expansion will later create another supply glut.

SK Hynix appears in [[tech-20251219-1219-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251219-1219-mp-tech-pod-128]] as one of the memory suppliers whose growth is described as strong during the AI memory cycle. The episode names it alongside [[MicronTechnology]] and [[Samsung]] while explaining how AI workloads increase demand for fast memory and storage.

Its wiki role from this source is comparative: SK Hynix helps show that [[HighBandwidthMemory]] demand is not only a Micron-specific earnings story, but part of broader [[AIHardwareSupplyChainPressure]] around AI data centers and accelerator-adjacent components.

[[cunchu-sanjutou-po-wanyi-shizhi-cunchu-chaoji-zhouqi-heshi-neng-jianding-s10e13-c47ff830-8cb5-4e58-b7d7-1a04e4e5a4c1]] makes SK Hynix one of the central beneficiaries of the [[AIStorageSupercycle]]. The source says SK Hynix led the cited HBM3E market share and relays the company's view that supply-demand conditions may remain tight through 2028.

[[vol-266-yi-ci-xing-gao-dong-etf-1002344828]] turns SK Hynix from an AI-memory supplier into the underlying asset for [[ETF7709HK|7709.HK]], a Hong Kong-listed two-times daily leveraged ETF. The episode argues that investor demand for AI memory exposure, plus difficulty directly buying the Korean stock, helped make the product large enough for its hedging to matter to the underlying share's volatility.
