# ISIS

ISIS appears in [[a-firm-andy-what-are-new-british-pms-plans-6a5deeca76f8ee25bb4bbf9c]] as one of the forces that made [[Syria]]'s war and failed-state period destructive. The episode also treats continuing ISIS threats as a risk to reconstruction because attacks can damage investor confidence even after most fighting has ended.

The source uses ISIS less as a standalone subject than as part of a reconstruction-risk mechanism. If violence persists or returns at high-profile moments, foreign capital from [[Qatar]], [[SaudiArabia]], the [[UnitedArabEmirates]], Europe, or the [[UnitedStates]] becomes harder to mobilize.
