# 孙静 / Sun Jing (late Han)

孙静 / Sun Jing (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1004-caocao-yingfeng-hanxiandi-shi-shui-de-zhuyi-lrrga-flmx5ajnhu1c42x7kooe94|Hanji 1004]] as [[SunCe|孙策]]'s uncle and tactical adviser during the attack on [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]]. When [[WangLangLateHan|王朗]] holds Sun Ce at Guling and repeated crossings fail, Sun Jing advises avoiding the fixed position, moving south by several dozen li, and striking the rear where Wang Lang is not prepared.

The source makes Sun Jing's advice a practical expression of condition-sensitive maneuver. Sun Ce follows the plan by lighting many camp fires to make the enemy think the army remains in place, then sending cavalry toward Gaoqian. Wang Lang's hurried response fails, and the defense collapses after Zhou Xing is defeated and killed.

The page is source-scoped. Hanji 1004 does not develop Sun Jing's wider biography; it records him as the adviser whose rear-attack plan lets Sun Ce turn a blocked frontal assault into a successful Kuaiji campaign.
