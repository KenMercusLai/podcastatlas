## Overview

身毒 / Shendu is the episode's intended destination for a Han attempt to find a southwestern route from Shu toward the wider world known through [[ZhangQianWesternHan|张骞]]'s report.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-306-2-hanchao-diyiwei-zouxiang-shijie-de-zhongguoren-lhm5be2xdxbj0v5zmkbqdoguf1z|Hanji 306-2]] begins from Shu bamboo staffs and fine cloth observed in [[DaxiaWesternHan|大夏]]. Their reported purchase farther southeast leads Zhang Qian to infer that the distant market may lie comparatively near Shu and could offer a route avoiding Qiang- and Xiongnu-exposed approaches.

Han envoys do not reach the target. Their reconnaissance instead reaches [[DianKingdomWesternHan|滇国]] and stops because passage farther west is blocked. Shendu therefore functions in the current evidence as an inferred destination and strategic hypothesis, not a polity directly described from successful Han diplomatic contact.

## Key Characteristics

- Proposed southwestern destination inferred from commodity circulation.
- Alternative-route objective intended to bypass more dangerous northern and northwestern approaches.
- Unreached target of a four-direction reconnaissance from Shu and Qianwei.
- Historical-geography identity complicated by inconsistent transcription in the supplied summary.

## Qualifications

The episode title identifies the target as 身毒, while the supplied body summary uses the uncertain forms 渊都/运都. This page preserves 身毒 as the normalized identity but does not independently settle the underlying text, pronunciation, exact geography, equivalence with a modern territory, or the identities of merchants and intermediaries. The goods demonstrate some form of circulation; they do not prove direct bilateral trade or a traversable state-controlled road from Shu.
