# 合肥侯 / Hefei Marquis (late Han)

合肥侯 / Hefei Marquis (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-941-shenchu-guanchang-ta-jing-xiang-ganfan-huangdi-liu-fqiz9pi7u6cltrn-cjmjz-r|Hanji 941]] as the proposed replacement ruler in [[WangFenLateHan|王芬]]'s 188 CE coup plan. The episode does not develop his biography; his significance is that Wang Fen's plan crosses from anti-eunuch action into imperial replacement.

This page is disambiguated from the modern place [[Hefei|合肥]]. In this source, "Hefei" is attached to a marquis title and a failed late-Han succession alternative, not to the contemporary city or its industrial-policy branch.
