# Montoneros

## Overview

Montoneros is the left-wing Peronist organization presented in the episode as a user of [[EvaPeron|Evita]]'s radical memory and an actor in Argentina's escalating 1970s violence.

## Current Profile

The episode places the Montoneros within a generation of students and radicals influenced by the Cuban Revolution and drawn to Evita as a symbol that could join nationalism, popular politics, and revolutionary legitimacy. Their place inside [[Peronism]] also exposes the movement's breadth and instability: Perón's return did not reconcile left-wing militants with the right-wing forces around him.

The source's final body-exchange episode is especially revealing. Montoneros stole former president Pedro Eugenio Aramburu's body and demanded Evita's return, using the dead as leverage in a struggle over memory and legitimacy.

## Key Characteristics

- They represented a left-wing and revolutionary current within Peronism.
- They used Evita's memory as a radical political symbol.
- Their conflict with right-wing Peronists became visible in the violence around Perón's return.
- They treated custody of bodies and burial as politically actionable.

- Radical memory: [[498-evita-the-mystery-of-the-missing-body-part-5-glt5587823308]] links the Montoneros to the late-1960s revival of Evita as a left-wing icon.
- Factional conflict: [[498-evita-the-mystery-of-the-missing-body-part-5-glt5587823308]] places left- and right-wing Peronist violence at Ezeiza in the movement's return-to-power sequence.
- Body leverage: [[498-evita-the-mystery-of-the-missing-body-part-5-glt5587823308]] says the Montoneros stole Aramburu's body and demanded Evita's body in return.
