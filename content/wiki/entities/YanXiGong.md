# 燕僖公 / Duke Xi of Yan

燕僖公 / Duke Xi of Yan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-2-lmfzo4sl9nd5kz3mrxdl1dl9efei]] as [[YanMinGong|燕闵公]]'s son and successor in [[YanState|燕国]]. The source treats the event as part of compact chronological movement.
