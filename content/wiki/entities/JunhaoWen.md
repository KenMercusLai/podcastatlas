# Junhao Wen

Junhao Wen is the computational neuroscientist at [[ColumbiaUniversity]] named in [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]]. The episode presents him as lead author of a May Nature study using [[UKBiobank]] data to study sleep duration through [[OrganSpecificAgingClock|organ-specific aging clocks]].

His source contribution is methodological caution. Wen says cause and effect are difficult to separate: lack of sleep may accelerate organ aging signals, or another condition may cause both poor sleep and older-looking biological clocks. That keeps [[SleepDurationUShape]] inside [[PopulationLevelHealthAdvice]] rather than personal diagnosis.
