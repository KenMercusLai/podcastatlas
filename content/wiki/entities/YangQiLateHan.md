# 杨琦 / Yang Qi (late Han)

杨琦 / Yang Qi (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-985-fanmu-chengchou-liguo-zhiluan-de-xijie-tanjiu-lt-t2un0zcudkige9ucl1txa2hhk|Hanji 985]] as the shizhong who urges [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] not to rebuke [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] after Li Jue sends rotten beef bones instead of usable food. Yang Qi warns that Li Jue already knows he has committed a grave rebellion and may move the emperor toward Chiyang's Huangbai city, so anger could worsen captivity rather than correct it.

His page is source-scoped. Yang Qi matters as a survival adviser: under ordinary sovereignty the emperor's anger could punish disrespect, but under Li Jue's control it becomes a bodily risk to be suppressed.
