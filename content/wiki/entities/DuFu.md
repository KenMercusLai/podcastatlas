## Overview

杜甫 / Du Fu appears in the current wiki as a Tang poet whose work is used for social portraiture and the emotional language of poverty, and whose fictionalized pre-rebellion self challenges the flattened image of a poet who was always old and sorrowful.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-3-liqingzhao-zhongnian-fuqi-libie-hejiu-jie-xiangsi-lnzxhrj2iulahccy2-pkm-wphdei|Qinji 131-3]] reads 《饮中八仙歌》 as a compact gallery of Chang'an drinkers. He Zhizhang, Su Jin, [[LiBai|李白]], Zhang Xu, and others become recognizable through wine scenes, making alcohol social, aesthetic, and character-revealing rather than only an object of praise or condemnation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-316-1-ta-gong-yue-bai-wang-weihe-zhi-xia-baixing-yuelaiyue-qiong-lu_tr4m36rmz9sx38j4nvlmof5am|Hanji 316-1]] uses 《百忧集行》 differently. Its movement from youthful vigor to age, sickness, lack of grain, a hungry child, and silent marital grief gives the host an emotional bridge into Western Han popular suffering under prolonged war. That analogy expands Du Fu's current role from wine portraiture to embodied poverty, but it does not make a Tang poem direct evidence for Han fiscal history.

[[064-maboyong-changande-lizhi-yuanzhu-jiexi-yiqi-hongchen-beihoude-zhichang-shengcun-faze-lscpfjeqg9izxpsfhabnvge0hdbn|Episode 064]] discusses Du Fu as a fictional character in [[TheLycheeRoadNovel|《长安的荔枝》]] around age forty-five, before the An Lushan Rebellion and the late-life image that dominates popular memory. The hosts value his vigor, humor, idealism, and affectionate admiration of [[LiBai|李白]], and treat his advice to [[LiShande|李善德]] as a small-person moral intervention. This enriches literary reception but is not direct biographical evidence for private conversations or personality.

## Key Characteristics

- Poet whose scenes make social types and emotional states concrete through daily material life.
- Writer of 《饮中八仙歌》, used here for wine-shaped portraits of Chang'an literati.
- Writer of 《百忧集行》, used here for hunger, illness, family strain, and muted grief.
- Historical figure fictionalized as vigorous, humorous, idealistic, and emotionally attached to Li Bai before later hardship dominates his public image.
- Literary figure whose work supplies analogy and emotional understanding rather than direct evidence for every historical setting to which a host applies it.

## Qualifications

All three sources are podcast interpretations rather than a full study of Du Fu's oeuvre, textual history, or biography. Hanji 316-1 uses a Tang poem as an emotional analogy for Western Han poverty; the wiki preserves that rhetorical function without treating the poem as contemporaneous evidence for Han households. Episode 064 interprets a fictional characterization, so its dialogue, private temperament, friendship details, and intervention in Li Shande's story must not be treated as documentary biography.

## What Changed

- Added the pre-rebellion fictional-reception layer of vigor, humor, hope, and friendship with Li Bai.
- Explicitly separated the novella's characterization from documentary biography.
