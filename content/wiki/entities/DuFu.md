# 杜甫 / Du Fu

杜甫 / Du Fu enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-3-liqingzhao-zhongnian-fuqi-libie-hejiu-jie-xiangsi-lnzxhrj2iulahccy2-pkm-wphdei|Qinji 131-3]] as the poet of 《饮中八仙歌》. The episode uses that poem to show how drinking can become a literary portrait method rather than only a behavior to praise or condemn.

In the source's reading, Du Fu writes a compact gallery of Chang'an drinkers: He Zhizhang's unstable but free-spirited drunkenness, Su Jin's mix of Buddhist devotion and love of drink, Li Bai's inspired poetic reputation, and other figures become recognizable through wine scenes. Du Fu therefore supports [[PoeticWineCulture|诗酒文化]] by making wine social, aesthetic, and character-revealing at once.
