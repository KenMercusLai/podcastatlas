# Judas Iscariot

## Overview

Judas Iscariot is the New Testament disciple remembered for betraying Jesus, represented in the bounded source only through a comic reality-television analogy.

## Current Profile

[[206-historical-love-island-glt8073929093]] assigns Judas the “traitor” role because of the canonical betrayal story and its association with thirty pieces of silver. The episode uses the cash prize to imagine financial suspicion around him, while acknowledging that it has no usable romantic biography for its pairing exercise.

Judas is paired with [[MarciaWilliams]] and eliminated before the public vote because the imagined couple is judged untrustworthy. That outcome describes the episode's narrative shorthand, not a historical or theological conclusion.

## Key Characteristics

- Disciple defined in Christian memory by betrayal of Jesus.
- Figure associated in the source with payment of thirty pieces of silver.
- Subject whose sparse romantic evidence exposes the artificiality of the dating-show format.
- Comic contestant eliminated on imagined trust rather than historical analysis.

The romantic speculation and cash-prize motive are jokes, and even the familiar payment story requires textual and theological context absent from this episode.
