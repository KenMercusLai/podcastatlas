# Social War / 同盟者战争

## Overview

The Social War / 同盟者战争 is the Italian allied revolt that turns the [[RomanAlliedCitizenshipCrisis]] from legal and status grievance into armed conflict inside the late [[RomanRepublic|Roman Republic]].

## Current Profile

In episode 702, the Social War grows from the contradiction that Italian allies share Rome's wars, overseas profit, and military burden but do not receive full Roman citizenship. [[MarcusLiviusDrusus]] tries to resolve the problem in 91 BC by extending citizenship, but the defeat of his legislation and his assassination help push Italian anger into revolt.

The war becomes a proving ground for Roman commanders. Rome suffers early reverses, [[GaiusMarius]] still wins despite age, [[GnaeusPompeiusStrabo]] is effective in Picenum and around Asculum, and [[LuciusCorneliusSulla]] emerges as the standout commander through Pompeii, Samnium, and Nola. The conflict therefore links citizenship failure to the command competition that follows.

## Key Characteristics

- The war begins from allied status resentment rather than a simple foreign invasion.
- The failure and killing of Drusus turn a reform path into a military crisis.
- Asculum, Campania, Nola, Pompeii, and Samnium make the conflict geographically internal to Italy.
- Roman commanders gain or lose prestige through the war, making citizenship conflict part of later command politics.
- Sulla's Social War success becomes a direct step toward the 88 BC consulship and the eastern command.

## Evidence

- Citizenship trigger: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] says Italian elites profited through Rome but remained second-class without full voting and legal status.
- Failed reform: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] presents Drusus's citizenship legislation, its defeat, and his assassination as the immediate political prelude.
- Violent opening: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] describes the revolt beginning violently at Asculum before spreading into Campania and Samnium.
- Command proving ground: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] places Marius, Pompeius Strabo, and Sulla inside the Roman military response.
- Sulla's rise: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] says Sulla captured Pompeii, invaded Samnium, stormed the Samnite capital, besieged Nola, and then won the consulship.
