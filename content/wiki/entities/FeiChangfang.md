# Fei Changfang / 费长房

Fei Changfang / 费长房 is presented in [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]] as a Han fangshu and immortal-story figure. The episode recounts his encounter with Hu Gong, entry into a hidden world inside a vessel, failed ordeal, talismanic ghost control, shrinking-distance ability, and eventual vulnerability after losing protective power.

The wiki value of Fei Changfang is narrative as much as biographical. He shows how [[FangshuSystematization|方术]] stories stage a relationship between master transmission, tests, hidden worlds, supernatural technique, and moral or bodily failure.

## Key Claims
- Immortal arts are narrated as dangerous disciplines, not only magical rewards.
- Talismans and ghost control appear as technical authority that can also be lost.
- The failed ordeal keeps transcendence conditional rather than automatic.
