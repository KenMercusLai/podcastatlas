# 《王尔德奇异故事集》 / Wilde Strange Stories

[[WildeStrangeStories|《王尔德奇异故事集》]] is the [[OscarWilde|Oscar Wilde / 王尔德]] story collection discussed in [[10-wang-erde-qiyi-gushiji-yong-zui-hui-sahuang-de-zuichun-qinwen-tianshi-545332195]] with translator [[FeiBi|菲比]]. The episode presents it as a corrective to the narrow memory of Wilde as only the writer of sorrowful fairy tales: the collection contains black comedy, social satire, ghost-story tenderness, forged-evidence obsession, and stories that end more gently than a reader might expect from [[TheHappyPrince|《快乐王子》]].

The source centers three stories. [[LordArthurSavilesCrime|《阿瑟·萨维尔勋爵的罪行》]] makes murder absurdly bureaucratic and responsible; [[ThePortraitOfMrWH|《W.H.先生的画像》]] turns literary interpretation into contagious belief; [[TheCantervilleGhost|《坎特维尔的幽灵》]] converts a haunted-house premise into Anglo-American comedy and melancholy rescue. Together they make the collection useful for [[LifeImitatesArt]], [[ArtAgainstMoralInstruction]], [[FictionalEvidenceSeduction]], and [[BlackComicMoralInversion]].
