# 为 AI 发电

## Overview
为 AI 发电 is a Chinese-language AI podcast represented in the wiki by episodes on [[MemoryCenteredAI|memory-centered AI]] and the [[DeepChat]] open-source agent client.

## Current Profile
Across the two bounded episodes, the show focuses on practical AI infrastructure and product architecture rather than model capability alone. One episode examines memory layers, local and hybrid inference, data sovereignty, and ownership; the other examines agent harnesses, Tape-based context, protocols, sandboxing, model routing, and open-source maintenance.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses interviews with builders to explain emerging AI products and infrastructure.
- Connects user-facing tools to underlying memory, harness, protocol, and runtime choices.
- Treats privacy, permissions, verification, and maintenance as part of product design.
- Preserves company and practitioner claims with explicit limits rather than independent product testing.
