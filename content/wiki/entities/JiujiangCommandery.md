# 九江郡 / Jiujiang Commandery

九江郡 / Jiujiang Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] as the commandery whose ordinary office name [[YuanShu|袁术]] changes after declaring himself emperor at [[Shouchun|寿春]]. The episode says Yuan Shu renames the commandery-level post into "淮南尹", imitating a capital-region administrative title.

The page is source-scoped. The source does not give a full administrative history of Jiujiang; it uses the renaming to show Yuan Shu converting local control into symbolic imperial infrastructure without having enough wider recognition to sustain the title.
