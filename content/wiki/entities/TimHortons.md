# Tim Hortons

## Overview
Tim Hortons is the restaurant chain discussed in the Ackman All-In interview as the asset [[PershingSquare]] wanted [[Wendys]] to spin off during one of [[BillAckman]]'s early activist campaigns.

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Tim Hortons is not analyzed as a full operating company. It serves as an example of how an activist investor can identify a separable asset inside a public company and use public analysis to pressure management when private engagement fails.

## Key Characteristics
- Appears as the spin-off target in Pershing's early Wendy's campaign.
- Functions as evidence of Ackman's older, more confrontational activist-investing style.
- Helps contrast early public-pressure campaigns with Pershing's later reputation-backed access.
