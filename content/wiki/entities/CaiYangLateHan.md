# 蔡阳 / Cai Yang (late Han)

## Overview
蔡阳 / Cai Yang is a Cao-side commander in the Hanji 1047 Guandu prelude. The episode introduces him when [[CaoCao|曹操]] sends him against [[LiuBei|刘备]] after Liu Bei returns to [[RunanCommanderyLateHan|汝南]] and joins [[GongDuLateHan|龚都]].

## Current Profile
Cai Yang's current wiki role is a narrow campaign marker. His defeat by Liu Bei shows that Cao Cao's side can beat Liu Bei through [[CaoRen|曹仁]] in the first Runan/Yingchuan movement, yet still faces continuing local pressure after Liu Bei leaves [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and rebuilds through Gong Du.

## Key Characteristics
- Cao-side military actor sent to suppress Liu Bei's renewed Runan activity.
- Appears after Liu Bei has left Yuan Shao and joined Gong Du.
- Killed by Liu Bei in the source's brief Runan branch.
- Functions as evidence that the southern flank remains active before the main Guandu camp standoff.
