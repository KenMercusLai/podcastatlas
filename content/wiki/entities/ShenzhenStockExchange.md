# Shenzhen Stock Exchange / 深圳证券交易所

Shenzhen Stock Exchange appears in [[vol-121-cong-tanhua-yixian-de-fenji-jijin-dao-fengtou-zhengjing-de-ganggan-etf-yongyuan-buyao-digu-renxing-de-fengkuang-lusagcitdozvzm8wausvecvi-qmb]] as one of the two mainland exchanges that issued structured-fund business rules after the 2015 crash. The source says the 2016 exchange guidance raised investor-access thresholds and required risk disclosure, helping move Chinese [[ChineseStructuredFund|structured funds]] from mass retail products into a shrinking legacy category.
