# Micron Clay Mega Fab

Micron Clay Mega Fab is the planned [[MicronTechnology|Micron]] chip fabrication facility discussed in [[tech-20260123-0123-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260123-0123-mp-tech-pod-128]]. The episode places it in [[ClayNewYork|Clay, New York]] near Syracuse and says the facility would make memory important to AI processing, tying the project to [[HighBandwidthMemory]] demand and the strategic push to rebuild U.S. semiconductor manufacturing capacity.

The facility matters in the wiki because the episode treats it as a community-accountability test, not only a supply-chain win. Civil rights, environmental, and labor groups reportedly support the project in principle but want enforceable promises around local hiring, diverse hiring, environmental care, emissions, and water pollution. That makes the fab a direct case for [[EnforceableCommunityBenefits]], [[TechManufacturingReshoring]], and [[AIMetabolicInfrastructure]].
