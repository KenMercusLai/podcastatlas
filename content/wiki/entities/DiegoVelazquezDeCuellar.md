## Overview

Diego Velazquez de Cuellar was the governor of Cuba who commissioned [[HernanCortes|Hernan Cortes]] to explore westward but did not authorize a conquest of Mexico.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Velazquez as sending Cortes to find Grijalva, explore Yucatan, investigate routes and reported peoples, spread Christianity, and gather information about gold. When he became alarmed by Cortes and tried to cancel the expedition, Cortes sailed anyway.

Velazquez's authority was then displaced through the [[VeraCruzLegalCoup|Vera Cruz legal coup]]. Cortes founded a municipality whose council appointed him chief justice and captain general, then sent treasure to Spain rather than Cuba in an appeal for direct royal recognition.

## Key Characteristics

- Governor constrained from traveling by crisis in Cuba.
- Sponsor of an exploratory and evangelical mission that stopped short of authorizing conquest.
- Superior who tried unsuccessfully to revoke Cortes's command.
- Rival authority bypassed through municipal legal form and direct appeal to the crown.

## Evidence

- Commission and revocation: [[385-the-fall-of-the-aztecs-the-woman-who-changed-the-world-part-2-glt5124604457]] distinguishes Velazquez's broad exploratory instructions from authorization to conquer and describes his failed cancellation.
- Loss of control: [[385-the-fall-of-the-aztecs-the-woman-who-changed-the-world-part-2-glt5124604457]] connects Vera Cruz's council, Cortes's new offices, and the treasure shipment to the bypassing of Cuban authority.

## Qualifications

This source gives Velazquez mainly as the authority Cortes evaded, not as a complete governor's biography. The commission's ambiguities gave Cortes room to reinterpret opportunity, but they do not amount to conquest authorization.

## What Changed

- Established the limited commission against which Cortes's later legal claims must be judged.
- Connected Velazquez's displacement to the founding of Vera Cruz and direct royal appeal.

## Relationships

- [[HernanCortes]] - subordinate who ignored his revocation and bypassed his jurisdiction.
- [[VeraCruzLegalCoup]] - municipal maneuver that displaced his authority over the expedition.
- [[SpanishConquestOfMexico]] - process that began outside the conquest authority he had granted.
