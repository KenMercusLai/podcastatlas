## Overview
Cao Zong is the Dunhuang administrator whose call to protect the Western Regions begins the Han re-entry attempt that immediately becomes contested as costly and militarily fragile.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-790-lishi-gaosu-ni-ruhe-he-lingdao-da-jiaodao-lulf0r1sdkod3luzwru0-kkrijxk|Hanji 790]] introduces Cao Zong after Northern Xiongnu power has recovered and Western Regions states have come under Xiongnu suzerainty. He requests renewed Han protection, prompting the court to send [[SuoBanLateHan|Suo Ban]] with more than one thousand men to cultivate and garrison Yiwu. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-791-zhichang-shang-zong-you-ren-beihou-yin-ni-yi-zhao-yingdui-llz74ebi88qmw-9050x2qaddjwqn|Hanji 791]] then shows Cao Zong pushing a five-thousand-man punitive response after Suo Ban's death, while [[BanYongLateHan|Ban Yong]] criticizes the plan as too expensive and poorly targeted.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents the frontier administrator's impulse to recover or protect abandoned strategic depth.
- Treats the Western Regions as a defensive shield for Dunhuang and the Hexi corridor.
- His policy preference moves from initial garrison re-entry to punitive expedition after disaster.
- Functions as Ban Yong's strategic foil in Hanji 791.
- Anchors a concrete administrative pathway by which frontier concerns reach the central court.

## Evidence
- Re-entry request: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-790-lishi-gaosu-ni-ruhe-he-lingdao-da-jiaodao-lulf0r1sdkod3luzwru0-kkrijxk|Hanji 790]] records Cao Zong asking the court to protect the Western Regions after Northern Xiongnu suzerainty expands.
- Yiwu deployment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-790-lishi-gaosu-ni-ruhe-he-lingdao-da-jiaodao-lulf0r1sdkod3luzwru0-kkrijxk|Hanji 790]] links his request to Suo Ban's Yiwu tuntian garrison and initial submissions from Cheshi Qian and Shanshan.
- Punitive escalation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-791-zhichang-shang-zong-you-ren-beihou-yin-ni-yi-zhao-yingdui-llz74ebi88qmw-9050x2qaddjwqn|Hanji 791]] records Cao Zong's proposal for a five-thousand-man expedition after Suo Ban is killed.
- Strategic debate: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-791-zhichang-shang-zong-you-ren-beihou-yin-ni-yi-zhao-yingdui-llz74ebi88qmw-9050x2qaddjwqn|Hanji 791]] preserves Ban Yong's argument that Cao Zong's approach is costly and less useful than targeted restoration of frontier nodes.

## Qualifications
The profile is bounded to Hanji 790 and 791. Cao Zong is not simply a reckless hawk: his concern arises from a real strategic loss after Northern Xiongnu turns the Western Regions into usable rear depth, but Hanji 791 challenges his chosen instrument.

## What Changed
- Created the page from Hanji 790 and Hanji 791.
- Made Cao Zong the named advocate of Western Regions protection before Ban Yong's counterproposal.
