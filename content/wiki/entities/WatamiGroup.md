# 和民集团 / Watami Group

## Overview

[[WatamiGroup|和民集团]] is a Japanese hospitality and services company presented in the current source as the employer legally responsible for conditions contributing to [[MoriMina|森美菜]]'s 2008 death.

## Current Profile

The source depicts a company whose genuine agriculture and social-purpose projects also supported a recruitment and control narrative. Rapid restaurant expansion, a crowded management-candidate funnel, understaffed late-night operations, minimal practical training, and founder-centered assessments shifted labor and recovery costs onto young employees. After initially emphasizing Mina's individual psychology and disputing the work status of nominally voluntary activities, Watami accepted legal responsibility in a 2015 settlement and agreed to governance and recurrence-prevention measures.

## Key Characteristics

- Combined restaurant, agriculture, elder-care, and social-purpose messaging under a broad corporate mission.
- Recruited many young management candidates relative to available promotion positions.
- Joined sparse staffing and late closing with weak practical preparation for frontline work.
- Used philosophy study, reports, volunteering, and evaluation to extend control beyond scheduled shifts.
- Suffered recruiting, consumer, and financial damage after becoming associated with the black-company debate.
- Ultimately accepted legal responsibility and changes through settlement while declining a specific overtime ceiling.

- **Operating model:** [[2026-qiuji-pian-fanwai-jinru-dachang-lianggeyue-ta-xuanzele-zisha-lmtfkbce-sjbl05lcdcvs8vhtnmc]] describes understaffing, late hours, minimal kitchen instruction, transport mismatch, and assessment-linked off-shift activity.
- **Response and accountability:** [[2026-qiuji-pian-fanwai-jinru-dachang-lianggeyue-ta-xuanzele-zisha-lmtfkbce-sjbl05lcdcvs8vhtnmc]] traces initial individualization of the death, resistance to legal liability, and the later settlement.
- **Commercial feedback:** [[2026-qiuji-pian-fanwai-jinru-dachang-lianggeyue-ta-xuanzele-zisha-lmtfkbce-sjbl05lcdcvs8vhtnmc]] attributes recruitment collapse, consumer distrust, losses, closures, and asset sales partly to the reputational crisis.

## Qualifications

The episode acknowledges that some agricultural and public-interest projects were real; this does not establish that all mission claims were false. Company scale, retention, finances, closure counts, procurement shares, later restructuring, and causal links between the case and business outcomes are source-scoped and require company or regulatory records.

## What Changed

- Created a profile that holds public-purpose activity and employee-protection failure in the same assessment.
