## Overview

杜乔 / Du Qiao is the late-Han official in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-830-donghan-ligu-wo-sui-si-yourong-lt-j7n9gotiw-negqxojoacf6ji|Hanji 830]] who is first protected by [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan|梁妠 / 梁太后]]'s judgment of his uprightness, then destroyed by [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] after the Li Gu case hardens into prison death and public intimidation.

## Current Profile

Hanji 830 places Du Qiao beside [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] as a loyal official targeted by Liang Ji's accusation field. Liang Ji initially wants both Li Gu and Du Qiao arrested, but Liang Na refuses to approve Du Qiao's arrest because she regards him as loyal and upright.

That protection proves temporary. Liang Ji later threatens Du Qiao with suicide as the price of preserving wife and children. Du Qiao refuses, is brought into the prison process, and dies in prison. His body is displayed with Li Gu's north of Luoyang, and [[YangKuangDuQiaoRetainer|杨匡]] later protects and returns it for burial.

## Key Characteristics

- Loyal late-Han official paired with Li Gu in Liang Ji's accusation campaign.
- Initially shielded by Liang Na's confidence in his uprightness.
- Refuses coerced suicide even when family safety is used as leverage.
- Dies in prison under Liang Ji's political pressure.
- Posthumous reputation is carried by Yang Kuang's guarded mourning and burial petition.

- Coerced suicide refused: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-830-donghan-ligu-wo-sui-si-yourong-lt-j7n9gotiw-negqxojoacf6ji|Hanji 830]] says Liang Ji sends someone to pressure Du Qiao to kill himself to protect his family, and Du Qiao refuses.
- Prison death: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-830-donghan-ligu-wo-sui-si-yourong-lt-j7n9gotiw-negqxojoacf6ji|Hanji 830]] says Du Qiao is later arrested and dies in prison.
- Posthumous defense: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-830-donghan-ligu-wo-sui-si-yourong-lt-j7n9gotiw-negqxojoacf6ji|Hanji 830]] says Yang Kuang guards Du Qiao's displayed body and helps secure return burial.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 830 and does not reconstruct Du Qiao's wider career, office history, or family. The source shows his loyalty through persecution, refusal, death, and posthumous mourning rather than through a full biography.
