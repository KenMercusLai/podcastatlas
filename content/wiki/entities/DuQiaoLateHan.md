## Overview

杜乔 / Du Qiao is the late-Han official whose current wiki profile now begins in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-829-junzi-weishenme-doubuguo-xiaoren-lthvnstkfu8n9thx1sz6yezl4yqq|Hanji 829]] as a taiwei who criticizes appointment and reward inversion under [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]], then continues in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-830-donghan-ligu-wo-sui-si-yourong-lt-j7n9gotiw-negqxojoacf6ji|Hanji 830]] as the [[LiGuLateHan|李固]]-paired official protected briefly by [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan|梁妠 / 梁太后]] and then destroyed by [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 829 supplies the earlier office-and-advice layer. After Li Gu is removed, court hope shifts toward Du Qiao, who becomes taiwei in 147 CE. His memorial argues that appointing worthy officials and matching rewards and punishments to merit are the state's first tasks, then criticizes rewards flowing to Liang-family and eunuch actors without merit. Huan gives no visible answer.

The same source shows why Du Qiao becomes personally dangerous to Liang Ji. After [[EmpressLiangYingLateHan|梁盈]] is appointed empress, Du Qiao refuses Liang Ji's attempt to use empress-level wedding rites beyond established institutions. He also refuses to recommend a corrupt candidate for shangshu. Liang Ji interprets both refusals as disobedience, and [[TangHengLateHan|唐衡]] plus [[ZuoGuanLateHan|左悺]] later slander Du Qiao and Li Gu to Huan by recoding their earlier succession stance as opposition to Huan personally.

Hanji 830 places Du Qiao beside Li Gu as a loyal official targeted by Liang Ji's accusation field. Liang Ji initially wants both Li Gu and Du Qiao arrested, but Liang Na refuses to approve Du Qiao's arrest because she regards him as loyal and upright.

That protection proves temporary. Liang Ji later threatens Du Qiao with suicide as the price of preserving wife and children. Du Qiao refuses, is brought into the prison process, and dies in prison. His body is displayed with Li Gu's north of Luoyang, and [[YangKuangDuQiaoRetainer|杨匡]] later protects and returns it for burial.

## Key Characteristics

- Taiwei whose 147 CE memorial criticizes unmerited reward and bad appointments around Liang-family and eunuch power.
- Procedural official who refuses Liang Ji's empress-rite overreach and corrupt shangshu recommendation.
- Loyal late-Han official paired with Li Gu in Liang Ji's accusation campaign.
- Initially shielded by Liang Na's confidence in his uprightness.
- Refuses coerced suicide even when family safety is used as leverage.
- Dies in prison under Liang Ji's political pressure.
- Posthumous reputation is carried by Yang Kuang's guarded mourning and burial petition.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 829 and Hanji 830 and does not reconstruct Du Qiao's full career, family, or all office history. Hanji 829 supplies the earlier taiwei, remonstrance, ritual, recommendation, and slander layer; Hanji 830 supplies the final persecution, prison death, and posthumous burial-defense layer.
