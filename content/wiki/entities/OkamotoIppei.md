# 冈本一平 / Okamoto Ippei

冈本一平 / Okamoto Ippei appears in [[08-laojichao-laoji-busi-shenzhi-buzeng-diaoling-541092994]] as [[OkamotoKanoko|冈本加乃子]]'s husband and one of the main figures in the episode's biographical account. The source says he pursued Okamoto, married her, betrayed her, and later promised to spend his life protecting her after her attempted suicide.

The episode uses him less as an independent artistic figure than as part of the domestic arrangement around Okamoto's later life. His acceptance of her unconventional household and relationships helps the hosts frame her biography as a rare reversal of ordinary gender expectations in her social setting.
