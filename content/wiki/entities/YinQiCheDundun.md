# 尹琪 / Yin Qi (nature writer)

尹琪 is the author of [[CheDundunYeShiji|《车墩墩野事记》]] and the guest in [[85-chedundun-yeshiji-zou-a-bandao-xiangxia-zuan-xiaoshuliner-qu-700935571]]. The source presents her as a former editor who became a freelance writer, nature picture-book creator, translator of nature popular-science books, and resident observer around [[ChedunTown|车墩镇]].

This page is intentionally distinct from [[YinQi]], the AI entrepreneur page. This 尹琪 is a nature writer whose work in the source centers on city-edge animals, local people, essays, and a gradually loosened relation to social judgment.

## Key Claims
- Her writing method starts from nearby observation rather than travel spectacle: balconies, storefronts, fields, drains, woods, markets, and community green space all become material.
- She treats birds, insects, bats, zhang/deerlike animals, raccoon dogs, and fireflies as living neighbors whose behavior can correct human assumptions.
- She repeatedly resists turning observation into instant moral or symbolic conclusion, making her work a source-specific extension of [[ObservationBeforeInference]].
- Her account of small woods includes fear, safety planning, trash, trapping, bodily functions, desire, and local informal activity, not only scenic nature.
- Her later turn toward "looking at people again" makes [[NatureContactSelfPerception]] a social practice as much as a private ecological one.
