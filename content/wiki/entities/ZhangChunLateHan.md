# 张纯 / Zhang Chun (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-938-lishi-de-jingxing-ruanruo-zhihui-dailai-quru-lnlnrketgfbadhfjbzdjjtrmqfsh|Hanji 938]] gives Zhang Chun's rebellion origin before the later pacification pages. He is a Yuyang man and former Zhongshan xiang whose request to command recruited [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] cavalry is denied by [[ZhangWenLateHan|张温]] in favor of [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]]. After the Wuhuan force is under-supplied and deserts near Ji, Zhang Chun's resentment feeds his alliance with [[ZhangJuLateHan|张举]] and [[QiuLijuLateHan|丘力居]]; he kills frontier officials, takes rebel titles, and sends documents demanding [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] abdicate.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-939-donghan-luanju-de-fengkuang-sanguo-xuqu-lthmvw8ihk96fuez9vamlryzcngy|Hanji 939]] backfills Zhang Chun as the target named in the court's [[YouzhouLateHan|幽州]] response. [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] is sent as Youzhou shepherd to handle the crisis, and the court orders [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] troops to support the campaign. That requisition triggers the [[QiangquChanyu|羌渠]] killing and [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]] succession chain captured by [[FrontierConscriptionBackfire|边疆征发反噬]].

[[ZhangChunLateHan|张纯]] is a late-Han rebel figure in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-942-yue-youxiu-de-ren-huode-yue-lei-heli-ma-lkwlrcgnt-kxxc2qqz6z6wkg7e5z|Hanji 942]], distinct from [[ZhangChunWesternHan|张纯 / Zhang Chun (Western Han)]]. The episode places him among the forces raiding [[QingzhouLateHan|青州]], [[Xuzhou|徐州]], [[YouzhouLateHan|幽州]], and [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]], with [[QiuLijuLateHan|丘力居]] and other frontier forces amplifying the disorder.

[[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] defeats Zhang Chun at Shimen and rescues captives, but the victory tempts him into an over-deep pursuit that ends with Qiu Liju surrounding him at Guanzi City. [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] later offers rewards for Zhang Chun and [[ZhangJuLateHan|张举]], helping turn the crisis from open rebellion into a surrender-and-dispersal sequence.
