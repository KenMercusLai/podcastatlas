# NIO / 蔚来

## Overview
NIO / 蔚来 is a Chinese electric vehicle company tied to Hefei's industrial investment strategy, premium EV competition, battery-swap infrastructure, and a 2026 source-scoped financial improvement signal.

## Current Profile
The bounded sources make NIO both a city-backed industrial-investment case and a premium-EV execution test. The Hefei source emphasizes how local-government capital helped NIO survive a near-collapse period, while the premium-EV source emphasizes battery swap, battery leasing, and high-end branding. [[daisen-jinru-diandong-yashua-lingyu-chuantong-jindian-jiameng-chixu-shousuo-1010976825]] adds that Q2 2026 revenue exceeded RMB 32 billion, deliveries exceeded 100,000 vehicles, average selling price rose, and losses narrowed sharply, but ongoing investment in battery swapping, technology, and new models still leaves sustained profitability unsettled.

## Key Characteristics
- Relies on Hefei-linked industrial support as part of its survival and growth history.
- Competes in China's high-end EV market through design, service, brand, and technology.
- Uses battery swap and battery leasing as signature differentiators.
- Expands volume and price coverage through models and sub-brands while protecting premium positioning.
- Shows source-reported revenue, delivery, average-selling-price, and loss-improvement momentum in Q2 2026.
- Remains exposed to profitability pressure from infrastructure, R&D, and model-launch spending.
