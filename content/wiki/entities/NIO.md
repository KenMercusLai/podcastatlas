# NIO / 蔚来

NIO / 蔚来 is the electric-vehicle company discussed in [[no-211-huishang-wangshi-cong-qianshi-buxiu-dao-zuiniu-fengtou-1002275189]] as the most visible modern [[Hefei]] rescue-and-cluster case. The episode says NIO was cash-strained after heavy losses in 2019, then signed a 2020 investment agreement with Hefei-backed entities that placed NIO China in Hefei.

The source treats the investment as both financial and industrial. Hefei's capital helped NIO survive a liquidity crisis and participate in a rebound, while the city gained an anchor for its new-energy-vehicle cluster. Later additional Hefei-linked support and continuing losses keep the case open rather than fully settled.

The episode therefore uses NIO to qualify the "best venture-capital city" label. Hefei's early paper gains and principal recovery matter, but the larger question is whether [[HefeiIndustrialInvestmentModel|industrial investment]] can turn one company rescue into durable employment, supply-chain density, and vehicle-production scale.

[[143-wei-xiao-li-de-gaoduan-tansuo-feng-tou-quan-1-143-1]] adds NIO as the strongest high-end-brand case among the "蔚小理" comparison. The episode links loyal users, premium design, improved vehicle gross margin, [[NIOES8]], [[EVBatteryLeasing]], and [[BatterySwapInfrastructure]] to a pricing model that lowers the upfront barrier without making NIO look like it is simply cutting prices.
