# Chesley Sullenberger

## Overview

Chesley “Sully” Sullenberger is the airline captain whose 2009 Hudson River landing anchors [[this-is-your-captain-speaking]]'s distinction between sounding calm and exercising effective judgment under extreme pressure.

## Current Profile

After a bird strike caused both engines to lose thrust, Sullenberger rejected landing options he judged impossible and announced the Hudson River landing in brief, controlled terms. The aircraft landed without fatalities.

The episode treats this communication as the deeper aspiration behind [[PilotVoice]]. Its value does not lie in resembling [[ChuckYeager]] or fitting a male vocal stereotype, but in preserving clarity while making consequential decisions as normal plans collapse.

## Key Characteristics

- Airline captain associated with the 2009 Hudson River emergency landing.
- Communicated concisely while evaluating rapidly narrowing options.
- Demonstrated adaptable human judgment after an abnormal systems failure.
- Used as a case where calm speech accompanied effective action rather than substituting for it.

## Evidence

### Emergency judgment

- [[this-is-your-captain-speaking]] reports the dual-engine thrust loss, rejection of unavailable landing options, Hudson decision, and fatality-free outcome.

## Qualifications

The page is based on one structured episode summary rather than the complete cockpit recording, investigation, flight record, crew testimony, or safety analysis. The successful case does not prove that calm delivery alone causes good decisions or that human intervention is always superior to automation.
