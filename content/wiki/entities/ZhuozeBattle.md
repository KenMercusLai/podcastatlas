# 浊泽之战 / Battle of Zhuoze

浊泽之战 / Battle of Zhuoze appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-3-lmhj4le2mwu1yhhl-qmuoqkfmhjf]] as the battle where [[HanState|韩国]] and [[ZhaoState|赵国]] defeat [[WeiState|魏国]] during Wei's succession crisis.

The battle's importance in the source is not only military. The victory leads to the siege of [[Anyi|安邑]], but Han and Zhao then fail to agree on whether to partition Wei or install [[GongzhongHuan|公中缓]] under territorial concessions. That makes Zhuoze the turning point from battlefield success to [[CoalitionSettlementFailure]].

## Key Claims
- Zhuoze shows that Wei's internal succession disorder had become an interstate-war opportunity.
- Han and Zhao achieve battlefield success but cannot convert it into a shared settlement.
- The battle therefore exposes the difference between winning a campaign and agreeing on the political design after victory.
