# 浊泽之战 / Battle of Zhuoze

浊泽之战 / Battle of Zhuoze is used in this wiki as a source-scoped home for battle notices attached to the Zhuoze name. It first appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-3-lmhj4le2mwu1yhhl-qmuoqkfmhjf]] as the battle where [[HanState|韩国]] and [[ZhaoState|赵国]] defeat [[WeiState|魏国]] during Wei's succession crisis.

The battle's importance in the source is not only military. The victory leads to the siege of [[Anyi|安邑]], but Han and Zhao then fail to agree on whether to partition Wei or install [[GongzhongHuan|公中缓]] under territorial concessions. That makes Zhuoze the turning point from battlefield success to [[CoalitionSettlementFailure]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-46-ma-ni-shi-ge-erbaiwu-de-lishi-laiyuan-lkz5hiamaojtvanllbunecy3g3ff]] adds a later Zhuoze notice after [[XiuyuBattle|修鱼之战]] in 317 BCE. This source has [[QinState|秦国]] continue its pursuit from Xiuyu to Zhuoze and capture Han generals rendered as Wei Sou and Shen Chai. The wiki keeps this as a separate source-scoped Qin-Han/Wei pursuit notice rather than merging it uncritically with the earlier Han-Zhao-vs-Wei succession-crisis battle.

## Key Claims
- Zhuoze shows that Wei's internal succession disorder had become an interstate-war opportunity.
- Han and Zhao achieve battlefield success but cannot convert it into a shared settlement.
- The battle therefore exposes the difference between winning a campaign and agreeing on the political design after victory.
- The later Zhouji 46 Zhuoze notice shows the same place-name/battle-name node reappearing in a different Qin pursuit context.
- When one place name anchors multiple terse annalistic notices, the wiki should preserve source scope instead of forcing them into one campaign.
