# 楚惠王 / King Hui of Chu

楚惠王 / King Hui of Chu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-2-zanmen-dou-yuanwang-le-beifu-2000-duonian-heiguo-de-yegong-haolong-lh-h-gezpqtvnyq40zlqac8s-5e]], where he is the ruler endangered during the [[BaiGongShengRebellion|白公胜之乱]]. After [[BaiGongSheng|白公胜]] defeats a [[WuState|吴国]] attack, he requests permission to enter the Chu capital without disarming while presenting captives. Chu Hui Wang, [[ZiXiChu|子西]], and the sima all allow it, giving Bai the opening for rebellion.

The episode does not develop Chu Hui Wang as a full ruler biography. His role is structural: the ruler's captured body makes Bai Gong Sheng's private revenge into a regime crisis, and [[YeGong|叶公]]'s later action becomes legitimate because it restores the endangered ruler rather than merely defeating a rival.
