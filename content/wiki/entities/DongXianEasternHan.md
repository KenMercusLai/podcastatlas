## Overview

Dong Xian / 董宪 is the Eastern Han rebel commander in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-690-jiaoyou-bushen-you-duo-kepa-kankan-tianrong-de-jingli-lpbms8pebzdo2ldiwho1cba81njk|Hanji 690]], distinct from [[DongXian|董贤]], Emperor Ai's favorite. In this source, Dong Xian responds after his general Ben Xiu offers Lanling to the Han side. He surrounds Lanling, lures [[GaiYanEasternHan|盖延]] through feigned retreat, and later captures the city and kills Ben Xiu.

## Current Profile

The source gives Dong Xian a compact military role. He is not introduced through a full biography; he appears as the active opponent in the Lanling-Tan field while Liu Xiu's commanders are deciding how to respond to Ben Xiu's defection.

Dong Xian's strength in this episode is tactical patience. He pretends to retreat, lets Gai Yan break through into Lanling, and then surrounds the city with the larger force. The result validates Liu Xiu's earlier indirect-relief advice: once Gai Yan has accepted Dong Xian's tempo, the later attack on Tan comes too late to save Lanling.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han rebel opponent in the Lanling branch, distinct from [[DongXian|董贤]].
- Commander whose subordinate Ben Xiu defects by offering Lanling to Han.
- Besieger who forces the Han side to choose between direct rescue and attacking Tan.
- Feigned-retreat actor who draws Gai Yan into a vulnerable position.
- Victor at Lanling who captures the city and kills Ben Xiu.
