# Byzantine Empire / 拜占庭帝国

## Overview
The Byzantine Empire / 拜占庭帝国 appears in the Ceuta episode as the eastern Roman power that reconquered North Africa from the [[VandalKingdom]] and held [[Ceuta]] as a far-western frontier outpost.

## Current Profile
The source places Byzantium between Vandal North Africa and Islamic conquest. Justinian's armies defeated the Vandals in 533-534, after which Ceuta became a western edge position that reportedly held out until the early eighth-century Islamic advance.

## Key Characteristics
- Restores Roman imperial authority in North Africa after the Vandal period.
- Turns Ceuta into a western frontier point rather than only a trade city.
- Serves as the bridge from late-antique imperial order to the Islamic conquest layer.
