# 西双版纳 / Xishuangbanna

西双版纳 / Xishuangbanna is the [[Yunnan|云南]] field setting in [[ep261-mifeng-weiji-ruguo-shiqu-mifeng-shijie-jianghui-zenyang-lqp-cwcdxx1ziixb35ijmmvd6cv]]. The episode uses it as a high-biodiversity region where [[LiuChang|刘畅]] reports on local bees, traditional beekeeping, wild honey collection, stingless bees, and the tension between rainforest pollination and cash-crop agriculture.

Within the wiki, Xishuangbanna matters because it shifts the bee discussion from honey and commercial hives toward [[NativeBeeEcologicalPollination|native-bee ecological pollination]]. The source says the region may contain hundreds of bee species and that [[XishuangbannaBotanicalGarden|西双版纳植物园]] began more systematic local bee investigation in 2024, making the place a case of both ecological richness and knowledge gaps.
