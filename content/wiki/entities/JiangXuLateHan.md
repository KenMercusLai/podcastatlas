## Overview

姜叙 / Jiang Xu is a late-Han resistance actor who joins [[YangFuLateHan|杨阜]] in rising against [[MaChao|马超]]. His household becomes central to the retaliatory violence when Ma Chao attacks Licheng and kills Jiang Xu's unnamed mother after she denounces him.

## Current Profile

The current source places Jiang Xu near the opening of the anti-Ma-Chao uprising rather than at the center of its detailed tactics. His durable significance is relational: he participates in the resistance, and Ma Chao's later seizure and killing of his mother turns political defeat into retaliation against a family member. Her defiance is prominent in the episode, but she remains unnamed in the supplied document and is not assigned an invented canonical identity.

## Key Characteristics

- Liangzhou-area participant in the uprising against Ma Chao.
- Closely associated with Yang Fu in the initial mobilization.
- Son of an unnamed woman killed after openly condemning Ma Chao at Licheng.
- Part of a resistance network whose political action produces severe household retaliation.
