# Bezalel Smotrich

Bezalel Smotrich appears in [[settler-bet-a-west-bank-flashpoint-6a842bb539e73ea8f3c13a2d]] as an Israeli minister who [[AnshelPfeffer]] says supports settlers and has funneled hundreds of millions to settlements. The source links Smotrich to the political backing behind [[WestBankOutpostEscalation]], not only to rhetoric.

The episode places Smotrich beside [[ItamarBenGvir]] as part of the far-right coalition pressure on [[BenjaminNetanyahu]]. That pressure matters because it can make protection of [[Palestinians]] and prevention of land grabs weaker than the formal fact of Israeli control over the [[WestBank|West Bank]] would imply.
