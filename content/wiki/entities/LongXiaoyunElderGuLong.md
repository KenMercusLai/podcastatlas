# 龙啸云（古龙人物） / Long Xiaoyun (sworn brother)

## Overview

龙啸云 is [[LiXunhuan|李寻欢]]'s sworn brother, [[LinShiyin|林诗音]]'s husband, and the father of [[LongXiaoyunYoungerGuLong|龙小云]] in [[SentimentalSwordsmanRuthlessSword|《多情剑客无情剑》]]. The route is disambiguated because his name and his son's are both commonly romanized “Long Xiaoyun.”

## Current Profile

The bounded episode interprets Long as a man whose original rescue of Li Xunhuan was likely sincere. Marriage does not free him from Li's shadow: his wife still loves Li, outsiders still associate the house with “李探花,” and public virtue requires him to praise the benefactor and rival whose gift makes his status feel derivative.

Gratitude, humiliation, jealousy, love, and injured pride gradually produce indirect betrayal while he maintains a loyal public identity. His final effort to rescue Li and death at the hands of ordinary gang members are read as remorse, debt repayment, and possible escape from collapse—not as proof that earlier harm never happened.

## Key Characteristics

- Begins from a plausibly sincere act of rescue rather than a lifelong conspiracy.
- Loves Lin Shiyin genuinely but mixes attachment with possession, rivalry, and demand for complete emotional recognition.
- Experiences the home and marriage as benefits that continually reproduce indebtedness and humiliation.
- Attacks Li indirectly while preserving an image of brotherly loyalty.
- Retains conscience and capacity for costly action without receiving a clean heroic redemption.

## Qualifications

The source's account of Long's original sincerity, psychological deformation, love, motives for betrayal, and intention in his final mission is interpretive. Explaining humiliation does not acquit his schemes, and costly remorse does not establish full redemption. The page distinguishes him from his son through a semantic route suffix rather than claiming a unique standard English rendering.
