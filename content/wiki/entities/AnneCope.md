# Anne Cope

## Overview
Anne Cope is the structural engineer featured guiding [[PlanetMoney]] through the [[InstituteForBusinessAndHomeSafety|Institute for Business and Home Safety]] laboratory. [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-9de180c7bff8bba31ae5f11fce51f1e1-34185790-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-9de180c7bff8bba31ae5f11fce51f1e1-34185790]] presents her work through full-scale tests of wildfire, wind, hail, roofs, and building assemblies.

## Current Profile
Cope represents the engineering side of insurer-funded resilience research: construct realistic test subjects, expose them to controlled hazards, measure failure, and translate the results into changes that builders, homeowners, insurers, and policymakers can use. The episode emphasizes the vivid experiments more than her biography or complete research record.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses full-scale structures and realistically arranged surroundings to study hazard pathways.
- Connects controlled laboratory testing to practical construction guidance.
- Examines interactions among materials, spacing, drought, embers, wind, water, and impact damage.
- Communicates technical research to a general audience through observable failure and survival.
