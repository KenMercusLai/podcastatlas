## Overview
于根伟 / Yu Genwei is the general manager and head coach of [[TianjinJinmenTiger|Tianjin Jinmen Tiger]] presented in the source as the central architect of its low-budget roster, tactics, player development, communication, and team culture.

## Current Profile
The episode credits Yu with adapting quickly when an ambitious early-season approach did not produce enough results. He shifted toward defensive stability and counterattacking, supported detailed contingency planning during his touchline suspension, and used midseason renewals to give players a shared future during a relegation crisis.

His combined executive and coaching authority aligns short-term selection with multi-year squad building. The same concentration creates a succession risk: player trust, recruitment judgment, public communication, tactical decisions, and much of the club's identity are all described as depending on him.

## Key Characteristics
- Combined general manager and head coach with responsibility beyond matchday tactics.
- Pragmatic adapter who accepted a less preferred style when survival required it.
- Patient developer of young, underused, or second-chance players.
- Trust-centered manager whose continued presence influenced players' willingness to renew.
- Public communicator who used livestreams to explain recruitment, contracts, and club constraints.
- High-leverage operator under limited spending, but also a concentration and succession risk.

## Evidence
- Tactical adaptation: [[vol-229-fafen-zhihou-tianjin-baojilu-ft-shenwei-gkwrijeosmquazu-rgttvpfk]] says Yu moved toward compact defending and counterattacks after the initial approach struggled.
- Contract and trust role: [[vol-229-fafen-zhihou-tianjin-baojilu-ft-shenwei-gkwrijeosmquazu-rgttvpfk]] reports that players wanted assurance he would remain before renewing during the relegation fight.
- Player development: [[vol-229-fafen-zhihou-tianjin-baojilu-ft-shenwei-gkwrijeosmquazu-rgttvpfk]] uses Han Pengfei and Ji Shengpan as examples of opportunity, coaching, and patience creating value.
- Cultural leadership: [[vol-229-fafen-zhihou-tianjin-baojilu-ft-shenwei-gkwrijeosmquazu-rgttvpfk]] describes personal care for injured or ill foreign players and a dressing room with limited local-foreign separation.
- Concentration risk: [[vol-229-fafen-zhihou-tianjin-baojilu-ft-shenwei-gkwrijeosmquazu-rgttvpfk]] explicitly links tactics, recruitment, management, and culture to Yu's presence.

## Qualifications
The assessment comes from a favorable club-focused discussion and cannot establish how Yu would perform with a larger budget or at another club. Claims about external interest, relative spending efficiency, refereeing pressure, and his causal contribution to results remain source-scoped.
