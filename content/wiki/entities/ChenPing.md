# 陈平 / Chen Ping

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-150-chenping-weihe-bei-cheng-zhiwei-chenggong-zhengzhijia-yangban-1-llale6fron0tkye7n0e-aanojqar|Hanji 150]] backfills Chen Ping's early profile before the later Chu-Han camp scenes. The episode presents him as a poor Yangwu/Huyou man whose parents die early and whose elder brother protects his room to read, socialize, and form ties instead of forcing him into farm labor.

The source's first major turning point is Chen Ping's marriage into [[ZhangFu|张负]]'s family. [[ZhangFu|Zhang Fu]] notices Chen Ping's bearing and visitor traffic, then marries [[ChenPingWife|his granddaughter]] to him and supplies the funds for betrothal and feast. The episode treats this as [[LocalSocialCapitalAccumulation|乡里社会资本积累]]: Chen Ping converts reading, friendships, service at funerals, and elite backing into a higher local standing.

The second turning point is the社祭分肉 scene. When villagers ask Chen Ping to distribute sacrificial meat, he allocates it fairly and earns public praise. Hanji 150 reads this as [[FairAllocationPoliticalSkill|公平分配式政治能力]], an early sign that Chen Ping's practical wisdom includes concrete benefit distribution and public trust, not only later court stratagems.

陈平 / Chen Ping enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-142-zhechang-juhui-xiangyu-shudiao-le-yiqie-ln7dv2jxrk6lt1c4szylsrb41k3r|Hanji 142]] as a trusted [[XiangYu|项羽]]-side officer during the later [[HongmenYan|鸿门宴]] sequence. After [[LiuBang|刘邦]] leaves the tent and does not return, Xiang Yu sends Chen Ping to look for him.

The source presents Chen Ping as still belonging to Xiang Yu's camp, not yet as a Han minister. Its interpretive move is foreshadowing: Chen Ping is said to have noticed Liu Bang's restraint and ability to use people, and when he meets [[ZhangLiang|张良]] outside the tent, he does not press the search hard enough to expose the escape. His restraint helps create the time gap that lets Liu Bang reach [[Bashang|霸上]] before Zhang Liang returns with the gifts.

For this source, Chen Ping's importance is transitional. He is a Xiang-camp agent whose conduct already points toward later compatibility with Liu Bang's side. The wiki therefore records him as a source-scoped hinge between Hongmen escape management and future Chu-Han personnel movement, without treating the episode as a completed defection.
