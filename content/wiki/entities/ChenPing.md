# 陈平 / Chen Ping

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-150-chenping-weihe-bei-cheng-zhiwei-chenggong-zhengzhijia-yangban-1-llale6fron0tkye7n0e-aanojqar|Hanji 150]] backfills Chen Ping's early profile before the later Chu-Han camp scenes. The episode presents him as a poor Yangwu/Huyou man whose parents die early and whose elder brother protects his room to read, socialize, and form ties instead of forcing him into farm labor.

The source's first major turning point is Chen Ping's marriage into [[ZhangFu|张负]]'s family. [[ZhangFu|Zhang Fu]] notices Chen Ping's bearing and visitor traffic, then marries [[ChenPingWife|his granddaughter]] to him and supplies the funds for betrothal and feast. The episode treats this as [[LocalSocialCapitalAccumulation|乡里社会资本积累]]: Chen Ping converts reading, friendships, service at funerals, and elite backing into a higher local standing.

The second turning point is the社祭分肉 scene. When villagers ask Chen Ping to distribute sacrificial meat, he allocates it fairly and earns public praise. Hanji 150 reads this as [[FairAllocationPoliticalSkill|公平分配式政治能力]], an early sign that Chen Ping's practical wisdom includes concrete benefit distribution and public trust, not only later court stratagems.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-150-chenping-weihe-bei-cheng-zhiwei-chenggong-zhengzhijia-yangban-2-lkbo2kwkn6aexyyygv-m84fnqx2h|Hanji 150 part 2]] carries that early setup into Chu-Han office politics. The episode presents Chen Ping as a figure whose wisdom is often uncomfortable because it works through [[CovertPoliticalTechnology|阴谋式政治技术]]: bribery, rumor, induced defection, hidden information, and enemy-side division rather than only upright counsel.

The source follows his movement through patrons. Chen Ping first serves [[WeiJiu|魏咎]] at Linji, leaves when his advice is ignored and he is slandered, then gains [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s trust. After the [[SimaAng|司马卬]] affair makes him vulnerable to Xiang Yu's anger, he returns his seal and gold and leaves with only a sword, making self-preservation a central part of his political skill.

The Yellow River boat scene makes that self-preservation concrete. Chen Ping reads the boatman's suspicion, realizes he may be killed for imagined hidden wealth, and exposes that he is carrying no treasure. The episode uses this as a sign of acute situational awareness rather than grand strategy.

At [[Xiuwu|修武]], [[WeiWuzhi|魏无知]] introduces Chen Ping to [[LiuBang|刘邦]]. Liu Bang quickly appoints him despite old generals' complaints, which the source reads through [[VeteranMeritCliqueSupervision|功臣旧将监督]] and [[OutsiderTalentSignaling|厚待外来人才信号]]: Chen Ping is useful because he is talented, outside the old Pei County circle, and visible proof that Liu Bang will empower non-hometown talent after the [[HanXin|韩信]] precedent.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-150-chenping-weihe-bei-cheng-zhiwei-chenggong-zhengzhijia-yangban-3-lhfbm3twyxhyvokphf8hc9ssblqj|Hanji 150 part 3]] completes the profile by following Chen Ping into later court survival. When Liu Bang confronts him over accusations including patron switching, sexual scandal, and taking money, Chen Ping answers pragmatically: Liu Bang needs his ability, and Chen Ping needs resources to live and work. Liu Bang then gives him large funds for enemy-side inducement without demanding accounts, making Chen Ping a trusted but morally uncomfortable carrier of [[CovertPoliticalTechnology|阴谋式政治技术]].

The same source frames Chen Ping's long career through [[PoliticalSurvivalFirst|安全第一政治生存]]. Under [[LuZhi|吕雉]] he does not openly make himself the anti-Lu martyr that [[WangLingHanMinister|王陵]] becomes, but after Lu Zhi dies he joins [[ZhouBo|周勃]] and the Liu-family restoration that installs [[LiuHeng|刘恒 / Emperor Wen of Han]]. The host therefore treats him as slippery and private-virtue-poor, yet unusually capable of staying alive and useful across Liu Bang, Lu Zhi, and Emperor Wen.

Hanji 150 part 3 also sharpens Chen Ping's role boundary through [[SecondInCommandRoleFit|二把手角色适配]]. Liu Bang can use him heavily but does not treat him as a person fit for sole independent responsibility; the source contrasts him with [[ZhangLiang|张良]], [[XiaoHe|萧何]], [[CaoShen|曹参]], and Zhou Bo as a different kind of founding-minister value.

陈平 / Chen Ping enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-142-zhechang-juhui-xiangyu-shudiao-le-yiqie-ln7dv2jxrk6lt1c4szylsrb41k3r|Hanji 142]] as a trusted [[XiangYu|项羽]]-side officer during the later [[HongmenYan|鸿门宴]] sequence. After [[LiuBang|刘邦]] leaves the tent and does not return, Xiang Yu sends Chen Ping to look for him.

The source presents Chen Ping as still belonging to Xiang Yu's camp, not yet as a Han minister. Its interpretive move is foreshadowing: Chen Ping is said to have noticed Liu Bang's restraint and ability to use people, and when he meets [[ZhangLiang|张良]] outside the tent, he does not press the search hard enough to expose the escape. His restraint helps create the time gap that lets Liu Bang reach [[Bashang|霸上]] before Zhang Liang returns with the gifts.

For this source, Chen Ping's importance is transitional. He is a Xiang-camp agent whose conduct already points toward later compatibility with Liu Bang's side. The wiki therefore records him as a source-scoped hinge between Hongmen escape management and future Chu-Han personnel movement, without treating the episode as a completed defection.
