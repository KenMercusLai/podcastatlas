# Bain & Company

## Overview
Bain & Company is the management-consulting firm that [[FedericaFornaciari]] says she chose for its entrepreneurial culture and where she developed structured analysis and problem anticipation.

## Current Profile
In the bounded interview, Bain represents both the strength and limit of classical consulting. It supplies rigor, independent perspective, and organization-wide diagnosis, but the wider consulting model can lose contact with execution when clients use advisers for board validation or responsibility transfer.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides the analytical and problem-structuring background in Fornaciari's account.
- Is presented as more entrepreneurial than the alternatives she considered.
- Represents an external perspective not embedded in a client's assumptions or products.
- Sits inside a broader critique of strategy work detached from implementation.
