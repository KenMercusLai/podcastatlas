# Brian Krebs

Brian Krebs is the cybersecurity journalist from [[KrebsOnSecurity]] interviewed by [[StephanieHughes]] in [[tech-20260415-0415-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260415-0415-mp-tech-pod-128]]. In the episode, he explains how botnets compromise routers, web cameras, computers, and TV streaming boxes, then use them for denial-of-service traffic or proxy activity.

Krebs' main contribution to the wiki is to make [[IoTBotnetRisk]] concrete for general listeners. He treats cheap pirated-streaming boxes as a consumer temptation with a hidden security cost, and he ties old routers to [[HomeRouterSecurityLifecycle]] because unsupported hardware is hard for ordinary owners to assess or clean.

## Key Claims
- Botnets can be built from ordinary internet-connected devices whose owners do not know they are infected.
- Some TV boxes that promise free access to paid streaming services may come with malware or push users toward risky app stores.
- Compromised devices can check in with command infrastructure and be repurposed for attacks, traffic relaying, or malware updates.
- Router replacement can be a practical consumer-security step when inspection and cleanup are unrealistic.
