# Jeff Buckley

## Overview

Jeff Buckley is the singer and guitarist whose recording of [[HallelujahSong|“Hallelujah”]] adapted [[JohnCale]]'s interpretation and became a major branch through which later audiences and performers encountered the song.

## Current Profile

The episode says Buckley discovered Cale's recording while house-sitting in Brooklyn, performed his own version at Sin-é, and recorded it for the 1994 album *Grace*. His interpretation replaced Cale's piano center with guitar and transformed the material through vocal performance, making it neither a direct copy of Cale nor an independent return to Cohen's first recording.

Initial commercial response was limited. The episode connects wider attention to Buckley's 1997 death, subsequent covers, and soundtrack use, but this remains a compressed account of reception rather than proof that tragedy caused the song's eventual prominence.

## Key Characteristics

- Discovered the song through Cale's comparatively obscure interpretation.
- Recast the arrangement around guitar and voice.
- Made performance and timbre central to the song's later public identity.
- Received much broader recognition after his death than at the album's initial release.
