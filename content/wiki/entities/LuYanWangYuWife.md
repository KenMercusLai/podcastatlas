## Overview

Lu Yan / 吕嫣 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-you-xinji-de-ren-you-duo-kepa-lnhkk70905eqg4lqwh3mp2sxzamc|Hanji 616]] as [[WangYuXinPrince|王宇]]'s pregnant wife, whose imprisonment and condemned postpartum death show the household reach of the [[DogBloodCase|狗血案]].

## Current Profile

The source gives Lu Yan little agency but high evidentiary weight. After Wang Yu is implicated and forced to die, Lu Yan is imprisoned while pregnant. Wang Mang orders that she be executed after childbirth, making her fate part of the case's cruelty and a precursor to the later Hanji 634 memory that Wang Yu's wife died miserably in prison.

## Key Characteristics

- Wife of Wang Yu and sister of Lu Kuan.
- Pregnant prisoner after the dog-blood case is exposed.
- Condemned to die after childbirth.
- Household victim whose fate links Wang Yu's death to broader family destruction.

- Postpartum execution order: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-you-xinji-de-ren-you-duo-kepa-lnhkk70905eqg4lqwh3mp2sxzamc|Hanji 616]] says Wang Mang orders her death after childbirth.
