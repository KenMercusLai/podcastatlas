# 傅干 / Fu Gan

傅干 / Fu Gan enters through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-937-zhanshen-zhisi-haoren-zongshi-duozai-duonan-lp7przsmag9ookq7eoisvsmnm5aw|Hanji 937]] as the thirteen-year-old son of [[FuXie|傅燮]]. When [[HanyangCommanderyLateHan|汉阳郡]] is surrounded and food is gone, Fu Gan urges his father to leave because the ruler is muddled and the city cannot be held.

Fu Xie rejects escape for himself but not for his son. He sends Fu Gan away under [[YangHuiLateHan|杨晖]]'s protection, using the Cheng Ying orphan-rescue analogy to define Yang Hui's role. The source says Fu Gan later lives up to his father's expectation and becomes an important historian; that later evaluation is kept source-scoped here.
