# 傅干 / Fu Gan

## Overview
傅干 / Fu Gan enters the wiki first as [[FuXie|傅燮]]'s surviving son in the Hanyang siege branch, then reappears as a Ma Teng-side adviser in the Guanzhong pressure branch after [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s death. Across the bounded evidence, Fu Gan is not only a rescued child of a loyal martyr; he becomes an adult voice who turns survival, legitimacy, and interest analysis into concrete military alignment.

## Current Profile
Hanji 937 presents Fu Gan as the thirteen-year-old son of Fu Xie. When [[HanyangCommanderyLateHan|汉阳郡]] is surrounded and food is gone, Fu Gan urges his father to leave because the ruler is muddled and the city cannot be held. Fu Xie rejects escape for himself but not for his son. He sends Fu Gan away under [[YangHuiLateHan|杨晖]]'s protection, using the Cheng Ying orphan-rescue analogy to define Yang Hui's role. The source says Fu Gan later lives up to his father's expectation and becomes an important historian; that later evaluation remains source-scoped.

Hanji 1056 supplies the adult political payoff. As a subordinate in [[MaTeng|马腾]]'s circle, Fu Gan persuades Ma Teng not to wait between [[CaoCao|曹操]] and [[YuanShang|袁尚]]'s side after Yuan Shang sends [[GuoYuanLateHan|郭援 / 郭元]] west and secretly contacts Ma Teng. Fu Gan frames Cao Cao as honoring the emperor and treating talent well, while Guo Yuan's side is tied to rebellion and the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]]. He argues that Ma Teng should join [[ZhongYao|钟繇]] in attacking Guo Yuan, after which Ma Teng sends troops to Zhong Yao's side.

## Key Characteristics
- Fu Xie's son, preserved through Yang Hui's rescue mission after the Hanyang siege.
- Early voice for survival under a failed ruler, contrasted with Fu Xie's official-duty death choice.
- Source-scoped later scholar/historian figure according to Hanji 937's evaluation.
- Ma Teng-side adviser who translates legitimacy and survival analysis into alignment advice.
- Guanzhong persuasion actor who helps detach Ma Teng from Yuan Shang's western pressure line.

## Qualifications
The wiki still lacks a full biography of Fu Gan. Hanji 937's claim about his later historiographical importance and Hanji 1056's exact office language are held as source-scoped. The current synthesis should not infer continuous service under Ma Teng from childhood onward; it only joins the two bounded appearances now present in the wiki.
