## Overview

韩说 / Han Shuo enters the wiki through Hanji 380-1 as the Youji General sent from Wuyuan with infantry during [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s large 97 BCE anti-[[Xiongnu|匈奴]] campaign. The episode uses him as one part of the broad mobilization that precedes [[LiLingWesternHan|李陵]]'s more tragic infantry defeat.

## Current Profile

The source gives Han Shuo a compact campaign role: he leads more than thirty thousand infantry from Wuyuan, then returns without achievement. His function in the synthesis is to show that even large Han forces in this operation produce limited returns.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Youji General in the episode's account.
- Leads infantry from Wuyuan during the 97 BCE anti-Xiongnu campaign.
- Returns without success, adding to the episode's sense of costly but inconclusive campaigning.
