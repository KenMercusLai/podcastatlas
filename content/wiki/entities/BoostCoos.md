# Boost Coos

## Overview

Boost Coos is a gluten-free couscous alternative made from chickpea, lentil, and pea flours. The episode uses the bootstrapped company to examine how a retail product can earn its way into food service.

## Current Profile

The company reports rapid box shipments, profitability on first orders, and strong protein and fiber content. Its next challenge is not just persuading a large chain to buy; it is proving cooking speed, holding stability, supply reliability, quality assurance, and organizational readiness. The proposed path starts with small restaurants and credible chefs before national fast-casual accounts.

## Key Characteristics
- Three-ingredient, gluten-free couscous alternative made from legume flours.
- Caller reports 18 grams of protein and 11 grams of fiber per serving.
- Bootstrapped business reporting early profitability and substantial box shipments.
- Has already appeared in creator and chef preparations beyond conventional couscous dishes.
- Needs restaurant proof, supply reliability, quality systems, and staff before large-chain food service.

## Qualifications

Nutrition, shipment, revenue-run-rate, profitability, and viral-content claims are caller-reported. The source does not establish repeat purchase, restaurant reorder rates, manufacturing headroom, or national-chain acceptance.
