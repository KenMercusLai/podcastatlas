# 大卫翁 / David Weng

In [[163-dang-guzhuyizhi-de-nianqingren-yushang-zhege-caotaibanzi-de-shijie-chuantai-qingdao-kuaima-lvxfvtz-wgwdl8mmtzyehwgwtbta]], he joins [[FuYu|傅宇]] and [[QingdaoKuaima|轻刀快马]] for a youth, randomness, and asset-allocation crossover. His role is to translate U.S. visiting-scholar observations, AI anxiety, and young people's [[FinancialNihilism]] into the show's recurring risk discipline: do not confuse structural frustration with permission for all-in bets; use [[StructureOverPrediction]], [[BoundedSpeculativeOptionality]], [[BarbellStrategy]], and [[PortfolioSuitability]] to remain in the repeated game.

In [[162-caifu-de-benzhi-yiji-ziyou-de-zhenzheng-hanyi-chuantai-shifen-xiyin-lofyxfhiprud7zvekufexsjhtust]], he hosts the wealth-and-freedom follow-up to the earlier [[ShifenXiyin|十分吸引]] money crossover. His role is to keep a cross-disciplinary wealth definition tied to ordinary investing and life design: [[WealthAsCapabilityAndRights]], [[BourdieuCapitalForms]], [[MainContradictionAllocation]], [[ResourceNetworkRepricing]], [[ClientCenteredWealthManagement]], and [[WealthDesireGap]] all become ways to ask whether assets create real freedom rather than only visible richness.

In [[161-quanqiu-hongguan-he-ziben-shichang-2026-yijidu-fupan-yu-zhanwang-lgzjnsgkur1mcrmkqqxsvxhzb0bm]], he hosts [[Ricky]] at a live Beijing first-quarter macro review after the 2026-03-23 Asia-market selloff. His role is to connect [[Iran]], [[DonaldTrump]], AI, private credit, gold, oil, China data, and A/H allocation back to the show's ordinary-investor discipline: forecasts are less reliable when [[GeopoliticalCycleMacro]] becomes a trend, so [[OneToOneToOneAllocation]], permanent cash, [[GoldAsCurrencySpareTire|gold sizing]], and [[PortfolioSuitability]] matter more than point targets.

In [[160-ruhe-yingdui-zhongguo-zichan-niushi-de-tiaozhengqi-xinshu-fenxianghui-chengdu-chang-shilu-lm8degdbgmgi6nnwspjy-gdzwqfz]], he turns a Chengdu [[AssetAllocationActionGuide|《资产配置行动指南》]] event into a live test of his allocation framework during a China-asset pullback. His main contribution is to keep long-term China exposure, medium-term AI/gold/geopolitical narratives, and short-term cash optionality separate, while making [[PortfolioSuitability]] depend on human capital, household cash flow, liabilities, and whether the investor can sleep through volatility.

In [[159-suanfa-de-liufu-miankong-ta-shi-ruhe-cong-chuli-shuju-biancheng-dingyi-women-shi-shui-de-lh5ng5mbvkworfxlq9l-coscepc7]], he opens an algorithm-and-media series with a literature-review style solo episode. His role shifts from market or institution analyst into media-theory synthesizer: [[TarletonGillespie]], [[TheRelevanceOfAlgorithms]], [[KyleChayka]], and [[Filterworld]] become a framework for explaining [[PublicRelevanceAlgorithms]], [[AlgorithmicEntanglement]], [[CalculatedPublics]], and why [[AlgorithmicDiversityDividend|multi-platform diversity]] can be a practical response to [[InformationCocoon]].

In [[158-boke-li-liao-le-si-nian-de-zichan-peizhi-wo-ba-ta-zuocheng-le-yiben-xingdong-zhinan-ljorcjal-gpuhmwodo1l3efr7k29]], he explains the origin and positioning of his first book, [[AssetAllocationActionGuide|《资产配置行动指南》]]. His role shifts from podcast host to author-builder: he uses the episode to distinguish [[PodcastToBookSystematization]] from transcript cleanup, thank the editorial path that led to [[CITICPress|中信出版社]], and restate that [[AssetAllocation]] starts with [[InvestmentWorldviewFit]] rather than a "wealth code."

In [[157-ruhe-daizou-niushi-de-shengli-guoshi-lory40ilowkjfe-lt-hiwjdsdbq2]], he returns to the 投资账 format after a profitable 2025 and positive early 2026, but moves the emphasis from opportunity discovery to keeping gains. His synthesis is [[BullMarketProfitPreservation]]: watch for [[BullMarketBezzleTrap|bezzle-like dream narratives]], use rules for assets already judged bubbly, avoid quick reentry after exit, and convert some floating profit into [[GainConversionAssetForm|more holdable forms]] such as physical gold, dividend assets, REITs, housing, insurance, or deposits.

In [[156-shengwu-yiyao-de-2026-dang-shichang-bu-zai-wei-bd-zaodong-zhongguo-yaoqi-de-xingchen-dahai-cai-ganggang-zhankai-lil-ugrzq8uvzviq3f8i-wm9ilup]], he returns to the annual biotech review with [[XiaoPTeacher|小P老师]]. His host synthesis is that 2025's record China innovation-drug BD is meaningful but incomplete: 2026 must test [[BiotechBDDataValidationPhase]], [[ProgressionFreeVsOverallSurvival]], [[ClinicalDataTrustRebuilding]], global cost-sharing, and payment policy before the "Chinese pharma globalization" story can be trusted.

In [[155-ruhe-lijie-huangjin-de-shishiji-bodong-lp3lcda5zskiv-dcezcugf2q93vi]], he records an addendum on gold and silver's extreme volatility, then turns it into an asset-allocation and political-economy lesson. His main contribution is [[CommodityTimeHorizonFramework]]: short-term gold depends on liquidity and momentum, medium-term gold on the distrust narrative, long-term gold on central-bank demand, and ultra-long gold on [[CurrencyAnchorTransition]]. He also gives a source-scoped [[KevinWarsh]] reading that connects Fed-chair succession to [[CentralBankIndependence]] and [[FederalFundsRateAsPolicySignal]].

In [[154-sishisui-ganyan-bu-zuo-nazhi-wenshuili-de-qingwa-lg-qj3p2lq-8sk-rn2vk209tq9x]], he turns the show inward for a forty-year-old personal retrospective. His role shifts from host-analyst to source subject: leaving the [[PeoplesBankOfChina|People's Bank of China]] system, experiencing the 2015 stock crash in private funds, building investor-education content, entering a top brokerage platform through [[FinancePlatformSocialCapital]], and later leaving finance all become evidence for [[CareerExitFriction]], [[TelosCrisis]], [[HabitPositiveFeedback]], and [[AutonomyUnderInformationFlow]].

In [[153-quanqiu-hongguan-he-ziben-shichang-2026-zhanwang-da-nian-zhihou-reng-shi-da-nian-lupeqjdszon-wp5zdq06w3ustw2d]], he hosts [[Ricky]] for the 2026 global macro and capital-market outlook. His role is to keep 2025's strong asset returns connected to ordinary-investor allocation questions: China/Hong Kong risk assets, gold rebalancing, insurance-fund inflows, A-share index constituents, U.S. AI/private-market bubble location, and whether the stock-market rally has outrun real-economy repair.

In [[152-guanyu-2026-nian-de-si-ge-caixiang-nhx-c5xjwaovpaqjpua24diohsaz]], he returns to the annual "four guesses" format and makes [[QuantityChangeQualityChange]] the organizing frame. His synthesis joins a possible Western [[AIBacklashPolitics|anti-AI backlash]], [[PrivateMarketBubbleOpacity]], [[ChinaExcessSavingsReallocation]], and [[WesternChinaMisreading]], while explicitly treating the claims as watchlist logic rather than guaranteed 2026 events.

In [[151-simu-xindai-private-credit-jiasu-ai-jianshe-de-tianshi-haishi-youfa-jinrong-weiji-de-emo-nlahybjwcrimit8dff7yhzpbupm1]], he turns earlier private-credit warnings into a full credit-market explainer. His synthesis separates the useful role of [[PrivateCreditMarket|private credit]] from the risk that [[DirectLending]], [[AssetBasedFinance]], [[PaymentInKindInterest|PIK]], [[SyntheticRiskTransfer|SRT]], [[RatedNoteFeeders]], insurer funding, bank channels, and [[AIDataCenterPrivateCreditFinancing]] can combine into opaque cycle-tested fragility.

In [[150-zhongguo-ruhe-tiaochu-pinkun-xianjing-xiandaihua-zhi-lu-you-hui-zouxiang-hefang-lgpvp04rngagkpubte2ac6i5ji3g]], he turns the show's China-analysis thread into a three-book modernization synthesis. His role is to connect [[LiLu|李璐]]'s civilization and investment frame, [[YuenYuenAng|洪源远]]'s poverty-trap and local-government fieldwork, and [[DanWang]]'s [[EngineeringState]] / [[LawyerState]] contrast, then ask what [[China]] still lacks after engineering and manufacturing capacity are no longer the main bottlenecks.

In [[149-baiwu-teji-he-liangwei-laomeitiren-mantan-boke-meijie-yiji-shengyin-shengtai-de-weilai-lihbq8vujsqxvb7h2-pz2xm-eqga]], he shifts from finance and Japan-system synthesis into explicit podcast and media reflection. His main additions are personal: he explains that podcasting became attractive when public-account writing felt constrained, that low production friction made audio easier than polished video, and that voice comments feel more intimate than text-reader responses. In the distribution debate, he is more skeptical than [[YangYi|杨一]] about algorithmic recommendation because it can concentrate attention and weaken small voices.

In [[147-zaitan-riben-yiliao-yu-zhaohu-hangye-zhi-wo-ceng-zai-beihaidao-de-yiyuan-dang-hushi-lgokb-l-nvnqsstfzlqwhtnkqzhp]], he hosts [[QiongQiong|琼琼]] for a follow-up to the Japanese healthcare fact-checking thread. His role is to move the discussion from "Japan's healthcare system is good or bad" into concrete care practice: [[JapaneseNoFamilyAttendantCare]], [[MultidisciplinaryHospitalCare]], [[MedicalSocialWorkDischargeCoordination]], [[CareSocialization]], [[PatientDignityInDailyCare]], and the limits of [[AIAndRoboticElderCareLimits|AI or robotic elder care]].

In [[146-meiguo-jingji-zheme-cha-meigu-hai-neng-jixu-zhang-ma-chuantai-meilun-meihuan-lrshkxgofprel-e-7lkiqu-en8gu]], he joins [[MeilunMeihuan|美轮美换]] for a U.S. macro and market-sentiment crossover. His main synthesis is that "the U.S. economy is bad" and "U.S. stocks are rising" can both be locally true when [[USEconomicExperienceSplit]], Big Tech earnings, buybacks, [[EquityRetirementAssetBinding]], AI capex, and [[PrivateCreditTailRisk]] are separated from everyday labor-market and price pain.

In [[145-jijin-tougu-zhide-xinren-ma-lrckug0zjqolcczni8ajikb0k5mi]], he continues the "金融机构是如何运作的" sequence by asking whether Chinese [[FundInvestmentAdvisory|fund advisory]] deserves trust. His contribution is to move the question away from simple performance ranking and toward [[BuySideInvestmentAdvisory]], [[InvestmentBehaviorCoaching]], [[GoalBasedClientProfiling]], visible fees, post-purchase service, and [[FundRecommendationConflictDisclosure|recommendation conflict disclosure]].

In [[144-nvxia-duan-sihe-he-tade-jiaxiang-mapingguan-women-ceng-yiwei-you-dian-you-lu-zhishi-meng-linmjqt-n-sa5sarpqgdxgtoeejt]], he hosts a field conversation with [[DuanSihe|段四合]] in [[Mapingguan|马坪关]]. His role is less market synthesis than listening and reframing: he starts from surprise at a rural homestay host's experience and turns the conversation toward [[VillageSelfGovernance]], [[RuralInfrastructureTransition]], [[MatsutakeCommonsManagement]], [[RuralWomenPublicParticipation]], and [[SlowRuralTourism]].

In [[bu-xi-deng-e02-bi-quan-shan-beng-mei-guo-zheng-fu-guan-men-first-brands-po-chan-wa-ha-ha-feng-bo-lt-ghzgmiroo9a-odaih4xkhsulu]], he co-hosts [[Buxideng|不熄灯]] with [[Yubai|雨白]] and [[XiaobaoQizhulou|小宝]]. His role is to keep a fast current-events episode tied to the show's existing macro and institution instincts: U.S. shutdown effects on everyday services and data, [[FirstBrands|First Brands]] as a [[PrivateCreditTailRisk]] warning, and [[WahahaGroup|娃哈哈]] as a family-business governance story rather than only a gossip cycle.

In [[141-jiageng-yinwei-boke-wo-shouyao-qu-gelunbiya-daxue-zuo-fangwenxuezhe-le-lluk9kruhveokkbmfo0adrj7etpj]], he records a personal addendum explaining that [[QizhulouYanBinke|起朱楼宴宾客]] led to a one-year visiting-scholar invitation from [[ColumbiaJournalismSchool|Columbia Journalism School]]. His role shifts from host to research subject and researcher: he frames podcasts, creators, subscriptions, algorithms, and social platforms as forces reshaping public and financial narratives, while also naming the fact-checking duties that follow from audience trust.

In [[142-riben-xiaofeizhe-weihe-ruci-chimi-jijie-xianding-chuantai-kuahai-dianbo-lgbjiahlutyyf4paylnyxy7hv0iq]], he hosts a [[KuahaiDianbo|跨海电波]] crossover on Japanese seasonal-limited consumption and China-to-Japan market entry. His role is to connect consumer observation to operating implications: [[JapaneseSeasonalLimitedConsumption]] explains why limited products can feel like annual participation, while [[JapanMarketEntryTrust]] explains why Chinese brands need channel, offline, after-sales, and slower relationship work in [[Japan]].

In [[140-jiudian-jituan-weishenme-dou-rezhongyu-dazao-huiyuan-he-jifen-tixi-chuantai-yuanxingzhe-yu-suibingjiang-lj-xa3wxzjog6-8zm9nfakh6ulew]], he hosts a hospitality crossover with [[YuanxingzheYuSuibingjiang|远行者与碎冰匠]] speakers [[MaocaoYuanxingzhe|猫草]] and [[CindyYuanxingzhe|Cindy]]. In [[139-cong-zuo-ri-de-shi-jie-dao-ru-jin-de-ou-zhou-mei-hao-nian-dai-belle-epoque-ta-hai-neng-hui-lai-ma-ltvsgov8ht9-l7yp9qamdgjmyrf]], he hosts [[FeiQizhulou|费]] for a Europe-and-literature discussion around [[StefanZweig|Stefan Zweig / 茨威格]] and [[TheWorldOfYesterday|《昨日的世界》]]. His role is to keep the episode moving between literary witness, [[BelleEpoque|Belle Epoque]] nostalgia, [[EuropeanUnion]] integration, [[PassportMobilityInequality]], the [[Ukraine]] war, tariff bargaining, and the question of whether a contemporary "beautiful age" belongs to a place or to a mobile class.

In [[132-dang-guodu-sikao-de-dagongren-yu-shang-di-yuwang-de-shidai-luaqswjqezbakxdrcshifxi0ermo]], he turns the Japan series from housing and systems analysis toward workplace experience, overthinking, and low desire. His role is to keep [[YoumamaMaomao|尤妈妈 / 猫猫]]'s personal account connected to broader frames: Japanese off-work boundaries, [[RuleBoundOverthinking]], Chinese always-on work, algorithmic feeds, AI usefulness anxiety, and whether concrete daily practices can preserve agency when ambition loses its obvious path.

In [[131-wo-zai-riben-mai-le-yi-tao-zizhu-fang-llofkgaj-hyajksrpkoylakuocxz]], he turns his Japan series into a first-person housing-system report. His contribution is to make an ordinary self-use condo purchase analytically useful: [[JapaneseNewCondoBuying]], [[JapaneseHousingPresaleProtection]], [[JapaneseCondoMaintenanceReserve]], [[JapaneseHousingInformationDisclosure]], [[JapaneseMortgageInsuranceBundle]], [[NegativeRealEstate]], [[HousingAsConsumptionGood]], [[JapaneseUrbanCoreRedevelopment]], and [[JapanesePropertyHoldingCost]] become a concrete way to test whether Japanese housing is a life infrastructure, a financial asset, or both.

大卫翁 is the [[QizhulouYanBinke|起朱楼宴宾客]] host and speaker in [[vol-104-putongren-ganggu-wanquan-shengcun-zhinan-chuantai-sandian-xiaban-lmzivgi6d3guv7wajyjw9cv9zz90]]. In [[vol-106-dibai-zhende-biandi-shi-huangjin-lpxchutxf7yomjdfykwzvvnmbaq8]], he hosts [[Ricky]]'s field report on [[Dubai]], [[AbuDhabi|Abu Dhabi]], capital flow, real estate, crypto, residency, and Chinese outbound-business opportunities. His role is mainly to test whether Dubai's opportunity story should be read as ordinary investing, migration, geopolitics, or city-development signal.

In [[vol-107-85hou-tiwen-95hou-ruguo-shiqing-bu-xuyao-zuo-xuanze-na-wo-jiu-zhuang-shiti-pangguan-llvntaqucnilovj19zoywrfdwzsa]], he shifts from investing and overseas opportunity into a generational interview role, asking [[SiriQizhulou|Siri]] how a 95后 thinks about youth "corpse mode", present-choice boundaries, work-life separation, finance careers, and relationship optionality.

In [[vol-108-riben-wuda-zonghe-shangshe-zhongfan-wutai-zhongyang-lhamp3hn14iihcicpa8po5dvh6gq]], he returns to Japan and investing-adjacent business history through [[JapaneseSogoShosha|Japanese sogo shosha]].
