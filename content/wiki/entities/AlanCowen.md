# Alan Cowen

## Overview
Alan Cowen is named in the Keltner-Huberman source as a computational emotion researcher whose large-scale video-analysis work helped expand the measured vocabulary of emotional expression across cultures.

## Current Profile
Within this source, Cowen matters as evidence that emotion science has moved beyond the older six-expression taxonomy associated with [[PaulEkman]]. Keltner cites Cowen's computational work as finding substantial cross-cultural overlap across a broader set of expressions, including laughter, love, compassion, awe, embarrassment, shame, and pain.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents the computational and large-scale video-analysis branch of emotion science in this source.
- Is used to support a broader taxonomy of emotional expression than the classical six-expression frame.
- Connects [[AweEmotion]] to measurable expression rather than only subjective report.

## Evidence
- Expression taxonomy: [[cultivating-awe-emotional-connection-in-daily-life-dr-dacher-keltner-scim6985403733]] says Keltner describes Cowen's computational video work as finding cross-cultural overlap across roughly twenty emotional expressions.
- Awe relevance: [[cultivating-awe-emotional-connection-in-daily-life-dr-dacher-keltner-scim6985403733]] places awe among the expanded expressions discussed in that emotion-science lineage.
