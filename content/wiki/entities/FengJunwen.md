## Overview
冯俊文 / Feng Junwen is presented as Xu Zhuoyun's late-life assistant, publishing collaborator, oral-history organizer, and cultural-estate executor.

## Current Profile
Feng first encountered Xu's writing as a student and began working with him as a publishing editor in 2011. Across roughly fifteen years, the source credits their collaboration with about sixteen mainland books, including several produced through Xu's oral narration, Feng's organization, and Xu's review. From late 2021 to early 2025, Feng lived for extended periods in Pittsburgh, helping manage writing, publication, visitors, and external affairs while protecting Xu's health and limited daily working time.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines editorial work with long-term personal assistance and oral-history organization.
- Converts concentrated oral narration into draft texts that remain subject to the speaker's review.
- Mediates public access by balancing communication opportunities against health and energy constraints.
- Serves as a close witness to Xu's late-life work, habits, relationships, and final arrangements.
- Holds an explicit stewardship role for Xu's cultural legacy and memorial responsibilities.
