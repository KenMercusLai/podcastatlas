# CBS

CBS appears in [[vol-265-kuayue-50-nian-de-meiguo-banben-zhizi-1001004591]] as one of the media assets implicated by [[DavidEllison]]'s [[Paramount]] expansion. The episode connects CBS to the broader question of whether a politically sensitive merger process can affect news independence.

The source also mentions a Trump lawsuit settlement involving CBS's 60 Minutes interview editing controversy. The page's wiki role is therefore to connect [[Paramount]], broadcast-news assets, and [[MediaOwnershipIndependenceRisk]], not to summarize CBS's full corporate history.

[[the-nfl-1]] adds CBS as a foundational NFL media-rights buyer. The episode says [[PeteRozelle]] negotiated a pooled national CBS deal after persuading [[NationalFootballLeague|NFL]] owners to centralize television rights, making CBS part of the league's [[SportsBroadcastingAct]] and [[SportsMediaRights]] history.
