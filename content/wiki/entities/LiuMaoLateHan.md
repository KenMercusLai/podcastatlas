## Overview

刘茂 / Liu Mao is the senior official in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] who joins [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] in petitioning for punished enforcement officials, then stops after impeachment pressure.

## Current Profile

The source gives Liu Mao a contrast role beside Chen Fan. Both officials initially defend [[ZhaiChaoLateHan|翟超]] and [[HuangFuDonghaiLateHan|黄浮]] after [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] punishes them under eunuch pressure. When the petition itself becomes dangerous and officials impeach the memorialists, Liu Mao becomes silent.

Liu Mao therefore marks the ordinary limit of remonstrance under a hostile court. His initial participation matters, but the source uses Chen Fan's continued memorial to show the difference between one-time institutional objection and sustained personal exposure.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior official who co-signs or joins the initial defense of punished enforcement officials.
- Remonstrance participant whose courage stops once retaliation reaches him directly.
- Contrast figure for Chen Fan's more durable resistance.
- Source-scoped witness to how court pressure narrows official speech.
