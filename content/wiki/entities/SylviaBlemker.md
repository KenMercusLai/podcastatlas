# Sylvia Blemker

## Overview

Sylvia Blemker is the biomedical engineering professor and [[SpringbokAnalytics]] co-founder featured for combining MRI with artificial intelligence to map individual muscles for sport and rehabilitation decisions.

## Current Profile

In [[pushkin-goes-to-the-olympics-pushkin-goes-to-the-olympics]], Blemker explains that elite athletic development can be highly uneven across muscles and that conventional aggregate testing may not identify the individual source of a side-to-side weakness. Her company's imaging converts a short MRI scan into a three-dimensional anatomical representation used to inspect muscle size, balance, and health.

The episode presents her work as an iterative decision system: locate specific weak muscles, design targeted exercises, use electromyography feedback to improve recruitment, and repeat imaging after several months. This is a company and episode account, not independent evidence that the method improves injury or performance outcomes.

## Key Characteristics

- Biomedical engineering professor working on muscle structure and function.
- Co-founder of Springbok Analytics.
- Uses MRI and AI to produce individual muscle maps.
- Frames athletic imbalance at muscle-specific rather than only limb-wide scale.
- Connects imaging to targeted exercise, biofeedback, and repeated assessment.
