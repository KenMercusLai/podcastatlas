# Francesca Gino

## Overview

Francesca Gino is the behavioral scientist and guest in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-47a9088936dc7be4136c0faa5df35a70-49678468-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-47a9088936dc7be4136c0faa5df35a70-49678468|Work 2.0: Rebel with a Cause]], where she presents “rebel talent” as constructive challenge to conventions rather than disruption for its own sake.

## Current Profile

The episode connects Gino's framework to [[MassimoBottura]]'s questioning of tradition, [[ChesleySullenberger]]'s adaptable expertise, and cases of career reinvention and public vulnerability. Its durable contribution is conditional: useful rebels pair curiosity and dissent with competence, shared purpose, respectful delivery, and safeguards that identify where strict compliance is required.

The current version also carries a decisive evidence qualification. It reports 2023 fabrication allegations, Gino's denial, and multiple retractions, including the karaoke study discussed in the episode. That study's findings are excluded from the wiki's evidentiary synthesis.

## Key Characteristics

- Presents constructive rule-challenge as a route to positive change rather than an end in itself.
- Connects curiosity and a beginner's mindset with resistance to expertise-driven rigidity.
- Emphasizes diverse perspectives and respectful delivery as conditions for useful dissent.
- Distinguishes broad permission to experiment from safety- and trust-critical compliance zones.
- Is covered under an explicit research-integrity warning that materially limits reliance on empirical claims.
