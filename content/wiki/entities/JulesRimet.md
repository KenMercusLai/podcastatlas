# Jules Rimet

## Overview

Jules Rimet was the French football administrator presented as the principal interwar advocate for creating the [[FIFAWorldCup|FIFA World Cup]].

## Current Profile

The episode places Rimet's project inside the aftermath of the First World War. A former French soldier influenced by Catholic social teaching and the internationalist atmosphere around the League of Nations, he hoped football could encourage human unity. His work helped turn FIFA-organized Olympic football into an independent tournament, although travel costs, European reluctance, national rivalry, and later authoritarian appropriation quickly exposed the limits of that ideal.

## Key Characteristics

- French football administrator associated with the World Cup's founding vision.
- Former soldier whose internationalism was shaped by the First World War's aftermath.
- Figure influenced by Catholic social teaching and interwar reconciliation ideals.
- Advocate for a tournament independent of Olympic amateurism and British football structures.
- Organizer who pressed reluctant European teams to attend Uruguay in 1930.
