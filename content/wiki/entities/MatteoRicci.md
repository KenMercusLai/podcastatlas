# 利玛窦 / Matteo Ricci

Matteo Ricci enters the wiki through [[ep259-yu-miao-shifu-mantan-xu-guangqi-sibainian-qian-ruhe-rangren-biancongming-lkirczxnx8skbbfpmz5-m-mivs4e]] as the [[Jesuits|Jesuit]] missionary whose maps, astronomical vocabulary, social performance, and elite strategy helped make [[LateMingWesternLearning|晚明西学东渐]] concrete for [[XuGuangqi|徐光启]]. The episode says Xu first encountered Ricci in Nanjing in 1600 and was drawn in by a world map before later studying geometry with him in Beijing.

Ricci's role is deliberately ambivalent. The source presents him as knowledgeable, charismatic, strategic, and skilled at selling curiosity: equator, poles, celestial spheres, longitude, latitude, and [[KunyuWanguoQuantu|《坤舆万国全图》]] opened a new explanatory world. At the same time, his route through elite access and calendar usefulness was tied to missionary goals rather than neutral knowledge exchange.

The episode therefore uses Ricci to connect [[HumanisticScienceCommunication|science communication]], [[ScienceReligionCivilizationTension|science-religion tension]], and [[ScienceValueTransmission|science value transmission]]. Western learning arrived as mathematics and geography, but also through religious institutions, social performance, and political opportunity.
