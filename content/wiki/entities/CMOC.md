# CMOC / 洛阳钼业

CMOC / 洛阳钼业 appears in [[vol-127-nianbaoji-zhong-de-zhenshi-zhongguo-2025-lqjy2pnfy09zyewj0ljxjuc3gxqv]] as a mining and battery-chain case. The source highlights CMOC's equity alliance with [[CATL]], where upstream raw-material supply, downstream demand, and financing support reinforce one another.

The episode uses CMOC to show that Chinese industrial ecosystems are not only final assembly clusters. Resource companies, battery makers, construction capability, engineering equipment, and overseas project execution can combine into an upstream version of [[SupplyChainSovereignty]] and [[ThreeLayerScaleEconomies]].

## Source Position
- CMOC is paired with [[ZijinMining]] as evidence of Chinese mining globalization.
- Its CATL relationship shows how upstream and downstream firms can coordinate through equity and demand.
- The source treats resource access as part of industrial competition, not only commodity trading.
- CMOC helps connect electric-vehicle supply chains to global resource ownership.
