# Flavorman

## Overview
Flavorman is a Louisville, Kentucky beverage-development company that helps clients turn drink ideas into formulas and production-ready prototypes.

## Current Profile
In the available source, Flavorman sits between creative concept and commercial manufacturing. Its role combines flavor construction, functional-ingredient formulation, prototyping, and advice about production economics, especially for clients without in-house food scientists. The company illustrates why [[BeverageDevelopmentFeasibility]] depends on sensory design and operating constraints being solved together.

## Key Characteristics
- Develops beverages and flavors for companies that may lack internal food-science capacity.
- Uses essential oils and aroma chemicals as composable building blocks rather than treating a named fruit as one fixed flavor.
- Integrates flavor, functional ingredients, and intended category into one prototype.
- Supports affordable test batches while recognizing that distribution and shelf access remain outside formulation alone.
