# 袁绍刺吕布未遂 / Yuan Shao-Lü Bu Assassination Attempt

袁绍刺吕布未遂 / Yuan Shao-Lü Bu assassination attempt enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-970-yuanshao-ansha-lvbu-weihe-hui-shibai-ln2pnvit-qgpnhn-pne7o-p1irrs|Hanji 970]] as the failed killing attempt after [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and [[LyuBu|吕布]] briefly cooperate against [[ZhangYanLateHan|张燕]] and the [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]]. The source frames the attempt as an alliance-collapse event: shared enemy pressure can create temporary cooperation, but it does not settle Yuan Shao's dislike of Lü Bu's retinue or Lü Bu's fear of remaining under a hostile patron.

The operational mechanism is [[CourtesyCoveredElimination|礼遇包装式清除]]. Yuan Shao uses a formal appointment in the emperor's name and a group of strong escort soldiers as the visible cover for removing Lü Bu. The honor-and-protection surface becomes the access channel for concealed violence, making the episode a late-Han variant of [[AssassinationAccessCredential|刺杀接近凭证]] rather than a battlefield duel.

The attempt fails because Lü Bu reads the escort as dangerous before the strike lands. The source says he keeps zither music sounding in the tent, leaves through a prepared passage, and lets the armed men destroy an empty bed and tent. Yuan Shao's next move is not pursuit but fear: once he learns Lü Bu is alive, he closes the city gates against retaliation. Lü Bu then returns to [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]], which helps explain the later route toward [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] and [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] in Hanji 974-975.
