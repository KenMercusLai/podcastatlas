# The AI Turning Point

The AI Turning Point is the book [[JimSpignardo]] mentions in [[ep-48-from-pilots-to-productivity-what-it-actually-takes-to-make-ai-work-in-the-enterprise]]. In the source, he describes it as a book about transformation and change management rather than a prescriptive technical manual.

The book's role in the wiki is source-scoped. It extends the episode's larger claim that enterprise AI adoption depends on business reflection, organizational readiness, governance, and ownership rather than only tool capability.
