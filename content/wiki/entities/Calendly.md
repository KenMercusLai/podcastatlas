# Calendly

## Overview
Calendly is [[TopeAwotona]]'s software company, historically associated with scheduling and described in the source as repositioning toward meeting assistance.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Calendly as both a mature software company and an advising reference point. Its reported product direction includes Cali, an AI assistant intended to join email conversations, understand preferences, and coordinate meetings. Its earlier organic spread supplies the analogy for making a product visible through use, while its scale illustrates the harder problem of innovating without abandoning existing revenue.

## Key Characteristics
- Reports more than 20 million users worldwide.
- Is shifting its category language from scheduling platform to meeting-assistant platform.
- Uses product-mediated interaction as a source of organic awareness.
- Treats customer support and success capacity as a possible bottleneck when organic growth outpaces operations.
- Remains led by Awotona as both CEO and chief product officer in the episode.
