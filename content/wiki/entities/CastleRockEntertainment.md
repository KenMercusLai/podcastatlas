# Castle Rock Entertainment

## Overview

Castle Rock Entertainment is the independent production company placed by the current source inside [[SeinfeldTVSeries|Seinfeld]]'s production and backend ownership structure.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Castle Rock to show how the pre-consolidation television market could separate production from first-run network distribution. [[NBC]] could earn advertising revenue while an outside producer assumed production risk and retained an interest in later exploitation. Castle Rock was later sold to Turner, and its Seinfeld interest ultimately entered the Warner Bros. library.

This made the company an important beneficiary of syndication even though its original owners sold before all later value was realized. The case illustrates both the upside of retained rights and the difficulty of capturing their full duration.

## Key Characteristics

- Independent producer rather than the original broadcast network.
- Participant in the show's backend and later syndication economics.
- Acquired by Turner before the catalog completed its long monetization cycle.
- Example of rights value persisting through corporate ownership changes.

- Ownership transition: [[seinfeld-ca0df1168651e0a51be7d6012432411f]] says Turner bought Castle Rock and the interest later entered the Warner Bros. library.
