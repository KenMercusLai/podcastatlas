# 《瘟疫传说》 / A Plague Tale

《瘟疫传说》 / A Plague Tale appears briefly in [[yi-fen-laizi-bingchuang-shang-de-xiangguan-shudan-598284093]] as the hosts' game-world association while discussing plague, war, and historical background. The episode says the second game was popular at the time and that the series draws on the Hundred Years' War and plague atmosphere.

Its wiki role is modest: it shows that plague narratives circulate not only through public-health history and novels such as [[PlagueNights|《瘟疫之夜》]], but also through interactive media where disease becomes setting, danger, mood, and historical pressure.
