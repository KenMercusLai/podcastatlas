## Overview
荔枝道 / Litchi Road is a route through the Daba mountain region from the Fuling-Changshou side through Liangping, Wanyuan, Zhenba, and Xixiang toward [[Hanzhong|汉中]] and the Ziwu corridor.

## Current Profile
The road has the thinnest continuous material record of the three routes in the source and is therefore the strongest test of evidence discipline. It may have begun as a local route, briefly became an official Tang road, declined in the Song, and later carried civilian trade in salt, cotton, and other goods during the Ming-Qing period.

Its name concentrates attention on the disputed origin of [[YangGuifei|杨贵妃]]'s lychees, but the episode argues that the cost of opening and maintaining a Daba crossing probably cannot be explained by fruit transport alone. Possible intelligence, military, or deterrence functions in relation to Tang-Nanzhao conflict remain hypotheses rather than settled conclusions.

## Key Characteristics
- Daba mountain corridor linking the Chongqing-Sichuan side toward Hanzhong.
- Shorter official history and fewer continuous visible remains than Jinniu or Baoxie roads.
- Route reconstructed heavily from texts, bridges, place names, oral memory, and terrain.
- Road whose court legend can obscure wider state and military functions.
- Later civilian trade corridor, especially for Sichuan salt and cotton.

## Evidence
- Chronology and route: [[ep285-tafang-gushudao-ganwen-lu-zai-hefang-li20senvn3oelioproznnykdajvt|EP285]] describes a possible local origin, brief Tang official use, Song decline, Ming-Qing revival, and the Fuling-to-Xixiang alignment.
- Trade function: [[ep285-tafang-gushudao-ganwen-lu-zai-hefang-li20senvn3oelioproznnykdajvt|EP285]] uses salt-market place names and a Xixiang restriction inscription to show practical Sichuan-salt movement.
- Material limits: [[ep285-tafang-gushudao-ganwen-lu-zai-hefang-li20senvn3oelioproznnykdajvt|EP285]] says route finding often depends on old bridges, gazetteers, Republican maps, water systems, local market-road memory, and walking.
- Legend scrutiny: [[ep285-tafang-gushudao-ganwen-lu-zai-hefang-li20senvn3oelioproznnykdajvt|EP285]] reports expert reasons to doubt the alleged Wanyuan tribute-fruit inscription.

## Qualifications
The Ba-Shu versus Lingnan origin of Yang Guifei's lychees remains unresolved. Ancient lychee-tree ages, climate-range claims, the suspect inscription, and military-purpose explanations all require corroboration.
