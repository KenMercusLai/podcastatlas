## Overview

灵游坊, also known as S-GAME, is the Chinese game studio founded by [[LiangQiwei|梁其伟]] and associated with [[RainBlood|《雨血》]], the *Phantom Blade* mobile games, and [[PhantomBladeZero|《影之刃零》]].

## Current Profile

The source portrays S-GAME as a creator-led studio that acquired commercial, project-management, financing, and organizational skills through mobile development before returning to a large premium single-player project. Its current strategy combines financially guarded operations, concentrated product ambition, self-publishing, and a culturally rooted “kung-fu punk” identity.

## Key Characteristics

- Originated around an independent creator project and an online contributor network.
- Used mobile-game success to develop commercial and management capability.
- Experienced strategic drift when expansion, hot sectors, IP packaging, and capital goals exceeded its creative fit.
- Re-centered around one large author-led premium game.
- Seeks control of international positioning, cultural expression, and publishing tone.
