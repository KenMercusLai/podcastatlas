# 申阳 / Shen Yang

申阳 / Shen Yang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-143-xiangyu-shi-ruhe-feijin-xinji-nongsi-chuhuaiwang-mixin-lstfzfvbd2h9i39f7y5dxrbxxwv2|Hanji 143]] as one of the lords in [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s post-Qin settlement. The episode says he first takes Sanchuan commandery and welcomes the Chu army, then is made Henan king with his capital at Luoyang.

His page records a narrow but important settlement function: Shen Yang illustrates the episode's principle that Xiang Yu rewards actors who cooperate with the Chu-led westward victory and help make the path into the Qin heartland usable.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-149-xichu-bawang-xiangyu-weihe-shijun-2-lpohbb3prsgvokpjipfd7i1ntiwn|Hanji 149 part 2]] gives the reversal. After Liu Bang reaches Shan County and pacifies the people outside the passes, Shen Yang surrenders. Liu Bang then turns his territory into [[HenanCommanderyChuHan|河南郡]], so Shen Yang shifts from rewarded settlement lord to an example of Liu Bang absorbing Xiang Yu's peripheral titles into commandery administration.
