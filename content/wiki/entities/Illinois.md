# Illinois

Illinois appears in [[tech-20260417-0417-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260417-0417-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the state considering an [[IllinoisAILiabilityBill|AI liability bill]] that would limit large-model developer liability for critical harms under conditions such as no intentional or reckless conduct and publication of a safety protocol. The episode uses Illinois as a state-level AI governance battleground because federal AI legislation appears stalled.

The source also frames Illinois as politically relevant because [[BillCunningham]] says final state law is unlikely to include sweeping liability relief for AI developers, and because [[MariaCurie|Maria Currie]] notes the state has a history of stronger privacy protection and willingness to regulate technology companies.

The wiki had an earlier Illinois data-center-policy mention in [[tech-20251216-1216-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251216-1216-mp-tech-pod-128]], where data centers reportedly tended to meet a 20-job tax-incentive threshold exactly. Together, the mentions make Illinois a state-policy case where technology rules can shape both infrastructure incentives and AI liability.

[[tech-20260109-0109-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260109-0109-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds Illinois to the [[StateAIRegulationPatchwork]] through laws limiting employer use of AI in hiring decisions. This connects Illinois' AI governance role beyond the later liability-shield bill into employment and worker-screening regulation.
