# Alberto Núñez Feijóo

## Overview
Alberto Núñez Feijóo is presented as the centre-right People's Party leader favored by source-dated polling to become Spain's next prime minister.

## Current Profile
The episode characterizes Feijóo as an experienced, honest public administrator rather than a charismatic campaigner. His apparent path to office depends not only on the People's Party's strength but also on whether it can govern alone or must accommodate the populist-right Vox party.

## Key Characteristics
- Leader of Spain's centre-right People's Party.
- Source-dated frontrunner to replace [[PedroSanchez]] as prime minister.
- Presented as administratively experienced and personally honest.
- Less associated with charisma than with conventional governing competence.
- Faces a coalition-choice problem involving the populist right.

## Evidence
- Electoral position: [[left-in-the-lurch-spains-general-election-f9a9608c40ea7371c98aaab648d8d8b0]] says year-long polling placed the combined right ahead of the left and presents Feijóo as the likely next prime minister if those polls hold.
- Governing profile: [[left-in-the-lurch-spains-general-election-f9a9608c40ea7371c98aaab648d8d8b0]] describes him as an experienced and honest administrator rather than a charismatic politician.
- Coalition constraint: [[left-in-the-lurch-spains-general-election-f9a9608c40ea7371c98aaab648d8d8b0]] identifies Vox's possible participation and demands as a central post-election question.

## Qualifications
The profile comes from one pre-election journalistic source. Polling, personality judgments, coalition arithmetic, and the forecast that Feijóo will become prime minister are time-bound rather than settled outcomes.
