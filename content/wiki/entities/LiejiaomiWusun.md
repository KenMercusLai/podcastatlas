## Overview

猎骄靡 / Liejiaomi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-370-2-yeye-ba-jiaoqi-jiagei-qinsunzi-nainai-yiye-bian-xifuer-lopw_h3yzk6qtcpksqdm9l2egrvp|Hanji 370-2]] as the elderly [[Wusun|乌孙]] ruler who accepts both a Han princess, [[LiuXijunPrincessWesternHan|刘细君]], and a [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] princess while balancing between the two powers. The episode's “列郊弥” is normalized here to the received name 猎骄靡.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Liejiaomi as a ruler whose household structure is foreign policy. He makes Xijun right wife and the Xiongnu princess left wife, preserving relations with both sides while placing the Xiongnu bride in the higher-ranked position given by the episode. His great age, separate residence, and limited communication with Xijun make the marriage diplomatically legible but personally thin.

Liejiaomi's succession arrangement carries the alliance into the next generation. Knowing he is old, he asks Xijun to marry his grandson and designated successor [[JunxumiWusun|军须靡]]. The request is coercive for Xijun but, in the episode's political reading, preserves succession continuity and Wusun's room to maneuver between Han and Xiongnu.

## Key Characteristics

- Elderly Wusun ruler positioned between Han and Xiongnu pressure.
- Uses simultaneous marriages to receive ties from both rival powers.
- Gives the Xiongnu princess the left-wife rank and Xijun the right-wife rank in the episode's account.
- Maintains limited day-to-day contact with Xijun despite the marriage's diplomatic visibility.
- Designates grandson Junxumi as successor and asks Xijun to marry him.
- Treats household succession as a mechanism for alliance continuity.
