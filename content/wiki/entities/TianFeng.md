# 田峰 / Tian Feng

田峰 / Tian Feng appears in [[baiwan-ge-jingguan-shehui-tingshuo-ni-ye-xiang-dang-zhubo-lgd0yapclwvsessg7xnhaarpyiwy]] as a [[ChineseAcademyOfSocialSciences|中国社科院]] researcher leading or explaining fieldwork on [[TalentGroupLivestreaming|才艺团播]]. His role is to translate backstage observation into a labor and organization account: livestreaming becomes visible as guild training, real-time operation, aesthetic testing, platform compliance, and post-session review rather than only a person facing a camera.

His useful methodological move is restraint. The episode says the team did not begin by forcing a single theory onto livestreaming; it first recorded rooms, roles, SOPs, practices, and accidents so later analysis could stay grounded in [[FieldworkAsKnowledgeMethod|field evidence]].

## Key Claims
- Contemporary livestreaming has moved from low-cost individual broadcasting toward guild-backed professionalization.
- Real-time data changes performance because operational decisions can be made during the livestream.
- Beauty in livestreaming is socially produced by makeup, camera, platform feedback, audience response, and organizational review.
- Loneliness can be understood as feeling time, and livestreaming can become a way to consume or forget time inside digital space.
