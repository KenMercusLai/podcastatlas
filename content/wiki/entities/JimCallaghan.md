# Jim Callaghan

Jim Callaghan appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] as the British home secretary handling the Northern Ireland crisis for [[HaroldWilson]]'s government. The episode says he pressured [[TerenceONeill|Terence O'Neill]] after the Derry march violence and later approved sending troops while returning from seeing Wilson in the Isles of Scilly.

Callaghan also visited the [[Bogside]] after the [[BritishArmy]] arrived and was cheered by Catholics. In the episode's interpretation, that reception did not solve the deeper problem: the army could separate communities, but Westminster still lacked a political strategy for [[TheTroubles]].
