# 长社之战 / Changshe Battle (late Han)

长社之战 / Changshe Battle enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-925-tuoxie-bushi-rensong-xuehui-tuoxie-lu-geng-changliang-limafqskgblrwetestyh39w-hzm4|Hanji 925]] as the 184 CE battle in [[YingchuanCommandery|颍川郡]] where [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]] defeats [[BoCaiLateHan|波才]]'s [[YellowTurbansLateHan|Yellow Turban]] force. After [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]] first loses to Bo Cai, Huangfu Song shelters in Changshe with a weaker visible force and keeps Zhu Jun positioned nearby.

The episode frames the battle as active retreat. Huangfu Song uses a weak posture to draw confidence, waits while repeated harassment and poor night discipline wear down Bo Cai's camp, then exploits wind and vegetation for a fire attack. Zhu Jun joins the strike and [[CaoCao|曹操]] arrives as a cavalry commander for the pursuit, producing a large Han victory.
