# Selby Jennings

## Overview
Selby Jennings is the financial-services recruitment firm whose workplace-AI survey is cited in [[tech-20261016-1016-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261016-1016-mp-tech-pod-128]].

## Current Profile
The source uses Selby Jennings as evidence that financial employers are moving from encouraging AI experimentation toward formal skill and return measurement. Its survey reportedly found that 42% of respondents had introduced criteria for evaluating employee AI proficiency and 30% had dedicated teams tracking AI-investment results.

## Key Characteristics
- Financial-services recruitment firm and survey source in the episode.
- Connects AI proficiency with career evaluation rather than tool exposure alone.
- Tracks organizational attempts to measure the return from AI investment.

## Evidence
- Skill-evaluation role: [[tech-20261016-1016-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261016-1016-mp-tech-pod-128]] attributes the formal-criteria finding to a Selby Jennings survey.
- Investment-measurement role: [[tech-20261016-1016-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261016-1016-mp-tech-pod-128]] attributes the dedicated tracking-team finding to the same survey.

## Qualifications
The episode does not provide the survey instrument, sample size, geography, respondent mix, dates, question wording, or industry breakdown. The percentages therefore remain source-scoped and should not be generalized to all employers.

## What Changed
- Created the profile as the survey source for formal workplace AI-skill and investment-return measurement.
