# 荀子 / Xunzi

荀子 / Xunzi enters this wiki branch through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-70-xihuan-huayang-zuosi-de-qiguo-junzhu-luuyasp5-nhnikdcozzaueosb-md]], where [[SimaGuang|司马光]] is said to quote the Wangba chapter after the death of [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]]. The episode does not yet develop Xunzi's biography; it uses him as the philosophical bridge from a ruler-collapse story into explicit "资治" reflection.

The source also previews the next episode's topic as Xunzi's alternative place inside Confucian thought and his claim about "人性本恶." For now, this page should remain source-scoped: Xunzi is attached to the interpretation of rulerly choice, political order, and [[ConfucianIdealGovernance|儒家理想治理]], not yet to a full account of his thought.
