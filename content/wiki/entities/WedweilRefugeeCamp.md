# Wedweil Refugee Camp / 维德维尔难民营

## Overview
Wedweil Refugee Camp / 维德维尔难民营 is the [[SouthSudan|南苏丹]] camp near [[Aweil|阿维尔]] where [[WangHongrui|王洪瑞]] observes hunger, registration delays, WFP aid cuts, nutrition screening, and school meals in [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]].

## Current Profile
The camp appears as a field site where humanitarian systems meet immediate need. The episode separates the registration and distribution area, where new arrivals wait under trees, from the wider residential area of improvised homes made from branches, plastic, grass, and thin earth walls.

Wedweil is also the source's main evidence that aid cuts change household behavior. Refugees depend heavily on WFP cash, some pawn aid cards to obtain food, and nutrition foods are available only to specific high-risk groups or severely malnourished children.

## Key Characteristics
- Camp near Aweil receiving refugees from Sudan and East Darfur.
- Registration site where families may wait before becoming eligible for WFP support.
- Residential site of improvised but cared-for homes under severe material constraint.
- Food-insecurity site where cash aid, nutrition thresholds, and aid-card pawning shape survival.
- Adjacent school-meal case where WFP support is linked to durable attendance.
