# Tina Barton

## Overview
Tina Barton is a Republican election official presented by the source as a practitioner of election-error correction and threat preparedness.

## Current Profile
Barton's case shows how a routine administrative correction can become a security and legitimacy crisis when stripped of context. The episode says she corrected a Rochester Hills, Michigan tally error within 24 hours in 2020, after which Trump allies misrepresented it as thousands of ballots being reassigned and she received a death threat. She later joined Ready for Tuesday, which uses tabletop exercises to help officials prepare for intimidation and disruption.

## Key Characteristics
- Corrected a human tally error promptly rather than concealing it.
- Experienced personal threat after the correction was reframed as fraud.
- Turned that experience toward practical preparedness for other election officials.
