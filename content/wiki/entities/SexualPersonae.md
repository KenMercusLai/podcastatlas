# 《性面具》 / Sexual Personae

## Overview
[[CamillePaglia|Camille Paglia / 卡米尔·帕利亚]]'s *Sexual Personae* / 《性面具》 is the principal text interpreted by [[367-shehui-gaige-neng-dailai-xingbie-hexie-ma-lmxou4dgsnvkdwplhgubtkmv7pl]] in its inquiry into whether social reform can eliminate gender conflict.

## Current Profile
In the episode's reading, the book rejects an original harmony disrupted only by society. Nature is blind and excessive; civilization builds forms and limits around sex, aggression, reproduction, dependence, and death. The source uses the book to interpret patriarchal institutions partly as defensive structures and maternal embodiment as a challenge to modern ideals of autonomous, bounded individuality.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a critique of social-environmental explanations that treat institutions as the sufficient source of conflict.
- Places sexuality and bodily reproduction inside a larger conflict between nature and cultural form.
- Interprets male cultural creation partly as compensation for bodily separation from maternal reproductive power.
- Reads recurring female and maternal symbols as records of attraction, dependence, reverence, and fear.
- Distinguishes causal or symbolic explanation from moral justification.
- Operates as cultural criticism and psychoanalytic interpretation in the current source, not as conclusive empirical proof.

## Evidence
- Social-environment critique - [[367-shehui-gaige-neng-dailai-xingbie-hexie-ma-lmxou4dgsnvkdwplhgubtkmv7pl]] uses the book to challenge the claim that reforming institutions can remove every source of gender tension.
- Nature-culture structure - [[367-shehui-gaige-neng-dailai-xingbie-hexie-ma-lmxou4dgsnvkdwplhgubtkmv7pl]] presents law, custom, religion, marriage, and morality as forms imposed on unruly natural processes.
- Maternal symbolism - [[367-shehui-gaige-neng-dailai-xingbie-hexie-ma-lmxou4dgsnvkdwplhgubtkmv7pl]] links pregnancy, birth, maternal dependence, and images of dangerous femininity to the book's account.
- Political boundary - [[367-shehui-gaige-neng-dailai-xingbie-hexie-ma-lmxou4dgsnvkdwplhgubtkmv7pl]] stresses that rights reform remains valid even if politics cannot remove every embodied or psychological tension.
