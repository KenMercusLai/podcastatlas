# Maria Leopoldina

## Overview

Maria Leopoldina appears in the episode as [[DomPedroI]]'s wife and the [[HabsburgDynasty|Habsburg]] link behind part of [[Brazil]]'s green-and-yellow national symbolism.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Maria Leopoldina in two ways. Symbolically, her Habsburg background helps explain the yellow in Brazil's later flag colors alongside Pedro's Braganza green. Politically and biographically, rumors about Pedro's conduct around her death contributed to the damage to his reputation before his abdication.

## Key Characteristics

- Wife of [[DomPedroI]].
- Habsburg dynastic figure tied to the episode's explanation of Brazilian color symbolism.
- Part of the green-and-yellow origin story behind the [[BrazilianFlag]].
- Her death and rumors about Pedro's conduct are presented as damaging to Pedro I's reputation.

## Evidence

- Color symbolism: [[681-brazil-the-emperors-anthem-part-5-glt5596929385]] says Brazil's green and yellow came from Braganza and Habsburg associations through Pedro and Maria Leopoldina.
- Reputation crisis: [[681-brazil-the-emperors-anthem-part-5-glt5596929385]] says Pedro's reputation suffered after Maria Leopoldina's death and rumors about his behavior toward her.
