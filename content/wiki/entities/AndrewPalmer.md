# Andrew Palmer

Andrew Palmer appears in [[settler-bet-a-west-bank-flashpoint-6a842bb539e73ea8f3c13a2d]] as the voice introducing a satirical [[BartlebyColumn|Bartleby]] workplace advice segment. The segment turns thinly disguised public controversies into management problems about meetings, unclear bosses, career transitions, flattery, impatience, legality, and time to think.

His contribution to the wiki is a lighter workplace branch after the episode's West Bank and India segments. It extends [[PublicFigureWorkplaceAdviceSatire]], while touching existing practical concepts such as [[UpwardManagement]] and [[WorkplacePacing]] without making the disguised allusions into settled claims on every public figure page.
