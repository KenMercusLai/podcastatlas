## Overview

聂翁壹 / Nie Wengyi is presented as a wealthy [[Mayi|马邑]] resident whose apparent local access makes the [[MayiAmbushWesternHan|马邑之谋]] believable. His role is to pose as an aggrieved defector, promise the city and its wealth to [[JunchanChanyu|军臣单于]], and supply visible evidence that officials inside Mayi have already been killed.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-260-mayi-zhimou-hanwudi-fanji-xiongnu-diyizhan-luji7sarjbc2y4l8ljsnilld8xjb|Hanji 260]] identifies Nie as a Mayi rich man who brings the plan to [[WangHuiWesternHan|王恢]]. He will claim a grievance against local officials and offer to kill them and surrender the city if Xiongnu cavalry arrives to support him.

The ruse combines incentive with verification. Nie reportedly promises livestock, property, and women, then has condemned prisoners killed and their heads displayed on the city wall as proof of his story. The episode says the chanyu believes him and mobilizes toward Mayi. Because the account ends before the ambush, Nie's later fate and the durability of his cover are not established here.

## Key Characteristics

- Wealthy local actor whose status and access are used to make false defection plausible.
- Originator or carrier of the operational proposal to Wang Hui in the episode.
- Deception intermediary who translates a state ambush into a credible local betrayal story.
- Provider of costly visible evidence through displayed prisoner heads.
- Source-bounded figure whose later fate and exact historical identity are not developed.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on one podcast episode. Nie's wealth, initiative, access to officials, exact arrangement with the state, use of condemned prisoners, promises, dialogue, and later fate remain episode-attributed. The transcript uses “聂翁一”; the page normalizes the name to 聂翁壹 and does not merge him with similarly named figures without further evidence.
