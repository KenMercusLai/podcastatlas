# 代成君 / Dai Chengjun

代成君 / Dai Chengjun appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-04-yurang-wei-zhiyao-fuchou-ln34tydha6iqwlrgmw7iugqfgfik]] as [[ZhaoBolu|赵伯鲁]]'s son. After [[ZhaoXiangzi|赵襄子]] conquers [[DaiState|代国]], he sends Dai Chengjun to Dai as part of his effort to honor or restore Zhao Bolu's displaced line.

The source says Dai Chengjun dies early, after which Zhao Xiangzi designates Dai Chengjun's son [[ZhaoXianzi|赵献子]] as successor. This makes Dai Chengjun a relay figure in the Zhao succession branch rather than an independently developed biography.

## Connections
- [[ZhaoBolu|赵伯鲁]] - father.
- [[ZhaoXiangzi|赵襄子]] - uncle and patron of the succession line.

- [[ZhaoXianzi|赵献子]] - son and later restored successor.
