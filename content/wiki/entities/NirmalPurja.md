# Nirmal Purja

Nirmal Purja is the Nepali mountaineer remembered in [[afghanistan-five-years-on-our-correspondent-visits-6a7ee547049c692a6b3ee0a9]]. [[AnneRowe]]'s segment says he was born within sight of Dhaulagiri in [[Nepal]], discovered his altitude-climbing ability around age 30, and made the fourteen peaks above 8,000 metres his life focus.

His central wiki role is [[ProjectPossible]], the attempt to climb all fourteen 8,000-metre peaks as quickly as possible. The source says he completed the set in six months and six days, a radical compression of the previous record, while drawing criticism for bottled oxygen, fixed ropes and helicopter movement between base camps.

The obituary keeps the achievement morally complicated through [[HighAltitudeClimbingEthics]]. Purja presented bottled oxygen as part of a rescue ethos and said his military background made leaving people behind unacceptable. The source also says he died on July 30 while attempting Broad Peak, close to completing a third round of the fourteen highest mountains.
