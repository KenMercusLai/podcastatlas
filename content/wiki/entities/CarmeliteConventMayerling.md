# Carmelite Convent at Mayerling

## Overview

The Carmelite Convent at Mayerling is the religious replacement for Rudolf's hunting lodge after the [[MayerlingIncident]].

## Current Profile

The episode treats the convent as ritual conversion of scandalous space. [[FranzJosephI]] orders the lodge destroyed and replaced by a Carmelite convent where prayers are said for [[CrownPrinceRudolf]], turning the death site into a controlled Catholic memorial rather than a preserved scandal scene.

## Key Characteristics

- The convent replaces the hunting lodge after Rudolf and [[MaryVetsera]] die there.
- It converts a dynastic scandal site into a prayer site.
- Its creation belongs to the court's broader symbolic repair strategy.

## Evidence

- Replacement order: [[690-a-murderous-affair-death-at-mayerling-part-2-glt7203783068]] says Franz Joseph orders the hunting lodge destroyed.
- Memorial function: [[690-a-murderous-affair-death-at-mayerling-part-2-glt7203783068]] says the replacement is a Carmelite convent where prayers are said for Rudolf.
- Suppression logic: [[690-a-murderous-affair-death-at-mayerling-part-2-glt7203783068]] places the convent after burial, public shock, and conspiracy discussion, making it part of the aftermath management.
