## Overview

[[ChenYiChangleWeiwei|陈祎]] is the Changle commandant in Hanji 1167 who enters [[WeiFengLateHan|魏讽]]'s planned Ye conspiracy but reports it to [[CaoPi|曹丕]] before action begins.

## Current Profile

The episode makes Chen Yi the hinge between planned and failed action. His command position is useful to Wei Feng's attempt at internal coordination, but he later reassesses the personal and family risk and discloses the plot. The observable reversal matters more than the episode's reconstructed night-time thoughts: a coalition built around promised success fails when a participant's survival calculation changes.

## Key Characteristics

- Changle commandant recruited for access useful to an internal plot.
- Initially willing participant whose commitment does not survive renewed risk assessment.
- Informant whose disclosure gives Cao Pi time to arrest the conspirators before action.
- Example of coalition fragility under severe personal and family exposure.
