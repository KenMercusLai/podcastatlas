# 皮皮鲁 / Pipilu

皮皮鲁 / Pipilu is one of [[ZhengYuanjie|郑渊洁]]'s best-known child characters and part of the childhood-reading memory around [[TonghuaDawang|《童话大王》]]. In [[23-feima-pai-qiche-anquan-shi-di-yi-wei-de-572699943]], he appears mainly through [[XunTuJi|《训兔记》]], where his resistance to rabbit-like obedience helps the episode frame Zheng's work as anti-training and anti-compliance.

This page is source-scoped. The wiki treats Pipilu as a character signal for [[AntiAuthoritarianEducation]] and [[ChildrensLiteratureComplexity]], not as a complete catalog of Zheng's Pipilu stories.
