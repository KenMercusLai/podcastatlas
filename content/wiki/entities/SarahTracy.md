# Sarah Tracy

## Overview

Sarah Tracy is the historian whose archival research recovers conscientious objectors as initiators and collaborators in the [[MinnesotaStarvationExperiment]], not only its experimental subjects.

## Current Profile

Tracy found correspondence from Keys's earlier vitamin studies showing that conscientious objectors resisted research with military applications and proposed a peacetime experiment their churches could support. According to the episode, they supplied both the humanitarian direction and grant funding for work aimed at postwar hunger relief.

This finding materially revises the experiment's origin story. It does not remove Keys's authority or the study's harms, but it strengthens evidence that volunteers helped define the project before consent and remained active through recruitment and publication.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses correspondence to reconstruct the origin of wartime human-subjects research.
- Identifies conscientious objectors' resistance to military applications.
- Recovers volunteer initiative in study design, purpose, and funding.
- Complicates a sole-originator narrative centered on [[AncelKeys]].
- Supplies historical evidence central to the episode's participant-agency argument.
