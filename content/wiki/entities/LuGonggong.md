# 鲁共公 / Duke Gong of Lu

鲁共公 / Duke Gong of Lu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl|Zhouji 09]] as Ji Fen, the son and successor of [[LuMugong|鲁穆公]]. The source gives him as one of the compact 377 BCE succession notices.

His source role is chronological. The episode does not develop a separate Lu political story here; it uses Lu's succession beside [[HanState|韩国]] and the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] Zhou-annals sequence.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-15-jiu-bu-chun-yinfa-de-shijie-dazhan-li-iojxthq8komhlhyumho3yvtem]] closes Lu Gong Gong's reign in the 355 BCE notice: he dies and [[LuKanggong|鲁康公]] succeeds. The same episode also uses a [[Zhuangzi|《庄子》]]-derived story in which Lu Gong Gong arrives late before [[ChuXuanwang|楚宣王]], presents poor wine, refuses to apologize on the grounds of Lu's Zhou-descended status, and thereby helps trigger the [[LuWineHandanWarCascade|鲁酒薄而邯郸围]] chain.
