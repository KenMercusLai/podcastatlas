# 鲁共公 / Duke Gong of Lu

鲁共公 / Duke Gong of Lu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl|Zhouji 09]] as Ji Fen, the son and successor of [[LuMugong|鲁穆公]]. The source gives him as one of the compact 377 BCE succession notices.

His source role is chronological. The episode does not develop a separate Lu political story here; it uses Lu's succession beside [[HanState|韩国]] and the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] Zhou-annals sequence.
