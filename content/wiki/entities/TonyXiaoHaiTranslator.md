# Tony (translator of Xiaohai)

## Overview
Tony is the translator identified by first name in the source who discovered Xiaohai's 《深圳南流记》, recommended it to Granta, and translated the work that became the English memoir [[YiKuaiGunShiBook|A Drift in the South]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Tony as both translator and interpretive advocate. He noticed literary texture beyond the category of worker writing, checked unfamiliar factory details directly with [[XiaoHaiPoet|Xiaohai]], and participated in a bilingual editorial process whose English and Chinese editions diverged in chapter structure and retained material. His critical position is that foreign readers should see the author's full range and their own relation to global production, not consume the book only as distant Chinese suffering or social-science evidence.

## Key Characteristics
- Identified literary and emotional qualities in 《深圳南流记》 beyond its worker-literature category.
- Connected the essay to Granta and the later book-development process.
- Treated uncertain labor vocabulary as a fact-checking problem requiring direct consultation with the author.
- Defended an artist-development reading against hardship-only and sociological reduction.
- Used English-edition structure to make consumer life and production-chain connection more visible.
