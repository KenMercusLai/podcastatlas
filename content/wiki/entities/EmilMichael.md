# Emil Michael

## Overview
Emil Michael is presented by [[AllIn|All-In]] as the U.S. undersecretary responsible for defense research and engineering and as a former [[Uber]] executive. In the supplied episode he explains the administration's approach to Iran, military AI, drones, model procurement, supply-chain resilience, and defense-industry modernization.

## Current Profile
Michael's profile in this wiki is a source-scoped defense-technology operator. He advocates shorter and more explicit military objectives, large inventories of low-cost autonomous systems, layered missile defense, multiple AI suppliers, and procurement terms allowing all lawful government use. He treats vendor policy and technical control as operational dependencies when a model is embedded in classified or time-sensitive workflows.

His industrial-policy branch joins that software position to physical capacity. He describes strategic lending, domestic production of rocket motors, batteries, fiberglass, and critical minerals, quicker contracts for newer companies, and continuing disruption even if firms such as [[Anduril]] and [[Palantir]] become new primes.

## Key Characteristics
- Defense official presented as responsible for research, engineering, and technology modernization.
- Advocate of AI-enabled drones, heterogeneous autonomy, layered interception, and mission-specific risk thresholds.
- Proponent of an all-lawful-use standard for defense AI vendors rather than pre-negotiated scenario exceptions.
- Treats vendor control of deployed models as a continuity and supply-chain concern.
- Supports multi-provider AI procurement, startup access, commercial-style contracts, and domestic defense production.
- Presents [[DARPA]] and strategic public lending as tools for long-horizon research and supply-chain rebuilding.

## Qualifications
This profile rests on one sympathetic interview and does not independently verify Michael's formal title or portfolio, operational claims, casualty percentages, contract interpretation, designation process, production figures, or descriptions of classified systems. Anthropic does not appear, and the source's use of “Department of War” is retained as episode language while the wiki links the institution to [[USDepartmentOfDefense]].
