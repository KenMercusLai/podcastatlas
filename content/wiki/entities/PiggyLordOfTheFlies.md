# Piggy / 猪崽子（《蝇王》）

## Overview
Piggy / 猪崽子 is Ralph's rational ally in [[LordOfTheFlies|《蝇王》]] as discussed in [[vol-351-yingwang-21shiji-de-huangdao-gushi-haineng-zenme-kai-naodong-1983-nuojiang-dezhu-gkwriueodap1a3taaattkgol]].

## Current Profile
The episode reads Piggy as the vulnerable carrier of reason, science, knowledge, and procedural speech. His glasses make fire possible, so his body and tools become materially central to rescue, power, and violence. His death marks the destruction of rational order after Simon's death has already destroyed the possibility that truth will be heard.

## Key Characteristics
- He is physically vulnerable but intellectually central.
- He supports Ralph's rule-oriented leadership and meeting procedure.
- His glasses tie knowledge to the technical power of fire.
- His death signals the collapse of rational and procedural order.
- He is a target of mockery, showing that civilized factions also contain cruelty.
