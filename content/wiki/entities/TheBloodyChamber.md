# 《染血之室》 / The Bloody Chamber

《染血之室》 / The Bloody Chamber appears in [[38-anjila-kate-jingguai-gushiji-o-mirende-hanfu-609607139]] as [[AngelaCarter|Angela Carter / 安吉拉·卡特]]'s own fairy-tale rewriting practice beside [[JingguaiGushiji|《精怪故事集》]]. The episode specifically highlights Carter's Bluebeard revision: rescue is shifted away from brothers or male saviors and toward the heroine's mother.

The page's role in the wiki is to anchor [[FeministFairyTaleRewriting]] as a change in action rights, not merely a change in label. The source treats Carter's version as part of a larger effort to restore women, mothers, desire, danger, and violence to story structures that later versions often made safer or more patriarchally tidy.

## Key Claims
- The source uses the Bluebeard rewrite to show that feminist fairy-tale work can alter who is allowed to act at the decisive moment.
- Carter's gothic mode keeps sex, danger, and family force visible rather than cleaning them out of the tale.
- The episode places the book beside [[JingguaiGushiji|《精怪故事集》]] to show continuity between Carter the editor/collector and Carter the writer.
