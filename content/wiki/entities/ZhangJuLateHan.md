# 张举 / Zhang Ju (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-938-lishi-de-jingxing-ruanruo-zhihui-dailai-quru-lnlnrketgfbadhfjbzdjjtrmqfsh|Hanji 938]] makes Zhang Ju the explicit title claimant in the Youzhou rebellion's origin layer. Allied with [[ZhangChunLateHan|张纯]] and [[QiuLijuLateHan|丘力居]], he declares himself emperor and demands that [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] abdicate, turning frontier disorder into a direct [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] challenge.

[[ZhangJuLateHan|张举]] appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-942-yue-youxiu-de-ren-huode-yue-lei-heli-ma-lkwlrcgnt-kxxc2qqz6z6wkg7e5z|Hanji 942]] as a rebel associated with [[ZhangChunLateHan|张纯]] and the late-Han [[YouzhouLateHan|幽州]] disorder. The episode treats him less as an independent narrative center than as a signal of how the province's crisis combines internal rebellion with frontier alignments.

[[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] offers rewards for the heads of Zhang Ju and Zhang Chun while also sending envoys to frontier groups. That mixed incentive structure helps produce surrender and dispersal without requiring a broad military escalation, making Zhang Ju part of the episode's case for [[PeopleBasedPoliticalSecurity|people-based political security]].
