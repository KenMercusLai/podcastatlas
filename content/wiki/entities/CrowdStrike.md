# CrowdStrike

CrowdStrike appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-the-future-of-everything-what-ceos-of-circle-crowdstrike-more-see-coming-in-2026-39870920]] through CEO [[GeorgeKurtz|George Kurtz]]'s discussion of AI-era cybersecurity. The source describes adversaries as nation-states, e-crime groups, and hacktivists, then argues that AI compresses attack timelines and lets less sophisticated attackers operate with more sophistication.

The episode turns CrowdStrike into a case for [[AIDetectionAndResponse|AI detection and response]]. Kurtz discusses [[PromptOnlyAutonomousMalware|prompt-only autonomous malware]], fake remote employees, identity-token theft, help-desk weakness, browser-layer exposure, the [[SeraphicSecurity|Seraphic]] acquisition, and the problem of employees eventually controlling many AI agents.
