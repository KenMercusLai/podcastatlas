# 马晓 / Ma Xiao (Peking University)

## Overview
马晓 / Ma Xiao is the Peking University School of Government speaker in the Shengdong Jixi aviation roundtable.

## Current Profile
Ma Xiao supplies the episode's infrastructure and city-policy lens. He moves from personal flight experience into aviation as a complex system of airports, oil depots, towers, airspace, route approvals, hub geography, cargo value, and local-government route strategy.

His role is strongest where the episode shifts from passenger anecdotes to city competition. He explains why Atlanta, Memphis, Dubai, Hong Kong, Suzhou, and Chinese second-tier international routes should be read through network position, regulation, and demand aggregation rather than through simple city size.

## Key Characteristics
- Academic guest representing an infrastructure, government, and city-network perspective.
- Explains aviation through fixed infrastructure, regulatory approval, cargo value, and route economics.
- Uses international and Chinese city cases to connect aviation to urban rank and global connectivity.
