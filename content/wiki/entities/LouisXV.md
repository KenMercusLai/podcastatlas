# Louis XV

## Overview

Louis XV was the French king at [[MarieAntoinette|Marie Antoinette]]'s arrival whose court, mistresses, and death framed her transition from dauphine to queen.

## Current Profile

The source presents Louis XV's late court as a world of royal libertinism, faction, and internal gossip. Marie Antoinette's refusal to acknowledge Madame du Barry became an early etiquette conflict, while the [[PetitTrianon|Petit Trianon]]'s prior association with royal mistresses later complicated the younger queen's use of it as a private retreat. Louis XV's death on 10 May 1774 brought [[LouisXVI|Louis XVI]] and Marie Antoinette to the throne while both were young.

## Key Characteristics

- Bourbon king ruling France when Marie Antoinette arrived in 1770.
- Maintained a court publicly associated with royal mistresses and libertinism.
- His household supplied factions and gossip that shaped the dauphine's early political exposure.
- His death in 1774 transferred the crown to Louis XVI.
