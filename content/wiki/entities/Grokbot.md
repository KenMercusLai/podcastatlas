# Grokbot

## Overview
Grokbot is represented in the wiki as [[Grok]]'s personalized-bot surface, now extended from consumer custom-bot creation toward persistent cloud-agent execution.

## Current Profile
[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-anthropics-2t-ipo-zucks-ai-manifesto-nvidias-500b-ai-bet-groks-comeback-42442555]] first places Grokbot in the personalized-AI branch. [[GavinBaker|Gavin Baker]] says it feels important because it makes custom AI bots easier and more democratic, while [[JasonCalacanis|Jason Calacanis]] connects it to [[MarkZuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]]'s manifesto idea that everyone should be able to create bots. The hosts compare it with tools from [[Claude]] and [[Perplexity]], but the page's emphasis is product surface rather than benchmark leadership.

The newest Grokbot evidence shifts the product from a bot-builder example toward [[PersistentCloudAgents]] and [[MultiAgentCollaboration]]. In [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-nvidias-historic-quarter-saas-comeback-bessent-vs-druck-americas-debt-crisis-cancer-vaccine-42597345]], [[DavidSacks|David Sacks]] says he liked that Grokbot-style agents could keep running in the cloud after his computer was off, and Jason imagines a multiplayer room where multiple agents and humans can work together.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a user-facing personalized-bot product in the [[XAI|xAI]] ecosystem.
- Is framed as more democratic and approachable than some custom-agent workflows.
- Connects consumer bot creation to the broader [[PersonalSuperintelligence]] and [[DecentralizedAIControl]] branch.
- Gains a new role as an example of cloud agents that can persist after the user's local machine is off.
- Raises product questions around memory, task state, permissioning, cost control, and multi-agent coordination.

## Qualifications
The sources discuss host impressions and product direction rather than a technical audit. They do not establish Grokbot's reliability, permission model, safety controls, or performance against other agent products. The persistent-agent claims should remain product-surface observations until later sources test the implementation.
