# Grokbot

Grokbot is the [[Grok]] personalized-bot product discussed in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-anthropics-2t-ipo-zucks-ai-manifesto-nvidias-500b-ai-bet-groks-comeback-42442555]]. [[GavinBaker|Gavin Baker]] says it feels important because it makes custom AI bots easier and more democratic, while [[JasonCalacanis|Jason Calacanis]] connects it to [[MarkZuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]]'s manifesto idea that everyone should be able to create bots.

In the source, Grokbot matters less as a benchmark claim and more as a product-surface claim. The hosts compare it with tools from [[Claude]] and [[Perplexity]], but argue that Grokbot feels simpler as a consumer entry point. That places it inside [[PersonalSuperintelligence]] and [[DecentralizedAIControl]] rather than only inside the [[XAI|xAI]] model race.
