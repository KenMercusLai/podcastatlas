# 德宏 / Dehong

## Overview
Dehong / 德宏 is the Yunnan coffee-producing region highlighted in [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]].

## Current Profile
The source treats Dehong as an important but still partly under-discovered Yunnan coffee region. It is compared with [[PuerYunnan|普洱]] as a larger commercial-coffee base and [[BaoshanYunnan|保山]] as an earlier specialty-coffee site, while Dehong's own value is tied to [[Mangshi|芒市]], local research resources, seedling guidance, small lots, and cultural context.

## Key Characteristics
- Important Yunnan coffee origin in the source's regional map.
- Benefits from local tropical-crop research and seedling or planting guidance resources.
- Contains potentially under-recognized plots, varieties, and small batches.
- Carries cultural and city-life context through Mangshi, not just agricultural output.

## Evidence
- Regional position: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] says Yu Wenjie regards Dehong as definitely an important Yunnan coffee area.
- Comparative map: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] contrasts Dehong with Pu'er as the large commercial-coffee base and Baoshan as an early specialty-coffee region.
- Research resources: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] notes Dehong's tropical-crop institute resources in seedling and planting guidance.
- Small-lot potential: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] mentions a small traditional natural lot in Yingjiang with yellow tropical fruit notes, while keeping availability uncertain.

## Qualifications
The region's production scale, official institutions, and lot availability are not independently validated in this wiki entry.
