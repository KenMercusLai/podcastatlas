# Gerlinde Rohr

## Overview

Gerlinde Rohr is a Leipzig sports historian whose research challenges the enlarged friendship story surrounding [[JesseOwens]] and [[LutzLong]].

## Current Profile

[[hitler-s-olympics-part-6-the-jiggle-the-giddy-up-hitler-s-olympics-part-6-the-jiggle-the-giddy-up|Hitler’s Olympics, Part 6]] presents Rohr and Volker Kluge comparing the familiar narrative with official Olympic documentation, Long's contemporary article, family records, and event logistics. Long's article recounts Owens's qualifying attempts without mentioning advice; family materials place Long away from the Olympic Village; and the reported letters could not be located or reconciled with Long's family chronology and wartime location.

Rohr's resulting judgment is narrow rather than totalizing: Owens and Long probably met only through the long-jump competition. That conclusion preserves visible cordiality while rejecting the unsupported series of private conversations and years of correspondence.

## Key Characteristics

- Leipzig-based sports historian focused here on Berlin 1936 evidence.
- Tests later memory against contemporary documents and family records.
- Uses chronology and location to evaluate private-meeting and letter claims.
- Distinguishes documented sportsmanship from an enlarged friendship narrative.

- Location and chronology: [[hitler-s-olympics-part-6-the-jiggle-the-giddy-up-hitler-s-olympics-part-6-the-jiggle-the-giddy-up]] connects family materials, lodging, marriage, parenthood, military service, and death to contradictions in Owens's versions.

## Qualifications

This profile comes from one structured episode summary rather than Rohr's publications, complete archive, or interview transcript. Missing letters and silence in a contemporary article weigh against the later story but cannot prove that no undocumented private exchange ever occurred.
