## Overview

杜季良 / Du Jiliang enters the wiki through Hanji 725 as the admired but dangerous counterexample in [[MaYuanEasternHan|Ma Yuan]]'s warning letter. The episode presents him as a famous youxia-style figure with wide friendships and generosity, but Ma Yuan warns that imitating such a person badly can produce frivolous posturing rather than real virtue.

## Current Profile

Hanji 725 does not portray Ma Yuan as hating Du Jiliang personally. The letter's point is more precise: Du Jiliang may have admirable traits, but he is a risky model for young men who already like public discussion, social display, and youxia association. Ma Yuan's warning is that failed imitation of Du Jiliang can become "drawing a tiger and ending up like a dog."

The political consequence is sharper than the family lesson. After the private letter circulates, Du Jiliang's enemy uses it to complain to Liu Xiu, accusing Du of frivolous conduct and social disorder. Liang Song and Dou Gu are also drawn in because they associate with him. The episode says Du is removed from office, while Liang and Dou are rebuked.

## Key Characteristics

- Youxia-style Eastern Han figure used as Ma Yuan's negative imitation example.
- Admired for generosity and wide friendships, but treated as a dangerous model for inexperienced young relatives.
- Target of hostile court accusation after Ma Yuan's private letter circulates.
- Political node whose friendships expose Liang Song and Dou Gu to imperial rebuke.
