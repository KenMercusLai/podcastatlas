# 傅饶 / Fu Rao

## Overview
傅饶 is an ophthalmology-side doctor in the [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] Physicians' Day roundtable, represented through emergency eye cases and an earlier endocrinology-rotation crisis intervention.

## Current Profile
The source presents Fu Rao through two limits of clinical work. In ophthalmic emergency care, she sees injuries and retinal disease embedded in very different lives; her most detailed case is a 16-year-old synchronized-swimming athlete whose month-long visual loss was found to be a long-standing retinal detachment involving the macula, narrowing the expected recovery despite surgery. During an endocrinology rotation, she also physically stopped a medically and socially overwhelmed patient who ran toward a rooftop after saying she did not want treatment or life.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses emergency ophthalmology cases to connect injury mechanism, delayed presentation, anatomy, and prognosis.
- Distinguishes fresh retinal detachment from a longer-standing detachment involving the macula.
- Recognizes that medical burden, employment rejection, and weak family support can converge into acute psychological crisis.
- Is represented by direct crisis intervention as well as specialty explanation.
