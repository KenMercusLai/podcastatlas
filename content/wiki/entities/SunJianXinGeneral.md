## Overview

孙建 / Sun Jian (Xin general) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-640-qipa-wangmang-ruhe-chedi-bifan-xiongnu-lhxbx6keibmfe8z6bcsmlpnr6eht|Hanji 640]] as the Xin立国将军 who turns frontier defection and a false Liu claimant into a proposal for anti-Han symbolic purge. This page is disambiguated from [[SunJian|Sun Jian]], the modern computer-vision researcher, and [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]], the late-Han warlord.

## Current Profile

Hanji 640 places Sun Jian inside [[WangMang|王莽]]'s effort to sever [[XinDynasty|新朝]] from remaining [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] memory. After [[ChenLiangXinDefector|陈良]] and [[ZhongDaiXinDefector|终带]] flee to Xiongnu, and after a man falsely claims to be the Liu royal figure Liu Ziyu, Sun Jian argues that the issue is not only border betrayal.

His proposal escalates from punishment of traitors to symbolic cleansing. He recommends abolishing Han royal temples in the capital and temporarily removing Liu-surname officials from office until Xin can reappoint them. Wang Mang accepts the proposal with exceptions for meritorious Liu-lineage figures and allows some to be granted the Wang surname.

## Key Characteristics

- Xin general whose current profile is grounded in one memorial episode.
- Treats frontier defection and false Han restoration claims as evidence that Han dynastic memory remains politically dangerous.
- Proposes destroying Han temple infrastructure in the capital.
- Proposes suspending Liu-surname officials as a category before selective reappointment.
- Helps convert a border-security crisis into [[DynasticMemorySeverance|dynastic memory severance]].
