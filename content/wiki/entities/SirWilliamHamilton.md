# Sir William Hamilton

## Overview

Sir William Hamilton was the British ambassador to Naples whose marriage to Emma Hamilton and tolerance of her affair with Horatio Nelson anchor the source's portrait of romantic devotion.

## Current Profile

[[357-historical-love-island-the-sequel-glt7719682575]] moves from Hamilton's sober, scholarly reputation and first marriage to Catherine Hamilton into his relationship with Emma Hart, whom he married and introduced into Neapolitan society as Lady Hamilton. Emma's later affair with [[HoratioNelson]] exposed Hamilton to gossip and mockery, yet the episode interprets his continued attachment as loyalty stronger than pride or conventional jealousy.

## Key Characteristics

- British diplomat associated with the court of Naples.
- Initially presented as scholarly, restrained, and devoted to his first wife.
- Husband and social sponsor of Emma Hamilton.
- Participant in a publicly scandalous emotional triangle with Emma and Nelson.
- Cast as “the devoted one” because attachment outweighed reputation and humiliation.
