# Sir William Hamilton

## Overview

Sir William Hamilton was the British envoy to Naples whose diplomatic household joined court access, antiquarian celebrity, devotion to Emma Hamilton, and an increasingly paternal attachment to Horatio Nelson.

## Current Profile

[[357-historical-love-island-the-sequel-glt7719682575]] moves from Hamilton's sober, scholarly reputation and first marriage to Catherine Hamilton into his relationship with Emma Hart, whom he married and introduced into Neapolitan society as Lady Hamilton. [[608-nelson-slaughter-in-naples-part-1-glt8594694484]] adds his operational diplomatic setting: as Britain's long-serving envoy, he and Emma received [[HoratioNelson|Horatio Nelson]] after the Nile and formed a household in which Hamilton's admiration resembled a father-mentor bond. He joined the royal flight to Palermo, feared death during the storm crossing, and remained inside Nelson and Emma's deepening intimacy. Emma's later affair with Nelson exposed him to gossip and mockery, yet the comic source interprets his continued attachment as loyalty stronger than pride or conventional jealousy.

## Key Characteristics

- British diplomat associated with the court of Naples.
- Initially presented as scholarly, restrained, and devoted to his first wife.
- Husband and social sponsor of Emma Hamilton.
- Diplomatic host and older mentor figure for Nelson after the Battle of the Nile.
- Participant in the royal court's evacuation from Naples to Palermo.
- Participant in a publicly scandalous emotional triangle with Emma and Nelson.
- Cast as “the devoted one” because attachment outweighed reputation and humiliation.
