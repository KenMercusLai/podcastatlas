# Eric (Feishu source)

Eric is a speaker in [[270-da-chang-yazhu-ai-bangong-feishu-he-dingding-que-xian-chengle-peijue-lmb4dgcgov3mr4cn7cikbghpfro4]] identified by the source as someone who worked in [[Feishu]] commercialization and product teams. His contribution is the inside-operating view of how Feishu, Volcano Engine, [[Doubao]], and enterprise AI sales overlap.

Eric argues that Feishu and Volcano Engine can face the same customers and budgets, so separated sales teams may duplicate effort. He also frames [[DoubaoEnterpriseEdition|Doubao enterprise edition]] as AI sold through a Feishu-like work surface, and he emphasizes Feishu's documents, meetings, org structure, permissions, and enterprise data as the basis for [[AIOfficeAgent|AI office agents]].
