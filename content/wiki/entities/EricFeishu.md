# Eric (Feishu source)

## Overview

Eric is a [[Luanfanshu|乱翻书]] speaker identified by the bounded sources as someone with [[Feishu]] commercialization and product background. His wiki role is to provide an inside-operating interpretation of Feishu, Volcano Engine, [[Doubao]], [[DoubaoWork|Doubao Work]], and enterprise AI-office packaging.

## Current Profile

Across the two Feishu/Doubao office episodes, Eric explains the business and product seams that appear when ByteDance tries to combine model capability, collaboration software, cloud sales, and enterprise buyers. In episode 270, he emphasizes overlap between Feishu and [[Volcengine]] customer budgets, and frames [[DoubaoEnterpriseEdition|Doubao enterprise edition]] as AI sold through a Feishu-like work surface.

Episode 272 extends that view into Doubao Work's current product shape. Eric treats Doubao Work as a consolidation of Feishu AI partner, Doubao, Trae, Coze, and Flow-era work, and he uses model-choice differences between Feishu and Doubao Work entrances to explain how token-cost responsibility may shape packaging. His comments make the source useful for reading product integration, not for independently proving internal ByteDance organization.

## Key Characteristics

- Feishu commercialization/product-background speaker in the Luanfanshu AI-office branch.
- Explains Feishu, Volcano Engine, Doubao, and enterprise AI sales overlap.
- Interprets Doubao Work as organizational and product consolidation across ByteDance AI lines.
- Connects model choice, personal pricing, enterprise packaging, and token-cost responsibility.
- Frames Feishu context as a practical advantage for work agents.
