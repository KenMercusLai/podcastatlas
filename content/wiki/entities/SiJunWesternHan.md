## Overview

驷钧 / Si Jun is presented as [[LiuXiangQiKingWesternHan|刘襄]]'s maternal uncle and a senior organizer of Qi's anti-Lü mobilization.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-205-nvhuangdi-lvzhi-jingran-yin-gou-er-si-2-ltgwfdhn9cdwphudy0blyh36fge1|Hanji 205 part 2]] places Si Jun in Liu Xiang's planning circle alongside [[WeiBoWesternHan|魏勃]] and [[ZhuWuWesternHan|祝午]]. After [[ZhaoPingQiChancellor|赵平]] is neutralized, Liu Xiang appoints Si Jun as chancellor, making kinship support part of the kingdom's reorganized command structure.

## Key Characteristics

- Maternal uncle and political ally of Liu Xiang.
- Participant in Qi's anti-Lü planning circle.
- Successor to Zhao Ping as chancellor in the episode's account.
