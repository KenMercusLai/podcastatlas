# 田叔 / Tian Shu (early Han)

田叔 / Tian Shu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-180-xihan-diyi-yinghan-guangao-de-gushi-1-li5u-dmsmb1lp4ny3rk02kaiyu4k|Hanji 180]] as a [[ZhangAo|张敖]] retainer who is not named in the arrest order after [[GuanGao|贯高]]'s assassination plot is exposed.

The episode uses him as a retainer-loyalty signal. Tian Shu, [[MengShuHan|孟叔]], and other followers shave their heads, wear chains and prison clothing, and follow Zhang Ao to Chang'an as if they were household slaves. This makes loyalty visible even before Guan Gao's courtroom resistance proves Zhang Ao's innocence.

This page uses the `TianShuHan` key to distinguish the early-Han Zhao retainer from any later Tian Shu name collisions.
