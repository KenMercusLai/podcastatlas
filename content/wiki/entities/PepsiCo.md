# PepsiCo

## Overview

PepsiCo appears in the bounded source as the diversified beverage-and-snack company losing the future U.S. [[KFC]] fountain contract to [[CocaCola|Coca-Cola]] after a restaurant relationship dating to 1986.

## Current Profile

The episode interprets the supplier change through portfolio focus rather than price alone. Pepsi reportedly submitted an attractive proposal, but Coca-Cola paired its beverage specialization with a future-sales-growth commitment. PepsiCo's profitable snack business had historically offset beverage share pressure; inflation, pricing, GLP-1 use, and a shift toward protein-oriented foods are now presented as pressures on that cushion. The evidence does not disclose either proposal or establish that portfolio breadth caused the contract loss.

## Key Characteristics

- Diversified consumer-goods company spanning beverages and snack foods.
- Former corporate owner of KFC and long-running U.S. restaurant beverage supplier.
- Participant in KFC's reported competitive supplier review with an attractive but unsuccessful proposal.
- Portfolio case in which snack profitability can buffer beverage weakness while also competing for investment and management attention.
