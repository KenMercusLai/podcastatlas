# WIC

WIC is the U.S. Women, Infants, and Children nutrition program discussed in [[bobbie-laura-modi-how-a-baby-formula-startup-took-market-share-from-two-industry-giants-4488842b-74d3-40ed-8776-5831c5934e69]]. The episode says WIC can restrict participating parents in a state to the formula brand that wins the relevant contract, making government procurement a major distribution and access constraint in [[InfantFormula]].

[[LauraModi]] says [[Bobbie]] is not yet large enough to serve WIC, even though the source describes WIC as covering a large share of formula-using U.S. babies. In the wiki, WIC therefore anchors [[WICFormulaContracting]]: product trust and customer pull do not automatically create access when public benefits, rebates, supply capacity, and state contracts shape the market.
