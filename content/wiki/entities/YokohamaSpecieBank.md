# Yokohama Specie Bank / 横滨正金银行

Yokohama Specie Bank is the semi-official Japanese bank at the center of [[168-yin-yu-xin-yibu-riben-laogong-yimin-de-kuayang-huikuan-shi-lmgfmmgkawoa-6xyucnm1ljebzwu]]'s remittance history. The episode says the bank opened a Honolulu branch in 1892, initially inside the Japanese consulate, and became central once migrant savings and cross-ocean remittances outgrew ministry-level handling.

The bank matters because it turns [[JapaneseLaborMigrationRemittances]] into infrastructure. It connects overseas wages, foreign exchange, consular administration, family support, and Meiji state-building. The source frames the bank as private in form but deeply tied to state modernization, overseas expansion, and the [[StateMediatedRemittanceSystem]].

## Key Claims
- The bank helped handle forced savings, remittances, and foreign-exchange settlement tied to Japanese overseas labor.
- Its Honolulu branch being housed in the consulate at first signals the blurred line between banking service and state governance.
- The bank made migrant remittances more legible to the Japanese state than more decentralized private courier systems did.
