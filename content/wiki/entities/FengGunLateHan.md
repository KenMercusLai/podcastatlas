## Overview
Feng Gun was a late-Han legal official whose current wiki profile centers on a punishment reversal during Emperor Huan's eunuch-dominated court. As tingwei, he becomes vulnerable after a eunuch relative dies in custody and palace accusation turns legal handling into supposed wrongdoing.

## Current Profile
Feng Gun's role is structurally similar to Li Ying and Liu You in the 165 CE cluster. He acts through formal legal office, but the death of Shan Qian in prison allows eunuch-aligned accusers to recode the matter as an offense against a protected network.

The later pardon context is important because it shows Feng Gun was not simply a disgraced outlier. He belongs to the same group of punished officials whom Ying Feng later tries to recover, making him part of a broader conflict between bureaucratic enforcement and eunuch patronage.

## Key Characteristics
- Legal official whose institutional role is exposed when a protected prisoner dies in custody.
- Target of palace accusation rather than beneficiary of a neutral review process.
- Companion case to Li Ying and Liu You in a shared left-workshop punishment episode.
- Later eligible for recovery through Ying Feng's remonstrance, showing the punishment was politically contestable.

## Qualifications
The wiki currently has only a narrow case profile for Feng Gun. It should not treat him as a fully synthesized late-Han reform faction figure until additional sources fill out his earlier offices, alliances, and later career.
