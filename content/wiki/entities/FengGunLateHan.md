## Overview
Feng Gun was a late-Han official whose current wiki profile now has two Huan-era vulnerability layers. In 163 CE, renewed disorder after his return lets eunuch enemies turn military relapse into dismissal; in 165 CE, his legal office becomes vulnerable when a eunuch-relative custody case is recoded against him.

## Current Profile
The 163 CE layer in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-858-weishenme-quan-ni-yiding-yao-huanwei-sikao-lobr-xznqo-ctjpc-ytpstubmahl|Hanji 858]] shows Feng Gun's earlier court-political exposure. The source says eunuchs already hated him; after he returned from campaign, local disorder broke out again, giving his enemies material to accuse him and have him dismissed.

Feng Gun's later role is structurally similar to Li Ying and Liu You in the 165 CE cluster. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-866-zhenzheng-huomingbai-de-ren-dongde-sanbuzhan-lhhmmyjfo6mkf-qs6bhhzu8hhln6|Hanji 866]] places him as tingwei. He acts through formal legal office, but the death of Shan Qian in prison allows eunuch-aligned accusers to recode the matter as an offense against a protected network.

The later pardon context is important because it shows Feng Gun was not simply a disgraced outlier. He belongs to the same group of punished officials whom Ying Feng later tries to recover, making him part of a broader conflict between bureaucratic enforcement and eunuch patronage.

## Key Characteristics
- Huan-era official already hated by eunuchs before renewed disorder gives them a dismissal opportunity.
- Legal official whose later institutional role is exposed when a protected prisoner dies in custody.
- Target of palace accusation rather than beneficiary of a neutral review process.
- Companion case to Li Ying and Liu You in a shared left-workshop punishment and pardon context.

## Qualifications
The wiki currently has only a narrow case profile for Feng Gun. Hanji 858's transcript form 冯滚 is normalized to [[FengGunLateHan|冯绲]] because the Huan-era eunuch-hostility role matches the established route, but the source gives limited detail about the earlier campaign and renewed rebellion. The page should not treat him as a fully synthesized late-Han reform faction figure until additional sources fill out his offices, alliances, and later career.
