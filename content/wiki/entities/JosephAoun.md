# Joseph Aoun

Joseph Aoun appears in [[hard-cedar-lebanon-seeks-trumps-help-6a5f41e97bddc0bea5542de5]] as [[Lebanon]]'s president and former army chief. The episode presents his White House visit as an attempt to turn renewed international attention into practical support after Lebanon's long leadership vacuum, war damage, and financial collapse.

The source frames Aoun's agenda around three asks from [[DonaldTrump]]: support for the Lebanese army, pressure on [[Israel]] to withdraw under the ceasefire arrangement, and American investment in ports, telecommunications, and other sectors. In the episode's logic, Aoun's political test is whether the state can become more useful to citizens than [[Hezbollah]] while also managing reconstruction and banking reform.
