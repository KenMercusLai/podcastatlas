# 阳晋 / Yangjin (Warring States)

阳晋 / Yangjin appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-60-zhongguo-di-yi-wei-bachi-chaozheng-de-taihou-jingshi-ta-ltazis9qo0dwvwmzxikk8mmpvosc]] as one of three Wei-side places [[QinState|秦国]] captures in 303 BCE, along with [[PubanWarringStates|蒲阪]] and [[FenglingWarringStates|封陵]]. The episode treats the group as a strategic set of crossings linking Hexi and Hedong.

The source does not give Yangjin an independent story. Its function is geographical: it makes Qin's anti-Han/Wei turn under [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]] concrete by showing which river-crossing and route assets Qin pressures.
