## Overview

郭公 / Guo Gong enters the wiki as the Eastern Han jurist used by Hanji 757 to connect office fit, family learning, and legal leniency. The episode presents him as a Yingchuan legal specialist who becomes tingwei under [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Emperor Zhang]] and identifies forty-one heavy-penalty clauses that can be handled more lightly.

## Current Profile

Hanji 757 makes Guo Gong a career-fit example rather than only a legal-office notice. His father Guo Hong had worked in law, Guo Gong learned Xiaodu law from childhood, and the family line continued in legal expertise across later descendants. The source uses that continuity to argue that inherited skill, temperament, and institutional role can reinforce one another.

His substantive legal action is also bounded. As tingwei, Guo Gong is described as cautious and lenient in judging cases. He reviews heavy-punishment provisions, extracts forty-one clauses suitable for lighter treatment, and submits them to Zhangdi, who accepts and implements them.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han legal specialist whose role fits a family tradition of judicial learning.
- Tingwei appointment under Zhangdi, making him part of the central judiciary rather than a local-only figure.
- Lenient case reviewer who looks for lawful room to reduce heavy punishment.
- Source example for career positioning through capacity, temperament, and inherited resources.
- Distinct from later [[GuoGongLateHan|郭贡 / Guo Gong]], whose wiki route belongs to a different late-Han field.
