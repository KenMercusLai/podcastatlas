# Banza

## Overview
Banza is a U.S. food brand co-founded by [[BrianRudolph]] and [[ScottRudolph]], initially built around chickpea pasta positioned through protein, fiber, gluten-free suitability, and a distinctive orange package.

## Current Profile
Banza's early advantage came from pairing an unmet nutritional and dietary use case with a memorable category name, visible packaging, media exposure, and retail access. Its history also shows two limits to consumer-brand momentum: a promising home recipe can fail during industrial transfer, and a trusted food brand can lose demand immediately when a viral safety allegation outruns its evidence response. By the interview, the company had expanded into multiple foods but Brian again identified chickpea pasta as its core.

## Key Characteristics
- Category-reframing brand that presents chickpea pasta as broadly useful rather than only a restrictive gluten-free substitute.
- Retail-led CPG company whose demand appeared before its first high-volume manufacturing process was reliable.
- Visually distinctive brand built around orange packaging and protein-and-fiber communication.
- Creator- and media-aware marketer that used television, social outreach, retail displays, and national accounts to compound awareness.
- Consumer-trust business that later made traceability, pesticide testing, certification, and public results part of its operating response.

### Trust response

## Qualifications
- Store count, market rank, growth, sales decline, and test comparisons are episode-attributed rather than independently verified here.
- The source does not resolve the competing glyphosate methods or establish the health significance of either result.
- Early customers' tolerance of product flaws does not show that differentiation can indefinitely substitute for food quality.

## What Changed
- Initial profile establishes Banza as a category-reframing chickpea-pasta company shaped by both manufacturing-transfer failure and later evidence-response pressure.
- Adds published testing and supply-chain traceability as part of the brand's post-crisis trust system.
- Records renewed emphasis on chickpea pasta after broader product expansion.
