# Chen Anni / 陈安妮

## Overview
Chen Anni / 陈安妮 is the founder and CEO of [[KuaikanManhua]] and the guest in [[ta-beipian-guo-beijiu-guo-beipeng-shang-shentan-guo-beitengtong-jidao-guo-jueding-zai-ai-shidai-jixu-maoxian-duitan-chen-anni-lomvn15fklk80bhkreied1iotuof]]. The source presents her as a comics creator turned platform founder who is trying to move Kuaikan from a comic platform into the AI-native content product [[LIVO]].

## Current Profile
The current profile is source-scoped to one interview. Chen's public role in the wiki is not a full biography; it is a founder-operator profile linking content taste, creator-platform responsibility, financing crises, commercial discipline, and AI-era product experimentation. She treats entrepreneurship as a way to make a missing product and to help more creators be seen, while also acknowledging that idealism without a sustainable business can endanger the very products and people it intends to protect.

## Key Characteristics
- Creator-to-platform founder: Chen begins from early comics and self-media success, then builds [[KuaikanManhua]] to support other comics creators rather than only her own work.
- Product taste under metric pressure: she describes cutting algorithmically successful but lower-quality content despite short-term data declines because it conflicted with Kuaikan's desired IP direction.
- Crisis-formed commercial discipline: the 2015 and 2021 cash-flow crises make survival, user willingness to pay, and reasonable profit part of her founder judgment.
- AI-native product ambition: she frames [[LIVO]] as a new storytelling carrier built around living worlds, agentic characters, and user-influenced fate.
- Humanist creative standard: she argues that AI does not replace the creator's will to move people; technology matters only when it supports that purpose.
