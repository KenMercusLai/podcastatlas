# Rob Rhinehart

Rob Rhinehart appears in [[tsr-s5-johncoogan-audiofile-1-tsr-s5-johncoogan-audiofile-1]] as the founder whose public Soylent experiment made [[Soylent]] legible to the internet. The episode says his team had originally been building a wireless mesh networking company, struggled after Demo Day, and then helped turn food-cost frustration into a meal-replacement product.

The source makes Rob central to [[ControversialLaunchVirality]]. His 30-day Soylent-only experiment and blog post reached [[HackerNews]], functioned like a public stress test, and pushed the team to abandon other small projects once pre-orders and media attention arrived.
