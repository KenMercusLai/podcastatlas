# Marcus Claudius Marcellus / 马库斯·克劳狄乌斯·马塞勒斯

## Overview

Marcus Claudius Marcellus / 马库斯·克劳狄乌斯·马塞勒斯 is the Roman commander who leads the siege and eventual capture of [[Syracuse]] during the [[SecondPunicWar]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Marcellus as an aggressive, experienced commander whose force includes survivors of the [[BattleOfCannae]]. In spring 213 BC he attacks Syracuse by land and sea, expecting Roman strength and coordination to overcome the city.

[[Archimedes]]' prepared machines stop the opening assaults and force a change from direct attack to patient siege. Roman troops later gain entry during a festival, but taking the entire city requires months more fighting. Marcellus therefore succeeds through persistence and exploitation of opportunity rather than by defeating the machines in a single contest.

His reported desire to preserve Archimedes contrasts with the scholar's death and the wider sack. The episode uses that contrast to separate a commander's stated intention from the violence that conquest releases and that his army carries out.

## Key Characteristics

- Commands coordinated Roman land and sea operations against Syracuse.
- Begins with aggressive assault but adapts after prepared defenses inflict losses.
- Leads veterans whose Cannae experience connects Roman catastrophe to continued mobilization.
- Exploits surprise to enter the city and persistence to complete the conquest.
- Is credited with wanting Archimedes spared but remains commander of the sack in which he dies.

## Evidence

- Command and force: [[640-romes-greatest-enemy-carthage-at-the-gates-part-1-glt9223169972]] identifies Marcellus as the siege commander and includes Cannae veterans in his army.
- Failed assault and adaptation: [[640-romes-greatest-enemy-carthage-at-the-gates-part-1-glt9223169972]] describes land-sea attacks checked by Archimedes' machines.
- Capture: [[640-romes-greatest-enemy-carthage-at-the-gates-part-1-glt9223169972]] connects festival entry to a further eight months of resistance before the whole city falls.
- Responsibility and intent: [[640-romes-greatest-enemy-carthage-at-the-gates-part-1-glt9223169972]] reports Marcellus's wish to save Archimedes alongside the killing and sack.

## Qualifications

This is a one-episode profile, not a full career. The date and detailed sequence, troop motivations, festival opportunity, eight-month interval, orders concerning Archimedes, and sack scenes remain mediated by ancient narrative. A wish to spare one famous individual does not remove command responsibility for conquest and plunder.

## What Changed

- Created a bounded profile of Marcellus's Syracuse command.
- Distinguished tactical adaptation and persistence from the later moralized Archimedes story.

## Relationships

- [[Syracuse]] - city he besieges and captures for Rome.
- [[Archimedes]] - defender he reportedly orders spared but cannot protect during the sack.
- [[BattleOfCannae]] - disaster survived by veterans serving in his later force.
- [[SecondPunicWar]] - wider conflict in which the Sicilian victory strengthens Roman control.
- [[RomanRepublic]] - state whose Sicilian strategy he executes.
