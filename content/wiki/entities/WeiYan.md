## Overview

魏延 / Wei Yan enters the current wiki as the commander [[LiuBei|刘备]] selects to hold [[Hanzhong|汉中]] after declaring himself King of Hanzhong in 219 CE.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1165-zhangji-zhuan-didiao-caishi-dingji-shengcun-zhihui-lr7x5qltfi_ikktqabowt1garphl|Hanji 1165]] presents Wei Yan's appointment as a conversion of campaign victory into durable frontier command. The episode says he had followed Liu Bei in war, earned merit, and was elevated to protect Hanchuan and serve as Hanzhong administrator.

His reported pledge distinguishes between a major invasion led by Cao Cao and a smaller attack by a subordinate, promising to block the former and destroy the latter. The statement supports Liu Bei's confidence in him within the episode, but does not independently establish later performance or exact institutional powers.

## Key Characteristics

- Liu Bei-side commander elevated after the Hanzhong victory.
- Entrusted with the strategically important Hanzhong defense rather than a ceremonial rank alone.
- Presented as expressing confidence calibrated to different scales of enemy attack.
