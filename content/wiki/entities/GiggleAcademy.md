## Overview
Giggle Academy is [[ChangpengZhao|CZ]]'s post-Binance education project, described as a free app for people who lack access to schooling or literacy.

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents Giggle Academy as a mission-driven alternative to CZ's exchange work. Its intended product combines gaming, psychology, and AI to deliver education without charging learners. CZ says he resists issuing a project token because speculation could redirect attention from education, making the project a test of whether crypto-derived wealth can fund broad educational distribution without importing token incentives into the learning product.

## Key Characteristics
- It targets learners without reliable school or literacy access.
- It is intended to remain free to users.
- Its proposed learning experience combines games, psychology, and AI.
- CZ explicitly treats token speculation as a possible threat to the education mission.
