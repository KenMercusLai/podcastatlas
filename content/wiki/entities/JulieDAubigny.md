# Julie d'Aubigny / Mademoiselle de Maupin / 莫庞小姐

Julie d'Aubigny, also presented by the episode as Mademoiselle de Maupin or 莫庞小姐, appears in [[78-xifang-juedou-shi-chujian-ba-weile-rongyu-687731000]] as the standout female duelist. The hosts describe her as an opera performer whose response to insult, public constraint, and male challengers makes women visible as direct actors inside [[HonorDuelCulture|honor duel culture]].

The source treats her story as extreme and partly anecdotal, but conceptually useful. She is not only a protected lady whose honor men defend; she becomes a person who claims honor, weapon competence, public recognition, and exit from insult for herself.
