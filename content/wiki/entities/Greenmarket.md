# Greenmarket

Greenmarket is the farmers-market network discussed in [[a-firm-andy-what-are-new-british-pms-plans-6a5deeca76f8ee25bb4bbf9c]]. The episode marks its 50th anniversary and describes it as the largest open-air farmers-market network in the [[UnitedStates]], with markets across [[NewYorkCity|New York City]]'s five boroughs depending on the season.

The source traces Greenmarket from a 12-vendor experiment in a fenced lot on 59th Street to an institution managed by [[GrowNYC]]. Its importance is practical and cultural: it supports regional farms, changes public space around [[UnionSquare]], enables welfare-benefit use across vendors, and links farmers directly with chefs and shoppers.
