# American Pride Bank

## Overview
American Pride Bank is the Macon, Georgia-based small lender used in the Marketplace Tech source as an example of [[CommunityBankAIAdoption]] outside the largest banking institutions.

## Current Profile
The source presents American Pride Bank through CEO [[DominicMiarten]]'s internal-use example. The bank used AI software to prepare executive scorecards covering loans made, interest income, and deposits by branch and region, and is also rolling out AI for annual performance reviews, budgeting, and market research.

## Key Characteristics
- Small-lender context makes AI efficiency valuable because fewer staff may have to cover reporting, planning, and market-research work.
- Its reported AI use is operational and managerial rather than final credit decisioning.
- The bank's example shows that smaller institutions may adopt AI in back-office and executive workflows before using it directly with customers.

## Evidence
- Operational reporting: [[tech-20260902-0902-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260902-0902-mp-tech-pod-128]] says Dominic Miarten used AI to prepare weekly executive scorecards in minutes.
- Broader rollout: [[tech-20260902-0902-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260902-0902-mp-tech-pod-128]] says the bank has been rolling out AI for performance reviews, budgeting, and market research.
