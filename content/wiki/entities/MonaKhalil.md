# Mona Khalil

Mona Khalil appears in [[the-elon-game-musks-vision-of-the-future-6a633594d19896314260e5c4]] as a Lebanese conservationist whose life became tied to turtle nesting on Mansouri Beach in southern [[Lebanon]]. After returning in 1999, she saw a green sea turtle nesting near the family beach house and treated that encounter as a call to protect the site.

Khalil and [[HabibaFayed]] restored the family house into the [[OrangeHouseLebanon|Orange House]], combining a B&B with training, education, volunteer work, and practical turtle protection. The episode credits her with protecting hatchlings, challenging dynamite and poison fishing, pressing local mayors to make the beach a reserve, and continuing the work despite the 2006 war, later checkpoints, and renewed Israeli attacks.
