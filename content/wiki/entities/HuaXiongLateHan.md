# 华雄 / Hua Xiong (late Han)

华雄 / Hua Xiong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-955-guanyu-zhan-huaxiong-qishi-shi-wei-lishi-lndkrj-fnepye8iu-ifgyh-vzysf|Hanji 955]] as the [[DongZhuo|董卓]]-side officer killed after [[LyuBu|吕布]] disrupts [[HuZhenLateHan|胡轸]]'s attack on [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]. The source places his death in the [[YangrenBattleLateHan|阳人之战]] sequence and credits Sun Jian's side, not [[GuanYu|关羽]], with the killing.

The page is source-scoped because the episode is interested less in Hua Xiong's biography than in the memory correction. [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]] makes "关羽温酒斩华雄" famous, but this source uses [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]]-style historical reading to separate literary reception from the episode's account of Sun Jian's actual campaign.
