## Overview

唐衡 / Tang Heng is a Huan-era eunuch marquis whose wiki profile now begins before the founding [[FiveMarquisesHuanEra|Five Marquises]] reward. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-829-junzi-weishenme-doubuguo-xiaoren-lthvnstkfu8n9thx1sz6yezl4yqq|Hanji 829]] makes him an early speech-channel actor who helps turn [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] and [[DuQiaoLateHan|杜乔]]'s accession stance into [[EmperorHuanOfHan|Emperor Huan]]'s resentment. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-847-nvren-biande-haoming-cong-dongde-zhe-liang-zi-kaishi-lrlov43jtz0vq-o-5m8r2cqo0etx|Hanji 847]] then makes him Huan's first private anti-[[LiangJiLateHan|Liang Ji]] confidant and recruiter of other discontented palace insiders; Hanji 848 gives the founding marquis reward, while Hanji 853 and Hanji 904 extend his network through brothers, appointments, and elite marriage.

## Current Profile

Hanji 829 supplies the earliest speech-access layer. Tang Heng and [[ZuoGuanLateHan|Zuo Guan]] slander Du Qiao and Li Gu to Huan by saying that both men opposed Huan's accession and considered him unfit. The source makes Tang a named example of eunuch proximity converting an old succession dispute into the ruler's personal resentment.

Hanji 847 supplies the pre-coup role. After the Xuan crisis reaches Emperor Huan, Huan privately asks Tang Heng which close attendants are unhappy with the Liang family. Tang identifies [[ShanChaoLateHan|Shan Chao]], Zuo Guan, [[XuHuangLateHan|Xu Huang]], and [[JuYuanLateHan|Ju Yuan]], then joins them in the oath-bound conspiracy. His function is therefore access intelligence: he translates Huan's need for action into a usable palace coalition.

Hanji 848 supplies the founding reward layer. Tang Heng is named as one of the five eunuchs rewarded after Huan destroys Liang Ji, receives a 10,000-household county marquisate, and is promoted to zhongchangshi with Zuo Guan.

Hanji 853 places Tang Heng in the later post-Liang Five Marquises field. The source names [[TangXuanLateHan|唐玹]] as Tang Heng's brother and says Tang Xuan, serving as Jingzhao official, destroys [[ZhaoQiLateHan|赵岐]]'s family through hostile accusation after old enmity. Tang Heng's significance in that branch is family power: the brother's office violence is intelligible because it sits near eunuch-marquis status.

Hanji 904 extends the same network logic into court office and elite marriage. The episode identifies [[TangZhenLateHan|唐珍]] as Tang Heng's younger brother and uses that relationship to explain Tang Zhen's sikong appointment. It also records that Tang Heng's daughter married [[XunYu|荀彧]], keeping Tang Heng as a late-Han network node across eunuch proximity, high office, and gentry marriage.

## Key Characteristics

- Huan-era eunuch who first appears as a slander-channel actor against Li Gu and Du Qiao.
- Later becomes Huan's first private anti-Liang confidant.
- Recruiter who identifies the other palace insiders available for the anti-Liang oath.
- Marquis named in the founding post-Liang reward field.
- Zhongchangshi promotion recipient after the anti-Liang coup.
- Family-power node whose brothers connect him to Zhao Qi's household destruction and Tang Zhen's later sikong appointment.
- Marriage-network node through Tang Heng's daughter and Xun Yu.

## Qualifications

This page preserves Tang Heng's source-note layers without reconstructing his complete career. Hanji 829 supplies an earlier slander-channel role but not a full account of Tang's office at that date. Hanji 847 supplies the pre-coup recruiter role; Hanji 848 supplies the founding reward and zhongchangshi promotion; Hanji 853's Tang Xuan branch is an early family-network and private-retaliation case; Hanji 904's Tang Zhen and Xun Yu branch is a later office and marriage case. The source spellings 唐恒 and 唐玄 are normalized to Tang Heng / 唐衡 and Tang Xuan / 唐玹 where the surrounding late-Han sequence supports the route.

## What Changed

- Hanji 829 adds Tang Heng's earliest current role as a slander-channel actor against Li Gu and Du Qiao.
- Hanji 847 adds Tang Heng's first-confidant role, identification of other anti-Liang palace insiders, and oath participation.
- Hanji 848 remains Tang Heng's founding Five Marquises reward and zhongchangshi promotion.
- The profile now includes the 160 CE family-retaliation layer around Tang Xuan and Zhao Qi.
- Tang Heng's network role now spans marquis reward, brotherly office violence, high-office appointment, and marriage linkage.
