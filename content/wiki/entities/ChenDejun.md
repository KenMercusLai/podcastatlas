# 陈德军

## Overview
陈德军 is a leading early operator and later head of [[STOExpress|申通]], connected by family and school ties to the [[TongluExpressCluster|桐庐帮]].

## Current Profile
The source describes Chen moving from unsuccessful manual trades and business debt into the early 盛彤 network, building Shanghai operations, and helping shift the company's center there. After [[NieTengfei|聂腾飞]] died, Chen and his sister 陈小英 continued the business under the 申通 name.

## Key Characteristics
- Early Shanghai network builder.
- Family successor after Nie Tengfei's death.
- School-network link to later 圆通 and 中通 founders.
