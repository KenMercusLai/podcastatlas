# 王婆 / Wang Po

王婆 is the tea-shop neighbor and intermediary in [[fanwai-15-banlatte-jiang-jinpingmei-la-erqie-haishi-luse-de-991508433]]. After [[XimenQing|西门庆]] is struck by the falling window pole and notices [[PanJinlian|潘金莲]], he repeatedly returns to Wang Po's shop to look across the street and gather information.

The episode reads Wang Po as a skilled broker rather than only comic background. She withholds, teases, misdirects, and prices information until Ximen Qing openly names his desire and offers silver. In the source's [[UrbanEverydayFiction]] frame, her shop is a small institution: neighborhood visibility, gossip, hospitality, and transaction all pass through it.
