## Overview

缪忌 / Miu Ji is the fangshi whom Hanji 259 part 2 credits with supplying the hierarchy and antiquity claim that helped move [[Taiyi|太一]] into [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s imperial ritual system.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-259-hanwudi-wo-ba-ni-dang-dashi-ni-ba-wo-dang-shazi-2-lo4brmkuzpoozgszshrqz4hbgrgt|Hanji 259 part 2]] says Miu Ji presents Taiyi as superior to the Five Emperors and claims that ancient rulers sacrificed to Taiyi in the southeast suburbs during spring and autumn. Those two propositions make the deity both supreme and historically appropriate for an emperor. Wudi initially approves without officiating personally, leaving the rite to sacrificial officials; the later Ganquan altar and winter-solstice worship show the proposal entering a larger court institution, without proving that Miu Ji alone designed the final system.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han fangshi associated with the early court advocacy of Taiyi worship.
- Uses divine hierarchy to rank Taiyi above the Five Emperors.
- Uses claimed ancient precedent to make a proposed rite compatible with imperial identity.
- Influences court ritual through a memorial whose later institutional consequences exceed the evidence for his continuing personal role.

## Evidence

- **Hierarchy and precedent:** [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-259-hanwudi-wo-ba-ni-dang-dashi-ni-ba-wo-dang-shazi-2-lo4brmkuzpoozgszshrqz4hbgrgt|Hanji 259 part 2]] attributes the “above the Five Emperors” hierarchy and ancient seasonal imperial rite to Miu Ji.
- **Court uptake:** [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-259-hanwudi-wo-ba-ni-dang-dashi-ni-ba-wo-dang-shazi-2-lo4brmkuzpoozgszshrqz4hbgrgt|Hanji 259 part 2]] says Wudi approves the memorial, first delegates worship to ritual officers, and later personally worships Taiyi at the Ganquan altar.

## Qualifications

The current profile rests on one structured podcast summary, not a primary-source or specialist ritual-history dossier. It does not establish Miu Ji's biography, office, exact memorial wording, sources for the claimed ancient precedent, role in altar design, or continuing influence after Wudi's initial approval. The court outcome is clearer than the truth or originality of his religious claims.

## What Changed

- Created a bounded profile separating Miu Ji's initial advocacy from the later court institution.
