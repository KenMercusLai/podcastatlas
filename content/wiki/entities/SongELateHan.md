## Overview

宋娥 / Song E is Emperor Shun's wet nurse, a palace-proximity figure whose current wiki profile now starts with an attempted private-favor title in Hanji 813. [[ZuoXiongLateHan|左雄]] and [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] both treat her marquisate as a public-order problem, Hanji 815 shows her removal from the palace and retaliation against Li Gu, and Hanji 818 preserves a later wet-nurse-linked corruption case.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-813-gudai-de-gaoguan-jing-ye-hui-bei-ouda-ln-dw3xohnmpavkpu-bdvasvietv|Hanji 813]] supplies Song E's earliest current layer. [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] wants to enfeoff her because she nursed him, but Zuo Xiong argues that private gratitude should be paid with money rather than land and title. Shun does not adopt the request to have Song E return the title, even after earthquake warnings are attached to the point.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-815-kebei-gudai-zhongchen-de-jiafeng-zhishang-llkhbxo-63pjw-gk9pihnbfr94v|Hanji 815]] shows the title problem turning into a palace-access problem. Shun accepts Li Gu's advice and orders Song E out of the palace, while eunuchs kneel and ask pardon. That correction briefly makes the court serious and restrained.

The correction does not end the palace network's ability to strike back. Song E and eunuchs resent Li Gu and forge anonymous letters or charges against him; Shun then orders Li Gu investigated by direct edict, bypassing the Shangshu Tai. Hanji 818 later records a separate Song E corruption branch in which bribing marquises are demoted or forced to return title documents and rent is reduced.

## Key Characteristics

- Wet-nurse figure whose private service becomes a public title problem.
- Palace-proximity actor whose access is treated by Li Gu as a governable court risk.
- Retaliation anchor after losing palace access, working through anonymous accusation with eunuchs.
- Example of how intimate service can distort normal bureaucratic review channels.
- Recurrent Shun-era caution because later corruption is still linked to her name.

## Qualifications

Song E is not yet a fully independent biographical subject in the wiki. Her page is justified by her role in the 133 CE title, palace-removal, and retaliation sequence plus the later Hanji 818 corruption notice, but the record here does not establish her full household, patronage network, exact title mechanics, or later fate.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 813 as the earliest Song E layer, before the Hanji 815 palace-removal and retaliation case.
- Reframed her significance around the conversion of wet-nurse intimacy into title, palace access, and accusation power.
- Preserved Hanji 818 as a later source-bounded corruption branch rather than a full career profile.
