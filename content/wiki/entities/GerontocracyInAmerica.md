# Gerontocracy in America

Gerontocracy in America is the book by [[SamuelMoyn]] discussed in [[older-workers-arent-retiring-should-they-be-forced-to]]. The [[PlanetMoney]] episode uses it to frame the claim that older Americans can hold disproportionate control over power, wealth, housing, and high-status work.

In the source, the book is not treated as a blanket anti-elder argument. It becomes a way to ask whether some roles should have endpoints or role transitions when older incumbents can afford to step aside but remain in positions younger workers cannot access.
