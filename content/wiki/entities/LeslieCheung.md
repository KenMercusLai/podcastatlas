## Overview

张国荣 / Leslie Cheung plays Ning Caichen in [[AChineseGhostStory1987|《倩女幽魂》(1987)]].

## Current Profile

The source credits Cheung with replacing a rigid scholar type with someone frightened, clumsy, soft, stubborn, sincere, and capable of courage. Bravery emerges from attachment and choice rather than a pre-existing heroic image.

## Key Characteristics

- Gives vulnerability dramatic value rather than treating it as simple weakness.
- Makes trust in Nie Xiaoqian the basis of later action.
- Supports a modern emotional register inside a stylized period setting.
