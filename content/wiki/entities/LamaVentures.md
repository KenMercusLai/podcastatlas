# Lama Ventures

Lama Ventures is the AI-focused venture platform discussed in [[e249-token-jingji-zhuandian-openclaw-hermes-dao-bendi-ziyan-de-agent-jinhua-zhi-lu-6242033d-a14a-44e3-a622-cbfc7d3c3817]]. [[Dongxu]] is introduced as a partner there, and [[ZhangHongjiang|张宏江]] says he joined because the platform is young, focused on AI, and positioned to support Chinese founders in Silicon Valley.

In the episode, Lama Ventures matters less as a fund profile than as a bridge between two AI centers: Silicon Valley and China. The guests use it to describe a moment when [[OpenSourceAIModels]], agent infrastructure, and [[AgentNativeSoftware]] create startup opportunities that draw from both ecosystems.
