# Ding Kefeng / 丁克峰

## Overview
Ding Kefeng / 丁克峰 is the colorectal-cancer and bowel-health specialist guest in the VOL.203 [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode on bowel symptoms, colorectal-cancer screening, colon polyps, and colorectal surgery.

## Current Profile
The current evidence presents Ding as a professor from [[ZhejiangUniversitySecondAffiliatedHospital|浙江大学医学院附属第二医院]] who translates colorectal-surgery and screening knowledge for a general audience. His role in the wiki is not a full biography; it is a source-scoped clinical-literacy role linking everyday gastrointestinal symptoms to structured evaluation, validated screening, polyp risk, and treatment quality-of-life.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains bowel symptoms without collapsing them into either casual reassurance or cancer panic.
- Treats colorectal-cancer screening as a staged process involving symptom observation, stool testing, risk concentration, and colonoscopy.
- Keeps tumor markers and emerging liquid-biopsy-style tests inside an evidence and maturity boundary.
- Frames colon-polyp management through pathology, size, dysplasia, and surveillance interval rather than generic fear.
- Presents colorectal surgery as a balance between cure, safe margins, organ function, and postoperative life quality.
