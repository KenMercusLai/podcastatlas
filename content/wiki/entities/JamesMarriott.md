# James Marriott

## Overview
James Marriott appears as the author of [[TheNewDarkAges]], a polemic about declining reading and smartphone-driven attention change.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Marriott as an interpreter of the shift from text toward oral and audiovisual media. Drawing on [[WalterOng]], his account contrasts writing's capacity for revised logical structure with speech's iterative, repetitive "back-looping," then applies that distinction to podcasts, political rhetoric, and long-form attention.

## Key Characteristics
- Writer associated with a public argument about reading decline.
- Uses media theory to distinguish oral and written modes of reasoning.
- Treats smartphones and feed media as major pressures on sustained reading.
- Supplies a diagnosis that the episode itself partially qualifies rather than fully endorses.

## Evidence
- **Reading diagnosis:** [[the-end-of-the-world-is-ai-an-existential-threat-6aaa68a64cef87591d2b659b]] presents Marriott's book as collecting evidence of declining reading and blaming smartphones for attention loss.
- **Ong framework:** [[the-end-of-the-world-is-ai-an-existential-threat-6aaa68a64cef87591d2b659b]] attributes the oral "back-looping" versus revised-writing distinction to Marriott's use of Walter Ong.
- **Qualification:** [[the-end-of-the-world-is-ai-an-existential-threat-6aaa68a64cef87591d2b659b]] also notes that print and text have long carried falsehood, rage, and persecution.

## Qualifications
This is an episode-mediated profile, not a full account of Marriott's work or an independent review of the book's evidence.
