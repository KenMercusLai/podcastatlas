# 稷下学宫 / Jixia Academy

稷下学宫 / Jixia Academy is the intellectual setting that [[30-mengzi-zhanguo-fenqing-de-chizi-zhixin-592494138]] uses to make [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] more than a single-school heir. The episode describes it as a Qi-supported gathering place near Linzi's Ji gate, where scholars from different states and traditions debated in a high-density Warring States thought market.

The source's claim is interpretive rather than strict biography: because Mencius's early life is poorly documented, Jixia functions as a plausible environment for explaining his exposure to multiple schools before he chose and sharpened the renyi path associated with [[Confucius|孔子]]. It also supports the episode's broader picture of [[WarringStatesPeriod|战国时期]] politics as a world where ideas and state competition were intertwined.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-3-ltca-ikuzo9ej7a0lfrgwv2-wulb]] gives Jixia an earlier state-building origin inside the Tian-family Qi branch. The episode credits [[TianQiHuanGong|田武 / 田氏齐桓公]] with creating or sponsoring the academy near [[Linzi|临淄]]'s Ji gate, and frames Qi's support for scholars as a deliberate willingness to fund "idle" talent before it becomes obviously useful. Jixia therefore becomes both an intellectual-history node and a state-capacity node for [[QiState|齐国]].
