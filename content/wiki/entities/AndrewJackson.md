# Andrew Jackson / 安德鲁·杰克逊

Andrew Jackson appears in [[78-xifang-juedou-shi-chujian-ba-weile-rongyu-687731000]] as the U.S. president used to show how deeply [[HonorDuelCulture|honor duel culture]] entered some American political and social settings. The episode presents him as a frequent duelist and uses that reputation to contrast American dueling with more rule-bound European examples.

Jackson's role in the source is not a full presidential biography. He functions as an example of how personal courage, insult, weapons, status, and public masculinity could become political capital in the [[UnitedStates]] before modern legal and democratic norms made private combat less acceptable.
