## Overview

陈俊 / Chen Jun is now synthesized as a recommended Nanyang talent who reaches [[LiuXiu|刘秀]] with [[JiaFuEasternHan|贾复]] before accession, and later as the Guangwu-side commander whose advice turns Liu Xiu's post-Shunshui recovery from pursuit into supply control. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-670-juejing-qiusheng-liuxiu-xianru-shengsi-da-weiji-ltz1nciofev4cqjkod-p8mmwovhe|Hanji 670]] supplies the talent-transfer layer, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-675-luanshi-fenzheng-zhizhong-liuxiu-ruhe-jueqi-lrvxu1q4-ysg4kfxkf25hswygfyo|Hanji 675]] supplies the operational advice layer.

## Current Profile

Hanji 670 places Chen Jun in the early transfer of useful people into Liu Xiu's camp. [[LiuJiaHanzhongKingEasternHan|Liu Jia]] recommends Jia Fu and the Nanyang man Chen Jun to Liu Xiu. The source then says they arrive near Bairen and gives Chen Jun an office title in a transcript-sensitive form.

Hanji 675 places Chen Jun after Liu Xiu has already suffered a serious battlefield setback at Shunshui, escaped danger, and regrouped after [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]] steadies the army. Chen Jun then changes the operational problem: instead of trying to win only through direct attack, Liu Xiu can use local denial, fortified settlements, and enemy hunger.

The episode makes Chen Jun a useful evidence point for early Guangwu command because Liu Xiu accepts the advice and praises the result. Chen Jun is therefore not only a named officer but a source case for subordinate counsel becoming battlefield leverage.

## Key Characteristics

- Nanyang-linked talent recommended by Liu Jia into Liu Xiu's early camp.
- Guangwu-side commander identified as Strong Crossbow General in Hanji 675.
- Adviser who diagnoses enemy vulnerability as dependence on plunder and local food.
- Operator of a light-cavalry forward screen that prepares civilians for resource denial.
- Source case for supply control replacing frontal attack as the decisive mechanism.
- Subordinate whose advice Liu Xiu accepts and publicly credits after victory.
