## Overview

陈俊 / Chen Jun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-675-luanshi-fenzheng-zhizhong-liuxiu-ruhe-jueqi-lrvxu1q4-ysg4kfxkf25hswygfyo|Hanji 675]] as the Guangwu-side commander whose advice turns Liu Xiu's post-Shunshui recovery from pursuit into supply control. The source identifies him as Strong Crossbow General and says he proposes sending light cavalry ahead of the retreating enemy so villages can fortify and clear resources, denying food and plunder until the enemy collapses.

## Current Profile

Chen Jun's current profile is source-scoped but strategically important. Hanji 675 places him after [[LiuXiu|刘秀]] has already suffered a serious battlefield setback at Shunshui, escaped danger, and regrouped after [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]] steadies the army. Chen Jun then changes the operational problem: instead of trying to win only through direct attack, Liu Xiu can use local denial, fortified settlements, and enemy hunger.

The episode makes Chen Jun a useful evidence point for early Guangwu command because Liu Xiu accepts the advice and praises the result. Chen Jun is therefore not only a named officer but a source case for subordinate counsel becoming battlefield leverage.

## Key Characteristics

- Guangwu-side commander identified as Strong Crossbow General in Hanji 675.
- Adviser who diagnoses enemy vulnerability as dependence on plunder and local food.
- Operator of a light-cavalry forward screen that prepares civilians for resource denial.
- Source case for supply control replacing frontal attack as the decisive mechanism.
- Subordinate whose advice Liu Xiu accepts and publicly credits after victory.
