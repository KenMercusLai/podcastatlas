# Nuestra Senora de la Concepcion

Nuestra Senora de la Concepcion appears in [[693-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-englands-greatest-hero-part-3-glt5069845329]] as the Spanish treasure ship captured by [[FrancisDrake]] in the Pacific. The source describes it as the great prize of the [[GoldenHind]]'s lone Pacific campaign.

The captured cargo includes silver reals, gold, and silver bars. The episode uses the prize to show why Drake's voyage mattered strategically: he was no longer only raiding Atlantic ports, but entering the Pacific side of Spain's imperial system and proving [[SpanishImperialMonopolyVulnerability]].

The hosts also say Drake treated the captured Spanish captain chivalrously and offered enslaved people aboard freedom and sailors' wages, adding another contradiction to the source's portrait of a former slaving participant.
