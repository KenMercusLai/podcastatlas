# Leon Festinger / 利昂·费斯廷格

Leon Festinger / 利昂·费斯廷格 appears in [[123-renxing-shiyan-dangxin-ni-ziji-779479569]] as part of the episode's history of [[Deindividuation]]. The source mentions Festinger and colleagues' 1952 account of how individuals can feel less individually present inside groups, lowering self-awareness and self-control.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-161-caiyun-he-renji-guanxi-de-liangzi-jiuchan-2-lttnuxlypca4pafz1mzdr3aizfcv|Hanji 161 part 2]] invokes "费斯丁格法则" as a popular 10/90 rule: a small share of life is uncontrollable event, while the larger share is shaped by reaction. The wiki records this as the source's usage for [[ReactionChoiceFrame|反应选择框架]], not as a settled attribution claim about Festinger's scholarship.
