# 卞和 / Bian He

卞和 / Bian He enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-74-huanyuan-wanbi-guizhao-zhenxiang-lg1irvbbc1wp8oph2kmpatfoxebk]] as the [[ChuState|楚国]] figure whose discovery becomes [[HeShiBi|和氏璧]]. The episode says he offers a jade-in-stone to successive Chu rulers, is judged a fraud twice, and loses both feet before a later ruler opens the stone and finds the jade.

The page's role is not only biographical. Bian He is the episode's strongest example of [[TreasureRecognitionRisk|识宝风险]]: a rare object can be real while still unreadable to ordinary appraisal, and the person insisting on its value can bear the punishment for others' failed recognition.
