# 徐他 / Xu Ta (late Han)

## Overview
徐他 / Xu Ta enters the wiki through Hanji 1039 as a guard in Xu Chu's unit who plots to assassinate Cao Cao and is killed before the attempt succeeds.

## Current Profile
The current evidence makes Xu Ta a close-guard risk case rather than a broad biography. His resentment toward strict guard discipline turns insider proximity into assassination risk, while [[XuChu|许褚]]'s unexpected return exposes and ends the plot.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves inside Xu Chu's guard command, giving him physical access to Cao Cao's security environment.
- Resents the strictness of Xu Chu's discipline.
- Plots to assassinate Cao Cao when Xu Chu is away on leave.
- Is detected with his accomplices because Xu Chu unexpectedly returns and reads their behavior as suspicious.
