# Star Cloud

Star Cloud appears in [[e239-spacex-yao-rang-taikong-suanli-cong-kehuan-zouxiang-xianshi-dan-ta-huasuan-ma-259291f5-2715-4dde-bcfe-b5beb4df5793]] as a startup example in the emerging orbital data-center narrative. The episode says it has raised financing around space data centers, but [[LouisHong]] is skeptical that a standalone startup can match [[SpaceX]] on the full stack of launch, satellite manufacturing, energy, communications, and operations.

The page matters as a contrast case for [[OrbitalDataCenterEconomics]]. Star Cloud makes the category visible beyond SpaceX, but the source suggests the most viable startup opportunities may sit in components such as [[OrbitalDataCenterThermalManagement]], semiconductor adaptation, or satellite supply-chain tools rather than in building a SpaceX-scale orbital compute platform directly.
