# 亮哥 / Liang Ge (Anesthesiologist)

## Overview
亮哥 is a source-scoped anesthesiologist, medical communicator, and pulmonary-embolism survivor introduced in [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] VOL.67 through the online labels “医生讲相声” and “相声讲医生.”

## Current Profile
In [[lswakifoozu5vkckl5n-nzndj43e-lswakifoozu5vkckl5n-nzndj43e|VOL.67]], 亮哥 combines clinician knowledge with patient experience. He recounts recognizing sudden breathlessness, low oxygen saturation, and hypotension after fracture-related immobility, receiving emergency imaging and thrombolysis for extensive pulmonary embolism, and entering respiratory intensive care. He also describes anesthesiology as an often invisible safety specialty and public education as a way to explain risks—such as preoperative eating or chewing gum—before misunderstanding causes harm.

VOL.200 adds his training, equipment-cleaning, and communication trajectory. He distinguishes reluctance to kill laboratory animals from the forward action required when treating severe trauma, describes his own early awkwardness with suturing, emphasizes cleaning shared clinical equipment, and says public communication later became part of his institutional work.
