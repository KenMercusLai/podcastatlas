## Overview

韩延寿 / Han Yanshou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-451-ruzhe-xiaowangzhi-shui-youxiu-wo-jiu-nongsi-shui-lo20q2_kdbmsw2zfhehoanceo2oa|Hanji 451]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-450-qingguan-bixu-si-jiemi-hanchen-hanyanshou-bisi-zhi-mi-lmdsplur02fn29qllfhnz3fhinkq|Hanji 450]] as a Western Han official whose strong local reputation becomes politically dangerous. The sources present him as a capable Yingchuan, East Commandery, and Zuo Fengyi administrator loved by officials and commoners, but also as someone with real old-account exposure from earlier local service.

## Current Profile

Hanji 450 and Hanji 451 make Han Yanshou a mixed case of achievement, vulnerability, and political defeat. Hanji 450 gives the longer local-governance arc: Han had governed difficult Yingchuan after Zhao Guanghan, then East Commandery, and was promoted back near the capital because his record stood out. Hanji 451 sharpens the Zuo Fengyi layer: northern Chang'an counties, officials, and commoners respect him and imitate his conduct. That success creates comparison pressure because [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]] had previously held the same region without producing the same result.

The fall begins when Xiao reports old East Commandery abuses. Hanji 450 compresses the charges as lending and lavish military-carriage decoration; Hanji 451 itemizes a broader public-spending case. [[BingJiWesternHan|丙吉]] prefers to let the matter go under the general amnesty, but Xiao keeps investigating. Han tries to counterattack by accusing Xiao of earlier financial abuse in Zuo Fengyi; that accusation fails because the sources treat the evidence as nonexistent or coerced. Xiao then reframes the dispute as Han resisting lawful central supervision. Once Han's own old spending violations are confirmed, [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] and the assembled ministers accept a death sentence.

The execution scene matters to the profile. Thousands of officials and commoners reportedly weep, hold the prison cart, shout, and offer wine as Han goes to execution. Hanji 450 adds the host's wider security reading: after Huo Guang's fall, an official promoted in the Huo-era personnel field, close to scholar-official networks such as [[YangYunWesternHan|杨恽]], and loved by local people could look like a latent threat to Xuan. The page therefore preserves a sharp contrast: Han is not presented as legally spotless, but his public affection and administrative achievement make his death a key example of [[PoliticalExecutionOverreach|政治诛杀过度]].

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official praised in the episode for unusually effective Zuo Fengyi local governance.
- Former Yingchuan and East Commandery administrator whose competence predates the final Zuo Fengyi posting.
- Politically exposed because his reputation and results threaten Xiao Wangzhi's status and self-confidence.
- Vulnerable to old-account investigation over East Commandery lending, spending, decoration, and public-resource use.
- Defeated after a failed counteraccusation against Xiao is reframed as obstruction and false accusation.
- Remembered through public grief at execution and through the host's claim that popular legitimacy and Huo-era association intensified punitive escalation.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 451 and Hanji 450. It does not reconstruct Han Yanshou's full official career, the complete East Commandery evidence record, the original memorials, or a definitive legal judgment independent of the podcast's retelling. The sources make two points at once: Han had real old-account exposure, and the final death sentence is still politically shaped by Xiao Wangzhi's pursuit, narrative framing, superior-subordinate hierarchy, and the host's interpretation of post-Huo-Guang security politics.
