## Overview

韩延寿 / Han Yanshou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-451-ruzhe-xiaowangzhi-shui-youxiu-wo-jiu-nongsi-shui-lo20q2_kdbmsw2zfhehoanceo2oa|Hanji 451]] as a Western Han official whose strong local reputation becomes politically dangerous. The episode presents him as a capable Zuo Fengyi administrator loved by officials and commoners, but also as someone with real old-account exposure from an earlier East Commandery tenure.

## Current Profile

Hanji 451 makes Han Yanshou a mixed case of achievement, vulnerability, and political defeat. His two-year work in Zuo Fengyi is described as unusually effective: northern Chang'an counties, officials, and commoners respect him and imitate his conduct. That success creates comparison pressure because [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]] had previously held the same region without producing the same result.

The fall begins when someone reports that Han spent public money lavishly in East Commandery. [[BingJiWesternHan|丙吉]] prefers to let the matter go under the general amnesty, but Xiao keeps investigating. Han tries to counterattack by accusing Xiao of earlier financial abuse in Zuo Fengyi; that accusation fails because the episode says the evidence was false and testimony coerced. Xiao then reframes the dispute as Han resisting lawful central supervision. Once Han's own old spending violations are confirmed, [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] and the assembled ministers accept a death sentence.

The execution scene matters to the profile. Thousands of officials and commoners reportedly weep, shout, and offer wine as Han goes to execution. The source therefore preserves a sharp contrast: Han is not presented as legally spotless, but his public affection and administrative achievement make his death a key example of [[PoliticalExecutionOverreach|政治诛杀过度]].

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official praised in the episode for unusually effective Zuo Fengyi local governance.
- Politically exposed because his reputation and results threaten Xiao Wangzhi's status and self-confidence.
- Vulnerable to old-account investigation over East Commandery spending and public-resource use.
- Defeated after a failed counteraccusation against Xiao is reframed as obstruction and false accusation.
- Remembered in the source through public grief at execution, making him a capable but not fully exonerated victim of punitive escalation.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 451. It does not reconstruct Han Yanshou's full official career, the complete East Commandery evidence record, the original memorials, or a definitive legal judgment independent of the podcast's retelling. The source makes two points at once: Han had real old-account exposure, and the final death sentence is still politically shaped by Xiao Wangzhi's pursuit, narrative framing, and superior-subordinate hierarchy.
