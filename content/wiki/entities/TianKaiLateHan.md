# Tian Kai (Late Han)

## Overview
田楷 / Tian Kai is a Gongsun Zan-aligned late-Han regional commander whose trace runs through Qingzhou proxy war, Liu Bei's early network, and the final Yuan-side victory sequence. The bounded sources make him less a central strategist than a marker of how the Gongsun-Yuan conflict spills across provinces and consumes allied subcommanders.

## Current Profile
Tian Kai first appears as the Qingzhou inspector aligned with Gongsun Zan and opposed by Yuan Shao's son [[YuanTan|袁谭]], in a devastated Qingzhou environment marked by famine and warlord extraction. He then appears in the Xuzhou relief context, where Tao Qian asks Tian Kai and [[LiuBei|刘备]] for help and Liu Bei's later rise diverges from Tian Kai's orbit. Hanji 1031 closes the arc by noting that Tian Kai is defeated by Yuan Tan, returns to his remaining territory, and later dies fighting Yuan Shao.

## Key Characteristics
- Gongsun Zan-aligned Qingzhou actor in the Yuan-Gongsun proxy conflict.
- Regional commander operating in a famine-struck, socially devastated war zone.
- Transitional figure in Liu Bei's early military-political environment.
- Casualty of Yuan-side consolidation after the wider Gongsun Zan collapse.

## Evidence
- Qingzhou proxy-war role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-969-yuanshi-xiongdi-neidou-shui-neng-jianlou-lsgj6sewo35jpsgfm-koivijtr6r|Hanji 969]] places Tian Kai against Yuan Tan in Qingzhou during the Yuan brothers' indirect contest and describes the surrounding famine and cannibalism.
- Xuzhou and Liu Bei context: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-973-changpingguan-zhizhan-shengsi-zhidi-zhanhou-jing-chonggui-yuhao-lgazc9dewfayb8qnkncfkfa12yet|Hanji 973]] shows Tao Qian seeking help from Tian Kai and Liu Bei during Cao Cao's Xuzhou campaign.
- Final defeat and death: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1031-gongsunzan-zhisi-cong-bianjiang-zhanshen-dao-kunshou-zifen-lmqoechccfwmfpmbdx8-tjmnm3jz|Hanji 1031]] notes Yuan Tan's defeat of Tian Kai and Tian Kai's later death while fighting Yuan Shao.

## Qualifications
The sources preserve Tian Kai mainly through interactions with stronger narrative centers: Gongsun Zan, Yuan Tan, Tao Qian, Liu Bei, Yuan Shao, and Cao Cao. They do not yet support a thick independent profile of his faction, administration, or ideology.

## What Changed
- Hanji 1031 adds the closing outcome of Tian Kai's Yuan-side defeat and death.
- The profile now links Qingzhou proxy war to the later Gongsun-Yuan endgame.
- Tian Kai's role is now framed as an allied-command collapse marker rather than only as Liu Bei's early superior.

## Relationships
- [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] - aligned northern patron whose conflict structures Tian Kai's Qingzhou role.
- [[YuanTan|袁谭]] - direct opponent who defeats Tian Kai in Qingzhou.
- [[YuanShao|袁绍]] - broader Yuan-side opponent against whom Tian Kai later dies fighting.
- [[QingzhouLateHan|青州]] - regional theater where Tian Kai's proxy-war role is concentrated.
- [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] - Xuzhou governor who asks Tian Kai for military help.
- [[LiuBei|刘备]] - early associated commander whose path later separates from Tian Kai's orbit.
- [[WarDisasterCompounding|兵灾复合放大]] - concept for the famine and social devastation surrounding Tian Kai's Qingzhou theater.
