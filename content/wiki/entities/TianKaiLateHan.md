# 田楷 / Tian Kai (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-969-yuanshi-xiongdi-neidou-shui-neng-jianlou-lsgj6sewo35jpsgfm-koivijtr6r|Hanji 969]] adds Tian Kai before the Xuzhou aid branch. Here he is [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]]'s Qingzhou inspector in the long confrontation with [[YuanTan|袁谭]], the [[YuanShao|袁绍]]-side actor. The source makes Tian Kai part of a proxy-war disaster in [[QingzhouLateHan|青州]], where two years of fighting exhausts grain and pushes civilians into catastrophe before [[ZhaoQiLateHan|赵岐]] mediates.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-973-changpingguan-zhizhan-shengsi-zhidi-zhanhou-jing-chonggui-yuhao-lgazc9dewfayb8qnkncfkfa12yet|Hanji 973]] later uses 田楷 / Tian Kai (late Han) as the Qingzhou inspector whom [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] asks for help when [[CaoCao|曹操]] threatens [[Xuzhou|徐州]]. [[LiuBei|刘备]] is initially attached to Tian Kai as Pingyuan chancellor, bringing only a few thousand soldiers.

The source uses Tian Kai as the before-and-after contrast for Liu Bei's rise. Once Tao Qian gives Liu Bei four thousand Danyang troops and recommends him as Yuzhou inspector, Liu Bei no longer simply follows Tian Kai but becomes a more independent actor stationed at [[PeiCounty|小沛]].
