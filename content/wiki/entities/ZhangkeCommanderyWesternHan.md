## Overview

牂柯郡 / Zhangke Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-512-xihan-chuzheng-yelang-wo-budang-dage-haoduonian-lufpmcdwb20dhx2qxfjws7-qdpkb|Hanji 512]] as the Western Han southwestern commandery endangered by fighting among [[YelangStateWesternHan|夜郎]], Gouding, and Louwo. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-513-yongchuang-duomingdao-hanchen-chenli-zhusha-yelang-guowang-lvfqkcbuzts119gtpzfipzo5wbo|Hanji 513]] adds the commandery-governor action layer through [[ChenLiWesternHan|陈立]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Zhangke as a vulnerable frontier commandery with limited Han population, many local groups, and a dangerous distance from Chang'an. Its governor judges that the fighting around Ye Lang may spill across the commandery boundary and reports urgently to the court.

Zhangke matters in the episode because it makes strategy concrete. The court cannot simply assume that central orders will arrive quickly or that a long garrison will be affordable. [[DuQingWesternHan|杜清]] therefore treats Zhangke as a place where timing, supply, geography, and personnel fit decide whether Han authority can act before local disorder consolidates.

Hanji 513 shows that Zhangke's governor is not merely a messenger to the center. Chen Li receives the office after Wang Feng decides on force, asks for authorization to execute Xing, then acts when distance delays a reply. The commandery becomes a test of delegated judgment: inspection cover, small selected staff, targeted killing, and follow-on logistics all substitute for a slow, large, centrally managed campaign.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han southwestern frontier commandery.
- Described by the source as roughly in present-day northern Guizhou.
- Contains relatively few Han people and many local groups in the episode's framing.
- Threatened by fighting among Ye Lang, Gouding, and Louwo.
- Hard to reinforce because of distance and cost.
- Requires a capable governor because the office sits at the junction of frontier defense, diplomacy, and local administration.
- Becomes the operational base for Chen Li's delegated suppression of Xing and the remaining Ye Lang revolt.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 512 and Hanji 513. The transcripts write 张科, which this wiki normalizes to 牂柯郡; the sources do not provide a full commandery history, administrative map, or list of governors beyond Chen Li's crisis role.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 513's delegated-governor layer through Chen Li's targeted killing and logistics-based pacification.
- Preserved Hanji 512's framing of Zhangke as a distant, costly, vulnerable frontier office.
