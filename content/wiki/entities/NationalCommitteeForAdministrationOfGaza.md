# National Committee for Administration of Gaza

The National Committee for Administration of Gaza appears in [[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]] as the proposed Palestinian technocratic government for [[Gaza]]. The episode says its timing, territory, and powers are still unclear, making it a case of [[GazaTechnocraticAdministration]] rather than a settled governing handover.

The committee matters because administrative control is the bridge between military withdrawal and reconstruction. If [[Hamas]] retains power in practice, if [[Israel]] controls most territory, or if militias hold enclaves, a nominal technocratic body may have too little authority to govern daily life.
