# Delete / 《删除》

## Overview
Delete / 《删除》 is the [[ViktorMayerSchonberger]] book used by [[episode-227-ershiyishiji-qingchun-wuyu-8211230332-278487]] to explain how digital systems make remembering the default and forgetting costly.

## Current Profile
The book is represented as a digital-memory reference in Ren Ning's youth-risk argument. Its role is to show that forgetting has social and moral value: people need room to change, be forgiven, and begin again, but searchable digital records make old speech and behavior easier to revive.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides the episode's language for digital memory as default.
- Treats forgetting as a precondition for change and forgiveness.
- Supports the episode's account of screenshot-conscious self-discipline.
- Connects privacy, memory, and youth risk avoidance rather than only technical storage.

## Evidence
- Memory reversal: [[episode-227-ershiyishiji-qingchun-wuyu-8211230332-278487]] says the book argues that digital technology reverses the historical balance between remembering and forgetting.
- Social value: [[episode-227-ershiyishiji-qingchun-wuyu-8211230332-278487]] uses the book to argue that forgetting allows people to change and restart.
- Platform conduct: [[episode-227-ershiyishiji-qingchun-wuyu-8211230332-278487]] ties the book's point to life under screenshots, search records, and public evidence.
