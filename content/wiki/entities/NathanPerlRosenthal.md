# Nathan Perl-Rosenthal

## Overview

Nathan Perl-Rosenthal is the maritime historian who gives [[PlanetMoney]] historical context on privateering and prize taking.

## Current Profile

Perl-Rosenthal explains prize taking as a commercialized form of wartime capture whose incentives generally favored intimidation over combat: fighting endangered crews and damaged the ships and cargo captors hoped to sell. His account describes the practice as comparatively bloodless while preserving that it was not nonviolent and could include the sale of enslaved people.

## Key Characteristics

- Interprets privateering through its legal and commercial incentives.
- Emphasizes that valuable captured property gave captors reasons to avoid destructive combat.
- Uses “comparatively bloodless” as a relative historical claim, not a claim of harmlessness.
