# Alien Franchise

The Alien franchise appears in [[the-real-horror-of-alien-and-how-it-explains-why-were-not-paid-enough]] as a recurring science-fiction setting where corporate power and bodily danger reinforce each other. The episode argues that the best entries do not only stage creature horror; they also make workers feel powerless before [[WeylandYutani]].

The source connects the franchise to [[LaborMonopsony]] by treating [[AlienFilm|Alien]] and [[AlienRomulus|Alien: Romulus]] as two versions of the same workplace problem: workers face dangerous jobs, hidden obligations, weak exit options, and administrative systems that convert company profit into worker exposure.

- [[AlienFilm|Alien]] and [[AlienRomulus|Alien: Romulus]] - main film cases discussed in the episode.
