# Costello (Andrew Huberman's dog)

## Overview
Costello was [[AndrewHuberman]]'s dog and a public presence in the [[HubermanLab]] community. The launch episode introduces him as a steady participant whose sounds may enter recordings; a later source makes his late-life decline central to a discussion of love, care, and grief.

## Current Profile
In [[scim5670648609-scim5670648609]], Huberman introduces the 90-pound bulldog mastiff as an active, steady podcast participant whose snoring and other sounds may be audible. In [[essentials-machines-creativity-love-dr-lex-fridman-scim3392253065]], he later describes Costello's worsening health, reduced mobility, and loss of pleasure in walking, sniffing, and marking. He also describes waking in tears, worrying about listeners who felt connected to Costello, and hoping Costello's combination of toughness, sweetness, and kindness would continue through the people who knew his story.

## Key Characteristics
- Companion dog whose life was shared publicly through Huberman's podcast and posts.
- Introduced at the show's launch as an ordinary audible presence rather than a polished production problem.
- Remembered for combining toughness with sweetness and kindness.
- Late-life decline made quality of life and end-of-life responsibility emotionally concrete.
- Public familiarity widened the circle of anticipated mourning beyond his immediate caregiver.

## Evidence
- Launch role: [[scim5670648609-scim5670648609]] calls Costello a 90-pound bulldog mastiff, warns that listeners may hear him, and presents him as a steady participant.
- Decline and care: [[essentials-machines-creativity-love-dr-lex-fridman-scim3392253065]] describes abscesses, behavior changes, joint pain, sleep problems, spinal degeneration, a fall, and reduced enjoyment of ordinary dog activity.
- Grief and public bond: [[essentials-machines-creativity-love-dr-lex-fridman-scim3392253065]] records Huberman's grief and concern for listeners who had formed a mediated connection to Costello.
- Remembered character: [[essentials-machines-creativity-love-dr-lex-fridman-scim3392253065]] identifies toughness, sweetness, and kindness as traits Huberman hoped others would carry forward.

## Qualifications
The sources are personal podcast accounts, not veterinary records or access to Costello's inner experience. The launch description is affectionate scene-setting; the later conversation does not resolve the timing or medical details of end-of-life decisions.
