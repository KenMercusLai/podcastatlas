# Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare / 厚生劳动省

The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare appears in [[vol-120-riben-yiliao-tixi-de-bengkui-yu-zhongsheng-yiyuan-yaopin-yibao-sanjiao-kunju-de-yizhong-jiefa-lp-p5p1cybbldphe0qajteggsitt]] as the Japanese government institution associated with post-2000 healthcare reform. The episode links the 2001 ministry reorganization to later reform around hospitals, insurance, drugs, long-term care, and professional roles.

The source does not prove that the institutional merger directly caused reform success, but it treats the ministry as the coordinating site for [[DPCDRGPaymentReform]], [[JapaneseDrugPricingReform]], [[CommunityIntegratedCare]], and [[PharmacistAsSecondDoctor]].
