## Overview

The Zhang-era Ma family is the outer-relative household whose profile now has an earlier military and exemplar-governance layer before the title and lost-patronage sequence. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-749-guanliren-bikan-fa-jiangjin-shi-guanli-de-zuijia-shouduan-ma-lnhhoh0oxlstuwakl3rsqvcss-cf|Hanji 749]] shows [[MaLiaoEasternHan|Ma Liao]] advising [[EmpressMaMingdeEasternHan|Empress Ma]] on elite example while [[MaFangEasternHan|Ma Fang]] receives heavy Qiang command despite Fifth Lun's warning, [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-750-jiemi-donghan-wanmei-nvxing-beihou-manshi-xisheng-lu-96lbdh7r0tsmiopbqqrj0q1gt|Hanji 750]] shows the family receiving marquisates but leaving office, and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-752-shiqu-kaoshan-de-ren-gai-ruhe-chushi-lt0qb7alyh0ipf6mk8hvz92cs297|Hanji 752]] uses the family as the main example of why formerly protected people must lower display and discipline guests.

## Current Profile

The family appears through several members rather than a single policy program. Hanji 749 gives two early layers. Ma Liao writes to Empress Ma that court and capital elites must visibly model frugality if lower officials are to imitate it. In the same episode, Zhangdi sends Ma Fang as acting chariot-and-cavalry general against Qiang rebels despite Fifth Lun's warning that relatives should not receive heavy offices. Ma Fang's victories over Buqiao prove capacity, while Geng Gong's later impeachment after offending Ma Fang leaves an accountability shadow.

Hanji 750 gives the upstream restraint layer. [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Zhangdi]] grants marquisates to Ma Liao, Ma Fang, and Ma Guang because he treats Empress Ma as his mother and the Ma clan as maternal kin. Empress Ma worries that external honors will damage the family's clean reputation. The brothers try to decline the titles, then accept the titles while resigning their offices and leaving the court center as special-ranking nobles.

Hanji 752 shows the later failure to sustain that discipline after Empress Ma dies. Ma Liao is cautious and sincere but fails to restrain younger relatives. Ma Fang and Ma Guang build large residences, keep hundreds of guests, accumulate horses, and are associated with exactions from Qiang and Hu groups. Ma Yu, Ma Liao's son, becomes part of the later consequence sequence after Ma-family demotion.

The political arc is fast once the household loses protection. [[YangZhongEasternHan|Yang Zhong]] warns that the household's public standing makes irresponsible behavior dangerous. Zhangdi dislikes the display and places restrictions on the family. Once the emperor's attitude shifts, guests withdraw, officials impeach Ma Fang and Ma Guang, and the family is ordered back to its fiefs, with Ma Guang retained only because he is comparatively more cautious.

## Key Characteristics

- Outer-relative household honored because Zhangdi recognizes Empress Ma as his mother.
- Family whose earliest current layer pairs Ma Liao's exemplar warning with Ma Fang's risky heavy command.
- Family whose Hanji 750 layer tries to separate noble title from direct court office.
- Formerly protected household whose safety declines after Empress Ma's death.
- Family whose wealth, guests, residences, horses, and frontier exactions become political liabilities.
- Household where Ma Liao's personal caution does not prevent younger relatives' overreach.
- Contrast family to the rising Dou household in the same Zhangdi-era court.

## Qualifications

This page aggregates the Ma household because the sources develop a family-risk pattern more than separate full biographies for every member. Hanji 749 now gives Ma Fang enough independent profile for a standalone page, but Ma Guang and Ma Yu remain source-scoped until additional source notes give them independent arcs. Hanji 750 shows genuine early restraint, so Hanji 752 should be read as a later failure of continuity rather than proof that the family was always openly reckless. Hanji 749 also shows Ma Fang's real military success, so Fifth Lun's warning is structural rather than a claim of incompetence.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 749 as the upstream exemplar-and-command layer for Ma Liao and Ma Fang.
- Promoted Ma Fang out of source-scoped status because Hanji 749 gives him an independent Qiang campaign and appointment-risk role.
- Reframed Hanji 750 as honor-with-restraint after an earlier warning about elite example.
- Reframed Hanji 752 decline as failure to sustain restraint after Empress Ma's protection disappears.
