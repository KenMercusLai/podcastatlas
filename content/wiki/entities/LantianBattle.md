# 蓝田之战 / Battle of Lantian

蓝田之战 / Battle of Lantian appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-51-chuhuaiwang-qin-xiao-er-kan-jiujiu-ruhe-mie-le-ni-ltfkdlivomnvux5xodh2rlfqf6ac]] as the second stage of [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]'s angry war against [[QinState|秦国]]. After the defeat at [[DanyangBattle|丹阳之战]] and the loss of [[Hanzhong|汉中]], Chu Huaiwang mobilizes the whole country again rather than resetting his objective.

The episode says Qin draws the [[ChuState|楚国]] army through [[Wuguan|武关]] and [[Shangyu|商於]] toward the Xianyang area, then counterattacks near Lantian after Chu has advanced too deeply and stretched its supply line. [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]] simultaneously threaten Chu's capital region and rear, forcing Chu to withdraw and cede cities for peace. Lantian therefore marks the moment when anger-driven persistence compounds the first defeat into strategic isolation.
