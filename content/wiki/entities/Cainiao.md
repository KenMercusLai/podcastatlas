# 菜鸟网络

## Overview
菜鸟网络 is an [[Alibaba]]-led logistics data and infrastructure platform launched with retailers, investors, and major carriers in 2013.

## Current Profile
The source presents 菜鸟's initial role as connecting ecommerce orders, warehouses, delivery companies, and last-mile information through shared interfaces rather than replacing carrier fulfillment. Its standardized electronic waybill reduced manual address coding and enabled finer automated routing, while also deepening carrier dependence on platform data and order entry.

## Key Characteristics
- Logistics data-interface and coordination platform.
- Promoter of standardized electronic waybills.
- Joint venture whose platform partner later invested directly in carriers.
- Source of both efficiency and dependency tension.

## Evidence
- Formation: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] lists Alibaba-linked capital and one-percent stakes for SF and the Tonglu carriers.
- Operating contribution: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] credits the 2014 public electronic waybill with machine-readable sorting codes and increasingly granular routing.
- Tension: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] says carriers feared losing order entry and consumer data, while 菜鸟 lacked direct control over service quality.
