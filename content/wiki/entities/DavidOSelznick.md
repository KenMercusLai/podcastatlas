# David O. Selznick

## Overview

David O. Selznick was the producer of [[AStarIsBorn1937|*A Star Is Born*]] (1937). [[star-struck]] centers his decision to remove a fatal drunk-driving collision from the screenplay after seeking an assessment from junior staff members Budd Schulberg and Ring Lardner Jr.

## Current Profile

The commissioned memo argued that Norman Maine could have no plausible future after causing deaths except imprisonment or degradation. Selznick's replacement lets Norman crash into a tree, receive limited punishment, return to Vicki's care, and remain available for a romantic-suicide ending.

The episode interprets that production decision as culturally meaningful because it protects Norman's sympathetic status while removing victims of his intoxicated driving. That reading does not establish Selznick's private moral beliefs or prove that he anticipated the public-policy implications attributed to the edit.

## Key Characteristics

- Hollywood producer of the 1937 *A Star Is Born*.
- Decision-maker who solicited junior-reader feedback on the screenplay's fatal collision.
- Producer who replaced deaths caused by Norman with a single-vehicle crash and limited legal consequence.
- Editorial figure through whom plot viability and moral framing became inseparable.

## Evidence

### Internal review

- [[star-struck]] says Selznick asked Schulberg and Lardner to assess the ending and received a memo focused on Norman's narratively impossible future.

### Revision

- [[star-struck]] reports that Selznick removed the fatal collision and used the tree crash, jail, and Vicki's supervision instead.

### Moral effect

- [[star-struck]] interprets the change as preserving Norman's romantic tragedy while deleting the strangers harmed by his impaired driving.
