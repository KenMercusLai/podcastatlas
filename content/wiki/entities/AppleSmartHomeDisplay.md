# Apple Smart Home Display

## Overview
Apple Smart Home Display is a source-reported small square household screen intended to combine smart-home control, communication, media, and a revised [[Siri]] interface.

## Current Profile
The episode says the device can sit on a surface or hang on a wall, recognize household members, and personalize calendars or reminders. Its strategic role is to make the home a persistent [[Apple]] interface, but the launch details remain reported plans rather than a confirmed product record.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines household controls, video calls, music, calendars, reminders, and Siri.
- Is described as a small square screen with tabletop and wall-mounted use.
- Uses household-member recognition to personalize information.
- Depends on improved Siri capability after source-reported delays.

## Evidence
- Interface and functions: [[jinghu-gaotie-zhongqiujie-qian-chuxian-jiangjia-xinbailun-qisu-dikanong-qinquan-1017386449]] lists device control, calling, music, and personalized household information.
