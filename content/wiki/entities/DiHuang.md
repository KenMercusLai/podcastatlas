# 翟黄 / Di Huang

翟黄 / Di Huang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-1-lk-6ejgmjhvukqgtlz2lowu8qx-v]] as the disappointed chancellor candidate who confronts [[LiKe|李克]] after [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] chooses [[WeiCheng|魏成]]. The source uses him to test whether visible referral service should outweigh a subtler kind of talent attraction.

Di Huang lists five recommendations: [[WuQi|吴起]] for the West River frontier, [[XimenBao|西门豹]] for Ye, [[LeYang|乐羊]] for [[ZhongshanState|中山国]], Li Ke for Zhongshan administration, and [[QuHoufu|屈侯鲋]] as teacher for the ruler's son. The episode does not deny these referrals are valuable, but Li Ke's reply says they remain ministerial recommendations rather than the teacher-level attraction associated with Wei Cheng.

After hearing the comparison, Di Huang accepts the judgment and says he is willing to become Li Ke's lifelong student. His wiki role is therefore not a failure case, but a bounded example inside [[TalentReferralQuality]]: quantity and usefulness matter, but level, effect on the ruler, and relationship quality matter too.
