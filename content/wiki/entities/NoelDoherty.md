# Noel Doherty

Noel Doherty appears in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as a protege of [[IanPaisley]] who founded the [[UlsterProtestantVolunteers]] in April 1966. The episode says the group evoked [[EdwardCarson|Carson]]'s volunteers, used the motto "For God and Ulster," and recruited among [[BSpecials|B Specials]] because they had access to weapons.

Doherty's role links religious-political mobilization to paramilitary infrastructure. In the source, loyalist organization emerges from sermons, economic anxiety, anti-IRA fear, and inherited armed memory rather than from one isolated gang decision.
