# William McMahon

## Overview

William McMahon was an Australian Liberal prime minister whose episode profile is dominated by hostile judgments about character, trust, and reputation rather than policy.

## Current Profile

[[188-australian-prime-ministers-harold-holt-malcolm-fraser-part-2-glt4635125602]] presents McMahon as [[JohnGorton|John Gorton's]] successor and [[GoughWhitlam|Gough Whitlam's]] defeated opponent. Quotations from Whitlam and journalist Laurie Oakes portray him as devious, dishonest, and personally distrusted, while a story about an allegedly stolen radio-station tape recorder serves as the episode's emblem of that reputation. The result is a vivid but evidentially uneven character portrait.
