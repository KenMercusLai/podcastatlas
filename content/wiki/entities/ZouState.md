# 邹国 / Zou State

邹国 / Zou State is the small polity that [[30-mengzi-zhanguo-fenqing-de-chizi-zhixin-592494138]] uses as [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]]'s place of origin and as one possible source of his political imagination. The episode notes that Zou was near Lu, linking Mencius geographically to the broader world associated with [[Confucius|孔子]].

The source also recounts a Zou ruler story in which moving the capital is said to benefit the people more than the ruler. The episode treats this as a possible local memory behind Mencius's belief that the people's welfare and the ruler's true good are connected. That claim feeds into [[MencianBenevolentGovernment]] and [[MencianPeopleBasedLegitimacy]].
