# Diretas Ja

## Overview

Diretas Ja appears in the episode as the mid-1980s Brazilian movement demanding direct presidential elections as military dictatorship weakened.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Diretas Ja to show how the [[BrazilianNationalAnthem]] could be reclaimed from state protocol. Cultural and sporting figures, including [[FafaDeBelem]] and [[SocratesFootballer]], joined or became associated with the movement, while anthem performances at mass rallies made democratic demand audible through an inherited national symbol.

## Key Characteristics

- Mid-1980s movement demanding direct presidential elections in Brazil.
- Emerged as the military dictatorship weakened.
- Included or attracted cultural and sporting figures.
- Made anthem performance part of democratic mobilization.
- Turns national-symbol politics from official ceremony into mass protest.
