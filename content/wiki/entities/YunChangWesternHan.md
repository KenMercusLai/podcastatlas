## Overview

Yun Chang / 云敞 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-you-xinji-de-ren-you-duo-kepa-lnhkk70905eqg4lqwh3mp2sxzamc|Hanji 616]] as [[WuZhangWesternHan|吴章]]'s student, whose decision to acknowledge his teacher and bury him contrasts with wider fear after the [[DogBloodCase|狗血案]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Yun Chang as a rare loyalty figure under political terror. After Wu Zhang is executed and burned, Wang Mang treats Wu Zhang's students as a dangerous network and bars them from office. Some students evade the stigma by changing names, but Yun Chang does the opposite: he submits a memorial identifying himself as Wu Zhang's student, resigns, and gathers and buries Wu Zhang's remains.

## Key Characteristics

- Student of Wu Zhang.
- Self-accusing officeholder who refuses to hide the teacher-student tie.
- Burial actor who preserves ritual duty after public corpse humiliation.
- Contrast figure against students who change names to preserve careers.
