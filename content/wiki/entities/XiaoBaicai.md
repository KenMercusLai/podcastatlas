## Overview

小白菜 / Xiao Baicai was the woman accused with [[YangNaiwu|Yang Naiwu]] of adultery and poisoning after her husband's sudden death, becoming the second central defendant in the [[YangNaiwuXiaoBaicaiCase|杨乃武与小白菜案]].

## Current Profile

[[29-dongxifang-de-yuanan-deleifusi-yangnaiwu-he-xiaobaicai-lmmhqfl-idiuetsvufgrmpso2l-8|episode 29]] says Xiao Baicai had been Yang's neighbor, ate with his household, and received literacy instruction there. Local rumor recoded that contact as an improper relationship, although the episode says the two reduced contact after hearing the gossip and rejects later romantic depictions as unsupported.

After her husband died following vomiting and post-mortem bleeding, his mother reported suspected poisoning. Officials quickly treated Xiao Baicai as a suspect and used torture to obtain a confession linking her to Yang. Capital review and a new post-mortem examination eventually cleared the murder accusation. The episode says she refused remarriage, became a Buddhist nun under the name Huibao, and lived until about 1930, while stressing that legal relief did not erase stigma or lost life possibilities.

## Key Characteristics

- Neighbor of Yang Naiwu whose ordinary household contact became sexualized rumor.
- Widowed defendant accused after an ambiguous sudden death was treated as poisoning.
- Victim of torture-produced confession and repeated institutional affirmance.
- Beneficiary of a correction campaign driven largely by Yang's family and outside supporters.
- Formally cleared of murder but still subjected to moral judgment and lasting reputational harm.
- Figure whose historical identity was later remade through romance-centered popular adaptations.
