# Colin McCarrick

Colin McCarrick appears in [[tech-20260407-0407-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260407-0407-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the [[BloombergNEF]] analyst explaining why [[China]] has already reached EV sticker-price competitiveness. He links that result to integrated supply chains and to the improvement of [[LithiumIronPhosphateBatteries|lithium iron phosphate batteries]].

His contribution is to connect battery chemistry to consumer price parity. The source says LFP became smaller and faster to charge after years of Chinese engineering work, turning what had been a cheaper but bulkier chemistry into the main global EV battery route.
