# T0 Finance

T0 Finance is the AI-native finance platform described by [[WuKai]] in [[11-nian-110-yi-meijin-ranhou-ne-duihua-airwallex-wu-kai-ai-shidai-xiayizhan-1000-yi-lr4tvdrq25by7fugoqkqojw6vwdk]]. The source says the name means a company can have a mini CFO or mini finance-operations team from day zero.

The product sits inside [[IntelligentFinance]] because it is framed as more than bookkeeping software. The source says T0 can support accounting, reconciliation, report generation, finance policies, cash-flow forecasts, and revenue/expense forecasts, using broader data and workflow context than a narrow finance SaaS tool.

## Key Points
- Described as an AI-native finance platform already released in North America, according to the source.
- Aimed at early-stage or growing companies that need finance operations before they have a large finance team.
- Combines finance workflow, reporting, forecasting, and policy work rather than only one accounting function.
- Illustrates why Airwallex sees AI as a possible consolidation force across financial SaaS categories.
