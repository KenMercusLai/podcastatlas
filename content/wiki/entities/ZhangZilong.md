# 张子龙 / Zhang Zilong

## Overview

张子龙 / Zhang Zilong is the son of Malawangzi founder Zhang Yudong and the episode's detailed [[FactorySecondGeneration|厂二代]] case for successor apprenticeship, public visibility, and generational brand disagreement.

## Current Profile

After graduating in 2022, Zhang joined [[Malawangzi|麻辣王子]] on a reported starting salary of RMB 4,000 and worked on branding, creator partnerships, and Douyin live-streaming. Viral reinterpretation of a video about family property made him publicly legible as a "hidden rich heir," after which his "麻辣儿子" account turned work routines, meetings, live streams, and clean-factory access into brand media.

He calls himself a salaried "internal entrepreneur" rather than a confirmed heir. The episode treats that distinction seriously: attention can support the company, but succession still requires production, supply-chain, channel, and management learning.

## Key Characteristics

- Zhang entered through brand, creator, and live-commerce work rather than immediate control of the firm.
- His public account converts everyday factory access into consumer-facing transparency and entertainment.
- Viral personal wealth narratives create attention that he and the company cannot fully control.
- Disagreement with his mother over visible logo placement illustrates a generational conflict between social-media aesthetics and explicit brand recognition.
- His father warns that personal traffic carries company-level responsibility.
- His successor status remains deliberately unsettled while operating experience accumulates.
