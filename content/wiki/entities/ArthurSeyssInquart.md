# Arthur Seyss-Inquart

## Overview

Arthur Seyss-Inquart was the Austrian Nazi politician whom [[AdolfHitler]] forced into a decisive government role before the [[Anschluss]] and then used at the transition from Austrian sovereignty to German rule.

## Current Profile

Hitler demanded that [[KurtVonSchuschnigg]] appoint Seyss-Inquart as interior minister, giving an Austrian Nazi influence over the police during the escalating crisis. When Schuschnigg announced an independence referendum, German pressure shifted toward his removal and Seyss-Inquart's appointment as chancellor.

The episode treats this appointment as part of the annexation's veneer of internal Austrian legality. It also notes that German invasion planning used an alleged Austrian request for help even though the political outcome was produced under ultimatum and threat.

## Key Characteristics

- Austrian Nazi elevated through German coercion rather than ordinary cabinet bargaining.
- Interior minister whose portfolio mattered because it included policing during the crisis.
- Replacement for Schuschnigg at the point of state collapse.
- Figure used to give external invasion an appearance of Austrian invitation.
