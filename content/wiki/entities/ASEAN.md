# ASEAN

ASEAN appears in [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]] as one of the legitimacy arenas the [[MyanmarMilitaryJunta]] wants to re-enter. The episode says the junta wants to return to high-level ASEAN meetings after years of international rejection.

In this source, ASEAN matters less as an institutional history than as diplomatic leverage. Allowing foreign diplomats to see [[AungSanSuuKyi]], or releasing her, could reduce opposition to normalization. The episode leaves open whether regional acceptance would help [[Myanmar]]'s population or mainly strengthen the junta.
