# Chris McKleroy

## Overview
Chris McKleroy is the founder of outdoor-optics company [[NocsProvisions]] and the episode's third caller.

## Current Profile
McKleroy is a repeat founder who chose to bootstrap Nocs after a venture-backed Bluetooth-speaker company was sold for parts. He now wants binoculars and monoculars to become everyday lifestyle tools beyond birding and hunting, while preserving their capacity to create attentive, analog experience.

## Key Characteristics
- Repeat founder whose earlier venture-backed company ended in a distressed sale.
- Bootstrapped Nocs after that capital experience.
- Builds colorful, compact optics for a wider audience.
- Seeks new occasions at sports, festivals, and everyday outdoor settings.
- Describes optics as a portal into presence even while considering phone-connected sharing.
