# 蒙阴 / Mengyin

蒙阴 / Mengyin is the Shandong mountain field site in [[ep261-mifeng-weiji-ruguo-shiqu-mifeng-shijie-jianghui-zenyang-lqp-cwcdxx1ziixb35ijmmvd6cv]]. [[DuanNongyu|段农裕]] reports there during the locust-flower season, using the county's slopes and elevation differences to show why beekeepers can sometimes extend a short bloom window by moving hives within a mountain landscape.

The source also makes Mengyin a practical case for [[MigratoryBeekeeperLivelihood|migratory beekeeper livelihood]]. Beekeepers there are not just producing honey; they are managing hive placement, transfer timing, local farmer relations, weather changes, and the risk that a single hailstorm or pesticide exposure can erase expected income from a flower season.
