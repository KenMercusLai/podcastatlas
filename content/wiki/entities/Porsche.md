# Porsche

Porsche appears in [[rolex-1]] as the preferred analogy for [[Rolex]]'s modern position. The hosts compare Rolex more to Porsche than to [[Ferrari]], with the [[RolexSubmariner]] compared to the Porsche 911: high-volume relative to rarer luxury peers, industrially reliable, emotionally premium, and highly recognizable.

For the wiki, Porsche is source-scoped as an analogy for [[HighVolumeLuxuryOperator]]. It helps separate Rolex's luxury model from both mass-market commodity watches and ultra-low-volume high horology.

[[ferrari-1]] adds Porsche as a modern automotive contrast. The episode says Ferrari's China exposure fell from a smaller base than Porsche's, reinforcing Ferrari's position as a lower-volume, more allocation-controlled luxury automaker rather than a broader premium-car producer.
