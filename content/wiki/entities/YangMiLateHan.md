# 杨密 / Yang Mi (late Han)

杨密 / Yang Mi (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-985-fanmu-chengchou-liguo-zhiluan-de-xijie-tanjiu-lt-t2un0zcudkige9ucl1txa2hhk|Hanji 985]] as the zhonglangjiang who prevents [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] from killing [[YangBiao|杨彪]] at the hostage banquet. After Yang Biao rebukes Guo Si for detaining ministers while [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] detains [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], Guo Si becomes enraged and reaches for lethal violence; Yang Mi's intervention keeps the rebuke from becoming an immediate execution.

The episode uses Yang Mi as a narrow restraint case, not a full biography. In a camp where official speech can trigger a sword, his role marks the thin practical boundary that sometimes prevents remonstrance from turning instantly into murder.
