# David M. Kennedy

## Overview

David M. Kennedy is the criminologist and violence-reduction strategist presented in [[wolfgang-s-law-part-two|Wolfgang's Law - Part Two]] as a principal architect of [[FocusedDeterrence]] and [[OperationCeasefireBoston|Boston's Operation Ceasefire]].

## Current Profile

The episode portrays Kennedy as moving from network diagnosis to an integrated intervention: identify the small connected group driving serious violence, communicate directly, offer practical help and community support, and make continued violence trigger predictable enforcement. His Baltimore experience also makes him a witness to the human cost of delayed and incomplete adoption.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses tightly scoped network analysis rather than treating an entire neighborhood or demographic group as equally dangerous.
- Combines support, community legitimacy, direct communication, and enforcement in one strategy.
- Treats faithful local implementation and durable institutional ownership as essential to results.
- Frames Baltimore's multi-decade delay as preventable harm rather than a neutral diffusion lag.

## Evidence

### Strategy design

- [[wolfgang-s-law-part-two]] credits Kennedy and colleagues with identifying Boston's concentrated violence network and developing the intervention later known as Operation Ceasefire.

### Implementation judgment

- [[wolfgang-s-law-part-two]] says Kennedy observed early replications weaken after external experts departed and Baltimore postpone comprehensive adoption for more than two decades.
