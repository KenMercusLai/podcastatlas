# Qin Qiangfeng / 秦强峰

## Overview

Qin Qiangfeng / 秦强峰 is the founder figure behind [[Mideer|迷鹿]] in [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]]. The episode notes that he often uses the name "谷子" and presents him as moving from children's offline-space work into reusable toy products.

## Current Profile

Qin's source role is to show a newer Ningbo entrepreneur using manufacturing capacity differently from older OEM or appliance cases. He did not only make toys cheaply; the episode highlights his emphasis on internationalized packaging, illustration, high-end retail entry, and the translation of children's experience into standardized product lines.

## Key Characteristics

- Qin is tied to Mideer's move from offline children's spaces into standardized puzzle products.
- His route emphasizes art, design, and brand positioning alongside manufacturing.
- The source uses him to connect local supply chains with international buyers and retail channels.
- His case represents a younger design-led branch of Ningbo manufacturing.
