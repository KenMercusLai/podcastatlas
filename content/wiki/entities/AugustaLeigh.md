# Augusta Leigh

## Overview

Augusta Leigh was [[LordByron|Lord Byron / 拜伦]]'s half-sister and the intimate family relationship at the center of the scandal that preceded his 1816 exile.

## Current Profile

[[442-lord-byron-dangerous-liaisons-part-3-glt1176702097]] presents Augusta as emotionally and physically compelling to Byron and describes their weeks together at Newstead in 1813-14 as exceptionally happy for both. Byron disclosed the relationship to [[LadyCarolineLamb|Caroline Lamb]], and the perceived danger helped [[LadyMelbourne|Lady Melbourne]] renew the plan to marry him to [[AnnabellaMilbank|Annabella Milbank]].

Augusta's presence within Byron's marriage intensified Annabella's suspicion because Byron treated his half-sister with warmth while behaving cruelly toward his wife. The episode treats an incestuous relationship as the central allegation behind the scandal, but its own source note acknowledges dependence on letters, testimony, memory, and contested accounts. The profile therefore distinguishes documented intimacy and scandal from certainty about its sexual nature.

## Key Characteristics

- Byron's half-sister and unusually important emotional attachment.
- Companion during the Newstead period preceding his renewed marriage proposal.
- Central presence in the emotional breakdown of Byron's marriage.
- Subject of an incest allegation that became socially devastating.
- Figure whose historical profile is constrained by interested and mediated evidence.

## Evidence

- Newstead intimacy: [[442-lord-byron-dangerous-liaisons-part-3-glt1176702097]] describes Byron and Augusta's 1813-14 period together and Byron's later disclosure to Caroline.
- Marriage crisis: [[442-lord-byron-dangerous-liaisons-part-3-glt1176702097]] places Augusta at Six Mile Bottom and later in the London household, where the contrast in Byron's behavior sharpened Annabella's suspicions.
- Public allegation: [[442-lord-byron-dangerous-liaisons-part-3-glt1176702097]] links Caroline's incest accusation to the rumor cascade preceding Byron's departure.

## Qualifications

The episode presents the incest allegation as highly consequential but not evidentially simple. Emotional intimacy, suspicious conduct, later accusation, and proof of a sexual relationship are not interchangeable; exact conversations and motives remain source-scoped.
