## Overview

路粹 / Lu Cui is the late-Han official normalized from Hanji 1090's transcript-corrupted “希律,” serving as the formal accuser in the episode's account of Kong Rong's execution.

## Current Profile

The current source places Lu Cui between [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s political interest and [[KongRong|孔融]]'s arrest. His memorial bundles military activity in Beihai, alleged disloyal association, speech with [[MiHengLateHan|祢衡]], and challenges to hierarchy into a case the episode treats as sufficient for execution. His profile is therefore procedural rather than biographical: he is the accusation channel through which a political conflict receives formal charges.

## Key Characteristics

- Cao-side official associated with a formal memorial against Kong Rong.
- Accuser who combines conduct, association, speech, and status offenses.
- Procedural intermediary between political hostility and public prosecution.
- Source-scoped figure whose wider career is not developed in the episode.

## Evidence

- Accusation channel: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1090-ta-ceng-maosi-cangxiong-wei-mu-kuxue-zhen-shi-buxiao-zhi-ren-ma-lg-z9lyx_y6dxbopsfrqxoyymdjj|Hanji 1090]] attributes the memorial triggering Kong Rong's arrest to the name transcribed as “希律,” normalized here as Lu Cui.
- Charge bundling: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1090-ta-ceng-maosi-cangxiong-wei-mu-kuxue-zhen-shi-buxiao-zhi-ren-ma-lg-z9lyx_y6dxbopsfrqxoyymdjj|Hanji 1090]] combines Beihai force-building, alleged hostile contact, mutual sage-comparison with Mi Heng, and court disrespect.
- Political interpretation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1090-ta-ceng-maosi-cangxiong-wei-mu-kuxue-zhen-shi-buxiao-zhi-ren-ma-lg-z9lyx_y6dxbopsfrqxoyymdjj|Hanji 1090]] presents the memorial as aligned with Cao Cao's prior wish to remove Kong Rong.

## Qualifications

The transcript does not preserve Lu Cui's name correctly and provides no independent biography. The normalization, exact memorial text, Lu Cui's degree of initiative, and the legal sequence should remain source-scoped pending primary-source verification.

## What Changed

- Initial profile created to preserve the formal accuser in Hanji 1090 without retaining the transcript's corrupt name as a separate identity.
