# 《精神吸血鬼》

《精神吸血鬼》 is the book at the center of [[18-ruhe-shibie-taoli-bing-bimian-chengwei-jingshen-xixuegui-563629000]], a [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] episode on high-drain relationships. The source describes it as a French psychiatrist's book that had not been published in simplified Chinese at the time of recording; the episode uses the book as a framework rather than naming its author in the source file.

The book's wiki role is to anchor the [[PsychicVampirePersonality|精神吸血鬼人格]] cluster: the host uses it to discuss attention capture, emotional exhaustion, manipulation, dependency, guilt, isolation, and the need to leave rather than rescue.

## Key Claims
- The episode treats the book's "psychic vampire" phrase as a relationship metaphor, not as literal supernatural or pseudoscientific energy language.
- The book frame lets the source distinguish ordinary emotional need from one-sided [[EmotionalEnergyExtraction|emotional-energy extraction]].
- The source's strongest practical claim is that recognition and exit matter more than diagnosing or saving the other person.
