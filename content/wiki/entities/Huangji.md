# 黄棘 / Huangji

黄棘 / Huangji enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-60-zhongguo-di-yi-wei-bachi-chaozheng-de-taihou-jingshi-ta-ltazis9qo0dwvwmzxikk8mmpvosc]] as the 304 BCE meeting site for [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] and [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]. The episode says the rulers reaffirm their alliance there while [[QinState|秦国]] returns [[Shangyong|上庸]] to [[ChuState|楚国]].

Huangji's role is diplomatic. It marks the high point of [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]]'s Qin-Chu repair before the later hostage incident involving [[MiHeng|芈横]] breaks the alignment.
