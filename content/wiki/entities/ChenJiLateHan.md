# 陈纪 / Chen Ji (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-949-lishishang-de-dongzhuo-daodi-duo-canbao-lk6idogwmc54nk1sdfc5wonyvgbb|Hanji 949]] adds an earlier Dong Zhuo recruitment layer for Chen Ji. The source names him among respected figures drawn into office, appointing him wuguan zhonglangjiang alongside [[XunShuangLateHan|荀爽]] and [[HanRongLateHan|韩融]].

This page keeps the figure on the existing late-Han Chen Ji route unless a later source requires a split. In Hanji 949, his value is not the later Jiujiang appointment but the scholar prestige [[DongZhuo|董卓]] tries to attach to coercive rule, making him part of [[CoercedTalentLegitimacy|胁迫征士合法性]].

陈纪 / Chen Ji (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] as the person [[YuanShu|袁术]] appoints to [[JiujiangCommandery|九江郡]] after previously promising that office to [[SunCe|孙策]]. The source names Chen Ji to make Yuan Shu's first broken promise concrete.

Chen Ji's page should stay distinct from other people named Chen Ji in the wiki. In this source he is not analyzed independently; his role is the appointment beneficiary whose selection shows how Yuan Shu's short-term control over offices damages long-term trust with Sun Ce.
