## Overview

车师后王沽沟 / Gougou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-613-quanli-kuangchao-jiemi-wangmang-de-tiexue-shouwan-lgfzc3ypqizddsmd6mvaasb1dxsd|Hanji 613]] as the Later Cheshi ruler whose resistance to a Han road project, detention by Xu Pu, flight to [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], extradition, and public execution make him a central case in early Wang Mang-era frontier coercion.

## Current Profile

Gougou's role is source-bounded to the 2 CE episode. Xu Pu's new route to Yumen Pass would pass through [[CheshiHouKingdomXin|车师后王国]], threatening to impose transport, reception, and provisioning burdens on Gougou's polity. Gougou refuses to cooperate with route checking and is detained.

His wife Gu Zizhou interprets the detention through an earlier killing of a Cheshi ruler by a Han official and advises flight to Xiongnu. Gougou escapes from Gaochang and reaches the chanyu, but the Han court forces his return and executes him publicly with [[TangdouRuoqiangKing|唐斗]] before assembled Western Regions rulers.

## Key Characteristics

- Dependent Western Regions ruler exposed to infrastructure-driven Han demands.
- Actor whose noncooperation begins as burden avoidance rather than open rebellion.
- Detainee whose flight is framed by fear of death under Han frontier authority.
- Refuge seeker accepted by Xiongnu but later surrendered under Han pressure.
- Publicly executed example in a broader coercive frontier deterrence performance.
