# 广东 / Guangdong

Guangdong / 广东 enters the wiki through [[ep260-yikou-feimei-zhifang-cangzhe-zhongguo-yangzhi-de-xin-gushi-linjq2fkzhqvzjcgrmhudq12vs4j]] as a major eel-production and food-processing province. The episode uses Guangdong, especially Taishan and [[Shunde|顺德]], to show how water quality, nursery work, grow-out ponds, oral seedling deals, and grilled-eel factories create a domestic supply base for Japanese-style eel products.

The province matters because it links local aquaculture to international restaurant demand. The source says part of Guangdong eel production serves domestic consumption while part is exported, including into Japanese and overseas Japanese-restaurant supply chains.
