# 酱油文化 / Jiangyou Wenhua

## Overview
酱油文化 / Jiangyou Wenhua is represented as a high-volume AI short-drama producer and a case of how rapid scale can reverse when cheap production creates more works than platforms and audiences can absorb.

## Current Profile
The earlier source uses Jiangyou Wenhua as the production-factory pole of the market: host-reported coverage described roughly 1,200 employees, more than 100 dramas per month, and monthly revenue around RMB50 million, contrasting that route with smaller director- and writer-centered studios.

The later reported phase is a correction: headcount had expanded from dozens to roughly 1,200 across multiple studios, a typical AI short drama could cost around RMB70,000-80,000 rather than the hundreds of thousands associated with live action, and broader access to production created accumulated inventory and platform release limits. The episode says headcount then fell to roughly 600. Together the sources make Jiangyou Wenhua a scale-to-glut case rather than either a simple success or failure story.

## Key Characteristics
- High-volume benchmark for factory-style AI short-drama production.
- Multi-studio organization that reportedly scaled from dozens of people to roughly 1,200.
- Cost-reduction case where generative production lowered per-work expense relative to live action.
- Company exposed to platform intake, distribution, and audience-time constraints after output expanded.
- Contraction case whose reported headcount later fell to roughly 600.

## Qualifications
All company revenue, production, staffing, wage, and cost figures are source-reported rather than audited here. The sources do not provide dates precise enough to construct a full staffing timeline, nor do they show whether the reduction came only from oversupply rather than model change, financing, platform policy, restructuring, or normal project cycles.
