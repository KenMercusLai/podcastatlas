# 小薇 / Xiaowei (Duanwen reporter)

## Overview
小薇 is the reporter-narrator in the [[DuanwenNewsPodcast|端闻]] episode on [[Polymarket]] and prediction markets. The source uses her one-month simulated trading experiment to test whether journalistic news judgment can become market edge.

## Current Profile
Xiaowei's profile is currently source-scoped to this episode. She enters the experiment as a finance-averse journalist who reads news constantly but doubts her trading ability. By using [[PolySimulator]] rather than real money, she turns the question into a bounded observation of how [[PredictionMarketTraderAlpha]], [[MarketExpectationGap]], and [[NewsAsTradingPosition]] feel from inside a reporter's workflow.

Her result is negative: after nine event predictions and ten trades, the simulation ends down 70.52 dollars, about a 7% loss. The more important shift is attentional: news alerts from [[Reuters]] and [[Bloomberg]] begin to feel like position risk rather than public events or human stories.

## Key Characteristics
- Reporter using news habits as a test input for prediction-market trading.
- Risk-averse participant who chooses simulation over real-money exposure.
- Case example for the difference between being informed and having tradable edge.
- Witness to how event-market exposure can change the felt meaning of news.
