# Edgar the Peaceful

## Overview

Edgar the Peaceful appears in the wiki as a tenth-century English king whose sacred ceremony, unitary institutions, and readiness to punish disorder reveal that royal peace depended on both legitimacy and coercive reach.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Edgar's Bath coronation in 973 as a display of biblical and imperial kingship over a realm with a unitary church, broadly shared language, and single royal coinage. His reign represents the mature authority built from the West Saxon sequence associated with [[AlfredTheGreat|Alfred]] and [[Athelstan]].

“Peaceable” does not mean passive. Edgar's punitive ravaging of Kent after York merchants were kidnapped shows royal peace as an enforced order. His death in 975 then opens a dangerous minority succession between Edward and [[AethelredTheUnready|Æthelred]], revealing how much stability still depended on the reigning king.

## Key Characteristics

- Sacred monarch whose coronation projects biblical and imperial authority.
- Ruler of a realm joined by church organization, language, and royal coinage.
- Enforcer whose punitive violence demonstrates the coercive basis of political peace.
- Dynastic pivot whose death exposes succession fragility beneath institutional order.
