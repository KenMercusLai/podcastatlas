# 汗青 / Hanqing

## Overview
汗青 / Hanqing is the [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] interview guest who founded [[AITalk|AI Talk]] and created [[UreyVirtualIdol|Yuri / 尤栗 / 优瑞]].

## Current Profile
In the source, Hanqing is a former internet-company product manager with Tsinghua Academy of Arts and Design and long-term drawing experience. His creator path starts from early AIGC curiosity, moves through AI-generated celebrity dialogue experiments, and then turns toward original AI-native IP.

His durable profile is not "model operator" alone. The episode presents him as a creator who combines product instincts, visual taste, content intuition, and multi-model workflow design. He treats [[UreyVirtualIdol|Yuri / 尤栗 / 优瑞]] as a personality and narrative system, not only a digital singer output by [[Suno]], [[OmniHuman]], [[VideoModels]], or image generators.

## Key Characteristics
- He connects product-management experience with visual-art and AI-content creation.
- He founded [[AITalk|AI Talk]] before shifting toward original AI-native IP.
- He treats early viral AI content as a novelty window and training ground rather than a stable formula.
- He emphasizes interest, intuition, aesthetic judgment, and long-term character building over tool lists.
- He is cautious about copyright, authorization, and real-time AI-character risk.
- He frames AI as a collaborator that still depends on human direction, editing, and final judgment.
