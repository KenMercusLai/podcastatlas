## Overview

任峻 / Ren Jun (Luoyang, late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-823-bingbuxueren-shou-zeikou-ta-ruhe-zuodao-de-luai1f0nhb-ddajli99dw4bxj9m2|Hanji 823]] as the Luoyang令 named among capable local officials under [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]]. He is disambiguated from [[RenJun|任峻]], the later Cao Cao-side屯田 official.

## Current Profile

Hanji 823 gives Ren Jun as a comparative official type after Zhang Gang's Guangling pacification. The episode says Luoyang令 after [[WangHuanLateHan|王涣]] had generally fallen short, while Ren Jun selected civil and military subordinates according to their abilities and made wrongdoing quickly detectable.

The profile is stricter than the humane cases around Zhang Gang and Wu You. The source says Ren Jun's威禁 was stronger than Wang Huan's, but his literary/administrative refinement and political education were not as good. He therefore functions as a staffing-and-deterrence example rather than a full humane-governance exemplar.

## Key Characteristics

- Luoyang令 identified as a capable late-Han local administrator.
- Uses personnel fit across civil and military subordinates as his main administrative tool.
- Makes wrongdoing quickly detectable and strengthens deterrence in the capital city.
- Compared favorably to Wang Huan in severity and enforcement, but less favorably in refinement and education.
- Source-bounded figure kept distinct from the later Cao Cao屯田 Ren Jun.
