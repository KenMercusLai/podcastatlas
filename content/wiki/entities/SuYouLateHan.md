## Overview
[[SuYouLateHan]] is a Ye defender under [[YuanShang]] whose failed plan to coordinate with [[CaoCao]] from inside the city exposes the internal weakness of the Yuan family's capital defense.

## Current Profile
In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1061-yuanshao-zhizi-he-shafu-chouren-jiecheng-qinjia-lnsnj6ipiyscw-vopnjjgoxpnt5r|Hanji 1061]], Yuan Shang leaves Su You and [[ShenPeiLateHan]] to defend [[YeLateHan]] while he again attacks [[YuanTan]] at Pingyuan. Su You plans to respond from inside Ye for Cao Cao, but the plot is exposed, forcing him to flee to Cao's camp.

## Key Characteristics
- Occupies a city-defense role at Ye during Yuan Shang's renewed fraternal campaign.
- Represents internal fracture inside the Yuan defense.
- Converts from potential insider to open defector after the plot leaks.

## Evidence
- Insider plot and flight: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1061-yuanshao-zhizi-he-shafu-chouren-jiecheng-qinjia-lnsnj6ipiyscw-vopnjjgoxpnt5r|Hanji 1061]] says Su You intends to serve as Cao Cao's internal response at Ye, is exposed, and flees to Cao.

## Qualifications
The page records Su You only through the Ye-siege episode. His prior factional position and later fate are not established by current wiki evidence.
