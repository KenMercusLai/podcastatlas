# Doubao Work Partner / 豆包工作伙伴

## Overview

Doubao Work Partner / 豆包工作伙伴 is the renamed Feishu AI partner discussed in [[272-cong-feishu-jizuo-dao-agent-youxian-doubao-gongzuo-all-in-one-jinzhui-workbuddy-lqmfcnfkkhoxt440qy26vwpnvswp]]. The episode says the former Feishu AI partner Ali, knowledge Q&A, and chat entrances were merged or renamed under the Doubao Work line.

## Current Profile

The page treats Doubao Work Partner as a transitional product surface inside [[DoubaoWork|Doubao Work]] and [[Feishu]], not as a fully settled standalone product. Its importance is that it makes the product-integration state visible: the same named assistant can behave differently depending on whether the user enters from Feishu or from Doubao Work.

The source specifically notes a model-choice difference. From Feishu, the partner inherited Ali-like model selection; from Doubao Work, the same partner did not expose model choice. Eric suggests the difference may reflect who pays token cost in personal, free, low-price, or enterprise contexts.

## Key Characteristics

- Originates from Feishu's AI partner Ali according to the source.
- Functions as a bridge between Feishu's collaboration context and the Doubao Work product line.
- Exposes product-integration inconsistency because model choice differs by entry point.
- Illustrates the cost and packaging tension between personal and enterprise AI-office use.
