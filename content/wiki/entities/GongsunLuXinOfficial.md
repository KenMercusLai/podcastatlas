## Overview

公孙禄 / Gongsun Lu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-659-youxie-hexin-rencai-buyao-qingyi-dong-lg1nzal53ihdw-krici2thek-ij|Hanji 659]] as a former left general who tells [[WangMang|王莽]] that the late-[[XinDynasty|新朝]] crisis comes from internal policy and personnel failure. His current role is the truth-telling diagnostic voice whose treatment shows [[SuppressedRootCauseReporting|根因上报压制]] at court.

## Current Profile

Gongsun Lu's memorial names specific internal failures. The source has him criticize false astrology, distorted classics, the Wang-field and six-monopoly programs, concealment of popular suffering, and the decision to make Xiongnu a main enemy. He argues that the trouble is inside the regime rather than primarily outside it.

Wang Mang's response makes Gongsun Lu politically important despite the page's limited biography. Wang Mang does not accept the systemic diagnosis; he orders armed guards to remove Gongsun Lu and only demotes Lu Kuang as a partial scapegoat. The episode therefore uses Gongsun Lu to show a court where accurate diagnosis is treated as attack.

## Key Characteristics

- Former Xin general who delivers a crisis diagnosis to Wang Mang.
- Remonstrant who shifts blame from external enemies and rebels toward internal policy, personnel, and misinformation.
- Critic of omen manipulation, administrative deception, and harmful reform instruments.
- Truth-teller whose removal shows Wang Mang's inability to hear root-cause criticism.
- Source-scoped figure whose biography is currently limited to one episode.
