# 张角 / Zhang Jiao (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-923-chengnianrende-dingji-henjin-guzi-lide-juejue-lifzh-gohpqnmdznnfv72tndqf8h|Hanji 923]] backfills Zhang Jiao before the open [[YellowTurbansLateHan|Yellow Turban]] emergency. The episode places his rise inside epidemic and poverty pressure: commoners who cannot reliably get medical help become receptive to his healing activity, and [[TaipingDao|太平道]] turns that trust into [[ReliefBasedReligiousMobilization|救济型宗教动员]].

The same source adds the planned-uprising layer. Zhang Jiao organizes followers into thirty-six `fang`, uses the "苍天已死，黄天当立，岁在甲子，天下大吉" slogan and "甲子" gate markings, and relies on actors such as [[MaYuanyiLateHan|马元义]] before [[TangZhouLateHan|唐周]] exposes the plan. After the disclosure, Zhang Jiao accelerates the uprising and takes the title 天公将军 while [[ZhangBaoYellowTurban|张宝]] and [[ZhangLiangYellowTurban|张梁]] take parallel titles.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-928-zhichang-buke-mingshuo-song-dui-li-banshi-geng-shunli-lgup21lsa-u1ykaiyabcko2vhp0o|Hanji 928]] backfills Zhang Jiao before the main-force destruction narrated in Hanji 929. In this episode he has retreated into Guangzong after [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]] defeats the Yellow Turban field force, and Lu Zhi is preparing a siege that appears close to success.

The source's Zhang Jiao contribution is therefore indirect but important. His position becomes the military object whose treatment is altered by court politics: [[ZuoFengLateHan|左丰]]'s report gets Lu Zhi recalled before the next episode shows [[DongZhuo|董卓]] failing as replacement and [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]] later finishing the main suppression sequence.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] gives Zhang Jiao's death and aftermath inside the 184 CE Yellow Turban suppression sequence. The source says he has already died of illness before [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]] defeats [[ZhangLiangYellowTurban|张梁]], but the court still exhumes Zhang Jiao's body, decapitates it, and sends the head to [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]].

The same episode pairs his death with [[ZhangBaoYellowTurban|张宝]]'s defeat at Quyang, marking the main [[YellowTurbansLateHan|黄巾军]] uprising as basically pacified while leaving later remnants for the post-184 disorder field.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-932-weishenme-ren-yue-hao-yue-nan-dang-daguan-lvwzel782wcxs1lpt3rg2vmkdy29|Hanji 932]] uses Zhang Jiao's uprising as the background for post-Yellow-Turban armed proliferation. The episode does not retell [[TaipingDao|太平道]], but says that after Zhang Jiao launched the rebellion, scattered groups including [[ZhangNiujiaoLateHan|张牛角]] and Chu Feiyan / [[ZhangYanLateHan|张燕]] grew amid continuing late-Han disorder.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-935-ren-huo-yi-kou-qi-nan-cheng-de-shi-ziji-lmy8tyrttv-gjvke7sdi6dx-oopw|Hanji 935]] adds Zhang Jiao as the recent rebellion marker in [[LiuTaoLateHan|刘陶 / 刘桃]]'s warning to [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]. The episode does not retell the Yellow Turban movement; it uses Zhang Jiao's disorder as the first crisis in a chain that now includes [[BianZhangLateHan|边章]] and [[HanSui|韩遂]] moving danger toward [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-995-ta-zuishang-cibei-weihe-duoci-cansha-enren-llicukoezccd5iycin4uq7qun51k|Hanji 995]] later uses Zhang Jiao as a comparator for late-Han religious mobilization. The host mentions Zhang Jiao's [[TaipingDao|太平道]] alongside [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]]'s [[WudoumiDao|五斗米道]] while discussing how religious organization can become a mass political tool.

The episode does not equate Zhang Jiao with [[ZeRong|笮融]]. Zhang Jiao functions as a historical comparator showing that late-Han religious networks could mobilize people at scale, while Ze Rong is framed more narrowly as an actor who uses Buddhist display to cover personal plunder and murder.
