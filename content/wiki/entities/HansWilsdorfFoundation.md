# Hans Wilsdorf Foundation

The Hans Wilsdorf Foundation is the ownership structure through which [[HansWilsdorf]] placed his [[Rolex]] stake after his first wife's death. In [[rolex-1]], the foundation is presented as central to Rolex's patience, secrecy, charitable orientation, and independence from public-market pressure.

The episode uses the foundation to explain why Rolex could avoid short-term reactions during the quartz crisis. This makes the foundation a concrete case of [[FoundationOwnedBrandStewardship]] and an adjacent example to [[StrategyFollowsStructure]].
