## Overview
萱 / Xuan is a source-scoped late-Han figure identified in Hanji 847 as Deng Meng's mother. Her importance is not administrative office but trigger position: Liang Ji's attempt to absorb Deng Meng into the Liang family makes Xuan a target, and her survival after a failed assassination attempt gives Emperor Huan the immediate reason to mobilize against Liang.

## Current Profile
Hanji 847 places Xuan at the junction of harem favor and natal-family danger. After Deng Meng's father dies, Xuan's remarriage links the household to Sun Shou's circle, enabling Deng Meng's palace entry and later making Liang Ji's adoption or surname plan politically valuable.

Her agency enters the narrative after Bing Zun warns her not to accept Liang Ji's plan. Liang Ji kills Bing Zun and tries to kill Xuan, but a neighbor detects the assassin's approach, raises the alarm, and allows Xuan to flee to the palace. By taking the matter to Emperor Huan, Xuan turns a family survival crisis into the proximate trigger for the anti-Liang conspiracy.

## Key Characteristics
- Maternal link between Deng Meng's natal household and Emperor Huan's harem politics.
- Vulnerable intermediary created by remarriage ties to Sun Shou's network.
- Target of Liang Ji's attempted cleanup after resistance to his adoption or surname strategy.
- Survival witness whose palace appeal forces Emperor Huan to confront Liang Ji's threat.
- Source-scoped trigger figure rather than a fully documented officeholding actor.
