# Judy Gaines

Judy Gaines is the soft-commodities consultant interviewed in [[reeses-heir-vs-chocolate-skimpflation]]. She explains the commodity and regulatory side of the episode, including chocolate liquor, cocoa butter, legal milk-chocolate standards, and the price pressure created by a [[CocoaSupplyShock]].

Her role is to move the story from [[BradReese]]'s taste and label complaint to the economics of chocolate manufacturing. She says high cocoa costs can lead companies to raise prices, shrink packages, and reformulate, making [[Skimpflation]] a plausible interpretation even while [[Hershey]] also cites shape, size, and consumer-preference reasons.
