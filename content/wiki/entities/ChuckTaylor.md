# Chuck Taylor

Chuck Taylor appears in [[vans-kuangwei-fengguang-buzai-jingdian-fanbuxie-weishenme-maibudongle-1006742982]] as the person who helped turn [[Converse]] basketball shoes into a durable product and cultural name. The source says he joined Converse, wore the shoes while traveling through basketball training camps, collected player feedback, and helped improve the product.

In 1934, according to the episode, Taylor's signature was added to the shoe patch. That makes him part of the route by which a functional basketball shoe became a named product line, then later a youth, rock, skate, and street-culture object.
