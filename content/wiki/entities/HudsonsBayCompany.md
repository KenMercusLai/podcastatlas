# Hudson's Bay Company

## Overview

The Hudson's Bay Company is the chartered trading company through which the episode connects the [[BeaverFurTrade|beaver fur trade]] to corporate monopoly, territorial claims, inland exploration, and British imperial power in Canada.

## Current Profile

Charles II's 1670 charter granted the company control over Rupert's Land, described in the episode as roughly 40% of modern Canada. The charter did not immediately settle Anglo-French rivalry, but it supplied a corporate framework for turning access to waters draining into Hudson Bay into trade, mapping, and territorial authority. [[DavidThompsonExplorer|David Thompson]] later mapped vast areas while working for the company and a rival concern.

## Key Characteristics

- Chartered by Charles II in 1670.
- Received a vast claim known as Rupert's Land.
- Organized trade around waters flowing into Hudson Bay.
- Joined commercial monopoly to territorial and imperial authority.
- Employed explorers and surveyors whose knowledge expanded corporate reach.

## Qualifications

The source uses the company mainly to explain fur-trade expansion and imperial geography. It does not provide a complete corporate history, Indigenous treaty history, labor history, or account of how the Rupert's Land grant was contested and administered.
