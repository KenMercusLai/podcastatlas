## Overview

赵涉 / Zhao She is the Western Han adviser who warns [[ZhouYafu|周亚夫]] that the direct route toward the eastern campaign may contain a Wu ambush. He is kept on a disambiguated route from [[ZhaoShe|赵奢]], the Warring States general whose name has a different second character.

## Current Profile

In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-234-zhouyafu-zhanchang-dalian-hanjingdi-na-lai-de-danzi-li6iqhtuxgq5xwgbn_vb7lljgyxr|Hanji 234]], Zhao She reasons from [[LiuBiWuKing|刘濞]]'s wealth and access to committed fighters that the obvious road through passes and narrow terrain is exposed to ambush. He recommends traveling south through Lantian and Wuguan before turning toward Luoyang. Zhou accepts the longer route, then reportedly sends scouts back and finds an ambush where Zhao had predicted it.

The episode says Zhou promotes Zhao to a military advisory role and continues consulting him. The durable profile is narrow: Zhao identifies a route risk, offers a safer alternative, and gains authority when subsequent reconnaissance confirms the warning.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han campaign adviser associated with Zhou Yafu during the Seven States crisis.
- Infers ambush risk from enemy resources, likely intent, and constricted terrain.
- Recommends a longer southern route rather than treating speed as the only operational value.
- Adviser whose warning is reportedly tested through later reconnaissance rather than accepted only on reputation.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on one popular-history episode. Zhao She's title, exact route, wording, the identity and location of the alleged ambush, reconnaissance procedure, promotion, and later advisory work remain source-scoped. The page does not infer a broader biography or merge him with [[ZhaoShe|赵奢]].
