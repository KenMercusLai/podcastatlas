## Overview

第五种 / Fifth Zhong enters the wiki as the Yanzhou inspector in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-851-hanhuandi-weihe-zhuding-yichou-wannian-ltgm1ix6cgqb-kqo9bztfrzjgeun|Hanji 851]]. His source-bounded profile is an anti-corruption official whose report against [[ShanKuangLateHan|单匡]] and [[ShanChaoLateHan|单超]] is overtaken by palace retaliation, exile danger, and rescue by a former subordinate.

## Current Profile

Fifth Zhong sends [[WeiYuLateHan|魏宇]] to investigate Shan Kuang, the Jiyin administrator and Shan Chao's nephew. The investigation reports large corrupt wealth and impeaches the family network, but Shan Kuang hires [[RenFangLateHan|任芳]] to kill Wei Yu and then tries to extract Ren Fang from custody after the plot is detected.

The enforcement chain turns against Fifth Zhong. [[YangBingLateHan|杨秉]] asks to bring Shan Kuang to Luoyang for confrontation, yet the court punishes Yang Bing instead. [[ShusunWujiLateHan|叔孙无忌]] then raids Xu and Yan territory, and Shan Chao uses the disorder to frame Fifth Zhong on vague charges. Fifth Zhong is exiled to Shuofang, where Shan Chao's maternal grandson [[DongYuanLateHan|董元]] creates a fresh threat. [[SunBinFifthZhongRetainerLateHan|孙斌]] rescues him on the road, and Fifth Zhong remains in flight until an amnesty.

## Key Characteristics

- Yanzhou inspector who turns local corruption into a case against Shan Kuang and Shan Chao.
- Anti-corruption actor whose evidence channel is punished rather than protected.
- Target of opportunistic accusation after Shusun Wuji's disorder weakens local control.
- Exile victim whose danger follows him through Shan Chao's family network.
- Survival case showing why private rescue and flight can replace formal legal protection.
