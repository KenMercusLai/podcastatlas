# Rene Spitz / 史匹茨

Rene Spitz / 史匹茨 is cited in [[18-ruhe-shibie-taoli-bing-bimian-chengwei-jingshen-xixuegui-563629000]] during the episode's explanation of why emotional nourishment matters. The source refers to Spitz's observations about infants whose development was damaged when maternal affection and bodily contact were absent.

The episode uses this reference carefully: it supports the general claim that people need emotional care and response, while still distinguishing healthy dependency from [[PsychicVampirePersonality|精神吸血鬼人格]] and one-sided [[EmotionalEnergyExtraction|emotional extraction]].
