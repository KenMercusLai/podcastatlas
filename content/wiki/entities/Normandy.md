# Normandy

## Overview

Normandy is the French duchy targeted by [[HenryVOfEngland|Henry V]] as a historically resonant and strategically usable territorial base after Agincourt.

## Current Profile

Henry invoked Normandy's association with William the Conqueror while conducting a systematic campaign beginning in 1417. Caen, Falaise, and other positions fell before the long siege of Rouen.

Rouen's capture in January 1419 gave England control of a major city and access along the Seine, turning Henry's claim into territorial government and bringing direct pressure toward Paris.

## Key Characteristics

- Former duchy invoked through its connection to the Norman Conquest of England.
- Principal target of Henry's 1417 invasion.
- Territory acquired through sieges and town-by-town conquest.
- Strategic corridor whose control threatened Paris through the Seine.

## Qualifications

The episode focuses on military and dynastic strategy rather than Norman civilian society or the full administrative settlement. It does not establish that historical association alone legitimized conquest.

## What Changed

- Created Normandy as the territorial bridge between Agincourt's prestige and the advance on Paris.
