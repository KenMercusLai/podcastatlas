# Nick Sleep

Nick Sleep is the investor associated in [[costco-1]] and [[vanguard-1]] with the phrase "scale economies shared." The Costco episode uses the phrase to describe a retailer that returns purchasing power to members through lower prices, while the Vanguard episode uses it as a comparison for a financial institution returning scale benefits to fund investors.
