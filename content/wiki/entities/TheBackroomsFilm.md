# The Backrooms / 《后室》

## Overview

The Backrooms is an internet-born horror concept, a breakout 2022 short by [[KaneParsons]], and the basis for a feature film discussed by [[DianyingJubian|电影巨辩]].

## Current Profile

The source traces the concept from an anonymous image and “noclip” story through collaborative online elaboration to Parsons's short. Its distinctive force lies in [[LiminalSpaceHorror]]: yellow corridors, obsolete image texture, distorted sound, empty transitional architecture, and an apparently endless environment make space rather than character or monster the organizing threat.

The feature adaptation is presented as a difficult translation. The hosts argue that adding character psychology, trauma, plot, and conventional horror beats makes the project legible as commercial cinema but reduces the unexplained spatial experience that made the short powerful.

## Key Characteristics

- Internet-native myth assembled through anonymous posting, community elaboration, and video adaptation.
- Horror environment built from empty, familiar-yet-wrong transitional space.
- Uses found-footage and analogue-horror textures to destabilize time, place, and perceptual trust.
- Breakout proof of concept for [[KaneParsons]] and the [[InternetNativeFilmmakingPipeline]].
- Feature adaptation criticized in the source for explaining and narrativizing the original uncertainty.
