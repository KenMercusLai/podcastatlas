# 梁鹄 / Liang Hu (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-911-gudai-gongwuyuan-ye-neng-bao-fenpei-ls3pxu9xlpuulsdailx8tcoylv9e|Hanji 911]] adds a Hongdu and calligraphy branch to Liang Hu. The episode pairs him with [[ShiYiguanLateHan|师宜官]] as evidence that [[HongduMenxue|鸿都门学]] could gather real artistic talent even while its office channel was politically disputed, and it notes the later tradition that [[CaoCao|曹操]] admired his writing.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] later uses Liang Hu as the Liangzhou inspector who fears being implicated after [[SuZhengheLateHan|苏正和]] reports corruption in the Wuwei commandery field. Liang Hu considers killing Su Zhenghe and asks [[GaiXunLateHan|盖勋]] to discuss the matter.

Gai Xun blocks the plan by framing it as disloyal and inhumane: killing a subordinate to hide a problem would damage Liang Hu's public office, not merely settle a private dispute. The episode later says Gai Xun's action was "for Liang shijun," making Liang Hu the officeholder whose authority is protected from his own panic.
