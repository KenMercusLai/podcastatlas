# 梁鹄 / Liang Hu (late Han)

梁鹄 / Liang Hu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] as the Liangzhou inspector who fears being implicated after [[SuZhengheLateHan|苏正和]] reports corruption in the Wuwei commandery field. Liang Hu considers killing Su Zhenghe and asks [[GaiXunLateHan|盖勋]] to discuss the matter.

Gai Xun blocks the plan by framing it as disloyal and inhumane: killing a subordinate to hide a problem would damage Liang Hu's public office, not merely settle a private dispute. The episode later says Gai Xun's action was "for Liang shijun," making Liang Hu the officeholder whose authority is protected from his own panic.
