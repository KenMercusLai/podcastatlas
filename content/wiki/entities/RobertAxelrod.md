# Robert Axelrod

Robert Axelrod appears in [[79-gewei-lingdao-danfan-zan-xuedian-boyilun-690004262]] through the source's reference to 《合作的进化》. The episode uses his cooperation work to move [[PrisonersDilemma]] away from a purely cynical reading and toward repeat interaction, reciprocity, and [[TitForTatCooperation]].

His wiki role is to connect [[GameTheory]] to institutional optimism: humans can fall into strategic traps, but repeated play and legible feedback can make cooperation stable without assuming saints.
