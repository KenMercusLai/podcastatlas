# 晋幽公 / Duke You of Jin

晋幽公 / Duke You of Jin appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-2-lmfzo4sl9nd5kz3mrxdl1dl9efei]] as a comparison case for politically opaque assassination. The episode cites [[ZhushuJinian|《竹书纪年》]] for the claim that he was killed by bandits directed by his wife [[QinYing|秦嬴]].

The source uses Jin You Gong to support its broader caution about "盗贼杀君": a record may name the physical killers while leaving out the political actor who arranged or benefited from the killing.
