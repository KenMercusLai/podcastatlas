## Overview

李广 / Li Guang enters the wiki through Hanji 380-1 as the famous Western Han "Flying General" whose family reputation frames [[LiLingWesternHan|李陵]]'s later tragedy. The episode does not recount Li Guang's own campaigns; it uses him as Li Ling's grandfather and as the inherited military name behind Li Ling's active anti-[[Xiongnu|匈奴]] service.

## Current Profile

The source presents Li Guang as a reputational ancestor rather than as the direct actor in the episode. His significance is genealogical and symbolic: Li Ling's identity as Li Guang's grandson makes the later surrender harder to flatten into ordinary defection, because the listener is asked to read the event against a lineage of frontier martial honor.

## Key Characteristics

- Famous Western Han general remembered as "Flying General."
- Grandfather of Li Ling in the episode's account.
- Functions as inherited military reputation behind Li Ling's frontier service.
