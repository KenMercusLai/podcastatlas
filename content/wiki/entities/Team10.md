# Team 10

## Overview
Team 10 is the social-media creator organization [[JakePaul|Jake Paul]] says he built after becoming interested in startups.

## Current Profile
Paul presents Team 10 as a label-like system that used his audience and creator-development experience to help other personalities grow. In his narrative it became the prototype for later efforts to combine personal distribution with a wider talent roster.

## Key Characteristics
- Organized around social-media creators and audience growth.
- Used Paul's existing reach as shared distribution infrastructure.
- Treated creator development as a repeatable organizational activity.
- Serves as an antecedent to Paul's combat-sports promotion model.

## Evidence
- **Creator-development model:** [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-jake-paul-the-chainsmokers-turning-fame-into-funds-jake-enters-polit]] says Team 10 helped multiple creators gain millions of followers.
- **Later reuse:** [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-jake-paul-the-chainsmokers-turning-fame-into-funds-jake-enters-polit]] suggests Paul carried the audience-plus-roster logic into boxing and promotion.

## Qualifications
The source does not document Team 10's contracts, economics, participant outcomes, governance, or harms; the growth account is Paul's own retrospective.
