# Afghanistan

[[afghanistan-five-years-on-our-correspondent-visits-6a7ee547049c692a6b3ee0a9]] adds a reported present-day political snapshot from [[TomSass]]. The source says the [[Taliban]] has reduced visible violence and imposed order in Kabul, Herat and Kandahar, but that [[TalibanHardlineRule]], poverty, hunger, unemployment, fear of speech and restrictions on women and girls define the cost of that order.

The same source widens Afghanistan from first-person literary testimony into a policy and state-capacity case. [[HibatullahAkhundzada]]'s hardline consolidation, [[GirlsEducationWorkaround]] in a Kabul madrasa, [[ForcedRefugeeReturn]] from [[Iran]] and [[Pakistan]], and [[HumanitarianIsolation]] through aid cuts, sanctions, frozen reserves and investment deterrence all make the country's civilian crisis structural rather than only postwar.

Afghanistan enters the wiki through [[156-yige-afuhan-nvren-de-laixin-zhishang-de-guang-he-chuban-beihou-de-gushi-922362198]], where [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] discusses [[HadiyaHaidari|哈迪亚·海达里]]'s [[YiGeAfuhanNvrenDeLaixin|《一个阿富汗女人的来信》]]. The episode focuses on Afghan women's lives under war, regime change, patriarchal control, religious and social pressure, and family-level survival.

The source does not provide a comprehensive political history of Afghanistan. It uses the country as a lived setting where [[ProtectionAsControl]], [[WarGenderedCivilianHarm]], and [[AfghanWomenFirstPersonWriting]] become concrete through ordinary actions such as teaching, leaving home, riding in a bus, listening to music, wearing a burqa, going to court, and seeking refuge.
