# Beijing-Tianjin Intercity Railway / 京津城际铁路

The Beijing-Tianjin Intercity Railway is the project example in [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]] for why high-speed rail is a system, not only vehicles and tracks. The episode says the 2008 line required coordination across rolling stock, power, dispatching, communications, signaling, civil works, and standards.

In the source, the line illustrates [[HighSpeedRailSystemIntegration]]. Foreign suppliers could provide top subsystems, but the Chinese side still had to make them operate under a coherent architecture, which depended on earlier domestic experimentation and the organizational authority of the [[MinistryOfRailwaysChina]].
