# Uplane

## Overview
Uplane is an AI-driven marketing automation company discussed in [[selling-before-building-1m-arr-in-six-months]]. In the episode, [[JuliusKurfgen]] presents it as a system for automating marketing research, creative and landing-page production, cross-channel campaign operations, monitoring, and performance iteration.

## Current Profile
The current source frames Uplane less as a pure self-serve SaaS tool than as a software company using a [[ManagedServiceAutomationLayer]]. It sells marketing outcomes to customers that often want someone to run the workflow, while engineers continue automating the underlying research, creative, publishing, and measurement stack.

Uplane's strategic posture is built around speed and feedback. It began with discovery-led outreach, one-week demo sprints, paid validation, fast founder responsiveness, and later enterprise work with guardrails. Its strongest current wiki role is therefore as a bridge between [[PreProductSelling]], [[AutomatedPerformanceMarketing]], [[ServiceProductization]], and [[AtomicContentGuardrails]].

## Key Characteristics
- Automates full-funnel paid-marketing work, not only ad copy or isolated creative generation.
- Targets companies with enough ad spend and performance data to support fast testing and iteration.
- Uses human account managers and AI marketing strategists where customers want outcomes rather than direct software operation.
- Prices with a fixed operating-cost layer plus variable performance-linked fees, often tied to managed ad spend.
- Treats brand rules, compliance constraints, approved content, evaluations, and human review as necessary for enterprise AI marketing.

## Evidence
- Full-funnel automation: [[selling-before-building-1m-arr-in-six-months]] says Uplane automates research, ad and landing-page production, cross-channel publishing, monitoring, and iteration on winning or losing assets.
- Customer profile: [[selling-before-building-1m-arr-in-six-months]] says Uplane works best for companies spending more than $100,000 per month on ads because testing needs enough budget and data.
- Delivery model: [[selling-before-building-1m-arr-in-six-months]] says many clients do not want to use software directly, so Uplane uses account managers and AI marketing strategists to operate the platform.
- Pricing and guardrails: [[selling-before-building-1m-arr-in-six-months]] describes fixed-plus-variable pricing, daily reporting, brand guardrails, code/evaluation checks, and account-manager review before ads go live.

## Qualifications
The source does not independently verify Uplane's ARR, margin profile, churn, exact attribution methodology, or customer performance lift. Its customer-results claims should be treated as founder-reported and source-scoped until corroborated by operating data or additional sources.
