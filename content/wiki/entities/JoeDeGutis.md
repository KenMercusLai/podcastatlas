# Joe DeGutis

## Overview

Joe DeGutis is the cognitive neuroscientist in [[face-value-face-value|Face Value]] who explains prosopagnosia and oversees the laboratory assessment reported for [[LucySullivan]].

## Current Profile

DeGutis describes face blindness as a “stealth disorder” because recognition failures may initially look like inattentiveness, indifference, or another condition. His key distinction is between seeing a face and identifying the person: someone may perceive features, emotion, and attractiveness while failing to retrieve identity.

He also supplies the episode's high-end assessment. After Sullivan completes a multi-test battery, DeGutis describes her as the complete super-recognizer package. The episode uses his contribution to connect individual stories to a spectrum model without treating anecdote alone as equivalent to controlled performance.

## Key Characteristics

- Cognitive neuroscientist explaining human face recognition for a general audience.
- Distinguishes facial perception from identity recognition.
- Frames prosopagnosia as easy to misread socially before it is recognized clinically.
- Oversees or represents the laboratory battery used to assess Sullivan.
- Keeps super-recognition tied to performance across multiple tasks rather than one memorable anecdote.
