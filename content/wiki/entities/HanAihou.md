# 韩哀侯 / Marquis Ai of Han

韩哀侯 / Marquis Ai of Han first appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]] through the episode's [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] variant of the [[NieZheng|聂政]] story. In that version, [[XiaLei|侠累]] and Han Aihou are both present at a large meeting, Xia Lei holds onto the ruler while fleeing, and Nie Zheng kills both.

The page is deliberately source-scoped. Its first role was to preserve the variant account so later sources could compare whether the assassination belongs to a ministerial feud, a ruler-killing crisis, or a mixed transmission.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl]] gives Han Aihou a direct succession notice: [[HanWenhou|韩文侯]] dies in 377 BCE and his son Han Aihou succeeds. That later source stabilizes his place in the Han ruler sequence even while the earlier assassination variant remains a separate source problem.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] makes Han Aihou the ruler under whom [[HanState|韩国]] destroys [[ZhengState|郑国]] in 375 BCE and moves its capital to Xinzheng. The episode reads that achievement as the visible result of [[GenerationalFamilyStrategy|several generations of Han pressure]], not as Han Aihou's isolated accomplishment.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-2-lmnqfjmydygqnfec6xhzpixralvi]] gives Han Aihou's death in the [[Shiji|《史记》]] / [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] branch: [[YanZhongzi|严遂]] sends an assassin against [[HanGui|韩廆]], and Han Aihou is killed while protecting Han Gui. The episode explicitly compares this with the earlier [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] / [[NieZheng|聂政]] variant rather than forcing the wiki to choose one merged account.
