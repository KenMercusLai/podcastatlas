# 韩哀侯 / Marquis Ai of Han

韩哀侯 / Marquis Ai of Han appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]] only through the episode's [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] variant of the [[NieZheng|聂政]] story. In that version, [[XiaLei|侠累]] and Han Aihou are both present at a large meeting, Xia Lei holds onto the ruler while fleeing, and Nie Zheng kills both.

The page is deliberately source-scoped. Its role is to preserve the variant account so later sources can compare whether the assassination belongs to a ministerial feud, a ruler-killing crisis, or a mixed transmission.
