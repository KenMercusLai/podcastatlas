# Annabella Milbank

## Overview

Annabella Milbank, later Lady Byron, was the mathematically gifted and religiously serious wife of [[LordByron|Lord Byron / 拜伦]] and the mother of [[AdaLovelace|Ada Lovelace / 艾达·洛夫莱斯]].

## Current Profile

[[442-lord-byron-dangerous-liaisons-part-3-glt1176702097]] portrays Annabella as initially resistant to Byron's celebrity but drawn into correspondence by a Christian hope that she might reform him. She rejected his first proposal, accepted a later one, and entered a marriage partly promoted by [[LadyMelbourne|Lady Melbourne]] as a way to contain the crises around [[LadyCarolineLamb|Caroline Lamb]] and [[AugustaLeigh|Augusta Leigh]].

The containment strategy failed. The episode describes Byron's cruelty, theatrical fatalism, financial disorder, drinking, sexual distress, and conspicuous attachment to Augusta as sources of Annabella's fear and suspicion. After Ada's birth, Annabella left with the child, sought medical and family advice, and pursued formal separation; her testimony is indispensable to the crisis but remains testimony rather than transparent access to Byron's mind.

## Key Characteristics

- Pious, analytically minded aristocrat presented as mathematically gifted.
- Initially skeptical observer of Byron's celebrity and character.
- Correspondent motivated partly by a moral-reform or redemptive project.
- Wife placed inside an elite strategy for containing scandal.
- Mother who left the marriage with Ada and helped initiate formal separation.

## Evidence

- Courtship and reform: [[442-lord-byron-dangerous-liaisons-part-3-glt1176702097]] describes Annabella's initial refusal, continued correspondence, and stated Christian duty to redeem Byron.
- Marriage crisis: [[442-lord-byron-dangerous-liaisons-part-3-glt1176702097]] follows the 1815 wedding, disastrous honeymoon, Six Mile Bottom visit, London household, debt, drinking, and conflict around Augusta.
- Departure and separation: [[442-lord-byron-dangerous-liaisons-part-3-glt1176702097]] records Ada's birth, Annabella's search of Byron's belongings, medical consultation, January 1816 departure, and the separation demand.

## Qualifications

The episode relies in part on Annabella's reports and later recollection. Her fear and the marriage's collapse are well established within the source, but reported dialogue, retrospective diagnosis, Byron's intentions, and the evidentiary weight of objects found among his belongings remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Created a profile distinguishing Annabella's own intellectual and religious outlook from her role in Byron's marriage and separation.

## Relationships

- [[LordByron]] - husband whose conduct and reputation made the marriage untenable.
- [[AdaLovelace]] - daughter whom Annabella took with her on leaving Byron.
- [[LadyMelbourne]] - aunt who promoted the marriage as a containment strategy.
- [[LadyCarolineLamb]] - earlier lover whose affair helped motivate the proposed match.
- [[AugustaLeigh]] - Byron's half-sister and central source of marital suspicion.
