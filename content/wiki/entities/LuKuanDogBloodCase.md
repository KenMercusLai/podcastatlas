## Overview

Lu Kuan / 吕宽 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-you-xinji-de-ren-you-duo-kepa-lnhkk70905eqg4lqwh3mp2sxzamc|Hanji 616]] as [[WangYuXinPrince|王宇]]'s wife's brother and the person who physically carries out the dog-blood act at [[WangMang|王莽]]'s gate.

## Current Profile

The source gives Lu Kuan a narrow but decisive role. Wang Yu, Wu Zhang, and Lu Kuan want to help [[EmpressDowagerWeiPingdi|卫太后]] reach [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]]. When memorial persuasion fails, Lu Kuan executes Wu Zhang's plan by spilling dog blood at Wang Mang's gate at night.

The act is discovered quickly, making Lu Kuan the exposed operational link that pulls Wang Yu and Wu Zhang into the case. A variant account has him flee to Guanghan and encounter Lou Hu before being arrested and returned to Chang'an. He is soon killed, and his household is exiled to Hepu.

## Key Characteristics

- Wife's brother of Wang Yu, linking Wang Yu's household to the operation.
- Direct executor of the dog-blood act.
- Operational weak point through whom the case is exposed.
- Fugitive in the Guanghan variant before Lou Hu sends him back.
- Punished quickly, with family exile extending the case beyond his own death.
