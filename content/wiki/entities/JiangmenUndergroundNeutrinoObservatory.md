# 江门中微子实验 / Jiangmen Underground Neutrino Observatory

The Jiangmen Underground Neutrino Observatory is a central project case in [[ep248-zai-gaoyuan-yu-dixia-tanxun-zhongguo-da-kexue-xiangmu-lidn7xxkcw4-66fw-y50fqqacwo]]. The episode describes it as a 700-meter underground laboratory built to study neutrinos while reducing cosmic-ray interference.

The project makes [[WeakSignalDetection|weak-signal detection]] concrete. The source explains that neutrino experiments need both a controlled signal source and a quiet environment, so the Jiangmen site combines underground shielding, proximity to nuclear reactors, a roughly 55-60 km reactor-distance baseline, and a large detector structure.

The episode's strongest physical image is the detector system: a giant sphere intended for tens of thousands of tons of liquid scintillator, placed inside a large high-purity-water pool. [[MiaoQian|苗千]] also emphasizes the engineering reality around the science, including underground excavation, rock conditions, and much higher-than-expected water pumping.
