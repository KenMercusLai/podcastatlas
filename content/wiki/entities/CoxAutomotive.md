# Cox Automotive

Cox Automotive appears in [[tech-20260407-0407-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260407-0407-mp-tech-pod-128]] through [[StephanieValdezStreaty]]. The episode uses the firm as an automotive-market source for the claim that [[UnitedStates]] EV demand is constrained primarily by sticker price.

Its role in the wiki is narrow but useful: Cox Automotive anchors the buyer-side diagnosis before the episode turns to battery chemistry, [[China]]'s supply chain, and expected [[ElectricVehiclePriceParity]].

[[tech-20251231-1231-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251231-1231-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds Cox Automotive as the data source for the post-credit sales swing and price-gap comparison. The source says monthly U.S. EV sales fell nearly 50% in October 2025 compared with September, while new EVs remained more than $9,000 above combustion vehicles and used EVs were about $2,700 above used gas cars.
