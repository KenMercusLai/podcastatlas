# Accelerate (Hacker House)

Accelerate is a hacker-house project discussed in [[tanfang-hacker-house-guigu-nianqingren-zhengzai-banjin-ai-chuangye-sushe-s10e10-f0870843-fd16-49f5-8f24-80281f9fff77]] as the rent-and-community side of the category. The source says [[PackySanDiego]] and [[DannyMorgan]] started with 15 San Francisco rooms and built attention partly through playful events, including a Mark Zuckerberg-themed party.

The episode treats Accelerate as meaningfully different from [[TheResidency]], [[HF0]], and [[AGIHouse]] because it does not center equity capture. Its revenue story is closer to rent plus talent services: the founders believe helping people find jobs or helping companies hire can become a real business alongside housing. The later "Hacker Hotel" on Market Street, described as roughly 120 rooms, makes Accelerate a concrete case for [[HackerHouseStartupInfrastructure]] without turning immediately into a fund.
