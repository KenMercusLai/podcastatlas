# 宗庆后 / Zong Qinghou

宗庆后 appears in [[bu-xi-deng-e02-bi-quan-shan-beng-mei-guo-zheng-fu-guan-men-first-brands-po-chan-wa-ha-ha-feng-bo-lt-ghzgmiroo9a-odaih4xkhsulu]] as the founder whose personal authority long helped hold [[WahahaGroup|娃哈哈]]'s complex ownership, employee, state, family, and channel relationships together.
