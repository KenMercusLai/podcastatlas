# 罗子君 / Luo Zijun

## Overview
罗子君 / Luo Zijun enters the wiki as the heroine of [[MyFirstHalfLife|《我的前半生》]], whose divorce, work return, and claimed independence anchor EP385's novel-versus-drama comparison.

## Current Profile
In the episode, Luo Zijun is less a simple inspirational heroine than a test case for what counts as independence. The hosts read the novel's Zijun as flawed but gradually rebuilt through work, women, and a colder middle-age understanding; they read the TV drama's Zijun as more spectacular but less convincing because her rise depends heavily on [[HeHan|贺涵]] and a loosened realism around career reentry.

## Key Characteristics
- Divorced heroine moving from household dependence toward paid work and self-rebuilding.
- Adaptation split between female-supported growth in the novel and male-mentor support in the drama.
- Character whose early TV styling and consumption mark immaturity and class privilege.
- Work-return figure used to discuss full-time homemaking, career gaps, and practical independence.
- Middle-age subject whose novel arc ends with craft labor, bodily cost, and a cooler view of love.
