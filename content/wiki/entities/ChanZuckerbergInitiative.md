# Chan Zuckerberg Initiative

## Overview
The Chan Zuckerberg Initiative (CZI) is the philanthropic organization founded by [[PriscillaChan|Priscilla Chan]] and [[MarkZuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]] and presented in the source as a long-horizon builder and funder of biomedical research infrastructure.

## Current Profile
CZI's stated science mission is to help scientists cure, prevent, or manage all diseases by the end of the century. The episode does not frame CZI as the organization that will deliver every cure. Its operating model combines research grants, open dissemination, reusable software and hardware, large biological datasets, nonprofit AI compute, patient-led rare-disease capacity, and the [[ChanZuckerbergBiohubNetwork|Biohub network]].

The strongest coherent strategy is [[BiomedicalResearchToolInfrastructure]]: improve what scientists can observe, organize, query, and model, then let a wider research and translation ecosystem use those capabilities. [[Cellxgene]], imaging software, microscopy hardware, cell atlases, and proposed [[VirtualCellWorldModel|virtual-cell models]] are different layers of that shared infrastructure.

## Key Characteristics
- Pursues a century-scale disease mission through broad scientific enablement rather than a single therapeutic pipeline.
- Funds scientists while also building software, hardware, data, compute, and institutional research capacity.
- Encourages rapid and open scientific dissemination, including preprints.
- Uses Biohubs for focused interdisciplinary work spanning institutions.
- Supports patient-led rare-disease organizations as research and translation infrastructure.
- Leaves much direct therapeutic implementation to specialized companies and other actors.

## Evidence
- Mission and scope — [[curing-all-human-diseases-the-future-of-health-technology-mark-zuckerberg-dr-priscilla-chan-scim6005948638]] states the cure, prevent, or manage goal and limits CZI's own role to enabling the wider scientific community.
- Shared tools — [[curing-all-human-diseases-the-future-of-health-technology-mark-zuckerberg-dr-priscilla-chan-scim6005948638]] names [[Cellxgene]], Napari, microscopy work, phase plates, cell atlases, and AI compute as examples of reusable infrastructure.
- Institutional design — [[curing-all-human-diseases-the-future-of-health-technology-mark-zuckerberg-dr-priscilla-chan-scim6005948638]] describes Biohubs as cross-university, interdisciplinary teams organized around problems that exceed one laboratory.
- Translation ecosystem — [[curing-all-human-diseases-the-future-of-health-technology-mark-zuckerberg-dr-priscilla-chan-scim6005948638]] distinguishes basic science and tool-building from direct therapeutic development and describes patient organizations as bridges to researchers and drug developers.

## Qualifications
The mission, examples, and expected leverage are organization-founder claims in a supportive interview. The source does not supply independent outcome evaluation, cost-effectiveness evidence, governance analysis, or proof that tool-building will produce disease progress at the stated scale or pace.

## What Changed
- Created a canonical page for CZI's biomedical research strategy.
- Defined tool, data, compute, institution, and patient-network layers as one enablement model.
- Kept the century-scale mission separate from demonstrated outcomes.
