# 庞统 / Pang Tong

庞统 / Pang Tong enters this wiki branch through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1027-jingzhou-zhizhu-liubiao-weihe-nan-cheng-bazhu-ll-ebguqllsf-5ycvpdogihzn3lj|Hanji 1027]] as one of the younger Jingzhou talents the host says is affected by [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s failure to use corrective advice well. The episode does not narrate Pang Tong's career directly; it uses him as part of the later talent-flow consequence after [[DengXiLateHan|邓羲]] leaves Liu Biao's circle.

In this source, Pang Tong is paired with [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] and [[LiuBei|刘备]] to show how a region can preserve talent socially while failing to convert it into loyalty or strategy for the incumbent ruler. That makes the entry source-scoped and tied to [[FenceSittingStrategicExposure|骑墙式战略暴露]] rather than a full biography.
