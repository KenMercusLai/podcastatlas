# Ian Hamilton

## Overview

Ian Hamilton enters the wiki through [[675-the-first-world-war-slaughter-at-gallipoli-part-5-glt4394132715]] as the British general assigned to command the land operation after the Dardanelles naval attack failed.

## Current Profile

The episode does not present Hamilton as a simple incompetent. It describes him as intelligent and cultured, but badly constrained by the decision chain above him: he received an almost impossible task, with about a month to plan a modern amphibious landing against prepared Ottoman defenses.

Hamilton's profile therefore belongs to [[FailedStrategicShortcutAttritionTrap]]. Once a naval shortcut failed, land command had to improvise a solution under time pressure, terrain uncertainty, and modern defensive firepower.

## Key Characteristics

- Hamilton was chosen to command the Gallipoli land operation.
- He had about a month to plan an unprecedented amphibious landing.
- The episode frames him as constrained rather than merely personally incompetent.
- His force mixed British, French, Royal Naval Division, and ANZAC troops.
- After the first day, he ordered the ANZACs to dig in when evacuation was not physically possible.

## Evidence

- Command assignment: [[675-the-first-world-war-slaughter-at-gallipoli-part-5-glt4394132715]] says Hamilton was chosen to command the land operation after the naval failure.
- Planning constraint: [[675-the-first-world-war-slaughter-at-gallipoli-part-5-glt4394132715]] says he had about a month to plan an unprecedented amphibious landing against modern weapons.
- First-day decision: [[675-the-first-world-war-slaughter-at-gallipoli-part-5-glt4394132715]] says naval officers told Hamilton immediate ANZAC evacuation was not physically possible, after which he ordered them to dig in and hold.
