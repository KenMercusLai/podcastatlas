# Casualty Actuarial Society

The Casualty Actuarial Society appears in [[data-risk-and-actuarial-science-in-insurance]] when [[MaryPatCampbell]] discusses the historical relationship between casualty actuarial work and statistics. The source uses the organization to argue that data science and actuarial practice have long overlapped, even if today's terminology emphasizes AI and machine learning.

Its wiki role is to mark the property-and-casualty side of [[ActuarialScience]]. The episode contrasts that side's shorter policy periods and faster claims feedback with the longer-duration mortality and annuity problems behind [[MortalityRiskPricing]].
