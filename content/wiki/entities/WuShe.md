# 武涉 / Wu She

武涉 / Wu She enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-165-1-liubang-zheju-hua-chadian-nongdiu-dahan-jiangshan-llxgisomsdo8qjeecgdg6frri-xq|Hanji 165-1]] as [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s persuader after [[LongJuChuGeneral|龙且]] dies at [[WeishuiBattle|潍水之战]]. Xiang Yu sends him to [[Linzi|临淄]] because [[HanXin|韩信]]'s control of [[QiState|齐国]] has made Han Xin the actor who can decide the Chu-Han war.

Wu She's argument has two layers. First, he tells Han Xin that [[LiuBang|刘邦]] is greedy, covenant-breaking, and likely to swallow other kings after defeating Chu. Second, he reframes Han Xin's safety as dependent on Xiang Yu's continued existence: if Chu disappears, Han Xin's autonomous Qi power becomes Liu Bang's next problem.

The source uses Wu She to introduce [[TripartiteBalanceStrategy|三分天下式制衡]]. Han Xin can tilt west and make Liu Bang win, tilt east and keep Xiang Yu alive, or preserve Qi as a third pole. Wu She fails because Han Xin answers from gratitude and remembered treatment: Xiang Yu ignored him, while Liu Bang trusted and promoted him.
