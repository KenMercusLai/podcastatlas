# 伍琼 / Wu Qiong (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-949-lishishang-de-dongzhuo-daodi-duo-canbao-lk6idogwmc54nk1sdfc5wonyvgbb|Hanji 949]] adds Wu Qiong's earlier recruitment-channel role before the fatal relocation dispute. The source names him with [[ZhouBiLateHan|周毖]], [[ZhengTaiLateHan|郑泰]], and [[HeYongLateHan|何颙]] as one of the officials used by [[DongZhuo|董卓]] to remove corrupt officials and bring respected people into office.

That makes Wu Qiong's later execution more than a simple dissent case. In the source sequence, Dong Zhuo first needs officials like Wu Qiong to make rule look reparative, then treats disagreement as punishable once the same officials no longer support his direction.

伍琼 / Wu Qiong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-951-tianjiang-hengcai-jiujing-shi-haoshi-haishi-huaishi-lovwgng2wrtfno-s9ttpybbhksvh|Hanji 951]] as the city-gate commandant who opposes [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s forced relocation from [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] to [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]]. The source summary renders him as "吴琼"; the wiki normalizes the name to 伍琼 while treating the transcript spelling as source-scoped.

His significance is the lethal limit of court opposition. After [[YangBiao|杨彪]] and [[HuangWanLateHan|黄琬]] are removed, Wu Qiong and [[ZhouBiLateHan|周毖]] are arrested and executed for resisting the move. Hanji 951 therefore makes him a compact case of feedback turning into punishment under [[CoerciveCapitalRelocation|coercive capital relocation]].
