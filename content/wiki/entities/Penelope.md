# Penelope

## Overview

Penelope is [[Odysseus]]' wife in [[TheOdyssey]], treated in [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] as the household's central figure of delay, testing, and marital recognition.

## Current Profile

In this source, Penelope is not only the prize waiting at the end of the journey. She preserves room for Odysseus' return by delaying remarriage through the shroud trick, questions the disguised beggar for evidence, proposes the bow contest, and finally tests the returned man through the secret of the immovable marriage bed.

## Key Characteristics

- Penelope maintains the household's unresolved status by delaying the suitors without openly defeating them.
- Her shroud trick turns domestic labor into a political tactic against forced remarriage.
- Her handling of the beggar and bow contest leaves deliberate ambiguity over how much she suspects before the final recognition.
- Her marriage-bed test makes private household knowledge the final proof of Odysseus' identity.

## Evidence

- Delaying remarriage: [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] recounts Penelope weaving Laertes' shroud by day and undoing it at night to hold off the suitors.
- Ambiguous recognition: [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] notes the hosts' suggestion that Penelope may already suspect the beggar is Odysseus before the bow contest.
- Contest design: [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] has Penelope propose marriage to whoever can string Odysseus' bow and shoot through twelve axe heads.
- Final proof: [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] makes the living-olive-tree bed the private sign that lets Penelope accept Odysseus.

## Qualifications

The source keeps Penelope's knowledge ambiguous. The page should not flatten her into either passive fidelity or complete secret control; the episode's value is that it preserves uncertainty around timing, recognition, and marital testing.
