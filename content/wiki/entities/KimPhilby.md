# Kim Philby / 金·菲尔比

Kim Philby / 金·菲尔比 appears in [[80-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-shang-691866649]] as a famous Soviet source inside British intelligence and one of the spy legends surrounding the [[KGB]] center. The episode uses him as background for the world [[OlegGordievsky|Oleg Gordievsky]] entered: intelligence services lived with institutional memory of penetration, betrayal, and reciprocal fear.

Philby's role in this source is mainly comparative. He represents earlier Soviet success against British intelligence, while Gordievsky later becomes a reverse case in which a KGB insider gives [[MI6]] access to Soviet secrets.
