# 蓝田 / Lantian (Late Qin)

蓝田 / Lantian (Late Qin) is the Lantian-area battlefield in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-138-wudu-qiannian-de-lishi-zhenxiang-zhaogao-bushi-taijian-2-lvtievjrclb1lxxxxr6k-q7kryto|Qinji 138 part 2]], where [[LiuBang|刘邦]] defeats Qin forces after bypassing [[Yaoguan|峣关]]. This page is disambiguated from the earlier [[LantianBattle|蓝田之战]], the Warring States Chu-Qin campaign reached through [[Wuguan|武关]].

In the late-Qin branch, Lantian marks the final narrowing of [[QinState|秦国]]'s military space. [[ZhangLiang|张良]] first helps turn Yaoguan into a psychological and negotiation problem, then Liu Bang moves around the pass, crosses mountain terrain, defeats Qin south of Lantian, and wins again north of Lantian.

## Key Claims
- This Lantian node belongs to Liu Bang's final Qin-collapse campaign, not the older Chu-Qin Lantian Battle.
- The episode treats victories south and north of Lantian as evidence that Ziying's post-Zhao-Gao recovery has no time to mature.
- Lantian completes the Wuguan-Yaoguan sequence: Qin's gateway problem becomes a battlefield defeat near the heartland.
