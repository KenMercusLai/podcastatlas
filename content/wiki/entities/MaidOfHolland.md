# Maid of Holland

## Overview

The Maid of Holland, or Hollandia, is a female personification of Holland and the [[DutchRepublic]] whose early-modern imagery joined liberty, defended territory, martial virtue, domestic order, and prosperity.

## Current Profile

In [[276-netherlands-the-maid-of-holland-glt8981353378]], the Maid first appears in the political world of the [[DutchRevolt]]. A rebel medal places her in a fenced garden with the words *libertas* and *patria*, adapting older enclosed-garden imagery while adding the liberty cap and classical-martial attributes. Her visual meaning is therefore composite rather than doctrinally pure.

The later domestic turn does not erase the military one. A 1615 pamphlet places her in a fertile garden protected by a Dutch lion against Spanish forces and praises Dutch housekeeping; other imagery can replace the spear with a mop. Through [[DutchDomesticCleanlinessCivicOrder]], the woman, house, garden, city, dike, church, and republic become nested spaces whose order must be maintained.

## Key Characteristics

- Female embodiment of Holland, liberty, homeland, and republican independence.
- Appears inside a fenced garden that signifies enclosure, protection, fertility, and ordered space.
- Combines Christian inheritance with a liberty cap and classical associations of wisdom and martial virtue.
- Draws patriotic force from women-resistance memory, especially the legend of [[KenauSimonsdochterHasselaer]].
- Moves between weapons and cleaning tools without losing political meaning.
- Makes gendered household labor stand for civic, religious, and national discipline.

## Evidence

- Revolt origin and symbolic mixture: [[276-netherlands-the-maid-of-holland-glt8981353378]] describes the rebel medal, *libertas*, *patria*, fenced garden, liberty cap, and classical-martial associations.
- Martial memory: [[276-netherlands-the-maid-of-holland-glt8981353378]] connects the Maid to Kenau's expanding Haarlem legend and to women defying Spain.
- Domestic-national turn: [[276-netherlands-the-maid-of-holland-glt8981353378]] uses the 1615 pamphlet, Dutch lion, fertile garden, mop, and housekeeping language to make domestic order politically legible.

## Qualifications

The episode reconstructs the figure through selected medals, pamphlets, travel reports, and later interpretation rather than a complete iconographic corpus. The Maid's Christian, classical, martial, and domestic meanings may not have been equally present for every maker or audience. Symbolic honor for feminized housekeeping does not establish equal status for actual women.

## What Changed

- Created the entity as a composite revolt, liberty, domestic-order, and prosperity symbol.
- Preserved the tension between positive female symbolism and gendered domestic expectations.

## Relationships

- [[DutchRepublic]] - polity whose liberty, prosperity, and order the Maid personifies.

- [[KenauSimonsdochterHasselaer]] - martial female memory associated with the allegory.
