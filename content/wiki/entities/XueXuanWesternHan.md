## Overview

薛宣 / Xue Xuan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-540-taihou-de-zangli-lnd6gmr_bk7dv8cbb1zzoagtbwsd|Hanji 540]] as the Western Han chancellor dismissed by [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] after [[QiongchengTaihouWesternHan|邛成太后]]'s rushed funeral. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-539-hanchengdi-yu-zhangfang-de-qinggan-jiuge-lk_wzo4h-c-xkipffhgikronrk3m|Hanji 539]] adds an immediately preceding family-adjacent impeachment layer: Xue joins [[ZhaiFangjinWesternHan|翟方进]] in impeaching [[ZhangFangWesternHan|张放]], the son of Xue's wife [[JingwuPrincessWesternHan|靖武公主]] by an earlier marriage.

## Current Profile

The source gives Xue Xuan a narrow but institutionally useful role. He is not developed through a full biography; he appears as the top minister held responsible when Chengdi decides that Qiongcheng Taihou's funeral was too hurried and improperly handled. His dismissal makes the episode's accountability logic concrete: ritual neglect can be charged upward to the officials responsible for state ceremony.

His replacement also qualifies the lesson. [[ZhaiFangjinWesternHan|翟方进]], who was also punished in the same funeral affair, soon becomes chancellor. Xue Xuan therefore marks the loss-of-office side of a personnel reshuffle whose moral logic is not reducible to who was punished most visibly at first.

Hanji 539 gives Xue an earlier and more ambiguous role. Zhang Fang is not Xue's biological son, but Xue is connected to him through Jingwu Princess. When Wang Zhengjun and the outer-relative channel pressure ministers to remove Zhang Fang from Chengdi's side, Xue participates in the impeachment. The source reads this as more than "大义灭亲": Xue avoids tying his household to a favorite who has become politically toxic, and his participation may help keep the result to outward reassignment rather than harsher punishment.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han chancellor under Han Chengdi in the Hanji 540 funeral case.
- Held accountable for rushed handling of Qiongcheng Taihou's burial.
- Family-adjacent impeacher of Zhang Fang after dowager pressure.
- Dismissed from office in winter of Yongshi 2 / 15 BCE.
- Serves as the contrast point for Zhai Fangjin's later promotion despite shared involvement in the case.

Dismissal:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-540-taihou-de-zangli-lnd6gmr_bk7dv8cbb1zzoagtbwsd|Hanji 540]] says Xue Xuan was removed from the chancellorship in the eleventh month.

Personnel contrast:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-540-taihou-de-zangli-lnd6gmr_bk7dv8cbb1zzoagtbwsd|Hanji 540]] says Zhai Fangjin, also punished in the funeral affair, was then promoted to chancellor.

Zhang Fang impeachment:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-539-hanchengdi-yu-zhangfang-de-qinggan-jiuge-lk_wzo4h-c-xkipffhgikronrk3m|Hanji 539]] says Xue Xuan and Zhai Fangjin jointly impeach Zhang Fang after Wang Zhengjun's pressure.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-539-hanchengdi-yu-zhangfang-de-qinggan-jiuge-lk_wzo4h-c-xkipffhgikronrk3m|Hanji 539]] says the host reads Xue's action as family self-protection because Zhang Fang is Jingwu Princess's son but not Xue's biological son.

## Qualifications

This page does not provide Xue Xuan's complete career or legal dossier. It preserves Hanji 540's source-scoped role in the Qiongcheng Taihou funeral accountability sequence and Hanji 539's interpretation of his Zhang Fang impeachment as family-adjacent political cutting. Xue's private intent, the exact legal basis of the charges against Zhang Fang, and his full marital chronology remain source-scoped.
