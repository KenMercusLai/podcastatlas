## Overview

薛宣 / Xue Xuan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-540-taihou-de-zangli-lnd6gmr_bk7dv8cbb1zzoagtbwsd|Hanji 540]] as the Western Han chancellor dismissed by [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] after [[QiongchengTaihouWesternHan|邛成太后]]'s rushed funeral.

## Current Profile

The source gives Xue Xuan a narrow but institutionally useful role. He is not developed through a full biography; he appears as the top minister held responsible when Chengdi decides that Qiongcheng Taihou's funeral was too hurried and improperly handled. His dismissal makes the episode's accountability logic concrete: ritual neglect can be charged upward to the officials responsible for state ceremony.

His replacement also qualifies the lesson. [[ZhaiFangjinWesternHan|翟方进]], who was also punished in the same funeral affair, soon becomes chancellor. Xue Xuan therefore marks the loss-of-office side of a personnel reshuffle whose moral logic is not reducible to who was punished most visibly at first.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han chancellor under Han Chengdi in the Hanji 540 funeral case.
- Held accountable for rushed handling of Qiongcheng Taihou's burial.
- Dismissed from office in winter of Yongshi 2 / 15 BCE.
- Serves as the contrast point for Zhai Fangjin's later promotion despite shared involvement in the case.
