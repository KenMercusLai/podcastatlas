## Overview

汉安帝 / Emperor An of Han now appears in the wiki through a deteriorating 124-125 CE court sequence before the Shun restoration. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]] shows him ignoring [[YangZhenLateHan|杨震]]'s anti-favorite memorials and executing [[ZhaoTengLateHan|赵腾]]; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-801-han-andi-weishenme-henxin-fei-taizi-lgnhbsqnp6vdigsmqfkxiqmx2vdy|Hanji 801]] shows him deposing the ten-year-old crown prince [[EmperorShunOfHan|刘保]]; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-802-andi-zhisi-donghan-wangchao-quanli-da-xipai-lgvne92e0jjzjnznzfkz7nvztnbj|Hanji 802]] shows his sudden death creating the information vacuum that lets [[EmpressDowagerYanLateHan|阎皇后 / 阎太后]] and [[YanXianLateHan|阎显]] exclude Liu Bao again.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]] places Emperor An in early 124 CE, before the crown-prince deposition. Fan Feng, Zhou Guang, Xie Yun, and other favorites forge imperial instructions for private construction while An is away on eastern tour. Yang Zhen uses earthquake and drought signs to attack eunuch power, then obtains evidence of the forged orders, but An's court does not convert that warning into correction.

The same source shows An treating harsh speech as crime. Zhao Teng submits a critical memorial, Yang Zhen asks for mitigation, and An still executes Zhao Teng. After Fan Feng's group slanders Yang Zhen as resentful and factional, An strips Yang of the taiwei seal and later orders him sent home after Geng Bao's accusation. Yang's suicide makes An's pre-deposition court look already captured by favorites and unable to protect loyal correction.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-801-han-andi-weishenme-henxin-fei-taizi-lgnhbsqnp6vdigsmqfkxiqmx2vdy|Hanji 801]] then places An in the Liu Bao crisis. Wang Sheng, [[FanFengEunuchLateHan|Fan Feng]], and others first attack Liu Bao's wet nurse Wang Nan and kitchen supervisor Bing Ji; Fan Feng then works with Yan Hou to fabricate evidence against Liu Bao and his palace officers. An becomes angry, convenes senior ministers, and pushes toward deposition despite objections from [[LaiLiLateHan|Lai Li]], [[HuanYanLateHan|Huan Yan]], and [[ZhangHaoRemonstratorLateHan|Zhang Hao]].

The same source makes An's ruler-security anxiety visible. After Liu Bao is formally demoted to Jiyin Wang on 124-09-07, more than ten officials remonstrate at Hongdu Gate that the child is blameless. The host reads An's reaction as fear that a ten-year-old prince with so much official sympathy could become an alternative political center. An's Zhongchangshi edict threatens the remonstrants, accuses them of hidden motive, and punishes Lai Li's office, family, and fief position after he persists.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-802-andi-zhisi-donghan-wangchao-quanli-da-xipai-lgvne92e0jjzjnznzfkz7nvztnbj|Hanji 802]] then places An in 125 CE, his Yan Guang fourth year. During a southern tour, he becomes ill at [[WanCounty|宛城]], continues traveling while sick, and dies near Ye County at age thirty-two.

Because An dies away from [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], Yan Hou, Yan Xian, and Fan Feng can hide the death, keep routine food and medicine appearances, and return to the capital before public announcement. Once inside the palace, Yan Hou becomes empress dowager, Yan Xian receives military authority, and the Yan side chooses a young Beixiang Hou instead of An's son Liu Bao. An's earlier deposition of Liu Bao makes that exclusion easier to perform.

## Key Characteristics

- Ruler whose court is already failing before the succession crisis, with favorite power overriding Yang Zhen's evidence and warning.
- Executor of Zhao Teng and remover of Yang Zhen, making direct speech and rescue speech dangerous under his authority.
- Father of Liu Bao, later Emperor Shun, whose 124 CE deposition of the crown prince creates the crisis's legitimacy wound.
- Threatened ruler whose edict recodes remonstrance as hidden factional motive.
- Dead ruler whose 125 CE tour death creates a temporary information vacuum.
- Patron whose body and routines are used to preserve the appearance of continuity during the return to Luoyang.
- Predecessor whose court networks become vulnerable once the Yan-backed succession is announced.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 800, Hanji 801, and Hanji 802. It does not reconstruct Emperor An's full reign, full palace-building policy, earlier relationship with the Yan family, or the complete background of Geng Bao, Fan Feng, Wang Sheng, Wang Nan, Bing Ji, Zhao Teng, and Yang Zhen. Hanji 801's claim that An feared Liu Bao's support is the host's interpretation of the recorded support field and threat edict. Hanji 800's moral judgment on Yang Zhen's innocence is preserved as source-grounded but not expanded into a full independent legal history.

- Preserved Hanji 802 as the 125 CE death-trigger and concealed-return layer of the Shun restoration crisis.
