# Truth Social

[[fuzhuang-pinpai-a-f-xunzhao-zhongguo-hezuo-huoban-fufei-tiqian-kan-telangpu-tiewen-fuwu-shangxian-1004810677]] adds Truth Social's data-product branch through [[TruthAPI]]. The episode says the platform is selling millisecond-level access to important posts, including [[DonaldTrump]]'s account, to financial institutions and media groups for as much as $1.2 million per year.

Truth Social is the social platform discussed in [[ep77-si-shi-wan-nian-xin-fu-ye-zhuan-le-san-shi-si-yi-te-lang-pu-jiao-ni-ruhe-gao-qian-lnrx8vkdjedxbmmcwxlnwow-pcab]] as the operating asset behind [[TrumpMediaAndTechnologyGroup]]. The episode says [[DonaldTrump]] launched it after being removed from major social platforms, then used a SPAC route to take the business public under DJT.

[[how-much-money-president-trump-and-his-family-have-made]] gives Truth Social a smaller but still presidency-dependent estimated gain of about $25 million, treating the platform's success as linked to Trump's status as president.

## Source Position
- The source frames Truth Social's market value as coming primarily from Donald Trump's political identity and audience rather than ordinary social-media operating strength.
- The hosts cite low daily activity, small revenue, large losses, and weak profit visibility to argue that its quoted market value should be treated through [[PoliticalMemeStock]] and [[PaperWealthVsCashValue]].
- Trump's own posts on the platform are treated as market-moving events, connecting Truth Social to [[PolicyAnnouncementTradingRisk]].
- Planet Money treats the platform as part of conservative [[OfficeLinkedProfitAccounting]] rather than only as a meme-stock valuation case.
- The Truth API source turns Trump's posts into a latency-priced data product, extending the page into [[PoliticalInformationLatency]].
