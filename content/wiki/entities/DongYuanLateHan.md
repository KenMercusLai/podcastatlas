## Overview

董元 / Dong Yuan is [[ShanChaoLateHan|单超]]'s maternal grandson and the Shuofang administrator in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-851-hanhuandi-weihe-zhuding-yichou-wannian-ltgm1ix6cgqb-kqo9bztfrzjgeun|Hanji 851]]. His source-bounded role is to make [[FifthZhongLateHan|第五种]]'s exile dangerous after Fifth Zhong has already been framed by Shan Chao.

## Current Profile

After Fifth Zhong's report against [[ShanKuangLateHan|单匡]] and Shan Chao, Shan Chao uses [[ShusunWujiLateHan|叔孙无忌]]'s disorder as a pretext to have Fifth Zhong demoted or exiled to Shuofang. Dong Yuan's position there matters because he is Shan Chao's family member and is described as planning to harm Fifth Zhong.

The episode gives Dong Yuan no independent policy profile. He functions as the endpoint of a retaliation network: central protection follows Fifth Zhong from investigation to exile, until [[SunBinFifthZhongRetainerLateHan|孙斌]] intercepts the route and sends Fifth Zhong home.

## Key Characteristics

- Shan Chao family member placed in a frontier exile destination.
- Shuofang administrator whose office turns Fifth Zhong's exile into personal danger.
- Retaliation endpoint in the Shan Kuang and Fifth Zhong case.
- Source-bounded actor with no independent agenda yet recorded.
