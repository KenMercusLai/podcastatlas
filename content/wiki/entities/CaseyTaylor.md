# Casey Taylor

## Overview
Casey Taylor is a [[ParadiseCalifornia|Paradise]] homeowner whose certified wildfire-resistant house became the episode's clearest test of whether mitigation would be recognized by insurers.

## Current Profile
[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-043ffe0bc2c7516bac64132229755e93-43026861-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-043ffe0bc2c7516bac64132229755e93-43026861]] says Taylor's house was the first certified under the IBHS Wildfire Prepared program. Despite being promoted as a model, its quoted premium rose sharply until a [[MercuryInsurance]] site visit led to an offer near the earlier price.

## Key Characteristics
- Owned the first home described as certified under the IBHS Wildfire Prepared program.
- Initially faced a quoted premium increase from roughly $800 to $8,000.
- Paid about $7,000 for FAIR Plan and supplemental coverage before private re-entry.
- Received an approximately $800 Mercury offer after direct inspection.
- Described insurance as essential to recovery and later as a barrier to reconstruction.
