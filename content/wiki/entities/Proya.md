# Proya / 珀莱雅

Proya is used here as the cautious canonical page for the beauty company rendered in [[buai-zhibo-daihuo-de-oumei-xiaofeizhe-weihe-zai-zhejia-pingtai-shang-daba-huaqian-1007104138]]'s transcript as "博莱亚." The source says the company planned two skincare series for U.S. distribution through an "AltoBeauty" channel, which this wiki normalizes cautiously to [[UltaBeauty|Ulta Beauty]] pending future source confirmation.

The episode frames the move as part of Chinese beauty brands seeking outbound growth as the domestic beauty market consolidates. It also says the company had earlier entered Malaysian stores and that recent quarterly revenue and profit were under pressure.
