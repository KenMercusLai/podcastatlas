# Laestrygonians

## Overview

The Laestrygonians are giant cannibals whose seemingly perfect harbor destroys eleven of [[Odysseus]]' twelve ships.

## Current Profile

In [[687-the-odyssey-hero-of-the-trojan-horse-part-1-glt2393125423]], the Laestrygonian harbor looks safe and attractive, but it becomes a trap once the giants hurl boulders from the cliffs and wreck nearly the whole fleet. Odysseus' own ship survives because he keeps it outside the harbor.

## Key Characteristics

- The Laestrygonians turn attractive geography into a concealed danger.
- Their attack reduces Odysseus' expedition from a fleet to a single ship.
- The episode reads the harbor as a settlement-risk image: a good anchorage may already belong to hostile inhabitants.
- Odysseus' partial caution saves only his own ship, not the larger command.

## Evidence

- Harbor trap: [[687-the-odyssey-hero-of-the-trojan-horse-part-1-glt2393125423]] describes the fleet entering a seemingly perfect harbor controlled by the Laestrygonians.
- Fleet destruction: [[687-the-odyssey-hero-of-the-trojan-horse-part-1-glt2393125423]] says the giants throw boulders from cliffs and destroy eleven ships.
- Cautious survival: [[687-the-odyssey-hero-of-the-trojan-horse-part-1-glt2393125423]] notes that Odysseus keeps his own ship outside and alone escapes.
