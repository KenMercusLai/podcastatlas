# John Ternus

## Overview
John Ternus is the Apple hardware-engineering leader the wiki treats as Tim Cook's source-described successor and then source-described CEO, with his early tenure framed around hardware credibility, AI-era device value, and continuity after the Cook operations era.

## Current Profile
Across the bounded sources, Ternus moves from possible successor to named successor and then to the figure described as formally taking over Apple on 2026-09-01. The sources read his hardware background as meaningful because Apple's next phase is being judged by foldable iPhone timing, Mac and iPhone pricing pressure, delayed Siri and Apple Intelligence integration, and whether the company can communicate product ambition beyond operational continuity.

## Key Characteristics
- He is framed as a hardware-engineering successor rather than a finance, services, or operations-only leader.
- His appointment is read as a product-reset signal after the [[SteveJobs]] product-founder era and the [[TimCook]] operations era.
- He inherits pressure around the next post-plateau phone form factor, including foldable devices and the lessons of [[VisionPro]].
- His CEO transition is tied to whether [[AppleIntelligence]] and a delayed [[Siri]] can make Apple devices feel newly useful.
- The Fengyan Fengyu source adds a continuity signal: Tim Cook is described as retaining an Apple role after the handoff.
- His public profile in the wiki remains source-scoped to technology-journalism and podcast discussion rather than primary Apple filings.
