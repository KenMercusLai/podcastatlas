# John Ternus

[[tech-20260424-0424-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260424-0424-mp-tech-pod-128]] names John Ternus as [[Apple]]'s next CEO after [[TimCook]] steps down and becomes executive chairman. [[AnitaRamaswamy]] emphasizes his hardware-engineering background and work around Apple Watch, iPhone models, and MacBooks, reading the appointment as a signal that product and device innovation may become more central to Apple's next chapter.

[[tech-20251212-1212-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251212-1212-mp-tech-pod-128]] had earlier mentioned Ternus as an Apple lead engineer who appeared in reporting as a possible future CEO after Cook. The April 2026 source updates that branch from a succession possibility into a source-attributed announcement.

In the wiki, Ternus adds a concrete person node to the Apple succession and AI-era leadership branch. His expected test is not only operational continuity but whether [[Apple]] can improve existing products, recover product-innovation confidence, and find a stronger answer to [[AIPlusTerminals]] and [[SmartphoneAIHub]] questions.
