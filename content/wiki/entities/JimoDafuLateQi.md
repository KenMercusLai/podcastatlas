# 即墨大夫（齐末） / Late-Qi Jimo Dafu

## Overview
即墨大夫（齐末） / Late-Qi Jimo Dafu is the unnamed late-[[QiState|齐国]] official who proposes turning Qi into a base for exiled Three Jin and Chu elites after [[YongmenSima|雍门司马]] prevents [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]] from entering Qin.

## Current Profile
He reads the successful remonstrance as evidence that Qi Wang Jian may still act. Drawing on [[JimoWarringStates|即墨]]'s history as a military stronghold and restoration center, he argues that Qi should recruit exiled officers, assign them troops, recover their former territories, and reverse Qin's conquest. The source admires his courage but treats his force estimates and operational confidence as unrealistic because Qi has not fought for decades while Qin is battle-hardened.

## Key Characteristics
- Offers the clearest active-resistance alternative in the episode.
- Tries to convert displaced elites into restoration forces for the Three Jin and Chu.
- Draws symbolic credibility from Jimo's earlier survival and Tian Dan's restoration campaign.
- Overstates or inherits overstated claims about Qi's territory and available troops.
- Is source-scoped as courageous in purpose but weak in practical military assessment.

- **Restoration coalition:** [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-4-shishang-neng-cheng-juying-de-junwang-qiwangjian-livcg5uz60ne-6oje-qzjfpghhph]] attributes to him the proposal to arm more than one hundred displaced Three Jin officers and additional Chu exiles.
- **Jimo political memory:** [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-4-shishang-neng-cheng-juying-de-junwang-qiwangjian-livcg5uz60ne-6oje-qzjfpghhph]] connects his office to the city that held out during Le Yi's conquest and became Tian Dan's restoration base.
- **Capability qualification:** [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-4-shishang-neng-cheng-juying-de-junwang-qiwangjian-livcg5uz60ne-6oje-qzjfpghhph]] rejects the proposal's expansive troop claims and contrasts Qi's long inactivity with Qin's combat experience.

## Qualifications
The source gives no personal name or biography, and “即墨大夫” is an office label. His force figures may be rhetorical or textually inflated. This page is intentionally distinct from [[JimoDafu|即墨大夫]], the official evaluated under [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] roughly a century earlier.
