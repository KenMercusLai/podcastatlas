# 不熄灯 / Buxideng

不熄灯 enters the wiki through [[bu-xi-deng-e02-bi-quan-shan-beng-mei-guo-zheng-fu-guan-men-first-brands-po-chan-wa-ha-ha-feng-bo-lt-ghzgmiroo9a-odaih4xkhsulu]], where [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] introduces it as a new show with [[Yubai|雨白]] and [[XiaobaoQizhulou|小宝]]. The format is a three-location current-events conversation across New York, [[HongKong]], and Beijing, carried inside the [[QizhulouYanBinke|起朱楼宴宾客]] distribution context.

The episode's mode is comparative rather than single-topic reporting. It moves from [[Japan]] and [[Thailand]] politics into crypto, U.S. fiscal operations, private credit, and Chinese corporate governance, looking for patterns such as [[PoliticalVetoPointBargaining]], [[CryptoLeverageLiquidityCascade]], [[PrivateCreditReceivablesOpacity]], and [[FamilyBusinessSuccessionOpacity]].
