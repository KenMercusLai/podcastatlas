# 张玮玮 / Zhang Weiwei

## Overview
张玮玮 / Zhang Weiwei is represented as a Baiyin-born musician, songwriter, singer, accordion player, and former [[WildChildrenBand|野孩子]] member whose route through Lanzhou, Guangzhou, Beijing, Yunnan, and Shanghai joins 1990s social dislocation to Chinese independent music and later electronic experimentation.

## Current Profile
Zhang's profile is organized by conversion rather than linear ascent. Music imposed by his father became transferable craft; bullying and factory-compound insecurity became social memory; bar performance and supporting work became stage training; the care and discipline of [[WildChildrenBand|野孩子]] became a professional model; and creative crisis became the reason to learn hip-hop, dance, synthesis, recording, bass, and drum production. 《米店》 and 《白银饭店》 established his authorship, while 《沙木黎》 is presented as the work that let him address his father's death and resume creating without repeating his acoustic-folk identity.

## Key Characteristics
- Musician whose classical and wind-instrument training became practical multi-instrumental fluency despite beginning as parental compulsion.
- Narrator who treats ordinary life, industrial-city memory, local language, and marginal experience as valid poetic material.
- Collaborator shaped by [[GuoLongMusician|郭龙]], [[WildChildrenBand|野孩子]], and Beijing's independent-music network.
- Reluctant front person who moved from supporting other people's projects toward responsibility for his own songs and albums.
- Artist whose view of popularity shifted from suspicion toward communication and consolation for listeners.
- Midlife learner who used new production tools and electronic sound to escape creative repetition.

## Qualifications
This profile rests on one long retrospective interview. Chronology, family interpretation, band and venue history, sales, royalties, recording process, and the therapeutic meaning of 《沙木黎》 are Zhang's source-scoped account rather than an independently verified biography or critical consensus.

## What Changed
- Established Zhang as a musician whose career links industrial-city memory, independent-scene apprenticeship, authorship, and electronic reinvention.
- Distinguished his narrative definition of folk from a fixed acoustic style.
