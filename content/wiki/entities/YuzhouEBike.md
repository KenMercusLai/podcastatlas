# 宇宙 E-Bike

## Overview
宇宙 E-Bike is the electric-assist bicycle brand founded by [[ChengHongningYuzhouEBike|程泓宁 / 陈鸿宁]] and discussed in the No.222 [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] episode.

## Current Profile
The source presents 宇宙 E-Bike as a small Chinese premium electric-assist bicycle company operating between traditional bicycles and electric mopeds. Its category definition is important: the product still requires pedaling, while motors, batteries, sensors, and control systems add a smoother, faster, longer-range riding experience.

The company's strategy is framed around [[ElectricAssistBicyclePremiumPositioning]]. It chooses a 10,000-30,000 yuan price band because the founder believes a high-quality riding experience needs stronger components, R&D, integrated tuning, more robust frames, and a service budget. The source says the brand's best-selling product is around 15,000 yuan and that the high-end line reaches about 30,000 yuan.

The source also makes 宇宙 E-Bike a small-company AI transformation case. [[ChengHongningYuzhouEBike|程泓宁 / 陈鸿宁]] wants the company to become an AI-native manufacturing company, using AI to externalize founder knowledge, support employee growth, record work, and reshape organizational capability.

## Key Characteristics
- It builds electric-assist bicycles rather than throttle-only electric mopeds.
- It uses a premium Chinese price band to fund product quality, R&D, and continuing owner service.
- Its product thesis emphasizes full riding experience: electric system, geometry, components, battery structure, sensors, control tuning, and rider fit.
- It treats China's market differently from Europe because electric mopeds already occupy much of the local short-distance mobility space.
- It is used in the source as a small manufacturing-company test case for founder-led AI transformation.
