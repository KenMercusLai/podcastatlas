# Hutian Kiln Site / 湖田古瓷窑址

Hutian Kiln Site / 湖田古瓷窑址 is one of the ceramic heritage components discussed in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode groups it with old-city production remains, [[GaolingVillage|高岭]] clay, [[ChanglingPorcelainStoneMine|长岭]] porcelain stone, and fuel areas to show that Jingdezhen heritage is a production landscape rather than a single preserved building.

The site helps anchor the long material record behind [[Jingdezhen]]'s reputation. It connects archaeological kiln remains to [[QingbaiPorcelain]], later ceramic specialization, and the source's larger [[JingdezhenPorcelainProductionSystem]] argument.
