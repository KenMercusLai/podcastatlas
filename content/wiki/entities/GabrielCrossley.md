# Gabriel Crossley

Gabriel Crossley is the source voice in [[socialist-mobility-a-telling-michigan-vote-6a74600ec3e1347e8b5bf216]] explaining the [[YangtzeRiver|Yangtze River]] recovery. He says fish biomass has risen by more than 200% since 2021, while stressing that the river is not pristine and that dams, extinct species, and social costs remain major caveats.

His contribution anchors [[YangtzeFishingBanRecovery]] and [[EnvironmentalRepairSocialCost]]: the source presents state-led ecological repair as effective in narrow biological terms but uneven in its treatment of former fishing communities.
