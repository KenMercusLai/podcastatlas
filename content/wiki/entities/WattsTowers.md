# Watts Towers

The Watts Towers appear in [[outsider-chance-ending-sudans-proxy-war-6a75b3695d765781a2b1b132]] as an early influence on [[BetyeSaar]]. The episode says Saar grew up in Watts and was shaped by seeing discarded plates, tiles, and other materials become something beautiful.

In the source, the towers matter as an artistic precedent for [[AssemblageArtMemoryWork]]. They show how ordinary, discarded, or broken materials can become a charged visual language rather than remaining waste.
