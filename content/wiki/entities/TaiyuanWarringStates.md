# 太原 / Taiyuan (Warring States)

太原 / Taiyuan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-1-lq4wvkd8qx3q7zji6fnnkxi3qcl1]] as a [[ZhaoState|赵国]] northwestern target in Qin's post-[[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] offensive. The episode names [[SimaGeng|司马梗]] as the Qin commander assigned to attack Taiyuan and [[Shangdang|上党郡]] while [[WangHe|王龁]] attacks [[WuanWarringStates|武安]] and [[Pilao|皮牢]].

Taiyuan's source role is to widen the campaign map. Qin's pressure after Changping is not just the emotional aftermath of a massacre; it is a military movement against surrounding strategic points that could open the road toward [[Handan|邯郸]].
