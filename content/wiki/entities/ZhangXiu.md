# 张绣 / Zhang Xiu

## Overview
张绣 / Zhang Xiu is a late-Han commander whose wiki profile centers on the instability and later recovery of surrender. He inherits [[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]]'s troops, attaches to [[LiuBiao|刘表]] through [[JiaXu|贾诩]]'s advice, submits to [[CaoCao|曹操]] at Wancheng, attacks after Cao-side desire and threat signals make the surrender unsafe, and later submits again before [[GuanduCampaign|官渡]].

## Current Profile
Zhang Xiu is not best read as a simple treachery example. The bounded sources make him an armed subordinate-holder whose choices depend on host safety, adviser judgment, status injury, and the strategic price of his troops. His first surrender collapses after Cao Cao takes Lady Zou, rewards Hu Che'er, and develops a secret plan against him; his second surrender succeeds because Jia Xu correctly reads Cao Cao's need for force, legitimacy, and public magnanimity before Guandu.

## Key Characteristics
- Inherited-force commander whose army remains valuable after Zhang Ji's death and Wancheng stationing.
- Adviser-dependent actor whose key patron moves run through Jia Xu's risk readings.
- Surrender-risk case where private humiliation, subordinate-poaching anxiety, and leaked violence turn submission into pre-emptive attack.
- Former enemy whose renewed submission becomes useful to Cao Cao because it removes a rear threat and adds fighting strength before Guandu.
- Reputation-cost figure for Cao Cao because the Wancheng attack kills Cao Ang, Cao Anmin, and Dian Wei, making later pardon politically visible.

## Evidence
- Inherited force and Liu Biao attachment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1010-sanguo-liubiao-jiujing-shibushi-piaoliang-de-caobao-lo58hzfo-qv0soauogtgfrkourrd|Hanji 1010]] places Zhang Xiu after Zhang Ji's Rangcheng death, with Jia Xu advising attachment to Liu Biao and stationing at Wan.
- Initial surrender setup: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1012-haose-caocao-luanshi-zhong-de-dute-qingshi-llvrucihuv2buvp1-lisz4usbprh|Hanji 1012]] presents Zhang Xiu's surrender to Cao Cao during the 197 CE southern campaign, with Jia Xu as the adviser he respects.
- Wancheng rupture: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-2-pandian-huangdimen-jiuhou-xiaopihao-livpg3zh7lsqffgca2pomiqnlvyd|Qinji 131-2]] connect Cao Cao's desire, Lady Zou, Hu Che'er, Dian Wei's disarming, and the deaths of Cao Ang and Cao Anmin to Zhang Xiu's attack.
- Renewed submission before Guandu: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1035-zhansha-caocao-qinzi-he-aijiang-ta-jing-zaidu-guijiang-caocao-lh10wwkwcqw5hnap3bodw6oxpt7v|Hanji 1035]] records Jia Xu's rejection of Yuan Shao, Zhang Xiu's submission to Cao Cao, Cao Cao's public reception and marriage alliance, and the claim that Zhang Xiu's force helps Cao Cao's Guandu preparation.

## Qualifications
The sources explain Zhang Xiu's attack through insult, fear, tactical opportunity, and leaked threat; they do not require treating all of his later conduct as trustworthy. The renewed submission source emphasizes Cao Cao's strategic incentive to pardon, but it does not by itself settle the long-term quality of Zhang Xiu's later service beyond the host's claim that he contributed at Guandu. Cao Jun is named in the source as the marriage link but does not yet require a canonical page.

## What Changed
- Reframed Zhang Xiu from a Wancheng attacker alone into a recoverable former enemy before Guandu.
- Added Jia Xu's patron-choice logic for rejecting Yuan Shao and choosing Cao Cao.
- Connected Zhang Xiu's renewed submission to strategic defector incorporation and Cao Cao's pre-campaign rear consolidation.

## Relationships
- [[JiaXu|贾诩]] - adviser whose host-risk and patron-choice judgments repeatedly shape Zhang Xiu's moves.
- [[CaoCao|曹操]] - first receives Zhang Xiu's surrender, suffers the Wancheng attack, then accepts his renewed submission before Guandu.
- [[YuanShao|袁绍]] - rival patron whom Jia Xu rejects as unable to tolerate capable commanders.
- [[WanCounty|宛城]] - setting where surrender breaks into attack and Cao-side losses.
- [[DianWei|典韦]] - guard whose neutralization and death mark the tactical cost of the Wancheng attack.
- [[CaoAng|曹昂]] - Cao Cao's son whose death makes later pardon of Zhang Xiu more politically conspicuous.
- [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] - strategic horizon that makes Zhang Xiu's force valuable to Cao Cao.
- [[StrategicDefectorIncorporation|纳叛安抚式整合]] - concept describing how dangerous former enemies can become usable capacity after hierarchy and incentive are reset.
