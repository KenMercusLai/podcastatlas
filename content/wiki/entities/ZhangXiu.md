# 张绣 / Zhang Xiu

张绣 / Zhang Xiu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-2-pandian-huangdimen-jiuhou-xiaopihao-livpg3zh7lsqffgca2pomiqnlvyd|Qinji 131-2]] as the commander in the Wancheng drinking story involving [[CaoCao|曹操]]. The episode says Cao Cao's desire and loosened guard create the opening, while Zhang Xiu's side uses drink to weaken [[DianWei|典韦]] and remove his weapon before attacking.

His role in this source is strategic rather than biographical. Zhang Xiu makes alcohol part of a battlefield-access problem: once the guard is drunk and disarmed, Cao Cao's personal indulgence becomes a lethal military exposure, killing [[CaoAng|曹昂]] and Cao Anmin even though Cao Cao escapes.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] gives the fuller pre-attack chain. After Zhang Xiu surrenders at [[WanCounty|宛城]], Cao Cao takes [[LadyZhouWancheng|周氏]] and then rewards Zhang Xiu's fierce subordinate [[HuCheEr|胡车儿]]. Zhang Xiu reads this as both insult and threat, and once Cao Cao's secret plan to kill him leaks, [[JiaXu|贾诩]] urges a pre-emptive move.

The episode therefore treats Zhang Xiu's rebellion as more than sudden treachery. It is a case where surrender remains unstable because private humiliation, subordinate-poaching anxiety, and an exposed murder plan make waiting look more dangerous than attacking.
