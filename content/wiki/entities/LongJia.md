# 龙甲 / Long Jia

龙甲 / Long Jia appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-14-qiweiwang-buming-zeyi-yiming-jingren-lrs6rbifyiqrojxg0mccpg7sf9rv]] as the [[WeiState|魏国]] general sent by [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]] to build a western wall after [[QinState|秦国]] defeats [[HanState|韩国]] at West Mountain. The page keeps the transcript's "龙甲" wording and uses Long Jia as the romanized handle for indexing.

His source role is military-infrastructure rather than biography. The wall-building notice turns [[QinEastwardPressure|Qin eastward pressure]] into a material defensive response, captured in [[WeiGreatWallAntiQinDefense|魏长城防秦]].
