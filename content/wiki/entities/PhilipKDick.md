# Philip K. Dick

## Overview

Philip K. Dick is the science-fiction author whose story “The Variable Man” supplied the premise for [[GaryGoldman]] and [[AngusFletcher]]'s unproduced film adaptation.

## Current Profile

The bounded source presents Dick as a writer whose speculative premises invite later creators to test emerging technological conditions. His original story's computer predicts a future war from statistical data; Goldman and Fletcher retained the prediction problem while extending it into intimate decision-making and AI-mediated life.

Goldman says work on multiple Dick adaptations became part of his Hollywood identity. The episode also situates *Total Recall* as an earlier Dick-derived project whose long development eventually found a workable star, director, and rewrite coalition.

## Key Characteristics

- Science-fiction writer used by the source as a model of intuitively future-facing speculation.
- Author of the predictive-computer premise behind “The Variable Man.”
- Source of multiple screen adaptations associated with Goldman's career.
- Provides conceptual starting points that adaptations can relocate into later technological contexts.
