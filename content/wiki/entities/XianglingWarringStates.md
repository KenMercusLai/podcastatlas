# 襄陵 / Xiangling

襄陵 / Xiangling appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] as a major [[WeiState|魏国]] place under coalition pressure in the 352-351 BCE cluster. The episode links the notice to the earlier siege context around "围魏救赵," then uses [[ZhushuJinian|《竹书纪年》]] for the claim that [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]] used [[HanState|韩国]] forces to defeat the coalition there.

This page is deliberately disambiguated from [[Xiangling|香菱]], the Hongloumeng character already present in the wiki. Here Xiangling is a Warring States place name used to track [[EarlyWarringStatesInterstateWar|early interstate war]] and Wei's effort to stabilize under pressure.
