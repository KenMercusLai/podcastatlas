# Skims

## Overview
Skims is a bodywear and apparel brand founded in 2019 by [[KimKardashian]] and partners. The wiki carries it as a celebrity-founded consumer brand whose category logic runs from shapewear into everyday clothing.

## Current Profile
The current source describes Skims as starting in women's shapewear with a full-size-range offer and then expanding into loungewear, sweatpants, and adjacent categories. That category spread is what makes the brand a plausible partner for [[Nike]] in [[NikeSkims|NikeSkims]], a separate sub-brand aimed at women's sportswear, and it makes Skims a case where a founder's cultural visibility and an inclusive sizing promise carry across product lines.

## Key Characteristics
- Founded in 2019 by [[KimKardashian]] and co-founders.
- Started in women's shapewear and moved into loungewear, sweatpants, and everyday apparel.
- Full-size-range positioning used as part of the brand promise.
- Celebrity founder visibility as an acquisition and attention channel.
- Category blurring that lets a bodywear brand reach athleisure and sportswear demand.
- Partner for a separate [[Nike]] sub-brand rather than only a standalone label.
