# 《问心》 / Ask the Heart (television drama)

## Overview
《问心》 is a Chinese medical television drama discussed in VOL.88 through [[WangJingProductionDesigner|王靖]]'s production design for the fictional Dongli Hospital and the homes of Lin Yi, Fang Xiaoran, and Zhou Xiaofeng.

## Current Profile
The episode frames the drama's visual world as an attempt to evoke a contemporary Chinese public tertiary hospital without claiming documentary equivalence. Most hospital action was staged in Qingdao-built sets whose old, middle-aged, and newer structures form a connected institution with visible expansion, crowding, wayfinding, staff adaptations, and specialist equipment.

The drama's homes extend the same realism into character biography. Lin Yi's crowded household makes care and medical vulnerability spatially unavoidable; Fang Xiaoran's warmer family home and Zhou Xiaofeng's colder apartment create a contrast in attachment and belonging. These readings reflect the production designer's stated intentions, not an independent assessment of the completed drama or its audience reception.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses a fictional city and hospital system rather than representing a named operating hospital.
- Builds hospital scale through connected sets, camera movement, repeated routes, and mixed architectural eras.
- Presents institutional life through patients, added beds, signs, paper notices, equipment, workspaces, and visible operational pressure.
- Uses character homes to externalize care burdens, privacy, emotional temperature, and family inclusion or exclusion.
- Seeks professional plausibility through medical advisers and clinician review while remaining a narrative drama.

## Qualifications
This profile is derived from a production-designer interview and therefore provides privileged evidence about stated design intent but not independent evidence of clinical accuracy, narrative quality, viewer response, construction figures, or whether every intention is legible onscreen. The episode explicitly rejects judging the drama as a documentary or medical teaching film.
