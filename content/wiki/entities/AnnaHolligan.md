# Anna Holligan

Anna Holligan is the [[BBC]] reporter whose field segment anchors [[tech-20251224-1224-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251224-1224-mp-tech-pod-128]], a [[MarketplaceTech]] episode on mushroom-based alternatives to polystyrene foam. Her reporting visits the woods and lab context around [[UniversityOfAlaska]] researchers working with [[FomesFomentarius]] and mycelium-grown boards.

In the source, Holligan's role is to make [[MyceliumInsulation]] concrete. The episode moves from forest fungus to a lab process and then to [[WildSource]] testing in [[SeafoodColdChainPackaging]], rather than treating biodegradable materials as an abstract climate promise.
