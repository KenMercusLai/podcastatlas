# 吴瀚坤

## Overview
吴瀚坤 is an actor, AI entrepreneur, and AI short-film creator discussed in [[no-232-wuhankun-ai-shidai-yanyuan-diyige-diufanwan-gkwriw4oa148aw4mzwtp-xj]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Wu as a cross-domain practitioner whose authority comes from doing the work rather than from abstract AI commentary. He entered acting through the [[CreationOfTheGodsI|《封神》]] training camp, faced a pandemic-era interruption, learned code and product building, worked on [[FrederickAI]], and then used AI video tools to make [[PinkParadox|《粉色悖论》]] and [[PopulationAnomaly|《人口异常》]].

His current profile is not "actor replaced by AI"; it is actor as AI-literate creator. Wu treats AI as a way to learn directing, scripting, editing, music, shot language, and production coordination, while arguing that embodied rhythm, real emotion, and life experience remain central to performance.

## Key Characteristics
- He combines acting, software learning, product execution, and AI video creation in one working identity.
- He approaches new work through practical entry: start making, then let feedback decide whether the direction is worth continuing.
- His AI-film practice is self-taught and workflow-wide, spanning script, storyboard, image, video, editing, music, and visual detail.
- He sees actors as having an advantage in AI video because performance rhythm and emotional timing are hard to specify only through prompts.
- He distinguishes commercial face authorization from an actor's own artistic expression.
- His answer to AI anxiety is direct use, boundary discovery, and returning to what the creator wants to express.
