# Winchester College

## Overview

Winchester College is the late-medieval school founded by [[WilliamOfWykeham|William of Wykeham]] to prepare scholars for New College, Oxford.

## Current Profile

[[368-the-history-behind-hogwarts-ancient-schools-and-revolting-students-glt6783238013]] uses Winchester to reveal both continuity and reversal in [[BritishPublicSchools|British public-school history]]. Its original model combined poor scholars, Latin, worship, austere discipline, and a monastic institutional form with limited access for influential families. By 1793, however, pupil hierarchy, collective punishment, weapons, weak supervision, and elite confidence helped turn a disciplinary dispute into an armed occupation requiring the Riot Act.

## Key Characteristics

- Founded in 1382 and first admitted pupils in 1394 as a feeder for New College.
- Organized seventy poor scholars around Latin speech, religious observance, and austere communal discipline.
- Admitted a limited number of noble or influential families' sons alongside its charitable intake.
- Supplied the institutional model later adapted by Eton.
- Became the site of a major 1793 pupil rebellion against collective punishment.
