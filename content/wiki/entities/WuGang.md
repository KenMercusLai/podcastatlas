# Wu Gang / 吴刚

## Overview

Wu Gang / 吴刚 is the lunar woodcutter whose endlessly regenerating tree makes punishment visible as repetitive labor without completion. The source supplies incompatible reasons for his sentence, including failed cultivation, distracted practice, killing after a powerful figure's son violated his marriage, and modern melodramatic combinations with other lunar figures.

## Current Profile

[[ep34-zhongqiu-teji-linghun-kaowen-biepa-jinye-kongxu-jimo-leng-wkgkk1unb1oxo9ngcvx-ivoggiw069|EP34]] treats Wu Gang less as a stable biography than as a flexible figure for [[EndlessPunishmentLabor]]. His task can signify futility, pessimism, disciplined persistence, resistance to unjust authority, or labor that indirectly benefits others when falling leaves are said to bring prosperity.

The episode repeatedly compares him with Sisyphus while also distinguishing their attributed offenses: one version makes Wu Gang a victim of elite power, whereas Sisyphus violates the life-death order. It keeps him separate from [[ChangE|Chang'e]] despite their shared lunar setting, partly because the figures may come from different story layers and partly because later marital ethics preserve Chang'e's attachment to Hou Yi.

## Key Characteristics

- Performs an endless task because the lunar tree repairs itself after every cut.
- Has no single stable offense across the variants reported by the episode.
- Supports both existential readings of futility and political readings of resistance to authority.
- Shares a setting with Chang'e without requiring a romance between the two figures.
- Can be reinterpreted from useless punishment into labor with unintended public benefit.

## Evidence

- **Regenerating labor:** [[ep34-zhongqiu-teji-linghun-kaowen-biepa-jinye-kongxu-jimo-leng-wkgkk1unb1oxo9ngcvx-ivoggiw069|EP34]] repeatedly compares the uncuttable tree with Sisyphus's returning stone.
- **Conflicting punishment stories:** [[ep34-zhongqiu-teji-linghun-kaowen-biepa-jinye-kongxu-jimo-leng-wkgkk1unb1oxo9ngcvx-ivoggiw069|EP34]] reports cultivation failure, distracted practice, homicide after sexual wrongdoing, and openly doubted modern combinations.
- **Interpretive range:** [[ep34-zhongqiu-teji-linghun-kaowen-biepa-jinye-kongxu-jimo-leng-wkgkk1unb1oxo9ngcvx-ivoggiw069|EP34]] moves among nihilism, optimism, defiance, unjust punishment, and the claim that falling leaves benefit people.

## Qualifications

The supplied summary does not identify primary texts, dates, regions, or a reliable sequence for the variants. Its comparison with Sisyphus is thematic, not proof of historical transmission or philosophical equivalence. The prosperity-leaf version, relationship stories, and attributed offenses remain episode-scoped.

## What Changed

- Created a profile that preserves the figure's incompatible offenses and interpretations.
- Distinguished endless labor, resistance to authority, and possible public benefit as separate readings.
