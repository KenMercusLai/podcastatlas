# Mark Sokolowski

Mark Sokolowski is the Chicago orthopedic surgeon and [[CabbageLabs]] founder who introduces [[CabDab]] in [[advice-line-with-bobbi-brown-of-jones-road-beauty-september-2025-427590c2-11bc-43fa-9c92-d1eccf05842a]]. He says the product grew from patients' use of cabbage leaf wraps for pain and inflammation and asks how to scale beyond personally giving the product to patients.

The advice to Sokolowski centers on preserving physician-linked trust while making it cheaper and more repeatable. [[BobbiBrown]] suggests smaller samples with cards and discounts instead of full-size giveaways, while [[GuyRaz]] points toward chiropractors, medical communities, conferences, and free trials.
