# Eight Sleep / 8Sleep

## Overview
Eight Sleep, referred to as 8Sleep in the current source set, is a consumer sleep-hardware company represented in the wiki by a water-cooled and water-heated smart mattress cover that monitors sleep and changes bed temperature across the night.

## Current Profile
The current evidence frames 8Sleep less as a generic mattress accessory than as a closed-loop sleep environment product. The earlier 面基 source used it as a sponsor-linked example inside [[SleepAsDailyHealthAccount]] and [[EnvironmentOverWillpower]]: sleep cannot be forced directly, but temperature, darkness, caffeine, alcohol, screens, and wake time can be arranged.

The 三五环 crossover adds product detail. The hosts describe a bed cover and water tank, separate bed zones, bed-surface sensing, heart-rate/HRV/respiration tracking, algorithmic learning over the first weeks, natural wake-up by temperature change, and dynamic temperature changes meant to align with entry into sleep, deep sleep, REM, and waking. The business layer adds that a high-priced sleep product may depend on subscription-backed software and personalization, while China adoption faces payment-habit, education, and explanation-cost hurdles.

## Key Characteristics
- It is presented as a smart bed-cover system rather than a wearable alone.
- It uses water circulation and zoned temperature control to alter the sleep environment.
- It connects sleep measurement to intervention through sensors, algorithms, and temperature adjustment.
- It is used as a concrete case for [[SleepTemperatureToolkit]] and [[BedBasedSleepSensing]].
- It is treated as a high-frequency health product with a hardware-plus-subscription business model.
- Its claims remain product-experience and source-scoped rather than clinical proof of broad sleep treatment.

## Evidence
- Environment-over-willpower example - [[zhongnian-san-zhanghu-xianjinliu-jirou-shuimian-lnyomru5v2yzo1-otuyw2mdj-vae]] describes 8Sleep as a water-cooled and water-heated mattress cover that changes bed temperature, monitors sleep, and wakes the user through temperature or light vibration.
- Sleep input framing - [[zhongnian-san-zhanghu-xianjinliu-jirou-shuimian-lnyomru5v2yzo1-otuyw2mdj-vae]] places 8Sleep inside sleep inputs such as temperature, darkness, screen exposure, caffeine, alcohol, and wake time.
- Product architecture - [[no-230-chuantai-wanwushengzhang-kafei-xuming-jiujing-zhumian-dangdairen-de-shuimian-shikong-yu-guanli-gkwrijiowpmzaipwcatimbil]] describes a bed cover, water tank, dual-zone temperature control, sensors, and gradual temperature adjustment.
- Sensing and personalization - [[no-230-chuantai-wanwushengzhang-kafei-xuming-jiujing-zhumian-dangdairen-de-shuimian-shikong-yu-guanli-gkwrijiowpmzaipwcatimbil]] says 8Sleep tracks heart rate, HRV, and respiration, then learns sleep habits before generating personalized temperature plans.
- Business model - [[no-230-chuantai-wanwushengzhang-kafei-xuming-jiujing-zhumian-dangdairen-de-shuimian-shikong-yu-guanli-gkwrijiowpmzaipwcatimbil]] discusses high price, old-user referral, influencer/biohacker adoption, and hardware-plus-subscription friction in China.

## Qualifications
Neither current source establishes independent clinical efficacy, sleep-stage accuracy, sleep-apnea diagnosis capability, long-term outcomes, or universal suitability. The 三五环 source explicitly warns that serious or impairing sleep problems should involve doctors, and that sleep-score data should not dominate emotion or self-diagnosis.
