# Eight Sleep / 8Sleep

## Overview
Eight Sleep, also written 8Sleep in the source set, is a consumer sleep-technology company whose Pod places sensors and a water-based temperature system on an existing mattress to measure sleep-related signals and alter the bed environment across the night.

## Current Profile
Across the sources, Eight Sleep develops from a sponsor-linked temperature example into a closed-loop sleep platform. The early account places it inside [[EnvironmentOverWillpower]]: users cannot force sleep, but they can shape temperature, darkness, caffeine, alcohol, screens, and wake time. The 三五环 crossover adds bed-surface sensing, dual zones, personalized temperature plans, natural waking, and hardware-plus-subscription economics.

The founder interview makes the intended product logic explicit: data is useful when it triggers action. The Pod is described as using sleep stage, physiology, preference, and daytime activity to adjust temperature, elevation, sound, and alarms without requiring the sleeper to wear or charge another device. It also outlines an expansion path from consumer sleep improvement toward personal-baseline alerts, pregnancy-specific adaptation, sleep-apnea risk recognition, and eventually preventive health.

That medical trajectory remains prospective. The interview is sponsored, and large efficacy, disease-correlation, data-scale, accuracy, profitability, international-sales, and China-growth claims come from the company without full study methods or independent verification. The company says sleep-apnea recognition and mitigation technologies are seeking FDA approval; the source does not report approval.

## Key Characteristics
- Smart mattress-cover system coupling [[BedBasedSleepSensing]] to automatic environmental intervention.
- Water-based, dual-zone temperature control that changes across sleep onset, sleep stages, and waking.
- Personalization based on physiology, sleep patterns, stated preferences, activity, and accumulated product use.
- Low-friction product philosophy: technology is embedded in the bed rather than requiring a charged wearable.
- Premium hardware and recurring-service economics supported by proprietary sensors, software, patents, manufacturing, and longitudinal data.
- Preventive-health ambition centered on individual baselines, abnormal-signal alerts, pregnancy support, and sleep-apnea applications.
- Consumer-health evidence profile whose medical claims still require independent validation, regulatory clearance, and clear escalation rules.

## Qualifications
The sources do not establish universal clinical efficacy, independent sleep-stage accuracy, diagnostic capability, long-term health outcomes, pregnancy safety or representativeness, causal disease prediction, or suitability for every sleeper. Company-reported improvement percentages, disease accuracy, data scale, market growth, and user cases remain source-scoped. Temperature and tracking cannot substitute for adequate sleep opportunity, structural schedule changes, or clinical assessment of serious symptoms.

## What Changed
- Reframed the company from a temperature accessory toward a sensing-and-intervention sleep platform.
- Added elevation, sound, silent alarm, partner-zone, pregnancy, and personal-baseline functions.
- Added manufacturing, China localization, premium-pricing, and proprietary-data strategy.
- Added the stated sleep-apnea regulatory path while separating application from approval.
- Tightened the boundary between consumer-health anecdotes and validated medical capability.
