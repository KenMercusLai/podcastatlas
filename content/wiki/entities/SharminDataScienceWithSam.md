# Sharmin (Data Science With Sam)

Sharmin is the founder of [[Livora]] and the guest in [[ep-28-the-ai-revolution-redefining-healthcare-financing]]. The source identifies her by first name only, so this page keeps her identity source-scoped rather than inferring a full name.

In the episode, Sharmin says she has 13 years of product design and design-leadership experience. She applies that background to [[IndependentHealthcareClinicFinancing]], arguing that many clinics need a financing process that understands bookings, operations, documents, borrower readiness, and lender criteria instead of beginning only with credit filters.

Her key contribution to the wiki is a healthcare-fintech workflow frame: [[DataDrivenClinicUnderwriting]], [[AIEnabledLoanDocumentAnalysis]], [[ClinicLenderMatching]], [[BorrowerReadinessFinancing]], and [[ConsentBasedLoanDataSharing]] have to work together if independent clinics are to access capital faster without losing control of sensitive information.
