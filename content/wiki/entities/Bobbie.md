# Bobbie

Bobbie is the infant formula company founded by [[LauraModi]] and discussed in [[bobbie-laura-modi-how-a-baby-formula-startup-took-market-share-from-two-industry-giants-4488842b-74d3-40ed-8776-5831c5934e69]]. The source presents it as a tiny entrant in a concentrated U.S. category dominated by [[Enfamil]] and [[Similac]], built around a European-style formula made for the U.S. market and a broader effort to reduce [[FormulaFeedingStigma]].

Bobbie's early path was unusually constrained. A German-made pilot sold as a "companion formula" triggered a [[FoodAndDrugAdministration|FDA]] recall, after which Bobbie rebuilt its product and supply chain with [[Perrigo]], waited for FDA clearance, and launched direct to consumer in January 2021. Later shortages and stockouts pushed the company toward [[FormulaSupplyChainResilience]] through the acquisition of [[NaturesOne]] and its Ohio facility.
