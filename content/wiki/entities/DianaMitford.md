# Diana Mitford

## Overview

Diana Mitford was a British aristocrat whose relationship with [[OswaldMosley|Oswald Mosley]], access to [[AdolfHitler]], and durable fascist commitment made her more institutionally important to [[BritishFascism|British fascism]] than her sister Unity, despite Unity's greater notoriety.

## Current Profile

The episode contrasts Diana's intellectual and sustained commitment with [[UnityMitford|Unity Mitford]]'s more instinctive, performative route. Diana's glamour and affair with Mosley drew Unity toward Mosley and fascist circles; Unity later introduced Diana and other relatives to Hitler. When Hitler warned the sisters at Bayreuth in 1939 that war was approaching, Diana returned to Britain while Unity stayed.

The postwar account emphasizes continuity rather than repentance. Diana remained loyal to Mosley and, in a 1989 *Desert Island Discs* appearance, questioned the figure of six million Holocaust victims while acknowledging that the killings were morally wrong. The episode treats the Mitford family's literary and celebrity afterlife as one reason her fascism could be remembered through glamour or eccentricity.

## Key Characteristics

- Glamorous Mitford sister whose social position helped make fascism part of an elite family network.
- Politically and intellectually committed supporter of Mosley rather than only a thrill-seeking fellow traveler.
- Member of the family circle introduced personally to Hitler.
- Fascist loyalist whose commitment outlasted the Second World War.
- Public figure whose later comments minimized an established Holocaust death figure.
