## Overview

丁不四 is a [[XiakeXingNovel|《侠客行》]] character whose lifelong pursuit of [[ShiXiaocui|史小翠]] is interpreted as a mixture of attachment, rivalry, vanity, and fixation, while his treatment of Mei Wenxin and [[MeiFanggu|梅芳姑]] makes responsibility the firmer ethical issue.

## Current Profile

The hosts disagree about the romantic core of Ding Busi's conduct. One reading sees continued love obstructed by fear or missed timing; another sees a competitive need to defeat [[BaiZizai|白自在]] after losing the original contest. The episode preserves the mixture rather than selecting one pure motive.

Motive ambiguity does not remove consequences. Ding Busi lives with Mei Wenxin and fathers Mei Fanggu without giving the relationship stable recognition or responsibility. His proclaimed constancy toward Shi Xiaocui therefore coexists with abandonment elsewhere, and the next generation absorbs the insecurity and resentment attached to that pattern.

## Key Characteristics

- Persistent pursuer whose attachment survives long after Shi Xiaocui's marriage.
- Rival of Bai Zizai, making love difficult to separate from status competition.
- Capable of romantic self-description without corresponding responsibility toward another partner and child.
- Part of an intergenerational chain of harm without being a complete explanation of Mei Fanggu's choices.
- Character whose uncertain motive is less ethically decisive than the consequences he leaves others to bear.

## Qualifications

The source does not establish one definitive motive or provide a complete account of his relationship with Mei Wenxin. Intergenerational influence explains vulnerability and learned patterns; it does not excuse abduction, abuse, retaliation, or other later choices by Mei Fanggu.
