# 小影科技 / Xiaoying Technology

## Overview
小影科技 is the consumer-video company led by [[HanShengXiaoying|韩晟]]. In the source, its development path runs from editing, templates, and generative video tools toward agents intended to understand a user's purpose, make director-like choices, and deliver an editable result.

## Current Profile
The company is used as a vertical-agent case rather than documented comprehensively. Its reported product learning is that mass-market users and small merchants often cannot specify creative acceptance criteria in advance, making an initial artifact and local revision loop more useful than a one-shot prompt. The company also reports targeted model optimization for everyday video scenarios, established user reach in Latin America and the Middle East, and a localization system combining local creators with observed product response.

## Key Characteristics
- Builds consumer and small-business video creation products.
- Is shifting its product frame from user-operated tools to outcome-oriented creative agents.
- Uses first drafts and selective revisions to discover underspecified creative intent.
- Evaluates proprietary model work through experience control and compute economics.
- Pursues overseas markets through cultural co-creation and behavioral feedback rather than translation alone.
