# The Residency

The Residency is the central hacker-house organization in [[tanfang-hacker-house-guigu-nianqingren-zhengzai-banjin-ai-chuangye-sushe-s10e10-f0870843-fd16-49f5-8f24-80281f9fff77]]. The source presents it as a hybrid of [[FounderLifestyleInfrastructure]], [[StartupAcceleratorBatchSelection]], [[StartupCommunityInfrastructure]], and [[EquityHackerHouseModel]]: selected founders receive housing, food, community, mentors, investor access, and fundraising support while contributing equity to a [[BatchEquityPool]].

The source says [[NickLink]] and [[PeterDAmbrosio]] founded The Residency after an earlier athlete-house idea was redirected by [[SamAltman]] toward founders and students. By the episode's account, The Residency operates as a C Corp rather than a traditional fund, has five San Francisco houses plus other houses in New York, Berlin, and Bangalore, and claims that companies from recent cohorts have reached roughly $3 billion in combined valuation.

The Residency is also the episode's risk case. [[SebastianTheResidency]] estimates annual San Francisco operating costs around $2 million to $2.5 million, while Peter says expansion through franchise-like partners could dilute quality. The model therefore depends on whether early equity value can eventually offset expensive hospitality operations without weakening founder trust.
