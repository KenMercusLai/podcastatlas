# Otto the Great

## Overview

Otto the Great was the Saxon ruler who defeated the Hungarians, pursued Roman imperial status through the papacy, and negotiated [[OttoII]]'s marriage to [[Theophano]].

## Current Profile

The episode places Otto within a fragmented Latin West in which military victory and Saxon power were insufficient for universal imperial standing. He presented himself as an heir to Charlemagne and required papal coronation and Roman prestige, then sought a Byzantine princess to strengthen recognition across the eastern-western divide.

The first embassy failed when its language demoted the Byzantine ruler to “emperor of the Greeks.” Negotiations succeeded after [[JohnITzimiskes]] took power, and Otto accepted Theophano despite court disappointment that she was a step-niece rather than daughter of the emperor.

## Key Characteristics

- Saxon military ruler strengthened by victory over the Hungarians.
- Claimant to Carolingian and Roman imperial inheritance.
- User of papal recognition and marriage diplomacy as legitimacy instruments.
- Negotiator across competing eastern and western definitions of Roman rule.
- Founder of the dynastic setting inherited by Otto II and Otto III.
