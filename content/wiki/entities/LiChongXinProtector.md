## Overview

李崇 / Li Chong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-652-wangmang-de-waike-shoushu-ltdiqldskazy-cb6igdmrv5huxm9|Hanji 652]] as Wang Mang's Western Regions protector whose remaining force retreats to [[QiuciKingdom|龟兹]] after [[WangJunXinGeneral|王骏]]'s failed expedition.

## Current Profile

Li Chong's current profile is an institutional-collapse profile rather than a full biography. As Western Regions protector under [[XinDynasty|新朝]], he is sent west with Wang Jun and [[GuoQingXinGeneral|郭清]] in the same mission that ends with Yanqi's ambush.

After Wang Jun's group is killed, Li Chong gathers the remaining troops and stays at Qiuci. The episode says that after Wang Mang's regime collapses and Li Chong dies, the Western Regions break contact with ancient China, making him a last thin institutional link in this source's frontier narrative.

## Key Characteristics

- Xin-era Western Regions protector.
- Co-dispatched with Wang Jun and Guo Qing during Wang Mang's failed western mission.
- Survives the ambush branch and consolidates remnants at Qiuci.
- Functions as a residual institutional link before later disconnection.
- Current evidence is limited to the Hanji 652 frontier episode.
