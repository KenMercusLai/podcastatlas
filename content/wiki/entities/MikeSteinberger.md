# Mike Steinberger

## Overview
Mike Steinberger is the reporter/explainer in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-8eb9e254581eaa220d9428df389214d5-27844515-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-8eb9e254581eaa220d9428df389214d5-27844515|Love in the time of Palantir]], where he distinguishes [[Palantir]]'s stated software role from the public fear that it personally holds everyone's data.

## Current Profile
Steinberger functions as the episode's interpretive source on Palantir. His core distinction is that Palantir does not own, buy, or sell data, but provides software that helps customers integrate and use their own data. He also supplies the civil-liberties warning: once software makes separate data sources easy to enrich, search, and act on, customer control rather than vendor ownership becomes the central risk.

## Key Characteristics
- Reporter/explainer focused on separating Palantir's data ownership claims from its operational power.
- Source for the episode's business-use example around [[Airbus]] and A350 production visibility.
- Source for the post-9/11 origin account linking Palantir to CIA counterterrorism data integration.
- Source for the ICE concern that software can make many datasets faster to search and operationalize.
- Boundary-setting voice who keeps Palantir's vendor role distinct from customer decisions.
