# Kirk Tanner

Kirk Tanner appears in [[reeses-heir-vs-chocolate-skimpflation]] through [[Bloomberg]] reporting cited by the episode. The source says Bloomberg reported that Tanner, as [[Hershey]] CEO, decided to change the ingredient approach shortly after taking the role, before [[BradReese]] made his public complaints.

The source uses Tanner mainly to keep causality disciplined. Hershey's announced return to classic milk and dark chocolate recipes by 2027 may validate the importance of [[ChocolateCompound|compound chocolate]] as an issue, but the episode does not prove that Brad's campaign caused the decision.
