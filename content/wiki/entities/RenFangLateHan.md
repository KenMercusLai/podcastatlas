## Overview

任芳 / Ren Fang is the assassin hired by [[ShanKuangLateHan|单匡]] in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-851-hanhuandi-weihe-zhuding-yichou-wannian-ltgm1ix6cgqb-kqo9bztfrzjgeun|Hanji 851]]. His source-bounded role is small but structurally important: he makes the Shan Kuang case a witness-suppression case, not merely a corruption accusation.

## Current Profile

Shan Kuang hires Ren Fang to kill [[WeiYuLateHan|魏宇]], the investigator whose report exposes Shan Kuang's corruption and implicates [[ShanChaoLateHan|单超]]. Wei Yu detects the plan, captures Ren Fang, and has him held in [[LuoyangLateHan|Luoyang]].

Ren Fang then remains important because Shan Kuang tries to arrange his escape. That jailbreak attempt gives [[YangBingLateHan|杨秉]] the procedural ground to request that Shan Kuang be brought to Luoyang for confrontation, but the court punishes Yang Bing instead.

## Key Characteristics

- Hired assassin in Shan Kuang's attempt to eliminate Wei Yu.
- Evidence node showing witness suppression inside a corruption investigation.
- Captured offender whose Luoyang custody becomes the basis for a confrontation request.
- Source-bounded minor actor whose value is procedural rather than biographical.
