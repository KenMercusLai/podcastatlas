## Overview

明朝 / Ming Dynasty enters this wiki page through Hanji 422-2 as the source's most developed example of an institutionalized tribute economy.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Ming tribute as a formalized diplomatic and economic system. Tribute goods could be assigned official values, envoys could receive hosted reception, and foreign polities or merchants could profit from return gifts and above-market pricing. The page currently does not attempt a full Ming-dynasty history; it records the dynasty as a tribute-system case.

## Key Characteristics

- Dynasty used by the source as a mature tribute-system example.
- Court whose tribute pricing,回赐, and reception practices could subsidize foreign recognition.
- Setting for fraud and conflict when tribute access became profitable.
- Counterpart in Mongol horse-tribute and Japanese tribute-competition cases in the episode's telling.
