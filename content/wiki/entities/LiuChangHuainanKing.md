# 刘长 / Liu Chang (Huainan king)

刘长 / Liu Chang (Huainan king) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-187-yingbu-panluan-liubang-weihe-tuobingti-qinzheng-lvjafotlnvmyqmwaeav3novz1dox|Hanji 187]] as the Liu-family replacement named after [[YingBu|英布 / 黥布]] openly rebels. This page is intentionally distinct from [[LiuChang|刘畅 / Liu Chang]], the modern podcast speaker already present in the wiki.

The source gives Liu Chang only as a title appointment, not as a developed biography. After [[XueGongHanAdviser|薛公 / Xue Gong (Han adviser)]] predicts that Ying Bu will choose the lower rebellion strategy, [[LiuBang|刘邦]] abolishes Ying Bu's title and makes Liu Chang king of [[HuainanKingdomEarlyHan|淮南国]].

In wiki terms, the appointment extends [[SameSurnameKingEnfeoffment|同姓王分封]] into the Huainan crisis. The center does not merely suppress a different-surname king; it immediately marks the intended successor structure by replacing the rebel's royal status with a Liu-clan title.
