# Boris Johnson

## Overview

Boris Johnson is a pivotal [[Brexit]] campaigner and former British prime minister whose referendum role, post-Brexit settlement, immigration record, fall from office, and influence over the succession appear across two sources.

## Current Profile

[[keep-qualms-and-carry-on-a-decade-after-brexit-6a3a53028a3c8822ab345d4b]] presents Johnson as a catalyst in Leave's narrow victory and the leader who later implemented a harder Brexit, with cumulative trade friction and migration outcomes that disappointed supporters of "take back control." [[246-the-fall-of-liz-truss-glt3484252092]] adds the immediate succession crisis: his fall was already chaotic, his levelling-up approach represented one post-Brexit project, and the hosts argue that resentment toward [[RishiSunak]] helped shape his support for [[LizTruss]].

## Key Characteristics

- High-impact Leave campaigner in the 2016 referendum.
- Prime minister associated with a harder Brexit settlement.
- Led a post-Brexit programme centered more on levelling up than Truss-style deregulation.
- Left office amid an unusually chaotic Conservative succession.
- Continued to influence the contest through personal and factional relationships.

## Evidence

- Referendum and settlement: [[keep-qualms-and-carry-on-a-decade-after-brexit-6a3a53028a3c8822ab345d4b]] links Johnson's Leave support to the narrow result and his government to hard Brexit.
- Migration tension: [[keep-qualms-and-carry-on-a-decade-after-brexit-6a3a53028a3c8822ab345d4b]] connects his post-Brexit system to migration levels that frustrated control expectations.
- Competing Brexit projects: [[246-the-fall-of-liz-truss-glt3484252092]] contrasts Johnson's levelling-up orientation with Truss's deregulatory programme.
- Succession politics: [[246-the-fall-of-liz-truss-glt3484252092]] attributes Johnson's stance toward Sunak and Truss partly to personal resentment.

## Qualifications

The sources are interpretive podcast accounts rather than a full biography or policy audit. Johnson's motives in the succession contest, the coherence of "Johnsonism," and the causal share of Brexit in later instability remain source-scoped.
