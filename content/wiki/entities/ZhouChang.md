# 周昌 / Zhou Chang

周昌 / Zhou Chang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-181-jiemi-liubang-feili-taizi-de-zhenshi-yuanyin-lgnbvnnfwxe4weupqgz-rh9cbp2s|Hanji 181]] as the yushi dafu who openly opposes [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s plan to replace crown prince [[LiuYing|刘盈]] with [[LiuRuyi|刘如意]]. The source emphasizes his stutter and agitation: even without a polished argument, he refuses to accept an order to depose the crown prince.

The remonstrance makes Zhou Chang politically important to [[LuZhi|吕雉]]. After the court scene, Lu Zhi thanks him because she reads the intervention as saving Liu Ying's succession. This makes Zhou Chang both a ministerial check on Liu Bang's preference and a person whom the empress branch must visibly respect.

The second half of the episode turns that prestige into a protective assignment. [[ZhaoYao|赵尧]] recommends that Liu Bang place a strong, feared minister beside Liu Ruyi in Zhao, and Liu Bang sends Zhou Chang as Zhao chancellor. The host treats this as inadequate protection: Zhou Chang loses central court position, lacks military command, and can protect Liu Ruyi only within tight limits.
