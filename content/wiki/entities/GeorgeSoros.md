# George Soros

[[179-xian-jiu-riyuan-zai-jiu-changzhai-jiuhuoduizhang-beisente-zai-zou-yi-tiao-zenyang-de-gangsi-lqjc6vz-1ungnkpdw9d5kbsa-ouo]] invokes George Soros as the hedge-fund and macro-trading context for [[ScottBessent|Scott Bessent / 贝森特]]. The source uses Bessent's work around Soros's sterling and yen trades to explain why Bessent sees currencies, fiscal capacity, central-bank credibility, and market timing as one system.

## Source Position
- Soros appears as background rather than as the episode's main actor.
- The source treats the 1992 sterling episode as a lesson in how clear policy-defense lines can become speculative targets.
- Soros's framework is contrasted with Bessent's later policy role: the former attacks contradictions from outside the state, while the latter must manage contradictions from inside the Treasury.
