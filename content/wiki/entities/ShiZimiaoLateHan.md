# 史子眇 / Shi Zimiao (late Han)

史子眇 / Shi Zimiao (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-943-quanshi-kaoyan-renxing-beihou-cangzhe-juda-xiongxian-lg7ia5xok-swdmhml35yv9qqkvt|Hanji 943]] as the figure who raises [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]] outside the palace in the source account. The episode uses him mainly to explain why Liu Bian is called "史侯" before accession.

The page is intentionally narrow. Shi Zimiao does not drive the court struggle in this episode, but his foster-care role helps mark the two-prince split: Liu Bian is associated with the Shi household and [[EmpressDowagerHe|何太后]], while Liu Xie, later [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], is associated with [[EmpressDowagerDongLateHan|董太后]] as "董侯."
