# Katy Milkman

## Overview

Katy Milkman is a behavioral scientist represented in the supplied source through research and practical frameworks for turning beneficial intentions into sustained action.

## Current Profile

In [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-2fc443663e259ff389aeeb81792c6086-50338835|You, But Better]], Milkman treats distraction, impulsiveness, forgetfulness, low enjoyment, procrastination, and [[PresentBias]] as design constraints rather than moral defects. Her main contribution is diagnostic: identify the barrier first, then match it with enjoyment, smaller steps, feedback, reminders, fresh starts, deadlines, defaults, automation, or precommitment.

## Key Characteristics

- Frames behavior change as an obstacle-identification and intervention-design problem.
- Developed [[TemptationBundling]] from pairing reserved entertainment with exercise.
- Connects temporal landmarks to both the motivating and potentially disruptive sides of the [[FreshStartEffect]].
- Uses large field experiments and familiar personal examples to translate behavioral research into practical design.
- Treats future-self protection as a legitimate use of deadlines, defaults, automation, and commitment devices.
