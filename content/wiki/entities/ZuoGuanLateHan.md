## Overview

左悺 / Zuo Guan is a late-Han eunuch marquis whose current profile links the post-Liang [[FiveMarquisesHuanEra|Five Marquises]] field in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-853-jiemi-hanchao-ban-de-zhaoshi-gu-er-lvsa2pby8iyfevfoobobpik52mij|Hanji 853]] to the reward-gatekeeping case in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-857-xuehui-gongying-cai-shi-zhenzheng-de-yingjia-lh3zx6pw-4ib7owiz8c68bv-9htn|Hanji 857]]. The combined evidence makes him a named example of eunuch power reaching both local office hierarchy and military reward processing.

## Current Profile

Hanji 853 supplies the earlier status and family layer. After [[ShanChaoLateHan|单超]] dies, the remaining marquises become more indulgent in the source's account. Zuo Guan is named as one of the Five Marquises, and his brother [[ZuoShengLateHan|左胜]] holds the Hedong commandery post. [[ZhaoQiLateHan|赵岐]], then Pishi county head, resigns rather than serve under that superior.

Hanji 857 gives Zuo Guan a second narrow but important role. [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]]'s frontier service should have positioned him for marquisate-level recognition, but Zuo Guan and [[XuHuangLateHan|徐璜]] repeatedly send guests to ask about his merit in a way the source reads as a request for gifts. Huangfu Gui refuses to pay or apologize. The eunuch side becomes angry and revives accusations against him, after which Huangfu's subordinates try to settle the matter while Huangfu himself will not bend.

## Key Characteristics

- Huan-era eunuch marquis in the post-Liang reward field.
- Family-power node whose brother Zuo Sheng's office triggers Zhao Qi's resignation.
- Paired with Xu Huang in gift-solicitation pressure around Huangfu Gui's military merit.
- Uses intermediary guests rather than formal public accusation at the first stage.
- Helps turn refusal to pay or apologize into renewed slander and punishment exposure.

## Qualifications

This page remains bounded to the two listed source notes and does not reconstruct Zuo Guan's full career. Hanji 853 supplies family-office and Five Marquises context; Hanji 857 supplies the Huangfu Gui reward-gatekeeping branch. ASR uncertainty around 左冠 is normalized to 左悺 because the Huan-era eunuch context and existing Xu Huang pairing match the received late-Han route.

## What Changed

- The profile now includes Hanji 853's Five Marquises status and Zuo Sheng/Zhao Qi local-office layer.
- The Huangfu Gui reward-gatekeeping role remains the main evidence for Zuo Guan's direct action.
