## Overview

左悺 / Zuo Guan is a late-Han eunuch marquis whose current profile now begins in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-829-junzi-weishenme-doubuguo-xiaoren-lthvnstkfu8n9thx1sz6yezl4yqq|Hanji 829]] with slander against [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] and [[DuQiaoLateHan|杜乔]], then runs from the founding [[FiveMarquisesHuanEra|Five Marquises]] reward in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-848-shunjian-kuatai-wo-weihe-shuo-ta-si-you-yu-gu-lgqvgmxlwga2fknmtxpj3ya9chk0|Hanji 848]] through the post-Liang arrogance field in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-853-jiemi-hanchao-ban-de-zhaoshi-gu-er-lvsa2pby8iyfevfoobobpik52mij|Hanji 853]] to the reward-gatekeeping case in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-857-xuehui-gongying-cai-shi-zhenzheng-de-yingjia-lh3zx6pw-4ib7owiz8c68bv-9htn|Hanji 857]]. The combined evidence makes him a named example of eunuch power reaching imperial resentment, palace coup reward, local office hierarchy, and military reward processing.

## Current Profile

Hanji 829 supplies the earliest current speech-access layer. Zuo Guan and [[TangHengLateHan|唐衡]] tell [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] that Du Qiao and Li Gu opposed Huan's accession and considered him unfit, making Zuo Guan part of the channel by which succession politics becomes the ruler's personal resentment.

Hanji 848 supplies the founding reward layer. Zuo Guan is named as one of the five eunuchs rewarded after Huan destroys [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]], receives a 10,000-household county marquisate, and is promoted to zhongchangshi with Tang Heng.

Hanji 853 supplies the family layer after the group is established. After [[ShanChaoLateHan|单超]] dies, the remaining marquises become more indulgent in the source's account. Zuo Guan is again named as one of the Five Marquises, and his brother [[ZuoShengLateHan|左胜]] holds the Hedong commandery post. [[ZhaoQiLateHan|赵岐]], then Pishi county head, resigns rather than serve under that superior.

Hanji 857 gives Zuo Guan a second narrow but important role. [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]]'s frontier service should have positioned him for marquisate-level recognition, but Zuo Guan and [[XuHuangLateHan|徐璜]] repeatedly send guests to ask about his merit in a way the source reads as a request for gifts. Huangfu Gui refuses to pay or apologize. The eunuch side becomes angry and revives accusations against him, after which Huangfu's subordinates try to settle the matter while Huangfu himself will not bend.

## Key Characteristics

- Huan-era eunuch whose earliest current role is slander against Li Gu and Du Qiao.
- Marquis named in the founding post-Liang reward field.
- Zhongchangshi promotion recipient after the anti-Liang coup.
- Family-power node whose brother Zuo Sheng's office triggers Zhao Qi's resignation.
- Paired with Xu Huang in gift-solicitation pressure around Huangfu Gui's military merit.
- Uses intermediary guests rather than formal public accusation at the first stage.
- Helps turn refusal to pay or apologize into renewed slander and punishment exposure.

## Qualifications

This page remains bounded to the listed source notes and does not reconstruct Zuo Guan's full career. Hanji 829 supplies an earlier slander-channel role but not a full office profile at that date. Hanji 848 supplies the founding reward and zhongchangshi promotion; Hanji 853 supplies family-office context; Hanji 857 supplies the Huangfu Gui reward-gatekeeping branch. ASR uncertainty around 左冠 and 左贯 is normalized to 左悺 because the Huan-era eunuch context and existing Xu Huang pairing match the received late-Han route.

## What Changed

- Hanji 829 adds Zuo Guan's earliest current role as a slander-channel actor against Li Gu and Du Qiao.
- Hanji 848 adds Zuo Guan's founding Five Marquises reward and zhongchangshi promotion.
- The profile now includes Hanji 853's Five Marquises status and Zuo Sheng/Zhao Qi local-office layer.
- The Huangfu Gui reward-gatekeeping role remains the main evidence for Zuo Guan's direct action.
