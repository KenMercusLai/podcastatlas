# 法迪奥 / Fadior

法迪奥 / Fadior is the [[Foshan|佛山]] [[Shunde|顺德]] stainless-steel home-furnishing company used in [[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw]] as the comparatively positive [[FactorySecondGeneration|厂二代]] succession case. The episode describes it as a mid-sized, leading company in high-end stainless-steel cabinets and whole-home customization.

The company matters because its second-generation path is staged rather than symbolic. [[ShenZhenwen|沈真文]] returned in 2018, worked from after-sales customer service through the chairman-assistant role, and then became general manager of marketing after the 2020 pandemic. Her brother returned in 2021 and took responsibility for management, technology, production, and R&D, while their father remained chairman.

## Source Position
- Fadior's stainless-steel home category required years of market education because it was more expensive than wood or panel alternatives.
- The source says consumer acceptance improved around 2018 as buyers recognized stainless steel's fit with humid southern environments and the brand's long warranty promise.
- The case illustrates succession when the younger generation enters a market that is becoming more favorable rather than a business already near collapse.
- Its internal division of labor makes [[GenderedFamilyBusinessRoles]] concrete: brand and dealer-facing work sits with Shen Zhenwen, while production and technology sit with her brother.
