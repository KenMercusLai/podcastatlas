# Sweden

Sweden appears in [[the-elon-game-musks-vision-of-the-future-6a633594d19896314260e5c4]] as the country case for a tougher youth-criminal-justice turn. The episode says Sweden is opening [[ChildPrisonUnits|child prison units]] in eight prisons and that children aged 15 or older convicted of serious crimes will be sent to them from early July.

The source also places Sweden inside the broader [[YouthCriminalResponsibility]] debate. Its government first wanted to lower the age of criminal responsibility to 13, withdrew that bill when support was insufficient, and planned to return with a 14-year-old threshold.
