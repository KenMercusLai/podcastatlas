# Global Institute for the Future

## Overview
The Global Institute for the Future is an organization that [[JorgeBrown]] says he created to consolidate lessons from work with leaders across sectors and examine decisions shaping the future.

## Current Profile
In [[defaultmp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-725b059f65d6ae95b04d1dbc8a6cf2dd-104571730-defaultmp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-725b059f65d6ae95b04d1dbc8a6cf2dd-104571730]], the institute functions as the organizational setting for Brown's cross-domain agenda. That agenda links AI, democracy, resources, institutional legitimacy, longevity, automation, security, commerce, and future generations, and seeks forums that move from competing views to measurable commitments.

## Key Characteristics
- Presented as a futures-oriented organization founded by [[JorgeBrown]].
- Draws on leadership experience across public, private, and social domains.
- Treats future challenges as connected rather than as independent policy silos.
- Emphasizes ethics, resilience, dialogue, and implementation.
