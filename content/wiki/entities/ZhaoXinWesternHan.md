## Overview

赵信 / Zhao Xin is presented as a Xiongnu-origin commander whose knowledge first serves Han reconnaissance under [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]], then becomes a strategic resource for the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] after he returns. The current evidence distinguishes him from [[ZhaoXinchenWesternHan|召信臣]] and other Zhao Xin names by using the Western Han-Xiongnu suffix.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-296-1-weiqing-guashuai-chuzheng-wei-hanchao-diaoda-xiongnu-lkr5lcogip_nx5uoycleuqzp9you|Hanji 296-1]] supplies the earlier Han-service layer. It calls Zhao Xin a former lesser Xiongnu king and chancellor, says he had surrendered to Han and received a marquisate, and places him at the front of Wei Qing's [[FirstDingxiangCampaignWesternHan|first Dingxiang sortie]]. The host interprets that assignment as an attempt to use his opponent knowledge to locate the chanyu's main force. Zhao instead first contacts a rear guard, whose defeat reveals that the decisive formation remains elsewhere.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-299-2-xihan-caizheng-da-weiji-hanwudi-ruhe-gao-qian-fqf4vv3uhemqey3qqk6cdiht_2cc|Hanji 299-2]] says Zhao Xin later returned to the Xiongnu after the campaign. The episode therefore describes the event as renewed defection rather than a first change of allegiance.

The chanyu reportedly grants him the title 自次王, marries an elder sister to him, and consults him on resistance to Han. Zhao Xin recommends moving north of the desert, refusing to stay close to the Han frontier, drawing Han forces into a long and exhausting advance, and attacking only after fatigue. His durable significance is thus intermediary knowledge: familiarity with both opponents can produce a strategy that avoids direct symmetry with the stronger force.

## Key Characteristics

- Xiongnu-origin lesser king who later served Han and then returned to the Xiongnu.
- Front commander whose opponent knowledge is used in Han's first Dingxiang search for the chanyu.
- Defector whose knowledge of Han operations becomes a strategic resource for his receiving side.
- Recipient of the episode-attributed title 自次王 and a marriage alliance with the chanyu's elder sister.
- Advocate of strategic depth, avoidance, induced fatigue, and delayed counterattack.
- Figure whose strategy responds to Han strength under [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] and [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] rather than erasing that strength.

## Qualifications

This page rests on two compact podcast summaries rather than a complete Zhao Xin biography or primary-text comparison. His Xiongnu offices, surrender to Han, Han marquisate, exact vanguard role, rear-guard contact, later return, 自次王 title, marriage, wording of the advice, degree of influence, and the later effectiveness of the strategy remain source-scoped. Familiarity with both sides makes his use as guide and adviser plausible within the episodes but does not prove that he uniquely located the rear guard or caused every later Xiongnu withdrawal and Han difficulty.

## What Changed

- Added the earlier Han-service phase in which Zhao Xin's Xiongnu knowledge is used for front-line search and first contact.
- Reframed his cross-system knowledge as a resource used successively by both sides rather than only after defection.

## Relationships

- [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] - polity of origin and receiving side after his return from Han service.
- [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] - opposing ruler whose deep anti-Xiongnu campaigns create the strategic problem Zhao Xin addresses.
- [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] - Han commander whose campaign provides the immediate defection setting.
- [[FirstDingxiangCampaignWesternHan|西汉第一次定襄出击]] - earlier operation in which Zhao Xin serves at the front before his later return to Xiongnu.
- [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] - powerful Han commander named by the episode as part of the military imbalance facing Xiongnu.
- [[ZhaoXinchenWesternHan|召信臣]] - distinct Western Han figure separated by canonical key and semantic suffix.
