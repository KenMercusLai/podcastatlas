## Overview

赵信 / Zhao Xin is presented as a Xiongnu-origin commander who had entered Han service, returned to the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], and converted knowledge of both military systems into strategic advice against Han deep advances. The current evidence distinguishes him from [[ZhaoXinchenWesternHan|召信臣]] and other Zhao Xin names by using the Western Han-Xiongnu suffix.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-299-2-xihan-caizheng-da-weiji-hanwudi-ruhe-gao-qian-fqf4vv3uhemqey3qqk6cdiht_2cc|Hanji 299-2]] says Zhao Xin was originally a lesser Xiongnu king, entered Han service, and then returned to the Xiongnu after the campaign. The episode therefore describes the event as renewed defection rather than a first change of allegiance.

The chanyu reportedly grants him the title 自次王, marries an elder sister to him, and consults him on resistance to Han. Zhao Xin recommends moving north of the desert, refusing to stay close to the Han frontier, drawing Han forces into a long and exhausting advance, and attacking only after fatigue. His durable significance is thus intermediary knowledge: familiarity with both opponents can produce a strategy that avoids direct symmetry with the stronger force.

## Key Characteristics

- Xiongnu-origin lesser king who later served Han and then returned to the Xiongnu.
- Defector whose knowledge of Han operations becomes a strategic resource for his receiving side.
- Recipient of the episode-attributed title 自次王 and a marriage alliance with the chanyu's elder sister.
- Advocate of strategic depth, avoidance, induced fatigue, and delayed counterattack.
- Figure whose strategy responds to Han strength under [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] and [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] rather than erasing that strength.

## Qualifications

This page rests on one compact podcast summary rather than a complete Zhao Xin biography or primary-text comparison. His earlier surrender to Han, offices, exact battlefield role, 自次王 title, marriage, wording of the advice, degree of influence, and the later effectiveness of the strategy remain source-scoped. Familiarity with both sides makes the advice plausible within the episode but does not prove that every later Xiongnu withdrawal or Han difficulty resulted from Zhao Xin alone.
