# Ratepayer Protection Act

## Overview

The Ratepayer Protection Act is federal legislation described by [[MarketplaceTech]] as requiring states to consider measures that make data-center operators bear more of their associated electricity costs.

## Current Profile

The source says the bill passed the House with overwhelming bipartisan support but failed to advance in the Senate by three votes. Senate Democrats called its “consider” standard too weak and promoted the [[GridSavingsAct]], while Republicans viewed passage as a potential achievement for Senator John Husted during a difficult reelection campaign.

## Key Characteristics

- Targets data-center electricity cost allocation.
- Uses a state-consideration requirement rather than the stronger mandate critics sought.
- Combined broad House support with a failed Senate vote.
- Became entangled with Senate campaign and chamber-control incentives.
