# 孔川 / Kong Chuan

孔川 / Kong Chuan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-64-gudai-gangjing-gongsunlong-yangchengji-lkrchesllfi9ipgfapft7av2mnh5]] as a [[LuState|鲁国]] visitor to [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. The episode identifies him by the courtesy name 子高 and as a seventh-generation descendant of [[Confucius|孔子]].

His main wiki role is the "臧三耳" exchange with [[GongsunLong|公孙龙]]. Kong Chuan cannot answer Gongsun Long immediately, but he later tells [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] that the apparent victory is hollow: saying a person has three ears is hard to prove and false, while saying a person has two ears is easy and true.

That reframing makes Kong Chuan the episode's first voice for [[TruthSeekingDebateNorm|求明理的辩论规范]]. He does not beat Gongsun Long by inventing a more elaborate paradox. He changes the evaluation criterion from who can corner an opponent to whether the claim is true, useful, and worth the argumentative cost.

## Key Claims
- Kong Chuan is described as a Lu man, courtesy name Zigao, and descendant of Confucius.
- He initially appears to lose to Gongsun Long's "臧三耳" argument.
- His counter is evaluative: difficult falsehood should not outrank easy truth.
- Pingyuanjun accepts Kong Chuan's standard and later tells Gongsun Long not to debate him again.
- The episode uses Kong Chuan to turn debate from performance into a truth and judgment problem.
