## Overview

王接 / Wang Jie enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-476-kongzi-de-12-shisun-kongba-jielu-zhichang-yinmi-ltjlhphlrqnazeqh2cc2hgsvrvwc|Hanji 476]] as the official appointed Da Sima and Cheqi Jiangjun after the Yongguang 1 disaster-era resignations.

## Current Profile

The episode gives Wang Jie a compact personnel-reshuffle role rather than a full biography. After [[YuDingguoWesternHan|于定国]], [[ShiGaoWesternHan|史高]], and [[XueGuangdeWesternHan|薛广德]] resign under famine pressure, Wang Jie is named to the Da Sima and Cheqi Jiangjun position. The host identifies him as son of Pingchang Hou Wang Wugu and as a kin-related figure tied to [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]'s maternal family line.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official appearing in a Yongguang 1 personnel transition.
- Appointed Da Sima and Cheqi Jiangjun after senior resignations.
- Identified by the source as son of Pingchang Hou Wang Wugu.
- Used structurally as part of the new court arrangement after disaster pressure.
