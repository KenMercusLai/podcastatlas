# 霍谞 / Huo Xu (late Han)

## Overview

Huo Xu enters the wiki through Hanji 877 as the rescuer whom [[FanPangLateHan|范滂]] visits after release from the first Party Prohibition imprisonment.

## Current Profile

The source does not reconstruct Huo Xu's full career. Its interest is relational and ethical: Fan Pang treats him as a genuine life-saving benefactor, yet refuses to stage gratitude as a public debt. The episode uses that restraint to show that in a politically dangerous reputation field, some obligations can be honored through tacit mutual understanding rather than conspicuous ritual.

Huo Xu is therefore important as the silent counterpart in a Party Prohibition relationship. His rescue matters enough to make Fan Pang visit, but the source's lesson depends on both sides not turning the rescue into display, claim, or reciprocal theater.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Han figure identified as Fan Pang's life-saving benefactor after the first Party Prohibition case.
- Relational counterpart whose importance is shown through Fan Pang's visit rather than through independent action in the episode.
- Silent-benefactor figure whose aid is not converted into public gratitude extraction.
- Source-bounded actor in the episode's ethics of non-display under political risk.
