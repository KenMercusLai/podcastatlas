# 种劭 / Zhong Shao (late Han)

种劭 / Zhong Shao (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-973-changpingguan-zhizhan-shengsi-zhidi-zhanhou-jing-chonggui-yuhao-lgazc9dewfayb8qnkncfkfa12yet|Hanji 973]] as a court remonstrance official in [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]]. He joins [[MaYuLateHan|马宇]] and [[LiuFanLateHan|刘范]] in trying to coordinate with [[MaTeng|马腾]] against [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]].

The episode gives Zhong Shao the most vivid message-security detail in the plot. He hides a secret letter inside a hollowed bamboo slip, but Li Jue's side has an infiltrator and reportedly identifies him through distinctive ink scent. After the plan leaks, Zhong Shao flees to Huaili and is killed when [[FanChouLateHan|樊稠]], [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]], and [[LiLiLateHan|李利]] attack.
