# 725沙龙 / 725 Salon

725沙龙 appears in [[149-baiwu-teji-he-liangwei-laomeitiren-mantan-boke-meijie-yiji-shengyin-shengtai-de-weilai-lihbq8vujsqxvb7h2-pz2xm-eqga]] as a New York Chinese cultural and arts dialogue project with more than a decade of history. The source says this episode is also the project's first attempt to make a purely online podcast.

For the wiki, 725沙龙 is a small but useful context for [[ChinesePodcastEcosystem]]: it shows how an offline cultural-dialogue project can test podcasting as a new distribution and conversation format without becoming a native podcast producer from the start.
