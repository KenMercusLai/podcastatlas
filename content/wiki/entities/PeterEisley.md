# Peter Eisley

Peter Eisley appears in [[under-strain-breaking-the-british-state-6a799563523b3dfcd83c7271]] as the survivor voice in the episode's [[ClergyMandatoryReportingConflict]] segment. The source says he grew up in a religious Catholic family in 1960s Wisconsin, attended a seminary high school, and says he was sexually assaulted and raped by a Catholic priest as a child.

His role in the wiki is not as a general biography but as a source-grounded child-safety witness. Eisley argues that carve-outs for confession are illogical and dangerous because confession can reveal crimes that otherwise remain hidden, including when abused children disclose guilt or shame.
