# 凌操 / Ling Cao (late Han)

## Overview

凌操 / Ling Cao enters the bounded wiki as [[LingTong|凌统]]'s father, whose death by [[GanNing|甘宁]] creates a blood feud that [[SunQuan|孙权]] must contain inside the Eastern Wu command structure.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1086-sunquan-beihao-zhuang-rentou-de-muhe-weihe-pianpian-fangguo-sufei-lt7xfjunv42n9532eqog-8k64odf|Hanji 1086]] says Gan Ning kills Ling Cao with an arrow while saving [[HuangZu|黄祖]]. The episode does not develop Ling Cao's wider career, but his death remains operationally important because Ling Tong later serves in the same regime as Gan Ning and seeks revenge until Sun Quan intervenes.

## Key Characteristics

- Father of Ling Tong.
- Killed by Gan Ning while Gan Ning serves Huang Zu's side.
- His death creates a durable private grievance inside Sun Quan's later officer corps.
