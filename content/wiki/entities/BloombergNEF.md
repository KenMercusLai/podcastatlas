# BloombergNEF

BloombergNEF appears in [[tech-20260407-0407-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260407-0407-mp-tech-pod-128]] through [[ColinMcCarrick]]. The episode uses the research organization as the source for the claim that EVs in [[China]] are now fully cost-competitive with internal-combustion vehicles on sticker price.

In this source, BloombergNEF functions as the bridge between battery-market analysis and consumer adoption. Its analyst links [[LithiumIronPhosphateBatteries|LFP batteries]], supply-chain integration, and [[ElectricVehiclePriceParity]] into one explanation for why the Chinese market is ahead of the U.S. price curve.
