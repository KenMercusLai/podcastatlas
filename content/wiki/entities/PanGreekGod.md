# Pan / Greek God

## Overview

Pan appears in the wiki as the Greek god whose reported encounter with Pheidippides gives the Marathon story a religious explanation for panic and later cult attention.

## Current Profile

The source says Pheidippides encountered Pan in the mountains after running to Sparta. Pan complains that Athens has neglected him and promises future help if properly honored. The hosts connect Pan to the Greek root behind "panic," making the divine story a way to explain fear overtaking the Persians in battle.

The page therefore treats Pan not as a general mythology entry, but as a Marathon source-layering case: divine agency becomes part of how a battle is remembered, explained, and ritualized.

## Key Characteristics

- Pan is linked to Pheidippides' reported return from Sparta.
- The god's complaint frames religious neglect and future honor as part of civic memory.
- The episode connects Pan to panic, giving divine shape to battlefield fear.
- Pan's role is evidence of mythic and religious layering around Marathon.
