# Picloram

## Overview
Picloram is presented in the source as a persistent herbicide flagged by an early-onset colorectal cancer research signal discussed in Friedberg's science segment.

## Current Profile
The episode describes picloram as a Dow-developed herbicide used on rangeland, roads, railroads, industrial sites, highways, and utility corridors. Friedberg says the discussed study linked county-level use estimates with early-onset colorectal cancer frequency and gene-expression patterns, making picloram a candidate for updated safety review rather than a settled causal agent.
