# 殷墟

殷墟 enters the wiki through [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] as the archaeological site that let the [[InstituteOfHistoryAndPhilology|历史语言研究所]] push the evidentiary basis of Chinese civilization history earlier. The episode says repeated excavations at Anyang made Chinese archaeology internationally visible.

The source also uses 殷墟 to show how field discovery, publication, and global recognition interact. [[FuSinian|傅斯年]] brings foreign scholarly attention to the site, while Lu Xun's reported criticism of early reports keeps the page tied to the difference between important excavation and uneven publication.

## Key Claims
- 殷墟 makes ancient history answerable to material excavation, not only transmitted texts.
- The site is a core example of [[FieldworkAsKnowledgeMethod]] in archaeology.
- International recognition matters in the episode, but it is grounded in Chinese institutional excavation work.
