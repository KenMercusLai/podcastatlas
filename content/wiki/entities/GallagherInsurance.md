## Overview
Gallagher is the traditional insurance-brokerage perspective in the Marketplace Tech discussion of coverage for AI errors.

## Current Profile
Through cyber-risk executive [[JohnFarleyGallagher|John Farley]], Gallagher describes an incumbent market moving from policies that often say nothing about AI toward explicit scrutiny, standardized language, and possible exclusions requiring regulatory approval.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents the established insurance market rather than an AI-insurance startup.
- Frames AI as a new and difficult-to-quantify business exposure.
- Tracks policy-language standardization and possible AI exclusions.
- Contrasts AI's thin loss history with mature catastrophe-risk modeling.

## Evidence

- [[tech-20260917-0917-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260917-0917-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports Farley's view that most traditional policies do not mention AI and that insurers are now examining explicit wording.
