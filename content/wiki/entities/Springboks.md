# Springboks

## Overview

The Springboks are the South African national rugby team presented as the central contested sport symbol in [[NelsonMandela]]'s post-apartheid reconciliation strategy.

## Current Profile

The episode treats the Springboks as more than a team. Under apartheid, the jersey and name were read by many black South Africans as symbols of exclusion and white rule, while for many [[Afrikaners]] they carried pride and identity. Mandela's decision to retain and embrace the team, work with [[FrancoisPienaar]], and appear in a Springbok shirt at the [[RugbyWorldCup1995|1995 Rugby World Cup]] final turned that charged symbol into a practical test of shared nationhood.

## Key Characteristics

- National rugby team whose identity had been tightly associated with [[Afrikaners|Afrikaner]] pride.
- Apartheid-era symbol that many black South Africans saw as representing exclusion.
- Team readmitted to international rugby before South Africa hosted the 1995 World Cup.
- Squad whose public learning of [[NkosiSikelelIAfrika|Nkosi Sikelel' iAfrika]] became part of the reconciliation ritual.
- Vehicle through which Mandela tried to make "one team, one country" visible.

## Evidence

- Apartheid-era meaning: [[682-south-africa-mandela-and-the-death-of-apartheid-part-6-glt6535658080]] says the Springbok jersey became an emblem of apartheid for many black South Africans.
- Readmission and tournament role: [[682-south-africa-mandela-and-the-death-of-apartheid-part-6-glt6535658080]] says South Africa was readmitted to international rugby in 1992 and awarded the World Cup in 1993.
- Mandela's choice: [[682-south-africa-mandela-and-the-death-of-apartheid-part-6-glt6535658080]] says Mandela refused to ban the Springbok name and green jersey despite pressure from some ANC radicals.
- Team composition: [[682-south-africa-mandela-and-the-death-of-apartheid-part-6-glt6535658080]] says [[ChesterWilliams]] was the squad's only non-white player.
- Anthem performance: [[682-south-africa-mandela-and-the-death-of-apartheid-part-6-glt6535658080]] says Springbok players received lessons in singing the ANC anthem before the final.

## Qualifications

The current profile is source-scoped to the 1995 political-symbol moment. It does not cover the team's full sporting history, later transformation debates, or later Rugby World Cup campaigns.
