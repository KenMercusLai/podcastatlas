# 汉阳郡 / Hanyang Commandery (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-930-ruhe-pochu-tanxin-he-buganxin-lkyysh5jbgxwjq8ayrfar4gsjdcd|Hanji 930]] adds an earlier Hanyang appointment branch before the later [[FuXie|傅燮]] siege. After [[GaiXunLateHan|盖勋]] survives defeat and captivity in the Qiang field, [[YangYongLateHan|杨雍]] recommends him and Gai Xun concurrently holds Hanyang, making the commandery part of Gai Xun's reputation-to-office arc.

汉阳郡 / Hanyang Commandery (late Han) enters through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-937-zhanshen-zhisi-haoren-zongshi-duozai-duonan-lp7przsmag9ookq7eoisvsmnm5aw|Hanji 937]] as the commandery administered by [[FuXie|傅燮]] during the Liang州 collapse. After [[GengBiLateHan|耿鄙]]'s army mutinies at [[DiDaoLateHan|狄道]], the rebels surround Hanyang while the city has too few troops and no food.

In the source, Hanyang is less a full regional profile than the enclosed setting for [[OfficeDutyMartyrdom|守官赴死]]. It is where Beidi Hu cavalry offer Fu Xie escape, where [[FuGan|傅干]] urges survival, and where [[HuangYanLateHan|黄衍]]'s surrender mission fails before Fu Xie dies.
