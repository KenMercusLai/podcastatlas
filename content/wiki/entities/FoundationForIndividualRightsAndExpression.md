# Foundation for Individual Rights and Expression

## Overview
The Foundation for Individual Rights and Expression, abbreviated FIRE, enters the wiki through [[metas-youth-safety-settlement-could-change-how-people-access-the-internet]] as the civil-liberties organization represented by legal director [[WillCreeley]].

## Current Profile
In this source, FIRE is positioned beside the [[ElectronicFrontierFoundation|Electronic Frontier Foundation]] and speech-oriented academics and journalists as a critic of Meta's youth-safety settlement. Its concern is not that child-safety harms are imaginary, but that enforcement through age or identity checks can weaken anonymous speech and shift access decisions toward platforms or government-backed pressure.

## Key Characteristics
- FIRE is presented as a speech and civil-liberties organization.
- Its source role is to scrutinize child-safety remedies for First Amendment and anonymous-speech costs.
- It worries that settlement terms can pressure other platforms without direct legislative scrutiny.
- It favors preserving room for families, users, and speakers to make choices about lawful online speech.
