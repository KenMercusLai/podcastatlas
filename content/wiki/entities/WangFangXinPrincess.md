Her case belongs to the same late-[[XinDynasty|新朝]] household-fear layer as [[WangZongXinPrince|王宗]].

## Current Profile

The episode says Wang Fang is accused of cursing family members and killing a maid to silence evidence. She and Wang Xing then die by suicide. The source uses the case as part of a compressed Wang-family tragedy, not as a complete legal dossier.

Her wiki role is therefore a bounded scandal node. Together with Wang Zong's imperial-image case, Wang Fang shows how Wang Mang's regime reads symbolic or household accusations as existential danger inside the ruler's own clan.
