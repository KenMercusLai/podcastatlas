## Overview

儋耳郡 / Dan'er Commandery is one of the two Western Han commanderies that Hanji 354 says were established on [[Hainan|海南]] after [[YangPuWesternHan|杨仆]] crossed from Xuwen. Later Hanji 471-472 place Dan'er beside [[ZhuyaCommandery|珠崖郡]] in the costly and unstable history of direct island rule.

## Current Profile

Hanji 354 supplies the origin layer: after the conquest of [[Nanyue|南越]], Han Wudi establishes nine commanderies across the former polity, and Yang Pu's reported occupation of Hainan is followed by the creation of Dan'er and Zhuya. The commandery therefore represents the administrative conversion of a military advance into direct imperial jurisdiction in the episode's narrative.

Hanji 471 and 472 supply the qualification. They treat the two-commandery island arrangement as difficult to sustain amid repeated unrest, dangerous sea movement, disease environment, fiscal cost, and wider famine pressure. By Han Yuandi's reign, the court abandons the island commandery project; the later sources emphasize that subsequent attachment or tribute should not be mistaken for continuous direct administration.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han commandery reportedly established on Hainan after the conquest of Nanyue.
- One half of the island's initial two-commandery structure alongside Zhuya.
- Administrative expression of military incorporation rather than evidence of immediate social integration.
- Later part of the costly island commitment abandoned under Han Yuandi.

## Qualifications

These podcast summaries do not settle Dan'er's exact boundaries, seat, county structure, local institutions, administrative chronology, or relationship to the modern [[Danzhou|儋州]] entity. The page distinguishes the ancient commandery from the modern place and does not infer uninterrupted institutional continuity. Hanji 354's dating, Yang Pu attribution, and Jiaozhou placement remain source-scoped pending primary-text comparison.

## What Changed

- Established a distinct ancient-commandery identity rather than conflating 儋耳郡 with modern Danzhou.
- Joined the conquest-era founding claim to later evidence of unrest, retrenchment, and non-continuous control.

## Relationships

- [[ZhuyaCommandery|珠崖郡]] - companion commandery in the initial Western Han two-commandery administration of Hainan.
- [[Hainan|海南]] - island territory on which the commandery is placed.
- [[YangPuWesternHan|杨仆]] - commander credited by Hanji 354 with crossing from Xuwen before the commandery settlement.
- [[Nanyue|南越]] - conquered polity whose administrative reorganization precedes the Hainan commanderies.
