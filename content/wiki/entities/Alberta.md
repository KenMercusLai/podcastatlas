# Alberta

Alberta appears in [[elbows-up-again-a-us-canada-trade-flare-up-6a8c0fa3a89d26398ee7a5db]] as the Canadian province that complicates [[MarkCarney]]'s retaliation options against [[DonaldTrump]]'s tariffs. [[HalHodson]] says Alberta has almost zero tariff exposure on exports to the [[UnitedStates]], while also supplying more than half of America's imported oil.

The source makes Alberta important because energy leverage is not simply a federal switch. Cutting off Canadian energy supplies could hurt the United States, but it would anger Alberta and interact with provincial separatist pressure. Alberta therefore helps ground [[TradeDiversificationStabilityParadox]] in domestic Canadian federal politics rather than treating Canada as a single unified tariff actor.
