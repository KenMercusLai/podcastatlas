# Stanislas Maillard

## Overview

Stanislas Maillard was an identifiable organizer associated with the improvised tribunals and killing teams of the [[SeptemberMassacres]].

## Current Profile

The episode uses Maillard cautiously: he gives the violence at least one named organizer, but the scarcity of identified perpetrators prevents him from becoming a substitute for the wider local networks involved. His role supports low-level coordination through Paris sections rather than proving a centralized order from national leaders.

## Key Characteristics

- He is one of the few perpetrators or organizers named by the episode.
- He was associated with the organized and tribunal-like character of the prison killings.
- His role points toward coordination through sections and local revolutionary networks.
- His identification does not establish that he designed or controlled the massacres as a whole.

## Evidence

### Identifiable organization

- [[544-the-french-revolution-the-september-massacres-part-1-glt8301042051]] names Maillard while arguing that “death squads” better captures the organized teams than an undifferentiated mob.

### Distributed responsibility

- [[544-the-french-revolution-the-september-massacres-part-1-glt8301042051]] places organization at a low level and distinguishes it from a proven command by Danton or Robespierre.

## Qualifications

- The source does not provide a full biography, a complete operational record, or a settled count of actions attributable to Maillard.
- Naming one organizer must not obscure the broader participation, official knowledge, and political rhetoric that enabled the killings.

## What Changed

- The wiki now has a named local organizer without collapsing distributed responsibility into a single mastermind claim.
