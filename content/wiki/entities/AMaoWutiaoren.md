# 阿茂 / A Mao (Wutiaoren)

## Overview
A Mao is represented as [[RenkeMusician|仁科]]'s long-term collaborator in [[WutiaorenBand|五条人]], an early record seller, a major writer of the band's early Haifeng-language songs, and a principal composer in the account of 《地球恋曲》.

## Current Profile
The source introduces A Mao through the informal economy around cutout records in Guangzhou. Renke sold records for him while A Mao traveled, and their relationship later became the core partnership behind Wutiaoren. Renke credits A Mao with most of the early Haifeng-language songwriting and says the music on 《地球恋曲》 came largely from him, making A Mao central to both the band's local-language foundation and its later work.

His perspective is available only through Renke. One consequential episode has Renke spending two hours persuading A Mao to join 《乐队的夏天》 during the pandemic period; this supports a picture of negotiated partnership but not a complete account of A Mao's motives or creative method.

## Key Characteristics
- Foundational collaborator in Wutiaoren's development.
- Early participant in Guangzhou's informal cutout-record trade.
- Major writer of the band's early Haifeng-language repertoire.
- Principal composer in Renke's account of 《地球恋曲》.
- Sparsely evidenced figure whose views are mediated through his collaborator.

## Evidence
- Early collaboration: [[lml-euxpi9ekjvk-ihnkfzwp4ysq-lml-euxpi9ekjvk-ihnkfzwp4ysq]] describes Renke selling A Mao's records on a revenue-sharing basis and later reconnecting around music.
- Songwriting: [[lml-euxpi9ekjvk-ihnkfzwp4ysq-lml-euxpi9ekjvk-ihnkfzwp4ysq]] says A Mao wrote many early Haifeng-language songs and most of the music for 《地球恋曲》.
- Mainstream transition: [[lml-euxpi9ekjvk-ihnkfzwp4ysq-lml-euxpi9ekjvk-ihnkfzwp4ysq]] says Renke persuaded A Mao to participate in 《乐队的夏天》, after which the band reached a much larger audience.

## Qualifications
The evidence is a single Renke-centered interview, not A Mao's own account. It does not establish a complete biography, full credit history, individual creative method, or the precise division of labor across Wutiaoren's catalog.
