# Erasmus Programme / 伊拉斯莫计划

The Erasmus Programme / 伊拉斯莫计划 appears in [[139-cong-zuo-ri-de-shi-jie-dao-ru-jin-de-ou-zhou-mei-hao-nian-dai-belle-epoque-ta-hai-neng-hui-lai-ma-ltvsgov8ht9-l7yp9qamdgjmyrf]] as one of the institutional ways Europe encourages students to study and live across borders. The episode places it alongside youth travel discounts, museums, transport access, and pre- or post-university travel traditions.

The source uses Erasmus as a small but important counterweight to fragmentation. It shows how European identity can be produced through repeated contact, not only through treaties, while still remaining unevenly available by class, language, age, and economic position.
