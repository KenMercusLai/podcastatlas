## Overview
Qiao Xuan is the Ba commandery scholar in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-713-liuxiu-nu-le-mingjiang-wuhan-jing-xuexi-chengdu-lv6555l99it33gw34uydsz4tkbje|Hanji 713]] who refuses Gongsun Shu's coerced office and survives when his son pays a ransom-like sum.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Qiao Xuan as another Shu scholar whom Gongsun Shu tries to force into service. Like Li Nie, he receives an office-or-poison ultimatum and prepares to die rather than accept the summons.

Qiao Xuan survives because his son Qiao Ying offers ten million cash, which Gongsun Shu accepts. After Shu is pacified, Liu Xiu sacrifices to Qiao Xuan and returns the money to the Qiao family, turning the coerced payment into a post-conquest act of moral and material repair.

## Key Characteristics
- Ba commandery scholar targeted by Gongsun Shu's coercive recruitment.
- Refuser who is prepared to die rather than convert forced summons into service.
- Family-mediated survivor whose son's payment interrupts the martyrdom outcome.
- Post-conquest recognition case through Liu Xiu's sacrifice and repayment.
- Evidence point for the difference between coerced survival and genuine consent.
