# 《法言》 / Fayan

《法言》 / Fayan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-56-qiaoqiao-liangqian-duo-nianqian-sha-shi-da-zhangfu-ltokbutqgsnrno-y29xmzwa3da41]] as the [[YangXiong|扬雄]] text used to judge [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] and [[SuQin|苏秦]] after the episode has introduced [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]]'s [[MencianGreatManCriterion|大丈夫标准]].

The cited passage asks whether the arts of Zhang Yi and Su Qin, associated with [[Guiguzi|鬼谷子]], should be approved because they may have made the realm more settled for a time. Yang Xiong's answer is negative: the episode presents him as calling the method deceptive and incompatible with the moral learning expected of a gentleman.

The text also supplies the episode's contrast between [[Zigong|子贡 / 端木赐]] and the zonghengjia pair. Similar diplomatic movement can receive different moral judgments when its purpose differs: Zigong is framed as preventing disorder and saving his state, while Zhang Yi and Su Qin are framed as chasing wealth and rank. In this wiki, Fayan therefore anchors [[MotiveBasedTalentEvaluation|动机导向的才干评价]] beside the older [[TalentVirtueDistinction|才德之分]] branch.
