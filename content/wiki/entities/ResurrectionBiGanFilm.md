# Resurrection / 《狂野时代》

## Overview
《狂野时代》 / Resurrection is represented as [[BiGan|毕赣]]'s century-spanning, six-part sensory film and his most explicit attempt to make industrial production carry multiple personal aesthetic systems.

## Current Profile
Bi describes the film as a fictional chronicle organized through five senses and consciousness. Each section required distinct research, styling, equipment, performance, and period feeling, making the production resemble several films rather than one repeated system. His preferred cut prioritizes the whole work's rhythm over a shorter conventional runtime, even while his editing rule permits removing beautiful and costly material that fails the whole.

The vampire passage supplies the episode's strongest case for [[LongTakeTemporalEmbodiment]]. A month of rehearsal and technical preparation, several attempts, actor emotion, and changing natural light culminated in accepting a take with minor imperfections because its temporal and emotional event could not simply be reproduced.

## Key Characteristics
- Six-part fictional chronicle organized through sensation and consciousness.
- Multi-aesthetic production requiring distinct research, styling, equipment, and performance systems.
- Star-led authored film treating casting as compatible with personal cinema rather than inherently compromising it.
- Long-take work prioritizing continuous emotion, real light, and spectator-character time over technical perfection.
- Editing approach that subordinates expensive or attractive parts to the shape of the complete film.

## Qualifications
The episode is promotional and primarily carries the director's interpretation. Runtime, preparation periods, take counts, actor process, historical structure, and claims about the film's beauty or emotional force remain source-scoped and are not independent reception evidence.
