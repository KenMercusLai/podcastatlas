# 太子建 / Crown Prince Jian of Chu

太子建 / Crown Prince Jian of Chu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-1-chaijie-jingke-ciqin-bi-bai-de-mimi-lnonhw9tlsu5dudu8750zvgeaz1p]] as the father of [[BaiGongSheng|白公胜]]. The episode identifies him as the eldest legitimate son of Chu Ping Wang and uses his fate to explain why Bai Gong Sheng could inherit a revenge-laden political identity.

In the source's compressed account, Chu Ping Wang takes Taizi Jian's intended bride and kills members of the Wu family, forcing Taizi Jian into exile in [[ZhengState|郑国]]. Taizi Jian is later drawn into a plot involving [[JinState|晋国]] and Zheng, and Zheng kills him after the plan fails.

The page keeps this as source-scoped background. The episode is not mainly about Taizi Jian; it uses him to set up the [[BaiGongSheng|白公胜]] comparison and the claim that inherited grievance does not automatically make later courage or fidelity righteous.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-2-zanmen-dou-yuanwang-le-beifu-2000-duonian-heiguo-de-yegong-haolong-lh-h-gezpqtvnyq40zlqac8s-5e]] makes that background operational. Bai Gong Sheng asks [[ZiXiChu|子西]] to attack [[ZhengState|郑国]] in revenge for Taizi Jian, and Zi Xi's later decision to rescue Zheng helps turn Bai's grievance into the [[BaiGongShengRebellion|白公胜之乱]].
