# 奇楠 / Qinan

奇楠 / Qinan is the high-value [[Agarwood|沉香]] category used in [[ep263-xianxiang-jueqi-faxian-zhongshi-xiangqi-de-meili-lq1lsqcprqyr7auo5gslwyymuiv]] to explain why the incense market's material base changed. The episode says growers around Guangdong Dianbai experimented with grafting qinan onto white-wood incense tree root systems; the technique became increasingly viable around 2015-2016, and planted qinan began producing usable fragrance around 2019-2020.

The source gives qinan two roles. Technically, it helps explain why agarwood could move from rare wild material into a more planted and price-accessible product system. Sensorially, it is the example of progression: when heated, qinan may shift from floral notes toward milky notes, a change the source calls 转韵.

## Key Claims
- Qinan functions as a premium agarwood category and as the technical breakthrough story behind more affordable contemporary incense products.
- Artificial planting did not make qinan cheap in a trivial way; it changed the supply base and enabled more standardized downstream products.
- The episode contrasts qinan's changing aroma stages with more stable ordinary agarwood scent.
- Qinan also marks a possible consumer upgrade path: after trying creative blended line incense, some users may move toward single-material agarwood or qinan appreciation.
