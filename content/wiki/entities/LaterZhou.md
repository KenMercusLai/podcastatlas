# 后周 / Later Zhou

Later Zhou / 后周 is the predecessor regime whose military and succession structure forms the background for [[169-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-shang-957052909]]. The episode uses [[GuoWei|郭威]], [[ChaiRong|柴荣]], child emperor Chai Zongxun, and the contest among [[ZhangYongde|张永德]], [[LiChongjin|李重进]], and [[ZhaoKuangyin|赵匡胤]] to show why command appointments could become dynastic questions.

The source's Later Zhou is a world where office, kinship, army control, rumor, and prophecy-like signs are inseparable. The "点检做天子" episode weakens Zhang Yongde and helps Zhao Kuangyin inherit the殿前都点检 position, while Chai Rong's early death leaves a child ruler dependent on generals whose loyalties are not purely institutional.

For the wiki, Later Zhou is useful because it shows why the [[NorthernSong|北宋]] founding cannot be understood only from later Song scholar-official culture. Song centralization begins as a response to a recent pattern of military men making and unmaking regimes.

[[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]] places Later Zhou inside the full [[FiveDynastiesTenKingdoms|五代十国]] sequence. It becomes the regime where a rare strong second generation under [[ChaiRong|柴荣]] still fails to stabilize succession, helping explain why [[ZhaoKuangyin|赵匡胤]] and Song institution-building inherit both opportunity and danger.
