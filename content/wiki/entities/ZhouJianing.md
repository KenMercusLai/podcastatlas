# 周佳宁 / Zhou Jianing

## Overview
周佳宁 / Zhou Jianing is the author of [[YongJieWuQingYou|《永结无情游》]], represented in the wiki through a [[CultureLimited|文化有限]] discussion of education, friendship, memory, and middle age.

## Current Profile
The current evidence is limited to one episode about one novel. Within that boundary, Zhou is presented as a contemporary novelist who follows a small experimental-class friendship across roughly three decades and makes nonlinear recollection part of the book's meaning. The episode reads her work as holding private disappointment together with institutional and historical change rather than turning either school selection or friendship loss into a single-cause explanation.

## Key Characteristics
- Her represented work uses a long time span to connect childhood selection with adult work, family, and relationship outcomes.
- She uses fragmented recollection and competing viewpoints to challenge a single ownership of the past.
- The novel places intimate friendship inside education, research, media, pandemic, and generational change.
- Sensory and apparently minor shared moments carry more durable emotional force than formal declarations of permanence.
