# Sarah B. Rogers

Sarah B. Rogers appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-under-secretary-of-state-sarah-b-rogers-on-dismantling-the-censorship-industrial-complex-39828360]] as the [[USDepartmentOfState|U.S. Department of State]] under secretary for public diplomacy. She explains public diplomacy as the relationship between the U.S. government and foreign publics, then argues that internet governance and disinformation disputes have made that work central to foreign policy.

In the episode, Rogers is the source for a Trump-administration-aligned free-speech critique of the [[UKOnlineSafetyAct|UK Online Safety Act]], the [[EuropeanUnionDigitalServicesAct|EU Digital Services Act]], trusted flaggers, debanking, demonetization, and foreign fines against American technology companies. Her position is not that every foreign government must adopt U.S. law; it is that American platforms and users should not be pressured into foreign speech standards through cross-border enforcement.

Rogers also connects AI deepfake concerns to existing law. She accepts that some disclosure or watermarking tools may be useful, but argues that fraud, defamation, cybercrime, and child-protection law should be applied before governments build broad AI-specific speech-control systems.
