# 荆国 / Jing Kingdom (early Han)

荆国 / Jing Kingdom enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-174-liubang-weihe-dafeng-zijide-chouren-1-lnzzvpieqtgatmvgogz3msyhs60u|Hanji 174]] as one half of [[HanXin|韩信]]'s former Chu kingdom after [[LiuBang|刘邦]] removes Han Xin's royal title. The source says Liu Bang assigns the Huai River southeast counties to [[LiuJia|刘贾]] as king of Jing.

The kingdom is a founding-control instrument. It converts territory once attached to a high-merit different-surname king into a same-surname royal jurisdiction, paired with [[ChuKingdomEarlyHan|楚国]] under [[LiuJiao|刘交]].
