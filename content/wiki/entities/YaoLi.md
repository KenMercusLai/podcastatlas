# 要离 / Yao Li

要离 / Yao Li enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-1-chaijie-jingke-ciqin-bi-bai-de-mimi-lnonhw9tlsu5dudu8750zvgeaz1p]] as one of the assassin figures classified by [[YangXiong|扬雄]]. The episode cites Yang Xiong to distinguish deaths that may look fierce, loyal, or memorable from conduct that deserves the name义.

The source gives only a typological use. Yao Li is not developed biographically here; he is paired with [[NieZheng|聂政]] and [[JingKe|荆轲]] so that Yang Xiong can deny that celebrated assassin deaths are automatically righteous.
