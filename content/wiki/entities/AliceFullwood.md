# Alice Fullwood

## Overview
Alice Fullwood appears in the wiki as the [[TheIntelligence]] speaker explaining [[Meta]]'s teen social-media settlement and its implications for platform liability.

## Current Profile
Her source-bounded role is to separate headline damages from product remedies and follow-on litigation incentives. She treats the settlement as large and template-like, but not yet equivalent to tobacco litigation in financial scale or business-model threat.

## Key Characteristics
- She emphasizes settlement structure rather than only the headline dollar figure.
- She treats behavioral product restrictions as potentially more important than the financial payment.
- She frames the Meta deal as a template that can pressure [[TikTok]], [[YouTube]], [[Snap]], and other platforms.
- She preserves limits to the tobacco analogy because Meta remains highly profitable and investors do not appear to treat the settlement as existential.

## Evidence
- Settlement-terms evidence: [[bots-on-the-ground-china-leads-humanoid-race-6a91564cee851f3f31239bbf]] attributes to Fullwood the two-part structure of financial damages plus teen restrictions on Facebook and Instagram.
- Product-remedy evidence: [[bots-on-the-ground-china-leads-humanoid-race-6a91564cee851f3f31239bbf]] uses her analysis to foreground age assessment, daily caps, nighttime blackouts, notification limits, hidden likes, and chronological-feed options.
- Template evidence: [[bots-on-the-ground-china-leads-humanoid-race-6a91564cee851f3f31239bbf]] says she sees the settlement as a template for deals with other social media companies.
- Tobacco-analogy evidence: [[bots-on-the-ground-china-leads-humanoid-race-6a91564cee851f3f31239bbf]] records her view that the analogy is useful but financially limited because Meta remains profitable.
