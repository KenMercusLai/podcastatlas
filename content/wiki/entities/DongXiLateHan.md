# 董袭 / Dong Xi (late Han)

## Overview
董袭 / Dong Xi is a Sun-family military officer introduced here as the reassurance voice who tells [[LadyWuSunJian|吴夫人]] that Jiangdong can hold after [[SunCe|孙策]]'s death.

## Current Profile
The current evidence does not yet give Dong Xi a full biography. Hanji 1052 uses him in a specific succession scene: Lady Wu asks whether Jiangdong can remain stable after Sun Quan's under-19 succession, and Dong Xi answers by naming the region's geography, Sun Ce's favor, Sun Quan's inheritance, and the continuing alignment of civil and military followers.

His role is therefore evidentiary more than biographical. He makes the military side of early [[SunQuan|孙权]] legitimacy visible, while also distinguishing that military confidence from [[ZhangZhaoLateHan|张昭]]'s administrative stabilization.

## Key Characteristics
- Appears as a loyal Sun-family officer in the post-Sun Ce succession crisis.
- Reassures Lady Wu that Jiangdong has enough geographic and personnel support to survive the transition.
- Treats Sun Ce's prior favor and Sun Quan's inheritance as politically meaningful continuity.
- Separates administrative order under Zhang Zhao from military readiness under the generals.
- Serves as a source-bounded witness for the confidence of Sun-family followers.

## Evidence
- Succession reassurance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1052-sanguo-zhongchen-zhanghong-jing-wannong-caocao-yu-guzhang-ljc3sd7meigo37uy-1fxnkx-psqg|Hanji 1052]] has Lady Wu ask Dong Xi whether Jiangdong can hold after Sun Ce's death, and Dong Xi replies that geography, Sun Ce's favor, Sun Quan's succession, and official-general alignment make the situation supportable.
- Civil-military distinction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1052-sanguo-zhongchen-zhanghong-jing-wannong-caocao-yu-guzhang-ljc3sd7meigo37uy-1fxnkx-psqg|Hanji 1052]] says Zhang Zhao handles affairs while generals fight, making Dong Xi's reassurance a military-confidence complement to civil administration.
- Sun-family continuity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1052-sanguo-zhongchen-zhanghong-jing-wannong-caocao-yu-guzhang-ljc3sd7meigo37uy-1fxnkx-psqg|Hanji 1052]] frames Dong Xi's claim around Sun Ce's inherited support and Sun Quan's succession rather than around Dong Xi's independent ambition.
