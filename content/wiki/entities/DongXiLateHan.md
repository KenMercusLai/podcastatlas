# 董袭 / Dong Xi (late Han)

## Overview
董袭 / Dong Xi is a Sun-family officer whose early reassurance about Jiangdong's viability becomes concrete battlefield leadership in the 208 CE breach of [[HuangZu|黄祖]]'s river barrier.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1052 first uses Dong Xi as the military reassurance voice after [[SunCe|孙策]]'s death. He tells [[LadyWuSunJian|吴夫人]] that geography, inherited favor, [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s succession, and continuing civil-military alignment make Jiangdong supportable.

Hanji 1085 adds direct operational evidence. Dong Xi and [[LingTong|凌统]] each lead a small armored assault party against the anchored warships at [[MianKouLateHan|沔口]]. Dong Xi cuts the mooring cables, turning a fixed defensive barrier into drifting ships and opening the route for Sun Quan's main force toward Xiakou.

## Key Characteristics
- Supports the Sun-family succession as a military voice after Sun Ce's death.
- Grounds early confidence in geography, inherited support, and civil-military alignment.
- Leads an armored assault party against Huang Zu's fixed river defense.
- Is credited with cutting the barrier's mooring cables and opening the channel.
- Connects succession-era declared loyalty with later high-risk battlefield execution.

## Evidence
- Succession reassurance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1052-sanguo-zhongchen-zhanghong-jing-wannong-caocao-yu-guzhang-ljc3sd7meigo37uy-1fxnkx-psqg|Hanji 1052]] has Lady Wu ask whether Jiangdong can hold and Dong Xi answer through geography, inheritance, and follower alignment.
- Civil-military distinction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1052-sanguo-zhongchen-zhanghong-jing-wannong-caocao-yu-guzhang-ljc3sd7meigo37uy-1fxnkx-psqg|Hanji 1052]] pairs Zhang Zhao's administration with the generals' fighting role.
- Assault leadership: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1085-dongwu-naxia-jiangxia-dengyu-wan-le-liubiao-de-xin-lrnhjns-qu0d6tsit3j_re96zk2u|Hanji 1085]] says Dong Xi and Ling Tong each lead more than one hundred heavily armored volunteers into the barrier.
- Cable breach: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1085-dongwu-naxia-jiangxia-dengyu-wan-le-liubiao-de-xin-lrnhjns-qu0d6tsit3j_re96zk2u|Hanji 1085]] credits Dong Xi with cutting the moorings and allowing the main force to advance.

## Qualifications
The current evidence still covers only two windows, not a full military career. Exact assault-party size, armor, cable number, warship construction, and Dong Xi's individual actions remain source-scoped pending verification against underlying historical texts.

## What Changed
- Extended Dong Xi from succession reassurance into direct battlefield command.
- Added the Mian Kou cable-cutting action as the operational proof behind the earlier military-support role.

## Relationships
- [[LadyWuSunJian|吴夫人]] - Sun-family elder whom Dong Xi reassures during the succession crisis.
- [[SunCe|孙策]] - deceased leader whose inherited support anchors Dong Xi's continuity argument.
- [[SunQuan|孙权]] - successor supported politically and served in the Jiangxia campaign.
- [[LingTong|凌统]] - fellow assault leader in the Mian Kou breach.
- [[MianKouLateHan|沔口]] - river chokepoint where Dong Xi's cable-cutting opens the advance.
- [[HuangZu|黄祖]] - opposing commander whose barrier Dong Xi helps break.
- [[SuccessionAuthorityConsolidation|继位权威整合]] - earlier framework for Dong Xi's declared support.
