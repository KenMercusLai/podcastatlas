# Steve Bernard

## Overview
Steve Bernard is the founder of [[CapeCodChips]] and father of [[NicoleBernardDawes]], discussed in [[late-july-snacks-nicole-bernard-dawes-crackers-and-cookies-were-failing-tortilla-chips-saved-them-97049a31-9144-42f5-856c-9590c5e6beb4]]. He functions in the wiki as both a product-world mentor and a governance-risk trigger in the Late July story.

## Current Profile
Steve built Cape Cod Chips after seeing potential in old-fashioned potato chips, sold and later repurchased the business, and eventually sold it again. He helped Nicole in [[LateJulySnacks]] after retirement, warning her about the difficulty of scaling expensive snack mix and working without salary in the company's early years. His death in March 2009 triggered a death-of-a-member loan clause, turning family loss into company financing risk.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder whose Cape Cod Chips experience supplied practical snack-category judgment.
- Parent and mentor whose advice pushed Nicole away from hard-to-scale early product ideas.
- Early Late July helper who returned from retirement and worked through lean years.
- Key-person risk case because his death contributed to a bank default event.
