# 幽州 / Youzhou (Late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-938-lishi-de-jingxing-ruanruo-zhihui-dailai-quru-lnlnrketgfbadhfjbzdjjtrmqfsh|Hanji 938]] supplies the rebellion origin before the later Liu Yu response. Wuhuan cavalry recruited for [[ZhangWenLateHan|张温]]'s campaign desert after supply and pay failure near Ji, while [[ZhangChunLateHan|张纯]], [[ZhangJuLateHan|张举]], and [[QiuLijuLateHan|丘力居]] kill frontier officials and convert Youzhou disorder into a direct challenge to [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-939-donghan-luanju-de-fengkuang-sanguo-xuqu-lthmvw8ihk96fuez9vamlryzcngy|Hanji 939]] adds the appointment prehistory before Liu Yu's pacification. Under [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s provincial-shepherd reform, [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] is sent as Youzhou shepherd to deal with [[ZhangChunLateHan|张纯]], while the court's requisition of [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] support for that campaign triggers [[FrontierConscriptionBackfire|边疆征发反噬]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-942-yue-youxiu-de-ren-huode-yue-lei-heli-ma-lkwlrcgnt-kxxc2qqz6z6wkg7e5z|Hanji 942]] backfills Youzhou before the Liu Yu/Gongsun Zan rupture. The province is destabilized by [[ZhangJuLateHan|张举]], [[ZhangChunLateHan|张纯]], [[QiuLijuLateHan|丘力居]], and frontier raids, while [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]]'s Shimen victory turns into overextension and siege at Guanzi City.

The same episode makes [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]]'s arrival a temporary stabilization point. His envoys, rewards, and surrender signaling induce [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] and [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] actors to submit or disperse, but leaving Gongsun Zan with cavalry at [[YoubeipingCommandery|右北平]] preserves the split between pacification and armed suppression.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-972-sanguo-liuyu-zhisi-taisha-taitianzhen-lvawrcandbh628rckukosgarh7v2|Hanji 972]] later makes Youzhou the northern region where Liu Yu and Gongsun Zan move from uneasy coexistence into open rupture. Liu Yu's humane but non-executable attack, his retreat to [[JuyongPass|居庸关]], and Gongsun Zan's marketplace execution of Liu Yu and his family create the region's later legitimacy wound.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] follows Youzhou as the northern region Gongsun Zan controls after killing Liu Yu. The episode's main Youzhou pattern is that conquest without people-support becomes hard to hold.

Once [[XianyuFu|鲜于辅]], [[YanRou|阎柔]], [[Wuhuan|乌桓]], [[Xianbei|鲜卑]], [[LiuHeLateHan|刘和]], and [[YuanShao|袁绍]]-aligned forces attack, several Youzhou commandery-level actors kill Gongsun Zan's officials and join the coalition. Youzhou therefore appears as a regional legitimacy and defection field, not only as Gongsun Zan's territory.
