# UCWeb / UC浏览器

## Overview
UCWeb is the mobile-browser company co-founded by [[HeXiaopeng]] that emerged from the UCMail mobile-email product and became his first major entrepreneurial success before its acquisition by Alibaba.

## Current Profile
The source presents UCWeb less as a fixed original idea than as a product-discovery case. UCMail included a small browser so users could open links, but usage showed that the browsing function was much more valuable than the email client. The team responded by building UCWeb, whose adoption eventually far exceeded UCMail. Early survival also depended on complementary founders, financing help, and a share structure that avoided excessive passive-investor control.

## Key Characteristics
- Emerged from observed use of an embedded feature rather than the team's initial mobile-email thesis.
- Built for the feature-phone and Nokia era of the Chinese mobile internet.
- Grew slowly at first despite later reaching more than ten million daily active users in the source's account.
- Used complementary founder roles because He Xiaopeng did not consider fundraising his strength.
- Became the financial and managerial base from which He later entered automotive entrepreneurship.

## Evidence
- Product discovery: [[lnmpqiptzutrevnwr4ot5zrwxuak-lnmpqiptzutrevnwr4ot5zrwxuak]] says users treated UCMail's embedded browser as the main product signal, leading the team to create UCWeb.
- Early financing and governance: [[lnmpqiptzutrevnwr4ot5zrwxuak-lnmpqiptzutrevnwr4ot5zrwxuak]] attributes early runway partly to an RMB 800,000 loan from Ding Lei and emphasizes co-founder, investor, and equity-structure choices.
- Scale and founder transition: [[lnmpqiptzutrevnwr4ot5zrwxuak-lnmpqiptzutrevnwr4ot5zrwxuak]] reports more than ten million daily active users on Nokia-era devices and describes the Alibaba acquisition as the point after which He achieved financial freedom before later joining XPeng full time.

## Qualifications
