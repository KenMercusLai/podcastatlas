# Daniel Ackerman

Daniel Ackerman appears in [[tech-20260818-tech-pod-128-tech-20260818-tech-pod-128]] as the reporter explaining why good rocket launch sites are hard to find. He identifies population distance, deep-water port access, and equator-facing launch geography as key filters behind [[SpaceportSitingConstraint]].

His reporting links the physical geography of launches to public costs: rockets can scatter debris, create loud noise, and interrupt commercial air travel. In the wiki, Ackerman therefore connects commercial launch growth to [[SpaceLaunchCapacityBottleneck]] and [[SpaceEconomyInfrastructure]] rather than treating launch pads as a simple real-estate problem.
