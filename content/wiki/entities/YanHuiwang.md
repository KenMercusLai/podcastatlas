# 燕惠王 / King Hui of Yan

燕惠王 / King Hui of Yan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-78-qiguo-mingyun-jing-bei-chengguan-gei-jiule-lpt3reyr6ok8g2lhcxrnd5qctyug]] as the successor to [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]]. The episode says he already had conflict with [[LeYi|乐毅]] when he was crown prince, so his accession changes Le Yi's protection environment.

That inherited resentment makes him the negative contrast to Yan Zhaowang. Where Yan Zhaowang publicly rejects slander, honors Le Yi's family, and accepts the risk of deep trust, Yan Huiwang accepts [[TianDan|田单]]'s rumors quickly and sends [[QiJie|骑劫]] to replace Le Yi.

For the wiki, Yan Huiwang is a concrete case of [[PatronSuccessionExposure|patron succession exposure]]. The military problem is not just that a new ruler dislikes an old commander; it is that the dislike becomes an operational decision at the front, producing [[BattlefieldCommanderReplacementRisk|battlefield commander replacement risk]] and damaging [[YanState|燕国]]'s position in conquered Qi.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-79-shishang-yong-gengniu-da-shengzhang-diyi-ren-lsrl0posblloqwugox160xsicp2p]] records the downstream cost. [[QiJie|骑劫]] mishandles Jimo, falls for Tian Dan's rumor bait, is killed in the [[FireOxNightRaid|fire-ox night raid]], and Yan loses the Qi occupation gains. Yan Huiwang then regrets the replacement and fears that [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] might use Le Yi, now honored in Zhao, against Yan.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-80-zhugeliang-de-ouxiang-daodi-you-duo-lihai-lse6qmqqhoyqdzqjl4qfuvioeiw0]] follows that anxiety into correspondence. Yan Huiwang writes to [[LeYi|乐毅]] in a tone the host reads as blame-shifting: he admits enough regret to reopen contact, but frames Le Yi's flight as abandonment of [[YanState|燕国]] and betrayal of [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]]'s old favor. After reading [[BaoYanHuiwangShu|《报燕惠王书》]], he is reassured enough to let [[LeXian|乐贤]] inherit the Changguo Jun title, so the episode adds a partial repair after his earlier battlefield mistake.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-85-xuantaihou-weihe-sitong-30-nian-hou-ansha-le-laoqingren-lg7zy6vqtdjcufou4-mox5qkg2ct5]] closes Yan Huiwang's branch with internal violence. The episode says his earlier loss of prestige after forcing out Le Yi and watching [[TianDan|田单]] restore Qi leaves [[YanState|燕国]] politically hollowed out. In 272 BCE, chief minister [[GongsunCao|公孙操]] kills Yan Huiwang and installs [[YanWuchengwang|燕武成王]], while Qin, Wei, and Chu attack Yan in the same turbulent frame.
