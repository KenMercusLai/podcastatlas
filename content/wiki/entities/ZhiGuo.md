# 智果 / Zhi Guo

## Overview
Zhi Guo is the Zhi-clan adviser who opposes [[ZhiYao|Zhi Yao]]'s succession, later warns him that public humiliation creates hidden retaliation, and separates his own branch after his first warning is rejected.

## Current Profile
Zhi Guo's judgment joins personnel evaluation with risk forecasting. He concedes Zhi Yao's visible advantages but treats lack of benevolence as the decisive flaw because ability expands the damage an unrestrained leader can cause. After Zhi Yao humiliates [[HanKangzi|Han Hu]] and [[DuanGuiWarringStates|Duan Gui]], Zhi Guo again focuses on delayed rather than immediate danger: weak actors may conceal resentment until conditions change.

## Key Characteristics
- Separates observable competence from the moral direction in which it will be used.
- Forecasts clan-level consequences rather than only the successor's personal success.
- Treats small public insults as stored political risk.
- Acts on rejected counsel by separating his branch from the endangered lineage.

## Evidence
- Succession objection: the episode credits Zhi Guo with listing Zhi Yao's strengths, identifying lack of benevolence as overriding, and predicting destruction of the clan. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-02-jiangyu-baizhi-bi-gufuzhi-lglk2_eu1uvegdac8sj6bfuwv1dw|Zhouji 02]]
- Retaliation warning: after the Han banquet humiliation, he argues that resentment need not be visible and that major actors avoid large trouble by taking small matters seriously. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-02-jiangyu-baizhi-bi-gufuzhi-lglk2_eu1uvegdac8sj6bfuwv1dw|Zhouji 02]]
