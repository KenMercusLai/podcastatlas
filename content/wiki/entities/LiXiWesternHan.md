## Overview

李息 / Li Xi is synthesized as a Western Han official with two bounded roles: a commander assigned to contain possible Xiongnu reinforcement during the [[HetaoCampaignWesternHan|河南地 campaign]], and the later 大行 who receives [[JiAnWesternHan|汲黯]]'s warning about [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]] but does not transmit it to [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] because he fears Zhang's power.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-280-heshuo-dajie-weiqing-ruhe-yizhan-fenghou-1-lu5pruhgiv4c2jetewxp65lmkwqc|Hanji 280 part 1]] supplies the earlier military role that the page had previously left unmerged. It says Li Xi departs from Dai while Wei Qing moves from Yunzhong toward Gaoque, with Li assigned to watch or block a larger Xiongnu relief force. This makes his role supporting containment rather than the main rearward maneuver; the exact route, force, office, and contact outcome remain source-scoped.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-333-2-ta-ai-da-huangdi-de-lian-weihe-neng-shanzhong-lhsygwstd8wysfxfgdbfkkyokscl|Hanji 333-2]] identifies Li Xi as 大行 when Ji An visits him before leaving for Huaiyang. Ji argues that Zhang Tang rejects correction, decorates error, tracks imperial desire, and expands punitive government, then warns that failure to clarify the danger early may eventually put Li himself at risk.

Li hears the warning but remains silent. His significance in the bounded source is therefore institutional rather than biographical: accurate private diagnosis cannot become governing feedback when the recipient fears the official being criticized. The episode says Han Wudi later learns of the conversation after Zhang's fall, but it does not provide Li's full career or the consequence for his silence.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official identified as 大行 in the episode.
- Supporting commander assigned to contain possible Xiongnu reinforcement while Wei Qing conducts the main Hetao maneuver.
- Receives Ji An's pre-departure warning about Zhang Tang.
- Does not report the warning to Han Wudi because he fears Zhang.
- Illustrates the gap between privately hearing correction and converting it into official action.

## Qualifications

The page joins two compact source appearances but does not establish Li Xi's complete biography, office sequence, the continuity between every transmitted name occurrence, his military force and result, exact knowledge, inner motive beyond the episode's fear attribution, whether reporting could have changed Zhang's career, or what consequence followed when the emperor learned of the conversation.
