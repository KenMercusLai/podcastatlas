# Warner Music Group

[[tech-20260817-tech-pod-128-tech-20260817-tech-pod-128]] adds Warner Music Group as the contrasting label-side case in the [[Suno]] story. [[TatianaCirasano]] says Warner Music Group has settled with Suno and is working with the company, while [[UniversalMusicGroup]] and [[SonyMusic]] remain in active lawsuits.

That contrast makes Warner Music Group the clearest example in the source of [[MusicIndustryAIResponse]] as a dual-track strategy. The same industry can litigate over [[AITrainingCopyrightDispute]] while also pursuing licensing, partnership, or monetization routes under [[AIContentLicensing]] and [[DigitalMusicLicensing]].
