# Sadiq Khan

## Overview

Sadiq Khan appears in the wiki as London mayor in two source-scoped roles: a procurement gatekeeper who blocked a proposed [[Palantir]] arrangement with the [[MetropolitanPolice]], and a public-safety policymaker associated with a violence-reduction approach to knife crime.

## Current Profile

The current profile is narrow and does not attempt a full account of Khan's mayoralty. The sources use him to show how city government mediates high-pressure public problems: technology procurement when police productivity arguments conflict with open competition, and youth-violence prevention when crime narratives focus on London even as the geography of knife crime changes.

## Key Characteristics

- London mayor named in the Palantir public-service procurement branch.
- Public actor associated with insisting on open competition before a police technology deal proceeds.
- Counterweight to productivity arguments for fast adoption of Palantir inside the Metropolitan Police.
- Mayor credited with adopting a Glasgow-inspired violence-reduction model in London.
- City-level policymaker whose public-safety role is now qualified by rural and county knife-crime patterns outside London.

## Evidence

- Procurement intervention: [[right-in-front-afd-could-win-german-state-6a97f0a5a4a22c581728b85f]] says Khan blocked a Metropolitan Police Palantir deal because there had been no open competition.
- Tradeoff framing: [[right-in-front-afd-could-win-german-state-6a97f0a5a4a22c581728b85f]] pairs Khan's decision with Sir Mark Rowley's warning that blocking the deal would cost productivity and affect frontline staffing.
- Violence-reduction adoption: [[bargaining-chips-nvidia-is-the-bank-of-ai-6a9a86ffe7a9fe2478c4fc41]] says Khan adopted a Glasgow-inspired public-health approach to knife violence in London.
- Geography qualification: [[bargaining-chips-nvidia-is-the-bank-of-ai-6a9a86ffe7a9fe2478c4fc41]] credits London's improving sharp-object assault data while warning that knife-crime risk has become more visible in some counties and rural areas.

## Qualifications

The sources do not profile Khan's wider mayoralty, party politics, policing record, or technology policy. The public-safety source attributes a model and reports city-level improvement, but it does not prove that Khan's policies alone caused those trends.
