# François Hollande

## Overview

François Hollande was the French president from 2012 whose “Mr Normal” identity is presented as a poor fit for the grand, personalized expectations of the [[FrenchFifthRepublic]].

## Current Profile

[[179-french-presidents-1981-2022-part-2-glt2206497694]] treats Hollande as the least compelling modern occupant of the office. His attempt to contrast himself with [[NicolasSarkozy]] through normality was overwhelmed in the episode's telling by weak approval, symbolic mishaps, and private-life scandal, while his recruitment of [[EmmanuelMacron]] created the route by which a younger and more theatrical figure displaced the established Socialist Party.

## Key Characteristics

- Presented himself as an ordinary, restrained alternative to Sarkozy.
- Had deep Socialist Party experience but struggled to project presidential grandeur.
- Suffered exceptionally weak public approval in the source's account.
- Saw private-life stories reinforce an existing political image of weakness.
- Brought Macron into government before Macron built an independent movement.

## Evidence

- Deliberate normality: [[179-french-presidents-1981-2022-part-2-glt2206497694]] describes Hollande's “Mr Normal” identity and the mocking “Monsieur Flamby” counter-image.
- Symbolic weakness: [[179-french-presidents-1981-2022-part-2-glt2206497694]] uses the rain-soaked inauguration, scooter photographs, and low approval as signs that embarrassment overwhelmed dignity.
- Succession: [[179-french-presidents-1981-2022-part-2-glt2206497694]] says Hollande recruited Macron from Rothschild into government before En Marche displaced the Socialist establishment.

## Qualifications

The source is intentionally anecdotal and gives little attention to Hollande's economic, security, European, or military record. Its symbolic judgment should not be mistaken for a complete account of policy performance or the causes of low approval.

## What Changed

- Created the page around normality as both an electoral contrast and a liability within a grand presidency.

## Relationships

- [[NicolasSarkozy]] - predecessor whose conspicuous style made normality initially attractive.
- [[EmmanuelMacron]] - minister who left Hollande's government and displaced the established party system.
- [[FrancoisMitterrand]] - earlier Socialist president whose grandeur the episode says Hollande lacked.
- [[PresidentialSymbolicLeadership]] - framework explaining why personal mishaps became institutionally consequential.
