# CR450

CR450 is the next-generation high-speed train program named near the end of [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]]. The episode says prototype trainsets were released at the end of 2024, with a target test speed of 450 km/h and commercial operation around 400 km/h.

Within the source's narrative, CR450 shows the forward edge of [[ChineseStandardEMU]] after [[Fuxinghao]]. It turns the earlier story of import, absorption, and standardization into a continuing speed, reliability, energy, and system-integration challenge rather than a completed victory.
