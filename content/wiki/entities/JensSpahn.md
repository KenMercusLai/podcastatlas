# Jens Spahn

Jens Spahn appears in [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]] as the parliamentary leader of [[Germany]]'s governing center-right coalition before a surrogacy scandal forces him out. The source says Spahn revealed that he and his husband had become parents through surrogacy abroad, even though he had defended Germany's domestic surrogacy ban and signed a February resolution supporting a full ban.

The episode frames the scandal as [[SurrogacyPolicyHypocrisy]] and [[PoliticalDoubleStandardBacklash]]. Spahn's private family decision becomes politically damaging because it conflicts with the rules he defended for others. The source also notes prior controversies around a Dahlem villa loan, pandemic-era face-mask contracts, and unpopular ties to Richard Grenell and Peter Thiel.
