## Overview

卜式 / Bu Shi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-362-2-bushi-weisha-yao-pengsha-sanghongyang-loz3ly12pudlvdrtjngxs8f1qi6_|Hanji 362-2]] as a figure [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] repeatedly held up for officials to emulate and as the author of an unusually severe attack on [[SangHongyangWesternHan|桑弘羊]] during a 110 BCE drought.

## Current Profile

The episode defines Bu Shi mainly through fiscal remonstrance. He argues that the court should live from taxation rather than place officials in shops to sell goods and seek profit. By saying that rain would come only after Sang Hongyang was cooked, he translates opposition to [[StateCommercialMonopolyExtraction|direct official commerce]] into the period's disaster-responsibility language.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han figure publicly promoted by Han Wudi as an example for other officials.
- Critic of officials entering ordinary market trade and pursuing commercial profit.
- Defender, in the episode's compressed formulation, of taxation as the court's proper support.
- Remonstrant who links drought relief to the removal and execution of the fiscal official he blames.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on one short podcast summary. It does not reconstruct Bu Shi's wider biography, offices, economic thought, relationship with Han Wudi, the full memorial text, or whether his execution-and-rain language was literal belief, rhetorical escalation, or both.
