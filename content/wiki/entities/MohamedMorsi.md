# Mohamed Morsi

Mohamed Morsi appears in [[spring-then-fall-a-weakened-muslim-brotherhood-6a7aef1b616564bf00d9b354]] as the [[MuslimBrotherhood]]-linked candidate who became [[Egypt]]'s first democratically elected president after the Arab Spring. The episode uses his election as the clearest sign that the Brotherhood briefly moved from social and opposition organizing into formal state power.

His presidency also anchors the reversal. [[AbdelFattahAlSisi]]'s coup is described as the "autocratic sledgehammer" that crushed the Brotherhood's Egyptian opening and became the leading case for [[PostArabSpringIslamistDecline]].
