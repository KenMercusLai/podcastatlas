## Overview
Konrad Zieliński is an entrepreneur, researcher, laryngectomee, and the founder-user presented in the source behind [[UhuraBionics]], a company developing non-invasive electronic speech systems.

## Current Profile
The interview presents Zieliński's lived experience and technical work as mutually informing but not interchangeable. After cancer treatment removed his biological larynx, he used earlier electronic speech components, recorded his prior voice, assembled collaborators, completed doctoral work, and developed devices intended to restore expressive control without another throat operation. His design position is that AI should preserve vocal identity while the person retains intention, emotional control, and authorship of speech.

## Key Characteristics
- Builds from direct experience of laryngectomy while explicitly rejecting the assumption that his needs represent every user.
- Treats timing, intonation, laughter, affection, and other non-word signals as central communication requirements.
- Favors non-invasive, wearable systems over implanted interfaces for broad accessibility.
- Combines acoustics, biomechanics, medicine, speech therapy, music, and device engineering.
- Advocates user ownership, consent, portability, and secure storage of recorded voice data.
- Frames bionic speech as an extension of human agency rather than a replacement of identity.
