# 凉州系军阀 / Liangzhou Warlord Clique

凉州系军阀 / Liangzhou Warlord Clique enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-984-liangzhouxi-junfa-neidou-ta-weihe-di-yige-daoxia-lurb2qfzsa7e-owh-dl-m-sccze5|Hanji 984]] as the late-Han military bloc around [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]], [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]], and [[FanChouLateHan|樊稠]] after [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s death. The page is source-scoped to this Chang'an branch rather than a full Liangzhou prosopography.

In the source, the bloc's shared control of [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] and the court is predatory from the start. The Sanfu / [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] area still has large population remnants, but repeated plunder by Li Jue, Guo Si, Fan Chou, and their soldiers combines with famine until social order breaks down.

[[JiaXu|贾诩]] can mediate among the commanders and preserve surface peace, but the underlying arrangement has no stable authority boundary. Fan Chou's battlefield success against [[MaTeng|马腾]] and [[HanSui|韩遂]] gives Li Jue both a useful victory and a threatening subordinate, so [[LiLiLateHan|李利]]'s report of Fan Chou's friendly field conversation with Han Sui becomes evidence inside [[MilitaryPrestigeThreatPurge|军功声望威胁清洗]].

After Fan Chou dies, the clique loses its internal balancing member. Guo Si's household suspicion then accelerates the Li-Guo rupture, and [[LiXianLateHan|李暹]]'s seizure of [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] for Li Jue turns the emperor into an open prize before the fully visible hostage symmetry in Hanji 985.
