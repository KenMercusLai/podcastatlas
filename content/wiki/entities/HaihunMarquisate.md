## Overview

海昏侯国 / Haihun Marquisate enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-430-chigua-qunzhong-qianren-yu-xianren-de-gongdou-daxi-lkfcjewfhmhfgw39vqfqkpa9umzq|Hanji 430]] as the reduced settlement granted to [[LiuHeHaihunHou|刘贺]] after his deposition from imperial and royal standing.

## Current Profile

In this source, Haihun is not primarily a local-history object. It is a political containment device: [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] gives Liu He a noble title and territory while denying him the royal privileges that could reconnect him to imperial ritual and court politics.

## Key Characteristics

- Reduced Western Han marquisate for a former emperor.
- Preserves noble status while narrowing ritual and political reach.
- Functions in the episode as a compromise between execution, restoration, and unmanaged legitimacy risk.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 430. It does not reconstruct Haihun's archaeology, administrative geography, tomb finds, or later reception history.
