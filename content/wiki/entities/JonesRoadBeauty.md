# Jones Road Beauty

Jones Road Beauty is the clean-makeup company [[BobbiBrown]] says she launched in 2020 after her 25-year non-compete expired, as discussed in [[advice-line-with-bobbi-brown-of-jones-road-beauty-september-2025-427590c2-11bc-43fa-9c92-d1eccf05842a]]. Brown says she wanted to improve clean makeup textures and liked building the new company with more control and less unnecessary spending than in her earlier corporate chapter.

In the episode, Jones Road functions as Brown's operating credibility for Advice Line calls. She uses the company to argue for [[ProductLedBrandLongevity]], a clear customer desire around natural-looking beauty, and channel discipline before founders commit to costly expansion.
