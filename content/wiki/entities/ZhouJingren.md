# 周靖人 / Jingren Zhou

## Overview
周靖人 / Jingren Zhou is identified by the source as chief scientist of [[Alibaba]], contributing the model, agent-system, and secure-execution perspective.

## Current Profile
Zhou separates improved model instruction-following from the full system needed for reliable transactions. Personal understanding requires organized history and context, ambiguity requires asking questions, and execution requires sandboxes, permissions, auditability, and a harness that keeps strong constraints from being overwritten by task data.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats intent understanding as a model-plus-agent-system problem.
- Emphasizes clarification when instructions are ambiguous.
- Locates reliable action in sandboxes, permissions, traceability, and the harness layer.
- Treats recommendation as an optimization pipeline whose objectives persist even when intermediate choices disappear from view.
