# Penelope Hanke

## Overview

Penelope Hanke is presented as [[JamesGundlach]]'s coauthor on the 1995 paper “Damned Upon Arrival,” which examined whether unequal transport and medical treatment affected homicide outcomes associated with Black and white women imprisoned in Alabama.

## Current Profile

The episode attributes to Hanke and Gundlach an offender-based comparison of when victims died: immediately, during transport, or after hospital admission. The reported racial pattern led them to argue that unequal survival systems could change both mortality and whether an offender was imprisoned for homicide.
