# Good Culture

## Overview
Good Culture is a U.S. dairy brand co-founded by [[JesseMerrill]] and [[AndersEisner]] to modernize cottage cheese through product texture, simple ingredients, clearer branding, and retail execution.

## Current Profile
The company is represented by one founder interview as a [[CategoryRevival]] business rather than a wholly new food invention. It reframed cottage cheese around protein, low sugar, live cultures, cleaner formulation, and a thicker eating experience, then converted retailer interest into repeatable sales through constrained rollout, packaging correction, [[InStoreDemos]], and close expiration-date management.

Good Culture later became a leading beneficiary of a TikTok cottage-cheese recipe wave. Its prior creator seeding and distribution made the brand visible when attention arrived, but insufficient dairy capacity left reported demand well above supply. [[LCatterton|L Catterton]] acquired a majority stake to support the next operating stage while Merrill remained CEO.

## Key Characteristics
- Modernizes a declining dairy category through texture, ingredients, nutrition framing, and contemporary brand design.
- Uses retail sampling and store-level [[SalesVelocity]] as core customer-education and shelf-retention mechanisms.
- Treats package legibility as operationally important after early yogurt-like design obscured the category.
- Built creator relationships before the viral recipe moment, creating [[PreparedVirality]].
- Faces manufacturing capacity as the main reported constraint after demand acceleration.
- Combines founder control with a majority growth partner chosen for scale experience and operating rigor.

## Evidence
### Product and category repositioning
- [[vmp7035807570-vmp7035807570]] describes texture experiments, fruit and savory variants, a short ingredient list, and the effort to make cottage cheese legible to contemporary consumers.

### Retail execution
- [[vmp7035807570-vmp7035807570]] reports a limited rollout, packaging changes, tasting, shelf-life monitoring, reclamation exposure, and weekly sell-through requirements.

### Viral growth and supply
- [[vmp7035807570-vmp7035807570]] connects years of creator seeding to the cottage-cheese ice-cream trend, while reporting that available capacity served less than half of demand.

### Growth partnership
- [[vmp7035807570-vmp7035807570]] says L Catterton acquired a majority stake after Good Culture had become profitable and that operating expertise was the main rationale.

## Qualifications
The profile relies on one retrospective founder interview. Revenue, category size, demand, market position, transaction timing, ingredient, health, sustainability, and culture claims are not independently verified here. Viral visibility does not establish durable repeat purchase, and supply scarcity can temporarily amplify apparent demand.

## What Changed
- Added Good Culture as a category-revival case joining product reformulation, shelf communication, retail velocity, creator preparation, and constrained manufacturing.
