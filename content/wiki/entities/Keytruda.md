# Keytruda / K药

## Overview
Keytruda / K药 is [[Merck|默沙东]]'s PD-1 oncology medicine and a recurring comparator for clinical differentiation, indication expansion, market saturation, and future patent-cliff pressure.

## Current Profile
Across the bounded sources, Keytruda's position reflects more than mechanism discovery. The 2025 biopharma episode emphasizes clinical-development choices that widened its lead; the Talk三联 episode describes expansion from later-line toward first-line use across several cancers and uses it as the incumbent comparator for [[Ivonescimab|伊沃西]]. Its scale also creates a portfolio-replacement problem as exclusivity approaches expiration.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as the benchmark PD-1 product against which newer immunotherapy combinations are judged.
- Built commercial breadth through indication and treatment-line expansion as well as pharmacology.
- Illustrates how clinical-development execution can create a durable product lead in a crowded class.
- Creates strategic replacement pressure for its owner as future exclusivity loss approaches.
