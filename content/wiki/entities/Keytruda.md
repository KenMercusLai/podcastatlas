# Keytruda / K药

Keytruda / K药 appears in [[vol-117-shengwu-yiyao-de-2025-chaodi-zhongguo-yanfa-jiaolv-he-xinwang-jiwei-lmhral0rmq6tohiqdwsgmfapnyn7]] as [[Merck|默沙东]]'s PD-1 blockbuster and the main comparator for the episode's [[PD1MarketSaturation|PD-1 market saturation]] and [[PD1VEGFBispecific|PD-1/VEGF bispecific]] discussion. The source says Keytruda's success came not only from being in the PD-1 class, but from clinical-development choices that broadened the product's commercial lead.

In the episode, Keytruda's scale becomes a strategic risk for Merck because patent expiration creates a revenue-replacement problem. That makes Chinese PD-1/VEGF data important even before mechanism and long-term survival evidence are fully settled.
