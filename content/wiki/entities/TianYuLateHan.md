# 田豫 / Tian Yu (Late Han)

## Overview
田豫 / Tian Yu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1032-duidai-pantu-caocao-weihe-renci-lmrr3c2f2dayectlwndt4p-katuy|Hanji 1032]] as the adviser who urges [[XianyuFu|鲜于辅]] to submit to [[CaoCao|曹操]] and the emperor-backed court after [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]]'s fall. His argument is that Cao's public deference to [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] gives submission a stronger legitimacy path than isolated regional balancing.

## Current Profile
Tian Yu currently appears as a Youzhou adviser who converts the Cao court's symbolic advantage into a practical submission recommendation. The page is therefore more about political reading than battlefield command: Tian Yu identifies the legitimacy signal that can let Xianyu Fu move from anti-Gongsun local force to court-recognized regional authority.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuyang-connected adviser in the post-Gongsun-Zan Youzhou settlement.
- Reads Cao Cao's emperor-backed command as a credible source of order.
- Advises Xianyu Fu to submit to Cao Cao rather than remain outside the court-facing structure.
- Helps connect local Youzhou force to formal appointment under Emperor Xian.
- Evidence point for legitimacy as a practical alignment mechanism before Guandu.

## Evidence
- Submission argument: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1032-duidai-pantu-caocao-weihe-renci-lmrr3c2f2dayectlwndt4p-katuy|Hanji 1032]] has Tian Yu advise Xianyu Fu that Cao Cao honors the emperor and can likely pacify the realm.
- Appointment consequence: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1032-duidai-pantu-caocao-weihe-renci-lmrr3c2f2dayectlwndt4p-katuy|Hanji 1032]] follows the advice with Emperor Xian's appointment of Xianyu Fu as Jianzhong general over six Youzhou commanderies.
- Legitimacy mechanism: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1032-duidai-pantu-caocao-weihe-renci-lmrr3c2f2dayectlwndt4p-katuy|Hanji 1032]] makes the emperor-backed name a reason local actors can change alignment without treating it as simple surrender to a private warlord.
