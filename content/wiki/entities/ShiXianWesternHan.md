## Overview

石显 / Shi Xian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-502-rang-laoban-bei-heiguo-shi-shui-zai-zhaosi-ls833sgrdjz9e1tupiiqillagohb|Hanji 502]] as the fallen [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]]-era power holder whose collapse creates the first [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] cleanup and personnel-reallocation field.

## Current Profile

The episode treats Shi Xian less as a full biography than as the center of a cleared power structure. Once Chengdi accedes, Shi Xian's previous concentration of power, posts, and benefits becomes a vacancy field. People who could not gain under the old arrangement now have reason to support cleanup, while former accommodators become exposed.

Shi Xian's fall also creates a speech trap for senior ministers. Criticizing him after the fact may be politically necessary, but explaining his former dominance can make Emperor Yuan look weak or culpable. In Hanji 502, that is the opening [[WangZunWesternHan|王尊]] uses against [[KuangHengWesternHan|匡衡]] and [[ZhangTanWesternHan|张谭]].

## Key Characteristics

- Yuan-era court power holder whose fall marks the early Chengdi cleanup.
- Treated by the episode as a former monopolizer of power, positions, and benefits.
- His collapse creates opportunities for new actors and exposes former accommodators.
- His case produces a predecessor-blame problem because criticizing him can indirectly dishonor Emperor Yuan.
