## Overview

石显 / Shi Xian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-499-liubang-fuwoqilai-dasi-liushi-zhegezeng-zengsunzi-lndfgjehpe7qc36hejxba3znlz0p|Hanji 499]], [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-500-taijian-shixian-zhisi-zhan-de-yuegao-si-de-yuecan-lvu7w8xa8mjn8_mbxew7e4afupra|Hanji 500]], and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-502-rang-laoban-bei-heiguo-shi-shui-zai-zhaosi-ls833sgrdjz9e1tupiiqillagohb|Hanji 502]] as the [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]]-era eunuch power holder whose rise illustrates Yuan's governing weakness and whose fall creates the first [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] cleanup and personnel-reallocation field.

## Current Profile

Hanji 499 supplies the broad political diagnosis: Shi Xian's fifteen-year influence under Yuan is treated as the rare Western Han case of eunuch power and as one accelerator of late-Western-Han corruption. Hanji 500 then supplies the formation layer before the cleanup. Shi Xian is presented as a punished young offender who became a eunuch, started from palace message relay, and rose because he combined legal knowledge, cultural literacy, work capacity, and close information access. The source treats him as vicious and manipulative, but not incompetent or merely flattering.

Once Chengdi accedes, Shi Xian's previous concentration of power, posts, and benefits becomes a vacancy field. Hanji 500 says Chengdi first moves him away from the inner document channel to Changxin zhong taipu; Kuang Heng and Zhang Tan then impeach him after the political wind changes. Hanji 502 emphasizes the downstream cleanup politics: people who could not gain under the old arrangement now have reason to support removal, while former accommodators become exposed.

Shi Xian's fall also creates a speech trap for senior ministers. Criticizing him after the fact may be politically necessary, but explaining his former dominance can make Emperor Yuan look weak or culpable. In Hanji 502, that is the opening [[WangZunWesternHan|王尊]] uses against [[KuangHengWesternHan|匡衡]] and [[ZhangTanWesternHan|张谭]].

## Key Characteristics

- Yuan-era court power holder whose fall marks the early Chengdi cleanup.
- Treated by Hanji 499 as a major sign of Yuan-era political decline and rare Western Han eunuch power.
- Legally trained and administratively useful eunuch whose influence rests on ability as well as flattery.
- Message-channel actor whose small-seeming relay work becomes a route into information power.
- Treated by the episodes as a former monopolizer of power, positions, and benefits.
- His case produces a predecessor-blame problem because criticizing him can indirectly dishonor Emperor Yuan.
- His collapse is ruler-dependent: losing Yuan and being moved by Chengdi turns his old network into liability.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 499, Hanji 500, and Hanji 502. It does not settle Shi Xian's full biography, exact legal record, full party network, degree of responsibility for earlier loyalist deaths, personal psychology, or the complete institutional boundaries of the Zhongshu and palace relay posts.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 500's formation and death layer: Shi Xian's legal skill, message-channel power, Chengdi-era transfer, impeachment, family removal, and road death now precede Hanji 502's cleanup-politics reading.
- Added Hanji 499's higher-level decline diagnosis, which makes Shi Xian a symptom of Yuan's mismatch between Confucian learning and practical governance.
