# Chaoshan / 潮汕

Chaoshan is the eastern Guangdong region centered on the Han, Rong, and Lian river basins and the cities of [[Shantou]], [[Chaozhou]], and [[Jieyang]]. In [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046]], [[Banlatte]] uses the region to explain how dense merchant talent, overseas Chinese ties, and first-batch special-zone status can coexist with weaker city-level industrial upgrading.

The episode frames Chaoshan as a geography of pressure and exit. Mountain barriers, limited land, high population density, disasters, and food demand pushed people toward maritime trade, Southeast Asia migration, [[RedHeadShips]], and [[QiaopiRemittanceNetworks]]. That history produced strong commercial networks, but the source's reform-era comparison argues that those networks did not automatically solve [[ChinaSpecialEconomicZoneAsymmetry]], [[RegionalAdministrativeFragmentation]], or [[ExportTaxFraudCreditCrisis]].
