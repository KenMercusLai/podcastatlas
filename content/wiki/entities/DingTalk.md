# DingTalk

DingTalk appears in [[ep117-doubao-yuehuo-guoyi-ali-zaizao-qianwen-shibushi-wanle-lmp0pzdig2ijow5k3cnnnvvqq6sa]] as one of [[Alibaba]]'s work and collaboration surfaces that could connect to a broader [[Qwen]] assistant. The source mainly uses DingTalk as part of the ecosystem argument: Alibaba's assistant can potentially span consumer life services and workplace tasks, not only shopping or search.

[[ai-chongji-qiye-ruanjian-jutou-yu-sap-yuanxin-liao-damoxing-to-b-de-dianfu-yu-bianjie-1-174-1]] adds DingTalk to a [[SAP]] China enterprise workflow case. The source first contrasts DingTalk and [[Feishu]] as collaboration tools against backend [[EnterpriseResourcePlanning|ERP]] systems, then later describes SAP ERP, DingTalk data, and an AI agent working together around a clothing-order fulfillment and scheduling scenario.

## Key Points
- DingTalk broadens Qwen's possible role from consumer services into office and organizational workflows.
- The source does not analyze DingTalk's AI features in detail; it treats DingTalk as one of Alibaba's strategic service assets.
- DingTalk connects the consumer assistant thesis to the wiki's broader themes around [[AgenticWorkflow]] and [[AIAssistantServiceEntry]].
- The SAP source adds DingTalk as a collaboration/data surface that can feed cross-system enterprise agents when backend ERP remains the system of record.
