# 史涣 / Shi Huan (late Han)

## Overview
史涣 / Shi Huan is a Cao-side officer whose current wiki role is limited to the pre-[[WuchaoRaidLateHan|乌巢]] logistics action in the [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]]. He is paired with [[XuHuangCaoGeneral|徐晃]] in the attack on [[HanMengLateHan|韩猛]]'s convoy.

## Current Profile
The source presents Shi Huan as an executor in Cao Cao's shift from exhausted standoff to supply-line pressure. After [[XunYou|荀攸]] advises that Han Meng can be attacked, [[CaoCao|曹操]] sends Shi Huan and Xu Huang to intercept the convoy.

The operation defeats Han Meng and burns [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s baggage. Shi Huan's current significance is therefore relational: he helps turn Xun You's assessment into a material hit on Yuan Shao's logistics before Xu You reveals the larger Wuchao supply vulnerability.

## Key Characteristics
- Cao-side officer in the Guandu campaign.
- Paired with Xu Huang against Han Meng's convoy.
- Tactical executor rather than independent adviser in the present evidence.
- Source-bounded minor figure tied to supply-line attack before Wuchao.
