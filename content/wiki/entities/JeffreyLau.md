## Overview

刘镇伟 / Jeffrey Lau is the credited director of [[TheEagleShootingHeroes1993|《东成西就》]].

## Current Profile

The bounded source presents Lau as a crisis-oriented comic filmmaker whose distinctive strength lies in making limited time, existing resources, star personalities, and live suggestions cohere into playable ensemble comedy. It does not present the film's script as tightly constructed; instead, it credits his coordination of actors, tone, recurring gags, and on-set adjustment for giving a loose production momentum.

This profile is deliberately narrow. It describes the episode's account of one film rather than Lau's full career, and it keeps the reported emergency origin and production anecdotes separate from independently established history.

## Key Characteristics

- Converts production constraint into an executable comic premise.
- Coordinates a high-profile ensemble without allowing one comic persona to dominate the whole film.
- Uses actor contribution and live adjustment to strengthen thinly written roles.
- Prioritizes immediate visual and performative comedy over strict wuxia logic or source fidelity.
