# 妫览 / Gui Lan (late Han)

## Overview
妫览 / Gui Lan is one of the Danyang conspirators in Hanji 1067. The source transcript writes his name as 归岚; the wiki normalizes the event to Gui Lan / 妫览 while preserving that spelling as source-scoped evidence.

## Current Profile
Gui Lan is presented as a local Danyang powerholder who joins [[DaiYuanLateHan|戴员]] in buying off [[BianHongLateHan|边鸿]] to kill [[SunYiLateHan|孙翊]]. After the murder, Gui Lan and Dai Yuan explain the killing as Bian Hong's madness, then kill [[SunHeLateHan|孙河]] when he comes to investigate.

Gui Lan's downfall comes from the same appetite he tries to convert into power. He moves into Sun Yi's residence, demands [[LadyXuSunYi|徐夫人]] as wife, and accepts her request to wait until the mourning rite. After her staged grief and marriage-ready performance convince his observers, he enters her room and is killed by [[SunGaoLateHan|孙高]] and [[FuYingLateHan|傅婴]].

## Key Characteristics
- Acts with Dai Yuan as a Danyang conspirator against Sun-family authority.
- Uses an intimate killer and a madness cover story rather than open challenge at first.
- Escalates the event by killing Sun He after Sun Yi's death.
- Attempts to convert occupation of Sun Yi's residence into sexual and household domination over Lady Xu.
- Becomes vulnerable because desire and apparent compliance override suspicion.
