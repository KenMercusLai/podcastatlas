# Barbuda

[[Barbuda]] is the small island case in [[sand-heists-and-property-rights-in-the-caribbean-summer-school]]. The episode presents it as a place where land long remained under [[CommunalLandTenure]] rather than ordinary private title, with about 1,500 residents using land through collective norms and later statutory recognition under the [[BarbudaLandAct]].

The island becomes a property-rights conflict after [[HurricaneIrma]] in 2017. [[GastonBrowne]] argues that titled plots would let residents borrow and rebuild, while speakers such as [[JohnMussington]] and [[NataliaJohn]] argue that private title could reduce Barbudans' collective control over land, food security, development approval, and environmental value.
