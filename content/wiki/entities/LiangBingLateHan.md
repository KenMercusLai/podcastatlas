## Overview
梁并 / Liang Bing is a late-Han frontier official in the 145 CE account. He appears as the counterpoint to corrupt Western Qiang campaign command: while generals waste men and money through theft and bribery, Liang Bing uses credibility, grace, and surrender policy to pacify Longyou.

## Current Profile
Liang Bing's current wiki profile is source-bounded to the Hanji 826 note. As Left Fengyi, he wins the trust of Qiang groups and induces Li Nan, Hu Nu, and roughly fifty thousand households to surrender. The episode treats that result as temporary but real administrative repair after a costly and corrupt frontier war.

The profile is not yet a full biography. Its durable value is comparative: Liang Bing shows that trust-based frontier governance can restore order where military finance corruption has hollowed out coercive capacity.

## Key Characteristics
- Frontier official whose authority works through credibility rather than extraction.
- Surrender-policy actor who makes Li Nan and Hu Nu's submission possible.
- Administrative counterexample to generals who steal pay, provisions, and campaign funds.
- Source-scoped Longyou pacifier whose success is real but not yet linked to a broader career record.
