## Overview

梁并 / Liang Bing is a late-Han frontier official whose current profile now spans two surrender-management settings. Hanji 820 places him in the 140 CE Southern Xiongnu revolt as a Xiongnu command figure who defeats rebels with [[MaXuLateHan|Ma Xu]] and receives [[XiuliChanyu|Xiuli]]'s apology before illness removes him; Hanji 826 later presents him as Left Fengyi, where trust and grace induce large-scale Qiang surrender after corrupt campaign command has damaged Longyou.

## Current Profile

Hanji 820 gives Liang Bing the earlier Xiongnu-frontier layer. Wusi and Che Niu rebel, and Liang Bing joins Ma Xu in defeating them with a mixed frontier force. When the Southern Xiongnu chanyu apologizes and is said not to have joined the plot, Liang Bing is the command recipient before illness forces his recall. His removal matters because [[ChenGuiDuliaoGeneralLateHan|Chen Gui]] then acts in the command role and coerces the chanyu and Left Wise King into suicide, making Liang Bing's absence part of the crisis chain.

Hanji 826 gives the later Longyou layer. As Left Fengyi, Liang Bing wins the trust of Qiang groups and induces Li Nan, Hu Nu, and roughly fifty thousand households to surrender. The episode treats that result as temporary but real administrative repair after a costly and corrupt frontier war.

## Key Characteristics

- Frontier official repeatedly associated with surrender credibility rather than indiscriminate coercion.
- Early Xiongnu-frontier commander whose illness-driven recall opens the way for Chen Gui's overreach.
- Later Left Fengyi whose trust-based governance makes large Qiang submission possible.
- Administrative counterexample to generals who steal pay, provisions, and campaign funds.
- Source-scoped figure whose two current layers suggest a broader frontier-pacification career without yet proving its full shape.

## Qualifications

The current evidence comes from two source notes and does not establish Liang Bing's whole career, family background, or later offices. Hanji 820's transcript form 梁炳 is normalized to 梁并. Li Nan and Hu Nu remain source-scoped Qiang leaders until more evidence justifies standalone pages.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 820 as Liang Bing's earlier Southern Xiongnu layer.
- Reframed Liang Bing from a one-source Longyou pacifier into a repeated surrender-management official whose absence also matters.
- Preserved Hanji 826's trust-based Qiang surrender as the later and clearer positive pacification case.
