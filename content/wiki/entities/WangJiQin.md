# 王稽 / Wang Ji (Qin envoy)

王稽 / Wang Ji appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-88-ni-zenme-kandai-fenghuangnan-1-lpuxkdq-rvnifuoneq212g53w5z5]] as the [[QinState|秦国]] envoy who notices the fugitive [[FanJu|范雎]] living under the alias 张禄. The source transcript renders his name as 王继; this page normalizes the identity as 王稽 while preserving the variant.

In the episode, Wang Ji's importance is that he turns Fan Ju's hidden survival into Qin recruitment. After judging 张禄 to be an exceptional talent, he brings him back to Qin and recommends him to [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-2-jiemi-xinlingjun-ping-shenme-shi-si-gongzi-zhi-shou-lodqntthbil9o8ezppt66lywzd-f]] returns to Wang Ji as one of Fan Ju's benefactors after Fan Ju has become powerful. The episode says Fan Ju arranges a Qin office for him, making Wang Ji part of the same private-gratitude personnel chain as [[ZhengAnping|郑安平]]. The source's critique is less about Wang Ji's independent actions than about Fan Ju's use of state appointments to repay personal debts.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-1-lishishang-diyi-chounan-nixi-zuo-qinxiang-lvmdrgbhvpbiyngm-nviunour0sk]] gives Wang Ji the negative outcome previewed by that chain. The episode says Wang Ji, as the Henei governor, is executed after secretly colluding with eastern lords. His death compounds Fan Ju's exposure after [[ZhengAnping|郑安平]]'s surrender, because both failed officeholders had benefited from Fan Ju's gratitude-backed recommendations.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-2-mingming-yilian-baixiang-de-caize-ping-shenme-baixiang-lhfyb6hzcqkpbzdrwsxajb7hhmhe]] returns to that endpoint when discussing Fan Ju's final year. The host reads the sparse "王稽、张禄死" style notice as evidence for a linked fate around Wang Ji, Fan Ju's alias 张禄, and Zheng Anping, while still treating Fan Ju's exact cause of death as source-limited.
