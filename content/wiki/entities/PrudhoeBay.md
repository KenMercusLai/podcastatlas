# Prudhoe Bay / 普拉德霍湾

Prudhoe Bay / 普拉德霍湾 appears in [[84-binglu-kuanghua-hao-guniang-ni-zhishi-wangle-yonggan-699443676]] as the northern destination of [[AmyButcher|Amy Butcher / 艾米]] and [[JoyWiebe|Joy "Joey" Wiebe / Joey]]'s trip on the [[DaltonHighway|Dalton Highway]]. The episode treats arrival there as a partial safety scene: hot water, air conditioning, food, and Joey's acquaintances briefly counter Amy's expectation of hostile or dismissive men.

The place also undercuts Amy's rescue fantasy. She hoped to swim in the Arctic Ocean and make the trip end in a decisive photograph, but the route was not open yet. The missed swim helps the episode argue that recovery does not depend on a single dramatic ritual; it can come from the journey's accumulated witness, fear, laughter, and decision-making.
