# Ted Kennedy

Ted Kennedy appears in [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] as the U.S. senator who addressed the [[UnitedStatesCongress|United States Congress]] on 20 October 1971 and described [[NorthernIreland]] as sliding toward repression, displacement, and civil war. The source says his comparison of Ulster to "Britain's Vietnam" was controversial but not meaningless, because British troops were becoming trapped in a conflict with no clear political solution.

For the wiki, Kennedy functions less as an analyst of exact military equivalence than as a marker of international reputational pressure on the [[UnitedKingdom]]. His intervention connects [[TheTroubles]] to Irish-American politics and to [[PublicIndifferencePolicyDrift]] in Britain, where mainland voters often wanted troops home but did not make Northern Ireland a central electoral issue.
