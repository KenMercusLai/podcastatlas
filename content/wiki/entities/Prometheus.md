# Prometheus / 普罗米修斯

## Overview

Prometheus is the mythic fire-giver and punished benefactor used in the wiki to interpret scientific creation, dangerous knowledge, maker responsibility, and the costs imposed on those who transform human capability.

## Current Profile

In [[141-zhizao-fulankensitan-kehuan-zhimu-he-tade-shenqi-shidai-857923931]], Prometheus enters through [[Frankenstein|《弗兰肯斯坦》]]'s subtitle. Divine fire becomes experimental science, electricity, physiology, and creation, while the myth asks what a maker owes to a being made and abandoned.

The Oppenheimer episode extends the figure from literary creation to state weapons science. [[JRobertOppenheimer|Oppenheimer]] resembles Prometheus because atomic knowledge offers extraordinary power and possible liberation while bringing mass-destructive danger and later punishment to the figure associated with giving humanity the new “fire.” The analogy frames ambivalence; it does not decide the history or distribute responsibility by itself.

## Key Characteristics

- Mythic giver of fire or transformative knowledge to humanity.
- Figure whose gift combines liberation, danger, transgression, and punishment.
- Romantic framework for scientific creation and neglected maker responsibility.
- Modern framework for atomic power and the compromised public scientist.

## Evidence

- Literary creation: [[141-zhizao-fulankensitan-kehuan-zhimu-he-tade-shenqi-shidai-857923931]] uses the “Modern Prometheus” subtitle to connect mythic creation with contemporary experiment and electricity.
- Atomic knowledge: [[343-oppenheimer-the-father-of-the-atom-bomb-part-1-glt9202576882]] uses the myth to frame the bomb as both scientific radiance and world-destroying terror.
- Punished maker: [[343-oppenheimer-the-father-of-the-atom-bomb-part-1-glt9202576882]] anticipates Oppenheimer's later political punishment while preserving his own agency and errors.

## Qualifications

Promethean analogy can illuminate the dual-use structure of knowledge but can also over-personalize collective institutions and romanticize harm. Victor Frankenstein, Oppenheimer, Los Alamos, and the myth differ in agency, victims, political authority, and historical evidence; none should be treated as a one-to-one retelling of another.

## What Changed

- Extended Prometheus from the Frankenstein creation problem to atomic knowledge, collective weapons science, and Oppenheimer's punished public identity.
- Qualified the analogy so myth does not replace institutional or historical responsibility analysis.
