# The Billion Dollar Spy / 《亿万美元间谍》

## Overview

*The Billion Dollar Spy* / 《亿万美元间谍》 is the book frame used by a [[HuzuoHuyou|忽左忽右]] episode to recount [[AdolfTolkachev|Adolf Tolkachev]]'s CIA source work and its Cold War context.

## Current Profile

In [[506-yiwan-meiyuan-jiandie-tuokaqiefu-yu-lengzhan-wanqi-meisu-qingbao-daan-lvqmgp0k-6r7o0whwbakbuct8kvn]], the book supports a narrative that joins street-level Moscow tradecraft, military-technical collection, Tolkachev's mixed motives, CIA institutional caution, and insider betrayal. The title's scale refers to reported intelligence value and avoided research expense, but the episode qualifies that valuation and refuses to treat one espionage case as the cause of Soviet dissolution.

## Key Characteristics

- Narrative history centered on a Soviet technical insider and CIA handling.
- Connects human-source access to aircraft, radar, air-defense, and missile technology.
- Uses operational detail to explain repeated meetings under Moscow surveillance.
- Places personal motive and institutional vulnerability inside late-Cold-War history.
- Supplies major value claims that remain difficult to verify against fully open records.
