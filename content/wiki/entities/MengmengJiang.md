## Current Profile
The available source presents her as a clinician whose responsibilities expanded beyond bedside work into teaching, scheduling, paperwork, complaint and dispute handling, coordination, and hospital-wide consultation. She describes this challenge as energizing at times but also exhausting enough to reduce motivation and capacity elsewhere.

Her account also makes clinical expertise compatible with vulnerability. After skipping food, she collapsed with suspected hypoglycemia and then developed frightening symptoms she interpreted as hyperventilation. Knowing the likely mechanism did not make it easy to regulate her breathing or accept help, and she connects the experience to greater empathy for patients.

Online, she prefers the identity of doctor over “internet celebrity” and says her public content should serve medical education and her department's direction. The same visibility brings recognition, scrutiny, abusive messages, copied content, and pressure to reconcile a friendly online image with a stricter teaching and workplace role.
