# Jim Thorpe

Jim Thorpe appears in [[the-nfl-1]] as the first president of the early professional football league that became the [[NationalFootballLeague|NFL]]. The episode says Thorpe lent legitimacy to a professional sport that still sat below college football in public prestige.

The source uses Thorpe less as a detailed biography than as a legitimacy signal. His presence shows how the early league needed recognizable athletic authority before later commissioners such as [[BertBell]] and [[PeteRozelle]] could turn professional football into a governed national media product.
