# Franz Kromer / 弗朗兹·克罗默

[[FranzKromer|Franz Kromer / 弗朗兹·克罗默]] is the older boy who extorts [[EmilSinclair|辛克莱尔]] in [[DemianNovel|《德米安》]] as summarized by [[20-demian-gaiyin-de-etou-shang-na-shenmi-de-yinji-567302734]]. Sinclair invents a theft story to look brave, and Kromer turns the lie into leverage: money demands, secrecy, shame, and escalating fear pull Sinclair out of the safe family world.

The source uses Kromer to show that the dark world is not abstract. Before [[CainMarkReinterpretation|Cain's mark]] or [[Abraxas|Abraxas]] become philosophical symbols, fear appears as a child's surrender of power to someone who knows his shame.

## Key Claims
- Kromer makes Sinclair's first fall into the dark world bodily and social rather than theoretical.
- The extortion plot exposes how shame can isolate a child from family protection.
- [[MaxDemian|德米安]]'s intervention matters because it breaks Kromer's hold but does not erase Sinclair's deeper inner division.
