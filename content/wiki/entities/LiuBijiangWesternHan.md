## Overview

刘碧江 / Liu Bijiang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-394-2-jiemi-huoguang-chengwei-xihan-diyi-quanchen-de-xinji-fmmyov0ecqykq37zkphi40o52bc6|Hanji 394-2]] as a Western Han imperial-clan figure chosen by [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] during [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]]'s early regency. The episode identifies him as a descendant of Chu Yuan Wang and says Huo appoints him Guanglu Dafu and concurrently Changle Palace Guard Commandant.

## Current Profile

The page is intentionally narrow. Liu Bijiang matters here less as an independent biographical subject than as evidence for Huo Guang's early stabilization move after being warned not to repeat the Lu-family mistake of excluding Liu royal kin. By placing Liu Bijiang in a visible court and palace-guard role, Huo can signal that imperial relatives who cooperate with the regency can receive office rather than be automatically marginalized.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han imperial-clan appointee in the early Han Zhao regency field.
- Identified by the source as a descendant of Chu Yuan Wang.
- Appointed Guanglu Dafu by Huo Guang.
- Given concurrent Changle Palace Guard Commandant responsibility.
- Evidence for Huo Guang's benefit-sharing strategy toward royal kin.
