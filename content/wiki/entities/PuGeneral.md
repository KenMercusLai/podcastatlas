# 蒲将军 / Pu General

蒲将军 / Pu General enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-134-ni-buzhidao-de-pofu-chenzhou-banben-ltnry1fva2hzplw4ft8nrfpoiolw|Qinji 134]] as the Chu commander paired with [[YingBu|英布]] in the first stage of [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s [[JuluBattle|巨鹿之战]] operation. The episode does not develop his biography, so this page keeps him source-scoped as a named military actor in the corridor attack.

After Xiang Yu kills [[SongYi|宋义]] and the Chu army reaches Pingyuanjin, Ying Bu and Pu General lead twenty thousand elite troops across the Yellow River before the main force. Their assignment is not the final head-on battle against [[WangLiQin|王离]], but the attack on Qin support units and protected yongdao lines supplying [[Julu|巨鹿]].

The result is decisive for the episode's interpretation. By breaking the corridor guarded for [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] and Wang Li's besieging army, Pu General and Ying Bu help turn Qin's numerical and supply advantage into vulnerability. Their work prepares the conditions for the later [[BreakingCauldronsAndSinkingBoats|破釜沉舟]] attack.
