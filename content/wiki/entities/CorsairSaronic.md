# Corsair (Saronic)

Corsair is [[Saronic]]'s 24-foot autonomous surface-vessel product line in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-saronic-founders-autonomous-warships-chinas-230x-advantage-swarms-of-robot-ships-42334230]]. [[DinoMavroukis]] says a Corsair helped support a recent [[StraitOfHormuz]] rescue mission, though operational details are deliberately limited in the source.

The product matters to the wiki because it makes [[AutonomousNavalVessels]] concrete at small scale. The episode contrasts Corsair with ordinary commercial boats and says autonomy, design, control authority, and production volume make the vessel part of a larger [[NavalShipbuildingThroughput]] argument rather than only a one-off rescue platform.

## Key Claims
- The source describes Corsair as a 24-foot fully autonomous speedboat.
- Dino says Saronic can build 2,000 Corsair vessels per year at its Texas manufacturing facility.
- The source frames Corsair as useful for persistence, scale, and risk reduction around chokepoints such as the [[StraitOfHormuz]].
