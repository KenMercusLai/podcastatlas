# Wally Funk

Wally Funk is the aviation pioneer profiled by [[AnnWroe]] in [[omission-accomplished-why-the-iran-war-cycle-spins-on-6a6c7475aa1642a30f17f1cb]]. The episode presents her as a determined pilot from childhood: she sought flight early, earned a pilot's licence by 17, and excelled in college aviation activities.

Funk joined [[WilliamRandolphLovelace]]'s women-in-spaceflight testing program at 21 and took the same tests as male astronaut candidates. The segment says she performed exceptionally, including a long sensory-deprivation test, but was excluded from the official astronaut corps because of gender barriers rather than ability.

Her later career kept her inside aviation even after that setback. Funk worked for the [[FederalAviationAdministration]], later investigated crashes for the [[NationalTransportationSafetyBoard]], set up a flight school in Taos, and continued pursuing space-related training. At 82, [[JeffBezos]] invited her onto a [[BlueOrigin]] rocket; the episode says the flight lasted ten minutes and reached 62 miles above Earth.
