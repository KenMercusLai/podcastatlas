# 《无人观看》 / Unwatched

## Overview
[[LiuTianzhao|刘天昭]]'s long novel 《无人观看》 is represented in the wiki through the [[CultureLimited|文化有限]] episode [[vol-352-wuren-guankan-ziwo-chayixing-jida-waibu-xiangsixing-jiqiang-de-women-gkwrijiofxaxaq8boatxoohj]].

## Current Profile
The episode reads 《无人观看》 as a contemporary urban novel about Beijing middle-class families whose lives are materially functional but spiritually jammed. Its scenes include a Friday-night traffic jam after an AI-and-housing-industry lecture, children treating [[Doubao|豆包]] as part of family information flow, parents managing schooling and elder care, couples losing speech with each other, and characters wondering whether [[ChatGPT]] or other AI systems can see them more clearly than people do. The hosts disagree about how friendly the novel is to readers, but they converge on the view that its slow, interior, multi-voice form fits its problem of unseen selfhood.

## Key Characteristics
- The novel uses ordinary urban middle-class routines as the site of philosophical pressure.
- It treats AI as part of intimate and family life rather than only as a workplace technology.
- Its form is described as dense, slow, interior, multi-voiced, and sometimes difficult.
- Its central recognition problem is that people can be socially visible while still not being truly understood.
- It connects aging, loneliness, family obligation, social media, and consumer identity into one selfhood crisis.
- Its ending is read as philosophical and affective rather than conventionally plot-resolving.

## Qualifications
The page reflects a podcast reading of the novel, not an independent literary history. The hosts note that the book may be stagnant, hard to enter, and reader-selective, so the current synthesis should not flatten their disagreement into unqualified praise.
