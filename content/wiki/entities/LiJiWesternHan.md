## Overview

郦寄 / Li Ji is a Western Han commander associated in the current evidence with the transfer of Northern Army control during the Lü-family crisis and the later siege of [[LiuSuiZhaoKingWesternHan|赵王刘遂]] at Handan.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-236-qiguo-zhiluan-pingding-hanjingdi-shafengle-2-lisudlhjt_yawpyv4i8chvxl37cr|Hanji 236 part 2]] introduces Li Ji as the son of Li Shang and recalls that he helped induce Lü Lu to leave the Northern Army, allowing Zhou Bo to enter and take control. In the Seven States crisis, he besieges Handan for more than seven months without success. [[LuanBu|栾布]] later returns from Qi, combines forces with him, and the army uses river water to break the city's defense.

## Key Characteristics

- Son of Li Shang in the episode's genealogy.
- Participant in the maneuver that transfers Northern Army control during the Lü-family crisis.
- Imperial commander responsible for the prolonged siege of Handan.
- Commander who completes the Zhao campaign only after combining with Luan Bu.
