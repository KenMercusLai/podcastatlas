## Overview

郦寄 / Li Ji is a Western Han commander whose friendship with [[LuLuWesternHan|吕禄]] becomes the interpersonal channel for transferring Northern Army control during the Lü-family crisis, and who later besieges [[LiuSuiZhaoKingWesternHan|赵王刘遂]] at Handan.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-236-qiguo-zhiluan-pingding-hanjingdi-shafengle-2-lisudlhjt_yawpyv4i8chvxl37cr|Hanji 236 part 2]] introduces Li Ji as the son of Li Shang and recalls that he helped induce Lü Lu to leave the Northern Army, allowing Zhou Bo to enter and take control. In the Seven States crisis, he besieges Handan for more than seven months without success. [[LuanBu|栾布]] later returns from Qi, combines forces with him, and the army uses river water to break the city's defense.

The earlier maneuver carries a reputational dispute. Contemporaries reportedly called Li Ji a betrayer of friendship, while Ban Gu defended him on the ground that Lü Lu's coercion of [[LiShang|郦商]] made action necessary to save his father, ruler, and state. This establishes a competing moral interpretation, not a final verdict on his motive. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-207-liubang-xiaoyizi-lvxu-bei-gunsha-1-lsrs42rb8-qref1eevqdnhe3mufv|Hanji 207 part 1]]

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-206-zhu-lv-an-liu-shishang-diyi-miemen-an-2-lpsj8exoquanifh7njkrhqszrhkh0|Hanji 206 part 2]] gives that maneuver its operational sequence. Because Lü Lu trusts Li Ji and hunts with him, Li Ji can support the claim that imperial authority has assigned the Northern Army to Zhou Bo and that refusal will bring disaster. Lü Lu then surrenders the general's seal and leaves. The episode supports Li Ji's access and persuasive role but does not establish the exact words, degree of coercion, or private priority among friendship, family rescue, and dynastic restoration.

## Key Characteristics

- Son of Li Shang in the episode's genealogy.
- Participant in the maneuver that transfers Northern Army control during the Lü-family crisis.
- Trusted friend whose personal access makes institutional command transfer possible without an initial battle.
- Figure whose anti-Lü conduct is disputed between friendship betrayal and public/familial rescue.
- Imperial commander responsible for the prolonged siege of Handan.
- Commander who completes the Zhao campaign only after combining with Luan Bu.

## Qualifications

The current profile rests on three popular-history summaries. The Northern Army maneuver, Li Ji's exact message, Lü Lu's coercion of Li Shang, Ban Gu's exact reasoning, Li Ji's motive, titles, siege duration, division of command, and hydraulic breach remain episode-attributed and require primary-text comparison.

## What Changed

- Added the trust-and-persuasion mechanism by which Li Ji helps detach Lü Lu from the Northern Army.
- Preserved the unresolved conflict between betrayal-of-friendship and family/state rescue interpretations.
