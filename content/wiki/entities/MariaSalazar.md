# Maria Salazar

## Overview
Maria Salazar is the Republican U.S. representative whom the bounded source places in a heavily Cuban-American Miami district during the 2026 midterms.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Salazar as a partial policy-distancing case. After previously winning by nearly 70,000 votes, she criticized parts of the Trump administration's immigration enforcement in English- and Spanish-language campaign advertising and was reported to be leading by only about one thousand votes with counting nearly complete. The source infers that distancing may have helped her avoid defeat but did not remove the wider Latino backlash.

## Key Characteristics
- Republican representative in a long-Republican Cuban-American Miami district.
- Publicly criticized some Trump-administration immigration enforcement as excessively harsh.
- Communicated that distinction in both English and Spanish campaign advertising.
- Reported as narrowly ahead rather than decisively re-elected when the episode was recorded.
- Illustrates both the possible value and the limits of candidate-level policy distancing.
