# Haofang / 浩方

Haofang / 浩方 appears in [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]] as one of [[Shanda|盛大]]'s acquired game-community assets. The episode remembers it as an important battle and LAN-style matchmaking platform for games such as DOTA and Red Alert before later platforms overtook it.

In Shanda's broader story, Haofang shows how [[FamilyInternetPortalStrategy]] tried to gather many entertainment behaviors around one company. Its later sale also fits the episode's pattern: Shanda often bought or built assets early, but many did not remain central under [[ChenTianqiao]]'s organization.
