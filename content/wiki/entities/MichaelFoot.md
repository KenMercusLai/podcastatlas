# Michael Foot / 迈克尔·富特

Michael Foot / 迈克尔·富特 appears in [[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] as a source-scoped and contested case from [[OlegGordievsky|Oleg Gordievsky]]'s London intelligence. The episode says Foot may have had a relationship with the [[KGB]] and at least received money, but it explicitly warns that whether he knowingly served Soviet intelligence remains hard to establish.

The page therefore treats Foot primarily as an uncertainty marker. In the episode, his case illustrates why [[StrategicIntelligenceInterpretation]] can matter more than lists of alleged agents: a name may be politically explosive while still requiring careful confidence discipline.
