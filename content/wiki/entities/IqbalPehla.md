# Iqbal Pehla

## Overview
Iqbal Pehla is the founder and CEO of [[InsightRx]] interviewed in [[ep-25-ai-revolution-in-marketing-from-traditional-to-transformational|Data Science With Sam EP25]]. The source describes a career path from computer science and early digital-marketing work into healthcare and pharmaceutical marketing analytics.

## Current Profile
Iqbal presents AI as a practical acceleration layer for marketing data, optimization, insight, and content rather than as a reason to automate indiscriminately. His operating rule is to begin with a concrete business outcome, test a bounded use case, and retain marketers who can interpret several data sources and decide what action should follow. His strongest claims concern InsightRx's source-reported ability to shorten outcomes-based reporting and react faster to regional campaign signals.

## Key Characteristics
- Computer-science graduate who entered digital marketing through SEO and paid-search experimentation.
- Healthcare-marketing practitioner focused on analytics, optimization, and measurable campaign outcomes.
- Founder and CEO of [[InsightRx]].
- Advocates outcome-first AI adoption instead of tool-led experimentation without a business need.
- Encourages small, low-risk experiments as a way to build practical AI fluency.
- Distinguishes internal, client-facing, and productized AI and accepts stopping ideas that are premature.
- Treats marketer judgment and data interpretation as more important as AI increases available information and output.
