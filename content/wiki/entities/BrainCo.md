# BrainCo

## Overview
BrainCo is a Chinese neurotechnology company represented through a residual-signal bionic hand and non-invasive consumer devices.

## Current Profile
The source uses BrainCo to show a lower-invasiveness route into [[BrainComputerInterface|neural-interface]] adoption. Its bionic hand reads signals remaining in an amputee’s arm rather than requiring brain surgery and is shown performing dexterous tasks. The company also markets consumer products for concentration and sleep, placing it at the boundary between assistive function and more weakly evidenced wellness uses.

## Key Characteristics
- Develops a non-surgical bionic hand controlled through residual arm signals.
- Demonstrates dexterity through tasks such as piano playing and calligraphy.
- Participates in China’s broader non-invasive consumer-neurotechnology market.
- Illustrates a commercialization path different from implanted cortical systems.
