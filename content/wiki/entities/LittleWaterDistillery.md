# Little Water Distillery

Little Water Distillery is the Atlantic City craft distillery discussed by [[MarkLittleWaterDistillery|Mark]] in [[advice-line-with-carlton-calvin-of-razor-c0de1a84-3d35-45f3-921c-6144693ad86e]]. The source says the business has operated since 2016 after Mark and his brother moved from buying their father a copper pot still to applying for an early craft distillation license and leaving their prior careers.

The episode frames Little Water as an operating-complexity case. Direct distribution preserved margin but made the founders responsible for manufacturing, marketing, sales, vehicles, staffing, hospitality, and retail; after post-COVID contraction and debt, the company recently decided to work through distributors. The Advice Line focus is to concentrate on [[48BlocksEspressoMartini]] and adjacent martini products instead of spreading scarce cash and attention across too many SKUs.
