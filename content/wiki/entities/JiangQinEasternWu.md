## Overview

[[JiangQinEasternWu|蒋钦]] is an Eastern Wu commander presented in Hanji 1157 as recommending [[XuShengEasternWu|徐盛]] despite an earlier grievance involving Jiang Qin's subordinate.

## Current Profile

The episode says Xu Sheng, while serving as a county magistrate, detained a subordinate of Jiang Qin and sought the man's execution. Jiang Qin nevertheless later praises Xu Sheng to [[SunQuan|孙权]] as loyal, diligent, forceful, and capable of leading a large force.

The profile is therefore one of bounded personnel judgment: Jiang Qin does not need to erase the earlier conflict to judge that public service requires useful talent to be recognized.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Wu commander inside Sun Quan's military organization.
- Has a prior subordinate-related grievance with Xu Sheng.
- Recommends Xu Sheng on perceived ability and loyalty despite that grievance.
- Separates personnel judgment from private resentment.
- Gains Sun Quan's respect in the episode's account.

- Prior grievance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1157-duanzan-de-suncao-lianmeng-yaobai-de-sunquan-lpngfqsoz-zjr2iytaaox89df1zcj|Hanji 1157]] reports Xu Sheng's detention of Jiang Qin's subordinate and request for execution.
- Recommendation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1157-duanzan-de-suncao-lianmeng-yaobai-de-sunquan-lpngfqsoz-zjr2iytaaox89df1zcj|Hanji 1157]] says Jiang Qin repeatedly recommends Xu Sheng's loyalty, diligence, force, courage, and command potential.
- Duty boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1157-duanzan-de-suncao-lianmeng-yaobai-de-sunquan-lpngfqsoz-zjr2iytaaox89df1zcj|Hanji 1157]] attributes the recommendation to the need for talent while the larger enterprise remains unfinished.

## Qualifications

The source does not establish the subordinate's identity, the underlying offense, the legal basis for detention, the exact recommendation wording, or every motive involved. Recommending Xu Sheng does not by itself settle whether the earlier official action was justified.
