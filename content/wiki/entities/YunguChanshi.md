# 云谷禅师 / Yungu Chanshi

云谷禅师 / Yungu Chanshi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-161-caiyun-he-renji-guanxi-de-liangzi-jiuchan-4-lml7lpd3u9l7esryknmhoampgx6v|Hanji 161 part 4]] as the Buddhist teacher who changes [[YuanLiaofan|袁了凡]]'s relationship to fate. The episode says Yuan had accepted a predicted life pattern until Yungu Chanshi pointed him toward 发心,积德行善, and embodied practice.

This page is source-scoped. The wiki records Yungu Chanshi as a teacher-function in the episode's argument: a fate prediction becomes changeable only when a person stops treating it as a sentence and begins treating conduct as the place where fate can be worked on.
