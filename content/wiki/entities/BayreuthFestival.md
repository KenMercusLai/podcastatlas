# Bayreuth Festival

The Bayreuth Festival appears in [[caracas-under-pressure-democracy-in-venezuela-6a7d8bbe7d85cbdf96e00822]] as the performance context for [[RichardWagner]]'s [[DerRingDesNibelungen|Ring cycle]]. The source says Bayreuth's festival house concealed the orchestra and focused attention on the stage, a theatrical design choice it presents as anticipating later cinema.

In the wiki, Bayreuth connects staging architecture to [[Gesamtkunstwerk]]: the room, audience attention, music, and spectacle are part of the artwork's operating system rather than neutral surroundings.
