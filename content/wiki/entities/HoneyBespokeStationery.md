# Honey Bespoke Stationery

Honey Bespoke Stationery is [[ShireenTippett]]'s stationery and paper-goods business in [[advice-line-with-carlton-calvin-of-razor-c0de1a84-3d35-45f3-921c-6144693ad86e]]. The company sells personalized stationery, birthday invitations, birth announcements, and licensed collegiate stationery.

The source says Honey Bespoke reached nearly $120,000 in revenue the prior year, mostly through Etsy, but the current year had slowed. [[CarltonCalvin]] and [[GuyRaz]] advise Shireen to reduce dependence on marketplace traffic by testing school-connected stationery stores, markets, sales reps, and founder-led content that shows the design process and makes the brand easier to trust.
