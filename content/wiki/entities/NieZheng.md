# 聂政 / Nie Zheng

聂政 / Nie Zheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]], where [[YanZhongzi|严仲子]] hires him to kill [[XiaLei|侠累]], the chancellor of [[HanState|韩国]]. The episode emphasizes two constraints before the killing: Yan Zhongzi offers wealth and a revenge commission, but Nie Zheng refuses while his mother is alive because he still has filial obligations.

After his mother's death, Nie Zheng carries out the assassination in a heavily guarded meeting. The source says he destroys his face, gouges out his eyes, and kills himself so his identity will not implicate his sister [[NieYing|聂英]]. His story therefore sits between [[PoliticalAssassinationEthics]] and [[AristocraticHonorOverLife]]: it is not presented as clean public justice, but as revenge, family protection, and name-making inside a Warring States honor world.
