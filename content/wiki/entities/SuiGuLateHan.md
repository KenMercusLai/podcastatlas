# 眭固 / Sui Gu (Late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] first places Sui Gu in the [[DongCommandery|东郡]] attack field with [[YuDuLateHan|于毒]] and [[BaiRaoLateHan|白绕]]. In this source, [[WangGongLateHan|王肱]] cannot resist their forces, and [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s defeat of Bai Rao helps Cao Cao gain the commandery office.

眭固 / Sui Gu (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-961-neihuang-zhizhan-caocao-chulu-fengmang-ljdlt6kiu8tg0qws-jlyej-yhgxr|Hanji 961]] as one of the forces [[CaoCao|曹操]] defeats after the indirect relief of [[DongWuyangLateHan|东武阳]]. The transcript does not develop Sui Gu's background; his role is battlefield-specific inside the [[NeihuangBattleLateHan|内黄之战]] chain.

The source pairs Sui Gu with [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]], the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] chanyu, after [[YuDuLateHan|于毒]] abandons the Dong Wuyang siege. Sui Gu therefore helps make the campaign more than a local rescue: Cao Cao first changes Yu Du's target decision, then presses into a broader Neihuang fight against multiple armed actors.
