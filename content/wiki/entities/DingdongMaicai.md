# 叮咚买菜 / Dingdong Maicai

## Overview
叮咚买菜 / Dingdong Maicai is the fresh-grocery platform that the wiki now connects to both fresh-grocery ecommerce economics and an earlier community O2O pivot.

## Current Profile
[[no-200-dianshang-sanguo-zhi-qunxiong-zhulu-yaogua-gongzhang-chijian-juqian-yiji-108-zhong-sifa-974601500]] introduced Dingdong Maicai as a front-warehouse fresh-grocery platform contrasted with [[Missfresh]], later profitability claims, and a reported sale to [[Meituan]]. The No.218 O2O episode adds its origin story from [[DingdongXiaoqu|叮咚小区]], a broad community O2O attempt that shrank toward errands and then found more concrete demand in buying fruit and vegetables.

The current synthesis is that Dingdong Maicai matters because it shows narrowing as survival. The earlier community-superapp idea tried to bundle many neighborhood services, but the later grocery focus made the economics more legible, though still constrained by [[FreshGroceryEcommerceEconomics]], delivery density, shrinkage, and local fulfillment cost.

## Key Characteristics
- Dingdong Maicai is a fresh-grocery and local-fulfillment platform in the wiki's ecommerce branch.
- It is contrasted with Missfresh as a front-warehouse platform whose later path was more durable in the source account.
- No.218 adds Dingdong Xiaoqu as its broad community O2O predecessor.
- Its pivot shows how narrowing from all-in-one services to a specific repeat demand can make a platform more coherent.
- Its later Meituan link keeps it connected to [[InstantRetail]] and local-services consolidation.
