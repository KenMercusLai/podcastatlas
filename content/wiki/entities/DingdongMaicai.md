# 叮咚买菜 / Dingdong Maicai

## Overview
叮咚买菜 / Dingdong Maicai is a Chinese fresh-grocery platform that narrowed from a broad community O2O predecessor into a front-warehouse model for fast local delivery.

## Current Profile
The platform illustrates both strategic narrowing and the durable demand for speed. [[DingdongXiaoqu|叮咚小区]] attempted to bundle many neighborhood services before shifting toward concrete grocery demand. Dingdong Maicai then used inventory held in neighborhood front warehouses to support roughly 30-minute delivery, a service tier distinct from community buying's next-day pickup.

This sharper use case does not remove grocery economics. Warehouses must be dense enough to cover rent, stock, labor, picking, riders, cold chain, shrinkage, and refunds. The 2021 episode reports Shanghai profitability and expansion pressure from community buying, but its broader judgment is coexistence: some users will pay for immediacy while others prefer a cheaper scheduled model.

## Key Characteristics
- Front-warehouse fresh-grocery model built around fast home delivery.
- Narrower and more legible demand than the earlier all-in-one Dingdong Xiaoqu proposition.
- Distinct from community group buying in inventory timing, delivery speed, and last-mile cost.
- Profitability depends on warehouse density, local demand, inventory control, and willingness to pay for speed.
- Remains tied to source-scoped later consolidation or Meituan reporting.

## Qualifications
Revenue, profitability, service-radius, transaction, sale, and current corporate-status claims are source-scoped. The June 2021 market account is useful for model comparison but is not current evidence. The sources do not establish how many users will sustainably pay the full cost of speed.

## What Changed
