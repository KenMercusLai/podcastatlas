## Overview
Binance is the global crypto exchange founded by [[ChangpengZhao|Changpeng Zhao / CZ]] in 2017 from an existing exchange-software system and financed through the [[BNB]] ICO.

## Current Profile
The bounded sources now show Binance as both a fast product and a regulatory-boundary failure case. Its launch was not a clean-sheet invention: CZ's team already sold exchange software, then reused that system when Chinese securities clients were shut down. An ICO and crypto-to-crypto design enabled a rapid launch, low-latency execution differentiated the service, and international users helped it survive China's September 2017 ban. Scale then expanded the regulatory and enforcement surface. CZ's own account accepts registration and KYC/AML weaknesses, describes the later [[BinanceUS|Binance.US]] separation, and treats earlier exclusion of U.S. users as the central hindsight lesson.

## Key Characteristics
- Binance was built from accumulated exchange infrastructure rather than from a single spontaneous startup idea.
- The BNB ICO financed launch without selling equity and paired fundraising with trading-fee utility.
- Low-latency order handling and international demand were central to early product differentiation and survival.
- Daily active users, rather than trading volume or revenue, were described as the main operating metric.
- Global scale created a difficult compliance surface spanning registration, KYC/AML, law-enforcement cooperation, and national boundaries.
- Binance's history includes founder pressure, China relocation, hacks, withdrawal pauses, U.S. settlement, leadership transition, and a prior investment in FTX.

## Evidence
- Infrastructure and launch: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-czs-untold-story-the-rise-fall-and-redemption-of-binances-founder-40056770]] connects Binance to CZ's low-latency trading career, a roughly thirty-client exchange-software business, the June 2017 ICO decision, and existing system reuse.
- Token and product advantage: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-czs-untold-story-the-rise-fall-and-redemption-of-binances-founder-40056770]] attributes roughly $15 million of financing, a 50% BNB fee discount, and visibly faster order placement to the launch model.
- China survival and international shape: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-czs-untold-story-the-rise-fall-and-redemption-of-binances-founder-40056770]] says Binance already had roughly 70% of users outside China when the September 2017 ban forced it to cut off Chinese users and move the team to Tokyo.
- Regulation and pressure: [[kangya-yingbian-yu-zhongsheng-dongli-cz-de-renshi-he-tianming-1736950d-420c-4021-9a56-a180810612d7]] records the hindsight case for avoiding U.S. users and engaging regulators earlier; the All-In source adds law-enforcement contact, Binance.US, DOJ negotiation, and admitted registration and KYC/AML weaknesses.
- Metrics and industry relationships: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-czs-untold-story-the-rise-fall-and-redemption-of-binances-founder-40056770]] names daily active users as the north-star metric and describes Binance's investment in and 2021 exit from [[FTX]].

## Qualifications
Both sources are participant retrospectives from CZ. They do not provide an independent corporate, legal, regulatory, employee, customer, or counterparty history. Revenue, user-share, ICO, token-allocation, performance, compliance, FTX, and enforcement claims should remain attributed unless corroborated by other evidence.
