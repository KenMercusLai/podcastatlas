## Overview
Binance is the crypto exchange associated with [[ChangpengZhao|Changpeng Zhao / CZ]] in the Fuyou Tiandi source.

## Current Profile
The current wiki evidence sees Binance through CZ's retrospective rather than through an independent corporate history. In that account, Binance's defining tension is scale under regulation: the company could move quickly on financial products, but a global exchange also had to face regulator expectations across many countries and especially difficult jurisdictional boundaries around U.S. users. The same source uses Binance's early hacks, trading continuity, and U.S. legal uncertainty to show how a crypto exchange can become a founder-pressure environment as much as a product company.

## Key Characteristics
- Binance is presented as a global crypto exchange whose regulatory surface extended across many national authorities.
- CZ's hindsight treats U.S. user exposure as a boundary that should have been avoided from the first day.
- Product speed and regulatory caution are not framed as opposites; CZ says some financial products could have launched earlier while certain regions should have been blocked more clearly.
- The company becomes a case for founder pressure because exchange hacks, withdrawal pauses, legal uncertainty, and regulator demands concentrated on leadership.
- Binance anchors CZ's shift from exchange-building toward broader blockchain judgment and later education work.

## Evidence
- Regulatory surface and U.S. boundary: [[kangya-yingbian-yu-zhongsheng-dongli-cz-de-renshi-he-tianming-1736950d-420c-4021-9a56-a180810612d7]] records CZ saying a restart would avoid U.S. users from day one and communicate more with regulators because Binance had to face more than one hundred national contexts.
- Product and speed tension: [[kangya-yingbian-yu-zhongsheng-dongli-cz-de-renshi-he-tianming-1736950d-420c-4021-9a56-a180810612d7]] says CZ would have moved faster on some products, including contracts and other financial products, while blocking some regions more clearly.
- Pressure environment: [[kangya-yingbian-yu-zhongsheng-dongli-cz-de-renshi-he-tianming-1736950d-420c-4021-9a56-a180810612d7]] cites the 2019 hack, a withdrawal pause with trading continuing, and later U.S. legal uncertainty as episodes that made the pressure hard to capture in writing.
- Post-Binance trajectory: [[kangya-yingbian-yu-zhongsheng-dongli-cz-de-renshi-he-tianming-1736950d-420c-4021-9a56-a180810612d7]] links CZ's continuing work to blockchain industry learning and a free education platform rather than only exchange operations.
