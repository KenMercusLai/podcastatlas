## Overview

小燕子 / Xiaoyanzi is a central protagonist of [[HuanzhuGege|《还珠格格》]], represented here as an improvisational, grass-roots outsider whose mistaken princess identity brings both danger and comic freedom into the Qing court.

## Current Profile

The bounded episode rejects a greed-only explanation for Xiaoyanzi's identity substitution. She reaches the palace while injured, wakes inside a coercive hierarchy, faces imperial expectation and hostile questioning, and lacks a safe route to explain the mistake. Her rebellious temperament and ability to build a story from limited information then help her continue it, while [[QianlongEmperor|乾隆]]'s guilt and hope lead him to supply explanations for gaps.

Her importance is not reducible to clumsiness or jokes. She is observant about people she knows, intensely loyal, quick under pressure, and capable of making hierarchy appear absurd. Her ordinary language, opposition to kneeling, and willingness to seat servants with masters embody [[LaughterAgainstAuthority]] through conduct.

Her limitations remain consequential. Improvisation creates danger for [[XiaZiwei|紫薇]], the Fu household, and imperial consort Ling, and her own guilt makes her assume Ziwei must hate her. The episode therefore treats sincerity and structural disruption together rather than using good intention to erase risk.

## Key Characteristics

- Grass-roots outsider whose vitality contrasts with palace ritual.
- Fast improviser able to use limited facts under hostile questioning.
- Loyal friend whose intimate knowledge helps her read Liu Qing and reconnect with Ziwei.
- Comic agent who punctures solemn authority through speech and everyday equality.
- Emotionally inexperienced protagonist whose love and guilt become clear later than her actions.

## Qualifications

This profile reflects one retrospective on the older version and is not a complete cross-version character history. Claims about inner motive, unconscious attraction, acting uniqueness, authorial intention, anti-feudal politics, and the relative quality of other performers remain interpretive. Explaining the coercive context of the identity deception does not erase its danger to Ziwei and others.

## What Changed

- Established coercive context and Qianlong's motivated belief as parts of the mistaken identity.
- Distinguished her comic force from mere foolishness.
- Preserved loyalty and sincerity alongside the risks created by improvisation.
