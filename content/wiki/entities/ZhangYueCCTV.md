# 张越 / Zhang Yue (television host)

## Overview
张越 / Zhang Yue is the television host associated in this source with the earlier 《半边天》 interview that brought [[LiuXiaoyang|刘晓漾]] and her refusal of numbness to public attention.

## Current Profile
The episode portrays Zhang as both an enabler of encounter and a cautious adviser. She invited Liu to Beijing and helped expose her to a wider cultural world, but reportedly discouraged her from trying to remain there without the economic base and work skills needed to withstand metropolitan life. This makes her role more complex than either rescuer or gatekeeper: public visibility opened possibility while also contributing to expectations Liu later felt obliged to satisfy.

## Key Characteristics
- Television interviewer who made an otherwise private dissatisfaction publicly legible.
- Connector who enabled Liu Xiaoyang to encounter Beijing and a wider audience.
- Cautious adviser attentive to urban survival costs and Liu’s sensitivity.
- Participant in a relationship later burdened by public expectation and gratitude.

- Access and warning: [[ep179-womende-neixin-dou-zhuzhe-yige-liuxiaoyang-zuoziji-shi-yao-fuchu-juda-daijia-ckwriaset1ykabaaaadc008h]] says Zhang invited Liu to Beijing but warned that city life without a livelihood could injure rather than liberate her.
- Expectation effect: [[ep179-womende-neixin-dou-zhuzhe-yige-liuxiaoyang-zuoziji-shi-yao-fuchu-juda-daijia-ckwriaset1ykabaaaadc008h]] reports Liu’s concern about failing Zhang and the public.

## Qualifications
The episode summarizes Zhang’s role through a later magazine profile and host commentary. It does not provide the complete original interview, Zhang’s full reasoning, or a comprehensive account of their later relationship.

## What Changed
- Established Zhang Yue’s dual role as opportunity-maker and material-reality adviser in Liu Xiaoyang’s public story.
