## Overview

皇甫规 / Huangfu Gui is a late-Han frontier official whose current wiki profile has three source-bounded layers. He first appears as a public-duty recommender who puts [[ZhangHuanLateHan|张奂]] forward for a command he has been summoned to hold, later tries unsuccessfully to exit another frontier appointment, and then treats Party Prohibition punishment as a missing moral credential.

## Current Profile

Huangfu Gui's earliest current layer is not simple command ambition. In the 163 CE frontier crisis, Huan's court recalls him toward the Duliao general role, but he argues that Zhang Huan is the stronger large-army commander and offers to serve below him. The court accepts that recommendation, making Zhang Huan Duliao general and Huangfu Gui zhonglangjiang. The same source says Zhang had been barred after the Liang Ji case and that Huangfu Gui defended him seven times, so the recommendation is part of a longer talent-rescue pattern rather than a one-off courtesy.

The later office-exit layer shows the opposite pressure. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-870-gudai-tizhinei-buganle-meiyou-tuilu-lpwbniu9ifpt6cp4ip9czqwsh5xd|Hanji 870]], Huangfu Gui receives a northern frontier office he does not want, submits repeated memorials claiming illness, and still cannot escape the assignment. His profile therefore marks a system where an official can yield rank for competence in one setting yet fail to control his own exit in another.

The Party Prohibition layer turns punishment into moral evidence. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-875-li-dapu-weihe-ta-yi-ruyu-wei-rong-lgffkjk-0yu-ehfsw59anoych-ld|Hanji 875]], Huangfu Gui narrowly avoids proscription through eunuch kinship and complains that missing the shared calamity leaves him ashamed beside fellow gentlemen who suffer for principle. His current profile combines talent-centered recommendation, constrained office duty, and the moral weight attached to persecution.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Han frontier official associated with Duliao general-level military service.
- Repeatedly defends Zhang Huan after the source-described Liang Ji case punishment and helps return him to usable office.
- Recommends Zhang Huan as the better commander and accepts a subordinate command role during a frontier emergency.
- Attempts to avoid a later unwanted frontier posting through illness memorials but cannot secure exit.
- Treats escape from Party Prohibition as a source of shame because it separates him from persecuted upright officials.
- Bridges [[ScholarOfficialPublicDuty|scholar-official public duty]], [[CareerExitFriction|office-exit friction]], and [[PunishmentAsMoralCredential|punishment-as-moral-credential]].
