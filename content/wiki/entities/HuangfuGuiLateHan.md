## Overview
皇甫规 / Huangfu Gui is a late-Han frontier and local-order official whose profile now begins with Taishan bandit pacification and then develops into root-cause Qiang policy, army morale repair, reward-gatekeeping conflict, talent recommendation, constrained office exit, and Party Prohibition shame. He is the wiki's strongest Huan-era example of an official whose effectiveness depends on diagnosis, credibility, and refusal to turn public office into a private reward channel.

## Current Profile
In the earliest local-order layer, [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-854-lishi-gaosu-ni-xuanguan-yongguan-hen-zhongyao-lhin-iwk6y1xr8vvzdmnjch3s2um|Hanji 854]] places Huangfu Gui after Shusun Wuji's Taishan bandit crisis and Zong Zi's military suppression: the court appoints him to Taishan, where he uses broad administrative strategy and pacifies the remaining disorder. This does not replace the later Qiang-frontier profile; it makes his competence visible before the more famous frontier memorials.

Across [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-855-ni-you-duo-wuqing-jiu-you-duo-qiangda-lulbpnufi8xfijrviufo6biuy-h1|Hanji 855]], [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-856-lishi-gaosu-ni-ren-ruo-xinluan-zhenjiao-zi-luan-lo7m-9mz-pvstxtfgavrne4bszks|Hanji 856]], and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-857-xuehui-gongying-cai-shi-zhenzheng-de-yingjia-lh3zx6pw-4ib7owiz8c68bv-9htn|Hanji 857]], Huangfu Gui is more than a battlefield official. He identifies official abuse as a driver of Qiang resistance, restores a diseased army's confidence, impeaches harmful subordinates, induces surrender, and then refuses eunuch-linked gift expectations after success. Later sources extend the same pattern into recommending Zhang Huan, trying unsuccessfully to exit office, and accepting shame during the Party Prohibition era.

## Key Characteristics
- Pacification official whose bounded record now starts with Taishan local disorder, not only Qiang frontier policy.
- Diagnoses causes of unrest before applying force, especially when official misconduct creates rebellion incentives.
- Repairs morale and order through credibility, discipline, and calm rather than theatrical coercion.
- Resists reward channels controlled by palace intermediaries, even when that refusal exposes him to slander.
- Treats office as a public burden he cannot easily escape when his capacity is still needed.
- Uses recommendation and self-limitation as governance tools, especially in the Zhang Huan and Party Prohibition layers.

## Evidence
- Taishan pacification: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-854-lishi-gaosu-ni-xuanguan-yongguan-hen-zhongyao-lhin-iwk6y1xr8vvzdmnjch3s2um|Hanji 854]] appoints Huangfu Gui after Shusun Wuji's crisis and credits him with using strategy to pacify the remaining bandits.
- Root-cause frontier diagnosis: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-855-ni-you-duo-wuqing-jiu-you-duo-qiangda-lulbpnufi8xfijrviufo6biuy-h1|Hanji 855]] frames Huangfu Gui's Qiang analysis as a move away from simple blame toward administrative causes.
- Morale repair and surrender: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-856-lishi-gaosu-ni-ren-ruo-xinluan-zhenjiao-zi-luan-lo7m-9mz-pvstxtfgavrne4bszks|Hanji 856]] shows him reviving a sickened army, removing abusive officials, and producing surrender through credible repair.
- Reward-gatekeeping refusal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-857-xuehui-gongying-cai-shi-zhenzheng-de-yingjia-lh3zx6pw-4ib7owiz8c68bv-9htn|Hanji 857]] puts Huangfu Gui against Xu Huang and Zuo Guan's gift expectations, showing the cost of refusing palace-mediated reward channels.
- Capacity recommendation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-859-gudai-haoren-weishenme-nan-dang-haoguan-llkyx18ssvserxncvrtkw7xfduad|Hanji 859]] has Huangfu Gui recommend Zhang Huan, making talent placement part of his public function.
- Constrained exit and shame: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-870-gudai-tizhinei-buganle-meiyou-tuilu-lpwbniu9ifpt6cp4ip9czqwsh5xd|Hanji 870]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-875-li-dapu-weihe-ta-yi-ruyu-wei-rong-lgffkjk-0yu-ehfsw59anoych-ld|Hanji 875]] extend him from field competence into the problem of leaving office and surviving reputationally under Party Prohibition.

## Qualifications
The current profile is strongest where the source notes directly narrate crisis response. It does not yet provide a full institutional account of Taishan administration after pacification, nor does it settle how much later shame reflects personal compromise versus coercive political environment. ASR variants such as 黄福贵 and 黄府规 are normalized to 皇甫规 inside this source-bounded route.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 854 as Huangfu Gui's earliest bounded action layer, before the Qiang-frontier memorial sequence.
- Reframed Taishan pacification as a local counterpart to his later root-cause frontier method.
- Connected Huangfu Gui more directly to appointment quality: the episode presents him as the right person moved into the right disorder field.
