## Overview

汉冲帝 / Emperor Chong of Han enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-825-lishi-gaosu-ni-dang-ni-meiyong-shi-renxing-zui-zisi-luv-8z9axuejnfhlkgsuxee3uked|Hanji 825]] as the child emperor whose death in 145 CE opens a high-risk succession under [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan|梁妠 / 梁太后]] and [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]]. His political importance in this source is mostly posthumous: the court must decide whether to announce the death immediately, whom to install next, and how costly the burial should be during war and unrest.

## Current Profile

Hanji 825 says Emperor Chong dies at Yutang Front Hall in the first month of 145 CE. Because Xu-Yang unrest and incoming royal candidates make the moment unstable, Liang Na first considers delaying the death announcement until the summoned princes and royal sons reach Luoyang.

[[LiGuLateHan|李固]] turns Chong's death into a public legitimacy problem. He argues that ministers should not jointly hide a ruler's death, recalls the concealed-death crisis after [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] and the Beixiang Hou precedent, and persuades Liang Na to announce Chong's death that night.

Chong's burial also becomes a fiscal and ritual issue. Li Gu warns that large construction would add taxes and labor while banditry and military spending are already heavy, so he recommends a modest tomb arrangement inside [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]]'s Xianling precinct.

## Key Characteristics

- Child emperor whose death creates a succession emergency under dowager and outer-relative control.
- Posthumous trigger for a public-announcement dispute over whether the court may conceal a ruler's death for transition management.
- Fiscal and ritual reference point in Li Gu's argument for restrained burial.
- Predecessor whose death leads directly to Liu Zuan's accession as [[EmperorZhiOfHan|汉质帝]] rather than [[LiuSuanLateHan|刘蒜]].

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 825 and does not reconstruct Emperor Chong's full reign, family background, age, illness, or burial archaeology. The source uses him mainly as the dead ruler around whom announcement timing, burial cost, and successor selection are contested.

## What Changed

- Created Emperor Chong as the 145 CE death and succession trigger before the Emperor Zhi and Liang Ji sequence.

## Relationships

- [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan|梁妠 / 梁太后]] - regent who initially considers delaying Chong's death announcement and then decides the succession.
- [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] - outer-relative powerholder who participates in selecting Chong's successor.
- [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] - minister who rejects concealed death, urges an older successor, and argues for restrained burial.
- [[EmperorZhiOfHan|汉质帝]] - successor installed after Chong's death.
- [[LiuSuanLateHan|刘蒜]] - alternative candidate rejected after Chong's death.
- [[ImperialDeathConcealment|秘不发丧式过渡控制]] - concept clarified by Li Gu's opposition to hiding Chong's death.
- [[ControllableChildAccession|可控幼主继位]] - succession pattern visible when the eight-year-old Liu Zuan is chosen.
