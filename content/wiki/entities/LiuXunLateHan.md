# 刘勋 / Liu Xun (late Han)

刘勋 / Liu Xun (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] as the person [[YuanShu|袁术]] appoints to [[LujiangCommandery|庐江郡]] after [[SunCe|孙策]] defeats [[LuKangLateHan|陆康]]. Yuan Shu had promised Sun Ce the commandery if he won, so Liu Xun's appointment becomes the second and sharper promise reversal after the [[JiujiangCommandery|九江郡]] case.

The source does not develop Liu Xun's later career. His wiki role is to mark how a formal appointment can be legal within Yuan Shu's camp and still destroy the subordinate trust needed to keep Sun Ce attached.
