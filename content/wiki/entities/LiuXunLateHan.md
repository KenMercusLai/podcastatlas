# 刘勋 / Liu Xun (late Han)

## Overview
刘勋 / Liu Xun is the late-Han Lujiang holder whose appointment first marks [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s broken promise to [[SunCe|孙策]] and whose later defeat shows Sun Ce turning that exposed office into a campaign prize.

## Current Profile
The current synthesis presents Liu Xun as a beneficiary of Yuan Shu's patronage who lacks the strategic judgment needed to hold a strong base after Yuan Shu's collapse. He receives [[LujiangCommandery|庐江郡]] in place of Sun Ce, ignores [[LiuYeLateHan|刘晔 / 刘业]] when Sun Ce's gifts and humility should look suspicious, leaves his rear open for the Shangliao grain expedition, loses [[WanChengLujiang|皖城]], fails to recover through [[HuangZu|黄祖]], and finally flees north to [[CaoCao|曹操]].

## Key Characteristics
- Embodies Yuan Shu's reward-credit failure because he receives Lujiang after Sun Ce had been promised it.
- Holds a position strong enough that Sun Ce initially avoids direct frontal assault.
- Misreads Sun Ce's gift-and-deference performance as opportunity rather than bait.
- Rejects Liu Ye's accurate warning about Shangliao difficulty and rear-base exposure.
- Loses his base, family members, people, and ships once he leaves Lujiang.
- Becomes dependent on outside rescue after defeat, first through Huang Zu and then through flight to Cao Cao.

## Evidence
- Broken-promise appointment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] says Yuan Shu promised Lujiang to Sun Ce but appointed Liu Xun after Sun Ce defeated Lu Kang.
- Strong-position problem: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1038-sanshiliu-ji-zhi-diaohu-lishan-sunce-zhiqu-wancheng-lhomk8hfdidl61degwbqtj2xkj9z|Hanji 1038]] frames Liu Xun's Lujiang as a difficult target that Sun Ce prefers to empty through deception.
- Ignored warning: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1038-sanshiliu-ji-zhi-diaohu-lishan-sunce-zhiqu-wancheng-lhomk8hfdidl61degwbqtj2xkj9z|Hanji 1038]] has Liu Ye warn that Shangliao is hard and that Sun Ce will attack the rear if Liu Xun marches out.
- Collapse after displacement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1038-sanshiliu-ji-zhi-diaohu-lishan-sunce-zhiqu-wancheng-lhomk8hfdidl61degwbqtj2xkj9z|Hanji 1038]] reports Liu Xun finding Shangliao empty, losing Wan City, being defeated near Pengze, failing again through Huang Zu's aid, and fleeing to Cao Cao.

## Qualifications
The page is bounded to Liu Xun's role in Yuan Shu's reward politics and Sun Ce's Lujiang campaign. It does not attempt to reconstruct his full later career under Cao Cao.
