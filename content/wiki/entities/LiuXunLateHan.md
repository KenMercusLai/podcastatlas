# 刘勋 / Liu Xun (late Han)

## Overview
刘勋 / Liu Xun is the late-Han Lujiang holder whose appointment marks Yuan Shu's broken promise to Sun Ce, whose later food pressure explains the Shangliao lure, and whose defeat shows Sun Ce turning Lujiang into a campaign prize.

## Current Profile
The current synthesis presents Liu Xun as a beneficiary of [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s patronage who gains a strong base but lacks the strategic and logistical judgment needed to hold it after Yuan Shu's collapse. Hanji 1037 adds the missing pressure layer: Liu Xun receives [[ZhengBaoLateHan|郑宝]]'s thousands from [[LiuYeLateHan|刘晔]] and then shelters [[YuanYinLateHan|袁胤]] with Yuan Shu's remnants and dependents, draining food. Hanji 1038 then shows Sun Ce exploiting that pressure with the Shangliao grain lure, while Liu Xun ignores Liu Ye's warning, leaves his rear open, loses [[WanChengLujiang|皖城]], and flees north to [[CaoCao|曹操]].

## Key Characteristics
- Embodies Yuan Shu's reward-credit failure because he receives Lujiang after Sun Ce had been promised it.
- Holds a base strong enough that Sun Ce initially avoids direct frontal assault.
- Accepts armed and dependent groups whose food and discipline costs strain his position.
- Faces grain pressure after absorbing Zheng Bao's followers and Yuan Yin's refugee/remnant group.
- Misreads Sun Ce's gift-and-deference performance as opportunity rather than bait.
- Rejects Liu Ye's accurate warning about Shangliao difficulty and rear-base exposure.
- Becomes dependent on outside rescue after defeat, first through Huang Zu and then through flight to Cao Cao.

- Strong-position problem: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1038-sanshiliu-ji-zhi-diaohu-lishan-sunce-zhiqu-wancheng-lhomk8hfdidl61degwbqtj2xkj9z|Hanji 1038]] frames Liu Xun's Lujiang as a difficult target that Sun Ce prefers to empty through deception.
- Ignored warning: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1038-sanshiliu-ji-zhi-diaohu-lishan-sunce-zhiqu-wancheng-lhomk8hfdidl61degwbqtj2xkj9z|Hanji 1038]] has Liu Ye warn that Shangliao is hard and that Sun Ce will attack the rear if Liu Xun marches out.
- Collapse after displacement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1038-sanshiliu-ji-zhi-diaohu-lishan-sunce-zhiqu-wancheng-lhomk8hfdidl61degwbqtj2xkj9z|Hanji 1038]] reports Liu Xun finding Shangliao empty, losing Wan City, being defeated near Pengze, failing again through Huang Zu's aid, and fleeing to Cao Cao.

## Qualifications
The page is bounded to Liu Xun's role in Yuan Shu's reward politics, the Zheng Bao/Yuan Yin logistics pressure, and Sun Ce's Lujiang campaign. It does not attempt to reconstruct his full later career under Cao Cao.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1037 as the logistics prelude explaining why extra followers and dependents make Liu Xun's grain problem acute before Hanji 1038.
- Reframed Liu Xun's Shangliao mistake as both strategic misreading and resource-pressure response.
