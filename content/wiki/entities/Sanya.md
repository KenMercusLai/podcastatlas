# Sanya / 三亚

## Overview

Sanya / 三亚 is the resort-oriented Hainan city that most strongly shapes external images of the island in the current source.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Sanya as a contrast with [[Haikou|海口]] and with less-developed central and western Hainan. It anchors the mature east-coast visitor route and the beach, hotel, and seafront-property image often generalized to the whole province. The source does not reject that appeal, but treats it as one part of a larger island whose residents, settlements, and development conditions are more varied.

## Key Characteristics

- Major resort city anchoring Hainan's external holiday image.
- Southern endpoint of the province's mature eastern tourism corridor.
- Source of a beach-and-seafront-property image that does not represent every Hainan resident.
- Example of visitor concentration alongside less-visited central and western areas.

## Qualifications

This is a source-bounded contrastive profile, not a full history or current assessment of Sanya's economy, residents, environment, property market, or tourism industry. The episode discusses the city mainly to explain how Hainan is perceived from outside.

## What Changed

- Created a bounded profile of Sanya as the strongest resort-facing part of Hainan's public image.
