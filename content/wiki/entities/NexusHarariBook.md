# Nexus (Yuval Noah Harari Book)

## Overview
Nexus is [[YuvalNoahHarari]]'s book discussed in [[an-interview-with-yuval-noah-harari-6a872b94c71d02aaba56ca7c]]. The episode frames it as a history of information networks from the Stone Age to artificial intelligence, used to interpret AI as a new form of social coordination and institutional power.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Nexus matters less as a standalone bibliography item than as the frame Harari brings into the interview: information systems make large-scale cooperation possible, but they also mediate trust, authority, error correction, and coercion. The book context anchors the episode's claims that AI risk is not only model intelligence; it is also the migration of trust, persuasion, legal authority, and geopolitical dependence into machine-run networks.

## Key Characteristics
- Nexus supplies the information-network lens behind Harari's AI governance warnings.
- The book is presented as spanning long-run human history through the AI era.
- The interview uses the book frame to connect technology, money, trust, politics, and empire.
- Nexus is not summarized as a technical AI-safety manual; it is invoked as historical and civilizational interpretation.
