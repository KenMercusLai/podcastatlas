## Overview

公乘兴 / Gongcheng Xing enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-511-taihou-tianhuaban-ta-ruhe-yitian-feng-wuge-didi-wei-hou-lhoz_ubecvc8-mgfoad2pya72uht|Hanji 511]] as the memorialist who defends [[WangZunWesternHan|王尊]] after [[ZhangZhongWesternHan|张忠]]'s impeachment removes him from Jingzhao office.

## Current Profile

Gongcheng Xing's importance is procedural. He does not simply praise Wang Zun; he argues that the accusation should be tested because [[YangFuWesternHan|杨辅]], who supplied the impeachment material, had a private grievance against Wang Zun. His memorial asks the court to review Wang Zun's ordinary conduct and then punish either Wang Zun or the false accusers according to the result.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han memorialist whose surname is explained in the episode as the compound surname 公乘.
- Defends Wang Zun by naming both his prior public order achievements and the possibility of private revenge.
- Frames the case as one requiring public review rather than one-sided factional judgment.
