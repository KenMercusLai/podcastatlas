# 阿福 / Ant Afu

## Overview
阿福 / Ant Afu is the Ant health-agent product discussed in [[273-guangwan-waitan-dahui-faxian-mayi-zhaodaole-xin-weizhi-lotxogfoqqwcjigxihtixvncbfc3]].

## Current Profile
The source treats 阿福 as more strategic than a medical Q&A bot. Its possible value comes from [[Alipay]]'s health-related assets: insurance-code access, appointment booking, healthcare digitization, [[Haodf|好大夫在线]], village-doctor assistance, and the potential to maintain longitudinal health records.

## Key Characteristics
- Targets health as a high-trust, high-frequency, high-importance intent domain.
- Depends on medical-service fulfillment, data continuity, and user trust rather than only model answers.
- Can support primary-care or village-doctor scenarios through triage and assistance.
- Needs users to contribute health data such as lab reports, wearables, body metrics, and medical documents.
- Connects personal health records with agentic service orchestration.
