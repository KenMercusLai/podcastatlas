# 阿福 / Ant Afu

## Overview
阿福 / Ant Afu is an Ant health-agent product discussed as both a strategic healthcare entrance and an older patient's everyday support tool.

## Current Profile
The strategy source treats 阿福 as more than a medical Q&A bot. Its possible value comes from [[Alipay]]'s health-related assets: insurance-code access, appointment booking, healthcare digitization, [[Haodf|好大夫在线]], village-doctor assistance, and the potential to maintain longitudinal health records.

[[vol-196-nianhou-kaigong-wo-zai-dachengshi-gaoqian-shui-lai-zuo-bamade-jiankang-peihu-lqwfbvcy2xj2ydeenegmhaarn4|VOL.196]] supplies a household-use case through [[BaoJieZheBing|宝姐]]. Voice input, large and emphasized text, audio playback, visible insurance information, remembered context, and patient follow-up make the product easier for her to use after surgery. The same source keeps the boundary explicit: it helps explain and organize, while doctors, examinations, and emergency services retain clinical authority.

## Key Characteristics
- Targets health as a high-trust, high-frequency, high-importance intent domain.
- Depends on medical-service fulfillment, data continuity, and user trust rather than only model answers.
- Can support primary-care or village-doctor scenarios through triage and assistance.
- Uses voice, visible emphasis, audio, and conversational follow-up as older-user access features in the supplied household case.
- Needs users to contribute health data such as reports, wearables, body metrics, and medical documents.
- Connects personal health records with agentic service orchestration.

- Data evidence: [[273-guangwan-waitan-dahui-faxian-mayi-zhaodaole-xin-weizhi-lotxogfoqqwcjigxihtixvncbfc3]] says 阿福's defensibility depends on continuous, trusted, callable personal health files.
- Older-user evidence: [[vol-196-nianhou-kaigong-wo-zai-dachengshi-gaoqian-shui-lai-zuo-bamade-jiankang-peihu-lqwfbvcy2xj2ydeenegmhaarn4]] describes Baojie using voice and follow-up questions for postoperative diet, movement, sleep, symptoms, reports, and risk explanations.

## Qualifications
The sources are product discussion and one favorable family experience, not independent medical or usability evaluation. They do not establish diagnostic accuracy, outcome improvement, privacy practice, regulatory scope, liability allocation, accessibility for older adults with greater impairment, or whether users will entrust enough data to make the product durable.

## What Changed
- Added an older patient's postoperative and report-reading use case.
- Sharpened the product boundary between explanation and clinical authority.
