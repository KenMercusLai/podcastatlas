# Union Square

Union Square is the flagship [[Greenmarket]] location in [[a-firm-andy-what-are-new-british-pms-plans-6a5deeca76f8ee25bb4bbf9c]]. [[AnnieCrabill]] describes it as becoming an open-air market four times a week, drawing older residents, lawyers, [[NYU]] students, chefs, actors, artists, and ordinary shoppers.

The episode presents Union Square as a public-space transformation case. A rundown 1970s area becomes a destination partly because market activity brings people, vendors, restaurants, and routine civic life into the same place.
