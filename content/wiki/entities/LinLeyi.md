## Overview

林乐怡 / Lin Leyi was [[JinYong|金庸]]'s long-term partner and third wife, represented through a relationship that reportedly began when she was sixteen and he was married and twenty-six years older.

## Current Profile

The episode treats a bar or restaurant where Lin was working as the most stable element of the couple's first-meeting story. Versions involving a returned tip, bread, ham steak, dialogue, and a later watch are too variable and polished to function as a reliable transcript. These stories also perform reputational work by presenting Lin as compassionate rather than money-seeking and Jin Yong as responding to care rather than youth alone.

Their relationship reportedly began around 1966 and continued for roughly a decade before Jin Yong and [[ZhuMei|朱玫]] divorced. The episode assigns primary responsibility for the marital breach to Jin Yong while preserving disagreement about Lin's responsibility and emphasizing that her reported age, Jin Yong's marital status, and the large power asymmetry cannot be reduced to a neutral age-gap preference.

Lin and Jin Yong remained together for more than four decades until his death in 2018. Recollections describe her as private and as having good relationships with his daughters, but motives such as guilt, compensation, or family preservation remain inference. Her preference for [[WhiteHorseNeighsInWesternWind|《白马啸西风》]] and perceived resemblance to fictional women do not establish direct autobiographical coding.

## Key Characteristics

- Third wife and partner of Jin Yong for more than four decades.
- Entered the relationship while Jin Yong was married to Zhu Mei.
- Was reportedly sixteen at the relationship's beginning, making age and power central qualifications.
- Maintained a comparatively private public profile.
- Became the subject of romanticized first-meeting stories and an unverified sterilization rumor.
