# 耿武 / Geng Wu (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] introduces Geng Wu as one of [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]]'s subordinates who argues against yielding [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] to [[YuanShao|袁绍]]. He reads the material balance differently from Han Fu: Jizhou has troops and long grain supply, while Yuan Shao is hungry, scattered, and dependent.

Geng Wu's warning becomes a negative-feedback case. The advice is not absent; Han Fu hears it and rejects it because fear and self-abasement have already made surrender look like virtue. When Yuan Shao enters [[YeLateHan|邺城]], Geng Wu and [[MinChunLateHan|闵纯]] attempt armed resistance and are killed.
