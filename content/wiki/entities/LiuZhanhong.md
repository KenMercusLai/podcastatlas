# 柳展鸿 Andy

## Overview
柳展鸿, known as Andy, is a beverage-development consultant and the first Chinese global champion represented here for the [[WorldCoffeeInGoodSpiritsChampionship|World Coffee in Good Spirits Championship]].

## Current Profile
Andy joins competition storytelling to commercial product discipline. His Saturn-themed drinks use cosmic imagery to express personal commitment, while his consulting work turns ideas for tea, coffee, and restaurant brands into proposals that can survive cost, safety, supply, and service constraints. He treats championship visibility as a platform for sharing possibilities rather than proof that one formula should govern every creator.

## Key Characteristics
- Builds drinks from a central theme before selecting materials, language, and presentation.
- Treats coffee, spirits, and other ingredients as coequal contributors requiring balance.
- Designs for aroma, texture, finish, visuals, atmosphere, and memory as well as taste.
- Brings commercial feasibility and stable output into creative product development.
- Uses team advice while retaining final responsibility for the work's theme and values.
