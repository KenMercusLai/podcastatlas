# Spiro Agnew

## Overview

Spiro Agnew appears as Maryland governor and [[RichardNixon]]'s 1968 vice-presidential running mate.

## Current Profile

The episode interprets Agnew's selection as coalition signaling. His harsh criticism of Black Baltimore leaders after the 1968 riots gave him law-and-order standing, while his border-state position helped Nixon address voters between the Deep South and the suburban or Northern Republican electorate.

## Key Characteristics

- Maryland governor selected as Nixon's running mate.
- Rose to national attention through his response to the Baltimore riots.
- Appealed to border-state and law-and-order voters.
- Helped embody the campaign's appeal to patriotic, churchgoing middle America.
