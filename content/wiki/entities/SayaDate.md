# Saya Date

Saya Date appears in [[currency-chaos-in-argentina-summer-school]] as a tech worker from [[India]] who traveled to [[Argentina]] for tango and became part of the first couple to represent India at the competition. The episode says that between her June arrival and the later reporting window, the peso weakened sharply against the dollar.

Date's delayed shopping becomes a small consumer example of [[CurrencyRisk]]. She waits because her dollars may buy more later, showing how exchange-rate expectations change timing decisions even for visitors making everyday purchases.
