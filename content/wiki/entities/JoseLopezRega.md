# José López Rega

## Overview

José López Rega is the Peronist household insider and social-welfare minister whom the episode connects to occultism, [[IsabelPeron|Isabel Perón]]'s rise, and right-wing political violence.

## Current Profile

The source presents López Rega's career as an access-to-power story. He attached himself to Isabel, entered [[JuanPeron|Juan Perón]]'s Madrid household, and became indispensable as Perón aged and grew ill. After the Peronist return, he gained ministerial authority and control over a large budget.

The episode associates him with funding the right-wing Argentine Anticommunist Alliance, or AAA, and with bizarre ritual stories involving [[EvaPeron|Evita]]'s body. His influence collapsed after Perón's death; he fled, was later arrested, and died before trial.

## Key Characteristics

- Household proximity was the foundation of his political power.
- Occult interests shaped his public reputation as "the warlock."
- His alliance with Isabel joined private influence to formal ministerial authority.
- The episode links him to the AAA and the escalation of right-wing violence.
- His Evita-related ritual acts remain reported allegations rather than settled evidence.
