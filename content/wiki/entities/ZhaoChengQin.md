# 赵成 / Zhao Cheng (Qin)

赵成 / Zhao Cheng (Qin) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-138-wudu-qiannian-de-lishi-zhenxiang-zhaogao-bushi-taijian-1-ljw5nzd-ap1a4hkdczxun3t0-vwy|Qinji 138]] as [[ZhaoGao|赵高]]'s younger brother. The episode says Zhao Gao had several brothers born in the same hidden-office environment, but Zhao Cheng is the only one whose name survives in the account.

The source gives Zhao Cheng a late-court role. He later takes Zhao Gao's place as 郎中令 and participates with Zhao Gao and [[YanLe|阎乐]] in the move against [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]]. This page is disambiguated from [[ZhaoChengWarringStates|赵成 / Zhao Cheng (Warring States)]], the Zhao royal elder connected to [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] and [[HufuQishe|胡服骑射]].

## Key Claims
- Zhao Cheng is Zhao Gao's brother, not the Warring States Zhao elder of the same name.
- The source places Zhao Cheng in the same hidden-office family background used to discuss [[YinguanStatusMisreading|隐官身份误读]].
- Zhao Cheng later becomes 郎中令 after Zhao Gao.
- He joins Zhao Gao and Yan Le in the palace-coup branch against Hu Hai.
