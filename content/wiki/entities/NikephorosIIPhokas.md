# Nikephoros II Phokas

## Overview

Nikephoros II Phokas was a Byzantine general-emperor whose military recovery strengthened the empire but whose court unpopularity and murder reshaped diplomacy with the Ottonians.

## Current Profile

The episode depicts Nikephoros as an ascetic, battle-hardened commander associated with the tenth-century Byzantine recovery. His victories included Crete and advances in Anatolia, but military prestige did not protect him from court hostility. [[JohnITzimiskes]] participated in his overthrow and succeeded him in 969.

Nikephoros also embodies the sensitivity of Roman titles. A western embassy seeking a bride for [[OttoII]] failed after papal language called him emperor of the Greeks rather than Romans.

## Key Characteristics

- Successful general associated with Byzantine territorial recovery.
- Ascetic and martial ruler portrayed as ill-suited to court politics.
- Defender of the empire's Roman identity against western diplomatic language.
- Murdered emperor whose succession changed marriage diplomacy.
