## Overview

陆小凤 / Lu Xiaofeng is [[GuLong|古龙]]'s investigator and social protagonist across [[GoldenRocDynastyGuLong|《金鹏王朝》]], [[EmbroideredBanditGuLong|《绣花大盗》]], and [[DuelBeforeAndAfterGuLong|《决战前后》]]. The bounded sources place his detection inside a wider social intelligence built from humor, appetite, trust, improvisation, friendship, romance, and exceptional martial skill.

## Current Profile

《金鹏王朝》 establishes Lu as an already accomplished “four-eyebrowed” hero whose drinking, leisure, wit, friendships, and martial ease make him legible before the case begins. His claimed laziness is qualified by repeated willingness to intervene. He initially refuses beauty and wealth, but enters the false restoration case when [[HuaManlou|花满楼]] appears endangered and his friend [[HuoXiuGuLong|霍休]] is implicated. Friendship rather than reward is therefore the practical trigger of his chivalry. The same source treats his dual role as detective and fighter as a series innovation while questioning whether late evidence, ignored warnings, and coincidence support the density of reversals.

In 《绣花大盗》, Lu investigates crimes attributed to a needle-wielding robber while moving among [[XueBingGuLong|薛冰]], [[GongsunDaniangGuLong|公孙大娘]], and [[JinJiulingGuLong|金九龄]]. His strongest method is not a closed chain of deduction but adaptive reading of people and situations. He lets Gongsun's trap appear to work, rejects three conspicuous escape targets, uses smell learned from Hua to identify a disguise, and turns contest into covert alliance. The hosts praise those reversals while questioning the later explanation of secret coordination and the path from clues to Jin.

The episode also gives him differentiated relationships. Everyday teasing and anxiety after Xue's disappearance ground romantic feeling; strategic parity makes Gongsun a compelling opponent and ally; Hua supplies a gentler life-affirming capacity. In the final fight, Lu's tiny embroidery needle answers Jin's enormous hammer by reversing expected weight and force. The visual and tactical design is stronger than the reported mechanics of the final kill.

In 《决战前后》, Lu becomes the humanly engaged contrast to two sword masters. He questions the conversion of combat into gambling, fame, and spectacle, tries to prevent the duel, and treats death for an invisible ranking as potentially empty. He still understands the duelists' self-testing and technical fidelity. His tiger-and-sparrow story protects 卜巨 from humiliation, but that source also questions friendships whose depth is declared more strongly than their shared history is shown.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines investigation, martial ability, appetite, humor, friendship, romance, and emotional openness.
- Enters danger for friends more readily than for money, status, or seduction.
- Reads fatigue, disguise, smell, expectation, and involuntary response as practical evidence.
- Uses feigned weakness and altered choice sets to make an opponent reveal more than direct pursuit would.
- Serves as a humanly engaged counterpoint to technical purity, ranking, gambling, and combat spectacle.
- Uses humor and attention to protect dignity, although some relationships are more asserted than enacted.
- Wins memorable contests through adaptation, while the bounded sources preserve doubts about inference and combat closure.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to three novels as discussed in three structured podcast summaries, not the full Lu Xiaofeng series or checked editions. His intelligence, confusion, romantic relationships, friendships, motives, exact investigations, dialogue, martial standing, secret communication, and final attacks remain source-scoped. “Fully formed” describes his entrance and limited development in the first case, not proof that he never changes. Criticism of asserted friendship, inference gaps, gender writing, coincidence, and combat plausibility is literary judgment rather than settled reception.

## What Changed

- Added the first case's immediately complete persona and wuxia-detective role.
- Added friendship rather than reward as the trigger for accepting the restoration case.
- Extended doubts about inference and closure to late evidence, ignored testimony, and coincidence.
