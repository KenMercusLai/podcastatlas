# West Africa

West Africa enters [[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]] as a cocaine transit region between South American production and European demand. [[OriOgunbiyi]] says geography is the most important reason the region is involved, while weaker enforcement capacity and corruption make the route more attractive to cartels.

The source frames the region through [[WestAfricaCocaineTransit]] rather than only domestic drug use. Cocaine is bulk-shipped into the region, repackaged, moved to mother vessels, and transferred at sea to fast boats heading toward Europe, making [[MaritimeDrugInterdictionCapacity]] and [[NarcoStateRisk]] central.
