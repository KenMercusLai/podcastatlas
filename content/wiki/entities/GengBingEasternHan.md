## Overview

耿秉 / Geng Bing is the Eastern Han general whose bounded profile now begins with the 74 CE Cheshi campaign and continues through the 88-89 CE Northern Xiongnu debates and [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]]'s later campaign. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-743-chuo-ren-xinwo-de-lishi-shunjian-mingdi-liuzhuang-de-wenqing-meng-lgwtuqtug5bh90qohwiskccx77hy|Hanji 743]] makes him the tactical voice who redirects [[DouGuEasternHan|Dou Gu]]'s Cheshi attack toward the rear king and then receives a Wu Colonel posting; Hanji 759-761 later make him a requested frontier adviser, Dou's deputy, and Yanran participant.

## Current Profile

Hanji 743 supplies the earliest layer. Geng Bing and Liu Zhang submit their command tallies to Dou Gu before the Dunhuang/Kunlun Barrier expedition. When Dou Gu wants to attack the Cheshi front king first, Geng Bing argues that the Cheshi rear king is the key point and leads the vanguard. The campaign kills thousands, Cheshi Hou king An surrenders, and the Cheshi front king follows. After Dou Gu petitions to restore Western Regions offices, [[EmperorMingOfHan|Mingdi]] approves, and Geng Bing is appointed Wu Colonel at Jinpu city in the later Cheshi area.

Hanji 759 introduces Geng in a later decision-advice role. When the [[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]] chanyu asks Han to join an attack on [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]] and specifically asks for Geng and [[DengHongHeEraLateHan|Deng Hong]], [[EmpressDowagerDouHeLateHan|Empress Dowager Dou]] consults Geng. He supports the proposal, arguing that the moment fits a use-barbarian-against-barbarian strategy and asking to serve personally.

Hanji 760 shows Geng's advice becoming part of Dou Xian's punishment-conversion path. After Dou Xian is implicated in the Liu Chang murder case and asks to redeem a capital offense by attacking Northern Xiongnu, he is appointed chariot-and-cavalry general with Geng as deputy.

Hanji 761 then makes Geng part of the victorious campaign memory. Dou and Geng depart by the Jilu route, other forces move separately, and the campaign produces the Jiluoshan victory, mass submission, and the Yanran commemoration. Geng's current profile is therefore not a fully independent command biography; it is the experienced frontier line that links Mingdi-era Western Regions restoration to later Dou-family northern campaigning.

## Key Characteristics

- Frontier general whose earliest current layer is the 74 CE Cheshi rear-king strategy.
- Subordinate command actor who submits tallies to Dou Gu but supplies the campaign's decisive target judgment.
- Vanguard commander whose Cheshi action leads to surrender and office restoration.
- Wu Colonel appointee at Jinpu city after the restored Western Regions structure is approved.
- Later adviser who supports using Southern Xiongnu against Northern Xiongnu during the latter's famine and disorder.
- Requested by Southern Xiongnu as a desired Han commander.
- Becomes Dou Xian's deputy and participates in the Jiluoshan-Yanran campaign memory.

## Qualifications

This page remains bounded to Hanji 743 and Hanji 759-761 and does not reconstruct Geng Bing's full military career. Hanji 743 credits Geng with the key Cheshi target judgment and vanguard action, but Dou Gu remains the senior commander and office-restoration petitioner. Hanji 759-761 use Geng mainly as an experienced frontier voice and campaign deputy, while Dou Xian remains the politically dominant actor in that branch.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 743 as Geng Bing's earliest current layer: Cheshi rear-king strategy, vanguard command, and Wu Colonel appointment.
- Reframed the later 88-89 CE Geng Bing profile as a recurrence of earlier Western Regions frontier experience.
- Linked his pro-expedition advice in Hanji 759 to the later deputy and Yanran roles already present in Hanji 760-761.
