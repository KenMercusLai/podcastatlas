## Overview

耿秉 / Geng Bing is an Eastern Han frontier strategist and commander whose current profile now begins with his 73 CE advice to [[EmperorMingOfHan|Mingdi]] on recovering the Western Regions, then runs through the 74 CE Cheshi campaign and the 88-89 CE [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]] debates and campaign. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-741-weishenme-gudai-xiongnu-zongyao-ruqin-zhongyuan-lllpgozgi6yd5pscw8z5ofidk5rp|Hanji 741]] normalizes the transcript's 耿炳 to 耿秉 and adds the earlier strategic-argument layer before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-743-chuo-ren-xinwo-de-lishi-shunjian-mingdi-liuzhuang-de-wenqing-meng-lgwtuqtug5bh90qohwiskccx77hy|Hanji 743]]'s Cheshi target judgment.

## Current Profile

Hanji 741 backfills Geng's earliest current layer. Asked about renewed Northern Xiongnu attacks, Geng argues from Han Wudi's precedent: Earlier Han success came from taking the Hetao and Hexi corridor, separating Xiongnu from western Qiang support, restoring frontier commanderies, and making the Western Regions a screen against steppe pressure. He contends that Eastern Han now faces a weaker Northern Xiongnu, so the court should retake the Western Regions rather than only defend passively.

The same episode gives Geng a command role, though not a decisive result. Mingdi appoints him to one of the four 73 CE routes. He and Qin Peng lead forces from the Zhangye/Juyan side, cross six hundred li of desert, and reach Sanmulou Mountain, but the Northern Xiongnu side avoids a decisive engagement. The episode therefore presents him first as a strategic mind whose campaign route proves the difficulty of finding mobile enemies.

Hanji 743 supplies the next current layer. Geng and Liu Zhang submit command tallies to [[DouGuEasternHan|Dou Gu]] before the Dunhuang/Kunlun Barrier campaign. When Dou wants to attack the Cheshi front king first, Geng argues that the rear king is the strategic hinge and leads the vanguard. The campaign kills thousands, Cheshi Hou king An surrenders, and the front king follows. Mingdi then approves restored Western Regions offices, and Geng is appointed Wu Colonel at Jinpu city.

Hanji 759-761 show Geng's later Northern Xiongnu role under [[EmpressDowagerDouHeLateHan|Empress Dowager Dou]] and [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]]. In 88 CE, when [[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]] asks Han to attack Northern Xiongnu and specifically requests Geng and [[DengHongHeEraLateHan|Deng Hong]], Geng supports the offensive, arguing that Southern Xiongnu can be used against Northern Xiongnu. Hanji 760 then makes him Dou Xian's deputy after Dou asks to redeem a capital offense by war service. Hanji 761 places him in the Jiluoshan-Yanran campaign memory.

## Key Characteristics

- Frontier strategist who links Western Regions recovery to older Han success in Hetao, Hexi, commandery restoration, and Qiang-Xiongnu separation.
- Commander in the 73 CE four-route campaign whose own route shows the difficulty of forcing contact with mobile Northern Xiongnu forces.
- Tactical adviser in 74 CE who redirects Dou Gu's Cheshi attack from the front king to the rear king.
- Vanguard commander whose Cheshi action leads to surrender and office restoration.
- Wu Colonel appointee at Jinpu after the restored Western Regions structure is approved.
- Later offensive advocate who supports using Southern Xiongnu against Northern Xiongnu during the latter's famine and disorder.
- Dou Xian's deputy and participant in the later Jiluoshan-Yanran victory memory.

## Qualifications

Geng's current profile should not be flattened into a single pro-war type. Hanji 741 shows strategic confidence but also a route with limited result; Hanji 743 gives him the decisive Cheshi target judgment; Hanji 759 records an offensive recommendation in a different famine-and-defection context. Qin Peng, Liu Zhang, Sanmulou geography, and detailed force counts remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 741 as Geng's earliest current layer before the 74 CE Cheshi campaign.
- Reframed Geng as a Western Regions strategist before he becomes Dou Xian's later deputy.
- Clarified that his 73 CE route is strategically important but not the campaign's main battlefield success.
