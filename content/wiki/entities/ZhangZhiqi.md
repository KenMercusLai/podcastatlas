# 张芝琪 / Zhang Zhiqi

张芝琪 / Zhang Zhiqi is a [[RandomFluctuations|随机波动]] speaker in [[ep258-women-ruhe-chongshi-shuiqian-dushu-lobyzxqk2oaov0v51cm355u8noix]]. Her contribution grounds reading in body and medium: severe dry eyes make light, screen exposure, and paper timing real constraints, while her broader reading stance rejects the idea that only paper books count.

She also articulates the episode's softer reading ethic. Reading can include books, phone articles, text-based video material, and AI-assisted discovery; bookstores create encounter; sleep reading should be comforting rather than overloading; and attention can be trained through activities such as tennis, stretching, and breathing as much as through books.
