# 巴蜀 / Ba-Shu

巴蜀 / Ba-Shu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2]] as the combined regional prize after [[QinState|秦国]] intervenes in the conflict between [[BaStateWarringStates|巴国]] and [[ShuStateWarringStates|蜀国]]. The episode describes the region as hard to enter and valuable enough that local control can tempt a governor toward independence.

The source emphasizes two layers of value. First, terrain creates defensive confidence: if Qin cannot easily enter, an ambitious local figure can imagine holding the region. Second, agricultural value makes the prize worth seizing, especially in the later frame where [[Dujiangyan|都江堰]] under [[LiBing|李冰]] helps make Shu a major food source for Qin.

Ba-Shu therefore anchors both Qin's strategic gain and [[ChenZhuangWarringStates|陈庄]]'s miscalculation. The same region that feeds Qin also makes delegated local authority dangerous if the central state cannot supervise it.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-143-xiangyu-shi-ruhe-feijin-xinji-nongsi-chuhuaiwang-mixin-lstfzfvbd2h9i39f7y5dxrbxxwv2|Hanji 143]] reuses Ba-Shu as [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s assigned Han-king territory after [[XiangYu|项羽]] rejects direct fulfillment of the [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]]. The episode treats the assignment as a strategic confinement: Ba-Shu is valuable and defensible, but its difficult roads and separation from [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] help Xiang Yu and [[FanZeng|范增]] contain Liu Bang through [[ThreeQinContainment|三秦压制]].
