# Edward Riou

## Overview

Edward Riou was the British captain assigned a flexible frigate role at the 1801 [[BattleOfCopenhagen1801|Battle of Copenhagen]] and killed while withdrawing under [[HydeParker|Hyde Parker's]] signal.

## Current Profile

The episode, which renders his surname “Rieu,” presents Riou as a trusted junior commander. He marked shoals during preparation and led a frigate squadron intended to adapt around Nelson's main line. When Parker ordered the action discontinued, Riou continued fighting briefly but believed his rank left him unable to disregard the signal as [[HoratioNelson]] did. His squadron's withdrawal exposed it more clearly to Danish fire, and Riou was killed. His death turns the celebrated story of Nelson's disobedience into a qualification: local initiative was enabled unevenly by rank, information, and freedom to reinterpret orders.

## Key Characteristics

- Trusted reconnaissance and frigate commander in shoal-constrained waters.
- Flexible supporting officer outside Nelson's main line of battle.
- Reluctant but ultimately obedient subordinate.
- Fatal example of the risk created by ambiguous withdrawal under fire.
