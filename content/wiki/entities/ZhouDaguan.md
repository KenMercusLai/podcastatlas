# 周达观 / Zhou Daguan

Zhou Daguan / 周达观 appears in [[ep267-tafang-meigonghe-yuji-hunfeiyi-yu-shuili-diguo-lkq2vxaatn4wxue7y5q1fc3-8yfk]] as the Yuan-era envoy whose account of Zhenla is used to glimpse [[Angkor|吴哥]] life. The episode cites him when describing local abundance, food, and the way external records help reconstruct a tropical polity where many local written materials did not survive.

His wiki role is as an evidence node for [[AngkorHydraulicEmpire]]. The source uses Zhou's account alongside temples, inscriptions, hydrology, and archaeological interpretation rather than as a standalone complete history of the [[KhmerEmpire|吴哥王朝]].
