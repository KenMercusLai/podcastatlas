# JD White Bar / 白条

## Overview
JD White Bar / 白条 is the internet credit-payment product named in the source alongside [[Huabei|花呗]] as pressure on bank credit-card usage.

## Current Profile
The source uses White Bar as a comparator rather than a full product case. Its wiki role is to show how platform-native credit tools can satisfy some everyday credit-payment needs that previously helped banks expand credit-card issuance.

## Key Characteristics
- Internet credit-payment tool in the source account.
- Competes with credit cards for young and platform-native users.
- Helps shift the credit-card business from issuance growth toward risk-managed existing customers.
- Functions as a platform-credit comparator beside Huabei.

## Evidence
- Competition evidence: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] names Baitiao / White Bar as an internet credit-payment tool that reduces young users' credit-card willingness.
- Scale context: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says China's credit-card and debit-credit-card base declined by 10 million from Q1 to Q2.
- Bank context: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says banks are focusing more on quality customers and high-frequency transaction scenes.
