# 安徽 / Anhui

Anhui / 安徽 enters the wiki through [[ep260-yikou-feimei-zhifang-cangzhe-zhongguo-yangzhi-de-xin-gushi-linjq2fkzhqvzjcgrmhudq12vs4j]] as one of the Chinese foie gras production regions. The episode focuses on Huoqiu as a place where local space constraints push some goose-raising into nearby provinces, while processing, veterinary knowledge, and feeding routines remain tied to the Anhui production network.

The province matters as a contrast with the more concentrated [[Guangxi|广西]] model described in the same source. In the Anhui case, dispersed grow-out can improve access to water and grass but also increases disease-management risk when birds return from multiple locations.
