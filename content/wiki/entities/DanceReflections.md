# Dance Reflections by Van Cleef & Arpels / 梵克雅宝舞蹈映像

## Overview
Dance Reflections by Van Cleef & Arpels / 梵克雅宝舞蹈映像 is the dance-support and public-cultural program discussed in EP286.

## Current Profile
The source says [[VanCleefArpels|Van Cleef & Arpels]] launched the program in 2020 around three linked commitments: creation, circulation, and education. Its Shanghai edition is presented as a four-week program of performances, workshops, masterclasses, and an exhibition, combining Chinese contemporary dance with international companies and using multiple venues to connect artists with different publics.

## Key Characteristics
- Supports dance creation, international circulation, and public education as one program.
- Works through international cultural partners rather than a single permanent venue.
- Uses performances, workshops, masterclasses, and exhibition formats.
- Treats audience development as an active curatorial responsibility.
- Gives Chinese contemporary dance a specific place within an international program.
