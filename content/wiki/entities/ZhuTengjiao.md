# 祝腾娇 / Zhu Tengjiao

## Overview
祝腾娇 / Zhu Tengjiao is the orthopedic guest from [[PekingUniversityThirdHospital|北医三院]] in [[vol-222-aoye-xintiao-kuangbiao-shi-cusi-qianzhao-aed-hui-dian-cuo-huoren-dai-ni-bikai-zhiming-changshi-shijie-jijiu-ri-teji-lnemogq8gb9o3e2835jvtfx3emiy]], a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] World First Aid Day episode.

## Current Profile
The current source uses Zhu Tengjiao as the orthopedic and trauma-side guest who explains when everyday injuries should leave home care and enter medical assessment. Her contribution centers on acute sports injuries, Achilles rupture, falls, joint swelling or locking, deep wounds, tetanus-risk punctures, tendon injury, bleeding control, burn cooling, and simple first-aid supplies.

This is a source-scoped profile rather than an independent biography.

## Key Characteristics
- Grounds the episode's orthopedic and trauma branch through sports injuries, falls, wounds, burns, and injury escalation signs.
- Explains [[AcuteSportsInjuryEscalation]] through sudden swelling, bruising, weight-bearing failure, deformity, locking, neurologic symptoms, and delayed Achilles-rupture treatment risk.
- Frames [[AcuteWoundBurnFirstAid]] around wound depth, bleeding control, contamination, tendon movement, tetanus risk, burn cooling, and clothing stuck to burns.
- Emphasizes early professional assessment when function changes or the injury is deeper than it first appears.
- Keeps ordinary supplies such as sterile gauze and bandages useful without treating home first-aid as definitive treatment.
