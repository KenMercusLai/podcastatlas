# Henjiuyiqian Lamb Skewers / 很久以前羊肉串

## Overview

Henjiuyiqian Lamb Skewers / 很久以前羊肉串 is a Chinese directly operated barbecue chain used by the source to explain why a recognizable dining brand can remain far below the store counts achieved by leading hotpot chains.

## Current Profile

The bounded source describes a roughly 18-year operating history, corporate restructuring and executive changes interpreted as preparation for capital-market activity, and 148 stores as of September 2026. Fifty-two were in Shanghai and 41 in Beijing, making the chain's scale materially dependent on two high-density, higher-spending cities.

Its operating model makes quality control visible but costly. The brand reportedly abandoned customer self-grilling after inconsistent results and relies on trained staff, while direct ownership keeps rent, fit-out, labor, and expansion capital on headquarters. Attempts to add lunch and delivery did not overcome the category's gathering-oriented meal rhythm or rapid quality loss after food cools.

## Key Characteristics

- A long-running barbecue chain with a geographically concentrated national footprint.
- A direct-operation model that favors execution control over franchise-funded opening speed.
- A mall-oriented format and near-RMB-90 average spend that may not transfer cleanly to lower-tier street-store markets.
- Staff-controlled grilling used to reduce the quality variation of customer self-cooking.
- Exposure to dinner, late-night, group-dining, delivery, and seasonal utilization constraints.

## Qualifications

Store count, city distribution, average spend, operating history, failed experiments, and capital-market intent are episode-reported rather than independently verified. One chain cannot establish the economics of all barbecue formats. Direct operation may support quality, but the source does not supply store-level revenue, margin, payback, closure, or cohort data needed to judge whether the model is profitable or merely slower to replicate.
