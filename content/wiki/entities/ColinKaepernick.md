# Colin Kaepernick

Colin Kaepernick appears in [[the-nfl-1]] as the quarterback whose 2016 national-anthem protest became a major NFL narrative-control and social-conflict test. The episode says he protested police brutality and racial inequality, was not signed after that season, and later reached a confidential settlement with the league.

In the wiki, Kaepernick connects the NFL's command-and-control media strength to its trust risks. A league that is excellent at packaging national attention can still mishandle player speech when political meaning, labor power, race, and fan identity collide.
