# 李菁 / Li Jing (Sanlian)

李菁 is the [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] host in [[ep265-dang-qing3xiu13-chongshang-resou-women-ruhe-gei-rensheng-fang-yige-changjia-lsloczlnkmcrrfpn9coh8rrqo-wb]], where she opens from the viral "请3休13" arrangement and keeps the conversation balanced between work-rest pressure and concrete [[Canada]] travel experience.

Her role in the source is framing rather than reportage. She turns a calendar trick into questions about whether people still know how to rest, how travel differs from tourism, and how a long holiday might make people move more lightly toward the next day.
