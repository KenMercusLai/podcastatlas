# Playfield

## Overview
Playfield is [[IvanaPlayfield]]'s modular dog-walking bag brand.

## Current Profile
Playfield sells a bag combining waste-bag access, a retractable bottle pocket, leash attachment, and personal-item storage. It has early multi-channel traction across direct sales, Chewy, boutiques, and a planned national retailer, but remains operationally dependent on its solo founder. The immediate company problem is therefore continuity: transfer routine operations without losing the product, brand, and relationship judgment concentrated in Ivana.

## Key Characteristics
- Launched in April 2024.
- Reportedly generated slightly more than $200,000 in its first full year.
- Sells through its own site, [[Chewy]], independent boutiques, and a planned national retailer.
- Uses a modular product design for combined dog-walking and personal needs.
- Has outsourced fulfillment, social media, and ambassador-program management.
