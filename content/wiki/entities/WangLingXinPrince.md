## Overview

Wang Ling / 王陵 is [[WangMang|王莽]]'s son in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-657-wangmang-fei-taizi-you-duo-erxi-lklx1rd39kgz-by4tbcpaagx4z-l|Hanji 657]], where succession language around his Luoyang-based title becomes part of Wang Mang's disaster-omen response. This page is distinct from [[WangLing|王陵]] the Qin general and [[WangLingHanMinister|王陵]] the Han minister.

## Current Profile

The source gives Wang Ling a narrow succession role. Earlier fu-ming language and court interpretation make his Luoyang establishment look like crown-prince status, but Wang Mang later uses illness and elder-brother order to declare the arrangement improper. He is therefore moved to Tongyi Yang Wang while [[WangAnXinPrince|王安]] is made Xin Qian Wang.

## Key Characteristics

- Xin prince whose title and Luoyang placement are interpreted as implying central succession status.
- Son whose illness lets Wang Mang describe the existing heir arrangement as defective.
- Younger-brother claimant displaced when Wang Mang reasserts elder-brother order through Wang An.
