# Sam Harris

## Overview
Sam Harris is the guest in [[essentials-using-meditation-to-focus-view-consciousness-expand-your-mind-dr-sam-harris-scim7293799235]], where he presents meditation as a first-person investigation of consciousness, selfing, attention, emotion, and ordinary freedom.

## Current Profile
In the current wiki evidence, Harris's profile is bounded to one [[HubermanLab]] Essentials conversation with [[AndrewHuberman]]. His main role is to move meditation away from only stress reduction or performance focus and toward [[NonDualMindfulness]]: examining the felt center of experience until the separate observer cannot be found. He also treats psychedelics and [[MDMA]] as early proof that radically different conscious states are possible, while insisting that the lasting goal is freedom available in ordinary waking life.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames meditation as inquiry into the felt self rather than only a relaxation method.
- Distinguishes thoughts as appearances in consciousness from the thinker people mistake them to be.
- Uses flow states, inner speech, anger loops, and social roles to make abstract consciousness claims practical.
- Separates concentration-induced altered states from mindfulness, equanimity, and non-dual recognition.
- Values psychedelics as possible entry points while warning against making peak experience the goal.
