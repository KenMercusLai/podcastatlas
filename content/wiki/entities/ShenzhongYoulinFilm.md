# 《身中有林》 / Shen Zhong You Lin

## Overview
《身中有林》 / Shen Zhong You Lin enters the wiki through [[ZhengZhiWriter|郑执]]'s [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] interview as Zheng's self-written and self-directed first feature film, discussed through story premise, title symbolism, adaptation choices, casting, injury risk, editing compression, and publicity pressure.

## Current Profile
The source describes the film as a family and crime-inflected story about two households, former lovers, a later reunion, a homicide case, and grievances that extend across years. Zheng stresses that the theatrical film is a compressed version of a larger intended world: rough-cut material and script ambition included more social background, layoffs, business lines, and a "micro-epic" historical scale.

The film is therefore useful less as a plot object than as a production object. The interview shows how a novelist-director adjusts actor background, works with a trusted crew, handles star casting, absorbs an on-set injury, and cuts a longer authorial design down to an audience-facing theatrical release.
