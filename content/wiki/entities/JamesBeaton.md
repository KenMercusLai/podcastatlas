# James Beaton

James Beaton appears in [[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] as [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]]' representative at the June 1583 Paris meeting behind the [[ThrockmortonPlot]]. The episode groups him with [[HenryDukeOfGuise|Henry, Duke of Guise]], [[WilliamAllen]], the papal nuncio, and other Catholic figures involved in invasion planning.

Beaton's role is source-scoped and diplomatic. He matters because Mary's claimant network extended beyond her English captivity into continental Catholic planning, making [[ConfessionalInvasionConspiracy]] more than a purely domestic English fear.
