# 《秦谜》 / Qin Mi

《秦谜》 / Qin Mi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-118-1-wangjian-lv-liushiwan-dajun-duizhan-xiangyan-haoxi-kaichang-le-lsf2puinwg0m3bgyi25znof7aznu]] as the [[LiKaiyuan|李开元]] work the host cites when moving beyond the compact [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] account of Qin's final Chu campaign.

In the episode's summary of the book, [[ChangpingJun|昌平君]] is reconstructed as Xiong Qi, a Chu royal son with a Qin maternal connection. The source uses that reconstruction to explain why Changping Jun's rebellion at Chenying could have been decisive for [[LiXinQin|李信]]'s earlier failed campaign and why the late Chu record may be more complex than the chronicle's outline suggests.

The wiki treats Qin Mi here as a cited reconstruction, not as settled baseline chronology. Its useful role is to mark a source-critical path for later ingests that may supply the missing argument in more detail.
