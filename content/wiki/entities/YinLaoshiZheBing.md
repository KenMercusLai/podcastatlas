# 尹老师 / Yin Laoshi (Zhe Bing speaker)

## Overview
尹老师 is a source-scoped speaker in the VOL.213 [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode on mosquito repellents, bite prevention, and post-bite care.

## Current Profile
Within the current source, 尹老师's role is consumer and prevention literacy. They explain why listeners should evaluate mosquito repellents by active ingredient, concentration, duration, application coverage, and reapplication after sweat or water rather than by product form or "natural" branding alone.

尹老师 also helps move the episode from product choice into everyday prevention. The source links their comments to contexts such as commuting, evening walks, longer outdoor exposure, clothing, household standing water, mosquito coils, and smoke or respiratory irritation.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains mosquito-repellent choice through active ingredients, concentration, duration, and usage context.
- Warns against weakly evidenced bracelets, patches, ultrasonic products, essential-oil mystique, and scent-only assumptions.
- Connects personal protection to correct application, reapplication, physical barriers, and household source reduction.
- Keeps the advice public and practical rather than presenting product choice as individualized medical treatment.
