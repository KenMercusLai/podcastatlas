# Shantou University / 汕头大学

Shantou University appears in [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046]] as the most visible public-good project associated with [[LiKaShing]] and [[Shantou]]. The episode treats the university as a serious hometown contribution from overseas Chinese capital and networks.

The source also uses Shantou University to mark a boundary. Education philanthropy can improve local capability and identity, but it does not by itself solve [[MerchantToIndustrialCityUpgrade]] or create the kind of industrial cluster that [[Shenzhen]] assembled through location, policy scale, supply chains, and external capital.
