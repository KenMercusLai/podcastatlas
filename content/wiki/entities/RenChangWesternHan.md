## Overview

任常 / Ren Chang is a Western Han chancellor's senior clerk and envoy presented by Hanji 329 as advocating a submission demand after Xiongnu's post-Mobei request for renewed heqin, then carrying that demand to the chanyu and being detained.

## Current Profile

Ren Chang represents the coercive diplomatic option inside the episode's post-Mobei policy dispute. He argues that recent Xiongnu defeat creates an opportunity to demand submission and a frontier audience rather than restore marriage diplomacy. [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] adopts the proposal, but the mission does not convert battlefield advantage into recognized hierarchy: the chanyu expects gifts or a princess, becomes angry at the submission demand, and detains Ren Chang without killing him.

## Key Characteristics

- Court official who advocates exploiting recent military advantage for a stronger submission demand.
- Envoy tasked with converting Han battlefield leverage into Xiongnu acknowledgment of hierarchy.
- Detained diplomat whose failed mission exposes the gap between coercive leverage and accepted settlement terms.

## Qualifications

This profile is limited to one structured podcast summary. Ren Chang's exact title, memorial wording, route, negotiations, detention conditions, and later fate are not established here. The episode's inference that the chanyu spared him from fear of Han is plausible within its narrative but not independently demonstrated.
