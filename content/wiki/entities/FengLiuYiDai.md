# 《风流一代》 / Caught by the Tides

[[FengLiuYiDai|《风流一代》]] is the recent work context in [[ep276-duihua-jiazhangke-shang-yige-shidai-zouyuan-yiding-shi-yinwei-lingyige-shidai-zai-kaojin-ni-lqzsj-ujv-gpem-796cvncekdewc]]. [[JiaZhangke|贾樟柯]] says making the film made him strongly feel that one era was receding, citing the end of globalization, new community forms, changed social management, changed emotional methods, and the approach of AI.

The source therefore uses the film as a hinge for [[EraTransitionProximity]]. It links Jia's older ordinary-life and memory concerns to the newer AI and civilization-change questions that EP277 develops more directly through [[FilmRealityBeyondRealism]] and [[AIVideoProductionWorkflow]].
