## Overview

陈立 / Chen Li enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-513-yongchuang-duomingdao-hanchen-chenli-zhusha-yelang-guowang-lvfqkcbuzts119gtpzfipzo5wbo|Hanji 513]] as the Jincheng Sima whom [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]] recommends as governor of [[ZhangkeCommanderyWesternHan|牂柯郡]] during the [[YelangStateWesternHan|夜郎]] crisis. His central act is killing [[YelangKingXingWesternHan|夜郎王兴]] before Xing can consolidate a wider frontier revolt.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Chen Li as the positive contrast to rash bravery. He acts boldly, but the boldness is embedded in timing, cover, and follow-through: he identifies Xing as the key actor, asks the court for permission to execute him, then moves before a reply arrives because frontier distance makes waiting dangerous.

Chen Li's method is not a large frontal campaign. He enters Xing's territory under the pretext of inspecting counties, brings only a few dozen tough officials, summons Xing to Qietong Pavilion, and has prepared axemen kill him during questioning. The shock makes allied rulers and outside troops surrender.

The aftermath is equally important. When Xing's father-in-law Wengzhi and son Yewu gather remnants and coerce villages, Chen Li cuts food routes, uses spies and counterintelligence, seizes the only water source, and lets internal collapse produce the surrender of heads. The source uses this to distinguish strategic courage from Wan Nian's failed independent assault.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han frontier official appointed from Jincheng Sima to Zhangke governor.
- Treats Xing as the key node whose removal can disrupt the Ye Lang threat.
- Uses inspection cover and a small selected party rather than an obvious large army.
- Creates deterrent shock by killing Xing in a staged meeting and displaying his head.
- Follows the killing with logistical and counterintelligence pressure against residual resistance.
- Serves as the episode's model of "courage with strategy" against Wan Nian's rash attack.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 513. It does not reconstruct Chen Li's full biography, exact offices beyond Jincheng Sima and Zhangke governor, later career, or the textual variants behind names such as Qietong, Wengzhi, Yewu, and Wan Nian. The source praises Chen Li's method, but the page keeps the ethical and administrative evaluation source-scoped.
