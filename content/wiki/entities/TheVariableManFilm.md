# The Variable Man (unproduced film)

## Overview

*The Variable Man* is an unproduced science-fiction screenplay by [[GaryGoldman]] and [[AngusFletcher]], adapted from a [[PhilipKDick]] story and reframed around predictive AI, free will, romance, and human self-destruction.

## Current Profile

The adaptation imagines a future organized by Plato, an AI that reliably selects food, gifts, partners, and other choices. When Plato predicts that humanity will destroy itself within twenty-four hours but cannot locate a remedy, the future retrieves Cole, a man absent from its data and therefore impossible to predict.

Cole is not a simple liberator from machine control. His obedience repeatedly succeeds and his independent choices repeatedly fail; the AI engineers his romance and asks him to kill the child he once saved, who later built the accelerated future. The film therefore makes unpredictability both a possible rescue and an unquantifiable danger.

The project attracted actor and producer interest but remained too expensive and conceptually difficult to finance without a trusted director. Its last reported opportunity ended before evaluation when an algorithmic screenplay-financing company failed after an earlier selection underperformed.

## Key Characteristics

- Philip K. Dick adaptation expanded from military prediction into everyday algorithmic governance.
- Centers an unpredictable protagonist whose agency is both necessary and dangerous.
- Refuses simple “trust yourself” and “trust the AI” resolutions.
- Uses an engineered romance to join intimate manipulation with species-level stakes.
- Large-budget project that gained interest without assembling a financeable creative coalition.
