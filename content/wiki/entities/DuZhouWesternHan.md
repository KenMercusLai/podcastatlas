## Overview

杜周 / Du Zhou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-402-2-shudiwushu-de-kuli-duzhou-weihe-zuizhong-deyi-shanzhong-lqrunv51se0w7_2fi2ifs_f7zub4|Hanji 402-2]] as a [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]-era cruel official whose later safe retirement makes him a political-survival case. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-381-1-hanwudi-ruhe-chengwei-xihan-zuichangshou-de-huangdi-lrrerer9sccohlnhy9kidsqjjnek|Hanji 381-1]] backfills his 95 BCE death and the court transition to [[BaoShengzhiWesternHan|鲍圣之]] as Yushi Dafu.

## Current Profile

The source portrays Du Zhou as an official who understands law less as an autonomous standard than as the execution of imperial intent. After [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]] and [[YiZongWesternHan|义纵]] fall, Du Zhou rises as a severe legal actor; when accused of unfair judging, he argues that the ruler's will is the real legal measure. The episode treats this as morally chilling but politically effective under Han Wudi's power structure.

Du Zhou's safe end is not explained as innocence or popularity. The episode says he offended many officials and had serious wealth problems, yet later softened his posture, became mild and thick-hearted in manner, and retired without further punishment. His page therefore begins as a negative survival profile: a harsh executor can endure when he tracks power closely and changes style as the environment changes.

Hanji 381-1 adds the endpoint from the late-Wudi chronology. It summarizes Du Zhou as opportunistic,阴狠, and "笑面虎"-like, emphasizing torture-heavy case handling and the contrast between victims' suffering and Wudi's view of him as a diligent maintainer of order. The episode then records his death in 95 BCE before Bao Shengzhi's appointment.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han cruel official associated with Han Wudi's harsh legal machinery.
- Figure linked in the episode to Sima Qian's memory of prison humiliation and punishment after the Li Ling case.
- Legal actor who treats imperial intent as the effective law.
- Court survivor who offends many officials but later shifts toward a milder posture.
- Father of Du Yannian, whose political tact the host reads as partly shaped by family experience.
- Official whose 95 BCE death marks an office transition to Bao Shengzhi.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to two podcast notes. It does not reconstruct Du Zhou's complete offices, case record, wealth investigation, relationship to Zhang Tang and Yi Zong, or the primary-source details of Sima Qian's punishment. Hanji 381-1 adds a death-and-appointment chronology but not a full obituary or exact Yushi Dafu tenure history. The source's claim about Sima Qian's restrained writing because Du Zhou was still alive is preserved as an interpretive caution, not as proof of motive.
