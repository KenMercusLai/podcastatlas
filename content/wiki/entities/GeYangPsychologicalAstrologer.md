# 葛阳 / Ge Yang (psychological astrologer)

## Overview

葛阳 / Ge Yang is the [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]] EP182 guest who translates modern psychological astrology through her earlier finance, audit, and insurance experience.

## Current Profile

The source presents 葛阳 as a finance-trained astrology practitioner whose interest began during career and relationship uncertainty around 2016. She uses financial-model language to explain a birth chart as a layered symbolic system and the map-versus-navigation metaphor to keep personal choice visible. Her strongest contribution is interpretive translation: she shows how astrologers combine elements, planets, signs, houses, rulers, and aspects, while the episode itself leaves the system's causal mechanism and empirical reliability unestablished.

## Key Characteristics
- Former finance, audit, and insurance worker using model language to explain astrological complexity.
- Modern psychological astrology practitioner rather than an asserted expert in Chinese divination traditions.
- Interpreter who resists single-sign stereotypes by layering multiple symbolic dimensions.
- Advocate of a map-not-navigation frame that preserves agency against strict fatalism.
- Source-scoped commentator whose medical, reproductive, criminal, national, and historical claims require external evidence.
