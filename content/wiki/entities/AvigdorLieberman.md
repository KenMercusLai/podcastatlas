# Avigdor Lieberman

## Overview
Avigdor Lieberman is an Israeli nationalist opposition leader whose coalition choices are pivotal in the government-formation attempt following the 2026 election.

## Current Profile
The source presents Lieberman as opposing [[BenjaminNetanyahu]] while also refusing a minority government dependent on Arab parties. He is independently pursuing an arrangement with ultra-Orthodox parties, making him both a potential route around and a continuing contributor to [[CoalitionExclusionDeadlock]].

## Key Characteristics
- Leads a nationalist opposition force central to post-election coalition arithmetic.
- Rejects dependence on Arab parties despite sharing the goal of replacing Netanyahu.
- Pursues a separate agreement with ultra-Orthodox parties.
- Has more coalition experience than [[GadiEisenkot]], but follows an agenda not controlled by him.

## Evidence
- **Pivotal exclusion:** Lieberman refuses the proposed minority government if it depends on Arab-party support. [[all-eyes-on-eisenkot-israels-could-be-prime-minister-6af19d18e7c6d309d1116743]]
- **Independent bargaining:** He is negotiating separately with ultra-Orthodox parties rather than simply joining Eisenkot’s initial plan. [[all-eyes-on-eisenkot-israels-could-be-prime-minister-6af19d18e7c6d309d1116743]]
- **Experience and autonomy:** The episode contrasts Eisenkot’s short party-leadership history with more seasoned opposition leaders whose cooperation he still needs. [[all-eyes-on-eisenkot-israels-could-be-prime-minister-6af19d18e7c6d309d1116743]]
