# Christopher Ramsden

## Overview

Christopher Ramsden is the NIH researcher who recovered and reanalyzed data from the [[SydneyDietHeartStudy]] and [[MinnesotaCoronaryExperiment]] to test the mortality effects of replacing saturated fat with linoleic-acid-rich oils.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Ramsden as shifting attention from whether saturated fat is harmful to whether the replacement intervention is beneficial or harmful. He traced surviving nine-track tapes from Sydney and then contacted [[RobertFrantz]], whose basement search recovered the larger Minnesota dataset.

His Minnesota reanalysis found cholesterol reduction without demonstrated lower coronary or all-cause mortality and reported an adverse association in an older, longer-exposed subgroup. The episode treats the two recovered trials as a challenge to dietary orthodoxy, while the wiki retains subgroup, historical, and broader-literature qualifications.

## Key Characteristics

- NIH investigator focused on the effects of linoleic-acid-rich vegetable oils.
- Located and reanalyzed two neglected randomized diet-trial datasets.
- Asked whether lowering a surrogate marker translated into mortality benefit.
- Consulted Robert about the family and legacy implications before publication.
