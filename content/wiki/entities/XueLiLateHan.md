# 薛礼 / Xue Li (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-993-shentingling-zhizhan-sunce-dazhan-taishici-lrivpk7col2l7k-s4dqzxxp2kqp1|Hanji 993]] backfills Xue Li as one of the Liu Yao-side figures whose position [[SunCe|孙策]] attacks during the 195 CE Jiangdong advance. The source places him in the military sequence after Niuzhu and before [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]]'s flight, while the later Hanji 995 source explains his prior Pengcheng displacement and killing by [[ZeRong|笮融]].

薛礼 / Xue Li enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-995-ta-zuishang-cibei-weihe-duoci-cansha-enren-llicukoezccd5iycin4uq7qun51k|Hanji 995]] as a Pengcheng chancellor displaced by [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]]. After [[ZeRong|笮融]] murders [[ZhaoYuLateHan|赵昱]] and plunders [[GuanglingCommandery|广陵]], Xue Li also receives him courteously and is killed by the same broad pattern.

Xue Li matters in the source because his death proves that Ze Rong's violence is not an accidental one-off. The episode uses the repeated betrayal to argue that Ze Rong's religious display and social dependence should not be confused with a moral constraint.
