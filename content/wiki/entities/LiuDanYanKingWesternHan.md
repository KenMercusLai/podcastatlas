## Overview

燕王刘旦 / Liu Dan, Yan King enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-402-1-sanghongyang-ceng-bang-hanwudi-zhifu-weihe-zuihou-canzao-miezu-ltpcos20d5mafcoywmfcb6iifwsv|Hanji 402-1]] as the royal rebel whose anti-[[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] action becomes the charge background for [[SangHongyangWesternHan|桑弘羊]]'s final destruction.

## Current Profile

The source does not narrate Liu Dan's rebellion in full. It uses him as the political frame around Sang Hongyang: Sang is said to have participated in anti-Huo rebellious activity and to have conspired with the Yan king. Liu Dan therefore functions here as a royal-kin rebellion node rather than a fully profiled prince.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Yan king in the Han Zhao regency field.
- Royal rebel associated with opposition to Huo Guang.
- Charge background for Sang Hongyang's execution and family destruction.
- Source-bounded actor whose full rebellion narrative is not yet reconstructed in the wiki.
