# 大盆 / Dapen (Podcast Guest)

## Overview
大盆 is a comedian and recurring patient-experience guest on [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]], where his unresolved symptoms and later chronic-disease history ground discussions of diagnostic uncertainty, long-term risk, medication review, and medical-service choice.

## Current Profile
VOL.182 uses 大盆's persistent hand numbness and morning finger stiffness to make an unresolved workup concrete. It follows social-media searching, concern about statin adverse effects, negative rheumatoid-related testing, clinician-directed temporary interruption, cervical imaging, MRI and electromyography planning, and several still-unresolved physical and psychological explanations.

VOL.224 broadens the profile from diagnostic uncertainty to chronic-risk management. 大盆 recounts gout, possible longstanding hypertension, dyslipidemia, carotid plaque, endoscopy and second-opinion experiences, and concurrent urate- and lipid-lowering medicines. His behavior is mixed but increasingly deliberate: severe gout pain strengthened adherence, he reports existing medicines to clinicians and follows professional dose changes, yet he had earlier treated resolved dizziness and stopped measurement as evidence against hypertension and improvised burdensome medicine spacing when worried about interactions.

His role remains experiential rather than clinical. Together the episodes show a patient moving from online possibility-matching toward more structured follow-up while still illustrating why symptoms, personal targets, report language, and commercial recommendations require qualified interpretation.

## Key Characteristics
- Describes persistent hand numbness, a tight “glove” sensation, and finger-joint stiffness.
- Uses social-media searches to identify several possible causes before a diagnosis has been established.
- Reports gout, lipid abnormality, carotid plaque, and possible prior hypertension while distinguishing felt pain from less perceptible long-term risk.
- Discloses existing medicines to clinicians and accepts clinician-guided interruption, resumption, dose adjustment, and review rather than claiming a universal regimen.
- Uses multiple specialties, imaging, endoscopy, and a second opinion while reflecting on platform marketing and diagnostic uncertainty.
- Maintains exercise and avoids smoking and alcohol, yet does not treat those habits as proof that disease is impossible.
