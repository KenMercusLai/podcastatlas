# Josh Spencer

## Overview
Josh Spencer is the correspondent voice in [[even-more-maximum-pressure-sanctions-on-iran-6a8d6a9b66c4a1e81a4c86a9]] explaining how AI is affecting Philippine offshoring work.

## Current Profile
Spencer presents the Philippine offshore industry as more resilient than a simple automation story would predict. He says the work includes call centers, IT tasks, and support for American and European companies, and that jobs continued growing through 2025. At the same time, he identifies pressure on basic tier-one work, slower future growth, and worker anxiety about AI monitoring and work intensity.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses the [[Philippines]] as a grounded case for AI's mixed effect on offshore services.
- Distinguishes messy customer-service work from simpler tasks that AI can automate more directly.
- Frames AI as both productivity aid and labor-market pressure.
- Connects sector-level growth to worker-level concerns about pay, pace, and monitoring.
