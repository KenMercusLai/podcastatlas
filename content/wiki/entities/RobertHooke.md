# Robert Hooke / 罗伯特·胡克

Robert Hooke appears in [[26-niudun-yu-weibi-zhizaozhe-keneng-shi-sineipu-de-yuanxing-580065317]] through the [[RoyalSociety|Royal Society]]'s experimental culture. The source emphasizes Hooke's instrument work, including improvements to thermometers, barometers, rain gauges, and wind gauges, and his interest in coordinated weather observation by British gentlemen.

Hooke's source role is not mainly as Newton's rival. He helps make [[ScientificRevolutionSocialInfrastructure]] concrete: early modern science advanced through devices, observations, societies, shared records, and people willing to turn weather, light, and motion into collectable evidence.
