# 麻辣王子 / Malawangzi

麻辣王子 / Malawangzi appears in [[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw]] as the consumer-brand case tied to second-generation short-video visibility. The episode does not profile the brand in detail, but uses it to show that [[FactorySecondGeneration|厂二代]] successors can make themselves and their factories visible through personal accounts and everyday content.

For the wiki, Malawangzi is a source-scoped example of [[FactorySecondGenerationPersonalIP]] and [[StoryLedConsumerBranding]]. Its relevance is not only that a successor can appear online, but that consumer manufacturing now asks family successors to compete in brand narrative, creator rhythm, trust-building, and platform distribution as well as production.

## Source Position
- Malawangzi is mentioned as part of the episode's wider case set beyond [[Fadior|法迪奥]] and [[WoteShoes|沃特鞋企]].
- The related Sanlian article by 夏洁逸 is said to cover the brand's self-media account operation.
- The case anchors the episode's claim that public visibility has become part of succession work for some consumer manufacturers.
