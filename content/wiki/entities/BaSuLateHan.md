## Overview

巴肃 / Ba Su is a late-Han figure introduced by [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-888-luanshi-zhizhong-zhijing-bupasi-de-ren-ll7s9e5bh8reuy4xzzjwq0psowvq|Hanji 888]] as a Bohai-associated participant in [[DouWuLateHan|Dou Wu]]'s failed anti-eunuch plan. The episode makes him a compact example of a person who chooses public responsibility over escape once punishment arrives.

## Current Profile

Ba Su's profile is defined by a single crisis sequence. He is initially not discovered when the plot against the eunuch side collapses, but he is later barred from office and then targeted by an arrest order. Instead of hiding or fleeing, he rides to the county court.

The county magistrate offers to abandon office and flee with him, turning survival into a real alternative rather than a purely abstract option. Ba Su refuses. His answer treats a minister's political plan and legal guilt as matters that cannot be hidden or evaded, and he is executed. The source uses that refusal to test the boundary between loyal responsibility and survival-first political prudence.

## Key Characteristics

- Ba Su is tied to the Dou Wu anti-eunuch plan and its post-defeat persecution field.
- His defining action is voluntary surrender after arrest becomes unavoidable.
- He refuses an official's offer of escape, making his death a chosen responsibility rather than a failed hiding attempt.
- The episode uses him to contrast honor-bound accountability with the practical argument for staying alive.
- His current wiki profile is source-scoped and does not yet establish a wider biography beyond this crisis.

## Qualifications

- The page is based on one podcast source note and should not be treated as a full prosopographical account of Ba Su.
- The episode does not require the reader to generalize Ba Su's choice into a universal rule; it stages the survival objection before presenting his death as admirable.
- His refusal also reduces the county magistrate's potential collateral exposure, but the source does not develop that institutional consequence in detail.
