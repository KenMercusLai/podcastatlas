# Sandy Dininger

Sandy Dininger is the founder of [[BrainBuffs]] and a caller in [[advice-line-strategy-sessions-4962a0d6-36bc-4797-8002-3bd8b748a89a]]. Her Advice Line question is how to offer direct-to-teacher access at an affordable price without weakening future school-district sales.

The advice from [[ShaziVisram]] and [[GuyRaz]] turns the pricing problem into a segmentation and proof problem. They suggest differentiating teacher and district offerings, using free or freemium access with influential math teachers, and collecting outcome data that can support later district conversations.
