# Guinness Peat Aviation / GPA

## Overview

Guinness Peat Aviation, or GPA, is the Irish aircraft-leasing pioneer founded by [[TonyRyan]] and presented as an origin point for the country's modern leasing cluster.

## Current Profile

The episode traces GPA to Ryan's placement of an idle Aer Lingus Boeing 747 with a Thai airline together with crew and maintenance support. That transaction revealed a reusable business opportunity: airlines could obtain operating capacity without purchasing an aircraft outright, while an intermediary could combine assets, services, and cross-border placement knowledge.

## Key Characteristics

- Early Irish aircraft-leasing company associated with [[TonyRyan]].
- Origin story built around placing an idle wide-body aircraft with another airline.
- Early transaction resembling a wet lease because it included crew and maintenance support.
- Company whose growth helped accumulate Irish leasing expertise and networks.
- Business that entered restructuring during the aviation downturn after the first Gulf War.
