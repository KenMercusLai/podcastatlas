# AB180 Blood Pump

The AB180 blood pump is the small rotating blood pump discussed in [[86-dakai-yike-xin-na-meihao-de-zhang-wo-yijing-daguo-le-702323437]]. The source says [[StephenWestaby|Stephen Westaby / 史蒂芬·维斯塔比]] learned about the device while looking for ways to support failing hearts beyond ordinary transplant availability.

Its central source case is Julie, a 21-year-old viral-myocarditis patient. The episode says the AB180 was restricted in the United States to last-resort use, but Westaby used it outside normal approval in a British setting after warning from a medical director. Julie survived even when monitors showed no normal pulse, because the pump maintained circulation until recovery became possible.

The device matters to the wiki as a concrete example of [[HighRiskSurgicalInnovation]]. It links [[ArtificialHeartBridgeTherapy]], [[MedicalDeviceClinicalValidation]], and [[BureaucraticRiskAvoidance]]: a device can be risky, insufficiently routine, and still clinically decisive when the alternative is near-certain death.
