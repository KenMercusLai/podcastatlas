## Overview

淳于长 / Chunyu Zhang is synthesized as a Chengdi-era favorite whose Wang-family access and imperial proximity first enable private extraction and then make his fall politically useful to [[WangMang|王莽]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-556-lishi-hanjian-shui-yao-gei-chengdi-dai-lvmaozi-licvtkyvoh-p3ucobowldj6g-id|Hanji 556]] supplies the pre-collapse layer: Chunyu Zhang builds wealth through bribery and exploits [[EmpressXuWesternHan|废后许氏]] with paid promises of restoration. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-557-wangmang-pk-chunyuchang-nanrenjian-de-jingxin-jiaoliang-lnvmnizx0mhrln-g1p1a8u_9kgnx|Hanji 557]] then supplies the collapse sequence: Wang Mang denounces him through Wang-family channels, Chengdi first dismisses him, but Chunyu Zhang's attempt to regain office through [[WangLiWesternHan|王立]] leads to renewed investigation and death in prison.

## Current Profile

Hanji 556 presents Chunyu Zhang as a man whose court value comes from access rather than demonstrated statecraft. As a relative in [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s wider outer-relative field and a trusted attendant around [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], he can collect gifts from officials and aristocrats while selling the image of influence. That same proximity lets him become credible to Empress Xu, who is confined away from Chengdi and cannot verify his claims.

Hanji 556 also makes the Empress Xu branch central rather than incidental. Through her sister Xu Mi, Chunyu Zhang receives money, carriages, garments, and other valuables while promising he can ask Chengdi to restore her, even to the impossible-sounding position of left empress. The host reads this as exploitation through hope, dependency, and [[SunkCostEntrapment|sunk cost]].

Hanji 557 presents Chunyu Zhang as politically exposed in three further stages. First, Wang Mang tells the ill [[WangGenWesternHan|王根]] that Chunyu Zhang is pleased by Wang Gen's illness and expects to replace him in assisting government. Wang Gen sends Wang Mang to Wang Zhengjun, and the accusation then reaches Chengdi.

Second, Chengdi's initial punishment is limited. Because Chunyu Zhang is still tied to the dowager's kin network and had helped [[ZhaoFeiyanWesternHan|赵飞燕]], Chengdi removes him from office but does not immediately kill him.

Third, Chunyu Zhang fails the exit test. Before returning to his fief, he gives valuables through Wang Li's son Wang Rong in the hope that Wang Li will speak for him. The suspicious reversal of Wang Li's attitude prompts investigation; Wang Rong's suicide and Chunyu Zhang's confession about insulting [[EmpressXuWesternHan|废后许氏]] and promising to make her left empress raise the case to grave disloyalty. He dies in prison, while his wife and children are exiled to Hepu and his mother is sent back to her original commandery.

## Key Characteristics

- Chengdi-era favorite and court actor whose fall precedes Wang Mang's Da Sima appointment.
- Converts Wang-family identity, imperial trust, and proximity to Chengdi into private extraction.
- Exploits Empress Xu's cold-palace vulnerability through paid restoration promises.
- Initially survives with dismissal rather than execution.
- Attempts to recover position through gifts and Wang Li's imperial access.
- Becomes fatally exposed when the case expands into palace scandal around Empress Xu.
- Functions as the source's negative example of refusing a survivable political exit.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 556 and Hanji 557. It does not reconstruct Chunyu Zhang's full earlier career, the independent truth of every accusation, the legal details of "大逆," Xu Mi's full biography, or his private motive beyond the episodes' accounts. Hanji 556's reading of his promises as empty and manipulative is treated as the host's interpretation of the political incentives, not as independently proven inner intent.

## What Changed

- Added the pre-collapse exploitation layer: Chunyu Zhang's access-based enrichment and long manipulation of Empress Xu now explain why the Hanji 557 confession becomes politically explosive.
