## Overview
Trina Spear is the co-founder and sole CEO of [[FIGS]]. In [[figs-trina-spear-and-heather-hasson-the-2-5-billion-scrubs-brand-that-investors-said-would-never-work-vmp6448511514]], she is presented as the financially and operationally oriented founder who researched the medical-apparel market, joined [[HeatherHasson]] in direct customer validation, and led fundraising and scale operations.

## Current Profile
Trina's profile combines analytical conviction with concentrated commitment. She left [[Blackstone]], invested the after-tax remainder of a liquidated retirement account while carrying school debt, and used research on uniform purchasing and replenishment to support FIGS's direct-to-clinician model. Later phases of her role included fundraising through repeated rejection, governance choices, litigation, the pandemic response, and public-company leadership.

## Key Characteristics
- She tested founder enthusiasm against market structure, purchaser behavior, cyclicality, and replenishment demand before joining full-time.
- She paired research with direct selling outside emergency rooms rather than relying only on financial analysis.
- She led operations and fundraising through hundreds of reported investor rejections and staged capital rounds.
- She treated founder equity as historically grounded rather than requiring an equal split with Heather.
- She explicitly separates fluctuating company valuation from personal worth and acknowledges the personal costs of intense company focus.
