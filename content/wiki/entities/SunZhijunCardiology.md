# 孙志军 (cardiology guest)

## Overview
孙志军 is the cardiology guest in a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode about hypertension diagnosis, home measurement, long-term treatment, organ risk, pregnancy, exercise, and emerging monitoring technology.

## Current Profile
The available source presents 孙志军 as the clinical voice translating hypertension from a familiar number into a longitudinal risk-management problem. He distinguishes an isolated elevated reading from diagnosis, explains technique that makes home records useful, and argues that apparently normal readings during treatment usually show control rather than cure. His emphasis is conservative: use established tools consistently, review side effects and organ function, individualize goals and medication with clinicians, and do not let wearables or new technology displace validated measurement.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains cardiovascular risk through cumulative organ damage rather than symptoms or one reading alone.
- Treats measurement quality as the foundation for diagnosis, risk stratification, and treatment adjustment.
- Presents lifestyle change, medication, combination therapy, and follow-up as complementary parts of care.
- Emphasizes individualized targets and special caution for pregnancy, older adults, comorbidity, and high-risk disease.
- Keeps new monitoring technologies subordinate to validated devices and clinical interpretation.
