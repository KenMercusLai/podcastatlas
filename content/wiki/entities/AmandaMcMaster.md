# Amanda McMaster

## Overview
Amanda McMaster is the Boston Dynamics representative interviewed in the All-In robotics special. She presents Boston Dynamics as a commercial deployment company whose strongest current product evidence comes through Spot.

## Current Profile
McMaster's source-scoped profile connects Boston Dynamics' research reputation to practical deployment requirements: customer ROI, reliability, inspection data, local robot control, cloud reasoning, supply-chain sovereignty, and anti-weaponization commitments. She is the episode's clearest voice on robot reliability metrics and strategic sourcing boundaries.

## Key Characteristics
- She says Boston Dynamics is no longer only a lab or R&D company, but is focused on real-world deployment.
- She frames Spot around industrial inspection, security, acoustic sensing, gauge reading, vibration detection, and asset monitoring.
- She uses customer ROI, battery life, docking, and mean time between intervention as commercial reliability tests.
- She separates physical-control brains on the robot from higher-level reasoning that can involve cloud partners.
- She argues that Chinese humanoid robots should not be allowed in the United States because of safety and data-leak concerns.
- She maintains Boston Dynamics' anti-weaponization stance while allowing non-weaponized government work such as explosive ordnance disposal.
