# 郭靖（ACE Studio） / Guo Jing (ACE Studio)

## Overview
郭靖 / Guo Jing is the founder and CEO of [[ACEStudio|ACE Studio]], represented by the bounded source as an AI-music product builder and [[HF0]] participant.

## Current Profile
Guo's contribution is a self-correcting account of startup growth. He credits HF0's single metric, weekly public review, peer pressure, and removal of daily-life friction with helping ACE Studio increase revenue and formalize acquisition systems, but he rejects the idea that those tactics create durable growth by themselves. His later judgment places model capability, product experience, and user value underneath pricing, funnel, and distribution gains.

He also translated the residency into a remote-company operating mechanism: selected teams temporarily co-locate for a bounded problem, with retreats, weekly reporting, shared living support, and a three-month target.

## Key Characteristics
- Advocates removing second-priority work so one company objective can dominate attention.
- Treats growth as a repeatable product-like system rather than a set of isolated campaigns.
- Distinguishes short-run revenue amplification from durable product-led value.
- Uses temporary co-location as a project mechanism inside a remote company.
- Connects professional-user behavior and aesthetic feedback to AI-music model improvement.
