# 《跑外卖：一个女骑手的世界》

[[PaoWaimai|《跑外卖：一个女骑手的世界》]] appears in [[154-sishisui-ganyan-bu-zuo-nazhi-wenshuili-de-qingwa-lg-qj3p2lq-8sk-rn2vk209tq9x]] through [[WangWan|王婉]]'s image of leaving the delivery/life flow briefly to pick up a stone by the river. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] uses that detail as a compact example of [[AutonomyUnderInformationFlow|autonomy under information flow]]: the person interrupts acceleration and notices the world directly.

The book's source role is not a full account of platform labor. In this wiki ingest, it anchors the episode's closing contrast between life as an automated conveyor belt and life as chosen attention.
