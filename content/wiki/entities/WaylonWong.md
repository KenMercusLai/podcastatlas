# Waylon Wong

## Overview
Waylon Wong is a host in the [[PlanetMoney]] and [[TheIndicator]] crossover material, translating macroeconomic and investing mechanisms into accessible indicators and examples.

## Current Profile
Across [[indicators-of-2025-and-what-to-watch-in-2026]] and [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-90166871d561fa45ba428e0a0fbc3e7e-20290737-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-90166871d561fa45ba428e0a0fbc3e7e-20290737]], Wong moves between monetary-policy monitoring and household-facing financial risk. He uses the federal funds rate to connect 2026 forecasting with Fed independence, then uses a simple borrowed Apple-stock example to introduce margin trading before the episode turns to forced selling and sports betting.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains macro and market mechanisms through compact indicators and ordinary examples.
- Connects Federal Reserve choices to political independence and household financial consequences.
- Hosts crossovers that distinguish warning signals from complete forecasts.
