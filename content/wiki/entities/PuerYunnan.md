# 普洱 / Pu'er, Yunnan

## Overview
Pu'er / 普洱 is the Yunnan coffee-producing area mentioned in [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]].

## Current Profile
The source uses Pu'er mainly as a regional comparison point. It is described as having a larger city and stronger commercial base than [[Mangshi|芒市]], and as a foundation for Yunnan's bulk coffee industry.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents the commercial-coffee base within the episode's Yunnan coffee map.
- Provides a contrast to Mangshi's smaller-city rhythm and Dehong's under-discovered small-lot angle.
- Appears in the high-altitude discussion as an area where some coffee planting was reported near 2100 meters.

- Commercial base: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] says Pu'er has a bulk-coffee industrial foundation.
- City comparison: [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] contrasts Pu'er as larger and more commercially grounded than Mangshi.
