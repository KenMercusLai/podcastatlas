# 林幼玉 / Lin Youyu

## Overview

林幼玉 was a Southern Song girl remembered for passing a child-prodigy assessment under Emperor Xiaozong and receiving the special title 孺人.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Lin as an exceptional female entrant into a male-dominated examination world. At age nine, she is said to have sought examination, passed testing through 中书后省, and received the title 孺人. Her case demonstrates recognition of learned ability but not ordinary admission to an official career: a special honor did not establish a durable female examination pathway.

The supplied episode summary calls her “林又玉” and describes a “中书生” assessment followed by the title “儒人”. Those forms are normalized to 林幼玉, 中书后省, and 孺人. Claims that she was simply the first woman to pass the regular civil-service examination or that her case directly produced a blanket ban are too broad for the evidence provided.

## Key Characteristics

- Southern Song female child candidate associated with Emperor Xiaozong's reign.
- Reportedly passed an exceptional child-prodigy assessment at age nine.
- Received the title 孺人 rather than a demonstrated ordinary official appointment.
- Serves as a rare case of female learning recognized inside a male-dominated selection order.
- Illustrates the difference between individual exception and institutional inclusion.

## Qualifications

The episode is a short popular-history summary rather than a complete institutional study. It does not establish Lin's full biography, the exact examination procedure, whether “first female candidate” includes every earlier selection form, or a direct legal sequence from her success to universal exclusion. Her result should be described as a child-prodigy assessment and special recognition, not silently converted into ordinary jinshi success or officeholding.
