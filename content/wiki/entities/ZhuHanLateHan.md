# 朱瀚 / Zhu Han (late Han)

朱瀚 / Zhu Han (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] as a once-low-ranking subordinate of [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] who gains position under [[YuanShao|袁绍]]. The source says Zhu Han uses that new authority to surround Han Fu's household, drive Han Fu into hiding, and break the leg of Han Fu's eldest son.

Zhu Han's importance is diagnostic. His revenge shows why Han Fu's post-Jizhou life becomes unsafe even before Yuan Shao personally moves against him: a displaced officeholder is now exposed to old subordinates, local resentments, and the new ruler's discipline. Yuan Shao executes Zhu Han the same day, so the case also shows Yuan Shao policing unauthorized violence while benefiting from the broader transfer of power.
