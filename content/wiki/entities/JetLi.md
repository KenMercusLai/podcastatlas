## Overview

李连杰 / Jet Li is the performer of [[ZhangJunbaoTaiChiMaster|张君宝]] in [[TaiChiMaster1993|《太极张三丰》]], bounded here to the episode's discussion of his movement and screen presence in that film.

## Current Profile

The hosts value Li's tai chi performance for looking expansive, controlled, and powerful without losing softness. His physical clarity helps translate yielding, circularity, and redirection into visible principles, while his restrained Junbao leaves more of the dramatic volatility to [[ChinSiuHo|钱小豪]]'s Tianbao. This is a narrow performance profile rather than a career survey.

## Key Characteristics

- Makes tai chi principles legible through controlled, flowing movement.
- Anchors the film's heroic development with a relatively restrained performance.
- Supports action that communicates learning and tactical adjustment.
- Functions in the episode as a major point of childhood wuxia memory.
