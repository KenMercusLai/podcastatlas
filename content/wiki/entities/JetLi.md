## Overview

李连杰 / Jet Li is a martial-arts performer bounded here to [[ZhangJunbaoTaiChiMaster|张君宝]] in [[TaiChiMaster1993|《太极张三丰》]] and the rewritten 张无忌 in [[KungFuCultMaster1993|《倚天屠龙记之魔教教主》]].

## Current Profile

The hosts value Li's tai chi performance for looking expansive, controlled, and powerful without losing softness. His physical clarity helps translate yielding, circularity, and redirection into visible principles, while his restrained Junbao leaves more of the dramatic volatility to [[ChinSiuHo|钱小豪]]'s Tianbao.

In [[KungFuCultMaster1993|《倚天屠龙记之魔教教主》]], he anchors a more suspicious, revenge-driven, and ambitious Zhang Wuji than the novel's protagonist. The episode's main judgment concerns the screenplay rather than Li's execution: the new personality is legible, but later inherited peacemaking and restraint are not fully rebuilt around it. This remains a two-film performance profile rather than a career survey.

## Key Characteristics

- Makes tai chi principles legible through controlled, flowing movement.
- Anchors the film's heroic development with a relatively restrained performance.
- Supports action that communicates learning and tactical adjustment.
- Functions in the episode as a major point of childhood wuxia memory.
- Anchors a commercially streamlined Zhang Wuji whose altered motive exposes screenplay continuity problems.
