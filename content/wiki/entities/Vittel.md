# Vittel

Vittel is the French mineral-water brand used in [[bali-shui-he-shengpeilu-hai-neng-zhuanqian-quechao-weihe-yao-boli-shui-yewu-1005452453]] to mark [[Nestle]]'s entry into the bottled-water business. The source says Nestle formally entered the category in 1969 through a stake in Vittel.

In the episode's history, Vittel is the starting point for a broader water portfolio that later included [[Perrier]], [[SanPellegrino]], [[NestlePureLife]], and other brands. The page is therefore a portfolio-history node rather than a standalone brand-analysis page.
