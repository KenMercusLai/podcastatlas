# 《阿瑟·萨维尔勋爵的罪行》 / Lord Arthur Savile's Crime

[[LordArthurSavilesCrime|《阿瑟·萨维尔勋爵的罪行》]] is the [[OscarWilde|Wilde]] story that [[10-wang-erde-qiyi-gushiji-yong-zui-hui-sahuang-de-zuichun-qinwen-tianshi-545332195]] uses as the clearest case of [[BlackComicMoralInversion]]. A palm reader tells Arthur that he is destined to murder someone, and Arthur reacts as a dutiful gentleman: before marriage, he tries to complete the crime so his future wife and family will not be harmed by a delayed scandal.

The episode emphasizes the story's absurd rationality. Arthur is not written as a passionate villain; he is comically organized, courteous, and responsible while pursuing an immoral act. That makes the story a useful Wilde case for [[ArtAgainstMoralInstruction]]: the fiction does not preach murder, but it exposes how respectable social duty can become ridiculous when moral language is mechanically obeyed.
