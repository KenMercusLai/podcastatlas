# Alok Jha

Alok Jha appears in [[i-robot-ai-and-consciousness-6a880840b0d5e24ea40c7015]] as the science voice explaining why the AI-consciousness question is harder than judging chatbot output. He distinguishes [[PhenomenalAccessConsciousness|phenomenal consciousness and access consciousness]], then uses [[ELIZA]], [[BlakeLemoine]], and modern systems such as [[Claude]], [[ChatGPT]], and [[Gemini]] to separate imitation from evidence of inner experience.

His source position is cautious but not dismissive. Jha says current LLMs are not considered conscious, yet the door cannot be closed if future systems become more sophisticated, more brain-like, or connected to [[OrganoidComputing|biological computing]].
