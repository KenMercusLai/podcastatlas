# 《所以》 / Suoyi

## Overview
《所以》 / Suoyi is [[LuoDayou|罗大佑]]'s 2026 song that [[RenNing]] reads beside [[BecauseSong|"Because"]] and [[TheOdyssey]] as a modern text about consequence, technical power, and return.

## Current Profile
The source treats 《所以》 as a song whose title word does too much to stay inside formal logic. "所以" can point toward a conclusion and toward a cause or ground; Luo's repeated use of the word keeps moving from image to image without completing a neat argument.

The song's world is dense with modern mediators: sea level, satellites, GPT, AI, big data, chips, outer space, capital, nets, capture, war, and calculation. That density matters because the subject is no longer only a self affected by world, wind, and sky. Human beings now act back on the world through systems, and the song's command not to leave the scene after doing harm becomes the episode's ethical hinge.

## Key Characteristics
- Repeats "所以" as a forward-moving poetic connector rather than a solved conclusion.
- Fills the lyric world with technological, climatic, financial, and geopolitical mediators.
- Makes human action visible as world-shaping rather than only world-receiving.
- Turns "行凶后别逃离现场" into an open-address ethical command.
- Reaches back toward true feeling, mother tongue, home, and companionship without returning to an unchanged home.

## Evidence
- Release and tribute context: [[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] says the song went online on 2026-07-10 and reports Luo's tribute to "Because."
- Repeated connector: [[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] describes the song as moving forward almost every step through "所以."
- Modern density: [[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] names satellites, GPT, AI, big data, chips, outer space, climate, and other system images as part of the song's world.
- Ethical command: [[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] uses "行凶后别逃离现场" to ask who must stay with the consequences of technology, capital, violence, or creation.
- Return movement: [[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] reads the ending's true feeling, mother tongue, home, and sunset as a modern Odyssey-like return.
