# Beijing-Shanghai High-Speed Railway / 京沪高铁

The Beijing-Shanghai High-Speed Railway is the strategic trunk project in [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]]. The episode says the corridor had unusually high population, GDP, passenger density, and capacity pressure, making it the natural site for early high-speed-rail planning and debate.

Its wiki role is to ground [[ChineseHighSpeedRail]] in demand rather than technological nationalism alone. The source says the project was repeatedly delayed by investment, cost-benefit, and route-choice disputes, then later became one of the clearest examples of high-speed rail's ability to absorb demand and eventually reach profitability.
