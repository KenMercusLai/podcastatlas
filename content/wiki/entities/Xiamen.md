# Xiamen / 厦门

Xiamen appears in [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046]] as one of China's first four special economic zones. The episode groups it with [[Zhuhai]] and [[ShantouSpecialEconomicZone]] as a more export-processing-oriented zone, while [[Shenzhen]] is described as a broader comprehensive special zone.

The page also connects the Chaoshan source to the wiki's existing Fujian maritime branch. Xiamen's role in the episode is not a full Minnan history, but it sits near [[Quanzhou]], [[MinnanMaritimeCommercialCulture]], and [[QiaopiRemittanceNetworks]] as another coastal node shaped by diaspora and external-facing policy.
