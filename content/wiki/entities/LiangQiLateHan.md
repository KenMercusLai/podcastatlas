## Overview
[[LiangQiLateHan]] is the She county chief whose surrender to [[CaoCao]] is paired with [[HanFanLateHan]] in [[XuHuangCaoGeneral]]'s argument for visible reward.

## Current Profile
In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1061-yuanshao-zhizi-he-shafu-chouren-jiecheng-qinjia-lnsnj6ipiyscw-vopnjjgoxpnt5r|Hanji 1061]], Liang Qi surrenders his county city during Cao Cao's Ye campaign. Xu Huang argues that because the Yuan brothers have not yet been eliminated and many counties are watching, Cao should reward Liang Qi and Han Fan, leading Cao to grant them Guannei Hou titles.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a county-level surrender case during Cao Cao's campaign against the Yuan position.
- Helps turn local submission into a public signal for other counties.
- Demonstrates how reward policy can function as operational pressure.

## Evidence
- Surrender and reward: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1061-yuanshao-zhizi-he-shafu-chouren-jiecheng-qinjia-lnsnj6ipiyscw-vopnjjgoxpnt5r|Hanji 1061]] says Liang Qi and Han Fan surrender county cities and receive reward after Xu Huang's advice.

## Qualifications
The current evidence does not develop Liang Qi's independent political history. His profile is limited to the surrender-reward episode.
