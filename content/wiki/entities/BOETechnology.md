# BOE Technology / 京东方

BOE Technology / 京东方 is the display-panel company used in [[no-211-huishang-wangshi-cong-qianshi-buxiu-dao-zuiniu-fengtou-1002275189]] as the classic [[HefeiIndustrialInvestmentModel]] case. The episode says Hefei backed BOE's 6th-generation LCD line when the project size was enormous relative to the city's fiscal resources.

The source frames the project as a structured risk-sharing deal rather than a simple subsidy. Hefei combined equity participation, strategic investors, bank lending, land, power infrastructure, and loan support, and ultimately benefited when market conditions made the financing less costly than the worst-case exposure.

BOE's role matters because it connects local industrial policy to upstream needs in television and home appliances. The episode treats the 2010 mass production of Hefei's 6th-generation line as a turning point in domestic large-screen LCD capability.
