# 李重进 / Li Chongjin

Li Chongjin / 李重进 is a senior [[LaterZhou|后周]] military figure in [[169-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-shang-957052909]]. The episode identifies him as [[GuoWei|郭威]]'s nephew and one of the commanders whose status and forces made the succession field dangerous.

The source discusses one hypothesis that Li Chongjin may have used the "点检做天子" sign to harm [[ZhangYongde|张永德]]. It also presents the rival hypothesis that [[ZhaoKuangyin|赵匡胤]] benefited too neatly to be ignored. Li Chongjin therefore functions less as a resolved conspirator than as part of the episode's evidence discipline around political rumor.
