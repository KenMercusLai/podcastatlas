# AGI House

AGI House is the AI-founder-focused hacker-house case in [[tanfang-hacker-house-guigu-nianqingren-zhengzai-banjin-ai-chuangye-sushe-s10e10-f0870843-fd16-49f5-8f24-80281f9fff77]]. The episode places it in a wealthy Silicon Valley residential setting, says it has its own VC fund, and describes it as able to offer AI founders up to roughly $1 million in investment.

The source uses AGI House to show both the appeal and fragility of [[EquityHackerHouseModel]]. It is more strictly protective of founder space than [[TheResidency]] by reportedly keeping investors out before Demo Day. The episode also notes that two AGI House co-founders split in 2023, leading to two AGI House projects and a trademark dispute, so the page records the source's claim as a governance and brand-control risk rather than settled external fact.
