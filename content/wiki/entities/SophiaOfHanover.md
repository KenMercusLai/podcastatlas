# Sophia of Hanover

## Overview

Sophia of Hanover is the Protestant dynastic link through whom the source connects the Thirty Years War generation to the Hanoverian succession in Britain.

## Current Profile

[[67-anglo-german-relations-glt1735608678]] places Sophia inside a family created by English-German Protestant politics. She was the daughter of Elizabeth Stuart and Frederick, Elector Palatine, whose failed Bohemian kingship and exile attracted English Protestant sympathy; her brother Prince Rupert later fought for the royalists in the English Civil War. Sophia's decisive historical role in the episode is succession: her line supplied the Protestant claimant who became George I in 1714, converting religious and dynastic connection into a German-linked British monarchy.

## Key Characteristics

- Daughter of Elizabeth Stuart and Frederick, Elector Palatine.
- Member of a family shaped by the Thirty Years War and English Protestant sympathy.
- Sister of the royalist commander Prince Rupert.
- Dynastic bridge from the Stuart family to the Hanoverian monarchy.
- Central figure in the Protestant succession that brought George I to Britain.
