# Union Square Hospitality Group

## Overview

Union Square Hospitality Group is the restaurant and hospitality organization founded by [[DannyMeyer]]. The episode associates it with restaurants including Union Square Cafe, Gramercy Tavern, and The Modern, as well as Meyer's role in [[ShakeShack]].

## Current Profile

The source uses the group primarily as the operating context for Meyer's culture philosophy. Its stated model replaces a vague family metaphor with explicit expected behaviors, asks management to create conditions for warmth and competence, and uses a day-one mentality to preserve opening-level pride and care in mature teams.

## Key Characteristics
- Multi-restaurant hospitality organization founded by Danny Meyer.
- Uses expected behaviors as the practical language of culture.
- Places responsibility for service climate primarily on management.
- Treats employee competence and enjoyment of one another as visible customer signals.
- Uses a day-one mentality to resist normalization of declining standards.

## Evidence
- Organization and leadership: [[advice-line-with-danny-meyer-of-shake-shack-c7199e93-5dba-43ab-8fb0-91403917c3ac]] identifies Meyer as founder and executive chairman and describes his present focus.
- Culture system: [[advice-line-with-danny-meyer-of-shake-shack-c7199e93-5dba-43ab-8fb0-91403917c3ac]] reports the expected-behavior formulation and the move away from family language.
- Service practice: [[advice-line-with-danny-meyer-of-shake-shack-c7199e93-5dba-43ab-8fb0-91403917c3ac]] describes warmth between employees and the day-one mentality as observable hospitality practices.

## Qualifications

The episode provides a leadership account of the culture system, not an independent employee or customer audit. It does not establish how uniformly these principles operate across every venue or period.

## What Changed
- Established the group as the organizational setting for Meyer's expected-behavior and day-one culture practices.
