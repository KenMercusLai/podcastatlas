# 赵盾 / Zhao Dun

赵盾 / Zhao Dun enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-2-lrlulw0b6v0fb2xpr2i5ifem1h4n]] as the first figure in the episode's four-dream reading of the [[Shiji|《史记》]] Zhao lineage.

The episode does not give him a full biography. Its relevant claim is structural: Zhao Dun's "先悲后喜" dream is read as the opening dream case in which Zhao-family danger and later recovery are foreshadowed before the later Zhao line continues through [[ZhaoJianzi|赵简子]], [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]], and [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]].
