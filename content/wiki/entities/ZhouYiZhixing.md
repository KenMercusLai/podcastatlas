# 周一 / Zhou Yi (知行小酒馆)

## Overview
周一 is a young-career [[ZhixingXiaojiuguan|知行小酒馆]] speaker whose E252 experience makes the behavioral and mobility costs of housing-finance choices concrete.

## Current Profile
In the bounded evidence, 周一 does not treat home ownership as a current life objective and resists long debt that could limit movement across cities or neighborhoods. Her Beijing rental withdrawal initially produced monthly cash, but the money was not reliably separated from daily spending. After learning of the source-reported account yield, she paused the withdrawal and reclassified the balance as forced saving.

Her case is not offered as a rule that everyone should retain funds. It shows that a theoretically higher-return withdrawal can fail in practice when account routing and spending habits do not preserve the intended purpose.
