# George Lusk

## Overview

George Lusk led the Mile End Vigilance Committee during the Whitechapel murders and received the “From Hell” letter with half of a human kidney in October 1888. His role connects citizen patrols and reward advocacy to the uncertain provenance of supposed killer communications.

## Current Profile

[[626-jack-the-ripper-the-killer-strikes-again-part-3-glt7980605076]] presents Lusk as the recipient of the most difficult major Ripper letter to dismiss outright. Doctors judged the kidney human, but no evidence tied it to [[CatherineEddowes|Catherine Eddowes]], and the handwriting differed from the “Dear Boss” letter and “Saucy Jacky” postcard.

His committee organized local patrols with unemployed men carrying whistles, sticks, and galoshes. The episode suggests that such patrols may have deterred attacks temporarily, but it does not establish that they changed the killer's behavior or caused a later move indoors.

## Key Characteristics

- Leader of the Mile End Vigilance Committee.
- Organizer associated with citizen patrols and pressure for stronger action.
- Recipient of the “From Hell” letter and a human kidney fragment.
- Central to an evidence trail that remains unresolved rather than authenticated.

## Qualifications

The source does not authenticate the letter, establish the kidney's origin, or prove the sender's identity. The committee's effect on crime, public fear, or later police tactics remains uncertain, and citizen patrols should not be assumed equivalent to accountable professional policing.
