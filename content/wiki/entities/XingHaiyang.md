# 邢海洋 / Xing Haiyang

[[ep267-tafang-meigonghe-yuji-hunfeiyi-yu-shuili-diguo-lkq2vxaatn4wxue7y5q1fc3-8yfk]] adds Xing Haiyang as the [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] senior writer who explains why he turned from domestic Chinese geography writing toward the [[MekongRiver|湄公河]] and [[MainlandSoutheastAsia|中南半岛]]. His role is to connect travel scenes, maps, climate, religious practices, historical sources, and water infrastructure into one geographic narrative.

The source presents his method as neither guidebook tourism nor abstract regional history. Finding hotels, solving local app problems, entering temples, joining an engagement ritual, seeing mating termites, riding the [[ChinaLaosRailway|中老铁路]], and later visiting [[Angkor|吴哥]] all become evidence for [[MekongRiverGeographicWriting]] and [[FieldworkAsKnowledgeMethod]].
