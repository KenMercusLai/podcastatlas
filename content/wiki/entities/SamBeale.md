# Sam Beale

## Overview
Sam Beale appears in the wiki as the [[TheIntelligence]] correspondent explaining the persistence and repurposing of America's post-9/11 counterterrorism powers.

## Current Profile
In [[out-numbered-ais-contentious-maths-milestone-6aa3ce622e8bb8424c920cfb]], Beale distinguishes overseas counterterrorism authorities from domestic security tools. His segment argues that the original jihadist threat has receded while the legal machinery created after 9/11 remains available for new uses.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains the post-9/11 security architecture as a mix of overseas war authority and domestic legal powers.
- Frames the 2001 AUMF, DHS, the Patriot Act, surveillance authority, and terrorist-financing statutes as durable institutional tools.
- Treats recent repurposing of terrorism categories as a democratic and civil-liberties concern.
