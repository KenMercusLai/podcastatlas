# Tahiti

## Overview

Tahiti is the Polynesian island where the Endeavour expedition established its astronomical post, observed the transit of Venus, and confronted the impossibility of separating scientific presence from social change.

## Current Profile

The source rejects an empty-island or untouched-Arcadia frame. Tahiti has its own political history and prior European contact, and Tahitians actively shape exchange around iron, sex, objects, status, and information. European journals nevertheless filter the encounter through expectations about paradise, sexuality, theft, and hierarchy.

Tahiti is also the point from which [[Tupia]] joins the voyage. His later translation work makes the island part of a wider Polynesian linguistic and navigational world rather than an isolated stop on a British route.

## Key Characteristics

- Established Polynesian society with history and agency before Cook's arrival.
- Astronomical site for the June 1769 transit-of-Venus observation.
- Exchange economy transformed by European demand and Tahitian demand for iron and nails.
- Contact zone marked by sex, disease risk, theft, punishment, killing, curiosity, and mutual learning.
- Departure point for Tupia's voyage and the expedition's recognition of wider Polynesian connections.

## Evidence

- Scientific mission: [[381-captain-cook-to-the-ends-of-the-earth-part-2-glt6950185057]] describes the observatory and successful transit measurement.
- Trade and disruption: [[381-captain-cook-to-the-ends-of-the-earth-part-2-glt6950185057]] records iron and nail demand, crew theft from stores, sexual exchange, and Cook's fear of venereal disease.
- Violence and discipline: [[381-captain-cook-to-the-ends-of-the-earth-part-2-glt6950185057]] recounts a Tahitian man's killing after a musket theft and punishment of a British butcher who threatened a woman.
- Cultural interpretation: [[381-captain-cook-to-the-ends-of-the-earth-part-2-glt6950185057]] uses Banks's notes on surfing, tattooing, language, and bodily display to preserve valuable observations while exposing European projection.

## Qualifications

The profile is dominated by British journals and a later British podcast. Tahitian meanings, political structures, women's perspectives, and evaluations of the visitors remain incomplete and should not be inferred directly from Banks or Cook.

## What Changed

- Created a Tahiti profile centered on local agency, scientific encounter, exchange, disruption, and mediated interpretation.
