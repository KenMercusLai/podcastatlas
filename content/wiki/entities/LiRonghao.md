# 李荣浩 / Li Ronghao

## Overview
李荣浩 / Li Ronghao is a singer-songwriter, instrumentalist, arranger, mixer, and live performer represented through a [[TianzhenButianzhen|天真不天真]] conversation about credible expression, technical craft, selective attention, audience time horizons, and AI music.

## Current Profile
Li presents musical authorship as a joined practice of lived observation and intensive technical control. He records concrete moments when they occur, writes from experiences he can believe, and then spends long periods arranging, mixing, practicing, testing playback systems, and resolving minute relationships among sounds. The resulting profile is neither spontaneous inspiration without craft nor technical polish without motive: credibility depends on both.

His current work pattern is deliberately narrow. Television and gala appearances have largely given way to albums, mixing, concerts, and solitary interests, reflecting [[CreativeFocusBySubtraction]]. He accepts that personal judgment can be incomplete without surrendering it to short release windows, and he treats live audience singing as the closing loop between private creation and shared meaning. On AI, he recognizes fast functional quality and labor pressure while retaining uncertainty about whether standardized efficiency can produce more durable work.

## Key Characteristics
- He grounds songwriting in concrete lived detail and material he personally believes.
- He combines composition with arrangement, mixing, instrumental practice, and repeated playback testing.
- His production style favors restraint and contextual balance over maximizing every individual part.
- He protects attention by refusing activities that do not compete with albums, technical work, and concerts.
- He treats market response as delayed evidence rather than an immediate verdict on artistic worth.
- He regards singing as ordinary emotional expression as well as a professional craft.
