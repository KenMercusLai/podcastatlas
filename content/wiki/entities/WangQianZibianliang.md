# 王前 / Wang Qian

## Overview
Wang Qian is the [[Zibianliang]] speaker in [[dang-jushen-zhineng-zoudao-shizilukou-duitan-sudu-mayi-lingbo-zibianliang-poke-sizhong-yixian-panduan-ls6w3mrjjrecuqg33yc3lbdmdzpf]]. He uses the episode to argue that embodied intelligence should be judged by data, validation, physical infrastructure, and customer payment rather than by a simple contest between robotics startups and general foundation-model labs.

## Current Profile
Wang's most distinctive claim is that intelligence lives in data: models distill and carry intelligence, but the decisive asset is the data and validation system behind them. He therefore treats the arrival of Astra-style models as important but not terminal for robotics companies, because physical action still requires robot data, simulators, hardware, and real-world testing.

## Key Characteristics
- Emphasizes data and validation infrastructure over model-brand competition.
- Argues that general model labs entering robotics inherit the same physical-world work as robotics startups.
- Uses sustained customer payment as the strongest commercialization signal.
- Treats logistics ROI as an example of robotics value becoming economically legible.
- Connects embodied intelligence to AGI's dependence on physical production and infrastructure.

## Qualifications
The source does not independently verify Wang's logistics ROI claim, company customer base, or technical implementation details. His strong long-run production claim should be treated as a strategic thesis, not a measured forecast.
