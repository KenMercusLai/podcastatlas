# 袁放 / Yuan Fang (HYROX)

袁放 / Yuan Fang appears in [[shangye-xiaoyang-47-jianshen-zuotijia-de-xinhuan-hyrox-daodi-you-shenme-huchenghe-1008179335]] as the China-region HYROX lead cited by the hosts. The episode uses Yuan Fang's comments to show that rights protection in China is not abstract: the local team has to communicate with gyms that run HYROX-style training and move them toward authorization.

His source-scoped role is to connect [[HYROX]]'s legal brand protection to the operating layer of [[AuthorizedFitnessTrainingNetwork]]. The gym network matters because official training, community events, and priority race access can make affiliation more attractive than simply copying the workout.
