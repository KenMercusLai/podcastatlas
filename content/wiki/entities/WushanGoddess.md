# 巫山神女 / Wushan Goddess

巫山神女 / Wushan Goddess enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-92-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhuming-tiangou-shi-shui-2-lt1nwraiuuvpy2tzo-zwaoqsajzx]] as the mythic figure who turns the Chu kings' political afterlife into literary memory. The episode retells the tradition that she is 瑶姬, a daughter of the Yan Emperor who dies young and becomes the cloud-and-rain deity of Wushan.

In the episode's story, [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] dreams of the goddess at Gaotang near Yunmengze. She describes herself through the morning-cloud and evening-rain image, and the king later builds a "朝云" shrine nearby. This makes her the source's explanation for the "朝云暮雨" and Wushan cloud-rain imagery.

The later scene belongs to [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]]. After [[SongYu|宋玉]] tells him the earlier story, Mi Heng sleeps at Gaotang hoping for the same encounter. The goddess appears in his dream but remains unreachable, and the episode reads that refusal as the basis for [[XiangwangYouyiShennvWuxin|襄王有意，神女无心]]: a ruler's desire does not make the desired figure reciprocate.
