## Overview

杨终 / Yang Zhong is the Zhang-era scholar-official whose current profile now joins frontier-policy remonstrance, classics consensus work, and household-risk warning. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-746-shenmeyang-de-lingdao-zhide-bei-sixintata-zhuisui-lvhbdgfbc69t6sdw92k4dipii-rx|Hanji 746]] adds his argument against continuing costly Northern Xiongnu and Western Regions policy, [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-750-jiemi-donghan-wanmei-nvxing-beihou-manshi-xisheng-lu-96lbdh7r0tsmiopbqqrj0q1gt|Hanji 750]] makes him the proposer behind the [[BaihuGuanConference|Baihu Guan Conference]], and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-752-shiqu-kaoshan-de-ren-gai-ruhe-chushi-lt0qb7alyh0ipf6mk8hvz92cs297|Hanji 752]] uses his letter to [[MaLiaoEasternHan|Ma Liao]] as the warning voice for the exposed [[MaFamilyZhangEraEasternHan|Ma family]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 746 gives Yang Zhong a policy-correction role. He argues that [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Zhangdi]] should not treat continued Northern Xiongnu campaigning and Western Regions garrisoning as filial loyalty to Mingdi if those inherited policies impose repeated labor and military burden on the people. When other ministers defend continuity with the prior emperor's policy, Yang answers with historical cases where sons should not continue harmful precedents. Zhangdi accepts his view.

Hanji 750 then gives Yang Zhong an institutional role. He argues that Zhangdi should imitate Emperor Xuan's Shiquge precedent and convene a court discussion of the Five Classics because scholars have become too attached to annotations and have lost the texts' main meaning. Zhangdi's response becomes the Baihu Guan conference, where officials and scholars debate classics differences and produce a recorded proceeding.

Hanji 752 gives Yang Zhong a family-risk role. He writes to Ma Liao because Ma's position is nationally visible, while younger Ma relatives are energetic, proud, and surrounded by reckless guests. His warning does not accuse one isolated fault; it diagnoses a household trajectory in which a protected family continues to behave as if imperial favor is permanent. The advice is sound but ineffective: Ma Liao does not sufficiently correct the household, and Zhangdi later restricts the Ma family before officials impeach Ma Fang and Ma Guang.

## Key Characteristics

- Zhangdi-era remonstrant who separates filial respect for a predecessor from harmful policy continuation.
- Frontier-policy critic who foregrounds the people's repeated labor and military burden.
- Eastern Han scholar-official who turns classics disagreement into an imperial agenda item.
- Proposer whose advice helps produce the Baihu Guan conference.
- Diagnoses young relatives and reckless guests as family-level political risk.
- Frames high rank as an obligation to become more restrained, not more visible.
- Early-warning figure whose advice can be accepted by Zhangdi in policy form but still fail inside Ma-family household discipline.

## Qualifications

Hanji 746 renders the name as 杨忠 in the supplied source, while Hanji 752 previously rendered it as 杨宗; the wiki normalizes both to 杨终 / Yang Zhong because the Zhangdi-era roles and existing canonical route match. The current page now has three bounded functions, but it still does not reconstruct Yang Zhong's broader scholarship, offices, or later life.
