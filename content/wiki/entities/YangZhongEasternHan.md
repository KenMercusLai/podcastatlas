## Overview

杨终 / Yang Zhong enters the wiki as the Zhang-era scholar-official whose letter warns [[MaLiaoEasternHan|Ma Liao]] that the [[MaFamilyZhangEraEasternHan|Ma family]] is exposed after losing its main protection. Hanji 752 uses him as the voice that turns a family scandal into a general survival rule: high status without discipline makes younger relatives, guests, and display dangerous.

## Current Profile

Yang Zhong's current bounded role is advisory. He writes to Ma Liao because Ma's position is nationally visible, while the younger Ma relatives are energetic, proud, and surrounded by reckless guests. His warning does not accuse one isolated fault; it diagnoses a household trajectory in which a protected family continues to behave as if imperial favor is permanent.

The episode treats the advice as sound but ineffective. Ma Liao does not sufficiently correct the household, and Zhangdi later restricts the Ma family before officials impeach Ma Fang and Ma Guang. Yang Zhong therefore functions as an early-warning figure: he sees the risk before the formal punishment arrives, but the warning does not convert into enough household restraint.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han scholar-official whose current wiki role is a warning letter to Ma Liao.
- Diagnoses young relatives and reckless guests as family-level political risk.
- Frames high rank as an obligation to become more restrained, not more visible.
- Early-warning figure whose advice is validated by later Ma-family decline.
