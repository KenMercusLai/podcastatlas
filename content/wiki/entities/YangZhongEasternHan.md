## Overview

杨终 / Yang Zhong is the Zhang-era scholar-official whose current profile joins classics policy and household warning. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-750-jiemi-donghan-wanmei-nvxing-beihou-manshi-xisheng-lu-96lbdh7r0tsmiopbqqrj0q1gt|Hanji 750]] makes him the proposer behind the [[BaihuGuanConference|Baihu Guan Conference]], while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-752-shiqu-kaoshan-de-ren-gai-ruhe-chushi-lt0qb7alyh0ipf6mk8hvz92cs297|Hanji 752]] uses his letter to [[MaLiaoEasternHan|Ma Liao]] as the warning voice for the exposed [[MaFamilyZhangEraEasternHan|Ma family]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 750 gives Yang Zhong an institutional role. He argues that [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Zhangdi]] should imitate Emperor Xuan's Shiquge precedent and convene a court discussion of the Five Classics because scholars have become too attached to annotations and have lost the texts' main meaning. Zhangdi's response becomes the Baihu Guan conference, where officials and scholars debate classics differences and produce a recorded proceeding.

Hanji 752 gives Yang Zhong a family-risk role. He writes to Ma Liao because Ma's position is nationally visible, while younger Ma relatives are energetic, proud, and surrounded by reckless guests. His warning does not accuse one isolated fault; it diagnoses a household trajectory in which a protected family continues to behave as if imperial favor is permanent. The advice is sound but ineffective: Ma Liao does not sufficiently correct the household, and Zhangdi later restricts the Ma family before officials impeach Ma Fang and Ma Guang.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han scholar-official who turns classics disagreement into an imperial agenda item.
- Proposer whose advice helps produce the Baihu Guan conference.
- Diagnoses young relatives and reckless guests as family-level political risk.
- Frames high rank as an obligation to become more restrained, not more visible.
- Early-warning figure whose advice is validated by later Ma-family decline.
- Source-bounded person whose wider works and offices remain only partially developed here.

## Qualifications

The source's transcript writes 杨宗 in the Hanji 752 warning context, but the Zhangdi-era figure is normalized here as 杨终 / Yang Zhong. The current page now has two bounded functions, classics proposal and household warning, but it still does not reconstruct Yang Zhong's broader scholarship, offices, or later life.

## What Changed
