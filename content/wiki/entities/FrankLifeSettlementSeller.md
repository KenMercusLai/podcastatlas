# Frank (life-settlement seller)

Frank appears in [[you-bet-your-life-insurance]] as the modern seller who makes the [[LifeSettlement|life settlement]] market concrete. He had $1.5 million in life insurance coverage, a history of rare stage-four lung cancer, remission through a new drug, and enough financial literacy to treat selling the policies as a spreadsheetable risk-return decision.

His case contrasts with the AIDS-crisis origin story. Unlike many early [[ViaticalSettlement|viatical settlement]] sellers, Frank is not presented as selling because he has no other way to survive; he can compare the bid, commissions, investment returns, family protection, and his remaining work-provided coverage.

## Key Claims
- Frank's cancer history increases the potential value of his policies to buyers because expected survival affects returns.
- [[EvergreenSettlements]] brokers the policies and [[CoventryLifeSettlements|Coventry]] offers $470,000 for both, with about $430,000 reaching Frank after commission.
- His spreadsheet frames the tradeoff as roughly needing 12% annual returns over 12 years to beat the $1.5 million death benefit.
- Selling gives Frank liquidity and optionality, but also creates the uncomfortable fact that another party now has a financial interest in his death.
