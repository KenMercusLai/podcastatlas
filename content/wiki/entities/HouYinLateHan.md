## Overview

侯音 / Hou Yin is a Nanyang garrison commander who, in the current source, detains the administrator [[DongliGunLateHan|东里衮]] after renewed levies and launches an improvised revolt that ends in his death and civilian catastrophe.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1163-gaogu-ziji-shibushi-zui-da-de-yuchun-lokzo7kcz69kf_lhgskx8kie-9_c|Hanji 1163]] presents Hou Yin's grievance as material: the region has already supplied grain, and further extraction threatens soldiers and residents. He surrounds Dongli Gun and recaptures him after an attempted escape, but has no clear next step once detention becomes open rebellion against [[CaoCao|曹操]].

Hou Yin then accepts [[ZongZiqingLateHan|宗子卿]]'s claim that releasing Dongli Gun will gain popular support. Zong uses Hou Yin's written authorization to free the captive and escape to [[CaoRen|曹仁]]. Cao Ren besieges the city, Hou Yin fails to break out and is killed, and the episode says the population is massacred afterward. His case therefore joins legitimate pressure, weak planning, susceptibility to manipulation, and consequences borne by people beyond the decision-maker.

## Key Characteristics

- Local commander responding to severe levy pressure.
- Converts resistance to extraction into detention of the commandery administrator.
- Enters open revolt without a durable political or military plan.
- Remains concerned with popular support but misjudges advice offered in that language.
- Dies during a failed breakout after Cao Ren's counterattack.

## Qualifications

The profile rests on one structured popular-history summary. Hou Yin's office, objectives, local support, force, planning, exact conduct toward Dongli Gun, death, and responsibility for the later civilian outcome require comparison with the historical texts. Calling him arrogant or foolish is the episode's moral framing; it should not erase coercive taxation or the asymmetry between local resistance and Cao-side force.
