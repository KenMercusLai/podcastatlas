# 曹洪 / Cao Hong

## Overview

曹洪 / Cao Hong is a Cao-family military supporter whose profile joins early resource contribution and life-saving battlefield loyalty with a later test of whether [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s legal order can constrain powerful kin.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1104 places Cao Hong in Cao Cao's first mobilization as a kinsman who contributes military resources, complementing the private funding attributed to [[WeiZiLateHan|卫兹]] and the organized manpower associated with [[XiahouDun|夏侯惇]] and [[CaoRen|曹仁]]. Hanji 953 then gives him the decisive rescue role after the [[BianshuiBattleLateHan|汴水之战]]: he yields his horse to the wounded Cao Cao and escorts him out, preserving the leader and therefore the wider political project after defeat.

Hanji 1006 adds the governance counterweight. Cao Hong's lawbreaking clients appeal through his kinship access, but [[ManChong|满宠]] executes them and Cao Cao backs the official. Cao Hong thus represents both the value and the danger of kin-organized power: family resources can make an early army possible, yet the resulting network must later be subjected to rules if it is not to become a protected privilege structure.

## Key Characteristics

- Early Cao-family contributor of military resources.
- Battlefield rescuer who prioritizes Cao Cao's survival over his own safety.
- Kinsman whose loyalty helps preserve the organization after its first major defeat.
- Patron whose clients later test the reach of official enforcement.
- Figure linking the productive and risky sides of kin-based capacity.

## Evidence

- Resource contribution: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1104-caocao-banbu-qiuxianling-xiyin-le-duoshao-dingji-rencai-lvrsdp13uv-80kzmwawnqwonkyny|Hanji 1104]] includes Cao Hong among the relatives who reduce Cao Cao's early mobilization cost.
- Rescue loyalty: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-953-luanshi-zhizhong-yaobuyao-jiangjiu-renyi-lrzfimgqatjxyw9zx9ry2kiwxkon|Hanji 953]] has Cao Hong give Cao Cao his horse and escort him out after Bianshui.
- Kinship-pressure boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1006-guojia-buman-yuanshao-weihe-que-neng-guishun-caocao-lup-p-inz1sdlhpnaggqjp0eavar|Hanji 1006]] uses his clients' execution to show Man Chong's enforcement and Cao Cao's support for discipline over kin protection.

## Qualifications

Hanji 1104 does not specify the type or amount of Cao Hong's early military resources. Hanji 1006 assesses a governance problem created by his clients rather than Cao Hong's complete conduct, so it should not erase the rescue evidence or support a total character judgment.
