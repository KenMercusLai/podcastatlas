# 潘鸿 / Pan Hong (late Han)

## Overview

潘鸿 / Pan Hong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-863-yi-ba-huo-shaochu-de-zhandouli-ljpfmj8pphunruioce64ywjk7yhb|Hanji 863]] as a [[GuiyangCommanderyLateHan|桂阳郡]] bandit leader paired with [[BuYangLateHan|卜阳]] in [[DuShangLateHan|度尚 / 杜尚]]'s 164 CE mountain campaign.

## Current Profile

Pan Hong's profile is source-scoped and paired. The episode presents him as part of the remaining Guiyang threat after Du Shang has won earlier victories and taken treasure from other rebels. Pan Hong and Bu Yang still hold enough wealth and terrain advantage for Du Shang to treat them as a serious follow-on target.

Pan Hong matters because the source uses the campaign against him to show a commander's manipulation of desire. After the soldiers' captured treasure burns, Du Shang points to the wealth still held by Pan Hong and Bu Yang, turning grief over loss into a demand to attack.

## Key Characteristics

- Guiyang bandit leader paired with [[BuYangLateHan|卜阳]].
- Mountain stronghold opponent whose refuge complicates immediate suppression.
- Remaining-wealth target after Du Shang's troops lose their first captured spoils.
- Defeated in the dawn assault that follows Du Shang's staged camp-fire shock.

- Mountain refuge: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-863-yi-ba-huo-shaochu-de-zhandouli-ljpfmj8pphunruioce64ywjk7yhb|Hanji 863]] says they plunder and hide in deep mountain terrain.
- Wealth incentive: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-863-yi-ba-huo-shaochu-de-zhandouli-ljpfmj8pphunruioce64ywjk7yhb|Hanji 863]] says Du Shang uses their accumulated wealth to redirect soldiers after the spoils fire.
- Defeat: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-863-yi-ba-huo-shaochu-de-zhandouli-ljpfmj8pphunruioce64ywjk7yhb|Hanji 863]] reports that Du Shang's dawn attack destroys the group when it is not on guard.
