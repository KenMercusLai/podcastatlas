# Gregory A. Victor

## Overview
Gregory A. Victor is identified in the source as a Glendale, Arizona fire inspector who later became fire marshal and sponsored an elevator-code amendment for longer emergency stretchers.

## Current Profile
Victor argued that elevators designed around six-foot-four-inch stretchers did not adequately fit equipment used in his jurisdiction. After a technical committee rejected a proposal tied to whatever stretcher a jurisdiction possessed, he returned with a standardized seven-foot requirement and an appeal centered on patients in medical emergencies.

The broader membership approved the revised proposal. The episode makes Victor the focal case for how a safety-motivated local concern can acquire national cost and accessibility consequences, but it does not establish his wider career, intentions beyond the proposal, or later assessment of the rule.

## Key Characteristics
- Fire-safety official concerned with fitting emergency stretchers into elevators.
- Revised an initially nonstandard proposal into a fixed seven-foot requirement.
- Used patient-emergency reasoning to support the amendment before the broader code membership.
- Reported no cost impact on the proposal form.
