# 王磬 / Wang Qing

## Overview
王磬 / Wang Qing is a 不合时宜 host represented here through interviews on autism and disability care and on poet Xiaohai's worker writing and bilingual memoir.

## Current Profile
Across the two represented episodes, Wang Qing moves from a medical-ethics case toward the systems and identities around it. In the autism conversation, she frames China's care gap, introduces [[XuYueAutismScholar|许悦]], and guides the discussion from the [[XiaoMeiGeneEditingCase]] into diagnosis, intervention, support systems, minority-family barriers, and respectful public language. In the conversation with [[XiaoHaiPoet|小海]] and [[TonyXiaoHaiTranslator|Tony]], she uses the memoir [[YiKuaiGunShiBook|《一块滚石》 / A Drift in the South]] to examine factory experience, artistic formation, translation, freedom, and the limits of “migrant worker poet” as a label.

## Key Characteristics
- Hosts a care-series episode that links intimate family pressure to public systems and medical ethics.
- Uses the Xiaomei case to ask why a child with a nonfatal developmental condition was pushed toward high-risk gene editing.
- Helps move the discussion from event outrage into autism concepts, intervention, and social support.
- Uses an author's life history and translation process to separate material labor context from reductive artistic labeling.
- Connects intimate first-person experience to broader care, publishing, and global-production structures.
