# Veronica Price / 维罗尼卡·普莱斯

Veronica Price / 维罗尼卡·普莱斯 appears in [[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] as an [[MI6]] figure central to keeping [[OperationPimlico]] actionable when [[OlegGordievsky|Oleg Gordievsky]] was trapped in Moscow. The episode presents her as the person who reminded the working group that the escape plan still existed after other officers saw little chance of rescue.

Price also appears on the Finnish side of the extraction, coordinating with British and Danish intelligence personnel before Gordievsky crossed the border. Her role makes [[SpySourceProtection]] practical: the service's promise to a source had to be preserved through memory, logistics, and willingness to act under diplomatic risk.
