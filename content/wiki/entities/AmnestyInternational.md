# Amnesty International

Amnesty International appears in [[for-bucks-sake-the-rise-of-self-made-billionaires-6a61e076562e3ad886d54c95]] as the monitoring organization whose count of [[Cambodia]] scam compounds is used to test whether the crackdown is working. The source says Amnesty counted 86 scam compounds in Cambodia in 2026, up from 53 a year earlier.

The page records Amnesty's role as evidence rather than as a full institutional profile. Its count supports the source's claim that scam compounds are adapting despite raids, sanctions, casino closures, and extradition pressure.
