## Overview
Rhea Jain is a Duke student and the founder of [[Voiceback]], an assistive voice-cloning and text-to-speech app.

## Current Profile
Jain built Voiceback from her grandmother's experience of speech loss from ALS and used that personal story in outreach to patients and clinicians. The source shows early initiative in securing a medical adviser and user advisory group, while also placing her at a transition point: free adoption must be distinguished from paid value, and awareness must be built through trusted communities without neglecting access, privacy, consent, or safety.

## Key Characteristics
- Built the product in response to a close family member's rejection of robotic voices.
- Launched and accumulated reported download volume before introducing payment.
- Conducts personalized outreach to clinicians and condition-specific communities.
- Recruited a medical adviser and a user advisory group with lived experience of speech loss.
- Is testing how to balance affordability, freemium access, and a sustainable paid product.
