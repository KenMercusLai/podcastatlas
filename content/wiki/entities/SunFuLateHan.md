# 孙辅 / Sun Fu (late Han)

## Overview
孙辅 / Sun Fu is a Sun-family administrator whose profile now runs from useful territorial staffing under [[SunCe|孙策]] to a dangerous kinship-insider case under [[SunQuan|孙权]]. He shows that family connection and earlier appointment do not automatically transfer into loyalty after succession.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1039 gives Sun Fu a constructive administrative role: after Sun Ce receives Hua Xin's surrender, Sun Fu is assigned to the newly separated [[LulingLateHan|庐陵]] commandery while [[SunBenLateHan|孙贲]] remains tied to [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章]]. Hanji 1053 sharply changes the profile. As Lujiang administrator, Sun Fu fears that Sun Quan cannot preserve Jiangdong and secretly asks [[CaoCao|曹操]] to send troops. A messenger reveals the plot to Sun Quan, who kills Sun Fu's intimates, breaks up his army, and moves Sun Fu eastward.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a Sun-family territorial appointee during Sun Ce's Yuzhang-Luling settlement.
- Moves from a family-staffed administrative asset into a succession-era internal risk.
- Misreads or distrusts Sun Quan's ability to hold Jiangdong and tries to invite Cao-side intervention.
- Is punished through isolation and command removal rather than recorded execution of Sun Fu himself.

## Evidence
- Luling appointment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] says Sun Ce makes Sun Fu administrator of Luling.
- Family-staffed administration: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] pairs Sun Fu's Luling role with Sun Ben's Yuzhang role after Hua Xin's surrender.
- Secret Cao-side appeal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1053-genzhe-sunquan-xuexi-ruhe-zhengdun-jituan-gaoguan-lq9hy25xonjzhj1sfisvauu3bwmi|Hanji 1053]] says Sun Fu, as Lujiang administrator, fears Sun Quan cannot hold Jiangdong and secretly asks Cao Cao to send troops.
- Punishment and neutralization: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1053-genzhe-sunquan-xuexi-ruhe-zhengdun-jituan-gaoguan-lq9hy25xonjzhj1sfisvauu3bwmi|Hanji 1053]] reports that Sun Quan kills Sun Fu's intimates, breaks up Sun Fu's army, and moves him east.

## Qualifications
Hanji 1053 gives Sun Fu's fear and secret appeal but not a full inner motive beyond distrust of Sun Quan's capacity. The source records punishment of his surrounding network and troops, not Sun Fu's execution. The page therefore treats him as a neutralized internal risk rather than a completed rebel commander.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1053, changing Sun Fu from a narrow Luling appointee into a succession-era kinship-insider threat.
- Clarified that Sun Quan's response isolates Sun Fu and removes his force without making Sun Fu himself the main executed target.

## Relationships
- [[SunCe|孙策]] - earlier appointing power behind Sun Fu's Luling role.
- [[SunQuan|孙权]] - successor whose capacity Sun Fu doubts and whose authority neutralizes him.
- [[SunBenLateHan|孙贲]] - paired Sun-family administrator in Yuzhang.
- [[LulingLateHan|庐陵]] - commandery Sun Fu is first assigned to administer.
- [[LujiangCommandery|庐江郡]] - later commandery setting for Sun Fu's secret Cao-side appeal.
- [[CaoCao|曹操]] - outside power whom Sun Fu tries to invite into Jiangdong's succession crisis.
- [[SuccessionAuthorityConsolidation|继位权威整合]] - concept explaining Sun Fu's case as an internal-loyalty test.
