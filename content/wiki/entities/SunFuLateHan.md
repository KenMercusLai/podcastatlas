# 孙辅 / Sun Fu (late Han)

## Overview
孙辅 / Sun Fu enters the wiki through Hanji 1039 as the Sun-family administrator assigned to the newly separated Luling commandery after Sun Ce receives Hua Xin's surrender.

## Current Profile
The current evidence gives Sun Fu a source-scoped administrative role. He helps show how [[SunCe|孙策]] converts surrender in [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]] into family-staffed territorial control by pairing [[SunBenLateHan|孙贲]] in Yuzhang with Sun Fu in [[LulingLateHan|庐陵]].

## Key Characteristics
- Assigned by Sun Ce as administrator of the newly created Luling commandery.
- Advances toward Da Luling when Tong Zhi is ill, showing active movement into a still-fragmented field.
- Paired with Sun Ben's Yuzhang appointment as part of Sun-family post-conquest administration.
