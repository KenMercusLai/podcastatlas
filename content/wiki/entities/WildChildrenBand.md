# 野孩子 / Wild Children

## Overview
野孩子 / Wild Children is represented as a Chinese independent folk band and working community whose disciplined rehearsal, Northwest musical grounding, informal care, and 合酒吧 venue gave [[ZhangWeiweiMusician|张玮玮]] a practical model for making music under material constraint.

## Current Profile
In Zhang's account, Wild Children mattered because the band was attainable rather than star-like. Metronome-led basement rehearsals showed durable craft; 小索 and 张佺 offered food, washing, conversation, instruction, and an entry into ensemble work; and 合酒吧 joined daytime rehearsal to nighttime livelihood before becoming a larger original-music meeting point. Zhang's accordion audition turned admiration into membership, while 小索's death, the band's interruption, and venue-ecology change later contributed to his creative disorientation.

## Key Characteristics
- Northwest-rooted independent band presented through disciplined, unglamorous rehearsal practice.
- Apprenticeship environment teaching musicians how to enter and serve an ensemble.
- Care network that joined artistic instruction to food, shelter-like support, conversation, and dignity.
- Venue-building community associated with 合酒吧 and a dense Beijing original-music scene.
- Fragile institution whose continuity depended on people, health, place, and a changing urban ecology.

## Qualifications
The page reflects one former member's retrospective account rather than a full band history. Membership chronology, catalog, internal perspectives, 合酒吧 operations, and the causes of each interruption require additional sources.

- Added venue-building and scene fragility to the profile rather than treating influence as musical style alone.
