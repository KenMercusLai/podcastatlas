# 《六韬》 / Six Secret Teachings

《六韬》 / Six Secret Teachings appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-30-suqin-de-kaigua-rensheng-lkmn-fruukdxgkddx7zb4lc3o9bw]] inside the episode's version discussion of [[YinfuJing|《阴符》]]. The source says one possible reading of [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]]'s 《太公阴符之谋》 points to the 《阴符》 chapter in this military text.

The host does not use that identification as the main explanation for [[SuQin|苏秦]]'s breakthrough. The episode says the 《六韬》 chapter is only around a hundred characters and mainly concerns military communication or signaling, which makes it less plausible as the basis for Su Qin's broader [[ChuaimoTechnique|揣摩]] method than the [[Shiji|《史记》]] label 《周书阴符》.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-1-guailaotou-yiqiao-san-shua-zhangliang-chuanshou-shenshu-lqwjo13zzfgtndpajzpuvrb2h4l2|Qinji 130-1]] gives the text a second role through [[ZhangLiang|张良]]. The episode identifies [[TaigongBingfa|《太公兵法》]] as 《六韬》 / 《太公六韬》 and describes it as six juan and sixty chapters, with strategic and tactical argument as its most important content. Here the text is not a disputed clue for Su Qin, but the book that moves Zhang Liang from failed assassin toward strategist.
