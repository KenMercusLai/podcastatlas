## Overview

《六韬》 / Six Secret Teachings is a military and statecraft text that the current wiki evidence links to the identities of 《太公兵法》 and 《太公阴符之谋》, to [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s strategist formation, and to a six-condition method for observing talent.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-30-suqin-de-kaigua-rensheng-lkmn-fruukdxgkddx7zb4lc3o9bw|Zhouji 30]] introduces the text through a source-identification dispute. The episode says 《战国策》's 《太公阴符之谋》 may refer to the short 《阴符》 chapter within 《六韬》, but treats that identification as less persuasive for [[SuQin|苏秦]] because its military-signaling focus does not fit the broader [[ChuaimoTechnique|揣摩]] method as well as 《史记》's label 《周书阴符》.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-1-guailaotou-yiqiao-san-shua-zhangliang-chuanshou-shenshu-lqwjo13zzfgtndpajzpuvrb2h4l2|Qinji 130-1]] gives the text a second role through Zhang Liang. The episode identifies [[TaigongBingfa|《太公兵法》]] as 《六韬》 / 《太公六韬》, describes it as six juan and sixty chapters, and treats strategic and tactical argument as its central value in Zhang Liang's movement from failed assassin toward counselor.

The personnel-governance branch comes from 《文韬·六守》 in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-214-tiemian-zhangshizhi-dui-huangdi-lan-taizi-2-lixkic0bkzjc0ehyupeolnqmuxs|Hanji 214 part 2]]. In the episode's rendering, wealth, honor, entrusted affairs, missions, danger, and practical tasks expose benevolence, righteousness, loyalty, trustworthiness, courage, and strategic ability. This extends the text beyond battlefield technique into [[SituationalCharacterTests|情境式识人考察]], while the wording, attribution to [[JiangTaigong|姜太公]], and historical dating remain source-scoped.

## Key Characteristics

- Military and statecraft text associated in the sources with Jiang Taigong and the title 《太公六韬》.
- One candidate, but not the preferred one in Zhouji 30, for the 《太公阴符之谋》 read by Su Qin.
- Text identified with the book Huangshi Gong gives Zhang Liang in the bridge story.
- Six-juan, sixty-chapter work in Qinji 130-1's account, with strategy and tactics presented as its strongest content.
- Source for a “六守” talent-assessment framework using conduct under six conditions rather than reputation alone.

## Evidence

Disputed 《阴符》 identification:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-30-suqin-de-kaigua-rensheng-lkmn-fruukdxgkddx7zb4lc3o9bw|Zhouji 30]] compares the 《六韬》 chapter with 《史记》's 《周书阴符》 and finds the latter more plausible for Su Qin's broad persuader method.

Zhang Liang strategist-formation text:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-1-guailaotou-yiqiao-san-shua-zhangliang-chuanshou-shenshu-lqwjo13zzfgtndpajzpuvrb2h4l2|Qinji 130-1]] identifies 《太公兵法》 with 《六韬》, reports its six-juan and sixty-chapter structure, and makes it the book received from Huangshi Gong.

Six-condition personnel assessment:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-214-tiemian-zhangshizhi-dui-huangdi-lan-taizi-2-lixkic0bkzjc0ehyupeolnqmuxs|Hanji 214 part 2]] attributes to 《文韬·六守》 tests involving wealth, honor, trust, missions, danger, and practical work, mapped respectively to 仁、义、忠、信、勇、谋.

## Qualifications

The three source notes are popular-history podcast retellings, not textual-critical studies of 《六韬》's composition, dating, chapter history, or attribution. Zhouji 30 explicitly rejects a simple equation between the short military-signaling chapter and the broader text Su Qin needs; Qinji 130-1's identification of Zhang Liang's book and Hanji 214 part 2's six-juan, chapter, wording, and Jiang Taigong claims require comparison with transmitted editions and scholarship. The “六守” conditions are evidence for an ancient personnel ideal, not validated modern selection instruments.
