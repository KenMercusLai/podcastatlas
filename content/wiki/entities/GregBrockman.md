# Greg Brockman

## Overview

Greg Brockman is an [[OpenAI]] leader whose bounded profile combines the November 2023 [[OpenAIBoardCrisis]] with a 2026 campaign-finance controversy involving [[LeadingTheFuture]].

## Current Profile

[[tsr-s4-samaltman-v4-tsr-s4-samaltman-v4]] presents Brockman as removed alongside [[SamAltman]] during the board crisis, making the episode a company-wide governance rupture rather than only a CEO transition. The newer [[MarketplaceTech]] source reports that Brockman and his wife withdrew a planned $25 million donation to Leading the Future after an earlier $25 million contribution, with Brockman calling the PAC a distraction at OpenAI. The sources do not provide Brockman's full accounts of either event.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior OpenAI figure implicated in the company's 2023 governance crisis.
- Reported major donor to an AI-linked political spending group.
- Publicly distanced himself from a planned second contribution when the PAC became a company distraction.
