# 毛文超 / Mao Wenchao

Mao Wenchao / 毛文超 is presented in [[no-214-xunzhao-tonglei-xiaohongshu-bilibili-yiji-wuhuabamen-de-naxie-shequ-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-26-1005977305]] as a cofounder of [[Xiaohongshu]]. The episode emphasizes his consulting, investment, and [[StanfordUniversity|Stanford]] MBA background as unusual for a Chinese internet founder profile more often associated with technical or grassroots origins.

The source links Mao to Xiaohongshu's early outbound shopping-guide idea, its move from PDF and PC forum into app-based notes, and later strategic commitment to community after self-operated ecommerce growth stalled.
