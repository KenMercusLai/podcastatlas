# Dan Dreyfus

Dan Dreyfus appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-dan-dreyfus-americas-critical-minerals-crisis-is-here-41594225]] as the [[AllIn|All-In]] guest arguing that the next economic cycle depends on physical infrastructure and constrained materials. His central claim is that AI data centers, reshoring, electrification, aerospace, defense, and power generation are all colliding with long mine timelines, old grid infrastructure, [[RareEarthProcessingBottleneck|rare-earth processing dependence]], and limited skilled labor.

Dreyfus's source role is investor and bottleneck mapper rather than neutral technical adjudicator. The page therefore keeps his copper-demand, power-shortage, and hard-asset return claims source-scoped while linking them to existing wiki frames for [[CriticalMineralsGeopolitics]], [[DataCenterPowerBottleneck]], [[StrategicIndustrialPolicy]], and [[CommodityTimeHorizonFramework]].
