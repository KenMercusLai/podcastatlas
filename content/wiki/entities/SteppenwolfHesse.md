# 《荒原狼》 / Steppenwolf

[[SteppenwolfHesse|《荒原狼》 / Steppenwolf]] appears in [[20-demian-gaiyin-de-etou-shang-na-shenmi-de-yinji-567302734]] as a key work in [[HermannHesse|Hermann Hesse / 赫尔曼·黑塞]]'s postwar American reception. The hosts mention an American professor who reportedly advised young people to read the book before taking psychedelics, using the anecdote to explain why Hesse became associated with counterculture.

The source does not close-read 《荒原狼》. Its function is reception history: it shows how Hesse's concern with fractured modern subjectivity could be pulled into [[HesseYouthCultureMisreading|youth-culture misreading]] and psychedelic self-experimentation, even when the episode argues that his deeper problem is self-knowledge under spiritual crisis.

## Key Claims
- 《荒原狼》 is used as a reception signal for Hesse's American countercultural afterlife.
- The episode treats that reception as partly understandable but not identical to Hesse's own literary purpose.
- The work sits beside [[DemianNovel|《德米安》]] and [[SiddharthaHesse|《悉达多》]] as part of Hesse's larger self-search canon.
