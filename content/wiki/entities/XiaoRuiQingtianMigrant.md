# 小瑞 / Xiaorui (Qingtian migrant)

## Overview
小瑞 / Xiaorui is the pseudonymous young woman at the center of a [[DuanwenNewsPodcast|端闻]] report on Qingtian children who grow up apart from migrant parents and later reunite with them in Europe.

## Current Profile
The source says Xiaorui lived apart from her parents for seventeen years and saw her mother only four times before age eighteen. During her final year of high school, she gave up the national university entrance examination and moved before the family-reunification residence window associated with minority closed. Her imagined European and family life quickly gave way to restaurant, milk-tea-shop, and bar work in Austria and Spain.

She adapted quickly: she combined English, German, and gesture with customer service, learned stock and cash operations, and ran a celebrity-card resale business. Yet work skill did not create secure belonging. Conflict over wages, shop help, health, paternal preference, and marriage introductions led her to keep her savings physically with her and imagine an independent home in Wenzhou with a cat.

## Key Characteristics
- Grew up in Qingtian through a seventeen-year parent-child separation sustained mainly by remote contact.
- Chose European reunification before adulthood over completing the gaokao route.
- Learned service, inventory, sales, language, and small-trading skills rapidly under difficult conditions.
- Resists treating family obligation as unlimited unpaid availability in the family shop.
- Connects economic independence to safety from parental marriage pressure and to control over her own home.
- Describes herself through weak belonging both in the parental household and in Europe.
