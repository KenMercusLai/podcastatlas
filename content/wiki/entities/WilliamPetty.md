# William Petty

## Overview

William Petty was a surveyor and early modern intellectual whose mapping of Ireland linked evidence-based administration and scientific ambition to colonial land redistribution.

## Current Profile

The episode places Petty within networks interested in measurement, economics, infrastructure, natural resources, and practical knowledge. Those capacities were not politically neutral: his Irish survey made conquered land legible for redistribution after mass dispossession.

Petty therefore embodies a central moral tension in state knowledge. Better maps and quantitative administration can increase descriptive and governing capacity while also making coercive transfer more systematic and scalable.

## Key Characteristics

- Surveyor of land in conquered Ireland.
- Connected to early modern scientific and quantitative inquiry.
- Interested in economics, infrastructure, and natural resources.
- Produced administrative knowledge used for land redistribution.
- Illustrates the overlap between rationalization and colonial violence.
