# 川端康成 / Kawabata Yasunari

川端康成 / Kawabata Yasunari appears in [[08-laojichao-laoji-busi-shenzhi-buzeng-diaoling-541092994]] as an important literary supporter and evaluator of [[OkamotoKanoko|冈本加乃子 / Okamoto Kanoko]]. The episode says Okamoto's entrance into fiction was connected to Kawabata's guidance and that he rated her work highly.

For this wiki, Kawabata's role is currently source-scoped. The page records him as part of the modern Japanese literary context that lets the episode position Okamoto not as an isolated late-life curiosity, but as a writer recognized by a major literary figure while still keeping her own warmer, less despairing tone.

## Key Claims
- The source uses Kawabata to legitimize Okamoto's fiction within Japanese literary history.
- The hosts compare his aesthetic proximity to Okamoto while saying they prefer her because her work feels less despairing.
