## Overview

石清 is a [[XiakeXingNovel|《侠客行》]] character presented as a mature mediator whose consistent choice of [[MinRou|闵柔]], firm boundaries with [[MeiFanggu|梅芳姑]], and response to [[ShiZhongyu|石中玉]] join relational judgment to public responsibility.

## Current Profile

The episode resists treating Shi Qing's refusal of Mei Fanggu as irrational blindness to a better candidate. His statement that she surpasses Min Rou in many visible traits may preserve her dignity, but his conduct never invites ambiguity. Attraction and shared life are not additive rankings, and his long familiarity and value alignment with Min Rou matter more than a list of accomplishments.

His broader style combines principle with face-saving. He can display strength, reduce humiliation, and search for an outcome that preserves relationships without pretending every conflict disappears. That balance becomes hardest when his son harms the Snow Mountain School: Shi Qing promises to deliver him for judgment and tries to add parental service and restitution, while refusing both pure concealment and effortless acceptance of his child's death.

## Key Characteristics

- Consistent romantic chooser who does not reward pursuit with false hope.
- Values temperament, shared history, and ethical alignment over comparative trait totals.
- Uses strength and face-saving together in conflict resolution.
- Shares grief and household responsibility with Min Rou without turning loss into mutual blame.
- Treats his son's wrongdoing as requiring confession and responsibility, while still seeking protection and restitution.

## Qualifications

This is a favorable character reading from one conversational source. Face-saving can shade into evasion, parental service cannot automatically compensate victims, and the episode does not settle what punishment would be just or lawful. Its account of Shi Qing's motives and of hidden tension within Shangqing Temple remains interpretive.
