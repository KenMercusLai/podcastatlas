# 《人间失格》 / No Longer Human

《人间失格》 / No Longer Human is the [[DazaiOsamu|太宰治]] novel discussed in [[33-renjian-shige-danxiaogui-de-zhuanglie-xianji-599924624]]. The episode presents it as a [[ShishosetsuAutobiographicalReading|私小说式自传阅读]] case, with [[ObaYozo|大庭叶藏]]'s life echoing Dazai's biography without becoming a one-to-one document.

The source reads the novel's structure through the frame narrator, three photographs, and Yozo's handnotes. The photographs become a visual sequence from forced childhood performance to polished inhuman beauty to an emptied face that has lost ordinary personhood.

The plot line moves from childhood fear and [[ComicMaskSurvival|comic masking]] into Tokyo drift, [[HorikiNoLongerHuman|堀木]]'s bad companionship, leftist illegality, the failed suicide with [[TsunekoNoLongerHuman|常子]], temporary domestic shelter with [[ShizukoNoLongerHuman|静子]], marriage to [[YoshikoNoLongerHuman|豪子]], the collapse of trust, addiction, the mental hospital, and rural removal after the father's death.

The episode's core claim is that the novel should not be reduced to either "丧" glamour or moral disgust. Its value is to make a damaged inner life legible: Yozo is weak and often irresponsible, but the source reads that weakness as evidence of terror before a world built around family authority, usefulness, social optimism, and [[CoercedNormality|normality coercion]].
