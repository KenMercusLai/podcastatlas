# Muhammad Ali of Egypt

## Overview

Muhammad Ali was an Albanian-born Ottoman commander and ruler presented as a central agent of Egyptian state modernization after the French occupation.

## Current Profile

The episode contrasts Muhammad Ali's interest in military, industrial, and administrative modernization with limited concern for pharaonic antiquities. British and French collectors removed objects while other monuments were damaged or reused, yet the modernization program also sent Egyptian students and scholars to Paris and supported translation of European knowledge for Egyptian purposes.

## Key Characteristics

- Pursued state strengthening through imported technical and institutional knowledge.
- Sent Egyptian study missions to France as part of modernization.
- Treated antiquities as secondary to immediate state-building priorities.
- Governed within an unequal European-Egyptian exchange that still allowed selective appropriation by Egypt.
