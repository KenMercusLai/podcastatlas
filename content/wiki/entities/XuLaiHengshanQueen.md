## Overview

徐来 / Xu Lai is [[LiuCiHengshanKingWesternHan|衡山王刘赐]]'s later queen and the principal challenger to crown prince [[LiuShuangHengshanCrownPrince|刘爽]] in Hanji 305-1 and 305-2. She tries to turn [[LiuXiaoHengshanPrince|刘孝]] into a controlled instrument while preserving the succession for her own son Liu Guang, but the strategy ends in reciprocal denunciation, imperial investigation, and her ordered death.

## Current Profile

Xu Lai's coalition is tactical from the beginning. After cultivating Liu Xiao against Liu Shuang, she arranges for a maid to seduce Liu Xiao and retains the encounter as leverage because she does not want him to become the final successor. When Liu Shuang later attempts to seduce her, she refuses and reports him to Liu Ci, helping trigger the confrontation that exposes the family's sexual allegations and alleged military preparations. Her maneuvers weaken the established heir but never install Liu Guang.

## Key Characteristics

- Later queen whose status and son's prospects conflict with the established heir.
- Coalition organizer who recruits grievances without sharing her allies' preferred succession outcome.
- Strategist who uses sexual compromise as leverage against Liu Xiao.
- Target of Liu Shuang's failed seduction attempt and immediate reporter of it to Liu Ci.
- Failed succession actor whose branch gains no durable settlement before the household is destroyed.

## Qualifications

The profile rests on two consecutive episodes from one popular-history series. The sorcery allegation in Hanji 305-1 is not adopted as fact. The maid's relationships, Xu Lai's knowledge and intent, exact dialogue, and legal fate remain episode-attributed. Hanji 305-2 calls the condemned queen “徐姬” in its closing section; the wiki treats that as Xu Lai in context but records the naming variation rather than creating another identity.

## What Changed

- Xu Lai's coalition now has an explicit control mechanism: she compromises Liu Xiao while withholding commitment to his succession.
- Her plan is now known to fail completely; neither Liu Xiao nor Liu Guang obtains a durable settlement.
- Her earlier coalition-building now connects directly to the confrontation and investigation that destroy the household.
