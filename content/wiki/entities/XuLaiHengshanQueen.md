## Overview

徐来 / Xu Lai is [[LiuCiHengshanKingWesternHan|衡山王刘赐]]'s later queen and the central challenger to crown prince [[LiuShuangHengshanCrownPrince|刘爽]] in Hanji 305-1. Her own son's succession interest makes the existing heir a direct threat to her maternal branch.

## Current Profile

Xu Lai enters as a favored consort with four children and becomes queen after 乘舒 dies. The episode says she aims to replace Liu Shuang with her son 刘广. After Liu Shuang wounds her brother, she repeatedly damages the crown prince's standing with Liu Ci and draws [[LiuXiaoHengshanPrince|刘孝]] and [[LiuWucaiHengshanPrincess|刘无采]] into a temporary coalition against him.

## Key Characteristics

- Later queen whose status and son's prospects conflict with the established heir.
- Target of a sorcery allegation circulated by rival consort [[JueJiHengshan|厥姬]].
- Political organizer who converts sibling grievances into an anti-heir coalition.
- Coalition partner whose preferred successor differs from Liu Xiao's goal.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on one short narrative source and does not cover Xu Lai's full life or later legal fate. The allegation that she killed 乘舒 through sorcery is presented as Jue Ji's accusation, not adopted as fact. Her motives are the episode's interpretation of the succession conflict.
