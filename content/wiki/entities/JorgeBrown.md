# Jorge Brown

## Overview
Jorge Brown is presented in the bounded source as a futurist and founder of the [[GlobalInstituteForTheFuture]] whose work spans sustainability, government, politics, leadership, sport, and tourism.

## Current Profile
[[defaultmp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-725b059f65d6ae95b04d1dbc8a6cf2dd-104571730-defaultmp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-725b059f65d6ae95b04d1dbc8a6cf2dd-104571730]] presents Brown as a cross-domain systems thinker. He argues that near-term choices may set long-lived institutional paths, that AI governance cannot be separated from democracy or resource infrastructure, and that longevity, employment, taxation, security, and inequality must be considered together. His practical response combines resilience, plural discussion, moral limits on technology, and commitments that can be measured rather than left as declarations.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats foresight as a present decision discipline rather than prediction for its own sake.
- Connects technology, institutions, resources, security, work, and inequality as interacting systems.
- Places values, virtue, legitimacy, and responsibility to future generations above efficiency alone.
- Supports liberal-democratic participation without arguing that one political model should be imposed universally.
- Favors structured disagreement, compromise, and measurable follow-through over ideological sorting or distant promises.
- Uses provocative proposals to open policy questions while leaving many implementation details unresolved.

## Qualifications

Brown's biography, institutional roles, forthcoming books, historical analogies, geopolitical judgments, quantitative illustrations, and policy forecasts are not independently verified here. Many proposals are agenda-setting questions rather than complete designs with costs, authority, evidence standards, or implementation plans.
