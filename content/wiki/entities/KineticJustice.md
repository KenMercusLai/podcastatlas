# Robert Earl Council / Kinetic Justice

## Overview

Robert Earl Council, also known as Kinetic Justice, is an incarcerated organizer in [[TheAlabamaSolution|The Alabama Solution]] whose case connects prison labor, parole, nonviolent protest, and the disclosure of conviction history.

## Current Profile

The episode identifies Council with a class-action challenge to what plaintiffs describe as Alabama's convict-leasing system. He is also part of the broader participant group whose recordings, work stoppages, hunger strikes, and consultation with filmmakers make incarceration visible from inside.

Jarecki gives a limited account of Council's conviction to illustrate [[DocumentaryContextualDisclosure]]: enough context may prevent audiences from imagining only extreme offenders, but the constitutional question of abuse should not depend on whether a viewer approves of the imprisoned person.

## Key Characteristics

- Connects incarcerated organizing to litigation over compelled labor.
- Participates in a documentary model that treats prisoners as collaborators.
- Illustrates the ethical tension around disclosing conviction history.
