## Overview

Wu Sansi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-536-3-jiemi-shangguan-waner-he-ta-beihou-de-nanrenmen-lqj7rpwwfllogrxrbav-xqwwnnnm|Hanji 536-3]] as the Wu-family figure around whom [[ShangguanWaner|上官婉儿]]'s post-Wu Zetian positioning becomes especially risky. The episode treats him as both a long-running intimacy/patronage tie and a factional liability.

## Current Profile

The source says [[WuZetian|武则天]] once considered Wu Sansi as a possible successor, making his proximity valuable to Shangguan Wan'er while Wu-family prospects still seemed strong. After the Shenlong Coup and [[TangZhongzong|李显]]'s restoration, Shangguan Wan'er continued a relationship with Wu Sansi, recommended him to [[EmpressWeiTang|韦后]], and helped form a three-person alliance. That alliance had short-term power but became dangerous once Wu Sansi and his son were killed in [[LiChongjunTang|李重俊]]'s failed coup.

## Key Characteristics

- Wu-family political figure whose possible succession role makes him valuable before Wu power collapses.
- Long-running relationship and alliance partner for Shangguan Wan'er in the episode.
- Gains access to Empress Wei through Shangguan Wan'er's recommendation.
- Uses the Shangguan-Wei connection to move against Zhang Jianzhi and other restoration figures.
- Becomes a liability after Li Chongjun's coup kills him and shifts Shangguan Wan'er toward Li-Tang fallback routes.
