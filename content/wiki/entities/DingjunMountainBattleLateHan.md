## Overview

The Battle of Dingjun Mountain is the turning point in the current [[HanzhongCampaign218LateHan|218–219 Hanzhong campaign]] evidence where repeated pressure and force redistribution expose [[XiahouYuan|夏侯渊]] to [[HuangZhong|黄忠]]'s fatal attack.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1163 says [[LiuBei|刘备]] moves from the long Yangping standoff across the Mian River and establishes a position at Dingjun Mountain. He divides a large elite force into multiple groups for night pressure on [[ZhangHeLateHan|张郃]]'s sector. Xiahou Yuan sends half his troops to assist Zhang He; a subsequent fire attack in Zouma Valley reportedly draws Xiahou Yuan out with four hundred men to fight the fire and repair the abatis.

[[FaZheng|法正]] identifies this as the attack window. Liu Bei orders Huang Zhong forward, the Cao-side force is defeated, and Xiahou Yuan is killed. [[DuXiLateHan|杜袭]] and [[GuoHuaiLateHan|郭淮]] then support Zhang He as commander, limiting immediate collapse despite the strategic loss.

## Key Characteristics

- Follows a long, initially unsuccessful Hanzhong confrontation.
- Uses pressure against one sector to induce reinforcement and force division.
- Adds fire and repair demands to expose a senior commander with a small escort.
- Converts tactical timing into command loss and campaign reversal.
- Produces rapid emergency succession rather than total Cao-side disintegration.

## Qualifications

The current page rests on one structured popular-history summary. The date, route, force totals, number of detachments, target of the fire, abatis repair, precise command relations, Fa Zheng's signal, Huang Zhong's route, identity of the other senior casualty, and location of the later river defense require primary-source comparison. The battle supports a force-dislocation reading but does not prove one personality trait caused Xiahou Yuan's death.
