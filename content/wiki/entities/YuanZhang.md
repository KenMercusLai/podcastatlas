# 袁掌 / Yuan Zhang

袁掌 is the guest in [[163-jiao-yi-zhi-sheli-wangji-dongwuyuan-xiye-de-gushi-bi-fengkuang-dongwucheng-2-geng-jingcai-939887179]] and the author of [[JiaoYiZhiSheliWangjiDongwuyuan|《教一只猞猁忘记动物园》]]. The source identifies him as a former deputy director of [[XiningWildlifePark|西宁野生动物园]] and as someone now working around science education and external communication at Xining Botanical Garden.

The page is source-scoped. In this episode, Yuan Zhang's importance is not a complete biography but his double role as a practical wildlife-care worker and a public communicator. He can explain [[Lynx|lynx]] classification, release timing, live-prey ethics, satellite monitoring, and public-attention risks while also admitting that [[TianxianBaobaoLynx|天线宝宝]] changed his own self-image as a "dead rationalist."

## Key Claims
- Yuan Zhang links technical wildlife work to public storytelling rather than treating them as separate jobs.
- His account of 天线宝宝 emphasizes restraint: affection for a rescued wild animal may require keeping it afraid of people.
- He treats emotion as useful but fallible, openly marking when his interpretation of an animal's behavior may be projection.
- He argues that conservation needs public understanding, and that stories can sometimes move people faster than abstract biodiversity language.
