# 鲁穆公 / Duke Mu of Lu

鲁穆公 / Duke Mu of Lu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl|Zhouji 09]] through his death in 377 BCE. The source says his son Ji Fen succeeds as [[LuGonggong|鲁共公]].

The page is source-scoped. Lu Mu Gong's significance here is annalistic: the episode places Lu's succession notice beside [[Zisi|子思]]'s political advice and [[HanState|韩国]]'s succession from [[HanWenhou|韩文侯]] to [[HanAihou|韩哀侯]].
