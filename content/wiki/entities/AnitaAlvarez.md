# Anita Alvarez

## Overview
Anita Alvarez is an American artistic swimmer presented through [[AndreaFuentes]]'s account of a 2022 competition blackout, rescue, medical investigation, return to competition, and later Olympic medal.

## Current Profile
During a solo performance in Budapest, Alvarez lost consciousness and sank in the pool. Fuentes says she entered when the lifeguard did not immediately do so, brought Alvarez to the surface, and helped move her toward medical personnel. The international federation then temporarily withheld competition clearance while neurological and cardiac causes were investigated.

In Fuentes's telling, later exertional monitoring did not identify a cardiac cause, and the team attributed the episode to an iron-related issue. Alvarez kept a rescue photograph visible and won an Olympic medal two years later; Fuentes interprets that return as a transformation of trauma into courage and leadership.

## Key Characteristics
- Elite artistic swimmer exposed to the sport's combined breath-hold and high-exertion demands.
- Survivor of a competition loss-of-consciousness event described by her coach.
- Underwent neurological, cardiac, and high-exertion investigation before returning.
- Returned to elite competition after the incident.
- Presented by Fuentes as using rather than hiding the rescue image.
