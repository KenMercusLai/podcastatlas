## Current Profile

The current profile has an early Huo-trusted-service layer and four linked restraint layers. Hanji 401 places Zhang Anshi in Han Zhao's Huo-centered appointment field: Huo asks that he be made right general and Guanglu Xun because the court lacks old ministers. Hanji 407 then places Zhang inside the narrow circle that plans Liu He's removal, but the episode gives him less public theatrical force than Tian Yannian: his role is secret alignment with Huo Guang before the formal procedure. Hanji 410-1 shows Zhang Anshi refusing to let Zhang He overread Liu Bingyi's future: the young man is receiving support but remains politically marginal, so a Zhang-family marriage would expose the family to an unbalanced bet. Hanji 430 then uses Zhang Anshi as the practical opposite of reward appetite. Even when [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] insists the reward is for Zhang He's old kindness rather than for Zhang Anshi, Zhang Anshi asks that the scale of tomb guards and honor be reduced. The host reads that restraint through [[RewardFitnessRisk|德位不配风险]].

Hanji 431 places that restraint inside daily court practice. Zhang worries that father and son have both been ennobled and tries to resign surplus revenue; after Huo-family power collapses, he also fears that his granddaughter's Huo marriage will draw implication. Xuan pardons the granddaughter, but Zhang still treats excessive favor as a danger. He privately advises Xuan on major decisions, then hides his involvement by sick leave or feigned surprise after edicts are issued. He recommends talent but refuses gifts and thanks from the appointee, and he asks that [[ZhangYanshouWesternHan|张延寿]] be sent outward to prevent Zhang-family visibility from becoming too concentrated.

Hanji 432 broadens that restraint from one reward scene into a career style. The episode says Zhang Anshi was valued by Huo Guang, served near the center under Han Zhaodi and Xuan, and became one of the Qilin Pavilion eleven. Its explanation is not passivity alone: Zhang is quiet, cautious, and generous with subordinates, but also capable and alert. The book-memory anecdote under Han Wudi shows practical talent, while his later separation from the Huo family shows political timing. The page therefore treats him as a cautious survivor whose low-profile ethic includes competence, role boundaries, and decisive repositioning when the power field changes.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior Western Han figure tied to Zhang He's family reward branch, cautious pre-accession marriage restraint, Liu Bingyi's enthronement, and later Qilin Pavilion memory.
- Huo Guang-backed old-minister appointee under Han Zhao, promoted to right general and Guanglu Xun in the episode's account.
- Early participant in Huo Guang's secret Liu He deposition planning.
- Attempts to reduce excessive-seeming honor rather than maximize family gain.
- Embodies a career-long low-profile style: cautious, few-spoken, generous, and careful not to expose excessive ambition or hidden influence.
- Combines survival restraint with practical ability and timing, illustrated by the lost-books memory story and later cooperation with Xuan's consolidation against Huo-family influence.
- Separates public service from private obligation by recommending talent without accepting gratitude gifts or patron-client display.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 401, Hanji 407, Hanji 410-1, Hanji 430, Hanji 431, and Hanji 432. It does not reconstruct Zhang Anshi's full biography, complete offices, full Huo-family purge role, or broader family politics. Hanji 407's leak-suspicion moment is preserved as Huo Guang's episode-level fear rather than proof of leakage, and Hanji 431's workplace-wisdom reading and Hanji 432's "善因厚德" character evaluation are preserved as host synthesis rather than complete historical proof of motive or causality.
