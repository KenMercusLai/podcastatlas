## Overview

张安世 / Zhang Anshi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-430-chigua-qunzhong-qianren-yu-xianren-de-gongdou-daxi-lkfcjewfhmhfgw39vqfqkpa9umzq|Hanji 430]] as the cautious Western Han official who tries to reduce rewards attached to [[ZhangHeWesternHan|张贺]] and [[ZhangPengzuWesternHan|张彭祖]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-432-kai-jinian-gongchen-zhi-xianhe-jiemi-qilinge-shiyi-gongchen-lj65dvmawrppq2iomuzarg1ah9ak|Hanji 432]] expands him into a representative [[QilinPavilionMeritHonors|麒麟阁功臣荣典]] figure: an elder minister who survives Huo Guang-era politics through competence, caution, low visibility, generosity, and timely alignment with [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] after Huo-family power becomes dangerous.

## Current Profile

The current profile has two linked restraint layers. Hanji 430 uses Zhang Anshi as the practical opposite of reward appetite. Even when [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] insists the reward is for Zhang He's old kindness rather than for Zhang Anshi, Zhang Anshi asks that the scale of tomb guards and honor be reduced. The host reads that restraint through [[RewardFitnessRisk|德位不配风险]]: accepting honor beyond merit can create danger.

Hanji 432 broadens that restraint from one reward scene into a career style. The episode says Zhang Anshi was valued by Huo Guang, served near the center under Han Zhaodi and Xuan, and became one of the Qilin Pavilion eleven. Its explanation is not passivity alone: Zhang is quiet, cautious, and generous with subordinates, but also capable and alert. The book-memory anecdote under Han Wudi shows practical talent, while his later separation from the Huo family shows political timing. The page therefore treats him as a cautious survivor whose low-profile ethic includes competence and decisive repositioning when the power field changes.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior Western Han figure tied to Zhang He's family reward branch and later remembered as a Qilin Pavilion meritorious minister.
- Attempts to reduce excessive-seeming honor rather than maximize family gain.
- Embodies a career-long low-profile style: cautious, few-spoken, generous, and careful not to expose excessive ambition.
- Combines survival restraint with practical ability, illustrated by the lost-books memory story.
- Preserves himself after Huo Guang by reading the power shift and cooperating with Xuan's consolidation against Huo-family influence.
- His restraint is interpreted morally by the host through "善因厚德" and politically through reward fitness and alignment risk.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 430 and Hanji 432. It does not reconstruct Zhang Anshi's full biography, complete offices, full Huo-family purge role, or broader family politics. Hanji 432's character evaluation and "善因厚德" reading are preserved as the host's synthesis rather than a complete historical proof of causality.
