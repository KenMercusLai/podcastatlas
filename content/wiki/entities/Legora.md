# Legora

## Overview
Legora is the legal AI company profiled in the All-In episode on AI voice, legal AI, and the possible end of the billable hour.

## Current Profile
The source presents Legora as a legal AI platform for law firms and enterprises, with value sitting in workflow, firm data, legal data, trust, compliance, and agent orchestration rather than in a general legal intelligence model. Legora says it works with partners such as [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]], spends heavily on tokens, avoids on-prem deployment, and uses narrow models for specific tasks such as contract-data extraction. Its commercial story is explicitly source-scoped: the episode reports 50% quarter-over-quarter growth for seven quarters, in-house acquisition diligence using its own tool, and legal engineers helping firms change their operating model.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames legal AI as software moving into a large services-heavy market.
- Positions agentic legal work around law-firm, enterprise, precedent, statutory, regulatory, and case data.
- Challenges billable-hour economics by shifting review, diligence, and drafting away from manual associate labor.
- Uses legal engineers as forward-deployed lawyers who translate law-firm work into AI-enabled workflows.
- Treats trust, compliance, and sensitive-data handling as core deployment constraints.
