# Cooks Who Feed

## Overview
Cooks Who Feed is the for-profit social enterprise founded by [[SeemaSanghavi]], ethically producing kitchen linens with a meal give-back model and a B2B-heavy customer base.

## Current Profile
In the episode, Cooks Who Feed has provided around two million meals through product sales, operates its own production facility north of Delhi, and hires and trains women from surrounding villages as seamstresses. It sells chef aprons, kids' aprons, tea towels, grocery totes, wine totes, linen napkins, server aprons, bar aprons, chef coats, tablecloths, and other customized linens.

The company is currently about 75% B2B, with customers including restaurant groups, Celebrity Cruises, and culinary schools. Its advice problem is how to convince businesses to switch vendors. [[DaymondJohn]] and [[GuyRaz]] push the company toward low-risk trials, hard referrals from existing clients, product quality and value, mission-aligned prospects, and sales language that makes the buyer's own sustainability and impact story stronger.

## Key Characteristics
- For-profit social enterprise with a meal give-back model and ethically produced kitchen linens.
- Operates a production facility north of Delhi that hires and trains women from nearby villages.
- Has a B2B-heavy revenue mix and offers extensive customization, including logos, colors, pocket styles, straps, and embroidery.
- Sells to hospitality, restaurant, cruise, culinary-school, and related institutional customers.
- Needs to make vendor switching feel low-risk while proving quality, price, service, and impact.
