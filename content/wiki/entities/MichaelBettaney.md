# Michael Bettaney / 迈克尔·贝塔尼

Michael Bettaney / 迈克尔·贝塔尼 appears in [[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] through the "Koba" letter episode. The source says a British-side intelligence figure tried to approach the [[KGB]] under that signature, but the KGB station initially dismissed the letter as a possible provocation or trap.

For the episode, Bettaney matters because [[OlegGordievsky|Oleg Gordievsky]] recognized the seriousness of the materials and warned [[MI6]]. The incident increased Gordievsky's counterintelligence value without immediately exposing him, but it also moved attention around the London KGB station and helped clear a path toward later station leadership.
