# 姜涛 / Jiang Tao (Psychiatrist)

## Overview
姜涛 / Jiang Tao is a psychiatrist at [[BeijingAndingHospital|北京市安定医院]] and the guest in [[shidai-zhenghou-yu-anding-cixin-lphaw0qek8ibr0jt44yygou6akql]] and [[shi-di-fu-shuo-466-jiangtao-5wan-jingshenke-yisheng-dui-1yi-jingshen-jibing-huanzhe-lpdsk96akpwtccxyesjdgtw8skbn]]. The sources present him as a clinician explaining severe mental illness, emotional disorders, psychiatric diagnosis, family care, psychotherapy boundaries, adolescent school refusal, crisis risk, medication, and stigma.

## Current Profile
Jiang Tao's profile in the wiki is clinical rather than celebrity-centered. He repeatedly pulls the conversation back from moralized emotion into risk, function, diagnosis, treatment, and realistic care resources.

His strongest stance is boundary-setting. He normalizes mental illness as illness, warns against online self-diagnosis, treats family guilt as different from care responsibility, and says professional psychiatric judgment must consider symptoms, functional impairment, course, background, and risk.

His public profile is also practice-centered. The newer evidence describes him as having spent more than 30 years at Anding Hospital, moving from young ward doctor to senior clinician, and using clinic experience to discuss schizophrenia recovery limits, adolescent school refusal, family overcorrection, psychiatrist-psychotherapist referral, workforce shortage, crisis hospitalization, and scams around informal healing services.

## Key Characteristics
- Psychiatrist associated with [[BeijingAndingHospital|北京市安定医院]] over a long clinical career.
- Explains serious mental illness through clinical insight, function, risk, and treatment rather than shame or mystification.
- Treats families as part of the care ecology while defending caregiver self-protection.
- Distinguishes psychiatry, psychotherapy, ordinary emotional distress, online scales, popular labels, and professional diagnosis.
- Uses contemporary anxiety, achievement pressure, somatization, and desire overload to connect clinic experience with social mood.
- Treats adolescent school refusal and youth self-harm as multi-system problems involving family, school, development, symptoms, and scarce care resources.
- Balances patience and kindness with a clear professional boundary around what doctors can and cannot control.
