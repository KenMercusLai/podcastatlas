# 江涛 / Jiang Tao (Psychiatrist)

## Overview
江涛 / Jiang Tao is a psychiatrist at [[BeijingAndingHospital|北京市安定医院]] and the guest in [[shidai-zhenghou-yu-anding-cixin-lphaw0qek8ibr0jt44yygou6akql]]. The episode presents him as a clinician explaining severe mental illness, emotional disorders, psychiatric diagnosis, family care, psychotherapy boundaries, somatization, medication, and stigma.

## Current Profile
Jiang Tao's profile in the wiki is clinical rather than celebrity-centered. He repeatedly pulls the conversation back from moralized emotion into risk, function, diagnosis, treatment, and realistic care resources.

His strongest stance is boundary-setting. He normalizes mental illness as illness, warns against online self-diagnosis, treats family guilt as different from care responsibility, and says professional psychiatric judgment must consider symptoms, functional impairment, course, background, and risk.

## Key Characteristics
- Psychiatrist associated with [[BeijingAndingHospital|北京市安定医院]].
- Explains serious mental illness through clinical insight, function, risk, and treatment rather than shame or mystification.
- Treats families as part of the care ecology while defending caregiver self-protection.
- Distinguishes psychiatry, psychotherapy, ordinary emotional distress, online scales, and professional diagnosis.
- Uses contemporary anxiety, achievement pressure, somatization, and desire overload to connect clinic experience with social mood.
