# 楚宣王 / King Xuan of Chu

楚宣王 / King Xuan of Chu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-2-lmnqfjmydygqnfec6xhzpixralvi]] as 熊良夫, the younger brother who succeeds after [[ChuSuwang|楚肃王]] dies without a son. The episode treats the notice briefly as one of the sixth-year succession events.

The page is source-scoped. Chu Xuan Wang matters here because the succession moves through a brother rather than a son, keeping [[ChuState|楚国]] inside the episode's wider concern with how ruler death is converted into political continuity or disorder.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-14-qiweiwang-buming-zeyi-yiming-jingren-lrs6rbifyiqrojxg0mccpg7sf9rv]] adds him as the likely groom behind [[YouyinHei|右尹黑]]'s mission to [[QinState|秦国]] to receive a bride. The episode uses this to explain marriage protocol and the limits of [[WarringStatesMarriageDiplomacy|战国联姻外交]].
