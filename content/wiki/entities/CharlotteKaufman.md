# Charlotte Kaufman

## Overview

Charlotte Kaufman is a co-director of [[TheAlabamaSolution|The Alabama Solution]] whose fieldwork connected the prison investigation to [[StephenDavis]]'s family and alleged institutional concealment.

## Current Profile

The episode describes Kaufman accompanying [[AndrewJarecki]] to a hospital after learning Davis had been badly beaten, then staying with his mother, Sandy Ray, while Ray tried to learn what had happened. This proximity allowed the production to record the family's calls with prison officials during the delay before formal notification.

Kaufman also shares the film's continuing relationship with incarcerated participants. Jarecki says the men remain part of both filmmakers' lives after release and that legal wellness checks continue because of retaliation risk.

## Key Characteristics

- Conducted sustained fieldwork with a bereaved family during an unfolding investigation.
- Helped preserve contemporaneous evidence rather than relying only on later reconstruction.
- Shared editorial dialogue and continuing care obligations toward incarcerated collaborators.
