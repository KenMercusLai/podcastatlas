# 车墩镇 / Chedun Town

车墩镇 / Chedun Town is the Shanghai-area town that gives [[CheDundunYeShiji|《车墩墩野事记》]] its title. In [[85-chedundun-yeshiji-zou-a-bandao-xiangxia-zuan-xiaoshuliner-qu-700935571]], [[YinQiCheDundun|尹琪]] treats it as an urban-edge living environment where rail commuting, storefronts, village paths, fields, small woods, balconies, community lights, and nearby wildlife all meet.

The source's Chedun is not a purified rural idyll. It includes bats on pipes, swallow nests over shops, possible zhang/deerlike animals moving from a nearby release base, pheasants hiding in fields, insects rescued from landscaping or shared bikes, raccoon-dog traces, fireflies affected by path lighting, discarded objects, informal uses of woods, and neighborly shop conversations.

## Key Claims
- Chedun functions as a concrete case of [[UrbanEdgeNature]] rather than as an abstract "countryside" ideal.
- Its ecological value appears in small, repeatable observations: droppings, footprints, nests, lights, sounds, and seasonal insect activity.
- The place also exposes the social side of [[UrbanEcology]]: store owners, residents, children, property management, commuters, and observers all affect what animals can do.
- It gives [[NatureContactSelfPerception]] a pragmatic version because living there depends on accepting tradeoffs around transport, schooling, commerce, safety, and personal needs.
