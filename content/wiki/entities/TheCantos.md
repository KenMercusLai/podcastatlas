# The Cantos

The Cantos is [[EzraPound]]'s long poem, discussed in [[i-robot-ai-and-consciousness-6a880840b0d5e24ea40c7015]] through [[MaryDeRachewiltz]]'s translation and defense of her father. The source says Pound continued work on the poem while institutionalized and that Mary produced the first dual-language English-Italian version in 1985.

In this source, The Cantos is not only a literary object. It is the work through which translation, family loyalty, modernist achievement, and [[IdeologicallyCompromisedArt|ideological compromise]] become entangled.
