# Liz Farmer

Liz Farmer appears in [[tech-20260414-0414-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260414-0414-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the [[PewCharitableTrusts]] public-finance expert interviewed by [[StephanieHughes]] about state interest in crypto investment and reserve laws.

Her contribution is to separate several ideas that can otherwise blur together. Some state officials view [[Bitcoin]] or other digital assets as high-risk, high-reward alternatives for diversification; [[StateCryptoReserves]] are still untested emergency tools; and [[Arizona]]'s law is more about [[SeizedDigitalAssetCustody]] than ordinary portfolio allocation.
