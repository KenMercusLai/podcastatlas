## Overview

耿宝 / Geng Bao is the high-prestige Grand General removed after the Yan-backed accession of [[LiuYiBeixiangHouLateHan|北乡侯刘懿 / 刘裔]]. Hanji 802 treats him as an old [[EmperorAnOfHan|Emperor An]]-era powerholder whose previous usefulness to the Yan side does not protect him once [[YanXianLateHan|阎显]] needs to monopolize the new court.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-802-andi-zhisi-donghan-wangchao-quanli-da-xipai-lgvne92e0jjzjnznzfkz7nvztnbj|Hanji 802]] says Geng Bao is Emperor An's maternal uncle and holds the Grand General position, giving him rank, prestige, and court weight. The host emphasizes that this office can exceed the practical power of the Three Excellencies, so Geng Bao's continued presence directly constrains the Yan family's new settlement.

The source also frames Geng Bao as a former interest ally rather than a simple Yan enemy. Earlier, during the Liu Bao displacement, the Yan side and Geng Bao's side could cooperate because their interests overlapped. After Yan Taihou and Yan Xian install Beixiang Hou, that shared interest disappears, and Geng Bao's office becomes the obstacle.

Yan Xian therefore has officials accuse Geng Bao, [[FanFengEunuchLateHan|樊丰]], Xie Yun, Zhou Guang, [[WangShengPalaceActorLateHan|王圣]], and others of factional misconduct and treasonous behavior. Fan Feng and others are imprisoned and executed, while Geng Bao and his nephew are demoted; Geng Bao kills himself on the road.

## Key Characteristics

- High-status An-era maternal-kin actor with Grand General authority.
- Former interest ally of the Yan side before the accession payoff changed.
- Threat to Yan Xian because military prestige and senior office complicate Yan family monopoly.
- Purge target accused together with eunuch and palace-linked old networks.
- Identity-disambiguation case distinct from [[GengBaoLateHan|耿包]], the later Yuan Shao-side adviser.
