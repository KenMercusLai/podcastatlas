## Overview

耿宝 / Geng Bao is the high-prestige Grand General who first complies with [[EmperorAnOfHan|Emperor An]]'s move against [[EmperorShunOfHan|刘保]], then is removed after the Yan-backed accession of [[LiuYiBeixiangHouLateHan|北乡侯刘懿 / 刘裔]]. Hanji 801 adds his 124 CE appointment and deposition-debate compliance; Hanji 802 treats him as an old An-era powerholder whose previous usefulness to the Yan side does not protect him once [[YanXianLateHan|阎显]] needs to monopolize the new court.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-801-han-andi-weishenme-henxin-fei-taizi-lgnhbsqnp6vdigsmqfkxiqmx2vdy|Hanji 801]] places Geng Bao's elevation before the death crisis: in 124 CE, he is appointed Grand General after serving as dahonglu. The source emphasizes the office's weight, then shows Geng Bao and others following Emperor An's intent when the ruler asks senior ministers to discuss deposing Liu Bao.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-802-andi-zhisi-donghan-wangchao-quanli-da-xipai-lgvne92e0jjzjnznzfkz7nvztnbj|Hanji 802]] says Geng Bao is Emperor An's maternal uncle and still holds the Grand General position, giving him rank, prestige, and court weight. The host emphasizes that this office can exceed the practical power of the Three Excellencies, so Geng Bao's continued presence directly constrains the Yan family's new settlement.

The sources together frame Geng Bao as a former interest ally rather than a simple Yan enemy. During the Liu Bao displacement, Geng Bao's compliance aligns with the anti-Liu Bao side. After Yan Taihou and Yan Xian install Beixiang Hou, that shared interest disappears, and Geng Bao's office becomes the obstacle.

Yan Xian therefore has officials accuse Geng Bao, [[FanFengEunuchLateHan|樊丰]], Xie Yun, Zhou Guang, [[WangShengPalaceActorLateHan|王圣]], and others of factional misconduct and treasonous behavior. Fan Feng and others are imprisoned and executed, while Geng Bao and his nephew are demoted; Geng Bao kills himself on the road.

## Key Characteristics

- High-status An-era maternal-kin actor elevated to Grand General in 124 CE.
- Compliant senior participant in the deposition-debate environment around Liu Bao.
- Former interest ally of the Yan side before the accession payoff changed.
- Threat to Yan Xian because military prestige and senior office complicate Yan family monopoly.
- Purge target accused together with eunuch and palace-linked old networks.
- Identity-disambiguation case distinct from [[GengBaoLateHan|耿包]], the later Yuan Shao-side adviser.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 801 and Hanji 802. It does not reconstruct Geng Bao's full family background, formal judicial process, exact private motive in Liu Bao's demotion, or the exact charges against each accused person. The page uses `GengBaoGrandGeneralLateHan` because the existing `GengBaoLateHan` route belongs to a different late-Han figure named 耿包.
