# 曹无伤 / Cao Wushang

曹无伤 / Cao Wushang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-1-jiemi-wuguang-bei-miekou-de-lishi-zhenxiang-lk1bkhqkwf9upbbj08pjkn8cstyp|Qinji 129-1]] as [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s left sima during early anti-Qin fighting. After Liu Bang attacks Xue County and the Sishui commandery governor Zhuang retreats, Cao Wushang captures and kills him.

The host flags Cao Wushang's later narrative importance by noting that he is the same figure who nearly gets Liu Bang killed at the [[HongmenYan|鸿门宴]]. This page keeps that as a forward link: in this source, his concrete action is still a successful battlefield capture on Liu Bang's side.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-140-xiangyu-yu-liubang-shi-zhen-youqing-haishi-xianghu-liyong-1-lmmhoq2wo3bs6b1johpc0ixuf662|Hanji 140 part 1]] gives that forward link its payoff. Cao Wushang sends a secret report to [[XiangYu|项羽]] claiming that [[LiuBang|刘邦]] intends to rule [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]], make [[ZiyingQin|子婴]] chancellor, hold the treasure, and seek confirmation from [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]]. The report becomes the immediate trigger for Xiang Yu's decision to attack Liu Bang before Hongmen.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-141-huanyuan-lishi-zhong-de-hongmenyan-lpmb-bcoocngtqjvtnpj0elm-siy|Hanji 141]] shows the consequence of that leak from the opposite side. When Liu Bang apologizes at [[HongmenYan|鸿门宴]] and blames slanderous "small people" for the split, Xiang Yu casually names Cao Wushang as the informer. The episode reads this as an [[InformerSourceProtection|告密者来源保护]] failure: Xiang Yu gains an intelligence source but then exposes him to the target.
