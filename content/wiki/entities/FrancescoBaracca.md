# Francesco Baracca

Francesco Baracca is the Italian World War I aviator whose prancing horse symbol becomes part of the [[Ferrari]] visual identity in [[ferrari-1]]. The episode says Baracca's mother encouraged [[EnzoFerrari]] to use the horse for luck.

The source treats the horse as more than a logo origin detail. Combined with the yellow Modena shield, Italian colors, and Rosso Corsa, it helped Ferrari turn racing memory and national identity into a durable [[ConsumerBrandMoat]].
