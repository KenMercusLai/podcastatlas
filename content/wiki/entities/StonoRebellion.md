# Stono Rebellion

## Overview

The Stono Rebellion was a 1739 uprising by enslaved people near South Carolina's Stono River. The episode uses it to make the slaveholding South's numerical vulnerability and dependence on organized armed repression concrete.

## Current Profile

Led by Jemmy, rebels seized weapons and marched while calling for liberty before an armed white force defeated them. The episode places the event at the beginning of a causal narrative: plantation owners already depended on coercion, but rebellion intensified patrols and restrictions on movement, education, earnings, food production, and assembly.

The rebellion is evidence of resistance and of the violent security system built against it. Its role in the episode is contextual; the supplied summary does not prove a direct documentary chain from Stono to [[JamesMadison]]'s drafting decades later.

## Key Characteristics

- 1739 armed uprising by enslaved people in South Carolina.
- Began with the seizure of guns and ammunition.
- Expanded while participants marched and called for liberty.
- Defeated by an armed white force assembled locally.
- Followed, in the episode's account, by stronger patrols and racial controls.

## Qualifications

The episode supplies a compressed narrative rather than a full history of the participants, casualties, law, chronology, or subsequent resistance. Its use of Stono as background to the Second Amendment is interpretive and does not establish a simple direct cause.
