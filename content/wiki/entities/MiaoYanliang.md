# 缪延亮 / Miao Yanliang

## Overview

缪延亮 is the economist interviewed by [[HouWangMianji|厚望]] and [[AmyInvestmentABC|Amiee]] about macroeconomic transmission, rates, the dollar, U.S. debt, gold, and AI. The source says he worked at the IMF, participated in euro-area crisis work, returned to China's foreign-exchange administration in 2013, and later analyzed macro policy and asset prices in financial markets.

## Current Profile

His represented method joins models with practice. He uses general-equilibrium and intertemporal reasoning to identify constraints, then checks how households, firms, markets, and policy feedback alter the result. The episode's distinctive extension is to treat AI not only as an industry or asset theme but as an aggregate investment, financing, labor, price, and distribution force.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses [[GeneralEquilibriumPolicyTransmission|general-equilibrium transmission]] to test local-policy intuition against system-wide responses.
- Treats models as organizing tools whose applicability and boundary conditions require practical judgment.
- Prioritizes interest rates and exchange rates as macro variables connecting policy to asset allocation and daily life.
- Separates real technological value from the valuation of technology-linked assets.
- Keeps debt sustainability and safe-asset analysis conditional on growth, rates, deficits, buyers, institutions, and market behavior.
