# Port Washington, Wisconsin

Port Washington, Wisconsin appears in [[tech-20260410-0410-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260410-0410-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the city where residents voted for a measure requiring voter approval before some large tax incentives for developers can move forward. The episode says the measure could restrict future large data-center development, while an existing large data-center project in the city would not be affected.

In the wiki, Port Washington is a concrete local case for [[DataCenterIncentiveReferendum]]. It turns [[DataCenterBacklash]] from general opposition into a procedural demand: residents want a direct say over the public incentives and burdens attached to data-center expansion.
