# Elisabeth Marie of Austria

## Overview

Elisabeth Marie of Austria is the daughter of [[CrownPrinceRudolf]] and [[PrincessStephanieOfBelgium]], named in the Mayerling prehistory as the later Red Archduchess.

## Current Profile

The source gives Elisabeth Marie a narrow dynastic role. She is present in the account because Rudolf and Stephanie's marriage produces a daughter but no continuing direct male heir for [[FranzJosephI]]. The later "Red Archduchess" label is noted as future context, but the episode does not develop her adult politics or biography.

## Key Characteristics

- Elisabeth Marie is the child of Rudolf and Stephanie inside the late [[HabsburgDynasty]] family network.
- Her existence shows that Rudolf's marriage produced a dynastic child without solving the male succession pressure.
- She is present with Stephanie at Rudolf's thirtieth birthday at Laxenburg while his parents send telegrams.
- The source names her later reputation as the Red Archduchess but does not explain that later life.
