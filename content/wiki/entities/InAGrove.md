# 《竹林中》 / In a Grove

《竹林中》 / In a Grove is the [[AkutagawaRyunosuke|芥川龙之介]] short story discussed in [[17-diyubian-zhongsheng-jie-ku-yi-nian-ru-mo-561780565]] as one of his strongest technical works. The episode summarizes its case: a married couple meets the bandit Tajomaru, the wife is assaulted, the husband dies, and the surviving or summoned speakers each give a self-consistent but mutually incompatible account.

The source treats the story as more than a puzzle. Its contradictory testimony creates a world where shame, self-justification, desire, and reputation shape what can be said. That makes the story a compact [[MultiPerspectiveMurderNarration]] case and one literary root of the [[RashomonEffect]].
