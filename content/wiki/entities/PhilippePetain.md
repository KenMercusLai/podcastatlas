# Philippe Pétain

## Overview

Philippe Pétain was the French marshal who became prime minister during the [[FallOfFrance1940]], sought an armistice with Germany, and led [[VichyFrance]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Pétain as joining a military judgment of defeat to an ideological diagnosis of French decline. He and [[MaximeWeygand]] feared that continued fighting would deepen destruction and disorder, while Pétain blamed decades of Marxism for weakening France. After rejecting the proposed Franco-British union as subordination to a doomed Britain, he replaced [[PaulReynaud]] on 17 June and announced that he would seek terms before the armistice was complete.

## Key Characteristics

- Treated military defeat as requiring an armistice rather than overseas continuation.
- Connected battlefield collapse to conservative fears of disorder and revolution.
- Rejected the proposed Franco-British union.
- Became prime minister on 17 June 1940 and requested terms.
- Led the southern French regime based at Vichy after the armistice.
