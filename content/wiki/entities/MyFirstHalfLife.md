# 《我的前半生》 / My First Half Life

## Overview
《我的前半生》 / My First Half Life enters the wiki as both [[YiShu|亦舒]]'s novel and its 2017 Chinese TV adaptation, reread in EP385 after the drama's renewed short-video popularity.

## Current Profile
In this source, the work is a useful adaptation case because the recognizable premise stays intact while the moral and gender center changes. The hosts argue that the novel uses work, female ties, and middle-age sobriety to rebuild [[LuoZijun|罗子君]], while the drama leans toward [[HeHan|贺涵]]'s male mentorship, triangle conflict, third-party drama, and elite urban spectacle. The result is a work whose characters and famous scenes still circulate strongly, but whose gender politics and workplace fantasy now feel dated.

## Key Characteristics
- Adaptation case where plot familiarity hides a changed gender and moral center.
- Old-drama revival object newly circulated through short-video clips and famous scenes.
- Late-2010s urban fantasy built around consulting, luxury housing, fashion, and Shanghai lifestyle.
- Female-growth story split between novel-centered support and TV-centered male mentorship.
- Melodrama machine with vivid family, divorce, mistress, and workplace-conflict scenes.
- Middle-age text whose novel ending is read as more sober than the drama's romance.
