# MacBook

MacBook is Apple's laptop product line. [[zhongguo-xiaofeizhe-daidong-lafu-laolun-zengzhang-donghang-youhua-jipiao-tuigaiqian-zhengce-1005631805]] adds it as a possible endpoint for [[ChangXinMemory|ChangXin Memory]] chip testing: the episode says [[Apple]] was testing ChangXin chips for products such as iPhone and MacBook, but that ChangXin's 2026 capacity was already full.

[[zhe-bannian-women-you-maile-naxie-keji-haowu-1]] adds a practical reuse branch. The source says headless MacBooks, older M1 Mac minis, KVM setups, and remote control became newly useful as local machines for [[OpenClaw]]/"longxia"-style AI workflows, tying Mac hardware to [[LocalAgentExecution]] and [[PersonalInfrastructureCostAccounting]].
