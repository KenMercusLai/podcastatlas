# 刘艾 / Liu Ai (late Han)

刘艾 / Liu Ai enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-988-shoujin-lingru-ta-shi-hanmo-beican-de-huangdi-lsdkpakvk5u-omegcri-wstup-zl|Hanji 988]] as the attendant who helps [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] survive the immediate [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] pressure in 195 CE. When Qiang-Hu soldiers harass the emperor's lodging, Emperor Xian sends Liu Ai to [[JiaXu|贾诩]], whose banquet-and-promise intervention detaches those supporters from Li Jue.

The episode then places Liu Ai at the bridge scene when the emperor leaves Chang'an. Liu Ai shouts that the carriage carries the Son of Heaven, and [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s visible rebuke makes Guo Si's soldiers stand aside. His page is source-scoped: he matters here as a court intermediary whose voice temporarily turns weak imperial title into a usable safety claim.
