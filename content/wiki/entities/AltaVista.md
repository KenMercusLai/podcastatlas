# AltaVista

AltaVista appears in [[tech-20260127-0127-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260127-0127-mp-tech-pod-128]] as one of the early major search engines associated with the basement of the Palo Alto [[Equinix]] data-center building around 1995. The episode contrasts that dark, cage-filled infrastructure space with the later image of gleaming big-tech campuses.

The source uses AltaVista to make early internet history physically specific. Search did not begin as an abstract cloud product; it depended on servers, rooms, network proximity, and the emerging [[ColocationDataCenter]] and [[NeutralInternetExchange]] environment around [[PaloAltoInternetExchange|PAIX]].
