## Overview

来历 / Lai Li is the late-Han official who becomes the clearest loyal-remonstrance figure in the 124 CE deposition of [[EmperorShunOfHan|刘保]]. Hanji 801 first places him among the senior objectors to [[EmperorAnOfHan|汉安帝]]'s plan, then makes him the lone official who keeps standing at Hongdu Gate after the emperor threatens the remonstrants.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-801-han-andi-weishenme-henxin-fei-taizi-lgnhbsqnp6vdigsmqfkxiqmx2vdy|Hanji 801]] introduces Lai Li as taipu during the crisis around Liu Bao. When Fan Feng and Yan Hou's accusations move Emperor An toward deposing the ten-year-old crown prince, Lai Li joins [[HuanYanLateHan|Huan Yan]] and [[ZhangHaoRemonstratorLateHan|Zhang Hao]] in arguing that an under-fifteen child should not bear full guilt and that Liu Bao did not know Wang Nan and Bing Ji's alleged plots.

After Liu Bao is made Jiyin Wang on 124-09-07, Lai Li joins more than ten officials at Hongdu Gate to remonstrate that the prince is blameless. Emperor An answers through a Zhongchangshi edict that recodes the protest as hidden motive and threatens punishment. Xue Hao submits, other officials withdraw, but Lai Li rebukes the retreat and remains for several days.

The source ends with punishment rather than persuasion. Chen Zhong and the shangshu group memorialize against Lai Li; Emperor An dismisses Lai Li and his brothers, reduces his fief tax, degrades Lai Li's mother Wu'an Princess, and bars her from palace remonstrance. Lai Li therefore models a remonstrant whose persistence forces the ruler to expose coercion rather than revise the succession decision.

## Key Characteristics

- Taipu who objects before the formal deposition decision, not only after the result.
- Defender of Liu Bao's age, ignorance, and need for loyal tutors rather than punishment.
- Hongdu Gate remonstrant who remains after collective protest breaks under threat.
- Case of family and fief pressure being used against a direct speaker.
- Source-bounded example of principled remonstrance under succession-security panic.

- Hongdu Gate persistence: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-801-han-andi-weishenme-henxin-fei-taizi-lgnhbsqnp6vdigsmqfkxiqmx2vdy|Hanji 801]] says Lai Li joins the gate remonstrance, rejects Xue Hao's retreat, and stays after others withdraw.
- Punishment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-801-han-andi-weishenme-henxin-fei-taizi-lgnhbsqnp6vdigsmqfkxiqmx2vdy|Hanji 801]] says Emperor An dismisses Lai Li and his brothers, reduces his fief revenue, degrades Wu'an Princess, and blocks her palace remonstrance.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 801. It does not reconstruct Lai Li's earlier career, later offices, full family background, exact fief, or the complete participant list at Hongdu Gate. Earlier source notes kept Lai Li source-scoped; Hanji 801 is the first current note strong enough to give him a canonical profile.

## What Changed

- Created Lai Li as the central persistent remonstrant in the 124 CE Liu Bao deposition crisis.

## Relationships

- [[EmperorAnOfHan|汉安帝]] - ruler whose deposition decision Lai Li opposes and whose threat later punishes him.
- [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝 / 刘保]] - crown prince Lai Li defends before and after demotion to Jiyin Wang.
- [[ZhangHaoRemonstratorLateHan|张皓]] - fellow objector who expands the warning through the Li Crown Prince precedent.
- [[HuanYanLateHan|桓焉]] - fellow senior official in the first anti-deposition objection.
- [[DirectRemonstranceCriminalization|直谏入罪]] - pattern Lai Li embodies when loyal protest is recoded as punishable hidden motive.
- [[HeirSupportThreatPerception|储君拥戴威胁感]] - succession-security anxiety that explains why support for Liu Bao becomes threatening to An.
