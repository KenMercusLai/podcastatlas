# Steven Spielberg / 斯皮尔伯格

## Overview

Steven Spielberg is represented in the wiki through two science-fiction film contexts: Spielberg-style wonder as an entry point into UFO skepticism, and [[AIArtificialIntelligenceFilm|《人工智能》]] as a completed Kubrick project about programmed love and artificial childhood.

## Current Profile

The UFO branch uses a Spielberg film as a friendly doorway into [[UFOConspiracyCulture]], [[CarlSagan|Carl Sagan]], and [[TheDemonHauntedWorld|《魔鬼出没的世界》]]. The source does not treat the film as an argument about aliens. Its role is cultural: Spielberg-style wonder can make UFO and miracle motifs emotionally available, while [[ScientificSkepticism]] asks the audience to separate story pleasure from evidential belief.

The AI-film branch centers on [[AIArtificialIntelligenceFilm|《人工智能》]]. It rejects a simple binary where [[StanleyKubrick|Kubrick]] is cold and Spielberg is warm. Instead, the source argues that many supposedly sentimental parts came from Kubrick's original conception, while Spielberg's completed film preserves a tender surface and a dark core around irreversible love, maternal fantasy, and artificial childhood.

## Key Characteristics

- Spielberg's science-fiction work can make wonder emotionally available without settling whether the wonder should become belief.
- The UFO branch uses his cinema as a route toward skepticism rather than credulity.
- The AI-film branch uses him as the director who completed Kubrick's long-developed modern Pinocchio story.
- The source complicates the warm-Spielberg/cold-Kubrick contrast by treating [[AIArtificialIntelligenceFilm|《人工智能》]] as both tender and cruel.
- Spielberg's role in the current wiki is cultural and interpretive, not a full career biography.
