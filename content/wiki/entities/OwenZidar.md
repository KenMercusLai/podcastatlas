# Owen Zidar

## Overview
Owen Zidar is an economist identified by [[middlegarchs-are-the-new-oligarchs-32587514]] as a coauthor of research connecting private businesses, their owners, and the growth of top incomes.

## Current Profile
The episode places Zidar in a three-economist team with [[EricZwick]] and [[DannyYagan]]. Their linked tax-record work is presented as closing a data gap that had obscured how much rising top income came from pass-through businesses in ordinary industries.

## Key Characteristics
- Researcher on taxation, private business, and income distribution.
- Co-builder of the administrative-data analysis anchoring the episode.
- Contributor to the finding that pass-through income was a major component of top-income growth.

## Evidence
Research team:
- [[middlegarchs-are-the-new-oligarchs-32587514]] says Zidar worked with Zwick, Yagan, and Treasury economists to link millions of businesses to owners across tax databases.

Substantive finding:
- [[middlegarchs-are-the-new-oligarchs-32587514]] attributes to the research team the conclusion that pass-through businesses accounted for more than half of the analyzed growth in income going to the top 1% and 0.1%.

## Qualifications
The episode supplies a high-level account rather than a full methodological review. Numerical estimates and industry rankings remain source-scoped.
