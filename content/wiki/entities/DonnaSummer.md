## Overview

Donna Summer was an American singer whose recordings with [[GiorgioMoroder]], especially "I Feel Love" and "Love to Love You Baby," exemplify [[Disco]]'s vocal and electronic possibilities.

## Current Profile

[[439-disco-sex-and-race-in-seventies-america-glt8206137792|The source]] introduces Donna Gaines, who moved to Munich and became Donna Summer after marrying Helmut Sommer and anglicizing the surname. Her Moroder work connects soaring vocals to electronic, producer-led construction. "I Feel Love" is treated as a leading candidate for disco's most important record, while alternatives remain acknowledged.

## Key Characteristics

- Born Donna Gaines and developed part of her career in Munich.
- Worked with Giorgio Moroder on major disco recordings.
- Joined expressive vocals to constructed studio sound.
- "I Feel Love" represents disco's electronic direction.
