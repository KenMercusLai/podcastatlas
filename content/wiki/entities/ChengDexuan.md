# 程德玄 / Cheng Dexuan

Cheng Dexuan / 程德玄 is part of the suspicious succession sequence in [[170-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-xia-958158115]]. When [[WangJien|王继恩]] goes to summon [[ZhaoGuangyi|赵光义 / 宋太宗]], Cheng is already present or arrives with a story that someone had called for the Jin王 several times, but no one was visible when the door was opened.

The episode uses this detail as part of the atmosphere around [[CandleShadowAxeSound|烛影斧声]]. Cheng's story does not prove a plot, but it makes the handoff look less like a simple accidental notification and more like a transfer surrounded by prepared or semi-mystical signs.
