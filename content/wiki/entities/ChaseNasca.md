# Chase Nasca

Chase Nasca was a 16-year-old [[TikTok]] user discussed in [[tech-20260812-0812-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260812-0812-mp-tech-pod-128]]. The episode says he was among the U.S. users excluded from a 2021 TikTok filter-bubble safety update and later died by suicide after receiving repeated sad, depressive, and suicide-related videos.

For the wiki, Chase Nasca is a case anchor for [[PlatformLegalCausation]], [[SocialMediaProductLiability]], and [[Section230DesignWorkaround]]. His parents' wrongful-death lawsuit was dismissed after TikTok successfully invoked [[Section230]], but the episode says internal documents about his watched-video history and the withheld safeguard are relevant to the broader shift toward platform-design liability.
