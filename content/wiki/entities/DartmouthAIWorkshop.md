# Dartmouth AI Workshop / 达特茅斯 AI 夏季研讨会

## Overview
The Dartmouth AI workshop is the 1956 summer research meeting at Dartmouth College that the source treats as a key naming and field-forming moment for artificial intelligence.

## Current Profile
The source frames the workshop, nicknamed "达夏" by Yang Bin, as important because it gathered young, cross-disciplinary researchers around a new term without forcing a final consensus. Its value was not that it settled AI, but that it allowed symbolic reasoning, neural-network thinking, probabilistic reasoning, and machine-learning questions to appear in the same loose field.

As a wiki entity, the workshop anchors a broader innovation-sociology branch. It is used to ask what kinds of locations, groups, time structures, and interpersonal norms make original ideas possible before they are legible to institutions.

## Key Characteristics
- 1956 summer workshop at [[DartmouthCollege|Dartmouth College]] in New England.
- Associated with [[JohnMcCarthy|John McCarthy]]'s proposal and the naming of "artificial intelligence."
- Supported by Rockefeller Foundation funding according to the source.
- Loosely organized, with plans quickly giving way to free discussion and shifting attendance.
- Later interpreted by the episode as a possibility-preserving field rather than a consensus conference.

## Evidence
- Naming and setting: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] says the meeting occurred from mid-June to mid-August 1956 at Dartmouth and that McCarthy's proposal joined "artificial" and "intelligence."
- Participant structure: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] describes roughly 47 total participants, with a smaller core and discussion groups often ranging from three to eight people.
- Research pluralism: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] contrasts later symbolic-AI memory with the source's claim that neural-network and probabilistic routes were also visible.
- Innovation-field role: [[401-bianyu-manju-manzou-women-haineng-yinglai-lingwai-yige-datemao-si-de-xiatian-ma-f7495e05]] uses the workshop to define [[SlowGatheringImprovisation]] and [[PeripheralInnovationField]].
