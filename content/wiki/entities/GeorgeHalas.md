# George Halas

George Halas appears in [[the-nfl-1]] as one of the early professional-football figures who helped drive the formation of what became the [[NationalFootballLeague|NFL]]. The episode uses Halas to show that the league began as a fragile association of teams trying to standardize rules, gain legitimacy, and distinguish itself from college football.

His role is mainly historical in this source: he anchors the NFL's survival period before [[BertBell]]'s parity design, [[PeteRozelle]]'s media centralization, and the [[AmericanFootballLeague|AFL]] merger made the league a national business system.
