# Zhang Hua / 张华

Zhang Hua / 张华 appears in [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]] as the authorial figure behind [[Bowuzhi|《博物志》]] and as a Wei-Jin example of strange knowledge. The episode uses stories about arsenal omens, animal transformation, Wu conquest timing, purple qi between Dipper and Ox mansions, and buried swords to show how omen reading, objects, and political events become linked.

The source treats Zhang Hua as a bridge between official historical memory and zhiguai-style knowledge collection. His stories connect [[ChineseAstrologicalPolitics]] with the wiki's broader [[ZhiguaiArchiveValue|zhiguai archive]] branch.

## Key Claims
- Strange knowledge can sit close to official history rather than only popular folklore.
- Star and qi interpretation can turn hidden objects into historical explanation.
- Zhang Hua's material extends the wiki's Chinese strange-prose branch beyond pure ghost stories.
