## Overview
谢安 / Xie An of Xiapi is the local actor who kills [[XuFengBanditLateHan|徐凤]] in the 145 CE anti-bandit campaign. His case shows how official rewards can mobilize local kinship groups into practical suppression work.

## Current Profile
Xie An appears after Teng Fu's reward notices and campaign victories have fractured the Xu-Yang bandit field. When Xu Feng escapes and burns Dongcheng, Xie An leads his family and local associates to ambush Xu Feng and cut off his head.

The court rewards Xie An by making him Marquis of Pingxiang. He is therefore a compact example of [[BountyMobilizedPacification|赏募动员式平乱]]: a local participant converts a public bounty into decisive action against a fugitive rebel.

## Key Characteristics
- Xiapi local actor who responds to anti-bandit reward incentives.
- Kinship mobilizer who acts with family and associates rather than a formal army.
- Killer of Xu Feng after the rebel leader burns Dongcheng.
- Reward recipient whose marquisate makes local service politically visible.
