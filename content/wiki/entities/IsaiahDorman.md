# Isaiah Dorman

## Overview

Isaiah Dorman was a Black interpreter with the Seventh Cavalry whose earlier life among the Lakota and violent death during the [[BattleOfLittleBighorn]] expose the battle's crossed racial, familial, and political loyalties.

## Current Profile

The episode describes Dorman as the battle's only Black participant. He had lived among the [[LakotaPeople|Lakota]], married a Lakota woman, and served the U.S. force as an interpreter. During [[MarcusReno]]'s retreat he was trapped beneath his horse, wounded, and killed by people who regarded his U.S. service as betrayal.

His death survives through conflicting testimony. Some accounts describe torture and mutilation; another says [[SittingBull]] gave him water and tried unsuccessfully to prevent his killing. The secure synthesis is therefore his socially complex position and death during the rout, not one fully resolved final scene.

## Key Characteristics

- His life crossed Black, U.S. military, and Lakota social worlds.
- His interpreter role made cultural knowledge operationally useful to the Seventh Cavalry.
- His death was shaped by perceptions of betrayal as well as battlefield violence.
- Conflicting protection and killing accounts make his final moments evidentially uncertain.

## Qualifications

- The episode's claim that he was the battle's only Black participant requires broader documentary verification.
- Torture, dialogue, and Sitting Bull's intervention remain contested recollections.
- This source does not provide a complete biography or Dorman's own surviving perspective.
