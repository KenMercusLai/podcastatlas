# Bad Women: The Ripper Retold

## Overview

*Bad Women: The Ripper Retold* is [[HallieRubenhold]]'s historical podcast series about Polly, Annie, [[ElizabethStride|Elizabeth]], Catherine, and Mary Jane, the five women commonly identified as victims of [[JackTheRipper|Jack the Ripper]]. The sampler presents the series as a corrective to histories that reduce them to disputed occupational labels and the mythology of their killer.

## Current Profile

The available preview centers the women's lives rather than reopening suspect identification. Its Stride narrative moves from rural Sweden and domestic service through pregnancy, disease regulation, coercive medical treatment, stillbirth, labor exclusion, and sex work. That sequence makes biography, institutions, and misogynistic historical framing the series' main interpretive objects.

## Key Characteristics

- Victim-centered historical podcast led by historian [[HallieRubenhold]].
- Reconstructs individual lives obscured by offender-centered true-crime mythology.
- Challenges the blanket characterization of the five women as prostitutes.
- Connects gender, class, labor, law, medicine, stigma, and sexual double standards.
- Uses incomplete records while preserving uncertainty about consent, motive, and causation.
