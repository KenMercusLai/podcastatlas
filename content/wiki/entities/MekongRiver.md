# Mekong River / 湄公河

The Mekong River / 湄公河 is the organizing geography of [[ep267-tafang-meigonghe-yuji-hunfeiyi-yu-shuili-diguo-lkq2vxaatn4wxue7y5q1fc3-8yfk]]. The episode treats it as a route that can connect [[China]]'s high mountain river system, [[Laos|老挝]] travel and ritual scenes, [[Cambodia|柬埔寨]] and [[Angkor|吴哥]], [[TonleSapLake|洞里萨湖]], and the [[Vietnam|越南]] [[MekongDelta|湄公河三角洲]].

For the wiki, the river matters because it makes [[MekongRiverGeographicWriting]] possible. A single river line can hold together monsoon seasonality, human mobility, river valleys, fishery abundance, rice irrigation, colonial canal building, and climate vulnerability without reducing the region to one country story.
