## Overview

李绪 / Li Xu is the surrendered Han officer whom the sources place behind the conduct mistakenly attributed to [[LiLingWesternHan|李陵]]. He is a narrow but decisive blame-transfer figure: reported military assistance to [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] is assigned to Li Ling, Li Ling's family is killed, and Li Xu is then killed in Li Ling's retaliation.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-380-1-liling-touxiang-xiongnu-ruhe-yanbian-cheng-gengdade-beiju-ls70jnzybc7z3of-tpfbdwb9ns89|Hanji 380-1]] only previews a Li Xu-related event that causes Li Ling to carry the black mark and lose his family. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-380-2-liling-sandai-zhongliang-weihe-yidai-bi-yidai-can-lmtnhwjsyzlsfwpzcw18lubbauu0|Hanji 380-2]] fills that branch: after [[GongsunAoWesternHan|公孙敖]] reports that Li Ling is teaching Xiongnu troops and making weapons, the episode says the person involved was actually Li Xu, another Han officer who had surrendered to Xiongnu.

Li Xu's importance is causal rather than biographical. The episode does not give a full account of his earlier service, surrender, methods, or intent; it says that Li Ling, whose mother and wife died after the mistaken report, sends someone to kill him. Li Xu therefore connects military knowledge transfer, unstable identification, collective punishment, and retaliatory violence.

## Key Characteristics

- Former Han officer presented as having surrendered to Xiongnu.
- Identified by Hanji 380-2 as the actual trainer behind conduct attributed to Li Ling.
- Decisive mistaken-identity node in the punishment of Li Ling's family.
- Killed on Li Ling's order in retaliation for the family catastrophe.
- Source-bounded figure whose role is much clearer than his wider biography.

## Qualifications

The supplied episode summary writes “李旭,” while the wiki retains the received-history “李绪 / Li Xu” identity. The sources do not settle Li Xu's complete biography, original office, surrender circumstances, precise training activity, why the captive report produced confusion, or the mechanics of his death. The claim that name similarity caused the error remains the episode's compact explanation.

## What Changed

- Replaced Hanji 380-1's preview with Hanji 380-2's specific identification of Li Xu as the officer behind the reported military assistance.
- Added Li Xu's surrendered status and death in Li Ling's retaliation.
- Preserved the 李旭/李绪 name discrepancy as an explicit source-scope qualification.
