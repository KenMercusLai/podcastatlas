# Abdel Fattah al-Sisi

Abdel Fattah al-Sisi appears in [[spring-then-fall-a-weakened-muslim-brotherhood-6a7aef1b616564bf00d9b354]] as the Egyptian leader whose coup against [[MohamedMorsi]] became the source's strongest example of authoritarian backlash against the [[MuslimBrotherhood]]. [[GarethBrown]] describes the coup as an "autocratic sledgehammer" against the movement.

The page is source-scoped. In this episode, Sisi matters less as a full Egyptian political profile than as the state actor who demonstrates how fast a democratic opening can close when governments, militaries, and non-Brotherhood actors see Islamist electoral power as intolerable or untrustworthy.
