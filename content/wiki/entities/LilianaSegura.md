# Liliana Segura

## Overview

Liliana Segura is presented as an Intercept senior reporter who has covered criminal justice and capital punishment for two decades.

## Current Profile

In the bounded source, Segura combines reporting experience, execution witnessing, legal history, and racial history to argue that capital punishment cannot be made fair through better administration. She treats botched procedures and secrecy as structural evidence rather than isolated malfunctions and connects Alabama's system to lynching and racialized protection narratives.

## Key Characteristics

- Investigates capital punishment through cases, institutions, and execution practice.
- Distinguishes visually smooth execution from evidence of a painless experience.
- Connects current death-penalty administration to racial and political history.
- Rejects “broken system” language when it implies that state killing could be repaired into justice.
