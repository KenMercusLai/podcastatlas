## Overview
杜诗 / Du Shi now enters the wiki through two Guangwu consolidation windows: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-679-zhuwei-shahai-liuxiu-gege-weihe-reng-neng-shanzhong-lqns6ktym8rgv3y5tlczcyfyzxdb|Hanji 679]] as the Luoyang pacification official who kills Xiao Guang after troop abuses, and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-705-1-diwang-moulue-liuxiu-ruhe-yi-sheling-nizhuan-caiyuan-fengyun-lq2m06p7eczj9ebysxpl84bkzzi|Hanji 705-1]] as the Nanyang taishou whose clean and fair administration becomes a local prosperity exemplar. Together, the current profile makes Du Shi a civilian-protection and welfare-delivery official rather than a battlefield commander.

## Current Profile
Hanji 679 supplies the earlier founding-capital layer. Liu Xiu sends Du Shi to Luoyang to comfort and settle the people after Zhu Wei's surrender. When Xiao Guang lets troops seize goods and harm civilians and refuses correction, Du Shi kills him and reports upward; Liu Xiu approves the action and rewards Du Shi because taking Luoyang must include protecting its people.

The source says Du Shi governs Nanyang with cleanliness and fairness, leaving people peaceful and free from anxiety. His practical contribution is material: he builds water conservancy, opens uncultivated land, and helps households increase livelihood security.

The popular comparison with Zhao Xinchen matters because it turns administrative performance into public memory. Nanyang people say there was once "Zhao father" and now "Du mother," presenting Du Shi as a nurturing local official whose authority is measured by durable welfare rather than coercive display.

## Key Characteristics
- Eastern Han official whose current profile begins with Luoyang civilian protection and later develops into Nanyang welfare governance.
- Clean and fair official whose governance is remembered as calming ordinary households.
- Military-discipline enforcer whose punishment of Xiao Guang shows that founding troops cannot abuse civilians with impunity.
- Practical public-works actor associated with water conservancy and land opening.
- Prosperity exemplar whose success links agricultural expansion to political stabilization.
- Locally praised official compared with Zhao Xinchen through the "Du mother" memory.

## Qualifications
This page is bounded to Hanji 679 and Hanji 705-1. It does not reconstruct Du Shi's full career, inventions, dates, or relationship to all Luoyang and Nanyang institutions. Hanji 679 supports an early civilian-protection discipline case, but Xiao Guang's full identity and exact command structure remain source-scoped. Hanji 705-1's durable evidence is local prosperity and official memory; specific technical details of the water works remain source-scoped.
