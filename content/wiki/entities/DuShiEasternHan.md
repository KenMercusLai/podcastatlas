## Overview
杜诗 / Du Shi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-705-1-diwang-moulue-liuxiu-ruhe-yi-sheling-nizhuan-caiyuan-fengyun-lq2m06p7eczj9ebysxpl84bkzzi|Hanji 705-1]] as the Nanyang taishou whose clean and fair administration becomes a local prosperity exemplar under Liu Xiu. The episode presents him not as a battlefield actor but as evidence that Guangwu consolidation also depends on local officials who deliver water works, land opening, and household stability.

## Current Profile
The source says Du Shi governs Nanyang with cleanliness and fairness, leaving people peaceful and free from anxiety. His practical contribution is material: he builds water conservancy, opens uncultivated land, and helps households increase livelihood security.

The popular comparison with Zhao Xinchen matters because it turns administrative performance into public memory. Nanyang people say there was once "Zhao father" and now "Du mother," presenting Du Shi as a nurturing local official whose authority is measured by durable welfare rather than coercive display.

## Key Characteristics
- Eastern Han Nanyang administrator whose current profile is built around local welfare rather than court or battlefield power.
- Clean and fair official whose governance is remembered as calming ordinary households.
- Practical public-works actor associated with water conservancy and land opening.
- Prosperity exemplar whose success links agricultural expansion to political stabilization.
- Locally praised official compared with Zhao Xinchen through the "Du mother" memory.
