## Overview

张于 / Zhang Yu enters the wiki as the Longxi administrator whom [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Emperor Zhang]] appoints Protector of the Qiang after [[FuYuEasternHan|Fu Yu]] dies. Hanji 757 presents him as a harsh frontier successor: he defeats [[MiWuQiangLeader|Mi Wu]], receives surrender contact, then uses a poisoned banquet and ambush to kill Mi Wu and hundreds of Qiang leaders.

## Current Profile

Hanji 757 first places Zhang Yu in emergency replacement. Zhangdi is alarmed by Fu Yu's death and appoints Zhang Yu from Longxi to the Qiang-protector post. Zhang Yu leads ten thousand Han troops to garrison Linqiang while the frontier situation remains unsettled.

The decisive episode begins after Mi Wu again unites Qiang forces and attacks Jincheng fortifications. Zhang Yu sends a subordinate force to fight in Mucheng Valley, and Mi Wu is defeated. Mi Wu then sends an interpreter-envoy to request surrender and comes to Linqiang with his commanders.

Zhang Yu turns that surrender setting into revenge. He holds a banquet, poisons the wine, uses hidden troops, kills Mi Wu and a reported eight hundred-plus Qiang leaders, and cuts off Mi Wu's head to sacrifice at Fu Yu's grave. He then attacks the remnants, but [[MiTangQiangLeader|Mi Tang]] rebuilds a broader coalition through marriages and hostages.

## Key Characteristics

- Emergency successor to Fu Yu as Protector of the Qiang.
- Frontier commander who restores battlefield initiative after Fu Yu's death.
- Uses surrender contact and banquet hospitality as a killing opportunity.
- Gains revenge but fails to settle the Qiang problem.
- Source-bounded route preserving Hanji 757's 张于 spelling.
