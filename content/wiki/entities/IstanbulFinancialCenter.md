# Istanbul Financial Center

The Istanbul Financial Center is the financial-services project discussed in [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]]. The episode says it was built in 2023 to attract international banks and financial firms to [[Istanbul]].

The source treats the project as evidence of ambition rather than proof of transformation. Officials describe interest from Gulf banks and consultancies, but the episode says many of the interested firms appear to be small boutique consultancies. That makes the project part of Turkey's [[FinancialHubCredibilityGap]]: buildings and tax breaks do not by themselves create Dubai- or Riyadh-level trust.
