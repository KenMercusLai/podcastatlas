# 李萍（金庸小说） / Li Ping (Jin Yong)

## Overview

李萍 is a fictional character in [[LegendOfTheCondorHeroes|《射雕英雄传》]], widow of 郭啸天 and mother of 郭靖. The bounded source presents her as an ordinary woman whose forced journey, solitary childbirth, labor, and moral clarity become the foundation for her son's identity and public duty.

## Current Profile

Li Ping's strength is constructed through action rather than inherited heroic status. After abduction and forced travel, she escapes, gives birth in snow, sustains herself and her child through labor on the grassland, and repeatedly tells 郭靖 who his father was and what conduct that memory requires. The episode treats this combination of endurance, explicit teaching, and approval of principled conduct as [[ParentalEmbodiedModeling|embodied family education]].

Her death is interpreted in relation to that long survival. When 成吉思汗 uses her to pressure 郭靖 into attacking the Song, she kills herself to remove the coercive choice and reiterates that she did not raise him to betray his country. The source values the act not as death in isolation, but as the endpoint of a life that had already demonstrated the value and difficulty of staying alive.

## Key Characteristics

- Survives abduction, forced travel, extreme weather, childbirth, and hard labor.
- Raises 郭靖 without elite protection or secure material conditions.
- Teaches identity, paternal memory, responsibility, and loyalty explicitly.
- Reinforces moral conduct through approval as well as instruction.
- Accepts hardship without treating survival as moral compromise.
- Uses her final choice to release 郭靖 from a coercive conflict between filial duty and public loyalty.

## Qualifications

The profile is based on one admiring podcast reading. It does not prove that Li Ping alone formed 郭靖: the source also credits the grassland environment, the 江南七怪, later experience, and his own choices. Her death is a fictional ethical act under extreme coercion, not a general recommendation that parents prove love or principle through self-harm.

## What Changed

- Created a bounded profile joining prolonged survival, explicit identity teaching, and moral modeling.
- Distinguished the significance of Li Ping's death from a general glorification of maternal self-sacrifice.
- Preserved mentors, environment, and personal choice as additional causes of 郭靖's development.
