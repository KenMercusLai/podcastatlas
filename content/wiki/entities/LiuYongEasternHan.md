## Overview

刘永 / Liu Yong is synthesized as a Liu-clan eastern rival whose current wiki profile begins in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-674-ruoguo-wuwaijiao-gengshi-huangdi-liuxuan-you-duo-biequ-lu59astedsljoefnl6yr9d4ilirh|Hanji 674]] with a Liang-king base, recruitment, and expansion across Shanyang, Pei, Chu, Huaiyang, Runan, and twenty-eight cities. Later source notes keep him visible as a politically important claimant whose title offers and commander network affect [[ChimeiRebellion|Chimei]]-era and Jianwu consolidation priorities until news of his death helps make [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|隗嚣]]'s hostage-only submission more urgent.

## Current Profile

Hanji 674 supplies the current origin layer. Liu Yong starts from a Liang-king position, recruits soldiers, appoints Zhou Jian, and extends influence through Shanyang, Pei, Chu, Huaiyang, Runan, and twenty-eight cities. He also appoints Jiang Qiang, [[DongXianEasternHan|董宪]], and [[ZhangBuEasternHan|张步]] over Qing and Xu-related fields, making him more than a local city-holder.

Hanji 680 keeps Liu Yong in the parallel-campaign field while [[LiuXiu|刘秀]] returns toward Luoyang and assigns commanders elsewhere. Hanji 686 then shows the Liu Yong network absorbing [[SuMaoEasternHan|苏茂]] after Su Mao rebels, kills Pan Jian, and occupies Guang'e/Guangle, giving Liu Yong a Da Sima-level subordinate inside the broader former-Gengshi cleanup.

Hanji 687 and Hanji 688 frame Liu Yong as a legitimacy-sensitive rival. Hanji 687 ties Gai Yan's Suiyang pressure and Fu Long's Qing/Xu mission to Liu Yong's weakening position. Hanji 688 says the host treats Liu Yong as more politically important than [[PengChongEasternHan|彭宠]] because a Liu-surnamed claimant carries stronger imperial-legitimacy leverage, and it shows Liu Yong trying to make Zhang Bu Qi king after making Dong Xian Haixi king.

Hanji 689-1 and Hanji 695 keep Liu Yong inside the multi-front cleanup background rather than making him the direct center of those episodes. Hanji 698 then notes that Wei Xiao sends [[WeiXunEasternHan|隗恂]] east after hearing Liu Yong and Peng Chong have been killed, so Liu Yong's disappearance from the field changes the risk calculus for other autonomous powers.

## Key Characteristics

- Liu-clan eastern claimant whose Liang-king base gives his rebellion more dynastic leverage than many local military rivals.
- Network builder who uses appointments to attach Zhou Jian, Jiang Qiang, Dong Xian, Zhang Bu, and Su Mao-related forces.
- Eastern-theater rival whose pressure stretches Liu Xiu's consolidation beyond Guanzhong and Shu.
- Title-granter whose kingship offers to Dong Xian and Zhang Bu test Han norms around Liu-family kingship.
- Defeated or removed rival whose death helps narrow Wei Xiao's strategic room.

## Qualifications

This page synthesizes compact source-note evidence and does not reconstruct Liu Yong's full biography, exact title chronology, or complete campaign order. Hanji 674 gives the entry layer but leaves Zhou Jian, Jiang Qiang, exact commandery control, and local routes source-scoped. Hanji 686 and Hanji 688 show Liu Yong through subordinate incorporation and title politics rather than his court institutions. Hanji 687, Hanji 689-1, Hanji 695, and Hanji 698 mostly preserve Liu Yong as a strategic-pressure reference. The page therefore treats his importance as structurally clear but keeps detailed operations and death mechanics source-scoped.
