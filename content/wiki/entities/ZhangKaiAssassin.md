# 张闿 / Zhang Kai (assassin)

张闿 / Zhang Kai enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1019-jiemi-chenminwang-liuchong-de-chuanqi-yisheng-ll2fma7wupp5gczudysfett5lhio|Hanji 1019]] as the assassin [[YuanShu|袁术]] sends against [[LiuChongChenKing|刘宠]] and [[LuoJun|骆俊]]. The episode says Zhang Kai pretends to surrender to [[ChenStateLateHan|陈国]], then uses the access created by that posture to kill both leaders.

The source also notes that Zhang Kai is the same figure associated with the earlier killing of [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s father Cao Song, but it does not develop that episode here. His role in Hanji 1019 is narrower: he is the operational mechanism through which Yuan Shu turns a failed grain demand into leadership removal.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-971-luanshi-miyun-shui-sha-le-caocao-laodie-ljh3hdhssigmv78bueo-hprfqwku|Hanji 971]] backfills that earlier Cao Song branch. In the [[HouHanshu|《后汉书》]] version presented by this source, Zhang Kai is [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]]'s subordinate at Yinping who escorts [[CaoSong|曹嵩]], sees the convoy wealth, and kills Cao Song and [[CaoDeLateHan|曹德]] for loot near the Hua/Fei route. The source keeps this as a competing attribution against [[Sanguozhi|《三国志》]]'s Tao Qian-sent-cavalry version.
