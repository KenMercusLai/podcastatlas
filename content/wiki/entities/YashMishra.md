# Yash Mishra

Yash Mishra appears in [[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]] as a hunger striker and protest voice in the [[CockroachMovement]]. The source quotes him comparing education to water and saying protesters felt energized by the fear they had induced in the government.

His role helps the wiki keep the movement's moral claim separate from the meme wrapper. For Mishra, the protest is about a basic public good, not only about one exam leak or one ministerial resignation.
