# 林太平（《欢乐英雄》） / Lin Taiping

## Overview

林太平 is the quietest of the four protagonists of [[HappyHeroesGuLong|《欢乐英雄》]]. [[YanQiHappyHeroes|燕七]] brings the stranded young man to 富贵山庄, where he joins the household before his family history is revealed late in the novel.

## Current Profile

The bounded discussion describes him as silent, delicate, and comparatively low in narrative visibility through the middle of the book. His mother 魏夫人, his father, and the later “陆上龙王” material concentrate his importance in the final portion, contributing to an imbalance between early ensemble life and late identity revelation.

His willingness to die for someone he loves gives him a clear ethical function: it answers the assertion that nobody will die for another person through action rather than abstract argument.

## Key Characteristics

- Quiet and physically delicate presence within the four-person ensemble.
- Rescued by Yan Qi before entering 富贵山庄.
- Receives less sustained middle-book attention than the other protagonists.
- Carries a late-revealed family history centered on 魏夫人 and his father.
- Demonstrates devotion through willingness to sacrifice himself for another.
