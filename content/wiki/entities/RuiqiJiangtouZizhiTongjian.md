# 芮淇讲透资治通鉴 / Ruiqi Jiangtou Zizhi Tongjian

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-04-yurang-wei-zhiyao-fuchou-ln34tydha6iqwlrgmw7iugqfgfik]] adds an earlier sequence point in the show's reading of [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]]'s Zhou annals. The episode uses [[ZhiYao|智瑶]] for [[TalentVirtueDistinction|才德之分]], [[YuRang|豫让]] for [[RetainerReciprocityEthic|士为知己者死]], [[ZhaoXiangzi|赵襄子]] for succession and conquest, and [[ThreeJinVassalRecognition|三晋受封]] for formal state recognition.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-05-guangcai-duomu-de-weiwenhou-1-lmkxzbmwhw3nhurape-eebydqcdl]] fills in the promised [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] leadership image. It reads respect for [[ZiXia|子夏]], [[TianZifang|田子方]], and [[DuanGanmu|段干木]], the rainy hunting appointment, and the Han-Zhao diplomacy story as [[RenhePoliticalPower|人和政治力量]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-1-lk-6ejgmjhvukqgtlz2lowu8qx-v]] develops the promised chancellor-selection question. The episode turns [[LiKe|李克]]'s advice to [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] into [[SituationalCharacterTests]] and [[TalentReferralQuality]], then uses [[WuQi|吴起]] to compare flawed talent against military need.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-2-lmfzo4sl9nd5kz3mrxdl1dl9efei]] continues the show's explanatory and analogy-driven style: it turns [[SimaRangju|司马穰苴]] and [[WuQi|吴起]] into organization and military-leadership cases before returning to compact chronology.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-1-lvkp-ju7pf5z5cz3thojv3s2fkyx]] extends the same reading style into [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]]'s early reign. The episode connects compact annals to [[ZhengState|郑国]]'s buffer-state dilemma, [[WangziDing|王子定]]'s exile, disaster-omen interpretation, and the move from aristocratic rules toward factional assassination.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]] continues the "two assassinations" arc by moving from [[CelestialOmenPoliticalResponsibility]] into [[NieZheng|聂政]], [[NieYing|聂英]], [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] variants, [[SongState|宋国]]'s lineage status, and [[FushuCity|负黍]] as a territorial-churn case.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-3-lsbx8l7wxeioae2lwuwkkkutpdwn]] turns the same title into a bridge episode rather than another assassination story. It carries [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]]'s chronology through years 9-15, using hollow [[JinState|晋国]] rulership, [[TianFamilyReplacementOfQi|田氏代齐]], Qin pressure on Han/Wei, and a doubtful [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] / [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]] notice to prepare the later [[WuQi|吴起]] and [[VirtueOverNaturalBarriers|德胜地险]] discussion.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-1-lq5r3xe-3y7al9nkbc8kyhntipll]] returns to [[WuQi|吴起]] and reads his [[WeiState|魏国]] career as organization politics. The episode moves from [[VirtueOverNaturalBarriers]] in the West River dialogue to [[TransitionFitOverMerit]] in the chancellor dispute with [[TianWenWeiChancellor|田文]], then to [[GongshuWeiChancellor|公叔]]'s court trap.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-2-lvaqlgyccrugfo0l-wuwf1mt1kcm]] continues that arc into [[ChuState|楚国]]. It reads Wu Qi's reforms under [[ChuDaoWang|楚悼王]] through [[WarringStatesReformBacklash]], then widens back to annalistic succession notices and a [[HanFei|韩非]]-derived [[LegalistRulerTechnique]] reading of [[ZhaoJinghou|赵靖侯]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-3-ltca-ikuzo9ej7a0lfrgwv2-wulb]] completes the Wu Qi title arc while widening the show's source-critical range. It connects [[QinXianGong|秦献公]]'s coup, Tian-family Qi succession drift, [[JixiaAcademy|稷下学宫]], solar-eclipse omen skepticism, and [[WuQi|吴起]]'s death into one early-Warring-States transition reading.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl]] moves from reform backlash into broad early Warring States disorder. The episode frames Qi, Yan, Han, Zhao, Wei, and Chu conflict as [[EarlyWarringStatesInterstateWar]], records the final endings of Jiang-line Qi and Jin, then turns [[Zisi|子思]]'s Wei advice into [[UseStrengthsOverFaults]] and [[CourtFeedbackCollapse]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] begins [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]]'s reign with a tighter statecraft lesson. It uses [[HanState|韩国]]'s destruction of [[ZhengState|郑国]] for [[GenerationalFamilyStrategy]] and [[StrategicCapitalRelocation]], then uses [[Shiji|《史记》]]'s [[TaishiDan|太史旦]] passage for [[KinshipLegitimacyDiplomacy]] between Zhou and Qin.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-2-lmnqfjmydygqnfec6xhzpixralvi]] continues that Zhou Lie Wang branch into assassination, succession, and administrative evaluation. The episode uses the [[HanAihou|韩哀侯]] killing for source-variant caution, [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]]'s unclear heir plan for [[SuccessionNonDesignationRisk]], and [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]]'s Jimo/Ayi investigation for [[IndependentOfficialAudit]] and [[ChronicleChronologyDrift]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-3-lmhj4le2mwu1yhhl-qmuoqkfmhjf]] completes that Wei succession arc. The episode turns [[GongzhongHuan|公中缓]]'s appeal to Zhao, Han-Zhao victory at [[ZhuozeBattle|浊泽之战]], and the coalition's failed settlement into [[SuccessionCrisisIntervention]] and [[CoalitionSettlementFailure]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-4-lhjz-m12pedkfszxyzgqtjnpsn3p]] supplements the same accession horizon from another angle. Instead of following the later civil war, it uses [[ZizhiTongjianBu|《资治通鉴补》]] to reconstruct how the future [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]] may have recovered heir status after being sent to [[ZhongshanState|中山国]]. The episode turns [[ZhaoCangtang|赵苍唐]]'s gifts, title correction, and [[Shijing|《诗经》]] allusions into [[PowerEtiquetteReading|power etiquette reading]] and [[PoetryAsElitePoliticalLanguage|poetry as elite political language]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-11-shijie-fengqi-yunyong-1-lt60b2gddiosf5kade35uopwohi5]] opens the show's Zhouji 11 and [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]] branch. It reads Qi, Zhao, Wei, Han, Song, and Qin war notices through [[EarlyWarringStatesInterstateWar]], then turns [[QinXianGong|秦献公]]'s [[ShimenBattle|石门]] victory and [[FufuRobe|黻服]] gift into [[QinEastwardPressure]] and [[RitualRecognitionOfMilitaryPower]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-11-shijie-fengqi-yunyong-2-llzzalowv5vmbvgd6l-jujltkbyf]] completes that Zhouji 11 transition by moving from Qin battlefield pressure to Qin Xiao Gong's need for state restoration. The episode ties Wei-Han-Zhao infighting, Qin's [[Shaoliang|少梁]] victory, [[WeyState|卫国]] title demotion, [[YanState|燕国]] succession, and [[QinDiplomaticExclusion|秦国外交排斥]] into the prelude for [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-12-gongsunyang-qiuzhi-lu-shang-de-la-guanxi-zou-houmen-lvv4-bz8sm9scezci-o-msapkvil]] fills the personnel bridge between that prelude and the reform episodes. It reads [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]]'s recruitment edict through [[QinMugong|秦穆公]] memory and Qin's lost-land shame, then follows [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] from [[GongshuWeiChancellor|公叔痤]]'s ignored recommendation in Wei to [[JingJian|景监]]'s [[PrivateAccessTalentChannel|private-access]] introduction in Qin.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-13-gongsunyang-keke-bianfa-re-shashen-1-lgmjapc2eecefbdshnpenzfcv6ga]] shifts from Qin's battlefield pressure to Qin's internal reform machinery. It follows [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]], [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]], [[GanLong|甘龙]], and [[YingSi|太子嬴驷]] through [[ShangYangReforms]], [[ReformWithoutPopularDeliberation]], [[CollateralPunishmentAndGuiltByAssociation|什伍连坐]], [[MilitaryMeritRankSystem|军功爵制]], and [[XimuLixin|徙木立信]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-14-qiweiwang-buming-zeyi-yiming-jingren-lrs6rbifyiqrojxg0mccpg7sf9rv]] returns to [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]]'s 358-356 BCE annals, combining Qin-Wei-Han pressure, Qin-Chu marriage diplomacy, and multi-state meetings with the [[QiChronologyDispute|齐威王编年争议]]. It then reads [[ChunyuKun|淳于髡]]'s [[AllusiveRemonstrance|隐语进谏]] as the trigger for [[QiWeiwangPoliticalTurnaround|齐威王一鸣惊人式转向]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]] extends the [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]] branch into 353 BCE. It uses [[SunBin|孙膑]], [[PangJuan|庞涓]], [[TianJi|田忌]], [[TianJiHorseRacing|田忌赛马]], and [[GuilingBattle|桂陵之战]] to show the show's move from personal ordeal to military strategy, then uses [[EasternWesternZhouSplit|东周西周分裂]] and [[JiangYi|江乙]]'s stories against [[ZhaoXixu|昭奚恤]] to return to royal decline and court rhetoric.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] extends the [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]] branch into 352-350 BCE. It pairs Qin's attacks on Wei and Wei's diplomatic repairs with [[ShenBuhai|申不害]]'s Han governance lessons and [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s second reform round through [[Xianyang|咸阳]], [[HouseholdSeparationLaw|分居令]], and [[QinAdministrativeStandardization]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-18-zhongguo-lishishang-di-yi-li-rentoushui-lgqnequk0rxejqgihwdqxqyqnsvq]] extends that branch into 349-343 BCE. It reads [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s fiscal reform as likely [[PollTax|人头税]], uses [[ZhaoFan|赵范]]'s failed attack on [[Handan|邯郸]] to explain [[MilitaryActionTerminology|伐/侵/袭 terminology]], and turns "诸侯会于京师" plus "王致伯于秦" into paired lessons about [[AudienceVsMeetingDiplomacy|朝会之别]] and [[ZhouHegemonRecognition|周室霸权承认]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-19-maling-zhi-zhan-lhbapt09x2ptdqx0uopkp8apbada]] extends the branch into 342-341 BCE and the [[MalingBattle|马陵之战]] setup. It reads the Qi rescue-Han debate through [[DelayedRescueStatecraft|晚救权谋]], the Wei appointment of [[PrinceShenWei|太子申]] through [[CrownPrinceCommandRisk|太子挂帅风险]], and [[SunBin|孙膑]]'s plan through [[SunziBingfa|《孙子兵法》]] and [[ForcedMarchOperationalRisk|急行军作战风险]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-20-pangjuan-si-ci-shu-xia-ls-8-3jhz5q1c5ca11mhdzmz4wg4]] completes that battle-story branch. It turns the setup into a clear causal chain: [[SunBin|孙膑]] stages [[DecreasingStovesDeception|减灶诱敌]], [[PangJuan|庞涓]] mistakes false weakness for desertion, rapid pursuit creates vulnerability, and the torchlit Maling ambush ends with Pang Juan's death, Wei defeat, and [[PrinceShenWei|太子申]]'s capture.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-21-zouji-xia-yinzhao-tianji-guowai-binan-lkvmjoxolgfgw-ig7ir-ngvjifij]] follows that victory into Qi court politics. It turns [[ZouJi|成侯邹忌]]'s dislike of [[TianJi|田忌]] into a staged divination case, uses textual differences between [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]], [[Shiji|《史记》]], and [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] to explain "doing great things" as treason-coded language, and closes with Tian Ji and [[SunBin|孙膑]] fleeing to [[ChuState|楚国]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-22-gongsunyang-zha-wei-gongzi-ang-lss1l7lojfwgwdtcje7m5ijodpxi]] returns from Qi court aftershock to Qin's attack on Wei in 340 BCE. It reads [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s advice to [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]] through [[QinEastwardPressure]], explains his personal command through [[FarmingWarStatecraft|耕战逻辑]], and treats the capture of [[GongziAng|公子卬 / 公子昂]] as [[PeaceParleyEntrapment|和谈诱捕]] powered by [[MotivatedBias|动机偏差]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-23-weihuiwang-hui-buyong-gongshu-zhi-yan-lveaqklo7oncmqudgmdk7l03qhsf]] completes that Qin-Wei and Shang Yang arc. It turns the capture into Wei's Hexi cession and move to [[Daliang|大梁]], contrasts [[SunBin|孙膑]]'s reusable deception with Shang Yang's [[TacticalCredibilityCost|战术信用成本]], then follows Qin Xiao Gong's death into [[GongziQian|公子虔]]'s accusation and Shang Yang's [[LegalistSelfBindingBackfire|作法自毙]] ending.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-24-shangyang-yue-wo-za-lie-le-lt4yd6pwlezqia5yljg-gankyhut]] revisits Shang Yang's ending as a structural problem rather than only a dramatic punishment. It adds [[PowerExitTrap|权力退场困境]], [[ZhaoLiang|赵良]]'s warning setup, [[BailiXi|百里奚]] and [[QinMugong|秦穆公]] as Qin precedent, the [[CaoCao|曹操]] analogy, and a [[TextualVariantPoliticalStakes|source-wording]] comparison between [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] and [[Shiji|《史记》]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-25-zhaoliang-dui-shangyang-shuo-ni-bupei-lnqmwjbrc9qomwrdt5brourf-78p]] completes the Zhao Liang branch. The episode turns the Baili Xi comparison against [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]], using plain conduct, public mourning, guard dependence, and the "危若朝露" warning to add [[PeopleBasedPoliticalSecurity|民心型政治安全]] to the show's Qin reform-backlash arc. It also uses [[Shiji|《史记》]] to preserve a richer but doubtful resignation-and-retirement route.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-26-mengzi-laile-ln4twwp2frmr-xpumu0rqg-2gpyn]] moves from Legalist exit risk to Mencian political language. It records [[ShenBuhai|申不害]]'s death, follows [[ZhangKaidi|张开地]] and [[ZhangPingHan|张平]] into the [[ZhangLiang|张良]] family background, keeps [[TaiqiuShe|宋太丘社]] as an unresolved omen, and uses [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s reading of [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] and [[Zisi|子思]] to add [[RenyiBenefitOrdering|仁义优先的利益秩序]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-27-weiguan-weihuiwang-he-qiweiwang-shi-za-shangye-hupeng-lminlxhrm5bethub9kyy-8blimrb]] returns from Mencian language to interstate positioning.
