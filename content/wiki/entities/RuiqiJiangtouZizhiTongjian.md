# 芮淇讲透资治通鉴 / Ruiqi Jiangtou Zizhi Tongjian

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-181-jiemi-liubang-feili-taizi-de-zhenshi-yuanyin-lgnbvnnfwxe4weupqgz-rh9cbp2s|Hanji 181]] continues the show's early-Han sequence from Zhao kingship into succession politics. It uses [[QiJi|戚姬 / 戚夫人]], [[LiuRuyi|刘如意]], [[LuZhi|吕雉]], [[LiuYing|刘盈]], [[ZhouChang|周昌]], and [[ZhaoYao|赵尧]] to argue that Liu Bang's failed heir-replacement idea is less a romance story than a political-base and protection problem.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-180-xihan-diyi-yinghan-guangao-de-gushi-1-li5u-dmsmb1lp4ny3rk02kaiyu4k|Hanji 180]] continues the show's post-Baideng early-Han branch from the origin of heqin into implemented compromise, Guanzhong population policy, and the Guan Gao case. It extends [[HanXiongnuHeqinPolicy|汉匈和亲政策]] through a substitute-princess mission, adds [[GuanzhongEliteRelocation|关中豪强迁徙]], and turns [[GuanGao|贯高]] into the episode's moral center through [[TortureResistantExoneration|刑讯守口式申冤]] for [[ZhangAo|张敖]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-179-lishishang-heqin-zenme-laide-lslks-ovbomneornk4j-q6-ns0x1|Hanji 179]] continues the show's post-Baideng branch into the origin and limits of [[HanXiongnuHeqinPolicy|汉匈和亲政策]]. It combines the failed 百人县 assassination attempt by [[GuanGao|贯高]] and [[ZhaoWuZhaoMinister|赵午]], an early [[HeavyAgricultureLightCommercePolicy|重农轻商政策]] note, and [[LouJing|刘敬 / 楼敬]]'s full heqin design involving [[LuYuanPrincess|鲁元公主]], [[MaoDunChanyu|冒顿单于]], goods, and envoys. The episode's interpretive move is to treat heqin as a practical frontier-management tool after military weakness, while keeping its family cost and inability to end Xiongnu raids visible.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-178-maodun-weihe-yao-fangguo-liubang-zhe-cai-shi-zhenxiang-1-lumszkbr-klnlodxmxdf-n8p5wym|Hanji 178]] completes the show's current Baideng branch by moving from [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s encirclement into the release decision and policy aftermath. It emphasizes [[ChenPing|陈平]]'s indirect approach to [[YanzhiXiongnuConsort|阏氏]], then argues that [[MaoDunChanyu|冒顿单于]] also weighs missing allies, Han reinforcements, occupation cost, and limited Xiongnu aims under [[EncirclementReleaseCalculus|围困开口式风险权衡]]. The episode ends by turning [[LouJing|刘敬 / 楼敬]] from punished warner into policy adviser through the proposed [[HanXiongnuHeqinPolicy|汉匈和亲政策]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-177-beikun-baidengshan-liubang-mingxuan-yixian-ljdmxf6bej8ju0gmvm9cp48gy0on|Hanji 177]] moves the show's early-Han branch from court ritual back to northern military danger. It follows [[HanWangXin|韩王信]] after [[Mayi|马邑]], has [[LouJing|刘敬 / 楼敬]] identify [[MaoDunChanyu|冒顿单于]]'s false weakness as a trap, and ends with [[LiuBang|刘邦]] surrounded at the [[BaidengEncirclement|白登之围]]. The episode's interpretive weight is on how frontier-vassal mistrust, reconnaissance misreading, winter conditions, and Xiongnu cavalry mobility combine before the later Chen Ping release story.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-176-shusuntong-ruhe-wei-dahan-zhiding-liyi-zhidu-loeoqccrfwbmtr634ukyau9xykqy|Hanji 176]] completes the show's Shusun Tong ritual branch. It uses Li Bai's frontier-poetry opening and the coming Baideng pressure as a frame, then moves to [[ChanglePalace|长乐宫]], where [[ShusunTong|叔孙通]]'s court audience makes [[LiuBang|刘邦]] feel imperial dignity through rank, silence, toasts, and censorial discipline. The episode also keeps the show's evaluative habit: [[SimaGuang|司马光]] is used for the positive governance theory of ritual, while [[YangXiong|扬雄]] and [[Fayan|《法言》]] supply a critique of ritual talent serving rulerly awe.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-175-bikan-liubang-de-huangjia-liyi-ke-los9hsomndnhyqpxafstgyquomzf|Hanji 175]] moves the show's early-Han sequence from reward settlement into a two-front founding problem. It explains [[MaoDunChanyu|冒顿单于]]'s rise through [[ToumanChanyu|头曼单于]], [[Yuezhi|月氏]], [[Donghu|东胡]], and [[AbsoluteCommandObedience|绝对服从式指挥训练]], then shows [[HanWangXin|韩王信]] surrendering [[Mayi|马邑]] under Xiongnu siege and Liu Bang's suspicion. The same episode uses [[ShusunTong|叔孙通]]'s ritual training to show the new court turning rough wartime merit-holders into officials governed by hierarchy.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-174-liubang-weihe-dafeng-zijide-chouren-2-luet7gqkako8idt9o3ccqq7cgpbt|Hanji 174 part 2]] completes the show's "why enfeoff enemies" title by turning reward delay into a public-credibility problem. It uses [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s advice to reward [[YongChi|雍齿]], [[EQianqiu|鄂千秋]]'s defense of [[XiaoHe|萧何]] over [[CaoShen|曹参]], and [[LiuTaigong|刘太公]]'s elevation to 太上皇 to move early Han founding order from merit anxiety into ranked reward, court privilege, and dynastic family status.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-174-liubang-weihe-dafeng-zijide-chouren-1-lnzzvpieqtgatmvgogz3msyhs60u|Hanji 174]] continues the show's early-Han founding sequence from Han Xin's demotion into reward distribution and regional settlement. It uses [[XiaoHe|萧何]]'s "功人" reward argument, [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s restrained Liu county grant, [[ChenPing|陈平]]'s credit to [[WeiWuzhi|魏无知]], Liu-family kingdoms, and [[HanWangXin|韩王信]]'s move toward [[Mayi|马邑]] to connect merit hierarchy with same-surname enfeoffment and frontier containment.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-172-liubang-yangyou-yunmengze-zhiqin-hanxin-lrpkye8y6ibqdqs9pz7artk-6chv|Hanji 172]] continues the show's early-Han post-founding suspicion sequence from Han Xin's Zhongli Mo crisis into arrest and demotion. It uses [[ChenPing|陈平]]'s [[RitualTourSummonsEntrapment|巡游会侯式诱捕]], [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s fake [[Yunmengze|云梦泽]] tour, the [[ChenCountyChuHan|陈县]] vassal gathering, [[ZhongliMo|钟离眜]]'s suicide, and [[HanXin|韩信]]'s reduction to [[Huaiyin|淮阴]] marquis to show how the new regime handles a contributor too dangerous to attack directly.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-171-zhangliang-jiao-ni-ruhe-mingli-shuangshou-2-lm1iff0cadjapwf2mggwyo8wcvxn|Hanji 171 part 2]] continues the show's early-Han post-founding sequence from ministerial exit into different-surname king suspicion. It uses [[ZangTu|臧荼]]'s rebellion, [[LuWan|卢绾]]'s trusted replacement kingship, [[LiJiChuHan|利几]]'s fear-driven revolt, the [[ChanglePalace|长乐宫]] / [[WeiyangPalace|未央宫]] construction sequence, and [[HanXin|韩信]]'s sheltering of [[ZhongliMo|钟离眜]] to show Liu Bang's new regime turning from accession and capital choice into trust management.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-171-zhangliang-jiao-ni-ruhe-mingli-shuangshou-1-lptgy7wxwsve8ljt79asetubin41|Hanji 171 part 1]] opens the show's next early-Han branch after the capital move. It turns [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s illness, seclusion, Daoist practice, and wish to follow [[Chisongzi|赤松子]] into a case of [[TranscendentExitPoliticalCamouflage|假托神仙式权力退场]], while also criticizing the habit of blaming [[HanXin|韩信]] and [[XiaoHe|萧何]] for harms caused by [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s suspicion of founding ministers.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-170-liubang-weisha-cong-luoyang-banjia-dao-changan-2-lpqtexvxjrbrpjgbw5hrgmhl9apk|Hanji 170 part 2]] completes the show's Luoyang-to-Chang'an title question after the defeated-elite cleanup of part 1. It pairs [[DingGong|丁公]] with [[JiBu|季布]] to distinguish former-enemy loyalty from private dereliction, then uses [[LouJing|楼敬]] and [[ZhangLiang|张良]] to show why [[LiuBang|刘邦]] moves from [[Luoyi|洛阳 / 洛邑]] to [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] and makes [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] the [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]] capital base.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-170-liubang-weisha-cong-luoyang-banjia-dao-changan-1-lpioq8izitba7uhbdybp7i03djdy|Hanji 170 part 1]] continues the show's early [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]] founding sequence after Liu Bang's accession. Although the title previews the Luoyang-to-Chang'an question, this part uses [[TianHeng|田横]]'s suicide and followers' deaths plus [[JiBu|季布]]'s pardon through [[ZhuJiaLu|朱家]] and [[XiahouYing|夏侯婴]] to show [[LiuBang|刘邦]] cleaning up defeated elites and redefining former enemy loyalty.
