# Adam Hamilton

## Overview
Adam Hamilton is a Kansas pastor and 2026 Democratic U.S. Senate candidate presented by the bounded source as the leader of America's largest United Methodist church and an unusually competitive challenger in a state that had not elected a Democratic senator since 1932.

## Current Profile
Hamilton's campaign joins a liberal Christian moral vocabulary to broadly conventional Democratic policy. He emphasizes poverty, food support, healthcare, humane immigration policy, and resistance to cruelty while also identifying as a capitalist and an independent-minded Democrat. His pastoral identity and Midwestern moderation are treated as possible bridges to voters who retain a Republican identity but are alienated from the party under [[DonaldTrump]].

## Key Characteristics
- Grounds his candidacy in Christian concern for compassion and opposition to cruelty.
- Opposes cuts to food assistance and healthcare and supports immigration.
- Calls for radical change to a militarized and politicized immigration-enforcement system.
- Uses moderate cultural presentation without abandoning Democratic policy positions.
- Avoids strong anti-billionaire populism and explicitly identifies as a capitalist.
- Tests whether religious language can support a Democratic coalition in a strongly Republican state.

- Policy profile: [[effective-non-altruism-elon-musks-foundation-6ae30277305baddee189d087]] records his positions on food support, healthcare, immigration, deportation, capitalism, and party independence.

## Qualifications
The profile reflects one campaign report during an unresolved race. Polling, election-model estimates, event impressions, audience composition, and candidate messaging do not establish the final result or prove that religious positioning caused cross-party support. The episode's description of church size and candidate biography is not independently verified here.
