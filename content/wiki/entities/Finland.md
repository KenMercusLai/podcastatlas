# Finland

Finland enters the wiki through [[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] as the critical border route in [[OperationPimlico]]. The episode says the escape plan had to account for Finland's Cold War pressure from the [[SovietUnion]], including the possibility that defectors from Soviet territory could be returned.

The Finnish branch shows how [[ColdWarSpycraft]] depends on diplomatic ambiguity. A Finnish security official is presented as leaving space for British action by implying that notification after the fact would be different from asking permission in advance. The episode also uses Sibelius's Finlandia as the signal that [[OlegGordievsky|Gordievsky]] had crossed the border.
