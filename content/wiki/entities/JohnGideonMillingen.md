# John Gideon Millingen / 约翰·基甸·米林根

John Gideon Millingen appears in [[78-xifang-juedou-shi-chujian-ba-weile-rongyu-687731000]] as the nineteenth-century author of [[XifangJuedouShi|《西方决斗史》 / The History of Duelling]]. The episode says he was a doctor and military surgeon who experienced the Peninsular War, Waterloo, and the occupation of Paris, which helps explain both his proximity to martial culture and his distaste for dueling's bloodshed.

The hosts read Millingen source-critically. He condemns [[HonorDuelCulture|honor duel culture]] as barbaric and hypocritical, but his writing can still admire courage and fair procedure; he also brings an English perspective that makes his treatment of [[France|French]] and [[UnitedStates|American]] dueling cultures partial rather than neutral.
