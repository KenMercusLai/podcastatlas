# RT-Mart / 大润发

## Overview
RT-Mart is a Chinese hypermarket chain used in the episode to illustrate the costly transition from a broad one-stop store toward a more community-oriented format.

## Current Profile
[[dafeixin-guonei-hangsi-tiaozheng-hangxian-buju-jianfeiyao-liuxing-huo-yingxiang-pijiuye-gkwriaiotragi7matv3jls]] reports that parent Sun Art Retail expected a larger half-year loss as traffic weakened and merchandise, supply-chain, and store changes raised costs. RT-Mart's repositioning responds to membership stores, discount retailers, and instant retail splitting demand that once concentrated in large stores.

## Key Characteristics
- Established large-format retailer under traffic pressure.
- Repositioning toward neighborhood and community demand.
- Absorbing simultaneous merchandise, supply-chain, and store-adjustment costs.
- Competing with specialized formats rather than one direct substitute.

## Evidence
### Format transition
- [[dafeixin-guonei-hangsi-tiaozheng-hangxian-buju-jianfeiyao-liuxing-huo-yingxiang-pijiuye-gkwriaiotragi7matv3jls]] links reported losses and lower traffic to a multi-year operating redesign.

## Qualifications
