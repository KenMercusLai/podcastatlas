# Chendai Town / 陈埭镇

## Overview
Chendai Town / 陈埭镇 is the [[Jinjiang]] footwear-cluster setting explained in [[500-jinjiang-chendaizhen-wangshi-huangziyi-tan-gaikai-jincheng-zhong-de-zhongguo-yundong-pinpai-lswtplovdbo4pl52yoxu6rgzyal2]] through diaspora resources, clan society, early township enterprise formation, quality crises, and the emergence of Chinese sportswear brands.

## Current Profile
The source presents Chendai as a dense local business ecology rather than only a factory town. Returned overseas money, vacant overseas-family houses, surplus labor, Ding-clan institutions, local religious plurality, Nike-linked process learning, and permissive reform-era governance allowed household workshops and pooled village investment to become a regional shoe industry.

## Key Characteristics
- Functions as the concrete town-level case inside the broader [[Jinjiang]] sportswear story.
- Converted "three idle" resources into capital, workshop space, and labor.
- Relied on clan and acquaintance networks that lowered early coordination and financing costs.
- Learned from outward-facing subcontracting and athletic-shoe process transfer.
- Survived reputation shocks through a shift from tolerance alone toward quality governance.
- Became associated with [[AntaSports|Anta]], [[Xtep|Xtep]], [[361Degrees]], and [[QiaodanSports]].
