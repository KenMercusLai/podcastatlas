# Hallie Miller

## Overview
Hallie Miller is a Baltimore Banner journalist featured in [[the-loan-at-the-heart-of-a-new-foreclosure-crisis]] for investigating Baltimore investor foreclosures tied to [[DSCRLoans|DSCR loans]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Miller as part of the reporting team that converted a foreclosure tip into a documented housing-finance investigation. She helps trace property records, pursue investor contacts, travel to New York, and connect loan mechanics to neighborhood conditions in Baltimore.

## Key Characteristics
- She worked with another Banner reporter to identify the scale of the Baltimore portfolio.
- Her reporting combined records analysis with direct attempts to contact investors.
- She helped distinguish confirmed facts from a suspected but unresolved fraud theory.
- She connected finance details to the lived condition of Baltimore row-home blocks.

## Evidence
Portfolio investigation:
- [[the-loan-at-the-heart-of-a-new-foreclosure-crisis]] says Jack and Hallie Miller checked public records and found more than 700 homes tied to two little-known investors.

Investor pursuit:
- [[the-loan-at-the-heart-of-a-new-foreclosure-crisis]] says Jack and Hallie traveled to Spring Valley, New York, to seek out [[EliezerGold]] after unsuccessful phone, email, letter, and attorney outreach.

Neighborhood reporting:
- [[the-loan-at-the-heart-of-a-new-foreclosure-crisis]] has Jack and Hallie take Planet Money to Edding Street to show the abandoned and boarded-up homes.
