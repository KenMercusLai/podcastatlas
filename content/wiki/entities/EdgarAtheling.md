# Edgar Atheling

## Overview

Edgar Atheling appears in the wiki as the young royal claimant elected after [[HaroldGodwinson]]'s death but unable to organize resistance to [[WilliamTheConqueror]].

## Current Profile

The episode uses Edgar to distinguish dynastic legitimacy from effective kingship. He had royal blood and Witan recognition, but lacked military resources, experience, and magnate commitment; after William's march isolated London, Edgar and the remaining leaders submitted at Berkhamsted.

## Key Characteristics

- Teenaged claimant with a credible Anglo-Saxon royal bloodline.
- Elected king by the Witan after Hastings but never crowned in the episode's account.
- Politically dependent on Edwin, Morcar, church leaders, and London elites who did not sustain resistance.
- Example of formal legitimacy failing without force, coordination, and timely leadership.

## Evidence

- Claim and limitation - [[557-1066-the-norman-conquest-part-4-glt5156716826]] says Edgar had royal blood but was a teenager without sufficient military resources.
- Election and submission - [[557-1066-the-norman-conquest-part-4-glt5156716826]] says the Witan elected Edgar before he and other leading figures submitted to William at Berkhamsted.
- Longer outcome - [[557-1066-the-norman-conquest-part-4-glt5156716826]] notes that Edgar survived into old age rather than being executed by William.
