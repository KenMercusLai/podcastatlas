# Colonel Madan (Everest pilot)

## Overview
Colonel Madan is the Nepali helicopter pilot named in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-842831df1049b1024b0dfd8bf11ec723-108598757-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-842831df1049b1024b0dfd8bf11ec723-108598757]] as a decisive figure in the [[BeckWeathers]] rescue. The source presents him as a person willing to become first when a risky rescue had no clear precedent.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Colonel Madan as an example of high-consequence action under uncertainty. Segarra says he stripped weight from the helicopter, flew with limited fuel, and saved two people when a ground carry through the icefall was not possible.

## Key Characteristics
- Nepali pilot identified in the source only as Colonel Madan.
- Rescue actor whose helicopter decision solved a high-altitude evacuation problem that the climbing team could not solve on foot.
- Example of acting under uncertainty when nobody can guarantee the outcome in advance.
- Leadership case for converting fear of being first into a reason to try while life remains recoverable.
