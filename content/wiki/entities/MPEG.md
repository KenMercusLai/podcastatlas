# MPEG / Moving Pictures Experts Group

MPEG appears in [[the-invention-invention]] as the video-compression standard that turns [[PatentPool|patent-pool]] history from a nineteenth-century sewing-machine story into a modern technology-infrastructure story. The source says MPEG became a shared format for compressed video, making it easier for electronics to store, send, receive, and play video across devices.

The episode's MPEG lesson is institutional. [[LeonardoMPEG|Leonardo]] helped gather innovations from different companies, while [[KenRubenstein]] and other lawyers had to design the [[MPEGPatentPool]] so cooperation around the standard would be treated as lawful collaboration rather than anticompetitive collusion.
