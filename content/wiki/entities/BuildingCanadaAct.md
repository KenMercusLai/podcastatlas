# Building Canada Act

## Overview
The Building Canada Act is presented as Mark Carney’s mechanism for accelerating federally endorsed infrastructure projects during a trade and national-unity crisis.

## Current Profile
The episode places the act within a supply-side response to American pressure. It is intended to shorten approval periods that have sometimes lasted about a decade, helping Canada build domestic capacity and trade infrastructure while pursuing export diversification.

## Key Characteristics
- Targets faster approval of nationally significant infrastructure.
- Sits alongside tax reductions and investment deductions in the government response.
- Connects infrastructure delivery to trade diversification and national resilience.
