## Overview

[[JinYong|金庸]]'s 《雪山飞狐》 is a short wuxia novel in which a single day on Jade Brush Peak unfolds into the reconstruction of [[HuYidao|胡一刀]]'s death twenty-seven years earlier and a four-family feud extending back a century.

## Current Profile

The novel's compression operates on three time scales. Present conflict remains concentrated in one day and place; testimony reopens the Hu-Miao duel; and that duel inherits the older misunderstanding among the Hu, Miao, Fan, and Tian families. The result resembles a dramatic unity continually perforated by memory.

Its testimony structure combines contradiction with supplementation. Yan Ji protects himself by claiming Hu Yidao killed himself, while Miao Ruolan's retelling, Ping A-si's witness, and Tao Baisui's information progressively identify a poisoned wound, the poison's route, and Tian Guinong's direction. The form therefore uses viewpoint bias without ending in the total indeterminacy often associated with a pure Rashomon effect.

The title belongs to [[HuFei|胡斐]], who drives the present action, but the episode regards Hu Yidao as the spiritual center. His remembered choices organize the novel's ideals of equality, courage, fairness, family responsibility, and restraint. The ending transfers those standards to Hu Fei: striking may kill Miao Renfeng, while withholding may mean his own death, leaving the moral conflict suspended at the point of action.

## Key Characteristics

- Compresses present action into one day and one mountain setting.
- Layers a twenty-seven-year mystery over a century of inherited revenge.
- Uses testimony as both interested contradiction and cumulative causal reconstruction.
- Separates the titular protagonist from the figure carrying the strongest moral presence.
- Joins chivalry, kinship, romance, and survival in an unresolved final blow.
- Gains dramatic force from misunderstandings that also strain causal credibility.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on one conversational close reading rather than a direct edition study. Comparisons to dramatic unities, 《雷雨》, 《罗生门》, or role-playing mysteries describe effects rather than prove direct influence. Character motives, martial rankings, exact chronology, and the claim that Hu Yidao is the true protagonist remain interpretations.

## What Changed

- Established the novel as a nested-time and layered-testimony design.
- Distinguished contradictory testimony from cumulative revelation.
- Identified the split among titular, plot-driving, and spiritual protagonists.
- Preserved communication failures as a qualification on structural praise.
