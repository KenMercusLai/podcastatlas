# Zhang Xue Motorcycle / 张雪机车

Zhang Xue Motorcycle / 张雪机车 appears in [[zhongguo-yinglai-hanbao-kaidianchao-guochan-huangningmeng-jiage-huiluo-1006920543]] as a Chinese motorcycle brand that reportedly received exclusive investment from 红杉中国. The source says the reported investment amount was 1.5 billion RMB and the post-money valuation was 6 billion RMB.

The episode treats the financing as useful but not sufficient. Zhang Xue Motorcycle was founded in 2024, entered the World Superbike Championship, and reportedly won the Portugal round in March, but the source also says 2025 R&D spending was close to 70 million RMB and losses exceeded 20 million RMB. Future growth is framed as a test of R&D, overseas market development, channels, warehousing, and after-sales against incumbents such as 春风动力 and 钱江.
