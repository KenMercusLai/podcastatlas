# Boll & Branch

## Overview
Boll & Branch is the bedding and consumer brand co-founded by [[ScottTannen]] and used as his operating reference in [[advice-line-with-scott-tannen-of-boll-branch-and-jamie-siminoff-of-ring-2025-e64c6623-10d1-4222-85d6-22eeab736221]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Boll & Branch as a founder-led brand that initially protected its vision through constrained financing, then accepted investment when personal debt made the existing path harder to sustain. It is also expanding physical retail: Tannen says seven stores opened during the year, another seven were planned for the next, and West Coast entry was expected in 2027.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder-led bedding and consumer brand.
- Case of outside investment used to reduce founder household risk and support growth.
- Retail-expansion story alongside its established brand operations.
- Source of Tannen's advice on education cost, positioning, and operational simplicity.
