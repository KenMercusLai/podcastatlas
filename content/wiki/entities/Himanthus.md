# Himanthus

## Overview

[[Himanthus]] is the photonics startup [[BillyEvans|Billy Evans]] describes as developing smaller and less expensive Raman-spectroscopy sensors for biological measurement and AI-assisted correlation discovery.

## Current Profile

The company is presently documented in the wiki only through its founder's promotional explanation. Its optical approach is materially different from [[Theranos]]'s advertised blood-testing platform, but a different mechanism does not establish validation, safety, clinical value, regulatory readiness, or commercial viability.

## Key Characteristics

- Proposed use of Raman spectroscopy to distinguish chemical structures through changes in scattered light.
- Hardware ambition centered on reducing sensor size and cost.
- Data-platform ambition to produce rich biological measurements for AI correlation analysis.
- Public-comparison burden created by Evans's relationship with [[ElizabethHolmes|Elizabeth Holmes]] and the shared diagnostic-access mission.
- Evidence state limited to a short founder description without independent performance data.
