# Fu Haoran / 傅浩然 (IBD Advocate)

## Overview
Fu Haoran / 傅浩然 is presented as a person living with Crohn's disease who shares illness experience through hospital visits and social media to help newly diagnosed patients find qualified care and hope.

## Current Profile
Fu's account is a delayed-diagnosis and recovery narrative. Suspected intestinal tuberculosis, a nonspecific ulcer explanation, and his own wishful delay preceded severe diarrhea, weight loss, bleeding, fistula, abscess, and fibrotic narrowing. He describes later treatment participation, nutritional discipline, family and friend support, professional care, weight recovery, and peer education as the basis for living with rather than surrendering to the disease.

## Key Characteristics
- Reports diagnostic delay and irregular early care before a systematic Crohn's disease assessment.
- Describes severe complications involving fistula, abdominal abscess, ileocecal fibrotic narrowing, and possible bowel resection or stoma.
- Attributes recovery to treatment cooperation, nutrition, mindset, family, friends, and clinicians rather than willpower alone.
- Extends personal experience into ward visits and online peer education.
