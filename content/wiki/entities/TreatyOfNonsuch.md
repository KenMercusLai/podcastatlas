# Treaty of Nonsuch

The Treaty of Nonsuch appears in [[695-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-the-shadow-war-part-5-glt5637968455]] as the 20 August 1585 agreement between [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]] and the Dutch rebels. The episode calls it the first treaty between the future Dutch Republic and a foreign power.

The treaty is the source's key [[StrategicInterventionThreshold|strategic intervention threshold]]. Elizabeth refuses sovereignty over the [[LowCountries|Low Countries]], but agrees to send infantry, cavalry, and [[RobertDudley|Robert Dudley / Earl of Leicester]] as commander while accepting cautionary towns.

For [[PhilipSidney|Sir Philip Sidney]], the treaty creates a path from frustrated courtly ambition into command. He helps prepare the agreement, is admired by Dutch envoys, has his [[Flushing]] appointment briefly reversed, tries to join [[FrancisDrake]], and is then sent to Flushing after all.
