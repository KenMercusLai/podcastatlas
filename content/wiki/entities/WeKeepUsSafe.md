# We Keep Us Safe

We Keep Us Safe is the investigative true-crime series promoted in [[20260721-pmoney-new-npr-series-we-keep-us-safe-from-the-embedded-podcast-trailer-20260721-pmoney-new-npr-series-we-keep-us-safe-from-the-embedded-podcast-trailer]]. The trailer identifies it as a new series from [[NPR]]'s [[EmbeddedPodcast|Embedded podcast]], [[TheSeattleTimes|The Seattle Times]], and [[KUOW]].

The series focuses on [[CapitolHillOrganizedProtest|CHOP]] and the unresolved killing of [[AntonioMaysJr|Antonio Mays Jr.]] after protesters fired on a Jeep they believed was a threat. Its central frame is [[SafetyIdealFatalContradiction]]: a space described as protecting Black life ended with a Black teenager dead.

## Key Claims
- [[WillJames]] and [[SydneyBrounstone]] say they spent more than a year investigating what happened that night.
- The trailer says the reporting involved rumors, new witnesses, and never-before-seen evidence.
- The series is presented as an investigation into both responsibility and how fear, armed security, and threat perception changed the protest zone.
