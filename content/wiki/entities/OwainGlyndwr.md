# Owain Glyndŵr

## Overview

Owain Glyndŵr was the leader of the Welsh revolt that became [[HenryVOfEngland|Prince Henry's]] formative military and administrative theatre during [[HenryIVOfEngland|Henry IV's]] reign.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Glyndŵr's revolt as a durable national and military challenge rather than a brief appendix to Hotspur's rebellion. It reached a high point around 1404, drew broad support, and established a parliament at Machynlleth. The proposed Tripartite Indenture placed Glyndŵr within a projected division of England and Wales alongside Northumberland and Mortimer.

The revolt also shaped Prince Henry. Command in Wales required him to combine tactics with finance, logistics, siege recovery, and economic blockade. The source therefore uses Glyndŵr's challenge to explain how the future Henry V acquired capacities later visible in France.

## Key Characteristics

- Leader of a broad Welsh revolt against Lancastrian rule.
- Political actor associated with a parliament at Machynlleth.
- Potential coalition partner in plans involving the Percys and Mortimer.
- Adversary whose resistance trained Prince Henry in sustained command.
- Figure surrounded by uncertainty about the purpose and provenance of coalition documents.

## Evidence

- Revolt strength: [[486-henry-iv-warrior-princes-and-fat-knights-part-2-glt8391310611]] places the movement's high point around 1404 and notes broad Welsh support and the Machynlleth parliament.
- Coalition politics: [[486-henry-iv-warrior-princes-and-fat-knights-part-2-glt8391310611]] describes the Tripartite Indenture and its proposed territorial division.
- Princely apprenticeship: [[486-henry-iv-warrior-princes-and-fat-knights-part-2-glt8391310611]] connects the Welsh campaign to Prince Henry's logistics, finance, stronghold recovery, and blockade experience.

## Qualifications

The episode suggests that the Tripartite Indenture may have served Welsh propaganda designed to worsen relations between Henry IV and Northumberland; it does not establish that interpretation. The source also provides only a compressed account of Glyndŵr's aims, institutions, campaigns, and later fate.

## What Changed

- Established Glyndŵr's revolt as the political-military school behind Prince Henry's later command profile.
