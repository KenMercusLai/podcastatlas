# Mississippi

Mississippi appears in [[tech-20251226-1226-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251226-1226-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the state where [[Bluesky]] stopped service for users because of legal-exposure concerns around age-verification requirements. The source uses the example to show how [[AgeVerificationPatchwork]] can affect access at the state line.

The wiki role is source-scoped: Mississippi is not developed as a full state profile here. It is a case where child-safety regulation, platform liability, and service availability meet, connecting [[OnlineAgeVerification]] to [[SocialMediaAgeGateSpeechBurden]] and compliance costs.

## Key Claims
- A state-level age-verification law can make a platform decide whether to comply, geoblock, or withdraw service.
- The practical effect of a patchwork can be different internet access depending on where a user is located.
- This creates pressure for federal attention because platforms and users face inconsistent rules across jurisdictions.
