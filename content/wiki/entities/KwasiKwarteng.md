# Kwasi Kwarteng

## Overview

Kwasi Kwarteng was [[LizTruss]]'s chancellor and the principal political ally associated with the failed 2022 mini-budget in [[246-the-fall-of-liz-truss-glt3484252092]].

## Current Profile

The episode portrays Kwarteng as highly educated and intellectually able but insufficiently cautious about implementing a radical tax package under severe economic constraints. His dismissal is compared with a ruler sacrificing a political favourite: the act removed the ally without restoring the leader's authority and instead made policy failure unmistakable.

## Key Characteristics

- Chancellor associated with Truss's low-tax economic programme.
- Presented as intellectually accomplished but lacking sufficient humility toward warnings and constraints.
- Removed when the government attempted to recover credibility after the mini-budget.
- Functions in the episode as the sacrificed ally within a wider authority-collapse mechanism.
