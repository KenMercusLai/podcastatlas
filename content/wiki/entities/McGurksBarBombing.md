# McGurk's Bar Bombing

McGurk's Bar bombing appears in [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] as the 4 December 1971 [[UlsterVolunteerForce|UVF]] attack that killed 15 people, including children and members of the landlord's family. The episode places it in an autumn 1971 sequence of sectarian bombing and retaliation after [[InternmentWithoutTrial]].

For the wiki, McGurk's Bar is a casualty marker in the loyalist side of the [[AtrocityRetaliationCycle]]. It appears after IRA attacks on Protestant venues and before further IRA bombing, showing how fear, revenge, and recruitment reinforced each other in [[Belfast]] and [[TheTroubles]].
