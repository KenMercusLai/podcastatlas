# Mark (Data Science With Sam)

Mark is the source-scoped guest in [[ep-17-ais-impact-on-creativity-a-consumers-perspective]], where [[SamDataScienceWithSam|Sam]] interviews him about practical generative-AI use from a consumer and non-specialist perspective. The source identifies him as a retired business development director, chemical engineer, MBA holder, [[TalkingHeadsToastmastersClub]] president in Houston, and University of Illinois alumni volunteer.

His contribution to the wiki is a grounded everyday-use case for [[AICreativeCollaboration]]. He uses [[ChatGPT]] for speech drafts, [[DALLE|DALL-E]] through ChatGPT for alumni event imagery, [[Suno]] for songs, and [[GoogleAppsScript]] help for spreadsheet automation, but repeatedly keeps editing, fact-checking, testing, and company licensing boundaries in the workflow.

Mark's stance is optimistic without being blind. He treats AI as a collaborator or first-draft engine, not a replacement for [[HumanJudgmentUnderAI]], and he frames beginner adoption as a matter of trying small concrete projects before expecting the tool to solve everything.
