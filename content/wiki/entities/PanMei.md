# 潘美 / Pan Mei

Pan Mei / 潘美 is a Song general discussed in [[170-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-xia-958158115]]. The episode uses him partly to complicate the simplified villain image attached to him in later popular Yang-family-general stories.

In this source, Pan Mei appears in the southern campaign context beside [[CaoBin|曹彬]], and in the story of how [[ZhaoKuangyin|赵匡胤 / 宋太祖]] treated Later Zhou descendants. The point is a source-critical one: familiar popular memory can flatten a historical actor, while specific episodes may show a more mixed role.
