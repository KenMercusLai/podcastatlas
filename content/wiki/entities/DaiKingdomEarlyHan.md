# 代国 / Dai Kingdom (early Han)

代国 / Dai Kingdom enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-174-liubang-weihe-dafeng-zijide-chouren-1-lnzzvpieqtgatmvgogz3msyhs60u|Hanji 174]] as a northern early Han kingdom granted to [[LiuXiDaiKing|刘喜]]. The source describes it as a fifty-three-county kingdom made from Yunzhong, Yanmen, Dai, and related commandery territory.

This page is distinct from [[DaiState|代国]], the older Dai polity and Warring States remnant context. In Hanji 174, Dai is part of Liu Bang's same-surname royal settlement and northern frontier map.
