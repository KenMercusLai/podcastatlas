# 阿邑大夫 / Ayi Dafu

阿邑大夫 / Ayi Dafu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-2-lmnqfjmydygqnfec6xhzpixralvi]] as the local [[QiState|齐国]] official praised daily at court but exposed by [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]]'s secret investigation. The source says Ayi's fields are neglected, people are poor, and the official fails during pressure from Zhao and Wei.

His source role is the negative half of [[IndependentOfficialAudit]]. Qi Wei Wang reads the praise as purchased court reputation, then punishes Ayi Dafu and the courtiers who had been praising him.
