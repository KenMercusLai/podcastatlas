# 闻人袭 / Wenren Xi (late Han)

## Overview

Wenren Xi is a late-Han taiwei removed from office in Hanji 891's 169 CE Three Excellencies turnover.

## Current Profile

The current wiki evidence gives Wenren Xi only as an office-churn marker. His removal helps set up the June 169 reshuffle in which Liu Chong becomes taiwei, Xu Xun becomes situ, and Liu Xiao becomes sikong. The source does not supply an independent reason for Wenren Xi's dismissal.

## Key Characteristics

- Taiwei named in the May 169 high-office removal sequence.
- Part of Emperor Ling's early Three Excellencies turnover.
- Context marker for the appointment sequence that later elevates Liu Xiao.
- Source-scoped official with no developed biography in the current wiki.
