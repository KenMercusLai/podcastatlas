# 芈兰 / Mi Lan

芈兰 / Mi Lan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-62-ji-er-kuang-ni-jiujiu-de-tui-a-lumch-ibj8m94abjh5nvr3qhiqff]] as a son of [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] who supports attending [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]]'s proposed meeting at [[Wuguan|武关]]. The source says he has married a Qin princess and treats that marriage tie, together with the kinship through [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]], as reason to trust Qin enough to go.

His role is source-scoped but structurally important. Mi Lan's advice turns [[WarringStatesMarriageDiplomacy|战国联姻外交]] from an alliance signal into a false comfort: family connection makes the trap seem less likely, even though [[QinState|秦国]] closes Wuguan and detains Chu Huaiwang in [[Xianyang|咸阳]].
