# Naveen Rao

## Overview
Naveen Rao is an AI-hardware entrepreneur and neuroscience-trained engineer presented in the source as co-founder and CEO of [[UnconventionalAI|Unconventional AI]].

## Current Profile
Rao's current thesis is that AI progress needs a new physical computing substrate because energy and data movement are becoming harder constraints than raw arithmetic throughput. He draws on electrical engineering, neuroscience, prior AI-chip work, and large-cluster infrastructure experience to advocate [[PhysicalDynamicalComputing|physical dynamical computing]] and [[IntelligencePerWatt|intelligence per watt]] as the central design target. The profile remains source-scoped: the episode is a founder presentation, not independent technical validation.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines an electrical-engineering and neuroscience background in his account of alternative AI hardware.
- Previously founded an AI-chip company acquired by [[Intel]] and then led Intel's AI group.
- Later built GPU-cluster infrastructure that joined [[Databricks]].
- Frames AI optimistically while treating energy efficiency as the critical substrate problem.
- Leads an interdisciplinary team spanning theory, models, circuits, chips, systems, and software.
- Uses ambitious product and efficiency targets that still require external validation.
