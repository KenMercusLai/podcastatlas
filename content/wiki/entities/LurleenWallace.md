# Lurleen Wallace

## Overview

Lurleen Wallace was George Wallace's wife and the Alabama governor elected in 1966 when term limits prevented him from immediately succeeding himself.

## Current Profile

The source presents Lurleen's candidacy as a transparent proxy arrangement: voters understood that electing her would preserve [[GeorgeWallace|George Wallace]]'s control. She reportedly had little appetite for politics and was ill with cancer, making the governorship part of the episode's broader case that Wallace subordinated intimate relationships to political ambition. Her death in May 1968 interrupted his presidential campaign only briefly.

## Key Characteristics

- Won the Alabama governorship in a landslide as a proxy for her term-limited husband.
- Entered electoral politics despite illness and limited personal political ambition.
- Became a vehicle for continuity of Wallace's state power.
- Died during Wallace's 1968 presidential campaign.

## Evidence

- Proxy candidacy: [[511-america-in-68-george-wallace-the-first-donald-trump-part-4-glt8855008412]] says Alabama voters understood that a vote for Lurleen was effectively a vote for George Wallace.
- Illness and political use: [[511-america-in-68-george-wallace-the-first-donald-trump-part-4-glt8855008412]] describes her cancer, reluctance, May 1968 death, public mourning, and Wallace's quick campaign return.

This profile is based on a source centered on George Wallace, not Lurleen's own perspective or a full history of her administration. Claims about her wishes, emotional state, her husband's motives, funeral decisions, and the speed of his return are source-scoped.
