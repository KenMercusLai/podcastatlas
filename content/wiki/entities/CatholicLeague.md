# Catholic League

The Catholic League appears in [[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] as the militant Catholic faction in [[France]] associated with [[HenryDukeOfGuise|Henry, Duke of Guise]]. The episode uses it to explain why French politics after the [[StBartholomewsDayMassacre]] could not be treated as a stable great-power partner for [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]].

The league's importance in the source is tied to claimant and invasion politics. Guise's Catholic position, ties to [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]], and involvement in the [[ThrockmortonPlot]] connect French factional war to English succession danger.

After [[FrancoisDukeOfAnjou|Francois, Duke of Anjou]] died, the league became one side of the [[WarOfTheThreeHenrys]], opposing the prospect that Protestant [[HenryOfNavarre]] might inherit France. That makes the league a bridge between French civil war and wider [[ConfessionalWarEscalation]].
