# 贯高 / Guan Gao

贯高 / Guan Gao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-178-maodun-weihe-yao-fangguo-liubang-zhe-cai-shi-zhenxiang-2-loluh53iyrzo4ucf3ttzmn3-otkv|Hanji 178 part 2]] as a Zhao minister under [[ZhangAo|张敖]]. After [[LiuBang|刘邦]] passes through [[Handan|邯郸]] and repeatedly humiliates Zhang Ao, Guan Gao and [[ZhaoWuZhaoMinister|赵午]] read the insult as an intolerable slight against their king.

The source makes Guan Gao important because he separates his assassination impulse from Zhang Ao's will. Zhang Ao refuses the plot and insists that Zhao owes everything to Liu Bang's grace toward [[ZhangEr|张耳]]'s line, but Guan Gao and the other ministers decide privately that they will bear the consequences themselves if the attack fails.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-179-lishishang-heqin-zenme-laide-lslks-ovbomneornk4j-q6-ns0x1|Hanji 179]] continues the plot into the failed 百人县 ambush. Guan Gao and Zhao Wu hide killers in the walls of the lodging where Liu Bang is expected to stay, but Liu Bang becomes uneasy after hearing the place name and leaves by night. The source therefore changes Guan Gao from a minister who merely intends assassination into an actor whose plan almost reaches execution.
