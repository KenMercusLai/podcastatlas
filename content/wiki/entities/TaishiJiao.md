# 太史角 / 太史敫 / Taishi Jiao

太史角 / Taishi Jiao appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-73-lishishang-ziyou-lianai-de-kaichuangzhe-shi-shui-lkumfsfialqgdl6qpb3c-hnz7h9u]] as a wealthy household head in [[JuWarringStates|莒城]]. After [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]] is killed, [[TianFazhang|田法章]] hides in Taishi Jiao's household as a servant while concealing his royal identity.

The source's main use of Taishi Jiao is setting. His household gives the fugitive prince a place to disappear, and his daughter later becomes [[JunWangHou|君王后]] after Tian Fazhang is restored as [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-79-shishang-yong-gengniu-da-shengzhang-diyi-ren-lsrl0posblloqwugox160xsicp2p]] uses the form 太史敫 for the same father branch and gives him a sharper ethical role. He refuses to see Jun Wang Hou because she married without the expected parental and matchmaker process. The source treats that refusal as strict [[MarriageRitualOrthodoxy|marriage ritual orthodoxy]], while also noting that Jun Wang Hou continues filial respect rather than answering with resentment.
