# ELIZA

ELIZA is the 1966 MIT chatbot introduced in [[i-robot-ai-and-consciousness-6a880840b0d5e24ea40c7015]] as an early example of people treating a conversational program as if it were thinking or even conscious. The episode uses ELIZA to show that [[ChatbotMindProjection]] predates modern large language models.

The source does not present ELIZA as conscious. Its importance is diagnostic: if simple pattern-based dialogue could produce the "Eliza effect," then responses to [[Claude]], [[ChatGPT]], and [[Gemini]] need to be separated from evidence about inner states.
