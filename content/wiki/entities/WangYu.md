# Wang Yu / 汪玉

Wang Yu, or 汪玉, appears in [[173-duihua-yao-song-shenjian-dongfang-kongjian-zaichufa-tiancai-shaonian-shinian-hou-1-173-1]] as the [[TsinghuaUniversity]] professor whose lab shaped [[YaoSong]]'s first startup path. Yao says the early direction of [[DeePhiTech]] came mainly from Wang, while Yao's role was to manage and push the company forward.

The source also describes Wang as a mentor who helped persuade Yao's parents that direct entrepreneurship after undergraduate study was a reasonable option. Later, Yao says [[StridingAI]] again cooperates with Wang's team, which had accumulated embodied-intelligence research through student work over the previous several years.
