## Overview

郝猛 / Hao Meng is the Dong Commandery appointee in Hanji 846 who is killed by [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] before he can even take up ordinary office life. The page keeps him distinct from [[HaoMeng|郝萌]], the later Lü Bu subordinate.

## Current Profile

Hanji 846 gives Hao Meng a narrow but sharp source role. He is newly appointed as Dong Commandery administrator and has not yet had time to visit Liang Ji. Liang treats that omission as enough to fabricate or find a charge against him and has him executed by waist-cutting.

The episode uses Hao Meng to show the breadth of Liang's danger. Unlike [[WuShuWanMagistrateLateHan|Wu Shu]], Hao Meng does not first kill Liang's retainers or deliver a visible admonition. His case shows that simple failure to perform deference can become lethal when a powerholder controls appointments and punishment.

## Key Characteristics

- Newly appointed Dong Commandery official whose career is cut off before arrival.
- Victim of lethal punishment for not first visiting Liang Ji.
- Evidence that Liang's retaliation does not require open resistance.
- Disambiguated from the later 郝萌 / Hao Meng associated with Lü Bu.

## Qualifications
