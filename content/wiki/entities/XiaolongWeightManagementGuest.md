# 小龙 / Xiaolong (Weight-Management Guest)

## Overview
小龙 / Xiaolong is the nutrition and weight-management guest in [[vol-217-tingzhi-shenhua-jianfeizhen-bu-aie-bu-jie-tanshui-zhe-cai-shi-putongren-gai-chao-de-jianzhong-zuoye-llat-yskrqmro0kdjwuoegdcalu5]] and [[vol-207-meitian-20-fenzhong-de-fanjiaolv-chifan-sop-dagongren-qing-zhijie-chaozuoye-ft-dawu-shiye-chengyuan-xiaolong-lhmsv-hzbmzfyiuptzskm1jblgo]], two [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episodes about food structure, breakfast routines, training, sleep, medical technology, and weight anxiety.

## Current Profile
The sources present Xiaolong as a nutrition-interested practitioner rather than as an independently verified clinician. His role is to turn balanced eating and weight management away from shortcut language and toward layered daily practice: start from the controllable breakfast, assess body composition instead of only scale weight, keep staple foods, increase protein, use resistance training, sleep enough, and treat restaurants or reward meals as adjustable contexts.

He also supplies the episode's medical-boundary stance. Weight-loss injections and other technologies can be useful for some people with high body weight, body fat, or related disease, but the choice belongs in regular hospital evaluation with attention to indications, contraindications, and limits.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames weight management as body composition, energy, mood, sleep, and habit change rather than only weight loss.
- Treats breakfast as the easiest anchor for implementing balanced eating across a workday.
- Gives practical food-choice advice around staple foods, fiber, protein, portion control, takeout, hot pot, and barbecue.
- Treats exercise, especially resistance training, as central to preserving or increasing muscle during fat loss.
- Keeps medication and injection-based weight loss inside formal clinical assessment.
- Warns against scale-only anxiety and points severe distress toward psychological or psychiatric support.
