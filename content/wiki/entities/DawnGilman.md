# Dawn Gilman

## Overview

Dawn Gilman is the [[ChangingHomelessness]] leader presented as explaining Jacksonville's by-name list, its housing constraint, and its annual memorial for people who died while homeless.

## Current Profile

Gilman's role connects operational casework with public remembrance. She describes the need for deeply affordable housing, while her winter-solstice reading of the dead restores family identities to people otherwise represented as points on a graph.

## Key Characteristics

- She interprets the by-name list as knowledge of individuals rather than anonymous demand.
- She identifies the shortage of deeply affordable units as a binding housing constraint.
- She argues that reducing street homelessness requires faster construction of suitable housing.
- She reads the names of people who died while homeless at an annual memorial service.
