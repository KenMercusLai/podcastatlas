# Link REIT / 領展

## Overview
Link REIT / 領展 appears in the Tung source as the buyer or vehicle associated with selling public-housing estate malls, which the episode treats as a lasting public-resource privatization grievance.

## Current Profile
The source uses Link REIT as part of a policy-legacy chain. It argues that selling estate malls, together with other land and infrastructure choices, turned public resources into marketized assets and became one source of later Hong Kong public discontent.

## Key Characteristics
- Hong Kong real-estate and public-asset node in the source.
- Associated with public-housing estate mall privatization.
- Used as evidence for long-run livelihood grievances.
- Connected to housing, land, and public-resource politics rather than only commercial property.

## Evidence
- Estate-mall sale: [[dong-jianhua-shishi-aiguozhe-yuanlao-de-xianggang-rensheng-7d743a8515cf65334add1a55cd20148e]] says Tung's administration sold estate malls to Link REIT.
- Public-grievance frame: [[dong-jianhua-shishi-aiguozhe-yuanlao-de-xianggang-rensheng-7d743a8515cf65334add1a55cd20148e]] treats that privatization as one source of later public resentment.
