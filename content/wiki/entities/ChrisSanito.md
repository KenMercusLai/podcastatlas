# Chris Sanito

Chris Sanito is the [[WildSource]] speaker in [[tech-20251224-1224-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251224-1224-mp-tech-pod-128]] testing a mycelium cooler at [[KodiakHarbour]]. His comments make the cost and weight side of [[SeafoodColdChainPackaging]] visible.

In the source, Sanito is important because he keeps [[MyceliumInsulation]] from being only a lab material. The episode uses his seafood-shipping context to show that [[BiodegradableFoamPackaging]] has to compete with polystyrene on durability, insulation, lightness, and price.
