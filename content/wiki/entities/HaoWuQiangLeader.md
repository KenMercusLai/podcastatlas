## Overview

浩武 / Hao Wu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-756-weishenme-quan-ni-de-rao-ren-chu-qie-rao-ren-ltn4tqzy1d-sodhjidncv8dyqxpj|Hanji 756]] as the Qiang leader whose capture and release give the episode its clearest frontier version of "得饶人处且饶人." His promise to disperse the Qiang force after release is fulfilled in the source, making him a hinge between coercion, trust, and temporary pacification.

## Current Profile

The source identifies Hao Wu as the younger brother of [[MiWuQiangLeader|Mi Wu / Miwu]] in a Shaodang Qiang rebellion. Hao Wu leads a light force into the Longxi frontier, but the raid fails and he is captured by Li Zhang, a subordinate in the commandery office.

Hao Wu's importance comes from the bargaining scene after capture. He tells [[ZhangYuEasternHan|Zhang Yu]] that killing one man will not damage the Qiang side, but releasing him will let him persuade the rebel force to withdraw and stop attacking the frontier. Zhang Yu accepts the offer.

The promise holds in the source. Hao Wu returns and disperses the Qiang troops. The various groups go back to their home areas, and Mi Wu withdraws north of the Yellow River to Guiyi City. The episode uses the case as evidence that restraint can sometimes buy more control than immediate punishment.

## Key Characteristics

- Qiang leader tied to the Shaodang Qiang rebellion field.
- Captured after a failed light incursion into Longxi.
- Negotiates release by promising practical withdrawal rather than pleading innocence.
- Keeps the promise by dispersing the rebel force after release.
- Source-bounded case for tactical trust on the frontier.
