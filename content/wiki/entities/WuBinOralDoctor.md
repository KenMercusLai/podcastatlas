# 吴斌 / Wu Bin (口腔科)

## Overview
吴斌 is the oral-health clinician in [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] VOL.116 who explains jaw dislocation caused by excessive mouth opening.

## Current Profile
In the current source, 吴斌 connects laughing, eating, yawning, sneezing, and prolonged mouth opening during dental care to temporomandibular-joint dislocation. His practical emphasis is prompt oral emergency assessment when the jaw cannot return, followed by soft food and restricted opening during recovery, with extra caution after a prior dislocation or when joint clicking and pain are already present.

## Key Characteristics
- Source-scoped oral-health and oral-surgery educator.
- Explains jaw dislocation through mouth-opening mechanics and prior joint vulnerability.
- Distinguishes prevention and aftercare from self-reduction.
- Emphasizes recurrence risk after an earlier dislocation.
