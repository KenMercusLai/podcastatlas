# 伶征 / Ling Zheng (late Han)

伶征 / Ling Zheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] as the protector of the Qiang killed when [[XianlingQiang|先零羌]] and associated rebel groups rise in the western frontier. The source names his death before [[BeigongBoyuLateHan|北宫伯玉]] and [[LiWenhouLateHan|李文侯]] become rebel commanders.

The page is source-scoped. Ling Zheng's significance here is structural: his killing marks the breakdown of the formal Qiang-management office before [[BianZhangLateHan|边章]] and [[HanSui|韩遂]] are coerced into the rebel command field.
