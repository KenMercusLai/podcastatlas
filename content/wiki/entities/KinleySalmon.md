# Kinley Salmon

Kinley Salmon appears in [[caracas-under-pressure-democracy-in-venezuela-6a7d8bbe7d85cbdf96e00822]] as the [[TheIntelligence]] correspondent explaining Venezuela's transition talks. The source has Salmon argue that the talks are largely a [[UnitedStates]] creation, shaped by [[MarcoRubio]]'s transition plan and by Washington's control over parts of Venezuelan oil revenue.

Her role is to keep the negotiations concrete. She identifies the agenda around aid, democratic institutions, party rights, the [[VenezuelanElectoralCouncil]], and the [[VenezuelanSupremeCourt]], while also stressing that [[MariaCorinaMachado]]'s absence weakens the process's opposition legitimacy.
