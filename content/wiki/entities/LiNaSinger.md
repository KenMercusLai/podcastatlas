# 厉娜 / Li Na (singer and actor)

## Overview
厉娜 is a Chinese singer and actor and former [[SuperGirl2006|2006《超级女声》]] contestant whose friendship with [[XuFeiSinger|许飞]] was revisited in [[HouHuiYouQiShow|《后会有期》]].

## Current Profile
The source remembers Li Na for appearance, personal style, and an emotionally direct competition presence. Her later work included acting and commercial performances, while she reported limited role choice and continued vocal study. In the program's central conflict, she suspects Xu Fei disclosed a private commercial engagement that preceded company punishment, but the available material does not establish that attribution.

## Key Characteristics
- Presented as direct, polished, and less verbally analytical than [[XuFeiSinger|许飞]].
- Publicly supported Xu Fei during a contestant vote and remained closely associated with her during the competition.
- Pursued acting, commercial performance, and continued vocal study after the talent show.
- Connects later career frustration to punishment after a private commercial performance.
- Responded quickly that she liked the present-day Xu Fei, without making restored intimacy a settled outcome.

## Evidence
- **Competition relationship:** [[ep392-houhuiyouqi-dabao-women-yiran-wei-zhenshide-qinggan-huadiweilao-gkwrimaov6-qafmdiw-t1kurp]] records her vote for Xu Fei and the remembered phrase “于公于私、于情于理.”
- **Elimination-era emotion:** [[ep392-houhuiyouqi-dabao-women-yiran-wei-zhenshide-qinggan-huadiweilao-gkwrimaov6-qafmdiw-t1kurp]] describes her choice of 《执着》 after Xu Fei's elimination, her own subsequent elimination, and her apology to invested fans.
- **Later career:** [[ep392-houhuiyouqi-dabao-women-yiran-wei-zhenshide-qinggan-huadiweilao-gkwrimaov6-qafmdiw-t1kurp]] says she worked mainly in acting and commercial appearances, reported limited role options, and continued taking vocal lessons.
- **Leak suspicion:** [[ep392-houhuiyouqi-dabao-women-yiran-wei-zhenshide-qinggan-huadiweilao-gkwrimaov6-qafmdiw-t1kurp]] explains that she believed she had mainly told Xu Fei about the engagement, while also noting that organizers, audiences, and others necessarily knew about it.

The causal link from company discipline to long-term career outcomes is not established, and the disclosure allegation remains unresolved.
