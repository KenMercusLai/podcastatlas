# 周毖 / Zhou Bi (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-949-lishishang-de-dongzhuo-daodi-duo-canbao-lk6idogwmc54nk1sdfc5wonyvgbb|Hanji 949]] adds Zhou Bi's earlier role before his execution in Hanji 951. The source names him with [[WuQiongLateHan|伍琼]], [[ZhengTaiLateHan|郑泰]], and [[HeYongLateHan|何颙]] as one of the officials through whom [[DongZhuo|董卓]] tries to clean corrupt government and recommend respected people.

That earlier usefulness sharpens the later danger. Zhou Bi can help supply a legitimacy channel for Dong Zhuo, but once policy disagreement over relocation appears, the same court environment turns official advice into exposure.

周毖 / Zhou Bi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-951-tianjiang-hengcai-jiujing-shi-haoshi-haishi-huaishi-lovwgng2wrtfno-s9ttpybbhksvh|Hanji 951]] as the military-supervision official who opposes [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s forced move from [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] to [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]]. The source summary renders the name as "周壁"; the wiki normalizes cautiously to 周毖 while preserving that transcript variation as source-scoped.

Zhou Bi is executed with [[WuQiongLateHan|伍琼]] after the relocation dispute. His page is intentionally narrow: the source uses him to show that once Dong Zhuo converts policy disagreement into disloyalty, official speech becomes exposure rather than constraint.
