## Overview

王立 / Wang Li is synthesized as a senior Wang-family relative whose access repeatedly becomes politically sensitive: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-546-haoyou-bianwei-choudi-neng-you-duo-kepa-lrtypxhdqvzkme_rgesxwoiv1asv|Hanji 546]] shows him recommending [[ChenXianWesternHan|陈贤]] as 方正, [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-557-wangmang-pk-chunyuchang-nanrenjian-de-jingxin-jiaoliang-lnvmnizx0mhrln-g1p1a8u_9kgnx|Hanji 557]] shows [[ChunyuZhangWesternHan|淳于长]] trying to buy his intercession after dismissal, and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-606-duan-diao-qin-shushu-wangmang-de-xinji-you-duo-shen-lswummzfg74d-_dm_k4vc7fg8eut|Hanji 606]] later shows [[WangMang|王莽]] removing him as an uncle and [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s younger brother.

## Current Profile

Hanji 606 says Wang Li was Hongyang Hou and no longer held active office at the time. Wang Mang still feared him because Wang Li could speak with Wang Zhengjun from inside the family relationship and potentially shape her view of court affairs.

Hanji 546 gives an earlier recommendation-risk layer. Wang Li uses the 方正 channel to recommend Chen Xian, whose appointment as Guanglu Dafu and Jishi Zhong immediately alarms Zhai Fangjin. Zhai impeaches Wang Li for improper recommendation, but Chengdi tells Zhai not to pursue Wang Li further because Wang Li is the emperor's uncle. Wang Li's kinship therefore works in two directions: his recommendation can restore a vulnerable actor, and his own status can shield him after the recommendation becomes politically contested.

Hanji 557 supplies the earlier Chunyu Zhang branch. After Chengdi initially dismisses Chunyu Zhang, Chunyu sends valuables to Wang Li through Wang Li's son Wang Rong and hopes Wang Li will ask Chengdi to restore him. Wang Li's resulting memorial looks suspicious because he had previously resented Chunyu Zhang over the辅政 succession field. The investigation of Wang Rong and his forced suicide escalates the case against Chunyu Zhang and leaves Wang Li himself removed to his fief rather than imprisoned because of his uncle status.

The episode's central action is Wang Li's removal. Kong Guang first impeaches him by citing his earlier defense of Chunyuzhang, who had been convicted of grave disloyalty, and by recalling Wang Li's proposal that Yang Ji's illegitimate son be made imperial heir. Wang Zhengjun initially refuses to send Wang Li away, but Wang Mang reframes the matter as a public-order issue: two emperors have died without sons, the child-emperor succession is fragile, and the dowager must not let kin feeling make ministers turn crooked. Wang Zhengjun then sends Wang Li back to his fief.

Wang Li therefore functions as an early obstacle-clearing case in Wang Mang's ascent. He is not shown organizing opposition; he is dangerous because kinship and access make him hard to control.

## Key Characteristics

- Hongyang Hou and senior Wang-family relative in late Western Han court politics.
- Younger brother of Wang Zhengjun and uncle of Wang Mang.
- Recommends Chen Xian through the 方正 channel, triggering Zhai Fangjin's objection.
- Receives Chunyu Zhang's gift-backed request for intercession after Chunyu's dismissal.
- No longer active in office during the episode, but still politically sensitive through dowager access.
- Target of Kong Guang's impeachment over Chunyuzhang and the Yang Ji heir proposal.
- Removed to his fief after Wang Mang reframes the case as public order overriding private kinship.

## Qualifications

The sources do not settle Wang Li's full earlier career, his own account of the Chen Xian, Chunyuzhang, or Yang Ji matters, whether he knowingly sold intercession, or whether he was actively opposing Wang Mang. The wiki records the narrower pattern: Wang Li became dangerous because family access, recommendation authority, old grievances, and intercession channels made him hard to control.
