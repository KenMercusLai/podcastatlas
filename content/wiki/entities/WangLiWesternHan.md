## Overview

王立 / Wang Li enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-606-duan-diao-qin-shushu-wangmang-de-xinji-you-duo-shen-lswummzfg74d-_dm_k4vc7fg8eut|Hanji 606]] as [[WangMang|王莽]]'s uncle and [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s younger brother. The source makes him important less as an officeholder than as a senior Wang-family relative whose informal access to the dowager could threaten Wang Mang's control of late Western Han court decisions.

## Current Profile

Hanji 606 says Wang Li was Hongyang Hou and no longer held active office at the time. Wang Mang still feared him because Wang Li could speak with Wang Zhengjun from inside the family relationship and potentially shape her view of court affairs.

The episode's central action is Wang Li's removal. Kong Guang first impeaches him by citing his earlier defense of Chunyuzhang, who had been convicted of grave disloyalty, and by recalling Wang Li's proposal that Yang Ji's illegitimate son be made imperial heir. Wang Zhengjun initially refuses to send Wang Li away, but Wang Mang reframes the matter as a public-order issue: two emperors have died without sons, the child-emperor succession is fragile, and the dowager must not let kin feeling make ministers turn crooked. Wang Zhengjun then sends Wang Li back to his fief.

Wang Li therefore functions as an early obstacle-clearing case in Wang Mang's ascent. He is not shown organizing opposition; he is dangerous because kinship and access make him hard to control.

## Key Characteristics

- Hongyang Hou and senior Wang-family relative in late Western Han court politics.
- Younger brother of Wang Zhengjun and uncle of Wang Mang.
- No longer active in office during the episode, but still politically sensitive through dowager access.
- Target of Kong Guang's impeachment over Chunyuzhang and the Yang Ji heir proposal.
- Removed to his fief after Wang Mang reframes the case as public order overriding private kinship.

## Qualifications

The source does not settle Wang Li's full earlier career, his own account of the Chunyuzhang or Yang Ji matters, or whether he was actively opposing Wang Mang. The wiki records the episode's narrower claim: Wang Li became removable because family access was politically threatening during Wang Mang's consolidation.
