# Autohome / 汽车之家

## Overview

Autohome / 汽车之家 is the automotive internet company built by [[LiXiangLiAuto|李想]] after his earlier PC-media work and before he founded [[LiAuto|理想汽车]].

## Current Profile

The bounded source presents Autohome as both a market pivot and a product-language training ground. Li says the PC category had moved into decline, so the team sought a larger growth market and gradually became interested in cars. Automotive reviews were translated from specification language into questions users could perceive directly, such as what fits in a cup holder or how much legroom remains.

The company also supplies Li's organization and crisis-learning layer. He describes intense but executable disagreement among senior partners, a 2008 shareholder challenge, a later equity problem that threatened the IPO, and a shift from privately carrying danger to sharing actionable context with the core team. Autohome's December 2013 New York listing is presented as the outcome of that sequence, not as evidence that every internal judgment was sound.

## Key Characteristics

- Automotive information company reached through a pivot away from a slowing PC-media market.
- User-language publisher translating vehicle specifications into recognizable experiences.
- Founder-learning environment for hiring, disagreement, finance, shareholder governance, and crisis communication.
- Predecessor organization whose lessons informed Li Auto's product and management approach.
- Source-scoped corporate history currently based on Li's retrospective account.
