# Pamela Wisniewski

## Overview
Pamela Wisniewski appears in the bounded source as a researcher focused on youth online privacy and safety, parenting, platform design, and AI-mediated risk intervention.

## Current Profile
Wisniewski argues for a developmental safety model that combines staged access, adult guidance, platform responsibility, practical risk education, and adolescent agency. Her examples span family technology rules, body shaming in group chats, AI moderation, AI-amplified scams and grooming, simulated predator conversations, and feedback interventions. The source presents her as resisting both unrestricted access and blanket restriction: young people need protection, but they also need realistic practice in judgment and self-regulation.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats online safety as skill development as well as harm prevention.
- Frames parents as guides who model reflective technology use and introduce access gradually.
- Supports safety-by-design while demanding evidence about effectiveness and autonomy costs.
- Studies AI as both a protective intervention layer and a threat amplifier.
- Centers adolescent voice, practical advice, and non-parentalizing feedback.
