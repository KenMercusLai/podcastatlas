# Boston Dynamics

[[yushu-shangshi-baozhang-dan-renxing-jiqiren-de-qian-daodi-cong-nali-zhuan-s10e26-4a50d4a3-a6ff-4c89-b754-367b73ce924b]] adds Boston Dynamics as a quadruped-route comparator for [[UnitreeRobotics]]. The source links early robot-dog demand to [[DARPA]] and terrain/load-carrying needs, using the example to show how a non-humanoid form can start from a clearer customer problem than general humanoid labor replacement.

Boston Dynamics is referenced in [[wo-yudao-le-di-yige-zhenzheng-xiang-mai-de-peiban-jiqiren-duihua-shibo-yueban-dongli-chuangshiren-gonglu-boke-lrydelizm0-hbk68u5cqe3ti-epb]] through [[Shibo]]'s admiration for [[MarcRaibert]]. In the source, it functions as a robotics inspiration point for autonomous movement and embodied systems.

[[tsr-s2-trevorblackwell-v2]] adds Boston Dynamics as the comparison point for [[TrevorBlackwell]]'s [[Anybots]] work. Blackwell says there were periods when his walking-robot work and Boston Dynamics alternated in relative progress, while recent Boston Dynamics systems are far ahead of what he reached. The source makes Boston Dynamics part of the longer [[DynamicBalancingRobotics]] history before the current foundation-model framing of [[EmbodiedAI]].
