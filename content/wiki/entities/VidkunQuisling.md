# Vidkun Quisling

## Overview

Vidkun Quisling was a Norwegian fascist politician whose defense knowledge assisted German invasion planning and whose attempted puppet government was rejected by Haakon VII.

## Current Profile

[[621-the-nazis-at-war-blitzkrieg-part-2-glt8711886512]] presents Quisling less as the leader of a mass constituency than as an insider useful to [[NaziGermany]]. A former defense minister and leader of Nasjonal Samling, he supplied information about Norwegian defenses after being introduced to Hitler by Grand Admiral Raeder.

During the invasion he attempted to head a collaborationist government. [[HaakonVII|Haakon VII's]] refusal denied that arrangement royal legitimacy, making Quisling the episode's clearest contrast between insider betrayal and constitutional resistance.

## Key Characteristics

- Norwegian fascist and leader of Nasjonal Samling.
- Former defense minister with access to useful knowledge of national defenses.
- Collaborator who supplied information to German leaders before invasion.
- Proposed head of a German-backed government rejected by the king.

## Evidence

- Insider value: [[621-the-nazis-at-war-blitzkrieg-part-2-glt8711886512]] links Quisling's previous defense office to information provided to Germany.
- German access: [[621-the-nazis-at-war-blitzkrieg-part-2-glt8711886512]] says Raeder introduced him to Hitler during invasion planning.
- Failed legitimation: [[621-the-nazis-at-war-blitzkrieg-part-2-glt8711886512]] contrasts his proposed government with Haakon's refusal and threatened abdication.

## Qualifications

The episode does not provide a full account of Nasjonal Samling, Quisling's ideology, the sequence of occupation governments, his wartime administration, or his postwar trial. The exact intelligence he supplied and its operational effect remain source-scoped.
