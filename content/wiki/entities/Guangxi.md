# Guangxi / 广西

[[ep260-yikou-feimei-zhifang-cangzhe-zhongguo-yangzhi-de-xin-gushi-linjq2fkzhqvzjcgrmhudq12vs4j]] adds Guangxi's foie gras production branch through Beihai. The episode contrasts a more concentrated local industry model with the more dispersed [[Anhui|安徽]]/Huoqiu model, making Guangxi part of a food-system discussion about [[FoieGras|鹅肝]], force-feeding, farmer income, export markets, and animal-welfare tradeoffs.

Guangxi / 广西 enters the wiki through [[ep245-yinian-yidu-xunwei-zhilv-jinnian-qu-chi-chuangyi-cai-li9wtavemokcckialbbj7unpmqsz]] as a regional cuisine case that is still becoming legible to outsiders. The source says many diners recognize Guangxi first through 螺蛳粉 and an acid-spicy impression, but that broader [[GuangxiCuisineLegibility|广西菜可识别化]] still requires stable memory points beyond one famous noodle dish.

The episode highlights 柠檬鸭, 老友鱼, 啤酒鱼, and 爆炒生料 as possible entry points. Its distinction from [[Guizhou|贵州]] matters: Guizhou sourness is often framed through tomato-based red sour soup, while Guangxi sourness can come from many separately fermented or preserved ingredients such as salty lemon, 山黄皮, 酸藠头, 酸姜, and 酸辣椒.
