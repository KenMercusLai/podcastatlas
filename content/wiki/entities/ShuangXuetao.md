# 双雪涛 / Shuang Xuetao

[[ShuangXuetao|双雪涛]] is the writer and [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] guest in [[ep243-jiang-qiming-x-li-xueqin-x-shuang-xuetao-xiang-zai-dongbei-shangtian-zhe-shi-er-haishi-tai-fuza-le-lkqbo0zg5y7p1fo4x90n8yq394t6]], where he discusses the adaptation of [[FeixingjiaFilm|《飞行家》]] from text to film. The source presents him as the person who can name the core shift: a literary interest in "failure" becomes, in the film, a more ordinary and relational account of one person's life.

The episode also uses Shuang's biography and memory to build [[NortheastAsMemoryStructure]]. He describes distance from Shenyang, the strengthening of Northeast things in himself after forty, vanished landmarks, factory smell, language, football fields, and the way earlier life keeps structuring later identity.
