## Overview

司隶校尉 / Sili Xiaowei enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-441-gaikuanrao-bei-hanxuandi-bisi-beihou-de-zhichang-zhenxiang-tai-fengci-lm7iqjl6m638ulcpvir1jv7gxho0|Hanji 441]] as [[GaiKuanraoWesternHan|盖宽饶]]'s office under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]. The episode describes it as a powerful supervisory role over the capital and nearby areas.

## Current Profile

The current profile is institutional rather than biographical. Hanji 441 uses the office to explain why Gai was both important and vulnerable: a capital-area supervisory official can expose misconduct and restrain corrupt actors, but that same duty generates enemies. [[ZhengChangRemonstratorWesternHan|郑昌]]'s defense of Gai depends on this point: the office's duty, combined with Gai's lack of powerful backing, makes many enemies and few protectors predictable.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han supervisory office associated in this source with capital and surrounding-area oversight.
- Role that carries high conflict exposure because it can investigate or criticize powerful actors.
- Institutional context for Gai Kuanrao's many enemies and limited protection.
