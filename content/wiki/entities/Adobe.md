# Adobe

## Overview
Adobe is a professional creative-software company represented through product analytics, AI-era creative-tool economics, and its abandoned $20 billion agreement to acquire [[Figma]].

## Current Profile
Adobe combines established creative workflows and products such as [[AdobePhotoshop]] with AI features and copyright-safety positioning. Its evidence also exposes two limits of incumbent scale: added inference cost still needs monetization, and a strategic acquisition can fail after extended regulatory review even when both parties sign the transaction.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates established professional creative-software workflows with brand, file-format, and user-habit advantages.
- Embeds AI into creative tools while emphasizing copyright-safe model training.
- Faces the application-layer challenge of converting higher inference cost into incremental customer value and revenue.
- Appeared in an earlier analytics landscape that did not answer [[Sonalight]]'s desired behavioral product questions.
- Signed a $20 billion Figma acquisition agreement that was abandoned after prolonged multi-jurisdiction review.
