## Overview
温小倩 / Wen Xiaoqian is presented in the source as a sports-psychology PhD and consultant who has provided psychological training and support to elite athletes, including work connected to national-team and National Games preparation cycles.

## Current Profile
[[yundongyuan-zui-da-de-duishou-shi-ziji-de-danao-lposnam7-4sk0b8p_lou4gnl4xae]] presents Wen as a practitioner who treats sport psychology as long-horizon performance and care infrastructure rather than pre-event encouragement. Her account moves between individual attention, emotion, motivation, imagery, and arousal; coach consultation and family communication; multidisciplinary monitoring and clinical referral; and the athlete's identity beyond one result.

Her practical position is acceptance-based and task-specific. Pressure cannot be fully removed, competition cannot perfectly reproduce training, and no single emotional state guarantees performance. The workable target is a range in which the athlete can notice current state, select a practiced regulation method, and return to a controllable movement or tactical cue.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames sport psychology as preparation across a season or Olympic cycle rather than a last-minute intervention.
- Separates wanting to win from attending to the next executable action.
- Uses individualized arousal, attention, imagery, grounding, and relaxation routines rather than one universal calm state.
- Treats anxiety and unwanted thought as information to understand and allow, not proof of weakness or commands that must disappear.
- Works through coaches, medical staff, physical trainers, families, and clinical professionals when athlete needs exceed one role.
- Keeps failure, career transition, and transferable capacities inside the psychological picture rather than equating the athlete with a result.
