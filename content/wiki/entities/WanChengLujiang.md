# 皖城 / Wan City (Lujiang)

## Overview
皖城 / Wan City is the Lujiang target captured by [[SunCe|孙策]] and [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] after [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]] leaves his rear exposed.

## Current Profile
The current source presents Wan City as a rear-base prize rather than an isolated battlefield. Its fall turns Sun Ce's inducement of Liu Xun into a decisive material transfer: family members, population, ships, and commandery control move from the Liu Xun/Yuan Shu orbit into Sun Ce's Jiangdong expansion.

## Key Characteristics
- Functions as Liu Xun's practical Lujiang base while his main army moves toward Shangliao.
- Becomes vulnerable only after Sun Ce draws Liu Xun out with gifts, humility, and the promise of joint gain.
- Falls to Sun Ce and Zhou Yu while [[SunBenLateHan|孙贲]] and [[SunFuLateHan|孙辅]] guard Pengze.
- Concentrates the material results of the operation: captives, population, ships, and appointment authority.
