# 皖城 / Wan City (Lujiang)

## Overview
皖城 / Wan City is a Lujiang strongpoint captured in two source windows: first by [[SunCe|孙策]] and [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] after [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]] leaves his rear exposed, and later by [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s force after [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] identifies its emerging supply-base value.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1038 presents Wan City as a rear-base prize rather than an isolated battlefield. Its fall turns Sun Ce's inducement of Liu Xun into a decisive material transfer: family members, population, ships, and commandery control move from the Liu Xun/Yuan Shu orbit into Sun Ce's Jiangdong expansion.

Hanji 1143 returns to the city in 214 CE under [[ZhuGuangLateHan|朱光]]. Rice cultivation and irrigation make it a prospective forward grain base for Cao's side. Lyu Meng urges attack before harvest and before reinforcement or falling water closes the window; the city falls in the episode's rapid-assault account before [[ZhangLiao|张辽]] can relieve it.

## Key Characteristics
- Functions as Liu Xun's practical Lujiang base while his main army moves toward Shangliao.
- Becomes vulnerable only after Sun Ce draws Liu Xun out with gifts, humility, and the promise of joint gain.
- Falls to Sun Ce and Zhou Yu while [[SunBenLateHan|孙贲]] and [[SunFuLateHan|孙辅]] guard Pengze.
- Concentrates the material results of the operation: captives, population, ships, and appointment authority.
- Reappears as a developing agricultural and military supply base under Zhu Guang.
- Is vulnerable to a narrow timing window defined by harvest, weak defenses, reinforcement distance, morale, and river level.
- Falls to Sun Quan's force in a rapid assault that the episode places before Zhang Liao's arrival.

## Qualifications
This page disambiguates 皖城 from [[WanCounty|宛县 / 宛城]]. Both source windows concern the Lujiang city, not the Nanyang route represented by Wan County. Hanji 1143 repeatedly writes “宛城,” but its Lujiang setting and campaign actors identify the target as 皖城; exact agricultural scale, forces, civilian captive total, assault duration, and relief timing remain source-scoped.
