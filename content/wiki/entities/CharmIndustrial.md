# Charm Industrial

Charm Industrial is the carbon-removal company [[PeterReinhardt]] discusses in [[peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted-peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted]]. The source says Charm grew from Peter's frustration with opaque carbon offsets and from an early idea of turning existing industrial processes from carbon emitters into carbon sinks.

In the episode, Charm's current model is [[BioOilCarbonRemoval]]: procure biomass such as wildfire residues, convert it into bio-oil, ship it for underground injection, and let customers pay for carbon tons that cannot be eliminated through emissions reductions. Peter names [[Stripe]], [[Microsoft]], [[Shopify]], [[JPMorganChase|JPMorgan Chase]], Figma, and other technology or finance customers as demand sources, with [[FrontierCarbonRemoval|Frontier]] as an important early buyer.

The source makes Charm a concrete [[ClimateStartupCommercializationGap]] case. The hard parts are not only chemistry or machinery; they include feedstock logistics, injection sites, ledger transparency, throughput, cost reduction, and [[CarbonRemovalPermitting]]. Peter says the first Louisiana permit shifted the company from regulatory waiting toward execution and cost work.
