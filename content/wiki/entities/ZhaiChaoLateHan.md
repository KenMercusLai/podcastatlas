## Overview

翟超 / Zhai Chao is the Shanyang commandery official in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] who places [[ZhangJianLateHan|张俭]] in a supervisory role and is punished after Zhang challenges [[HouLanLateHan|侯览]]'s local abuses.

## Current Profile

The source gives Zhai Chao a compact but structurally important role. He is not the main remonstrant or executioner; he is the appointing superior whose local enforcement chain hits palace-protected power. By appointing Zhang Jian as an eastern-area supervisory official, Zhai Chao enables the investigation of Hou Lan's seized property, overbuilt houses, and lavish tomb construction.

When eunuch retaliation reaches [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]], Zhai Chao is punished alongside [[HuangFuDonghaiLateHan|黄浮]]. His case shows how liability can move upward from the acting official to the superior who authorized or enabled enforcement.

## Key Characteristics

- Commandery-level official whose appointment of Zhang Jian makes anti-Hou-Lan enforcement possible.
- Local superior exposed to palace retaliation after a subordinate attacks eunuch-linked abuses.
- Collateral punishment case in the same field as Huang Fu's execution of Xu Xuan.
- Source-scoped bridge between local governance and Huan-era eunuch politics.
