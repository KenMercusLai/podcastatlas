## Overview
《重新回到生活：科技时代的工作、情绪、爱情和死亡》 is [[WangXiaowei|王小伟]]'s book discussed by [[BuHeShiYi|不合时宜]] as a technology-philosophy route into AI-era work, emotion, love, death, and everyday life.

## Current Profile
The book enters the wiki through one interview rather than through direct full-text ingestion. In that source it works as the organizing anchor for a conversation about how technology, especially AI, changes intimacy, work meaning, emotional language, education, governance, and self-understanding.

The episode's strongest reading of the book is that "returning to life" is not a method checklist. It is a resistance to letting technology's logic of smoothness, measurability, control, and optimization define all experience. The title's "life" includes embodied rhythm, ordinary support networks, non-instrumental time, scenery, conversation, waiting, vulnerability, and the parts of work and love that do not become better merely by becoming more efficient.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses technology philosophy to connect AI with work, emotion, love, death, and everyday practice.
- Treats AI's convenience as a philosophical pressure because it changes what people expect from relationships, judgment, and self-analysis.
- Resists turning "return to life" into a productivity plan or new metric.
- Values embodied, slow, uncertain, relational experience as a counterweight to technical optimization.
- Serves as the book anchor for the episode's concepts of [[TechnologyPhilosophy]], [[AIJudgmentInfrastructure]], and [[LifeTimeVsMachineTime]].
