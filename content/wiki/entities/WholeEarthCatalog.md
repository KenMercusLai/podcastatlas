# Whole Earth Catalog / 《全球目录》

## Overview
Whole Earth Catalog / 《全球目录》 is the counterculture publication [[RenNing]] uses through [[StewartBrand]] to connect space-age Earth imagery, environmental consciousness, tools, and later technology culture.

## Current Profile
The current source treats the catalog as a media object where technical imagery becomes a cultural reorientation device. Stewart Brand wanted a complete Earth image to change human scale perception, and the catalog's first cover used a satellite whole-Earth color image. The episode then connects the catalog's later cultural afterlife to [[SteveJobs]] and to Brand's line about humans becoming godlike and needing to learn how to handle that power.

## Key Characteristics
- Links counterculture publishing with space-age visual technology.
- Uses a whole-Earth image as a way to change scale perception.
- Helps convert technical distance from Earth into environmental and cultural self-recognition.
- Connects tool culture and later computing imagination through the source's Steve Jobs reference.
- Supports the episode's closing problem of learning to handle enlarged human power.

## Evidence
- Cover and image: [[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] says the catalog's first cover used the 1967 whole-Earth color image.
- Brand's question: [[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] describes Brand asking why there was not yet a complete Earth photograph and believing it would alter human thinking.
- Jobs afterlife: [[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] notes that Steve Jobs later mentioned the catalog in his Stanford commencement speech.
- Power ethic: [[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] uses Brand's "as gods" line to close the episode's responsibility argument.
