# 陕城 / Shancheng (Warring States)

陕城 / Shancheng appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-37-suqin-liuguo-jituan-ceo-de-bukan-qingshi-fj2kdqlaxzyy2nqtcutktuwrq5fo]] as the [[WeiState|魏国]] place taken by [[QinState|秦国]] during [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s 324 BCE attack. The episode locates it around present-day Shanzhou District, Sanmenxia, Henan.

The source says Shancheng had been returned to Wei in 327 BCE, then was taken again by Qin in 324 BCE. It therefore belongs to the same frontier-pressure pattern as [[Jiaoyi|焦邑]], [[QuwoWarringStates|曲沃]], [[PuyangWarringStates|蒲阳]], and [[Shangjun|上郡]]: territory can be seized, returned, or bargained over while Qin keeps shaping Wei's room for action.

The episode's distinctive detail is that Qin takes the city but sends the people back to Wei. That makes Shancheng a case where [[QinEastwardPressure|秦国东进压力]] is not described as slaughter but as selective territorial control.
