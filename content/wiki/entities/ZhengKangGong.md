# 郑康公 / Duke Kang of Zheng

郑康公 / Duke Kang of Zheng appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-1-lvkp-ju7pf5z5cz3thojv3s2fkyx]] as the ruler installed after [[ZiyangZheng|子阳]]'s remaining faction kills the Zheng ruler two years after Ziyang's own death. The episode uses the succession to show that assassination can become a factional route to state capture.

The page is source-scoped. It records the episode's political use of Zheng Kang Gong rather than attempting a complete biography.
