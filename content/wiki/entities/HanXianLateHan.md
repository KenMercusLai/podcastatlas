# 韩暹 / Han Xian (late Han)

## Overview
韩暹 / Han Xian is a former [[BaiboArmy|白波军]]-linked military actor whose late-Han role moves from emergency emperor escort to court predator, coalition switcher, and finally fugitive remnant. His repeated proximity to [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] gives him legitimacy language, but the sources consistently show that he lacks the food discipline, governance capacity, and durable alignment needed to turn escort merit into stable authority.

## Current Profile
Han Xian's profile is defined by [[ProtectorCaptorAmbiguity|护驾与挟持模糊性]]. He helps rescue and escort the emperor, but he and allied armed actors also dominate the court, grant offices, and plunder civilians. This behavior makes him both useful and intolerable: [[DongChengLateHan|董承]] appeals to [[CaoCao|曹操]] against him, while [[LyuBu|吕布]] later uses Han-court legitimacy and promised spoils to detach him from [[YuanShu|袁术]]. Hanji 1021 closes the arc by showing Han Xian reduced to flight with a few riders and killed before he can rebuild a base.

## Key Characteristics
- Former Baibo-linked commander mobilized for Emperor Xian's emergency protection.
- Protector-predator whose court merit coexists with office-granting and civilian plunder.
- High-rank armed obstacle whose behavior helps give Cao Cao a reason to enter Luoyang.
- Coalition-switching actor vulnerable to legitimacy-plus-spoils persuasion.
- Final remnant whose flight and death expose the limits of mobile armed prestige.

## Evidence
- Emergency escort role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-991-zhengduo-xiandi-zhizhan-shui-neng-zuizhong-qusheng-lkbsl5uzqxouzm6mlewrnuns0x6p|Hanji 991]] places Han Xian with [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], [[LiLeLateHan|李乐]], [[HuCaiLateHan|胡才]], and [[QubeiSouthernXiongnu|去卑]] in the reinforcement field during Emperor Xian's flight.
- Protector-predator pattern: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu|Hanji 999]], [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1000-hao-meng-panluan-jingbi-lvbu-fanqiang-taopao-lluthd9hv-0nqbot4fuctjcuka68|Hanji 1000]], and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1004-caocao-yingfeng-hanxiandi-shi-shui-de-zhuyi-lrrga-flmx5ajnhu1c42x7kooe94|Hanji 1004]] show Han Xian around the Anyi/Luoyang court, where escort merit does not become governance capacity.
- Cao entry pretext: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1003-sanguo-zui-bei-digu-de-moushi-dongzhao-llc7q-loennxw26v3u5cbhitikw1|Hanji 1003]] says Han Xian's high rank and White Wave-linked network make Cao Cao's entry dangerous, while Dong Cheng's fear of Han Xian gives Cao Cao an overt reason to come to Luoyang.
- Coalition fragility: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1018-ta-weihe-rang-caocao-jidan-sunce-weiju-liubei-wanxi-lqccdhrjmero-gt7vsaq2ejuhhw8|Hanji 1018]] shows Lü Bu persuading Han Xian and Yang Feng to reverse sides against Yuan Shu's commander [[ZhangXunYuanShu|张勋]] through legitimacy and material reward.
- Final dispersal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1021-caocao-shouwo-zhongquan-weishenme-rongbuxia-yangbiao-lr-y4xqanwjyslvf4fbwxjuymtun|Hanji 1021]] says Han Xian tries to flee toward Bingzhou with only about ten riders and is killed by the Zhuchiu county magistrate Zhang Xuan.

## Qualifications
Han Xian should not be reduced to either loyal protector or simple bandit. The evidence gives both sides: without his armed support the emperor's flight is harder, but his troops' food needs and predatory behavior make the court unsafe. His final killing is recorded narrowly; the wiki does not infer a larger Zhang Xuan or Zhuchiu branch from this source alone.
