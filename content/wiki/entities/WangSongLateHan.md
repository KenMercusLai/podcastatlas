# 王嵩 / Wang Song (late Han)

## Overview
王嵩 / Wang Song (late Han) enters the wiki through Hanji 1070 as the local actor occupying Zhuo commandery during the post-Ye northern settlement. He matters because [[LiuFangLateHan|刘放]] persuades him to submit the commandery to [[CaoCao|曹操]].

## Current Profile
Hanji 1070 gives Wang Song a compact role. He holds Zhuo commandery while Cao Cao is absorbing the northern field after Yuan-family collapse. Liu Fang advises him to submit, and that advice brings Zhuo commandery into Cao Cao's side. Wang Song is therefore a local-holder case rather than a fully developed regional power in the current evidence.

## Key Characteristics
- Local holder of Zhuo commandery in the Youzhou-side transition.
- Receives Liu Fang's submission advice.
- Becomes part of Cao Cao's expanding northern settlement without a described battle.
