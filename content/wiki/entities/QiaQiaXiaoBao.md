# 恰恰小豹 / Qia Qia Xiao Bao

## Overview
恰恰小豹 / Qia Qia Xiao Bao is the birdwatching podcast represented here through a crossover with [[ChizaoGengxin|迟早更新]] on birdsong playback, field ethics, ritual, and speech.

## Current Profile
The current wiki evidence is limited to one crossover source. In that episode, 恰恰小豹 supplies the birdwatching-problem context: whether observers should use playback or sound luring to bring birds into view, and why the practice can feel ethically different from slower waiting, tracking, or ordinary presence.

The show therefore enters the wiki as a birdwatching ethics node rather than as a fully described media project. Its represented role is to make a concrete field practice available for [[RenNing]]'s wider discussion of [[BirdsongPlaybackEthics]], ritualized apology, and human disturbance of other species.

## Key Characteristics
- Functions as the birdwatching counterpart in the Episode 226 crossover.
- Brings the sound-playback controversy into the episode's opening frame.
- Treats birdwatching as a practice with norms, not just species appreciation.
- Makes observer desire, field technique, and nonhuman disturbance part of the same discussion.
