## Overview

王尊 / Wang Zun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-507-ta-zhansha-xiongnu-chanyu-weihe-fanbei-xiayu-lhfkej7pqtikx5st73sls-kwo_ze|Hanji 507]] as the former Gaoling magistrate recommended to [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]] when Nanshan bandits led by Peng Zong have resisted suppression for more than a year. The episode says Wang Zun is summoned, acts as Jingzhao-yin, clears the bandits within a month, and is then formally promoted. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-502-rang-laoban-bei-heiguo-shi-shui-zai-zhaosi-ls833sgrdjz9e1tupiiqillagohb|Hanji 502]] backfills an earlier new-reign impeachment role: as Sili Xiaowei, Wang Zun attacks [[KuangHengWesternHan|匡衡]] and [[ZhangTanWesternHan|张谭]] after [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]]'s fall, but [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] protects Kuang Heng and demotes Wang Zun. ## Current Profile

The source uses Wang Zun as a personnel-fit contrast before turning to [[ChenTangWesternHan|陈汤]]. A small but persistent local-security failure had already defeated a thousand-soldier suppression effort. Wang Zun's quick result makes the problem look less like insufficient force alone and more like a mismatch between office, local command, and competent execution.

The page therefore treats Wang Zun narrowly as a Chengdi-era local-capacity figure. The episode does not describe his full career, but it does make his appointment evidence that the court could still solve concrete problems when recommendation, office authority, and practical ability aligned.

Hanji 511 makes that earlier capacity politically fragile. Wang Zun's public-order record does not prevent impeachment as cruel and arrogant, yet it becomes central to Gongcheng Xing's argument that the court should not let private resentment rewrite a capable official as a villain. Chengdi's later Xuzhou appointment shows partial restoration without proving that every accusation was false.

Hanji 502 gives Wang Zun a sharper court-politics profile before the local-security and later-review cases. His impeachment of Kuang Heng and Zhang Tan may have a substantive point: they had not confronted Shi Xian while he was powerful and then used language that shifted responsibility toward Emperor Yuan. But the episode reads the timing as politically dangerous. A new ruler trying to stabilize the court can punish the accuser if the accusation threatens senior-office continuity, predecessor dignity, and the need for ministers who can absorb future blame.

## Key Characteristics

- Former Gaoling magistrate recommended as capable of handling the Nanshan bandit problem.
- Summoned through Wang Feng's channel and made acting Jingzhao-yin.
- Suppresses Peng Zong's bandit group within a month after earlier efforts failed.
- Becomes an example of local-security capacity through correct office fit rather than brute-force escalation alone.
- Earlier uses Sili Xiaowei impeachment authority against Kuang Heng and Zhang Tan after Shi Xian's fall.
- Later impeached, defended through a public-review argument, and partially restored as Xuzhou inspector.

## Qualifications

It records the podcast's local-security example, later impeachment-defense branch, and earlier new-reign impeachment against Kuang Heng and Zhang Tan, but does not reconstruct Wang Zun's full biography, methods, legal measures, final adjudication, motive, or later career beyond the Xuzhou inspector appointment.
