# Henry II of England

## Overview

Henry II of England appears in this source as [[EmpressMatilda|Matilda]]'s son, Duke of Normandy, and the dynastic beneficiary of the settlement that ended her struggle with [[StephenOfBlois|Stephen of Blois]].

## Current Profile

The episode uses Henry less as a fully developed ruler than as the solution to Matilda's legitimacy trap. By the late 1140s, Matilda recognized that she could not unite the Anglo-Norman barons around her own female leadership. Her political judgment lay in transferring effort from her own coronation to Henry's succession.

Henry's recognition as Duke of Normandy in 1150 and heir to Stephen under the 1153 Treaty of Winchester mark the point where Matilda's failed personal claim becomes a successful dynastic claim. The later note that Henry sought Matilda's advice presents her as an elder stateswoman in his regime, though the episode also notes he ignored her warning about Thomas Becket.

## Key Characteristics

- Son of Matilda and Geoffrey of Anjou in the source's succession narrative.
- Male dynastic successor who made Matilda's claim politically more acceptable.
- Recognized as Duke of Normandy before the English settlement.
- Became Stephen's agreed heir under the Treaty of Winchester.
- Sought Matilda's advice as king, making her later authority indirect but real.
- Illustrates the episode's distinction between transmitting a claim and ruling directly.
