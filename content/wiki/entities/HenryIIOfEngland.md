# Henry II of England

## Overview

Henry II of England appears as [[EmpressMatilda|Matilda]]'s son and dynastic beneficiary in the English succession story, and as the king who entered Ireland in 1171 after the first Anglo-Norman lordly interventions.

## Current Profile

The Matilda episode uses Henry less as a fully developed ruler than as the solution to his mother's legitimacy trap. By the late 1140s, Matilda recognized that she could not unite the Anglo-Norman barons around her own female leadership. Her political judgment lay in transferring effort from her own coronation to Henry's succession.

Henry's recognition as Duke of Normandy in 1150 and heir to Stephen under the 1153 Treaty of Winchester mark the point where Matilda's failed personal claim becomes a successful dynastic claim. The later note that Henry sought Matilda's advice presents her as an elder stateswoman in his regime, though the episode also notes he ignored her warning about Thomas Becket.

The Ireland episode extends his profile into 1171. After lords including the Fitzgeralds, de Bourgs, and Strongbow entered from 1169, Henry came to Ireland, took key towns into the royal domain, and allowed barons to hold other lands. The source treats this as a durable sovereignty claim and settlement threshold, not the completion of a uniform conquest over the island.

## Key Characteristics

- Son of Matilda and Geoffrey of Anjou in the source's succession narrative.
- Male dynastic successor, recognized as Duke of Normandy, who made Matilda's claim politically more acceptable.
- Became Stephen's agreed heir under the Treaty of Winchester.
- Sought Matilda's advice as king, making her later authority indirect but real.
- Illustrates the episode's distinction between transmitting a claim and ruling directly.
- Entered Ireland in 1171 and converted prior lordly intervention into a royal claim and territorial foothold without completing island-wide conquest.

## Evidence

- Dynastic pivot: [[empress-matilda-civil-war-and-the-fight-for-the-throne-glt3399401666]] says Matilda shifted from her own claim toward securing Henry's future.
- Norman position: [[empress-matilda-civil-war-and-the-fight-for-the-throne-glt3399401666]] says Henry was recognized as Duke of Normandy in 1150.
- English settlement: [[empress-matilda-civil-war-and-the-fight-for-the-throne-glt3399401666]] says the 1153 Treaty of Winchester made Henry Stephen's heir.
- Later counsel: [[empress-matilda-civil-war-and-the-fight-for-the-throne-glt3399401666]] says Henry sought Matilda's advice, including before the Becket appointment.
- Irish intervention: [[336-ireland-celts-conquest-and-cromwell-part-1-glt6280042699]] places Henry in Ireland in 1171, taking royal towns and recognizing baronial lands after the 1169-1170 interventions.

## Qualifications

This remains a source-scoped profile. Henry II's wider reign, Angevin empire, legal reforms, family politics, Becket conflict, papal authority claims, and detailed Irish diplomacy require later sources before full synthesis. The Ireland source explicitly rejects treating his arrival as a completed conquest.

## What Changed

- Added Henry's 1171 Irish intervention and distinguished royal foothold from complete conquest.
