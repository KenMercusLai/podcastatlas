# Paul Atkins

## Overview
Paul Atkins appears as chair of the [[SEC]] discussing U.S. capital-market modernization, public-company formation, private-market access, tokenization, and coordination with the [[CommodityFuturesTradingCommission|CFTC]].

## Current Profile
Atkins presents a market-opening agenda bounded by material disclosure, fraud enforcement, and market stability. He argues that public-company burdens and deep private capital have moved growth returns away from ordinary public investors, while definitional uncertainty has impeded crypto and cross-jurisdictional products. The source records his policy direction, not final rule outcomes.

## Key Characteristics
- Favors reviewing SEC rules around materiality, filer status, reporting cadence, and public-company compliance burden.
- Supports widening investment access through knowledge- or credential-based qualification rather than wealth alone.
- Treats tokenization as an efficiency opportunity that still inherits securities law when the underlying asset is a security.
- Prioritizes SEC-CFTC coordination to prevent products from dying in jurisdictional gaps.
- Pairs market opening with leverage controls, disclosure, fraud prevention, and investor education.
