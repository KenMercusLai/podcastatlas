# Elias Howe Jr.

Elias Howe Jr. appears in [[the-invention-invention]] as the inventor whose sewing-machine patent claims forced [[IsaacMerrittSinger]] into settlement. The source says Singer eventually paid Howe $15,000 plus per-machine royalties because Singer's machine used part of Howe's invention.

Howe later became part of the [[SewingMachineCombination]], the pool that turned mutually blocking patent claims into a shared licensing arrangement. In the episode, his role shows why patents can both reward prior inventors and make downstream production harder when many claims overlap.
