# Trinitite

## Overview
Trinitite is presented in the bounded source as [[DustinAllen]]'s privacy and AI-governance company. Its name refers to the glass-like material formed after the Trinity nuclear test, a metaphor for containing and examining a transformative technology.

## Current Profile
The represented product approach places a governance layer between users and AI agents. It maps workflows, defines intended outcomes and risks, tests edge cases, and uses specialized adapters or smaller models to enforce boundaries. Trinitite also claims local-device and private-cloud operation so preference alignment can be evaluated without collecting the user's underlying sensitive content. The source describes both enterprise work and a free consumer application, but supplies no independent product evaluation.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames agent governance as testing expected outcomes rather than relying only on static rules.
- Uses deterministic inference, low-rank adapters, and smaller specialized models as reported enforcement techniques.
- Treats workflow mapping, output validation, and audit trails as part of safety.
- Supports user-specific rules and private or local deployment.
- Serves enterprise and consumer use cases according to the founder's account.
