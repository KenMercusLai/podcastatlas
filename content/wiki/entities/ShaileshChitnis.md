# Shailesh Chitnis

Shailesh Chitnis appears in [[wake-up-haul-an-ozempic-moment-for-the-brain-6a85798d1ad8716bb6078744]] as the [[TheIntelligence]] voice explaining why [[OrexinAgonists]] have attracted post-[[GLP1Agonists|GLP-1]] attention. His segment introduces orexin as a wakefulness-related neuropeptide, then moves from [[NarcolepsyWakeSleepBoundary]] to possible but uncertain future uses in depression, ADHD, and addiction.

The source uses him as a cautioning expert voice. He frames the [[Ozempic]] comparison as a way to understand pharmaceutical excitement, while warning that drug discovery has many plausible ideas that never become broad commercial markets.
