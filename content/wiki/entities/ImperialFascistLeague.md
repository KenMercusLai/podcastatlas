# Imperial Fascist League

## Overview

The Imperial Fascist League was an intensely antisemitic British fascist organization founded in 1928 by [[ArnoldLeese|Arnold Leese]], distinguished by elaborate Blackshirt-style uniforms but described as politically marginal.

## Current Profile

The organization belongs to the pre-Mosley phase of British fascism. Its ideology centered on Leese's conspiratorial antisemitism, including the belief that Jewish marriages were corrupting the British aristocracy.

Its members wore black shirts, khaki breeches, puttees, berets, and cummerbunds. The visual politics connected it to the wider fascist language of uniforms and disciplined masculinity, yet the episode contrasts ideological menace with practical marginality.

The League therefore shows that explicit antisemitism and fascist imitation were present before the [[BritishUnionOfFascists|British Union of Fascists]] became the dominant organization, without demonstrating that the League itself approached mass political power.

## Key Characteristics

- British fascist organization founded in 1928.
- Led by veterinarian and camel specialist Arnold Leese.
- Defined by intense and conspiratorial antisemitism.
- Uniformed movement using black shirts and paramilitary visual style.
- Ideologically sinister but politically marginal in the episode's assessment.
