# Squamish Nation

The Squamish Nation enters the wiki through [[the-skyscrapers-that-nimbys-and-zoning-couldnt-stop]] as the Indigenous nation developing [[SinakVillageSite|a returned village site]] near downtown [[Vancouver]]. The source presents the nation as both a land-rights actor and a housing developer: after regaining about 10.5 acres in 2003, it chose a high-density rental project that ordinary municipal zoning likely would have blocked or slowed.

The episode's economic point depends on the nation retaining control over its land-use choice. [[SovereignHousingDevelopment]] lets the Squamish convert valuable urban land into long-term revenue, while [[IndigenousDevelopmentVisibility]] makes the towers a cultural and political return rather than only a real estate asset.
