# Epimetheus / 厄庇米修斯

## Overview

Epimetheus is Prometheus's brother and Pandora's recipient, used here to represent hindsight and the gap between receiving a risk warning and acting on it.

## Current Profile

[[Prometheus]] warns Epimetheus not to accept any gift from [[Zeus]], yet Epimetheus accepts [[Pandora]] and recognizes the mistake only after harm occurs. The episode does not reduce this failure to ignorance: the warning exists, but attraction and immediate acceptance override it. His role therefore makes [[RiskKnowledgeActionGap]] part of the Pandora story.

## Key Characteristics

- Mythic brother and contrast figure to foresighted Prometheus.
- Recipient of an explicit warning against accepting Zeus's gift.
- Actor who accepts Pandora and understands the danger retrospectively.
- Example of risk knowledge failing to become preventive action.
