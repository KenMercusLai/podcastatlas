# ARID

ARID is the Airwallex product described in [[11-nian-110-yi-meijin-ranhou-ne-duihua-airwallex-wu-kai-ai-shidai-xiayizhan-1000-yi-lr4tvdrq25by7fugoqkqojw6vwdk]] as starting from one-click checkout. The source says the goal is for a consumer who has paid once on a merchant platform to avoid re-entering card or payment details on later transactions.

The strategic role of ARID is larger than checkout convenience. [[WuKai]] links it to a possible future consumer wallet and agent-to-agent payment settlement layer, connecting [[Airwallex]] to [[AgentPaymentInfrastructure]] and [[AgenticCommerce]] rather than only merchant acquiring.

## Key Points
- Starts from one-click checkout and remembered payment information across merchant experiences.
- Presented as a future-facing product that could support agent-to-agent payment or settlement scenarios.
- Depends on trust, identity, payment credential handling, and merchant readiness.
- Extends the Airwallex AI strategy from enterprise finance operations toward buyer-side payment experience.
