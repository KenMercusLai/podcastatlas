# Hang Seng Index / 恒生指数

The Hang Seng Index / 恒生指数 appears in [[vol-269-xiao-lishi-bu-yao-pa-shi-jishu-xing-tiaozheng-1004793119]] as the market thermometer for the [[HongKong1973StockMarketCrash|1973 Hong Kong stock market crash]]. The episode says the index moved from roughly 346 in March 1972 to more than 1,700 in March 1973, then fell to around 500 by mid-September 1973.

For this source, the index does not stand alone as a valuation tool. It is the visible price record of a broader cycle involving the [[HongkongLandDairyFarmTakeover|置地饮牛奶]] case, retail chasing, exchange fragmentation, stock-split confusion, and [[HongKongDollarHotMoneyCycle|Hong Kong dollar liquidity shifts]].
