# 48 Blocks Espresso Martini

48 Blocks Espresso Martini is the premium espresso-martini product from [[LittleWaterDistillery]] discussed in [[advice-line-with-carlton-calvin-of-razor-c0de1a84-3d35-45f3-921c-6144693ad86e]]. [[MarkLittleWaterDistillery|Mark]] describes it as one of the company's best sellers and says he wants it to become known as an industry-standard espresso martini.

The product anchors the episode's [[SKUFocusUnderCashConstraint]] lesson. [[GuyRaz]] and [[CarltonCalvin]] identify 48 Blocks Espresso Martini and the chocolate martini as the most central products, advise Mark to tell the ingredient and distilling story, and treat possible cans or line extensions as tests of consumer culture rather than reasons to keep every existing product equally resourced.
