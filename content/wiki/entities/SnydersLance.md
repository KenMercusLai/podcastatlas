# Snyder's-Lance

## Overview
Snyder's-Lance is the strategic investor and later controlling owner in the [[LateJulySnacks]] story told by [[late-july-snacks-nicole-bernard-dawes-crackers-and-cookies-were-failing-tortilla-chips-saved-them-97049a31-9144-42f5-856c-9590c5e6beb4]]. Its role makes Late July a case in strategic capital, manufacturing access, liquidity pressure, and acquisition contract risk.

## Current Profile
The episode says Snyder's Lance bought about 20% of Late July in 2007 and gave the company access to a Georgia manufacturing facility. Around 2014, it increased its ownership to about 80%, giving early investors liquidity while Nicole and Peter retained effective board control through deal structure. [[CampbellSoupCompany]] later acquired or merged with Snyder's Lance in 2018, bringing Late July into Campbell's portfolio.

## Key Characteristics
- Strategic CPG investor that helped Late July with manufacturing access before full control.
- Liquidity path for early investors after years of high operating risk.
- Ownership counterparty whose later transaction with Campbell changed Nicole's ability to buy back the brand.
- Governance case where board-control terms and transaction wording mattered after the founder no longer held simple ownership control.
