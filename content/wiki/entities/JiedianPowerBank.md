# 街电 / Jiedian

## Overview
街电 / Jiedian is the shared-power-bank company the episode uses as the highest-profile example of a sharing-economy category that survived better than most.

## Current Profile
The source introduces Jiedian through Jumei Youpin's Chen Ou buying a controlling stake in 2017 and Wang Sicong publicly questioning whether shared power banks could work. In retrospect, the episode treats the category as one of the few validated sharing-economy models because small standardized batteries, high phone-dependence, commercial venues, and urgent demand fit the rental formula.

## Key Characteristics
- It is a central company in the episode's shared-power-bank branch.
- Its 2017 Jumei Youpin deal made the category highly visible.
- It sits in the remembered "three dian and one beast" competitive set with Laidian, Xiaodian, and Monster Charging.
- The source uses it to show that some shared-asset businesses had better category fit than shared cars, umbrellas, gyms, or wardrobes.
