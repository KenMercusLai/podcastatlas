# Gaurav Karna

## Overview
Gaurav Karna is the researcher named in [[even-more-maximum-pressure-sanctions-on-iran-6a8d6a9b66c4a1e81a4c86a9]] for work separating worker effects from city place premiums using LinkedIn job histories.

## Current Profile
The episode identifies Karna as a University of California, San Diego researcher whose study used job histories from 500 million [[LinkedIn]] users. The research is used to argue that a large share of wage gaps between cities reflects place-specific effects rather than only the kinds of workers who live there.

## Key Characteristics
- Associated with large-scale labor-market measurement using professional-network job histories.
- Separates worker talent or prior earning power from city-specific wage effects.
- Provides the empirical anchor for the episode's claim that place premiums are large within countries and even larger across borders.
- Connects urban economics to migration policy, housing, transport, and worker-firm matching.

## Evidence
- Data and method: [[even-more-maximum-pressure-sanctions-on-iran-6a8d6a9b66c4a1e81a4c86a9]] says Karna used 500 million LinkedIn job histories to track workers as they moved.
- Finding: [[even-more-maximum-pressure-sanctions-on-iran-6a8d6a9b66c4a1e81a4c86a9]] cites the study for large place-effect shares in city wage gaps, including stronger effects across borders.
