# George W. Gordon

## Overview

George W. Gordon was a former Confederate officer credited by the source with writing the first Ku Klux Klan's Prescript, the document that formalized its ranks, vocabulary, and membership commitments during Reconstruction.

## Current Profile

The Prescript made a local Pulaski club legible as a larger secret order. It supplied titles such as Grand Cyclops, Grand Dragon, and Grand Wizard and used membership questions to expose opposition to Republicanism, Black equality, and Reconstruction. The resulting hierarchy supported coordination and an image of national authority, but it did not eliminate the autonomy of local dens.

Gordon's role therefore connects organization design to political violence: theatrical offices and ritual did not sit outside the Klan's program but helped turn white-supremacist assumptions into membership identity, secrecy, and intimidation.

## Key Characteristics

- Former Confederate officer associated with the first Klan's organizational development.
- Author of the Klan Prescript in the episode's account.
- Designer of an elaborate vocabulary of ranks and jurisdictions.
- Formalizer of membership commitments against Black equality, Republicanism, and Reconstruction.
- Example of how written hierarchy can coexist with operational decentralization.
