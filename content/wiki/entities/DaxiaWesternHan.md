## Overview

大夏 / Daxia is a Western Regions polity that appears both as a destination in [[ZhangQianWesternHan|张骞]]'s delegated diplomatic network and as the observation point for Shu-associated goods that inspire a proposed southwestern route.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-306-2-hanchao-diyiwei-zouxiang-shijie-de-zhongguoren-lhm5be2xdxbj0v5zmkbqdoguf1z|Hanji 306-2]] describes Daxia as lying southwest of [[DayuanWesternHan|大宛]] and as a settled polity with agriculture and urban life. More importantly for the episode's strategic sequence, Zhang Qian reports seeing bamboo staffs and fine cloth associated with Shu there. Their reported acquisition from a southeastern market becomes evidence for a possible route toward [[ShenduWesternHan|身毒]] from southwest China.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-341-1-yongchui-qingshi-zhangqian-ruhe-zaokong-xiyu-lrjtsfu1auxhbgbawt-e5dm_fbgo|Hanji 341-1]] places Daxia inside a later networked mission rather than a single direct itinerary. Zhang Qian takes credentialed deputies to [[Wusun|乌孙]], then sends them with gifts and local guides toward Daxia and other western polities; the episode says most later return with foreign representatives.

Together the sources make Daxia both a contact destination and an information node. Observed objects can reveal indirect circulation without proving the route, while delegated missions can widen state contact without settling Daxia's precise identity or political relationship with Han.

## Key Characteristics

- Western polity described in the current source set as settled, agricultural, and urban.
- Observation point for Shu-associated goods that trigger a southwestern route hypothesis.
- Destination in Zhang Qian's later delegated envoy network.
- Part of the transition from exploratory intelligence to wider Western Regions exchange.
- Source-scoped identification with the Greco-Bactrian kingdom.

## Qualifications

The page rests on two short structured podcast summaries. It does not establish Daxia's borders, government, chronology, relationship to Bactria, the provenance chain of the Shu-associated goods, the identity of the returned representative, or the exact route and outcome of either contact sequence. Hanji 306-2's distances, direction, settlement description, objects, market report, and geographic inference remain episode-attributed. “Daxia = Greco-Bactrian kingdom” remains source-scoped because Hanji 341-1 gives no chronological or philological support for the equation.

## What Changed

- Added Daxia's role as the observation point for goods associated with Shu.
- Added the distinction between indirect commodity circulation and proof of a direct southwestern route.
- Retained the later delegated-contact layer and the qualified Greco-Bactrian identification.
