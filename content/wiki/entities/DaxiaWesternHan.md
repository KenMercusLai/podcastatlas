## Overview

大夏 / Daxia is a Western Regions polity reached in this source through one of [[ZhangQianWesternHan|张骞]]'s delegated diplomatic branches.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-341-1-yongchui-qingshi-zhangqian-ruhe-zaokong-xiyu-lrjtsfu1auxhbgbawt-e5dm_fbgo|Hanji 341-1]] places Daxia inside a networked mission rather than a single direct itinerary. Zhang Qian takes several credentialed deputy envoys to [[Wusun|乌孙]], then sends them with gifts and local guides toward Daxia and other western polities. More than a year later, the episode says most deputies return to Han with foreign representatives.

The source calls Daxia the Greco-Bactrian kingdom, but gives no chronological or philological support for that identification. The current profile therefore retains the equivalence as episode-attributed and treats the firmer claim as diplomatic contact with the polity Han sources called 大夏.

## Key Characteristics

- Western polity named as a destination in Zhang Qian's delegated envoy network.
- Contacted through deputy envoys carrying Han gifts and imperial credentials.
- Part of the episode's transition from bilateral Wusun diplomacy to wider Western Regions exchange.
- Source-scoped identification with the Greco-Bactrian kingdom.

## Qualifications

The page rests on one short structured podcast summary. It does not establish Daxia's borders, government, chronology, relationship to Bactria, the identity of the returned representative, or the exact route and outcome of the deputy mission. “Daxia = Greco-Bactrian kingdom” remains source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Created the page to represent Daxia as a distinct destination in Zhang Qian's delegated diplomatic network.
- Preserved the Greco-Bactrian identification as a qualification rather than settled identity.
