# MI5

## Overview
MI5 is Britain's domestic security service. In [[foiled-plot-did-iran-plan-british-base-attack-6aba32b170b66274d96fb345]], its director-general's public account of more than 20 potentially lethal Iran-backed plots in a 12-month period supplies the broader threat context for, but not proof about, the alleged [[RAFFairford]] plot.

## Current Profile
The episode places MI5 at the boundary between counterterrorism, counterintelligence, and state-linked grey-zone threats inside Britain. Its reported tracking of Iranian-backed activity helps explain why investigators and commentators might consider [[Iran]], yet the source does not say MI5 attributed the Fairford incident itself. That distinction is central to [[HybridWarfareAttributionThreshold]].

## Key Characteristics
- Britain's domestic security and counterintelligence service.
- Tracks potentially lethal state-backed plots as well as conventional terrorist threats.
- Supplies threat-pattern context that must be distinguished from incident-specific attribution.

## Evidence
- Threat environment: [[foiled-plot-did-iran-plan-british-base-attack-6aba32b170b66274d96fb345]] reports the director-general's statement that MI5 tracked more than 20 potentially lethal Iran-backed plots in Britain over 12 months.
- Attribution boundary: [[foiled-plot-did-iran-plan-british-base-attack-6aba32b170b66274d96fb345]] says authorities had not confirmed who, if anyone, directed the Fairford incident.
- Target pattern: [[foiled-plot-did-iran-plan-british-base-attack-6aba32b170b66274d96fb345]] contrasts the military-base case with earlier alleged threats against Jewish communities and Iranian dissidents in Britain.

## Qualifications
The numerical threat claim is source-reported and lacks case-level detail in the supplied episode note. It should not be used to attribute any separate incident without incident-specific evidence or an official finding.
