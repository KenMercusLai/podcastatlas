# Three Mile Island Accident

The [[ThreeMileIslandAccident|Three Mile Island accident]] appears in [[04-qieernuobeili-de-wuye-woyao-jin-jianyu-le-530594843]] as a comparison case for how nuclear accidents are publicly narrated. The episode says the [[SovietUnion]] did not loudly exploit the American accident because doing so could have invited questions about Soviet nuclear safety too.

This page is source-scoped. Its role is to support [[NuclearSafetyPropaganda]] by showing that nuclear-risk communication can be constrained by what a state wants people to believe about its own systems.
