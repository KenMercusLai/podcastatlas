# Roy Black

## Overview

Roy Black was a defense lawyer in [[USVKhoury|U.S. v. Khoury]] whose closing argument used [[CatherineKhoury]]'s contemporaneous messages to challenge the prosecution's theory of a planned false injury.

## Current Profile

The episodes present Black as helping move the defense through two stages: [[BrendaSmithGeorgetown]]'s emails first weakened Georgetown's institutional narrative, then private family evidence supplied a direct alternative explanation of disputed intent. He also publicized Georgetown's excluded donor-capacity report after the acquittal and disputed the university's complaint that the document was confidential.

## Key Characteristics

- Pressed Georgetown witnesses about Salesforce and prospective-donor research.
- Entered trial believing the defense faced poor odds, according to Part 1.
- Used Smith's emails to confront denials about wealth-focused fundraising work.
- Characterized Catherine's testimony as the defense's decisive “knockout punch.”
- Read Catherine's angry messages during closing while standing near her father.
- Joined institutional context to a family-centered reasonable-doubt account.

## Qualifications

The profile is bounded to sympathetic episodes relying heavily on defense interviews. The summaries do not isolate which lawyer performed every examination, and effective advocacy plus an acquittal do not independently validate every defense characterization or moral conclusion.
