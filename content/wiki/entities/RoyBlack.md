# Roy Black

## Overview

Roy Black was a defense lawyer in [[USVKhoury|U.S. v. Khoury]] whose closing argument used [[CatherineKhoury]]'s contemporaneous messages to challenge the prosecution's theory of a planned false injury.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Black as turning institutional context and private family evidence into a coherent reasonable-doubt narrative. He also publicized Georgetown's excluded donor-capacity report after the acquittal and disputed the university's complaint that the document was confidential.

## Key Characteristics

- Pressed Georgetown witnesses about Salesforce and prospective-donor research.
- Characterized Catherine's testimony as the defense's decisive “knockout punch.”
- Read Catherine's angry messages during closing while standing near her father.
- Argued that the messages showed she was not under her father's control.
- Publicly discussed the excluded fundraising report after the verdict.
