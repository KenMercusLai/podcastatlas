## Overview

郭贵人 / Guo Guiren is the Huan-era consort in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-868-ying-le-ye-shi-shu-jia-donghan-hougong-zhengyan-ji-lmmjgx23nlkeqr3mkzliij-zwe2v|Hanji 868]] who defeats or outlasts [[EmpressDengHuanLateHan|邓皇后]] in palace rivalry but does not secure lasting favor from [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]].

## Current Profile

Guo Guiren's current profile is a single-source harem-rivalry case. The source presents her as the rival who helps push Empress Deng out of the center, but the episode immediately qualifies that apparent victory. Huan's affection later cools, and the sequence moves on to Dou Miao and Tian Sheng.

That role matters because Guo shows the source's title claim in miniature: winning a palace contest can still fail as protection if the result depends on an unstable ruler's desire. Her case sits between Deng's deposition and the later formal-status conflict around Dou Miao.

## Key Characteristics

- Huan-era guiren who competes with Empress Deng.
- Rivalry winner whose success does not produce durable affection.
- Transitional figure between the Deng branch and the Dou Miao / Tian Sheng branch.
- Source-scoped example of palace victory without stable protection.
