# Taliban

The Taliban appears in [[afghanistan-five-years-on-our-correspondent-visits-6a7ee547049c692a6b3ee0a9]] as the governing movement in [[Afghanistan]] five years after returning to power. [[TomSass]] describes it as having a tight grip on the country: visible violence has fallen, but social control, gender restrictions, poverty and international isolation shape daily life.

The source's internal politics are centered on [[HibatullahAkhundzada]] and [[TalibanHardlineRule]]. Hardliners are described as consolidating authority from Kandahar, taking control of revenue sources including mining contracts, and leaving more moderate Taliban figures too weak or frightened to challenge them.
