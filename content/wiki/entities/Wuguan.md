# 武关 / Wuguan

武关 / Wuguan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-51-chuhuaiwang-qin-xiao-er-kan-jiujiu-ruhe-mie-le-ni-ltfkdlivomnvux5xodh2rlfqf6ac]] as the pass route through which [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] tries to push against [[QinState|秦国]] after [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s six-li reversal. The first attack moves north from Danyang toward Wuguan and ends in [[DanyangBattle|丹阳之战]].

The second attack uses the same route in a more dangerous way. Qin retreats through Wuguan and [[Shangyu|商於]], drawing the [[ChuState|楚国]] army deeper toward Lantian, where the Chu force becomes isolated and supply-stretched before defeat at [[LantianBattle|蓝田之战]]. In this source, Wuguan is less a neutral place than a corridor where anger keeps pulling Chu into Qin's chosen terrain.
