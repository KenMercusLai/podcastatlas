# 陈睿 / Chen Rui

Chen Rui / 陈睿 is presented in [[no-214-xunzhao-tonglei-xiaohongshu-bilibili-yiji-wuhuabamen-de-naxie-shequ-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-26-1005977305]] as the investor, adviser, and later full-time executive who helped turn [[Bilibili]] from hobbyist community into company. The episode notes his background at Kingsoft and Cheetah-era security work, while emphasizing that he was himself a daily Bilibili user and anime viewer.

The source links Chen to Bilibili's "legalization, licensed content, commercialization" route and to the platform's no-forced-pre-roll identity. His 2016 statement that Bilibili might fail but should not "变质" becomes the episode's shorthand for [[CreatorTrustAdvertising]] and [[OnlineCommunityCommercializationFriction]].
