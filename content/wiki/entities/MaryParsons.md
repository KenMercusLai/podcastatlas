# Mary Parsons

## Overview

Mary Parsons, born Mary Lewis, is presented as a Monmouthshire migrant who sought a new life in Puritan New England and later accused another woman, her husband [[HughParsons]], and herself of witchcraft.

## Current Profile

Mary's story joins migration vulnerability, domestic labor, marriage, childbirth, child death, religious despair, community rumor, and possible mental illness. The episode holds modern postpartum-psychosis interpretation alongside a Puritan worldview in which spiritual worthlessness and unwilling service to the devil could feel real.

## Key Characteristics

- She left an abusive and deserting first husband and entered a radical Protestant community before migrating.
- She worked as a maidservant in [[WilliamPynchon]]'s family network.
- After securing an annulment, she married Hugh and built a labor-intensive household.
- Her early accusation against Mercy Marshfield led to a slander action.
- After marital deterioration and Samuel's death, she accused Hugh and herself of witchcraft.
