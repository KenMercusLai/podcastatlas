# 耳鼻喉吕博士 / Dr. Lv (ENT)

## Overview
耳鼻喉吕博士 / Dr. Lv (ENT) is the source-scoped otolaryngology guest in the VOL.208 [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode on headphone-heavy listening, ear-canal inflammation, tinnitus, hearing risk, and sudden hearing-change escalation.

## Current Profile
The episode presents 吕博士 as an ear, nose, and throat doctor from [[ShenzhenPeopleHospital|深圳市人民医院]] who also does public otolaryngology education on multiple platforms. His role is not to rank headphone products as safe or unsafe in isolation. He turns the topic into a clinical-risk map: sound dose, source distance, environmental noise, sudden peaks, sealed pressure, canal ventilation, cleaning, ear-picking habits, and personal sensitivity interact.

His practical profile in the source is clinician-educator. He separates external ear-canal problems from middle-ear disease, explains why fungal infection, eczema, repeated scratching, and long sealed wear can keep ears irritated, and treats tinnitus or sudden one-sided symptoms as signals needing context rather than casual self-diagnosis. The strongest public guidance is behavioral: lower volume, reduce continuous wear, keep earphones clean and dry, let ears ventilate, and seek qualified care when hearing changes suddenly.

## Key Characteristics
- Source-scoped otolaryngology doctor and public-education guest in the VOL.208 episode.
- Explains headphone risk through combined exposure, ear anatomy, device fit, and use behavior rather than a single product category.
- Distinguishes external ear-canal inflammation from middle-ear disease in ordinary earphone-related complaints.
- Treats earwax, ventilation, moisture, cleaning, and avoidance of repeated ear picking as protective daily-care issues.
- Frames tinnitus and sudden hearing change as clinically contextual signals that should not be ignored when sudden, unilateral, asymmetric, dizzying, or sound-distorting.
- Keeps public podcast guidance bounded as general medical literacy rather than individualized diagnosis or treatment.
