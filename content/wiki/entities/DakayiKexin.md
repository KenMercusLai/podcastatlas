# 《打开一颗心》 / Open Heart

《打开一颗心》 / Open Heart is the final book recommended in [[yi-fen-laizi-bingchuang-shang-de-xiangguan-shudan-598284093]]. The hosts describe it as a memoir or professional record by a top cardiac surgeon with roughly fifty years of experience and more than ten thousand heart operations.

The book closes the episode by balancing [[MedicalKnowledgeBoundary]] with medical courage. After the source has emphasized pandemic fear, public-health failure, expert uncertainty, and medicine's limits, this recommendation returns to the operating room: doctors must treat the heart as an organ while knowing each operation belongs to a full human life.
