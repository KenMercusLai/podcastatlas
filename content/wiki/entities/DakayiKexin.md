# 《打开一颗心》 / Open Heart

《打开一颗心》 / Open Heart is the cardiac-surgeon memoir at the center of [[86-dakai-yike-xin-na-meihao-de-zhang-wo-yijing-daguo-le-702323437]]. The episode presents it through [[StephenWestaby|Stephen Westaby / 史蒂芬·维斯塔比]]'s working-class childhood, surgical training, failed and successful heart operations, artificial-heart work, and anger at institutions that block difficult care.

The book was first recommended in [[yi-fen-laizi-bingchuang-shang-de-xiangguan-shudan-598284093]], where the hosts described it as a memoir or professional record by a top cardiac surgeon with roughly fifty years of experience and more than ten thousand heart operations.

The book closes the episode by balancing [[MedicalKnowledgeBoundary]] with medical courage. After the source has emphasized pandemic fear, public-health failure, expert uncertainty, and medicine's limits, this recommendation returns to the operating room: doctors must treat the heart as an organ while knowing each operation belongs to a full human life.

Episode 86 makes that balance concrete. Westaby's cases add [[HighRiskSurgicalInnovation]], [[ArtificialHeartBridgeTherapy]], the [[AB180BloodPump|AB180 blood pump]], and [[SurgicalOutcomeMetricDistortion]] to the wiki's medicine branch: surgery can save lives exactly where evidence, permission, money, ICU capacity, and mortality statistics are hardest to reconcile.
