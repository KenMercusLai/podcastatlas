# 赵烈侯 / Marquis Lie of Zhao

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-04-yurang-wei-zhiyao-fuchou-ln34tydha6iqwlrgmw7iugqfgfik]] adds Zhao Liehou's earlier lineage position. The episode identifies him as Zhao Ji, son of [[ZhaoXianzi|赵献子]], and one of the three Jin leaders whom [[ZhouWeilieWang|周威烈王]] formally recognizes as vassals through [[ThreeJinVassalRecognition|三晋受封]].

赵烈侯 / Marquis Lie of Zhao appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-1-lvkp-ju7pf5z5cz3thojv3s2fkyx]] as the [[ZhaoState|赵国]] ruler whose death is followed by the installation of his younger brother [[ZhaoWuhou|赵武侯]]. The episode places the notice alongside the Han and Qin successions to show a wider first-generation transfer of power.
