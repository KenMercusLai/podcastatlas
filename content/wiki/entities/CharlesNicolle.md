# Charles Nicolle / 尼科勒

Charles Nicolle / 尼科勒 appears in [[16-shishang-shouhaizhe-jing-luse-huo-wei-zui-du-yanse-559889282]] as the French bacteriologist used to explain why clothing can become infectious infrastructure. The episode says Nicolle found that lice could be blocked by clean clothing and soap, making garment hygiene part of disease prevention rather than only domestic tidiness.

His role in the source connects clothing history to public health. The episode moves from secondhand garments and contaminated textiles into soldiers' lice control in World War I, showing how efforts to make clothing safer could also introduce chemical exposure.

## Key Claims
- The source uses Nicolle to connect clothing, lice, soap, and disease control.
- The example makes fashion risk broader than poison: pathogens and parasites can also travel through textiles.
- War makes the tradeoff harsher because delousing can push people toward dangerous chemical treatments.
