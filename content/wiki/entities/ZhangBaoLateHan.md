# 张苞 / Zhang Bao (late Han)

张苞 / Zhang Bao (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-985-fanmu-chengchou-liguo-zhiluan-de-xijie-tanjiu-lt-t2un0zcudkige9ucl1txa2hhk|Hanji 985]] as a zhonglangjiang inside [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]]'s party who secretly works with [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]]. During Guo Si's night attack on Li Jue's camp, Zhang Bao and others set fires from inside the camp; after the attack fails, he takes his troops over to Guo Si.

The page is source-scoped. Zhang Bao matters because the Li-Guo feud is not only an external camp battle: factional trust inside Li Jue's own force is already breaking, so Guo Si can combine outside assault with internal sabotage.
