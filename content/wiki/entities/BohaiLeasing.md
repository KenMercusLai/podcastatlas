# Bohai Leasing / 渤海租赁

## Overview

Bohai Leasing is the Chinese leasing company presented as acquiring [[Avolon]] in 2016 and shifting its business emphasis from container leasing toward aircraft leasing.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Bohai Leasing mainly as Avolon's ownership context. Through the acquisition, the parent gained a large Ireland-based aircraft-leasing platform and increased its exposure to a market where fleet scale, funding access, manufacturer orders, and global customer relationships reinforce one another.

## Key Characteristics

- Parent company of [[Avolon]] in the episode's account.
- Acquirer that entered the Irish aircraft-leasing cluster through a 2016 transaction.
- Leasing group presented as reducing emphasis on containers.
- Owner increasingly focused on aircraft leasing and its scale economics.
