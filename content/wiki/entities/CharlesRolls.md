# Charles Rolls

Charles Rolls appears in [[vol-268-liang-ge-lao-si-lai-si-1003563933]] as the sales, elite-network, and aviation side of the early Rolls-Royce partnership. The episode contrasts him with [[HenryRoyce]]: Rolls supplied upper-class customer access and enthusiasm for flight, while Royce supplied engineering capability.

The source gives Rolls a stronger role in the aircraft-engine origin story than a simple car-founder account would. His early flying activity and death in a 1910 aviation accident make him the person whose interests foreshadowed the aero-engine branch that later became strategically more important than the car business.
