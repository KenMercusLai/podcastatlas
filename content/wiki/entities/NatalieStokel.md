## Overview
Natalie Stokel appears in [[the-continent-nobody-owns-everyone-benefits-from-summer-school]] as the economist who gives the episode its Antarctic valuation frame.

## Current Profile
Stokel's role is to make [[Antarctica]] economically legible without reducing it to ordinary commerce. She argues that economics can study global resources even where markets are sparse, then uses tourism, fisheries, and especially climate services to estimate an annual Antarctic value of about $180 billion.

## Key Characteristics
- She is identified in the source as a professor of economics at the University of Tasmania.
- Her strongest contribution is [[NonMarketEnvironmentalValuation]] for Antarctica and the [[SouthernOcean]].
- She is explicit about discomfort with pricing nature but treats valuation as a defensive tool for decision-making.
- Her governance account emphasizes trust, collaboration, respect, and sanctions rather than ordinary ownership.
