# Cordeliers Club

## Overview

The Cordeliers Club was a radical revolutionary association that helped turn the post-Varennes collapse of royal trust into a mass republican petition campaign.

## Current Profile

The source associates the Cordeliers with [[GeorgesDanton|Georges Danton]], [[JeanPaulMarat|Jean-Paul Marat]], and Camille Desmoulins. After [[LouisXVI|Louis XVI]] fled and the Assembly chose to retain him, the club pressed beyond personal condemnation of the king toward abolition of monarchy. Its proposed gathering at the Champ de Mars became the setting for the [[ChampDeMarsMassacre|Champ de Mars massacre]].

## Key Characteristics

- Radical political club rooted in petition, press, and street mobilization.
- Early institutional center for post-Varennes republican demands.
- Associated with Danton, Marat, and Desmoulins.
- Opponent of the Assembly's decision to preserve Louis under a constitution.
- Organizer within the coalition behind the Champ de Mars petition.

## Evidence

### Republican petition politics

- [[503-the-french-revolution-bloodbath-in-paris-part-1-glt8447202194]] links the Cordeliers to the emergence of republicanism, radical leaders, and the petition demanding an end to monarchy.

### Repression and polarization

- [[503-the-french-revolution-bloodbath-in-paris-part-1-glt8447202194]] places the club's mobilization in the sequence leading to the massacre and subsequent arrests.

## Qualifications

- The source does not reconstruct the club's full membership, internal debates, or responsibility for every act in the crowd.
- Association with prominent radicals does not make the club a unified command structure for all street action.
- The gathering contained non-political visitors as well as committed petitioners.

## What Changed

- Establishes the Cordeliers as the main organizational bridge from Varennes to mass republican petitioning.

## Relationships

- [[GeorgesDanton]] - prominent radical associated with the club.
- [[JeanPaulMarat]] - radical journalist associated with its political milieu.
- [[ChampDeMarsMassacre]] - violent repression of the petition gathering it helped promote.
- [[JacobinClub]] - neighboring revolutionary club with a distinct post-split trajectory.
- [[FeuillantClub]] - constitutional-monarchist opponent formed during the same crisis.
