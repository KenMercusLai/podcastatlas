## Overview

Shervin Pishevar is presented in the source as an Iranian-American technology investor, supporter of [[RezaPahlavi|Reza Pahlavi]], and participant in the [[IranProsperityProject|Iran Prosperity Project]]. He helped arrange Pahlavi's [[AllIn|All-In]] interview and then supplied the episode's diaspora, security, historical, and economic case for political transition in [[Iran]].

## Current Profile

Pishevar's advocacy combines personal and strategic registers. He recounts his family's flight from post-revolutionary Iran, arrival in the [[UnitedStates]] with little money, and his father's taxi work, then connects that immigrant history to his own technology career and desire to help rebuild Iran.

He endorses Pahlavi as a transitional rather than permanent leader and describes the Iran Prosperity Project as a sequenced program for stabilization, referendum, constitutional assembly, constitutional ratification, and rebuilding. He also argues that a democratic Iran could improve regional security, deepen ties with [[Israel]], and unlock the productive capacity visible in the Iranian diaspora.

The source is advocacy, not independent verification. Pishevar's casualty figure, account of military outreach, estimates of popular support, nuclear-intent claim, historical interpretations, and economic forecasts remain attributed to him.

## Key Characteristics

- Iranian-American entrepreneur and investor using a family migration story to explain his transition advocacy.
- Public supporter of Reza Pahlavi as a unifying transitional figure.
- Participant in economic and institutional planning through the Iran Prosperity Project.
- Advocate for Israeli-Iranian cooperation and a security case for regime change.
- Promoter of a large diaspora-led economic dividend from a democratic Iran.

## Qualifications

The wiki currently has one source for this profile, and it is a sympathetic interview in which Pishevar is an organizer and advocate. The episode does not independently establish his role in the Iran Prosperity Project, the membership or reach of the Israeli-Iranian alliance, the reported casualty and military-response figures, the share of Iranians favoring secular government or Pahlavi, or the scale and timing of the economic dividend. His family history is recorded as his own account.
