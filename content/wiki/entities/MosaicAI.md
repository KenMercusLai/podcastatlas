# Mosaic AI

## Overview

Mosaic AI is an enterprise software company led by [[AlanTalmor]] that specializes in automating technical support for B2B software organizations. It evolved from the broader [[AskAIMosaic|Ask AI]] enterprise assistant.

## Current Profile

Mosaic combines company knowledge, product information, code, logs, customer context, historical tickets, and workflow automation to assist or automate technical-support work. Its commercial position is outcome-led: diagnose automatable cases and knowledge gaps, estimate savings, and compete primarily with customers' internal AI-building efforts rather than only with an established software category.

## Key Characteristics

- Focuses on complex technical support rather than generic transactional customer service.
- Uses historical-ticket analysis to identify automation opportunities and create an initial business case.
- Combines knowledge, logs, code, customer context, and human approval in support workflows.
- Prices and sells against operating-cost reduction and representative productivity rather than feature count alone.
- Targets larger B2B software organizations with meaningful support costs and complex product portfolios.

Company scale:
- [[chatgpt-should-have-killed-his-ai-saas-instead-hes-nearing-10m]] reports roughly 100 customers, 70-100 employees, and seven-figure revenue approaching eight figures.

## Qualifications

The company scale, customer outcomes, cost ranges, savings targets, and productivity gains are founder-reported and not independently verified. A short diagnostic pilot does not establish complete integration, adoption, security, change management, or long-run savings. The source does not provide customer retention, margin, contract composition, or controlled outcome data.
