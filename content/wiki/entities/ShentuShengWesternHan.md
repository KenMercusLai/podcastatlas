## Overview

申屠圣 / Shentu Sheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-523-gudai-nongmin-qiyi-zong-shibai-meilixiang-or-jianshiduan-ltwfeupz2zzzvo76fhm7-6txjiup|Hanji 523]] as the named leader of the Yangshuo 3 / 22 BCE [[YingchuanIronOfficeUprising|颍川铁官徒起义]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Shentu Sheng less as a developed biography than as the catalytic leader of an iron-office convict revolt. He organizes about 180 iron-office laborers, claims general status, seizes weapons, kills local officials, and moves the force out into nearby areas.

His significance in the episode is double. At the event level, he gives the uprising a leader and a starting act. At the interpretive level, his rapid but failed mobilization lets the host argue that suffering and local support can ignite revolt without supplying the discipline, legitimacy, and governing capacity needed for durable success.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han iron-office convict uprising leader.
- Active in Yangshuo 3 / 22 BCE under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]].
- Leads about 180 iron-office convicts at the start of the revolt.
- Claims the title of general after seizing weapons.
- Functions in the source as a concrete case for rebel grievance without durable organization.
