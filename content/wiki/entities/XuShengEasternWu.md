## Overview

[[XuShengEasternWu|徐盛]] is an Eastern Wu officer in Hanji 1157, distinct from the 174 CE rebel [[XuShengLateHan|许生]]. He appears both as a commander initially resistant to [[ZhouTaiEasternWu|周泰]]'s authority and as the subject of [[JiangQinEasternWu|蒋钦]]'s grievance-crossing recommendation.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Xu Sheng as proud, forceful, and already known for service, which makes subordination to Zhou Tai difficult until [[SunQuan|孙权]] publicly explains Zhou Tai's sacrifice. An earlier county-office conflict also places Xu Sheng against one of Jiang Qin's subordinates, yet Jiang Qin later judges him suitable for larger command.

Together the scenes distinguish a difficult or proud officer from an unusable one: public evidence can make hierarchy acceptable, while a former adversary can still recognize command potential.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Wu officer serving in the Ruxu command structure.
- Presented as initially resistant to Zhou Tai's appointment.
- Accepts the appointment after Sun Quan makes Zhou Tai's service publicly legible.
- Previously exercises severe county-level authority over Jiang Qin's subordinate.
- Receives Jiang Qin's later endorsement for loyalty, diligence, courage, and command capacity.
