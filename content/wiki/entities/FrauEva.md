# Frau Eva / 夏娃夫人

[[FrauEva|Frau Eva / 夏娃夫人]] is [[MaxDemian|德米安]]'s mother in [[DemianNovel|《德米安》]] and one of the strongest symbolic figures in [[20-demian-gaiyin-de-etou-shang-na-shenmi-de-yinji-567302734]]. The episode presents her as mother, beloved, goddess-like guide, and inner image at once. [[EmilSinclair|辛克莱尔]] discovers that the bird painting he sent to Demian hangs above her, making her arrival feel like a convergence of earlier symbols.

The source connects Frau Eva to [[CarlJung|Jungian]] psychology because she resembles an anima or collective inner guide more than a simple realist character. Her final kiss through Demian at the war ending reinforces the source's point that outside guides must eventually become inner responsibility.

## Key Claims
- Frau Eva gathers mother, beloved, and divine-guide meanings into one figure.
- Her name matters because "Eva" makes origin, desire, and forbidden knowledge visible without turning her into a single allegory.
- In the source's psychological reading, she is both a person in the story and a condensation of Sinclair's inward search.
