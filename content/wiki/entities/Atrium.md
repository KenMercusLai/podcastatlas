# Atrium

Atrium appears in [[tsr-s5-justinkan-v2-audio-tsr-s5-justinkan-v2-audio]] as [[JustinKan]]'s later legal-services startup after the [[Twitch]] acquisition. Justin says he started it partly because he compared himself with other successful founders and wanted to build a bigger company.

The source uses Atrium as a founder-psychology and service-business lesson rather than a detailed legal-tech case. It repeats the question Justin had already encountered with [[Exec]]: whether a human, expert-heavy service can be systematized enough to scale like software. It also makes Atrium part of [[FounderMotivationEvolution]], because Justin later connected the company to external approval rather than durable intrinsic motivation.
