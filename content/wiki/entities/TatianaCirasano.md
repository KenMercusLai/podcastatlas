# Tatiana Cirasano

Tatiana Cirasano is the [[MIDiAResearch]] analyst interviewed in [[tech-20260817-tech-pod-128-tech-20260817-tech-pod-128]] about [[Suno]], [[SunoSpark]], and AI music's uneasy relationship with human artists. She frames Suno Spark as both artist support and a sign that Suno is moving further into the music value chain.

Cirasano's contribution is to separate the technology promise from the music-business bottleneck. She says [[GenerativeAIMusic]] can lower the barrier to making music, but artists still need to be heard; the same episode uses her comments to connect AI music to [[ArtistDiscoveryFragmentation]], [[CreativeLaborAIBacklash]], and [[MusicIndustryAIResponse]].
