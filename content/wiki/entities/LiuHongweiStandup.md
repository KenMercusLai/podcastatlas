## Overview
刘宏伟 / Liu Hongwei appears as a panel participant in a [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] and [[SixiaoComedy|四孝喜剧]] live episode about relationship thought experiments, AI partners, pain-sharing, memory deletion, and perfect compatibility.

## Current Profile
Within the current evidence inventory, Liu Hongwei functions as a married-life and comic-pragmatist voice. He says his present marital state already resembles the imagined loyalty drug because he is interested only in his wife, while also joking that uncertainty and challenge can keep a relationship alive.

His later answers keep that mixture of warmth, probability thinking, and comic self-description. He is relatively open to sharing pain because he describes himself as low in negative emotion and able to generate happiness, chooses to delete infidelity memories if the relationship will continue, and rejects the 100% match because he has not experienced it and doubts that frictionless perfection is necessarily better than an imperfect relationship with low points and repair.

## Key Characteristics
- Married panel voice used to test technology against existing relationship experience.
- Treats loyalty as partly life-stage and habit rather than only formal rule.
- More open than other panelists to pain-sharing and memory deletion when the mechanism seems to reduce suffering.
- Uses probability and uncertainty to resist assuming that a perfect match is automatically better.
