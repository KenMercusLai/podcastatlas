# KK / AI Director

## Overview
KK is an AI filmmaker and former advertising practitioner interviewed in [[ai-wuxian-rensheng-youxian-duitan-kk-shanyin-yixian-ai-chuangzuozhe-lmk4luckjgn-cpivd-7dy9vmsl0u|AI 无限，人生有限]]. The source presents [[MengusiFilm|《蒙古斯》]] as his representative case.

## Current Profile
KK's account joins roughly a decade of 4A advertising experience to a story-first AI-film practice. He fixes the emotional and structural core before using AI to discuss, expand, visualize, and execute it, while treating real-world observation as the scarce input that generation cannot replace.

His phrase “在现场” summarizes the profile: tools can increase output and lower the cost of unrealizable images, but the creator still needs to have seen, heard, and felt something worth translating into film language. His advertising background also makes him a case of [[CreativeReinventionThroughNewTools]] because AI provides an outlet for ideas previously lost to budget and client compromise.

## Key Characteristics
- Story-first creator who establishes narrative structure and emotional purpose before extensive AI execution.
- Former 4A advertising practitioner whose accumulated visual and commercial craft remains useful in AI filmmaking.
- Treats lived experience and being “on the scene” as non-substitutable creative input.
- Uses AI as a script-discussion, visualization, and production partner rather than the authorial center.
- Accepts concentrated responsibility for quality when a small AI workflow removes traditional departments and excuses.
