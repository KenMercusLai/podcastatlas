# Jean-Claude Biver

Jean-Claude Biver appears in [[rolex-1]] as the watch entrepreneur who bought [[Blancpain]] and repositioned it as a mechanical-only luxury brand after the quartz crisis. The source uses him to show that mechanical watches could be reframed around rarity, artistry, and history rather than raw timekeeping performance.

His role in the wiki is comparative. Biver's Blancpain work makes [[MechanicalWatchRepositioning]] explicit, while [[Rolex]] represents a more continuous and industrially scaled version of the same category shift.
