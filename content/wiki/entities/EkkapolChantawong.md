## Overview
Ekkapol “Ek” Chantawong is the Thai youth soccer coach presented as sustaining twelve trapped players during the 2018 Tham Luang cave crisis.

## Current Profile
The episode portrays Chantawong as converting an uncontrollable emergency into a structured group effort. He organized digging shifts and daily routines, located water, redistributed support toward weaker boys, used meditation, and maintained an image of eventual return. His monastery experience supplied both practical fasting knowledge and practiced calm, while his apology to parents kept responsibility visible.

## Key Characteristics
- Creates routine and shared work under confinement.
- Uses hopeful language and vivid future imagery to sustain direction.
- Protects weaker group members through explicit mutual support.
- Applies meditation and prior fasting experience to acute survival.
- Accepts responsibility while reassuring families.
