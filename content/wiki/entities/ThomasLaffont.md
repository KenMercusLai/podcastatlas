# Thomas Laffont

## Overview
Thomas Laffont is an investor associated with [[Coatue]] who appears in the All-In source as the presenter of a data-driven late-stage venture and AI public-market framework.

## Current Profile
In this source, Laffont's profile is not biographical; it is analytical. He uses private-market cohort data, funding concentration, IPO expectations, frontier AI revenue forecasts, and mega-cap probability comparisons to argue that the [[UnicornEconomy|unicorn economy]] has recovered in a narrower and more AI-centered form. His strongest contribution is the [[TenXParadox|10X paradox]]: the claim that very large technology companies may now have better odds of another 10x outcome than smaller unicorns.

## Key Characteristics
- Laffont is presented as a Coatue investor using market data, cohort analysis, and valuation models rather than a product-operator narrative.
- He treats AI as the dominant force in current venture fundraising and private-market value concentration.
- He frames 2026 IPOs as a possible ecosystem-reset event for venture liquidity and public-market discipline.
- He argues that OpenAI and Anthropic are scaling faster than earlier cloud and software leaders, while keeping public-market scrutiny as the decisive test.
- He uses SpaceX as a platform-style infrastructure valuation case rather than only a launch-provider case.
