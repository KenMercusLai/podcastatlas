## Overview

建成侯敖 / Ao, Marquis of Jiancheng is the insider whom Hanji 356-2 credits with joining [[JuguDongyueKing|繇王居股]] to kill [[YuShanDongyueKing|余善]] and surrender followers to Han.

## Current Profile

Ao appears only at the decisive end of the [[DongyueWesternHan|东越]] campaign. He and Jugu convert external Han pressure into regime collapse by killing Yu Shan from within and then surrendering. The episode says Han Wudi subsequently rewards Ao, making his case one of violent elite defection followed by incorporation rather than an ordinary battlefield victory.

## Key Characteristics

- Dongyue insider identified by the title Marquis of Jiancheng.
- Co-conspirator in the killing of Yu Shan.
- Surrender leader incorporated and rewarded by Han.
