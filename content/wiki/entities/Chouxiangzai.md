# 抽象仔 / Chouxiangzai

抽象仔 / Chouxiangzai is a former CCTV documentary director and [[ChinaCommunicationUniversity|中国传媒大学]] graduate interviewed in [[cong-yangshi-jilupian-dao-baokuan-ai-duanju-di-yi-pi-zhuanshen-de-daoyan-s10e11-3c05e3d5-d8f6-44c1-97ca-698261d7b2bc]]. The source says he previously worked on projects including 《航拍中国》 and 《楚国八百年》, then moved from traditional documentary, advertising, and promotional-film work into [[AIShortDrama]] and AI-video creator accounts after the pandemic changed traditional film demand.

His role in the wiki is to anchor the director-side interpretation of AI short drama. He describes early AI-video production as slow, draw-heavy, and difficult to explain to clients, then argues that better [[VideoModels]] such as [[Seedance]]/C-DANCE 2.0 shift the bottleneck toward scripts, IP, storyboards, asset libraries, directing language, editing, and audience psychology.

The source also uses him as the overseas case operator. As total director on [[WuGeGeGeDouChongWo]], he localized domestic short-drama logic for [[RodeoAI]] by changing status symbols, settings, and fantasy scale. He later uses [[YiWanFuWengHuiGui]], [[XinYuanYangHuDieMeng]], and [[XiSongFengyun]] to explain why story, model choice, and performance judgment matter more than raw generation novelty.

## Key Points
- His early viral AI-account method mixed global pop-culture events with Chinese internet contrast, familiar melodies, and humorous cultural translation.
- He treats [[AIDirectorCoreWorkflow]] as a creative-management problem: the person must know what scene, emotion, movement, and performance to ask from the model.
- He argues that [[AIShortDrama]] audiences can tolerate visible flaws when story and audience resonance are strong.
- He distinguishes AI-suitable short-drama performance from film-grade restrained emotion, where strong human actors can still add unexpected value.
