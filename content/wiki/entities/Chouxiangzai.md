# 抽象仔 / 抽象宅 / Chouxiangzai

## Overview
抽象仔 / 抽象宅 / Chouxiangzai is a former CCTV documentary director and AI-video creator whose wiki role is to connect traditional directing, internet-native expression, [[AIShortDrama]], and commercial AI-content services.

## Current Profile
The current source set presents him as a trained director who did not enter AI video as a tool-only novice. The What's Next source emphasizes his move from documentary, advertising, and promotional-film work into [[AIShortDrama]] and overseas platform projects. The 三五环 source adds the personal and creator-account layer: after a 2024 gap period, he used AI-video tools to turn older skills in shot language, storytelling, production coordination, client communication, meme literacy, and audience judgment into viral content and then into B-side services.

Across both sources, his position is that AI reduces execution barriers but does not replace the creator's expressive problem. AI-video success depends on [[AIDirectorCoreWorkflow]], story fit, cultural contrast, platform timing, and the ability to judge generated performances. Commercially, he treats C-side viral accounts as a visibility and sample-work surface that can feed B-side services without fully collapsing into client-service exhaustion.

## Key Characteristics
- He transfers documentary, advertising, and internet-content experience into AI video rather than treating AI generation as a standalone skill.
- He treats [[AIDirectorCoreWorkflow]] as a creative-management problem: the creator must know what scene, emotion, movement, performance, and shot to ask from the model.
- His viral AI-account method mixes global pop culture, Chinese internet contrast, familiar melodies, meme fluency, and sincere fan or creator emotion.
- He treats [[AIShortDrama]] and overseas short-drama work as story, audience-psychology, platform, paid-traffic, and ROI problems as much as generation problems.
- He uses C-side attention to build brand and sample credibility, then translates that credibility into B-side AI-content work for brands and platforms.
- He distinguishes useful AI amplification from replacement: without taste, expression desire, traffic understanding, and judgment, the tool does not produce meaningful content by itself.
