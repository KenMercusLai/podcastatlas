# Monsanto

## Overview
Monsanto appears in this episode through [[Roundup]] litigation now borne by [[Bayer]].

## Current Profile
The source treats Monsanto primarily as the legacy company behind Roundup, not as a current independent operating story. Its wiki relevance here is legal: the Supreme Court argument asks whether a federally approved pesticide label can block state-law failure-to-warn claims.

## Key Characteristics
- Legacy agricultural company connected to Roundup.
- Appears through product-liability litigation rather than product development.
- Linked to Bayer as owner and litigation-risk holder.
- Serves as the company name attached to a federal preemption dispute over pesticide warnings.
