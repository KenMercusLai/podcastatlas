# 朱熹 / Zhu Xi

朱熹 / Zhu Xi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-26-mengzi-laile-ln4twwp2frmr-xpumu0rqg-2gpyn]] as the later scholar cited for the coherence of [[MenciusText|《孟子》]]. The episode says Zhu Xi believed that even if some passages involved disciple writing, the book's final shape had been reviewed by [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] himself.

The page keeps that claim source-scoped. Zhu Xi's role here is textual reception: he helps explain why the episode treats Mencius as a coherent authorial voice when discussing the opening with [[LiangHuiWang|梁惠王]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-67-zhanguo-mieba-baiqi-de-junshi-caineng-jiujing-ruhe-ljw0lltowxd4qz75v-wcnlkwqskv]] adds Zhu Xi by omission through [[ZizhiTongjianGangmu|《资治通鉴纲目》]]. The host notes that Zhu Xi's outline tradition does not preserve the [[MangMao|芒卯]] notice, reading the omission as a judgment that "诈说" stories were not useful enough for the work's governing sequence.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-109-2-chunshenjun-bei-huangdi-dajiuge-quanzu-miemen-locma2plu7v-vsssm5dr5frc3szw]] adds Zhu Xi's outline tradition through phrasing. The episode says [[ZizhiTongjianGangmu|《资治通鉴纲目》]] records [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]]'s death as "盗杀黄歇," using both the thief label and the personal-name choice as visible moral judgment. This extends Zhu Xi's page from Mencius reception and omission to evaluative chronicle language.
