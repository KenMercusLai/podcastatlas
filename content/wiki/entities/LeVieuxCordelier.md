# Le Vieux Cordelier

## Overview

*Le Vieux Cordelier* was [[CamilleDesmoulins|Camille Desmoulins's]] newspaper, launched with approval for attacks on [[JacquesHebert|Jacques Hébert]] before becoming a prominent public argument against the Terror.

## Current Profile

The publication moved across the government's factional boundary. Its early attack on Hébert served [[MaximilienRobespierre|Robespierre's]] struggle against the radical left, but later issues demanded clemency for prisoners and criticized terror itself. Public readings and strong sales made the paper politically consequential; Robespierre tried to preserve Desmoulins while suppressing the text, and printers refused an even more critical final issue.

## Key Characteristics

- It began as an anti-Hébertist intervention tolerated by Robespierre.
- It became the leading journalistic voice of the [[Indulgents]].
- It advocated a Committee of Clemency and relief for undeserving prisoners.
- Its arguments circulated through sales and public readings in cafés.
- Its suppression marked the point at which bounded criticism became treated as factional threat.

## Qualifications

- The source summarizes the paper's political trajectory rather than providing a complete issue-by-issue textual history.
- Popularity in cafés does not establish representative national opinion.
- Exact circulation, wording, authorship influence, and audience reception remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- A canonical profile now records how an authorized factional newspaper became a clemency campaign and then a suppressed threat.

## Relationships

- [[CamilleDesmoulins]] - editor whose political fate became inseparable from the paper.
- [[Indulgents]] - moderate faction for which it supplied the strongest public voice.
- [[JacquesHebert]] - initial radical target attacked with Robespierre's approval.
- [[MaximilienRobespierre]] - former ally who tried to separate Desmoulins from the condemned publication.
- [[RevolutionaryFactionalOutflanking]] - dynamic by which yesterday's approved attack became tomorrow's punishable moderation.
