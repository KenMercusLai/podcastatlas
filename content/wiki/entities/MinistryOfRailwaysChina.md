# Ministry of Railways (China) / 中国铁道部

The Ministry of Railways is the central institution in [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]]. The episode presents it as buyer, planner, operator, technical coordinator, and internal hierarchy for [[ChineseHighSpeedRail]] before the 2013 split that moved administrative oversight to a regulator and enterprise operation toward [[ChinaRailwayCorporation]].

Its importance comes from concentration. Because the ministry controlled procurement and access to railway demand, it could turn orders into leverage over [[KawasakiHeavyIndustries]], [[Alstom]], [[Bombardier]], and [[Siemens]], while also coordinating domestic manufacturers that later fed into [[CRRC]]. That same concentration is the institutional background for [[StrongmanInfrastructureGovernance]], corruption risk, and the safety-management failures exposed by the [[WenzhouTrainCollision]].
