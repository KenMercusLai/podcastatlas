# Nathan Most

Nathan Most enters the wiki through [[StateStreet]] and [[vanguard-1]] as the ETF pioneer who offered [[Vanguard]] a chance to launch an exchange-traded index product before [[JohnBogle]] rejected the idea. The existing wiki context says Most later worked with State Street on the SPDR [[SP500|S&P 500]] ETF, making him central to the first major U.S. ETF wave.

[[vol-266-yi-ci-xing-gao-dong-etf-1002344828]] retells the same invention story but names him "Nelson Most" in the source summary. This page keeps the established wiki name Nathan Most while recording the naming discrepancy as source-scoped.

## Key Claims
- Most's core idea was to make fund shares trade on an exchange while preserving fund-like exposure to a basket of securities.
- His ETF concept separated [[PassiveInvesting|passive index exposure]] from the old mutual-fund purchase and redemption experience.
- Bogle's rejection shows that ETF convenience was controversial even among index-fund advocates because intraday trading could encourage speculation.
