# Alok Kanojia

## Overview
Alok Kanojia, also called Dr. K in the source, is the psychiatrist and contemplative-practice educator interviewed by [[AndrewHuberman]] about unlearning behavior patterns, distress tolerance, ego, digital life, and inner work.

## Current Profile
Kanojia presents psychotherapy and Eastern contemplative models as complementary maps. His central distinction is between forcing behavior through willpower and changing the tendencies, identities, and conditioned wants from which behavior emerges. He treats emotions as information rather than commands, combines acceptance with deliberate response choice, and uses ego, samskara, shunya, yoga nidra, and sankalpa to describe progressively deeper observation and revision of mental patterns.

The same framework shapes his discussion of digital life. Social media can amplify emotionally activating examples and comparison standards; pornography can become both stimulation and pain relief; and agreeable AI can weaken reality testing. His clinical and autobiographical examples are educational rather than individualized treatment evidence, and his bridges between contemplative language and neuroscience remain hypotheses where the supplied source does not provide detailed studies.

## Key Characteristics
- Integrates psychiatry, psychotherapy, meditation, and yoga-derived models of mind.
- Emphasizes changing underlying tendencies and self-concepts rather than relying only on willpower.
- Treats emotional awareness, distress tolerance, and uncertainty tolerance as response capacities rather than suppression.
- Uses his own gifted-child, gaming-addiction, college-failure, and delayed-independence history to discuss nonlinear development.
- Applies mental-health analysis to social media, dating, AI, pornography, appearance, and men's social outcomes.
- Preserves a stated boundary between personally meaningful mystical experience and scientific certainty.
