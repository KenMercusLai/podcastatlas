# Keith Zhai

Keith Zhai is a guest in [[e246-hewei-zhengliu-liaoliao-guigu-ruhe-kan-zhongguo-kaifang-moxing-bijin-qianyan-5fd236d7-9a72-4b15-9e84-e83ceadd1b41]], where he frames [[KimiK3|Kimi K3]] and Chinese open-weight models from the Silicon Valley adoption, policy, and business side. He emphasizes that enterprises move from "use the best AI" toward cost, deployment, and [[ModelSovereignty]] once AI usage scales.

His contribution to the wiki is the practical buyer view: some sectors or governments may avoid Chinese models, but many companies will still evaluate models by cost, performance, local control, and whether they can operate or tune them. He also treats [[ModelDistillation]] as a normal technique across companies such as [[OpenAI]], [[Google]], and [[XAI|xAI]], while keeping unauthorized large-scale data collection as a separate compliance question.
