# Louis XIV

## Overview

Louis XIV is the French king whose absolutist state, prison system, and culture of royal secrecy form the political setting of the [[ManInTheIronMask]] case.

## Current Profile

[[411-the-man-in-the-iron-mask-glt3787137343]] places the case within a monarchy strengthened after the Fronde and made visibly magnificent at Versailles while retaining coercive instruments such as lettres de cachet. Louis and his war minister Louvois could keep a prisoner confined without a public charge or defined term. The episode does not prove why [[EustacheDege]] was targeted, but it treats the short chain between king, minister, [[SaintMars]], and prisoner as the institutional condition that made long-term disappearance possible.

## Key Characteristics

- Monarch associated with the consolidation of French royal absolutism.
- Shaped by the Fronde's challenge to royal authority.
- Used Versailles as a visible expression and social instrument of monarchy.
- Exercised authority through administrative secrecy as well as ceremonial splendour.

## Evidence

### Royal authority and detention

- [[411-the-man-in-the-iron-mask-glt3787137343]] connects Louis's letters, Louvois, lettres de cachet, and Saint-Mars to Degé's isolation.

### Political setting

- [[411-the-man-in-the-iron-mask-glt3787137343]] places the case after the Fronde and alongside Versailles, Fouquet's arrest, and centralized royal power.

## Qualifications

- The episode's absolutism frame is contextual rather than a complete account of Louis XIV's government.
- It does not establish that Louis personally originated every security detail or that Degé's unknown secret concerned the Treaty of Dover.
- Later royal-twin and royal-father stories are literary or speculative claims, not evidence about Louis's family.

## What Changed

- Adds the masked-prisoner case as an example of secrecy and indeterminate detention beneath Versailles's visible monarchy.

## Relationships

- [[EustacheDege]] - prisoner isolated under royal authority.
- [[SaintMars]] - trusted jailer within the royal command chain.
- [[ManInTheIronMask]] - legend later attached to Louis's family and legitimacy.
- [[SecrecyDrivenHistoricalMyth]] - process through which royal concealment acquired an enlarged afterlife.
