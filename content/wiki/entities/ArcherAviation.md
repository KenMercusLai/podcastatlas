# Archer Aviation

Archer Aviation appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-the-future-of-everything-what-ceos-of-circle-crowdstrike-more-see-coming-in-2026-39870920]] through CEO [[AdamGoldsteinArcher|Adam Goldstein]]'s discussion of eVTOL commercialization. The source says certification is the hardest part of bringing electric air taxis to market, with early U.S. city launches, public-comfort flights, safety redundancy, and eventual autonomy all depending on regulators and air-traffic infrastructure.

The episode also moves Archer into defense technology. Archer has a partnership with [[Anduril]] on [[ProjectNix|Project Nix]], described as an autonomous collaborative attack helicopter drone intended to deliver attack-helicopter-like capability at lower cost without risking pilots. That makes Archer a bridge between [[EVTOLCertificationRamp|eVTOL certification ramp]] and [[DefenseAIProcurement]].
