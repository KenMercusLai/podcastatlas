# ClassMetric

ClassMetric is the first startup idea [[PeterReinhardt]] describes in [[peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted-peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted]]. The product gave students a button to anonymously indicate confusion during lectures while professors saw a graph of confusion over time.

The episode uses ClassMetric as a [[FounderIdeaPivot]] case. [[YCombinator]] accepted the team despite skepticism from [[PaulGraham]] and [[RobertMorris]], but real classroom use showed that laptops often created distraction rather than useful feedback. The failed classroom product left behind an analytics routing need that later produced [[AnalyticsJS|analytics.js]] and [[Segment]].
