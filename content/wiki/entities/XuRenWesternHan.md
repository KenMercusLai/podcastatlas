## Overview

徐仁 / Xu Ren enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-402-1-sanghongyang-ceng-bang-hanwudi-zhifu-weihe-zuihou-canzao-miezu-ltpcos20d5mafcoywmfcb6iifwsv|Hanji 402-1]] as the Shaofu and son-in-law of [[TianQianqiuWesternHan|田千秋]], whose handling of the Hou Shiwu case puts both himself and the chancellor's household under [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]]'s pressure.

## Current Profile

Xu Ren's episode profile combines legal judgment and kinship exposure. He joins Tingwei [[WangPingWesternHan|王平]] in judging [[HouShiWuWesternHan|侯史吴]] as pardonable after the amnesty because Hou only sheltered [[SangQianWesternHan|桑迁]] briefly and was not personally a rebel. Huo Guang rejects that reading, other officials reverse the classification, and Xu Ren's tie to Tian Qianqiu leads the chancellor to convene officials in a failed effort to protect him. Xu Ren ultimately dies by suicide in prison.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Shaofu involved in the Hou Shiwu harboring case.
- Son-in-law of Chancellor Tian Qianqiu, making his prosecution politically sensitive.
- Co-author of the initial narrower amnesty reading with Wang Ping.
- Accused after Huo Guang's review of indulging rebellion by sparing Hou Shiwu.
- Dies by suicide in prison after the case turns against him.
