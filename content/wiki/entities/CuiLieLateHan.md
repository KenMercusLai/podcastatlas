# 崔烈 / Cui Lie (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-933-weiguan-zhidao-yousuo-wei-yousuo-buwei-lopulnufsagoqyx7nngvcqi2nhrj|Hanji 933]] backfills Cui Lie before the later office-churn marker. The episode presents him as a Jizhou notable and tingwei who obtains the situ office after paying five million cash through [[ChengFurenLateHan|程夫人]], [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s wet nurse. Emperor Ling's regret that he might have charged ten million makes Cui Lie's appointment the source case for [[OfficeSaleLegitimacyDamage|买官名节损耗]].

The damage is visible through [[CuiJunLateHan|崔钧]]. Cui Jun says people did not previously think Cui Lie unworthy of the Three Excellencies, but after the purchase they describe a "stink" around him. The episode therefore treats Cui Lie as a figure who gains office while losing the public reputation that made the office socially plausible.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-934-sha-yiren-ze-tianxia-an-ni-sha-busha-ltar8hvdkqct8oizm3k22z38b1v8|Hanji 934]] adds the policy consequence after that bought-office branch. Cui Lie proposes abandoning [[LiangzhouLateHan|凉州]] because rebellion and tax pressure have made it burdensome, but [[FuXie|傅燮]] attacks the proposal as dangerous enough to justify "斩司徒，天下乃安."

This makes Cui Lie more than a reputational caution. Hanji 934 turns him into the negative case for [[StrategicFrontierAbandonment|弃边失屏]]: a senior minister reads a troubled frontier as removable cost, while Fu Xie reads the same province as the western shield whose loss would strengthen future enemies.

崔烈 / Cui Lie (late Han) also appears through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-938-lishi-de-jingxing-ruanruo-zhihui-dailai-quru-lnlnrketgfbadhfjbzdjjtrmqfsh|Hanji 938]] as the situ who becomes taiwei after [[ZhangWenLateHan|张温]] is removed for ineffective pacification. The same episode later removes Cui Lie and appoints [[CaoSong|曹嵩]] as taiwei.

Together the two sources make Cui Lie a late-Han office-order marker. Hanji 933 shows appointment credibility weakened by purchase; Hanji 938 shows high-office movement failing to restore crisis capacity during Liangzhou, Youzhou, Wuhuan, Changsha, and Xiongnu-linked disorder.
