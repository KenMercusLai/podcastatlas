# 薛鹏 / Xue Peng

薛鹏 / Xue Peng is a [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] speaker in [[ep238-2025-nian-ni-wan-guo-zenyang-de-xiaozhong-yundong-lpaw-kela5vk6avl-s11h-yexwlg]], where he explains [[ArmoredCombat|盔甲格斗]] as a mixture of sport, historical reenactment, equipment culture, and close-contact fighting. His segment makes the episode's [[UrbanNicheSports|城市小众运动]] frame concrete by showing a case where technical rules are only part of the barrier.

The episode uses his reporting and first-hand try-on experience to describe padded underclothes, heavy helmets, hand-made armor, historical-authenticity requirements, one-on-one and team formats, and the high anaerobic intensity of short bouts. His contribution also anchors the episode's commercialization question: armored combat may be spectacular to watch, but deeper participation requires enough fitness, money, knowledge, and community commitment that it cannot scale like a low-barrier fitness class.
