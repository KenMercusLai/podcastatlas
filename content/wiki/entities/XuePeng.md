## Overview
薛鹏 / Xue Peng is a [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] journalist and [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] speaker whose source profile spans field reporting on materials, food, sports, and historical routes.

## Current Profile
Xue Peng's reporting repeatedly starts with a concrete material or bodily threshold and expands outward. Agarwood resin, armor weight, regional fermentation, road paving, cypress corridors, and mountain gradients become entry points into supply chains, cultural memory, participation barriers, and infrastructure.

In the Shu-roads episode, his [[JinniuRoad|金牛道]] reporting distinguishes the road's long historical continuity from the much later age of many visible remains. He uses Mingyue Gorge, Cuiyun Corridor, Qiqu Mountain, and route changes to show how political centers, terrain, and modern construction shape what a “historic road” means on the ground.

## Key Characteristics
- Field reporter who grounds broad cultural topics in material processes and site detail.
- Explains supply chains and historical infrastructure without reducing them to consumer or tourism stories.
- Moves across lifestyle topics while preserving distinctions among firsthand observation, expert interpretation, and uncertain history.
- Uses embodied experience—wearing armor, smelling materials, walking roads—to make technical thresholds legible.
