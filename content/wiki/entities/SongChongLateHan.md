## Overview

宋冲 / Song Chong is the Hanji 861 admirer who urges [[GuoTaiLateHan|郭泰]] to enter office. His source role is to make Guo Tai's refusal visible as a considered choice rather than a lack of opportunity.

## Current Profile

Song Chong's profile is source-scoped. Hanji 861 presents him as deeply impressed by Guo Tai's virtue and learning, to the point of treating Guo Tai as unmatched in the Han. He repeatedly encourages Guo Tai to serve, but Guo Tai declines on the ground that Heaven and human affairs show a collapsing order that cannot be propped up.

In the wiki, Song Chong matters because his pressure clarifies Guo Tai's boundary. Guo Tai is not absent from public life because no one values him; he is valued precisely when he chooses teaching and talent encouragement over office.

## Key Characteristics

- Admirer of Guo Tai's virtue and learning.
- External voice pressing Guo Tai toward official service.
- Source-scoped figure whose main role is to frame Guo Tai's refusal.
- Evidence that Guo Tai's non-service is a chosen boundary under recognized reputation.
