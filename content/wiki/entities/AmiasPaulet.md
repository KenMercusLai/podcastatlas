# Amias Paulet

Amias Paulet appears in [[696-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-killing-the-queen-of-scots-part-6-glt2334035496]] as the strict jailer of [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]] at [[ChartleyManor]]. After earlier plots, he tightens Mary's communications, searches household members, and restricts access to the outside world.

Paulet is also central to the episode's distinction between legal execution and private killing. [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]] and ministers hope Mary might be removed privately, but Paulet refuses to shed blood without law or warrant, reinforcing [[RoyalExecutionLegitimacy]] as a practical constraint rather than an abstract doctrine.
