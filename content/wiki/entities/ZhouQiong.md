## Current Profile
The source presents Zhou Qiong as translating a complex syndrome into a risk-and-action pathway. She connects upstream cardiac disease to [[HeartFailure|heart failure]], distinguishes acute presentation from chronic decompensation, and places symptoms beside examination, biomarkers, and echocardiography rather than encouraging self-diagnosis. Her practical emphasis is comprehensive care: identify the cause, use indicated medication with gradual titration, monitor clinical parameters, and build [[HeartFailureSelfManagement|daily self-management]] around fluid and salt, weight, activity, vaccination, and timely escalation.

## Key Characteristics

- Emphasizes hypertension and coronary disease as important modifiable upstream pathways.
- Combines symptom recognition with examination, biomarkers, and cardiac imaging.
- Treats medication titration as individualized and parameter-guided.
- Uses weight, blood pressure, pulse, fluid intake, and function as practical longitudinal signals.
- Preserves urgent-care boundaries for orthopnea, severe edema, reduced urine, or major physiologic change.
