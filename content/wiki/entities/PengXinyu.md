# 彭新宇 / Peng Xinyu

彭新宇 is the [[Alibaba]] executive and [[Lingyang|瓴羊]] CEO interviewed in [[e248-yi-ge-cui-fahuo-ai-yao-paotong-260-bu-he-ali-lingyang-pengxinyu-liaoliao-zhongguoshi-fde-9e923c4c-1c87-499b-90a4-9a21cc83e4b1]]. The source presents him as one of Alibaba's data middle-platform method founders and uses his account to define [[ChineseStyleFDE]] around AI acuity, industry depth, data breadth, and measurable business results.

His main wiki contribution is a concrete operating account of enterprise agents in China. He argues that [[ForwardDeployedEngineer|FDE]] work differs from ordinary on-site development when the delivery unit becomes business effect: lower service cost, higher customer-service throughput, improved marketing conversion, faster sales execution, or better ROI. He also emphasizes that in China, enterprise AI teams often have to rebuild data and process foundations before agents can work.

## Key Claims
- Enterprise AI should begin from "耗人、耗钱、耗时" workflows rather than generic automation enthusiasm.
- Useful enterprise agents resemble newly hired employees: they need permissions, goals, company data, expert teachers, and controlled rollout.
- FDE is better understood as a team capability combining business analysts, AI architects, and customer-side domain experts, not only one exceptional generalist.
- [[OutcomeBasedAIPricing]] is natural where AI work can be tied to labor replacement, work volume, conversion uplift, or business ROI.
