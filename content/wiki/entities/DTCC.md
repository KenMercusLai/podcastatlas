# DTCC

## Overview
DTCC is the U.S. post-trade market-infrastructure institution named in the source's explanation of why [[Robinhood]] restricted purchases during the 2021 [[GameStop]] volatility.

## Current Profile
In the episode's account, DTCC raised deposit requirements as concentrated, volatile, unsettled trades increased member exposure. This turns clearing into a visible constraint: a broker may offer immediate trading to customers while still needing enough capital to cover settlement and counterparty risk before trades complete.

## Key Characteristics
- Sits in the clearing and settlement chain behind customer-facing brokerages.
- Uses member deposits or collateral to contain unsettled-trade risk.
- Can transmit volatility into immediate broker funding needs.
- Became central to the public legitimacy dispute around GameStop purchase restrictions.

## Evidence
- **Deposit pressure:** [[ep159-gamestop-gupiao-touzi-yulehua-weishenme-jiazhi-touzi-bu-chixiangle-ckwrirwd-dwbabaaaachv6pg]] reports that DTCC sought substantially higher deposits from members during extreme GameStop trading.
- **Broker response:** [[ep159-gamestop-gupiao-touzi-yulehua-weishenme-jiazhi-touzi-bu-chixiangle-ckwrirwd-dwbabaaaachv6pg]] links the requirement to Robinhood's effort to reduce new one-way exposure by limiting purchases.

## Qualifications
The source is a practitioner explanation recorded during the event, not DTCC documentation or a complete regulatory reconstruction. Its aggregate deposit figure and causal emphasis remain source-scoped.

## What Changed
- Created a clearing-infrastructure profile for the GameStop restriction mechanism.
