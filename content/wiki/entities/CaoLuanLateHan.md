# 曹鸾 / Cao Luan (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-907-suoyou-de-bing-doushi-ni-de-xinbing-ltqzyohcauduwuanlvqph9l81d79|Hanji 907]] introduces Cao Luan as the Yongchang commandery administrator who memorializes for party figures in 176 CE. He argues that the banned group includes elders, moral persons, and talented士大夫 who could serve the state, and he connects disasters such as flood, drought, and epidemic to the court's unjust treatment of them through [[NaturalDisasterPoliticalOmen|自然灾害政治征兆]].

The source makes Cao Luan a boundary case for late-Han remonstrance. His memorial reaches [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]], but instead of producing review or mercy, it triggers his arrest, transport to [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], interrogation, and death. The aftermath expands party-prohibition liability to students, old subordinates, close family, and five-degree kin, so Cao Luan's case becomes the opening source for [[PartyProhibitionKinshipExpansion|党锢亲属牵连扩大]] and a severe branch of [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]].
