# Saudi Public Investment Fund

## Overview
Saudi Public Investment Fund is Saudi Arabia's sovereign-wealth fund in the wiki, appearing both as foreign capital in a U.S. political-influence story and as the backer of high-profile sports investments.

## Current Profile
[[ep77-si-shi-wan-nian-xin-fu-ye-zhuan-le-san-shi-si-yi-te-lang-pu-jiao-ni-ruhe-gao-qian-lnrx8vkdjedxbmmcwxlnwow-pcab]] introduces the fund through its alleged investment into [[JaredKushner]]'s private-equity firm, using it as a case for foreign capital routed through political relationships. [[chainsaw-sputtering-mileis-experiment-falters-6aa1299999455f9a93f7de9b]] adds the sports branch: the fund tried to disrupt global sports, especially golf through [[LIVGolf]], but ran into fan, broadcast, institutional, and time-horizon limits.

## Key Characteristics
- Sovereign-wealth fund tied to Saudi state strategy and external influence.
- Appears as a large foreign investor in politically connected private finance.
- Backs sports investments meant to diversify Saudi Arabia's economy and project soft power.
- Shows the limits of capital when legitimacy depends on fans, institutions, and history.

## Evidence
- Political-finance branch: [[ep77-si-shi-wan-nian-xin-fu-ye-zhuan-le-san-shi-si-yi-te-lang-pu-jiao-ni-ruhe-gao-qian-lnrx8vkdjedxbmmcwxlnwow-pcab]] says the fund invested about $2 billion in [[JaredKushner]]'s vehicle and frames management fees as economically important.
- Sports-investment branch: [[chainsaw-sputtering-mileis-experiment-falters-6aa1299999455f9a93f7de9b]] says the fund spent about $5 billion on [[LIVGolf]] since 2021 and invested across other sports.
- Legitimacy constraint: [[chainsaw-sputtering-mileis-experiment-falters-6aa1299999455f9a93f7de9b]] says LIV Golf lacked enough fans and broadcast income despite attracting high-profile players.

## Qualifications
The fund is not analyzed comprehensively as a portfolio institution in either source. The current evidence covers politically salient investments and sports strategy.
