# Stacy Sims

## Overview
Stacy Sims is an exercise physiologist and nutrition scientist presented by [[HubermanLab]] as a specialist in female-specific training, nutrition, environmental physiology, and performance across the life course.

## Current Profile
In the source, Sims's defining stance is that women should not inherit exercise and nutrition rules from male-dominant evidence without checking sex, hormonal state, age, training status, and goal. She turns that stance into three practical branches: fuel hard training sufficiently, track menstrual and life-stage patterns without treating them as destiny, and make resistance, power, impact, and recovery central as estrogen declines.

Her advice spans clinical and performance topics, so the profile must preserve evidence boundaries. The episode mixes established training principles with precise protein, ferritin, supplement, temperature, diagnostic, pregnancy, and performance-stack claims that cannot be treated as universal prescriptions from this source alone.

## Key Characteristics
- Centers female physiology rather than assuming male-derived protocols transfer unchanged.
- Treats training quality, recovery, and endocrine signaling as linked to energy availability.
- Favors personal cycle and symptom tracking over rigid phase-based programming.
- Prioritizes strength, power, impact loading, and independence in midlife and later life.
- Distinguishes true high-intensity stimuli from workouts that are merely prolonged or exhausting.
- Combines research interpretation with concrete coaching protocols, some of which remain preliminary or source-scoped.

## Evidence
- Professional frame - [[female-specific-exercise-nutrition-for-health-performance-longevity-dr-stacy-sims-scim3511503121]] introduces Sims as an exercise physiologist and nutrition scientist focused on women.
- Fueling stance - [[female-specific-exercise-nutrition-for-health-performance-longevity-dr-stacy-sims-scim3511503121]] has Sims discourage routine fasted training for active women and connect under-fueling to weaker intensity, recovery, and reproductive-endocrine signaling.
- Individualization stance - [[female-specific-exercise-nutrition-for-health-performance-longevity-dr-stacy-sims-scim3511503121]] has her use cycle tracking and a ten-minute readiness rule rather than universal phase prescriptions.
- Midlife stance - [[female-specific-exercise-nutrition-for-health-performance-longevity-dr-stacy-sims-scim3511503121]] has her emphasize resistance, sprint or high-intensity work, jump training, protein, and recovery through perimenopause and aging.
- Evidence boundary - [[female-specific-exercise-nutrition-for-health-performance-longevity-dr-stacy-sims-scim3511503121]] acknowledges limited research in areas including menstrual-fluid diagnostics, competitive hormone shifts, pregnancy interventions, and cold exposure for endometriosis.

## Qualifications
This is a profile of Sims as represented in one long-form interview, not a complete assessment of her publications or consensus standing. Numerical nutrition, supplement, ferritin, heat, cold, menstrual, pregnancy, and performance recommendations require population-specific evidence, contraindication screening, and sometimes qualified clinical supervision.
