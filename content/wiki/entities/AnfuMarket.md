# 安福市场 / Anfu Market

## Overview

Anfu Market is the Putian footwear trading hub presented by the episode as a night-time interface among factories, stalls, hidden warehouses, outside buyers, ecommerce sellers, inspection services, and logistics.

## Current Profile

[[no-225-putian-xie-wangshi-gkwrijeowkkqa6hffgt1ih2w]] traces the market from the relocation of more than one hundred shoe sellers near Putian University into Anfu's later ecommerce identity. Its “ghost market” schedule combined practical coordination with online and overseas customers and reduced daytime visibility. Legitimate or unbranded displays could coexist with infringing stock stored elsewhere and transacted through private messaging.

## Key Characteristics

- Concentrated wholesale and ecommerce coordination rather than only final retail.
- Night-time schedule linking factory delivery, stall receipt, buyer pickup, and parcel packing.
- Distributed storage and private communication that separated visible premises from actual inventory.
- Supported guides, sourcing, inspection, cleaning, minor finishing, and fulfillment services.
- Functioned as a hub inside a wider network rather than as the whole counterfeit trade.

## Evidence

- **Formation and schedule:** [[no-225-putian-xie-wangshi-gkwrijeowkkqa6hffgt1ih2w]] describes seller relocation, ecommerce redevelopment, and concentrated late-evening operations.
- **Distributed inventory:** [[no-225-putian-xie-wangshi-gkwrijeowkkqa6hffgt1ih2w]] describes street-facing compliant displays alongside stock kept in residential buildings or underground storage.
- **Service ecology:** [[no-225-putian-xie-wangshi-gkwrijeowkkqa6hffgt1ih2w]] describes guides and teams handling sourcing, checking, cleaning, finishing, and shipment.

## Qualifications

- The account combines reporting from different periods and does not establish the market's complete current composition.
- Presence in Anfu does not by itself prove a seller or product is infringing.
- Night work can have commercial scheduling reasons as well as concealment value.

## What Changed
