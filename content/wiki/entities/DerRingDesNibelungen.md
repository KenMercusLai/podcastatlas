# Der Ring des Nibelungen / The Ring Cycle

Der Ring des Nibelungen, or the Ring cycle, is the four-part music-drama cycle by [[RichardWagner]] discussed in [[caracas-under-pressure-democracy-in-venezuela-6a7d8bbe7d85cbdf96e00822]]. The source marks 150 years since the cycle was first performed in full and describes it as roughly 15 hours of gods, heroes, myth, betrayal, power, and collapse.

The episode treats the Ring as formally influential because its [[Leitmotif|leitmotifs]], continuous musical writing, and [[Gesamtkunstwerk]] ambition changed what opera and later screen music could do. It also treats the work as politically open: Wagner's antisemitism and later Nazi reception matter, but the source reads the work itself as anti-royal, anti-capitalist, and repeatedly available for new interpretations, including environmental stagings.
