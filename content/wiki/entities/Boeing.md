# Boeing

[[150-zhongguo-ruhe-tiaochu-pinkun-xianjing-xiandaihua-zhi-lu-you-hui-zouxiang-hefang-lgpvp04rngagkpubte2ac6i5ji3g]] adds Boeing as a source-scoped example of [[ProcessKnowledge]] fragility. The episode uses Boeing alongside [[Intel]] and nuclear-safety examples to argue that technical systems depend on tacit operating memory that can be difficult to rebuild once teams, supplier practice, and manufacturing routines erode.

Boeing appears in [[tech-20260320-0320-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260320-0320-mp-tech-pod-128]] as a legacy aerospace and defense contractor that may face pressure as the U.S. government partners more with technology startups. The source uses Boeing as a comparison point, not as a detailed company case.

[[vol-268-liang-ge-lao-si-lai-si-1003563933]] adds Boeing as both competitive context and later engine-customer context around the [[RollsRoyceRB211|RB211]] story. The episode frames Boeing's 747 as part of the wide-body market pressure facing [[LockheedL1011TriStar|Lockheed's L-1011 TriStar]], then says RB211's later improvements helped [[RollsRoyce|Rolls-Royce]] enter broader civil-aircraft supplier systems including Boeing.
