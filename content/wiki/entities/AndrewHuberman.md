# Andrew Huberman

## Overview
Andrew Huberman is the [[HubermanLab]] host voice in source notes on nutrition and mental health, motivation and attention neuroscience, creativity process, emotion regulation, female and male hormone health, human-centered AI, neuromodulator-based self-regulation, dopamine/procrastination tools, immune-system biology, discipline-centered leadership, broad listener Q&A, meditation/consciousness inquiry, adult learning/brain health, bipolar-disorder psychiatry, OCD diagnosis and treatment, dog psychology as companion-animal self-regulation, movement practice as mind-body self-transformation, evidence-based nutrition for body composition, AI-supported education redesign, relationship science, flexibility/stretching practice, agency/risk/mental-health conversation, sleep-wake timing, and stress biology.

## Current Profile
The current evidence shows Huberman translating guest expertise, solo Essentials explanations, and listener questions into practical questions about biology, attention, emotion, health, behavior, technology, leadership, consciousness, learning, serious psychiatric care, companion-animal handling, movement practice, body-composition nutrition, education, relationships, flexibility, and sleep-wake timing. With [[ChrisPalmer]], he frames nutrition and ketosis through psychiatric supervision boundaries. With [[MasudHusain]], he presses motivation and procrastination beyond simple dopamine advice. With [[RickRubin]], he treats creativity as attention, feedback, and felt process. With [[RalphAdolphs]], he connects emotion science to regulation without flattening emotions into one brain region or one tool. With [[SaraGottfried]], he frames women's hormone health through longitudinal measurement and individualized clinical decisions. With [[KyleGillett]], he frames male hormone optimization through bloodwork, lifestyle foundations, fertility risk, supplement caution, and supervised androgen intervention. With [[FeiFeiLi]], he extends that host role into AI and human agency. In the neuromodulator episode, he works without a guest to map dopamine, epinephrine, serotonin, and acetylcholine into timed tools and explicit safety boundaries. With [[MaxKrummel]], he turns immune function, vaccines, sleep, peptides, cancer, aging, and brain-body claims into mechanism questions with clear uncertainty boundaries. With [[JockoWillink]], he draws discipline, exercise, adversity, identity, detachment, and leadership into the same practical-science frame without making Willink's lived military and parenting claims universal protocols. In the Q&A episode, he applies the same style across sleep, circadian rhythm, memory, smell, fertility, menopause, surgery recovery, and passion while defining optimization as realistic support rather than protocol perfection. In the dedicated sleep-toolkit episode, he presents [[SleepWakeTimingToolkit]] as a 24-hour routine built from morning light, evening darkness, temperature direction, caffeine delay, food timing, alcohol/THC caution, supplements, melatonin caution, consistent wake times, and [[TemperatureMinimumClockShifting]]. With [[SamHarris]], he uses questions about the self, flow, internal commentary, psychedelics, and emotional loops to elicit a meditation frame centered on [[NonDualMindfulness]] and ordinary freedom rather than stress reduction alone. With [[TommyWood]], he returns to neuroplasticity directly, pressing adult learning, flow versus clutch states, exercise, nutrition, dementia, concussion, and supplement claims through mechanism and safety boundaries. In the bipolar-disorder and OCD Essentials episodes, he explains serious psychiatric conditions through diagnosis, mechanism, treatment evidence, and clinical boundaries: bipolar disorder through lithium and integrated care, and [[ObsessiveCompulsiveDisorder]] through [[OCDCompulsionReinforcementLoop]], [[CorticostriatalThalamicLoop]], [[YaleBrownObsessiveCompulsiveScale]], and [[ExposureResponsePreventionForOCD]]. With [[CesarMillan]], he uses Costello and Strummer to make dog training concrete while linking leash behavior, greeting rituals, feeding waits, barking response, and end-of-life care to owner state, impulse control, and [[CalmAssertiveEnergy]]. With [[IdoPortal]], he turns movement practice into questions about will, play, sleep transitions, bodily resolution, and model change while keeping Portal's experiential claims separate from clinical protocols. With [[LayneNorton]], he frames eating for health, fat loss, and lean muscle through energy balance, protein, ultra-processed foods, non-nutritive sweeteners, seed oils, creatine, and the priority of hard training over optimization minutiae.

With [[JoeLiemandt]], he frames education redesign through [[AlphaSchool]], AI-supported mastery, prerequisite repair, motivation, human guides, and builder afternoons while keeping performance and scaling claims source-scoped.

With [[PaulEastwick]], he frames attraction, dating apps, mate selection, attachment, social support, similarity, sexual satisfaction, and long-term relationships as research questions whose practical force depends on context rather than simple dating formulas.

In the flexibility Essentials episode, he frames stretching through [[FlexibilityNeuralSafetyGating]], [[StaticStretchingDoseProtocol]], [[InsularPainToleranceTraining]], and [[StretchingMobilitySafety]], making range of motion a question of neural protection, warmup, low-intensity static holds, weekly dose, and pain-boundary judgment.

With [[AndyStumpf]], he frames agency, risk, discipline, and vulnerability through [[Drownproof]], [[ConcernInfluenceSorting]], [[SlightlyHarderChoice]], wingsuiting and base-jump risk, divorce, parenting estrangement, pain disclosure, suicide discussion, and the need to define enough before success becomes too expensive.

With [[ElissaEpel]], he frames stress as a biological, psychological, metabolic, and aging-related response shaped by appraisal, rumination, recovery, eating reward, mindfulness, radical acceptance, uncertainty tolerance, and controlled positive stress.

In the dopamine/procrastination solo episode, he returns to the neuromodulator frame with more emphasis on baseline, peaks, troughs, reward prediction, addiction narrowing, intrinsic motivation, and effort. The episode adds [[DopaminePeakTroughBaseline]], [[RewardPredictionErrorLearning]], [[DopamineBaselineMaintenance]], [[SafeEffortProcrastinationReset]], and [[EffortAsReward]], while keeping prescription stimulants, supplements, cold exposure, addiction recovery, and psychiatric concerns inside safety boundaries.

- Bridges neuroscience questions into AI and education by comparing machine pattern learning with human embodiment, motivation, emotion, agency, and school design.
- Keeps uncertainty visible when topics involve clinical decisions, future robotics, creative work, or education policy.
