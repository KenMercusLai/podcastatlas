## Overview

马武 / Ma Wu is synthesized as an Eastern Han frontier commander who appears in both Guangwu and early Mingdi frontier pressure. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-714-shi-shui-di-yi-ge-han-chu-nan-er-dang-ma-ge-guo-shi-lpkqxrl3dsok4ui0lij4c3x8zfpi|Hanji 714]] now adds an earlier Guangwu-era defensive deployment at the Hutuo River against Xiongnu pressure, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-1-lishi-suo-yiwang-de-diwang-zhishi-mingjun-liuzhuang-lor7vhzzpwz1rv9tijksogukjqvr|Hanji 733-1]] presents him as the commander who later suppresses the early Mingdi [[ShaodangQiang|Shaodang Qiang]] revolt after earlier Han responses fail.

## Current Profile

Hanji 714 supplies Ma Wu's earlier current role. In Jianwu 13 / 37 CE, Liu Xiu sends the Bulu General Ma Wu to station troops at the Hutuo River to guard against Xiongnu disturbance. The episode does not make Ma Wu the branch's decisive attacker; it presents him as part of a defensive containment layer while [[LuFangEasternHan|Lu Fang]] and Xiongnu forces pressure Yunzhong and then fracture through [[SuiYuEasternHan|Sui Yu]]'s defection.

Hanji 733-1 places Ma Wu beside [[DouGuEasternHan|Dou Gu]] when Mingdi sends a major force against the Shaodang Qiang crisis. Zhang Hong had been defeated at Yunwu, and the new reign needed a stronger military response after Dian Wu's advance into Longxi.

Ma Wu's distinct action comes later in the same year, when he attacks the Qiang and achieves suppression. The page's current evidence is therefore narrow but clear: Ma Wu is the successful field resolver of this early frontier test.

## Key Characteristics

- Guangwu-era frontier commander stationed defensively against Xiongnu pressure.
- Military actor in Mingdi's first Shaodang Qiang crisis.
- Sent with Dou Gu as part of a larger escalated response under Mingdi.
- Receives the clearest current credit for the later Shaodang Qiang suppressive attack.
- Functions as a contrast to the failed initial command response.

## Qualifications

Hanji 714 does not describe a battle result for Ma Wu's Hutuo deployment, so the page should not infer victory from that notice alone. Hanji 733-1 does not provide Ma Wu's broader career, nor does it detail the tactics of the final suppression. Dou Gu's role in the final stage is less specified in Hanji 733-1 than Ma Wu's.
