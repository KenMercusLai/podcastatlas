# Amanirenas

## Overview

Amanirenas is the Kushite Kandake figure named by the episode as an ancient African example of female royal and military authority.

## Current Profile

In this source, Amanirenas functions as the concrete name behind the [[KandakeQueens]] example. The episode places her in the first century BC and uses her to show that women rulers connected to military campaigns appear early in African history, before the better-known early-modern and modern-popular-history cases around [[QueenNjinga]] and the [[Agojie]].

## Key Characteristics

- Named Kushite queen in the source's ancient female-authority example.
- Associated with the [[KandakeQueens]] title or institution.
- Placed by the source in the first century BC.
- Used as evidence against treating African women warriors and rulers as only Dahomey-era or film-driven subjects.

## Evidence

- Named example: [[317-african-amazons-glt5973082485]] says Kush had Kandake queens, including Amanirenas in the first century BC.
- Military-authority frame: [[317-african-amazons-glt5973082485]] says Kandake queens led campaigns against Egyptians and Romans.
- Comparative role: [[317-african-amazons-glt5973082485]] places Amanirenas before the source's later case studies of [[QueenNjinga]], [[AsanteQueenMothers]], [[RanavalonaI]], and [[Agojie]].

## Qualifications

The source note's transcription renders the name as "Amani Renos"; this page normalizes to Amanirenas while keeping the identification source-scoped. The episode does not provide a full biography.
