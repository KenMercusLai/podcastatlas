# Pure Fitness / Pure

## Overview
Pure Fitness / Pure is the high-end fitness brand named as [[MayaActive|Maya Active]]'s partner for a Shanghai fitness space.

## Current Profile
The source uses Pure narrowly: it is the fitness-operator partner that gives Maya Active a credible offline exercise setting. Pure's role is not analyzed as a standalone fitness-chain strategy, but as infrastructure for Maya Active's attempt to turn apparel customers into a more embodied brand community.

## Key Characteristics
- High-end fitness brand in the source account.
- Partner for a large Shanghai facility with Maya Active.
- Supplies exercise-space credibility to an apparel brand's user-operation strategy.
- Connects sportswear retail to class, coaching, and community experience rather than only product display.

- Partnership evidence: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says Maya Active will open the Shanghai fitness space jointly with Pure.
