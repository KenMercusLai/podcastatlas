# 田大夫 / Doctor Tian (TCM Guest)

## Overview
田大夫 is the source-scoped Chinese-medicine clinician guest in [[vol-10-zhongyi-shaoshangxue-fangxue-zhende-neng-zhi-sangzi-tong-me-daodi-gai-bu-gai-duandiao-hanxing-de-niunai-ljojejbtrsnpuu1aa-10klw6reds|VOL.10]] of [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]]. The episode says he began independent practice in 2009 and had practiced for thirteen years by late 2022; no fuller identity or credential record is supplied.

## Current Profile
In the available source, Tian explains Chinese medicine through individualized pattern differentiation rather than disease-name matching. He argues against both “Chinese medicine is always slow” and “herbal medicine has no side effects,” varies advice by person, season, place, constitution, and dose, and supports cooperation rather than a simple Chinese-versus-Western medical opposition.

His acute-care, bloodletting, patent-remedy, pregnancy, pediatric, food-therapy, milk, and dosage-form examples remain public interview claims. The durable contribution is a reasoning boundary—do not copy another patient's prescription or universalize one food or remedy—while efficacy, indication, dosing, and safety require qualified, current assessment.

## Key Characteristics
- Presents pattern differentiation as the central logic of Chinese-medicine treatment.
- Uses “different diseases, same treatment” and “same disease, different treatment” to resist diagnosis-label prescribing.
- Emphasizes person, timing, geography, constitution, dose, and changing bodily state.
- Rejects both automatic harmlessness and automatic toxicity for herbs or food-derived remedies.
- Favors an integrative goal of improved health rather than categorical Chinese-versus-Western opposition.
- Discusses convenient modern dosage forms while preserving uncertainty about equivalence to traditional decoction.
