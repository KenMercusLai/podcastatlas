# 巫君 / Wu Jun (indie game creator)

Wu Jun / 巫君 is the independent game creator interviewed in [[ep242-duli-youxi-shi-yitiao-gaoqian-hao-saidao-ma-lrqbzrceumbuggzuero5qb4jv3zu]]. He describes entering game making through [[RPGMaker]], [[SixtySixRPG|66RPG]], and [[ProjectOneForum|Project One论坛]], then later moving from hobbyist forum production into [[Steam]]-published [[IndieGameCommercialization|independent game commercialization]].

His most important source role is practical calibration. He argues that small teams can sometimes survive by combining craft, vertical community knowledge, low initial cost, and long-tail revenue, but he also lists the platform, tax, publisher, production, and reinvestment layers that turn large gross sales into modest personal income.
