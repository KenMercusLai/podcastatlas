# Ctrip Wendao

Ctrip Wendao is the travel-industry vertical model discussed in [[ep91-dingfang-dingpiao-dingjiangshan-xiecheng-51-yi-wei-aoman-maidan-lovhfkz4rklv1ik-uqyeswrdf3uw]]. The episode says [[Ctrip]] released it in July 2023 after accumulating long-running OTA transaction and travel-content data.

The source uses Wendao as a possible recovery asset rather than as proof that Ctrip's trust problem is solved. If a travel model can reduce choice time and connect planning to booking, service, itinerary changes, and emergency support, it extends [[AITravelPlanning]] and [[TravelSuperAppConvenience]]; if pricing, cancellation, ranking, or merchant rules remain opaque, the same assistant surface could deepen [[PlatformDataRegulation]] concerns.
