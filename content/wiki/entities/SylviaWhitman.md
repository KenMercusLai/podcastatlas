# Sylvia Whitman

## Overview
Sylvia Whitman is the current owner of [[ShakespeareAndCompany]] in the episode's cultural segment.

## Current Profile
The source presents Whitman as an inheritor and steward of a literary institution built partly through history and partly through myth. She explains how guides and visitors often repeat imaginative versions of the shop's story, while her father's own mythmaking helped shape the shop's public aura.

## Key Characteristics
- She is tied to Shakespeare and Company's present ownership and public interpretation.
- She distinguishes the shop's actual history from tourist myths and guide stories.
- Her name links the current shop to [[SylviaBeach]], because George Whitman named his daughter Sylvia.
- She preserves a shop whose appeal depends on literary memory, ritual, and offline visitation.

## Evidence
- Ownership evidence: [[bots-on-the-ground-china-leads-humanoid-race-6a91564cee851f3f31239bbf]] identifies Sylvia Whitman as the current owner of Shakespeare and Company.

- Family-history evidence: [[bots-on-the-ground-china-leads-humanoid-race-6a91564cee851f3f31239bbf]] says George Whitman named his daughter Sylvia and encouraged some myths around the shop.
