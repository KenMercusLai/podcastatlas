# Starbase

Starbase appears in [[e239-spacex-yao-rang-taikong-suanli-cong-kehuan-zouxiang-xianshi-dan-ta-huasuan-ma-259291f5-2715-4dde-bcfe-b5beb4df5793]] through [[LouisHong]]'s site memory. He contrasts a 2018 scene of open land and temporary structures with a later buildout of headquarters space, sub-assembly buildings, megabay-scale facilities, and a denser launch-and-manufacturing environment.

In the episode, Starbase is less a tourist backdrop than a visible sign of [[SpaceX]]'s industrialization thesis. Seeing [[Starship]] and the production base in person makes launch scale, manufacturing cadence, and [[ReusableRocketEconomics]] more concrete than abstract cost-per-kilogram claims.
