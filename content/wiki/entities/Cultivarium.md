# Cultivarium

## Overview

Cultivarium is presented as a [[FocusedResearchOrganizations|focused research organization]] cataloguing fungi and microbes beyond the small set of familiar laboratory organisms.

## Current Profile

Its significance in the source is institutional as much as biological: the intended output is a publicly accessible research resource, not primarily a conventional product or a sequence of academic papers. It therefore illustrates how time-bounded, mission-focused organizations can build shared tools that fall between university and commercial incentives.

## Key Characteristics

- Studies understudied fungi and microbes beyond standard laboratory organisms.
- Produces shared research infrastructure rather than optimizing only for papers or a saleable product.
- Serves as a concrete example of an engineering-heavy, use-driven research organization.
