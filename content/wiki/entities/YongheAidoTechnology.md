# 永和爱豆科技 / Yonghe Aido Technology

## Overview
[[YongheAidoTechnology]] is the [[YongheCounty]] data-annotation company at the center of the episode's account of digital employment, labor control, and technological displacement.

## Current Profile
The source reports that the company gathered more than 170 employees between 2021 and 2023 and briefly became one of the county's largest employers. Its work included image boxes, text transcription, result checking, safety review, and later temporary image-review and dialect-collection projects; orders were reportedly supplied internally by or connected through [[AntGroup]] to businesses including Alipay, Amap, and Alibaba Cloud.

By 2023, basic task volume and prices had fallen while new work demanded specialist knowledge and university-educated cohorts. The company reportedly dissolved its large-model annotation group after it could not recruit enough qualified local workers, leaving a much smaller and more intermittent operation by 2026.

## Key Characteristics
- County-based outsourced data-annotation employer promoted within a digital-poverty-relief and women's-employment frame.
- Workforce concentrated among mothers needing stable work near home and school schedules.
- Used per-item output, speed, accuracy, comparison, and retraining controls.
- Handled both routine annotation and psychologically hazardous content review.
- Grew rapidly on abundant basic tasks, then contracted as demand automated and specialized.
