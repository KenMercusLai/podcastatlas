# David Kohan

## Overview

David Kohan is a writer and producer who co-created [[WillAndGrace|Will & Grace]] with [[MaxMutchnick]].

## Current Profile

The bounded source presents Kohan as Mutchnick's development partner in turning a real friendship into a sustainable sitcom premise. Together they used romantic-comedy expectations while preserving the central obstacle that Will and Grace could love each other without becoming a conventional sexual couple.

The episode also places Kohan beside his sister Jenji Kohan as a media-system comparison. David's network project moved through pilot development, executive resistance, advertiser concerns, and mass-audience calibration; Jenji's *Orange Is the New Black* received a full-season Netflix commission with greater representational and production freedom. The comparison separates creative liberty from audience scale or persuasive reach.
