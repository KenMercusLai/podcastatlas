# Jingdezhen Ceramic University / 景德镇陶瓷大学

Jingdezhen Ceramic University / 景德镇陶瓷大学 appears in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]] as the institutional descendant of earlier ceramic education and industrialization efforts. The episode traces it back to the Chinese ceramic school founded in the late Qing and notes its later university status.

The university matters because it connects [[CeramicIndustrializationTransition]] to the city's modern knowledge base. It anchors the education, design, material science, and industrial talent side of [[Jingdezhen]] alongside older workshop craft and contemporary [[AdvancedCeramicsIndustrialUpgrade|advanced ceramics]].
