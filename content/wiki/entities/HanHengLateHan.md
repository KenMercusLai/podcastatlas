# 韩恒 / Han Heng (late Han)

## Overview
韩恒 / Han Heng (late Han) enters the wiki through Hanji 1070 as the official who refuses [[JiaoChuLateHan|焦触]]'s blood-oath surrender to [[CaoCao|曹操]]. The episode uses him less as a military actor than as a visible moral exception whose survival helps Cao Cao and Jiao Chu avoid making loyal refusal into martyrdom.

## Current Profile
At Jiao Chu's oath assembly, everyone else fears the death threat attached to refusing surrender. Han Heng openly says that he received deep favor from [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and his sons and cannot again surrender to Cao Cao without losing integrity. The crowd expects Jiao Chu to kill him, but Jiao Chu lets him leave. The host reads this as politically useful: one tolerated loyalist can make the new regime look confident enough to spare moral dissent.

## Key Characteristics
- Loyal refusal figure inside a coerced surrender assembly.
- Defines his position through received Yuan-family favor and personal integrity.
- Creates a visible exception to Jiao Chu's death-threat oath discipline.
- Functions as a reputational asset for the victor when he is spared rather than executed.

## Qualifications
The source calls Han Heng's posture "愚忠" in interpretive terms while also presenting it as politically valuable. This page preserves that tension rather than resolving whether the refusal should be praised as principle or criticized as inflexible loyalty.
