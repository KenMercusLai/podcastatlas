# Jiang Chun / 江春

Jiang Chun / 江春 is the Qing Huizhou salt merchant highlighted in [[no-211-huishang-wangshi-cong-qianshi-buxiu-dao-zuiniu-fengtou-1002275189]]. The episode says he was born in 1720, came from Jiangcun in Shexian, and inherited a family position in Yangzhou's salt business.

His role as a long-serving total merchant makes him a useful case for the blurred boundary between business and fiscal administration. In the source, Jiang Chun helped manage salt merchants, tax collection, anti-smuggling responsibilities, donations, river works, military needs, and imperial reception around the [[LianghuaiSaltMonopoly]].

The episode also uses Jiang Chun to show risk. During the Yu Li Yin investigation, officials and merchants were held accountable for a large hidden fund, and Jiang Chun was taken to Beijing for questioning before being pardoned while still facing loss of honor and repayment pressure.
