# 周金涛

## Overview

周金涛 is the Chinese macro-strategy researcher whose public image in the wiki is tied to [[KondratievCycle]] research, real-estate and commodity cycles, and the phrase "人生发财靠康波." Earlier wiki evidence used him as a lineage figure for long-cycle asset research; EP94 makes him the central subject through a dramatized reconstruction of his public ideas.

## Current Profile

The current profile should treat Zhou less as a fortune-teller and more as a mechanism-oriented cycle researcher. [[e162-kangbo-zhouqi-zhong-de-ai-xin-jishu-zong-zai-xiaotiao-qi-baofa-bad-times-make-good-people-limyzch9la0bbwe8y9geofgqargl]] places his strategy lineage inside professional macro research at [[ChinaSecuritiesCo]], where long waves, real estate, commodities, gold, and asset expression were studied as connected market problems.

The public-facing investor-education version comes from [[ep94-chuanyue-zhoujintao-rensheng-facai-kao-kangbo-shouzhu-jiadi-kao-shao-cuo-lndimnd_sb98tfmzv-kukbbnbtg8]]. It credits Zhou's reputation to cases such as U.S. subprime-risk warning, 2016 commodity rebound, and 2019-ish asset opportunity, while explicitly qualifying the format as dramatized interpretation rather than original interview audio. The episode's strongest use of Zhou is cautionary: cycles can discipline thinking about environments, but household investors still need cash-flow, liquidity, leverage, and suitability boundaries.

## Key Characteristics

- Associated with [[KondratievCycle]] analysis and nested macro cycles rather than single-asset stock picking.
- Used by podcast sources as a bridge from macro history to [[MacroAssetExpression]] and ordinary investor questions.
- Publicly remembered for several striking timing calls, but the wiki treats those as source-scoped examples rather than proof of infallible prediction.
- His slogan "人生发财靠康波" is now qualified by EP94's risk-control half: "守住家底靠少错."
- Relevant to AI-era investing because long-cycle thinking separates technology importance from immediate profit and valuation timing.

## Qualifications

EP94 is not a Zhou Jintao interview or verbatim transcript; it is a dramatized reconstruction based on public materials. The page therefore treats its dialogue, examples, and timing comparisons as source interpretation. Specific past calls remain mixed and source-scoped, especially the property-sale timing and U.S. real-estate phase claims.
