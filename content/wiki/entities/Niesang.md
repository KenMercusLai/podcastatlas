# 啮桑 / Nie Sang

啮桑 / Nie Sang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-37-suqin-liuguo-jituan-ceo-de-bukan-qingshi-fj2kdqlaxzyy2nqtcutktuwrq5fo]] as the 323 BCE meeting site where [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]], serving [[QinState|秦国]], meets the chief ministers of [[QiState|齐国]] and [[ChuState|楚国]].

The episode gives two possible readings of the meeting. It may show Qin trying to improve relations with Qi and Chu after earlier pressure on Wei, or it may be Zhang Yi's response to [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]] as Gongsun Yan begins organizing anti-Qin contacts from [[WeiState|魏国]].

For the wiki, Nie Sang is a small but useful meeting node: it marks the move from individual persuasion stories into open multi-state diplomacy among rival strategist camps.
