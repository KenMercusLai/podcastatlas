# Mistral AI

Mistral AI is named in [[featherless-ai-when-your-weekend-experiment-makes-more-than-your-startup]] through the Mistral model family that [[FeatherlessAI|Featherless AI]] served in its weekend experiment. [[EugeneChia]] says that experiment with [[Llama]] and Mistral models produced stronger revenue than the company's original [[RWKV]] platform.

For the wiki, this page preserves the source's role for Mistral: a mainstream open-model demand signal that helped [[FeatherlessAI]] move from an own-model bet toward hosting the wider [[OpenSourceAIModels|open-source model]] ecosystem.
