# Julien Sorel / 于连·索雷尔

Julien Sorel is the protagonist of [[Stendhal|Stendhal]]'s [[RedAndBlackNovel|《红与黑》]], discussed in [[21-hong-yu-hei-ta-si-yu-zhencheng-569042001]] as a young man who is ambitious, proud, wounded, sincere, theatrical, and morally difficult at the same time. The episode resists reducing him to a "渣男" or "凤凰男" because the novel's force depends on his inner contradictions and on the blocked social world around him.

The source follows Julien from a loveless sawmill household into a tutor's position, seminary training, and aristocratic service. His admiration for [[NapoleonBonaparte|Napoleon]] gives him a heroic self-image, but [[FrenchRestoration|Restoration France]] redirects his ambition toward clerical and aristocratic masks, making him a central case of [[ClassMobilityPerformance]].

Julien's tragedy is that calculation never fully wins. His relationships with [[MadameDeRenal|Madame de Renal]] and [[MathildeDeLaMole|Mathilde]] begin in pride, resentment, conquest, fantasy, and self-display, but the source argues that his final collapse reveals [[SincerityAgainstSocialHypocrisy]] and [[PassionAgainstSelfControl]] rather than pure opportunism.
