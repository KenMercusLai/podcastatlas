# Aaron Mackey

Aaron Mackey appears in [[tech-20260406-0406-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260406-0406-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the [[ElectronicFrontierFoundation|Electronic Frontier Foundation]] speaker assessing [[CaliforniaAB1709]], California's proposed under-16 social media ban. His role is to frame child-safety legislation as a constitutional speech problem, not only as platform regulation.

Mackey argues that minors have rights to speak and access lawful online speech, and that age-verification or parental-consent gates can also burden adult users. He connects that objection to EFF's preferred policy route: [[ComprehensiveConsumerDataPrivacy]] rules that reduce surveillance, opaque targeting, and platform data extraction without cutting young people off from major communication spaces.
