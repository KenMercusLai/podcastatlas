# 张宁 / Zhang Ning (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-930-ruhe-pochu-tanxin-he-buganxin-lkyysh5jbgxwjq8ayrfar4gsjdcd|Hanji 930]] mentions Zhang Ning as the inspector who had reported [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]'s earlier merit to the court, after which Sun Jian received only a county-assistant appointment. The source uses this as background for why [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]]'s later decision to recruit Sun Jian matters.

This page is deliberately source-scoped. The short transcript does not provide enough office, locality, or variant-name evidence to merge Zhang Ning with another late-Han official. It is kept distinct from the unrelated modern [[ZhangNingPonyAI|Zhang Ning (Pony.ai)]] page.
