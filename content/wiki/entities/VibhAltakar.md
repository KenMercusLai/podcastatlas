# Vibh Altakar

Vibh Altakar appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-saronic-founders-autonomous-warships-chinas-230x-advantage-swarms-of-robot-ships-42334230]] as a co-founder of [[Saronic]]. His main contribution to the source is the technical and manufacturing explanation of why uncrewed vessels can be designed differently from crewed boats.

Vibh argues that removing humans from a vessel removes many subsystems and changes the optimization problem around range, payload, architecture, and control authority. He also describes [[SoftwareDefinedShipbuilding]] through digitized components, software APIs, remote control, sensors, onboard compute, and selective in-house electronics.
