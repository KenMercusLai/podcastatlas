# 周市 / Zhou Shi (Rebel)

周市 / Zhou Shi (Rebel) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-8-yi-ming-zhaoguo-chuishibing-nixi-chengwei-waijiao-dachen-lhfwk-hjge5iuqet6astmbclhyri|Qinji 128-8]] as the Zhang Chu commander who moves through old [[WeiState|魏国]] territory after the early anti-Qin uprising. The source transcript renders him as "周氏," so this page keeps the identification source-scoped while using the standard Zhou Shi form for the canonical page name.

His defining action is refusal of self-coronation. After capturing Wei territory, people around him urge him to become king, but he insists that the morally and politically stronger route is to restore the former Wei royal house. He repeatedly asks [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] to release [[WeiJiu|魏咎]], a surviving Wei royal descendant, so Wei Jiu can become Wei king.

Once Chen Sheng agrees, Wei Jiu goes to Wei territory, is made king, and Zhou Shi serves as chancellor. The source therefore makes Zhou Shi a positive counterexample inside [[AntiQinCoalitionFragmentation|反秦阵营诸侯化分裂]]: restoration still creates a separate polity, but Zhou Shi tries to ground it in inherited legitimacy rather than personal opportunism.
