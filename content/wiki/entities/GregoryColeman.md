# Gregory Coleman

Gregory Coleman appears in [[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]] as [[Liberia]]'s police inspector-general. The episode uses his comments to show why a major seizure can be politically fragile when public trust in authorities is low.

Coleman says officers and state actors have been identified or charged, and that the aim is to dismantle the cartel network in Liberia. His role connects [[NarcoStateRisk]] to the internal credibility of law enforcement, not only to external cartel pressure.
