## Current Profile

The sources now present Jun Buyi as a judicial official whose later crisis decisiveness rests on an earlier ethic of careful adjudication. Hanji 394-1 says that after taking office as Jingzhao Yin in 86 BCE, he reviews county cases and prisoners, then returns home to a mother who asks whether he has vindicated the wronged and saved people from death. Her pleasure or displeasure makes justice a repeated family standard. This is not sentimental background only: the episode links that daily maternal evaluation to Jun's evidence-conscious, fair, and benevolent style.

Hanji 396-2 then shows the political version of the same official temperament. Other senior officials hesitate because the claimant resembles Liu Ju and because a mistaken denial could be dangerous. Jun Buyi instead treats the claim as an order problem: even if Liu Ju had somehow survived the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]], he had offended the former emperor and fled, so he could not simply reappear as a legitimate claimant. This allows the court to act before rumor, crowd attention, and verification paralysis widen the crisis.

## Key Characteristics

- Jingzhao Yin whose mother repeatedly evaluates his office by whether he redresses wrongful cases and saves lives.
- Judicial official associated with evidence, fairness, benevolence, and caution around life-and-death decisions.
- Western Han Jingzhao Yin who intervenes in the palace-gate impostor crisis.
- Official who prioritizes dynastic order and former-emperor authority over resemblance-based uncertainty.
- Classical-reasoning actor who invokes the Wei crown-prince precedent to justify arresting a possible claimant.
- Crisis handler praised by Han Zhao and Huo Guang after the arrest.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 396-2 and Hanji 394-1. It does not reconstruct Jun Buyi's complete career, the exact legal status of Liu Ju after the Wugu affair, the primary text of the Wei crown-prince precedent, the full deliberation among all officials at the palace gate, the complete Western Han Jingzhao Yin procedure, or every case Jun reviewed. Hanji 394-1's parenting interpretation is recorded as the episode's family-education frame, not as a full causal proof of Jun's personality.
