## Overview

隽不疑 / Jun Buyi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-396-2-hanwudi-zhizi-liuju-wugu-zhi-an-hou-fuhuo-le-lhj6dzjsikkmbbcjlfgnz1bbok-1|Hanji 396-2]] as the Jingzhao Yin who resolves the sudden false-[[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]] crisis at [[WeiyangPalace|未央宫]] under [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Jun Buyi as a crisis official whose value lies in reframing the decision. Other senior officials hesitate because the claimant resembles Liu Ju and because a mistaken denial could be politically dangerous. Jun Buyi instead treats the claim as an order problem: even if Liu Ju had somehow survived the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]], he had offended the former emperor and fled, so he could not simply reappear as a legitimate claimant. This allows the court to act before rumor, crowd attention, and verification paralysis widen the crisis.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Jingzhao Yin who intervenes in the palace-gate impostor crisis.
- Official who prioritizes dynastic order and former-emperor authority over resemblance-based uncertainty.
- Classical-reasoning actor who invokes the Wei crown-prince precedent to justify arresting a possible claimant.
- Crisis handler praised by Han Zhao and Huo Guang after the arrest.
- Reputation figure whose decisive action raises his standing among capital officials.
