# Lucius Cornelius Sulla / 苏拉

## Overview

Lucius Cornelius Sulla / 苏拉 is the wiki's Roman precedent for military victory becoming civil violence, dictatorship, and public terror before [[JuliusCaesar|Caesar]]. Episode 701 adds his earlier place inside [[GaiusMarius]]'s African command rather than only his later role as Marius's enemy.

## Current Profile

The Caesar source presents Sulla as a violent political memory: his conflict with Marius, public death lists, and pressure on young Caesar show that the [[RomanRepublic|Roman Republic]] had already crossed into civil terror before Caesar's dictatorship. Episode 701 moves Sulla earlier, showing him as Marius's subordinate who negotiates [[Jugurtha]]'s surrender with the king of Mauritania. That role gives Sulla a claim to credit inside Marius's triumph and starts the prestige conflict that later hardens into rivalry.

Sulla's profile now has two stages in the wiki. First, he is the effective junior operator whose diplomatic success helps end the Jugurthine War. Second, he becomes the dictatorial precedent whose proscriptions and march-on-Rome future make the Marius age a direct prelude to the Caesar age.

## Key Characteristics

- Sulla begins in the current evidence as a talented subordinate inside Marius's command network, not only as a later dictator.
- His Jugurthine War role turns shared victory into contested personal credit.
- His later use of army force against Roman politics makes him a central example of republican norm collapse.
- His terror and death-list precedent forms a contrast with Caesar's later clemency and exposure.
- His conflict with Marius marks the pre-Caesar civil-war layer that episode 701 is setting up.

## Evidence

- African command origin: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] says Sulla negotiated with the king of Mauritania, producing [[Jugurtha]]'s surrender while serving under [[GaiusMarius]].
- Credit conflict: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] records Sulla advertising the surrender on a signet ring, irritating Marius and foreshadowing rivalry.
- Civil terror precedent: [[124-kaisa-zhisi-lishi-de-yunjiao-shifou-yi-shixiao-781894361]] treats Sulla's conflict with Marius and death lists as evidence that republican politics had already become violent before Caesar.
- Caesar contrast: [[124-kaisa-zhisi-lishi-de-yunjiao-shifou-yi-shixiao-781894361]] uses Sulla's demand that young Caesar divorce Cornelia and the later contrast with Caesar's clemency to show how different personal-rule styles still emerged from the same crisis.

## Qualifications

Episode 701 foreshadows Sulla's later march on Rome, proscriptions, and dictatorship but does not narrate them fully. The page therefore keeps Sulla's mature dictatorship grounded mainly in the Caesar source while adding the earlier Marius-subordinate layer from the new episode.

## What Changed

- Added Sulla's Jugurthine War role as a subordinate negotiator whose success created early rivalry with Marius.
- Reframed Sulla's later dictatorship as a development from shared command credit into civil-war politics, not a disconnected precedent.

## Relationships

- [[GaiusMarius]] - commander and later rival whose African command first places Sulla in the current evidence.
- [[Jugurtha]] - surrendered king whose capture became contested military and diplomatic credit.
- [[RomanRepublic]] - polity whose norms Sulla's later violence helped break.
- [[LateRomanRepublicCrisis]] - structural crisis in which Sulla is the pre-Caesar terror precedent.
- [[MilitaryPersonalization]] - mechanism connecting Sulla's later army-backed politics to commander-centered loyalty.
- [[JuliusCaesar]] - later dictator contrasted with Sulla's terror style and shaped by Sulla's early persecution.
