# Lucius Cornelius Sulla / 苏拉

## Overview

Lucius Cornelius Sulla / 苏拉 is the wiki's Roman precedent for military success turning into command rivalry, civil violence, dictatorship, and public terror before [[JuliusCaesar|Caesar]]. Episode 702 moves him from foreshadowed enemy of [[GaiusMarius]] to the commander whose stripped eastern command creates the immediate rupture threshold.

## Current Profile

The Caesar source presents Sulla as a violent political memory: his conflict with Marius, death lists, and pressure on young Caesar show that the [[RomanRepublic|Roman Republic]] had already crossed into civil terror before Caesar's dictatorship. Episode 701 moves Sulla earlier, showing him as Marius's subordinate who negotiates [[Jugurtha]]'s surrender and turns that success into personal credit through a signet-ring claim that irritates Marius.

Episode 702 deepens Sulla's career before open civil war. He is patrician but not securely wealthy, stylish, ruthless, politically gifted, and militarily talented. He breaks from Marius toward [[QuintusLutatiusCatulus]] and the conservative senatorial interest, gains further prestige in the [[SocialWar]], wins the consulship for 88 BC, and receives the command against [[MithridatesVI]]. The crisis arrives when Marius and [[PubliusSulpiciusRufus]] use street politics and a plebiscite to remove that command and send an officer to take control of Sulla's six legions.

## Key Characteristics

- Sulla begins in the current evidence as a talented subordinate inside Marius's command network, not only as a later dictator.
- His Jugurthine War role turns shared victory into contested personal credit.
- His aristocratic identity and relative early poverty make him Marius's mirror as well as opposite.
- His Social War performance makes him the standout Roman commander and helps win the 88 BC consulship.
- His Mithridatic command shows how consulship, foreign war, glory, and wealth become fused.
- His humiliation by Marius and Sulpicius turns command transfer into a test of whether armies obey office or commander.
- His later terror remains grounded in the Caesar source, while episode 702 stops at the pre-march threshold.

## Evidence

- African command origin: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] says Sulla negotiated with the king of Mauritania, producing [[Jugurtha]]'s surrender while serving under [[GaiusMarius]].
- Credit conflict: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] records Sulla advertising the surrender on a signet ring, irritating Marius and foreshadowing rivalry.
- Character and alignment: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] presents Sulla as patrician, sophisticated, ruthless, talented, and later aligned with Catulus and conservative senatorial interests.
- Social War rise: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] says Sulla captured Pompeii, invaded Samnium, stormed the Samnite capital, besieged Nola, and won the consulship easily.
- Command crisis: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] says Sulla was assigned the Mithridatic command as consul, then stripped of it by a plebiscite transferring command to Marius.
- Civil terror precedent: [[124-kaisa-zhisi-lishi-de-yunjiao-shifou-yi-shixiao-781894361]] treats Sulla's conflict with Marius and death lists as evidence that republican politics had already become violent before Caesar.

## Qualifications

Episode 702 ends before Sulla's response to the plebiscite, so this page treats the march-on-Rome threshold as immediate but not yet narrated by the new source. Sulla's dictatorship, proscriptions, and Caesar contrast remain grounded mainly in the Caesar source until a later Roman Civil War episode supplies fuller evidence.

## What Changed

- Added Sulla's character profile, aristocratic alignment, Social War command record, and 88 BC consulship.
- Moved Sulla's rivalry with Marius from early Jugurthine credit conflict into a live struggle over the Mithridatic command.
- Clarified that episode 702 stops at the command-stripping threshold before Sulla's later civil-war actions.

## Relationships

- [[GaiusMarius]] - former commander and rival who tries to take Sulla's eastern command.
- [[PubliusSulpiciusRufus]] - tribune whose bill and plebiscite remove Sulla from command.
- [[MithridatesVI]] - Pontic king whose war creates Sulla's command prize.
- [[SocialWar]] - conflict in which Sulla's command reputation becomes politically decisive.
- [[QuintusLutatiusCatulus]] - aristocratic counterweight to Marius whose circle Sulla joins.
- [[Jugurtha]] - surrendered king whose capture became contested military and diplomatic credit.
- [[RepublicanCommandTransferCrisis]] - immediate institutional crisis centered on Sulla's stripped command and six legions.
- [[MilitaryPersonalization]] - mechanism connecting Sulla's later army-backed politics to commander-centered loyalty.
- [[LateRomanRepublicCrisis]] - structural crisis in which Sulla is the pre-Caesar terror precedent.
- [[JuliusCaesar]] - later dictator contrasted with Sulla's terror style and shaped by Sulla's early persecution.
