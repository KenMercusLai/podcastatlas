# John Connally

## Overview

John Connally was the governor of Texas who helped host Kennedy's November 1963 visit and was wounded in the Dallas motorcade shooting.

## Current Profile

The episode places Connally at the intersection of electoral coalition management and the assassination itself. His conflict with Senator Ralph Yarborough divided Texas Democrats as Kennedy looked toward the 1964 election, making party reconciliation one purpose of the presidential trip. Kennedy required Vice President Lyndon Johnson and Yarborough to ride together while Connally and his wife Nellie shared the presidential car.

In Dealey Plaza, Connally recognized the first sound as rifle fire when many others assumed a firecracker or backfire. The official sequence followed by the hosts has him wounded during the later shots, with his lower and offset seat position central to the series' subsequent discussion of bullet trajectory.

## Key Characteristics

- Texas governor central to the state's divided Democratic coalition.
- Political host whose relationship with Ralph Yarborough complicated Kennedy's 1964 planning.
- Passenger seated ahead of John and Jacqueline Kennedy in the presidential car.
- Witness who identified rifle fire during the initial confusion.
- Shooting victim whose wounds became central to later ballistic debate.
