# 赵金昭 / Dr. Claire

## Overview
赵金昭, also called Dr. Claire in [[shi-di-fu-shuo-472-zhao-jinzhao-huajie-jiaolv-zuijia-fangfa-chaijie-wenti-wenrou-xiangdai-lqw9h-jqfzrsrveagfslnakhcgjh]], is presented as a counseling psychologist and Texas clinical psychologist interviewed by [[SteveShiDiFuShuoHost|Steve]] about anxiety.

## Current Profile
The source presents Claire as a clinically experienced therapist who explains anxiety through bodily signals, perceived demand-resource mismatch, family atmosphere, developmental history, and relational pacing. Her advice emphasizes reducing stimulation, building capacity and support, naming the feared object, and approaching anxious people with gentleness.

## Key Characteristics
- Identified as a University of Denver counseling psychology PhD and Texas clinical psychologist.
- Uses direct clinical experience, including more than ten thousand direct consultation hours, to explain anxiety patterns.
- Defines anxiety as anticipatory fear of the unknown and links it to body activation, avoidance, compulsion, and interpersonal contagion.
- Emphasizes decomposition, support, confidence, bodily grounding, and gentle relationship repair rather than urgent reassurance.
- Keeps anxiety work bounded by context: some anxiety can become challenge energy, but overload needs stabilization first.
