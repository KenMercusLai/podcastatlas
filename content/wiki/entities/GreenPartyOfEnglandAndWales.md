# Green Party of England and Wales

The Green Party of England and Wales is the Green Party branch profiled through [[ZackPolanski]] in [[its-not-easy-being-green-zack-polanski-6a82d1a0ff328abd84724cf0]]. The episode calls it the British Green Party and says Polanski has made it more visible among voters in their late teens and early twenties.

The source's main party claim is that the Greens are no longer framed only as environmentalists. Under Polanski, they become a vehicle for [[EcoPopulism]]: still green on climate and ecology, but louder on economic grievance, wealth redistribution, rent pressure, public ownership, anti-monarchy politics, nuclear disarmament, and alternatives to [[NATO]].

The episode also emphasizes vulnerability. A liberal migration policy, Gaza-focused campaigning, and radical platform commitments may help the party differentiate itself from [[LabourPartyUK]] but may also give opponents ways to define it as risky before a future election.
