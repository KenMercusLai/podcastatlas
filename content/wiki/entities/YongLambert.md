# Yong Lambert

## Overview
Yong Lambert is identified by the source as chief product officer of [[Mastercard]] and a panelist on global agent-payment standards and infrastructure.

## Current Profile
Lambert argues that payment systems designed around human consumers and merchants must be rebuilt for machine actors. His framework centers on authenticating the user, validating the agent, preserving [[VerifiableIntent]], providing recourse, and distributing standards globally so consumers can trust transactions even when an agent or merchant errs.

## Key Characteristics
- Approaches agent commerce from payment-network standards and governance.
- Treats consumer confidence and recourse as harder adoption constraints than writing a technical protocol.
- Proposes KYA alongside identity authentication and intent confirmation.
- Anticipates permissioned machine credentials and high-speed settlement for agent-to-agent commerce.
