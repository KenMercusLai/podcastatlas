# Oriental Selection / 东方甄选

Oriental Selection appears in [[qishui-yinyue-chongji-tengxun-wangyi-tesila-deng-cheqi-zai-guonei-kaizhan-daguimo-zhaohui-1008396395]] as a livestream-commerce company trying to make self-operated products the center of the business. The episode says full-year revenue exceeded RMB 5.7 billion, the company returned to profit, and self-operated goods exceeded half of platform GMV for the first time after more than 1,000 products were launched.

The source's strategic point is [[EfficiencyOverScaleExpansion]]: Oriental Selection is trying to reduce dependence on star-anchor traffic by changing anchor exposure, commission structure, team breadth, and supply-chain standardization. That makes it a creator-commerce case where [[CreatorTrustAdvertising]] has to become repeatable retail operations.
