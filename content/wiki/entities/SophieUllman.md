# Sophie Ullman

## Overview

Sophie Ullman is [[HedwigUllmann]]'s great-granddaughter and a family witness who frames lost artworks as markers of exile, inherited grief, gratitude, and possible healing.

## Current Profile

In [[hedwig-s-lost-van-gogh-hedwig-s-lost-van-gogh|Hedwig’s Lost Van Gogh]], Ullman explains that her family's survival and resettlement carry gratitude alongside sadness. She initially worried that [[ClaudeUllin]]'s search for the lost collection would reactivate inherited pain, but came to understand the claims as a form of healing rather than a pursuit of objects alone.

Her account gives restitution its intergenerational meaning. Artworks organize memory around a visible object, and the voluntary return of a Hans Thoma panel could therefore complete a family circle without erasing persecution or restoring the collection as it once existed.

## Key Characteristics

- Great-granddaughter interpreting Hedwig Ullmann's exile across generations.
- Family witness who holds gratitude for survival together with grief over loss.
- Initially cautious about the emotional cost of renewed provenance research.
- Advocate for understanding restitution as acknowledgment and healing rather than only possession.
