## Overview

Wang An / 王安 is [[WangMang|王莽]]'s third son in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-658-erzi-queshi-buyinggai-wangmang-nusha-taizi-ln4tu6ly8eie6jspvrurzn2rjjp|Hanji 658]]. His death in the same month as [[WangLinXinCrownPrince|王林]]'s suicide completes the episode's picture of Wang Mang losing all publicly recognized sons.

## Current Profile

The source gives Wang An a narrow but important succession role. He is named as the Xin prince who dies of illness after Wang Lin has already died and [[LiuYingWangLinWife|刘英]] has killed herself. His death means that Wang Mang's publicly known sons are gone, forcing the succession field toward private sons [[WangXingXinPrince|王兴]] and [[WangKuangXinPrince|王匡]].

## Key Characteristics

- Third son of Wang Mang in the episode's succession accounting.
- Xin prince whose death is reported as illness rather than scandal punishment.
- Final public-son loss that turns household disaster into succession emergency.
