## Overview

Wang An / 王安 is [[WangMang|王莽]]'s son whose [[XinDynasty|新朝]] succession role is clarified across Hanji 634, Hanji 657, and Hanji 658. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-634-huozai-nanren-yingzi-beihou-de-ta-can-can-can-lm3wub0l70yxwwyvwi15j93i9lwk|Hanji 634]] first says Wang Mang bypasses him because he dislikes his perceived dullness and instead favors [[WangLinXinCrownPrince|王林]], while appeasing Wang An with the Xin Jia Pi title. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-657-wangmang-fei-taizi-you-duo-erxi-lklx1rd39kgz-by4tbcpaagx4z-l|Hanji 657]] later shows him restored over [[WangLingXinPrince|王陵]] as Xin Qian Wang after Wang Mang reads disaster as proof of wrong heir naming; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-658-erzi-queshi-buyinggai-wangmang-nusha-taizi-ln4tu6ly8eie6jspvrurzn2rjjp|Hanji 658]] then says his illness death completes the loss of Wang Mang's publicly recognized sons.

## Current Profile

The sources give Wang An a narrow but important succession role. Hanji 634 places him at the first accession settlement: after [[WangYuXinPrince|王宇]] and [[WangHuoXinPrince|王获]] are dead, seniority would point to Wang An, but Wang Mang chooses Wang Lin instead and gives Wang An the compensatory Xin Jia Pi title.

Hanji 657 later places him in a correction of heir status: Wang Mang says Wang Ling's apparent crown-prince position is improper because Wang Ling is ill and has an elder brother, then names Wang An Xin Qian Wang. Hanji 658 supplies the reversal: after Wang Lin has died and [[LiuYingWangLinWife|刘英]] has killed herself, Wang An also dies of illness. His death means that Wang Mang's publicly known sons are gone, forcing the succession field toward private sons [[WangXingXinPrince|王兴]] and [[WangKuangXinPrince|王匡]].

## Key Characteristics

- Third son of Wang Mang in the episode's succession accounting.
- Bypassed heir candidate in the 9 CE accession settlement because Wang Mang prefers Wang Lin.
- Appeased with the Xin Jia Pi title after being skipped.
- Elder-brother successor figure restored over Wang Ling in Hanji 657's disaster-omen correction.
- Xin prince whose death is reported as illness rather than scandal punishment.
- Final public-son loss that turns household disaster into succession emergency.

- Succession effect: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-658-erzi-queshi-buyinggai-wangmang-nusha-taizi-ln4tu6ly8eie6jspvrurzn2rjjp|Hanji 658]] says Wang Mang's four public sons are all dead after Wang An's death.

## Qualifications

The page remains source-scoped because the episodes provide only Wang An's succession title role, bypass, illness, and succession consequence, not a full biography. Hanji 634's characterization of him as dull is preserved as source judgment rather than independent diagnosis.

- Added Hanji 634's earlier bypass-and-appeasement layer, where Wang An is skipped for Wang Lin despite seniority after Wang Yu and Wang Huo's deaths.
- Preserved Hanji 657's succession-correction layer and Hanji 658's death notice.
