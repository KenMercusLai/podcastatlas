## Overview

Wang An / 王安 is [[WangMang|王莽]]'s son whose late-[[XinDynasty|新朝]] succession role is clarified across Hanji 657 and Hanji 658. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-657-wangmang-fei-taizi-you-duo-erxi-lklx1rd39kgz-by4tbcpaagx4z-l|Hanji 657]] shows him restored over [[WangLingXinPrince|王陵]] as Xin Qian Wang after Wang Mang reads disaster as proof of wrong heir naming; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-658-erzi-queshi-buyinggai-wangmang-nusha-taizi-ln4tu6ly8eie6jspvrurzn2rjjp|Hanji 658]] then says his illness death completes the loss of Wang Mang's publicly recognized sons.

## Current Profile

The sources give Wang An a narrow but important succession role. Hanji 657 places him in a correction of heir status: Wang Mang says Wang Ling's apparent crown-prince position is improper because Wang Ling is ill and has an elder brother, then names Wang An Xin Qian Wang. Hanji 658 supplies the reversal: after [[WangLinXinCrownPrince|王林]] has died and [[LiuYingWangLinWife|刘英]] has killed herself, Wang An also dies of illness. His death means that Wang Mang's publicly known sons are gone, forcing the succession field toward private sons [[WangXingXinPrince|王兴]] and [[WangKuangXinPrince|王匡]].

## Key Characteristics

- Third son of Wang Mang in the episode's succession accounting.
- Elder-brother successor figure restored over Wang Ling in Hanji 657's disaster-omen correction.
- Xin prince whose death is reported as illness rather than scandal punishment.
- Final public-son loss that turns household disaster into succession emergency.

- Seniority argument: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-657-wangmang-fei-taizi-you-duo-erxi-lklx1rd39kgz-by4tbcpaagx4z-l|Hanji 657]] says Wang Mang invokes Wang Ling's elder brother as part of the claim that the earlier arrangement was improper.
- Death notice: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-658-erzi-queshi-buyinggai-wangmang-nusha-taizi-ln4tu6ly8eie6jspvrurzn2rjjp|Hanji 658]] says Wang An dies of illness in the same month.
- Succession effect: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-658-erzi-queshi-buyinggai-wangmang-nusha-taizi-ln4tu6ly8eie6jspvrurzn2rjjp|Hanji 658]] says Wang Mang's four public sons are all dead after Wang An's death.
- Contrast with Wang Lin: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-658-erzi-queshi-buyinggai-wangmang-nusha-taizi-ln4tu6ly8eie6jspvrurzn2rjjp|Hanji 658]] places Wang An's illness after Wang Lin's forced-death scandal, showing different paths to the same succession collapse.
