# Arthur (The Residency founder)

Arthur is one of the resident founders interviewed in [[tanfang-hacker-house-guigu-nianqingren-zhengzai-banjin-ai-chuangye-sushe-s10e10-f0870843-fd16-49f5-8f24-80281f9fff77]]. The source identifies him as Ukrainian, an AI master's graduate, and a serial founder who previously created Colab Venture Studio and organized data-science volunteers around COVID-19 and the Ukraine war.

Arthur's source role is fundraising efficiency. He chooses [[TheResidency]] because investor meetings can be organized into a concentrated time and place, making him a concrete example of [[FounderNetworkArbitrage]] and [[StartupPitchCompression]] for founders entering the [[SanFranciscoBayArea]] network.
