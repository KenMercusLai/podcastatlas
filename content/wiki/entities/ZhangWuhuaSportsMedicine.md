# 张五花 / Zhang Wuhua (sports medicine doctor)

## Overview
张五花 is presented as an orthopedic joint and sports-medicine doctor in a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode on winter sport, knee and ankle injury, joint stability, and rehabilitation.

## Current Profile
The source presents Zhang Wuhua as a clinician whose main areas include anterior cruciate-ligament and meniscal injury as well as shoulder and rotator-cuff injury. She explains joint anatomy through function: cruciate ligaments limit abnormal forward and backward translation, menisci and collateral structures can be injured by twisting, and muscle capacity can partly support stability without restoring damaged tissue.

Her advice is organized around prevention, response, and escalation. Prevention uses warmup, appropriate equipment, capacity-matched movement, and progressive training. Acute response prioritizes immobilization and prevention of secondary injury before ice or transport. Longer-term advice distinguishes strength and rehabilitation from diagnosis, medication, surgery, or supplement claims.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains knee, ankle, shoulder, and cartilage problems through stability, loading, and injury mechanism.
- Emphasizes warmup, protective equipment, appropriate range, and gradual skill progression.
- Prioritizes immobilization and prevention of secondary injury after acute trauma.
- Uses pain, swelling, function, instability, examination, and imaging to separate ordinary symptoms from structural injury.
- Treats quadriceps work as supportive infrastructure for knee stability and rehabilitation.
- Keeps supplements and food claims separate from established treatment.
