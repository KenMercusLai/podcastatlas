# 钟繇 / Zhong Yao

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-968-jiemi-sanguo-zui-lihai-de-yangmou-lg4qfkdgw-stixrebhjdoshil6yj|Hanji 968]] adds Zhong Yao inside the first court-contact attempt after [[CaoCao|曹操]] adopts [[MaoJie|毛玠]]'s "奉天子" strategy. When [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] suspect Cao Cao's envoy, Zhong Yao argues that Cao Cao had already fought after Dong Zhuo burned Luoyang and that rejecting him would push eastern powers away from the Han court.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-969-yuanshi-xiongdi-neidou-shui-neng-jianlou-lsgj6sewo35jpsgfm-koivijtr6r|Hanji 969]] then gives the result of this persuasion before the later Guanzhong stabilization branch. Here Zhong Yao is the Yingchuan-linked official who persuades [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] to stop blocking Cao Cao's appointment and to return formal recognition plus horses to [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]]. The source uses him as an early case of adviser selection under disorder: cultural judgment, elite networks, and force-backed court titles are already being converted into camp choice.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] adds Zhong Yao as one name in [[XunYu|荀彧]]'s wider personnel-referral chain. The source does not develop Zhong Yao's Guanzhong work; it uses the recommendation list to show how adviser value can include building a durable talent network for [[CaoCao|曹操]].

钟繇 / Zhong Yao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] as the official [[XunYu|荀彧]] recommends for stabilizing [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] while [[CaoCao|曹操]] turns attention eastward. Cao Cao gives him broad authority over the region's military affairs, and Zhong Yao works from [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] through documents that explain the interests of submission.

The source's emphasis is on persuasion under fragmented military conditions. [[MaTeng|马腾]], [[HanSui|韩遂]], and other western leaders are not presented as already loyal or as ready to become one offensive bloc; Zhong Yao's role is to make submission and hostage-sending look safer than joining Yuan Shao's possible western pressure.
