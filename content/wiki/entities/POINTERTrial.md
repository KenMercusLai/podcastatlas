# POINTER Trial

## Overview
POINTER is the U.S. multidomain dementia-prevention trial named in [[accelerate-learning-increase-cognitive-capacity-dr-tommy-wood-scim4201272551]].

## Current Profile
Within the source, POINTER is used as a major example of dementia-prevention research that combines diet, exercise, cardiovascular-risk monitoring and treatment, and brain training. Its role is to make prevention operational: dementia risk is treated as a system of cognitive, vascular, metabolic, sensory, and activity inputs rather than as a single late-stage disease target.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents U.S. multidomain dementia-prevention research.
- Combines diet, exercise, cardiovascular-risk monitoring and treatment, and brain training in the source description.
- Reinforces the idea that prevention can start before clinical dementia rather than waiting for end-stage impairment.
