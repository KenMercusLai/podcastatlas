# Suyash Pasi

Suyash Pasi appears in [[tech-20251223-1223-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251223-1223-mp-tech-pod-128]] as a research analyst and editor at the [[HumanRightsResearchCenter|Human Rights Research Center]]. In the [[MarketplaceTech]] interview, he explains the 2025 alignment between major technology leaders and the [[DonaldTrump]] administration through incentives rather than a single ideology.

Pasi's contribution is to separate the committed [[TechnologyRightPowerNetwork|tech right]] from technology leaders who may be accommodating the administration for access, deregulation, visas, or government business. He treats AI deregulation, crypto, immigration, and military technology as linked surfaces where federal power can reward or punish companies differently.
