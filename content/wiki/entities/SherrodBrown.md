# Sherrod Brown

Sherrod Brown appears in [[tsr-s5-ronconway-v5-tsr-s5-ronconway-v5]] as a key Senate actor during the [[SiliconValleyBank]] weekend. [[RonConway]] says Senator Mark Kelly connected him with Brown, and that he explained why SVB's failure was not only a wealthy-VC problem but a startup, small-business, and payroll-risk problem.

The Sunday call is the source's strongest Brown moment. Conway says he warned Brown that there were only hours left before the Tokyo market open and urged him to call Biden, Jeff Zients, [[JanetYellen]], and [[JeromePowell]] to push for a deposit guarantee.
