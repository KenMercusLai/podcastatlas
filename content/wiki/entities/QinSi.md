# 秦思 / Qin Si

秦思 / Qin Si is a [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] reporter and [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] speaker in [[xianchang-xianchang-2025-nianzhong-teji-sanlian-nvjizhe-jiemi-xinwen-beihou-de-ku-yu-guang-loz-sggelhx166n2me8v6d8k9a3c]]. Her role in the episode is to reject a narrow image of investigative reporters as necessarily aggressive, rough, or "江湖气", and to show how persistence, social-science training, and field contact can also produce hard reporting.

The source gives Qin Si two main branches. First, she turns [[FemaleJournalistFieldwork|female journalist fieldwork]] into a practical method question: women reporters may sometimes lower defensiveness in interviews or covert investigation, but they still need preparation, risk judgment, and the ability to enter difficult scenes. Second, she links [[FieldworkAsKnowledgeMethod|fieldwork as knowledge method]] to journalism, saying that reporting let her move from academic training toward real people, domestic scenes, and first-hand information.

Her discussion of the Hunan Leishui heavy-metal pollution reporting adds [[ReporterSourceTrustNetwork|reporter-source trust networks]] to the wiki. Local environmental volunteers helped her reach remote mountain areas, interpret the river's changes, and understand why a simple official water-quality statement missed residents who did not drink tap water.
