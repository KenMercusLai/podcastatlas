# 秦思 / Qin Si

## Overview
秦思 / Qin Si is a [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] reporter and [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] speaker whose wiki profile centers on field contact, social-science reasoning, and the practical work of turning real scenes into public knowledge.

## Current Profile
Across the bounded sources, Qin Si rejects a narrow image of reporting as masculine aggression or purely desk-based analysis. Her reporting and commentary emphasize preparation, patience, source trust, field entry, and the humility to let real scenes correct inherited frames. EP280 extends that profile from journalism into art education: she reads Guangzhou art students' fieldwork as a process of awakening curiosity, learning interview technique, and unlearning concept-first routines.

## Key Characteristics
- Qin Si treats fieldwork as a disciplined way to meet people, test assumptions, and produce grounded understanding.
- She resists stereotypes that equate investigation with aggression, arguing for persistence, social-science reasoning, and careful evidence chains.
- She is attentive to the practical mechanics of contact: preparation, translators or helpers, wording, trust, and the emotional conditions of an interview.
- In the art-education source, she frames the hardest work as "unlearn" rather than simply acquiring another method.
- Her public role in these sources links journalism and education through the same question: how people learn to see beyond inherited concepts.
