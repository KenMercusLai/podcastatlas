## Overview

《明报》 / Ming Pao is the Hong Kong newspaper founded by [[JinYong|金庸]] whose early operation in this source overlapped with [[ZhuMei|朱玫]]'s reporting, management, and family labor.

## Current Profile

The marriage episode uses Ming Pao less as a complete institutional history than as the setting in which Jin Yong and Zhu Mei's professional partnership and conflict became inseparable from family life. Zhu is described as an early female reporter who also participated in management and related publications. A recurring story says she sold jewelry or dowry property to keep the paper operating, but the episode does not treat that claim as fully verified.

Jin Yong's own reported account emphasized fiction fees and royalties as startup capital and included the assertion that his wife had no say in the decision. The source interprets that statement as evidence that a major entrepreneurial commitment may not have rested on full marital agreement, even while both spouses worked in the enterprise.

## Key Characteristics

- Hong Kong newspaper founded by Jin Yong.
- Early workplace in which publishing labor and family partnership overlapped.
- Received reporting and operational work from Zhu Mei.
- Became part of disputed narratives about startup finance, sacrifice, and marital decision-making.
