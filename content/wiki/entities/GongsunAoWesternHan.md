## Overview

公孙敖 / Gongsun Ao enters the wiki through Hanji 380-1 as the Yinwu General sent from Yanmen during [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s large anti-[[Xiongnu|匈奴]] expedition. In this source, he is a supporting example of how the campaign's scale does not translate into decisive success.

## Current Profile

The episode says Gongsun Ao leads cavalry and infantry from Yanmen to attack Xiongnu, fights the Left Worthy King, loses, and withdraws. His role is campaign-contextual: his defeat helps prepare the narrative atmosphere for Li Ling's later isolated battle and surrender.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Yinwu General in the episode's campaign list.
- Leads cavalry and infantry from Yanmen against Xiongnu.
- Loses against the Xiongnu Left Worthy King and withdraws.
