## Overview

公孙敖 / Gongsun Ao is a Western Han general whose repeated command, defeat, punishment, survival, and final destruction make him both a participant in [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s anti-[[Xiongnu|匈奴]] wars and an agent in [[LiLingWesternHan|李陵]]'s family tragedy. The sources move him from a campaign-list supporting figure to a case where self-protective reporting and repeated pursuit of military rank carry consequences far beyond the failed commander.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-380-1-liling-touxiang-xiongnu-ruhe-yanbian-cheng-gengdade-beiju-ls70jnzybc7z3of-tpfbdwb9ns89|Hanji 380-1]] places Gongsun Ao in a large multi-route expedition. As Yinwu General, he leads cavalry and infantry from Yanmen, loses to the Xiongnu Left Worthy King, and withdraws; his failure helps establish the inconclusive campaign environment before Li Ling's isolated defeat.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-380-2-liling-sandai-zhongliang-weihe-yidai-bi-yidai-can-lmtnhwjsyzlsfwpzcw18lubbauu0|Hanji 380-2]] gives him a more consequential role. Wudi sends him into Xiongnu territory to find Li Ling, but after returning without success he reports captive testimony that Li Ling is teaching Xiongnu troops and making weapons. The episode presents this report as the immediate trigger for Wudi killing Li Ling's family, then says the accusation actually concerned [[LiXuWesternHan|李绪]].

The same source compresses Gongsun Ao's career into repeated appointment and jeopardy: multiple generalships, three death sentences, another costly defeat, escape by feigned death, years as a fugitive, recapture, and eventual execution with his household after his wife is implicated in a Wugu case. The host interprets this pattern through [[DesireCapacityFit|欲望与能力匹配]], but inability is an episode-level moral reading rather than a complete military assessment.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han general repeatedly entrusted with anti-Xiongnu command despite costly or unsuccessful outcomes.
- Defeated Yanmen-route commander in Hanji 380-1's large multi-route campaign.
- Failed retrieval commander whose report about Li Ling triggers family punishment in Hanji 380-2.
- Self-preserving actor whose report is later reframed by the episode as mistaken attribution involving Li Xu.
- Repeatedly condemned, reprieved or escaped, restored, and exposed again before final household destruction.
- Source-scoped example of ambition for rank outrunning ability, judgment, and self-knowledge.

## Qualifications

These sources give a compressed popular-history career rather than a complete chronology of Gongsun Ao's commands, convictions, pardons, offices, escape, or Wugu case. Hanji 380-2 presents his report as self-protective blame transfer, but does not reproduce the full testimony or legal process. The host's conclusion that insufficient ability, not divided loyalty, explains his fate is an interpretive lesson and should not replace fuller military and political evaluation.

## What Changed

- Expanded Gongsun Ao from a defeated supporting commander into the failed retrieval agent whose report precipitates Li Ling's family destruction.
- Added the repeated-command, death-sentence, escape, recapture, and Wugu-linked household-destruction arc.
- Qualified the host's desire-versus-ability judgment as a source-scoped interpretation rather than a settled biography.
