# 李卫 / Li Wei

李卫 / Li Wei is the Qing official who searches the [[LuLiuliang|吕留良]] household in [[11-yongzheng-wangchao-zhi-dayi-juemi-shijingqian-bi-xia-de-qiangu-qi-an-547265513]]. The episode presents his operation as theatrical and investigative: he posed as an admirer buying books, entered the family book space, and then seized people, diaries, manuscripts, and printed material.

Li Wei's role shifts the [[ZengJing|曾静]] case from a letter and interrogation into a broader [[QingLiteraryInquisition]]. The direct link between the Lu family and Zeng Jing was thin, but the search made books, students, descendants, and Zhejiang literary networks usable evidence for [[YongzhengEmperor|雍正]]'s political interpretation of the case.

## Connections
- [[LuLiuliang|吕留良]] - household and writings searched.

- [[YongzhengEmperor|雍正]] - ruler to whom the case was escalated.
