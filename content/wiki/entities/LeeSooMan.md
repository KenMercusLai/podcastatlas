# 李秀满 / Lee Soo-man

## Overview
[[LeeSooMan|李秀满]] is presented as a singer-turned-company founder, planner, and producer whose accumulated K-pop system informs [[A2OLight]].

## Current Profile
The episode describes a coordination system combining experimental music, complete songs, group-first visuals, explicit performance roles, fictional worlds, multinational lineups, subunits, member recombination, and virtual-real technology. A2O Light is interpreted as post-SM reuse of this grammar. Continuity can create recognition and coherence, but timing, performer ability, audience mood, later repertoire, and chance remain necessary.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses a system-level rather than song-only approach to idol creation.
- Links group identity, roles, choreography, concepts, and fictional worlds.
- Reuses methods associated in the episode with Super Junior, EXO, NCT, and aespa.
- Treats multinational casting and cross-group design as market and narrative infrastructure.
- Raises execution quality without eliminating dependence on repertoire, performers, timing, or chance.
