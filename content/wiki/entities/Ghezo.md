# Ghezo

## Overview

Ghezo is the Dahomey king under whom the episode says the [[Agojie]] became a more formal fighting unit.

## Current Profile

In this source, Ghezo is a supporting but important ruler because he marks the transition from palace bodyguard to more formalized female military force. The episode says the number of women warriors reportedly grew from about 800 to around 3,000 under him. His current wiki role is therefore tied to institutionalization of [[Dahomey]]'s women warriors rather than to a full reign profile.

## Key Characteristics

- Dahomey king named in the source.
- Associated with formalizing the [[Agojie]] as a fighting unit.
- Linked to a reported growth in women-warrior numbers.
- Part of the episode's distinction between palace role, military institution, and European spectacle.
