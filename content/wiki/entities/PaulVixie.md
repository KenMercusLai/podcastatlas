# Paul Vixie

Paul Vixie appears in [[tech-20260128-0128-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260128-0128-mp-tech-pod-128]] as an early internet innovator who walks [[MarketplaceTech]] host [[MeganMcCartyCorino|Megan McCarty-Carino]] through Menlo Park telecom infrastructure. The source says he helped build the [[PaloAltoInternetExchange|Palo Alto Internet Exchange]] and dot-com-era fiber networks, and now works as a vice president at [[AmazonWebServices|AWS Security]].

Vixie's interpretive role is more important than his current title. He argues that the fiber buildout should not be reduced to a simple glut: companies failed, but the installed capacity later became useful infrastructure for search, social media, streaming, cryptocurrency, and AI. That makes him a source voice for [[ProductiveBubbleSpillovers]], [[DarkFiber]], and the [[TechnologyInstallationCycle]] branch of the wiki.
