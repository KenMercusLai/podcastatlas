# James Craig

James Craig appears in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as Northern Ireland's first prime minister. The episode uses his "Protestant parliament" language to show that [[Stormont]] was not experienced by many Catholics as a neutral regional government.

Craig's role anchors the source's account of [[InstitutionalSectarianDiscrimination]]: Protestant dominance in government, policing, courts, civil service, and unionist associational life helped make Catholic distrust of the state structurally predictable.
