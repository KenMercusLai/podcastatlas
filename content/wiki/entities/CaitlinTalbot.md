# Caitlin Talbot

Caitlin Talbot appears in [[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]] as the [[TheIntelligence]] contributor walking through London in search of [[Invader]] mosaics. Her segment uses Covent Garden, James Street, and Maiden Lane to show how [[FlashInvaders]] changes ordinary urban attention.

The wiki role is a culture-and-interface case. Talbot's walk turns [[StreetArtGameLayer]] from an abstract app concept into a physical search practice: players use phones to score points, but the activity rewards noticing small public-art details in the city.
