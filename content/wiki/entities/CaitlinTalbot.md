# Caitlin Talbot

[[its-not-easy-being-green-zack-polanski-6a82d1a0ff328abd84724cf0]] adds Talbot in a travel-and-AI role. Her segment argues that human advisers remain useful for high-stakes or complex travel because they provide taste, curation, supplier relationships, VIP perks, and support when trips fail, making [[TravelAgentResilience]] a case of [[HumanJudgmentUnderAI]].

[[taken-littorally-spains-sudden-crisis-in-ceuta-6a70712c034f16a52ebfaed7]] adds Talbot as the contributor explaining why [[AIWritingDetection]] is becoming harder. Her segment contrasts opaque detector tools such as [[Pangram]] with closer stylistic comparison of human and chatbot prose from [[ChatGPT]], [[Claude]], [[Gemini]], and [[Grok]].

Caitlin Talbot appears in [[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]] as the [[TheIntelligence]] contributor walking through London in search of [[Invader]] mosaics. Her segment uses Covent Garden, James Street, and Maiden Lane to show how [[FlashInvaders]] changes ordinary urban attention.

The wiki role is a culture-and-interface case. Talbot's walk turns [[StreetArtGameLayer]] from an abstract app concept into a physical search practice: players use phones to score points, but the activity rewards noticing small public-art details in the city.
