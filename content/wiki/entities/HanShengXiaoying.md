# 韩晟（小影科技） / Han Sheng (Xiaoying Technology)

## Overview
韩晟 is the founder and CEO of [[XiaoyingTechnology|小影科技]], presented in the source as a practitioner moving consumer video products from editing and generation tools toward goal-directed creative agents. This page is semantically suffixed to distinguish him from [[HanSheng|韩生]], the Qin–Han adviser already represented in the wiki.

## Current Profile
His product thesis places responsibility for interpretation and professional judgment inside the agent: a user should be able to state an outcome while the system asks for missing information, proposes a direction, produces a first version, and helps refine it. He treats general agents and vertical agents as complementary, argues that proprietary model work needs either core-experience control or material cost advantage, and favors rapid market tests grounded in the team's accumulated capabilities.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames agents as accountable result-delivery systems rather than conversational wrappers around tools.
- Treats consumer creative intent as something discovered through drafts and revision, not captured completely in one prompt.
- Sees general agents as context-rich coordinators and vertical agents as specialist executors.
- Applies a core-experience and cost test to proprietary model investment.
- Emphasizes founder capability fit, rapid validation, and locally informed overseas expansion.
