# 即墨 / Jimo

即墨 / Jimo enters the wiki as a city through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-78-qiguo-mingyun-jing-bei-chengguan-gei-jiule-lpt3reyr6ok8g2lhcxrnd5qctyug]]. After [[YanState|燕国]] and [[LeYi|乐毅]] occupy most of [[QiState|齐国]], Jimo remains one of only two Qi-held cities, alongside [[JuWarringStates|莒城]].

The episode says Jimo's local official goes out to fight Yan and is killed. The people of Jimo then choose [[TianDan|田单]] as commander because his earlier cart-axle decision during flight from Anping showed practical judgment under crisis.

This page is separate from [[JimoDafu|即墨大夫]], the earlier official praised in Qi Wei Wang's administrative audit. In Zhouji 78, Jimo is a defensive city and future counterattack base, not the same official-evaluation example.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-79-shishang-yong-gengniu-da-shengzhang-diyi-ren-lsrl0posblloqwugox160xsicp2p]] turns that future counterattack into the city's defining event. Jimo becomes the place where Tian Dan stages divine-sign theater, hardens morale after [[QiJie|骑劫]]'s atrocities, performs a [[DeceptiveSurrenderLull|false surrender]], and launches the [[FireOxNightRaid|fire-ox night raid]] that breaks Yan's occupation of Qi.
