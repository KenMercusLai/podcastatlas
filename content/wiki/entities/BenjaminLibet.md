# Benjamin Libet / 利比特

Benjamin Libet is the neuroscientist behind the experiment discussed in [[ep256-ai-shidai-ziyou-yizhi-hai-cunzai-ma-lk9aci8oqnwerk26jy683nbdddcx]]. In the episode, [[TuMotuo|土摩托]] describes visiting Chapman University to experience a Libet-style button-press task: participants report when they consciously intended to press a button while brain activity is recorded.

Libet matters to the wiki because the episode treats him as evidence and warning at the same time. The readiness-potential result is important for [[FreeWill|free-will]] debates, but [[LibetExperimentInterpretation]] warns that random button pressing should not be stretched into a full account of meaningful decision, responsibility, or life direction.
