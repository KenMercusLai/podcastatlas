# AI Weirdness Blog

The Marketplace Tech episode uses Shane's explanations to turn foreign-language slips into [[ChatbotCodeSwitching]], [[ChatbotDomainBleedthrough]], and [[ChatbotSelfExplanationUncertainty]] rather than treating them as isolated anecdotes.
