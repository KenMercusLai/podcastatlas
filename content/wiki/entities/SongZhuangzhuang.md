# 宋壮壮 / Song Zhuangzhuang

## Overview

宋壮壮 is represented as a 地都会-affiliated urban observer who guides a cycling reading of Beijing's physical and policy changes over the preceding decade.

## Current Profile

In the bounded source, Song combines place memory, planning interpretation, and street-level observation. He reads individual sites neither as isolated attractions nor as unambiguous successes or failures: heritage repair can narrow public use, storefront control can displace rather than erase commerce, and ecological restoration can create valued public space while remaining a conservative response to uncertain land demand.

His method is comparative and spatial. Moving from Jingshan to Sanlitun lets him connect the 2016-era capital plan to visible changes in roads, bridges, hutongs, waterways, building projects, and commercial districts while preserving uncertainty about exact dates, intent, and causation.

## Key Characteristics

- Beijing urban observer using cycling and walking to connect planning policy with visible street change.
- Interpreter of heritage protection as a negotiation among conservation, mobility, tourism, and civic use.
- Attentive to the displacement and later recomposition of small commercial spaces after spatial governance.
- Advocate for ecology, greenery, pedestrian continuity, and public-space quality beyond traffic throughput.
- Skeptical of overconfident long-range forecasting while retaining a role for urban planning.

This is a single-episode, role-bounded profile, not a full biography or account of 地都会. The source relies substantially on field observation and recollection; dates, policy intent, and causal claims should not be treated as independently verified solely because Song presents them.
