# 北京东方新天地 / Beijing Oriental Plaza

Beijing Oriental Plaza appears in [[vol-270-da-shi-dai-liu-zai-le-ta-de-shi-dai-1006063835]] as the Wangfujing shopping-center location where the episode says Beijing's last [[FoodRepublic|大食代 / Food Republic]] closed on June 15. The source uses the basement food-court closure less as a full mall case and more as a visible signal that the old [[MallFoodCourtDecline|mall food-court]] model has lost its earlier inevitability.

The location matters because it shows how a once-useful tenant category can become replaceable inside a mature city-center mall. When landlords can lease restaurants directly, bring in chain brands, or redesign food areas themselves, the value of an external [[FoodCourtSecondLandlord|food-court second landlord]] falls.
