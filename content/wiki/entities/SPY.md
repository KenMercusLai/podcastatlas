# SPY / SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust

SPY is the [[SP500|S&P 500]] ETF case attached to [[StateStreet]] and [[NathanMost]] in the wiki's ETF history branch. [[vanguard-1]] uses SPDR as the product that made Vanguard's missed ETF opportunity visible after [[JohnBogle]] rejected the format.

[[vol-266-yi-ci-xing-gao-dong-etf-1002344828]] adds SPY as the first true U.S. ETF milestone in its simplified product-history account. The source uses SPY to show how [[ExchangeTradedFund|ETF]] design converted index-fund exposure into an exchange-traded security.

## Key Claims
- SPY made broad S&P 500 exposure tradeable like a listed security.
- The case distinguishes the index-investing idea from the ETF distribution and trading wrapper.
- SPY's success helped show why [[ETFCreationRedemption|creation-redemption]] could become infrastructure for much larger ETF markets.
