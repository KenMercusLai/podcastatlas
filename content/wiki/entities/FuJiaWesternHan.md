## Overview

傅嘉 / Fu Jia is synthesized as a late [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] attendant whose current wiki role spans both enabling [[DongXian|董贤]]'s fabricated merit and later becoming a target of [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]'s partial personnel correction. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-586-weile-aiqing-gudai-huangdi-you-duo-chongdong-lizfxc8a4do88e6vyrcupyet3vs|Hanji 586]] makes him the adviser who proposes altering the Dongping king Liu Yun accusation record so Dong Xian can appear meritorious. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-599-gan-dui-qianguize-shuo-bu-chengxiang-wangjia-you-duo-yong-lpptx-fzls9xbakw52b8n1n6xow0|Hanji 599]] later shows Aidi removing him after [[WangJiaWesternHan|王嘉]]'s death, accusing him of slandering worthy officials and causing senior talent to remain out of office.

## Current Profile

Hanji 586 places Fu Jia in the earlier Dong Xian reward problem. Seeing Aidi's wish to enfeoff Dong Xian, Fu Jia proposes revising the written record of Xifu Gong and Sun Chong's accusation against Liu Yun so Dong Xian appears among the meritorious actors. The source treats the proposal as procedural fabrication rather than discovery of real Dong Xian merit.

Hanji 599 places Fu Jia in Aidi's partial correction after Wang Jia's prison death. Aidi restores [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] and [[HeWuWesternHan|何武]], then removes Fu Jia on the charge that, while serving as shizhong, he maligned worthy men, falsely accused great ministers, and kept outstanding talent away from office for a long time.

The source does not make Fu Jia's independent motives visible. He matters because his dismissal shows Aidi recognizing some damage done by nearby ministers while still failing to change the deeper Dong Xian-centered court pattern.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han attendant close enough to influence Aidi's view of senior officials.
- Adviser who suggests altering a case record to supply Dong Xian with a merit basis for reward.
- Accused by Aidi of slandering worthy talent and falsely implicating great ministers.
- Removed and sent back to a distant commandery after Wang Jia's death.
- Functions as one sign of Aidi's partial but limited correction.
