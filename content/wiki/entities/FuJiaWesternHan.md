## Overview

傅嘉 / Fu Jia enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-599-gan-dui-qianguize-shuo-bu-chengxiang-wangjia-you-duo-yong-lpptx-fzls9xbakw52b8n1n6xow0|Hanji 599]] as an attendant whom [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] removes after [[WangJiaWesternHan|王嘉]]'s death, accusing him of slandering worthy officials and causing senior talent to remain out of office.

## Current Profile

Hanji 599 places Fu Jia in Aidi's partial correction after Wang Jia's prison death. Aidi restores [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] and [[HeWuWesternHan|何武]], then removes Fu Jia on the charge that, while serving as shizhong, he maligned worthy men, falsely accused great ministers, and kept outstanding talent away from office for a long time.

The source does not make Fu Jia's independent motives visible. He matters because his dismissal shows Aidi recognizing some damage done by nearby ministers while still failing to change the deeper Dong Xian-centered court pattern.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han attendant close enough to influence Aidi's view of senior officials.
- Accused by Aidi of slandering worthy talent and falsely implicating great ministers.
- Removed and sent back to a distant commandery after Wang Jia's death.
- Functions as one sign of Aidi's partial but limited correction.
