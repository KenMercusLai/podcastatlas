## Overview

赵绾 / Zhao Wan is an early-Wudi Confucian-aligned official whose proposal to stop reporting major court decisions to the Eastern Palace directly challenges [[EmpressDowagerDouWesternHan|窦太后]]'s governing influence and ends in investigation and death.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Zhao Wan as 御史大夫 and a supporter of [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s Confucian program. His decisive act is institutional rather than doctrinal: he privately recommends that major state decisions no longer be reported to the Eastern Palace. The proposal would convert an ideological dispute with Dowager Dou into a practical loss of review and veto power.

Dowager Dou responds by ordering the investigation of Zhao and [[WangZangWesternHan|王臧]]. Wudi then stops the Mingtang project and hands them over for judgment; both reportedly die by suicide. The episode suspects Zhao acted with imperial encouragement, but the private authorization is not established by the supplied text.

## Key Characteristics

- Confucian-aligned official identified as 御史大夫.
- Proposes ending Eastern Palace review of major state decisions.
- Converts a philosophical disagreement into a direct institutional challenge.
- Investigated with Wang Zang after Dowager Dou learns of the proposal.
- Dies after Wudi retreats from confrontation and abandons the reform line.

## Qualifications

The supplied text writes 赵婉; this page normalizes the name to 赵绾 where the historical referent is clear. The episode does not independently establish the exact wording of the proposal, whether Zhao acted on Wudi's instruction, the charges, prison sequence, or the degree of voluntariness in the reported suicide. Calling the move a “test” or Zhao a discarded pawn remains interpretation rather than settled motive.
