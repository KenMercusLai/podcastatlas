# 《比特本位》 / The Bit Standard

《比特本位》 / The Bit Standard is the book by [[ZhouLuohua|周洛华]] discussed in [[jifa-dongwu-jingshen-chuangzao-gengduo-jihui-lndyfdmtgo-9l5cr8oy82t1xnlfe]]. The episode says the revised version substantially changes the original book, especially the later sections, and is meant to treat [[Bitcoin]] inside monetary history rather than as only an investment object.

The book's source-scoped role is to connect [[ProofOfWorkMonetaryValidation|proof of work]], gold coinage, fiat money, capital markets, AI agents, and social production relations. It becomes the episode's container for arguing that monetary systems should be judged by whether they reduce risk and create opportunity.
