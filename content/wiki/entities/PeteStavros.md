# Pete Stavros

Pete Stavros is the [[KKR]] figure at the center of [[a-pro-worker-experiment-in-private-equity]]. The episode says his interest in worker ownership came partly from his father's experience as a union road grader in Chicago, where workers and management fought over paid time, lunch breaks, and travel time.

At KKR, Pete treated private equity as a test environment for [[PrivateEquityWorkerOwnership]]. [[CapitalSafety]] became the first experiment, but he later called KKR's communication there an "F" because workers such as [[CindyCordes]] did not understand the equity before the company was sold to [[ThreeM|3M]].

The stronger case in the episode is [[GSI]], where workers were told about ownership from the start. Pete argues that the model has reached 85 companies and more than 190,000 workers, but he also says outcomes vary and appear to depend heavily on leadership quality, empathy, and trust-building.
