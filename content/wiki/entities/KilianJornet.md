# Kilian Jornet

## Overview
Kilian Jornet appears as the trail runner whose race performance helped validate and popularize Salomon's vest-shaped hydration pack.

## Current Profile
In [[vol-274-chengwei-saluomeng-1013268300]], Jornet is a bridge between elite athlete proof, specialist equipment development, and wider product adoption. The source says that at age 20 he won the 2008 UTMB in record time while wearing an improved Salomon hydration vest, making the equipment visible as a solution to trail running's need for close fit, ventilation, low weight, water, and supplies.
