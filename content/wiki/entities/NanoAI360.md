# Nano AI / 纳米 AI

## Overview
Nano AI / 纳米 AI is the [[Qihoo360|360]] application-layer AI product described by [[ZhouHongyi|周鸿祎]] in [[lhg2boyb8begpx0hyq-n6qur47ks-lhg2boyb8begpx0hyq-n6qur47ks]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Nano AI as an evolving product rather than a fixed chatbot. It moved among AI search, AI browser, access to multiple domestic models, model routing, natural-language agent construction, and multi-agent workflows. Its strategic role is to test 360's decision to build applications, vertical models, and an agent factory instead of competing directly in the largest general-model training race.

## Key Characteristics
- Application-layer product spanning AI search, browser assistance, and agent workflows.
- Multi-model interface that routes work according to task and model strengths.
- Agent-building surface intended to lower construction barriers through natural language and reusable components.
- Product under rapid iteration whose exact scope and capability remain source-scoped.

## Evidence
- Product evolution: [[lhg2boyb8begpx0hyq-n6qur47ks-lhg2boyb8begpx0hyq-n6qur47ks]] says Nano AI repeatedly adjusted among AI search, browser, and application scenarios.
- Model aggregation and routing: [[lhg2boyb8begpx0hyq-n6qur47ks-lhg2boyb8begpx0hyq-n6qur47ks]] attributes access to many domestic models and task-sensitive routing to 360's product strategy.
- Agent construction: [[lhg2boyb8begpx0hyq-n6qur47ks-lhg2boyb8begpx0hyq-n6qur47ks]] describes natural-language, component-based, and multi-agent planning routes for assembling new capabilities.

## Qualifications
The page is grounded in one founder interview rather than an independent product evaluation. Model counts, feature breadth, output quality, adoption, pricing, and competitive advantage are not independently verified here, and rapid product iteration may make the described scope time-bound.
