# John Hawkins

John Hawkins appears in [[693-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-englands-greatest-hero-part-3-glt5069845329]] as [[FrancisDrake]]'s relative and an English captain deeply involved in Atlantic slaving. The episode says Hawkins launched what the hosts call the first English slaving voyage in 1562 and that Drake definitely joined Hawkins's third slaving expedition in 1567.

The source uses Hawkins to make Drake's early career morally and commercially explicit. The expedition mixes Protestant identity, patriotism, greed, violence, and illegal access to Iberian-controlled markets, making Hawkins central to [[AtlanticSlaveTradePrivateeringLink]].

Hawkins's fleet is then broken at [[SanJuanDeUlua]] in 1568. He and Drake escape, but the losses of ships, sailors, prisoners, and money help turn failed trade into anti-Spanish grievance and push Drake toward raiding as a more profitable strategy.
