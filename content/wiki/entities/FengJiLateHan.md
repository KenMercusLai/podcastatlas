# 逢纪 / Feng Ji (late Han)

## Overview
逢纪 / Feng Ji (late Han) is a [[YuanShao|袁绍]] adviser whose bounded wiki profile now runs from Jizhou personnel-list presence to a morally mixed post-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] role: he slanders [[TianFengLateHan|田丰]], defends [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] despite private hostility, then helps install [[YuanShang|袁尚]] after Yuan Shao's death and is killed by [[YuanTan|袁谭]].

## Current Profile
Feng Ji enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] as one of the advisers associated with Yuan Shao after the takeover of [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]]. That source uses his name in the same personnel field as [[JuShou|沮授]], Shen Pei, Tian Feng, [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]], and [[XunChenLateHan|荀谌]], mainly to show that Yuan Shao's post-Jizhou position includes an expanded adviser roster.

Hanji 1050 gives Feng Ji an active, morally mixed role after Yuan Shao's defeat at Guandu. When Yuan Shao regrets not listening to Tian Feng, Feng Ji claims Tian Feng laughed at the defeat because his prediction came true; Yuan then orders Tian Feng killed. The same source later shows a different side: after Shen Pei's two sons are captured by Cao Cao and other Yuan-camp figures suspect him, Feng Ji says his private quarrel with Shen Pei should not determine a public matter and defends Shen Pei's uprightness.

Hanji 1056 shifts Feng Ji from post-defeat adviser testimony into succession action. Because Feng Ji and Shen Pei both fear that Yuan Tan would retaliate against them if he inherited, they claim Yuan Shao left a final order and install Yuan Shang before Yuan Tan returns for mourning. Yuan Shang then gives Yuan Tan only limited troops and sends Feng Ji to supervise. When Shen Pei's side refuses Yuan Tan's request for more troops, Yuan Tan kills Feng Ji, making Feng Ji's death the first concrete bloodshed in the brothers' succession split.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan Shao adviser first visible as part of the Jizhou-side personnel cluster.
- Source-bounded figure whose independent career remains mostly undeveloped in the current wiki.
- Post-Guandu slander actor whose claim about Tian Feng helps turn correct advice into a death sentence.
- Post-Guandu integrity witness who defends Shen Pei despite private hostility.
- Succession-faction actor who helps turn Yuan Shao's private preference for Yuan Shang into a claimed final-order transfer.
- Supervisory delegate whose presence with Yuan Tan becomes the immediate target of Yuan Tan's resentment.

## Qualifications
The current evidence does not provide Feng Ji's full biography or a complete account of his factional ties. Hanji 1050 makes his profile internally mixed: the same person appears as destructive toward Tian Feng and fair toward Shen Pei. Hanji 1056 explains his Yuan Shang alignment through fear of Yuan Tan's retaliation, but the authenticity of the claimed final order remains source-scoped. The transcript renders his name as "庞季/庞纪" in some Hanji 1050 passages; the wiki normalizes to 逢纪 / Feng Ji because the surrounding Yuan Shao adviser sequence matches the established historical role and existing page.
