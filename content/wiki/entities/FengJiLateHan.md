# 逢纪 / Feng Ji (late Han)

## Overview
逢纪 / Feng Ji (late Han) is a [[YuanShao|袁绍]] adviser whose bounded wiki profile now runs from Jizhou personnel-list presence to a sharper post-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] contrast: he helps destroy [[TianFengLateHan|田丰]] through slander but later defends [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] by separating private hostility from public judgment.

## Current Profile
Feng Ji enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] as one of the advisers associated with Yuan Shao after the takeover of [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]]. That source uses his name in the same personnel field as [[JuShou|沮授]], [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]], [[TianFengLateHan|田丰]], [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]], and [[XunChenLateHan|荀谌]], mainly to show that Yuan Shao's post-Jizhou position includes an expanded adviser roster.

Hanji 1050 gives Feng Ji an active, morally mixed role after Yuan Shao's defeat at Guandu. When Yuan Shao regrets not listening to Tian Feng, Feng Ji claims Tian Feng laughed at the defeat because his prediction came true; Yuan then orders Tian Feng killed. The same source later shows a different side: after Shen Pei's two sons are captured by Cao Cao and other Yuan-camp figures suspect him, Feng Ji tells Yuan that his private quarrel with Shen Pei should not determine a public matter and defends Shen Pei's uprightness. Feng Ji's current profile is therefore not simply malicious or loyal; it is an adviser-case in how private rivalry, public judgment, and ruler susceptibility interact after defeat.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan Shao adviser first visible as part of the Jizhou-side personnel cluster.
- Source-bounded figure whose independent career remains mostly undeveloped in the current wiki.
- Post-Guandu slander actor whose claim about Tian Feng helps turn correct advice into a death sentence.
- Post-Guandu integrity witness who defends Shen Pei despite private hostility.
- Example of adviser influence depending on the ruler's susceptibility to shame, suspicion, and selective testimony.

## Qualifications
The current evidence does not provide Feng Ji's full biography or a complete account of his factional ties. Hanji 1050 makes his profile internally mixed: the same person appears as destructive toward Tian Feng and fair toward Shen Pei. The transcript renders his name as "庞季/庞纪" in the Shen Pei and Tian Feng passages; the wiki normalizes to 逢纪 / Feng Ji because the surrounding Yuan Shao adviser sequence matches the established historical role and existing page.
