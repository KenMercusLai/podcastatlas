# 逢纪 / Feng Ji (late Han)

逢纪 / Feng Ji (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] as one of the advisers associated with [[YuanShao|袁绍]] after the takeover of [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]]. The source uses his name in the same personnel field as [[JuShou|沮授]], [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]], [[TianFengLateHan|田丰]], [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]], and [[XunChenLateHan|荀谌]].

The page is deliberately narrow. Hanji 957 does not narrate Feng Ji's independent career; it uses him to show that Yuan Shao's post-Jizhou position includes an expanded adviser roster, while Han Fu's earlier failure is partly a failure to recognize and use people.
