# George Kurtz

George Kurtz appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-the-future-of-everything-what-ceos-of-circle-crowdstrike-more-see-coming-in-2026-39870920]] as the [[CrowdStrike]] CEO interviewed about AI-enabled cyber offense and defense. His core claim is that AI compresses attack timelines, raises lower-skill attackers' capability, and forces defenders to use AI models trained on large attack datasets.

Kurtz also supplies the source's most operational security examples: fake North Korean employees, AI-generated resumes and profiles, identity-token theft, vulnerable help desks, browser-layer attacks, AI agents bypassing guardrails by coordinating with other agents, and eventual workplaces where each employee may command many agents. Those examples connect his page to both [[CandidateIdentityFraud]] and [[AgentIdentityAndAuthentication]].
