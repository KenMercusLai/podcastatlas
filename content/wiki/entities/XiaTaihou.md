# 夏姬 / 夏太后 / Lady Xia

夏姬 / 夏太后 / Lady Xia enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-101-1-zhe-wei-daomei-junwang-dengji-santian-ji-cusi-lll-bcc7basaf6-nzwqtmcb5qw6w]]. After [[QinXiaowenwang|秦孝文王]] dies and [[QinZhuangxiangwang|子楚 / 秦庄襄王]] succeeds, the episode says Zichu honors his biological mother Xia Ji as 夏太后.

Her source role is positional but important. [[HuayangFuren|华阳夫人]] remains the adoptive, status-making mother whose line made Zichu's succession possible through [[MaternalStatusSuccessionLeverage|子以母贵式继承杠杆]], while Xia Ji marks the biological maternal line that is also ritually elevated once Zichu becomes king.

## Connections
- [[QinZhuangxiangwang|异人 / 子楚 / 秦庄襄王]] - son who honors her as queen dowager.

- [[HuayangFuren|华阳夫人]] - adoptive mother honored in parallel as 华阳太后.
- [[MaternalStatusSuccessionLeverage|子以母贵式继承杠杆]] and [[WrittenSuccessionCredential|书面继承凭证]] - succession background that explains why the adoptive and biological maternal lines both matter.
