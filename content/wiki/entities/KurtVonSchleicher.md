# Kurt von Schleicher

## Overview

Kurt von Schleicher was a German general and political intriguer who sought an army-centered authoritarian settlement, helped remove parliamentary constraints, and failed to split or contain the Nazi movement.

## Current Profile

The episode places Schleicher behind successive elite experiments after the 1930 parliamentary breakdown. He wanted an authoritarian order that would free the army from parliamentary control, permit rearmament, and restore German power, while treating mass parties as instruments to be divided or incorporated.

He helped elevate [[FranzVonPapen]], supported another Reichstag dissolution, and participated in secret bargaining with [[AdolfHitler]]. As chancellor he tried to detach Gregor Strasser and a Nazi faction, but the plan failed. Hindenburg's circle then abandoned him while Papen reopened the route to Hitler's appointment.

## Key Characteristics

- Political general seeking an army-centered authoritarian order.
- Intriguer behind chancellorial appointments and election gambles.
- Advocate of rearmament and reversal of Versailles constraints.
- Negotiator who underestimated Hitler's refusal of subordinate power.
- Failed chancellor whose attempted Nazi split accelerated his isolation.
