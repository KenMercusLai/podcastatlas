## Overview

曹窋 / Cao Ku is presented as a Western Han official who overhears the warning delivered to [[LuChanWesternHan|吕产]] and carries it to [[ChenPing|陈平]] and [[ZhouBo|周勃]], then helps close palace access against Lü Chan.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-206-zhu-lv-an-liu-shishang-diyi-miemen-an-2-lpsj8exoquanifh7njkrhqszrhkh0|Hanji 206 part 2]] calls Cao Ku the Pingyang marquis and imperial counselor. While meeting Lü Chan, he hears [[JiaShouWesternHan|贾寿]] report that Guan Ying, Qi, and Chu have aligned against the Lü family. Cao Ku immediately informs Chen Ping and Zhou Bo, giving their side the urgency to seize the Northern Army. He later carries Zhou Bo's direction to the palace security official so Lü Chan cannot enter the inner gate.

## Key Characteristics

- Court official positioned close enough to Lü Chan to hear an urgent strategic warning.
- Intelligence relay who transfers that warning to Chen Ping and Zhou Bo.
- Access-control intermediary who helps turn coalition planning into a closed palace gate.
