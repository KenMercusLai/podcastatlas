# El Obeid

El Obeid is the North Kordofan city described in [[outsider-chance-ending-sudans-proxy-war-6a75b3695d765781a2b1b132]] as a major hotspot in [[Sudan]]'s war. [[TomGardner]] says it is under sustained [[RapidSupportForces|RSF]] aerial assault, with displaced civilians reporting repeated shelling and evening drone strikes.

The source treats El Obeid as strategically important because an RSF capture could reconnect western fronts and threaten [[Khartoum]] again from the west. It is also the clearest site in the episode for [[LowCostDroneWarfare]] hitting civilian life and infrastructure.
