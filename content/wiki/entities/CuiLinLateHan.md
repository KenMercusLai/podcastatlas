## Overview

[[CuiLinLateHan|崔林]] is Cui Yan's relative and defender, presented as a capable official whose more adaptive career under Cao Wei contrasts with Cui Yan's fatal collision with concentrated power.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1156 places Cui Lin in a discussion with [[ChenQun|陈群]] about Jizhou figures. When Chen Qun criticizes [[CuiYanLateHan|崔琰]], Cui Lin answers that a capable person also needs the opportunity of an enlightened ruler and should not be judged solely by personal preservation.

The episode then contrasts the relatives' paths. Cui Lin retains respect for Cui Yan's worth but is described as more practical and better adapted to the political order, later becoming situ and Marquis of Anyang. This is an episode-level contrast rather than proof that adaptation alone caused his advancement.

## Key Characteristics

- Relative who defends Cui Yan's ability and political worth.
- Rejects survival alone as the measure of an official's value.
- Presented as practical and adaptive within the Cao-Wei order.
- Later reaches senior office and receives a marquis title in the episode's account.
