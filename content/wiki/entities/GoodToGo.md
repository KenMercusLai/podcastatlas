# Good To Go

Good To Go was the keto-oriented brand launched by [[RiversideNaturalFoods]] in 2018 in [[madegood-salma-and-nima-fotovat-lost-their-first-business-they-grew-their-next-one-into-a-snack-giant-cf5715bf-e9e0-45e0-a8a9-1256a7a4ed0d]]. The episode treats it as a focus lesson inside a fast-growing CPG company rather than as a successful second brand.

## Key Points
- [[NimaFotovat]] describes himself as vulnerable to "shiny object syndrome" when discussing Good To Go.
- The brand had quality issues, required reformulation, did not grow enough, and was eventually discontinued.
- [[SalmaFotovat]] says the brand carried opportunity cost because resources spent on Good To Go were not focused on [[MadeGood]].
