# 何嘉琛 / He Jiachen

## Overview
何嘉琛 is an AI founder associated in the source with [[AFFiNE]], a Vue-related infrastructure company, and enterprise-Agent company [[AFK]].

## Current Profile
His founder thesis joins product-form change with organizational learning. He argues that AI has moved from assistance inside a fixed editor toward proactive, cross-environment agents, and that enterprise value comes from letting one governed system learn across employees and workflows rather than multiplying isolated personal assistants. His strongest claims about AFK, small models, and software's decline remain first-party descriptions and forecasts.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames entrepreneurship as cumulative learning across technology, product, market, collaboration, and monetization.
- Treats organization-wide shared learning as the main distinction between enterprise and personal agents.
- Advocates distilling repeated frontier-model work into cheaper task-routing and enterprise-specific models.
- Expects product, design, and engineering work to converge around shared code and executable artifacts.
- Makes strong forecasts about fixed workflows and software interfaces losing value as model capability rises.
