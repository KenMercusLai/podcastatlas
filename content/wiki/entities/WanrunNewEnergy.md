# Wanrun New Energy / 万润新能源

Wanrun New Energy / 万润新能源 appears in [[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-21-shiyan-yizuo-yin-qiche-erqi-you-bugan-bei-qiche-paoxia-de-chengshi-1001770066]] as a [[Shiyan]] battery-materials company. The source describes it as working in lithium-battery cathode materials, including [[LithiumIronPhosphateBatteries|lithium iron phosphate]]-related segments, and serving customers such as [[CATL]].

The source uses Wanrun to show that Shiyan's diversification is not limited to vehicle assembly. Materials suppliers can connect a legacy auto city to the wider [[PowerBatteryIndustryChain]], though the episode treats this as one branch of a transition effort rather than proof that batteries have replaced the old auto base.
