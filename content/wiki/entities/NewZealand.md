# New Zealand / 新西兰

## Overview

New Zealand is represented through Māori-European encounter, Polynesian settlement and navigation, island conservation, macroeconomic policy design, and [[AnzacSpirit]] national memory.

## Current Profile

The ecology branch uses New Zealand as the setting for the [[Kakapo|kakapo]] recovery story in [[186-rang-ni-xiao-dao-tou-diao-reng-bu-wang-sikao-weida-de-xiaoshi-shijie-manyou-zhinan-998655210]]. The macro branch uses New Zealand as the origin case for modern [[InflationTargeting]], where credibility and expectations helped turn a numeric target into central-bank practice after painful disinflation.

The Gallipoli source gives New Zealand a memory branch. New Zealand appears with Australia as part of the Anzac story: Gallipoli becomes a public sacrifice narrative and Anzac Day remains a civic commemoration.

The earliest branch currently held by the wiki concerns first contact, translation, and possession. [[Maori|Māori]] communities resist, negotiate, trade, and communicate through [[Tupia]] while suffering lethal British responses. Cook's circumnavigation maps the two main islands for European use and ends in a possession claim that does not represent Māori consent.

## Key Characteristics

- New Zealand is an island-ecology case where introduced predators turned once-adaptive flightlessness into extinction risk.
- It is a conservation-recovery case through protected-island management and kakapo population rebuilding.
- It is a monetary-policy origin case for modern inflation targeting.
- Its policy branch emphasizes credibility, expectations, and painful transition costs.
- Its Gallipoli branch links New Zealand to Anzac memory and national identity.
- Its first-voyage branch joins Māori agency and Polynesian navigation to British violence, cartography, and claim without consent.

## Evidence

- Conservation branch: [[186-rang-ni-xiao-dao-tou-diao-reng-bu-wang-sikao-weida-de-xiaoshi-shijie-manyou-zhinan-998655210]] describes kakapo decline from human arrival and introduced animals, then recovery through protected-island management, tracking, breeding attention, funding, and public visibility.
- Monetary-policy branch: [[our-mission-find-the-worlds-best-economic-ideas-summer-school-world-tour]] presents Arthur Grimes, Don Brash, and the Reserve Bank of New Zealand as the origin case for a 0%-2% inflation target, credibility management, and disinflation with high unemployment costs.
- Anzac memory branch: [[676-the-first-world-war-churchills-calamity-part-6-glt1275431911]] pairs New Zealand with Australia in Gallipoli memory and Anzac Day commemoration.
- Cook encounter branch: [[381-captain-cook-to-the-ends-of-the-earth-part-2-glt6950185057]] follows Māori resistance, Tupia's translation, several lethal clashes, Cook's coastal survey, and a possession ceremony witnessed by only one local elder.

## Qualifications

The page is not a general New Zealand profile. The Cook branch is reconstructed mainly through British journals and cannot recover all Māori intentions, names, affiliations, or judgments. Mapping the coast and raising a British flag did not establish meaningful local consent.
