# 洛弛 / LuoChi Footwear

## Overview

洛弛 is the Putian outdoor-footwear brand founded by Chen Yinghong in 2006 and used by the episode to show why factory knowledge does not automatically become a durable consumer brand.

## Current Profile

[[no-225-putian-xie-wangshi-gkwrijeowkkqa6hffgt1ih2w]] presents LuoChi as a serious original-brand attempt built around hiking and trekking shoes. Small orders and a category mismatch with mainstream local sports-shoe capacity pushed the company to build its own factory, hire product and design talent, license technology, defend trademarks, and construct dealer and retail channels. It expanded to roughly 200 domestic stores and entered Europe and Korea, but rapid growth strained cash; by 2022, the episode says roughly four-fifths of the business was ODM for overseas brands.

## Key Characteristics

- Original outdoor-footwear brand built from Putian manufacturing experience.
- Required proprietary product development and a dedicated factory when large contractors would not accept small orders.
- Faced high material, patent, trademark, channel, store, and working-capital costs.
- Achieved meaningful distribution before expansion pressure damaged financial resilience.
- Retained the brand while relying mainly on ODM revenue by the episode's 2022 snapshot.

## Evidence

- **Capability build:** [[no-225-putian-xie-wangshi-gkwrijeowkkqa6hffgt1ih2w]] describes factory construction, senior product hiring, Korean design input, and specialized materials.
- **Brand infrastructure:** [[no-225-putian-xie-wangshi-gkwrijeowkkqa6hffgt1ih2w]] connects patents, trademark conflict, dealers, stores, and overseas entry to the cost of legitimate brand operation.
- **Business-model outcome:** [[no-225-putian-xie-wangshi-gkwrijeowkkqa6hffgt1ih2w]] reports cash strain after fast expansion and a later ODM-dominant revenue mix.

## Qualifications

- Store count, geographic reach, revenue mix, and causal claims about the cash crisis remain episode-scoped rather than independently audited.
- Returning to ODM does not prove the brand had no value; it shows that manufacturing services remained the more important reported revenue base.

## What Changed

- Added LuoChi as a bounded case of genuine brand formation, expansion risk, and partial return to manufacturing services.
