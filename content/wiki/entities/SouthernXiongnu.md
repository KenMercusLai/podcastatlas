# 南匈奴 / Southern Xiongnu

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-914-xinren-de-ren-weihe-zong-shang-ni-zui-shen-lvwit-rzrkscn5a3h6wblntxppso|Hanji 914]] adds the earliest Southern Xiongnu succession marker now on this page. In 178 CE the chanyu dies and his son [[HuzhengChanyu|呼征]] succeeds, creating the baseline before the next sources turn Southern Xiongnu succession into a Han frontier-command problem.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-917-ruhe-xunsu-zhaodao-shengzhi-jiaxin-de-pojudian-lk2zt5c3onvui9a4ppbowshy66tg|Hanji 917]] later adds the first Southern Xiongnu succession intervention. [[ZhangXiuProtectorOfXiongnu|张脩]], protector of Xiongnu, clashes with chanyu [[HuzhengChanyu|呼征]], kills him without prior court authorization, and installs the right wise king [[QiangquChanyu|羌渠]]. The court later punishes Zhang Xiu, making the event a command-authority problem before the later 188 requisition backlash.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-939-donghan-luanju-de-fengkuang-sanguo-xuqu-lthmvw8ihk96fuez9vamlryzcngy|Hanji 939]] supplies the earliest current Southern Xiongnu succession crisis. The Han court orders Southern Xiongnu troops to support [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] against [[ZhangChunLateHan|张纯]]; [[QiangquChanyu|羌渠]] sends cavalry, but fear of repeated requisition leads [[XiluoSouthernXiongnu|醯落]] and the [[TugeHu|屠各胡]] to rebel, kill Qiangqu, and install [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-949-lishishang-de-dongzhuo-daodi-duo-canbao-lk6idogwmc54nk1sdfc5wonyvgbb|Hanji 949]] backfills the Southern Xiongnu internal crisis before the later Cao Cao and emperor-escort branches. The source says [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]] faces a rival succession backed by rebels, goes to [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] to seek court judgment, and finds the Han court unable to act amid [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s takeover and [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s death.

The source also explains why Yufuluo remains in the central-plains force market. His attempt to join [[BaiboArmy|白波军]] attacks against commanderies fails because local people hide in fortified settlements, and his own people refuse his return, leaving him in Pingyang while the wider late-Han order fragments.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] adds an earlier Southern Xiongnu contact in [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]]'s attachment path. The source says Zhang Yang and a Southern Xiongnu chanyu attach to [[YuanShao|袁绍]], but after conflict with Yuan Shao the chanyu holds Zhang Yang as a hostage and carries him to [[PuyangLateHan|濮阳]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-961-neihuang-zhizhan-caocao-chulu-fengmang-ljdlt6kiu8tg0qws-jlyej-yhgxr|Hanji 961]] adds the Southern Xiongnu to Cao Cao's early campaign field. [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]] is identified as chanyu when [[CaoCao|曹操]] advances to [[NeihuangBattleLateHan|内黄之战]] after forcing [[YuDuLateHan|于毒]] away from [[DongWuyangLateHan|东武阳]].

The source does not give a full Southern Xiongnu narrative here. Its value is chronological: Yufuluo appears as an active military actor before the later Yuan Shu support branch and before Hanji 998 records succession to [[HuchuquanChanyu|呼厨泉]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-991-zhengduo-xiandi-zhizhan-shui-neng-zuizhong-qusheng-lkbsl5uzqxouzm6mlewrnuns0x6p|Hanji 991]] first uses Southern Xiongnu forces in the emperor-flight branch. [[QubeiSouthernXiongnu|去卑]], identified as a right wise king, brings cavalry into the emergency coalition called by [[DongChengLateHan|董承]] and [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] to protect [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] from [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]].

南匈奴 / Southern Xiongnu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] as the frontier succession note closing the episode. The source says [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]] dies, his younger brother [[HuchuquanChanyu|呼厨泉]] succeeds as chanyu, and the group is stationed at Pingyang.

The source does not connect this note deeply to Gongsun Zan's collapse. It preserves a late-Han frontier transition alongside the Youzhou branch and links the page to the broader [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] archive.
