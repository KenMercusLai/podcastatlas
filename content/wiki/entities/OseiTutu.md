# Osei Tutu

## Overview

Osei Tutu is the ruler the episode presents as the political founder of Asante greatness and the partner of priest-statesman [[OkomfoAnokye]].

## Current Profile

The source places Osei Tutu between inherited subordination and a new integrated polity. His alleged experience at the [[Denkyira]] court gives him knowledge of the dominant power, while his alliance with Okomfo Anokye joins political organization to sacred legitimacy. The [[GoldenStool]], [[Kumasi]], military professionalization, and victory over Denkyira become linked parts of state formation rather than isolated legends or reforms.

## Key Characteristics

- Nephew and successor figure after Obiri Yeboah's death.
- Politically formed, in the source's account, by time spent at the Denkyira court.
- Partner of [[OkomfoAnokye]] in unifying scattered groups.
- Ruler associated with the descent of the [[GoldenStool]].
- Founder linked to [[Kumasi]] and a disciplined professional army.

## Evidence

- Formation and succession: [[269-ghana-the-ashanti-empire-glt3638102749]] connects Osei Tutu's rise to Obiri Yeboah's defeat and Osei Tutu's experience under Denkyira.
- Sacred-political partnership: [[269-ghana-the-ashanti-empire-glt3638102749]] pairs him with Okomfo Anokye in the unification story and the Golden Stool tradition.
- Institutional consolidation: [[269-ghana-the-ashanti-empire-glt3638102749]] associates him with choosing Kumasi, building the army, and defeating Denkyira.

## Qualifications

The episode repeatedly moves between oral tradition, political inference, and firmer narrative claims. The river-shrine conception story, court scandal, hostage status, tree test, magical interventions, speeches, and exact personal division of labor remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Created Osei Tutu as the episode's political founder of Asante integration and expansion.

## Relationships

- [[OkomfoAnokye]] - sacred and strategic partner in the episode's founding narrative.
- [[GoldenStool]] - symbol said to descend into his lap and embody the new people.
- [[Denkyira]] - overlord whose methods he learns and whose power he defeats.
- [[Kumasi]] - capital attributed to his selection and consolidation.
- [[Asante]] - polity whose founding greatness the source associates with him.
