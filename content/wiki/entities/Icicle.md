# 之禾 / ICICLE

## Overview

之禾 / ICICLE is a Chinese fashion brand positioned as understated light luxury through natural materials, integrated manufacturing, restrained aesthetics, and long customer relationships.

## Current Profile

ICICLE is presented as the slow-brand counterpart to celebrity-led demand. Founded in 1997 and manufacturing in its own factory since 2005 according to the source, it links material development, garment production, high-end retail, print publishing, and a low-logo Chinese aesthetic into one system. The result is a premium proposition aimed primarily at affluent women who value consistency and cultural fit over conspicuous branding.

The profile's strongest commercial signal is repeat purchase rather than first-launch attention. The source reports roughly RMB 4 billion in annual sales, average Tmall pricing around RMB 2,400, and a 68% annual repurchase rate, but these figures remain host-attributed. Rising imported-material use and a shift toward RMB 5,000-10,000 products show deliberate premiumization rather than accidental price drift.

## Key Characteristics

- Natural-material and understated Chinese-aesthetic positioning.
- Own-factory and integrated production capability developed over a long operating horizon.
- High-end mall distribution and low-logo appeal for affluent, repeat customers.
- Art and photography publishing used to express nature, material origin, and environmental values.
- Active movement toward more imported inputs and higher price bands.
- Repeat purchase presented as the core proof of brand value.

## Evidence

- Integrated product system - [[ep150-ep150]] says ICICLE was founded in 1997, built its own factory in 2005, and connects fabric development to garment manufacturing.
- Aesthetic and cultural positioning - [[ep150-ep150]] describes natural materials, restrained low-logo design, annual art photography publications, and a customer preference for understated Chinese aesthetics.
- Premiumization - [[ep150-ep150]] says imported Italian raw-material use rose and the assortment shifted away from sub-RMB 5,000 products toward RMB 5,000-10,000 products.
- Repeat-customer economics - [[ep150-ep150]] reports high-end mall presence, roughly RMB 4 billion in sales, and 68% annual repurchase, using a roughly 400,000-customer estimate to illustrate niche scale.

## Qualifications

Sales, average price, customer count, input mix, and repurchase figures are not independently verified. The episode does not define the repurchase cohort, period, channel coverage, return treatment, or profitability. Natural materials, own manufacturing, and cultural publishing can support distinction but do not by themselves prove environmental performance or defensible economics.

## What Changed

- Created the profile as a Chinese light-luxury case built around integrated manufacturing and restrained aesthetics.
- Identified repeat purchase and a bounded affluent customer group as the core commercial thesis.
- Preserved reported scale and premiumization figures as source-scoped rather than audited facts.
