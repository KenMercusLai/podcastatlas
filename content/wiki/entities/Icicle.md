# 之禾 / ICICLE

## Overview

之禾 / ICICLE is a Chinese fashion brand positioned as understated light luxury through natural materials, integrated manufacturing, restrained aesthetics, premium retail, and long customer relationships.

## Current Profile

ICICLE is presented as the slow-brand counterpart to celebrity-led demand. Founded in 1997 and manufacturing in its own factory since 2005 according to [[ep150-ep150]], it links material development, garment production, high-end retail, print publishing, and a low-logo Chinese aesthetic. Rising imported-material use and a shift toward RMB 5,000-10,000 products indicate deliberate premiumization.

Both sources report a 68% annual repurchase rate, making retention the profile's strongest commercial claim. Episode 151 also describes year-over-year Double 11 sales doubling and cites Kering's investment as a signal of international strategic interest. All sales, price, growth, investment-significance, and repurchase claims remain source-scoped; the repurchase cohort, channel coverage, return treatment, and profitability are not defined.

## Key Characteristics

- Natural-material and understated Chinese-aesthetic positioning.
- Own-factory and integrated production capability developed over a long operating horizon.
- High-end mall distribution and low-logo appeal for affluent repeat customers.
- Art and photography publishing used to express nature, origin, and environmental values.
- Deliberate movement toward imported inputs and higher price bands.
- Repeat purchase presented as the core proof of brand value.
- International strategic investment presented as recognition, not proof of global consumer adoption.

## Evidence

- Integrated product system - [[ep150-ep150]] says ICICLE was founded in 1997, built its own factory in 2005, and connects fabric development to garment manufacturing.
- Aesthetic and cultural positioning - [[ep150-ep150]] describes natural materials, restrained design, annual art photography publications, and preference among customers for understated Chinese aesthetics.
- Premiumization - [[ep150-ep150]] says imported Italian raw-material use rose and the assortment shifted toward RMB 5,000-10,000 products.
- Repeat-customer economics - [[ep150-ep150]] reports roughly RMB 4 billion in sales and a 68% annual repurchase rate; [[ep151-ep151]] repeats the 68% figure.
- New momentum and recognition - [[ep151-ep151]] reports Double 11 sales doubling and presents Kering's investment as a milestone for Chinese fashion.

## Qualifications

Sales, average price, customer count, input mix, growth, and repurchase figures are not independently verified. Neither source defines the repurchase calculation. Natural materials, own manufacturing, cultural publishing, and investment can support distinction but do not by themselves prove environmental performance, defensible economics, or overseas consumer recognition.
