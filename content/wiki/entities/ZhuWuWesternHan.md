## Overview

祝午 / Zhu Wu is presented as a Qi official who joins [[LiuXiangQiKingWesternHan|刘襄]]'s anti-Lü planning group and becomes internal secretary after the kingdom's command reorganization.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-205-nvhuangdi-lvzhi-jingran-yin-gou-er-si-2-ltgwfdhn9cdwphudy0blyh36fge1|Hanji 205 part 2]] names Zhu Wu as Liu Xiang's 郎中令 during the initial planning. After [[ZhaoPingQiChancellor|赵平]] is defeated, Liu Xiang appoints Zhu Wu as 内史, placing him within the administrative structure that supports the outward mobilization.

## Key Characteristics

- Qi court official aligned with Liu Xiang.
- Participant in the anti-Lü planning circle.
- Internal secretary after the kingdom's senior-office reorganization.
