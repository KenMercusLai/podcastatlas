# 程杭 / Cheng Hang

程杭 / Cheng Hang is the [[Hupu|虎扑]] founder in [[no-212-cengjing-de-jingshen-jiaoluo-douban-zhihu-tieba-hupu-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-25-1003510980]]. The episode describes him as a sports fan studying in the United States who rented a server in 2004 to build HoopChina and translated foreign basketball coverage every day for Chinese NBA fans.

The source treats Cheng's early operating work as community formation through credibility. HoopChina's timely sports information and its coverage of Wang Zhizhi's return helped it become a serious basketball-fan gathering place before the later Hupu brand broadened into sports portal, forum, and Pedestrian Street culture.

Cheng also supplies the episode's "barbecue-stall culture" framing. For the wiki, that makes him a useful counterpoint to pure content-platform logic: [[Hupu|虎扑]] was sticky because users returned to a place, mood, rules, arguments, and jokes, not only to consume sports news.
