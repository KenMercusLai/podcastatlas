# Petit Trianon

## Overview

The Petit Trianon was [[MarieAntoinette|Marie Antoinette]]'s retreat from [[Versailles]] ceremony and a politically ambiguous stage for sentimental simplicity, privacy, and allegations of mistress-like excess.

## Current Profile

Given to Marie Antoinette by [[LouisXVI|Louis XVI]], the Petit Trianon let her cultivate an informal circle and a Rousseauian language of nature, virtue, spontaneity, and rural simplicity. The decorative hamlet, animals, mill, charity, and family instruction complicated a flat image of indifference, but the property's earlier association with royal mistresses and its restricted intimacy also helped opponents frame the queen as behaving outside her formal role.

## Key Characteristics

- Private retreat from Versailles etiquette.
- Site of Marie Antoinette's sentimental and rustic self-presentation.
- Included a decorative hamlet and rural features.
- Carried an inherited association with royal mistresses.
- Became both an escape from court pressure and a source of reputational vulnerability.
