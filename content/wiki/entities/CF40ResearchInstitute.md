# 中国金融四十人研究院 / CF40 Research Institute

## Overview

The 中国金融四十人研究院 is the research institution whose 中国经济新范式 report frames economic-transition conversations on [[QizhulouYanBinke|起朱楼宴宾客]] and [[ZhixingXiaojiuguan|知行小酒馆]].

## Current Profile

In the bounded source, the institute studies whether China's prolonged property adjustment is moving into a later phase while AI, new manufacturing, and other new-economy activity become more important. Its represented method combines contribution estimates, household and firm adaptation, sector clearing, credit structure, inflation, and fiscal transmission rather than defining a turning point as an immediate rebound.

The report's headline estimate is that new-economy contribution to GDP growth exceeded real estate's contribution in 2025. The supplied summary does not reproduce definitions, calculations, tables, or the report itself, so the estimate remains an attributed institutional claim.

## Key Characteristics

- Frames economic transition through changing marginal growth contributions rather than a simple boom-versus-recession binary.
- Separates aggregate resilience from household welfare and asset outcomes.
- Treats AI, new manufacturing, property, credit, consumption, inflation, and fiscal policy as one transition system.
- Uses the reported 2025 new-economy/property crossover as evidence for a possible turning point.
- Emphasizes fiscal transmission to residents as a missing link in the emerging growth regime.
