# BJ Swami

BJ Swami is the tech engineer discussed in [[older-workers-arent-retiring-should-they-be-forced-to]] after a [[Reddit]] post about older workers not retiring. In the source, Swami describes feeling stuck beneath a 71-year-old boss who repeatedly suggested retirement was near while remaining in place.

Swami's case sharpens the episode's generational loop. The frustration is real, but the source also notes that workers who cannot save enough during blocked mid-career years may later become the older workers others resent for staying.
