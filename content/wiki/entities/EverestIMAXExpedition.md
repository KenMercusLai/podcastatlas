# Everest IMAX Expedition

## Overview
The Everest IMAX Expedition is the high-altitude film expedition discussed in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-842831df1049b1024b0dfd8bf11ec723-108598757-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-842831df1049b1024b0dfd8bf11ec723-108598757]]. [[AraceliSegarra]] says [[DavidBreshears]] invited her to join the team after meeting her at Everest base camp.

## Current Profile
The source frames the expedition as both a filming project and a team-rescue case. Its operational world includes a heavy [[IMAX]] camera, Sherpa-carried loads, constant base-camp filming, storm-driven tragedy, exhausted rescue response, depleted supplies, recovery, and a later summit.

## Key Characteristics
- High-altitude film expedition built around an [[IMAX]] documentary goal.
- Team selected for collaboration, gender equity, diversity, and shared belonging, not only elite climbing credentials.
- Operationally constrained by large-format camera weight, batteries, tripod, setup time, and Sherpa load distribution.
- Crisis-response case during the 1996 Everest disaster because the team redirected tents, food, oxygen, and equipment toward distressed climbers.
- Source example for [[SummitAsTransformation]] because the team's memory centers trust, humanity, and return rather than the summit alone.
