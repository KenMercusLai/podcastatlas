# European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control

The European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control, abbreviated ECDC in [[i-robot-ai-and-consciousness-6a880840b0d5e24ea40c7015]], is the public-health body cited in the episode's [[EuropeanSTISurge|European STI surge]] segment. The source says ECDC data and clinicians indicate that increased testing and post-pandemic service restoration do not fully explain the rise in gonorrhea and syphilis.

In this ingest, ECDC functions as a surveillance and interpretation node rather than as a policy actor with a complete causal explanation. Its role is to ground the episode's uncertainty: the rise appears real, but the specific mix of behavior, testing, prevention, and healthcare access remains unresolved.
