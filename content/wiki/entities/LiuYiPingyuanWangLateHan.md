## Overview
平原王刘裔 / Liu Yi (Pingyuan Wang) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-794-yidai-nvjun-dengsui-qushi-hou-jing-quanjia-zaoyang-llmlhabdd09opca6-pr80q4mfoxi|Hanji 794]] as the royal relative around whom [[EmperorAnOfHan|Emperor An]]'s fear of replacement crystallizes after [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]] delays returning power. He is distinct from [[LiuYiBeixiangHouLateHan|Beixiang Hou Liu Yi / Liu Yi]], the later Yan-backed child ruler.

## Current Profile
Hanji 794 presents Liu Yi as a political possibility more than an active plotter. Empress Dowager Deng has brought sons of the Jibei and Hejian royal houses to the capital; Liu Yi, described as a handsome son of the Hejian king, is made heir to Pingyuan Huai Wang and kept in Luoyang. That visibility is enough for Wang Sheng and other palace actors to warn Emperor An that Deng may replace him.

After Deng dies, the accusation against the Deng household claims that Deng Kui, Deng Hong, and others asked Shangshu Deng Fang for old files on the deposed emperor so they could install Liu Yi. Emperor An responds by purging the Deng family and demoting Liu Yi from Pingyuan Wang to Duxiang Hou before sending him back to Hejian. Liu Yi's survival strategy is withdrawal: he closes his doors, avoids visitors, and does not create a visible support network.

## Key Characteristics
- Royal relative retained in Luoyang after Empress Dowager Deng summons candidates from collateral royal lines.
- Alleged alternative claimant whose existence feeds Emperor An's fear of being deposed.
- Not shown as directing the anti-An accusation or the Deng household's alleged plan.
- Demoted from Pingyuan Wang to Duxiang Hou and sent back to Hejian after the Deng purge begins.
- Survives by avoiding contact, closing his doors, and making himself politically unavailable.
- Disambiguated from the later Beixiang Hou Liu Yi because the title, succession context, and outcome differ.

## Qualifications
This page is single-source and source-bounded. It does not prove whether Liu Yi knew of, supported, or benefited from any real Deng plan; the current evidence shows that he was treated as a plausible alternative around which slander and fear could organize. The route uses `LiuYiPingyuanWangLateHan` to avoid merging him with [[LiuYiBeixiangHouLateHan|Beixiang Hou Liu Yi / Liu Yi]] or [[LiuYiLateHan|刘翊 / Liu Yi]].
