# 胡才 / Hu Cai (late Han)

胡才 / Hu Cai (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-991-zhengduo-xiandi-zhizhan-shui-neng-zuizhong-qusheng-lkbsl5uzqxouzm6mlewrnuns0x6p|Hanji 991]] as one of the former [[BaiboArmy|白波军]] commanders who join [[DongChengLateHan|董承]] and [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] during [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s flight from [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]].

The episode uses him as part of the rearguard and reinforcement field rather than as an independent character. Hu Cai's role helps show why emergency protection is not the same as restored state capacity: outside cavalry can blunt one attack, but the escort field still collapses under renewed pursuit.
