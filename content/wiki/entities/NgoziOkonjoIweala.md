# Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala

Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala appears in [[piles-of-cash-and-a-town-of-solutions-in-kenya-nigeria-summer-school]] as Nigeria's finance minister at the time of the [[YouWinProgram|YouWin Program]]. The source says she was responding to high youth unemployment and the difficulty many young Nigerians faced in getting capital for small businesses.

Her role links [[Nigeria]]'s job-creation problem to [[EvidenceBasedDevelopmentPolicy]]. The program had to allocate money at scale while limiting corruption risk, which pushed the design toward outside judging, anonymized applications, training, and randomization.
