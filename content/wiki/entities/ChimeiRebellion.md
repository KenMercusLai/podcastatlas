## Overview

The Red Eyebrows / 赤眉 are synthesized as a popular-revolt force that begins as a sign of [[XinDynasty|Xin dynasty]] collapse, reveals hollow regime authority through [[LiuPenziEasternHan|刘盆子]]'s failed abdication in Jianwu 2, becomes a destructive Guanzhong military and famine problem, and ends as a Jianwu 3 surrender-settlement test under [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]. [[126-wangmang-zhisi-shensheng-diguo-de-huixuanbiao-he-huixuanbiao-de-huixuanbiao-786255780|Wang Mang 126]] supplies the Xin-collapse context, [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-683-fangniuwa-liupenzi-buxiang-dang-huangdi-dan-meiyudi-lt7hmrorydsazpkuwby1ntd7t1eh|Hanji 683]] supplies the internal discipline and figurehead-rule layer, [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-687-si-hou-200-nian-luzhi-jing-zao-qi-chi-da-ru-lhakzy89w1l4vhazjuyenbjrw9iz|Hanji 687]] supplies snow loss, Chang'an reentry, mausoleum looting, famine pressure, and Huayin attrition, and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-688-cong-yongbing-baiwan-dao-kuibuchengjun-dengyu-jingli-le-shenme-lpabdvtmhtrf37puwvgi4c9ajp60|Hanji 688]] supplies final defeat, mass surrender, provisioning, relocation, and punishment boundaries.

## Current Profile

Wang Mang 126 frames the Red Eyebrows as one of the popular military pressures that expose the failure of Wang Mang's ideological and administrative state project. The page's earliest role therefore remains a collapse marker: Red Eyebrows appear alongside [[LulinRebellion|绿林]] and the [[KunyangBattle|Battle of Kunyang]] as signs that legitimacy had failed at the social and military level.

Hanji 683 shows that Chimei's own regime cannot turn a Liu-family imperial title into command discipline. Liu Gong sees internal collapse coming and urges Liu Penzi to return the seal and ribbon. Liu Penzi publicly says that the commanders still plunder and the people do not trust them, but the commanders refuse to let him abdicate and carry him back to the throne. The result is a brief recovery of public confidence in Chang'an, followed by renewed looting.

Hanji 687 gives the movement's destructive Guanzhong layer. Wei Xiao's side blocks Chimei's attempted move west into Long, heavy snow kills many people, and the movement retreats into the Chang'an-Sanfu field. The source also preserves the historical-record tradition that Chimei troops looted Western Han imperial mausoleums and allegedly violated [[LuZhi|吕雉]]'s corpse. Because the host marks the exact truth uncertain, this page treats that story as source-scoped memory of ritual collapse rather than settled event reconstruction.

The same source shows Chimei as a famine and attrition problem. Deng Yu attacks and loses, Chimei reenter [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]], Yan Cen and Chimei fighting devastates Sanfu, and civilians face empty cities, bones, corpses, and cannibalism. As Chimei can no longer plunder enough to survive, a large eastward movement fractures through desertion while [[FengYiEasternHan|冯异]] holds at [[HuayinLateHan|华阴]] and receives early surrenders.

Hanji 688 gives the Jianwu 3 endgame. The Red Eyebrows draw [[DengYuEasternHan|邓禹]] into repeated attacks by hungry troops, then suffer defeat under Feng Yi after he uses Chimei-style clothing and concealed troops to produce confusion. The movement's collapse becomes a settlement problem rather than only a battlefield result: Liu Penzi and leaders surrender with seal and ribbon, weapons are piled west of Yiyang, Liu Xiu displays the imperial army at the Luo River, and food is prepared for the remaining surrendered population.

That settlement is coercive but not indiscriminate annihilation. Leaders' households are moved to Luoyang with land and housing, Liu Penzi survives, and surrendered people are fed. Later plotting by Fan Chong and Feng An is punished with death, so the Chimei endpoint combines pardon, public submission, provisioning, relocation, and a hard boundary against renewed rebellion.

## Key Characteristics

- Popular-revolt force that marks Xin collapse and the failure of Wang Mang's regime at the social level.
- Regime-building failure whose Liu-family figurehead cannot stop commander plunder or make the people trust Chimei rule.
- Mobile Jianwu 2 military problem whose westward movement fails under Wei Xiao-side interception and snow losses.
- Destructive Guanzhong force tied to Chang'an reentry, Sanfu famine, imperial mausoleum looting, and source-scoped corpse-humiliation memory.
- Enemy movement that exposes Deng Yu's weakening command before his larger Hanji 688 rout.
- Defeated movement whose endpoint is mass surrender under overwhelming force and controlled provisioning.
- Settlement case in which pardon, relocation, food, office assignment, and later punishment coexist.

## Evidence

- Xin-collapse role: [[126-wangmang-zhisi-shensheng-diguo-de-huixuanbiao-he-huixuanbiao-de-huixuanbiao-786255780|Wang Mang 126]] names Red Eyebrows as an uprising during the unraveling of Wang Mang's state.
- Crisis context: [[126-wangmang-zhisi-shensheng-diguo-de-huixuanbiao-he-huixuanbiao-de-huixuanbiao-786255780|Wang Mang 126]] connects the uprising to displacement, inflation, natural disaster, frontier stress, and popular violence under the Xin regime.
- Figurehead-rule failure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-683-fangniuwa-liupenzi-buxiang-dang-huangdi-dan-meiyudi-lt7hmrorydsazpkuwby1ntd7t1eh|Hanji 683]] says Liu Penzi tries to return the seal and ribbon because Chimei commanders still plunder and the people do not trust them.
- Short-lived discipline: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-683-fangniuwa-liupenzi-buxiang-dang-huangdi-dan-meiyudi-lt7hmrorydsazpkuwby1ntd7t1eh|Hanji 683]] says commanders briefly restrain themselves after the assembly before renewed looting resumes.
- Westward failure and snow loss: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-687-si-hou-200-nian-luzhi-jing-zao-qi-chi-da-ru-lhakzy89w1l4vhazjuyenbjrw9iz|Hanji 687]] says Chimei attempts to move west into Long but is blocked by Yang Guang's Wei Xiao-side force, then suffers heavy losses in snow.
- Chang'an and tomb memory: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-687-si-hou-200-nian-luzhi-jing-zao-qi-chi-da-ru-lhakzy89w1l4vhazjuyenbjrw9iz|Hanji 687]] records Chimei's return into Chang'an and the mausoleum-looting tradition, including the source-scoped Lu Hou corpse report.
- Famine and attrition: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-687-si-hou-200-nian-luzhi-jing-zao-qi-chi-da-ru-lhakzy89w1l4vhazjuyenbjrw9iz|Hanji 687]] describes Sanfu famine, empty cities, cannibalism, desertion, eastward movement, and Feng Yi's Huayin pressure before the final settlement.
- Deng Yu deterioration: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-687-si-hou-200-nian-luzhi-jing-zao-qi-chi-da-ru-lhakzy89w1l4vhazjuyenbjrw9iz|Hanji 687]] says Deng Yu is defeated near Yu Yi, retreats to Yunyang, then loses again around Chang'an after Chimei reentry.
- Deng Yu final failure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-688-cong-yongbing-baiwan-dao-kuibuchengjun-dengyu-jingli-le-shenme-lpabdvtmhtrf37puwvgi4c9ajp60|Hanji 688]] says Deng Yu repeatedly attacks Chimei with hungry troops, ignores Feng Yi's advice, and suffers a severe defeat.
- Feng Yi defeat of Chimei: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-688-cong-yongbing-baiwan-dao-kuibuchengjun-dengyu-jingli-le-shenme-lpabdvtmhtrf37puwvgi4c9ajp60|Hanji 688]] says Feng Yi later uses selected troops in Chimei-style clothing and ambush to defeat the exhausted movement.
- Mass surrender and settlement boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-688-cong-yongbing-baiwan-dao-kuibuchengjun-dengyu-jingli-le-shenme-lpabdvtmhtrf37puwvgi4c9ajp60|Hanji 688]] says Liu Penzi and other leaders submit with the imperial seal and ribbon, the remaining people are fed, leaders' households are relocated, and Fan Chong plus Feng An are later killed after plotting.

## Qualifications

This page does not reconstruct the full Chimei chronology, internal leadership structure, or exact force numbers. Wang Mang 126 gives the collapse-context layer rather than a Chimei-centered account. Hanji 683 gives a focused figurehead-and-discipline scene but not a complete Chimei political institution. Hanji 687 gives a focused Jianwu 2 Guanzhong and famine layer, but exact snow-loss numbers, force counts, and minor commanders remain source-scoped. The Lu Zhi corpse-violation report is recorded as a historical tradition but not asserted as certain. Hanji 688 gives a focused Jianwu 3 surrender layer, but Liu Gong, Xu Xuan, Yang Yin, Fan Chong, Feng An, Xie Lu, Deng Hong, and exact surrender counts remain source-scoped unless more evidence accumulates.
