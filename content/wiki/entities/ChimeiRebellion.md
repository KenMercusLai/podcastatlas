## Overview

The Red Eyebrows / 赤眉 are synthesized as a popular-revolt force that begins as a sign of [[XinDynasty|Xin dynasty]] collapse, scales through Hongnong merger and victories over Gengshi commanders, presses Gengshi Chang'an into internal rupture before formal occupation, manufactures a Liu-family imperial banner through [[LiuPenziEasternHan|刘盆子]], occupies [[ChanglePalace|长乐宫]] while alienating Sanfu through immediate plunder, kills the fallen [[LiuXuanGengshiEmperor|更始帝刘玄]] when sympathy for him becomes dangerous, exposes hollow regime authority through later palace banquet disorder and failed abdication, destroys the old capital, becomes a Guanzhong famine and attrition problem, and ends as a Jianwu 3 surrender-settlement test. [[126-wangmang-zhisi-shensheng-diguo-de-huixuanbiao-he-huixuanbiao-de-huixuanbiao-786255780|Wang Mang 126]] supplies the Xin-collapse context, [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-675-luanshi-fenzheng-zhizhong-liuxiu-ruhe-jueqi-lrvxu1q4-ysg4kfxkf25hswygfyo|Hanji 675]] supplies the early Jianwu 1 escalation layer in Hongnong, [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-677-liuxuandi-dangnian-zhen-shi-yishou-haopai-da-xilan-lu8hizigjkqeps6ulqfqw9gfxnkv|Hanji 677]] supplies the pre-entry pressure layer near Zheng and [[HuayinLateHan|华阴]], [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-678-liupenzi-wode-huangdi-shi-zhuajiu-lai-de-lkamwdktxz0ksazh2h4am8fjmfiq|Hanji 678]] supplies the shamanic, genealogical, and lottery selection layer, [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-680-ming-sang-chimei-zhe-wei-qiyi-huangdi-maigu-hechu-lurtvfqerb0vf3a33msmd-txr9pv|Hanji 680]] supplies the Changle Palace, Sanfu-alienation, Liu Xuan, and [[DengYuEasternHan|邓禹]] contrast layer, Hanji 682-684 supply the palace-disorder, failed-abdication, and Chang'an destruction sequence, and Hanji 687-688 supply the famine, battlefield, surrender, provisioning, relocation, and punishment-boundary endgame.

## Current Profile

Wang Mang 126 frames the Red Eyebrows as one of the popular military pressures that expose the failure of Wang Mang's ideological and administrative state project. The page's earliest role therefore remains a collapse marker: Red Eyebrows appear alongside [[LulinRebellion|绿林]] and the [[KunyangBattle|Battle of Kunyang]] as signs that legitimacy had failed at the social and military level.

Hanji 675 gives the earliest current Eastern Han-era growth layer. Two Chimei forces merge successfully in Hongnong, and Liu Xuan's response fails: Su Mao is sent as a trouble-suppression commander but is defeated, the movement is organized into thirty camps of more than ten thousand each, and Li Song later loses heavily at Yaoxiang before Chimei presses north toward Hucheng. This source moves the page's Chimei profile from generic Xin-collapse pressure into a concrete Jianwu 1 military escalation before the Liu Penzi claimant layer.

Hanji 677 adds the pre-entry pressure layer immediately before the Liu Penzi selection. Chimei activity near Zheng and Huayin already makes Chang'an's Gengshi commanders doubt that the city can be held. Zhang Ang's group proposes plundering the capital and retreating toward Nanyang, and Liu Xuan's failed response turns the regime's fear of Chimei into internal armed rupture before Chimei's formal Chang'an occupation.

Hanji 678 adds the movement's transition from military force to claimant regime. As Chimei approaches Chang'an, a Qi-region shaman claims Chengyang Jing King Liu Zhang is angry that the army remains thieves instead of taking a heavenly title. Fang Yang advises the commanders to enthrone a Liu descendant, and Fan Chong's group searches among seized Liu-family youths.

The selection ritual makes the regime's weakness visible at birth. The commanders build an altar north of Zheng, create three wooden lots with one marked "Shangjiangjun," and treat Liu Penzi's draw as heavenly selection. Yet Liu Penzi is a terrified fifteen-year-old herdboy who bites and discards the wooden lot, continues under Liu Xiaqing's control, and is not consulted by the commanders after they receive their offices.

Hanji 680 now adds the earliest current Eastern Han restoration layer. After Liu Penzi enters Changle Palace, Sanfu commanderies and local camps send envoys, but Chimei troops rob goods and sometimes kill people. The episode contrasts that coercive occupation with Deng Yu's disciplined army, which receives thousands of daily submissions because it does not abuse civilians and because Deng Yu comforts those who come over.

The same source also shows Chimei turning the deposed Liu Xuan into a liability. Sanfu people suffer under Chimei and hope to save Liu Xuan, while Zhang Ang's group worries that he will remain a rallying figure. Liu Xuan is strangled at night under Chimei control; Liu Gong hides the body, and Liu Xiu later orders Deng Yu to bury him at Bali.

Hanji 682 shows the palace-level disorder before Liu Penzi's failed abdication. At Chimei's laji banquet, argument and fighting erupt before the celebration begins; soldiers leap over walls, break palace gates, seize wine and meat, and kill one another. Liu Penzi is young, frightened, and often crying alone. The source makes regime crisis visible as internal troop disorder, not only as later battlefield defeat.

Hanji 683 then shows that Chimei's own regime cannot turn a Liu-family imperial title into command discipline. Liu Gong sees internal collapse coming and urges Liu Penzi to return the seal and ribbon. Liu Penzi publicly says that the commanders still plunder and the people do not trust them, but the commanders refuse to let him abdicate and carry him back to the throne. The result is a brief recovery of public confidence in Chang'an, followed by renewed looting.

Hanji 684 adds the direct old-capital destruction layer immediately after that renewed looting. Chimei generals and soldiers search for valuables, burn palace buildings, streets, lanes, and commoner homes, then move west while plundering cities on the route into Anding and Beidi. The same source juxtaposes this with troops at Kunming Pool paying respects at the high temple, moving Western Han imperial tablets to Luoyang, and setting guards around imperial parks and tombs. The page treats this as a key transition: Chang'an's ritual-political center is being hollowed out while Liu Xiu's Luoyang order receives Han symbolic inheritance.

Hanji 687 gives the movement's destructive Guanzhong layer. Wei Xiao's side blocks Chimei's attempted move west into Long, heavy snow kills many people, and the movement retreats into the Chang'an-Sanfu field. The source also preserves the historical-record tradition that Chimei troops looted Western Han imperial mausoleums and allegedly violated [[LuZhi|吕雉]]'s corpse. Because the host marks the exact truth uncertain, this page treats that story as source-scoped memory of ritual collapse rather than settled event reconstruction.

The same source shows Chimei as a famine and attrition problem. Deng Yu attacks and loses, Chimei reenter [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]], Yan Cen and Chimei fighting devastates Sanfu, and civilians face empty cities, bones, corpses, and cannibalism. As Chimei can no longer plunder enough to survive, a large eastward movement fractures through desertion while [[FengYiEasternHan|冯异]] holds at [[HuayinLateHan|华阴]] and receives early surrenders.

Hanji 688 gives the Jianwu 3 endgame. The Red Eyebrows draw [[DengYuEasternHan|邓禹]] into repeated attacks by hungry troops, then suffer defeat under Feng Yi after he uses Chimei-style clothing and concealed troops to produce confusion. The movement's collapse becomes a settlement problem rather than only a battlefield result: Liu Penzi and leaders surrender with seal and ribbon, weapons are piled west of Yiyang, Liu Xiu displays the imperial army at the Luo River, and food is prepared for the remaining surrendered population.

That settlement is coercive but not indiscriminate annihilation. Leaders' households are moved to Luoyang with land and housing, Liu Penzi survives, and surrendered people are fed. Later plotting by Fan Chong and Feng An is punished with death, so the Chimei endpoint combines pardon, public submission, provisioning, relocation, and a hard boundary against renewed rebellion.

## Key Characteristics

- Popular-revolt force that marks Xin collapse and then scales in Jianwu 1 through Hongnong merger, Gengshi defeats, and large-camp organization.
- Legitimacy-seeking army that uses shamanic warning, Liu genealogy, altar ritual, and lottery selection to turn Liu Penzi into an imperial banner.
- Pre-entry pressure force whose presence near Zheng and Huayin helps break Gengshi command before Chimei formally enters Chang'an.
- Regime-building failure whose Liu-family figurehead's fear and failed abdication cannot turn imperial title into command discipline.
- Old-capital destroyer whose Chang'an plunder, fires, and westward looting contrast with Luoyang's reception of Han ritual symbols.
- Destructive Guanzhong force tied to Chang'an reentry, Sanfu famine, imperial mausoleum looting, and source-scoped corpse-humiliation memory.
- Defeated movement whose endpoint is mass surrender under overwhelming force and controlled provisioning.

## Qualifications

This page does not reconstruct the full Chimei chronology, internal leadership structure, or exact force numbers. Wang Mang 126 gives the collapse-context layer rather than a Chimei-centered account. Hanji 675 gives the Hongnong, Su Mao, Li Song, thirty-camp, Yaoxiang, and Hucheng escalation layer, but Gong Lin, Li Song's full career, force counts, camp sizes, and local geography remain source-scoped. Hanji 677 gives a focused pre-entry pressure layer and keeps Zhang Ang, Liao Zhan, Hu Yin, Shentu Jian, Chen Mu, Cheng Dan, Zhao Meng, and route details source-scoped. Hanji 678 gives the Liu Penzi selection layer but keeps Chengyang Jing King Liu Zhang, Fang Yang, Liu Mao, Liu Xiaqing, Fan Chong, Xu Xuan, Feng An, and the office roster source-scoped. Hanji 680 gives an early Changle Palace and Liu Xuan death layer, but `三府`, `泄露`, `刘次`, and minor route details remain source-scoped transcript forms. Hanji 682 gives a focused palace-disorder scene but keeps the exact banquet participants and riot mechanics source-scoped. Hanji 683 gives a focused figurehead-and-discipline scene but not a complete Chimei political institution. Hanji 684 gives an old-capital destruction and tablet-transfer juxtaposition; exact officials, numbers, and the full custody chain for the tablets remain source-scoped. Hanji 687 gives a focused Jianwu 2 Guanzhong and famine layer, but exact snow-loss numbers, force counts, and minor commanders remain source-scoped. The Lu Zhi corpse-violation report is recorded as a historical tradition but not asserted as certain. Hanji 688 gives a focused Jianwu 3 surrender layer, but Xu Xuan, Yang Yin, Fan Chong, Feng An, Xie Lu, Deng Hong, and exact surrender counts remain source-scoped unless more evidence accumulates.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 675 as the early Jianwu 1 escalation layer: Hongnong merger, defeats of Su Mao and Li Song, thirty-camp organization, and northward pressure toward Hucheng.
- Preserved Hanji 677 as the pre-entry pressure layer: Chimei movement near Zheng and Huayin helps drive Gengshi commanders toward plunder, retreat, and internal armed rupture before formal occupation.
- Added Hanji 678 as the pre-Chang'an legitimacy-manufacture layer: shamanic pressure, Liu-line selection, lottery enthronement, and Liu Penzi's immediate powerlessness.
- Reframed Chimei's later figurehead failure as a condition visible at the founding of Liu Penzi's title, not only after palace disorder.
- Preserved Hanji 680, Hanji 682-684, and Hanji 687-688 as the palace occupation, disorder, destruction, famine, defeat, surrender, and settlement sequence.
