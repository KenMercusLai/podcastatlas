## Overview

The Red Eyebrows / 赤眉 are synthesized as a popular-revolt force that begins in the wiki as part of the [[XinDynasty|Xin dynasty]] collapse and now gains a later Jianwu 3 surrender-settlement layer under [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]. [[126-wangmang-zhisi-shensheng-diguo-de-huixuanbiao-he-huixuanbiao-de-huixuanbiao-786255780|Wang Mang 126]] places the uprising in the wider breakdown of [[WangMang|王莽]]'s order, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-688-cong-yongbing-baiwan-dao-kuibuchengjun-dengyu-jingli-le-shenme-lpabdvtmhtrf37puwvgi4c9ajp60|Hanji 688]] follows the movement into defeat, mass surrender, and post-surrender control.

## Current Profile

Wang Mang 126 frames the Red Eyebrows as one of the popular military pressures that expose the failure of Wang Mang's ideological and administrative state project. The page's older role therefore remains a collapse marker: Red Eyebrows appear alongside [[LulinRebellion|绿林]] and the [[KunyangBattle|Battle of Kunyang]] as signs that legitimacy had failed at the social and military level.

Hanji 688 adds the Jianwu 3 endgame. The Red Eyebrows move after the Long'an/Chang'an struggle, draw [[DengYuEasternHan|邓禹]] into repeated attacks by hungry troops, and later suffer defeat under [[FengYiEasternHan|冯异]] after he uses Chimei-style clothing and concealed troops to produce confusion. The same source makes the movement's collapse a settlement problem rather than only a battlefield result.

That settlement is coercive but not indiscriminate annihilation. [[LiuPenziEasternHan|刘盆子]] and other leaders surrender with the seal and ribbon, weapons are piled west of Yiyang, Liu Xiu displays the imperial army at the Luo River, and food is prepared for the remaining surrendered population. Leaders' households are moved to Luoyang with land and housing, but later plotting by Fan Chong and Feng An is punished with death.

## Key Characteristics

- Popular-revolt force that marks Xin collapse and the failure of Wang Mang's regime at the social level.
- Persistent post-Xin military problem that survives into Liu Xiu's Jianwu consolidation.
- Enemy force that exploits hunger and command impatience during Deng Yu's failed attacks.
- Defeated movement whose endpoint is mass surrender under overwhelming force and controlled provisioning.
- Settlement case in which pardon, relocation, food, office assignment, and later punishment coexist.
- Contrast case between Liu Penzi's survival and other Chimei leaders' renewed plotting and execution.

- Xin-collapse role: [[126-wangmang-zhisi-shensheng-diguo-de-huixuanbiao-he-huixuanbiao-de-huixuanbiao-786255780|Wang Mang 126]] names Red Eyebrows as an uprising during the unraveling of Wang Mang's state.
- Crisis context: [[126-wangmang-zhisi-shensheng-diguo-de-huixuanbiao-he-huixuanbiao-de-huixuanbiao-786255780|Wang Mang 126]] connects the uprising to displacement, inflation, natural disaster, frontier stress, and popular violence under the Xin regime.

- Mass surrender: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-688-cong-yongbing-baiwan-dao-kuibuchengjun-dengyu-jingli-le-shenme-lpabdvtmhtrf37puwvgi4c9ajp60|Hanji 688]] says Liu Penzi, Xu Xuan, and other leaders submit with the imperial seal and ribbon.
- Provisioning and display: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-688-cong-yongbing-baiwan-dao-kuibuchengjun-dengyu-jingli-le-shenme-lpabdvtmhtrf37puwvgi4c9ajp60|Hanji 688]] says Liu Xiu has weapons piled near Yiyang, feeds the remaining Chimei people, and displays troops at the Luo River.
- Settlement boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-688-cong-yongbing-baiwan-dao-kuibuchengjun-dengyu-jingli-le-shenme-lpabdvtmhtrf37puwvgi4c9ajp60|Hanji 688]] says leaders' households are relocated to Luoyang with land and housing, while Fan Chong and Feng An are killed after later plotting.

## Qualifications

This page does not reconstruct the full Chimei chronology, internal leadership structure, or exact force numbers. Wang Mang 126 gives the collapse-context layer rather than a Chimei-centered account. Hanji 688 gives a focused Jianwu 3 surrender layer, but Liu Gong, Xu Xuan, Yang Yin, Fan Chong, Feng An, Xie Lu, Deng Hong, and exact surrender counts remain source-scoped unless more evidence accumulates.
