# Pope Manufacturing Company

Pope Manufacturing Company is [[AlbertPope]]'s bicycle company in [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]]. The episode uses it and the Columbia bicycle brand to show how U.S. bicycle commercialization joined patents, manufacturing, pricing discipline, lifestyle marketing, and organized road lobbying.

The company is a useful wiki node because it sits between [[BicycleProductEvolution]] and [[GoodRoadsMovement]]. Its growth depended on the technical appeal of faster bicycles, but it also required legal control, road improvements, and consumer finance that made high-priced bicycles easier to buy.
