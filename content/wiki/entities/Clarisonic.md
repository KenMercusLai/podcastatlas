# Clarisonic

## Overview
Clarisonic is the beauty-device company where [[KarissaBodnar]] worked before founding [[ThriveCausemetics]].

## Current Profile
In the source, Clarisonic functions as Bodnar's pre-founder product-development training ground. The episode describes the company as pioneering a facial cleansing device and then being acquired by [[LOreal]], which gave Bodnar exposure to chemists, engineers, and luxury beauty product work.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a beauty-device product-development context for Bodnar before Thrive Causemetics.
- Connects hands-on cosmetic interest with corporate beauty operations.
- Becomes part of the L'Oreal branch after acquisition in the source account.
