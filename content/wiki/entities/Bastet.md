# Bastet / 巴斯特

Bastet / 巴斯特 is the Egyptian cat deity foregrounded in [[34-maomi-mishi-he-yuchun-you-keai-de-renlei-601590607]]. The episode presents her as the strongest example of [[CatReligiousMythology]]: a divine figure linked to protection, family, household security, and the special status of cats in ancient Egypt.

The source also uses Bastet to connect religious image, law, festival, and city. Bubastis appears as a dedicated cult center, cat amulets and legal penalties make reverence material, and the episode's retelling of Herodotus-style festival scenes turns cat worship into a public social world rather than a private pet preference.

## Key Claims
- Bastet helps explain why Egyptian cat reverence is not only affection but a religious and civic order around protection.
- The episode uses Bastet as a hinge between practical cat usefulness and sacred symbolism.
- The Bastet material should be stored as source-scoped cultural history unless corroborated by separate sources.
