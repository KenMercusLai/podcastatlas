# Wang Bo / 汪波

Wang Bo is the semiconductor researcher and author interviewed in [[zhenzheng-gaibian-shijie-de-jishu-weishenme-yikaishi-dou-bu-bei-kanhao-s10e16-8c95b3dc-d75a-4bdd-84d4-2c06fd2d85b1]]. The episode identifies him as the author of [[ABriefHistoryOfChips|《芯片简史》]] and uses his account to connect semiconductor history with AI-era technology anxiety.

His main contribution in this source is historical framing. He treats [[MooreLaw]] as an industry-coordination target, explains why [[MOSFET|MOS transistors]] were initially unattractive to [[BellLabs]], compares [[Huawei]]'s [[TauLaw]] with earlier law-like semiconductor roadmaps, and argues that AI does not remove the need for [[DomainKnowHowMoat|domain know-how]].
