# 郑繻公 / Duke Xu of Zheng

郑繻公 / Duke Xu of Zheng appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]] as the [[ZhengState|郑国]] ruler killed by the remaining faction of [[ZiyangZheng|子阳]]. After his death, his younger brother becomes [[ZhengKangGong|郑康公]].

The page extends the Zheng sequence from part 1. There, the episode described Ziyang's killing and treated it as a sign of [[SpringAutumnToWarringStatesPoliticalViolence]]; this later notice shows the revenge-and-installation phase of the same factional conflict.
