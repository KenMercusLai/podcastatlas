# Operation RYAN / 莱恩行动

Operation RYAN / 莱恩行动 is the [[KGB]] global intelligence effort discussed in [[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]]. The episode says [[YuriAndropov]] told Soviet intelligence that the [[UnitedStates]] might be preparing a first nuclear strike, after which KGB networks were ordered to watch for signs of NATO attack preparation.

The source presents RYAN as [[ColdWarNuclearMisperception]] in operational form. Instead of asking whether a war scare was true, the intelligence system was pushed to find evidence for a leadership fear. Its indicators could become absurd, including attention to religious leaders, bankers, slaughterhouses, and blood-donation patterns.
