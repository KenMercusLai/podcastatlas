# Operation RYAN / 莱恩行动

## Overview

Operation RYAN was a Soviet global intelligence effort directing [[KGB]] networks to search for indicators that the [[UnitedStates]] and [[NATO]] were preparing a nuclear first strike.

## Current Profile

[[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] presents RYAN as [[ColdWarNuclearMisperception]] in operational form: [[YuriAndropov]]'s leadership fear became a collection requirement, giving officers incentives to report confirmation rather than test alternatives. [[92-nuclear-weapons-glt2236644203]] adds how ordinary activity could acquire strategic meaning, including blood-donor appeals and lights in government buildings. By the time of [[AbleArcher83|Able Archer 83]], the warning system helped turn a labeled exercise and an accumulation of unrelated events into a plausible attack narrative.

## Key Characteristics

- Global Soviet intelligence collection program focused on warning of Western nuclear attack.
- Leadership premise translated into indicators and reporting incentives across a bureaucracy.
- Included military warning signs alongside highly ambiguous civilian or administrative observations.
- Risked confirming the fear it was meant to investigate.
- Made later exercises and incidents legible within a preexisting first-strike narrative.

## Qualifications

The sources explain the operation through Western-facing podcast narratives and do not provide its complete directives, reporting record, internal dissent, or Soviet archival context. The new episode calls it “Operation Riyadh,” which this wiki treats as a transcription or rendering variant of the already established Operation RYAN identity. Absurd indicators show vulnerability to confirmation bias but do not establish that every participant or report was irrational.
