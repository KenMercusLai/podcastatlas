# United Auto Workers / UAW

The United Auto Workers appear in [[socialist-mobility-a-telling-michigan-vote-6a74600ec3e1347e8b5bf216]] as part of the traditional Democratic base in [[Michigan]]. [[JohnPrideaux]] names the car industry and a large UAW presence when explaining why a left-wing statewide primary win in Michigan is more politically revealing than a victory in a safely liberal city.

In this source, the UAW is not analyzed as an organization in detail. It functions as a labor and industrial signal inside the [[StatewideLeftPrimaryTest]] around [[AbdulElSayed]].
