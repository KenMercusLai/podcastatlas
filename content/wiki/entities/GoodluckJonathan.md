# Goodluck Jonathan

Goodluck Jonathan appears in [[piles-of-cash-and-a-town-of-solutions-in-kenya-nigeria-summer-school]] as Nigeria's president when the [[YouWinProgram|YouWin Program]] was proposed. The source says he supported [[NgoziOkonjoIweala]]'s idea for a national business-plan competition and told her to work out the implementation.

His role is mainly institutional. The episode uses the program's later shutdown after a presidential change to show why [[EvidenceBasedDevelopmentPolicy]] also depends on political continuity, not only measured program effects.
