# Jake Loosararian

## Overview
Jake Loosararian is the co-founder and CEO of [[GeckoRobotics]], interviewed by All-In at Davos about industrial inspection, defense, energy, physical-world data, and the path from robot diagnosis to repair.

## Current Profile
Loosararian's core thesis is that industrial AI is constrained by missing field data. Internet text and consumer video do not describe the internal condition of ships, bridges, dams, refineries, or power assets, so robots must enter those environments and collect decision-grade measurements through useful paid work.

He favors high-ROI, purpose-built industrial robots over household humanoids. His roadmap keeps humans in the loop while robots expand from sensing and diagnosis into supervised repair, manufacturing, and welding, with the goal of reducing hazardous work and making industrial expertise easier to deploy.

## Key Characteristics
- Focuses on defense, energy, manufacturing, and built infrastructure.
- Treats robotics as a physical-world data-acquisition layer for AI.
- Prioritizes measurable asset and production ROI over general-purpose humanoid form.
- Advocates rapid field deployment, failure, prototyping, and manufacturing iteration.
- Retains supervision and teleoperation during the transition toward greater autonomy.
- Connects Pittsburgh's industrial history to a broader reindustrialization agenda.
