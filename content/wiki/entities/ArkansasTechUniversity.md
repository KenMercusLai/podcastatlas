# Arkansas Tech University

## Overview

Arkansas Tech University is the institution whose digital content creation degree provides the educational case in a [[MarketplaceTech]] episode about college training for creators.

## Current Profile

[[tech-20261013-1013-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261013-1013-mp-tech-pod-128]] presents the university through [[CharlieRandall]]'s student experience rather than through an institutional audit. The program reportedly combines platform analytics, editing, attention tactics, repeated publishing, and collaboration with creative writing and acting students. Randall says enrollment has grown since the degree began roughly three years before the interview.

## Key Characteristics

- Offers the digital content creation degree discussed in the episode.
- Uses practical publishing assignments alongside analysis of audience and platform performance.
- Connects content creation with creative writing, theater, filming, camera work, and performance.
- Allows creator skills to support broader goals such as acting portfolios and organizational social-media work.
- Appears to be attracting more students, according to Randall's observation.
