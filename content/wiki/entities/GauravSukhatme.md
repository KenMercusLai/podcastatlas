# Gaurav Sukhatme

Gaurav Sukhatme appears in [[tech-20260730-0730-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260730-0730-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the leader of the [[USCStevensSchoolForComputingAndArtificialIntelligence]]. He explains USC's new AI school as a campus-wide education project rather than only a computer-science expansion.

His main contribution to the wiki is [[ProjectDrivenAICurriculum]]. Sukhatme argues that project-driven teaching lets a university refresh AI electives as the technology changes, while academia can also ask long-horizon AI questions that industry incentives and compute races may not prioritize.
