# 朱鸡石 / 朱吉石 / Zhu Jishi

朱鸡石 / 朱吉石 / Zhu Jishi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-3-chensheng-zhisi-weishenme-dele-minxin-hanle-renxin-lnx4zs-vsi3zeoixnqskkpexmr3|Qinji 129-3]] as the partner of [[QinJiaRebel|秦嘉]] in a local anti-Qin rising. The earlier transcript wrote 朱吉石; [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-2-lishi-jilu-shouci-tucheng-lm4kuzdpnqn-hicyuvvb4p2wdudm|Qinji 130-2]] writes 朱鸡石. This page preserves both forms under the existing route.

The source gives Zhu Jishi little independent action after naming him. His role is therefore source-scoped as part of the Tan-area rebel force whose relationship to [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]] becomes unstable when Qin Jia rejects [[WuPingJunPan|武平君畔]] and kills him under a false Chen Sheng order.

Qinji 130-2 gives him a later battlefield endpoint after [[XiangLiang|项梁]] defeats Qin Jia and absorbs Qin Jia's troops. Xiang Liang sends Zhu Jishi and [[YuFanjun|余樊君]] against [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] near Li County; Zhu Jishi loses and flees to Huling while Yu Fanjun dies. Xiang Liang then kills Zhu Jishi after entering Xue County, and the episode suggests the deployment may reflect Xiang Liang using newly attached troops before risking his own core force.
