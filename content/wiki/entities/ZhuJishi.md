# 朱吉石 / Zhu Jishi

朱吉石 / Zhu Jishi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-3-chensheng-zhisi-weishenme-dele-minxin-hanle-renxin-lnx4zs-vsi3zeoixnqskkpexmr3|Qinji 129-3]] as the partner of [[QinJiaRebel|秦嘉]] in a local anti-Qin rising. The episode says the two gather troops and surround the Donghai commandery governor at Tan.

The source gives Zhu Jishi little independent action after naming him. His role is therefore source-scoped as part of the Tan-area rebel force whose relationship to [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]] becomes unstable when Qin Jia rejects [[WuPingJunPan|武平君畔]] and kills him under a false Chen Sheng order.
