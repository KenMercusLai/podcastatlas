# Angus Fletcher

## Overview
Angus Fletcher is a narrative scholar and friend of [[GaryGoldman]] who brings Goldman's Zootopia dispute to [[MalcolmGladwell]] in [[zootopia-exposed-part-one-zootopia-exposed-part-one]].

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Fletcher serves as advocate, narrative analyst, and hypothesis builder. He treats Goldman's proposed animal world as a myth-scale concept and reads Gary De'Snake's stolen family invention in [[Zootopia2Film|Zootopia 2]] as an allegory of Goldman's allegation against Disney. His interpretation organizes the episode's mystery but is not independent evidence of copying or filmmaker intent.

## Key Characteristics
- Narrative scholar who frames the dispute as a layered story requiring extended investigation.
- Friend and advocate who introduces Goldman's account to Gladwell.
- Interpreter of Goldman's original concept as a world capable of supporting many stories.
- Proponent of the theory that the sequel allegorizes Goldman's dispute.
- Source of hypotheses rather than a participant in Disney's production or the underlying litigation.

## Evidence
### Investigative role
- [[zootopia-exposed-part-one-zootopia-exposed-part-one]] says Fletcher contacted Gladwell on Goldman's behalf and argued that the story's layers required two episodes.

### Narrative interpretation
- [[zootopia-exposed-part-one-zootopia-exposed-part-one]] attributes to Fletcher the view that Goldman's concept could sustain a mythic universe and that the sequel turns his dispute into allegory.
- [[zootopia-exposed-part-one-zootopia-exposed-part-one]] also records Fletcher's suggestion that a favorable portrayal of Gary somehow passed through Disney's legal and corporate systems.

## Qualifications
The profile is limited to one structured podcast summary. Friendship and advocacy give Fletcher access to Goldman's perspective but do not establish access to Disney's internal decisions. His claims about allegory, corporate review, and concealed intent remain interpretations rather than production evidence.
