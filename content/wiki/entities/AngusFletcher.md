# Angus Fletcher

## Overview

Angus Fletcher is a narrative scholar and screenwriter represented here through his collaboration with [[GaryGoldman]], his interpretation of Goldman's Zootopia dispute, and his critique of stories that surrender audience reasoning to predetermined answers.

## Current Profile

In [[the-variable-man-with-gary-goldman-and-angus-fletcher-development-hell-the-variable-man-with-gary-goldman-and-angus-fle|The Variable Man]], Fletcher explains how research into Pixar's storytelling led to screenwriting and collaboration with Goldman. Their adaptation makes passive acceptance part of the form as well as the theme: if viewers cannot reason through Cole's conflict, they risk surrendering judgment to Plato or to a familiar plot answer just as the future society does.

That concern continues in [[in-defense-of-paw-patrol-in-defense-of-paw-patrol|In Defense of PAW Patrol]], where Fletcher's term “vacuous agon” describes conflict that leaves little genuine anxiety because competent authority immediately supplies the solution. In the Zootopia source, he instead acts as advocate and hypothesis builder, reading Gary De'Snake's stolen family invention as an allegory of Goldman's allegation. Across all three roles, he treats narrative uncertainty as an invitation to active thought while keeping interpretive hypotheses distinct from production evidence.

## Key Characteristics

- Narrative scholar whose film work connects story structure to audience cognition.
- Co-writer of an AI thought experiment designed to prevent easy surrender to intuition or computation.
- Critic of predetermined narrative when it removes counterfactual and interpretive work.
- Friend and advocate who introduces Goldman's Zootopia account to [[MalcolmGladwell]].
- Builder of literary hypotheses that require separation from legal, causal, or developmental proof.

## Qualifications

The profile is limited to three structured podcast summaries. Fletcher's screenwriting account does not supply the screenplay, research, or cited paper on computational narrative limits. His PAW Patrol claims lack a developmental study, exposure threshold, or child-outcome data. Friendship and advocacy give access to Goldman's perspective but do not establish Disney's production intent or the facts of the underlying dispute.
