# Kirsten Korosek

Kirsten Korosek appears in [[tech-20251229-1229-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251229-1229-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the [[TechCrunch]] transportation editor interviewed by [[MarketplaceTech]] about robotaxis in 2025. Her role is to turn the year's visible [[Waymo]] expansion into a broader industry status check covering hype cycles, consolidation, permits, competitors, regulation, public reaction, and profitability.

Korosek's framing is useful because it refuses to treat the 2025 robotaxi moment as either pure breakthrough or pure hype. She points to long development work and commercial permits as real progress, while still emphasizing that [[RobotaxiEconomics]], [[AutonomousVehicleRegulatoryPatchwork]], and [[RobotaxiLocalAcceptance]] remain open in 2026.
