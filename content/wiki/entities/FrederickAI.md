# Frederick AI

## Overview
Frederick AI is the early agent startup project [[WuHankun|吴瀚坤]] describes in [[no-232-wuhankun-ai-shidai-yanyuan-diyige-diufanwan-gkwriw4oa148aw4mzwtp-xj]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Frederick AI as a practical startup-task assistant for founders rather than as a settled company profile. Wu says it helped entrepreneurs execute early-stage tasks such as due diligence, pitch decks, and simple websites, and that building it taught him frontend, backend, React, TypeScript, and demo construction.

Within the wiki, its importance is mainly developmental. Frederick AI explains why Wu could later approach AI video as a maker with product and engineering habits, not only as an actor experimenting with visual tools.

## Key Characteristics
- It is described as an early agent product for startup-adjacent execution tasks.
- Its task examples include due diligence, PPT/pitch materials, and simple websites.
- It gave Wu hands-on frontend and backend experience.
- It connects AI creation to product management and execution discipline.
- It is source-scoped to Wu's interview account rather than independently profiled.
