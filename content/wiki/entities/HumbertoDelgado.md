# Humberto Delgado

## Overview

Humberto Delgado was the Portuguese air-force general whose 1958 presidential challenge and 1965 murder exposed the coercive limits of the [[EstadoNovoPortugal|Estado Novo]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Delgado as an establishment military figure who became a direct political threat when he promised to dismiss [[AntonioDeOliveiraSalazar|Salazar]]. His official quarter of the vote did not resolve suspicions that a free contest might have produced a different result. Expulsion, exile, entrapment, and murder by the secret police then made his career a concentrated case of how the regime handled credible opposition.

## Key Characteristics

- Air-force general who challenged the regime from inside Portugal's military establishment.
- Independent presidential candidate in 1958 who promised to remove Salazar.
- Officially defeated in an election whose freedom and result were widely doubted.
- Expelled and exiled after becoming a political threat.
- Murdered in 1965 after being lured to the Spanish border.

## Evidence

- Electoral challenge: [[230-portugal-football-fado-and-fascism-part-4-glt9147423732]] records Delgado's promise to sack Salazar and his official 25 percent result.
- Repression: [[230-portugal-football-fado-and-fascism-part-4-glt9147423732]] says the secret police lured him to the Spanish border and murdered him in 1965.

## Qualifications

The source gives a compressed account rather than a full biography, campaign history, archival reconstruction of the result, or investigation of the murder. The counterfactual claim that Delgado might have won a free election remains source-scoped.
