# George Armistead

## Overview

George Armistead appears in the wiki as the Fort McHenry commander associated with the large flag that became the visual center of [[StarSpangledBanner|The Star-Spangled Banner]].

## Current Profile

The episode uses Armistead less as a full military biography than as the officer who wanted a very large American flag for [[FortMcHenry]] and had it raised after the British bombardment. That decision helped turn a limited-damage military episode into a durable national image.

## Key Characteristics

- Major commanding [[FortMcHenry]] during the September 1814 British attack.
- Ordered the large American flag made by [[MaryPickersgill]].
- Associated with raising the garrison flag after the bombardment.
- Secondary but necessary actor in the anthem's visual origin story.
- Military figure whose public memory is mediated through Key's poem and the flag.

## Evidence

- Command role: [[677-usa-the-star-spangled-banner-part-1-glt7890467734]] identifies Armistead as the Fort McHenry commander.
- Flag order and raising: [[677-usa-the-star-spangled-banner-part-1-glt7890467734]] says he ordered a very large flag and had it raised at dawn on 14 September.
- Anthem role: [[677-usa-the-star-spangled-banner-part-1-glt7890467734]] connects that flag scene to [[FrancisScottKey]]'s poem.
