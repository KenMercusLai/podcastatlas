# 横店影视城 / Hengdian World Studios

## Overview
横店影视城 / Hengdian World Studios appears in the supplied [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]] episode as a physical-production center affected by contraction and transition in China's screen-production economy.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Hengdian as a labor-market signal rather than as its main subject. [[MiaAIShortDrama|Mia]] reports that some companies there are subletting property or selling equipment as actors and production workers face fewer opportunities, while some practitioners move toward AI production. The evidence supports a dated transition signal, not a complete causal account of Hengdian's economy.

## Key Characteristics
- Physical hub for actors, crews, equipment, and live-action production.
- Used in the source as a visible indicator of screen-industry contraction.
- Connected to practitioner transition toward AI-assisted and generated production.

## Evidence
- **Industry activity:** [[gkwrirwo23ybaabwuwuauyns-gkwrirwo23ybaabwuwuauyns]] reports fewer opportunities and some property or equipment retrenchment among Hengdian-based industry participants.
- **Transition signal:** [[gkwrirwo23ybaabwuwuauyns-gkwrirwo23ybaabwuwuauyns]] says some peers in Hengdian are moving toward AI production as live-action work weakens.

## Qualifications
The page is grounded in one participant account. The source provides no comprehensive employment series, production count, property data, seasonal comparison, financing analysis, or evidence that AI alone caused the reported slowdown.
