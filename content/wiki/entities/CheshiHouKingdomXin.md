## Overview

车师后王国 / Later Cheshi Kingdom (Xin) enters the wiki as a Western Regions polity repeatedly destabilized by Wang Mang-era frontier pressure. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-613-quanli-kuangchao-jiemi-wangmang-de-tiexue-shouwan-lgfzc3ypqizddsmd6mvaasb1dxsd|Hanji 613]] adds the 2 CE [[GougouCheshiKing|沽沟]] layer, where a road project and detention push the king toward [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] refuge before public execution. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-640-qipa-wangmang-ruhe-chedi-bifan-xiongnu-lhxbx6keibmfe8z6bcsmlpnr6eht|Hanji 640]] adds the later 10 CE [[XinDynasty|新朝]] rupture, where anti-Xiongnu mobilization and burden fear produce another Cheshi flight branch. This page is disambiguated from [[CheshiHouKingdomLateHan|车师后王国 / Later Cheshi Kingdom (late Han)]], which covers later Eastern Han layers.

## Current Profile

Hanji 613 gives the earlier Western Han regency-era version. Xu Pu's proposed shorter route to Yumen Pass would pass through Later Cheshi, and Gougou fears that Han envoys and officials will make his polity bear reception and supply costs. His refusal to cooperate leads to detention, his wife Gu Zizhou reads detention as lethal danger, and Gougou flees to Xiongnu before being extradited and publicly executed.

Hanji 640 presents the later Xin-era version as a dependent Western Regions polity caught between Xin military demands and Xiongnu attraction. King Xuzhili fears that once Xin forces move west, his kingdom will be required to provide grain, money, troops, and costly reception. The burden expectation makes flight to Xiongnu look preferable.

The result is a local succession and allegiance rupture. Xuzhili's plan leaks and he dies under Western Regions Protector Dan Qin. His brother Hulan Zhi then leads more than two thousand people to Xiongnu. The chanyu welcomes them and uses them with Xiongnu forces to attack Cheshi and the Western Regions Protectorate's military staff.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Regions polity whose Wang Mang-era profile now has a 2 CE road-pressure case and a later 10 CE Xin rupture case.
- Illustrates how road construction and envoy traffic can look like new burdens to dependent frontier states.
- Illustrates how central mobilization can look like extraction to dependent frontier states.
- Its 2 CE king Gougou flees after detention, while its later king Xuzhili fears war-support costs more than he trusts Xin protection.
- Its elite split produces flight to Xiongnu after Xuzhili's death.
- Becomes a battlefield where Xiongnu support and local defection combine against Xin-linked authority.

## Qualifications

The sources do not provide a full Cheshi political history or reconcile these Wang Mang-era branches with later Eastern Han Cheshi episodes. Hanji 613 supports Gougou's detention, flight, extradition, and execution but keeps Gu Zizhou, Xu Pu, the prior Cheshi killing, and road geography source-scoped. Hanji 640 supports the Xuzhili and Hulan Zhi rupture but keeps Xuzhili, Hulan Zhi, Dan Qin, exact locations, and local motives source-scoped until more source notes support expansion.
