# 陈豨之乱 / Chen Xi Rebellion

陈豨之乱 / Chen Xi Rebellion enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-182-weishenme-shuo-chengye-xiaohe-baiye-xiaohe-1-lhe3h27wdbju3cgsvukdvgkri1a9|Hanji 182]] as a broad northern revolt that begins with [[ChenXi|陈豨]]'s refusal to answer [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s summons and his self-declaration as Dai king. The episode presents it as lasting roughly one year and two months and spreading from Han, Dai, and Zhao into a much wider north-China security crisis.

The rebellion's trigger chain begins before open fighting. [[HanXin|韩信]] privately tells Chen Xi that repeated accusations will eventually make Liu Bang personally campaign, leaving Han Xin a possible inside actor at court. [[ZhouChang|周昌]] then reports Chen Xi's large retainer train and frontier troop control, causing a directed investigation that makes Chen Xi believe return to the capital is unsafe.

The rebel side is not only Chen Xi. [[WangHuang|王黄]] and [[ManqiuChen|曼丘臣]] help connect him to [[HanWangXin|韩王信]] and [[ZhaoLi|赵利]], making the revolt overlap with the earlier frontier-containment failure around Han Wang Xin and Xiongnu-backed pressure.

The suppression side shows Liu Bang's practical range. He criticizes Chen Xi for abandoning [[Handan|邯郸]], refuses to execute ordinary officials who lacked the force to hold lost counties, rewards Zhao local strongmen at high cost, honors [[LeYi|乐毅]]'s descendant [[LeShu|乐叔]], and uses money to split Chen Xi's merchant-background generals. The event therefore extends both [[InvestigationDrivenRebellionEscalation|investigation-driven rebellion escalation]] and [[OccupationPacificationStrategy|occupation pacification strategy]].
