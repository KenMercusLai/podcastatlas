# 即刻 / Jike

Jike / 即刻 appears in [[no-214-xunzhao-tonglei-xiaohongshu-bilibili-yiji-wuhuabamen-de-naxie-shequ-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-26-1005977305]] as a product that began with interest-based information alerts and then added stronger community features such as comments, user-created topics, recommendations, likes, reposts, and profiles.

The source says Jike stopped service for 333 days in 2019. During that period, the team launched several apps, including [[Xiaoyuzhou|小宇宙]] in 2020, making Jike part of the same product lineage as China's podcast-platform community.
