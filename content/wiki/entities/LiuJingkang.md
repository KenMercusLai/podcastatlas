# 刘靖康 / Liu Jingkang

## Overview
刘靖康 / Liu Jingkang is represented as the founder of [[Insta360|影石 Insta360]], an applied-computing maker who became a global consumer-hardware operator across panoramic cameras, action cameras, AI-assisted imaging, and drones.

## Current Profile
[[nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352-nk78yhgkzrza-pj5ex42hwz7n352]] traces Liu's path from childhood programming and image interaction through university product experiments, a failed live-streaming startup, and the move into panoramic cameras. His recurring strength is connecting technical possibility to a user experience, but the profile also emphasizes acquired disciplines: market education, supply-chain cooperation, manufacturing reliability, legal defense, capital stewardship, and public expression.

Liu presents creation as personally more motivating than survival while rejecting that preference as a complete corporate mandate. His current operating position is therefore dual: pursue unsolved recording and sharing problems boldly, but judge the company through commercial viability, employee development, customer value, shareholder return, and its ability to survive long enough to build the next capability.

## Key Characteristics
- Applied-computing maker whose imaging interest began with software, cameras, interaction, and product use rather than traditional photography.
- Founder willing to enter new categories from an imperfect initial thesis and revise the product around observed user behavior.
- Hardware operator shaped by the difference between prototypes and reliable mass production.
- Mission-led strategist who frames competition around unsolved customer outcomes rather than defeating one named rival.
- AI adopter who treats machine learning as a change in the imaging-production route, not merely a feature label.
- Public-company leader who explicitly joins creative ambition to commercial, employee, customer, and shareholder duties.
