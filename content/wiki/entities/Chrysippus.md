# Chrysippus / 克律西波斯

Chrysippus appears in [[ep256-ai-shidai-ziyou-yizhi-hai-cunzai-ma-lk9aci8oqnwerk26jy683nbdddcx]] as the source's causal-chain example. [[TuMotuo|土摩托]] uses him to explain why any present act can be traced backward to earlier causes, many of which eventually fall outside the individual's control.

The page is source-scoped. Chrysippus matters here because he gives the episode a bridge from ancient philosophical causality to modern worries about [[CausalDeterminism]], addiction, social background, and [[MoralResponsibilityUnderDeterminism]].
