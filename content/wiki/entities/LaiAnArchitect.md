# 赉安 / 赖安 / Lai An (architect)

## Overview
赉安, also rendered in the source as 赖安, is the French architect whom [[WuFeipeng|吴飞鹏]] presents as a major shaper of modern architecture in the former [[ShanghaiFrenchConcession|上海法租界]] and today's [[HengfuHistoricDistrict|衡复风貌区]].

## Current Profile
The episode frames Lai An as a Paris-trained architect who brought modernist and Art Deco vocabulary into Shanghai while maintaining unusually high construction standards. His confirmed Shanghai corpus is bounded by Wu to 122 buildings, with emblematic works including [[GardenHotelShanghai|花园饭店 / the former French Club]], [[GascoigneApartments|盖斯康公寓]], and [[MajesticApartmentsShanghai|迈奇公寓]]. Lai An is contrasted with [[HudecShanghaiArchitect|乌达克]]: the source treats both as central but gives Lai An a stronger French academic and modernist-theory profile tied to [[LeCorbusier|柯布西耶]].

## Key Characteristics
- Lai An is presented as central to the built form of the former French Concession and Hengfu area.
- His work combines modernism, Art Deco, French training, and local Shanghai residential needs.
- His buildings are described as technically durable and unusually well preserved.
- Rounded, curved, and balcony forms become a visible personal design signature.
- His later life is shaped by war, business collapse, protection of Jewish connections, and unresolved disappearance/death details.

## Evidence
- Geographic evidence: [[498-xunzhao-laian-wei-jindai-shanghai-wutongqu-chuangzao-fengmao-de-faguo-jianzhushi-lskf8bdkhs71lbwjomt3gu1ko8be]] says many Lai An works cluster in the former French Concession, Xuhui, Hengfu, Gao'an Road, Anting Road, and nearby apartment/villa districts.
- Style evidence: [[498-xunzhao-laian-wei-jindai-shanghai-wutongqu-chuangzao-fengmao-de-faguo-jianzhushi-lskf8bdkhs71lbwjomt3gu1ko8be]] links Lai An to early Shanghai modernism, Art Deco, New Art influences, and the international modernist circle around Le Corbusier.
- Preservation evidence: [[498-xunzhao-laian-wei-jindai-shanghai-wutongqu-chuangzao-fengmao-de-faguo-jianzhushi-lskf8bdkhs71lbwjomt3gu1ko8be]] says only two of 122 confirmed buildings had been demolished, and uses Garden Hotel's foundations and continuing condition as quality evidence.
- Signature evidence: [[498-xunzhao-laian-wei-jindai-shanghai-wutongqu-chuangzao-fengmao-de-faguo-jianzhushi-lskf8bdkhs71lbwjomt3gu1ko8be]] identifies small balconies, semi-circular balconies, curved corners, and "Lai An balcony" vocabulary as recurring clues.
- Late-life evidence: [[498-xunzhao-laian-wei-jindai-shanghai-wutongqu-chuangzao-fengmao-de-faguo-jianzhushi-lskf8bdkhs71lbwjomt3gu1ko8be]] links his 1937-1946 decline to war, a Jewish wife, loss of business, extortion pressure after the war, and a 1946 disappearance followed by a letter containing a bankbook and will.

## Qualifications
The page follows the source's Chinese rendered name rather than asserting a full French legal name not present in the source. Claims about Lai An's influence on Le Corbusier, possible competition had he stayed in Paris, and his mental state before disappearance remain interpretive and source-scoped.
