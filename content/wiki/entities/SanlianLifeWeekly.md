# 三联生活周刊 / Sanlian Life Weekly

三联生活周刊 / Sanlian Life Weekly is the Chinese magazine and media context named in [[sanlian-chuantai-shen-ci-gei-ren-putao-shijie-weixun-le-585125156]]. The episode introduces [[Heimai|黑麦]] as one of its writers and uses his magazine work to explain how lifestyle reporting can move among culture, fashion, food, [[Wine|wine]], and ordinary changes in taste.

In this source, Sanlian Life Weekly matters less as an institution to profile and more as a reporting setting. Its annual wine reports, interviews with winery owners, and food/lifestyle coverage give 黑麦 the access and comparative material needed to treat wine as [[WineAsAgriculturalCulture]], [[EverydayFoodCultureHistory]], and [[FoodWritingAsLifeExperience]] rather than as simple buying advice.

[[ep236-lao-wu-kuai-qian-xinlihua-fufei-de-peiban-shi-zhende-ma-ltvuoeluoeu4-a3j3lamhc3buudx]] adds Sanlian Life Weekly's emotion-economy reporting side through [[WeiQianSanlian|魏茜]] and [[RuanJianing|阮佳宁]]. Their [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] conversation turns magazine reporting on plush toys, paid listening, wellness venues, ritual hope, and [[AIFriendProducts|AI companionship]] into a broader map of [[EmotionEconomy|情绪经济]] and [[ListeningTherapyBoundary|mental-health boundaries]].

[[ep238-2025-nian-ni-wan-guo-zenyang-de-xiaozhong-yundong-lpaw-kela5vk6avl-s11h-yexwlg]] adds the magazine's niche-sports lifestyle reporting branch. The cover-feature discussion links [[Heimai|黑麦]], [[SunRuoxi|孙若曦]], [[XuePeng|薛鹏]], and [[WuShubin|吴淑彬]] to [[UrbanNicheSports|城市小众运动]], showing how Sanlian-style lifestyle reporting follows venues, equipment, communities, bodies, and city conditions rather than treating sports only as competitions.
