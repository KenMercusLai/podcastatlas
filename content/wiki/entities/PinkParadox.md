# 《粉色悖论》 / Pink Paradox

## Overview
《粉色悖论》 / Pink Paradox is the first AI short film [[WuHankun|吴瀚坤]] discusses in [[no-232-wuhankun-ai-shidai-yanyuan-diyige-diufanwan-gkwriw4oa148aw4mzwtp-xj]].

## Current Profile
The work began as a small farewell to the role Zhu Yangqi after Wu acted in 《陷入我们的热恋》. He initially imagined a roughly 20-second demo in which the character could fulfill unrealized wishes in a parallel universe, but the project expanded as storyboards and story ideas accumulated.

For the wiki, Pink Paradox is a concrete early AI-video case: the source says script work was collaborative, while storyboard, image generation, video generation, editing, and music were largely done by Wu. It shows AI video as small-team feasible but still laborious and dependent on final-stage polish.

## Key Characteristics
- It started as a role-farewell project rather than a conventional commissioned film.
- It grew from a short demo idea into a longer AI short-film exercise.
- Its workflow required multiple production roles to be handled by one creator.
- Its value in the source is partly pedagogical: Wu learned directing and postproduction through making it.
- It demonstrates that AI lowers crew requirements without removing craft, iteration, or late-stage judgment.

## Evidence
- Origin - [[no-232-wuhankun-ai-shidai-yanyuan-diyige-diufanwan-gkwriw4oa148aw4mzwtp-xj]] says Wu made the film after playing Zhu Yangqi in 《陷入我们的热恋》 and wanting to say goodbye to the role.
- Scope growth - [[no-232-wuhankun-ai-shidai-yanyuan-diyige-diufanwan-gkwriw4oa148aw4mzwtp-xj]] reports that a 20-second demo idea expanded as story and storyboard work continued.
- Workflow - [[no-232-wuhankun-ai-shidai-yanyuan-diyige-diufanwan-gkwriw4oa148aw4mzwtp-xj]] says the script involved a teammate while later storyboard, images, video, editing, and music were mostly Wu's work.
- Final polish - [[no-232-wuhankun-ai-shidai-yanyuan-diyige-diufanwan-gkwriw4oa148aw4mzwtp-xj]] records Wu's claim that the last 5% of completion made the work become presentable.
