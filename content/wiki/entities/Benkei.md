# Benkei

## Overview
Benkei is the warrior monk attached to [[MinamotoNoYoshitsune]]'s legend in [[661-dawn-of-the-samurai-the-shogun-triumphant-part-4-glt7837412375]].

## Current Profile
The episode treats Benkei as part of Yoshitsune's legendary aura. It recalls earlier stories of their encounter and later narrates Benkei's final stand, where he is said to die upright, leaning on his naginata and held by arrows while Yoshitsune commits seppuku inside the fort.

Benkei therefore works less as an independently developed biography in this source than as a loyalty image. His death scene intensifies Yoshitsune's afterlife as a heroic, tragic figure.

## Key Characteristics
- Benkei is remembered as Yoshitsune's warrior-monk retainer.
- His final stand is one of the episode's clearest examples of legend shaping historical memory.
- The naginata visually links him to the warrior-monk and samurai-death tradition.
- His story reinforces Yoshitsune's tragic charisma after military victory turns into political defeat.

## Evidence
- Legendary pairing: [[661-dawn-of-the-samurai-the-shogun-triumphant-part-4-glt7837412375]] recalls Yoshitsune's encounter with Benkei as part of the hero's childhood and early legend.
- Final stand: [[661-dawn-of-the-samurai-the-shogun-triumphant-part-4-glt7837412375]] describes Benkei dying standing while Yoshitsune commits seppuku.

## Qualifications
The source presents Benkei mainly through legendary scenes and does not ask which details are independently verifiable.
