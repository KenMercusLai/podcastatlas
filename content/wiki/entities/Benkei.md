# Benkei

## Overview
Benkei is the warrior monk attached to [[MinamotoNoYoshitsune]]'s legend across the Dawn of the Samurai episodes.

## Current Profile
The episodes treat Benkei as part of Yoshitsune's legendary aura. [[660-dawn-of-the-samurai-japans-greatest-warrior-part-3-glt3581345898]] opens with the bridge duel in which the weapon-collecting warrior monk is defeated by the slight young Yoshitsune and becomes his loyal follower. [[661-dawn-of-the-samurai-the-shogun-triumphant-part-4-glt7837412375]] later narrates Benkei's final stand, where he is said to die upright, leaning on his naginata and held by arrows while Yoshitsune commits seppuku inside the fort.

Benkei therefore works less as an independently developed biography in this source than as a loyalty image. His death scene intensifies Yoshitsune's afterlife as a heroic, tragic figure.

## Key Characteristics
- Benkei is remembered as Yoshitsune's warrior-monk retainer, first attached through the bridge-duel legend.
- His final stand is one of the episode's clearest examples of legend shaping historical memory.
- The naginata visually links him to the warrior-monk and samurai-death tradition.
- His story reinforces Yoshitsune's tragic charisma after military victory turns into political defeat.

## Evidence
- Legendary pairing: [[660-dawn-of-the-samurai-japans-greatest-warrior-part-3-glt3581345898]] recounts Benkei's 999-sword bridge challenge, defeat by Yoshitsune, and conversion into a follower; [[661-dawn-of-the-samurai-the-shogun-triumphant-part-4-glt7837412375]] recalls the encounter as part of the hero's childhood and early legend.
- Final stand: [[661-dawn-of-the-samurai-the-shogun-triumphant-part-4-glt7837412375]] describes Benkei dying standing while Yoshitsune commits seppuku.

## Qualifications
The sources present Benkei mainly through legendary scenes. Part 3 notes that early sources mention a warrior monk named Benkei in Yoshitsune's following, while the huge armored bridge-fighter is a later elaboration.
