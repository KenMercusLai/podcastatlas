# 曼丘臣 / Manqiu Chen

曼丘臣 / Manqiu Chen enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-182-weishenme-shuo-chengye-xiaohe-baiye-xiaohe-1-lhe3h27wdbju3cgsvukdvgkri1a9|Hanji 182]] as one of [[HanWangXin|韩王信]]'s old followers who helps [[ChenXi|陈豨]] connect his rebellion with other northern anti-Han forces. The episode pairs him with [[WangHuang|王黄]] in the contact channel toward Han Wang Xin and [[ZhaoLi|赵利]].

The page is intentionally narrow because the source gives no separate motive or biography. Manqiu Chen matters here as a relay actor inside [[ChenXiRebellion|陈豨之乱]], showing how former frontier followers can keep a rebellion network alive after the earlier Han Wang Xin branch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-182-weishenme-shuo-chengye-xiaohe-baiye-xiaohe-2-lqk-jifupm9lbghfzaf09igfufgb|Hanji 182 part 2]] gives Manqiu Chen an outcome parallel to [[WangHuang|王黄]]. Liu Bang puts a heavy reward on both men, and their own followers seize and hand them over. The source uses that capture to show how quickly the rebel network can become internally tradable once the Han court changes the incentive field.
