# 主父偃 / Zhufu Yan

主父偃 / Zhufu Yan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-29-suqin-lizhi-shuangwen-zhishi-gaibian-mingyun-lrf5cqvoriyep2lkbozju-lyjaxv]] inside [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s poem to [[WuZhicai|吾之才]]. The poem pairs him with [[SuQin|苏秦]] as an example of a talented person who may be dismissed by close relations while still capable of later prominence.

The source uses Zhufu Yan as a reception analogy, not as a developed Han-dynasty biography. His wiki role here is to show how Su Qin's story became part of a larger consolation vocabulary for frustrated literati.
