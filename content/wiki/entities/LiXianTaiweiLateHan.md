## Overview

李贤 / Li Xian is the late-Han taiwei who, in the wiki's current sources, risks death to defend [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦皇太后]]'s right to be buried with [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]].

## Current Profile

Li Xian's current profile centers on the 172 CE court debate: despite illness, he carries poison, publicly supports [[ChenQiuLateHan|陈球 / 陈求]], and memorializes [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]. His argument treats Dou's recognized office, support for Ling's accession, and proper sacrificial status as matters of political order that should not be erased by her family's defeat.

The exact substance remains source-dependent. One episode identifies only poison, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-331-3-guren-jingran-yong-huajiao-zisha-lohtg2tifzlc63cxkcjkekoyk9w3|Hanji 331-3]] says Li Xian crushes and carries pepper, threatening to use it to die if the joint burial is refused. Together the sources make him a case of [[DeathDefyingRemonstrance|赴死式直谏]], but only the later-retold episode supplies the pepper detail.

## Key Characteristics

- Ill senior minister who nevertheless enters a politically dangerous burial-status dispute.
- Grounds his position in recognized empress-dowager status, accession support, precedent, and [[LiAsPoliticalOrder|礼制政治秩序]].
- Uses willingness to die to make silent or suppressible advice harder to ignore.
- Reinforces Chen Qiu's first intervention and converts it into a memorial the emperor accepts.
- Appears in one source with generic poison and in another with crushed pepper, leaving the exact substance source-dependent.

## Evidence

- Court role and argument: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-901-jixingzi-de-ren-hui-shi-shenme-xiachang-lh1yfyjzqptxvtnv9taiqms9yjud|Hanji 901]] describes Li Xian's illness, attendance, support for Chen Qiu, ritual argument, memorial, and successful burial outcome.
- Death-facing posture: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-901-jixingzi-de-ren-hui-shi-shenme-xiachang-lh1yfyjzqptxvtnv9taiqms9yjud|Hanji 901]] says he carries poison and expects not to return alive if Dou's status is denied.
- Pepper identification: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-331-3-guren-jingran-yong-huajiao-zisha-lohtg2tifzlc63cxkcjkekoyk9w3|Hanji 331-3]] says he crushes and carries pepper as the threatened means of death.

## Qualifications

Both inputs are short podcast summaries rather than critical readings of the underlying chronicles. Hanji 901 identifies poison without naming it, while Hanji 331-3 identifies crushed pepper; that extra specificity remains source-scoped rather than independently corroborated. The sources do not establish the preparation, dose, likely physiological effect, or whether Li Xian actually ingested anything. This page is distinct from [[LiXianLateHan|李暹 / Li Xian]], the Li Jue-side military actor in the 195 CE imperial-relocation branch.
