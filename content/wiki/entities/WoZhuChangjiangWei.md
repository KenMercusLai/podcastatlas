## Overview
[[WoZhuChangjiangWei|《我住长江尾》]] is [[DuanZhiqiang|段志强]]'s book discussed in [[yu-duan-zhiqiang-tan-chongming-xiangye-bowuzhi-wo-zhu-changjiang-wei-8212963740-775078]], where it is framed as a rural-life, farming, reading, and essay-writing project rooted in [[Chongming|崇明]].

## Current Profile
The book is presented as a work that grew from short farming notes rather than from a predesigned thesis. Its form mixes field detail, childhood memory, food-history curiosity, classical quotation, agricultural texts, and present-day village life, with the title preserved because it fits the book's plain starting point better than a more market-polished alternative.

## Key Characteristics
- Built from accumulated notes about planting, weather, food, neighbors, and rural routines.
- Uses farming practice to make old agricultural texts and literary references feel answerable to present experience.
- Refuses a tight argumentative center, favoring sensory scenes, loose association, and lived simultaneity.
- Treats Chongming rural life as future evidence of what was planted, made, reused, and felt in this period.
- Values restrained physical design, including the absence of jacket, obi, and celebrity blurbs in the episode's account.
