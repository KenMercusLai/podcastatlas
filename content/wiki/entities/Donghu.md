# 东胡 / Donghu

东胡 / Donghu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-3-jingke-ciqin-weihe-jiujiu-bu-xingdong-zai-deng-shui-liihe-kgwigu3bggxmyofgvotged]] as the northern frontier opponent defeated by [[QinKai|秦开]] in the episode's background note on [[QinWuyang|秦武阳]]. The source says Qin Kai had once been a hostage among northern Hu peoples, then returned to [[YanState|燕国]] and forced Donghu back more than a thousand li.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-175-bikan-liubang-de-huangjia-liyi-ke-los9hsomndnhyqpxafstgyquomzf|Hanji 175]] turns Donghu from a background frontier memory into the first major victim of [[MaoDunChanyu|冒顿单于]]'s steppe consolidation. The source says Donghu asks first for Touman's prized horse and then for one of Modu's wives; Modu yields both, making Donghu assume Xiongnu fear. When Donghu next demands a large empty borderland, Modu treats land as a state root, kills advisers willing to give it away, and attacks before Donghu is prepared.

This episode therefore gives Donghu a structural role in the Xiongnu branch. Its overconfidence is the condition Modu exploits, and its destruction clears space for the stronger Xiongnu frontier pressure that soon reaches early [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]].
