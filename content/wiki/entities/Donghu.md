东胡 / Donghu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-3-jingke-ciqin-weihe-jiujiu-bu-xingdong-zai-deng-shui-liihe-kgwigu3bggxmyofgvotged]] as the northern frontier opponent defeated by [[QinKai|秦开]] in the episode's background note on [[QinWuyang|秦武阳]].
