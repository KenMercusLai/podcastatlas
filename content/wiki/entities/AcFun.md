# AcFun / A站

AcFun / A站 appears in [[no-214-xunzhao-tonglei-xiaohongshu-bilibili-yiji-wuhuabamen-de-naxie-shequ-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-26-1005977305]] as the early Chinese bullet-comment video community that preceded [[Bilibili]]. The episode says creator 西林 built AC FAN in 2007 after seeing [[Niconico]], and A站 introduced bullet comments in 2008.

The source treats AcFun as culturally important but institutionally unstable. Many early remix videos, memes, and anime subculture practices appeared there, while loose management, founder exit, ownership changes, and later data-leak and operating problems weakened the platform.

For the wiki, AcFun matters as the predecessor that made [[BulletCommentCommunity]] legible in China and as a contrast to Bilibili's later professionalization.

## Connections
- [[Niconico]] - Japanese bullet-comment model that influenced AcFun.
- [[Bilibili]] - later platform that absorbed users and extended the format.
- [[Kuaishou]] - company that acquired AcFun in 2018 in the source account.
