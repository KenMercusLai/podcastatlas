# 岳钟琪 / Yue Zhongqi

岳钟琪 / Yue Zhongqi is the Sichuan-Shaanxi governor-general targeted by [[ZhangXi|张熙]] in [[11-yongzheng-wangchao-zhi-dayi-juemi-shijingqian-bi-xia-de-qiangu-qi-an-547265513]]. The anti-Qing letter addressed him as a military savior and tried to activate his status as a Han general and supposed descendant of Yue Fei.

The episode treats Yue's response as official self-preservation under a dangerous court climate. He had served under [[NianGengyao|年羹尧]], held a post associated with recent purges, and knew that mishandling the letter could implicate him. He therefore involved Manchu witnesses, interrogated Zhang Xi, used deception to expand the confession, and reported carefully to [[YongzhengEmperor|雍正]] through [[SecretMemorialPoliticalIntelligence|secret memorials]].

Yue's later fate also matters: despite his loyal conduct in the case, he remained vulnerable to suspicion, was later punished, and was only reused when [[QianlongEmperor|乾隆]] needed military competence. The source uses this to show that loyalty under personal rule did not remove exposure.
