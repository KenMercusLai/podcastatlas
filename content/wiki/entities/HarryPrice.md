# Harry Price

## Overview

Harry Price is presented as the paranormal investigator most closely associated in the episode with [[BorleyRectory]].

## Current Profile

[[66-ghosts-glt2309926019]] describes Price renting Borley Rectory for six months and investigating it with expensive, contemporary technical equipment. [[RogerClarke]] interprets his career as a movement from professional skepticism toward professional belief because the latter offered greater publicity and profit.

Price therefore appears less as a neutral verifier than as a figure at the junction of instrumentation, performance, commerce, and public appetite for proof.

## Key Characteristics

- Paranormal investigator associated with Borley Rectory.
- Used advanced equipment available in his period.
- Combined investigation with publicity and public storytelling.
- Accused in the source of following commercial incentives toward belief.

## Evidence

- Investigative role - [[66-ghosts-glt2309926019]] describes his six-month Borley tenancy and technical inquiry.
- Commercial interpretation - [[66-ghosts-glt2309926019]] reports Clarke's view that belief became more profitable than skepticism.
- Media afterlife - [[66-ghosts-glt2309926019]] places Price within the photographs, fraud disputes, ruins, and tourism surrounding Borley.

## Qualifications

This source supplies neither a complete biography nor an independent audit of Price's methods. The claim that profit drove his changing position is Clarke's interpretation and should not be treated as established private motive.

## What Changed

- Created a bounded profile of Price as investigator, media figure, and commercially interpreted believer.
