# 赵郝 / Zhao Hao

赵郝 / Zhao Hao appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-1-lq4wvkd8qx3q7zji6fnnkxi3qcl1]] as the [[ZhaoState|赵国]] minister left in [[QinState|秦国]] after [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] is released from his peace mission. The page is source-scoped because the supplied source does not develop Zhao Hao's biography. His function is procedural: the episode separates the symbolic release of Zhao's ruler by [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] from the still-unfinished bargaining over land.
