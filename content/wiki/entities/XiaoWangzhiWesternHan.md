## Overview

萧望之 / Xiao Wangzhi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-501-hanyuandi-jing-ba-taijian-dang-fuqin-lml1qwuw3iujobnltbjymeyojd4g|Hanji 501]] as [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]]'s teacher and the earliest major figure in the episode to oppose Yuan's favor toward [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 501 treats Xiao Wangzhi as a high-capacity but temperamentally vulnerable counterweight to Shi Xian. The episode says Shi Xian regards him as dangerous because Xiao has ability and will not easily spare people he dislikes. That makes Xiao a priority target once Shi Xian begins consolidating trusted access to Yuan.

The source's main contribution is not a full Xiao biography but a political-dynamics role. Shi Xian is said to exploit both Xiao's pride and Yuan's resentment toward his teacher, setting a trap that ends with Xiao's suicide. In the source's reading, Xiao's death removes an important early constraint on Shi Xian's rise.

## Key Characteristics

- Yuan-era teacher figure whose authority makes him an early opponent of Shi Xian's favor.
- Presented as capable and unwilling to let disliked actors off easily.
- Vulnerable in this account because pride and offense make him easier for Shi Xian to manipulate.
- His death marks the weakening of organized resistance to Shi Xian in Yuan's court.
