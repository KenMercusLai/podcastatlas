## Overview

萧望之 / Xiao Wangzhi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-501-hanyuandi-jing-ba-taijian-dang-fuqin-lml1qwuw3iujobnltbjymeyojd4g|Hanji 501]] as [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]]'s teacher and an early opponent of Yuan's favor toward [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-469-weishenme-junzi-dou-buguo-xiaoren-kankan-xiaowangzhi-de-xiachang-lj8iji-c3scj6vznntavm6awolho|Hanji 469]] adds the first-stage fall: Xiao misjudges [[ZhengPengWesternHan|郑朋]], cuts him off too late, and is pulled into a Tingwei-custody and pardon sequence that damages his reputation. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-470-xiao-wangzhi-shasi-wode-chule-taijian-haiyou-wode-yufu-lof1c5nctali_zsy0hotqyocjgoe|Hanji 470]] then gives the fuller Chuyuan 2 death case: Xiao is restored to honor, but his son [[XiaoJiWesternHan|萧吉]] asks for vindication, [[HongGongWesternHan|弘恭]] and Shi Xian turn the request into a punitive arrest, and Xiao commits suicide after his residence is surrounded.

## Current Profile

Hanji 501 treats Xiao Wangzhi as a high-capacity but temperamentally vulnerable counterweight to Shi Xian. The episode says Shi Xian regards him as dangerous because Xiao has ability and will not easily spare people he dislikes. Hanji 469 and Hanji 470 fill in the earlier case behind that summary: first Xiao's circle is breached by Zheng Peng, the Tingwei procedure, and a face-saving pardon; then Yuan grants Xiao Guannei Hou status and contemplates high office, yet the hostile inner-court field remains intact.

The current source set makes Xiao's death a shared-failure case. Hong Gong and Shi Xian exploit Xiao's pride and Yuan's uneasy responsibility for the earlier case; Xiao Ji's vindication petition gives them an opening; Yuan allows a "temporary lesson" to become a public humiliation; Xiao's wife urges endurance, but [[ZhuYunWesternHan|朱云]] counsels suicide; Xiao chooses reputation over survival. The host still grants Xiao major status as an imperial teacher, Confucian minister, and Qilin Pavilion figure, but also stresses earlier conduct against Zhang Chang, Han Yanshou, and Bing Ji to keep him from becoming a spotless martyr.

## Key Characteristics

- Yuan-era teacher figure whose authority makes him an early opponent of Shi Xian's favor.
- Presented as capable and unwilling to let disliked actors off easily.
- Restored to honor under Yuan but still exposed because hostile inner-court actors control access and procedure.
- Poorly served by late personnel boundary-setting: he identifies Zheng Peng as corrupt only after Zheng has entered the circle and gathered usable information.
- Vulnerable because pride, moral reputation, and public humiliation make him easier for Hong Gong and Shi Xian to manipulate.
- His death marks the weakening of organized resistance to Shi Xian in Yuan's court while also showing the limits of reputation-first politics.

Opposition to Shi Xian:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-501-hanyuandi-jing-ba-taijian-dang-fuqin-lml1qwuw3iujobnltbjymeyojd4g|Hanji 501]] says Xiao Wangzhi is the earliest figure to oppose Yuan's favoring of Shi Xian.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-470-xiao-wangzhi-shasi-wode-chule-taijian-haiyou-wode-yufu-lof1c5nctali_zsy0hotqyocjgoe|Hanji 470]] says Hong Gong, Shi Xian, and related court groups resent Xiao's side even after Yuan restores honor to Xiao.

Trap and death:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-469-weishenme-junzi-dou-buguo-xiaoren-kankan-xiaowangzhi-de-xiachang-lj8iji-c3scj6vznntavm6awolho|Hanji 469]] says Xiao initially accepts Zheng Peng into his circle, later warns Zhou Kan away from him, but is then exposed when Zheng defects and accuses Xiao's side.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-469-weishenme-junzi-dou-buguo-xiaoren-kankan-xiaowangzhi-de-xiachang-lj8iji-c3scj6vznntavm6awolho|Hanji 469]] says Hong Gong and Shi Xian use the Tingwei procedure and a pardon formula to make Xiao leave prison alive but politically marked as guilty.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-501-hanyuandi-jing-ba-taijian-dang-fuqin-lml1qwuw3iujobnltbjymeyojd4g|Hanji 501]] says Shi Xian uses Xiao's pride and Yuan's dissatisfaction with his teacher to set a trap, after which Xiao commits suicide.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-470-xiao-wangzhi-shasi-wode-chule-taijian-haiyou-wode-yufu-lof1c5nctali_zsy0hotqyocjgoe|Hanji 470]] says Xiao Ji's petition for vindication lets hostile officials accuse the Xiao side, and Hong Gong and Shi Xian persuade Yuan that arresting Xiao will only teach him a lesson.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-470-xiao-wangzhi-shasi-wode-chule-taijian-haiyou-wode-yufu-lof1c5nctali_zsy0hotqyocjgoe|Hanji 470]] says guards surround Xiao's residence, Xiao's wife urges endurance, Zhu Yun advises suicide, and Xiao kills himself because old-age imprisonment feels intolerably shameful.

Court consequence:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-501-hanyuandi-jing-ba-taijian-dang-fuqin-lml1qwuw3iujobnltbjymeyojd4g|Hanji 501]] says Xiao's death leaves Shi Xian's power much harder to block.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-470-xiao-wangzhi-shasi-wode-chule-taijian-haiyou-wode-yufu-lof1c5nctali_zsy0hotqyocjgoe|Hanji 470]] says Yuan grieves, reproaches attendants, and keeps annual sacrifices for Xiao, but does not punish Hong Gong or Shi Xian.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 501, Hanji 470, and Hanji 469. It does not settle Xiao Wangzhi's complete career, exact legal case, factional network, responsibility distribution among Zheng Peng, Shi Xian, Hong Gong, Yuan, Zhu Yun, Xiao Ji, and Xiao himself, or the historical record behind the host's negative references to Xiao's earlier conduct toward Zhang Chang, Han Yanshou, and Bing Ji.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 469's prequel layer: Zheng Peng's attachment and betrayal, the Tingwei-custody trap, and Yuan's pardon narrative make Xiao's later vindication wound intelligible before Hanji 470's suicide scene.

## Relationships

- [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] - ruler and former student whose resentment toward Xiao becomes part of Shi Xian's trap.
- [[ZhengPengWesternHan|郑朋]] - rejected follower whose defection helps create the first accusation field against Xiao's side.
- [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]] and [[HongGongWesternHan|弘恭]] - court favorites who treat Xiao as dangerous and help engineer the arrest pressure that ends in suicide.
- [[XiaoJiWesternHan|萧吉]] - son whose vindication petition gives Xiao's enemies a procedural opening.
- [[ZhuYunWesternHan|朱云]] - student whose advice supports suicide rather than survival.
- [[TrustedEunuchPowerFormation|信任型宦官权力形成]] - Xiao's removal is one early consequence of Shi Xian's trusted access hardening into coercive court power.
