## Overview

萧望之 / Xiao Wangzhi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-501-hanyuandi-jing-ba-taijian-dang-fuqin-lml1qwuw3iujobnltbjymeyojd4g|Hanji 501]] as [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]]'s teacher and an early opponent of Yuan's favor toward [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-470-xiao-wangzhi-shasi-wode-chule-taijian-haiyou-wode-yufu-lof1c5nctali_zsy0hotqyocjgoe|Hanji 470]] adds the fuller Chuyuan 2 death case: Xiao is restored to honor, but his son [[XiaoJiWesternHan|萧吉]] asks for vindication, [[HongGongWesternHan|弘恭]] and Shi Xian turn the request into a punitive arrest, and Xiao commits suicide after his residence is surrounded.

## Current Profile

Hanji 501 treats Xiao Wangzhi as a high-capacity but temperamentally vulnerable counterweight to Shi Xian. The episode says Shi Xian regards him as dangerous because Xiao has ability and will not easily spare people he dislikes. Hanji 470 fills in the earlier case behind that summary: Yuan grants Xiao Guannei Hou status and contemplates high office, yet the hostile inner-court field remains intact.

The current source set makes Xiao's death a shared-failure case. Hong Gong and Shi Xian exploit Xiao's pride and Yuan's uneasy responsibility for the earlier case; Xiao Ji's vindication petition gives them an opening; Yuan allows a "temporary lesson" to become a public humiliation; Xiao's wife urges endurance, but [[ZhuYunWesternHan|朱云]] counsels suicide; Xiao chooses reputation over survival. The host still grants Xiao major status as an imperial teacher, Confucian minister, and Qilin Pavilion figure, but also stresses earlier conduct against Zhang Chang, Han Yanshou, and Bing Ji to keep him from becoming a spotless martyr.

## Key Characteristics

- Yuan-era teacher figure whose authority makes him an early opponent of Shi Xian's favor.
- Presented as capable and unwilling to let disliked actors off easily.
- Restored to honor under Yuan but still exposed because hostile inner-court actors control access and procedure.
- Vulnerable because pride, moral reputation, and public humiliation make him easier for Hong Gong and Shi Xian to manipulate.
- His death marks the weakening of organized resistance to Shi Xian in Yuan's court while also showing the limits of reputation-first politics.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 501 and Hanji 470. It does not settle Xiao Wangzhi's complete career, exact legal case, factional network, responsibility distribution among Shi Xian, Hong Gong, Yuan, Zhu Yun, Xiao Ji, and Xiao himself, or the historical record behind the host's negative references to Xiao's earlier conduct toward Zhang Chang, Han Yanshou, and Bing Ji.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 470's fuller death case, shifting Xiao from a briefly summarized anti-Shi-Xian actor to a reputation-vulnerable minister whose suicide follows a son-triggered vindication petition, eunuch manipulation, Yuan's weakness, and Zhu Yun's bad advice.

## Relationships

- [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] - ruler and former student whose resentment toward Xiao becomes part of Shi Xian's trap.
- [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]] and [[HongGongWesternHan|弘恭]] - court favorites who treat Xiao as dangerous and help engineer the arrest pressure that ends in suicide.
- [[XiaoJiWesternHan|萧吉]] - son whose vindication petition gives Xiao's enemies a procedural opening.
- [[ZhuYunWesternHan|朱云]] - student whose advice supports suicide rather than survival.
- [[TrustedEunuchPowerFormation|信任型宦官权力形成]] - Xiao's removal is one early consequence of Shi Xian's trusted access hardening into coercive court power.
