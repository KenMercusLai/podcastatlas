# 赵云 / Zhao Yun

## Overview
赵云 / Zhao Yun is a [[LiuBei|刘备]] officer whose current evidence joins family rescue during the [[ChangbanBattle|长坂坡之战]] to later territorial administration in Guiyang.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1094 places Zhao Yun inside the Changban collapse. A report says he has fled north, where defection toward Cao Cao is plausible, but Liu Bei rejects the accusation. Zhao Yun returns after rescuing Liu Bei's family members and carrying [[LiuChan|刘禅]], turning apparent desertion into a loyalty-under-disorder case.

Hanji 1109 then gives Zhao Yun an administrative-military role in Liu Bei's post-Red-Cliffs consolidation. His concurrent Guiyang appointment helps turn surrender into governed territory while [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] supervises revenue across the southern commanderies.

## Key Characteristics
- Preserves family continuity during battlefield collapse despite an accusation of defection.
- Appears at the later transition from mobile campaigning to territorial administration.
- Receives responsibility for Guiyang rather than only a battlefield assignment.
- Connects Liu Bei's personnel network to the new southern Jingzhou tax base.

## Qualifications
This page remains source-bounded and does not project Zhao Yun's wider literary reputation backward. Hanji 1094 supplies a rescue sequence but not a complete operational reconstruction; the exact family members rescued, route, dialogue, promotion, and later “seven entries and exits” imagery require broader evidence.

## What Changed
- Extended the profile backward from Guiyang administration to the Changban rescue.
- Added the false-defection report as a qualification on how loyalty is judged under battlefield disorder.
