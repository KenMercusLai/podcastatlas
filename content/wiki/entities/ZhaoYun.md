# 赵云 / Zhao Yun

## Overview
赵云 / Zhao Yun enters the current wiki evidence as [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s officer appointed to hold Guiyang after the southern Jingzhou commanderies submit.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1109 gives Zhao Yun a narrow administrative-military role in Liu Bei's post-Red-Cliffs consolidation. His concurrent Guiyang appointment helps turn surrender into governed territory while [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] supervises revenue across the southern commanderies.

## Key Characteristics
- Appears at the transition from mobile campaigning to territorial administration.
- Receives responsibility for Guiyang rather than only a battlefield assignment.
- Connects Liu Bei's personnel network to the new southern Jingzhou tax base.

## Evidence
- Appointment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1109-xizaochi-nu-pi-caocao-duonian-jiye-hui-yu-yidan-lukll8jb60nx2brhwhsw7gfrd-kt|Hanji 1109]] says Liu Bei appoints Zhao Yun concurrently to Guiyang after its surrender.
- Consolidation context: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1109-xizaochi-nu-pi-caocao-duonian-jiye-hui-yu-yidan-lukll8jb60nx2brhwhsw7gfrd-kt|Hanji 1109]] places the appointment alongside Liu Qi's provincial title and Zhuge Liang's fiscal supervision.
- Strategic effect: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1109-xizaochi-nu-pi-caocao-duonian-jiye-hui-yu-yidan-lukll8jb60nx2brhwhsw7gfrd-kt|Hanji 1109]] treats the commanderies' revenue as a durable base for Liu Bei.
