# José I of Portugal / 若泽一世

## Overview

José I appears as the Portuguese king whose confidence enabled [[MarquisDePombal|Pombal]] to exercise extraordinary ministerial authority during reform, disaster reconstruction, war, and political repression.

## Current Profile

The source describes José as relatively disengaged from routine rule and willing to delegate extensive power to Pombal. That relationship gave the minister room to reorganize finance and education, manage the [[LisbonEarthquake1755|Lisbon earthquake]], confront aristocratic and Jesuit power, and direct resistance to the 1762 invasion.

The 1758 attempt on José's life became the trigger for exceptional trials and a broad purge of noble opponents. Yet Pombal's inability to survive the king's death shows that the delegated authority remained the monarch's personal grant: Maria I could immediately reverse protection and force the minister out.

## Key Characteristics

- Monarch who delegated unusually broad governing power to Pombal.
- Patron whose confidence shielded reform and coercive centralization.
- Target of the 1758 attempted assassination used to justify exceptional prosecution.
- Source of personal protection that did not bind his successor.
- Reign associated in the episode with earthquake reconstruction and wartime defense.

## Evidence

- Delegation and reform: [[44-jianchen-ziji-tiao-chulaile-yansong-vs-pengbaer-houjue-lmx4poxetwxy625kriodizxlubou]] says José entrusted extensive authority to Pombal and later made him chief minister.
- Attempted assassination and purge: [[44-jianchen-ziji-tiao-chulaile-yansong-vs-pengbaer-houjue-lmx4poxetwxy625kriodizxlubou]] links the attack on José to special trials, torture, noble executions, and the attack on the Jesuits.
- Succession limit: [[44-jianchen-ziji-tiao-chulaile-yansong-vs-pengbaer-houjue-lmx4poxetwxy625kriodizxlubou]] says Maria I removed Pombal immediately after succeeding José.

## Qualifications

The source treats José primarily as Pombal's patron and does not provide a complete royal biography. The king's level of disengagement, knowledge of repression, role in individual decisions, and responsibility for the purge require broader evidence. Delegation does not eliminate royal agency.

## What Changed

- Established José I as the personal patron whose reign enabled Pombal's capacity and coercion but whose death exposed its institutional fragility.

## Relationships

- [[MarquisDePombal|彭巴尔侯爵]] - chief minister whose exceptional power rested on José's confidence.
- [[Portugal]] - kingdom governed through their delegated partnership.
- [[LisbonEarthquake1755]] - catastrophe that expanded the practical importance of Pombal's administration.
- [[CourtDependentMinisterialPower|君主依附型权臣权力]] - mechanism showing why José's protection could not survive succession.
