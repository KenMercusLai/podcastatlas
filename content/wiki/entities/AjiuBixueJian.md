## Overview

阿九（《碧血剑》） / A Jiu is the princess who shares a mutual but unrealized attachment with [[YuanChengzhi|袁承志]] and later becomes the nun 九难.

## Current Profile

The episode reads A Jiu and Yuan as parallel figures: both feel deeply, protect dignity, and fail to turn affection into decisive action. [[WenQingqing|温青青]]'s prior bond, A Jiu's status, dynastic collapse, her father's death, and her lost arm make the relationship structurally harder than a private confession can solve.

Edition changes shift the balance between implication and declaration. Older versions let address, danger, and farewell carry the feeling; revision adds physical intimacy, betrothal-like commitment, waiting, and Yuan's recurrent thoughts. This may strengthen A Jiu's emotional importance while weakening the coherence of Yuan's eventual nonselection.

## Key Characteristics

- Loves Yuan Chengzhi without converting that attachment into a shared future.
- Combines reserve and pride with willingness to wait.
- Bears political collapse, bereavement, injury, and religious withdrawal.
- Functions as Wen Qingqing's most credible rival because the feeling appears mutual.
- Changes most sharply across editions through increased romantic explicitness.

## Qualifications

The profile follows one conversational comparison of novel editions. It does not establish Jin Yong's precise revision intention, and A Jiu's religious future, motives, and emotional choices are compressed around the romance rather than treated as a complete character study.
