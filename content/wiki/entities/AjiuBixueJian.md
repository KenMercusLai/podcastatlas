## Overview

阿九（《碧血剑》） / A Jiu is the princess who shares a mutual but unrealized attachment with [[YuanChengzhi|袁承志]] and later becomes 九难, a teacher and parent-like figure to [[WeiXiaobao|韦小宝]] in [[LuDingJi|《鹿鼎记》]].

## Current Profile

The episode reads A Jiu and Yuan as parallel figures: both feel deeply, protect dignity, and fail to turn affection into decisive action. [[WenQingqing|温青青]]'s prior bond, A Jiu's status, dynastic collapse, her father's death, and her lost arm make the relationship structurally harder than a private confession can solve.

Edition changes shift the balance between implication and declaration. Older versions let address, danger, and farewell carry the feeling; revision adds physical intimacy, betrothal-like commitment, waiting, and Yuan's recurrent thoughts. This may strengthen A Jiu's emotional importance while weakening the coherence of Yuan's eventual nonselection.

The later 九难 episode adds a second life stage shaped by dynastic loss, injury, isolation, and revenge. Her treatment of Wei differs from her instrumental use of 阿珂: she selects 神行百变 to fit his aptitude, accepts practical care, shares danger, entrusts him with the dragon-vein map, and develops a bond the hosts read as reciprocal family attachment. Wei's companionship may soften her without erasing the revenge logic governing other relationships.

## Key Characteristics

- Loves Yuan Chengzhi without converting that attachment into a shared future.
- Combines reserve and pride with willingness to wait.
- Bears political collapse, bereavement, injury, and religious withdrawal.
- Functions as Wen Qingqing's most credible rival because the feeling appears mutual.
- Changes most sharply across editions through increased romantic explicitness.
- Adapts instruction to Wei Xiaobao's abilities rather than imposing a full inherited curriculum.
- Combines genuine care for Wei with revenge-driven instrumental treatment of other pupils.

## Qualifications

The profile follows two conversational literary discussions rather than a complete comparison of the novels and their editions. It does not establish Jin Yong's revision intention, and claims about 九难's psychology, the effect of Wei's companionship, 阿琪's background, and the precise purpose of training 阿珂 remain interpretive or speculative.

## What Changed

- Extended the profile from unrealized romance into 九难's later adaptive teaching and reciprocal care with Wei Xiaobao.
- Qualified her mentor role through the revenge-driven treatment of 阿珂 and uncertain account of 阿琪.
