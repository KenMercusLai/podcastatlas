## Overview
Shen Yue / 申越 is the AI trainer and enterprise consultant interviewed in [[yi-ge-zhongguo-fde-de-guanghuan-luocha-yu-jiuhuo-richang-s10e27-587619bb-62c2-485d-9f27-f0be0115a986]] about the everyday work behind Chinese [[ForwardDeployedEngineer|FDE]] projects.

## Current Profile
The source presents 申越 as a practitioner who moved from roughly twelve years of internet product-management work into AI training, consulting, and implementation projects. His account matters less as a formal company biography than as a field report on [[ChineseStyleFDE]]: he describes FDE work as business access, customer coordination, MVP rescue, document cleanup, expectation management, and acceptance negotiation under vendor-side constraints.

## Key Characteristics
- He is source-scoped as an AI trainer and enterprise consultant with prior internet product-management experience.
- He uses the FDE label cautiously, saying he mostly used to describe the work as AI projects before the role became fashionable.
- His state-owned enterprise case emphasizes authority and business access: without a customer-side owner who can mobilize departments, AI demos cannot prove value.
- His private-enterprise case emphasizes ROI pressure, measurable usage, token cost, labor-saving expectations, and the risk of projecting business anxiety onto AI.
- His capability model places communication, industry experience, and AI cognition before technical implementation methods.
- His implementation examples make data governance, RAG document cleanup, internal rules, and accuracy expectations part of FDE work rather than peripheral support tasks.
