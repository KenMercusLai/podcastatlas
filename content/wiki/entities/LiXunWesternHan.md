## Overview

李寻 / Li Xun is synthesized as a Western Han court figure whose authority comes from omen and disaster interpretation joined to practical credibility. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-579-zoujin-kexue-guyao-shi-yao-ma-lof7pvrnlzv2lmbzjshbx7vi2c2n|Hanji 579]] shows him reading a palace sound as Hongfan "鼓妖" and advising [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] to dismiss the newly appointed [[ZhuBoWesternHan|朱博]] and [[ZhaoXuanWesternHan|赵璇]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-581-han-aidi-zai-guishen-zhidao-you-duo-yuchun-ltok7kbzmuf-os84spseww0qjbrm|Hanji 581]] then shows the same omen-literate credibility helping [[XiaHeliangWesternHan|夏贺良]] reach Aidi's illness-and-renewed-mandate decision environment.

## Current Profile

Hanji 579 gives Li Xun a direct court-interpretation role. When a bell-like sound is heard in the palace as Zhu Bo and Zhao Xuan are about to receive appointment documents, Aidi asks Li Xun and Yang Xiong about it. Li Xun identifies the phenomenon as "鼓妖," a formless sound category in which the sign is heard but not physically located. He reads the event as warning that the ruler has admitted improper or empty-name figures into important office, then narrows the timing to an immediate personnel recommendation: dismiss the chancellor and Yushi Dafu.

The episode presents Li Xun as a more institutionally credible bridge than Xia Heliang alone. He is said to understand astronomy and disasters and to have earned influence through effective water-control work. That standing makes his alignment with Gan Zhongke's renewed-mandate method politically consequential: Aidi's search for cure and order reaches Xia through an adviser who already has court weight.

Li Xun later becomes one of the proposed beneficiaries when Xia's group argues that existing ministers cannot understand Heaven's intention. Aidi spares Li from execution but exiles him to Dunhuang with [[JieGuangWesternHan|解光]].

## Key Characteristics

- Combines omen expertise with practical political credibility from water-control success.
- Interprets the "鼓妖" sound as a personnel warning against the new chancellor and Yushi Dafu.
- Functions as the bridge between Aidi's problem-seeking and Xia Heliang's renewed-mandate method.
- Shows how technical prestige can lend court force to unverifiable ritual claims.
- Becomes a proposed governing assistant after the symbolic cure fails.
- Is punished by exile rather than execution.

## Qualifications

Li Xun's full administrative record, the exact nature of his water-control achievement, and how much he personally shaped Xia Heliang's proposals remain source-scoped. Hanji 579 supports his interpretation of the palace sound and dismissal advice, but not the physical cause of the sound, the full Hongfan tradition, or his private motive.
