# 金国威 / Jimmy Chin

## Overview
Jimmy Chin is a documentary director, climber, and high-altitude photographer who appears in the source as co-director of [[EverestBeyondTheCloudsFilm|《珠穆朗玛：云中彼岸》]] and friend of [[JimMorrisonSkiMountaineer|吉姆·莫里森]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Chin less as an advocate for danger in itself than as a participant who refuses to let fear alone determine a life. He wants his children to find purpose and become themselves, while acknowledging that responsibility to children and responsibility to self may remain irreconcilable. After [[HilareeNelson|希拉里·尼尔森]]'s death, he says he supported Morrison as a friend without directing whether the Everest project should continue.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines documentary filmmaking with climbing and high-altitude image-making.
- Treats adventure broadly as a purpose-bearing pursuit rather than only extreme mountaineering.
- Argues that avoiding risk can also cost growth, creation, relationship, and lived experience.
- Acknowledges conflict between parental duty and personal vocation instead of claiming a universal resolution.
- Practices non-directive friendship in the aftermath of catastrophic loss.

## Evidence
- Risk and family paradox: [[83178c10-cba6-4869-8b69-559da582009c-83178c10-cba6-4869-8b69-559da582009c]] records Chin's wish that his children follow meaningful callings and his admission that duties to children and self may not fully align.
- Cost of avoidance: [[83178c10-cba6-4869-8b69-559da582009c-83178c10-cba6-4869-8b69-559da582009c]] attributes to Chin the argument that the Everest attempt created experience, friendship, and a film that non-participation would have foreclosed.
- Grief support: [[83178c10-cba6-4869-8b69-559da582009c-83178c10-cba6-4869-8b69-559da582009c]] describes Chin accompanying Morrison through grief while staying neutral about whether he should continue.
