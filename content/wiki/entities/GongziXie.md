# 公子泄 / Gongzi Xie

公子泄 / Gongzi Xie appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] as a Zhao prince who contests the succession after [[ZhaoChenghou|赵成侯]] dies in 350 BCE. He loses to the crown prince, who becomes [[ZhaoSuhou|赵肃侯]], and then flees to [[HanState|韩国]].

The source uses him briefly, but the notice keeps succession conflict visible as one more pressure point in the same years that [[QinState|秦国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]] are negotiating and [[ShangYangReforms|商鞅变法]] is entering its second phase.
