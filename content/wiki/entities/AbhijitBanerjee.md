# Abhijit Banerjee

Abhijit Banerjee is named in [[piles-of-cash-and-a-town-of-solutions-in-kenya-nigeria-summer-school]] as one of the economists who won Nobel recognition alongside [[MichaelKremer]] and [[EstherDuflo]] for randomized approaches to poverty alleviation. The source uses the prize to mark the mainstreaming of [[RandomizedControlledTrials]] in [[DevelopmentEconomics]].

Within this wiki, his role is mostly contextual for the Busia branch. The episode is centered on [[Kenya]], [[BusiaKenya]], and specific studies, while Banerjee helps identify the broader research movement those studies came to represent.

## Connections
- [[MichaelKremer]] and [[EstherDuflo]] - co-recognized economists in the episode's Nobel context.
- [[RandomizedControlledTrials]] and [[EvidenceBasedDevelopmentPolicy]] - methodology and policy frame.
- [[DevelopmentResearchEthics]] - downstream question of who benefits from randomized evidence.
