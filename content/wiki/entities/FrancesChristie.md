# Frances Christie

## Overview

Frances Christie appears as a Sotheby's modern British and Irish art specialist and the curator whose Platinum Jubilee exhibition supplies the object-led structure of “The New Elizabethan Age.”

## Current Profile

[[186-the-new-elizabethan-age-glt7640926802]] presents Christie as Deputy Chairman of Sotheby's and curator of a free public festival built around royal portraits, tiaras, modern British art, talks, and family events. Her selections connect [[ElizabethII|Elizabeth II's]] reign to celebrity imagery, coronation faith, punk provocation, postwar artistic creativity, Commonwealth cultural diplomacy, and [[DianaPrincessOfWales|Diana's]] place in the royal-family story.

Her interpretive method is object-led rather than biographical. A portrait, cartoon, Bible, poster, painting, tiara, or sculpture becomes evidence for how monarchy works through image, ritual, ownership, display, and cultural memory.
