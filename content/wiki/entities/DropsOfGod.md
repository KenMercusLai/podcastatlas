# 《神之水滴》 / The Drops of God

《神之水滴》 / The Drops of God is the wine manga discussed in [[sanlian-chuantai-shen-ci-gei-ren-putao-shijie-weixun-le-585125156]]. [[Heimai|黑麦]] treats it as a major popular gateway into [[Wine|wine / 葡萄酒]] because it puts technical tasting language, grape and region knowledge, and collector desire inside an inheritance quest rather than presenting them as dry instruction.

The source emphasizes that the manga's usefulness also has a time limit. Many wines in the story came from older vintages and a different taste era, so a contemporary reader should not assume that the same estates or labels will taste identical decades later. In the wiki, this makes the manga a case for both [[TasteTraining]] and [[WineTerroir]]: stories can teach attention, but taste still changes with time, people, and production.
