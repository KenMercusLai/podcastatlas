# Valley of Shadows

## Overview

Valley of Shadows is an investigative podcast series introduced through a [[RevisionistHistory]] feed episode about the 1998 disappearance of deputy [[JohnAjay]] and the [[LosAngelesCountySheriffsDepartment|LASD]] response.

## Current Profile

The opening episode, [[from-valley-of-shadows-the-devil-s-punchbowl-from-valley-of-shadows-the-devil-s-punchbowl|The Devil's Punchbowl]], follows [[BetsyShepherd]] and [[HaleyFox]] as they reconstruct Ajay's last known movements, the wilderness search, the disputed suicide explanation, and allegations of restricted institutional scrutiny.

The series preview expands from an unresolved missing-person case toward claims involving the regional drug trade, a possibly unbooked gun, burial information, and departmental concealment. The opening episode explicitly treats these as leads for later investigation, not established conclusions.

## Key Characteristics

- Long-form investigative series centered on an unresolved deputy disappearance.
- Combines site reporting, witness chronology, former-colleague testimony, and public-records efforts.
- Treats institutional conduct as a second mystery linked to the first.
- Uses later-season previews to identify leads while withholding final judgment.
