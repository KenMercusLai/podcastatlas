# Arsinoe IV

## Overview

Arsinoe IV was a Ptolemaic princess who escaped [[JuliusCaesar|Julius Caesar]]'s palace control during the [[AlexandrianWar]] and became a rival royal claimant against [[CleopatraVII|Cleopatra VII]].

## Current Profile

[[196-julius-caesar-cleopatra-part-2-glt3216275988]] presents Arsinoe as more than a passive royal hostage. She escaped with her tutor Ganymede, entered the anti-Caesarian camp, and was proclaimed ruler, creating a second dynastic center alongside [[PtolemyXIII|Ptolemy XIII]].

After Caesar's victory she was taken to Rome and displayed in his Egyptian triumph. Her survival at this stage left her as a continuing dynastic rival, a vulnerability later resolved through Cleopatra's alliance with [[MarkAntony]].

## Key Characteristics

- Ptolemaic princess caught inside Caesar's palace occupation.
- Escaped claimant who joined the Alexandrian resistance.
- Rival queen whose claim competed with Cleopatra and Ptolemy XIII.
- Captive displayed in Caesar's Egyptian triumph.
- Surviving dynastic threat after Cleopatra's restoration.

## Evidence

- Escape and claim: [[196-julius-caesar-cleopatra-part-2-glt3216275988]] says Arsinoe escaped with Ganymede and was elevated by the opposing force.
- Rival leadership: [[196-julius-caesar-cleopatra-part-2-glt3216275988]] places her within the fragmented royal politics of the Alexandrian War.
- Roman captivity: [[196-julius-caesar-cleopatra-part-2-glt3216275988]] records her display in Caesar's Egyptian triumph.

## Qualifications

The source gives limited access to Arsinoe's independent decisions, support base, command authority, or relationship with Ganymede. Her precise role in military operations and the reasons Caesar spared her after the triumph remain outside the supplied evidence.

## What Changed

- Established Arsinoe as an active rival claimant in the Alexandrian War and a surviving dynastic threat after Caesar's victory.
