# Tencent WorkBuddy

## Overview

Tencent WorkBuddy is Tencent's office-agent product in the wiki's Chinese AI-office branch. The bounded sources connect it to [[TencentCSIG]], [[TencentHunyuan]], enterprise productivity, and the wider contest with [[DoubaoWork|Doubao Work]] and [[QwenOffice|Qwen Office]].

## Current Profile

WorkBuddy now has three bounded roles. First, it is a product loop for Tencent's model organization: [[yao-shunyu-laidao-tengxun-300tian-1-176-1]] places WorkBuddy alongside [[Yuanbao]] as a likely early seed user for Hunyuan's reinforcement-learning platform. Second, it is a commercial AI-office contender: [[tengxun-ali-zijie-zhengduo-dagongren-hulianwang-dachang-weihe-jiti-jiama-ai-bangong-1008598460]] names it in Tencent's office-productivity revenue push. Third, [[272-cong-feishu-jizuo-dao-agent-youxian-doubao-gongzuo-all-in-one-jinzhui-workbuddy-lqmfcnfkkhoxt440qy26vwpnvswp]] uses it as the direct comparator that pressures Doubao Work on harness quality, first-party Tencent context, and AI-native product design.

The page remains cautious about identity. Episode 270 primarily names [[TencentWorkBody]] for Tencent's "AI-era Office" route, while later sources name WorkBuddy directly. The wiki keeps those pages separate until a source proves a rename, translation variant, or product-line relationship.

## Key Characteristics

- Tencent office-agent product connected to [[TencentCSIG]] and the Hunyuan application loop.
- Potential post-training, evaluation, and [[AgentRL]] signal source for [[TencentHunyuan]].
- Competes with [[DoubaoWork]] and [[QwenOffice]] in Chinese AI-office workflows.
- Reported by the newest source as temporarily ahead of Doubao Work at the harness and AI-native product-design layer.
- Benefits from first-party Tencent context such as [[TencentMeeting]], Tencent Docs, WeCom-style communication, and Tencent ecosystem connectors.
- Has unresolved naming relationship with [[TencentWorkBody]].

## Evidence

- **Tencent product loop:** [[yao-shunyu-laidao-tengxun-300tian-1-176-1]] names WorkBuddy among [[TencentCSIG]] AI applications and says [[TencentHunyuan]]'s reinforcement-learning platform would likely start with [[Yuanbao]] and WorkBuddy.
- **AI-office commercial route:** [[tengxun-ali-zijie-zhengduo-dagongren-hulianwang-dachang-weihe-jiti-jiama-ai-bangong-1008598460]] says Tencent and Alibaba executives described AI-office products as possible new revenue engines and names WorkBuddy as Tencent's focus.
- **Product-name boundary:** [[270-da-chang-yazhu-ai-bangong-feishu-he-dingding-que-xian-chengle-peijue-lmb4dgcgov3mr4cn7cikbghpfro4]] centers [[TencentWorkBody]] as Tencent's AI-office route, while later sources name WorkBuddy, so the wiki keeps the identity boundary open.
- **Harness comparison:** [[272-cong-feishu-jizuo-dao-agent-youxian-doubao-gongzuo-all-in-one-jinzhui-workbuddy-lqmfcnfkkhoxt440qy26vwpnvswp]] records participant judgment that WorkBuddy looks stronger than Doubao Work on multi-agent/harness behavior, even though visible function lists are converging.
- **Connector context:** [[272-cong-feishu-jizuo-dao-agent-youxian-doubao-gongzuo-all-in-one-jinzhui-workbuddy-lqmfcnfkkhoxt440qy26vwpnvswp]] contrasts smoother Tencent-side integrations with harder WorkBuddy-to-Feishu authorization, supporting a first-party context advantage rather than a simple connector-count story.

## Qualifications

- WorkBuddy's claimed harness lead is a source-scoped product-test judgment, not a stable market result.
- The page still lacks direct customer case studies, pricing, retention, or revenue data.
- WorkBuddy and WorkBody may be the same product, adjacent products, a rename, or a source/translation mismatch; the wiki does not merge them yet.
- Tencent's first-party context advantage may weaken for customers whose work data sits primarily in Feishu, DingTalk, or non-Tencent systems.
