# Tencent WorkBuddy

Tencent WorkBuddy is the office-agent product named in [[yao-shunyu-laidao-tengxun-300tian-1-176-1]] as one of Tencent's main AI applications under [[TencentCSIG]]. The source treats it as more than a product list item because it is likely to be one of the first seed users for [[TencentHunyuan]]'s reinforcement-learning platform, alongside [[Yuanbao]].

WorkBuddy therefore links Tencent's enterprise and productivity AI ambitions to [[AgentRL]]. If work-agent traces can be used for post-training, evaluation, and product feedback, the assistant becomes part of the model-improvement loop rather than only a distribution channel.

[[270-da-chang-yazhu-ai-bangong-feishu-he-dingding-que-xian-chengle-peijue-lmb4dgcgov3mr4cn7cikbghpfro4]] primarily names [[TencentWorkBody]] for Tencent's "AI-era Office" route and mentions WorkBuddy-like office use in an individual productivity example. The wiki keeps [[TencentWorkBody]] separate from WorkBuddy until a source confirms whether this is a rename, a related product, or a distinct office-agent surface.
