# Tencent WorkBuddy

## Overview

Tencent WorkBuddy is the office-agent product named in [[yao-shunyu-laidao-tengxun-300tian-1-176-1]] and [[tengxun-ali-zijie-zhengduo-dagongren-hulianwang-dachang-weihe-jiti-jiama-ai-bangong-1008598460]] as part of [[Tencent]]'s AI application portfolio. The wiki treats it as a Tencent office/productivity AI surface connected to [[TencentCSIG]], [[TencentHunyuan]], and the wider [[AIOfficeAgent|AI office agent]] competition.

## Current Profile

WorkBuddy has two main roles in the bounded sources. First, it is a product loop for Tencent's model organization: the Hunyuan source places WorkBuddy alongside [[Yuanbao]] as a likely early seed user for Hunyuan's reinforcement-learning platform, making office-agent traces part of post-training and evaluation infrastructure. Second, it is a commercial AI-office contender: the Shengdong source says Tencent leadership sees AI-office products as a potential new revenue engine at a time when every model call carries token and compute cost.

The page remains cautious about product identity. Episode 270 primarily names [[TencentWorkBody]] for Tencent's "AI-era Office" route and only links it to WorkBuddy-like office use. The newest source names WorkBuddy directly. Until the wiki has a source confirming the relationship, [[TencentWorkBody]] and Tencent WorkBuddy remain separate but adjacent Tencent office-agent pages.

## Key Characteristics

- Tied to [[TencentCSIG]] as one of Tencent's main AI application surfaces outside WeChat.
- Potentially feeds [[TencentHunyuan]] through product usage, office-agent traces, post-training, evaluation, and [[AgentRL]].
- Competes in AI office as a work-productivity and enterprise-service route rather than only a consumer chatbot.
- Carries Tencent's broader strategic need to convert model use into higher-value payment because token and compute costs scale with usage.
- Product identity remains unresolved relative to [[TencentWorkBody]].

## Evidence

- **Tencent product loop:** [[yao-shunyu-laidao-tengxun-300tian-1-176-1]] names WorkBuddy among [[TencentCSIG]] AI applications and says [[TencentHunyuan]]'s reinforcement-learning platform would likely start with [[Yuanbao]] and WorkBuddy.
- **AI-office commercial route:** [[tengxun-ali-zijie-zhengduo-dagongren-hulianwang-dachang-weihe-jiti-jiama-ai-bangong-1008598460]] says Tencent and Alibaba executives described AI-office products as possible new revenue engines and names WorkBuddy as Tencent's focus.
- **Product-name boundary:** [[270-da-chang-yazhu-ai-bangong-feishu-he-dingding-que-xian-chengle-peijue-lmb4dgcgov3mr4cn7cikbghpfro4]] centers [[TencentWorkBody]] as Tencent's AI-office route, while [[tengxun-ali-zijie-zhengduo-dagongren-hulianwang-dachang-weihe-jiti-jiama-ai-bangong-1008598460]] names WorkBuddy, so both pages remain separate pending clearer evidence.

## Qualifications

- The source set does not yet provide direct WorkBuddy product tests, customer case studies, pricing, retention, or revenue data.
- The source-reported WorkBody and WorkBuddy naming pattern may reflect a rename, translation difference, adjacent products, or a source error; the wiki does not merge them yet.
- WorkBuddy's value as an RL/post-training loop is a source-scoped expectation rather than a verified public result.
