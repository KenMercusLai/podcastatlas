# National City Bank / 国民城市银行

National City Bank appears in [[170-1929-de-paomo-zhixia-sange-daibiao-renwu-he-tamen-zai-dangxia-zhouqi-de-yingzi-lo7uv7umozr0jrqfjzyhdbzicx1j]] as [[CharlesEMitchell]]'s institution and as a predecessor context for modern Citibank. The episode uses the bank's retail network and securities-distribution activity to show how ordinary depositors or savers can be moved toward stocks, bonds, and foreign debt through a trusted financial brand.

In the wiki, National City Bank anchors the historical side of [[FinancialInstitutionTrustTransmission]]. The source's point is not only that a bank sold risky securities; it is that a trusted institution can suppress internal research concerns, frame financial innovation as access, and make risk travel from professional markets into household portfolios.
