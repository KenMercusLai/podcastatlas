# 咪仔 / Mizi

## Overview
咪仔 / Mizi is the creator of [[BlackCatDetectiveAgency|黑猫侦探社]] and the guest in [[vol-56-duitan-mizi-rensheng-shi-renling-ziji-yu-queli-shiming-de-guocheng-lgrbpfubc8s6y59jb6tsd6glz-c]], where she describes a path through vocal study, newsroom work, overseas journalism education, corporate public relations, underemployment in Switzerland, and independent crime-podcast production.

## Current Profile
The source presents Mizi as a nonfiction audio storyteller whose current craft joins abilities that once looked unrelated. Vocal training sharpened her hearing; newsroom practice gave her a strong fact-and-public-interest ethic; family storytelling provided an early narrative environment; and the Swiss employment crisis forced an experiment outside institutional roles. She plans episodes through writer, director, performer, and listener perspectives, using voice, timing, music, and verified detail to create mental scenes.

Her creator identity is commercially serious but ethically constrained. She wants the show to support its team, yet resists withholding essential public-event context merely to create a paywall. She also describes public vulnerability, criticism, grief, depression recurrence, and trigger recognition as learned parts of sustainable creator work rather than evidence that a creator has failed.

## Key Characteristics
- She combines vocal training, journalistic verification, and audio composition in nonfiction crime storytelling.
- She treats complete public-interest context as a listener right and paid material as enrichment rather than ransom for missing facts.
- She learned production experimentally, beginning with phone recording and self-taught editing before building a small team.
- She understands storytelling spatially and emotionally, designing what listeners should see and feel through sound alone.
- She is exploring courses and broader applications of narrative skill while remaining cautious about claiming expertise.
- She treats honest disclosure and trigger awareness as practices that can reconnect a creator with supportive listeners.
