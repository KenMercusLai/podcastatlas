# People's Action Party / 人民行动党

## Overview

The People's Action Party appears in the bounded source as Singapore's governing party during self-government, merger with Malaysia, the 1965 separation, and the construction of an independent state.

## Current Profile

The episode presents the PAP as both an anti-colonial coalition and an organization divided by language, class, ideology, and Cold War strategy. Its leadership used merger to secure decolonization and contain the left, then entered federal politics through a multiracial Malaysian project that threatened the Alliance's communal balance. Separation ended that national project but allowed the PAP to consolidate a Singapore-centered governing order.

## Key Characteristics

- Joined English-educated democratic socialists with a stronger Chinese-educated left before the 1961 split.
- Treated merger as a route to decolonization, common market, and political survival.
- Participated with British and Malayan authorities in Operation Coldstore before federation.
- Broke the practical noncompetition arrangement by contesting federal politics after Alliance intervention in Singapore.
- Promoted “Malaysian Malaysia” as both a multiracial ideal and a bid to reshape federal power.
- Combined public-service state building with strong political, labor, legal, and security control after separation.

## Qualifications

The episode is a retrospective political interpretation rather than a complete party history. It gives limited space to left-wing detainees, grassroots organizations, Malay political perspectives, later opposition, or independent evaluation of the PAP's electoral and security practices.
