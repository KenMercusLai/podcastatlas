# 《孤愤》 / Gu Fen

《孤愤》 / Gu Fen enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-112-2-qinshihuang-yeshi-tade-shumi-llkoy-zvhjjr6af9tflcw1xvogwz]] as one of [[HanFei|韩非]]'s major writings after his advice fails inside [[HanState|韩国]]. The episode presents the text inside Han Fei's broader frustration with rulers who cannot employ worthy and practically useful people while elevating shallow courtiers.

The source also gives the text an unusual political afterlife. [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] reads 《孤愤》 together with [[Wudu|《五蠹》]], admires the writing intensely, and wants to meet the author. In the wiki, 《孤愤》 therefore marks both Han Fei's failed internal advice channel and the beginning of his dangerous attraction to the Qin court.
