# Coherent Spark

Coherent Spark appears in [[ep-11-growing-technology-footprints-in-insurance-sector]] as [[Coherent]]'s product for converting [[MicrosoftExcel|Excel]] spreadsheet logic into APIs. [[NickBlamer]] says Spark can identify spreadsheet inputs and outputs, generate version-controlled and auditable APIs, and let those calculations run faster and scale in the cloud.

The product matters in the wiki because it gives [[SpreadsheetToAPIGovernance]] a concrete insurance example. Instead of treating spreadsheets as either disposable prototypes or uncontrolled end-user computing, Spark is presented as a way to preserve familiar business logic while making it reusable through [[BusinessLogicAPIs]] and connected enterprise systems.

The episode keeps the division of labor explicit. Business units can continue owning the business logic, while IT handles integration, plumbing, production promotion, and connections to enterprise platforms. That makes Spark an infrastructure example adjacent to [[ActuaryDataScientistPartnership]] and [[InsuranceTechnologyModernization]].
