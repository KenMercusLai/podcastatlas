# Shanghai Gold Exchange / 上海黄金交易所

Shanghai Gold Exchange / 上海黄金交易所 appears in [[ep239-he-daka-liaoliao-jinjia-you-shuang-ruo-zhuo-biao-le-putongren-hai-neng-shangche-ma-lgcy2trgzt7expvr5bcybybrummq]] as the institutional marker for China's gold-market transition. [[WangLixin|王立新]] says its formal establishment in 2002 was an important step from a planned gold system toward a marketized trading system.

The episode uses that marketization point to explain why gold's investment role depends on infrastructure as well as tradition. Gold can function as a liquid financial asset only when buyers have transparent prices, trusted channels, standardized products, and reliable routes for purchase and liquidation.
