# Carlton Calvin

Carlton Calvin is the founder and CEO of [[RazorUSA]] and the [[HowIBuiltThis]] Advice Line guest in [[advice-line-with-carlton-calvin-of-razor-c0de1a84-3d35-45f3-921c-6144693ad86e]]. The episode presents him as a consumer-products operator whose experience with scooters, copycats, retail distribution, and sales reps gives him a practical bias toward demand proof and channel execution.

His advice across the calls emphasizes [[ConsumerTrendSpotting]], retail presence, founder-led social content, and delegation. He pushes [[SeanBarassa]] to treat licensing as unlikely without stronger proof for [[EulogyGame|Eulogy]], encourages [[ShireenTippett]] to make [[HoneyBespokeStationery]] visible through stores and founder content, and tells [[MarkLittleWaterDistillery|Mark]] to concentrate [[LittleWaterDistillery]] around the products customers already want.
