# Gerry Adams

Gerry Adams appears in [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] as the later republican figure whose IRA membership is disputed in public memory. The source says Adams denies ever having been in the [[IrishRepublicanArmy|IRA]], while academic historians generally believe he was.

The episode uses Adams to connect the early [[ProvisionalIRA]] story to later republican arguments over settlement. Some older republicans' resentment toward the Good Friday Agreement is presented as rooted in their belief that terrible violence had been justified only by the goal of a united Ireland.
