# Exor

Exor is the Agnelli-family holding-company context for [[Ferrari]] in [[ferrari-1]]. The source says Exor and [[PieroFerrari]] retain major voting influence even though public shareholders own most of the economic interest after the spinout.

For the wiki, Exor is part of Ferrari's governance layer. It helps explain why Ferrari can be public while still protecting [[LuxuryScarcityDiscipline]] and resisting a simple volume-growth mandate.
