# 唐晶 / Tang Jing

## Overview
唐晶 / Tang Jing enters the wiki as the friend, career woman, and comparison figure in [[MyFirstHalfLife|《我的前半生》]] whose treatment helps EP385 distinguish the novel's female-support structure from the TV drama's triangle.

## Current Profile
The episode treats Tang Jing as the character who should have carried much of Luo Zijun's rebuilding structure. In the novel comparison, her long friendship and later life reversal make female support and female choice visible. In the TV drama, the hosts argue, Tang Jing is made less satisfying because her relationship to [[HeHan|贺涵]] and her service to [[LuoZijun|罗子君]] turn her into a tool of a male-centered plot.

## Key Characteristics
- Career-woman figure closer to the hosts' image of a Yi Shu heroine.
- Female-support node whose friendship with Luo Zijun is stronger in the novel context.
- Adaptation casualty whose TV arc is pulled into He Han's romantic and mentor gravity.
- Comparison figure showing that female independence can become loneliness or burden.
- Relationship-test case for whether female friendship is grounded or merely functional.
