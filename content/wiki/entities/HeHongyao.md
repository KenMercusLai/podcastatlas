## Overview

何红药 / He Hongyao is a [[BixueJian|《碧血剑》]] character whose unreturned attachment to [[XiaXueyi|夏雪宜]] moves from transgressive sacrifice through punishment into possession, revenge, and self-destruction.

## Current Profile

He Hongyao risks her position and her community's sacred property to help Xia obtain the Golden Snake weapons and treasure map. Exposure brings mutilating punishment, loss of status, and years of wandering. When she later finds that Xia loves [[WenYiBixueJian|温仪]] and regards their past intimacy as play, the meaning she assigned to her sacrifice collapses.

The episode assigns responsibility in both directions. Xia exploited ambiguity and desire for instrumental gain, while He Hongyao wagered collective property and her entire future on a relationship without an explicit reciprocal commitment. Her later attempt to imprison and torment him turns injury into control rather than release.

## Key Characteristics

- Converts intense attachment into theft, institutional betrayal, and extreme personal exposure.
- Bears bodily, social, and economic punishment after helping Xia Xueyi.
- Encounters explicit nonreciprocity only after sacrifice has become identity-defining.
- Refuses both clean departure and limited retaliation, seeking to compel return through captivity and pain.
- Ends with love, hatred, pursuit, and self-destruction no longer separable.

## Qualifications

“情魔” and “恋爱脑” are literary judgments quoted or debated by the hosts, not diagnoses. Lack of an explicit promise does not erase Xia's manipulative use of ambiguity, while exploitation does not make He Hongyao's theft, coercion, or torture inevitable or justified.
