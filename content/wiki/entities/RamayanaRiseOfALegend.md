# Ramayana: Rise of a Legend

## Overview
*Ramayana: Rise of a Legend* is presented as an exceptionally expensive Indian film seeking to turn the ancient *Ramayana* into an international screen and merchandise franchise.

## Current Profile
The production combines a reported $430 million budget, international creative talent, large-scale visual effects, and plans for spin-offs, games, merchandise, and theme parks. Early reception in the source is mixed: the visuals are praised and the retelling is considered faithful enough to avoid a major domestic controversy, but episodic plotting, wooden acting, and the dramatic imbalance between righteous Ram and flamboyant Ravana complicate both storytelling and global appeal.

## Key Characteristics
- Large-budget adaptation of the *Ramayana* made for Indian and international audiences.
- Uses international music and visual-effects talent to pursue global-blockbuster scale.
- Designed as the base for a wider entertainment franchise.
- Praised for spectacle but criticized for narrative and performance weaknesses.
- Constrained by the sacred status and cultural familiarity of its source story.
- Reported early worldwide revenue remains well below its stated production budget.

## Evidence
- Scale and ambition: [[left-in-the-lurch-spains-general-election-f9a9608c40ea7371c98aaab648d8d8b0]] reports a budget of about $430 million and plans for films, games, merchandise, and theme parks.
- International production: [[left-in-the-lurch-spains-general-election-f9a9608c40ea7371c98aaab648d8d8b0]] names Hans Zimmer and DNEG as contributors to the global-blockbuster presentation.
- Reception: [[left-in-the-lurch-spains-general-election-f9a9608c40ea7371c98aaab648d8d8b0]] reports roughly $150 million in worldwide earnings after several weeks, mostly from [[India]], alongside praise for visuals and criticism of plot and acting.
- Sacred-source constraint: [[left-in-the-lurch-spains-general-election-f9a9608c40ea7371c98aaab648d8d8b0]] contrasts the film's relative faithfulness with the backlash, lawsuits, ban demands, and edits associated with the 2023 *Adipurush* adaptation.

## Qualifications
Budget, revenue, reviews, audience reactions, and franchise plans are early, source-reported figures and judgments. The episode does not establish the final box office, full production economics, long-term international reception, or whether later installments will be made.

## What Changed

- Kept early revenue and global-franchise prospects explicitly provisional.
