# JAX

JAX appears in [[e228-guge-tpu-neng-handong-yingweida-ma-qian-tpu-gongchengshi-shouci-jiemi-fd17090c-0d72-4c0d-aa3e-9b00bc062149]] as one of the software paths that can expose [[Google]] [[TPU]] performance. The episode says teams that want to fully use TPUs often need to move closer to a [[JAX]] and [[XLACompiler|XLA]] stack rather than treating TPU as a drop-in [[GPU]] replacement.

The source makes JAX part of the TPU adoption barrier. It can help [[XLACompiler|XLA]] optimize computation graphs for [[TPUPodSystemOptimization|TPU Pods]], but it also raises migration, debugging, and engineering-talent requirements for external customers.
