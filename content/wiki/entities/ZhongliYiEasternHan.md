## Overview

钟离意 / Zhongli Yi is an Eastern Han official whose current wiki profile rests on Yongping 3 scenes of grounded remonstrance. He turns omen language into a concrete argument about people's farming burden and also recommends Liu Ping as a trustworthy local administrator.

## Current Profile

Zhongli Yi's strongest role is not abstract moralizing but practical translation. When a star appears north of the Tianchuan constellation and Mingdi begins North Palace construction, Zhongli Yi kneels at the gate, cites the self-blame pattern associated with Shang Tang, and argues that palace works are preventing commoners from returning home to farm. Mingdi stops construction, reduces unnecessary expenses, apologizes to ministers, and rain soon follows.

His second memorial shows the limit of that influence. He reads abnormal sky and weather as evidence that officials have failed to spread imperial grace and that harshness has become fashionable, urging moral transformation and lighter punishments. Mingdi does not adopt the advice, but protects Zhongli Yi as a sincere remonstrator. The same episode also links Zhongli Yi to Liu Ping's later service by making him Liu Ping's recommender.

## Key Characteristics

- Omen interpreter who redirects celestial-warning language toward concrete labor and farming burdens.
- Gate-level remonstrator willing to confront an active imperial construction project directly.
- Policy translator whose successful advice produces halted works, spending cuts, and an imperial apology.
- Leniency advocate who frames harsh punishment as a governance fashion in need of moral correction.
- Talent recommender who helps move Liu Ping from county governance into central service.

## Qualifications

The present evidence is single-source and concentrated in Yongping 3. Zhongli Yi's successful construction remonstrance should not be generalized into continuous control over Mingdi, since the later leniency memorial is respected but not accepted.
