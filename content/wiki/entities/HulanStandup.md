## Overview
呼兰 / Hulan appears as a comedian and panel participant in the [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] and [[SixiaoComedy|四孝喜剧]] live episode on "势利." This route is semantically suffixed to distinguish him from the late-Han rebel [[HuLanLateHan|胡兰]].

## Current Profile
In the source, Hulan supplies a relatively low-friction and principle-aware contrast to more emotionally reactive answers. He says he is less sensitive to transactional warmth and does not spend much energy guessing why people contact or stop contacting him. In the scheduling dilemma, he emphasizes prior commitments: if an ordinary friend's dinner was already arranged, he leans toward keeping it rather than abandoning it for a better opportunity.

The later school-admission and club-management sections complicate that stance. Hulan initially defends fairness for the poor gifted student against a donor-backed mediocre student, but admits that a sufficiently large donation changes the calculation. When he joins [[KaihuaClub|开花俱乐部]] as CEO in the source discussion, he argues that a workplace cannot function if every employee treats the boss as an always-available target for jokes.

## Key Characteristics
- Panel voice who often lowers interpersonal interpretation costs by not over-reading transactional contact.
- Comedian whose scheduling answer stresses prior commitment and relationship consistency.
- Fairness-oriented participant in the school-admission dilemma, while still recognizing scale effects.
- Management voice in the Kaihua Club discussion, emphasizing authority boundaries and work order.
- Source-scoped figure currently represented only through this single live-panel episode.
