# Ivan Brown

Ivan Brown was an Australian expatriate manufacturing partner in the Philippines who helped [[RoySeiders]] and [[RyanSeiders]] develop YETI coolers, as discussed in [[yeti-ron-and-ryan-seiders-how-two-brothers-turned-a-400-cooler-into-a-2-billion-brand-d7f8e0e8-51a5-4444-8c1d-f5c43c353285]]. The episode presents him as central to early product engineering and as the key-person dependency exposed by his death on September 23, 2008.

## Key Points
- Brown's Philippines factory looked more capable than the brothers' earlier Thailand production route, with better floors, equipment, and engineering discipline.
- He worked with the brothers and mold makers on what became the [[YETITundra]] line.
- His death hit just as YETI was moving from roughly $1 million to $3 million in sales and had only a small team.
- The crisis forced YETI to digitize designs, work with U.S. rotomolders, and reduce dependence on one overseas factory and one person.
