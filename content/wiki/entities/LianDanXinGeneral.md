## Overview

廉丹 / Lian Dan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-660-2-yi-ji-kan-dong-chimei-jun-de-dansheng-lgqk1gkvh-0cwder6g5-wryi6mce|Hanji 660-2]] as a Xin general sent with [[WangKuangXinGeneral|王匡]] to suppress [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]] and other eastern rebels. His profile is narrow but important: official army abuse damages the regime's popular standing, then Wang Mang's harsh edict and [[FengYanEasternHan|冯衍]]'s warning put Lian Dan between loyalist duty and a collapsing political field.

## Current Profile

Hanji 660-2 first presents Lian Dan as part of a large Xin military response. Wang Mang sends him and Wang Kuang east with major forces, but the army fails the civilian-discipline test: soldiers are allowed to harm people along the route, producing the saying that meeting Chimei is better than meeting the official commanders.

The episode then narrows to Lian Dan's decision point. Wang Mang blames scarcity, empty treasuries, and public crisis on the need for furious suppression, demanding that Lian Dan risk death in the field. Feng Yan advises him to recognize that heroes never sincerely accepted Xin and that people remember Han, but Lian Dan refuses. The page therefore treats him as a late-Xin loyalist trapped inside a failing command and legitimacy environment.

## Key Characteristics

- Xin-side general sent east against Chimei and other rebels.
- Paired with Wang Kuang in an anti-rebel army whose civilian abuse backfires.
- Receives a severe Wang Mang edict demanding battlefield sacrifice.
- Hears Feng Yan's Han-restoration public-sentiment warning but rejects it.
- Loyalty case whose resolve may worsen survival under a collapsing regime.

## Qualifications

The page is source-bounded to Hanji 660-2 and does not reconstruct Lian Dan's full career or death. The episode's evaluation of loyalty is interpretive: it presents Lian Dan as having a loyalist disposition, but the exact motives, command authority, and later battlefield result are reserved for subsequent source notes.
