## Overview

George Floyd is the man whose fatal restraint by Minneapolis officers anchors [[memorial-day-2020-part-2-memorial-day-2020-part-2|Memorial Day, 2020 | Part 2]].

## Current Profile

The episode follows Floyd's worsening medical condition during an arrest over an alleged counterfeit $20 bill. Its central institutional question is not whether warning signs existed, but why officers who recognized them did not end the restraint. Floyd's death is then used to connect hierarchy during the encounter with the department's earlier failure to remove [[DerekChauvin]].

## Key Characteristics

- He was approached after a Cup Foods report concerning an allegedly counterfeit $20 bill.
- He resisted placement in the squad car and suffered a mouth injury before officers escalated the ambulance request.
- Officers observed loss of consciousness and then could not detect a pulse while restraint continued.
- His death is presented as the culmination of individual action, failed intervention, peer influence, and institutional non-removal.

## Qualifications

The source is a structured podcast summary rather than the body-camera record, trial record, medical evidence, or a complete history of Floyd's life. Its “excited delirium” discussion is an officer's training-based interpretation, and the episode itself notes that the label is not an officially recognized clinical diagnosis. The page does not adopt Gladwell's unprovable counterfactual about what would have happened without Chauvin.
