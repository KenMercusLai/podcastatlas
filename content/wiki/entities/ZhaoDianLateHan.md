## Current Profile

Zhao Dian's role is narrow but structurally useful. He is not developed as an independent political actor; he supplies the recommendation channel that brings Xun Shuang into the court examination field where harem burden and palace extravagance can be criticized under a formally acceptable filial-recommendation process.
