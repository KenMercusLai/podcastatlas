# Mayo Clinic

## Overview
Mayo Clinic is the medical institution named in [[essentials-diet-nutrition-for-mental-health-dr-chris-palmer-scim2465421786]] as the place where [[RussellWilder]] developed the ketogenic diet for epilepsy in 1921.

## Current Profile
The source uses Mayo Clinic as institutional context for the ketogenic diet's clinical origin. Its role here is historical and evidentiary: the episode treats ketogenic diet as a neurological treatment with a century-long clinical lineage before discussing possible psychiatric use.

## Key Characteristics
- Mayo Clinic is associated in the source with the 1921 ketogenic diet design for epilepsy.
- The institution functions as a credibility anchor for the episode's distinction between clinical ketogenic diet and wellness trend.
- The source uses Mayo Clinic's role to connect fasting, seizure control, and diet-induced brain metabolism changes.

## Evidence
- Institutional origin - [[essentials-diet-nutrition-for-mental-health-dr-chris-palmer-scim2465421786]] says [[RussellWilder]] at Mayo Clinic developed the ketogenic diet in 1921.
- Clinical framing - [[essentials-diet-nutrition-for-mental-health-dr-chris-palmer-scim2465421786]] says the diet was created for epilepsy rather than for weight loss or as a universal human diet.
- Mechanism bridge - [[essentials-diet-nutrition-for-mental-health-dr-chris-palmer-scim2465421786]] uses the epilepsy history to make diet-brain effects biologically plausible.
