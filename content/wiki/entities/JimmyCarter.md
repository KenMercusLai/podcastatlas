# Jimmy Carter

## Overview

Jimmy Carter was a United States president, former peanut farmer, evangelical Christian, and husband of Rosalynn Carter, represented in the bounded source through a deliberately comic dating-show sketch.

## Current Profile

[[206-historical-love-island-glt8073929093]] casts Carter as the wholesome “sweet one” whose sincerity would attract audience affection more readily than romantic success. The sketch draws on his teetotal evangelical identity, peanut-farming background, long marriage to Rosalynn, and public admission that he had experienced lust without committing adultery.

The episode ultimately pairs him with [[Olympias]] after [[Sporus]] disrupts the original couples. That contrast is an entertainment device, not evidence about Carter's presidency, marriage, theology, or private compatibility.
