# 杨奉 / Yang Feng (late Han)

杨奉 / Yang Feng (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1018-ta-weihe-rang-caocao-jidan-sunce-weiju-liubei-wanxi-lqccdhrjmero-gt7vsaq2ejuhhw8|Hanji 1018]] as [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]]'s partner in the temporary anti-[[LyuBu|吕布]] coalition assembled by [[YuanShu|袁术]]. Like Han Xian, he is described through the prior achievement of protecting [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], which gives Lü Bu language for a court-loyalty appeal.

The episode uses Yang Feng to show how quickly a coalition can change sides when legitimacy language and material incentives point together. After Lü Bu promises Yuan Shu's military supplies, Yang Feng and Han Xian reverse sides and help collapse [[ZhangXunYuanShu|张勋]]'s attack.
