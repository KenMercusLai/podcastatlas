# 杨奉 / Yang Feng (late Han)

杨奉 / Yang Feng (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1005-sunce-zhuding-zaoshi-shi-xingge-jueding-mingyun-lpqnere2cwcrngggyezd-bsqahx4|Hanji 1005]] as a former emperor-protection actor whose relationship with [[CaoCao|曹操]] breaks after Cao Cao moves [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] to [[Xudu|许都]]. The source says Yang Feng had tried to block the move from Liang but arrived too late; Cao Cao later attacks him, Yang Feng flees south to [[YuanShu|袁术]], and Cao Cao occupies Yang Feng's Liang-area camps.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1018-ta-weihe-rang-caocao-jidan-sunce-weiju-liubei-wanxi-lqccdhrjmero-gt7vsaq2ejuhhw8|Hanji 1018]] later adds Yang Feng as [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]]'s partner in the temporary anti-[[LyuBu|吕布]] coalition assembled by Yuan Shu. Like Han Xian, he is described through the prior achievement of protecting Emperor Xian, which gives Lü Bu language for a court-loyalty appeal.

The episode uses Yang Feng to show how quickly a coalition can change sides when legitimacy language and material incentives point together. After Lü Bu promises Yuan Shu's military supplies, Yang Feng and Han Xian reverse sides and help collapse [[ZhangXunYuanShu|张勋]]'s attack.
