# 杨奉 / Yang Feng (late Han)

## Overview
杨奉 / Yang Feng is a late-Han military actor whose path runs from [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]]'s camp to emperor escort, route-control bargaining, failed resistance to [[CaoCao|曹操]], coalition switching under [[YuanShu|袁术]], and death at [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s hands. He is one of the clearest examples of mobile armed power that can matter briefly because it controls troops and routes, but cannot stabilize itself once food, legitimacy, and patronage shift.

## Current Profile
Yang Feng's profile is built around tactical usefulness without durable political settlement. He weakens Li Jue, helps move [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], and controls the route Cao Cao needs before [[DongZhaoLateHan|董昭]] reframes Cao as a grain-backed ally. That same route-control value disappears after the Xu relocation: Yang Feng tries to block Cao too late, flees to Yuan Shu, switches with [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]] when [[LyuBu|吕布]] offers legitimacy and spoils, and finally becomes a food-short remnant near the Xu-Yang border before Liu Bei kills him at Pei.

## Key Characteristics
- Former Li Jue-side commander whose defection weakens the Chang'an power field.
- Emperor-escort actor whose protection is inseparable from armed self-interest.
- Route-control obstacle temporarily converted by Dong Zhao's grain-and-troops persuasion.
- Failed resistor to Cao Cao's Xu relocation order who loses his Liang-area camps.
- Coalition-switching actor who follows legitimacy and material incentives away from Yuan Shu.
- Final remnant whose lack of food and durable base makes him vulnerable to Liu Bei's trap.

## Qualifications
The page normalizes the transcript's occasional "杨凤" form to Yang Feng because the surrounding chronology matches the same late-Han actor. Yang Feng is not treated as uniquely irrational: his moves often follow immediate force, food, and safety incentives, but those incentives never become a durable political architecture.
