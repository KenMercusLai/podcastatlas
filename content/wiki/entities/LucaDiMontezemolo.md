# Luca di Montezemolo

Luca di Montezemolo is the Ferrari executive whom [[ferrari-1]] gives two distinct roles. In the 1970s, [[EnzoFerrari]] brought him into [[ScuderiaFerrari]], where he helped move the team beyond an engine-only mindset and returned it to success with [[NikiLauda]].

After Enzo's death, Luca returned as chairman and rebuilt [[Ferrari]] as a luxury business. The source credits him with cutting overproduction, improving the product line, tightening the customer experience, restoring F1 dominance with [[JeanTodt]], [[RossBrawn]], and [[MichaelSchumacher]], and deepening [[LuxuryScarcityDiscipline]].

## Source Position
- Luca's turnaround paired product discipline with customer ritual, waitlists, and customized delivery.
- His conflict with [[SergioMarchionne]] over the IPO reflects [[PublicMarketLuxuryGrowthTension]].
- The source treats him as the executive who made Ferrari's modern luxury operating model explicit.
