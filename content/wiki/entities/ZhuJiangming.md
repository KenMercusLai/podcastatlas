# Zhu Jiangming / 朱江明

## Overview
Zhu Jiangming / 朱江明 is presented in the source as the founder of [[Leapmotor|零跑汽车]] and an engineering entrepreneur whose earlier career at [[DahuaTechnology|大华]] shaped Leapmotor's self-research, manufacturing, and cost-control orientation.

## Current Profile
The current evidence frames Zhu less as a charismatic storyteller than as a technical founder who learns through hands-on systems work. The episode links his Yiwu upbringing, electronics hobby, school-factory power-supply development, Dahua co-founding, embedded DVR breakthrough, and set-top-box management to the later decision to build an EV company at about age 48. His strongest current profile is a late-stage hardware founder who underestimated car-making difficulty but turned prior engineering organization experience into Leapmotor's full-stack self-research strategy.

## Key Characteristics
- Engineering-first founder whose early electronics practice shaped a hands-on view of complex systems.
- Repeat hardware entrepreneur moving from Dahua's security-video products into new-energy vehicles.
- Low-profile, moderately socially anxious CEO who prefers product and technical discussion over public performance.
- Operator willing to admit major misjudgments around capital needs, S01 product definition, and early consumer marketing.
- Long-term industrial builder emphasizing self-research, platformization, cost discipline, and team stability.
- Globalization-minded automaker leader who treats trade barriers and overseas operating systems as central risks.
