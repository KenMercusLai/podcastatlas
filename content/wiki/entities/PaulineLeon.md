# Pauline Léon

## Overview

Pauline Léon was a French revolutionary activist presented by the episode as an advocate of women's armed participation.

## Current Profile

The source places Léon alongside [[TheroigneDeMericourt|Théroigne de Méricourt]] and the Fernig sisters among women who sought military roles. Her appearance demonstrates that demands for female citizenship were not confined to salons or written declarations but could include a claim to bear arms for the Republic.

## Key Characteristics

- She advocated women's armed participation.
- She belonged to a wider, minority challenge to the separation of male public duty from female domestic patriotism.
- Her military claim connected rights to reciprocal service for the Republic.
