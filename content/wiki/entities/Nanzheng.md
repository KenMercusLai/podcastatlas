# 南郑 / Nanzheng

南郑 / Nanzheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-143-xiangyu-shi-ruhe-feijin-xinji-nongsi-chuhuaiwang-mixin-lstfzfvbd2h9i39f7y5dxrbxxwv2|Hanji 143]] as the capital assigned to [[LiuBang|刘邦]] after [[XiangYu|项羽]] makes him Han king. In the episode's reading, the capital placement matters because Liu Bang is not given the open [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] kingship promised by the [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]], but is instead pushed into [[BaShu|巴蜀]] and [[Hanzhong|汉中]].

Nanzheng therefore anchors the practical side of Liu Bang's demotion. The title "Han king" can still be made to sound connected to the broader Guanzhong promise, but the capital marks a constrained and remote base behind difficult terrain and behind the [[ThreeQinContainment|Three Qin containment]] layer.
