# 公叔 / Gongshu (Wei chancellor)

公叔 / Gongshu is the [[WeiState|魏国]] chancellor who succeeds [[TianWenWeiChancellor|田文]] in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-1-lq5r3xe-3y7al9nkbc8kyhntipll]]. The source presents him as a court competitor who fears [[WuQi|吴起]] because Wu Qi has ability, achievements, and ambition.

Gongshu's importance is not policy but maneuver. A servant advises him to make [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]] test Wu Qi's willingness to stay by offering him a princess in marriage. Because Gongshu himself is married to a Wei princess, he then stages a scene where his wife publicly humiliates him in front of Wu Qi. The intended effect is to make Wu Qi refuse a similar marriage and thereby look uncommitted to Wei.

The episode uses Gongshu to shift from open argument over merit to hidden court manipulation. He extends the source's organization-politics reading: once Wu Qi cannot be beaten on record, rivals can target trust between ruler and minister.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl]] names 公叔痤 as the person to whom [[Zisi|子思]] privately criticizes Wei court habits. This ingest keeps that under the existing Wei chancellor page, while recording the new role as a listener in the [[CourtFeedbackCollapse]] branch rather than as the earlier Wu Qi intrigue case.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-11-shijie-fengqi-yunyong-2-llzzalowv5vmbvgd6l-jujltkbyf]] likely preserves the same figure through a capture notice rendered as 公孙措 in the transcript. Qin defeats [[WeiState|魏国]] at [[Shaoliang|少梁]] in 362 BCE and captures him. Because the broader wiki already uses 公叔痤 / Gongshu for this Wei chancellor, the notice is kept here as a name-variant issue rather than split into a new person page.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-23-weihuiwang-hui-buyong-gongshu-zhi-yan-lveaqklo7oncmqudgmdk7l03qhsf]] brings back Gongshu as the absent advice behind [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]]'s regret. After [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] defeats Wei and forces the Hexi cession, Liang Hui Wang says he regrets not following Gongshu's earlier warning about Shang Yang. The page keeps this as the same Gongshu / 公叔痤 branch already used by the wiki.
