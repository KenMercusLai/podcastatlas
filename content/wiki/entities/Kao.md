# Kao / 花王

Kao / 花王 is the Japanese daily-chemical and industrial-chemical company profiled in [[mai-xiangzao-qijia-de-bainian-huawang-weishenme-chengle-bandaoti-zhizao-de-guanjiang-qiye-1004213131]]. The episode traces the company from [[NagaseTomiro|长濑富郎]]'s 1890 facial soap through laundry detergent, household cleaning, skin care, cosmetics, and then into industrial cleaning chemistry.

The source's main claim is that Kao's semiconductor role is a technical migration rather than a brand-level pivot. The same capability that let Kao remove oil, dirt, and residue from skin, fibers, and household surfaces became useful in [[SemiconductorPrecisionCleaning|semiconductor precision cleaning]], where particles, metal contamination, organic residue, and photoresist residue must be removed without damaging the wafer.

Kao's bridge capability is [[InterfaceScience|interface science]] supported by [[SurfactantTechnology|surfactant technology]]. The episode says Kao uses formulations that disperse particles into water, prevent redeposition, and improve wafer wetting. It also presents [[CleanThrough]] as the Freon-alternative product that helped Kao enter electronics cleaning after older solvents were phased out.

The company becomes strategically visible in the source through customer proximity. [[KaoHsinchuPrecisionCleaningCenter|Kao's Hsinchu precision cleaning center]] lets engineers simulate customer production conditions near [[TSMC]] and [[MediaTek]], while resident engineering at fabs turns cleaning formulas into validated process steps with high switching costs.

## Source Position
- Kao is a consumer-products company whose industrial-chemical branch shows how ordinary cleaning knowledge can become semiconductor process knowledge.
- The source treats Kao's advantage as accumulated [[ChemicalControlAsManufacturingCapability|chemical control as manufacturing capability]], not as a sudden entry into a fashionable industry.
- Kao is used to widen [[SemiconductorSupplyChain]] from chips, equipment, and packaging toward invisible materials and cleaning steps.
