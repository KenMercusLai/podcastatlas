# 张弘亮 / 玉米

## Overview
张弘亮, also called 玉米 in the source, is the [[WanwuShengzhang|万物生长]] host who appears in the [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] crossover on sleep management and consumer sleep technology.

## Current Profile
The current wiki evidence presents Zhang as a podcast host using personal sleep history, health-product use, and consumer-health observation to explain sleep as a practical management problem. He connects his internet-company experience of weekday sleep debt and failed weekend recovery with a later interest in sleep stages, morning light, caffeine timing, alcohol effects, sleep apnea care, and [[EightSleep|8Sleep]] as an example of dynamic sleep hardware.

His role in the source is not clinical authority. He translates reading, device experience, and health-industry context into listener-facing heuristics: prioritize rhythm, use daytime energy as a sleep-quality signal, avoid late caffeine when possible, treat alcohol sedation cautiously, and seek medical care when sleep impairment affects work, driving, or suspected sleep-disorder symptoms.

## Key Characteristics
- He is identified as a [[WanwuShengzhang|万物生长]] host and guest in a [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] crossover.
- He uses his former internet-company work experience to explain stress-related sleep disruption.
- He interprets sleep through stage composition, rhythm, daytime energy, caffeine, alcohol, and morning light.
- He treats [[EightSleep|8Sleep]] as a useful product case while keeping severe sleep problems inside a medical boundary.
- He connects consumer sleep technology to Chinese health-hardware markets, subscription resistance, and user education.
