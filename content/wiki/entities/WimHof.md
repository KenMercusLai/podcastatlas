# Wim Hof

## Overview
Wim Hof is the breathwork and cold-exposure figure named in the [[control-stress-for-healthy-eating-metabolism-aging-dr-elissa-epel-scim4455888803]] Huberman Lab source note as a collaborator or design influence for a UCSF positive-stress study.

## Current Profile
The current wiki evidence uses Hof narrowly, not as a full biography. In the Epel source, [[ElissaEpel]] says her experience with Wim Hof breathing helped her think about high-arousal positive stress, and Hof helped design a study comparing low-arousal practices such as mindfulness or slow breathing with high-arousal practices such as exercise and the Wim Hof method. The cortisol-and-adrenaline episode places Wim Hof or Tummo-style breathing among deliberate sympathetic-activation tools and cites a human endotoxin study as a source-scoped immune-response example.

The profile remains source-scoped because biomarker work is pending in one source and the other does not supply full study methods or justify infection-prevention claims. The page does not independently validate Hof's method, safety, claims, or broader public profile.

## Key Characteristics
- Appears as the named figure behind the Wim Hof method discussed in Epel's positive-stress branch.
- Functions in the source as a high-arousal body-based practice example rather than as the central guest.
- Is tied to preliminary research questions about positive emotion, stress, telomerase, mitochondria, gene expression, and autonomic pathways.
- Is also used as an example of voluntarily raising sympathetic activity, with recovery and safety treated as essential boundaries.

## Evidence
- Positive-stress role - [[control-stress-for-healthy-eating-metabolism-aging-dr-elissa-epel-scim4455888803]] says Epel became interested in body-based positive stress after experiencing Wim Hof breathing.
- Study-design role - [[control-stress-for-healthy-eating-metabolism-aging-dr-elissa-epel-scim4455888803]] says Hof helped design a UCSF study comparing low-arousal and high-arousal stress-regulation methods.
- Evidence boundary - [[control-stress-for-healthy-eating-metabolism-aging-dr-elissa-epel-scim4455888803]] says stress, anxiety, and depression fell across groups, but telomerase, mitochondrial, gene-expression, sympathetic, and parasympathetic analyses were still planned or pending.
- Sympathetic and immune context - [[essentials-boost-your-energy-immune-system-with-cortisol-adrenaline-scim1515778634]] groups Wim Hof or Tummo-style breathing with deliberate arousal tools and summarizes a controlled endotoxin study without establishing treatment of infection.
