## Overview

The Traitorous Eight were eight leading Shockley Semiconductor employees whose coordinated departure became a major transmission point from one laboratory into Silicon Valley's wider semiconductor-company network.

## Current Profile

[[silicon-valley-on-the-couch]] frames the group as the productive consequence of organizational failure. [[WilliamShockley]] had recruited exceptional scientists to Mountain View, but his control, suspicion, and attempted lie-detector testing helped make continued work untenable. The eight remained in the area partly because they had already bought homes, allowing accumulated talent and local commitment to seed new companies rather than disperse nationally.

The label should not imply that disloyalty is the main historical fact. In the episode's telling, their exit is better understood as a response to destructive management and as a mechanism of regional knowledge diffusion.

## Key Characteristics

- Comprised eight leading employees recruited into Shockley's Mountain View laboratory.
- Left together after escalating conflict and distrust under Shockley's management.
- Retained geographic attachment through homes already purchased in the area.
- Helped transfer semiconductor talent from one company into a regional network.
- Included figures whose later firms became central to the industry's growth.

## Evidence

- Coordinated exit - [[silicon-valley-on-the-couch]] says eight leading employees departed together after severe management conflict.
- Local persistence - [[silicon-valley-on-the-couch]] reports that home purchases contributed to their decision to remain in the region.
- Company genealogy - [[silicon-valley-on-the-couch]] connects their departures to new firms and the transformation of Santa Clara Valley into Silicon Valley.

## Qualifications

This source summary does not provide a full collective biography, allocate individual credit among the eight, or establish that housing was their only reason for staying. The regional consequences depended on capital, customers, institutions, labor, infrastructure, and later entrepreneurs as well as their departure.

## What Changed

- Created a group profile centered on organizational exit, local persistence, and talent diffusion.
