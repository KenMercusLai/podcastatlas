# Caribbean

[[tech-20260820-tech-pod-128-tech-20260820-tech-pod-128]] adds the Caribbean as a target region for planned [[UnitedStates]] funding to replace [[UnderseaDataCables]]. In this source, the region is a connectivity and [[TrustedNetworkGeopolitics]] case: public support can make trusted cable routes more viable where purely commercial investment may be weaker.

[[Caribbean]] is the regional frame in [[sand-heists-and-property-rights-in-the-caribbean-summer-school]]. The episode treats Caribbean islands as small open economies whose exposure to hurricanes, oil prices, global interest rates, debt, and tourism dependence makes economic concepts unusually visible.

The region also supplies the episode's institutional contrasts. [[Barbuda]] shows how [[CommunalLandTenure]], [[PropertyRightsAsInvestmentIncentive]], and [[PathDependence]] can collide after disaster, while [[Jamaica]] shows how beach tourism, resort demand, and limited enforcement capacity make [[SandScarcity]] and [[SandTheftExternality]] concrete.
