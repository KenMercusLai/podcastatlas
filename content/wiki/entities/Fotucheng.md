# Fotucheng / 佛图澄

Fotucheng / 佛图澄 is the Buddhist monk in [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]] who anchors the episode's [[BuddhistMiraclePolitics|Buddhist miracle politics]] branch. The source presents him as an Indian monk active around Later Zhao rulers Shi Le and Shi Hu, surrounded by stories of extreme longevity, fasting, light from his body, lotus miracles, ghost command, water retrieval, rescue, and prophecy.

The episode's reading is politically cautious. Fotucheng served violent rulers, but his positive image is explained through miracle authority, protective intervention, life-preserving counsel, and the spread of Buddhism in northern China.

## Key Claims
- Buddhist supernatural power could become politically useful in court settings.
- Miracle stories helped authorize foreign religious specialists in Chinese elite environments.
