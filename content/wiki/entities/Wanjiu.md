# 万九 / Wanjiu

万九 / Wanjiu is the Beijing small bistro discussed in [[ep246-rang-nvsheng-anxin-de-yuehui-xiaojiuguan-shenme-yang-lpd5zw9ryd3kplmnaywsoqokvakm]]. [[Moku]] says it opened in 2016 as an ordinary eat-and-drink space, then gradually became a familiar social platform for customers from different countries, industries, and backgrounds.

In the source, Wanjiu matters because it shows how [[ChineseBistroLocalization|中式小酒馆本土化]] can become relationship infrastructure. Single-person portions, a bar where the owner can talk to solo guests, tables sized for two people, regular-customer recognition, and enough ambient activity make the venue usable for solo drinking, early dates, introductions, breakups, engagement displays, and long-term customer life changes.
