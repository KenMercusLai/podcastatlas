# 黄琬 / Huang Wan (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-939-donghan-luanju-de-fengkuang-sanguo-xuqu-lthmvw8ihk96fuez9vamlryzcngy|Hanji 939]] backfills Huang Wan's first current page marker before the Dong Zhuo court branch. After [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]] proposes stronger provincial shepherds, the court appoints Huang Wan as Yu Province shepherd alongside Liu Yan in [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] and [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] in [[YouzhouLateHan|幽州]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-948-dongzhuo-feidi-quanchen-ruhe-gaibian-huangquan-youxi-ltekg1a-5b5u37aeekjm3dm2m6kk|Hanji 948]] adds Huang Wan before the relocation debate and anti-Dong-Zhuo planning branches. [[DongZhuo|董卓]] appoints him as situ after the forced replacement of [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]] with [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], using a senior court office to broaden the legitimacy surface of the new order.

The source treats the appointment as part of [[CourtOfficeCapture|朝廷开府任官失序]] and [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] rather than proof that Huang Wan endorses Dong Zhuo's rule. Later pages show Huang Wan de-escalating danger around [[YangBiao|杨彪]] and then joining anti-Dong-Zhuo planning.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-951-tianjiang-hengcai-jiujing-shi-haoshi-haishi-huaishi-lovwgng2wrtfno-s9ttpybbhksvh|Hanji 951]] backfills Huang Wan's earlier court-risk role during [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s forced relocation from [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] to [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]]. When [[YangBiao|杨彪]]'s opposition angers Dong Zhuo, Huang Wan helps soften the confrontation; Dong Zhuo later removes him from office and then restores him only after apology.

This makes Huang Wan part of the pre-assassination [[DefensiveSpeechUnderTyranny|defensive speech]] field before his later anti-Dong-Zhuo planning role. The source shows that even senior officials could sometimes reduce immediate danger, but could not stop [[CoerciveCapitalRelocation|强制迁都]] once Dong Zhuo chose punishment and military force.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-962-dongzhuo-yu-lvbu-daodi-weishenme-juelie-lgp3kxfqnhokibf6kpli6p4j8iti|Hanji 962]] backfills Huang Wan's anti-[[DongZhuo|董卓]] role before his Hanji 966 execution. The episode names him with [[WangYunLateHan|王允]], [[ShiSunRuiLateHan|士孙瑞]], [[YangZanLateHan|杨瓒]], and other officials who repeatedly plan how to remove Dong Zhuo while Dong Zhuo's close-guard layer around [[LyuBu|吕布]] remains the operational obstacle.

黄琬 / Huang Wan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-966-meiyou-yexin-budong-quanmou-jiu-bie-dang-lingdao-lldn3hi1gl5v3axvklwakgf6vx3u|Hanji 966]] as a court official tied to [[WangYunLateHan|王允]]'s anti-[[DongZhuo|董卓]] settlement. The episode says Huang Wan had helped Wang Yun plan Dong Zhuo's removal and helped stabilize the immediate scene after the killing.

His later arrest and execution after [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] take [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] makes him part of the purge field before Hanji 967's hidden-merit contrast around [[ShiSunRuiLateHan|士孙瑞]]. Huang Wan is not developed as a full biography here; his role is to show that visible participation in the anti-Dong-Zhuo order becomes lethal once Dong Zhuo's remnant commanders return with soldiers and captured offices.
