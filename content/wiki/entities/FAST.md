# 贵州天眼 / FAST

FAST, discussed in [[ep248-zai-gaoyuan-yu-dixia-tanxun-zhongguo-da-kexue-xiangmu-lidn7xxkcw4-66fw-y50fqqacwo]] as "贵州天眼," is the episode's most publicly accessible large-science site. [[MiaoQian|苗千]] describes visiting it as a regular tourist through Guiyang, Pingtang Astronomy Town, and the managed scenic-area route.

The episode uses FAST to show how [[BigScienceInfrastructure|big-science infrastructure]] can become public culture without becoming an ordinary tourist object. Visitors must store phones, watches, and other signal-emitting devices before entering the viewing area, and the scenic area offers film-camera photography because mobile phones are not allowed near the telescope.

FAST's role in the source is less technical than Ali or Jiangmen, but it strengthens the [[HumanisticScienceCommunication|public-science communication]] branch. The radio-quiet rules, travel route, and visual encounter with "天眼" show how a scientific instrument becomes legible to ordinary visitors while still being governed by research constraints.
