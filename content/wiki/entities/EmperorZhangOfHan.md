## Overview

Emperor Zhang of Han is the Eastern Han ruler whose current profile now begins with Hanji 746's 76 CE listening-and-leniency layer, then follows Hanji 747-758 through early frontier decisions, Ma-family restraint, classics consensus, accusation handling, burden relief, legal restraint, ritual codification, and kinship-boundary problems. Across the bounded source set he is unusually receptive to remonstrance, but the same evidence also shows recurring weakness where household affection, protected relatives, palace accusation, and known wrongdoing test accountability.

## Current Profile

Hanji 746 supplies the earliest current layer. Zhangdi opens his reign by accepting [[BaoYuEasternHan|Bao Yu]]'s indirect redress advice around the Chu-king case, accepting [[YangZhongEasternHan|Yang Zhong]]'s argument against mechanically continuing people-burdening Northern Xiongnu and Western Regions policy, issuing an agriculture-and-seasonal-justice edict, and accepting [[ChenChongLateHan|Chen Chong]]'s remonstrance against inherited harsh official practice. The same episode adds the rescue-recognition branch: [[FanQiangEasternHan|Fan Qiang]] rescues [[GengGongEasternHan|Geng Gong]], [[ZhengZhongFrontierEasternHan|Zheng Zhong]] memorializes the survivors' endurance, and the court appoints Geng Gong cavalry commandant.

Hanji 747-750 continue the early-reign picture. Zhangdi accepts [[LiuCangDongpingKing|Liu Cang]]'s opposition to imperial-tomb counties, oversees Ban Chao's recall and return crisis around Western Regions dependency, receives [[EmpressMaMingdeEasternHan|Empress Ma]]'s prevention of Ma-family enfeoffment, allows Ma-family titles but not immediate office power after Ma's death, convenes the [[BaihuGuanConference|Baihu Guan Conference]], searches for practical talent, and authorizes [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]]'s low-cost Western Regions strategy through [[XuGanEasternHan|Xu Gan]].

Hanji 749 also adds a warning boundary. Zhangdi sends [[MaFangEasternHan|Ma Fang]] to heavy Qiang command despite [[FifthLunEasternHan|Fifth Lun]]'s warning that imperial in-laws in major office create a law-versus-kinship dilemma. Ma Fang succeeds militarily, but Geng Gong's later removal after offending Ma Fang leaves the appointment-risk concern alive.

Hanji 751-752 develop the household-risk side. Zhangdi accepts the accusation that destroys [[SongGuirenZhangHeLateHan|Song Guiren]]'s standing and demotes [[LiuQingQingheKing|Liu Qing]], while his ritual care for Liu Cang and low-disturbance travel orders show the same reign's humane and affective side. He can restrict the Ma family and rebuke [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] over the [[QinshuiPrincessEasternHan|Qinshui Princess]] estate, but Dou Xian is not punished, making this the profile's clearest known-misconduct non-punishment case.

Hanji 753-758 widen the governance profile. Zhangdi rejects [[LiYiEasternHan|Li Yi]]'s slander against Ban Chao through behavioral evidence, accepts [[ZhengHongEasternHan|Zheng Hong]] on Jiaozhi burden relief, hears [[WeiBiaoEasternHan|Wei Biao]] on office fit, limits interrogation tools, tolerates Taixue historical criticism, supports population relief, restores court deliberation during the Xiongnu restitution dispute, accepts legal and ritual work from Guo Gong and [[CaoBaoEasternHan|Cao Bao]], and receives warnings from [[HeChangEasternHan|He Chang]] and [[SongYiEasternHan|Song Yi]] that kin generosity has exceeded fiscal and ritual proportion.

Hanji 770 adds the posthumous legitimacy layer. The revelation that [[LiangGuirenHeLateHan|Liang Guiren]] was [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]]'s birth mother shows that Zhangdi's household order did not settle maternal identity, adoptive motherhood, and outer-relative consequences cleanly.

## Key Characteristics

- Receptive-remonstrance ruler who repeatedly lets advice change legal, frontier, household, and burden policy.
- Burden-aware administrator who treats drought, agriculture, seasonal justice, public works, tribute, fertility, travel, and frontier deployment as governance tests.
- Filial and maternal-recognition ruler whose desire to reward or honor kin is repeatedly checked by stronger advisers.
- Evidence-testing frontier authorizer who backs Ban Chao's mission and judges accusations by command behavior.
- Legal-temperament reformer open to mercy, reduced torture, seasonal case timing, and correction of harsh official culture.
- Household-risk ruler whose Song Guiren decision, Dou Xian non-punishment, and royal-kin overfavor expose limits in accountability.
- Ritual and classics ruler who uses Baihu Guan, Cao Bao's ritual commission, teacher honor, and royal mourning to stabilize public order.

- Maternal legitimacy, classics, and Western Regions strategy: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-750-jiemi-donghan-wanmei-nvxing-beihou-manshi-xisheng-lu-96lbdh7r0tsmiopbqqrj0q1gt|Hanji 750]] shows Zhangdi honoring childless Empress Ma as mother, convening Baihu Guan, calling for criticism after a solar eclipse, and sending Xu Gan to reinforce Ban Chao.
- Succession accusation failure and ritual care: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-751-dou-huanghou-gaosu-ni-nvren-bu-hen-diwei-bu-wen-luvqadctbfuzit2ackrqifour2c6|Hanji 751]] shows Zhangdi accepting the Song Guiren accusation and demoting Liu Qing, while also limiting travel burden and honoring Liu Cang.

- Speech-space preservation and Xiongnu setup: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-754-gudai-ban-qieting-fengyun-jieju-shuangwen-ltvymh-2r-ic5diqfh-2ic9cudq2|Hanji 754]] shows Zhangdi refusing to punish fact-based Taixue historical critique and gives the Northern Xiongnu trade setup later resolved through restitution.
- Population and frontier forbearance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-755-zhege-chaodai-huangshang-jiu-kaishi-guli-shengwa-le-lj7bdkxkdtjhpo469yxhskjyfdco|Hanji 755]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-756-weishenme-quan-ni-de-rao-ren-chu-qie-rao-ren-ltn4tqzy1d-sodhjidncv8dyqxpj|Hanji 756]] develop Zhangdi's fertility relief, low-disturbance administration, Xiongnu restitution decision, and preservation of ministerial disagreement.
- Law, ritual, omen, and proportion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-757-rensheng-xiang-shao-zou-wanlu-xian-zhaozhun-dingwei-lri1zyiq8210ymqjakznyp3v4k6x|Hanji 757]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-758-suoyou-gaoqingshang-beihou-doushi-fencungan-lr-6tzffmwdtzidkf3y8ule35qf7|Hanji 758]] show Guo Gong's legal mercy, Cao Bao's ritual commission, He Chang's omen warning, Ban Chao's Shache deception, and warnings that royal-kin generosity has exceeded fiscal and ritual limits.

## Qualifications

Zhangdi's profile should not be flattened into benevolent ritualism, weak leniency, or court fragility alone; the source set shows all three under different conditions. Hanji 746 adds a strong listening-and-leniency layer, but it also leaves Mou Rong, Wang Meng, Duan Peng, the exact Chu-case lists, and the Geng Gong fortress geography source-scoped. The page's frontmatter source order preserves the established bounded inventory order and appends Hanji 746 as the newest evidence, even though the body treats it chronologically as the earliest current layer. Several identity cautions remain active: Hanji 746's Yang Zhong spelling is normalized to [[YangZhongEasternHan|杨终]], its Zheng Zhong is kept separate from [[ZhengZhongEasternHan|the eunuch Zheng Zhong]], and Zhu Hui in Hanji 753 remains [[ZhuHuiZhangEraEasternHan]], not [[ZhuHuiHeEraLateHan]].

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 746 as the new earliest current layer for Zhangdi's accepted advice from Bao Yu, Yang Zhong, and Chen Chong.
- Reframed Zhangdi's "listening" as policy conversion rather than only a gentle temperament.
- Added Geng Gong's rescue and recognition as an early Zhangdi-era command-memory case.
- Preserved existing household-risk and known-misconduct non-punishment limits around Song Guiren, Liu Qing, and Dou Xian.
