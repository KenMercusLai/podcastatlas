## Overview

汉章帝 / Emperor Zhang of Han enters the current wiki through Hanji 770 as the deceased emperor whose tomb and spouse order frame the posthumous status dispute around [[EmpressDowagerDouHeLateHan|Dou Taihou]] and [[LiangGuirenHeLateHan|Liang Guiren]]. The page is source-bounded and does not yet attempt a full reign biography.

## Current Profile

Hanji 770 uses Emperor Zhang mainly as a ritual anchor. After Dou Taihou dies and the Liang birth-mother truth reaches [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]], officials ask the emperor to remove Dou's title and deny her burial with Emperor Zhang. Emperor He refuses, saying he had served Dou as mother for ten years and will not separate her from the deceased emperor's burial order.

The same source later says Liang Guiren, now posthumously honored, and Liang Da Guiren are reburied west of Emperor Zhang's tomb. Emperor Zhang's current role is therefore indirect but important: his tomb field becomes the spatial and symbolic field where adoptive motherhood, birth motherhood, and posthumous legitimacy are ordered without collapsing into a single winner-loser settlement.

## Key Characteristics

- Deceased Eastern Han emperor referenced through burial and spouse-status questions in Hanji 770.
- Posthumous spouse of Dou Taihou, whose burial pairing Emperor He preserves.
- Father-context figure for Emperor He's birth-mother redress, though the source does not develop his reign.
- Tomb-field anchor for Liang Guiren's reburial after posthumous elevation.
