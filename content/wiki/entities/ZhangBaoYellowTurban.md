# 张宝 / Zhang Bao (Yellow Turban)

张宝 / Zhang Bao (Yellow Turban) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] as another brother of [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]] in the late-184 [[YellowTurbansLateHan|黄巾军]] collapse sequence. After [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]] kills [[ZhangLiangYellowTurban|张梁]] at Guangzong, he attacks Zhang Bao at Quyang in November and kills him as well.

The page is source-scoped to this suppression branch. Zhang Bao's death marks the point where the episode says the main Yellow Turban uprising is basically pacified, even though later remnants continue until [[CaoCao|曹操]] settles them in 192 CE.

This route is disambiguated from [[ZhangBaoLateHan|张苞 / Zhang Bao (late Han)]], the later Li Jue-side officer already represented in the wiki.
