# Lepton AI

Lepton AI is [[JiaYangqing|Jia Yangqing]]'s first startup in the AI infrastructure and [[NeoCloud]] space. In [[jia-yangqing-wo-suo-jingli-de-rengongzhineng-yisi-dao-ai-dianfu-shijie-de-shunian-jubian-chuantai-shengdongjixi-s10e24-a3884ade-4669-4d5c-ab2e-f98aa580f429]], Jia says the team moved from prototype to stable customer use quickly and was later acquired by [[Nvidia]].

The source uses Lepton AI to explain why AI infrastructure startups are not just cloud resellers. AI workloads require accelerator scheduling, connected [[GPU|GPU]] clusters, model serving, observability, and customer-facing workflows that differ from traditional CPU-centered cloud.

## Source Position
- Jia frames Lepton AI as a fast execution case: prototype in months, stable customer usage within roughly eight months, and acquisition after about two years.
- The company sits in the [[NeoCloud]] branch because AI customers need tightly coupled accelerator capacity and software layers.
- Jia's retrospective is that he may have over-optimized resource use when the market rewarded growth, public visibility, and customer expansion.
- The source treats the startup as a practical lesson in [[AIStartupUnitEconomics]] and [[AIInfrastructureAsProduct]].
