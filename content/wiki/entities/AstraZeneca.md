# AstraZeneca / 阿斯利康

AstraZeneca / 阿斯利康 appears in [[vol-117-shengwu-yiyao-de-2025-chaodi-zhongguo-yanfa-jiaolv-he-xinwang-jiwei-lmhral0rmq6tohiqdwsgmfapnyn7]] as one of the multinational pharma companies buying or licensing Chinese biotech assets. The episode points to AstraZeneca's acquisition of a Chinese CAR-T company around late 2023 as an early marker for the 2024 pattern of "bottom fishing" Chinese pipelines.

The source uses AstraZeneca as a transaction signal, not as a full strategic profile. Its role is to show that MNCs may treat depressed Chinese biotech valuations as an opportunity when their own pipeline pressure is rising.
