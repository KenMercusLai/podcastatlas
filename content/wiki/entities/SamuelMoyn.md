# Samuel Moyn

Samuel Moyn is the Yale history and law professor interviewed in [[older-workers-arent-retiring-should-they-be-forced-to]] about [[GerontocracyInAmerica]] and older Americans' hold on jobs, wealth, housing, and authority. In the [[PlanetMoney]] source, Moyn argues that some older workers in powerful or high-paying roles can create unfairness for younger workers when they stay indefinitely.

Moyn's argument is narrower than a universal demand that older people stop working. The episode presents him as focused on people who can afford to retire but remain in elite or scarce positions such as politics, law, academia, journalism, executive leadership, and engineering.
