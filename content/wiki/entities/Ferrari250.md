# Ferrari 250

The Ferrari 250 is the Ferrari line that [[ferrari-1]] presents as one of the company's most beautiful and important product families. The source uses it to connect [[Pininfarina]] design, racing credibility, and collector mythology.

For the wiki, the 250 is a product-level example of [[FunctionalAlibi]]. Beauty and status matter, but they are supported by engineering, racing heritage, and provenance, which makes the willingness to pay feel grounded rather than arbitrary.
