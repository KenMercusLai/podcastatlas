# Wang Tianmu / 王天木

## Overview

王天木 appears as a senior Military Statistics Bureau operative who organized the Shanghai assassination of [[ChenLu|陈箓]] and later defected to the Japanese-backed security system after capture and an attempted killing by his former organization.

## Current Profile

The source places Wang at the intersection of anti-collaborationist violence and internal intelligence rivalry. It attributes the Chen operation to his Shanghai network and suggests that personnel conflict and competition for results helped shape the mission alongside its public patriotic rationale.

Months later, Wang was captured and released by the collaborationist No. 76 security organization. Suspicion from Dai Li and a subsequent attempt on Wang's life preceded his defection. A later theory that the defection was an intentional intelligence operation is mentioned but not accepted as established.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior Nationalist intelligence operative active in wartime Shanghai.
- Principal organizer attributed with the Chen Lu assassination.
- Worked inside an organization where patriotic aims and internal competition could overlap.
- Became suspect after capture and release by the opposing security service.
- Defected after his former organization attempted to kill him.

- Internal politics: [[32-erzhanzhong-de-xingci-cisha-nacui-motou-haidelixi-cisha-hanjian-chenlu-ltpexjdbtjpcqohufhfktot5hnhz]] connects the operation to personnel tension and performance rivalry inside the intelligence service.
- Defection sequence: [[32-erzhanzhong-de-xingci-cisha-nacui-motou-haidelixi-cisha-hanjian-chenlu-ltpexjdbtjpcqohufhfktot5hnhz]] describes capture, release, suspicion, attempted assassination, and defection.

## Qualifications

The source does not verify the later claim that Wang's defection was a mission arranged by Dai Li. Its descriptions of internal motives, his exact operational control, and his postwar treatment remain episode-attributed.

## What Changed

- Created a profile connecting the Chen operation to Wang's later capture, suspicion, and defection.
