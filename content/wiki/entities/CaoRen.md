# 曹仁 / Cao Ren

曹仁 / Cao Ren enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-974-caocao-xiang-ta-tuo-qi-xian-zi-weihe-zhuanshen-ai-daozi-lhfabab7xv2vfsgnhr9ozwqhmhd|Hanji 974]] as part of the source's retrospective explanation of how [[CaoCao|曹操]] adjusts after [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] and [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] betray him in [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]]. The episode says Cao Cao later strengthens clan control of core military power, naming Cao Ren alongside [[XiahouDun|夏侯惇]] as trusted kin-commanders.

The page is source-scoped. Hanji 974 does not narrate Cao Ren's campaigns; it uses him as a personnel-architecture marker in Cao Cao's post-crisis move toward broader recruitment plus tighter control over decisive armed force.
