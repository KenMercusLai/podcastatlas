# 芒芭芭 / Mangbaba (无时差研究所)

## Overview
芒芭芭 / Mangbaba is a [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]] host who, in EP393, approaches career anxiety through reversibility, personal autonomy, and identities beyond employment.

## Current Profile
She is reluctant to make difficult-to-reverse commitments such as a mortgage or childbearing without strong safety conditions. She does not present non-parenthood as a fixed universal answer; instead, she questions whether work or reproduction must be every person's central life narrative. Her practical response is to keep participating in work and creating opportunities for women while also investing in relationships, family, health, hobbies, and creation.

## Key Characteristics
- Life-design voice prioritizing reversibility and retained choice.
- Skeptic of treating career as the only route to growth or meaning.
- Advocate of multiple life projects and embodied creative process even under AI substitution.
- Participant in workplace and public activity who seeks greater visibility and opportunity for women.
