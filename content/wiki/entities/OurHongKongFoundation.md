# Our Hong Kong Foundation / 團結香港基金

## Overview
Our Hong Kong Foundation / 團結香港基金 appears in the Tung source as a post-tenure organization founded by [[TungCheeHwa|董建華]] during the Umbrella Movement period.

## Current Profile
The source uses the foundation as evidence that Tung remained an establishment political figure after resigning as chief executive. It is grouped with [[HongKongCoalition]] as part of his late-career role in Hong Kong's pro-Beijing camp.

## Key Characteristics
- Post-chief-executive organization associated with Tung.
- Founded in the period of the Umbrella Movement according to the source.
- Treated as part of Hong Kong establishment organization.
- Indicates Tung's continued role beyond formal chief-executive office.
