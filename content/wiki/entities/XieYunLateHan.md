## Overview

谢恽 / Xie Yun is a late-Han favorite in the Emperor An-era palace field. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-799-donghan-mingchen-yangzhen-ruhe-jujue-zou-houmen-lmufb7lvb-kzsvjtn6iu-zrpiptf|Hanji 799]] shows [[YangZhenLateHan|杨震]] criticizing him and [[ZhouGuangLateHan|周广]] for relying on favored insiders, soliciting offices, and producing corrupt appointment outcomes; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]] places him with [[FanFengEunuchLateHan|Fan Feng]] and Zhou Guang in the forged-edict construction scandal that helps destroy Yang Zhen; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-802-andi-zhisi-donghan-wangchao-quanli-da-xipai-lgvne92e0jjzjnznzfkz7nvztnbj|Hanji 802]] shows him executed in [[YanXianLateHan|Yan Xian]]'s later purge of old An-era networks.

## Current Profile

In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-799-donghan-mingchen-yangzhen-ruhe-jujue-zou-houmen-lmufb7lvb-kzsvjtn6iu-zrpiptf|Hanji 799]], Xie Yun belongs to the favored insider network named in Yang Zhen's memorial against Wang Sheng's mansion and court corruption. The source says Fan Feng, Zhou Guang, Xie Yun, and others encourage each other and form a party for private benefit. Yang then criticizes Zhou and Xie for relying on favored actors, soliciting from commanderies and states, accepting bribes, and allowing barred corrupt officials to hold key posts.

In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]], Xie Yun's group becomes more reckless when Emperor An ignores Yang Zhen's remonstrance. The group forges imperial authorization, uses state stores and labor for private mansions and gardens, and then faces exposure when Yang obtains the forged orders.

The same source makes Xie Yun part of the preemptive attack. Rather than wait for the evidence to reach the emperor, the group uses an astrological report and Yang Zhen's failed rescue of [[ZhaoTengLateHan|Zhao Teng]] to portray him as resentful, factional, and dangerous. Xie Yun's individual words are not preserved in the source note, but his group role is part of the mechanism that removes Yang Zhen from the taiwei office and sends him toward suicide.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-802-andi-zhisi-donghan-wangchao-quanli-da-xipai-lgvne92e0jjzjnznzfkz7nvztnbj|Hanji 802]] supplies the later consequence. Once Emperor An dies and Yan Xian tries to consolidate the Yan-backed succession, Xie Yun is grouped with Fan Feng, Zhou Guang, [[GengBaoGrandGeneralLateHan|Geng Bao]], and [[WangShengPalaceActorLateHan|Wang Sheng]] as an old-network target. Fan Feng, Xie Yun, and Zhou Guang are imprisoned and executed.

## Key Characteristics

- Favored court insider in the Emperor An-era network.
- Participant in the private-benefit and solicitation field named in Yang Zhen's first current memorial.
- Participant in forged imperial authorization for private construction.
- Member of the group that attacks Yang Zhen before his evidence can be used.
- Later purge victim under the Yan-backed succession settlement.
- Source-bounded figure whose significance comes through recurring group action.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 799, Hanji 800, and Hanji 802. It does not reconstruct Xie Yun's title, biography, household ties, or exact share of responsibility inside Fan Feng's group. The source evidence supports his canonical presence because he recurs in Yang Zhen's first memorial, the forged-edict scandal, and the later purge.
