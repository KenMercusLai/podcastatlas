# Jonathan Wiesen

## Overview

Jonathan Wiesen is a historian whose interpretation in the episode clarifies Heinrich Krieger's ideological admiration for American racial ordering and his administrative criticism of its practice.

## Current Profile

In [[hitler-s-olympics-part-8-vater-it-is-to-be-fayetteville-hitler-s-olympics-part-8-vater-it-is-to-be-fayetteville|Hitler’s Olympics, Part 8]], Wiesen describes [[HeinrichKrieger]] as simultaneously impressed by American racial awareness and dissatisfied with inconsistent implementation. Krieger's horror at lynching concerned uncontrolled, disorganized violence rather than a rejection of racist premises.

Wiesen's contribution helps distinguish moral opposition from bureaucratic preference. A Nazi jurist could criticize American violence while seeking to translate its classifications and exclusions into more centralized state policy.
