# 屈匄 / Qu Gai

屈匄 / Qu Gai appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-51-chuhuaiwang-qin-xiao-er-kan-jiujiu-ruhe-mie-le-ni-ltfkdlivomnvux5xodh2rlfqf6ac]] as the [[ChuState|楚国]] commander captured after [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] attacks [[QinState|秦国]] from anger over [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s promise reversal. The source says Qin captures him at [[DanyangBattle|丹阳之战]] along with many senior Chu nobles.

Qu Gai's page is source-scoped. The episode does not develop him as an independent strategist; his significance is that his capture makes the cost of [[AngerDrivenWarDecision|因怒兴师]] concrete at the command level, not only as abstract loss of face or territory.
