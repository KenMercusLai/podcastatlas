# Guy Molinari

## Overview

Guy Molinari was the Staten Island borough president who championed secession while helping build [[RudyGiuliani]]'s outer-borough route to the New York City mayoralty.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Molinari's secession campaign and Giuliani alliance as complementary rather than contradictory. On the night Staten Islanders celebrated their referendum landslide, Molinari was in Manhattan at Giuliani's victory event. He had encouraged Giuliani's political ambitions, supported his first mayoral campaign, and shared an Italian American, working-class, outer-borough outlook with him.

Molinari's broader project was therefore not only exit from New York City. It also sought to remake city government around the values and grievances of peripheral boroughs. The turnout produced by [[StatenIslandSecession1993]] helped elect Giuliani, whose ferry, waterfront, ballpark, and landfill policies then delivered visible benefits to Molinari's constituency.

## Key Characteristics

- Staten Island borough president and leading secession advocate.
- Early supporter and political sponsor of Giuliani's mayoral ambitions.
- Broker between borough grievance and a citywide outer-borough coalition.
- Shared a working-class Italian American political identity with Giuliani.
- Used both exit pressure and electoral leverage to pursue borough benefits.
