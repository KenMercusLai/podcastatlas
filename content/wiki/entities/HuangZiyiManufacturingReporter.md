# 黄子毅 / Huang Ziyi

## Overview
黄子毅 / Huang Ziyi is represented in the wiki as a [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] reporter whose source roles connect flexible manufacturing, consumer demand, Chinese sportswear industrial history, and body-rhythm public health reporting.

## Current Profile
In [[ep271-tanfang-rouxing-zhizao-gongchang-shi-shui-rang-ni-xiangyao-jiu-neng-maidao-lqyrioptggnbb8hl-llej0-5ow0v]], Huang helps define [[FlexibleManufacturing|柔性制造]] and broadens it from factory switching into demand identification, product design, and manufacturing execution. In [[500-jinjiang-chendaizhen-wangshi-huangziyi-tan-gaikai-jincheng-zhong-de-zhongguo-yundong-pinpai-lswtplovdbo4pl52yoxu6rgzyal2]], he appears as the author of [[XieBangBook|《鞋邦：中国运动品牌四十年》]], using [[Jinjiang]], [[ChendaiTown|陈埭镇]], [[AntaSports|Anta]], [[LiNing|Li-Ning]], and [[Nike]] to explain Chinese sportswear as regional industrial history. EP283 adds a health-reporting branch where he turns late sleep, light exposure, meal timing, school schedules, and body monitoring into [[SocialJetlag|社会时差]] rather than a simple self-discipline problem.

## Key Characteristics
- Reports manufacturing as a linked system of demand, design, production, supply chain, and retail.
- Uses factory and regional cases rather than abstract industrial-policy slogans alone.
- Keeps Chinese manufacturing stories comparative, including global brands and other regional models.
- Treats brand outcomes as rooted in local social organization, market timing, and governance.
- Extends the same systems-reporting style into health by linking sleep to light, institutions, food timing, bowel routines, exercise timing, and metric anxiety.
- Maintains source-spelling caution because the wiki already has several Huang Ziyi name variants.
