# 黄子毅 / Huang Ziyi

## Overview
黄子毅 / Huang Ziyi is represented in the wiki as a [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] manufacturing and business-history reporter whose source roles connect flexible manufacturing, consumer demand, and Chinese sportswear industrial history.

## Current Profile
In [[ep271-tanfang-rouxing-zhizao-gongchang-shi-shui-rang-ni-xiangyao-jiu-neng-maidao-lqyrioptggnbb8hl-llej0-5ow0v]], Huang helps define [[FlexibleManufacturing|柔性制造]] and broadens it from factory switching into demand identification, product design, and manufacturing execution. In [[500-jinjiang-chendaizhen-wangshi-huangziyi-tan-gaikai-jincheng-zhong-de-zhongguo-yundong-pinpai-lswtplovdbo4pl52yoxu6rgzyal2]], he appears as the author of [[XieBangBook|《鞋邦：中国运动品牌四十年》]], using [[Jinjiang]], [[ChendaiTown|陈埭镇]], [[AntaSports|Anta]], [[LiNing|Li-Ning]], and [[Nike]] to explain Chinese sportswear as regional industrial history.

## Key Characteristics
- Reports manufacturing as a linked system of demand, design, production, supply chain, and retail.
- Uses factory and regional cases rather than abstract industrial-policy slogans alone.
- Keeps Chinese manufacturing stories comparative, including global brands and other regional models.
- Treats brand outcomes as rooted in local social organization, market timing, and governance.
- Maintains source-spelling caution because the wiki already has several Huang Ziyi name variants.
