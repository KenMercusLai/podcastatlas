# Superintelligence Force

## Overview

The Superintelligence Force is a Trump-administration AI task force described by [[MarketplaceTech]] as having 120 days to recommend how the U.S. government should foster AI development while addressing risk.

## Current Profile

The source places four lead roles across intelligence, consumer protection, defense, and federal personnel management. That composition suggests a remit spanning national security, consumers, federal adoption, workforce responsibility, and relations with AI companies, but no report or final policy exists in the bounded evidence.

## Key Characteristics

- Has a 120-day recommendation mandate.
- Is expected to emphasize industry dialogue and self-regulation.
- Combines intelligence, consumer-protection, defense, and personnel-management perspectives.
- Includes leaders with source-described technology, investment, or data-center experience.
- Operates inside a U.S.-China technological-competition and national-security frame.

## Evidence

- **Mandate and orientation:** [[tech-20261009-1009-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261009-1009-mp-tech-pod-128]] supplies the 120-day deadline and expected industry-engagement approach.

- **Conflict boundary:** [[tech-20261009-1009-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261009-1009-mp-tech-pod-128]] contrasts the administration's expertise rationale with watchdog concerns over past industry ties.

## Qualifications

The source does not provide the establishing order, complete membership, authority, meeting record, conflict rules, or eventual recommendations. Expected self-regulation and inferred policy scope are reporting judgments, not completed task-force decisions.
