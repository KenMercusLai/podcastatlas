# Beyond Home Textile / 博洋家纺

## Overview

Beyond Home Textile / 博洋家纺 is the Ningbo home-textile company in [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]]. The episode uses it to extend Ningbo manufacturing from factories and supply chains into brand categories, design spaces, and creative-city ambition.

## Current Profile

The source traces Boyang's roots to Ningbo Yongfeng Cloth Factory, founded in 1958, and presents the 1990s reform and 1994 brand move as an early domestic home-textile branding step. It also says Boyang helped popularize the "家纺" category, grouping bedding, towels, curtains, and fabric goods as household textiles.

Its later role is spatial. Around 2017, Boyang began converting older factory and industrial-park resources into design and entrepreneurship space, which later connects to [[NingboFashionCreativeCenter|NFCC 宁波时尚创意中心]].

## Key Characteristics

- Boyang connects old textile manufacturing to a domestic home-textile brand category.
- The source presents it as an early user or promoter of "家纺" as a consumer category.
- Its later renewal route uses old factory resources for design, entrepreneurship, and city-space functions.
- The company is tied to [[RongJunchuan|容俊川]]'s hope for Ningbo to grow a modern fashion industry from the city center.
