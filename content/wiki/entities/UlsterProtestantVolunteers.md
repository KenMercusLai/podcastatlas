# Ulster Protestant Volunteers

The Ulster Protestant Volunteers appear in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as the group founded by [[NoelDoherty]] in April 1966. The episode says it evoked [[EdwardCarson|Carson]]'s 1912 volunteers, used the motto "For God and Ulster," and recruited among [[BSpecials|B Specials]] because they had access to weapons.

The group is important because it links Paisley-aligned mobilization to later loyalist violence and sabotage. In [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]], Paisley-linked Ulster Protestant Volunteer networks are tied to infrastructure bombings falsely blamed on the [[IrishRepublicanArmy|IRA]].
