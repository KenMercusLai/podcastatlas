# 张俭 / Zhang Jian (late Han)

## Overview

Zhang Jian is the late-Han official whose profile now begins with enforcement against [[HouLanLateHan|侯览]] before becoming the 169 CE party-prohibition fugitive whose flight creates a chain of shelter cases, including the Li Du/Mao Qin escape scene, the Kong-family responsibility contest, and the later [[WangmenTouzhi|望门投止]] memory.

## Current Profile

The current evidence presents Zhang Jian as both an enforcement official and a persecuted survivor whose flight creates a severe collateral dilemma. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] gives the upstream enforcement layer: [[ZhaiChaoLateHan|翟超]] appoints Zhang Jian to supervise the eastern area, Zhang memorializes against Hou Lan's property, tomb, and household abuses, and Hou Lan intercepts the reports before they reach [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]. Hanji 893 then gives the later accusation prelude: Hou Lan resents Zhang Jian and uses [[ZhuBiLateHan|朱璧]]'s memorial to frame Zhang and twenty-four others as party figures endangering the court.

Hanji 894 then gives the flight route: Hou Lan's accusation forces Zhang Jian to flee, [[LiDuLateHan|李笃]] shelters him in Donglai, [[MaoQinLateHan|毛钦]] declines to arrest him, and the route through [[XiziRanLateHan|戏子然]] helps him escape beyond the frontier. Hanji 895 concentrates the ethical problem in the Kong household, where [[KongRong|孔融]], [[KongBaoLateHan|孔褒]], and [[KongRongMother|孔融母]] each try to assume responsibility after Zhang escapes.

Zhang Jian's survival is therefore not converted into a simple martyr story. The sources preserve his desperation, his moral reputation, the courage of people who helped him, and the cost transferred onto those helpers. Later return, office acceptance, and withdrawal before [[CaoCao|曹操]] give him a long survival arc, but the page keeps Xia Fu's criticism attached to that arc.

## Key Characteristics

- Enforcement official whose attack on Hou Lan's abuses gives the later accusation a direct grievance prelude.
- Party-prohibition fugitive whose danger in 169 CE develops through Hou Lan and Zhu Bi's accusation chain.
- Reputation-backed fugitive whose moral standing persuades multiple households and local actors to help despite lethal risk.
- Friend-network user whose reliance on Kong Bao's household endangers the people who receive him.
- Source of the [[WangmenTouzhi|望门投止]] idiom, where desperate lodging-seeking preserves survival but signals social cost.
- Later survivor who returns after party prohibitions loosen and briefly accepts a Weiwei appointment.
- Withdrawn elder official who distances himself after reading Cao Cao's ambition.

## Evidence

- Accusation construction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-893-rongyu-bang-ruhe-biancheng-siwang-mingdan-lmxzqoauf79-swwnk-0cevav0gq|Hanji 893]] says Hou Lan resents Zhang Jian and Zhu Bi accuses Zhang Jian and twenty-four others of forming a party and endangering the court.
- Enforcement prelude: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] says Zhai Chao appoints Zhang Jian as eastern-area supervisory official and Zhang Jian memorializes against Hou Lan's illegal wealth, housing, tomb, and family abuses.
- Blocked channel: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] says Hou Lan intercepts Zhang Jian's memorials, keeping the accusation from reaching the ruler.
- Flight route: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-894-yi-chang-taowang-yinfa-de-da-tusha-lhulp9hqu07uayyfl3eg-xwrtkeu|Hanji 894]] says Zhang Jian flees through a difficult route, reaches Li Du's house in Donglai, then moves through Xizi Ran toward Yuyang and beyond the frontier.
- Local protection: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-894-yi-chang-taowang-yinfa-de-da-tusha-lhulp9hqu07uayyfl3eg-xwrtkeu|Hanji 894]] presents Li Du and Mao Qin as a shelter-and-non-arrest scene where Zhang Jian's reputation and the helpers' moral judgment keep the escape alive.
- Kong-family shelter chain: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-895-xuexing-tudao-xia-de-gurou-qingshen-llsplyyrjxhqkpi2aiuqzulgm6w3|Hanji 895]] says Zhang Jian flees to Kong Bao, is hidden by sixteen-year-old Kong Rong, and escapes after the matter is exposed.
- Collateral cost: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-894-yi-chang-taowang-yinfa-de-da-tusha-lhulp9hqu07uayyfl3eg-xwrtkeu|Hanji 894]] says many who sheltered Zhang Jian are punished and more than ten die; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-895-xuexing-tudao-xia-de-gurou-qingshen-llsplyyrjxhqkpi2aiuqzulgm6w3|Hanji 895]] preserves Xia Fu's criticism that Zhang Jian's individual escape spreads disaster to many households.
- Later life: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-895-xuexing-tudao-xia-de-gurou-qingshen-llsplyyrjxhqkpi2aiuqzulgm6w3|Hanji 895]] records Zhang Jian's return after party prohibitions loosen, Jian'an-era Weiwei appointment, withdrawal after seeing Cao Cao's ambition, and death at [[Xudu|许都]] at eighty-four.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped. Hanji 872 gives the enforcement and blocked-memorial prelude, Hanji 893 gives the Zhu Bi accusation prelude, Hanji 894 gives route points and the non-arrest scene, and Hanji 895 supplies the Kong-family responsibility case and later-life note. The current wiki does not yet reconcile every route stop into a full chronology. The Hanji 872 source note dates its case to 166 CE, while Hanji 893-895 cover the later 169 CE party-prohibition consequences. The Hanji 895 raw note renders the name as 张简; this page normalizes the historical late-Han figure as 张俭.

## What Changed

- Hanji 872 adds Zhang Jian's enforcement against Hou Lan before the later accusation and flight sequence.
- Hanji 893 remains the Hou Lan/Zhu Bi accusation prelude before Zhang Jian's flight.
- Hanji 894 adds the Li Du/Mao Qin non-arrest scene, Xizi Ran's route point, and broader punishment to helpers.
- The current profile now treats the Kong-family case as one concentrated shelter scene inside a longer enforcement, accusation, and flight chain.
