## Overview

傅喜 / Fu Xi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-604-xihan-wangmang-duoquan-hou-ruhe-qingxi-quanli-geju-ltwscyyiobc1ocvgti3fd-deajru|Hanji 604]] as a Western Han Fu-family figure who survives [[WangMang|王莽]]'s first cleanup of the Fu and Ding networks after [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]'s death. The episode makes him important because he is not treated as merely lucky: his earlier refusal to follow [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]]'s political line makes him separable from the faction that is being punished.

## Current Profile

Hanji 604 presents Fu Xi as the Fu-clan exception inside a general purge. [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] praises his gravity, caution, loyal thought, and refusal to change his conduct even when Fu Taihou's side punished him by driving him back to his fief. That earlier nonalignment becomes a survival asset once Wang Mang returns to power and the Fu and Ding families lose office, titles, and residence security.

The episode's second emphasis is Fu Xi's response to restored rank. He is recalled to Chang'an and made tejin, a position treated as equivalent to the Three Excellencies, but he responds with fear rather than delight. The host reads that fear as political intelligence: in a court where yesterday's favored houses can collapse overnight, high status is exposure as much as reward.

Fu Xi's later return to his fief and old-age death make him the episode's "善终" example. The profile therefore links him to [[HighStatusCautionSelfProtection|高位谨慎自保]] and [[GoodDeathPoliticalSurvival|乱局善终]] rather than to factional victory.

## Key Characteristics

- Fu-family figure distinguished from the Fu/Ding faction after Aidi's death.
- Earlier nonalignment with Fu Taihou's political line makes him legible as loyal and cautious.
- Recalled and honored by Wang Zhengjun after Wang Mang's return to power.
- Treats sudden rank recovery as dangerous rather than simply fortunate.
- Later returns to his fief and dies at old age, making him the episode's good-end countercase.
