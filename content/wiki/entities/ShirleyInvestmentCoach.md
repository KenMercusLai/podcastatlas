# Shirley（投资教练） / Shirley (Investment Coach)

## Overview
Shirley is a finance professional and part-time investment coach presented in E250 of [[ZhixingXiaojiuguan|知行小酒馆]].

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Shirley describes more than twenty years across investment banking, primary markets, and secondary markets, followed by a personal-investing transition beginning in 2014–2015. She says the 2015 bull-bear reversal led her to distinguish favorable timing from repeatable skill, build an ETF-oriented value system through a full cycle, and later test individual-stock investing at much greater research cost.

Since 2022 she has coached a small number of people through one-to-one, long-duration conversations. Her stated method begins with life purpose and investor fit, may conclude that a person needs only basic financial management, and uses records, historical context, environmental friction, and emotional support to help clients act without transferring final responsibility to the coach.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames investment as a route to time and work choice rather than a contest for maximum wealth.
- Uses full-market-cycle experience to distinguish luck, process, and assumptions that remain untested.
- Favors an ETF-oriented value framework for many ordinary investors while treating individual-stock research as materially more demanding.
- Practices individualized coaching instead of standardized teaching or delegated portfolio control.
- Uses written logs, precommitment, and reduced account salience to lower emotional interference.
- Treats “do less” or “do not participate deeply” as legitimate recommendations when investment lacks personal value or fit.
