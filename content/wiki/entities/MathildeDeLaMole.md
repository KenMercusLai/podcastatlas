# Mathilde de La Mole / 马蒂尔德·德·拉莫尔

Mathilde de La Mole is the aristocratic young woman in [[Stendhal|Stendhal]]'s [[RedAndBlackNovel|《红与黑》]], discussed in [[21-hong-yu-hei-ta-si-yu-zhencheng-569042001]] as proud, intelligent, theatrical, and dissatisfied with the safe men around her. The episode presents her attraction to [[JulienSorel|Julien Sorel]] as partly a recognition that he is not willing to live entirely on his knees.

Her relationship with Julien is read as a contest of pride and role-playing. Unlike [[MadameDeRenal|Madame de Renal]]'s plain sincerity, Mathilde is drawn to drama, family legend, aristocratic romance, and the feeling that love should be historically grand or dangerous.

The episode refuses to make Mathilde a thin "bad woman" or a mere obstacle. Her pregnancy, persistence, and attempts to rescue Julien after the shooting show agency under aristocratic constraint, making her part of the source's [[FemaleSubjectivityInClassicReading]] branch.
