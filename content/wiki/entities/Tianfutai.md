# 天福台 / Tianfutai

## Overview

天福台 is a three-level riverside hospitality and cultural venue in [[Changsha|长沙]], presented as a combined restaurant, teahouse, bar, performance space, and local “city living room.”

## Current Profile

The source treats 天福台 as a more programmatically varied counterpart to [[DongmaojieTeahouse|东茅街茶馆]]. Its floors and dayparts combine local snacks, tea, alcohol, traditional performance, vegetarian dining, quieter late-evening use, and social gathering. That mix is meant to support repeat local use and improve space utilization rather than produce a one-time tourist photo set.

The venue is also the episode's main [[MerchantOwnedDestinationDemand]] case: if the place itself motivates the trip, local platforms can remain discovery and navigation tools without controlling the voucher, checkout, or customer relationship.

## Key Characteristics

- Multi-level, multi-format space combining food, drink, performance, and social use.
- Day-to-night programming intended to serve different moods and improve utilization.
- Local-repeat orientation framed as a “city living room,” not only a tourist attraction.
- Destination-led demand that reportedly operates without platform group-buying vouchers.

## Evidence

- Program design - [[ep149-ep149]] describes a three-floor riverside venue with Changsha snacks, tea, alcohol, traditional performance, vegetarian dining, and late-night social use.
- Repeat-local orientation - [[ep149-ep149]] contrasts a lived urban gathering place with a one-time photo backdrop.
- Merchant control - [[ep149-ep149]] says the venue uses platform information visibility without offering group-buying vouchers or platform checkout.

## Qualifications

The source is favorable and connected to a promoted study trip and a host-disclosed investment relationship. It provides no independently verified revenue, utilization, repeat-rate, margin, or durability evidence. A broad activity mix can increase relevance across dayparts but can also increase operating complexity.
