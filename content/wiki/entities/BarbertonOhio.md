# Barberton, Ohio

## Overview
Barberton, Ohio is the Akron-area city used in the Marketplace Tech episode to make industrial job loss and municipal tax-base damage concrete.

## Current Profile
The source presents Barberton through a former large tire factory that closed in 1980 and later housed smaller businesses. That closure is the episode's local public-finance example: a lost industrial employer quickly becomes frozen hiring and lost income-tax revenue for the city. Barberton therefore anchors the move from labor-market disruption to government revenue risk.

## Key Characteristics
- Barberton is presented as an Akron-area manufacturing locality affected by rubber-industry decline.
- Its former tire factory closure turns technology-driven industrial disruption into a municipal-budget problem.
- The city's source role is evidentiary: it gives the episode's concrete tax-loss figure rather than a broad city profile.
