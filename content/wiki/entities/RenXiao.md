# 任嚣 / Ren Xiao

任嚣 / Ren Xiao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-185-yi-zhang-li-zui-shoufu-zhongguo-wanli-jiangtu-lps0moshdfvosnyhtdhrkdvswibh|Hanji 185]] as the dying Qin Nanhai commandery officer who hands the Lingnan problem to [[ZhaoTuo|赵佗]]. The source says that under [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]], Ren Xiao sees the Qin center collapsing under violence and rebellion, so he summons Zhao Tuo from Longchuan and urges him to use the far southern terrain as a separate political base.

His advice is concrete. Ren Xiao points to the Five Ridges routes, the mountains and sea around Nanhai and Panyu, and the distance from the Central Plains as conditions for self-preservation. He then appoints Zhao Tuo to manage [[NanhaiCommandery|南海郡]], giving Zhao the institutional opening that later becomes [[Nanyue|南越]].

Ren Xiao matters because he turns Qin's earlier [[Baiyue|百越]] commandery expansion into a post-Qin succession problem. The same administrative frontier that Qin created through conquest becomes the base from which Zhao Tuo blocks passes, removes Qin-appointed officials, absorbs [[GuilinCommandery|桂林郡]] and [[XiangCommandery|象郡]], and later has to be drawn into Han order through [[LuJia|陆贾]]'s mission.
