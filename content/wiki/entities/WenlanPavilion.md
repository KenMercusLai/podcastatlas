# 文澜阁 / Wenlan Pavilion

## Overview
文澜阁 / Wenlan Pavilion appears in the [[BowuzhiPodcast|博物志]] Hangzhou episode as a book-heritage site near West Lake and as one of the southern repositories associated with the [[SikuQuanshu|《四库全书》]] tradition.

## Current Profile
The source treats Wenlan Pavilion as a rare heritage space where architecture, garden setting, smell, access, and book history work together. Its value is not only that it belonged to the Qing imperial library network, but that visitors can enter an environment where collection history remains spatially legible.

## Key Characteristics
- Located at West Lake's Gushan area in the episode's account.
- Connected to the Qing [[SikuQuanshu|《四库全书》]] library system.
- Remembered in the source as destroyed during the Taiping-war period and later rebuilt.
- Valued by the host for garden completeness, wooden atmosphere, and visitor access.

## Evidence
- Site description - [[246-hangzhou-bowuguan-yiwei-hangzhi-haojiu-mei-kan-guo-zheme-tuijian-de-zhanlan-le-8217911423-973793]] places Wenlan Pavilion at Gushan and inside the old Zhejiang Museum compound.
- Library network - [[246-hangzhou-bowuguan-yiwei-hangzhi-haojiu-mei-kan-guo-zheme-tuijian-de-zhanlan-le-8217911423-973793]] names Wenlan Pavilion alongside Wenyuan, Wenjin, Wensu, Wenzong, and Wenhui pavilions.
- Visitor experience - [[246-hangzhou-bowuguan-yiwei-hangzhi-haojiu-mei-kan-guo-zheme-tuijian-de-zhanlan-le-8217911423-973793]] emphasizes atmosphere, wood smell, and the ability to enter.

## Qualifications
The page follows the episode's travel account. It does not verify architectural chronology or the full surviving collection history beyond the source's claims.
