# 醯落 / Xiluo (Southern Xiongnu)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-939-donghan-luanju-de-fengkuang-sanguo-xuqu-lthmvw8ihk96fuez9vamlryzcngy|Hanji 939]] introduces 醯落 / Xiluo as a right-division [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] rebel actor. After the Han court orders Southern Xiongnu cavalry to support [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] against [[ZhangChunLateHan|张纯]], Xiluo helps lead the backlash against repeated requisition.

The source says Xiluo joins with the [[TugeHu|屠各胡]], gathers a large rebel force, kills [[QiangquChanyu|羌渠]], and opens the succession of [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]]. His page is source-scoped as an internal-revolt node rather than a full biography.
