# 《惊魂记》 / Psycho

[[PsychoFilm|《惊魂记》 / Psycho]] is the central film example in [[27-xiqukeke-yizai-nin-zhuoxia-anzhi-zhadan-582370829]]. The episode retells the stolen-money opening, the motel murder, the disappearance of the money into the swamp, the investigation by the sister and private detective, and the final revelation around the motel owner's mother persona.

The source uses the film to explain [[MacGuffin|麦格芬]] and [[NarrativeMisdirection]]. The forty thousand dollars looks like the story's main concern, but it becomes disposable once the suspense shifts toward identity, hidden violence, and what the audience does or does not know about the motel household.
