## Overview

孟昶 / Meng Chang is presented by the supplied episode as the last ruler of [[LaterShu|后蜀]]. He enters its era-name story through his surrender to [[ZhaoKuangyin|赵匡胤]] and the subsequent transfer of a consort identified as [[LadyHuaruiLaterShu|花蕊夫人]] into the Song palace.

## Current Profile

The episode gives Meng Chang only a compressed conquest-and-court profile. His fall supplies the movement path for people and objects from Later Shu to the Northern Song court; the alleged 乾德 mirror therefore links a conquered Later Shu household to the forgotten chronology of the earlier [[FormerShu|前蜀]]. The same source associates Meng Chang with Chengdu gardens, peonies, and Lady Huarui's literary and aesthetic memory.

## Key Characteristics

- Last ruler of Later Shu in the episode's account.
- Surrendered ruler whose household entered the Northern Song conquest order.
- Political link between Lady Huarui's palace transfer and Zhao Kuangyin's 乾德 discovery story.
- Patron associated by the episode with Chengdu peonies and court culture.

## Evidence

### Conquest and household transfer

- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-301-tai-liqi-jiemi-nianhao-zui-da-wulong-shijian-lrsbd_9zfkcofnosolvrjvfcdfyg|Hanji 301]] says Meng Chang surrendered after the Song conquest and died shortly afterward, after which Lady Huarui entered the Song palace.

### Court-culture memory

- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-301-tai-liqi-jiemi-nianhao-zui-da-wulong-shijian-lrsbd_9zfkcofnosolvrjvfcdfyg|Hanji 301]] associates Meng Chang with extensive peony planting and an ambition to make Chengdu's flowers surpass Luoyang's.

## Qualifications

- This is not a complete biography or political history of Meng Chang or Later Shu.
- The episode's year of conquest, seven-day death interval, cause-neutral “sudden death” account, household sequence, and garden claims require independent verification.
- The identity of the transferred consort, the ownership of the mirror, and the relationship between the object and Meng Chang's household remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Added Meng Chang as the Later Shu ruler connecting conquest, household transfer, and the reported 乾德 mirror story.
- Added a source-scoped court-culture association with Chengdu peonies.
