# Sarah Friar

## Overview
Sarah Friar appears in the wiki as OpenAI's CFO in an [[AllIn|All-In]] interview focused on financing, compute scarcity, enterprise adoption, product strategy, and infrastructure buildout.

## Current Profile
In this source, Friar is the finance executive translating [[OpenAI]]'s product demand into capital allocation and infrastructure strategy. She treats an IPO as a financing milestone rather than the destination, argues that consumer and enterprise adoption can grow together, and frames compute as the scarce input that determines how quickly OpenAI can serve demand.

Her profile is operational rather than purely financial. Friar discusses power, land, regulation, racks, chips, memory, talent, local trust, cloud-provider structures, custom chips, pricing, advertising, and consumer hardware as connected parts of OpenAI's growth model.

## Key Characteristics
- Finance-led strategist who frames IPO timing, private fundraising, and debt access as tools for preserving OpenAI's flexibility.
- Infrastructure-focused operator who treats compute scarcity as a constraint spanning energy, land, regulation, chips, memory, talent, and community trust.
- Platform-oriented executive who describes OpenAI as an AI intelligence or infrastructure layer serving consumers, businesses, enterprises, and governments.
- Commercial translator who links enterprise AI value to productivity, revenue growth, patient screening, FDA approval speed, and value-based pricing.
- Trust-sensitive monetization voice who is open to advertising in [[ChatGPT]] while emphasizing answer integrity and an ad-free tier.
