# Cape Town

## Overview
Cape Town is the episode's strongest South African scenery recommendation and the departure point for [[RobbenIsland]].

## Current Profile
王妈妈 describes Cape Town as one of the most beautiful cities she has visited, with coastline, food, wine, and access to nearby nature. In the episode's route, it balances the heavier Johannesburg apartheid-memory itinerary by showing why South Africa remains highly rewarding for travelers.

The city is not detached from history. The ferry route to [[RobbenIsland]] makes Cape Town a scenic and historical node at once: the same coastal beauty sits beside prison memory, Mandela memory, and the unfinished question of whether post-apartheid South Africa has been reborn.

## Key Characteristics
- Scenic high point of the episode's South Africa recommendation.
- Coastal city associated with seafood, wine, nearby wildlife viewing, and self-drive routes.
- Departure point for [[RobbenIsland]] and its prison-memory itinerary.
- Counterweight to the security-heavy [[Johannesburg]] opening.
- Example of South Africa's high reward for travelers who can manage complexity.
