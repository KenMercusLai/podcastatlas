## Overview

张敞 / Zhang Chang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-469-weishenme-junzi-dou-buguo-xiaoren-kankan-xiaowangzhi-de-xiachang-lj8iji-c3scj6vznntavm6awolho|Hanji 469]] as a Western Han official proposed by [[ZhengPengWesternHan|郑朋]] as a possible crown-prince teacher under [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 469 presents Zhang Chang as competent but informal. Xiao Wangzhi acknowledges his ability, especially in difficult local administration, yet judges him insufficiently learned and dignified for the role of crown-prince teacher. Yuan summons Zhang for another office, but Zhang dies before taking it. The host uses the familiar story of Zhang painting his wife's eyebrows as a contrast to Xiao Wangzhi: Zhang is portrayed as more internally stable under outside judgment.

## Key Characteristics

- Capable Western Han official associated in this source with practical local governance ability.
- Proposed by Zheng Peng for crown-prince education but judged unsuitable by Xiao Wangzhi.
- Used by the host as a case of stable self-possession under social criticism.
- His full career remains outside the current source's bounded evidence.
