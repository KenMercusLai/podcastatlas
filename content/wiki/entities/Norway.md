# Norway

[[how-to-beat-the-resource-curse-in-norway-summer-school]] adds Norway as the positive countercase to the wiki's existing oil-resource-curse branch. The [[PlanetMoney]] Summer School episode presents the country as rich and high-trust, but not frictionless: [[HildeBjornland]] notes debates over entrepreneurship, productivity, technology, AI readiness, wealth taxes, and public spending.

The oil branch centers on [[FaroukAlQassem]], [[EkofiskOilField|Ekofisk]], and the [[NorwegianOilFund]]. Norway avoided a simple boom-spending path by preparing early, regulating oil companies, limiting license blocks, taxing heavily, reinvesting in offshore technical capacity, and saving later revenues through [[SovereignOilFundGovernance]].

The salmon branch turns Norway from resource manager into market creator. With excess salmon supply and cultural resistance to raw salmon in [[Japan]], Norwegian officials and [[BjornAdekOlsen]] used [[ExportMarketCoordination]], [[NationalExportBranding]], and a deal with [[Nishire]] to help normalize salmon sushi.
