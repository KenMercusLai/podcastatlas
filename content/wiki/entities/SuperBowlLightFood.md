# Super Bowl / 超级碗（轻食品牌）

## Overview

Super Bowl / 超级碗 is the Chinese light-food restaurant brand discussed in episode 148. The semantic suffix distinguishes it from the existing [[SuperBowl|Super Bowl championship event]].

## Current Profile

The source presents the brand as a direct-operated light-food chain focused on office-heavy districts in major cities. It competes inside a health-oriented meal set, where a price above ordinary fast food can still feel reasonable relative to more expensive healthy meals. Direct operation supports fresh-ingredient and quality control but makes expansion slower and more capital-intensive than franchise-heavy beverage chains or [[KPro]], which inherits KFC infrastructure.

## Key Characteristics

- Light-food chain offering a filling health-positioned meal rather than salad alone.
- Office-worker and mixed office-mall site-selection focus.
- Direct-operated expansion model tied to fresh procurement and quality control.
- Price evaluated against other health meals as well as ordinary fast food.
- Smaller brand that may benefit when large chains educate consumers about light food.
