# Elizabeth Holmes

## Overview

[[ElizabethHolmes|Elizabeth Holmes]] is the founder and former public face of [[Theranos]]. The bounded evidence now contains two sharply different vantage points: [[TylerShultz|Tyler Shultz]] describes validation failure, secrecy, credibility leverage, and whistleblower pressure, while Holmes's spouse [[BillyEvans|Billy Evans]] describes her private character, family life, continuing claim of innocence, and imprisonment.

## Current Profile

Holmes remains the central decision and narrative figure in the wiki's Theranos case. Evans's account materially humanizes her and documents consequences for her children, but it does not answer the lab-level evidence in Shultz's account or independently overturn her investor-fraud convictions. The current profile therefore separates personhood and family impact from technical validation, corporate conduct, and legal guilt.

## Key Characteristics

- Mission-driven founder whose diagnostic-access story attracted employees, investors, board members, and public attention.
- Skilled relationship and narrative builder whose persona became part of both the company's credibility and later criticism.
- Executive whom Shultz's sources associate with ignored validation concerns, secrecy, and pressure against dissent.
- Convicted defendant who, according to Evans, continues to maintain innocence of the investor-fraud counts.
- Incarcerated mother whose family maintains regular visits and navigates separation from two young children.

## Evidence

- **Founder credibility and operational conflict:** [[tsr-s4-tylershultzpart1-final-tsr-s4-tylershultzpart1-final]] describes Holmes attracting Shultz through mission and persona, then forwarding his evidence-based escalation to [[SunnyBalwani|Sunny Balwani]] and framing him as hostile after resignation.
- **External pressure and persistent loyalty:** [[tsr-s4-tylershultzpart2-v3-tsr-s4-tylershultzpart2-v3]] describes Holmes and Balwani inferring Shultz's role in the [[WallStreetJournal|Wall Street Journal]] investigation, while [[GeorgeShultz|George Shultz]] continued to trust her after regulatory scrutiny.
- **Private and family counter-perspective:** [[billy-evans-ais26-audio-88-billy-evans-ais26-audio-88]] presents Evans's account of Holmes as an attentive partner and mother, her silence during proceedings, the verdict, imprisonment, and family visits.

## Qualifications

The Shultz episodes are first-person whistleblower accounts supported in their narration by emails, reporting, and later regulatory action, but they are not complete trial records. Evans is Holmes's spouse and advocate; his descriptions of innocence, revenue projections, judicial guidance, sentence calculations, and future evidence remain attributed and untested in the episode. Acquittal on patient-fraud counts does not negate separate validation concerns, while conviction does not make every media characterization of Holmes fair or relevant.
