# Josh Roberts

Josh Roberts is the capital-markets correspondent in [[stock-options-how-to-hedge-an-ai-bubble-698efe7e7301331f1f674bd5]]. He explains why investors can believe in AI's long-term importance while still worrying that AI-linked equity prices and hyperscaler capex assumptions have become fragile.

[[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]] adds Roberts as the explainer for an [[AdvanceNewsTradingExperiment]] in which people were shown tomorrow's headlines but still struggled to make money. His practical lesson is that markets move against expectations, not headlines alone, and that [[PositionSizing]] can matter more than a correct directional guess.
