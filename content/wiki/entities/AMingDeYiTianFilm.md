# 《阿明的一天》 / A Ming's Day

## Overview
《阿明的一天》 is an AI short by [[ShanYinAIDirector|山音]] discussed in [[ai-wuxian-rensheng-youxian-duitan-kk-shanyin-yixian-ai-chuangzuozhe-lmk4luckjgn-cpivd-7dy9vmsl0u|AI 无限，人生有限]]. Its central device is that changing clothes produces a different social personality.

## Current Profile
The film represents a co-evolving rather than screenplay-first workflow. Shanyin began from one sentence, accepted some AI-proposed character forms, rejected others, and allowed text, visuals, and edit needs to reshape the work until it expressed the exhaustion of moving among work, business, and studio roles.

The reported three-day production makes it an extreme example of compressed execution, but its relevance is not speed alone. The film's premise came from the creator's own time fragmentation, supporting the distinction between fast production and slow accumulation of meaningful material.

## Key Characteristics
- Built from the premise “changing clothes changes personality.”
- Uses costume changes to represent fatigue across multiple social roles.
- Developed through feedback among AI suggestions, writing, generated visuals, and editing.
- Reportedly moved from initial idea to completed work in three days.
- Grounds a stylized device in the creator's own experience of fragmented attention and responsibility.

## Qualifications
The work's production duration, festival history, release details, and reception are reported by the interview rather than independently verified. A fast workflow should not be generalized to all AI films or treated as evidence that experience, writing, or finishing costs have disappeared.
