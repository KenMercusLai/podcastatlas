# 田忌 / Tian Ji

田忌 / Tian Ji appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]] as the [[QiState|齐国]] general who recognizes [[SunBin|孙膑]] and gives him protected status as a guest. His first major source role is the horse-racing story, where Sun Bin studies the relative quality of Tian Ji's horses and proposes the arrangement later named [[TianJiHorseRacing|田忌赛马]].

Tian Ji then becomes the commander in the rescue of [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] initially wants Sun Bin to lead, but Sun Bin argues that his punished body and social position would make army authority difficult. Tian Ji takes formal command while Sun Bin supplies the strategic design that leads to [[EncirclingWeiToRescueZhao|围魏救赵]] and [[GuilingBattle|桂陵之战]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-19-maling-zhi-zhan-lhbapt09x2ptdqx0uopkp8apbada]] gives Tian Ji another rescue-war role. In the Qi court debate over [[HanState|韩国]], he argues that Qi should rescue early because letting Wei absorb Han would break the balance. [[SunBin|孙膑]] wins the timing argument with [[DelayedRescueStatecraft|delayed rescue]], but Tian Ji remains one of the formal commanders when Qi finally moves toward [[MalingBattle|马陵之战]].
