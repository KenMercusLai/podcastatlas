# Throckmorton Plot

The Throckmorton Plot appears in [[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] as the 1583 Catholic conspiracy linking foreign invasion, English Catholic support, and [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]]' claim against [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]]. Its continental planning centered on [[HenryDukeOfGuise|Henry, Duke of Guise]], Catholic exiles such as [[WilliamAllen]], papal support, [[PhilipII|Philip II]]'s money, and diplomatic support from [[BernardinoDeMendoza]].

The plan called for a main landing in northwest [[England]], especially Catholic Lancashire, and a smaller landing in Sussex. [[FrancisThrockmorton]] served as the English link among Mary, London conspirators, Paris conspirators, and embassy channels.

[[FrancisWalsingham]]'s network exposed the plot through embassy penetration, copied cipher correspondence, and surveillance of Throckmorton. The plot's failure led to Throckmorton's execution, Mendoza's expulsion, tighter controls on Mary, and the [[BondOfAssociation]].
