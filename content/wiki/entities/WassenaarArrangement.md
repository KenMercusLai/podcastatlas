# Wassenaar Arrangement / 瓦森纳安排

## Overview
The Wassenaar Arrangement is a multilateral framework for coordinating controls on conventional arms and dual-use goods and technologies.

## Current Profile
[[2b2e96d8aea7-2b2e96d8aea7]] treats the arrangement as the looser successor context to [[CoCom]]. Its inclusion of Russia after the Soviet Union's collapse illustrates the episode's broader claim that export-control coalitions follow changing strategic interests rather than permanent friend-enemy categories.

## Key Characteristics
- Coordinates information and controls around conventional arms and dual-use items.
- Is presented as less centralized and less restrictive than CoCom.
- Reflects post-Cold War membership and threat assumptions rather than the earlier bloc structure.
- Serves as a comparison point for proposals to coordinate AI, chip, and compute controls.

## Evidence
Succession and design:
- [[2b2e96d8aea7-2b2e96d8aea7]] describes Wassenaar as taking over part of CoCom's control function with looser reporting and coordination.
