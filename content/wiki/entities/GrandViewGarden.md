# 大观园 / Grand View Garden

[[ritan-chuantai-hongloumeng-haishi-keyi-du-yidu-de-587167638]] reads 大观园 as the protected youth space at the center of [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]]. Opened by [[JiaYuanchun|贾元春]]'s authorization, it temporarily gathers [[JiaBaoyu|贾宝玉]], [[LinDaiyu|林黛玉]], [[XueBaochai|薛宝钗]], [[Qingwen|晴雯]], [[Lingguan|龄官]], [[ShiXiangyun|史湘云]], [[Miaoyu|妙玉]], and other young figures before ordinary household and marriage order fully closes around them.

The episode's point is not that the garden is safe in any permanent sense. It is a dream-like interval where poetry, games, friendship, and [[TruthfulTemperamentInRedChamber|truthful temperament]] become visible, but its protection depends on imperial and family conditions that eventually fail.
