# 王继恩 / Wang Jien

Wang Jien / 王继恩 is the palace eunuch whose actions shape the succession sequence in [[170-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-xia-958158115]]. After [[ZhaoKuangyin|赵匡胤 / 宋太祖]] dies, [[SongEmpress|宋皇后]] reportedly tells him to summon [[ZhaoDefang|赵德芳]].

The episode says Wang Jien instead goes to [[ZhaoGuangyi|赵光义 / 宋太宗]] at the Kaifeng office, where he also encounters [[ChengDexuan|程德玄]]. Wang then urges Zhao Guangyi to move quickly, warning that delay could let the throne fall to someone else.

For the source, Wang Jien is a small actor with large institutional meaning. His route turns a palace instruction into a completed succession path, making the process look organized before open recognition catches up.
