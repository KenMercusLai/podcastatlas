# 张颢 / Zhang Hao (late-Han taiwei)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-911-gudai-gongwuyuan-ye-neng-bao-fenpei-ls3pxu9xlpuulsdailx8tcoylv9e|Hanji 911]] brings 张颢 / Zhang Hao into the wiki as the Changshan official promoted from taichang to taiwei shortly after the Guanghe first-year amnesty and era change under [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]. The podcast summary renders him as 张昊, but this page normalizes to 张颢 because the chronicle line for the taiwei appointment uses that character.

The episode uses Zhang Hao less as a policy designer than as an appointment-channel example. His rise is tied to a Zhongchangshi brother, so the case belongs with [[CourtOfficeCapture|朝廷开府任官失序]]: a top court office can be reached through palace or eunuch attachment rather than transparent public selection.

Zhang Hao is also attached to a gold-seal omen story. While serving in Liang, a bird-and-stone incident produces a seal marked "忠孝侯印"; the court treats it as favorable, and the episode reads the story skeptically as [[AuspiciousOmenPolitics|祥瑞政治]] joined to career aspiration.
