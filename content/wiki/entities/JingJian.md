# 景监 / Jing Jian

景监 / Jing Jian appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-12-gongsunyang-qiuzhi-lu-shang-de-la-guanxi-zou-houmen-lvv4-bz8sm9scezci-o-msapkvil]] as the favored figure who introduces [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] to [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]]. His practical role is gatekeeping: Qin has issued an open recruitment edict, but Shang Yang still needs a person close to the ruler before his proposals can be heard.

The episode identifies 景 as his clan name and links the Jing lineage to the [[ChuState|楚国]] royal-family branch of the Mi surname. It also discusses the label "辟臣," which the host presents as a morally suspect favorite rather than a settled bureaucratic office.

The episode explicitly resists a simple "eunuch" reading. Because earlier terms for eunuchs include 寺人, 阍, 竖, and 小臣, and because the name 监 alone is not enough evidence, this page keeps Jing Jian as a court favorite / access broker rather than a eunuch unless later sources require revision.
