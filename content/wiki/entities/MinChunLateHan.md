# 闵纯 / Min Chun (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] introduces Min Chun as one of [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]]'s subordinates resisting the surrender of [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] to [[YuanShao|袁绍]]. He is paired with [[GengWuLateHan|耿武]] in both warning and action.

Min Chun's source role is to show that Han Fu's collapse is not caused by total informational darkness. Han Fu has people willing to state the province's military and grain advantages and later willing to resist Yuan Shao's entry, but their warnings do not become policy.
