## Overview

一言莫衍 / Yi Yan Moyan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-510-hanchengdi-de-zhedao-shengzhi-weihe-dachen-gan-yangfengyinwei-ljxebrollq4yydnmg8otocckmth4|Hanji 510]] as a [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] envoy sent to [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]] who unexpectedly asks to defect before returning north.

## Current Profile

The source uses Yi Yan Moyan as the test case for a Chengdi-era diplomatic judgment. His request looks attractive under older Han practice, when accepting Xiongnu defectors could weaken a hostile frontier power. [[GuYongWesternHan|谷永]] and [[DuQinWesternHan|杜钦]] argue that the context has changed because the chanyu is now formally submissive; accepting the envoy could damage trust with the chanyu or reward a deliberate test.

Chengdi accepts the refusal logic. After questioning, Yi Yan Moyan says he had been mad when he asked to defect, and Han sends him back to Xiongnu. The episode says his office is preserved after return, but the chanyu never lets him meet Han envoys again.

## Key Characteristics

- Xiongnu envoy to Western Han during Chengdi's Heping 2 context.
- Requests defection before returning north, threatening suicide if Han refuses.
- Becomes a diplomatic credibility test because his ruler is treated as a submitted chanyu rather than an open enemy.
- Later retracts the request by claiming madness after Han questions him.
