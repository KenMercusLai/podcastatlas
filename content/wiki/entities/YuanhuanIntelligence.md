# Yuanhuan Intelligence / 圆环智能

Yuanhuan Intelligence is the company or lab vehicle connected to [[LiuZiming|Liu Ziming]] in [[149-qinli-zhongmei-new-labs-ziben-kuangchao-he-qinghua-liuziming-liao-ai-for-ai-jizhi-kejieshixing-he-max-tegmark-lm33q4n6w8tzcd2fxdbuk9unc2xv]]. The source presents it as part of the 2026 [[NewLabOrganization|New Lab]] wave around [[AIForAI]], [[AIForScience]], and [[WorldModels]], where young research-heavy teams can raise capital before conventional product proof.

The company's technical route in the source is not described as an ordinary AI application. It is attached to Liu's [[PhysicsOfAI]] thesis: structure AI research through [[OPHISResearchWorkflow]], collect research-process data, build [[MetaModelTrainingCurvePrediction|meta-models]] for training curves, and eventually produce [[TrainingAutopilot]] or [[VibeTraining]] systems.

## Key Claims
- Yuanhuan is used as a concrete example of research talent moving from university and institute settings into venture-backed AI lab formation.
- The source frames its market environment as unusually hot, especially around AI for Science, world models, and AI for AI.
- Its near-term route is R&D, demos, and product prototype exploration before harder commercialization decisions.
