## Overview

The Battle of Kunyang / 昆阳之战 is a turning-point battle in the collapse of [[WangMang|王莽]]'s [[XinDynasty|Xin dynasty]] and the later restoration reputation of [[LiuXiu|刘秀 / 光武帝]]. Its current wiki role is to expose the gap between symbolic state grandeur and battlefield capacity while preserving Liu Xiu's sortie, reinforcement, morale, and legendary mandate layers.

## Current Profile

In the current wiki, Kunyang is less a complete operational history than a compact proof point. Wang Mang's side appears numerically and ritually immense but functionally brittle, while Liu Xiu's actions become evidence of battlefield judgment under asymmetry. Liu Xiu breaks out with a small cavalry group, returns with reinforcements, uses victory information to shift morale, and attacks the opposing command center.

The battle also carries a legendary layer. Meteor and cloud descriptions are useful for understanding Liu Xiu's later reputation as "天命之子", but they remain 史书传奇/mandate narration rather than a modern proof claim.

## Key Characteristics

- Turning-point battle that makes Wang Mang's regime look much weaker in actual coercive capacity than in symbolic scale.
- Numerical-asymmetry battle where Liu Xiu's tactical initiative matters more than raw troop counts.
- Morale and information case: reported victories and battlefield confidence are treated as active parts of the battle.
- Mandate-memory site where meteor, cloud, and "天命之子" narration attach legendary legitimacy to Liu Xiu.
- Conceptual proof point for the contrast between ritualized legitimacy and usable military-political capacity.

## Qualifications

The current evidence does not reconstruct the whole campaign in military-detail form. Hanji 699 PLUS gives figures such as Wang Mang's claimed 400,000 troops, Liu Xiu's initial 7,000, roughly 17,000 reinforcements, and a 3,000-person strike force, but those numbers should remain source-scoped. The meteor and cloud material belongs to legendary mandate narration rather than factual verification of supernatural signs.
