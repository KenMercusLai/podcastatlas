## Overview
丝绸之路 / Ancient Silk Road is created as a disambiguated ancient-route page because the existing [[SilkRoad]] page refers to the online marketplace. In this source, the ancient Silk Road appears as part of the east-west route world organized around [[LongshanGansuShaanxi|陇山 / 龙山]] and the [[WesternRegionsProtectorate|Western Regions]].

## Current Profile
The episode does not offer a comprehensive Silk Road history. Its narrower claim is that ancient east-west traffic between Guanzhong, Longyou, Hexi, and the Western Regions had to deal with the Longshan crossing problem. The Silk Road therefore appears as a route system whose eastern approach is shaped by northwest Chinese mountain geography rather than as a detached transcontinental trade label.

## Key Characteristics
- Ancient route frame distinct from the online-marketplace Silk Road page.
- East-west passage system tied in this source to Longshan, Guanlong roads, Hexi, and the Western Regions.
- Historical-geography object where mountain passes and political capacity matter as much as trade imagery.
- Route context for Han Wudi's westward strategy and Zhang Qian-related place memory.
- Source-scoped branch rather than a full trade, cultural, or diplomatic history.
