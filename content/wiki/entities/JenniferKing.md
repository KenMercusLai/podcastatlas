# Jennifer King

## Overview
Jennifer King appears in the wiki as the Stanford privacy researcher interviewed by [[MarketplaceTech]] about California data-broker compliance, deletion rights, and AI developers' potential demand for brokered personal data.

## Current Profile
King's source role is to translate a Stanford report into an operational privacy critique. She treats California's privacy law as unusually strong, but argues that many registered data brokers are not complying with required practices. Her analysis links legal rights, user-experience friction, enforcement capacity, and generative-AI data demand.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as the expert voice for the Stanford data-broker compliance report discussed in the episode.
- Frames compliance as a practical workflow problem rather than only a legal-status question.
- Connects deletion rights to enforcement scale, including a recommendation for private lawsuits.
- Treats generative AI developers as a possible new buyer class for brokered personal data.
