# William Durant / 威廉·杜兰特

William Durant is one of the three 1929 role portraits in [[170-1929-de-paomo-zhixia-sange-daibiao-renwu-he-tamen-zai-dangxia-zhouqi-de-yingzi-lo7uv7umozr0jrqfjzyhdbzicx1j]]. The episode presents him as the founder associated with [[GeneralMotors]] and Chevrolet who had real industrial insight before becoming deeply absorbed in stock-market speculation, stock pools, and public conflict with credit tightening.

The source uses Durant to separate industry vision from market mastery. Understanding cars, consumers, and company-building did not mean he could survive a leveraged financial cycle. In [[BubbleRoleAnalogy]], Durant becomes the entrepreneur-speculator figure whose past success strengthens conviction until reputation, wealth, and self-proof are tied to a market that can turn against him.
