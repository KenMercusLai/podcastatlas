# Sarah Gilbert

## Overview
Sarah Gilbert is presented in the supplied [[MarketplaceTech]] source as a researcher at Cornell University's Citizens and Technology Lab who studies online communities and platform governance.

## Current Profile
In this source, Gilbert interprets [[Reddit]]'s login wall as an ecosystem problem rather than a narrow product change. AI summaries can reduce source visits, access restrictions can reduce public benefit and future contribution, and identity verification can deter bots while excluding people who rely on pseudonymity. Her proposed response distributes responsibility across regulators, platforms, AI companies, funders, and users.

## Key Characteristics
- Research lens centered on online communities, participation incentives, and platform design.
- Treats current human contribution as infrastructure for both public knowledge and future AI systems.
- Connects access policy to contributor motivation rather than measuring success only through platform revenue.
- Preserves the tension between bot resistance and privacy-protecting pseudonymity.
