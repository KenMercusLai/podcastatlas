## Overview

濡须坞 / Ruxu Fortress is the defensive position that [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] urges [[SunQuan|孙权]] to build at the Ruxu waterway as protection against a renewed [[CaoCao|曹操]] offensive.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1128-meiyou-heikeji-guren-ruhe-diaocha-nimingxin-lqdm57jzw9wqo_umlasz2j3vlzqh|Hanji 1128]] says other commanders consider a fort unnecessary because troops can land for battle and return to ships afterward. Lyu Meng rejects the assumption that reboarding will always remain possible: battles can be lost, pursuit can be fast, and soldiers may need immediate cover before reaching the fleet.

Sun Quan accepts the argument and funds construction. In this bounded account, Ruxu is therefore not merely a fixed wall; it is operational fallback infrastructure connecting land combat, river mobility, retreat timing, and continued resistance after an unfavorable encounter.

## Key Characteristics

- Riverine defensive work tied to both land and naval operations.
- Built in anticipation of renewed Cao-side pressure.
- Designed around the possibility of defeat rather than assumed victory.
- Provides a temporary refuge when pursuit prevents orderly re-embarkation.
- Material expression of Lyu Meng's risk-sensitive advice and Sun Quan's acceptance.

## Qualifications

The source summary does not establish the fortification's exact date, engineering form, dimensions, labor system, cost, location boundaries, or performance in later battles. “Ruxu Fortress” is a functional English label for the source's 濡须坞, not a claim that it matched a standardized fortress type.
