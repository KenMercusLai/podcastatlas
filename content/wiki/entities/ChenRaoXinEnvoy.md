## Overview

陈饶 / Chen Rao is a [[XinDynasty|Xin]] envoy whose destruction of an old [[Xiongnu|Xiongnu]] seal makes [[WangMang|Wang Mang]]'s hidden status downgrade irreversible. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-639-zhengshou-suodeshui-di-yi-ren-shi-zhewei-zuzong-ljwyj6k7u0zpegj74rbwbdgkzrcq|Hanji 639]] supplies the decision and destruction scene; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-640-qipa-wangmang-ruhe-chedi-bifan-xiongnu-lhxbx6keibmfe8z6bcsmlpnr6eht|Hanji 640]] supplies the chanyu's later discovery and Wang Mang's reward.

## Current Profile

In Hanji 639, Chen Rao serves as Five Authority Right Marshal within [[WangJunXinGeneral|王骏]]'s seal-exchange mission. The envoys obtain the old “匈奴单于玺” without disclosing that the replacement says “新匈奴单于章.” After drinking with the chanyu, Chen Rao anticipates that inspection of the new wording could produce a demand for reversal. He therefore smashes the old seal with an axe, removing the simplest route back to the previous status formula.

Hanji 640 shows the consequence. Once the chanyu discovers the downgrade, the envoys can display only the destroyed old seal and invoke Xin mandate logic. Wang Mang then rewards Chen Rao, signaling that the court approves the irreversible humiliation rather than treating the destruction as unauthorized damage.

## Key Characteristics

- Xin envoy and Five Authority Right Marshal in the Xiongnu seal mission.
- Recognizes that delayed inspection may reopen an apparently completed exchange.
- Uses physical destruction to remove the counterpart's reversal option.
- Converts concealed wording into an irreversible diplomatic fact.
- Is rewarded by Wang Mang after the act, making court approval visible.
- Current profile is bounded to the seal-exchange episode and its immediate aftermath.

## Qualifications

The two sources provide a compact diplomatic-action profile rather than a full biography. Chen Rao's exact authority relative to Wang Jun, whether the destruction was planned before the mission, the legal treatment of envoy property, and his private motives remain source-scoped. The interpretation that “玺” to “章” necessarily lowers status is the episodes' reading of the exchange and is preserved with that attribution.
