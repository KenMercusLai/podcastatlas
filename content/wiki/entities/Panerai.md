# Panerai

Panerai appears in [[rolex-1]] as an early professional-use watch context before the [[RolexSubmariner]] existed. The source says [[Rolex]] made early dive watches for Panerai's Italian Navy contract, connecting Rolex technology to military and underwater use cases.

The Panerai reference helps show that Rolex's later dive-watch identity did not emerge from pure lifestyle branding. It had roots in technical and professional needs that later fed [[ProfessionalToolBranding]] and [[MechanicalWatchRepositioning]].
