# 《我不相信神话》

《我不相信神话》 is the [[OrianaFallaci|Oriana Fallaci / 奥利亚娜·法拉奇]] book discussed in [[77-ta-shi-ziyou-de-yegou-ta-shi-falaqi-685710187]]. The episode says its Italian title means that only Fallaci can tell her own story, and presents the Chinese volume as a posthumous selection and reorganization of her articles and life material rather than an authorized biography she planned.

The book matters in the source because it fits Fallaci's refusal to become someone else's monument. [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] treats the assembled writings as a better route into her than a smooth biography: they show the great reporter, the angry witness, the ordinary woman, the controversial public speaker, and the person who kept resisting myth after resisting rulers' myths.
