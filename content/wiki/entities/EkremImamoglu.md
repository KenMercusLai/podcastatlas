# Ekrem Imamoglu

Ekrem Imamoglu is the Istanbul mayor and opposition figure mentioned in [[stock-options-how-to-hedge-an-ai-bubble-698efe7e7301331f1f674bd5]]. The source says he has been in prison since March 2025 on charges the correspondent describes as trumped up, making his treatment part of the political context around [[RecepTayyipErdogan]]'s electoral and succession strategy.

[[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]] makes Imamoglu more central to the election field. The episode describes him as the candidate best placed to challenge Erdogan and says he now faces roughly 2,300 years in prison, turning his case into a core example of [[JudicializedOppositionSuppression]].
