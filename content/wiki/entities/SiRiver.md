# 泗水 / Si River

泗水 / Si River enters the wiki first through the [[Xuzhou|彭城 / 徐州]] and [[Jiuding|九鼎]] branch in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-122-3-qinshihuang-jing-he-shenxian-si-qilai-le-ljnw5estg6dvgfllm3kfcq6ulhxu|Qinji 122-3]]. In that source, [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] searches the river near Pengcheng for a lost ding, making the Si River part of a failed imperial attempt to recover a Zhou legitimacy symbol.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-151-huanyuan-pengcheng-zhizhan-xiangyu-zai-fengshen-2-lqy1uu29xbfipo9e0kq6obkl3oay|Hanji 151 part 2]] gives the same regional water system a battle role. After [[XiangYu|项羽]] breaks [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s coalition at the [[PengchengBattle|彭城之战]], the source describes Han troops being pressed around the north banks of Gu water and Si River before the pursuit continues toward [[SuiRiver|睢水]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-971-luanshi-miyun-shui-sha-le-caocao-laodie-ljh3hdhssigmv78bueo-hprfqwku|Hanji 971]] gives the river a late-Han atrocity marker. In the episode's citation of [[HouHanshu|《后汉书》]], [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s [[XuzhouMassacreLateHan|徐州屠杀]] kills men and women at the Si River until the water is said not to flow, making the same Pengcheng-region river system a site of civilian massacre rather than only Qin ritual search or Chu-Han pursuit geography.

The page therefore connects Pengcheng's sacred-political geography to its battlefield geography. Around the same city, water first marks unrecovered legitimacy under Qin and later marks the terrain through which Chu cavalry turns Han retreat into rout.
