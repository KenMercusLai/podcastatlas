# 华阴 / Huayin (late Han)

华阴 / Huayin (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-989-ningwei-taipingquan-buzuo-luanshi-ren-lkoycygmjnltuvpq-6wjv-ls61s6|Hanji 989]] as the corridor point where [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] arrives after escaping fire and attempted westward coercion. The episode describes Huayin as north of Mount Hua, south of the Yellow River corridor, east toward Tongguan, west toward [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]], and administratively tied to Hongnong in the Eastern Han setting.

The source's Huayin is not just a map note. Its strategic position makes [[DuanWeiLateHan|段煨]]'s supplies politically charged: food, clothing, vehicles, and a defensible camp can preserve the imperial party, but they also look like route control to [[YangDingLateHan|杨定]], [[DongChengLateHan|董承]], and their allies. Huayin therefore becomes a concrete stage for [[ProtectorCaptorAmbiguity|护驾与挟持模糊性]] and [[PoliticalSurvivalFirst|安全第一政治生存]].

Hanji 990 continues the same setting by turning the suspicion around Duan Wei into open attack. Huayin's value lies in the combination of road access, supplies, and proximity to the emperor's body: whoever can secure the site can help the court, but can also be accused of intending to capture it.
