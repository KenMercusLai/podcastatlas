## Overview
郑诗亮 / Zheng Shiliang is the episode 504 guest and a former editor of [[LinXingzhi|林行止]]'s columns.

## Current Profile
Zheng provides an editor's retrospective on Lin and the [[HongKongEconomicJournal|《信报》]], supplementing his own working experience with a preparatory conversation with Lu Hao, who had known Lin for more than two decades. His role gives the episode access to editorial practice and relationship history while also defining its limits as a close, sympathetic recollection.

## Key Characteristics
- Brings direct column-editing experience to the discussion.
- Connects biography with newspaper practice and Hong Kong media history.
- Uses an additional long-term acquaintance as an oral-history input.
- Offers a sympathetic rather than fully adversarial evaluation.
