# City Storage Systems / CloudKitchens

## Overview
City Storage Systems is the company framework [[TravisKalanick|Travis Kalanick]] uses in the source for digitized physical infrastructure, with CloudKitchens described as its U.S. food-business identity.

## Current Profile
The episode presents City Storage Systems as a vertically integrated attempt to treat manufacturing, real estate, and logistics as a physical analogue of compute, storage, and networking. Its first “computer” is prepared food: kitchens manufacture meals, physical sites hold capacity, and delivery moves output. The source says related operations used local names in different countries and stayed unusually quiet while scaling, but it does not provide audited operating, customer, unit-economic, or ownership data.

## Key Characteristics
- Post-[[Uber]] operating platform associated with Kalanick.
- Uses manufacturing, real estate, and logistics as one integrated physical system.
- Applies the system first to prepared-food production and delivery.
- Operated under different local brands, including CloudKitchens in the United States and Kitchen Valley in Korea.
- Pursues a cost goal of bringing prepared and delivered meals closer to grocery-store economics.
