## Overview
魏宇珍 / Wei Yuzhen is presented as a physician in the neuroinfection and immunology department at [[BeijingTiantanHospital|北京天坛医院]] and a clinical guest in a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode about [[MyastheniaGravis|重症肌无力]].

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents Wei as a clinician emphasizing risk screening before glucocorticoid treatment, protective measures during treatment, individualized drug choice, and functional monitoring. Wei connects symptoms to an efficient clinical timeline—what changed, when it changed, what treatment was used, and what happened next—and keeps new targeted medicines within indication, access, response, and evolving-use boundaries.

## Key Characteristics
- Emphasizes infection, bone, pregnancy, gastrointestinal, and related risk assessment before glucocorticoid treatment.
- Connects treatment initiation to protective and monitoring measures rather than discussing efficacy alone.
- Structures patient histories around symptoms, timing, treatment exposure, and response.
- Treats targeted medicines and dosing patterns as individualized, evolving clinical choices.
- Uses persistent worsening and bulbar, respiratory, or neck weakness as reasons for timely escalation.
