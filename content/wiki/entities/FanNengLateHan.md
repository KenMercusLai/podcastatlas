# 樊能 / Fan Neng (late Han)

樊能 / Fan Neng (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] as one of [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]]'s defensive officers after Liu Yao pushes [[WuJingLateHan|吴景]] and [[SunBenLateHan|孙贲]] away from [[QuALateHan|曲阿]]. Liu Yao sends Fan Neng and [[YuMiLateHan|于糜]] to guard [[HengjiangLateHan|横江]] against [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s side.

Fan Neng's source role is positional rather than biographical. He marks the first defensive line Liu Yao builds once the Yangzhou governorship becomes a real territorial contest.
