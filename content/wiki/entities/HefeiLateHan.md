# 合肥 / Hefei (late Han)

## Overview
Late-Han Hefei is the broken city that [[LiuFuLateHan|Liu Fu]] turns into a provincial office and Jiang-Huai frontier base, then the strongpoint preserved through infrastructure, deceptive relief, standing garrison, and the asymmetric defense of 215 CE.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1051 presents Hefei as a weak starting point rather than an inherited stronghold. Liu Fu arrives with court appointment but little direct force, establishes the [[YangzhouLateHan|Yangzhou]] administrative seat there, and then uses the city as a platform for reassurance, population return, livelihood repair, schooling, walls, and defensive stores. Its importance grows because governance and logistics turn a damaged empty city into a Jiang-Huai strategic base.

Hanji 1112 shows that accumulated capacity under active pressure. A long siege by [[SunQuan|孙权]], repeated rain, damaged walls, and an epidemic-obstructed relief force leave the city exposed until [[JiangJiLateHan|蒋济]] makes a fictitious forty-thousand reinforcement claim credible through apparently intercepted messages. Sun Quan withdraws; Cao Cao later reaches the city, repairs Shaobei, promotes tuntian, and stations [[ZhangLiao|张辽]], [[LiDian|李典]], and Yue Jin there with a reported seven thousand troops.

Hanji 1154 shows the later garrison system operating during the 215 [[HefeiBattle215|合肥之战]]. With Cao Cao far away and Sun Quan's larger army arriving, sealed instructions divide the defenders into an attack arm under Zhang Liao and Li Dian and a city-defense role under [[LeJinLateHan|乐进]]. The initial assault weakens Wu morale and strengthens the garrison's confidence; the walls then absorb the continuing siege until failed attacks, supply pressure, and epidemic contribute to withdrawal.

## Key Characteristics
- Begins as a nearly empty and damaged city, not as a secure capital or prepared fortress.
- Gains political meaning when Liu Fu locates the Yangzhou office there and makes the imperial appointment visible.
- Accumulates capacity through local incorporation, water works, agriculture, crafts, education, walls, and defensive stores.
- Survives a prolonged siege through information effects when immediate reinforcement is unavailable.
- Becomes a durable Cao-Sun Jiang-Huai frontier base supported by logistics and a named standing garrison.
- Converts standing personnel and advance contingency design into a layered 215 defense: early shock, fortress holding, and withdrawal pursuit.

It is not a full urban or campaign history.
