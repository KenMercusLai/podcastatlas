# Greatness Wins

Greatness Wins is [[ChrisRiccobono]]'s sportswear brand discussed in [[advice-line-with-chris-riccobono-of-untuckit-c087247f-1f85-4c15-b4aa-4ffea0368c4c]]. The source says the company moved toward wholesale earlier than expected, especially through green-grass golf shops.

Riccobono treats golf-shop placement as both revenue and discovery: a customer may first encounter a shirt in a pro shop, then later buy other products online. In the wiki, the case makes [[WholesaleAsMarketing]] concrete and connects sports-retail placement to [[SubcultureLedMarketing]], because golf shops are not generic apparel racks; they put the product in a real use context.
