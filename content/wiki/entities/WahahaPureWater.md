# 娃哈哈纯净水 / Wahaha Pure Water

## Overview
娃哈哈纯净水 is the bottled-water product used in the 大小电波 source to mark [[WahahaGroup|娃哈哈]]'s full transition into the beverage market.

## Current Profile
The source presents the product as a 1996 launch that paired mass pricing with a simple purification claim and celebrity advertising. Its strategic role was not only selling water; it helped Wahaha move from children's nutrition and adjacent foods into high-frequency bottled beverages.

## Key Characteristics
- High-frequency bottled-water product launched during Wahaha's 1996 strategic turn.
- Used a purification-quality claim and lower price against imported-water perceptions.
- Relied on celebrity endorsement and national distribution to become a core revenue line.

## Evidence
- Strategic marker: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] treats pure water as the product that signaled Wahaha's shift from health-food roots to beverage-company identity.
- Price and message: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] says the product emphasized "27-layer purification" and a price below imported-water alternatives.
- Marketing and scale: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] connects the product to Jinggangshan and Wang Leehom endorsements and gives source-scoped 2023 sales context.

## Qualifications
The source is a podcast account of business history; it does not evaluate water quality independently. Celebrity timing, sales amounts, and market-share implications should remain tied to the episode.
