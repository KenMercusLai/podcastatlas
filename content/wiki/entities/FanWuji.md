# 樊无忌 / Fan Wuji

樊无忌 / Fan Wuji enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-2-yanguo-cisha-qinshihuang-weihe-hui-xuan-jingke-lsewjrmgup1yanf8ipn-i0ykqwjx]] as a [[QinState|秦国]] fugitive who flees to [[YanState|燕国]] and is received by [[YanTaiziDan|燕太子丹]]. The source-scoped name here follows the transcript's 樊无忌 rendering.

His presence changes Yan's risk calculus. [[JuWu|居武]] argues that Zhao and Wei are already damaged and afraid of Qin, so Yan's sheltering of a Qin fugitive is especially dangerous; he advises sending Fan Wuji north into [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] territory. Taizi Dan refuses by invoking the duty to protect someone who has entrusted his life to him.

Fan Wuji then becomes the key material in [[JingKe|荆轲]]'s proposed access plan. Jing Ke tells him that his head can both remove danger from Yan and help him take revenge on [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]], because presenting the fugitive's head would help Jing Ke approach the Qin king.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-3-jingke-ciqin-weihe-jiujiu-bu-xingdong-zai-deng-shui-liihe-kgwigu3bggxmyofgvotged]] completes that branch. After [[YanTaiziDan|燕太子丹]] refuses to kill or hand over Fan Wuji directly, Jing Ke visits Fan privately and frames suicide as revenge, protection for Yan, and the necessary first step toward reaching Ying Zheng. Fan Wuji accepts this logic and cuts his own throat; Dan arrives too late and can only mourn before placing the head in a box for the mission.

This makes Fan Wuji a morally central part of the assassination plan rather than a disposable plot device. His protected status is exactly what made Dan's honor legible in 114-2, but his death becomes the strongest [[AssassinationAccessCredential|assassination access credential]] in 114-3.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-115-1-jingke-ciqin-sihu-you-dianer-caoshuai-le-lgz1adsn1t8jjiaobmtfedpr-ixn]] moves Fan Wuji's head from preparation into performance. [[JingKe|荆轲]] and [[QinWuyang|秦武阳]] bring it to [[Xianyang|咸阳]], and [[MengJia|蒙嘉]] helps translate it into proof that [[YanState|燕国]] is serious about submitting to Qin. The head's meaning shifts again: it is no longer only Fan's revenge sacrifice, but one half of a surrender credential paired with the [[DuKangMap|督亢地图]].
