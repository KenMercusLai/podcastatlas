# 徐金金 / Xu Jinjin

## Overview
Xu Jinjin is a writer, artist, translator, and former student of [[AnneCarson|Anne Carson]] who appears in [[ep289-zai-anni-kasen-de-maxituan-ketang-chaidiao-wenxue-de-tiaotiaokuangkuang-lhqupidadktnqhxizmxzja9hkjhp|EP289]].

## Current Profile
Xu describes Carson's work as an early demonstration that scholarship, ancient material, poetry, and formal experiment could coexist. In the classroom, that permission extended into performance, image, dance, radio, and collaborative projects. Xu frames her own cross-field position between Chinese and English writing and visual art not only as marginality but as freedom from any one circle's expectations.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects classical learning with experimental contemporary writing.
- Works across literary and visual-art forms rather than accepting one medium as a complete identity.
- Treats creative marginality as room for autonomy, slower development, and unexpected collaboration.
- Distinguishes intimate recollection from claims to explain another artist's work.
