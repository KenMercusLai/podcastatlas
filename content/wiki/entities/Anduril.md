# Anduril

## Overview

Anduril is a defense technology company in the wiki's startup-procurement, autonomous-systems, private-market valuation, and reindustrialization branches.

## Current Profile

Anduril first appears as a newer defense contractor gaining ground in military procurement and as [[ArcherAviation|Archer Aviation]]'s partner on [[ProjectNix|Project Nix]], an autonomous collaborative attack helicopter drone. The private-markets source then places Anduril beside [[SpaceX]], [[Anthropic]], and [[OpenAI]] as a late-stage private company attracting secondary-market demand.

The Palantir-Anduril All-In episode deepens the company profile from "startup winning contracts" into a possible new defense prime. [[TreyStephens|Trey Stephens]] says Anduril invests private R&D before government demand is fully specified, builds software-defined and hardware-enabled products, and is constructing [[ArsenalOne|Arsenal One]] as modular production capacity. The company's wiki role is therefore both product and factory: autonomous systems, private capital, defense contracts, and [[FactoryBasedDeterrence]] all meet in one case.

## Key Characteristics

- Newer defense contractor used as evidence that startups can win major military work.
- Partner with [[ArcherAviation|Archer Aviation]] on [[ProjectNix|Project Nix]], a defense-aviation autonomy branch.
- Late-stage private-market demand example in the secondaries and AI IPO valuation discussion.
- [[PrivateCapitalDefensePrime]] case: pre-funds products and production capacity before full government specification.
- Builder of [[ArsenalOne|Arsenal One]], a modular factory campus used to support [[FactoryBasedDeterrence]].
- Peer and successor-case to [[Palantir]] in the wiki's defense-technology procurement lineage.
