# Bobbi Brown

Bobbi Brown is the beauty founder and [[HowIBuiltThis]] Advice Line guest in [[advice-line-with-bobbi-brown-of-jones-road-beauty-september-2025-427590c2-11bc-43fa-9c92-d1eccf05842a]]. The episode presents her through two company chapters: [[BobbiBrownCosmetics]], where she helped popularize a more understated natural makeup look, and [[JonesRoadBeauty]], which she launched in 2020 after a 25-year non-compete expired.

Brown's advice emphasizes scrappy testing, product quality, founder story, and growth restraint. She tells [[CabbageLabs]] to convert free full-size tubes into cheaper samples, tells [[AbbyRose]] to make live selling less dependent on one founder, and tells [[TannenOralCare]] to widen from a coffee-rinse origin story into a broader oral-care promise.
