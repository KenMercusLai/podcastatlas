## Overview

军须靡 / Junxumi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-370-2-yeye-ba-jiaoqi-jiagei-qinsunzi-nainai-yiye-bian-xifuer-lopw_h3yzk6qtcpksqdm9l2egrvp|Hanji 370-2]] as the grandson and successor of [[LiejiaomiWusun|猎骄靡]]. Under Wusun custom and with [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s strategic approval, he marries his grandfather's Han wife [[LiuXijunPrincessWesternHan|刘细君]], later succeeds as Wusun ruler, and has a daughter, Shaofu, with Xijun. The transcript form “君须弥” is normalized to 军须靡.

## Current Profile

Junxumi's current profile is defined by succession continuity rather than an independent policy program. Liejiaomi selects him as heir and asks Xijun to marry him. Xijun objects, but the Han court orders compliance because it values [[Wusun|乌孙]] cooperation against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]]. The marriage therefore makes Junxumi the hinge between local succession custom and Han alliance strategy.

After Liejiaomi's death, Junxumi succeeds and the episode says the marriage becomes an ordinary conjugal relationship in addition to its diplomatic function. Xijun gives birth to Shaofu. When Xijun dies, Han sends [[XieyouPrincessWesternHan|解忧公主]] to marry Junxumi, extending the same alliance through a second Han princess.

## Key Characteristics

- Grandson and designated successor of Liejiaomi.
- Recipient of Xijun through Wusun succession and remarriage custom.
- Benefits from Han Wudi's decision to prioritize alliance continuity over Xijun's objection.
- Succeeds to the Wusun rulership after Liejiaomi.
- Father of Shaofu with Xijun in the episode's account.
- Later husband of Xieyou, making him a bridge between the two Han princess missions.

## Qualifications

The current page is bounded to one compact episode summary. It does not establish Junxumi's age, personal view of either marriage, full reign, complete children, or later succession settlement. The episode alternates “昆末 / 昆弥” as rulership language; this page uses Junxumi's received personal name and does not create separate identities from those titles.
