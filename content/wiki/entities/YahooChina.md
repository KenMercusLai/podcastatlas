# Yahoo China / 雅虎中国

## Overview
Yahoo China / 雅虎中国 is the China-market Yahoo operation discussed in [[no-216-shi-bu-sha-yi-ren-hongyi-dapao-zhouhongyi-de-sanshinian-quanmian-zhanzhengshi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-27-1008693437]] after [[Yahoo]] acquired [[3721]] and made [[ZhouHongyi|周鸿祎]] president of the local business in 2004.

## Current Profile
The source presents Yahoo China as a case where local execution improved mail, search, revenue, and profit but remained constrained by a slower global organization. It is therefore both a peak in Zhou's operating reputation and a transition point toward his post-Yahoo investment period and [[Qihoo360|Qihoo 360]] return.

## Key Characteristics
- Post-acquisition operating vehicle created when [[Yahoo]] bought [[3721]].
- Local China internet business that the episode says grew free email, search traffic, revenue, and profit under Zhou.
- Large-company constraint case where headquarters communication, resource limits, cultural mismatch, and management conflict shaped the exit.
- Reputational bridge because Yahoo Assistant inherited the 3721 lineage and later appeared on 360's disliked-software list.

## Evidence
- Acquisition and appointment: [[no-216-shi-bu-sha-yi-ren-hongyi-dapao-zhouhongyi-de-sanshinian-quanmian-zhanzhengshi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-27-1008693437]] says Yahoo offered $120 million for 3721 and Zhou became Yahoo China president on 22 March 2004.
- Operating performance: [[no-216-shi-bu-sha-yi-ren-hongyi-dapao-zhouhongyi-de-sanshinian-quanmian-zhanzhengshi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-27-1008693437]] says Yahoo China's free email reached second place nationally, search volume approached [[Baidu]], annual revenue reached about $40 million, and profit reached about $10 million.
- Exit causes: [[no-216-shi-bu-sha-yi-ren-hongyi-dapao-zhouhongyi-de-sanshinian-quanmian-zhanzhengshi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-27-1008693437]] attributes Zhou's August 2005 departure to slow headquarters communication, limited resources, cultural mismatch, and management conflict.
- Later reputation loop: [[no-216-shi-bu-sha-yi-ren-hongyi-dapao-zhouhongyi-de-sanshinian-quanmian-zhanzhengshi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-27-1008693437]] says Yahoo Assistant, formerly 3721, later appeared in 360's negative software list during the anti-rogue-software period.

## Qualifications
The performance figures and internal-culture explanation are episode-attributed. This page does not attempt a complete independent history of Yahoo China, Alibaba's later Yahoo China deal, or every local Yahoo product.
