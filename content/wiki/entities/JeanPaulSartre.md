# Jean-Paul Sartre / 萨特

## Overview
Jean-Paul Sartre / 萨特 is held in the wiki as both a major [[Existentialism|existentialist]] writer-philosopher and a figure whose public authority is repeatedly qualified by source context. His role now spans author myth, politics, intimate-life contradictions, and the focused interpretation of [[NoExit|No Exit / 《禁闭》]] as a drama of judgment and damaged relations.

## Current Profile
[[135-xianliao-weida-zuojiamen-de-bagua-di-san-dan-823746670]] presents Sartre as an important philosopher whose public hero image is sharply qualified. The source acknowledges his stature while contrasting it with wartime passivity, collaboration-era career progress, left politics, private relationships, drug use, poor health, and late-life reconsideration of despair and atheism. It links Sartre to [[BeingAndNothingness|Being and Nothingness / 《存在与虚无》]] and [[NoExit|No Exit / 《禁闭》]] as works that succeeded during occupation-era Paris.

Sartre also extends [[FreeLovePracticeGap]] through his long open relationship with [[SimoneDeBeauvoir|Simone de Beauvoir / 西蒙娜·德·波伏娃]] and the episode's later account of his secret support for Arlette Elkaim. Politically, the source criticizes his alliance with Communism, sympathy for revolutionary figures such as [[FidelCastro|Fidel Castro]], and silence or pressure around testimony about abuses in the [[SovietUnion]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-965-bangguo-bieren-de-mang-weishenme-yao-shao-ti-ltjd-cv59hnm-n8ar9jxdogp9q0q|Hanji 965]] briefly invokes Sartre's "他人即地狱" line as an interpretive analogy for why being seen by others can feel like judgment or pressure. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-395-2-sanren-beihou-de-renxing-gushi-lnxghfd6esj7jbir-m6qflth2ckp|Hanji 395-2]] gives that line a fuller dramatic explanation: Sartre's hell is a damaged social room where people become dependent on, and trapped by, one another's judgment.

## Key Characteristics
- Major philosopher-writer associated with [[Existentialism|existentialism]] and postwar meaning-making.
- Public-intellectual figure whose moral and political authority is source-scopely contested.
- Author of [[NoExit|No Exit / 《禁闭》]], now used in the wiki to ground [[OtherAsHell|他人即地狱]].
- Case for separating philosophical influence from author sanctification.
- Figure whose relationship practice and political commitments extend the wiki's doctrine-practice caution pages.

## Evidence
- Philosophical-literary importance: [[135-xianliao-weida-zuojiamen-de-bagua-di-san-dan-823746670]] names [[BeingAndNothingness|《存在与虚无》]] and [[NoExit|《禁闭》]] as major Sartre works.
- Author myth qualification: [[135-xianliao-weida-zuojiamen-de-bagua-di-san-dan-823746670]] treats Sartre's wartime conduct, left politics, intimate life, and late-life comments as complications to hero worship.
- Relational judgment line: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-965-bangguo-bieren-de-mang-weishenme-yao-shao-ti-ltjd-cv59hnm-n8ar9jxdogp9q0q]] uses Sartre as a brief analogy for why being seen by others can feel like pressure.
- Dramatic explanation of the line: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-395-2-sanren-beihou-de-renxing-gushi-lnxghfd6esj7jbir-m6qflth2ckp]] explains "他人就是地狱" through [[NoExit|《禁闭》]] as a bad-relation and judgment-dependence problem.

## Qualifications
The wiki stores negative biographical and political claims as source-scoped episode material, not as a final biography of Sartre. The Hanji 395-2 source is a short explanatory detour rather than a complete account of Sartre's philosophy.
