# 程普 / Cheng Pu (late Han)

## Overview
程普 / Cheng Pu is a Jiangdong commander whom the current sources place in the initial anti-Cao deployment and the post-[[RedCliffsBattle|赤壁之战]] contest around [[JianglingLateHan|江陵]].

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1102-lusu-yijuhua-chuoxing-sunquan-toujiang-caocao-de-houguo-you-duo-kepa-lh0fwhkq9g9imfm1esblolh5gzjz|Hanji 1102]] names Cheng Pu with [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] when [[SunQuan|孙权]] turns the resistance decision into a field deployment. The episode gives them an initial force, ships, provisions, and weapons, while [[LuSu|鲁肃]] serves in a planning role and Sun Quan retains responsibility for reinforcement.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1108-ganning-qixi-yiling-zhanshu-jing-he-yalishanda-zhuangche-lia4azq39ja4pt6yfpjggjl-g3vc|Hanji 1108]] places Cheng Pu and Zhou Yu opposite [[CaoRen|曹仁]] and [[XuHuangCaoGeneral|徐晃]] around Jiangling after Red Cliffs. The bounded evidence therefore supports a continuity role from coalition mobilization into the corridor campaign, but not a complete biography or independent reconstruction of his exact command authority.

## Key Characteristics
- Senior field commander in Sun Quan's anti-Cao deployment.
- Paired with Zhou Yu in both mobilization and the Jiangling campaign setting.
- Part of a role-divided command structure linking political decision, planning, field command, and reinforcement.

## Evidence
- Initial deployment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1102-lusu-yijuhua-chuoxing-sunquan-toujiang-caocao-de-houguo-you-duo-kepa-lh0fwhkq9g9imfm1esblolh5gzjz|Hanji 1102]] names Cheng Pu and Zhou Yu as the commanders sent forward after Sun Quan rejects surrender.
- Campaign continuity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1108-ganning-qixi-yiling-zhanshu-jing-he-yalishanda-zhuangche-lia4azq39ja4pt6yfpjggjl-g3vc|Hanji 1108]] places the same pair against Cao Ren and Xu Huang in the Jiangling contest.

## Qualifications
The two source notes mention Cheng Pu mainly through command placement. They do not establish a full career, detailed battlefield actions, or a precise hierarchy between Cheng Pu and Zhou Yu. The translated “left/right commander” language and exact force allocation remain bounded to Hanji 1102's popular-history presentation.

## What Changed

- Distinguished documented command placement from unsupported claims about detailed battlefield conduct.
