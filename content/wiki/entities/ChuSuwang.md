# 楚肃王 / King Su of Chu

楚肃王 / King Su of Chu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl|Zhouji 09]] as the newly enthroned ruler of [[ChuState|楚国]] during the early Warring States melee. The source says Zhao seeks Chu's help after being pressured by Wei and Qi, but Chu Su Wang does not simply rescue Zhao; he attacks [[WeiState|魏国]]'s border instead.

The episode makes him a case of opportunistic alliance behavior. In the [[EarlyWarringStatesInterstateWar|interstate-war]] frame, help and self-interest are not separable: Chu's move relieves pressure on Zhao while also exploiting Wei's vulnerability.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-2-lmnqfjmydygqnfec6xhzpixralvi]] records Chu Su Wang's death. Because he has no son, his younger brother 熊良夫 succeeds as [[ChuXuanwang|楚宣王]], making this a brother-succession notice rather than a direct father-son transfer.
