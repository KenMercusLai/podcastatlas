# 边鸿 / Bian Hong (late Han)

## Overview
边鸿 / Bian Hong is the intimate killer of [[SunYiLateHan|孙翊]] in the Hanji 1067 Danyang episode. The source transcript writes the name as 边红; the wiki normalizes the clear event to Bian Hong / 边鸿.

## Current Profile
Bian Hong is presented as the inside actor bought off by [[GuiLanLateHan|妫览]] and [[DaiYuanLateHan|戴员]]. During Sun Yi's banquet for county officials, Bian Hong attacks from behind as Sun Yi rises to send guests off. Gui Lan and Dai Yuan then try to contain responsibility by claiming Bian Hong killed because he was mad.

## Key Characteristics
- Has enough proximity to Sun Yi to serve as an intimate assassin.
- Makes the murder possible through access rather than open battle.
- Is used by Gui Lan and Dai Yuan as the visible killer behind a deeper conspiracy.
- Becomes the object of a false madness explanation meant to conceal political motive.
