# Maxine Waters

Maxine Waters appears in [[tsr-s5-ronconway-v5-tsr-s5-ronconway-v5]] as one of the congressional figures [[RonConway]] identifies as important during the [[SiliconValleyBank]] weekend. Conway says the group understood the [[FederalDepositInsuranceCorporation|FDIC]] as answerable to Congress and treated Waters, alongside [[SherrodBrown]], as someone whose view could matter to the guarantee decision.

The source does not give Waters an extended scene. Her wiki role is therefore as part of the congressional oversight and influence layer in [[DepositGuaranteeCrisisResponse]], not as a fully developed episode subject.
