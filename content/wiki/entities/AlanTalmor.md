# Alan Talmor

## Overview

Alan Talmor is the founder and CEO of [[MosaicAI]], previously the founder of [[AskAIMosaic|Ask AI]], and a former co-founder of Bluetail who later served as chief data scientist for Salesforce Data.com and pursued AI and natural-language-processing research.

## Current Profile

The current source presents Talmor as an enterprise AI founder who combines early technical exposure with customer-led market selection. His main operating pattern is to seek enterprise evidence before fundraising, disclose material setbacks directly, sell the first customers himself, and narrow a broad product when platform progress changes the competitive boundary.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses enterprise conversations and design partners to identify costly, repeated problems before scaling a product.
- Treats model-provider progress as a reason to re-evaluate application scope rather than defend a generic category by default.
- Frames enterprise selling around measurable operating outcomes and diagnostic evidence.
- Favors transparent investor communication even when bad news may weaken a financing process.
- Supports automation while retaining human approval and longer implementation work where deployment requires it.

## Evidence

Customer and product judgment:
- [[chatgpt-should-have-killed-his-ai-saas-instead-hes-nearing-10m]] says Talmor began enterprise conversations before fundraising, built with Yotpo, validated beyond one partner, and later narrowed Ask AI into technical-support automation.

- [[chatgpt-should-have-killed-his-ai-saas-instead-hes-nearing-10m]] attributes the first roughly 25 customers primarily to Talmor's own outreach and describes his disclosure of a prospective co-founder's departure during the seed round.
