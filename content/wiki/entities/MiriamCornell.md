## Overview

Miriam Cornell / 米里亚姆·康奈尔 is [[RolandBaines|Roland Baines / 罗兰·贝恩斯]]'s adult piano teacher in [[LessonsNovel|《钢琴课》 / Lessons]]. The episode classifies her sexual relationship with fourteen-year-old Roland as abuse of a minor despite its remembered music, pleasure, and emotional intensity.

## Current Profile

Miriam occupies an unequal teacher-student relationship in which musical guidance, bodily proximity, secrecy, and sexual control become entangled. Roland initially treats the experience as distinction and later names its harm; their late confrontation reverses some immediate leverage but does not restore what the relationship altered.

## Key Characteristics

- Holds adult, teacher, and musical authority over an adolescent student.
- Uses a relationship capable of producing pleasure and beauty without making it ethically equal.
- Becomes a lasting reference point for Roland's sexuality, attachment, music, and self-understanding.
- Is confronted years later through documentary evidence and the prospect of police scrutiny.
- Does not provide Roland with a clean confession, explanation, or reparative ending.
