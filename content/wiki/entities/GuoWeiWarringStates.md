# 郭隗 / Guo Wei (Warring States)

郭隗 / Guo Wei appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2]] as the adviser [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]] consults after returning to a damaged [[YanState|燕国]]. This page uses `GuoWeiWarringStates.md` to avoid conflating him with [[GuoWei|郭威]], the Later Zhou founder.

Guo Wei's role is to turn recruitment into credible signaling. Through the "千金买骨" story, he tells Yan Zhaowang that if the ruler visibly honors him first, people more capable than Guo Wei will believe Yan is serious and come from afar.

The episode treats the point as practical statecraft rather than flattery. Talent attraction needs a public proof of treatment: Yan's promise becomes believable because the ruler pays cost, lowers status, and lets the first adviser become evidence.
