# 中山国 / Zhongshan State

中山国 / Zhongshan State appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl|Zhouji 09]] as a state that had been conquered by [[WeiState|魏国]] under Wei Wenhou but revives during the later melee. The source says Wei Wuhou's brother [[WeiZhi|魏挚]] had been guarding the area, which was a detached territory separated from Wei proper by [[ZhaoState|赵国]].

The episode uses Zhongshan to show how frontier control can reverse quickly when the main powers are distracted. After Di forces drive out Wei Zhi, Zhongshan revives but still continues relations with Wei and aligns with Wei against Zhao, making it a compact case of [[EarlyWarringStatesInterstateWar|early Warring States]] pragmatism.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-4-lhjz-m12pedkfszxyzgqtjnpsn3p]] gives Zhongshan an earlier succession-politics role through [[ZizhiTongjianBu|《资治通鉴补》]]. The episode says [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] sends the future [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]] there, and the host reads the assignment as suspicious because Zhongshan is physically detached from Wei by [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. In that reading, Zhongshan is not only a frontier holding but also a place where a potential heir can be removed from court.
