# XTX Markets

## Overview

XTX Markets is a London quantitative trading and market-making firm used by the source as its main example of compute-intensive, neural-network-led finance.

## Current Profile

The episode describes XTX as founded in 2015, initially active in foreign exchange, deliberately small in headcount, and unusually willing to invest in GPUs, data centers, automation, and neural-network experimentation. Its significance in the source is organizational: high researcher leverage and heavy infrastructure are presented as complements, not substitutes, and no proprietary strategy is disclosed.

## Key Characteristics

- Quantitative trading and market-making firm with roots in foreign exchange.
- Small-team, high-automation operating model in the source's account.
- Early user of neural networks for investment research.
- Heavy investor in compute and automated infrastructure.
- Example of finance and frontier-AI laboratories competing for similar talent and resources.
