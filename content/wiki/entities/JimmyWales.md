# Jimmy Wales

## Overview
Jimmy Wales is the founder interviewed about the transition from [[Nupedia]]'s expert-led encyclopedia process to [[Wikipedia]]'s open collaborative model and the later nonprofit institution built to sustain it.

## Current Profile
The source presents Wales as a mathematically trained former options trader who used [[Bomis]] to experiment with community-organized information before launching Nupedia and Wikipedia. His central operating judgment was that contribution had to become easier, faster, and more enjoyable without abandoning correction, neutrality, or reliable sourcing. As Wikipedia grew, his role shifted from initiating the experiment to persuading and supporting volunteers he could not command, then protecting the mission through nonprofit ownership and small-donor funding.

## Key Characteristics
- Combined mathematics, finance, programming, and early internet experimentation before building online knowledge projects.
- Treated Nupedia's slow output and his own discomfort with its review process as evidence that the contribution architecture needed redesign.
- Accepted useful partial contributions rather than requiring every participant to produce a finished expert article.
- Relied on persuasion, patience, and community legitimacy because volunteers could not be directed like employees.
- Rejected advertising as a structural incentive that could redirect editorial priorities.
- Described Wikipedia as a starting point for inquiry whose citations and discussion pages should be inspected.

## Evidence
- Path to online collaboration - [[vmp5702291377-vmp5702291377]] traces Wales from mathematics and options trading through self-taught programming and Bomis experiments.
- Contribution redesign - [[vmp5702291377-vmp5702291377]] contrasts Nupedia's seven-stage review with Wikipedia's immediate small edits and rapid early output.
- Community leadership - [[vmp5702291377-vmp5702291377]] has Wales describe distributed volunteer repair, policy formation, and leadership through reason rather than command.
- Mission protection - [[vmp5702291377-vmp5702291377]] links nonprofit status, advertising refusal, and small donations to editorial independence and free access.

## Qualifications
This profile is based on Wales's retrospective account in a founder-interview format. It is not a complete independent history of Wikipedia's creation, and it gives limited attention to other founders, early contributors, staff, governance conflicts, or later institutional controversies.
