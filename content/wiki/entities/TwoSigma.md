# Two Sigma

[[e247-duihua-shengying-xai-infra-de-langman-sglang-kaiyuan-pingquan-yu-zhenhuanchuan-6c9d13b1-ac9a-4a7a-a35b-99bfb8374668]] mentions Two Sigma as [[ShengYing|盛颖]]'s brief post-[[ColumbiaUniversity|Columbia University]] workplace before her PhD path fully formed. The episode says she wanted to experience finance and a dense concentration of smart people, then later remembered Two Sigma as highly organized with stable infrastructure.

In this wiki branch, Two Sigma is a contrast case. Its organized finance-infrastructure environment differs from the open-source, high-uncertainty AI-infra path that later leads Sheng toward [[SGLang]], [[XAI|xAI]], and [[RadixARC|Redix ARK]].
