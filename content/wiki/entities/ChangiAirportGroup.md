# Changi Airport Group / 樟宜机场集团

## Overview
Changi Airport Group appears as a mature airport operator that has extended Singapore-hub expertise into foreign airport consulting, investment, expansion, and long-term operation.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Changi as a peer to [[IncheonInternationalAirportCorporation]]. Its overseas activity reportedly began with consulting and later moved into longer-duration participation, including expansion work at Rio de Janeiro's Galeão airport. The case supports the wider claim that airport-management experience can become a cross-border asset when local bidders lack the operating record required by concession tenders.
