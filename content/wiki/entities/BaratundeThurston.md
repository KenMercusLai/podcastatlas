# Baratunde Thurston

## Overview
Baratunde Thurston is the writer and producer in the Long Now conversation on AI and interdependence.

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Thurston evaluates AI through relationship, power, and habit rather than capability alone. He draws on [[HaudenosauneeConfederacy]] governance, colonial and racial histories, land stewardship, cognitive agency, and his own inconsistency around restorative practice to argue that technological integration should preserve selfhood, collective choice, and responsibility to others.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames AI as a relationship that already changes conduct whether or not machines are alive.
- Connects obedient-AI narratives to longer histories of ownership, extraction, slavery, and domination.
- Distinguishes capability from agency, including choice and control over one's representation.
- Uses Indigenous democracy and ecological belonging to challenge ownership-centered social stories.
- Treats care, accountability, and cognitive liberty as necessary responses to technological acceleration.
