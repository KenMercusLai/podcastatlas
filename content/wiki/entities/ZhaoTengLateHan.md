## Overview

赵腾 / Zhao Teng is a source-bounded late-Han remonstrant in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]]. His critical memorial angers [[EmperorAnOfHan|Emperor An]], and [[YangZhenLateHan|Yang Zhen]]'s failed attempt to save him becomes part of the later slander used against Yang.

## Current Profile

Hanji 800 introduces Zhao Teng through his memorial on court gains and losses. The source says Emperor An is enraged, has him arrested and examined in prison, and treats the memorial as deception against the ruler and grave disloyalty rather than as usable criticism.

Yang Zhen then tries to separate harsh speech from violent treason. He argues that Zhao Teng's language may be extreme but should not be judged like an armed crime, asking An to reduce the sentence and preserve Zhao's life. An refuses and has Zhao Teng executed, with the body exposed in the street.

Zhao Teng's case does not end with his own death. When Yang Zhen later obtains evidence that Fan Feng, Zhou Guang, and Xie Yun forged orders for private construction, the group invokes Zhao Teng's death to claim Yang is resentful and politically suspect. Zhao Teng is therefore a compact source case where direct remonstrance is punished and rescue speech becomes dangerous to the rescuer.

## Key Characteristics

- Memorial speaker whose criticism of court affairs is treated as criminal deception.
- Executed direct-remonstrance case under Emperor An.
- Object of Yang Zhen's failed clemency plea.
- Posthumous accusation resource used to frame Yang Zhen as resentful.
- Source-bounded figure whose biography is known only through the Hanji 800 crisis.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 800 and does not reconstruct Zhao Teng's office, family, prior career, exact memorial text, or the full prison procedure. The page treats the host's moral reading of Zhao Teng sympathetically but keeps the legal accusation and the historical outcome separate.
