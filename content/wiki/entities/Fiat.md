# Fiat

Fiat is the Italian automaker that became [[Ferrari]]'s strategic owner in [[ferrari-1]]. After the failed [[FordMotorCompany]] acquisition attempt and [[EnzoFerrari]]'s later illness, Fiat acquired 50% of Ferrari in 1969 with an agreement to acquire another 40% after Enzo's death, while [[PieroFerrari]] retained 10%.

The source frames Fiat as a continuity mechanism. It gave Ferrari industrial backing and succession stability, but it also set up the later [[FiatChryslerAutomobiles|FCA]] debt and spinout context where [[SergioMarchionne]] pushed Ferrari into public markets.
