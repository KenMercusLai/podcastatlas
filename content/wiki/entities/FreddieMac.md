# Freddie Mac

Freddie Mac appears in [[can-trump-make-buying-a-home-more-affordable]] as one of the government-backed mortgage institutions in the episode's mortgage-backed-securities discussion. The source treats it together with [[FannieMae]], not as a standalone institutional history.

Its relevance is the risk-bearing side of rate relief. If government-backed institutions buy more mortgage-backed securities, demand for those bonds can lower mortgage rates, but [[SusanWachter]] says taxpayers absorb additional interest-rate risk through that balance-sheet exposure.
