# 王朗 / Wang Lang (late Han)

王朗 / Wang Lang (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1005-sunce-zhuding-zaoshi-shi-xingge-jueding-mingyun-lpqnere2cwcrngggyezd-bsqahx4|Hanji 1005]] as the [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]] governor whom [[SunCe|孙策]] defeats before taking the commandery for himself. The episode does not develop Wang Lang's biography; his main function is to mark the local regime Sun Ce replaces.

His defeated office still matters because Sun Ce retains [[YuFan|虞翻]], Wang Lang's merit officer. That retention lets the episode show Sun Ce's capacity to absorb local personnel before turning to the different problem Yu Fan identifies: the new ruler's personal-security habits do not match his expanded status.
