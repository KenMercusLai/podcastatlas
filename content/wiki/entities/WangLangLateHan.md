# 王朗 / Wang Lang (late Han)

王朗 / Wang Lang (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1004-caocao-yingfeng-hanxiandi-shi-shui-de-zhuyi-lrrga-flmx5ajnhu1c42x7kooe94|Hanji 1004]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1005-sunce-zhuding-zaoshi-shi-xingge-jueding-mingyun-lpqnere2cwcrngggyezd-bsqahx4|Hanji 1005]] as the [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]] governor whom [[SunCe|孙策]] defeats before taking the commandery for himself. Hanji 1004 gives the campaign detail: Wang Lang rejects [[YuFan|虞翻]]'s advice to avoid Sun Ce's edge, holds Guling, and is then outflanked after [[SunJingLateHan|孙静]] proposes a rear attack.

His defeated office still matters because Sun Ce later retains Yu Fan, Wang Lang's merit officer. That retention lets the episode sequence show Sun Ce's capacity to absorb local personnel before turning to the different problem Yu Fan identifies in Hanji 1005: the new ruler's personal-security habits do not match his expanded status.
