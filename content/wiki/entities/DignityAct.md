# Dignity Act

The Dignity Act appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-rahm-emanuel-trumps-foreign-policy-china-europes-decline-immigration-dsa-vs-democrats-42416310]] as the immigration compromise [[RahmEmanuel|Rahm Emanuel]] says he would pass even though he would not have written it exactly the same way. The source says Emanuel describes support from 23 Republicans and 23 Democrats.

In the wiki, the Dignity Act is a source-scoped example of [[PragmaticImmigrationCompromise]]. It links legalization, border control, and workplace enforcement rather than treating immigration politics as only restriction or only openness. It also qualifies [[MeritBasedImmigrationFilter]] by showing a bipartisan-compromise route beside high-skill selectivity and fiscal-contribution frames.
