# 李鸿伟

## Overview
李鸿伟 is the [[RayNeo|雷鸟创新 / RayNeo]] CEO interviewed by [[LiuFei|刘飞]] on [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] about AI glasses, AR/VR/MR, and smart glasses as a possible next computing platform.

## Current Profile
In the current source set, Li is a source-scoped practitioner voice for consumer AI glasses. He frames the category around real-world augmentation rather than phone replacement: the glasses should help users see, understand, translate, record, prompt, and interact with their surroundings when taking out a phone would add friction.

His strongest contribution is a product-and-systems account. He links RayNeo's X, V, and R/GT lines to staged category learning; explains [[TieredOnDeviceSensing]] as an answer to always-on camera power limits; treats [[WearableOpticalDisplayFriction]] as central to daily adoption; and argues that first-person AI differs from phone AI because glasses process continuous, sparse, wearer-perspective data.

## Key Characteristics
- He positions AI glasses as real-world augmentation devices, not simple phone replacements.
- He treats optical display, on-glasses AI, spatial computing, low-power chips, and product understanding as the decisive smart-glasses stack.
- He uses RayNeo's staged product lines to learn from display-heavy, capture-first, and portable-screen use cases before a unified product arrives.
- He is technically optimistic about AI glasses while acknowledging battery, display, comfort, commercial-interruption, and adoption friction.
- His market-timing claims remain source-scoped because the episode does not independently verify RayNeo's share, roadmap, or penetration forecasts.
