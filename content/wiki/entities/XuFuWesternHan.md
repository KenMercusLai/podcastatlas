## Overview

徐福 / Xu Fu enters the Western Han branch through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-424-huoguang-qushi-hou-huojia-shi-ruhe-fumie-de-ltisydawuqdhqpffdjv_xkwts90p|Hanji 424]] as the Maoling man who repeatedly warns [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] to restrain the Huo family before its arrogance becomes rebellion. This page is deliberately disambiguated from [[XuFu|徐福]] the Qin sea-voyage fangshi and [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负]] the physiognomist.

## Current Profile

The source gives Xu Fu a narrow but conceptually important role. Before the Huo collapse, he reportedly writes three times that excessive luxury and indulgence will make the Huo household arrogant, offend the ruler, and eventually move toward grave crime. After the Huo purge, Xuan rewards the men who exposed the conspiracy but initially overlooks the adviser who had urged prevention.

Xu Fu becomes memorable through the "曲突徙薪" analogy. A petitioner compares him to the guest who advised moving firewood and bending a chimney before a fire, while the household rewarded only those who fought the flames afterward. Xuan then grants Xu Fu silk and appoints him as a lang official.

## Key Characteristics

- Preventive adviser whose warnings focus on restraining the Huo family before rebellion emerges.
- Evidence for the political value of early risk diagnosis rather than post-crisis reward alone.
- Overlooked contributor because Xuan first rewards conspiracy informants but not the person who urged prevention.
- Source of the "曲突徙薪" court lesson in this episode.
- Disambiguated Western Han figure separate from the Qin Xu Fu and the physiognomist Xu Fu.
