# Gecko Robotics

## Overview
Gecko Robotics is a Pittsburgh robotics company that uses purpose-built robots, sensors, and AI to inspect ships and heavy infrastructure. The supplied sources connect it to naval readiness, energy assets, industrial data collection, and a longer-term path from diagnosis toward repair and manufacturing action.

## Current Profile
Gecko's near-term product is not a general humanoid worker. It deploys robots into valuable, hazardous, data-poor environments to measure metal and structural condition, then combines inspection and operating data to guide maintenance, asset-life, output, and turnaround decisions. This makes paid inspection both a customer service and a way to build a proprietary industrial-data base.

The longer-term roadmap extends from finding and monitoring defects toward supervised repair or production actions such as welding. That remains a roadmap rather than proof of general autonomous maintenance; human supervision, teleoperation, safety, security, and procurement performance remain central.

## Key Characteristics
- Builds purpose-specific inspection robots rather than centering humanoid form.
- Serves defense, energy, manufacturing, and other infrastructure-heavy sectors.
- Collects physical-world condition data that is scarce in internet-scale training corpora.
- Uses AI to model present defects and future asset risk.
- Targets asset uptime, life extension, manufacturing speed, and hazardous-work reduction.
- Plans to move from inspection toward supervised repair and manufacturing actions.

## Qualifications
Contract value, business mix, speed improvements, asset ROI, and roadmap timing are source- or company-attributed. Defense and industrial deployments require reliability, cybersecurity, data governance, safety, integration, trained operators, and procurement oversight. Inspection success does not prove autonomous repair readiness.

## What Changed
- Expanded the profile from Navy inspection into energy and broader built-world infrastructure.
- Added proprietary industrial data as a strategic asset created through paid work.
- Added the supervised repair and welding roadmap while preserving the inspection-to-action gap.
