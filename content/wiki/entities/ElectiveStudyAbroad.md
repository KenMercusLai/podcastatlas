# Elective Study Abroad

## Overview
Elective Study Abroad is the adult study-abroad business founded by [[KariAngoff]] in [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]], offering week-long seminars that combine a single subject with the place where it is studied.

## Current Profile
The company designs one-week, ten-person maximum courses for adults, beginning from a theme rather than an itinerary: southern Spain through architecture and food, Istanbul through world religions, each taught by instructors with deep ties to the subject and the destination. Roughly eighteen months in, the business is planning five courses and twenty-six students by year end. Kari describes strong interest signals and weak conversion, with roughly $6,200 as the average course price.

## Key Characteristics
- Experience business sold as education, with a place-linked theme rather than a tour itinerary.
- Deliberately small cohorts: no more than ten participants, with peer discussion treated as part of the product.
- Audience skews to retirees, though not exclusively.
- Conversion gap between interest and enrolment is the central operating problem.
- Positioning risk: differentiating from sightseeing can weaken direct comparison against travel products.

## Evidence
- Product design: [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]] describes week-long seminars on themes such as architecture and food in southern Spain and world religions in Istanbul, taught by local instructors connected to both theme and destination.
- Cohort design: [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]] says classes are capped at ten because retired participants bring varied backgrounds and the discussion nearly equals the curriculum.
- Traction and price: [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]] reports five courses and twenty-six students by year end and an average price of about $6,200.
- Funnel problem: [[advice-line-with-michelle-wahler-of-beyond-yoga-c2626aae-0d71-4025-842f-3b854ccc3ca6]] records strong clicks, detailed email questions, and steady calls alongside weak enrolment despite deadlines and discounts.

## Qualifications
Scale, pricing, and demand claims come from the founder within one episode. The source does not establish completion rates, repeat enrolment, marketing spend, or the effectiveness of the recommended changes.
