# Qatar

[[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]] adds Qatar as part of the regional pressure channel on [[Hamas]]. The episode groups Qatar with [[Egypt]] and [[Turkey]] as countries important to Hamas's regional operations and involved in pressure aligned with the Trump administration's Gaza plan.

Qatar appears in [[a-firm-andy-what-are-new-british-pms-plans-6a5deeca76f8ee25bb4bbf9c]] as one of the Gulf states most interested in [[Syria]]'s reconstruction after [[AhmadAlSharah]] takes power. The episode says Qatar has already started work, including support around a refurbished airport in Damascus.

The source positions Qatar inside [[PostwarReconstructionFinance]] rather than as a generic Gulf actor. Its practical involvement contrasts with slower [[SaudiArabia|Saudi]] efforts and a more cautious [[UnitedArabEmirates|UAE]] shift from distance toward engagement.
