## Overview

王逢 / Wang Feng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-511-taihou-tianhuaban-ta-ruhe-yitian-feng-wuge-didi-wei-hou-lhoz_ubecvc8-mgfoad2pya72uht|Hanji 511]] as one of [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s brothers enfeoffed by [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] on the same day in 27 BCE.

## Current Profile

This page disambiguates 王逢 from the existing [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]] page. In Hanji 511, 王逢 matters as one member of the [[ChengdiFiveMarquisesWesternHan|成帝五侯]] group; the episode does not provide a separate biography or later career for him.

## Key Characteristics

- Wang-family brother of Wang Zhengjun in Hanji 511.
- One of five same-day marquis recipients under Chengdi.
- Source-scoped group member rather than an individually developed actor in the episode.
