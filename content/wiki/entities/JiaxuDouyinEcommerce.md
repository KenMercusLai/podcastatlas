# Jiaxu / 嘉旭 (Douyin E-commerce)

## Overview
Jiaxu / 嘉旭 is the [[DouyinEcommerce|抖音电商]] staff member in the No.224 [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] episode explaining platform operations for AI-enabled infringement and impersonation.

## Current Profile
In this source, Jiaxu is the operator voice. He explains how infringement appears across short videos, livestreams, product listings, accounts, and authorization submissions, and he emphasizes that the platform strongly prohibits identity-based deceptive selling.

His most important wiki contribution is operational specificity. He describes active portrait protection, future voice-information recognition, rights-holder data submission, complaint and appeal channels, false-positive categories, reported enforcement figures, and the difficulty of maintaining offline authorization relationships inside a platform system.

## Key Characteristics
- He is presented as working on imitation and infringement issues at Douyin ecommerce.
- He treats AI-enabled voice and likeness misuse as a commerce trust and consumer-protection issue.
- He describes platform governance across pre-review, post-reporting, enforcement, appeal, and authorization maintenance.
- He emphasizes that legitimate authorization can be missed if rights data is not maintained in the platform.
- He frames infringement governance as a long-running adversarial process rather than a one-time cleanup.
