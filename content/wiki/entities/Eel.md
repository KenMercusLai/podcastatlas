# 鳗鱼 / Eel

Eel / 鳗鱼 is the aquaculture and processing case in [[ep260-yikou-feimei-zhifang-cangzhe-zhongguo-yangzhi-de-xin-gushi-linjq2fkzhqvzjcgrmhudq12vs4j]]. The episode treats eel as unusually financialized because farmers still depend on wild-caught seedlings and must make stocking decisions before knowing price, survival, and market demand.

The source follows eel from seedling capture, white-to-black nursery growth, heated early-stage ponds, soil-pond grow-out, "吊水" deodorizing, factory killing and deboning, and long grilled-eel production lines that replicate Japanese live-eel preparation at industrial scale. [[Guangdong|广东]] and [[Shunde|顺德]] anchor the Japanese-eel branch, while [[Fujian|福建]] is noted as more associated with American eel in the episode's buying advice.
