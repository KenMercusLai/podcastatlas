# 许振华 / Xu Zhenhua

## Overview

许振华 appears in the bounded source as a [[HuzuoHuyou|忽左忽右]] guest interpreting the 1965 Singapore-Malaysia separation through the “Albatross File” exhibition, political history, and travel observation.

## Current Profile

Xu connects archival display with the longer structures of British decolonization, Cold War anti-communism, ethnic politics, and competing ideas of Malaysia. He emphasizes [[GohKengSwee|Goh Keng Swee]]'s negotiating initiative while resisting a one-person explanation, then uses Singapore's neighborhoods and institutions to connect political history with lived urban form.

## Key Characteristics

- Treats exhibitions and oral-history archives as active constructions of national memory.
- Connects elite negotiation to colonial institutions, regional security, and ethnic political organization.
- Distinguishes a qualified archival revision from a total reversal of the established separation narrative.
- Uses travel, neighborhoods, and public institutions to make political history spatially visible.
