# Nathaniel Herz

## Overview

Nathaniel Herz is the executive editor of the [[AnchoragePress|Anchorage Press]] who interviewed [[WalterAINewsAgent|Walter]] and assessed its role in Alaska journalism.

## Current Profile

Herz offers a qualified rather than categorical rejection of newsroom AI. He sees value in monitoring dispersed public information, discovering leads, and supporting reporters, particularly across a large state with thin local coverage. He draws the boundary at editorial prioritization, human interest, investigation, accountability, and the transparent judgment for which a person can be questioned.

## Key Characteristics

- Evaluates Walter through direct text interaction at [[KaleGreen]]'s workstation.
- Distinguishes information-processing scale from community relevance and public-interest judgment.
- Supports AI for lead discovery and backstage newsroom work under human editorial safeguards.
- Treats investigation and accountability reporting as non-substitutable human responsibilities.
- Connects responsible AI use to audience confidence and newsroom ethics.

## Evidence

- Interview experience: [[tech-20261022-1022-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261022-1022-mp-tech-pod-128]] recounts Herz questioning Walter and comparing the evasiveness of some answers with an ordinary interview.
- Editorial judgment: [[tech-20261022-1022-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261022-1022-mp-tech-pod-128]] attributes to Herz the distinction between broad processing and deciding what people should care about.
- Responsible use: [[tech-20261022-1022-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261022-1022-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports his support for newsroom-by-newsroom ethical decisions, human safeguards, and assistance rather than replacement.

## Qualifications

The profile is limited to one short interview about Walter and does not establish Herz's full career, reporting record, or the complete AI policy of the [[AnchoragePress|Anchorage Press]]. The source summarizes rather than transcribes most of his remarks.
