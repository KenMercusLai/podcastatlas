## Overview

窦太后 / Empress Dowager Dou now has a pre-empress selection layer before the existing Zhangdi and He-era material. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-749-guanliren-bikan-fa-jiangjin-shi-guanli-de-zuijia-shouduan-ma-lnhhoh0oxlstuwakl3rsqvcss-cf|Hanji 749]] shows Dou Xun's daughter being selected as guiren and then made empress under [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Emperor Zhang]], [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-751-dou-huanghou-gaosu-ni-nvren-bu-hen-diwei-bu-wen-luvqadctbfuzit2ackrqifour2c6|Hanji 751]] shows her as the childless empress who raises [[EmperorHeOfHan|Liu Zhao]] and attacks [[SongGuirenZhangHeLateHan|Song Guiren]] and [[LiuQingQingheKing|Liu Qing]], and the later sources show her regency, Dou-family protection, and posthumous legitimacy challenge.

## Current Profile

Hanji 749 gives the selection layer. After an omen notice in the early Zhangdi reign, Zhangdi selects Dou Xun's daughter as guiren. In 78 CE, he makes her empress. The page keeps Dou Xun and the exact selection mechanics source-scoped, but this layer matters because it places Dou's later family-power story inside a formal rise from consort to empress before the Liu Zhao succession conflict.

Hanji 751 gives the first origin of the later legitimacy problem. Empress Dou has no son, raises Liu Zhao as her own, and therefore has a direct interest in replacing Liu Qing as crown prince. The source says she works through her mother and family to collect Song-family faults, monitor the Song sisters, and transform Song Guiren's request for rabbit meat during illness into a curse accusation. Zhangdi accepts the charge, Liu Qing is demoted to Qinghe king, Liu Zhao becomes crown prince, and the Song sisters die after examination by [[CaiLunLateHan|Cai Lun]].

Hanji 752 gives the second Zhangdi-era layer. Liu Zhao's elevation makes Liang Guiren's family politically meaningful because Liang is the birth mother of the child Dou has raised. The source links Dou jealousy and the accusation route against Liang Song to imprisonment, exile of Liang relatives to Jiuzhen, and the grief deaths of Liang Guiren and her sister. This makes Dou's later adoptive-mother status rest beside a suppressed birth-mother line rather than on uncontested household order.

Hanji 759-761 present the living dowager as both ruler-adjacent authority and family protector. After Emperor Zhang dies, Emperor He is a child ruler and Dou governs from the empress-dowager position. Her authority lets Dou Xian and the Dou brothers stand close to the emperor and dowager, and her protection lets Dou Xian's murder liability become a Northern Xiongnu campaign request instead of ordinary punishment. She then ignores anti-campaign remonstrance, orders mansions for Dou Du and Dou Jing, and favors Dou in the Zhi Shou case before He Chang secures mitigation.

Hanji 770 begins after Dou Taihou's death in 97 CE. Liang relatives reveal that Liang Guiren, Emperor He's birth mother, had not received proper recognition. Senior officials propose stripping Dou's dowager title and burial pairing with Emperor Zhang, but Emperor He refuses because he had served her as mother for ten years. Her current profile is therefore ambivalent: she is an active suppressor and Dou-family enabler, but she remains ritually protected as the emperor's adoptive mother.

## Key Characteristics

- Zhangdi-era consort selected as guiren and then made empress before the later succession conflict.
- Childless empress who builds a foster-maternal claim by raising Liu Zhao.
- Palace accuser whose campaign removes Song Guiren and Liu Qing from the crown-prince line.
- Maternal-status actor whose anxiety helps suppress Liang Guiren's birth-mother line.
- Emperor He's adoptive mother and formal empress dowager whose authority opens Dou-family access around a child emperor.
- Family protector who lets Dou Xian seek military redemption instead of ordinary punishment.
- Retains dowager dignity because Emperor He preserves the adoptive mother-son relationship and burial order.

## Qualifications

This page remains bounded to Hanji 749, Hanji 751, Hanji 752, Hanji 759, Hanji 760, Hanji 761, and Hanji 770 and does not reconstruct Dou Taihou's full life, independent defense, or every regency decision. Hanji 749 adds a formal selection and empress layer but leaves Dou Xun and the exact selection process source-scoped. Hanji 751 and Hanji 752 are pre-dowager empress layers; the page keeps them here because they explain the later adoptive-mother and birth-mother conflict. Hanji 770 gives a posthumous status and victim-redress layer.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 749 as the earliest layer: Dou Xun's daughter selected as guiren and then made empress in 78 CE.
- Reframed Hanji 751 as the next pre-dowager layer: childlessness, Liu Zhao adoption, Song Guiren accusation, Liu Qing deposition, and Song-sister deaths.
- Reframed Hanji 752's Liang suppression as the second stage of Dou's maternal-status problem after Liu Zhao has already displaced Liu Qing.
- Preserved her living-dowager profile around Dou-family access, Liu Chang, campaign authorization, mansion construction, and Zhi Shou.
- Preserved Hanji 770's posthumous revelation as the delayed exposure of Zhangdi-era conflicts.
