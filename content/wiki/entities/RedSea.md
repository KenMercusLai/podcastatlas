# Red Sea

The Red Sea appears in [[outsider-chance-ending-sudans-proxy-war-6a75b3695d765781a2b1b132]] as one reason outside powers care about [[Sudan]]'s political future. The episode says Sudan has around 800 kilometers of Red Sea coastline along a vital and contested waterway.

In this source, the Red Sea is not discussed as a standalone shipping case; it is part of Sudan's strategic value alongside gold reserves and arable land. That value helps explain why foreign backers may resist letting their preferred Sudanese partners lose outright.
