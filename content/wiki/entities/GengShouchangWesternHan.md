## Overview

耿寿昌 / Geng Shouchang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-1-yi-ming-ji-huangwei-jiemi-hanwudi-zui-qipa-erzi-ltdqudgpmgkulrhhxkzvjsvgjlo5|Hanji 456-1]] as a Western Han Da Sinong subordinate who proposes two linked grain reforms under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]: substitute nearer purchases for part of costly Guandong transport, and build [[ChangpingGranarySystem|长平仓]] as a price-stabilizing reserve system.

## Current Profile

The source presents Geng Shouchang as an administrator who sees both logistics and price effects. Consecutive harvests lower grain prices and hurt farmers, while the old capital-supply system uses large labor commitments to move grain from east of Hangu Pass. Geng proposes buying grain from Sanfu, Hongnong, Hedong, Shangdang, Taiyuan, and neighboring areas to supply the capital, reducing transport labor from Guandong by more than half in the episode's summary.

He then proposes frontier granaries that buy when grain is cheap and sell when grain is expensive. Xuan accepts the idea and rewards him as Guannei Hou. In the wiki this makes Geng the first focused figure for [[StateGrainPriceStabilization|state grain price stabilization]] rather than merely a minor economic-policy name.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han fiscal-agricultural official whose proposals combine logistics, price stabilization, and famine preparation.
- Diagnoses low grain prices after repeated harvests as a farmer-income problem rather than only a consumer benefit.
- Replaces distant supply movement with nearer regional procurement around the capital.
- Designs or advocates Changping granaries as countercyclical public reserves.
- Receives Xuan's acceptance and Guannei Hou reward in the episode.
