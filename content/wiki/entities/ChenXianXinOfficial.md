## Overview

陈贤 / Chen Xian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-645-bidu-zhichang-quanmou-mingzhebaoshen-or-niliu-ershang-lvdqcakza0zqextrhympnpswxyjy|Hanji 645]] as a former Han legal specialist and shangshu who withdraws when [[WangMang|王莽]]'s [[XinDynasty|新朝]] turns institutional change into political danger. The episode uses him as a disciplined case of withdrawal that protects family, principle, and Han institutional memory.

## Current Profile

Chen Xian is presented as someone close enough to law and office to understand the regime's direction. Wang Mang changes Han institutions while in power, and the deaths of He Wu and Bao Xuan make the danger concrete. Chen Xian cites the timing principle "见几而作，不俟终日," asks to retire, refuses Wang Mang's later summons by claiming illness, and tells his three official sons to resign.

The profile is not simple passivity. Chen Xian keeps his household sacrifices on the former Han schedule and answers critics by saying his ancestors will not know Wang-family ritual dates. He also stores Han legal texts in a wall, making withdrawal a form of low-visibility institutional preservation rather than public collaboration with the new order.

## Key Characteristics

- Former Han legal specialist whose office experience helps him read Wang Mang's institutional rupture.
- Withdraws after politically motivated deaths show that continued service is no longer protected.
- Refuses renewed summons and extends withdrawal to his sons, treating family exposure as part of the risk.
- Preserves Han ritual timing despite New Dynasty calendrical and ritual pressure.
- Hides legal texts, turning private concealment into institutional-memory preservation.
- Serves as a cleaner example of principled exit than opportunistic status preservation.

## Qualifications

The page is source-bounded to Hanji 645 and does not reconstruct Chen Xian's full career or later fate. The source supports a withdrawal-and-preservation profile, but not a claim that Chen Xian coordinated resistance or openly opposed Wang Mang after retirement.
