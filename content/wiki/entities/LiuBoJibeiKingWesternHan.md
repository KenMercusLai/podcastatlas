## Overview

济北王刘勃 / Liu Bo is a Western Han prince, son of [[LiuChangHuainanKing|淮南王刘长]], who rules Hengshan during the Rebellion of the Seven States and is moved to Jibei after remaining loyal to the court.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-238-qiguo-zhiluan-huainanwang-liuan-ruhe-zhuanweiweian-1-lvolhayetcfp4km8mewo9qr-aq0d|Hanji 238]] presents Liu Bo as the loyal contrast to his brother [[LiuAn|刘安]]. When Wu's envoy reaches Hengshan, Liu Bo reportedly secures the city rather than joining the rebellion. He later appears at Chang'an, receives [[HanJingdi|汉景帝]]'s praise, and is transferred from the low, humid southern Hengshan territory to Jibei. The episode records his death two years later without developing a broader reign profile.

## Key Characteristics

- Son of Liu Chang and brother of Liu An and [[LiuCiHengshanKingWesternHan|刘赐]].
- King of Hengshan during the Rebellion of the Seven States.
- Rejects Wu's appeal and keeps his fortified city aligned with the Han court.
- Appears at Chang'an after the rebellion and is praised for loyalty.
- Reassigned as king of Jibei in a reward framed partly through regional climate and geography.
- Dies in 152 BCE in the episode's chronology.

## Qualifications

The current profile rests on one compact popular-history summary rather than a full reign study or primary-text collation. The episode does not reconstruct Liu Bo's administration, the precise military measures at Hengshan, the legal form of his reassignment, or the cause of death. This page is deliberately disambiguated from [[LiuBoChangshanKingWesternHan|常山王刘勃]] and [[LiuBoChangyiKingWesternHan|昌邑王刘髆]].
