# 郑振铎 / Zheng Zhenduo

郑振铎 is cited in [[fanwai-15-banlatte-jiang-jinpingmei-la-erqie-haishi-luse-de-991508433]] during the reception-history section on [[JinPingMei|《金瓶梅》]]. The episode uses his evaluation alongside [[LuXun|鲁迅]], 毛主席, [[LiYuWriter|李渔]], and [[ZhangZhupo|张竹坡]] to move the novel out of a narrow scandal frame and into literary, historical, and social-documentary discussion.

His source role is to strengthen [[FictionAsHistoricalEvidence]] and [[UrbanEverydayFiction]] readings. The point is not that a critic's praise settles the novel's meaning, but that modern literary history has long recognized the book's value for everyday life, social institutions, and later fiction such as [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]].
