# Afrikaners

## Overview

Afrikaners are presented as the white South African community whose historical memory, religious identity, and attachment to rugby made the [[Springboks]] one of the most charged symbols in the post-apartheid settlement.

## Current Profile

The episode links Afrikaner identity to Dutch Calvinism, Huguenot settlement, the Great Trek, conflict with British imperial power, and a self-understanding shaped by chosen-people language. Rugby becomes a cultural language for this identity, so [[NelsonMandela]]'s embrace of the Springboks is meaningful because it recognizes a symbol many black South Africans associated with [[Apartheid|apartheid]] and many Afrikaners associated with pride.

## Key Characteristics

- White South African community tied in the episode to Dutch, Calvinist, Huguenot, frontier, and anti-British memory.
- Political community associated with the 1948 government that institutionalized [[Apartheid|apartheid]].
- Rugby-attached identity group for whom the [[Springboks]] carried deep emotional value.
- Community whose sporting isolation mattered because rugby exclusion struck at a major pride point.
- Audience Mandela tries to bring into post-apartheid [[SouthAfrica]] rather than simply defeat symbolically.

## Evidence

- Historical identity: [[682-south-africa-mandela-and-the-death-of-apartheid-part-6-glt6535658080]] connects Afrikaner history to Dutch Calvinism, Huguenot settlement, the Great Trek, British conflict, and chosen-people belief.
- Apartheid state connection: [[682-south-africa-mandela-and-the-death-of-apartheid-part-6-glt6535658080]] says an Afrikaner-dominated government came to power in 1948 and institutionalized apartheid.
- Rugby attachment: [[682-south-africa-mandela-and-the-death-of-apartheid-part-6-glt6535658080]] says rugby became especially associated with Afrikaners and Afrikaner identity.
- Boycott vulnerability: [[682-south-africa-mandela-and-the-death-of-apartheid-part-6-glt6535658080]] says sporting boycotts mattered because rugby exclusion hurt Afrikaner pride.
- Reconciliation audience: [[682-south-africa-mandela-and-the-death-of-apartheid-part-6-glt6535658080]] presents the Afrikaner crowd chanting Mandela's name at Ellis Park as a major reconciliation moment.

## Qualifications

This page summarizes only the episode's identity and rugby-symbol account. It should not be read as a complete social, linguistic, political, or religious history of Afrikaners.
