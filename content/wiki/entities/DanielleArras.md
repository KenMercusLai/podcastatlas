# Danielle Arras

Danielle Arras appears in [[burning-questions-a-more-fire-prone-world-6a687ea64207b2a60119df8b]] as the speaker explaining U.S. [[NightlifeMayor]] roles. She frames the job as a bridge between nighttime businesses, residents, regulators, and city officials rather than as a simple cheerleader for bars and clubs.

Her segment ties [[NighttimeEconomyGovernance]] to both red tape and social life. Cities have to handle noise, licensing, crowd management, and [[LiquorLicenseQuotaBarrier|liquor-license barriers]], but they also face [[SocializingDecline]] and a broader need to make lawful after-dark activity easier to sustain.
