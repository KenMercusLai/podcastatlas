# Saronic

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-saronic-founders-autonomous-warships-chinas-230x-advantage-swarms-of-robot-ships-42334230]] presents Saronic as a defense technology company building autonomous surface vessels for naval missions. Co-founders [[DinoMavroukis]] and [[VibhAltakar]] argue that the company is trying to rebuild American maritime capacity through [[AutonomousNavalVessels]], [[SoftwareDefinedShipbuilding]], private R&D, and a new shipyard project called [[PortAlpha|Port Alpha]].

The company's wiki role is a concrete case of [[PrivateCapitalDefensePrime]]. Saronic is framed as neither a pure software vendor nor a legacy shipyard: the episode says it works on vessel design, autonomy, manufacturing, supply chain, electronics, sensors, compute, and production facilities in one vertically integrated model.

## Key Claims
- The source says Saronic has raised $2.5 billion in private capital and used internal R&D to build [[MarauderSaronic|Marauder]].
- The company presents [[CorsairSaronic|Corsair]] as a smaller autonomous vessel that can be produced at high annual volume.
- Saronic argues that autonomous vessels should complement rather than wholly replace the [[USNavy|U.S. Navy]]'s crewed fleet.
- The company's strategic claim is that naval power increasingly depends on software, autonomy, mass, cost, and shipbuilding throughput.
