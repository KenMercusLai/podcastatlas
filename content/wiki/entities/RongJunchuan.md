# Rong Junchuan / 容俊川

## Overview

Rong Junchuan / 容俊川 is the Boyang chairman figure named in [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]]. The episode uses him to voice the ambition that [[Ningbo|宁波]] could grow a modern fashion industry from its city center.

## Current Profile

Rong's source role is limited but important: he connects [[BeyondHomeTextile|博洋家纺]] and [[NingboFashionCreativeCenter|NFCC 宁波时尚创意中心]] to a broader city-upgrade claim. Manufacturing capability alone is not enough for the fashion branch; the city also needs designers, artists, display spaces, retail channels, international buyers, and young talent.

## Key Characteristics

- Rong is presented as Boyang's chairman in the source.
- His quoted ambition connects Ningbo to Paris and Milan as fashion-city comparisons.
- He represents the design-and-city-space branch rather than the episode's older factory-founder branch.
