# Indian Muslims

Indian Muslims appear in [[settler-bet-a-west-bank-flashpoint-6a842bb539e73ea8f3c13a2d]] as the minority community most directly affected by state-level [[UniformCivilCodeIndia|uniform civil codes]]. The episode says Muslims in [[Uttarakhand]] have been barred from following many customary family-law practices since January 2025, especially around marriage, divorce, and inheritance.

The source keeps both sides of the issue visible. Some Muslim women may welcome more equal inheritance and bans on polygamy, but [[KiraHuyu|Kira Huju]] says the broader political effect is to make Muslims conform to rules rooted in Hindu legal traditions, creating a [[MajoritarianLegalUniformity]] problem and risking deeper distance from the legal mainstream.
