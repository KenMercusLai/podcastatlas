# 楚怀王 / King Huai of Chu

楚怀王 / King Huai of Chu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] through the 329 BCE succession notice. [[ChuWeiwang|楚威王]] dies, and his son succeeds as the new ruler of [[ChuState|楚国]].

The source treats the notice as a preview rather than a developed biography. It says Chu Huai Wang will become one of the more dramatic rulers in Warring States history and notes that the Qu Yuan-era narrative is approaching, but this episode keeps him mainly as a marker that the political map is widening beyond the Qin-Wei front.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-39-lishishang-zhangyi-shi-zenyang-nudui-qiangdi-de-lv2gc2wtcxuhugfgixk7rbbhpa6x]] gives him his first active court role in the wiki. After [[HuiShi|惠施 / 惠师]] flees from [[WeiState|魏国]] to Chu, [[FengHaoWarringStates|冯浩]] advises Chu Huai Wang to send him onward to [[SongState|宋国]] rather than offend [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]. Later, [[ChenZhenWarringStates|陈枕]] regains Chu favor by using [[ZuoShuang|左爽]]'s [[SituationalPoliticalPivot|因事立道式政治转身]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-42-gei-mengchangjun-ti-yijian-de-houguo-lr1jp0tpiaboaca3j5-yvyhyn4vf]] gives a gift-court scene involving Mengchang Jun. The source calls the donor the Chu king; because the wiki's preceding Zhouji chronology has Chu Huai Wang succeeding in 329 BCE, this branch links the scene here cautiously. The gift is an ivory bed whose refusal becomes a case of [[ContentOverMotiveRemonstrance|content-over-motive remonstrance]] through [[GongsunXu|公孙须]] and [[DengTuzhi|邓图直]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-45-yi-shi-wei-jian-junwang-daodi-gai-bu-gai-sharen-chengxing-lo3cok7cfwzrxesp-i8miz1fc1db]] makes him the named leader of the 318 BCE five-state attack on [[QinState|秦国]]. Under his alliance leadership, Chu, Zhao, Wei, Han, and Yan reach [[HanguPass|函谷关]], but the source says the states do not attack in unity. Qin cuts Chu's grain route, Chu withdraws first, and the coalition breaks, making Chu Huai Wang part of [[CoalitionSelfPreservationFailure|联盟自保失灵]] rather than a successful hezong victory.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-50-zhangyi-shi-zenyang-ba-chuhuaiwang-huyou-que-de-loxg3ylhmskvdm-axtco5y-acege]] makes him the central decision-maker in [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s deception. Chu Huai Wang accepts the promise of six hundred li of [[Shangyu|商於]], rejects [[ChenZhenWarringStates|陈枕]]'s advice to verify Qin's delivery before breaking with [[QiState|齐国]], and then escalates the break by insulting [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]]. When Zhang Yi changes the promise to six li, Chu Huai Wang refuses Chen Zhen's second containment advice and attacks Qin, turning diplomatic humiliation into military exposure.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-51-chuhuaiwang-qin-xiao-er-kan-jiujiu-ruhe-mie-le-ni-ltfkdlivomnvux5xodh2rlfqf6ac]] follows that exposure into disaster. The episode explains his credulity through the size of the promised gain, misplaced trust in [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] and Zhang Yi's office, and Zhang Yi's prior influence over Chu elites, including pressure against [[QuYuan|屈原]]. Chu Huai Wang then turns rage into war: [[DanyangBattle|丹阳之战]] costs Chu eighty thousand soldiers, [[QuGai|屈匄]] is captured, [[Hanzhong|汉中]] is lost, and the renewed push through [[Wuguan|武关]] ends near [[LantianBattle|蓝田之战]]. The source makes him the main case for [[AngerDrivenWarDecision|因怒兴师]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2]] keeps him in the cliffhanger position rather than adding a new completed decision. After Yan and Qin-Shu material, the episode returns to Chu Huai Wang's hatred of [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] and sets up Zhang Yi entering Chu despite the earlier Shangyu deception and Qin-Chu battlefield losses.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-53-qinhuiwang-yong-zhangyi-huan-dipi-lvmo2cwbafcg2gnxliuo18fewimk]] follows that hatred into a new decision. Chu Huai Wang initially rejects [[YingSi|秦惠文王]]'s land exchange and demands Zhang Yi in place of [[Qianzhong|黔中]], then imprisons Zhang Yi and prepares to kill him. He reverses after [[ZhengXiu|郑袖]] warns of Qin retaliation and a possible Qin ransom package involving [[Shangyong|上庸]], and he later accepts Zhang Yi's argument for Qin-Chu brotherhood because releasing Zhang Yi lets him avoid ceding Qianzhong or violating the [[EnvoyKillingTaboo|不斩来使]] norm. The episode therefore treats him as vulnerable to Zhang Yi again, but also as a ruler seeking an exit from an overcommitted revenge posture.
