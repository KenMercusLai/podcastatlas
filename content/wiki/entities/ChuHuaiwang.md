# 楚怀王 / King Huai of Chu

楚怀王 / King Huai of Chu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] through the 329 BCE succession notice. [[ChuWeiwang|楚威王]] dies, and his son succeeds as the new ruler of [[ChuState|楚国]].

The source treats the notice as a preview rather than a developed biography. It says Chu Huai Wang will become one of the more dramatic rulers in Warring States history and notes that the Qu Yuan-era narrative is approaching, but this episode keeps him mainly as a marker that the political map is widening beyond the Qin-Wei front.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-39-lishishang-zhangyi-shi-zenyang-nudui-qiangdi-de-lv2gc2wtcxuhugfgixk7rbbhpa6x]] gives him his first active court role in the wiki. After [[HuiShi|惠施 / 惠师]] flees from [[WeiState|魏国]] to Chu, [[FengHaoWarringStates|冯浩]] advises Chu Huai Wang to send him onward to [[SongState|宋国]] rather than offend [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]. Later, [[ChenZhenWarringStates|陈枕]] regains Chu favor by using [[ZuoShuang|左爽]]'s [[SituationalPoliticalPivot|因事立道式政治转身]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-42-gei-mengchangjun-ti-yijian-de-houguo-lr1jp0tpiaboaca3j5-yvyhyn4vf]] gives a gift-court scene involving Mengchang Jun. The source calls the donor the Chu king; because the wiki's preceding Zhouji chronology has Chu Huai Wang succeeding in 329 BCE, this branch links the scene here cautiously. The gift is an ivory bed whose refusal becomes a case of [[ContentOverMotiveRemonstrance|content-over-motive remonstrance]] through [[GongsunXu|公孙须]] and [[DengTuzhi|邓图直]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-45-yi-shi-wei-jian-junwang-daodi-gai-bu-gai-sharen-chengxing-lo3cok7cfwzrxesp-i8miz1fc1db]] makes him the named leader of the 318 BCE five-state attack on [[QinState|秦国]]. Under his alliance leadership, Chu, Zhao, Wei, Han, and Yan reach [[HanguPass|函谷关]], but the source says the states do not attack in unity. Qin cuts Chu's grain route, Chu withdraws first, and the coalition breaks, making Chu Huai Wang part of [[CoalitionSelfPreservationFailure|联盟自保失灵]] rather than a successful hezong victory.
