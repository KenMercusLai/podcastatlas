# 蒋子文 / Jiang Ziwen

蒋子文 / Jiang Ziwen is the local religious figure discussed near the end of [[06-soushenji-ershisi-xiao-zhe-kou-guo-ganbao-bu-bei-536821044]]. The episode presents him as a Nanjing-area belief figure who, after death, demands worship from Sun Quan, becomes tied to later underworld imagination, and appears in stories about divine coercion, offended ritual authority, and unexpected romantic restraint.

The source uses Jiang Ziwen to extend [[LocalDeityGovernance]] and [[UnderworldBureaucracy]]. He is not only a ghost-story character; he makes local cult formation, temple legitimacy, divine punishment, and afterlife office legible in personal narrative form. The closing story about Wangzi also supports [[StrangeTaleMoralPluralism]] because the deity can be coercive in one story and surprisingly non-retaliatory in another.
