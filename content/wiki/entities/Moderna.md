# Moderna

Moderna appears in [[kafeidou-chuantong-meishi-guangchang-jielian-bidian-dashidaimen-yudao-naxie-fazhan-zuai-1007530222]] as [[Merck|Merck / 默沙东]]'s partner on a personalized mRNA cancer vaccine. The source says the vaccine is made after surgery by analyzing a patient's tumor mutations, building an individualized mRNA vaccine, and training the immune system to recognize corresponding cancer cells.

The source keeps the claim bounded: it says the companies reported lower melanoma recurrence or spread risk in a 1,000-plus-patient trial, while detailed data were not disclosed in the episode and the production process remains complex and expensive. In the wiki, Moderna therefore extends [[CancerVaccinePlatform]] as a large-company personalized-vaccine case rather than as settled proof that the platform is broadly solved.
