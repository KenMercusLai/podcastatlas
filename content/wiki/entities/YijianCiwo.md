# 一间次卧 / Yijian Ciwo

## Overview
一间次卧 is the online bedroom-sharing platform discussed in [[vol-272-2026-nian-le-zenme-hai-you-ren-yao-zuo-zhongguo-ban-airbnb-1008880863]], where [[TanDing|谭丁]] argues that China may now have the supply and demand conditions for paid stays in ordinary spare rooms.

## Current Profile
The platform is positioned between [[Airbnb]]'s original room-sharing idea, Chinese homestay platforms, local lodging demand, and social discovery on [[Xiaohongshu]]. It is not presented as a full hotel substitute: its pitch is lower price, domestic facilities, host context, and more personal but less standardized stays.

## Key Characteristics
- Focuses on paid spare-bedroom stays rather than professionally managed hotel-like inventory.
- Uses low price, longer stays, and domestic amenities to serve guests who may be traveling for jobs, exams, interviews, medical visits, short-term living, or local trips.
- Depends on ordinary homeowner supply shaped by mortgage pressure, excess space, and changed income expectations.
- Uses trust and fulfillment mechanisms including payment custody, real-name payment rails, offline room checks, guest feedback, and host/guest matching expectations.
- Treats lodging rooms as brand-experience channels, using product placement and barter to support growth and host incentives.
- Experiments with AI and [[RetrievalAugmentedGeneration|RAG]] search because fixed hotel-like tags cannot capture highly variable bedrooms and host preferences.

## Evidence
- Category focus: [[vol-272-2026-nian-le-zenme-hai-you-ren-yao-zuo-zhongguo-ban-airbnb-1008880863]] describes the platform as centered on rooms in ordinary homes, with hosts setting subjective prices and the platform taking about 15% commission.
- Demand profile: [[vol-272-2026-nian-le-zenme-hai-you-ren-yao-zuo-zhongguo-ban-airbnb-1008880863]] reports low average prices, mostly young female guests, and more than half of surveyed guests using stays for non-leisure mobility such as exams, job search, interviews, or temporary living.
- Supply motivation: [[vol-272-2026-nian-le-zenme-hai-you-ren-yao-zuo-zhongguo-ban-airbnb-1008880863]] ties host willingness to spare rooms, mortgage pressure, household unemployment risk, and the need for extra monthly cash flow.
- Trust and fulfillment: [[vol-272-2026-nian-le-zenme-hai-you-ren-yao-zuo-zhongguo-ban-airbnb-1008880863]] describes payment custody, offline room visits, feedback loops, and concern about direct transfers and off-platform transactions.
- Growth and product design: [[vol-272-2026-nian-le-zenme-hai-you-ren-yao-zuo-zhongguo-ban-airbnb-1008880863]] traces cold start through [[Xiaohongshu]], mini-program traffic, referrals, brand barter, AI-assisted coding, and natural-language search experiments.

## Qualifications
The platform metrics, supply counts, user growth figures, guest composition, and compliance details are source-scoped to the episode. The page records the current business thesis, not proof that the model will scale.
