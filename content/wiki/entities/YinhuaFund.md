# 银华基金 / Yinhua Fund

银华基金 appears in [[vol-121-cong-tanhua-yixian-de-fenji-jijin-dao-fengtou-zhengjing-de-ganggan-etf-yongyuan-buyao-digu-renxing-de-fengkuang-lusagcitdozvzm8wausvecvi-qmb]] through 银华深证 100 指数分级 and 银华锐进. The source presents this product as a model for the third-generation structured-fund design, including index transparency, [[StructuredFundPairConversion|pair conversion]], and upper/lower conversion mechanics.

The source uses 银华锐进's strong rebound to show why leverage became emotionally compelling to retail investors once rising markets made the B share's upside visible.
