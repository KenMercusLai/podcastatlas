# Sajit Channa

## Overview
Sajit Channa is the [[BeneficialStateBank]] representative quoted in the Marketplace Tech episode on responsible AI adoption in banking.

## Current Profile
Channa contributes the values-based governance argument. He says banks should develop AI guidelines grounded in values and morals, then explicitly consider labor-market impact, privacy, access to credit, and whether AI systems discriminate.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats bank AI policy as a moral and institutional design question.
- Connects discrimination control to credit access rather than only reputational risk.
- Frames responsible AI as potentially aligned with bank growth because nondiscriminatory systems can reach broader communities.
