# Leapmotor S01 / 零跑 S01

## Overview
Leapmotor S01 / 零跑 S01 is presented as [[Leapmotor|零跑汽车]]'s first vehicle and the company's formative product-definition failure.

## Current Profile
The source treats S01 as a wrong lesson rather than a successful launch. Zhu Jiangming says the two-door coupe decision came from early shareholders' informal product discussion, not mature consumer understanding. Its weak sales exposed Leapmotor's lack of 2C product planning and marketing capability, then forced the company to take product definition more seriously before T03 and C11.

## Key Characteristics
- First Leapmotor vehicle in the source's company-history arc.
- Two-door coupe priced around RMB 110,000-120,000 in the source description.
- Chosen through immature early product planning rather than disciplined consumer segmentation.
- Sold poorly enough that factory production was only one or two hundred units a month.
- Became the negative reference point for later product-definition improvement.
