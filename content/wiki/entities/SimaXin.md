# 司马欣 / Sima Xin

司马欣 / Sima Xin enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-3-chensheng-zhisi-weishenme-dele-minxin-hanle-renxin-lnx4zs-vsi3zeoixnqskkpexmr3|Qinji 129-3]] as one of the Qin figures [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] sends to assist [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] in suppressing the anti-Qin rebels. The episode names him together with [[DongYiQin|董义]].

The source does not yet give Sima Xin independent decisions. His page therefore records him as part of the Qin recovery apparatus around Zhang Han: while [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]] is losing commanders and cohesion, Qin still has officials who can be added to the field effort.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-137-shendu-jiedu-zhiluweima-zhong-de-quanli-boyi-lgkjmddvrcelw2ltdwt92e4poguj|Qinji 137]] gives Sima Xin a decisive independent role. After [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] rebukes [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] for the post-[[JuluBattle|巨鹿之战]] defeats, Zhang Han sends Sima Xin back to [[Xianyang|咸阳]] to report and ask for instructions. Sima Xin waits at [[ZhaoGao|赵高]]'s gate for three days without audience, learns that Zhao Gao is preparing something dangerous, avoids Zhao Gao's pursuers, and returns to warn Zhang Han that the court has become unusable.

His advice reframes Zhang Han's military problem as a political survival trap. If Qin wins, Zhao Gao may envy and eliminate the successful commander; if Qin loses, defeat itself is punishable. Sima Xin therefore becomes the messenger who converts failed court access into the logic for surrender.
