# 周安王 / King An of Zhou

周安王 / King An of Zhou appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-2-lmfzo4sl9nd5kz3mrxdl1dl9efei]] as [[ZhouWeilieWang|周威烈王]]'s son and successor. The source says the next chronological year begins as King An's first year.

The page is currently a succession node for the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] Zhou-annals branch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-1-lvkp-ju7pf5z5cz3thojv3s2fkyx]] turns King An's early reign into a chronology frame for Qin-Wei war, Han/Zhao/Qin succession notices, [[WangziDing|王子定]]'s flight to [[JinState|晋国]], the Guo mountain disaster, and [[ZhengState|郑国]]'s crisis between Chu pressure and internal assassination.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]] continues that frame through King An's fifth to eighth years: a solar eclipse, [[NieZheng|聂政]]'s killing of [[XiaLei|侠累]], [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]]'s death, the Zheng ruler's murder, Song succession, one blank year, and [[FushuCity|负黍]]'s return to Han.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-3-lsbx8l7wxeioae2lwuwkkkutpdwn]] carries King An's ninth to fifteenth years through a state-formation bridge: [[JinState|晋国]]'s ruler is treated as a puppet, [[QinState|秦国]] attacks Han and likely Wei under lingering "Jin" labels, [[TianHe|田和]] moves [[QiKangGong|齐康公]] and seeks recognition, and the chronologically doubtful Wei notice sets up [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]] and [[WuQi|吴起]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-1-lq5r3xe-3y7al9nkbc8kyhntipll]] then develops the [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]] and [[WuQi|吴起]] West River dialogue, turning the reign frame from succession and assassination notices toward statecraft, virtue, terrain, and ministerial fit.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-2-lvaqlgyccrugfo0l-wuwf1mt1kcm]] continues King An's fifteenth and sixteenth years through a wider chronology: [[QinHuiGong|秦惠公]], [[ZhaoWuhou|赵武侯]], and [[HanLiehou|韩烈侯]] die; [[QinChugong|秦出公]], [[ZhaoJinghou|赵靖侯]], and [[HanWenhou|韩文侯]] succeed; and the Zhou court formally recognizes [[TianHe|田和]] in [[QiState|齐国]]. The same source also keeps King An's reign as the frame for Wu Qi's Chu reforms and the [[HanFei|韩非]] reading of Zhao Jinghou.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-3-ltca-ikuzo9ej7a0lfrgwv2-wulb]] continues King An's seventeenth to twenty-first years. The cluster includes the coup installing [[QinXianGong|秦献公]], [[QiState|齐国]] and [[HanState|韩国]] attacks, Tian-family Qi succession disorder through [[TianYan|田衍]] and [[TianQiHuanGong|田武]], a blank eighteenth year, [[WeiState|魏国]] defeating [[ZhaoState|赵国]] at Tutai, a solar eclipse read through [[CelestialOmenPoliticalResponsibility]], and [[WuQi|吴起]]'s death after [[ChuDaoWang|楚悼王]] dies.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl]] carries King An's reign from years 22-26: interstate war around Qi, Yan, Han, Zhao, Wei, and Chu; [[QiKangGong|齐康公]]'s death and Jiang-line Qi's end; [[JinState|晋国]]'s last succession and extinction; and King An's own death in 376 BCE, followed by [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]].
