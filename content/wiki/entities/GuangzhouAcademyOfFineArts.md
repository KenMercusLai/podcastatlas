# 广州美术学院 / Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts

## Overview
广州美术学院 / Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts is the art-school institution at the center of EP280's education-reporting case. The page tracks the school's source-scoped role in an undergraduate reform that sends first-year art students into social field sites as part of foundational art learning.

## Current Profile
In EP280, Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts appears less as a static institution than as a reform site. Its first-year curriculum has been reorganized around broad professional foundation courses, ending in a "method and practice" course where students enter antique markets, wet markets, urban villages, community spaces, family conversations, campus service work, and other concrete scenes. The school is presented as trying to reconnect art education with reality while still operating inside ordinary university constraints: grades, professional tracks, employment pressure, and uneven teacher preparation.

## Key Characteristics
- The school is using first-year reform to test whether art education can restore contact with lived reality before students specialize.
- The reform sends a large cohort into field sites, making observation, interview, handmade books, and documentary video part of the curriculum.
- Teacher retraining is part of the institutional change because many instructors also have to learn how to guide field inquiry.
- The school treats art practice as a way to perceive people and public problems, not only as studio technique or polished output.
- The reform remains institutionally bounded by grading rules, professional skill expectations, and later employment pressure.

- Field-site model: [[ep280-guangzhou-meiyuan-fangwenji-dang-meishusheng-kaishi-zuo-tianye-diaocha-lr4-jmpdo-oo7w8sdmcgcw-chqc9]] names brush sellers, antique markets, food markets, urban villages, cleaners, body models, square dancing, and community garbage notices as learning sites.
- Teacher retraining: [[ep280-guangzhou-meiyuan-fangwenji-dang-meishusheng-kaishi-zuo-tianye-diaocha-lr4-jmpdo-oo7w8sdmcgcw-chqc9]] says teachers received training before translating field methods into their own classrooms.
- Institutional boundary: [[ep280-guangzhou-meiyuan-fangwenji-dang-meishusheng-kaishi-zuo-tianye-diaocha-lr4-jmpdo-oo7w8sdmcgcw-chqc9]] records grading-curve pressure and the return to specialized professional coursework after the first-year reform space.

## Qualifications
The source is a reported podcast discussion rather than a longitudinal evaluation of learning outcomes. It provides rich cases and teacher-student reflections, but does not settle whether the reform is scalable across cohorts, durable after first year, or transferable to non-art universities.
