# Sony Magic Link

Sony Magic Link is the [[GeneralMagic]]-powered device discussed in [[we-almost-had-a-smartphone-in-the-90s-why-did-it-fail]]. The episode presents it as a chunky mini-tablet-like product launched in 1994 with functions such as faxing, check tracking, reading, and games.

The source's product lesson is that technical novelty did not create enough demand. The Magic Link cost about $800 in 1990s dollars, sold fewer than 3,000 units according to the episode, and still lacked the phone function implied by the broader smartphone-like vision.

In the wiki, the Magic Link is a compact case for [[PrematureEcosystemBuild]] and [[FeatureCreep]]: the device attempted a broad future before the surrounding customer habits, infrastructure, and product priorities were ready.
