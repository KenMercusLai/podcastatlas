# Hallidon Hill

## Overview

Hallidon Hill appears as Edward III's 1333 victory over Scottish forces, important in this source because it pushes David II toward France and activates the [[FrancoScottishAlliance|Franco-Scottish alliance]] as a pressure on Anglo-French diplomacy.

## Current Profile

The episode treats Hallidon Hill as more than a Scottish battlefield success. [[EdwardIIIOfEngland|Edward III]] supports the Balliol cause and responds when Scottish forces besiege Berwick. His victory is crushing, but the strategic effect is not simple security: David II flees to [[France]], and [[PhilipVIOfFrance|Philip VI]] must support him under the Franco-Scottish agreement.

The battle also foreshadows the military branch the episode leaves for later. The hosts mention the longbow's devastating use at Hallidon Hill while saving detailed discussion for the next episode.

## Key Characteristics

- 1333 English victory over Scottish forces in the episode's account.
- Connected to Edward III's support for the Balliol cause.
- Linked to Berwick and the Scottish succession/power struggle.
- Sends David II into French protection.
- Makes Scotland a direct Anglo-French diplomatic condition.
- Foreshadows the longbow-centered military explanation saved for later.
