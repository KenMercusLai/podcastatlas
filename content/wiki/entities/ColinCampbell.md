# Colin Campbell

Colin Campbell is the [[RedwoodMaterials]] chief technology officer interviewed in [[tech-20260129-0129-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260129-0129-mp-tech-pod-128]]. In the [[MarketplaceTech]] episode, he explains how reused EV battery packs can become grid-scale storage for data centers and why AI customers' urgency changes the power-infrastructure market.

His contribution is to make [[SecondLifeEVBatteryStorage]] an operating claim rather than only a sustainability idea. Campbell points to Redwood's [[Nevada]] project, says the local data center is disconnected from the grid, and frames battery storage paired with renewables as faster than some grid-interconnection or natural-gas turbine paths.
