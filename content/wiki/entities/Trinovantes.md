# Trinovantes

## Overview

The Trinovantes were a southeastern British polity whose rivalry with [[Cassivellaunus]] made them important allies of [[JuliusCaesar]] in 54 BC.

## Current Profile

The episode places the Trinovantes at the center of the second expedition's British politics. Their prince [[Mandubracius]] had fled after Cassivellaunus overthrew his father. When Caesar advanced, the Trinovantes submitted, requested Mandubracius's restoration, supplied provisions, and helped widen the coalition against Cassivellaunus.

Their role qualifies any simple Rome-versus-Britain narrative. The campaign could also be experienced as a British dynastic and inter-polity struggle in which Roman force changed the balance.

## Key Characteristics

- Southeastern British polity linked to cross-Channel diplomacy.
- Dynastic community represented by the exiled Mandubracius.
- Local ally that supplied Caesar and weakened Cassivellaunus.
- Example of British political plurality before Roman provincial rule.

## Evidence

- Dynastic conflict: [[499-the-roman-conquest-of-britain-julius-caesars-invasion-part-1-glt9770560726]] connects the Trinovantes to Mandubracius's exile after his father's defeat.
- Coalition politics: [[499-the-roman-conquest-of-britain-julius-caesars-invasion-part-1-glt9770560726]] describes their submission, provisioning, and role in attracting further British allies.
- Settlement: [[499-the-roman-conquest-of-britain-julius-caesars-invasion-part-1-glt9770560726]] makes Mandubracius's restoration and protection a condition imposed on Cassivellaunus.

## Qualifications

The episode relies chiefly on Caesar for this political sequence. “Tribe” can oversimplify changing leadership and affiliation, and the source does not establish how widely Mandubracius was supported or how the settlement affected ordinary members of the polity.

## What Changed

- Established the Trinovantes as active participants in the 54 BC coalition rather than a passive population encountered by Rome.

## Relationships

- [[Mandubracius]] - exiled prince restored as their ruler.
- [[Cassivellaunus]] - rival whose pressure pushed them toward Roman alliance.
- [[JuliusCaesar]] - foreign commander they provisioned and used against a local rival.
- [[CaesarsBritishExpeditions]] - intervention whose outcome depended on their alliance.
- [[RomanConquestOfBritain]] - later process shaped by the same pattern of British rivalry and Roman client politics.
