# Norwegian Oil Fund

The Norwegian Oil Fund is the sovereign savings institution discussed in [[how-to-beat-the-resource-curse-in-norway-summer-school]]. [[HildeBjornland]] says the fund is about $2.3 trillion, roughly $400,000 per Norwegian, while emphasizing that this does not mean the country can spend it all immediately.

In the episode, the fund works as a [[SovereignOilFundGovernance]] mechanism. It separates temporary oil revenue from current political spending pressure, preserves wealth for the future, and shows how [[Norway]] converted oil income into a broader public balance-sheet asset rather than only near-term consumption.
