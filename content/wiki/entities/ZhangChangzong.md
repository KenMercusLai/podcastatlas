## Overview

Zhang Changzong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-536-3-jiemi-shangguan-waner-he-ta-beihou-de-nanrenmen-lqj7rpwwfllogrxrbav-xqwwnnnm|Hanji 536-3]] as the palace-intimacy figure whose relationship with [[ShangguanWaner|上官婉儿]] triggers punishment by [[WuZetian|武则天]]. The episode uses him to show how sexual access, information, safety, and bodily discipline intersect in the court.

## Current Profile

The source identifies Zhang Changzong as Wu Zetian's male favorite. It says Shangguan Wan'er became involved with him out of a desire for greater security or power and access to favor and information. When Wu Zetian discovered the relationship, Zhang was soon released while Shangguan Wan'er was punished with facial marking; the later red-plum makeup tradition is presented as Shangguan Wan'er's adaptive transformation of that scar.

## Key Characteristics

- Male favorite of Wu Zetian in the episode's court-intimacy branch.
- Provides a route to favor, information, and security for Shangguan Wan'er.
- Triggers an asymmetric punishment episode when Wu Zetian discovers the relationship.
- Functions as a case where sexual politics and administrative survival overlap.
- Connected to the red-plum makeup story through Shangguan Wan'er's marked face.
