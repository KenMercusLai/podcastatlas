# Yuri Andropov / 尤里·安德罗波夫

Yuri Andropov / 尤里·安德罗波夫 appears in [[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] as the Soviet leader tied to the launch of [[OperationRYAN]]. The episode says he warned intelligence officials that the [[UnitedStates]] might be preparing a first nuclear strike against the [[SovietUnion]], turning leadership fear into a global KGB collection task.

Andropov's role in the wiki is therefore less biographical than institutional. He represents the moment when strategic anxiety becomes bureaucratic demand, making [[ColdWarNuclearMisperception]] and [[AdministrativeOpacityAsControl]] mutually reinforcing: information is gathered to satisfy power's fear rather than to test whether the fear is true.
