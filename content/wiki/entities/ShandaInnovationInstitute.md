# Shanda Innovation Institute / 盛大创新院

Shanda Innovation Institute / 盛大创新院 appears in [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]] as [[Shanda|盛大]]'s high-talent research and incubation organization launched after the company's game success. [[ChenDanian]] is presented as a central figure, and the source lists projects including Bambook, cloud storage, WebOS-like work, check-in products, and [[WiFiMasterKey|Wi-Fi 万能钥匙]].

The institute is important because it sharpens the episode's [[FounderVisionTimingMismatch]] theme. Shanda had enough cash, ambition, and engineers to explore many future-looking products, but the parent company's platform position and execution system were not always able to turn early prototypes into enduring standalone businesses.
