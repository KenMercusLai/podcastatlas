# L'Oreal for the Future / 欧莱雅为明天

L'Oreal for the Future / 欧莱雅为明天 is the [[LOreal|L'Oreal / 欧莱雅]] sustainability vision referenced in [[lvdian-xiaoyang-yao-tuiguang-tihuanxin-de-ba-meizhuang-chongzuo-yibian-1006757937]]. The source says L'Oreal had already made clearer sustainability commitments from 2013 and then further specified targets through this 2020 vision.

In the episode, the program matters less as corporate messaging than as the strategic backdrop for refillable products. [[RefillableBeautyPackaging]] becomes easier to sustain when the company can treat packaging reduction as part of product design, operations, distribution, and long-term brand value rather than as an isolated cost line.
