# DMT

DMT appears in [[a-trip-to-the-magic-mushroom-megachurch]] as one of the psychoactive substances available as sacrament at [[ZydorChurch]], including through vape pens. The source says Dave gives a sample donation amount of about $100 depending on the product, making DMT part of the episode's [[SacramentDonationModel]] and legal-risk analysis.

In the episode, DMT also connects Zydor to the broader legal history of ayahuasca churches. The 2006 [[SupremeCourt]] ayahuasca decision is presented as a precedent signal for [[ReligiousControlledSubstanceExemption]], even as the source warns that newer churches remain exposed without a granted exemption.
