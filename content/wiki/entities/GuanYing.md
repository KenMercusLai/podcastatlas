# 灌婴 / Guan Ying

灌婴 / Guan Ying enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-144-xiangyu-jituan-fumie-de-yuanyin-1-lr0ft8-jupxgvutn6c89mfs8r4wb|Hanji 144]] as one of [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s officers who backs an immediate attack on [[XiangYu|项羽]] after Liu Bang is made Han king in [[Hanzhong|汉中]]. The episode groups him with [[ZhouBo|周勃]] and [[FanKuai|樊哙]] as part of the military faction whose anger must be acknowledged before [[XiaoHe|萧何]] can make retreat acceptable.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-148-1-hanxin-yupan-le-zhanghan-de-yupan-lodhpuxpossjno87gorjkfrtitbe|Hanji 148-1]] gives Guan Ying an active role in [[HanXin|韩信]]'s Three Qin operation. He is sent out by the Ziwu route to pressure [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]]'s left-side attention, then enters [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] after the central breakthrough. The episode also highlights his cavalry talent, calling him a major commander of long-range cavalry movement and linking him to the seizure of Liyang after Zhang Han's position breaks.
