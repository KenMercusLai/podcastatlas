## Overview
恒大足校 / Evergrande Football School is the youth-football institution treated by the source as the most tangible surviving asset of [[Evergrande]]'s football project. The episode says [[GuangzhouSportUniversity|广州体育学院]] acquired the school for 600 million yuan and kept students, elite teams, operations, coaches, and daily life running.

## Current Profile
The school's profile is mixed. It began with a scale-first "万人足校" impulse consistent with [[XuJiayin]] and Evergrande's maximum-size style, and [[PanWeili]] criticizes that early model as poorly matched to elite football training. Yet the same source says later age groups benefited from more standardized, professional selection and are now producing players across Chinese professional leagues.

The transfer to Guangzhou Sport University changes the school's risk profile. Instead of remaining a distressed developer asset or falling to a non-football holder, the academy becomes tied to an education institution that can provide schooling, possible higher-education routes, and continuity for families.

## Key Characteristics
- Surviving academy asset from Evergrande's football project after the property group's collapse.
- Early mass-scale experiment whose "largest school" logic is criticized as unsuitable for elite football development.
- Later youth-development pipeline where the source says selection standards, process, and professionalism improved.
- Education-linked institution after acquisition by [[GuangzhouSportUniversity]], with primary, secondary, high-school, and possible university pathways.
- Player-supply channel for professional and semi-professional teams, including [[GuangdongChenxingJuli]].

## Evidence
- Asset transfer: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] says Guangzhou Sport University bought the school for 600 million yuan and replaced Evergrande signage with university marks.
- Operational continuity: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] says 400-plus students, age groups, elite squads, managers, operators, and coaching staff were retained.
- Training-model qualification: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] says early student intake often lacked clear football foundation or static talent, while later cohorts improved after selection professionalized.
- Talent output: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] names players such as Bunyamin, Zhang Hongfu, Liu Jiale, Zhang Zhixiong, and Xu Bin as signs that the academy's output is becoming visible.

## Qualifications
The source does not prove the school's long-term financial stability, graduation outcomes, or professional conversion rate. It also treats possible team migrations or mergers around [[GuangdongChenxingJuli]] as speculative.
