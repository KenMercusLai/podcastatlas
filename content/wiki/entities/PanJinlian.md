# 潘金莲 / Pan Jinlian

[[fanwai-15-banlatte-jiang-jinpingmei-la-erqie-haishi-luse-de-991508433]] presents 潘金莲 as one of the title figures of [[JinPingMei|《金瓶梅》]] and as a character whose social path matters before moral judgment hardens. The source traces her from a poor tailor's family through sale into wealthier households, skill training, coercive sexual attention from 张大户, forced marriage to [[WuDalang|武大郎]], and a later fall into constrained shopfront life.

Her dissatisfaction is therefore tied to status loss, bodily comparison, talent, coercion, and limited choices, not only to desire. The episode then follows her attraction to [[WuSong|武松]], his rejection, her retaliatory accusation, and the later accident that lets [[XimenQing|西门庆]] notice her from the street. This makes her a key figure for [[ClassicReadingComplexity]], because the source resists both excuse and flattening.
