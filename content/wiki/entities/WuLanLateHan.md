## Overview

[[WuLanLateHan|吴兰]] is a Liu-side commander whose separated and weaker force is selected as the opening target of Cao Hong's response to the Hanzhong offensive.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1161 places Wu Lan at Xiabian with [[ZhangFei|张飞]] and [[MaChao|马超]], but says the three forces are stationed separately. [[CaoHong|曹洪]] avoids a direct attack on the more formidable pair and concentrates against Wu Lan. Wu Lan seeks help, is defeated, and is reported killed, making his case a warning about local vulnerability inside a wider multi-unit plan.

## Key Characteristics

- Liu-side field commander in the opening Hanzhong phase.
- Leader of the comparatively weaker separated wing.
- Target chosen through relative-force comparison rather than random contact.
- Defeated before distributed allied strength becomes effective concentration.
