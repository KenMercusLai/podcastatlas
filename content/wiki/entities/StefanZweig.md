# Stefan Zweig / 茨威格

Stefan Zweig / 茨威格 is the Austrian Jewish writer whose memoir [[TheWorldOfYesterday|《昨日的世界》 / The World of Yesterday]] structures [[139-cong-zuo-ri-de-shi-jie-dao-ru-jin-de-ou-zhou-mei-hao-nian-dai-belle-epoque-ta-hai-neng-hui-lai-ma-ltvsgov8ht9-l7yp9qamdgjmyrf]]. The episode presents him as a witness of pre-World War I European cultural confidence, wartime rupture, nationalism, exile, and the destruction of a world he had experienced as unusually open.

The source keeps his cosmopolitanism double-edged. Zweig's [[Vienna]] cultural world, education, multilingual network, and friendship with figures such as [[RomainRolland|Romain Rolland / 罗曼·罗兰]] support the episode's anti-nationalist reading, but his access to that world also depended on wealth, family background, Jewish bourgeois status, and cultural capital. His later exile through the [[UnitedKingdom]] and [[Brazil]] turns the remembered [[BelleEpoque|Belle Epoque]] from a golden age into evidence of how fragile elite mobility can be when politics turns violent.
