# Lee C. Camp

## Overview

Lee C. Camp is the theologian and host of [[NoSmallEndeavor]] who interviews [[MalcolmGladwell]] about curiosity, storytelling, happiness, failure, and committed work.

## Current Profile

Camp frames the conversation as an inquiry into the experiences and habits behind Gladwell's public work rather than an assessment of his published arguments alone. A *Ted Lasso* scene supplies the opening method: curiosity can interrupt shallow judgment by asking what history or experience made another person intelligible.

His questions connect personal biography to moral philosophy. He contrasts rule-centered ethics with story-shaped character, introduces Aristotelian and Thomistic accounts of educated desire, and proposes that a happy life may be one a person can accept in its entirety rather than one stripped of difficulty.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses curiosity to move from public persona toward formative experience.
- Connects personal narrative with theological and philosophical questions about moral formation.
- Tests happiness as educated desire and whole-life acceptance rather than continuous positive feeling.
- Lets a guest's concrete memories carry abstract questions about vocation, failure, and responsibility.
