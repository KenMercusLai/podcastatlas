# 李晨阳 / Li Chenyang

## Overview
李晨阳 / Li Chenyang is the Shanghai Putuo notary discussed in the [[BuHeShiYi|不合时宜]] episode about [[FrontWave2Documentary|《前浪2》]].

## Current Profile
Li's profile in the wiki is source-scoped to his appointed-guardianship and elder-property cases as presented through the documentary discussion. He becomes a practical bridge between law, family change, and care: his work involves handling lonely elders' affairs, checking estate rules, mediating undocumented wishes, and considering how new legal arrangements can fit nontraditional care relationships. The source treats him less as a courtroom figure than as a frontline institutional actor adapting formal law to aging-city realities.

## Key Characteristics
- Works around appointed guardianship and elder affairs in a Shanghai notary-office context.
- Makes the tension between legal form, lived intention, inheritance rules, and care relationships visible.
- Handles cases where non-kin carers, absent children, solitary elders, and older adults' property choices do not fit traditional family assumptions.
- Frames decisions around reasonableness for each person rather than only around moral suspicion.
- Represents an institutional response to urban aging, vacant homes, and decision-maker gaps.
