# Ferrari Luce

Ferrari Luce is the first Ferrari electric vehicle as described in [[ferrari-1]]. The source says [[Ferrari]] developed it with [[LoveFrom]], [[JonyIve]], and [[MarcNewson]], and revealed internal tactile elements before revealing the exterior.

The source treats the Luce as a strategic test. Electric performance makes raw speed less scarce, so Ferrari has to make the experience emotionally distinctive through design, tactility, heritage, and [[FunctionalAlibi]].
