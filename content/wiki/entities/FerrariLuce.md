# Ferrari Luce

## Overview
Ferrari Luce is presented by the bounded sources as [[Ferrari]]'s first electric vehicle and a strategic test of whether the brand can create emotional distinction when high electric performance is increasingly common.

## Current Profile
[[ferrari-1]] places the product inside Ferrari's broader luxury system: racing heritage, scarcity, design, tactility, and [[FunctionalAlibi]] must do more work when raw acceleration is no longer exclusive. [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-debt-spiral-or-new-golden-age-super-bowl-insider-trading-booming-token-budgets-ferraris-new-ev-40104725]] adds source-attributed specifications, an expected May 2026 launch, a glass key, physical controls, and the autonomy question. Together they make Luce less a simple drivetrain change than a test of whether manual driving can remain a desirable luxury experience.

## Key Characteristics
- First Ferrari battery-electric vehicle in the sources' description.
- Developed with design involvement from [[LoveFrom]], [[JonyIve]], and [[MarcNewson]].
- Uses tactile controls and material detail to resist an all-screen interior.
- Reportedly combines four motors, more than 1,000 horsepower, sub-2.5-second acceleration, and roughly 330 miles of range.
- Tests whether brand experience can remain scarce as electric performance and autonomous transport spread.

## Evidence
- Brand-strategy evidence: [[ferrari-1]] treats Luce as the EV expression of Ferrari's dream, heritage, design, and emotional-distinction problem.
- Product evidence: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-debt-spiral-or-new-golden-age-super-bowl-insider-trading-booming-token-budgets-ferraris-new-ev-40104725]] supplies the episode-reported launch timing, performance, range, glass key, and tactile-control details.
- Autonomy boundary: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-debt-spiral-or-new-golden-age-super-bowl-insider-trading-booming-token-budgets-ferraris-new-ev-40104725]] debates whether [[Waymo]]-style autonomy, insurance costs, and full self-driving will make manual driving a smaller luxury niche.

## Qualifications
The launch timing, exterior expectations, power, acceleration, and range are episode-reported and not independently verified here. The sources disagree only in emphasis: Acquired asks whether Ferrari can preserve emotional distinction, while All-In adds subjective reactions to an expected exterior and broader speculation about autonomous driving.
