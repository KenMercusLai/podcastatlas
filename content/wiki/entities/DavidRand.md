# David Rand

## Overview
David Rand is a Cornell researcher cited by [[MarketplaceTech]] for experimental work on political persuasion through conversational AI.

## Current Profile
In the episode, Rand describes a purpose-built chatbot tested before the 2024 U.S. presidential election. An average conversation lasted about six minutes and shifted some participants' stated preferences between [[KamalaHarris]] and [[DonaldTrump]] in both directions. He characterizes the measured movement as roughly three to four times the effect associated with watching a standard political advertisement.

Rand's role in the wiki is therefore narrow and evidence-bound: he supplies a research claim that interactive, information-rich dialogue may produce stronger short-term political persuasion than a conventional ad. The episode does not provide enough methodological detail to establish durability, population-level electoral impact, or equivalence to ordinary use of [[ChatGPT]], [[Claude]], or [[Gemini]].

## Key Characteristics
- Rand studies how conversational systems can affect political attitudes.
- His reported 2024 experiment used a purpose-built chatbot rather than an unspecified commercial assistant.
- The reported movement occurred toward both major presidential candidates rather than in only one partisan direction.
- He interprets interactive access to substantial policy information as one possible reason for the measured effect.
- His result is presented as a comparative experimental effect, not as proof of real-world election outcome change.
