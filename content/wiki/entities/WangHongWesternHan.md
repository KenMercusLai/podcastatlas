## Overview

王洪 / Wang Hong (Western Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-601-1-chule-yuhui-tade-wenhua-shuiping-ye-neng-gaiming-lnt4p2mo2nwfm3xqhg0dmfwiip1n|Hanji 601-1]] as a late [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] palace official connected by marriage to [[XiaoXianWesternHan|萧贤]] and present near [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] and [[DongXian|董贤]]. His profile centers on two linked acts: he diplomatically helps Xiao Xian avoid a dangerous Dong-family marriage, then directly blocks Aidi's banquet remark about imitating Yao's abdication to Shun.

## Current Profile

The source first presents Wang Hong as the intermediary in a marriage proposal. Dong Xian's father wants Dong Kuanxin to marry Xiao Xian's daughter and asks Wang Hong, Xiao Xian's son-in-law, to transmit the proposal. After Xiao Xian explains that the phrase "云止其中" in Dong-family appointment language signals dangerous abdication-coded favor, Wang Hong understands the warning and refuses the match without naming the political reason.

Wang Hong's more public act occurs at Qilin Hall. When Aidi drunkenly says he intends to imitate Yao yielding to Shun with Dong Xian, Wang Hong speaks before Dong Xian can answer. He argues that the empire belongs to Gaozu's dynastic line, that Aidi should transmit it through the ancestral line, and that the Son of Heaven cannot speak casually about succession. Aidi reacts by expelling him from palace attendance.

After [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] later apologizes with Wang Hong and Aidi recalls him, Wang Hong submits a longer criticism of Dong Xian. The memorial says Three Excellencies office requires real merit and virtue, contrasts Dong Xian with earlier favorites who were not placed so high, attacks Dong-family enrichment and promotion, and warns through the Bao Si analogy that favorite-driven disorder must not become a precedent.

## Key Characteristics

- Palace-adjacent official whose family tie to Xiao Xian gives him access to private risk interpretation before public crisis.
- Diplomatic intermediary who avoids exposing Xiao Xian's political warning while refusing the Dong-family marriage proposal.
- Direct remonstrator who interrupts Aidi's abdication remark before Dong Xian can answer.
- Dynastic-boundary defender who states that the realm is Gaozu's realm rather than the emperor's private property.
- Memorial writer who criticizes Dong Xian's office, military command, family reward, fiscal burden, and lack of merit.
