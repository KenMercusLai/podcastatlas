# webOS

## Overview
webOS appears in the No.229 [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] source as the Palm successor platform that gave [[ZhiJiWang|主机网]] its peak expectations and then its major strategic disappointment.

## Current Profile
The episode frames webOS as a promising but failed platform ecology. 老麦 says HP once hoped to connect computers, printers, phones, and tablets through webOS, and 主机网 expected domestic device entry, possible official cooperation, and a continuing developer ecosystem.

The turning point is HP's abandonment of webOS hardware. The source presents this as a practical shock for users, developers, and the community: even committed developers could continue only temporarily, and the lack of [[WeChat]] support made the platform increasingly impossible for ordinary Chinese smartphone use.

## Key Characteristics
- It is framed as an ecosystem-level bet across multiple device categories, not only a phone OS.
- It gave 主机网 a short-lived growth peak and expectation of official collaboration.
- Its hardware cancellation turned a community growth path into a maintenance and morale problem.
- It still supported committed developers long enough for donations and software-store experiments.
- Its China viability was weakened by missing essential social applications such as WeChat.
