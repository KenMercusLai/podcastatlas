# 仁科 / Renke

## Overview
Renke is represented as a songwriter, performer, and member of [[WutiaorenBand|五条人]] whose public artistic identity grew from [[HaifengGuangdong|海丰]] childhood, migration to [[Guangzhou|广州]], street trade, informal music networks, and collaboration with [[AMaoWutiaoren|阿茂]].

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents Renke less as a career planner than as an artist who repeatedly converted immediate circumstances into practice. Family business failure, inexpensive books, shared records, improvised instruments, book and cutout-disc sales, record-store activity, low-resource performances, and local speech supplied material and competence before they became a named aesthetic. He attributes much of early Wutiaoren songwriting to A Mao, while describing his own later lyric work, stage presence, popular-song experiments, and interest in unfinished Haifeng-centered material.

Mainstream recognition after 《乐队的夏天》 changed scale and privacy: larger audiences, street recognition, variety shows, and criticism from some earlier listeners entered the band's life. Renke nonetheless resists a simple sellout narrative, presenting participation, onstage song choice, and later experimentation as situational decisions. His comments on old-song arrangements show strong present-tense artistic autonomy moderated by recognition that ignoring listeners completely can become extreme.

## Key Characteristics
- Songwriter and performer whose artistic vocabulary draws on Haifeng speech, street life, small commerce, and visual vernacular.
- Contingency-led creator who describes major choices as responses to concrete situations rather than a master plan.
- Collaborator whose musical history is inseparable from A Mao and the formation of Wutiaoren.
- Stage personality able to move between humor, anecdote, seriousness, and music.
- Artist negotiating wider fame, television work, older-fan expectations, and continued experimentation.
- Source-scoped AI optimist who predicts eventual machine superiority in composition without supplying evidence or timing.

## Qualifications
This profile rests on one long-form interview summarized rather than transcribed verbatim. Memories, chronology, authorship proportions, health details, audience response, private motives, and future plans are self-reported. The source does not provide a complete discography, independent testimony from A Mao, or measured evidence for Renke's AI forecast.
