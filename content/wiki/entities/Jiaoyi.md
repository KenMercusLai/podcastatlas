# 焦邑 / Jiaoyi

焦邑 / Jiaoyi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] as one of the two [[WeiState|魏国]] strongholds [[QinState|秦国]] pressures after Wei has already ceded [[Yinjin|殷晋]]. Qin surrounds Jiaoyi and [[QuwoWarringStates|曲沃]] in 330 BCE, and the episode says Jiaoyi is eventually taken in 329 BCE.

The source keeps Jiaoyi important through its later return. After Qin has gained from repeated coercion and from [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s [[PuyangWarringStates|蒲阳]] maneuver, it returns Jiaoyi and Quwo to Wei, making the place part of the episode's closing puzzle about why Qin sometimes gives back captured territory.
