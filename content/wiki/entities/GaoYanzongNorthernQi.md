## Overview

高延宗 / Gao Yanzong enters the wiki as a Northern Qi royal figure whose capture, humiliation, and reported death by pepper provide one of Hanji 331-3's historical “吞椒自尽” examples.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-331-3-guren-jingran-yong-huajiao-zisha-lohtg2tifzlc63cxkcjkekoyk9w3|Hanji 331-3]] identifies Gao Yanzong as a younger brother of the Prince of Lanling, says he was briefly raised to the imperial position for two days during the conflict involving Gao Wei and Yuwen Yong, and reports that Yuwen Yong captured and humiliated him. The episode then attributes his death at age thirty-four to “以椒塞口而死.”

The profile is deliberately narrow. The source uses Gao's fate to connect alleged pepper suicide with military defeat, lost political identity, and humiliation; it does not supply enough historical or toxicological evidence to settle the chronology, agency, or physiological cause of death.

## Key Characteristics

- Northern Qi royal identified by the source as the Prince of Lanling's younger brother.
- Briefly elevated as emperor during a military and dynastic crisis.
- Captured and subjected to humiliation in the source's account.
- Reported to have died at age thirty-four by filling his mouth with pepper.
- Functions in the episode as a case of suicide associated with defeat and political-status collapse.

## Qualifications
