# 《老妓抄》 / Rogisho

《老妓抄》 / Rogisho is the [[OkamotoKanoko|冈本加乃子 / Okamoto Kanoko]] short-story collection discussed in [[08-laojichao-laoji-busi-shenzhi-buzeng-diaoling-541092994]]. The episode treats the book as aesthetically complete but demanding: its stories often avoid strong plot mechanics and instead depend on atmosphere, emotional restraint, gendered power, Buddhist feeling, and reader patience.

The source discusses several stories inside the collection. 《鲤鱼》 makes desire and Buddhist transformation inseparable; 《蝙蝠》 turns motherly possessiveness into a subtle psychological relation; 《仲夏夜之梦》 lets a brief romantic encounter become a treasured marital memory; the title story uses an old retired geisha and the young repairman Ugi to expose dream, dependence, patronage, and self-deception; 《五妓》, 《寿司》, and 《东海道》 broaden the collection into food, travel, modernity, and the life cycle of women attached to geisha culture.

## Key Claims
- The collection's weakness for some readers is also its literary method: it can feel "too light" because plot is not the main engine.
- The title story is a key case for [[GenderedPatronageInversion]], reversing the familiar male-patron and young-woman structure.
- The arrangement of stories matters. The episode reads 《老妓抄》 and 《五妓》 as structural echoes across age, innocence, sadness, and experience.
- The book extends [[NonInstrumentalLiteraryReading]] because the hosts say its value cannot be recovered from plot summary alone.
