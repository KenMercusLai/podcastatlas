# Ulster Defence Association

The Ulster Defence Association appears in [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] as a loyalist defense group founded in autumn 1971 amid fear of civil war. The episode says the UDA later had between 25,000 and 40,000 associated members at its peak and was responsible for more than 400 murders, mostly of Catholic civilians.

In the wiki, the UDA extends the loyalist branch beyond the earlier [[UlsterVolunteerForce|UVF]]. It shows how communal fear and vigilante organization could scale after [[InternmentWithoutTrial]], IRA attacks, and British policy failures made the conflict feel existential to many Protestants.
