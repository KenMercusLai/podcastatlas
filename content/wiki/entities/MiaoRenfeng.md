## Overview

苗人凤 / Miao Renfeng is a major martial figure in [[FeihuWaizhuan|《飞狐外传》]] and [[XueshanFeihu|《雪山飞狐》]]. The bounded sources combine his trust, restraint, courage, and honor with a violent approach to ending inherited hatred and difficulty making love emotionally intelligible.

## Current Profile

Miao Renfeng's duel with 胡一刀 belongs to the causal chain of Hu Fei's father's death, but the source rejects a simple equation between that fact and intentional murder. Hu Fei lacks the complete truth and nevertheless discovers through direct contact that Miao cannot be treated as an ordinary villain or target.

Miao's stature appears most clearly under vulnerability. Blinded and attacked, he trusts Hu Fei on the strength of a sincere statement, accepts help, and later entrusts his daughter to him. He can spare the direct agent of his poisoning, face the possibility of Hu Fei's revenge without evasion, and teach martial timing through an ordinary act such as picking up cabbage. Injury and failed marriage do not erase his composure.

The relationship therefore turns enemy classification into moral encounter. Hu Fei's belief that Miao would share mortal danger with him does not resolve the death or eliminate accountability, but it makes mechanical vengeance inadequate.

The fuller account of the duel clarifies that [[HuYidao|胡一刀]] died from poison placed on a weapon through Tian Guinong's plot rather than from Miao's deliberate murder. Miao insists on single combat and rejects collective treachery, but his plan to end the four-family feud remains paradoxical: he refuses to teach his daughter martial arts and intends no disciple succession, yet still imagines one last lethal settlement as the route to peace.

His domestic profile is less successful. The episode reads his repeated praise of Hu Yidao's wife before Nan Lan as evidence that sincere love can fail when it does not recognize the form of care the other person needs. When Nan Lan leaves with Tian Guinong, Miao neither kills nor forcibly retrieves them; that restraint matters, but it does not undo the earlier emotional distance. His educated daughter [[MiaoRuolan|苗若兰]] becomes a possible cross-family bridge that his own generation could not complete.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines exceptional martial reputation with trust and composure under vulnerability.
- Belongs to the causal history of Hu Yidao's death without being reduced to deliberate murder.
- Trusts Hu Fei before possessing complete information about his identity and motives.
- Shows restraint toward a person involved in his poisoning.
- Faces possible revenge openly rather than manipulating Hu Fei's ignorance.
- Teaches through practical perception as well as formal martial authority.
- Attempts to terminate the feud through non-transmission and a final duel, combining restraint with unresolved violence.

## Qualifications

The profile reflects two podcast literary interpretations rather than direct textual collation. Trust, magnanimity, martial parity, marriage psychology, implied willingness to die beside Hu Fei, and the moral meaning of specific scenes remain interpretive. The poisoned-weapon account separates Miao from deliberate murder without making lethal dueling or his approach to inherited hatred beyond criticism.

## What Changed

- Added the poisoned-weapon reconstruction that separates honorable combat from Tian Guinong's plot.
- Added his paradoxical attempt to end revenge through both non-transmission and one final duel.
- Expanded the profile to emotional inarticulacy, marital restraint, and his daughter's cross-family role.
