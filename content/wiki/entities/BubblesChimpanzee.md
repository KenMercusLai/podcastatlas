# Bubbles the Chimpanzee

## Overview

Bubbles is a chimpanzee whose life with Michael Jackson made him an unusually visible example of the celebrity primate as pet, performer, publicity symbol, and later sanctuary resident.

## Current Profile

The episode says Jackson acquired Bubbles at eight months old from a Texas research facility. At Neverland he reportedly slept in Jackson's room, wore a diaper, ate at the table, watched films, and traveled publicly, including a visit to Japan.

The same story that humanized Bubbles also exposed the limits of domestic celebrity life for a maturing chimpanzee. He later left Jackson's household, went to a trainer in 2003, and moved to the Center for Great Apes in Florida in 2005.

## Key Characteristics

- Chimpanzee acquired in infancy from a research-facility setting.
- Integrated into a celebrity household through strongly humanized routines.
- Became part of Jackson's public image and international publicity.
- Maturation made the pet arrangement unsustainable.
- Later moved into specialist sanctuary care.

## Qualifications

The episode supplies a concise popular account, not a full welfare or veterinary history. Reports about Bubbles's preferences and emotional state should be read as interpretation unless independently documented.
