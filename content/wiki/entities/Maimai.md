# 脉脉 / Maimai

Maimai / 脉脉 appears in [[no-214-xunzhao-tonglei-xiaohongshu-bilibili-yiji-wuhuabamen-de-naxie-shequ-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-26-1005977305]] as a workplace-networking and anonymous-workplace-community product founded by [[LinFanMaimai|林凡]]. The episode says it initially resembled a Chinese [[LinkedIn]] with professional identity and second-degree connections.

Its stronger community layer came from workplace anonymous discussion. The source says Maimai's anonymous area became a daily-use source and a media-leak channel, but also drew governance pressure over rumors, insults, defamation, and privacy.
