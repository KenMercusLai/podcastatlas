# 大伦丁老师 / 大仑丁 (hypertension guest)

## Overview
大伦丁老师 is the source-scoped medical-education guest in a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode about younger-adult hypertension, home measurement, medication continuity, follow-up, complications, and lifestyle support.

## Current Profile
The available source presents 大伦丁老师 as the explanatory clinical voice beside host [[Atang|阿汤]] and patient guest [[XiaoLeiHypertensionGuest|小磊]]. The guest explains how chronic tension can become normalized, why symptom absence does not prove control, how home blood-pressure technique affects readings, why antihypertensive medication should not be stopped casually, and how long-term vascular pressure can damage multiple organs.
