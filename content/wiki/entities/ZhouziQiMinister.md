# 周子 / Zhouzi (Qi minister)

周子 / Zhouzi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-2-lvqctrazn8ddkcnp-lhspzzmzmns]] as the [[QiState|齐国]] minister who argues that Qi should lend grain to [[ZhaoState|赵国]] during [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]]. His core warning is strategic as well as moral: Zhao protects Qi and [[ChuState|楚国]] like a shield or lips protecting teeth, so allowing Zhao to collapse will bring future danger closer.

The source makes Zhouzi a failed remonstrance case. [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]] and [[JunWangHou|君王后]] still refuse Zhao's grain request, preferring short-term non-intervention over preserving an anti-Qin buffer. Zhouzi therefore anchors [[ShieldStateRescueFailure|屏障国救援失灵]] rather than a successful rescue pattern.
