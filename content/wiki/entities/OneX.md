# 1X

## Overview
1X is the home-humanoid robotics company represented in the All-In robotics special by founder and CEO Bernt Bornich. The episode positions the company around Neo, a household robot and open robotics platform.

## Current Profile
1X's source-scoped profile is a high-variance consumer and developer-platform bet. The episode says the company plans a limited 2026 Neo launch, expects early roughness, may rely on teleoperation or system guidance, and wants outside developers and model builders to use the platform.

## Key Characteristics
- 1X targets home humanoids rather than only industrial or warehouse robots, making household safety, trust, privacy, and general usefulness central.
- Neo is presented as both a product and an open platform with an operating system, fleet management, data-collection tools, and fine-tuning support.
- Teleoperation is framed as a bridge to autonomy and a useful remote-presence mode rather than only a fallback.
- The company's data strategy leans on human-like embodiment so internet and human video can become more relevant to robot training.
- The episode's 2026 launch and preorder claims are ambitious but explicitly paired with expectations that early robots will be imperfect.
- Bernt Bornich's hard-takeoff forecast makes 1X the most aggressive timeline voice in the episode.

## Qualifications
1X's preorder count, payment model, 2026 shipping, autonomy level, and hard-takeoff timeline are source-scoped claims. The same episode says early robots may fall and may still need human or system assistance, so the wiki treats 1X's home-humanoid promise as unresolved rather than as established product-market fit.
