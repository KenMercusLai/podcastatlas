# 孟京辉 / Meng Jinghui

## Overview
孟京辉 / Meng Jinghui is a Chinese theatre director represented through one long-form autobiographical interview as a modernist experimenter, rehearsal-room collaborator, repertoire builder, and festival organizer.

## Current Profile
Meng traces his formation from literature and the concentrated arrival of translated modernist culture in the 1980s into Central Academy of Drama experiments and small-theatre work during the 1990s theatre-market slump. The account makes “rebellion” practical rather than merely stylistic: blocked or improvised productions, peer argument, international viewing, and repeated work with actors became ways to discover what theatre could express.

His mature practice combines aesthetic authority with increasing willingness to absorb collaborators' ideas and admit error in rehearsal. 《恋爱的犀牛》 is both an artistic landmark and a durable economic base; its continuing casts require renewed directing rather than mechanical reproduction. Festival work expands the same commitment from individual productions to the conditions in which creators and audiences can meet.

## Key Characteristics
- Director shaped by literature, absurdism, modernism, film, music, peer exchange, and resistance to inherited theatrical conventions.
- Rehearsal-centered practitioner who treats performance as revisable and increasingly describes directing as collective trial plus final integration.
- Aesthetic decision-maker who uses poetic distance, fragmentation, comedy, and non-classical form without claiming incomprehensibility as a goal.
- Repertoire builder whose long-running productions create continuity across actor generations and provide economic support for experimentation.
- Festival organizer who treats venues, visiting work, young creators, audiences, and informal encounter as one cultural ecology.
- Source-scoped autobiographical figure whose broader production history and collaborators' perspectives are not established by the current evidence inventory.
