# 叫兽易小星 / Jiaoshou Yi Xiaoxing

## Overview
叫兽易小星 / Jiaoshou Yi Xiaoxing is an early Chinese web-video creator and director whose wiki profile now connects the older UGC-to-professional path with a current AI-filmmaking practitioner view.

## Current Profile
The Banlatte China-internet source places 易小星 among creators who moved from online shorts and platform-native humor toward larger professional entertainment work. That makes him part of [[UGCToProfessionalCreatorPipeline]]: early distribution, audience testing, and low-cost video tools helped creators move from amateur or semi-professional internet video into film, animation, and web variety.

His current AI-filmmaking stance continues that tool history. In discussing [[NvwaZhiSi|《女娲之死》]], 易小星 frames AI as another step after digital cameras, phones, and short-video tools lowered the cost of making images. He is excited by the possibility that one creator can realize more ideas directly, but he keeps the creative bottleneck in story, taste, audience emotion, comedy timing, source verification, and final responsibility rather than in tool access alone.

## Key Characteristics
- Early web-video creator associated with the movement from platform-native online video into professional entertainment.
- Uses his own history of solo and small-team creation to understand why AI video feels powerful for individual creators.
- Treats AI as a production and ideation tool rather than as an automatic author.
- Emphasizes audience emotion, story, aesthetic judgment, and the creator's mind over the specific tool used.
- Keeps AI-film optimism qualified by comedy timing, editability, integration with live footage, hallucination, and responsibility limits.
