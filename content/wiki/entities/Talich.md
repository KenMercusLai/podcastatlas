# Talich

Talich appears in [[vol-128-guanshui-zhan-xiayibu-zouxiang-hefang-meiguo-renmin-ruhe-kandai-telangpu-bairi-xinzheng-kuangxi-bokejie-duihua-zhongshu-talich-ltivoqhue1t-q2ejjlzlenormkhb]] as a live-dialogue participant on [[DonaldTrump]]'s tariff war, early second-term governance, and U.S.-China bargaining. His remarks focus on how other countries can give Trump a deal that is useful domestically, how consumer-price pain may force exemptions, and how administrative-control tools can matter even when headline politics is about trade.

His clearest policy frame is [[TrumpVictoryNarrativeDealMaking]]. He uses the [[Ukraine]] case to explain how a counterparty can offer a plan that lets Trump claim benefit while tying the [[UnitedStates]] more closely to the counterparty's own needs. He then contrasts that with [[ChinaAsTrumpNarrativeEnemy]], where victory packaging is harder because China has been made central to the campaign narrative.

Talich also extends the wiki's institutional branch. He links [[DepartmentOfGovernmentEfficiency|DOGE]], [[OfficeOfManagementAndBudget|OMB]], financial-regulation review, and possible pressure on the next [[FederalReserve]] chair to [[IndependentAgencyControlPressure]], while keeping the immediate [[JeromePowell]] question separate from the longer institutional succession risk.
