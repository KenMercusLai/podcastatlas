# Jasmine Garsd

Jasmine Garsd appears in [[currency-chaos-in-argentina-summer-school]] as the [[NPR]] reporter whose memory of Argentina's December 2001 crisis gives the episode its social-collapse frame. She recalls protests near the presidential palace, police violence, hunger, hospital shortages, supermarket break-ins, and the state of siege that preceded the president's departure.

Her role in the source is to keep [[MonetaryVolatility]] and [[CurrencyRisk]] grounded in lived institutional breakdown. Argentina's collapse is not treated only as debt, inflation, or exchange-rate mechanics; it becomes a story about fear, public order, and the failure of ordinary state reliability.
