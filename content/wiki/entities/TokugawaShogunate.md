# Tokugawa Shogunate / 德川幕府

The Tokugawa Shogunate / 德川幕府 is the governing order that forms around [[TokugawaIeyasu|Tokugawa Ieyasu]] in [[14-wushi-weilian-dahanghai-shidai-de-riben-he-xifang-555771285]]. The episode uses [[WilliamAdams|William Adams / 三浦按针]] to watch the shift from late-Sengoku war and the [[SekigaharaBattle|Battle of Sekigahara]] toward a more stable Tokugawa order.

In the source, the shogunate's foreign policy is not presented as instant isolation. Ieyasu still wants trade, shipbuilding, technical information, and diplomatic optionality. The later turn comes from the regime's fear that Christian missions and Iberian connections could generate loyalty problems and political disorder. That makes the shogunate the institutional center of [[TokugawaForeignTradeAmbivalence]] and later [[SakokuDutchWindow]].

## Key Claims
- The regime's early openness to Adams and trade should be separated from its later restrictive settlement.
- The source links the withdrawal of English traders and the survival of a restricted Dutch channel to the loss of Adams's personal protection and the successors' lower tolerance for foreign and Christian risk.
- The shogunate's policy is framed as risk management, not simply xenophobia: it sacrifices possible gains to reduce perceived threats to rule.
