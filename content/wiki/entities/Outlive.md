# 超越百岁 / Outlive

## Overview
《超越百岁》 / Outlive is [[PeterAttia]]'s longevity book as it appears in this wiki through a midlife muscle-and-sleep source and a [[HubermanLab]] interview focused on lifespan, healthspan, prevention, exercise capacity, and emotional health.

## Current Profile
The first bounded source uses Outlive as a metaphor engine for midlife preparation: skeletal muscle becomes a long-term reserve account, and strength training resembles retirement saving because later illness, aging, or falls can draw down capacity quickly. That source links the book mainly to [[MuscleAsLongevityInfrastructure]], [[EmbodiedAging]], sleep-supported recovery, and pragmatic self-care rather than to a full book summary.

The Attia interview supplies the book's broader healthspan frame. Outlive is named as the backdrop for separating lifespan from [[HealthspanThreeDomains|physical, cognitive, and emotional healthspan]], then treating cardiovascular risk, ApoB, blood pressure, cancer screening, neurodegenerative risk, exercise pillars, deaths of despair, and relationship repair as part of one prevention-oriented longevity model.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a longevity reference point for building reserve before decline is obvious.
- Connects muscle and strength training to older-age autonomy rather than appearance alone.
- Frames longevity through healthspan domains, not only extended survival.
- Supports earlier measurement and prevention around cardiovascular, cancer, metabolic, brain, physical, and emotional risks.
- In this wiki, functions more as an organizing book context than as a chapter-by-chapter book note.
