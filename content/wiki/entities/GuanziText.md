# 《管子》 / Guanzi

《管子》 / Guanzi enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-75-1-qiongshe-jichi-de-zushiye-guanzhong-lmeeh8t6dobgzrkoouato9vhjjuj]], where the host cites it for the luxury-consumption branch attributed to [[GuanZhong|管仲]]. The page is source-scoped: the episode does not survey the whole text or resolve questions about its compilation history.

In the episode's reading, the relevant Guanzi material argues that elite luxury can generate employment and income for poorer people. The examples are deliberately extravagant: carved firewood, painted eggshells, and other labor-heavy goods are used to show how rich people's discretionary spending can pull craft labor and household income into circulation.

This makes the Guanzi node useful as a textual bridge between Guan Zhong's reputation as a statecraft figure and [[LuxuryConsumptionEconomicCirculation|luxury consumption as economic circulation]]. It also keeps the claim distinct from [[StrategicLuxurySabotage|奢侈诱导式削弱]], where induced luxury appears as a hostile strategy rather than a domestic economic policy.
