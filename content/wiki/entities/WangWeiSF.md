# 王卫（顺丰）

## Overview
王卫 is the founder associated with [[SFHolding|顺丰控股]] and its shift from early franchising to direct operation.

## Current Profile
The source places Wang's origin in time-sensitive sample delivery between the Pearl River Delta and Hong Kong. Around 1999-2002, he bought out or removed franchisees to regain control over local operations, producing a direct-operated contrast to the franchise networks of the Tonglu carriers.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder from the Pearl River Delta document and sample-delivery niche.
- Converted an early franchise network into direct operation.
- Built a service and control model distinct from 三通一达.

## Evidence
- Origin: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] links early demand to Shunde textile samples and Hong Kong customers.
