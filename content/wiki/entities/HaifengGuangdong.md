# 海丰 / Haifeng, Guangdong

## Overview
Haifeng is represented as the Guangdong childhood and cultural setting through which [[RenkeMusician|仁科]] and [[WutiaorenBand|五条人]] encountered dialect, local commerce, fashion, poverty, street life, and social memory later recomposed in music and visual identity.

## Current Profile
The source does not offer a general geography or history of Haifeng. It presents the place through Renke's formation: family businesses, school relationships, cheap books, shared records, early music experiments, and a local speech variety described as belonging to the Minnan language system. Haifeng therefore functions in the evidence as lived material rather than a decorative hometown label.

Wutiaoren's artistic use of plastic bags, salons, older styles, town characters, and dialect does not make the band a transparent record of the place. The episode instead shows selection and recomposition: local experience becomes a portable aesthetic language, while later Mandarin writing and Guangzhou life complicate any single-place identity.

## Key Characteristics
- Childhood setting for Renke's family, school, reading, and early musical experience.
- Dialect environment whose tones are described as shaping songwriting choices.
- Source of commercial, visual, and social material used in Wutiaoren's work.
- Remembered locality mediated by later migration and artistic reconstruction.
- Incomplete place profile bounded by one musician's autobiographical account.

## Evidence
- Formation: [[lml-euxpi9ekjvk-ihnkfzwp4ysq-lml-euxpi9ekjvk-ihnkfzwp4ysq]] places Renke's childhood, family business instability, reading, music discovery, and early recording in or around Haifeng.
- Language: [[lml-euxpi9ekjvk-ihnkfzwp4ysq-lml-euxpi9ekjvk-ihnkfzwp4ysq]] describes Haifeng speech as part of the Minnan language system and says tonal patterns affect melody.
- Aesthetic material: [[lml-euxpi9ekjvk-ihnkfzwp4ysq-lml-euxpi9ekjvk-ihnkfzwp4ysq]] connects local plastic objects, salons, old fashion, poverty, and street experience to Wutiaoren's imagery and songs.

## Qualifications
This is not a comprehensive account of Haifeng's population, history, languages, economy, or culture. The language classification, childhood memories, and aesthetic interpretations remain episode-attributed and should not be generalized from Renke and Wutiaoren to all residents.

## What Changed

- Separated lived locality from a claim that Wutiaoren transparently represents the whole place.
