# 郭蒙 / Guo Meng

郭蒙 / Guo Meng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-182-weishenme-shuo-chengye-xiaohe-baiye-xiaohe-2-lqk-jifupm9lbghfzaf09igfufgb|Hanji 182 part 2]] as a Han commander in the suppression of [[ChenXiRebellion|陈豨之乱]]. He appears in the Qi-linked western movement under the broader [[CaoShen|曹参]] line.

The episode says Cao Shen gathers Qi troops, after which Guo Meng defeats [[ZhangChunWesternHan|张春]] near Liaocheng and crosses the Yellow River. His role marks the rebellion's eastern or Qi-facing edge being closed before the Han forces converge on the main northern theater.
