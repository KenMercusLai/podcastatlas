# Alan Newman

## Overview

Alan Newman is a co-founder of [[SeventhGeneration]] and later [[MagicHatBrewingCompany]]. His wiki profile centers on operations, mail-order systems, brand creation, growth discipline, and the founder-relationship lessons he drew from losing operational control of Seventh Generation.

## Current Profile

Alan turned an abandoned environmental-products catalog into Seventh Generation by renaming it, persuading a printer to share risk, and translating environmental features into education, savings, and customer benefits. His operations and catalog expertise complemented [[JeffreyHollender]]'s financing access and responsible-business vision, but exhaustion, recession, an ambiguous sabbatical, and months of silence ended the partnership. Alan later built Magic Hat with clearer authority and a more restrained growth posture, though that company also encountered recession-linked financial pressure and was sold.

## Key Characteristics

- Operational founder with experience in fulfillment, computer systems, catalogs, and workplace culture.
- Opportunity-driven builder who accepted the abandoned catalog despite limited capital and uncertain demand.
- Customer educator who learned to sell benefits rather than assume environmental features would sell themselves.
- Founder whose sabbatical expectations were not documented or actively maintained with his partner or board.
- Later operator who applied slower-growth and clearer-authority lessons at Magic Hat.
