## Overview

庾乘 / Yu Cheng is a late-Han low-status talent-recognition case in Hanji 861. The episode identifies him as a young county-gate functionary whom [[GuoTaiLateHan|郭泰]] notices and encourages despite his humble position.

## Current Profile

Yu Cheng's wiki role is deliberately narrow. Hanji 861 does not give an extended biography; it uses him with [[ShentuPanLateHan|申屠蟠]] to show that Guo Tai's eye for people cuts across occupational and status filters. A gatekeeping job that might invite social dismissal instead becomes the setting where latent capacity can be recognized.

This makes Yu Cheng a support case for the episode's teacher model: good mentorship is not simply instruction after a student has already entered elite channels, but the ability to notice people before status has made them legible.

## Key Characteristics

- Low-status late-Han county functionary identified as a potential worthy.
- Talent-recognition case where humble office does not block Guo Tai's judgment.
- Paired with Shentu Pan as evidence that occupational status is a poor talent filter.
- Source-scoped figure whose broader career is not developed in this wiki yet.
