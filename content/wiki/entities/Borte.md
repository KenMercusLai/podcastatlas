# Borte

## Overview

Borte was [[GenghisKhan|Temujin]]'s betrothed and wife, whose abduction by the Merkit became an early crisis in Temujin's rise.

## Current Profile

[[165-the-rise-of-genghis-khan-glt3370086758]] places Borte inside the kinship and alliance politics of the Mongol steppe. Promised to Temujin while both were young, she was later abducted by the Merkit. Temujin could not recover her alone and appealed to his blood brother [[Jamukha]] and to Toghrul, the Wang Khan, turning a family crisis into a demonstration of alliance mobilization.

The episode interprets wife-taking as a political act involving humiliation, possession, and relations among kin groups, not merely a private romantic episode. Borte's role is nevertheless bounded by a narrative primarily interested in Temujin's development, so it gives little independent account of her agency, later influence, or life after the recovery.

## Key Characteristics

- Betrothed to Temujin during their youth.
- Abducted by the Merkit in a conflict shaped by steppe kinship politics.
- Recovered through Temujin's alliance with Jamukha and Toghrul.
- Central to an early episode showing that Temujin's power initially depended on negotiated support.

## Qualifications

The episode derives much of this early-life material from the [[SecretHistoryOfTheMongols|Secret History of the Mongols]] and does not independently verify every detail. Its account is Temujin-centered and should not be mistaken for a complete biography of Borte or a full reconstruction of her choices and political influence.
