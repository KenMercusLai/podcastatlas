# Tony DeRosa

Tony DeRosa is the founder-owner of [[HearsayBrewingAndTheater]] and a caller in [[advice-line-strategy-sessions-4962a0d6-36bc-4797-8002-3bd8b748a89a]]. His Advice Line question is whether to remain the sole owner or bring in partners with "skin in the game" to run parts of a growing hospitality, comedy, and workshop business.

The advice from [[JeffreyHollender]] and [[GuyRaz]] reframes Tony's problem as an operating-design and hiring-discipline issue before it is an equity issue. They suggest identifying Tony's highest-value role, hiring or developing leaders for other functions, and using [[ControlPreservingIncentives]] before transferring meaningful ownership or control.
