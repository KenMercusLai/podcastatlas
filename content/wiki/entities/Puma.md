# Puma

## Overview

Puma is a global sportswear company that appears in the wiki as both [[BjornGulden|Bjørn Gulden / 古尔登]]'s prior company and as a named participant in the ballet-coded sneaker trend. The sources do not yet give Puma a full operating profile, but they place it inside the same sportswear competition and fashion-refresh field as [[Adidas]].

## Current Profile

Puma's evidence base is narrower than Adidas' in this corpus. The Adidas turnaround episode uses Puma mainly to establish Gulden as an operator from a direct competitor, while the ballet-footwear episode names Puma among sportswear brands applying ballet elements to sneaker products.

That makes Puma useful as a comparison point: sportswear competition is shaped by executives and channels, but also by how quickly brands can borrow aesthetic codes when a footwear silhouette becomes widely adopted.
