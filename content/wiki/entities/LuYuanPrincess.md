## Overview

鲁元公主 / Princess Lu Yuan is [[LiuBang|刘邦]] and [[LuZhi|吕雉]]'s daughter in the supplied evidence. Her profile moves from endangered child and Han-Zhao marriage link to the person whose status becomes contested in two different bargains: a blocked marriage to [[MaoDunChanyu|冒顿单于]] and a reported queen-mother settlement through which [[LiuFeiQiKing|刘肥]] seeks survival.

## Current Profile

The earliest source branch pairs Princess Lu Yuan with [[LiuYing|刘盈]] during Liu Bang's flight after the [[PengchengBattle|彭城之战]]. Liu Bang repeatedly pushes the children from the cart under pursuit, while [[XiahouYing|夏侯婴]] repeatedly retrieves them. The episode uses her first as evidence of wartime family danger and the moral harshness of collapse, not as an independent political actor. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-152-xiangyu-3wanren-ruhe-wansheng-liubang-56wan-dajun-1-lrtpxlak0dlyej8bozmmhdtfczno|Hanji 152 part 1]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-152-xiangyu-3wanren-ruhe-wansheng-liubang-56wan-dajun-2-lq9ecpjb4r-ij0-e-iu-ezfavowj|part 2]]

The later frontier sources place her at the collision between family protection and diplomacy. Her marriage to [[ZhangAo|张敖]] is presented as Han-Zhao alliance infrastructure. After the [[BaidengEncirclement|白登之围]], [[LouJing|刘敬 / 楼敬]] nevertheless urges Liu Bang to send his real legitimate daughter to Modu so kinship can support [[HanXiongnuHeqinPolicy|汉匈和亲政策]]. Liu Bang accepts in principle, Lu Zhi blocks the plan through pleading, and the policy proceeds with another woman assigned the legitimate-princess title. Princess Lu Yuan is therefore the avoided bearer of the first implementation's household cost. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-178-maodun-weihe-yao-fangguo-liubang-zhe-cai-shi-zhenxiang-1-lumszkbr-klnlodxmxdf-n8p5wym|Hanji 178 part 1]], [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-178-maodun-weihe-yao-fangguo-liubang-zhe-cai-shi-zhenxiang-2-loluh53iyrzo4ucf3ttzmn3-otkv|part 2]], [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-179-lishishang-heqin-zenme-laide-lslks-ovbomneornk4j-q6-ns0x1|Hanji 179]], and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-180-xihan-diyi-yinghan-guangao-de-gushi-1-li5u-dmsmb1lp4ny3rk02kaiyu4k|Hanji 180]]

A later court-survival episode adds a different form of political leverage. After Liu Fei reportedly faces a poison threat from Lu Zhi, he grants Princess Lu Yuan [[ChengyangCommandery|城阳郡]] as a [[TangmuYi|汤沐邑]] and honors his younger half-sister as queen mother. The account makes her the beneficiary and symbolic center of a [[StatusReversalSurvivalBargain|名分倒置式求生]], but it does not establish her initiative, consent, or role in designing the arrangement. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-195-lvhou-zhangquan-liubang-zhangzi-jimang-renmei-wei-niang-lokajlf0bhft1b7fo-hwazyqretn|Hanji 195]]

## Key Characteristics

- Daughter of Liu Bang and Lu Zhi in the supplied source set.
- Endangered child rescued with Liu Ying during the Pengcheng rout.
- Wife of Zhang Ao and a link in Han-Zhao marriage politics.
- Intended but protected-against bride in the first post-Baideng heqin design.
- Beneficiary of Chengyang revenue and an extraordinary queen-mother title in Liu Fei's reported survival bargain.
- Dynastic figure whose political importance is often created by others' use of her marriage, rank, and kinship position.

## Qualifications

The page synthesizes structured popular-history notes rather than a full biography. The Pengcheng sources use Princess Lu Yuan mainly inside Liu Bang and Xiahou Ying's conduct, and do not recover her experience. The frontier sources do not settle the marriage chronology, legal meaning of “legitimate elder princess,” Modu's likely reaction to substitution, or Princess Lu Yuan's own views. Hanji 195 does not establish her participation in the Chengyang plan, the ceremony, revenue arrangements, or the exact reason Lu Zhi releases Liu Fei. The queen-mother title is a political status claim and should not be restated as a biological change of kinship.
