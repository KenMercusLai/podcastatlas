# Nihon Kotsu / 日本交通

## Overview
Nihon Kotsu / 日本交通 is a major Tokyo taxi operator. The wiki holds it as the local incumbent partner in [[Waymo]]'s planned Japanese robotaxi service.

## Current Profile
According to the current source, Waymo announced a partnership with Nihon Kotsu and the GO ride-hailing app to launch a commercial robotaxi service in Tokyo next year, with fares benchmarked to existing Tokyo taxi prices and with Nihon Kotsu responsible for vehicle maintenance, management, and operations. The episode uses Nikkei's reading that Japanese taxi firms may keep unusually strong control as autonomous taxis arrive, and notes that the industry has opposed ride-hailing on safety grounds while treating robotaxis more positively because they can address labor shortages.

## Key Characteristics
- Incumbent Tokyo taxi operator rather than a technology developer.
- Operating partner for vehicle maintenance, management, and daily service operations.
- Fare discipline set by reference to existing taxi pricing rather than to ride-hailing discounts.
- Part of a local industry that resisted ride-hailing but is receptive to robotaxis.
- Positioned to retain operational control even as the autonomy stack comes from abroad.
- Labor shortage treated in the source as the industry's reason to accept automation.

## Qualifications
The service is planned rather than operating, and the source gives no fleet size, investment, permit status, or revenue split. Waymo still needs Japanese regulatory approvals, which the episode lists as an open condition.
