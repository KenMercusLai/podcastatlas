# 长坂坡之战 / Battle of Changban

## Overview
长坂坡之战 / Battle of Changban is the 208 CE defeat that destroys [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s immediate material position, tests commander loyalty under pursuit, and creates the recovery and coalition opening later developed around Xiakou and [[LuSu|鲁肃]].

## Current Profile
Hanji 1094 now supplies the battle-and-retreat layer. Cao-side elite cavalry overtakes Liu Bei's civilian-heavy movement; [[ZhangFei|张飞]] covers the withdrawal, [[ZhaoYun|赵云]] returns through the disorder to rescue family members, and [[CaoChunLateHan|曹淳]] is associated with captured daughters and baggage. Liu Bei then changes route toward Hanjin, reconnects with [[GuanYu|关羽]], receives [[LiuQiLateHan|刘琦]]'s support, and regroups around Xiakou.

Hanji 1095 supplies the wider loss frame and motivational interpretation: Cao-side pursuit scatters Liu Bei's group, takes baggage, and separates or captures family members. The episode argues—source-scopedly—that a leader can lose visible resources without surrendering the belief required to continue.

Hanji 1099 begins after the fighting. Liu Bei lacks a secure base, names [[WuJuLateHan|吴巨]] as a possible refuge, and is redirected by Lu Su toward cooperation with [[SunQuan|孙权]]. Changban therefore connects destroyed capacity first to force reunion and then to urgent alliance formation.

## Key Characteristics
- Severe defeat that strips Liu Bei of baggage, family security, and a stable base.
- Contains distinct survival actions: rear-guard deterrence, family rescue, route change, and separated-force reunion.
- Narrows his credible refuge and bargaining options.
- Creates the immediate setting for Lu Su's Sun Quan proposal.
- Becomes a motivational symbol in Hanji 1095, though the host's psychological claims exceed the battle evidence.

## Qualifications
The sources still do not establish a complete primary-source reconstruction. Exact troop totals, civilian numbers, pursuit speed, cavalry losses, bridge condition, route distances, rescue details, dialogue, and the claimed twenty-thousand regrouped force remain episode-presented. Hanji 1095's broad family-loss phrasing sits beside the more specific two-daughter capture, while the alleged Copper Bird Terrace fate remains rumor rather than settled history.

## What Changed
- Added the battlefield, rescue, route-change, and Xiakou regrouping sequence.
- Reframed the event as a bridge from material collapse through force reconstitution to alliance formation.
