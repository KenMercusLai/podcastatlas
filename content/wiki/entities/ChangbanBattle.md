# 长坂坡之战 / Battle of Changban

## Overview
长坂坡之战 / Battle of Changban is the defeat that destroys [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s immediate material position while creating the urgent coalition opening later developed by [[LuSu|鲁肃]].

## Current Profile
Hanji 1095 supplies the loss frame: Cao-side pursuit scatters Liu Bei's group, takes baggage, and separates or captures family members. The episode uses that collapse to argue—source-scopedly—that a leader can lose visible resources without surrendering the belief required to continue.

Hanji 1099 begins after the fighting. Liu Bei lacks a secure base, names [[WuJuLateHan|吴巨]] as a possible refuge, and is redirected by Lu Su toward cooperation with [[SunQuan|孙权]]. Changban therefore matters in the current synthesis less as a reconstructed battle than as the shock connecting destroyed capacity to urgent alliance formation.

## Key Characteristics
- Severe defeat that strips Liu Bei of baggage, family security, and a stable base.
- Narrows his credible refuge and bargaining options.
- Creates the immediate setting for Lu Su's Sun Quan proposal.
- Becomes a motivational symbol in Hanji 1095, though the host's psychological claims exceed the battle evidence.

## Evidence
- Material and family loss: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1095-liubei-shujin-yiqie-hou-ping-shenme-jianxin-neng-fanshen-lmflbj9kshnmgsztdghnqj2foav3|Hanji 1095]] describes Liu Bei fleeing with a small group while baggage and family members are lost or captured.
- Political aftermath: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1099-liubei-kuai-pochan-shi-ruhe-nixi-panshang-sunquan-llnev7pzbraogd00u0h7i7ba65eq|Hanji 1099]] opens with Lu Su reaching Liu Bei and asking where he will go.
- Alliance consequence: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1099-liubei-kuai-pochan-shi-ruhe-nixi-panshang-sunquan-llnev7pzbraogd00u0h7i7ba65eq|Hanji 1099]] connects Liu Bei's exposed position to acceptance of cooperation with Sun Quan.

## Qualifications
Neither source reconstructs the full troop movements, civilian flight, rescue sequence, casualty totals, or command chronology. Hanji 1095's broad claim that Liu Bei lost his wife and children sits beside its more specific claim that two daughters were captured, so exact family outcomes remain source-scoped.
