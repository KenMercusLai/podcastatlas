# 长坂坡之战 / Battle of Changban

## Overview
长坂坡之战 / Battle of Changban is the defeat that leaves [[LiuBei|刘备]] without a secure destination and creates the immediate opening for [[LuSu|鲁肃]]'s alliance proposal.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1099 begins after the battlefield action rather than narrating the battle itself. Its focus is the political aftermath: Liu Bei has escaped, his position is precarious, and his stated plan to join [[WuJuLateHan|吴巨]] is vulnerable to Lu Su's counterproposal that he cooperate with [[SunQuan|孙权]]. The battle therefore functions in the current wiki as the shock that makes alliance sequencing urgent.

## Key Characteristics
- Major defeat for Liu Bei's force in the episode's immediate background.
- Leaves Liu Bei without a secure territorial base or convincing fallback.
- Creates the bargaining context for Lu Su's Sun Quan proposal.
