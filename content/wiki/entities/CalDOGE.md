# CalDOGE

## Overview
CalDOGE is [[SteveHilton|Steve Hilton]]'s campaign-branded California government-efficiency project in the All-In interview.

## Current Profile
In the source, CalDOGE is less a formal state agency than a campaign mechanism for making waste, fraud, abuse, and spending transparency central to Hilton's affordability platform. Hilton says the project examined published spending data and identified examples involving climate mitigation funds, cannabis tax funds, and Project Homekey homelessness spending.

## Key Characteristics
- Campaign audit project designed to support a broader affordability and spending-reduction argument.
- Modeled rhetorically on "DOGE" government-efficiency language rather than presented as an existing California department.
- Paired with [[HerbMorgan]]'s controller campaign as the enforcement route for audits and payment stops.
- Source-scoped estimate of very large waste, fraud, and inefficiency over a five-year period.

The page records the campaign claim; it does not independently audit CalDOGE's figures or legal authority. The source also does not establish whether CalDOGE would exist as a formal government body if Hilton won.
