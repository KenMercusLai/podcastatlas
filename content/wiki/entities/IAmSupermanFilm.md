# I Am Superman (Patty Jenkins film project)

## Overview

*I Am Superman* is [[PattyJenkins]]'s unproduced R-rated film project about Bandit, an abused fighting pit bull condemned to death, and an incarcerated man who reluctantly receives responsibility for rehabilitating him through a prison-dog program.

## Current Profile

The project is designed as a dark fairy tale with the dog as its central dramatic character. Its human protagonist has returned to prison after an accusation, dislikes dogs, and is treated as dangerous despite Jenkins's insistence that he has already changed. Bandit enters the program by mistake instead of being euthanized. Their developing bond creates hope, but institutional corruption, closure of the program, and Bandit's removal for killing interrupt a conventional redemptive resolution.

The proposed form is inseparable from the story. Jenkins wanted to capture hostility, curiosity, fear, affection, and loss of control through patient observation, a small crew, concealed digital cameras, and an actor able to work with the dog outside ordinary scene mechanics. She considered a real prison and training selected incarcerated people as crew members. After repeated development discussions, including with Ryan Gosling, the project remained unfinanced; Jenkins says any revival would likely require assembled financing and protected control.

## Key Characteristics

- Adult fairy tale centered on a traumatized dog rather than a conventional human redemption vehicle.
- Pairs two figures classified as dangerous to test whether institutions can recognize existing change.
- Makes systemic sabotage and punitive labeling central obstacles rather than treating goodness as a guaranteed route to reward.
- Requires a long, low-footprint, animal-centered production process for its intended emotional performance.
- Remains unproduced because its moral ambiguity, adult rating, unfamiliar market category, and unusual process resisted conventional financing.
