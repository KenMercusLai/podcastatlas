# 《资治通鉴纲目》 / Zizhi Tongjian Gangmu

《资治通鉴纲目》 / Zizhi Tongjian Gangmu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-67-zhanguo-mieba-baiqi-de-junshi-caineng-jiujing-ruhe-ljw0lltowxd4qz75v-wcnlkwqskv]] as a reception layer for [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]]. The episode says [[ZhuXi|朱熹]]'s outline tradition omits the [[MangMao|芒卯]] notice, treating that absence as meaningful rather than accidental.

The page's role is source-scoped. In this episode, the omission marks a stricter didactic filter: a story centered on "诈说" may be interesting in Warring States persuasion literature, but it may not satisfy the governing-use standard that the Gangmu tradition wants to preserve.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-109-2-chunshenjun-bei-huangdi-dajiuge-quanzu-miemen-locma2plu7v-vsssm5dr5frc3szw]] adds a wording case rather than an omission case. The host says the Gangmu tradition records [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]]'s death as "盗杀黄歇": "盗" morally marks [[LiYuanWarringStates|李源 / 李元]]'s killing, while using Huang Xie's personal name rather than title or office also withholds honor. The page therefore now covers both selective omission and terse evaluative phrasing.
