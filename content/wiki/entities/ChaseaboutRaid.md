# Chaseabout Raid

## Overview

The Chaseabout Raid was the 1565 Scottish confrontation in which [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]] pursued [[JamesStewartEarlOfMoray|Moray]] and allied Protestant lords until they fled into [[England]].

## Current Profile

The conflict followed Mary's marriage to [[HenryStuartLordDarnley|Darnley]] and her announcement that he would be king. Moray refused the settlement and joined other Protestant lords in raising private forces, while Mary restored [[JamesHepburnEarlOfBothwell|Bothwell]] and assembled a royal army.

Mary rode armed alongside Darnley, Lennox, and Bothwell. The rebels avoided decisive battle, moved through the Lowlands, and ultimately crossed into England. The episode presents the outcome as a striking but temporary royal victory: it displayed Mary's authority and military nerve, yet the exiles soon became potential allies for Darnley when the marriage collapsed.

## Key Characteristics

- Armed resistance to Mary's Darnley marriage and his proposed kingship.
- Mobile pursuit in which the rebel lords avoided a decisive engagement.
- Temporary demonstration of Mary's personal and military authority.
- Crisis that raised Bothwell while sending Moray and allies into English exile.
- Prehistory to the Rizzio coup, when Darnley bargained for the exiles' return.

## Qualifications

The episode emphasizes Mary's dramatic leadership and calls the result a triumph, but supplies a narrative rather than a full campaign history. Force sizes, local support, English assistance, exact routes, and the relative weight of religion, faction, and dynastic politics remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Established the raid as both Mary's clearest early military success and the exile-making precondition for the later Rizzio coalition.

## Relationships

- [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]] - monarch whose armed pursuit forced the rebels into exile.
- [[JamesStewartEarlOfMoray|James Stewart / Earl of Moray]] - leading rebel and Mary's half-brother.
- [[JamesHepburnEarlOfBothwell|James Hepburn / Earl of Bothwell]] - restored loyalist commander raised by the crisis.
- [[HenryStuartLordDarnley|Henry Stuart / Lord Darnley]] - royal husband whose elevation helped trigger the rising and who later allied with its exiles.
- [[RizzioMurder|Rizzio murder]] - later coup that reunited Darnley with the men defeated in the raid.
