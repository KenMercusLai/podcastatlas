# 叮咚小区 / Dingdong Xiaoqu

## Overview
叮咚小区 / Dingdong Xiaoqu is the community O2O predecessor route that the episode connects to [[DingdongMaicai|叮咚买菜]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Dingdong Xiaoqu as an all-in-one community O2O attempt covering errands, repairs, childcare, courier services, payments, group buying, cleaning, unlocking, grocery, elder care, social connection, queueing, pet care, watering, and medical accompaniment. Its rapid advertising spend, financing, and layoffs show the community-superapp version of O2O overreach. The later pivot toward buying fruit and vegetables becomes the path to Dingdong Maicai.

## Key Characteristics
- It is the episode's clearest community O2O overreach case.
- Its original scope tried to bundle many neighborhood services into one platform.
- Its early growth relied on large advertising spend and fast financing.
- Its collapse into layoffs exposed weak breadth-first economics.
- Its pivot toward grocery demand connects it to Dingdong Maicai's later fresh-grocery route.

## Evidence
- Broad service scope - [[no-218-o2o-yu-gongxiangjingji-shuyu-danche-chongdianbao-shangmenmeijia-he-anmo-de-mohuan-saqian-niandai-1013073318]] lists community services from dog walking and repairs to grocery, elder care, queueing, and pet care.
- Overheated growth - [[no-218-o2o-yu-gongxiangjingji-shuyu-danche-chongdianbao-shangmenmeijia-he-anmo-de-mohuan-saqian-niandai-1013073318]] says the company spent heavily on Shanghai and Beijing ads, completed a large angel round quickly, and later cut staff sharply.
- Pivot signal - [[no-218-o2o-yu-gongxiangjingji-shuyu-danche-chongdianbao-shangmenmeijia-he-anmo-de-mohuan-saqian-niandai-1013073318]] says founder Liang Changlin cut peripheral services, focused on errands, saw demand for fruit and vegetables, and renamed the business Dingdong Maicai in 2017.

## Qualifications
The page treats revenue, profit, sale, advertising, and staffing numbers as source-scoped. It does not independently verify Dingdong Maicai's later 2025-2026 figures.
