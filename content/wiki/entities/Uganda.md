# Uganda

Uganda appears in [[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]] as one of the expected early personnel contributors to the [[InternationalStabilizationForce]] for [[Gaza]]. The source says the initial force may include only a few hundred personnel from [[Morocco]] and Uganda.

The source uses Uganda as part of the plan's capacity question. Naming troop contributors makes the stabilization concept more concrete, but the episode still asks whether such a small early force can affect territorial control, security, and administrative handover.
