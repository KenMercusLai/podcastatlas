# Ben Graham

## Overview
Ben Graham is the value-investing figure [[BillAckman]] invokes in the All-In interview to distinguish older liquidation-oriented value investing from modern [[QualityValueInvesting]].

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Graham's durable lesson is that a stock represents ownership in a business rather than a trading token. Ackman argues that Graham's historical opportunity set involved harder-to-access information and more liquidation-style bargains, while modern investors must place more weight on business quality, management talent, and disruption risk.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents the foundational business-ownership logic of value investing.
- Serves as a contrast case for liquidation-style opportunities in older markets.
- Is used by Ackman to justify updating value investing toward quality and durability.
