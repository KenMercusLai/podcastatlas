# NewsBreak

## Overview

NewsBreak is represented as an algorithmic news aggregator used by North Carolina farmer [[EthanJordan]] for local, national, farming, and agricultural stories.

## Current Profile

In the bounded source, NewsBreak illustrates how aggregation can substitute for direct newspaper subscription in a thin local-news environment. Its relevance system gives Jordan material connected to his interests during long hours in a tractor.

The profile is functionally narrow: the source shows usefulness in discovery but does not establish where the displayed stories originate, how ranking works, what verification occurs, or whether aggregation supports or weakens original [[LocalJournalism]].

- Personalizes toward farming and agricultural interests in the described case.
- Serves as one component of a broader substitute media diet.
