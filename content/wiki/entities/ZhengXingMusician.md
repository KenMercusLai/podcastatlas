# 郑兴 / Zheng Xing (musician)

## Overview
[[ZhengXingMusician|郑兴]] is a singer-songwriter whose source-backed profile connects Beijing listening, Taipei study, autobiographical albums, collaborative production, and live performance.

## Current Profile
Zheng Xing's early work grew from folk roots, broad live listening, and [[ChenXiaoxia|陈小霞]]'s example of melody fitted closely to language. During graduate study in Taipei, songwriting became an outlet for isolation and academic pressure; rehearsing with a band, entering a competition, and holding a first ticketed show then made music feel like a viable route beyond his original film-making plan.

His albums are described as personal chronicles, but that method eventually risked depletion. A later project therefore handed more songwriting and production authority to collaborators. The resulting songs initially carried other writers' signatures, yet touring let Zheng Xing inhabit them through voice, bodily repetition, and audience response. His current profile is consequently defined less by a fixed “郑兴 style” than by movement between personal control and deliberate openness.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes from memory, movement, place, family, and the viewpoint of a temporary resident or traveler.
- Treats folk as a creative root while drawing on rock, theatre, regional music, and collaborative arrangement.
- Uses albums and tours as integrated conceptual forms rather than collections of isolated songs.
- Alternates between self-directed projects and projects that deliberately give collaborators greater authority.
- Uses live performance to deepen or create personal connection with repertoire, including songs not written alone.
