# Roger Mortimer

## Overview

Roger Mortimer appears as the English exile and ally of [[IsabellaOfFrance|Isabella of France]] whose overthrow of [[EdwardIIOfEngland|Edward II]] creates the regency regime that [[EdwardIIIOfEngland|Edward III]] later destroys.

## Current Profile

The episode treats Mortimer as a transitional power figure in the origin story of the [[HundredYearsWar|Hundred Years' War]]. He hates Edward II, joins Isabella in France, and participates in the invasion that deposes the king.

Mortimer then rules through young Edward III and signs the 1328 Scottish settlement recognizing Scottish independence. The source says that treaty becomes known in England as a shameful peace, helping explain Edward III's later drive to reverse the settlement and reassert authority.

## Key Characteristics

- English exile opposed to Edward II.
- Political and romantic ally of Isabella in the episode's account.
- Participant in Edward II's overthrow.
- Regency power ruling through young Edward III.
- Associated with the 1328 Scottish treaty treated as humiliating in England.
- Overthrown and executed by Edward III in 1330.
