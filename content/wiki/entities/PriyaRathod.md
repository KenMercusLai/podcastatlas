# Priya Rathod

Priya Rathod is the [[Indeed]] representative interviewed in [[tech-20260803-0803-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260803-0803-mp-tech-pod-128]] about workplace AI expectations and readiness. Her role in the wiki is to ground the episode's labor-market framing: employers increasingly ask for AI skills, but workers and managers are still adjusting to what those expectations mean in practice.

Rathod's main conceptual contribution is [[AINativeWorker|AI native worker]] as a behavior-based label rather than an age-based one. She describes AI natives as people comfortable using AI to design, execute, and scale workflows end to end, while also stressing that younger workers' faster adoption can reflect exposure rather than superior capability.
