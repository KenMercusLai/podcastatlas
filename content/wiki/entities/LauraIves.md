# Laura Ives

Laura Ives appears in [[kyiv-brawl-ukraines-top-general-fired-6a609aec4fac21e67f1be165]] as a lawyer in the U.S. police-killings segment. She describes a case involving an elderly man with a small pocket knife who was hit with a pepper ball and died, helping the episode show how low-level threat, police tactics, and medical fragility can interact.

Her contribution sharpens [[PoliceConsentDecreeCultureGap]] and [[MentalHealthCrisisPoliceResponse]]. The source later uses her argument that officers should sometimes be willing to wait when a human life is at stake, making tactical patience part of the reform problem.
