# Irish Republican Brotherhood

## Overview

The Irish Republican Brotherhood was a clandestine republican organization that supplied the central planning network for the [[EasterRising|Easter Rising]].

## Current Profile

The IRB carried a post-famine Fenian tradition with American links, money, and access to guns, but it had not launched a serious revolution since 1867 and could appear obsolete. [[TomClarke]]'s return and younger organizers converted that inherited network into a military council active from spring 1915.

The Brotherhood worked through rather than simply replacing public organizations. It gained influence inside the [[IrishVolunteers|Irish Volunteers]], coordinated with [[JamesConnolly]] and the [[IrishCitizenArmy|Irish Citizen Army]], sought German arms, and deceived [[EoinMacNeill]] about imminent suppression. Its practical work complicates a martyrdom-only reading, even though the final military design proved inadequate.

## Key Characteristics

- Secret republican organization rooted in the Fenian tradition.
- Network with American financing and arms connections.
- Institution renewed through cooperation between older and younger revolutionaries.
- Military-council center for planning the 1916 rebellion.
- Clandestine current operating inside broader volunteer organization.
- Coalition builder willing to use deception and foreign wartime assistance.

## Evidence

- Organizational inheritance: [[339-ireland-the-easter-rising-1916-part-4-glt7095481415]] traces post-famine roots, the 1867 failure, American links, and Clarke's role.
- Planning: [[339-ireland-the-easter-rising-1916-part-4-glt7095481415]] dates active military-council work to spring 1915 and records fundraising and arms efforts.
- Coalition and deception: [[339-ireland-the-easter-rising-1916-part-4-glt7095481415]] follows IRB influence in the Volunteers, coordination with Connolly, and the altered Dublin Castle document shown to MacNeill.

## Qualifications

The source does not reconstruct the IRB's complete membership, command structure, transatlantic finances, or internal debate. Its centrality does not make the Rising an IRB-only event, and practical preparation does not prove that its battlefield plan offered a plausible route to victory.

## What Changed

- Established the IRB as the organizational core that connected Fenian continuity, volunteer infiltration, wartime arms seeking, and military planning.

## Relationships

- [[TomClarke]] - veteran organizer central to the Brotherhood's renewal.
- [[PatrickPearse]] - military-council member who became the Rising's public voice.
- [[IrishVolunteers]] - public armed body within which IRB organizers operated.
- [[IrishCitizenArmy]] - distinct labour militia brought into the revolutionary coalition.
- [[EoinMacNeill]] - Volunteer leader deceived and then opposed by IRB planners.
- [[EasterRising]] - operation for which the Brotherhood supplied the central conspiracy.
