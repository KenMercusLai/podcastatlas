# Sarah's Gluten-Free Goods

## Overview
Sarah's Gluten-Free Goods is [[SarahGodfrey]]'s fresh gluten-free and allergy-friendly bakery in Westfield, Massachusetts, and the second caller case in [[advice-line-with-ben-goodwin-of-olipop-f9d3fe17-eb2c-440d-b223-990fed970727]].

## Current Profile
The source presents the bakery as a trust-led local food business whose differentiation creates its scaling constraint. Freshness, never-frozen positioning, and allergy-safe production make the products valuable to customers, but they also create perishability, liability, cross-contamination, facility, and founder-capacity pressure.

## Key Characteristics
- Makes gluten-free desserts and other baked goods for customers with allergies such as dairy, nut, corn, and oat allergies.
- Operates from home under a cottage food permit and sells at three farmers markets.
- Supplies two or three small local grocery stores through weekly drop-offs.
- Emphasizes fresh, never-frozen goods, though some items such as blondies and muffins freeze well.
- Moved from roughly negative $2,000 in net profit last year toward an expected $20,000 net profit in the current year.
- Faces scaling friction from perishability, cross-contamination risk, allergy liability concerns, and founder workload.
