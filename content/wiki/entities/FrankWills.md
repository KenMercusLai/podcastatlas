# Frank Wills

## Overview

Frank Wills was the Watergate security guard whose attention to tape placed on self-locking doors led police to the Democratic National Committee burglary in progress.

## Current Profile

The episode makes Wills the contingency at the center of the break-in's failure. He removed tape that prevented a door from locking, later found it had been replaced, and called police. That routine act of observation converted a covert political operation into arrests, physical evidence, and an investigative trail toward [[HowardHunt]] and the Nixon White House.

## Key Characteristics

- Security guard working at the Watergate complex on June 17, 1972.
- Noticed tape defeating the building's self-locking doors.
- Removed the tape and recognized its suspicious replacement.
- Called police, enabling the burglars' arrest in the offices.
- Demonstrates how ordinary procedural attention can disrupt a high-level covert operation.
