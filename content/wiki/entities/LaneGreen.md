# Lane Green

## Overview
Lane Green appears in the wiki through [[slip-the-surly-bonds-scott-bessent-goes-on-a-yield-trip-6a8eb9200c15e359f9599e1a]] as the [[TheIntelligence]] voice explaining British accents, class, and political authority. His source role is to turn [[AndyBurnham]]'s northern accent into a wider discussion of received pronunciation, regional speech, and credibility in Westminster.

## Current Profile
In the source, Green argues that Burnham would make [[Number10DowningStreet|Number 10]] sound different because his northern pronunciation breaks with the older prime-ministerial soundscape associated with Oxbridge, the BBC, and Westminster. He does not treat accent as a cosmetic issue. The segment uses accent to ask who sounds intelligent, serious, or authoritative in British politics.

Green also gives historical range. The episode compares Burnham with earlier leaders such as [[JimCallaghan]] and [[HaroldWilson]], then contrasts that with the long prestige of received pronunciation and the continuing evidence that some regional accents are judged as less intelligent.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains language as a political signal rather than only pronunciation.
- Uses historical prime-ministerial accents to separate regional identity from current class prejudice.
- Connects Burnham's northern voice to wider changes in [[UnitedKingdom]] political credibility.
