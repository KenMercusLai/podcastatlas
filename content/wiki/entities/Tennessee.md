# Tennessee

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-adam-carolla-on-californias-collapse-fires-failed-leadership-and-gyno-fascism-39710360]] adds [[Tennessee]] as one of [[AdamCarolla|Adam Carolla]]'s destination-state examples in the [[SafeSpacesVsOctagons]] migration frame. He groups it with [[Texas]] and [[Florida]] as a place people may choose when they want lower regulation, more perceived order, or more room for business and family life than they associate with [[California]].

The source does not independently analyze Tennessee policy. It uses the state as part of a cultural-geographic contrast inside a California-focused political argument.
