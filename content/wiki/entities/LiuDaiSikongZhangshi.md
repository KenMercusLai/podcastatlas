# 刘岱 / Liu Dai (Sikong changshi)

## Overview
刘岱 / Liu Dai (Sikong changshi) enters the wiki through Hanji 1040 as one of [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s commanders sent against [[LiuBei|刘备]] after Liu Bei retakes [[Xuzhou|徐州]].

## Current Profile
This page deliberately separates the 200 CE Sikong-changshi Liu Dai from [[LiuDaiLateHan|the earlier Yanzhou inspector Liu Dai]], who dies in the wiki's earlier late-Han branch. In Hanji 1040, this Liu Dai matters as the failed first response to Liu Bei's renewed force.

## Key Characteristics
- Cao-side officer sent to attack Liu Bei after the Xuzhou breakaway.
- Paired with [[WangZhongLateHan|王忠]] in a failed campaign.
- Shows that Liu Bei's position after leaving Cao Cao is strong enough to defeat an initial punitive force.
- Kept as a disambiguated page because another Liu Dai already exists and dies earlier.

## Evidence
- Campaign role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1040-luanshi-yingxiong-caocao-weihe-dudu-kanhao-liubei-lrazzwo4bjspvwmnkx5hoar1iq3x]] says Cao Cao sends Sikong changshi Liu Dai and zhonglangjiang Wang Zhong against Liu Bei.
- Outcome: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1040-luanshi-yingxiong-caocao-weihe-dudu-kanhao-liubei-lrazzwo4bjspvwmnkx5hoar1iq3x]] reports that Liu Dai and Wang Zhong fail to defeat Liu Bei.
- Identity boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1040-luanshi-yingxiong-caocao-weihe-dudu-kanhao-liubei-lrazzwo4bjspvwmnkx5hoar1iq3x]] provides the later date and office setting that separate this figure from the earlier Liu Dai page.

## Qualifications
The episode does not establish this Liu Dai's prior biography. The disambiguation is an inference from the wiki's existing chronology plus the source's 200 CE office/campaign context.

## What Changed
- Created a separate Liu Dai page to avoid merging a 200 CE Cao-side officer with the earlier deceased Yanzhou inspector.
