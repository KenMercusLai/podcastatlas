# Junior

Junior is [[Kuse]]'s AI employee product in [[women-shi-ruhe-dingyi-openclaw-for-teams-xin-chanpin-xingtai-de-duitan-kuse-junior-lianchuang-jian-cto-yuhao-lkp1a0todflxoyycyo3zhrap3ebv]]. [[Yuhao]] frames it as a worker embedded in company tools rather than a personal assistant: each Junior can have responsibilities, projects, work accounts, email, phone number, and persistent context.

The product is the wiki's concrete case for [[OpenClawForTeams]]. Its design pressure comes from [[EnterpriseAgentMemory]], [[AgentIdentityAndAuthentication]], and [[AgentPermissionBoundaries]]: a Junior may need to register accounts, pay for services, join meetings, write outbound email, inspect sales data, or work with coworkers, but those capabilities make phishing, prompt injection, unauthorized disclosure, and account misuse first-order risks.

The source says Kuse considered salary-like pricing for Junior, with possible per-agent monthly prices such as $2,000 or $5,000 plus excess token credits. That makes Junior a bridge between [[DigitalEmployees]], [[OutcomeBasedAIPricing]], and [[AIInferenceCostStructure]] rather than a normal seat-priced SaaS product.
