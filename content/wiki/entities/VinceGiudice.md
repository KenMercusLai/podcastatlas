# Vince Giudice

Vince Giudice is the cofounder/operator of [[Everloop]] and a caller in [[advice-line-with-curt-richardson-of-otterbox-e94bf774-de11-4434-843b-a551309a6773]]. He previously worked in environmentally friendly toys and says that products made from recycled materials can still end up in landfill when recycling centers do not recognize them.

His Advice Line question is how to choose marketing channels when baby-registry purchasing creates a long lag between discovery and purchase. [[CurtRichardson]] and [[GuyRaz]] advise Vince to run smaller measurable tests, call customers directly, and consider whether Everloop's buyback program is the practical customer hook that makes its sustainability promise easier to understand.
