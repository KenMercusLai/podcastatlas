# Fannie Mae

Fannie Mae appears in [[can-trump-make-buying-a-home-more-affordable]] as one of the government-backed mortgage institutions implicated in the episode's mortgage-bond purchase plan. The source does not analyze Fannie Mae's full history; it uses the institution as part of the channel through which public balance-sheet capacity can buy mortgage-backed securities and possibly reduce mortgage rates.

In the episode's frame, Fannie Mae matters because the rate benefit and taxpayer risk travel together. More mortgage-backed-security purchases can support demand for mortgage bonds, but [[SusanWachter]] says the public takes on extra interest-rate risk when Fannie Mae and [[FreddieMac]] hold more of that exposure.
