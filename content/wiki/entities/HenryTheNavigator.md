# Henry the Navigator / 恩里克王子

## Overview
Henry the Navigator / 恩里克王子 appears in the Ceuta episode as one of [[JohnIOfPortugal|John I of Portugal]]'s sons who joined the 1415 attack on [[Ceuta]] and later became associated with Portuguese maritime expansion.

## Current Profile
The source keeps Henry important but not mythically omniscient. He helps hold the Ceuta target when some Portuguese voices suggest attacking [[Gibraltar]] instead, is knighted after the victory, and later appears in the failed 1437 Tangier campaign where he promises Ceuta as ransom but Portugal does not return it.

## Key Characteristics
- Participant in the 1415 Ceuta campaign.
- Associated with the later Portuguese expansion narrative.
- Used by the source to show the gap between heroic exploration memory and messy military-political decisions.
