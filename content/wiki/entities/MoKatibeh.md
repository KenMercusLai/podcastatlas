# Mo Katibeh

## Overview

Mo Katibeh is presented as chief marketing officer of [[TMobile]] for Business and the episode's principal explainer of [[NetworkSlicing|5G network slicing]]. He connects network configuration to event operations, first response, enterprise mobility, security, satellite coverage, healthcare, and the path toward 6G.

## Current Profile

Katibeh frames slicing as the ability to configure network characteristics around a business outcome rather than treating mobile service as uniform. His examples move from large events to T-Priority and SuperMobile, then toward prospective healthcare guidance and AI-enabled work. He also presents customer conversations as inputs to future network design.

Because he speaks for the sponsor, his contribution is best read as an operator's product and strategy account, not independent technical validation.

## Key Characteristics

- Translates network characteristics into operational use cases.
- Presents adaptive connectivity, security, and satellite communication as a combined enterprise offer.
- Uses large events and first response to explain congestion-sensitive reliability.
- Extends current deployments into prospective healthcare and AI-assisted scenarios.
- Treats customer workflow problems as feedback for future network development.
