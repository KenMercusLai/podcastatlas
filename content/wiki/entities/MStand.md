# M Stand

M Stand appears in [[zhongguo-yinglai-hanbao-kaidianchao-guochan-huangningmeng-jiage-huiluo-1006920543]] as a coffee-chain-adjacent entrant into burgers. The episode names it alongside [[Haidilao]] and [[PizzaHutChina]] to show that burger expansion in China is not limited to pure burger chains.

The case connects coffee retail to restaurant category extension. A coffee brand entering burgers has to test whether its existing store, customer occasion, and brand identity can support a more meal-like product without losing the beverage-led visit reason.
