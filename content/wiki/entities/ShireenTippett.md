# Shireen Tippett

Shireen Tippett is the founder behind [[HoneyBespokeStationery]] in [[advice-line-with-carlton-calvin-of-razor-c0de1a84-3d35-45f3-921c-6144693ad86e]]. The source presents her as a stationery founder with meaningful online traction, collegiate licenses, and a desire to move customers toward her own website rather than remaining primarily dependent on a marketplace.

[[GuyRaz]] and [[CarltonCalvin]] advise her to make the founder and design process more visible through short videos, voiceover, behind-the-scenes content, and stores connected to the schools she licenses. Her case turns discomfort on camera into a practical [[FounderVisibilityMarketing]] problem rather than a generic social-media mandate.
