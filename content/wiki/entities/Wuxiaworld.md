# Wuxiaworld / 武侠世界

Wuxiaworld / 武侠世界 appears in [[ep240-ba-zong-tian-chong-zai-haiwai-duanju-chuhai-de-chanye-mima-ltvxvfj5ko6djhsjatiphdy-dzek]] as an early English-language Chinese web-fiction translation site founded by [[LaiJingping|赖靖平]] in 2014. The episode says it grew from a personal blog into a translator community around works such as 《盘龙》 and 《诛仙》.

The source treats Wuxiaworld as market-validation infrastructure. Although many early communities were unauthorized fan translations, they proved that overseas readers could follow Chinese serialized web fiction, helping formal platforms recognize the demand that later fed [[Webnovel|起点国际版]] and [[ShortDramaOverseasExpansion|短剧出海]].
