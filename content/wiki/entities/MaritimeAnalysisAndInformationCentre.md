# Maritime Analysis and Information Centre

The Maritime Analysis and Information Centre appears in [[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]] through [[PauloSilva]], who discusses cocaine flows through [[WestAfrica]]. The source names it as an EU-linked maritime analysis body concerned with cocaine routes toward Europe.

The institution functions as the enforcement-side anchor for [[MaritimeDrugInterdictionCapacity]]. The episode says cargo from countries such as [[Brazil]] and Suriname has come under more scrutiny, pushing traffickers toward more departure points and faster boats that are dangerous to intercept.
