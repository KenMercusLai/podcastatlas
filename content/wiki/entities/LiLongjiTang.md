## Overview

Li Longji enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-536-3-jiemi-shangguan-waner-he-ta-beihou-de-nanrenmen-lqj7rpwwfllogrxrbav-xqwwnnnm|Hanji 536-3]] as the prince who leads the [[TanglongCoup|唐隆之变]] against the [[EmpressWeiTang|韦后]] faction and decides to execute [[ShangguanWaner|上官婉儿]]. The episode uses him as the point at which Shangguan Wan'er's multi-sided survival record fails.

## Current Profile

The source identifies Li Longji as [[LiDanTang|李旦]]'s son. In 710, after [[TangZhongzong|唐中宗]]'s death and Empress Wei's power consolidation, Li Longji launches the Tanglong Coup to clear the Wei faction. Shangguan Wan'er meets him holding a lantern and an edict that she believes proves her alignment with the Li-Tang royal line, but Li Longji still orders her death.

## Key Characteristics

- Coup leader in the episode's account of the 710 Tanglong Coup.
- Son of Li Dan and representative of hard Li-Tang restoration loyalty.
- Clears the Wei faction after Zhongzong's death.
- Evaluates Shangguan Wan'er's self-protective edict but rejects it as insufficient.
- Functions as the final judge of Shangguan Wan'er's factional balancing strategy.
