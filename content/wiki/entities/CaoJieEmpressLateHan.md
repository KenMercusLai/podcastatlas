## Overview

曹节皇后 / Empress Cao Jie is a daughter of [[CaoCao|曹操]] who enters [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s household and becomes empress after [[FuShouEmpress|伏皇后]] is removed.

## Current Profile

In Hanji 1150, Cao Jie embodies the household layer of Cao-family control: marriage places Cao Cao's daughter beside the emperor after an anti-Cao empress is eliminated. She later accompanies Liu Xie's post-abdication life in the Shanyang fief, connecting coercive court replacement with the predecessor house's continued domestic status.

## Key Characteristics

- Daughter of Cao Cao and one of three Cao daughters placed in Emperor Xian's household.
- Becomes empress after Fu Shou's removal.
- Links the Cao family to the Han imperial household before formal abdication.
- Remains paired with Liu Xie in the episode's post-abdication account.

## Qualifications

This source does not provide a complete biography or establish Cao Jie's own political preferences. It uses marriage primarily to explain Cao-family access and the later Shanyang household. She is distinct from [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]], the late-Han eunuch.
