## Overview

Paracelsus / 帕拉塞尔苏斯 appears in the episode as a European physician-alchemist who emphasized mineral and chemical substances in medicine.

## Current Profile

The source places Paracelsus at the medicine-facing edge of [[WesternAlchemy|Western alchemy]]. His significance in this bounded account is not that alchemical medicine was uniformly effective, but that matter transformation, pharmacy, mineral remedies, and healing remained intertwined before chemistry and medicine acquired later disciplinary boundaries.

## Key Characteristics

- Connects European alchemy to medicine and pharmacy.
- Emphasizes mineral and chemical substances as potential remedies.
- Illustrates the unstable early-modern boundary among alchemy, chemistry, and medicine.

## Evidence

- **Chemical medicine:** [[46-guren-ruhe-changsheng-bulao-dongxifang-de-lianjinshu-liandanshu-yu-changshengyao-liporj2ydsu1pwehpgflapgm6zpx]] names Paracelsus among European natural philosophers and alchemists who treated mineral or chemical materials as medically useful.

## Qualifications

The current page is source-scoped and does not establish the safety or efficacy of any remedy, nor does it survey Paracelsus's full theology, medical theory, writings, or reception. Mineral use can produce medicine or poison depending on substance, preparation, dose, and evidence.
