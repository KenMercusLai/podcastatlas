# Alexander McQueen / 亚历山大·麦昆

Alexander McQueen / 亚历山大·麦昆 appears in [[16-shishang-shouhaizhe-jing-luse-huo-wei-zui-du-yanse-559889282]] through the episode's example of a studded belt recalled after radioactive cobalt-60 was found in the metal. The source uses the case to show that modern luxury accessories can inherit risks from scrap handling, waste dumping, and globalized material flows.

The example matters because the danger is not a visible design feature. It belongs to [[FashionSupplyChainExternality]]: radioactive waste that should have been controlled can reappear in consumer goods when supply chains turn discarded metal into fashionable hardware.

## Key Claims
- The source uses the belt case to show that modern fashion risk can come from supply-chain contamination rather than intentional toxic ingredients.
- Waste governance and material sourcing are part of product safety even for prestige goods.
- The case extends [[ToxicFashionMaterialRisk]] from historical dyes and cosmetics into contemporary accessory hardware.
