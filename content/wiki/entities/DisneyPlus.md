# Disney+

Disney+ is Disney's direct-to-consumer streaming service in [[disney-the-renaissance-and-the-empire-1]]. The source frames it as strategically necessary once [[Netflix]] controlled discovery, cable declined, and old theatrical and home-video windows weakened.

[[kafeidou-he-niu-ziyou-cheng-zizhu-canting-maidian-guijia-guanghuan-cong-he-er-lai-1004978054]] adds a current funnel-and-price-discrimination idea. The source says [[TheWaltDisneyCompany]] is considering a free ad-supported streaming product to reach price-sensitive users, sell more advertising, and potentially move some viewers into paid Disney+ subscriptions.

The episode is deliberately mixed on Disney+. Launch demand proved the strength of [[TheWaltDisneyCompany]]'s IP, but streaming also brought subscriber acquisition, churn, technology cost, and constant content demand. That makes Disney+ a concrete case for [[StreamingContentTreadmill]] rather than a simple extension of the older [[EntertainmentIPFlywheel]].
