# Artemis 2

[[tech-20260410-0410-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260410-0410-mp-tech-pod-128]] updates Artemis 2 from a coming lunar flyby into an underway public-media event. The episode says four astronauts aboard the test flight around the [[Moon]] traveled about 250,000 miles from Earth, farther than any humans before, and that [[NASA]] used continuous [[YouTube]] coverage, iPhone photos, and astronaut clips to widen public attention.

Artemis 2 appears in [[tech-20260211-0211-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260211-0211-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the [[NASA]] mission that could send humans around the [[Moon]] for the first time in more than five decades. The episode treats the mission as a near-term milestone inside a longer effort to build sustained lunar presence and prepare for deeper human spaceflight.

The source's main contribution is not mission hardware detail, but the policy frame around the mission.
