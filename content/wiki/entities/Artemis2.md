# Artemis 2

## Overview
Artemis 2 is a crewed [[NASA]] test flight around the [[Moon]] and the near-term human milestone in a wider program for sustained lunar presence.

## Current Profile
The bounded sources show a three-step chronology: a February 2026 source described the mission as forthcoming, an April source described four astronauts as already flying around the Moon, and [[JaredIsaacman|Jared Isaacman]] later described their safe return. Beyond the flight itself, Artemis 2 functions as a public-attention event and as the policy gateway to lunar governance, resource use, power, landing systems, and deeper-space preparation.

## Key Characteristics
- Crewed lunar test flight positioned after more than five decades without comparable human travel around the Moon.
- Public-media event amplified by continuous video, astronaut photographs, and social clips.
- Program milestone connecting NASA's near-term flight sequence to sustained lunar operations.
- Policy trigger for [[LunarResourceGovernance]], [[ArtemisAccords]], energy infrastructure, and competition with [[China]].
