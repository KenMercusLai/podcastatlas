# Ferrari Classiche

Ferrari Classiche is the certification, restoration, and originality program discussed in [[ferrari-1]]. The source says it certifies originality for a fee and reinforces [[Ferrari]]'s service and restoration ecosystem.

The program matters because it lets Ferrari participate in the meaning and value of older cars after the first sale. It is a direct mechanism inside [[AutomotiveCollectorEconomics]], where provenance, originality, maintenance, and brand-backed authentication sustain resale confidence.
