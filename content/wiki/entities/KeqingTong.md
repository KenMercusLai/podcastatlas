# 客卿通 / Keqing Tong

客卿通 / Keqing Tong appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-60-zhongguo-di-yi-wei-bachi-chaozheng-de-taihou-jingshi-ta-ltazis9qo0dwvwmzxikk8mmpvosc]] as the Qin commander sent to rescue [[ChuState|楚国]] when [[QiState|齐国]], [[HanState|韩国]], and [[WeiState|魏国]] attack Chu. The episode calls him a keqing, making him an outside or guest minister/commander serving [[QinState|秦国]].

His action proves that the Qin-Chu alignment under [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]] is operational, not merely marital rhetoric. After [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] sends [[MiHeng|芈横]] to Qin as hostage, Qin sends Keqing Tong's army, and the attacking coalition withdraws.
