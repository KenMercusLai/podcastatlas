# Madhya Pradesh

Madhya Pradesh appears in [[settler-bet-a-west-bank-flashpoint-6a842bb539e73ea8f3c13a2d]] as one of the [[BharatiyaJanataParty|BJP]]-ruled states that has passed a [[UniformCivilCodeIndia|uniform civil-code]] version. The episode lists it after [[Uttarakhand]] alongside [[Assam]] and [[Gujarat]].

The state functions as part of the source's diffusion evidence. The key wiki claim is not a detailed Madhya Pradesh legal account, but that multiple state governments are turning [[MuslimPersonalLawIndia]] and family-law uniformity into a recurring political test.
