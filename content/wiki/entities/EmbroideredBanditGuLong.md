## Overview

《绣花大盗》 / The Embroidery Bandit is a [[GuLong|古龙]] novel in the Lu Xiaofeng sequence. A criminal uses an embroidery needle to blind skilled opponents and leaves black-peony cloth at a series of robberies, drawing [[LuXiaofeng|陆小凤]] into an investigation involving [[XueBingGuLong|薛冰]], [[GongsunDaniangGuLong|公孙大娘]], and [[JinJiulingGuLong|金九龄]].

## Current Profile

The bounded episode values the novel less as rigorous detection than as a sequence of character encounters, strategic reversals, and visually opposed fights. Lu and Gongsun's three contests move from exhaustion and drink to swordplay, false targets, smell, disguise, and concealed cooperation. The final duel converts a heavy hammer into a quiet, agile weapon and a tiny needle into a source of hard pressure, making contrast itself the combat design.

The culprit reveal is memorable because Jin participates in the investigation and occupies Lu's circle of trust. Its support is less secure: luxurious consumption hints at financial pressure, but the hosts find the money motive, perfect-crime ambition, inference chain, and some retroactive explanations too thin. The novel's emotional center therefore lies in friendship, play, trust, and resilience as much as in solution mechanics.

## Key Characteristics

- Organizes a martial-arts mystery around repeated robberies, blinded experts, an embroidered token, and a culprit inside the investigation.
- Uses three contests to turn rivalry into covert cooperation between Lu Xiaofeng and Gongsun Daniang.
- Makes disguise, fatigue, smell, deliberate weakness, and expectation management more important than raw force alone.
- Designs the final duel through reversals of heavy and light, hard and soft, loud and silent.
- Gives friendship and life-affirming empathy greater emotional stability than romance or procedural proof.
- Combines an effective final surprise with motives and causal bridges that the bounded reading finds under-supported.
