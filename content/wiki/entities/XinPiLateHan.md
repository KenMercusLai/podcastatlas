## Overview
[[XinPiLateHan|辛毗]] is introduced in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1058-shaonian-sunquan-chuzhang-jiangdong-ruhe-yingdui-caocao-fanan-lgqs4-a9qgf21woeh-ppinw4z9jg|汉纪1058]] as [[XinPingLateHan|辛评]]'s younger brother and [[YuanTan|袁谭]]'s envoy to [[CaoCao|曹操]]. His role marks the moment Yuan Tan turns from resisting Cao Cao with [[YuanShang|袁尚]] to seeking Cao Cao's aid against his own brother.

## Current Profile
After Yuan Shang defeats Yuan Tan and then besieges him at Pingyuan, Yuan Tan sends Xin Pi to Cao Cao for help. The episode treats this mission as the downstream result of Guo Jia's diagnosis: once Cao Cao relaxes pressure, Yuan-family infighting becomes so severe that one claimant seeks outside support.

Xin Pi is not developed as an independent strategist in this source. His profile is important because his mission makes the factional split actionable for Cao Cao.

## Key Characteristics
- Envoy carrying Yuan Tan's request for Cao Cao's aid against Yuan Shang.
- Kin-linked figure in the Xin family cluster, identified as Xin Ping's younger brother.
- Marker of the Yuan-family conflict crossing from internal dispute into outside-power dependence.
- Source-scoped actor whose broader career is not yet synthesized in this wiki.
