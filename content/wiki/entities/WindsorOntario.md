# Windsor, Ontario

Windsor, Ontario appears in [[outsider-chance-ending-sudans-proxy-war-6a75b3695d765781a2b1b132]] as the [[Canada|Canadian]] side of the [[GordieHoweBridge]] and [[AmbassadorBridge]] crossings with [[Detroit]]. The source uses Windsor to locate the trade corridor rather than to profile the city independently.

The bridge segment says the new crossing integrates better with Canada's highway system than the older Ambassador Bridge. That practical logistics improvement is what makes the later political dispute a case of [[CrossBorderInfrastructureTradeTension]].
