# Mary Quant

## Overview

Mary Quant is the British designer-retailer whose Bazaar boutique is associated in the episode with informal, colorful youth fashion and the rise of the miniskirt.

## Current Profile

Quant's significance lies less in a settled claim to single-handed invention than in making a broader fashion shift visible and purchasable. Bazaar, opened in 1955, helped connect shorter hemlines, lighter clothing, color, informality, and boutique retail to a growing youth market.

## Key Characteristics

- Opened the Bazaar boutique in 1955.
- Associated with the popularization of the miniskirt.
- Helped move fashion toward informal, colorful, lighter clothing.
- Joined design innovation to youth-oriented boutique retail.
