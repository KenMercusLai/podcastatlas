# Lamar Hunt

Lamar Hunt is the sports entrepreneur in [[the-nfl-1]] who helped found the [[AmericanFootballLeague|American Football League]] after failing to secure an NFL franchise. The episode presents him as a key rival-league builder whose willingness to start a competing football league forced the [[NationalFootballLeague|NFL]] to respond.

Hunt's AFL strategy mattered because the league pursued centralized national TV rights before playing a game. That media-first posture, combined with talent competition and [[AlDavis]]'s later escalation, pushed the NFL toward merger negotiations and the eventual [[SuperBowl]] structure.
