# Robin Dunbar / 罗宾·邓巴

Robin Dunbar / 罗宾·邓巴 enters the wiki through [[83-shumao-bagua-ji-yuyan-de-jinhua-renji-guanxi-dengbashu-zan-bu-fu-bu-xing-698132861]], where [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] presents him as an evolutionary psychologist associated with [[DunbarNumber|Dunbar Number / 邓巴数]] and the book [[GroomingGossipAndEvolutionOfLanguage|《梳毛、八卦及语言的进化》]]. The episode uses his work to argue that language, gossip, and everyday conversation can be understood as social-bond maintenance rather than as trivial leftover speech.

The page is source-scoped. The episode treats Dunbar's model as powerful and memorable, but also reminds listeners that evolutionary explanations, especially around sex differences and courtship behavior, should not become deterministic judgments about individuals.

## Key Claims
- Dunbar is presented as the theorist behind the approximate 150-person relationship-capacity claim.
- His language-origin argument ties together primate group size, grooming time, neocortex capacity, and human conversation.
- The episode uses Dunbar to revalue small talk, gossip, art, religion, and podcasting as relationship-maintenance systems.
- The wiki should keep Dunbar's claims as explanatory models, not as hard limits or moral rules.
