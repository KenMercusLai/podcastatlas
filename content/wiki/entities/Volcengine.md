# Volcengine / 火山引擎

Volcengine, called 火山引擎 in the source, appears in [[kafeidou-chuantong-meishi-guangchang-jielian-bidian-dashidaimen-yudao-naxie-fazhan-zuai-1007530222]] as the [[ByteDance]] infrastructure and services channel announcing that [[Tesla]] China's in-car system had formally connected to [[Doubao]]. The episode frames the integration as Tesla's first introduction of a third-party large model after entering China.

The source presents Doubao as an independent in-car app with continuous conversation and information search, but says the current version cannot directly control the vehicle. Volcengine's role is therefore the enabling enterprise/vehicle integration layer rather than the user-facing assistant brand.
