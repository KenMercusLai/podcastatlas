# 史湘云 / Shi Xiangyun

[[ritan-chuantai-hongloumeng-haishi-keyi-du-yidu-de-587167638]] uses 史湘云 to show that [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]]'s women are not arranged only around [[JiaBaoyu|贾宝玉]]. The episode describes her as quick, open, talented, and somewhat boyish, with a social brightness that initially makes [[XueBaochai|薛宝钗]]'s care attractive to her.

The Mid-Autumn linked-poem scene with [[LinDaiyu|林黛玉]] makes Xiangyun's relation to Daiyu more serious. Their poetic play grows increasingly sad, and [[Miaoyu|妙玉]]'s later intervention shows how [[GrandViewGarden|大观园]] friendship can become a shared intuition of fate rather than simple character rivalry.
