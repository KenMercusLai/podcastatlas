# Zhao Chongguo / 赵充国 (Western Han)

## Overview
Zhao Chongguo is the Western Han general whom Hanji 689-3 uses to connect the "讨南羌" brocade fragment with a 63 BCE frontier crisis. In this source, his role is the elderly commander who makes a celestial-omen formula operational in a campaign against Qiang-Xiongnu disruption of the Han-Western-Regions route.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Zhao as already in his seventies when Qiang groups influenced by [[Xiongnu]] planned to attack Shanshan and Dunhuang and cut Han communications with the Western Regions. Under [[EmperorXuanOfHan]], Zhao volunteers for the campaign, and the source links his operation to an omen-worded command formula that makes the [[FiveStarsOutOfTheEastBrocade]] intelligible as more than decorative text.

## Key Characteristics
- Zhao is portrayed as an elderly but still active Western Han frontier commander.
- His campaign answers a coordinated Qiang-Xiongnu threat to Han-Western-Regions communications.
- The source connects his mission to Emperor Xuan's five-star omen formula.
- His victory is the military background for the brocade's "讨南羌" reconstruction.
- Zhao's case shows omen language operating inside concrete logistics and frontier security problems.

## Qualifications
The source's Zhao page is campaign-centered, not a full biography. The brocade-to-campaign connection is plausible inside the episode's interpretation, but the artifact's precise production, recipient, and circulation remain uncertain.
