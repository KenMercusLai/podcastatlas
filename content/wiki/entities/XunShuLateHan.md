## Overview

荀淑 / Xun Shu is a late-Han Yingchuan county-magistrate figure currently grounded by the [[YingchuanFourMagistrates|颍川四长]] label. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-842-shenme-shi-haoguan-ta-wei-baixing-gan-pinming-lj2okcbcvcmrfqrj3d-k1ylqdi2si|Hanji 842]] groups him with [[HanShaoLateHan|韩韶]], [[ZhongHaoYingchuanLateHan|钟皓]], and [[ChenShiLateHan|陈寔]] as local officials remembered for virtuous governance and public benefit.

## Current Profile

Xun Shu's current profile is deliberately compact because the source note supplies cohort evidence rather than a separate episode about his decisions. His significance in this wiki is that he helps define the Four Magistrates as a shared local-governance ideal instead of leaving Han Shao's granary relief as an isolated case.

The page therefore treats Xun Shu as a member of a county-level reputation cluster. Additional sources are needed before assigning him specific methods, offices, or family-network roles beyond the Hanji 842 claim.
