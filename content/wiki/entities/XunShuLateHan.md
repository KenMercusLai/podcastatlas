## Overview

荀淑 / Xun Shu is a late-Han Yingchuan county-magistrate and reputation figure now grounded by three bounded layers. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-842-shenme-shi-haoguan-ta-wei-baixing-gan-pinming-lj2okcbcvcmrfqrj3d-k1ylqdi2si|Hanji 842]] groups him with [[HanShaoLateHan|韩韶]], [[ZhongHaoYingchuanLateHan|钟皓]], and [[ChenShiLateHan|陈寔]] as one of the [[YingchuanFourMagistrates|颍川四长]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-832-wei-lingdao-beiguo-ta-shi-sha-haishi-zhi-lmfpuixmi4odrf6lu31rf6-66-lq|Hanji 832]] adds a reputation comparison with Zhong Hao. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-798-donghan-shiqi-de-baixing-you-duo-ku-loolveumdtv4eo-omubfi5z2p2r5|Hanji 798]] now adds his direct recognition of fourteen-year-old [[HuangXianLateHan|黄宪]] as an unusually deep moral figure.

## Current Profile

Xun Shu's current profile remains reputation-heavy because the source notes supply more evaluation than administrative detail. Hanji 842 makes him part of the Yingchuan Four Magistrates, a county-office cohort remembered for virtuous governance and benefit to the people. His significance in that layer is that he helps define the Four Magistrates as a shared local-governance ideal instead of leaving Han Shao's granary relief as an isolated case.

Hanji 832 adds a second reputation layer. The episode says Zhong Hao and Xun Shu enjoyed comparable standing, and Li Ying's comment treats Xun Shu's清识 or lofty discernment as difficult to match. This strengthens Xun Shu as a famous-worthy benchmark, but it still does not give a concrete administrative act.

Hanji 798 gives Xun Shu a more concrete people-judgment scene. While lodging in Shenyang, he meets the fourteen-year-old Huang Xian, whose father is described as a veterinarian. Xun Shu bows to him, speaks with him for an extended period, and later tells [[YuanLangLateHan|袁朗]] that Runan has a Yan Hui-like person. Yuan identifies that person as Huang Xian. This adds direct evaluative action to Xun Shu's profile: he recognizes moral depth across status and age boundaries.

## Key Characteristics

- Yingchuan county-magistrate figure named as one of the Yingchuan Four Magistrates.
- Associated with 德政 and people-benefiting local administration through the cohort label.

- Famous-worthy evaluator whose清识 is praised as difficult to match.
- Early recognizer of Huang Xian across low birth and youth.

## Qualifications

The current evidence does not provide Xun Shu's independent biography, exact county posting, or specific governing acts. Hanji 832 adds reputation praise but not a new action scene, while Hanji 798 adds a recognition scene rather than an administrative policy. The page should not import later Xun-family detail unless a source note explicitly grounds it.
