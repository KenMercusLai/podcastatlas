## Overview

荀淑 / Xun Shu is a late-Han Yingchuan county-magistrate figure grounded by the [[YingchuanFourMagistrates|颍川四长]] label and by Hanji 832's reputation comparison with [[ZhongHaoYingchuanLateHan|钟皓]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-842-shenme-shi-haoguan-ta-wei-baixing-gan-pinming-lj2okcbcvcmrfqrj3d-k1ylqdi2si|Hanji 842]] groups him with [[HanShaoLateHan|韩韶]], Zhong Hao, and [[ChenShiLateHan|陈寔]] as local officials remembered for virtuous governance and public benefit.

## Current Profile

Xun Shu's current profile is still compact because the source notes supply reputation evidence rather than a separate episode about his decisions. His significance in this wiki is that he helps define the Four Magistrates as a shared local-governance ideal instead of leaving Han Shao's granary relief as an isolated case.

Hanji 832 adds a second reputation layer. The episode says Zhong Hao and Xun Shu enjoyed comparable standing, and Li Ying's comment treats Xun Shu's清识 or lofty discernment as difficult to match. This strengthens Xun Shu as a famous-worthy benchmark, but it still does not give a concrete administrative act. Additional sources are needed before assigning him specific methods, offices, or family-network roles beyond the current claims.
