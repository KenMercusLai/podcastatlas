# Alpha School

## Overview
Alpha School is an education project first named in the wiki as part of [[StandTogether|Stand Together]]'s school-experiment portfolio and now profiled directly through a [[HubermanLab]] episode with [[JoeLiemandt]].

## Current Profile
The current evidence changes Alpha School from a source-scoped portfolio mention into a directly described school model. In the new episode, Alpha compresses core academics into roughly two hours of individualized app-based learning, uses mastery and prerequisite repair to target each student's current level, and assigns human guides to motivation, identity, standards, and development. The rest of the day is framed around [[BuilderBasedSchoolDay]] activities: sports, leadership, teamwork, entrepreneurship, financial literacy, storytelling, public speaking, labs, music, coding, and passion projects. The strongest performance and scaling claims remain source-scoped because the source reports planned or underway MIT Blueprint Labs testing rather than completed independent validation.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses a roughly two-hour individualized academic block instead of full-day age-grade classroom pacing.
- Builds academics around [[AIMasteryLearningModel]]: right-level lessons, mastery thresholds, prerequisite repair, retention checks, and measured learning rate.
- Pairs high standards with high support through human guides who focus on motivation, confidence, identity blocks, and development.
- Treats motivation as school infrastructure through goals, peer belonging, incentives, public projects, sports access, and adult relationships.
- Uses afternoons for builder work, life skills, labs, communication, entrepreneurship, sports, and passion projects.
- Presents ambitious performance, voucher, Founder School, GT Squared, and lower-cost scaling claims that remain source-scoped.

## Evidence
- Portfolio evidence: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-charles-chase-koch-on-how-they-quietly-built-a-150b-empire-41270895]] names Alpha School as one education effort supported by [[StandTogether|Stand Together]] in a broader school-experiment and barrier-removal portfolio.
- Academic design: [[how-to-accelerate-learning-improve-education-joe-liemandt-scim7393383815]] describes the two-hour app-based academic block, mastery thresholds, prerequisite repair, spaced recall, and learning-rate measurement.
- Human guide and motivation design: [[how-to-accelerate-learning-improve-education-joe-liemandt-scim7393383815]] says guides focus on motivation and identity blocks while citing the high-standards/high-support frame.
- Builder day: [[how-to-accelerate-learning-improve-education-joe-liemandt-scim7393383815]] describes entrepreneurship, financial literacy, public speaking, sports, Alpha X, Alpha City Limits, labs, coding with [[Cursor]], and other applied work.
- Scaling and validation boundary: [[how-to-accelerate-learning-improve-education-joe-liemandt-scim7393383815]] gives Alpha's internal performance, voucher, GT Squared, and Founder School claims while saying independent testing and RCTs with [[MITBlueprintLabs]] are planned or underway.

## Qualifications
The direct Alpha profile depends on one participant account. Claims about top-percentile performance, average SAT scores, two-hour academic productivity, grade-level repair, voucher demand, Founder School guarantees, GT Squared scale, and lower-cost models are not independently validated by the current source set. The prior Koch-family source only establishes Alpha as a supported school experiment, not as evidence for the operating model.
