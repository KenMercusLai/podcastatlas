# James Yorke Scarlett

## Overview

Sir James Yorke Scarlett was the British officer appointed to command the Heavy Brigade during the [[CrimeanWar|Crimean War]].

## Current Profile

[[722-the-charge-of-the-light-brigade-the-tsars-terrible-siege-part-2-glt7837711407]] presents Scarlett as another product of an appointment system that could place officers without combat experience in senior cavalry command. Unlike the more openly quarrelsome Light Brigade leadership, however, Scarlett recognized the gap and surrounded himself with officers who had served in India.

His profile therefore complicates a simple experience-versus-inexperience division. Scarlett lacked battlefield experience at appointment, but the source treats his use of experienced subordinates as a partial compensating practice rather than proof that the gap did not matter.

## Key Characteristics

- Entered command of the Heavy Brigade without prior combat experience.
- Served inside the same divided cavalry structure as Lucan and Cardigan.
- Recruited or relied on officers with Indian service to compensate for his own experience gap.
- Appears less personally disruptive than the commanders of the Light Brigade chain.

## Evidence

- Appointment gap - [[722-the-charge-of-the-light-brigade-the-tsars-terrible-siege-part-2-glt7837711407]] states that Scarlett had not seen combat before receiving brigade command.
- Compensating staff choice - [[722-the-charge-of-the-light-brigade-the-tsars-terrible-siege-part-2-glt7837711407]] emphasizes his decision to surround himself with India-experienced officers.
- Command structure - [[722-the-charge-of-the-light-brigade-the-tsars-terrible-siege-part-2-glt7837711407]] places his Heavy Brigade beside Cardigan’s Light Brigade under Lucan’s overall cavalry command.

## Qualifications

The episode provides only a short pre-battle portrait and does not assess Scarlett’s later battlefield performance. Lack of prior combat experience is not sufficient by itself to determine command quality, while the exact composition and influence of his experienced staff remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Established Scarlett as an inexperienced appointee who responded by drawing on experienced subordinates.
