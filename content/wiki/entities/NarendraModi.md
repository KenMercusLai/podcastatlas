# Narendra Modi

[[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]] adds Narendra Modi as the political target and response figure around India's [[CockroachMovement]]. The source says the protests challenged his image of being resistant to public pressure, first through the unexpected resignation of [[DharmendraPradhan]] and then through Modi's late-night apology video, which Gen Z protesters mocked online.

Narendra Modi appears in [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]] as a foreign leader who asked [[MinAungHlaing]] about [[AungSanSuuKyi]]'s welfare on June 1. The episode uses that exchange as evidence that proof-of-life pressure has reached high-level diplomatic conversations.

His role in the Myanmar source is narrowly about [[ProofOfLifeDiplomacy]]. That episode does not treat him as the main subject; it uses his question to show that the [[MyanmarMilitaryJunta]] is being pressed by outsiders despite its control over access.
