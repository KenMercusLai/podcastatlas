# Fede Alvarez / Fede Álvarez

Fede Alvarez appears in [[the-real-horror-of-alien-and-how-it-explains-why-were-not-paid-enough]] as co-writer and director of [[AlienRomulus|Alien: Romulus]]. He tells [[PlanetMoney]] that strong [[AlienFranchise|Alien]] movies foreground individual powerlessness before a larger machine, which makes labor themes central rather than incidental.

The episode also uses Alvarez's background in [[Uruguay]] as a comparison point. His comments about guaranteed vacation, healthcare, and severance protections help connect Alien's corporate dystopia to [[PaidVacationAsLaborRight]], [[EmployerBargainedBenefits]], and labor institutions in the [[UnitedStates]].
