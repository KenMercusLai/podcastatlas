# 闵贡 / Min Gong (late Han)

闵贡 / Min Gong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-946-zhichang-douzheng-shi-yourouguaduan-you-duo-zhiming-lpyyweaajonzg7kxuhwm9z7ae7p5|Hanji 946]] as the official who catches up with the fleeing imperial party near [[Xiaopingjin|小平津]]. After [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]] and [[DuanGuiLateHan|段珪]] take [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]] and Liu Xie, later [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], toward the Yellow River, Min Gong rebukes Zhang Rang and kills several eunuchs before Zhang Rang and the remaining party jump into the river.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-947-dongzhuo-shoufu-lvbu-kaiqi-chengba-zhilu-lvr-24l6j8f6zvpfcmqgknmgywa1|Hanji 947]] then makes Min Gong a named figure in the continued recovery scene after the [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] palace disorder. The source says the Chenliu prince and Min Gong share a horse while following the fleeing young emperor.

His page is intentionally narrow. Min Gong matters here because he marks the physical vulnerability of the imperial party before [[DongZhuo|董卓]] arrives: the emperor and prince are still being moved by fragile personal escort rather than by a secure court, which lets Dong Zhuo's later "rescue" immediately become [[ProtectorCaptorAmbiguity|护驾与挟持模糊性]].
