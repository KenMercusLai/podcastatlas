# VOO / Vanguard S&P 500 ETF

VOO appears in [[vol-266-yi-ci-xing-gao-dong-etf-1002344828]] as the scale endpoint of the low-fee broad-index ETF race. The source says Vanguard's S&P 500 ETF became the first ETF with more than $1 trillion in assets in June 2026, a market-size claim recorded here as source-scoped.

The page's wiki role is comparative. [[SPY]] represents the early [[StateStreet]] ETF breakthrough that [[JohnBogle]] originally resisted, while VOO represents [[Vanguard]] eventually embracing the ETF wrapper after [[PassiveInvesting]] and fee compression had become central to household investing.

## Key Claims
- VOO is used by the source as evidence that broad, low-fee index ETFs can absorb enormous assets without the same capacity problem as many active funds.
- The source attributes part of VOO's recent scale growth to the strength of large technology companies inside the [[SP500|S&P 500]].
- VOO shows how an institution built around mutual funds and index ideology can later adopt the ETF wrapper at scale.
