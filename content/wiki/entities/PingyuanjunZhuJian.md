## Overview

Zhu Jian / 朱建, styled Pingyuan Jun, appears as a cautioning adviser before [[YingBu|英布 / 黥布]]'s rebellion and later as the intermediary who uses [[HongRu|闳孺]]'s access to [[LiuYing|汉惠帝刘盈]] to avert the threatened killing of [[ShenYiji|审食其]].

## Current Profile

In the [[HuainanKingdomEarlyHan|淮南国]] decision crisis, Zhu Jian counsels Ying Bu not to rebel after [[BenHe|奔鹤]]'s accusation and a Han investigation, but loses to [[LiangfuHou|梁富侯]]'s claim that revolt is the only remaining path.

A later palace dispute supplies a successful mediation case. Zhu Jian tells Hong Ru that Shen Yiji's death would be attributed to the favorite's private influence and would provoke [[LuZhi|吕雉]]. By converting another man's danger into Hong Ru's self-protection problem, he activates the access channel that changes Liu Ying's decision.

Together, the sources portray Zhu Jian as a risk reader who prefers de-escalation and indirect leverage to irreversible action. The Huainan advice fails because the ruler judges danger already unavoidable; the palace mediation succeeds because Zhu Jian can align the access-holder's survival with restraint.

## Key Characteristics

- He argues against rebellion when investigation still leaves a possible nonviolent course.
- He recognizes when direct access to the ruler is blocked or ineffective.
- He maps the incentives of ruler, favorite, intended victim, and stronger palace authority together.
- His successful warning turns abstract factional conflict into an immediate survival risk for Hong Ru.
- His influence depends on persuasion and risk framing rather than command authority.

## Qualifications

Both sources are structured podcast summaries rather than primary texts. Hanji 186 calls him “平原君诸建,” while Hanji 372-3 uses the historically normalized “平原君朱建”; the wiki treats these as one person and keeps him disambiguated from the Warring States [[Pingyuanjun|平原君 / Lord Pingyuan]]. Hanji 372-3 also corrupts Hong Ru and Shen Yiji's names, and neither source establishes Zhu Jian's full career or exact words.
