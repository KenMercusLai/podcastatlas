# 平原君诸建 / Pingyuanjun Zhu Jian

平原君诸建 / Pingyuanjun Zhu Jian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-186-yi-chang-yin-bajie-lingdao-laopo-yinfa-de-miemen-can-an-lqx14uzf60oi6wnnzdkottd6kx1n|Hanji 186]] as the Huainan chancellor who argues against [[YingBu|英布 / 黥布]]'s decision to rebel after [[BenHe|奔鹤]] has fled and Han investigators arrive. The source names him as "平原君诸建"; this page is deliberately disambiguated from the Warring States [[Pingyuanjun|平原君 / Lord Pingyuan]].

His role is brief but structurally important. He represents the counsel that rebellion is not the only possible answer to [[DifferentSurnameKingSuspicion|异姓诸侯王猜忌]] and the Han investigation. In the episode's account, Ying Bu rejects that advice and instead accepts the harder line associated with [[LiangfuHou|梁富侯]], making Pingyuanjun Zhu Jian the losing voice in the Huainan decision sequence.
