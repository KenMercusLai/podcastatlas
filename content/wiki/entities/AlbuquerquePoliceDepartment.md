# Albuquerque Police Department

The Albuquerque Police Department enters the wiki through [[kyiv-brawl-ukraines-top-general-fired-6a609aec4fac21e67f1be165]], where it is the institutional focus of a segment on police killings in [[Albuquerque]] and [[NewMexico]]. The episode says the department spent 11 years under a federal consent decree after a [[USDepartmentOfJustice|U.S. Department of Justice]] report criticized excessive force.

For the source, APD is a test case for [[PoliceConsentDecreeCultureGap]]. Policies on drawing guns, tasers, body cameras, and oversight can change practice, but [[AlfredMathewson]] argues the decree did not accomplish its central purpose if shootings continued and culture remained aggressive or risk-averse in the wrong ways.
