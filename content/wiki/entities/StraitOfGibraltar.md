# Strait of Gibraltar / 直布罗陀海峡

## Overview
The Strait of Gibraltar / 直布罗陀海峡 is the narrow maritime passage between North Africa and the [[IberianPeninsula|Iberian Peninsula]] that structures the Ceuta episode's geography.

## Current Profile
In the source, the strait makes [[Ceuta]] disproportionately important. Ceuta sits on the North African shore near [[Morocco]], across from [[Gibraltar]] and southern [[Spain]], so control over the city touches Mediterranean-Atlantic passage, invasion routes, trade, and modern EU border politics.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects the Mediterranean Sea with the Atlantic Ocean.
- Places Ceuta and Gibraltar in a paired north-south gateway relationship.
- Gives small coastal strongpoints large strategic and symbolic value.
- Serves as the episode's spatial hinge between migration news and long maritime history.
