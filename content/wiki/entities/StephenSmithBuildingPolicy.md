# Stephen Smith

## Overview
Stephen Smith is the founder of the [[CenterForBuildingInNorthAmerica]] and the principal researcher interviewed about American elevator scarcity, cost, labor, and cabin-size rules.

## Current Profile
Smith approaches elevators as a housing and accessibility system rather than a single product. His comparative examples link installation price and cabin footprint to whether small apartment buildings can include any elevator at all.

His personal experience with postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome gives the argument an everyday mobility stake: severe fatigue sometimes prevented him from leaving a third-floor apartment without an elevator. The profile remains source-scoped because the episode does not supply the underlying cross-country dataset or a full causal cost decomposition.

## Key Characteristics
- Investigates overlooked building-code and construction-cost differences across North America and Europe.
- Connects elevator design standards to housing feasibility and disability access.
- Distinguishes labor-cost contributions from the larger effects he attributes to cabin size and regulation.
- Uses personal mobility experience alongside comparative institutional research.
