# 楼烦 / Loufan

楼烦 / Loufan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-65-wo-chuanyue-qu-gei-langman-de-chuwang-nian-shou-shi-lvyd7pve-tfu4ixsqrbdvhm6g64j]] when [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] inspects Zhao's northern frontier and meets a Loufan chieftain in the West River region. The episode says Zhao Wuling Wang recruits Loufan fighters after earlier recruiting Linhu troops in the [[HufuQishe|胡服骑射]] reform sequence.

Loufan matters here as manpower and frontier expertise. The source treats the recruitment as another step in Zhao's military strengthening: cavalry reform is not only clothing and technique, but also the incorporation of northern fighters into [[ZhaoState|赵国]]'s war capacity.
