## Overview

乌孙 / Wusun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-591-1-yichang-bingjia-ruhe-xianxie-yinbao-liangguo-zhizhan-lrq-xdhvrnuarzwgrykp7j-x8j-a|Hanji 591-1]] as the Western Regions polity whose internal weakness is folded into a late [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] court debate over a delayed [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] chanyu visit. The episode does not make Wusun the central actor; it shows how Wusun becomes a strategic object in Han court threat modeling.

## Current Profile

The source describes Wusun through a narrow diplomatic-risk frame. [[XiFuGongWesternHan|息夫躬]] claims that Wusun's large and small kunmi are weak, that a fugitive or powerful military figure has links with Xiongnu, and that the situation could let Xiongnu absorb Wusun influence and endanger the Western Regions.

Wusun's current wiki significance is therefore indirect. The episode uses Wusun to show how frontier complexity can be mobilized inside Han court politics: uncertain information about western polities becomes the basis for a proposed false-envoy and hostage-pressure operation.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Regions polity invoked in a late Western Han court debate.
- Described through internal division between large and small kunmi.
- Used as part of a Xiongnu threat model rather than as an independently narrated actor.
- Connected to hostage diplomacy and coerced petition theater in Xi Fu Gong's plan.
- Currently source-scoped to Hanji 591-1.
