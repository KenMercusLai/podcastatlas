# 厂二代GoGoGo

厂二代GoGoGo is the [[FactorySecondGeneration|厂二代]] community described in [[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw]]. The episode says it was started by Sherry after she posted about the pain of returning from overseas study to take over a family business, and that it had grown to more than 5,000 members by the time of the discussion.

The community matters because it turns succession from an isolated family obligation into peer infrastructure. Members can compare role confusion, parent-child conflict, self-media attempts, government courses, and business-school learning, making [[ChineseFamilyBusinessSuccession]] a social and knowledge-sharing field.

## Source Position
- The community gives successors a place to name pressures that are hard to discuss inside the family firm.
- It overlaps with [[FactorySecondGenerationPersonalIP]] because some members use videos, accounts, and public storytelling to build visibility.
- The episode also notes gender difference in willingness to appear publicly: some male successors reject video collaboration as too exposed.
