# 孔斌 / Kong Bin

孔斌 / Kong Bin enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-2-lutsvwvnwzsezoorhe-vu7yjthyx]] as the [[WeiState|魏国]] minister who pushes back against his court's opportunistic reading of the [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]]. When [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] asks whether Qin's attack on [[ZhaoState|赵国]] benefits Wei, most ministers answer yes: if [[QinState|秦国]] wins, Wei can submit to the stronger side; if Qin stalls, Wei may look for a chance to strike.

Kong Bin rejects that logic. He argues that Qin has been hard to stop since [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]], now has [[BaiQi|白起]] in command, and will not end its expansion simply because Zhao suffers first. His warning makes him a source case for the non-obvious part of [[CoalitionSelfPreservationFailure|联盟自保失灵]]: weak states may treat a neighbor's disaster as profit even when the same threat is moving toward them.

The episode's metaphor gives Kong Bin an interpretive role beyond Wei court politics. Like swallows under a burning roof, Wei's ministers see their immediate perch but not the structural fire. In the host's reading, that fire is Qin's post-[[ShangYangReforms|商鞅变法]] war machine: [[FarmingWarStatecraft|耕战逻辑]] gives Qin continuing incentives to seek battle, reward, and territory.
