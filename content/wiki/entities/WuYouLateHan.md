## Overview

吴祐 / Wu You is the general-in-chief's longshi in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-830-donghan-ligu-wo-sui-si-yourong-lt-j7n9gotiw-negqxojoacf6ji|Hanji 830]] who argues for [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] after [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] reasserts the accusation. His defense cannot save Li Gu, but it marks an officeholder still willing to make the cost of the accusation public.

## Current Profile

Hanji 830 gives Wu You a short but sharp intervention. When Liang Ji again accuses Li Gu after the release order, Wu You argues on Li Gu's behalf and challenges [[MaRongLateHan|马融]] over the written charge, asking how they will answer the world if Li Gu dies.

Liang Ji does not accept the warning. After Li Gu and Du Qiao die, Liang moves Wu You out as Hejian kingdom minister; Wu You refuses the posting, resigns, and later dies at home. The page treats him as a failed correction voice inside Liang Ji's own administrative orbit.

## Key Characteristics

- General-in-chief's longshi who speaks for Li Gu inside the accusation process.
- Uses public accountability language against the written criminal charge.
- Cannot prevent Li Gu's prison death once Liang Ji insists.
- Is moved away from the court center through a Hejian appointment.
- Chooses resignation rather than accepting Liang Ji's relocation.
