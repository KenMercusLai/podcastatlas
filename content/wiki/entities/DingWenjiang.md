# 丁文江

丁文江 is central to [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] as a geologist, organizer, and model of [[FieldworkAsKnowledgeMethod]]. The episode says he became the first head of the [[GeologicalResearchInstitute|地质研究所]] at a very young age and helped train a first generation of modern Chinese geologists through field survey, mining investigation, mapping, and laboratory practice.

The source also makes 丁文江 a reader and reinterpreter of [[XuXiake|徐霞客]]. Carrying 《徐霞客游记》 in the field, he recognizes that Xu had reached many places long before modern geology, then later edits and republishes Xu's text to restore the knowledge-seeking meaning behind the older travel record. His later administrative work for the central academy is presented as institution-building that advanced Chinese scholarship but also consumed his own research time.

## Key Claims
- Ding's fieldwork rejects the stereotype of Chinese scholars as book-bound and unwilling to walk the land.
- His geology connects science to mines, labor, national resources, and global events.
- His recovery of Xu Xiake gives modern field science a Chinese genealogy.
- The episode treats him as one of the clearest cases of [[ModernChineseAcademicInstitutionBuilding]] as scholarship.
