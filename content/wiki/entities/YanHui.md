# 颜回 / Yan Hui

颜回 / Yan Hui enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-994-genzhe-dongwu-zhongchen-lvfan-xuexi-zhichang-zhihui-lgqp2vqmh1mss96gcwmflp-ccpfu|Hanji 994]] as the [[Analects|《论语》]] figure behind the phrase "无伐善，无施劳." The host explains the line as not boasting about one's virtue and not pressing one's labor or merit onto others.

The episode uses Yan Hui to extend a management lesson into self-cultivation. After praising [[SunCe|孙策]] for not envying [[ZhangZhaoLateHan|张昭]]'s reputation, it turns to [[ZengGuofan|曾国藩]] and Yan Hui to say that people also need to avoid making others uncomfortable by self-display. Yan Hui therefore anchors the internal side of [[SubordinateSuccessNonJealousy|下属成就不嫉妒]]: not only resisting jealousy toward others, but also restraining one's own need to advertise merit.
