# National Health Commission of China / 国家卫健委

The National Health Commission of China appears in [[177-riben-yibao-jijin-weihe-yao-zhichi-mianfei-de-guomin-tijian-lh06qonrzgjprqdwl8dygn4hoxpk]] as the Chinese public-health authority behind recent obesity and weight-management signals. The source names obesity diagnosis guidance, weight-management clinics, chronic-disease management, and broader national-health planning as signs that [[China]] is shifting more policy attention from treatment to active health management.

The episode contrasts the commission's public-health role with [[NationalHealthcareSecurityAdministration|国家医保局]] as the payer-side institution. Its comparison point is [[Japan]], where [[JapaneseSpecificHealthCheckups]] are more directly tied to insurer duties and [[HealthInsurerPreventionAccountability|prevention accountability]].

[[bombs-and-bombshells-zelensky-under-pressure-6a86d3561ad8716bb64646f0]] extends the public-health branch from chronic disease into mental health. [[SarahWu]] describes a nationwide psychological-support and crisis-intervention push by 2030, including school counseling rooms, county-level psychiatric services, drug treatment, psychotherapy, and more professionals, while [[ChinaMentalHealthAccessGap]] and [[MentalHealthServiceRegulationGap]] remain unresolved.
