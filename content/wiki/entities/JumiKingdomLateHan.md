## Overview

居弥国 / Jumi Kingdom enters the wiki through Hanji 838 as the Western Regions polity whose ruler Chengguo exploits hostility toward Khotan to redirect Han investigation authority. The page is source-bounded to the episode's Jumi-Khotan rivalry rather than a full regional history.

## Current Profile

Jumi appears because Zhao Ping's son passes through it while escorting Zhao's coffin. [[JumiKingChengguoLateHan|居弥王成国]] uses that transit point to claim that Zhao Ping was not merely dead from illness but murdered at [[KhotanKingJianLateHan|于阗王建]]'s direction through a Xiongnu doctor.

That accusation becomes politically operative when [[WangJingXiyuLateHan|王敬]] also passes through Jumi. Chengguo tells Wang that Khotanese people want Chengguo as king, turning Jumi from a transit polity into the source of the narrative that drives Wang's intervention in Khotan.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Regions polity placed between Zhao Ping's death report and Wang Jing's mission.
- Rival polity whose ruler has an existing grievance against Khotan's king.
- Information channel through which an accusation becomes a frontier-policy trigger.
- Source case for how local rivalry can capture Han investigative authority.
