# Peacock

Peacock is the [[NBCUniversal]] streaming service discussed in [[tech-20260731-0731-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260731-0731-mp-tech-pod-128]]. The episode says Peacock content, including sports, original series, and Universal films, will become available through [[YouTubePremium|YouTube Premium]] under a multi-year deal.

In the source's framing, Peacock matters because it is no longer only a standalone streaming app competing for direct attention. Its content can become part of [[YouTube]]'s platform-native bundle, connecting legacy-studio libraries and live sports to [[StreamingPlatformBundling]] and [[YouTubeMediaDominance]].
