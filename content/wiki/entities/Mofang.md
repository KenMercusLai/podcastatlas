# 摩方 / Mofang

摩方 appears in [[137-qinli-riben-shengyu-butie-guojia-bang-wo-yangwa-guilai-reng-shi-tunjinshou-lowze7haomxbmqrrajpl51dvvxe7]] as a [[NihongDianbo|霓虹电波]] guest and father raising a child in [[Japan]]. His most vivid example is a newborn NICU stay for patent ductus arteriosus observation and treatment where the family ultimately paid only 1,800 yen, mostly for formula, making [[JapaneseChildMedicalCostCoverage|child medical cost coverage]] more concrete than an abstract subsidy table.

He also adds the father-side labor and cost view. The episode uses his examples of male colleagues taking childcare leave, private-school and cram-school spending, and a month with about 250,000 yen in education-related costs to show why [[JapaneseFertilitySupportSystem|Japan's support system]] can reduce baseline risk without eliminating [[EastAsianEducationCostPressure|education-driven household pressure]].
