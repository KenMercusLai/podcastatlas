# Gibraltar / 直布罗陀

## Overview
Gibraltar / 直布罗陀 appears in the Ceuta episode as the European-side counterpart to [[Ceuta]] across the [[StraitOfGibraltar|Strait of Gibraltar]].

## Current Profile
The source uses Gibraltar in two ways. First, its name is traced to Tariq ibn Ziyad's landing route in the Islamic conquest of Iberia. Second, British control after 1704 becomes a later comparison point for why strait strongpoints remain contested long after their original conquest.

## Key Characteristics
- Functions as the northern counterpart to Ceuta in the strait geography.
- Carries Islamic-conquest memory through the "Tariq's mountain" naming explanation.
- Serves as a later European great-power strongpoint after British capture.
