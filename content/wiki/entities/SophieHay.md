# Sophie Hay

## Overview

Sophie Hay is an archaeologist represented through twenty-five years of work at [[Pompeii]] and nearby sites, public communication for the archaeological park, and research on women archaeologists at Pompeii.

## Current Profile

Hay connects field practice, conservation, and public interpretation. She welcomes visitor interest while describing how tourism affects excavation work and how rotating closures expose only part of the site. Her interpretive method resists spectacular certainty: Pompeii is a [[PompeiiArchaeologicalPalimpsest|layered city]], conservation can be more important than fresh excavation, and bodies, inscriptions, food remains, graffiti, and religious traces must be kept within their evidentiary limits.

## Key Characteristics

- Archaeologist with long-term field experience at Pompeii and adjoining sites.
- Public communicator who values visitor interest while recognizing its practical effects on work.
- Advocate for conservation-led excavation and attention to small, contextual finds.
- Source critic who separates human connection from confident projection onto the dead.
- Researcher recovering the history of women archaeologists at Pompeii.
