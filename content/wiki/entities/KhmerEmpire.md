# Khmer Empire / 吴哥王朝

The Khmer Empire / 吴哥王朝 is the historical case at the center of [[ep267-tafang-meigonghe-yuji-hunfeiyi-yu-shuili-diguo-lkq2vxaatn4wxue7y5q1fc3-8yfk]]'s "water empire" discussion. The episode describes Angkor-era rule as lasting for roughly six centuries and leaving temple, sculpture, inscription, and hydraulic traces that make [[Angkor|吴哥]] a key site for understanding the [[MekongRiver|Mekong]] region.

In the source, Khmer imperial power is not only military or religious. It depends on [[AngkorHydraulicEmpire]]: rain-season storage, dry-season irrigation, [[TonleSapLake|Tonle Sap]] flood pulses, rice surplus, labor mobilization, and temple-building kingship all reinforce one another. The episode also notes that political succession could be unstable while new kings still restored legitimacy through sacred construction.
