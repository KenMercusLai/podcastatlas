# 《鸿门宴》

《鸿门宴》 is the power-scene from [[SimaQian|司马迁]]'s historical writing discussed in [[164-guwen-de-liliang-qing-shou-xia-zhe-fen-qiannian-wei-bian-de-shenqing-dongjian-he-yongqi-940236576]]. The episode treats it as more than a famous story about [[FanKuai|樊哙]]'s courage or [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s escape: the seat order, titles, warnings, performance, gifts, and anger all reveal an unstable power field.

The source reads the scene through [[PowerEtiquetteReading]]. [[XiangYu|项羽]] uses seating to humiliate Liu Bang, [[ZhangLiang|张良]] calibrates speech and gift-giving, [[FanZeng|范增]] sees danger but mishandles influence, and Fan Kuai's apparent bluntness works because it grants Xiang Yu the recognition he wants.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-140-xiangyu-yu-liubang-shi-zhen-youqing-haishi-xianghu-liyong-1-lmmhoq2wo3bs6b1johpc0ixuf662|Hanji 140 part 1]] adds the pre-banquet crisis setup. Before the literary banquet scene itself, [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s first-entry advantage in [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]], his closure of the pass, [[CaoWushang|曹无伤]]'s leak, [[FanZeng|范增]]'s warning, and [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s anger produce the decision to attack Liu Bang the next morning. [[XiangBo|项伯]] then appears as the personal channel through which the attack plan starts to reach [[ZhangLiang|张良]] and Liu Bang.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-140-xiangyu-yu-liubang-shi-zhen-youqing-haishi-xianghu-liyong-2-lvdgirxmwxcckh2loh-yk2zzer2e|Hanji 140 part 2]] fills in the night before the meeting. Xiang Bo warns Zhang Liang at [[Bashang|霸上]], Zhang Liang refuses to escape without Liu Bang, and Liu Bang uses ritual courtesy toward Xiang Bo to turn the expected attack into a next-morning personal audience. The banquet crisis therefore begins with a cancelled attack order, not only with seating and speech inside the banquet.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-141-huanyuan-lishi-zhong-de-hongmenyan-lpmb-bcoocngtqjvtnpj0elm-siy|Hanji 141]] begins the banquet itself. [[LiuBang|刘邦]] and [[ZhangLiang|张良]] enter while [[FanKuai|樊哙]], [[XiahouYing|夏侯婴]], [[JiXin|纪信]], [[JinQiang|金强]], and the mounted followers remain outside the gate. The episode reads the seating order, Liu Bang's apology, [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s exposure of [[CaoWushang|曹无伤]], [[FanZeng|范增]]'s ignored jade-ring signals, [[XiangZhuang|项庄]]'s sword dance, and [[XiangBo|项伯]]'s protective counter-dance as one unstable sequence where etiquette, source protection, face, and adviser authority collide.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-142-zhechang-juhui-xiangyu-shudiao-le-yiqie-ln7dv2jxrk6lt1c4szylsrb41k3r|Hanji 142]] completes the escape. [[FanKuai|樊哙]] forces his way in, rebukes Xiang Yu through the [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]] and Liu Bang's restraint in [[Xianyang|咸阳]], and gives the room a way to stand down. Liu Bang then leaves under a bathroom pretext, returns by small roads to [[Bashang|霸上]], while [[ZhangLiang|张良]] waits, presents the white bi and jade dipper, and converts flight into a managed ritual explanation. The same source adds [[ChenPing|陈平]] as the Xiang-camp searcher whose restraint helps the escape, and [[FanZeng|范增]]'s smashed jade dipper as the failed adviser's public verdict on Xiang Yu.

## Key Claims
- Ritual detail is political evidence, not decoration.
- Fan Kuai's courage is partly a staged speech act shaped by Zhang Liang's strategy.
- Fan Zeng's failure shows that strategic insight can fail when it cannot move the person with actual decision power.
- The scene opens a broader question about [[MoralFaceUnderPowerCompetition]]: whether power struggles must reward shamelessness.
- Hanji 140 part 1 adds that the banquet crisis begins before the seating order: pass control, private leaks, anger, and missing public justification set the room's danger level.
- Hanji 140 part 2 adds that the room exists because Xiang Bo's warning and Liu Bang's courtesy performance move Xiang Yu from immediate attack to face-to-face explanation.
- Hanji 141 adds the first banquet-room payoff: Xiang Yu's loose disclosure of Cao Wushang, Fan Zeng's ignored signals, and Xiang Zhuang's sword dance show that the cancelled attack order has become an internal Xiang-camp coordination failure.
- Hanji 142 adds that Liu Bang's escape is a coordinated role sequence: Fan Kuai breaks the danger, Zhang Liang controls timing and gifts, Liu Bang accepts temporary submission, Chen Ping does not expose the flight, and Fan Zeng's rage comes too late to change Xiang Yu's decision.
