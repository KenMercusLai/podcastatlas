# 《鸿门宴》

《鸿门宴》 is the power-scene from [[SimaQian|司马迁]]'s historical writing discussed in [[164-guwen-de-liliang-qing-shou-xia-zhe-fen-qiannian-wei-bian-de-shenqing-dongjian-he-yongqi-940236576]]. The episode treats it as more than a famous story about [[FanKuai|樊哙]]'s courage or [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s escape: the seat order, titles, warnings, performance, gifts, and anger all reveal an unstable power field.

The source reads the scene through [[PowerEtiquetteReading]]. [[XiangYu|项羽]] uses seating to humiliate Liu Bang, [[ZhangLiang|张良]] calibrates speech and gift-giving, [[FanZeng|范增]] sees danger but mishandles influence, and Fan Kuai's apparent bluntness works because it grants Xiang Yu the recognition he wants.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-140-xiangyu-yu-liubang-shi-zhen-youqing-haishi-xianghu-liyong-1-lmmhoq2wo3bs6b1johpc0ixuf662|Hanji 140 part 1]] adds the pre-banquet crisis setup. Before the literary banquet scene itself, [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s first-entry advantage in [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]], his closure of the pass, [[CaoWushang|曹无伤]]'s leak, [[FanZeng|范增]]'s warning, and [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s anger produce the decision to attack Liu Bang the next morning. [[XiangBo|项伯]] then appears as the personal channel through which the attack plan starts to reach [[ZhangLiang|张良]] and Liu Bang.

## Key Claims
- Ritual detail is political evidence, not decoration.
- Fan Kuai's courage is partly a staged speech act shaped by Zhang Liang's strategy.
- Fan Zeng's failure shows that strategic insight can fail when it cannot move the person with actual decision power.
- The scene opens a broader question about [[MoralFaceUnderPowerCompetition]]: whether power struggles must reward shamelessness.
- Hanji 140 part 1 adds that the banquet crisis begins before the seating order: pass control, private leaks, anger, and missing public justification set the room's danger level.
