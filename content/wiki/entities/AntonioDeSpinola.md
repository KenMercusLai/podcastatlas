# Antonio de Spinola

## Overview

Antonio de Spinola was a Portuguese general who became the senior figurehead of the [[CarnationRevolution]] and first president of the post-coup order.

## Current Profile

Spinola gave dissatisfied officers senior legitimacy after criticizing the [[PortugueseColonialWars|African wars]], and his rank enabled [[MarceloCaetano|Caetano]] to surrender without submitting directly to a captain. He then sought controlled liberalization, but occupations and accelerated decolonization outran his design; two failed coup attempts moved him from transitional president to defeated right-wing conspirator.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior military critic of Portugal's African-war strategy.
- Symbolic bridge between junior coup organizers and state authority.
- Favored a liberalized but controlled post-dictatorship settlement.
- Lost power as social mobilization and decolonization accelerated.
- Attempted failed interventions in September 1974 and March 1975.

- Failure evidence: [[273-portugal-the-carnation-revolution-glt3297148398]] follows his two failed coups, Brazilian exile, and effort to organize an extreme-right paramilitary group.
