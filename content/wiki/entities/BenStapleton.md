# Ben Stapleton

Ben Stapleton appears in [[tech-20260420-0420-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260420-0420-mp-tech-pod-128]] as CEO of the [[USGreenBuildingCouncilOfCalifornia|U.S. Green Building Council of California]]. He argues that a major barrier to sustainable construction materials is not only technical feasibility, but market visibility: buyers often do not know what options exist, and manufacturers may not invest without clear demand.

Stapleton's role is to turn [[California]]'s [[EmbodiedCarbonBuildingCode]] into a market-shaping policy. Because California is a large economy, he suggests that manufacturers adapting to its requirements may make [[LowCarbonConstructionMaterials]] more available elsewhere.
