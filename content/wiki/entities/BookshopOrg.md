# Bookshop.org

Bookshop.org is the online bookselling partner named in [[tech-20260417-0417-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260417-0417-mp-tech-pod-128]], where [[Spotify]] lets [[Android]] users in the [[UnitedStates|U.S.]] and [[UnitedKingdom|U.K.]] buy physical books through its app. [[MariaCurie|Maria Currie]] says Bookshop.org sources from independent local bookstores, which may make the partnership more attractive to consumers who want an alternative to buying books from [[Amazon]].

The page also connects back to [[bookstore-economics]], where Bookshop.org appears as one of the distribution channels for the [[PlanetMoneyBook]]. In the current source, Bookshop.org becomes more than a channel: it is the mechanism that lets a technology platform participate in physical-book commerce while claiming some alignment with independent bookstores.
