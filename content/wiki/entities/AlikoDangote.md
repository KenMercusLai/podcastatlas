# Aliko Dangote

## Overview

Aliko Dangote is the Nigerian industrialist presented by the episode as preparing a local listing of [[DangotePetroleumRefinery]] and framing it as both an ownership opportunity and an energy-security project.

## Current Profile

Dangote's role in this source joins industrial scale to public-market access. He proposes a Nigerian listing at a reported $50bn valuation and says he wants ownership broad enough to include ordinary Nigerian, African, rural, and diaspora investors. The episode also attributes the refinery's Nigerian location partly to African energy security.

The shareholder-democratization framing remains prospective. Demand before trading is described as intense, but the listing price, shareholder distribution, market performance, and possibility of a later New York listing were unsettled when the episode was produced.

## Key Characteristics

- Nigerian billionaire and industrial founder in the episode's account.
- Sponsor of the proposed Dangote Petroleum Refinery listing.
- Advocate of broad African retail participation in the offering.
- Business figure linking refinery ownership with African energy-security aims.
- Prospective cross-listing actor considering a later New York market presence.
