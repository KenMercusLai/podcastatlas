# Lawn Tennis Association

The Lawn Tennis Association appears in [[spring-then-fall-a-weakened-muslim-brotherhood-6a7aef1b616564bf00d9b354]] as the British source for [[Padel]] participation and court-growth estimates. The episode says Britain moved from about 87 courts and 15,000 players five years earlier to more than 2,000 courts and around one million players.

The LTA's role links padel to tennis infrastructure. Padel can fit several courts into the footprint of one tennis court, giving clubs a revenue reason to add capacity, but the episode also says British court fees remain high, making [[PadelCourtEconomics]] part of the access problem.
