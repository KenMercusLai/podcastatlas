## Overview

Roy Disney was Walt Disney's brother, practical supporter, and finance-operating partner, making creative expansion survivable through fundraising, debt discipline, negotiation, and institutional continuity.

## Current Profile

[[the-walt-disney-company-1]] presents Roy as the recurring counterweight to [[WaltDisney]]'s costly bets. He helped fund the studio, challenged financial risk, navigated outside capital, and after Walt's death completed [[WaltDisneyWorld]] while scaling back the planned-city vision and avoiding debt.

[[614-walt-disney-the-great-american-storyteller-glt5465018421]] supplies the earlier personal layer: Roy protected and encouraged Walt, was recovering from tuberculosis in Los Angeles when Walt moved there, and co-founded the Disney Brothers Studio. The relationship therefore began as family support before becoming a durable creative-operating partnership.

## Key Characteristics

- Protective older brother and early source of practical support.
- Co-founder of the Disney Brothers Studio.
- Finance and operating counterweight to Walt's creative risk-taking.
- Negotiator through debt, capital needs, and project expansion.
- Continuity leader who completed Walt Disney World after Walt's death.
