# Abdul El-Sayed

Abdul El-Sayed is the former public-health official and podcaster who wins Michigan's Democratic Senate primary in [[socialist-mobility-a-telling-michigan-vote-6a74600ec3e1347e8b5bf216]]. The source presents him as a left-wing candidate with a Rhodes Scholar and Columbia medical background, defeating establishment-backed [[HaleyStevens]] while still taking less than 50% of the primary vote.

His platform in the episode includes opposition to aid to [[Israel]], removing corporate money from politics, abolishing the filibuster, term limits for [[SupremeCourt]] justices, higher taxes on billionaires, and Medicare for All. The source also notes his earlier openness to defunding the police, making him a concrete case for [[StatewideLeftPrimaryTest]] and [[PrimaryElectorateDistortion]] in a competitive state.
