# International Stabilization Force

The International Stabilization Force appears in [[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]] as the security body expected to support the Gaza plan. The source says it is expected to begin with only a few hundred personnel from [[Morocco]] and [[Uganda]].

In the episode, the force illustrates the gap between institutional naming and operating capacity. A stabilization force can make a plan sound concrete, but the source leaves open whether a small initial deployment could protect territory, support [[GazaTechnocraticAdministration]], or manage the risks around [[Hamas]] and [[Israel|Israeli]] withdrawal.

## Connections
- [[Gaza]] - deployment theater.
- [[Morocco]] and [[Uganda]] - countries named as early personnel sources.
- [[NationalCommitteeForAdministrationOfGaza]] - civilian-administration body that would need security conditions.
- [[DisarmamentWithdrawalSequencing]] and [[GazaDealImplementationGap]] - implementation problems the force cannot solve alone.
