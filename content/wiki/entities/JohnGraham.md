# John Graham

John Graham is a [[Toast]] co-founder in [[toast-aman-narang-how-a-long-wait-for-the-dinner-check-launched-a-2-billion-business-6eca81bc-b0e1-47e2-a87d-f3b243e76a97]]. The source links him to [[Endeca]] and says he left after [[SteveFredette]] while [[AmanNarang]] initially stayed at [[Oracle]].

The episode presents John as one of the early builders of the original mobile payment app. His role helps make the Toast story a [[FounderIdeaPivot]] case: the first app was technically possible but not behaviorally or operationally strong enough, while the underlying restaurant POS problem became the larger company.
