## Overview

孟达 / Meng Da enters the current wiki as the Liu Bei-side commander who advances north from Zigui and attacks Fangling during the expansion following the Hanzhong victory.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1165-zhangji-zhuan-didiao-caishi-dingji-shengcun-zhihui-lr7x5qltfi_ikktqabowt1garphl|Hanji 1165]] says Meng Da attacks Fangling, where administrator Kuai Qi is killed. Liu Bei then sends [[LiuFengLateHan|刘封]] to take control of Meng Da's force, and the two continue toward Shangyong. The episode records the command transfer but does not establish whether it reflected distrust, reinforcement, hierarchy, or another motive.

## Key Characteristics

- Liu Bei-side field commander in the Fangling–Shangyong expansion.
- Leads the initial northern advance from Zigui.
- Continues under or alongside Liu Feng after a ruler-directed command transfer.
