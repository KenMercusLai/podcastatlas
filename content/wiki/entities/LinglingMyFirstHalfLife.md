# 玲玲 / Lingling (《我的前半生》)

## Overview
玲玲 / Lingling enters the wiki as the ordinary-coworker third-party figure in the TV version of [[MyFirstHalfLife|《我的前半生》]], interpreted in EP385 as effective at first and over-villainized later.

## Current Profile
The source treats Lingling as unsettling because she is not a glamorous stock antagonist. Her appeal to [[ChenJunsheng|陈俊生]] is tied to work support, reliability, and shared fatigue, making the affair socially plausible. The hosts argue that the drama later weakens this realism by turning her into an increasingly manipulative obstacle for [[LuoZijun|罗子君]] and [[HeHan|贺涵]]'s plot.

## Key Characteristics
- Ordinary coworker rather than stylized mistress.
- Work-support figure whose reliability helps explain the affair.
- Gendered audience-reception case because the actor/role absorbed heavy public anger.
- Over-villainized later antagonist whose early ambiguity is reduced.
- TV-adaptation addition that shifts attention away from the novel's self-rebuilding focus.
