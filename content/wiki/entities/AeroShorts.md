# AeroShorts

AeroShorts is the hybrid-shorts brand founded by [[AdrianAlvarez]] and discussed in [[advice-line-with-chris-riccobono-of-untuckit-c087247f-1f85-4c15-b4aa-4ffea0368c4c]]. The shorts are positioned for beach, pool, gym, hiking, volleyball, surfing, golf, and other active uses, while staying around a $35 price point.

The Advice Line question is how AeroShorts can become a mainstream athletic brand after about $100,000 in first-year sales, a second-year run rate around $400,000, and a source-scoped 60% repeat-purchase rate. [[GuyRaz]] and [[ChrisRiccobono]] push Adrian toward [[CoreTribePositioning]]: volleyball players and younger college/high-school buyers may be a better first wedge than a broad "shorts for everyone" promise.
