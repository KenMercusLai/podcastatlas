# 薛衣人 / Xue Yiren

## Overview

薛衣人 / Xue Yiren is the master swordsman whom the bounded [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episode regards as the richest character in [[BorrowedCorpseReturnsSoulGuLong|《借尸还魂》]]. His authority, restraint, lethal ability, family burden, and attempt to assume his brother's guilt prevent mastery from becoming a simple heroic credential.

## Current Profile

The novel first frames 薛衣人 through contrast: a disorderly household and a brother performing childish madness precede the appearance of a controlled, perceptive, and formidable swordsman. His invitation to inspect swords and his sudden test of [[ChuLiuxiang|楚留香]] join cultivated authority to real danger. The later duel preserves his status because 楚留香 does not defeat him by superior swordsmanship; he survives, disrupts certainty, traps the sword, refuses a cheap strike, and receives the swordsman's concession.

Family obligation supplies the darker half of the profile. When 薛笑人 is exposed as the likely leader of a professional assassin organization, 薛衣人 claims responsibility. The episode reads this primarily as an effort to absorb guilt for his brother, while preserving uncertainty over whether he knew of or shared any organizational role. 薛笑人的 suicide rejects that final protection and turns fraternal hierarchy into tragedy.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines exceptional swordsmanship with judgment, restraint, coldness, and formal courtesy.
- Gains depth through contrast with a household that appears unable to match his discipline.
- Tests others abruptly, making refinement coexist with lethal threat.
- Accepts a loss of terms rather than being shown as plainly inferior in force.
- Bears family responsibility beyond ordinary protection, attempting to take his brother's criminal blame.
- Remains morally qualified because the extent of his knowledge about the assassin organization is unresolved.

## Qualifications

The profile comes from one podcast's reading of one novel. The episode favors the interpretation that 薛笑人 is the real assassin leader and 薛衣人 is attempting to take responsibility, but it leaves open whether the elder brother knew of or participated in any part of the organization. Relative martial ranking is also bounded: the duel demonstrates an effective asymmetric strategy, not an uncontested hierarchy across the whole series.

## What Changed

- Created a profile joining martial mastery to family responsibility, moral uncertainty, and fraternal tragedy.
