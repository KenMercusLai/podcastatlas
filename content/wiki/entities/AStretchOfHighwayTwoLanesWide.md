# 《一段双车道公路》

[[AStretchOfHighwayTwoLanesWide|《一段双车道公路》]] is one of the major [[SarahPinsker|Sarah Pinsker / 莎拉·平斯克]] stories discussed in [[166-youyou-changfeng-wenrou-de-huanxiang-zhi-ge-fuping-jiaolv-de-gouhe-948043059]]. In the episode's account, Andy loses an arm in a farming accident, receives a brain-computer-interface prosthetic, and begins to experience the arm as a specific two-lane road in eastern Colorado.

The story is useful to the wiki because it makes [[EmbodiedIdentityBoundary]] concrete. Andy's attachment is not only to a device, a lost limb, an old lover's tattoo, or a hallucinated place; it becomes an unnamed bond among body, chip, landscape, memory, and self-recognition. The episode reads the ending, where Susan converts the old Laurie tattoo into "科罗拉多", as a memorial to an experience that cannot fit inside ordinary relationship labels.
