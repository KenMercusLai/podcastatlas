# Wembley Stadium

Wembley Stadium appears in [[working-memory-the-surprising-decline-of-dementia-6a6b0eeca585d3be78cd9d1b]] as the venue used to demonstrate the scale of [[MyChemicalRomance]]'s comeback. The episode says the band filled the 90,000-seat stadium for three nights during its [[TheBlackParade]] anniversary tour.

In the source, Wembley is less a standalone venue story than a demand signal. It shows that [[EmoRevivalIdentityCulture]] had moved beyond club nostalgia into stadium-scale live culture.
