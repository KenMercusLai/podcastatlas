# First Republic Bank

First Republic Bank appears in [[tsr-s5-ronconway-v5-tsr-s5-ronconway-v5]] as the immediate contagion-risk example during the [[SiliconValleyBank]] weekend. [[RonConway]] says the group worried that if SVB deposits were not guaranteed before markets opened, bank-run fear could spread, with First Republic already rumored to be at high risk.

The source uses First Republic less as a standalone bank history and more as evidence for [[MoralHazardContagionTradeoff]]. If the issue were only one failed bank, moral hazard would dominate; if other banks could be pulled into runs, the episode argues that [[DepositGuaranteeCrisisResponse]] became a systemic-stability question.
