# Yuen Yuen Ang / 洪源远

Yuen Yuen Ang / 洪源远 enters the wiki through [[150-zhongguo-ruhe-tiaochu-pinkun-xianjing-xiandaihua-zhi-lu-you-hui-zouxiang-hefang-lgpvp04rngagkpubte2ac6i5ji3g]] as the author of [[HowChinaEscapedPovertyTrap|《中国如何跳出贫困陷阱》]]. The episode uses her fieldwork and theory to explain [[China]]'s early reform growth as [[DevelopmentAsVariationSystem]] rather than as a linear institutional recipe.

Her role in the source is to defend a developmental sequence that begins from available, often imperfect institutions. [[WeakInstitutionsMarketBuilding]], [[DirectedImprovisation]], [[LocalGovernmentPolicyExperimentation]], and [[InformalPropertyRightsProtection]] are presented as mechanisms that helped start markets before stronger formal governance became possible or necessary.
