# 10 Downing Street

10 Downing Street enters [[for-bucks-sake-the-rise-of-self-made-billionaires-6a61e076562e3ad886d54c95]] as the setting for the episode's lighter closing segment on [[LarryTheCat]]. The source treats the building less as constitutional infrastructure than as a media stage where symbols, photographers, world leaders, prime ministers, and unofficial mascots can all shape public perception.

The page's wiki role is therefore narrow: it anchors [[PoliticalImageMaintenance]] in a recognizable British political setting. The same source also connects the setting to [[KeirStarmer]] through cats kept away from Larry and to [[AndyBurnham]] as part of the broader British political backdrop in adjacent The Intelligence episodes.
