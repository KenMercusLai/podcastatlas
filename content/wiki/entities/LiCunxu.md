# 李存勖 / Li Cunxu

Li Cunxu / 李存勖 is connected to the [[LaterTang|后唐]] cluster in [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]]. The episode names him among the figures tied to adoptive-son and military-kinship practices that carried from late Tang into the Five Dynasties.

For the source's purposes, Li Cunxu helps make regime building personal and martial. Power is not only an office chart; it is also inherited, adopted, renamed, rewarded, and contested inside commander-centered networks.
