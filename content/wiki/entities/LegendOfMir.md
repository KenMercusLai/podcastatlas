# 《传奇》 / Legend of Mir

《传奇》 / Legend of Mir is the Korean online game that [[Shanda|盛大]] operated in China and that anchors [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]]. The source presents it as [[ChenTianqiao]]'s decisive opportunity: a low-threshold MMORPG that fit the internet-cafe, prepaid-card, and early broadband conditions of 2001-2002 China.

The episode emphasizes that Legend's value was not only nostalgia. Its three-class design, equipment scarcity, PK rules, guilds, and Sabak siege warfare made it an early laboratory for [[OnlineGameSocialOrganization]], while rights disputes among [[Actozsoft]], [[WeMade]], [[Shanda|盛大]], [[KaiyingNetwork]], and later operators made it a long-running case in [[OnlineGameIPRightsDisputes]] and [[GameIPLongTail]].
