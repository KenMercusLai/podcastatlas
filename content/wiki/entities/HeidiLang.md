# Heidi Lang

## Overview
Heidi Lang is a resident and town council member of [[ParadiseCalifornia|Paradise, California]], whose experience links the 2018 Camp Fire, fire-resilient rebuilding, and the subsequent insurance-availability crisis.

## Current Profile

She escaped the Camp Fire, rebuilt with hardened materials, helped organize neighborhood mitigation, and still faced remote-data errors, a premium increase from roughly $1,000 to nearly $10,000, and reliance on the [[CaliforniaFAIRPlan]].

## Key Characteristics
- Survived a roughly four-and-a-half-hour evacuation from the Camp Fire.
- Rebuilt with stucco, a metal roof, specialized vents, and other fire-resistant features.
- Successfully challenged an attempted cancellation based on stale aerial imagery.
- Helped organize collective Firewise mitigation with [[JennyGoodland]].
- Declined to seek one of the first limited new private policies because she did not want preferential treatment as a council member.
