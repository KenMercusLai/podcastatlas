# Jacob Lewis III

Jacob Lewis III appears in [[the-skyscrapers-that-nimbys-and-zoning-couldnt-stop]] as a [[SquamishNation|Squamish]] figure helping oversee the high-rise development at [[SinakVillageSite|Sinak]]. The source uses his construction-site tour to show the project as both a major rental-housing buildout and a cultural-return statement.

His account makes [[IndigenousDevelopmentVisibility]] central. The tower forms, orange accents, and Squamish design references matter because the source says Squamish people experienced erasure and want visitors to know whose land they are on. His response to nearby criticism also grounds [[LocalVetoHousingPolitics]], especially where opposition questions why Indigenous development should look modern and high-density.
