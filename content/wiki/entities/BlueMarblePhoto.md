# 1967 Whole-Earth Color Image / Blue Marble Source-Scoped

## Overview
The 1967 whole-Earth color image is the satellite photograph that [[RenNing]] says [[StewartBrand]] used through [[WholeEarthCatalog|Whole Earth Catalog]] to make Earth visible as a whole.

## Current Profile
The source presents this image as an early full-color, high-clarity view of Earth from space and treats it as one of modern environmental consciousness's visual symbols. Its role in the episode is not technical photography history by itself, but the way space-age instruments gave culture a new image of home.
