# 仓亭津 / Cangting Ford (Late Han)

仓亭津 / Cangting Ford enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-979-zaoyu-tongliao-beici-ta-ruhe-wei-caocao-liwan-kuanglan-luu58a2ww11g-radh-5g4l7fcis|Hanji 979]] as the Yellow River crossing [[ChengYu|程昱]] moves to block when [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] tries to reach [[DongALateHan|东阿]] during [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] crisis.

The source's military point is logistical and geographic. Cheng Yu does not defeat Chen Gong by a full field battle; he sends light cavalry ahead, secures the crossing, and pulls boats away so Chen Gong cannot move across the river in time. This makes a ford, not a city wall, the first preserved layer of Cao Cao's remaining base.
