# Dutch Revolt

The Dutch Revolt appears in [[695-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-the-shadow-war-part-5-glt5637968455]] as the rebellion in the [[LowCountries|Low Countries]] whose survival becomes a strategic problem for [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]]'s [[England]]. The episode treats the revolt as both confessional and geopolitical: a Spanish victory would strengthen Catholic power, endanger English trade, and create a possible invasion platform.

The revolt's crisis deepens after [[WilliamOfOrange|William of Orange]] is assassinated in July 1584. [[AlexanderFarnese|Alexander Farnese / Duke of Parma]] then consolidates Spanish recovery in the south, Ghent surrenders, and [[Antwerp]] falls in August 1585 after a 13-month siege.

The English response is the [[TreatyOfNonsuch]]. The source presents the treaty as a threshold moment: Elizabeth refuses sovereignty over the Dutch provinces but sends troops, cavalry, commanders, and accepts cautionary towns such as [[Flushing]].
