# Montbell

Montbell appears in [[142-riben-xiaofeizhe-weihe-ruci-chimi-jijie-xianding-chuantai-kuahai-dianbo-lgbjiahlutyyf4paylnyxy7hv0iq]] as the episode's clearest regional-limited product example. The source describes city-limited T-shirts whose designs incorporate local mountains, faith, pilgrimage, or outdoor identity, and whose purchase is restricted to the local store rather than being broadly available online.

The case turns a T-shirt into a record of place. That makes Montbell useful for [[PlaceBasedBrandPerception]] and [[JapaneseSeasonalLimitedConsumption]]: scarcity matters because it is tied to travel, local memory, and a story the buyer can repeat.
