# 阳安 / Yang'an (late Han)

## Overview
阳安 / Yang'an is the late-Han locality in Hanji 1046 where [[LiTongLateHan|李通]] and [[ZhaoYanLateHan|赵俨]] face the problem of proving loyalty to [[CaoCao|曹操]] without provoking local unrest through urgent cotton-silk collection.

## Current Profile
The source places Yang'an in the Jian'an 5 / 200 CE pressure field around the [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]]. Neighboring commanderies are unstable or rebelling, while Yang'an remains aligned with the court-and-Cao side. That loyal status does not remove risk: forcing households to send cotton-silk quickly could give discontented actors an opening and turn a supportive region into a liability.

## Key Characteristics
- Functions as a local loyalty test during the Cao-Yuan confrontation.
- Shows that a politically aligned locality can still be fragile under emergency taxation.
- Makes cotton-silk collection a stability problem rather than only a fiscal procedure.
- Becomes settled after the levy is returned and local burden is eased.

## Qualifications
The source uses Yang'an as a local-governance setting, not as a full administrative history. The wiki keeps its exact late-Han administrative status source-scoped and distinguishes this locality from unrelated uses of "Yang'an" elsewhere.
