# 《荒野上的大师》

《荒野上的大师》 first appeared as a future-topic preview in [[kaitianchuang-moyu-ganxie-chui-dali-553650149]]. [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] turns it into a full source through a live conversation with its author [[ZhangQuan|张泉]].

The book is presented as a nonfiction account of modern Chinese scholarship's founding generation. Its four institutional peaks are [[GeologicalResearchInstitute|地质研究所/地质调查所]], [[TsinghuaGuoxueInstitute|清华国学研究院]], [[InstituteOfHistoryAndPhilology|历史语言研究所]], and [[SocietyForResearchInChineseArchitecture|中国营造学社]]. The episode's reading emphasizes scholars who built methods and institutions in literal and historical wilderness: field sites, mines, temples, archaeology pits, dialect routes, wartime classrooms, and unstable offices.

## Key Claims
- The book's central interest is not celebrity admiration but how modern Chinese academic disciplines were made.
- [[FieldworkAsKnowledgeMethod]] is the book's strongest methodological thread.
- [[ScholarshipInWartime]] gives the title its emotional force: research continues while bodies, books, funding, and institutions are vulnerable.
- [[ModernChineseAcademicInstitutionBuilding]] matters as much as individual brilliance in the episode's reading.
- The book connects patriotic urgency with [[CosmopolitanChineseScholarship]] rather than treating international method as foreignness.
