## Overview

邓万世 / Deng Wanshi is a Huan-era favorite or Deng-household figure whose current wiki profile is source-scoped to two short notices. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-852-dang-meinv-ruyun-shi-fu-haishi-huo-luml30ytbc-awpvk-7-23ehzbqpx|Hanji 852]] presents him as [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]'s pre-accession friend who receives excessive favor, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-865-zhichang-boss-ye-rongyi-bei-zhezhong-ren-beici-ljfhfevljqnx6zceakjfbw5gwfma|Hanji 865]] later places the same name among Deng figures executed after [[EmpressDengHuanLateHan|邓皇后]]'s fall.

## Current Profile

In Hanji 852, Deng Wanshi matters less as an independent policy actor than as evidence of Emperor Huan's post-Liang favor system. Huan treats him as an old friend from before accession, grants him high honor, and summons him into the palace for games often enough that [[YuanHongChenliuLateHan|袁闳]] treats the intimacy as a breach of ruler-minister propriety. Deng's case also anchors [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]]'s criticism that marquisates and food revenue are being detached from great merit.

Hanji 865 adds a darker downstream notice. After Empress Deng loses the harem struggle with [[GuoGuirenHuanLateHan|郭贵人]], the same source note says Deng Wanshi and Deng Hui are executed. The wiki therefore treats Deng Wanshi as part of the unstable Deng palace/favorite field: favor can produce title and access, but it does not guarantee durable safety when harem politics turns.

## Key Characteristics

- Emperor Huan's pre-accession friend whose favor becomes politically visible after the Liang clan is cleared.
- Beneficiary of reward criticized as exceeding ordinary merit and institutional rank.
- Palace intimate whose access and gambling companionship become a remonstrance target for Yuan Hong.
- Deng-field actor whose later execution is tied by Hanji 865 to Empress Deng's deposition and household punishment.
- Source-scoped figure whose independent offices, family position, and agency remain thinly evidenced.

## Evidence

- Favor-based reward: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-852-dang-meinv-ruyun-shi-fu-haishi-huo-luml30ytbc-awpvk-7-23ehzbqpx|Hanji 852]] says Huan grants Deng Wanshi exceptional favor because of old friendship before accession.
- Merit-order criticism: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-852-dang-meinv-ruyun-shi-fu-haishi-huo-luml30ytbc-awpvk-7-23ehzbqpx|Hanji 852]] places Deng Wanshi in Chen Fan's broader criticism of reward detached from great merit.
- Palace intimacy: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-852-dang-meinv-ruyun-shi-fu-haishi-huo-luml30ytbc-awpvk-7-23ehzbqpx|Hanji 852]] says Yuan Hong criticizes Huan for frequently summoning Deng into palace games and blurring ruler-minister distance.
- Household-punishment notice: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-865-zhichang-boss-ye-rongyi-bei-zhezhong-ren-beici-ljfhfevljqnx6zceakjfbw5gwfma|Hanji 865]] says Deng Wanshi and Deng Hui are executed after Empress Deng's deposition.

## Qualifications

The profile is intentionally narrow. Hanji 852 identifies Deng Wanshi through favor, title, and palace intimacy; Hanji 865 names him in an execution notice after Empress Deng's fall. The sources do not yet provide enough evidence to reconstruct his exact kinship with Empress Deng, his formal offices beyond the Huan-friend/favorite field, or the legal grounds for the later execution.
