# 许飞 / Xu Fei (singer-songwriter)

## Overview
许飞 is a Chinese singer-songwriter and former [[SuperGirl2006|2006《超级女声》]] contestant whose friendship with [[LiNaSinger|厉娜]] was revisited in [[HouHuiYouQiShow|《后会有期》]].

## Current Profile
The source remembers Xu Fei as a literary, creative contestant who remained associated with songwriting after leaving her original company and later entering a military arts ensemble. In the reality-program material, her precise memories, sensory detail, emotional pauses, and reluctance to provide a simple explanation make her compelling to viewers while leaving the central commercial-performance disclosure allegation unresolved.
