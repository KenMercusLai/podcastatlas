# Three Gorges Dam

The Three Gorges Dam appears in [[socialist-mobility-a-telling-michigan-vote-6a74600ec3e1347e8b5bf216]] as the world's biggest hydroelectric facility and as a caveat to the [[YangtzeRiver|Yangtze River]] recovery story. [[GabrielCrossley]] says dams are bad for fish because they interrupt movement up and down the river for spawning and other needs.

The page is source-scoped. In this episode, the dam represents the infrastructure constraint inside [[YangtzeFishingBanRecovery]]: stopping fishing can help biomass recover, but it does not remove every ecological barrier in the river system.
