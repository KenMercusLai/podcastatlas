# 羌渠 / Qiangqu Chanyu

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-917-ruhe-xunsu-zhaodao-shengzhi-jiaxin-de-pojudian-lk2zt5c3onvui9a4ppbowshy66tg|Hanji 917]] first places Qiangqu as a right wise king who is installed as [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] chanyu after [[ZhangXiuProtectorOfXiongnu|张脩]] kills [[HuzhengChanyu|呼征]]. This origin is coercive and unauthorized from the Han-official side, so it belongs to [[UnauthorizedFrontierCommandIntervention|边疆将领擅断干预]] before Qiangqu's later death becomes a requisition-backlash case.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-939-donghan-luanju-de-fengkuang-sanguo-xuqu-lthmvw8ihk96fuez9vamlryzcngy|Hanji 939]] introduces 羌渠 / Qiangqu as the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] chanyu ordered to provide troops for [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]]'s campaign against [[ZhangChunLateHan|张纯]]. Qiangqu sends the left wise king with cavalry toward [[YouzhouLateHan|幽州]], but the episode says Southern Xiongnu people fear repeated Han requisitions.

That fear turns lethal for Qiangqu. [[XiluoSouthernXiongnu|醯落]] in the right division rebels, joins with the [[TugeHu|屠各胡]], and kills Qiangqu; the Southern Xiongnu then install his son [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]] as chanyu. Qiangqu's page is therefore the origin point for the wiki's Yufuluo succession branch and for [[FrontierConscriptionBackfire|边疆征发反噬]].
