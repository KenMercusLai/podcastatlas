## Overview

刘长乐 / Liu Changle enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-394-2-jiemi-huoguang-chengwei-xihan-diyi-quanchen-de-xinji-fmmyov0ecqykq37zkphi40o52bc6|Hanji 394-2]] as a Western Han imperial-clan figure appointed by [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] during [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]]'s early reign. The source lists him with [[LiuBijiangWesternHan|刘碧江]] as part of Huo's response to advice that the regency should include Liu royal relatives rather than monopolize authority.

## Current Profile

The current evidence gives Liu Changle as a source-scoped appointee rather than a full biographical figure. His importance is relational: Huo Guang's appointment of him as Guanglu Dafu helps show a deliberate effort to reduce royal-kin alienation and make cooperation with the regency materially rewarding.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han imperial-clan appointee in the early Han Zhao regency field.
- Appointed Guanglu Dafu by Huo Guang.
- Paired with Liu Bijiang as evidence of royal-kin inclusion.
- Part of a stabilization move responding to the Lu-family cautionary precedent.
- Evidence for benefit-sharing as a tool of court coalition management.
