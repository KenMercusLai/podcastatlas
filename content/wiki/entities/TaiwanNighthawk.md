# Taiwan Nighthawk / 夜鷹

## Overview
[[TaiwanNighthawk]] is the low-elevation nocturnal bird whose rooftop nesting livestream opened the Duanwen episode's discussion of introduced mynas and alien-species moralization in Taiwan.

## Current Profile
The episode presents nightjars as ground-nesting nocturnal insect hunters whose camouflage works well on bare gravel or riverbank-like surfaces. In recent decades, they have also adapted to urban rooftops, where open roof surfaces can resemble their preferred bare nesting sites.

The livestream matters because viewers formed emotional attachment to a chick and unhatched egg before a common myna appeared. That attention made the myna incident socially explosive even though the episode keeps the egg's failed hatching causality uncertain.

## Key Characteristics
- Low-elevation nocturnal bird in Taiwan.
- Lays eggs directly on bare ground rather than building a conventional nest.
- Has adapted in recent decades to breeding on open urban rooftops.
- Became the emotionally focal native bird in a livestream-driven alien-species controversy.
