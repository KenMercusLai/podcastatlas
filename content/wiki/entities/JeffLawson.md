# Jeff Lawson

Jeff Lawson appears in [[peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted-peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted]] as the [[Twilio]] leader who repeatedly argued that Twilio and [[Segment]] would be better together. His thesis was that Twilio had the communications layer while Segment had the customer-data layer.

The episode uses Lawson less as a biography than as the source of a platform-combination argument. [[PeterReinhardt]] says the argument only became compelling once customers themselves wanted communication and data capabilities to work together, making Lawson's role a case where strategic narrative needed [[CustomerEvidenceStrategy]] before it could support an acquisition.
