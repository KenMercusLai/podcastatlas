# 晴雯 / Qingwen

[[ritan-chuantai-hongloumeng-haishi-keyi-du-yidu-de-587167638]] presents 晴雯 as one of [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]]'s clearest figures of [[TruthfulTemperamentInRedChamber|真]]. She is a maid, but the episode emphasizes her self-respect, sharpness, and ability to face [[JiaBaoyu|贾宝玉]] without treating him only as a master.

That equality makes her close to [[LinDaiyu|林黛玉]] and [[Lingguan|龄官]] in the source's reading: all three are vivid because they refuse to become smooth social instruments. It also makes her vulnerable inside the world beyond [[GrandViewGarden|大观园]], where [[GrandViewGardenYouthUtopia|youthful freedom]] gives way to hierarchy, marriage, status, and family discipline.
