## Overview

秦红棉 / Qin Hongmian is a former lover of [[DuanZhengchun|段正淳]] in [[TianLongBaBu|《天龙八部》]] whose seclusion, hostility toward men, and control of her daughter preserve rather than resolve the attachment that injured her.

## Current Profile

Calling herself “幽谷客,” Qin Hongmian withdraws from men and requires her daughter 木婉清 to veil herself and bind exposure of her face to marriage or killing. The episode reads these rules as romantic injury widened into generalized distrust and transmitted to the next generation.

Her withdrawal is not detachment. She continues practicing the 五罗轻烟掌 taught by Duan Zhengchun, conceals the mother-daughter relation, and remains readily moved by references to him. The protective shell therefore narrows both lives while leaving its supposed object of rejection emotionally central.

## Key Characteristics

- Responds to failed attachment through seclusion and categorical distrust of men.
- Converts personal injury into restrictive rules governing her daughter.
- Preserves the former relationship through martial practice even while performing rejection.
- Remains emotionally vulnerable to Duan Zhengchun beneath a controlled exterior.

## Qualifications

The profile derives from one conversational reading. It does not diagnose trauma or establish every motive for seclusion, concealment, or parental control. Protective intent may coexist with harm, and unresolved attachment explains neither the full character nor every action toward her daughter.
