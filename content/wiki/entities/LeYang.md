# 乐羊 / Le Yang

乐羊 / Le Yang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-1-lk-6ejgmjhvukqgtlz2lowu8qx-v]] as another person [[DiHuang|翟黄]] says he recommended to [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]]. In Di Huang's account, Le Yang is the recommendation for attacking [[ZhongshanState|中山国]].

The source mentions Le Yang mainly as evidence in Di Huang's chancellor argument. His page is therefore source-scoped and connects the episode's personnel-judgment question to military expansion and [[TalentReferralQuality]], rather than treating him as a full military biography.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2]] returns to Le Yang as a lineage marker for [[LeYi|乐毅]]. The episode uses Le Yi's descent from Le Yang to connect Yan Zhaowang's new talent recruitment to an older Wei military-service precedent.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-57-gudai-fa-dushi-haiyou-zhe-zuoyong-lj6wi61osaytowgmdp8dtkrwb-si]] reuses Le Yang as [[GanMao|甘茂]]'s ruler-trust example before the [[YiyangWarringStates|宜阳]] campaign. In Gan Mao's telling, Le Yang takes three years to conquer Zhongshan, and [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] later shows him a box of slanderous letters to prove that the ruler's trust made the victory possible. Le Yang therefore becomes a case for [[RumorErosionOfTrust|谣言侵蚀信任]] and [[OathBoundCommandTrust|盟誓约束下的统帅信任]], not only referral quality.
