## Overview

乐羊 / Le Yang is a [[WeiState|魏国]] general associated with the three-year conquest of [[ZhongshanState|中山国]]. His wiki significance lies in three linked roles: evidence for [[DiHuang|翟黄]]'s referral record, ancestor of [[LeYi|乐毅]] in a later talent-recruitment story, and a durable example of how ruler trust and post-victory hierarchy shape military merit.

## Current Profile

Le Yang's Zhongshan campaign survives sustained criticism because [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] continues to support him. Later tellings use the campaign to show that long operations are vulnerable to court rumor. Zhouji 05 part 3 adds the settlement after victory: Le Yang becomes proud, Wei Wenhou presents the hostile memorials he had resisted, and Le Yang returns credit to the ruler. The result is neither simple humiliation nor unqualified reward; it is a staged reminder that command success was jointly produced by battlefield execution and political protection.

## Key Characteristics

- Military recommendee used by Di Huang as evidence of personnel judgment.
- Commander whose Zhongshan campaign takes three years and attracts repeated opposition.
- Beneficiary of ruler trust that keeps a delayed operation politically viable.
- High-merit subordinate whose post-victory pride is corrected through visible evidence of that support.
- Lineage predecessor used to connect early Wei service with Le Yi's later career.

## Evidence

- Referral role: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-1-lk-6ejgmjhvukqgtlz2lowu8qx-v|Zhouji 06 part 1]] says Di Huang recommended Le Yang for the Zhongshan campaign.
- Trust during delay: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-57-gudai-fa-dushi-haiyou-zhe-zuoyong-lj6wi61osaytowgmdp8dtkrwb-si|Zhouji 57]] uses the three-year campaign and box of hostile letters as [[GanMao|甘茂]]'s warning that court rumor can destroy a commander without ruler commitment.
- Post-victory correction: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-05-guangcai-duomu-de-weiwenhou-3-lqbr7i9s82pi1jptizs18gidsipi|Zhouji 05 part 3]] explicitly frames the same box as Wei Wenhou's response to Le Yang's pride and says Le Yang returns credit upward.
- Lineage afterlife: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2|Zhouji 52]] identifies Le Yi as Le Yang's descendant within Yan's talent-recruitment branch.

## Qualifications

The bounded sources use Le Yang mainly as a personnel and trust example, not as a complete military biography. The exact number and content of memorials, the boundaries of the Lingshou reward, the full Zhongshan campaign chronology, and the lineage details connecting Le Yang to Le Yi remain episode-attributed. The credit-return scene can demonstrate hierarchy repair without proving that Le Yang's achievement was unreal or wholly attributable to the ruler.
