# 赵范 / Zhao Fan

赵范 / Zhao Fan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-18-zhongguo-lishishang-di-yi-li-rentoushui-lgqnequk0rxejqgihwdqxqyqnsvq]] as a [[ZhaoState|赵国]] prince who attacks [[Handan|邯郸]] in 347 BCE and is killed after failing. The host links the attempt to Zhao's recent succession disorder after [[ZhaoChenghou|赵成侯]]'s death and [[GongziXie|公子泄]]'s failed challenge.

The page is source-scoped. Zhao Fan matters because the word used for his attack lets the source explain [[MilitaryActionTerminology|伐/侵/袭 terminology]] and because his failure shows [[ZhaoSuhou|赵肃侯]] surviving an early challenge to his new rule.
