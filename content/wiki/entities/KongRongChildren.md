## Overview

Kong Rong's children are the two unnamed young household victims whose deaths give Hanji 1090 its “覆巢之下，安有完卵” image of family destruction after political prosecution.

## Current Profile

The source presents the children playing a board game when [[KongRong|孔融]] is arrested. Kong Rong pleads that his guilt should not reach them, but the older child answers that intact eggs cannot remain beneath an overturned nest. Both are later shown facing execution without panic. Their role is not an independent biography; it is the episode's clearest evidence that the punishment destroys a household rather than ending with the accused official.

## Key Characteristics

- Unnamed young children caught in their father's political prosecution.
- Speakers of the episode's “覆巢” recognition that family survival is impossible after household destruction.
- Presented as composed at arrest and execution.
- Relational entity preserving victims who would otherwise disappear behind Kong Rong's biography.
