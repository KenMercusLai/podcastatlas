# Natalia John

[[NataliaJohn]] is a Barbudan speaker in [[sand-heists-and-property-rights-in-the-caribbean-summer-school]]. She says she supports more development on [[Barbuda]], but wants Barbudans to keep meaningful control over which projects are approved.

Her role is important because it prevents the episode's property-rights dispute from collapsing into development versus no development. The actual conflict is closer to [[PropertyRightsCommunityObligations]]: who gets approval authority, who captures island value, and whether [[CommunalLandTenure]] can coexist with investment.
