## Overview

苏康 / Su Kang is a late-Han zhongchangshi introduced by [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-884-zhichang-chujian-yao-dui-shui-xian-kaidao-lvtc3uqlso242olkyin3fvz5a7ys|Hanji 884]] as a co-target in the first selective anti-eunuch punishment authorized by [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦妙 / 窦皇太后]]. The source gives him less individual detail than [[GuanBaLateHan|管霸]], but places both men in the same arrest-and-execution step.

## Current Profile

Su Kang's current profile is a narrow companion case. He matters because the first action against eunuch power is plural, not purely symbolic: Dou Miao allows Guan Ba and Su Kang to be arrested and executed after rejecting a complete purge.

At the same time, Su Kang's case shows the limit of that action. The source does not present his execution as removing [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]], [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]], or the broader near-palace access environment that Chen Fan and Dou Wu see as dangerous.

## Key Characteristics

- Su Kang is a zhongchangshi attached to the first anti-eunuch arrest step in Hanji 884.
- His individual biography remains thin in the current evidence.
- His execution confirms that Dou Miao accepts some punishment of eunuch actors.
- His case also shows the incompleteness of selective punishment when the larger access network survives.

## Evidence

- Co-target status: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-884-zhichang-chujian-yao-dui-shui-xian-kaidao-lvtc3uqlso242olkyin3fvz5a7ys|Hanji 884]] says Su Kang is arrested and executed with Guan Ba.
- Selective-purge context: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-884-zhichang-chujian-yao-dui-shui-xian-kaidao-lvtc3uqlso242olkyin3fvz5a7ys|Hanji 884]] places his punishment immediately after Dou Miao says only lawbreaking eunuchs should be punished.
- Limited individual detail: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-884-zhichang-chujian-yao-dui-shui-xian-kaidao-lvtc3uqlso242olkyin3fvz5a7ys|Hanji 884]] names Su Kang without giving the same power-and-ability description attached to Guan Ba.
- Unresolved target field: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-884-zhichang-chujian-yao-dui-shui-xian-kaidao-lvtc3uqlso242olkyin3fvz5a7ys|Hanji 884]] leaves Cao Jie and Wang Fu in the broader removal question after the first executions.

## Qualifications

The source does not provide a complete biography, separate accusation, or family network for Su Kang. The page should therefore remain a source-bounded entity until later sources add more evidence.

## What Changed

- The page creates Su Kang as the paired execution case in Dou Miao's limited purge.
- The current profile keeps his significance tied to the first anti-eunuch action rather than treating him as a later countercoup organizer.
