# Civilization (game series)

## Overview

Civilization is a historical strategy-game series used in the source to examine how games turn long-run history into variables, choices, progression, and counterfactual outcomes.

## Current Profile

[[40-history-as-entertainment-glt9519276470]] describes Civilization as moving from an ancient starting point toward technologically advanced victory. This gives players broad agency and memorable counterfactual possibilities, but it also turns civilization into manageable inputs and milestones.

The episode's central criticism is model-based: when industrialization, colonization, scientific advance, expansion, and eventual spaceflight define successful progression, one historically specific development path can appear universal or inevitable. The game's freedom therefore operates inside a designed funnel.

## Key Characteristics

- Models long-run historical development through explicit resources, technologies, institutions, and victory conditions.
- Gives players agency to redirect recognizable civilizations and historical figures.
- Makes counterfactual surprise a major source of enjoyment.
- Encodes a developmental sequence that can naturalize expansion, industrialization, colonization, and technological ascent.

## Evidence

- **Progression model:** [[40-history-as-entertainment-glt9519276470]] describes play from an ancient beginning to a space-age endpoint through distinct developmental variables.
- **Determinism critique:** [[40-history-as-entertainment-glt9519276470]] argues that the rules can make European-style industrialization and colonization appear normative.
- **Counterfactual play:** [[40-history-as-entertainment-glt9519276470]] uses the aggressive-Gandhi story to illustrate the pleasure of unexpected outcomes within a familiar historical frame.

## Qualifications

This profile reflects one conversational treatment of the series rather than a comparison across editions, expansions, difficulty settings, playable strategies, or player communities. The aggressive-Gandhi glitch story is presented as famous game lore in the episode and is not independently verified here.

## What Changed
