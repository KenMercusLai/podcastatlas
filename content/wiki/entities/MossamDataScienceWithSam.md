# Mossam (Data Science With Sam)

Mossam appears in [[data-ai-and-scientific-research-a-coffee-chat]] as a [[StanfordUniversity]] research scientist with a background in organic chemistry, radiochemistry, and biomedical imaging. The source identifies him by first name only, so this page keeps the identity scoped to the episode.

His main contribution is the chemistry and imaging branch of [[AIForScience]]. He explains [[RetrosynthesisAI]], [[RadiochemistryImagingTracers]], [[BloodBrainBarrierPrediction]], and the value of [[NegativeResultsAsScientificData]] for making chemistry ML systems more reliable.

Mossam is also the source's clearest safety boundary. He argues that AI-generated synthetic schemes still need human reproduction and oversight, and that radiochemistry cannot be treated as routine unsupervised automation because radioactive work carries serious risk.
