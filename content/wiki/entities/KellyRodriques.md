# Kelly Rodriques

Kelly Rodriques appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-inside-the-private-stock-market-boom-spacex-anthropic-openai-the-rise-of-secondaries-41564170]] as the [[ForgeGlobal|Forge Global]] CEO explaining how private-market access is moving from ad hoc transactions toward platform and fund infrastructure. She argues that staying private can remain attractive for founders if employees and investors still get structured liquidity.

Rodriques is the episode's clearest voice for [[RegulatedSPVPrivateMarketAccess]] and [[RetailPrivateMarketAccess]]. She frames the [[CharlesSchwab|Charles Schwab]] relationship as a way to put private-company exposure into regulated products, while also warning that retail investors should look beyond the most famous daily-CNBC names and pay attention to valuation, timing, and access structure.
