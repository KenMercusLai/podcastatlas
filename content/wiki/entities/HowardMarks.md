# Howard Marks / 霍华德·马克斯

Howard Marks / 霍华德·马克斯 enters this wiki through [[vol-124-xinxi-guozai-hou-ruhe-baochi-lengjing-touzi-zhang-fupan-ltpmll0jmcw-dl0-32qesddwem4l]], where [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] uses Marks's April 2025 memo "Nobody Knows II" as a discipline for investing during tariff shock, trade-war anxiety, and market volatility.

The episode's takeaway from Marks is not a specific market forecast. It is a three-step behavioral filter: receive information, ask whether anyone truly knows what will happen, and only then decide whether portfolio action is justified. That makes Marks a reference point for [[InformationOverloadKnowledgeTrap]], [[MacroEventTrendDistinction]], and [[InvestmentRiskManagement]].
