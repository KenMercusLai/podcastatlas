# 不合时宜 / Bu He Shi Yi

## Overview
不合时宜 / Bu He Shi Yi is the Chinese podcast represented in this wiki by an episode on dementia care, family caregivers, and elder-care systems.

## Current Profile
The current wiki profile is source-scoped to one episode. In that evidence, the show uses an interview with [[TangYongSociologist|唐勇]] and his book [[YiWangDeShiJieDementiaCareBook|《遗忘的世界：失智老人的照护图景》]] to move dementia from a private family anxiety into a public-care question involving [[CareSocialization]], [[ElderCareStateCapacity]], [[DementiaCaregiverBurden]], and [[ChinaLongTermCareInsurance]].

## Key Characteristics
- Public-issue interview format that combines macro data, academic fieldwork, and personal care cases.
- Uses dementia care to connect younger and middle-aged listeners' future family risk with current elder-care institutions.
- Frames care as relational and systemic rather than only as filial morality or medical management.

- Public-care framing - [[manchang-de-gaobie-shizhizheng-zhaohuzhe-yu-zhongjiang-laoqu-de-women-lscotoglqyoznytqatlefdwv3jez]] treats dementia care as a problem for families, communities, professional workers, medical systems, insurance, and long-term-care policy.
