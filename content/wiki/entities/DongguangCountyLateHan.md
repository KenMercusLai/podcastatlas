# 东光县 / Dongguang County (late Han)

东光县 / Dongguang County (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-958-jiemi-shishang-zui-duanming-de-tongchoudikai-lve41q9gyijbop8wk-b7e6shogju|Hanji 958]] as the area south of which [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] attacks the [[QingzhouYellowTurbans|青州黄巾军]]. The source says he uses a much smaller steppe-and-cavalry force to defeat a large Yellow Turban movement preparing to link with the [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]].

The place matters because it is the first visible accelerator in the episode. Gongsun Zan's victory, captures, and equipment gains make him a larger regional actor before the later [[YuanShao|袁绍]] rivalry and [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] rupture become central.
