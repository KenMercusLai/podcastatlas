# 金颖 / Jin Ying (breast-cancer survivor)

## Overview
金颖 appears in [[ep291-meikong-shengbing-de-women-ruhe-guoshang-ruxian-youhao-de-shenghuo-lmzevsk2atc-6psjqeogg-qhuyq2|EP291]] as a documentary director, screenwriter, novelist, and long-term breast-cancer survivor.

## Current Profile
Her account connects a missed annual check, diagnosis, surgery, sentinel-node assessment, treatment, later childbirth under specialist monitoring, years of recurrence anxiety, and eventual life reconstruction. She treats medical adherence, peer contact, and direct communication of support needs as practical resources while refusing to reduce life after cancer to permanent fear.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses long-term survivorship to discuss both treatment and the years of uncertainty that follow it.
- Describes fertility and pregnancy as clinician-guided, monitored decisions rather than general reassurance.
- Values peer groups for information, recognition, and reduced isolation among younger patients.
- Frames life reconstruction as making room for bodily limits, relationships, work, and sensory experience.

## Qualifications
This profile rests on one retrospective podcast account. It does not establish diagnosis, prognosis, fertility safety, treatment efficacy, or a universal emotional timetable for survivors.
