# Gadi Eisenkot

## Overview
Gadi Eisenkot is a former commander-in-chief of the Israel Defence Forces who entered politics in 2022, opposed [[BenjaminNetanyahu]]’s wartime strategy, and received the president’s mandate to attempt to form a government after the 2026 election.

## Current Profile
Eisenkot’s military credibility helped him lead the largest opposition party, but the source contrasts that reputation with limited coalition-building experience: he had led a party for only a little over a year when asked to assemble a government. His initial route is a four-party minority coalition controlling barely one third of the Knesset, dependent on outside support or abstention from Arab and ultra-Orthodox parties and on compromise from nationalist partners such as [[AvigdorLieberman]].

## Key Characteristics
- Former IDF chief whose security record anchors his political credibility.
- Entered Netanyahu’s early post-October-7 war cabinet, then resigned after criticizing its strategy.
- Became leader of the largest opposition party before developing long coalition experience.
- Holds a time-limited presidential mandate to form a government and win Knesset confidence.
- Prioritizes replacing Netanyahu through a minority-government strategy before constructing a broader governing alliance.

- **Security-to-politics transition:** The earlier election source presents Eisenkot as a former IDF chief, wartime cabinet member, critic of Netanyahu’s strategy, and rising opposition figure. [[youve-come-a-long-way-bibi-israels-crucial-election-6a59f9ae461a6a41901ae410]]
- **Mandate and time limit:** After certification of a result without a majority, the president gave Eisenkot at most six weeks to form a government and pass an inaugural confidence vote. [[all-eyes-on-eisenkot-israels-could-be-prime-minister-6af19d18e7c6d309d1116743]]
- **Minority-government route:** His proposed four-party coalition holds only about one third of Knesset seats and therefore needs Arab or ultra-Orthodox outside support or abstention. [[all-eyes-on-eisenkot-israels-could-be-prime-minister-6af19d18e7c6d309d1116743]]
- **Experience constraint:** The episode says he entered politics in 2022 and still depends on more seasoned opposition leaders with their own coalition agendas. [[all-eyes-on-eisenkot-israels-could-be-prime-minister-6af19d18e7c6d309d1116743]]

## Qualifications
The government-formation attempt was unresolved when the source was produced. A presidential mandate does not establish that Eisenkot has a majority, will become prime minister, or can sustain a government after confidence. “Aizenkot” in the supplied summary and “Eisenkot” in its title are treated as transliteration variants of the same person.

- Added limited coalition experience as a constraint on his security-based political profile.
- Added the minority-government and external-support route as his immediate strategy.
