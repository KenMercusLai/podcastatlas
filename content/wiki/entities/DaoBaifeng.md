## Overview

刀白凤 / Dao Baifeng is [[DuanZhengchun|段正淳]]'s wife in [[TianLongBaBu|《天龙八部》]], caught between political marriage, genuine attachment, a monogamous norm, and retaliatory self-injury after repeated betrayal.

## Current Profile

The bounded episode presents her as the daughter of a Bai chieftain whose marriage has political significance but also contains real affection. Its account of a one-spouse norm makes Duan Zhengchun's refusal of monogamy a conflict of relationship expectation as well as jealousy. She withdraws into Daoist life but does not thereby resolve the attachment.

Her most extreme retaliation is to seek out the man she regards as most degraded, not knowing he is the fallen [[DuanYanqing|段延庆]], and have sex with him in order to injure her husband symbolically. The episode explicitly rejects the act as revenge through self-damage: it cannot restore trust or control Duan's conduct, and its most direct consequences fall on her own body and life.

## Key Characteristics

- Occupies a marriage joining political alliance and genuine feeling.
- Understands exclusivity as a norm incompatible with her husband's repeated outside relationships.
- Uses religious withdrawal as distance without achieving emotional release.
- Retaliates through an act intended to degrade herself in her husband's eyes.
- Demonstrates how self-directed revenge can deepen rather than redistribute relational harm.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on one conversational interpretation. Ethnic custom, political-marriage structure, consent, motive, emotional state, and the meaning of religious withdrawal require fuller textual and historical evidence. Calling the retaliation self-harm describes the episode's ethical reading, not a diagnosis, and does not reduce her to that act.

## What Changed

- Created a profile linking political marriage and incompatible exclusivity norms to relational conflict.
- Preserved the episode's rejection of self-directed retaliation without treating it as the whole character.
