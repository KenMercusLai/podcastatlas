## Current Profile

Aviona explains [[TransferPricing]] through the long-running dispute between [[CocaCola|Coca-Cola]] and the [[InternalRevenueService|IRS]]. Her account keeps a necessary business practice distinct from abusive profit shifting and treats the case as a broader test of whether tax authorities can sustain complex litigation against highly resourced multinational firms.
