# John Rabe

## Overview

John Rabe was a German businessman and Nazi Party member who became a leading organizer of the Nanking International Safety Zone during the [[NanjingMassacre]].

## Current Profile

[[dchha63-supernova-in-the-east-ii-dchha63-supernova-in-the-east-ii|Supernova in the East II]] presents Rabe as one of a small group of foreign residents who remained in Nanjing after Chinese authorities withdrew. The group designated a safety zone, sheltered civilians, appealed repeatedly to Japanese authorities, and created a contemporary documentary record through diaries, letters, telegrams, and reports.

Rabe's party membership makes the case morally and historically dissonant rather than simple. The source uses his protective action and documentation as evidence that a compromised political identity does not predetermine every act, while neither the rescue work nor his value as a witness rehabilitates Nazism.

## Key Characteristics

- German businessman resident in Nanjing during the 1937 Japanese occupation.
- Nazi Party member whose affiliation gave him unusual, though limited, standing with Japanese authorities.
- Leading participant in the Nanking International Safety Zone.
- Helped protect and shelter civilians amid mass violence.
- Contributed contemporary written evidence used in later accounts of the massacre.
