## Overview
陈勇 / Chen Yong is the football reporter guest in the [[Budongqiu|不懂球]] episode on [[ChineseSuperLeague]] expansion, cross-year calendar research, foreign-player policy, and Chinese professional-football finance.

## Current Profile
Within the wiki, Chen Yong functions as an industry-observer source rather than as a full biographical subject. His contribution is a league-governance reading: Chinese football can expand only if calendar design, lower-tier match supply, club-community operation, promotion-relegation design, and financial discipline are handled together.

## Key Characteristics
- Guest expert used to interpret Chinese professional-football policy and club operations.
- Advocates advance notice and procedural seriousness for [[ChineseFootballLeagueExpansion]].
- Treats city football markets and matchday economies as evidence that [[ChineseSuperLeague]] value can recover.
- Emphasizes financial discipline, tax and wage arrears control, and debt boundaries as expansion prerequisites.
- Frames cross-year calendar reform as worth studying but not currently ready for simple adoption.
