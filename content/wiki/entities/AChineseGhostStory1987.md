## Overview

[[AChineseGhostStory1987|《倩女幽魂》(1987)]] is a Hong Kong fantasy-romance film directed by [[ChingSiuTung|程小东]] and produced by [[TsuiHark|徐克]], adapting 《聂小倩》 from [[LiaozhaiZhiyi|《聊斋志异》]].

## Current Profile

The film mixes wuxia, romance, horror, fantasy, and comedy. [[LeslieCheung|张国荣]]'s vulnerable Ning Caichen and [[JoeyWong|王祖贤]]'s spectral Nie Xiaoqian create a reciprocal arc: she protects him before he understands the danger, and he later risks himself to free her. Music, costume, movement, water, wind, cold light, ambiguous demon voices, and abstract darkness are as important as plot.

The bounded source also finds weak causality in Yan Chixia's tolerance of predation and the abruptly escalated final battle. The film is therefore a case for judging audiovisual invention, character feeling, and plot coherence separately.

## Key Characteristics

- Reworks a Qing tale into a tragic, emotionally exclusive romance.
- Builds character through performance, costume, motion, light, sound, and music.
- Makes mutual protection and sacrifice the central relationship motor.
- Combines several genres without equal logical rigor across them.
- Leaves images and songs that can survive incomplete plot memory.
