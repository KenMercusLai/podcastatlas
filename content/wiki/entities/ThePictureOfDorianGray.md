# 《道连·格雷的画像》 / The Picture of Dorian Gray

[[ThePictureOfDorianGray|《道连·格雷的画像》 / The Picture of Dorian Gray]] appears in [[10-wang-erde-qiyi-gushiji-yong-zui-hui-sahuang-de-zuichun-qinwen-tianshi-545332195]] as a reference point for [[OscarWilde|Wilde]]'s aphoristic density. The episode notes that Wilde's novels and plays can feel packed with dazzling sentences, to the point that some readers suspect the epigrams strain the work's overall shape.

Within this source, the novel supports [[ArtAgainstMoralInstruction]] and [[ClassicReadingComplexity]] rather than receiving a full plot reading. It helps show the tension between Wilde's surface brilliance and the harder question of whether an artwork can remain whole when every line wants to shine.
