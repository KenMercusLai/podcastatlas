# 陈恢 / Chen Hui (Nanyang)

陈恢 / Chen Hui enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-136-meinv-xijiao-liubang-ye-hao-zhe-kouer-lplgzwzahepwx9p7krfc4x0cnz4o|Qinji 136]] as a retainer of the [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]] governor at [[WanCounty|宛县]]. The transcript writes the name as "陈辉"; this page uses 陈恢 as the canonical historical form and keeps the variant source-scoped.

When Wan is surrounded, the Nanyang governor prepares to kill himself. Chen Hui stops him and asks to negotiate with [[LiuBang|刘邦]], then frames the issue through Liu Bang's own incentive: under [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]], Liu Bang needs to reach [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] quickly and cannot afford a costly siege or a strong enemy left in the rear.

Chen Hui's proposal lets both sides claim a gain. Liu Bang accepts surrender, rewards the governor with a marquis title, gives Chen Hui a thousand-household fief, and continues west with a safer rear. The source treats this as the moment when Liu Bang begins to govern Qin people as future subjects rather than merely punish them as enemies.
