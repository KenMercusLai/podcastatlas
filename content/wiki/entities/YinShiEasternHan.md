## Overview

阴识 / Yin Shi is synthesized as a Guangwu-era maternal relative whose political value is qualified by public conduct and public criteria. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-721-ta-sha-jianu-ma-gongzhu-weihe-fanbei-shang-lryzcejdnrm4-92esnddfvyjkqli|Hanji 721]] presents him as a careful, loyal model around Crown Prince Liu Zhuang; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-728-weihe-mingchen-nan-shanzhong-beihou-de-yuanyin-ni-gai-dong-ltjl2bgpyyz1bmwe-4mfcyd9e6cf|Hanji 728]] later makes his tutor candidacy the case that triggers [[CrownPrinceTutorPublicInterest|太子师傅公器化]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 721 presents Yin Shi before the later tutor-selection debate. He is Crown Prince Liu Zhuang's maternal uncle, is made acting zhijinwu, and is described as loyal, upright, and careful with official information. Liu Xiu uses him as an example for royal relatives and attendants because his conduct keeps public office distinct from careless private talk.

Hanji 728 reuses that same status in a sharper selection scene. When [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] asks who can serve as the crown prince's teacher, the officials and emperor converge on Yin Shi. His candidacy is plausible inside kinship and rank: he is the crown prince's maternal uncle, holds the zhijinwu office, and is Yuanlu Marquis.

The point is not that Yin Shi is personally unworthy. [[ZhangYiEasternHan|张义]] asks whether the crown prince is being established for the Yin family or for the realm. If for the Yin family, Yin Shi can be teacher; if for the realm, the court should choose worthy talent from the realm. Liu Xiu accepts the correction and appoints Zhang Yi instead.

## Key Characteristics

- Maternal uncle of Crown Prince Liu Zhuang in the Guangwu court.
- Acting or holding zhijinwu office and Yuanlu Marquis status across the bounded sources.
- Loyal and upright official whose private speech is disciplined around public affairs.
- Court model Liu Xiu can cite to royal relatives and attendants.
- Plausible family-and-rank candidate for crown-prince tutor before a public-interest objection changes the decision.
- Not condemned personally by the tutor-selection source; the issue is the appointment criterion.

## Qualifications

This page is not a complete biography or a negative judgment on Yin Shi's competence. Hanji 721 makes him a positive maternal-kin model, while Hanji 728 says even a plausible relative needs a public-role justification for heir education. The transcript form 殷实 is normalized to 阴识 because the maternal-uncle, zhijinwu, and crown-prince context matches the existing route.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 721 as Yin Shi's earlier positive profile: acting zhijinwu, disciplined private speech, and royal-relative model.
- Reframed Hanji 728's objection as a limit on selection criteria rather than a condemnation of Yin Shi.
