## Overview

阴识 / Yin Shi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-728-weihe-mingchen-nan-shanzhong-beihou-de-yuanyin-ni-gai-dong-ltjl2bgpyyz1bmwe-4mfcyd9e6cf|Hanji 728]] as the crown prince's maternal uncle, zhijinwu, and Yuanlu Marquis whom officials and [[LiuXiu|Liu Xiu]] initially favor as crown-prince tutor before [[ZhangYiEasternHan|张义]] objects.

## Current Profile

Hanji 728 presents Yin Shi through a personnel-selection scene rather than a full career. When Liu Xiu asks who can serve as the crown prince's teacher, the officials and emperor converge on Yin Shi. His candidacy is plausible inside kinship and rank: he is the crown prince's maternal uncle, holds the zhijinwu office, and is Yuanlu Marquis.

The episode's point is not that Yin Shi is personally unworthy. Zhang Yi asks whether the crown prince is being established for the Yin family or for the realm. If for the Yin family, Yin Shi can be teacher; if for the realm, the court should choose worthy talent from the realm. Liu Xiu accepts the correction and appoints Zhang Yi instead.

## Key Characteristics

- Maternal-kin candidate for crown-prince tutor under Liu Xiu.
- Holds the zhijinwu office and Yuanlu Marquis status in the source scene.
- Represents the obvious family-and-rank choice before a public-interest objection changes the decision.
- Not condemned personally by the source; the issue is the selection criterion.
