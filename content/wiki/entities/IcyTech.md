# Icy Tech

Icy Tech is the rotomolded cooler brand [[RoySeiders]] distributed in Texas before co-founding [[YETI]], as discussed in [[yeti-ron-and-ryan-seiders-how-two-brothers-turned-a-400-cooler-into-a-2-billion-brand-d7f8e0e8-51a5-4444-8c1d-f5c43c353285]]. The episode presents it as both market validation and a failure-prone precursor: it proved that serious outdoor users might want tougher coolers, while also showing why the brothers needed better design control and supply resilience.

## Key Points
- Roy first saw Icy Tech-style heavy-duty coolers after Ryan encountered them at an Ace Hardware trade-show context in Florida.
- The coolers were Australian-associated and Thailand-manufactured, with thick rotomolded polyethylene construction.
- Roy built roughly 100 retailer relationships while distributing the product, creating a channel base for early YETI.
- Product failures and the loss of U.S. naming rights pushed the brothers to create their own brand and improve drains, lids, tie-down slots, feet, gaskets, hinges, and lid texture.
- The weaker Thailand manufacturing path contrasts with the later Philippines factory associated with [[IvanBrown]].
