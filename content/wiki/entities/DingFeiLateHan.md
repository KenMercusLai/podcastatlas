## Overview

丁斐 / Ding Fei (Late Han) enters the current evidence as the Cao-side officer whose improvised release of cattle and horses disrupts pursuit during [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s dangerous Yellow River crossing in the 211 CE [[WeinanBattleLateHan|渭南之战]].

## Current Profile

The profile is narrow but operationally distinct. While [[XuChu|许褚]] provides immediate physical protection on the boat, Ding Fei changes the pursuers' incentives on shore: releasing valuable animals draws attention away from Cao Cao and creates the time needed for escape. The episode therefore presents survival as a combined result of close protection, improvisation, and enemy indiscipline rather than personal courage alone.

## Key Characteristics

- Cao-side participant in the Yellow River crossing phase of the Weinan campaign.
- Responds to an immediate pursuit crisis through improvised diversion rather than direct combat.
- Uses cattle and horses to redirect the pursuers' attention away from Cao Cao.
- Complements Xu Chu's close protection by changing conditions on shore.

## Evidence

- Pursuit diversion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1121-tongguan-shengsi-jie-caocao-weile-ying-yishen-fanxian-lvfxitrmo0wq9hl1fmzzuagsobbl|Hanji 1121]] says Ding Fei releases cattle and horses while Ma Chao's riders attack the crossing, causing pursuers to turn toward the animals.
- Escape sequence: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1121-tongguan-shengsi-jie-caocao-weile-ying-yishen-fanxian-lvfxitrmo0wq9hl1fmzzuagsobbl|Hanji 1121]] places the diversion alongside Xu Chu's shielding and rowing as the action that gives Cao Cao's boat time to reach safety.
