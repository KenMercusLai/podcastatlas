# Gavin Newsom

## Overview
Gavin Newsom is tracked as a California governor whose wiki profile spans Silicon Valley crisis response, state AI procurement, criticism of California governance, and source-dated 2028 Democratic primary vulnerability.

## Current Profile
Newsom's current profile is double-edged. He has state-executive visibility in technology and crisis contexts: California's AI contractor order makes him a state procurement and federalism actor, while the SVB account gives him a minor crisis-coordination role. But the All-In political sources also make him a symbol of California governance and Democratic continuity, which [[NateSilver]] argues is a difficult 2028 case when voters want change.

## Key Characteristics
- California executive tied to technology policy and Silicon Valley crisis networks.
- State AI procurement actor using government buying power for privacy, security, bias, supply-chain, and watermarking rules.
- California governance lightning rod in All-In criticism of permitting, recovery, taxation, and one-party leadership.
- Democratic 2028 contender whose national brand is vulnerable to continuity with Biden, Harris, and California governance.
- Tactical partisan fighter whose redistricting move may not solve a broader change-election problem.

## Qualifications
The Carolla and Silver claims are source-attributed political analysis, not settled evaluations of California governance or live 2028 odds. The SVB source gives Newsom only a minor team-level role, so it should not be inflated into a primary crisis-leadership claim.
