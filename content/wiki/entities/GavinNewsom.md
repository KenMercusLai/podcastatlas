# Gavin Newsom

Gavin Newsom appears in [[tech-20260403-0403-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260403-0403-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the [[California]] governor behind an AI executive order for state contractors. The [[MarketplaceTech]] episode says the order requires safety and privacy guardrails when AI companies contract with the state, including review of security, privacy, model bias, supply-chain-risk concerns, and watermarking for state-released generative AI material.

The source frames Newsom's action as both procurement policy and federalism politics. [[PareshDave|Paresh Dave]] says the order responds partly to [[DonaldTrump]] administration efforts to discourage or preempt state AI laws, making Newsom a state-level actor in [[StateAIProcurementGuardrails]] and [[AIGovernanceAndCompliance]].
