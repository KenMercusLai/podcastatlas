# 申通快递

## Overview
申通快递 is one of China's major franchise express carriers and the earliest large branch of the [[TongluExpressCluster|桐庐帮]].

## Current Profile
The source traces 申通 from the early 1990s 盛彤 document-delivery business founded by [[NieTengfei|聂腾飞]] and associates. After Nie's death, 陈小英 and [[ChenDejun|陈德军]] continued the company under the 申通 name; its early operator network became both a national carrier and the training ground from which several competitors emerged.

## Key Characteristics
- Early Hangzhou-Shanghai commercial-document carrier.
- Foundational company in the Tonglu founder genealogy.
- Franchise network later reinforced by hubs, trunk routes, and automation.
- Ecommerce carrier exposed to prolonged price competition.
