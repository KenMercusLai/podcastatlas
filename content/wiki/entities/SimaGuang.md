# 司马光 / Sima Guang

Sima Guang appears in [[105-su-dongpo-yiqiang-haomai-zhiqi-yi-dupi-buhe-shiyi-741072645]] as a leading old-party figure after [[SongShenzong|宋神宗]]'s death and the political reversal against [[WangAnshi|王安石]]'s reforms. The episode uses him to show that [[SuShi|苏轼]] was often counted as old party but did not become a faction loyalist.

When Sima Guang and the old party returned to power, Su Shi objected to indiscriminate suppression of new-party measures and people. That makes Sima Guang important to the source's [[ConscienceOverFaction]] frame: Su Shi's standard is not whether "his side" wins, but whether policy and punishment remain answerable to public need and conscience.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-04-yurang-wei-zhiyao-fuchou-ln34tydha6iqwlrgmw7iugqfgfik]] adds Sima Guang's explicit personnel lesson through [[ZhiYao|智瑶]]. The host presents Sima Guang's [[TalentVirtueDistinction|才德之分]] as one of [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]]'s opening principles: talent without virtue can be worse than stupidity because it gives harmful intent more force.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-05-guangcai-duomu-de-weiwenhou-1-lmkxzbmwhw3nhurape-eebydqcdl]] adds Sima Guang's trust lesson through [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]]. The episode presents "小信诚则大信立" as a governing principle and pairs it with a Song court reward case to show that state promises must remain credible after people act on them.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-13-gongsunyang-keke-bianfa-re-shashen-2-lk-d9uibhneerfol6z2rrle-6ypm]] returns to that trust lesson through [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]. The episode says Sima Guang criticizes Shang Yang's harshness but still praises his "信," then broadens the point through [[QiHuanGong|齐桓公]], [[JinWenGong|晋文公]], [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]], and [[XimuLixin|徙木立信]]. The result is a sharper distinction between trust as Confucian moral foundation and trust as Legalist enforcement tool.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-1-lk-6ejgmjhvukqgtlz2lowu8qx-v]] uses Sima Guang more indirectly. The host suggests that [[LiKe|李克]]'s legalist-reform importance may be understated in [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]], and contrasts Sima Guang's severe [[TalentVirtueDistinction|talent-virtue]] instinct with Li Ke's willingness to recommend [[WuQi|吴起]] for a role where military skill mattered.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-2-lmfzo4sl9nd5kz3mrxdl1dl9efei]] adds Sima Guang's signature historical work indirectly through [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]]. The episode does not discuss Sima Guang's Song politics; instead, it shows how the work's compact chronology can be read for military leadership, succession, posthumous names, and politically ambiguous assassination records.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-1-lvkp-ju7pf5z5cz3thojv3s2fkyx]] extends that source-selection frame. The host says Sima Guang does not explain why [[ChuState|楚国]] besieged [[ZhengState|郑国]] or why Zheng killed [[ZiyangZheng|子阳]], treating the silence as evidence of limits in the available record rather than as a solved motive.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]] adds another source-limit case through the blank 395 BCE year, which the episode treats as [[ChronicleSourceGaps]] rather than as Sima Guang's carelessness.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl]] adds a source-selection case through [[Zisi|子思]]. The host argues that Sima Guang's inclusion of Zisi's advice on [[GouBian|苟变]], talent use, and ruler-minister flattery shows the "资治" purpose directly: these speeches teach governing judgment, not only chronology.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] adds the opposite source-selection case: [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]]'s second year is blank in [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]], so the host turns to [[Shiji|《史记》]] for [[TaishiDan|太史旦]]'s visit to Qin and suggests Sima Guang may have left it out because of its prophecy-like elements.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-2-lmnqfjmydygqnfec6xhzpixralvi]] adds a more sympathetic error-frame. The host says several early [[WarringStatesPeriod|战国时期]] dates may be wrong in [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]], but treats that as a consequence of scarce, damaged, and [[Shiji|《史记》]]-mediated source material rather than simple compiler negligence.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-4-lhjz-m12pedkfszxyzgqtjnpsn3p]] adds a selection-and-education contrast. The episode says [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] omits the [[ZhaoCangtang|赵苍唐]] / [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]] restoration story even though [[ZizhiTongjianBu|《资治通鉴补》]] later treats it as worth preserving. It then contrasts the story's practical value of [[Shijing|《诗经》]] fluency with Sima Guang's opposition to poetry-prose testing, a point on which the host says he aligned with [[WangAnshi|王安石]] despite their broader factional divide.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-11-shijie-fengqi-yunyong-1-lt60b2gddiosf5kade35uopwohi5]] adds another compact-annals reading. The host follows [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] into [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]]'s reign, marks blank years, supplements some notices with [[Shiji|《史记》]], and uses [[FufuRobe|黻服]] to show how a terse gift record can carry [[RitualRecognitionOfMilitaryPower|political recognition]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-14-qiweiwang-buming-zeyi-yiming-jingren-lrs6rbifyiqrojxg0mccpg7sf9rv]] adds a direct correction case. The host says Sima Guang's 370 BCE notice about [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] visiting [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]] cannot stand literally because Zhou Lie Wang had died, and treats the problem as inherited early-Warring-States source weakness rather than simple compiler carelessness.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]] adds Sima Guang's ministerial focus as an explicit interpretive topic. The host says the appointment of [[ZhaoXixu|昭奚恤]] as Chu chancellor matters because Song literati could imagine state affairs as governed through emperor-minister cooperation, which the wiki records as [[ChancellorCenteredGovernance]] rather than as a direct claim about Chu institutions.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] continues the compact-annals reading through 352-350 BCE. The episode follows Sima Guang's notices on Qin-Wei war, Shen Buhai's Han service, Shang Yang's second reforms, and Zhao succession while supplementing some details from [[Shiji|《史记》]] and [[ZhushuJinian|《竹书纪年》]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-24-shangyang-yue-wo-za-lie-le-lt4yd6pwlezqia5yljg-gankyhut]] adds a more political reading of Sima Guang's compilation choices. The host contrasts [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]]'s broad "人多怨之" framing of resentment against [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] with [[Shiji|《史记》]]'s focus on royal kin and nobles, then argues that in Sima Guang's Song context this ancient reform story could function as an indirect argument about [[WangAnshi|王安石]] and the [[WangAnshiReforms|王安石变法]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-26-mengzi-laile-ln4twwp2frmr-xpumu0rqg-2gpyn]] adds Sima Guang's reconciliation of [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] and [[Zisi|子思]] on benefit. The episode says Sima Guang treats their apparently opposite statements as one argument about [[RenyiBenefitOrdering|仁义优先的利益秩序]], while also noting that he personally disliked Mencius and wrote [[YiMeng|《疑孟》]] in a Song context where [[WangAnshi|王安石]] elevated Mencius.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-29-suqin-lizhi-shuangwen-zhishi-gaibian-mingyun-lrf5cqvoriyep2lkbozju-lyjaxv]] adds Sima Guang as a poet using [[SuQin|苏秦]] reception. The episode cites his "赠吾之才," addressed to [[WuZhicai|吾之才]], where Su Qin and [[ZhuFuYan|主父偃]] become consolation examples for someone mocked or dismissed by close relations while still capable of later achievement. This makes Su Qin's story not only a pre-Qin biography but a durable [[InspirationalNarrativeCommercialization|励志叙事商业化]] resource.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-42-gei-mengchangjun-ti-yijian-de-houguo-lr1jp0tpiaboaca3j5-yvyhyn4vf]] adds Sima Guang's two-sided reading of [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]]. He criticizes indiscriminate养士 as a private-power use of state-derived resources, but praises Mengchang Jun for accepting correction in the ivory-bed story. The episode makes Sima Guang's standard precise: personnel networks must be justified by public service, while advice should still be judged by correctness even when the adviser has mixed motives.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-44-yi-ge-gongsi-weishenme-buneng-you-liang-wei-ceo-lsz0xk6-0wwznkc62k-xkoxgz-r]] adds a source-selection and Song-political resonance case. The host says a [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] story about [[HanXuanhuiwang|韩宣惠王]] appointing two chief ministers may have been included because it was useful for rule, then connects the lesson to Sima Guang's own era: emperors trying to act often had to choose between new-party and old-party personnel rather than keep both in durable balance.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-48-junwang-weisha-buneng-chuanwei-zaixiang-lnwhljnn888mzlj7let2ptn1pcdm]] adds a direct rule-order reading through [[YanWangKuai|燕王哙]] and [[ZiZhi|子之]]. The host reads Sima Guang's discussion of 礼 as more than ceremony: it is the layered rule system that keeps ruler, minister, father, son, succession, and command legible. That makes [[LiAsPoliticalOrder|礼制政治秩序]] another "资治" principle, adjacent to his earlier personnel and trust lessons.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-49-mengzi-yue-wo-jiao-ni-zhiguo-ni-que-ba-wo-dang-huaping-lhy14pbf9thcfdiaulcmf8dwcef8]] adds another direct "资治" reading through [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]], [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]], and [[YanState|燕国]]. The episode says the chronicle preserves the exchange not only as a classical quotation but as ruler instruction: conquest must become benevolent governance, and known mistakes must be corrected rather than excused. It also flags the Qi Xuan Wang death notice as a source chronology problem.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-67-zhanguo-mieba-baiqi-de-junshi-caineng-jiujing-ruhe-ljw0lltowxd4qz75v-wcnlkwqskv]] adds a direct moral-political criticism through [[MiHeng|芈横 / 楚顷襄王]]. Sima Guang condemns Chu for renewing marriage with Qin after Qin had killed or destroyed [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]], and the host reads this as another case where an ancient notice may also speak to Song rulers about whether expedient settlements erase political shame.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-71-xunzide-xingbenelun-lnyfxoxauexhwbrwkdevs8m4mr2j]] adds Sima Guang's use of [[Xunzi|荀子]] after [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]]'s death. The episode says the Wangba passage from [[XunziText|《荀子》]] is reproduced with adjustment in [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] to teach rulers that state power can become kingly order, hegemonic credibility, or self-destroying deception depending on how it is used.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-84-fangzhi-qinguo-miechu-jing-yong-zhe-zhao-llgh12eistejijsl6yildzkixocm]] adds a source-editing case through [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]]'s letter. The host says Sima Guang's chronicle draws from [[Shiji|《史记》]] while trimming some literary display, leaving a clearer political lesson: a ruler should notice when an apparently victorious campaign creates rear-route risk, ally betrayal risk, and benefit for third parties.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-109-2-chunshenjun-bei-huangdi-dajiuge-quanzu-miemen-locma2plu7v-vsssm5dr5frc3szw]] adds Sima Guang's judgment on the [[WarringStatesFourLords|战国四公子]] through his quotation of [[YangXiong|扬雄]]'s [[Fayan|《法言》]]. The episode reads Sima Guang's stance through the Song "资治" problem: powerful private patrons who gather retainers and bend court politics may look attractive in anti-Qin narrative, but from a centralizing ruler-instruction perspective they also model the loss of sovereign control.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-111-2-wo-yu-lvbuwei-de-gekong-duihua-ltpbkg1echouxwjyhsfax2swfzch]] adds Sima Guang in two roles. First, the episode says he cites [[YangXiong|扬雄]]'s [[Fayan|《法言》]] to close the judgement on [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]], making Lu Buwei another case where ability without morally stable public service becomes dangerous. Second, the host notes that Sima Guang's drought notice omits "天下" from a Shiji-style "天下大旱," a small wording change recorded here under [[TextualVariantPoliticalStakes|史书异文政治重量]] rather than treated as neutral compression.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-112-3-hanfei-yu-yaojia-lunzhan-qinwang-dang-caipan-ljxxlok4uh-ajjirmolybodlspv9]] adds a direct challenge to Sima Guang's Han Fei judgment. The episode says [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] condemns [[HanFei|韩非]] for serving Qin against his own state and treats death as deserved, while [[Shiji|《史记》]] instead presents Han Fei as trying to preserve [[HanState|韩国]]. The host's strongest claim is source-scoped: she argues that Sima Guang's [[SongFactionalPolitics|Song factional]] and anti-[[WangAnshi|王安石]] context shaped or distorted this Legalist reception.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-118-2-chi-gua-le-wangshi-de-gua-bao-tian-lns7jt-kbv3qunm66hzltdgugbco]] adds a source-selection case rather than a direct moral judgment. The host argues that Sima Guang keeps what has "资治" value and omits some fuller [[Shiji|《史记》]] narrative material, so the chronicle's compact Chu endpoint leaves [[ChangpingJun|昌平君]]'s identity, [[MiWan|楚考烈王]]'s sons, and scandal tradition to be reconstructed from other layers. This extends Sima Guang's page as a compiler whose omissions can be principled while still creating later source gaps.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-1-chaijie-jingke-ciqin-bi-bai-de-mimi-lnonhw9tlsu5dudu8750zvgeaz1p]] adds one of Sima Guang's clearest moral-political judgments in the Qinji branch. After [[YanState|燕国]] is destroyed, he refuses to call [[YanTaiziDan|燕太子丹]]贤 and rejects [[JingKe|荆轲]]'s assassination as a thief-like shortcut. The positive standard is ruler instruction: select officials by ability, make policy by reason, calm the people through仁, and build neighboring trust through信; visible bravery or repayment without that [[RenyiGroundedVirtue|仁义为本的信勇]] standard is not enough.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-127-4-qinshihuangling-de-jingtian-mimi-lhgwr2of7zcmg2ggbcdzragwstyi]] adds Sima Guang's balanced judgment on [[MengTian|蒙恬]]. The episode says Sima Guang follows [[YangXiong|扬雄]] in refusing full praise for Meng Tian because he served Qin Shi Huang's harsh rule and helped push labor-heavy works, but still praises Meng Tian's final refusal to rebel or betray the dead ruler. This becomes a Qinji case where Sima Guang separates final臣节 from career-level [[LoyalServiceMoralLiability|忠臣事暴政的罪责]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-171-zhangliang-jiao-ni-ruhe-mingli-shuangshou-1-lptgy7wxwsve8ljt79asetubin41|Hanji 171 part 1]] adds Sima Guang's reading of [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s post-founding withdrawal. Sima Guang argues that Zhang Liang should have known immortality claims were empty, so the wish to follow [[Chisongzi|赤松子]] shows another kind of wisdom: using transcendent language to step away from [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s power field. The host records that insight while criticizing the tendency to blame [[HanXin|韩信]] and [[XiaoHe|萧何]] for not escaping dangers created by ruler suspicion.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-176-shusuntong-ruhe-wei-dahan-zhiding-liyi-zhidu-loeoqccrfwbmtr634ukyau9xykqy|Hanji 176]] adds a positive Sima Guang ritual theory through [[ShusunTong|叔孙通]]'s Changle Palace ceremony. The episode has Sima Guang praise 礼 as a governance system that orders personal conduct, family boundaries, local custom, ruler-minister sequence, administration, and vassal obedience. This extends [[LiAsPoliticalOrder|礼制政治秩序]] from the Yan abdication case into early Western Han court institution-building.
