# Deutsche Bahn

## Overview
Deutsche Bahn appears in [[selling-before-building-1m-arr-in-six-months]] as an enterprise customer case for [[Uplane]]. The episode uses the relationship to show how a narrow marketing operations pain can open a long, multi-stakeholder enterprise adoption process.

## Current Profile
In the current source, Deutsche Bahn is not treated as a general rail-company profile. Its wiki role is specific: it anchors Uplane's enterprise-sales example. A slow ad-formatting workflow, where assets moved through an agency and returned after about two weeks, gave Uplane a concrete automation wedge.

That wedge broadened into conversations with creative, media buying, analytics, compliance, and purchasing stakeholders. The case therefore connects marketing automation to [[EnterprisePOCDiscipline]], [[SaaSTrustMoat]], and [[AtomicContentGuardrails]] rather than only to faster ad production.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as Uplane's enterprise customer example rather than a broad company-history subject in this wiki.
- Reveals a narrow operational wedge: slow ad-formatting work across agency handoffs.
- Shows that enterprise marketing automation involves creative, media-buying, analytics, compliance, and purchasing stakeholders.
- Illustrates why AI marketing tools need brand and compliance guardrails before production use.
- Demonstrates a long enterprise timeline, with the episode describing about nine months of conversations.

## Evidence
- Enterprise wedge: [[selling-before-building-1m-arr-in-six-months]] says Uplane first discussed an ad-formatting process where assets had to be sent to an agency and returned after about two weeks.
- Expansion path: [[selling-before-building-1m-arr-in-six-months]] says automating the formatting step opened conversations with creative, media buying, and analytics teams.
- Procurement complexity: [[selling-before-building-1m-arr-in-six-months]] says the process involved stakeholders, compliance, purchasing, and about nine months of conversations.
- Marketing relevance: [[selling-before-building-1m-arr-in-six-months]] gives a Deutsche Bahn creative example around targeting grandparents with messages about visiting grandchildren.

## Qualifications
The source gives Deutsche Bahn only through Uplane's founder interview. It does not provide the buyer-side view, contract terms, deployment scope, performance lift, or independent confirmation of the implementation.
