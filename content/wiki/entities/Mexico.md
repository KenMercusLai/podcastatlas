# Mexico

Mexico appears in [[vol-128-guanshui-zhan-xiayibu-zouxiang-hefang-meiguo-renmin-ruhe-kandai-telangpu-bairi-xinzheng-kuangxi-bokejie-duihua-zhongshu-talich-ltivoqhue1t-q2ejjlzlenormkhb]] in two trade-war roles. First, it is part of the source's early 2025 tariff timeline, where [[DonaldTrump]] initially pressures China, Mexico, and Canada before the broader April tariff shock. Second, it appears as a possible routing node in [[DeglobalizationTradeIntermediation]] when Chinese goods or trade intermediaries look for ways around high U.S. tariff exposure.

The page is source-scoped: the episode does not provide a full Mexico political or economic analysis. Its wiki role is to mark Mexico as both a direct North American tariff counterparty and a possible intermediate channel in [[TariffExemptionPressureValve]] and [[SupplyChainSovereignty]].

[[big-lettuce-meets-small-intestine]] adds Mexico as a fresh-produce sourcing and inspection case. The [[PlanetMoney]] episode says [[TaylorFarms]] increasingly sources lettuce from Mexico, and that at the time of recording on July 22, 2026 the [[FoodAndDrugAdministration|FDA]] believed tainted [[Cyclospora]] lettuce came from a Mexican farm. The source keeps that attribution unresolved, using it to explain [[ImportedProduceVerification]] rather than to make a final fault claim.
