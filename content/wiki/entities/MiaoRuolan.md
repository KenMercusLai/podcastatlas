## Overview

苗若兰 / Miao Ruolan is [[MiaoRenfeng|苗人凤]]'s daughter in [[XueshanFeihu|《雪山飞狐》]], a non-martial but culturally literate interpreter of jianghu conduct whose testimony and relationship with [[HuFei|胡斐]] connect the feuding families.

## Current Profile

Miao Ruolan carries knowledge without martial force. Her retelling of her father's account corrects Yan Ji's suicide story by establishing that Hu Yidao died after a poisoned wound, although later witnesses are still needed to locate responsibility. She therefore occupies an intermediate position in the novel's truth reconstruction: more reliable than the self-protective first account, but not omniscient.

The episode also treats her as evidence that understanding wuxia values does not require practicing martial arts. She knows jianghu history, recognizes chivalric conduct, and navigates social situations with education and composure. Her cultural fluency meets Hu Fei's poetry, music, and literary knowledge, giving their attraction a basis in reciprocal interpretation rather than physical rescue alone.

That relationship offers a possible repair across generations, yet communication remains incomplete. Her failure to tell Miao Renfeng Hu Fei's identity contributes to the final duel, making her both a bridge beyond inherited enmity and a participant in the silence that sustains it.

## Key Characteristics

- Carries a more accurate but still incomplete account of Hu Yidao's death.
- Understands jianghu ethics without possessing martial skill.
- Combines education, music, poetry, and social judgment with knowledge of family history.
- Forms a reciprocal cultural and emotional connection with Hu Fei.
- Bridges the two family lines while also withholding decisive information.

## Qualifications
