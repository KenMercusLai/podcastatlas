# 典韦 / Dian Wei

典韦 / Dian Wei appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-2-pandian-huangdimen-jiuhou-xiaopihao-livpg3zh7lsqffgca2pomiqnlvyd|Qinji 131-2]] as [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s guard in the Wancheng drinking story. The source says [[ZhangXiu|张绣]]'s side gets him drunk and hides his weapon before launching the attack.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] names [[HuCheEr|胡车儿]] as the person who drinks with Dian Wei and steals his heavy double halberds before the night attack. That detail makes Dian Wei's vulnerability operational: the problem is not only that he drank, but that the weapon and guard layer around Cao Cao were deliberately stripped away.

The episode still treats Dian Wei as physically loyal and effective: even while drunk and deprived of his normal weapon, he fights at the door long enough for Cao Cao to escape. His death therefore sharpens the source's point that alcohol is not only personal weakness; in a command setting it can open a security failure whose costs fall on loyal subordinates and family.
