# Henry Fok / 霍英東

## Overview
Henry Fok / 霍英東 appears in the Tung source as a Beijing-linked Hong Kong business figure whose investment helped rescue [[OrientOverseas|東方海外]] during its 1985 crisis.

## Current Profile
The page currently captures Fok as a supporting actor in the Tung political-turn story. The source presents his capital, alongside Beijing-related support, as crucial to the Tung family's business survival and therefore to the later relationship between [[TungCheeHwa|董建華]] and Beijing.
