# 刘娜 / Liu Na (psychiatrist)

## Overview
刘娜 is presented as a Shanghai Mental Health Center psychiatric guest discussing sexual-minority mental health, family work, and transgender care pathways.

## Current Profile
Liu's contribution separates sexual orientation or gender identity from the distress that can surround it. She rejects treating self-accepted homosexuality as illness, describes anxiety, depression, insomnia, eating problems, or safety risk as legitimate clinical concerns, and places attempted family “correction” outside appropriate treatment. For transgender patients, she describes exploratory, differential, and co-occurring-condition assessment within a multidisciplinary pathway rather than a compulsory route to hormones or surgery.

## Key Characteristics
- Separates minority identity from clinically significant distress or impairment.
- Rejects attempts to change homosexuality through treatment.
- Treats family acceptance and family therapy as possible parts of support.
- Describes transgender care as multidisciplinary and individualized.
- Emphasizes exploration, informed choice, co-occurring conditions, and social adaptation.
