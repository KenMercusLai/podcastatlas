# 夏侯婴 / Xiahou Ying

夏侯婴 / Xiahou Ying enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-1-jiemi-wuguang-bei-miekou-de-lishi-zhenxiang-lk1bkhqkwf9upbbj08pjkn8cstyp|Qinji 129-1]] as part of [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s early anti-Qin field activity. After Liu Bang defeats the Sishui commandery supervisor Ping, Xiahou Ying and [[XiaoHe|萧何]] persuade Ping to surrender Huling County to Liu Bang.

For this source, Xiahou Ying matters as an operational connector inside Liu Bang's local campaign. The episode does not yet develop his later Han role; it uses him to show that Liu Bang's side is already combining battlefield pressure with persuasion and local surrender while [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]] faces command breakdown elsewhere.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-141-huanyuan-lishi-zhong-de-hongmenyan-lpmb-bcoocngtqjvtnpj0elm-siy|Hanji 141]] adds Xiahou Ying to [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s trusted Hongmen entourage. The episode identifies him as a life-and-death friend who often served as Liu Bang's driver; at [[HongmenYan|鸿门宴]], he travels with Liu Bang from [[Bashang|霸上]] but remains outside the camp gate with [[FanKuai|樊哙]], [[JiXin|纪信]], [[JinQiang|金强]], and the cavalry.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-142-zhechang-juhui-xiangyu-shudiao-le-yiqie-ln7dv2jxrk6lt1c4szylsrb41k3r|Hanji 142]] gives Xiahou Ying a return-route role. After Liu Bang leaves the tent and chooses the small-road escape back to [[Bashang|霸上]], Xiahou Ying is listed with Fan Kuai, [[JiXin|纪信]], and the other close attendants who go through the Lishan-Zhiyang route while Liu Bang rides ahead.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-146-qiannian-jingdian-xiaohe-yuexia-zhui-hanxin-lq0oc3vqr-tsr0r-iwztifxeezl|Hanji 146]] gives Xiahou Ying a talent-recognition role. When [[HanXin|韩信]] is about to be executed under military law, Han Xin's challenge about whether the Han king wants the world catches Xiahou Ying's attention. Xiahou Ying releases him, talks with him, and reports him to [[LiuBang|刘邦]], creating the first [[PrivateAccessTalentChannel|私人进身通道]] before [[XiaoHe|萧何]] later makes the stronger recommendation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-152-xiangyu-3wanren-ruhe-wansheng-liubang-56wan-dajun-2-lq9ecpjb4r-ij0-e-iu-ezfavowj|Hanji 152 part 2]] gives Xiahou Ying his strongest family-protection scene. During Liu Bang's escape after the [[PengchengBattle|彭城之战]], Liu Bang pushes [[LiuYing|刘盈]] and [[LuYuanPrincess|鲁元公主]] off the cart under Chu pursuit, while Xiahou Ying repeatedly stops to bring them back. The episode says Liu Ying and [[LuZhi|吕雉]] later remember this rescue, and it also frames Xiahou Ying's long survival under three rulers through humility and careful role discipline.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-162-2-liubang-ruhe-shoushi-wei-gao-quan-zhong-de-xiashu-lqvye2zmqhu3otk0uwu7lakf2prz|Hanji 162-2]] adds another escape-driver role. When [[LiuBang|刘邦]] abandons [[Chenggao|成皋]] under renewed [[XiangYu|项羽]] pressure, Xiahou Ying rides with him north across the Yellow River toward [[HanXin|韩信]] and [[ZhangEr|张耳]]'s camp, where Liu Bang then executes [[CommandAuthorityRecapture|统帅权再收束]].
