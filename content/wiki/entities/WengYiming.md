# 翁一鸣 / Weng Yiming

## Overview
翁一鸣 is a reproductive-medicine guest in VOL.136 of [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]], explaining how prolonged sitting can combine local heat, pressure, occupational stress, and associated habits in male reproductive-health discussions.

## Current Profile
The source presents Weng as a clinician who resists single-cause explanations. He connects prolonged sitting with a warmer local environment and potentially unfavorable sperm-health conditions, while also naming stress, smoking, irregular food, driving exposure, inactivity, and delayed urination as overlapping occupational factors.

His practical advice is based on changing state rather than buying one perfect device. Standing desks can help interrupt sitting but prolonged standing has its own vascular cost; cycling can create perineal pressure or friction; and wider saddles, pauses, walking, hydration, and position changes are bounded adjustments rather than guarantees.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains male reproductive-health concerns through interacting heat, posture, pressure, stress, and lifestyle factors.
- Distinguishes tense work sitting from physically similar but less stressful sitting without endorsing inactivity.
- Rejects the idea that leanness automatically removes sedentary reproductive risk.
- Treats standing desks and bicycle setup as partial adjustments rather than universal solutions.
- Connects intentional urine retention with bladder and infection concerns rather than “training.”
