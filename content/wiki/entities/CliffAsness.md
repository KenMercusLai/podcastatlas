# Cliff Asness

## Overview

Cliff Asness is presented as the head of quantitative investment firm AQR Capital and the coauthor, with [[AaronBrownFinance|Aaron Brown]], of the hockey paper behind the [[PullTheGoalieDecisionRule]].

## Current Profile

The episode uses Asness to distinguish low dependence on approval from mere obnoxiousness. His willingness to say what he finds funny, work through quantitative puzzles, and recommend an embarrassing-looking hockey strategy makes him Gladwell's initial example of [[SituationalDisagreeableness]], but the closing currency story shows that he can set analytical correction aside when a harmless answer comforts a relative.

## Key Characteristics

- Applies quantitative reasoning to decisions whose conventional timing lacks a stated empirical basis.
- Accepts a greater visible risk of lopsided defeat when it improves the modeled probability of recovery.
- Describes himself as willing to risk social trouble by saying what he finds funny.
- Collaborates with Brown by building an initial spreadsheet model that Brown refines computationally.
- Treats independence from approval as compatible with considerate, low-cost accommodation.

## Evidence

### Quantitative challenge to convention

- [[malcolm-gladwell-s-12-rules-for-life-malcolm-gladwell-s-12-rules-for-life]] attributes the initial goalie-pull spreadsheet to Asness and reports much earlier optimal timings after Brown's refinement.

### Approval independence

- [[malcolm-gladwell-s-12-rules-for-life-malcolm-gladwell-s-12-rules-for-life]] contrasts Asness's low concern for embarrassment with Gladwell's reluctance to ask for help after filling a recorder card.

### Context-sensitive kindness

- [[malcolm-gladwell-s-12-rules-for-life-malcolm-gladwell-s-12-rules-for-life]] says Asness gives his aunt an arbitrary currency-exchange date because precise daily prediction is unavailable and the answer makes her happy without measurable harm.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on one structured episode summary rather than Asness's publications, investment record, interviews, the underlying hockey paper, or an independent personality assessment. “Disagreeable” is the episode's interpretive frame and should not be treated as a clinical trait measurement. The paper's recommended timings remain model-dependent, and the aunt story does not establish a general rule for financial advice.

## What Changed

- Established a focused profile of Asness as a quantitative convention-challenger whose approval independence is explicitly situational rather than uniformly abrasive.
