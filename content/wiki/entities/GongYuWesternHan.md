## Overview

贡禹 / Gong Yu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-488-yixue-dashi-jingfang-ruhe-shenxian-bisi-ju-llf_4yvk8urtkgidnj2qhvca3vah|Hanji 488]] as the respected Confucian scholar whose reputation [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]] courts after the death of [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]].

## Current Profile

Gong Yu's source role is reputational rather than policy-centered. Hanji 488 says Shi Xian worries that the death of Xiao Wangzhi may make Confucian scholars across the empire attack him. Shi Xian therefore sends greetings and gifts to Gong Yu and recommends him to [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]], leading to Gong Yu's promotion to a high office.

The episode's synthesis is that Shi Xian repairs more than imperial trust. He also works on public opinion by using an admired scholar's acceptance and advancement to soften blame around Xiao Wangzhi's death.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Confucian scholar presented as highly respected.
- Serves as a reputation-repair target for Shi Xian.
- His promotion helps shift opinion around responsibility for Xiao Wangzhi's death.
- Source role is about symbolic legitimacy rather than detailed policy action.
