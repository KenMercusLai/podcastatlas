## Overview

贡禹 / Gong Yu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-466-gongyu-tanguan-beihou-wangji-he-gongyu-de-gushi-lo54ac1v8plqft5zxmorlk4-vx0t|Hanji 466]] as the upright classicist summoned by [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] near the start of Chuyuan 1 / 48 BCE. The episode links him to [[WangJiWesternHan|王吉]] through the "贡禹弹冠" idiom: after Wang Ji is summoned, Gong Yu reads the call as evidence that their shared values may again have court space. Hanji 466 also gives the first positive thrift-remonstrance layer, where Gong Yu criticizes palace, harem, horse, manufacturing, burial, and mausoleum-service expenses during epidemic, famine, and relief pressure. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-488-yixue-dashi-jingfang-ruhe-shenxian-bisi-ju-llf_4yvk8urtkgidnj2qhvca3vah|Hanji 488]] later presents him as the respected Confucian scholar whose reputation [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]] courts after the death of [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-478-chushi-zhihui-zhezhong-ren-tai-jiduan-yudao-qing-yuanli-lq3hhg58lohdi-tbpmcuhwajjo7h|Hanji 478]] adds the critical layer: Gong Yu's thrift advice is formally correct but evasive because it avoids the central problem of slanderers and corrupt favorites. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-474-guji-kangkai-fusi-wo-weihe-renwei-ta-yuchun-lp-07zvaxr21dt2mtva2ndp6b9by|Hanji 474]] adds a practical caution layer: Gong Yu joins [[KuangHengWesternHan|匡衡]] in advising that [[GuJiWesternHan|谷吉]] escort Zhizhi Chanyu's hostage son only to the frontier rather than to the chanyu's court. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-473-xihan-yongren-chenwannian-weihe-neng-bei-zairu-shice-ljxaektybs1lvddzuuqca-rpjwxy|Hanji 473]] adds his Chuyuan 5 appointment layer: after [[ChenWannianWesternHan|陈万年]] dies, Yuan refuses Gong Yu's old-age resignation and appoints him Yushi Dafu.

## Current Profile

Gong Yu's profile now begins as a policy and reputation figure together. Hanji 466 presents him as part of a paired moral network with Wang Ji: one friend's summons becomes the other's signal that principled service may be possible again. After Wang Ji dies en route, Gong Yu reaches court as Jianyi Dafu and gives a concrete austerity memorial that links ruler example, palace consumption, women's enforced palace/mausoleum placement, royal horses, manufacturing offices, thick burial, and famine relief. The episode treats Yuan's acceptance as a positive early receptive-remonstrance case.

The combined profile is ambivalent but no longer only evasive. Hanji 488 says Shi Xian repairs more than imperial trust: he also works on public opinion by using an admired scholar's acceptance and advancement to soften blame around Xiao Wangzhi's death. Hanji 473 shows why that reputation was politically useful: Yuan praises Gong Yu's integrity and Confucian standing even when Gong Yu asks to retire, then brings him into the Yushi Dafu post after Chen Wannian's death. Hanji 478 adds the counterweight that respected moral speech can still fail if it stays with safe topics while avoiding the decisive institutional danger. Hanji 474 adds that Gong Yu can also give concrete risk-limiting advice when a mission's danger is foreseeable.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Confucian scholar presented as highly respected and linked to Wang Ji through the "贡禹弹冠" idiom.
- Early Jianyi Dafu whose thrift memorial connects palace consumption, harem numbers, royal horses, manufacturing offices, thick burial, and famine relief.
- Serves as a reputation-repair target for Shi Xian.
- Appointed Yushi Dafu after Chen Wannian's death despite asking to retire because of age and infirmity.
- Offers thrift advice that the source treats as correct but insufficient.
- Gives practical mission-limiting advice in the Gu Ji hostage-escort case.
- Becomes an example of moral reputation that does not automatically equal direct remonstrance.

## Evidence

- Wang Ji connection: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-466-gongyu-tanguan-beihou-wangji-he-gongyu-de-gushi-lo54ac1v8plqft5zxmorlk4-vx0t|Hanji 466]] says Wang Ji and Gong Yu are friends, and explains "贡禹弹冠" as Gong Yu preparing for office after Wang Ji's summons signals possible opportunity.
- Early thrift memorial: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-466-gongyu-tanguan-beihou-wangji-he-gongyu-de-gushi-lo54ac1v8plqft5zxmorlk4-vx0t|Hanji 466]] says Gong Yu urges Yuan to cut luxury goods, harem numbers, imperial horses, hunting grounds, thick burial, and mausoleum service so resources can turn toward commoner relief.
- Reputation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-488-yixue-dashi-jingfang-ruhe-shenxian-bisi-ju-llf_4yvk8urtkgidnj2qhvca3vah|Hanji 488]] says Gong Yu is skilled in Confucian classics and has high moral standing.
- Shi Xian's approach: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-488-yixue-dashi-jingfang-ruhe-shenxian-bisi-ju-llf_4yvk8urtkgidnj2qhvca3vah|Hanji 488]] says Shi Xian sends greetings and heavy gifts through intermediaries.
- Promotion and opinion shift: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-488-yixue-dashi-jingfang-ruhe-shenxian-bisi-ju-llf_4yvk8urtkgidnj2qhvca3vah|Hanji 488]] says Shi Xian recommends Gong Yu to Yuan, Gong Yu rises to a nine-minister rank, and public opinion gradually turns more favorable to Shi Xian.
- Yushi Dafu appointment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-473-xihan-yongren-chenwannian-weihe-neng-bei-zairu-shice-ljxaektybs1lvddzuuqca-rpjwxy|Hanji 473]] says Gong Yu asks to resign because of age and sensory decline, but Yuan praises his upright reputation and later appoints him Yushi Dafu after Chen Wannian's death.
- Evasive advice: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-478-chushi-zhihui-zhezhong-ren-tai-jiduan-yudao-qing-yuanli-lq3hhg58lohdi-tbpmcuhwajjo7h|Hanji 478]] says Gong Yu advises Yuan to economize and reduce expense, while Sima Guang criticizes him for not addressing Yuan's trust in slanderers and corrupt favorites.
- Mission caution: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-474-guji-kangkai-fusi-wo-weihe-renwei-ta-yuchun-lp-07zvaxr21dt2mtva2ndp6b9by|Hanji 474]] says Gong Yu and Kuang Heng advise that Gu Ji escort Zhizhi Chanyu's hostage son only to the frontier because Zhizhi has not truly submitted.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 466, Hanji 474, Hanji 488, Hanji 478, and Hanji 473 and does not settle Gong Yu's full biography, writings, offices, motives for accepting Shi Xian's outreach, full memorial wording, exact Gu Ji mission advice wording, or independent judgment of Xiao Wangzhi's death. Hanji 466 supports the early thrift memorial and Wang Ji friendship layer, but not the complete implementation record of Yuan's accepted reforms.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 466's early positive layer: Gong Yu's friendship with Wang Ji, the "贡禹弹冠" idiom, and the accession-era thrift memorial before later criticism qualifies him.
