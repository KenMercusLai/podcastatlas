## Overview

贡禹 / Gong Yu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-488-yixue-dashi-jingfang-ruhe-shenxian-bisi-ju-llf_4yvk8urtkgidnj2qhvca3vah|Hanji 488]] as the respected Confucian scholar whose reputation [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]] courts after the death of [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-478-chushi-zhihui-zhezhong-ren-tai-jiduan-yudao-qing-yuanli-lq3hhg58lohdi-tbpmcuhwajjo7h|Hanji 478]] adds a more critical layer: Gong Yu's thrift advice to [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] is treated as formally correct but evasive because it avoids the central problem of slanderers and corrupt favorites. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-474-guji-kangkai-fusi-wo-weihe-renwei-ta-yuchun-lp-07zvaxr21dt2mtva2ndp6b9by|Hanji 474]] adds a practical caution layer: Gong Yu joins [[KuangHengWesternHan|匡衡]] in advising that [[GuJiWesternHan|谷吉]] escort Zhizhi Chanyu's hostage son only to the frontier rather than to the chanyu's court. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-473-xihan-yongren-chenwannian-weihe-neng-bei-zairu-shice-ljxaektybs1lvddzuuqca-rpjwxy|Hanji 473]] adds his Chuyuan 5 appointment layer: after [[ChenWannianWesternHan|陈万年]] dies, Yuan refuses Gong Yu's old-age resignation and appoints him Yushi Dafu.

## Current Profile

Gong Yu's source role is reputational rather than policy-centered. Hanji 488 says Shi Xian worries that the death of Xiao Wangzhi may make Confucian scholars across the empire attack him. Shi Xian therefore sends greetings and gifts to Gong Yu and recommends him to [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]], leading to Gong Yu's promotion to a high office.

The combined profile is ambivalent but no longer only evasive. Hanji 488 says Shi Xian repairs more than imperial trust: he also works on public opinion by using an admired scholar's acceptance and advancement to soften blame around Xiao Wangzhi's death. Hanji 473 shows why that reputation was politically useful: Yuan praises Gong Yu's integrity and Confucian standing even when Gong Yu asks to retire, then brings him into the Yushi Dafu post after Chen Wannian's death. Hanji 478 adds that respected moral speech can still fail if it stays with safe topics while avoiding the decisive institutional danger. Hanji 474 adds that Gong Yu can also give concrete risk-limiting advice when a mission's danger is foreseeable.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Confucian scholar presented as highly respected.
- Serves as a reputation-repair target for Shi Xian.
- Appointed Yushi Dafu after Chen Wannian's death despite asking to retire because of age and infirmity.
- His promotion helps shift opinion around responsibility for Xiao Wangzhi's death.
- Offers thrift advice that the source treats as correct but insufficient.
- Gives practical mission-limiting advice in the Gu Ji hostage-escort case.
- Becomes an example of moral reputation that does not automatically equal direct remonstrance.

## Evidence

- Reputation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-488-yixue-dashi-jingfang-ruhe-shenxian-bisi-ju-llf_4yvk8urtkgidnj2qhvca3vah|Hanji 488]] says Gong Yu is skilled in Confucian classics and has high moral standing.
- Shi Xian's approach: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-488-yixue-dashi-jingfang-ruhe-shenxian-bisi-ju-llf_4yvk8urtkgidnj2qhvca3vah|Hanji 488]] says Shi Xian sends greetings and heavy gifts through intermediaries.
- Promotion and opinion shift: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-488-yixue-dashi-jingfang-ruhe-shenxian-bisi-ju-llf_4yvk8urtkgidnj2qhvca3vah|Hanji 488]] says Shi Xian recommends Gong Yu to Yuan, Gong Yu rises to a nine-minister rank, and public opinion gradually turns more favorable to Shi Xian.
- Yushi Dafu appointment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-473-xihan-yongren-chenwannian-weihe-neng-bei-zairu-shice-ljxaektybs1lvddzuuqca-rpjwxy|Hanji 473]] says Gong Yu asks to resign because of age and sensory decline, but Yuan praises his upright reputation and later appoints him Yushi Dafu after Chen Wannian's death.
- Evasive advice: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-478-chushi-zhihui-zhezhong-ren-tai-jiduan-yudao-qing-yuanli-lq3hhg58lohdi-tbpmcuhwajjo7h|Hanji 478]] says Gong Yu advises Yuan to economize and reduce expense, while Sima Guang criticizes him for not addressing Yuan's trust in slanderers and corrupt favorites.
- Mission caution: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-474-guji-kangkai-fusi-wo-weihe-renwei-ta-yuchun-lp-07zvaxr21dt2mtva2ndp6b9by|Hanji 474]] says Gong Yu and Kuang Heng advise that Gu Ji escort Zhizhi Chanyu's hostage son only to the frontier because Zhizhi has not truly submitted.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 474, Hanji 488, Hanji 478, and Hanji 473 and does not settle Gong Yu's full biography, writings, offices, motives for accepting Shi Xian's outreach, full memorial wording, exact Gu Ji mission advice wording, or independent judgment of Xiao Wangzhi's death. Hanji 473 supports Yuan's praise and Gong Yu's Yushi Dafu appointment, but not the full record of his later memorials.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 473's appointment layer: Yuan refuses Gong Yu's age-based withdrawal and makes him Yushi Dafu after Chen Wannian's death.
