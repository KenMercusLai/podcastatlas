# Andreas Karlstadt

## Overview

Andreas Karlstadt was a Wittenberg reformer whose iconoclasm, sacramental disagreement, status renunciation, and criticism of feudal wealth carried reform beyond limits accepted by [[MartinLuther]].

## Current Profile

Karlstadt led change in Wittenberg while Luther was at Wartburg, but his removal of images and understanding of the Eucharist split the alliance. Luther feared that his program would alienate princely protection, helped secure his censorship and exile, and continued to attack him personally.

Karlstadt abandoned academic titles, dressed in peasant gray, farmed briefly, and associated reform with poverty and resistance to rulers who opposed God's law. During the [[GermanPeasantsWar]] he was threatened from multiple directions, accepted Luther's protection under recantation and publishing restrictions, and later moved to Switzerland.

## Key Characteristics

- Advanced iconoclasm and Eucharistic positions beyond Luther's preferred reform.
- Renounced status symbols and tried to identify materially with peasants and the poor.
- Treated divine law as superior to earthly rule and allowed resistance to ungodly rulers.
- Was constrained through censorship, banishment, recantation, and geographic limits.
- Helped transmit more radical reform currents toward Switzerland and later Protestant traditions in the episode's account.

## Qualifications

The source's long-range path from Karlstadt through Swiss reform to the Netherlands, Britain, and America is interpretive and does not demonstrate a simple line of transmission. His humility may reflect conviction, performance, or both; the episode favors a sympathetic reading but cannot settle motive.

## What Changed

- Established Karlstadt as a new canonical entity.
- Added status renunciation and poverty ethics alongside theological disagreement.
- Preserved coercion by Luther and princes as part of the reform conflict.

## Relationships

- [[MartinLuther]] - former colleague who opposed, censored, exiled, and later conditionally protected him.
- [[ThomasMuntzer]] - distinct radical reformer paired with Karlstadt in Luther's appeal to Saxon princes.
- [[GermanPeasantsWar]] - crisis that endangered Karlstadt from both nobles and rebels.
- [[RevolutionaryOutflanking]] - pattern in which his reforms made Luther appear conservative.
- [[TwoKingdomsDoctrine]] - Luther's answer to Karlstadt's willingness to judge rulers by divine law.
