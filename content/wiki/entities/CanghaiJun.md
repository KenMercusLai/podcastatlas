# 沧海君 / Canghai Jun

沧海君 / Canghai Jun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-123-1-shen-zhi-yuyan-wangqin-zhe-hu-jing-shi-zhi-lg7m3vfdbcweb4joxe4jvhxjx6y8]] as the eastern contact through whom [[ZhangLiang|张良]] finds the strongman used in the [[Bolangsha|博浪沙]] attack on [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]. The episode keeps his identity uncertain and mentions a later scholarly possibility that he may have been an outside-Qin leader associated with the Canghai commandery name used in [[SimaQian|司马迁]]'s era.

The page is intentionally narrow. Canghai Jun's source function is to mark the non-Qin or far-eastern channel through which Zhang Liang's assassination resources become plausible, especially the heavy iron hammer detail that raises questions after Qin's earlier [[PostConquestDisarmament|post-conquest disarmament]].
