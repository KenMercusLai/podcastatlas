# Aisha (Gaza Letters)

Aisha is the Gaza-based interview subject in [[ChengJing|程静]]'s "加沙来信" reporting, discussed in [[xianchang-xianchang-2025-nianzhong-teji-sanlian-nvjizhe-jiemi-xinwen-beihou-de-ku-yu-guang-loz-sggelhx166n2me8v6d8k9a3c]]. Cheng Jing met her through Palestinian contacts in October 2023, then maintained intermittent contact through months of war, disconnection, displacement pressure, and family decision-making.

The source presents Aisha as educated, English-speaking, born in 1993 like Cheng Jing, and formerly connected to Turkey through study and language. That profile matters because Cheng Jing explicitly cautions that Aisha cannot stand for all Gaza residents, even though her own suffering is already severe. Aisha therefore becomes a case for [[RemoteReportingPresence|remote reporting presence]] and for the humility required when [[DeepReportingEmotionalLabor|deep reporting emotional labor]] crosses large differences in danger and lived experience.
