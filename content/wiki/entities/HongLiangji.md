# 洪亮吉 / Hong Liangji

## Overview
洪亮吉 appears as an early Qing thinker of population pressure and dynastic condition whose court position gave him access to materials about Qianlong-era governance.

## Current Profile
The source uses Hong as an opening figure rather than its darkest pessimist. He recognized that population growth could outpace available means and that more people were not automatically beneficial, while his work on the Qianlong Veritable Records is presented as a source of institutional knowledge.

## Key Characteristics
- Diagnosed population-resource pressure before the mid-century crisis became open collapse.
- Is presented as a Hanlin compiler with access to central records.
- Serves as an earlier point in the episode's lineage of cycle diagnosis.
- Appears less pessimistic than Gong Zizhen and Wei Yuan in Sun Ming's comparison.
