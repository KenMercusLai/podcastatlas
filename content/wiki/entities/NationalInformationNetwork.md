# National Information Network

The National Information Network, or NIN, is the domestic network that [[AmirRashidi]] says remains available inside [[Iran]] during the blackout described in [[tech-20260402-0402-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260402-0402-mp-tech-pod-128]]. The episode presents it as fully controlled by the regime and distinct from ordinary access to the global internet.

Its wiki importance is that it turns an internet blackout into a selective-access system. Domestic apps and services may continue operating, but [[Google]], social media, international news, and outside alert systems are blocked or unreachable, while [[DomesticServiceCensorship]] can remove sensitive terms inside the domestic search layer.

## Key Claims
- The NIN has physical infrastructure and application-layer services built on top of it.
- Domestic equivalents for email, search, and delivery-style services can keep working on the NIN.
- Rashidi says access to outside services such as [[Google]], [[BBC]], [[CNN]], and social media is cut off.
- Searching for "war" in the national search service returns no results in Rashidi's example.
- The NIN does not solve civilian alerting or outside communication because wartime reporting and tools such as [[MahsaAlert]] still require internet access.
