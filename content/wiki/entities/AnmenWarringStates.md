# 岸门 / Anmen (Warring States)

岸门 / Anmen appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-48-junwang-weisha-buneng-chuanwei-zaixiang-lnwhljnn888mzlj7let2ptn1pcdm]] as the place where [[QinState|秦国]] defeats [[HanState|韩国]] in [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s first-year frame. The episode locates it south of present-day Hejin in Shanxi.

The notice matters because Han responds by sending crown prince Han Cang, later [[HanXiangwang|韩襄王]], to Qin as a hostage. Anmen is therefore a compact marker of [[QinEastwardPressure|秦国东进压力]]: Qin's battlefield success turns directly into hostage diplomacy and Han's search for peace.
