# 封陵 / Fengling (Warring States)

封陵 / Fengling appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-60-zhongguo-di-yi-wei-bachi-chaozheng-de-taihou-jingshi-ta-ltazis9qo0dwvwmzxikk8mmpvosc]] as one of the [[WeiState|魏国]] places captured by [[QinState|秦国]] in 303 BCE. The episode groups Fengling with [[PubanWarringStates|蒲阪]] and [[YangjinWarringStates|阳晋]] as important crossing positions between Hexi and Hedong.

Like Yangjin, Fengling is source-scoped as a route asset rather than a developed locality. It shows how Qin's diplomatic turn toward [[ChuState|楚国]] is paired with practical pressure on Han and Wei.
