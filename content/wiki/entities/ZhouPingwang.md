# 周平王 / King Ping of Zhou

周平王 / King Ping of Zhou appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] through the episode's account of [[QinState|秦国]] becoming a recognized state. The source says [[QinXiangGong|秦襄公]] escorts him east, after which Zhou Ping Wang grants Qin vassal status and land west of Qishan.

The grant matters because the episode says Qin still had to recover that land from the Quanrong. Qin's recognition by [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]] therefore becomes both a legitimacy claim and a territorial assignment.
