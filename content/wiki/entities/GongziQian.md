# 公子虔 / Gongzi Qian

公子虔 / Gongzi Qian appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-23-weihuiwang-hui-buyong-gongshu-zhi-yan-lveaqklo7oncmqudgmdk7l03qhsf]] as the leader or emblem of the Qin faction that accuses [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] of rebellion after [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]] dies and [[YingSi|嬴驷]] succeeds.

The episode treats him as part of [[WarringStatesReformBacklash|战国变法反噬]]. Shang Yang's reforms had offended entrenched, high-status groups under Qin Xiao Gong's protection; once that ruler dies, Gongzi Qian's accusation turns accumulated hostility into a charge that makes Shang Yang flee.
