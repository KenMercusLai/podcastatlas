# 中国营造学社

中国营造学社 enters the wiki through [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] as the architectural-history institution associated with [[LiangSicheng|梁思成]] and [[LinHuiyin|林徽因]]. It is one of the four institutional peaks named in [[HuangyeShangDeDashi|《荒野上的大师》]].

The episode treats the society's work as a demanding field practice. Scholars had to travel to remote temples and towns, persuade local custodians, endure physical hardship, measure buildings, and connect observed structures to texts such as 《营造法式》. During the war, the same institution had to survive budget scarcity and displacement.

## Key Claims
- 中国营造学社 makes architecture a field science of built evidence, not only an art-historical topic.
- The source uses the society to correct gossip-centered readings of Liang and Lin.
- Its wartime funding struggle shows how research preservation depends on mundane institutional support.
