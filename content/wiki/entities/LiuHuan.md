# 刘欢 / Liu Huan

## Overview
刘欢 / Liu Huan is a Chinese singer, songwriter, and teacher represented here through an [[ZhankaiJiangjiang|展开讲讲]] retrospective on television themes, public-event songs, original music, and the making of a durable public persona.

## Current Profile
The source reads Liu less as a sequence of famous songs than as a bridge between private fate and public narration. Themes associated with 《便衣警察》, 《雪城》, 《北京人在纽约》, 《水浒传》, 《宝莲灯》, 《笑傲江湖》, and 《甄嬛传》 circulated through a television system capable of producing shared attention; public performances from 《亚洲雄风》 to 《我和你》 then made his voice part of changing national self-presentation.

His long hair, black clothing, teaching identity, selective public presence, and controlled delivery are interpreted as one consistent form of independence without theatrical rebellion. The episode also credits him with supporting original music and treats 《中国好歌曲》 as an intervention in a difficult industry environment.

## Key Characteristics
- Voice associated with both individual life stories and large public narratives.
- Career formed inside the shared-attention ecology of broadcast television and major public events.
- Public image combining recognizable individuality with restraint, stability, and mainstream legibility.
- Maintained teaching and musical roles rather than allowing celebrity identity to absorb the whole career.
- Connected in the source to advocacy for original music and concern about copyright.

- Personal proportion: [[shi-yue-guangbo-dianshibao-wo-yijing-buzai-shi-wo-er-ni-que-yiran-shi-ni-gkwridooptsjbelolwtoxdnu]] connects long hair, black clothing, teaching, sparse celebrity performance, and vocal control to a stable sense of distance.
- Industry role: [[shi-yue-guangbo-dianshibao-wo-yijing-buzai-shi-wo-er-ni-que-yiran-shi-ni-gkwridooptsjbelolwtoxdnu]] attributes to Liu concern for original music, copyright, and emerging songwriters.

## Qualifications
This profile is based on one interpretive retrospective. Song-production accounts, interview recollections, recording speed, wardrobe preparation, institutional roles, and claims about national reception remain episode-attributed; the page does not establish a complete biography, discography, or reception history.
