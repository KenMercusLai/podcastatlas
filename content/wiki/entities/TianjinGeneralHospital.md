# 天津总医院 / Tianjin General Hospital

## Overview
天津总医院 / Tianjin General Hospital is the hospital affiliation named for ophthalmologist [[MaoChunjie|毛春杰]] in the VOL.36 and VOL.38 [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episodes.

## Current Profile
The current wiki profile is narrow and source-scoped. The two sources use Tianjin General Hospital as the institutional context for Mao Chunjie's ophthalmology expertise, not as a broad profile of the hospital. Its relevance is therefore indirect: it anchors public education covering diabetic fundus disease, dry eye, myopia surgery, red eye, eye-drop self-medication, visual fatigue, contact lenses, glaucoma context, environmental protection, and presbyopia fitting.

## Key Characteristics
- Named as the institutional setting for [[MaoChunjie|毛春杰]]'s ophthalmology role.
- Appears in the wiki through a public medical-literacy podcast rather than through an institutional source.
- Grounds the episode's discussion of ophthalmology, eye screening, dry eye, myopia surgery, and eye-drop caution.
- Grounds a second episode's practical discussion of office-worker eye fatigue, optical devices, ambient light, and age-related near correction.
