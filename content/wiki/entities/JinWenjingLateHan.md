## Overview

晋文经 / Jin Wenjing is a late-Han figure from Hanzhong whose wiki role is source-scoped to Hanji 862. The episode pairs him with [[HuangYunLateHan|黄允]] as a person whose talent reputation grows large enough to influence elite appointment opinion before his conduct has been publicly proved.

## Current Profile

The source presents Jin Wenjing through a reputation mechanism rather than a full biography. He and Huang Yun both rely on talent and fame, refuse summons to office, and stay in Luoyang under the pretext of illness while making themselves hard to meet. That scarcity increases their prestige: senior officials send disciples to ask after them, and Three Excellencies-level selection choices are influenced by their praise or criticism.

The turning point comes when [[FuRongLateHan|符融]] warns [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]] that the two men's conduct and achievements are not actually known. The source says their reputation then declines quickly, visitors decrease, and both later become disgraced through crime. Jin Wenjing therefore helps define [[EmptyReputationRealityGap|空誉违实]] as a public-selection risk, not merely a private character flaw.

## Key Characteristics

- Hanzhong reputation figure paired with Huang Yun.
- Talent-famous but conduct-unproven actor in the source.
- Refuses office and uses illness as a posture that preserves scarcity and mystique.
- Indirectly influences official selection through elite consultation.
- Loses public standing after Fu Rong challenges the gap between fame and evidence.
- Source case for reputation becoming appointment-channel noise.

## Qualifications

This page does not settle a full biography of Jin Wenjing. It records the role preserved by Hanji 862: a talent reputation that becomes socially and institutionally influential before conduct has been tested. The source note normalizes the transcript's "金文金" to 晋文经.
