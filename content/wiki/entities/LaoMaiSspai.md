# 老麦 / Michael

## Overview
老麦 / Michael is the [[Sspai|少数派]] founder and former [[ZhiJiWang|主机网]] community organizer interviewed by [[LiuFei|刘飞]] in the No.229 [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] source.

## Current Profile
The source presents 老麦 as a community-first technology media founder. His path starts with early smartphone enthusiasm around Motorola Linux phones, [[Palm]] devices, ROM sharing, and BBS culture, then moves through the rise and collapse of [[WebOS|webOS]] expectations into the creation of [[Sspai|少数派]] as an application-recommendation and digital-life media platform.

His current wiki profile is not mainly a hardware nostalgia profile. The episode uses his story to explain how trust can migrate: blog readers became QQ-group members, QQ-group members built a forum, forum trust made carrier content work possible, and paid author systems, courses, memberships, product co-creation, and offline space extended community value beyond traffic.

## Key Characteristics
- He built from personal device enthusiasm and sharing rather than a formal media plan.
- He treated community memory and searchable discussion as more durable than QQ-group chat alone.
- He responded to webOS collapse by supporting developers and later redirecting operating trust into 少数派.
- He defines 少数派's value through user and creator density rather than maximum platform scale.
- He connects media work to author compensation, paid courses, product co-creation, and offline gathering.
- He treats AI-era content abundance as a reason to emphasize real human experience and deep usage writing.
