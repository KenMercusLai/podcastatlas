# John Ball

## Overview

John Ball was the radical preacher whom the episode connects to the egalitarian and anticlerical currents within the [[PeasantsRevolt1381|Peasants’ Revolt of 1381]].

## Current Profile

Ball appears as an ideological influence rather than the episode’s main narrative actor. The hosts associate him with the movement’s religious radicalism and with [[WatTyler|Wat Tyler’s]] Smithfield demands to reduce church hierarchy and property. Possible overlap with John Wycliffe’s ideas is discussed, but the relationship is not established as a direct line of influence.

After the revolt spread beyond London, Ball was captured, tried, and executed during the wider suppression. His role helps explain why the rising cannot be reduced to taxation or labor grievance alone: arguments about Christian truth, clerical wealth, hierarchy, and justice interacted with material demands and [[RebelRoyalism|royalist allegiance]].

## Key Characteristics

- Radical preacher associated with egalitarian and anticlerical strands of the rising.
- Ideological figure linking material grievance to religious claims about truth and justice.
- Influence invoked to interpret Tyler’s church-related demands at Smithfield.
- Captured and executed during the post-London suppression.

## Evidence

- Religious program: [[414-the-peasants-revolt-londons-burning-part-2-glt6726049231]] connects Ball to Tyler’s attack on church hierarchy and property.
- Wider ideological setting: [[414-the-peasants-revolt-londons-burning-part-2-glt6726049231]] discusses proto-Protestant currents and possible Wycliffite affinities without treating them as settled.
- Fate: [[414-the-peasants-revolt-londons-burning-part-2-glt6726049231]] records Ball’s capture, trial, and execution during suppression.

## Qualifications

The episode does not reconstruct Ball’s full teaching, writings, or movements. Proto-Protestant language is retrospective, and any direct intellectual dependency on John Wycliffe remains possible rather than demonstrated.

## What Changed

- Added Ball as the religious-radical bridge between the revolt’s material demands and its attack on ecclesiastical hierarchy.
