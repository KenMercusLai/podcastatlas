# Hostess Brands

## Overview

Hostess Brands is represented in [[nooks-crannies-nooks-crannies|Nooks & Crannies]] as the competing bakery company that hired [[ChrisBotticella]] while he was still a senior [[BimboBakeries]] executive.

## Current Profile

Hostess matters to the episode primarily as the destination of Botticella's planned job move. Botticella reportedly accepted the role in October 2009, promised Hostess not to disclose Bimbo's confidential information, and remained at Bimbo through year-end; Bimbo learned of the move through a Hostess press release shortly before his planned start.

The injunction prevented the employment from beginning, and Hostess later moved on. The supplied source does not claim that Hostess received Bimbo information or participated in misappropriation.

## Key Characteristics

- Direct bakery competitor in the episode's employment transition.
- Prospective employer whose public announcement alerted Bimbo.
- Required Botticella not to disclose his former employer's confidential information.
- Did not ultimately employ him after the injunction.
