# Orlando B. Potter

Orlando B. Potter appears in [[the-invention-invention]] as the lawyer who proposed turning the sewing-machine patent war into the [[SewingMachineCombination]]. The source says Potter's idea was for rival sewing-machine companies to stop suing each other and combine their patents into one pool.

Potter's role makes the episode's title concrete: the "invention invention" is not another machine part but an institution for making overlapping inventions commercially usable.
