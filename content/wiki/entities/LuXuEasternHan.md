## Overview

[[LuXuEasternHan|陆续 / Lu Xu]] is the Wu-commandery official caught in the Chu-prison case in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-740-rang-ni-tihuguanding-de-zhichang-zhihui-zhichang-bibei-lih5b-ighkyoycawm5c-dbrnuiew|Hanji 740]]. His refusal to produce divergent testimony under torture, and his recognition of food prepared by his mother, become character evidence that helps [[EmperorMingOfHan|汉明帝]] distinguish one trapped group from the wider implication cascade.

## Current Profile

Lu Xu's profile is a narrow but important case of non-confession evidence inside coercive legal pressure. He appears as a subordinate of Yin Xing alongside Liang Hong and Si Xun; all are jailed after the Chu-king case expands. The episode emphasizes that torture does not make their testimony diverge, and that Lu Xu's grief over his mother's precisely cut food reveals family discipline and trustworthiness.

The result is partial mercy rather than full vindication. Mingdi pardons Yin Xing's group but still imposes lifelong office exclusion in the chronicle setting. Lu Xu therefore supports a careful synthesis: personal character can interrupt coercive suspicion, but it does not undo the structural damage of a treason panic.

## Key Characteristics

- Wu-commandery official pulled into the Chu-prison case through association with Yin Xing.
- Torture-resistant witness whose group does not produce mutually destructive testimony under pressure.
- Filial-recognition figure whose knowledge of his mother's food preparation becomes character evidence.
- Partial-redress figure: his case helps release a group, but not erase all penalties.
- Evidence-boundary actor who shows why coercive confession should not be the only legal signal.

## Qualifications

Lu Xu is not currently a broad official biography. His page is limited to the Chu-prison episode and the way family discipline, torture resistance, and non-confession evidence function inside it. The food-recognition detail should not be generalized into a rule that character evidence is sufficient by itself; it matters here because it interrupts a coercive case environment.
