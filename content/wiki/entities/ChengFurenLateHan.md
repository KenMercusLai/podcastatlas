# 程夫人 / Madam Cheng (late Han)

程夫人 / Madam Cheng (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-933-weiguan-zhidao-yousuo-wei-yousuo-buwei-lopulnufsagoqyx7nngvcqi2nhrj|Hanji 933]] as [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s wet nurse and palace-access broker in [[CuiLieLateHan|崔烈]]'s purchase of the situ office. The episode says Cui Lie sends five million cash through her to obtain the post.

Her role shows why office sale is not only a fiscal problem. When Emperor Ling regrets not charging ten million, Madam Cheng defends the price by saying Cui Lie was a famous [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] figure and hard to persuade. She therefore appears as the intimate channel that translates elite ambition, palace access, and royal pricing into a public appointment.
