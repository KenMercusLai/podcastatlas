# Pedro Sanchez

Pedro Sanchez enters the wiki through [[taken-littorally-spains-sudden-crisis-in-ceuta-6a70712c034f16a52ebfaed7]] as the Spanish prime minister whose immigration policy and [[WesternSahara]] stance come under pressure after the [[Ceuta]] crossing surge. [[MichaelReid]] says Spanish opposition figures were asking whether the government had been forewarned and how the situation was allowed to happen.

The source connects Sanchez to two policy pressures. First, he shifted Spain from supporting a UN-backed Western Sahara referendum toward [[Morocco]]'s autonomy plan, angering [[Algeria]] and many inside Spain. Second, Spain's migrant amnesty became an EU complaint after expected legalisation demand was far exceeded.
