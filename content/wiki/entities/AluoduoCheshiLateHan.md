## Overview

阿罗多 / Aluoduo is the Cheshi Hou king in Hanji 839 whose rebellion, flight, return, and negotiated restoration make him a compact case of frontier-vassal management. He is not presented as innocent; his importance is that restoring him becomes safer for Han order than replacing him when he still has local support and can seek [[Xiongnu|北匈奴]] backing.

## Current Profile

Aluoduo first appears in conflict with the Han official responsible for the area. The disagreement turns military: he rebels, surrounds Han屯田 troops, and causes casualties. Once part of the Cheshi population defects to Han authorities, Aluoduo loses room and flees north with around one hundred cavalry.

The later turn is political rather than purely punitive. Aluoduo returns from the Northern Xiongnu side and still commands support inside the kingdom. [[YanXiangCheshiLateHan|阎祥]] decides that recognition can reduce a larger frontier risk, promises restoration if Aluoduo surrenders, and secures his return to the throne.

## Key Characteristics

- Local king whose conflict with Han authority escalates into armed rebellion.
- Causes direct military harm by attacking Han屯田 forces.
- Loses internal cohesion when local elites and residents defect to the Han side.
- Retains enough local support after flight to make simple replacement risky.
- Accepts restored kingship as a face-saving surrender path.
- Functions as a late-Han example of recognition being used to neutralize rather than reward rebellion.

## Qualifications

The current profile is source-bounded and does not reconstruct Aluoduo's earlier reign, family line, or later conduct. His restoration is treated as a security calculation, not as moral vindication or proof that rebellion carried no cost.
