# 张司祺 / Zhang Siqi

## Overview

张司祺 is the commodity researcher featured in [[e254-chaoji-eerninuo-laile-womende-richang-suoxu-zhenhui-yin-ta-zhangjia-ma-0b644f51-461c-4ddb-b707-d2a39b9a7d80]]. The episode describes her as having nearly thirteen years of industry experience and uses her trading and supply-chain perspective to analyze climate, agriculture, logistics, and prices.

## Current Profile

Her analytical method resists a one-variable forecast. She combines large-scale climate indicators with regional weather, crop calendars, inventories, trade concentration, logistics, policy, and market data, then separates an upstream futures move from the slower and less complete movement into consumer prices.

The profile is source-bounded: the supplied episode establishes her role and method but does not provide a full employer, education, publication, or career history.

## Key Characteristics

- **Conditional causal reasoning:** treats El Niño as one input whose effect depends on location, timing, inventory, substitution, policy, and transport.
- **Multi-scale evidence use:** combines climate indices, rainfall anomalies, temperature, soil moisture, reservoir levels, vegetation data, crop stages, and balance sheets.
- **Supply-chain interpretation:** traces shocks through processing, feed, logistics, hedging, branding, and retail rather than equating futures with shelf prices.
- **Risk-management orientation:** emphasizes agronomy, infrastructure, insurance, contracts, futures, and options as complementary controls.
