## Overview

若羌去胡来王唐斗 / Tangdou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-613-quanli-kuangchao-jiemi-wangmang-de-tiexue-shouwan-lgfzc3ypqizddsmd6mvaasb1dxsd|Hanji 613]] as a Ruoqiang ruler whose failed appeal for Han frontier protection, blocked entry at Yumen Pass, flight to [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], extradition, and public execution make him the second major case in the episode's coercive frontier-control sequence.

## Current Profile

Tangdou's source role is not the same as [[GougouCheshiKing|沽沟]]'s infrastructure case. He fights repeatedly with Chishui Qiang, loses, and asks Western Regions Protector Dan Qin for help. Dan Qin does not rescue him in time, and Tangdou's attempted eastward escape fails when Yumen Pass will not let him enter.

Tangdou then takes his family and more than a thousand people to Xiongnu. The chanyu accepts him and later hands him over after Han pressure. The Han court rejects leniency and executes Tangdou alongside Gougou before assembled Western Regions rulers and troops.

## Key Characteristics

- Ruoqiang ruler whose defection begins from failed protection rather than road-burden resistance.
- Frontier actor caught between local Qiang conflict, Han nonresponse, and pass-entry refusal.
- Leader of a group flight including family members and more than a thousand people.
- Refuge seeker accepted by Xiongnu but surrendered under Han diplomatic pressure.
- Publicly executed example used to warn other dependent frontier states.
