# 赵厮养卒 / Zhao Servant Envoy

赵厮养卒 / Zhao Servant Envoy is the unnamed low-status figure at the center of [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-8-yi-ming-zhaoguo-chuishibing-nixi-chengwei-waijiao-dachen-lhfwk-hjge5iuqet6astmbclhyri|Qinji 128-8]]. The episode explains 厮养卒 as a menial military camp role associated with cooking and support work rather than recognized command status.

His political importance comes from the crisis after [[YanState|燕国]] captures [[WuChen|武臣]], the rebel [[ZhaoState|赵国]] king. Zhao's formal envoys fail and are killed. The servant envoy volunteers, meets Yan's commander, and argues that killing Wu Chen would not help Yan because [[ZhangEr|张耳]] and [[ChenYu|陈馀]] could use Wu Chen's death as a righteous pretext to attack Yan.

The episode treats his argument as the source behind "养卒说燕" and "左提右挈." The wiki keeps the figure source-scoped because the source does not name him or trace a later career, but his scene is a clear case of [[LowStatusStrategicPersuasion|低位者战略说服]]: a low-status actor changes a state decision by mapping the other side's incentives more sharply than official envoys did.
