# Diogo Rau

## Overview
Diogo Rau is presented as [[EliLilly|Eli Lilly's]] chief information and digital officer, bringing experience from [[Apple]] and earlier technology work to pharmaceutical discovery, operations, and patient-facing care.

## Current Profile
Rau's distinctive role in the source is not conventional IT stewardship. He treats technology as an operating-model intervention spanning molecule discovery, secure products, clinical-trial sensing, patient data, treatment access, and continuing engagement. His Apple-derived method starts with the intended experience and works backward, but he acknowledges that Lilly has not fully reached that standard.

His proposals combine speed with long-horizon discipline. He wants appropriate treatment to become available on the same day a person seeks help and wants faster feedback after treatment begins, yet he also emphasizes that medicine development can take a decade or more and fail after years of work. That tension makes reversibility and rapid learning useful inside a field whose final safety and efficacy judgments remain slow.

## Key Characteristics
- Pharmaceutical technology executive whose remit spans discovery through patient-facing delivery.
- Advocates [[PatientCenteredHealthcareDelivery|patient-centered healthcare delivery]] designed backward from the intended experience.
- Supports patient access to and governance of personal health data.
- Uses secure packaging and sensing as possible safety and engagement infrastructure rather than as technology demonstrations alone.
- Treats treatment feedback as a promising but unproven adherence mechanism.
- Sees AI-assisted drug discovery as valuable only across long development and validation horizons.
