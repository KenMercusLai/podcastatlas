## Overview

John Sloss is the founder of [[CineticMedia|Cinetic Media]] and a veteran independent-film sales agent profiled by [[PlanetMoney|Planet Money]] at the 2026 [[SundanceFilmFestival|Sundance Film Festival]].

## Current Profile

Sloss works between filmmakers and a small set of distributors or streaming buyers. The episode presents his role as selection, positioning, relationship management, and negotiation: he helps films create credible demand signals without allowing hype to outrun audience response, then presses competing buyers on money, release plans, awards support, and filmmaker fit. His accounts of *Napoleon Dynamite*, *Little Miss Sunshine*, and *Precious* emphasize that no single sales formula works across films.

## Key Characteristics

- Treats film sales as uncertainty management rather than a simple auction.
- Calibrates publicity and expectations to the film and likely audience.
- Uses audience evidence, buyer competition, and release commitments in negotiation.
- Maintains long-term festival relationships and deal-making infrastructure.
- Treats luck as useful only when a team recognizes and acts on it.
