# Apartheid Museum Johannesburg

## Overview
The Apartheid Museum Johannesburg is the episode's strongest museum recommendation and the clearest curated entry into apartheid's institutional history and violent transition.

## Current Profile
王妈妈 presents the museum as the one Johannesburg museum she would prioritize if a traveler could visit only one. Its design matters: entry tickets divide visitors into "white" and "non-white" routes, and the exhibits guide different time budgets while moving from the origins of [[Apartheid]] through resistance, escalation, and the 1994 transition.

The museum is also treated cautiously. The episode values its historical density but recognizes that any museum makes narrative choices; 王妈妈 notes its propagandistic or didactic quality without dismissing its necessity as a traveler's historical entry point.

## Key Characteristics
- High-priority Johannesburg museum for understanding [[Apartheid]].
- Curated experience that makes racial classification visible from the entrance.
- Historical route from apartheid's origins through resistance and the 1994 political transition.
- Site where transition violence before the first democratic election is presented through difficult imagery.
- Memory institution whose narrative power is useful but not neutral.
