## Overview

赵王刘遂 / Liu Sui is the Zhao ruler whose defense of Handan forms the last armed-resistance branch in the current [[RebellionOfTheSevenStatesWesternHan|Seven States]] evidence.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-236-qiguo-zhiluan-pingding-hanjingdi-shafengle-2-lisudlhjt_yawpyv4i8chvxl37cr|Hanji 236 part 2]] says Liu Sui withdraws to Handan and withstands [[LiJiWesternHan|郦寄]] for more than seven months. After [[LuanBu|栾布]] returns from Qi and joins the siege, the imperial forces divert river water into the city, the wall collapses, and Liu Sui dies by suicide. The source makes his death the end of Zhao's resistance but does not supply a wider biography or independent account of his decision to rebel.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han king of Zhao in the Seven States crisis.
- Defender of Handan during a prolonged imperial siege.
- Opponent first of Li Ji and then of the combined Li Ji-Luan Bu force.
- Ruler whose suicide follows the hydraulic breach of the city in the episode's account.
