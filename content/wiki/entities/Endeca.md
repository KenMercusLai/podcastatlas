# Endeca

Endeca is the enterprise search and business-intelligence company where [[AmanNarang]], [[SteveFredette]], and [[JohnGraham]] worked before founding [[Toast]] in [[toast-aman-narang-how-a-long-wait-for-the-dinner-check-launched-a-2-billion-business-6eca81bc-b0e1-47e2-a87d-f3b243e76a97]]. The source says Endeca's core business was e-commerce search and that Aman worked on an internal business-intelligence effort that felt like a startup inside the company.

The episode uses Endeca as the founder-team formation environment. [[Oracle]] acquired Endeca around the end of 2011, which gave the future Toast founders enough financial room to explore ideas while also freeing them from the original company. [[StevePapa]] is also tied to this period because he encouraged the mobile business internally and later funded Toast.
