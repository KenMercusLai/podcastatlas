## Overview

莎车国 / Shache Kingdom is a Western Regions polity whose current wiki profile now begins with a Han-loyal King Yan and King Kang branch before the later contested recognition, opportunistic expansion, external control, and defeat by [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]]'s misdirection. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-699-gan-yu-liuxiu-zhengxiong-de-ta-weihe-candan-shouchang-lth-8mndudj3khf9cnsouj86c1-z|Hanji 699]] presents [[ShacheKingYanEasternHan|King Yan]] as a former Chang'an hostage who instructs descendants to serve Han, and [[ShacheKingKangEasternHan|King Kang]] as a successor who resists [[Xiongnu|Xiongnu]] pressure, protects Han personnel, and receives [[DouRongEasternHan|Dou Rong]]'s recognition. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-719-guangwudi-liuxiu-suanbusuan-zhanan-lk8eme3wwyk5qkjjfxkp0zsc77ix|Hanji 719]] then gives the King Xian layer: Xian asks [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] for restored Western Regions authority, receives a protectorate-level package, then loses that package after [[PeiZunEasternHan|裴遵]] warns against overrecognition. Hanji 722 and Hanji 723 then show Xian's pressure on smaller polities after Han's authority remains limited, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-705-4-banchao-donghan-zui-weida-de-zhanlueji-waijiaojia-luohuafhlmnbws52bya4vjoevrg7|Hanji 705-4]] strengthens the later Ban Chao campaign layer.

## Current Profile

Hanji 699 supplies the earliest current Shache layer. King Yan had lived as a hostage in Chang'an under Han and told his descendants to serve Han across generations. After Yan's death, Kang inherits, resists Xiongnu pressure, protects former Han Western Regions officials and families, and asks Hexi for central news. Dou Rong follows precedent by recognizing Kang with titles, while the episode says many Western Regions states become subordinate to Shache.

Hanji 719 supplies the earliest current layer. Shache King Xian sends tribute and asks Han to restore Western Regions authority through him. Liu Xiu initially grants a protectorate-level seal, ribbon, vehicle, flags, gold, and silk, but Pei Zun warns that elevating Shache so highly would disappoint other states and create a bad hierarchy. Liu Xiu downgrades Xian to Great General, and Pei Zun forcibly retrieves the stronger seal. Xian resents Han but continues acting as if he holds protectorate authority, and smaller states still follow his commands.

Hanji 722 supplies the next layer. In the 45 CE Western Regions notices, King Xian grows arrogant and tries to annex other states. Smaller polities fear his attacks and burdens, so they send hostage princes and tribute to Luoyang and ask Han for protection. When Liu Xiu initially sends the hostages back, they ask through Dunhuang that the hostages be allowed to stay so they can warn Shache that Han backing may arrive.

Hanji 723 supplies the next layer. After Western Regions hostage princes return home and Han does not send a protector, King Xian reads Han absence as a strategic opening. He attacks [[ShanshanKingdomLateHan|Shanshan]] and kills the [[QiuciKingdom|Qiuci]] king, which pressures Shanshan King An to seek renewed Han hostage submission and protectorate protection. The source therefore makes Shache a regional pressure actor in [[CapacityBoundFrontierCommitment|capacity-bound frontier commitment]]: its expansion exposes what happens when Han prestige is not backed by immediate capacity.

Hanji 735 supplies a later expansion and reversal layer. King Xian occupies [[KhotanKingdomLateHan|Khotan]], Dawan, and Qiuci and stations governors, but Khotanese actors kill the Shache governor Junde and raise Xiumoba. Xian's attack on Khotan is defeated, Xiumoba dies in pursuit, Guangde is installed, and the immediate settlement returns Guangde's detained father and marries Xian's daughter to Guangde.

Hanji 736 supplies the next current layer. [[KhotanKingGuangdeLateHan|Guangde of Khotan]] attacks Shache, lures and kills King Xian, and annexes the polity. Northern Xiongnu treats Khotan's expansion as unacceptable, surrounds Khotan with Western Regions forces, and installs Xian's hostage son Bujuzheng as Shache king.

The settlement does not last. Guangde later attacks again, kills Bujuzheng, and installs Bujuzheng's younger brother Qili. Hanji 758 then adds a later military layer: during Zhangdi's late reign, [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]] defeats Shache through false retreat and timing after the opposing relief force is split. Hanji 705-4 retells that 87 CE campaign with more tactical detail: Shache calls on Qiuci, the relief force moves toward the route Ban Chao pretends to use for withdrawal, and Ban Chao instead strikes Shache's exposed central camp and captures the king. Taken together, Shache is a contested control point rather than a stable independent actor.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Regions polity whose earliest current profile begins with King Yan and King Kang's Han-loyal protective branch.
- Early proxy partner that protects Han personnel and receives Dou Rong's recognition before King Xian's later contested title package.
- King Xian later seeks protectorate-level authority, loses the stronger seal, and still acts as if he has regional command.
- Aggressive pressure actor whose expansion drives smaller states to seek Han hostages and protection.
- Expansionary power over Khotan, Dawan, and Qiuci before Khotan's revolt.
- Later becomes vulnerable to neighboring Khotan's expansion, with kingship repeatedly reset through killing, hostage succession, and replacement.
- Later appears as an opponent defeated by Ban Chao's false-retreat and exposed-center attack.

## Qualifications

This page does not reconstruct Shache's full dynastic sequence or geography. Hanji 699 adds an early Yan/Kang branch, but the exact title formula, "fifty-five states" scale, and administrative meaning of Kang's recognition remain source-scoped. Hanji 719 adds the later recognition-reversal layer, but the precise administrative meaning of the initial protectorate-level package remains source-scoped. Hanji 722, Hanji 723, and Hanji 735 all name King Xian as an expansionary actor, but the page treats exact chronology, throne sequence, and campaign geography as source-bounded until more source notes develop them. Junde, Xiumoba, Dawan, Xian, Bujuzheng, Qili, Wensu, Gumo, Weitou, force counts, casualty figures, and exact coalition details remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 699 as the earliest Shache layer: King Yan's Han memory, King Kang's protection of Han personnel, anti-Xiongnu posture, and Dou Rong recognition.
- Preserved Hanji 719 as the later King Xian recognition layer: request, Pei Zun's objection, title downgrade, seal retrieval, and resulting resentment.
- Reframed Hanji 722 as the next pressure layer after Shache's protectorate-level claim fails to become stable Han-backed authority.
- Preserved Hanji 723 as the next Shache layer: attack on Shanshan and killing of the Qiuci king during Han absence.
- Preserved Hanji 735-736's Khotan-Dawan-Qiuci, Khotan-Xiongnu king-making branch, and Hanji 758/Hanji 705-4's later Ban Chao campaign retelling.
