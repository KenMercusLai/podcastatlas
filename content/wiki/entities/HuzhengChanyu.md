# 呼征 / Huzheng Chanyu

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-917-ruhe-xunsu-zhaodao-shengzhi-jiaxin-de-pojudian-lk2zt5c3onvui9a4ppbowshy66tg|Hanji 917]] introduces 呼征 / Huzheng as the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] chanyu killed by [[ZhangXiuProtectorOfXiongnu|张脩]], the Han protector of Xiongnu. The source says Zhang Xiu and Huzheng are at odds, Zhang kills him without authorization, and [[QiangquChanyu|羌渠]] is then installed as chanyu.

Huzheng's page is source-scoped and exists mainly to keep the 179 CE Southern Xiongnu succession branch distinct from the later 188 CE [[QiangquChanyu|Qiangqu]] killing and [[YufuluoChanyu|Yufuluo]] succession crisis.
