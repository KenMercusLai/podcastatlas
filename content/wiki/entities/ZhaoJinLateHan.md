## Overview

Zhao Jin is the small yellow-gate eunuch whose Taiyuan abuses make the parallel case to Zhang Fan of Wan. Hanji 871 uses him to show that eunuch-protected local harm could be punished by local officials and then converted into a court accusation against those officials.

## Current Profile

Zhao Jin's source profile is negative and concise. He is described as greedy, forceful, and a major harm to one county. [[LiuZhiTaiyuanLateHan|刘质]], the Taiyuan administrator, sends [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] to arrest him, and Zhao Jin is killed after an amnesty.

The episode makes Zhao Jin's death politically consequential because it is paired with the Zhang Fan case. Both enforcement actions occur against protected actors after an amnesty, and both become the basis for eunuch-side accusations. Zhao Jin therefore anchors the eunuch-office branch of the same enforcement-rebound mechanism.

## Key Characteristics

- Small yellow-gate eunuch connected to local abuse in Taiyuan.
- Described as greedy and unrestrained enough to become a county-level harm.
- Enforcement target of Liu Zhi and Wang Yun.
- Parallel to Zhang Fan as a protected actor whose punishment rebounds on officials.
