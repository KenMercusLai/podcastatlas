# 梁富侯 / Liangfu Hou

梁富侯 / Liangfu Hou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-186-yi-chang-yin-bajie-lingdao-laopo-yinfa-de-miemen-can-an-lqx14uzf60oi6wnnzdkottd6kx1n|Hanji 186]] as the Huainan-side adviser whose view [[YingBu|英布 / 黥布]] accepts when deciding to rebel. The excerpt does not develop his biography, so this page records only the source-scoped role in the decision sequence.

In the episode's structure, Liangfu Hou is paired against [[PingyuanjunZhuJian|平原君诸建]]. Pingyuanjun Zhu Jian opposes rebellion, while Liangfu Hou's line makes revolt the only survivable path once [[BenHe|奔鹤]] has accused Ying Bu and Han investigators have arrived in [[HuainanKingdomEarlyHan|淮南国]]. Liangfu Hou therefore belongs to the final step of [[InvestigationDrivenRebellionEscalation|调查推动式反叛升级]] rather than to the earlier private-access trigger.
