# NewLimit

NewLimit appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bryan-johnson-i-just-took-the-most-powerful-dose-of-dmt-in-the-world-heres-what-it-was-like-40626165]] as a [[BryanJohnson|Bryan Johnson]] investment connected to cellular reprogramming. The episode uses the company to discuss computational discovery around Yamanaka-factor-like interventions and the need to control dosing tightly enough to avoid cancer or other severe failures.

For the wiki, NewLimit sits between [[LifeReprogrammingVision]] and [[CellularReprogrammingControlProblem]]. It is not presented as proof that rejuvenation is solved; it is a company example inside the broader claim that longevity biology may become programmable only if control, safety, and validation improve.
