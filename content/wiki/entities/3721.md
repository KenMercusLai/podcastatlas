# 3721

## Overview
3721 is the Chinese-keyword internet access product and company in [[no-216-shi-bu-sha-yi-ren-hongyi-dapao-zhouhongyi-de-sanshinian-quanmian-zhanzhengshi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-27-1008693437]], founded by [[ZhouHongyi|周鸿祎]] after he left Founder. The episode treats it as both a genuine local access innovation and a source of lasting browser-plugin trust damage.

## Current Profile
The page's current synthesis is that 3721 sits at the start of [[ChineseInternetEntryPointCompetition]]: it recognized that English URLs were a friction point for ordinary Chinese users and turned Chinese keywords into an address, sales, and advertising product. Its downside is equally central: aggressive browser-plugin tactics, public conflicts, and adverse legal outcomes made it a byword for "rogue software" controversy and later gave 360's anti-rogue turn a charged prehistory.

## Key Characteristics
- Chinese-keyword access product designed to make web navigation easier for users who did not remember English URLs.
- Business-development-heavy company that sold and auctioned keywords to enterprises, anticipating some mechanics of search advertising.
- Missed-search case because Zhou kept positioning it as Chinese domain/address infrastructure and resisted a fuller search-engine direction.
- Controversial browser-plugin distributor associated in the episode with popups, difficult uninstall, and anti-uninstall competition against rivals.
- Acquisition target that was sold to [[Yahoo]] and became the bridge into [[YahooChina|Yahoo China]].
