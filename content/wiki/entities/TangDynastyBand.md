# 唐朝乐队 / Tang Dynasty Band

## Overview
唐朝乐队 is presented as an important Chinese rock band associated with large-scale spiritual imagery and intense public anticipation around early rock albums.

## Current Profile
The episode uses the band's first album and its reception to show how artistic ambition, resource scarcity, and listener hunger reinforced one another. Its work was part of the breakthrough preceding the later symbolic focus on the 1994 Hong Kong concert.

## Key Characteristics
- Early Chinese rock band associated with expansive spiritual or mythic imagery.
- Album-era case of listeners waiting in winter conditions to buy newly available music.
- Example of major creative achievement preceding its consolidation into a 1994 public legend.
