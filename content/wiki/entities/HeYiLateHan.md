# 何仪 / He Yi (late Han)

何仪 / He Yi (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu|Hanji 999]] as one of the Runan-Yingchuan Yellow Turban remnant leaders preparing to attach to [[YuanShu|袁术]]. [[CaoCao|曹操]] defeats this group in the episode's court-support branch.

The source does not make He Yi a major biographical subject. His importance is positional: if such remnants join Yuan Shu, the late-Han field around [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], and the Xuzhou-Yuan Shu conflict becomes more dangerous. Cao Cao's defeat of them therefore appears as a small stabilizing move before the later Xu-court sequence.
