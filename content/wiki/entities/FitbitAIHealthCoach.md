# Fitbit AI Health Coach

Fitbit AI Health Coach appears in [[tech-20260204-0204-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260204-0204-mp-tech-pod-128]] as [[Fitbit]]'s premium AI fitness assistant. [[NicoleNguyen]] says it can personalize workouts around sleep, heart-rate, energy, and schedule constraints, making it useful for people whose routines are inconsistent.

The same source also makes Fitbit AI Health Coach a concrete [[AIHallucination]] case. Nguyen encountered a workout where the coach claimed it had programmed a move that did not appear, so the product illustrates both the promise and failure mode of [[AIHealthManagement]] when generative AI is applied to consumer wellness.
