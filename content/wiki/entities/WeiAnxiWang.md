# 魏安釐王 / King Anxi of Wei

魏安釐王 / King Anxi of Wei enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-82-daqin-de-hewuqi-baiqi-laile-lo0w8mifkijhzmx7fdwcimmv7ade]] as the [[WeiState|魏国]] ruler who succeeds [[WeiZhaowang|魏昭王]] in 277 BCE, just as [[QinState|秦国]] is reorganizing conquered Chu territory and preparing renewed central-plains pressure.

The episode gives him an immediately hostile accession environment. In 276 BCE, [[BaiQi|白起]] attacks Wei and takes two cities; in 275 BCE, [[WeiRan|魏冉]] attacks twice, defeats a Han relief force, forces Wei to cede eight cities, then defeats [[MangMao|芒卯]] and extracts another concession around Wen. The host reads this as a deliberate Qin "first lesson" for a new ruler: Wei Anxi Wang wants to fight, loses, and then has to concede.

The same source also introduces his younger brother Wei Wuji as [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]], placing Wei Anxi Wang beside the [[WarringStatesFourLords|战国四公子]] branch before later episodes develop the brothers' tension. For this page, the key point is that his reign opens under humiliation, territorial loss, and the unresolved question of whether Wei can avoid becoming a subordinate in Qin's order.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-83-zhuhou-bailan-jing-chengle-jingdian-ji-zhong-ji-ljp7nbqnrctr5mw6i9u982abmx-1]] answers that unresolved question in a negative but more complicated way. Wei Anxi Wang first tries a weak [[QiState|齐国]] alignment, then joins [[ZhaoState|赵国]] to attack [[HanState|韩国]] at [[HuayangBattle|华阳之战]], only to trigger Qin's rescue of Han and suffer another disastrous defeat. When [[SuDai|苏代]] warns him that [[DuanGanzi|段干子]]'s land-for-peace proposal is [[LandForPeaceExhaustionTrap|抱薪救火式割地求和]], the king understands the logic but still cedes land, making his weakness procedural as well as military.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-89-qinwang-jing-bei-ta-dui-mabaonan-ljugedx8qxhtuzd1ja-xiwxgse19]] gives the next Wei-side complaint. In 268 BCE [[QinWangWan|武大夫绾 / 王绾]] takes Huai from Wei, and in 266 BCE Qin takes nearby Xingqiu. The host summarizes Wei Anxi Wang's dilemma as one in which refusing Qin invites attack, but submitting does not stop attack either.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-2-lutsvwvnwzsezoorhe-vu7yjthyx]] returns him to the Qin-Zhao crisis from the sidelines. At the opening of [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]], Wei ministers tell Wei Anxi Wang that Qin's attack on [[ZhaoState|赵国]] may benefit Wei, either by letting Wei follow the stronger Qin or by creating a chance to strike if Qin bogs down. [[KongBin|孔斌]] is the dissenting minister: he warns that Qin's appetite and [[BaiQi|白起]]'s command make Zhao's suffering a preview of Wei's danger, not a gift.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-2-ltlobdujy-hdipimfdupvwod2ech]] shows Wei Anxi Wang acting on both fear and caution during the [[Handan|邯郸]] siege. He sends [[JinBi|晋鄙]] with 100,000 troops to rescue Zhao, but after [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] warns that Qin will attack Wei next, he orders Jin Bi to stop at [[YeWarringStates|邺城]]. His policy therefore becomes partial aid without decisive commitment.

The same episode makes [[XinyuanYan|新垣衍]] a Wei channel into Zhao. Because the stopped Jin Bi army and Wei transit problem leave Chu's promised aid uncertain, Xin Yuan Yan enters Handan and proposes that Wei and Zhao honor Qin Zhaoxiang Wang as western emperor. Wei Anxi Wang's crisis posture now links [[CoalitionSelfPreservationFailure|coalition self-preservation]] to [[SubmissionThroughStatusElevation|status-submission appeasement]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-3-lj-cr4vg4e1qdfjs6yez85-3zuiv]] adds the critique of that posture. [[LuZhonglian|鲁仲连]] argues to Xin Yuan Yan that honoring Qin as emperor would not protect Wei's court; it would put Wei's ruler, ministers, and favored people under Qin's superior authority. Wei Anxi Wang's halted rescue therefore looks less like safety and more like a path toward managed dependence.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-5-lp6yfcxoysxgcnmg8v1uljfyptof]] returns to Wei Anxi Wang's fear as the immediate reason the narrative turns to [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]]. The episode does not add a new policy decision by the king; it uses his continued unwillingness to move against Qin to make Xinlingjun's private prestige and retainer network the next possible route for breaking the [[Handan|邯郸]] crisis.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-6-lteqe-0ykjtv01cmxv1fhx6z0vdi]] shows the consequence of that royal paralysis. Because Wei Anxi Wang will not truly order [[JinBi|晋鄙]] forward, [[HouYing|侯嬴]] tells [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] to bypass the king by using [[RuJi|如姬]] to steal the king's half of the [[TigerTallyCommandSystem|tiger tally]]. Wei Anxi Wang's bedroom therefore becomes the hidden institutional bottleneck in [[StealingTigerTalliesToRescueZhao|窃符救赵]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-2-jiemi-xinlingjun-ping-shenme-shi-si-gongzi-zhi-shou-lodqntthbil9o8ezppt66lywzd-f]] records the post-rescue compromise from Wei's side. Xinlingjun does not return to Wei because he has become a [[FuChen|负臣]] who corrected the king by taking unauthorized power. Yet Wei Anxi Wang also does not formally destroy him: the episode says he preserves Xinlingjun's title and leaves the relationship open enough for a possible future repair.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-100-1-huangdi-zhong-de-chaochang-daiji-wang-guoshi-le-lt75scavlm8qjn6ulpd-b2kwzwjf]] adds a darker downward-power case. In the same late-Warring-States environment where Wei has to lower itself before [[QinState|秦国]], Wei Anxi Wang kills [[WeyHuaijun|卫怀君]] when the [[WeyState|卫国]] ruler comes to see him, then installs [[WeyYuanjun|卫元君]]. The episode reads this as [[WeakOverWeakPredation|弱者向更弱者转嫁压迫]]: a ruler humiliated by a stronger power can still be violently dominant toward a weaker neighbor.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-101-1-zhe-wei-daomei-junwang-dengji-santian-ji-cusi-lll-bcc7basaf6-nzwqtmcb5qw6w]] gives him a different, reflective role. After [[LuZhonglian|鲁仲连]] refuses reward for the [[LiaochengWarringStates|辽城]] episode, Wei Anxi Wang asks [[ZiShun|子顺]] whether the world has true high-minded士 and complains that Lu Zhonglian seems too performative. Zi Shun's reply turns the question into [[VirtuePracticeInternalization|修养内化]]: repeated gentlemanly performance can become character.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-101-2-ruhe-kandai-xingbenshan-yu-xingbene-lmawsqkttjmitwkczhyfs7f7-mqt]] returns to Wei Anxi Wang as a negative strategic case. The episode argues that if he had kept a durable national strategy and used [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] consistently, [[WeiState|魏国]] could have grown stronger; instead, his fear and private suspicion made him oscillate between anti-Qin alignment and cooperation with [[QinState|秦国]], then call on Xinlingjun only in emergency.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-103-1-simaqian-kanzhong-de-xinlingjun-suan-shi-xiaoren-ma-lvty1pdnibvqpk0m16kkxzw4t-ck]] records that emergency recall. After [[MengAo|蒙敖]] attacks Wei and takes Gaodu and Ji city, Wei Anxi Wang sends to [[ZhaoState|赵国]] for Xinlingjun, whom he had not been able to absorb safely after [[StealingTigerTalliesToRescueZhao|窃符救赵]]. The source makes the later reunion emotionally charged: the king embraces Xinlingjun and weeps before appointing him supreme commander.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-103-2-simaqiande-idol-weihe-siyu-jiuse-lvmqg1ojmka9pqagfhgvo4albpjx]] shows that repair failing again. Qin uses [[CounterintelligenceRumorWedge|反间流言]] after losing on the battlefield, having [[JinBi|晋鄙]]'s former retainers warn that the lords know Xinlingjun rather than Wei Anxi Wang and sending congratulatory messages that imply Xinlingjun may already be acting like king. Wei Anxi Wang believes the suspicion, replaces Xinlingjun, and takes back command, completing the pattern already visible in his fear-driven use of his brother.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-106-jingren-faxian-xinlingjun-de-feizhengchang-siwang-lsj3kd2tb4tqtuvhwvsdwkh-h5el]] turns that fear into a possible succession motive. The episode says [[Shiji|《史记》]] records Wei Anxi Wang and [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] dying in the same year, with Xinlingjun dying first, and infers that the king may have eliminated his brother before his own death so [[WeiJingminWang|魏景湣王 / 魏增]] could inherit without facing a far more famous uncle. The wiki keeps this as [[XinlingjunDeathSuspicion|信陵君非正常死亡疑云]], not as established fact.
