# Denis Johnson (Bicycle)

Denis Johnson is the English carriage maker discussed in [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]] as an early commercializer of [[KarlDrais]]'s running machine. The source says he bought rights around the Drais design in Britain, opened a manufacturing operation, and even built a riding-school layer around the novelty.

His page's wiki role is to show that [[BicycleProductEvolution]] had a business-model problem from the beginning. Early bicycles needed manufacturing, instruction, road access, social permission, and pricing that ordinary riders could accept, not just a clever two-wheeled device.
