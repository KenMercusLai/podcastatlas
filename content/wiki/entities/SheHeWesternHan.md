## Overview

涉何 / She He is the Western Han envoy whose killing of a Joseon escort officer and subsequent appointment in Liaodong form the immediate escalation point before war with [[WimanJoseon|卫氏朝鲜]].

## Current Profile

The episode says She He fails to make [[UgeoWimanJoseon|卫右渠]] accept the Han demand, then kills the escort officer who accompanies him to the frontier and reports the act as a victory. [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] appoints him eastern commandant of Liaodong rather than punishing him. Joseon later attacks across the frontier and kills She He, after which Wudi orders the invasion. The sequence presents envoy violence, court ratification, and retaliation as a chain rather than an isolated border incident.

## Key Characteristics

- Han envoy sent to press Wiman Joseon to accept an imperial demand.
- Diplomatic agent who turns a failed mission into lethal frontier violence.
- Official rewarded with a Liaodong appointment after reporting the killing.
- Immediate target of Joseon's retaliatory frontier attack.
- Escalation figure whose death becomes the stated trigger for Han invasion.

## Qualifications

This is a source-bounded profile of the immediate escalation sequence. The episode does not supply the full imperial instruction, She He's legal authority, the killed officer's received name, or an independent chronology. Its summary writes 涉和; this page uses the received form 涉何.
