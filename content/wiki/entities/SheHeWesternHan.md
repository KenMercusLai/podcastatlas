## Overview

涉何 / She He is the Western Han envoy sent to challenge [[UgeoWimanJoseon|卫右渠]]'s blockade of neighboring rulers' court access. His killing of a Joseon escort officer and subsequent appointment in Liaodong form the immediate escalation point before war with [[WimanJoseon|卫氏朝鲜]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 364-2 defines the demand: neighboring small states must not be prevented from petitioning or attending the Han court. She He fails to make Ugeo accept it, then kills the escort officer who accompanies him to the frontier and reports the act as a victory. [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] appoints him eastern commandant of Liaodong rather than punishing him. Hanji 366-1 continues the same chain through Joseon's attack and She He's death, after which Wudi orders the invasion. The sequence presents court-access conflict, envoy violence, court ratification, and retaliation as connected rather than as an isolated border incident.

## Key Characteristics

- Han envoy sent to press Wiman Joseon to accept an imperial demand.
- Agent tasked with reopening neighboring rulers' direct access to the Han court.
- Diplomatic agent who turns a failed mission into lethal frontier violence.
- Official rewarded with a Liaodong appointment after reporting the killing.
- Immediate target of Joseon's retaliatory frontier attack.
- Escalation figure whose death becomes the stated trigger for Han invasion.

## Qualifications

This is a source-bounded profile of the immediate escalation sequence. The episodes do not supply the full imperial instruction, She He's legal authority, the killed officer's securely normalized identity, or an independent chronology. Their summaries write 涉和; this page uses the received form 涉何.
