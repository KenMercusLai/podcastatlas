# Counterpoint Research

## Overview
Counterpoint Research is a market-research organization cited by [[MarketplaceTech]] for smartphone pricing and replacement-cycle findings.

## Current Profile
In the available wiki evidence, Counterpoint Research functions as the episode's quantitative market source rather than as an independently reviewed dataset. Its figures support a directional account of global smartphone inflation and longer ownership, but their definitions, sample, weighting, and underlying report are not included in the supplied source.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides market estimates about global smartphone prices.
- Tracks consumer-device replacement cycles and regional market differences.
- Is cited secondhand in the supplied episode rather than through an attached primary report.

## Evidence
- Smartphone pricing: [[tech-20261029-1029-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261029-1029-mp-tech-pod-128]] attributes a 15% increase in average global phone prices to Counterpoint Research.
- Replacement behavior: [[tech-20261029-1029-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261029-1029-mp-tech-pod-128]] describes the replacement cycle as beyond 42 months and approaching 48 to 50 months.
- Regional distribution: [[tech-20261029-1029-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261029-1029-mp-tech-pod-128]] says India and Southeast Asia have experienced some of the largest percentage price increases.

## Qualifications
The supplied source does not identify the report title, publication date, methodology, geographic weights, phone-model mix, nominal-versus-quality-adjusted price measure, or whether every figure is directly from Counterpoint Research. The claims therefore remain source-attributed rather than independently verified.
