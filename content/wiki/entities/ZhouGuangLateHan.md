## Overview

周广 / Zhou Guang is a late-Han court favorite whose current wiki profile is bounded to the [[EmperorAnOfHan|Emperor An]] and Yan-backed succession crisis. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]] shows him cooperating with [[FanFengEunuchLateHan|Fan Feng]] and [[XieYunLateHan|Xie Yun]] in forged-edict private construction and anti-[[YangZhenLateHan|Yang Zhen]] slander; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-802-andi-zhisi-donghan-wangchao-quanli-da-xipai-lgvne92e0jjzjnznzfkz7nvztnbj|Hanji 802]] shows him executed when [[YanXianLateHan|Yan Xian]] later purges old An-era networks.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]] introduces Zhou Guang as part of the favored court group around Fan Feng. During An's eastern tour, the group forges imperial instructions and diverts public resources and labor into private estates. Yang Zhen's repeated memorials and discovery of the forged orders make the group vulnerable, so they move first by slandering Yang through a taishi report and claims of resentful factional motive.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-802-andi-zhisi-donghan-wangchao-quanli-da-xipai-lgvne92e0jjzjnznzfkz7nvztnbj|Hanji 802]] then shows the reversal. After Emperor An dies and the Yan side installs Beixiang Hou, Yan Xian accuses old-network actors including [[GengBaoGrandGeneralLateHan|Geng Bao]], Fan Feng, Xie Yun, Zhou Guang, and [[WangShengPalaceActorLateHan|Wang Sheng]]. Zhou Guang is executed with Fan Feng and Xie Yun, making him both a perpetrator in Yang Zhen's destruction and a later purge victim.

## Key Characteristics

- Favored Emperor An-era actor grouped with Fan Feng and Xie Yun.
- Participant in a forged-edict construction scheme that converts public resources into private benefit.
- Anti-Yang Zhen slander participant when documentary evidence threatens the group.
- Old-network purge victim executed after the Yan-backed accession.
- Source-bounded figure whose available profile comes through group action, not an independent biography.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 800 and Hanji 802. It does not settle Zhou Guang's full title, household background, exact role in drafting the forged orders, or whether he held eunuch office. The source evidence is strong enough for a canonical page because he recurs across the Yang Zhen case and the later Yan-side cleanup, but the profile remains group-bounded.
