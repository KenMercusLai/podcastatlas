## Overview

周广 / Zhou Guang is a late-Han court favorite whose current wiki profile is bounded to the [[EmperorAnOfHan|Emperor An]] and Yan-backed succession crisis. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-799-donghan-mingchen-yangzhen-ruhe-jujue-zou-houmen-lmufb7lvb-kzsvjtn6iu-zrpiptf|Hanji 799]] shows [[YangZhenLateHan|杨震]] criticizing him, [[XieYunLateHan|谢恽]], and [[FanFengEunuchLateHan|樊丰]] for partying for private benefit and soliciting offices; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]] shows the same group in forged-edict private construction and anti-Yang slander; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-802-andi-zhisi-donghan-wangchao-quanli-da-xipai-lgvne92e0jjzjnznzfkz7nvztnbj|Hanji 802]] shows him executed when [[YanXianLateHan|Yan Xian]] later purges old An-era networks.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-799-donghan-mingchen-yangzhen-ruhe-jujue-zou-houmen-lmufb7lvb-kzsvjtn6iu-zrpiptf|Hanji 799]] introduces Zhou Guang as a shizhong in Yang Zhen's memorial against luxury construction and favorite abuse. Yang says Fan Feng, Zhou Guang, Xie Yun, and others encourage each other for private benefit, and he criticizes Zhou and Xie for relying on favored palace actors, soliciting from commanderies and states, accepting bribes, and letting barred corrupt officials hold important posts.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]] escalates Zhou Guang's group role. During An's eastern tour, the group forges imperial instructions and diverts public resources and labor into private estates. Yang Zhen's repeated memorials and discovery of the forged orders make the group vulnerable, so they move first by slandering Yang through a taishi report and claims of resentful factional motive.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-802-andi-zhisi-donghan-wangchao-quanli-da-xipai-lgvne92e0jjzjnznzfkz7nvztnbj|Hanji 802]] then shows the reversal. After Emperor An dies and the Yan side installs Beixiang Hou, Yan Xian accuses old-network actors including [[GengBaoGrandGeneralLateHan|Geng Bao]], Fan Feng, Xie Yun, Zhou Guang, and [[WangShengPalaceActorLateHan|Wang Sheng]]. Zhou Guang is executed with Fan Feng and Xie Yun, making him both a perpetrator in Yang Zhen's destruction and a later purge victim.

## Key Characteristics

- Favored Emperor An-era actor grouped with Fan Feng and Xie Yun.
- Shizhong criticized by Yang Zhen for private-benefit partying, solicitation, bribery, and corrupt appointment outcomes.
- Participant in a forged-edict construction scheme that converts public resources into private benefit.
- Anti-Yang Zhen slander participant when documentary evidence threatens the group.
- Old-network purge victim executed after the Yan-backed accession.
- Source-bounded figure whose available profile comes through group action, not an independent biography.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 799, Hanji 800, and Hanji 802. It does not settle Zhou Guang's full title beyond the source's shizhong label, household background, exact role in drafting the forged orders, or whether he held eunuch office. The source evidence is strong enough for a canonical page because he recurs across Yang Zhen's first memorial, the forged-edict case, and the later Yan-side cleanup.
