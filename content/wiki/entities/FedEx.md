# FedEx

FedEx is the delivery company named in [[tech-20260723-0723-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260723-0723-mp-tech-pod-128]], where [[DylanThompson]] tried to resolve a missing $1,700 e-bike delivery. The episode says FedEx opened and closed a claim through an automated phone chatbot, then followed up by email; by the time Thompson reached a human representative, he was told the case was closed and nothing could be done.

In the wiki, this is a source-scoped customer-service case rather than a comprehensive profile of FedEx. It connects logistics operations to [[CustomerServiceSludge]] by showing how an automated claims path can move a case to closure before a human has meaningfully reviewed the consumer's situation.
