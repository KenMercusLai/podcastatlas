# FedEx

## Overview
FedEx is a logistics and delivery company that appears in the wiki through two distinct frames: consumer-support failure and Memphis-centered air-logistics hub strategy.

## Current Profile
The Marketplace Tech source uses FedEx as a customer-service sludge case. A missing e-bike claim moved through automated channels, closed before meaningful human resolution, and became evidence for how chatbot-first support can damage trust.

The aviation roundtable gives FedEx a different role. Memphis is described as FedEx's headquarters city and as a logistics node where aviation amplifies river, rail, highway, and continental route advantages. Together, the sources make FedEx useful for contrasting logistics operations with customer-facing exception handling.

## Key Characteristics
- Delivery company named in a missing-package support failure.
- Automation-sludge case where claims handling became difficult to escalate.
- Air-logistics anchor for Memphis as a multimodal freight hub.
- Company node connecting parcel delivery, air cargo, and trust in service operations.

## Evidence
- Support failure: [[tech-20260723-0723-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260723-0723-mp-tech-pod-128]] describes Dylan Thompson's missing e-bike claim, automated FedEx claim handling, and the inability to get a useful human resolution after closure.
- Customer-trust risk: [[tech-20260723-0723-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260723-0723-mp-tech-pod-128]] connects FedEx to customer-service sludge, chatbot-first friction, and service metrics that can improve while customers give up.
- Memphis logistics role: [[403-cong-bashi-nian-feixing-shi-kan-chengshi-shehui-he-women-ziji-de-gaibian-guotai-hangkong-x-shengdongjixi-53eaa77d]] names FedEx as the Memphis anchor in a multimodal air, river, rail, road, and continental logistics hub.

## Qualifications
The sources do not provide a comprehensive FedEx operations, financial, or aviation-network profile. One source is a consumer incident and the other is a city-network example, so company-wide conclusions should remain limited.
