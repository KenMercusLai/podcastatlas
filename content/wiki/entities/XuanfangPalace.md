## Overview

宣房宫 / Xuanfang Palace is the palace complex that the current source places at the repaired Huzi breach of the [[YellowRiver|黄河]] under [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] in 109 BCE. It is presented as both a flood-control installation and a political monument to successful repair.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-363-hanwudi-jiandao-shenxian-zhihou-de-chuanshuo-ll1skj3ygm2fop-pz1etkn3qexw5|Hanji 363]] says the palace was ordered after [[GuoChangWesternHan|郭昌]], Ji Ren, mass laborers, and court attendants closed the Huzi breach. The episode assigns it a practical role in reducing pressure at the former breach and a symbolic role in showing that the emperor could subdue the river and protect the affected Liang-Chu region.

The palace therefore sits between infrastructure and commemoration in the current evidence. Its meaning depends on the surrounding work—breach closure, brushwood mobilization, channel cutting, and continued maintenance—rather than on a residential-palace function alone.

## Key Characteristics

- Built at or near the repaired Huzi breach in the episode's account.
- Associated with the 109 BCE Yellow River repair under Han Wudi.
- Presented as having both hydraulic and commemorative functions.
- Material marker of mass labor and direct imperial supervision.
- Symbolically connects public protection with imperial legitimacy.

## Evidence

- Repair setting: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-363-hanwudi-jiandao-shenxian-zhihou-de-chuanshuo-ll1skj3ygm2fop-pz1etkn3qexw5|Hanji 363]] places construction after the Huzi breach is closed through large-scale mobilization.
- Hydraulic claim: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-363-hanwudi-jiandao-shenxian-zhihou-de-chuanshuo-ll1skj3ygm2fop-pz1etkn3qexw5|Hanji 363]] says the complex helped reduce the impact of future high water on the former breach.
- Political meaning: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-363-hanwudi-jiandao-shenxian-zhihou-de-chuanshuo-ll1skj3ygm2fop-pz1etkn3qexw5|Hanji 363]] interprets Xuanfang Palace as a public sign that the emperor cared for subjects and could control the Yellow River.
- Long memory: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-363-hanwudi-jiandao-shenxian-zhihou-de-chuanshuo-ll1skj3ygm2fop-pz1etkn3qexw5|Hanji 363]] associates the repair and palace with an episode-attributed period of more than eighty years without a major Yellow River disaster.

## Qualifications

The current profile rests on one compact podcast summary. It does not establish the palace's exact location, plan, construction chronology, maintenance regime, independent hydraulic effect, or survival. The claim that it materially reduced flood pressure and the reported eighty-plus-year outcome require specialist hydrological and primary-source verification; the page therefore treats them as episode-attributed rather than settled engineering conclusions.

## What Changed

- Created the canonical entity to separate Xuanfang Palace's hydraulic and political-symbolic roles from generic Han palace construction.
