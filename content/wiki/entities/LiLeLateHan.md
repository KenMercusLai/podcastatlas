# 李乐 / Li Le (late Han)

李乐 / Li Le (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-991-zhengduo-xiandi-zhizhan-shui-neng-zuizhong-qusheng-lkbsl5uzqxouzm6mlewrnuns0x6p|Hanji 991]] as one of the former [[BaiboArmy|白波军]] commanders secretly invited by [[DongChengLateHan|董承]] and [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] after [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]], [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]], and [[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]] keep pursuing [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]. The transcript renders the name as "李越"; this page uses the canonical Li Le form for the surrounding historical sequence.

In the episode, Li Le's source role is practical rather than biographical. His cavalry helps create a brief counterattack window, and he later participates in the emergency river-crossing arrangement that lets the emperor reach Dayang.
