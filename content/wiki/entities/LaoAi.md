# 嫪毐 / Lao Ai

嫪毐 / Lao Ai enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-108-1-lishi-gan-dangmian-dingzhuang-qinshihuang-diyi-ren-lg0dssyicp387foiraohkv1x5aij]] as the man [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] introduces to [[ZhaoJi|赵姬]] while disguising him as a eunuch. The source says Zhao Ji favors him, bears two sons with him, and lets his position grow until he is enfeoffed as Changxin Hou, receives [[TaiyuanWarringStates|太原]], has many retainers, and participates in deciding state affairs.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-108-2-laoai-de-hougong-mishi-lof4xuuxfkehm9igpdvihv7exqdy]] backfills that introduction. In this source, Lu Buwei does not simply offer Lao Ai openly; he first lets Lao Ai's sexualized reputation circulate so Zhao Ji can become curious, then arranges a fake eunuch entry by making him appear to have undergone corrosion/castration punishment. The episode also explains Lao Ai's unusual name, foregrounds the false-castration disguise, and treats his later "假父" boast as the moment when private intimacy openly challenges [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]'s royal authority.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-108-3-huangtaihou-he-qingfu-laoai-qingse-beihou-de-qishi-lthiqkpc0nbnlkxhcmivufvgl25b]] carries Lao Ai's branch from palace favorite into exposed succession threat. The episode emphasizes [[ZhaoJi|赵姬]]'s move to [[YongchengQin|雍城]], the secret raising of two sons, Lao Ai's title, [[TaiyuanWarringStates|太原]] fief, servants, and retainers, and the informant claim that Lao Ai had talked about making one of those sons king after Ying Zheng's death. His defeat therefore becomes the source case for [[PalaceScandalPowerConsolidation|宫廷丑闻式收权]]: Ying Zheng can destroy Lao Ai's faction while also disciplining the queen-mother and pressuring [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]].

The episode treats Lao Ai as more than a sexual-scandal figure. Once his false eunuch status is exposed near [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]'s personal-rule threshold, he steals or misuses royal seals to mobilize troops and attempts rebellion around [[YongchengQin|雍城]]. Qin suppresses the rebellion under figures including [[ChangpingJun|昌平君]], after which Ying Zheng destroys Lao Ai's kin network and punishes his retainers.
