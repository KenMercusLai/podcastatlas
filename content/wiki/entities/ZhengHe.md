# Zheng He / 郑和

## Overview

Zheng He / 郑和 was a Muslim-born Yunnan captive castrated in Ming service who rose at court and commanded seven major state voyages across the Indian Ocean.

## Current Profile

[[45-top-ten-eunuchs-glt5221212176]] presents Zheng He as the largest-scale example of coerced entry followed by imperial authority. Captured and castrated during Ming expansion into Yunnan, he rose through court service rather than through an ordinary hereditary or examination route.

He then commanded seven expeditions linking the [[MingDynasty|Ming dynasty]] to South and Southeast Asia and East Africa. The episode emphasizes diplomatic display, collection of exotic animals, anti-piracy warfare, and coercion against the king of Ceylon, so the voyages appear as both maritime connection and imperial force. His reported death during a voyage and burial at sea complete a career inseparable from the fleet.

## Key Characteristics

- Muslim-born Yunnan captive incorporated into Ming court service through castration.
- Trusted imperial servant who rose rapidly despite violent entry into the court system.
- Commander of seven large Indian Ocean voyages.
- Diplomatic and military actor associated with tribute, trade, animals, piracy suppression, and coercion.
- Maritime figure whose career demonstrates eunuch authority at imperial scale.

## Evidence

- Capture and court ascent - [[45-top-ten-eunuchs-glt5221212176]] connects Yunnan origin, Muslim background, capture, castration, and rapid rise.
- Voyage command - [[45-top-ten-eunuchs-glt5221212176]] credits Zheng He with seven expeditions reaching Africa and returning with animals including a giraffe.
- Coercive capacity - [[45-top-ten-eunuchs-glt5221212176]] includes fighting pirates and forcing the king of Ceylon to submit.
- Death tradition - [[45-top-ten-eunuchs-glt5221212176]] reports death on a voyage and burial at sea.

## Qualifications

The episode compresses the voyages and does not establish fleet sizes, route detail, commercial effects, diplomatic categories, exact chronology, or the circumstances of Zheng He's death. “Exploration” should not erase known Indian Ocean networks or reduce the expeditions to peaceful exchange.

## What Changed

- Created a profile joining coerced court entry to maritime, diplomatic, and military command.
