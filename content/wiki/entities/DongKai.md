# Dong Kai / 董凯

Dong Kai / 董凯 is introduced in [[zhongting-zhuque-sanhao-fuzongshi-huojian-huishou-zhiyou-cheng-yu-bai-meiyou-zhongjiantai-s9e41-d9e11e3b-7c7e-4beb-b19f-6f90fff38714]] as [[LandSpace|蓝箭航天]]'s [[Zhuque3|朱雀三号]] overall deputy chief designer, responsible for the rocket's overall design work. The episode uses him as an engineering source rather than a public biography source.

His contribution to the wiki is a detailed reusable-rocket operating view. Dong treats recovery as a binary engineering result, but he also argues that failed flight attempts can produce data on re-entry ignition, aerodynamic control, thermal environment, and terminal landing that directly improve the next vehicle.

He also explains why Zhuque-3 was designed for reuse from the beginning: liquid oxygen/methane, stainless-steel tanks, engine choice, recovery range, manufacturing cadence, and safety regulation are framed as parts of the same route toward large payload, high launch frequency, and lower cost.
