## Overview

沁水公主 / Qinshui Princess enters the wiki through the estate case that exposes [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] before [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Emperor Zhang]]. Hanji 752 treats her as a protected royal woman who is still unable to resist Dou's palace-backed pressure, making her case a compact measure of how far outer-relative power had already spread.

## Current Profile

The source says Dou Xian relies on palace prestige and forces the purchase of Qinshui Princess's estate at a low price. The princess fears him and does not contest the seizure. Her importance is therefore structural: if a princess can be pressured into surrendering property, ordinary households would be even more exposed.

The case becomes visible when Zhangdi passes the estate and asks Dou Xian about it. Dou tries to prevent the truth from being spoken, and Zhangdi later rebukes him by comparing the attempted suppression of reality to Zhao Gao's "pointing deer as horse" episode. Zhangdi makes Dou return the estate but does not legally punish him.

## Key Characteristics

- Royal woman whose estate seizure exposes Dou Xian's overreach.
- Property holder pressured despite high status and imperial kinship.
- Evidence that palace-backed outer-relative power can reach beyond ordinary subjects into the royal family.
- Trigger for Zhangdi's strongest direct rebuke of Dou Xian in Hanji 752.
- Source-bounded figure whose wider biography is not developed here.
