## Overview

况天佑 is a vampire and investigator who helps protect the residents during the 佳佳大厦 arc of [[MyDateWithAVampire|《我和僵尸有个约会》]].

## Current Profile

Kuang Tianyou wants distance from supernatural conflict, exposure, and renewed romantic entanglement, yet repeatedly acts when others face immediate danger. He investigates the matching marks on the victims, confronts [[PingMaMyDateWithAVampire|平妈]], rescues 王珍珍, and joins [[MaXiaoling|马小玲]]'s return-night operation despite the risk that his lack of human vitality will reveal his own identity.

That condition becomes both limitation and resource. He cannot light the protective candle, but can use the soul crystal and vampire form when ordinary participants fail. The episode reads his involvement as a conduct-based characterization: attempted withdrawal does not survive contact with preventable harm.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines investigative attention with supernatural ability.
- Tries to avoid exposure and intimacy without becoming indifferent.
- Notices A Ping's concealment as a moral and factual clue.
- Has limitations within human-centered ritual rules.
- Accepts personal risk when others need immediate protection.
