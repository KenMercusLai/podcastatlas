## Overview

况天佑 is the identity used by 况国华, a resistance fighter transformed into a vampire in 1938 who later works as a police investigator in [[MyDateWithAVampire|《我和僵尸有个约会》]].

## Current Profile

Kuang's immortality combines unusual ability with repeated social loss. After Jiangchen bites him during the 红溪村 conflict, he survives unchanged into 1998, lives with [[FushengMyDateWithAVampire|复生]] under a father-son cover, and relies on blood-bank supplies or substitutes rather than killing. He uses vampire capacity in police work, yet unchanged appearance forces new identities and prevents a stable place in ordinary generational time.

His reunion with the dying 阿秀 makes the burden ethically difficult. Kuang refuses her request to transform her because he does not want her to inherit endless feeding, concealment, and bereavement. The episode treats that refusal as both compassionate and contestable: his experience gives him knowledge she lacks, but [[ImmortalityTransformationConsent|it does not automatically give him the right to decide for her]].

In the Japan and 佳佳大厦 arcs, attempted withdrawal repeatedly gives way to responsibility. He investigates the 初春 case and Yamamoto connection, notices matching marks on later victims, confronts [[PingMaMyDateWithAVampire|平妈]], rescues 王珍珍, and joins [[MaXiaoling|马小玲]]'s operation even when human-centered ritual rules expose his own condition. His defining pattern is not a wish for danger but an inability to ignore preventable harm.

## Key Characteristics

- Carries a 1938 resistance identity into a concealed 1998 police life.
- Avoids killing and treats vampire survival as an ethical constraint rather than entitlement.
- Experiences unchanged appearance, repeated identities, and bereavement as costs of immortality.
- Combines investigative attention with supernatural ability.
- Tries to avoid exposure and intimacy without becoming indifferent.
- Accepts personal risk when others need immediate protection.
- Protects 阿秀 from what he sees as a curse while raising an unresolved autonomy problem.

## Qualifications

This profile covers two consecutive arcs through podcast retellings rather than the character's complete continuity. Jiangchen's motive, vampire ranks, feeding effects, appearance control, romance, and later development remain incomplete or source-scoped. The judgment that Kuang should have left the final decision to 阿秀 is an ethical interpretation, not a settled rule within the story.

## What Changed

- Added Kuang's identity as 况国华, his 1938 transformation, and his nonlethal life with Fusheng.
- Reframed immortality as temporal displacement, repeated concealment, and bereavement as well as supernatural capacity.
- Added the unresolved conflict between protecting 阿秀 and respecting her stated wish.
- Connected the Japan investigation directly to his later pattern of reluctant but decisive intervention.
