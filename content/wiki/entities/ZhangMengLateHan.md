# 张猛 / Zhang Meng (late Han)

## Overview
张猛 / Zhang Meng is the late-Han Wuwei actor who kills inspector [[HandanShangLateHan|邯郸商]], forbids public mourning, and then spares [[PangYuLateHan|庞淯]] when Pang Yu's open loyalty makes further violence reputationally costly.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1076 presents Zhang Meng through a sharp reversal. He first uses killing and a mourning ban to control the aftermath of Handan Shang's death. When Pang Yu nevertheless resigns, mourns, and comes armed for revenge, Zhang Meng refuses his soldiers' demand to seize him, permits the funeral, and argues that killing a loyal man would make honorable people cease trusting him.

## Key Characteristics
- Kills Handan Shang in the episode's opening Wuwei incident.
- Tries to suppress the victim's public mourning through a death threat.
- Recognizes Pang Yu's open loyalty as a constraint on further punishment.
- Uses clemency to limit the reputational damage caused by his earlier violence.
- Remains distinct from [[ZhangMengWesternHan|张猛 / 张萌]], the Western Han official.
