# Douai and Reims Seminaries

The Douai and Reims seminaries appear in [[692-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-massacre-in-paris-part-2-glt4665799025]] as [[WilliamAllen]]'s training infrastructure for English Catholic priests. The source says Allen founded seminaries at Douai and Reims to prepare priests for missions back into [[England]], and that by 1579 about 100 priests were working undercover there.

For the wiki, the seminaries anchor [[SeminaryPriestMission]]. They make the Catholic mission durable: training, exile networks, papal backing, and undercover return routes let religious maintenance continue even as [[FrancisWalsingham]] and [[WilliamCecil|William Cecil / Lord Burleigh]] treat the same network as a possible foreign-backed security threat.
