# Khurshid Ahmed

Khurshid Ahmed appears in [[settler-bet-a-west-bank-flashpoint-6a842bb539e73ea8f3c13a2d]] as a 71-year-old Muslim social activist in [[Uttarakhand]] who objects that the state's legal changes contradict Quranic dictates. The source uses his case to make [[UniformCivilCodeIndia]] concrete at the level of religious duty and ordinary family law.

Ahmed's role is to show why the civil-code debate is not only a constitutional or party-politics argument. For [[IndianMuslims]], the same statute can be presented by the state as equal citizenship and experienced by some believers as coercive interference with [[MuslimPersonalLawIndia]].
