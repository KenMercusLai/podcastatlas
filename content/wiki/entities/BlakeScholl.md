# Blake Scholl

## Overview
Blake Scholl is the founder and CEO of [[BoomSupersonic]], a commercial supersonic-aircraft company developing [[OvertureSupersonicAirliner|Overture]], the [[XB1SupersonicDemonstrator|XB-1]] demonstrator, and an internally controlled engine path.

## Current Profile
Across the two bounded sources, Scholl is an outsider hard-tech founder who moved from software and [[BarcodeHero|mobile commerce]] into aviation through personal motivation, self-education, market modeling, [[RecursiveExpertRecruiting]], and staged technical proof. The newer interview shifts his profile from founder-story and crisis response toward industrial operator: he presents XB-1's supersonic flight, integrated turbine manufacturing, overland regulatory advocacy, and a proposed data-center generator as mutually reinforcing parts of Boom's strategy.

## Key Characteristics
- He chose faster passenger travel as a mission he considered worth the durable pain of founding a company, despite lacking a conventional aerospace résumé.
- He used textbooks, market models, expert chains, [[YCombinator]], customer intent, and XB-1 to replace assumed credentials with successive evidence.
- He interprets the [[RollsRoyce|Rolls-Royce]] break as a strategic error corrected through [[CrisisForcedVerticalIntegration|engine ownership]] and in-house manufacturing.
- He combines product advocacy with regulatory entrepreneurship, using visible flight proof and [[BoomlessCruise]] to argue for performance-based overland rules.
- He now frames [[GroundPowerEngineValidationFlywheel|ground power]] as an adjacent market that could finance aviation and generate engine-reliability evidence.

## Qualifications
Both pages are interviews centered on Scholl's own account. They establish his reasoning and company narrative more strongly than independent technical feasibility, certification timing, generator demand, environmental performance, or commercial outcomes. His roughly 2030 passenger target is explicitly uncertain, and claimed bipartisan progress does not by itself establish a complete durable regulatory settlement.
