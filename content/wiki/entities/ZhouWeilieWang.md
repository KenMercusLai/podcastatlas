# 周威烈王 / King Weilie of Zhou

周威烈王 / King Weilie of Zhou appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-2-lmfzo4sl9nd5kz3mrxdl1dl9efei]] as the [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]] ruler whose death marks a transition to [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]]. The source dates the notice to 402 BCE, described as King Weilie's twenty-fourth year.
