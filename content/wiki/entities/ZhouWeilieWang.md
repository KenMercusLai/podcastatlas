# 周威烈王 / King Weilie of Zhou

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-04-yurang-wei-zhiyao-fuchou-ln34tydha6iqwlrgmw7iugqfgfik]] adds King Weilie's most important role in the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] opening: he formally recognizes [[WeiWenhou|魏斯 / 魏文侯]], [[ZhaoLiehou|赵籍 / 赵烈侯]], and [[HanJinghou|韩虔 / 韩景侯]] as vassals. The episode treats this as [[ThreeJinVassalRecognition|三晋受封]], where Zhou title-making catches up to power already held by the three Jin families.

周威烈王 / King Weilie of Zhou appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-2-lmfzo4sl9nd5kz3mrxdl1dl9efei]] as the [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]] ruler whose death marks a transition to [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]]. The source dates the notice to 402 BCE, described as King Weilie's twenty-fourth year.
