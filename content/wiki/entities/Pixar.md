# Pixar

Pixar is the computer-animation company that moves from technical partner to creative savior in [[disney-the-renaissance-and-the-empire-1]]. The source traces Pixar from [[GeorgeLucas]]'s Lucasfilm computer graphics group through [[SteveJobs]]'s purchase, [[JohnLasseter]]'s animation leadership, and [[EdCatmull]]'s technical and organizational role.

For Disney, Pixar first matters through CAPS and computer-assisted production, then through Toy Story and the first Disney-Pixar distribution deals. The episode argues that [[BobIger]]'s acquisition of Pixar repaired [[TheWaltDisneyCompany]]'s creative core because Pixar brought not only hit IP but a story-development and leadership system that could revive Disney animation.
