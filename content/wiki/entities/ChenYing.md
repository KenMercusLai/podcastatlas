# 陈婴 / Chen Ying

陈婴 / Chen Ying enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-1-guailaotou-yiqiao-san-shua-zhangliang-chuanshou-shenshu-lqwjo13zzfgtndpajzpuvrb2h4l2|Qinji 130-1]]. The supplied transcript writes the name as 陈英; this page uses the historical 陈婴 form while preserving the source wording where needed.

In the episode, Chen Ying has occupied Dongyang and is asked by [[XiangLiang|项梁]] to join forces. The decisive scene belongs to Chen Ying's mother. She says that after marrying into the family she has never heard of prominent ancestors, so sudden fame is inauspicious; the better path is to belong to a stronger power, gaining reward if the cause succeeds and avoiding being named as the chief culprit if it fails.

Chen Ying follows that advice and does not dare become king. The wiki treats the episode's use of him as a case of [[ClaimRestraintThroughAttachment|有所归属式名位节制]]: in a fractured anti-Qin field, refusing the highest title can be a practical survival strategy rather than weakness.
