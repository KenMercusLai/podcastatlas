# PyTorch Foundation

PyTorch Foundation appears in [[148-dui-you-kaichao-3-xiaoshi-fangtan-kaiyuan-infra-he-moxing-co-design-ruguo-vllm-shibai-women-hui-houhui-yibeizi-lg-fhgpmq4r-8l-5-yrimxgkims]] as the foundation home to which [[VLLM|vLLM]] was donated. The source distinguishes this governance role from day-to-day technical decision-making: the foundation protects the project name and open-community ownership, while maintainers and [[Infract]] continue much of the technical and operational work.

The foundation matters in the wiki because it turns vLLM into an example of [[OpenSourceAIInfrastructure]] whose trust depends on legal and governance placement as much as code quality. The episode says the donation helps ensure that vLLM will not simply become a closed company asset later.
