## Overview

*Steamboat Willie* is the animated short presented as [[MickeyMouse]]'s mass-audience breakthrough through the tight integration of animation and synchronized sound.

## Current Profile

[[614-walt-disney-the-great-american-storyteller-glt5465018421]] calls the film the "Jazz Singer of animation." Its importance in this account is not that it single-handedly invented sound animation, but that sound, rhythm, character, exhibition, and brand recognition combined into a compelling public event.

## Key Characteristics

- Breakout vehicle for Mickey Mouse.
- Landmark in the mass uptake of synchronized-sound animation.
- Example of technical form strengthening character and audience appeal.
- Early node in Disney's later brand and merchandising system.

## Evidence

### Sound and mass breakthrough

- [[614-walt-disney-the-great-american-storyteller-glt5465018421]] identifies synchronized sound as the mechanism that brought Mickey to a mass audience and treats the film as an animation-industry turning point.

## Qualifications

The episode's "Jazz Singer" comparison signals historical importance, not a precise claim of sole invention. Priority, surviving technical precedents, exhibition history, production credit, and contemporary reception require film-history evidence beyond this survey.
