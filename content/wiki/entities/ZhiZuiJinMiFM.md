# 纸醉金迷FM / Zhi Zui Jin Mi FM

## Overview

纸醉金迷FM is the podcast behind [[121-quhua-qizhongwuqi-zhi-kongqueling-gulong-xiegei-chengnianrende-xinxin-yuyan-lixp1oc4wx0wiksvzo4zehqj45b4]], an episode devoted to [[GuLong|古龙]]'s [[PeacockFeatherGuLong|《孔雀翎》]].

## Current Profile

In the bounded source, the show operates as an informal group reading rather than a formal literary lecture. The hosts reconstruct the full plot, compare reactions, test motivations, recall screen adaptations, and repeatedly distinguish a compelling theme from the credibility of the devices used to reach it. Tangents and jokes make the discussion loose, but disagreement is itself part of the method.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses detailed plot retelling so listeners can follow the interpretation without recently reading the book.
- Treats emotional response and causal scrutiny as compatible forms of literary criticism.
- Allows hosts to disagree about characterization, especially the portrayal of 双双.
- Separates textual facts, strong implications, and reader speculation.
- Mixes wuxia memory, adaptation commentary, humor, and close discussion of character motives.
