# 纸醉金迷FM / Zhi Zui Jin Mi FM

## Overview

纸醉金迷FM is a conversational literary podcast represented here by episodes on [[GuLong|古龙]]'s [[LongevitySwordGuLong|《长生剑》]], [[PeacockFeatherGuLong|《孔雀翎》]], and [[TianyaMingyueDaoGuLong|《天涯·明月·刀》]], plus the opening installment of a character-centered [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]] series focused on [[WangXifeng|王熙凤]].

## Current Profile

Across the bounded sources, the show operates as an informal group reading rather than a formal lecture. The hosts reconstruct plots and character arcs, compare reactions, test motives, and distinguish textual fact, strong implication, and speculation. In the 《长生剑》 discussion, one host's first-listener position adds live prediction to this method: proposed explanations become visible and revisable as each new reversal arrives. Disagreement remains central—they can retain the force of an allegory while challenging its plot mechanics, or agree that Wang Xifeng is complex while differing over literacy, sincerity, culpability, and ending.

The method is most persuasive when sympathy and judgment stay together. The show resists flattening 双双 into a sentimental device or Wang Xifeng into either heroine or villain, yet it continues to ask who is harmed and whether the discussed text supports the proposed causal chain. Its Gu Long discussions distinguish explicit authorial morals from operative plot mechanisms; the 《天涯·明月·刀》 episode also makes changing age and rereading context part of the evidence, asking why formerly powerful stylization can later feel mannered. Humor and tangents keep the tone accessible, though they can interrupt sustained argument and make the discussion less systematic.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses detailed retelling so listeners can follow interpretation without a recent reading of the work.
- Treats emotional response and causal scrutiny as compatible forms of criticism.
- Preserves multi-host disagreement instead of manufacturing one authoritative interpretation.
- Uses first-listener prediction to expose how suspense, clues, and reversal expectations operate in real time.
- Separates textual fact, strong implication, and reader speculation.
- Reads characters through mixed motives, relationships, institutions, and moral consequences.
- Mixes literary memory, reception commentary, humor, and rereading across life stages without treating one response as definitive.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on four episodes and may not represent every format produced by the show. Conversational repetition, humor, tangents, and personal reading experience sometimes substitute for systematic textual demonstration. Claims about character identity, specific disputed acts, combat ranking, or lost portions of 《红楼梦》 therefore remain source-scoped rather than settled conclusions.

## What Changed

- Added adult rereading and changing aesthetic response as a distinct part of the show's method.
- Extended its Gu Long criticism from compact 《七种武器》 stories to poetic novel form.
- Qualified the wider editorial profile because only four episodes are currently bounded.
