# 纸醉金迷FM / Zhi Zui Jin Mi FM

## Overview

纸醉金迷FM is a conversational literary podcast represented here by an episode on [[GuLong|古龙]]'s [[PeacockFeatherGuLong|《孔雀翎》]] and the opening installment of a character-centered [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]] series focused on [[WangXifeng|王熙凤]].

## Current Profile

Across the bounded sources, the show operates as an informal group reading rather than a formal lecture. The hosts reconstruct plots and character arcs, compare reactions, test motives, and distinguish textual fact, strong implication, and speculation. Disagreement is part of the method: they can retain the force of a confidence allegory while challenging its plot mechanics, or agree that Wang Xifeng is complex while differing over literacy, sincerity, culpability, and ending.

The method is most persuasive when sympathy and judgment stay together. The show resists flattening 双双 into a sentimental device or Wang Xifeng into either heroine or villain, yet it continues to ask who is harmed and whether the discussed text supports the proposed causal chain. Humor and tangents keep the tone accessible, though they also limit how systematic any single argument becomes.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses detailed retelling so listeners can follow interpretation without a recent reading of the work.
- Treats emotional response and causal scrutiny as compatible forms of criticism.
- Preserves multi-host disagreement instead of manufacturing one authoritative interpretation.
- Separates textual fact, strong implication, and reader speculation.
- Reads characters through mixed motives, relationships, institutions, and moral consequences.
- Mixes literary memory, adaptation or reception commentary, humor, and close discussion of motive.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on two episodes and may not represent every format produced by the show. Conversational repetition, humor, and personal reading experience sometimes substitute for systematic textual demonstration. Claims about specific disputed acts or lost portions of 《红楼梦》 therefore remain source-scoped rather than settled conclusions.
