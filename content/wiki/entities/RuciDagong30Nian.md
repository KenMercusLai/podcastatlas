# 《如此打工30年》 / Working Like This for 30 Years

《如此打工30年》 is the book and archive project discussed in [[ruci-dagong-30-nian-liushuixian-shang-de-miankong-yu-shijian-lodsuktwtcfxnhmzwum5iesbzbme]]. The source says the project was originally called "打工印记" before publication, and that its materials include [[ZhanTeacher|占老师]]'s photos, QQ posts, letters to his wife, work surveys, community observation, and personal experience.

The project's importance is structural rather than only autobiographical. It treats the fragmented traces of [[Dongguan|东莞]] migrant-worker life as a [[WorkerPhotographicArchive|worker photographic archive]], connecting factory shifts, dorms, canteens, transport, wages, documents, aging, consumption, and small entrepreneurship into one long record of [[PearlRiverDeltaManufacturingTransformation|珠三角制造业变迁]].

## Key Claims
- The book's "fragmented" quality is part of its evidence value because worker life itself is distributed across documents, posts, routes, factories, and relationships.
- The archive is close to self-ethnography: the recorder belongs to the worker world he records.
- The project turns ordinary labor memory into a source for [[FieldworkAsKnowledgeMethod]] and [[PersonalArchiveAsHistory]].
