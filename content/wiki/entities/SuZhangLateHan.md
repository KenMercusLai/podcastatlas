## Overview

苏章 / Su Zhang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-823-bingbuxueren-shou-zeikou-ta-ruhe-zuodao-de-luai1f0nhb-ddajli99dw4bxj9m2|Hanji 823]] as the Jizhou inspector who refuses to let old friendship override public law when investigating a corrupt Qinghe administrator. His profile is a late-Han local-enforcement case where legal impartiality wins a strong reputation but also creates political danger after he attacks powerful local elites.

## Current Profile

Hanji 823 presents Su Zhang after Zhang Gang and Ren Jun as another way local office can restore order. When he is Jizhou inspector, Qinghe administrator is an old friend. Su Zhang first shares private hospitality with him, then makes clear that the next day's investigation belongs to public law, not personal favor.

The result is hard enforcement against a connected person. Su Zhang prosecutes the Qinghe administrator, and the episode says the province becomes awed by his conduct. The same source then qualifies the result: Su Zhang later attacks powerful elites too sharply, is dismissed, and is not restored to office despite the people's longing for him.

## Key Characteristics

- Jizhou inspector whose profile centers on public-law impartiality.
- Separates private friendship and official judgment rather than pretending relationships do not exist.
- Prosecutes a corrupt old friend after warning that next-day investigation belongs to the office, not the banquet.
- Creates province-wide awe through visible enforcement against a personally connected administrator.
- Politically exposed official whose campaign against powerful elites leads to dismissal and non-reuse.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 823. It does not reconstruct Su Zhang's full career, the Qinghe administrator's name, the exact charges, the later factional mechanics behind his dismissal, or the meaning of the people's later longing beyond the source's statement. The transcriptal 济州刺史 is normalized to 冀州刺史 from the received Tongjian passage.
