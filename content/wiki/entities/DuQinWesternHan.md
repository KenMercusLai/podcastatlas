## Overview

杜钦 / Du Qin enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-505-xihan-de-huicheng-yaoyan-da-le-shui-de-lian-ljuwccxx_aexasafkpbl-y2rvos5|Hanji 505]] as [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]]'s adviser who answers [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s eclipse-earthquake inquiry by blaming harem favoritism, jealousy, and succession danger rather than Wang-family outer-relative power.

## Current Profile

The source gives Du Qin a narrow omen-politics role. He is not profiled independently; he appears beside [[GuYongWesternHan|谷永]] as an interpreter whose explanation helps move disaster pressure away from Wang Feng's power field. In the episode's reading, many officials see Wang-family dominance as the likely political meaning of the disasters, while Du Qin and Gu Yong make the explanation about palace women and heir risk.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han adviser in Wang Feng's orbit.
- Uses disaster-omen interpretation to discuss harem favoritism and succession risk.
- Helps illustrate how omen language can redirect pressure from outer relatives to consorts.
