## Overview

杜钦 / Du Qin enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-505-xihan-de-huicheng-yaoyan-da-le-shui-de-lian-ljuwccxx_aexasafkpbl-y2rvos5|Hanji 505]] as [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]]'s adviser who answers [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s eclipse-earthquake inquiry by blaming harem favoritism, jealousy, and succession danger rather than Wang-family outer-relative power. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-510-hanchengdi-de-zhedao-shengzhi-weihe-dachen-gan-yangfengyinwei-ljxebrollq4yydnmg8otocckmth4|Hanji 510]] adds a second role: Du Qin and [[GuYongWesternHan|谷永]] advise Chengdi not to accept [[YiYanMoyan|一言莫衍]]'s Xiongnu defection, because the apparent gain could damage the submitted chanyu relationship or be a test.

## Current Profile

The sources give Du Qin two narrow advisory roles rather than a full biography. In Hanji 505, he appears beside [[GuYongWesternHan|谷永]] as an omen interpreter whose explanation helps move disaster pressure away from Wang Feng's power field and toward palace women and heir risk. In Hanji 510, the same pairing appears in frontier diplomacy: Du Qin and Gu Yong treat a Xiongnu minister's defection request as a credibility risk because Han now benefits from preserving the chanyu's submissive trust.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han adviser in Wang Feng's orbit.
- Uses disaster-omen interpretation to discuss harem favoritism and succession risk.
- Helps illustrate how omen language can redirect pressure from outer relatives to consorts.
- Helps argue for diplomatic restraint when accepting a defector could damage a larger frontier relationship.
