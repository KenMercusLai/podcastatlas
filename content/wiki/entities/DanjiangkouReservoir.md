# Danjiangkou Reservoir / 丹江口水库

Danjiangkou Reservoir / 丹江口水库 appears in [[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-21-shiyan-yizuo-yin-qiche-erqi-you-bugan-bei-qiche-paoxia-de-chengshi-1001770066]] as a water-resource anchor for [[Shiyan]]. The source identifies it as the water-source context for the [[SouthToNorthWaterTransferProject|South-to-North Water Transfer]] and says bottled-water producers such as Nongfu Spring and CR C'estbon have factories around the water source.

The episode uses Danjiangkou to show that Shiyan's diversification story is not only industrial. Water quality, hydropower, possible compute siting, and resource branding sit beside [[CommercialVehicleIndustrialBase|commercial vehicles]], batteries, [[WudangMountain|Wudang Mountain]], Chinese medicine, and turquoise.
