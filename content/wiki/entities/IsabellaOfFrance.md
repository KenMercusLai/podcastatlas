# Isabella of France

## Overview

Isabella of France appears as [[PhilipIVOfFrance|Philip IV]]'s daughter, [[EdwardIIOfEngland|Edward II]]'s queen, [[EdwardIIIOfEngland|Edward III]]'s mother, and the dynastic conduit through which Edward III later claims the French throne.

## Current Profile

The episode gives Isabella two connected roles. Politically, her marriage links the English royal house to the [[CapetianDynasty|Capetians]], making Edward III's later claim to [[France]] possible. Operationally, her 1320s mission to Paris becomes the turning point in Edward II's fall when she becomes involved with [[RogerMortimer|Roger Mortimer]] and keeps young Edward abroad.

The source treats Isabella and Mortimer's invasion as the immediate domestic background for Edward III's later self-assertion. Their regime rules through the young king until he overthrows Mortimer in 1330.

## Key Characteristics

- Daughter of Philip IV and wife of Edward II.
- Mother of Edward III and conduit for his French claim.
- Negotiator sent to Paris during the Aquitaine crisis.
- Political actor allied with Roger Mortimer against Edward II.
- Participant in the invasion and deposition that precede Edward III's direct rule.
- Regency-era power whose control Edward III later escapes.
