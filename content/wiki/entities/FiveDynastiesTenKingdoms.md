# 五代十国 / Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms

Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms / 五代十国 is the historical period and category explained in [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]]. The source treats it as the key middle layer between late Tang collapse and [[NorthernSong|北宋]] consolidation, not as a disposable interval of chaos.

The episode's Five Dynasties are the central-plain sequence of [[LaterLiang|后梁]], [[LaterTang|后唐]], [[LaterJin|后晋]], [[LaterHan|后汉]], and [[LaterZhou|后周]]. The "Ten Kingdoms" side is more historically constructed: [[FiveDynastiesTenKingdomsConceptFormation]] explains how later writing and Song political vision turned multiple local regimes into a canonical category.
