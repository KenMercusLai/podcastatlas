# 李小杰 / Li Xiaojie

[[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw]] adds Li Xiaojie's [[FactorySecondGeneration|厂二代]] reporting branch. Her interview with [[ShenZhenwen|沈真文]] at [[Fadior|法迪奥]] grounds the episode's account of second-generation successors entering through after-sales, chairman-assistant work, marketing, exhibitions, live-streaming, and dealer relations rather than only through formal ownership.

Li Xiaojie is a [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] reporter and [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] speaker in [[ep241-xiaoqi-hezuo-shi-xinyidai-de-tiefanwan-ma-lnmpsbvoqbexeardxyx88hywufig]]. Her reporting segment focuses on [[ElderCareWorkforce|养老照护人才培养]], especially why elder-care students need real contact with older people instead of only classroom theory, mannequins, or peer simulation.

Her account makes [[SchoolEnterpriseCooperation|校企合作]] concrete in a care setting rather than only a factory setting. She compares [[MinzhengVocationalUniversity|民政职业大学]], [[TaikangHome|太康之家]], and a Beijing public-private elder-care institution to show how industry demand, student reluctance, stigma, wages, and hands-on practice shape whether trained students stay in elder care.

[[ep244-jizhe-yanzhong-de-hao-gongzuo-shenme-yang-lk8iz9cllzx2jou5-sw9yt2ogpyb]] adds her reporter-self-understanding branch. 李小杰 describes how early confidence in journalistic authority weakened as public institutions, interview subjects, and online audiences increasingly questioned reporters' motives; she later moved toward longer-cycle, less confrontational reporting while still valuing interview travel and simple working relations.

[[ep266-dang-ai-zhonggou-daxue-women-gai-ruhe-dingyi-hao-zhuanye-llbzcgsefktcvkq-f37hoan79dd]] adds her basic-science and [[AIForScience|AI for Science]] education branch. 李小杰 explains why chemistry, physics, mathematics, computation, and theory may become more important when AI enters scientific discovery, and why students need [[AIForScienceTalent|AI for Science talent]] built on foundations rather than only a shallow AI course.
