## Overview

赵胡 / Zhao Hu is presented as the second ruler of [[Nanyue|南越]] and the dynastic bridge between founder [[ZhaoTuo|赵佗]] and [[ZhaoYingqiNanyue|赵婴齐]]. His current evidence joins internal imperial-style symbolism to external dependence: one source uses his reported “Nanyue Wendi” seal to show retained autonomy, while another says he asks Han for help when Dongyue attacks.

## Current Profile

Hanji 349-2 gives Zhao Hu a narrow but politically important role. He preserves Zhao Tuo's “Nanyue Wudi” seal and reportedly commissions a “Nanyue Wendi” seal for himself. This makes royal symbolism cumulative across generations rather than a one-time reaction by the founder.

Hanji 269-1 adds an interstate-security layer. When Dongyue attacks, Zhao Hu seeks Han assistance rather than resolving the conflict alone. Han responds with forces under Wang Hui and Han Anguo; the episode says Dongyue people kill their own ruler and submit before the campaign develops into a full account of invasion.

Together the sources show that autonomy and dependence can coexist. Zhao Hu reportedly retains an internal imperial seal while using his recognized relationship with Han to request military support. The new source does not supply his wider reign, the precise diplomatic terms, or his role after the request.

## Key Characteristics

- Second Nanyue ruler in the succession presented by the episode.
- Preserver of Zhao Tuo's imperial seal.
- Reported commissioner of a “Nanyue Wendi” seal for himself.
- Dynastic link between Zhao Tuo and Zhao Yingqi.
- Evidence in the source's model of outward submission and internal imperial-style rule.
- Ruler who asks Han for aid after a Dongyue attack.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to two short popular-history summaries. The precise inscription, dating, use, and institutional meaning of the reported seals are not independently established here. Hanji 269-1 does not give the aid request's diplomatic text, the attack's scale, Zhao Hu's direct voice, or a full campaign chronology. Neither source provides his alternate-name traditions, full biography, reign chronology, or court structure.

## What Changed

- Added Zhao Hu's request for Han aid against Dongyue as an external-dependence layer beside internal imperial symbolism.
- Reframed outward submission and retained autonomy as compatible with selective security reliance.

## Relationships

- [[ZhaoTuo|赵佗]] - predecessor whose “Nanyue Wudi” seal Zhao Hu reportedly preserves.
- [[ZhaoYingqiNanyue|赵婴齐]] - son and successor who later conceals the inherited seals.
- [[Nanyue|南越]] - kingdom in which Zhao Hu reportedly exercises imperial-style privilege.
- [[DongyueWesternHan|东越]] - attacker whose action prompts Zhao Hu's request for Han aid.
- [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] - ruler whose court responds to the aid request with a two-route force.
