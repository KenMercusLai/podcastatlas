# 赵道襄王 / King Daoxiang of Zhao

赵道襄王 / King Daoxiang of Zhao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-104-yinmou-shisuan-rang-qinguo-bantu-you-you-you-pengzhang-le-lgnyhbduqutegtksrjwcvhmn8-t6]]. The source identifies him as Zhao Yan, successor to [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]], and makes his first important act the removal of [[LianPo|廉颇]] from command.

His replacement choice, [[LeCheng|乐乘]], triggers Lian Po's angry attack and flight to [[WeiState|魏国]]. Later, after [[QinState|秦国]] repeatedly pressures [[ZhaoState|赵国]], Zhao Daoxiang Wang considers recalling Lian Po but relies on an envoy whose report is corrupted by [[GuoKai|郭开]]'s bribe. The episode therefore makes him a ruler whose distrust and bad information leave Zhao unable to recover a major veteran commander.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-105-zhanzheng-huayang-zhaoguo-limu-zui-hui-shua-ljtqdg46a8o13zzsjv-p-fpol5oh]] adds the immediate successor-commander branch. After Lian Po's departure, Zhao Daoxiang Wang appoints [[LiMu|李牧]], who attacks [[YanState|燕国]] and takes 五遂 and 方城. The episode therefore complicates the ruler's earlier command failure: Zhao loses Lian Po through distrust and corrupted reporting, yet still has enough military depth to activate Li Mu as the next major commander.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-111-1-ruoda-de-zhaoguo-jing-wang-yu-yige-jinv-zhi-shou-lhrqb8-swlkuyoydgjyn8ldnlkzc]] records Zhao Daoxiang Wang's death and makes his succession decision central. The source says he displaced the original crown prince [[ZhaoJia|赵嘉]] and installed [[ZhaoYoumiuWang|赵迁 / 赵幽缪王]], son of a favored woman whom the episode identifies as a sex worker. This turns Zhao Daoxiang Wang's wiki role from command-trust failure into broader institutional damage: he loses or blocks major generals and also leaves Zhao to a contested, morally criticized heir while Qin attacks.
