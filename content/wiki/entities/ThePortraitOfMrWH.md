# 《W.H.先生的画像》 / The Portrait of Mr. W.H.

[[ThePortraitOfMrWH|《W.H.先生的画像》]] is the [[OscarWilde|Wilde]] story that [[10-wang-erde-qiyi-gushiji-yong-zui-hui-sahuang-de-zuichun-qinwen-tianshi-545332195]] treats as a fiction about obsession, literary proof, and the seduction of an elegant theory. In the episode's summary, a young reader believes he has discovered the hidden identity behind [[WilliamShakespeare|Shakespeare]]'s sonnets, lacks sufficient evidence, forges a portrait, is exposed, and kills himself; the friend who unmasks him then begins to inherit the same belief.

The story extends [[InterpretationAndOverinterpretation]] because the problem is not only false evidence. Wilde makes the theory beautiful enough that readers may feel pulled into it even after they know the portrait is forged. The episode compares that sensation to books such as [[TheNameOfTheRose|《玫瑰的名字》]] and emphasizes [[FictionalEvidenceSeduction]]: art can make a false proof aesthetically and emotionally powerful without making it true.
