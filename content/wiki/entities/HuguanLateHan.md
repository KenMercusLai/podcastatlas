## Overview

壶关 / Huguan (Late Han) is the fortified [[Shangdang|上党]] position where [[GaoGanLateHan|高干]]'s rebellion meets [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s 206 CE campaign and where an extermination threat initially prevents surrender.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1073-huguan-zhi-zhan-caocao-de-tucheng-jihua-haican-le-shui-lspli7p63ouktn6k0zbgvggj59di|Hanji 1073]] makes Huguan a compact case in siege incentives. Cao Cao's “城拔皆坑之” order tells soldiers and civilians that capture means death, so continued resistance becomes the safer available choice and the siege lasts for months. [[CaoRen|曹仁]] advises leaving a route to survival; after Cao Cao withdraws the order, the city surrenders. The place therefore marks both Gao Gan's final defensive position and the difference between coercion that closes surrender and pressure that preserves it.

## Key Characteristics

- Fortified route position within the Shangdang-Bingzhou rebellion field.
- Final major defensive position in Gao Gan's resistance as presented by Hanji 1073.
- Site where an extermination threat hardens military and civilian resistance.
- Site where withdrawing that threat makes negotiated capitulation possible.

## Qualifications

This is a bounded late-Han place profile, not a full history or geography of Huguan. The episode does not reconstruct the exact siege works, defender command structure, negotiation procedure, casualty total, or the time between withdrawal of the order and capitulation.
