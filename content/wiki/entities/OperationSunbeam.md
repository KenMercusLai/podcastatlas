# Operation Sunbeam / 阳光计划

Operation Sunbeam / 阳光计划 is the MI6 handling project around [[OlegGordievsky|Oleg Gordievsky]] in [[80-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-shang-691866649]]. The episode presents it as a carefully staged recruitment process that began from files, Danish observation, Kaplan's test contact, and gradual direct disclosure by British officers.

The operation's first ethical and practical tension was that Gordievsky did not want to be treated as a purchased asset. He initially asked [[MI6]] not to harm his [[KGB]] colleagues, not to secretly record or photograph him, and not to pay him. Those conditions make [[IdeologicalDefection]] explicit while also foreshadowing later compromises in real intelligence work.

[[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] shows Sunbeam reviving after Gordievsky signals that he is learning English and then reaches London. The operation's mature phase is no longer only recruitment: MI6 must preserve his KGB credibility, use his reporting on [[OperationRYAN]], and prepare for the moment when [[OperationPimlico]] becomes necessary.
