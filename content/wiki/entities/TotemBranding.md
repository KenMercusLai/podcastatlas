# Totem Branding

## Overview
Totem Branding is the branding company associated with [[AndyStalman]] in [[how-modern-brands-build-loyalty-purpose-and-identity-andy-stalman-shekhar-natarajan-podcast]]. The source describes it as a privately owned branding company doing end-to-end work across country, city, hospitality, airline, consumer-goods, technology, agriculture, and institutional brands.

## Current Profile
Totem's source-scoped profile is a branding consultancy built around identity, purpose, values, value proposition, promise, talent, leadership, and impact. Its method is used in the episode to explain why organizations can often state what they do but struggle to state why they exist and who they are.

## Key Characteristics
- Privately owned branding company described by [[AndyStalman]].
- Founded after an intended name, Tribe, was unavailable; Totem became the registered name.
- Works across place brands, consumer brands, technology, agriculture, hotels, airlines, and institutions.
- Uses a broad brand methodology centered on identity, personality, purpose, values, promise, leadership, and impact.
- Source case for [[WholeCompanyBranding]] rather than only logo or campaign design.
