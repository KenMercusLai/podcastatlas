# Vasco Goncalves

## Overview

Vasco Goncalves was a Portuguese general and prime minister associated with decolonization, nationalization, and the radical phase of the [[PortugueseRevolutionaryProcess]].

## Current Profile

Appointed by [[AntonioDeSpinola|Spinola]] in the hope that he would contain upheaval, Goncalves instead accelerated decolonization and presided over sweeping state acquisition of banks and major industries. His August 1975 speech and dismissal by Costa Gomes mark the source's turning point from peak radicalization toward a moderate military reaction.

## Key Characteristics

- Rose through the post-coup military government rather than electoral party leadership.
- Advanced rapid decolonization against Spinola's preference.
- Oversaw extensive nationalization across finance, industry, transport, media, and agriculture.
- Became a symbol of revolutionary radicalization and foreign communist anxiety.
- Lost office as military moderates organized against the radical course.
