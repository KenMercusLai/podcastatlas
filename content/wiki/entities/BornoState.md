# Borno State

## Overview
Borno State is the northeastern Nigerian setting where the episode connects prolonged jihadist violence, damaged livelihoods, crowded schools, aid withdrawal, severe malnutrition, unsafe travel, and low voter turnout.

## Current Profile
The source presents Borno as a compounding-crisis case. Armed groups attack security forces, contest territory, kidnap residents, and loot or tax farmers; the resulting insecurity reduces work, trade, schooling, food access, medical recovery, and safe electoral participation.

The state also reveals how weak services and external aid interact. A Maiduguri primary school has almost 5,000 pupils and may lose funding, while clinicians treat children who cannot safely return to food-scarce villages under militant control.

## Key Characteristics
- Long-running site of jihadist violence, territorial insecurity, kidnapping, and pressure on farmers.
- Local economy weakened by lost work, customers, agricultural output, and safe movement.
- Education system represented by severe overcrowding and impending aid withdrawal.
- Food-security emergency with episode-reported malnutrition at its worst level in a decade.
- Electoral environment where danger and cynicism contribute to exceptionally low turnout.

## Evidence
- Security and livelihoods: [[songs-of-disenchantment-the-sorrow-of-nigeria-6275426999307c5f9d5c7073a5b16e0d]] describes attacks, kidnappings, farmer extraction, unemployment, and weak trade.
- Education and aid: [[songs-of-disenchantment-the-sorrow-of-nigeria-6275426999307c5f9d5c7073a5b16e0d]] describes a nearly 5,000-pupil Maiduguri school facing funding loss.
- Nutrition and participation: [[songs-of-disenchantment-the-sorrow-of-nigeria-6275426999307c5f9d5c7073a5b16e0d]] links hunger and unsafe villages to clinical malnutrition and reports turnout below 20% in 2023.

## Qualifications
The episode uses selected school, clinic, resident, and correspondent observations rather than a comprehensive state survey. Population conditions, territorial control, nutrition prevalence, aid continuity, and electoral behavior are time-sensitive and source-scoped.
