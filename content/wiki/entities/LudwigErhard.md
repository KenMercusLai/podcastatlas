# Ludwig Erhard

## Overview

Ludwig Erhard appears as the economist and short-serving West German chancellor who followed [[KonradAdenauer]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Erhard as more technocrat than political operator. His economic expertise and pro-American orientation linked him to [[WestGermany]]'s reconstruction, but the source uses his roughly three-year chancellorship to show that technical authority did not reproduce Adenauer's political command.

## Key Characteristics

- Economist elevated from reconstruction-era expertise to the chancellorship.
- Successor to Adenauer in the West German leadership sequence.
- More technocratic and pro-American than politically dominant in the episode's account.

## Evidence

- Formation and role: [[102-germany-from-adenauer-to-angela-glt6085378369]] describes Erhard's Bavarian background, First World War wound, and later work as an economist.
- Leadership limit: [[102-germany-from-adenauer-to-angela-glt6085378369]] presents his short tenure as evidence that economic expertise did not guarantee durable political authority.

## Qualifications

The source offers only a compressed portrait and does not reconstruct Erhard's economic policies, the social market economy, coalition politics, or the causes of his fall in detail.

## What Changed

- Created Erhard as the technocratic bridge between Adenauer and the late-1960s reckoning with the Nazi past.
