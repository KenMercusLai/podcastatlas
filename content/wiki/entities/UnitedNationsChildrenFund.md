# UNICEF

## Overview
UNICEF is represented in the wiki as a child-focused United Nations institution cited in a source about why parents alone cannot manage youth social-media harms.

## Current Profile
The current source cites UNICEF for the argument that parents are asked to do an impossible job when they are expected to monitor platforms, apps, algorithms, and design systems they did not create. In the episode, this supports a platform-responsibility frame: youth protection should not rely only on household discipline when the product environment is built by large technology companies.

The page's current wiki role is narrow and source-scoped. It connects the youth social-media branch to child-rights and institutional responsibility rather than trying to summarize UNICEF's full mandate.
