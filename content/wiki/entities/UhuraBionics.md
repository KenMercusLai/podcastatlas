## Overview
Uhura Bionics is the company led by [[KonradZielinski|Konrad Zieliński]] that develops non-invasive electronic speech devices for people who have undergone laryngectomy, with longer-term plans for other speech conditions and broader wearable use.

## Current Profile
The represented company begins with an assistive need rather than a general voice-agent product. Its earlier-generation system amplifies electrolarynx speech and reshapes selected frequencies; its demonstrated next generation aims to add more natural intonation, recognizable voice, and emotional control at conversational speed. The roadmap depends on embedded inference, hardware-model co-design, reimbursement-aware pricing, direct user learning, and a division of labor that preserves user intention.

## Key Characteristics
- Develops non-invasive hardware-software systems for atypical speech after laryngectomy.
- Treats expressive prosody and conversational timing as product requirements, not cosmetic upgrades.
- Uses acoustic and biomechanical constraints alongside learned voice models.
- Plans device-side real-time inference after model training and distillation elsewhere.
- Combines distributor-led reach with direct sales and short user consultations.
- Prices across reimbursement-sensitive and higher-end product tiers.
- Envisions expansion to additional speech conditions and eventually mainstream wearable use.
