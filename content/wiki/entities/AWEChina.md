# AWE China / 中国家电及消费电子博览会

AWE China / 中国家电及消费电子博览会 is the Shanghai appliance and consumer-electronics expo referenced in [[ep253-baohuo-de-ai-haowu-daodi-shi-zhenxiang-haishi-zhishangshui-lgt0cdkotgnzjl0mu2tx41p9fw-4]]. [[Heimai|黑麦]] uses his visit to describe how many traditional appliances were being given AI or smart modules, with uneven results.

In the source, AWE matters as a cross-section of consumer AI labeling. Some devices may improve cleaning, sleep, or household experience; others resemble earlier waves of adding screens or "smart" labels to appliances without solving a concrete problem.
