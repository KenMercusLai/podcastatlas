# Claire Isabel Webb

## Overview
Claire Isabel Webb is a Long Now interviewer and speaker whose bounded profile connects mathematical neuroscience and AI-consciousness questions to the history and science of collective biological agency.

## Current Profile
Across the complete source set, Webb makes unfamiliar intelligence legible without collapsing measurement, agency, intelligence, and consciousness into one category. Her interview with [[NinaMiolane]] moves from population geometry and spatial representations toward time, affect, regret-like replay, and social complexity. In Entangled Intelligences, she traces collective behavior, organismal agency, Lamarck's questions, xenobots, regeneration, and cellular memory, presenting development as coordinated correction while preserving the difference between revived questions and accepted mechanisms.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects technical research to public debates about intelligence and consciousness.
- Uses social and multi-agent cases to test the limits of neat mathematical models.
- Treats agency and memory as relevant across animals, collectives, and cellular systems.
- Reopens historical questions about organismal participation without endorsing rejected mechanisms wholesale.
- Distinguishes coordinated biological problem-solving from proof of human-like consciousness.
