# Centers for Disease Control and Prevention

The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-supercharging-a-new-fda-marty-makary-on-science-power-patients-39750050]] through [[MartyMakary|Marty Makary]]'s vaccine-schedule and public-trust discussion. The source says the CDC still recommends all vaccines, while the administration presented a hierarchy of core essential vaccines after reviewing recommendations across 20 developed countries.

The CDC's wiki role in this source is not general disease surveillance. It is the institution whose vaccine guidance sits inside [[VaccineScheduleTrustRebuilding]]: Makary argues that public-health authorities can increase trust by distinguishing priority and uncertainty rather than treating every recommendation as rhetorically identical.
