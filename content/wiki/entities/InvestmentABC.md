# 投资ABC / Investment ABC

## Overview

投资ABC is represented as an ordinary-investor education podcast. Its two current sources translate institutional process and macroeconomic transmission into questions about household constraints, behavior, allocation, and daily life.

## Current Profile

The institutional crossover frames the show as a retail-investor counterpart to professional finance: clearer goals, target weights, lower decision frequency, and passive exposure matter more than imitating institutional trading. The macro collaboration extends that role from portfolio process to the economic environment itself, asking how rates, currencies, trade, debt, and AI reach mortgages, employment, consumption, and opportunity.

## Key Characteristics

- Starts from ordinary-investor goals and constraints rather than product promotion or market prediction.
- Translates institutional discipline into rules households can actually use.
- Connects macroeconomic systems to concrete life and portfolio effects.
- Uses collaboration with specialist guests and adjacent shows to widen its educational range.

## Qualifications

This profile covers two collaborations rather than the complete 投资ABC archive. Guest claims, dated market figures, and policy forecasts remain source-scoped, and the show should not be treated as endorsing every statement made in an interview.
