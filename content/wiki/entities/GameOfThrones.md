# 《权力的游戏》 / Game of Thrones

[[GameOfThrones|《权力的游戏》 / Game of Thrones]] appears in [[31-bing-yu-huo-zhi-ge-suiran-cong-bu-zicheng-lishi-594509848]] as the screen-facing route into [[ASongOfIceAndFire|《冰与火之歌》 / A Song of Ice and Fire]]. The source notes that the television series shows only the current political crisis of a much older invented world; the deeper prehistory around Westeros, Essos, Valyria, the First Men, the Andals, and the Targaryens is part of what makes the setting feel historically thick.

The episode also uses the adaptation to mark limits. Some examples, such as the look of Qarth, are discussed as partly shaped by filming and production constraints rather than as direct evidence of Martin's historical model. That keeps the page tied to [[InterpretationAndOverinterpretation]]: visual resemblance in an adaptation is useful but weaker evidence than repeated textual and worldbuilding patterns.
