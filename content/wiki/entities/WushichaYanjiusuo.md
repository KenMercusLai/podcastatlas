# 无时差研究所 / Wushicha Yanjiusuo

## Overview
无时差研究所 / Wushicha Yanjiusuo enters the wiki as the podcast show hosting the EP385 rereading of [[MyFirstHalfLife|《我的前半生》]].

## Current Profile
In this source, the show functions as a culture-commentary venue that connects revived popular media to gender politics, class taste, workplace fantasy, economic memory, and current audience fatigue. Its method is conversational rather than archival: the hosts use close recollection of scenes, comparison with [[YiShu|亦舒]]'s novel, and contemporary viewer response to produce a source-scoped rereading.

## Key Characteristics
- Culture-commentary show using old popular works to diagnose present audience feeling.
- Conversational format combining personal memory, plot review, and adaptation comparison.
- Gender-aware reading venue skeptical of male-rescuer independence narratives.
- Media-circulation observer linking short-video revival to long-drama scenes and characters.
- Source-scoped commentator rather than a verified industry-data source.
