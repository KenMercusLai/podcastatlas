# Mago Barca / 马戈·巴卡

## Overview

Mago Barca / 马戈·巴卡 is [[HannibalBarca]]'s younger brother and a Carthaginian commander who fights in Iberia, loses the decisive [[BattleOfIlipa]] to [[ScipioAfricanus]], and later opens a final but unsuccessful front in northern Italy.

## Current Profile

Mago's career links Hannibal's early victories to Carthage's shrinking reinforcement options. After service at Trebia and Cannae, he displays rings taken from dead Roman elites to the Carthaginian Senate and helps secure additional troops, cavalry, elephants, and money.

In Iberia he helps maintain local loyalty and participates in the destruction of the older Scipios' armies. By 206 BC, however, he and his allies face Scipio at Ilipa. Roman preparation, cavalry performance, and envelopment destroy the last effective Carthaginian field army in Spain.

Mago survives, fails to retake New Carthage, establishes a base in the Balearics, and invades northern Italy. He captures Genoa and sustains a campaign for several years, but he is wounded, recalled, and dies on the voyage to Carthage.

## Key Characteristics

- Connects Hannibal's Italian victories to requests for Carthaginian reinforcement.
- Helps sustain Carthaginian alliances and command in Iberia.
- Participates in the defeat of the older Scipio commanders.
- Loses the decisive Iberian field campaign at Ilipa.
- Continues resistance through a later invasion of northern Italy.
