# 郭友霖 / Guo Youlin

郭友霖 / Guo Youlin is the worker-to-entrepreneur case used by [[ZhanTeacher|占老师]] in [[ruci-dagong-30-nian-liushuixian-shang-de-miankong-yu-shijian-lodsuktwtcfxnhmzwum5iesbzbme]]. The source says he left home at 16, entered an electroplating factory, learned from Hong Kong technical staff, and later built a business around electroplating additives, formula testing, and quality control.

The case matters because it shows [[ContinuousLearningAgainstDisplacement|continuous learning against displacement]] in a small manufacturing context. The source does not treat entrepreneurship as guaranteed upward mobility; it uses Guo to show that technical apprenticeship, reading, computer learning, lab work, and niche process knowledge can convert factory experience into a more durable role.

## Key Claims
- Guo Youlin illustrates learning-based mobility rather than simple luck or credential advantage.
- His path depends on [[ManufacturingMicroFragmentation|specialized small manufacturing]] and process knowledge.
- The case supports the episode's broader ethic of not giving up the self under changing factory conditions.
