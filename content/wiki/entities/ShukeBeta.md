# 舒克贝塔 / Shuke and Beta

舒克贝塔 / Shuke and Beta are [[ZhengYuanjie|郑渊洁]] characters named in [[23-feima-pai-qiche-anquan-shi-di-yi-wei-de-572699943]] as part of the childhood-reading field around [[TonghuaDawang|《童话大王》]]. The episode says the later Shuke and Beta material could move into black-market merchants, social debate, money, and adult social problems.

Their wiki role is to support the source's broader claim about [[ChildrensLiteratureComplexity]]: Zheng's children's stories did not stay sealed inside harmless fantasy, but kept bringing readers toward markets, rules, public arguments, and social observation.
