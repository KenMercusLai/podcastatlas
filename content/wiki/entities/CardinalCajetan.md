# Cardinal Cajetan

## Overview

Cardinal Cajetan was the Dominican theologian and papal legate who met [[MartinLuther]] at Augsburg in October 1518 and demanded his recantation.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Cajetan as a learned, devout, and morally serious representative of Catholic tradition rather than a caricature of corruption. His Augsburg mission joined theological discipline to wider papal diplomacy because he was also helping coordinate resistance to the Ottoman threat.

Across three meetings, Cajetan sought submission to papal judgment while Luther insisted that papal authority remained subject to God's word. Their failure to agree made the conflict one between rival sources of final authority, not simply between sincerity and hypocrisy.

## Key Characteristics

- Dominican cardinal and respected scholastic theologian.
- Papal legate charged with obtaining Luther's recantation.
- Presented as personally serious and intellectually capable.
- Defended papal and church authority against Luther's scriptural appeal.
- Operated within both Reformation controversy and Ottoman-era diplomacy.

## Qualifications

The episode is centered on Luther and does not provide a complete account of Cajetan's theology, diplomacy, or later career. Its dialogue and personality judgments remain retrospective and source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Established Cajetan as the strongest institutional Catholic interlocutor in Luther's Augsburg crisis.
- Distinguished theological disagreement from a simple corruption narrative.
