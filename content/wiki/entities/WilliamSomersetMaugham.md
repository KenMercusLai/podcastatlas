# William Somerset Maugham / 毛姆

William Somerset Maugham appears in [[21-hong-yu-hei-ta-si-yu-zhencheng-569042001]] as a critical foil for [[Stendhal|Stendhal]]'s [[RedAndBlackNovel|《红与黑》]]. The episode says Maugham praised the novel's power but criticized its later movement, especially the logic that leads [[JulienSorel|Julien]] toward violence and execution.

The hosts use Maugham's objection productively. QinZong suggests that his British and spy-like sensitivity to self-control may make Julien's final passionate break look like a flaw, while the episode's own reading treats that break as what proves Julien cannot become a perfect mask.

The source also mentions biographical material about Stendhal through Maugham. This belongs to [[LiteraryGossipAsContext]]: the anecdote can illuminate ladders, desire, and pursuit in the novel, but it should not replace close reading.
