# 66RPG

66RPG is the Chinese game-making forum [[WuJunIndieGameCreator|巫君]] names in [[ep242-duli-youxi-shi-yitiao-gaoqian-hao-saidao-ma-lrqbzrceumbuggzuero5qb4jv3zu]] as an early entry point into game creation. The source says the community later became [[ProjectOneForum|Project One论坛]] and was strongly associated with [[RPGMaker]]-based hobby projects.

Its role is to show that independent games did not begin only when [[Steam]] lowered publishing friction. Earlier forums already provided examples, open projects, peer feedback, and a social environment where students and amateurs could learn by opening, modifying, and sharing small games.

## Connections
- [[RPGMaker]] — common tool in the forum ecosystem.
- [[ProjectOneForum|Project One论坛]] — successor community named in the source.
- [[MakerCommunity]] and [[IndieGameCommercialization]] — community-to-commercialization path.
