# Shira Ovide

## Overview
Shira Ovide appears in the wiki as the technology-business speaker in the [[TheIntelligence]] segment on [[Reddit]]'s AI-era traffic, licensing, and community-labor problem.

## Current Profile
Her current source-bounded role is to explain why Reddit is unusually exposed to AI search: its human discussions are valuable enough for chatbot training and product grounding, but the same AI answer surfaces can weaken search referrals and investor confidence.

## Key Characteristics
- Technology-business analyst focused in this source on Reddit's hybrid identity as advertising platform and volunteer-moderated community.
- Connects [[GoogleAIOverviews|Google AI Overviews]] to the risk that users may get Reddit-like answers without clicking through to Reddit.
- Treats Reddit's API charges, moderator strike, data deals, and AI lawsuits as one monetization and governance conflict.
- Frames Reddit's strategic goal as converting users into direct app/site participants rather than becoming only an AI data mine.
