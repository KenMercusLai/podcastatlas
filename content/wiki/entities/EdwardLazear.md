# Edward Lazear

Edward Lazear is the economist whose deferred-compensation theory appears in [[older-workers-arent-retiring-should-they-be-forced-to]]. The [[PlanetMoney]] episode uses his model to explain why mandatory retirement once fit a workplace bargain where younger workers were underpaid relative to productivity and older workers were later overpaid.

In the source, Lazear's model becomes [[ImplicitContractRetirementTheory]]. A predictable endpoint can make the bargain work, but the episode also argues that job mobility and pension decline make the old bargain less stable today.
