# 中信出版社 / CITIC Press

中信出版社 appears in [[158-boke-li-liao-le-si-nian-de-zichan-peizhi-wo-ba-ta-zuocheng-le-yiben-xingdong-zhinan-ljorcjal-gpuhmwodo1l3efr7k29]] as the publisher context for [[AssetAllocationActionGuide|《资产配置行动指南》]]. The source says an earlier editor introduced the project to the CITIC team after leaving the original process, and the CITIC side then passed the topic through its selection meeting.

The source-scoped role is editorial and positioning rather than broad corporate history. The CITIC team helped move the project away from sounding like a loose podcast transcript, proposed the "行动指南" title frame, and warned that keeping "资产配置" in the title could raise the entry threshold even though [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] wanted the term to remain central.
