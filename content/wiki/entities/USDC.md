# USDC

USDC appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-the-future-of-everything-what-ceos-of-circle-crowdstrike-more-see-coming-in-2026-39870920]] as [[Circle]]'s dollar stablecoin network. The source describes it as the second-largest stablecoin and the largest regulated stablecoin network, with adoption tied to lower-rate expectations, money velocity, cross-border payments, remittances, and on-chain markets.

The episode's most concrete USDC use cases are small-business settlement, cross-border workers, prediction markets, electronic market makers, Intuit-style invoices and refunds, and DeFi lending against crypto collateral. Its forward-looking claim is that [[Stablecoins]], AI, and smart contracts could make underwriting and credit settlement more software-like.
