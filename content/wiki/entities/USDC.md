# USDC

## Overview
USDC is [[Circle]]'s dollar stablecoin network and a major product in [[Coinbase]]'s payment and trading ecosystem. The sources present it as regulated digital-dollar infrastructure for settlement, remittances, on-chain markets, prediction markets, and software-mediated credit.

## Current Profile
USDC's claimed differentiation is institutional legibility: reserves, audits, U.S. and European compliance, and distribution through platforms such as Coinbase. The use-case case is broader than crypto trading, covering B2B cross-border settlement, invoices, refunds, market-maker liquidity, and DeFi credit. Its position is not exclusive, however; Coinbase says it also supports other stablecoins, and regulated status does not remove issuer, reserve, AML, smart-contract, or transfer-finality risks.

## Key Characteristics
- Dollar-pegged stablecoin issued by [[Circle]].
- Presented as the largest regulated stablecoin network in the supplied sources.
- Used across payments, remittances, on-chain markets, prediction markets, and credit.
- Benefits from Coinbase distribution without an exclusive Coinbase arrangement.
- Operates within the issuer-and-platform distinction described under the [[GENIUSAct|GENIUS Act]].
