## Overview

Wang Yu / 王宇 is synthesized as [[WangMang|王莽]] and [[EmpressXiaomuXin|孝睦皇后]]'s eldest son, whose attempt to help [[EmpressDowagerWeiPingdi|卫太后]] reach [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]] becomes the [[DogBloodCase|狗血案]] and whose imprisonment and death later form part of Wang Mang's damaged household background. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-you-xinji-de-ren-you-duo-kepa-lnhkk70905eqg4lqwh3mp2sxzamc|Hanji 616]] supplies the origin: Wang Yu opposes Wang Mang's isolation of Pingdi from the Wei birth family, works with [[WuZhangWesternHan|吴章]] and [[LuKuanDogBloodCase|吕宽]], and is forced to die after Lü Kuan's dog-blood act is exposed. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-634-huozai-nanren-yingzi-beihou-de-ta-can-can-can-lm3wub0l70yxwwyvwi15j93i9lwk|Hanji 634]] later remembers this as the prior imprisonment and household disaster behind Wang Mang's 9 CE accession settlement.

## Current Profile

The source set now presents Wang Yu as both a political actor and a household victim. In Hanji 616, Wang Yu is dissatisfied with Wang Mang's refusal to let Pingdi's Wei maternal relatives enter Chang'an but is too afraid to confront his father directly. He first routes action through Wei Taihou's deferential memorial, then accepts Wu Zhang's riskier staged-anomaly plan. Once Lü Kuan spills dog blood at Wang Mang's gate and the case is traced back, Wang Yu is imprisoned and forced to take poison.

Hanji 634 then shows the political afterlife of the same disaster. Wang Mang can later enfeoff Wang Yu's six sons as state dukes to display family and regime order, but the earlier death of Wang Yu and the prison fate of [[LuYanWangYuWife|吕嫣]] keep the gesture closer to political repair than restored kinship trust.

## Key Characteristics

- Eldest son of Wang Mang in Hanji 616 and Hanji 634's household accounting.
- Frightened internal critic who tries indirect action rather than direct remonstrance against his father.
- Co-planner of the dog-blood case with Wu Zhang and Lü Kuan.
- Imprisoned son forced to die after the case is exposed.
- Husband of Lu Yan, whose pregnant imprisonment and condemned postpartum death extend the punishment chain.
- Father of six grandsons later enfeoffed by Wang Mang as state dukes.
- Early evidence that Wang Mang's family order is already damaged before Xin stabilizes.

## Qualifications

The current profile is bounded to Hanji 616 and Hanji 634. Hanji 616 supports Wang Yu's motive, indirect planning, imprisonment, and forced death, but does not settle whether his motive was primarily filial sympathy, political self-protection, or rebellion against his father. Hanji 634 supports the later memory of imprisonment, wife death, and grandson enfeoffment, but not the later careers of the six sons.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 616 as the origin layer behind Wang Yu's imprisonment: Wei Taihou access, Wu Zhang's staged-anomaly plan, Lü Kuan's dog-blood act, Wang Yu's forced death, and Lu Yan's prison fate.
- Reframed Hanji 634's grandson enfeoffment as later political repair after a concrete earlier household disaster.
