## Overview

Wang Yu / 王宇 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-634-huozai-nanren-yingzi-beihou-de-ta-can-can-can-lm3wub0l70yxwwyvwi15j93i9lwk|Hanji 634]] as [[WangMang|王莽]] and [[EmpressXiaomuXin|孝睦皇后]]'s eldest son, whose imprisonment and death form the earlier family-disaster background to Wang Mang's 9 CE accession.

## Current Profile

The source presents Wang Yu as a son lost before Wang Mang's formal imperial settlement. Hanji 634 says Wang Yu was put into prison because of an earlier incident and died there, while his wife later also died miserably in prison. This earlier household violence frames the later appointment of Wang Yu's six sons as state dukes.

The page therefore treats Wang Yu as part of Wang Mang's public-private contradiction. Wang Mang can use enfeoffment of grandsons to display family and regime order, but the episode keeps the prior death and imprisonment visible, so the gesture reads as political repair rather than restored kinship trust.

## Key Characteristics

- Eldest son of Wang Mang in Hanji 634's household accounting.
- Imprisoned son whose death precedes Wang Mang's accession settlement.
- Husband whose wife is also said to die miserably in prison.
- Father of six grandsons later enfeoffed by Wang Mang as state dukes.
- Early evidence that Wang Mang's family order is already damaged before Xin stabilizes.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 634. It does not reconstruct the full earlier "撒狗血" incident, Wang Yu's legal status, his wife's identity, or the later careers of his six sons.
