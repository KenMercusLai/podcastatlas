# David Vases / 大维德瓶

The David Vases / 大维德瓶 are the dated blue-and-white porcelain pair used in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]] to explain a change in modern porcelain scholarship. The episode says their inscription dates them to 1351, giving researchers a benchmark for recognizing [[YuanBlueAndWhitePorcelain|Yuan blue-and-white porcelain]].

The vases matter less as isolated treasures than as evidence. Once treated as a reliable comparison point, they helped overturn the older assumption that the Yuan did not produce mature blue-and-white wares, connecting museum objects back to [[Jingdezhen]] production and overseas collections.
