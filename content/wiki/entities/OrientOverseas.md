# Orient Overseas / 東方海外

## Overview
Orient Overseas / 東方海外 is the Tung-family shipping company whose 1985 debt crisis is presented as a turning point in [[TungCheeHwa|董建華]]'s political alignment.

## Current Profile
In the source, Orient Overseas functions less as an operating-company case than as a political-economy hinge. Its reported 2.68 billion U.S. dollar debt crisis created a moment when Beijing-linked support and [[HenryFok|霍英東]]'s investment were described as crucial, after which Tung Chee-hwa's relationship with Beijing became newly important.

## Key Characteristics
- Tung-family shipping company.
- Connected to Hong Kong's merchant-elite world.
- Reportedly suffered a large 1985 debt crisis.
- Used by the source as the financial turning point in Tung Chee-hwa's Beijing alignment.
