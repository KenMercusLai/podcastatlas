# Costco Logistics

Costco Logistics is the logistics capability discussed in [[costco-1]] as part of [[Costco]]'s "Costco-flavored" e-commerce path. The source says Costco spent $1 billion on a company that became Costco Logistics, with a focus on big and bulky items such as appliances, sheds, refrigerators, washers, and water heaters.

The point is not that Costco becomes [[Amazon]]. Costco Logistics lets the company extend member value into categories where delivery and installation matter while preserving more of the warehouse-club economic logic.
