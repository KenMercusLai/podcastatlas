# Mehmet Simsek

Mehmet Simsek is the finance minister discussed in [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]]. The episode contrasts Turkey's earlier macroeconomic disorder with Simsek's more orthodox policy direction.

His importance in the source is credibility under political constraint. The episode says growth improved and inflation fell by 24 percentage points while still remaining high at 35%. Investors remain wary because the central bank sells dollars to support the lira and because rumors of tension between [[RecepTayyipErdogan]] and Simsek make policy durability uncertain.
