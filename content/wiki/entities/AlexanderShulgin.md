# Alexander Shulgin

## Overview
Alexander Shulgin is presented in the source as the chemist who rediscovered and explored MDMA decades after its early synthesis and helped move knowledge of the compound into a small therapeutic and medical network.

## Current Profile
In [[the-science-of-mdma-its-therapeutic-uses-benefits-risks-scim6952643449]], Shulgin functions as a bridge between laboratory chemistry, self-experimentation, underground therapeutic interest, and the later clinical-research history of [[MDMA]]. His book PIHKAL is named as a historical source on phenethylamine chemistry and experience. The episode does not supply a complete biography, publication history, or ethical assessment of his methods.

## Key Characteristics
- Chemist associated with MDMA's modern rediscovery and exploration.
- Shared compounds and observations within a circle that included therapists and physicians.
- Connected chemical experimentation to later therapeutic interest in MDMA.
- Co-authored PIHKAL, cited in the episode as a history and chemistry source.
