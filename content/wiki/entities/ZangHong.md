# 臧洪 / Zang Hong

臧洪 / Zang Hong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-997-zanghong-zhisi-shi-yibaoyuntian-haishi-qingjie-nandu-lln2liblq-kyjrsvitnbh7cspbax|Hanji 997]] as a late-Han official whose death is deliberately left morally double-edged. The source says he resists [[YuanShao|袁绍]] after Yuan Shao refuses to rescue Zang Hong's old superior [[ZhangChaoLateHan|张超]] from [[CaoCao|曹操]].

The episode gives Zang Hong the dignity of courage and old-master loyalty, but it does not leave him as a simple martyr. The host argues that Zang Hong is also serving Yuan Shao, that Yuan Shao and Cao Cao are allies, and that Zang Hong's refusal to recognize that wider structure turns personal feeling into political catastrophe.

When Yuan Shao captures him in 195 CE, Zang Hong refuses to submit and directly condemns Yuan Shao's conduct. Yuan Shao first wants to spare him, but Zang Hong's continued defiance makes survival impossible in the source's telling. His death therefore becomes a case where [[RetainerReciprocityEthic|士为知己者死]], [[DeathPartyLoyalty|死党之义与守职奉上]], and [[EmotionalAttachmentPoliticalDistortion|情执政治判断失衡]] overlap.
