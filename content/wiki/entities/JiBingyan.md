# 姬冰雁 / Ji Bingyan

## Overview

姬冰雁 / Ji Bingyan is [[ChuLiuxiang|楚留香]]'s old friend and the cold, preparation-oriented wing of the trio in [[GreatDesertGuLong|《大沙漠》]]. The bounded episode values his reliability while preserving disagreement over his initial attempt to avoid the journey by pretending to be ill.

## Current Profile

姬冰雁 turns care into logistics, judgment, watchfulness, and responsibility. He prepares desert supplies, reads people and situations, protects companions, and accepts risk for the loyal 石驼. His reserve therefore does not equal indifference; the source's preferred formula is “cold but not thin.”

The feigned illness complicates that profile. One reading treats it as an inconsistent reversal imposed on a later candid and loyal figure. Another treats it as the avoidance of a journey he knows to be deadly and cannot comfortably refuse face to face. [[ChuLiuxiang|楚留香]]'s refusal to expose him keeps both interpretations open while modeling friendship without coercion.

## Key Characteristics

- Converts affection into preparation, vigilance, and burden-bearing.
- Supplies analysis and execution rather than social warmth.
- Protects companions while maintaining an austere manner.
- Carries authority that can feel more intimidating than 楚留香's accessibility.
- Balances [[HuTiehua|胡铁花]]'s impulse inside the trio.
- Remains qualified by the disputed coherence of his feigned illness.
