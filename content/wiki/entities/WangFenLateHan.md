# 王芬 / Wang Fen (late Han)

王芬 / Wang Fen (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-941-shenchu-guanchang-ta-jing-xiang-ganfan-huangdi-liu-fqiz9pi7u6cltrn-cjmjz-r|Hanji 941]] as the [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] inspector who leads a failed anti-eunuch coup plan in 188 CE. With [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]], [[ChenYiLateHan|陈逸]], and others, he uses a reported [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]] threat as the pretext for mobilizing troops.

The plan is not only anti-eunuch. Wang Fen's group intends to intercept [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] during a northern trip, kill the eunuchs, and install [[HefeiMarquisLateHan|合肥侯]] as emperor. The episode therefore makes him the source case for [[ImperialDepositionCoupRisk|imperial deposition coup risk]]: the political target is so large that tactical secrecy, reliable force, and legitimacy all have to work at once.

The plot collapses before execution. [[CaoCao|曹操]] rejects the invitation, [[HuaXin|华歆]] talks [[TaoqiuHongLateHan|陶丘洪]] out of joining, and a northern red qi omen leads the court to cancel the trip. When Wang Fen is ordered to disperse his soldiers and come to court, he flees and kills himself.
