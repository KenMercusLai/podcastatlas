# 严氏 / Lady Yan (Lü Bu)

严氏 / Lady Yan (Lü Bu) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-987-dingtao-zhizhan-caocao-he-lvbu-dianfeng-duijue-lofq0pq8n9rrfvfttunahisy0ave|Hanji 987]] as [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s wife in the reception scene after Lü Bu loses [[DingtaoBattleLateHan|定陶之战]] and flees to [[LiuBei|刘备]] in [[Xuzhou|徐州]]. The source says Lü Bu invites Liu Bei into his tent, seats him on Lady Yan's bed, and has Lady Yan salute him before hosting a banquet.

The page is source-scoped because the episode does not develop Lady Yan as an independent actor. Her importance is that the gesture around her makes Lü Bu's social misreading visible. By mixing a private marital space, female household ritual, public guest reception, and overly familiar address to Liu Bei, Lü Bu creates the etiquette breach that the host says makes Liu Bei inwardly unhappy.
