# Canada

[[the-skyscrapers-that-nimbys-and-zoning-couldnt-stop]] adds Canada through a federal support and Indigenous land-development branch. The episode says the Canadian government provided a C$1.4 billion low-interest loan for the [[SquamishNation|Squamish Nation]]'s high-rise rental project, while the source's longer arc starts with the nation suing the Canadian government in 1977 and regaining part of [[SinakVillageSite|the site]] in 2003.

Canada is the national context for [[the-business-of-heated-rivalry]]. The episode uses [[HeatedRivalry|Heated Rivalry]] to show how Canadian television can be financed through a blend of public support, tax credits, license fees, distribution advances, producer reinvestment, and retained IP.

The source's Canada branch is about production structure, not national culture alone. [[CanadianTelevisionFinancing]] and [[ConstraintDrivenMediaProduction]] let a lower-budget series become internationally legible, while [[Crave]], [[BellMedia]], and [[SphereAbacus]] show the institutional path from domestic commissioning to wider distribution.

[[ep265-dang-qing3xiu13-chongshang-resou-women-ruhe-gei-rensheng-fang-yige-changjia-lsloczlnkmcrrfpn9coh8rrqo-wb]] adds a travel and vacation branch. [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] uses Canada as a concrete case for [[LongVacationRecovery|long-vacation recovery]], [[NatureCityProximity|nature-city proximity]], and [[LocalLifeDensityTravel|local-life density travel]]: [[Vancouver]], [[BritishColumbia|British Columbia]], [[Whistler]], [[BanffNationalPark|Banff]], [[QuebecCity|Quebec City]], [[Halifax]], northern lights, ferries, food, parks, museums, and local communities all become ways to restore perception rather than only consume destinations.
