# Oppenheimer

## Overview

Oppenheimer is the [[ChristopherNolan]] film used in this source as a comparison point for Nolan's [[TheOdyssey]].

## Current Profile

The episode treats Oppenheimer as part of Nolan's late turn from individual puzzle films toward history, guilt, and civilization-scale danger. Its relevance here is comparative: [[Odysseus]]' [[TrojanHorse]] is read as an ancient version of the same moral problem, where a war-winning tool creates responsibility that exceeds victory.

## Key Characteristics

- The film anchors Nolan's recent concern with guilt, history, and civilization-level risk.
- It supplies the comparison for treating the Trojan Horse as an ancient destructive technology.
- Its moral structure helps the episode read Nolan's Odyssey as a companion piece about tool creation and ethical aftermath.
