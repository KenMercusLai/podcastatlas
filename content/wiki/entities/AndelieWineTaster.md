# 安德烈 / Andelie (wine taster)

## Overview

安德烈 is presented as a documentary director, wine consultant, training organizer, and Chinese competitive blind-tasting champion preparing for the world championship.

## Current Profile

The bounded source uses 安德烈's rapid competitive progress to argue that [[CompetitiveWineBlindTasting|blind tasting]] is largely a learned classification and team-decision practice. He entered systematic training in 2023, paired formal study with frequent competition, and describes high performance as the result of repeated comparison, controlled presentation, feedback, and event-specific preparation rather than bottle-recognition mystique.

His interpretation of [[WineTerroir|terroir]] and [[ChineseWineIndustryDevelopment|China's wine industry]] is useful practitioner testimony, not independently verified industry research.

## Key Characteristics

- Documentary director whose wine work includes brand collaboration and restaurant-list consulting.
- Organizer of a Beijing blind-tasting practice called “每周杯”.
- Presented as a 2025 national individual and team champion and Chinese national-team member.
- Treats blind tasting as evidence-based elimination supported by comparative knowledge.
- Emphasizes team vocabulary and decision reconciliation alongside individual sensory skill.
- Defines terroir primarily through prior natural conditions.
