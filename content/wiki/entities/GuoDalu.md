# 郭大路 / Guo Dalu

## Overview

郭大路 is one of the four protagonists of [[HappyHeroesGuLong|《欢乐英雄》]]. He reaches 富贵山庄 intending to rob the rich, finds no wealth, and becomes part of the household instead.

## Current Profile

The bounded source reads 郭大路's apparent foolishness as generosity, impulsiveness, and insufficient suspicion rather than low intelligence. He trusts invented distress, gives away resources, and enters danger quickly, yet the same openness sustains his loyalty and capacity for action.

His martial learning is similarly indirect. Having studied under unimpressive teachers, he notices their errors and avoids repeating them; courage and improvisation then let him disrupt stronger opponents' expectations. His bond with [[YanQiHappyHeroes|燕七]] gradually becomes preferential attachment before he understands her disguised gender.

## Key Characteristics

- Generous to the point of losing inherited security.
- Gullible because he defaults toward belief in others' goodness.
- Learns by detecting and avoiding teachers' errors.
- Fights through courage, instinct, and disruption rather than settled superiority.
- Carries concealed hurt from a former fiancée's flight with a servant and money.
- Recognizes romantic preference before correctly identifying Yan Qi's gender.
