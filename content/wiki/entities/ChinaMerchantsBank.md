# China Merchants Bank / 招商银行

China Merchants Bank / 招商银行 appears in [[vol-127-nianbaoji-zhong-de-zhenshi-zhongguo-2025-lqjy2pnfy09zyewj0ljxjuc3gxqv]] as a consumer-credit and household-balance-sheet signal. The source cites bank annual-report data on declining card issuance and credit-card transaction volumes, then uses China Merchants Bank's mortgage non-performing, concern, and overdue indicators to show pressure inside household repayment behavior.

The episode does not profile the bank as an investment case. It uses CMB as part of [[AnnualReportMacroReading]]: bank disclosures can expose weakening consumption, mortgage stress, and household confidence before those patterns are fully visible in headline macro slogans.

[[136-yinhang-licai-hai-neng-zenme-mai-llc7n0f3g2-jrz4xkoefz1nui5lt]] adds the bank wealth-management version. The episode names 招银理财 as a leading [[BankWealthManagementSubsidiary|bank wealth-management subsidiary]] and uses CMB-linked wealth management inside a broader [[BankWealthProductSuitability]] frame: product category, institution capability, fee/yield display, and asset mix matter more than parent-bank brand alone.

## Source Position
- Credit-card transaction decline is treated as evidence of consumption weakness.
- Mortgage-quality indicators are read as a household-balance-sheet stress signal.
- The bank case complements retail, REIT, and industrial data rather than standing alone.
- CMB's role is diagnostic: it helps verify the source's claim that 2024 pressure was broad.
- Episode 136 adds CMB as a bank wealth-management institution reference, not as a guarantee that every product sold through the bank is suitable.
