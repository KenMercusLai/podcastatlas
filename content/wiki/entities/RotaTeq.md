# RotaTeq

## Overview

RotaTeq is the rotavirus vaccine at the center of [[the-rfk-jr-problem-the-rfk-jr-problem|The RFK Jr. Problem]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents RotaTeq as the result of a 26-year development program led in part by [[PaulOffit]] and more than $1 billion in reported investment by [[Merck]]. Its approximately 68,000-infant trial is used as a public example of why vaccine benefits and harms must be read through randomized group comparisons rather than lists of events after vaccination.

The vaccine is also placed inside a wider child-health transition. Rotavirus vaccines, oral rehydration, sanitation, and broader care improvements jointly reduced the burden that once filled dedicated childhood-diarrhea wards, so the source does not attribute the entire mortality decline to RotaTeq alone.

## Key Characteristics

- Live oral vaccine developed to prevent severe rotavirus disease in infants.
- Released in 2006 after a reported 26-year development effort.
- Studied in an approximately 68,000-infant placebo-controlled trial across 12 countries.
- Presented as highly effective against severe diarrhea and gastroenteritis.
- Shows no meaningful serious-adverse-event difference from placebo in the package-insert comparison described by the episode.

## Evidence

### Efficacy and trial scale

- [[the-rfk-jr-problem-the-rfk-jr-problem]] reports one diarrhea or gastroenteritis case in the vaccine group versus 51 in the placebo group and describes the wider trial design.

### Safety interpretation

- [[the-rfk-jr-problem-the-rfk-jr-problem]] says the serious-adverse-event table showed no meaningful difference between vaccine and placebo recipients.

### Public-health role

- [[the-rfk-jr-problem-the-rfk-jr-problem]] connects rotavirus vaccination with the disappearance of many inpatient cases and a wider decline in childhood diarrheal death.

## Qualifications

This profile is based on one openly argumentative episode summary rather than the package insert, trial publication, regulatory history, post-market surveillance, or current clinical guidance. The episode distinguishes RotaTeq's contribution from sanitation, oral rehydration, other rotavirus vaccines, and broader health-system change.

## What Changed

- Created the product profile and separated randomized safety evidence from post-vaccination event counts.
