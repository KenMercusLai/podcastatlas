# Sirhan Sirhan

## Overview

Sirhan Sirhan is identified by the source as the Palestinian Christian immigrant who assassinated [[RobertFKennedy|Robert F. Kennedy]] at Los Angeles's Ambassador Hotel after the 1968 California Democratic primary.

## Current Profile

The episode says Sirhan was born in Jerusalem in 1944, moved to the United States at twelve, and shot Kennedy because of Kennedy's support for Israel and proposed fighter-jet sales. It cites Sirhan's diary statement that Kennedy must die before June 5, describes his death sentence and its later commutation to life imprisonment, and rejects theories that the CIA brainwashed him.

## Key Characteristics

- Palestinian Christian born in Jerusalem and later resident in the United States.
- Kennedy assassin who fired in the Ambassador Hotel kitchen corridor.
- Actor whose stated motive centered on Kennedy's support for Israel.
- Prisoner whose death sentence was commuted to life imprisonment.
- Subject of conspiracy theories rejected by the episode.
