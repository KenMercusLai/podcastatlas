# Ganesha

## Overview
Ganesha appears in [[chainsaw-sputtering-mileis-experiment-falters-6aa1299999455f9a93f7de9b]] as the elephant-headed Hindu god used to show elephants' cultural importance in [[India]].

## Current Profile
The source uses Ganesha as symbolic context rather than as a religious-study subject. His role is to sharpen the contrast between reverence for elephants and the practical failure to protect shrinking [[IndianElephantCorridors]].
