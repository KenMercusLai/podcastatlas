# 魏咎 / Wei Jiu

魏咎 / Wei Jiu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-117-1-papa-dalian-lishishang-shuangwen-de-kaishan-bizu-lr0h-kfeguvatattnj-mz8fgvnc]] as a Wei royal survivor after Qin destroys [[WeiState|魏国]]. The episode pairs him with [[WeiBao|魏豹]]: after [[WangBenQin|王贲]] floods [[Daliang|大梁]] and [[WeiWangJia|魏王假]] surrenders, the two survive temporarily as commoners.

The source does not develop Wei Jiu's own biography. His role is connective: he marks the way a destroyed Warring States royal house can leave loose surviving figures who later become available in the Chen Sheng and Chu-Han era.

- [[WeiState|魏国]] and [[WeiWangJia|魏王假]] - fallen polity and surrendered ruler.
