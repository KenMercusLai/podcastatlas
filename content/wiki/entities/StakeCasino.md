# Stake (gambling network)

## Overview
Stake is a gambling network described by the source as operating cryptocurrency-casino services in some countries and “social casino” services in places including the United States, with distribution closely connected to [[KickStreaming|Kick]] streams.

## Current Profile
The episode treats Stake as a possible financial engine inside the manosphere creator economy. Streamers play its games during broadcasts, turning gambling into visible entertainment and customer acquisition. A co-founder's circulation of a third-party screenshot claiming $18 billion in deposits is used to suggest scale, but the programme could not determine whether gambling is the sector's largest revenue source.

The legal status is contested. Lawsuits cited by the episode allege that some streamed games constitute illegal online gambling, while Stake, related affiliates, and participating streamers deny facilitating or advertising illegal activity.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses different casino classifications across jurisdictions in the source's account.
- Gains visibility through streamer play and entertainment-oriented broadcasts.
- Is linked corporately and commercially to [[KickStreaming|Kick]].
- Is presented as very large, though the cited deposit screenshot is third-party material rather than audited evidence.
- Faces legal allegations that it and associated parties dispute.

## Evidence
- Operating model: [[social-insecurity-numbers-the-money-behind-the-manosphere-7eb4966be1685ee4f19adde69e240aaa]] distinguishes cryptocurrency-casino and “social casino” operations by jurisdiction.
- Streaming distribution: [[social-insecurity-numbers-the-money-behind-the-manosphere-7eb4966be1685ee4f19adde69e240aaa]] says prominent Kick streamers spend portions of broadcasts playing Stake games.
- Scale signal: [[social-insecurity-numbers-the-money-behind-the-manosphere-7eb4966be1685ee4f19adde69e240aaa]] reports a co-founder sharing a third-party screenshot suggesting $18 billion in deposits.
- Legal dispute: [[social-insecurity-numbers-the-money-behind-the-manosphere-7eb4966be1685ee4f19adde69e240aaa]] describes lawsuits alleging illegal online gambling and denials from Stake, affiliates, and streamers.

## Qualifications
The page does not adjudicate the cited lawsuits or independently verify deposits, revenue, customer losses, ownership structure, or jurisdiction-specific legality. Deposits are not equivalent to revenue or profit. The episode explicitly could not establish whether gambling is the largest engine of manosphere monetization.
