# 荀谌 / Xun Chen (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] gives Xun Chen his active Jizhou persuasion role. Sent with [[GaoGanLateHan|高干]], [[XinPingLateHan|辛评]], and [[GuoTu|郭图]], he walks [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] through comparisons with [[YuanShao|袁绍]] in benevolence, courage/judgment, and generosity, then turns Han Fu's admitted inferiority into an argument for yielding [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] then shows him as one of the advisers named in Yuan Shao's Jizhou personnel field after Han Fu loses power. The episode treats him as part of the same advisory environment as [[JuShou|沮授]], [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]], [[TianFengLateHan|田丰]], [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]], and [[FengJiLateHan|逢纪]].

This source does not provide a full Xun Chen biography. His local value in the wiki is as a named member of the talent pool that makes Yuan Shao look stronger after taking Jizhou, while also sharpening the recurring question of whether gathered talent becomes usable state capacity.
