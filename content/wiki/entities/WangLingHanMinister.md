## Overview

王陵 / Wang Ling is a Pei County notable and later Han minister whose profile joins initially conditional alignment with [[LiuBang|刘邦]], family-hostage coercion that backfires, open opposition to Lu-family kingships, and a life-saving intervention for [[ZhangCang|张苍]] that Zhang Cang later repays through long-term respect for Wang Ling's widow.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-148-2-peng-le-wangling-muqin-xiangyu-sharen-zhuxin-ljyv8bow4rj1l5ths3znx2evgvtm|Hanji 148-2]] presents Wang Ling as a Pei County notable whose starting status is higher than Liu Bang's and whose tie to [[YongChi|雍齿]] helps explain why he does not immediately follow Liu Bang. After Han success in Guanzhong makes Liu Bang a stronger patron, Wang Ling becomes more willing to align with him. [[XiangYu|项羽]] then captures [[WangLingMother|王陵母亲]]; her suicide removes the hostage leverage, and the later destruction of her body drives Wang Ling more firmly toward Han in the episode's reading.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-150-chenping-weihe-bei-cheng-zhiwei-chenggong-zhengzhijia-yangban-3-lhfbm3twyxhyvokphf8hc9ssblqj|Hanji 150 part 3]] extends him into early Western Han court politics. Wang Ling openly opposes [[LuZhi|吕雉]]'s move to make Lu-family members kings and is removed from the chancellor track, while [[ChenPing|陈平]] and [[ZhouBo|周勃]] preserve room to maneuver. He therefore becomes a contrast case for [[PoliticalSurvivalFirst|安全第一政治生存]]: principled opposition is legible but carries immediate office risk.

A reciprocal-loyalty branch presents Wang Ling as a benefactor whose intervention creates durable obligation. When Zhang Cang is sentenced to death after joining Liu Bang, Wang Ling reportedly sees him and intercedes successfully. Zhang Cang later treats Wang Ling as a father; after Wang Ling's death, the chancellor visits his widow during leave and brings food and gifts. The anecdote's visual detail, dialogue, procedure, and visit schedule remain source-scoped to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-227-he-rennai-xuming-baisui-yang-qiqie-bairen-ta-shi-shui-liu5ugz2bds9e0rgl9xytk0a4pvj|Hanji 227]].

This page is distinct from [[WangLing|王陵]], the Qin general connected to the post-[[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] attack on [[Handan|邯郸]], and from [[WangLingXinPrince|王陵]], the Xin prince. The stable suffix `HanMinister` prevents a canonical collision among the three subjects.

## Key Characteristics

- Pei County notable whose early relation to Liu Bang is older and less subordinate than a simple follower story.
- Pragmatic ally whose commitment strengthens after Han military success changes the political field.
- Target of family-hostage coercion that fails through his mother's self-sacrifice.
- Direct opponent of Lu-family kingships who accepts the office cost of open resistance.
- Benefactor whose intercession reportedly saves Zhang Cang from execution.
- Recipient of Zhang Cang's durable filial-style gratitude and support for his widow.

## Qualifications

This three-source profile is not a complete biography. Hanji 148-2 leaves Wang Ling's early status, motives, exact military-political timing, hostage exchange, speeches, suicide, and corpse treatment episode-attributed. Hanji 150 part 3 does not reconstruct the full Lu-regency debate or every consequence of his opposition. Hanji 227 does not independently establish Zhang Cang's offense, sentencing procedure, Wang Ling's intercession, the physical description attached to it, or the later visit schedule and gifts. Reciprocal gratitude is a supported episode theme, not proof of every private motive.
