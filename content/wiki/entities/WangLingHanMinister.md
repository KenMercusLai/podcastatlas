# 王陵 / Wang Ling (Han minister)

王陵 / Wang Ling enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-148-2-peng-le-wangling-muqin-xiangyu-sharen-zhuxin-ljyv8bow4rj1l5ths3znx2evgvtm|Hanji 148-2]] as a [[PeiCounty|沛县]] notable whose relationship with [[LiuBang|刘邦]] is older and more ambiguous than a simple subordinate story. The episode says Wang Ling's background and starting point were higher than Liu Bang's, that Liu Bang once treated him as an elder brother, and that his closeness to [[YongChi|雍齿]] helps explain why he did not immediately follow Liu Bang after [[Xianyang|咸阳]] fell.

This page is distinct from [[WangLing|王陵]], the earlier Qin general connected to the post-[[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] attack on [[Handan|邯郸]]. The shared Chinese name is a canonical-page collision, so this Han figure uses the stable suffix `HanMinister`.

In the source's reading, Wang Ling's posture changes after Liu Bang breaks back into [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] through [[HanXin|韩信]]'s campaign. Liu Bang now looks strong enough that Wang Ling is willing to help him recover [[LiuTaigong|刘太公]], [[LuZhi|吕雉]], and the family line. The episode treats that turn as practical political gravity: friendship and old ties matter, but strength makes attachment easier.

[[XiangYu|项羽]] then tries to reverse that movement by capturing [[WangLingMother|王陵母亲]] and using her as family leverage. The coercion fails because Wang Ling's mother tells the envoy to instruct Wang Ling to serve Liu Bang and not become divided because of her. After she kills herself and Xiang Yu has her body cooked, the episode says Wang Ling fully commits to Liu Bang and later becomes an important Han official.
