# 魏齐 / Wei Qi (Warring States)

魏齐 / Wei Qi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-88-ni-zenme-kandai-fenghuangnan-1-lpuxkdq-rvnifuoneq212g53w5z5]] as the [[WeiState|魏国]] chancellor who punishes [[FanJu|范雎]] after [[XuJia|须贾 / 徐谷]] accuses him of selling state secrets. The page name is disambiguated because "Wei Qi" can also be read as a state pair or other personal names.

In the episode's telling, Wei Qi does not conduct a serious investigation. He has Fan Ju beaten during a banquet, ignores his denials, leaves him near death, and humiliates him before guests as an example to anyone who might betray Wei or speak loosely abroad.

The source uses Wei Qi for [[PrivatePunishmentWithoutProcedure|无程序私刑]]. He is not just an angry official; he is the figure through whom suspicion, elite display, and punitive violence replace adjudication.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-90-jiemi-xianqin-zui-wanmei-de-fuchou-liu7gbl-bffknkw6dx5udqcq4tcm]] makes Wei Qi the next target after Fan Ju humiliates [[XuJia|须贾]]. Fan Ju orders Xu Jia to tell the Wei ruler to send Wei Qi's head and threatens [[Daliang|大梁]] if Wei refuses. Wei Qi abandons his chancellorship and flees to [[ZhaoState|赵国]], hiding with [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]], which turns the original Wei punishment into an interstate refuge problem.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-3-lsrmq1-rjqnsja8g07bhaqvhxhpi]] resolves that flight. [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] detains Pingyuanjun and demands Wei Qi's head, so [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] sends troops to surround Pingyuanjun's house. Wei Qi escapes to [[YuQing|虞卿]], who gives up office and takes him through [[Daliang|大梁]] toward [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] and a possible Chu refuge.

Wei Qi dies before the refuge network can save him. After hearing that Xinlingjun initially hesitated to receive him, he treats that hesitation as unbearable dishonor and kills himself. Zhao then exchanges his head for Pingyuanjun, closing Fan Ju's demand while leaving the episode's moral question unresolved.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-4-lphaverx7gyxntncak-lb5hsrqa]] reopens Wei Qi as an interpretive problem rather than as a new actor. The host argues that his final suicide makes a purely cynical "he tricked righteous people" explanation less persuasive; Wei Qi looks more like a man inside an extreme honor world where recognition, rejection, and humiliation can become life-or-death matters.

The source does not absolve Wei Qi's earlier violence against Fan Ju. It instead explains why his protectors could still be admired: when a defeated great-state minister entrusts his life to another士, refusing him would damage the host's reputation inside [[AristocraticFriendshipStateConflict|贵族义气与国家秩序冲突]] and [[RetainerReciprocityEthic|士为知己者死]].
