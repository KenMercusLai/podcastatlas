# 魏齐 / Wei Qi (Warring States)

魏齐 / Wei Qi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-88-ni-zenme-kandai-fenghuangnan-1-lpuxkdq-rvnifuoneq212g53w5z5]] as the [[WeiState|魏国]] chancellor who punishes [[FanJu|范雎]] after [[XuJia|须贾 / 徐谷]] accuses him of selling state secrets. The page name is disambiguated because "Wei Qi" can also be read as a state pair or other personal names.

In the episode's telling, Wei Qi does not conduct a serious investigation. He has Fan Ju beaten during a banquet, ignores his denials, leaves him near death, and humiliates him before guests as an example to anyone who might betray Wei or speak loosely abroad.

The source uses Wei Qi for [[PrivatePunishmentWithoutProcedure|无程序私刑]]. He is not just an angry official; he is the figure through whom suspicion, elite display, and punitive violence replace adjudication.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-90-jiemi-xianqin-zui-wanmei-de-fuchou-liu7gbl-bffknkw6dx5udqcq4tcm]] makes Wei Qi the next target after Fan Ju humiliates [[XuJia|须贾]]. Fan Ju orders Xu Jia to tell the Wei ruler to send Wei Qi's head and threatens [[Daliang|大梁]] if Wei refuses. Wei Qi abandons his chancellorship and flees to [[ZhaoState|赵国]], hiding with [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]], which turns the original Wei punishment into an interstate refuge problem.
