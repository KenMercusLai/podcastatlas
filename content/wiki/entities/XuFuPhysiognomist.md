# 许负 / Xu Fu (physiognomist)

许负 / Xu Fu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-117-1-papa-dalian-lishishang-shuangwen-de-kaishan-bizu-lr0h-kfeguvatattnj-mz8fgvnc]] as the physiognomist consulted by [[WeiBao|魏豹]]. She predicts that [[BaoJi|薄姬]] will bear a future royal or imperial son, a statement Wei Bao interprets as good news for himself.

The episode uses Xu Fu as a fate-reading hinge rather than as a full biography. Her prediction becomes famous in the source's telling because it is fulfilled only after Bao Ji enters [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s orbit and gives birth to [[LiuHeng|刘恒]], later Emperor Wen of Han.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-135-fanwaipian-1-zhengshi-jizai-kan-nvxiangshi-ruhe-gaiming-ls8lfj4pq-ncsk7x3er7nlb0ltkk|Qinji 135 番外篇1]] turns Xu Fu herself into the subject. The transcript renders her name as 徐复 / 徐父, but the episode's woman is linked here to the existing 许负 branch because the story is the female physiognomist line rather than [[XuFu|徐福]] the sea-voyage fangshi. This source adds a legendary origin: birth in 221 BCE near Wen County, miraculous bagua imagery, early fortune-sensing reactions to visitors, and study of [[ZhouyiDivination|《周易》占卜]] and 相术.

The same source gives Xu Fu a Qin-collapse avoidance story. After [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] summons her to [[Xianyang|咸阳]], she feigns illness and tells her father that Qin is about to fall, making imperial face-reading a likely death trap. The provided transcript then cuts off after she recognizes [[LiuBang|刘邦]] and asks her father to give up the county seal, so the later Liu Bang branch remains incomplete in this ingest.

This page is intentionally separate from [[XuFu|徐福]], the Qin fangshi attached to immortal-island voyages. The two names are homophonous in pinyin but use different Chinese characters and belong to different story branches.
