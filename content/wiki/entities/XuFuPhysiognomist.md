# 许负 / Xu Fu (physiognomist)

许负 / Xu Fu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-117-1-papa-dalian-lishishang-shuangwen-de-kaishan-bizu-lr0h-kfeguvatattnj-mz8fgvnc]] as the physiognomist consulted by [[WeiBao|魏豹]]. She predicts that [[BaoJi|薄姬]] will bear a future royal or imperial son, a statement Wei Bao interprets as good news for himself.

The episode uses Xu Fu as a fate-reading hinge rather than as a full biography. Her prediction becomes famous in the source's telling because it is fulfilled only after Bao Ji enters [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s orbit and gives birth to [[LiuHeng|刘恒]], later Emperor Wen of Han.

This page is intentionally separate from [[XuFu|徐福]], the Qin fangshi attached to immortal-island voyages. The two names are homophonous in pinyin but use different Chinese characters and belong to different story branches.
