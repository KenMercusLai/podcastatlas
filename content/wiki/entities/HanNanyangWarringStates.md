# 韩国南阳 / Han Nanyang (Warring States)

韩国南阳 / Han Nanyang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-92-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhuming-tiangou-shi-shui-1-lmwf0q0-3hxb6d4hro8fdmc3zdxp]] as the Han-front place or region that [[BaiQi|白起]] takes for [[QinState|秦国]] in [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s fifty-second year, 263 BCE. The source pairs it with Qin's attack on [[TaihangRoad|太行道]], making it part of a route-and-geography campaign rather than a standalone conquest notice.

This page is kept distinct from [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]]. The episode's Nanyang is source-scoped to the [[HanState|韩国]] front and the split between Han's northern and southern spaces, while the existing Nanyang Commandery page refers to a Qin administrative unit tied to earlier Chu-region conquest and reorganization.

## Connections
- [[HanState|韩国]] - state whose internal geography is being cut apart.

- [[TaihangRoad|太行道]] and [[Shangdang|上党]] - connected route and isolated northern territory.
- [[TransportCorridorSeverance|交通线切割]] and [[YuanjiaoJingongStrategy|远交近攻]] - strategic concepts that explain why the episode treats Nanyang as more than a captured place.
