# Jagat Seth

## Overview

Jagat Seth is the banking house or banking power through which the episode explains how Indian finance helped the [[EastIndiaCompany|East India Company]] displace Siraj ud-Daulah and expand in Bengal.

## Current Profile

[[75-the-east-india-company-glt7809915873]] presents Jagat Seth as threatened by Siraj ud-Daulah and therefore aligned with Company interests. The bankers encouraged [[RobertClive|Robert Clive]] to move against Siraj and offered major payments to Clive personally and to the Company.

The episode embeds that decision in a wider commercial comparison. Indian financiers valued the Company's understanding of credit, interest, repayment, and enforceable commercial commitments, while lending to some regional warlords could expose bankers to confiscation or violence. Their support was therefore an exercise of Indian agency under dangerous political conditions, but it also helped finance a corporation whose later extraction imposed severe costs on Bengal.

## Key Characteristics

- Major Bengali banking interest involved in the coalition against Siraj ud-Daulah.
- Financier whose support helped make a small European corporate presence militarily effective.
- Commercial actor attracted by the Company's relative credit discipline and reliability.
- Political actor responding to threats from a regional ruler rather than simply serving a British national project.
- Collaborator whose short-term security calculus contributed to a more extractive Company order.

## Evidence

- Anti-Siraj coalition: [[75-the-east-india-company-glt7809915873]] says Jagat Seth encouraged Clive's move and offered large payments to him and the Company.
- Credit comparison: [[75-the-east-india-company-glt7809915873]] explains continuing Indian finance through the Company's familiarity with credit and repayment and the danger of lending to some warlords.
- Structural importance: [[75-the-east-india-company-glt7809915873]] makes Indian banking, alongside Indian soldiers, essential to the corporate-conquest thesis.
