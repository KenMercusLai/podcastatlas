# 百晓生（古龙人物） / Bai Xiaosheng (Gu Long character)

## Overview

百晓生 is the compiler of [[WeaponsRankingGuLong|兵器谱]] in [[SentimentalSwordsmanRuthlessSword|《多情剑客无情剑》]]. The bounded episode treats him as a paradoxical authority: his classification is influential and often directionally useful, yet he is neither omniscient nor politically disinterested.

## Current Profile

His ranking gives Jianghu a common language for strength, status, and danger. Its influence survives his death because participants continue to seek, defend, and resent their assigned places. The source therefore locates his power less in perfect measurement than in the collective desire for a single recognized order.

The later revelation of private interests undermines the fantasy of a detached judge. Missing fighters, excluded women, stale information, and possible favoritism show that the list is produced by a situated person. The episode does not identify a specific placement as definitively corrupt, so fallibility and compromised credibility are preserved without treating every ranking as fabricated.

## Key Characteristics

- Converts personal knowledge into public classificatory authority.
- Becomes more socially powerful than the evidence behind any one placement.
- Cannot keep the list current after fighters age, improve, decline, or reveal hidden skill.
- Misses or excludes people outside his informational and gender boundary.
- Appears as a partisan antagonist rather than the neutral observer his reputation suggests.
