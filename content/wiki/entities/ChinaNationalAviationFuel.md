# China National Aviation Fuel / 中国航油

China National Aviation Fuel appears in [[shangye-xiaoyang-46-mai-jipiao-shi-weishenme-zongyao-duojiao-liang-bi-qian-1005487014]] as the aviation-fuel distributor whose regulated distribution spread is one input in China's domestic [[AviationFuelSurcharge|aviation fuel surcharge]] formula. The episode uses this detail to show that the surcharge is built from a chain of fuel-market, currency, distribution, and airline-efficiency variables rather than from crude-oil headlines alone.

The entity matters because it sits between global jet-fuel pricing and domestic airline cost recovery. Its named spread helps translate [[CommodityPriceExposure|commodity price exposure]] into the passenger-facing surcharge system.
