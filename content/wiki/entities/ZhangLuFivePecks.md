# 张鲁 / Zhang Lu (Five Pecks of Rice)

## Overview
张鲁 / Zhang Lu is the late-Han [[WudoumiDao|五斗米道]] figure whose wiki profile now runs from Liu Yan's Hanzhong route-control instrument to an autonomous religious-administrative ruler. His Hanzhong branch shows religion as local governance infrastructure, while [[YanPuLateHan|阎圃]]'s counsel shows that Zhang Lu can preserve real power without claiming a royal title.

## Current Profile
Hanji 959 gives Zhang Lu an early operational role inside [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s Yi Province autonomy. Zhang Lu's family tradition and his mother's access to Liu Yan help explain why Liu Yan appoints him and sends him with [[ZhangXiuFivePecks|张修]] to attack Hanzhong administrator Su Gu. The Hanzhong action then supports route politics: the Yizhou side blocks the [[XieguRoadLateHan|斜谷道]], kills court envoys, and lets Liu Yan claim that "rice-bandits" have cut communications with the court.

Hanji 995 uses Zhang Lu as a bounded late-Han comparator for religiously organized rule alongside [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]] and [[TaipingDao|太平道]]. Hanji 1055 supplies the institutional detail: Zhang Lu, as Zhang Daoling / 张道陵's grandson, uses confession, prayer, and healing practice; appoints local "jijiu" officers; receives Yi Province refugees and local adherence; and receives nominal court accommodation as Zhenmin zhonglangjiang and Hanning administrator while offering only low-value local tribute. When a jade-seal omen leads some to urge kingship, Yan Pu warns that the title adds danger without adding substance, and Zhang Lu does not claim it.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines religious authority with local administration in Hanzhong.
- Begins in the wiki as part of Liu Yan's Hanzhong and pass-control strategy.
- Maintains nominal Han accommodation while preserving substantial local autonomy.
- Uses Wudoumi Dao offices and healing-confession practices as social organization.
- Accepts Yan Pu's advice that kingship would expose rather than strengthen him.
- Is disambiguated from [[ZhangLu|张璐 / Zhang Lu]], the modern AI/investing figure.

## Qualifications
Zhang Lu is not treated as equivalent to [[ZeRong|笮融]]. Hanji 995 uses him as a comparator for religious organization, while Hanji 1055 gives a more durable governance case. The healing claims are recorded as part of the episode's description of belief-practice, not independently verified medical claims.
