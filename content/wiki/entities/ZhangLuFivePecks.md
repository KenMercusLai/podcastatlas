## Overview

张鲁 / Zhang Lu is the late-Han [[WudoumiDao|五斗米道]] ruler whose profile runs from Liu Yan's Hanzhong route-control instrument through autonomous religious administration, selective military patronage, and finally negotiated incorporation by [[CaoCao|曹操]]. His repeated pattern is exposure management: he accepts usable authority and talent while limiting titles, integration, destruction, and resistance that would remove future options.

## Current Profile

Hanji 959 places Zhang Lu inside [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s route-control strategy; Hanji 1053 then shows his break with [[LiuZhang|刘璋]] after killing [[ZhangXiuFivePecks|张修]], absorbing the troops, and holding [[Hanzhong|汉中]]. Hanji 995 uses him only as a bounded religious comparator, while Hanji 1055 supplies the positive administrative detail: confession and healing practices, local “jijiu” officers, refugee reception, nominal court accommodation, and [[YanPuLateHan|阎圃]]'s successful warning against a dangerous kingship claim.

Hanji 1140 adds selective patronage after Weinan. Zhang Lu shelters [[MaChao|马超]] and lends troops but blocks the deeper trust of marriage and permanent integration. Hanji 1151 completes the autonomy arc. After Yangping falls, Yan Pu advises delay so surrender retains value. Zhang Lu withdraws without burning government stores, seals them intact, and thereby signals that he seeks incorporation rather than destructive resistance. Cao Cao responds with reassurance and later grants rank to Zhang Lu and associated leaders.

The current synthesis does not turn Zhang Lu into a consistently pacific actor. He builds autonomy through force and rupture, yet repeatedly avoids converting every advantage or defeat into an irreversible maximum claim.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines religious authority, local offices, relief practice, and route control in Hanzhong.
- Moves from delegated force under Liu Yan to autonomous rule after breaking with Liu Zhang.
- Preserves nominal Han accommodation while limiting the exposure of an unnecessary royal title.
- Uses Ma Chao's military value without granting full family or command integration.
- Preserves public assets during withdrawal as a credible signal of intended submission.
- Exchanges independent authority for household safety, rank, and incorporation under Cao Cao.

## Qualifications

Zhang Lu is not equivalent to [[ZeRong|笮融]]; the religious-governance evidence is more durable and administratively specific. Healing claims remain reported belief-practice rather than verified medicine. The marriage dialogue, Yangping sequence, Yan Pu's advice, storehouse intention, surrender timing, offices, and rewards require comparison with transmitted histories. Preserving the stores is strong evidence of a cooperation signal in this episode, but not proof of altruism or a fixed doctrine across Zhang Lu's rule.

## What Changed

- Added Zhang Lu's loss of Yangping and transition from autonomous rule to negotiated incorporation.
- Added preservation of government stores as a credible non-destruction and surrender signal.
- Reframed title restraint, selective patronage, and negotiated exit as bounded forms of exposure management.
