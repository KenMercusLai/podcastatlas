# 张鲁 / Zhang Lu (Five Pecks of Rice)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-959-ruguo-ni-chuanyue-dao-sanguo-gai-ruhe-cunhuo-lroaihmpnrelmfcugym9zx2h2dhq|Hanji 959]] gives Zhang Lu an earlier operational role inside [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s Yizhou autonomy. The source says Zhang Lu comes from the [[WudoumiDao|五斗米道]] tradition, that his mother has access to Liu Yan's household, and that Liu Yan appoints him督义司马 before sending him with [[ZhangXiuFivePecks|张修]] to attack Hanzhong administrator Su Gu.

The episode makes Zhang Lu part of route politics before it makes him an independent religious ruler. After the Hanzhong action, the Yizhou side blocks the [[XieguRoadLateHan|斜谷道]], kills court envoys, and lets Liu Yan claim that "rice-bandits" have cut communications with the court.

张鲁 / Zhang Lu (Five Pecks of Rice) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-995-ta-zuishang-cibei-weihe-duoci-cansha-enren-llicukoezccd5iycin4uq7qun51k|Hanji 995]] as a late-Han comparator for religiously organized rule. The host mentions Zhang Lu's [[WudoumiDao|五斗米道]] alongside [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]]'s [[TaipingDao|太平道]] while discussing how religious systems can become forms of social and political organization.

This page is disambiguated from [[ZhangLu|张璐 / Zhang Lu]], the modern AI/investing figure already present in the wiki. Hanji 995 does not provide a full biography of Zhang Lu; it uses him as a bounded comparator, not as an equivalent to [[ZeRong|笮融]]'s Buddhist display and repeated murder of benefactors.
