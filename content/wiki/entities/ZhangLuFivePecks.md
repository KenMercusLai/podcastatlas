## Overview

张鲁 / Zhang Lu is the late-Han [[WudoumiDao|五斗米道]] figure whose profile runs from Liu Yan's Hanzhong route-control instrument to an autonomous religious-administrative ruler. The current evidence also shows him as a selective patron of displaced military talent: he can use [[MaChao|马超]]'s reputation and lend troops without granting full kinship trust or removing the option to distance himself later.

## Current Profile

Hanji 959 gives Zhang Lu an early operational role inside [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s Yi Province autonomy. Zhang Lu and [[ZhangXiuFivePecks|张修]] attack Hanzhong, after which route blockage helps separate Yizhou from the court. Hanji 1053 changes the Liu-family relationship from delegation to rupture: Zhang Lu kills Zhang Xiu, absorbs his troops, holds [[Hanzhong|汉中]], and faces [[LiuZhang|刘璋]]'s lethal retaliation against his family.

Hanji 995 uses Zhang Lu as a bounded comparator for religious organization, while Hanji 1055 supplies the administrative detail. Confession, prayer, healing practice, local "jijiu" officers, refugee reception, and nominal court accommodation support a durable local order. When a jade-seal omen prompts calls for kingship, [[YanPuLateHan|阎圃]] warns that the title adds danger without substance, and Zhang Lu accepts restraint.

Hanji 1140 adds a military-patronage branch after Weinan. Zhang Lu reportedly shelters Ma Chao and considers a marriage alliance, but an officer's warning blocks the match. He still lends Ma Chao troops for a Qishan attack. The combination is consistent with earlier title restraint: Zhang Lu is willing to exploit useful capacity while limiting integration and exposure, though the episode's claim that he might trade Ma Chao to Cao Cao remains interpretive.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines religious authority with local administration in Hanzhong.
- Moves from Liu Yan's route-control agent to an autonomous ruler after breaking with Liu Zhang.
- Maintains nominal Han accommodation while preserving substantial local autonomy.
- Uses Wudoumi Dao offices and healing-confession practices as social organization.
- Accepts Yan Pu's advice that kingship would expose rather than strengthen him.
- Shelters and arms Ma Chao while withholding the deeper trust implied by marriage and permanent integration.

## Qualifications

Zhang Lu is not treated as equivalent to [[ZeRong|笮融]]; the religious-governance evidence is more durable and administratively specific. Healing claims remain reported belief-practice rather than verified medical effects. Hanji 1140's marriage dialogue, officer identification, hidden motives, threat to exchange Ma Chao, troop grant, and family punishments require primary-source comparison. Selective patronage is a supported pattern in this episode, but not proof that Zhang Lu followed one consistent doctrine across his rule.

## What Changed

- Added Zhang Lu's shelter and limited military sponsorship of Ma Chao after Weinan.
- Added the failed marriage plan as source-scoped evidence of a boundary between usefulness and full trust.
- Connected this selective integration to Zhang Lu's broader exposure-management profile without claiming a fixed doctrine.
