# 淮南 / Huainan Region

淮南 / Huainan Region enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] as the regional frame around [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s self-coronation at [[Shouchun|寿春]]. The episode's most concrete administrative detail is that Yuan Shu changes the [[JiujiangCommandery|九江郡]] office into "淮南尹" after claiming imperial status.

This page uses `HuainanRegion` to distinguish the late-Han regional label from other Huainan kingdom pages in the wiki. In this source, Huainan matters because Yuan Shu tries to turn a regional base into an imperial capital field before his legitimacy and coalition position can support it.
