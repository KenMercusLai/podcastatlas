# Jose Armas

Jose Armas appears in [[kyiv-brawl-ukraines-top-general-fired-6a609aec4fac21e67f1be165]] as the individual case anchoring [[DanielKnowles]]'s report on police killings in [[Albuquerque]]. The source says he was 23 years old, was threatening suicide on May 26, and was shot dead after his family called emergency services hoping social workers would arrive.

The wiki uses this page cautiously as a source case, not as a full biography. Armas's death illustrates [[MentalHealthCrisisPoliceResponse]]: when suicidal crisis is routed primarily through police, the response can move from care-seeking to fatal force.
