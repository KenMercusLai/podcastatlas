## Overview

徐璜 / Xu Huang is the late-Han eunuch whose kinship tie to [[XuXuanLateHan|徐宣]] lets a county-level murder case become a court retaliation case in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Xu Huang through his protective and accusatory function rather than a full biography. His nephew Xu Xuan, serving as Xiapi magistrate, abducts and kills [[LiGaoRunanLateHan|李暠]]'s daughter after failing to obtain her as a concubine. [[HuangFuDonghaiLateHan|黄浮]] executes Xu Xuan, and Xu Huang complains to [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]].

Xu Huang's importance is therefore structural. A local criminal case becomes politically inverted because the offender has a palace-connected uncle. The ruler hears the eunuch grievance and punishes Huang Fu and [[ZhaiChaoLateHan|翟超]], while [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] has to defend enforcement after the fact.

## Key Characteristics

- Eunuch patron or kinship protector attached to Xu Xuan's abuse.
- Palace complaint channel that turns local punishment into a court case.
- Example of how family ties to eunuch power can shield violent subordinates.
- Source-scoped Huan-era figure in the pre-Party-Prohibition court field.

## Evidence

- Kinship: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] identifies Xu Xuan as Xu Huang's nephew.
- Local abuse: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] says Xu Xuan abuses his Xiapi office and kills Li Gao's daughter after coercive concubine-seeking fails.
- Complaint channel: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] says the eunuch side complains to Emperor Huan after Huang Fu executes Xu Xuan.
- Retaliation effect: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] says Huan becomes angry and punishes the enforcing officials.

## Qualifications

This page records only the Hanji 872 bounded role. It does not attempt to cover Xu Huang's broader career among Emperor Huan's powerful eunuchs except where that role matters to the Xu Xuan case.

## What Changed

- Hanji 872 creates Xu Huang's canonical page as the eunuch kinship channel behind Xu Xuan's protection and retaliation.
