## Overview

徐璜 / Xu Huang is a late-Han eunuch whose current wiki profile has two source-bounded functions. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-857-xuehui-gongying-cai-shi-zhenzheng-de-yingjia-lh3zx6pw-4ib7owiz8c68bv-9htn|Hanji 857]], he helps turn [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]]'s military reward path into a gift-solicitation and slander channel; in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]], his kinship tie to [[XuXuanLateHan|徐宣]] lets a county-level murder case become a court retaliation case.

## Current Profile

The earliest current layer is reward-channel pressure. Hanji 857 says Xu Huang and [[ZuoGuanLateHan|左悺]] repeatedly send intermediaries to ask about Huangfu Gui's merit because they expect gifts around the marquisate decision. Huangfu Gui refuses to pay them or apologize, after which old accusations are revived and Huangfu is jailed. Xu Huang's importance here is not kinship protection but gatekeeping: palace-access actors can make reward recognition conditional on side payments and then convert refusal into vulnerability.

The later layer presents Xu Huang through protective and accusatory kinship. His nephew Xu Xuan, serving as Xiapi magistrate, abducts and kills [[LiGaoRunanLateHan|李暠]]'s daughter after failing to obtain her as a concubine. [[HuangFuDonghaiLateHan|黄浮]] executes Xu Xuan, and Xu Huang complains to [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]].

Together, the two source layers make Xu Huang a structural eunuch-power case rather than a full biography. In one case, he pressures a meritorious frontier official through the reward channel; in the other, a local criminal case becomes politically inverted because the offender has a palace-connected uncle. The ruler hears the eunuch grievance and punishes Huang Fu and [[ZhaiChaoLateHan|翟超]], while [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] has to defend enforcement after the fact.

## Key Characteristics

- Eunuch gatekeeper who helps make Huangfu Gui's reward path dependent on gift and apology expectations.
- Accusatory court-channel actor whose pressure can revive or amplify charges against an upright official.
- Eunuch patron or kinship protector attached to Xu Xuan's abuse.
- Palace complaint channel that turns local punishment into a court case.
- Example of how eunuch access can distort both merit reward and legal enforcement.
- Source-scoped Huan-era figure in the pre-Party-Prohibition court field.

## Evidence

- Reward solicitation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-857-xuehui-gongying-cai-shi-zhenzheng-de-yingjia-lh3zx6pw-4ib7owiz8c68bv-9htn|Hanji 857]] says Xu Huang and Zuo Guan repeatedly send guests around Huangfu Gui's merit review to imply a request for gifts.
- Retaliation through accusation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-857-xuehui-gongying-cai-shi-zhenzheng-de-yingjia-lh3zx6pw-4ib7owiz8c68bv-9htn|Hanji 857]] says Huangfu Gui's refusal is followed by renewed slander, imprisonment, and hard-labor sentencing before petitions and amnesty release him.
- Kinship: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] identifies Xu Xuan as Xu Huang's nephew.
- Local abuse: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] says Xu Xuan abuses his Xiapi office and kills Li Gao's daughter after coercive concubine-seeking fails.
- Complaint channel: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] says the eunuch side complains to Emperor Huan after Huang Fu executes Xu Xuan.
- Retaliation effect: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-872-ta-quqie-bucheng-jing-sharen-quanjia-ltbegc7gbet2-hlhwhtevvltgnlc|Hanji 872]] says Huan becomes angry and punishes the enforcing officials.

## Qualifications

This page records only the Hanji 857 and Hanji 872 bounded roles. It does not attempt to cover Xu Huang's broader career among Emperor Huan's powerful eunuchs except where those roles matter to Huangfu Gui's reward vulnerability and Xu Xuan's protection-retaliation case.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 857's earlier reward-solicitation and slander-pressure role around Huangfu Gui.
- Reframed Xu Huang as a eunuch-access actor who distorts both military reward and legal enforcement, rather than only a kinship protector in the Xu Xuan case.
