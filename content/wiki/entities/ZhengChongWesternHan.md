## Overview

郑崇 / Zheng Chong is synthesized as a late [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] Shangshu Pushe whose remonstrance career under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] now has two current layers. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-584-xihan-hushan-lishi-moufan-shijian-shimo-lmpoeruya1v3qmixnvi8ewaffqfo|Hanji 584]] shows him refusing to endorse [[FuShangWesternHan|傅商]]'s enfeoffment by using disaster precedent and Han institutional language against Fu-family privilege. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-585-ta-yu-ta-de-aiqing-keyi-duo-fuza-lltwzxxbjedik65yonehtfaeefsa|Hanji 585]] then shows his warning against Aidi's overfavor toward [[DongXian|董贤]] turning into imprisonment and death.

## Current Profile

Hanji 584 presents Zheng Chong as an official willing to block a reward before the Dong Xian crisis becomes central. When Aidi wants to enfeoff Fu Shang, Zheng Chong cites earlier Chengdi-era enfeoffments and the ominous signs attached even to those more defensible rewards. He argues that Fu Shang's title lacks proper basis, damages Han institutions, and violates Heaven and public feeling. Fu Taihou becomes angry, and Aidi grants the marquisate anyway, making Zheng Chong's objection a failed but clear example of omen-coded institutional remonstrance.

Hanji 585 presents Zheng Chong as a direct but exposed remonstrant in the Dong Xian field. Seeing Aidi's extraordinary promotion, reward, palace, household, architectural, and funerary treatment of Dong Xian, Zheng Chong submits criticism. Aidi then repeatedly uses official business to blame him, making the favorite issue a standing political danger.

The crisis sharpens after [[ZhaoChangWesternHan|赵昌]] accuses Zheng Chong of excessive contact with Liu royal kin. Aidi's interrogation shifts from that charge to Zheng Chong's criticism of his own associations, asking why Zheng's household is crowded while he interferes with the ruler's contacts. Zheng Chong answers with the "门如市，心如水" defense, but the episode reads the line as further provoking Aidi because it contrasts his own clean intention with the emperor's Dong Xian attachment. He is jailed, repeatedly interrogated, and dies in prison after [[SunBaoWesternHan|孙宝]]'s defense fails.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Western-Han Shangshu Pushe under Han Aidi.
- Uses disaster precedent and institutional argument to oppose Fu Shang's enfeoffment.
- Critic of Aidi's extraordinary favor toward Dong Xian.
- Politically exposed once correction of favorite politics becomes personal offense to the ruler.
- Target of Zhao Chang's accusation about contacts with Liu royal kin.
- Uses "门如市，心如水" as a self-defense of external sociability and inner purity.

## Qualifications

The page is source-scoped to Hanji 584 and Hanji 585. It does not reconstruct Zheng Chong's full career, exact memorial wording, full visitor network, legal charges, interrogation record, illness, or precise death circumstances. Hanji 584 preserves the Fu Shang objection as a court episode rather than proving Zheng Chong could have stopped Fu-family reward politics. Hanji 585's reading that the "门如市，心如水" answer further angered Aidi is recorded as source-backed interpretation, not independent proof of Aidi's inner state.

## What Changed
