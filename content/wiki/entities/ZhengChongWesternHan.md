## Overview

郑崇 / Zheng Chong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-585-ta-yu-ta-de-aiqing-keyi-duo-fuza-lltwzxxbjedik65yonehtfaeefsa|Hanji 585]] as a late [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] Shangshu Pushe whose warning against [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]'s overfavor toward [[DongXian|董贤]] turns into imprisonment and death.

## Current Profile

Hanji 585 presents Zheng Chong as a direct but exposed remonstrant. Seeing Aidi's extraordinary promotion, reward, palace, household, architectural, and funerary treatment of Dong Xian, Zheng Chong submits criticism. Aidi then repeatedly uses official business to blame him, making the favorite issue a standing political danger.

The crisis sharpens after [[ZhaoChangWesternHan|赵昌]] accuses Zheng Chong of excessive contact with Liu royal kin. Aidi's interrogation shifts from that charge to Zheng Chong's criticism of his own associations, asking why Zheng's household is crowded while he interferes with the ruler's contacts. Zheng Chong answers with the "门如市，心如水" defense, but the episode reads the line as further provoking Aidi because it contrasts his own clean intention with the emperor's Dong Xian attachment. He is jailed, repeatedly interrogated, and dies in prison after [[SunBaoWesternHan|孙宝]]'s defense fails.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Western-Han Shangshu Pushe under Han Aidi.
- Critic of Aidi's extraordinary favor toward Dong Xian.
- Politically exposed once correction of favorite politics becomes personal offense to the ruler.
- Target of Zhao Chang's accusation about contacts with Liu royal kin.
- Uses "门如市，心如水" as a self-defense of external sociability and inner purity.
- Dies in prison after interrogation and failed exoneration.

## Qualifications

The page is currently source-scoped to Hanji 585. It does not reconstruct Zheng Chong's full career, exact memorial wording, actual visitor network, legal charges, interrogation record, illness, or precise death circumstances. The episode's reading that the "门如市，心如水" answer further angered Aidi is recorded as source-backed interpretation, not independent proof of Aidi's inner state.
