# Polisario Front

The Polisario Front enters the wiki through [[taken-littorally-spains-sudden-crisis-in-ceuta-6a70712c034f16a52ebfaed7]] as the [[WesternSahara]] movement whose leader, [[BrahimGhali]], was admitted to [[Spain]] for medical treatment in 2021. The source says Spain did not tell [[Morocco]], and that Morocco responded by allowing a large surge into [[Ceuta]].

The page's role is source-scoped: the episode does not analyze the Polisario Front's full history. It uses the movement to explain why Ceuta can become a pressure point in Spain-Morocco diplomacy.
