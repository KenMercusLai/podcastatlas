# 高洋 / Northern Qi Emperor Wenxuan

高洋 / Northern Qi Emperor Wenxuan is the darkest case in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-2-pandian-huangdimen-jiuhou-xiaopihao-livpg3zh7lsqffgca2pomiqnlvyd|Qinji 131-2]]. The source says he had once been a capable ruler, but alcohol addiction and private excess later made his rule violently unstable.

The episode presents Gao Yang's drunken conduct as emperor-level impunity rather than ordinary loss of manners. It includes public nakedness, cross-dressing, violence toward his mother and in-laws, sexual violence against women around ministers and relatives, killings, and grotesque banquet scenes. The point is not only that Gao Yang drank; it is that drunkenness combined with unchecked power made everyone around him vulnerable.

In the source's interpretive frame, Gao Yang shows [[AlcoholAsPowerCatalyst|酒作为权力催化剂]] at maximum damage radius. Alcohol does not create all evil by itself, but it releases and intensifies cruelty, revenge, desire, and theatrical violence when no institution can stop the ruler.
