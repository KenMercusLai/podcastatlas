# Seema Sagayar

## Overview

Seema Sagayar is the expert interlocutor in [[way-to-go-ohio-way-to-go-ohio|Way to Go, Ohio]] who helps [[MalcolmGladwell]] separate vaccine delivery form, needle fear, framing, convenience, behavior, and consent.

## Current Profile

Sagayar identifies an open research question rather than claiming a settled explanation: vaccine-hesitancy studies had not adequately tested whether resistance changes when an intervention is inhaled, swallowed, or injected. She cites research from India on perceived hierarchies among powders, pills, injections, and intravenous treatment while warning against transferring those findings automatically to the United States.

In the episode's impossible vaccine-in-salt thought experiment, Sagayar entertains the possibility that “supplement” framing and routine consumption could bypass associations attached to vaccines and needles. She also keeps the ethical problem visible. Convenience can alter action without changing belief, but withholding material information or making avoidance impractical introduces deception, coercion, and possible backlash.

## Key Characteristics

- Frames delivery form as an under-tested contributor to vaccine acceptance rather than a proven dominant cause.
- Treats cross-cultural findings as hypotheses whose transfer requires evidence.
- Distinguishes stated ideological objections from possible unacknowledged physical discomfort without treating that interpretation as established fact.
- Explains how low-friction environments can change behavior before beliefs change.
- Recognizes that concealment and lack of meaningful choice can undermine the legitimacy of an effective intervention.
