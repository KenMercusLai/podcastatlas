# Shiseido / 资生堂

## Overview
Shiseido / 资生堂 appears in the source as the beauty company suing [[Aldi|奥乐齐]] over alleged unfair competition involving Aldi private-label cosmetics.

## Current Profile
The episode makes Shiseido a brand-protection case. Its relevance is that a high-end beauty brand owner may treat discount-retailer lookalike packaging as a threat to identity, consumer recognition, and premium trust.

## Key Characteristics
- Beauty company and plaintiff in the source-scoped lawsuit.
- Connected to [[CleDePeauBeaute|CPB / Clé de Peau Beauté]] as the high-end brand allegedly resembled by Aldi's own-brand cosmetics.
- Evidence point for [[PrivateLabelLookalikeRisk]] in beauty and discount retail.
