# California FAIR Plan

## Overview
The California FAIR Plan is an insurer-of-last-resort arrangement mandated by the state and operated and funded by private insurers for properties unable to obtain ordinary private coverage.

## Current Profile
[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-043ffe0bc2c7516bac64132229755e93-43026861-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-043ffe0bc2c7516bac64132229755e93-43026861]] presents the FAIR Plan as a residual safety net under pressure to substitute for a retreating private market. Its fire-focused protection is incomplete, often requiring a supplemental policy for theft, liability, water damage, and other homeowners risks.

## Key Characteristics
- Originated in a response to insurance redlining and urban market abandonment in the 1960s.
- Is state-mandated but not described by the episode as a government-funded insurer.
- Provides limited property protection when standard private coverage is unavailable.
- Can require combination with supplemental coverage to approximate a full homeowners policy.
- Faces growth pressure when private insurers withdraw from climate-exposed areas.
