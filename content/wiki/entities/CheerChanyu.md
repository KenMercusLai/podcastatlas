## Overview

[[CheerChanyu|车儿]] is a Southern Xiongnu chanyu whose wiki profile now has an earlier succession notice in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-830-donghan-ligu-wo-sui-si-yourong-lt-j7n9gotiw-negqxojoacf6ji|Hanji 830]] and a later 158 CE retention dispute in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-844-ruhe-liyu-bubaizhidi-lsp49uw8atyiufrf-ryo-9ctos5z|Hanji 844]]. The combined profile is still narrow: Che'er matters here because his accession and later restoration show Han attention to Southern Xiongnu hereditary legitimacy.

## Current Profile

Hanji 830 supplies the succession notice. After the Southern Xiongnu chanyu dies, his younger brother Che'er, identified as Left Wise King, succeeds. The notice functions as a chronological marker near Emperor Huan's coming-of-age rite rather than a full frontier-policy story.

Hanji 844 then supplies the active dispute. After [[ZhangHuanLateHan|张奂]]'s campaign fractures the [[Wuhuan|乌桓]]-[[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] alignment and the Xiongnu submit, Zhang wants to detain Che'er and replace him with the Left Guli King. [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] refuses the proposal, cites the principle that succession belongs to the son, and orders Che'er released back to the royal court because a chanyu sincerely facing Han should not be punished by deposition.

## Key Characteristics

- Southern Xiongnu ruler whose earliest current layer is a succession notice after a chanyu's death.
- Frontier ruler later caught between Zhang Huan's capacity judgment and Emperor Huan's legitimacy judgment.
- Submitted chanyu whose restoration shows Han concern for credible surrender terms.
- Source-bounded figure whose page should not be expanded into a full biography without additional sources.

## Evidence

- Succession notice: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-830-donghan-ligu-wo-sui-si-yourong-lt-j7n9gotiw-negqxojoacf6ji|Hanji 830]] says the Southern Xiongnu chanyu dies and Che'er, the younger brother and Left Wise King, succeeds.
- Post-surrender dispute: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-844-ruhe-liyu-bubaizhidi-lsp49uw8atyiufrf-ryo-9ctos5z|Hanji 844]] says Zhang Huan wants to detain Che'er after judging him unable to govern the Xiongnu.
- Imperial reversal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-844-ruhe-liyu-bubaizhidi-lsp49uw8atyiufrf-ryo-9ctos5z|Hanji 844]] says Emperor Huan rejects the deposition plan and returns Che'er to the royal court.
- Legitimacy logic: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-844-ruhe-liyu-bubaizhidi-lsp49uw8atyiufrf-ryo-9ctos5z|Hanji 844]] frames the decision through Chunqiu-style hereditary order and the need to avoid punishing a loyal submitter.

## Qualifications

This page is intentionally narrow. Hanji 830 only supplies a succession notice, and Hanji 844 only supplies the later retention dispute; neither source gives Che'er's broader biography, earlier record, full lineage, or later career. The page normalizes the transcript's 车尔 to the received Tongjian form 车儿.
