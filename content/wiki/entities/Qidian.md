# Qidian / 起点中文网

Qidian / 起点中文网 appears in [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]] as the core web-fiction asset inside [[ShandaLiterature|盛大文学]]. The episode uses it to show that [[Shanda|盛大]]'s entertainment ambition was not limited to games: serialized online literature could become a content library, user community, and IP source.

Qidian matters because it became a bridge from Shanda's "network Disney" idea to [[YuewenGroup|阅文集团]]. In the source, it is one of the clearest examples where [[FamilyInternetPortalStrategy]] did not fully work as a hardware-home portal but did leave an enduring content-platform asset.
