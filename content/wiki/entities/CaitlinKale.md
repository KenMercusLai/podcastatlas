# Caitlin Kale

## Overview
Caitlin Kale is the Orange County founder of [[KaomiSleep]] and a caller in [[advice-line-with-kip-tindell-of-the-container-store-93508940-11b5-4b29-ba7b-eb077edee062]]. The episode presents her as a USC Marshall MBA student who launched a personal-origin sleep product and quickly faced the problem of turning virality into a lasting brand.

## Current Profile
Caitlin's founder profile combines strong product-story resonance, rapid organic distribution, and immediate copycat pressure. Guy and Kip advise her to capture current attention, build around the feeling of security and sleep comfort, and avoid letting infringement fights consume the work of brand and customer development.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder whose product idea came from her own anxiety-linked sleep behavior as a teenager.
- Early operator who launched Kaomi Sleep in November 2025 while in a full-time MBA program.
- Viral-growth founder managing pre-orders, IP steps, scam accounts, and broader brand definition.
