# Eli Lilly

Eli Lilly appears in [[e227-meiguo-yiliao-shichang-ai-zhengduozhan-jutou-yazhu-chuangye-gongsi-neng-ying-ma-f14f8686-a6e2-47ea-92c1-ca7e71199f67]] as a large pharmaceutical company signal in the healthcare AI adoption cycle. [[ZhangLu]] says Eli Lilly and [[Nvidia]] announced a strategic cooperation with an initial budget around $1 billion.

The source uses the example narrowly. It is evidence that large pharma and AI infrastructure companies are committing to AI integration, not proof that any one product or workflow will dominate medical AI.

[[vol-117-shengwu-yiyao-de-2025-chaodi-zhongguo-yanfa-jiaolv-he-xinwang-jiwei-lmhral0rmq6tohiqdwsgmfapnyn7]] adds Eli Lilly through [[GLP1Agonists|GLP-1]] competition. The episode says Lilly's tirzepatide data looked stronger than [[NovoNordisk|Novo Nordisk]]'s semaglutide in the source's account, while 2024 sales still disappointed some expectations, making GLP-1 competition a consumer-awareness and commercialization problem as well as a clinical-data problem.
