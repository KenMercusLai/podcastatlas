# 许秋 / Xu Qiu (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-930-ruhe-pochu-tanxin-he-buganxin-lkyysh5jbgxwjq8ayrfar4gsjdcd|Hanji 930]] names Xu Qiu as the provincial inspector who joins [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]] in the prolonged campaign against [[ZhaoHongLateHan|赵弘]] and the [[YellowTurbansLateHan|黄巾军]]. The source does not develop him beyond this supporting command role.

The page is intentionally narrow. Xu Qiu is kept as a source-scoped late-Han official until a later source supplies stronger office, chronology, or identity evidence.
