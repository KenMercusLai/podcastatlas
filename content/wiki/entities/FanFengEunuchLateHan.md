## Overview

樊丰 / Fan Feng is a late-Han eunuch actor whose role in the Shun succession crisis begins before [[EmperorAnOfHan|汉安帝]] dies. Hanji 801 shows him helping remove the crown prince [[EmperorShunOfHan|刘保]] through slander and fabricated accusation; Hanji 802 then places him in the concealed-death circle before he becomes a purge victim when [[YanXianLateHan|阎显]] cleans out old An-era networks.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-801-han-andi-weishenme-henxin-fei-taizi-lgnhbsqnp6vdigsmqfkxiqmx2vdy|Hanji 801]] first shows Fan Feng in the 124 CE campaign against Liu Bao. Wang Sheng, Fan Feng, and others attack Liu Bao's wet nurse Wang Nan and kitchen supervisor Bing Ji; when Liu Bao mourns them, Fan Feng fears future retaliation and works with Yan Hou to fabricate evidence against the prince and his palace officers.

That pre-death role explains why Fan Feng matters before the 125 CE concealment. He is not only reacting to Emperor An's death; he has already helped make Liu Bao vulnerable by pushing An toward deposition. Hanji 801's source chain ties him to the initial heir-displacement mechanism that later makes Liu Bao easier to exclude as Jiyin Wang.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-802-andi-zhisi-donghan-wangchao-quanli-da-xipai-lgvne92e0jjzjnznzfkz7nvztnbj|Hanji 802]] then identifies Fan Feng as a eunuch general in the small circle that responds to Emperor An's death on tour. With Yan Hou and Yan Xian, he participates in the decision to hide the death, continue routine appearances, and return to Luoyang before the court can rally around Liu Bao.

The same source then makes Fan Feng an example of unstable post-accession alliance. After the Yan side installs [[LiuYiBeixiangHouLateHan|北乡侯刘懿 / 刘裔]], Yan Xian has officials accuse [[GengBaoGrandGeneralLateHan|耿宝]], Fan Feng, Xie Yun, Zhou Guang, [[WangShengPalaceActorLateHan|王圣]], and others. Fan Feng, Xie Yun, and Zhou Guang are imprisoned and executed, and their households are exiled.

Fan Feng's role is therefore not simply "eunuch versus outer relative." Hanji 801 and Hanji 802 show him cooperating with the Yan side during heir displacement and death concealment before the Yan monopoly strategy turns parts of the old palace field into targets.

## Key Characteristics

- Eunuch actor who helps create the fabricated-accusation chain against Liu Bao before Emperor An dies.
- Early Yan-side collaborator in both heir displacement and concealed-death transition.
- Political self-protector whose fear of later retaliation helps drive escalation against the prince.
- Co-accused with Geng Bao, Xie Yun, Zhou Guang, Wang Sheng, and related old networks.
- Executed purge victim whose household is exiled.
- Source-bounded evidence for why remaining eunuchs would fear Yan Xian's cleanup.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 801 and Hanji 802. It does not settle Fan Feng's full prior career, precise office powers, relationship with Wang Sheng, exact role in drafting accusations, or relationship with other eunuch groups beyond this crisis.
