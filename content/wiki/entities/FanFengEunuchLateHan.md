## Overview

樊丰 / Fan Feng is a late-Han eunuch actor in the first 125 CE Yan succession settlement after [[EmperorAnOfHan|汉安帝]] dies. Hanji 802 places him in both phases of the crisis: he joins [[EmpressDowagerYanLateHan|阎皇后]] and [[YanXianLateHan|阎显]] in concealing An's death, then becomes a purge victim when Yan Xian cleans out old An-era networks.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-802-andi-zhisi-donghan-wangchao-quanli-da-xipai-lgvne92e0jjzjnznzfkz7nvztnbj|Hanji 802]] first identifies Fan Feng as a eunuch general in the small circle that responds to Emperor An's death on tour. With Yan Hou and Yan Xian, he participates in the decision to hide the death, continue routine appearances, and return to Luoyang before the court can rally around [[EmperorShunOfHan|刘保]].

The same source then makes Fan Feng an example of unstable post-accession alliance. After the Yan side installs [[LiuYiBeixiangHouLateHan|北乡侯刘懿 / 刘裔]], Yan Xian has officials accuse [[GengBaoGrandGeneralLateHan|耿宝]], Fan Feng, Xie Yun, Zhou Guang, [[WangShengPalaceActorLateHan|王圣]], and others. Fan Feng, Xie Yun, and Zhou Guang are imprisoned and executed, and their households are exiled.

Fan Feng's role is therefore not simply "eunuch versus outer relative." Hanji 802 shows him cooperating with the Yan side during death concealment before the Yan monopoly strategy turns parts of the old palace field into targets.

## Key Characteristics

- Eunuch general inside the initial concealed-death decision circle after Emperor An dies.
- Early Yan-side collaborator before the accession settlement changes incentives.
- Co-accused with Geng Bao, Xie Yun, Zhou Guang, Wang Sheng, and related old networks.
- Executed purge victim whose household is exiled.
- Source-bounded evidence for why remaining eunuchs would fear Yan Xian's cleanup.
