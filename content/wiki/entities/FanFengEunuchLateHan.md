## Overview

樊丰 / Fan Feng is a late-Han eunuch actor whose current profile now begins with [[YangZhenLateHan|Yang Zhen]]'s death rather than only the Shun succession crisis. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]] shows Fan Feng's group forging orders, extracting public resources, and slandering Yang Zhen; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-801-han-andi-weishenme-henxin-fei-taizi-lgnhbsqnp6vdigsmqfkxiqmx2vdy|Hanji 801]] shows him helping remove crown prince [[EmperorShunOfHan|刘保]]; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-802-andi-zhisi-donghan-wangchao-quanli-da-xipai-lgvne92e0jjzjnznzfkz7nvztnbj|Hanji 802]] shows him joining [[EmperorAnOfHan|Emperor An]]'s death concealment before becoming a Yan-side purge victim.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]] first places Fan Feng among the favorites emboldened by Emperor An's rejection of Yang Zhen's advice. During An's eastern tour, Fan Feng, [[ZhouGuangLateHan|Zhou Guang]], [[XieYunLateHan|Xie Yun]], and others forge imperial instructions, requisition public resources and labor, and build private mansions and gardens.

That scheme makes Fan Feng a direct antagonist to Yang Zhen. Yang Zhen obtains evidence and prepares to report it after An returns, so Fan Feng's group uses a taishi star report, Yang's failed rescue of [[ZhaoTengLateHan|Zhao Teng]], and Yang's old Deng-family association to recode the investigator as resentful and disloyal. Fan Feng's power is therefore less a stable institutional office than an access-and-slander capacity around a ruler who already dislikes correction.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-801-han-andi-weishenme-henxin-fei-taizi-lgnhbsqnp6vdigsmqfkxiqmx2vdy|Hanji 801]] then shows Fan Feng in the 124 CE campaign against Liu Bao. Wang Sheng, Fan Feng, and others attack Liu Bao's wet nurse Wang Nan and kitchen supervisor Bing Ji; when Liu Bao mourns them, Fan Feng fears future retaliation and works with Yan Hou to fabricate evidence against the prince and his palace officers.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-802-andi-zhisi-donghan-wangchao-quanli-da-xipai-lgvne92e0jjzjnznzfkz7nvztnbj|Hanji 802]] identifies Fan Feng as a eunuch general in the small circle that responds to Emperor An's death on tour. With Yan Hou and Yan Xian, he participates in hiding the death, preserving routine appearances, and returning to Luoyang before the court can rally around Liu Bao.

The same source then makes Fan Feng an example of unstable post-accession alliance. After the Yan side installs [[LiuYiBeixiangHouLateHan|北乡侯刘懿 / 刘裔]], Yan Xian has officials accuse [[GengBaoGrandGeneralLateHan|耿宝]], Fan Feng, Xie Yun, Zhou Guang, [[WangShengPalaceActorLateHan|王圣]], and others. Fan Feng, Xie Yun, and Zhou Guang are imprisoned and executed, and their households are exiled.

## Key Characteristics

- Eunuch favorite whose group uses forged imperial instructions to extract public resources for private construction.
- Slander operator who helps turn Yang Zhen's evidence and Zhao Teng rescue attempt into proof of supposed resentment.
- Pre-death heir-displacement actor who helps create the fabricated-accusation chain against Liu Bao.
- Early Yan-side collaborator in both heir displacement and concealed-death transition.
- Political self-protector whose fear of later retaliation helps drive escalation against Liu Bao.
- Co-accused with Geng Bao, Xie Yun, Zhou Guang, Wang Sheng, and related old networks.
- Executed purge victim whose household is exiled.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 800, Hanji 801, and Hanji 802. It does not settle Fan Feng's full prior career, precise office powers, relationship with Wang Sheng, exact role in drafting accusations, or relationship with other eunuch groups beyond this crisis. Hanji 800 makes Fan Feng active in Yang Zhen's destruction, while Hanji 802 shows the later purge that also destroys him.
