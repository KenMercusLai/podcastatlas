# 叶弗根尼亚 / Yevgenia

叶弗根尼亚 / Yevgenia is [[NataschaWodin|纳塔莎·沃丁]]'s mother in [[05-ta-laizi-maliwuboer-ruguo-ni-kanjian-guo-wo-ceng-jiandao-de-533375925]]. The episode says she came from the family world around [[Mariupol]], grew up without the stability her older sister [[LidiyaSheCameFromMariupol|莉蒂娅]] once knew, and later became an [[OstarbeiterForcedLabor|Eastern forced laborer]] in [[NaziGermany|Nazi Germany]].

The source interprets her suicide not as an isolated personal failure but as cumulative collapse. Poverty, war, labor-camp life, fear of return to the [[SovietUnion]], postwar hiding in [[Germany]], marital violence, humiliation, and loneliness all accumulate until she can no longer hold together a life for herself or her children. Her absence becomes the wound that drives [[SheCameFromMariupol|《她来自马里乌波尔》]].
