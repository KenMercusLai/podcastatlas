## Overview

终带 / Zhong Dai (Xin defector) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-640-qipa-wangmang-ruhe-chedi-bifan-xiongnu-lhxbx6keibmfe8z6bcsmlpnr6eht|Hanji 640]] as a Xin frontier figure who joins [[ChenLiangXinDefector|陈良]] in killing Wuyi Colonel Diao Hu and defecting to [[Xiongnu|匈奴]].

## Current Profile

Zhong Dai's current profile is paired with Chen Liang. The episode does not give an independent biography; it presents him as one of the people who read the Western Regions and Xiongnu crisis as a survival threat. With Chen Liang, Han Xuan, Ren Shang, and others, he chooses defection by first eliminating the local commander.

The political significance of Zhong Dai comes from scale and aftermath. The mutineers take a large dependent group into Xiongnu territory and receive Xiongnu titles, which makes the event visible enough for [[SunJianXinGeneral|孙建]] to connect it to Han-restoration danger in his memorial.

## Key Characteristics

- Xin frontier defector whose role is source-bounded to one mutiny.
- Paired with Chen Liang as a named leader.
- Participates in killing Diao Hu and fleeing with more than two thousand people.
- Receives a Xiongnu-appointed Wuben commandant title.
- Becomes part of the evidence used for Xin anti-Han symbolic policy.
