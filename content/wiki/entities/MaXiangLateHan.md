# 马相 / Ma Xiang (late Han)

马相 / Ma Xiang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-940-de-minxin-zhe-de-tianxia-ni-xin-bu-xin-loipf7b0p5a-yh0rj2njnsml8a1j|Hanji 940]] as a [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] rebel who rises with [[ZhaoZhiLateHan|赵祗]] at [[MianzhuLateHan|绵竹]] under a Yellow Turban banner. The source says their opening succeeds partly because [[XiJianLateHan|郗俭]] has already lost local support through extraction and abuse.

The episode treats Ma Xiang as a negative short-run power case. After killing Xi Jian and spreading toward [[BaCommanderyLateHan|巴郡]] and [[QianweiCommanderyLateHan|犍为郡]], he proclaims himself Son of Heaven. That title escalation is read as ambition outrunning durable administrative and popular foundations.

[[JiaLongLateHan|贾龙]] then gathers officials and commoners to attack, defeats Ma Xiang after several days, and forces him to flee. Ma Xiang therefore becomes a source case for the difference between insurgent momentum and [[PeopleBasedPoliticalSecurity|民心型政治安全]] that can actually stabilize a region.
