# Antonio Ramalho Eanes

## Overview

Antonio Ramalho Eanes was a Portuguese military officer who emerged from the moderate victory of 25 November 1975 and became president through the 1976 election.

## Current Profile

Eanes represents the institutional turn from revolutionary military competition toward electoral consolidation. The episode credits the moderate officers around his camp with defeating [[OteloSaraivaDeCarvalho|Otelo]]'s forces without casualties and reads his large presidential victories as evidence that Portugal's political center preferred pluralist order over renewed dictatorship or revolutionary council rule.

## Key Characteristics

- Emerged through the moderate military counter-move of 25 November 1975.
- Helped close the most unstable phase of the revolutionary process.
- Won the 1976 presidential election with a large majority.
- Defeated Otelo decisively again in the 1980 election.
