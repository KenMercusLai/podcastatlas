# Crown Prince Rudolf

## Overview

Crown Prince Rudolf is the [[AustriaHungary]] heir whose affair with [[MaryVetsera]] culminates in the [[MayerlingIncident]] in January 1889.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Rudolf as a politically frustrated and emotionally unstable heir inside the [[HabsburgDynasty]]. He is thirty years old, unhappy in his dynastic role, physically unwell, hostile to [[KaiserWilhelmII]], and increasingly drawn to morbid language before taking Mary to Mayerling. His death matters not only as personal tragedy but as a dynastic, Catholic, and public-information crisis for [[FranzJosephI]].

## Key Characteristics

- Rudolf is portrayed as trapped by court role, family expectation, marriage, illness, and succession duty.
- His affair with [[MaryVetsera]] is framed through gothic imagery, secrecy, and death-pact language rather than ordinary court romance.
- His political despair includes anxiety about Germany, Serbia, hostile press coverage, and the future of [[AustriaHungary]].
- His death forces the court to convert suicide into a burials-and-legitimacy problem under [[CatholicBurialLegitimacy]].
- His absence destabilizes succession because [[FranzJosephI]] has no other direct male heir.
