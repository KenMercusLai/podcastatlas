# 苗炜 / Miao Wei

## Overview
苗炜 / Miao Wei is a [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] writer and recurring [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] guest whose reported essays connect historical or literary subjects to the practical question of how a reader should live, learn, and pay attention.

## Current Profile
Across EP259 and EP284, Miao works as an essayistic reporter-reader rather than a narrow subject specialist. He combines field visits, interviews, scholarly comparison, close reading, personal memory, and present-day tools to make remote figures answer contemporary questions. The Xu Guangqi episode asks how knowledge trains reason and serves life; the Rilke episode asks how poetry becomes intelligible through mortality, language, and spiritual awakening. In both, he introduces conflicting sources and marks uncertainty rather than hiding the interpretive choices behind a smooth narrative.

## Key Characteristics
- He builds long-form magazine subjects through interviews, site visits, books, and comparison among competing judgments.
- He connects intellectual history to lived practice, from proof and agriculture to grief, fear, translation, and reading.
- He uses personal experience as an interpretive opening while resisting the reduction of a work or historical figure to one explanatory key.
- He treats readers as capable of complexity but still supplies the biographical and historical basics needed for access.
- He uses AI as a question-rich aid to slow original-language reading rather than as an unquestioned authority.

## Qualifications
Both sources are podcast presentations of Miao's reporting rather than a complete professional biography. EP259's transcript spells his given name “苗伟,” while EP284 uses “苗炜”; this page normalizes the recurring Talk三联 “苗师傅” identity as 苗炜 without treating the spelling variance as a second person. His personal memories and syntheses remain source-scoped interpretations, not independent proof of every historical or literary claim.

## What Changed
