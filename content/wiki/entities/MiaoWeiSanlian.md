# 苗伟 / Miao Wei

Miao Wei is the [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] guest in [[ep259-yu-miao-shifu-mantan-xu-guangqi-sibainian-qian-ruhe-rangren-biancongming-lkirczxnx8skbbfpmz5-m-mivs4e]]. The episode introduces him through his new [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] cover article on [[XuGuangqi|徐光启]] and mentions his earlier small book 《面包会友》, where he had already used a Xu Guangqi-related figure.

His role in the source is to turn Xu Guangqi from a textbook name into a problem of [[IntellectualLifeAsPractice|智识生活]]. Miao frames the episode around the desire to "make oneself smarter": learning [[EuclidsElementsChinese|《几何原本》]], understanding astronomy through [[ChongzhenLishu|《崇祯历书》]], treating agriculture as survival knowledge, and asking why science did not become institutionally continuous after the early Jesuit encounter.

Miao's explanatory style is strongly essayistic. He moves among field visits to Xu Guangqi's Shanghai tomb and [[MatteoRicci|利玛窦]]'s Beijing tomb, historical interpretation, book references, and present-day analogies to chips and AI. That makes him a [[HumanisticScienceCommunication|humanistic science communication]] node rather than only a history guest.
