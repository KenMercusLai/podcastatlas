# Tom Sass

Tom Sass appears in [[afghanistan-five-years-on-our-correspondent-visits-6a7ee547049c692a6b3ee0a9]] as the [[TheIntelligence]] correspondent reporting from [[Afghanistan]]. The source says he spent about ten days in the country, visiting Kabul, Herat and Kandahar and taking a roughly 1,000-kilometre road trip.

His role is to hold the episode's central tension together. He reports that violence has fallen and urban life can look calmer than expected, while also describing [[Taliban]] checkpoints, vice-and-virtue policing, poverty, hunger, girls' school restrictions, and the failure of outside coercion to moderate [[TalibanHardlineRule]].
