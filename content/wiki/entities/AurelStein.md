# Aurel Stein

## Overview
Aurel Stein enters the wiki through Hanji 689-2 as the expedition figure whose Niya collecting helped expose the [[NiyaSite]] and its documents to foreign scholarship. The source presents him ambivalently: he is important for discovery and identification, but also for the removal and dispersal of evidence from the site.

## Current Profile
In the current source, Stein follows Kharosthi-document clues to the Niya ruins after acquiring wooden documents in the Taklamakan region. His role then expands from discovery to cultural loss: the episode says he removed hundreds of Kharosthi documents, dozens of Han wooden pieces, and multiple cases of artifacts, after which outside explorers and looters intensified pressure on Niya.

## Key Characteristics
- Stein is presented as a foreign expedition collector who recognized the importance of Kharosthi documents in Xinjiang.
- His Niya investigation helped make the ruins legible as evidence for Jingjue.
- His collecting removed large batches of documents and artifacts from their original context.
- The source uses Stein to mark the double edge of archaeological discovery and cultural-heritage loss.
