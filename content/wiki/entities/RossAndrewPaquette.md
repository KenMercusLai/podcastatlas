# Ross Andrew Paquette

## Overview
Ross Andrew Paquette is the founder and CEO of [[Maropost]]. In [[bff406f4]], he describes turning prior marketing-automation experience and customer relationships into a bootstrapped SaaS company, then navigating rapid growth, investor conflict, a buyback, acquisitions, and the long transition away from founder dependence.

## Current Profile
The current evidence presents Ross as a deeply involved product seller and operator. His advantage came from combining customer context, demonstration skill, decision authority, and close product collaboration in one interaction. The same operating intensity later became a scaling constraint: Maropost repeatedly struggled to install sales and customer-success leadership that could assume his responsibilities, while Ross continued to believe his presence belonged in strategically important deals.

## Key Characteristics
- Bootstrapped founder who funded early product development with employment income and existing customer relationships.
- Product-literate enterprise seller who used short discovery conversations and tailored demonstrations to connect features to buyer outcomes.
- High-intensity operator who reports monitoring revenue, marketing, customer satisfaction, and internal communication while remaining highly responsive during travel.
- Control-oriented owner who sold a minority stake, found the investor relationship disappointing, and later bought the stake back.
- Long-horizon company builder whose acquisition strategy seeks to expand Maropost across the e-commerce customer lifecycle.

## Evidence
- Early funding and customer access: [[bff406f4]] says Ross kept his job for roughly a year to a year and a half, funded development from salary, and converted several prior customer relationships at launch.
- Founder-led product selling: [[bff406f4]] describes Ross running outcome-focused demonstrations with deep functional knowledge while product requests could be implemented rapidly.
- Organizational dependence: [[bff406f4]] says the move beyond “Ross and Co.” took about a decade and included seven or eight unsuccessful sales-leadership hires.
- Ownership and capital: [[bff406f4]] reports that investors acquired about 25% and that Ross later repurchased their position for approximately $37 million.
- Operating intensity and expansion: [[bff406f4]] connects Ross's near-continuous availability to Maropost's distributed coordination and later acquisition-led e-commerce strategy.

## Qualifications
This profile is based on one founder interview. Company metrics, transaction values, response times, investor contributions, hiring outcomes, and acquisition judgments are Ross's retrospective account rather than independently verified findings. His preference for concentrated ownership, no original co-founder, and near-continuous work is personal and should not be treated as a universal founder model.

## What Changed
- Added Ross Andrew Paquette as the founder whose sales skill accelerated Maropost while also contributing to long-lived organizational dependence.
- Recorded the minority-investment buyback as both a control decision and a deeper long-term commitment to Maropost.
