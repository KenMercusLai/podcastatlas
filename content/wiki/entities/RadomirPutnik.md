# Radomir Putnik

## Overview

Radomir Putnik was the veteran Serbian commander whose defensive planning, logistical recovery, and counterattack helped defeat repeated Austro-Hungarian invasions in 1914.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Putnik as a foil to [[OskarPotiorek]] and [[FranzConradVonHotzendorf|Conrad]]. Released by [[AustriaHungary]] while returning from medical treatment, he withdrew from exposed Belgrade, waited for the direction of attack, and used experienced troops and difficult terrain rather than answering numerical disadvantage with premature offense. After ammunition shortage and retreat in the third invasion, he integrated French shells that had to be refitted for Serbian guns and launched the December counterattack that retook Belgrade.

## Key Characteristics

- Veteran of the Balkan Wars with an experienced but poorly equipped army.
- Preferred strategic withdrawal and observation to defending exposed ground at any cost.
- Matched operational decisions to terrain, weather, supply, and enemy overextension.
- Recovered from ammunition shortage through improvised allied logistics.
- Combined military planning with the morale effect associated with King Petar's trench visits.
