# 揽佬 / Lan Lao

## Overview

揽佬 is a rapper presented by [[ep290-fangyan-fanhong-xianzai-de-nianqingren-weisha-aishang-shuo-tuhua-lhyo15cl0i3lzhsnz22jbm888zzk|Talk三联 EP290]] as a creator who turned from globally legible hip-hop styles toward strongly Guangdong-marked mixed speech and local social experience.

## Current Profile

The episode describes 揽佬 as coming from a family combining Sichuan and Chaoshan backgrounds. His earlier work used overseas hip-hop styles and English without a clear regional signature; around 2023, he began foregrounding a Guangdong accent and writing about local, relatively traditional human relations.

He calls the resulting language “广普” or “惠州普通话”: accented Mandarin mixed with Cantonese, Chaoshan, and Hakka vocabulary. In this profile, mixed speech is not a deficient copy of standard Mandarin but a record of migration and everyday urban contact. His stated reason for the shift was intuitive—he thought it would become popular—so cultural interpretation should not erase commercial instinct.

## Key Characteristics

- Rapper with combined Sichuan and Chaoshan family background in the episode's account.
- Shifted around 2023 from less regionally marked styles toward a strong Guangdong speech signature.
- Uses “广普/惠州普通话” mixing accented Mandarin with several regional vocabularies.
- Writes about local interpersonal and social life as well as sound.
- Attributes the turn partly to an intuition that the style would become popular.
