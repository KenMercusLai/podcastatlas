# Fedco

Fedco is the nonprofit federal-employee membership club in [[costco-1]] that helped show [[SolPrice]] how pooled buying power, restricted access, and membership could support low prices. The episode treats it as a precursor rather than a direct predecessor: Fedco refused Price's San Diego expansion proposal, which pushed him to found [[FedMart]].

## Connections
- [[SolPrice]] and [[FedMart]] - membership-discount lineage.
- [[MembershipRetail]] and [[WarehouseClubModel]] - later models informed by the Fedco template.
