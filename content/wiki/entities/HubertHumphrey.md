# Hubert Humphrey

## Overview

Hubert Humphrey appears as the Democratic vice president and presidential nominee who nearly erased [[RichardNixon]]'s large lead in the [[UnitedStatesPresidentialElection1968|1968 election]].

## Current Profile

Humphrey begins the episode trapped between loyalty to [[LyndonBJohnson]] and anti-war Democrats who heckle him over Vietnam. His September 30 television speech supporting a halt to bombing North Vietnam creates political separation from Johnson, reduces protest pressure, brings donors back, and helps Democratic organizations turn the race into a near tie.

## Key Characteristics

- Johnson's vice president and the Democratic nominee in 1968.
- Initially burdened by the administration's Vietnam policy and anti-war hostility.
- Regained political room by endorsing a bombing halt on national television.
- Benefited from a strong economy and renewed Democratic organizational activity.
- Lost narrowly after a substantial late campaign recovery.

## Evidence

- Political constraint: [[513-america-in-68-nixons-great-comeback-part-6-glt3853956463]] presents Humphrey as heckled by anti-war protesters while remaining tied to Johnson's policy.
- Recovery mechanism: [[513-america-in-68-nixons-great-comeback-part-6-glt3853956463]] links the Salt Lake City speech to fewer protests, returning liberal donors, and a narrowing Nixon lead.
- Electoral outcome: [[513-america-in-68-nixons-great-comeback-part-6-glt3853956463]] describes Humphrey briefly leading the popular vote on election night before Nixon prevailed.

## Qualifications

The episode treats Humphrey mainly as Nixon's late-closing opponent. It does not provide a full account of his earlier career, domestic program, convention strategy, or independent campaign organization.

## What Changed

- Added Humphrey as a constrained incumbent-party nominee whose policy separation enabled a late but incomplete recovery.

## Relationships

- [[RichardNixon]] - victorious Republican opponent.
- [[LyndonBJohnson]] - president whose Vietnam policy constrained Humphrey.
- [[DemocraticParty]] - party organization that reactivated behind his campaign.
- [[UnitedStatesPresidentialElection1968]] - election in which his late surge fell narrowly short.
