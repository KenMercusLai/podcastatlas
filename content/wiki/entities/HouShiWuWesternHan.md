## Overview

侯史吴 / Hou Shiwu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-402-1-sanghongyang-ceng-bang-hanwudi-zhifu-weihe-zuihou-canzao-miezu-ltpcos20d5mafcoywmfcb6iifwsv|Hanji 402-1]] as the man who shelters [[SangQianWesternHan|桑迁]] and then becomes the test case for whether harboring linked to a rebellion can be pardoned by amnesty.

## Current Profile

Hou Shiwu's source profile is defined by one act and two interpretations. [[WangPingWesternHan|王平]] and [[XuRenWesternHan|徐仁]] interpret his short-term sheltering as pardonable because he was not himself a rebel and had self-surrendered after the amnesty. [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]]'s review rejects that boundary: sheltering Sang Qian becomes sheltering a rebel, and the earlier lenient judgment becomes official indulgence of rebellion.

## Key Characteristics

- Shelterer of Sang Qian after the Sang Hongyang purge.
- Self-surrendering actor after an amnesty promises non-pursuit for previously undiscovered related officials or conduct.
- Initially treated by Wang Ping and Xu Ren as pardonable because he was not personally a rebel.
- Reclassified under Huo Guang's review as an unpardonable harborer of a rebel.
- Legal hinge through which the case expands from a fugitive family member to officials who favored leniency.
