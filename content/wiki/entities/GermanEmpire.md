# German Empire

## Overview

The German Empire was the state founded under [[OttoVonBismarck]] in 1871 and dissolved amid defeat and revolution in 1918.

## Current Profile

The episode presents the empire as politically mixed rather than simply authoritarian. Broad male suffrage and a very large socialist party made mass electoral politics real, while the Kaiser and an army outside ordinary parliamentary control preserved powerful anti-democratic habits. During the [[FirstWorldWar]], fiscal strain and the growing dominance of military leaders weakened civilian politics before sudden defeat and revolution produced the [[WeimarRepublic]].

## Key Characteristics

- German state created under Prussian leadership in 1871.
- Constitutional order with meaningful elections but limited parliamentary control over the executive and army.
- Society containing strong socialist organization as well as militarized institutions.
- Wartime state increasingly dominated by military leadership.
- Empire whose abrupt 1918 collapse shaped the legitimacy crisis of its democratic successor.

## Evidence

- Political structure: [[295-the-rise-of-the-nazis-part-1-glt8045984312]] contrasts broad suffrage and Social Democratic strength with the army's insulation from parliament.
- Wartime transformation: [[295-the-rise-of-the-nazis-part-1-glt8045984312]] links blockade, casualties, debt finance, and military dominance to the empire's crisis.
- Collapse: [[295-the-rise-of-the-nazis-part-1-glt8045984312]] places the Kaiser's fall and republican proclamation inside sudden defeat and revolutionary disorder.

## Qualifications

The page reflects one episode's high-level prehistory, not a complete constitutional or social history. Mixed institutions did not make Nazism inevitable, and the source warns against reading every pre-1914 feature backward from the Holocaust.
