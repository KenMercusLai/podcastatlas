## Overview

平妈 is [[APingMyDateWithAVampire|阿平]]'s mother and the living-corpse antagonist of the 佳佳大厦 arc in [[MyDateWithAVampire|《我和僵尸有个约会》]].

## Current Profile

Ping Ma loves her son, fears losing him, and organizes her entire moral world around protecting and directing him. The episode's central judgment is that this love has become possessive: she decides which women are respectable, what kind of wife he should have, and what he ought to want, leaving a man in his thirties unable to describe an independent life.

Zombie blood does not create the pattern from nothing. In the hosts' reading, it magnifies suspicion, categorical moral judgment, and possessiveness into murder. Her decisive change occurs when [[PPMyDateWithAVampire|P.P.]], one of her victims, explains that the son's death followed from control presented as sacrifice. Ping Ma's apology releases A Ping's resentment, but the ending does not erase the victims or make acknowledgment equivalent to complete restitution.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines genuine maternal attachment with possessive control.
- Sorts women and neighbors through rigid moral and class-coded categories.
- Treats A Ping's adult autonomy as a threat to maternal duty and identity.
- Escalates from insult and interference to murder after becoming a living corpse.
- Protects her self-image by blaming outsiders until consequences become undeniable.
- Ultimately names her role in the harm and apologizes to her son.

## Qualifications

This is a fictional-character profile bounded to one podcast account. The claim that zombie blood amplifies preexisting traits is interpretive story logic. Love and sacrifice can explain her self-understanding without excusing control or murder, and the supernatural reconciliation does not establish that severe real-world harm is repaired by one apology.
