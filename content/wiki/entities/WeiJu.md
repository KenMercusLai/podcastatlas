# 魏驹 / Wei Ju

## Overview
Wei Ju is the Wei-house leader in the [[BattleOfJinyang|Battle of Jinyang]] who moves from Zhi Yao's coalition to the secret Wei-Han-Zhao counteralliance.

## Current Profile
Wei Ju initially helps [[ZhiYao|Zhi Yao]] besiege Zhao, but the water attack and Zhi Yao's remark about destroying states make Anyi's vulnerability concrete. He and [[HanKangzi|Han Hu]] recognize the danger before [[ZhangMengtan|Zhang Mengtan]] approaches them; the envoy succeeds by giving their existing fear a shared strategic formula. Wei Ju accepts the secrecy risk and joins the flank attack after Zhao reverses the flood.

## Key Characteristics
- Nominal ally whose cooperation rests on coercion rather than durable trust.
- Reads a successful weapon as a future threat to his own base.
- Communicates suspicion covertly with Han Hu before open negotiation.
- Accepts a high-risk reversal when self-preservation incentives align.

## Evidence
- Threat recognition: the episode says Wei Ju reacts when Zhi Yao connects water with state destruction because the Fen River could threaten Anyi. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-03-weiju-hanhu-wujiandao-lkiwf0zdnylmaqjgsix-dktxea0l|Zhouji 03]]
- Secret alliance and battle action: Zhang Mengtan secures his agreement, after which Wei and Han attack Zhi's disordered force from both sides. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-03-weiju-hanhu-wujiandao-lkiwf0zdnylmaqjgsix-dktxea0l|Zhouji 03]]
