# 魏驹 / Wei Ju

## Overview
Wei Ju is the Wei-house leader who first concedes land to [[ZhiYao|Zhi Yao]] on [[RenZhangWarringStates|Ren Zhang]]'s advice, then moves from Zhi Yao's Jinyang coalition to the secret Wei-Han-Zhao counteralliance.

## Current Profile
When Zhi Yao demands territory without justification, Wei Ju initially wants to refuse. Ren Zhang persuades him that concession will increase Zhi Yao's arrogance and carelessness while aligning the coerced houses; Wei Ju therefore gives up a large city as a calculated delay. He later helps Zhi Yao besiege Zhao, but the water attack and Zhi Yao's remark about destroying states make Anyi's vulnerability concrete. He and [[HanKangzi|Han Hu]] recognize the danger before [[ZhangMengtan|Zhang Mengtan]] approaches them; the envoy succeeds by giving their existing fear a shared strategic formula. Wei Ju accepts the secrecy risk and joins the flank attack after Zhao reverses the flood.

## Key Characteristics
- Nominal ally whose cooperation rests on coercion rather than durable trust.
- Accepts a near-term territorial loss to worsen the aggressor's long-term strategic position.
- Reads a successful weapon as a future threat to his own base.
- Communicates suspicion covertly with Han Hu before open negotiation.
- Accepts a high-risk reversal when self-preservation incentives align.
