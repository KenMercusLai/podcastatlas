## Overview

废后许氏 / Empress Xu is synthesized as the deposed empress of [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] whose cold-palace isolation makes her vulnerable before it becomes fatal. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-556-lishi-hanjian-shui-yao-gei-chengdi-dai-lvmaozi-licvtkyvoh-p3ucobowldj6g-id|Hanji 556]] shows her seeking restoration through [[ChunyuZhangWesternHan|淳于长]] by way of her sister Xu Mi, paying heavily for promises that the host treats as empty. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-557-wangmang-pk-chunyuchang-nanrenjian-de-jingxin-jiaoliang-lnvmnizx0mhrln-g1p1a8u_9kgnx|Hanji 557]] then shows that same hidden connection becoming evidence in the case that ends with her forced poisoning.

## Current Profile

The sources use Empress Xu less as an independent political actor than as a trapped former insider whose remaining resources make her exploitable. Hanji 556 says she has lost ordinary access to Chengdi and therefore tries to act through Xu Mi and Chunyu Zhang. Her payments to Chunyu Zhang are explained through hope, narrowed channels, inability to verify his claims, and [[SunkCostEntrapment|沉没成本困局]]: the more she has paid, the harder it becomes to accept that the promised restoration will not arrive.

Hanji 557 then turns this vulnerable position into the tragic endpoint of a palace scandal. Her life remains intact until Chunyu Zhang's confession brings her back into the court's danger zone. The host emphasizes that she has endured a decade of lonely cold-palace life before Chengdi sends [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] with poison.

Empress Xu's profile therefore sits at the intersection of palace vulnerability and factional cleanup. Chunyu Zhang's behavior offends Chengdi's dignity, threatens the status of favored women such as [[ZhaoHedeWesternHan|赵合德]], and makes the former empress a liability rather than a forgotten survivor.

## Key Characteristics

- Former empress of Han Chengdi living in confinement before the case.
- Seeks a restoration channel through her sister Xu Mi and Chunyu Zhang after losing ordinary access to Chengdi.
- Pays repeated valuables for promises of renewed palace status.
- Becomes implicated through Chunyu Zhang's confession about insult and a promised left-empress restoration.
- Is poisoned by imperial order after the case reaches grave disloyalty.
- Functions in the episode as the most tragic personal cost of the factional case.

Cold-palace survival:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-556-lishi-hanjian-shui-yao-gei-chengdi-dai-lvmaozi-licvtkyvoh-p3ucobowldj6g-id|Hanji 556]] says Empress Xu has been cold-shouldered and hopes at least to regain some palace status.

Exploitative restoration channel:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-556-lishi-hanjian-shui-yao-gei-chengdi-dai-lvmaozi-licvtkyvoh-p3ucobowldj6g-id|Hanji 556]] says she reaches Chunyu Zhang through Xu Mi, gives money and valuables over years, and is kept engaged by promises that he can ask Chengdi for restoration.

Scandal exposure:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-557-wangmang-pk-chunyuchang-nanrenjian-de-jingxin-jiaoliang-lnvmnizx0mhrln-g1p1a8u_9kgnx|Hanji 557]] says Chunyu Zhang confessed to insulting her and promising to make her left empress.

Forced death:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-557-wangmang-pk-chunyuchang-nanrenjian-de-jingxin-jiaoliang-lnvmnizx0mhrln-g1p1a8u_9kgnx|Hanji 557]] says Chengdi sends Kong Guang with poison, after which Empress Xu dies.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 556 and Hanji 557. It does not reconstruct Empress Xu's earlier rise, deposition, full kin network, complete legal status, Xu Mi's full biography, or her own voice inside the case. Hanji 556's sunk-cost reading explains the host's interpretation of her continued payments; it does not prove her private psychology by itself.

## What Changed

- Added the pre-fatal vulnerability layer: Empress Xu's isolated restoration effort and repeated payments to Chunyu Zhang now explain why the later confession is both personally tragic and politically dangerous.
