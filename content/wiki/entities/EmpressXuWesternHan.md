## Overview

废后许氏 / Empress Xu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-557-wangmang-pk-chunyuchang-nanrenjian-de-jingxin-jiaoliang-lnvmnizx0mhrln-g1p1a8u_9kgnx|Hanji 557]] as the former empress of [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] whose cold-palace survival ends after [[ChunyuZhangWesternHan|淳于长]]'s case exposes insult, manipulation, and a promise to make her left empress.

## Current Profile

The source uses Empress Xu less as an independent political actor than as the tragic endpoint of a palace scandal. She has already been deposed and confined, but her life remains intact until Chunyu Zhang's confession brings her back into the court's danger zone. The host emphasizes that she has endured a decade of lonely cold-palace life before Chengdi sends [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] with poison.

Empress Xu's profile therefore sits at the intersection of palace vulnerability and factional cleanup. Chunyu Zhang's behavior offends Chengdi's dignity, threatens the status of favored women such as [[ZhaoHedeWesternHan|赵合德]], and makes the former empress a liability rather than a forgotten survivor.

## Key Characteristics

- Former empress of Han Chengdi living in confinement before the case.
- Becomes implicated through Chunyu Zhang's confession about insult and a promised left-empress restoration.
- Is poisoned by imperial order after the case reaches grave disloyalty.
- Functions in the episode as the most tragic personal cost of the factional case.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 557. It does not reconstruct Empress Xu's earlier rise, deposition, kin network, complete legal status, or her own voice inside the case.

## What Changed
