## Overview

乐昌侯王商 / Wang Shang is the Western Han official whose calm handling of the Chang'an flood rumor in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-505-xihan-de-huicheng-yaoyan-da-le-shui-de-lian-ljuwccxx_aexasafkpbl-y2rvos5|Hanji 505]] precedes his appointment as chancellor in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-514-da-xiao-wang-duijue-ni-cai-shui-neng-ying-lnlvthkkqbp_zo2lfpp4bqmh5nsu|Hanji 514]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-515-shi-shui-lan-xia-le-xihan-chengxiang-de-jubaoxin-lshhivowk9jpigkr7ssjiysefm6r|Hanji 515]] supplies the conflict itself: his attempted impeachment of [[YangRongWesternHan|杨荣]] is blocked by [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]], after which private-conduct accusations and continued political pressure end in removal and death.

## Current Profile

The three sources form a compact rise-and-fall sequence. Wang Shang first gains [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s confidence by refusing to let an unverified flood rumor become an official evacuation order. After becoming chancellor, he again insists on formal action by trying to impeach Yang Rong over the Langya disaster. This time his judgment collides with channel power: Wang Feng's marriage connection to Yang Rong and control of the Shangshu route keep the memorial from the emperor.

Hanji 515 then shows the contest moving away from Yang Rong's official responsibility. [[GengDingWesternHan|耿定]] accuses Wang Shang of sexual misconduct and possible involvement in a killing, while Zhang Kuang uses an eclipse to label him a harmful minister. Chengdi initially treats the private allegations as too uncertain, but later removes Wang Shang. The source says Wang Shang dies three days later after vomiting blood; it does not establish a covert killing or a single medical cause.

## Key Characteristics

- Chancellor distinguished explicitly from the Chengdu Hou Wang-family outer relative of the same name.
- Crisis judge who refuses to amplify an unverified Chang'an flood rumor.
- Senior accuser whose formal authority is defeated by control of the memorial channel.
- Political target attacked through private-conduct claims after the original policy dispute.
- Removed official whose eldest son retains the Lechang marquisate despite the wider household purge from palace service.

- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-515-shi-shui-lan-xia-le-xihan-chengxiang-de-jubaoxin-lshhivowk9jpigkr7ssjiysefm6r|Hanji 515]] says Geng Ding's private-conduct accusations are unverifiable and Chengdi initially resists treating them as sufficient.

- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-515-shi-shui-lan-xia-le-xihan-chengxiang-de-jubaoxin-lshhivowk9jpigkr7ssjiysefm6r|Hanji 515]] says Wang Shang is stripped of the chancellor's seal, dies three days later, and is succeeded as Lechang Hou by his eldest son Wang An.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 505, Hanji 514, and Hanji 515. The sources do not reconstruct Wang Shang's complete family line, office chronology, the legal standard for Yang Rong's responsibility, the full accusation record, Chengdi's final reasoning, or the cause of death. Hanji 515's explicit two-Wang distinction corrects the prior merged wiki identity but does not by itself settle every earlier unattributed “王商” reference outside this source set.
