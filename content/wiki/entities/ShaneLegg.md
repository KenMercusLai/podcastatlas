# Shane Legg

Shane Legg is one of the [[DeepMind]] cofounders in [[e226-liaoliao-deepmind-chuangshiren-hasabisi-yige-kexuejia-yu-shikong-de-ai-jingsai-7abda28b-99c6-4ebc-8c0d-37bcc77f6a73]]. The episode presents him as a connector between [[DemisHassabis]], Silicon Valley networks, and early AGI-oriented communities.

His role in the wiki is source-scoped: he makes the early [[ReinforcementLearningAGIPath]] and AGI movement more institutional. The source says he helped connect Hassabis to fundraising and attention channels that made a London AGI lab legible to nonstandard investors such as [[FoundersFund]].
