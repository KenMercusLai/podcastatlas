# Luxihe / 泸溪河

## Overview
Luxihe / 泸溪河 is a Chinese bakery chain used in the current source as a case of a vertical bakery brand professionalizing its management during a category reshuffle.

## Current Profile
The episode reports that Luxihe hired Domino's China's former chief marketing officer as chief growth officer and a former [[McDonalds|McDonald's]] China executive as general manager after a March funding round. It frames those moves through industry analysis that Chinese bakery chains have entered a deep reshuffle in which competition shifts from expansion at scale to fine-grained operations, with supermarkets, fresh-snack stores, and tea chains all entering the category. Store count is described as above 650 with the earlier pace of more than 100 new stores a year no longer continuing, and insiders say new management is developing a new store model even though most stores remain in malls.

## Key Characteristics
- Vertical bakery chain rather than a multi-category restaurant group.
- Store base above 650 with expansion pace slowed from its earlier level.
- Distribution concentrated in shopping malls, which the source treats as a weak fit for breakfast-frequency demand.
- Management being professionalized with executives imported from Western quick-service chains.
- Operating in a category being invaded by supermarkets, fresh-snack retailers, and tea chains.
- Financing and a new store model framed as the response to margin pressure rather than as growth capital alone.

## Qualifications
Store counts, funding timing, and the shifting executive roles are source-reported, and the episode does not give revenue, margin, or unit economics. The breakfast-and-community repositioning is presented as 推测 rather than a decided strategy.
