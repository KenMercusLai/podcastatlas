# 黄盖 / Huang Gai

黄盖 / Huang Gai enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-993-shentingling-zhizhan-sunce-dazhan-taishici-lrivpk7col2l7k-s4dqzxxp2kqp1|Hanji 993]] as one of the thirteen riders with [[SunCe|孙策]] during the [[ShentinglingBattle|神亭岭之战]] encounter. The source names him beside [[HanDang|韩当]], marking Sun Ce's small party as a serious military retinue rather than an ordinary patrol.

The page is source-scoped to that role. Hanji 993 does not develop Huang Gai's later career; it uses his presence to sharpen the contrast with [[TaishiCi|太史慈]], who has only one rider and still engages Sun Ce directly.
