# 黄盖 / Huang Gai

## Overview
黄盖 / Huang Gai is a Jiangdong veteran who appears first in [[SunCe|孙策]]'s compact Shentingling retinue and later as the officer who identifies the fire vulnerability in [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s linked fleet before [[RedCliffsBattle|赤壁之战]].

## Current Profile
The bounded evidence shows continuity from trusted retainer to tactical initiator. Hanji 993 names Huang Gai among Sun Ce's thirteen riders at [[ShentinglingBattle|神亭岭之战]], where his presence signals that Sun Ce's scouting party is a serious military group.

Hanji 1106 gives him an independent operational role under [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]]. He judges prolonged confrontation against a larger force undesirable, recognizes that Cao Cao's linked ships share a concentrated fire risk, prepares ten vessels with fuel and escape boats, and uses a false-surrender message to approach before the attack narrated in Hanji 1107.

## Key Characteristics
- Long-serving Jiangdong military retainer visible in Sun Ce's early field circle.
- Tactical observer who converts an enemy adaptation into an attack surface.
- Designer of a combined deception, fire-ship, wind, and withdrawal arrangement.
- Subordinate whose proposal becomes usable through Zhou Yu's command approval.

- Vulnerability diagnosis: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1106-caocao-zai-huarongdao-jingli-le-zenyang-de-juewang-lpsjmdpl6-2bd1qtda-egvx0zawv|Hanji 1106]] has Huang Gai identify the linked Cao fleet as suitable for fire attack.
- Operational design: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1106-caocao-zai-huarongdao-jingli-le-zenyang-de-juewang-lpsjmdpl6-2bd1qtda-egvx0zawv|Hanji 1106]] describes ten prepared vessels, combustible material, oil, stern escape boats, and a false-surrender message.

## Qualifications
The current sources do not provide a full biography. Hanji 993 only establishes early retinue membership, while Hanji 1106 stops before the fire attack occurs. Exact ship configuration, counts, dialogue, wind conditions, and Cao Cao's reason for accepting the surrender message remain source-scoped.
