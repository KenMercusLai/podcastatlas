# Krebs on Security

Krebs on Security is the cybersecurity publication associated with [[BrianKrebs]] in [[tech-20260415-0415-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260415-0415-mp-tech-pod-128]]. The episode says Krebs had recently written about several large botnets, including [[KimWolfBotnet|KimWolf]], before joining [[MarketplaceTech]] to explain the consumer-device risk.

In the wiki, the publication anchors a reporting branch around botnets and compromised consumer hardware. Its role in this source is not institutional analysis; it supplies the reporting context behind Krebs' explanations of [[IoTBotnetRisk]], [[PiratedStreamingBoxMalware]], and [[MaliciousProxyNetworks]].
