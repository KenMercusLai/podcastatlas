## Overview

杨荣 / Yang Rong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-515-shi-shui-lan-xia-le-xihan-chengxiang-de-jubaoxin-lshhivowk9jpigkr7ssjiysefm6r|Hanji 515]] as the Qi commandery governor whom [[WangShangChancellorWesternHan|乐昌侯王商]] tries to impeach after a large disaster in Langya.

## Current Profile

Yang Rong matters primarily as the protected official at the center of a channel-control case. The episode says he is connected to [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]] by marriage. Wang Feng first asks Wang Shang to treat the disaster as natural and stop the accusation, then intercepts the memorial when Wang Shang persists.

## Key Characteristics

- Qi commandery governor linked by marriage to Wang Feng.
- Intended target of Wang Shang's blocked impeachment.
- Source case showing how patronage can prevent a local-accountability claim from reaching the ruler.
