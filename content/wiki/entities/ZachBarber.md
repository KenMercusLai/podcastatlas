# Zach Barber

Zach Barber is the RMS U.S. CEO interviewed in [[what-makes-a-toy-go-viral]]. His account turns [[SquishyDumplings]] from a funny fad into a business case about product tweaking, surprise, scarcity, social-media distribution, and inventory discipline.

Barber's main product lesson is [[SmallIInnovation]]: a hit toy does not have to be a wholly new category if a company can adapt an existing idea to the right trend and buying ritual. In the source, he ties the dumpling's growth to [[MysteryToyPackaging]], [[CollectibleDropScarcity]], and [[TikTok]] unboxing behavior.

His operating lesson is [[ViralToySupplyChain]]. Barber says [[ReallyMagicalStuff|RMS]] works close to ship dates, keeps production schedules flexible, and avoids holding large U.S. inventory, preferring to refuse some demand rather than accept orders that would create dangerous stock exposure.
