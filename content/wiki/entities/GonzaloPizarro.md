# Gonzalo Pizarro

## Overview

Gonzalo Pizarro was a Spanish conquistador whose abuse of Manco Inca's household helped destroy collaboration before he fought in the defense of Cusco and was imprisoned by Diego de Almagro.

## Current Profile

In [[648-the-fall-of-the-incas-battle-for-the-sacred-city-part-5-glt6392035011]], Gonzalo's pursuit of Manco's wife and sister Cura Ocllo joins his brother Juan's kidnapping of Inquilcoya and the broader abuse of Manco's household. Manco's attempts to protect Cura Ocllo with silver and a disguised substitute fail, making Gonzalo a direct actor in the humiliation that precedes revolt.

Gonzalo later helps [[HernandoPizarro]] retake Sacsayhuaman during [[SiegeOfCusco1536|the siege of Cusco]]. The episode ends this phase with [[DiegoDeAlmagro]] taking the city and imprisoning both brothers.

## Key Characteristics

- Pizarro brother whose coercion of Manco's household contributed to political rupture.
- Participant in sexualized conquest and the pursuit of high-status Inca women.
- Defender of Cusco during Manco's uprising.
- Assault commander in the capture of Sacsayhuaman.
- Prisoner of Almagro after the return from Chile.

## Qualifications

The page is limited to the 1535–37 sequence and does not cover Gonzalo's later rebellion against the New Laws. The hosts' account of desire, contempt, and the political value of elite Inca women is interpretive; it does not reduce the coercion described.
