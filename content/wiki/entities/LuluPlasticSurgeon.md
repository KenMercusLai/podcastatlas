# Lulu (plastic-surgery doctor)

## Overview
Lulu is the plastic-surgery doctor interviewed in [[vol-159-cong-yi-mei-zhi-ming-dao-xianshi-zhengxing-yisheng-jiedu-juzhong-mei-xishuo-de-yimei-neimu-lksohygmb6epma5qgaf76pddx2fk]], a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode using [[YiMeiZhiMingDrama|《以美之名》]] to discuss medical-aesthetic safety and clinical judgment.

## Current Profile
The source presents Lulu as a clinician who can appreciate medical drama without mistaking it for instruction. She distinguishes plausible clinical mechanisms from dramatized details, then turns the cases into patient-facing boundaries around [[BotulinumToxinInjectionSafety]], [[UnapprovedMedicalAestheticProducts]], [[MedicalAestheticProviderVerification]], and [[CosmeticProcedureExpectationManagement]].

Her recurring position is that appearance medicine remains medicine. Product identity, dose, injection point, timing, institution type, anesthesia and monitoring support, prior treatment history, patient motivation, function, and complication severity all affect whether a requested intervention is reasonable.

## Key Characteristics
- Source-scoped plastic-surgery and medical-aesthetics guest.
- Separates credible medical-drama education from literal clinical instruction.
- Explains botulinum-toxin action and poisoning as dose-, site-, history-, and symptom-dependent questions.
- Warns against unapproved products and non-medical venues performing medical procedures.
- Treats aesthetic judgment as inseparable from anatomy, function, psychology, and patient safety.
- Supports redirecting, staging, or refusing requests whose risk outweighs likely benefit.
