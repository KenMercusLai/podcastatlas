# Texas Chicken / Church's Chicken

## Overview
Texas Chicken / Church's Chicken appears in the source as a U.S.-origin fried-chicken brand entering China through a local partner. The page uses the international Texas Chicken name because the episode says Church's Chicken commonly uses that name in overseas markets.

## Current Profile
The source says Church's Chicken announced China entry in May 2026 and planned 600 China stores. Its local partner is [[LangxingDingsheng|朗行鼎盛]], a restaurant operator whose portfolio and mall-site capability make the case less about brand import alone and more about partner-led execution.

The brand therefore helps define [[USFastFoodChinaExpansion]] as a franchise-and-operator story. It enters a market where fried chicken is familiar through KFC and other chains, so the challenge is not only awareness but price, product differentiation, site selection, and repeat demand.

## Key Characteristics
- U.S.-origin fried-chicken brand entering China under the overseas Texas Chicken identity.
- Source-scoped plan targets 600 China stores after a May 2026 entry announcement.
- China execution is tied to [[LangxingDingsheng|朗行鼎盛]] as local partner rather than only to global brand recognition.
- The case demonstrates how franchise-led expansion can amplify both speed and execution risk.
