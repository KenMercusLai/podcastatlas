## Overview

栾大 / Luan Da is a Western Han fangshi whose current sources trace an extreme court cycle: desire-aligned claims and a “斗棋” demonstration win initial trust; privileged access to immortals then wins rank, wealth, multiple seals, and marriage to [[WeiZhangPrincessWesternHan|卫长公主]]; nondelivery and a report contradicted by surveillance finally end in execution. The Hanji sources write his surname as “鸾”; this page uses the established “栾大” form while retaining the variant as a source-level caution.

## Current Profile

The broad fangshu survey places Luan Da among Qin-Han specialists who exploit distance, props, ambiguous proof, and a ruler's demand for death-defying certainty. It says he claims repeated maritime contact with immortals, receives offices and the 乐通侯 title from [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], marries the emperor's daughter, avoids an actual sea voyage, and fabricates an encounter after going to Taishan.

Hanji 344-1 adds the entry mechanics. Introduced by [[DingYiWesternHan|丁义]], Luan Da reads Wudi's desire for immortality, presents his own low rank as the obstacle to sacred access, and invokes the earlier killing of 少翁 to negotiate patience before accepting responsibility. When Wudi asks for proof, the episode says Luan Da makes two game pieces collide without being touched; the host speculates about hidden magnetism, but the mechanism is not established. The visible effect is enough to convert talk into court confidence and rapid titles.

Hanji 344-2 then makes the political conversion public. Ceremonial seals, a large household, an imperial marriage, a personal visit, and gifts from officials advertise Luan Da's access and encourage imitation by other fangshi. Yet the same elevated status makes indefinite delay dangerous. Once Wudi checks his movements, the claim that he met his teacher becomes a falsifiable false report, and the episode presents continued fabrication—not mere technical failure—as the immediate reason for waist-cutting.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han fangshi who gains access by aligning his claim with Wudi's immortality desire and regret over a previously killed practitioner.
- Court entrant who combines an unverifiable sacred promise with a visible “斗棋” demonstration.
- Specialist who treats higher rank and privileged reception as prerequisites for obtaining immortal teachings.
- Public favorite whose promotion creates a visible incentive for other fangshi to seek court access.
- Favored specialist whose repeated nondelivery erodes trust until surveillance turns sacred ambiguity into a checkable itinerary conflict and execution.

## Evidence

- **Court-fangshi pattern:** [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997]] places Luan Da among practitioners whose miracle promises receive imperial resources and says his fabricated travel claim is eventually discovered.
- **Entry, bargaining, and staged proof:** [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-344-1-ta-bushi-zhoujielun-que-ping-yichang-moshu-jiang-gongzhu-pian-shangchuang-lho5giyqfpigbvfhguckpnbsy6oc|Hanji 344-1]] reports Ding Yi's introduction, Luan Da's low-status argument, his use of 少翁's death to seek protection, and the “斗棋” display that wins Wudi's confidence.
- **Patronage and public authority:** [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-344-2-hanwudi-dangnian-weihe-yaozhan-qinnvxu-llmavvgeaaxwcsz_pxirfulbfpaa|Hanji 344-2]] reports the 乐通侯 title, household rewards, six seals, imperial marriage, personal visit, and wider court gift-giving.
- **Failure, verification, and punishment:** [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-344-2-hanwudi-dangnian-weihe-yaozhan-qinnvxu-llmavvgeaaxwcsz_pxirfulbfpaa|Hanji 344-2]] says repeated failure precedes a monitored trip, a false teacher-meeting report, and execution in 112 BCE.

## Qualifications

All three sources are podcast summaries, and one is a broad thematic survey rather than an independent primary-source study. They do not establish supernatural access, the mechanism of “斗棋,” the exact audience dialogue, every title or seal, the itinerary, the surveillance record, legal procedure, or whether every failure involved deliberate fraud. The Hanji titles' sexualized princess framing is absent from 344-1's supplied body and is not evidence about the later marriage. Material totals, dates, inner reactions, and quoted causal formulas remain source-attributed. Public imitation is a plausible consequence reported by the episode, not a complete prosopography of coastal fangshi.

## What Changed

- The profile now distinguishes three stages: desire-aligned entry and staged proof, public certification through patronage, and monitored collapse after nondelivery.
- Luan Da's status demand and use of 少翁's death now appear as deliberate risk negotiation before the later accountability trap closes.
