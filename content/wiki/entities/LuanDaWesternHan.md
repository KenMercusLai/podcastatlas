## Overview

栾大 / Luan Da is a Western Han fangshi whose current sources trace an extreme court cycle: claims of privileged access to immortals win rank, wealth, multiple seals, and marriage to [[WeiZhangPrincessWesternHan|卫长公主]], but nondelivery and a report contradicted by surveillance end in execution. The requested source writes his surname as “鸾”; this page uses the established “栾大” form while retaining the variant as a source-level caution.

## Current Profile

The broad fangshu survey places Luan Da among Qin-Han specialists who exploit distance, props, ambiguous proof, and a ruler's demand for death-defying certainty. It says he claims repeated maritime contact with immortals, receives offices and the 乐通侯 title from [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], marries the emperor's daughter, avoids an actual sea voyage, and fabricates an encounter after going to Taishan.

Hanji 344-2 makes the political conversion more concrete. Wudi's patronage is not only private belief: ceremonial seals, a large household, an imperial marriage, a personal visit, and gifts from officials make Luan Da's access publicly visible and encourage imitation by other fangshi. Yet the same elevated status makes indefinite delay dangerous. Once Wudi checks his movements, the claim that he met his teacher becomes a falsifiable false report, and the episode presents that continued fabrication—not mere technical failure—as the immediate reason for waist-cutting.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han fangshi presented as claiming access to immortals and their teachings.
- Court entrant whose unverifiable promise is converted into unusual rank, wealth, seals, and imperial kinship.
- Public favorite whose promotion creates a visible incentive for other fangshi to seek court access.
- Specialist whose repeated nondelivery gradually erodes the ruler's trust.
- Subject of imperial surveillance that turns an inaccessible sacred claim into a checkable itinerary conflict.
- Executed after the episode says he falsely claimed to have met his teacher.

## Evidence

- **Court-fangshi pattern:** [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997]] places Luan Da among practitioners whose miracle promises receive imperial resources and says his fabricated travel claim is eventually discovered.
- **Patronage and public authority:** [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-344-2-hanwudi-dangnian-weihe-yaozhan-qinnvxu-llmavvgeaaxwcsz_pxirfulbfpaa|Hanji 344-2]] reports the 乐通侯 title, household rewards, six seals, imperial marriage, personal visit, and wider court gift-giving.
- **Failure, verification, and punishment:** [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-344-2-hanwudi-dangnian-weihe-yaozhan-qinnvxu-llmavvgeaaxwcsz_pxirfulbfpaa|Hanji 344-2]] says repeated failure precedes a monitored trip, a false teacher-meeting report, and execution in 112 BCE.

## Qualifications

Both sources are podcast summaries, and one is a broad thematic survey rather than an independent primary-source study. They do not establish supernatural access, the mechanics of any performance, every title or seal, the exact itinerary, the surveillance record, legal procedure, or whether every failure involved deliberate fraud. The Hanji source's material totals, dates, and quoted causal formula remain source-attributed. Public imitation is a plausible consequence reported by the episode, not a complete prosopography of coastal fangshi.
