# Anchorage Press

## Overview

The Anchorage Press is the Alaska newsroom represented in the episode by executive editor [[NathanielHerz]].

## Current Profile

The source positions the newsroom as a human-edited contrast and potential user of carefully bounded AI. Herz says it increasingly uses AI behind the scenes and is discussing ethical boundaries, while maintaining that investigation, accountability, prioritization, and the human stakes of stories require editorial responsibility.

## Key Characteristics

- Participates in Alaska's local-news ecosystem alongside automation-first experiments.
- Uses some AI in backstage newsroom work rather than presenting it as a replacement for reporting.
- Is described as actively considering which AI uses are ethical.
- Retains human judgment as the safeguard for public-interest and accountability work.
