# 夏馥 / Xia Fu (late Han)

## Overview

Xia Fu is the late-Han figure who criticizes [[ZhangJianLateHan|张俭]]'s flight for spreading danger to helpers, then withdraws by disguise and hard labor.

## Current Profile

Hanji 895 presents Xia Fu as the episode's stern counterweight to the moving Kong-family rescue story. He does not deny the human desire to live, but he objects that Zhang Jian's survival path can implicate many innocent households. Xia Fu then embodies his own standard by erasing his appearance, hiding in remote conditions, working in a metal-smelting household, and refusing his brother Xia Jing's money because help itself might become danger.

## Key Characteristics

- Moral critic of fugitive flight when survival shifts punishment onto other households.
- Withdrawal actor who uses disguise, remote labor, and anonymity as self-protection.
- Anti-implication figure who rejects family assistance to keep danger from following the helper.
- Source-scoped comparison for [[CrisisWithdrawalSelfProtection|危局抽身自保]] under party-prohibition pressure.
