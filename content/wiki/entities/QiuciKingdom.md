## Overview

龟兹 / Qiuci Kingdom is a Western Regions polity whose current wiki role is to show how submission, imposed pro-Han kingship, Han protectorate placement, and regional rivalries shaped Eastern Han frontier control after [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 764 supplies the earliest current Qiuci layer. Qiuci, Gumo, and Wensu submit to Han, which leads the court to reestablish the Western Regions Protector and Wuji Colonel offices and appoint Ban Chao as Protector. [[YaoGuangLateHan|Yao Guang]] escorts the hostage prince Ba Ba back toward Qiuci; Ban Chao and Yao Guang then coerce Qiuci, depose the old king Youliduo, install Ba Ba, and move Youliduo to [[LuoyangLateHan|Luoyang]] as a hostage. Ban Chao's protectorate is placed at Qiuci's Taqian City, giving Qiuci early administrative centrality in the restored Han Western Regions order.

Hanji 790 first uses Qiuci as part of a regional balance field: [[ShuleKingdom|Shule]] becomes strong enough to balance Qiuci and [[KhotanKingdomLateHan|Khotan]] after succession and alignment shifts under renewed Northern Xiongnu suzerainty. In that layer, Qiuci matters as one of the major Western Regions powers affected by Han withdrawal and attempted re-entry.

Hanji 779 gives Qiuci the more concrete early layer. The episode says Qiuci initially remains loyal to Han because Ban Chao had previously installed the pro-Han king Ba Ba. After [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]]'s harsh governance triggers Western Regions rebellion, [[DuanXiLateHan|Duan Xi]] and [[LiangJinLateHan|Liang Jin]] move the protectorate seat into Qiuci. That move creates a new local-elite problem: Qiuci nobles resent the protectorate presence and join with Gumo and Wensu forces to attack the city. Liang Jin defeats the attack, but the court still recalls the Western Regions officials and troops.

## Key Characteristics

- Major Western Regions polity in the Hanji 764-790 frontier field.
- Early submitter whose surrender helps justify reestablishing Han Western Regions offices.
- Kingdom where Ban Chao and Yao Guang depose Youliduo and install the pro-Han hostage prince Ba Ba.
- Protectorate host whose administrative centrality begins with Ban Chao's Taqian City placement.
- Initially pro-Han in Hanji 779 because Ban Chao had installed Ba Ba as king.
- Protectorate host whose local elite reaction shows that allied territory can still resist intrusive Han office placement.
- Regional balancing actor beside Shule and Khotan in later Western Regions debates.

## Qualifications

The page does not reconstruct Qiuci's full history or the internal politics of Ba Ba, Youliduo, Gumo, and Wensu. The current evidence is focused on Eastern Han frontier control as represented by three podcast source notes. Qiuci's pro-Han king does not mean all Qiuci elites accepted a Han protectorate seat inside the city.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 764 as the early submission, Ba Ba installation, and protectorate-placement layer.
- Created the page from Hanji 779 and Hanji 790 to make Qiuci a durable Western Regions node rather than leaving it as repeated source-scoped background.
