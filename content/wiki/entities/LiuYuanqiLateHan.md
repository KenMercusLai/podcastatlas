## Overview
刘元起 / Liu Yuanqi enters the wiki as a Liu-clan relative who repeatedly supports the young [[LiuBei|刘备]] and helps make elite study possible after Liu Bei's household falls into poverty.

## Current Profile
The bounded source identifies Liu Yuanqi as the father of Liu Deran and a relative from Liu Bei's wider lineage. He supports Liu Bei from the study period into adulthood, which the episode translates into the modern language of an early “angel investor.” His importance lies less in a single gift than in sustained backing before Liu Bei holds office, territory, or proven military power.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides repeated material support to a poorer younger relative.
- Helps convert clan membership into access to [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]]'s educational network.
- Backs Liu Bei before later success makes support politically rewarding.
