# 芈八子 / 宣太后 / Xuan Taihou

芈八子 / 宣太后 / Xuan Taihou appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-58-zhaowulingwang-ruhe-dazao-qiangguo-lq9mgpib0whh0w-jkv0kxcafbnnz]] as the mother of Ying Ji, the future [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]]. The episode names her briefly while explaining how Qin handles the succession after [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] dies without a son.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-59-yichao-junwang-yichaochen-ljq-qkueix0rnzfekzuadicce1bh]] keeps her in that same threshold role while adding [[WeiRan|魏冉]]. The episode says Wei Ran uses hard measures to make his nephew Ying Ji succeed after Qin Wuwang's brothers fight for the throne, tying Xuan Taihou's branch to Qin's first post-Wuwang power struggle.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-60-zhongguo-di-yi-wei-bachi-chaozheng-de-taihou-jingshi-ta-ltazis9qo0dwvwmzxikk8mmpvosc]] turns that threshold into the central story. Because [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] is young, Xuan Taihou holds court power, hands major authority to Wei Ran, promotes her kin and the ruler's brothers, and shapes early Qin policy through [[QueenMotherRegency|太后摄政]] and [[ForeignRelativeCourtPolitics|楚系外戚政治]]. The episode also makes her diplomatic effect concrete: she arranges a Chu bride for Qin Zhaoxiang Wang, warms [[QinState|秦国]]-[[ChuState|楚国]] relations, and helps move Qin away from Han and Wei until the [[MiHeng|芈横]] hostage incident breaks the alignment.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-62-ji-er-kuang-ni-jiujiu-de-tui-a-lumch-ibj8m94abjh5nvr3qhiqff]] uses Xuan Taihou indirectly as the kinship premise behind [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]'s fatal confidence. Chu Huaiwang believes [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]], as Xuan Taihou's son and therefore his nephew, is unlikely to trap him. The source turns that assumption into a limit case for [[ForeignRelativeCourtPolitics|楚系外戚政治]]: maternal Chu ties can shape Qin diplomacy, but they do not prevent Qin from detaining the Chu king when state advantage is available.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-85-xuantaihou-weihe-sitong-30-nian-hou-ansha-le-laoqingren-lg7zy6vqtdjcufou4-mox5qkg2ct5]] returns to Xuan Taihou as a direct actor on Qin's northern frontier. The episode says she maintained a roughly thirty-year relationship with the [[YiquState|义渠]] king and had two sons by him, while Yiqu did not raid Qin's rear during Qin's major campaigns. Because the sources cannot prove motive, the page records the strategic effect without claiming certainty about affection versus calculation. When Qin's timing matures, Xuan Taihou lures the Yiqu king to [[GanquanPalace|甘泉宫]], kills him, and lets [[QinState|秦国]] destroy Yiqu and create [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]], making her the source case for [[IntimateFrontierPacification|亲密关系式边患安抚]].
