# 芈八子 / 宣太后 / Xuan Taihou

芈八子 / 宣太后 / Xuan Taihou appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-58-zhaowulingwang-ruhe-dazao-qiangguo-lq9mgpib0whh0w-jkv0kxcafbnnz]] as the mother of Ying Ji, the future [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]]. The episode names her briefly while explaining how Qin handles the succession after [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] dies without a son.

This page should stay source-scoped for now. The episode uses her as a marker of the next Qin power configuration, not yet as a full narrative about regency, court politics, or Qin policy.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-59-yichao-junwang-yichaochen-ljq-qkueix0rnzfekzuadicce1bh]] keeps her in that same threshold role while adding [[WeiRan|魏冉]]. The episode says Wei Ran uses hard measures to make his nephew Ying Ji succeed after Qin Wuwang's brothers fight for the throne, tying Xuan Taihou's branch to Qin's first post-Wuwang power struggle.
