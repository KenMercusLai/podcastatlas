# Benjamin Todd

Benjamin Todd is the author of [[EightyThousandHours|80,000 Hours]] in [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]]. The source presents his book as more helpful than passion-centered advice because it starts from uncertainty, job availability, skill development, and social usefulness.

Todd's advice is summarized as a move from [[PassionTrapCareerAdvice]] to [[UsefulWorkCareerCompounding]]. Rather than treating pre-existing passion as the starting point, he argues that people should get very good at something that helps others and let passion follow. The source also notes that his impact frame draws on [[EffectiveAltruism]] and can include fields such as pandemic prevention and AI regulation.
