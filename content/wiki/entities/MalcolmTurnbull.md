# Malcolm Turnbull

## Overview

Malcolm Turnbull was an Australian prime minister, lawyer, and republican campaigner who displaced Tony Abbott but was himself removed after about three years.

## Current Profile

[[189-australian-prime-ministers-bob-hawke-scott-morrison-part-3-glt2222829202]] presents Turnbull as a socially liberal, cosmopolitan contrast to [[TonyAbbott|Abbott]]. An Oxford graduate and prominent lawyer in the Australian *Spycatcher* litigation, he later chaired the republican campaign in the 1999 referendum. His replacement of Abbott appeared to shift conservative leadership toward liberal republicanism, but Abbott's faction helped remove him and [[ScottMorrison|Scott Morrison]] succeeded him.

## Key Characteristics

- Oxford-educated Australian lawyer and politician.
- Prominent participant in the Australian *Spycatcher* case.
- Leading republican and chair of the 1999 referendum campaign.
- Socially and culturally more liberal than Tony Abbott.
- Became prime minister by displacing Abbott.
- Was removed after roughly three years and succeeded by Scott Morrison.
