# 子阳 / Ziyang of Zheng

子阳 / Ziyang of Zheng appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-1-lvkp-ju7pf5z5cz3thojv3s2fkyx]] as the [[ZhengState|郑国]] prime minister killed by the Zheng ruler. The episode treats his death as one of the political killings behind the title's assassination theme.

The source says Ziyang's faction later kills the Zheng ruler and installs [[ZhengKangGong|郑康公]]. That sequence makes Ziyang a focal point for [[SpringAutumnToWarringStatesPoliticalViolence]]: court factions no longer merely exile or sideline rivals, but answer one killing with another.
