# Happy Monday Club

## Overview
Happy Monday Club is a newsletter and resource channel associated with [[WorkshopInternalComms|Workshop]]. In the available source, [[RickKnudtson]] presents it as a pre-product audience-building effort for internal communications practitioners.

## Current Profile
Happy Monday Club functions in the wiki as a compact example of [[AudienceBeforeProductMarketing]]. It gave Workshop a way to serve and learn from a target practitioner community before the product thesis had stabilized, and later became part of Workshop's community-led growth surface.

## Key Characteristics
- Launched from the earliest days of Workshop as a resource for internal communicators.
- Publishes practical content, templates, examples, and educational materials rather than only product marketing.
- Reportedly reached roughly 50,000 subscribers by the time of the episode.
- Helped Workshop learn practitioner language, build trust, and sustain demand generation during the product-market-fit search.
- Anchors Rick Knudtson's advice that founders should start marketing before they have something ready to sell.

## Evidence
- Timing and audience: [[rick-knudtson-workshop-the-email-signal-he-ignored-for-9-months]] says the newsletter started on day one and targeted internal communications practitioners.
- Scale and channel role: [[rick-knudtson-workshop-the-email-signal-he-ignored-for-9-months]] reports roughly 50,000 subscribers and connects the newsletter to webinars, expert collaboration, and resources.
- Strategic meaning: [[rick-knudtson-workshop-the-email-signal-he-ignored-for-9-months]] uses Happy Monday Club to argue for marketing and audience development before direct selling.

## Qualifications
The current evidence is self-reported by Workshop's founder in one podcast episode. Subscriber count, conversion impact, and attribution to sales are not independently verified in the wiki.

## What Changed
- Added Happy Monday Club as the audience and community artifact supporting Workshop's go-to-market.
- Connected the newsletter to audience-before-product marketing and community-led SaaS growth.
