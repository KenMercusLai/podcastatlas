# Major Projects Office (Canada)

## Overview
Major Projects Office (Canada) is represented in the wiki as [[MarkCarney]]'s approval-speed response to trade unpredictability and the desire for nationally important infrastructure.

## Current Profile
The episode says Carney opened a Major Projects Office to speed nationally important infrastructure projects through approvals. Its source role is tied to the oil segment: Canada had new [[TransMountainExpansion]] capacity, oil remained exempt from many American tariffs, and the trading relationship with the [[UnitedStates]] had become less predictable.

The office therefore appears as a governance mechanism for [[CanadianOilSandsBoom]] and [[TradeDiversificationStabilityParadox]]. It reflects the source's claim that Canada wants greater energy independence and export optionality, while needing approval processes that can deliver infrastructure quickly enough to matter.

## Key Characteristics
- Federal infrastructure-approval mechanism described in the Canadian oil segment.
- Connected to trade unpredictability rather than only domestic project management.
- Uses energy infrastructure as the immediate example of why faster approvals may matter.
- Extends Mark Carney's wiki profile from tariff retaliation into project-delivery politics.
