# 百度贴吧 / Baidu Tieba

Baidu Tieba / 百度贴吧 is the keyword-driven community case in [[no-212-cengjing-de-jingshen-jiaoluo-douban-zhihu-tieba-hupu-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-25-1003510980]]. The episode places Tieba inside [[Baidu]]'s PC-era traffic loop: users searched for a star, school, novel, game, place, or interest and could enter a corresponding "bar" without a platform editor first designing a formal community map.

For the wiki, Tieba is a strong example of [[KeywordCommunityArchitecture]]. The same architecture made long-tail gathering cheap and legible, but also made governance hard at scale. The source describes both the upside, including Super Girl fan bars, Li Yi Bar, "屌丝", "弱智吧", Jia Junpeng, and novel/game subcultures, and the downside: raids, spam, pornography, gambling, scams, hidden old posts, and creator asset loss.

The episode also turns Tieba into a [[PlatformCommunityGovernance]] warning. Baidu's later commercial pressure, including more ads, bar sales, KPI demands on bar owners, and official bar-owner interventions, weakened community legitimacy because old users felt that a relationship space had been treated as inventory.
