# Li Auto ONE / 理想 ONE

## Overview

Li Auto ONE / 理想 ONE is the first production vehicle discussed in the bounded account of [[LiAuto|理想汽车]]'s family-SUV and [[RangeExtendedEV|range-extension]] strategy.

## Current Profile

The source presents ONE as a combined solution to several early market constraints: six-seat household travel, discomfort with MPV symbolism, a middle-premium price target, new-energy policy, sparse charging, and long-trip replenishment anxiety. The product's significance is therefore its joined definition rather than range extension in isolation.

The same source makes ONE a quality-correction case. [[LiXiangLiAuto|李想]] acknowledges early suspension-joint strength and sunroof leakage problems, attributing them to limited new-company experience and high novelty while describing testing and component replacement as remedies. That explanation records the company's response but does not independently settle severity, responsibility, or user impact.

## Key Characteristics

- Six-seat family SUV built around household travel rather than a generic technology showcase.
- Range-extended EV chosen to preserve electric drive while reducing early charging anxiety.
- Product-positioning case combining space, price, energy route, and family identity.
- Early quality-learning case involving suspension and water-ingress problems.
- Source-scoped success story whose sales and remediation claims require independent evidence.
