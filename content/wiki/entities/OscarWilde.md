# Oscar Wilde / 王尔德

Oscar Wilde enters the wiki through [[60-xianliao-weida-zuojiamen-de-bagua-di-yi-dan-648693204]], where [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] uses him as the opening case for literary celebrity, persecution, style, and posthumous reputation. The source presents him as a writer whose aesthetic performance, sexuality, courtroom disaster, prison suffering, illness, exile, and tomb afterlife all became part of the public story around his work.

The episode keeps Wilde inside [[AuthorMythDeflation]] rather than pure martyrdom or pure scandal. His dorm-room peacocking, bullying by schoolmates, relationship with Alfred Douglas, prosecution under an unjust sexual regime, and later poverty in France make his image both glamorous and materially painful.

The Paris tomb segment makes Wilde useful for [[LiteraryGossipAsContext]]. The episode treats vandalism, later fan kisses, and restored memorial symbolism as signs that an author's public meaning changes after death as sexual politics and literary celebrity cultures shift.

[[10-wang-erde-qiyi-gushiji-yong-zui-hui-sahuang-de-zuichun-qinwen-tianshi-545332195]] shifts Wilde from gossip case to close-reading case. With translator [[FeiBi|菲比]], the episode reads [[WildeStrangeStories|《王尔德奇异故事集》]], [[DeProfundis|《自深深处》]], [[LordArthurSavilesCrime|《阿瑟·萨维尔勋爵的罪行》]], [[ThePortraitOfMrWH|《W.H.先生的画像》]], and [[TheCantervilleGhost|《坎特维尔的幽灵》]] through social performance, [[LifeImitatesArt]], [[ArtAgainstMoralInstruction]], [[FictionalEvidenceSeduction]], and [[BlackComicMoralInversion]].
