# Kristin McLean

Kristin McLean is the toy-industry analyst quoted in [[what-makes-a-toy-go-viral]]. She supplies the category context around [[SquishyDumplings]], saying 2026 had been a strong year for toys and that squishy toys had grown sharply since January.

McLean connects the broader squishy, sensory, slimy, and fidget-toy category to customers looking for playfulness, joy, calm, and relief from anxiety. Her role in the source is to keep the dumpling case from looking like pure randomness: [[TikTok]] may have accelerated the hit, but the toy also sat inside a wider sensory-toy trend.

She also identifies Beanie Babies as an earlier model for limited editions, special releases, and timed drops, linking the source's [[CollectibleDropScarcity]] discussion to a longer toy-industry pattern.
