# 王累 / Wang Lei (Late Han)

## Overview

王累 / Wang Lei enters the current evidence as a Guanghan official who uses a dramatic physical protest at [[Chengdu|成都]] to oppose [[LiuZhang|刘璋]]'s invitation of [[LiuBei|刘备]] into [[YizhouLateHan|益州]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 1124 says Wang Lei hangs himself upside down from a Chengdu gate to urge Liu Zhang not to invite Liu Bei. The protest follows [[HuangQuanLateHan|黄权]]'s rejected strategic warning and demonstrates that opposition inside Liu Zhang's administration is not absent; the ruler has received the danger signal in more than one form but does not change course.

The episode gives Wang Lei's action symbolic force but little wider biography. His durable role in the current wiki is therefore limited to high-cost dissent at the invitation stage.

## Key Characteristics

- Opposes bringing Liu Bei into Yizhou before the protector gains a local base.
- Uses bodily risk and public spectacle to make an ignored warning harder to overlook.
- Shows that Liu Zhang's decision persists despite repeated internal opposition.

## Evidence

- Gate protest: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1124-liuzhang-qing-liubei-ru-shu-shi-qiusheng-haishi-songsi-lkzau4jsd4ocgkgw8cg9jjwshhlm|Hanji 1124]] says Wang Lei hangs upside down at a Chengdu gate while remonstrating against Liu Bei's invitation.
- Decision outcome: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1124-liuzhang-qing-liubei-ru-shu-shi-qiusheng-haishi-songsi-lkzau4jsd4ocgkgw8cg9jjwshhlm|Hanji 1124]] says Liu Zhang refuses to reverse course despite the protest.

## Qualifications

The source does not provide a full biography, the precise mechanics or duration of the protest, Wang Lei's exact office, or the wording of his remonstrance. The dramatic scene requires direct textual corroboration and should not be expanded into claims about all of his political views.

## What Changed

- Created as the high-cost dissenter who opposes Liu Bei's invitation after Huang Quan's warning fails.

- [[Chengdu|成都]] - setting of Wang Lei's public protest.
