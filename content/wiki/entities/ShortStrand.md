# Short Strand

Short Strand appears in [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] as the Catholic enclave in East [[Belfast]] where some of the fiercest fighting occurred after [[OrangeOrder]] parades on 27 June 1970. The episode describes armed [[ProvisionalIRA]] members confronting loyalists and the [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]] around St Matthew's Church, with about 30 injured and three killed.

The source treats Short Strand as a credibility-making event for the Provisionals. The fight made the organization appear to many Catholics as a force willing to defend vulnerable districts, helping shift republican legitimacy after the earlier weakness of the old IRA.
