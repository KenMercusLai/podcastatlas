# Ricky

In [[153-quanqiu-hongguan-he-ziben-shichang-2026-zhanwang-da-nian-zhihou-reng-shi-da-nian-lupeqjdszon-wp5zdq06w3ustw2d]], Ricky returns for the 2026 annual macro and capital-market outlook. He describes 2025 as a capital-market and asset-management big year while stressing [[ChinaEquityRealEconomyGap]], then leans risk-on for 2026 through A/H exposure, especially H shares, provided investors watch [[CentralBalanceSheetDemandSupport]], [[ChinaDeflationDemandRepair]], RMB appreciation, AI capex confirmation, gold sizing, and [[ChinaEquityStructuralSelection]] rather than assuming another broad beta year.

Ricky is the guest in [[vol-106-dibai-zhende-biandi-shi-huangjin-lpxchutxf7yomjdfykwzvvnmbaq8]], where he speaks with [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] after a two-week visit to [[Dubai]] and [[AbuDhabi|Abu Dhabi]]. His perspective is finance-oriented: the episode follows what he heard from bankers, developers, brokers, lawyers, and businesspeople about capital flow, residency, real estate, crypto, and Chinese outbound companies.

Ricky's role in the wiki is as a short-field-visit observer, not a definitive authority on the UAE. He repeatedly qualifies his claims, especially around [[DubaiRealEstateMarket]], [[DubaiGoldenVisaResidency]], and investment opportunities, by saying listeners should discount the observations and seek correction from people who know the Middle East better.

In [[vol-115-quanqiu-hongguan-he-ziben-shichang-2025-zhanwang-duanqi-wenti-bu-jiejue-jiu-meiyou-zhongqi-he-changqi-le-lrfl2e5nuf7-ubq-shybqytjhuhx]], Ricky returns as the macro and investing guest. He frames China's 2025 policy challenge through [[ShortTermDemandBeforeLongTermReform]], fiscal transmission, [[RMBExchangeRatePolicy]], and an [[AHShare2025Barbell]] combining [[DefensiveDividendAssets]] with [[NewQualityProductiveForces]], while treating U.S. mega-cap technology as a right-side but increasingly belief-heavy trade.

[[133-quanqiu-hongguan-he-ziben-shichang-2025-nianzhong-pandian-zhongguo-de-sange-wencha-he-meiguo-de-sange-yuqicha-lhj-qcswiuqskvzn-f693vti6xsc]] adds Ricky's mid-year update to that framework. He emphasizes the split between ordinary and professional investor experience, Hong Kong's stronger first-half China-asset role, fiscal-space preservation, RMB-first rate constraints, continued gold preference, and the risk that private-credit or credit-like products only reveal volatility under stress.
