## Overview

卫长公主 / Princess Wei the Elder is presented in Hanji 344-2 as the eldest child of [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] and [[EmpressWeiZifuWesternHan|卫子夫]], the widow of [[CaoXiangWesternHan|曹襄]], and the imperial daughter later married to [[LuanDaWesternHan|栾大]]. Her role in the current evidence is primarily relational: the second marriage demonstrates how far Wudi converted confidence in a fangshi into dynastic trust.

## Current Profile

The episode says her birth rebutted rumors that Wudi could not have children and treats her “长公主” title as an exceptional elevation for an imperial daughter. It describes Cao Xiang as her first husband and reports that they had a son before his death. Wudi then gives her in marriage to Luan Da with a very large gold transfer. Because Luan Da is executed within roughly a year, the marriage functions in the episode as the sharpest measure of his rapid rise and collapse, not as a full biography of the princess.

## Key Characteristics

- Imperial daughter identified by the source as the first child of Han Wudi and Wei Zifu.
- Princess whose elevated title is presented as evidence of exceptional paternal favor.
- Widow of Cao Xiang and reported mother of a son.
- Later wife of Luan Da in a marriage used to seal extraordinary imperial confidence.
- Figure whose own views and later life are absent from the supplied source.

## Qualifications

The current profile rests on one short structured podcast summary. It does not independently verify the title history, birth-rumor story, marriage dates, gold amount, age at widowhood, the son's name and life, or the princess's own agency. The page therefore uses her marriage to Luan Da only as evidence of Wudi's patronage decision, not as evidence that she shared his beliefs or approved the match.
