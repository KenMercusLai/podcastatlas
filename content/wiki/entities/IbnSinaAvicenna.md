## Overview

Ibn Sina / Avicenna / 伊本·西那 appears as an internal critic of alchemical transmutation in the episode's account of Islamic intellectual history.

## Current Profile

Against theories that one metal could be converted into another by rearranging qualities, the source attributes to Ibn Sina the view that alchemists can alter appearance without changing a substance's underlying kind. His role is therefore methodological as much as biographical: visible color, texture, or surface change does not by itself prove the stronger claim of genuine transmutation.

## Key Characteristics

- Supplies an internal rather than modern external critique of alchemy.
- Distinguishes altered appearance from altered substance.
- Shows that Islamic-world alchemical thought contained disagreement over what material operations could establish.
