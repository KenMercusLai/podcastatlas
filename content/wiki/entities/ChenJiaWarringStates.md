# 陈甲 / Chen Jia (Warring States)

陈甲 / Chen Jia appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-49-mengzi-yue-wo-jiao-ni-zhiguo-ni-que-ba-wo-dang-huaping-lhy14pbf9thcfdiaulcmf8dwcef8]] as the minister or emissary who goes to [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] after [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]] regrets not following Mencius's advice about [[YanState|燕国]].

His role is rhetorical. By asking whether even [[ZhouGong|周公]] made mistakes when Guan Shu rebelled, Chen Jia tries to create a less humiliating frame for Qi Xuan Wang's failure. Mencius accepts the possibility of understandable error but turns the conversation toward [[CorrectableLeadershipError|犯错能改的领导力]]: the problem is not only making a mistake, but refusing to correct it.
