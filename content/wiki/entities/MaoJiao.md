# 茅焦 / Mao Jiao

茅焦 / Mao Jiao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-108-1-lishi-gan-dangmian-dingzhuang-qinshihuang-diyi-ren-lg0dssyicp387foiraohkv1x5aij]] as a [[QiState|齐国]] guest who asks to remonstrate with [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] after the [[LaoAi|嫪毐]] crisis. The source says twenty-seven people have already been killed for advising about [[ZhaoJi|赵姬]], and Mao Jiao presents himself as willing to become the twenty-eighth, linking the death count to the twenty-eight lunar mansions.

His argument is direct and political. Mao Jiao tells Ying Zheng that tabooing death does not make people immortal and tabooing state failure does not make a state survive. He then names the ruler's actions - killing Lao Ai, killing the two younger sons, confining his mother, and killing remonstrants - as behavior that will make people stop turning toward Qin. In the source, this courage breaks the feedback barrier: Ying Zheng raises him to upper minister and brings Zhao Ji back from [[YongchengQin|雍城]] to [[Xianyang|咸阳]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-108-3-huangtaihou-he-qingfu-laoai-qingse-beihou-de-qishi-lthiqkpc0nbnlkxhcmivufvgl25b]] repeats Mao Jiao's aftermath role from a more family-restoration angle. After the Lao Ai purge and Lu Buwei's removal, the episode says Mao Jiao's advice leads Ying Zheng to bury the twenty-seven killed remonstrants, go personally to Yongcheng, and restore Zhao Ji to the palace under the "复为母子如初" formula.
