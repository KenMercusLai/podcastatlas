# Katerra

## Overview
Katerra was a technology-driven construction company that pursued vertical integration and off-site production before filing for bankruptcy in 2021, as summarized in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-76efcd195d1b26a4faab0a64b31c1972-52966951-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-76efcd195d1b26a4faab0a64b31c1972-52966951]].

## Current Profile
The episode uses Katerra as a mixed institutional case. More than $800 million in reported [[SoftBank]] backing enabled factories, projects, and rapid experimentation, but did not overcome the attempt to remake too many linked parts of an old, fragmented industry at once. Bankruptcy ended the firm, while trained specialists, demonstrated foreign methods, and factories reused by others may have left residual industry capacity.

## Key Characteristics
- Pursued a vertically integrated, technology-centered construction model.
- Used abundant capital to accelerate factories, projects, and organizational expansion.
- Faced operational complexity, fragmented regulation, and insufficiently stable demand.
- Failed as a firm while potentially leaving skills, demonstrations, and physical capacity behind.
