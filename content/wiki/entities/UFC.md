# UFC

UFC appears in [[e237-yangshi-he-fifa-tanpan-fenzheng-beihou-tiyu-saishi-zhuanboquan-de-boyi-yu-shengyi-c3607c08-6eb6-48bd-8250-b41b5b8272e6]] as one of the U.S. top-rights examples where live sports prices continue to rise. The episode contrasts China's more cautious post-bubble market with American rights inflation, naming UFC's newer media-rights cycle alongside the [[NationalFootballLeague|NFL]] and [[NationalBasketballAssociation|NBA]].

The wiki role of UFC here is comparative. It helps show that rights inflation can still be rational when a market has strong paid viewing habits, intense streaming competition, and platforms that treat live sports as retention and subscription infrastructure.
