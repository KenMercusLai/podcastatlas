## Overview
Snackish is [[TaraBosch]]'s high-protein potato-chip venture introduced in [[advice-line-with-tara-bosch-of-smartsweets-8aab80a1-b0c5-4103-b875-f5d4823d07ce]].

## Current Profile
The proposed product occupies a gap between conventional chips that deliver emotional and sensory satisfaction without much functional nutrition and protein-oriented chips that may sacrifice taste. Snackish is therefore an early expression of taste-first functional positioning rather than evidence of proven product-market fit.

## Key Characteristics
- Aims to offer high protein in a familiar potato-chip experience.
- Was inspired in part by Bosch observing her young daughter's attraction to chips.
- Treats flavor, texture, and emotional appeal as adoption requirements rather than secondary benefits.
- Remains an early venture whose commercial performance is not established by the source.
