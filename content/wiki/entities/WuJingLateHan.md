# 吴景 / Wu Jing (late Han)

吴景 / Wu Jing (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] as [[SunCe|孙策]]'s maternal uncle and the immediate public reason Sun Ce gives [[YuanShu|袁术]] for leaving Yuan Shu's platform. Wu Jing is attacking [[HengjiangLateHan|横江]] but cannot break through, so Sun Ce asks to help him and then recruit soldiers in his home region.

The source makes Wu Jing's problem a launch channel rather than only a family detail. Helping Wu Jing gives Sun Ce a legitimate mission, while success at Hengjiang becomes the first crossing-point opening for the later Jiangdong base.
