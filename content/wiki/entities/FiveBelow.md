# Five Below

Five Below is the inexpensive impulse-retail channel named in [[what-makes-a-toy-go-viral]]. [[ReallyMagicalStuff|RMS]] had difficulty getting into major U.S. retailers, so [[ZachBarber]] focused on lower-price stores where small toys could be bought with less deliberation.

In the source, [[FiveBelow]] matters because the channel fits the behavior around [[SquishyDumplings]]: a roughly five-dollar toy, a chance of rare variants, and the fear that stores might run out all support repeat trips. The retailer therefore connects [[MysteryToyPackaging]] and [[CollectibleDropScarcity]] to [[RetailShelfPlacement]], [[RetailInventoryVelocity]], and low-friction [[CustomerPull]].
