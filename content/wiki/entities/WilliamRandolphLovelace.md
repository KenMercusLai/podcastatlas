# William Randolph Lovelace

William Randolph Lovelace appears in [[omission-accomplished-why-the-iran-war-cycle-spins-on-6a6c7475aa1642a30f17f1cb]] as the doctor whose program tested women for space flight in the early space age. [[WallyFunk]] joined the program at 21 and, according to the episode, took the same tests as men.

The source uses Lovelace's program as evidence that the women had been medically and physically tested rather than merely aspiring to fly. The later exclusion of Funk and the other women therefore becomes a [[GenderBarrierSpaceflight]] case.
