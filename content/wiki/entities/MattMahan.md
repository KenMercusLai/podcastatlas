# Matt Mahan

## Overview
Matt Mahan is the mayor of [[SanJoseCalifornia|San Jose]] and a Democratic candidate for governor of [[California]] whose supplied interview centers on measurable outcomes, administrative accountability, affordability, and basic public services.

## Current Profile
Mahan presents himself as a working-class Californian, former Teach for America teacher, civic-technology entrepreneur, and local executive. His political identity is deliberately heterodox: he remains a Democrat while criticizing public-sector union influence, trial-lawyer incentives, wealth taxes, state single-payer proposals, regulatory accumulation, and performative conflict from both parties.

His statewide case depends on transferability. He argues that San Jose's dashboards, lower-cost shelter, housing approvals, and program reallocation show that government can improve outcomes without continually raising revenue. The source supports a coherent campaign profile, not independent verification that every municipal result was caused by his policies or would scale statewide.

## Key Characteristics
- Performance-first Democrat who treats public goals, cost metrics, audits, and consequences for failed programs as the core governing system.
- Local-government executive using [[SanJoseCalifornia|San Jose]] as the main evidence for a statewide campaign.
- Supply-oriented housing reformer who emphasizes zoning, permitting, fees, codes, litigation, and construction liability.
- Interventionist on severe addiction and mental illness, including support for involuntary treatment under defined public-harm conditions.
- Fiscal and regulatory moderate who opposes a state wealth tax and state-run universal healthcare while acknowledging inequality and access problems.
- Pragmatic immigration voice combining border security and removal of violent offenders with legal status for long-settled nonviolent residents.
