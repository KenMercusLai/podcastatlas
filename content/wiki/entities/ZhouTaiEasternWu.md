## Overview

[[ZhouTaiEasternWu|周泰]] is the Eastern Wu commander whom [[SunQuan|孙权]] appoints to hold Ruxu after the 217 CE Cao-Sun confrontation.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1157 presents Zhou Tai's authority as initially contested by officers with stronger pedigree or more visible reputation. Sun Quan answers at a banquet by having Zhou Tai identify the scars received while protecting him during an earlier Xuancheng attack, then publicly explains that the appointment repays demonstrated life-preserving service.

The episode's durable contribution is organizational rather than a complete biography: the ruler turns private knowledge of sacrifice into shared evidence, allowing formal rank to acquire recognition among subordinates.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Wu commander appointed to hold the Ruxu defense.
- Presented as lacking the pedigree that made rank immediately acceptable to some peers.
- Carries extensive wounds attributed to repeated protection of Sun Quan.
- Receives explicit public backing from the ruler.
- Becomes a case of command authority made legible through shared evidence.
