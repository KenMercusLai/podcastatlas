## Overview

魏征 / Wei Zheng enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-467-guanchang-li-jiang-zhenhua-shuo-shihua-you-duo-nan-ls_zlyqolvpfttspah8wery4e3fs|Hanji 467]] as the positive contrast to [[GongYuWesternHan|贡禹]]'s selective truth-telling. The episode invokes him as a Tang official whose direct remonstrance became a model of loyal speech under [[EmperorTaizongOfTang|唐太宗]].

## Current Profile

The source does not build a full Wei Zheng biography. It uses him as a public-duty exemplar: an official should not keep dangerous words in the throat when crisis is visible. His later remembrance through Taizong's mirror metaphor gives the episode a ruler-side condition as well: direct remonstrance becomes durable when the ruler can receive it.

## Key Characteristics

- Tang official used as a model of direct remonstrance.
- Cited for treating unspoken warning as disloyal when danger is visible.
- Remembered through Emperor Taizong's mirror metaphor after his death.
- Functions as a contrast case to true but evasive speech.
