# Luo Yonghao

## Overview
Luo Yonghao is represented as the founder of [[Smartisan]] and host of [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]]. Across the evidence set, his throughline is public explanation under pressure: founder taste meets manufacturing limits, while interview framing turns craft, status, debt, relationships, emotion, AI, mortality, film, and music into explicit disagreements about value and responsibility.

## Current Profile
The smartphone-history source makes Luo a case for both the cultural power and operating limits of a founder-centered product narrative: design, interface taste, and launch attention could not by themselves solve supply-chain, yield, quality, and delivery problems. His later panels reuse personal experience—including debt repayment, ADHD, appearance, reviews, blocking, marriage, aging, and AI use—as material for examining public responsibility rather than presenting a comprehensive biography.

As an interviewer, Luo is persistent, category-driven, and willing to embody the audience's objection. With [[ShougongGeng|手工耿]], he separates entertainment, playful function, and practical function; in comedy panels he converts jokes into status, money, relationship, emotional, and technology dilemmas; with film guests he presses production and market constraints; with [[CaiKangyong|蔡康永]] he exposes his own anxiety about aging and AI. The Cui Jian interview adds a mature fan's challenge: Luo argues for original arrangements, sing-along legibility, and official live releases, while allowing Cui's answers to recast nostalgia as a question of artistic authority and continuing creation.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder and presenter whose Smartisan history shows both the force and execution limits of taste-led consumer technology.
- Long-form interviewer who turns biography and work practice into questions about operating logic, value, and responsibility.
- Panel moderator who uses counterfactuals, disagreement, and humor to clarify social and ethical tradeoffs.
- Public figure willing to use personal debt, health labels, appearance, conflict, marriage, aging, and fear as discussion evidence.
- Audience advocate who can press creators on usability, accessibility, familiar form, release strategy, and commercial follow-through.
- Source-scoped figure whose teaching, livestream operations, full business history, and broader media career remain outside the bounded evidence set.

## Qualifications
The evidence mixes one secondary smartphone history with self-presented interviews and comedy panels. Debt figures, medical and treatment comments, relationship choices, conflict stories, AI forecasts, concert preferences, and personal memories remain source-scoped. The sources illuminate recurring public roles but do not establish a complete or neutral biography.
