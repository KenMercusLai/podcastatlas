# Alfred Terry

## Overview

Alfred Terry was the U.S. Army commander whose Dakota column joined [[JohnGibbon]] before he released [[GeorgeArmstrongCuster]] and the Seventh Cavalry toward the [[BattleOfLittleBighorn]].

## Current Profile

The episode places Terry inside [[PhilipSheridan]]'s three-column plan. After a winter advance failed, his summer column moved west from Fort Abraham Lincoln and met Gibbon's force on the Yellowstone. Their information suggested that a large Native village lay near the Rosebud or Little Bighorn, but neither commander knew that [[GeorgeCrook]] had been stopped at the [[BattleOfRosebud]].

Terry sent [[MarcusReno]] to locate the village and report rather than fight, then released Custer after Reno returned. His written instructions directed Custer along the Rosebud and toward cooperation with Terry and Gibbon, yet allowed him to attack if he reached the village first and judged it prudent. The episode treats that flexibility as both conventional discretion and political cover for the aggressive conduct Terry and other officers expected.

## Key Characteristics

- He commanded one of the separated columns in a campaign dependent on convergence.
- His situational picture lacked Crook's defeat and withdrawal.
- He used reconnaissance to locate rather than immediately engage the village.
- His orders to Custer combined a cooperative plan with substantial tactical discretion.
- He expected Custer to move quickly and probably reach the village first.

## Qualifications

- This profile is limited to one popular-history episode's campaign reconstruction.
- The exact legal and military meaning of Terry's written orders would require the original text and wider command correspondence.
- Describing the orders as “cover” is the hosts' interpretation of incentives and expectations, not direct evidence of Terry's private intent.
- The source does not establish what Terry would have ordered had he known Crook was no longer advancing.
