# Xu Huazhe

## Overview
Xu Huazhe is a robotics scientist and founder tracked first through [[Xinghaitu]] and later through [[PokeRobotics]]. The wiki treats him as one of the clearest advocates for household robots and [[PhysicalAGI]] rather than only industrial or body-hardware robotics.

## Current Profile
Xu's profile now has three layers: his departure from Xinghaitu, his own Poke Robotics household-robot thesis, and his response to Astra in the four-company debate. He credits general models with stronger semantic and spatial understanding while arguing that complex contact, task success, and deployment economics remain the decisive robotics frontier.

## Key Characteristics
- Emphasizes [[PhysicalAGI]] over humanoid form factor alone.
- Argues that household robots force generalization, active agency, safety boundaries, and product definition.
- Prefers [[RobotActiveUseMetrics]] over shipment counts or staged demos.
- Sees AI-native robotics as different from traditional robotics decomposition and self-driving-style scene closure.
- Treats complex physical contact as a remaining defensible frontier even after strong general-model progress.
- Is skeptical that raw robot-data hours alone guarantee capability.

## Qualifications
Xu's Physical AGI and household-robot timeline remains a founder forecast. The new Astra and data-scale claims are grounded in source-reported tests and industry observation, not a public benchmark.
