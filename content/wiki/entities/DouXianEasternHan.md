## Overview

窦宪 / Dou Xian now appears as the He-era outer-relative powerholder whose northern military victory, court dominance, frontier proposal, palace removal, and aftermath form one connected sequence. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-764-ta-weile-huoming-jing-jianjie-mie-le-luoma-diguo-lpk0c6muneabhk1oonzyjvawwf4|Hanji 764]] supplies the earlier layer: Dou defeats the [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]], regains high position, and turns victory into a patronage machine that intimidates officials. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-765-donghan-fengyun-nengchen-nengbuneng-gandiao-bahu-waiqi-ltnvdqnyofuelaklssbquguwivan|Hanji 765]] supplies the policy layer: Dou urges [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]] to recognize [[YuChuNorthernXiongnu|Yu Chu / 于除]] as a Northern Xiongnu chanyu despite Yuan An and Ren Kui's credibility, Wuhuan-Xianbei, and fiscal objections. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-766-lishi-jiaogei-ni-weishenme-yao-guyi-fa-piqi-lrs-mguyfmnxe970hdwjvmevl8oc|Hanji 766]] then supplies the pre-collapse layer, and Hanji 767 shows the consequences: [[LiHeEasternHan|Li He]] avoids a gift connection, [[BanGuEasternHan|Ban Gu]] dies during the Dou-family cleanup, and Yu Chu abandons the Dou-backed Northern Xiongnu arrangement after Dou loses power.

## Current Profile

Hanji 764 presents Dou Xian before the recognition dispute and palace operation. His defeat of the Northern Xiongnu is treated as real frontier achievement, but the episode's judgment is that military merit does not cancel the political danger of arrogance after victory. Dou's postwar bloc uses [[GengKuiLateHan|Geng Kui]] and [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]] as armed agents, Deng Die and Guo Huang as trusted confidants, and Ban Gu and Fu Yi as literary staff. Appointments across provinces, commanderies, and counties flow through his recommendation network, creating corruption and extraction around his household.

The same source also shows how resistance forms before the later palace cleanup. Yuan An and Ren Kui impeach dozens of Dou-linked officials. [[LeHuiEasternHan|Le Hui]] exposes officials and advises the emperor's maternal uncles to yield power, but Dou pressures local authorities after Le Hui retires and Le Hui dies by suicide. [[HanLengEasternHan|Han Leng]] blocks a proposed "wansui" salute to Dou, refusing to let court ritual treat a minister like the ruler. This makes Dou Xian's power problem visible through patronage, retaliation, and ritual hierarchy before the later coup mechanics appear.

Hanji 765 presents Dou Xian as a frontier-policy sponsor before the palace crisis is visible. After the Northern Xiongnu chanyu disappears, Dou recommends treating Yu Chu's submission as a Southern Xiongnu-style recognition package: install him as chanyu and supervise him through Han command. Yuan An and Ren Kui object that this would betray the older Southern Xiongnu settlement, anger Wuhuan and Xianbei because of their conflict with Youliu chanyu's line, and add a costly new client beyond existing frontier burdens. Emperor He still accepts Dou's proposal, and the source later treats Yu Chu's rebellion as confirmation that the objections were substantive.

Hanji 766 then presents Dou Xian before and during the collapse. His outer-relative status and Northern Xiongnu military achievement have made his household a shadow authorization center: new governors and two-thousand-shi officials visit the Dou residence before taking office. Opposition from [[YuanAnEasternHan|Yuan An]], [[RenKuiEasternHan|Ren Kui]], [[ZhouRongEasternHan|Zhou Rong]], and [[DingHongEasternHan|Ding Hong]] shows that the court can still name the danger, but the emperor cannot safely reach outside ministers and must plan through [[ZhengZhongEasternHan|Zheng Zhong]] and [[LiuQingQingheKing|Liu Qing]].

The same source makes the fall operational. Emperor He closes palace gates, mobilizes palace guards and northern army command, executes immediate palace conspirators, removes Dou Xian's general-in-chief seal, demotes him to Champion Marquis, and sends the Dou brothers to their fiefs. [[ZhangPuEasternHan|Zhang Pu]] later persuades the emperor to spare [[DouGuiEasternHan|Dou Gui]], making the cleanup severe but not wholly indiscriminate.

Hanji 767 then presents the social and frontier aftershocks. Commanderies and kingdoms had been sending gifts when Dou took a concubine, and the Hanzhong administrator is afraid not to participate. Li He judges Dou as arrogant and unvirtuous, predicts disaster, and delays the gift mission until Dou's family falls. Ban Gu is swept into prison after an older private grievance becomes actionable during the arrest of Dou-family guests. On the frontier, Dou had installed Yu Chu as a Northern Xiongnu remnant leader and planned to escort him back to the royal court, but Yu Chu repudiates the arrangement after learning Dou has been removed.

## Key Characteristics

- Northern-campaign victor whose military achievement is politically overshadowed by postwar arrogance and domination.
- Outer-relative powerholder whose household becomes an unofficial authorization and recommendation center.
- Patronage operator whose armed agents, confidants, writers, and appointment channels create corruption and extraction.
- Frontier-policy sponsor whose Yu Chu recognition plan prevails despite ministerial credibility, neighbor-reaction, and fiscal objections.
- Target of ministerial opposition, memorial criticism, ritual boundary-setting, omen-coded warning, and Emperor He's secret palace planning.
- Fallen general-in-chief whose seal removal turns formal title into unusable authority.
- Patron whose collapse triggers a purge of officials and guests linked to him.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 764, Hanji 765, Hanji 766, and Hanji 767 and still does not reconstruct Dou Xian's full biography, family rise, or relationship with Empress Dowager Dou. Hanji 764 supplies only a summarized view of Dou's Northern Xiongnu victory and a qualified long-range migration association; it should not be used as proof that Dou directly destroyed Rome. Hanji 766 contains a local tension: it compresses the Dou brothers as secretly forced to die after reaching their fiefs and later says Dou Gui survives after Zhang Pu's memorial. The wiki records Dou Gui's survival as the specific resolved case.
