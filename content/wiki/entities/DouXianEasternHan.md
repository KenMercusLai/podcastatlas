## Overview

窦宪 / Dou Xian enters the wiki here as the fallen He-era outer-relative powerholder whose collapse turns gifts, private grievance, and frontier agreements into political exposure. Hanji 767 sees him mainly through the consequences of his fall: [[LiHeEasternHan|Li He]] avoids a gift connection, [[BanGuEasternHan|Ban Gu]] dies during the Dou-family cleanup, and [[YuChuNorthernXiongnu|Yu Chu]] abandons a Northern Xiongnu return arrangement after Dou loses power.

## Current Profile

The source presents Dou Xian as already powerful and dangerous at the moment of collapse. Commanderies and kingdoms are sending gifts when he takes a concubine, and the Hanzhong administrator is afraid not to participate. Li He judges Dou as arrogant and unvirtuous, predicts disaster, and then delays the gift mission until Dou's family falls.

Dou's fall also reshapes other fields. Ban Gu is swept into prison after an older private grievance becomes actionable during the arrest of Dou-family guests. On the frontier, Dou had installed Yu Chu as a Northern Xiongnu remnant leader and planned to escort him back to the royal court, but Yu Chu repudiates the arrangement after learning Dou has been removed.

## Key Characteristics

- Outer-relative powerholder whose social orbit makes gift exchange politically dangerous.
- Fallen patron whose collapse triggers a purge of officials who had interacted with him.
- Court danger node through which private grievance can become public punishment.
- Northern-frontier actor whose earlier Yu Chu arrangement unravels after his removal.
- Negative reference point for judging Li He's political timing and Emperor He's post-Dou consolidation.
