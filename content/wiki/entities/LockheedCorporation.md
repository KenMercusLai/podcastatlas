# Lockheed Corporation

Lockheed Corporation appears in [[vol-268-liang-ge-lao-si-lai-si-1003563933]] as the U.S. aerospace and defense company trying to re-enter civil airliners through the [[LockheedL1011TriStar|L-1011 TriStar]]. The episode says Lockheed chose [[RollsRoyce|Rolls-Royce]] as the exclusive engine supplier for the TriStar, making the aircraft's market promise depend heavily on the [[RollsRoyceRB211|RB211]] engine.

The source uses Lockheed to show that contract allocation does not eliminate engineering interdependence. When RB211 slipped, Lockheed could not simply switch engines because the aircraft had been designed around RB211. That turned the aircraft into an [[AirframeEngineLockIn]] case and helped push Lockheed toward a U.S. government loan-guarantee fight.

Lockheed later becomes the source's corruption branch. The episode says pressure to win overseas aircraft orders contributed to payments through intermediaries in [[Japan]], making [[LockheedBriberyScandal]] and [[OverseasBriberyExportSales]] part of the same historical tail as the TriStar and RB211.
