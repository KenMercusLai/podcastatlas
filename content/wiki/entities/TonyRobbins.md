# Tony Robbins

## Overview
Tony Robbins enters the current wiki evidence as an early educational influence, later friend, and business partner of [[DeanGraziosi]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Robbins through Graziosi's recollection rather than a direct interview. Graziosi says an infomercial introduced him to Robbins' programs and to the idea of using negative experience as fuel rather than an anchor. A later brief meeting expanded into a day-long conversation and friendship; the pair reportedly waited about three years before forming business ventures in coaching education and practical AI use.

Within this bounded profile, Robbins' main role is relational and developmental: he supplies an early framework for adversity, later asks Graziosi what kind of father, husband, leader, and man he wants to become, and becomes a partner only after trust develops.

## Key Characteristics
- Presented as a formative self-development teacher in Graziosi's entrepreneurial path.
- Frames adversity as usable fuel and identity as an intentional future choice.
- Develops a friendship with Graziosi before entering a business partnership.
- Participates in ventures that combine established business education with practical AI use.
