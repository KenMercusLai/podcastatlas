# 陆康 / Lu Kang (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-932-weishenme-ren-yue-hao-yue-nan-dang-daguan-lvwzel782wcxs1lpt3rg2vmkdy29|Hanji 932]] backfills an earlier remonstrance layer before Lu Kang's Lujiang branch. As Le'an commandery administrator, Lu Kang opposes [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s added palace-construction and bronze-statue tax, but the eunuch-side interpretation around [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]] and [[ZhaoZhongLateHan|赵忠]] turns the memorial into disrespect toward the emperor.

That earlier branch makes Lu Kang a case of clean official exposure before the Yuan Shu conflict. He is imprisoned under the commandant of justice and is spared after [[LiuDaiLateHan|刘岱]] defends him, but he is still sent home, showing how [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]] and [[PalaceConstructionFeeExtraction|修宫钱转嫁盘剥]] punish even bounded fiscal remonstrance.

陆康 / Lu Kang (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] as the [[LujiangCommandery|庐江郡]] administrator who refuses [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s demand for thirty thousand hu of grain. The source explains his refusal through several layers: Lu Kang is tied to the Wu commandery Lu clan, remains a Han official, faces a huge extraction demand, and judges Yuan Shu's conduct and reputation as illegitimate.

Lu Kang is also the trigger for [[SunCe|孙策]]'s second broken-promise grievance. Yuan Shu sends Sun Ce to attack Lujiang and promises the commandery if Sun Ce succeeds. After Sun Ce captures it, Yuan Shu appoints [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]] instead, turning Lu Kang's resistance and Sun Ce's victory into a major case of [[PromiseCredibilityTalentRetention|承诺信用与人才流失]].
