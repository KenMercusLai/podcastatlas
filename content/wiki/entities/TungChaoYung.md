# Tung Chao-yung / 董浩雲

## Overview
Tung Chao-yung / 董浩雲 appears as the shipping magnate father of [[TungCheeHwa|董建華]] in the Tung obituary source.

## Current Profile
The source uses Tung Chao-yung primarily as a family and business-origin node. His status as "world shipping king" gives Tung Chee-hwa a merchant-elite background, while the later [[OrientOverseas|東方海外]] debt crisis becomes the source's bridge from family shipping capital to Beijing-linked political realignment.

## Key Characteristics
- Shipping magnate whose family moved from Shanghai to Hong Kong.
- Father of Tung Chee-hwa.
- Background figure behind the Tung family's Taiwan, U.S., and Hong Kong business networks.
- Indirect source of the business-elite identity later valued in Tung's chief-executive selection.

## Evidence
- Family origin: [[dong-jianhua-shishi-aiguozhe-yuanlao-de-xianggang-rensheng-7d743a8515cf65334add1a55cd20148e]] says Tung Chee-hwa was born in Shanghai in 1937 as Tung Chao-yung's eldest son and moved to Hong Kong in 1948.
- Business-political bridge: [[dong-jianhua-shishi-aiguozhe-yuanlao-de-xianggang-rensheng-7d743a8515cf65334add1a55cd20148e]] connects the family's shipping company crisis to Tung Chee-hwa's later political turn.

## Qualifications
The source gives only a brief family-history sketch. Tung Chao-yung's independent shipping career is not yet synthesized beyond its relevance to Tung Chee-hwa.

## What Changed
- Created the family-background node for the Tung branch.
