# Chris Riccobono

Chris Riccobono is the co-founder of [[UNTUCKit]] and the [[HowIBuiltThis]] Advice Line guest in [[advice-line-with-chris-riccobono-of-untuckit-c087247f-1f85-4c15-b4aa-4ffea0368c4c]]. The episode presents him as an apparel founder who built from a narrow product insight, then had to manage a more mature brand through COVID debt, high interest rates, tariffs, and a move into wholesale.

His Advice Line recommendations emphasize focus, cash, channel fit, and proof. For [[AeroShorts]], he pushes [[AdrianAlvarez]] to sharpen the customer tribe and differentiate beyond price; for [[SnugSafety]], he treats radio, earned media, and trusted intermediaries as better fits than default paid social; for [[HockeyNinja]], he argues that safety equipment needs visible professional credibility.
