# Sébastien Lecornu

## Overview

Sébastien Lecornu is the French prime minister in the episode's account of nationwide secondary-school protests.

## Current Profile

Lecornu's response combines a limited listening intervention with concern that a school dispute could become a national political movement. He announced a half-day suspension of normal classes so students could use councils and headteacher discussions to present grievances. The measure addresses communication inside schools, but the source does not show that it resolves the wider pressures around public spending, policing, living costs, petrol prices, or presidential politics.

## Key Characteristics

- Leads the government response described in the school-protest segment.
- Uses school councils and headteacher discussions as a de-escalation channel.
- Faces criticism from opposing factions over both violence and policing.
- Treats wider national mobilization as a material political risk.
