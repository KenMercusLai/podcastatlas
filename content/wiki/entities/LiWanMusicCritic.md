# 李皖 / Li Wan (music critic)

## Overview
李皖 is presented as a music critic, scholar, long-term listener, and participant-observer of Chinese rock's development from the 1980s to the present.

## Current Profile
His account combines personal listening memory with a historical framework. He treats Chinese rock as a local cultural response shaped by social opening, individual awakening, media scarcity, peer exchange, industry infrastructure, and shared historical feeling. His standards emphasize truthful personal expression, the ability to voice an era, and emotional force grounded in meaningful content.

## Key Characteristics
- Critic who uses lived listening history while distinguishing memory from a complete social history.
- Interpreter of rock through truth, historical experience, and emotional force rather than genre style alone.
- Analyst of media and industry conditions from radio and copied tapes to record companies and downloads.
- Qualified advocate for contemporary Chinese rock who separates mass attention from artistic quality.

## Qualifications
The source is a structured podcast summary centered on Li Wan's retrospective judgments. It does not independently verify chronology, industry causation, comparative artistic quality, or international reception, and it underrepresents regional, female, post-2000, and younger-listener perspectives.
