# Mithridates VI / 米特里达梯六世

## Overview

Mithridates VI / 米特里达梯六世 is the king of Pontus whose invasion of Roman Asia creates the lucrative eastern command that [[GaiusMarius]] and [[LuciusCorneliusSulla]] contest in episode 702.

## Current Profile

The source presents Mithridates as prepared, informed, and alert to Greek resentment against Roman rule in Asia. While Rome is still strained by the [[SocialWar]], [[ManiusAquillius]] helps provoke conflict, Mithridates invades Asia, Roman power collapses locally, and Aquillius flees.

Mithridates matters in the wiki less as a full Pontic biography than as the external enemy whose war becomes domestic political prize. Roman elites read the eastern campaign as both revenge and opportunity: glory, money, and prestige are expected to flow to whoever receives the command.

## Key Characteristics

- Mithridates is portrayed as a prepared ruler who understands Roman weakness during the Social War.
- His invasion succeeds partly because Greek communities in Asia resent Roman and Italian exploitation.
- The collapse of Roman power in Asia threatens Roman and Italian business interests.
- The war against him is treated in Rome as attractive because it promises glory and wealth.
- His campaign turns a foreign war into the immediate object of Marius-Sulla rivalry.

## Qualifications

The current page covers Mithridates only as episode 702 uses him: the Asian war trigger and command prize. It does not settle the wider chronology of the Mithridatic Wars, Pontic policy, or the massacre and settlement traditions associated with later narratives.
