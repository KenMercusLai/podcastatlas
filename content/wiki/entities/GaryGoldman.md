# Gary Goldman

## Overview

Gary Goldman is a filmmaker and screenwriter represented here through an unsuccessful Zootopia authorship claim and his development of [[TheVariableManFilm|The Variable Man]], an unproduced [[PhilipKDick]] adaptation co-written with [[AngusFletcher]].

## Current Profile

The Zootopia sources present Goldman as the creator of an animal-world project called Zootopia who submitted an outline to Disney in 2009, later identified similarities in Disney's film, and lost a seven-year legal fight. The episodes distinguish failure to establish infringement from proof about whether Disney used the pitch. Their reading of Gary De'Snake and the stolen-invention plot in *Zootopia 2* offers Goldman possible symbolic recognition, not legal or material remedy.

Goldman's adaptation and development practice appears most directly in [[the-variable-man-with-gary-goldman-and-angus-fletcher-development-hell-the-variable-man-with-gary-goldman-and-angus-fle|The Variable Man]]. His earlier *Total Recall* experience suggests that long-troubled projects can move when star leverage, a director's vision, and a rewrite align. His own AI-centered project attracted interest but lacked a director whom investors trusted to carry a roughly $120 million production, showing that an experienced screenwriter's conceptual authority may not translate into financeable directorial authority.

## Key Characteristics

- Screenwriter associated by the bounded sources with multiple Philip K. Dick adaptations.
- Co-creator of an AI-era adaptation that resists both intuitive heroism and automatic machine deference.
- Uses earlier development experience to diagnose why praised screenplays remain unproduced.
- Originator of the Zootopia project described in the podcast's appropriation account.
- Unsuccessful claimant whose grievance is later interpreted through possible symbolic acknowledgment.
- Creator whose professional experience does not by itself eliminate legal, institutional, or financing dependence.

## Evidence

### Adaptation and development practice

- [[the-variable-man-with-gary-goldman-and-angus-fletcher-development-hell-the-variable-man-with-gary-goldman-and-angus-fle]] associates Goldman with four Dick adaptation projects and describes his role in solving a late-stage problem on *Total Recall*.
- [[the-variable-man-with-gary-goldman-and-angus-fletcher-development-hell-the-variable-man-with-gary-goldman-and-angus-fle]] attributes *The Variable Man*'s continued nonproduction chiefly to the absence of a trusted director for a large, difficult film.

### Authorship dispute

- [[zootopia-exposed-part-one-zootopia-exposed-part-one]] says Goldman devised Zootopia around 2000, gave Disney a 2009 outline for *Looney*, and later lost three rounds of litigation because the similarities did not establish copyright infringement.
- [[zootopia-exposed-part-two-zootopia-exposed-part-two]] restates the pitch and later links Goldman's grievance to Gary De'Snake's stolen family invention.

### Film interpretation and response

- [[zootopia-exposed-part-one-zootopia-exposed-part-one]] joins Gary's name, appearance, Louisiana-associated setting, and stolen invention into a possible allusion while preserving coincidence, mockery, apology, and internal maneuver as alternatives.
- [[zootopia-exposed-part-two-zootopia-exposed-part-two]] reports that Goldman found the Bush-family restoration reading meaningful, sent [[JaredBush]] a letter, and received no reply.

## Qualifications

The current profile is source-scoped and does not adjudicate infringement, appropriation, screenplay quality, or the causes of nonproduction. The underlying pitches, complete court record, screenplays, contracts, attachments, budgets, financing records, and filmmaker testimony are absent. Failure to prove infringement is not a finding that Disney used the pitch, while symbolic resemblance cannot establish intent or substitute for legal or material restitution. Goldman's explanation of *The Variable Man*'s failure may coexist with script, market, cost, timing, or other production concerns.
