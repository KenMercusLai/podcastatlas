# Patrick Rooney

Patrick Rooney appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] as the nine-year-old killed in his bed when [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]] armoured cars fired heavy machine guns into Divis flats during the August 1969 Belfast violence. The episode uses his death as a marker of how street conflict had become lethal for civilians.

The source says seven people were killed overall, about 1,000 injured, more than 100 suffered gunshot wounds, 200 homes were destroyed, and nearly 2,000 families fled. Rooney's page therefore anchors the civilian-cost side of the [[CivilRightsToStreetWarThreshold]].
