# Yuan Xin / 原欣

Yuan Xin / 原欣 is the [[SAP]] guest in [[ai-chongji-qiye-ruanjian-jutou-yu-sap-yuanxin-liao-damoxing-to-b-de-dianfu-yu-bianjie-1-174-1]], a [[LateTalk]] episode on whether large models, AI coding, and agents will disrupt enterprise software. The source presents him as explaining SAP's view of [[EnterpriseResourcePlanning|ERP]], [[AutonomousEnterprise]], [[SAPJoule]], enterprise AI deployment, and the boundary between model capability and production business systems.

## Source Position
- Treats [[EnterpriseResourcePlanning|ERP]] as a business-process, data, compliance, and trust system rather than only a software UI.
- Argues that AI will change enterprise interaction, customization, coding cost, and pricing while leaving complex processes and human review in place.
- Frames [[ForwardDeployedEngineer|FDE]] as a business-and-technology hybrid role rather than pure implementation engineering.
- Positions [[SAP]] as stronger in business-process know-how and model companies such as [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]] as stronger in frontier models and coding.
