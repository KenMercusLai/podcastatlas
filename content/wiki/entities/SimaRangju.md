# 司马穰苴 / Sima Rangju

司马穰苴 / Sima Rangju appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-2-lmfzo4sl9nd5kz3mrxdl1dl9efei]] as a [[QiState|齐国]] military figure from the [[SpringAndAutumnPeriod|春秋时期]]. The source says he had strong ability but low status, and that [[YanYing|晏婴]]'s recommendation to [[QiJinggong|齐景公]] made his appointment possible.

The episode uses Sima Rangju as the first version of a military-management problem. It says "司马" is not his family name but an office title, then emphasizes that his command did not depend mainly on changing people or rewriting systems; it depended on military discipline, morale, and execution.

- [[WuQi|吴起]] - later commander framed by the episode as an intensified version of Sima Rangju's method.
