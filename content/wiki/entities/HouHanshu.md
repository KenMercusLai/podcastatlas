# 《后汉书》 / Hou Hanshu

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-971-luanshi-miyun-shui-sha-le-caocao-laodie-ljh3hdhssigmv78bueo-hprfqwku|Hanji 971]] introduces [[HouHanshu|《后汉书》]] as a competing source tradition for [[CaoSongMurder|曹嵩遇害]] and the [[XuzhouMassacreLateHan|徐州屠杀]]. For the murder, the episode presents this line as making [[ZhangKaiAssassin|张闿]] the escort-side subordinate who kills [[CaoSong|曹嵩]] and [[CaoDeLateHan|曹德]] for convoy wealth.

The same episode cites a Hou Hanshu atrocity line for [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s retaliation at the [[SiRiver|泗水]]. Both uses make the text important for the wiki's late-Han branch: it shifts the immediate Cao Song killing from direct Tao Qian command toward subordinate robbery, while also preserving the severity of Cao Cao's revenge in Xuzhou.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-967-ta-canyu-mousha-dongzhuo-jingneng-taoguo-sishen-weilie-lv24biyswj9ljdld5htynhauhph3|Hanji 967]] adds another late-Han atrocity use. The host cites Hou Hanshu for the plunder of [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] after [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] return, preserving the catastrophic consequence of [[JiaXu|贾诩]]'s counterattack advice without making the psychological reading of Jia Xu's guilt a textual certainty.
