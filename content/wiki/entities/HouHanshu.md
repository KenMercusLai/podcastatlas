## Overview
《后汉书》 / Hou Hanshu is currently used in the wiki as both a front-four-history orientation text and a late-Han source tradition for atrocity and attribution comparisons.

## Current Profile
Hanji 591-2 introduces Hou Hanshu as Fan Ye's Eastern Han history and one of the front four histories beside [[Shiji|《史记》]], [[HanShu|《汉书》]], and [[Sanguozhi|《三国志》]]. The later late-Han source notes use it more concretely: Hanji 967 cites it for the catastrophic plunder of [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] after Jia Xu's counterattack advice, while Hanji 971 uses it as the tradition in which [[ZhangKaiAssassin|张闿]] kills [[CaoSong|曹嵩]] during an escort-turned-robbery rather than simply making [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] the direct sender.

## Key Characteristics
- Introduced as an Eastern Han official-history text and one of the front four histories.
- Preserves late-Han atrocity language used in the wiki's Chang'an and Xuzhou branches.
- Provides an alternative attribution line for Cao Song's murder that foregrounds Zhang Kai's immediate agency.
- Functions as a source-critical contrast rather than a final arbiter in contested late-Han responsibility cases.

- Cao Song attribution: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-971-luanshi-miyun-shui-sha-le-caocao-laodie-ljh3hdhssigmv78bueo-hprfqwku|Hanji 971]] presents the Hou Hanshu line as making Zhang Kai the escort-side subordinate who kills Cao Song and Cao De for convoy wealth.

## Qualifications
This page is episode-mediated and source-scoped. It does not independently analyze Fan Ye's compilation, Li Xian commentary, or the complete Hou Hanshu textual tradition. Hanji 971 preserves a genuine attribution split with [[Sanguozhi|《三国志》]], so the wiki should not collapse Zhang Kai's immediate killing and Tao Qian's political responsibility into one settled account.
