# 张献 / Zhang Xian (late Han)

张献 / Zhang Xian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1027-jingzhou-zhizhu-liubiao-weihe-nan-cheng-bazhu-ll-ebguqllsf-5ycvpdogihzn3lj|Hanji 1027]] as the [[ChangshaCommanderyLateHan|Changsha]] administrator who becomes the southern pressure point against [[LiuBiao|刘表]]. The episode describes him as a hard-edged local official from a Nanyang great family, with enough local support to make resistance politically meaningful rather than merely personal.

Under [[HuanJieLateHan|桓阶]]'s advice, Zhang Xian joins [[ChangshaCommanderyLateHan|长沙郡]], [[LinglingCommanderyLateHan|零陵郡]], and [[GuiyangCommanderyLateHan|桂阳郡]] into a [[SouthernJingzhouLateHan|southern Jingzhou]] alignment against Liu Biao and submits to [[CaoCao|曹操]]. Cao Cao then uses court authority to recognize him as Zhengnan general, turning a local commandery dispute into a strategic constraint that keeps Liu Biao from easily aiding [[YuanShao|袁绍]].
