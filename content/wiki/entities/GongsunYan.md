# 犀首公孙衍 / Gongsun Yan

犀首公孙衍 / Gongsun Yan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] as the figure Qin uses in 332 BCE to damage the still-fragile [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] line. The episode explains that "犀首" is a title or nickname rather than a personal name, glossed as a mark of fierceness, and identifies him as a native of [[Yinjin|殷晋]] in [[WeiState|魏国]].

In the source's causal chain, Gongsun Yan matters because he converts [[QinState|秦国]]'s pressure into alliance disruption. By misleading or maneuvering [[QiState|齐国]] and Wei into a joint attack against [[ZhaoState|赵国]], he helps make [[ZhaoSuhou|赵肃侯]] blame [[SuQin|苏秦]] and helps break the coalition arrangement that had just been dramatized in the earlier Su Qin episodes.

The episode also previews him as [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s future chief political enemy. That makes him part of [[ZonghengjiaDiplomacy|纵横家外交]] not only as a one-off agent of disruption, but as a rival within the persuader field that will define Qin-facing and anti-Qin strategies.
