## Overview

范明友 / Fan Mingyou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-402-2-shudiwushu-de-kuli-duzhou-weihe-zuizhong-deyi-shanzhong-lqrunv51se0w7_2fi2ifs_f7zub4|Hanji 402-2]] as [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]]'s son-in-law and the Western Han general appointed to respond when [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] prepares to attack [[Wuhuan|乌桓]]. His importance in this source is operational: he turns an aborted Xiongnu interception into a punitive strike on Wuhuan.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Fan as a Huo-connected commander whose assignment depends on court trust as much as battlefield skill. Huo Guang first hears [[ZhaoChongguoWesternHan|赵充国]]'s caution that Han should not rescue Wuhuan from Xiongnu, then asks Fan, who supports sending troops. Fan is appointed Duliao general with twenty thousand cavalry.

Fan's campaign becomes a fallback action. Xiongnu withdraws after hearing of Han's large force, so Fan follows Huo's prior instruction not to return empty-handed and attacks Wuhuan, who have just been weakened by Xiongnu. The episode records six thousand-plus heads and the death of three Wuhuan kings, reading the result as both suppression of Wuhuan rebellion and deterrence toward Xiongnu.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han commander and Huo Guang's son-in-law in the source account.
- Appointed Duliao general for a planned anti-Xiongnu response.
- Executes Huo Guang's fallback instruction by attacking Wuhuan after Xiongnu withdraws.
- Associated with opportunistic frontier punishment against an exhausted target.
- Source-scoped figure whose broader career is not reconstructed here.
