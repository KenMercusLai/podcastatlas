# 余博 / Yu Bo (Comedian)

## Overview
余博 is a stand-up comedian and patient-experience guest in [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] whose reported diabetic ketoacidosis, ICU admission, and subsequent type 1 diabetes management ground a discussion of acute danger and long-term adaptation.

## Current Profile
The source presents Yu Bo's illness story in two phases. Before diagnosis, breathing difficulty, a glucose meter reading of “HI,” marked weight loss, and eventual collapse were misunderstood or not acted on until family brought him to hospital. During intensive care, severe illness, tubes, sedation, weakness, uncertain time, and confused behavior made treatment difficult to remember coherently. After recovery, he converted parts of the experience into comedy and uses exercise, food choices, and [[ContinuousGlucoseMonitoring]] to manage diabetes.

His durable contribution is not a universal diabetes routine. It is an example of how a frightening diagnosis can become a managed part of life without becoming the person's whole identity. He describes glucose feedback as something he can observe and act on, while accepting that vulnerability, repeated effort, and imperfect days remain part of chronic care.

## Key Characteristics
- Grounds [[DiabetesAcuteCrisisRecognition]] in a first-person account of diabetic ketoacidosis, collapse, shock, and ICU treatment.
- Makes delayed recognition concrete through a misunderstood “HI” meter display, rapid weight loss, breathing difficulty, and attribution of illness to drinking or fatigue.
- Describes fragmented ICU memory, sedation, tubes, weakness, confused behavior, and distorted time cues.
- Uses humor to process the hospital experience without claiming that every ICU survivor will avoid trauma.
- Uses glucose monitoring, exercise, and food structure as ongoing management tools rather than presenting chronic illness as a solved event.
- Frames diabetes as one characteristic of a person rather than a moral defect or total identity.
