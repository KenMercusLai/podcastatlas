# 睡虎地秦简 / Shuihudi Qin Slips

睡虎地秦简 / Shuihudi Qin Slips enter the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-138-wudu-qiannian-de-lishi-zhenxiang-zhaogao-bushi-taijian-1-ljw5nzd-ap1a4hkdczxun3t0-vwy|Qinji 138]] as part of the evidence base for reading "隐宫" as "隐官." The episode pairs the Qin slips with [[ZhangjiashanHanmuZhujian|张家山汉墓竹简]] to argue that the term belongs to legal and labor status, not to palace castration.

For this wiki, Shuihudi matters here as an excavated legal-administrative source. It helps keep [[ZhaoGao|赵高]]'s background source-scoped: being connected to hidden-office space can imply low status and penal-administrative environment without proving eunuch identity.

## Key Claims
- The source uses Shuihudi Qin-slip material to support a legal-status reading of "隐官."
- The episode treats "隐宫" as likely related to or mistaken for "隐官."
- This reading undermines the inference from Zhao Gao's family background to castration.
- The evidence is used for [[YinguanStatusMisreading|隐官身份误读]], not for moral rehabilitation of Zhao Gao's later actions.
