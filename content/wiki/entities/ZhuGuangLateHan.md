## Overview

朱光 / Zhu Guang is the Cao-side Lujiang administrator whom the episode places at [[WanChengLujiang|皖城]], developing rice cultivation and irrigation before [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] urges a preventive attack.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1143-guren-lihun-neng-you-duo-qipa-lutfgjyzwfwwh4ovzuevene8xupy|Hanji 1143]] presents Zhu Guang less through biography than through the capacity he is building. Cultivated land, water works, grain, and a forward city could let Cao's side sustain a larger force close to Jiangdong; this makes Zhu Guang's administrative work strategically visible before harvest.

The same source says Sun Quan's force captures Zhu Guang when Wan City falls. The page therefore treats him as a source-bounded logistics and frontier-administration actor rather than inferring a fuller career from one campaign account.

## Key Characteristics

- Serves the Cao-side Lujiang position from Wan City in the episode's account.
- Uses cultivation and irrigation to build prospective military supply capacity.
- Becomes strategically important because grain can support future force concentration.
- Is captured when Wu takes the city before Cao-side relief arrives.

## Evidence

- Agricultural base-building: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1143-guren-lihun-neng-you-duo-qipa-lutfgjyzwfwwh4ovzuevene8xupy|Hanji 1143]] attributes rice-field opening and water works around Wan City to Zhu Guang.
- Threat recognition: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1143-guren-lihun-neng-you-duo-qipa-lutfgjyzwfwwh4ovzuevene8xupy|Hanji 1143]] says Lyu Meng reads the coming harvest as capacity for a larger Cao-side force.
- Capture: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1143-guren-lihun-neng-you-duo-qipa-lutfgjyzwfwwh4ovzuevene8xupy|Hanji 1143]] places Zhu Guang among those captured after the rapid assault.

## Qualifications

The current evidence comes from one short popular-history episode. Zhu Guang's exact title, construction program, cultivated area, command role, troop strength, conduct during the assault, and later fate require direct textual corroboration.
