# Golden Door

## Overview
Golden Door is a high-end wellness retreat used by [[MarketplaceTech]] as the luxury-service case in the expanding [[DigitalDetoxEconomy]].

## Current Profile
The retreat does not require total disconnection. Guests may use phones and Wi-Fi in their rooms, while phones are prohibited in common areas and a supplied "sleep bag" keeps devices out of sight. [[PaulaMejia]] describes the arrangement as a gradual, environment-led reduction in use rather than a hard ban. The episode reports an entry price of roughly $9,000 and a clientele becoming younger.

## Key Characteristics
- Device-light rather than completely device-free retreat design.
- Phone prohibition in common areas while preserving private-room access.
- Physical concealment of devices through a supplied phone pouch.
- High price point that frames protected disconnection as a luxury service.
- Reported increase in younger guests seeking more control over technology use.
