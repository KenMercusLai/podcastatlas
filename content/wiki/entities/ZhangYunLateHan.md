# 张允 / Zhang Yun (Late Han)

## Overview
张允 / Zhang Yun (Late Han) is a Jingzhou official whom Hanji 1092 places with [[CaiMao|蔡瑁]] in the succession coalition that blocks [[LiuQiLateHan|刘琦]] from the dying [[LiuBiao|刘表]] and supports [[LiuCongLateHan|刘琮]].

## Current Profile
The episode gives Zhang Yun a narrow but consequential role. He helps prevent a final Liu Biao-Liu Qi meeting that might change the succession, then participates in the elite environment surrounding Liu Cong's accession and surrender. The page does not infer a fuller biography beyond that source-bounded role.

## Key Characteristics
- Member of Liu Biao's late Jingzhou court circle.
- Acts with Cai Mao to control access during the succession crisis.
- Supports the political transition from Liu Biao to Liu Cong.
- Appears within an elite coalition that prefers capitulation to military risk.
