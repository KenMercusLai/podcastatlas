# Roger Seiders

Roger Seiders is the father of [[RoySeiders]] and [[RyanSeiders]], discussed in [[yeti-ron-and-ryan-seiders-how-two-brothers-turned-a-400-cooler-into-a-2-billion-brand-d7f8e0e8-51a5-4444-8c1d-f5c43c353285]] as an important family-business influence behind [[YETI]]. A shop teacher and fishing-rod builder, he developed a clear flexible epoxy that became [[Flexcoat]] and supported the family.

## Key Points
- Roger's work made fishing rods, materials, customer use, and small manufacturing normal parts of the brothers' upbringing.
- The episode says Flexcoat supported the family and helped put four children through college.
- His example gives the YETI story an intergenerational [[FounderProductFit]] layer: the brothers did not discover outdoor-product users from a spreadsheet.
- Roger later sold Flexcoat after turning 80, after Roy and Ryan had already built YETI into a much larger consumer brand.

## Connections
- [[RoySeiders]] and [[RyanSeiders]] - sons and YETI co-founders.
- [[Flexcoat]] - product and business Roger developed.
- [[YETI]], [[WaterlooRods]], and [[FounderProductFit]] - downstream company, Ryan's prior business, and concept shaped by the family context.
