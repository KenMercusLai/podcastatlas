## Overview

马何罗 / Ma Heluo enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-390-1-xihan-diyi-da-an-jinridi-ruhe-jiu-le-hanwudi-xingming-ls4snv189gvasy6yke7aw0kwhz8m|Hanji 390-1]] as a Western Han palace attendant who attempts to assassinate [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] after the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]] turns against [[JiangChongWesternHan|江充]] and his associates.

## Current Profile

The source presents Ma Heluo less as an ideological rebel than as a court insider trapped by political exposure. Because he is close to Jiang Chong, and because his brother [[MaTongWesternHan|马通]] had fought against [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]] during the Wugu crisis, the later purge of Jiang Chong's side makes the brothers fear implication. Their assassination plot therefore appears as a self-protective escalation inside a suspicious late-Wudi court.

Ma Heluo's action fails because [[JinMidiWesternHan|金日磾]] has already noticed the brothers' abnormal behavior. Jin quietly guards Han Wudi's side, waits near the sleeping quarters at Lingguang Palace, startles Ma Heluo as he approaches with a hidden blade, and seizes him while shouting that Ma is rebelling.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han palace insider tied by friendship or factional proximity to Jiang Chong.
- Self-protective conspirator whose fear grows from the aftermath of the Wugu Incident.
- Assassination actor who exploits Jin Midi's temporary illness but still finds Jin guarding the critical doorway.
- Failed rebel whose capture demonstrates the value of close-servant vigilance around an aging ruler.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 390-1. It does not reconstruct Ma Heluo's full office history, the full prosecution of every brother and participant, the primary-source wording around his relationship with Jiang Chong, or the full geography and guard procedure of Lingguang Palace.
