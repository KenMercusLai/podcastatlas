# 乐毅 / Le Yi

乐毅 / Le Yi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2]] as one of the major talents who come to [[YanState|燕国]] after [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]] honors [[GuoWeiWarringStates|郭隗]]. The episode says Le Yi had been in [[ZhaoState|赵国]] and connects him to [[LeYang|乐羊]], who had earlier served [[WeiState|魏国]] in the Zhongshan campaign.

Yan Zhaowang appoints Le Yi as yaqing and entrusts state affairs to him. The episode previews Le Yi's later reputation: he will command the five-state attack that breaks [[QiState|齐国]] and lets Yan avenge Qi's earlier attack.

In the source, Le Yi is less a complete biography than the strongest proof that Yan Zhaowang's visible respect for talent works. His arrival makes [[TalentAttractionThroughRespect]] and [[WarringStatesCareeristMobility]] concrete at state scale.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-69-bikan-gudai-diwang-qipa-sifa-da-pandian-lukvjsktg-pgfjhizvypwmdmw-13]] begins to cash out that preview. When [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]] wants revenge against [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]], Le Yi argues that [[YanState|燕国]] should not attack Qi alone because Qi is large and populous. He recommends bringing in Zhao, Chu, and Wei, making his first fully operational role in the wiki a coalition-design role rather than a battlefield scene.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-70-xihuan-huayang-zuosi-de-qiguo-junzhu-luuyasp5-nhnikdcozzaueosb-md]] turns that design into command. Le Yi receives the multi-state seals, defeats [[QiState|齐国]] west of the Ji River, and then separates allied interests: [[QinState|秦国]] and [[HanState|韩国]] can return because they cannot easily occupy Qi land, while [[WeiState|魏国]] and [[ZhaoState|赵国]] take nearer territory. Against [[JuXin|剧辛]]'s warning, Le Yi leads [[YanState|燕国]] deeper, betting that Qi Min Wang's lost people-support will make the state collapse. His entry into [[Linzi|临淄]] and reward as Changguo Jun make him more than a recruited talent: he becomes the agent of Yan's long revenge.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-72-shenme-yang-de-ren-cai-shi-zhongzhen-zhishi-logxegnqexgc7z-lodw6tr6-8f28]] shifts Le Yi from coalition commander to occupation governor. The episode says Yan forces under this campaign discipline troops, forbid plunder, honor Qi recluses, lighten taxes, remove harsh laws, restore older good governance, and absorb Qi elites through titles and fiefs. It also records the failed recruitment of [[WangZhu|王蠋]], which exposes the darker side of honoring worthies after conquest: public respect can become coercion when refusal is answered with threats against a locality.
