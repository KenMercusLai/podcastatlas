# 杨天真 / 天真

## Overview
杨天真 / 天真 is a host, public creator, and media operator represented through consecutive [[TianzhenButianzhen|天真不天真]] conversations with [[XieyinMelody|携隐 Melody]], [[MiziCrimePodcaster|咪仔]], and [[LiRonghao|李荣浩]]. The sources connect podcast priority, media and creator-company design, monetization ethics, emotional practice, learning, and the relationship between creative work and audience judgment.

## Current Profile
Yang treats podcasting as a slower but high-value medium because it preserves thought, voice, human connection, and durable conversation. Her operating view is commercial without being purely metric-driven: she studies behavior, titles, conversion, platform logic, subscriptions, advertising, and creator portfolios, while rejecting formats or payment designs that depend mainly on exploiting weakness, anxiety, or strategically missing information.

Across the Mizi and Li Ronghao conversations, her interviewer role joins operating questions to personal experimentation. She proposes a [[SuperCreatorPortfolioModel|超级创作者组合模型]], discusses vulnerability and [[EmotionalTriggerSourceSeparation|情绪触发源分离]], and describes using public live-streaming as external structure for English study. With Li, she also tests whether creativity can be taught as a repeatable method and contributes a functional-use case for [[GenerativeAIMusic]] through [[ChatGPT]] and [[Suno]], while accepting his distinction between fast task completion and durable artistic value.

## Key Characteristics
- She prioritizes podcasting as a long-form human-connection and thinking surface despite weaker immediate commercial efficiency.
- She evaluates media through both user value and business mechanics, including behavior design, pricing, conversion, and monetization ethics.
- She proposes creator-company capabilities that combine complementary editorial, investigative, performance, and operating roles.
- Her public learning and interviewing methods use external structure, personal examples, and direct comparison rather than detached abstraction.
- Her emotional practice separates present facts and responsibility from older control, abandonment, or grievance triggers.
- She treats creative methods and AI tools pragmatically while preserving questions about talent, motive, credibility, and lasting value.
