# 杨天真 / 天真

## Overview
杨天真 / 天真 is a host, public creator, and media operator represented through consecutive [[TianzhenButianzhen|天真不天真]] conversations and a listener question-and-answer episode. The sources connect podcast priority, media and creator-company design, monetization ethics, emotional practice, learning, career exit, founder delegation, and the relationship between action and audience judgment.

## Current Profile
Yang treats podcasting as a slower but high-value medium because it preserves thought, voice, human connection, and durable conversation. Her operating view is commercial without being purely metric-driven: she studies behavior, titles, conversion, platform logic, subscriptions, advertising, and creator portfolios, while rejecting formats or payment designs that depend mainly on exploiting weakness, anxiety, or strategically missing information.

Across the Mizi and Li Ronghao conversations, her interviewer role joins operating questions to personal experimentation. She proposes a [[SuperCreatorPortfolioModel|超级创作者组合模型]], discusses vulnerability and [[EmotionalTriggerSourceSeparation|情绪触发源分离]], and describes using public live-streaming as external structure for English study. With Li, she tests whether creativity can be taught as a repeatable method and contributes a functional-use case for [[GenerativeAIMusic]] while accepting his distinction between fast task completion and durable artistic value.

The Q&A episode makes her decision method more explicit. [[ForwardLookingExitDecision]] separates capability from title, asks whether accumulated assets expand or restrict choice, and measures the next commitment rather than defending the last one. Her founder advice extends [[DirectionSettingDelegatedGovernance]] through outcome standards, matched authority and responsibility, staged feedback, and error budgets. Her promotion advice turns [[EvaluationAttachmentRelease]] into bounded action: test a channel, evaluate customer value, and stop treating each visible attempt as a global judgment of the self.

## Key Characteristics
- She prioritizes podcasting as a long-form human-connection and thinking surface despite weaker immediate commercial efficiency.
- She evaluates media through both user value and business mechanics, including behavior design, pricing, conversion, and monetization ethics.
- She proposes creator-company capabilities that combine complementary editorial, investigative, performance, and operating roles.
- Her public learning and interviewing methods use external structure, personal examples, and direct comparison rather than detached abstraction.
- Her emotional practice separates present facts and responsibility from older control, abandonment, or grievance triggers.
- She treats creative methods and AI tools pragmatically while preserving questions about talent, motive, credibility, and lasting value.
- She treats stopping and delegation as active operating capabilities grounded in forward cost, explicit boundaries, and tolerance for temporary status loss.

## Qualifications
The profile is bounded to four podcast episodes and should not be treated as a complete biography, clinical account, company history, or proof that proposed production, career, delegation, and creator-business methods will work in every setting. Business, emotional-development, learning, audience, financial-readiness, and AI-use claims are speaker-reported.

## What Changed
- Added a forward-looking exit framework that separates portable capability from external status and tests motive, runway, and downside.
- Added founder delegation through outcome standards, matched decision rights, staged review, and bounded error.
- Added action experiments that reduce the identity-level force of familiar people's evaluation.
