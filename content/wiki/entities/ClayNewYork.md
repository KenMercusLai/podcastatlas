# Clay, New York

Clay, New York appears in [[tech-20260123-0123-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260123-0123-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the planned site of [[MicronTechnology|Micron]]'s [[MicronClayMegaFab|mega fab]] near Syracuse. The episode uses the town as the concrete location where AI-era semiconductor manufacturing, wetlands and former farmland, jobs, emissions, water, and local legal commitments collide.

In the wiki, Clay is a place-based test for [[TechManufacturingReshoring]] and [[EnforceableCommunityBenefits]]. The source does not present the community coalition as rejecting the project; it frames the dispute as a demand for enforceable terms before a long-lived, resource-intensive facility is locked in.
