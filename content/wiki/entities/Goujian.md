# 越王勾践 / King Goujian of Yue

越王勾践 / King Goujian of Yue appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-57-gudai-fa-dushi-haiyou-zhe-zuoyong-lj6wi61osaytowgmdp8dtkrwb-si]] through [[Zigong|子贡 / 端木赐]]'s rescue chain. When [[Fuchai|吴王夫差]] worries that Yue may attack [[WuState|吴国]] from behind, Zigong persuades Goujian to appear submissive, send gifts, and promise military support, while waiting for Wu to exhaust itself against [[QiState|齐国]] and [[JinState|晋国]].

The episode then turns Goujian from patient avenger into dangerous victor. After Wu is destroyed and Fuchai dies, [[FanLi|范蠡]] sees that ministers who know the ruler's humiliating past may become unsafe. Fan Li leaves, warns [[WenZhongYue|文种]], and Wen Zhong's later forced suicide makes Goujian part of the source's post-victory minister-risk lesson.
