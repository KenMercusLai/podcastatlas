# 妙玉 / Miaoyu

In [[ritan-chuantai-hongloumeng-haishi-keyi-du-yidu-de-587167638]], 妙玉 enters through the Mid-Autumn linked-poem discussion. After [[LinDaiyu|林黛玉]] and [[ShiXiangyun|史湘云]] push the poem into an increasingly bleak register, Miaoyu joins and pulls the mood back from excessive sadness.

The source uses this moment as evidence that [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]] gives women relation and poetic agency beyond competition for [[JiaBaoyu|贾宝玉]]. Miaoyu is therefore connected to [[GrandViewGarden|大观园]] as a place where literary play, friendship, temperament, and fate become visible together.
