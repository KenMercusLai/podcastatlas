# Jonathan Hurst

## Overview
Jonathan Hurst is the Agility Robotics cofounder and chief robot officer voice in the All-In robotics special. He explains why useful humanoids require more than humanlike appearance and why warehouses are a practical early market.

## Current Profile
Hurst's profile in the wiki is as the episode's most measured robotics-progress voice. He accepts that perception, AI, and engineering effort have changed the field, but stresses robot-control data scarcity, the limits of simulation, shared learning across robots, safety redesign, and task-specific form-factor justification.

## Key Characteristics
- He distinguishes robots that look human from robots that can do useful work in human spaces.
- He argues that language models are a rising tide, but robot-control data such as torque commands and sensor-action traces is missing at internet scale.
- He treats world models and simulation as useful but not sufficient because real dynamics and object variation still matter.
- He rejects a single singularity moment while accepting compounding improvement through shared robot learning.
- He grounds humanoid value in specific workflows that need arms, balance, and whole-body control.
- He emphasizes safety systems, supervisory circuits, emergency stops, and design constraints that prevent harm to people.
