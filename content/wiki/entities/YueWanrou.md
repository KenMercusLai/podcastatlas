# 岳宛柔 / Yue Wanrou

## Overview
岳宛柔 is the nutrition and food-literacy guest in the VOL.215 [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode on sugar, carbohydrates, food labels, and practical sugar control.

## Current Profile
The current wiki evidence presents 岳宛柔 only through this episode. Her role is to move the conversation away from fear or shame around sweet foods and toward mechanisms: evolved sweet preference, industrial food design, sugar form, food structure, ingredient lists, nutrition panels, low-GI marketing, sweeteners, and practical reduction of liquid or unconscious sugar.

Her advice is framed for ordinary listeners rather than for individualized metabolic disease treatment. She repeatedly separates public nutrition literacy from rigid dieting: staple foods should not be equated with added sugar, fruit should not be treated the same as juice, and occasional dessert can remain compatible with long-term health when it does not become the default.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains sugar preference through physiology, stress, sleep, exercise, emotion, and food environment rather than moral weakness.
- Distinguishes sugars, carbohydrates, staple foods, fruit, juice, added sugar, free sugar, sweeteners, and label claims for lay listeners.
- Uses daily examples such as milk tea, juice, honey, cereals, yogurt, kombucha, cold rice, white porridge, noodles, and desserts.
- Critiques health marketing around low-GI honey, zero-sucrose products, anti-sugar pills, and invisible sugar claims.
- Keeps nutrition advice practical and bounded by total intake, food structure, and qualified care for people with metabolic disease.
