# 乐贤 / Le Xian

乐贤 / Le Xian appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-80-zhugeliang-de-ouxiang-daodi-you-duo-lihai-lse6qmqqhoyqdzqjl4qfuvioeiw0]] as [[LeYi|乐毅]]'s son. After [[YanHuiwang|燕惠王]] reads Le Yi's reply and becomes less afraid that Zhao will use Le Yi against [[YanState|燕国]], he lets Le Xian inherit the Changguo Jun title.

The page is source-scoped. Le Xian matters here less for independent action than as a sign that Le Yi's restrained answer helps stabilize the family-title relationship between Yan and Zhao after the failed Qi occupation.
