# Drew Harwell

Drew Harwell is the [[WashingtonPost|Washington Post]] technology reporter interviewed in [[tech-20251226-1226-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251226-1226-mp-tech-pod-128]], a [[MarketplaceTech]] episode on [[OnlineAgeVerification]] becoming mainstream in 2025. He explains how state laws, a [[SupremeCourt]] ruling involving [[Texas]], and international rules are pushing websites toward age checks.

His role in the wiki is to connect child-safety regulation to the practical architecture of web access. Harwell tracks the vendors, costs, data risks, false positives, VPN shifts, and responsibility disputes behind [[AgeVerificationComplianceIndustry]], [[AgeVerificationPatchwork]], [[PlatformAgeEstimation]], and [[BehavioralAgeInference]].

## Key Claims
- Age verification has moved from niche adult sites toward a broader legal and platform-compliance issue.
- Compliance often depends on outside contractors that ask for IDs, face scans, or other sensitive signals.
- The evidence that these systems make children safer is still unclear because users may move to noncompliant sites or VPNs.
- The responsibility question remains unsettled across websites, app stores, device platforms, and federal regulators.
