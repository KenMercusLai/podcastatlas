# Wi-Fi Master Key / Wi-Fi 万能钥匙

Wi-Fi Master Key / Wi-Fi 万能钥匙 appears in [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]] as one of the visible projects associated with the later [[ShandaInnovationInstitute|盛大创新院]] and Chen Danian's independent operating branch. The episode treats it as part of Shanda's still-visible legacy after the parent company receded from games and content.

Within the source, Wi-Fi Master Key helps connect Shanda's PC-era game and entertainment history to the mobile-internet era. It is not the main subject, but it supports the claim that [[Shanda|盛大]] produced people and projects beyond [[LegendOfMir|《传奇》]] even when its own platform strategy fragmented.
