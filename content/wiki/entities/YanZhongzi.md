# 严仲子 / Yan Zhongzi

严仲子 / Yan Zhongzi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]] as a Puyang man with an unresolved feud against [[XiaLei|侠累]], the chancellor of [[HanState|韩国]]. The episode says he prepares a large gold gift for [[NieZheng|聂政]]'s mother as a way to ask Nie Zheng to take revenge for him.

His wiki role is to show the patronage side of assassination. Unlike the Zheng factional killings in [[SpringAutumnToWarringStatesPoliticalViolence]], Yan Zhongzi's case is narrated as private feud outsourced through honor, wealth, and personal bravery.
