## Overview

齐王刘弘 / Liu Hong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-362-2-bushi-weisha-yao-pengsha-sanghongyang-loz3ly12pudlvdrtjngxs8f1qi6_|Hanji 362-2]] as [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s favored second son, granted the wealthy kingdom of Qi but dead in adolescence without an heir.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Liu Hong as a brief royal-family notice rather than a developed political actor. Imperial favor gives him a rich territorial position, while early death and the absence of a son prevent that allocation from becoming a continuing lineage; Qi is consequently abolished.

## Key Characteristics

- Identified by the episode as Han Wudi's second son.
- Favored by his father and granted wealthy Qi as his kingdom.
- Given a generally positive but undeveloped reputation in the host's account.
- Dies in adolescence without a son, ending the kingdom's immediate line.
