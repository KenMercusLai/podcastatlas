# Act for the Queen's Safety

The Act for the Queen's Safety appears in [[696-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-killing-the-queen-of-scots-part-6-glt2334035496]] as the 1585 legal mechanism that lets Elizabethan ministers try and execute any claimant implicated in plots against [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]]. The episode presents it as the bridge between the [[BabingtonPlot]] evidence and [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]]' public trial.

For [[WilliamCecil|William Cecil / Lord Burleigh]] and [[FrancisWalsingham]], the act helps frame Mary's execution as consequence of law rather than personal royal vengeance. For Elizabeth, that same frame remains dangerous because it still implies that legal and parliamentary procedure can authorize the killing of an anointed queen.
