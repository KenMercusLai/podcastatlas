# Steve Shaw

Steve Shaw appears in [[tech-20260408-0408-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260408-0408-mp-tech-pod-128]] as a postdoctoral researcher and co-author of a [[WhartonSchool]] report on how people use AI in decisions. He introduces [[CognitiveSurrender]] as a pattern where users defer to AI answers, and [[ArtificialCognition]] as a "system three" layer added to fast and slow thinking.

Shaw's position in the wiki is practical rather than anti-AI. He says he uses AI daily and allows students to use it, but he teaches them to think first, generate their own ideas, and then prompt. After doing the research, he sometimes goes offline or avoids AI so he can work through a task himself.
