## Overview

管霸 / Guan Ba is a late-Han zhongchangshi introduced by [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-884-zhichang-chujian-yao-dui-shui-xian-kaidao-lvtc3uqlso242olkyin3fvz5a7ys|Hanji 884]] as the first visible target in [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦妙 / 窦皇太后]]'s limited anti-eunuch purge. The source presents him as powerful and capable, which makes him a practical "first cut" after Dou Miao refuses [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] and [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]]'s broader removal logic.

## Current Profile

Guan Ba's current profile is narrow and episode-bound. He matters because Dou Miao's first action against the eunuch field does not begin with the broader targets Cao Jie and Wang Fu; it begins with a selective punishment of someone described as especially powerful and strategically able among the zhongchangshi.

The result clarifies the compromise structure of the 168 CE decision window. Guan Ba and [[SuKangLateHan|苏康]] are arrested and executed, but the source does not show this as a completed removal of the palace proximity network around Dou Miao.

## Key Characteristics

- Guan Ba is a powerful zhongchangshi singled out in the first purge step.
- The source describes him as both influential and capable, making him more than a generic eunuch name.
- His execution represents selective punishment rather than acceptance of a blanket eunuch purge.
- His case helps mark the gap between removing offenders and disabling the broader palace-access field.

- Power and ability: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-884-zhichang-chujian-yao-dui-shui-xian-kaidao-lvtc3uqlso242olkyin3fvz5a7ys|Hanji 884]] describes Guan Ba as a zhongchangshi with great power, ability, and planning capacity.
- Shared punishment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-884-zhichang-chujian-yao-dui-shui-xian-kaidao-lvtc3uqlso242olkyin3fvz5a7ys|Hanji 884]] says Guan Ba and Su Kang are arrested and executed together.
- Limited-purge meaning: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-884-zhichang-chujian-yao-dui-shui-xian-kaidao-lvtc3uqlso242olkyin3fvz5a7ys|Hanji 884]] places the execution after Dou Miao says only lawbreakers should be punished, not all eunuchs.

## Qualifications

The source does not supply Guan Ba's full biography, family network, office history, or role after the execution because the page is limited to the Hanji 884 decision window. Transcript 管爸 is normalized to 管霸 based on the late-Han context.

## What Changed

- The page creates Guan Ba as the selective first target in the pre-Hanji 885 anti-eunuch sequence.
- The limited evidence keeps him separate from the later Cao Jie and Wang Fu countercoup leadership.
