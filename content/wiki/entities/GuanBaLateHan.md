## Overview

管霸 / Guan Ba is a late-Han zhongchangshi whose current wiki profile now includes both property privilege under [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] and later execution in [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦妙 / 窦皇太后]]'s limited anti-eunuch purge. The earlier layer makes his later punishment more concrete: before being singled out as powerful and capable, he is already shown acquiring valuable land and residences at underpriced terms that local officials fear to challenge.

## Current Profile

Guan Ba's current profile is no longer only an execution note. In the 165 CE layer, he and [[SuKangLateHan|苏康]] obtain good land and residences cheaply, and [[LiuYouLateHan|刘祐]] orders confiscation. The court response punishes Liu You, not Guan Ba, which makes Guan Ba a concrete beneficiary of the same eunuch-protection field that later reformers try to cut back.

In the 168 CE layer, Dou Miao's first action against the eunuch field does not begin with the broader targets Cao Jie and Wang Fu; it begins with selective punishment of someone described as especially powerful and strategically able among the zhongchangshi.

The result clarifies the compromise structure of the 168 CE decision window. Guan Ba and [[SuKangLateHan|苏康]] are arrested and executed, but the source does not show this as a completed removal of the palace proximity network around Dou Miao.

## Key Characteristics

- Guan Ba is a powerful zhongchangshi whose privilege includes underpriced property acquisition before his later execution.
- Liu You's failed confiscation order shows Guan Ba's property position had palace protection.
- Guan Ba is singled out in the first purge step after the later reform coalition gains authority.
- The source describes him as both influential and capable, making him more than a generic eunuch name.
- His execution represents selective punishment rather than acceptance of a blanket eunuch purge.

## Qualifications

The current sources do not supply Guan Ba's full biography, family network, office history, or role after execution. His profile should remain centered on property privilege, palace protection, and selective purge status. Transcript 管爸 is normalized to 管霸 based on the late-Han context.

## What Changed

- The profile now includes Guan Ba's earlier property-acquisition privilege and Liu You's failed confiscation order.
- The later execution remains selective punishment rather than proof that the broader palace-access field was disabled.
