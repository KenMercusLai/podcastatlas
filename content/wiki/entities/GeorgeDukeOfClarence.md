# George, Duke of Clarence

## Overview

George, Duke of Clarence, was Edward IV's younger brother whose alliance with Warwick, repeated change of side, and eventual execution exposed the instability inside the Yorkist royal family.

## Current Profile

Clarence joined Warwick against Edward amid resentment of Woodville influence, participated in the 1470 regime reversal, and then returned to Edward's side during the Yorkist restoration. His later imprisonment and death removed one adult royal brother, leaving Richard of Gloucester as Edward IV's principal surviving brother by 1483.

## Key Characteristics

- Royal duke with a potentially powerful dynastic claim.
- Ally of [[RichardNevilleEarlOfWarwick]] against his brother Edward IV.
- Participant in factional defection and later reconciliation.
- Imprisoned and killed after renewed conflict with Edward's regime.
- Removal narrowed the adult Yorkist family structure before Edward IV's death.

## Qualifications

The episode does not reconstruct Clarence's full political program or establish the literal truth of drowning in a butt of malmsey. His removal shaped the later family balance without making the 1483 crisis inevitable.

## What Changed

- Created a profile connecting Clarence's reversals and death to the narrowing Yorkist succession structure.

- [[EdwardIV]] - elder brother, opponent, reconciled king, and authority behind Clarence's imprisonment.
- [[RichardIII]] - younger brother left as the principal adult royal uncle after Clarence's death.
- [[RichardNevilleEarlOfWarwick]] - ally in rebellion and regime reversal.
- [[WarsOfTheRoses]] - dynastic conflict that made Clarence's claim and defections politically consequential.
