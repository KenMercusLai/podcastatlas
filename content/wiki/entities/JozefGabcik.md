# Jozef Gabčík / 约瑟夫·盖伯齐克

## Overview

Jozef Gabčík was the Slovak member of the two-person strike team that carried out [[OperationAnthropoid|Operation Anthropoid]] against [[ReinhardHeydrich]] in Prague.

## Current Profile

After British-supported insertion into occupied Czechoslovakia, Gabčík and [[JanKubis|Jan Kubiš]] spent months preparing an ambush. At the chosen bend on 27 May 1942, Gabčík's Sten gun jammed at close range, turning the planned shooting into an improvised bomb attack by Kubiš. Gabčík escaped the scene but later died by suicide with other resisters during the German siege of their church refuge.

## Key Characteristics

- Slovak soldier selected for a high-risk exile-government mission.
- Worked with a Czech partner, giving the team a shared-state symbolic meaning in the episode.
- Continued the attack sequence after his primary weapon failed.
- Survived the roadside action but not the subsequent manhunt.
- Became part of Czech and Slovak national resistance memory.
