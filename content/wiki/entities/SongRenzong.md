# 宋仁宗 / Song Renzong

Song Renzong appears in [[164-guwen-de-liliang-qing-shou-xia-zhe-fen-qiannian-wei-bian-de-shenqing-dongjian-he-yongqi-940236576]] in the episode's account of [[SuZhe|苏辙]]'s special examination. The source says he asked candidates for direct criticism, was angered by Su Zhe's severe accusations, but ultimately accepted that inviting speech meant not punishing the speaker for speaking plainly.

This makes Song Renzong part of the episode's [[PoliticalTruthTelling]] branch. His role is not a full imperial biography; he functions as a ruler whose limited tolerance helps make Su Zhe's youthful force legible.
