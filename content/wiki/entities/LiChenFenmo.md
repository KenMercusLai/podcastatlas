# 李晨 / Li Chen (粉墨)

## Overview
李晨 is the [[FenmoTeam|粉墨]] director interviewed in the 三五环 episode about AI filmmaking and [[NvwaZhiSi|《女娲之死》]].

## Current Profile
The source presents 李晨 as a practitioner who moved from using AI video for long-script visual demos into a more organized AI short-film workflow. His role in [[NvwaZhiSi|《女娲之死》]] was to translate [[JiaoshouYiXiaoxing|叫兽易小星]]'s creative idea and script into executable visual plans: shot breakdown, storyboard images, model-specific video generation, editing, deletion, regeneration, and finishing.

His contribution to the wiki is practical rather than biographical. He argues that AI lowers the cost of seeing and testing images that would be expensive to shoot, but good work still depends on knowing what the shot should do, which model fits the movement or performance, and when an 80% result needs more expensive human and technical polishing.

## Key Characteristics
- Director associated in the source with [[FenmoTeam|粉墨]] and its AI short-film experiments.
- Uses AI video to prototype scenes that would be expensive or difficult in live-action demo form.
- Treats AI-video production as a traditional creative pipeline using new tools, not as simple prompt luck.
- Emphasizes storyboard-first workflow, model-specific choice, editing, and professional finishing.
- Keeps cost optimism qualified by compute spending, regeneration time, tool instability, and the labor of moving from rough output to polished work.
