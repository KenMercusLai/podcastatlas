# Genome Ark

## Overview
Genome Ark is presented as a repository for high-quality genome assemblies intended to preserve reusable genomic references across species.

## Current Profile
[[ErichJarvis]] describes Genome Ark as the archival and access side of a wider effort to improve genome quality. Its role is not simply to accumulate sequences: assemblies must be accurate enough to support evolutionary comparison, trait research, and conservation use without carrying forward the gaps and false duplications found in some older references.

The source also mentions approaches from conservation and de-extinction organizations interested in endangered or extinct-species projects. Those examples establish demand for reference genomes, not that an assembly alone can restore a species or guarantee a conservation outcome.

## Key Characteristics
- Stores and exposes high-quality genome assemblies across species.
- Connects reference-genome production to long-term biodiversity research.
- Supports comparative, evolutionary, and conservation-oriented reuse.
- Serves organizations interested in endangered and extinct-species genomics while remaining distinct from a de-extinction guarantee.
