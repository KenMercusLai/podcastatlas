# Hermann Göring

## Overview

Hermann Göring was a senior Nazi leader whose control of Prussian police institutions and direction of the Berlin phase made him a central operator in the [[NightOfTheLongKnives|Night of the Long Knives]].

## Current Profile

The episode places Göring in the institutional preparation for the purge. In Prussia, political-police and intelligence functions were reorganized under Nazi control, helping create the Gestapo and giving the leadership an apparatus capable of arrest and killing.

After Hitler moved against [[ErnstRohm|Röhm]] and the [[Sturmabteilung|SA]] in Bavaria, Göring received the “colibri” signal and directed the Berlin action. That operation reached beyond stormtrooper leaders to conservative critics, former Nazi rivals, and other selected targets.

His role illustrates how the purge required more than Hitler's rage or personal authority: rival Nazi power centers had built police capacity that could turn factional decisions into coordinated state violence.

## Key Characteristics

- He converted Prussian political policing into Nazi institutional power.
- He helped create the Gestapo through police and intelligence reorganization.
- He prepared the Berlin target operation before the purge began.
- He acted on Hitler's signal to widen the killings beyond the SA.
- His role joined factional rivalry to operational state violence.
