# 周氏 / Lady Zhou (Wancheng)

周氏 / Lady Zhou (Wancheng) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] as [[ZhangXiu|张绣]]'s aunt by marriage and the woman [[CaoCao|曹操]] takes after Zhang Xiu's surrender at [[WanCounty|宛城]]. The source says Cao Cao becomes absorbed in her company after the surrender.

Her page is deliberately named with a Wancheng suffix because "周氏" is not a stable unique key in the wiki. In this episode, Lady Zhou matters less as an independent biography than as the status and household trigger that makes Zhang Xiu read surrender as humiliation. When Cao Cao also rewards [[HuCheEr|胡车儿]], the insult becomes a perceived security threat.
