# 张鹤 / Zhang He (Zhang Chu)

张鹤 / Zhang He enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-3-chensheng-zhisi-weishenme-dele-minxin-hanle-renxin-lnx4zs-vsi3zeoixnqskkpexmr3|Qinji 129-3]] as the commander of the remaining [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]] base-defense force around Chen County. The supplied transcript writes the name as 张鹤 and treats him as the last usable commander close to [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] after the main armies have been spent under Zhou Wen and Tian Zang.

When [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] presses toward Chen County, Zhang He builds a western defense but is defeated and killed. The episode uses his fall to show that Chen Sheng's crisis has moved from remote battlefield setbacks into the collapse of his immediate military base.
