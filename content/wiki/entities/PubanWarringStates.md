# 蒲阪 / Puban (Warring States)

蒲阪 / Puban enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-60-zhongguo-di-yi-wei-bachi-chaozheng-de-taihou-jingshi-ta-ltazis9qo0dwvwmzxikk8mmpvosc]] as one of the [[WeiState|魏国]] places captured by [[QinState|秦国]] in 303 BCE. The episode groups it with [[YangjinWarringStates|阳晋]] and [[FenglingWarringStates|封陵]] as important crossing points between Hexi and Hedong.

Puban then becomes a diplomatic object. In 302 BCE, after the Qin-Chu hostage rupture makes Qin repair relations with [[HanState|韩国]] and Wei, [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] returns Puban to Wei around the [[LinjinWarringStates|临晋]] meeting.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-155-2-zhuan-gei-ni-piqi-da-de-pengyou-weiwang-panhan-jing-shi-liubang-ai-maren-ls5nvunmhakvefd2oxn4zdyoob4l|Hanji 155-2]] gives Puban a later military role. [[WeiBao|魏豹]] masses troops there to block [[HanXin|韩信]]'s expected crossing from [[LinjinWarringStates|临晋]], but Han Xin uses that expectation as the false front and crosses at [[XiayangChuHan|下阳]] instead.
