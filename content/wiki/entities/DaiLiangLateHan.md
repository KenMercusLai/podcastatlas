## Overview

戴良 / Dai Liang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-798-donghan-shiqi-de-baixing-you-duo-ku-loolveumdtv4eo-omubfi5z2p2r5|Hanji 798]] as a talented but proud Runan peer who becomes one of the clearest witnesses to [[HuangXianLateHan|黄宪]]'s effect on other people.

## Current Profile

The episode describes Dai Liang as gifted and self-confident before he encounters Huang Xian. His reaction becomes evidence for Huang's reputation: after meeting Huang, Dai returns home unsettled and says that before seeing Huang he had thought no one exceeded him, but afterward he knows he is inferior.

Dai Liang is therefore not developed as an independent policy actor. He is a reputation witness whose humbled response helps turn Huang Xian's otherwise hard-to-describe presence into social evidence.

## Key Characteristics

- Talented Runan figure whose pride makes his later humility narratively important.
- Witness to Huang Xian's moral and reputational force.
- Contrast case showing that Huang's effect works on capable peers, not only admirers.
- Source-bounded actor currently known through one evaluative anecdote.
