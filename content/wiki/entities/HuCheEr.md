# 胡车儿 / Hu Che'er

胡车儿 / Hu Che'er enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] as a fierce subordinate of [[ZhangXiu|张绣]]. The source first makes him a threat signal: [[CaoCao|曹操]] gives him gold and silver after taking [[LadyZouWancheng|邹氏]], and Zhang Xiu reads that reward as Cao Cao trying to pull away one of his strongest men.

Hu Che'er then becomes the operational hinge of the Wancheng attack. Because [[DianWei|典韦]] is too dangerous to face directly, Hu Che'er drinks with him, gets him drunk, and steals his heavy double halberds before Zhang Xiu's night attack.
