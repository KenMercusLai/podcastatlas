# 释迦牟尼佛 / Shakyamuni Buddha

释迦牟尼佛 / Shakyamuni Buddha appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-161-caiyun-he-renji-guanxi-de-liangzi-jiuchan-3-ll74kq2hg2vp-zx-cqxkadxahamd|Hanji 161 part 3]] as the episode's contrast figure for different levels of perception. The host says ordinary people and Shakyamuni Buddha do not see the same "appearance," using the contrast to argue that修行境界 changes what world is experienced.

The page records only this source role. In this ingest, Shakyamuni Buddha anchors [[BuddhistConsciousnessProjectionFrame|佛教心识投射框架]] and the episode's claim that meditation and awakened perception can break attachment to surface form.
