# Coach / 蔻驰

Coach appears in [[zhongguo-yinglai-hanbao-kaidianchao-guochan-huangningmeng-jiage-huiluo-1006920543]] as a fashion-brand China growth case. The source says Coach's latest quarter delivered 14% sales growth overall and 30% growth in Greater China, while volume was roughly flat and average selling price continued to rise.

The episode frames Coach's recovery as a brand-control problem. After earlier overreliance on logo-heavy bags and broad distribution weakened image and profit, Coach is described as reducing discounting, launching lower-logo products from 2019 onward, putting some full-price goods into outlet channels, and using both smaller emerging-city stores and flagship stores to balance reach with brand image.
