# 唐举 / Tang Ju

唐举 / Tang Ju appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-1-lishishang-diyi-chounan-nixi-zuo-qinxiang-lvmdrgbhvpbiyngm-nviunour0sk]] as a Warring States physiognomist associated with Liang, glossed by the host as roughly today's Kaifeng. His main role in the episode is to interpret [[CaiZe|蔡泽]]: he names Cai Ze's physically ugly features but then says a person's future rank cannot be reduced to appearance.

The episode uses Tang Ju less as an independently analyzed historical actor than as a hinge between appearance, confidence, and talent assessment. His forecast helps explain why Cai Ze keeps trying to find office despite repeated failure and mockery, and it lets the host move from 相面 to [[WenWangGuanrenFa|文王官人法]] and other Chinese traditions of judging people.
