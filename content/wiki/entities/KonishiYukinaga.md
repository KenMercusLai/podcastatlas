# Konishi Yukinaga / 小西行长

Konishi Yukinaga / 小西行长 appears in [[14-wushi-weilian-dahanghai-shidai-de-riben-he-xifang-555771285]] as a Christian daimyo on the losing side of the [[SekigaharaBattle|Battle of Sekigahara]]. The episode uses him to show that Japan was already legible enough to some Europeans for letters, martyr-like reports, and theatrical fantasies to circulate.

In the source, Konishi's death reaches Roman Catholic audiences through a letter transmitted by [[Jesuits|Jesuit]] channels. A later European stage work reportedly imagines him defeating [[TokugawaIeyasu|Tokugawa Ieyasu]], which the episode reads as evidence of cross-cultural imagination rather than accurate Japanese political knowledge.

## Key Claims
- Konishi makes Japanese Christianity concrete inside the Sekigahara story.
- His European afterlife shows [[EarlyModernJapanEuropeContact]] working through reports, translation, mourning, and fantasy, not only through trade.
- The source uses him to complicate the idea that Europe knew nothing about Japan before later modern contact.
