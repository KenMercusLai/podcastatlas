# 《资治通鉴补》 / Zizhi Tongjian Bu

《资治通鉴补》 / Zizhi Tongjian Bu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-4-lhjz-m12pedkfszxyzgqtjnpsn3p]] as a Ming supplement that restores material omitted from [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]]. The episode says it includes the story of [[ZhaoCangtang|赵苍唐]] using gifts, titles, and [[Shijing|《诗经》]] allusions to help the future [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]] return from [[ZhongshanState|中山国]] and regain heir status.

The page is source-scoped. It records the episode's claim that the supplement places the story under Zhou An Wang's fifteenth year, 387 BCE, the same year in which the base chronicle has Wei Wuhou succeeding [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]]. The episode also uses the supplement's size and detail to contrast later compilation appetite with [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s much more selective annalistic design.
