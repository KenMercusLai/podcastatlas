# 蒯良 / Kuai Liang

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-953-luanshi-zhizhong-yaobuyao-jiangjiu-renyi-lrzfimgqatjxyw9zx9ry2kiwxkon|Hanji 953]] introduces Kuai Liang as a Jingzhou adviser consulted by [[LiuBiao|刘表]] when Liu Biao has an official appointment but lacks a secure army and administrative base. The transcript renders his name as "怀良"; the wiki normalizes it to Kuai Liang because the paired counsel with [[KuaiYue|蒯越]] and the Jingzhou setting identify the role.

Kuai Liang supplies the people-support half of Liu Biao's consolidation design. He argues that disorder persists because officials do not love the people, and that if Liu Biao governs with benevolence and righteousness, local attachment can solve the shortage of troops and base. In the episode's moral frame, Kuai Liang shows why "仁义" can still matter in a chaotic age, but only when it is connected to practical state-building.
