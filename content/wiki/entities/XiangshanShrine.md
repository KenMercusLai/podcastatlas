# 湘山祠 / Xiangshan Shrine

湘山祠 / Xiangshan Shrine appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-122-3-qinshihuang-jing-he-shenxian-si-qilai-le-ljnw5estg6dvgfllm3kfcq6ulhxu]] as a sacred site on [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]]'s return route during the 219 BCE eastern tour. The emperor reaches the area after the Pengcheng/Xuzhou search for [[Jiuding|九鼎]] and is blocked by strong wind while attempting to cross.

The episode makes the shrine a test case for conquered sacred geography. Once the wind is attributed to [[XiangJun|湘君]], Qin Shi Huang responds by sending three thousand convicts to cut the mountain's trees, leaving the red earth exposed. The site therefore marks the turn from Qin's former-Qi ritual incorporation to punitive humiliation of a Chu-associated local deity space.
