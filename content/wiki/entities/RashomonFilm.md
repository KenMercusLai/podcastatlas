# 黑泽明《罗生门》 / Rashomon Film

黑泽明《罗生门》 / Rashomon is the film adaptation discussed in [[17-diyubian-zhongsheng-jie-ku-yi-nian-ru-mo-561780565]]. The episode explains that the film's main murder-and-testimony structure comes from [[InAGrove|《竹林中》]], while its gate setting and title come from [[RashomonAkutagawa|《罗生门》]].

The source emphasizes the adaptation's authorial force. By adding another viewpoint and framing the story under the gate, [[AkiraKurosawa|黑泽明]] does not merely transfer Akutagawa to film; he changes how audiences remember both stories and helps make [[RashomonEffect]] a broad cultural term for irreconcilable accounts.
