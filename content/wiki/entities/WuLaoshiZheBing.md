# 吴老师 / Wu Laoshi (Zhe Bing speaker)

## Overview
吴老师 is a source-scoped speaker in [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episodes on insect-bite escalation and the clinical consequences of concealed history.

## Current Profile
VOL.213 uses 吴老师 mainly as an experiential and regional-expansion voice. 吴老师 contributes a rove-beetle-like skin reaction example and adds southern-exposure concerns around chiggers, eschar, and rickettsial infection risk, helping the episode avoid treating every bite as an ordinary mosquito bump.

VOL.131 adds a clinical-history branch: when the patient's account does not fit the examination or disease pattern, clinicians may suspect missing information, and implausible explanations for a child's injury can create safeguarding concerns. The page remains narrow and records only these source-supported roles.

## Key Characteristics
- Supplies personal or experiential examples that make severe insect-related skin reactions concrete.
- Helps widen the episode from mosquitoes into rove beetles, chiggers, and southern field-exposure risks.
- Reinforces the distinction between ordinary itch and symptoms that should be taken to qualified care.
- Explains that mismatch between reported history and clinical findings can signal concealment or a safeguarding problem.
