# Betsy Shepherd

## Overview

Betsy Shepherd is a journalist investigating [[JohnAjay]]'s disappearance and the institutional response for [[ValleyOfShadowsPodcast|Valley of Shadows]].

## Current Profile

In [[from-valley-of-shadows-the-devil-s-punchbowl-from-valley-of-shadows-the-devil-s-punchbowl|The Devil's Punchbowl]], Shepherd works with [[HaleyFox]] to reconstruct Ajay's timeline, visit the Antelope Valley landscape, interview former colleagues and park personnel, and seek public records from the [[LosAngelesCountySheriffsDepartment]]. The episode frames the reporting task as two linked investigations: what happened to Ajay and why the department allegedly closed, categorized, and restricted the case as it did.

## Key Characteristics

- Investigative journalist and co-reporter on the Ajay case.
- Uses witness interviews, site reporting, insider testimony, and records requests.
- Keeps the disappearance and the institutional response analytically linked but unresolved.
- Receives explicit warnings about the possible risk of continued investigation.
