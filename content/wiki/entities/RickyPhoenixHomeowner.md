# Ricky (Phoenix Homeowner)

## Overview

Ricky is the Phoenix-area homeowner whose new-build purchase and move-in experience anchors [[ep160-wo-zai-meiguo-ziji-jian-fangzi-meiguo-ye-you-xuequ-fang-ckwriueecxq5abaaaacki6jm|EP160]]. The semantic suffix distinguishes him from the wiki's separate macro-investing commentator [[Ricky]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Ricky as a technically engaged first-time buyer who chose a builder-developed home after initially considering resale property. He combined family-supported financing with close observation of construction, selective builder upgrades, prewiring, owner-installed smart controls, solar planning, DIY installation, and a backlog of landscaping and furnishing work. His experience makes homeownership legible as both a financial commitment and an evolving hands-on relationship with a place.

## Key Characteristics
- Phoenix-area buyer choosing long-term settlement near work over short-term housing flexibility.
- Beneficiary of parental down-payment help and no student-loan burden, which he explicitly treats as an advantage.
- Hands-on owner who followed construction, planned infrastructure, and completed installation and improvement work himself.
- Buyer willing to trade a weaker school-district reputation for a preferred courtyard plan, commute, gated setting, and expected neighborhood development.
