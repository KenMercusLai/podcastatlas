# 范仪 / Fan Yi (Late Han)

范仪 / Fan Yi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-978-gushen-quantui-shuwan-dijun-ta-zenme-zuodao-de-lhgd8vsvuaf5oi9aefh0wxabmzwv|Hanji 978]] as the figure [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s side sends toward [[FanCountyLateHan|范县]] during [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] crisis. His source role is brief but decisive: once he enters the county, [[JinYunLateHan|靳允]] ambushes and kills him.

Fan Yi matters because his death marks the county's refusal to defect after [[ChengYu|程昱]] persuades Jin Yun. The episode therefore uses him as the visible test of whether family hostage pressure and province-wide panic will overcome local officeholder judgment.
