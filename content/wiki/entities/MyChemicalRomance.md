# My Chemical Romance

My Chemical Romance is the emo band used in [[working-memory-the-surprising-decline-of-dementia-6a6b0eeca585d3be78cd9d1b]] to ground [[EmoRevivalIdentityCulture]]. The source says the band broke up in 2013, began a world tour tied to the 20th anniversary of [[TheBlackParade]], and filled [[WembleyStadium|Wembley Stadium]] for three nights despite not releasing music since 2008.

The segment presents the band as more than a nostalgia act. [[GerardWay]]'s theatrical staging, political satire, costumes, and fan participation turn the shows into identity performance for original millennial fans, younger listeners, and families.
