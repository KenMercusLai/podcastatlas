# Kenneth Rogoff

## Overview
Kenneth Rogoff is an economist presented by [[PlanetMoney|Planet Money]] as [[CarmenReinhart]]'s coauthor on influential 2010 research and as a continuing advocate for changing the U.S. fiscal trajectory.

## Current Profile
Rogoff rejects both a mechanical 90%-of-GDP deadline and the inference that no precise threshold means unlimited debt is safe. In the 2026 update, he treats debt-service cost as more important than a single debt ratio and expects adjustment through taxes, spending, growth, or some combination before or after recurring rate and inflation shocks.

## Key Characteristics
- Coauthor of the Reinhart-Rogoff high-debt and growth research.
- Explicitly denies that the 90% category was intended as a precise crisis deadline.
- Frames rising debt as accumulated vulnerability rather than guaranteed immediate ruin.
- Emphasizes debt-service cost, fiscal trajectory, and capacity to absorb future shocks.
