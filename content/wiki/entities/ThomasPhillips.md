# Thomas Phillips

Thomas Phillips appears in [[696-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-killing-the-queen-of-scots-part-6-glt2334035496]] as [[FrancisWalsingham]]'s cryptanalyst. The episode says [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]]' reply to [[AnthonyBabington]] did not go first to Babington; it went to Phillips.

Phillips decrypts Mary's letter, copies it for Walsingham, draws a gallows on it, and labels it the "bloody letter." In the source, his work converts [[GilbertGifford]]'s controlled courier route into actionable evidence for the [[ActForTheQueensSafety|Act for the Queen's Safety]] prosecution.
