# 逢泽 / Fengze

逢泽 / Fengze appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-18-zhongguo-lishishang-di-yi-li-rentoushui-lgqnequk0rxejqgihwdqxqyqnsvq]] as the meeting site where [[GongziShaoguan|公子少官]] brings [[QinState|秦国]] troops to join the lords before going to audience with the Zhou king. The event belongs to [[ZhouHegemonRecognition|Zhou recognition of Qin hegemon status]] after [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]] is elevated among the vassals.

The source notes conflicting locations for Fengze: one tradition places it north of present-day Xingyang, while another maps it south of present-day Kaifeng. The host infers that a location west of [[Luoyi|洛邑]] would make more sense, because Qin forces would otherwise have to pass through the Zhou capital.
