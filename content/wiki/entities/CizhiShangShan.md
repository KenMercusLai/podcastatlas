# 《辞职上山》

《辞职上山》 is [[LiChuang|李闯]]'s book discussed in [[shan-shang-shan-xia-jie-renjian-wushi-xiaoyao-ji-shenxian-lhjiguq74flhatdxnbrnzhjg-acp]]. The episode uses the book title against its own easy reading: "辞职" and "上山" can sound like a clean exit from work and society, but the source insists that mountain life remains ordinary human life.

The book's wiki role is therefore not a lifestyle prescription. It is a nonfiction record of [[RetreatRomanceDeflation]] in which [[WudangMountain|武当山]] contains [[QuanzhenDaoism|全真道教]], scenic-area logistics, damp rooms, cold nights, animals, duty rosters, ritual form, young Daoist practitioners, and pilgrims who arrive with unsolved human problems.

## Key Claims
- The book is presented as a demystifying account of Wudang rather than a guide to quitting a job.
- Its evidence depends on proximity: chores, shifts, conversations with Daoist masters, observation of pilgrims, and Li Chuang's own bodily discomfort.
- Its central claim is that cultivation starts from returning to reality, not from leaving "the human world."
