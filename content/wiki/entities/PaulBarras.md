# Paul Barras

## Overview

Paul Barras was a Convention deputy who commanded forces against Robespierre during Thermidor, became a leading Directory figure, selected Napoleon during the Vendémiaire crisis, and became a decisive patron in Napoleon's rise.

## Current Profile

The sources present Barras as experienced, cynical, politically exposed, wealthy, and exceptionally connected. During the [[ThermidorianCoup|Thermidorian coup]], fear of Robespierre joined him to the defensive coalition and the Convention entrusted him with organizing forces against the Hôtel de Ville. After Thermidor, his brokerage matched the regime's need for a commander willing to fire on insurgents with [[NapoleonBonaparte|Napoleon]]'s ambition and artillery competence. His relationship with [[JosephineDeBeauharnais|Josephine]] also placed him at the junction of Napoleon's political and private advancement.

## Key Characteristics

- He had military experience, including service against Britain in India.
- He helped bring down Robespierre and became a leading figure in the Directory.
- He commanded the Convention's counter-mobilization after the Commune recovered the arrested Robespierrists.
- He recognized Napoleon as a useful commander during the Vendémiaire crisis.
- He translated the suppression of revolt into office and patronage for Napoleon.
- He linked political power, social networks, and Napoleon's relationship with Josephine.

- [[383-young-napoleon-the-shadow-of-the-guillotine-part-2-glt2658259929]] connects Barras's Thermidorian position to Napoleon's selection, the defeat of the uprising, and appointment as commander of the Army of the Interior.

### Thermidorian command

- [[712-the-terror-the-fall-of-robespierre-part-6-glt1154404105]] places Barras among threatened deputies and records his appointment to organize Convention forces against the Hôtel de Ville.

### Social and personal network

- [[383-young-napoleon-the-shadow-of-the-guillotine-part-2-glt2658259929]] connects Barras to Josephine and presents several possible motives for encouraging her relationship with Napoleon.

## Qualifications

The profile rests on two narrative episodes rather than an audited political biography. Barras's precise motives are inferred, while reported dialogue, corruption detail, sexual relationships, military command detail, and retrospective versions of Thermidor and Vendémiaire remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Extended Barras backward from post-Thermidorian brokerage to his threatened status and Convention command during the coup.
