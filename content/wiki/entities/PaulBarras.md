# Paul Barras

## Overview

Paul Barras was a Convention deputy and leading Directory figure who helped overthrow Robespierre, selected Napoleon during the Vendémiaire crisis, and became a decisive patron in Napoleon's rise.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Barras as experienced, cynical, corrupt, wealthy, and exceptionally connected. His importance lies in brokerage: after Thermidor he could match the regime's need for a commander willing to fire on insurgents with [[NapoleonBonaparte|Napoleon]]'s ambition and artillery competence. His relationship with [[JosephineDeBeauharnais|Josephine]] also placed him at the junction of Napoleon's political and private advancement.

## Key Characteristics

- He had military experience, including service against Britain in India.
- He helped bring down Robespierre and became a leading figure in the Directory.
- He recognized Napoleon as a useful commander during the Vendémiaire crisis.
- He translated the suppression of revolt into office and patronage for Napoleon.
- He linked political power, social networks, and Napoleon's relationship with Josephine.
