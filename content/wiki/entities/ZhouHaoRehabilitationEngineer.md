# 周浩 / Zhou Hao (rehabilitation engineer)

## Overview
周浩 / Zhou Hao is the rehabilitation-engineering guest in a 2024 [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode about musculoskeletal modeling, digital twins, elite-sport performance, injury prevention, and personalized rehabilitation.

## Current Profile
The source associates 周浩 with the [[NationalOrthopedicSportsRehabilitationClinicalResearchCenter|国家骨科与运动康复临床医学研究中心]] and describes his work as human musculoskeletal modeling and digital twins. He explains a spectrum from consumer wearables to motion capture, force measurement, image-based reconstruction, musculoskeletal models, and finite-element analysis.

His episode role is both technical and practical. He proposes uses in swimming, football, gait, equipment, insoles, clothing, and postoperative rehabilitation, while repeatedly returning to purpose, precision, sensor burden, affordability, and implementation. The profile therefore treats his examples as a map of possible applications rather than evidence that every application is mature or clinically effective.

## Key Characteristics
- Translates digital-twin ideas from industrial simulation into human movement and rehabilitation contexts.
- Connects motion capture, pressure or force data, medical imaging, musculoskeletal models, and simulation.
- Uses elite-sport examples to explain small-margin performance and training decisions.
- Extends the same modeling logic to gait, muscle behavior, insoles, equipment, and individualized rehabilitation.
- Treats cost, sensor precision, use case, and workflow integration as adoption constraints.
