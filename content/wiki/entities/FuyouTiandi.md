## Overview
蜉蝣天地 / Fuyou Tiandi is [[HanYang]]'s video-podcast project built around long-form conversations that let a guest's world, interests, objects, and concrete perceptions become visible.

## Current Profile
The show begins from an anti-compression stance: not every episode needs to deliver conclusions, forecasts, public advice, or obvious information gain. Across its current wiki sources, Fuyou Tiandi demonstrates that stance in nature, AI engineering, historical method, football, founder/crypto reflection, and religious-philosophical inquiry. The [[ChangpengZhao|CZ]] episode adds a shorter and more topical variant, while the [[BaoZhiguang|包智光]] interview uses a long multilingual conversation in a church to keep science, faith, freedom, love, conscience, and suffering open without forcing them into a debate win or a simple answer.

## Key Characteristics
- The show values [[LongFormConversation]] as a way to preserve hesitation, side paths, and private fascination.
- It treats video as part of the understanding surface because gestures, images, objects, architecture, and presence can carry context.
- It resists extracting guests only as experts, promoters, or trend forecasters.
- It can host technical topics without collapsing them into tool hype, as shown by the vector-model and RAG episode.
- It can host experiential and humanistic topics without reducing them to therapy or takeaway lessons.
- It uses guest-world structure across history, sports, nature, engineering, founder practice, and high-status business topics rather than a single subject vertical or strategy-only summary.
- The Bao Zhiguang episode shows the same method can hold confessional testimony, scientific limits, honest doubt, and unresolved suffering without collapsing them into apologetics or refutation.
