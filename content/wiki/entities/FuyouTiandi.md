## Overview
蜉蝣天地 / Fuyou Tiandi is [[HanYang]]'s video-podcast project built around long-form conversations that let a guest's world, interests, objects, and concrete perceptions become visible.

## Current Profile
The show begins from an anti-compression stance: not every episode needs to deliver conclusions, forecasts, public advice, or obvious information gain. Across its current wiki sources, Fuyou Tiandi demonstrates that stance in nature, AI engineering, historical method, football, and founder/crypto reflection. The [[ChangpengZhao|CZ]] episode adds a shorter and more topical variant: instead of a systematic business interview, it uses message handling, sleep, stress, regulatory hindsight, blockchain adoption, and education work to show how a high-pressure founder thinks and keeps moving.

## Key Characteristics
- The show values [[LongFormConversation]] as a way to preserve hesitation, side paths, and private fascination.
- It treats video as part of the understanding surface because gestures, images, objects, architecture, and presence can carry context.
- It resists extracting guests only as experts, promoters, or trend forecasters.
- It can host technical topics without collapsing them into tool hype, as shown by the vector-model and RAG episode.
- It can host experiential and humanistic topics without reducing them to therapy or takeaway lessons.
- It uses guest-world structure across history, sports, nature, engineering, and founder practice rather than a single subject vertical.
- The CZ episode shows the format can handle high-status business and crypto topics through operating texture instead of only strategy summary.
