## Overview
蜉蝣天地 / Fuyou Tiandi is [[HanYang]]'s video-podcast project built around long-form conversations that let a guest's world, interests, objects, and concrete perceptions become visible.

## Current Profile
The show begins from an anti-compression stance: not every episode needs to deliver conclusions, forecasts, public advice, or obvious information gain. Across its current wiki sources, Fuyou Tiandi demonstrates that stance in nature, AI engineering, historical method, football, founder/crypto reflection, religious-philosophical inquiry, and wine culture. The [[ChangpengZhao|CZ]] episode adds a shorter and more topical variant, the [[BaoZhiguang|包智光]] interview keeps science, faith, and suffering open, and the [[MengLeiChampagne|孟磊]] conversation moves freely across sensory pleasure, agricultural technique, appellation law, climate, commerce, and China-France cultural translation without reducing [[Champagne|香槟]] to either luxury or liquid.

## Key Characteristics
- The show values [[LongFormConversation]] as a way to preserve hesitation, side paths, and private fascination.
- It treats video as part of the understanding surface because gestures, images, objects, architecture, and presence can carry context.
- It resists extracting guests only as experts, promoters, or trend forecasters.
- It can host technical, experiential, and humanistic topics without collapsing them into tool hype, therapy, or takeaway lessons.
- It uses guest-world structure across history, sports, nature, engineering, founder practice, and high-status business topics rather than a single subject vertical or strategy-only summary.
- The Bao Zhiguang episode shows the same method can hold confessional testimony, scientific limits, honest doubt, and unresolved suffering without collapsing them into apologetics or refutation.
- The Meng Lei episode shows that a consumer object can be understood simultaneously through pleasure, skilled production, institutional rules, climate exposure, and cultural mediation.

## Qualifications
The wiki currently sees the show through eight source notes. Its broader catalog, production model, and audience reception are not fully represented here. The CZ episode is explicitly framed as a shorter friend-like conversation rather than a comprehensive interview; the Bao episode records one Catholic priest's testimony rather than a comparative survey; and the Champagne episode is a practitioner's account with an unresolved 孟蕾/孟磊 title-body identity discrepancy.

## What Changed
- Added the CZ episode as a founder, crypto, and pressure-management case for the show's guest-world method.
- Added the Bao Zhiguang episode as a religion, philosophy, and science-boundary case that preserves unresolved doubt and suffering.
- Added the Meng Lei episode as a wine, appellation-governance, climate, and China-France cultural-mediation case.
