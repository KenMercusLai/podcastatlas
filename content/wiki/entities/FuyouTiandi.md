## Overview
蜉蝣天地 / Fuyou Tiandi is [[HanYang]]'s video-podcast project built around long-form conversations that let a guest's world, interests, objects, and concrete perceptions become visible.

## Current Profile
The show begins from an anti-compression stance: not every episode needs to deliver conclusions, forecasts, public advice, or obvious information gain. Across its current wiki sources, Fuyou Tiandi demonstrates that stance in several domains. The program-statement episode explains the format; the [[RenNing]] episode shows non-instrumental natural observation; the [[NStudent]] episode makes technical retrieval bottlenecks legible; and the [[LiJiHistorian|李纪]] episode uses history, archives, and fieldwork to ask how ordinary people pass through large history.

## Key Characteristics
- The show values [[LongFormConversation]] as a way to preserve hesitation, side paths, and private fascination.
- It treats video as part of the understanding surface because gestures, images, objects, architecture, and presence can carry context.
- It resists extracting guests only as experts, promoters, or trend forecasters.
- It can host technical topics without collapsing them into tool hype, as shown by the vector-model and RAG episode.
- It can host experiential and humanistic topics without reducing them to therapy or takeaway lessons.
- The Li Ji episode extends the format into historical method, ordinary-life evidence, and source-grounded complexity.
