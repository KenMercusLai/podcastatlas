## Overview

[[WeiFengLateHan|魏讽]] is a late-Han official whose talent, eloquence, and reputation win [[ZhongYao|钟繇]]'s recommendation before his planned uprising at [[YeLateHan|邺城]] is disclosed and suppressed in 219 CE.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1167 presents Wei Feng as socially influential and capable of recruiting followers, but also as a case where visibility and ambition outrun secure timing. 郑袤 reportedly detects danger in his conduct, while Zhong Yao emphasizes talent and helps him enter office. Wei Feng later organizes a plot intended to exploit Cao-side external commitments, but participant [[ChenYiChangleWeiwei|陈祎]] reports it to [[CaoPi|曹丕]] before action begins.

## Key Characteristics

- Politically ambitious official with a strong reputation for eloquence and influence.
- Beneficiary of Zhong Yao's personnel judgment and sponsorship.
- Organizer who recruits actors inside the Ye political and military center.
- Failed conspirator whose coalition collapses before execution begins.
- Negative case for separating visible talent from conduct, motive, and timing judgment.

## Qualifications

The current profile rests on one structured podcast summary rather than a transcript, primary-text edition, or full biography. Exact offices, participant scope, dialogue, motives, execution totals, and the claim that Cao Pi deliberately widened the case into a purge require broader verification. Reputation and persuasive ability do not independently establish either guilt or the complete cause of failure.
