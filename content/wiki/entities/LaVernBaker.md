# LaVern Baker

## Overview

LaVern Baker is the Black R&B performer whose “Tweedle Dee” arrangement is presented in [[in-a-metal-mood-in-a-metal-mood]] as the source closely reproduced by [[GeorgiaGibbs]].

## Current Profile

The episode uses Baker's recording to define the harm of insufficient creative distance. Gibbs's celebrated cover is said to reproduce the arrangement closely, making the adaptation more capable of substituting for Baker under unequal market access than of directing listeners back to her.

## Key Characteristics

- R&B source performer associated with “Tweedle Dee.”
- Originator in the episode's clearest close-copying comparison.
- Illustrates how distribution advantage can turn similarity into displacement.
