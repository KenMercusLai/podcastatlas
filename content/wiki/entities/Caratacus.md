# Caratacus

## Overview

Caratacus was a Catuvellaunian leader who helped displace [[Verica]], resisted the AD 43 Roman invasion, and sustained opposition from Wales until his capture in AD 51.

## Current Profile

Episode 500 presents Caratacus as both a cause of Roman intervention and the conquest's most persistent early opponent. His expansion against Verica's Atrebatic position helped give [[Claudius]] a client-protection justification. After defeat by [[AulusPlautius]]'s invasion force, he escaped west rather than accept collaboration.

Among the Silures and other Welsh groups, Caratacus brought experience of Roman methods into a difficult terrain war. His resistance ended after a pitched defeat and flight to Cartimandua, a Roman ally who handed him over. Taken to Rome, he impressed his captors and was spared, turning a defeated enemy into another demonstration of imperial power and clemency.

## Key Characteristics

- Catuvellaunian dynast whose expansion helped precipitate Roman intervention.
- Survivor of the initial AD 43 defeat.
- Organizer and symbol of prolonged Welsh resistance.
- Opponent whose capture depended on another British ruler's alliance with Rome.
- Captive whose survival allowed Rome to stage both domination and clemency.

## Qualifications

The episode depends heavily on Roman literary accounts and cannot recover Caratacus's own strategy or words directly. The composition and continuity of the Welsh coalition, Cartimandua's motives, the capture sequence, and the famous Roman performance of clemency remain filtered through imperial sources.

## What Changed

- Created a profile connecting southeastern expansion to prolonged Welsh resistance.
- Added collaboration among British rulers as a condition of his capture.
- Framed his pardon as part of imperial victory performance.

## Relationships

- [[Verica]] - client ruler displaced by Caratacus's expansion.
- [[AulusPlautius]] - Roman commander whose invasion forced him west.
- [[Claudius]] - emperor who displayed and spared him after capture.
- [[RomanConquestOfBritain]] - conflict in which he became the leading early resistance figure.
- [[RomanBritain]] - emerging provincial order he resisted.
