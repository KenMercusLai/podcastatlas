# 崔嘉宾 / Cui Jiabin

## Overview
崔嘉宾 / Cui Jiabin is a psychiatrist and guest in a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode using [[LoveTranslationKDrama|《爱情怎么翻译》]] to discuss attachment, shame, indirect communication, dissociation-like split expression, and self-facing in intimacy.

## Current Profile
The source presents 崔嘉宾 as the guest who keeps the drama reading connected to clinical language while preserving diagnostic boundaries. She interprets "push away" behavior as possible self-protection after attachment injury, treats "you go" as a concealed request for support, and reads the second-personality device as a dramatized split between over-adapted social self and unspoken desire.

Her practical emphasis is not on diagnosing characters or listeners. It is on noticing fear, control, shame, and real needs; replacing pure guessing with feedback; and treating love as one possible mirror for growth rather than as guaranteed therapy.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses psychiatric concepts to interpret relationship behavior without turning a drama discussion into formal diagnosis.
- Connects push-pull intimacy to attachment insecurity, shame, trauma memory, and defensive self-protection.
- Emphasizes communication feedback, self-observation, and the need to translate one's own inner state.
- Treats love as potentially healing but unreliable unless paired with self-facing, action, and growth.
- Keeps personality-disorder and dissociation language source-scoped and cautious.
