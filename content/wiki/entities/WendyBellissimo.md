# Wendy Bellissimo

Wendy Bellissimo is the premium crib-bedding designer mentioned in [[serena-lily-serena-dugan-and-lily-kanter-they-built-a-20m-brand-then-one-investor-almost-destroyed-it-31a4ddcd-1cc1-48d0-9369-fd85e8714e66]]. In [[LilyKanter]]'s account, Bellissimo told independent specialty stores around Memorial Day weekend 2004 that she was moving her premium crib-bedding brand to Babies "R" Us.

That channel move created a timing opening for [[SerenaAndLily|Serena & Lily]]. Its first catalog reached specialty retailers just as they were losing an incumbent premium supplier, making the new brand more legible and urgent than it would have been in a full channel.

## Key Claims
- Bellissimo's move is used as the episode's clearest market-opening moment.
- The event shows how a competitor's channel decision can create distribution pull for a new entrant.
- Serena & Lily's timing mattered because specialty stores were ready for a premium replacement before the founders had fully mature operations.
