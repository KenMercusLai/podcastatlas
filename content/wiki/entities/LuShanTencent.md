# Lu Shan / 卢山

Lu Shan, or 卢山, appears in [[yao-shunyu-laidao-tengxun-300tian-1-176-1]] as the [[TencentTEG]] president to whom [[YaoShunyu]] also reports while rebuilding [[TencentHunyuan]]. The source gives him less narrative weight than [[MartinLau]], but his role matters because Hunyuan's roots and reporting line remain tied to Tencent's technical and engineering organization.
