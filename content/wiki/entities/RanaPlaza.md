# Rana Plaza

Rana Plaza appears in [[getting-entrepreneurial-in-korea-summer-school]] as the disaster that keeps [[Bangladesh]]'s garment boom from being read as a simple development success story. The episode says the factory collapse killed more than a thousand people and uses it to mark the cost of fast industrial growth under weak institutions and infrastructure.

In the wiki, Rana Plaza qualifies [[TextileKnowledgeTransfer]] and the [[IndustrialLadder]]. Garment manufacturing can create employment and export capacity, but without safety, regulation, and public capacity, the same production model can expose workers to catastrophic risk.
