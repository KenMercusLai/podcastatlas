# 秦惠公 / Duke Hui of Qin

秦惠公 / Duke Hui of Qin appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-1-lvkp-ju7pf5z5cz3thojv3s2fkyx]] as [[QinJianGong|秦简公]]'s son and successor. The source keeps him within a compact succession sequence that also includes [[HanState|韩国]] and [[ZhaoState|赵国]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-2-lvaqlgyccrugfo0l-wuwf1mt1kcm]] records Qin Hui Gong's death in 387 BCE and the accession of his son [[QinChugong|秦出公]]. The episode again uses him as part of compact early Warring States chronology rather than as a detailed biography.
