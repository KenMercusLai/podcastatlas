# Laurie Santos

## Overview
Laurie Santos is the Yale psychologist interviewed in [[how-to-achieve-true-happiness-using-science-based-protocols-dr-laurie-santos-scim5061134579|How to Achieve True Happiness Using Science-Based Protocols]]. The episode presents her as a researcher and educator translating happiness science into everyday experiments involving connection, attention, time, gratitude, emotion, work, and generosity.

## Current Profile
Santos's organizing claim is that people frequently miswant: they overestimate how much changed circumstances, status, income, achievement, or low-effort digital reward will improve life and underestimate live social contact, helping, presence, time affluence, and using personal strengths. Her account of happiness includes both emotional experience and cognitive life evaluation, so it does not reduce wellbeing to constant positive affect.

She also treats negative feeling and adaptation as functional constraints. Sadness, loneliness, anger, grief, and overwhelm may carry information, while positive outcomes lose intensity through adaptation. Her practical approach therefore combines behavior change with attention to comparison, finitude, savoring, and process rather than promising a permanently elevated mood.

## Key Characteristics
- Studies happiness as both emotional experience and reflective life evaluation.
- Emphasizes systematic forecasting errors about money, achievement, social interaction, and digital convenience.
- Recommends live connection, attention, gratitude or delight, time affluence, helping, and strengths use as testable practices.
- Treats negative emotions as potentially informative rather than evidence of failed happiness.
- Uses hedonic adaptation, comparison, and mortality awareness to explain why savoring requires active renewal.
