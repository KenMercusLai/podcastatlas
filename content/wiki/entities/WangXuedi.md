# 王雪迪 / Wang Xuedi

## Overview
王雪迪 is an emergency physician appearing in [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] VOL.120, where she describes the interdependence between emergency departments and ambulance crews and the physical and safety burden of repeated prehospital calls.

## Current Profile
In [[vol-120-zenme-da-zenme-shuo-zenme-jie-he-5-wei-yiwu-gongzuozhe-liaoliao-120-jijiu-de-naxie-gushi-lqpbn4u9wc7uutce2hgk7cl77wla|VOL.120]], 王雪迪 speaks from the receiving emergency-department side while expressing respect for 120 crews. She describes night shifts with repeated dispatches, delayed clock-off, motion sickness, heavy lifting, intoxicated or violent patients, and the difficulty of delivering urgent treatment in uncontrolled settings. Her contribution adds workforce sustainability to [[PrehospitalEmergencyMedicalResponse]] rather than treating ambulance availability as only a vehicle count.

The VOL.99 source names an emergency speaker as 王雪笛 and covers public recognition of cardiac arrest, hypertensive emergency, stroke, myocardial infarction, and hypoglycemia, plus the boundary around household emergency medicines. The matching pronunciation, role, and show context make linkage plausible, but the different written character is retained as an explicit source uncertainty rather than silently normalized.

## Key Characteristics
- Identified in the source as an emergency physician.
- Explains the close operational relationship between emergency departments and 120 crews.
- Describes high-frequency night dispatch and delayed end-of-shift work.
- Emphasizes physical strain from vehicle motion, lifting, and field treatment.
- Highlights violence and accidental injury as occupational risks for prehospital staff.
- Calls for greater public understanding and support for ambulance workers.
- Teaches family-facing emergency recognition and early 120 escalation in the VOL.99 source, subject to a name-spelling qualification.

## Qualifications
