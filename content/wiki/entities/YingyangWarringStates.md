# 荥阳 / Yingyang (Warring States)

荥阳 / Yingyang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-103-1-simaqian-kanzhong-de-xinlingjun-suan-shi-xiaoren-ma-lvty1pdnibvqpk0m16kkxzw4t-ck]] as a Qin-held point near [[Xingze|荥泽]] in the campaign setting around [[GuanchengWarringStates|管城]]. The episode says Yingyang has already been absorbed into [[SanchuanWarringStates|三川]] commandery.

Its role is not a stand-alone battle narrative. Yingyang helps make the map legible: Qin's control north-west of Xingze and around Guancheng leaves [[Daliang|大梁]] exposed to water and route pressure even after [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] defeats [[MengAo|蒙敖]] in the field.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-3-chensheng-wuguang-zudui-chenggong-locnaq-fo1fztyud5nghwkteams|Qinji 128-3]] adds Yingyang as an early westward target of [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]]. After [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] declares himself Chu king, he makes [[WuGuang|吴广]] acting Zhang Chu king and sends him to supervise generals attacking Yingyang. The source does not yet narrate the battle result; it uses Yingyang to mark Zhang Chu's push from Chen County toward Qin-held strategic space.
