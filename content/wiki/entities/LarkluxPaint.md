# Larklux Paint

Larklux Paint is [[LariatAlhassan]]'s Abuja paint business in [[piles-of-cash-and-a-town-of-solutions-in-kenya-nigeria-summer-school]]. The episode uses the company as a concrete example of a viable tiny firm that could not easily signal credibility or scale without capital.

After Lariat won a [[YouWinProgram|YouWin]] grant, the company gained sales and marketing help and a proper showroom. In the source's development frame, Larklux is a small example of how [[DirectEntrepreneurGrants]] can help a business cross part of the [[SmallBusinessFinancingGap]], even though later financing remained hard.
