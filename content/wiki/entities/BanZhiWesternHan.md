## Overview

班至 / Ban Zhi enters the wiki as the Guangping chancellor in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-625-jiemi-renxing-anmian-weihe-pangren-chenggong-zong-ling-women-xinsheng-jihen-li64ngkv8nujwegbix6iororjmbo|Hanji 625]] whose refusal or failure to report auspicious signs becomes dangerous under [[WangMang|王莽]]'s praise-heavy 5 CE court. His survival depends on [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s distinction between non-praise and disaster fabrication, plus his own visible retreat into a lower cemetery-guard role.

## Current Profile

The current source presents Ban Zhi as a nonconforming official rather than an open rebel. He does not join the courtwide production of auspicious reports and praise songs, which makes him vulnerable once [[ZhenFengXinOfficial|甄丰]] encourages local accusations against him and [[GongsunHongLangyaWesternHan|公孙洪]].

Ban Zhi survives because his case has a protectable boundary. Wang Zhengjun remembers his family link to [[BanJieyuWesternHan|班婕妤]] and says that failing to publicize virtue is not the same as fabricating disaster reports. Ban Zhi then reads Gongsun Hong's death as a warning, thanks the court, returns his chancellor seal, and asks to serve at Emperor Cheng's Yanling. The episode treats this as survival through lowered visibility, not vindication of innocence by normal law.

## Key Characteristics

- Guangping chancellor whose nonparticipation in auspicious reporting becomes politically exposed.
- Ban-family figure protected indirectly through Wang Zhengjun's memory of Ban Jieyu.
- Survival case where an official avoids death by accepting demotion and reducing political visibility.
- Contrast figure to Gongsun Hong, whose more dangerous disaster talk and lack of protection end in execution.

## Qualifications

This page is source-bounded to Hanji 625. It does not reconstruct Ban Zhi's full career, the exact legal status of the accusation, or whether his silence came from principle, caution, administrative habit, or factional distance.
