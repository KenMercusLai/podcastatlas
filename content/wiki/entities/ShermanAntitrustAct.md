# Sherman Antitrust Act

The Sherman Antitrust Act appears in [[the-invention-invention]] as the U.S. competition-law backdrop for deciding when a [[PatentPool]] becomes illegal coordination. The source says the glass-container case violated the Sherman Act because the pool restricted output, fixed prices, and blocked competitors.

In the episode, the Act matters less as doctrine than as the reason [[CollaborationVsCollusion]] has legal stakes. A pool can make invention usable, but the same collective structure can suppress competition if it controls price, output, or access.
