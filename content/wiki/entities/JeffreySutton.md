# Jeffrey Sutton

## Overview

Jeffrey Sutton is a U.S. federal appellate judge and former Supreme Court clerk whom [[the-tortoise-and-the-hare-the-tortoise-and-the-hare|The Tortoise and the Hare]] presents as a counterexample to prestige-based legal selection.

## Current Profile

The episode says Sutton attended Ohio State after Michigan rejected him, later clerked for [[AntoninScalia]], became a federal appellate judge, and earned recognition for legal scholarship and Supreme Court advocacy. Scalia reportedly regarded him as one of his finest clerks while conceding that his normal elite-school rule would initially have excluded him.

Sutton's importance in the source is diagnostic rather than merely biographical. His career shows how a slower, deliberate cognitive style can be poorly represented by a timed admissions signal yet highly valuable in work requiring sustained reading and analysis.

## Key Characteristics

- Followed a non-T14 legal-education path through Ohio State after rejection by Michigan.
- Became a Supreme Court clerk despite the prestige filter Scalia said he normally used.
- Was later praised by Scalia as one of his finest clerks.
- Built a career as a federal appellate judge, scholar, and Supreme Court advocate.
- Represents the episode's “tortoise” style of careful, persistent legal reasoning.
