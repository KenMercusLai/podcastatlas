# Starwood Hotels and Resorts

Starwood Hotels and Resorts appears in [[140-jiudian-jituan-weishenme-dou-rezhongyu-dazao-huiyuan-he-jifen-tixi-chuantai-yuanxingzhe-yu-suibingjiang-lj-xa3wxzjog6-8zm9nfakh6ulew]] as the hotel group acquired by [[MarriottInternational]] in 2016. The source treats Starwood as important because its brands and [[StarwoodPreferredGuest]] loyalty program created unusually strong member attachment.

The episode uses Starwood to show that hotel acquisitions can be about more than rooms or management contracts. They can also transfer brand meaning, lifestyle positioning, and a community of high-value repeat guests into a larger [[HotelBrandPortfolio]].
