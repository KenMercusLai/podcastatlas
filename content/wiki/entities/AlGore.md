# Al Gore

## Overview
Al Gore is presented in the bounded source as a U.S. political and climate leader who helped move climate change from scientific discussion into public, political, and economic debate.

## Current Profile
[[JuanVerde]] describes working with Gore to establish Climate Reality Project chapters and credits him with two linked lessons: climate advocacy needs public institutions and awareness, while business adoption at scale requires a credible economic case as well as moral conviction. The source therefore places Gore at the bridge between climate communication and [[SustainabilityAsCompetitiveStrategy]].

## Key Characteristics
- Political figure associated in the source with climate awareness and public advocacy.
- Mentor and collaborator in Verde's transition toward climate and sustainability work.
- Advocate for connecting moral purpose with a practical business case for change.
