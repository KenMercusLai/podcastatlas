## Overview

史小翠 is a [[XiakeXingNovel|《侠客行》]] character whose arranged marriage to [[BaiZizai|白自在]], martial resistance, and remembered alternative in [[DingBusi|丁不四]] organize the episode's account of resentment, loyalty, and long companionship.

## Current Profile

The episode does not treat her references to Ding Busi as transparent proof that she preferred him. It reads them partly as protest: Bai Zizai married her but did not respect her, so the unrealized alternative becomes a weapon and an imagined measure of a better life. Creating the Golden Crow saber method similarly converts subordination into active resistance.

Resistance also enters the couple's injury cycle. Repeated comparison attacks Bai Zizai's insecurity and helps produce renewed domination, even though the original power imbalance is not erased. Her later rejection of Ding Busi and return to Bai Zizai support a more layered attachment made of love, kinship, habit, history, and responsibility rather than a simple choice between two suitors.

## Key Characteristics

- Entered marriage through family arrangement rather than a clearly narrated autonomous choice.
- Resists marital hierarchy through martial invention and verbal comparison.
- Uses an unrealized alternative life as both consolation and weapon.
- Rejects the inference that decades of complaint prove continuing preference for Ding Busi.
- Returns to Bai Zizai with a bond shaped by shared history as well as romantic feeling.

## Qualifications

The motives behind her comparisons are inferred rather than directly established. Explaining them as protest does not make retaliatory injury harmless, while describing the later bond as love, kinship, or habit remains an interpretive choice rather than access to a single inner truth.
