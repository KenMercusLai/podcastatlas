# 食物学堂 / Food School

## Overview

食物学堂 is a small educational institution founded by [[OuyangYanqin|欧阳艳琴]] in 2018 to explore education and occupational transition for young people, especially children of migrant workers who cannot rely on a continuous conventional academic path.

## Current Profile

Food School's current source-described form is a one-plus-two model: students spend one year preparing for self-support and then receive two years of continuing contact and transition support while entering work. The model replaced an earlier three-year program that combined whole-person education with programming, graphic design, glasswork, and other specialties.

The school now treats career exploration, real responsibilities, community participation, workplace exposure, and weekly individual conversations as ways to develop self-knowledge and agency. It asks applicants whether they are prepared to work after one year, avoids promising a matching job or credential, and treats a first job as a developmental starting point rather than a final placement.

## Key Characteristics

- Mission summarized as cultivating valuable ordinary people rather than standardized success.
- One year of school followed by two years of transition and community support.
- Real responsibilities in cooking, administration, events, community service, commercial orders, internships, and paid work.
- Career exploration organized around work scenes, practitioners, preferences, strengths, and limitations.
- Emphasis on agency, reliable action, communication, collaboration, problem-solving, and help-seeking.
- Rules centered on self-care, non-harm, and helping others when able.
- Mixed service-income and donation model with deliberately limited claims about scale.

## Qualifications

The institution is represented by one founder interview rather than independent observation, audited outcomes, or a longitudinal graduate study. Reported student trajectories show possibilities but cannot separate program effects from selection, prior traits, family context, or later employer support. The one-plus-two model's financial durability, reach, comparative effectiveness, and transferability remain unresolved.

## What Changed

- Established the current one-plus-two model as a transition system rather than a short credential program.
- Preserved the failed employment assumptions behind the move away from fixed specialties.
