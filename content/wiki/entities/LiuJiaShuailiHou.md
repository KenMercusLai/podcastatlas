## Overview

刘嘉 / Liu Jia enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-629-liujiaren-shui-kangqi-fankang-wangmang-de-daqi-lmhws7-jptdh9tf45bqtlfvzdw55|Hanji 629]] as a distant Liu-family relative of [[LiuChongAnzhongMarquis|安众侯刘崇]] who survives the failed rising by surrendering and presenting a loyalist petition to [[WangMang|王莽]].

## Current Profile

Liu Jia is the beneficiary side of Zhang Song's petitionary strategy. After Liu Chong's attack fails, Liu Jia fears implication, submits himself to court, and receives pardon. [[ZhangSongXinPetitioner|张竦]] writes a memorial in his name praising Wang Mang and denouncing Liu Chong.

The result is reward rather than punishment. Wang Mang makes Liu Jia Shuaili Hou and makes his seven sons Guannei marquises. In the source's political reading, Liu Jia shows how some Liu relatives survive Wang Mang's advance by publicly separating themselves from anti-usurpation resistance.

## Key Characteristics

- Distant Liu-family relative connected to Liu Chong's failed rebellion.
- Responds to exposure through self-reporting and loyalist denunciation.
- Benefits from Zhang Song's petition language.
- Receives Shuaili Hou title and rewards for his sons.
- Illustrates Liu-clan fragmentation under Wang Mang's pressure.
