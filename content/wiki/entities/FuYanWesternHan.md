## Overview

傅晏 / Fu Yan is synthesized as a late [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] court actor whose current profile now has a title-flattery layer and a later frontier-escalation layer. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-577-guanchang-ni-taotai-weishenme-yongguan-sheng-de-gengkuai-lopfolhcheqohjagb9gxt6cvsmtu|Hanji 577]] shows him as [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]]'s Kongxiang Hou kinsman, flattering her and working with [[ZhuBoWesternHan|朱博]] to push her title demand while accusing resisters. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-591-1-yichang-bingjia-ruhe-xianxie-yinbao-liangguo-zhizhan-lrq-xdhvrnuarzwgrykp7j-x8j-a|Hanji 591-1]] later places him with [[XiFuGongWesternHan|息夫躬]] in a faction that uses a delayed [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] chanyu visit as an opening for advancement.

## Current Profile

Hanji 577 gives Fu Yan a clearer court-politics role than the later frontier episode. He flatters Fu Taihou, obeys her wishes, and works with Zhu Bo after Zhu Bo becomes Jingzhao Yin. Their political value comes from translating Fu Taihou's status desire into language Aidi can use: promoting the title can be presented as filial piety, while opposition from [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] and [[FuXiWesternHan|傅喜]] can be described as selfishness, faction, and failure to help government.

Hanji 591-1 keeps Fu Yan on the ambition side of the debate. He is not the author of the detailed plan in that source note; Xi Fu Gong supplies the argument about Wusun weakness, false envoys, intentional leakage, border inspection, and exemplary killing. Fu Yan's significance is that he belongs to the factional field that makes such a security escalation politically useful.

Fu Yan therefore currently marks a broader advancement pattern: he can attach himself to a powerful patron's desired title outcome in one episode and to a merit-making frontier crisis narrative in another.

## Key Characteristics

- Late Western Han court figure active under Han Aidi.
- Fu-family kinsman who flatters Fu Taihou and follows her title demand.
- Works with Zhu Bo to accuse title resisters as self-protective or factional.
- Paired with Xi Fu Gong in a later advancement-seeking faction.
- Uses the chanyu's illness-delayed visit as a political opportunity.
- Represents the incentive to convert patron desire or uncertain frontier signals into advancement channels.

## Qualifications

This page is limited to Hanji 577 and Hanji 591-1. Hanji 577 supports Fu Yan's title-flattery and accusation role but not his full office history, private motive, or complete relationship to Fu Taihou. Hanji 591-1 does not reconstruct Fu Yan's full career, later punishment, exact office history, or independent policy program apart from his pairing with Xi Fu Gong.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 577's earlier Fu Taihou title-flattery and accusation layer.
- Reframed Fu Yan as an advancement-seeking court actor across title politics and frontier-crisis narration.
- Preserved Hanji 591-1's late-Aidi Xiongnu illness-delay debate as a later layer.
