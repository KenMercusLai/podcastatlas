# A Brief History of Chips / 芯片简史

A Brief History of Chips / 《芯片简史》 is the book by [[WangBo|汪波]] named in [[zhenzheng-gaibian-shijie-de-jishu-weishenme-yikaishi-dou-bu-bei-kanhao-s10e16-8c95b3dc-d75a-4bdd-84d4-2c06fd2d85b1]]. The episode uses the book's semiconductor-history background to move from current AI demand for GPUs, CPUs, and memory chips into older adoption stories around [[MooreLaw]], integration, and [[MOSFET|MOS]].

In the source, the book functions as an entry point into the pattern that early technology judgments are often path-dependent. Technologies that are expensive, unstable, or under-tooled at birth can become central once [[SemiconductorSupplyChain]] learning, [[ElectronicDesignAutomation|EDA]], and manufacturing iteration change the surrounding system.
