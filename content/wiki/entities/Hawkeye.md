# Hawkeye

The source uses Hawkeye to make [[BaseballStrikeZoneAutomation]] concrete. ABS is not presented as generic "AI"; it is a measured sports-technology stack that depends on cameras, calibration, strike-zone placement, and rules for what happens when tracking fails.
