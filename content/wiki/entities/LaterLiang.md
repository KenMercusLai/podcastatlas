# 后梁 / Later Liang

Later Liang / 后梁 is the first of the five central-plain dynasties in [[FiveDynastiesTenKingdoms|五代十国]] as presented by [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]]. The episode places it in the phase after [[HuangChao|黄巢]]'s rebellion, when late Tang order had broken and rival military powers competed for central legitimacy.

The source uses the Later Liang-[[LaterTang|后唐]] struggle as part of its first periodization: "天下大乱、群雄并起" and then a northern contest over whether central power could be rebuilt.
