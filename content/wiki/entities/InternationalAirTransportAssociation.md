# International Air Transport Association / IATA

## Overview
The International Air Transport Association / IATA is cited in the Shengdong Jixi aviation roundtable for the contrast between air cargo's small weight share and high economic-value share.

## Current Profile
IATA's role in the current wiki is evidence-adjacent rather than institutional. Ma Xiao invokes an IATA statistic to make aviation cargo legible: air freight may represent a small share of physical tonnage while carrying a much larger share of cargo value.

That citation supports the wiki's new air-cargo branch by explaining why aircraft matter disproportionately for fresh goods, vaccines, electronics, and other high-value or time-sensitive items.

## Key Characteristics
- Aviation-industry organization cited for cargo-value statistics.
- Evidence anchor for separating tonnage from economic value in air freight.
- Source-scoped statistical reference rather than a full institutional profile.
