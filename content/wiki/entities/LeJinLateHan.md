# 乐进 / Le Jin (late Han)

## Overview

乐进 / Le Jin enters the wiki as a Cao-side commander whose strategically adjacent force must be coordinated with [[YuJin|于禁]] despite personal incompatibility.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1088 places Le Jin in the central defense arrangement before [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s southern campaign. The source portrays him as brave and forceful but difficult to coordinate with Yu Jin, leading Cao Cao to send [[ZhaoYanLateHan|赵俨]] as a delegated mediator across the commands.

## Key Characteristics

- Cao-side field commander in the 208 central-defense branch.
- Presented as brave and forceful in the episode's character contrast.
- Strategically adjacent to Yu Jin but not naturally cooperative with him.
- Participates in a command problem solved through delegated coordination.
