# CJ Finn

CJ Finn appears in [[tech-20260407-0407-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260407-0407-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the [[PwC]] voice describing U.S. EV adoption as an economics problem. Finn says the battery-electric premium over internal-combustion vehicles needs to shrink before adoption accelerates.

The episode uses Finn to turn [[ElectricVehiclePriceParity]] into a sales-mix forecast. Once parity arrives, Finn expects U.S. new-vehicle sales to split much more evenly across EVs, hybrids, and internal-combustion vehicles.
