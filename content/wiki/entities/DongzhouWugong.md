# 东周武公 / Dongzhou Wugong

东周武公 / Dongzhou Wugong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-76-shui-geile-chuqingxiangwang-de-zixin-lsut4cfic7xbbrmbmimrruf7n6y0]] as the envoy [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]] sends after hearing that [[ChuState|楚国]] may attack the [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]]. He goes to Chu's令尹昭子 and argues that attacking Zhou would not pay.

His persuasion is a compact case of [[ZhouRoyalSymbolicDeterrence|Zhou royal symbolic deterrence]]. Zhou's land and people are too small to make Chu richer or stronger, but the royal house still carries the name of the Son of Heaven; an attack could look like regicide, and taking [[Jiuding|九鼎]] could bring wider military pressure against Chu.
