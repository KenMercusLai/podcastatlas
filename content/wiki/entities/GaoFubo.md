## Overview

高福波 / Gao Fubo enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-496-qiong-pa-le-jiemi-tanguan-zouxiang-tanfu-de-xinli-youyin-lnskbcqvjhtpfi4ikq44kpbbfnol|Hanji 496]] as a modern corruption case used to open a historical and managerial discussion about whether "穷怕了" can explain or excuse official greed.

## Current Profile

The source does not develop Gao Fubo as a full contemporary political biography. It uses him as the first example in a moral contrast: his poverty explanation is paired with the fictional Zhao Dehan and then contrasted with [[GaoDerong|高德荣]], whose hardship memory is framed as responsibility toward poor communities. Gao Fubo's wiki role is therefore limited but important: he anchors the concept [[PovertyMemoryCorruptionExcuse|贫穷记忆贪腐借口]].

## Key Characteristics

- Presented as the former chairman of Jilin Trust in the episode's opening anti-corruption example.
- Associated in the source with a large corruption case and a childhood-poverty explanation.
- Used as an example of "穷怕了" rhetoric that the host rejects as a corruption excuse.
- Contrasted with Gao Derong to show that poverty memory does not mechanically produce greed.
- Functions as a contemporary entry point into the episode's broader governance lesson.
