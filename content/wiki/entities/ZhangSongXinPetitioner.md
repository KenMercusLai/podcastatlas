## Overview

张竦 / Zhang Song enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-629-liujiaren-shui-kangqi-fankang-wangmang-de-daqi-lmhws7-jptdh9tf45bqtlfvzdw55|Hanji 629]] as [[ZhangShaoFengguoChancellor|张绍]]'s cousin and the writer of the petition that helps [[LiuJiaShuailiHou|刘嘉]] survive and profit after [[LiuChongAnzhongMarquis|刘崇]]'s failed anti-[[WangMang|王莽]] rising.

## Current Profile

Zhang Song's current role is a political-language operator. After Liu Chong and Zhang Shao fail, Zhang Song and Liu Jia fear implication and surrender to the court. Zhang Song then writes a petition that praises Wang Mang, condemns Liu Chong, and offers to help erase Liu Chong's ritual-political base.

The petition succeeds materially. Wang Mang rewards Liu Jia and his sons, then later rewards Zhang Song himself. The Chang'an saying about seeking appointment through Zhang Bosong rather than battlefield merit makes Zhang Song a compact case of petition-based loyalism under a rising usurper.

## Key Characteristics

- Cousin of Zhang Shao who responds to failed rebellion through surrender rather than resistance.
- Petition writer who praises Wang Mang and denounces Liu Chong.
- Converts fear of collective implication into rewarded political speech.
- Associated with the Chang'an satire about clever petitions outranking combat.
- Source-scoped actor in Wang Mang's early loyalty-reward ecology.
