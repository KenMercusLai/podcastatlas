# RMS / Really Magical Stuff

RMS, short for Really Magical Stuff, is the toy company behind [[SquishyDumplings]] in [[what-makes-a-toy-go-viral]]. The episode uses the company as a modern consumer-products case where product tweaks, mystery packaging, scarcity, social video, and supply-chain timing mattered more than a fully new invention.

[[ZachBarber]], RMS's U.S. CEO in the source, describes the company's product method as [[SmallIInnovation]]: identify a trend, adjust an existing product idea, and package it in a way that fits current consumer behavior. RMS found the dumpling concept through [[Yiwu]]'s open-market toy ecosystem, then turned it from a decent 2020 product into a much larger 2023-era hit through [[MysteryToyPackaging]] and [[CollectibleDropScarcity]].

Operationally, RMS is a [[ViralToySupplyChain]] case. The company focused on inexpensive impulse channels such as [[FiveBelow]], ran short production schedules, approved production close to ship dates, delivered from factories directly to retailers, and avoided U.S. warehousing. That model helped convert [[TikTok]] demand while reducing [[HardwareInventoryRisk]] and dead-stock exposure.
