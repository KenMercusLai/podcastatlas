# Cimarrones

The Cimarrones appear in [[693-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-englands-greatest-hero-part-3-glt5069845329]] as escaped African fighters in Panama who ally with [[FrancisDrake]] against [[Spain]]. [[DiegoDrakeAlly|Diego]] introduces Drake to them after defecting from Spanish slavery.

The episode treats the alliance as strategically serious. The Cimarrones know the terrain, want revenge on Spanish power, and help Drake attack the treasure route around [[NombreDeDios]], making the Spanish authorities recognize the English-Cimarron combination as a threat.

For the wiki, the page is source-bounded: it does not try to summarize all maroon history, but records how this episode uses the Cimarrones to complicate Drake's slaving past and to show anti-Spanish alliance-building from below.
