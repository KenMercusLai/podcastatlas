# Calypso

## Overview

Calypso is the island nymph who receives and holds [[Odysseus]] on Ogygia between the first-half shipwreck and the second-half release sequence of [[TheOdyssey]].

## Current Profile

Calypso now sits at the hinge between two Rest Is History Odyssey episodes. Episode 687 ends with Odysseus alone after the destruction of his ship, drifting for nine days and reaching Ogygia, where Calypso welcomes and cares for him but will not let him leave. Episode 688 begins from the same stalled-homecoming problem: Odysseus has spent seven years on her island, a place described as outside ordinary geography and time, until Zeus sends Hermes and Calypso must release him. Her offer of immortality sharpens his choice of mortal return over timeless suspension.

## Key Characteristics

- Calypso's island is a suspension point where Odysseus remains alive after total crew loss but outside home, time, and social recognition.
- Her offer of immortality makes Odysseus' commitment to mortal homecoming explicit.
- Her release of Odysseus depends on divine command rather than voluntary surrender.
- She provides practical departure support by allowing Odysseus to build a boat and by giving provisions.

## Evidence

- Arrival after wreck: [[687-the-odyssey-hero-of-the-trojan-horse-part-1-glt2393125423]] says Odysseus reaches Ogygia after every shipmate is dead and after drifting for nine days and nights.
- Stalled return: [[687-the-odyssey-hero-of-the-trojan-horse-part-1-glt2393125423]] says Calypso welcomes and cares for Odysseus but will not let him leave, and [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] says she has held him for seven years while he longs for Penelope.
- Immortality contrast: [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] frames Calypso's offer as a rejected alternative to mortal marriage and home.
- Compelled release: [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] has Zeus send Hermes to order Calypso to release Odysseus.
- Departure assistance: [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] says she permits boat building and gives provisions after accepting the command.

## Qualifications

This page records Calypso's role in the ingested Rest Is History homecoming structure. It does not settle broader mythological traditions about Calypso beyond these retellings.
