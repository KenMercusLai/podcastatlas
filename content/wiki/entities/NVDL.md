# NVDL

NVDL appears in [[vol-121-cong-tanhua-yixian-de-fenji-jijin-dao-fengtou-zhengjing-de-ganggan-etf-yongyuan-buyao-digu-renxing-de-fengkuang-lusagcitdozvzm8wausvecvi-qmb]] as the source's [[Nvidia]]-linked [[SingleStockLeveragedETF|single-stock leveraged ETF]] example. The episode says these products target short daily exposure, not a stable multiple of the stock's cumulative return over weeks or months.

The source treats NVDL as a modern echo of Chinese structured-fund B-share premium risk. A hot underlying stock can push secondary-market demand above NAV, but volatility, daily reset, and premium compression can punish late buyers even when the underlying stock has not fully collapsed.
