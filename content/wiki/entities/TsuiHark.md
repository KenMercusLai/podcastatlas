## Overview

徐克 / Tsui Hark is the credited producer of [[AChineseGhostStory1987|《倩女幽魂》(1987)]] and a source-described contributor to its conception and visual invention.

## Current Profile

The source presents Tsui as deeply involved without collapsing producer into director. Interviews and recollections associate him with overall design, some guidance, the tree demon's voice, and Black Mountain imagery, while [[ChingSiuTung|程小东]] retains the directing credit.

## Key Characteristics

- Connects production oversight to conception and art design.
- Is associated with bold fantasy imagery and monster ideas.
- Has a non-trivial role that must be described without erasing the director.
