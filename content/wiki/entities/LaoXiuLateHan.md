## Overview

劳修 / Lao Xiu is a late-Han accusation figure whose current wiki profile is source-bounded to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-874-zhenjing-muzi-xiangcan-tianli-nanrong-lpr3eglbnwbve1clrttapw6k5zdq|Hanji 874]]. He appears as [[ZhangChengAstrologerLateHan|张成]]'s disciple and the named channel through which eunuch-backed retaliation against [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]] becomes the first Party Prohibition arrest order.

## Current Profile

Hanji 874 gives Lao Xiu a narrow but important role. After Li Ying executes Zhang Cheng father and son despite an amnesty, Zhang Cheng's eunuch ties seek retaliation. Lao Xiu then submits an accusation that Li Ying and others praise Taixue-associated游士, receive students, mutually praise one another, form parties, slander the court, and disorder customs.

The page therefore treats Lao Xiu as an accusation conduit. He is not yet a full political organizer in the wiki evidence, but his memorial matters because it translates a private grievance into a court-legible public-order case. Once [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] accepts the framing, arrests begin and figures such as [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]], [[ChenShiLateHan|陈寔]], and [[FanPangLateHan|范滂]] enter the early Party Prohibition evidence field.

## Key Characteristics

- Disciple or associate of Zhang Cheng in the Hanji 874 source field.
- Named memorialist who converts Zhang Cheng's execution into accusation against Li Ying and other士人.
- Channel for eunuch-backed retaliation rather than an independently profiled court power.
- Accuser who frames reputation, teaching, mutual praise, and Taixue ties as political disorder.
- Immediate source-side bridge from criminal enforcement to first Party Prohibition arrests.

## Qualifications

This page does not infer a full biography, office, or independent faction for Lao Xiu beyond Hanji 874. His significance here is procedural and rhetorical: he is the named route by which accusation language reaches the emperor. The transcript leaves his wider life outside the bounded evidence.
