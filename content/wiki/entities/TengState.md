# 滕国 / Teng State

滕国 / Teng State enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-45-yi-shi-wei-jian-junwang-daodi-gai-bu-gai-sharen-chengxing-lo3cok7cfwzrxesp-i8miz1fc1db]] as one of the smaller states or territories swallowed by [[SongJunYan|宋康王 / 宋君偃]] during [[SongState|宋国]]'s aggressive expansion. That first notice makes Teng part of the pattern where Song's military success creates enemies around it.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-68-jingtan-songkangwang-huangyinwudu-de-gouxue-lishi-2-lkn073gbtpmg11hcrcm8h6uvawzt]] gives Teng a sharper interpretive role. The episode says Teng was one of the Spring-and-Autumn remnant small states and the one Warring States polity willing to experiment with [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]]'s program, including a return to well-field-style agrarian order. Because [[MenciusText|《孟子》]] preserves a dedicated Teng Wen Gong branch, Song Kang Wang's destruction of Teng becomes more than conquest: it gives Confucian critics a morally charged reason to condemn him.

In this source, Teng is therefore a small-state vulnerability case and a reputation trigger. Its fall helps convert Song Kang Wang from a successful but violent expansionist into the kind of ruler whose defeat can be justified through [[MoralizedWarPretext|道德化战争借口]] and later remembered through [[VanquishedRulerReputationConstruction|亡国君污名建构]].
