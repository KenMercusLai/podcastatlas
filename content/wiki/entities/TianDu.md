# 田都 / Tian Du

田都 / Tian Du enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-143-xiangyu-shi-ruhe-feijin-xinji-nongsi-chuhuaiwang-mixin-lstfzfvbd2h9i39f7y5dxrbxxwv2|Hanji 143]] as the Qi-side figure [[XiangYu|项羽]] makes king of Qi in the nineteen-lord settlement. His elevation sits beside [[TianShi|田巿]]'s move to Jiaodong and [[TianAn|田安]]'s appointment in Jibei.

The source uses Tian Du's kingship as part of Xiang Yu's broader principle: the settlement rewards those aligned with Xiang Yu's campaign order and reshapes old-state titles according to recent participation, not inherited title alone.
