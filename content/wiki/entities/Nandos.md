# Nando's

## Overview

Nando's appears in the wiki as a South African chicken-focused restaurant chain used by the KFC-versus-McDonald's episode to show that chicken brands have strong local fast-food demand.

## Current Profile

The episode does not analyze Nando's as a full business. It uses the chain as category evidence: the second-most popular South African fast-food brand named in the episode is also chicken-led, reinforcing the claim that KFC's menu fit is not isolated.

## Key Characteristics

- Chicken-focused South African restaurant brand in the source's fast-food market comparison.
- Category signal that South African quick-service demand favors chicken formats.
- Supporting case rather than the episode's central company analysis.
