# Mak Chi-ho / 麦志豪

## Overview
[[MakChiHoAnimalAdvocate|Mak Chi-ho / 麦志豪]] is identified in the source as executive chairman of the Hong Kong Non-Profit Veterinary Services Society and as a long-time animal-welfare advocate who compares earlier Hong Kong campaigns with the 2026 旺旺 solidarity response.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Mak Chi-ho to connect the 旺旺 response with a longer Hong Kong animal-protection history. He is cited as having organized a 2015 march against the [[Guangxi]] Yulin dog-meat festival and as arguing in 2026 that animal protection is a universal goal but that advocacy must now account for different legal, social, and public-perception frameworks. His comments also ground the source's account of stalled Hong Kong animal-law reform around "Duty of Care."

## Key Characteristics
- He is presented as a veteran Hong Kong animal-welfare advocate.
- His 2015 example shows earlier Hong Kong campaigns using clearer marches and mainland-facing demands.
- His 2026 comments mark a more cautious advocacy environment with more attention to legal and political context.
- He expresses disappointment that Hong Kong animal-welfare law reform remains stalled despite earlier public consultation support.

## Evidence

- Legal-reform context: [[liulangquan-wangwang-zhisi-he-yichang-kuajing-dongbao-shengyuan]] reports his disappointment over Hong Kong's delayed Duty of Care reform and worsening abandonment, corpse disposal, and breeding conditions.
