# 支付宝碰一下 / Alipay Tap Network

## Overview
支付宝碰一下 / Alipay Tap Network is the offline Alipay interaction network discussed in [[273-guangwan-waitan-dahui-faxian-mayi-zhaodaole-xin-weizhi-lotxogfoqqwcjigxihtixvncbfc3]].

## Current Profile
The source treats 碰一下 as more than a payment interaction. Its large offline touchpoint base can become a physical entry point for merchant intelligence, local offers, membership operations, and store-level agents such as 小语.

## Key Characteristics
- Extends Alipay from app-based payment into dense offline interaction points.
- Gives merchant agents a physical-world event stream for analysis, membership, subsidies, and store visits.
- Can connect payments, local advertising, coupons, and merchant operations under measurable outcomes.
- Represents an offline counterpart to software-only [[AgentFacingInterfaces]].

## Evidence
- Scale evidence: [[273-guangwan-waitan-dahui-faxian-mayi-zhaodaole-xin-weizhi-lotxogfoqqwcjigxihtixvncbfc3]] reports tens of millions of touchpoints and high daily transaction volume as source-scoped figures.
- Merchant evidence: [[273-guangwan-waitan-dahui-faxian-mayi-zhaodaole-xin-weizhi-lotxogfoqqwcjigxihtixvncbfc3]] names the 小语 merchant operating agent as a tool for analysis, membership operations, subsidies, and distribution to stores.
- Strategy evidence: [[273-guangwan-waitan-dahui-faxian-mayi-zhaodaole-xin-weizhi-lotxogfoqqwcjigxihtixvncbfc3]] suggests the network could host offline agents rather than remain only a payment convenience.
