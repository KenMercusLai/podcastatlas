## Overview

车师国 / Cheshi Kingdom enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-429-jiaobingbibai-de-gushi-zenme-laide-lokha4dmrik4ez4vtpunb9rwhzav|Hanji 429]] as a Western Regions polity whose land, fertility, and屯田 potential make it a direct pressure point between [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]] and [[Xiongnu|匈奴]]. The episode treats Cheshi less as an independent biography than as the frontier location where food storage, garrisoning, and rescue logistics turn into high-level strategy.

## Current Profile

The source says Han attacks Cheshi in 64 BCE. Cheshi first seeks Xiongnu help, but when support does not arrive in time it submits to Han. [[ZhengJiWesternHan|郑吉]] leaves three hundred soldiers there and returns to [[QuliWesternRegions|渠犁]], but Xiongnu then attacks Cheshi because it sees Han control and屯田 accumulation there as a future threat.

Cheshi's importance is geographical and logistical. It is far from Quli, and Zheng Ji cannot hold it cheaply once Xiongnu moves in force. Xuan's final settlement after [[WeiXiangWesternHan|魏相]]'s remonstrance is limited: Chang Hui extracts Zheng Ji, Han installs Junxiu as Cheshi king, moves Cheshi's people to Quli, and leaves the old Cheshi land to Xiongnu. The episode therefore presents Cheshi as a contested forward position that Han temporarily trades away rather than a stable acquisition.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Regions polity contested by Han and Xiongnu in 64 BCE.
- Fertile frontier land whose屯田 and grain-storage potential makes it strategically sensitive.
- Local submission case where delayed Xiongnu support pushes Cheshi toward Han.
- Rescue-logistics problem because Zheng Ji's small garrison and distant Quli base cannot easily hold it.
- Settlement object in which Han preserves personnel and political arrangement while yielding the old land to Xiongnu.

## Qualifications
