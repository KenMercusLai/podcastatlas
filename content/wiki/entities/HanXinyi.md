# 韩新毅 / Han Xinyi

## Overview
韩新毅 / Han Xinyi is identified by the source as chief executive of [[AntGroup|蚂蚁集团]] and a panelist on agent commerce and payment trust.

## Current Profile
Han presents Ant as a catalyst for a two-sided agent-commerce market: merchants want AI-era growth but hesitate without users, while users lack useful merchant agents. His trust model combines authorization, user and business identity, KYA, agent capability assessment, fund safety, fraud control, auditability, and recourse.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames agent-commerce adoption as a merchant-user cold-start problem.
- Treats payment trust as layered infrastructure rather than a single protocol.
- Connects traceability and recourse to long-term consumer confidence.
- Expects commerce distribution to shift from attention time toward user intent.
