# 恒山国 / Hengshan State

恒山国 / Hengshan State enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-75-4-weishenme-shuo-guanzhong-caishi-jingji-zhan-de-bizu-lrdleqozazh4-xdwas4karzgbmza]], where it is the target in [[GuanZhong|管仲]]'s "恒山之谋." The episode says Hengshan is known for weapons production, which lets [[QiState|齐国]] use unusually high prices for arms to redirect local labor toward forging and away from agriculture.

The source then adds a second pressure point: Qi buys grain at high prices, drawing food out of Hengshan and neighboring markets before closing passes, stopping purchases, and attacking. Hengshan therefore functions in the wiki as a source-scoped case where industrial specialization, staple-food exposure, and border dependence become military vulnerability.
