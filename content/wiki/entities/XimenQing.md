# 西门庆 / Ximen Qing

[[fanwai-15-banlatte-jiang-jinpingmei-la-erqie-haishi-luse-de-991508433]] introduces 西门庆 as the local center of [[JinPingMei|《金瓶梅》]] rather than only as a stock libertine. He inherits a prosperous Qinghe drug business, grows up spoiled after his parents' deaths, and becomes a young rich man with servants, wives and concubines, money to lend, official relationships, and a circle of dependent friends.

The episode frames his power as a network. [[WuYueniang|吴月娘]] manages the inner household and sees through his unreliable sworn brothers, while the 玉皇庙结拜 episode shows how face, gifts, ritual, and dependency gather around him. His later first contact with [[PanJinlian|潘金莲]] starts from a comic accident but immediately becomes a test of how desire, money, and [[WangPo|王婆]]'s brokerage can move through [[HouseholdAsSocialWeb]].
