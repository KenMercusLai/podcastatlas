# Roberto Ridolfi

## Overview

Roberto Ridolfi appears as the Florentine banker in London whose movement of papal money gives Cecil and Walsingham a concrete connection among [[ThomasHowardDukeOfNorfolk|Norfolk]], [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary]], papal support, and possible foreign-backed conspiracy. The episode also keeps his role ambiguous, citing Stephen Alford's view that Ridolfi may have been a plant or may have shifted roles.

## Current Profile

Ridolfi's importance is evidentiary. [[FrancisWalsingham]] interrogates him in Italian after he is caught bringing papal money into England, and that money is traced toward Norfolk's servants and the Bishop of Ross, Mary's London agent. By 1571, the wider Ridolfi Plot is said to involve Mary, papal agents, ambassadors, a London rebellion, a Spanish army from the Low Countries, and Mary's proclamation as queen.

The episode's uncertainty is central to his profile. Ridolfi survives, returns safely to Italy, later becomes a papal senator in Florence, and lives into old age, which makes a simple conspirator-only profile too narrow.

## Key Characteristics

- Ridolfi is a Florentine banker whose financial role makes Catholic conspiracy materially traceable.
- His papal-money channel links domestic English actors to Mary and foreign Catholic support.
- His exact status remains unstable between conspirator, plant, double agent, and opportunistic survivor.

## Evidence

- Financial link: [[691-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-the-queens-spymaster-part-1-glt8312620561]] says Ridolfi is caught bringing papal money into England and that the money is traced toward Norfolk's servants and Mary's agent.
- Intelligence handling: [[691-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-the-queens-spymaster-part-1-glt8312620561]] shows Walsingham interrogating Ridolfi in Italian, making him an early intelligence target before the later Walsingham operations.
- Ambiguous afterlife: [[691-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-the-queens-spymaster-part-1-glt8312620561]] cites the possibility that Ridolfi was a plant and notes that he returned safely to Italy and later became a papal senator.

## Qualifications

Ridolfi should not be treated as a cleanly settled villain in the wiki. The source says there is definitely a plot, but it deliberately leaves the balance among conspiracy, exposure, and possible manufacture unresolved.
