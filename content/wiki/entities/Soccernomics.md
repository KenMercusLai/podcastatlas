# Soccernomics / 《足球经济学》

Soccernomics / 《足球经济学》 is the book discussed directly in [[22-zuqiu-jingjixue-duzhe-bubi-shi-qiumi-570874427]]. The [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] episode presents it as a book for non-fans as well as fans because football gives economics an unusually clear empirical setting: clubs, players, coaches, national teams, fans, and tournaments all produce visible choices and outcomes.

The book's wiki role is now broader than its earlier mention in [[how-to-win-a-penalty-shootout-with-game-theory]]. That Planet Money source used it as related football-economics context for penalties, [[GameTheory]], and [[MixedStrategy]]. The 蜜獾吃书 source turns it into a central branch around [[FootballAsEconomicLaboratory]], [[FootballLaborMarketDiscrimination]], [[FootballManagerMythDeflation]], [[OpenFootballTalentMarkets]], [[SoftBudgetFootballClubs]], and [[SportsMegaEventWelfareAccounting]].

The episode also preserves the Chinese publishing story. [[QinZong|秦总]] says the edition she had worked on was delayed after requested deletions around Franco, corruption, mistresses, and China-football content, while the source explicitly praises [[LiuYangyang|刘洋洋]] for translation work that required both football knowledge and economic-language control.
