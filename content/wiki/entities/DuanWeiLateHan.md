# 段煨 / Duan Wei (late Han)

段煨 / Duan Wei (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1010-sanguo-liubiao-jiujing-shibushi-piaoliang-de-caobao-lo58hzfo-qv0soauogtgfrkourrd|Hanji 1010]] as the Huayin-based general who receives [[JiaXu|贾诩]] after [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] leaves Chang'an. The transcript renders him as "段威"; this page uses the canonical historical form 段煨.

Duan Wei treats Jia Xu well, but Jia Xu judges that the courtesy is unstable because Duan Wei fears his prestige inside the army. Jia Xu therefore leaves for [[ZhangXiu|张绣]], reasoning that Zhang Xiu needs a strategist while Duan Wei will still protect Jia Xu's family after the departure. The source uses Duan Wei mainly as a contrast case for [[PoliticalSurvivalFirst|安全第一政治生存]]: good treatment is not enough if the host's incentives make suspicion likely.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1012-haose-caocao-luanshi-zhong-de-dute-qingshi-llvrucihuv2buvp1-lisz4usbprh|Hanji 1012]] mentions the same move as background before Jia Xu advises Zhang Xiu to surrender to [[CaoCao|曹操]].
