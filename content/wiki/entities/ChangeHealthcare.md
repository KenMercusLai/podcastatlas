# Change Healthcare

[[ChangeHealthcare|Change Healthcare]] appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-nikesh-arora-mythos-is-real-analytical-saas-is-dead-and-google-can-be-a-10t-company-41577435]] as [[NikeshArora|Nikesh Arora]]'s example of how a cyber incident can ripple through ordinary healthcare operations. In the source, the point is not a detailed incident reconstruction but a systems-risk example: ransomware or credential compromise can disrupt physician offices, billing, and daily economic activity far beyond the originally compromised system.

The page links healthcare cybersecurity to the wiki's broader AI-security branch. Arora's concern is that AI-enabled vulnerability discovery may not need to defeat the best-defended national-security systems to create damage; weak or widely used ordinary systems can be enough.
