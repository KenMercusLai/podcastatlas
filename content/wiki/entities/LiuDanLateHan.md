# 刘诞 / Liu Dan (late Han)

刘诞 / Liu Dan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]] as one of [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s sons in [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]]. He is paired with [[LiuFanLateHan|刘范]] in the episode's account of Liu Yan's secret support for [[MaTeng|马腾]] and [[HanSui|韩遂]] against [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]].

When Li Jue detects the plot, Liu Dan dies during the failed escape. The page is source-scoped because the episode uses him mainly to show how Liu Yan's external intervention rebounds into a family and succession crisis inside [[YizhouLateHan|益州]].
