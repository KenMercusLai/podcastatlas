## Overview

左原 / Zuo Yuan is the Hanji 861 correction-after-offense case. After being expelled from study for an offense, he meets [[GuoTaiLateHan|郭泰]], receives hospitality and admonition, and later abandons a revenge plan when Guo Tai's presence activates shame rather than defiance.

## Current Profile

Zuo Yuan's profile is built around corrigibility. The source does not make his original offense the final judgment on him. Guo Tai treats him with food, drink, examples, and counsel, telling him not to deepen resentment but to turn toward self-examination.

The later revenge episode is the practical test of that intervention. Zuo Yuan gathers people to retaliate against other students, but Guo Tai's presence makes him feel he has failed the earlier trust. He gives up the plan, which turns Guo Tai's mercy from indulgence into preventive moral pressure.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Han student figure expelled or rejected after an offense.
- Corrigible offender whom Guo Tai refuses to define only by prior fault.
- Case where hospitality and counsel are used as correction tools.
- Revenge-risk figure redirected by shame and remembered trust.
- Source-scoped evidence for mentorship that prevents escalation.

## Qualifications

The source does not erase Zuo Yuan's fault or present correction as guaranteed. It shows a bounded case where trusted admonition interrupts later escalation; the wiki does not generalize it into a universal justice model.
