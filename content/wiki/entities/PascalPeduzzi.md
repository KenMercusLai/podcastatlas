# Pascal Peduzzi

[[PascalPeduzzi]] is the [[UnitedNations]] environmental official cited in [[sand-heists-and-property-rights-in-the-caribbean-summer-school]]. In the source, he is in [[Jamaica]] researching sand when locals tell him about armed overnight sand theft.

Peduzzi expands the story from one stolen beach into [[SandScarcity]] and [[IllegalSandMining]] as global problems. The episode attributes to him the report title "Sand Rarer Than One Thinks" and the claim that sand extraction has become valuable, violent, and environmentally destructive in multiple countries.
