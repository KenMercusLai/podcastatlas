## Overview

严延年 / Yan Yannian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-448-shenme-jiao-zuozinie-bukehuo-kuli-yanyannian-zhisi-gaosu-ni-lknsltb-jkkxuw1v8gfa-f3vwoin|Hanji 448]] as a Western Han cruel official whose governing skill is inseparable from fear. The episode presents him as a severe, legally sharp, death-sentence-prone administrator whose end shows how terror can rebound against the person who uses it.

## Current Profile

Yan Yannian's profile is currently narrow but coherent. He is remembered as a "屠伯" type: powerful, intimidating, and good at writing judgments that make targets difficult to defend. That competence does not become stable authority. After [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] cancels an expected Zuo Fengyi appointment because of Yan's cruel reputation, Yan resents the court and handles a Henan locust crisis with contemptuous indifference.

The fall begins inside his own office. A府丞 who had once been favored by Yan fears that Yan's harshness will eventually turn on him. He therefore travels to Chang'an, files a ten-count accusation, and kills himself with poison to prevent the case from being dismissed as ordinary insubordination. The accusations are not all proven, but enough resentment and political-slander evidence remains for Xuan to order punishment; Yan is executed by public beheading.

The episode makes Yan's mother a moral witness. She sees his killing-based reputation before his death, refuses to celebrate his office, tells him that a governor should use humane teaching rather than power-backed killing, and prepares a grave for him before the punishment arrives. The page therefore treats Yan less as an isolated bad official than as a case of [[TerrorRuleBackfire|恐怖治理反噬]].

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official identified with the "cruel official" tradition and remembered for severe punishments.
- Legally skilled judge whose written rulings make accused people difficult to defend.
- Administrator whose harshness makes even favored subordinates feel unsafe.
- Neglectful crisis actor in the Henan locust episode after a blocked promotion leaves him resentful.
- Target of a self-sacrificial subordinate accusation that turns internal fear into court exposure.
- Moral warning case whose mother predicts that excessive killing will bring punishment.
- Execution case where not every accusation is proven, but enough political resentment evidence supports capital punishment.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to one podcast episode. It does not reconstruct Yan Yannian's whole career, the original legal text of the ten accusations, the exact evidentiary threshold for the resentment charge, or the full list of his punishments. The episode's retribution framing is a moral interpretation; the institutional mechanism preserved here is narrower: cruel governance made subordinates afraid enough to expose him and made Xuan unwilling to promote or protect him.

## What Changed

- Created Yan Yannian as a Western Han cruel-official case grounded in Hanji 448.
- Framed his importance around terror governance, internal fear, disaster neglect, maternal warning, and public execution.
