## Overview

严延年 / Yan Yannian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-448-shenme-jiao-zuozinie-bukehuo-kuli-yanyannian-zhisi-gaosu-ni-lknsltb-jkkxuw1v8gfa-f3vwoin|Hanji 448]] as a Western Han cruel official whose governing skill is inseparable from fear. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-447-siyi-shaqiu-xihan-kuli-yanyannian-ruhe-ziqu-miewang-lhhh0plqayyodt7sluycfh7y1m0l|Hanji 447]] now supplies the setup: before the fatal accusation, Yan is already known for legal skill, secret judgment writing, concentrated winter executions, and the "屠伯" reputation.

## Current Profile

Yan Yannian's profile is now coherent across setup and downfall. He is remembered as a "屠伯" type: powerful, intimidating, and good at writing judgments that make targets difficult to defend. Hanji 447 stresses that this capacity has a public-order side: he targets豪强 who injure weaker people and knows the law well enough to make rulings appear technically sound. The same source says the capacity is already corrupted by secrecy, mass killing, and private intention.

That competence does not become stable authority. Hanji 447 contrasts Yan with [[HuangBaWesternHan|黄霸]], whose lenient [[YingchuanCommandery|颍川郡]] governance attracts people away from harsh Henan rule and wins [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]'s reward. After Xuan later cancels an expected Zuo Fengyi appointment because of Yan's cruel reputation, Yan resents the court and handles a Henan locust crisis with contemptuous indifference.

The fall begins inside his own office. A府丞 who had once been favored by Yan fears that Yan's harshness will eventually turn on him. He therefore travels to Chang'an, files a ten-count accusation, and kills himself with poison to prevent the case from being dismissed as ordinary insubordination. The accusations are not all proven, but enough resentment and political-slander evidence remains for Xuan to order punishment; Yan is executed by public beheading.

The episode makes Yan's mother a moral witness. She sees his killing-based reputation before his death, refuses to celebrate his office, tells him that a governor should use humane teaching rather than power-backed killing, and prepares a grave for him before the punishment arrives. The page therefore treats Yan less as an isolated bad official than as a case of [[TerrorRuleBackfire|恐怖治理反噬]].

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official identified with the "cruel official" tradition and remembered for severe punishments and mass executions.
- Legally skilled local administrator who can punish powerful offenders but turns difficult-to-challenge rulings toward intimidation.
- Administrator whose harshness makes even favored subordinates feel unsafe.
- Rival contrast to Huang Ba's humane Yingchuan governance and omen-backed local success.
- Neglectful crisis actor in the Henan locust episode after a blocked promotion leaves him resentful.
- Target of a self-sacrificial subordinate accusation that turns internal fear into court exposure.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 447 and Hanji 448. It does not reconstruct Yan Yannian's whole career, the original legal text of the ten accusations, the exact evidentiary threshold for the resentment charge, or the full list of his punishments. Hanji 447's claim that Yan punishes豪强 who injure the weak prevents a flat caricature: his legal ability and public-order role are real in the source. The institutional mechanism preserved here is narrower than retribution language: cruel governance made subordinates afraid enough to expose him and made Xuan unwilling to promote or protect him.
