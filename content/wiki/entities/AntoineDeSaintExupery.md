# Antoine de Saint-Exupery / 圣埃克苏佩里

[[AntoineDeSaintExupery|Antoine de Saint-Exupery / 圣埃克苏佩里]] is the writer and pilot whose [[TheLittlePrince|《小王子》]] anchors [[07-yong-rongge-lilun-fenxi-xiaowangzi-yongheng-shaonian-ji-qita-539091165]]. The episode reads him through biography as well as text: early father loss, intense attachment to his mother, childhood experience of a younger brother's death, drawing, aviation, marriage, desert crash, wartime return to flying, and eventual death in the air all become context for the story's mood.

The source does not reduce Saint-Exupery to pathology. It argues that his work and life show [[PuerAeternus|eternal-youth]] traits, including fantasy, flight, romanticized death, and difficulty with ordinary commitment, but it also emphasizes courage, rescue work, wartime responsibility, and a life serious enough to complicate any simple moralizing judgment.

## Key Claims
- The episode treats [[TheLittlePrince|《小王子》]] as strongly autobiographical without claiming that every symbol has one settled biographical key.
- Saint-Exupery's flying life matters both literally, through the desert and aircraft material behind the book, and symbolically, through the eternal-youth desire to remain above ordinary life.
- His relations with mother, wife, and lover are used by the source to connect [[MotherComplex|mother complex]] with the rose and fox images.
- His courage and final wartime death keep the source from turning psychological critique into dismissal.
