## Overview

凤天南 / Feng Tiannan is a local strongman and martial leader in [[FeihuWaizhuan|《飞狐外传》]]. The bounded episode uses his persecution of 钟阿四's family to show how wealth, violence, institutional collusion, and tactical deception can make intervention on behalf of strangers a test of侠义.

## Current Profile

Feng Tiannan seeks to acquire an ordinary family's vegetable plot cheaply, uses an accusation against their young child, and benefits from official coercion. The family's catastrophic attempt to prove innocence shows that the harm is not only personal cruelty: private power and failed justice reinforce each other.

When [[HuFei|胡斐]] initially forces a concession, Feng responds through diversion and escape rather than accountability. By the time Hu Fei returns, the remaining family members have been killed. This makes pursuit a response to completed and continuing public harm, not merely a contest between martial rivals.

[[YuanZiyi|袁紫衣]]'s request that Hu Fei spare Feng then places the victim-centered claim against romantic and family pressure. The episode treats Hu Fei's refusal as a defining ethical choice, while the exact obligations surrounding Yuan remain qualified.

## Key Characteristics

- Converts economic power and local authority into coercion against an ordinary family.
- Uses accusation and institutional collusion rather than open combat alone.
- Evades accountability through deception and flight.
- Functions as the clearest object of Hu Fei's stranger-directed redress.
- Creates a conflict between victim-centered justice and Yuan Ziyi's relational appeal.
