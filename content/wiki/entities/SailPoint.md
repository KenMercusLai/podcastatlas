# SailPoint

## Overview
SailPoint is presented in the current source as an enterprise identity-governance company extending access discovery, provisioning, monitoring, and certification from human accounts to bots and AI agents.

## Current Profile
The represented product thesis treats data as the protected asset and identities, devices, bots, and agents as paths to it. SailPoint proposes discovering non-human identities, assigning accountable owners, applying fine-grained contextual entitlements, and making AI-supported policy decisions inspectable. This is a vendor account of a governance architecture, not independent evidence that the controls prevent breaches or harmful agent behavior.

## Key Characteristics
- Governs access lifecycle across applications, entitlements, and data.
- Extends identity ownership and certification to non-human identities.
- Uses [[Entro]] as a discovery and categorization layer for machine identities and endpoints.
- Advocates contextual, dynamic authorization and reduced standing privilege.
- Positions transparency and consistency as requirements for auditing AI-supported decisions.

Revenue, customer count, breach prevalence, product integration, suite capability, and risk-reduction claims are not independently verified, and identity governance constrains access without proving agent output quality, ethical behavior, or complete legal accountability.
