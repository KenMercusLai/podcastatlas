# 吴宾 / Wu Bin

## Overview
吴宾 is an oral-surgery doctor in the [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] Physicians' Day roundtable, represented through an early major facial-trauma case and two suspected oral sarcoma cases in adolescents.

## Current Profile
The source contrasts trust received by a young doctor with the limits of later cancer care. Wu Bin recalls treating a cooperative middle-aged man with a large facial wound during an early independent emergency shift, aided by a senior colleague; the patient later became a friend and introduced him to amateur radio. He also recalls recognizing unusual rapid growth after a young patient's mandibular procedure and referring the patient for care that reportedly led to a sarcoma diagnosis, plus another adolescent with a tongue lesion whose proposed operation may have required tongue removal.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats patient cooperation and senior-colleague support as important conditions in early clinical growth.
- Connects longitudinal contact with trust that can extend beyond the immediate encounter.
- Treats rapidly abnormal postoperative or oral-tissue growth as a reason for escalation and referral.
- Frames recognition and transfer as responsible action when definitive treatment exceeds the clinician's setting or capability.
- Explicitly rejects the idea that doctors can guarantee cure or know every later outcome.
