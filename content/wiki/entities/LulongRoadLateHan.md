## Overview

卢龙道 / Lulong Road is the difficult northern route that Hanji 1110 identifies as the approach [[TianChou|田畴]] proposed for [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s surprise movement against [[Wuhuan|乌桓]].

## Current Profile

The current source does not reconstruct the road's full geography or the entire campaign. Its role is causal and commemorative: Tian Chou's route knowledge helps turn a dangerous northern expedition into victory, and the value of that guidance later grounds Cao Cao's repeated attempt to enfeoff him.

## Key Characteristics

- Difficult northern approach associated with the Wuhuan campaign.
- Local-route knowledge supplied by Tian Chou.
- Surprise axis presented as an important cause of Cao Cao's victory.
- Campaign contribution later converted into a disputed merit-reward claim.

## Evidence

- Route and guide: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1110-caocao-gei-ta-si-ci-fengshang-ta-jing-si-bu-daying-ln8zkblsgq3zi7krrhgjpnb4mokb|Hanji 1110]] says Tian Chou advises Cao Cao to lead the army through Lulong Road for a surprise attack.
- Reward consequence: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1110-caocao-gei-ta-si-ci-fengshang-ta-jing-si-bu-daying-ln8zkblsgq3zi7krrhgjpnb4mokb|Hanji 1110]] makes this route advice the principal merit behind Cao Cao's repeated enfeoffment orders.

## Qualifications

Hanji 1110 recalls the campaign only to explain the later reward dispute. Exact geography, march conditions, chronology, and the contribution of other commanders remain outside the source's short treatment.

## What Changed

- Established Lulong Road as the route connecting Tian Chou's frontier knowledge to Cao Cao's Wuhuan victory and later reward dispute.

## Relationships

- [[TianChou|田畴]] - guide whose route proposal gives the road its episode significance.
- [[CaoCao|曹操]] - commander who uses the route and later tries to reward Tian Chou.
- [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] - campaign opponent reached through the surprise approach.
- [[EarnedRewardRefusal|有功辞赏]] - later reward conflict grounded in the route contribution.
