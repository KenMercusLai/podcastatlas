# 《武士威廉》

《武士威廉》 first entered the wiki through [[kaitianchuang-moyu-ganxie-chui-dali-553650149]] as a future-topic preview connected to [[Nioh|《仁王》]]. [[14-wushi-weilian-dahanghai-shidai-de-riben-he-xifang-555771285]] turns that preview into a full historical branch by using the book to discuss [[WilliamAdams|William Adams / 三浦按针]], [[TokugawaIeyasu|Tokugawa Ieyasu / 德川家康]], late-Sengoku [[Japan]], European maritime rivalry, and the narrowing of Tokugawa foreign contact.

The source's value in the wiki is not only the unusual image of a white samurai. It uses Adams to connect [[OceanicVoyageAttrition]], [[ConfessionalTradeConflict]], [[EarlyModernJapanEuropeContact]], [[TokugawaForeignTradeAmbivalence]], and [[SakokuDutchWindow]] while keeping heroic counterfactual claims constrained.

## Key Claims
- The book is now a developed [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] history source rather than only a preview.
- The episode treats [[WilliamAdams|Adams]] as an important mediator, shipbuilding aide, and diplomatic witness, not as a lone person who changed Japan's national course.
- Its core historical setting is the clash among English/Dutch Protestant traders, Iberian Catholic trade and missionary networks, and Tokugawa state-building.
- The source preserves uncertainty around more dramatic claims, especially the possible role of European cannon in the [[SekigaharaBattle|Battle of Sekigahara]].
