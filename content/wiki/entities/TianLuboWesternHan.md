## Overview

田禄伯 / Tian Lubo is a Wu-side commander in the [[RebellionOfTheSevenStatesWesternHan|Rebellion of the Seven States]] whose current wiki role is defined by a rejected proposal to open a separate theater westward through Changsha, Huainan, Wuguan, and Guanzhong.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-235-liubi-zhisi-jielu-zhichang-shengcun-qian-guize-lp1wxe_buqe_zrilsfwsfzqoa2ef|Hanji 235]] says Tian asks [[LiuBiWuKing|刘濞]] for fifty thousand troops. He proposes moving along the Yangtze and Huai river system, taking [[ChangshaKingdom|长沙国]] and Huainan, entering [[Wuguan|武关]], and meeting Liu Bi in Guanzhong. The plan attempts to widen the war, gain additional support, and divide court pressure.

The proposal is rejected after a Wu heir argues that a rebellion leader cannot trust delegated command because a subordinate may also turn against him. Tian's current profile is therefore not a completed campaign record but a bounded case of a plausible second-front option defeated by internal mistrust before execution.

## Key Characteristics

- Wu-side commander during the Seven States rebellion.
- Advocate of a separate fifty-thousand-person western movement.
- Planner who links river movement, additional kingdoms, Wuguan, and Guanzhong into one route.
- Subordinate whose proposal is rejected because delegated command is treated as a defection risk.

## Qualifications

The current evidence is one compact popular-history episode. The size of the requested force, hydrological route, military feasibility, exact objective, dialogue, office, and reasons for rejection require primary-source comparison. Because the plan is never attempted, the page does not claim that it would have succeeded or reversed the campaign.
