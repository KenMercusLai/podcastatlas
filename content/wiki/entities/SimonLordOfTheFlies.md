# Simon / 西蒙（《蝇王》）

## Overview
Simon / 西蒙 is the truth-bearing and quasi-prophetic figure in [[LordOfTheFlies|《蝇王》]] as discussed in [[vol-351-yingwang-21shiji-de-huangdao-gushi-haineng-zenme-kai-naodong-1983-nuojiang-dezhu-gkwriueodap1a3taaattkgol]].

## Current Profile
The episode treats Simon as the child who comes closest to understanding that the beast is not simply an external monster. He discovers the dead parachutist, encounters the pig-head "Lord of the Flies" image, and tries to bring truth back to the group. His death in storm, dance, chant, and collective excitement is the episode's clearest example of truth being destroyed by fear-driven group violence.

## Key Characteristics
- He is read as mysterious, fragile, drifting, and morally alert.
- He seeks the truth of the beast rather than using fear as power.
- His encounter with the pig head makes the title image a direct human-nature problem.
- His murder shows how a crowd can turn the truth-bearer into the enemy.
- His death is the literary hinge where the novel's atmosphere and symbolism become unavoidable.
