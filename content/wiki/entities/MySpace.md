# MySpace

MySpace appears in [[tech-20260807-0807-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260807-0807-mp-tech-pod-128]] as a legacy social network whose possible relaunch has become a nostalgia signal. [[JoannaStern]] frames the interest less as a proven business opportunity than as dissatisfaction with modern social feeds shaped by bots, creators, influencers, platform distrust, and algorithmically optimized content.

The source contrasts MySpace with [[Facebook]]. Facebook is said to have lasted partly because it began from college communities and built a more concrete real-world social graph, while MySpace was more disparate and did not adapt as aggressively as [[MarkZuckerberg]] continued to pivot Facebook.

## Key Claims
- MySpace nostalgia points toward desire for chronological, friend-centered social media rather than purely algorithmic feeds.
- A relaunch would need more than nostalgia because users now expect better control, moderation, portability, and trust.
- The source uses [[Bluesky]] as the more current comparison for user control and decentralized social-network ideas.
