# Oriana Fallaci / 奥利亚娜·法拉奇

Oriana Fallaci / 奥利亚娜·法拉奇 is the central figure in [[77-ta-shi-ziyou-de-yegou-ta-shi-falaqi-685710187]], where [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] reads her through [[WoBuXiangxinShenhua|《我不相信神话》]]. The episode presents her as a journalist and writer whose public life was shaped by [[ItalianFascism|Italian fascism]], childhood resistance work, war reporting, political interviews, and a refusal to let powerful people become sacred figures.

The source's Fallaci is not a neutral-professional ideal or a clean ideological emblem. She praises courage and attacks cruelty across camps, criticizes [[MohammadRezaPahlavi|Mohammad Reza Pahlavi]] and [[RuhollahKhomeini|Ruhollah Khomeini]], challenges the chador and gender segregation in Iran, survives the [[TlatelolcoMassacre|Tlatelolco massacre]], and treats [[AlexandrosPanagoulis|Alexandros Panagoulis]] as a loved person whose resistance matches her own freedom standard.

Fallaci's importance in the wiki is the connection between journalism and dignity. [[PowerMythDeflation]] names her refusal to sanctify rulers; [[JournalisticIndependenceAgainstFaction]] names her refusal to become a camp's mouthpiece; [[FreedomAsDignity]] names the episode's claim that freedom remains worth pursuing even when absolute freedom is impossible.
