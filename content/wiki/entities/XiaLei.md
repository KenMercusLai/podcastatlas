# 侠累 / Xia Lei

侠累 / Xia Lei appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]] as the chancellor of [[HanState|韩国]] whom [[NieZheng|聂政]] kills at [[YanZhongzi|严仲子]]'s request. The source says Xia Lei is presiding over a meeting with heavy security when Nie Zheng breaks in and strikes him in the throat.

The episode also records a [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] variant in which Xia Lei grabs [[HanAihou|韩哀侯]] while trying to escape, and Nie Zheng kills them both. This page keeps that as a source variant rather than reconciling it with the base annalistic notice.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-2-lmnqfjmydygqnfec6xhzpixralvi]] revisits the same source problem from the other side. The host says [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] may have treated Xia Lei's death and [[YanZhongzi|严遂]]'s later killing of [[HanAihou|韩哀侯]] as one event, while [[Shiji|《史记》]] and [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] separated them.
