# 邓老板

邓老板 appears in [[22-zuqiu-jingjixue-duzhe-bubi-shi-qiumi-570874427]] as the former serious football fan and finance-industry voice in the [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] discussion of [[Soccernomics|《足球经济学》 / Soccernomics]]. The source uses his football memory and financial framing to keep the episode from becoming either pure fan nostalgia or abstract economics.

His role is especially clear in the episode's treatment of [[ArseneWenger|Arsene Wenger / 温格]], club finance, and [[GameTheory]]. He helps explain why football can be a "clean" economic example: the sport has enough history and data to test claims, yet the situations remain concrete enough for non-specialist readers.
