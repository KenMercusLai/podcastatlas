# Peter Ward

Peter Ward appears in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as the 18-year-old Catholic man killed after leaving the [[MalvernArms]] on 25 June 1966. The episode says [[GustySpence]] and his gang wrongly identified Ward and three companions as IRA men before gunmen opened fire.

Ward's death is the episode's closing murder case and a marker of how loyalist fantasy about hidden IRA enemies could become lethal. It connects the pre-1968 roots of [[TheTroubles]] to the later civil-rights escalation covered in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]].
