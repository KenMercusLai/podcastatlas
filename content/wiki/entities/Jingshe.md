# 景舍 / Jing She

景舍 / Jing She appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] through the episode's use of [[ZhushuJinian|《竹书纪年》]]. The source says [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] sought peace with [[WeiState|魏国]] through Jing She after [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]] and [[HanState|韩国]] defeated a coalition around [[XianglingWarringStates|襄陵]].

The page is source-scoped. Jing She matters as a diplomatic channel rather than as an independently developed biography: the episode uses him to explain how a military reversal around Wei can lead quickly into peace-seeking through a third-party Chu figure.
