# Los Angeles

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-adam-carolla-on-californias-collapse-fires-failed-leadership-and-gyno-fascism-39710360]] adds [[LosAngeles|Los Angeles]] as the main city-government target in [[AdamCarolla|Adam Carolla]]'s critique of [[California]] decline. The source presents Los Angeles through post-fire rebuilding delay, homelessness, crime, school-closure memory, production flight, permitting, and leadership criticism aimed especially at [[KarenBass|Karen Bass]] and [[EricGarcetti|Eric Garcetti]].

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-spencer-pratt-on-fixing-la-wildfires-homelessness-corruption-the-fight-to-take-it-back-41236810]] adds [[SpencerPratt|Spencer Pratt]]'s mayoral-campaign version of the same city-governance critique. Pratt ties the [[PacificPalisades|Pacific Palisades]] fire, [[EmergencyWarningInfrastructure]], [[MandatoryTreatmentHomelessnessPolicy]], [[MunicipalNonprofitAccountability]], [[PublicSafetyFirstUrbanGovernance]], [[MunicipalTransparencyDashboard|spending dashboards]], [[PermitAutomationForZonedProjects]], schools, transit, and independent production into a basic-services platform.

[[tech-20260112-0112-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260112-0112-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds a construction-adaptation version through homes lost in the [[PacificPalisades|Pacific Palisades]] and [[Altadena]]. That source asks how rebuilding can use [[FireResilientConstruction]] while balancing speed, affordability, carbon, and neighborhood memory.

[[burning-questions-a-more-fire-prone-world-6a687ea64207b2a60119df8b]] adds Los Angeles as an [[ExtremeWildfire]] damage example, reinforcing that wildfire risk is not only rural or abstract climate risk when dense, wealthy, insured urban regions burn.
