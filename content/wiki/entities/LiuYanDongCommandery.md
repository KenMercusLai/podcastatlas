# 刘延 / Liu Yan (Dong Commandery)

## Overview
刘延 / Liu Yan (Dong Commandery) is the East Commandery administrator defended by [[CaoCao|曹操]] during the [[BaimaReliefLateHan|White Horse relief]] before [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]]. This page is deliberately disambiguated from [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉 / Liu Yan (late Han)]], the Yi Province powerholder.

## Current Profile
The bounded evidence gives Liu Yan a narrow but important campaign role. [[YuanShao|袁绍]] sends [[YanLiangLateHan|颜良]] to attack him at White Horse, making Liu Yan's position a forward pressure point on Cao Cao's side. Cao Cao's April northward move to rescue him then creates the setting for the feigned Yellow River crossing, Yuan Shao's westward division of force, and Guan Yu's killing of Yan Liang.

## Key Characteristics
- Dong Commandery administrator rather than the Yi Province Liu Yan.
- Source-bounded defensive target at White Horse in Jian'an 5 / 200 CE.
- His exposure forces Cao Cao to solve a relief problem under numerical disadvantage.
- Functions mainly as a campaign-position node, not as a fully developed biography.
