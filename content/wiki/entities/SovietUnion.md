# Soviet Union

[[80-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-shang-691866649]] adds the Soviet Union as the state-security environment behind [[OlegGordievsky|Oleg Gordievsky]]'s [[IdeologicalDefection|ideological defection]]. The [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] episode connects family fear, [[KGB]] career discipline, Berlin Wall violence, [[PragueSpring|Prague Spring]], and restricted cultural access into a case of [[TotalitarianDoubleLife|totalitarian double life]].

[[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] extends that branch into the late Cold War decision environment. [[OperationRYAN]] shows Soviet leaders and the [[KGB]] treating a feared U.S. first strike as an intelligence premise, while [[OlegGordievsky|Gordievsky]] helps the West understand Soviet fear, bureaucratic inefficiency, and later arms-race pressure around [[MikhailGorbachev|Gorbachev]].

[[04-qieernuobeili-de-wuye-woyao-jin-jianyu-le-530594843]] adds the Soviet Union as the institutional setting for [[ChernobylNuclearPowerPlant|Chernobyl]]. The [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] episode reads [[MidnightInChernobyl|《切尔诺贝利的午夜》]] as a case where [[Pripyat]]'s planned-city modernity, production targets, material shortages, [[NuclearSafetyPropaganda]], and information control turned nuclear engineering into [[TechnologicalDisasterAsInstitutionalFailure]].

[[05-ta-laizi-maliwuboer-ruguo-ni-kanjian-guo-wo-ceng-jiandao-de-533375925]] adds a family-history layer through [[SheCameFromMariupol|《她来自马里乌波尔》]]. The episode follows [[NataschaWodin|纳塔莎·沃丁]]'s family from [[Mariupol]] through revolution, civil war, famine, class suspicion, Stalinist purges, exile, and postwar fear that former [[OstarbeiterForcedLabor|Eastern forced laborers]] could be punished as traitors if returned to Soviet control.

The Soviet Union appears in [[dark-times-for-cubas-economic-experiment]] as [[Cuba]]'s first major external patron after the 1959 revolution. The [[PlanetMoney]] episode says the Soviet Union bought Cuban goods above market value and sold Cuba oil below market value, letting Cuba's state-led system survive under the U.S. embargo despite poverty and economic inefficiency.

The Soviet collapse in 1991 becomes the first major test of [[ExternalPatronDependence]]. The source says Cuba's GDP fell by 35%, food became scarcer, and the Special Period began after cheap oil and favorable trade terms disappeared.

[[135-xianliao-weida-zuojiamen-de-bagua-di-san-dan-823746670]] adds a literary-intellectual politics mention. The source criticizes [[JeanPaulSartre|Jean-Paul Sartre / 萨特]] for loving or defending the Soviet side while staying silent about abuses suffered by left-wing comrades, making the Soviet Union part of the episode's [[IdeologicalTotalizationRisk]] branch.
