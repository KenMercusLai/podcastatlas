## Overview

马睦 / Ma Mu is a source-bounded late-Han envoy in the 162 CE southern crisis. His wiki role is limited but structurally important: after defeat by rebels in Jingzhou, he flees, helping mark the episode's theme that command fear can spread faster than organized response.

## Current Profile

Ma Mu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-856-lishi-gaosu-ni-ren-ruo-xinluan-zhenjiao-zi-luan-lo7m-9mz-pvstxtfgavrne4bszks|Hanji 856]] as an imperial envoy sent against southern unrest. His defeat by the Ai County rebel branch is followed by flight together with [[LiuDuLateHan|刘度]], then by a harsher downstream collapse when [[LiSuNanCommanderyLateHan|李肃]] tries to flee Nan Commandery and kills [[HuShuangLateHan|胡爽]] for opposing him.

Ma Mu's importance is comparative. He is not presented as the deepest moral failure in the source, because Emperor Huan reduces his death penalty by one degree. But his flight still belongs to the southern command chain's loss of self-command: officials facing rebellion protect themselves first, leaving local defense and duty to fragment.

## Key Characteristics

- Imperial envoy involved in suppressing southern Jingzhou unrest in 162 CE.
- Defeated by the Ai County rebel branch according to the source's normalized sequence.
- Flees after defeat rather than becoming a stabilizing command center.
- Receives reduced punishment below death, distinguishing him from Li Su's harsher case.
- Serves as an early link in the episode's panic-driven command-collapse chain.
