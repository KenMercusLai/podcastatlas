## Overview

吕嘉 / Lü Jia is the Nanyue resistance leader who destroys [[HanQianqiuWesternHan|韩千秋]]'s force near Panyu, returns the killed Han envoys' tallies with a professed apology, and fortifies the frontier. Hanji 352-2 treats these actions as the point at which [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] recognizes open rebellion and prepares conquest.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Lü Jia as combining battlefield force, diplomatic theater, and defensive preparation. His troops reverse Han Qianqiu's early advance and kill the entire invading group. He then boxes the Han tallies, attaches an apologetic message, and places them at the border while reinforcing the passes.

That sequence does not restore peace. The apology is described as false, and the defensive measures confirm that Lü Jia expects retaliation. In the source's escalation narrative, his actions close the space for further soft pressure and trigger the larger Han mobilization against [[Nanyue|南越]].

## Key Characteristics

- Nanyue chancellor and resistance leader in the episode's rebellion narrative.
- Commander or political leader behind the destruction of Han Qianqiu's advancing force.
- User of an apologetic border message after killing Han personnel.
- Organizer of frontier defense in anticipation of Han retaliation.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to the episode's Han-centered account. It does not reconstruct Lü Jia's earlier career, domestic political support, command structure, reasons for resisting Han, or later fate. Calling the apology false follows the host's interpretation; the source does not provide the full text or an independent Nanyue perspective.
