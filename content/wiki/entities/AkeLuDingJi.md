## Overview

阿珂 / A Ke is a woman in [[LuDingJi|《鹿鼎记》]] whose beauty becomes [[WeiXiaobao|韦小宝]]'s strongest romantic projection and the episode's sharpest test of whether intense desire amounts to love.

## Current Profile

Wei experiences his first sight of A Ke as overwhelming and treats her as the world's most beautiful woman. Because other characters do not respond with comparable intensity, the episode reads part of that beauty as subjective projection. His willingness to give things up for her suggests real emotional force, while his repeated use of money, price, conquest, and brothel comparison keeps possession inside the attachment.

A Ke initially despises Wei, prefers Zheng Keshuang, and repeatedly exposes Wei to danger. Her eventual marriage follows pregnancy and Zheng's abandonment, but the episode finds the psychological transition from rejection to consent underwritten. It therefore treats the ending as marked by narrative and historical constraints rather than as clean proof of reciprocal love.

## Key Characteristics

- Becomes Wei's most intense beauty-centered fixation.
- Receives a subjective idealization not shared equally by other characters.
- Initially rejects Wei and prefers Zheng Keshuang.
- Is criticized by the hosts for vanity, selfishness, and limited responsibility while remaining more than a moral label.
- Marries after pregnancy and abandonment alter her options.
- Exposes the gap between desire, acquisition, and freely developed reciprocity.

## Qualifications

This profile represents one modern conversational reading of a fictional character. Judgments about beauty, vanity, selfishness, pregnancy, consent, motive, and authorial limitation require direct textual and edition-specific scrutiny; restricted options should not be mistaken for uncomplicated romantic choice.
