## Overview

张苍 / Zhang Cang appears both as a character in [[MaBoyong|马伯庸]]'s [[QinErshiMustDie|《秦二世必须死》]], where he bridges old aristocratic society and Qin central administration, and as a Western Han senior minister whose profile joins court cosmology, the chancellorship, recommendation accountability, technical scholarship, reciprocal loyalty, and sensational longevity anecdotes.

## Current Profile

The literary sources begin with Zhang Cang investigating accounts and the “二世死而地分” meteor inscription before Zhao Cheng's purge drives him into Xu Fu's conspiracy. They map him primarily to “术,” the operational layer of inquiry, escape, survival, and task execution, while his aristocratic origin and central-government experience make him the novel's strongest carrier of the commandery-county/enfeoffment comparison. He supports institutional continuity without becoming an uncomplicated defender of Qin cruelty. His reflections on the unattainable are read as restraint toward desire and timing, while the hosts disagree over whether his relationship with 易水 is earned.

A bounded historical-court layer places Zhang Cang in a water-versus-earth legitimacy dispute. He argues that Han belongs to water virtue and rejects [[GongsunChenWesternHan|公孙臣]]'s rival earth-virtue thesis. When a yellow dragon is reported at Chengji, [[LiuHeng|汉文帝刘恒]] treats it as confirmation, recalls Gongsun Chen, and lets earth-virtue scholars plan changes to institutions and official colors. Zhang Cang then loses influence. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-225-2-weishenme-shuo-xinyuanjieyi-de-zuxian-shi-zhongguoren-lmk2n0ved6gt9tgoxs1qb2s0kuc3|Hanji 225-2]] supports a reversal in court authority, but not the host's speculation that the omen was staged.

Hanji 215 part 1 supplies the beginning of the chancellorship otherwise visible mainly at its end. After [[GuanYing|灌婴]] dies in 176 BCE, Liu Heng appoints Zhang Cang from the御史大夫 position. The episode attributes the choice to accumulated Qin-Han administrative, fiscal, royal-kingdom, legal, and calendrical experience. Its claims that he studied under Xunzi alongside Li Si and Han Fei and served fourteen years as Huainan chancellor remain source-scoped biographical details. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-215-qiangu-zhiyuan-wendi-bianzhe-jiayi-jing-shi-weile-ta-hao-1-lpq3wokrephgsjylhpgnj4h_va86|Hanji 215 part 1]]

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-227-he-rennai-xuming-baisui-yang-qiqie-bairen-ta-shi-shui-liu5ugz2bds9e0rgl9xytk0a4pvj|Hanji 227]] separates that background from the stated end of Zhang Cang's chancellorship: he is accused of recommending a中候官 for private gain, is rebuked, and resigns after roughly fifteen years in office. The episode reaches backward to his Qin archival service, his joining [[LiuBang|刘邦]], and rescue from a death sentence through [[WangLingHanMinister|王陵]]; it then uses Zhang Cang's later visits and gifts to Wang Ling's widow as evidence of durable gratitude.

The episode also attributes to Zhang Cang major work on Han calendars, weights and measures, and recension of 《九章算术》. Its closing milk diet, many wives and concubines, sexual-practice, death-date, and centenarian claims add biographical color but not a verified medical account of longevity. The defensible synthesis is a many-sided official-intellectual profile, with technical contributions and office accountability kept distinct from spectacular private-life anecdotes.

## Key Characteristics

- Fictional bridge between old-aristocratic and Qin central-government experience who carries both the “术” layer and the enfeoffment/direct-administration comparison.
- Historical advocate of Han water virtue whose position is displaced by an accepted earth-virtue omen.
- Chancellor appointed after Guan Ying's death on the strength of long cross-regime administrative experience.
- Long-serving chancellor whose stated removal charge concerns private benefit through recommendation.
- Qin-Han archival and technical figure associated with calendars, metrology, and 《九章算术》.
- Recipient of Wang Ling's life-saving intercession who later supports Wang Ling's widow.

## Qualifications

This page is built from two literary-discussion sources and three popular-history episodes, not an independently sourced biography. Fictional events, characterization, thoughts, or relationships must not be treated as documentary evidence. Hanji 215 part 1 does not independently verify the precise appointment date, career sequence, Xunzi connection, peer-student claim, kingdom tenures, or technical expertise. Hanji 225-2 does not independently verify the dragon, precise reforms, court procedure, or Gongsun Chen's intent. Hanji 227 does not independently establish the charge, office duration, early career, rescue mechanics, scholarly attribution, comparative history of algorithms, household size, sexual conduct, diet, exact age, or cause of longevity.
