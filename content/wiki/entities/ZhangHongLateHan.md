# 张纮 / Zhang Hong (Late Han)

## Overview
张纮 / Zhang Hong is a Jiangdong adviser whose current wiki profile spans family-level Sun Ce trust, administrative bench work, court-facing diplomacy, and a decisive post-Sun Ce mediation that restrains Cao Cao from attacking mourning Jiangdong.

## Current Profile
Zhang Hong first appears as someone [[SunCe|孙策]] can trust with his household before openly building Jiangdong power. Later evidence places him in Sun Ce's adviser bench with [[ZhangZhaoLateHan|张昭]], [[QinSongLateHan|秦松]], and [[ChenDuanLateHan|陈端]], then turns him into the court-facing channel through which Sun Ce seeks recognition from the Xu court.

Hanji 1052 makes that diplomatic role sharper. Sun Ce sends Zhang Hong to Xudu; [[CaoCao|曹操]] admires him and wants to keep him, but Zhang Hong remains tied to Sun-family interests. After Sun Ce's death, Zhang Hong argues that attacking [[SunQuan|孙权]] during mourning would be morally ugly and strategically risky because a failed attack could create a new enemy. Cao Cao accepts the restraint frame and instead recommends Sun Quan for office.

## Key Characteristics
- Carries family-level trust before Jiangdong consolidation, not merely routine adviser service.
- Belongs to the civil-administrative bench that lets Sun Ce's organization operate beyond the battlefield.
- Acts as a court-facing diplomatic channel between Jiangdong and Cao Cao's Han-court recognition field.
- Advises against premature imperial ambition, keeping Sun Ce inside a Han-subject legitimacy posture.
- Uses reputation and enemy-creation risk to restrain Cao Cao from opportunistic attack during Sun Quan's mourning.
- Remains legible as a Sun-family adviser even when Cao Cao values him and the court appoints him.

## Evidence
- Family trust: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] has Sun Ce entrust his mother and younger siblings to Zhang Hong in [[GuanglingCommandery|广陵]] before going to [[Shouchun|寿春]] to serve [[YuanShu|袁术]].
- Jiangdong adviser bench: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-994-genzhe-dongwu-zhongchen-lvfan-xuexi-zhichang-zhihui-lgqp2vqmh1mss96gcwmflp-ccpfu|Hanji 994]] places Zhang Hong with Zhang Zhao, Qin Song, and Chen Duan as staff depth for Sun Ce's expanding regional organization.
- Court recognition work: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1028-xiaobawang-sunce-liwei-chengba-jiangdong-lgur-3fmpr55wy3aji-0rm38y7x|Hanji 1028]] has Sun Ce send Zhang Hong with tribute, after which Cao Cao recommends Sun Ce for office and draws Jiangdong toward the Han-court legitimacy field.
- Loyalty and mediation under Cao Cao's attention: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1052-sanguo-zhongchen-zhanghong-jing-wannong-caocao-yu-guzhang-ljc3sd7meigo37uy-1fxnkx-psqg|Hanji 1052]] says Cao Cao admired Zhang Hong and wanted to keep him, but Zhang Hong returned to Jiangdong and remained usable by the Sun side.
- Post-Sun Ce restraint: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1052-sanguo-zhongchen-zhanghong-jing-wannong-caocao-yu-guzhang-ljc3sd7meigo37uy-1fxnkx-psqg|Hanji 1052]] shows Zhang Hong arguing against Cao Cao's attack plan during Sun Quan's mourning and helping turn the moment toward recommending Sun Quan as 讨虏将军 and Kuaiji administrator.

## Qualifications
Hanji 1052's title frames Zhang Hong as if he "played" Cao Cao, but the body supports a more bounded judgment: he mediates a dangerous incentive conflict through reputation, ritual, and strategic-risk language. The page therefore does not treat him as a Cao-side defector or as someone who literally controlled Cao Cao. The evidence is still episodic and does not yet synthesize Zhang Hong's full later career or writings.

## Relationships
- [[SunCe|孙策]] - leader who entrusts family, recruits Zhang Hong, uses him in diplomacy, and receives his anti-usurpation counsel.
- [[SunQuan|孙权]] - successor whose mourning-period vulnerability Zhang Hong helps protect from Cao Cao's attack plan.
- [[CaoCao|曹操]] - outside power whom Zhang Hong restrains by invoking reputation and enemy-creation risk.
- [[ZhangZhaoLateHan|张昭]] - paired senior Jiangdong adviser under Sun Ce and Lady Wu.
- [[LadyWuSunJian|吴夫人]] - Sun-family elder who entrusts Zhang Hong and Zhang Zhao with assisting young Sun Quan.
- [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]] - office field attached to Sun Quan after Cao Cao shifts from attack to recommendation.
- [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] - legitimacy frame for Zhang Hong's court-facing recommendation work.
- [[ReputationConstrainedWarRestraint|名声约束下的战争节制]] - concept directly modeled by Zhang Hong's advice to Cao Cao.
- [[SuccessionAuthorityConsolidation|继位权威整合]] - succession frame that Zhang Hong helps stabilize before Sun Quan's later internal tests.
