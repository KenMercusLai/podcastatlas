# 张纮 / Zhang Hong (Late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] backfills Zhang Hong before the Jiangdong staff branch. After [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]'s death, [[SunCe|孙策]] entrusts his mother and younger siblings to Zhang Hong in [[GuanglingCommandery|广陵]] before going to [[Shouchun|寿春]] to serve [[YuanShu|袁术]]. The episode distinguishes ordinary trust from family-level entrustment and treats Zhang Hong's reputation as part of Sun Ce's political and social backing.

张纮 / Zhang Hong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-994-genzhe-dongwu-zhongchen-lvfan-xuexi-zhichang-zhihui-lgqp2vqmh1mss96gcwmflp-ccpfu|Hanji 994]] as a member of [[SunCe|孙策]]'s early Jiangdong administrative bench. Sun Ce appoints him as `正议校尉`, and the episode says Zhang Hong and [[ZhangZhaoLateHan|张昭]] are often used as a paired arrangement: one remains behind while the other follows Sun Ce on campaign.

The source uses Zhang Hong less as a standalone biography than as evidence that Sun Ce's organization is becoming more than a battlefield following. Together with Zhang Zhao, [[QinSongLateHan|秦松]], and [[ChenDuanLateHan|陈端]], he represents the staff depth needed for an expanding regional power to deliberate, administer, and keep continuity while the military leader moves.

Because the episode's main leadership contrast is Sun Ce's trust in Zhang Zhao's public reputation, Zhang Hong's current wiki role is to round out that talent bench rather than carry the anti-jealousy claim directly. Future sources can expand this page if they develop Zhang Hong's later career or writings.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1028-xiaobawang-sunce-liwei-chengba-jiangdong-lgur-3fmpr55wy3aji-0rm38y7x|Hanji 1028]] adds Zhang Hong as the court-facing channel for Sun Ce after Jiangdong momentum is established. [[SunCe|孙策]] sends Zhang Hong with tribute, and [[CaoCao|曹操]] responds by recommending Sun Ce for office and drawing him toward the Han-court legitimacy field. Zhang Hong's role therefore expands from family-trust and staff depth into external recognition work between Jiangdong and the Xu court.
