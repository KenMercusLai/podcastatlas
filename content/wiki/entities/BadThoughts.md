# Bad Thoughts

## Overview
*Bad Thoughts* is a Tom Segura screen-comedy project discussed in a [[HubermanLab]] conversation about comedy, creativity, acting, and performance.

## Current Profile
[[TomSegura]] describes *Bad Thoughts* as a collection of short stories that lets him play many roles and realize an early ambition to make movies and shows. In the source, the project matters less as a complete production history than as evidence that a stand-up comic's observational voice, dark material, character work, and ear for personality can move into scripted screen forms.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses a short-story or mini-movie structure rather than one continuous stand-up set.
- Gives Segura room to act across multiple roles and voices.
- Extends his dark comic sensibility into scripted visual storytelling.
- Involves creative fulfillment across writing, production, performance, and editing.

## Evidence
- Format - [[the-science-art-of-comedy-creativity-tom-segura-scim7538555033]] has Segura describe the series as a collection of short stories and mini-movies.
- Performance - [[the-science-art-of-comedy-creativity-tom-segura-scim7538555033]] says the format allowed him to play many roles.
- Creative trajectory - [[the-science-art-of-comedy-creativity-tom-segura-scim7538555033]] connects the project to his teenage acting and improv experience and original desire to make movies.

## Qualifications
The source is promotional and reflective rather than a complete account of the project's cast, release, reception, financing, or production history. Huberman's praise and sleep-laughter anecdote are personal responses, not audience-wide evidence.
