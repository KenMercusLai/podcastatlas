# 殷通 / Yin Tong

殷通 / Yin Tong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-7-liubang-he-xiangyu-youhe-butong-llwcqatndck4zita2piufwhjpxp1|Qinji 128-7]] as the [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]] governor who tries to respond to the anti-[[QinState|秦国]] wave after [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] rises. He invites [[XiangLiang|项梁]] to discuss rebellion and imagines using Xiang Liang and [[HuanChu|桓楚]] as commanders.

The episode treats Yin Tong as trying to hold both sides of the situation: he remains the Qin-appointed governor but wants to move into anti-Qin action before events overtake him. That calculation fails because Xiang Liang refuses to become someone else's subordinate. Xiang Liang uses the search for Huan Chu as a pretext to bring armed [[XiangYu|项羽]] into the room, and Xiang Yu kills Yin Tong during the meeting.

Yin Tong's death is the hinge of [[KuaijiCommanderyCoup|会稽郡守府夺权]]. The commandery resources he hoped to direct against Qin instead become the base of the Xiang family's independent rebel force.
