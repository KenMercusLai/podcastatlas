# 宋国 / Song State

宋国 / Song State enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]], where the episode pauses during the [[SongDaogong|宋悼公]] to [[SongXiugong|宋休公]] succession notice to explain why Song was special among pre-Qin states. The source says Song preserved the Shang lineage through [[WeiziQi|微子启]] and received high ritual status under Zhou rule.

The page's wiki role is not a full institutional history. It anchors [[ShangRemnantStateLegitimacy]], the idea that a conquered dynasty's surviving lineage can be incorporated into the new order as a ritually honored guest rather than being eliminated.

The episode also uses Song to connect later classical authority back to lineage: [[Confucius|孔子]] is said to descend from Song nobility through [[KongfuJia|孔父嘉]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] adds the later succession from [[SongXiugong|宋休公]], whose personal name is given as Song Tian, to [[SongPigong|宋辟公]] in 373 BCE.
