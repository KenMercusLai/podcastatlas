# Bündnis Sahra Wagenknecht / BSW

## Overview

Bündnis Sahra Wagenknecht, or BSW, is the populist-left German party that enters the Saxony-Anhalt parliament in the source and becomes a possible kingmaker after [[AlternativeForGermany|AfD]]'s large victory.

## Current Profile

The source identifies BSW as populist-left, pro-Russian, and anti-elite. It matters less because of its size than because of coalition arithmetic: with five of 83 seats and no categorical refusal to work with AfD, it could affect whether AfD gains practical influence despite the [[BrandmauerCordonSanitaire|Brandmauer]].

The party's possible cooperation areas also show why ideological labels alone are insufficient. The episode lists Russia sanctions, energy policy, and school curricula as areas where BSW's lead candidate could imagine project-by-project cooperation with AfD.

## Key Characteristics

- Populist-left German party in the Saxony-Anhalt result.
- Possible kingmaker because it enters parliament with five of 83 seats.
- Party not ruling out cooperation with AfD in the episode's account.
- Pro-Russian and anti-elite actor whose issue overlap with AfD may matter around sanctions, energy, and schools.
