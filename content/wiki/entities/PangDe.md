# 庞德 / Pang De

## Overview
庞德 / Pang De enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1057-da-bu-liao-zhang-de-jiangjun-bushi-youxiu-de-shufajia-ljhy16leevjd8i-7vmuxtqm96ytg|Hanji 1057]] as a Ma Teng-side officer in the Pingyang campaign. His role is compact but important: he kills [[GuoYuanLateHan|郭元]], presents the head to [[ZhongYao|钟繇]], and triggers a kinship-versus-public-duty scene.

## Current Profile
The source presents Pang De as operating in [[MaTeng|马腾]]'s military line, under Ma Chao in the episode's account, during Zhong Yao's campaign against Guo Yuan and the Southern Xiongnu. Pang De does not recognize Guo Yuan as Zhong Yao's nephew when he presents the head. Zhong Yao's answer shifts the scene away from private apology and toward the claim that Guo Yuan's rebellion against the court made the killing legitimate.

## Key Characteristics
- Military officer tied to Ma Teng's western force field.
- Battlefield actor credited with killing Guo Yuan.
- Unwitting participant in a kinship-duty dilemma.
- Helps mark Ma Teng's cooperation with Cao-side stabilization after Zhong Yao's persuasion.
