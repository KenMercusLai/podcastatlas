# 冯浩 / Feng Hao (Warring States adviser)

冯浩 / Feng Hao appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-39-lishishang-zhangyi-shi-zenyang-nudui-qiangdi-de-lv2gc2wtcxuhugfgixk7rbbhpa6x]] after [[HuiShi|惠施 / 惠师]] flees from [[WeiState|魏国]] to [[ChuState|楚国]]. The episode says Feng Hao advises [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] to send Hui Shi onward to [[SongState|宋国]].

His importance is pragmatic rather than biographical. Feng Hao's advice treats Zhang Yi's influence as a cross-court risk: sheltering Hui Shi is less valuable than avoiding a direct offense to [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]. The episode uses the moment to show that Zhang Yi's position in Wei already affects how other rulers manage displaced advisers.
