# 《煎饼侠》 / Jian Bing Man

## Overview
《煎饼侠》 is represented as [[DapengDirector|大鹏]]'s commercially successful first feature and the project that converted an existing web-comedy persona into a film-director identity.

## Current Profile
The source describes the film as opportunity-led: a purchased big-screen extension of web comedy preceded Dapeng's certainty that he should direct it. The decisive solution was to let him play a version of the already-famous “Dapeng,” making his public persona part of the narrative rather than an obstacle to casting.

## Key Characteristics
- Web-persona-to-feature adaptation rather than a wholly separate author project.
- Large commercial opening that created a difficult benchmark for later films.
- Early case of internet-native creator recognition converting into theatrical opportunity.

## Evidence
- Project origin - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt|导演大鹏×罗永浩！我这两辈子]] describes the film as emerging from purchased big-movie rights and a long search for why Dapeng himself had to star.
- Commercial benchmark - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] attributes roughly RMB 1.16 billion box office to the film and says later work was judged against that unusually large debut.
- Compensation tension - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] says Dapeng remained salaried inside Sohu and did not directly receive director profit from the box office.

## Qualifications
Revenue, compensation, rights, and development claims are attributed to the interview and are not independently verified here. Commercial success does not settle artistic quality or reception.
