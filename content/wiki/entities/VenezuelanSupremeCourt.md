# Venezuelan Supreme Court

The Venezuelan Supreme Court appears in [[caracas-under-pressure-democracy-in-venezuela-6a7d8bbe7d85cbdf96e00822]] as a target of negotiation and reform. The source says recent talks produced an initial agreement around court reform, and that a "real" Supreme Court would be one condition under which [[MariaCorinaMachado]] might more fully support the process.

This page is distinct from the wiki's [[SupremeCourt]] page, which tracks the United States institution. Here the court is part of Venezuela's disputed transition architecture, paired with the [[VenezuelanElectoralCouncil]] and party-rights guarantees.
