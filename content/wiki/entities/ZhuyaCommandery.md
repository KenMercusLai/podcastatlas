## Overview

珠崖郡 / Zhuya Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-472-cong-qizhi-zhuya-dao-zairu-bantu-jiemi-hainan-lishi-lhvp37w_0z8rebkka6squgfgledp|Hanji 472]] as the Western Han commandery on Hainan whose repeated unrest forces [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] to choose between suppression, garrisoning, and abolition.

## Current Profile

Hanji 472 treats Zhuya as a test of how far direct frontier administration should extend when state capacity is strained. The episode says local disorder had repeatedly killed officials and commoners, while the wider empire faced Guandong famine, thin stores, and commoner hunger. Yuan therefore abolishes the commandery and allows willing people to attach to the interior without forcing the unwilling.

The profile is not one of simple abandonment or simple restoration. The episode says some scholars read post-abolition Hainan as attached to Hepu Commandery, but it also stresses that the move from two commanderies and sixteen counties to one commandery/one county and then county abolition marks a major loss of effective control. Later Eastern Han references to Ma Yuan and Zhuya are treated as restored contact and tribute, with the Zhuya county seat on the mainland at Xuwen rather than a restoration of direct island rule.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han island-frontier commandery associated with Hainan.
- Repeatedly troubled by local violence and rebellion against Han officials and people.
- Abolished by Han Yuandi in 46 BCE amid famine and fiscal-capacity pressure.
- Continued as a name and loose attachment problem after formal commandery abolition.
- Later Eastern Han contact is framed as tribute and reception rather than restored direct island administration.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 472. It does not settle the full administrative geography of early Hainan, the exact chronology of every county-level change, local Hainan social structures, or the scholarly debate over Hepu jurisdiction. "Zhuya" is treated here as an administrative and memory label, not as proof of continuous direct rule.

## What Changed

- Created Zhuya Commandery as an island-frontier capacity case centered on Han Yuandi's abolition decision.
- Preserved the episode's distinction between formal or nominal attachment and effective direct control.
