# 袁术 / Yuan Shu

袁术 / Yuan Shu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1020-sanguo-baijiazi-yuanshu-ruhe-ba-hao-pai-da-de-xilan-lhwr9hfmanr-zktleufy3kvl38u0|Hanji 1020]] as the late-Han powerholder whose imperial claim lets [[CaoCao|曹操]] ask [[HeKui|何夔]] for a diagnosis. The episode does not build a full biography; it treats Yuan Shu as the negative case for reading "势" badly.

The source summarizes his failure in three connected ways: he does not align with the political moment, he does not keep trust, and he cannot value capable people. He Kui's refusal to serve him becomes evidence of that talent failure, while the later [[YangBiao|杨彪]] case shows how Yuan Shu's family and marriage network can create political risk for old-Han elites under Cao Cao.
