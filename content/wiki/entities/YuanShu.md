# 袁术 / Yuan Shu

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-946-zhichang-douzheng-shi-yourouguaduan-you-duo-zhiming-lpyyweaajonzg7kxuhwm9z7ae7p5|Hanji 946]] adds Yuan Shu's earliest local role in the Luoyang collapse before his later regional and imperial-title branches. After [[HeJin|何进]] is killed, Yuan Shu joins [[WuKuangLateHan|吴匡]] and others in the gate assault, burns the South Palace Qing-suo Gate, and helps turn the anti-eunuch response into open palace warfare.

This source does not yet show the later Yuan Shu of [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] failure, but it does show the family and factional world he comes from: armed retaliation, private office struggle, and weak court procedure already surround his first wiki appearance.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-952-danxing-haoshi-weishenme-mowen-qiancheng-li9vvaeaues9-fxy4e4yp-f4pugj|Hanji 952]] adds an earlier sponsorship layer before Yuan Shu's grain cutoff in Hanji 955. After [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] kills [[ZhangZiLateHan|张咨]] and secures [[NanyangCommandery|Nanyang]] compliance, he reaches Luyang and joins Yuan Shu; Yuan Shu then recommends him as acting General Who Smashes the Caitiffs and Yu Province inspector. Because the imperial court is controlled by [[DongZhuo|董卓]], the source treats these titles as practical Yuan-Shu patronage rather than clean court authorization.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-954-renxingbenshan-haishi-renxingbene-lkurz9dbb25jrjyl5c-rrahda39h|Hanji 954]] adds Yuan Shu's earliest imperial-ambition shadow before the later [[HeirloomImperialSeal|传国玉玺]] and self-coronation branches. When [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] propose installing [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] as an alternative emperor, Yuan Shu publicly rejects the plan by defending [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] and blaming [[DongZhuo|董卓]] rather than the young ruler.

The source reads that loyalist answer against Yuan Shu's private ambition. A respected adult Liu Yu would make Yuan Shu's own future title claim harder, so his opposition differs from [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s legitimacy argument even when both reject replacement. Hanji 954 therefore makes Yuan Shu's later [[TitleRealityRestraint|名实相符式名号节制]] failure visible before the seal and prophecy arguments appear.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-955-guanyu-zhan-huaxiong-qishi-shi-wei-lishi-lndkrj-fnepye8iu-ifgyh-vzysf|Hanji 955]] backfills Yuan Shu as both Sun Jian's supplier and his rear constraint before the later Shouchun and imperial-title branches. When rumors warn that [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] will become hard to control after taking [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], Yuan Shu cuts his grain; Sun Jian rides more than one hundred li to confront him, points to the campaign map, and reframes continued supply as service to the Han state and revenge for the Yuan family's dead.

The source therefore gives Yuan Shu an early version of the resource-and-suspicion pattern seen later in his dealings with Sun Ce and Lü Bu. He can back useful force, but he also prices that force as a future autonomy threat. The episode also places Yuan Shu before the later [[HeirloomImperialSeal|传国玉玺]] branch by having Sun Jian discover the seal at Luoyang after the restored supply lets the campaign continue.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-958-jiemi-shishang-zui-duanming-de-tongchoudikai-lve41q9gyijbop8wk-b7e6shogju|Hanji 958]] adds an earlier Yuan Shu branch before the later Shouchun and imperial-title failures. When [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] tries to reach [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] through Liu Yu's son, the envoy reaches [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]] and Yuan Shu detains him. The episode reads Yuan Shu's promise to escort the emperor as a self-interested attempt to draw Liu Yu's cavalry into his own field.

The same source shows Yuan Shu competing with [[YuanShao|袁绍]] inside the [[AntiDongZhuoCoalition|反董卓联盟]]. Yuan Shu sends [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] against Dong Zhuo's forces, then sends [[GongsunYueLateHan|公孙越]] to help Sun Jian retake Yangcheng from [[ZhouAngLateHan|周昂]]. This gives Yuan Shu an early pattern later repeated in other forms: he can use allies and symbols, but the surrounding coalition remains brittle because offices, troops, and recognition are being privately priced.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-967-ta-canyu-mousha-dongzhuo-jingneng-taoguo-sishen-weilie-lv24biyswj9ljdld5htynhauhph3|Hanji 967]] backfills Yuan Shu as [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s first major host after Lü Bu leaves [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]]. Yuan Shu initially treats him generously because Lü Bu helped kill [[DongZhuo|董卓]], but Lü Bu's public overclaiming of that merit and his soldiers' plunder make Yuan Shu resent him. This early failed hosting prefigures the later Yuan Shu-Lü Bu marriage, grain, and coalition failures.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-969-yuanshi-xiongdi-neidou-shui-neng-jianlou-lsgj6sewo35jpsgfm-koivijtr6r|Hanji 969]] backfills Yuan Shu before the later Shouchun, Jiangdong, and imperial-title branches. He defeats [[YuanYiLateHan|袁遗]] in the fight over [[YangzhouLateHan|扬州]], later comes under pressure from [[LiuBiao|刘表]], gathers support from [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]] remnants and [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]], and is driven south by [[CaoCao|曹操]]. After occupying [[Shouchun|寿春]], Yuan Shu calls himself Yangzhou inspector and Xuzhou lord, then receives a left-general, Yangzhai-marquis, and `假节` package from [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]], making his later title overreach intelligible as a sequence of useful but increasingly hollow recognitions.

袁术 / Yuan Shu is developed across the Hanji 983, Hanji 999-1002, Hanji 1008, and Hanji 1015-1020 late-Han branch as the powerholder whose resources, titles, symbols, and alliances repeatedly become liabilities. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu|Hanji 999]] shows an early Xuzhou manipulation before the title-ambition branch. Yuan Shu attacks [[LiuBei|刘备]], then uses promised grain to pull [[LyuBu|吕布]] into the [[Xiapi|下邳]] breach created after [[ZhangFei|张飞]] kills [[CaoBaoLateHan|曹豹]]. The source treats this as [[GrainLeverageAllianceBetrayal|粮食诱导式同盟背叛]]: Yuan Shu's resource promise helps remove Liu Bei's base even though Yuan Shu later stops supplying the grain.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-982-ba-yi-ba-liubei-ru-xu-beihou-de-liyi-zhi-zheng-ljajdc95xtylhkn5teefz26yyyz1|Hanji 982]] adds the earlier reason Xuzhou elites do not choose Yuan Shu before that later attack. The episode says [[MiZhu|糜竺]], [[ChenDeng|陈登]], and [[KongRong|孔融]] see his [[HuainanRegion|淮南]] base, rough reputation, [[HeirloomImperialSeal|传国玉玺]], and imperial ambition as an absorption and legitimacy threat. Yuan Shu is therefore the rejected high-force option in [[LocalEliteProxySelection|地方豪强代理人选择]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] supplies the earlier Sun Ce trust-failure branch. Yuan Shu uses [[WuJingLateHan|吴景]] and [[SunBenLateHan|孙贲]] to reach [[DanyangCommandery|丹阳郡]], then sends [[SunCe|孙策]] to recruit there instead of immediately granting all of [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]'s old troops. He later promises Sun Ce [[JiujiangCommandery|九江郡]] but appoints [[ChenJiLateHan|陈纪]], and promises [[LujiangCommandery|庐江郡]] after Sun Ce attacks [[LuKangLateHan|陆康]] but appoints [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]]. The source therefore makes [[PromiseCredibilityTalentRetention|承诺信用与人才流失]] an early mechanism behind Sun Ce's eventual separation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] supplies an earlier Sun Ce dependency branch. Yuan Shu has repeatedly failed to honor promises to [[SunCe|孙策]], but still permits Sun Ce to help [[WuJingLateHan|吴景]] at [[HengjiangLateHan|横江]] because he assumes [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]] and [[WangLangLateHan|王朗]] will prevent Sun Ce from succeeding in Jiangdong. The result is an unintended off-ramp: Yuan Shu's underestimation lets Sun Ce turn a subordinate mission into the start of an independent base.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-994-genzhe-dongwu-zhongchen-lvfan-xuexi-zhichang-zhihui-lgqp2vqmh1mss96gcwmflp-ccpfu|Hanji 994]] supplies a compact earlier connection: Yuan Shu recommends [[SunCe|孙策]] for an acting `讨寇将军` title, setting up Sun Ce's expanding Jiangdong camp before Yuan Shu's later title overreach and break with Sun Ce.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-995-ta-zuishang-cibei-weihe-duoci-cansha-enren-llicukoezccd5iycin4uq7qun51k|Hanji 995]] adds an earlier Yuzhang office branch. Yuan Shu has appointed [[ZhugeXuan|诸葛玄]] to [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]], while [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]] sends [[ZhuHaoLateHan|朱皓]] as a competing administrator. [[XuShao|许劭]] also treats Yuan Shu as violent and unlikely to last, making Yuan Shu a background condition in Liu Yao's safer-route calculation before the later self-coronation sequence.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1000-hao-meng-panluan-jingbi-lvbu-fanqiang-taopao-lluthd9hv-0nqbot4fuctjcuka68|Hanji 1000]] adds a later shadow role: [[CaoXingLateHan|曹性]] says Yuan Shu encouraged [[HaoMeng|郝萌]]'s revolt against [[LyuBu|吕布]], and the episode ends by teeing up why Yuan Shu will soon think he can claim the imperial title.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1001-weishenme-quan-ni-buyao-fanzhongnu-lolurkcjpie14nnczk0tu7iqerke|Hanji 1001]] supplies the earliest title-ambition setup: Yuan Shu reads "代汉者当涂高" through his own name, combines it with [[YinYangFivePhasesPoliticalTheory|five-phases]] succession logic, and treats the [[HeirloomImperialSeal|传国玉玺]] as supporting evidence.

The same source shows the claim failing before it is public. Yuan Shu's generals give no support, [[YanXiangLateHan|阎象]] warns that Yuan family prestige and Han weakness do not equal [[ZhouWenwang|周文王]]'s accumulated virtue, and [[ZhangChengLateHan|张承]] tells him rule is "in virtue, not in strength." Yuan Shu's later disaster therefore begins as a failure of [[PeopleBasedPoliticalSecurity|民心型政治安全]], [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]], and [[TitleRealityRestraint|名实相符式名号节制]], not only as a battlefield or supply problem.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1008-ruhe-pingjia-caocao-de-tuntianzhi-ls8wvh9ox-pcjmt0z8w82opbha-k|Hanji 1008]] supplies an earlier coalition-management failure: Yuan Shu tries to reduce [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s risk through a marriage proposal, then sends [[JiLing|纪灵]] against [[LiuBei|刘备]], but Lü Bu sees the encirclement danger and stops the campaign through辕门射戟.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1002-yuanshu-chengdi-hou-sunce-chengwei-zui-da-ying-llya3c2-pefujwxdxefvqxwxjizjo|Hanji 1002]] supplies the warning stage before the formal self-coronation account. Yuan Shu is already considering the imperial title, and [[SunCe|孙策]] writes to argue that the current [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] has not lost the moral standing that would justify replacement. The source makes Yuan Shu's ambition a title-reality failure before it becomes a military coalition problem.

That warning also shows Yuan Shu losing usable support before the larger anti-usurpation response. Sun Ce tells him that flatterers and prophecy-readers will not bear the consequences, Yuan Shu replies only with anger and illness, and the relationship breaks. In this source, unsupported imperial ambition first destroys an ally relationship, then later becomes the open [[AntiUsurpationCoalitionPressure|讨逆同盟压力]] developed in Hanji 1015.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1028-xiaobawang-sunce-liwei-chengba-jiangdong-lgur-3fmpr55wy3aji-0rm38y7x|Hanji 1028]] adds the post-break proxy phase. After Sun Ce separates from him, Yuan Shu secretly gives [[ZuLangLateHan|祖郎]] a seal and tries to use Zu Lang and [[Shanyue|山越]] forces to disrupt Sun Ce in [[DanyangCommandery|丹阳郡]]. The same episode shows [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] and [[LuSu|鲁肃]] rejecting Yuan Shu's county-level appointments and going to Sun Ce, so Yuan Shu is losing both subordinate trust and mobile talent while trying to weaponize local resistance.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] supplies the self-coronation scene: Yuan Shu declares himself emperor at [[Shouchun|寿春]], names the regime "仲家", changes the [[JiujiangCommandery|九江郡]] office into a [[HuainanRegion|淮南]] capital-style post, and performs imperial ritual before he has enough recognition to make the title secure.

That episode treats the claim as a failure of [[TitleRealityRestraint|名实相符式名号节制]]. Yuan Shu has family prestige and regional force, but the imperial name outruns his actual position and creates [[AntiUsurpationCoalitionPressure|讨逆同盟压力]]: [[CaoCao|曹操]], [[LiuBei|刘备]], [[SunCe|孙策]], [[YuanShao|袁绍]], and [[LyuBu|吕布]] all gain reasons to oppose him because ignoring the claim would damage their own legitimacy or reputation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1016-sanguo-luanshi-li-chengui-jiazu-de-mingyun-jueze-ltuckdcxpyxk-xmzp8rt-2gjfzcs|Hanji 1016]] frames his claim through [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]]: Yuan Shu takes the imperial name before he has enough support to make it stick, so [[CaoCao|曹操]] can attack him under [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s name.

That episode also shows Yuan Shu trying to compensate for isolation through personal pressure and marriage. He appeals to [[ChenGui|陈珪]], holds [[ChenDeng|陈登]] as leverage, offers [[JinShang|金尚]] the taiwei title, and sends [[HanYinLateHan|韩胤]] to arrange a marriage with [[LyuBu|吕布]]. The effort fails because Chen Gui turns the marriage into [[MarriageAllianceWarExposure|联姻同盟战场暴露]] for Xuzhou and because Lü Bu can still be moved away from Yuan Shu.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1020-sanguo-baijiazi-yuanshu-ruhe-ba-hao-pai-da-de-xilan-lhwr9hfmanr-zktleufy3kvl38u0|Hanji 1020]] later makes Yuan Shu the late-Han powerholder whose imperial claim lets Cao Cao ask [[HeKui|何夔]] for a diagnosis. The episode does not build a full biography; it treats Yuan Shu as the negative case for reading "势" badly.

The source summarizes his failure in three connected ways: he does not align with the political moment, he does not keep trust, and he cannot value capable people. He Kui's refusal to serve him becomes evidence of that talent failure, while the later [[YangBiao|杨彪]] case shows how Yuan Shu's family and marriage network can create political risk for old-Han elites under Cao Cao.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1018-ta-weihe-rang-caocao-jidan-sunce-weiju-liubei-wanxi-lqccdhrjmero-gt7vsaq2ejuhhw8|Hanji 1018]] supplies the immediately preceding coalition failure. After [[LyuBu|吕布]] breaks the marriage alliance, Yuan Shu sends [[ZhangXunYuanShu|张勋]], [[QiaoRui|桥蕤]], [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]], and [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] against him. [[ChenGui|陈珪]] reads the coalition as shallow, and Lü Bu's legitimacy-and-spoils letter makes Han Xian and Yang Feng defect, turning Yuan Shu's troop advantage into [[InterestStructuredAllianceFragility|利益结构式联盟脆弱性]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1019-jiemi-chenminwang-liuchong-de-chuanqi-yisheng-ll2fma7wupp5gczudysfett5lhio|Hanji 1019]] supplies the immediately preceding destructive case. Yuan Shu knows [[ChenStateLateHan|陈国]] is wealthy and asks it for grain; when [[LiuChongChenKing|刘宠]] and [[LuoJun|骆俊]] do not supply him, Yuan Shu sends [[ZhangKaiAssassin|张闿]] under a surrender pretext to kill both leaders. The episode reads this as a desperate "困兽" moment: Yuan Shu still has enough reach to destroy a local order, but the act also belongs to the phase in which his broader position is collapsing.
