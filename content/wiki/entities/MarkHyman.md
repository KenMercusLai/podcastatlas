# Mark Hyman

## Overview
Mark Hyman is the physician and functional-medicine advocate interviewed by [[AndrewHuberman]] in [[how-to-improve-your-vitality-heal-from-disease-dr-mark-hyman-scim9845833582]]. The episode presents him as a clinician who interprets chronic illness through interacting biological systems, personal exposures, nutrition, and longitudinal measurement.

## Current Profile
Within the bounded source, Hyman's clinical identity combines a [[FunctionalMedicineSystemsModel|systems-level explanatory model]] with practical foundations and broad biomarker use. His own account of severe illness and mercury exposure serves as the origin story for looking across gut, immune, mitochondrial, endocrine, metabolic, environmental, behavioral, and social inputs rather than assigning every symptom to a separate specialty.

His intervention hierarchy is uneven but recognizable: begin with minimally processed food, movement, sleep, recovery, air and water quality, connection, and common nutrient gaps; measure relevant markers; then consider drugs, supplements, diets, screening, or advanced therapies in context. He is cautious about some risks, including muscle loss with GLP-1 drugs and indiscriminate peptide use, but also discusses several emerging interventions through clinical experience and anecdote rather than detailed trial evidence.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames functional medicine as a systems-biology operating model rather than a collection of supplements or alternative tests.
- Looks for multiple causes across diet, microbiome, toxins, infection, stress, trauma, sleep, movement, hormones, and nutrient status.
- Prioritizes whole-food and lifestyle foundations while also supporting individualized supplements and clinical testing.
- Uses a [[TestInterveneRetestLoop|test-intervene-retest]] posture to compare a person's response with their own baseline.
- Connects individual chronic disease to food systems, environmental exposure, regulation, healthcare incentives, and public policy.
- Discusses emerging peptides, regenerative treatments, and longevity interventions more favorably than their evidence is established within this source.

## Evidence
- Systems model - [[how-to-improve-your-vitality-heal-from-disease-dr-mark-hyman-scim9845833582]] has Hyman describe the body as a network or ecosystem and functional medicine as a meta-framework for biology.
- Personal and clinical origin - [[how-to-improve-your-vitality-heal-from-disease-dr-mark-hyman-scim9845833582]] recounts his severe illness, attributed mercury exposure, and a multi-diagnosis patient case used to motivate root-cause care.
- Foundations - [[how-to-improve-your-vitality-heal-from-disease-dr-mark-hyman-scim9845833582]] emphasizes minimally processed food, exercise, sleep, air, water, relationships, meaning, and selected nutrient testing.
- Measurement - [[how-to-improve-your-vitality-heal-from-disease-dr-mark-hyman-scim9845833582]] discusses ApoB, insulin resistance, body composition, nutrients, hormones, heavy metals, inflammation, and changing biomarkers over different time scales.
- Intervention boundaries - [[how-to-improve-your-vitality-heal-from-disease-dr-mark-hyman-scim9845833582]] warns about GLP-1-related lean-mass loss and adverse effects, casual gut cleanses, continuous BPC-157 use, and unsupervised peptide use.
- Emerging-treatment posture - [[how-to-improve-your-vitality-heal-from-disease-dr-mark-hyman-scim9845833582]] includes personal or clinical anecdotes involving chelation, NAD infusions, exosomes, stem-cell care, cancer screening, and multi-causal Alzheimer treatment.

## Qualifications
This profile is bounded to one long-form interview and is not a complete biography, credential review, or independent assessment of Hyman's clinical claims. Case reports and personal experiences cannot establish efficacy by themselves. Supplement doses, elimination diets, chelation, ketogenic diets, GLP-1 drugs, peptides, hormones, cancer screening, NAD-related products, exosomes, and stem cells require different levels of evidence, regulation, monitoring, and professional supervision.
