# 蒙武 / Meng Wu

蒙武 / Meng Wu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-117-1-papa-dalian-lishishang-shuangwen-de-kaishan-bizu-lr0h-kfeguvatattnj-mz8fgvnc]] as the Qin general moving south with [[LiXinQin|李信]] against [[ChuState|楚国]] while [[WangBenQin|王贲]] attacks [[WeiState|魏国]]. The source uses him mainly as part of Qin's multi-front pressure rather than as the center of a separate episode.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-117-2-xuezhedian-qinchao-wujiang-jingran-ruci-gaoqingshang-lsyw3eitcnzfexl1l6vfh2gbtaoy]] gives him a clearer command role after Li Xin and [[MengTian|蒙恬]] are defeated by [[XiangYan|项燕]]. When [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] asks [[WangJianQin|王翦]] to return for the Chu campaign, Wang Jian requires 600,000 troops and asks that Meng Wu serve as deputy. In this source, Meng Wu therefore marks Wang Jian's preferred command structure for the corrected campaign.

The page keeps a source-critical boundary: an earlier Zhouji 69 source says the chronicle's 285 BCE "Meng Wu" notice should be corrected to [[MengAo|蒙敖]], Meng Wu's father. That correction does not erase Meng Wu's later Qinji 117 role in the Chu campaign branch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-118-1-wangjian-lv-liushiwan-dajun-duizhan-xiangyan-haoxi-kaichang-le-lsf2puinwg0m3bgyi25znof7aznu]] keeps Meng Wu as Wang Jian's deputy during the successful second Chu campaign. He shares in the refusal to answer Xiang Yan's challenges, the fortified waiting phase, and the later 223 BCE capture of [[ChuFuchu|熊负刍 / 楚王负刍]] with Wang Jian.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-118-2-chi-gua-le-wangshi-de-gua-bao-tian-lns7jt-kbv3qunm66hzltdgugbco|Qinji 118-2]] keeps Meng Wu in the last cleanup of Chu resistance. After Wang Jian takes Chenying and Shouchun, Meng Wu remains paired with him in the final attack where [[ChangpingJun|昌平君]] dies and [[XiangYan|项燕]] commits suicide. His page therefore records both the captured-ruler endpoint and the extinction of the last royal-resistance branch.
