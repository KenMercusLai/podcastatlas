# Abbie Hoffman

## Overview

Abbie Hoffman was a Yippie organizer who made theatrical provocation and television visibility central to anti-war protest around the [[Chicago1968DemocraticConvention|1968 Democratic National Convention]].

## Current Profile

Hoffman understood political action as a media event: modern revolutionaries should seek the television studio, and absurdity could expose institutional violence or hypocrisy. In Chicago, however, the Yippies' mockery, exaggerated threats, chaotic planning, and dismissal of local warnings collided with authorities prepared to take such language literally. The resulting violence gave the protest enormous visibility but did not secure sympathetic mass interpretation.

## Key Characteristics

- Used humor, happenings, psychedelic imagery, and mock institutions as political tactics.
- Treated television attention as a primary terrain of modern activism.
- Helped revive plans for a Chicago festival after Johnson's withdrawal and Kennedy's assassination.
- Discounted local organizers' warnings about police violence and organizational weakness.
- Became both a preemptive arrest target and later a Chicago Seven defendant.
