# Apprentice Boys of Derry

The Apprentice Boys of Derry appear in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] through the 12 August 1969 parade commemorating the relief of Derry in 1689. The episode says [[JamesChichesterClark]] and [[JimCallaghan]] both had reasons not to ban the parade, so it went ahead despite the explosive local atmosphere.

The parade became the immediate setting for insults, thrown objects, scuffles, an [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]] baton charge into the [[Bogside]], and the beginning of the [[BattleOfTheBogside]]. The group therefore functions in the wiki as part of contested public ritual rather than only as an organization.
