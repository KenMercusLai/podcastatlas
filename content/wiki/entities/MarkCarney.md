# Mark Carney

Mark Carney enters the wiki through [[elbows-up-again-a-us-canada-trade-flare-up-6a8c0fa3a89d26398ee7a5db]] as [[Canada]]'s prime minister during a renewed [[UnitedStates]] trade confrontation. The episode says he called off talks that had seemed close to agreement, promised to match [[DonaldTrump]]'s tariffs dollar for dollar, and then set a September 8 waiting period before matching American tariffs.

His role is defined by [[TradeDiversificationStabilityParadox]]. The source says Carney wants to reduce Canadian dependence on the U.S. market, but needs enough stability with the United States to attract the investment required for diversification. Retaliation is also federally constrained: [[Alberta]]'s oil leverage and [[Quebec]]'s tariff and language-politics exposure make a single national response hard to sustain.
