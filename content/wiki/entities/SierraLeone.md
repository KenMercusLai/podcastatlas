# Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone appears in [[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]] as an earlier [[WestAfricaCocaineTransit]] case. [[OriOgunbiyi]] says a Dutchman with a 24-year prison sentence in the Netherlands was described as hiding there, illustrating how weak enforcement and corruption can let traffickers use the region as a safe operating base.

The source also treats Sierra Leone as a route-displacement example. Increased scrutiny of shipments from Sierra Leone is said to have pushed traffickers toward other countries, including [[Liberia]] and [[Ghana]], making [[CocaineRouteDisplacement]] a regional rather than single-country problem.
