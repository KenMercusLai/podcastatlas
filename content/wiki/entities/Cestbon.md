# C'estbon / 怡宝

## Overview
C'estbon / 怡宝 is the pure-water brand in the source's packaged-water segment, presented as a leading incumbent whose share is being pressured by price competition and channel change.

## Current Profile
The source frames C'estbon as a strong but squeezed pure-water brand. It remains associated with the two-yuan price band while lower-priced pure-water competitors and new purchase channels make the category harder to defend. The page is therefore a narrow source-scoped brand-pressure node rather than a full profile of parent ownership or beverage operations.

## Key Characteristics
- Leading pure-water brand facing share decline in the source account.
- Competes in a category where pure water is gaining share inside packaged water while individual brand positions shift.
- Holds a higher price band than some aggressive competitors.
- Faces pressure from rivals such as [[NongfuSpring|农夫山泉]] and [[WahahaPureWater|娃哈哈纯净水]].
- Is exposed to [[BottledWaterChannelFragmentation|channel fragmentation]] as traditional offline terminals lose traffic to instant retail and discount formats.

- Share pressure: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says C'estbon's leading pure-water share fell sharply and that Wahaha moved closer.
- Price-band evidence: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says C'estbon remained around the two-yuan price band while competitors pushed one-yuan or lower prices.
