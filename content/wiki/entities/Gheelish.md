# Gheelish

## Overview
Gheelish is [[DarrenAlpert]]'s organic ghee-based snack brand and the first caller case in [[advice-line-with-ben-goodwin-of-olipop-f9d3fe17-eb2c-440d-b223-990fed970727]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Gheelish as a young wholesale-first snack company with fast store growth and a messaging problem. Its products have an unfamiliar ingredient story around ghee, but Goodwin and Guy Raz argue that mainstream trial should be led by taste and simple comparative nutrition before deeper education.

## Key Characteristics
- Makes organic snacks based on ghee, starting with popcorn and expanding into a pop chip or PopCorners-style product.
- Reached about 4,000 stores within roughly two years.
- Began with homemade samples and a cold email that led to nationwide Sprouts placement.
- Reported about $1.8 million in 2025 sales and expected about $3.5 million in the current year.
- Sells wholesale without DTC or Amazon in the source account.
- Faces a communication challenge because many shoppers recognize ghee only as clarified butter in a jar.
