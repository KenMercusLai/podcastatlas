# Reginald Maudling

Reginald Maudling appears in [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] as [[EdwardHeath]]'s home secretary during the 1970-1971 [[NorthernIreland]] crisis. The episode portrays him as clever and liberal-minded but also lazy, corrupt, alcohol-dependent, and dismissive of Northern Ireland, making him a poor fit for a fast-moving insurgency and legitimacy crisis.

Maudling is tied to both [[OperationDemetrius]] and the later abuse scandal. The source says he misleadingly claimed internment had been done on army advice, and that he and Lord Carrington approved interrogation methods later condemned through the [[FiveTechniques]] controversy.
