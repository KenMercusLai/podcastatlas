# Jiangxi / 江西

Jiangxi / 江西 is the province context for [[Jingdezhen]] in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode places Jingdezhen in northeastern Jiangxi, where access to local ceramic materials and water transport toward Poyang Lake and the [[YangtzeRiver|Yangtze River]] helped make fragile porcelain shipment more feasible.

The source also uses provincial economic comparison to sharpen Jingdezhen's modern profile: the city has modest GDP scale inside Jiangxi but unusually high cultural status, tourism attention, creator attraction, and ceramic-industry identity. That makes Jiangxi relevant to both [[HeritageTourismCommercialization]] and [[AdvancedCeramicsIndustrialUpgrade]].
