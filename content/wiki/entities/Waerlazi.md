## Overview

瓦尔拉齐 / Walrazi is a teacher-antagonist in [[WhiteHorseNeighsInWesternWind|《白马啸西风》]] whose sincere instruction and intermittent concern coexist with murder, attempted collective poisoning, possessiveness, and a final effort to kill [[LiWenxiu|李文秀]].

## Current Profile

The bounded source treats Walrazi as morally complex but not morally balanced. Rejection by 雅丽仙 becomes a grievance against her, 车尔库, and their wider community; he kills the person he claims to love and plans to poison the tribe's well. His self-interest also shapes his social judgment, because his willingness to betray makes other people appear equally untrustworthy.

Yet Walrazi teaches Li seriously, gives her his martial knowledge, warns her away from danger in the 高昌 maze, and does not immediately kill her when she sides with 苏普's group. Those actions make him more than a featureless villain, but his final poisoned-needle attempt shows why tenderness or hesitation cannot by itself establish transformation. The profile therefore separates evidence of attachment from evidence of safety, accountability, or redemption.

## Key Characteristics

- Converts unreturned love into possessive revenge against both individuals and a community.
- Plans indiscriminate poisoning and personally kills 雅丽仙.
- Interprets others through his own self-interest and distrust.
- Teaches Li with seriousness and transmits real skill.
- Shows warning, hesitation, and limited concern without sustained moral change.
- Ends by returning to lethal action against his student.

## Qualifications

This profile is based on one structured podcast summary rather than direct textual comparison. Walrazi's motives, the depth of his affection for Li, the sequence of his hesitation, and the source's claim that selfishness is his core vice remain interpretive. Moral complexity means that contradictory behavior is represented; it does not imply equal weighting of care and attempted mass violence.
