# Rose Bertin

## Overview

Rose Bertin was the dressmaker whose unusually direct collaboration with [[MarieAntoinette|Marie Antoinette]] made fashion part of the queen's personal influence and public exposure.

## Current Profile

The source presents Bertin as a dressmaker with unusual access to the queen's apartments. Their collaboration moved Marie Antoinette away from rigid court silks and structured dress toward white muslin, flowers, and pastoral simplicity, helping turn clothing into a field of personal and frequently changing choice rather than a fixed expression of hierarchy.

## Key Characteristics

- Dressmaker with direct access to Marie Antoinette.
- Collaborator in simpler muslin and pastoral styles.
- Participant in a shift from fixed court dress toward personal fashion choice.
- Example of a commercial specialist gaining unusual proximity to royal image-making.
