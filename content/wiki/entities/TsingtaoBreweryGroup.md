# Tsingtao Brewery Group / 青岛啤酒集团

Tsingtao Brewery Group / 青岛啤酒集团 appears in [[bali-shui-he-shengpeilu-hai-neng-zhuanqian-quechao-weihe-yao-boli-shui-yewu-1005452453]] as the buyer of [[Nestle]]'s China mainland water business in 2020. The source says the transaction included the local production and sales responsibility for [[NestlePureLife|雀巢优活]].

The page matters for the wiki because it shows that Nestle's water withdrawal was staged before the later [[PlatinumEquity]] joint venture. The China sale is an earlier example of shifting a water business toward a more local beverage operator.
