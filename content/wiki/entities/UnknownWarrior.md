# Unknown Warrior

## Overview

The Unknown Warrior is the unidentified First World War serviceman buried in Westminster Abbey in 1920 as a representative focus for Britain's missing dead and bereaved families.

## Current Profile

The episode traces the project to [[DavidRailton]], an army chaplain who proposed bringing an unidentified soldier from the Western Front to London. Four bodies from different battle areas were gathered, one coffin was chosen without revealing its origin, and the remains were transported through France and Britain with military, royal, religious, and popular honors.

The burial's force came from anonymity. Because no family could claim a known biography, any bereaved household could imagine the Warrior as its own missing person. Placement in Westminster Abbey and passage by the newly permanent [[Cenotaph]] combined Christian burial, monarchy, military service, and democratic representation in one national ritual.

## Key Characteristics

- Unidentified British imperial serviceman selected from Western Front remains.
- Proposed by army chaplain David Railton.
- Transported from France to Britain through an elaborate public ceremony.
- Buried in Westminster Abbey in 1920.
- Representative symbol allowing many families to identify with one unknown body.
- Joined sacred burial, national ceremony, monarchy, and mass participation.
