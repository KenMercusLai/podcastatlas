# Blue Origin

Blue Origin appears in [[omission-accomplished-why-the-iran-war-cycle-spins-on-6a6c7475aa1642a30f17f1cb]] as the spaceflight company that carried [[WallyFunk]] to space at age 82. The episode does not analyze Blue Origin's business or technology; it uses the flight as the final act of Funk's lifelong pursuit of space after being excluded from the early astronaut corps.

In this source, Blue Origin matters because private spaceflight becomes a belated recognition channel. [[JeffBezos]]'s invitation gave Funk the trip that public space institutions had denied her in the early space age.

## Connections
- [[WallyFunk]] - passenger whose late flight anchors the source's biography segment.
- [[JeffBezos]] - founder and host who invited Funk as his guest.
