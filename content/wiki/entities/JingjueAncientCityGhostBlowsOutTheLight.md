# 《精绝古城》 / The Ancient City of Jingjue

## Overview

《精绝古城》 is the first-book origin expedition in [[GhostBlowsOutTheLight|《鬼吹灯》]]. Two bounded [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] discussions follow 胡八一 from inherited strange tales, rural youth and military service through his first tomb raid with 王胖子, recruitment by Shirley 杨, and the archaeological party's desert journey into a fictional city built around the historical memory of the [[JingjueKingdom|精绝古国]].

## Current Profile

The novel's first half establishes competence before the desert destination appears. 胡国华's paper-person, mouse and corpse stories transmit the 《十六字阴阳风水秘术》 as family legend; 胡八一's educated-youth and military episodes supply field judgment, endurance and combat experience. His first tomb raid with 王胖子 tests the candle rule against greed, then breaks from a red-haired corpse into a Japanese underground fortress, preserved child bodies and a large animal threat. 大金牙's appraisal shows that survival skill does not equal object expertise, and Shirley 杨's recruitment finally converts backstory into the Jingjue expedition.

The expedition then moves from environmental attrition into a layered underground mystery. Storm, thirst, ants and black snakes first establish that navigation and survival depend on distributed expertise. Murals, eye imagery, the jade eye, the queen's coffin and the ghost cave turn archaeology into a chain of speculative explanations involving imaginary space, ancestry and prophecy. The corpse-fragrance konjac produces the strongest reversal: after one prophecy correctly predicts four entrants, a forged continuation exploits that credibility to make the survivors suspect an “evil ghost” among them. Shirley 杨's photographic expertise exposes the image tampering even though her dreams, ancestry clues and resemblance to the queen made her the most plausible false suspect.

The novel's historical texture remains distinct from historical reconstruction. The episode connects Jingjue to Han records, Niya archaeology and Western Regions geography but also notes that the fictional chronology and supernatural state differ from the real polity. The escape completes the desert adventure while the ancestry and curse questions remain open for [[LonglingMazeGhostBlowsOutTheLight|《龙岭迷窟》]] and the later search for the [[MuchenPearl|雮尘珠]].

## Key Characteristics

- Grounds 胡八一's later competence in family transmission, rural life, military service and a morally compromised first raid.
- Establishes the central trio's complementary functions inside a larger specialist expedition.
- Escalates from desert survival and external creatures to hallucination, mistrust and identity uncertainty.
- Uses tiered architecture to make social hierarchy, information and danger unfold as staged progression.
- Builds its principal deception by letting a true prophecy authenticate a forged continuation.
- Recombines historical Jingjue and Niya material with fictional chronology, geomancy, cult, queen and speculative space.
- Closes the local escape while leaving Shirley 杨's lineage and the ghost-cave curse unresolved.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on two conversational retellings rather than the novel text, a critical edition or independent historical research. Plot details, character functions, chronology, version differences, authorial planning, archaeological comparison, geomancy, botanical effects, imaginary space and curse rules remain source-scoped. The historical [[JingjueKingdom|Jingjue Kingdom]] must not be inferred from the novel's queen, ghost cave, cult or supernatural chronology.

## What Changed

- Added the violated candle rule and appraisal scene as evidence that competence, greed and expertise remain distinct.
