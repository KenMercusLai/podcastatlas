# 《精绝古城》 / The Ancient City of Jingjue

## Overview

《精绝古城》 is the first-book origin expedition in [[GhostBlowsOutTheLight|《鬼吹灯》]]. The bounded [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] discussion follows 胡八一、王胖子、Shirley 杨 and an archaeological party across the desert into a fictional city built around the historical memory of the [[JingjueKingdom|精绝古国]].

## Current Profile

The expedition moves from environmental attrition into a layered underground mystery. Storm, thirst, ants and black snakes first establish that navigation and survival depend on distributed expertise. Murals, eye imagery, the jade eye, the queen's coffin and the ghost cave then turn archaeology into a chain of speculative explanations involving imaginary space, ancestry and prophecy. The corpse-fragrance konjac produces the strongest reversal: after one prophecy correctly predicts four entrants, a forged continuation exploits that credibility to make the survivors suspect an “evil ghost” among them. Shirley 杨's photographic expertise exposes the image tampering even though her dreams, ancestry clues and resemblance to the queen made her the most plausible false suspect.

The novel's historical texture remains distinct from historical reconstruction. The episode connects Jingjue to Han records, Niya archaeology and Western Regions geography but also notes that the fictional chronology and supernatural state differ from the real polity. The escape completes the desert adventure while the ancestry and curse questions remain open for [[LonglingMazeGhostBlowsOutTheLight|《龙岭迷窟》]] and the later search for the [[MuchenPearl|雮尘珠]].

## Key Characteristics

- Establishes the central trio's complementary functions inside a larger specialist expedition.
- Escalates from desert survival and external creatures to hallucination, mistrust and identity uncertainty.
- Uses tiered architecture to make social hierarchy, information and danger unfold as staged progression.
- Builds its principal deception by letting a true prophecy authenticate a forged continuation.
- Recombines historical Jingjue and Niya material with fictional chronology, geomancy, cult, queen and speculative space.
- Closes the local escape while leaving Shirley 杨's lineage and the ghost-cave curse unresolved.

## Qualifications
