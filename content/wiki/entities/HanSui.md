# 韩遂 / Han Sui

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] backfills Han Sui's coerced entry into the Liangzhou rebel field. After [[XianlingQiang|先零羌]] and associated rebels kill [[LingZhengLateHan|伶征]], elevate [[BeigongBoyuLateHan|北宫伯玉]] and [[LiWenhouLateHan|李文侯]], and need Han-side military-administrative skill, they force [[BianZhangLateHan|边章]] and Han Sui to handle military affairs.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-935-ren-huo-yi-kou-qi-nan-cheng-de-shi-ziji-lmy8tyrttv-gjvke7sdi6dx-oopw|Hanji 935]] backfills Han Sui before the later consolidation branch. In this earlier 185 CE stage, he is paired with [[BianZhangLateHan|边章]] as a western rebel actor who defeats [[ZhangWenLateHan|张温]] at Meiyang before [[DongZhuo|董卓]] and right Fufeng-side forces counterattack.

After the defeat, Han Sui and Bian Zhang withdraw toward Yuzhong. The episode's operational point is that the rebel side survives pursuit by cutting [[ZhouShenLateHan|周慎]]'s grain road after Zhou Shen rejects [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]'s proposal to cut theirs first.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-937-zhanshen-zhisi-haoren-zongshi-duozai-duonan-lp7przsmag9ookq7eoisvsmnm5aw|Hanji 937]] supplies Han Sui's pre-coalition consolidation before the later Chen Cang and Guanzhong branches. The episode says Han Sui kills or absorbs [[BianZhangLateHan|边章]], [[BeigongBoyuLateHan|北宫伯玉]], and [[LiWenhouLateHan|李文侯]], gathers a force of more than 100,000, attacks [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]], and draws [[LiXiangruLateHan|李相如]] into rebellion. The source notes a variant in which Bian Zhang dies of illness, but follows the Zizhi Tongjian killing account.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-938-lishi-de-jingxing-ruanruo-zhihui-dailai-quru-lnlnrketgfbadhfjbzdjjtrmqfsh|Hanji 938]] supplies the formation stage after [[GengBiLateHan|耿鄙]]'s Liangzhou command collapses. Han Sui aligns with [[MaTeng|马腾]] and [[WangGuoLateHan|王国]] and helps turn provincial military failure into a wider rebel coalition.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-942-yue-youxiu-de-ren-huode-yue-lei-heli-ma-lkwlrcgnt-kxxc2qqz6z6wkg7e5z|Hanji 942]] backfills Han Sui before the later Guanzhong and Changpingguan branches. After [[WangGuoLateHan|王国]] fails at [[ChenCang|陈仓]] and is removed, Han Sui coerces [[YanZhongLateHan|阎忠]] into nominal leadership of the rebel coalition.

Yan Zhong's quick death leaves Han Sui's side weakening through internal attacks, making this an early source case for [[InterestStructuredAllianceFragility|利益结构式联盟脆弱性]]. The episode also makes Han Sui the military target used by [[JianShuoLateHan|蹇硕]] and the attendants when they try to send [[HeJin|何进]] away from [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-973-changpingguan-zhizhan-shengsi-zhidi-zhanhou-jing-chonggui-yuhao-lgazc9dewfayb8qnkncfkfa12yet|Hanji 973]] supplies the fuller lead-in to Han Sui's 194 CE contact with [[FanChouLateHan|樊稠]]. Han Sui comes from Jincheng with Qiang-Hu cavalry as a mediator between [[MaTeng|马腾]] and [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]], but the episode dramatizes him as being drawn onto Ma Teng's side through drink and shared western ties.

After the defeat at [[ChangpingguanBattleLateHan|长平观之战]], Han Sui withdraws, takes imperial horses, and later persuades Fan Chou not to treat the campaign as private hatred. The episode also preserves the 安乡将军 / 安羌将军 title variant and favors 安羌 because it better matches Ma Teng's 安狄 title.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]] adds the pre-Fan-Chou setup. Han Sui joins [[MaTeng|马腾]] against [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] after Ma Teng's grain-road conflict, giving the western pressure a broader coalition form. The episode says [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]] also supports Ma Teng with Shu troops and Chang'an-side sons, but Li Jue detects the plot and destroys the internal link.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-984-liangzhouxi-junfa-neidou-ta-weihe-di-yige-daoxia-lurb2qfzsa7e-owh-dl-m-sccze5|Hanji 984]] backfills Han Sui's earlier contact with [[FanChouLateHan|樊稠]]. After Fan Chou defeats and pursues [[MaTeng|马腾]] and Han Sui toward [[ChenCang|陈仓]], Han Sui frames the fight as public business rather than private enmity and invokes shared hometown ties. [[LiLiLateHan|李利]] reports the friendly conversation to [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]], turning ambiguous field courtesy into evidence inside [[MilitaryPrestigeThreatPurge|军功声望威胁清洗]].

韩遂 / Han Sui enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] as one of the strongest western leaders in [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]], paired with [[MaTeng|马腾]] in [[XunYu|荀彧]]'s assessment for [[CaoCao|曹操]]. The episode treats him as a major self-preserving regional actor rather than as a fully committed member of a unified western bloc.

[[ZhongYao|钟繇]]'s letters from [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] are said to persuade Han Sui, Ma Teng, and related forces to submit to the Han court and send sons to the court. In the source's strategic sequence, this helps Cao Cao reduce western uncertainty before concentrating on [[LyuBu|吕布]] and the larger [[YuanShao|袁绍]] problem.
