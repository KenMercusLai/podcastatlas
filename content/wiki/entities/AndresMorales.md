# Andres Morales

## Overview
Andres Morales is the creative technologist and [[RedMage]] founder interviewed in [[ep-27-ai-and-the-creative-arts-innovation-or-appropriation|Data Science With Sam EP27]]. The source places his work across technology, artists, nonprofits, underrepresented communities, and Fort Collins-area conversations about AI and creative practice.

## Current Profile
Andres takes a creator-centered but not categorically anti-AI position. He supports compensation when non-public-domain creative work trains models, treats process and lived experience as part of artistic value, resists transferring human agency to model outputs, and favors systems in which people meaningfully lead. His strongest positive example uses sensors and machine learning to turn dancers' movement into music while keeping human performers in control.

## Key Characteristics
- Creative technologist connecting AI questions to the working conditions of artists and community organizations.
- Founder of [[RedMage]] and participant in Fort Collins-area creative and AI initiatives.
- Advocate for creator compensation when protected work is used in AI training.
- Treats lived experience, cultural perspective, process, and accountability as essential parts of creativity.
- Distinguishes meaningful human authority from merely placing a nominal reviewer in an AI workflow.
- Supports human-led experimentation where machine learning expands expression without replacing the artist.
