# 郑朱 / Zheng Zhu

郑朱 / Zheng Zhu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-1-loxyybm3njrtnudydafwzu9jp9jz]] as the [[ZhaoState|赵国]] minister sent by [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] to [[Xianyang|咸阳]] during the [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] prelude. His mission follows the direct-peace path associated with [[LouChang|娄昌]], against [[YuQing|虞卿]]'s advice to rebuild [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] support first.

The episode's point is that Zheng Zhu's reception becomes useful to [[QinState|秦国]] even without producing peace. Qin treats him with visible respect, making other states believe Zhao is already negotiating with Qin and therefore no longer a reliable coalition object. Qin then avoids real settlement, leaving Zhao isolated.
