# 叶律 / Ye Lu

## Overview
叶律 is the [[Mureka]] product lead interviewed in [[shuo-de-hao-ting-ep56-dang-ai-xiege-buzai-shi-jishu-qiguan-women-haiyao-taolun-shenme-6c1b89cdfc157b23f26fac509d36d7fc]] about AI music generation, workflow integration, copyright checks, and settlement questions.

## Current Profile
In the episode, 叶律 argues that the basic listenable-song threshold for [[GenerativeAIMusic]] has largely been crossed for ordinary listeners, while professional standards still reveal gaps. His larger position is pragmatic: AI music should be understood as a creative tool like earlier production technologies, increasingly embedded in musician workflow, potentially shaped by skills or agents, and constrained by copyright detection and unresolved payment rules.

## Key Characteristics
- He represents the generation-product side of the episode through [[Mureka]].
- He treats listenable generation as solved for ordinary listeners but not equivalent to a professional finished-song standard.
- He frames AI as an assistive creative tool that can carry personal habits, voice, and style into reusable assets.
- He is skeptical that AI-generated music should automatically receive the same payout as human-created music.
