# 陈贺 / Chen He

陈贺 / Chen He enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-167-jianzheng-xiangyu-zuizhong-zhizhan-2-li14pizmfrzhpg8m5bmxdx8ibw7p|Hanji 167 part 2]] as the right-side commander in [[HanXin|韩信]]'s first line at the [[GaixiaBattle|垓下之战]]. The source pairs him with [[KongXi|孔熙]], who holds the left.

The episode does not develop Chen He as an independent character. His importance lies in the movement of Han Xin's wings: while Xiang Yu's main force drives through the center, Chen He and Kong Xi keep inserting toward Chu's rear. This helps turn Xiang Yu's breakthrough into overextension under [[LayeredDepthDefense|纵深分层防御]].
