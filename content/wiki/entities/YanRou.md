# 阎柔 / Yan Rou

阎柔 / Yan Rou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] as a frontier actor with enough prestige to be selected as Wuhuan sima. The episode says he gathers several tens of thousands of Hu and Han people after [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] is killed.

Yan Rou's concrete battlefield role is defeating [[ZhouDanLateHan|周丹]], the Yuyang taishou sent by [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]]. The victory helps open the larger anti-Gongsun coalition with [[XianyuFu|鲜于辅]], [[Wuhuan|乌桓]], [[Xianbei|鲜卑]], [[LiuHeLateHan|刘和]], and [[YuanShao|袁绍]].
