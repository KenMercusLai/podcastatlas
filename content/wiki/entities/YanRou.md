# 阎柔 / Yan Rou

## Overview
阎柔 / Yan Rou is a Youzhou frontier actor whose influence rests on mixed Hu-Han mobilization and Wuhuan connection. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] introduces him as a figure chosen as Wuhuan sima after [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]]'s death. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1032-duidai-pantu-caocao-weihe-renci-lmrr3c2f2dayectlwndt4p-katuy|Hanji 1032]] then shows [[YuanShao|袁绍]] treating him favorably as part of the post-[[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] northern settlement.

## Current Profile
Yan Rou currently bridges the anti-Gongsun coalition and Yuan Shao's Wuhuan-facing rear policy. First, he gathers tens of thousands of Hu and Han people and defeats [[ZhouDanLateHan|周丹]], weakening Gongsun Zan's Youzhou position. Later, after Gongsun Zan's fall, Yuan Shao keeps Yan Rou inside the frontier settlement alongside [[TadunLateHan|蹋顿]] and other Wuhuan leaders.

## Key Characteristics
- Frontier mediator selected as Wuhuan sima in the anti-Gongsun sequence.
- Capable of mobilizing mixed Hu and Han forces at scale.
- Battlefield actor who defeats Gongsun Zan's Yuyang appointee Zhou Dan.
- Partner figure to [[XianyuFu|鲜于辅]] in the Youzhou backlash after Liu Yu's killing.
- Useful to Yuan Shao's postwar Wuhuan and Youzhou stabilization after Gongsun Zan's defeat.
