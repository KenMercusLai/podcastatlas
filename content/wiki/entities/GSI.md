# GSI

GSI is the company in [[a-pro-worker-experiment-in-private-equity]] that does emergency landslide repairs, rockfall mitigation, and related stabilization work. [[KKR]] bought the company in 2018 and used it as a more explicit version of [[PrivateEquityWorkerOwnership]] than the earlier [[CapitalSafety]] rollout.

Unlike Capital Safety workers, GSI workers were told about ownership from the beginning. [[MikePavelko]] says that made employees feel more involved and gave them a reason to care about company targets, even though the equity did not include voting power and might not stay with employees who left.

The episode says KKR announced GSI's sale in October 2024 and that Mike received a large payout. [[PeteStavros]] also says GSI's annual quit rate fell from about half the workforce to around 15% over five years, making GSI the source's clearest case for connecting employee ownership to [[EmployeeRetentionEconomics]].
