# 刘子明 / Liu Ziming (sports medicine doctor)

## Overview
刘子明 / Liu Ziming is a source-scoped sports-medicine doctor in the VOL.212 [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode on safe exercise, joint protection, and injury prevention for sedentary workers.

## Current Profile
The source identifies 刘子明 as a doctor from [[PekingUniversityThirdHospital|北医三院]] sports medicine, with a stated focus involving lower-limb, knee, and hip joint directions. His role in the episode is practical sports-medicine translation: he helps ordinary listeners understand why safe training depends on muscle capacity, progressive loading, appropriate exercise choice, and symptom escalation rather than either avoiding movement or copying professional athletes.

This page is disambiguated from [[LiuZiming|Liu Ziming / 刘子鸣]], the existing AI researcher page. The source names this doctor as 刘子明 and gives a sports-medicine context, so the page uses the stable suffix `SportsMedicine`.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains exercise safety from a sports-medicine and lower-limb joint perspective.
- Treats strength training as a practical foundation for knee, hip, and joint protection.
- Emphasizes gradual progression for running, HYROX-style functional fitness, weekend exercise, and gym training.
- Uses ordinary-worker scenarios such as long sitting, weekend workouts, and beginner gym routines rather than elite-athlete goals.
- Keeps pain, swelling, locking, acute injury, kinesiology tape, braces, and rehabilitation tools inside professional judgment boundaries.
