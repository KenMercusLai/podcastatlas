## Overview

Foundation / 基地 is [[IsaacAsimov|Isaac Asimov]]'s science-fiction sequence, discussed as a direct transformation of Roman imperial decline into a galactic future.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Foundation as the clearest “Rome in space” case in its comparison set. A decaying [[GalacticEmpire]] centered on Trantor is read through Edward Gibbon, while Hari Seldon's psychohistory turns the hope of learning laws from history into a mathematical technology for shortening the coming dark age. The Mule then demonstrates the model's boundary: an anomalous charismatic conqueror can overturn a large-scale forecast.

## Key Characteristics

- Galactic-imperial decline explicitly associated with Gibbon's Rome.
- Psychohistory as mathematical, population-scale historical prediction.
- The Foundation as an institution designed to preserve knowledge through collapse.
- The Mule as a charismatic anomaly that disrupts deterministic planning.

## Evidence

- Roman-decline model: [[412-romans-in-space-star-wars-dune-and-beyond-glt3832078172]] says the sequence was directly inspired by Gibbon and begins near the Galactic Empire's collapse.
- Prediction and preservation: [[412-romans-in-space-star-wars-dune-and-beyond-glt3832078172]] describes Seldon's forecast and the Foundation's role as a beacon through the coming darkness.
- Model failure: [[412-romans-in-space-star-wars-dune-and-beyond-glt3832078172]] uses the Mule's conquest to show how an unforeseen individual can defeat a grand historical model.

## Qualifications

This page records the episode's historical reading rather than a complete account of the novels. The Mule–Muhammad comparison and the discussion of older historiography about Islam remain the hosts' interpretations.
