# 唐代长安 / Tang Chang'an

唐代长安 / Tang Chang'an is the capital-space node in [[ep264-tafang-tangshi-zhilu-shengtang-qixiang-shaonianxin-loo2dfuxffizpbl4si5nwcixnngi]]. The episode presents it as a cosmopolitan metropolis, poetry circulation center, court-performance space, Buddhist public-space network, and later a lost city whose surface architecture largely disappeared after late-Tang destruction.

The source's Chang'an branch is not only nostalgic. [[AiJiangtao|艾江涛]] uses sites such as Daming Palace, Qujiang, Qinglong Temple, Da Ci'en Temple, Xiangji Temple, Caotang Temple, surviving Tang towers, and the Beilin stele bearing [[WangWei|王维]]'s name to show how material traces and poems can still be read together.
