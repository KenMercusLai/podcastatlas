# 朱良 / Zhu Liang (Luoyang magistrate, late Han)

## Overview

Zhu Liang is the Luoyang magistrate in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-815-kebei-gudai-zhongchen-de-jiafeng-zhishang-llkhbxo-63pjw-gk9pihnbfr94v|Hanji 815]] who impeaches [[PangCanLateHan|Pang Can]] after Pang's household murder scandal.

## Current Profile

Zhu's role is narrow but structurally important. When Pang Can's current wife kills the son of Pang's former wife, Zhu uses the case as an impeachment basis. That turns private household disorder into an actionable political vulnerability for a top minister already under slander pressure.

The page name uses a semantic suffix because this 朱良 is distinct from [[ZhuLiangLateHan|祝良]], the Jiaozhi official already represented elsewhere in the wiki.

## Key Characteristics

- Operates as Luoyang magistrate, giving him jurisdictional proximity to elite household scandal in the capital region.
- Converts a domestic murder case into a formal attack on a sitting taiwei.
- Functions as the prosecutorial hinge in Pang Can's dismissal rather than as an independently developed policy actor.
- Requires disambiguation from the existing Zhu Liang / 祝良 page because the normalized romanization collides while the Chinese names and contexts differ.
