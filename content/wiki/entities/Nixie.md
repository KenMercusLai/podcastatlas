# Nixie

## Overview
Nixie is the organic sparkling water and sparkling soda company founded by [[NicoleBernardDawes]] after [[LateJulySnacks]], introduced in [[late-july-snacks-nicole-bernard-dawes-crackers-and-cookies-were-failing-tortilla-chips-saved-them-97049a31-9144-42f5-856c-9590c5e6beb4]]. It extends Nicole's taste-first organic CPG playbook from snacks into beverages.

## Current Profile
The episode says Nicole started Nixie after seeing few organic beverage options and many products with sugar or plastic-packaging drawbacks. Nixie uses organic flavors in sparkling water and later sparkling soda, with no calories and no sugar. Nicole initially self-funded the company so she could learn velocity and bestsellers before considering outside investors.

## Key Characteristics
- Beverage brand applying an organic ingredient standard to sparkling water and soda.
- Taste-first CPG extension rather than a purely mission-led or wellness-only product.
- Self-funded early stage used to learn velocity before taking outside capital.
- Launch path affected by COVID-era retail disruption.
