# Zhejiang University School of Medicine Second Affiliated Hospital / 浙江大学医学院附属第二医院

## Overview
Zhejiang University School of Medicine Second Affiliated Hospital / 浙江大学医学院附属第二医院 appears in [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] as the affiliation of vascular surgeons [[ChenBinVascularSurgery|陈斌]] and [[FengYanfeiVascularSurgery|冯燕飞]], endocrinologist [[ZhengChaoEndocrinologist|郑超]], and neurosurgeon [[YanFengNeurosurgeon|严锋]]. The sources connect the hospital to public education on travel-related venous thrombosis, multidisciplinary weight management, and emergency-to-specialty diagnostic collaboration.

## Current Profile
Within the current wiki evidence, the hospital is still represented through named clinical programs and guests rather than a comprehensive institutional profile. VOL.204 supplies its vascular-surgery branch through [[EconomyClassSyndromeTravelThrombosis|economy-class syndrome]], thrombosis prevention, pulmonary-embolism risk, anticoagulation, and emergency routing. VOL.199 adds an endocrinology and weight-management branch: 郑超 is presented as leading endocrinology and the weight-management center, whose “8+X” model coordinates cause assessment, nutrition, exercise rehabilitation, mental health, selected procedures or surgery, and digital follow-up. VOL.195 adds a neurosurgical and acute-care collaboration branch through Yan Feng's discussion of neurological assessment, diagnostic revision, operative bleeding risk, and family communication.

## Key Characteristics
- Named as the hospital affiliation of vascular-surgery clinicians in VOL.204 and an endocrinology and weight-management leader in VOL.199.

- Presented as operating a multidisciplinary “8+X” weight-management pathway rather than a nutrition-only service.

- Extends the wiki's [[ZhejiangUniversity|Zhejiang University / 浙江大学]] cluster into affiliated-hospital clinical education and coordinated care.
