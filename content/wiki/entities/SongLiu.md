# 宋刘 / Song Liu

宋刘 / Song Liu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-4-jiuquangui-zhangliang-ruhe-zai-liubang-shouxia-anshen-lp6qwg3ma5hdy15g4box7pe23jav|Qinji 129-4]] as a [[ChenSheng|陈胜]]-sent commander who had taken [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]] and moved toward Wu Pass before Chen Sheng's death. Once Chen Sheng's death spreads, the episode says morale collapses, Nanyang returns to Qin, and Song Liu surrenders.

[[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] responds by ordering Song Liu torn apart by chariots. The source treats the punishment as politically self-defeating: if a surrenderer is publicly killed, later rebels have less reason to surrender and more reason to fight on.

Song Liu therefore anchors [[PunitiveSurrenderDeterrence|杀降纳降反噬]] inside the Qin-collapse branch. His page records the episode's policy reading, not an independent claim that Qin's later failures can be reduced to this single execution.
