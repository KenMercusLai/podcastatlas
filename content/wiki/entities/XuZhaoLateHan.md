# 徐昭 / Xu Zhao (late Han)

## Overview
徐昭 / Xu Zhao enters the wiki through Hanji 1039 as a local actor whom Sun Ce chooses not to attack because Xu Zhao's protection of others reveals useful loyalty.

## Current Profile
The current evidence makes Xu Zhao a selective-leniency test. He shelters a former superior and later receives Yan Baihu after Yan Baihu flees, but [[SunCe|孙策]] refuses to attack him because the same conduct marks him as loyal to old relationships and therefore politically legible.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides shelter to a threatened old superior, making loyalty rather than opportunism his defining source trait.
- Later receives Yan Baihu after Sun Ce defeats Yan Baihu.
- Is proposed as an attack target by Sun Ce's camp but spared by Sun Ce.
- Qualifies Sun Ce's local-strongman suppression by showing that loyal conduct can change the treatment of a local actor.

## Qualifications
The page is based on one episode and does not yet settle whether Xu Zhao is best understood as a local strongman, refuge provider, or moral exemplar. Current use should stay tied to Sun Ce's selective-leniency judgment.
