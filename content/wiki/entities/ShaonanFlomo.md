## Overview
少楠 is presented as a co-founder of [[Flomo]] and the guest in a [[ZhixingXiaojiuguan|知行小酒馆]] conversation about AI, information anxiety, personal records, learning, and finite attention.

## Current Profile
In the bounded episode, 少楠 treats information anxiety primarily as a response to uncertainty and abundant choice rather than a simple shortage of answers. His current practice uses AI selectively: he experiments with products and explanations, writes important prose himself before seeking feedback, records personally meaningful changes rather than generic excerpts, and asks AI to widen or filter his information diet.
