# Jiang Hong / 江宏

## Overview
[[JiangHongAnimalVolunteer|Jiang Hong / 江宏]] is an animal-welfare volunteer interviewed in the 旺旺 episode about mainland platform filtering, attempted public projections, and official discouragement of offline animal-protection expression.

## Current Profile
The source uses Jiang Hong as a mainland frontline voice. He describes posting around [[Xiaohongshu]] and Douyin review systems, helping organize a local projection campaign after seeing actions in other cities, accepting softer wording and cartoon imagery to satisfy venues, and then seeing the display quickly removed before police contact and station phone calls discouraged further spread. His role grounds [[AnimalProtectionCodedExpression]] and the public-space side of [[ChinaAnimalProtectionLawGap]].

## Key Characteristics
- He is presented as a mainland animal-welfare volunteer rather than a formal institutional spokesperson.
- His testimony shows platform filtering moving supporters toward euphemism, homophones, images, and indirect wording.
- His projection attempt shows commercial venues avoiding direct animal-cruelty language even after the message was softened.
- His account links online moderation with offline police and community pressure.

## Evidence
- Platform and projection account: [[liulangquan-wangwang-zhisi-he-yichang-kuajing-dongbao-shengyuan]] reports Jiang Hong's daily struggle with platform review, group organizing, venue negotiation, takedown, and police contact.
- Advocacy constraint: [[liulangquan-wangwang-zhisi-he-yichang-kuajing-dongbao-shengyuan]] uses his experience to show that even mild "care for small animals" language could become controlled in public space.
