# Patek Philippe

Patek Philippe appears in [[rolex-1]] as a high-end watch-house comparison for [[Rolex]]. The hosts contrast Rolex's scale and average price with brands such as Patek Philippe, [[AudemarsPiguet]], and [[VacheronConstantin]], which sell fewer watches at much higher average prices.

The comparison helps the wiki distinguish Rolex from artisanal high luxury. Rolex can still be luxury, but its strategic shape is closer to [[HighVolumeLuxuryOperator]]: industrial scale, reliable product continuity, brand power, and controlled access.
