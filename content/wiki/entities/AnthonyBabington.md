# Anthony Babington

Anthony Babington appears in [[696-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-killing-the-queen-of-scots-part-6-glt2334035496]] as the Catholic conspirator whose plan gives the [[BabingtonPlot]] its name. The episode says his proposal combined six men to kill [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]], the release of [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]], a domestic Catholic rising, foreign invasion, and restoration of Catholicism in [[England]].

Babington's correspondence reaches Mary through [[GilbertGifford]]'s controlled beer-barrel channel. Once Mary's reply is decrypted by [[ThomasPhillips]], Babington becomes part of the evidentiary chain that lets [[FrancisWalsingham]] and [[WilliamCecil|William Cecil / Lord Burleigh]] move against Mary.

Babington is arrested after Mary's arrest and is executed on 20 September 1586. The episode notes that he and other conspirators confessed to the release, invasion, and Catholic restoration plan while denying Elizabeth's murder plan.
