# Cabbage Labs

Cabbage Labs is the company behind [[CabDab]], introduced by [[MarkSokolowski]] in [[advice-line-with-bobbi-brown-of-jones-road-beauty-september-2025-427590c2-11bc-43fa-9c92-d1eccf05842a]]. The source describes it as a physician-led consumer health business trying to turn one doctor's trusted patient sampling into a broader but still credible growth path.

The episode says the business has been profitable since day one, has sold or distributed about 30,000 tubes, and has another 10,000 tubes ordered. Its next bottleneck is not product existence but distribution: the company needs PR, local evangelism, professional-channel trial, and cheaper sampling mechanics.
