# 赵佗 / Zhao Tuo

赵佗 / Zhao Tuo enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-184-lishi-kongbupian-liubang-de-pengshi-roujiang-2-liebzrdrvo1okonpdjvrjatuwx9i|Hanji 184]] as the former Qin [[NanhaiCommandery|南海郡]] governor who builds [[Nanyue|南越]] after Qin's collapse. The source says he breaks free after 207 BCE, calls himself Yue king in 203 BCE, and controls a large southern field tied to Nanhai, [[GuilinCommandery|桂林郡]], and [[XiangCommandery|象郡]].

For this episode, Zhao Tuo matters less as an internal biography than as the ruler Liu Bang chooses to recognize after stabilizing the Han interior. [[LiuBang|刘邦]] names him Nanyue king and sends [[LuJia|陆贾]] with seal and tally, hoping to bring the southern polity into Han order without immediate war.

Zhao Tuo therefore turns [[Nanyue|南越]] from an outside refuge mentioned in earlier Hanji 170 material into a concrete frontier-diplomacy problem. The episode frames the relationship as "先礼后兵": recognition is a first move toward submission and border quiet, not a sign that the southern frontier is irrelevant.
