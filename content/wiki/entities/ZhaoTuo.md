# 赵佗 / Zhao Tuo

赵佗 / Zhao Tuo enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-184-lishi-kongbupian-liubang-de-pengshi-roujiang-2-liebzrdrvo1okonpdjvrjatuwx9i|Hanji 184]] as the former Qin [[NanhaiCommandery|南海郡]] governor who builds [[Nanyue|南越]] after Qin's collapse. The source says he breaks free after 207 BCE, calls himself Yue king in 203 BCE, and controls a large southern field tied to Nanhai, [[GuilinCommandery|桂林郡]], and [[XiangCommandery|象郡]].

For this episode, Zhao Tuo matters less as an internal biography than as the ruler Liu Bang chooses to recognize after stabilizing the Han interior. [[LiuBang|刘邦]] names him Nanyue king and sends [[LuJia|陆贾]] with seal and tally, hoping to bring the southern polity into Han order without immediate war.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-185-yi-zhang-li-zui-shoufu-zhongguo-wanli-jiangtu-lps0moshdfvosnyhtdhrkdvswibh|Hanji 185]] supplies the backstory behind that frontier problem. [[RenXiao|任嚣]] tells Zhao Tuo to block the Five Ridges routes and use [[NanhaiCommandery|南海郡]]'s remoteness after the Qin center begins to collapse. After Ren Xiao dies, Zhao Tuo seals passes, removes Qin-appointed officials, installs trusted subordinates, takes [[GuilinCommandery|桂林郡]] and [[XiangCommandery|象郡]], and calls himself Nanyue Wuwang.

The same episode completes the [[LuJia|陆贾]] mission. Zhao Tuo begins the meeting in a deliberately non-Han posture, but Lu Jia forces the issue by invoking Zhao's Central Plains origin, kin, ancestral graves, and the scale of Liu Bang's victory. Zhao Tuo then tests his own rank against [[XiaoHe|萧何]], [[CaoShen|曹参]], [[HanXin|韩信]], and Liu Bang; Lu Jia grants talent similarity with the ministers but denies imperial equivalence.

Zhao Tuo therefore turns [[Nanyue|南越]] from an outside refuge mentioned in earlier Hanji 170 material into a concrete frontier-diplomacy problem. The completed case shows [[RecognitionBackedFrontierDiplomacy|册封威慑式边疆外交]]: Zhao Tuo accepts the Han title and submission while retaining enough self-regard to joke that he might have rivaled Liu Bang had he stayed in the Central Plains.
