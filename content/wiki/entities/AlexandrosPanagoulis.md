# Alexandros Panagoulis / 亚历山德罗斯·帕纳古利斯

Alexandros Panagoulis / 亚历山德罗斯·帕纳古利斯, called 阿里克斯 in [[77-ta-shi-ziyou-de-yegou-ta-shi-falaqi-685710187]], is presented as the Greek resistance figure who became [[OrianaFallaci|Oriana Fallaci]]'s central love. The episode frames him as a person who fought the Greek military dictatorship and whose courage made him spiritually legible to Fallaci.

The source explicitly pushes back against reducing Fallaci to a romance plot. Panagoulis matters because the relationship is interpreted as two lonely, freedom-oriented people recognizing the same refusal of domination. His 1976 death in a car crash is source-presented through Fallaci's conviction that it was an assassination.

The episode's remembered "不" on a Greek hillside turns Panagoulis into a concrete image for [[FreedomAsDignity]] and [[PrincipledDisobedience]]: freedom is not only a political doctrine, but the capacity to refuse a power that wants silence.
