# Dunlop Pneumatic Tyre Company

Dunlop Pneumatic Tyre Company appears in [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]] as a key asset inside the late-1890s bicycle-market frenzy. The episode says capital operators packaged and listed bicycle-related companies, including Dunlop-linked tire assets, during a period when any bicycle or cycle label could draw investor excitement.

The company's wiki role is to ground [[BicycleCapitalBubble]] in a real component business. Tires genuinely improved [[BicycleProductEvolution]], but the surrounding financial packaging still ran ahead of durable demand and helped set up the later bust.
