# Edmond Halley / 哈雷

Edmond Halley appears in [[26-niudun-yu-weibi-zhizaozhe-keneng-shi-sineipu-de-yuanxing-580065317]] as the catalyst who pushed [[IsaacNewton|Isaac Newton / 牛顿]] from private calculation toward publication. The episode has Halley ask what orbit would follow from an inverse-square attraction, then recognize the importance of Newton's answer and protect Newton's priority at the [[RoyalSociety|Royal Society]].

Halley's role supports [[ScientificRevolutionSocialInfrastructure]]: even Newton's most famous theoretical system needed a questioner, courier, editor, advocate, and institution-facing sponsor before it became a public scientific event.
