# “意味杭之：馆藏明清古籍展” / Yiwei Hangzhi Exhibition

## Overview
“意味杭之：馆藏明清古籍展” is the [[HangzhouMuseum|杭州博物馆]] exhibition discussed in the [[BowuzhiPodcast|博物志]] episode [[246-hangzhou-bowuguan-yiwei-hangzhi-haojiu-mei-kan-guo-zheme-tuijian-de-zhanlan-le-8217911423-973793]].

## Current Profile
The exhibition is presented as a compact but unusually legible ancient-books show. Its value lies in explaining what ancient books are, how their material form works, why Ming and Qing printed books changed, and how Hangzhou's local publishing culture and West Lake writing made books part of city memory.

## Key Characteristics
- Organized around four sections: initial recognition of ancient books, book-form art, Ming-Qing printing characteristics, and Hangzhou local publishing and narration.
- Uses modern interpretive devices such as red-line text highlights, white-language explanation boxes, glass labels, and short videos.
- Treats ancient books as both textual content and physical objects.
- Connects object display to Hangzhou's publishing, travel-writing, and local-document traditions.
- Makes rare holdings usable for ordinary visitors rather than only impressive to specialists.
