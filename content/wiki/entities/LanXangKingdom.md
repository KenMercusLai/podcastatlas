# Lan Xang Kingdom / 澜沧王国

The Lan Xang Kingdom / 澜沧王国 is mentioned in [[ep267-tafang-meigonghe-yuji-hunfeiyi-yu-shuili-diguo-lkq2vxaatn4wxue7y5q1fc3-8yfk]] as part of the Lao historical and environmental imagination around the [[MekongRiver|湄公河]]. The episode links place names, natural resources, and early political identities to a landscape where rivers, forests, animals, temples, and village ritual remain close to everyday life.

The source does not give a detailed political history of Lan Xang. Its wiki role is therefore bounded: it supports the episode's claim that [[Laos|老挝]] should be read through local riverine history and cultural memory rather than only through contemporary tourism or infrastructure.
