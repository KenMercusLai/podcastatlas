# Ernst Röhm

## Overview

Ernst Röhm was the Nazi stormtrooper leader whose command of the mass [[Sturmabteilung|SA]], demand for a “second revolution,” and ambition to absorb the professional army made him the central target of the [[NightOfTheLongKnives|Night of the Long Knives]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Röhm as a First World War veteran who romanticized trench comradeship, street fighting, and revolutionary activism. His personal standing with Hitler and loyalty among stormtroopers protected him for a time despite hostility elsewhere in the Nazi movement.

By 1933–34, the SA's claimed membership dwarfed the Versailles-limited army. Röhm argued that the Nazi revolution had lost momentum and proposed making the SA Germany's principal defense force, leaving the traditional army to provide training. Those demands threatened Hitler's authority and his need for army support.

The episode rejects the later claim that Röhm was actually preparing a coup. Hitler arrested him at Bad Wiessee, delayed his execution, then authorized SS men to kill him after Röhm refused an invitation to take his own life.

## Key Characteristics

- He built authority through SA loyalty and a culture of militant comradeship.
- He represented the revolutionary, street-fighting wing of Nazism.
- His “second revolution” demand challenged the post-1933 settlement.
- His defense-force proposal directly threatened the professional army.
- His conflict with Hitler was real even though the coup allegation was false.
- His arrest and killing symbolized the destruction of autonomous SA power.

## Qualifications

This is a source-bounded profile rather than a full biography of Röhm or the SA. Membership figures, reported insults toward Hitler, the alleged French payment, dialogue at arrest, and execution detail remain episode-attributed. Rejecting the coup allegation does not erase the SA's coercion, violence, or genuine institutional challenge.

- Distinguished revolutionary challenge from fabricated coup evidence.
