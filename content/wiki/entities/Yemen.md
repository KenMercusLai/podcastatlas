# Yemen

## Overview

Yemen appears in the wiki as the conflict setting in which [[Houthis|Houthi]] power, fragmented anti-Houthi forces, Saudi intervention, humanitarian devastation, and thin diplomatic settlements interact.

## Current Profile

[[omission-accomplished-why-the-iran-war-cycle-spins-on-6a6c7475aa1642a30f17f1cb]] introduced Yemen indirectly as an unresolved Saudi-Houthi file where broad agreement language left blockades and infrastructure attacks unsettled. [[saudi-under-siege-houthis-attack-airport-cc6579d3f0d15fe71826fc6b995c02cc]] adds the underlying war trajectory: the Houthis seized much of the country in 2014, the Saudi-led intervention failed to reverse that outcome, and a 2022 ceasefire froze rather than resolved front lines before renewed 2026 fighting returned to stalemate.

The current synthesis is that Yemen's conflict exports regional costs while remaining internally catastrophic. Fragmented Saudi-backed allies and improved Houthi strike capacity make decisive military resolution unlikely, yet negotiation faces disputes over economic benefits and airspace restrictions. Meanwhile, most Yemenis are described as poor and roughly two-thirds as dependent on aid.

## Key Characteristics

- Civil-war theater shaped by Houthi territorial control and Saudi-led intervention.
- Humanitarian crisis in which poverty and aid dependence persist after more than a decade of war.
- Example of a ceasefire freezing front lines without settling the political and security conflict.
- Setting where divided anti-Houthi forces weaken the prospect of decisive counteroffensives.
- Source of cross-border airport, energy, aviation, and investor-confidence risk for Saudi Arabia.
