## Overview

Yang Bing was a late-Han minister and son of [[YangZhenLateHan|杨震]] whose current wiki role now begins with a failed 159 CE enforcement move: as Henan Yin, he asks to bring [[ShanKuangLateHan|单匡]] to Luoyang for confrontation after [[WeiYuLateHan|魏宇]] exposes corruption and witness suppression, but he is punished instead. Later sources show a restored Yang Bing turning that same public-office discipline into accepted eunuch-linked office review, appointment restraint, [[HouLanLateHan|侯览]] impeachment, clean compensation, and "three refusals" integrity.

## Current Profile

The earliest current Yang Bing layer is a failed Henan Yin enforcement move in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-851-hanhuandi-weihe-zhuding-yichou-wannian-ltgm1ix6cgqb-kqo9bztfrzjgeun|Hanji 851]]. He acts after [[FifthZhongLateHan|第五种]] and Wei Yu expose Shan Kuang's corruption and Shan Kuang hires [[RenFangLateHan|任芳]] against Wei Yu. Yang Bing asks that Shan Kuang be sent to Luoyang for confrontation, but the court punishes Yang Bing with left-workshop labor instead of confronting the protected offender.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-858-weishenme-quan-ni-yiding-yao-huanwei-sikao-lobr-xznqo-ctjpc-ytpstubmahl|Hanji 858]] later shows him restored as taiwei and paired with [[ZhouJingLateHan|周景]]. Together they turn anti-eunuch criticism into a concrete review of eunuch relatives, guests, and unqualified officeholders through named reporting channels, after which Yang Bing impeaches more than fifty officials.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-863-yi-ba-huo-shaochu-de-zhandouli-ljpfmj8pphunruioce64ywjk7yhb|Hanji 863]] shows Yang Bing applying the same office discipline to casual 郎 appointments during Emperor Huan's southern tour. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-865-zhichang-boss-ye-rongyi-bei-zhezhong-ren-beici-ljfhfevljqnx6zceakjfbw5gwfma|Hanji 865]] gives his strongest successful case: he uses [[HouCanLateHan|侯参]]'s corruption to pressure Hou Lan and defends Three Excellencies oversight of inner-court eunuchs through precedent. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-866-zhenzheng-huomingbai-de-ren-dongde-sanbuzhan-lhhmmyjfo6mkf-qs6bhhzu8hhln6|Hanji 866]] then makes the personal discipline behind that public role explicit through clean compensation, gift refusal, anti-corruption action, and restraint from wine, sexual entanglement, and money.

## Key Characteristics

- Henan Yin punished after seeking formal confrontation in Shan Kuang's witness-suppression case.
- Taiwei who later converts anti-eunuch criticism into an accepted review of eunuch-linked and unqualified officeholders.
- Appointment-restraint voice who treats casual 郎 grants as a palace-security and local-governance risk.
- Impeachment actor who turns Hou Can's corruption into a wider attack on Hou Lan's palace access.
- Defender of senior-minister jurisdiction over inner-court eunuchs through precedent-backed argument.
- Late-Han senior minister whose public credibility rests on clean-family reputation and personal restraint.
- Model of clean compensation, gift refusal, and "three refusals" toward wine, sexual entanglement, and money.

## Qualifications

Yang Bing is disambiguated from [[YangBing|杨冰 / Yang Bing]], the modern Hupu and Dewu founder. Hanji 851 creates an earlier punishment layer, not a contradiction of later restoration: the current synthesis treats the later taiwei, southern-tour, and Hou Lan cases as subsequent or restored roles after the Shan Kuang reversal. Hanji 858 proves a bounded cleanup order, not a permanent purge of eunuch-linked appointments. Hanji 865 remains an anti-Hou Lan success, but later source notes may still show Hou Lan or his network active.

## What Changed

- Hanji 851 adds Yang Bing's earlier punishment for trying to confront Shan Kuang in Luoyang.
- The page now treats later Yang Bing successes as restorations after a prior enforcement reversal, not as evidence that he was always institutionally protected.
- Hanji 858, Hanji 863, Hanji 865, and Hanji 866 remain later layers of executable review, appointment restraint, jurisdictional impeachment, and clean-office discipline.
