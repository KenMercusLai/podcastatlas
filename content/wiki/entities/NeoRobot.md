# Neo Robot

## Overview
Neo is 1X's household humanoid robot in the All-In robotics special. The episode frames it as a consumer robot, developer platform, teleoperation endpoint, and data-collection surface for embodied AI.

## Current Profile
Neo is presented as an ambitious home humanoid whose earliest units are expected to be limited and imperfect. Its strategic value in the source comes from the combination of household use, remote presence, human-like embodiment, fleet management, third-party models, and teleoperation data that can improve autonomy over time.

## Key Characteristics
- Neo is aimed at homes, which makes safety, privacy, reliability, and user expectations more difficult than bounded industrial deployments.
- The product is intended to ship first to a handful of early customers, with managed expectations around falls and rough edges.
- Teleoperation can let a person use Neo as a remote avatar for presence, inspection, conversation, and meetings.
- The platform is meant to allow outside developers and model builders to run or fine-tune capabilities on the robot.
- Neo's human-like body is important to 1X's claim that internet and human video data can transfer into robot learning.

## Qualifications
Neo's first shipping date, preorder count, subscription pricing, autonomy level, developer openness, and remote-operation boundaries are not independently verified by this source. Household teleoperation also raises privacy and trust questions that are less severe in warehouses or remote facilities.
