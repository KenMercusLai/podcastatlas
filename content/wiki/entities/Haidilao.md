# Haidilao / 海底捞

## Overview
Haidilao / 海底捞 is a Chinese hot-pot restaurant chain whose wiki profile now spans overseas experience design, adjacent fast-food experiments, and delivery-led occasion expansion.

## Current Profile
Across the bounded sources, Haidilao's core identity remains restaurant experience: service, social dining, category explanation, and hot-pot ritual make the brand more than a menu. The newer source adds that Haidilao is also searching for growth outside the classic dine-in hot-pot occasion: burgers test standardized meal formats, while delivery and one-person meal products such as bibimbap extend the brand into daily solo meals even as restaurant revenue remains the largest business.

## Key Characteristics
- Haidilao's strongest brand asset is experience design around service, explanation, atmosphere, and social hot-pot dining.
- Overseas expansion requires [[RestaurantCulturalLegibility]] because hot pot asks unfamiliar diners to understand equipment, sauces, procedure, and sharing norms.
- The company can test adjacent standardized meals, such as burgers, when value-seeking consumers want filling and affordable occasions.
- Delivery is now a material growth branch: the source says it became Haidilao's fastest-growing business and reached roughly 9% of group revenue.
- New formats still carry profit and execution uncertainty because delivery, big-stall formats, hot pot, sushi, and other sub-brands may not match mature restaurant margins at scale.

## Evidence
- Experience and cultural legibility: [[e232-canyin-chuhai-you-xin-zhaor-ma-cong-dingtaifeng-yu-gongcha-naxia-meiguo-chudai-xiaoguan-liaoqi-3bb14dd4-0b13-4173-9d5b-0e63bf98ae1d]] frames Haidilao's overseas value around service, explanation, room energy, and cultural performance.
- Adjacent meal format: [[zhongguo-yinglai-hanbao-kaidianchao-guochan-huangningmeng-jiage-huiluo-1006920543]] names Haidilao in China's burger opening wave, showing category-extension interest beyond hot pot.
- Delivery expansion: [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] says delivery was the fastest-growing first-half business, helped by one-person meal products and denser delivery sites.
- Margin qualification: [[waimai-chengwei-haidilao-zengzhang-zuikuai-de-yewu-legao-biaoshi-buhui-yong-ai-lai-sheji-chanpin-1008870590]] cites a research view that delivery and new-brand businesses still have lower profitability.

## Qualifications
The delivery-growth source does not prove that delivery can become as profitable or brand-protective as dine-in hot pot. The overseas source also cautions that experience design has to make the category understandable, not merely distinctive.
