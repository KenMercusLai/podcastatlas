# 杨熊 / Yang Xiong (Qin General)

杨熊 / Yang Xiong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-136-meinv-xijiao-liubang-ye-hao-zhe-kouer-lplgzwzahepwx9p7krfc4x0cnz4o|Qinji 136]] as a Qin general defeated by [[LiuBang|刘邦]] around Baima and Quyu. The transcript writes "杨雄," but this page uses 杨熊 to avoid collision with the existing [[YangXiong|扬雄]] Western Han author page.

The episode's main interest is not Yang Xiong's biography but the Qin command environment. After defeat, Yang Xiong retreats to [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]], and [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] has him executed as a warning. That response reinforces the wiki's Hu Hai branch: Qin still has coercive force, but its punishment reflex can destroy commanders instead of solving battlefield pressure.
