## Overview
古蜀道网络 / Shu Road Network is the historical route system crossing the Qinling and Daba mountains between northern political centers, [[Hanzhong|汉中]], [[BaShu|巴蜀]], [[Chengdu|成都]], and the upper Yangtze region.

## Current Profile
The source rejects the image of one timeless road. The network joined northern approaches such as [[BaoxieRoad|褒斜道]] to southern routes such as [[JinniuRoad|金牛道]], while [[LitchiRoad|荔枝道]] connected the Chongqing-Sichuan side toward Hanzhong and the Ziwu corridor. Its active lines, surfaces, and purposes changed with capitals, war, settlement, trade, water transport, and engineering capacity.

The network is both infrastructure and archive. Trestle holes, cliff roads, inscriptions, bridges, old place names, salt-trade memory, modern road overlays, and walkable fragments preserve different periods without forming one continuous untouched artifact.

## Key Characteristics
- Plural road system rather than a single fixed itinerary.
- Cross-mountain connector among political centers, Hanzhong, Ba-Shu, and the upper Yangtze.
- Infrastructure used for warfare, official communication, migration, trade, and ordinary travel.
- Layered heritage in which routes were destroyed, rerouted, resurfaced, and reused.
- Network whose importance rose or fell with political geography and transport technology.

## Evidence
- Network form: [[ep285-tafang-gushudao-ganwen-lu-zai-hefang-li20senvn3oelioproznnykdajvt|EP285]] distinguishes broad Shu roads from the narrower Qinling-Daba network and connects multiple northern and southern routes through Hanzhong.
- Changing function: [[ep285-tafang-gushudao-ganwen-lu-zai-hefang-li20senvn3oelioproznnykdajvt|EP285]] follows military roads into later civilian and trade use, including salt movement along the Litchi Road.
- Layered remains: [[ep285-tafang-gushudao-ganwen-lu-zai-hefang-li20senvn3oelioproznnykdajvt|EP285]] uses Mingyue Gorge to place trestles, old road, Republican-era highway, railway, expressway, and high-speed rail in one corridor.
- Political geography: [[ep285-tafang-gushudao-ganwen-lu-zai-hefang-li20senvn3oelioproznnykdajvt|EP285]] links route prominence to the location of capitals and the later rise of Yangtze water transport.

## Qualifications
The episode is a reporter-and-expert synthesis, not a definitive route gazetteer. Individual alignments, dates of origin, and the relationship between legendary names and material remains remain unevenly evidenced.

- [[LitchiRoad|荔枝道]] - eastern-southern route with contested legend and later trade use.
- [[Hanzhong|汉中]] - strategic and cultural hinge joining northern and southern approaches.
