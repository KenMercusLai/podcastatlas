# 周幽王 / King You of Zhou

## Overview
周幽王 / King You of Zhou is the Western Zhou crisis ruler who appears in the wiki through a Qin-origin digression and a later [[BaoSi|褒姒]] legend episode. He marks the collapse context that lets [[QinXiangGong|秦襄公]] aid Zhou and later receive recognition from [[ZhouPingwang|周平王]].

## Current Profile
The current profile is deliberately source-bounded. In the Qin-origin branch, Zhou Youwang is the crisis-era ruler whose fall gives [[QinState|秦国]] an opportunity to move from attached fief toward recognized regional power through Qin Xiang Gong's service and Zhou Pingwang's later grants.

The Bao Si episode adds a reception-heavy version: Zhou Youwang attacks Bao in 779 BCE, receives Bao Si, tries to make her smile through the beacon-fire story, and is killed near Lishan in 771 BCE after Shen Hou, Quanrong, and Zeng attack. Because the episode itself warns that Bao Si's supernatural biography is myth-like, Zhou Youwang's page preserves the collapse narrative without making Bao Si's smile a complete causal explanation.

## Key Characteristics
- Western Zhou end-crisis ruler whose fall becomes a hinge for later Qin recognition.
- Narrative partner to Bao Si in the famous beacon-fire and "一笑亡国" tradition.
- Ruler whose failure is remembered through both military-political collapse and moralized court legend.
- Source-bounded figure where the Bao Si material needs myth-and-reception qualification.
