# 周幽王 / King You of Zhou

周幽王 / King You of Zhou appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] as the crisis-era Zhou ruler in the [[QinState|秦国]] origin story. The source says [[QinXiangGong|秦襄公]] aided Zhou during this Western Zhou collapse context.

His role is a hinge between older [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]] authority and Qin's later rise. The episode uses the crisis to explain why [[ZhouPingwang|周平王]] later rewards Qin with formal rank and western land claims.
