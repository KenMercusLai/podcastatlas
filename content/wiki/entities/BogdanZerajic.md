# Bogdan Zerajic

## Overview

Bogdan Zerajic was the failed assassin whose 1910 attack on Bosnia's Austro-Hungarian governor and subsequent suicide became a martyr model for [[GavriloPrincip|Gavrilo Princip]] and his radical circle.

## Current Profile

In June 1910, Zerajic fired five shots at Governor Marian Varesanin, missed, and killed himself with the sixth. The episode reports that police and the governor treated his corpse brutally, while pamphlets and poems subsequently recast the failed attack as heroic sacrifice.

Zerajic's historical importance in this branch lies less in the attack's direct political effect than in its afterlife. Princip reportedly called him a model, visited his grave at night, and absorbed a script in which an otherwise powerless young radical could acquire meaning through violence, failure, and death.

## Key Characteristics

- Would-be assassin who attacked an Austro-Hungarian governor in 1910 and then killed himself.
- Operational failure whose death acquired symbolic power through repression, pamphlets, poetry, and grave visitation.
- Hero figure for Princip and parts of the Young Bosnia milieu.
- Precedent joining political violence to self-sacrifice and posthumous recognition.
- Historical actor whose later myth should be distinguished from a complete account of his own motives.

## Qualifications

The source provides a concise narrative, not a complete biography or archival reconstruction. Zerajic's private motives, the precise treatment of his corpse, the circulation and reach of commemorative texts, and the causal weight of his example remain source-scoped. His influence on Princip was meaningful but operated alongside nationalism, poverty, alienation, peer networks, and later logistical support.

## What Changed

- Established Zerajic as a concrete predecessor whose failed attack became politically usable through martyr memory.
- Distinguished his symbolic influence from direct operational control over the later Sarajevo plot.

- [[AustriaHungary]] - imperial authority against whose Bosnian governor his attack was directed.
