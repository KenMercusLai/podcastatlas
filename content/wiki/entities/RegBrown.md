# Reg Brown

## Overview

Reg Brown is the [[DefenseIntelligenceAgency]] counterintelligence analyst who first assembled concerns about [[AnaBelenMontes]] after the 1996 [[BrothersToTheRescue]] aircraft shootdown in [[the-queen-of-cuba-the-queen-of-cuba|The Queen of Cuba]].

## Current Profile

Brown connected several circumstantial signals: Cuba's source inside Brothers to the Rescue, an advance warning delivered through a briefing Montes arranged, an earlier apparent leak, reports of a highly placed Cuban source in U.S. intelligence, and Montes's unusual phone call and departure during the crisis. He nevertheless recognized the professional risk of accusing a decorated colleague without stronger proof.

His role illustrates the episode's distinction between signal detection and adjudication. Brown's suspicions were directionally important, but the facts available to him did not uniquely identify espionage; [[ScottCarmichael]] could verify innocent parts of Montes's account and initially closed the inquiry.

## Key Characteristics

- DIA counterintelligence analyst alert to possible Cuban penetration.
- Connected the shootdown warning, Montes's briefing role, prior leak concerns, and crisis behavior.
- Escalated his concerns despite the reputational risk of accusing a prominent colleague.
- Produced an early circumstantial hypothesis rather than the later decisive evidentiary match.
