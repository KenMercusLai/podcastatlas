## Overview

杨得意 / Yang Deyi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-358-2-simaxiangru-diao-fengliu-guafu-lphrip8prodrc46x__y6elbrkigz|Hanji 358-2]] as the dog supervisor near [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] who identifies [[SimaXiangru|司马相如]] as a living writer and helps bring him before the emperor.

## Current Profile

The episode makes Yang Deyi a compact example of how low formal rank and high practical proximity can diverge. His work caring for imperial hunting dogs sounds minor, but his access lets him answer Han Wudi's literary curiosity at the decisive moment and arrange a summons.

The host treats the recommendation ambivalently: Yang may be flattering the emperor and his claimed hometown connection is questioned, yet his intervention still gives genuine talent a route into court. His importance lies in brokerage, not in independent literary evaluation.

## Key Characteristics

- Close imperial attendant described as a dog supervisor rather than as a senior minister.
- Recognizes an opening when Han Wudi admires a fu attributed to Sima Xiangru.
- Converts proximity into an actionable introduction and summons.
- Embodies the mixed motives of court brokerage: flattery, self-positioning, and useful recommendation can coexist.

## Evidence

- Office and proximity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-358-2-simaxiangru-diao-fengliu-guafu-lphrip8prodrc46x__y6elbrkigz|Hanji 358-2]] explains “狗监” as an office caring for Han Wudi's hunting dogs.
- Recommendation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-358-2-simaxiangru-diao-fengliu-guafu-lphrip8prodrc46x__y6elbrkigz|Hanji 358-2]] says Yang tells the emperor about Sima and is ordered to arrange the writer's appearance.
- Hometown claim: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-358-2-simaxiangru-diao-fengliu-guafu-lphrip8prodrc46x__y6elbrkigz|Hanji 358-2]] reports Yang calling Sima a fellow townsman but explicitly questions the reliability of that connection.
- Cultural afterlife: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-358-2-simaxiangru-diao-fengliu-guafu-lphrip8prodrc46x__y6elbrkigz|Hanji 358-2]] invokes “杨意不逢” to make Yang's brokerage a symbol for talent needing access.

## Qualifications

The page does not establish Yang Deyi's broader career, precise relationship with Sima Xiangru, or whether the hometown claim was sincere. The episode's work-title sequence is ambiguous, and its claim about Yang's motives is interpretation rather than direct evidence.

## What Changed

- Created Yang Deyi as a distinct Western Han court-access broker.
- Separated the practical value of his recommendation from the episode's speculation about flattery and hometown identity.
