# 韩暨 / Han Ji (late Han)

## Overview
韩暨 / Han Ji is a Nanyang figure whose attempted avoidance of both [[YuanShu|袁术]] and [[LiuBiao|刘表]] ends in reluctant office before he later enters [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s administration.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1096 depicts Han Ji repeatedly using geographic withdrawal to avoid recruitment. He first moves into the mountains to escape Yuan Shu's summons, then hides again when Liu Biao seeks to appoint him. Liu Biao's anger makes continued refusal unsafe, and Han Ji finally accepts a county post. After Cao Cao takes Jingzhou, the episode places him among the personnel absorbed into the Cao-side administration.

## Key Characteristics
- Avoids recruitment from more than one late-Han patron.
- Uses concealment and relocation before accepting office under pressure.
- Illustrates that withdrawal options depend on a patron's coercive reach.
- Later transfers administrative capacity into Cao Cao's regime after Jingzhou changes hands.
