# Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution

The Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution appears in [[tech-20260728-0728-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260728-0728-mp-tech-pod-128]] through [[AdamSubhas]], who studies [[OceanAlkalinityEnhancement]]. The episode uses his comments to frame alkaline materials such as olivine as a possible "antacid" for seawater.

In this source, Woods Hole supplies the cautious research voice. Subhas describes the approach as accelerating a natural geological process, but says more research is needed before claims about scale, speed, ecological tradeoffs, and climate usefulness can be treated as settled.
