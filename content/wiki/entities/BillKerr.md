# Bill Kerr

## Overview
Bill Kerr is an economist cited by [[PlanetMoney]] for comparative research on why some immigrant groups concentrate in particular industries.

## Current Profile
The episode attributes to Kerr a study of nearly 40 years of census data across 131 immigrant groups. Its reported findings associate stronger specialization with smaller group populations and denser social ties, using within-community marriage as one proxy alongside language and social interaction. The episode also reports an earnings advantage for self-employed immigrants working inside their community's cluster compared with similar people outside it.

## Key Characteristics
- Studies immigrant entrepreneurship and industry concentration comparatively rather than through one community alone.
- Treats social connectedness as an explanatory correlate, not merely cultural description.
- Uses marriage patterns as one imperfect proxy for community ties.
- Connects clustering to practical information exchange and reported earnings differences.
- Allows contingent historical events to seed clusters that networks later reproduce.
