# Rolex Datejust

Rolex Datejust is the 1945 anniversary model discussed in [[rolex-1]] as a postwar bridge between technical wristwatch credibility and elite identity. The source says [[Rolex]] gave milestone chronometer watches to figures such as Winston Churchill and Dwight Eisenhower, and later used advertising around men who guided the world.

The Datejust helps mark the move from tool proof toward [[ProfessionalToolBranding]] and lifestyle status. Its date window and Cyclops magnifier also keep the source's theme that small functional details can become recognizable brand memory.
