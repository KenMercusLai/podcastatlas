# TBPN

TBPN appears in [[tsr-s5-johncoogan-audiofile-1-tsr-s5-johncoogan-audiofile-1]] as the daily live technology and business media show co-hosted by [[JohnCoogan]] and [[JordiHayes]]. The episode describes it as a two-host format that comments on breaking technology news, hosts many guests, and combines serious business analysis with humor and a deliberately over-the-top brand style.

The source makes TBPN a [[DailyLiveMediaCadence]] case. Unlike software or a static archive, the show depends on the hosts appearing live at the scheduled time; Coogan uses this to explain why interviews, jokes, technical problems, guest booking, and repetition all become part of the product.

TBPN also extends the wiki's [[VideoPodcastAffordance]] and [[MediaFormConstraint]] branches. Coogan says the show can cover news even without the ideal guest because the two-host format itself carries analysis, while guests, anonymous online accounts, and timely interviews can add live curiosity or news value.

## Source Position
- The source treats TBPN as a media product, not only a podcast feed.
- The full name is described as emerging from a joke around "Technology Brothers"; Coogan says TBPN originally stood for "technology business" on launch day.
- The episode frames TBPN's interview style as pro-technology and non-gotcha while still willing to ask hard business and technology questions.
