# Bert Bell

Bert Bell is the NFL commissioner in [[the-nfl-1]] associated with making competitive balance a design principle. The episode uses his "Any Given Sunday" philosophy to explain why the league wanted weak teams to have real hope and strong teams to remain beatable.

The source connects Bell to reverse-order draft logic, scheduling, shared ticket revenue, and the early version of [[LeagueFirstOperatingModel]]. His role matters because the NFL's product was not only star talent or big markets; it was uncertainty that made games worth watching across the whole league.
