# François Chabot

## Overview

François Chabot was a revolutionary deputy whose East India Company corruption became one strand in the foreign-conspiracy case used against [[GeorgesDanton|Georges Danton]] and his associates.

## Current Profile

The source presents Chabot as both corrupt and accusatory. While attempting to hide his own conduct, he alleged a foreign plot connected with the French East India Company. That story was merged with [[PhilippeFabreDEglantine|Fabre d'Églantine's]] fabricated allegations, allowing genuine fraud to serve as the evidentiary core of a much broader political conspiracy.

## Key Characteristics

- He was involved in East India Company corruption.
- He alleged foreign coordination while trying to conceal his own conduct.
- His story overlapped with Fabre's separately fabricated plot.
- His prosecution was used to assemble unlike defendants into one apparent network.
