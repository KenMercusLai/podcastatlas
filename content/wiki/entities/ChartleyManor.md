# Chartley Manor

Chartley Manor appears in [[696-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-killing-the-queen-of-scots-part-6-glt2334035496]] as the place where [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]] is held under [[AmiasPaulet]]'s stricter custody before her arrest. The source emphasizes the tension between royal protocol and prison control: Mary retains servants and ceremony while searches and communication restrictions make captivity humiliating.

Chartley is also the physical setting for the beer-barrel channel arranged by [[GilbertGifford]]. Because that channel is controlled by [[FrancisWalsingham]]'s network, the site becomes the trap in which [[AnthonyBabington]]'s letter and Mary's reply are intercepted and processed by [[ThomasPhillips]].
