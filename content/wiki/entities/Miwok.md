# Miwok

The Miwok appear in [[693-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-englands-greatest-hero-part-3-glt5069845329]] in the episode's account of [[FrancisDrake]] reaching the Bay Area during the [[GoldenHind]] voyage. The hosts say Drake meets the Miwok, is given a feathered crown, and claims the land for [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]] as [[NewAlbion]].

For the wiki, this is a narrow source-context page. The episode mainly uses the encounter to mark Drake's Pacific reach and English imperial imagination; it does not develop Miwok society on its own terms.
