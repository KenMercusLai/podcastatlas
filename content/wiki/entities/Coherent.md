# Coherent

Coherent appears in [[ep-11-growing-technology-footprints-in-insurance-sector]] as the global technology company where [[NickBlamer]] works as Senior Technology Director. The source says the company is primarily based in New York and works on software used largely by actuarial users and Excel users.

The episode's Coherent branch is centered on [[CoherentSpark]]. The product is described as turning [[MicrosoftExcel|Excel]] spreadsheets into version-controlled, auditable APIs that can run faster and scale in cloud environments. In the wiki, Coherent therefore connects insurance technology to [[SpreadsheetToAPIGovernance]] rather than only to generic digital transformation.
