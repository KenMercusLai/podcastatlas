# 咸鱼 / Xianyu (Stand-up Comedian)

## Overview
咸鱼 / Xianyu appears as a comedian and panel participant in the [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] and [[SixiaoComedy|四孝喜剧]] live episode on trend-following, borrowing, and money.

## Current Profile
In the source, Xianyu functions as the small-money and everyday-consumption witness. His stories move from short-video-influenced purchases and paid home cooking to a 500-yuan friend loan, childhood shared spending, post-championship income changes, new-phone awkwardness, and a past commercial collection experience.

## Key Characteristics
- Comedian whose episode role links trend-following consumption to ordinary friendship money.
- Uses small debts and shared spending to show how close relationships blur repayment clarity.
- Supplies a commercial collection story that expands the episode from friend loans into business debt pressure.
