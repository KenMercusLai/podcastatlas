## Overview
Kevin Bass is presented in the All-In Epstein special as a citizen journalist using AI-assisted document analysis to investigate released files, especially records involving [[ReidHoffman|Reid Hoffman]].

## Current Profile
Bass describes a workflow using AI tools, vectorized SQL databases, MCP-related tools, and agents to test public statements against a large document collection. His episode contribution is narrower than a theory of Epstein's whole network: he claims that the files show far more Hoffman contact, meetings, financial activity, and travel than Hoffman's public account acknowledged. The episode does not independently reproduce or audit his dataset, so the counts remain source-attributed.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses computational search and agentic tools to organize a large released-document corpus.
- Frames analysis around falsifiable public claims rather than a total theory of the Epstein network.
- Alleges a large discrepancy between Hoffman's public description and the records he reviewed.
- Accepts that factual rigor must constrain both accusatory and exculpatory narratives.
