# 雮尘珠 / Muchen Pearl

## Overview

The 雮尘珠, also called 凤凰胆 in the bounded episode, is the central fictional artifact sought to reverse the 扎格拉玛 curse in [[GhostBlowsOutTheLight|《鬼吹灯》]]. Its uncertain route links ancestral error, [[ZheguShao|鹧鸪哨]]'s failed expedition, Shirley 杨's inheritance and the later journey to [[YunnanWormValleyGhostBlowsOutTheLight|《云南虫谷》]].

## Current Profile

The artifact matters less as treasure than as a moving causal promise. 扎格拉玛 descendants believe the genuine jade eye can correct the disaster produced by their ancestors' imitation. Reports place it first in ancient royal possession and later possibly in Maoling or the Western Xia treasury, but the Black Water City search yields only an inscribed bone plate.

That failure changes the geography of the quest. 孙教授's reading links the eye sign to phoenix language without giving a location, while an eye-like jade beast and 陈瞎子's map make 献王墓 the next actionable hypothesis. The episode treats all of these routes as fictional lore rather than verified artifact history.

## Key Characteristics

- Functions as a proposed cure for an inherited fictional curse.
- Moves through uncertain names, legends and burial locations rather than one verified provenance chain.
- Motivates the formation and persistence of the 搬山道人 mission.
- Links 鹧鸪哨's failure to Shirley 杨's later expedition.
- Operates as a cross-book clue object rather than ordinary portable wealth.

## Qualifications

The 雮尘珠 and its provenance are fictional. Similarity to lost treasures, jade eyes, royal burial goods or historical legends supplies narrative familiarity but does not establish the artifact's existence or authenticate any medical, ritual or archaeological claim.
