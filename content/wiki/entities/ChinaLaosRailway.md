# China-Laos Railway / 中老铁路

The China-Laos Railway / 中老铁路 appears in [[ep267-tafang-meigonghe-yuji-hunfeiyi-yu-shuili-diguo-lkq2vxaatn4wxue7y5q1fc3-8yfk]] as a concrete infrastructure contrast. [[XingHaiyang|邢海洋]] says the journey from Luang Prabang to Vientiane can take only a few hours by rail, where older road travel could take far longer.

The source does not treat the railway as a total modernization shortcut. Ticket purchase, app registration, local agents, transfer vehicles, and station access still matter. That makes the railway a good example of how built infrastructure and local service systems have to be read together in [[MekongRiverGeographicWriting]].
