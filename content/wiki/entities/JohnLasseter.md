# John Lasseter

John Lasseter is the Disney-trained animator and Pixar creative leader in [[disney-the-renaissance-and-the-empire-1]]. The source says Disney fired him after he pushed computer animation ideas around The Brave Little Toaster, turning a lost internal talent into a key figure at [[Pixar]].

In the episode, Lasseter helps make computer animation emotionally legible rather than merely technical. After [[BobIger]] buys Pixar, Lasseter and [[EdCatmull]] also take leadership roles over Disney animation, making him central to [[CreativeCoreRenewal]] and the post-Pixar revival of the [[EntertainmentIPFlywheel]].
