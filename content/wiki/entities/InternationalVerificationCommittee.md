# International Verification Committee

The International Verification Committee appears in [[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]] as the body meant to verify [[Hamas]] disarmament. The source says its membership and procedures are still unknown.

The committee is central to the episode's [[DisarmamentWithdrawalSequencing]] problem. [[Israel]] says withdrawal depends on complete disarmament first, while Hamas wants withdrawal before giving up leverage; without credible verification, both sides can claim the other has failed to move.
