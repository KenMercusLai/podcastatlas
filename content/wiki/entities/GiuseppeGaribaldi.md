# Giuseppe Garibaldi

## Overview

Giuseppe Garibaldi appears in the wiki as an Italian unification leader whose military reputation became a mass British liberal celebrity.

## Current Profile

[[73-england-v-italy-glt3184835237]] presents Garibaldi through British reception. His support for Italian unification and opposition to Bourbon and papal power made him attractive to working-class and liberal audiences who interpreted the Risorgimento as a European freedom cause. His Newcastle reception, 1860 Expedition of the Thousand, and enormous 1864 London crowds show political sympathy becoming popular celebrity.

That memory also entered material culture. The episode uses biscuits, pubs, street names, merchandise, and Nottingham Forest's red shirts to show how a foreign revolutionary could become domesticated inside British everyday life. These examples establish reception more securely than they provide a complete account of Garibaldi's campaigns or politics.

## Key Characteristics

- Military and political leader associated with Italian unification.
- Popular British symbol of liberal nationalism and European freedom.
- Anti-Bourbon and anti-papal figure legible within British Protestant politics.
- Transnational celebrity welcomed in Newcastle and London.
- Historical figure whose name and red-shirt image entered British consumer and sporting culture.
