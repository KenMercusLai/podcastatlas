## Overview

殷离（金庸小说） / Yin Li is a [[HeavenSwordDragonSaber|《倚天屠龙记》]] character whose bond with [[ZhangWujiJinYong|张无忌]] is interpreted through childhood memory, shared hardship, family injury, and romantic exclusivity.

## Current Profile

The episode argues that Yin Li remains attached to the remembered child who once resisted her rather than fully accepting the adult Zhang Wuji before her. Their later suffering together creates gratitude, warmth, and mutual support, but the hosts read Zhang's feeling primarily as pity and appreciation rather than reciprocal selection.

Her insistence that love should be exclusive is linked to witnessing the harm created by her father's plural household. The same family violence is used to contextualize her anger and poisonous martial practice. These connections give her position a coherent history without turning adversity into a diagnosis or excusing harmful conduct.

## Key Characteristics

- Anchors love in a durable childhood image rather than the partner's present identity.
- Shares genuine care and mutual support with Zhang Wuji during hardship.
- Defends romantic exclusivity against the plural arrangement imagined by others.
- Carries anger and distrust associated with injury inside her family.
- Receives pity, gratitude, and affection that do not clearly become reciprocal partnership.

## Qualifications

This is a source-attributed literary profile. The causal link among family harm, martial practice, anger, and exclusivity is interpretive, and a preference for monogamy does not require trauma to be valid. Exact motives, chronology, dialogue, diagnosis, and the final status of Yin Li's attachment remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Created a profile separating remembered love and mutual survival care from present-day reciprocity.
- Preserved romantic exclusivity as a principled position rather than merely an obstacle to “four women in one boat.”
