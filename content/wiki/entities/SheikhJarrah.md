# Sheikh Jarrah

## Overview
Sheikh Jarrah is an East Jerusalem neighborhood used by the source to show how property law, displacement history, and unequal return rules can turn individual eviction cases into a national political conflict.

## Current Profile
The episode says Palestinian refugee families were housed there after 1948 and later faced claims by Jewish owners or successors invoking older title after Israel occupied East Jerusalem in 1967. Its significance lies less in one deed dispute than in the asserted asymmetry between recovery rights available to some Jewish claimants and the absence of equivalent remedies for Palestinians displaced from homes inside Israel.

## Key Characteristics
- East Jerusalem neighborhood at the center of 2021 eviction protests.
- Property dispute embedded in the histories of 1948 displacement and post-1967 Israeli control.
- Illustrates the source's claim that formally legal processes can produce identity-asymmetric outcomes.
