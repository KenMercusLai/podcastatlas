# Beike / 贝壳

Beike appears in [[qishui-yinyue-chongji-tengxun-wangyi-tesila-deng-cheqi-zai-guonei-kaizhan-daguimo-zhaohui-1008396395]] as a real-estate platform whose growth is shifting from network expansion toward efficiency. The episode says first-half revenue fell 12% while net profit rose nearly 80%, second-quarter second-hand housing transactions rose about one quarter, and second-hand homes exceeded 50% of national housing transactions in the first half of 2026.

The source treats Beike as a [[EfficiencyOverScaleExpansion]] case. When housing transactions move toward existing-home stock and store/agent counts are basically flat, profit improvement depends more on operating discipline than on simply adding more outlets or brokers.
