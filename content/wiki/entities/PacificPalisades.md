# Pacific Palisades

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-adam-carolla-on-californias-collapse-fires-failed-leadership-and-gyno-fascism-39710360]] makes the [[PacificPalisades|Pacific Palisades]] fire anniversary the entry point for a broader [[LosAngeles|Los Angeles]] and [[California]] governance critique. The host cites thousands of destroyed structures and very little visible rebuilding, while [[AdamCarolla|Adam Carolla]] uses the case to argue that regulation, permitting, and ownership fatigue can turn disaster recovery into long delay.

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-spencer-pratt-on-fixing-la-wildfires-homelessness-corruption-the-fight-to-take-it-back-41236810]] adds the survivor-candidate version through [[SpencerPratt|Spencer Pratt]], who says he watched his house burn remotely, could not reach family, and later turned the event into a campaign against failed [[EmergencyWarningInfrastructure]], fire deployment, water readiness, and city accountability.

[[tech-20260112-0112-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260112-0112-mp-tech-pod-128]] also uses Pacific Palisades as part of its L.A. wildfire rebuilding frame, where [[FireResilientConstruction]] involves materials, off-site fabrication, cost, speed, and memory rather than only replacing what burned.
