## Overview

韩棱 / Han Leng appears as an Eastern Han official who uses investigative judgment, memorial speech, and ritual boundary work against [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]]'s overreach. Hanji 760 shows him redirecting the [[LiuChangDuxiangHouEasternHan|Liu Chang]] murder inquiry away from the framed brother [[LiuGangEasternHan|Liu Gang]] and back toward the capital. Hanji 761 places him among officials whose objections to the Northern Xiongnu expedition are ignored, while Hanji 764 shows him blocking a ruler-like "wansui" salute to Dou Xian.

## Current Profile

Hanji 760 supplies Han Leng's earliest current role. When officials are interrogating Liu Gang after Liu Chang's murder, Han argues that the culprit must be in the capital and that the investigation should not avoid the nearer, more dangerous direction. [[EmpressDowagerDouHeLateHan|Empress Dowager Dou]] reacts angrily, but Han keeps the judgment in place.

Hanji 761 supplies Han Leng's anti-campaign role. As one of the officials who memorialize against Dou Xian's Northern Xiongnu expedition, he belongs to the first failed-feedback layer before Empress Dowager Dou allows the campaign and mansion construction to proceed.

Hanji 764 gives the more durable ritual scene. Han Leng does not attack Dou Xian through a large policy memorial in that source; instead, he prevents a public act that would symbolically upgrade Dou from powerful minister to quasi-ruler. He stops other officials from kneeling and shouting "wansui" to Dou, arguing that no ritual permits giving a ruler's acclamation to a minister.

The page therefore treats Han Leng as a boundary official. His political value lies in making hierarchy visible when ordinary officials are tempted to normalize outer-relative dominance through public language.

## Key Characteristics

- Court official whose murder-case judgment redirects attention from Liu Gang to the capital culprit.
- Official who appears in the early anti-expedition remonstrance field.
- Ritual boundary actor who blocks ruler-like acclamation for a powerful minister.
- Example of using etiquette to preserve hierarchy under outer-relative pressure.
- Small-action resistor whose intervention prevents public normalization of Dou Xian's dominance.
- Source of the episode's "Han Leng's sword" memory, kept here as source-scoped phrasing.

## Qualifications

This is not a full biography of Han Leng. The page records three bounded anti-Dou or boundary roles: murder-case direction, early campaign objection, and later ritual speech boundary. The "Han Leng's sword" phrase is retained as the podcast's interpretive memory rather than expanded into a separate idiom history.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 760 as Han Leng's earliest current layer: redirecting the Liu Chang murder inquiry under dowager pressure.
- Reframed Han Leng as an investigative, remonstrance, and ritual-boundary official rather than only a one-scene ritual actor.
