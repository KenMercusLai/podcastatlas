## Overview

韩棱 / Han Leng appears as an Eastern Han official who uses both memorial speech and ritual boundary work against [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]]'s overreach. Hanji 761 places him among officials whose objections to the Northern Xiongnu expedition are ignored, while Hanji 764 shows him blocking a ruler-like "wansui" salute to Dou Xian.

## Current Profile

Hanji 761 supplies Han Leng's early anti-campaign role. As one of the officials who memorialize against Dou Xian's Northern Xiongnu expedition, he belongs to the first failed-feedback layer before [[EmpressDowagerDouHeLateHan|Empress Dowager Dou]] allows the campaign and mansion construction to proceed.

Hanji 764 gives the more durable ritual scene. Han Leng does not attack Dou Xian through a large policy memorial in that source; instead, he prevents a public act that would symbolically upgrade Dou from powerful minister to quasi-ruler. He stops other officials from kneeling and shouting "wansui" to Dou, arguing that no ritual permits giving a ruler's acclamation to a minister.

The page therefore treats Han Leng as a boundary official. His political value lies in making hierarchy visible when ordinary officials are tempted to normalize outer-relative dominance through public language.

## Key Characteristics

- Court official who appears in the early anti-expedition remonstrance field.
- Ritual boundary actor who blocks ruler-like acclamation for a powerful minister.
- Example of using etiquette to preserve hierarchy under outer-relative pressure.
- Small-action resistor whose intervention prevents public normalization of Dou Xian's dominance.
- Source of the episode's "Han Leng's sword" memory, kept here as source-scoped phrasing.

## Qualifications

This is not a full biography of Han Leng. The page records two bounded anti-Dou roles: early campaign objection and later ritual speech boundary. The "Han Leng's sword" phrase is retained as the podcast's interpretive memory rather than expanded into a separate idiom history.
