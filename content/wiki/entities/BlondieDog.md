# Blondie (Hitler's dog)

## Overview

Blondie was Adolf Hitler's German shepherd, used in Nazi publicity and killed in the Berlin bunker when Hitler ordered cyanide tested on her shortly before his own death.

## Current Profile

The episode says Martin Bormann gave Blondie to Hitler in 1941. Her breed fitted Nazi associations among shepherd dogs, wolves, German identity, and martial strength, while photographs of Hitler with Blondie offered propaganda a scene of apparently ordinary affection.

Blondie's final days destroy any easy inference from animal affection to general moral character. She accompanied Hitler into the bunker, where cyanide was tested on her on 29 April 1945. Hitler's distress at her death coexisted with responsibility for war, dictatorship, persecution, and genocide.

## Key Characteristics

- German shepherd given to Hitler during the Second World War.
- Companion animal incorporated into Nazi leader imagery.
- Breed carried source-described Germanic and lupine symbolism.
- Accompanied Hitler into the Berlin bunker.
- Killed in a cyanide test immediately before the regime's end.
