# 兰陵 / Lanling (Warring States)

兰陵 / Lanling enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-2-mingming-yilian-baixiang-de-caize-ping-shenme-baixiang-lhfyb6hzcqkpbzdrwsxajb7hhmhe]], where the transcript renders it as "蓝陵" in the discussion of [[Xunzi|荀子]]'s first government office. The episode places it in the area of today's Linyi/Lanling region and treats it as a significant county-level post, not a symbolic sinecure.

Its importance is strategic and cultural. The source says [[ChuState|楚国]] gained the area while [[QinState|秦国]] was absorbed by the Changping and Handan conflicts, taking ground from or near [[LuState|鲁国]]. Because Lu carries strong Zhou-ritual and scholar-cultural prestige, [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]]'s appointment of Xunzi to Lanling becomes more than staffing: it is an attempt to govern a new territory through a figure local elites could recognize.

Lanling also makes Xunzi politically exposed. The episode says his governance makes agriculture, craft, and commerce prosper and helps maintain friendly relations with [[QiState|齐国]], but those same Qi ties and the county's border position later give slanderers material to make him look dangerous to Chunshenjun.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-4-shui-shi-kongzi-hou-zui-weidade-sixiangjia-lr4phoq9chc4tz0yb8y95gy21qdd]] gives Lanling the later return and final-life branch. After [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] dies and [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] does not use [[Xunzi|荀子]], [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] regrets the earlier dismissal and personally presses Xunzi to return in 251 BCE. The source credits Xunzi's second Lanling tenure with applying his philosophy and statecraft to produce unusual local prosperity. After Chunshenjun is killed in 238 BCE, Xunzi can no longer hold office, but he remains in Lanling to teach, write, die, and be buried there.
