# 阳球 / Yang Qiu (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-915-shishang-meiyou-zhenzheng-de-kouwu-zhehua-dui-ma-lgxcodjxnaozvyegxqurgzji5gus|Hanji 915]] supplies Yang Qiu's prosecutorial high point before his Hanji 916 transfer. After denouncing [[WangJiLateHan|王吉]]'s cruelty, he becomes sili xiaowei, uses [[YangBiao|杨彪]]'s evidence against [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]]'s retainers, and persuades [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] to authorize arrests against Wang Fu, [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]], Wang Ji, and [[WangMengLateHan|王萌]].

The episode also complicates Yang Qiu's anti-eunuch profile. His attack exposes a corrupt network, but his prison methods include torture, mouth-stopping, beatings, corpse display, confiscation, and family exile. His plan to continue against [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] and the Yuan elite sets up the overconfidence that later sources punish.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-916-ming-hao-he-yun-hao-nage-geng-zhongyao-lmzudjq5zfkfsukmhdeiumesm9qu|Hanji 916]] backfills Yang Qiu's loss of the sili xiaowei position before Hanji 917-918. After [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] uses [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]]'s roadside corpse to rally the eunuch side, [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] transfers Yang Qiu to weiwei; Yang Qiu begs for another month to make the "奸臣" confess, but the eunuchs shout him down and the transfer sticks.

The episode therefore shows Yang Qiu's anti-eunuch leverage being stripped before his later overconfident target list. He remains dangerous, but his removal from sili xiaowei gives Cao Jie and [[ZhuYuLateHan|朱瑀]] room to recover.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-917-ruhe-xunsu-zhaodao-shengzhi-jiaxin-de-pojudian-lk2zt5c3onvui9a4ppbowshy66tg|Hanji 917]] places Yang Qiu just before the failed anti-[[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] plot. The source says that as 司隶校尉 he had already destroyed [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]] and pulled [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]] into the same prosecution field, then wanted to continue against Cao Jie and [[ZhuYuLateHan|朱瑀]].

That setup makes Yang Qiu a dangerous anti-eunuch instrument rather than only a victim. Hanji 917 presents him as overreading one successful strike as proof that the same method can be used against favorites closer to [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]], which sets up the rebound in Hanji 918.

[[YangQiuLateHan|阳球]] was Weiwei in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-918-shiqing-bu-shunli-shi-weishenme-yao-bizui-lh-tbohgo96qw8bjqu3x1yp6czv5|Hanji 918]] and the operational link in the failed plot against [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]].

The episode's crucial detail is domestic: Yang Qiu's concubine has a connection to [[ChengHuangLateHan|程璜]], who is bribed and threatened until he tells Cao Jie. Yang Qiu therefore makes the anti-eunuch plan an example of [[LethalPlanLeakageRisk|lethal plan leakage risk]]: a plot can fail through household access before the formal political move begins.
