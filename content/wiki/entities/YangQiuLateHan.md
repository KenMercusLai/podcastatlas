# 阳球 / Yang Qiu (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-917-ruhe-xunsu-zhaodao-shengzhi-jiaxin-de-pojudian-lk2zt5c3onvui9a4ppbowshy66tg|Hanji 917]] places Yang Qiu just before the failed anti-[[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] plot. The source says that as 司隶校尉 he had already destroyed [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]] and pulled [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]] into the same prosecution field, then wanted to continue against Cao Jie and [[ZhuYuLateHan|朱瑀]].

That setup makes Yang Qiu a dangerous anti-eunuch instrument rather than only a victim. Hanji 917 presents him as overreading one successful strike as proof that the same method can be used against favorites closer to [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]], which sets up the rebound in Hanji 918.

[[YangQiuLateHan|阳球]] was Weiwei in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-918-shiqing-bu-shunli-shi-weishenme-yao-bizui-lh-tbohgo96qw8bjqu3x1yp6czv5|Hanji 918]] and the operational link in the failed plot against [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]].

The episode's crucial detail is domestic: Yang Qiu's concubine has a connection to [[ChengHuangLateHan|程璜]], who is bribed and threatened until he tells Cao Jie. Yang Qiu therefore makes the anti-eunuch plan an example of [[LethalPlanLeakageRisk|lethal plan leakage risk]]: a plot can fail through household access before the formal political move begins.
