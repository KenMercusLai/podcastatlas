# McDonnell Douglas DC-10

The McDonnell Douglas DC-10 appears in [[vol-268-liang-ge-lao-si-lai-si-1003563933]] as the direct market competitor to the [[LockheedL1011TriStar|Lockheed L-1011 TriStar]]. The episode says the TriStar's delay let the DC-10 enter service earlier and win market momentum in the wide-body aircraft race.

The DC-10's source role is comparative. It makes the cost of the [[RollsRoyceRB211|RB211]] delay visible: when aircraft programs compete on airline fleet decisions, a late engine can change not only one supplier's finances but the competitive fate of the airframe built around it.
