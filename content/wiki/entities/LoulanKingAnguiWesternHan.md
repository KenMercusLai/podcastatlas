## Overview

安归 / Angui enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-403-2-dujian-zhan-loulan-bugai-bei-yiwang-de-chaoji-waijiaoguan-fujiezi-lssxm3z-fkdblz8nfoy1pojnetja|Hanji 403-2]] as the [[LoulanKingdomWesternHan|Loulan]] king killed by [[FuJieziWesternHan|傅介子]] during a Western Han mission in 77 BCE.

## Current Profile

The source presents Angui as the Loulan ruler whose balancing between [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] and [[Xiongnu|Xiongnu]] becomes intolerable to Han after earlier attacks on Han envoys and goods. Fu Jiezi exploits Angui's attraction to Han valuables, draws him into a private setting under the pretext of a confidential imperial message, and has two Han warriors stab him from behind.

Angui's death is politically important because Fu immediately converts it into theater. He displays the head, warns Loulan nobles that Han troops will destroy the country if they resist, announces Angui's offenses, and installs the Chang'an hostage [[WeiTuqiWesternHan|Wei Tuqi]] as successor.

## Key Characteristics

- Loulan king targeted as the personal node of Han's punitive Western Regions strategy.
- Accused in the source of disrespecting Han, cooperating with Xiongnu, killing Han envoys, and taking Han goods and seals.
- Drawn into danger through valuables and confidential-audience language.
- Killed in a palace-side private meeting by Fu Jiezi's two armed attendants.
- Becomes the visible object of deterrence when Fu displays his head before Loulan elites and later sends it to Chang'an.
