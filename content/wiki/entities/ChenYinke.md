# 陈寅恪

陈寅恪 enters the wiki through [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] as a scholar normally presented as politically detached but willing to act when the scholarly desk itself was threatened. The episode discusses his role after [[LiangQichao|梁启超]]'s death and [[WangGuowei|王国维]]'s death, especially the effort to defend the [[TsinghuaGuoxueInstitute|清华国学研究院]] against internal power struggles and accusations.

The source uses Chen's memorial language for Wang to connect scholarship with "思想之自由". In this ingest, Chen extends [[AcademicFreedom]] beyond contemporary campus speech into the institutional defense of research, teaching, and the right to maintain a serious intellectual environment.

## Key Claims
- Chen Yinke's nonpolitical stance is not framed as passivity; the source shows him intervening to defend scholarly autonomy.
- His connection to Wang Guowei supplies the episode's clearest language of free thought.
- The episode makes Chen a bridge between classical learning, institution defense, and [[AcademicFreedom]].
