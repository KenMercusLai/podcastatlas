# 魏文侯 / Marquis Wen of Wei

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-04-yurang-wei-zhiyao-fuchou-ln34tydha6iqwlrgmw7iugqfgfik]] adds Wei Wenhou's formal identity in the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] opening notice. The episode identifies him as Wei Si, one of the three Jin grandees recognized by [[ZhouWeilieWang|周威烈王]], and previews him as the most capable and prestigious leader among the three.

魏文侯 / Marquis Wen of Wei enters the wiki through the title of [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-2-lmfzo4sl9nd5kz3mrxdl1dl9efei]]. The source note itself says the short transcript does not fully unfold the title question of whom Wei Wenhou should choose as chancellor, so this page stays deliberately source-scoped.

The episode still connects Wei Wenhou's world to [[WeiState|魏国]]'s early rise and [[WuQi|吴起]]'s military discipline. Its use here is as a title-frame node for later ingests from the same [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] sequence.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]] supplies that later succession notice: Wei Wenhou dies in 397 BCE and his son [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]] succeeds him.
