# ABTA

ABTA appears in [[its-not-easy-being-green-zack-polanski-6a82d1a0ff328abd84724cf0]] as the British travel-industry association cited by [[CaitlinTalbot]]. The source says ABTA estimates that about a third of British travellers booked holidays through a human last year.

The association's role is evidentiary rather than biographical. It provides a market signal for [[TravelAgentResilience]]: even as [[AITravelPlanning]] improves, many travellers still value human help for judgment, reassurance, curation, and support when trips become complex or risky.
