## Overview

吕岱 / Lyu Dai is the Sun-Wu commander who ends [[ShiHui|士徽]]'s resistance in [[JiaozhiCommandery|交趾郡]] by securing the surrender of six Shi brothers and then executing them.

## Current Profile

In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1119-ta-shengqian-guishun-sunquan-sihou-weihe-jiazu-bei-ganjin-shajue-ll4mblhk7ydn3pdxg-5zucstokzz|Hanji 1119]], Lyu Dai enters after [[SunQuan|孙权]] attempts to replace the post-[[ShiXie|士燮]] local order with direct appointments. The source says he recognizes the depth of Shi family power and avoids beginning with a costly frontal assault.

Instead, Lyu Dai uses Shi Kuang to assure Shi Hui that confession and surrender will preserve the family and its property. Once Shi Hui and five brothers come out, Lyu Dai reads the indictment, executes all six, and sends their heads to Wuchang. The method is operationally efficient but morally and institutionally corrosive: it removes the local leadership at low battlefield cost by making a safety promise the source says is not honored.

## Key Characteristics

- Sun-Wu commander implementing post-Shi-Xie centralization in Jiaozhou.
- Pragmatic assessor who avoids immediate frontal assault against a rooted local family.
- User of a kin intermediary to open a surrender channel.
- Commander associated with a safety assurance followed by execution.
- Agent of decisive local-elite decapitation and direct Wu control.

## Qualifications

The page rests on one podcast source. The exact terms, authority, and reliability of the surrender assurance need broader textual verification, as do the campaign route and legal procedure. Effective consolidation should not be confused with legitimate procedure: on the source's own account, Lyu Dai's success depends on a promise that made peaceful submission appear survivable and was then violated.

## What Changed

- Created Lyu Dai as the operational agent of Sun-Wu consolidation in Jiaozhi.
- Kept battlefield efficiency and surrender-credibility damage visible in the same profile.
