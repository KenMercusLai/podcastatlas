## Overview

张易之 / Zhang Yizhi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-plus-pan-dian-shi-shang-de-hao-se-nv-ren-men-lgsf8_asfrictdmsptsyfizykpon|Hanji 616 plus]] as a later male favorite of [[WuZetian|武则天]] whose access is linked to [[PrincessTaiping|太平公主]] and then extended to his brother [[ZhangChangzong|张昌宗]].

## Current Profile

The episode says Princess Taiping introduced Zhang Yizhi to Wu Zetian in order to displace [[XueHuaiyi|薛怀义]], whom Taiping blamed for her husband Xue Shao's death. Zhang Yizhi then allegedly brought Zhang Changzong into the palace. The narrative makes Zhang Yizhi a broker of intimate access as well as a beneficiary of it, while the revenge motive and exact introduction route remain source-scoped.

## Key Characteristics

- Male favorite of Wu Zetian in the source account.
- Presented as an instrument in Princess Taiping's revenge against Xue Huaiyi.
- Benefits from a shift in the ruler's intimate attention.
- Connects his brother Zhang Changzong to the same palace-favorite network.
