# 蚂蚁国际 / Ant International

## Overview
蚂蚁国际 / Ant International is Ant Group's global payment and financial-technology business discussed in [[273-guangwan-waitan-dahui-faxian-mayi-zhaodaole-xin-weizhi-lotxogfoqqwcjigxihtixvncbfc3]].

## Current Profile
The source says Ant International has moved beyond serving Chinese outbound tourists through Alipay acceptance. Its newer value is a network strategy: connect many local wallets through Alipay+, support cross-border transactions through WorldFirst/万里汇, and export payment, risk-control, and anti-fraud know-how to partners.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects global local-wallet ecosystems rather than only expanding one Chinese wallet abroad.
- Uses Alipay+, WorldFirst/万里汇, risk-control, and anti-fraud experience as infrastructure assets.
- Is part of Ant's organizational split into independently governed and valued businesses.
- Extends Ant's agent-era thesis from domestic services into cross-border commerce and payments.

## Evidence
- Network evidence: [[273-guangwan-waitan-dahui-faxian-mayi-zhaodaole-xin-weizhi-lotxogfoqqwcjigxihtixvncbfc3]] says Alipay+ connects more than 50 wallets as a source-scoped claim.
- Cross-border evidence: [[273-guangwan-waitan-dahui-faxian-mayi-zhaodaole-xin-weizhi-lotxogfoqqwcjigxihtixvncbfc3]] names WorldFirst/万里汇 and a source-scoped transaction-volume figure.
- Capability evidence: [[273-guangwan-waitan-dahui-faxian-mayi-zhaodaole-xin-weizhi-lotxogfoqqwcjigxihtixvncbfc3]] says Ant can productize payment, risk-control, and anti-fraud experience for Southeast Asian wallets, banks, and partners.
