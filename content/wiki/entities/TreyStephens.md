# Trey Stephens

## Overview

Trey Stephens appears in the wiki as an [[Anduril]] executive and [[FoundersFund|Founders Fund]] partner whose All-In interview connects [[Palantir]] experience, defense venture investing, and a product-led new-prime model.

## Current Profile

Stephens's source profile begins with [[Palantir]], where he joined early after encountering the company through intelligence work and helped build the government business. In the All-In episode, he uses that experience to explain why [[Anduril]] was founded: he saw very few investable defense startups, believed hardware and software could be rebuilt together, and wanted a company that would pre-fund products before government requirements fully caught up.

His argument is moral, industrial, and financial at once. War is bad, but credible deterrence requires adversaries to believe the United States can respond. That means low-cost attritable systems, modular manufacturing, disciplined private capital, and willingness by technology workers to contribute to national security.

## Key Characteristics

- Early [[Palantir]] government-business builder who carries field-validation lessons into Anduril.
- [[Anduril]] executive associated with the software-defined, hardware-enabled prime thesis.
- Defense venture investor who says the shortage of serious defense startups helped motivate Anduril's creation.
- Advocate for private R&D funding before formal procurement requirements are settled.
- Proponent of low-cost attritable autonomous mass to complement expensive high-end weapons.
- Argues that abstaining from national-security technology is not morally neutral.
