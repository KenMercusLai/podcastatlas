# 西门豹 / Ximen Bao

西门豹 / Ximen Bao appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-1-lk-6ejgmjhvukqgtlz2lowu8qx-v]] as one of [[DiHuang|翟黄]]'s cited recommendations to [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]]. When the ruler is worried about Ye, Di Huang says he recommended Ximen Bao.

The source does not develop Ximen Bao's later administrative reputation; it uses him as part of Di Huang's referral record. His page therefore anchors a thin but useful node for [[TalentReferralQuality]], showing the kind of competent ministerial placement that Di Huang could point to in his chancellor claim.
