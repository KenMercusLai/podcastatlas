## Overview

公孙臣 / Gongsun Chen is a Western Han earth-virtue advocate whose current source presents a successful yellow-dragon prediction as the event that converts a rejected cosmological claim into court office and influence over ritual and institutional planning.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-225-2-weishenme-shuo-xinyuanjieyi-de-zuxian-shi-zhongguoren-lmk2n0ved6gt9tgoxs1qb2s0kuc3|Hanji 225-2]] places Gongsun Chen against [[ZhangCang|张苍]] in a dispute over Han's dynastic virtue. Zhang Cang assigns the dynasty to water, while Gongsun Chen assigns it to earth and predicts that a yellow dragon will appear as confirmation. Zhang Cang rejects the proposal.

After a yellow dragon is reportedly seen at Chengji in 165 BCE, [[LiuHeng|汉文帝刘恒]] summons Gongsun Chen and appoints him as a博士. Gongsun Chen and other scholars then participate in plans to align institutions and official colors with earth virtue, while Zhang Cang loses influence. The case shows a political sequence from forecast to reported confirmation to patronage and institutional access.

The host suspects that the dragon event may have been arranged to displace Zhang Cang and advance Gongsun Chen, but the source supplies no mechanism or independent evidence of staging. His current profile is therefore that of a successful omen entrepreneur, not a proven fabricator.

## Key Characteristics

- Advocate of an earth-virtue classification for the Han dynasty.
- Predictor who attaches a concrete yellow-dragon sign to a disputed cosmological claim.
- Court entrant whose forecast appears confirmed by a later report from Chengji.
- Beneficiary of imperial acceptance through appointment as a博士.
- Participant in proposed changes to ritual, institutions, and official colors.

## Qualifications

The profile rests on one popular-history podcast note rather than a primary-source dossier. The yellow dragon's nature, the report's origin, the precise institutional proposals, and Gongsun Chen's own intent remain unverified here. The host's staging hypothesis is recorded only as speculation, so the page does not classify him as a proven fraud.
