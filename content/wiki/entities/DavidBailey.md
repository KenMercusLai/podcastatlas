# David Bailey

## Overview

David Bailey is the London fashion photographer presented here as a leading maker and celebrity of the Swinging London image.

## Current Profile

Bailey brought an East End background and a more kinetic, informal visual language into a field previously associated with stiffer studio conventions. His work with [[JeanShrimpton]], including the first Sunday Times colour supplement in 1962, helped make photographer, model, clothing, and new print technology parts of the same public spectacle.

## Key Characteristics

- London-born photographer from an East End background.
- Developed his interest in photography during national service in Singapore.
- Used kinetic, informal images that broke with earlier fashion conventions.
- Worked closely with Jean Shrimpton and became a celebrity in his own right.
