# 韩嵩 / Han Song (late Han)

## Overview
韩嵩 / Han Song (late Han) is the Jingzhou-linked official whose mission to [[Xudu|许都]] turns into a test of loyalty between [[LiuBiao|刘表]] and the Han court under [[CaoCao|曹操]].

## Current Profile
The current source presents Han Song as a duty-bound envoy rather than a simple defector. After reaching Xudu, he accepts court appointments, returns to Jingzhou praising the court and Cao Cao, and advises Liu Biao to send a son to court. When Liu Biao threatens execution, Han Song answers that Liu Biao has failed him, not the reverse, because he had warned beforehand that going to court would place him inside the court's recognized order.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves Liu Biao's Jingzhou regime but treats the Han court's recognized title order as morally weightier than local patronage.
- Accepts court appointment as attendant-in-ordinary and Lingling administrator after the Xudu mission.
- Frames his choice through "圣达节，次守节": he cannot flex above norms like a sage, so he keeps fixed duty.
- Publicly pressures Liu Biao toward court alignment by praising Cao Cao and urging a son-at-court signal.
- Survives the execution threat but is imprisoned after Liu Biao finds no proof of concrete betrayal.
- Functions as a trigger exposing Liu Biao's suspicion and Jingzhou elite anxiety before Guandu.
