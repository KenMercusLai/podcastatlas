# Amoy Food / 淘大

## Overview
Amoy Food / 淘大 appears in the source as the Hong Kong-linked condiment and frozen-food brand acquired by [[HaitianFlavoring|海天味业]].

## Current Profile
The episode positions Amoy as an older overseas-channel and localization asset. Its value to Haitian lies in brand history, distribution networks, and operating knowledge built through decades of overseas expansion.

## Key Characteristics
- Brand originating in Xiamen in 1908 before moving headquarters to Hong Kong.
- Product portfolio includes soy sauce, condiments, and frozen dim sum in the source account.
- Overseas expansion asset with past production bases and supermarket/restaurant channels abroad.
- Acquired brand inside Haitian's outbound strategy.
