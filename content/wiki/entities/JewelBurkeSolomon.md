# Jewel Burke Solomon

## Overview
Jewel Burke Solomon is a [[CollabCapital]] investor and recurring [[MarketplaceTech]] guest who interprets technology-business developments through financing, operational control, platform strategy, and measurable adoption.

## Current Profile
Across three Bytes appearances, Solomon moves between infrastructure finance and applied technology governance. She reads [[Alphabet]]'s long-duration borrowing as a commitment whose returns still need testing, treats early agent-social systems as interesting but insecure, and argues that AI-assisted code should receive production review.

The newest source brings those themes together through proof and discipline. AI lenders are slowing to reassess physical execution and repayment; streaming companies such as [[Netflix]] must prove efficient mature economics; and employers increasingly evaluate AI proficiency through judgment and business outcomes rather than raw usage.

## Key Characteristics
- Technology-business commentator connecting finance, product adoption, media, and operational risk.
- Treats AI infrastructure as a long-horizon commitment exposed to construction, power, demand, and repayment constraints.
- Favors human review and guardrails when AI enters production engineering or sensitive systems.
- Distinguishes technology exposure or usage from durable user, employee, or business value.
- Uses market corrections as information without treating every repricing as proof of sector collapse.
