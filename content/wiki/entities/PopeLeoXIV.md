# Pope Leo XIV

## Overview
Pope Leo XIV appears in the wiki through an All-In discussion of a source-described AI encyclical that frames artificial intelligence as a moral and institutional power question rather than a neutral tool.

## Current Profile
In this source, Pope Leo XIV is used as a religious-institutional counterparty to Silicon Valley's AI power. The hosts summarize his position as neither anti-technology nor accelerationist: AI can be useful, but its effects depend on the values of the builders, financiers, controllers, and institutions that govern deployment.

## Key Characteristics
- He is presented as treating AI as a technology whose moral effect depends on human control and institutional incentives.
- His source-described safeguards include worker retraining, child safety, guardrails, and autonomous-weapons limits.
- His intervention is compared with Leo XIII's 1891 response to the Industrial Revolution.
- His position becomes a foil for All-In's debate over whether AI regulation protects human dignity or creates centralized control.
