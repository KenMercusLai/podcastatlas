# Ethan Lyon

## Overview
Ethan Lyon is the principal AI engineer interviewed in [[ep-53-cognitive-surrender-the-ai-engineering-mistake-that-kills-projects|Data Science With Sam EP53]]. The supplied source presents him as a practitioner who has built and discarded multiple data and AI architectures and who uses those failures to explain trustworthy conversational analytics, structured knowledge graphs, planning-first development, and cognitive surrender.

## Current Profile
Lyon's engineering approach treats trust as an architectural outcome. Reliable AI needs constrained access, documented schemas, stable business definitions, repeatable evaluation, and a human who understands the system well enough to question unusual output. His personal workflow applies the same principle before coding: explore requirements through dialogue, turn them into text and diagrams, red-team the architecture, then implement.

He also treats memory as a governed structure rather than an unlimited archive. His rebuilt knowledge graph represents people, companies, and tasks explicitly, matches new evidence against existing entities, and uses surrounding context to reduce mistaken merges and duplicate records.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats data ownership, context, semantics, and evaluation as primary enterprise AI bottlenecks.
- Uses authenticated, constrained tool access instead of allowing agents to search unrestricted business data.
- Advocates golden datasets and repeatable good, bad, and edge-case tests for agents and skills.
- Distinguishes learning-supportive cognitive offloading from work delegated without understanding.
- Plans through requirements, architecture, questioning, and adversarial review before implementation.
- Designs knowledge graphs around explicit types, identity resolution, and controlled ingestion.
