# Steve Fredette

Steve Fredette is a [[Toast]] co-founder in [[toast-aman-narang-how-a-long-wait-for-the-dinner-check-launched-a-2-billion-business-6eca81bc-b0e1-47e2-a87d-f3b243e76a97]]. [[AmanNarang]] met him at [[Endeca]], and the source says Fredette left after the [[Oracle]] acquisition before Aman did.

In the episode, Steve is part of the technical founding pair that first built the phone-based payment app with [[JohnGraham]]. That app did not become the durable product, but it exposed the integration and reliability problems behind restaurant systems and pushed the team toward [[RestaurantOperatingSystem]] and [[MissionCriticalRestaurantSoftware]].
