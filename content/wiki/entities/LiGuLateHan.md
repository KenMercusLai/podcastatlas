# 李固 / Li Gu (late Han)

李固 / Li Gu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] as [[LiXieLateHan|李燮]]'s father and the earlier official behind the Luoyang saying that "the father would not establish an emperor and the son would not establish a king." The source recalls Li Gu's opposition to installing the later Emperor Huan, preferring [[LiuSuanLateHan|刘蒜]] as an older and more capable candidate.

The page is source-scoped to that succession-memory role. Li Gu matters in this ingest because his earlier refusal supplies a family and political template for Li Xie's later objection to restoring [[LiuXuAnpingPrince|刘续]].
