# eBay

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-gamestop-ceo-ryan-cohens-56b-plan-to-take-over-ebay-41752370]] turns eBay from background career context into the target of a contested [[GameStop]] acquisition proposal. [[RyanCohen|Ryan Cohen]] argues that eBay remains a valuable marketplace with first-mover advantage and strength in collectibles, refurbished technology, and secondhand goods, but that it has undergrown broader ecommerce and allowed seller tooling, live commerce, and cost discipline to weaken.

The source presents Cohen's plan as a three-part strategy: remove about $2 billion of cost, fix [[MarketplaceLiveCommerce]], and build an [[InGameAssetMarketplace]] around useful game-native assets. Cohen also argues that GameStop's store footprint could become [[StoreNetworkMarketplaceInfrastructure]] for sellers, creators, fulfillment, photography, and [[AuthenticationLedMarketplaceTrust|authentication-led trust]]. These claims are one side of a contested public-company transaction, so seller dissatisfaction, cost-cutting room, management incentives, and financing certainty remain source-scoped.

eBay appears in [[socialradarspod-tonyxu-final]] as the company where [[TonyXu]] worked before Stanford. The episode names [[BobSwan]] as the CFO Xu worked for and [[JohnDonahoe]] as the CEO who encouraged Xu to apply to business school.

The source does not analyze eBay's marketplace model. Its role is mainly biographical: eBay gave Xu exposure to senior operators before he moved into [[StanfordUniversity]], [[StanfordStartupGarage]], [[PaloAltoDelivery]], and eventually [[DoorDash]].
