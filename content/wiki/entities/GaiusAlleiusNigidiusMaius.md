# Gaius Alleius Nigidius Maius

## Overview

Gaius Alleius Nigidius Maius is a Pompeian notable whose partial biography has been assembled from a rental notice, advertisements for amphitheatre games, and a probable tomb inscription discovered across roughly two centuries.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Maius to show how dispersed inscriptions can recover a life absent from continuous narrative. A notice first connected his name to property rental; later advertisements linked him to games; and a recently found monumental tomb inscription probably associates him with a banquet for nearly seven thousand people and a public role during Nero's ban on games at [[Pompeii]]. The identification is cumulative and probable rather than complete.

## Key Characteristics

- Pompeian property holder known first through a rental notice.
- Organizer or sponsor associated with amphitheatre games.
- Probable owner of a monumental tomb with an unusually detailed public inscription.
- Example of biography reconstructed from scattered evidence rather than a surviving life narrative.

## Evidence

- Rental and games evidence: [[16-pompeii-glt3349011345]] links the name found in 1810 to later notices concerning amphitheatre events.
- Tomb evidence: [[16-pompeii-glt3349011345]] reports that a tomb inscription found around 2018 probably belongs to Maius and records a mass banquet and civic intervention.
- Methodological significance: [[16-pompeii-glt3349011345]] emphasizes that the reconstruction required evidence from different locations over about two hundred years.

## Qualifications

The tomb attribution is probable in the episode, not certain. The banquet size, precise office, role during the games ban, chronology of discoveries, and completeness of the reconstructed biography remain source-scoped.
