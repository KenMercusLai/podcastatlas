## Overview
骆友梅 / Luo Youmei is presented as co-founder and operating leader of the [[HongKongEconomicJournal|《信报》]].

## Current Profile
The source corrects a founder-only account of the newspaper. Luo brought media experience and relationships to administration, staffing, financing, and crisis management, helping the paper survive its early cash-flow problems and supporting its long-term operation with [[LinXingzhi|林行止]].

## Key Characteristics
- Co-founded 《信报》 rather than merely assisting its public-facing founder.
- Led important administrative and personnel work.
- Used media experience and networks to support financing and survival.
- Makes the newspaper's history legible as organizational collaboration.

- Operational role: [[504-lin-xingzhi-xinbao-yu-xianggang-jingji-huangjin-niandai-lsqh0b2fi11uc5udp330ibwuyih]] attributes administration, personnel, financing, and network support to her.
