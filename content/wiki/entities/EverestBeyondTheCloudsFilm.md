# 《珠穆朗玛：云中彼岸》

## Overview
《珠穆朗玛：云中彼岸》 is the documentary discussed in the source, centered on [[JimMorrisonSkiMountaineer|吉姆·莫里森]], [[HilareeNelson|希拉里·尼尔森]], a high-risk Everest north-face ski objective, and the meaning of continuing after Nelson's death.

## Current Profile
The interview presents the film as more than an expedition record. The attempted climb and descent create its adventure structure, but Nelson's competing loves, the pain borne by her children and partner, Morrison's bereavement, and director [[JimmyChin|金国威]]'s refusal to prescribe a decision turn it into an inquiry about risk, family duty, identity, friendship, death, and change.

## Key Characteristics
- Begins from a technically severe and potentially fatal Everest north-face objective.
- Keeps family attachment and the consequences of risk visible beside achievement.
- Carries Nelson's presence through Morrison's and Chin's recollections.
- Treats continuation after loss as a personal decision rather than a heroic requirement.
- Leaves the central ethical conflict open rather than supplying one correct value ordering.

## Evidence
- Expedition premise: [[83178c10-cba6-4869-8b69-559da582009c-83178c10-cba6-4869-8b69-559da582009c]] describes a 2018 plan to climb and ski a steep route on Everest's north face.
- Ethical focus: [[83178c10-cba6-4869-8b69-559da582009c-83178c10-cba6-4869-8b69-559da582009c]] frames the film through the conflict among dream, children, partner, and life itself.
- Grief and continuation: [[83178c10-cba6-4869-8b69-559da582009c-83178c10-cba6-4869-8b69-559da582009c]] records Morrison's hope that the film preserves Nelson's authentic character and shows that people can move through devastating change.
