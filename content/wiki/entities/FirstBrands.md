# First Brands

First Brands appears in [[bu-xi-deng-e02-bi-quan-shan-beng-mei-guo-zheng-fu-guan-men-first-brands-po-chan-wa-ha-ha-feng-bo-lt-ghzgmiroo9a-odaih4xkhsulu]] as a U.S. auto-parts company whose bankruptcy turns [[PrivateCreditTailRisk]] into a concrete case. The source describes the company as having real products such as motor oil, wipers, filters, and brake components, and says its revenue grew quickly through acquisitions before financing stress caught up.

The episode uses First Brands to show why a borrower can look safer than it is. Real sales and real products do not remove [[PrivateCreditReceivablesOpacity]] when growth depends on off-balance-sheet financing, receivables borrowing, private credit, and unclear cash controls.
