## Overview

高峻 / Gao Jun is a Guangwu-era Wei-side commander and later holdout whose current profile now has two source-bounded stages. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-703-donghan-kaiguo-hou-zui-xianhe-de-jiazu-pingsha-shi-ta-lgg-fra8ostp2xsuugph6o-ilzac|Hanji 703]] names him as one target of [[MaYuanEasternHan|Ma Yuan]]'s cavalry-backed persuasion mission against [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|Wei Xiao]]'s camp. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-709-mian-dui-bangjia-an-guren-weihe-buguan-renzhi-sihuo-luknor8yw839qwxogeyzwluo6qe2|Hanji 709]] later places him at Gaoping with more than ten thousand troops after the Wei Xiao camp has weakened, and makes his surrender depend on [[KouXunEasternHan|寇恂]]'s execution of [[HuangfuWenEasternHan|皇甫文]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 703 supplies Gao Jun's earliest current placement. He appears as one of the Wei-side commanders whom Ma Yuan visits or targets while moving with more than five thousand cavalry. The source does not report Gao Jun's response, so this layer is evidence of persuasion pressure and faction-fracture strategy, not yet evidence of surrender.

Hanji 709 then uses Gao Jun as a stubborn but psychologically vulnerable holdout. Militarily, he still has an elite fortified position and enough force that [[GengYanEasternHan|耿弇]] has reportedly besieged him for a year without taking the city. Politically, he no longer appears as an expanding rival; he is a remaining node whose surrender can complete a local pacification problem.

The decisive feature is dependence on advice and morale. Gao Jun sends Huangfu Wen to meet Kou Xun, and Huangfu Wen's hard reply signals that the holdout's resistance is still intellectually and emotionally organized. Once Kou Xun kills Huangfu Wen and sends back the surviving deputy with an ultimatum, Gao Jun surrenders the same day. The current synthesis therefore treats Gao Jun less as an independent strategist than as the defeated subject of a [[MouDestructionStrike|伐谋式斩首]].

## Key Characteristics

- Guangwu-era anti-Han holdout associated with Gaoping.
- Wei Xiao-side commander first visible as a target of Ma Yuan's cavalry-backed persuasion.
- Military defender with a strong city, elite troops, and a long siege history.
- Politically pressured target whom Liu Xiu considers important enough to address.
- Adviser-dependent actor whose confidence collapses after Huangfu Wen is killed.
- Surrender case showing how coercion can work through morale and planning rather than city assault alone.

## Qualifications

This page is not a complete biography of Gao Jun. Hanji 703 only establishes him as a persuasion target in Ma Yuan's anti-Wei mission and does not establish his response. It keeps the Wei Xiao alignment, Gaoping geography, force count, and siege duration source-bounded to Hanji 709 until corroborating notes are ingested. Transcript forms 高俊 and 高郡 are normalized to 高峻 based on the Guangwu-era Gao Jun surrender context.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 703 as the earlier Ma Yuan persuasion-target layer.
- Preserved Hanji 709 as the substantive holdout side of Kou Xun's forced-surrender case.
- Kept Gao Jun's response to Ma Yuan source-scoped while retaining the later same-day surrender evidence.
