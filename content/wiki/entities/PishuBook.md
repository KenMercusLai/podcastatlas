# 《屁书》

[[PishuBook|《屁书》]] is the Dutch children's book used by [[162-pishu-xianliao-renjian-zhi-qi-yitian-jici-zui-jiankang-938004270]] as a permissive frame for discussing flatulence. The episode says the book approaches a socially embarrassing subject with humor, turning a childish joke into a route through body knowledge, language, etiquette, religion, performance, law, and animal behavior.

The book's role in the wiki is not as a full literary close reading. It functions as a low-threshold object: because farting is both universal and socially awkward, a children's book can let adult hosts talk about [[FlatulenceAsHealthSignal]], [[BodilyTabooHumor]], and [[CivilizedBodyDiscipline]] without making either shame or seriousness the only available tone.

## Key Claims
- A children's book can make a stigmatized body function discussable without turning it into either pure vulgarity or medical panic.
- The source uses the book as a bridge from ordinary curiosity into history, culture, and animal science.
- The episode keeps the book's humor compatible with practical caution: bodily functions are normal, but social settings and health warning signs still matter.
