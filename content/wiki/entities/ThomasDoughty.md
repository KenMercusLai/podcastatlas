# Thomas Doughty

Thomas Doughty appears in [[693-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-englands-greatest-hero-part-3-glt5069845329]] as a gentleman and secretary to [[ChristopherHatton]] who joins [[FrancisDrake]]'s 1577 expedition. His presence creates class and command tension with Drake, a non-gentleman sea captain operating under a politically sensitive and partly deniable mission.

The episode says Drake tries giving Doughty commands, including the Pelican, but relations worsen through accusations, mutinous remarks, and Drake's suspicion of witchcraft. At San Julien, Drake puts Doughty on trial for treason and incitement to mutiny on 30 June.

Doughty's demand to see Drake's commission exposes the [[PiracyPrivateeringBoundary|piracy-privateering boundary]] at the center of the voyage: Drake has no written commission from [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]]. Doughty is found guilty, shares a final meal with Drake, and is beheaded.
