# W. T. Stead

## Overview

W. T. Stead was a prominent late-Victorian newspaper editor, campaigner, sensationalist, and spiritualist who traveled aboard [[RMSTitanic|RMS Titanic]] in [[429-titanic-countdown-to-disaster-part-3-glt1273657377]].

## Current Profile

The episode uses Stead to connect Titanic's passenger world to mass journalism and the eccentric cultural atmosphere of late-Victorian and Edwardian Britain. His public campaigns ranged from General Gordon and naval spending to the “Maiden Tribute” investigation, illustrating how popular journalism could combine reform, agitation, and sensation. His spiritualism, including a family ghost story recounted by the hosts, adds a belief system that later disaster memory can easily make seem prophetic even though the episode supplies no such proof.

## Key Characteristics

- Influential newspaper editor associated with populist and sensational campaigning.
- Public agitator on imperial, naval, and sexual-exploitation issues.
- Example of mass media's growing ability to create political pressure.
- Committed spiritualist whose beliefs formed part of his public and family world.
- Titanic passenger linking the ship to journalism, reform culture, and fin-de-siecle belief.
