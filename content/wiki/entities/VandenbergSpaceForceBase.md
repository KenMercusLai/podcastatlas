# Vandenberg Space Force Base

Vandenberg Space Force Base appears in [[tech-20260818-tech-pod-128-tech-20260818-tech-pod-128]] as a U.S. launch site in [[California]], named alongside the [[CapeCanaveral]] area and [[SpaceX]]'s Gulf Coast site in [[Texas]]. The source uses it as part of the existing launch-site inventory before arguing that companies still want more places to launch.

The page connects Vandenberg to [[SpaceLaunchCapacityBottleneck]] and [[SpaceportSitingConstraint]] because the episode treats launch geography as a scarce operating input for [[SpaceEconomyInfrastructure]], not merely a list of bases.
