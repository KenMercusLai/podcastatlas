# Rohingya

The Rohingya appear in [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]] as the community whose persecution complicates [[AungSanSuuKyi]]'s democratic-icon status. The episode says she defended the army against genocide charges over atrocities against the Rohingya.

The source uses the Rohingya issue to prevent a one-dimensional hero narrative. Aung San Suu Kyi can be both a detainee whose proof of life matters and a former leader whose record contains grave political failures. That tension is the core of [[DemocraticIconComplexLegacy]].
