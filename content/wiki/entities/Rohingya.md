# Rohingya

## Overview
The Rohingya are a persecuted community from [[Myanmar]] whose denial of citizenship, forced displacement, and legal recognition appear across the wiki's Myanmar coverage.

## Current Profile
The earlier source uses persecution of the Rohingya to complicate [[AungSanSuuKyi]]'s democratic-icon status and to warn against allowing attention to one detained leader to displace a wider population. The new episode adds the source-reported [[InternationalCourtOfJustice]] judgment that Myanmar committed genocide. Survivors describe the ruling and the court's use of “Rohingya” as recognition and restored dignity, while continuing war, destroyed homes, territorial fragmentation, and distrust make return and reparation uncertain.

## Key Characteristics
- Myanmar denied the Rohingya official-minority status and citizenship according to the new source.
- The 2017 clearance operation drove more than 700,000 people into Bangladesh according to the episode.
- The court judgment supplies legal recognition of genocide and of the community's chosen name.
- Recognition does not by itself provide safe return, homes, compensation, or citizenship.
- Rohingya persecution remains central to evaluating Aung San Suu Kyi's political legacy.

## Evidence
- Political-legacy evidence: [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]] says Aung San Suu Kyi defended the army against genocide charges and uses that record to resist a one-dimensional hero narrative.
- Atrocity and displacement evidence: [[6593564fdb356af5bba0c4c825a6b361-6593564fdb356af5bba0c4c825a6b361mp3aid6b0029dc322405712ca22bffchidd556eb54-6160-4281]] reports killing, sexual violence, attacks on children, forced flight, citizenship denial, and a finding of genocidal intent.
- Recognition evidence: [[6593564fdb356af5bba0c4c825a6b361-6593564fdb356af5bba0c4c825a6b361mp3aid6b0029dc322405712ca22bffchidd556eb54-6160-4281]] records survivors' response to the judgment and the significance of the court naming the Rohingya.
- Remedy constraints: [[6593564fdb356af5bba0c4c825a6b361-6593564fdb356af5bba0c4c825a6b361mp3aid6b0029dc322405712ca22bffchidd556eb54-6160-4281]] links uncertain return to continuing fighting, destroyed homes, distrust, and limited enforcement power.

## Qualifications
Both sources are journalistic episode summaries rather than the judgment, underlying evidence record, refugee survey, or implementation report. Numbers, holdings, intentions, and remedy prospects remain source-attributed. The new judgment changes the legal-status evidence in the wiki, but it does not establish that ordered remedies have been implemented.
