# Nicholas Eberstadt

Nicholas Eberstadt is the North Korea expert cited in [[getting-entrepreneurial-in-korea-summer-school]]. The source uses him to emphasize the scale gap between [[SouthKorea|South Korea]] and [[NorthKorea|North Korea]], including the claim that South Korea's economy is vastly larger and that even Wyoming has a bigger economy than North Korea.

Eberstadt also supplies the warning that North Korea's limited market growth may have helped finance a faster nuclear-testing tempo. His role in the wiki is to keep [[DonjuMarketClass]] and [[MarxistLeninistStateCapacity]] inside regime incentives rather than treating market emergence as automatic liberalization.
