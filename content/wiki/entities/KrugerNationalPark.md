# Kruger National Park

## Overview
Kruger National Park is the public Safari setting in the episode's South Africa route, paired with private parks to compare wildlife experience, traveler appetite, and visible labor patterns.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Kruger and nearby private Safari parks as a major reason South Africa feels unusually rich as a destination. 王妈妈 and 老王 spend three days and two nights on Safari, see the major sought-after animals, and experience both awe and fatigue.

Kruger also helps the episode notice social structure through tourism labor. 王妈妈 says public-park guides and drivers were mostly black, while private-park staff were mostly white, using this as one more travel-facing clue that [[PostApartheidStructuralInequality]] remains visible in who performs which kind of work.

## Key Characteristics
- Public national-park Safari setting in the South Africa itinerary.
- Wildlife-viewing entry point suitable for travelers testing their appetite for Safari.
- Source of intense scenic and emotional moments around sunrise, sunset, and close animal encounters.
- Travel experience whose repetition can become tiring for less animal-focused visitors.
- Tourism-labor setting where racialized public/private staffing patterns become noticeable.
