# 《康熙来了》 / Kangxi Coming

## Overview
《康熙来了》 / Kangxi Coming is represented as a Taiwanese variety-show memory whose wiki role has widened from a single example of [[XiaoS|小S]]'s visible female imperfection into a broader case of era-bound media chemistry. The current evidence treats the show as culturally durable because it combined [[CaiKangyong|蔡康永]]'s structuring presence, Xiao S's comic looseness, permissive variety-show conditions, and later short-video afterlife.

## Current Profile
In [[129-ruhe-chengwei-zhenzheng-de-buwanmei-zhuyizhe-hunshen-shi-jiner-799220151]], the show appears as a memory of public female looseness. [[QinZong|秦总]] uses Xiao S on 《康熙来了》 to imagine a woman who can be funny, imperfect, relaxed, and still self-possessed, making the show a counterexample to gendered image control and perfectionist avoidance.

The media-history and public-memory layer now matters as much as the anti-perfectionist example. [[LuoYonghao|罗永浩]] treats the show as a hard-to-replace peak in Chinese-language entertainment, while Cai emphasizes that Xiao S's talent worked in a specific pairing and era. The current evidence also notes the show's continued life through short-video circulation among younger viewers, while acknowledging that many of its jokes might not survive present-day correctness constraints.

## Key Characteristics
- Variety show remembered as a high-impact Chinese-language entertainment program rather than only one performer showcase.
- Media setting where [[XiaoS|小S]] became a visible model of funny, imperfect, relaxed female self-possession.
- Host-pair chemistry between [[CaiKangyong|蔡康永]] and Xiao S is treated as central, with Cai providing structure and Xiao S providing rare comic liveliness.
- Era-bound program whose looseness depended on television conditions and correctness boundaries that may not be reproducible now.
- Archive-afterlife case where short-video circulation lets younger audiences encounter an older variety-show form.
