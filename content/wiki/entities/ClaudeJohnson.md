# Claude Johnson

Claude Johnson appears in [[vol-268-liang-ge-lao-si-lai-si-1003563933]] as the commercial operator who helped connect [[CharlesRolls]] and [[HenryRoyce]] and turn early Rolls-Royce engineering into a sellable luxury brand. The episode treats him as the third part of the founding triangle rather than a minor administrator.

His source role is to show that high-end industrial brands are not built from engineering alone. Customer access, performance demonstration, commercial packaging, and brand discipline helped make Rolls-Royce's early cars legible as expensive, scarce, and reliable.
