# 刘濞 / Liu Bi (King of Wu)

刘濞 / Liu Bi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-189-liubang-siqian-zuihou-yibo-lllydj2xyk4cr7b9d2qx0hvfw-dz|Hanji 189]] as [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s nephew, the son of [[LiuXiDaiKing|刘喜]]. After [[YingBu|英布 / 黥布]]'s defeat, Liu Bang changes [[JingKingdomEarlyHan|荆国]] into [[WuKingdomEarlyHan|early Han Wu Kingdom]] and makes Liu Bi king of Wu over three commanderies and fifty-three counties.

The episode does not yet narrate Liu Bi's later career. His immediate role is institutional: Liu Bang is still converting exposed southern and eastern territory into Liu-family royal assignments after removing dangerous or defeated vassal branches.
