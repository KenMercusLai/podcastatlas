## Overview

樛乐 / Jiu Le is identified in Hanji 352-1 as [[QueenDowagerJiuNanyue|樛太后]]'s brother and the co-leader sent with [[HanQianqiuWesternHan|韩千秋]] and two thousand troops into [[Nanyue|南越]]. The following episode appears to preserve his death-and-heir-reward branch under an uncertain transcription, so the identity link remains qualified.

## Current Profile

Hanji 352-1 supplies Jiu Le's family and mission roles. His kinship ties the Han expedition directly to the queen dowager's endangered court faction, while Han Qianqiu supplies the volunteer military leadership. Their entry into Nanyue triggers Lü Jia's open rebellion and the murder of the royal party and Han envoys.

Hanji 352-2 then says a slain envoy rendered “樛罗” has a son rendered “樛广德” who receives the Longkang marquisate. The sequence and shared surname make continuity with Jiu Le plausible, but the supplied summaries do not provide the underlying text needed to settle the name forms or prove the identification.

## Key Characteristics

- Brother of Queen Dowager Jiu.
- Co-leader of the two-thousand-person Han mission into Nanyue.
- Kinship bridge between Han intervention and the pro-Han royal faction.
- Plausible but textually uncertain match for the slain envoy whose son is rewarded in Hanji 352-2.

## Qualifications

The supplied Hanji 352-1 summary writes the name as “纠勒,” normalized here to the received form 樛乐. Hanji 352-2 instead gives “樛罗” and “樛广德” without textual apparatus. The wiki therefore does not treat Jiu Le's death, the son's name, or the reward relationship as fully settled. His earlier career, exact command authority, actions inside Nanyue, and personal position on incorporation are absent.
