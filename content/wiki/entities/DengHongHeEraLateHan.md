## Overview

邓鸿 / Deng Hong (He-era commander) is a disambiguated Eastern Han frontier commander whose current profile now spans two Xiongnu campaigns. Hanji 761 places him in the three-route Northern Xiongnu expedition from Guyang Pass, while Hanji 769 later sends him against the [[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]] Fenghou rebellion and records his execution for delay and lost opportunity.

## Current Profile

Hanji 761 supplies the earlier Northern Xiongnu layer. During [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]]'s campaign, Deng Hong serves as Duliao general and leads one of the three planned routes from Guyang Pass toward Zhuoye Mountain. His role is not the central battlefield command in the source, but it marks him as part of the large mobilization that surrounds the [[JiluoshanBattleLateHan|Jiluoshan]] victory.

Hanji 769 places this Deng Hong in Emperor He's later campaign response after fifteen Southern Xiongnu tribes rebel and force Fenghou, son of a previous chanyu, into the chanyu position. [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]] orders Deng Hong to act as cheqi jiangjun and campaign with Feng Zhu, Zhu Hui, Han troops, border forces, [[Wuhuan|Wuhuan]], and [[Xianbei|Xianbei]] contingents.

The second campaign defeats parts of the rebel force, but Fenghou escapes beyond the passes and Han troops cannot continue pursuit. After the army returns, Deng Hong is accused of delaying and missing the military opportunity. The source says he is imprisoned and executed, making his profile a contrast between earlier participation in a successful campaign and later post-campaign accountability.

## Key Characteristics

- He-era military commander linked to both Northern and Southern Xiongnu operations.
- Duliao general in the three-route expedition against Northern Xiongnu.
- Later commander against the Southern Xiongnu Fenghou revolt.
