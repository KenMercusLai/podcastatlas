## Overview

邓鸿 / Deng Hong (He-era commander) is the Hanji 769 frontier commander sent against the [[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]] Fenghou rebellion and later executed for delay and lost military opportunity. This page is intentionally distinct from [[DengHongLateHan|Deng Hong]], the already indexed Deng-family figure tied to a later source.

## Current Profile

Hanji 769 places this Deng Hong in Emperor He's 95 CE campaign response after fifteen Southern Xiongnu tribes rebel and force Fenghou, son of a previous chanyu, into the chanyu position. Emperor He orders Deng Hong to act as cheqi jiangjun and campaign with Feng Zhu, Zhu Hui, Han troops, border forces, [[Wuhuan|Wuhuan]], and [[Xianbei|Xianbei]] contingents.

The campaign defeats parts of the rebel force, but Fenghou escapes beyond the passes and Han troops cannot continue pursuit. After the army returns, Deng Hong is accused of delaying and missing the military opportunity. The source says he is imprisoned and executed, making him a compact case of post-campaign accountability rather than battlefield glory.

## Key Characteristics

- He-era military commander linked to the Southern Xiongnu Fenghou revolt.
