# 严纲 / Yan Gang (late Han)

严纲 / Yan Gang (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-960-caowei-dingji-zhinang-xunyu-weihe-qi-yuan-tou-cao-lmor1txqpm88itt4incalusconjr|Hanji 960]] as [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]]'s appointed Jizhou inspector during [[JieqiaoBattleLateHan|界桥之战]]. The episode says Yan Gang is killed when [[QuYiLateHan|麹义]]'s infantry and crossbow formation breaks Gongsun Zan's charge.

Yan Gang's role is narrow but useful. His death makes the battle's political stakes visible: Jieqiao is not only a casualty count or a tactical demonstration, but a blow against Gongsun Zan's attempt to project appointment authority into the Jizhou field against [[YuanShao|袁绍]].
