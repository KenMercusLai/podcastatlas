# Mercury Insurance

## Overview
Mercury Insurance is a private insurer used by the source to illustrate how direct observation of wildfire mitigation can alter underwriting judgments that broad classifications or remote data had missed.

## Current Profile
In [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-043ffe0bc2c7516bac64132229755e93-43026861-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-043ffe0bc2c7516bac64132229755e93-43026861]], Mercury executives toured [[ParadiseCalifornia|Paradise]] after [[JennyGoodland]] challenged the industry's failure to recognize local hardening. The carrier then offered [[CaseyTaylor]] a much cheaper policy and announced a limited return aimed initially at a few hundred hardened homes.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as the episode's example of insurer learning from site-level evidence.
- Reassessed homes after comparing actual mitigation with broad risk classifications.
- Became the first major carrier since the Camp Fire, in the episode's account, to announce new Paradise policies.
- Limited its initial offer rather than restoring town-wide coverage.
- Later employed Goodland to help other communities reduce wildfire risk and seek private insurance.
