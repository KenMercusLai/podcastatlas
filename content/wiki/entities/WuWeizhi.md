# 吴伟志 / Wu Weizhi

吴伟志 is the [[ZhongouRuibo|中欧瑞博]] investor interviewed in [[a-gu-de-chun-xia-qiu-dong-zhongshu-zhongliang-zhongcai-ljzicmfbl9guxoqmcdd8ikdn6jbx]]. The source presents him as an A-share practitioner whose framework starts from market cycles, company classification, and strategy fit rather than from a single slogan about value, growth, or trend.

His main contribution in the episode is to make investing language operational. [[AShareMarketSeasons|A 股四季框架]] is used to decide broad exposure and sector temperature; [[ASharePlantingCompanyTaxonomy|种树、种粮、种菜]] separates growth, value, and more tactical company types; and [[InvestmentStrategyFit|投资策略适配]] warns against entering a position for one reason and defending it with another.

The source also presents Wu as an organization builder. He describes 中欧瑞博's investment committee, hedging capability, quant and AI use, [[MAPERInvestmentResearchFramework|MAPER]] research, five research groups, and [[ResearchIndexPortfolioConstruction|research indexes]] as ways to turn individual research into repeatable portfolio behavior.
