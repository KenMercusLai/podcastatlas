# Carrie Buck / 卡丽·巴克

Carrie Buck / 卡丽·巴克 appears in [[37-zhishang-ceshi-qingwen-ni-shi-zhili-poluomen-ma-608020679]] as the source's central American forced-sterilization case. The episode says Buck became pregnant after being raped by a relative of her foster family, was framed as a "feeble-minded" pregnant girl, and was then drawn into institutional and legal proceedings that ended in sterilization.

The source emphasizes procedural and gendered injustice. Buck's lawyer was connected to the institution, later evidence undermined claims that she or her daughter Vivian were intellectually disabled, and the case shows how [[ForcedSterilizationByEugenics|forced sterilization by eugenics]] could punish sexual victimization while presenting itself as public welfare.
