# 雍齿 / Yong Chi

雍齿 / Yong Chi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-4-jiuquangui-zhangliang-ruhe-zai-liubang-shouxia-anshen-lp6qwg3ma5hdy15g4box7pe23jav|Qinji 129-4]] as the man [[LiuBang|刘邦]] leaves to hold [[FengCounty|丰邑 / 丰县]] during his early anti-Qin expansion. The episode says Yong Chi has followed Liu Bang but has never respected him as a superior.

When [[ZhouShiRebel|周市]]'s Wei-side force sends people to recruit him, Yong Chi uses Liu Bang's absence to hand Feng over. Liu Bang returns, attacks Feng, and fails to retake it, making Yong Chi's defection an early example of Liu Bang's vulnerability before his later state-founding network becomes secure.

The page keeps the episode's explanation source-scoped. Yong Chi's betrayal is not treated as random opportunism alone; the source connects it to status disdain, local military exposure, and the separate-state pull created by [[AntiQinCoalitionFragmentation|反秦阵营诸侯化分裂]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-2-lishi-jilu-shouci-tucheng-lm4kuzdpnqn-hicyuvvb4p2wdudm|Qinji 130-2]] gives the immediate reversal. After [[XiangLiang|项梁]] grants Liu Bang five thousand men and ten fifth-rank commanders, Liu Bang attacks [[FengCounty|丰邑 / 丰县]] again and finally succeeds. Yong Chi flees to Wei, turning the earlier betrayal into a temporary but serious setback rather than a permanent loss.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-174-liubang-weihe-dafeng-zijide-chouren-2-luet7gqkako8idt9o3ccqq7cgpbt|Hanji 174 part 2]] returns to Yong Chi after Liu Bang has become emperor. The source transcript writes the name as "庸耻," but the described old hatred and Shifang marquis reward point to the same figure. [[ZhangLiang|张良]] advises [[LiuBang|刘邦]] to enfeoff Yong Chi first precisely because Liu Bang hates him; once other generals see even Yong Chi rewarded for merit, their fear of being forgotten or punished can subside.
