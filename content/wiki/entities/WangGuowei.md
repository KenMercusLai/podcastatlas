# 王国维

王国维 enters the wiki through [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] as one of the figures around the [[TsinghuaGuoxueInstitute|清华国学研究院]]. The episode does not make him the central biography, but his death becomes a turning point for the institute and for [[LiangQichao|梁启超]] and [[ChenYinke|陈寅恪]].

The source treats Chen Yinke's memorial writing for Wang as a compressed statement of scholarly independence. Wang therefore anchors the episode's link between modern Chinese academic institutions and the defense of "free thought" as more than a slogan.

## Key Claims
- Wang Guowei's death is presented as a shock that changed the institutional atmosphere around Qinghua scholarship.
- His memorialization by Chen Yinke gives the episode its explicit academic-independence language.
- The source uses him to connect classical learning, modern institutions, and [[AcademicFreedom]].
