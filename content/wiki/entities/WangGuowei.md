# 王国维

## Overview

王国维 appears across the current sources as a modern Chinese scholar linking excavated evidence, transmitted texts, classical learning, and institutional independence.

## Current Profile

[[244-shuxie-gei-guoqu-weilai-de-ren-yu-shenming-zhongguo-wenming-zhong-de-shuxie-chuantong-yu-changqi-zhuyi-8225426843-437457]] emphasizes Wang's comparison of oracle-bone names with the *Shiji*'s Shang genealogy, presenting it as a major evidentiary advance whose success must not be generalized beyond what the material supports. [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] places his death and memorialization within the [[TsinghuaGuoxueInstitute|清华国学研究院]], where [[ChenYinke|陈寅恪]]'s memorial language makes him an anchor for [[AcademicFreedom|scholarly independence]].

## Key Characteristics

- Compares excavated inscriptions with transmitted historical writing to test early Chinese history.
- Represents evidence expansion rather than permission for unrestricted inference.
- Connects classical scholarship to modern research institutions.
- Becomes a memorial symbol for independent thought after his death.

## Evidence

- Oracle-bone comparison - [[244-shuxie-gei-guoqu-weilai-de-ren-yu-shenming-zhongguo-wenming-zhong-de-shuxie-chuantong-yu-changqi-zhuyi-8225426843-437457]] describes his identification of Shang ancestral and royal names across inscriptions and *Shiji*.
- Method boundary - [[244-shuxie-gei-guoqu-weilai-de-ren-yu-shenming-zhongguo-wenming-zhong-de-shuxie-chuantong-yu-changqi-zhuyi-8225426843-437457]] warns that Shang mutual support does not automatically establish claims about Xia history.
- Institutional setting - [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] places Wang around the Qinghua institute and treats his death as a turning point in its atmosphere.
- Memorial afterlife - [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] connects Chen Yinke's memorial writing to an explicit defense of free thought.

## Qualifications

Neither source provides a full intellectual biography or systematic account of Wang's “double evidence” method. The Shang example establishes a bounded success, not a general rule that transmitted claims become true when archaeology is available.
