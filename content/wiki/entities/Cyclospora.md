# Cyclospora

Cyclospora is the parasite behind the outbreak framing in [[big-lettuce-meets-small-intestine]]. The [[PlanetMoney]] source says more than 1,600 people had been infected in a July 2026 outbreak, with many sick people apparently having eaten at [[TacoBell]] and the [[FoodAndDrugAdministration|FDA]] tracing the likely source to shredded lettuce from [[TaylorFarms]].

The episode treats Cyclospora as a practical regulatory problem because scientists still do not fully understand where it hides, how it spreads, or how best to test for it on vegetables. That uncertainty makes the parasite central to [[FoodbornePathogenUncertainty]] rather than only a named cause of illness.

## Key Claims
- The source says the current outbreak was still unresolved at the time of recording on July 22, 2026.
- The FDA believed the tainted lettuce came from a farm in [[Mexico]], according to the episode's time-stamped account.
- The parasite's uncertain transmission makes prevention harder for farms, processors, importers, and regulators.
