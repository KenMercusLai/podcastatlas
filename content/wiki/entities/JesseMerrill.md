# Jesse Merrill

## Overview
Jesse Merrill is the co-founder and CEO of [[GoodCulture]], a dairy company built around reviving cottage cheese for contemporary consumers.

## Current Profile
Merrill’s operating profile combines experiential marketing with broad early-stage company work. Film-production and brand-ambassador jobs led into Teany, Honest Tea, and Activate Drinks, where he learned sampling, retail sales, storytelling, procurement, supply chain, and financial discipline. He later applied those lessons to Good Culture’s narrow retail rollout, package revision, creator outreach, and manufacturing expansion.

His founder thesis is that persistence and repeated preparation increase readiness for favorable timing without controlling it. The company’s viral lift therefore appears as an accelerator of years of work, while capacity shortfalls show that attention alone does not complete scale.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects brand storytelling to street-level sampling and observed consumer response.
- Treats distribution, expiration dates, manufacturing, and unit economics as part of brand building.
- Uses failures at Activate Drinks to argue for slower hiring, stronger fundamentals, and disciplined spending.
- Reframed an existing food’s latent nutritional benefits instead of inventing an entirely new proposition.
- Continued as CEO after the L Catterton majority investment.
- Describes luck as consequential but preparation and grit as the controllable foundation.
