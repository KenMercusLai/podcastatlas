# Battle of the Nile

## Overview

The Battle of the Nile was the 1798 British naval victory at Aboukir Bay that destroyed or captured most of the French battle fleet supporting Napoleon's Egyptian expedition.

## Current Profile

The French anchored behind shallows believed to protect one flank, but [[HoratioNelson]] sent British ships around that position and attacked from both sides. The destruction of the French fleet, including the explosion of *L'Orient*, stranded the expedition and turned an apparent land conquest into strategic isolation.

## Key Characteristics

- Followed a prolonged British search after the French armada evaded interception.
- Exploited an assumed geographic protection as an attack route.
- Used double-sided engagement against an anchored fleet.
- Destroyed French maritime mobility without immediately ending the occupation.
- Became a foundation for Nelson's aggressive naval reputation before Trafalgar.
