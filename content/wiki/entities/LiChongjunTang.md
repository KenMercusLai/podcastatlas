## Overview

Li Chongjun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-536-3-jiemi-shangguan-waner-he-ta-beihou-de-nanrenmen-lqj7rpwwfllogrxrbav-xqwwnnnm|Hanji 536-3]] as the crown prince whose failed coup kills [[WuSansi|武三思]] and nearly reaches [[ShangguanWaner|上官婉儿]]. The episode uses him as the shock that pushes Shangguan Wan'er away from Wu-Wei reliance and toward Li-Tang fallback planning.

## Current Profile

The source says Li Chongjun was bullied by Wu Sansi's family and Princess Anle, then launched a coup that killed Wu Sansi and his son. His troops then moved to arrest Shangguan Wan'er. She fled to [[TangZhongzong|李显]] and framed the threat as escalating from herself to the empress and emperor; Zhongzong's public response helped break the coup, and Li Chongjun was killed by his own men.

## Key Characteristics

- Crown prince whose resentment against Wu Sansi and Princess Anle turns into coup action.
- Kills Wu Sansi and his son in the episode's account.
- Threatens Shangguan Wan'er directly because of her connection to the Wu-Wei faction.
- His failed coup teaches Shangguan Wan'er that her current patron network may not protect her next time.
- Serves as the hinge between Shangguan Wan'er's Wu-Wei alliance and Li-Tang fallback strategy.
