# 傅斯年

傅斯年 enters the wiki through [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] as a historian, organizer, and uncompromising institutional actor. The episode links him to the founding and operation of the [[InstituteOfHistoryAndPhilology|历史语言研究所]], the [[Yinxu|殷墟]] excavations, later university administration, and public criticism of political figures.

The source presents a tradeoff: Fu's greatest work may have been building and defending scholarly institutions, but that labor repeatedly interrupted his own writing. His late Taiwan University years are narrated through strict public-resource ethics, physical exhaustion, concern for poor students, and a final collapse after a day of meetings and public attack.

## Key Claims
- Fu Sinian is treated as a designer of institutions more than a scholar measured only by personal output.
- His role in [[InstituteOfHistoryAndPhilology|史语所]] makes modern archaeology and language-history research organizationally possible.
- The episode links his public criticism of politicians to [[ScholarOfficialPublicDuty]] without presenting him as simply a politician.
- His final years dramatize the human cost of [[ModernChineseAcademicInstitutionBuilding]].
