# 美团浣熊食堂 / Meituan Huanxiong Shitang

Meituan Huanxiong Shitang appears in [[vol-270-da-shi-dai-liu-zai-le-ta-de-shi-dai-1006063835]] as [[Meituan]]'s platform-led foodservice space. The episode describes it as a canteen-like format that brings branded restaurants into stall-like spaces and can partly replace the demand once served by office-park or commercial-property food courts.

The source's caveat is that platform entry can raise the operating bar for existing food-city owners. Renovation standards, environmental requirements, app-linked systems, and new fees may add traffic and brand value, but they can also make payback harder for operators already facing weaker office crowds.

[[kafeidou-chuantong-meishi-guangchang-jielian-bidian-dashidaimen-yudao-naxie-fazhan-zuai-1007530222]] adds a more operational description: Meituan leases the venue, finishes decoration, utilities, and stall infrastructure, then rents units to restaurants built mainly for delivery. This makes the format closer to platform-organized foodservice infrastructure than to a neutral food court.
