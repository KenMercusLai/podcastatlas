# YSL Beauty / 圣罗兰美妆

YSL Beauty / 圣罗兰美妆 appears in [[lvdian-xiaoyang-yao-tuiguang-tihuanxin-de-ba-meizhuang-chongzuo-yibian-1006757937]] as one of [[LOreal|L'Oreal / 欧莱雅]]'s brands with refillable beauty products. The episode mentions YSL cushion products as part of L'Oreal's broader refill portfolio.

Its wiki role is to show that [[RefillableBeautyPackaging]] applies beyond jars and bottles. Cushion formats can have larger price-gap room for refills, but they still need a replacement mechanism that preserves sealing, cleanliness, and premium feel.
