# Jonathan Brill

## Overview
Jonathan Brill is represented by one [[TomorrowToday]] interview as an inventor, futurist, industrial designer, and adviser on innovation, resilience, and systemic change.

## Current Profile
Brill approaches products, organizations, and forecasts as linked systems. He starts with physical and economic constraints, tests whether an invention can be made, sold, timed, and scaled, and combines frontline observation with top-down strategic analysis. His AI outlook favors distributed capability and flatter coordination while retaining human responsibility for framing, exceptions, trust, and accountability.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses [[PhysicalConstraintForecasting]] to bound forecasts through measurable capacity and deployment conditions.
- Evaluates applied invention through [[WholeSystemInnovationViability]] rather than desirability alone.
- Treats recurring disruption as a strategic design condition through [[ShockReadyStrategicResilience]].
- Distinguishes feature innovation, category invention, systemic disruption, and operational scaling.
- Connects non-linear, graph-like thinking with deliberate work on sequencing, communication, meditation, and complementary collaborators.
- Expects AI to distribute context and decision capability while preserving human judgment and corporate accountability.
