# Natalie Crawford

## Overview
Natalie Crawford is the fertility and reproductive-health physician featured in a [[HubermanLab]] episode on female fertility, hormone health, IVF, egg freezing, and reproductive planning.

## Current Profile
In the current wiki evidence, Crawford is a public medical educator whose Huberman Lab appearance reframes fertility as a whole-body health signal. Her emphasis is early access to reproductive information: AMH testing, ovulation tracking, semen analysis, pregnancy-loss evaluation, and clearer understanding of what IVF and egg freezing can and cannot do.

Her profile is clinically boundary-conscious. She gives practical categories such as "trimester zero," the five non-negotiables of sleep, stress, muscle, food, and toxins, and risk priorities around cannabis, nicotine, plastics, NSAIDs around ovulation, and endocrine disruptors, but the source keeps individualized treatment decisions inside clinician-guided care.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames fertility as a marker of hormonal, metabolic, cellular, inflammatory, and long-term health.
- Advocates earlier reproductive data access, including AMH, ovulation tracking, and semen analysis.
- Separates ovarian reserve, egg quality, and IVF/egg-freezing attrition so fertility decisions are not reduced to slogans.
- Treats pregnancy loss and secondary infertility as evaluation-worthy rather than dismissible if prior conception occurred.
- Uses lifestyle guidance around sleep, stress, muscle, food quality, toxins, cannabis, nicotine, and inflammation while preserving medical boundaries.
- Discusses emerging interventions such as GLP-1s, human growth hormone, PRP, red light, and NAD-related compounds as context-dependent rather than universal fertility tools.
