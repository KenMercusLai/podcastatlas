## Overview

殷皇后 / Empress Yin is the Emperor-He-era empress whose jealousy, reported threat against the Deng family, wugu accusation, deposition, and family punishment make [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Deng Sui]]'s pre-regency danger politically concrete. Her current role remains source-scoped, but Hanji 773 now moves her beyond a harem foil by adding the legal and kinship aftermath of her fall.

## Current Profile

Hanji 775 portrays Empress Yin as the formal empress before Deng's rise. The source contrasts her with Deng through conduct and status management: Yin is described as small in stature and sometimes mocked by attendants for behavior that fails court etiquette, while Deng quietly covers for her rather than joining the ridicule.

The conflict intensifies when [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]] increasingly favors Deng. Yin's jealousy is presented not as private resentment only, but as potential lethal family politics. During Emperor He's illness, she reportedly says that if she gains power she will not leave the Deng family alive. Deng hears this, despairs, and nearly kills herself before a palace maid's false report of the emperor's recovery interrupts the attempt.

Hanji 773 adds the earlier 102 CE deposition mechanics. It says Yin's favor had declined after Deng entered as guiren, then repeats the threat against the Deng family and connects Yin's fall to a wugu accusation involving her maternal grandmother Deng Zhu. Emperor He assigns Zhang Shen and Chen Bao to investigate; the case produces severe kin punishment, including deaths under torture, Yin Gang's suicide, exile of relatives to Rinan, and Yin's removal to Tong Palace.

The current synthesis is therefore sharper than simple jealousy. Yin's fall shows how harem rivalry can become legal-political catastrophe when future dowager power, natal-family revenge, and wugu language combine. It also marks the opening through which Deng's restraint becomes legible as political capacity rather than only personal virtue.

## Key Characteristics

- Formal empress in Emperor He's court before Deng Sui's appointment.
- Rival whose jealousy turns Deng's palace favor into Deng-family survival risk.
- Negative foil for Deng's self-effacing etiquette and restraint in Hanji 775.
- Actor in an early Eastern Han wugu case whose investigation destroys or exiles members of her and Deng Zhu's family network.
- Deposed on 102-06-22 and moved to Tong Palace in Hanji 773's account.
- Source-scoped harem actor whose broader biography and independent defense are not yet developed in the wiki.

## Qualifications

The current page is bounded to two podcast source notes and preserves their negative portrayal cautiously. It does not reconstruct Empress Yin's independent defense, the full formal charge record, or the complete kinship network around Yin and Deng Zhu. Hanji 773 strengthens the deposition and punishment layer, but the wiki still treats exact investigative procedure, torture details, and the truth of the wugu accusation as source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 773 as the wugu-deposition and family-punishment layer behind Yin's fall.
- Reframed Yin from a jealousy foil alone into an example of harem rivalry becoming legal and kinship catastrophe.
