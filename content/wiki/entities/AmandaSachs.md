# Amanda Sachs

## Overview

Amanda Sachs is the architect presented as spending two to three years arranging the 2,982 names at the [[NationalSeptember11Memorial]].

## Current Profile

Sachs implemented [[MichaelArad]]'s principle of meaningful adjacency. She first grouped names by workplace, flight, or emergency-response affiliation, then tried to place people near others with whom they had significant relationships.

Her work makes a name layout into relational information architecture: identity is preserved not only by inscription but by proximity to other lives.

## Key Characteristics

- She translated thousands of names and relationship requests into a physical memorial layout.
- She combined institutional grouping with personal adjacency rather than relying on alphabetical order.
- Her work treated relational context as part of individual identity.
- Some requested relationships could not be satisfied because spatial and grouping constraints conflicted.
