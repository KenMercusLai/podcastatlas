# Kim Jong Un

Kim Jong Un appears in [[getting-entrepreneurial-in-korea-summer-school]] as the North Korean ruler who declared goals of building the weapons program and growing the economy. The source says his regime allowed more room for [[DonjuMarketClass|donju]], permitted entrepreneurship and policy training through figures such as [[AndreAbrahamian]], and saw local businesses and foreign investment grow for a time.

The episode also treats that opening as regime-bounded. [[NicholasEberstadt]] argues that added prosperity likely helped fund a higher tempo of nuclear testing, while the host calls Kim the "ultimate donju" after describing weapons sales to [[Russia]] and cryptocurrency theft. This makes Kim's economic role a case of market energy being captured for state power rather than protected through [[PropertyRightsAsInvestmentIncentive]].
