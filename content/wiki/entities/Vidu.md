# Vidu

Vidu is the [[ShengshuTechnology]] video model/product family discussed in [[kuai-yidian-zai-kuai-yidian-kuai-dao-shijie-neng-shishi-shengcheng-he-shengshu-keji-zhang-jintao-liao-vidu-s1-tuili-jiasu-shishi-jiaohu-shipin-lsb53bqrjojiadnlq2qe4sta-b13]]. The episode focuses on [[ViduS1]], a real-time interactive product built around streaming generated video rather than only producing finished clips.

The source uses Vidu to make a broader point about [[VideoModels]]: as generation becomes faster, the product question shifts from sample quality alone toward live sessions, voice-driven interaction, video understanding, latency, and cost.
