## Overview

皇甫文 / Huangfu Wen is Gao Jun's adviser and negotiation representative in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-709-mian-dui-bangjia-an-guren-weihe-buguan-renzhi-sihuo-luknor8yw839qwxogeyzwluo6qe2|Hanji 709]]. The episode makes him the crucial planning-and-morale node: [[KouXunEasternHan|寇恂]] kills him, sends his deputy back to [[GaoJunEasternHan|高峻]], and thereby forces the holdout's surrender.

## Current Profile

Hanji 709 gives Huangfu Wen a brief but structurally important role. He is not described through a long biography; he appears because Gao Jun sends him to meet Kou Xun and because his unyielding reply shows that Gao Jun's resistance still has a confident adviser at its center.

Kou Xun reads Huangfu Wen as more than a messenger. The episode's "上兵伐谋" interpretation turns Huangfu Wen into the object of a [[MouDestructionStrike|mou-destruction strike]]: by killing the person who supplies Gao Jun's counsel and confidence, Kou Xun destroys the holdout's plan before storming the city. Huangfu Wen therefore matters as a strategist-target, not merely as a casualty.

## Key Characteristics

- Gao Jun's adviser in the Gaoping holdout case.
- Negotiation representative sent to meet Kou Xun.
- Unyielding speaker whose stance signals that Gao Jun is not ready to submit.
- Strategic node whose removal breaks the holdout's planning and morale.
- Victim of a norm-straining execution in an envoy/adviser context.
- Source-bounded example of how a named adviser can become the real center of resistance.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to one episode and one source note. It does not establish Huangfu Wen's wider career, rank, or culpability beyond his role as Gao Jun's adviser. Because he is killed after being sent to speak with Kou Xun, the page keeps [[EnvoyKillingTaboo|不斩来使]] as a qualifying relationship even though the episode's focus is strategy rather than diplomatic law.
