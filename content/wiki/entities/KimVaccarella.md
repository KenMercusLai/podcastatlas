## Overview
Kim Vaccarella is the founder of [[BoggBag]] and the interview subject in [[bogg-bag-kim-vaccarella-the-100-million-business-she-almost-abandoned-e1aadd32-1dc4-43fb-b654-32148b3f6cee]]. The episode presents her as a self-taught physical-product founder whose path ran through financial pressure, service work, commercial real estate lending, patents, factory trouble, family strain, and control-sensitive scaling.

## Current Profile
Kim's current profile in the wiki is a founder who repeatedly converted constraints into operating judgment. She improvised pricing and sales before formal startup infrastructure existed, shut down after a defective shipment, restarted when customer pull appeared, and later chose investor support that preserved majority control over immediate full liquidity.

## Key Characteristics
- Kim's money sensitivity and work intensity are tied in the source to a financially constrained childhood and years of practical work.
- She pursued patents and manufacturing before she fully understood product-company building.
- She used rejection and manufacturing failure as emotional catalysts, though the same intensity also created personal strain.
- She grew Bogg Bag through face-to-face selling, small retailers, trade shows, social media, and persistent reorder work.
- She treated control as a major decision criterion when evaluating acquisition and investment offers.
