# Keith Richards

## Overview

Keith Richards is the Rolling Stones guitarist whose blues friendship and songwriting partnership with [[MickJagger]] became the band's creative center before his Redlands country house became a stage for drug, class, and celebrity conflict.

## Current Profile

Richards came from a more working-class Dartford background, attended Sidcup Art College after expulsion from school, and reconnected with Jagger through shared blues records. Their early group joined [[BrianJones]]'s project, but original songwriting and [[AndrewLoogOldham]]'s management elevated Jagger-Richards over the founder's blues-purist direction.

Richards was later charged with allowing cannabis smoking and initially received twelve months before his conviction was overturned. His respectable country house captured the class contradiction inside the rebellious image. His relationship with Anita Pallenberg deepened Jones's isolation, while the Jagger-Richards partnership supported the blues-rooted musical reboot and later rock identity.

## Key Characteristics

- Blues records and art-school culture shaped his route into the band.
- His partnership with Jagger became the band's governing and creative center.
- Redlands made his home a stage for drug and class anxiety.
- Initial punishment was severe relative to the uncertain premises charge.
- Private historical interests complicated the decadent image.
- His relationship with Pallenberg intensified Jones's exclusion.
