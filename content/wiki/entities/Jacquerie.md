# Jacquerie

## Overview

The Jacquerie is the peasant uprising that spreads north of Paris during France's post-Poitiers collapse and turns military defeat into a wider crisis of social and noble authority.

## Current Profile

The episode places the revolt alongside Parisian revolution, royal captivity, civil war, and free-company violence. It spreads through pockets including Amiens and Normandy rather than appearing as a single conventional front.

[[CharlesTheBad|Charles the Bad]] suppresses it and temporarily wins noble support. Its role in the narrative is therefore double: it demonstrates how far order has broken down, and it becomes an opportunity for an elite rival to present himself as defender of the nobility.

## Key Characteristics

- Peasant uprising north of Paris after Poitiers.
- Revolt spreading through multiple local pockets.
- Symptom of collapsing military and social authority.
- Threat that temporarily unifies nobles behind Charles the Bad.
- Crisis nested inside civil and international war.
