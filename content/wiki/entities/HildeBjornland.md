# Hilde Bjornland

Hilde Bjornland is the Norwegian economics professor guiding [[how-to-beat-the-resource-curse-in-norway-summer-school]]. She frames [[Norway]] as a useful but imperfect case: the country is wealthy and has a broad welfare state, yet still debates productivity, entrepreneurship, public spending, wealth taxes, technology, and AI.

Her main contribution is the institutional reading of Norwegian luck. Bjornland explains [[DutchDisease]], the resource curse, [[FreeRiderProblem|free riders]], the role of social trust, and why the [[NorwegianOilFund]] cannot simply be spent as current income.
