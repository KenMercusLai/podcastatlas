# Konrad Adenauer

## Overview

Konrad Adenauer appears as the West German chancellor who argued for restoring *Das Lied der Deutschen* as the national anthem.

## Current Profile

The episode places Adenauer on the continuity side of the postwar anthem dispute. Against [[TheodorHeuss]]'s concern that the song was Nazi-tainted, Adenauer argued for restoring the familiar anthem and eventually secured a compromise in which [[WestGermany]] used only the third verse.

## Key Characteristics

- West German chancellor associated with restoring the older anthem.
- Political actor prioritizing continuity with pre-Nazi German national tradition.
- Negotiator in the third-verse compromise with Heuss.

## Evidence

- Restoration pressure: [[679-germany-the-song-hitler-stole-part-3-glt6217148052]] says Adenauer argued for restoring *Das Lied der Deutschen* after West Germany initially lacked an agreed anthem.
- Political conflict: [[679-germany-the-song-hitler-stole-part-3-glt6217148052]] contrasts Adenauer's position with Heuss's concern that the song was Nazi-tainted.
- Compromise outcome: [[679-germany-the-song-hitler-stole-part-3-glt6217148052]] says Adenauer eventually persuaded Heuss to approve the song with only the third verse sung.
