## Current Profile

Wang Shun's current profile is narrow. After Wang Mang usurps the throne, Wang Shun's heart-palpitating illness worsens under fear and worry, and he eventually dies. The narrative then turns from his death to Wang Mang's institutional response: appointing former high officials as four teachers and adding multiple祭酒 posts for the crown prince's educational and ritual entourage.

His significance is therefore contextual rather than biographical. Wang Shun marks an early Xin court stress point and helps frame Wang Mang's reliance on ritualized office titles and respected figures after a senior position opens.
