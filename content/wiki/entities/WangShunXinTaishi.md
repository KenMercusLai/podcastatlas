## Overview

王舜 / Wang Shun is synthesized as a Wang Mang-aligned senior assistant whose page now spans pre-accession regency staffing and post-usurpation court stress. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-629-liujiaren-shui-kangqi-fankang-wangmang-de-daqi-lmhws7-jptdh9tf45bqtlfvzdw55|Hanji 629]] places him in 6 CE as taifu and left assistant when [[WangMang|王莽]] installs [[RuziYing|孺子婴 / 刘婴]] as crown prince. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-644-shui-gan-he-huangdi-shizhe-taojiahuanjia-lkgn0idimbu6twz-symr5012f8sn|Hanji 644]] later presents him as Wang Mang's taishi after the Xin usurpation and uses his death as a court-transition point before Wang Mang expands the crown prince's teacher-friend and祭酒 offices.

## Current Profile

Wang Shun's current profile is still narrow, but no longer only terminal. Hanji 629 shows him inside Wang Mang's regency apparatus: he receives one of the major assistant posts created around Liu Ying's crown-prince status, helping make the child-ruler arrangement look institutionally staffed. Hanji 644 then supplies the later post-usurpation endpoint: his heart-palpitating illness worsens under fear and worry, and he eventually dies. The narrative turns from his death to Wang Mang's institutional response: appointing former high officials as four teachers and adding multiple祭酒 posts for the crown prince's educational and ritual entourage.

His significance is therefore contextual rather than biographical. Wang Shun marks an early Xin court stress point and helps frame Wang Mang's reliance on ritualized office titles and respected figures after a senior position opens.

## Key Characteristics

- Xin taishi associated with Wang Mang's post-usurpation court.
- Pre-accession taifu and left assistant in Wang Mang's 6 CE regency staffing.
- Dies after fear and illness worsen under the new regime.

- Remains a source-scoped court figure until additional evidence expands his profile.
