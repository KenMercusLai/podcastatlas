## Overview

王舜 / Wang Shun is synthesized as a Wang Mang-aligned senior assistant whose page now spans Wang-family succession operations, pre-accession regency staffing, and post-usurpation court stress. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-604-xihan-wangmang-duoquan-hou-ruhe-qingxi-quanli-geju-ltwscyyiobc1ocvgti3fd-deajru|Hanji 604]] places him at the first post-Aidi transition, when [[WangMang|王莽]] has his cousin made chariot-and-cavalry general and sends him with Zuo Xian to bring the Zhongshan king Liu Jizi, later [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]], to Chang'an. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-629-liujiaren-shui-kangqi-fankang-wangmang-de-daqi-lmhws7-jptdh9tf45bqtlfvzdw55|Hanji 629]] later places him in 6 CE as taifu and left assistant when Wang Mang installs [[RuziYing|孺子婴 / 刘婴]] as crown prince. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-644-shui-gan-he-huangdi-shizhe-taojiahuanjia-lkgn0idimbu6twz-symr5012f8sn|Hanji 644]] then presents him as Wang Mang's taishi after the Xin usurpation and uses his death as a court-transition point before Wang Mang expands the crown prince's teacher-friend and祭酒 offices.

## Current Profile

Wang Shun's current profile is still narrow, but no longer only terminal. Hanji 604 shows him as a trusted Wang-family operator in the succession handoff after Aidi: his appointment and mission make Wang-family presence visible in the act of bringing the new heir to Chang'an. Hanji 629 then shows him inside Wang Mang's regency apparatus, receiving one of the major assistant posts created around Liu Ying's crown-prince status and helping make the child-ruler arrangement look institutionally staffed. Hanji 644 supplies the later post-usurpation endpoint: his heart-palpitating illness worsens under fear and worry, and he eventually dies. The narrative turns from his death to Wang Mang's institutional response: appointing former high officials as four teachers and adding multiple祭酒 posts for the crown prince's educational and ritual entourage.

His significance is therefore contextual rather than biographical. Wang Shun marks an early Xin court stress point and helps frame Wang Mang's reliance on ritualized office titles and respected figures after a senior position opens.

## Key Characteristics

- Xin taishi associated with Wang Mang's post-usurpation court.
- Wang-family operator in the first post-Aidi heir-transfer mission.
- Pre-accession taifu and left assistant in Wang Mang's 6 CE regency staffing.
- Dies after fear and illness worsen under the new regime.
- Functions in the episode as a transition into Wang Mang's crown-prince office expansion.
- Remains a source-scoped court figure until additional evidence expands his profile.

## Qualifications

The source set does not provide Wang Shun's complete career or direct evidence about his inner politics. Hanji 604 supports his 1 BCE heir-transfer mission, Hanji 629 supports his 6 CE regency-office placement, and Hanji 644 supports his later illness and death. The page keeps him source-scoped as a Wang Mang court figure whose offices frame succession handling, title design, and staffing expansion.
