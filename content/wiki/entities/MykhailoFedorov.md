# Mykhailo Fedorov

[[kyiv-brawl-ukraines-top-general-fired-6a609aec4fac21e67f1be165]] presents Mykhailo Fedorov as the reformist Ukrainian official whose dismissal by [[VolodymyrZelensky]] triggered public protests and exposed a clash over [[Ukraine]]'s wartime military model. The source says Fedorov had previously served as minister of digital transformation and was well regarded by Ukraine's allies before being described as the dismissed defence minister in this episode.

Fedorov's wiki significance is [[UkraineMilitaryCommandReform]] through technology and incentives. The source credits him with scaling Ukraine's drone use and changing procurement so more effective brigades could receive more drones and ammunition, turning him into the central actor for [[DroneProcurementIncentives]].

The episode also presents Fedorov as a political problem for Zelensky. Because he publicly criticized [[OleksandrSyrskyi|Oleksandr Sersky]] and had public support, his dismissal became part of [[WartimeLeadershipAccountability]] rather than a narrow cabinet matter.

[[bombs-and-bombshells-zelensky-under-pressure-6a86d3561ad8716bb64646f0]] extends that political problem after the dismissal. The source says Fedorov called for a presidential election, reused language once associated with support for Zelensky, and hinted at corruption among those close to the president. The wiki keeps the source spelling "Mikhail Fyodorov" as an alias while preserving [[MykhailoFedorov]] as the canonical page.
