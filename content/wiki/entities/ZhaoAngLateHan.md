## Overview

赵昂 / Zhao Ang is a late-Han Liangzhou official presented through the resistance to [[MaChao|马超]] around Ji. His role joins local military participation to an extreme family-collateral decision: he and [[WangYiZhaoAngWife|王异]] send their son Zhao Yue as Ma Chao's hostage and continue supporting the uprising despite the risk.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1137 places Zhao Ang in the conspiracy before open action. [[YangFuLateHan|杨阜]] and [[JiangXuLateHan|姜叙]] recruit him as an outside military participant while Zhao Qu and Liang Kuan prepare to act inside Ji. Because Zhao Ang controls force, Ma Chao demands Zhao Yue as a hostage; Zhao Ang asks [[WangYiZhaoAngWife|王异]] whether refusal would reveal the plot.

Hanji 1138 supplies the answer and outcome. Zhao Ang sends Zhao Yue after Wang Yi supports accepting the risk, participates in the Qishan position while Ji is closed against Ma Chao, and continues the resistance before Ma Chao kills the hostage. The source presents this as loyalty, but the transferred family cost remains part of the profile rather than incidental background.

## Key Characteristics

- Local late-Han official aligned with resistance to Ma Chao around Ji.
- Outside military participant recruited into a plan coordinated with insiders at Ji.
- Husband of Wang Yi and father of Zhao Yue, whom Ma Chao holds as a hostage.
- Participates in the Qishan branch while internal collaborators close Ji against Ma Chao.
- Embodies a loyalty judgment whose political objective and family cost cannot be separated.

## Qualifications

The current profile rests on two sequential structured popular-history summaries rather than a primary-source collation. Zhao Ang's office, troop control, exact operational sequence, dialogue, family deliberation, and responsibility for accepting Zhao Yue's exposure require broader verification. “Loyalty” records the sources' moral framing and does not erase the ethical problem of transferring mortal risk to a child.

## What Changed

- Established Zhao Ang as a disambiguated late-Han entity.
- Joined his resistance role to the hostage cost borne by Zhao Yue.
- Backfilled his recruitment, outside-force role, and pre-decision fear that refusing the hostage demand would expose the conspiracy.
