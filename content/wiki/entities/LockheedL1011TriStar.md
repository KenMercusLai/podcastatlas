# Lockheed L-1011 TriStar

The Lockheed L-1011 TriStar is the wide-body aircraft project in [[vol-268-liang-ge-lao-si-lai-si-1003563933]]. The episode presents it as [[LockheedCorporation|Lockheed]]'s civil-aircraft comeback attempt, competing against the [[McDonnellDouglasDC10|McDonnell Douglas DC-10]] while facing the broader market shadow of [[Boeing]]'s 747.

The TriStar's key source role is dependency. Lockheed marketed the aircraft around performance, weight, efficiency, and cabin economics that depended on the [[RollsRoyceRB211|RB211]] engine. Once RB211 ran late, the TriStar could not easily swap to another engine without reworking the aircraft, making it the episode's main [[AirframeEngineLockIn]] case.

The episode says the delay helped the DC-10 reach market earlier and left TriStar sales below the level Lockheed needed for break-even. It then connects the aircraft to the [[LockheedBriberyScandal]] because overseas orders, including the [[AllNipponAirways|All Nippon Airways]] purchase in [[Japan]], became more important after U.S. market momentum weakened.
