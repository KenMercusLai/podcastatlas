# 《阅微草堂笔记》

《阅微草堂笔记》 enters this wiki branch through [[28-liaozhai-zhiyi-buwei-junwang-chang-zange-pianxiang-cangsheng-shuo-guihua-587843930]], where [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] compares [[JiYun|纪昀]]'s strange-tale notebook with [[PuSongling|蒲松龄]]'s [[LiaozhaiZhiyi|《聊斋志异》]]. Both works contain ghosts, foxes, and moral anecdotes, but the episode treats their temperaments as different.

In the source's contrast, 《阅微草堂笔记》 is more orthodox, scholar-official, and notebook-like. Ji Yun can write strange events, but he is presented as less willing to inhabit the interiority of fox women, ghosts, commoners, and fantasy-driven readers. That contrast helps the source explain why 《聊斋》 feels psychologically and socially more unruly.
