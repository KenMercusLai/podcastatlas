# Against the Rules

## Overview

Against the Rules is [[MichaelLewisAuthor|Michael Lewis's]] investigative podcast series, represented here by a season examining the rapid expansion and platform design of legal U.S. sports betting.

## Current Profile

The bounded source presents the season as an investigation of a new “sports gambling industrial complex” joining mobile operators, professional leagues, sports media, lobbyists, and state governments. Its method combines institutional reporting with market history and bettor biography, especially the careers of [[RoxyRoxborough]] and [[RufusPeabody]].

The production's stated refusal of advertising money from [[DraftKings]] and [[FanDuel]] becomes part of its editorial position: the season argues that heavy operator spending can discourage critical scrutiny in sports media.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses individual market participants to explain changes in institutions and product design.
- Treats legalization as a change in incentives and information power, not merely legal status.
- Examines advertising, lobbying, taxation, league partnerships, and customer data together.
- Centers the contrast between older price discovery and modern account-level customer selection.
- Takes an explicitly skeptical stance toward the mobile sportsbook industry.
