## Overview

苏仆延 / Supuyan is a [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] leader first mentioned in Yuan Shao's northern-rear settlement and later killed by [[GongsunKangLateHan|公孙康]] after fleeing the White Wolf Mountain defeat with the Yuan brothers.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1032 places Supuyan among the Wuhuan leaders favored by [[YuanShao|袁绍]] with chanyu seals after [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]]'s fall. Hanji 1079 supplies the endpoint of that alignment: after [[TadunLateHan|蹋顿]] is killed and Wuhuan is defeated, Supuyan accompanies [[YuanShang|袁尚]] and [[YuanXiLateHan|袁熙]] to Liaodong. Gongsun Kang then treats the refugees as a danger to his own autonomy, kills all three, and sends their heads to [[CaoCao|曹操]].

## Key Characteristics

- Wuhuan leader inside Yuan Shao's post-Gongsun-Zan northern settlement.
- Recipient of Yuan-side chanyu recognition in the current evidence.
- Refugee after the White Wolf Mountain defeat and Tadun's death.
- Companion of Yuan Shang and Yuan Xi in the flight to Liaodong.
- Killed by Gongsun Kang when the host-refugee alignment becomes a security risk.

## Qualifications

The Hanji 1079 transcript renders the name inconsistently as forms such as "素铺丸/素铺炎." This page uses 苏仆延 / Supuyan as the cautious canonical route already reflected in Hanji 1032's normalized summary. His precise title, independent policy, and the mechanics of his death remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Created a stable identity connecting Yuan Shao's Wuhuan recognition policy to the Liaodong refugee killings.
