# Red Bull

Red Bull is the energy-drink company profiled in [[hard-cedar-lebanon-seeks-trumps-help-6a5f41e97bddc0bea5542de5]]. The episode presents it as a brand-media machine built around extreme sports, football, [[FormulaOne]], events, and cultural spectacle rather than a conventional beverage manufacturer.

The source says [[DietrichMateschitz]] founded Red Bull in 1984 after adapting a Thai energy tonic for European tastes. It describes Red Bull as having roughly 28% of the global energy-drink market, selling 14bn cans last year, employing 22,000 people in 178 countries, and spending about a third of revenue on marketing.

The company's strength is also its regulatory exposure. The episode says the brand's extreme-sports and football marketing can appeal to children even though Red Bull says the drink is not recommended for them, making [[EnergyDrinkYouthRegulation]] the clearest challenge discussed.
