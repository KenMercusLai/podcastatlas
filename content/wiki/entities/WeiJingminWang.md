# 魏景湣王 / 魏增 / King Jingmin of Wei

魏景湣王 / 魏增 / King Jingmin of Wei enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-106-jingren-faxian-xinlingjun-de-feizhengchang-siwang-lsj3kd2tb4tqtuvhwvsdwkh-h5el]] as the son and successor of [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]]. The episode places his succession in the same 243 BCE cluster as [[MengAo|蒙敖]]'s attack on [[WeiState|魏国]] and the deaths of both Wei Anxi Wang and [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]].

The source makes Wei Zeng important less for his own action than for the succession environment around him. Because [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] had enormous reputation, recent command prestige, and an independent retainer network, the host argues that Wei Anxi Wang may have seen him as a threat to Wei Zeng's ability to sit securely on the throne. This becomes the political motive inside [[XinlingjunDeathSuspicion|信陵君非正常死亡疑云]].

His accession therefore marks the moment when Wei's internal security logic and Qin's external pressure converge. The episode says [[QinState|秦国]] soon keeps pressing Wei, and by the time [[DongCommandery|东郡]] is created from captured Wei territory, the state has already lost the figure most able to organize a broad anti-Qin response.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-107-zuihou-yici-hezong-faqin-fa-le-ge-jimo-lrzq1qks7z-oromd-5f-dgqjwjhw]] places Wei Jingmin Wang's early reign inside the failed final [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] field. Wei is one of the states in both the Chunshenjun and Pang Nuan traditions, but neither version produces a Qin-facing gain. The source therefore makes his reign part of the transition from coalition hope to [[TerminalHezongFailure|terminal hezong failure]].

The follow-up notices keep Wei under pressure. In 240 BCE Qin attacks Wei and takes Ji; in 239 BCE Wei gives Ye to [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. Those entries extend the Qinji 106 pattern: after Xinlingjun's death and Qin's commandery creation, Wei remains active enough to appear in coalition lists but not strong enough to stop Qin's eastward pressure.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-2-yanguo-cisha-qinshihuang-weihe-hui-xuan-jingke-lsewjrmgup1yanf8ipn-i0ykqwjx]] closes his reign with a brief death notice. [[WeiWangJia|魏王假]] succeeds him, but the episode immediately says Wei is already beyond recovery, making the succession a marker of late Wei exhaustion rather than a new strategic turn.
