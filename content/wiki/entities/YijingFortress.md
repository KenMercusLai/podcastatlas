# Yijing Fortress

## Overview
易京 / Yijing Fortress is Gongsun Zan's late-stage fortified base and final death setting. It begins as a materially strong defensive refuge with trenches, towers, gates, grain, and family concentration, but the wiki now treats it chiefly as a case where physical security cannot compensate for political isolation, broken subordinate trust, and siege adaptation by Yuan Shao.

## Current Profile
Yijing's strategic meaning changes across the bounded sources. It first appears as the place Gongsun Zan enters after losing Youzhou support, where walls and storage substitute for restoring people-based rule. The later siege sources make the structure's failure operational: Gongsun Zan shuts himself behind iron gates, refuses to rescue commanders under attack, plans a desperate fire-signal sortie with outside forces, is deceived when Yuan Shao intercepts the letter, and then faces tunnel-and-fire siege work that collapses the tower line.

## Key Characteristics
- Fortified refuge for Gongsun Zan after Youzhou's legitimacy and coalition situation turns against him.
- Material security system built around trenches, towers, gates, stored grain, and concentrated household protection.
- Command-isolation device that limits adviser access, field initiative, and shared-risk obligations.
- Siege environment where Yuan Shao turns intercepted communications and engineering against the defender.
- Final proof-point for the illusion that walls can replace morale, responsive command, and local support.

- Fortified refuge after political loss: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] frames Yijing as Gongsun Zan's response after Liu Yu's execution, defections, and anti-Gongsun coalition pressure.
- Material security and command isolation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1030-shouwo-hao-pai-gongsunzan-weihe-da-de-xilan-lpkntyov4viqyzgxgcujn36qhv8|Hanji 1030]] emphasize deep defensive works, gates, refusal to receive advisers, and refusal to rescue endangered subordinates.
- Failed relief and siege adaptation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1031-gongsunzan-zhisi-cong-bianjiang-zhanshen-dao-kunshou-zifen-lmqoechccfwmfpmbdx8-tjmnm3jz|Hanji 1031]] shows the intercepted fire-signal plan, failed sortie, tunnel excavation, burning of support timbers, collapse toward the central tower, and Gongsun Zan's death.

## Qualifications
Yijing is not treated as a claim that fortification is inherently irrational. The problem is the substitution of fixed defense for political repair, trusted command, and mobile initiative. The available sources center Gongsun Zan and Yuan Shao; they do not provide a technical engineering history of the fortress.

## What Changed
- Hanji 1031 turns Yijing from a threatened siege site into Gongsun Zan's completed death scene.
- The current profile now includes Yuan Shao's intercepted fire-signal deception and tunnel-and-fire assault.
- The fortress is more clearly tied to failed rescue reciprocity rather than only to static defense.
