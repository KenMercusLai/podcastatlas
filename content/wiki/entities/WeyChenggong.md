# 卫成公 / 卫成侯 / Duke-Marquis Cheng of Wey

卫成公 / Duke Cheng of Wey appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] as Wei Xun, successor to [[WeyShenggong|卫声公]] after the latter dies in 373 BCE.

The page's main function is disambiguation and chronology. It belongs to [[WeyState|卫国]], not [[WeiState|魏国]], and keeps the episode's compact succession notice linkable.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-11-shijie-fengqi-yunyong-2-llzzalowv5vmbvgd6l-jujltkbyf]] revisits the figure through the title [[WeyChenggong|卫成侯]]. The episode says [[Shiji|《史记》]] has the ruler demote from duke to marquis in his sixteenth year, and it reads the move as a small-state attempt to show stronger neighbors that Wey is not claiming equal rank. Because the existing wiki page already has the duke-title form, this page keeps both title stages while flagging the 373/362 chronology issue.
