# Marcelo Caetano

## Overview

Marcelo Caetano succeeded [[AntonioDeOliveiraSalazar|Salazar]] as head of Portugal's [[EstadoNovoPortugal|Estado Novo]] and was overthrown in the [[CarnationRevolution]].

## Current Profile

Caetano appears as a constrained reformer inside an authoritarian system. Limited easing of censorship, trade-union restrictions, and rural pension exclusion did not solve the [[PortugueseColonialWars|colonial-war]] burden, while oil shock and hardline resistance narrowed reform; his 1973 officer-promotion change then helped organize the military constituency that removed him.

## Key Characteristics

- Conservative Catholic academic and institutional successor to Salazar.
- Attempted bounded social and political liberalization without abandoning dictatorship or empire.
- Retreated under hardline and economic pressure after the 1973 oil crisis.
- Misjudged an officer-promotion reform that threatened academy-trained captains' status.
- Surrendered only after senior general [[AntonioDeSpinola|Antonio de Spinola]] arrived to receive it.

## Evidence

- Reform evidence: [[273-portugal-the-carnation-revolution-glt3297148398]] credits Caetano with easing some censorship, permitting independent trade unions, and extending rural pensions.
- Constraint evidence: [[273-portugal-the-carnation-revolution-glt3297148398]] connects his retreat to colonial war, oil shock, and regime hardliners.
- Coup evidence: [[273-portugal-the-carnation-revolution-glt3297148398]] traces junior-officer mobilization partly to Caetano's militia-promotion reform and follows his surrender at Largo do Carmo.

## Qualifications

The source interprets Caetano's personnel reform as a catalytic mistake but does not reduce the coup to that measure; war, fiscal strain, authoritarian closure, and decolonization demands were also decisive.

## What Changed

- Added a profile distinguishing limited liberalization from the structural continuity of dictatorship and empire.

## Relationships

- [[AntonioDeOliveiraSalazar]] - predecessor whose authoritarian system Caetano inherited.
- [[EstadoNovoPortugal]] - regime Caetano tried and failed to reform from within.
- [[ArmedForcesMovementPortugal]] - officer movement catalyzed partly by his promotion reform.
- [[CarnationRevolution]] - coup that ended his government.
