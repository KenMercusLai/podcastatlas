# U.S. v. Khoury

## Overview

U.S. v. Khoury was the federal prosecution of [[AymanKhoury]] arising from a $180,000 payment to Georgetown tennis coach Gordie Ernst connected to [[CatherineKhoury]]'s admission.

## Current Profile

The represented episode centers the government's allegation that the payment and a planned false shoulder injury formed an admissions conspiracy. Catherine's testimony and contemporaneous messages supplied an alternative explanation for why she did not play tennis, and the jury acquitted Ayman Khoury on every count.

## Key Characteristics

- Criminal case joining college admission, athletic recruitment, private payment, and disputed intent.
- Government theory relied in part on immunized witness Timothy Donovan's account of a planned injury excuse.
- Defense theory used Catherine's family rupture and messages to challenge the alleged plan.
- Georgetown's donor-capacity research complicated the institutional narrative but was not admitted through the Salesforce document.
