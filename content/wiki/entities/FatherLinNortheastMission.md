## Overview
林神父 / Father Lin is the priest addressed by the three [[DuFamilyGirls|杜氏女孩]] letters discussed in [[100-nian-qian-dongbei-nvhai-xie-gei-bali-de-xin-duitan-lishixuezhe-liji-putongren-ruhe-chuanguo-da-lishi-3d36f181-94ad-4883-8db3-5f040b5ed45b]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Father Lin through the letters more than through an independent biography. He appears as a priest whose return to Paris for illness around 1870 disrupted a religious and emotional arrangement in [[SantaiZiCatholicVillage|三台子]]. The letters likely did not reach him because he died soon after returning, making him an absent recipient whose role is reconstructed from the girls' writing and the mission archive.

## Key Characteristics
- He is important primarily as the addressee who makes the girls' writing historically legible.
- His relationship with the girls is framed through religious instruction, Catholic kinship language, and longing rather than a simple romantic plot.
- His illness and return to Paris explain why the letters were sent across the mission network.
- His death before delivery turns the letters into archived traces rather than completed correspondence.
- His case shows how one absent person can connect village life, Paris archives, gendered expression, and mission hierarchy.
