# 陈懿 / Chen Yi (Jincheng)

陈懿 / Chen Yi (Jincheng) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] as the Jincheng administrator killed when [[BeigongBoyuLateHan|北宫伯玉]], [[LiWenhouLateHan|李文侯]], and the wider rebel field attack the commandery. His death leads into the siege of [[ZuoChangLateHan|左昌]] at Ji County.

The page is source-scoped to this Liangzhou crisis. Chen Yi's death matters because it turns earlier warning about rebel momentum into a concrete collapse of commandery authority, forcing [[GaiXunLateHan|盖勋]] to decide whether duty requires rescuing a hostile superior.

This route is disambiguated from [[ChenYiLateHan|陈逸 / Chen Yi (late Han)]], the different late-Han figure in [[WangFenLateHan|王芬]]'s coup circle.
