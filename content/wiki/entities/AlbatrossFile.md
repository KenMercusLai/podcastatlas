# Albatross File / 信天翁档案

## Overview

The Albatross File appears in the bounded source as a Singapore National Library exhibition and archival frame built around oral histories and records concerning merger with Malaysia and the 1965 separation.

## Current Profile

The exhibition uses sound, moving image, and testimony from founding leaders to foreground Goh Keng Swee's initiative. Its public-history effect is additive: it moves the national story from pure expulsion toward active self-determination without erasing Lee Kuan Yew's grief, Malaysian federal action, or the collapse of the merger project.

## Key Characteristics

- Takes its name from Goh Keng Swee's literary metaphor for federation becoming a burden.
- Centers oral-history testimony that Lee Kuan Yew reportedly encountered while preparing his memoirs.
- Uses audiovisual exhibition design to make confidential negotiation part of public memory.
- Rebalances agency among Goh, Lee, Malaysian leaders, and structural political conflict.
- Supports a more confident contemporary Singaporean founding narrative.
