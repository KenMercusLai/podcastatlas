# Pandora / 潘多拉

## Overview

Pandora is the deliberately manufactured divine gift in [[Hesiod]] whose attraction, capacities, and harmful purpose make her a literary case about designed desire, technical success, gendered blame, and distributed responsibility.

## Current Profile

In [[369-panduola-shi-renlei-zuizao-de-keji-emeng-ma-hesie-de-gongzuo-yu-shiri-lkr0ibzoden-ip-es5xpnhnbvz7]], [[Zeus]] commissions Pandora as retaliation after [[Prometheus]] restores fire to humanity. [[Hephaestus]] shapes her from earth and water, while other gods add skill, beauty, charm, language, and cunning. She succeeds as designed because [[Epimetheus]] accepts her, but successful persuasion serves the designer's harmful objective rather than proving moral worth.

The episode distinguishes Hesiod's account from the popular curious-woman narrative. Pandora opens a large storage jar, not a small box, and the poem does not provide the later extended scene of resisting and yielding to temptation. Treating her as the sole author of human suffering therefore hides Zeus's plan, the gods' manufacture, Epimetheus's acceptance, and the externalized cost to humanity.

## Key Characteristics

- Manufactured by multiple divine specialists under Zeus's retaliatory objective.
- Designed to attract acceptance through beauty, skill, language, and persuasion.
- Immediate actor in opening the jar without being the sole designer or beneficiary of the plan.
- Later reception target of a gendered curiosity-and-blame narrative not fully supplied by Hesiod.
- Literary analogy for artifacts whose performance can be excellent while their purpose remains harmful.

- Manufacture and objective: [[369-panduola-shi-renlei-zuizao-de-keji-emeng-ma-hesie-de-gongzuo-yu-shiri-lkr0ibzoden-ip-es5xpnhnbvz7]] describes Zeus commissioning a multi-god construction as punishment for humanity.
- Desire design: [[369-panduola-shi-renlei-zuizao-de-keji-emeng-ma-hesie-de-gongzuo-yu-shiri-lkr0ibzoden-ip-es5xpnhnbvz7]] treats her attractive form and persuasive capacities as features intended to secure acceptance.
- Reception and blame: [[369-panduola-shi-renlei-zuizao-de-keji-emeng-ma-hesie-de-gongzuo-yu-shiri-lkr0ibzoden-ip-es5xpnhnbvz7]] distinguishes Hesiod's jar-opening from later stories centered on uncontrollable female curiosity.

## Qualifications

This profile follows one podcast's reading of Hesiod rather than a complete account of Greek variants, cult, iconography, later literature, or biblical reception. Calling Pandora “divine artificial intelligence” is an interpretive analogy; she is a mythic person and artifact, not a historical technology or computational system.

## What Changed

- Established Pandora as a designed participant inside a wider responsibility structure rather than the sole cause of suffering.
- Separated Hesiod's storage jar and sparse motivation from the later “box” and curiosity-centered retelling.

## Relationships

- [[Zeus]] - commissioner who sets the retaliatory purpose of her manufacture.
- [[Hephaestus]] - divine specialist who shapes her body from earth and water.
- [[Epimetheus]] - recipient who accepts her despite Prometheus's warning.
- [[Prometheus]] - fire-giver whose conflict with Zeus precipitates her creation.
- [[WorksAndDays|《工作与时日》]] - poem supplying the labor, jar, disaster, and hope narrative.
- [[DesignedTechnologicalDesire]] - design pattern embodied by attraction used to secure acceptance.
- [[DistributedTechnologyResponsibility]] - framework that prevents responsibility from collapsing onto her alone.
