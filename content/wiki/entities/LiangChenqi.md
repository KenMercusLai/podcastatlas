# Liang Chenqi / 梁琛奇

## Overview
Liang Chenqi / 梁琛奇 is a consumer-product founder and former [[ByteDance]] product leader whose path spans [[Douyin]] social features, an internal real-time social incubation project, [[ByteDanceFLOW]], [[Maoxiang|猫箱]], and the startup [[DongnianYinxian|动念引线]].

## Current Profile
Liang's stated direction is AI-native entertainment that gives broad audiences meaningful, repeatable ways to play, express themselves, and enter worlds designed by other people. His method combines subjective product taste with measurable validation, creator-centered content design, cost discipline, and small-team experimentation.

## Key Characteristics
- Translates subjective taste and human motivation into product hypotheses that engineering and data teams can test.
- Treats entertainment as invention of a compelling activity, not only fulfillment of an already legible user task.
- Uses failed incubation work to reduce the number of simultaneous unverified assumptions in later products.
- Preserves a central role for human creators while using AI to expand authored worlds into real-time branches.
- Connects consumer experience to inference economics, subjective-feedback data, organization design, and focused early supply.
