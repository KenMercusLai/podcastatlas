# Catherine of Valois

## Overview

Catherine of Valois is the French princess whose marriage to [[HenryVOfEngland|Henry V]] gave the [[TreatyOfTroyes|Treaty of Troyes]] its dynastic mechanism.

## Current Profile

Catherine fled Paris with the French royal family as English pressure increased. Her June 1420 marriage to Henry joined the Valois and Lancastrian houses and was meant to make their descendants heirs to separate English and French kingdoms under one crown.

Her coronation in England and the birth of a son in December 1421 appeared to complete that design. Henry's death when the child was nine months old instead turned the settlement into an infant inheritance.

## Key Characteristics

- Daughter of Charles VI and member of the displaced French royal court.
- Dynastic bride at the center of the Troyes settlement.
- Queen crowned in England after entering the Anglo-French regime.
- Mother of the infant heir on whom the dual-monarchy project depended.
