# Aldrich Ames / 奥德里奇·艾姆斯

Aldrich Ames / 奥德里奇·艾姆斯 enters the wiki through [[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] as the [[CIA]] officer whose betrayal of Soviet sources exposed [[OlegGordievsky|Oleg Gordievsky]] to the [[KGB]]. The episode presents him as capable enough to be placed near sensitive Soviet-East European work, but financially pressed, careless, and vulnerable to selling access.

His role makes [[IntelligenceLiaisonRisk]] concrete. [[MI6]] sanitized material from Gordievsky when sharing with the [[UnitedStates]], but American analysts still wanted to identify the British source. Once Ames knew the outline and later sold information to the [[KGB]], the allied intelligence relationship became an exposure path.
