# Aldrich Ames / 奥德里奇·艾姆斯

## Overview

Aldrich Ames / 奥德里奇·艾姆斯 was a [[CIA]] officer who sold information about Soviet sources to the [[KGB]], exposing human networks and demonstrating the danger created when broad institutional access meets weak insider detection.

## Current Profile

[[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] presents Ames as the insider whose access to Anglo-American reporting helped expose [[OlegGordievsky|Oleg Gordievsky]] after CIA efforts to identify Britain's protected source. [[506-yiwan-meiyuan-jiandie-tuokaqiefu-yu-lengzhan-wanqi-meisu-qingbao-daan-lvqmgp0k-6r7o0whwbakbuct8kvn]] adds a distinct role in the [[AdolfTolkachev|Tolkachev]] case: [[EdwardLeeHoward|Edward Lee Howard]] reportedly gave the initiating lead, while Ames's later disclosures confirmed information after the KGB investigation had begun. Together the cases make Ames a repeated source-exposure mechanism without assigning him sole responsibility for every compromised operation.

## Key Characteristics

- CIA officer positioned near sensitive Soviet and East European intelligence.
- Sold protected source information to the KGB for money.
- Turned allied sharing and internal distribution into paths for source exposure.
- Compromised multiple sources rather than one isolated operation.
- Displayed conspicuous spending that later contributed to suspicion.
- Played different causal roles across cases: direct exposure in the Gordievsky account and later confirmation in the Tolkachev account.

- [[506-yiwan-meiyuan-jiandie-tuokaqiefu-yu-lengzhan-wanqi-meisu-qingbao-daan-lvqmgp0k-6r7o0whwbakbuct8kvn]] associates Ames's eventual detection with sudden expensive purchases while noting that he was not arrested until 1994.

## Qualifications

The sources do not provide complete case files, disclosure lists, or a definitive causal allocation for every lost source. Financial pressure, carelessness, greed, status, and institutional access are narrative explanations rather than a full psychological account. The Tolkachev sequencing qualifies any simplified statement that Ames alone exposed him, but it does not minimize Ames's wider betrayal.
