# 曹操 / Cao Cao

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-950-budong-quanli-beihou-de-renxing-bi-bai-wuyi-lu8-u78yqvvwwarsz8dlevvzchxr|Hanji 950]] introduces Cao Cao inside the first [[AntiDongZhuoCoalition|反董卓联盟]] deployment before his direct attack in Hanji 953. He is one of the figures gathered at [[SuanzaoCamp|酸枣大营]], but the episode's sharper point comes through [[BaoXin|鲍信]], who judges that the person able to plan great affairs and restore order is Cao Cao rather than the more visibly prestigious [[YuanShao|袁绍]].

This creates an early contrast between convening prestige and executable judgment. Cao Cao is not yet the coalition's formal center, but Hanji 950 makes him a latent capacity figure before later sources show his isolated westward attack, East Commandery opening, and adviser-attraction sequence.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-953-luanshi-zhizhong-yaobuyao-jiangjiu-renyi-lrzfimgqatjxyw9zx9ry2kiwxkon|Hanji 953]] shows Cao Cao before he has a durable territorial base. After [[DongZhuo|董卓]] burns Luoyang and moves the emperor west, Cao Cao argues that the [[AntiDongZhuoCoalition|anti-Dong-Zhuo coalition]] should attack immediately, advances with limited support from [[WeiZiLateHan|魏兹]], and is defeated by [[XuRongLateHan|徐荣]] at [[BianshuiBattleLateHan|Bianshui]]. His survival through [[CaoHong|曹洪]]'s rescue is paired with his later critique at [[SuanzaoCamp|Suanzao]], where he lays out a [[StrategicPassControl|pass-control]] containment plan that the coalition refuses to execute.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-954-renxingbenshan-haishi-renxingbene-lkurz9dbb25jrjyl5c-rrahda39h|Hanji 954]] adds Cao Cao's earliest legitimacy-positioning in the anti-[[DongZhuo|董卓]] coalition. When [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] consider installing [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] as a new emperor, Cao Cao rejects the plan because [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] has not committed a legitimacy-destroying fault and because the coalition's strength depends on "师出有名."

The source makes Cao Cao's later welcome-the-emperor branch more intelligible. Before he can operationalize [[LegitimacyWindowSeizure|合法性窗口抢占]], he already treats the recognized emperor's name as a scarce public asset: replacing the emperor would recode anti-Dong action as rebellion, while preserving the title keeps [[MoralizedWarPretext|道德化战争借口]] available.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] adds Cao Cao's first [[DongCommandery|东郡]] foothold before the Hanji 961 indirect-relief campaign. [[BaoXin|鲍信]] reads [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s absorption of [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] as a danger and proposes that Cao Cao hold Dong Commandery, link west with [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]], and keep a northern line toward [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]].

The source then makes Cao Cao's early position practical rather than merely aspirational. When [[WangGongLateHan|王肱]] cannot resist [[YuDuLateHan|于毒]], [[BaiRaoLateHan|白绕]], [[SuiGuLateHan|眭固]], and their large armed following, Cao Cao enters the commandery, defeats Bai Rao around [[PuyangLateHan|濮阳]], receives Yuan Shao's recommendation as administrator, and places the commandery office at [[DongWuyangLateHan|东武阳]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-960-caowei-dingji-zhinang-xunyu-weihe-qi-yuan-tou-cao-lmor1txqpm88itt4incalusconjr|Hanji 960]] backfills the moment when [[XunYu|荀彧]] chooses Cao Cao over [[YuanShao|袁绍]]. The source frames Cao Cao's advantage less as current size than as usable judgment: Xun Yu believes Yuan Shao cannot convert gathered talent into action, while Cao Cao receives Xun Yu as a major adviser and is said to compare him to Zhang Liang.

This addition strengthens the earlier personnel foundation behind later Cao-side pages. Before Xun Yu appears as crisis detector, legitimacy strategist, and campaign-sequencing adviser, Hanji 960 shows Cao Cao becoming attractive because a mobile elite believes his camp can make advice executable. The source remains dramatic in its details, but it supports the existing [[TalentAttractionThroughRespect|礼贤下士式人才吸附]], [[TalentAsStateTreasure|以贤臣为宝]], and [[AdviserStateArchitecture|谋士政权架构]] branches.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-961-neihuang-zhizhan-caocao-chulu-fengmang-ljdlt6kiu8tg0qws-jlyej-yhgxr|Hanji 961]] adds Cao Cao's earlier [[DongCommandery|东郡]] command layer before the Yanzhou succession branch in Hanji 964. While stationed at [[DunqiuLateHan|顿丘]], Cao Cao faces [[YuDuLateHan|于毒]]'s attack on [[DongWuyangLateHan|东武阳]] and chooses an indirect move: threaten Yu Du's base rather than rush to the visible siege.

The source makes [[NeihuangBattleLateHan|内黄之战]] an early command-capacity case. Yu Du withdraws, Cao Cao advances to Neihuang, and he defeats [[SuiGuLateHan|眭固]] and [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]]. The episode's broad interpretation is source-scoped praise of Cao Cao as a resilient and integrated commander: [[EncirclingWeiToRescueZhao|围魏救赵]] target shifting, adviser depth through [[ChengYu|程昱]] and [[XunYu|荀彧]], and later resource systems such as [[QingzhouSoldiers|青州兵]] all feed into [[IntegratedCommandCapacity|全维度统帅能力]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-964-bie-jiaoao-bie-jiaoao-bie-jiaoao-lo2-edzffu0v9ixoabc06rx-oqoq|Hanji 964]] backfills Cao Cao's first [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] opening before the later Qingzhou-surrender and legitimacy-logistics branches. After [[LiuDaiLateHan|刘岱]] dies against the [[QingzhouYellowTurbans|青州黄巾军]], the transcript's "成公" persuasion channel, [[BaoXin|鲍信]], and [[WanQianLateHan|万潜]] lead local officials toward inviting Cao Cao into the province.

The source makes this opening both costly and irregular. Cao Cao defeats the Qingzhou Yellow Turbans after hard fighting, but Bao Xin dies while saving him; Cao Cao later buries a wooden image because Bao Xin's body cannot be found. The takeover also lacks clean formal title legitimacy because the court appoints [[JinShang|金尚]] as Yanzhou inspector, so Cao Cao's practical foothold precedes clean office recognition.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-968-jiemi-sanguo-zui-lihai-de-yangmou-lg4qfkdgw-stixrebhjdoshil6yj|Hanji 968]] backfills Cao Cao's early system-building before the [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-969-yuanshi-xiongdi-neidou-shui-neng-jianlou-lsgj6sewo35jpsgfm-koivijtr6r|Hanji 969]] appointment branch. Cao Cao absorbs a large Yellow Turban surrender associated with [[QingzhouLateHan|青州]], turns selected fighters into [[QingzhouSoldiers|青州兵]], and uses the wider population as a farming and supply base. The episode makes this a case of [[DefeatedPopulationConversion|降众资源化]] rather than only battlefield victory.

The same source adds [[MaoJie|毛玠]] as the adviser who pairs political legitimacy with logistics: "奉天子以令不臣" on one side, and farming, stockpiling, and military supply on the other. Cao Cao then tries to make that strategy operational through [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]]'s [[HeneiCommandery|河内郡]] route, [[DongZhaoLateHan|董昭]]'s persuasion, and [[ZhongYao|钟繇]]'s defense of the envoy before [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-969-yuanshi-xiongdi-neidou-shui-neng-jianlou-lsgj6sewo35jpsgfm-koivijtr6r|Hanji 969]] backfills Cao Cao before the Xuzhou revenge and Yanzhou-crisis branch. [[ZhongYao|钟繇]] persuades the Li-Guo court side to recognize Cao Cao as east-conquering general, while Cao Cao reads the [[YuanShao|袁绍]]-[[YuanShu|袁术]] conflict as a two-tigers opportunity and then drives Yuan Shu south from Fengqiu toward Jiujiang. This adds an earlier version of Cao Cao's [[XingshiqingStrategicAssessment|行势情战略评估]]: his opportunity comes not from a clean court order, but from hollow titles, rival exhaustion, and rapid pursuit.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-971-luanshi-miyun-shui-sha-le-caocao-laodie-ljh3hdhssigmv78bueo-hprfqwku|Hanji 971]] backfills the immediate personal trigger behind Cao Cao's first Xuzhou revenge branch. His father [[CaoSong|曹嵩]] and younger brother [[CaoDeLateHan|曹德]] are killed while moving toward [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]], with the episode preserving both the [[Sanguozhi|《三国志》]] attribution to [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] and the [[HouHanshu|《后汉书》]] attribution to [[ZhangKaiAssassin|张闿]]'s robbery. Cao Cao then attacks [[Xuzhou|徐州]], and the source treats the resulting [[XuzhouMassacreLateHan|徐州屠杀]] as the point where private family revenge becomes mass civilian catastrophe.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-973-changpingguan-zhizhan-shengsi-zhidi-zhanhou-jing-chonggui-yuhao-lgazc9dewfayb8qnkncfkfa12yet|Hanji 973]] supplies the immediate campaign setting before the Yanzhou-betrayal explanation. Cao Cao first pulls back from [[Xuzhou|徐州]] because grain runs out, then attacks [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] again, damaging towns and defeating [[LiuBei|刘备]] east of Tan.

The source makes Cao Cao's Xuzhou pressure collapse for a different reason the second time: [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] and [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] invite [[LyuBu|吕布]] into [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]], forcing him to return with only [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]], [[FanCountyLateHan|范县]], and [[DongALateHan|东阿]] still holding. This is the military trigger that Hanji 974 then explains through friendship, fear, and local-elite politics.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-974-caocao-xiang-ta-tuo-qi-xian-zi-weihe-zhuanshen-ai-daozi-lhfabab7xv2vfsgnhr9ozwqhmhd|Hanji 974]] backfills the relationship and local-elite conditions behind Cao Cao's first [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] collapse. The source stresses that Cao Cao once protected [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] against [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and trusted him enough to send his household toward him if Cao died in the [[Xuzhou|徐州]] campaign, making the later betrayal a case of [[PersonalTrustPoliticalFragility|私交信任的政治脆弱性]].

The same episode also makes Cao Cao's early severity politically costly. [[BianRangLateHan|边让]]'s public challenge to Cao Cao's legitimacy is followed by a purge that alarms [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] and other Yanzhou local actors; the source's explanation of Bian Rang's opposition through early land and [[TuntianSystem|屯田制]] pressure remains source-scoped. The longer consequence is institutional: Cao Cao survives by broadening recruitment beyond one local cluster while also tightening trusted military control through kin and commanders such as [[XiahouDun|夏侯惇]] and [[CaoRen|曹仁]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-975-yanzhou-zhiluan-xunyu-ruhe-bang-caocao-shou-dabenying-ln0hh4jrokkwtqsmxmqhdjzcosdr|Hanji 975]] backfills the first rupture in Cao Cao's [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] base before the later adviser and Puyang branches. The episode begins from Cao Cao's habit of reading grain, terrain, weather, and people as operational details, then shows why that kind of attention matters when Cao Cao is away attacking [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] and [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] plus [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] invite [[LyuBu|吕布]] into Yanzhou.

Cao Cao is mostly offstage in this source, but his survival depends on delegated crisis judgment. [[XunYu|荀彧]] recognizes the danger from title wording and route mismatch, then recalls [[XiahouDun|夏侯惇]] to [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]]; the episode therefore makes Cao Cao's early order a case of [[DetailBasedCrisisDetection|细节式危机识别]] and [[RootBasePriority|根本根据地优先]] before those themes are developed in Hanji 976-987.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] adds a synthetic reading of Cao Cao's adviser system before the more concrete late-Han episodes. The source describes Cao Cao's camp as a "cloud platform" in which [[XunYu|荀彧]] handles legitimacy and structure, [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] supplies bold strategic judgment, and the transcript's "陈玉" is treated cautiously here as a likely [[ChengYu|程昱]]-cluster reference to gap-filling operational advice.

This source's contribution is not a new battle sequence. It ties Cao Cao's strength to [[AdviserStateArchitecture|谋士政权架构]]: the combination of Han-court legitimacy, [[TuntianSystem|屯田制]], Yingchuan talent networks, and multiple adviser roles makes the regime more durable than personal command alone.

曹操 / Cao Cao appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-24-shangyang-yue-wo-za-lie-le-lt4yd6pwlezqia5yljg-gankyhut]] as a later analogy for [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s [[PowerExitTrap|权力退场困境]]. The episode does not offer a full biography; it uses Cao Cao's refusal to hand power back as an example of a political actor with too many enemies to expect a safe private retirement.

The host invokes Cao Cao's own self-justifying writing to make the point that withdrawal can be more dangerous than continued control. In this reading, public condemnation and moral pressure do not change the underlying security problem: once a person has reshaped the political field and accumulated enemies, surrendering power can mean surrendering protection.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-80-zhugeliang-de-ouxiang-daodi-you-duo-lihai-lse6qmqqhoyqdzqjl4qfuvioeiw0]] adds Cao Cao as a reception witness for [[LeYi|乐毅]] rather than as a direct actor. The host says Cao Cao reportedly cried when reading [[BaoYanHuiwangShu|《报燕惠王书》]], making him a later reader who recognizes the political and emotional force of Le Yi's restrained reply to [[YanHuiwang|燕惠王]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-2-pandian-huangdimen-jiuhou-xiaopihao-livpg3zh7lsqffgca2pomiqnlvyd|Qinji 131-2]] adds Cao Cao as a cautionary drinking-and-desire case. In the source's Wancheng story, [[ZhangXiu|张绣]]'s side gets [[DianWei|典韦]] drunk and hides his weapon, then attacks after Cao Cao's private indulgence has already made the situation unstable. Cao Cao escapes, but [[CaoAng|曹昂]], Cao Anmin, and Dian Wei die, making the episode's point that alcohol around commanders can turn private desire into a security failure.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-978-gushen-quantui-shuwan-dijun-ta-zenme-zuodao-de-lhgd8vsvuaf5oi9aefh0wxabmzwv|Hanji 978]] adds the earliest visible survival layer of Cao Cao's [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] crisis in this branch. Cao Cao is mostly offstage, but the source makes his continued position depend on subordinates: [[XunYu|荀彧]] deters [[GuoGongLateHan|郭贡]] at [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]], and [[ChengYu|程昱]] persuades [[JinYunLateHan|靳允]] to keep [[FanCountyLateHan|范县]] despite [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s family-hostage pressure.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-979-zaoyu-tongliao-beici-ta-ruhe-wei-caocao-liwan-kuanglan-luu58a2ww11g-radh-5g4l7fcis|Hanji 979]] adds the immediately earlier base-preservation layer before the Puyang crisis. After much of [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] turns against him, Cao Cao's survival depends on [[ChengYu|程昱]] blocking [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] at [[CangtingFordLateHan|仓亭津]] and [[ZaoZhi|枣祗]] organizing [[DongALateHan|东阿]] for defense. Cao Cao's reported thanks to Cheng Yu is kept source-scoped, but the episode's durable claim is that command survives because a remaining base, crossings, people, and cities still hold.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-980-lvbu-xiansha-caocao-kankan-yi-ju-hua-ruhe-niuzhuan-lishi-lgggw3vmonu-3wvzabvg943v5qcg|Hanji 980]] adds the earlier [[PuyangBattleLateHan|濮阳之战]] crisis before the Yuan Shao dependency question in Hanji 981. Cao Cao trusts [[PuyangTianClan|濮阳田氏]]'s inside-response signal, enters [[PuyangLateHan|濮阳]], burns the east gate, and is nearly trapped by [[LyuBu|吕布]] until [[DianWei|典韦]] holds the front and Cao Cao escapes by misdirecting a pursuer toward a yellow horse.

This source makes Cao Cao's capability visible in an exposed, losing moment rather than in a clean victory. The host reads his survival as [[CrisisDeceptionSurvival|临危欺敌求生]], then extends the point through Cao Cao's immediate guard reward, troop reassurance, and willingness to continue fighting until locust and grain pressure turn the campaign into [[WarCostTempoDiscipline|战争成本与速决纪律]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]] adds the crisis before the later Yanzhou recovery branch. After losing much of [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] and running short of grain, Cao Cao considers [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s request that his family move to Ye. [[ChengYu|程昱]] stops him by naming the fear behind the idea, downgrading Yuan Shao's judgment, and reminding Cao Cao that he still has cities, soldiers, [[XunYu|荀彧]], and Cheng Yu himself.

This source makes Cao Cao's later independence contingent on accepting correction before dependency hardens. The episode treats the family-to-Ye proposal as a [[HostageBackedDependencyTrap|家属质押式依附陷阱]], and Cao Cao's strength lies in being able to convert blunt advice into a changed decision.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-986-tianyu-qiwang-bixian-lingqi-kuang-lvlvu057-tnz3hal6oodhydvzvbd|Hanji 986]] supplies the compact prelude to the later Dingtao branch. Cao Cao kills [[XueLanLateHan|薛兰]] and [[LiFengLateHan|李封]], two [[LyuBu|吕布]] commanders around Juye, then defeats Lü Bu's rescue force by ambush and recovers much of [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]]. The episode closes with Cao Cao tempted by [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]]'s death and [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s fresh control of [[Xuzhou|徐州]], setting up the adviser warning developed in Hanji 987.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-987-dingtao-zhizhan-caocao-he-lvbu-dianfeng-duijue-lofq0pq8n9rrfvfttunahisy0ave|Hanji 987]] adds Cao Cao's earlier 195 CE base-recovery decision. After [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] dies, Cao Cao is tempted to attack [[Xuzhou|徐州]], but [[XunYu|荀彧]] argues that [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] is Cao Cao's root base and must be secured before expansion. Cao Cao accepts the advice, faces [[LyuBu|吕布]] and [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] at [[DingtaoBattleLateHan|定陶之战]], and uses terrain and伏兵 to win despite having far fewer soldiers in camp.

The episode therefore makes Cao Cao's strength partly a willingness to be corrected before a tempting campaign. The victory at Dingtao is not framed as isolated battlefield brilliance: it follows a decision to harvest grain, preserve the rear, and turn [[RootBasePriority|根本根据地优先]] into action before the later Yanzhou/Yongqiu branch closes.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-989-ningwei-taipingquan-buzuo-luanshi-ren-lkoycygmjnltuvpq-6wjv-ls61s6|Hanji 989]] adds the pre-Zang-Hong Yanzhou turn. Cao Cao surrounds [[YongqiuLateHan|雍丘]] while [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] tries to leave for [[YuanShu|袁术]], but Zhang Miao is killed by [[LiuYiLateHan|刘翊]] before that exit can strengthen an enemy camp. The episode then reports Cao Cao's appointment as Yanzhou governor, linking battlefield recovery in Yanzhou to the wider emperor-flight chronology.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu|Hanji 999]] adds a compact pre-Xu-court action. Cao Cao defeats [[HeYiLateHan|何仪]] and other Runan-Yingchuan Yellow Turban remnants before they can attach to [[YuanShu|袁术]], which indirectly supports the fragile [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] return environment later developed by the Luoyang and Xu-court episodes.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-996-luanshi-zanghong-jiangyiqi-de-ren-xiachang-zui-biequ-lgpa-m6h6bvhyintnuj6zztopui76|Hanji 996]] adds Cao Cao as the attacker at [[YongqiuLateHan|雍丘]]. Because [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] had earlier welcomed [[LyuBu|吕布]] into Yanzhou against Cao Cao, the source says Cao Cao will not spare Zhang Miao's brother [[ZhangChaoLateHan|张超]]. The fall of Yongqiu and the destruction of Zhang Chao's family become the grievance that drives [[ZangHong|臧洪]] against [[YuanShao|袁绍]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-997-zanghong-zhisi-shi-yibaoyuntian-haishi-qingjie-nandu-lln2liblq-kyjrsvitnbh7cspbax|Hanji 997]] continues this branch by making Cao Cao part of the alignment structure behind Zang Hong's death. The source does not develop Cao Cao's campaign mechanics further; it uses Cao Cao's alliance with Yuan Shao to explain why Yuan Shao refuses Zang Hong's demand to rescue Zhang Chao.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1011-miheng-zhisi-zuiju-beiju-secai-de-jiedao-sharen-ltrzpchwdfcyp4ntmum9giu-5mva|Hanji 1011]] adds Cao Cao's reputation-preserving treatment of [[MiHengLateHan|祢衡]]. After Kong Rong recommends Mi Heng and Mi Heng insults him, Cao Cao tries to reduce him by making him a drummer at a public banquet. When Mi Heng's naked drumming turns the shame back on Cao Cao, the source says Cao Cao chooses not to kill him directly because that would look narrow-minded. Instead, he sends Mi Heng toward [[LiuBiao|刘表]], opening the chain later read as [[BorrowedKnifeKilling|借刀杀人]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1005-sunce-zhuding-zaoshi-shi-xingge-jueding-mingyun-lpqnere2cwcrngggyezd-bsqahx4|Hanji 1005]] backfills Cao Cao's early Xu-court consolidation after [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] reaches [[Xudu|许都]]. Cao Cao breaks with [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], attacks him, and takes his Liang-area camps after Yang Feng flees to [[YuanShu|袁术]]. He then manages [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s resentment over rank by yielding the great-general title, a case of [[StrategicTitleConcession|战略性名位让步]] that trades visible precedence for strategic breathing room.

The same source also previews Cao Cao's adviser system. The transcript appears to misrender [[XunYu|荀彧]], [[XunYou|荀攸]], and [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] as "徐玉", "徐悠", and "过家", but the office sequence is treated cautiously as the Xun Yu recommendation channel that Hanji 1006 then develops.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1003-sanguo-zui-bei-digu-de-moushi-dongzhao-llc7q-loennxw26v3u5cbhitikw1|Hanji 1003]] supplies the prior entry problem before the relocation decision. Cao Cao is still trying to reach [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] from Xu, but [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] controls the route and [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]] dominates the court environment. [[DongZhaoLateHan|董昭]] acts in Cao Cao's name to win Yang Feng's cooperation, and [[DongChengLateHan|董承]]'s later request against Han Xian gives Cao Cao a public reason to march to [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]].

This branch makes Cao Cao's early court success partly adviser-enabled before Cao Cao is physically present. The host reads Dong Zhao's effort as evidence that capable people were already selecting Cao Cao as a usable leader and then solving his next bottleneck through [[ProactiveAdviserInitiative|主动补位式谋臣]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1002-yuanshu-chengdi-hou-sunce-chengwei-zui-da-ying-llya3c2-pefujwxdxefvqxwxjizjo|Hanji 1002]] supplies the deliberation before that entry problem. Cao Cao is in Xu and wants to receive [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], but his subordinates worry that the east is still unsettled and that [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]], [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], and other court-side military actors will be hard to control.

[[XunYu|荀彧]] reframes the risk as a short legitimacy window. By citing Duke Wen of Jin receiving King Xiang of Zhou and Liu Bang mourning Yi Emperor, he argues that welcoming the emperor can stabilize public feeling, give Cao Cao a recognized command vocabulary, and attract talent. The episode's "大顺、大略、大德" formula becomes [[LegitimacyWindowSeizure|合法性窗口抢占]], with [[CaoHong|曹洪]]'s blocked mission to Luoyang setting up the route-clearing mechanics Hanji 1003 then develops.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1004-caocao-yingfeng-hanxiandi-shi-shui-de-zhuyi-lrrga-flmx5ajnhu1c42x7kooe94|Hanji 1004]] supplies the prior relocation decision. After reaching [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], Cao Cao asks [[DongZhaoLateHan|董昭]] what to do next, and Dong Zhao argues that remaining in a ruined, factionalized Luoyang will leave Cao Cao unable to control the court safely. Moving [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] to [[Xudu|许县 / 许都]] uses a real supply crisis as the public reason while shifting the emperor into Cao Cao's stronger operating zone.

The episode therefore makes Cao Cao's "welcome the emperor" move less like a single coup phrase and more like a sequenced control problem. He punishes and rewards court officials, reassures [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] long enough to reduce obstruction, and then turns geography, food, and title legitimacy into the starting conditions for the Xu-court order developed by Hanji 1005.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1006-guojia-buman-yuanshao-weihe-que-neng-guishun-caocao-lup-p-inz1sdlhpnaggqjp0eavar|Hanji 1006]] backfills two foundations for Cao Cao's late-Han order. First, Guo Jia leaves Yuan Shao after judging that Yuan Shao cannot use people well, then recognizes Cao Cao as the ruler who can turn strategic talent into action. Second, Cao Cao supports [[ManChong|满宠]] after Man Chong executes [[CaoHong|曹洪]]'s lawbreaking clients, making [[RuleOverKinshipPower|法度压过亲缘特权]] part of Cao Cao's internal control system.

The episode therefore presents Cao Cao's strength as paired recruitment and enforcement. He can absorb advisers such as Guo Jia and [[XunYou|荀攸]], but he also has to make officials believe that clan access will not always override law. Talent use and legal severity are treated as mutually reinforcing mechanisms inside the same camp.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1008-ruhe-pingjia-caocao-de-tuntianzhi-ls8wvh9ox-pcjmt0z8w82opbha-k|Hanji 1008]] adds Cao Cao's grain-statecraft branch. In the late-Han famine and military supply crisis, [[ZaoZhi|枣祗]] proposes [[TuntianSystem|屯田制]], and Cao Cao rapidly appoints Zao Zhi and [[RenJun|任峻]] to implement it around [[Xudu|许都]]. The episode treats this as the production base behind his later campaigns: Cao Cao does not only notice that war consumes grain; he builds an office system that produces it.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1009-liubei-touben-caocao-xianxie-langru-hukou-llm0ds4krkptjdtb71hbusnpzbdc|Hanji 1009]] adds an earlier reputation constraint through [[LiuBei|刘备]]. After Liu Bei flees [[LyuBu|吕布]], advisers warn Cao Cao that Liu Bei is a future danger, but [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] argues that killing a desperate guest would damage Cao Cao's ability to attract heroes. Cao Cao accepts the logic of [[ReputationConstrainedTalentProtection|名声约束下的人才保护]] and sends Liu Bei toward [[PeiCounty|小沛]] with troops and supplies to keep pressure on Lü Bu.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1012-haose-caocao-luanshi-zhong-de-dute-qingshi-llvrucihuv2buvp1-lisz4usbprh|Hanji 1012]] supplies the pre-attack Wancheng setup. Cao Cao's southern campaign receives Zhang Xiu's surrender through [[JiaXu|贾诩]]'s advice, but Cao Cao then takes [[LadyZouWancheng|邹氏]], the widow of [[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]] and Zhang Xiu's respected aunt by marriage. The episode makes Cao Cao's private desire a surrender-management problem before the later attack has begun.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] sharpens the Wancheng mechanics. It first uses [[LadyDuCaoCao|杜夫人]] and [[LadyYinCaoCao|尹夫人]] to establish Cao Cao's household-desire pattern, then makes the Wancheng disaster specific: Cao Cao takes [[LadyZouWancheng|邹氏]] after [[ZhangXiu|张绣]] surrenders, rewards [[HuCheEr|胡车儿]], lets a plan to kill Zhang Xiu leak, and is then caught when [[JiaXu|贾诩]] and Hu Che'er turn the situation into a night attack. The episode adds [[DesireDrivenCommandExposure|私欲驱动的统帅暴露]] as a failure mode before the later pages show Cao Cao recovering organizational discipline.

Hanji 1014 returns to Cao Cao from the Wancheng aftermath and the pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] strategic setup. It adds the household branch through [[LadyDingCaoCao|丁夫人]], [[LadyBianCaoCao|卞夫人]], [[CaoAng|曹昂]], and [[FenxiangMailu|分香卖履]], then shows Cao Cao rewarding [[YuJin|于禁]] after Yu Jin restores order in retreat through [[CrisisFirstMilitaryDiscipline|临危先务军纪]]. The final section has [[XunYu|荀彧]] and [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] answer [[YuanShao|袁绍]] through [[TenVictoriesTenDefeats|十胜十败论]], making Cao Cao's comparative strength a matter of discipline, talent use, legitimacy, and decisiveness rather than troop count alone.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] adds Cao Cao's late-Han strategic sequencing. [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] and [[XunYu|荀彧]] push him to remove [[LyuBu|吕布]] while [[YuanShao|袁绍]] is occupied against [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]], and Xun Yu recommends [[ZhongYao|钟繇]] to keep [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] stable through [[MaTeng|马腾]] and [[HanSui|韩遂]]. The same episode also shows why [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s self-coronation gives Cao Cao a public "讨逆" role under [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1020-sanguo-baijiazi-yuanshu-ruhe-ba-hao-pai-da-de-xilan-lhwr9hfmanr-zktleufy3kvl38u0|Hanji 1020]] adds Cao Cao as a direct late-Han actor rather than only a later analogy or cautionary example. He asks [[HeKui|何夔]] to judge [[YuanShu|袁术]], then reads Yuan Shu's failure through talent and trust: a regime that cannot accommodate people like He Kui has already lost usable capacity. The same episode shows Cao Cao absorbing [[XuChu|许褚]]'s local armed following into his close guard while also controlling the court around [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] through the politically risky [[YangBiao|杨彪]] case.

This addition keeps Cao Cao double-edged. He is severe enough that He Kui fears humiliation and Yang Biao can be arrested under a thin accusation, but he is also shown adjusting when [[ManChong|满宠]] warns that punishment without evidence would damage popular confidence. In this source, Cao Cao's strength is not moral purity; it is the ability to identify talent, absorb force, and step back from a reputationally costly prosecution.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1016-sanguo-luanshi-li-chengui-jiazu-de-mingyun-jueze-ltuckdcxpyxk-xmzp8rt-2gjfzcs|Hanji 1016]] adds the legitimacy pressure behind that Yuan Shu branch. Cao Cao's campaign against [[YuanShu|袁术]] is described through the Han-court formula of punishing an illegitimate claimant, and [[HanYinLateHan|韩胤]]'s execution after [[LyuBu|吕布]] reverses the marriage alliance makes Cao Cao's court the destination for anti-Yuan-Shu signaling. Cao Cao's letter and court appointment for Lü Bu also create the opening for [[ChenDeng|陈登]] to travel toward him in the next branch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1017-sanguo-yincang-yingxiong-chendeng-de-chuanqi-wangshi-lhcvtm8fobuzjfyo9uirdcgx2plq|Hanji 1017]] adds the earlier [[ChenDeng|陈登]] channel. Cao Cao hears Chen Deng's judgment that [[LyuBu|吕布]] is [[QingYuQuJiu|轻于去就]], promotes [[ChenGui|陈珪]], appoints Chen Deng to [[GuanglingCommandery|广陵]], and asks him to manage eastern affairs as an inside channel. In this source, Cao Cao's strength is practical caution: he can use the Chen family against Lü Bu without pretending Lü Bu can be safely kept for long.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1018-ta-weihe-rang-caocao-jidan-sunce-weiju-liubei-wanxi-lqccdhrjmero-gt7vsaq2ejuhhw8|Hanji 1018]] continues that Chen Deng channel. Cao Cao rewards Chen Deng and Chen Gui, but does not satisfy Lü Bu's request for the [[Xuzhou|徐州]] governor title. The episode's reported "raise a tiger" image makes Cao Cao's caution toward Lü Bu the source for [[JiFuBaoYang|饥附饱扬]]: Cao Cao can use Lü Bu, but does not mistake dependency for trust.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1019-jiemi-chenminwang-liuchong-de-chuanqi-yisheng-ll2fma7wupp5gczudysfett5lhio|Hanji 1019]] adds the immediate setup to that Yuan Shu branch. Cao Cao uses court authorization to recognize [[SunCe|孙策]] with titles under [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s name, trying to pull a strong regional actor into his orbit. The episode later has Cao Cao eastward against [[YuanShu|袁术]], defeating [[QiaoRui|桥蕤]]'s force after Yuan Shu abandons the army and retreats across the Huai River.
