# 曹操 / Cao Cao

曹操 / Cao Cao appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-24-shangyang-yue-wo-za-lie-le-lt4yd6pwlezqia5yljg-gankyhut]] as a later analogy for [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s [[PowerExitTrap|权力退场困境]]. The episode does not offer a full biography; it uses Cao Cao's refusal to hand power back as an example of a political actor with too many enemies to expect a safe private retirement.

The host invokes Cao Cao's own self-justifying writing to make the point that withdrawal can be more dangerous than continued control. In this reading, public condemnation and moral pressure do not change the underlying security problem: once a person has reshaped the political field and accumulated enemies, surrendering power can mean surrendering protection.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-80-zhugeliang-de-ouxiang-daodi-you-duo-lihai-lse6qmqqhoyqdzqjl4qfuvioeiw0]] adds Cao Cao as a reception witness for [[LeYi|乐毅]] rather than as a direct actor. The host says Cao Cao reportedly cried when reading [[BaoYanHuiwangShu|《报燕惠王书》]], making him a later reader who recognizes the political and emotional force of Le Yi's restrained reply to [[YanHuiwang|燕惠王]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-2-pandian-huangdimen-jiuhou-xiaopihao-livpg3zh7lsqffgca2pomiqnlvyd|Qinji 131-2]] adds Cao Cao as a cautionary drinking-and-desire case. In the source's Wancheng story, [[ZhangXiu|张绣]]'s side gets [[DianWei|典韦]] drunk and hides his weapon, then attacks after Cao Cao's private indulgence has already made the situation unstable. Cao Cao escapes, but [[CaoAng|曹昂]], Cao Anmin, and Dian Wei die, making the episode's point that alcohol around commanders can turn private desire into a security failure.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu|Hanji 999]] adds a compact pre-Xu-court action. Cao Cao defeats [[HeYiLateHan|何仪]] and other Runan-Yingchuan Yellow Turban remnants before they can attach to [[YuanShu|袁术]], which indirectly supports the fragile [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] return environment later developed by the Luoyang and Xu-court episodes.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-996-luanshi-zanghong-jiangyiqi-de-ren-xiachang-zui-biequ-lgpa-m6h6bvhyintnuj6zztopui76|Hanji 996]] adds Cao Cao as the attacker at [[YongqiuLateHan|雍丘]]. Because [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] had earlier welcomed [[LyuBu|吕布]] into Yanzhou against Cao Cao, the source says Cao Cao will not spare Zhang Miao's brother [[ZhangChaoLateHan|张超]]. The fall of Yongqiu and the destruction of Zhang Chao's family become the grievance that drives [[ZangHong|臧洪]] against [[YuanShao|袁绍]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-997-zanghong-zhisi-shi-yibaoyuntian-haishi-qingjie-nandu-lln2liblq-kyjrsvitnbh7cspbax|Hanji 997]] continues this branch by making Cao Cao part of the alignment structure behind Zang Hong's death. The source does not develop Cao Cao's campaign mechanics further; it uses Cao Cao's alliance with Yuan Shao to explain why Yuan Shao refuses Zang Hong's demand to rescue Zhang Chao.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1011-miheng-zhisi-zuiju-beiju-secai-de-jiedao-sharen-ltrzpchwdfcyp4ntmum9giu-5mva|Hanji 1011]] adds Cao Cao's reputation-preserving treatment of [[MiHengLateHan|祢衡]]. After Kong Rong recommends Mi Heng and Mi Heng insults him, Cao Cao tries to reduce him by making him a drummer at a public banquet. When Mi Heng's naked drumming turns the shame back on Cao Cao, the source says Cao Cao chooses not to kill him directly because that would look narrow-minded. Instead, he sends Mi Heng toward [[LiuBiao|刘表]], opening the chain later read as [[BorrowedKnifeKilling|借刀杀人]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1005-sunce-zhuding-zaoshi-shi-xingge-jueding-mingyun-lpqnere2cwcrngggyezd-bsqahx4|Hanji 1005]] backfills Cao Cao's early Xu-court consolidation after [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] reaches [[Xudu|许都]]. Cao Cao breaks with [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], attacks him, and takes his Liang-area camps after Yang Feng flees to [[YuanShu|袁术]]. He then manages [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s resentment over rank by yielding the great-general title, a case of [[StrategicTitleConcession|战略性名位让步]] that trades visible precedence for strategic breathing room.

The same source also previews Cao Cao's adviser system. The transcript appears to misrender [[XunYu|荀彧]], [[XunYou|荀攸]], and [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] as "徐玉", "徐悠", and "过家", but the office sequence is treated cautiously as the Xun Yu recommendation channel that Hanji 1006 then develops.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1003-sanguo-zui-bei-digu-de-moushi-dongzhao-llc7q-loennxw26v3u5cbhitikw1|Hanji 1003]] supplies the prior entry problem before the relocation decision. Cao Cao is still trying to reach [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] from Xu, but [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] controls the route and [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]] dominates the court environment. [[DongZhaoLateHan|董昭]] acts in Cao Cao's name to win Yang Feng's cooperation, and [[DongChengLateHan|董承]]'s later request against Han Xian gives Cao Cao a public reason to march to [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]].

This branch makes Cao Cao's early court success partly adviser-enabled before Cao Cao is physically present. The host reads Dong Zhao's effort as evidence that capable people were already selecting Cao Cao as a usable leader and then solving his next bottleneck through [[ProactiveAdviserInitiative|主动补位式谋臣]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1002-yuanshu-chengdi-hou-sunce-chengwei-zui-da-ying-llya3c2-pefujwxdxefvqxwxjizjo|Hanji 1002]] supplies the deliberation before that entry problem. Cao Cao is in Xu and wants to receive [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], but his subordinates worry that the east is still unsettled and that [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]], [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], and other court-side military actors will be hard to control.

[[XunYu|荀彧]] reframes the risk as a short legitimacy window. By citing Duke Wen of Jin receiving King Xiang of Zhou and Liu Bang mourning Yi Emperor, he argues that welcoming the emperor can stabilize public feeling, give Cao Cao a recognized command vocabulary, and attract talent. The episode's "大顺、大略、大德" formula becomes [[LegitimacyWindowSeizure|合法性窗口抢占]], with [[CaoHong|曹洪]]'s blocked mission to Luoyang setting up the route-clearing mechanics Hanji 1003 then develops.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1004-caocao-yingfeng-hanxiandi-shi-shui-de-zhuyi-lrrga-flmx5ajnhu1c42x7kooe94|Hanji 1004]] supplies the prior relocation decision. After reaching [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], Cao Cao asks [[DongZhaoLateHan|董昭]] what to do next, and Dong Zhao argues that remaining in a ruined, factionalized Luoyang will leave Cao Cao unable to control the court safely. Moving [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] to [[Xudu|许县 / 许都]] uses a real supply crisis as the public reason while shifting the emperor into Cao Cao's stronger operating zone.

The episode therefore makes Cao Cao's "welcome the emperor" move less like a single coup phrase and more like a sequenced control problem. He punishes and rewards court officials, reassures [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] long enough to reduce obstruction, and then turns geography, food, and title legitimacy into the starting conditions for the Xu-court order developed by Hanji 1005.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1006-guojia-buman-yuanshao-weihe-que-neng-guishun-caocao-lup-p-inz1sdlhpnaggqjp0eavar|Hanji 1006]] backfills two foundations for Cao Cao's late-Han order. First, Guo Jia leaves Yuan Shao after judging that Yuan Shao cannot use people well, then recognizes Cao Cao as the ruler who can turn strategic talent into action. Second, Cao Cao supports [[ManChong|满宠]] after Man Chong executes [[CaoHong|曹洪]]'s lawbreaking clients, making [[RuleOverKinshipPower|法度压过亲缘特权]] part of Cao Cao's internal control system.

The episode therefore presents Cao Cao's strength as paired recruitment and enforcement. He can absorb advisers such as Guo Jia and [[XunYou|荀攸]], but he also has to make officials believe that clan access will not always override law. Talent use and legal severity are treated as mutually reinforcing mechanisms inside the same camp.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1008-ruhe-pingjia-caocao-de-tuntianzhi-ls8wvh9ox-pcjmt0z8w82opbha-k|Hanji 1008]] adds Cao Cao's grain-statecraft branch. In the late-Han famine and military supply crisis, [[ZaoZhi|枣祗]] proposes [[TuntianSystem|屯田制]], and Cao Cao rapidly appoints Zao Zhi and [[RenJun|任峻]] to implement it around [[Xudu|许都]]. The episode treats this as the production base behind his later campaigns: Cao Cao does not only notice that war consumes grain; he builds an office system that produces it.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1009-liubei-touben-caocao-xianxie-langru-hukou-llm0ds4krkptjdtb71hbusnpzbdc|Hanji 1009]] adds an earlier reputation constraint through [[LiuBei|刘备]]. After Liu Bei flees [[LyuBu|吕布]], advisers warn Cao Cao that Liu Bei is a future danger, but [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] argues that killing a desperate guest would damage Cao Cao's ability to attract heroes. Cao Cao accepts the logic of [[ReputationConstrainedTalentProtection|名声约束下的人才保护]] and sends Liu Bei toward [[PeiCounty|小沛]] with troops and supplies to keep pressure on Lü Bu.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1012-haose-caocao-luanshi-zhong-de-dute-qingshi-llvrucihuv2buvp1-lisz4usbprh|Hanji 1012]] supplies the pre-attack Wancheng setup. Cao Cao's southern campaign receives Zhang Xiu's surrender through [[JiaXu|贾诩]]'s advice, but Cao Cao then takes [[LadyZouWancheng|邹氏]], the widow of [[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]] and Zhang Xiu's respected aunt by marriage. The episode makes Cao Cao's private desire a surrender-management problem before the later attack has begun.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] sharpens the Wancheng mechanics. It first uses [[LadyDuCaoCao|杜夫人]] and [[LadyYinCaoCao|尹夫人]] to establish Cao Cao's household-desire pattern, then makes the Wancheng disaster specific: Cao Cao takes [[LadyZouWancheng|邹氏]] after [[ZhangXiu|张绣]] surrenders, rewards [[HuCheEr|胡车儿]], lets a plan to kill Zhang Xiu leak, and is then caught when [[JiaXu|贾诩]] and Hu Che'er turn the situation into a night attack. The episode adds [[DesireDrivenCommandExposure|私欲驱动的统帅暴露]] as a failure mode before the later pages show Cao Cao recovering organizational discipline.

Hanji 1014 returns to Cao Cao from the Wancheng aftermath and the pre-[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] strategic setup. It adds the household branch through [[LadyDingCaoCao|丁夫人]], [[LadyBianCaoCao|卞夫人]], [[CaoAng|曹昂]], and [[FenxiangMailu|分香卖履]], then shows Cao Cao rewarding [[YuJin|于禁]] after Yu Jin restores order in retreat through [[CrisisFirstMilitaryDiscipline|临危先务军纪]]. The final section has [[XunYu|荀彧]] and [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] answer [[YuanShao|袁绍]] through [[TenVictoriesTenDefeats|十胜十败论]], making Cao Cao's comparative strength a matter of discipline, talent use, legitimacy, and decisiveness rather than troop count alone.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] adds Cao Cao's late-Han strategic sequencing. [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] and [[XunYu|荀彧]] push him to remove [[LyuBu|吕布]] while [[YuanShao|袁绍]] is occupied against [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]], and Xun Yu recommends [[ZhongYao|钟繇]] to keep [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] stable through [[MaTeng|马腾]] and [[HanSui|韩遂]]. The same episode also shows why [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s self-coronation gives Cao Cao a public "讨逆" role under [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1020-sanguo-baijiazi-yuanshu-ruhe-ba-hao-pai-da-de-xilan-lhwr9hfmanr-zktleufy3kvl38u0|Hanji 1020]] adds Cao Cao as a direct late-Han actor rather than only a later analogy or cautionary example. He asks [[HeKui|何夔]] to judge [[YuanShu|袁术]], then reads Yuan Shu's failure through talent and trust: a regime that cannot accommodate people like He Kui has already lost usable capacity. The same episode shows Cao Cao absorbing [[XuChu|许褚]]'s local armed following into his close guard while also controlling the court around [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] through the politically risky [[YangBiao|杨彪]] case.

This addition keeps Cao Cao double-edged. He is severe enough that He Kui fears humiliation and Yang Biao can be arrested under a thin accusation, but he is also shown adjusting when [[ManChong|满宠]] warns that punishment without evidence would damage popular confidence. In this source, Cao Cao's strength is not moral purity; it is the ability to identify talent, absorb force, and step back from a reputationally costly prosecution.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1016-sanguo-luanshi-li-chengui-jiazu-de-mingyun-jueze-ltuckdcxpyxk-xmzp8rt-2gjfzcs|Hanji 1016]] adds the legitimacy pressure behind that Yuan Shu branch. Cao Cao's campaign against [[YuanShu|袁术]] is described through the Han-court formula of punishing an illegitimate claimant, and [[HanYinLateHan|韩胤]]'s execution after [[LyuBu|吕布]] reverses the marriage alliance makes Cao Cao's court the destination for anti-Yuan-Shu signaling. Cao Cao's letter and court appointment for Lü Bu also create the opening for [[ChenDeng|陈登]] to travel toward him in the next branch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1017-sanguo-yincang-yingxiong-chendeng-de-chuanqi-wangshi-lhcvtm8fobuzjfyo9uirdcgx2plq|Hanji 1017]] adds the earlier [[ChenDeng|陈登]] channel. Cao Cao hears Chen Deng's judgment that [[LyuBu|吕布]] is [[QingYuQuJiu|轻于去就]], promotes [[ChenGui|陈珪]], appoints Chen Deng to [[GuanglingCommandery|广陵]], and asks him to manage eastern affairs as an inside channel. In this source, Cao Cao's strength is practical caution: he can use the Chen family against Lü Bu without pretending Lü Bu can be safely kept for long.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1018-ta-weihe-rang-caocao-jidan-sunce-weiju-liubei-wanxi-lqccdhrjmero-gt7vsaq2ejuhhw8|Hanji 1018]] continues that Chen Deng channel. Cao Cao rewards Chen Deng and Chen Gui, but does not satisfy Lü Bu's request for the [[Xuzhou|徐州]] governor title. The episode's reported "raise a tiger" image makes Cao Cao's caution toward Lü Bu the source for [[JiFuBaoYang|饥附饱扬]]: Cao Cao can use Lü Bu, but does not mistake dependency for trust.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1019-jiemi-chenminwang-liuchong-de-chuanqi-yisheng-ll2fma7wupp5gczudysfett5lhio|Hanji 1019]] adds the immediate setup to that Yuan Shu branch. Cao Cao uses court authorization to recognize [[SunCe|孙策]] with titles under [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s name, trying to pull a strong regional actor into his orbit. The episode later has Cao Cao eastward against [[YuanShu|袁术]], defeating [[QiaoRui|桥蕤]]'s force after Yuan Shu abandons the army and retreats across the Huai River.
