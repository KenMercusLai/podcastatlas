# Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI

## Overview
The Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI appears in this wiki source as [[JenniferKing]]'s Stanford affiliation and the institutional context for a report on California data-broker compliance.

## Current Profile
In the episode, Stanford HAI functions as a research-policy anchor rather than a general AI lab profile. Its relevance is that a human-centered AI institution is examining privacy infrastructure: whether data brokers honor deletion rights, whether enforcement is adequate, and whether brokered personal data is being sold into generative-AI markets.
