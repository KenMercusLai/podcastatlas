# Kodiak Harbour

Kodiak Harbour appears in [[tech-20251224-1224-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251224-1224-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the [[Alaska]] fishing-industry setting where [[ChrisSanito]] of [[WildSource]] tests a mycelium cooler. The location grounds [[SeafoodColdChainPackaging]] in a real shipping environment rather than a purely laboratory demonstration.

The source's Kodiak context matters because seafood transport makes packaging tradeoffs concrete. A substitute for polystyrene has to keep fish chilled, stay light, and tolerate handling while reducing plastic-waste risk through [[BiodegradableFoamPackaging]].
