# 上海法租界 / Shanghai French Concession

## Overview
The former Shanghai French Concession is the main urban setting for the episode's discussion of [[LaiAnArchitect|Lai An]], [[ShanghaiFrenchConcessionArchitecture]], and the built environment that later became associated with tree-lined "梧桐区" memory.

## Current Profile
The source presents the French Concession as a development zone shaped by architects, public bodies, foreign communities, Chinese elites, leisure facilities, and real-estate growth. It rejects a purely French stylistic reading: the area was administratively French but culturally and architecturally mixed, with Russian emigres, Jewish residents, British firms, Chinese architects, and local Shanghai practices all visible.

## Key Characteristics
- The French Concession supplied many of Lai An's commissions through French municipal and real-estate networks.
- Its built form is central to today's Hengfu and western Shanghai urban memory.
- Its cultural life is described as multinational rather than simply French.
- Its buildings carry both architectural style history and everyday residential memory.

- Geography evidence: [[498-xunzhao-laian-wei-jindai-shanghai-wutongqu-chuangzao-fengmao-de-faguo-jianzhushi-lskf8bdkhs71lbwjomt3gu1ko8be]] links Gao'an Road, Hengshan Road, Huaihai Road, Anting Road, Xuhui, and Hengfu-area apartments and villas to the old concession landscape.
- Multicultural evidence: [[498-xunzhao-laian-wei-jindai-shanghai-wutongqu-chuangzao-fengmao-de-faguo-jianzhushi-lskf8bdkhs71lbwjomt3gu1ko8be]] says many residents and cultural forms associated with "French" Shanghai were shaped by Russian Jewish and White Russian communities.
