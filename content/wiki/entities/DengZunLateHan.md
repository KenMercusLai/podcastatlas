## Overview
Deng Zun is a Deng-family military actor who received decisive court credit for ending the Langmo phase of the Qiang war and later remains part of the Deng-family political field disrupted after Empress Dowager Deng's death.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-790-lishi-gaosu-ni-ruhe-he-lingdao-da-jiaodao-lulf0r1sdkod3luzwru0-kkrijxk|Hanji 790]] casts Deng Zun as Duliao general and Deng-family insider. After the Xihe Qiang surrendered to him, he arranged through Diaohu for the assassination of the Xianling leader Langmo. The court rewarded the proximate assassin and enfeoffed Deng Zun as Wuyang marquis with a three-thousand-household grant, even though [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]] and [[MaXianLateHan|Ma Xian]] had performed the earlier battlefield pursuit. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-794-yidai-nvjun-dengsui-qushi-hou-jing-quanjia-zaoyang-llmlhabdd09opca6-pr80q4mfoxi|Hanji 794]] then places Deng Zun inside the broader Deng-family reversal after Empress Dowager Deng's death.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines military office with Deng-family proximity to the regency center.
- Receives recognition for decisive completion rather than for the initial battlefield pursuit.
- Becomes the superior-linked actor against whom Ren Shang's credit competition becomes dangerous.
- Illustrates how frontier success could be translated into marquisate status during Deng-family ascendancy.
- Belongs to the wider Deng-family vulnerability exposed after the regent's death.

## Qualifications
The source note for Hanji 790 renders the name as "邓尊"; this page normalizes the figure as Deng Zun / 邓遵 because the political role, family placement, and common historical form align. Later evidence should preserve the source spellings if a split becomes necessary.

- Established Deng Zun as the Deng-family counterpart to Ren Shang in the Langmo credit dispute.
