## Overview

邓遵 / Deng Zun is a Deng-family military actor whose Qiang-front profile now begins with 116 CE cavalry operations and continues through the Langmo assassination reward that makes him the superior-linked counterpart to Ren Shang.

## Current Profile

Hanji 789 places Deng Zun in the 116 CE Qiang crisis before the Hanji 790 credit dispute. He is paired with [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]] against Qiang forces and uses Southern Xiongnu cavalry against [[LinChangQiang|Lin Chang]] at Linzhou, showing a practical cavalry adaptation during the Deng regency's northwest emergency.

Hanji 790 then casts Deng Zun as Duliao general and Deng-family insider. After Xihe Qiang surrender to him, he arranges through Diaohu for the assassination of Langmo. The court rewards the proximate assassin and enfeoffs Deng Zun as Wuyang marquis with a three-thousand-household grant, even though Ren Shang and [[MaXianLateHan|Ma Xian]] had performed the earlier battlefield pursuit. Hanji 794 keeps Deng Zun inside the wider Deng-family political field that becomes vulnerable after [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]] dies.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines military command with Deng-family proximity to the regency center.
- Uses allied cavalry and proxy assassination rather than only direct Han infantry pressure.
- Receives recognition for decisive completion after others have done costly pursuit work.
- Becomes the superior-linked actor against whom Ren Shang's credit competition becomes dangerous.
- Belongs to the wider Deng-family field later exposed by the post-Deng purge.

## Qualifications

The source notes render the name with both 邓尊 and 邓遵. This page keeps the normalized form 邓遵 / Deng Zun because the office, family placement, and campaign role align, while preserving source-specific spelling caution.

- Reframed Deng Zun as using a combined toolkit of allied cavalry, surrender management, and proxy killing.
