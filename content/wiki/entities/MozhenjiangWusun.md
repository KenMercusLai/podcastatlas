## Overview

末振将 / Mozhenjiang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-550-sha-diguo-taizi-xiatui-jiqian-zhuibing-ta-zenme-zuodao-de-lvzhcjutqnu4or4elds_qg4s1gto|Hanji 550]] as the [[Wusun|乌孙]] small kunmi installed with [[DuanHuizongWesternHan|段会宗]]'s support, then condemned after he has the large kunmi Cilim killed.

## Current Profile

The source presents Mozhenjiang as both beneficiary and violator of Han-backed order. After the earlier small kunmi Anri is killed, Duan Huizong helps install Mozhenjiang as small kunmi to restore stability. Mozhenjiang later fears being swallowed by the strong large kunmi Cilim and sends a noble, Wuriling, in a feigned-surrender plot that kills Cilim.

Mozhenjiang is then killed by the large kunmi side and [[AnlimiWusun|安犁靡]] replaces him as small kunmi. Han's remaining punitive demand falls on Mozhenjiang's son [[FanqiuWusun|番丘]], whom Duan later kills. Mozhenjiang therefore marks the failure of the two-kunmi balance: the actor installed to stabilize one side uses deception to kill the other.

## Key Characteristics

- Wusun small kunmi installed after Anri's death.
- Initially benefits from Han-backed succession repair.
- Fears absorption by the stronger large kunmi Cilim.
- Uses feigned surrender through Wuriling to kill Cilim.
- Becomes the cause of later Han punitive action against Fanqiu.
