# North Korea

[[getting-entrepreneurial-in-korea-summer-school]] adds North Korea as the constraint case against South Korea's development story. The episode says entrepreneurial talent exists through [[JessieNorthKorea]] and the [[DonjuMarketClass]], and that [[KimJongUn]] allowed some business education and market activity through actors such as [[AndreAbrahamian]] and [[Manmulsang]], but weak property rights and regime fear of alternative power centers block broad-based development.

North Korea appears in [[tech-20260212-0212-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260212-0212-mp-tech-pod-128]] as a state-linked crypto threat. [[AriRedbord]] says North Korea continues to attack cryptocurrency exchanges, making it part of the episode's broader [[VirtualAssetAMLRisk]] and national-security branch.

The country also appears indirectly in [[dhaka-matters-an-election-for-bangladesh-698c5a3afeb59e13a3b8a94d]] through [[CandidateIdentityFraud]]. That source says [[Amazon]] blocked almost 2,000 applications from North Koreans applying for remote IT jobs. Together, the pages make North Korea a recurring wiki example of remote digital access turning into security, compliance, and financial-crime exposure.
