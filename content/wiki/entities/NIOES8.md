# NIO ES8 / 蔚来 ES8

NIO ES8 / 蔚来 ES8 is the premium SUV case in [[143-wei-xiao-li-de-gaoduan-tansuo-feng-tou-quan-1-143-1]]. The episode says the model carries much of [[NIO]]'s current brand and volume story, while also making the company's [[EVBatteryLeasing]] logic visible: a roughly 400,000 RMB list-price vehicle can feel closer to a sub-300,000 RMB purchase when the battery is leased.

The source uses ES8 to show why [[BatterySwapInfrastructure]] matters commercially. If battery swapping reduces anxiety about pack aging and residual value, the consumer can accept leasing as a rational payment structure rather than a simple affordability compromise. That helps NIO defend [[HighEndEVBranding]] while broadening the pool of buyers willing to try the brand.
