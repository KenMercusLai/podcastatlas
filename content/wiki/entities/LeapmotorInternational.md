# Leapmotor International / 零跑国际

## Overview
Leapmotor International / 零跑国际 is presented as the [[Stellantis]] and [[Leapmotor|零跑汽车]] joint venture responsible for Leapmotor business outside Greater China.

## Current Profile
The source frames Leapmotor International as an overseas commercialization vehicle rather than a conventional domestic-market joint venture. Stellantis owns the larger stake in the source description, while Leapmotor contributes EV technology and products. The current synthesis is that the company is meant to accelerate entry into Europe, South America, and other markets by borrowing Stellantis's local channels and service systems while still exposing Leapmotor to partner-speed and commitment constraints.

## Key Characteristics
- Reverse joint venture where Chinese EV technology is paired with overseas market infrastructure.
- Source-described 51% Stellantis and 49% Leapmotor ownership structure.
- Responsible for business outside Greater China.
- Uses partner channels, brand familiarity, service, finance, and insurance to reduce overseas entry friction.
- Faces coordination risk from different decision speeds and foreign-market protection.
