# Rachel Wyman

## Overview

Rachel Wyman is a baker and baking instructor who leads the attempt to reverse-engineer [[ThomasEnglishMuffins|Thomas' English muffins]] in [[the-formula-the-formula|The Formula]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Wyman as combining commercial product-development experience, ingredient knowledge, sensory testing, and iterative baking practice. Working with students at the [[CulinaryInstituteOfAmerica]], she adjusts hydration, proofing, flour, griddle preparation, fermentation, refrigeration, and storage until the muffins visually reproduce the target structure.

Her work also changes the experiment's goal. The vinegar-based version approaches the commercial product but remains distinguishable, while her sourdough variation is preferred by nearly 80 percent of testers. In this bounded case, expertise means learning the mechanism well enough to improve it rather than claiming an exact copy.

## Key Characteristics

- Baker and pastry-arts educator represented through one structured episode summary.
- Former commercial product developer experienced in reconstructing packaged foods.
- Uses iterative changes to both ingredients and process conditions.
- Involves students in observation, testing, and sensory evaluation.
- Distinguishes visual similarity, perceptual identity, and preference as separate outcomes.
