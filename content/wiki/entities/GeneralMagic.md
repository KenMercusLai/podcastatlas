# General Magic

General Magic is the Silicon Valley company at the center of [[we-almost-had-a-smartphone-in-the-90s-why-did-it-fail]]. The episode presents it as an early 1990s attempt to build a smartphone-like portable communication device and the surrounding computing ecosystem long before the [[IPhone]] made that category mainstream.

The source's main General Magic lesson is not that vision is useless. The company had elite talent, major partners such as [[Apple]], [[ATT|AT&T]], [[Motorola]], [[Sony]], and [[Panasonic]], and ideas that later resembled modern mobile computing. Its failure came from trying to build too much at once without enough [[ClearCustomerDefinition]], deadlines, budget discipline, or reuse of existing components.

General Magic therefore becomes a source case for [[ConstraintDrivenProductDiscipline]]. The same failure that produced the [[SonyMagicLink]] also gave [[TonyFadell]] a negative blueprint for later [[Apple]] work on the [[IPod]] and [[IPhone]].
