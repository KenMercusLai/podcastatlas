# 青少年黄斑变性关爱中心 / Juvenile Macular Degeneration Care Center

## Overview
青少年黄斑变性关爱中心 is a patient- and family-led information initiative described by [[HeiDeng|黑灯]] for people affected by juvenile macular degeneration, including [[StargardtDisease|Stargardt disease]].

## Current Profile
The current source describes a public account run by 黑灯 with two other affected people or family members. It emerged because newly diagnosed families found reliable, current information hard to locate and ordinary offline patient meetings did not necessarily surface global research developments.

Its reported role is information intermediation: monitor material across languages, track hospitals and research, make it findable for patients and parents, and help a small population articulate needs to clinicians, researchers, funders, and the public. This makes the center an example of [[PatientLedRareDiseaseInfrastructure]].

## Key Characteristics
- Organized from patient and family experience of information scarcity.
- Aggregates disease and research information across language and institutional boundaries.
- Seeks to reduce fear by making daily life, technology, work, and medical information more legible.
- Helps a small patient population become visible beyond ordinary market incentives.

## Qualifications
The page relies on the episode's account and does not independently audit the center's governance, clinical review, research partnerships, funding, update frequency, or current status. Aggregated information can support navigation but does not replace retinal-specialist diagnosis or treatment.
