# 乌骓马 / Wuzhui Horse

乌骓马 / Wuzhui Horse enters the wiki through the final [[GaixiaBattle|垓下之战]] sequence around [[XiangYu|项羽]]. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-168-bawangbieji-qian-fashengle-shenme-1-lowxwc8oq8as1trjo-gjimmuhnr3|Hanji 168 part 1]], the horse is part of [[GaixiaSong|《垓下歌》]]'s emotional compression: Xiang Yu's world has shrunk from overwhelming force to the horse's inability or refusal to move.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-168-bawangbieji-qian-fashengle-shenme-2-liqgict85wh-bf4fn2bytygmk-ca|Hanji 168 part 2]] then gives the horse a final concrete action. At [[WujiangChuHan|乌江]], Xiang Yu tells the [[WujiangPavilionChief|乌江亭长]] that the horse is still in its prime, has followed him in unstoppable campaigns, and should not die with him. He gives Wuzhui to the pavilion chief before fighting on foot.

The source therefore uses Wuzhui as more than battlefield color. The horse marks the boundary between what Xiang Yu still tries to preserve and what he refuses to preserve: he can save the horse, but not his own future or the remnants of his cause.
