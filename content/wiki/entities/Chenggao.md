# 成皋 / Chenggao

成皋 / Chenggao enters the wiki through the [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]] branch as the companion front to [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]] in the Chu-Han central theater. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-159-1-liubang-xijiao-de-yuren-zhishu-lga3puexunjzeu3s8pvt8saaa8n3|Hanji 159-1]] places it inside the broader struggle over the Xingyang, Chenggao, and [[Aocang|敖仓]] logistics zone.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-162-1-ta-ti-liubang-qu-si-rujin-chengle-zhengzhou-de-shouhushen-li3su2zlmbm1ift9nibujgtcrxwm|Hanji 162-1]] makes Chenggao part of [[YuanShengChuHan|袁先生 / 袁生]]'s strategic timing argument. After [[LiuBang|刘邦]] escapes Xingyang, Yuan Sheng advises him to draw [[XiangYu|项羽]] south by way of [[Wuguan|武关]] and to avoid battle, so that the Xingyang-Chenggao front can rest and repair.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-162-2-liubang-ruhe-shoushi-wei-gao-quan-zhong-de-xiashu-lqvye2zmqhu3otk0uwu7lakf2prz|Hanji 162-2]] gives Chenggao another reversal. Liu Bang retakes it while Xiang Yu goes east against [[PengYue|彭越]], but Xiang Yu later returns, retakes the line, and makes Liu Bang abandon the position. Chenggao's fall becomes the immediate trigger for Liu Bang's flight north and [[CommandAuthorityRecapture|统帅权再收束]] at [[HanXin|韩信]] and [[ZhangEr|张耳]]'s camp.
