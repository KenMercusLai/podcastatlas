# 上郡 / Shangjun

上郡 / Shangjun appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] as the much larger territorial gain [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] extracts from [[WeiState|魏国]] after [[QinState|秦国]] returns [[PuyangWarringStates|蒲阳]]. The source says [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]] gives Qin fifteen counties of Shangjun.

The place matters because it turns a seemingly generous concession into a net Qin victory. Puyang is the visible gift, but Shangjun is the strategic payoff, making the episode's [[StrategicConcessionDiplomacy|欲擒故纵式让利外交]] a land-transfer mechanism rather than only a rhetorical trick.
