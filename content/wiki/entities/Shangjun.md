# 上郡 / Shangjun

上郡 / Shangjun appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] as the much larger territorial gain [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] extracts from [[WeiState|魏国]] after [[QinState|秦国]] returns [[PuyangWarringStates|蒲阳]]. The source says [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]] gives Qin fifteen counties of Shangjun.

The place matters because it turns a seemingly generous concession into a net Qin victory. Puyang is the visible gift, but Shangjun is the strategic payoff, making the episode's [[StrategicConcessionDiplomacy|欲擒故纵式让利外交]] a land-transfer mechanism rather than only a rhetorical trick.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-85-xuantaihou-weihe-sitong-30-nian-hou-ansha-le-laoqingren-lg7zy6vqtdjcufou4-mox5qkg2ct5]] reuses Shangjun from a Qin-Wei acquisition as part of Qin's northern frontier structure. After [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]] helps Qin destroy [[YiquState|义渠]] and create [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]], the episode names Shangjun with [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]] and Beidi as the connected Qin defensive line against northern Hu groups.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-123-2-gudai-shiqi-de-daochamen-daodi-you-duo-weixian-lgcknwubwm3uzqmzgbgdlxlvcwxy]] moves Shangjun into the imperial frontier branch. After [[MengTian|蒙恬]] defeats [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], recovers the Hetao region, and helps connect older defensive works into the long-wall system, the source says he sits at Shangjun for more than a decade. Shangjun therefore becomes not only a Warring States territorial gain but a command base for Qin's post-unification northern war.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-127-2-lisi-weisha-bu-bang-yingming-de-fusu-lq-vny5duwwzfubpeijr21oazxbg]] makes Shangjun the place from which [[FuSu|扶苏]] and [[MengTian|蒙恬]] receive the forged death order after Qin Shi Huang's final-tour death. Fusu's frontier assignment from the坑儒 branch therefore becomes the very location that lets [[ZhaoGao|赵高]], [[LiSi|李斯]], and [[QinErshi|胡亥]] remove the legitimate heir while still away from Xianyang.
