# 刘范 / Liu Fan (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-973-changpingguan-zhizhan-shengsi-zhidi-zhanhou-jing-chonggui-yuhao-lgazc9dewfayb8qnkncfkfa12yet|Hanji 973]] names Liu Fan directly inside the Chang'an plot. He works with [[ZhongShaoLateHan|种劭]] and [[MaYuLateHan|马宇]] to support [[MaTeng|马腾]]'s attack against [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]], but the plan leaks and the plotters flee to Huaili, where they are killed after [[FanChouLateHan|樊稠]]'s side wins.

刘范 / Liu Fan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]] as one of [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s sons serving in [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]]. In the episode's account, Liu Yan secretly supports [[MaTeng|马腾]] and [[HanSui|韩遂]] against [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]], while Liu Fan and [[LiuDanLateHan|刘诞]] are expected to function as internal partners.

The plan is exposed by Li Jue's intelligence network. Liu Fan is killed while fleeing, making him part of the failed Guanzhong intervention that damages Liu Yan's family position before the [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] succession moves to [[LiuZhang|刘璋]].
