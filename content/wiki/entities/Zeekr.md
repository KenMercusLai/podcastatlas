# Zeekr / 极氪

Zeekr appears in [[buai-zhibo-daihuo-de-oumei-xiaofeizhe-weihe-zai-zhejia-pingtai-shang-daba-huaqian-1007104138]] as [[GeelyAutomobile|Geely Automobile]]'s EV brand and integration pressure point. The source says Zeekr was founded in 2021 and had become roughly one third of Geely Auto revenue in the latest half-year financials cited by the episode.

The source treats Zeekr as a sign of Geely's EV transition rather than as a complete standalone company profile. Its relevance is that leadership succession from [[LiShufu|李书福]] to [[AnConghui|安聪慧]] happens while the group is still trying to integrate and scale new-energy businesses.
