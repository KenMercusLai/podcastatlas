# 李昊 / Li Hao (late Han)

[[LiHaoLateHan|李昊]] appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-903-weishenme-quan-ni-yuanyuan-xiangbao-heshi-le-lokpt93muc8kasvn44vgsjki8bom|Hanji 903]] as the target of [[SuBuweiLateHan|苏不韦]]'s revenge. Su fails to kill Li directly, then violates Li's father's grave, decapitates the corpse, and displays the head publicly. Li reports Su but the pursuit is weak, and the episode says Li later dies from anger and fear.

Li's narrative function is to show how a feud can continue after formal complaint and inadequate enforcement. His relationship with [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]] also matters because Duan later uses private coercion to destroy Su's household, extending Li's injury into a new wave of violence.
