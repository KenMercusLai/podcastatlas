# Symmachus

## Overview

Symmachus was a late Roman senator and pagan spokesman represented in the source through his defense of restoring the Altar of Victory and tolerating Rome's inherited civic religion.

## Current Profile

In [[46-culture-wars-glt4237848862]], Symmachus supplies the accommodation side of a proto-culture-war dispute. He argues for inherited plurality and toleration after the altar's removal, while [[AmbroseOfMilan|Ambrose]] rejects compromise with sacrifice understood as demonic. The episode uses this opposition as an analogy for later disputes over whether inherited symbols can be contextualized or must be removed.

## Key Characteristics

- Late Roman senatorial defender of traditional civic religion.
- Advocate for restoration of the Altar of Victory.
- Represents toleration and inherited pluralism in the episode's interpretive contrast.

## Evidence

- Altar dispute: [[46-culture-wars-glt4237848862]] presents his appeal for toleration against Ambrose's refusal to compromise with pagan sacrifice.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one conversational episode and its culture-war analogy. It does not reconstruct Symmachus's wider career, texts, political network, or the full legal and religious history of the altar dispute.
