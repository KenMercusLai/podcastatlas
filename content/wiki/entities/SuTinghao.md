# Su Tinghao / 苏廷昊

Su Tinghao / 苏廷昊 is the teenage AI researcher interviewed in [[151-17sui-bei-2026-nian-icml-shoulu-lunwen-de-xiao-shaonian-wo-bet-kaixin-kaixin-kaixin-lgs-qedm2hdrxrfkgnsphg4i5h5u]]. The source says he was born in 2009, moved from Shanghai to [[HongKong|Hong Kong]], attends [[GermanSwissInternationalSchool|香港德瑞国际学校]], and had a high-school [[AttentionProjectionResiduals|attention projection residual]] paper accepted by [[InternationalConferenceOnMachineLearning|ICML 2026]].

The source presents him as a concrete [[AINativeYouthResearch|AI-native youth research]] case. He enters AI through [[ChatGPT]], public courses, [[AndrejKarpathy|Andrej Karpathy]] videos, paper reading, and repeated small-model experiments rather than through a traditional university lab path. His experience also exposes the limits of that route: compute cost, messy code, checkpoint failures, scarce same-age peers, and the need for [[ResearchTaste]] remain real bottlenecks.

His life framing is as important as the technical story. Su worries about [[AIJobSecurityAnxiety]], AI extinction risk, and younger people's motivation under smarter AI, but he answers those anxieties through [[HumanAgencyUnderAI]], human relationships, and the goal of making himself and people he likes happier.
