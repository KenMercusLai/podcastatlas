# William Franklin

## Overview

William Franklin is Benjamin Franklin's son and the loyalist governor of New Jersey who appears in the episode as the family-level counterpoint to Franklin's move toward American independence.

## Current Profile

The source uses William Franklin sparingly but sharply. His importance is not an independent governorship narrative; it is the way his loyalism makes Benjamin Franklin's break with Britain concrete. When he tries to persuade his father to remain neutral, Benjamin Franklin's preference for independence shows that the imperial rupture has reached inside the family.

## Key Characteristics

- Son of [[BenjaminFranklin|Benjamin Franklin]].
- Loyalist governor of New Jersey during the revolutionary crisis.
- Tried to persuade Franklin to remain neutral rather than support independence.
- Embodies the personal and familial cost of [[ColonialLoyaltyRupture|colonial loyalty rupture]].
