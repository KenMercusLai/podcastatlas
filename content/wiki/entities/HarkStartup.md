# Hark Startup

## Overview
Hark is a source-described startup used as a case of exchanging a small consumer benefit for access to personal account data.

## Current Profile
The episode says Hark offered free pizza to people who registered and connected Gmail. The promotion is relevant less as a settled company profile than as a stress test: it reportedly attracted enough concurrent demand to produce incorrect charges, delivery to wrong addresses, and system failure.

The case connects incentive design to [[AgentTrustCalibration]] and [[LongHorizonAgentReliability]]. A user may accept broad data access for immediate convenience, but that willingness does not demonstrate informed consent, reliable execution, or adequate operational capacity.

## Key Characteristics
- Used free pizza as an incentive for registration and Gmail connection.
- Illustrates that small benefits can motivate access to valuable personal data.
- Reportedly suffered incorrect charging and delivery-address errors.
- Reportedly failed under high concurrent demand.
- Provides a source-scoped caution about data incentives, permissions, and execution capacity.
