# 卫声公 / Duke Sheng of Wey

卫声公 / Duke Sheng of Wey appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] as the [[WeyState|卫国]] ruler who dies in 373 BCE. The source says Wei Xun then succeeds as [[WeyChenggong|卫成公]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-11-shijie-fengqi-yunyong-2-llzzalowv5vmbvgd6l-jujltkbyf]] creates a tension with that earlier page state by placing a Wey ruler rendered as 卫声公 dying in 362 BCE and by linking the successor's title to [[WeakStateStatusDemotion|weak-state status demotion]]. Until the underlying name/title sequence is rechecked, this page keeps both notices visible rather than flattening them into one settled date.

The page is source-scoped and mainly supports the episode's compact succession list for [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]]'s third year.
