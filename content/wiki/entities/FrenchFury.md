# French Fury

The French Fury appears in [[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] as [[FrancoisDukeOfAnjou|Francois, Duke of Anjou]]'s failed January 1583 coup attempt in Antwerp. Anjou had been proclaimed Prince and Lord of the Netherlands but wanted greater authority than the [[DutchRevolt]] leadership was willing to grant.

The coup failed and Anjou left for [[France]] by June 1583. The episode treats this failure as the collapse of [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]]'s preferred diplomatic solution for the [[LowCountries|Low Countries]] crisis: a French-backed Dutch settlement might have constrained [[Spain]] without forcing England into direct intervention.

In the wiki, the French Fury is therefore an inflection point before [[TreatyOfNonsuch]]. It helps explain why the later English move across the [[StrategicInterventionThreshold]] followed failed French alternatives rather than immediate English enthusiasm for war.
