# 新垣衍 / Xinyuan Yan

新垣衍 / Xinyuan Yan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-2-ltlobdujy-hdipimfdupvwod2ech]] as the [[WeiState|魏国]] envoy or commander sent secretly into [[Handan|邯郸]] while [[JinBi|晋鄙]]'s rescue army is stalled at [[YeWarringStates|邺城]]. The episode treats him as [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]]'s representative rather than as a detached private adviser.

His proposal is to persuade [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] through [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] that Wei and Zhao should honor [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] as western emperor. The source reads this not as an empty honorific but as a surrender-like settlement route: by acknowledging Qin's superior imperial status, Zhao might induce Qin to withdraw from Handan.

The episode emphasizes that Xin Yuan Yan's plan becomes plausible because outside rescue is failing. [[ChuState|楚国]] has agreed to help through [[MaoSui|毛遂]]'s negotiation, but it cannot easily reach Zhao without Wei transit; Wei's own main army has stopped. Xin Yuan Yan therefore marks the moment when stalled coalition rescue converts into a proposed status concession to Qin.
