# 乌浒蛮 / Wuhu Man (Jiaozhi)

乌浒蛮 / Wuhu Man (Jiaozhi) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-910-shehui-shengcun-wo-quan-ni-duodian-chengfu-lp2fvuyb8ampzoeyr7znztnr0fgk|Hanji 910]] as a southern rebel group whose 178 CE outbreak stirs JiuZhen and Rinan people and breaks local commandery/county order. This page uses the semantic suffix "Jiaozhi" to avoid collision with unrelated Wuhu place names and to keep the southern-frontier identity clear.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-920-mingwu-zhege-daoli-caineng-zhuan-daqian-lqmabg3bpyz6ct2v4arx-iun57ws|Hanji 920]] later shows the rebellion as a long-running southern crisis around [[JiaozhiCommandery|交趾郡]]. The source transcript renders the name as "乌虎蛮"; it says commandery and province officials could not settle the rebellion, [[LiangLongLateHan|梁龙]] and other Jiaozhi people then rose as well, and [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]] was sent as inspector to kill the rebel leaders and restore order in less than a month.
