# 乌浒蛮 / Wuhu Man (Jiaozhi)

乌浒蛮 / Wuhu Man (Jiaozhi) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-920-mingwu-zhege-daoli-caineng-zhuan-daqian-lqmabg3bpyz6ct2v4arx-iun57ws|Hanji 920]] as a southern rebel group around [[JiaozhiCommandery|交趾郡]]. The source transcript renders the name as "乌虎蛮"; this page uses the semantic suffix "Jiaozhi" to avoid collision with unrelated Wuhu place names and to keep the southern-frontier identity clear.

The source says the Wuhu Man rebellion lasted long enough that commandery and province officials could not settle it. [[LiangLongLateHan|梁龙]] and other Jiaozhi people then rose as well, and [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]] was sent as inspector to kill the rebel leaders and restore order in less than a month.
