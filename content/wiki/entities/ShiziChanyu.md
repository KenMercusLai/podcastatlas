## Overview
师子 / Shizi was a Southern Xiongnu aristocrat and later chanyu whose current page begins with his survival of [[AnGuoSouthernXiongnu|An Guo's]] jealous plot. He represents the problem of subordinate or collateral success inside a fragile hierarchy.

## Current Profile
Hanji 768 identifies Shizi as the son of the earlier chanyu Xuan. He had served as right guli king and then left wise king, was brave and strategic, and enjoyed respect from former chanyus, the Han court, and Southern Xiongnu people. When An Guo plotted to kill him, [[HuangfuLengLateHan|Huangfu Leng]] protected him by keeping him from court meetings; after An Guo's failed attack and death, Shizi was installed as chanyu.

## Key Characteristics
- High-status heir-line figure with both military reputation and political legitimacy.
- Popular across multiple audiences: earlier Xiongnu leadership, the Han court, and Xiongnu followers.
- Survived because he avoided exposed meetings and had protection from Huangfu Leng.
- Became chanyu after An Guo's failed attempt to eliminate him.
- Serves as a negative counterpart to stable delegation: his success made an insecure superior more dangerous.

## Evidence
- Reputation and legitimacy: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-768-lishi-gaosu-ni-jidu-shi-shaxin-lgk8qoxvs5ax8oikgecrxqvispna|Hanji 768]] describes Shizi as brave, strategic, trusted by prior chanyus and the Han court, and respected by Xiongnu people.
- Protection from exposure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-768-lishi-gaosu-ni-jidu-shi-shaxin-lgk8qoxvs5ax8oikgecrxqvispna|Hanji 768]] says Huangfu Leng kept Shizi from attending An Guo's court meetings because An Guo intended to kill him.
- Installation as chanyu: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-768-lishi-gaosu-ni-jidu-shi-shaxin-lgk8qoxvs5ax8oikgecrxqvispna|Hanji 768]] says An Guo's uncle Xi killed An Guo and installed Shizi after the crisis.
