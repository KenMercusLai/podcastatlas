# 《顺流而上》 / Upstream Documentary

《顺流而上》 / Upstream Documentary is the outdoor-sports documentary referenced in [[ep250-zoujin-shanye-shunliu-ershang-xunzhao-huwai-yundong-zhong-de-xinliu-shike-lpv4egumbxcjrxk3jypehyxcqfuo]]. The episode uses it to discuss [[YangXiaohuaSkier|杨小华]], women athletes, skiing practice, and the idea that a person may orient life around a long-term direction without extracting a peak experience from every single day.

Within the source, the documentary matters less as a film-review object than as a phrase and frame. The speakers read "顺流而上" as following an inner current: choosing outdoor practice, rest, parks, harder goals, or ordinary movement according to one's own direction while still preparing and protecting oneself. That makes the documentary part of the episode's [[OutdoorFlowState]] and [[WomenOutdoorVisibility]] branch.
