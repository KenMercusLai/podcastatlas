# Ashwaira Khanduja

## Overview
Ashwaira Khanduja is the speaker who opens the Entangled Intelligences event by linking intelligence to uncertainty, inquiry, institutional support, and revisable action.

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Khanduja argues that cells, organisms, people, and institutions must sense, remember, coordinate, and act without seeing the whole system. Her practical emphasis is not indecision but responsible experimentation: preserve time for exploration, listen without predetermining the answer, attend to consequences, and build systems capable of changing as they learn.

## Key Characteristics
- Defines uncertainty as a condition that makes intelligence necessary.
- Treats inquiry as capable of changing the original question.
- Critiques funding and institutional processes that demand predicted discoveries in advance.
- Connects courage with attention, consequence, and willingness to revise.
