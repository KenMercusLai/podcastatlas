## Current Profile

The source set gives Yu Dingguo as a high-status predecessor rather than a full biography. Hanji 524 says he replaces Huang Ba as chancellor, is enfeoffed as Xiping Hou, remains respected after Han Yuan's accession, and is later allowed to retire with carriage, horses, and gold. Hanji 471 gives one concrete policy judgment before that retirement: Yu sides with Jia Juanzhi's capacity-bound argument that Zhuya and Dan'er should be abandoned under famine and campaign-cost pressure. Hanji 476 places his eventual retirement in a disaster-accountability sequence, where resignation absorbs political responsibility after a famine shock. He dies in his seventies, and Hanji 524 treats that end as relatively fortunate.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han senior official and father of [[YuYongWesternHan|于永]].
- Replaces Huang Ba as chancellor in the episode's retrospective.
- Receives the Xiping Hou title.
- Retains respect under Han Yuan.
- Supports Jia Juanzhi's Zhuya-abandonment recommendation against a suppression line.
- Resigns during the Yongguang 1 disaster-accountability sequence and retires with honor gifts.
- Dies in old age, treated by Hanji 524 as a relatively fortunate official end.
