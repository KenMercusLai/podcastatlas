## Overview

于定国 / Yu Dingguo enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-524-weishenme-youxie-laoban-xihuan-rang-gaoguan-neidou-lsueswk4fqtcytd-m_gziubwmlsz|Hanji 524]] as the father of [[YuYongWesternHan|于永]] and a Western Han senior official whose career background helps explain Yu Yong's prestige. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-476-kongzi-de-12-shisun-kongba-jielu-zhichang-yinmi-ltjlhphlrqnazeqh2cc2hgsvrvwc|Hanji 476]] adds the Yongguang 1 / 43 BCE exit scene: after cold, crop failure, famine, and displacement, Yu Dingguo submits a responsibility-taking resignation alongside [[ShiGaoWesternHan|史高]] and [[XueGuangdeWesternHan|薛广德]], and [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] accepts it with honor gifts.

## Current Profile

The source set gives Yu Dingguo as a high-status predecessor rather than a full biography. Hanji 524 says he replaces Huang Ba as chancellor, is enfeoffed as Xiping Hou, remains respected after Han Yuan's accession, and is later allowed to retire with carriage, horses, and gold. Hanji 476 places that retirement in a disaster-accountability sequence, where resignation absorbs political responsibility after a famine shock. He dies in his seventies, and Hanji 524 treats that end as relatively fortunate.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han senior official and father of [[YuYongWesternHan|于永]].
- Replaces Huang Ba as chancellor in the episode's retrospective.
- Receives the Xiping Hou title.
- Retains respect under Han Yuan.
- Resigns during the Yongguang 1 disaster-accountability sequence and retires with honor gifts.
- Dies in old age, treated by Hanji 524 as a relatively fortunate official end.
