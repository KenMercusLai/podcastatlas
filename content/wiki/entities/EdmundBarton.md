# Edmund Barton

## Overview

Edmund Barton was Australia's first prime minister and a federation leader whom the episode uses to connect constitutional nation-building, cricket, and a public ideal of fair play.

## Current Profile

[[187-australian-prime-ministers-edmund-barton-robert-menzies-part-1-glt3017584065]] presents Barton less through a full policy biography than through federation and the 1879 Sydney cricket riot. His support for the umpire's authority becomes an emblem of rule-bound public conduct, while federation is placed within both a desire for unity and contemporary strategic and racial anxiety.

## Key Characteristics

- First prime minister of federated [[Australia]].
- Advocate and institutional figure of Australian federation.
- Associated in the episode with cricket and fair-play norms.
- Positioned within a federation politics shaped partly by regional-security anxiety.
