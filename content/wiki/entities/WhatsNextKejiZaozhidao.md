# What's Next｜科技早知道

[[zhongting-zhuque-sanhao-fuzongshi-huojian-huishou-zhiyou-cheng-yu-bai-meiyou-zhongjiantai-s9e41-d9e11e3b-7c7e-4beb-b19f-6f90fff38714]] adds a replayed aerospace branch through [[DongKai]] of [[LandSpace]]. The episode connects [[Zhuque3]], [[RocketRecoveryBinaryOutcome]], [[ReuseFirstRocketDesign]], [[RecoveryRangePayloadTradeoff]], [[ReusableRocketEconomics]], and [[ReusableRocketTurnaround]] to the show's existing recovery-route discussion from S10E21.

[[yushu-shangshi-baozhang-dan-renxing-jiqiren-de-qian-daodi-cong-nali-zhuan-s10e26-4a50d4a3-a6ff-4c89-b754-367b73ce924b]] adds the show's public-robotics and valuation branch through [[UnitreeRobotics|宇树科技]]. The episode treats Unitree's listing surge as a market milestone rather than proof that humanoid robots already have full [[HumanoidRobotCommercialization|commercial PMF]], connecting [[WangXingxing|王兴兴]], [[RobotRepurchaseDemand]], [[UnitreeIPOValuation]], [[DisneyRobotExperienceCommercialization]], [[WorldLabs]], and [[NewtonPhysicsEngine]].

[[yong-ai-rang-women-bianbenle-ma-s10e25-a6b360da-07f7-4d9e-ab1b-64a7130e9254]] adds the show's AI-and-cognition branch through [[Yaxian|雅贤]]. Instead of asking only whether AI can do more work, the episode asks what happens when users outsource memory, search, organization, reasoning, and writing practice. It connects [[CognitiveOffloading]], [[CognitiveDebt]], [[AIGuidedLearningGuardrails]], [[DesirableDifficulty]], [[Neuroplasticity]], [[MemoryConsolidationWindows]], and [[SleepAsDailyHealthAccount]].

[[jia-yangqing-wo-suo-jingli-de-rengongzhineng-yisi-dao-ai-dianfu-shijie-de-shunian-jubian-chuantai-shengdongjixi-s10e24-a3884ade-4669-4d5c-ab2e-f98aa580f429]] adds the show's AI-history and agent-reliability branch through a crossover with [[ShengdongJixi|声东击西]]. [[XuTao|徐涛]] interviews [[JiaYangqing|贾扬清]] on the path from [[Caffe]] and [[GoogleBrain|Google Brain]] to [[FacebookAIInfra|Facebook AI Infra]], [[AlibabaCloud|Alibaba Cloud]], [[LeptonAI|Lepton AI]], [[Nvidia]], [[AgentReliabilityVerification]], and [[WhatOverHowWorkShift]].

[[7000-yi-meiyuan-za-xiang-ai-zhe-shi-xiayidai-hulianwang-haishi-paomo-chongyan-s10e12-7af0955b-e3b5-4b40-9ccf-90ec061bbf52]] adds the show's AI capex and market-bubble branch through [[AaronWhatsNext|Aaron]]. The episode connects [[AICapexReturnWindow]], [[AIRevenueLegibility]], [[AICircularInfrastructureFinancing]], and [[ChinaUSAIValuationAsymmetry]] to the practical question of whether hyperscaler data centers, GPUs, cloud capacity, agents, and consumer AI applications can become visible revenue fast enough for public markets.

[[tanfang-hacker-house-guigu-nianqingren-zhengzai-banjin-ai-chuangye-sushe-s10e10-f0870843-fd16-49f5-8f24-80281f9fff77]] adds the show's Silicon Valley startup-infrastructure branch through [[TheResidency]]. The episode connects hacker houses, [[FounderLifestyleInfrastructure]], [[FounderNetworkArbitrage]], and [[EquityHackerHouseModel]] to the broader question of how AI-era founders find peers, capital, and legitimacy before ordinary fundraising rounds.

[[huawei-de-tao-dinglv-shi-chuangxin-haishi-xuetou-bonus-e471f937-616b-4f49-a7ae-49137d32dbe5]] adds a short semiconductor bonus episode with [[ZhangHaijun]] on [[Huawei]]'s [[TauLaw]]. It links the show's existing AI infrastructure and memory coverage to [[CellToCellLogicStacking]], [[ElectronicDesignAutomation|EDA]], [[AdvancedPackaging]], and the question of when a system-time metric becomes proven chip engineering.

[[guochan-ai-suanli-neng-ping-chaojiedian-wandao-chaoche-ma-waic-shendu-guancha-s10e23-a6c6ab3e-72b2-470b-aefd-04b19679d37f]] adds the show's [[WAIC]] hardware-infrastructure branch through [[ZhangHaijun]], [[HuaweiCM384]], [[NvidiaGB200NVL72]], and Chinese [[AIAcceleratorSupernode|AI supernodes]]. The episode connects domestic chip catch-up to [[ScaleUpAIInterconnect]], [[ProprietaryAIInterconnectFragmentation]], power, cooling, optical/copper tradeoffs, and [[DomesticAIChipOrderValidation]].

[[zhenzheng-gaibian-shijie-de-jishu-weishenme-yikaishi-dou-bu-bei-kanhao-s10e16-8c95b3dc-d75a-4bdd-84d4-2c06fd2d85b1]] adds the show's longer semiconductor-history branch through [[WangBo|汪波]] and [[ABriefHistoryOfChips|《芯片简史》]]. The episode connects [[MooreLaw]], [[MOSFET]], [[DisruptiveInnovation]], [[TauLaw]], and [[DomainKnowHowMoat]] to AI-era technology anxiety and professional judgment.

[[yi-ren-gongsi-de-lingyizhong-keneng-ai-fuze-jingying-renlei-fuze-reai-yingwen-fangtan-s10e14-33e95bf5-9dd2-45d7-9b5f-6e05a078f2d7]] adds the show's [[SahilLavingia]] and [[Gumroad]] branch. The episode treats [[OnePersonCompany]] as a small-company operating question rather than a literal solo endpoint: AI can reduce engineering, support, and operations workload, but [[CustomerPull]], [[FounderLedSales]], [[TrustAsBusinessAsset]], and [[HumanJudgmentUnderAI]] still decide whether a product becomes a business.

[[google-de-ai-celve-bu-du-moxing-du-shenme-google-cloud-next-xianchang-s10e09-073d7ee7-7bac-4958-b45a-083cc2f866e6]] adds the show's Google Cloud Next branch. The episode connects [[Google]], [[GoogleCloud]], [[TPU]], [[Gemini]], and enterprise agents to the show's existing AI-work and AI-terminal discussions, with the main emphasis on [[FullStackAIPlatform]], [[EnterpriseAgentGovernance]], and [[CapabilityOverhang]].
