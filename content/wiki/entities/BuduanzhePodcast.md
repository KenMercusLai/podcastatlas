# 不端者（端传媒播客）

## Overview
[[BuduanzhePodcast|《不端者》]] is a Duan Media podcast introduced in an October 2026 programming announcement as a companion program to [[DuanwenNewsPodcast|《端闻》]].

## Current Profile
The bounded evidence establishes a launch format built around a named guest and a new book: author [[FengZheyun|冯哲云]] is announced for the first episode to discuss *为故土焚心：叙利亚战场上的维吾尔人*. It also establishes distribution and cadence rather than a demonstrated editorial method: the show alternates with 《端闻》 on Fridays and is promoted across major podcast/video platforms and Duan Media's own channels.

The announcement suggests attention to contested, cross-regional, or overlooked public subjects, but it contains none of the first interview. The program therefore cannot yet be characterized from episode-level evidence as investigative, argumentative, or representative of any stable editorial line.

## Key Characteristics
- It launches within Duan Media's existing podcast network alongside [[DuanwenNewsPodcast|《端闻》]].
- Its first announced format joins an author guest to discussion of a new nonfiction book.
- Its first advertised subject concerns Uyghurs connected to the Syrian battlefield and their relationship to homeland.
- It alternates with 《端闻》 in the Friday release slot.
- It is distributed through Apple Podcasts, [[Spotify]], [[YouTube]], and Duan Media's app and website.
