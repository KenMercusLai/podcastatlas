# Andy Stumpf

## Overview
Andy Stumpf is the [[HubermanLab]] guest in the [[the-mental-frame-specific-daily-actions-to-succeed-andy-stumpf-scim2498586099]] source note, where [[AndrewHuberman]] frames him as a retired Navy SEAL, former Red Bull high-performance wingsuiter, world-record-setting wingsuit pilot, podcaster, business owner, jiu-jitsu practitioner, father, and author of [[Drownproof]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Stumpf less as an invulnerable elite performer than as a practical teacher of agency under pressure. His durable role in the wiki is to connect military experience, extreme sport, family hardship, mental-health vulnerability, and daily discipline through concrete tools. [[ConcernInfluenceSorting]] gives his attention-management frame, [[SlightlyHarderChoice]] gives his micro-discipline frame, and the wingsuiting material gives a risk-calibration frame where survival is not automatically proof of competence. His divorce, estrangement, surgery, and suicide discussion make vulnerability part of the profile rather than a side note.

## Key Characteristics
- Translates elite military and extreme-sport experience into ordinary self-management tools rather than a heroic image.
- Uses [[ConcernInfluenceSorting]] to redirect attention from diffuse concern toward direct influence and response choice.
- Treats discipline as repeated small actions that are often invisible to other people.
- Calibrates risk by asking whether apparent success came from competence or from merely surviving a narrow margin.
- Keeps vulnerability visible through divorce, parenting estrangement, pain disclosure, veteran transition, and suicide discussion.
- Judges success against relationships, family, fulfillment, and knowing enough rather than endless goal escalation.
