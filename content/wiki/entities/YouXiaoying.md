# 游晓颖 / You Xiaoying

游晓颖 is presented in [[ep254-you-xiaoying-x-wenqi-x-du-sujuan-nvxing-zhe-yisheng-dou-zai-fanfu-xuexi-xuke-ziji-lm-w7ryj4zay6vldsrkadohj997d]] as the screenwriter of [[WoXukeFilm|《我许可》]]. Her role in the episode is both creative and autobiographical: she explains why [[WenQi|文淇]] could carry the character's inner world, then uses her own menarche, postpartum exam, body acceptance, driving, accounting, and screenwriter-credit stories to make [[SelfPermission|self-permission]] practical.

The source makes her a strong case for [[FemaleSelfPossession]] because she links permission to work discipline as well as personal feeling. After gaining more industry status, she says she would not accept a credited screenwriter role unless the words were actually written by her, turning authorship into a boundary rather than a prestige label.
