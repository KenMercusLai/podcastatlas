# Marcus Livius Drusus / 马库斯·李维乌斯·德鲁苏斯

## Overview

Marcus Livius Drusus / 马库斯·李维乌斯·德鲁苏斯 is the Roman reformer whose failed 91 BC citizenship proposal and assassination become the immediate political prelude to the [[SocialWar]] in episode 702.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Drusus to clarify why Italian citizenship was not a marginal issue. His proposal to extend full Roman citizenship to the allies is presented by supporters as a way to expand manpower and stabilize Rome's relationship with Italy. Opponents fear that the city-state institutions of the [[RomanRepublic|Roman Republic]] cannot absorb millions of new citizens spread across the peninsula.

Drusus's defeat and assassination convert a constitutional problem into a legitimacy rupture. After his death, Italian anger no longer stays contained inside Roman reform politics and the Social War begins.

## Key Characteristics

- Drusus represents the citizenship-reform path before allied revolt.
- His proposal links rights expansion to Roman manpower and Italian stabilization.
- Opposition to him centers on whether Rome's institutions can absorb a much larger citizen body.
- His assassination helps shift the crisis from legislation into war.
