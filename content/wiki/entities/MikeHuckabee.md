# Mike Huckabee

Mike Huckabee appears in [[settler-bet-a-west-bank-flashpoint-6a842bb539e73ea8f3c13a2d]] as the American ambassador in Jerusalem who became involved after a [[Kusra]] house owned by an American citizen was targeted in a settler incident. The source notes that Huckabee is usually very pro-settler, which makes his criticism of the attackers unusually salient.

In the episode, Huckabee's intervention matters because it shows one way local [[WestBank|West Bank]] incidents can reach the [[UnitedStates]] administration. The source treats the American-owned house as a pressure channel, not as proof that isolated criticism has changed the broader [[WestBankOutpostEscalation]] trend.
