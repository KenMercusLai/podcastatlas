# Bernt Bornich

## Overview
Bernt Bornich is the 1X founder and CEO voice in the All-In robotics special. He presents Neo as a household humanoid, open platform, telepresence surface, and route toward embodied AI.

## Current Profile
The episode gives Bornich the most expansive long-term thesis among the robotics guests. He combines near-term caution about early Neo units with a strong conviction that human-like robots, teleoperation data, broad video pretraining, and recursive physical automation could compress the path from household robots to robots building physical infrastructure.

## Key Characteristics
- He sets expectations that early Neo robots may be rough while still aiming for useful autonomy.
- He treats teleoperation as both a practical customer support layer and a high-quality data source.
- He argues that human-like form matters because it makes human video and internet video more relevant for robot learning.
- He wants Neo to support outside developers, model builders, and fine-tuned skills rather than operate only as a closed appliance.
- He makes a hard-takeoff forecast in which robots help build robots, data centers, chip fabs, mines, and refineries.
