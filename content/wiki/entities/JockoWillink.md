# Jocko Willink

## Overview
Jocko Willink appears in the wiki through a full [[HubermanLab]] conversation and its later Essentials cut, where [[AndrewHuberman]] uses his military, leadership, training, and identity experience to discuss discipline, resilience, detachment, distributed command, and action.

## Current Profile
The two source notes present Willink as a practical leadership and self-command figure. He does not define discipline as high motivation; he defines it as doing the required action without repeated internal negotiation. His profile is built around movement, recoverable discomfort, resilience after failure, emotional steadiness after wins and losses, and detachment as a concrete decision skill. The full conversation adds an organizational layer: communicate intent, distribute judgment, retain enough doctrine for inexperienced leaders, and fit people to the work they repeatedly do well. It also treats autonomy as a reward for competent performance and military transition as evidence that elite status in one setting does not guarantee fit in another.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats discipline as action that continues when motivation is absent.
- Uses physical training and deliberate discomfort as routes to energy, readiness, and resilience.
- Frames leadership as emotional counterbalance rather than emotional amplification.
- Converts loss and setbacks into mourning, analysis, and next action.
- Presents detachment as a practical field-of-view and breathing skill for decisions.
- Connects identity to independence, performance, and willingness to stand outside mainstream approval.
- Favors intent-led distributed judgment and differentiated team roles over detailed-order dependence or status-based placement.
