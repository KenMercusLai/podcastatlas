# Jocko Willink

## Overview
Jocko Willink appears in the wiki through a [[HubermanLab]] Essentials episode where [[AndrewHuberman]] uses his military, leadership, training, and identity experience to discuss discipline, resilience, detachment, and action.

## Current Profile
The current source presents Willink as a practical leadership and self-command figure. He does not define discipline as high motivation; he defines it as doing the required action without repeated internal negotiation. His profile in this note is built around movement, deliberate discomfort, resilience after failure, emotional steadiness after wins and losses, and detachment as a concrete decision skill. The episode also links his identity formation to military performance and hardcore music's do-it-yourself independence, but the wiki keeps that as source-bounded context rather than a full biography.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats discipline as action that continues when motivation is absent.
- Uses physical training and deliberate discomfort as routes to energy, readiness, and resilience.
- Frames leadership as emotional counterbalance rather than emotional amplification.
- Converts loss and setbacks into mourning, analysis, and next action.
- Presents detachment as a practical field-of-view and breathing skill for decisions.
- Connects identity to independence, performance, and willingness to stand outside mainstream approval.

- Leadership and morale - [[essentials-how-to-become-resilient-forge-your-identity-lead-others-jocko-willink-scim2090153799]] compares group morale to mob mentality and says leaders must counterbalance arrogance after success and collapse after failure.
