# 《长安的荔枝》 / The Lychee Road (film)

## Overview
《长安的荔枝》 is represented as [[DapengDirector|大鹏]]'s source-described most mature film, combining historical storytelling, demanding physical production, modern musical choices, and high award expectations.

## Current Profile
The interview uses the film to show Dapeng's increased execution range and his difficulty accepting sunk creative labor: he trained in riding and completed a complex real-horse shot that the final film did not use. It also shows authorial insistence, since he retained a modern rap passage after previews warned that it could break immersion.

## Key Characteristics
- Historical feature treated by its director as his most technically mature work to date.
- Production case where difficult and expensive work can be removed from the final cut.
- Authorial-choice case where a deliberately modern musical passage survives known audience resistance.
- Award-recognition case linking craft confidence to continuing external-validation pressure.

## Evidence
- Execution - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] describes year-long riding preparation and an unused horse-treadmill shot.
- Musical choice - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] says Dapeng retained a rap passage intended to bring a 755 CE setting closer to modern viewers despite preview objections.
- Recognition - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] describes multiple Golden Rooster nominations and Dapeng's disappointment at not receiving the major awards for which he was nominated.
