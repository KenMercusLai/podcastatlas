# 大泽乡起义 / Dazexiang Uprising

大泽乡起义 / Dazexiang Uprising enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-2-qilai-bei-yapo-de-renmen-lpx9-iicwog4nj-nudaj-i4ajb25|Qinji 128-2]] as the first major anti-Qin rupture after [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]]'s accession. The source places it in Qin Er Shi's first year, 209 BCE, after harsher law, revived construction, capital-guard mobilization, and commandery supply burdens have already intensified public misery.

In the source's direct-cause account, [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] and [[WuGuang|吴广]] are among nine hundred conscripted soldiers traveling toward Yuyang when heavy rain at Dazexiang makes the arrival deadline impossible. Because the source presents Qin law as executing late arrivals regardless of reason, the force is caught between death by compliance and death by flight. The event therefore becomes the main case for [[FatalDeadlineRebellionTrigger|误期死局式起义触发]].

Chen Sheng and Wu Guang first kill the officers commanding the unit, then turn shared danger into collective action. The source stresses that the uprising also needs a believable story: it invokes [[FuSu|扶苏]] and [[XiangYan|项燕]] as still-living symbols and uses fish-belly writing plus fox-cry rumor to create [[RebelMandateManufacture|起义天命制造]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-3-chensheng-wuguang-zudui-chenggong-locnaq-fo1fztyud5nghwkteams|Qinji 128-3]] extends the event from outbreak into expansion and regime formation. The rebels take Dazexiang and Qixian, gather more troops while capturing nearby counties, and enter Chen County with chariots, cavalry, and tens of thousands of infantry. The uprising therefore becomes the platform for a strategic choice between [[SixStateRestorationAntiQinStrategy|六国复国式反秦策略]] and Chen Sheng's direct creation of [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-5-wo-you-zhengju-zanmen-buneng-shuo-qinershi-sha-ye-bushi-ljuome-xea6sjv4hhzmrr0b0-1hg|Qinji 128-5]] follows the uprising into two divergent outcomes. On the Qin front, [[ZhouWenRebel|周文]]'s fast approach to [[Xianyang|咸阳]] fails against [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] and [[CapitalDefenseMobilization|都城防卫动员]]. On the rebel front, [[WuChen|武臣]] becomes Zhao king and chooses expansion over coordinated western attack, turning the uprising's success into [[AntiQinCoalitionFragmentation|反秦阵营诸侯化分裂]].
