# Japan Disaster Management Agency

## Overview

The Japan Disaster Management Agency is the dedicated national body described by [[in-the-life-of-prime-ais-latest-maths-triumph-0543079e679f77fa9c07ce929a73b21f]] as Japan’s attempt to replace fragmented, rotating disaster administration with durable expertise and pre-disaster coordination.

## Current Profile

The episode says the agency has roughly 350 staff, is operationally overseen by a minister, is formally led by the prime minister, and can recommend action to other ministries. Its practical test is not the organizational chart alone but whether it can align hospitals, evacuation plans, local government, communities, and households before Tokyo or Nankai Trough earthquakes.

## Key Characteristics

- Dedicated institution intended to accumulate disaster-management knowledge across events and official rotations.
- Cross-ministry coordinator with formal prime-ministerial leadership and ministerial daily oversight.
- Prevention-oriented body emphasizing preparation before a catastrophe rather than ad hoc response afterward.
- Planner for hospital continuity, prolonged blackouts, patient evacuation, shelter conditions, and public readiness.
- Dependent on local execution, mutual aid, and household preparation as well as central authority.
