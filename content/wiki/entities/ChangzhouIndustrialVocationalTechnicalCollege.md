# 常州工业职业技术学院 / Changzhou Industrial Vocational Technical College

Changzhou Industrial Vocational Technical College / 常州工业职业技术学院 is the vocational-school case in [[ep241-xiaoqi-hezuo-shi-xinyidai-de-tiefanwan-ma-lnmpsbvoqbexeardxyx88hywufig]]. [[LiuChang|刘畅]] describes its cooperation with [[LiAuto|理想汽车]], including a campus [[IndustrialCollege|产业学院]], vehicles and training facilities, and company training activity that takes place inside the school.

The school matters because it sits inside [[Changzhou|常州]]'s new-energy-vehicle supply-chain setting. The source says weaker employment and enrollment in some traditional majors made the school more willing to adjust toward enterprise-linked training, micro-specializations, and skills that map to nearby industry demand.
