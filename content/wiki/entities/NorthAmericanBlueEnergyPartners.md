# North American Blue Energy Partners

## Overview
[[NorthAmericanBlueEnergyPartners]] is the company named in the episode as the vehicle for a source-scoped Venezuelan oil concession. The wiki currently treats the entity as uncorroborated outside this source.

## Current Profile
In the episode, [[JasonCalacanis]] says the company received a 100-year concession over 17 Venezuelan oil fields totaling about 65 billion barrels after [[DonaldTrump]] closed a deal following the capture of [[NicolasMaduro]] and the rise of "President Rodriguez." The described structure gives the U.S. government majority control, the [[USDepartmentOfDefense]] an equity stake, and the [[USDepartmentOfState]] rights to buy some output at cost. The claim materially extends the wiki's Venezuela-oil coverage, but it also raises unresolved legality, sovereignty, and property-rights questions.

## Key Characteristics
- It is framed as a concession operator or deal vehicle for reopening Venezuelan oil production.
- The concession is described as unusually long and large: 100 years, 17 fields, and 65 billion barrels.
- The deal is presented as strategically tied to U.S. energy security and Gulf Coast refinery compatibility with Venezuelan heavy crude.
- Its legal durability is uncertain because the source also reports objections that the government granting the deal may lack proper authority.
