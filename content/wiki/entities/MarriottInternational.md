# Marriott International

Marriott International is a central hotel-group case in [[140-jiudian-jituan-weishenme-dou-rezhongyu-dazao-huiyuan-he-jifen-tixi-chuantai-yuanxingzhe-yu-suibingjiang-lj-xa3wxzjog6-8zm9nfakh6ulew]]. The episode uses its 2016 acquisition of [[StarwoodHotelsAndResorts]] to show how hotel scale, [[HotelBrandPortfolio]], and [[HotelLoyaltyPrograms]] can be part of the same strategic asset.

The source contrasts Marriott's more businesslike image with Starwood's stronger lifestyle and loyalty attachment. It also uses Marriott as an example of [[HotelAssetLightFranchiseModel|asset-light hotel economics]], where a global group can own brands, reservation systems, and member relationships without owning every property.
