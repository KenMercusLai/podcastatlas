# Cindy Cordes

Cindy Cordes appears in [[a-pro-worker-experiment-in-private-equity]] as a [[CapitalSafety]] manufacturing lead overseeing about 40 production-floor workers when [[KKR]] bought the company. Her initial worry was that private-equity ownership might close the plant, move work overseas, or cut jobs.

Cindy's story shows the weak version of [[PrivateEquityWorkerOwnership]]. KKR granted Capital Safety workers equity, but workers did not learn about it clearly until the 2015 sale to [[ThreeM|3M]]. Cindy received a five-figure payout and used it to pay off credit cards, yet she says workers might have tried harder to grow the company if the ownership stake had been communicated earlier.
