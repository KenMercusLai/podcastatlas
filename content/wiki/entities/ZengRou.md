## Overview

曾柔 / Zeng Rou is a woman in [[LuDingJi|《鹿鼎记》]] whose attraction to [[WeiXiaobao|韦小宝]] begins in gratitude and heroic projection after he spares members of the Wangwu group.

## Current Profile

The episode reads Zeng Rou as fundamentally decent but narratively thin. Wei deliberately loses a gambling game and releases her companions, giving her a concrete act on which to build admiration. She later learns that he does not match the heroic image she initially constructed.

Because the novel gives the relationship little development, it functions primarily as a case of affection produced by one memorable favor and an imagined hero rather than a richly evidenced account of mutual knowledge.

## Key Characteristics

- Responds to mercy and a strategically staged gambling loss.
- Begins from gratitude and hero worship.
- Revises her image after discovering Wei is not the hero she imagined.
- Remains one of the least developed of the seven partners.
