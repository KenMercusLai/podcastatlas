# Dylan Thompson

Dylan Thompson is the [[Wired]] writer whose missing e-bike experience anchors [[tech-20260723-0723-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260723-0723-mp-tech-pod-128]], a [[MarketplaceTech]] episode on chatbot-mediated customer service. The episode says Thompson ordered two e-bikes, received only one, and then spent weeks contacting [[FedEx]], the bicycle company, his bank, his credit-card company, and a local police department after a $1,700 package went missing.

His role in the wiki is as the consumer case behind [[CustomerServiceSludge]]. The story makes the abstract support-design problem concrete: even when an organization eventually exposes a human representative, automated loops and closed claims can make that human access arrive too late to restore accountability.
