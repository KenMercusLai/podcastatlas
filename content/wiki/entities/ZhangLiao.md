# 张辽 / Zhang Liao

张辽 / Zhang Liao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1000-hao-meng-panluan-jingbi-lvbu-fanqiang-taopao-lluthd9hv-0nqbot4fuctjcuka68|Hanji 1000]] as part of the Bingzhou-origin military core inside [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s organization. The episode does not build a full biography of Zhang Liao; it uses him with [[GaoShun|高顺]] to identify one side of Lü Bu's internal faction structure.

That placement matters because [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] is interpreted as representing a different support channel: the Yanzhou士族 and adviser network. Zhang Liao therefore functions in this source as a factional marker rather than a central event actor in [[HaoMeng|郝萌]]'s revolt.

The page remains compact until later sources add Zhang Liao's independent military career.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1026-jiemi-sanguo-di-yi-mengjiang-lvbu-de-luomu-lhjf-agujuliglqchumtff3tftxg1|Hanji 1026]] supplies the transition out of Lü Bu's camp. After Cao Cao captures [[Xiapi|下邳]] and executes Lü Bu, Zhang Liao leads a surrender and is appointed zhonglangjiang, making him a clean case where [[CaoCao|曹操]] separates usable military capacity from the defeated leader's fatal unreliability.
