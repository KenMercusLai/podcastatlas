# Mary Nichols

## Overview
Mary Nichols is identified in [[tech-20261127-1127-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261127-1127-mp-tech-pod-128]] as a former chair of the California Air Resources Board who argues for greater public visibility into data-center operations, resource use, and electrical-grid demand.

## Current Profile
Nichols connects corporate climate reporting to community governance. Her source-reported position is that communities cannot assess large data centers only as abstract technology infrastructure; they need information about what facilities do, what resources they consume, and how much grid capacity they use. The episode uses this rationale to support [[CaliforniaCorporateEmissionsDisclosure]] while leaving open whether disclosure by itself changes company behavior.

## Key Characteristics

- Former California Air Resources Board chair in the episode's account.
- Frames data-center transparency as a community information need as well as a climate-accounting need.
- Connects facility resource use to electrical-grid capacity and public oversight.
- Supports greater disclosure without the source establishing that transparency alone reduces emissions.
