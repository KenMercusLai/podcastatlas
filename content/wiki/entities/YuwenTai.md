## Overview
宇文泰 was the dominant military-political leader of [[WesternWei|Western Wei]] and the architect of the coalition and institutions inherited by [[NorthernZhou|Northern Zhou]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Yuwen Tai as an organizer working from structural weakness against Gao Huan's richer eastern base. After the Six Garrisons crisis and Erzhu fragmentation opened his path, he received and then eliminated Emperor Xiaowu, installed a more controllable Yuan ruler, recast origins and surnames, organized the [[EightPillarsFubingSystem|Eight Pillars–fubing hierarchy]], used [[ZhouliInstitutionalReconstruction|Zhouli reconstruction]], and exploited southern disorder to secure Hanzhong and Ba-Shu. His authority held senior commanders together during his lifetime; the danger they posed after his death reveals the personal element inside the institutional order.

## Key Characteristics
- Rose from the fragmented Northern Wei military world rather than an initially dominant regional base.
- Integrated heterogeneous military and civil elites through identity remaking and formal hierarchy.
- Combined Xianbei political symbols with classical Zhou institutional language.
- Failed to win decisive eastern supremacy around Luoyang but built advantage through southward expansion.
- Exercised personal authority that successors such as Yuwen Hu could not immediately reproduce.

## Qualifications
The source summary compresses contested chronology and institutional development. It presents Yuwen Tai's killing of Emperor Xiaowu as probably preventive, but motive remains inferred. State-building success also depended on rivals' mistakes and Southern Liang's civil war, not his agency alone.
