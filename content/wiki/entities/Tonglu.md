# 桐庐

## Overview
桐庐 is a mountainous county under Hangzhou and the hometown associated with the founders of [[STOExpress|申通]], [[YTOExpress|圆通]], [[ZTOExpress|中通]], and [[YundaExpress|韵达]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Tonglu less as a naturally advantaged transport hub than as the social origin of a business network. Hometown, kinship, classmate, and coworker ties helped early carriers recruit trusted operators, after which ecommerce scale turned that network into the [[TongluExpressCluster|桐庐快递产业集群]]. Since 2019, local government has tried to bring headquarters functions and related technology businesses back through a “快递回归工程.”

## Key Characteristics
- Small-county social network behind several national carriers.
- Trust and recruitment base rather than an original geographic logistics hub.
- Local identity later converted into an industrial-development strategy.

## Evidence
- Social origin: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] traces founders and early operators through relatives, classmates, coworkers, and neighbors.
- Industrial return: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] reports a 2019 return initiative, a dedicated private-express development center, second-headquarters projects, and a logistics technology town.

## Qualifications
The episode's population, company-share, revenue, firm-count, and government-program figures are source-scoped. Tonglu's importance should not be mistaken for an initial transport-location advantage.
