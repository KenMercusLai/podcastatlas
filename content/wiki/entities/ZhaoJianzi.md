# 赵简子 / Zhao Jianzi

## Overview
Zhao Jianzi is the Zhao patriarch whose succession testing selects [[ZhaoXiangzi|Zhao Wuxu]] over [[ZhaoBolu|Zhao Bolu]] and whose advance preparation of [[Jinyang|Jinyang]] gives that successor a refuge in the later clan crisis.

## Current Profile
Zhao Jianzi combines personnel testing with contingency planning. He gives two sons written instructions and evaluates retention three years later; Zhao Wuxu can recite and produce the tablet, which the host reads as evidence of unusual preparation as well as ability. He also tells [[YinDuo|Yin Duo]] to govern Jinyang as a fallback rather than a revenue source and explicitly directs Zhao Wuxu there if disaster comes. The later succession episode connects this choice to Zhao Wuxu's conquest of Dai and his eventual attempt to restore Zhao Bolu's line, while the dream episode places Zhao Jianzi inside a Shiji literary pattern of Zhao-family rise and disorder.

## Key Characteristics
- Tests successors through retained instruction rather than birth order alone.
- Recognizes preparation and execution as evidence of fitness.
- Sacrifices immediate extraction to preserve a defensible fallback.
- Communicates a specific crisis destination to the chosen successor.
- Appears in later historiography as part of the Zhao lineage's dream-foreshadowing structure.

## Evidence
- Succession test and Jinyang policy: Zhouji 02 narrates the three-year tablet test, Zhao Wuxu's success, Yin Duo's refuge mandate, and the instruction to flee to Jinyang in crisis. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-02-jiangyu-baizhi-bi-gufuzhi-lglk2_eu1uvegdac8sj6bfuwv1dw|Zhouji 02]]
- Consequences of selection: Zhouji 04 says Zhao Jianzi passes over Zhao Bolu for Zhao Wuxu because the younger son understands and can execute the strategic task. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-04-yurang-wei-zhiyao-fuchou-ln34tydha6iqwlrgmw7iugqfgfik|Zhouji 04]]
- Historiographical afterlife: Zhouji 93 part 2 places his journey-to-Juntian dream in a four-dream Shiji structure for Zhao-family fortune. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-2-lrlulw0b6v0fb2xpr2i5ifem1h4n|Zhouji 93 part 2]]

## Qualifications
All evidence comes from one modern podcast series reading transmitted narratives. The tablet test, exact instructions, physiognomic judgment, policy dialogue, and dream meaning remain source-scoped. The host explicitly suspects that keeping the tablet ready for three years may show calculation, so the test is not a transparent measure of virtue.
