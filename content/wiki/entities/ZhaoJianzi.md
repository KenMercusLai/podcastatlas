# 赵简子 / Zhao Jianzi

赵简子 / Zhao Jianzi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-04-yurang-wei-zhiyao-fuchou-ln34tydha6iqwlrgmw7iugqfgfik]] as the Zhao patriarch whose succession choice shapes the later Zhao branch. The episode says he passes over his eldest son [[ZhaoBolu|赵伯鲁]] and selects [[ZhaoXiangzi|赵无恤 / 赵襄子]] because Zhao Wuxu best understands the task and can carry out the family's strategic plan.

The page's role is source-scoped: Zhao Jianzi is important here less as a full biographical subject than as the person whose succession judgment enables Zhao Wuxu's conquest of [[DaiState|代国]] and later complications around restoring Zhao Bolu's line.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-2-lrlulw0b6v0fb2xpr2i5ifem1h4n]] adds Zhao Jianzi to a different Zhao-line pattern. The episode says [[Shiji|《史记》]] uses his dream of traveling to Juntian as one of the four dreams that structure Zhao-family fortune, linking his dream to later conflict with Zhonghang and Zhi power and making him part of [[HistoricalDreamForeshadowing|梦兆叙事伏笔]].
