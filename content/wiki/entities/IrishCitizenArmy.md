# Irish Citizen Army

## Overview

The Irish Citizen Army was a socialist labour militia led by [[JamesConnolly]] that emerged from Dublin industrial conflict and joined the [[EasterRising|Easter Rising]].

## Current Profile

The source grounds the Citizen Army in the 1913 lockout and the severe poverty of Edwardian Dublin. It represented a social grievance distinct from, but eventually allied with, the conspiratorial republicanism of the [[IrishRepublicanBrotherhood|IRB]]. Its members operated from Liberty Hall and fought at positions including the GPO and St Stephen's Green.

The Citizen Army's role shows that the Rising cannot be interpreted only as constitutional nationalism, Catholic intellectual revolt, or cultural performance. At the same time, participation in a common rebellion did not erase ideological differences or guarantee gender equality within the movement.

## Key Characteristics

- Labour militia formed from the 1913 Dublin lockout environment.
- Socialist organization associated above all with James Connolly.
- Armed response to poverty, industrial conflict, and political exclusion.
- Coalition partner rather than subsidiary expression of the IRB.
- Organization in which women including Constance Markievicz held active roles.

## Evidence

- Labour origin: [[339-ireland-the-easter-rising-1916-part-4-glt7095481415]] connects the militia to the lockout, one-room housing, and missing sanitation.
- Revolutionary participation: [[339-ireland-the-easter-rising-1916-part-4-glt7095481415]] places the Citizen Army at Liberty Hall and in the Dublin fighting.
- Women organizers: [[339-ireland-the-easter-rising-1916-part-4-glt7095481415]] emphasizes Markievicz's work while preserving internal gender limits.

## Qualifications

The episode does not provide membership totals, a full organizational history, or a systematic account of the Citizen Army's socialist program. Its labour origins should not be generalized into a claim that every rebel or later republican accepted Connolly's politics.

## What Changed

- Established the Citizen Army as the Rising's labour-socialist institutional branch.
- Distinguished coalition from ideological uniformity.

## Relationships

- [[JamesConnolly]] - socialist leader and commander who defines the source's account of the militia.
- [[ConstanceMarkievicz]] - organizer and combatant associated with its St Stephen's Green position.
- [[IrishRepublicanBrotherhood]] - separate conspiratorial organization with which it coordinated.
- [[IrishVolunteers]] - larger nationalist armed body participating in the rebellion.
- [[EasterRising]] - coalition event through which the militia entered national memory.
