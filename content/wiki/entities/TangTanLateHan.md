## Overview
Tang Tan is a Yuzhang-associated adviser who refuses to turn a local lingzhi discovery into court flattery because outer relatives are strong and the ruler is weak.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-790-lishi-gaosu-ni-ruhe-he-lingdao-da-jiaodao-lulf0r1sdkod3luzwru0-kkrijxk|Hanji 790]] presents Tang Tan as a political reader of omen rhetoric. When Yuzhang administrator Liu Zhi considers presenting a lingzhi as an auspicious sign, Tang Tan advises against it: the court environment is one in which outer relatives are powerful and the emperor is weak, so celebratory omen talk would misdescribe the political reality.

## Key Characteristics
- Reads omen presentation as political speech, not as neutral ritual reporting.
- Treats outer-relative dominance and weak imperial agency as disqualifying conditions for auspicious claims.
- Provides a local-administrative window into broader Deng-regency legitimacy anxiety.
- Connects court feedback problems to symbolic reporting from the provinces.
