# Tom Segura

## Overview
Tom Segura appears in the wiki as a comedian, writer, actor, and director discussing stand-up craft and screen comedy with [[AndrewHuberman]] on [[HubermanLab]].

## Current Profile
The current evidence presents Segura as a long-form storyteller whose work develops through live testing rather than fully scripted advance writing. He captures small ideas, learns from involuntary audience response, trims and reframes material, and eventually retires it so that earlier success does not prevent new work. His account joins technical iteration to social sensitivity: performance depends on crowd state, playfulness, vulnerability, cultural context, and a durable desire to make strangers laugh. [[BadThoughts]] expands that comic worldview into acting, writing, directing, and short-form screen stories.

## Key Characteristics
- Develops stand-up through captured kernels, cue words, repeated club performances, and revision.
- Uses audience laughter as decisive but not always fully explainable feedback.
- Favors long-form storytelling over compact setup-punchline construction.
- Treats looseness, play, vulnerability, and enjoyment of people as performance capacities.
- Accepts bombing and the retirement of old material as costs of creative development.
- Uses observation of family, voices, cultural rules, insecurity, and dark thought as material.
- Pursues screen work as a continuation of an early ambition to make movies and shows.

## Evidence
- Iterative craft - [[the-science-art-of-comedy-creativity-tom-segura-scim7538555033]] records Segura capturing kernels, testing them in clubs, and revising information, setup, lines, and framing.
- Audience relation - [[the-science-art-of-comedy-creativity-tom-segura-scim7538555033]] has him distinguish private funniness from showing strangers a comic worldview and describe vulnerability as an invitation.
- Performance state - [[the-science-art-of-comedy-creativity-tom-segura-scim7538555033]] records his preference for a calm green room, a loose onstage mindset, and following a successful act rather than rebuilding a room after a bomb.
- Renewal - [[the-science-art-of-comedy-creativity-tom-segura-scim7538555033]] says audiences can detect boredom and that dropping proven material creates room for new work.
- Screen ambition - [[the-science-art-of-comedy-creativity-tom-segura-scim7538555033]] describes [[BadThoughts]] as fulfilling his earlier desire to make movies and play many roles.
