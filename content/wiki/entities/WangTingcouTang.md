## Overview

王廷凑 / Wang Tingcou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-341-4-bei-digu-de-ta-kancheng-zhongguo-zui-yinghe-de-shiren-lv78zkmugscl4fwhts-rak6mhyep|Hanji 341-4]] as the Chengde military figure whose 821 mutiny, seizure of command, and confrontation with [[HanYu|韩愈]] organize the episode's late-Tang crisis.

## Current Profile

The episode says Wang Tingcou's forces kill Chengde military governor Tian Hongzheng and elevate Wang as successor. A court campaign fails, after which Han Yu is sent to pacify the area. Wang then assembles troops to intimidate the envoy, but Han answers with examples of the destructive afterlives of earlier rebellions.

This profile is intentionally narrow. The source uses Wang chiefly as the coercive counterpart that makes Han's duty and courage visible; it does not provide enough evidence for a full account of Wang's background, authority, later rule, or negotiations with the court.

## Key Characteristics

- Chengde military actor associated by the source with the 821 Zhenzhou mutiny.
- Successor who takes the military-governor position after Tian Hongzheng is killed.
- Beneficiary of a failed central punitive campaign that leaves the court seeking pacification.
- Coercive host who uses assembled troops to pressure Han Yu during the mission.
- Source-bounded antagonist whose longer political career is not reconstructed by this episode.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on one compressed, Han-Yu-centered podcast summary. The source does not independently establish Wang's personal responsibility for every act of the mutiny, the formal mechanics of his appointment, the composition of the troops, the exact encounter, or the degree to which Han Yu's speech changed Wang's later behavior. “郑州兵变” in the supplied text is treated as a likely transcription error for 镇州兵变.
