# 史弼 / Shi Bi (late Han)

## Overview

Shi Bi enters the wiki through Hanji 877 as the Pingyuan chancellor who refuses to fabricate party-member names under campaign pressure.

## Current Profile

Hanji 877 places Shi Bi inside the administrative aftermath of the first Party Prohibition. As commanderies and kingdoms report lists of party figures, Shi Bi reports that Pingyuan has none. When Qingzhou pressure arrives, he refuses the logic that every jurisdiction must produce names because neighboring jurisdictions have done so.

The source's profile of Shi Bi is therefore not passive noncompliance. He states a reasoned boundary: differences in local custom and evidence matter, and using superior pressure to frame innocent households would make all Pingyuan residents vulnerable. His near-punishment and later local respect make him the episode's clearest case of official integrity under a coercive reporting campaign.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Han Pingyuan chancellor who reports no party figures under his administration.
- Evidence-bound official who rejects inference from other jurisdictions' reports to his own jurisdiction.
- Protector of ordinary households who refuses to turn innocent people into party cases.
- Death-risking official whose refusal remains meaningful even though amnesty later limits punishment.
- Source case for accusation-quota refusal under Party Prohibition pressure.

## Evidence

- No-name report: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-877-ta-weihe-lengluo-jiuming-enren-loofs4rugl-q8kunfb-jpj5mgvhi|Hanji 877]] says Shi Bi reports that Pingyuan has no party figures while other commanderies and kingdoms submit many names.
- Superior pressure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-877-ta-weihe-lengluo-jiuming-enren-loofs4rugl-q8kunfb-jpj5mgvhi|Hanji 877]] says repeated orders and Qingzhou questioning pressure him to produce a list.
- Evidence boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-877-ta-weihe-lengluo-jiuming-enren-loofs4rugl-q8kunfb-jpj5mgvhi|Hanji 877]] says Shi Bi argues that different places can have different customs, so other jurisdictions' reports do not prove Pingyuan has party figures.
- Innocent-household protection: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-877-ta-weihe-lengluo-jiuming-enren-loofs4rugl-q8kunfb-jpj5mgvhi|Hanji 877]] says Shi Bi refuses to falsely accuse good people merely to satisfy official expectations.
- Amnesty outcome: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-877-ta-weihe-lengluo-jiuming-enren-loofs4rugl-q8kunfb-jpj5mgvhi|Hanji 877]] says an amnesty prevents heavier punishment, leaving only a fine of several months' salary.

## Qualifications

The page is source-bounded to the Party Prohibition reporting case. The transcript form 史璧 is normalized to 史弼, and the source does not supply a broader career account beyond the Pingyuan episode.
