# Loblaw

Loblaw is the first major retailer [[MadeGood]] approached in [[madegood-salma-and-nima-fotovat-lost-their-first-business-they-grew-their-next-one-into-a-snack-giant-cf5715bf-e9e0-45e0-a8a9-1256a7a4ed0d]]. The episode says a buyer whose son had a peanut allergy understood the gap for snacks that were safe, better-ingredient, and actually tasted good.

## Key Points
- Loblaw initially listed four MadeGood items.
- The products began in the natural aisle and on the top shelf, so packaging clarity and repeat purchase mattered.
- The buyer's personal allergy context turned [[SchoolSafeCPGPositioning]] from abstract category research into a concrete retail problem.
