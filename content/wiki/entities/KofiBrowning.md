# Kofi Browning

Kofi Browning appears in [[ep-4-a-i-talk-with-a-rocket-scientist-from-nasa]] as a [[NASA]] engineer and deputy chief information officer interviewed by [[SamDataScienceWithSam|Sam]] on [[DataScienceWithSam]]. The source keeps his role source-scoped: he is introduced as a rocket scientist, but the conversation focuses on career path, risk management, leadership, and AI use cases rather than a technical biography.

Kofi traces his NASA interest to a childhood visit to the Kennedy Space Center, then describes a route through mechanical engineering, NASA contracting, private-sector work, a return to NASA contracting, and a civil-servant role. His day-to-day work in the episode centers on risk management, mitigation, controls, and leading engineers.

His advice to students and experienced professionals grounds [[NASACareerPathways]]. Internships are presented as a strong entry route, while experienced hires can bring engineering, IT, technical, business, HR, or other professional skills and learn the space-specific context after joining. He also frames [[MissionDrivenGovernmentEngineering]] as a career choice: NASA work is ordinary engineering tied to unusually meaningful goals.

In the AI section, Kofi is the source voice for [[SpaceflightAIDatasetScarcity]], [[SpaceImageryAI]], [[EVAGloveInspectionAI]], and [[AIModelBiasGovernance]]. He is optimistic about machine learning where the data and task fit, but repeatedly stresses practical application, human review, and bias awareness.
