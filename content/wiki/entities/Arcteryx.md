# Arc'teryx / 始祖鸟

Arc'teryx / 始祖鸟 appears in [[lanjian-hangtian-wancheng-zhongguo-shouci-ludi-huojian-huishou-yushu-keji-shizhi-chaoguo-3000-yi-1007302506]] as the premium outdoor brand driving [[AmerSports|Amer Sports / 亚玛芬体育]]'s China growth. The source says Arc'teryx opened a Chengdu flagship store in the second quarter and plans 10-12 new stores in Greater China during the year.

The episode frames Arc'teryx as having become the largest high-end outdoor brand in China's domestic market, while management expects growth to return toward a more normal pace. That makes the brand a case in scaling premium outdoor retail after a hype-heavy adoption period.
