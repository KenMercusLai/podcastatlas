# Seres / 赛力斯

Seres / 赛力斯 appears in [[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-21-shiyan-yizuo-yin-qiche-erqi-you-bugan-bei-qiche-paoxia-de-chengshi-1001770066]] as a source-scoped example of [[Shiyan]]'s indirect connection to newer electric-vehicle companies. The episode says the guest noticed Seres signage near the Shiyan airport, then traced the company back through Dongfeng Xiaokang and Shiyan-related production.

The point is not that Seres is now a Shiyan-centered company. The source says its headquarters later moved to Chongqing, making it another example of how older industrial roots can remain in production footprints while corporate visibility and headquarters identity shift elsewhere.

[[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] adds the [[AITO|问界]] branch. In that source, Seres is the first automaker willing to enter Huawei's deep cooperation: Seres keeps qualification, factories, supply chain, manufacturing, and quality management, while [[Huawei]] supplies product definition, intelligent systems, launches, retail, and service. The episode treats the new M7 turnaround, M9 high-end validation, and Seres's 2024 profitability as evidence that the partnership became commercially meaningful after early SF5 testing.
