## Overview

Ban Chao is the Eastern Han Western Regions commander whose wiki profile now begins with the 74 CE Shule intervention in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-743-chuo-ren-xinwo-de-lishi-shunjian-mingdi-liuzhuang-de-wenqing-meng-lgwtuqtug5bh90qohwiskccx77hy|Hanji 743]], then continues through the 76-77 CE recall crisis in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-747-banchao-gaosu-ni-zhenzheng-de-congming-ren-dou-you-shiminggan-lj5z9tayvosa-jcufyxwuc35dzvi|Hanji 747]], the 78 CE allied Shicheng operation in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-749-guanliren-bikan-fa-jiangjin-shi-guanli-de-zuijia-shouduan-ma-lnhhoh0oxlstuwakl3rsqvcss-cf|Hanji 749]], the 80 CE memorial strategy in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-750-jiemi-donghan-wanmei-nvxing-beihou-manshi-xisheng-lu-96lbdh7r0tsmiopbqqrj0q1gt|Hanji 750]], and the later slander, Shule, Shache, Yuezhi, Qiuci, Yanqi, Gan Ying, succession, and retirement layers. Across the bounded corpus, he is less a simple battlefield hero than a long-horizon frontier operator whose authority depends on credibility, timing, local political manipulation, proxy use, and refusal to let formal exit or private anger govern public command.

## Current Profile

Hanji 743 supplies the earliest current layer. [[QiuciKingdom|Qiuci]] king Jian, backed by Xiongnu power, kills the Shule king and installs Duti, a non-Shule subject, over [[ShuleKingdom|Shule]]. Ban Chao sends Tian Lu to arrest Duti, enters Pantuo, gathers Shule officials, denounces Qiuci's interference, and installs [[WangZhongShuleKing|Zhong]] from the previous Shule royal line. When local officials want Duti killed, Ban Chao releases him instead, using mercy to display Han grace and authority toward Qiuci.

Hanji 747 then gives the recall-crisis layer. After the court abolishes Western Regions offices and recalls him, Shule and [[KhotanKingdomLateHan|Khotan]] react as if his departure will collapse their security. Shule duwei Li kills himself, Khotan's ruler and officials plead with him to stay, and two Shule cities defect toward Qiuci and Wutou. Ban Chao returns to Shule, kills the rebels, defeats Wutou, and restores stability. This makes his early profile a case of [[MissionDrivenFrontierResponsibility|mission-driven frontier responsibility]] before the later proxy-conquest strategy is formalized.

Hanji 749 gives Ban Chao the first allied-field layer. In 78 CE, he leads allied forces from Shule, Kangju, Yutian, Jumi, and related Western Regions partners against Gumo's Shicheng. The force reportedly exceeds ten thousand people, captures Shicheng, and kills seven hundred, showing Ban Chao already using allied local manpower before the later memorial formalizes the low-cost proxy strategy.

Hanji 750 makes Ban Chao a strategist of low-cost frontier advance. After years in Shule with a small following, he argues that Han can complete the Western Regions program by restoring Qiuci hostage prince Ba Ba, drawing Western Regions states into the campaign, using Shache and Shule's fertile supply base, exploiting the weak local legitimacy of Qiuci-installed rulers in Gumo and Wensu, and coordinating with Wusun. [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Zhangdi]] accepts the plan enough to send [[XuGanEasternHan|Xu Gan]] with a limited force and contact Wusun, making Ban Chao's strategy a case of [[FrontierProxyConquestStrategy|frontier proxy conquest]] rather than central-army saturation.

Hanji 753 then makes Ban Chao an object of accusation before it makes him an expanding conqueror. [[LiYiEasternHan|Li Yi]] claims that Ban Chao is attached to foreign comfort because he has wife and children abroad, but Zhangdi trusts the harder behavioral evidence: homesick Han troops continue to follow Ban Chao. Ban Chao removes his family to cut off the charge's surface plausibility, then sends Li Yi back with the Wusun hostage prince rather than keep him under command for revenge.

The later Western Regions layers show how that credibility is used and how it eventually reaches its limits. Ban Chao uses timing and counter-deception in the Shule succession crisis, blocks a Yuezhi advance through food-line judgment, turns Qiuci's removal of Ba Ba into a campaign to install a more favorable order, conducts the Yanqi operation in a harsher punitive register, sponsors Gan Ying's westward mission, warns that only his old subordinate Ren Shang can take over, and asks to return east after decades of low-support service.

## Key Characteristics

- Early Shule regime-restoration operator who combines local legitimacy, swift coercion, public denunciation, and selective mercy.
- Recall-crisis commander whose Western Regions authority becomes visible when Shule and Khotan panic at his departure.
- Allied-force operator who takes Shicheng with Western Regions partners.
- Proxy-strategy planner who turns hostage kingship, local supplies, allied polities, and limited Han reinforcement into an operating model.
- Accusation-resilient commander judged by the behavior of soldiers who still follow him despite homesickness.
- Public-duty actor who sends Li Yi back rather than using command to satisfy private resentment.
- Counter-deception and supply-line strategist who survives Shule succession crisis and defeats the Yuezhi threat through timing and grain judgment.

## Qualifications

The page preserves all prior source slugs while compressing prose; not every date, subordinate, hostage, or oasis polity from the source notes is promoted into a standalone claim. Hanji 743 adds Duti, Tian Lu, Pantuo, and Zhong as source-scoped figures within the Shule intervention. Duti's release is strategic mercy, not evidence that Ban Chao generally avoids coercion. Ban Chao's return in Hanji 747 does not prove a general right to ignore recall orders; the evidence supports a bounded judgment under immediate allied collapse. Ban Chao's non-retaliation toward Li Yi does not prove personal forgiveness; the evidence supports a public-duty boundary, not emotional reconciliation. His later Yanqi conduct complicates any purely humane portrait.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 743 as the earliest current Ban Chao layer: Qiuci-backed Duti, Shule royal-line installation, and Duti's release.
- Connected the Duti release to strategic forbearance and the Zhong installation to frontier proxy strategy.
- Kept Hanji 747's recall crisis as the next layer of mission-driven frontier responsibility.
- Preserved Hanji 749's Shicheng operation and Hanji 750's memorial as successive allied-force and proxy-strategy layers.
- Preserved Li Yi's slander, Zhangdi's troop-behavior evidence test, and Ban Chao's non-retaliation as the command-trust profile.
