## Overview

班超 / Ban Chao appears in the current wiki as the Eastern Han Western Regions commander whose low-support frontier achievement, local-balancing method, and ignored succession advice become the benchmark against which later Deng-regency withdrawal is judged.

## Current Profile

Hanji 780 presents Ban Chao as the figure who turned Zhang Qian's opening of the Western Regions into durable Eastern Han control. The episode emphasizes his low-support operating environment: limited direct troops, long distance from the capital, local balancing among Western Regions polities, and garrison farming rather than constant heavy expedition.

Hanji 779 adds the handoff failure. Before [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]] takes over the protectorate, Ban Chao warns that many frontier garrison soldiers are difficult men and that Western Regions polities are proud, volatile, and easy to alienate. His advice is deliberately plain: govern loosely, keep rules simple, ignore small faults, and hold the larger pattern. Ren Shang treats the advice as ordinary and then applies harsh control, triggering rebellion.

The same source also gives Ban Chao a concrete institutional afterlife in [[QiuciKingdom|Qiuci]]. Qiuci remains initially pro-Han because Ban Chao had earlier installed Ba Ba as king, but even that inherited alignment cannot prevent backlash once the protectorate seat moves into the city. Ban Chao therefore functions both as a strategic baseline and as evidence that frontier control depended on local legitimacy, not only force.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Regions commander remembered here for making Han influence effective after Zhang Qian's earlier opening.
- Low-support operator whose success depends on local balancing, limited force, and garrison farming.
- Adviser whose plain handoff counsel stresses loose, simple rule and tolerance of minor faults.
- Strategic benchmark for judging Ren Shang's failure and the Deng court's later Western Regions withdrawal.
- Ban-family predecessor to later [[BanYongLateHan|Ban Yong]] debates over how much Western Regions presence the Eastern Han can sustain.

## Qualifications

This page remains source-bounded. It does not attempt a full Ban Chao biography, campaign chronology, or Western Regions administrative history. Hanji 779 reports Ban Chao's advice through the episode's interpretive frame; it shows the later value of his experience, but it does not prove that his exact method could have survived every later fiscal and political constraint.
