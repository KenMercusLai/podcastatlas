## Overview

班超 / Ban Chao appears in the current wiki as the Eastern Han Western Regions commander whose low-support frontier achievement, late return, and ignored light-touch handoff advice become the benchmark against which later Deng-regency withdrawal and Ban-family recovery debates are judged.

## Current Profile

Ban Chao's profile now ends with both achievement and exit. Hanji 774 presents him after more than thirty years in the Western Regions, old enough to fear dying outside the passes. He asks to return, sends [[BanYongLateHan|Ban Yong]] inward with an Anxi mission, and is finally recalled after [[BanZhaoLateHan|Ban Zhao]] warns [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]] that leaving an aged commander in place could waste decades of frontier work. He reaches [[LuoyangLateHan|Luoyang]], receives the she sheng xiaowei appointment, and dies the next month in 102 CE.

His operating method is clearest in the handoff to [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]]. Ban Chao warns that frontier garrison personnel are not easy to discipline and that Western Regions polities are proud, volatile, and easy to alienate. The advice is deliberately plain: govern loosely, keep rules simple, forgive minor faults, and hold the larger pattern. Hanji 774 emphasizes Ren Shang's private contempt for this advice, while Hanji 779 records the later consequences when Ren governs harshly and rebellion follows.

Hanji 780 supplies the broader achievement frame: Ban Chao turns Zhang Qian's opening of the Western Regions into durable Eastern Han control through limited direct troops, local balancing, and garrison farming. Hanji 779 also gives that achievement an institutional afterlife in [[QiuciKingdom|Qiuci]], whose initial pro-Han stance is attributed to Ban Chao's earlier installation of Ba Ba as king. Ban Chao therefore functions as both a practical model and a warning that frontier control depends on local legitimacy, not only force.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Regions commander remembered here for making Han influence effective after Zhang Qian's earlier opening.
- Low-support operator whose success depends on local balancing, limited force, and garrison farming.
- Late-return figure whose final recall depends on Ban Zhao's petition and Emperor He's acceptance.
- Adviser whose plain handoff counsel defines [[LightTouchFrontierGovernance|light-touch frontier governance]] through simple rule, tolerance of minor faults, and big-picture control.
- Strategic benchmark for judging Ren Shang's failure and the Deng court's later Western Regions withdrawal.
- Ban-family predecessor to later [[BanYongLateHan|Ban Yong]] debates over how much Western Regions presence the Eastern Han can sustain.

## Qualifications

This page remains source-bounded. It does not attempt a full Ban Chao biography, campaign chronology, or Western Regions administrative history. Hanji 774 and Hanji 779 report Ban Chao's advice through the episode's interpretive frame; they show the later value of his experience, but they do not prove that his exact method could have survived every later fiscal and political constraint.
