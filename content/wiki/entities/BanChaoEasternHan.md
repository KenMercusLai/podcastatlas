## Overview

班超 / Ban Chao appears in the current wiki as the Eastern Han Western Regions commander whose protectorate restoration, Qiuci kingship intervention, forceful Yanqi conquest, low-support frontier achievement, far-western envoy sponsorship, late return, and ignored light-touch handoff advice become the benchmark against which later Deng-regency withdrawal and Ban-family recovery debates are judged.

## Current Profile

Hanji 764 now supplies the earliest current protectorate layer. After Qiuci, Gumo, and Wensu submit, the court reestablishes the Western Regions Protector and Wuji Colonel offices, appoints Ban Chao as Protector, and makes Xu Gan his chief clerk. Ban Chao and [[YaoGuangLateHan|Yao Guang]] then coerce [[QiuciKingdom|Qiuci]], depose Youliduo, install the pro-Han hostage prince Ba Ba, and base Ban Chao's protectorate at Qiuci's Taqian City while Xu Gan remains at [[ShuleKingdom|Shule]]. The episode says most Western Regions states submit, with Yanqi, Weixu, and Weili still hesitant because of the earlier killing of a Han protector.

Hanji 769 now supplies the earliest current campaign layer. Ban Chao mobilizes more than seventy thousand troops from [[QiuciKingdom|Qiuci]], Shanshan, and other Western Regions states against [[YanqiKingdomLateHan|Yanqi]], kills Yanqi king Guang and the Weili king at Chen Mu's old city, sends their heads to [[LuoyangLateHan|Luoyang]], installs Yuanmeng, and forces broad hostage submission from more than fifty Western Regions states. The source reads the campaign as brutally effective, making Ban Chao's control visible as coercive alliance management as well as frontier skill.

Hanji 771 shows Ban Chao using the Western Regions command as a platform for long-distance knowledge gathering: he sends [[GanYingEasternHan|Gan Ying]] toward [[DaQin|Da Qin]] / the [[RomanEmpire|Roman Empire]] and [[TiaozhiKingdom|Tiaozhi]]. Gan Ying reaches [[ParthianEmpire|Parthia]]'s western boundary, turns back after hearing the sea route described as dangerous, and reports mediated far-western knowledge to the Han court.

Hanji 774 then presents Ban Chao after more than thirty years in the Western Regions, old enough to fear dying outside the passes. He asks to return, sends [[BanYongLateHan|Ban Yong]] inward with an Anxi mission, and is finally recalled after [[BanZhaoLateHan|Ban Zhao]] warns [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]] that leaving an aged commander in place could waste decades of frontier work. He reaches [[LuoyangLateHan|Luoyang]], receives the she sheng xiaowei appointment, and dies the next month in 102 CE.

His operating method is clearest in the handoff to [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]]. Ban Chao warns that frontier garrison personnel are not easy to discipline and that Western Regions polities are proud, volatile, and easy to alienate. The advice is deliberately plain: govern loosely, keep rules simple, forgive minor faults, and hold the larger pattern. Hanji 774 emphasizes Ren Shang's private contempt for this advice, while Hanji 779 records the later consequences when Ren governs harshly and rebellion follows.

Hanji 780 supplies the broader achievement frame: Ban Chao turns Zhang Qian's opening of the Western Regions into durable Eastern Han control through limited direct troops, local balancing, and garrison farming. Hanji 779 also gives that achievement an institutional afterlife in [[QiuciKingdom|Qiuci]], whose initial pro-Han stance is attributed to Ban Chao's earlier installation of Ba Ba as king. Ban Chao therefore functions as both a practical model and a warning that frontier control depends on local legitimacy, not only force.

## Key Characteristics

- Protector and local-kingship operator appointed after Western Regions office restoration, with Qiuci's Ba Ba installation anchoring protectorate placement.
- Western Regions commander remembered here for making Han influence effective after Zhang Qian's earlier opening.
- Forceful campaign operator whose Yanqi victory uses allied Western Regions troops, killing, replacement kingship, and hostage submission.
- Low-support operator whose wider success also depends on local balancing, limited force, and garrison farming.
- Sponsor of Gan Ying's mission toward Da Qin and Tiaozhi, showing Western Regions control as a diplomatic and geographic-intelligence platform.
- Late-return adviser whose final recall and handoff counsel define the contrast with Ren Shang's harsh rule.
- Strategic benchmark for judging Ren Shang's failure, the Deng court's later Western Regions withdrawal, and Ban Yong's constrained recovery.

## Qualifications

This page remains source-bounded. It does not attempt a full Ban Chao biography, campaign chronology, Western Regions administrative history, or reconstruction of Gan Ying's exact route. Hanji 764's Ba Ba, Youliduo, Xu Gan, Taqian City, Gumo, Wensu, Weixu, and Weili details remain source-scoped unless later notes require durable pages. Hanji 769's Yanqi material shows a hard coercive campaign, not merely light-touch governance; the later handoff advice in Hanji 774 and Hanji 779 therefore should be read as context-specific frontier management, not as a claim that Ban Chao avoided force.
