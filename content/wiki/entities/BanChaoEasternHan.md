## Overview

班超 / Ban Chao appears in the current wiki as the Eastern Han frontier commander whose earlier Western Regions achievement becomes the benchmark against which Deng-regency withdrawal is judged.

## Current Profile

Hanji 780 presents Ban Chao as the figure who turned Zhang Qian's opening of the Western Regions into durable Eastern Han control. The episode emphasizes his low-support operating environment: limited direct troops, long distance from the capital, local balancing among Western Regions polities, and garrison farming rather than constant heavy expedition.

His page matters because the Deng court later abandons the Western Regions after weighing cost, distance, fiscal exhaustion, and court instability. Ban Chao therefore functions less as a biography in this bounded evidence than as the strategic baseline for [[StrategicFrontierAbandonment|frontier-abandonment]] judgment: a hard-won buffer can look unaffordable to a later court that inherits the expense without the founding commander's leverage.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Regions commander remembered here for making Han influence effective after Zhang Qian's earlier opening.
- Low-support operator whose success depends on local balancing, limited force, and garrison farming.
- Strategic benchmark for judging the Deng court's later Western Regions withdrawal.
- Frontier stabilizer whose achievement becomes vulnerable once fiscal exhaustion and court instability reduce support.
- Ban-family predecessor to later [[BanYongLateHan|Ban Yong]] debates over how much Western Regions presence the Eastern Han can sustain.
