# Anubis

## Overview

Anubis is an Egyptian jackal- or canine-headed god associated with embalming, regeneration, and accompanying the dead into the underworld.

## Current Profile

[[59-the-world-cup-of-gods-part-2-glt6474253297]] emphasizes Anubis as a psychopomp rather than simply a judge: he escorts the dead and, in later stories, embalms Osiris as the first mummy. His black coloring is connected in the episode both to death and to the fertile Nile soil, while the proposed origin in wild dogs disturbing burials links divine form to funerary danger.

## Key Characteristics

- Canine- or jackal-headed figure strongly recognizable as ancient Egyptian imagery.
- Psychopomp who accompanies the dead into the underworld.
- Associated with embalming and the regeneration of Osiris.
- Black coloring joins death symbolism to fertile Nile soil in the episode's account.

## Evidence

- Funerary role: [[59-the-world-cup-of-gods-part-2-glt6474253297]] distinguishes escorting the dead from judging them and identifies Anubis as Osiris's embalmer in later narrative.
- Visual identity: [[59-the-world-cup-of-gods-part-2-glt6474253297]] links black coloring, Nile fertility, burial danger, and canine recognizability.
