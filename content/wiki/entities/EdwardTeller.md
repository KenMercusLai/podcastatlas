# Edward Teller

## Overview

Edward Teller appears in [[344-oppenheimer-the-witch-hunt-part-2-glt1594914557]] as the physicist who pressed for the hydrogen bomb, resented [[JRobertOppenheimer|J. Robert Oppenheimer]]'s resistance, and delivered the hearing testimony most damaging to Oppenheimer's clearance.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Teller to show that the Oppenheimer controversy was also a conflict within the scientific community about nuclear escalation and trust. Teller did not testify that Oppenheimer was treacherous; he said national interests should be placed in more trustworthy hands, turning policy and judgment into a security verdict.

That distinction did not protect Teller's reputation among many scientists. His testimony made him a symbol of betrayal within the professional community even though the episode also places it within a genuine strategic dispute over the hydrogen bomb.

## Key Characteristics

- Early and forceful advocate of the fusion “super” or hydrogen bomb.
- Scientific-policy opponent of Oppenheimer's resistance to further nuclear escalation.
- Witness who framed the clearance question around trust rather than proof of treachery.
- Figure whose testimony helped decide the case and made him a pariah among many peers.

## Qualifications

This source does not provide Teller's full scientific career, strategic reasoning, testimony transcript, or later defense. Its betrayal frame records the episode's account of scientific-community reaction rather than proving that Teller acted only from resentment.
