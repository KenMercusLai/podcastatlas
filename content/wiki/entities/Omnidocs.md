# Omnidocs

Omnidocs is the older document-automation business that preceded [[Templafy]] in [[enterprise-sales-with-no-product-landing-a-big-four-customer]]. [[ChristianLund]] says Omnidocs had solved a similar enterprise document problem through an earlier technology model, but the cloud shift around 2012-2014 forced the founders to reconsider the product foundation.

The source treats Omnidocs as both an advantage and a constraint. It gave the Templafy team domain expertise, enterprise relationships, and credibility, but it also represented the old architecture that could not simply be made "cloud-ish" without limiting the next company. That makes Omnidocs part of [[TechnologyResetRebuild]] and [[SelfDisruptionDiscipline]].
