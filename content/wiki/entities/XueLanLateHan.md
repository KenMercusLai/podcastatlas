# 薛兰 / Xue Lan (late Han)

薛兰 / Xue Lan (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-986-tianyu-qiwang-bixian-lingqi-kuang-lvlvu057-tnz3hal6oodhydvzvbd|Hanji 986]] as a [[LyuBu|吕布]] subordinate stationed with [[LiFengLateHan|李封]] around Juye. [[CaoCao|曹操]] attacks them during the [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] recovery campaign and kills both, prompting Lü Bu to come to the rescue and fall into Cao Cao's ambush.

The page is source-scoped. Xue Lan matters here mainly as the trigger for the compact pre-[[DingtaoBattleLateHan|定陶之战]] sequence: Cao Cao first removes local Lü Bu commanders, then uses the rescue response to recover momentum in Yanzhou.
