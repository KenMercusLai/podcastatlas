## Overview

杜清 / Du Qing enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-520-daji-duishou-weishenme-yao-xian-faqi-yulunzhan-lh1ibhc9xsihtrh6mhzznqq9ioso|Hanji 520]] as the official who twice advises [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]] after [[WangZhangWesternHan|王章]]'s death and the planned impeachment of [[FengYewangWesternHan|冯野王]]. His role is not presented as personal rescue alone; he converts a narrow disciplinary case into a warning about legal credibility, public interpretation, and remonstrance channels.

## Current Profile

The source presents Du Qing as a procedural and political adviser inside a dangerous Wang-family power field. In the Feng Yewang case, he argues that punishment should not be built from uncertain or retroactive rules: if the emperor had approved sick leave and no statute forbade crossing the commandery boundary, the safer state posture is to forgive rather than punish.

His second intervention is broader. Because Wang Zhang's charge record is secret, Du Qing worries that people will believe Wang Zhang died for speaking directly against Wang Feng. He therefore recommends calling on officials and attendants to speak freely, so the court can show that direct remonstrance remains legitimate. Du Qing's strength in the episode is this move from individual case handling to system reputation.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han adviser active in the Wang Feng power field.
- Defends Feng Yewang through legal clarity, mercy in doubtful punishment, and non-retroactivity.
- Reads Wang Zhang's death as a public-trust crisis because the formal charges are not publicly understood.
- Recommends reopening speech channels to prove that direct remonstrance is not punishable.
- Works through Wang Feng rather than directly against him, making advice possible inside concentrated power.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 520. It does not reconstruct Du Qing's full office, biography, factional affiliations, or later career. The source frames him as advising from a larger political view, but his private motive and relationship to Feng Yewang are not independently established.
