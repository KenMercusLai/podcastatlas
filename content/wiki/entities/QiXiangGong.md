# 齐襄公 / Duke Xiang of Qi

齐襄公 / Duke Xiang of Qi enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-75-2-qiongshe-jichi-de-zushiye-guanzhong-lo8uei2u1qwj9tszftel1b4lswoa]] as the ruler whose private and political disorder sets up [[GuanZhong|管仲]]'s later statecraft problem. The episode uses him to illustrate "君不君" role collapse: he maintains a scandalous relationship with [[WenJiang|文姜]], and [[LuHuanGong|鲁桓公]] is killed during the resulting Qi-Lu crisis.

The episode says Qi Xiang Gong then kills the killer, but that does not repair the damage. His conduct leaves [[QiState|齐国]] exposed to internal violence: [[GongsunWuzhi|公孙无知]] kills him and briefly takes power, creating the vacancy that brings [[QiHuanGong|姜小白]] and [[GongziJiu|公子纠]] racing home from abroad.

This page is distinct from [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王 / King Xiang of Qi]], the later Warring States ruler restored after the Yan conquest of Qi.
