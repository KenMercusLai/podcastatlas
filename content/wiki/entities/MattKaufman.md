# Matt Kaufman

Matt Kaufman is named in [[tech-20260424-0424-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260424-0424-mp-tech-pod-128]] as [[Roblox]]'s chief safety officer. His source role is to explain Roblox's age-verification tool, which uses a face snapshot to estimate a user's age and is described as having about 1.4 years of accuracy for users under 18.

Kaufman's comments make [[PlatformAgeEstimation]] concrete: Roblox presents age estimation as a maturity, scale, and safety response, while [[AnitaRamaswamy]] frames the rollout as also shaped by legal and regulatory pressure after child-safety allegations.
