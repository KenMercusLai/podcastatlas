# 刘巴 / Liu Ba

## Overview
刘巴 / Liu Ba is presented in Hanji 1146 as a technically capable but personally difficult adviser whose monetary proposal helps [[LiuBei|刘备]] address the treasury crisis after taking Chengdu.

## Current Profile
The current evidence is one structured popular-history episode. It says Liu Ba recommends issuing “值百钱,” defining one new coin as equivalent to one hundred old coins, channeling important goods through the new currency, establishing an official market, and using the money to purchase grain and cloth. The episode treats the plan as a rapid fiscal solution and as evidence that practical expertise can force a ruler to revise a negative personal assessment.

The profile remains bounded: the supplied summary does not establish Liu Ba's exact office, the policy's complete institutional design, its longer-run effects, or how much of the result should be attributed to him alone.

## Key Characteristics
- Offers a concrete fiscal response to an immediate post-conquest treasury shortage.
- Combines coin denomination with market rules and official procurement rather than treating minting as an isolated act.
- Is portrayed as professionally useful despite a proud manner and earlier lack of confidence in Liu Bei.
- Changes Liu Bei's assessment through problem-solving capacity rather than personal accommodation.

## Evidence
- Treasury response: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1146-liubei-qu-guafu-beihou-de-suanji-taishenle-lv4if2rvouqruziv8pt-klrcasfp|Hanji 1146]] attributes the high-denomination coin proposal to Liu Ba after the Chengdu treasury is depleted.
- Policy bundle: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1146-liubei-qu-guafu-beihou-de-suanji-taishenle-lv4if2rvouqruziv8pt-klrcasfp|Hanji 1146]] joins issuance to controlled exchange of important goods, an official market, and state purchasing.
- Revised evaluation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1146-liubei-qu-guafu-beihou-de-suanji-taishenle-lv4if2rvouqruziv8pt-klrcasfp|Hanji 1146]] says the result leads Liu Bei to recognize Liu Ba's practical value despite earlier personal friction.

## Qualifications
The source is a structured episode summary rather than a verified transcript or primary text. The coin standard, exchange rate, monopoly scope, official-market design, fiscal proceeds, price effects, civilian burden, chronology, and Liu Ba's motives require broader historical and numismatic verification. A short-run treasury gain does not by itself establish sustainable monetary success.
