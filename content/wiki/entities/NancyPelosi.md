# Nancy Pelosi

Nancy Pelosi appears in [[tsr-s5-ronconway-v5-tsr-s5-ronconway-v5]] as one of [[RonConway]]'s central political contacts during the [[SiliconValleyBank]] weekend. Conway says he called Pelosi while she was flying to San Francisco, and that she told him to come early to a dinner and set up a private war room with her and [[BarackObama]].

In Conway's account, Pelosi quickly understood the payroll and job-creation stakes and helped frame the policy tension as [[MoralHazardContagionTradeoff]]. She also asked Conway to join a California congressional delegation call while she was briefly unavailable, making her both a strategic adviser and a congressional-routing actor in the episode.
