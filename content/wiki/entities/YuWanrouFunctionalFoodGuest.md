# 余婉柔 / Yu Wanrou (functional food guest)

## Overview
余婉柔 is the nutrition and functional-food guest named in the VOL.209 [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode on supplements, special-use foods, electrolyte drinks, fish oil, meal replacements, and health-product marketing claims.

## Current Profile
The current wiki evidence presents 余婉柔 through one source-scoped episode. Her role is to move supplement discussion away from "more expensive," "more natural," "medical grade," or "higher absorption" claims and toward product identity, applicable population, label reading, actual nutrition gaps, and qualified medical or nutrition guidance.

Her explanation is consumer-facing rather than a personalized supplement protocol. She treats drugs, foods for special medical purposes, special dietary foods, blue-hat health foods, ordinary foods, and market-facing functional foods as different categories, then applies those distinctions to everyday products such as protein powder, electrolyte drinks, fish oil, collagen, multivitamins, meal replacements, and viral wellness drinks.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains functional-food and supplement questions through regulatory identity, product purpose, and applicable population.
- Separates special medical nutrition, ordinary health foods, and drug treatment rather than treating all health-coded products as equivalent.
- Uses protein powder, electrolyte drinks, fish oil, collagen, meal replacements, multivitamins, turmeric drinks, and sleep drinks as practical examples.
- Critiques social-media and brand claims around naturalness, nano-scale formulation, high absorption, medical-grade framing, and one-dose effects.
- Keeps supplementation subordinate to regular meals, sleep, exercise, diagnosis, medication context, and clinician or nutritionist guidance.
