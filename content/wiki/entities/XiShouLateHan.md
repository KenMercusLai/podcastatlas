# 习授 / Xi Shou (Late Han)

## Overview
习授 / Xi Shou (Late Han) is the colleague who reports [[LouGuiLateHan|娄圭]]'s ambiguous remark to [[CaoCao|曹操]], making him the transmission link between private speech and fatal political punishment in Hanji 1092.

## Current Profile
The episode says Xi Shou and Lou Gui travel together in 211 CE and see Cao Cao and his son in a grand procession. Xi Shou praises the scene; Lou Gui replies that one should accomplish matters oneself rather than merely admire others. Xi Shou interprets the comment as ridicule or disloyalty and reports it the same day. The page records the reporting act without claiming that Xi Shou's interpretation was correct.

## Key Characteristics
- Colleague and travel companion of Lou Gui in the episode.
- Interprets ambiguous speech through a political-loyalty frame.
- Reports the remark directly to Cao Cao.
- Becomes the immediate information channel preceding Lou Gui's death.

## Evidence
- Conversation setting: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1092-fu-ke-di-guo-de-lougui-yin-yiju-gankai-bei-caocao-cisi-lutwkvmtn_7cv4r5l-mwknmgyjhs|Hanji 1092]] places Xi Shou and Lou Gui in the same carriage watching Cao Cao's procession.
- Denunciation effect: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1092-fu-ke-di-guo-de-lougui-yin-yiju-gankai-bei-caocao-cisi-lutwkvmtn_7cv4r5l-mwknmgyjhs|Hanji 1092]] says Xi Shou reports the remark before Cao Cao imprisons and kills Lou Gui.

## Qualifications
The source does not establish Xi Shou's complete biography, personal motive, exact wording, or whether he expected execution rather than investigation. The identification of Lou Gui's remark as mockery remains Xi Shou's reported interpretation, not a settled fact.
