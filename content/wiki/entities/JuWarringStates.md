# 莒 / Ju

莒 / Ju appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-73-lishishang-ziyou-lianai-de-kaichuangzhe-shi-shui-lkumfsfialqgdl6qpb3c-hnz7h9u]] as one of the surviving Qi-held places after [[YanState|燕国]] and [[LeYi|乐毅]] shatter [[QiState|齐国]]. The episode calls it 莒城 when [[TianFazhang|田法章]] hides there after [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]]'s death.

In this source, Ju is both refuge and restoration base. Tian Fazhang disappears into [[TaishiJiao|太史角]]'s household there, [[WangSunGu|王孙古]]'s revenge against [[ZhuoChi|淖齿 / 卓齿]] clears the immediate killer, and Qi ministers finally install Tian Fazhang at Ju as [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-78-qiguo-mingyun-jing-bei-chengguan-gei-jiule-lpt3reyr6ok8g2lhcxrnd5qctyug]] keeps Ju in the same final-holdout role. Along with [[JimoWarringStates|即墨]], it is one of the two remaining Qi-held cities while [[LeYi|乐毅]] surrounds but does not storm them, preferring [[OccupationPacificationStrategy|occupation pacification]] over a costly assault.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-151-huanyuan-pengcheng-zhizhan-xiangyu-zai-fengshen-1-lspx-bqe5kczq6bmn6thlerfbrmu|Hanji 151 part 1]] returns to Ju through the Chengyang geography note. The host explains that this Chu-Han Chengyang should be understood around the Ju/Juxian area, not the earlier Chengyang mentioned during a Pengcheng retreat. [[TianHeng|田横]]'s resistance there after [[TianRong|田荣]]'s death reuses Ju's older Qi-survival pattern: in both the Yan conquest and the Xiang Yu campaign, the location marks a last-base refusal to let Qi become fully pacified.
