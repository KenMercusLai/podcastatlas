# Eliezer Gold

## Overview
Eliezer Gold is the real estate investor whose LLCs bought more than 500 Baltimore homes in the portfolio examined by [[the-loan-at-the-heart-of-a-new-foreclosure-crisis]].

## Current Profile
In the source, Gold is the central investor figure in the Baltimore DSCR-loan failure. His entities accumulated hundreds of row homes, many later entered foreclosure, and reporters struggled to obtain comment. The episode says the FBI opened an investigation into Gold, [[BenjaminEidlis]], and others, but it does not establish legal culpability.

## Key Characteristics
- His LLCs bought more than 500 homes in the Baltimore portfolio.
- Reporters found little local visibility around him before the foreclosure problems.
- He did not respond to repeated outreach through phone, email, letters, and attorneys.
- His name became part of an unresolved FBI investigation and private-lender blacklist.
- His Edding Street properties anchor the episode's neighborhood-harm section.

## Evidence
Portfolio scale:
- [[the-loan-at-the-heart-of-a-new-foreclosure-crisis]] says Gold and his LLCs bought more than 500 of the homes.

Reporter pursuit:
- [[the-loan-at-the-heart-of-a-new-foreclosure-crisis]] says Jack and [[HallieMiller]] traveled to Spring Valley, New York, after failing to reach Gold through other channels.

Neighborhood effects:
- [[the-loan-at-the-heart-of-a-new-foreclosure-crisis]] says Gold's LLC bought 20 row houses on one side of Edding Street that later went into foreclosure.

## Qualifications
The source is careful that suspected fraud remains unresolved. Gold declined to comment, and the episode leaves open fraud, incompetence, being overwhelmed, or mixed causes.
