# 格森星 / Gethen

格森星 / Gethen, also called Winter in the episode, is the planet at the center of [[UrsulaKLeGuin|Le Guin]]'s [[TheLeftHandOfDarkness|《黑暗的左手》]]. In [[36-heian-de-zuoshou-liufang-yu-ai-de-weilai-wangshi-605254522]], [[GenlyAi|金利·艾 / Genly Ai]] arrives there as an envoy of the [[Ekumen|艾库曼 / Ekumen]] and tries to persuade its nations to join a wider alliance.

The source treats Gethen as a social thought experiment, not just an alien landscape. Its inhabitants do not live with fixed male/female division, and their monthly kemmer cycle makes sex, parenthood, protection, vulnerability, and social hierarchy work differently. The planet also contains political contrast: [[Karhide|卡海德 / Karhide]] is honor-bound and courtly, while [[Orgoreyn|欧格瑞恩 / Orgoreyn]] is centralized, bureaucratic, and surveilled.
