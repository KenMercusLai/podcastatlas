# Anna Sorokin / 安娜·索罗金

Anna Sorokin / 安娜·索罗金 is the case used by [[162-caifu-de-benzhi-yiji-ziyou-de-zhenzheng-hanyi-chuantai-shifen-xiyin-lofyxfhiprud7zvekufexsjhtust]] to explain how performed capital can roll until cash-flow and credit verification stop it. The episode presents her as someone who fabricated economic capital, borrowed the signs of cultural capital, entered social networks, and tried to turn symbolic capital into bank financing.

The source's deeper point is not celebrity gossip. Sorokin shows a failed [[CapitalConversionLoop]]: social belief can temporarily make an identity act like wealth, but without enforceable assets, cash flow, and credible credit, the conversion breaks. The episode also notes that after conviction, the story itself became a monetizable form of controversial symbolic capital.
