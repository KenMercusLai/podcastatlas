# Hi3D

## Overview

Hi3D is a [[ShumeiWanwu|Shumei Wanwu]] product described in the source as a one-stop AI 3D workflow for generating, editing, preparing, and routing 3D models toward manufacturing devices.

## Current Profile

The product is presented as more than image-to-3D conversion. In the episode, Hi3D includes semantic understanding, model splitting, connector placement, automatic arrangement, material and printer parameter handling, and export into downstream slicing or manufacturing workflows.

Its strategic role is to turn AI 3D from a visual asset into a production surface for Makers and potentially 2B private workflows.

## Key Characteristics

- Starts from one image or concept and generates a high-quality 3D model.
- Supports production-oriented operations such as segmentation, splitting, and connector addition.
- Handles automatic arrangement and equipment/material parameters before downstream slicing or manufacturing.
- Represents Shumei Wanwu's attempt to build a workflow product around the model rather than only expose model output.
- Can become a modular canvas-like AI 3D workflow where atomized capabilities are assembled by natural-language intent.
- May serve both individual Makers and business customers needing private, controllable production pipelines.
