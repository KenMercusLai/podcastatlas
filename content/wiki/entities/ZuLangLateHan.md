# 祖郎 / Zu Lang (late Han)

祖郎 / Zu Lang (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] as a local strongman near [[JingCountyLateHan|泾县]] in [[DanyangCommandery|丹阳郡]]. He attacks [[SunCe|孙策]] while Sun Ce is moving through the region, nearly killing him.

The source uses Zu Lang to show that Sun Ce's Jiangdong entry was not a smooth family takeover. Local powerholders could resist an outside force associated with [[YuanShu|袁术]], so Sun Ce's later growth depends on surviving local hostility before converting Danyang and Jiangdong resources into a base.
