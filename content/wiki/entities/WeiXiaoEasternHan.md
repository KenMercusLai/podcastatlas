## Overview

隗嚣 / Wei Xiao is synthesized as a source-bounded western rival in the Guangwu founding war. Hanji 708 does not give a full rise-and-fall biography; it preserves his late crisis, death under illness and famine, and the continuation of his faction through Wei Chun with [[GongsunShuEasternHan|Gongsun Shu]] support.

## Current Profile

Hanji 708 begins from Liu Xiu's failed attempt to overwhelm Wei Xiao with a large siege before Gongsun Shu's reinforcements disrupt the plan. The same western conflict field includes [[WenXuEasternHan|Wen Xu]]'s capture by Wei Xiao's subordinate Gou Yu and suicide after refusing surrender.

Wei Xiao himself appears near the end of life: old, sick, caught in famine, unable to eat properly, and dying in resentment. His death does not settle the war. Wang Yuan and Zhou Zong make his young son Wei Chun the ruler, continue the army, and receive Gongsun Shu's support through Zhao Kuang and Tian Yan.

## Key Characteristics

- Western rival of Liu Xiu whose late position is still militarily meaningful in Jianwu 9.
- Actor whose side is strengthened by Gongsun Shu's reinforcement and later support.
- Political-military node whose death does not end the Longyou resistance field.
- Source-bounded figure currently defined by final illness, famine, succession, and continuing factional resistance.

## Qualifications

The page does not reconstruct Wei Xiao's earlier career, claim to authority, relation with local elites, or full military geography. Hanji 708 is a late-stage source note, so the current profile should remain centered on final crisis and factional continuation until earlier sources are ingested.
