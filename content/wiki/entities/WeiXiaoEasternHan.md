## Overview
隗嚣 / Wei Xiao is synthesized as a western rival in the Guangwu founding war whose current profile now starts with a Gengshi-court self-protection move before the later Chang'an exit, Tianshui camp formation, talent recruitment, and western reputation, then Liu Xiu's later courtship, Longyou origin backfill, Jianwu 5 loyalty testing, breakaway, and battlefield failure. ## Current Profile
Hanji 674 now supplies an earlier Gengshi-court self-protection layer. Wei Cui and Wei Yi, already submitted to Liu Xuan, plot to return to Tianshui. Wei Xiao fears being implicated, reports them to Liu Xuan, and survives the purge: Liu Xuan kills Wei Cui and Wei Yi, then appoints Wei Xiao yushi dafu. This makes Wei Xiao's first visible profile one of risk avoidance within a collapsing court, before he openly exits toward Tianshui.

Hanji 677 supplies the next pre-Tianshui exit layer. In Chang'an, Wei Xiao joins Zhang Ang, Liao Zhan, Hu Yin, and Shentu Jian in planning to seize Liu Xuan at the autumn ancestral rite after Liu Xuan rejects the plunder-and-retreat proposal. When Liu Xuan summons the group, Wei Xiao senses danger, claims illness, gathers Wang Zun, Zhou Zong, and troops for self-defense, then breaks out of the crisis and returns to Tianshui. This makes his later Tianshui camp formation appear as an exit from Gengshi collapse, not only a fresh regional beginning.

Hanji 681 supplies the earliest current camp-formation layer. After returning to Tianshui, Wei Xiao resumes earlier ambitions, calls himself West State shangjiangjun, and receives Sanfu refugees and scholars with unusual courtesy. The source names figures such as [[ZhengXingEasternHan|Zheng Xing]], [[ShentuGangEasternHan|Shentu Gang]], [[DuLinEasternHan|Du Lin]], [[MaYuanEasternHan|Ma Yuan]], and [[BanBiaoEasternHan|Ban Biao]] inside or around this western reputation field.

The same source shows why later Liu Xiu courtship has something to work with. Wei Xiao's name travels east of the Xiao mountains, and his camp temporarily looks like a scholar-attracting western order rather than only a local military holdout. That early positive recruitment layer sharpens the later contrast with suspicion, talent exit, and inability to choose.

Hanji 689-1 supplies the next current courtship layer. Lai Xi tells Liu Xiu that he knew Wei Xiao in Chang'an and that Wei Xiao originally rose while restoring Han, so winning Wei Xiao over would leave Gongsun Shu isolated. Liu Xiu sends Lai Xi west, receives Wei Xiao's contact, and answers with high ceremony, treating Wei Xiao almost as a peer state and using his courtesy name. The episode treats that warmth as tactical relationship management, not as proof of settled allegiance, because Wei Xiao is already characterized as deeply suspicious.

Hanji 691 supplies the cooperative-but-unstable alignment layer before the later refusal sequence. Wei Xiao sends Ma Yuan to Chengdu to observe Gongsun Shu, accepts Ma Yuan's advice to contact Liu Xiu more wholeheartedly, and sends him onward with a letter. Liu Xiu's handwritten reply credits Wei Xiao with blocking Gongsun Shu and northern Qiang/Hu pressure while enabling Feng Yi's Sanfu operations. Wei Xiao even kills Gongsun Shu's envoy after receiving Da Sikong and Fu'an Wang insignia, but the host treats his cooperation as unstable because he helps Liu Xiu while continuing to watch the field rather than making his position unequivocal.

Hanji 694 supplies the warning-before-test layer. Wei Xiao is presented as moving from a survivable `求不败` posture toward `求全胜` and kingship ambition. Ban Biao's `王命论` tries to arrest that shift by arguing that imperial authority depends on mandate, historical legitimacy, virtue, and human support rather than on force or opportunity alone. Wei Xiao does not accept the counsel, and Ban Biao leaves him for Dou Rong.

Hanji 698 supplies the earliest current alignment-test layer. Liu Xiu sends Wei Xiao the Guanzhong commanders' petition proposing an attack on Gongsun Shu, but Wei Xiao refuses by citing Sanfu weakness and [[LuFangEasternHan|Lu Fang]]'s northern threat. Liu Xiu then repeatedly sends Lai Xi and Ma Yuan to persuade Wei Xiao to come to Luoyang. Wei Xiao speaks deferentially but will not put his own body under court control, so he sends Wei Xun instead.

The same source makes Wei Xiao's internal problem visible before open rebellion. [[ZhengXingEasternHan|Zheng Xing]] leaves through a filial-burial request despite Wei Xiao's attempt to retain him; Ma Yuan moves east with his household; Wang Yuan urges Wei Xiao to close [[HanguPass|Hangu Pass]], use Tianshui's intact army and terrain, and wait; [[ShentuGangEasternHan|Shentu Gang]] urges him to read human support and mandate as already turning toward Liu Xiu. Wei Xiao prefers the holdout logic, and the episode frames the result as talent loss produced by suspicion, dissatisfaction, and inability to choose.

Hanji 699 supplies the origin and reputation-pressure layer. It presents Wei Xiao as a Longyou/Tianshui Chengji magnate whose relatives and local associates move first against Wang Mang, then press him despite his warning that war is destructive. The source makes him shangjiangjun in 23 CE, so his later western challenge grows out of local elite demand and anti-Xin mobilization before it becomes a contest with Liu Xiu.

Hanji 700 supplies the transition layer before open rebellion. Liu Xiu sees Wei Xiao and Gongsun Shu as the two remaining unsettled powers, but Wei Xiao's eldest son Wei Xun is already in Luoyang as hostage, so Liu Xiu pauses direct military resolution and uses repeated letters to explain the danger of non-submission. In Jing Han's later argument to Gongsun Shu, Wei Xiao becomes a warning: he held Yongzhou, had a strong army and reputation, but wanted to be a Zhou Wenwang-style western hegemon while deferentially serving Han, allowing Liu Xiu to avoid a two-front crisis.

Hanji 701 supplies the breakaway signal. Liu Xiu orders Wei Xiao to attack Gongsun Shu from Tianshui, but Wei Xiao answers with road, plank-path, and timing excuses. Liu Xiu reads this as evidence that Wei Xiao can no longer be used; Lai Xi then carries an imperial order, rebukes Wei Xiao for listening to smaller men, and narrowly survives when Wei Xiao's camp considers killing him. The same source shows Wei Xiao moving from evasion to rebellion, blocking Longdi, defeating the first Han campaign, and pursuing until Ma Wu's cavalry rearguard lets the Han commanders return.

Hanji 702 supplies the earliest current tactical-pressure layer. After the Han side retreats from Longshan, Wei Xiao sends Wang Yuan and Xing Xun with more than 20,000 troops down the mountain to take a target city before Feng Yi arrives. The plan shows Wei Xiao still capable of exploiting a Han setback, but Feng Yi secretly occupies the city first and turns Xing Xun's arrival into a rout.

The same source turns that battlefield failure into an alignment problem. Ji Zun defeats Wang Yuan on another line, Beidi local strongmen begin moving away from Wei Xiao, and Liu Xiu sends Feng Yi through Yiqu against Jia Lan and Xiongnu-linked forces. Beidi, Shangjun, and Anding then submit, making the failed first-move attempt an early sign that Wei Xiao's local field can flip after visible defeat.

Hanji 703 supplies the next pressure layer. Dou Rong writes Wei Xiao that changing course after earlier Han loyalty would destroy old merit, renew commoner suffering, and leave him with weak alternatives such as Gongsun Shu or [[LuFangEasternHan|Lu Fang]]. Wei Xiao ignores this. Liang Tong then kills Wei Xiao's envoy, breaks the relationship, and discards Wei Xiao's general seal and ribbon, while Ma Yuan explains to Liu Xiu that his old relationship with Wei Xiao and earlier eastward report were meant to guide Wei Xiao toward the stronger side.

The same source shows Liu Xiu turning Ma Yuan into a campaign instrument. Ma Yuan asks to present anti-Wei strategy, then moves with more than five thousand cavalry among Wei-side commanders and Qiang leaders, including [[GaoJunEasternHan|Gao Jun]], to persuade and divide them. He also writes Yang Guang, pressing the whole-realm momentum against Wei Xiao and invoking Wei Xun's hostage status in Luoyang as a family-disaster warning. The host reads Wei Xiao's failure through hesitation, suspicion, procrastination, and weak decision capacity.

Hanji 704 then supplies the face-saving political-choice layer. After defeat, Wei Xiao is ridiculed for claiming to protect homeland, ancestral tombs, and descendants while losing the practical ground for those claims, and for rejecting Gongsun Shu's incorporation before turning back toward him. Ma Yuan warns Yang Guang that Wei Xiao holds only two commanderies against the wider realm and should be remonstrated with or abandoned. Wei Xiao then sends Liu Xiu an apology that keeps臣节 language while justifying evasion; Liu Xiu offers title and stipend preservation if Wei Xiao restrains himself and sends another hostage, but Wei Xiao turns to Gongsun Shu.

Hanji 705-1 backfills the active-threat layer. Wei Xiao invades Anding with more than thirty thousand troops, but Feng Yi and Ji Zun prevent success. Liu Xiu prepares to campaign personally before heavy rain and Wei Xiao's withdrawal interrupt the plan. The next year, Lai Xi's surprise capture of Lüeyang forces Wei Xiao to concentrate Wang Yuan, Xing Xun, Wang Meng, Niu Han, and personally led forces around a position Liu Xiu understands as Wei Xiao's shield. Gongsun Shu sends Li Yu and Tian Yan to help, but even flood attempts fail and Wei Xiao's elite troops are stuck for months.

In Hanji 707, Wei Xiao's western position is under sharper pressure. Liu Xiu's side wins over subordinate commanders, counties, and large populations, then offers Wei Xiao a chance to surrender personally, see his son again, and avoid destruction. Wei Xiao does not accept; Liu Xiu responds by killing Wei Xun and sending commanders against Xicheng and Shanggui.

The campaign still does not end cleanly. [[WuHanEasternHan|Wu Han]] and [[CenPengEasternHan|Cen Peng]] besiege Xicheng, while [[GengYanEasternHan|Geng Yan]] and Gai Yan pressure Shanggui. Cen Peng's water attack nearly succeeds, but Wang Yuan, Xing Xun, and Zhou Zong arrive with Gongsun Shu-backed reinforcements, break into Xicheng, and rescue Wei Xiao. Wu Han's food crisis and retreat then let Wei Xiao retake Anding, Beidi, Tianshui, and Longxi.

Hanji 708 gives the end of that personal arc. Wei Xiao is old, sick, caught in famine, unable to eat properly, and dies in resentment. His death does not settle the war. Wang Yuan and Zhou Zong elevate his young son Wei Chun, continue the army, and receive further Gongsun Shu support through Zhao Kuang and Tian Yan.

## Key Characteristics
- Western rival whose current arc begins with Gengshi-court self-protection before Chang'an exit, Tianshui return, talent recruitment, courtship, cooperative alignment, warnings, and loyalty tests.
- Ambiguous ally whose kingship or total-victory imagination makes later refusal to attack Gongsun Shu more than a tactical delay.
- Holdout whose competing counsel, defection pressure, and family-hostage risk precede his face-saving apology.
- Self-justifying holdout who wants dignity, title, and safety without making submission credible enough for Liu Xiu.
- Suspicious and hesitant decision-maker whose own camp is being divided through letters, visible breaks, and insider persuasion before open collapse.
- Commander who can exploit Han setbacks, invade Anding, and draw Gongsun Shu support, yet fails to convert that pressure into durable strategic recovery.
- Holdout who refuses a survival offer after major subordinate and county surrender.

## Qualifications
Hanji 674 supplies a Gengshi-court self-protection layer but keeps Wei Cui, Wei Yi, exact kinship or factional structure, Tianshui route mechanics, and Liu Xuan's appointment process source-scoped. Hanji 677 supplies a Chang'an exit layer but keeps Zhang Ang, Liao Zhan, Hu Yin, Shentu Jian, Wang Zun, Zhou Zong, and the full reason for Wei Xiao's choices source-scoped. Hanji 681 captures an early camp-formation and scholar-attraction layer but leaves Fan Jun, exact appointments, Sanfu refugee composition, and the full guest status of Ban Biao source-scoped. Hanji 689-1 and Hanji 691 capture later courtship and cooperative stages and should not be read as contradicting the later refusal, rebellion, and Gongsun Shu alignment sequence. Hanji 689-1 leaves the exact first memorial wording, courtesy-name protocol, and Liu Xiu's full strategic calculation source-scoped. Hanji 691 leaves Qin Feng, Zhu Hu, Fu Jun, Li Yu, Cheng Yan, Lü Wei, exact title insignia, and Taishan commandery details source-scoped. Hanji 694 supplies Ban Biao's warning and the host's ambition diagnosis, but it does not reconstruct Wei Xiao's own full reply to `王命论` or every reason he rejected it. Hanji 698 supplies an earlier alignment-test layer but leaves Liu Yong, exact Wei Xun court-title wording, Shanglinyuan farming logistics, Wang Yuan's later service, and the full list of departing followers source-scoped. Hanji 699 supplies an origin layer but still does not reconstruct Wei Xiao's full early career, complete claim to authority, or local faction map. Hanji 700 preserves the hostage-and-letter transition and Jing Han's outside evaluation of Wei Xiao, but it does not reconstruct Wei Xiao's own internal reasoning in that pause. Hanji 701 supports the shift from evasive noncooperation to open rebellion, but Wang Zun, Niu Han, Wang Yuan's exact blocking details, and Wei Xiao's internal deliberation remain source-scoped. Hanji 702 leaves the target city, exact Wang Yuan and Xing Xun routes, Jia Lan's role, and the Beidi strongmen source-scoped. Hanji 703 and Hanji 704 both carry strong host interpretation of Wei Xiao's psychology, but the faction's internal fears, exact timing, hostage-risk calculations, Zhang Xue, Ren Yu, Wang Yuan's advice, and unnamed Qiang leaders remain source-scoped. Hanji 705-1 and Hanji 707 preserve different stages of pressure: Lüeyang is an earlier attrition trap, while Xicheng and Shanggui are later near-collapse and rescue. The transcript forms 韦敖, 魏敖, 魏嚣, and 魏瑶 are normalized to 隗嚣 because the Longyou/Tianshui, western campaign, Gongsun Shu alliance, Lüeyang/Xicheng, and Hanji 708 continuation contexts match the established route.
