## Overview
隗嚣 / Wei Xiao is synthesized as a western rival in the Guangwu founding war whose current profile now starts before his apology test, with ignored warnings, elite breaks, and family-security pressure. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-703-donghan-kaiguo-hou-zui-xianhe-de-jiazu-pingsha-shi-ta-lgg-fra8ostp2xsuugph6o-ilzac|Hanji 703]] shows [[DouRongEasternHan|Dou Rong]] warning him not to waste earlier Han loyalty, [[LiangTongEasternHan|Liang Tong]] publicly severing ties, and [[MaYuanEasternHan|Ma Yuan]] helping [[LiuXiu|Liu Xiu]] divide Wei-side commanders. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-704-weixiao-wo-da-ni-shi-bu-dui-dan-ni-ping-sha-hai-shou-lgi-cjyledgzldrie6kwpvejwsls|Hanji 704]] then shows him apologizing while preserving status claims, receiving a conditional hostage-and-title offer, and turning toward [[GongsunShuEasternHan|Gongsun Shu]] when Liu Xiu refuses ambiguity. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-705-1-diwang-moulue-liuxiu-ruhe-yi-sheling-nizhuan-caiyuan-fengyun-lq2m06p7eczj9ebysxpl84bkzzi|Hanji 705-1]] shows him still able to invade Anding and coordinate with Gongsun Shu, but also vulnerable when [[LaiXiEasternHan|Lai Xi]] seizes Lüeyang. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-707-gan-yu-liuxiu-zhengxiong-de-ta-weihe-candan-taowang-lndwxm9lmckt1ps6eowqdi-v82pv|Hanji 707]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-708-jizun-bingshi-weihe-yinfa-baiguan-jingkong-lhfuvjyiiirlaf-ugrufkaspuauq|Hanji 708]] carry that unresolved western field into refusal, temporary rescue, famine, death, and Wei Chun's continuation.

## Current Profile
Hanji 703 supplies the earliest current pressure layer. Dou Rong writes Wei Xiao that changing course after earlier Han loyalty would destroy old merit, renew commoner suffering, and leave him with weak alternatives such as Gongsun Shu or [[LuFangEasternHan|Lu Fang]]. Wei Xiao ignores this. Liang Tong then kills Wei Xiao's envoy, breaks the relationship, and discards Wei Xiao's general seal and ribbon, while Ma Yuan explains to Liu Xiu that his old relationship with Wei Xiao and earlier eastward report were meant to guide Wei Xiao toward the stronger side.

The same source shows Liu Xiu turning Ma Yuan into a campaign instrument. Ma Yuan asks to present anti-Wei strategy, then moves with more than five thousand cavalry among Wei-side commanders and Qiang leaders, including [[GaoJunEasternHan|Gao Jun]], to persuade and divide them. He also writes Yang Guang, pressing the whole-realm momentum against Wei Xiao and invoking Wei Xun's hostage status in Luoyang as a family-disaster warning. The host reads Wei Xiao's failure through hesitation, suspicion, procrastination, and weak decision capacity.

Hanji 704 then supplies the face-saving political-choice layer. After defeat, Wei Xiao is ridiculed for claiming to protect homeland, ancestral tombs, and descendants while losing the practical ground for those claims, and for rejecting Gongsun Shu's incorporation before turning back toward him. Ma Yuan warns Yang Guang that Wei Xiao holds only two commanderies against the wider realm and should be remonstrated with or abandoned. Wei Xiao then sends Liu Xiu an apology that keeps臣节 language while justifying evasion; Liu Xiu offers title and stipend preservation if Wei Xiao restrains himself and sends another hostage, but Wei Xiao turns to Gongsun Shu.

Hanji 705-1 backfills the active-threat layer. Wei Xiao invades Anding with more than thirty thousand troops, but Feng Yi and Ji Zun prevent success. Liu Xiu prepares to campaign personally before heavy rain and Wei Xiao's withdrawal interrupt the plan. The next year, Lai Xi's surprise capture of Lüeyang forces Wei Xiao to concentrate Wang Yuan, Xing Xun, Wang Meng, Niu Han, and personally led forces around a position Liu Xiu understands as Wei Xiao's shield. Gongsun Shu sends Li Yu and Tian Yan to help, but even flood attempts fail and Wei Xiao's elite troops are stuck for months.

In Hanji 707, Wei Xiao's western position is under sharper pressure. Liu Xiu's side wins over subordinate commanders, counties, and large populations, then offers Wei Xiao a chance to surrender personally, see his son again, and avoid destruction. Wei Xiao does not accept; Liu Xiu responds by killing Wei Xun and sending commanders against Xicheng and Shanggui.

The campaign still does not end cleanly. [[WuHanEasternHan|Wu Han]] and [[CenPengEasternHan|Cen Peng]] besiege Xicheng, while [[GengYanEasternHan|Geng Yan]] and Gai Yan pressure Shanggui. Cen Peng's water attack nearly succeeds, but Wang Yuan, Xing Xun, and Zhou Zong arrive with Gongsun Shu-backed reinforcements, break into Xicheng, and rescue Wei Xiao. Wu Han's food crisis and retreat then let Wei Xiao retake Anding, Beidi, Tianshui, and Longxi.

Hanji 708 gives the end of that personal arc. Wei Xiao is old, sick, caught in famine, unable to eat properly, and dies in resentment. His death does not settle the war. Wang Yuan and Zhou Zong elevate his young son Wei Chun, continue the army, and receive further Gongsun Shu support through Zhao Kuang and Tian Yan.

## Key Characteristics
- Western rival of Liu Xiu whose current profile starts with ignored counsel, defection pressure, and family-hostage risk before his face-saving apology.
- Self-justifying holdout who wants dignity, title, and safety without making submission credible enough for Liu Xiu.
- Suspicious and hesitant decision-maker whose own camp is being divided through letters, visible breaks, and insider persuasion before open collapse.
- Commander who can invade Anding and draw Gongsun Shu support, yet fails to retake the key Lüeyang shield.
- Holdout who refuses a survival offer after major subordinate and county surrender.
- Actor whose resistance survives through Gongsun Shu-backed relief even after severe encirclement pressure.
- Political-military node whose death does not end the Longyou resistance field.

## Qualifications
The page still does not reconstruct Wei Xiao's earlier career, claim to authority, local-elite base, or full military geography. Hanji 703 and Hanji 704 both add strong host interpretation of Wei Xiao's psychology, but the faction's internal fears, exact timing, hostage-risk calculations, Zhang Xue, Ren Yu, Wang Yuan's advice, and unnamed Qiang leaders remain source-scoped. Hanji 705-1 and Hanji 707 preserve different stages of pressure: Lüeyang is an earlier attrition trap, while Xicheng and Shanggui are later near-collapse and rescue. The transcript forms 魏敖, 魏嚣, and 魏瑶 are normalized to 隗嚣 because the western campaign, Gongsun Shu alliance, Lüeyang/Xicheng/Longyou context, and Hanji 708 continuation match the established route.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 703 as the earliest current pressure layer: Dou Rong's warning, Liang Tong's public break, Ma Yuan's cavalry persuasion mission, and family-hostage warning through Yang Guang.
- Reframed Hanji 704 as the later failed face-saving apology layer after earlier counsel and faction-fracture pressure.
- Added Hanji 705-1 as the earlier Anding and Lüeyang layer, showing Wei Xiao still dangerous but increasingly trapped by Liu Xiu's timing.
- Preserved Hanji 707 as Wei Xiao's pre-death collapse, refused surrender offer, Xicheng rescue, and temporary western recovery layer.
- Preserved Hanji 708 as the final illness, famine, death, and Wei Chun continuation layer.
