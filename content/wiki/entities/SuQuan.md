## Overview

苏荃 / Su Quan is a mature strategist in [[LuDingJi|《鹿鼎记》]] whose marriage to [[WeiXiaobao|韦小宝]] is read as a practical alliance, capability partnership, and source of household leadership.

## Current Profile

The episode emphasizes Su Quan's age, experience, composure, and ability to solve complicated problems. Her value in the relationship comes less from romantic passion than from complementary competence: Wei consults her, and she advises, coordinates, and mediates within the household.

Calling the relationship a political marriage or strong alliance does not make it empty. It locates intimacy partly in shared survival, decision-making, and the labor of sustaining a complicated family.

## Key Characteristics

- Older, experienced, and strategically capable.
- Serves as an adviser when Wei faces complex problems.
- Acts as the practical leader and mediator among the wives.
- Forms a capability-complementary alliance with Wei.
- Represents partnership through household labor and judgment more than romantic intensity.
