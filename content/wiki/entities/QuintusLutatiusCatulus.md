# Quintus Lutatius Catulus / 昆图斯·卢塔提乌斯·卡图卢斯

## Overview

Quintus Lutatius Catulus / 昆图斯·卢塔提乌斯·卡图卢斯 is the aristocratic counterweight whom conservative Romans promote beside [[GaiusMarius]] after the defeat of the [[TeutonesAndCimbri]].

## Current Profile

Episode 702 presents Catulus as Marius's consular colleague in the final northern campaign and as a figure whom conservative aristocrats can use to dilute Marius's overwhelming credit. Catulus shares in the final victory, receives a triumph alongside Marius, and later builds a temple just as Marius does.

Catulus matters mainly as a political symbol. The episode treats him as an aristocratic counterweight to Marius, but it makes [[LuciusCorneliusSulla]] the more serious rival because Sulla combines military talent, aristocratic positioning, and later command ambition.

## Key Characteristics

- Catulus represents aristocratic credit-balancing after Marius's victories.
- His shared command and triumph show that victory memory can be distributed politically.
- His temple-building parallels Marius's public prestige work.
- His importance is symbolic compared with Sulla's deeper rivalry with Marius.
