# 鄂千秋 / E Qianqiu

鄂千秋 / E Qianqiu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-174-liubang-weihe-dafeng-zijide-chouren-2-luet7gqkako8idt9o3ccqq7cgpbt|Hanji 174 part 2]] as the figure who defends [[XiaoHe|萧何]]'s first-place ranking among early Han merit-holders. When other officials argue that [[CaoShen|曹参]] has more visible combat wounds and captured-city achievements, E Qianqiu reframes the comparison as "一时之功" versus "万世之功."

His argument is that Liu Bang's armies repeatedly collapsed, dispersed, or had to flee during the long struggle with [[XiangYu|项羽]], while Xiao He kept sending troops and grain from [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]]. The source uses E Qianqiu to make the reward hierarchy legible: battlefield excellence matters, but the rear system that prevents the state from dying can rank higher.

After hearing the explanation, [[LiuBang|刘邦]] rewards E Qianqiu by making him Anping marquis. The source therefore gives him a narrow but important role as a recommender and interpreter of merit, not as an independent strategist.
