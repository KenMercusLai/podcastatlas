# 曾鸣 / Zeng Ming

## Overview
曾鸣 / Zeng Ming is the strategy scholar and former [[Alibaba]] chief strategy officer interviewed in [[153-he-zengming-liao-chanye-shiguan-canku-de-zhenxiang-hui-xiaowang-de-gongsi-youxiu-budengyu-zhuoyue-oai-anth-dagailv-bushi-yuansheng-shidai-da-yingjia-luzvhptdkotbkvr-shfrkvvuim2p]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Zeng as a strategy thinker whose authority comes from combining academic training, Alibaba's formative strategic decisions, and long-cycle industry-history comparison. His AI-era profile is not a model-builder's view from inside a frontier lab; it is a stage-theory and organization-design view that asks which capabilities, institutions, and business forms fit each phase of a general-purpose technology.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses industry history to compare AI with the internet, mobile internet, electricity, automobiles, and consumer appliances.
- Treats [[Taobao]], [[AntGroup|Alipay]], and [[AlibabaCloud]] as Alibaba-era examples of high-conviction strategic decisions made before consensus formed.
- Frames AI model companies as likely infrastructure providers rather than guaranteed native-era application winners.
- Emphasizes product originality, user empathy, mission, and contrarian judgment as the route from excellent execution to greatness.
- Connects AI strategy to organization form, arguing that tasks, high agency, and partner-like contribution may matter more than traditional job positions and hierarchy.
