## Overview

荀彘 / Xun Zhi is the Western Han left general whose conflict with [[YangPuWesternHan|楼船将军杨仆]] fragments the campaign against [[WimanJoseon|卫氏朝鲜]] before he receives the combined army and completes the conquest.

## Current Profile

Xun Zhi argues that Yang Pu repeatedly fails to meet agreed attack dates and may create a larger danger through his separate dealings with Joseon. [[GongsunSuiWesternHan|公孙遂]] accepts this account, arrests Yang Pu, and gives Xun Zhi unified control. Intensified attack contributes to elite defections, [[UgeoWimanJoseon|卫右渠]]'s death, and the final fall of resistance, but victory does not erase the command dispute: [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] later executes Xun Zhi for rivalry over merit and conflicting plans.

## Key Characteristics

- Left general commanding the land-force branch of the Joseon campaign.
- Rival commander who repeatedly seeks coordinated attack dates with Yang Pu.
- Accuser whose interpretation drives Yang Pu's arrest and army merger.
- Beneficiary of unified command during the campaign's final phase.
- Victorious commander later executed for destructive rivalry and command conflict.

- Command dispute: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-366-2-hanwudi-henxin-zhansha-gongsunsui-ls1izjhow_j4ubgmm0lm8ruaurox|Hanji 366-2]] says Xun Zhi blames Yang Pu's missed coordination and suspects his separate dealings.
- Unified assault: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-366-2-hanwudi-henxin-zhansha-gongsunsui-ls1izjhow_j4ubgmm0lm8ruaurox|Hanji 366-2]] says the combined army attacks more urgently and the Joseon ruling group fractures.
- Postwar punishment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-366-2-hanwudi-henxin-zhansha-gongsunsui-ls1izjhow_j4ubgmm0lm8ruaurox|Hanji 366-2]] says Han Wudi publicly executes Xun Zhi for rivalry over merit and incompatible planning.

## Qualifications

The source supports a serious command conflict but does not make Xun Zhi's accusation fully self-validating. Yang Pu's missed coordination, caution, prior defeat, and surrender channel can coexist with Xun Zhi's own status interest and aggressive command style. The exact legal wording and full record of the postwar judgment remain outside the supplied episode.

- Established Xun Zhi's profile across divided command, unified assault, victory, and postwar execution.
