# 《中国如何跳出贫困陷阱》 / How China Escaped the Poverty Trap

[[HowChinaEscapedPovertyTrap|《中国如何跳出贫困陷阱》]] enters the wiki through [[150-zhongguo-ruhe-tiaochu-pinkun-xianjing-xiandaihua-zhi-lu-you-hui-zouxiang-hefang-lgpvp04rngagkpubte2ac6i5ji3g]] as [[YuenYuenAng|洪源远]]'s book on how [[China]] began market development without first possessing the fully formal institutions usually associated with advanced markets. The episode reads it as the best micro-institutional complement to [[LiLu|李璐]]'s civilization framework and [[DanWang]]'s [[Breakneck]].

The book's source-scoped lesson is not that weak institutions are good. It is that early development may need to use the institutions, networks, incentives, and political language already available, then upgrade them as markets form. That makes the book central to [[WeakInstitutionsMarketBuilding]], [[DirectedImprovisation]], [[InformalPropertyRightsProtection]], and [[LocalGovernmentPolicyExperimentation]].
