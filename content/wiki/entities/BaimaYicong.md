# 白马义从 / Baima Yicong

白马义从 / Baima Yicong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] as the famous white-horse cavalry associated with [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]]. The episode uses the unit mainly as contrast: Gongsun Zan once had an aggressive mounted force and a reputation as a white-horse general, but later hides in [[YijingFortress|易京]] and stops campaigning.

The page is source-scoped. Hanji 998 does not reconstruct the unit's full formation or campaigns; it uses Baima Yicong as the symbol of lost initiative.
