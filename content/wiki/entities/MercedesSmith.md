## Overview
Mercedes Smith is a Utah Symphony principal flutist and the founder of [[BlackstradConcertAttire]].

## Current Profile
Smith turns two decades of firsthand concert-attire frustration into a specialized apparel business. Her professional standing supplies product knowledge and credibility, but she remains responsible for design, advertising creative, and social media while performing full time. The source therefore frames selective focus and additional operating capacity as more important than simply accelerating every product revision.

## Key Characteristics
- Has deep user and professional context in orchestral performance clothing.
- Built an early-revenue company while maintaining a full-time music career.
- Retains central responsibility for design and marketing work.
- Has already delegated fulfillment, paid advertising, and bookkeeping to outside providers.
- Seeks a balance between product quality, lower returns, and faster launches.
