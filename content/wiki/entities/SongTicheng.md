# 宋剔成 / Song Ticheng

宋剔成 / Song Ticheng appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-2-lmnqfjmydygqnfec6xhzpixralvi]] as the son and successor of [[SongPigong|宋辟公]] after Song Pi Gong dies. The episode gives this as the final succession notice in its Zhou Lie Wang sixth-year sequence.

His source role is chronological. He extends the [[SongState|宋国]] ruler branch already tied to [[ShangRemnantStateLegitimacy|Shang-remnant legitimacy]], but the source does not develop a separate political narrative for his reign.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] gives his reign an endpoint in the 329 BCE coup notice. His younger brother [[SongJunYan|宋君偃]] overthrows him, and Song Ticheng flees to [[QiState|齐国]]. The source turns him from a compact succession name into a displaced ruler in Song's Warring States instability branch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-68-jingtan-songkangwang-huangyinwudu-de-gouxue-lishi-2-lkn073gbtpmg11hcrcm8h6uvawzt]] adds a policy contrast. Song Ticheng is described as also having come to power through rebellion, but preferring survival through deference to stronger states, including expensive annual offerings to [[QiState|齐国]]. The episode uses him as the foil for [[SongJunYan|宋康王 / 宋君偃]]: one brother tries dependence and diplomacy, while the other chooses forceful self-strengthening and expansion.
