# Memnon of Rhodes

## Overview

Memnon of Rhodes appears as an experienced Persian-side commander who uses fortification, mercenaries, naval mobility, and operations in Alexander's rear to prolong resistance in western Anatolia.

## Current Profile

At [[Halicarnassus]], Memnon commands a defense built around walls, elevation, citadels, selected sorties, attacks on siege machinery, and access to the Persian fleet. Although Alexander enters the burning city, Memnon evacuates much of his force and remains able to operate among offshore islands and retake positions behind the Macedonian advance. His later death during a siege removes a commander capable of exploiting the risk created by Alexander's reduced fleet.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines land defense with Persian naval mobility.
- Uses mercenaries, fortifications, sorties, and attacks on siege equipment.
- Avoids total destruction by evacuating troops from Halicarnassus.
- Threatens Alexander's rear after most of the Macedonian fleet is disbanded.
- Shows that Alexander's coastal gains do not immediately eliminate Persian resistance.

## Evidence

- Halicarnassus defense: [[dchha74-mania-for-subjugation-iv-dchha74-mania-for-subjugation-iv]] attributes the contested siege and evacuation to Memnon's command.
- Rear operations: [[dchha74-mania-for-subjugation-iv-dchha74-mania-for-subjugation-iv]] records island operations and recovered positions before his death.
