# Burning Glass Institute

## Overview
Burning Glass Institute appears in [[working-through-it-the-ai-jobs-boom-dawns-6aa27a3fa6e9aef4cc7741b8]] as the labor-market organization behind an estimate that about 1% of U.S. professional jobs can now be classified as AI jobs.

## Current Profile
The source uses Burning Glass Institute as supporting evidence for the professional-job side of the AI boom. Its estimate is presented as roughly corresponding to 1 million AI jobs in the United States.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides labor-market classification evidence in the episode's AI jobs segment.
- Helps separate AI-specific professional work from broader professional employment.
- Supports the source's claim that AI creates new occupational demand as well as displacement anxiety.

## Evidence
- [[working-through-it-the-ai-jobs-boom-dawns-6aa27a3fa6e9aef4cc7741b8]] cites [[BurningGlassInstitute]] through Gad Levanon's estimate that around 1% of U.S. professional jobs can be classified as AI jobs.
- [[working-through-it-the-ai-jobs-boom-dawns-6aa27a3fa6e9aef4cc7741b8]] uses that estimate alongside The Economist's counterfactual job-creation calculation.

## Qualifications
The source gives the estimate but not the full classification method. The entity page should therefore treat the figure as source-scoped support, not as a complete labor-market taxonomy.
