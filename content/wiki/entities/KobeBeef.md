# Kobe Beef / 神户牛肉

Kobe Beef is the regional [[Wagyu|wagyu]] brand highlighted in [[kafeidou-he-niu-ziyou-cheng-zizhu-canting-maidian-guijia-guanghuan-cong-he-er-lai-1004978054]]. The source presents it as the most famous example of Japan's many regional wagyu brands, built from origin, feeding and production standards, quality gates, and the need to differentiate beef that may otherwise share breed and grade labels.

The episode also uses Kobe Beef to explain how overseas premium perception can outrun precise category knowledge. In the U.S. market, the source says Kobe Beef was initially better known than wagyu itself, so restaurants and media often used Kobe-like language to make fatty Japanese-style beef legible as a luxury ingredient.
