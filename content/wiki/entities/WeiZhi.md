# 魏挚 / Wei Zhi

魏挚 / Wei Zhi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl|Zhouji 09]] as [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]]'s brother and the Wei figure stationed in [[ZhongshanState|中山国]] after Wei's earlier conquest. The source stresses the logistical awkwardness: Zhongshan was a detached territory separated from [[WeiState|魏国]] by [[ZhaoState|赵国]].

Wei Zhi's role is to make the frontier-control problem concrete. When Di forces drive him out, Zhongshan revives, showing how a powerful state can still lose a distant holding when war pressure pulls attention elsewhere.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-4-lhjz-m12pedkfszxyzgqtjnpsn3p]] adds an earlier succession-competition context. After [[ZhaoCangtang|赵苍唐]]'s mission helps the future [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]] return from [[ZhongshanState|中山国]] and regain heir status, the source says the brother is sent to Zhongshan instead. The transcript spelling appears as 魏智, so this page treats the connection to existing [[WeiZhi|魏挚]] source-scopingly.
