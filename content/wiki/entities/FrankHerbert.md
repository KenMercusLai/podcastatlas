## Overview

Frank Herbert is the author of [[Dune]], represented here through the episode's historical reading of its imperial, Islamic, ecological-resource, and mythic layers.

## Current Profile

The source treats Herbert as a builder of future history from several pasts at once. Dune's aristocratic houses and galactic empire coexist with a desert resource monopoly, Islamic vocabulary and historical echoes, a prophetic conqueror, and an Arthurian destiny pattern.

## Key Characteristics

- Science-fiction author using layered historical rather than single-source worldbuilding.
- Creator of a desert empire organized around control of spice.
- Writer whose Paul Atreides combines prophetic, imperial, Islamic-history, and Arthurian echoes.

## Evidence

- Layered worldbuilding: [[412-romans-in-space-star-wars-dune-and-beyond-glt3832078172]] connects Dune to aristocratic competition, oil-age resource politics, Islamic history, and Arthurian myth.
- Charismatic conquest: [[412-romans-in-space-star-wars-dune-and-beyond-glt3832078172]] reads Paul Atreides / Muad'Dib as a prophetic leader whose jihad consumes the Galactic Empire.
