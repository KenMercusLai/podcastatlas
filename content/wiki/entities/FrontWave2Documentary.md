# 《前浪2》 / Front Wave 2 Documentary

## Overview
《前浪2》 is the aging documentary discussed by [[BuHeShiYi|不合时宜]] in the source episode and directed by [[FanShiguang|范世广]]'s team.

## Current Profile
The documentary is presented as a long-observation record of contemporary older adults in China. Its cases include appointed guardianship, solitary elders, non-kin caregiving, late-life companionship, dementia-like decline, elder financial vulnerability, family responsibility, and the practical work of a Shanghai notary. The source stresses that the series gains force by refusing a simple victim/rescuer/villain structure and by allowing older people, children, carers, and institutions to appear in morally mixed situations.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses several concrete elder-life samples rather than a single representative type.
- Connects aging to law, property, kinship, intimacy, care labor, fraud risk, and urban governance.
- Makes [[LiChenyangNotary|李晨阳]]'s notary practice a way to observe new institutional responses to family thinning.
- Treats older adults as subjects with desire, stubbornness, fear, humor, consent, and contradiction.
- Relies on [[DocumentaryEthicalRestraint]] by abandoning stories when the team cannot responsibly interpret the subject's inner world.

- Guardianship and law - [[women-ruhe-laoqu-cong-qianlang2-kuijian-zhongguo-laonian-shenghuo-de-zhenshi-tujing-lkybur9dgim4vxxyjuq1eav4i-up]] foregrounds appointed-guardianship notarization, estate execution, legal-heir search, and undocumented wishes.
- Intimacy and care - [[women-ruhe-laoqu-cong-qianlang2-kuijian-zhongguo-laonian-shenghuo-de-zhenshi-tujing-lkybur9dgim4vxxyjuq1eav4i-up]] discusses Li Kangmei and Lao Li's long companionship, as well as the cancer patient's relationship with a non-kin caregiver.
