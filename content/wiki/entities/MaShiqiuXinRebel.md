## Overview

Ma Shiqiu / 马士求 is a Julu man in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-657-wangmang-fei-taizi-you-duo-erxi-lklx1rd39kgz-by4tbcpaagx4z-l|Hanji 657]] who plots to raise Yan and Zhao forces against [[WangMang|王莽]]. His failed conspiracy becomes evidence for late-[[XinDynasty|新朝]] local resistance and mass punitive response.

## Current Profile

The source gives Ma Shiqiu a single but revealing role. His plan shows that anti-Xin resistance is not limited to already established rebel movements; local actors also imagine regional mobilization against Wang Mang. Once Wang Dan reports the plot, Wang Mang sends high officials to arrest people across commanderies and kingdoms, and more than a thousand implicated people are killed.

## Key Characteristics

- Julu plotter against Wang Mang.
- Local-resistance signal in the late-Xin crisis field.
- Figure whose failed conspiracy triggers broad investigation and mass execution.
