# 许渊冲 / Xu Yuanchong

## Overview
许渊冲 / Xu Yuanchong is the translator invoked near the episode’s conclusion as an example of treating the work itself, rather than fame, as a lifelong goal.

## Current Profile
The source presents Xu through a single ethical contrast: he translated day after day because translation was the practice through which he pursued value, with recognition emerging from rather than substituting for the work. The episode uses that image to argue that ideals become livable when attached to a concrete repeated activity. It does not provide an independent account of Xu’s translations, theory, career, or historical reception.

## Key Characteristics
- Translator used as an example of lifelong concrete practice.
- Figure for work-centered rather than fame-centered motivation.
- Source of the episode’s distinction between declaring a way and living it.

## Evidence
- Long-practice example: [[ep179-womende-neixin-dou-zhuzhe-yige-liuxiaoyang-zuoziji-shi-yao-fuchu-juda-daijia-ckwriaset1ykabaaaadc008h]] presents translation as the activity through which Xu answered how to live a non-mediocre life.
- Goal-ordering comparison: [[ep179-womende-neixin-dou-zhuzhe-yige-liuxiaoyang-zuoziji-shi-yao-fuchu-juda-daijia-ckwriaset1ykabaaaadc008h]] contrasts doing valued work with using work mainly as a route to fame.

## Qualifications
The source offers an inspirational vignette rather than a full scholarly profile. It does not independently evaluate Xu Yuanchong’s methods, translations, reputation, private motives, or working conditions.
