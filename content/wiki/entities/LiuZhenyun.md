# 刘震云 / Liu Zhenyun

## Overview
刘震云 / Liu Zhenyun is represented through a long [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] interview as a novelist who looks for philosophy in ordinary speech, county-town relationships, private shame, and small injuries that public accounts often dismiss.

## Current Profile
Liu's account joins literary method to biography. Poverty, his illiterate grandmother's decision to fund school, military service in the Gobi, mathematics study, the restored college entrance examination, Peking University, repeated rejection, and an editor's early support all become evidence against a simple self-made-success story. Effort matters, but so do historical openings, material conditions, mentors, and paths not taken.

His current creative standard is non-repetition. [[XianDeWanXiao|《闲得玩笑》]] must be new in direction rather than merely newly printed, and an older work's defect can become the entrance to the next work. He favors restrained, readable prose without treating ornament or popular storytelling as inherently shallow. Dialogue, structure, character relations, adaptation, and translation matter insofar as they reveal the reasons carried by life.

## Key Characteristics
- Novelist who treats [[LiteratureAsEverydayPhilosophy|everyday philosophy]] as fiction's underlying work.
- Observer of ordinary people whose speech, pain, dignity, and imaginative oddity resist reduction to social type.
- Writer who joins humor to suffering without assuming that comic expression cancels harm.
- Creator who treats self-recognized formal weakness as material for renewal.
- Reader-facing author who frames intelligibility and attention as ethical responsibilities.
- Source-scoped autobiographical narrator whose memories are evidence for his self-understanding, not a complete independent biography.

## Qualifications
The bounded evidence is a single long promotional interview centered on a new novel. Childhood memories, publication history, sales, institutional circumstances, quoted dialogue, and causal explanations remain Liu's retrospective account as summarized by the source. His full bibliography, public life, and critical reception are outside this profile.
