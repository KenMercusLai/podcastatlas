# 孙观 / Sun Guan (late Han)

孙观 / Sun Guan (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1026-jiemi-sanguo-di-yi-mengjiang-lvbu-de-luomu-lhjf-agujuliglqchumtff3tftxg1|Hanji 1026]] as one of the local armed actors whom [[ZhangBaLateHan|张霸]] persuades to submit to [[CaoCao|曹操]] after [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s defeat. The source later connects the Taishan-area troops to pressure on [[YuanTan|袁谭]] in [[QingzhouLateHan|青州]].

Sun Guan's source role is therefore not only surrender but strategic depth. Bringing local force leaders into Cao Cao's orbit helps turn the eastern settlement into an asset before the [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and [[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] horizon.
