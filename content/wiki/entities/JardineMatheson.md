# Jardine Matheson / 怡和

Jardine Matheson / 怡和 is the controlling commercial system behind [[HongkongLand|香港置地]] in [[vol-269-xiao-lishi-bu-yao-pa-shi-jishu-xing-tiaozheng-1004793119]]. The episode explicitly notes Jardine's early opium-trade history as an unflattering origin, then follows its later role in Hong Kong property, finance, retail, and conglomerate control.

For this source, Jardine matters because it gave Hongkong Land a stronger control base than [[DairyFarmInternational|牛奶公司]] had. The episode contrasts the Keswick-family-centered Jardine/Hongkong Land structure with Dairy Farm's scattered shareholders and interlocked colonial board, turning the takeover into a case of [[ConglomerateControlInColonialHongKong]].
