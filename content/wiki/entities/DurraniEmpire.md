# Durrani Empire

## Overview
The Durrani Empire was the eighteenth-century Pashtun-led polity founded by [[AhmadShahDurrani|Ahmad Shah Durrani]] and presented by the source as a major precursor to modern [[Afghanistan]].

## Current Profile
[[87-afghanistan-part-1-glt7464087190]] describes a realm formed after Nadir Shah's assassination in 1747 through Ahmad Shah's access to treasure and his consolidation of support among regional power-holders. It extended beyond the borders of modern Afghanistan into substantial parts of what is now [[Pakistan]].

The polity is best understood here as one stage in a longer sequence of imperial and regional formations. Its later territorial losses to the Sikh Empire helped create the setting in which Persian, Russian, and British interests converged in the [[GreatGame|Great Game]].

## Key Characteristics
- Pashtun-led imperial polity founded in 1747.
- Larger than the later Afghan nation-state.
- Linked Kandahar-centered power to territories toward the Indian subcontinent.
- Depended on coalition-building among regional elites and armed power-holders.
- Historical bridge between earlier Persian-Mughal systems and later Afghan state formation.

## Evidence
- Foundation: [[87-afghanistan-part-1-glt7464087190]] connects Ahmad Shah's post-1747 consolidation to the creation of the Durrani realm.
- Territorial scale: [[87-afghanistan-part-1-glt7464087190]] states that the realm included a large part of present-day Pakistan.
- Later contraction: [[87-afghanistan-part-1-glt7464087190]] links losses to the Sikh Empire with the geopolitical setting of the Great Game.

## Qualifications
The source calls this an early Afghan polity but does not provide a full institutional history or settle whether "empire," "kingdom," or another label best fits every phase. Modern borders and national identities should not be projected backward unchanged.

## What Changed
- Added the Durrani polity as the state-formation bridge between earlier empires and modern Afghanistan.

## Relationships
- [[AhmadShahDurrani]] - founder and namesake.
- [[Afghanistan]] - modern state for which the polity is an important precursor.
- [[MughalEmpire]] - neighboring imperial order whose decline opened space for Durrani expansion.
- [[GreatGame]] - later geopolitical rivalry emerging after regional territorial realignment.
