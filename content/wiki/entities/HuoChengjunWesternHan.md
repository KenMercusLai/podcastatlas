## Overview

霍成君 / Huo Chengjun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-424-huoguang-qushi-hou-huojia-shi-ruhe-fumie-de-ltisydawuqdhqpffdjv_xkwts90p|Hanji 424]] as [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]]'s daughter, [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]'s empress, and a palace figure whose fall follows the Huo family's failed power retention and attempted threat to crown prince [[HanYuandi|刘奭]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Huo Chengjun as both a beneficiary and casualty of Huo household politics. Her empress position extends the family's access to the palace after Huo Guang's death, but the source places the main driving agency on her mother [[HuoXianWesternHan|霍显]], who is said to manipulate and push the family toward dangerous conduct.

After the Huo conspiracy is exposed, Xuan deposes Huo Chengjun in the eighth month, accusing her of viciousness and attempted poisoning of Liu Shi. She is moved first to Zhaotai Palace in Shanglin苑, then later to Yunlin Lodge, where the episode says she kills herself twelve years after deposition.

## Key Characteristics

- Huo-family empress whose palace position makes the household's political risk immediate.
- Succession-risk figure because the episode attaches her fall to attempted harm against Liu Shi.
- Daughter shaped by Huo Xian's ambition and Huo Guang's earlier decision to place her in the palace.
- Deposed consort whose removal marks Xuan's post-Huo consolidation.
- Tragic endpoint of the Huo family purge, ending in later forced relocation and suicide.
