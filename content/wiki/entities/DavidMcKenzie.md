# David McKenzie

David McKenzie is the [[WorldBank]] economist in [[piles-of-cash-and-a-town-of-solutions-in-kenya-nigeria-summer-school]] who suggested randomizing many [[YouWinProgram|YouWin Program]] winners after applications had been screened. The episode presents this as a design move that reduced discretion and made the program easier to evaluate.

McKenzie's role connects [[DirectEntrepreneurGrants]] to [[RandomizedControlledTrials]]. Instead of only asking whether judges could identify the best entrepreneurs, the program created comparable winner and non-winner groups that later supported job-creation estimates.
