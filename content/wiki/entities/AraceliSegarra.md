## Current Profile
The source presents Segarra as a climber whose authority comes from lived high-risk experience rather than abstract advice. She uses mountains to explain psychological honesty, preparation, acclimatization, team trust, fear, gratitude, and the difference between reaching a summit and becoming a different person through the climb.

## Key Characteristics
- Mountaineer whose formative story links childhood fear, outdoor exposure, and the feeling that mountains chose her.
- Everest expedition participant whose account includes high-altitude filming, the 1996 disaster, rescue support, recovery, and later summit completion.
- Leadership voice who treats a real team as a product of generosity, communication, empathy, belonging, and diverse perspectives.
- Risk thinker who distinguishes danger from risk and treats uncertainty as something to manage through attention, preparation, and mindset.
- AI-era humanist who argues that AI can help with the "how" but cannot supply human purpose, judgment, or the "why."
