# Han Zheng / 韩正

## Overview
Han Zheng is the [[SuduTechnology]] speaker in the wiki's embodied-AI branch. Across [[e244-duan-dao-duan-vs-shangxia-fenceng-jiqiren-lujing-zhizheng-zhengzai-zhuanxiang-fc9a3737-81a9-49cf-a7d6-530c77df836e]] and [[dang-jushen-zhineng-zoudao-shizilukou-duitan-sudu-mayi-lingbo-zibianliang-poke-sizhong-yixian-panduan-ls6w3mrjjrecuqg33yc3lbdmdzpf]], he argues that robotics progress depends on reliable manipulation, useful simulation, and layered skill execution rather than humanlike appearance or polished demos.

## Current Profile
Han's position is that open-world manipulation needs [[Structured3DRobotData]], [[Sim2Real]], hardware/software co-design, and short skills that generalize. The later four-company debate adds a commercial layer: high success rate and low post-training cost are the threshold between research demos and deployable robots.

## Key Characteristics
- Argues that scarce real robot data makes simulation and structured physical data necessary for open-world manipulation.
- Supports [[LayeredRobotArchitecture]] where upper-level models plan and lower-level skills execute reliably.
- Treats real-world data as useful but not sufficient by itself.
- Emphasizes business success requirements: high first-pass success, fast correction, object/environment generalization, and low adaptation cost.
- Is relatively optimistic that stable skills across major task classes can improve over the next one to two years.

## Qualifications
Han's claims are operator judgments grounded in Sudu's route and source-reported demos. The wiki should keep them alongside competing real-data and general-model views rather than treating simulation-led manipulation as a settled consensus.
