# Nocs Provisions

## Overview
Nocs Provisions is [[ChrisMcKleroy]]'s bootstrapped company making colorful compact binoculars, monoculars, and scopes.

## Current Profile
Nocs sells through its own site, Amazon, major outdoor retailers, and international markets. The source says it exceeded $10 million in annual sales, but its next problem is cultural rather than purely distributive: making optics feel normal at sports, festivals, and other everyday experiences. The episode leaves two possible product stories open—phone-assisted capture and sharing, or screen-light presence.

## Key Characteristics
- Compact, colorful optics intended to broaden the category's appeal.
- Bootstrapped after the founder's difficult venture-backed experience.
- Multi-channel distribution including REI and Dick's Sporting Goods in the source's account.
- Lifestyle-expansion opportunity beyond birding and hunting.
- Unresolved choice between connected content creation and analog attention.
