# 洛邑 / Luoyi

洛邑 / Luoyi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-18-zhongguo-lishishang-di-yi-li-rentoushui-lgqnequk0rxejqgihwdqxqyqnsvq]] as the Zhou "京师," or royal capital, where the lords meet in 344 BCE. The host's key point is lexical: the notice says the lords "会" at the capital, not that they "朝" the king, which becomes [[AudienceVsMeetingDiplomacy|evidence of a meeting not centered on royal audience]].

The place also matters for the following year's [[Fengze|逢泽]] geography. The host infers that Fengze being west of Luoyi would better fit a Qin route that does not require troops to pass through the royal capital before the meeting.
