# 1929 / 《1929》 (Andrew Ross Sorkin)

[[NineteenTwentyNineSorkin|《1929》]] is [[AndrewRossSorkin]]'s book on the 1929 crash and early Depression period as read in [[170-1929-de-paomo-zhixia-sange-daibiao-renwu-he-tamen-zai-dangxia-zhouqi-de-yingzi-lo7uv7umozr0jrqfjzyhdbzicx1j]]. The episode says the book proceeds chronologically from 1929 into 1933 and draws heavily on telegrams, letters, newspapers, and archival material.

The episode does not use the book as a full macroeconomic explanation of the Great Depression. It treats the book as a behavioral-finance object: market euphoria, margin lending, ordinary-investor participation, institutional rescue theater, and the social shock of trusted figures failing become more important than choosing one school of Depression causation.

Its central value for the wiki is the three-person map. [[RichardWhitney]] supplies the gatekeeper credibility story, [[WilliamDurant]] supplies the entrepreneur-speculator story, and [[CharlesEMitchell]] supplies the financial-institution distribution story. Together they feed [[BubbleRoleAnalogy]], [[SpeculativeBubblePsychology]], and [[FinancialInstitutionTrustTransmission]].
