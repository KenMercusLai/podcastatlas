# Aegler

Aegler is the Swiss movement supplier in [[rolex-1]] whose miniature, accurate movements gave [[HansWilsdorf]] the technical base for early [[Rolex]] wristwatches. The source identifies the relationship as unusually long-running: Wilsdorf worked with Jean Aegler in Bienne, and Rolex eventually bought Aegler in 2004.

For the wiki, Aegler is a supplier-to-integration case. It starts as the technical partner that makes wristwatch credibility possible, then becomes part of [[VerticalIntegrationForQualityControl]] when Rolex brings movements deeper into its own manufacturing system.
