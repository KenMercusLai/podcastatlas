# White-Vented Myna in Taiwan / 白尾八哥

## Overview
[[WhiteVentedMynaTaiwan]] is an introduced myna species discussed alongside [[CommonMynaTaiwan|common myna]] in the Duanwen episode's Taiwan alien-species debate.

## Current Profile
The episode treats white-vented mynas as clearly alien to Taiwan because the bird is described as endemic to Java and Bali and as having a human-mediated introduction history in Taiwan. It is part of the public-education triad of black mynas that also includes [[CrestedMynaTaiwan|crested myna]].

The source's main point is not species-by-species blame. White-vented mynas help show how identification cards and native/alien labels can educate while also producing a crude enemy distinction if they are detached from evidence and management context.

## Key Characteristics
- Human-introduced alien bird in Taiwan.
- Commonly discussed with common myna as part of Taiwan's introduced-myna expansion.
- Used in public education as a contrast with native crested myna.

## Evidence
- Origin status: [[wailai-bage-yeying-zhibo-yu-taiwan-wailai-wuzhong-nanti-db02e7aa04c129b49b1f36f750155b42]] identifies white-vented myna as Java and Bali endemic and introduced to Taiwan.
- Education triad: [[wailai-bage-yeying-zhibo-yu-taiwan-wailai-wuzhong-nanti-db02e7aa04c129b49b1f36f750155b42]] describes public materials contrasting crested, white-vented, and common mynas.
- Management caution: [[wailai-bage-yeying-zhibo-yu-taiwan-wailai-wuzhong-nanti-db02e7aa04c129b49b1f36f750155b42]] treats the species as part of a broader introduced-myna group whose impacts need local evidence and differentiated management.
