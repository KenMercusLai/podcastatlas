# Dinora Figueroa

Dinora Figueroa appears in [[caracas-under-pressure-democracy-in-venezuela-6a7d8bbe7d85cbdf96e00822]] as the opposition-side leader in the Caracas negotiations. The source identifies her with the 2015 National Assembly, making her role part of the opposition's institutional continuity claim.

In the episode's framing, Figueroa's participation gives the talks an opposition channel but does not fully solve legitimacy because [[MariaCorinaMachado]] is absent. She therefore sits between the negotiation's practical opening and its [[OppositionLegitimacyGap]].
