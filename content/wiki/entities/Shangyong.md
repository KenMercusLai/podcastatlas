# 上庸 / Shangyong

上庸 / Shangyong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-53-qinhuiwang-yong-zhangyi-huan-dipi-lvmo2cwbafcg2gnxliuo18fewimk]] as the six-county ransom package a Chu favorite claims [[QinState|秦国]] might offer to save [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]. The source does not develop Shangyong independently; it functions as a persuasive object inside the palace backchannel.

The argument to [[ZhengXiu|郑袖]] is that Qin may send Shangyong's six counties and a beautiful woman to redeem Zhang Yi. The territory gives the warning political weight, while the woman gives it immediate court-status stakes for Zheng Xiu.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-60-zhongguo-di-yi-wei-bachi-chaozheng-de-taihou-jingshi-ta-ltazis9qo0dwvwmzxikk8mmpvosc]] changes Shangyong from possible ransom object to returned diplomatic territory. In 304 BCE, [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] and [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] meet at [[Huangji|黄棘]], renew the Qin-Chu alliance, and Qin returns Shangyong to [[ChuState|楚国]]. The same place therefore appears in two adjacent Qin-Chu episodes as a bargaining and repair object.
