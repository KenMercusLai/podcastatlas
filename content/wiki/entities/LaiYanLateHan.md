# 来艳 / Lai Yan (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-911-gudai-gongwuyuan-ye-neng-bao-fenpei-ls3pxu9xlpuulsdailx8tcoylv9e|Hanji 911]] introduces 来艳 / Lai Yan as the taichang promoted to sikong after [[ChenDanLateHan|陈耽]] is removed in the Guanghe first-year sequence under [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]. The episode places the appointment immediately after earthquake and animal-omen notices, so Lai Yan functions mainly as a senior-office turnover marker before the omen-remonstrance branch in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-912-mimi-daodi-gai-bugai-shuo-chukou-lhsgipxwc17pahc-r6jl7xdyrs2a|Hanji 912]].

The page keeps the role narrow. Lai Yan is not treated here as a driver of policy; he marks how late-Han high office moves within a field of [[NaturalDisasterPoliticalOmen|自然灾害政治征兆]], [[CelestialOmenPoliticalResponsibility|天象政治责任]], and [[CourtOfficeCapture|朝廷开府任官失序]].
