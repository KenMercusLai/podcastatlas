## Overview

苟参 / Gou Can enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-511-taihou-tianhuaban-ta-ruhe-yitian-feng-wuge-didi-wei-hou-lhoz_ubecvc8-mgfoad2pya72uht|Hanji 511]] as [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s half-brother, born after her mother Li remarried [[GouBinWesternHan|苟宾]].

## Current Profile

Gou Can's importance is as a boundary case. Wang Zhengjun wants [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] to make him marquis by analogy to Tian Fen, but Chengdi refuses the marquisate as unreasonable and instead appoints him Shizhong and Shuiheng Duwei. The episode reads this as a rare moment when Chengdi limits maternal-family expansion.

## Key Characteristics

- Half-brother of Wang Zhengjun through her mother's remarriage.
- Proposed by Wang Zhengjun for a marquisate after the Wang brothers' same-day enfeoffment.
- Receives office but not marquis rank after Chengdi refuses the precedent.
