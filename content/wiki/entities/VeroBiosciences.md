# Vero Biosciences

## Overview
Vero Biosciences is presented as a company co-founded by [[TonyWyssCoray]] to translate organ-age profiling into earlier, more individualized chronic-disease risk assessment.

## Current Profile
The source describes the Vero Compass as combining proteomic biological signatures with clinical and wearable data to identify organs showing accelerated aging, select an intervention, and retest whether the signal changes. Its proposed value is iterative prevention rather than diagnosis from one score: find an organ-level vulnerability, connect it to standard care or lifestyle action, and measure again.

The description is an intended product model supplied by a co-founder, not evidence in this record that the test is clinically validated, improves patient outcomes, or identifies the best intervention for a given organ.

## Key Characteristics
- Centers organ-specific rather than whole-body biological-age estimates.
- Combines molecular signatures with clinical and wearable data.
- Proposes repeated measurement before and after an intervention.
- Aims to tailor prevention or treatment to the organ showing elevated risk.
- Operates within a biomarker-to-outcome validation boundary.

## Qualifications
The bounded source is a public interview with a reported co-founder and supplies no independent validation cohort, calibration statistics, error rates, intervention trial, regulator assessment, pricing, availability, or patient-outcome data. An organ-age score may stratify risk without diagnosing disease or proving that lowering the score improves healthspan. Clinical decisions require established assessment beyond a commercial biological-age result.
