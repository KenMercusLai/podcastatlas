## Overview
Deloitte is a professional-services firm appearing in the bounded sources as an institutional research reference for an AI-insurance market forecast and a workplace study of identity covering.

## Current Profile
Across the bounded sources, Deloitte functions as a producer or collaborator behind cited estimates rather than as the principal operating subject. A Marketplace Tech episode cites its projection that global AI insurance could approach $5 billion by 2032. [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b9a08666432cea3e0a417d731ebdaf41-104957641-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b9a08666432cea3e0a417d731ebdaf41-104957641|Dropping the Mask]] cites a Deloitte collaboration reporting widespread [[Covering|covering]] and substantial self-reported harm to respondents' sense of self.

Neither source supplies the underlying report, method, questionnaire, category definitions, or uncertainty analysis. Deloitte's role is therefore evidentiary and source-scoped: the figures frame emerging issues but do not independently settle market size, causal harm, or subgroup comparison.

## Key Characteristics
- Supplies a market-size forecast used to frame AI insurance as an emerging commercial category.
- Appears as a collaborator behind survey findings used to frame covering as a widespread workplace and identity experience.
- Is not presented as underwriting the AI policies, testing the agents, or acting as the principal interview subject in either source.
- Provides quantitative context while both sources omit the underlying methodology needed for independent evaluation.
