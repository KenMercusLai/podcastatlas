# 冯禅 / Feng Chan (late Han)

## Overview

Feng Chan is the imperial envoy in Hanji 891 who is sent to Hanyang to persuade remaining Qiang forces to surrender before Duan Jiong argues for attack instead.

## Current Profile

Feng Chan's wiki role is narrow but useful. He represents the court's surrender-first channel in the 169 CE Qiang campaign, while [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]] supplies the counterargument that accepting or pursuing surrender may create provisioning and false-compliance risk when the enemy is hungry and local farm labor is strained.

## Key Characteristics

- Imperial envoy sent under [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s edict to Hanyang.
- Associated with an attempted surrender/pacification channel toward remaining Qiang forces.
- Foil for Duan Jiong's tactical assessment of unreliable surrender.
- Source-scoped actor whose independent biography is not developed in the current wiki evidence.

## Evidence

- Envoy role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-891-gao-duanwei-de-muqin-shi-jia-de-dinghai-shenzhen-lmqkzsnhtiwc9wzcee869kuu57m5|Hanji 891]] says Emperor Ling sends Feng Chan to Hanyang to persuade remaining Qiang forces to put down weapons and submit.
- Strategic contrast: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-891-gao-duanwei-de-muqin-shi-jia-de-dinghai-shenzhen-lmqkzsnhtiwc9wzcee869kuu57m5|Hanji 891]] immediately contrasts the mission with Duan Jiong's argument that surrender may be false or unsustainable because of food and provisioning incentives.
- Frontier setting: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-891-gao-duanwei-de-muqin-shi-jia-de-dinghai-shenzhen-lmqkzsnhtiwc9wzcee869kuu57m5|Hanji 891]] places Feng Chan's mission in the same Hanyang/Qiang field that later Hanji 892 treats as a pacification and moral-critique case.

## Qualifications

The source gives Feng Chan as a mission actor rather than a developed official. The page should not infer his policy views beyond the surrender mission attributed to the court.
