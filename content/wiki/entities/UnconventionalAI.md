# Unconventional AI

## Overview
Unconventional AI is the AI-hardware company led by [[NaveenRao|Naveen Rao]] that is developing coupled-oscillator computing as an energy-efficient alternative substrate for AI workloads.

## Current Profile
The company works from trainable dynamical-system models toward circuits, chips, boards, and rack-scale products. Its [[UNO]] program is presented as both a simulated image generator and an early fabricated prototype, while its broader design joins sparse coupling, local state, three-dimensional integration, and time-varying physical behavior. The company targets a 1,000-fold improvement in [[IntelligencePerWatt|intelligence per watt]], but the current source does not independently establish production-scale performance or economics.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats energy and data movement as the primary architectural constraints on AI scaling.
- Uses coupled oscillators and temporal dynamics rather than standard matrix multiplication as the physical mechanism.
- Tests trainable models in simulation before translating them into circuits and systems.
- Uses sparsity to reduce quadratic connection growth and reports benefits to efficiency, training, and performance.
- Is developing a Python-facing programming layer for stochastic, time-varying elements.
- Plans a network-accessible data-center rack while acknowledging that existing models must be ported.
