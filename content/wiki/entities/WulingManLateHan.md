## Overview

武陵蛮 / Wuling Man is the southern Jingzhou frontier group that appears in Hanji 839 through a short rebellion-and-surrender notice. The episode names Zhanshan and others as rebel leaders and credits [[YingFengLateHan|应奉]], then Wuling administrator, with bringing them back into submission.

## Current Profile

The current profile is deliberately narrow. Hanji 839 does not give a full account of Wuling society or the causes of the uprising; it records the renewed rebellion and the administrative response. The branch matters because it supplies the earliest current evidence for Ying Feng as an official who can pacify a local frontier crisis before his later campaign-aide and memorial roles.

## Key Characteristics

- Southern Jingzhou frontier population represented in Hanji 839 by a rebellion notice.
- Led in the source branch by Zhanshan and others, whose details remain source-scoped.
- Re-enters submission through Ying Feng's招降 rather than through a narrated punitive campaign.
- Functions as an early local-pacification setting before later Huan-era Jingzhou unrest.

## Qualifications

The page should not be read as a full ethnic, regional, or military history of Wuling Man communities. It records only the Hanji 839 notice and leaves Zhanshan, the prior rebellion, and settlement terms source-scoped.
