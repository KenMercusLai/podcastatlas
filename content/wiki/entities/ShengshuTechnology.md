# 生数科技 / Shengshu Technology

Shengshu Technology is the AI video company discussed in [[kuai-yidian-zai-kuai-yidian-kuai-dao-shijie-neng-shishi-shengcheng-he-shengshu-keji-zhang-jintao-liao-vidu-s1-tuili-jiasu-shishi-jiaohu-shipin-lsb53bqrjojiadnlq2qe4sta-b13]] through [[ZhangJintao]]'s work on [[ViduS1]]. The source positions the company inside China's [[VideoModels]] competition, with a product direction aimed at [[RealTimeInteractiveVideoGeneration]] rather than only offline clip generation.

In this source, Shengshu's technical emphasis is not only model capability. The discussion treats [[InferenceAccelerationStack|inference acceleration]], [[StreamingVideoGeneration]], product latency, and deployment efficiency as core to making [[Vidu]] usable for live character interaction.
