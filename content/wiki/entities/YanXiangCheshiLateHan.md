## Overview

阎祥 / Yan Xiang is the Han Wuji xiaowei figure in Hanji 839 who resolves the Cheshi Hou kingdom crisis by restoring [[AluoduoCheshiLateHan|阿罗多]] after surrender. The page uses the "Cheshi" suffix because [[YanXiangLateHan|阎象 / Yan Xiang]] already names a different late-Han actor.

## Current Profile

Yan Xiang enters the wiki as a frontier official facing a choice between punitive replacement and risk-managed restoration. After [[CheshiHouKingdomLateHan|车师后王国]] king Aluoduo attacks Han屯田 forces, loses some local support, flees north, and returns, Dunhuang administrator Song Liang proposes using hostage prince Beijun as a replacement ruler.

Yan Xiang reads the problem differently. Because Aluoduo still has local backing and has access to the Northern Xiongnu side, excluding him may widen the conflict. Yan Xiang therefore meets Aluoduo directly, offers restored kingship for surrender, and turns recognition into a containment device.

## Key Characteristics

- Wuji xiaowei operating in the Western Regions frontier field.
- Reads rebel restoration as a security choice rather than as softness.
- Weighs local support and outside Xiongnu involvement before choosing a settlement.
- Uses direct, candid negotiation to make surrender compatible with royal status.
- Keeps Beijun available for settlement without forcing him into a destabilizing kingship.
