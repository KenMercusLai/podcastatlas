# Oakland, California

Oakland, California appears in [[a-trip-to-the-magic-mushroom-megachurch]] as the physical setting for [[ZydorChurch]]. The episode describes the church as hidden inside a warehouse amid auto shops and row houses, guarded by armed security, metal detection, and cash logistics that reflect both religious unconventionality and legal risk.

The place also matters because Oakland is cited as a city where mushrooms have been decriminalized or made a lower enforcement priority. That local posture supports [[LocalPsychedelicDecriminalization]], but the source stresses that it does not remove [[ControlledSubstancesAct]] exposure or broader state and federal risk.

Oakland also appears in [[tech-20260716-0716-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260716-0716-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the court setting for the California social-media youth-harm case against [[Meta]]. Together, the pages make Oakland a local anchor for national regulatory conflict rather than only a generic place name.
