# Ping An Bank / 平安银行

Ping An Bank / 平安银行 appears in [[136-yinhang-licai-hai-neng-zenme-mai-llc7n0f3g2-jrz4xkoefz1nui5lt]] as a balance-sheet cleanup example. The source says Ping An Bank recognized large non-credit asset impairment losses and cumulative write-offs related to returned wealth-management and other non-credit non-performing assets around the post-资管新规 transition.

The episode uses the case to make old [[ChineseBankWealthManagement|bank wealth-management]] risk concrete. Products that seemed smooth to customers could leave credit-like or non-standard asset losses somewhere in the banking group once implicit guarantees, fund pools, and off-balance-sheet structures were forced into cleaner treatment.

## Source Position
- Ping An Bank is a cleanup and impairment example, not a recommendation.
- The case shows why breaking implicit guarantees can surface losses that were previously hidden by product structure or accounting treatment.
- It reinforces the source's warning that [[BankWealthProductSuitability]] must look through product labels to asset quality, valuation, and institutional incentives.
