# Popeyes

## Overview
Popeyes appears in the coffee-bean episode as a U.S. fried-chicken chain expanding in China alongside burger brands. Its wiki role is to show that the renewed U.S. fast-food China push is not limited to hamburgers; chicken chains are part of the same search for growth, mall traffic, and local operating fit.

## Current Profile
The source says Popeyes opened a new Beijing Wangfujing store in April 2026 and planned further stores in Shanghai, Nanjing, Hangzhou, and other cities. It also says Popeyes' North American same-store sales declined in the first half of the year, placing the chain on both sides of the expansion logic: U.S. demand pressure and China opportunity.

Popeyes therefore functions as a category-widening example. It connects [[FastFoodDemandReset]] to China expansion, but its long-term China prospects remain dependent on price, product fit, site quality, repeat visits, and local operator execution.

## Key Characteristics
- U.S. fried-chicken chain used as a China expansion case parallel to burger brands.
- Beijing Wangfujing opening gives the source a high-visibility city-center example.
- Planned expansion beyond Beijing makes it part of a multi-city fast-food push.
- North American same-store-sales pressure links the brand to broader U.S. fast-food demand weakness.
