# Huainanzi / 《淮南子》

[[Huainanzi|《淮南子》]] is the Western Han compilation discussed in [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997]] through [[LiuAn|刘安]]'s circle. The episode presents it as a text that mixes Daoist-leaning philosophy with Confucian, yin-yang, five-phase, legalist, military, technical, astronomical, and immortal-arts material.

The source uses the text to complicate a simple superstition/science split. Its fangshu and alchemical material can be dangerous or false in immortality claims, while still preserving observations about astronomy, seasons, animals, weather, devices, and substances.

## Key Claims
- The text is treated as a synthetic knowledge archive, not a single-doctrine manual.
- Its astronomy and calendrical material show the proximity of fangshu to real observation.
- Its alchemical and technical material links the episode to [[AlchemyChemistryContinuity]].
- Its later legendary associations with Liu An show how textual production, political defeat, and immortal storytelling can overlap.
