# 郑安平 / Zheng Anping

郑安平 / Zheng Anping appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-88-ni-zenme-kandai-fenghuangnan-1-lpuxkdq-rvnifuoneq212g53w5z5]] as the Wei man who helps [[FanJu|范雎]] escape after [[WeiQiWarringStates|魏齐]]'s punishment. The episode keeps his role brief: he moves Fan Ju out of immediate danger after Fan Ju has survived by feigning death and bargaining with a guard.

His wiki role is intermediary rather than independent strategist. Zheng Anping makes Fan Ju's later Qin career possible by converting a near-execution into flight, concealment under the alias 张禄, and eventual discovery by [[WangJiQin|王稽 / 王继]].
