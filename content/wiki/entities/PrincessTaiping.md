## Overview

Princess Taiping enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-536-3-jiemi-shangguan-waner-he-ta-beihou-de-nanrenmen-lqj7rpwwfllogrxrbav-xqwwnnnm|Hanji 536-3]] as the Li-Tang royal contact through whom [[ShangguanWaner|上官婉儿]] seeks a fallback after the [[WuSansi|武三思]] and [[EmpressWeiTang|韦后]] alignment becomes dangerous. She also appears as the figure who honors Shangguan Wan'er after death.

## Current Profile

The episode says that after surviving [[LiChongjunTang|李重俊]]'s failed coup, Shangguan Wan'er feared the next political upheaval might kill her. She therefore distanced herself from Empress Wei and drew closer to Li-Tang royal figures, especially Princess Taiping. After [[TangZhongzong|唐中宗]]'s death, Shangguan Wan'er believed an edict drafted with Princess Taiping and including [[LiDanTang|李旦]] could prove her political stance to [[LiLongjiTang|李隆基]], but the document did not save her.

An earlier, highly compressed family-and-favorite narrative presents Taiping as using intimate access politically. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-plus-pan-dian-shi-shang-de-hao-se-nv-ren-men-lgsf8_asfrictdmsptsyfizykpon|Hanji 616 plus]] says she blamed [[XueHuaiyi|薛怀义]] for the death of her husband Xue Shao and introduced [[ZhangYizhi|张易之]] to [[WuZetian|武则天]] to displace him. The profile retains this as a source-scoped account, not settled proof of causation or motive.

## Key Characteristics

- Li-Tang royal figure used by Shangguan Wan'er as a fallback route after Wu-Wei politics become risky.
- Connected with the 710 edict that placed Li Dan in the power core.
- Represents a possible bridge from Shangguan Wan'er to the restored Li royal line.
- Unable to protect Shangguan Wan'er from Li Longji's decision during the Tanglong Coup.
- Honors Shangguan Wan'er after death by arranging burial and preserving her writings in the episode's account.
- Widow linked by Hanji 616 plus to a disputed revenge narrative after Xue Shao's death.
- Presented as strategically introducing Zhang Yizhi into Wu Zetian's favorite network.

## Qualifications

This page is limited to Princess Taiping's roles in the two supplied episodes. It does not cover her full political career, later conflict with Li Longji, or broader Tang princess politics. Hanji 616 plus's claim that Xue Huaiyi caused Xue Shao's death, its revenge motive, and its route of introduction for Zhang Yizhi remain disputed or compressed rather than settled biography.
