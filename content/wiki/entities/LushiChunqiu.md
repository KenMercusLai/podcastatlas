# 《吕氏春秋》 / Lushi Chunqiu

《吕氏春秋》 / Lushi Chunqiu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-23-weihuiwang-hui-buyong-gongshu-zhi-yan-lveaqklo7oncmqudgmdk7l03qhsf]] as the text used to criticize [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] after the capture of [[GongziAng|公子卬 / 公子昂]]. The episode says the criticism centers on two problems: the parley trap cannot be repeated, and it damages Shang Yang's personal trustworthiness.

The page is source-scoped. Its role here is not to summarize the whole work, but to preserve a source tradition that reads Shang Yang's victory through [[TacticalCredibilityCost|战术信用成本]] rather than only through battlefield success.
