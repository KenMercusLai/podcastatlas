# 西南联大

西南联大 enters the wiki through [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] as both a wartime education institution and a fragile memory site. The episode discusses [[LiangSicheng|梁思成]] and [[LinHuiyin|林徽因]]'s architectural plans for the university, then contrasts those ideals with the poor materials and tin-roofed classrooms that made rain itself interrupt teaching.

The source also narrates ZhangQuan's later visits to Yunnan and Beijing, where students often did not know the memorials or offices tied to the university. This makes 西南联大 part of the episode's concern that major intellectual history can be forgotten in ordinary campus life.

## Key Claims
- 西南联大 is a key example of [[ScholarshipInWartime]]: teaching and research continue in improvised conditions.
- Its remembered and forgotten memorials show that institutional history needs active preservation.
- The episode uses its architecture and classroom conditions to make wartime scholarly constraint material.
