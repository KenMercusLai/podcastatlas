# Official IRA

The Official IRA appears in [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] as the older republican organization that remained after the December 1969 split which produced the [[ProvisionalIRA]]. The episode ties the split to frustration with the post-border-campaign leadership's turn away from guerrilla violence and toward Marxist politics under figures such as [[CathalGoulding]].

The source also places the Official IRA in the [[FallsCurfew]] sequence: British troops entered the Lower Falls looking for an Official IRA arms cache and did find weapons, but the curfew and searches damaged Catholic trust in the [[BritishArmy]] and helped the Provisionals rather than isolating them.
