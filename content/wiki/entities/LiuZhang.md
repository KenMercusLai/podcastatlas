# 刘璋 / Liu Zhang

## Overview
刘璋 / Liu Zhang is [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s surviving son and successor in [[YizhouLateHan|益州]]. The wiki's current synthesis treats him as a provincial ruler whose legitimacy is pragmatically accepted, but whose regime repeatedly exposes integration and trust failures among inherited Dongzhou forces, local elites, old Liu-family allies, and capable Ba-region figures.

## Current Profile
Before succession, Liu Zhang is sent from the court side toward Yi Province to persuade Liu Yan, but Liu Yan retains him there. After Liu Yan's other sons are dead or unavailable, Yi Province figures including [[ZhaoWeiLateHan|赵韪]] accept Liu Zhang because he seems mild and kind, and the Han court eventually recognizes him because it lacks practical leverage over the province. That recognition does not solve the regime's internal structure: Dongzhou troops, older local families, and frontier commandery elites remain difficult to bind into one order.

Hanji 1055 shows the trust problem after Zhao Wei's revolt has been suppressed. The court sends a new inspector and summons Liu Zhang, but he refuses to leave Yi Province. Inside the province, he becomes suspicious of [[PangXiLateHan|庞羲]], even though Pang Xi has old Liu-family favor and a household tie through Liu Zhang's eldest son [[LiuXunYizhouHeirLateHan|刘循]]. Liu Zhang can still reward a loyal signal, however: after [[ChengJiLateHan|程畿]] refuses Pang Xi's armed self-protection request, Liu Zhang promotes Cheng Ji to Jiangyang administrator.

## Key Characteristics
- Holds Yi Province through inherited autonomy rather than through strong central appointment control.
- Inherits a fragmented provincial order split among Dongzhou troops, local Yi Province elites, and Ba-Hanzhong frontier actors.
- Loses or alienates capable local talent, with [[GanNing|甘宁]] as the clearest early case.
- Responds to potential strongmen through suspicion, even when they have old household and political ties to the Liu family.
- Can recognize loyal office behavior when Cheng Ji refuses Pang Xi's unrighteous mobilization.

## Qualifications
Liu Zhang's "mild and kind" profile is the reason other actors accept him in Hanji 981, not a complete psychological explanation. Hanji 1055 shows suspicion toward Pang Xi, but it does not prove a settled purge policy; the branch is a trust crisis that Cheng Ji helps defuse. The page also keeps Liu Zhang's son [[LiuXunYizhouHeirLateHan|刘循]] distinct from Lujiang [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]].
