# 刘璋 / Liu Zhang

## Overview
刘璋 / Liu Zhang is [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s surviving son and successor in [[YizhouLateHan|益州]]. The wiki's current synthesis treats him as a provincial ruler whose legitimacy is pragmatically accepted, but whose mildness and weak authority fail to integrate frontier religious-military power, Dongzhou troops, local elites, old Liu-family allies, and Ba-region strongmen into one order.

## Current Profile
Before succession, Liu Zhang is sent from the court side toward Yi Province to persuade Liu Yan, but Liu Yan retains him there. After Liu Yan's other sons are dead or unavailable, Yi Province figures including [[ZhaoWeiLateHan|赵韪]] accept Liu Zhang because he seems mild and kind, and the Han court eventually recognizes him because it lacks practical leverage over the province.

Hanji 1053 shows what that recognition does not solve. [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]] breaks from Liu Zhang, kills [[ZhangXiuFivePecks|张修]], absorbs his troops, and holds [[Hanzhong|汉中]], while Liu Zhang kills Zhang Lu's mother, brothers, and family. [[PangXiLateHan|庞羲]] is sent against Zhang Lu, fails, and is then stationed in the Ba/Langzhong field to defend against Hanzhong. His private recruitment of Cong forces deepens Liu Zhang's suspicion. At the same time, Liu Yan's Dongzhou troops bully local Yi Province people, Liu Zhang cannot control them, and Zhao Wei uses local resentment to rebel with broad commandery response.

Hanji 1055 shows the trust problem after Zhao Wei's revolt has been suppressed. The court sends a new inspector and summons Liu Zhang, but he refuses to leave Yi Province. Inside the province, he remains suspicious of Pang Xi even though Pang has old Liu-family favor and a household tie through Liu Zhang's eldest son [[LiuXunYizhouHeirLateHan|刘循]]. Liu Zhang can still reward a loyal signal, however: after [[ChengJiLateHan|程畿]] refuses Pang Xi's armed self-protection request, Liu Zhang promotes Cheng Ji to Jiangyang administrator.

## Key Characteristics
- Holds Yi Province through inherited autonomy rather than through strong central appointment control.
- Inherits a fragmented provincial order split among Dongzhou troops, older local Yi Province elites, and Ba-Hanzhong frontier actors.
- Loses Hanzhong authority to Zhang Lu after the Liu Zhang-Zhang Lu rupture becomes familial and military.
- Fails to control Dongzhou troops' pressure on locals, letting resentment become a platform for Zhao Wei's rebellion.
- Responds to potential strongmen through suspicion, even when they have old household and political ties to the Liu family.
- Can recognize loyal office behavior when Cheng Ji refuses Pang Xi's unrighteous mobilization.

## Qualifications
Liu Zhang's "mild and kind" profile is the reason other actors accept him in Hanji 981, not a complete psychological explanation. Hanji 1053 and 1055 show suspicion and weak authority, but they do not make every subordinate simply disloyal: Zhang Lu, Pang Xi, Zhao Wei, Dongzhou troops, local elites, and Cheng Ji occupy different positions in a fragmented provincial order. The page also keeps Liu Zhang's son [[LiuXunYizhouHeirLateHan|刘循]] distinct from Lujiang [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]].

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1053 as the missing bridge between Liu Zhang's succession problem and Hanji 1055's later Pang Xi-Cheng Ji trust crisis.
- Added Zhang Lu's rupture, Pang Xi's earlier failed campaign and private recruitment, Dongzhou bullying, and Zhao Wei's rebellion to the current profile.
- Clarified that Liu Zhang's problem is not just personal suspicion but failure to bind inherited military, frontier, and local-elite blocs into one order.
