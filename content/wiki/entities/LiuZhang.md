# 刘璋 / Liu Zhang

## Overview
刘璋 / Liu Zhang is [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s successor in [[YizhouLateHan|益州]], holding a pragmatically accepted but fragmented provincial order whose internal integration weakness eventually constrains its external alliance choices.

## Current Profile
Before succession, Liu Zhang is sent from the court side to persuade Liu Yan, who retains him in Yizhou. After Liu Yan's other sons die or become unavailable, provincial figures accept the mild Liu Zhang and the court recognizes the outcome because it lacks practical leverage.

The inherited structure remains divided. [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]] breaks away in Hanzhong; [[PangXiLateHan|庞羲]]'s force and private recruitment produce suspicion; Dongzhou troops antagonize locals; [[ZhaoWeiLateHan|赵韪]] turns resentment into revolt; and [[GanNing|甘宁]] leaves after failed rebellion rather than becoming integrated capacity. Liu Zhang can reward [[ChengJiLateHan|程畿]]'s loyal refusal, but that case does not repair the wider fragmentation.

Hanji 1109 adds the external consequence. Liu Zhang sends [[ZhangSongLateHan|张松]] to [[CaoCao|曹操]] after Cao's Jingzhou advance. When Zhang Song returns alienated and recommends breaking with Cao Cao in favor of [[LiuBei|刘备]], Liu Zhang accepts, showing how a weakly integrated province relies heavily on emissary judgment when stronger neighboring blocs are taking shape.

## Key Characteristics
- Holds Yizhou through inherited autonomy rather than strong central appointment control.
- Inherits division among Dongzhou troops, older local elites, and Ba-Hanzhong frontier actors.
- Loses effective Hanzhong authority after the Zhang Lu rupture becomes familial and military.
- Responds to strong insiders through suspicion while struggling to restrain coercive troops.
- Can recognize loyal office behavior in individual cases without solving systemic fragmentation.
- Allows Zhang Song's failed Cao mission to redirect Yizhou toward Liu Bei.

## Qualifications
Liu Zhang's mildness is an acceptance condition, not a complete psychological explanation. Zhang Lu, Pang Xi, Zhao Wei, Dongzhou troops, local elites, Cheng Ji, Gan Ning, and Zhang Song occupy different positions in a fragmented order. Hanji 1109's alliance reversal is an added external symptom, not proof that one offended emissary alone determined Yizhou's future.

## What Changed
- Added Zhang Song's Cao mission and the resulting Liu Bei alliance recommendation.
- Connected the external pivot to the already established internal integration weakness without reducing it to one cause.
