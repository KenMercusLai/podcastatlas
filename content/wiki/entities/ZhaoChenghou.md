# 赵成侯 / Marquis Cheng of Zhao

赵成侯 / Marquis Cheng of Zhao appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] as Zhao Zhong, who succeeds after [[ZhaoJinghou|赵靖侯]] dies in 375 BCE. The source treats this as one of [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]]'s first-year notices.

The page is source-scoped. Zhao Chenghou matters here because the following fourth-year notice shows [[ZhaoState|赵国]] taking an energetic posture against [[WeiState|魏国]] soon after the succession.
