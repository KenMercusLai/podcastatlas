# Maserati

Maserati appears in [[ferrari-1]] as the related automotive brand that [[Fiat]] placed under [[Ferrari]] management. The episode frames this as a way to offer customers a family-car or adjacent luxury alternative without forcing Ferrari itself into a four-door dilution path.

For the wiki, Maserati is a brand-architecture contrast. It shows how Ferrari could protect [[LuxuryScarcityDiscipline]] by routing some demand into a sibling brand rather than expanding Ferrari's own product identity too quickly.
