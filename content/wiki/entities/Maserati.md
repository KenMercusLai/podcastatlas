# Maserati

## Overview
Maserati is represented in the wiki as both a Ferrari-adjacent luxury brand-architecture contrast and, in the newer source, a pressured premium automaker reportedly seeking Chinese intelligent-vehicle cooperation.

## Current Profile
The Ferrari source uses Maserati as a related brand that [[Fiat]] placed under [[Ferrari]] management, letting Ferrari protect scarcity by routing adjacent family-car or luxury-utility demand into a sibling brand. The newest business-news source changes the emphasis: Maserati is described as a weak-performing brand under [[Stellantis]] that may need technical and production cooperation with [[Huawei]] and [[JACGroup|JAC]] to regain relevance.

## Key Characteristics
- Functions historically as an adjacent luxury brand that could absorb demand Ferrari did not want to serve directly.
- Serves as a contrast case for [[LuxuryScarcityDiscipline]] and brand-extension limits.
- Is described in the newer source as suffering very low shipments and losses.
- Is reportedly connected to talks around Huawei's intelligent-vehicle platform and JAC manufacturing cooperation.
- May become a case where Chinese smart-vehicle systems support an older European luxury marque.

## Evidence
- Brand-architecture evidence: [[ferrari-1]] frames Maserati as a sibling or adjacent brand used to protect Ferrari from four-door dilution.
- Scarcity evidence: [[ferrari-1]] uses Maserati in the context of Ferrari's careful product expansion and later [[FerrariPurosangue]] boundary.
- Performance pressure: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says Maserati shipped fewer than 8,000 vehicles last year and lost about USD 230 million.
- Cooperation report: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says Stellantis was reported to be discussing long-term cooperation with Huawei and JAC, potentially using a dual-brand strategy with [[Maextro|尊界]] in China and Maserati internationally.

## Qualifications
The cooperation branch is reported rather than confirmed in the source. The sources do not provide Maserati's full financial statements, model plan, brand-health data, or proof that Huawei/JAC cooperation would solve its demand problem.
