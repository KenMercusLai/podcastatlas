# GrowNYC

GrowNYC is the organization that manages [[Greenmarket]] in [[a-firm-andy-what-are-new-british-pms-plans-6a5deeca76f8ee25bb4bbf9c]]. The episode says GrowNYC vets farms, decides when to create new neighborhood markets, and built a system that lets people use food welfare benefits across vendors.

In the wiki, GrowNYC is the operating layer behind [[FarmersMarketUrbanism]]. The market network's durability depends not only on good produce or neighborhood demand, but on governance, vendor standards, payment infrastructure, and relationships between farmers, shoppers, and restaurants.
