## Overview

胡武 / Hu Wu is the Taiyuan figure in Hanji 846 whose association with [[YuanZhuRemonstrantLateHan|Yuan Zhu]] and exclusion of [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] from a talent-recommendation letter lead to the destruction of more than sixty members of his household. This page is distinct from [[HuWuZhangChu|胡武]] of Zhang Chu.

## Current Profile

Hanji 846 pairs Hu Wu with [[HaoJieTaiyuanLateHan|Hao Jie]]. The two like startling public arguments, are close to Yuan Zhu, and once jointly write to senior court officials recommending talented people without sending the letter to Liang Ji. After Yuan Zhu is killed, Liang reopens that omission as a grievance and orders their arrest.

Hu Wu's case is the most severe family-punishment branch in the episode. The source says more than sixty members of his household are exterminated. In the episode's structure, Hu Wu shows how a powerholder's retaliation can expand beyond the named actor into kinship destruction.

## Key Characteristics

- Taiyuan associate of Yuan Zhu and Hao Jie.
- Participant in a talent-recommendation letter that bypasses Liang Ji.
- Victim whose case turns omission and association into lethal liability.
- Household-extermination case involving more than sixty family members.
- Disambiguated from the anti-Qin Zhang Chu official with the same written name.
