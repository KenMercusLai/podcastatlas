# Shahed 136

## Overview
Shahed 136 is the Iranian one-way attack-drone reference system used in the wiki's [[LowCostDroneWarfare]] branch. Russia's use and adaptation of Shahed-style systems connects inexpensive long-range attack capacity to decoys, commercial-component supply chains, and increasingly demanding interception.

## Current Profile
[[tech-20260319-0319-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260319-0319-mp-tech-pod-128]] establishes the cheap, componentized, good-enough model and compares the U.S. [[LucasDrone]] with it. [[drone-truth-russia-has-the-upper-hand-6aad03f98a1508074de8337e]] adds jet-powered Russian variants that fly higher and faster, shifting the defense problem toward prototype jet interceptors and greater production pressure.

## Key Characteristics
- Supports long-range one-way attack at lower cost than high-end missiles or aircraft.
- Relies substantially on commercially available or commercially derived components.
- Creates attrition and cost pressure through armed systems and decoys.
- Remains adaptable, including faster and higher-flying jet-powered variants.
- Forces defenders to combine classification, electronic warfare, and physical interception.

## Evidence
### Low-cost and componentized design
- [[tech-20260319-0319-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260319-0319-mp-tech-pod-128]] describes Shahed-style systems as good-enough long-range weapons built substantially from commercial materials, electronics, and engine lineage.

### Russian adaptation and interception pressure
- [[drone-truth-russia-has-the-upper-hand-6aad03f98a1508074de8337e]] reports jet-powered variants, higher flight profiles, faster speeds, and Ukrainian work on small jet interceptors.

## Qualifications
- “Shahed-style” covers variants and decoys that should not be treated as one fixed technical configuration.
- Production targets and fielded-system counts in the newer episode remain source-scoped estimates.

## What Changed
- Added jet-powered variants and the corresponding fast-interceptor requirement.
- Reframed Shahed as an evolving system family rather than a static reference design.
