# DeepZ

## Overview
DeepZ is an AI model/API provider represented in the wiki through short business-news segments on API pricing and domestic inference infrastructure.

## Current Profile
The current sources treat DeepZ as a company whose product economics and infrastructure buildout are linked. [[yue-mai-yue-gui-de-shanxia-yousong-men-ruhe-chixu-qiangzhan-qingshe-baodai-shichang-1008110631]] uses its August 2026 image-understanding release, size-based image token billing, and weekend off-peak discounting to show API providers shaping usage through price. [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] adds a reported plan to buy [[HuaweiAscend950DT|Huawei Ascend 950DT]] chips for a large Inner Mongolia data center, shifting DeepZ from pricing evidence into a domestic AI inference-capacity case.

## Key Characteristics
- DeepZ is presented as an AI model/API provider with an image-understanding multimodal release.
- Its image input pricing is described as size-based token billing.
- Its off-peak policy extended to full weekends beginning August 23, 2026.
- Its pricing design may encourage developers to shift nonurgent work to cheaper windows.
- It is now also tied to reported domestic AI chip procurement and data-center deployment for inference workloads.
- Its case connects API pricing strategy, chip supply, and broader [[AIInferenceCostStructure]] concerns.
