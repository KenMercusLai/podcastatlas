# World Food Programme / 世界粮食计划署

## Overview
The World Food Programme / 世界粮食计划署 appears in [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] as the [[UnitedNations]] food-aid agency where [[WangHongrui|王洪瑞]] works and through which refugee cash assistance, nutrition support, and school meals are described in [[SouthSudan|南苏丹]].

## Current Profile
In this source, WFP is both a logistics institution and a field-facing moral pressure point. Wang Hongrui first describes headquarters supply-chain work that moves wheat and flour toward receiving countries, then contrasts that with field work where refugees ask directly for food that the current program cannot fully provide.

The profile is defined by constrained delivery. WFP can provide emergency cash after [[UNHCR]] registration, fortified nutrition foods to narrowly eligible children and pregnant or postpartum women, and school-meal support, but funding cuts have already removed the fuller food basket for refugees in the described camp.

## Key Characteristics
- UN food-aid organization represented through supply-chain, partnership, field, nutrition, and school-meal work.
- Provider of emergency cash and high-energy biscuits after refugee registration in the South Sudan case.
- Operator under funding scarcity, where a reduced cash-only benefit replaces earlier food-basket assistance.
- Nutrition triage actor using child upper-arm measurement to target fortified foods toward severe malnutrition.
- Education-support actor through school meals that keep hungry children enrolled.
