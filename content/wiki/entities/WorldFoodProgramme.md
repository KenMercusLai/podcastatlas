# World Food Programme / 世界粮食计划署

## Overview
The World Food Programme / 世界粮食计划署 is the [[UnitedNations]] food-aid agency represented through refugee cash, nutrition support, school meals, field logistics, and hunger measurement in South Sudan and northeastern Nigeria.

## Current Profile
In this source, WFP is both a logistics institution and a field-facing moral pressure point. Wang Hongrui first describes headquarters supply-chain work that moves wheat and flour toward receiving countries, then contrasts that with field work where refugees ask directly for food that the current program cannot fully provide.

The profile is defined by constrained delivery. WFP can provide emergency cash after [[UNHCR]] registration, fortified nutrition foods to narrowly eligible children and pregnant or postpartum women, and school-meal support, but funding cuts have already removed the fuller food basket for refugees in the described camp.

The Nigeria episode broadens the profile from direct field delivery to regional warning. It cites WFP's estimate that malnutrition in [[BornoState]] is at its worst level in a decade, while weak harvests, insecurity, aid cuts, and high energy and food costs prevent some treated children from returning safely to adequately supplied villages.

## Key Characteristics
- UN food-aid organization represented through supply-chain, partnership, field, nutrition, and school-meal work.
- Provider of emergency cash and high-energy biscuits after refugee registration in the South Sudan case.
- Operator under funding scarcity, where a reduced cash-only benefit replaces earlier food-basket assistance.
- Nutrition triage actor using child upper-arm measurement to target fortified foods toward severe malnutrition.
- Education-support actor through school meals that keep hungry children enrolled.
- Regional evidence source connecting conflict, market pressure, aid cuts, and severe malnutrition in Borno State.

- Aid delivery sequence: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] describes WFP cash and high-energy biscuit support after UNHCR registration.
- Scarcity and program cuts: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] says refugees had received grain and a broader food basket before 2026 cuts left cash assistance at a minimum operating level.
- Nutrition and school meals: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] describes fortified nutrition food for severely malnourished children and WFP-funded school meals that coincide with sharply higher enrollment.

## Qualifications
The South Sudan episode foregrounds WFP through a staff member's experience, not an independent operational audit, while the Nigeria episode cites an aggregate estimate without supplying its underlying method. Funding gaps, program thresholds, timing warnings, and regional prevalence should remain source-scoped.

## What Changed
- Added Borno as a second case linking conflict, aid cuts, food prices, unsafe return, and severe child malnutrition.
