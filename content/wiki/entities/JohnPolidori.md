# John Polidori / 约翰·波利多里

## Overview

John Polidori was [[LordByron|Lord Byron / 拜伦]]'s young doctor and an aspiring writer whose *The Vampyre* transformed Byron's public persona into an influential [[AristocraticVampire|aristocratic vampire]].

## Current Profile

The earlier sources place Polidori inside the storm-bound 1816 [[VillaDiodati|Villa Diodati / 迪奥达蒂别墅]] gathering with Byron, [[MaryShelley|Mary Shelley / 玛丽·雪莱]], [[PercyShelley|Percy Shelley / 珀西·雪莱]], and [[ClaireClairmont|Claire Clairmont / 克莱尔·克莱尔蒙特]]. That social and climatic setting joins the origin memories of *Frankenstein* and modern vampire fiction.

The Byron episode sharpens Polidori's authorship and literary agency. His awkward subordinate relationship with Byron did not prevent him from recognizing the Gothic potential of Byron's rank, beauty, scandal, and supposed destructiveness. When the 1819 story appeared under Byron's name, misattribution fused the fictional predator with the celebrity model and obscured Polidori's authorship.

## Key Characteristics

- Physician traveling in a socially unequal and difficult relationship with Byron.
- Participant in the 1816 Geneva ghost-story circle.
- Author of *The Vampyre*, not merely recorder of Byron's fragment.
- Writer who converted celebrity persona into a durable Gothic social type.
- Figure whose authorship was obscured by publication under Byron's name.

## Evidence

- Geneva setting: [[141-zhizao-fulankensitan-kehuan-zhimu-he-tade-shenqi-shidai-857923931]] connects Polidori to the Villa Diodati creation scene and shared Frankenstein-vampire afterlife.
- Literary-biographical role: [[82-xianliao-weida-zuojia-de-bagua-di-er-dan-695228589]] identifies him as Byron's doctor and connects *The Vampyre* to the gentlemanly Dracula image.
- Authorship and misattribution: [[443-lord-byron-death-of-a-vampire-part-4-glt3247503776]] states that Polidori wrote the story and that initial attribution to Byron made it seem confessional.

## Qualifications

The sources treat Polidori mainly through his relationship to Byron and the genre afterlife of one story. His medical career, other writing, independent biography, publication history, and exact influence on later vampire texts require broader evidence.

## What Changed

- Clarified the distinction between Polidori's authorship and Byron's role as fictional model.
- Added the 1819 misattribution as a mechanism joining celebrity scandal to genre formation.

## Relationships

- [[LordByron]] - employer, difficult companion, and model for Polidori's aristocratic predator.
- [[AristocraticVampire]] - literary type Polidori helped establish through *The Vampyre*.
- [[MaryShelley]] - fellow Geneva-circle writer associated with the parallel *Frankenstein* origin story.
- [[VillaDiodati]] - gathering place for the ghost-story exchange.
- [[YearWithoutASummer]] - climatic condition shaping the indoor creation scene.
- [[LiteraryGossipAsContext]] - framework for using persona and anecdote without confusing them with authorship or complete biography.
