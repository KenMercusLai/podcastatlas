# Reveri

## Overview
Reveri is a self-hypnosis app and website presented in [[HubermanLab]] as a translation of [[DavidSpiegel]]'s Stanford research into brief guided exercises.

## Current Profile
The episode describes Reveri as a consumer route for practicing problem-focused self-hypnosis. Its exercise areas include pain, stress, focus, insomnia, eating behavior, and smoking cessation. The product follows Spiegel's broader model: narrow attention, regulate bodily state, imagine or reframe a defined problem, and preserve the user's ability to enter and leave the exercise voluntarily.

The source does not establish that an app reproduces clinician-guided treatment or that every listed use has equal evidence. Reveri therefore belongs inside the same screening and referral boundary as [[ClinicalHypnosis]]: unexplained or serious symptoms need appropriate assessment, and trauma or psychiatric problems may require a licensed professional.

The Essentials edit repeats the same module range and describes sessions from roughly fifteen minutes down to one- or two-minute refreshers. This adds delivery detail but no app-specific comparative evidence.

## Key Characteristics
- Delivers guided self-hypnosis exercises through a consumer app and website.
- Draws on Spiegel's clinical and research framework for focused attention.
- Organizes exercises around specific goals rather than undirected relaxation.
- Covers pain, stress, focus, sleep, eating behavior, and smoking cessation in the episode's account.
- Preserves a distinction between self-practice and licensed clinical care.
