# Hadi Partovi

## Overview

Hadi Partovi is a technology executive and college friend of [[RickSmith|Rick Smith]] whom Smith credits with helping [[Axon]] move from a weapons-centered hardware company toward cloud software.

## Current Profile

In the bounded source, Partovi joins Axon's board around 2010 and functions for roughly five years as a part-time technology leader. His role is presented as organizational and architectural: help recruit a software team, move fragile self-hosted infrastructure to AWS and Azure, and support renaming Taser International as Axon to match a broader product identity.

The account illustrates how a board member can supply missing technical leadership during a business-model transition. It remains Smith's retrospective attribution rather than a complete or independently corroborated history of Axon's engineering organization.

## Key Characteristics

- Longstanding personal connection to Smith preceding the board role.
- Board-level technical support during Axon's software transition.
- Help with software-team formation and cloud-infrastructure migration.
- Strategic support for changing the narrow Taser identity to Axon.
- Example of temporary executive-like contribution from a director during capability building.
