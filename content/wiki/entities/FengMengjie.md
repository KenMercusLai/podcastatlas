# 馮孟傑 / Feng Mengjie

## Overview
[[FengMengjie]] is introduced in the Duanwen episode as a birder and freelance worker whose work includes bird surveys, bird writing, drawing, and museum bird-specimen handling.

## Current Profile
Feng grounds the episode's ethical stance in practical bird knowledge. He can identify mynas as introduced or native in Taiwan's current bird discourse while also refusing to treat dislike, inconvenience, or alien status as permission for casual cruelty.

His closing position is deliberately mixed: he likes mynas, including introduced mynas, because they are intelligent and socially complex, but he also accepts that some species can create ecological, noise, feces, or crop problems. The management question is therefore not whether humans feel affection or hatred, but whether any intervention has evidence, a clear purpose, and ethical accountability.

## Key Characteristics
- Works from birder practice, bird surveys, writing, drawing, and museum-specimen handling.
- Uses field education to distinguish species status without turning introduced birds into moral enemies.
- Treats lethal or removal-based management as an accountable choice that requires knowing why a life is being taken.
