# 盖斯康公寓 / Gascoigne Apartments

## Overview
盖斯康公寓 is one of the Lai An-designed Shanghai apartment buildings used in the episode to show the modernity, hierarchy, and high-end residential organization of former French Concession housing.

## Current Profile
The episode describes Gascoigne Apartments as visually modern and almost time-displacing near Huaihai Middle Road. It also presents the building as a high-grade apartment with differentiated floors, large upper-level units, service circulation, separate domestic-worker routes, and a vehicle turnaround, making class and service structure visible in spatial design.

## Key Characteristics
- Gascoigne Apartments is treated as a representative Lai An apartment work.
- Its visual form is described as unusually modern for old Shanghai.
- Its plan reflects high-end residential hierarchy and service infrastructure.
- Its post-1945 residents included Americans and consular or diplomatic personnel.
