# 管修 / Guan Xiu (Chu)

管修 / Guan Xiu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-2-zanmen-dou-yuanwang-le-beifu-2000-duonian-heiguo-de-yegong-haolong-lh-h-gezpqtvnyq40zlqac8s-5e]] as the respected Chu figure whose death changes [[YeGong|叶公]]'s reading of the [[BaiGongShengRebellion|白公胜之乱]]. The episode says Ye Gong first treats [[BaiGongSheng|白公胜]]'s seizure of the court as accidental success, then becomes certain Bai cannot win after hearing that Bai has killed Guan Xiu.

The page is intentionally source-scoped. Guan Xiu matters here less for an independent biography than for the social signal attached to his reputation: killing someone with public standing helps Ye Gong infer that common people will not attach themselves to Bai Gong Sheng's rebellion.
