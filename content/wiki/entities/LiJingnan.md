# Li Jingnan / 李景南

## Overview
Li Jingnan / 李景南 is presented as a gastroenterology chief physician at Peking Union Medical College Hospital and the clinical expert in a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode on inflammatory bowel disease.

## Current Profile
Within the source, Li explains ulcerative colitis and Crohn's disease as chronic immune-mediated intestinal conditions rather than ordinary infectious gastroenteritis. His practical emphasis is [[InflammatoryBowelDiseaseManagement|remission-oriented long-term management]]: warning-sign recognition, qualified diagnosis, continuous medication and follow-up, nutrition and complication monitoring, timely surgery when necessary, and shared decisions that acknowledge life-quality tradeoffs.

## Key Characteristics
- Gastroenterology clinician providing the episode's main disease-mechanism and treatment explanation.
- Distinguishes public symptom awareness from online self-diagnosis.
- Frames symptom disappearance as different from durable remission or cure.
- Supports clinician-patient shared decisions around medicines, surgery, stoma, risk, and quality of life.
- Rejects unsupported remedies as substitutes for regular treatment and follow-up while distinguishing them from formal Chinese medicine.
