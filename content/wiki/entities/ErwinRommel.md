# Erwin Rommel

## Overview

Erwin Rommel was a German commander whose exceptional fame is qualified in the episode by weaknesses in logistics and operational restraint.

## Current Profile

Rommel serves as a warning that celebrated aggression and battlefield reputation do not equal complete command competence. The source criticizes his logistical judgment and his ability to recognize when an advance should pause, placing sustainment at the center of any assessment of generalship.

## Key Characteristics

- Widely remembered as a famous Second World War general.
- Associated with aggressive operational movement.
- Criticized for weak logistical judgment.
- Criticized for failing to recognize when to stop or consolidate.
