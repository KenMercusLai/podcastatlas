# San Juan de Ulua

San Juan de Ulua appears in [[693-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-englands-greatest-hero-part-3-glt5069845329]] as the Spanish port near Veracruz where [[JohnHawkins]]'s 1567-1568 expedition collapses. After weather drives the English fleet there, a Spanish attack sinks four ships, kills more than 300 sailors, and leaves 114 prisoners.

For [[FrancisDrake]], the event becomes a formative grievance. The source treats San Juan de Ulua as the hinge between attempted participation in Spanish-controlled trade and a later strategy of raiding that tests [[SpanishImperialMonopolyVulnerability]].
