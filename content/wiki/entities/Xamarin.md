# Xamarin

Xamarin appears in [[peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted-peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted]] as the customer in [[Segment]]'s first major sales negotiation with [[NatFriedman]]. The episode says the contract landed around $18,000 per year, far above Segment's original $10 monthly price.

The source uses Xamarin as a [[ProductLedWillingnessToPay]] and [[FounderLedSales]] lesson. The point is not only that the price was higher; it is that serious customers valued the removal of repeated data-integration work enough to change Peter Reinhardt's understanding of what Segment was selling.
