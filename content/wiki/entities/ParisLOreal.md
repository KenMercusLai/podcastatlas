# Paris L'Oreal / 巴黎欧莱雅

Paris L'Oreal / 巴黎欧莱雅 appears in [[lvdian-xiaoyang-yao-tuiguang-tihuanxin-de-ba-meizhuang-chongzuo-yibian-1006757937]] as part of [[LOreal|L'Oreal / 欧莱雅]]'s refill-product portfolio. The source mentions Paris L'Oreal shampoo as a mass-consumer example alongside prestige skincare, cushion cosmetics, and professional hair oil.

The brand matters because refill behavior is already more familiar in daily-chemical categories such as laundry liquid, so shampoo can sit closer to an existing refill habit than prestige makeup or skincare. The episode still treats the same adoption requirements as necessary: easy buying, visible options, clear price advantage, and a replacement experience that does not feel messy.
