# Foxconn Industrial Internet / 工业富联

## Overview
Foxconn Industrial Internet / 工业富联 is represented in the wiki as a Chinese manufacturing case for [[HardAIInfrastructure]], especially AI servers, racks, and data-center equipment.

## Current Profile
The annual-report source presents Foxconn Industrial Internet as one of the clearest Chinese company cases for AI's physical buildout: servers, modules, chips, power equipment, and energy infrastructure give manufacturing firms tangible exposure to the AI boom. The newer business-news source adds operating detail: Foxconn can assemble components into AI servers and whole racks, support modular data-center buildout, and benefit from demand that is less seasonal than smartphone consumer-electronics work.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a manufacturing-side AI infrastructure company case rather than a model or application company.
- Benefits from AI server, rack, and modular data-center demand.
- Links manufacturing capability to power, networking, cooling, and data-center deployment needs.
- Shows how AI infrastructure demand can partly offset smartphone-seasonality exposure.
- Remains a hardware-strength case, not proof of application-layer AI value capture.

## Evidence
- Annual-report frame: [[165-nianbaoji-zhong-de-zhenshi-zhongguo-2026-lpredevu-gakn92dwutmulytmslo]] presents Foxconn Industrial Internet as a Chinese hard-AI-infrastructure company case.
- Physical buildout evidence: [[165-nianbaoji-zhong-de-zhenshi-zhongguo-2026-lpredevu-gakn92dwutmulytmslo]] ties AI prosperity to servers, optical modules, chips, power equipment, batteries, and storage.
- Revenue mix evidence: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says products including AI servers exceeded half of Foxconn's revenue for the first time in the second quarter.
- Operating capability evidence: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says executives described AI server assembly, full rack assembly, and modular data-center support involving power, networking, and liquid cooling.

## Qualifications
The sources do not independently verify Foxconn's customer mix, margins, exact AI-server revenue share, or long-term competitive advantage. They support a physical-infrastructure exposure thesis, not a claim that Foxconn controls the most valuable AI software layer.
