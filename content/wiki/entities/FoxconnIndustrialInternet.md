# Foxconn Industrial Internet / 工业富联

Foxconn Industrial Internet / 工业富联 appears in [[165-nianbaoji-zhong-de-zhenshi-zhongguo-2026-lpredevu-gakn92dwutmulytmslo]] as the source's clearest [[HardAIInfrastructure]] company case. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] presents it as a manufacturer that moved from smartphone contract-manufacturing associations toward global AI server leadership.

The source uses the company to explain why Chinese markets may respond more strongly to AI's physical buildout than to model or application narratives. Servers, modules, chips, power equipment, and energy infrastructure become tangible places where Chinese manufacturing capability can participate in the AI boom, even while the episode warns that hardware strength alone may not secure application-layer value capture.
