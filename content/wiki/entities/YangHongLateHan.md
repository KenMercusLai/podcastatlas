## Overview

[[YangHongLateHan|杨洪]] is an Yi-region official presented as urging immediate reinforcement of Liu Bei's Hanzhong campaign and then stabilizing the rear administration that supports it.

## Current Profile

When [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] hesitates to draw heavily on an incompletely consolidated rear, Yang Hong argues that [[Hanzhong|汉中]] is Yi's strategic throat: losing it would endanger the province itself. Zhuge Liang accepts the advice, mobilizes troops and supplies, and appoints Yang Hong to acting local authority, where the episode credits him with maintaining government and public stability.

## Key Characteristics

- Adviser who connects a frontier position to the survival of the rear region.
- Advocate of immediate mobilization despite internal consolidation risk.
- Local administrator whose competence is used to offset the risks of troop extraction.
- Link between strategic argument, personnel assignment, and logistical delivery.

## Qualifications

This is a narrow campaign profile. The exact office, scale of mobilization, fiscal and recruitment mechanisms, public response, and causal share of Yang Hong's administration in later victory require verification beyond the episode summary.
