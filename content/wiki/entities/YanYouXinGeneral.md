## Overview

严尤 / Yan You is the Xin-side adviser whose correct warnings are repeatedly ignored in late-[[XinDynasty|新朝]] crisis. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-643-weihuo-zhengfa-xiongnu-weihe-hui-bei-bishi-lr-5-qxjm4fqlirvwhdwelo1elg5|Hanji 643]] now gives his earliest current layer: he criticizes costly anti-[[Xiongnu|匈奴]] precedent through a Zhou-Han-Qin comparison and warns that [[WangMang|王莽]]'s planned mass expedition faces severe logistics, climate, disease, tempo, and terrain risk. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-645-bidu-zhichang-quanmou-mingzhebaoshen-or-niliu-ershang-lvdqcakza0zqextrhympnpswxyjy|Hanji 645]] adds his Goguryeo frontier warning: coercing and blaming [[GoguryeoLateHan|Goguryeo]] can turn a limited problem into wider revolt. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-655-wanzhong-guixin-dao-minyuan-feiteng-wangmang-zuocuo-le-na-yibu-lseopbzgmas0hyjrliikzgp5oxko|Hanji 655]] adds his pre-Kunyang policy layer: he warns Wang Mang not to sacrifice a useful Xiongnu information position and later argues that Shandong rebels should take priority over Xiongnu. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-664-kunyang-zhizhan-jueding-guoyun-de-lishi-zhuanzhedian-lihrwawp4x1w1pn4aibdohq4-l-4|Hanji 664]] then shows his Kunyang battlefield warnings being rejected by [[WangYiXinGeneral|王邑]].

## Current Profile

Yan You's earliest current layer is cost-based frontier strategy rather than battlefield tactics. In Hanji 643 he ranks Zhou's limited expulsion of Xianyun as middle strategy, Han Wudi's deep anti-Xiongnu offensives as lower strategy, and Qin's Great Wall exhaustion as no strategy. The ranking makes him a source case for [[FrontierStrategyCostTypology|frontier strategy cost typology]]: visible military achievement can still be strategically inferior if objective, expense, and long-run state capacity do not fit.

The same source makes Yan You a concrete logistics critic. He argues that Wang Mang's planned 300,000-man expedition faces delayed assembly, morale decay, frontier supply shortage, interior transport gaps, transport-animal death risk, cold and wind baggage, disease from dry rations, slower movement, inability to catch a withdrawing enemy, and danger to long columns in difficult terrain. His alternative is to use the troops already at the frontier for a rapid strike if force must be used. Wang Mang rejects that advice and continues the original transport plan.

In Hanji 645 he argues that Goguryeo's disorder is not necessarily led by its ruler and should be handled by appeasement first; otherwise pressure may produce full rebellion and encourage Buyeo and related groups to respond. In Hanji 655 he argues that Xu Bu Dang's frontier position still gives Xin useful Xiongnu intelligence, and that bringing him to Chang'an will turn him into an ordinary Hu dependent while losing the information channel. He also opposes treating the Xiongnu campaign as the main emergency when Shandong rebellion is already the more immediate danger. Wang Mang dismisses him, making Yan You an early case of correct advice becoming politically unacceptable.

Yan You later advises Wang Yi to bypass Kunyang and move quickly toward Wan because [[LiuXuanGengshiEmperor|刘玄]] is there. In the episode's framing, this would have attacked the political center of the Gengshi field rather than spending time on a smaller city.

When Wang Yi chooses siege instead, Yan You gives a second warning. A besieged force without any exit will not surrender easily; leaving a passage can redirect enemy incentives toward flight and survival. Wang Yi again refuses, so Yan You becomes the defeated side's internal evidence that the disaster was not caused by ignorance alone.

## Key Characteristics

- Xin-side strategist whose earliest current advice judges frontier policy by objective, cost, logistics, and state capacity.
- Anti-Xiongnu expedition critic who identifies assembly delay, supply, transport animals, climate, disease, march speed, and terrain exposure as strategic risks.
- Later Kunyang adviser whose advice identifies the enemy's political center at Wan.
- Frontier and policy-priority adviser who warns against coercive Goguryeo handling, lost Xiongnu intelligence, and frontier escalation before Shandong rebellion.
- Warns against letting spectacle, reputation, or large-plan inertia override operational priority.
- Applies the "围师必阙" logic that a siege should leave an escape path.
- Functions as an ignored-adviser contrast to both Wang Mang's crisis sequencing and Wang Yi's command rigidity.

## Qualifications

The current evidence does not prove that Yan You had a complete winning strategy, only that the source notes preserve warnings that fit later defeat conditions. Hanji 643 supports his strategic-cost and logistics critique but keeps the Zhou-Xianyun comparison, Luanti-family actors, exact force counts, and the counterfactual value of a rapid strike source-scoped. Hanji 645 supports his Goguryeo appeasement warning but keeps the ruler Zhou, Buyeo link, and local command details source-scoped. Hanji 655 supports his Xiongnu intelligence and Shandong-priority warnings but keeps Xu Bu Dang's full identity, exact offices, and wider Xiongnu strategy source-scoped. Hanji 664 supports the Kunyang advice chain but not a full counterfactual victory path.
