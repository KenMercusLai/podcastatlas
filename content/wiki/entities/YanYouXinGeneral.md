## Overview

严尤 / Yan You is the Xin-side adviser whose correct warnings are repeatedly ignored in late-[[XinDynasty|新朝]] crisis. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-645-bidu-zhichang-quanmou-mingzhebaoshen-or-niliu-ershang-lvdqcakza0zqextrhympnpswxyjy|Hanji 645]] now gives his earliest current layer: he warns that coercing and blaming [[GoguryeoLateHan|Goguryeo]] can turn a limited frontier problem into wider revolt. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-655-wanzhong-guixin-dao-minyuan-feiteng-wangmang-zuocuo-le-na-yibu-lseopbzgmas0hyjrliikzgp5oxko|Hanji 655]] adds his pre-Kunyang policy layer: he warns [[WangMang|王莽]] not to sacrifice a useful Xiongnu information position and later argues that Shandong rebels should take priority over [[Xiongnu|匈奴]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-664-kunyang-zhizhan-jueding-guoyun-de-lishi-zhuanzhedian-lihrwawp4x1w1pn4aibdohq4-l-4|Hanji 664]] then shows his Kunyang battlefield warnings being rejected by [[WangYiXinGeneral|王邑]].

## Current Profile

Yan You's earliest current layer is frontier de-escalation rather than battlefield tactics. In Hanji 645 he argues that Goguryeo's disorder is not necessarily led by its ruler and should be handled by appeasement first; otherwise pressure may produce full rebellion and encourage Buyeo and related groups to respond. In Hanji 655 he argues that Xu Bu Dang's frontier position still gives Xin useful Xiongnu intelligence, and that bringing him to Chang'an will turn him into an ordinary Hu dependent while losing the information channel. He also opposes treating the Xiongnu campaign as the main emergency when Shandong rebellion is already the more immediate danger. Wang Mang dismisses him, making Yan You an early case of correct advice becoming politically unacceptable.

Yan You later advises Wang Yi to bypass Kunyang and move quickly toward Wan because [[LiuXuanGengshiEmperor|刘玄]] is there. In the episode's framing, this would have attacked the political center of the Gengshi field rather than spending time on a smaller city.

When Wang Yi chooses siege instead, Yan You gives a second warning. A besieged force without any exit will not surrender easily; leaving a passage can redirect enemy incentives toward flight and survival. Wang Yi again refuses, so Yan You becomes the defeated side's internal evidence that the disaster was not caused by ignorance alone.

## Key Characteristics

- Xin-side strategist at Kunyang whose advice identifies the enemy's political center at Wan.
- Earlier frontier and policy-priority adviser who warns against coercive Goguryeo handling, lost Xiongnu intelligence, and frontier escalation before Shandong rebellion.
- Warns against letting reputation concerns override operational priority.
- Applies the "围师必阙" logic that a siege should leave an escape path.
- Functions as an ignored-adviser contrast to both Wang Mang's crisis sequencing and Wang Yi's command rigidity.
- Survives the rout, making him a witness to a failed advice chain rather than a battle winner.

## Qualifications

The current evidence does not prove that Yan You had a complete winning strategy, only that the source notes preserve warnings that fit later defeat conditions. Hanji 645 supports his Goguryeo appeasement warning but keeps the ruler Zhou, Buyeo link, and local command details source-scoped. Hanji 655 supports his Xiongnu intelligence and Shandong-priority warnings but keeps Xu Bu Dang's full identity, exact offices, and wider Xiongnu strategy source-scoped. Hanji 664 supports the Kunyang advice chain but not a full counterfactual victory path.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 645 as Yan You's earliest current frontier-warning layer around Goguryeo coercion.
- Added Hanji 655 as Yan You's earlier policy-priority and Xiongnu-intelligence warning layer before the Kunyang defeat.
- Preserved Hanji 664 as the later Wan-first and siege-exit warning layer.
