# 魏郡 / Wei Commandery (Late Han)

魏郡 / Wei Commandery (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-970-yuanshao-ansha-lvbu-weihe-hui-shibai-ln2pnvit-qgpnhn-pne7o-p1irrs|Hanji 970]] as the commandery whose soldiers revolt while [[YuanShao|袁绍]] is at Boluojin. The episode says the Wei commandery troops join [[YuDuLateHan|余毒]]'s [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]] forces, attack [[YeLateHan|邺城]], and kill the Wei commandery administrator.

The page's current value is administrative and military. Wei Commandery is not developed as a full regional history here; it marks the place where Yuan Shao's rear-area control suddenly fails and turns his family, headquarters, and official households into immediate hostages to the wider mountain-force crisis.
