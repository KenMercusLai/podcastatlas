# 《压岁钱》 (1937 film)

## Overview

《压岁钱》 is a 1937 Shanghai film represented here as a social panorama organized around the circulation of one child's New Year coin.

## Current Profile

[[11-yasuiqian-yu-shengdan-liwu-de-qiyuan-luf0cjj9qquztmuhmzrghb-9dneq|怪东西 episode 11]] describes a silver dollar leaving a girl's New Year packet and passing among people from different social strata. The object's movement lets the film connect family custom, spending, class, labor, and national-crisis themes without confining 压岁钱 to childhood sentiment.

The episode also reads the film materially. The coin is wrapped and placed beneath a pillow at the beginning, while paper currency appears in the following New Year scene; this is interpreted alongside the 1935 currency reform as evidence that festival objects can register monetary change.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses one circulating object to connect otherwise separated lives and social classes.
- Turns a family New Year custom into a device for observing urban society.
- Places childhood money beside national crisis and collective responsibility.
- Makes the transition from silver coin to paper currency visible within festival practice.

## Evidence

- Circulation structure - [[11-yasuiqian-yu-shengdan-liwu-de-qiyuan-luf0cjj9qquztmuhmzrghb-9dneq]] says the plot follows one silver dollar after a girl spends it.
- Social panorama - [[11-yasuiqian-yu-shengdan-liwu-de-qiyuan-luf0cjj9qquztmuhmzrghb-9dneq]] describes the coin's movement through different Shanghai classes and livelihoods.
- Monetary materiality - [[11-yasuiqian-yu-shengdan-liwu-de-qiyuan-luf0cjj9qquztmuhmzrghb-9dneq]] links the opening coin and closing paper money to the 1935 legal-currency reform.
- Political context - [[11-yasuiqian-yu-shengdan-liwu-de-qiyuan-luf0cjj9qquztmuhmzrghb-9dneq]] associates the film with post-1931 national-salvation and unity themes.
