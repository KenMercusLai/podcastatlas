# Sony-TSMC Image Sensor Joint Venture

The Sony-TSMC image sensor joint venture appears in [[xingbake-huiying-mixue-bingcheng-daigong-deng-chuanwen-li-ning-fouren-yu-mubapei-qianyue-1006054195]] as a roughly $4.7 billion project announced on 2026-08-11. The source says the venture will mainly produce next-generation smartphone image sensors and is expected to begin mass production in 2029.

The source presents [[Sony]] as keeping image-sensor technology, product planning, and design closer to itself while using [[TSMC]] manufacturing capability for scale. That makes the venture a concrete case for [[ImageSensorAssetLightManufacturing]]: a company can protect higher-value design and customer-roadmap work while shifting more fabrication burden to a specialist manufacturing partner.
