# 张春 / Zhang Chun (Western Han rebel)

张春 / Zhang Chun (Western Han rebel) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-182-weishenme-shuo-chengye-xiaohe-baiye-xiaohe-2-lqk-jifupm9lbghfzaf09igfufgb|Hanji 182 part 2]] as a rebel infantry commander aligned with [[ChenXi|陈豨]]. The semantic suffix distinguishes him from any later or unrelated Zhang Chun entries.

The episode places Zhang Chun around the Liaocheng theater. [[CaoShen|曹参]] gathers Qi-region troops westward, and [[GuoMeng|郭蒙]] defeats Zhang Chun near Liaocheng before crossing the Yellow River to join the wider suppression.

Zhang Chun's role is narrow but structurally useful: he shows that Chen Xi's rebellion is not only a Dai/Zhao frontier affair, but a network with columns that Han commanders have to cut off from multiple directions.
