# Dyna Robotics

## Overview
Dyna Robotics is the robotics company founded and led by [[LindonDyna|Lindon]], represented in the bounded source through folding systems, hotel-laundry workflows, embodied models, robot hardware, and supply-chain work.

## Current Profile
The company describes itself as a full-stack, product-research organization. Its route begins with bounded manipulation tasks that can tolerate retries, uses customer deployments to locate reliability and workflow gaps, and then feeds those gaps into pretraining, post-training, control, hardware, and product design. The current profile is promising but company-attributed: the source reports high folding success, complete-workflow trials, large-scale first-person data, and early production without supplying independent performance, unit-economics, or retention evidence.

## Key Characteristics
- Product direction is used to prioritize research rather than treating papers or deployment count as ends in themselves.
- Models, controls, hardware, and supply chain are developed as one coupled system.
- Early tasks emphasize flexible-object manipulation, retry tolerance, and measurable daily output.
- Commercial expansion proceeds from a single skill toward the full workflow needed to remove continuous human supervision.
- B2B laundry is favored before home robotics because the environment and service standard can be bounded more tightly.
- Pretraining uses human first-person experience alongside robot and simulation data, according to the company account.

## Qualifications
The bounded evidence is one founder interview. Reported success rates, daily output, generalization, data scale, customer demand, market size, production status, and technical leadership are not independently audited here. A complete workflow demonstrated across sites is not yet evidence of repeatable installation, sustained uptime, positive unit economics, renewal, or product-market fit.
