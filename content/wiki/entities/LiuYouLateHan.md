## Overview
Liu You was a late-Han fiscal official whose current wiki profile centers on a confiscation order against eunuch-held property. His case matters because it shows that even formal action against visibly improper acquisitions by powerful eunuchs could be punished as political offense.

## Current Profile
Liu You appears as dasinong, an office responsible for state finance and property. When Su Kang and Guan Ba obtain valuable land and residences at low prices, Liu You orders confiscation, positioning himself on the side of fiscal rectification rather than palace deference.

The result is not restitution but punishment: Emperor Huan sends Liu You to left-workshop labor along with Li Ying and Feng Gun. A later pardon effort places him among officials whose punishments were politically reversible and morally contestable.

## Key Characteristics
- Fiscal official whose case centers on property confiscation rather than speech alone.
- Direct challenger of Su Kang and Guan Ba's underpriced acquisitions.
- Punished alongside Li Ying and Feng Gun after enforcement threatens eunuch interests.
- Later included in Ying Feng's pardon campaign, marking his punishment as part of a broader court correction issue.
