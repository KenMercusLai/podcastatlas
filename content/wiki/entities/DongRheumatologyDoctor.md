# 董飞 / Dong Fei

## Overview
董飞 is a source-scoped rheumatology physician affiliated in the later episode with Xiyuan Hospital of the China Academy of Chinese Medical Sciences. The later [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] source explicitly says Dong Fei had appeared in the earlier lupus discussion, resolving the earlier guest label “董老师” within the supplied corpus.

## Current Profile
Across the two episodes, Dong Fei moves between disease-specific rheumatology education and everyday seasonal-health discussion. The lupus episode presents a clinically bounded account of immune dysregulation, multi-system warning signs, remission, maintenance, side-effect prevention, adherence, and severe-care coordination. The spring-fatigue episode emphasizes sleep, short rest, moderate movement, food restraint, stress reduction, and medical evaluation for excessive sleepiness, while also using TCM liver, qi, yang, dampness, phlegm, tea, and acupoint explanations that remain source-scoped rather than established diagnostic rules.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains lupus through immune dysregulation, variable organ involvement, specialist assessment, disease-activity monitoring, and long-term management.
- Distinguishes clinical remission and near-normal daily life from a claim of complete cure.
- Treats glucocorticoids and immunosuppressants as risk-benefit decisions requiring monitoring, prevention, adherence, and gradual adjustment.
- Distinguishes mild transient tiredness from excessive or function-limiting sleepiness that may need sleep, endocrine, metabolic, or other assessment.
- Favors regular sleep, brief rest, modest movement, ordinary diet, and gradual habit change over indiscriminate tonics or repeated stimulant use.
- Uses TCM seasonal and metabolic interpretations whose mechanisms and treatment implications are not independently established by the supplied sources.

## Evidence
- Disease explanation and recognition: [[vol-138-hongbanlangchuang-manxingbing-lsea-weckd5-5cswf0ghcabw0vek]] has 董老师 explain autoimmune attack, systemic involvement, typical rash, blood-count changes, urinary findings, joint or muscle pain, Raynaud-like symptoms, and diagnostic difficulty.
- Treatment and remission: [[vol-138-hongbanlangchuang-manxingbing-lsea-weckd5-5cswf0ghcabw0vek]] has the guest describe acute suppression, gradual dose reduction, maintenance, disease-activity scoring, organ protection, and life goals.
- Adherence and prevention: [[vol-138-hongbanlangchuang-manxingbing-lsea-weckd5-5cswf0ghcabw0vek]] has the guest discuss side effects honestly, oppose unilateral stopping, emphasize sun protection, and coordinate severe cases with ICU care.
- Identity and institutional context: [[vol-197-kafei-shi-gaolidai-shuimian-shi-chuxuka-zhejie-dagongrende-chunkun-zijiu-zhinan-lkcj1kymw09hwvfcbwcr-noydrad]] names the guest as 董飞 of Xiyuan Hospital's rheumatology department and states that the guest previously joined the lupus episode.
- Fatigue and recovery: [[vol-197-kafei-shi-gaolidai-shuimian-shi-chuxuka-zhejie-dagongrende-chunkun-zijiu-zhinan-lkcj1kymw09hwvfcbwcr-noydrad]] has Dong Fei distinguish mild tiredness from warning signs, discourage overriding pronounced sleepiness with more coffee, and propose short rest, regular sleep, and moderate activity.
- TCM framework: [[vol-197-kafei-shi-gaolidai-shuimian-shi-chuxuka-zhejie-dagongrende-chunkun-zijiu-zhinan-lkcj1kymw09hwvfcbwcr-noydrad]] attributes the liver, qi, yang, dampness, phlegm, flower-tea, and acupoint account to Dong Fei.

## Qualifications
The identification of the earlier “董老师” as Dong Fei rests on the later episode's explicit retrospective statement and has not been independently verified outside the supplied sources. Both episodes are public education, not individualized rheumatology, immunology, sleep, endocrine, cardiometabolic, medication, nutrition, TCM, pregnancy, fertility, ICU, or emergency-care advice. The spring episode does not establish its proposed seasonal blood-flow mechanism or the efficacy, safety, dose, and contraindications of its teas, acupoints, or TCM pattern claims.

## What Changed
- Resolved the earlier partial “董老师” label to 董飞 using the later episode's explicit retrospective identification.
- Added Xiyuan Hospital rheumatology affiliation as a source-scoped institutional detail.
- Expanded the profile from lupus education to daytime-sleepiness triage, sleep, stimulants, movement, and seasonal-health discussion.
- Preserved the boundary between practical routines and unvalidated TCM mechanisms or remedies.
