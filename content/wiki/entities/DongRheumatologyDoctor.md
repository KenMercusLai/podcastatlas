# 董老师 / Dong Rheumatology Doctor

## Overview
董老师 is the source-scoped rheumatology guest in a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode on systemic lupus erythematosus.

## Current Profile
The episode presents 董老师 as the principal clinical explainer for [[SystemicLupusErythematosusManagement]]. She moves from immune mechanism and multi-system warning signs to acute treatment, clinical remission, maintenance, side-effect prevention, sun protection, fertility and work goals, and severe-care coordination. Her recurring stance is that patients need an honest risk-benefit account and a long-term plan rather than either false reassurance or fear-driven treatment abandonment.

## Key Characteristics
- Corrects the idea that lupus is contagious or merely a state of low immunity.
- Explains SLE through variable multi-organ involvement, specialist assessment, and disease-activity monitoring.
- Distinguishes clinical remission and near-normal daily life from a claim of complete cure.
- Treats glucocorticoids and immunosuppressants as risk-benefit decisions requiring monitoring, prevention, and gradual adjustment.
- Emphasizes adherence, transparent side-effect communication, sun protection, and continuing follow-up.

## Evidence

- Treatment and remission: [[vol-138-hongbanlangchuang-manxingbing-lsea-weckd5-5cswf0ghcabw0vek]] has her describe acute suppression, gradual dose reduction, maintenance, disease-activity scoring, organ protection, and life goals.
