# Jingdezhen Imperial Kiln Factory / 景德镇御窑厂

The Jingdezhen Imperial Kiln Factory / 景德镇御窑厂 is the court-production institution in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode says the Ming court established the imperial ware factory near Zhushan, while the Qing-era name "御窑厂" became familiar for the palace-focused porcelain system.

The factory matters because it makes [[ImperialKilnSystem]] concrete. Court standards, workshop subdivision, reject destruction, rare pigments, record keeping, outsourcing, and people such as [[TangYing]] show that official porcelain relied on the surrounding [[JingdezhenPorcelainProductionSystem]] rather than replacing it.
