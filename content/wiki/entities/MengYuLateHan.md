# 孟郁 / Meng Yu (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-909-2025-ruhe-yangcheng-yi-shen-guiqi-lomndmphrybunjbbbbn9ebafcsj|Hanji 909]] introduces Meng Yu as the official appointed taiwei after [[LiuKuanLateHan|刘宽]] is removed in the aftermath of a solar eclipse. The source uses him mainly as an office-transition marker in the late-Han pattern where disaster and celestial signs accompany senior personnel churn.
