# Citadel Securities

## Overview
Citadel Securities is the market maker identified by the source in its explanation of [[Robinhood]]'s [[PaymentForOrderFlow|payment-for-order-flow]] business model.

## Current Profile
The episode says Robinhood sent customer orders to market makers such as Citadel and earned routing revenue. This makes Citadel Securities part of the hidden economic chain behind “zero commission”: retail flow can be valuable to an execution venue even when the customer pays no explicit ticket charge. The source does not evaluate the firm's execution quality or establish misconduct.

## Key Characteristics
- Market maker receiving retail brokerage order flow.
- Named counterparty in the episode's payment-for-order-flow explanation.
- Part of the market structure connecting customer trades, spreads, execution, and broker revenue.
- Distinct subject from the investment firm represented by [[Citadel]].
