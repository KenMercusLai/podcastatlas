## Overview

天地会 / Tiandihui is the anti-Qing organization led by [[ChenJinnan|陈近南]] in [[LuDingJi|《鹿鼎记》]], represented here as a nationwide resistance network whose shared purpose cannot eliminate internal or inter-organizational factionalism.

## Current Profile

The episode treats Tiandihui as evidence of Chen's real organizing ability: maintaining a geographically broad clandestine network requires more than personal martial prestige. Yet the Qingmu Lodge succession dispute also shows unresolved internal governance, while conflict with the Mu Prince's Residence over rival Ming claimants demonstrates how a common anti-Qing slogan can conceal incompatible legitimacy commitments.

The organization's historical frame remains deliberately uncertain. The hosts mention several origin traditions and later relationships among Tiandihui, Hongmen, and Triads, but explicitly do not present this overview as settled scholarship. The current profile therefore keeps fictional function separate from historical genealogy.

## Key Characteristics

- Operates as a geographically broad clandestine resistance organization.
- Draws legitimacy from anti-Qing and Ming-restoration commitments.
- Depends heavily on Chen Jinnan's personal leadership and mediation.
- Contains lodge-level succession and authority disputes.
- Competes with nominal allies over rival restoration claimants.
- Accumulates origin legends that require historical caution.
