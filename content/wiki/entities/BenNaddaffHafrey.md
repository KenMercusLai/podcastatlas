# Ben Naddaff-Hafrey

## Overview

Ben Naddaff-Hafrey is a reporter and host whose work for [[RevisionistHistory]] connects Staten Island's modern secession politics and outsider identity to its Revolutionary-era Loyalist history.

## Current Profile

The trailer identifies Naddaff-Hafrey as the host of *The Staten Island Problem* and presents the project as his deep investigation of a largely forgotten four-year secession battle. Its broad claim is interpretive: the local breakdown of trust anticipated forces dividing the United States, including an outer-borough political trajectory associated with [[RudyGiuliani]] and [[DonaldTrump]].

In the crossover source, Naddaff-Hafrey explains that the historical material grew out of reporting for *The Staten Island Problem*, a series about the borough's attempt to leave New York City. A section on opposition to the [[AmericanRevolution|American Revolution]] was cut from that project and became the basis for a longer discussion with [[TheDollop]].

His contribution is both archival and interpretive: he credits historian Phil Pappas, preserves uncertainty around releases and the peace conference, and argues that commerce and fear of disruption made early Loyalism intelligible before occupation produced the losses residents had hoped rebellion would avoid.

## Key Characteristics

- Reporter linking local political identity to deeper historical structure.
- Host and investigative reporter for Revisionist History's Staten Island series.
- Crossover guest translating cut reporting into a longer narrative format.
- Interpreter attentive to commercial incentives and changing allegiance.
- Explicitly preserves uncertainty around incomplete records.
