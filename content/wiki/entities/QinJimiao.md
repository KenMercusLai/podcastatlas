[[QinJimiao|极庙]] is the ritual building discussed in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-121-yingzheng-zao-shijie-zuizao-gaosu-gonglu-2200nian-huangcao-busheng-ls1u8dmuvs5bbrzadf2dtd00js-p|Qinji 121]]. The episode says Qin Shi Huang had a palace completed south of the Wei River, first called Xin Gong and then renamed Ji Miao after his return to [[Xianyang]].

The name is read as significant: ji points toward the heavenly pivot or supreme celestial center, while miao marks the building as a ritual/sacrificial site. The host therefore infers that Ji Miao's ritual object was probably [[Taiyi]], not simply the Qin ancestral line.

## Key Claims
- [[QinJimiao]] is treated as a ritual center rather than an ordinary palace name change.
- The building's south-of-Wei-River placement and name help link [[Xianyang]] to [[QinCosmicCapitalPlanning]].
- Its likely connection to [[Taiyi]] marks a move from clan ancestor-centered legitimacy toward cosmic imperial legitimacy.
- The Taiyi identification is an interpretive inference in the source, not a quoted base-chronicle statement.
