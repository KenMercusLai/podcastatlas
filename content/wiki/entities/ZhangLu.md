# 张璐 / Zhang Lu

## Overview
张璐 / Zhang Lu is the founder and managing partner of [[FusionFund]] and an investment-side commentator on enterprise AI, healthcare AI, semiconductors, inference infrastructure, and regulated vertical deployment.

## Current Profile
Across the bounded sources, Zhang evaluates AI from the demand and deployment side rather than treating benchmark capability as sufficient. In healthcare, she emphasizes administrative workflows, privacy, liability, local models, and clinician responsibility. In infrastructure, she frames inference as recurring demand while keeping power, packaging, cloud operations, system integration, and incumbent platform power in view.

The latest [[LateTalk]] episode extends that framework to [[Anthropic]] and large enterprises. Zhang argues that the next phase depends on integrating AI into existing workflows at controllable cost, proving retention and return, and navigating data, regulation, and organizational change. Her opportunity map favors regulated verticals, enterprise back offices, inference efficiency, local deployment, and specialized models over generic wrappers around frontier APIs.

## Key Characteristics
- Demand-side investor lens focused on whether AI becomes accepted, renewable work rather than only a capable model or demo.
- Full-stack infrastructure view connecting inference demand with chips, memory, power, networking, cloud operations, and software deployment.
- Regulated-market focus on healthcare, finance, privacy, audit, liability, and local or specialized models.
- Workflow-first commercialization view that distinguishes automation and cost savings from later business optimization and revenue growth.
- Startup-moat test centered on proprietary data, domain workflows, compliance, distribution, and neutrality toward enterprise customers.

## Qualifications
The sources are podcast summaries and investment commentary, not audited customer cohorts, procurement records, chip-delivery data, or clinical evidence. Budget, retention, model-share, valuation, and deployment-timeline figures in the latest episode remain source-attributed. Zhang's opportunity map is a strategic judgment and does not establish that every regulated vertical or specialized model will produce attractive returns.

## What Changed
- Extended Zhang's healthcare and infrastructure views into a unified enterprise-deployment framework.
- Added her two-stage distinction between workflow automation and deeper business optimization.
- Added her startup-moat test around proprietary data, regulation, domain workflows, and customer trust.
