# Lord Liverpool

## Overview

Lord Liverpool was a long-serving British prime minister from 1812 to 1827 whose stability and longevity are acknowledged here but whose colorless image, weak name recognition, and association with Peterloo limit his public reputation.

## Current Profile

[[35-the-prime-ministers-world-cup-glt6124252189]] places Liverpool in the difficult transition after the Napoleonic Wars. A fifteen-year premiership implies political durability, and the hosts acknowledge the stability of his government, but describe him as colorless and associate his period with Peterloo and reactionary politics.

That combination left him especially vulnerable to [[ClementAttlee|Clement Attlee]], who defeated him with 81%, the largest margin discussed in the episode. The hosts suspect many voters knew little about Liverpool, making the result as much a measure of recognition as comparative government.

## Key Characteristics

- British prime minister from 1812 to 1827.
- Long-serving leader associated with post-Napoleonic stability.
- Public image described as colorless.

- Weak modern name recognition in the episode's audience.
