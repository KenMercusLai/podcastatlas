# Premier Padel

Premier Padel is the professional padel circuit visited by [[HamishClayton]] in [[spring-then-fall-a-weakened-muslim-brotherhood-6a7aef1b616564bf00d9b354]]. The episode describes the London event near Wimbledon as the first elite professional padel tournament held in Britain, with players from 15 countries and a prize pot far smaller than Wimbledon but large enough to signal professionalization.

The source says the circuit is funded by [[Qatar]], making Premier Padel part of the sport's move from friendly participation scene toward a global event product. That shift connects [[PadelParticipationBoom]] to [[SportsEntertainmentFlywheel]], but the episode keeps the spectator future uncertain.
