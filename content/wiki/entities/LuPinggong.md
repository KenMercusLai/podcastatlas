# 鲁平公 / Duke Ping of Lu

鲁平公 / Duke Ping of Lu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-65-wo-chuanyue-qu-gei-langman-de-chuwang-nian-shou-shi-lvyd7pve-tfu4ixsqrbdvhm6g64j]] through a compact 297 BCE succession notice. The episode says Lu Ping Gong, personal name Ji Lv, dies and his son Ji Jia succeeds as [[LuMingong|鲁湣公]].

The page is source-scoped. Lu Ping Gong's role is annalistic rather than interpretive: the episode records the transition before returning to [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] and the Qin-Chu branch.
