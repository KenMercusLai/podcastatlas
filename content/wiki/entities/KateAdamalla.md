# Kate Adamalla

Kate Adamalla is the [[UniversityOfMinnesota]] lab leader named by [[OliverMorton]] in [[its-not-easy-being-green-zack-polanski-6a82d1a0ff328abd84724cf0]]. The episode credits her lab with creating [[SyntheticSpudCells]], synthetic cells built from chemicals rather than descended from earlier biological cells.

Her source role is to make the [[LifeDefinitionSpectrum]] concrete. The spud-cell line can reproduce for a limited number of generations, but it lacks robust metabolism and perfect reproduction, so the episode treats it as a boundary object between engineering, biology, and definitions of life.
