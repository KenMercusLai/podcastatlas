# 徐翕 / Xu Xi (late Han)

徐翕 / Xu Xi (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1026-jiemi-sanguo-di-yi-mengjiang-lvbu-de-luomu-lhjf-agujuliglqchumtff3tftxg1|Hanji 1026]] as a former [[CaoCao|曹操]] subordinate who had betrayed him during the [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] crisis and then taken shelter with [[ZhangBaLateHan|张霸]]. After Cao Cao captures [[Xiapi|下邳]], he asks through [[LiuBei|刘备]] for Zhang Ba to send Xu Xi and [[MaoHuiLateHan|毛晖]]'s heads.

The outcome is the source's clearest former-defector integration case. Zhang Ba refuses to betray friends under his protection, and Cao Cao turns that refusal into a public signal by praising Zhang Ba, sparing Xu Xi and Mao Hui, and appointing Xu Xi as Langya administrator.
