# 时代峰峻 / Shidai Fengjun

## Overview

[[ShidaiFengjun]] is a Chinese entertainment and idol-management company discussed as the institutional center of the August 17 Chongqing confrontation examined by [[DuanwenNewsPodcast|端闻]].

## Current Profile

[[yi-chang-wuli-yiyi-shang-de-nannv-duili-chongqing-shidai-fengjun-shijian]] describes the company as the operator of a trainee-based idol system that launched TFBOYS in 2013 and continues to manage many underage trainees. Its premises on the eighteenth floor of Chongqing's Changjiang International building became a recurring fan destination because trainees and idols entered and left there.

The source does not investigate the company's internal operations. Its relevance is spatial and symbolic: a commercial entertainment site became the setting for conflict over fandom, masculinity, public order, gender, and political legitimacy.

## Key Characteristics

- It operates a trainee-based entertainment and idol-management model.
- It launched TFBOYS in 2013 according to the episode.
- Its Chongqing premises attracted fans hoping to see and support idols in person.
- Its artists and trainees are called "楼人" by fans, who call themselves "楼丝," linking fandom identity to the building.
- It sits inside competing public narratives that promote patriotic youth idols while criticizing fan-circle disorder and gender-nonconforming aesthetics.
