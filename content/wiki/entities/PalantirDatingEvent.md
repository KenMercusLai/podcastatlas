# Singles Dating Matching Using Palantir Data

## Overview
Singles Dating Matching Using Palantir Data is the satirical New York dating event at the center of [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-8eb9e254581eaa220d9428df389214d5-27844515-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-8eb9e254581eaa220d9428df389214d5-27844515|Love in the time of Palantir]].

## Current Profile
The event matters because it turns abstract surveillance concerns into a social scene. About 60 people reportedly bought tickets and most gave [[JamesSteinberg]] their names and phone numbers. Steinberg says he did not use [[Palantir]] software or Palantir data, but used Palantir-inspired profile-building methods to match attendees and reveal what data sources could assemble about them.

## Key Characteristics
- Satirical singles event built around Palantir's public association with surveillance and national-security work.
- Demonstration that names and phone numbers can lead to much richer personal profiles.
- Mixed-accuracy matching experiment where incorrect identity resolution is itself part of the privacy lesson.
- Social debrief setting where attendees process both curiosity and discomfort about searchable personal information.
- Brand-conflict case because Palantir reportedly sent a cease-and-desist email over use of its name.

## Qualifications
The event is a media-reported, comedic experiment rather than a validated dating or data-science system. The page should not treat its matches, profiles, or success counts as independently audited.
