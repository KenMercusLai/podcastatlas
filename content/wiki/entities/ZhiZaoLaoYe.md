# 纸造 / 老爷

纸造 / 老爷 is one of the speakers in [[chuantai-ni-chi-xiangcai-ma-nvshengban-ni-yuanyi-he-xingzhuan-zhihou-de-ziji-zai-yiqi-ma-555771723]]. She initially argues against dating a gender-swapped self because a completely similar partner would remove the mystery, discovery, and complementary strengths she associates with romantic growth.

Her examples make [[ComplementaryRelationshipSupport]] concrete: she describes herself as emotional and anxious enough to need steadier people in intimate and work relationships. During the position-swap segment, she also gives the strongest argument for [[SelfSimilarityInIntimacy]], saying a nearly identical partner would reduce negotiation costs and prevent some misunderstandings because both people would already know how the other thinks.
