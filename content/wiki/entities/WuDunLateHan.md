# 吴敦 / Wu Dun (late Han)

吴敦 / Wu Dun (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1026-jiemi-sanguo-di-yi-mengjiang-lvbu-de-luomu-lhjf-agujuliglqchumtff3tftxg1|Hanji 1026]] as one of the local armed actors whom [[ZhangBaLateHan|张霸]] persuades to submit to [[CaoCao|曹操]] after the fall of [[LyuBu|吕布]] at [[Xiapi|下邳]]. The source does not give Wu Dun an independent biography; it treats him as part of Cao Cao's Taishan-area pacification field.

His role is therefore structural. Wu Dun helps show how a defeated campaign can be converted into local submission once Cao Cao uses Zhang Ba as a broker rather than relying only on coercive pursuit.
