# 咸阳 / Xianyang

咸阳 / Xianyang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] as the new [[QinState|秦国]] capital selected at the opening of [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s second reform round in 350 BCE. The episode treats the move from Liyang to Xianyang as more than an address change: it is part of [[ShangYangReforms|商鞅变法]] becoming a durable state geography.

The source explains the name through location: south of the Jiuzong mountains and north of the Wei River, with "xian" glossed as total or complete. It also notes that Qin remains centered there through [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]]'s unification and the Qin dynasty's end.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-55-zhangyi-tongshi-guiren-qinhuiwang-lsclfbwn06fgzooipf5up9sdkedy]] adds Xianyang as the destination Zhang Yi does not reach before learning that [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] has died. The road back toward the Qin capital becomes the place where successful [[LianhengStrategy|连横]] work turns into [[PatronSuccessionExposure|靠山更替暴露风险]] under [[QinWuwang|秦武王]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-61-shui-shi-lishishang-daner-zui-fei-de-diwang-lvpoaszanascnlrmu2atinlxzmk1]] adds Xianyang as the imagined target of [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]]'s anti-Qin plan. After entering Qin in disguise to inspect terrain and [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]], Zhao Wuling Wang decides the usual [[HanguPass|函谷关]] route is too difficult and considers moving south from Yunzhong and Jiuyuan toward Xianyang.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-62-ji-er-kuang-ni-jiujiu-de-tui-a-lumch-ibj8m94abjh5nvr3qhiqff]] adds Xianyang as the detention and office setting after the [[Wuguan|武关]] trap. [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] is taken there after Qin seizes him, then pressured by [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] to cede [[WuJunWarringStates|巫郡]] and [[Qianzhong|黔中]]. The same episode closes by saying [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]] arrives at Xianyang and is made Qin chancellor.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-63-degao-wangzhong-mengchangjun-shi-weijunzi-lmd0owe3rq-faponbwzhkaigprin]] resolves that chancellor setup by making the Qin court a danger zone for Mengchangjun. Qin Zhaoxiang Wang suspects his Qi loyalty, replaces him with [[LouHuan|楼缓]], imprisons him, and releases him only after a palace favorite is persuaded through the stolen white fox-fur robe.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-65-wo-chuanyue-qu-gei-langman-de-chuwang-nian-shou-shi-lvyd7pve-tfu4ixsqrbdvhm6g64j]] makes Xianyang the start and end point of [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]'s final failed escape. Chu Huaiwang leaves the Qin capital, cannot return through [[Wuguan|武关]], is captured after being refused by Zhao, and dies back in Qin captivity in 296 BCE. The capital therefore carries both administrative centrality and the reputational burden of a detained foreign ruler's death.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-115-1-jingke-ciqin-sihu-you-dianer-caoshuai-le-lgz1adsn1t8jjiaobmtfedpr-ixn]] adds Xianyang as the palace setting for [[JingKe|荆轲]]'s access attempt. [[MengJia|蒙嘉]] helps turn [[YanState|燕国]]'s surrender script, [[FanWuji|樊无忌]]'s head, and the [[DuKangMap|督亢地图]] into a grand court audience. The capital's ceremony then becomes active pressure: [[QinWuyang|秦武阳]] freezes before the assembled Qin kin and ministers, forcing Jing Ke to proceed alone.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-117-2-xuezhedian-qinchao-wujiang-jingran-ruci-gaoqingshang-lsyw3eitcnzfexl1l6vfh2gbtaoy]] adds Xianyang as the property target in [[WangJianQin|王翦]]'s self-protection performance. Before and during the Chu campaign departure, Wang Jian repeatedly asks [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] for fields and houses near the capital, making [[GreedPersonaSelfProtection|贪财人设式自保]] legible as a capital-centered property signal rather than an ordinary reward request.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-120-4-bing-liuguo-hou-qinshihuang-jiao-ni-ruhe-shou-jiangshan-lgwsd0c3wkl-cphma8sm3ftkowwr]] makes Xianyang the post-unification concentration point for people, metal, and display. Confiscated weapons from the conquered realm are brought there and cast into palace figures, while 120,000 powerful households are forced to move to the capital so [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] can enrich the center, monitor potential opposition, and detach local elites from their old networks. The same source describes Qin copying conquered states' palaces around the Wei River area, turning Xianyang into a visible archive of empire.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-121-yingzheng-zao-shijie-zuizao-gaosu-gonglu-2200nian-huangcao-busheng-ls1u8dmuvs5bbrzadf2dtd00js-p]] makes Xianyang both the center the emperor leaves and the center rebuilt as cosmic hub. Qin Shi Huang departs from the capital toward [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]], [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]], and Qin old lands, then returns to a construction program in which [[QinJimiao|极庙]] south of the Wei River, roads toward [[Lishan|骊山]], covered passages, and [[QinChidao|驰道]] fold the capital into [[QinCosmicCapitalPlanning|秦代宇宙化都城规划]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-125-1-qinshihuang-xiujian-shishang-zuida-lanweilou-afanggong-lhqc6jw4jzoyrg7jzc2mbxdebj4]] adds the overcrowded and hidden-palace version of Xianyang. The episode says the earlier relocation of 120,000 powerful households makes the capital population surge, helping explain the move of offices south of the Wei River and the planning of [[AfangPalace|阿房宫]]. It also makes the Xianyang region the center of [[ImperialItinerarySecrecy|帝王行踪保密]]: hundreds of palaces are connected, the emperor's residence is concealed, and senior officials wait at Xianyang Palace because the ruler's movements are deliberately opaque.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-127-2-lisi-weisha-bu-bang-yingming-de-fusu-lq-vny5duwwzfubpeijr21oazxbg]] makes Xianyang the endpoint that gives [[ImperialDeathConcealment|秘不发丧式过渡控制]] its timing. [[LiSi|李斯]], [[ZhaoGao|赵高]], and [[QinErshi|胡亥]] keep Qin Shi Huang's death hidden until the convoy returns; only at Xianyang do they announce the death and present Hu Hai's succession as a completed fact.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-127-3-haiwai-huigui-yipi-zhujian-jiekai-huhai-lidi-zhi-mi-lja0pankcx4ugtylbxxqkwuavdnw]] keeps Xianyang as the public-announcement endpoint in the traditional branch, but the [[ZhaoZhengShu|《赵正书》]] branch changes what had already happened before the return. If Hu Hai was chosen during Qin Shi Huang's final illness, Xianyang remains where accession is announced and mourning is formalized, not necessarily where the heir decision is invented.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-139-ta-zaici-yingxiang-hanchao-lp1zujuq0o3ohxc79xqi1phpb5zo|Hanji 139]] makes Xianyang the newly occupied capital whose meaning splits between temptation and records. [[LiuBang|刘邦]] enters the Qin palace and wants to stay with its wealth and women until [[FanKuai|樊哙]] and [[ZhangLiang|张良]] persuade him to return to [[Bashang|霸上]]. At the same time, [[XiaoHe|萧何]] goes to the chancellor's office and preserves maps, registers, and archives, turning the capital from a palace prize into the information base of [[AdministrativeArchiveCapture|administrative archive capture]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-142-zhechang-juhui-xiangyu-shudiao-le-yiqie-ln7dv2jxrk6lt1c4szylsrb41k3r|Hanji 142]] gives Xianyang its Xiang Yu aftermath. After the Hongmen escape, [[XiangYu|项羽]] enters the capital, kills [[ZiyingQin|子婴]], destroys the Qin royal line, burns the palace complex, and seizes wealth and women before leaving east. The episode uses that conduct as a direct contrast with Liu Bang's earlier palace withdrawal and archive-preserving occupation posture.
