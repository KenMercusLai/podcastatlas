# 咸阳 / Xianyang

咸阳 / Xianyang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] as the new [[QinState|秦国]] capital selected at the opening of [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s second reform round in 350 BCE. The episode treats the move from Liyang to Xianyang as more than an address change: it is part of [[ShangYangReforms|商鞅变法]] becoming a durable state geography.

The source explains the name through location: south of the Jiuzong mountains and north of the Wei River, with "xian" glossed as total or complete. It also notes that Qin remains centered there through [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]]'s unification and the Qin dynasty's end.
