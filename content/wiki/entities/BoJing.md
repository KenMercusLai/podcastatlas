# 博靖 / Bo Jing

博靖 / Bo Jing is the [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] reporter mentioned in [[ep240-ba-zong-tian-chong-zai-haiwai-duanju-chuhai-de-chanye-mima-ltvxvfj5ko6djhsjatiphdy-dzek]] for coverage of young people working inside domestic short-drama production teams. The episode uses that companion reporting to complicate the simple view that short drama is only low-status or low-quality entertainment.

In EP240, Bo Jing's referenced angle matters because [[LiveActionShortDrama]] and [[ShortDramaIndustrialization]] also provide work for new graduates from film, television, and media schools. That labor-market role sits beside the format's aesthetic controversy and overseas growth story.
