## Overview
Heather Hasson is the co-founder and board chair of [[FIGS]]. In [[figs-trina-spear-and-heather-hasson-the-2-5-billion-scrubs-brand-that-investors-said-would-never-work-vmp6448511514]], she is presented as the product-led founder who connected fashion experience, charitable uniform work in Kenya, and direct observation of clinicians to the opportunity to redesign medical scrubs.

## Current Profile
Heather's profile centers on close user observation and hands-on product development. She moved from tailoring a friend's scrubs and selling early batches outside emergency rooms to working directly with factories, dye houses, and yarn mills. Her concept of [[TechnicalComfort]] gave the company a design language that joined clinical function with comfort, fit, and dignity.

## Key Characteristics
- She approaches product creation through observation, tailoring, material development, and repeated contact with users.
- She linked the commercial opportunity to respect for healthcare workers rather than treating scrubs as a purely fashion-led category.
- She initially preferred founding alone but accepted a partnership when [[TrinaSpear]]'s operating and financial skills proved complementary.
- She remained closely involved in manufacturing and unconventional early styles that later became strong sellers.
- She stepped down as co-CEO in 2022 and continued as FIGS's board chair.
