# The Metamorphosis / Kafka's 《变形记》

The Metamorphosis / Kafka's 《变形记》 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-901-jixingzi-de-ren-hui-shi-shenme-xiachang-lh1yfyjzqptxvtnv9taiqms9yjud|Hanji 901]] as the episode's opening example for impatience and exaggerated self-importance. The host recalls Gregor Samsa waking as an insect but still worrying about work, a train, and whether his family can live without him.

The episode uses the story pragmatically rather than as literary criticism. Gregor's family later finds its own survival paths, so the anecdote supports the host's warning that anxious urgency can overstate one's indispensability and damage the people and tasks around it. This makes the work a source case for [[EmotionRegulationToolkit|情绪调节工具箱]] and [[ReactionChoiceFrame|反应选择框架]].
