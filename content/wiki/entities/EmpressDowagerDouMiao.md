# 窦妙 / Empress Dowager Dou Miao

窦妙 / Empress Dowager Dou Miao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-901-jixingzi-de-ren-hui-shi-shenme-xiachang-lh1yfyjzqptxvtnv9taiqms9yjud|Hanji 901]] as the deceased empress dowager whose burial status becomes a 172 CE court test. The source presents her as [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]'s empress and as a dowager whose earlier support helped [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] inherit the throne.

Her political danger comes from family and factional memory. [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] and [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] had opposed eunuch power and been destroyed, and the source says Dou Miao was then confined away from ordinary authority. After her death, [[ZhaoZhongLateHan|赵忠]], [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]], and [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]] resist giving her the full burial and co-sacrificial status that would publicly honor her.

[[ChenQiuLateHan|陈球 / 陈求]] and [[LiXianTaiweiLateHan|李贤]] argue that Dou's own role should not be erased by family-guilt logic. [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] accepts Li Xian's memorial, and the episode says Dou is buried at Xuanling with the posthumous title Huan Si Empress. Her page therefore anchors [[ImperialBurialLegitimacy|帝后合葬名分]] and connects earlier anti-eunuch grievance to later [[PartyProhibitionRehabilitation|党人翻案合法性]].
