# 马坪关 / Mapingguan

马坪关 is the mountain village at the center of [[144-nvxia-duan-sihe-he-tade-jiaxiang-mapingguan-women-ceng-yiwei-you-dian-you-lu-zhishi-meng-linmjqt-n-sa5sarpqgdxgtoeejt]]. The source places it in [[Yunnan|云南]] near [[ShaxiAncientTown|沙溪古镇]], with a remembered connection to Qing salt-tax collection and horse-caravan traffic along a broader [[TeaHorseRoad|茶马古道]] landscape.

The episode's 马坪关 is not a scenery-only rural destination. It is presented through electricity in 2009, road access in 2015, matsutake mountain rules, long village meetings, school consolidation pressure, women's organizations, outside support, tourism pacing, and the question of how children will remember life before infrastructure.

## Key Claims
- The source presents 马坪关 as a village with strong internal organization before external visitors arrived; outside support made some methods clearer but did not create collective capacity from nothing.
- [[RuralInfrastructureTransition]] matters because electricity and road access changed daily rhythm, visitor access, schooling, transport, and the imagination of what was possible.
- [[MatsutakeCommonsManagement]] makes 马坪关 a resource-governance case: mountain access, harvesting rules, contracts, per-capita distribution, and reserve funds turn local ecology into collective finance.
- The village's tourism stance is cautious. It wants income and outside connection but tries to keep guest flow, homestay opening, and online visibility within local absorptive capacity.
- The planned museum and costume/display work would make 马坪关 part of [[HeritageTourismCommercialization]] only if memory, objects, and village self-understanding remain stronger than visitor extraction.
