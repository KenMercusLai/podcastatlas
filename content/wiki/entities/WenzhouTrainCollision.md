# Wenzhou Train Collision / 723温州动车事故

The Wenzhou Train Collision is the 2011 accident treated in [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]] as the major safety and legitimacy shock in China's high-speed-rail story. The episode says lightning and equipment faults interacted with serious software and fail-safe design defects, causing one train to strike another and killing 40 people while injuring 172.

The source's interpretation is narrower than a rejection of high-speed rail. It frames the accident as a management, signaling, redundancy, testing, emergency-response, and communication failure. The official response and media handling made [[HighSpeedRailSafetyTrust]] collapse more sharply, while the aftermath pushed project pauses, lending stress, speed reductions, and the eventual end of the [[MinistryOfRailwaysChina]] structure.
