# Anatoly Fomenko / 福缅科

Anatoly Fomenko / 福缅科 appears in [[271-tangchao-douyao-bucunzai-le-weishenme-weishilun-hui-zai-jinnian-da-baofa-lg7-wdrdw1b9h7lwa4vwkvmyb4un]] as the Russian pseudohistory figure whom [[HeSenbao|何森堡]] names as a major source for later [[PseudoHistoryConspiracy|伪史论]]. In the source's account, Fomenko's framework centers Russian history and declares large parts of European, American, and Chinese history false; Chinese imitators are said to borrow the denial method while omitting the part that would also negate Chinese history.
