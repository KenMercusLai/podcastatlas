# Pedro Ara

## Overview

Pedro Ara was the anatomy professor and embalming specialist assigned to preserve [[EvaPeron|Eva Perón]] for public display after her death.

## Current Profile

The episode places Ara at the threshold between Evita's public funeral and her later life as a [[PoliticalCorpseRelic]]. [[JuanPeron|Juan Perón]] intended permanent preservation and display, while Ara's technical task required replacing blood and halting decay after prolonged viewing had already damaged the body.

His role was therefore both medical and political: the state expected preservation technology to sustain the image of an incorruptible national martyr.

## Key Characteristics

- He was presented as an anatomy professor with specialist embalming experience.
- He was waiting to begin preservation immediately after Evita's death.
- His technique was described as replacing blood with glycerin.
- Public display and delayed access complicated his attempt to prevent decay.
- His work connected bodily preservation to Peronist monumental and devotional aims.

## Evidence

- Professional role and technique: [[497-evita-death-of-a-martyr-part-4-glt5046116399]] identifies Ara as an anatomy professor and embalmer known for glycerin-based preservation.
- Political commission: [[497-evita-death-of-a-martyr-part-4-glt5046116399]] says Perón planned permanent public preservation and that Ara prepared a laboratory at the trade-union headquarters.
- Material difficulty: [[497-evita-death-of-a-martyr-part-4-glt5046116399]] describes condensation, decomposition, and alterations to the glass-lidded coffin before Ara could complete his work.
