## Overview

赵璇 / Zhao Xuan is synthesized as a Western Han court official whose current profile now has an earlier crown-prince tutor error and a later [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]-era Yushi Dafu attack sequence. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-555-ta-weile-dang-huangdi-weisha-yao-xisheng-qindie-lq3kkkmk9nh4yhxuovmy_uktalrv|Hanji 555]] shows him advising the crown prince [[HanAidi|刘欣]] to thank [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] for preserving the Dingtao birth-father line; Chengdi treats that gratitude as politically wrong and demotes Zhao Xuan. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-579-zoujin-kexue-guyao-shi-yao-ma-lof7pvrnlzv2lmbzjshbx7vi2c2n|Hanji 579]] later shows him as Yushi Dafu receiving appointment documents when the palace "鼓妖" sound occurs, then joining [[ZhuBoWesternHan|朱博]] in punishing [[ShiDanWesternHan|施丹]] after the title dispute. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-580-huigu-sizhou-shuishi-nide-kaoshan-liid3tymhojmajkyeqoq6vryprhz|Hanji 580]] then shows him pursuing [[WangMang|王莽]], but the request to strip Wang Mang's title and fief fails because [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] remains Wang Mang's decisive shelter.

## Current Profile

Hanji 555 places Zhao Xuan earlier as the crown prince's taifu. When Liu Xin wants to thank Chengdi after Chengdi preserves the Dingtao birth-father line through Liu Jing, Zhao Xuan recommends gratitude. The source contrasts him with [[YanCongWesternHan|严从]], who advises silence because Liu Xin has already become Chengdi's son. Chengdi's anger and Zhao Xuan's demotion make Zhao Xuan a case where loyalty and ordinary filial feeling do not equal correct political judgment.

Hanji 579 places Zhao Xuan later at the moment of appointment. Aidi appoints Zhu Bo chancellor and Zhao Xuan Yushi Dafu after both had supported Fu Taihou's title side. As they are about to receive their documents, a bell-like palace sound is interpreted by Li Xun and Yang Xiong as a warning against the new ministers; Aidi ignores that interpretation.

The same source shows Zhao Xuan using the new office position almost immediately. After Aidi adopts Zhu Bo's advice and grants the Dingtao-line title settlement, Zhu Bo and Zhao Xuan memorialize against Shi Dan, saying Shi Dan had attacked Dong Hong's title proposal and damaged Aidi's filial duty. Aidi approves their request and strips Shi Dan.

Hanji 580 places Zhao Xuan inside the anti-Wang pressure sequence. After Zhu Bo and Zhao Xuan help persuade Aidi against Shi Dan's title and fief, they continue with a memorial against Wang Mang. They argue that Wang Mang once held the highest military-administrative rank but failed to support Aidi's filial-title order, so his offense deserves public execution if not for the pardon and should still cost him marquisate and fief.

The case fails at the final step. Aidi sends Wang Mang back to his fief but leaves his title and fief intact because Wang Zhengjun remains the protective senior dowager. Zhao Xuan therefore marks the limit of formal accusation when the target still has a stronger kinship-patron channel.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Yushi Dafu active in Jianping 2 / 5 BCE.
- Earlier crown-prince taifu whose gratitude advice to Liu Xin is punished by Chengdi.
- Rises as part of the Fu Taihou title-settlement coalition alongside Zhu Bo and is targeted by Li Xun and Yang Xiong's "鼓妖" interpretation.
- Works with Zhu Bo to convert victory in the title dispute into punishment of Shi Dan.
- Frames Wang Mang's prior Da Sima status as aggravating evidence.
- Seeks removal of Wang Mang's title and fief after pardon removes execution from reach.
- Fails because Wang Zhengjun's senior authority protects Wang Mang from full reduction.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 555, Hanji 579, and Hanji 580. Hanji 555 supports Zhao Xuan's taifu advice and demotion, but not the full office sequence between that demotion and his later Yushi Dafu role. Hanji 579 supports Zhao Xuan's appointment, omen exposure, and Shi Dan memorial, but not his full career or exact memorial text. Hanji 580 supports the failed Wang Mang attack but not the full political history of the Wang clan. The page does not settle whether Zhao Xuan's name should be normalized with another transmitted form.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 555's earlier crown-prince taifu advice and demotion layer.
- Preserved Hanji 579's appointment, "鼓妖" warning, title-settlement, and Shi Dan punishment layers before Hanji 580's Wang Mang attack.
