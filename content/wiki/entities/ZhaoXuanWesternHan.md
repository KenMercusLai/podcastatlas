## Overview

赵璇 / Zhao Xuan is synthesized as the [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]-era Yushi Dafu who rises with [[ZhuBoWesternHan|朱博]] through the [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]] title-settlement coalition. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-579-zoujin-kexue-guyao-shi-yao-ma-lof7pvrnlzv2lmbzjshbx7vi2c2n|Hanji 579]] shows him receiving appointment documents when the palace "鼓妖" sound occurs, then joining Zhu Bo in punishing [[ShiDanWesternHan|施丹]] after the title dispute. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-580-huigu-sizhou-shuishi-nide-kaoshan-liid3tymhojmajkyeqoq6vryprhz|Hanji 580]] then shows him pursuing [[WangMang|王莽]], but the request to strip Wang Mang's title and fief fails because [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] remains Wang Mang's decisive shelter.

## Current Profile

Hanji 579 places Zhao Xuan at the moment of appointment. Aidi appoints Zhu Bo chancellor and Zhao Xuan Yushi Dafu after both had supported Fu Taihou's title side. As they are about to receive their documents, a bell-like palace sound is interpreted by Li Xun and Yang Xiong as a warning against the new ministers; Aidi ignores that interpretation.

The same source shows Zhao Xuan using the new office position almost immediately. After Aidi adopts Zhu Bo's advice and grants the Dingtao-line title settlement, Zhu Bo and Zhao Xuan memorialize against Shi Dan, saying Shi Dan had attacked Dong Hong's title proposal and damaged Aidi's filial duty. Aidi approves their request and strips Shi Dan.

Hanji 580 places Zhao Xuan inside the anti-Wang pressure sequence. After Zhu Bo and Zhao Xuan help persuade Aidi against Shi Dan's title and fief, they continue with a memorial against Wang Mang. They argue that Wang Mang once held the highest military-administrative rank but failed to support Aidi's filial-title order, so his offense deserves public execution if not for the pardon and should still cost him marquisate and fief.

The case fails at the final step. Aidi sends Wang Mang back to his fief but leaves his title and fief intact because Wang Zhengjun remains the protective senior dowager. Zhao Xuan therefore marks the limit of formal accusation when the target still has a stronger kinship-patron channel.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Yushi Dafu active in Jianping 2 / 5 BCE.
- Rises as part of the Fu Taihou title-settlement coalition alongside Zhu Bo and is targeted by Li Xun and Yang Xiong's "鼓妖" interpretation.
- Works with Zhu Bo to convert victory in the title dispute into punishment of Shi Dan.
- Frames Wang Mang's prior Da Sima status as aggravating evidence.
- Seeks removal of Wang Mang's title and fief after pardon removes execution from reach.
- Fails because Wang Zhengjun's senior authority protects Wang Mang from full reduction.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 579 and Hanji 580. Hanji 579 supports Zhao Xuan's appointment, omen exposure, and Shi Dan memorial, but not his full career or exact memorial text. Hanji 580 supports the failed Wang Mang attack but not the full political history of the Wang clan. The page does not settle whether Zhao Xuan's name should be normalized with another transmitted form.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 579's appointment, "鼓妖" warning, title-settlement, and Shi Dan punishment layers before Hanji 580's Wang Mang attack.
