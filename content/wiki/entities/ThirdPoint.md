# Third Point

Third Point is the investment firm led by [[DanLoeb|Dan Loeb]] in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-dan-loeb-the-lost-art-of-short-selling-and-why-stock-picking-is-back-41552605]]. The source describes the early firm as centered on [[EventDrivenInvesting|event-driven investing]] in takeovers, spin-offs, bankruptcies, privatizations, demutualizations, and risk arbitrage, where dislocation, opacity, timing, and incentives could create alpha.

Loeb says the platform later broadened into the main hedge fund, equity long-short, structured credit, high yield, CLOs, private credit, direct lending, workouts, insurance-related investment-grade assets, and venture investing. In the source, that expansion mirrors the strategic shift from isolated transaction complexity toward [[StockPicking|stock picking]], [[ShortSelling|short selling]], thematic judgment, technology literacy, and [[ManagementQualityAsInvestmentEdge|management assessment]].
