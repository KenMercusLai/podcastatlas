## Overview

[[ChenGuiDuliaoGeneralLateHan|陈龟]] is a Huan-era frontier official whose known wiki profile is source-bounded to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-844-ruhe-liyu-bubaizhidi-lsp49uw8atyiufrf-ryo-9ctos5z|Hanji 844]]. He appears as a former Duliao general caught between frontier service, an old grievance with [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]], and [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]'s refusal or inability to answer his anti-Liang memorial.

## Current Profile

Chen Gui's profile is primarily a political-danger case rather than a full career biography. After conflict with Liang Ji, he is accused of seeking fame at state expense, recalled from the frontier, and replaced by [[ZhongHaoLateHan|种暠]]. When summoned again to serve as shangshu, he memorializes Emperor Huan with charges against Liang, receives no visible action, and dies after a seven-day hunger strike.

## Key Characteristics

- Chen Gui is presented as a frontier official whose prior military or border actions already put him in conflict with Liang Ji.
- His danger comes from a center where accusation by a dominant outer-relative can outweigh official service.
- His final memorial makes him an anti-Liang witness before the later 159 CE anti-Liang sequence becomes executable.
- His death by hunger strike turns failed remonstrance into a marker of court paralysis and personal protest.
- He must be kept distinct from [[ChenGui|陈珪]], the later Xuzhou adviser with a separate Three Kingdoms-era profile.

## Evidence

- Frontier office and replacement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-844-ruhe-liyu-bubaizhidi-lsp49uw8atyiufrf-ryo-9ctos5z|Hanji 844]] says Chen Gui is recalled after Liang Ji's accusation and that Zhong Hao succeeds him as Duliao general.
- Anti-Liang witness role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-844-ruhe-liyu-bubaizhidi-lsp49uw8atyiufrf-ryo-9ctos5z|Hanji 844]] presents Chen Gui's memorial as a direct list of Liang's misconduct addressed to Emperor Huan.
- Court paralysis and death: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-844-ruhe-liyu-bubaizhidi-lsp49uw8atyiufrf-ryo-9ctos5z|Hanji 844]] says Huan receives the memorial without acting and that Chen Gui then dies after seven days of hunger strike.
- Identity distinction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-844-ruhe-liyu-bubaizhidi-lsp49uw8atyiufrf-ryo-9ctos5z|Hanji 844]] uses an ASR-like 陈归 form, but the received passage supports 陈龟; this page is separate from [[ChenGui|陈珪]].

## Qualifications

The page is bounded to one podcast source note and should not infer a complete biography from this episode alone. The source presents the moral and political function of Chen Gui's death clearly, but it does not develop his earlier career, exact memorial wording, or all institutional procedures around his recall.
