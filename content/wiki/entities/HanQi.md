# 韩琦 / Han Qi

Han Qi is the senior official addressed by [[SuZhe|苏辙]] in [[ShangShumiHanTaiweiShu|《上枢密韩太尉书》]] in [[164-guwen-de-liliang-qing-shou-xia-zhe-fen-qiannian-wei-bian-de-shenqing-dongjian-he-yongqi-940236576]]. The episode introduces him as the powerful figure whom the young Su Zhe wanted to meet as part of expanding experience and entering the official world.

The source also says Han Qi later persuaded [[SongRenzong|宋仁宗]] to delay a special examination so Su Zhe could participate after illness. That makes him part of the episode's broader claim that patronage, literary recognition, and official opportunity were intertwined in Northern Song elite careers.
