# Lucasfilm

Lucasfilm is the film and technology company Disney acquires in [[disney-the-renaissance-and-the-empire-1]]. The source connects it to both sides of the episode: [[GeorgeLucas]]'s computer graphics group is part of the prehistory of [[Pixar]], and the later Lucasfilm acquisition adds Star Wars, ILM, and Skywalker Sound to [[TheWaltDisneyCompany]]'s IP empire.

The episode treats Lucasfilm as valuable but more complicated than [[MarvelEntertainment|Marvel]]. Its IP is culturally deep, but the Star Wars slate is presented as harder to coordinate into a durable post-acquisition film flywheel.

[[aodesai-ba-baomihua-zhuangjin-teluoyi-muma-haolaiwu-dapian-weihe-zhadui-dingzhi-baomihuatong-1004599628]] adds a small merchandise validation case from the [[StarWars|Star Wars]] side of Lucasfilm IP. The source says an R2-D2 popcorn bucket helped show theaters that fan-recognizable objects could become [[CustomMoviePopcornBuckets|custom movie popcorn buckets]] with high willingness to pay.
