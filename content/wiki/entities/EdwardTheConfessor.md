# Edward the Confessor

## Overview
Edward the Confessor appears in the wiki as the English king whose succession politics open the [[BayeuxTapestry|Bayeux Tapestry]]'s account of the Norman Conquest.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Edward as the figure who sends [[HaroldGodwinson]] to Normandy, setting up Harold's apparent oath to [[WilliamTheConqueror]] and the later conflict over the English crown.

## Key Characteristics
- Succession-frame figure in the source's Bayeux Tapestry narrative.
- English monarchy reference point whose decision links Harold, William, Normandy, and England.
- Historical-memory node because the tapestry turns succession into visual political argument.
