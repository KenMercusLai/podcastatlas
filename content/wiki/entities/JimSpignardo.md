# Jim Spignardo

Jim Spignardo is the [[DataScienceWithSam]] guest in [[ep-48-from-pilots-to-productivity-what-it-actually-takes-to-make-ai-work-in-the-enterprise]]. The episode identifies him as Director of Cloud Strategy and AI Enablement at [[Proarc]], with a background spanning technical support, training, networking, servers, data centers, cloud, cybersecurity, and AI strategy.

His core contribution is an enterprise operating view of AI. He argues that organizations do not get value from AI simply by buying tools such as [[MicrosoftCopilot|Microsoft 365 Copilot]]; they need business-aligned use cases, [[AIDataReadiness]], [[AIGovernanceAndCompliance]], [[AIAdoptionBaselineMeasurement]], ownership, training, champions, and practical transition from pilot to operations.

Jim also frames [[ShadowAI]] as a useful warning signal. Employees who use unapproved AI tools are not only creating risk; they may be showing where the organization's formal systems fail to handle dull, draining, or distracting work.
