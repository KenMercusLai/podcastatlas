# 管城 / Guancheng (Warring States)

管城 / Guancheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-103-1-simaqian-kanzhong-de-xinlingjun-suan-shi-xiaoren-ma-lvty1pdnibvqpk0m16kkxzw4t-ck]] as the city [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] wants to take after defeating [[MengAo|蒙敖]]. The episode treats Guancheng as part of the remaining Qin pressure on [[WeiState|魏国]] after the immediate battlefield victory.

Its importance is geographical. The source says Qin already controls [[Xingze|荥泽]] southeast of Guancheng and [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]] to the northwest of Xingze, meaning Qin may threaten [[Daliang|大梁]] by controlling water routes. Guancheng therefore turns a successful anti-Qin coalition victory into a follow-on city-control problem.

Guancheng is also the site behind the source's ethical conflict. [[SuGao|苏高]] refuses to attack because his son is defending the city for [[QinState|秦国]], while [[AnlingJun|安陵君]] refuses to surrender Su Gao to Xinlingjun. The city therefore links military necessity to [[StateSurvivalKinshipDutyConflict|国存亡与父子君臣义冲突]].
