# 邓羲 / Deng Xi (late Han)

## Overview
邓羲 / Deng Xi (late Han) is a Jingzhou adviser whose rejected warning under [[LiuBiao|刘表]] is followed by renewed office under [[CaoCao|曹操]].

## Current Profile
Hanji 1027 presents Deng Xi as Liu Biao's governing aide who warns that accepting captured-court titles while remaining close to [[YuanShao|袁绍]] will turn [[Jingzhou|荆州]] into an exposed buffer. Liu Biao dismisses the warning, after which Deng Xi falls silent and withdraws on illness grounds.

Hanji 1095 supplies the later reversal. After Cao Cao takes Jingzhou, he appoints Deng Xi palace attendant. The two episodes together make Deng Xi a failed-feedback case under one regime and an absorbed local adviser under its successor.

## Key Characteristics
- Warns Liu Biao against unstable double alignment.
- Withdraws after his remonstrance is rejected.
- Marks a broken feedback channel inside Liu Biao's government.
- Receives appointment as palace attendant after Cao Cao takes Jingzhou.
