# 邓羲 / Deng Xi (late Han)

邓羲 / Deng Xi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1027-jingzhou-zhizhu-liubiao-weihe-nan-cheng-bazhu-ll-ebguqllsf-5ycvpdogihzn3lj|Hanji 1027]] as [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s governing aide who warns against maintaining tribute to the Li-Guo-controlled court while also staying close to [[YuanShao|袁绍]]. The episode treats his warning as more than formal loyalism: he sees that Liu Biao's double alignment can turn [[Jingzhou|荆州]] into a passive buffer rather than an active power.

His resignation is important because it marks a failed feedback channel inside Liu Biao's regime. When Liu Biao dismisses the warning, Deng Xi falls silent and leaves on illness grounds; the source reads this as one reason later Jingzhou talent such as [[PangTong|庞统]] and [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] may seek a more usable patron through [[LiuBei|刘备]].
