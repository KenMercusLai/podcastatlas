# Tonle Sap Lake / 洞里萨湖

Tonle Sap Lake / 洞里萨湖 is the hydrological hinge in [[ep267-tafang-meigonghe-yuji-hunfeiyi-yu-shuili-diguo-lkq2vxaatn4wxue7y5q1fc3-8yfk]]'s account of [[Cambodia|柬埔寨]] and [[Angkor|吴哥]]. The episode explains that rainy-season [[MekongRiver|Mekong]] flow pushes water into the lake, expanding it several times over, before water flows back out after the rainy season.

The source uses this flood pulse to connect [[WetlandAdaptation]], [[FisheriesResourceManagement]], and [[AngkorHydraulicEmpire]]. Seasonal expansion brings nutrients and fish resources, while Angkor's water system stored and redirected rainy-season water for dry-season rice agriculture.
