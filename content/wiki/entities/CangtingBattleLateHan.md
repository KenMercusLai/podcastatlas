# 苍亭之战 / Cangting Battle (late Han)

## Overview
苍亭之战 / Cangting Battle enters the wiki through Hanji 1054 as the post-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] blow by which [[CaoCao|曹操]] keeps pressure on [[YuanShao|袁绍]] before Yuan can recover.

## Current Profile
The battle matters less as a standalone tactical set piece than as a tempo marker. [[XunYu|荀彧]] argues that Cao Cao should continue attacking Yuan Shao while Yuan's morale and organization remain damaged after Guandu, instead of moving far south against [[LiuBei|刘备]]. Cao accepts the sequencing, advances along the Yellow River in the fourth month, attacks Yuan's Cangting force, and wins before returning to [[Xudu|许都]] in the ninth month.

## Key Characteristics
- Post-Guandu continuation rather than an isolated new war.
- Converts Yuan Shao's recent defeat into further loss of recovery space.
- Shows Cao Cao accepting Xun Yu's priority judgment over the tempting Liu Bei target.
- Anchors a [[PostVictoryThreatSequencing|胜后威胁排序]] case: the wounded main rival remains more urgent than a secondary threat.

## Qualifications
The current source gives only a compact campaign account and host interpretation. It does not reconstruct order of battle, losses, exact terrain, or all source traditions. This page also keeps [[CangtingFordLateHan|仓亭津 / Cangting Ford]] distinct: that earlier page is the Yanzhou crossing where [[ChengYu|程昱]] blocks [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]], not this post-Guandu battle against Yuan Shao.
