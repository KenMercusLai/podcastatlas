# 万达集团 / Wanda Group

Wanda Group appears in [[vol-264-ba-shijiebei-zuowei-fangfa-999416048]] as part of the Chinese sponsor history around the [[FIFAWorldCup]]. The episode says Wanda signed a 15-year agreement with [[FIFA]] in 2016 and entered the highest sponsorship tier, marking a later stage after [[YingliGreenEnergy]] became the first Chinese World Cup sponsor in 2010.

For the 2026 tournament, the episode says the highest-tier Chinese position previously associated with Wanda is now occupied by [[Lenovo]]. That transition helps the source show how Chinese World Cup sponsorship moved from symbolic first entries and conglomerate visibility toward technology and overseas-business alignment.

[[vol-270-da-shi-dai-liu-zai-le-ta-de-shi-dai-1006063835]] adds Wanda Group as a shopping-center expansion context for [[FoodRepublic|大食代]]. The source says many new first- or second-tier-city Food Republic stores followed Wanda Plaza openings, making Wanda an example of how mall rollout could distribute a [[FoodCourtSecondLandlord|food-court second-landlord]] model during China's commercial-real-estate growth phase.

[[shangye-xiaoyang-47-jianshen-zuotijia-de-xinhuan-hyrox-daodi-you-shenme-huchenghe-1008179335]] adds Wanda Group through [[InfrontSportsMedia|Infront Sports & Media]], which the episode says Wanda acquired in 2015. In this branch, Wanda is not the direct event case; it is part of the sports-marketing ownership context that helps explain how [[HYROX]] could build sponsor, gym, and event networks quickly.
