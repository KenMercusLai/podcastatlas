# 蒋奇 / Jiang Qi (late Han)

## Overview
蒋奇 / Jiang Qi is a proposed Yuan-side protective commander in the pre-[[WuchaoRaidLateHan|乌巢]] logistics stage of the [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]]. He appears in [[JuShou|沮授]]'s warning that [[YuanShao|袁绍]] should send an additional force to patrol around [[ChunyuQiong|淳于琼]]'s grain convoy.

## Current Profile
Jiang Qi's current role is counterfactual but concrete. Ju Shou does not merely warn that supplies are vulnerable; he names Jiang Qi as the commander who should be assigned to an outer protective layer around the convoy.

Yuan Shao's refusal leaves the convoy protection thinner than Ju Shou thinks necessary. Jiang Qi therefore functions in the wiki as the unchosen protective option that reveals the practical specificity of Ju Shou's logistics warning before Cao Cao moves on the Wuchao supply target.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan-side commander named as a proposed convoy-protection actor.
- Part of Ju Shou's practical logistics warning before Wuchao.
- Evidence that the Yuan camp had a specific defensive alternative available.
- Source-bounded figure whose importance is tied to an unadopted assignment.
