# Eton College

## Overview

Eton College is the royal school founded by Henry VI in 1440 and modeled on [[WinchesterCollege|Winchester College]].

## Current Profile

[[368-the-history-behind-hogwarts-ancient-schools-and-revolting-students-glt6783238013]] presents Eton as an early case of royal prestige intensifying a school model that already combined charitable purpose with elite access. Henry VI recruited Winchester headmaster William Waynflete to help establish the College of Our Lady Mary at Eton near Windsor. Its survival after Edward IV attempted to close it also illustrates how alumni influence and institutional networks could defend a foundation even when royal favor shifted.

## Key Characteristics

- Founded by Henry VI in 1440 under royal and religious patronage.
- Adapted Winchester's feeder-school model and recruited its headmaster William Waynflete.
- Gained prestige through proximity to Windsor and association with the crown.
- Survived Edward IV's closure attempt through old-boy lobbying, though it lost lands.
- Later exemplified limited teaching hours, oversized classes, prefect authority, and hazardous hazing in the episode's account.

## Evidence

- Foundation and model: [[368-the-history-behind-hogwarts-ancient-schools-and-revolting-students-glt6783238013]] links Henry VI, Waynflete, Winchester, and the 1440 foundation.
- Institutional survival: [[368-the-history-behind-hogwarts-ancient-schools-and-revolting-students-glt6783238013]] attributes survival under Edward IV partly to former pupils' lobbying while noting land confiscation.
- Later school life: [[368-the-history-behind-hogwarts-ancient-schools-and-revolting-students-glt6783238013]] describes short teaching days, very large classes, prefect discipline, junior service, and violent initiation practices.

## Qualifications

The profile derives from one conversational history episode. The precise role of alumni lobbying, scale and representativeness of classroom conditions, and hazing anecdotes remain source-scoped rather than a complete institutional judgment across Eton's history.

## What Changed

- Created a profile joining Eton's Winchester-derived design to royal prestige and alumni influence.
- Added qualified evidence about later weak supervision and pupil hierarchy.
