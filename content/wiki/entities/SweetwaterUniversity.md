# Sweetwater University

Sweetwater University is the sales-engineer training system described in [[sweetwater-chuck-surack-how-a-customer-service-strategy-built-a-billion-dollar-online-pro-audio-and-music-company-50c2b5e3-3bc3-4039-a92d-26228b8ac908]]. The source says [[Sweetwater]] sales engineers complete a 13-week, full-day training program taught by many internal teachers before speaking with customers.

The program matters because it turns [[ChuckSurack]]'s founder-led service habits into an institutional [[SalesEngineerModel]]. Training, role play, product knowledge, conversation practice, and technical confidence help Sweetwater make [[RelationshipLedSales]] repeatable. The source frames this as part of a [[ServiceLedRetailMoat]] because customers can call a dedicated, knowledgeable person rather than rely only on product pages or lowest-price listings.
