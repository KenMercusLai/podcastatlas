# 李瓶儿 / Li Ping'er

李瓶儿 is one of the three title-name figures of [[JinPingMei|《金瓶梅》]]. [[fanwai-15-banlatte-jiang-jinpingmei-la-erqie-haishi-luse-de-991508433]] does not yet tell her full plot, but it explains that the title can be read both symbolically and as a direct triad of [[PanJinlian|潘金莲]], Li Ping'er, and [[PangChunmei|庞春梅]].

In this source, Li Ping'er is therefore mainly a structural promise. Her name signals that the novel is not only about [[XimenQing|西门庆]] or one scandal, but about a wider household and social network whose later arcs will make [[HouseholdAsSocialWeb]] and [[UrbanEverydayFiction]] more visible.
