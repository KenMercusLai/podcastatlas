# Liu Geqing / 刘戈青

## Overview

刘戈青 appears as a Taiwan-born university student who joined the Military Statistics Bureau from strong anti-Japanese commitment and directly participated in the assassination of [[ChenLu|陈箓]].

## Current Profile

The source identifies Liu as the direct attacker within the Shanghai operation organized through [[WangTianmu|Wang Tianmu]]'s network. His role gives the killing an individual-volunteer dimension alongside its intelligence-agency planning: personal anti-Japanese conviction was converted into clandestine lethal action against a collaborationist minister.

## Key Characteristics

- Taiwan-born university student in the source's account.
- Entered Nationalist intelligence from an avowed anti-Japanese commitment.
- Served as a direct attacker in the Chen Lu operation.
- Joined personal motivation to an organized “traitor-elimination” campaign.
