# 赵玉顺 / Zhao Yushun

## Overview

赵玉顺 is a Chinese rural documentary creator and co-founder of [[YuzhenJishi|玉真记事]] whose work follows farmers, village elders, children, crops, and changing land relations.

## Current Profile

The bounded source presents Zhao as a journalism graduate whose public-interest reporting was blocked early in his newspaper career. After photographing and recording fishing-village life on Hainan's Xidao, unstable Beijing employment, and a return south, he and [[YuanZhengzheng|袁征征]] built an independent field practice around places and people overlooked by metropolitan media.

His current project joins reporting with long-duration historical preservation. Economic survival remains precarious, but independence lets him pursue a version of the journalistic ideal that he could not sustain inside a newspaper.

## Key Characteristics

- Journalism-trained interviewer oriented toward public-interest and ordinary-person reporting.
- Co-creator of a village-and-town documentary practice conducted outside a large media institution.
- Uses crops, land, schools, pensions, and personal testimony to connect structural change to daily life.
- Treats repeated visits and decade-long recording as a way to observe change rather than capture one-off spectacle.
- Holds that future archival value can justify work whose immediate platform reach is uncertain.
