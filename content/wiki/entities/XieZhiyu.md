# 谢治宇 / Xie Zhiyu

Xie Zhiyu appears in [[175-gongmu-jijin-erjibao-jizhi-de-baotuan-yu-gelie-zhihou-lv20uprxrcw0tde5ugohu29tmlg6]] as a fund-manager voice warning against overpaying for currently tight AI-infrastructure links. The source uses his second-quarter report to add a risk-control counterpoint inside the broader technology-growth rally.

His argument is that the market can become dangerous when it gives very high forward prices and valuation multiples to scarce upstream segments. He compares the risk to earlier expansion cycles, where stock-price turning points can come before the visible fundamental price peak.

## Key Claims
- Xie Zhiyu's view qualifies [[AIEquityValuationRisk]] by asking whether tight AI links are priced for too much future scarcity.
- The source says he still kept AI and broad technology exposure, but preferred longer-demand durability, stable competition, lower-positioned technology stocks, innovation drugs, and strong traditional leaders.
- His emphasis on falling infrastructure cost connects AI investment to downstream adoption: excessive upstream price increases can hurt the applications that justify the capex.
- In the source, he expects third-quarter rebalancing after an extreme second-quarter split.
