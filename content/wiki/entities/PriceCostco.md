# PriceCostco

PriceCostco is the combined company formed by the 1993 merger of [[Costco]] and [[PriceClub]] in [[costco-1]]. The episode describes it as close to a merger of equals, with Costco shareholders receiving 52% and Price Club shareholders receiving 48%, and says the combined business had about 200 stores and roughly $16 billion in revenue.

The merger matters because it united the original Price Club model with Costco's faster scaling and helped prevent [[SamsClub|Sam's Club]] from dominating the warehouse-club category.
