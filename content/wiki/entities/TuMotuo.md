# 土摩托 / Tu Motuo

土摩托 / Tu Motuo is the guest in [[ep256-ai-shidai-ziyou-yizhi-hai-cunzai-ma-lk9aci8oqnwerk26jy683nbdddcx]], where he discusses a long reporting project on [[FreeWill|自由意志]]. The episode presents him as a translator between philosophy, religion, neuroscience, biology, and AI rather than as a single-discipline specialist.

His main contribution is to reframe "agent" as [[BiologicalAgency|能动体]] rather than only "智能体." This lets the conversation move from ancient [[CausalDeterminism|determinism]] and [[LibetExperimentInterpretation|neural readiness-potential experiments]] into [[MeaningAsEvolvedFunction|meaning]], [[EmbodiedIntelligence|embodied intelligence]], and [[AIFreeWillRisk|AI free-will risk]].
