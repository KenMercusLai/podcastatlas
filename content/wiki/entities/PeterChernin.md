# Peter Chernin

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-spencer-pratt-on-fixing-la-wildfires-homelessness-corruption-the-fight-to-take-it-back-41236810]] adds [[PeterChernin|Peter Chernin]] as the media-industry figure [[SpencerPratt|Spencer Pratt]] cites when discussing what a [[LosAngeles|Los Angeles]] mayor can and cannot do for production. Pratt says Chernin told him a mayor cannot fix the whole Hollywood economy, but can help independent filmmakers and production activity.

In the source, Chernin's role is not biographical; he is used to narrow the policy claim. Local government can reduce fees, improve public safety, coordinate city resources, and make neighborhoods workable for crews even if national or international production economics remain outside city control.
