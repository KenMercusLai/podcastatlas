# Gordon Food Service

Gordon Food Service is the food distributor that helped [[Toast]] expand beyond Boston in [[toast-aman-narang-how-a-long-wait-for-the-dinner-check-launched-a-2-billion-business-6eca81bc-b0e1-47e2-a87d-f3b243e76a97]]. The source says [[StevePapa]] connected the Toast team with GFS around early 2014, supporting launches in markets such as Miami and Chicago.

The episode uses Gordon Food Service as a distribution bridge. Because restaurant sales, installation, and service had to happen in person, the partnership helped Toast reach more customers before its own national sales and support organization had fully matured.
