# 田和 / Tian He

田和 / Tian He appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-2-lvaqlgyccrugfo0l-wuwf1mt1kcm]] as the [[QiState|齐国]] grandee whom the Zhou court formally recognizes as a ruler in 386 BCE, [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]]'s sixteenth year. The host notes that the decision had already been accepted earlier and that Tian He had already died by the time this formal notice appears, so the point is less personal action than completed recognition.

The page is source-scoped. It anchors the episode's claim that annalistic recognition can matter even when the practical power shift and the actor's life no longer line up neatly.
