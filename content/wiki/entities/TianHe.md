# 田和 / Tian He

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-3-lsbx8l7wxeioae2lwuwkkkutpdwn]] gives Tian He the active displacement role behind [[TianFamilyReplacementOfQi|田氏代齐]]. The source says he moves [[QiKangGong|齐康公]] to a coastal city, leaves him tax revenue for ancestral offerings, and then meets [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] and other lords' representatives at Zhuoze to seek help becoming a recognized lord.

The same source frames Tian He as the beneficiary of a precedent. After [[ThreeJinVassalRecognition|三晋受封]], the Zhou court has already accepted one formal break with older polity hierarchy, making Tian He's recognition easier to request and easier for [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]] to approve.

田和 / Tian He appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-2-lvaqlgyccrugfo0l-wuwf1mt1kcm]] as the [[QiState|齐国]] grandee whom the Zhou court formally recognizes as a ruler in 386 BCE, [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]]'s sixteenth year. The host notes that the decision had already been accepted earlier and that Tian He had already died by the time this formal notice appears, so the point is less personal action than completed recognition.

The page is source-scoped. It anchors the episode's claim that annalistic recognition can matter even when the practical power shift and the actor's life no longer line up neatly.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl]] continues the result of that transition. With [[QiKangGong|齐康公]]'s death, the source treats Jiang-line Qi as ended, while the Tian line continues through [[TianQiHuanGong|Tian-family Qi Huan Gong]] and [[TianYingqi|田英齐]].
