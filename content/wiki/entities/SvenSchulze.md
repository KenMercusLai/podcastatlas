# Sven Schulze

## Overview

Sven Schulze is the [[ChristianDemocraticUnionGermany|CDU]] premier of [[SaxonyAnhalt|Saxony-Anhalt]] in the source and the incumbent whose government is destabilized by [[AlternativeForGermany|AfD]]'s 44% showing.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Schulze as a weakened incumbent rather than as the central protagonist. The CDU vote roughly halved to 17%, some voices called for his resignation, and yet state law may let him remain acting premier if the parliament cannot elect a successor.

Schulze therefore becomes the person attached to [[GermanCaretakerStalemate]]: AfD's victory creates pressure for change, but the absence of a clear majority or acceptable coalition could preserve the incumbent in a caretaker role.

## Key Characteristics

- CDU state premier of Saxony-Anhalt in the episode.
- Incumbent weakened by AfD's unexpectedly large result and the CDU's reported fall to 17%.
- Acting-premier fallback if the state parliament cannot elect a new minister-president.
- Figure showing how electoral shock can coexist with institutional continuity.
