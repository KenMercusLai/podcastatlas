# Detroit

Detroit appears in [[outsider-chance-ending-sudans-proxy-war-6a75b3695d765781a2b1b132]] as the [[Michigan]] side of the [[GordieHoweBridge]] and [[AmbassadorBridge]] crossings with [[WindsorOntario|Windsor, Ontario]]. The segment treats the Detroit-Windsor corridor as one of the most important trade links between the [[UnitedStates]] and [[Canada]].

The source uses Detroit less as a city profile than as a node in cross-border logistics. The [[GordieHoweBridge]] is framed as saving time, fuel, idling cost, and truck wear even while the project becomes a political symbol in [[CrossBorderInfrastructureTradeTension]].
