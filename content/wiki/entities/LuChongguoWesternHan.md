## Overview

路充国 / Lu Chongguo is a Western Han envoy presented as the high-status representative sent to return a dead Xiongnu noble's remains, carry substantial funeral compensation, and continue negotiations. The chanyu accepts the material offering but detains him.

## Current Profile

Lu Chongguo enters the episode after Xiongnu says it will speak candidly only with a Han notable. Han marks him as sufficiently senior by giving him a two-thousand-shi seal and sends him with the noble's remains and funeral goods. His rank and conciliatory mission are intended to repair trust after an accidental death in Chang'an.

The repair fails. The chanyu treats the noble's death as a Han killing, retains the goods, and prevents Lu Chongguo from returning. He therefore becomes evidence that high status, explanation, compensation, and respectful mortuary handling cannot by themselves resolve an adversarial attribution of blame.

## Key Characteristics

- High-status envoy selected in response to Xiongnu's demand for a Han notable.
- Carries the dead noble's remains and substantial funeral value as a conciliatory package.
- Represents Han's attempt to explain an accidental death and continue talks.
- Is detained despite the mission's rank signaling and material accommodation.
- Illustrates how blame attribution can override procedural repair measures.

## Qualifications

The supplied note writes “陆充国,” normalized here to the received name 路充国. The current source does not cover his full detention, eventual return, office history, personal choices, or the exact chronology and value units of the funeral mission. It also does not establish whether the chanyu sincerely believed murder had occurred or used the death as a diplomatic pretext.

## What Changed

- Created the canonical 路充国 entity and normalized the supplied note's “陆充国” transcript form.
- Established high-status mortuary diplomacy followed by detention as his current evidence-backed profile.
