# Eileen Guo

## Overview
Eileen Guo is a technology reporter who appears in the wiki through an investigation with MIT Technology Review and The Times of San Diego into deaths near U.S. southern-border surveillance towers.

## Current Profile
Guo's contribution is investigative and systems-oriented. She separates camera range from actual terrain visibility and refuses to collapse uncertain cases into one cause: a death may reflect failed detection, failed classification, an unmonitored alert, a tactical delay, or a response that came too late. She also identifies missing agency data as an accountability failure because it prevents evaluation of which stage broke down.

## Key Characteristics
- Investigative reporter focused here on the operational consequences of surveillance technology.
- Uses topographic analysis alongside interviews with former agents and humanitarian responders.
- Distinguishes sensor coverage, model classification, human attention, decision priorities, and response time.
- Treats both undetected people and detected-but-unassisted people as systemic failures requiring different diagnoses.
- Emphasizes the limits created by incomplete CBP records and discovered-remains data.
