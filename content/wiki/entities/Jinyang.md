# 晋阳 / Jinyang

晋阳 / Jinyang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-155-2-zhuan-gei-ni-piqi-da-de-pengyou-weiwang-panhan-jing-shi-liubang-ai-maren-ls5nvunmhakvefd2oxn4zdyoob4l|Hanji 155-2]] as the place [[HanXin|韩信]] occupies after defeating the Dai forces around [[EyuBattle|阏与]]. The episode treats Jinyang as the immediate administrative hinge for setting up [[TaiyuanWarringStates|太原郡]].

Jinyang matters because the northern campaign is not only a raid. Once Han Xin takes the place, the Han side can convert the victory into a commandery base while continuing toward the Zhao-front problem involving [[ZhangEr|张耳]], [[ChenYu|陈馀]], and [[ZhaoXie|赵歇]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-174-liubang-weihe-dafeng-zijide-chouren-1-lnzzvpieqtgatmvgogz3msyhs60u|Hanji 174]] returns to Jinyang as [[HanWangXin|韩王信]]'s initial capital after Liu Bang moves him from the old Han interior to the Taiyuan / Han frontier kingdom. Han Wang Xin argues that Jinyang is too far from the Xiongnu-facing edge, so he asks to move the capital to [[Mayi|马邑]].
