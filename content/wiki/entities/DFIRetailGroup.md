# DFI Retail Group

## Overview
DFI Retail Group is the retailer the source says will take control of [[Starbucks]] franchise operations spanning seven Asian markets and more than 1,100 stores.

## Current Profile
The episode supplies DFI as a regional operating-control counterpart rather than a full company history. The transfer extends the wiki's restaurant-localization theme beyond China: a global brand may retain brand identity while moving multi-market franchise control toward an Asian retail operator.

## Key Characteristics
- Regional retail operator presented as a Starbucks franchise-control partner.
- Reported scope covers seven Asian markets.
- Reported operating footprint exceeds 1,100 Starbucks stores.
