# Bob Chapek

Bob Chapek is the Disney executive who succeeds [[BobIger]] in the COVID and early [[DisneyPlus|Disney+]] period in [[disney-the-renaissance-and-the-empire-1]]. The source presents him as a leader chosen for a calmer operating environment just as parks shutdowns, streaming losses, internal reorganization, and political conflict made the job more difficult.

The episode uses Chapek mainly as a contrast case for [[StrategyFollowsStructure]] and [[StreamingContentTreadmill]]. Reorganizing around streaming did not by itself solve the economics of subscriber growth, churn, content cost, park experience, and brand governance, and Disney's board eventually brings Iger back.
