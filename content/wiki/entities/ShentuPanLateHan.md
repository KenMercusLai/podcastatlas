# 申屠蟠 / Shentu Pan (late Han)

申屠蟠 / Shentu Pan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-949-lishishang-de-dongzhuo-daodi-duo-canbao-lk6idogwmc54nk1sdfc5wonyvgbb|Hanji 949]] as the scholar who refuses [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s summons while other famous men are being pulled into the court. The source presents his response as quiet resistance: others urge him to comply, but he remains unmoved and Dong Zhuo cannot easily convert anger into punishment.

The episode builds Shentu Pan's refusal from earlier moral conduct. At fifteen, he advocates for Gou Yu and helps reduce her death sentence; after his parents die, he avoids wine and meat for more than a decade; while studying at the imperial academy, he carries Wang Ziju's body home after Wang dies and refuses official assistance. These stories make his later avoidance of office a source-scoped extension of [[MoralizedShiIdentity|士的道德化身份]] rather than simple passivity.

Shentu Pan also repeatedly rejects ordinary appointment channels before Dong Zhuo. The source lists refusals of local summons, [[HuangWanLateHan|黄琬]], [[HeJin|何进]], [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]], and finally Dong Zhuo. In the source's argument, survival and principle overlap: refusing a violent powerholder preserves his moral reputation and avoids becoming decoration for [[CoercedTalentLegitimacy|胁迫征士合法性]].
