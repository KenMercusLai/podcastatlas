## Overview

庞德公 / Pang Degong is a Jingzhou scholar whose appraisal helps make [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] and [[PangTong|庞统]] visible within a reputation-sensitive elite network.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1083-1084 present Pang Degong as a respected evaluator in the Jingzhou scholar field. Hanji 1084 emphasizes Zhuge Liang's deferential visits and the appraisal chain; Hanji 1083 adds his refusal of [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s recruitment and early recognition of [[PangTong|庞统]]. His praise does not itself create strategic ability, but it reduces uncertainty around lesser-known talent and gives later recommendation by [[SimaHuiLateHan|司马徽]] a credible social foundation.

## Key Characteristics

- Occupies an evaluator role in the Jingzhou scholar network.
- Receives Zhuge Liang's repeated respectful visits in the episode's account.
- Applies elevated reputational labels to Zhuge Liang and Pang Tong.
- Converts private judgment into socially portable recognition.
- Represents endorsement as a complement to preparation rather than a substitute for it.
- Is presented as maintaining distance from office through refusal of Liu Biao's recruitment.

- Evaluator authority: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1084-zhugeliang-ping-shenme-chengwei-sanguo-dingliu-zhezhao-juele-lqfgxebdupqxezz0jewqn3iapqrd|Hanji 1084]] presents Pang Degong as a respected scholar whose judgment carries weight in the relevant elite circle.
- Talent recognition: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1084-zhugeliang-ping-shenme-chengwei-sanguo-dingliu-zhezhao-juele-lqfgxebdupqxezz0jewqn3iapqrd|Hanji 1084]] attributes to him early praise of Zhuge Liang and Pang Tong.
- Independent position and early appraisal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1083-weihe-liubei-zaoqi-kong-you-dingji-zhanli-que-wu-lizu-zhi-di-lkrforfxvqjw-dl1ejvfkjivz3f3|Hanji 1083]] pairs refusal of Liu Biao's recruitment with recognition of Pang Tong before wider local opinion.

## Qualifications

The profile is bounded to two adjacent popular-history episodes. Exact visits, wording, labels, kinship, recruitment circumstances, chronology, and the modern KOL analogy remain source-scoped; endorsement is evidence of contemporary reputation, not independent proof of every later achievement. The source's idealized “邦有道则出、邦无道则隐” reading is an interpretation rather than a complete political biography.

## What Changed

- Established Pang Degong as a distinct Jingzhou evaluator rather than leaving his endorsement implicit on other pages.
- Added recruitment refusal and early Pang Tong recognition while retaining uncertainty about wording and kinship.

## Relationships

- [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] - emerging talent whose reputation Pang Degong helps certify.
- [[PangTong|庞统]] - younger kinsman or associated talent whom the episode places in the same appraisal network.
- [[SimaHuiLateHan|司马徽]] - fellow evaluator whose later recommendation circulates the two reputations more widely.
- [[EliteEndorsementReputationMaking|名士背书式造势]] - mechanism through which evaluator standing reduces uncertainty around talent.
