# 老宝 / Laobao (travel leader)

## Overview
老宝 / Laobao is the [[DaocaorenTravel|稻草人旅行]] senior trip leader interviewed by [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]] about National Day travel pricing, route design, leader work, and guided-versus-independent travel.

## Current Profile
The episode presents 老宝 as both a travel-industry operator and a traveler who can translate behind-the-scenes constraints into usable planning advice. She explains why Golden Week is expensive, how travelers can substitute landscape elements for crowded destination IP, and how travel agencies prepare routes and leaders for high-pressure holiday departures.

Her authority in the source is practical rather than statistical. She contributes professional experience from Daocaoren's route and leader system, but her destination examples, fare observations, and demand judgments remain bounded to the episode's context.

## Key Characteristics
- Senior travel leader who explains peak-holiday tourism from both industry and traveler perspectives.
- Uses element-substitution logic to help travelers avoid famous but crowded scenic IP.
- Treats airfare timing as uncertain and advises psychological price thresholds rather than gambling on last-minute drops.
- Describes leader preparation as research-heavy, detail-oriented, and logistics-sensitive.
- Handles holiday backup work that requires route familiarity, calm judgment, and emergency response.
- Frames guided travel as especially useful for culture-heavy or uncertainty-heavy destinations such as [[Egypt]].
