# Allegra Byron

## Overview

Allegra Byron was the daughter of [[LordByron|Lord Byron / 拜伦]] and [[ClaireClairmont|Claire Clairmont / 克莱尔·克莱尔蒙特]], born in January 1817 and first named Alba by the Shelley circle.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Allegra's short life to expose the human cost beneath Byron's mobile celebrity world. Byron acknowledged the child and renamed her Allegra, but held Claire at an emotional distance; Allegra later lived within his Venetian household and then died young in a convent.

Her death appears alongside the loss of [[PercyShelley|Percy Shelley / 珀西·雪莱]] as part of the collapse of Byron's Italian circle. The source does not provide enough detail to settle the decisions surrounding her upbringing or death.

## Key Characteristics

- Daughter connecting Byron to Claire Clairmont and the Shelley circle.
- Renamed Allegra by Byron after initially being called Alba.
- Child moved through an unstable transnational adult network.
- Early death that intensified the losses around Byron's Italian exile.

## Evidence

- Parentage and naming: [[443-lord-byron-death-of-a-vampire-part-4-glt3247503776]] identifies Claire as her mother and describes the Alba-to-Allegra renaming.
- Venetian household: [[443-lord-byron-death-of-a-vampire-part-4-glt3247503776]] places Allegra among Byron's crowded palazzo household.
- Death and aftermath: [[443-lord-byron-death-of-a-vampire-part-4-glt3247503776]] states that she died young in a convent shortly before the Italian circle broke apart further after Shelley's death.
