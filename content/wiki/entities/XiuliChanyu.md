## Overview

[[XiuliChanyu|休利 / Xiuli]] is the Southern Xiongnu chanyu in Hanji 820's 140 CE revolt layer. The wiki currently treats him as a legitimacy casualty: the episode says he is not part of [[WusiSouthernXiongnu|Wusi]] and [[CheNiuChanyu|Che Niu]]'s plot, yet [[ChenGuiDuliaoGeneralLateHan|Chen Gui]]'s coercion drives him to suicide.

## Current Profile

Hanji 820 presents Xiuli as the recognized chanyu during the Southern Xiongnu revolt. After Wusi and Che Niu attack, Xiuli goes to [[LiangBingLateHan|Liang Bing]] to apologize, and the episode stresses that he is not actually involved in the rebellion. Once Liang Bing is recalled for illness, Chen Gui acts in the Xiongnu command role and forces Xiuli and Xiuli's younger brother, the Left Wise King, to suicide.

Xiuli's death is not described as restoring order. Instead, it unsettles surrendering or potentially loyal Southern Xiongnu groups and helps create the space in which Wusi installs Che Niu as an alternative chanyu.

## Key Characteristics

- Court-recognized Southern Xiongnu chanyu caught between rebels and Han frontier commanders.
- Treated by the episode as uninvolved in the initial revolt.
- Becomes the victim of Chen Gui's unauthorized coercion, not a battlefield opponent.
- His death worsens legitimacy conditions for surrender and succession.

## Qualifications

The transcript uses the form 修立. This page uses 休利 cautiously as the normalized Southern Xiongnu chanyu label for this passage, while keeping the evidence bounded to Hanji 820's account.
