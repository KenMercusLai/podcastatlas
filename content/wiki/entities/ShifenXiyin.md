# 十分吸引 / Shifen Xiyin

十分吸引 is the crossover partner in [[129-huobi-de-benzhi-yiji-huangjin-de-zhenzheng-jiazhi-chuantai-shifen-xiyin-lsjbfttqxf58uk-a4g8-srretkwb]], a [[QizhulouYanBinke|起朱楼宴宾客]] episode where [[ShiLei|时雷]] and [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] discuss money, gold, Bitcoin, central banking, and currency-anchor uncertainty. The episode expands the show's wiki role from an asset-allocation reference into a source for [[MoneyAsFlow]], [[EndogenousMoneyCreation]], [[PaymentClearingNetwork]], [[CurrencyAnchorTransition]], and [[GoldAsCurrencySpareTire]].

十分吸引 previously appeared in [[vol-105-ruhe-panduan-yige-touzi-zuhe-shifou-shihe-ziji-lmaowq8820pa0jyjw6z93b6hstpe]] as Shi Lei's podcast. That source mentions it while discussing complex adaptive systems and attempts to translate [[BridgewaterAssociates|Bridgewater]] all-weather-style thinking into more local asset-allocation language.
