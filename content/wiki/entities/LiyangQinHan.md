# 栎阳 / Liyang (Qin-Han)

栎阳 / Liyang (Qin-Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-149-xichu-bawang-xiangyu-weihe-shijun-2-lpohbb3prsgvokpjipfd7i1ntiwn|Hanji 149 part 2]] as the capital to which [[LiuBang|刘邦]] moves after recovering the Guanzhong operating space. The key is disambiguated because other historical places can be romanized as Liyang.

The source places the move inside Liu Bang's consolidation of [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] and its frontier. After the capital move, Han forces take [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]] and then [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]], while [[XiaoHe|萧何]] and [[HanXin|韩信]] anchor rear governance and military order.

For this wiki, Liyang is a narrow capital-base marker: Liu Bang is no longer merely breaking out of Hanzhong but turning the recovered Qin heartland into an administrative and military base for eastward action.
