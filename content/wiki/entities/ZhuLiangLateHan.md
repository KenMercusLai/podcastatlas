## Overview
祝良 / Zhu Liang is a late-Han official in Hanji 819 whose appointment turns [[LiGuLateHan|李固]]'s southern-frontier strategy into action. The episode presents him as a former Bingzhou inspector from Changsha who becomes Jiuzhen administrator and personally induces mass surrender.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-819-2-ren-gong-bi-4-wanren-tade-quanmou-lingren-paianjuejue-lsslj3y-krnd8h-c8x5pgsqeinff|Hanji 819]] introduces Zhu Liang when Li Gu argues against sending 40,000 troops toward Jiaozhi. Li Gu names Zhu Liang as a trustworthy official whose courage, judgment, and humane authority fit the crisis better than a distant large expedition.

After [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] accepts Li Gu's plan, Zhu Liang is appointed administrator of Jiuzhen. The source's defining scene has him drive into the rebel camp with a single carriage, where his strategy and credibility induce tens of thousands to surrender.

## Key Characteristics
- Li Gu-recommended official selected for frontier pacification capacity.
- Former inspector associated with prior administrative and military trust.
- Jiuzhen administrator appointed as part of a two-person solution with [[ZhangQiaoLateHan|张乔]].
- High-credibility pacifier whose single-carriage entry replaces escalation with surrender.
