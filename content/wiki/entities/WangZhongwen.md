# 王仲闻 / Wang Zhongwen

王仲闻 / Wang Zhongwen enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-3-liqingzhao-zhongnian-fuqi-libie-hejiu-jie-xiangsi-lnzxhrj2iulahccy2-pkm-wphdei|Qinji 131-3]] as the scholar whose Li Qingzhao annotation is cited for a quantitative claim about wine in her ci corpus. The source says his annotated collection includes 57 lyrics, 29 of which involve wine.

The page is source-scoped. Wang Zhongwen matters here less as an independent biography than as the scholarly support behind the episode's claim that [[LiQingzhao|李清照]] is not an incidental drinking poet; wine is structurally frequent in the corpus as presented by the host.
