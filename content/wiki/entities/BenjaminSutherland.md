# Benjamin Sutherland

Benjamin Sutherland appears in [[wake-up-haul-an-ozempic-moment-for-the-brain-6a85798d1ad8716bb6078744]] as the [[TheIntelligence]] voice explaining procrastination research. He frames procrastination as [[ProcrastinationSelfRegulationFailure]] with measurable links to income, health, pain, self-esteem, and depression.

The segment uses him to separate stronger intervention evidence from weaker associations. [[MentalContrastingImplementationIntentions]] and [[ExerciseSelfMasteryForProcrastination]] are presented as plausible tools, while diet correlations and medication evidence are kept more limited.
