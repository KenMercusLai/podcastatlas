# Risk Society / 《风险社会》

## Overview
Risk Society / 《风险社会》 is [[UlrichBeck]]'s book as used by [[episode-227-ershiyishiji-qingchun-wuyu-8211230332-278487]] to explain how modern society distributes hazards and makes individuals experience structural risk personally.

## Current Profile
In this source, the book functions as the sociological base for [[IndividualizedRiskResponsibility]]. Ren Ning uses it to frame a shift from distributing wealth to distributing bad outcomes, then applies that frame to unemployment, housing, career selection, and youth caution.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides the source's main risk-society vocabulary.
- Treats risk distribution as a central feature of modern society.
- Links structural insecurity to personal blame and self-protection.
- Helps explain why caution can grow even when stated political values are progressive.

## Evidence
- Risk-distribution thesis: [[episode-227-ershiyishiji-qingchun-wuyu-8211230332-278487]] summarizes the book as shifting attention from the distribution of goods to the distribution of bads.
- Individualized experience: [[episode-227-ershiyishiji-qingchun-wuyu-8211230332-278487]] uses the book to describe how systemic risks are felt as personal responsibility.
- Youth-culture application: [[episode-227-ershiyishiji-qingchun-wuyu-8211230332-278487]] applies this frame to younger people's heightened caution.
