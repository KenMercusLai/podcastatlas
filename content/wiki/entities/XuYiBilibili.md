# 徐逸 / Xu Yi

Xu Yi / 徐逸 is presented in [[no-214-xunzhao-tonglei-xiaohongshu-bilibili-yiji-wuhuabamen-de-naxie-shequ-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-26-1005977305]] as the anime enthusiast who built MikuFans, later renamed [[Bilibili]]. The episode frames his early work as closer to a hobbyist community than a conventional startup.

In the source, Xu's importance is the founding community layer: early users treated Bilibili as a better-running alternative to [[AcFun]], and some helped with servers, apps, and other practical support before the company professionalized.
