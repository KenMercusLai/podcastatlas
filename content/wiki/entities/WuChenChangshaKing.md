# 吴臣 / Wu Chen (Changsha king)

吴臣 / Wu Chen (Changsha king) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-189-liubang-siqian-zuihou-yibo-lllydj2xyk4cr7b9d2qx0hvfw-dz|Hanji 189]] as the [[ChangshaKingdom|长沙国]] ruler whose envoys lure the fleeing [[YingBu|英布 / 黥布]] by saying they can escape together toward [[Nanyue|南越]].

The page is intentionally disambiguated from [[WuChen|武臣 / Wu Chen]], the anti-Qin Zhao rebel. Hanji 189's Wu Chen is a different person in a Western Han vassal-king setting.

In the episode, Ying Bu trusts the invitation but is killed at Zixiang in Poyang County. Wu Chen's role is therefore narrow but consequential: he helps convert Ying Bu's battlefield defeat into final elimination.
