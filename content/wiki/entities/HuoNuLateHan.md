# 霍奴 / Huo Nu (late Han)

## Overview
霍奴 / Huo Nu (late Han) enters the wiki through Hanji 1070 as the Youzhou rebel paired with [[ZhaoDuLateHan|赵独]]. The source uses him to mark the persistence of local armed disorder after Cao Cao's apparent northern settlement.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1070 says Huo Nu and Zhao Du rebel in Youzhou and kill high local officials. Cao Cao's later campaign against Zhao Du and the connected relief of Kuangping place Huo Nu inside the same post-surrender security problem, though the episode does not give him an independent arc beyond the paired rebellion notice.

## Key Characteristics
- Youzhou rebel named with Zhao Du.
- Involved in the killing of the Youzhou inspector and Zhuo commandery administrator.
- Part of the disorder that forces Cao Cao to keep campaigning after receiving surrenders.
