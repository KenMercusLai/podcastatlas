# 秦昭襄王 / King Zhaoxiang of Qin

秦昭襄王 / King Zhaoxiang of Qin appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-58-zhaowulingwang-ruhe-dazao-qiangguo-lq9mgpib0whh0w-jkv0kxcafbnnz]] as the succession answer after [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] dies without a son. The episode says Qin Wuwang's younger half-brother Ying Ji was then a hostage in [[YanState|燕国]] and was brought back to succeed.

The source keeps him at the threshold of power rather than narrating his reign. His function here is to show how Qin moves from Qin Wuwang's impulsive death into a new branch tied to [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]], who is named as Ying Ji's mother and later political figure.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-59-yichao-junwang-yichaochen-ljq-qkueix0rnzfekzuadicce1bh]] moves him from accession threshold into first political context. The episode has Qin Zhaoxiang Wang send [[XiangShou|向寿]] to administer the newly taken [[YiyangWarringStates|宜阳]] region, then accept [[GanMao|甘茂]]'s proposal to return [[WusuiWarringStates|武遂]] to [[HanState|韩国]]. It also says Qin Wuwang's brothers fight for the throne after the sonless death, and [[WeiRan|魏冉]] uses forceful methods to secure Ying Ji's succession.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-60-zhongguo-di-yi-wei-bachi-chaozheng-de-taihou-jingshi-ta-ltazis9qo0dwvwmzxikk8mmpvosc]] gives his early reign its governing structure. Because he is young, [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]] controls the court and empowers [[WeiRan|魏冉]], who guards [[Xianyang|咸阳]] and suppresses [[YingZhuang|嬴壮]]'s revolt. The episode then follows Qin Zhaoxiang Wang through two diplomatic turns: marriage and alliance repair with [[ChuState|楚国]] at [[Huangji|黄棘]], then repair with [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]] at [[LinjinWarringStates|临晋]] after [[MiHeng|芈横]] kills a Qin official and flees.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-61-shui-shi-lishishang-daner-zui-fei-de-diwang-lvpoaszanascnlrmu2atinlxzmk1]] puts Qin Zhaoxiang Wang opposite [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] in a disguised reconnaissance story. Zhao Wuling Wang enters Qin as if he were a Zhao envoy in order to inspect Qin's mountains and rivers and assess Qin Zhaoxiang Wang personally. Qin Zhaoxiang Wang senses after the meeting that the envoy is not an ordinary minister, but Zhao Wuling Wang has already crossed the border before Qin can detain him.

The source makes Qin Zhaoxiang Wang a target of strategic reading, not merely a young ruler under regency. Zhao Wuling Wang's planned route from Yunzhong and Jiuyuan toward [[Xianyang|咸阳]] also marks Qin's capital as something that could be imagined from beyond the usual [[HanguPass|函谷关]] approach.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-62-ji-er-kuang-ni-jiujiu-de-tui-a-lumch-ibj8m94abjh5nvr3qhiqff]] makes Qin Zhaoxiang Wang the planner or beneficiary of the [[Wuguan|武关]] trap against [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]. Qin invites Chu Huaiwang to a meeting, uses kinship and alliance repair language to lower his guard, then closes Wuguan and takes him to [[Xianyang|咸阳]]. The episode frames Qin's demand for [[WuJunWarringStates|巫郡]] and [[Qianzhong|黔中]] as coercive statecraft backed by a captured ruler.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-63-degao-wangzhong-mengchangjun-shi-weijunzi-lmd0owe3rq-faponbwzhkaigprin]] makes Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's suspicion turn inward toward his foreign chancellor. After hearing that [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]] may privilege [[QiState|齐国]] interests over Qin's, he replaces Mengchangjun with [[LouHuan|楼缓]], imprisons him, and prepares to kill him. The same episode says he releases Mengchangjun after a palace favorite intercedes, then regrets it and sends pursuers too late.

The source also continues Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's Chu pressure. Once [[ChuState|楚国]] reports that [[MiHeng|芈横 / 楚顷襄王]] has already been installed, detaining Chu Huaiwang loses much of its coercive value. Qin then attacks Chu through [[Wuguan|武关]], kills fifty thousand troops, and takes sixteen cities.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-65-wo-chuanyue-qu-gei-langman-de-chuwang-nian-shou-shi-lvyd7pve-tfu4ixsqrbdvhm6g64j]] follows that coercive branch to its reputational cost. [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] escapes from [[Xianyang|咸阳]] but is intercepted after Qin blocks his return route; he dies in Qin the next year, and Qin sends the coffin back to Chu. The same episode says [[QiState|齐国]], [[HanState|韩国]], [[WeiState|魏国]], [[ZhaoState|赵国]], and [[SongState|宋国]] then attack Qin as far as [[EzhiWarringStates|阏氏]], before Qin returns [[WusuiWarringStates|武遂]] to Han and [[FenglingWarringStates|封陵]] to Wei to split the pressure.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-67-zhanguo-mieba-baiqi-de-junshi-caineng-jiujing-ruhe-ljw0lltowxd4qz75v-wcnlkwqskv]] turns his reign into the first [[BaiQi|白起]]-centered military acceleration. Qin first defeats Wei at Xiecheng, then Bai Qi destroys the Han-Wei coalition at [[YiqueBattle|伊阙之战]] and captures [[GongsunXiWarringStates|公孙喜]], while the same annalistic sequence has Qin threaten [[MiHeng|芈横 / 楚顷襄王]] into renewed marriage diplomacy. The page therefore adds Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's court as a setting where victory, intimidation, territorial timing, and alliance repair operate together.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-69-bikan-gudai-diwang-qipa-sifa-da-pandian-lukvjsktg-pgfjhizvypwmdmw-13]] places Qin Zhaoxiang Wang in the anti-Qi environment after [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]] destroys [[SongState|宋国]]. The source says he meets [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]] at Wan and [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] at Zhongyang, then Qin attacks Qi and captures nine cities under [[MengAo|蒙敖]]. Qin's action here is a response to Qi's sudden overstrength as much as a continuation of Qin expansion.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-72-shenme-yang-de-ren-cai-shi-zhongzhen-zhishi-logxegnqexgc7z-lodw6tr6-8f28]] adds Qin Zhaoxiang Wang to the same-year diplomatic aftermath of Qi's collapse. The episode says he meets [[WeiZhaowang|魏昭王]] and [[HanXiwang|韩釐王]] at the Zhou capital around [[Luoyi|洛邑]], showing Qin using interstate convening while [[YanState|燕国]] is converting victory in Qi into occupation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-73-lishishang-ziyou-lianai-de-kaichuangzhe-shi-shui-lkumfsfialqgdl6qpb3c-hnz7h9u]] adds the more forceful sequel. Qin Zhaoxiang Wang meets [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] at Rangcheng, and Qin then attacks [[WeiState|魏国]], takes [[AnchengWarringStates|安城]], and advances near [[Daliang|大梁]] before withdrawing. The episode reads this as Qin refusing to be satisfied by anti-Qi coalition treasure and returning to [[QinEastwardPressure|eastward pressure]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-74-huanyuan-wanbi-guizhao-zhenxiang-lg1irvbbc1wp8oph2kmpatfoxebk]] places Qin Zhaoxiang Wang in the "完璧归赵" exchange. He offers fifteen cities for [[HeShiBi|和氏璧]], receives and admires the jade, but does not move to deliver the cities. [[LinXiangru|蔺相如]]'s response turns Qin's non-performance into the visible problem; once the jade has already returned to [[ZhaoState|赵国]], Qin Zhaoxiang Wang releases Lin rather than killing an envoy for no recoverable gain.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-76-shui-geile-chuqingxiangwang-de-zixin-lsut4cfic7xbbrmbmimrruf7n6y0]] turns that Qin-Zhao tension into war pressure. After [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] refuses to join Qin in attacking [[QiState|齐国]], Qin Zhaoxiang Wang attacks Zhao, takes cities, attacks again in 281 BCE, and captures [[ShichengWarringStates|石城]]. The episode also records his reappointment of [[WeiRan|魏冉]] as chancellor and previews his invitation to meet Zhao Huiwen Wang at [[Mianchi|渑池]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-77-lianpo-yu-linxiangru-shishang-zui-zhuming-de-yidui-hao-jiyou-lmelnhhtfuzezvmmsuw4yf10plsb]] continues his pressure pattern into 280-279 BCE. Qin Zhaoxiang Wang uses [[BaiQi|白起]] against [[ZhaoState|赵国]] and later [[ChuState|楚国]], while [[SimaCuo|司马错]] leads a hard southwestern route through [[BaShu|巴蜀]] toward Chu's Qianzhong region. At [[Mianchi|渑池]], however, his attempt to place [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] in a lower-status performance scene is checked when [[LinXiangru|蔺相如]] forces a matching Qin gesture.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-82-daqin-de-hewuqi-baiqi-laile-lo0w8mifkijhzmx7fdwcimmv7ade]] shows the same reign using victory to reorder territory and future campaign routes. Under Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's Qin, Bai Qi takes the Chu capital region, Qin creates [[NanCommandery|南郡]], and the following year Qin takes [[WuJunWarringStates|巫郡]] and [[Qianzhong|黔中]]. The episode then explains the attack on [[WeiState|魏国]] under [[BaiQi|白起]] and [[WeiRan|魏冉]] as a way to pressure the passage states before any deeper strike at [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]]'s new position.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-84-fangzhi-qinguo-miechu-jing-yong-zhe-zhao-llgh12eistejijsl6yildzkixocm]] shows Qin Zhaoxiang Wang accepting a check on that southern campaign path. [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] writes from the Chu side but argues in Qin's own interest: if Qin trusts [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]] as corridor partners while attacking [[ChuState|楚国]], Qin risks the kind of rear betrayal associated with [[Fuchai|吴王夫差]] and [[ZhiYao|智瑶]]. Qin Zhaoxiang Wang accepts the reframing, orders [[BaiQi|白起]] to stop, dismisses Han-Wei troops, and sends Huang Xie back to arrange Qin-Chu friendship.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-85-xuantaihou-weihe-sitong-30-nian-hou-ansha-le-laoqingren-lg7zy6vqtdjcufou4-mox5qkg2ct5]] adds the northern frontier counterpart to that outward pressure. By Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's thirty-fourth year, [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]]'s long relationship with the [[YiquState|义渠]] king has helped keep Qin's rear quiet while Qin campaigns against other states. The episode then has Xuan Taihou kill the Yiqu king at [[GanquanPalace|甘泉宫]], after which Qin destroys Yiqu and establishes [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]]. The source therefore makes Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's reign depend on both external diplomacy and [[QinFrontierBufferManagement|frontier buffer management]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-87-xialu-xiangfeng-yongzhe-sheng-zhongyu-zhaodao-chuchu-le-loppr83sdq5hhesmqihckwmhpl2h]] returns his reign to the Zhao front. After Zhao does not complete a prior settlement involving 公子吴 and returned land, Qin attacks [[EyuBattle|阏与 / 燕宇之战]]. The campaign begins as another expression of Qin coercive pressure, but Zhao She's staged delay, spy manipulation, forced march, and seizure of the northern mountain make Qin misread the operation and lose the siege.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-88-ni-zenme-kandai-fenghuangnan-1-lpuxkdq-rvnifuoneq212g53w5z5]] adds two internal-court implications for his reign. First, [[WeiRan|魏冉]]'s recommendation of [[KeqingZao|客卿灶 / 客卿造]] to attack Qi for territory near [[Dingtao|陶 / 定陶]] shows how a powerful minister can bend Qin force toward [[PrivateFiefExpansionThroughStateWar|private fief expansion]]. Second, [[WangJiQin|王稽 / 王继]]'s discovery of [[FanJu|范雎]] under the alias 张禄 previews a new talent channel that may help Qin Zhaoxiang Wang counter Wei Ran's overgrown authority.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-88-ni-zenme-kandai-fenghuangnan-2-lpzyziww177yvvjksmuioa-p-1qi]] shows Qin Zhaoxiang Wang accepting that talent channel. He clears attendants, repeatedly asks Fan Ju to speak, and explicitly permits advice even about his mother and ministers. Fan Ju's [[ProtectiveRemonstranceSequencing|protective remonstrance sequence]] lets the ruler hear a direct charge that [[XuanTaihou|宣太后]] and [[WeiRan|魏冉]] overshadow royal authority; his adoption of [[YuanjiaoJingongStrategy|远交近攻]] then turns court recovery and strategic correction into the same problem.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-89-qinwang-jing-bei-ta-dui-mabaonan-ljugedx8qxhtuzd1ja-xiwxgse19]] gives that problem sharper succession and personnel stakes. Qin continues pressure against Zhao and Wei through [[HuShangHuyang|胡伤 / 胡阳]] and [[QinWangWan|武大夫绾]], while [[QinDaotaizi|秦悼太子]] dies as a hostage in [[WeiState|魏国]] and [[QinXiaowenwang|安国君柱]] later becomes crown prince. Inside the court, Fan Ju tells him that [[XuanTaihou|宣太后]], [[WeiRan|魏冉]], [[HuayangJunQin|华阳君]], [[JingyangJunQin|泾阳君]], and [[GaolingJunQin|高陵君]] leave him isolated, so the episode's "妈宝男" joke is really about hollow royal authority.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-90-jiemi-xianqin-zui-wanmei-de-fuchou-liu7gbl-bffknkw6dx5udqcq4tcm]] records the follow-through. The episode argues that Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's ability to reduce [[XuanTaihou|宣太后]] and [[WeiRan|魏冉]] shows he was not fully captive before; he still had enough institutional base once he chose to act. His recovered authority then becomes the political condition that lets [[FanJu|范雎]] use Qin pressure on [[WeiState|魏国]] for both state strategy and personal revenge.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-91-chulong-ruhe-qiaokai-shougua-zhaotaihou-de-men-1-lmgd7kdl1nrfwcrtkqttumtlpqok]] keeps him in the 265 BCE transition. After [[XuanTaihou|宣太后]] dies and [[WeiRan|魏冉]] is removed, Qin Zhaoxiang Wang establishes [[QinXiaowenwang|安国君柱]] as crown prince. The same source places Qin's attack on [[ZhaoState|赵国]] beside attacks on Han, using the [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] accession and Wei Qi refuge problem to explain why Qin now presses Zhao.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-92-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhuming-tiangou-shi-shui-1-lmwf0q0-3hxb6d4hro8fdmc3zdxp]] keeps Qin Zhaoxiang Wang behind two decisions. First, under [[FanJu|范雎]]'s advice, Qin uses [[BaiQi|白起]] against [[HanState|韩国]], cutting [[HanNanyangWarringStates|韩国南阳]] and [[TaihangRoad|太行道]] so [[Shangdang|上党]] loses contact with Han's capital region. Second, when [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] asks for [[MiWan|太子完]] to return to sick [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]], Qin Zhaoxiang Wang refuses immediate release and wants verification first, showing how hostage suspicion limits alliance repair.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-92-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhuming-tiangou-shi-shui-2-lt1nwraiuuvpy2tzo-zwaoqsajzx]] adds the consequence of that hesitation. Once Mi Wan has escaped and Huang Xie reveals the cover plan, Qin Zhaoxiang Wang first wants to kill Huang Xie. He accepts [[FanJu|范雎]]'s advice to spare and return him, making this source a case where Qin anger gives way to post-failure strategic salvage.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-1-loxyybm3njrtnudydafwzu9jp9jz]] puts Qin Zhaoxiang Wang at the decisive information-control point before [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] opens fully. Qin first benefits when Zhao's envoy [[ZhengZhu|郑朱]] is received in Qin, because the visible mission makes other states less likely to rescue Zhao. Later, once [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] replaces [[LianPo|廉颇]] with [[ZhaoKuo|赵括]], Qin Zhaoxiang Wang secretly appoints [[BaiQi|白起]] as main commander over [[WangHe|王龁]] and orders capital punishment for leaks.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-2-lvqctrazn8ddkcnp-lhspzzmzmns]] moves Qin Zhaoxiang Wang from command secrecy into emergency mobilization. Once he hears that Zhao's grain route has been cut, he travels from Xianyang to Henei, grants local rank incentives, and mobilizes males over fifteen to block Zhao relief and supplies. His role is therefore not only choosing Bai Qi, but turning Bai Qi's operational encirclement into a state-level sealing effort.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-3-ljwz8ebk0iyfleivkefvvoonrqb9]] adds the post-victory tension. Qin Zhaoxiang Wang tries to exploit Changping by sending [[WangLing|王陵]] against [[Handan|邯郸]] and then wanting [[BaiQi|白起]] to replace him when the attack goes badly. Bai Qi's refusal, based on Qin's depleted people and food after Changping, makes the king's pursuit of momentum clash with [[WarCostTempoDiscipline|战争成本与速决纪律]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-1-lq4wvkd8qx3q7zji6fnnkxi3qcl1]] presents the earlier policy choice that pauses Qin's momentum. After [[SuDai|苏代]] works on [[FanJu|范雎]]'s fear of [[BaiQi|白起]]'s future merit, Fan Ju tells Qin Zhaoxiang Wang that the army should rest and that Qin should accept cessions from [[HanState|韩国]] and [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. Qin receives [[Yuanyong|垣雍]] from Han and six Zhao cities rather than immediately forcing the destruction of Zhao.

The same source adds a reputation layer. When [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] personally comes for peace and is detained, Qin Zhaoxiang Wang eventually releases him after the framework agreement, leaving [[ZhaoHao|赵郝]] to negotiate details. The episode reads this as the king seeking both practical gain and a better international image.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-2-lutsvwvnwzsezoorhe-vu7yjthyx]] turns that accepted pause into a new grievance. Zhao's failure to hand over the promised cities gives Qin Zhaoxiang Wang a reason to ask for an accounting, while Zhao's internal mood after Changping makes compliance politically hard. The episode therefore presents him less as a chooser between peace and war than as the ruler whose temporary settlement can become a renewed punitive campaign once Zhao defects from it.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-3-lsrmq1-rjqnsja8g07bhaqvhxhpi]] shows Qin Zhaoxiang Wang using social form as coercion. He writes to [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] in private-friendship language, invites him to Qin, then detains him and demands [[WeiQiWarringStates|魏齐]]'s head in service of [[FanJu|范雎]]'s revenge. Qin's threat is explicit: without Wei Qi's head, Pingyuanjun will not be released and Qin may attack Zhao.

The exchange does not become peace. Zhao eventually sends Wei Qi's head and recovers Pingyuanjun, but Qin Zhaoxiang Wang still continues the military pressure through [[WangLing|王陵]] against [[Handan|邯郸]]. The episode therefore links his reign's hostage methods to both [[PersonalRevengeThroughStatePower|以国力行私仇]] and the unfinished post-Changping campaign.
