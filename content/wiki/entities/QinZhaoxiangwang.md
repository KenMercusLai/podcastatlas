# 秦昭襄王 / King Zhaoxiang of Qin

秦昭襄王 / King Zhaoxiang of Qin appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-58-zhaowulingwang-ruhe-dazao-qiangguo-lq9mgpib0whh0w-jkv0kxcafbnnz]] as the succession answer after [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] dies without a son. The episode says Qin Wuwang's younger half-brother Ying Ji was then a hostage in [[YanState|燕国]] and was brought back to succeed.

The source keeps him at the threshold of power rather than narrating his reign. His function here is to show how Qin moves from Qin Wuwang's impulsive death into a new branch tied to [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]], who is named as Ying Ji's mother and later political figure.
