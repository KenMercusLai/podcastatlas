# Zangshu / 《葬书》

[[Zangshu|《葬书》]] is the fengshui text discussed in [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]] through [[GuoPu|郭璞]]. The episode presents it as an early important fengshui work in received tradition while warning that many scholars see the Guo Pu attribution as likely later.

The text's source-scoped value is conceptual. It gives the episode a vocabulary of qi, burial, wind, water, and landscape containment that becomes [[FengShuiVitalEnergyTheory|风水生气论]].

## Key Claims
- Attribution caution matters: a traditional author name should not become proof of dating.
- The text concentrates the episode's discussion of hidden qi and site selection.
- Fengshui reasoning links body, family, earth, and political-social fate through landscape form.
