# 张燕 / Zhang Yan (Late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-932-weishenme-ren-yue-hao-yue-nan-dang-daguan-lvwzel782wcxs1lpt3rg2vmkdy29|Hanji 932]] supplies a pre-origin notice: Changshan Chu Feiyan / Chu Yan is named among armed groups spreading after [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]]'s uprising. The episode does not yet explain his adoption of the Zhang surname, but it places him in the same disorder field as [[ZhangNiujiaoLateHan|张牛角]] before Hanji 933 develops the succession story.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-933-weiguan-zhidao-yousuo-wei-yousuo-buwei-lopulnufsagoqyx7nngvcqi2nhrj|Hanji 933]] backfills Zhang Yan's origin before the later Yuan Shao campaign. It presents him first as Chu Yan / Chu Feiyan, successor named by [[ZhangNiujiaoLateHan|张牛角]] after the attack on Yingtao. After taking the Zhang surname, he gathers mountain bands into the [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]] and becomes too large for the court to suppress directly.

The same source adds the surrender-and-incorporation branch. Zhang Yan sends an envoy to [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], and [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] grants him a Zhonglangjiang office plus responsibility for order in the northern Yellow River mountain districts, including annual xiaolian recommendations. This makes his early military field a case of [[OfficeBasedRebelIncorporation|授官式招安]], not only an enemy force.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-970-yuanshao-ansha-lvbu-weihe-hui-shibai-ln2pnvit-qgpnhn-pne7o-p1irrs|Hanji 970]] later develops Zhang Yan as a major Black Mountain leader in the Changshan campaign against [[YuanShao|袁绍]]. The episode says Zhang Yan has tens of thousands of strong troops and thousands of horses, and that Xiongnu and Wuhuan elements are also present in the field.

Zhang Yan's role is structural as well as military. He is the common enemy who makes Yuan Shao and [[LyuBu|吕布]] cooperate for more than ten days, but that shared campaign does not produce durable trust. After both sides withdraw exhausted, Yuan Shao's hostility toward Lü Bu and Lü Bu's own insecurity lead into the [[YuanShaoLyuBuAssassinationAttempt|袁绍刺吕布未遂]].
