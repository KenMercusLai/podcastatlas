## Overview
Lindsay Cole is a forensic psychology professor and parent used by [[PlanetMoney]] as the working-household case in its 2026 episode on inflation, guilt, and the divided economy.

## Current Profile
The source presents Lindsay as financially strained despite professional stability. She and her husband both work full time as professors, own a home, and pay for childcare, but stagnant real wages, a summer-pay change, household costs, and vehicle trouble leave little budget flexibility.

## Key Characteristics
- She represents a professional household that looks stable in status terms but experiences daily financial pressure.
- Her summer-course compensation change turns institutional pay design into a direct household setback.
- Her budgeting examples center on small repeated decisions about water, electricity, snacks, baking, and children's activities.
- Her story anchors [[FinancialStressCognitiveTax]] because saving money requires attention, guilt, and constant tradeoff calculation.

## Qualifications
The source explicitly says Lindsay's household is not at the bottom of the economy because it owns a house and can afford childcare. Her profile should be read as a case of constrained professional stability, not as a general claim about all academics or all U.S. households.
