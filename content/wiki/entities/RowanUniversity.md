# Rowan University

## Overview

Rowan University is the New Jersey public institution formerly known as Glassboro State College whose engineering expansion after [[HankRowan]]'s $100 million gift supplies the central case in [[my-little-hundred-million-my-little-hundred-million|My Little Hundred Million]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Rowan as a regional, access-oriented alternative to elite-university philanthropy. Its engineering school was designed for about 500 students, later accommodated roughly 750, and planned further expansion instead of using demand mainly to increase exclusivity. The source emphasizes local public-school graduates, applied projects, practical manufacturing research, comparatively accessible in-state tuition, and reported employment outcomes.

The institution's significance in this wiki is allocative rather than a complete university profile. It is evidence for the proposition that a large gift can alter capacity, identity, and opportunity more visibly when the recipient begins with limited endowed resources. That does not by itself prove superior long-run social return over every elite research or scholarship program.

## Key Characteristics

- Public New Jersey university renamed after a transformative engineering gift.
- Regional institution serving many working- and middle-class students from public schools.
- Engineering school organized around expansion rather than increasing exclusivity.
- Applied institution emphasizing manufacturing and “blue-collar research.”
- Comparatively accessible program in the episode's Stanford contrast.
- Central example of high marginal institutional impact from a mega-gift.

## Evidence

### Capacity and access

- [[my-little-hundred-million-my-little-hundred-million]] reports growth beyond the engineering school's planned capacity, in-state net tuition of about $9,000, and a continued preference for expansion.

### Applied regional mission

- [[my-little-hundred-million-my-little-hundred-million]] describes local students building an endurance-racing vehicle and reports president Ali Houshmand's characterization of Rowan as a “blue-collar university.”

### Gift-driven institutional change

- [[my-little-hundred-million-my-little-hundred-million]] connects the university's engineering identity and later name to [[HankRowan]]'s 1992 gift.

## Qualifications

The source supplies an episode-selected snapshot rather than audited longitudinal evidence. Tuition, capacity, graduate-employment, student-background, and research descriptions remain source-scoped, and successful expansion does not isolate the gift's effect from leadership, public funding, labor-market demand, or later investment. The contrast with [[StanfordUniversity]] is useful for opportunity-cost analysis but does not compare like-for-like missions or outcomes.
