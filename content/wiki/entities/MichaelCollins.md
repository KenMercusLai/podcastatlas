# Michael Collins

## Overview
Michael Collins was the [[Apollo11]] command module pilot who remained in lunar orbit while [[NeilArmstrong]] and [[BuzzAldrin]] landed on the [[Moon]].

## Current Profile
The episode uses Collins to expose the burden hidden behind Apollo 11's surface imagery. He maintained the return vehicle and rehearsed a departure alone if the lunar module failed to launch or rendezvous, making contingency readiness a condition of collective mission success.

## Key Characteristics
- Piloted the Apollo 11 command module in lunar orbit.
- Maintained the spacecraft required for crew rendezvous and Earth return.
- Prepared for the possibility that Armstrong and Aldrin would remain stranded.
- Embodied isolation, technical responsibility, and asymmetric contingency.
- Made the landing inseparable from orbital and return work.
