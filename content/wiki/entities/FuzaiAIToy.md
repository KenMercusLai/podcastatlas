# 福仔 / Fuzai AI Toy

福仔 / Fuzai AI Toy is the speech-centered AI toy route named in [[ep253-baohuo-de-ai-haowu-daodi-shi-zhenxiang-haishi-zhishangshui-lgt0cdkotgnzjl0mu2tx41p9fw-4]]. In the episode, [[WangYiranSanlian|王依然]] contrasts it with [[Ropet]]: Fuzai tries to learn how to talk in ways that make users happy, while Ropet avoids speech to protect a pet-like emotional experience.
