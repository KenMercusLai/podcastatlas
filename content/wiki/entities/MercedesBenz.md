# Mercedes-Benz

Mercedes-Benz appears in [[zhongguo-xiaofeizhe-daidong-lafu-laolun-zengzhang-donghang-youhua-jipiao-tuigaiqian-zhengce-1005631805]] as one of the German luxury automakers under pressure in China. The episode groups Mercedes with [[BMW]] and [[Audi]], saying all three had first-half automotive-business margins below 4% and China sales down by roughly 20% year over year.

The source uses Mercedes to frame [[GermanLuxuryCarChinaPressure]]: demand for imported or joint-venture luxury fuel cars is weakening while local Chinese new-energy brands enter the RMB 300,000-500,000 range. Cutting discounts may protect brand price, but it can also weaken volume and factory utilization.
