# 《地狱变》 / Hell Screen

《地狱变》 / Hell Screen is the [[AkutagawaRyunosuke|芥川龙之介]] short story at the center of [[17-diyubian-zhongsheng-jie-ku-yi-nian-ru-mo-561780565]]. The episode presents it as a story about an ugly, gifted painter Yoshihide, his daughter in Horikawa's household, and a commissioned screen of Buddhist hell torments that finally requires the sight of a woman burning in a noble carriage.

The source's reading turns the plot away from a simple "mad artist" lesson. Yoshihide's demand for real suffering, his cruelty to apprentices, and his freezing before his daughter's death remain horrifying, but the hosts also emphasize Horikawa's power, the narrator's servile praise, and the possibility that the completed screen is [[ArtAsRevenge|artistic revenge]] against a world already organized as hell.
