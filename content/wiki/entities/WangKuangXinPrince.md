## Overview

Wang Kuang / 王匡 is one of Wang Mang's private sons recalled in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-658-erzi-queshi-buyinggai-wangmang-nusha-taizi-ln4tu6ly8eie6jspvrurzn2rjjp|Hanji 658]] after Wang Mang's public sons die. This page disambiguates him from later same-name figures such as [[WangKuangLateHan|王匡]].

## Current Profile

The source pairs Wang Kuang with [[WangXingXinPrince|王兴]]. Both are said to come from Wang Mang's earlier relationship with an attendant, both remain in Xindu because their status is unclear, and both become succession-relevant only after [[WangLinXinCrownPrince|王林]] and [[WangAnXinPrince|王安]] are gone.

## Key Characteristics

- Private son of Wang Mang whose identity needs disambiguation from other Wang Kuang pages.
- Succession fallback made visible by the deaths of the public sons.
- Source-scoped figure tied to the contradiction between Wang Mang's moral image and private household reality.
