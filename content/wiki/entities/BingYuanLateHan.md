## Overview

[[BingYuanLateHan]] is a late-Han official whose Hanji 1111 profile centers on refusing [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s request for a posthumous marriage between their deceased children.

## Current Profile

After [[CaoChongLateHan|曹冲]] dies, Cao Cao proposes joining him in marriage and burial with Bing Yuan's deceased daughter. Bing Yuan rejects the request because arranging marriage for dead children violates the ritual standard he accepts.

His argument is also institutional and personal. Cao Cao valued him for adherence to principle; yielding to a powerful patron's grief-driven exception would erase the trait that justified the relationship. Cao Cao accepts the criticism and abandons the proposal, making Bing Yuan's refusal a case of direct counsel that succeeds without changing its substantive objection into flattery.

## Key Characteristics

- Treats marriage as bounded by ritual procedure even when the request comes from the most powerful figure in his political environment.
- Uses consistency between office, reputation, and conduct as the core of his refusal.
- Accepts the risk of directly opposing Cao Cao rather than converting the request into ceremonial compliance.
- Succeeds because Cao Cao recognizes the principle and withdraws the request.
