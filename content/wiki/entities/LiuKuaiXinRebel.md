## Overview

刘快 / Liu Kuai enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-636-wangmang-dang-huangdi-hou-shenjing-cuoluan-le-lq-essrurb6jdefzjrfgoujw5uby|Hanji 636]] as the Quxiang marquis and Liu-line rebel whose failed attack on Jimo tests early [[XinDynasty|新朝]] control shortly after [[WangMang|王莽]]'s accession.

## Current Profile

Liu Kuai's current profile is source-bounded. The episode presents him as a former Han royal-kinsman actor who gathers several thousand followers and rises against Wang Mang in summer 9 CE, but whose force fails to win the support of Jimo's officials or commoners.

His defeat is used less as a full biography than as evidence about the early Xin order. Liu Kuai attacks the former Jiaodong capital, while his elder brother [[LiuYinFuchongDuke|刘音]] closes the gates and imprisons himself to avoid implication. The episode reads Liu Kuai's action as brave but strategically shallow because Wang Mang's local legitimacy and coercive control were still strong.

## Key Characteristics

- Liu-line Quxiang marquis active in the first year of Xin rule.
- Leads a small anti-Wang-Mang rising with several thousand followers.
- Fails to capture Jimo because local officials and residents resist him.
- Dies during retreat through Changguang county.
- Functions in the wiki as an early resistance test that fails before later Xin collapse.

## Qualifications

This page is limited to Hanji 636. It does not reconstruct Liu Kuai's full genealogy, title history, exact troop composition, route, or tactical sequence. The host's "brave but unwise" reading is preserved as source interpretation rather than independent character diagnosis.

- Captured his failed rising as evidence that early Xin control had not yet collapsed.
