# 《铁拳教育》 / True Education

## Overview
《铁拳教育》, named 《真教育》 in the Korean context described by the source, is a Korean drama used by [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] as a hook for discussing school authority, family control, youth violence, and workplace bullying.

## Current Profile
The episode treats the drama less as entertainment coverage than as a visible fantasy about failed institutions. Its slapback and mirroring scenes let viewers feel that abusive parents, violent students, or coercive adults are finally forced to experience what they do to others.

The source immediately bounds that fantasy. It uses the drama to ask what real people can do when authority is inverted, captured, or abusive, but it rejects violence and harassment as real-life solutions. The drama therefore functions in the wiki as a media object that exposes [[StructuralPowerImbalance|structural power imbalance]] and the public desire for repair when normal education or workplace systems fail.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a drama hook for discussing education, authority, and coercion rather than as a standalone plot archive.
- Makes revenge and mirroring emotionally legible while the source rejects real-world violence.
- Connects school conflict to wider workplace and medical-social metaphors such as "铁拳职场" and "铁拳医疗."
- Helps the episode move from student-parent-teacher dynamics into adult [[WorkplaceBullying|workplace bullying]] and psychological boundaries.
