# 卡海德 / Karhide

卡海德 / Karhide is one of the two major nations on [[Gethen|格森星 / Gethen]] in [[TheLeftHandOfDarkness|《黑暗的左手》]]. In [[36-heian-de-zuoshou-liufang-yu-ai-de-weilai-wangshi-605254522]], [[GenlyAi|金利·艾 / Genly Ai]] first tries to persuade Karhide's king to join the [[Ekumen|艾库曼 / Ekumen]], but the mission fails amid court suspicion, gender disgust, and the removal of [[Estraven|伊斯特拉凡 / Estraven]].

The source describes Karhide as not simply a centralized monarchy. Its politics work through clans, court rivalry, indirect speech, pride, and [[ShifgrethorPoliticalFace]], while the border dispute with [[Orgoreyn|欧格瑞恩 / Orgoreyn]] lets Tayber mobilize [[PatriotismAsHatred]] against both Estraven and the neighboring state.
