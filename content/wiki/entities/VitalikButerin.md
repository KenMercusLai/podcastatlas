# Vitalik Buterin

## Overview
Vitalik Buterin is the Ethereum co-founder interviewed about effective altruism, philanthropy, AI risk, institutional incentives, and defensive decentralized acceleration.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Buterin as a sympathetic but revisionist participant in the effective-altruist ecosystem. Comparative impact led him toward malaria prevention, global health, longevity, and existential-risk work, but he stresses that neglectedness changes and institutions can diverge from donor intent. On AI, he combines serious concern with uncertainty, skepticism toward corporate moral authority, and distributed defense.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses comparative impact and neglectedness to allocate philanthropic resources.
- Treats AI catastrophe as serious but highly uncertain rather than precisely forecastable.
- Separates technical safety research from company incentives and political legitimacy.
- Prefers protective and decentralized technologies to one frontier controller.
- Revises judgments when issue attention or institutional behavior changes.
