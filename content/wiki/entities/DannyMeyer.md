# Danny Meyer

## Overview

Danny Meyer is the founder and executive chairman of [[UnionSquareHospitalityGroup]], the restaurateur associated with [[ShakeShack]], and the guest adviser in [[advice-line-with-danny-meyer-of-shake-shack-c7199e93-5dba-43ab-8fb0-91403917c3ac]].

## Current Profile

Meyer's advice treats hospitality as an operating system rather than a restaurant-only gesture. He connects culture to observable behavior, packaged branding to a feeling of belonging, channel expansion to controlled learning, and scale to the company's ability to preserve quality and develop people. In his executive-chairman role, he says he focuses on culture, culinary products, and creative opportunities rather than daily execution.

## Key Characteristics
- Defines culture through expected and tolerated behavior rather than a business-as-family metaphor.
- Judges hospitality through technical competence plus visible warmth among employees.
- Uses brand experience and community as growth tools even when the product is packaged.
- Recommends bounded experiments before committing capacity to a new channel.
- Treats supply, quality systems, staffing, and culture as prerequisites for scale.
- Frames business downturns as survivable cycles that reward better problem-solving and strong colleagues.
