# China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission / 银保监会

China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission / 银保监会 appears in [[136-yinhang-licai-hai-neng-zenme-mai-llc7n0f3g2-jrz4xkoefz1nui5lt]] as the banking-and-insurance regulator that approved the first bank wealth-management subsidiaries after [[AssetManagementNewRules|资管新规]]. The source says it approved [[ChinaConstructionBank|China Construction Bank]] and [[BankOfChina|Bank of China]] to establish wealth-management subsidiaries on 2018-12-26.

The episode uses the regulator as part of a broader institutional cleanup story. Earlier bank wealth-management expansion had involved banking, securities, trust, fund, and insurance channels, so regulation had to confront cross-sector arbitrage rather than only one bank product.

## Source Position
- The regulator is presented as a key actor in moving bank wealth management from bank departments toward separate [[BankWealthManagementSubsidiary|wealth-management subsidiaries]].
- Its approvals are tied to the post-资管新规 transition rather than to a standalone company story.
- The source also treats institution-based regulation as one of the constraints on wealth subsidiaries' later investment culture and product design.
