# Sydney

Sydney appears in [[lanjian-hangtian-wancheng-zhongguo-shouci-ludi-huojian-huishou-yushu-keji-shizhi-chaoguo-3000-yi-1007302506]] as the episode's closing lifestyle recommendation. The source presents Sydney as an outdoor-friendly city and links that reputation to beach safety services in [[NewSouthWales|New South Wales]].

The episode says New South Wales expanded drone lifeguard patrols from July 1, 2026. In the source's description, drones patrol from the air, warn swimmers when sharks appear nearby, and help make beach leisure feel more routine and publicly supported.
