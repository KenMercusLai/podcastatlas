# 曲沃 / Quwo (Warring States)

曲沃 / Quwo appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] as a [[WeiState|魏国]] stronghold paired with [[Jiaoyi|焦邑]] in the 330 BCE Qin offensive. This page uses the Warring States disambiguation to avoid collapsing the episode's Wei-Qin frontier place into other historical Quwo references.

The episode makes Quwo part of two movements: first, [[QinState|秦国]] uses attacks around Jiaoyi and Quwo to keep squeezing Wei into further cessions; later, Qin returns Jiaoyi and Quwo to Wei after the [[PuyangWarringStates|蒲阳]] and [[Shangjun|上郡]] exchange. That return is presented as the next strategic question rather than a simple act of kindness.
