## Overview

孔僖 / Kong Xi now has two Zhangdi-era source roles. Hanji 754 presents him as the Taixue figure who defends historically grounded criticism after [[LiangYuEasternHan|Liang Yu]] reports his discussion with [[CuiYinEasternHan|Cui Yin]]. Hanji 755 presents him as the Confucius descendant whose modest answer to [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Emperor Zhang]] at Qufu earns appointment.

## Current Profile

Hanji 754 makes Kong Xi a speech-boundary figure. He and Cui Yin discuss Emperor Wu's early merits and later failures; Liang Yu reports the discussion as defaming a former emperor and mocking the current ruler. Kong Xi's memorial argues that defamation means invented slander, while citing the historical record is different. If criticism is true, it should prompt correction; if mistaken, punishment still risks making people afraid to speak.

Emperor Zhang stops pursuing the case and appoints Kong Xi as Lantai lingshi. This makes Kong Xi an example of [[HistoricalCritiqueTolerance|historical critique tolerance]]: his role is not just personal eloquence but a case where ruler judgment keeps recorded criticism from becoming criminal slander.

Hanji 755 then places Kong Xi inside Zhangdi's ritual visit to Qufu. After sacrificing to [[Confucius|Confucius]] and the seventy-two disciples, Zhangdi gathers adult Kong-lineage men and asks whether the ceremony brings honor to the family. Kong Xi answers by refusing to make the rite a private family triumph. He says the emperor's visit honors the former teacher and displays sage virtue, while the Kong lineage does not dare claim personal glory. Zhangdi praises him and appoints him as langzhong.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han Taixue figure who distinguishes historical criticism from fabricated slander.
- Co-accused discussant with Cui Yin after Liang Yu reports overheard imperial criticism.
- Appointment-worthy speaker under Emperor Zhang in both the Lantai and Qufu scenes.
- Kong-lineage representative whose modest ritual answer redirects honor from family vanity to teacher reverence.
- Identity-disambiguated from the existing Kong Xi / 孔熙 Gaixia commander page.

## Qualifications

This page remains bounded to Hanji 754 and Hanji 755. It does not reconstruct Kong Xi's full career, the whole Kong lineage's Eastern Han status, or the legal procedure behind the speech case. The Hanji 754 spelling 孔熙 is treated as the episode's transcript form for the Eastern Han Kong Xi/孔僖 subject, not as evidence for merging with the Chu-Han Kong Xi page.

## What Changed
