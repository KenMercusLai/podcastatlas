# Indonesia

Indonesia appears in [[burning-questions-a-more-fire-prone-world-6a687ea64207b2a60119df8b]] as a wildfire-risk country to watch. [[OliverMorton]] links its risk to deliberately set slash-and-burn fires, biofuel demand, and [[ElNino]]-driven drying in Southeast Asia.

The source uses Indonesia to show why [[LandscapeFirePrevention]] is not only a firefighting question. Agricultural incentives, commodity demand, enforcement, and climate cycles can create conditions where local land-clearing fires become part of a wider [[ExtremeWildfire]] and [[WildfireSmokeHealthBurden]] problem.
