# Indonesia

## Overview

Indonesia appears in the wiki as a Southeast Asian wildfire and haze case where agricultural land-clearing incentives, combustible peat, biofuel or plantation demand, climate drying, enforcement, and regional sovereignty interact.

## Current Profile

[[burning-questions-a-more-fire-prone-world-6a687ea64207b2a60119df8b]] first identifies Indonesia as a fire-risk country because deliberately set slash-and-burn fires, biofuel demand, and [[ElNino|El Nino]] drying can coincide. [[dunes-and-gloom-mbss-annus-horribilis-0a44ecf1bba6689b3b1e02af9ca13b2c]] supplies an active case across Borneo and Sumatra: peatlands spread fire, smoke reaches neighboring countries, and emergency response coexists with unresolved incentives and enforcement gaps.

The current profile is therefore not simply one of inadequate firefighting. Durable prevention requires cheaper alternatives to burning, enforceable corporate and landholder duties, peat rewetting, early action on climate forecasts, and cooperation with affected neighbors. Refusing foreign firefighting help may preserve sovereignty, but it does not remove the cross-border public-health cost.

## Key Characteristics

- Archipelagic state exposed to deliberately set agricultural and plantation fires.
- Peatland geography can make ignition persistent and difficult to suppress.
- El Nino drying can amplify seasonal fire and food-security risks.
- Origin jurisdiction for smoke that affects Malaysia, the Philippines, and Singapore.
- Uses firefighting, water bombing, cloud seeding, corporate cases, and restoration policy as response tools.
- Faces a prevention gap while burning remains financially attractive and enforcement is weak.

## Evidence

- **Structural risk:** [[burning-questions-a-more-fire-prone-world-6a687ea64207b2a60119df8b]] links slash-and-burn agriculture, biofuel demand, and El Nino drying.
- **Fire and haze event:** [[dunes-and-gloom-mbss-annus-horribilis-0a44ecf1bba6689b3b1e02af9ca13b2c]] reports fires on Borneo and Sumatra and smoke across several Southeast Asian states.
- **Domestic response:** [[dunes-and-gloom-mbss-annus-horribilis-0a44ecf1bba6689b3b1e02af9ca13b2c]] describes 98 corporate cases, thousands of firefighters, water bombing, cloud seeding, and a peatland and mangrove restoration agency.
- **Cross-border tension:** [[dunes-and-gloom-mbss-annus-horribilis-0a44ecf1bba6689b3b1e02af9ca13b2c]] records Indonesia's rejection of foreign help and objection to Singapore's extraterritorial haze law.

## Qualifications

The sources are structured podcast summaries, not a complete account of Indonesia's land tenure, concession ownership, smallholder livelihoods, fire attribution, enforcement record, or national climate policy. The new case totals, pollution descriptions, restoration announcement, and El Nino timeline are episode-date claims and require current independent verification for operational use.
