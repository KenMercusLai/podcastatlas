# 中通快递

## Overview
中通快递 is a major Chinese franchise express carrier founded by [[LaiMeisong|赖梅松]] in 2002.

## Current Profile
The source presents 中通 as a slow-starting entrant that gained control and cost advantage by investing early in scheduled interprovincial vehicles, sorting centers, direct routes, and automated handling. It also formalized paid delivery fees between outlets, helping the network absorb ecommerce's geographically imbalanced parcel flows.

## Key Characteristics
- Infrastructure-led franchise carrier.
- Early builder of self-controlled trunk transport.
- Innovator in paid inter-outlet delivery fees.
- High-volume operator with strong reported margins.

## Evidence
- Infrastructure strategy: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] links scheduled trunk routes to improving parcel volume and network confidence.
- Network economics: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] describes a rebuilt settlement system and regionally differentiated delivery fees.
- Current scale: [[no-219-kuaidi-jianghu-sanshinian-tonglubang-taobao-pinduoduo-yu-jiage-zhan-1015471748]] places 中通 first by parcel volume among the major carriers.
