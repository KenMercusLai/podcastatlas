# Omni-Human

## Overview
Omni-Human is the model named in the Yuri source as an enabling window for expressive AI singing and virtual-idol performance.

## Current Profile
The source treats Omni-Human as important because it improved lip-sync and performance freedom enough for [[UreyVirtualIdol|Yuri / 尤栗 / 优瑞]] to become plausible as a singing AI character. It is not presented as the whole production system; Hanqing instead describes Yuri's workflow as combining many model capabilities across audio, image, and video.

## Key Characteristics
- The source dates Omni-Human's relevant appearance to March 2025.
- It is associated with better lip-sync freedom and emotional performance in AI character singing.
- It functions as an enabling model window for [[AINativeVirtualIdol]] rather than a complete IP asset by itself.
- Its value depends on being combined with music, image, video, editing, and personality-system work.
