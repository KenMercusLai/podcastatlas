# Platinum Equity

Platinum Equity is the U.S. private-equity firm named in [[bali-shui-he-shengpeilu-hai-neng-zhuanqian-quechao-weihe-yao-boli-shui-yewu-1005452453]] as [[Nestle]]'s counterparty for the water and premium-beverage joint venture. The source says Platinum Equity agreed to pay roughly $3.4 billion in cash for 50% of the business, with both parties holding equal stakes in the new company.

In the wiki, Platinum Equity matters less as a standalone investor profile than as the buyer-side mechanism in [[BottledWaterPortfolioSpinout]]. The episode frames private-equity participation as a way to give [[Perrier]], [[SanPellegrino]], [[AcquaPanna]], and related brands more independent operating space while Nestle reduces direct burden and keeps upside exposure.

## Source Position
- Platinum Equity provides the external ownership and operating-capital layer for Nestle's water portfolio move.
- The transaction is presented as a joint venture rather than a full sale, so Platinum Equity becomes part of a shared-risk, shared-upside structure.
- The source does not analyze Platinum Equity's broader portfolio or strategy.
