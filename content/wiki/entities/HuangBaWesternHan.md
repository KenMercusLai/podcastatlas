## Overview

黄霸 / Huang Ba enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-455-zhichang-renshi-wenti-bie-ti-yijian-kankan-huangba-de-xiachang-lhaztu17ulzaartaa6y0ehaqvnzx|Hanji 455]] as a Western Han chancellor whose recommendation that [[ShiGaoWesternHan|史高]] become taiwei angers [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]. The episode treats the incident less as a biography of Huang Ba than as a personnel-boundary case: an official already weakened after a prior bird-omen embarrassment tries to move into a ruler's sensitive appointment field and learns that apology and retreat are safer than explanation.

## Current Profile

The source presents Huang Ba in a narrow court-politics moment. He appears after an earlier incident involving auspicious birds and Zhang Chang has left him politically embarrassed. In that insecure position, he recommends Shi Gao, a trusted imperial relative and shizhong, for the taiwei office. The host reads the move as apparently flattering but structurally dangerous because taiwei is tied to military authority, Shi Gao belongs to the emperor's intimate circle, and Xuan controls both the appointment decision and its timing.

Xuan's response defines Huang Ba's role boundary. He says Huang should attend to instruction, transmission of people's conditions, reduction of unjust imprisonment, and suppression of theft, not propose top-level military appointments. Huang Ba removes his cap, apologizes, waits several days for a decision, escapes further punishment, and thereafter does not make such requests. The wiki therefore treats him here as a case for [[SensitivePersonnelRecommendationBoundary|sensitive personnel recommendation boundaries]], not as a complete judgment on his wider career.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han chancellor appearing in a short personnel-boundary episode.
- Official whose earlier bird-omen embarrassment leaves him politically exposed in the host's reading.
- Recommends Shi Gao for taiwei, touching appointment authority, military command, and outer-relative politics at once.
- Receives a direct rebuke from Xuan about the proper scope of chancellor duties.
- Survives by apologizing instead of defending the recommendation.
- Becomes a workplace-analogy case for not volunteering opinions on sensitive personnel matters outside one's authority lane.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 455. It does not reconstruct Huang Ba's full official biography, local-governance reputation, relationship with Zhang Chang, or the original Han shu account beyond the episode's summary. The host's modern workplace lesson is interpretive and should not be read as a universal ban on all personnel advice; the episode's narrower issue is unsolicited intervention in a sensitive, ruler-controlled appointment.
