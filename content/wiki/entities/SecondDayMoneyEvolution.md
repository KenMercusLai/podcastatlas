# 《第二天金钱进化论》 / Second-Day Money Evolution

## Overview
《第二天金钱进化论》 is [[FangYanWealth|方言]]'s book discussed in [[no-220-qian-weishenme-bushi-wannengliangyao-liaoliao-naxie-fanpin-de-gushi-gkwridon0rm-apxkiasvnper]]. In the episode, it is presented as a book about how people relate to money after getting it.

## Current Profile
The book's wiki profile is source-limited to Fang Yan and [[LiuFei|刘飞]]'s discussion. Its central contrast is between earning money and surviving the life system money creates. It treats sudden wealth, entrepreneurship, stock-option gains, real-estate windfalls, family strain, social-circle change, and investment temptation as one linked "second day" problem.

The book's positive answer is not only frugality. It asks the newly wealthy or temporarily enriched person to define an endpoint, control desire, preserve family and judgment, avoid leverage and social traps, and turn income into assets that can buy time. This makes it a book adjacent to [[FinancialFreedomVsLifestyleFreedom]], [[WealthDesireGap]], and [[ValueInvesting]] as life philosophy.

## Key Characteristics
- It focuses on what happens after obtaining money rather than on wealth acquisition tactics.
- It frames money as a tool that can improve life or amplify greed, vanity, comparison, and bad judgment.
- It uses bankruptcy, luxury-property, banking, entrepreneurship, and family cases to make re-poverty risk concrete.
- It treats reading, plain living, and value-investing discipline as ways to survive the "second day."
- It defines mature wealth around passive income, controlled spending, family stability, and time freedom.
