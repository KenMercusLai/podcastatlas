# 李历 / Li Li (Han Fu retainer)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] introduces this Li Li as a [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] retainer warning against surrendering [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] to [[YuanShao|袁绍]]. The stable suffix distinguishes him from [[LiLiLateHan|李利]], the Li Jue-side figure in later Chang'an material.

Li Li's warning belongs to the same dissenting cluster as [[GengWuLateHan|耿武]] and [[MinChunLateHan|闵纯]]. The cluster argues that Jizhou can raise large forces and has long grain reserves, while Yuan Shao is not yet materially secure.
