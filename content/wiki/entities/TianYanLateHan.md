# 田晏 / Tian Yan (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-909-2025-ruhe-yangcheng-yi-shen-guiqi-lomndmphrybunjbbbbn9ebafcsj|Hanji 909]] introduces Tian Yan as a late-Han figure who had previously been punished and later pardoned, then sought military merit through a new expedition against [[Xianbei|鲜卑]]. He asks the eunuch [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]] to recommend him to [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]], and Wang Fu's support helps make him Po Xianbei zhonglangjiang.

The appointment makes Tian Yan part of a court-access problem before it is a battlefield problem. He leads the Yunzhong column of the three-route offensive, enters Xianbei territory with more than 10,000 cavalry, and is defeated by [[TanshihuaiXianbei|檀石槐]]. Like [[XiaYuLateHan|夏育]] and [[ZangMinLateHan|臧旻]], he escapes with only a few dozen riders, is jailed in [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], redeems guilt through payment and connections, and is demoted to commoner.
