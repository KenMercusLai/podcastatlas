## Overview

杨桥 / Yang Qiao is a source-bounded late-Han figure in Hanji 878 whose fatal refusal of an imperial princess marriage becomes the episode's main case for unbounded personal power.

## Current Profile

The current profile is entirely grounded in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-878-bu-shou-yueshu-de-quanli-hairen-buqian-lvevnahcxshgbz5zyqbaf36pa7nk|Hanji 878]]. The source says [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] recommended Yang Qiao among several talented men in 167 CE, and [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] admired Yang's talent and appearance. Huan then tried to marry a princess to Yang; Yang refused, stopped eating, and died after seven days.

Yang's page therefore records a narrow but important late-Han evidence point: when imperial favor is not bounded by consent or office procedure, personal preference can become lethal pressure. The source's contrast with [[SongHongEasternHan|宋弘]] and [[LiuXiu|刘秀 / 光武帝]] makes Yang a negative counterpart to a ruler-minister marriage exchange that stops short of coercion.

## Key Characteristics

- Yang Qiao appears as a recommended talent in Dou Wu's 167 CE court recommendation field.
- His talent and appearance make him an object of Emperor Huan's personal attention rather than merely an appointment candidate.
- His refusal of a princess marriage ends in death by hunger strike, making him a source case for coerced imperial marriage.

## Evidence

- Recommendation and attraction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-878-bu-shou-yueshu-de-quanli-hairen-buqian-lvevnahcxshgbz5zyqbaf36pa7nk|Hanji 878]] says Dou Wu recommended Yang Qiao and that Huan liked him after seeing his ability and appearance.
- Forced marriage pressure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-878-bu-shou-yueshu-de-quanli-hairen-buqian-lvevnahcxshgbz5zyqbaf36pa7nk|Hanji 878]] says Huan wanted Yang to marry a princess despite Yang's resistance.
- Fatal refusal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-878-bu-shou-yueshu-de-quanli-hairen-buqian-lvevnahcxshgbz5zyqbaf36pa7nk|Hanji 878]] says Yang refused by hunger strike and died after seven days.

## Qualifications

The page should not infer a full biography, office history, or factional alignment for Yang Qiao from the current bounded source. The stable claim is the coercive marriage episode and its political meaning inside Hanji 878.
