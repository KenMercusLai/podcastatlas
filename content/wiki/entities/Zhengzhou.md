# 郑州 / Zhengzhou

郑州 / Zhengzhou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-162-1-ta-ti-liubang-qu-si-rujin-chengle-zhengzhou-de-shouhushen-li3su2zlmbm1ift9nibujgtcrxwm|Hanji 162-1]] as the present-day city used by the source to frame the memory of [[JiXin|纪信]]. The episode says that the people of ancient [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]] honored Ji Xin as a [[Chenghuang|城隍]] after his death, and connects that protective cult to today's Zhengzhou.

The page should be treated as a memory and geography bridge rather than a simple identity replacement. The source's point is that Ji Xin's sacrificial death at [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]] remained legible in later local worship, with the city-god temple surviving cycles of damage and repair.
