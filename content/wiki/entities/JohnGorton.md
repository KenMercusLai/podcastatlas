# John Gorton

## Overview

John Gorton was an Australian Liberal prime minister presented as a decorated airman, anti-communist U.S. ally, cultural-policy supporter, and larrikin leader whose own party removed him in 1971.

## Current Profile

[[188-australian-prime-ministers-harold-holt-malcolm-fraser-part-2-glt4635125602]] builds Gorton's profile from contrasts: Oxford education and wartime distinction sit beside a plain-speaking, drinking, irreverent public persona. He continued support for the [[UnitedStates|U.S. alliance]] and Vietnam, won the 1969 election, and is credited with helping the Australian film industry's revival. His fall through internal party pressure makes him an early Australian case for [[PrimeMinisterialRegicide|prime ministerial regicide]].

## Key Characteristics

- Air Force veteran whose face was reconstructed after wartime injury.
- Anti-communist supporter of the U.S. alliance and Vietnam commitment.
- Plain-speaking, larrikin political persona.
- Winner of the 1969 election.
- Government supporter of Australian film-industry development.
- Removed after losing authority within his own party in 1971.
