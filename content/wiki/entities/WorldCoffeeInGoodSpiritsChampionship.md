# World Coffee in Good Spirits Championship / 世界咖啡与烈酒大赛

## Overview
The World Coffee in Good Spirits Championship is represented here as a competition combining coffee, spirits, creative drink construction, and live presentation.

## Current Profile
In this source, the championship is not merely a recipe test. [[LiuZhanhong|柳展鸿 Andy]] describes a judged experience in which flavor balance, stable ingredients, thematic coherence, service, language, visuals, timing, and performance must work together. The event therefore exposes both creative method and the operational support hidden behind a short final presentation.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines coffee and alcohol without requiring coffee to dominate every composition.
- Rewards coherent relationships among ingredients, theme, sensory experience, and presentation.
- Makes ingredient stability and rehearsed execution strategically important.
- Compresses long preparation and distributed team support into a short judged performance.
