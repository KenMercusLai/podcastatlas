## Overview

霍休 / Huo Xiu is the concealed organizer in [[GoldenRocDynastyGuLong|《金鹏王朝》]], using a false restoration claim and other people's motives to pursue sole control of the fallen kingdom's wealth.

## Current Profile

The bounded source presents Huo Xiu as a strategist who rarely needs to act openly. A false king and [[ShangguanFeiyanGuLong|上官飞燕]] as a false princess direct [[LuXiaofeng|陆小凤]] toward 阎铁珊 and 独孤一鹤; 霍天青's attachment to Shangguan helps weaken those targets; and the reputation of royal ministers as traitors makes removal look like justice. Huo's design therefore weaponizes friendship, chivalric intervention, romance, and incomplete history.

His initial explanation that the three ministers remained loyal is partly corrective, but it also redirects suspicion away from himself. The final revelation turns Shangguan and 霍天青 from apparent organizers into agents inside his scheme. Yet the ending does not demonstrate complete mastery: his escape mechanism fails for no settled reason, leaving accident, prior intervention, mechanical decay, or authorial convenience as competing explanations.

## Key Characteristics

- Pursues monopoly over shared royal wealth.
- Operates through false claimants, reputational framing, romantic leverage, and heroic intervention.
- Supplies a partial truth about ministerial loyalty that also functions as misdirection.
- Makes active co-conspirators into instruments within a larger plan.
- Depends on opponents acting from friendship, justice, and duel conventions.
- Loses through an unexplained mechanism failure that qualifies his apparent control.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one episode's reconstruction rather than direct comparison with the novel. The exact division of planning among Huo Xiu, Shangguan Feiyan, and 霍天青, the ministers' earlier conduct, and the cause of the mechanism failure remain source-scoped or disputed. Calling him a mastermind describes plot position, not flawless execution.
