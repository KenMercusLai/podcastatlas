# Hazel Grove Customs

## Overview
Hazel Grove Customs is the personalized and engraved gift business owned by [[DavidHyam]] in [[advice-line-with-kip-tindell-of-the-container-store-93508940-11b5-4b29-ba7b-eb077edee062]]. The company sells giftable kitchenware, home goods, accessories, novelty goods, golf ball markers, and train whistles.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Hazel Grove Customs as a small acquired business whose wholesale channel has grown to just over half of year-to-date sales. Guy and Kip advise David to deepen what is already working: more independent retailers, commission-based reps, analysis of best wholesale customers, and exceptional service for inbound corporate gifting accounts.

## Key Characteristics
- Personalized-gifts business acquired by David in 2016 after being founded in 2012.
- Wholesale-heavy growth case where larger orders can improve margins compared with low-priced individual ecommerce shipments.
- Candidate for [[WholesaleAsMarketing]] through independent retailers and corporate gifting accounts.
- Service-sensitive business because personalization and gifting create high expectations around reliability and follow-up.

## Qualifications
