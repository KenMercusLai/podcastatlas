# Chris Schmitz

Chris Schmitz is the academic mentioned in [[under-strain-breaking-the-british-state-6a799563523b3dfcd83c7271]] for counting examples of AI pressure on bureaucratic systems. The source says Schmitz identified 93 examples across 11 countries, including Japan, Brazil, Germany, Canada, and the [[UnitedStates]].

Schmitz's role in the wiki is to make [[AIBureaucracyArmsRace]] a comparative phenomenon rather than only a British anecdote. The count suggests that AI-assisted appeals, claims, requests, and applications can stress many administrative systems when rules are complex and challenge mechanisms are easy to invoke.
