# 方言 / Fang Yan

## Overview
方言 is an entrepreneur-author whose two bounded interviews connect wealth preservation, value investing, business experience, and time freedom through his book [[SecondDayMoneyEvolution|《第二天金钱进化论》]].

## Current Profile
The wiki knows Fang Yan through a [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] interview and an E251 [[ZhixingXiaojiuguan|知行小酒馆]] conversation. He describes starting a business in 1997, benefiting from an era tailwind, accumulating property, entering stocks after a large property sale, and later leaving his advertising/media business. Both interviews are retrospective self-accounts that deliberately reduce the role of personal mastery in his success.

His distinctive profile is not that of a technical wealth manager. He speaks as an operator who saw rapid enrichment, nearby ruin, and his own profitable speculation. E251 adds detail: roughly RMB 13 million became about RMB 120 million before his March 2015 exit, but he calls the result valuation and sentiment profit containing substantial luck rather than strict long-term investment. His positive model is a [[ValueInvestingPrerequisiteStack]] joining patient capital, continuing cash flow, operating knowledge, narrow competence, and a low-noise life.

## Key Characteristics
- He frames wealth after success as a psychological, social, and family-risk problem.
- He treats his own entrepreneurial wealth partly as era luck rather than pure individual mastery.
- He distinguishes profitable speculation from returns grounded in long-run business ownership.
- He treats stable capital, ongoing cash flow, operating experience, and behavioral control as prerequisites for active value investing.
- He criticizes advertising, real-estate packaging, and status consumption when they detach money from real social value.
- He defines the final aim of wealth as time freedom and a stable life, not a higher public wealth ranking.

## Qualifications
The page is source-limited to Fang Yan's self-account across two podcast episodes. Neither his returns nor his generalized claims about entrepreneurs, company understanding, active-investor win rates, or wealthy families are independently verified here. The disambiguated filename avoids collision with [[Fangyan|《方言》]], Yang Xiong's dialectological text.
