# 方言 / Fang Yan

## Overview
方言 is the entrepreneur-author guest in [[no-220-qian-weishenme-bushi-wannengliangyao-liaoliao-naxie-fanpin-de-gushi-gkwridon0rm-apxkiasvnper]], where he discusses wealth after windfalls through his book [[SecondDayMoneyEvolution|《第二天金钱进化论》]].

## Current Profile
The wiki knows Fang Yan through a single [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] interview. He describes starting a business in 1997, benefiting from a large era tailwind, going through a nouveau-riche period, and later exiting a still-profitable advertising/media business because he came to see parts of the work as socially hollow or harmful.

His distinctive profile is not that of a technical wealth manager. He speaks as someone who saw both rapid enrichment and nearby ruin: friends overleveraged, entrepreneurs lost focus in capital games, wealthy people were drawn into status and financial traps, and families broke under wealth pressure. He uses [[CharlieMunger]], [[WarrenBuffett]], [[DuanYongping]], reading, plain living, and [[ValueInvesting]] as examples of disciplined judgment rather than as a narrow stock-tip system.

## Key Characteristics
- He frames wealth after success as a psychological, social, and family-risk problem.
- He treats his own entrepreneurial wealth partly as era luck rather than pure individual mastery.
- He uses reading and value-investing examples to argue for inversion, leverage avoidance, plain living, and long-horizon judgment.
- He criticizes advertising, real-estate packaging, and status consumption when they detach money from real social value.
- He defines the final aim of wealth as time freedom and a stable life, not a higher public wealth ranking.
