# 许都 / Xudu

许都 / Xudu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1008-ruhe-pingjia-caocao-de-tuntianzhi-ls8wvh9ox-pcjmt0z8w82opbha-k|Hanji 1008]] as the area where [[CaoCao|曹操]] first organizes large-scale [[TuntianSystem|屯田制]] after accepting [[ZaoZhi|枣祗]]'s proposal. The episode treats Xudu less as a court capital than as an agricultural-logistics node: land, displaced people, administrators, and nearby military need are combined into a grain base.

The source says the Xudu-area experiment succeeds enough that other州郡 follow with屯田 offices and that warehouses gradually fill. Xudu therefore anchors the episode's claim that Cao Cao's later military freedom depends on a production base near his political center.
