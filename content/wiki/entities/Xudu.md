# 许都 / Xudu

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1003-sanguo-zui-bei-digu-de-moushi-dongzhao-llc7q-loennxw26v3u5cbhitikw1|Hanji 1003]] uses Xu first as [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s operating base before the court relocation. Cao Cao wants to move from Xu toward [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]], but the route runs through [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]]'s position at Liang, which is why [[DongZhaoLateHan|董昭]]'s advance letter matters before the later move of [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] to Xu.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1004-caocao-yingfeng-hanxiandi-shi-shui-de-zhuyi-lrrga-flmx5ajnhu1c42x7kooe94|Hanji 1004]] supplies the decision that makes Xudu the late-Han court site. [[DongZhaoLateHan|董昭]] advises [[CaoCao|曹操]] to move [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] from ruined [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] to Xu county because Luoyang lacks food and reliable control while Xu is easier to supply and defend from Cao Cao's base.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1005-sunce-zhuding-zaoshi-shi-xingge-jueding-mingyun-lpqnere2cwcrngggyezd-bsqahx4|Hanji 1005]] then backfills the immediate aftermath. The relocation breaks Cao Cao's unstable relationship with [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]], who had tried to obstruct the move from Liang but failed to arrive in time.

In this source, Xudu matters as the setting for early court-title management. Emperor Xian's name is used to reproach and appoint [[YuanShao|袁绍]], while Cao Cao later accepts a lower formal title after yielding great general to Yuan Shao. The place therefore joins court control and [[StrategicTitleConcession|战略性名位让步]] before the later屯田 branch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1008-ruhe-pingjia-caocao-de-tuntianzhi-ls8wvh9ox-pcjmt0z8w82opbha-k|Hanji 1008]] later develops Xudu as the area where [[CaoCao|曹操]] first organizes large-scale [[TuntianSystem|屯田制]] after accepting [[ZaoZhi|枣祗]]'s proposal. The episode treats Xudu less as a court capital than as an agricultural-logistics node: land, displaced people, administrators, and nearby military need are combined into a grain base.

The source says the Xudu-area experiment succeeds enough that other州郡 follow with屯田 offices and that warehouses gradually fill. Xudu therefore anchors the episode's claim that Cao Cao's later military freedom depends on a production base near his political center.
