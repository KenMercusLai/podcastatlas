# Discord

Discord appears in [[featherless-ai-when-your-weekend-experiment-makes-more-than-your-startup]] as one of the early community surfaces where [[FeatherlessAI|Featherless AI]] found users. The source groups Discord with [[Reddit]] and similar technical communities where local-LLM and open-model users were already asking how to run, fine-tune, and access niche models.

In this source, Discord functions as a [[CustomerPull]] and [[DistributionLedProductBuilding]] surface rather than as a product studied on its own. It matters because developer and hobbyist communities can reveal demand for infrastructure before a startup has broad brand awareness.
