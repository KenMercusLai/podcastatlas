# 周鸿祎 / Zhou Hongyi

## Overview
周鸿祎 / Zhou Hongyi is the founder and China-internet figure profiled in [[no-216-shi-bu-sha-yi-ren-hongyi-dapao-zhouhongyi-de-sanshinian-quanmian-zhanzhengshi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-27-1008693437]], where his career runs from early software experiments through [[3721]], [[YahooChina|Yahoo China]], [[Qihoo360|Qihoo 360]], [[ThreeQWar|3Q 大战]], search, mobile software, enterprise security, investing, and founder-media identity.

## Current Profile
The source presents Zhou as a technically curious, unusually combative founder whose career is best understood through repeated entry-point fights. He helped push Chinese-keyword access, free security, browser distribution, and search competition into the center of Chinese PC internet history, but the same record carries heavy qualifications around "rogue software" accusations, lawsuits, public feuds, and aggressive platform tactics.

## Key Characteristics
- Technical idealist with early exposure to computing, software development, antivirus products, and a stated product-changing-world ambition.
- Combat-oriented founder whose public identity was shaped by direct disputes with internet peers, media arguments, lawsuits, and "red cannon" branding.
- Entry-point strategist who repeatedly tried to own user access through Chinese keywords, browser plugins, security tools, browsers, navigation pages, search, and mobile assistants.
- Trust-reversal actor who moved from the disputed [[3721]] browser-plugin reputation into [[Qihoo360|360 Security Guard]] and anti-rogue-software positioning.
- Platform-era survivor whose later turns include enterprise security, financial technology, auto investment, content/live products, and livestream-era founder influence.
