# 周鸿祎 / Zhou Hongyi

## Overview
周鸿祎 / Zhou Hongyi is the founder and China-internet figure whose evidence arc runs from early software, [[3721]], [[YahooChina|Yahoo China]], and [[Qihoo360|Qihoo 360]] through [[ThreeQWar|3Q 大战]], enterprise security, founder media, and an application-led AI strategy.

## Current Profile
Across the two sources, Zhou is a technically curious and publicly combative entry-point strategist who later reframes visibility as [[EntrepreneurPublicCommunication|enterprise communication]] rather than conflict alone. His earlier career joined genuine user problems to aggressive plugins, defaults, security labels, lawsuits, and platform warfare; his later account acknowledges overexpansion after 3Q and presents “supporting actor” positioning as service to industry digitization, security, and traditional-sector AI adoption.

The AI interview extends the same entry-point logic into browsers, search, routing, tools, and agents. Zhou argues that language models are foundational but insufficient by themselves: narrow roles, vertical models, multi-agent organization, transparent execution, and human approval turn model capability into work. He positions [[Qihoo360|360]] around applications and security rather than training the largest general model, while treating AI claims, product performance, and forecasts as source-scoped.

## Key Characteristics
- Technical product idealist whose early formation and stated ambition center on widely used software.
- Conflict-shaped founder whose public identity includes direct disputes, legal battles, and later reflection on overexpansion.
- Entry-point strategist spanning keywords, security, browsers, search, mobile distribution, and AI-assisted browser work.
- Public communicator who treats founder visibility as product education, trust defense, and stakeholder access, with backlash and time costs.
- Application-led AI advocate emphasizing specialized agents, tools, routing, multi-agent coordination, and human supervision.
- Cybersecurity operator who expects AI to accelerate both defensive analysis and offensive automation.

## Qualifications
