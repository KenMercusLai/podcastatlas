## Overview

马援 / Ma Yuan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-728-weihe-mingchen-nan-shanzhong-beihou-de-yuanyin-ni-gai-dong-ltjl2bgpyyz1bmwe-4mfcyd9e6cf|Hanji 728]] as the warning voice who reads [[WangPanEasternHan|王磐]]'s elite socializing and broader princely guest recruitment as danger before punishment arrives.

## Current Profile

Hanji 728 does not build Ma Yuan's full military biography. It uses him as a political-risk diagnostician. He tells his nephew Cao Xun that Wang Pan comes from a family whose position had declined and should therefore stay low-key, but Wang Pan instead moves to the capital, socializes with powerful relatives and aristocrats, offends people, and is heading toward disaster.

The later events validate Ma Yuan's reading in the episode's structure. Wang Pan is killed for a crime more than a year later, and his son [[WangSuEasternHan|Wang Su]] still enters princely mansions. Ma Yuan separately warns Sima Lyu Zhong that as Liu Xiu's sons grow up, old prohibitions against princely guest-gathering should be restored; otherwise a major disaster may occur. The subsequent prince-guest purge makes that warning a case of danger seen before it becomes legally visible.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han figure used here as a source of court-patronage risk warnings.
- Reads Wang Pan's high-profile capital association as unsafe because family decline calls for restraint.
- Treats princely guest recruitment as a structural danger, not only an individual moral flaw.
- Warns before the purge, making his role diagnostic rather than retrospective.
- Later family links to [[EmpressMaMingdeEasternHan|明德马皇后]] remain important but outside this page's current bounded evidence.
