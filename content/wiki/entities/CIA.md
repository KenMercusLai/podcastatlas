# CIA / Central Intelligence Agency / 中情局

[[CIA|CIA / Central Intelligence Agency / 中情局]] appears in [[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] as the [[UnitedStates|American]] intelligence service that received British warnings derived from [[OlegGordievsky|Oleg Gordievsky]] but also tried to identify the hidden [[KGB]] source behind them. The episode treats this as a mixed ally relationship: intelligence sharing helped reduce strategic misunderstanding, while competitive curiosity created new risk.

The CIA branch is inseparable from [[AldrichAmes|Aldrich Ames]]. Ames's access to Soviet-counterintelligence work meant that once he sold information to the [[KGB]], the source-protection problem moved from hostile penetration to allied-system leakage.
