# 林徽因

林徽因 enters the wiki through [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] as a co-builder of modern Chinese architectural history with [[LiangSicheng|梁思成]]. The episode explicitly pushes against the habit of reducing Liang and Lin to social gossip by returning attention to their field travel, building surveys, and wartime intellectual labor.

In the source, Lin is tied to the search for Tang wooden architecture, the [[SocietyForResearchInChineseArchitecture|中国营造学社]]'s site work, and the failed gap between architectural ideals and wartime conditions. The episode treats her presence in the field as part of the larger claim that modern scholarship required bodies in difficult places, not only library learning.

## Key Claims
- Lin Huiyin's significance in the episode is scholarly and methodological, not merely biographical.
- Her work with Liang Sicheng connects architecture to [[FieldworkAsKnowledgeMethod]].
- The source uses her wartime experience to show how fragile serious research becomes when institutions, homes, health, and funding are displaced.
