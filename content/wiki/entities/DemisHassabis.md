# Demis Hassabis

## Overview
Demis Hassabis is the DeepMind founder and Google DeepMind leader synthesized here as a scientist-operator whose ambition for AGI, AI-for-science, and careful governance now sits inside Google-scale product and execution pressure.

## Current Profile
The existing wiki sources present Hassabis as a research-first founder associated with [[DeepMind]], [[AlphaGo]], [[AlphaFold]], and the long project of using AI to accelerate science. He is also treated as a public-facing AI-governance figure who argues that frontier development needs international oversight and a staged approach to extremely powerful systems.

The newest source changes the operating profile more than the intellectual profile. It reports that Hassabis no longer handles day-to-day [[GoogleDeepMind]] operations and has moved toward DeepMind chair and Alphabet chief scientist roles. Zhou Jiangong interprets the move as partly being "kicked upstairs" amid [[Gemini]] and coding-agent pressure, while still noting that Hassabis may prefer returning to research. That makes Hassabis a key example of [[GoogleDeepMindOrganizationalFriction]], where scientist-founder identity and product-speed demands pull in different directions.

## Key Characteristics
- Scientist-founder identity: the sources repeatedly connect Hassabis to games, neuroscience, AGI, AlphaGo, AlphaFold, and AI-for-science ambition.
- Google DeepMind leadership: he remains central to how sources understand the merged DeepMind/Google AI organization and its Gemini-era responsibilities.
- Research-versus-operation tension: the newest source frames his reported role shift as a signal that research orientation and daily product execution may have diverged.
- Governance voice: one source uses Hassabis to discuss international AI oversight, phased deployment, and the limits of purely voluntary industry self-regulation.
- Source-scoped ambiguity: the wiki preserves uncertainty over whether the latest role change reflects demotion, voluntary research focus, internal politics, or a mixture of factors.

## Qualifications
- The latest role-shift details are reported and interpreted by the episode; they are not treated here as independently verified corporate records.
- Hassabis's research orientation is not automatically a weakness. The same profile underlies DeepMind's most important scientific achievements.
- The governance material and the Google DeepMind operating material come from different source contexts and should not be collapsed into a single motive.
