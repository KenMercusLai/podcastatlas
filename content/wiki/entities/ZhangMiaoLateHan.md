# 张邈 / Zhang Miao (Late Han)

张邈 / Zhang Miao (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-997-zanghong-zhisi-shi-yibaoyuntian-haishi-qingjie-nandu-lln2liblq-kyjrsvitnbh7cspbax|Hanji 997]] as the brother of [[ZhangChaoLateHan|张超]] and the relationship through which [[ZangHong|臧洪]] indicts [[YuanShao|袁绍]]. The episode says Yuan Shao had called Zhang Miao his elder brother, so Zang Hong treats Yuan Shao's refusal to rescue Zhang Chao as a violation of that brotherly tie.

The page is source-scoped. Hanji 997 does not develop Zhang Miao's independent career; it uses his named friendship with Yuan Shao to show how private relationship language can become a binding moral claim when crisis comes.
