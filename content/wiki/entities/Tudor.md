# Tudor

Tudor is the lower-priced related watch brand discussed in [[rolex-1]]. The source says [[HansWilsdorf]] started Tudor in 1926, and the hosts frame it as a brand that historically borrowed Rolex-like cases and bracelets while using third-party movements.

The episode treats Tudor as strategically useful because it lets [[Rolex]] and [[HansWilsdorfFoundation]] serve a lower price point and possibly test ideas without making Rolex itself "punch down." In the wiki, Tudor connects to [[LuxuryScarcityDiscipline]] and [[LuxuryRetailChannelControl]] because related-brand architecture can absorb demand without weakening the crown brand.
