# 石门之战 / Battle of Shimen

石门之战 / Battle of Shimen enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-11-shijie-fengqi-yunyong-1-lt60b2gddiosf5kade35uopwohi5]], where [[QinXianGong|秦献公]] defeats the Three Jin coalition in 364 BCE and the notice credits Qin with killing sixty thousand soldiers.

The battle matters because it turns the earlier [[QinState|秦国]] legitimacy and diplomacy branch into visible military power. After Wei and Han already fail against Qin at Luoyang Jinjiao, Shimen becomes the stronger sign that Qin's eastern pressure is no longer a frontier nuisance but a structural problem for [[WeiState|魏国]], [[HanState|韩国]], and [[ZhaoState|赵国]].

The episode also makes Shimen a ritual-political event. [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]] responds by giving [[QinXianGong|秦献公]] [[FufuRobe|黻服]], which the host reads as [[RitualRecognitionOfMilitaryPower|ritual recognition of military power]].
