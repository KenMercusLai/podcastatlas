## Overview

Liu Ying / 刘英 is [[WangLinXinCrownPrince|王林]]'s wife in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-658-erzi-queshi-buyinggai-wangmang-nusha-taizi-ln4tu6ly8eie6jspvrurzn2rjjp|Hanji 658]]. The episode makes her an astrology-linked actor whose prediction is interpreted by Wang Lin as support for the plot, then weaponized after his death to implicate her.

## Current Profile

The source says Liu Ying came from a prominent family and was the daughter of the state teacher Liu Xing. She knew astrology and predicted a coming "white-clothed gathering," which Wang Lin reads as a sign that his plan against [[WangMang|王莽]] will succeed or that court mourning is near.

After Wang Lin's death, Wang Mang orders a formal denunciation that shifts responsibility for the astrological branch toward Liu Ying. She kills herself after learning of the accusation, adding one more death to the collapse of Wang Mang's household succession field.

## Key Characteristics

- Crown-prince consort tied to a prominent Liu family.
- Astrology-literate figure whose omen reading becomes part of Wang Lin's decision environment.
- Secondary target after Wang Lin's death, when Wang Mang needs to distribute blame.
- Suicide victim in the broader Wang household collapse.
