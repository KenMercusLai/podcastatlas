# 南郡 / Nan Commandery

南郡 / Nan Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-82-daqin-de-hewuqi-baiqi-laile-lo0w8mifkijhzmx7fdwcimmv7ade]] as the [[QinState|秦国]] commandery created after [[BaiQi|白起]] conquers the [[ChuState|楚国]] capital region around Ying in 278 BCE. The episode treats this not as a mere place-name notice, but as evidence that Qin can turn a broken royal center into an administered frontier of its own state.

The commandery therefore links battlefield violence to institutional incorporation. Bai Qi's capture of Ying and burning of Yiling damage Chu's symbolic center, while Nan Commandery marks the Qin side of the same process: conquered territory is not only raided, but folded into commandery-county governance.

Nan Commandery also sits beside the episode's Qianzhong discussion. In 277 BCE Qin captures [[WuJunWarringStates|巫郡]] and [[Qianzhong|黔中]] and reorganizes Qianzhong as a Qin commandery, so Nan Commandery becomes part of a broader pattern in which Qin's southern conquests are stabilized through administrative naming.
