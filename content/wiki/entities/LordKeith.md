# Lord Keith

## Overview

Lord Keith was the senior British Mediterranean naval commander whose disagreement with [[HoratioNelson|Horatio Nelson]] joined personal disapproval, competing strategic priorities, and a direct challenge to command authority.

## Current Profile

The source presents Keith as more focused on Menorca, Spain, and Gibraltar, while Nelson treated Italy, Sicily, and the Malta blockade as central. Keith also disapproved of Nelson's relationship with [[EmmaHamilton|Emma Hamilton]], but the clash cannot be reduced to scandal: it concerned where British naval power should concentrate and whether Nelson could substitute his own judgment for orders.

In early 1800 Nelson ignored Keith's instruction to move toward Menorca and instead participated in the capture of *Les Genereux* during operations connected to Malta. The success reinforced Nelson's heroic pattern of result-producing disobedience, while Keith's failure to congratulate him and redirection of prize money deepened resentment. Keith later permitted an ill and dissatisfied Nelson to return home and offered a frigate that Nelson declined in favor of an overland journey with the Hamiltons.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior commander balancing western Mediterranean priorities against Nelson's focus on Italy and Malta.
- Formal superior whose orders Nelson did not consistently obey.
- Critic of Nelson's relationship with Emma Hamilton and its effect on professional dignity.
- Counterweight to victory-centered accounts that treat successful disobedience as self-justifying.

- [[609-nelson-the-gathering-storm-part-2-glt8067718474]] links prize-money resentment, lack of congratulations, illness, repeated requests for leave, and Keith's eventual permission and frigate offer.

## Qualifications

The bounded source is structured around Nelson and provides limited independent access to Keith's orders, constraints, or assessment of the Mediterranean theater. Motives, exact command communications, prize-money responsibility, and the counterfactual merits of either strategy remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Established Keith as a strategic commander rather than only a moral critic of Nelson's affair.
- Made command authority and the retrospective reward for successful disobedience explicit qualifications of Nelson's Malta record.

## Relationships

- [[HoratioNelson]] - subordinate whose strategy and disobedience challenged Keith's authority.
- [[EmmaHamilton]] - focus of Keith's concern about Nelson's conduct and reputation.
- [[Malta]] - contested operational priority around which Nelson defended his judgment.
- [[RoyalNavy]] - institution whose hierarchy and theater-wide obligations framed the conflict.
