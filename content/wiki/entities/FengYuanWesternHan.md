## Overview

冯媛 / 冯昭仪 / Feng Yuan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-485-yi-shen-dang-xiong-jiu-huangdi-gudai-zui-yong-feizi-jing-shi-ta-lqn6f6h7zuvdr62ja3mgufy0anty|Hanji 485]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-576-dangxiong-jiu-huangdi-de-ta-weihe-bei-qingdi-bisi-ll3pgr_smwlebpgo1vlufhe6qll6|Hanji 576]] as a [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] consort whose bear-rescue merit first brings promotion and favor, then later becomes the remembered wound behind a fatal palace accusation during [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]'s reign. Hanji 576 calls her 冯太后 because she is grandmother and caretaker to the Zhongshan king Liu Jizi, later [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 485 supplies the origin scene. During a tiger-enclosure viewing, a black bear approaches Han Yuandi. Other attendants and consorts flee, but Feng Yuan steps between the emperor and the bear. In the episode's telling, she explains that a beast might stop once it has taken one person, so she placed herself in front of Yuan. Yuan then promotes her to Zhaoyi and favors her more, making her a palace focus and creating resentment from [[FuZhaoyiWesternHan|傅昭仪]].

Hanji 576 gives the later reversal. When [[ZhangYouWesternHan|张尤]] leaves Zhongshan and falsely claims Feng Yuan is cursing Aidi and Fu Taihou, the charge enters a court where [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]] already has old grievance from the bear-rescue comparison. [[ShiLiWesternHan|史立]] then uses interrogation and torture to build a treason story around Feng Yuan's alleged plan to kill Aidi and enthrone Liu Jizi. Feng Yuan does not provide the confession sought from her, but Shi Li's reference to the old bear-rescue scene convinces her that palace resentment is directing the case. She poisons herself before the prosecution fully resolves.

Her profile therefore centers on a reversal of remembered merit into vulnerability: an act that once displayed loyalty and courage later marks her as the object of another woman's jealousy and gives the investigator a clue that exposes the case's political sponsor.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han consort of Han Yuandi remembered for physically protecting him from a bear.
- Promoted to Zhaoyi and favored after the rescue in Hanji 485's account.
- Grandmother and caretaker of Liu Jizi, the Zhongshan king who later becomes Han Pingdi.
- Target of Zhang You's false curse accusation after he tries to excuse leaving Zhongshan.
- Victim of Fu Taihou's old jealousy and Shi Li's coercive investigation.
- Dies by suicide after recognizing that the case is politically directed from the palace.

## Qualifications

The page does not reconstruct Feng Yuan's full palace career, family lineage, formal title history, or the exact received-text details of the bear incident. Hanji 485's transcript form "风节鱼" is normalized to Feng Yuan / Feng Zhaoyi because the bear-rescue incident and title trajectory match the later Hanji 576 page. Hanji 576 presents the curse charge as false and politically driven, but the wiki has not independently reconstructed the full legal file.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 485's origin scene: bear rescue, promotion to Zhaoyi, and immediate palace jealousy.
- Reframed Hanji 576's accusation case as a later reversal of the same remembered merit.
