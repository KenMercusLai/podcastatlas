## Overview

冯媛 / Feng Yuan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-576-dangxiong-jiu-huangdi-de-ta-weihe-bei-qingdi-bisi-ll3pgr_smwlebpgo1vlufhe6qll6|Hanji 576]] as a former [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] consort, remembered for shielding the emperor from a bear and later dying under a politically useful accusation during [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]'s reign. The episode calls her 冯太后 because she is grandmother and caretaker to the Zhongshan king Liu Jizi, later [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 576 gives Feng Yuan two linked images. The first is courageous palace presence: when a black bear threatens Han Yuandi during a garden outing, she steps forward to protect him, gaining imperial favor and creating resentment among other consorts, especially [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]].

The second image is victim of accusation politics. When [[ZhangYouWesternHan|张尤]] leaves Zhongshan and falsely claims Feng Yuan is cursing Aidi and Fu Taihou, the charge enters a court where Fu Taihou already has old grievance. [[ShiLiWesternHan|史立]] then uses interrogation and torture to build a treason story around Feng Yuan's alleged plan to kill Aidi and enthrone Liu Jizi. Feng Yuan does not provide the confession sought from her, but Shi Li's reference to the old bear-rescue scene convinces her that palace resentment is directing the case. She poisons herself before the prosecution fully resolves.

Her profile therefore centers on a reversal of remembered merit into vulnerability: an act that once displayed loyalty and courage later marks her as the object of another woman's jealousy and gives the investigator a clue that exposes the case's political sponsor.

## Key Characteristics

- Former Western Han consort of Han Yuandi remembered for physically protecting him from a bear.
- Grandmother and caretaker of Liu Jizi, the Zhongshan king who later becomes Han Pingdi.
- Target of Zhang You's false curse accusation after he tries to excuse leaving Zhongshan.
- Victim of Fu Taihou's old jealousy and Shi Li's coercive investigation.
- Dies by suicide after recognizing that the case is politically directed from the palace.
- Source-bounded figure whose current wiki role rests on one episode rather than a full biography.
