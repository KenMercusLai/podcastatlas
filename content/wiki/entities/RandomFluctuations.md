# 随机波动 / Random Fluctuations

随机波动 / Random Fluctuations is the podcast context for [[LengJianguo|冷建国]] and [[ZhangZhiqi|张芝琪]] in [[ep258-women-ruhe-chongshi-shuiqian-dushu-lobyzxqk2oaov0v51cm355u8noix]]. The episode presents them as cultural readers whose personal habits, bodily constraints, and media experience help [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] move the bedtime-reading question away from self-discipline advice and toward [[ReadingAsLifeExperience]].

In this source, the connection matters because both guests resist a moralized reading culture. They treat unfinished books, bookstore browsing, literary taste, phone reading, AI recommendation, and nightly routines as ordinary negotiations rather than tests of cultural worth.
