# 魏昭王 / King Zhao of Wei

魏昭王 / King Zhao of Wei appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-65-wo-chuanyue-qu-gei-langman-de-chuwang-nian-shou-shi-lvyd7pve-tfu4ixsqrbdvhm6g64j]] as Wei Su, the son who succeeds after [[WeiXiangwang|魏襄王]] dies in 296 BCE.

The notice arrives in the same episode that records the five-state anti-Qin pressure reaching [[EzhiWarringStates|阏氏]] and Qin returning [[FenglingWarringStates|封陵]] to Wei. The source does not directly attribute the coalition or concession decision to Wei Zhao Wang; his role is mainly the succession marker as Wei continues to operate inside the anti-Qin field.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-72-shenme-yang-de-ren-cai-shi-zhongzhen-zhishi-logxegnqexgc7z-lodw6tr6-8f28]] gives him a compact diplomatic notice in 284 BCE. The episode says Wei Zhao Wang meets [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] and [[HanXiwang|韩釐王]] at [[Luoyi|洛邑]], the Zhou capital region, in the same year that Yan consolidates its conquest of Qi.
