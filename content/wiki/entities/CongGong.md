# 枞公 / Cong Gong

枞公 / Cong Gong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-162-1-ta-ti-liubang-qu-si-rujin-chengle-zhengzhou-de-shouhushen-li3su2zlmbm1ift9nibujgtcrxwm|Hanji 162-1]] as one of the figures [[LiuBang|刘邦]] leaves in [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]] after escaping from the [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]]. The host notes that his personal name is not supplied in the source and infers that he was likely close to [[ZhouKe|周苛]] and part of Liu Bang's older fighting circle.

Cong Gong's episode role is the execution of [[WeiBao|魏豹]] with Zhou Ke. The source treats the killing as a defensive morale decision: removing a previously defecting king reduces the risk of internal surrender politics and helps bind the garrison to a no-retreat posture against [[XiangYu|项羽]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-162-2-liubang-ruhe-shoushi-wei-gao-quan-zhong-de-xiashu-lqvye2zmqhu3otk0uwu7lakf2prz|Hanji 162-2]] completes the garrison branch by reporting that Cong Gong is killed after Xiang Yu retakes the Xingyang/[[Chenggao|成皋]] line. The episode develops [[ZhouKe|周苛]]'s refusal speech more fully, while Cong Gong remains mainly the paired loyalist killed in the same collapse.
