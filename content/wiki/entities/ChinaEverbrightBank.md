# China Everbright Bank / 光大银行

China Everbright Bank / 光大银行 appears in [[136-yinhang-licai-hai-neng-zenme-mai-llc7n0f3g2-jrz4xkoefz1nui5lt]] as an early bank wealth-management milestone. The source says Everbright Bank issued a 2004 RMB bank wealth product for ordinary retail customers using expected-return presentation and mainly investing in interest-rate bonds.

The episode later mentions Everbright-linked wealth management as one of the bank wealth-management institutions worth watching, while still keeping the main recommendation category-bound: cash-management and low-volatility fixed-income products fit bank wealth-management strengths better than complex equity or multi-asset promises.

## Source Position
- The 2004 product is used as an origin point for mass-market [[ChineseBankWealthManagement|Chinese bank wealth management]].
- Everbright's case shows how expected-return presentation helped shape customer expectations before net-value transformation.
- The source does not claim all Everbright products are suitable; it uses the bank inside a broader [[BankWealthProductSuitability]] frame.
