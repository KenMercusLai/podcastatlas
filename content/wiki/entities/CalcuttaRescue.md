# Calcutta Rescue

## Overview
Calcutta Rescue is the humanitarian organization that the source says grew from [[JackPrager|Jack Prager]]'s free pavement clinic in Kolkata into medical, educational, nutritional, and civil-document support for destitute residents.

## Current Profile
The episode portrays Calcutta Rescue as a practical response to linked barriers rather than a single-purpose clinic. Medical treatment remained central, including HIV care, but the organization also provided schooling, meals, and identity documents because illness, poverty, exclusion, and administrative invisibility reinforce one another. Access was described as independent of religion or caste.

## Key Characteristics
- Originated in free street-level medical treatment.
- Expanded into a formal organization with lawful operating status.
- Combined healthcare with education, meals, and identity-document assistance.
- Served across caste and religious boundaries.
- Addressed severe need while acknowledging that one organization could not transform the city's overall deprivation.

## Evidence
### From clinic to organization
- [[invidious-position-finding-jensen-huangs-successor-d443e5022186571d4b5372ea9e5287f5]] says Prager began treating patients beneath a flyover in 1979 and registered Calcutta Rescue in 1991.

### Integrated provision
- [[invidious-position-finding-jensen-huangs-successor-d443e5022186571d4b5372ea9e5287f5]] reports medical care, education, school meals, identity documents, and a pioneering HIV clinic as parts of the organization's work.

### Non-sectarian boundary
- [[invidious-position-finding-jensen-huangs-successor-d443e5022186571d4b5372ea9e5287f5]] describes services as available without distinction of caste or religion.

## Qualifications
The source is a short obituary-style account centered on Prager, not a current organizational profile or audited evaluation. It does not establish present programmes, governance, funding, staffing, patient outcomes, geographic coverage, or legal status. The half-million-person estimate and claim about West Bengal's first HIV clinic remain source-attributed.

## What Changed
- Established the organization as an integrated service institution rather than only a clinic.
- Made non-sectarian access and administrative support part of its current wiki profile.
