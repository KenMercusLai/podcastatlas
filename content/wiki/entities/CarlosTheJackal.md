# Carlos the Jackal

## Overview

Carlos the Jackal was an international terrorist associated in the source with bombings and assassinations before French intelligence captured him in Sudan in 1994.

## Current Profile

In [[the-road-to-damascus-the-road-to-damascus|The Road to Damascus]], Carlos matters less as a fully reconstructed biographical subject than as the proof and peril of an unnamed [[CIA]] asset's value. The former terrorist reportedly supplied the decisive tip that enabled French intelligence to find him. Publishing that rare operational achievement later gave the asset's former associates a possible route to identify him despite the omission of conventional personal details.
