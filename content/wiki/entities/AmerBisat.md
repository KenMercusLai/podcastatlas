# Amer Bisat

Amer Bisat appears in [[hard-cedar-lebanon-seeks-trumps-help-6a5f41e97bddc0bea5542de5]] as [[Lebanon]]'s economy minister. The episode cites him for the estimate that war damage totals around 40% of Lebanon's GDP and that the slowdown could push the country into recession.

In the source, Bisat's role is to make the reconstruction problem concrete. Damage, currency collapse, inflation, and unresolved bank losses mean that security policy toward [[Hezbollah]] cannot be separated from [[BankingLossAllocationPolitics]] and [[PostwarReconstructionFinance]].
