# Oleksandr Syrskyi / Sersky

Oleksandr Syrskyi is the Ukrainian commander whom [[kyiv-brawl-ukraines-top-general-fired-6a609aec4fac21e67f1be165]] says [[VolodymyrZelensky]] removed after protests over [[MykhailoFedorov]]'s dismissal. The source spelling is "Sersky"; the page normalizes to Syrskyi to reduce duplicate person pages.

In the episode's account, Syrskyi represents the older command-culture side of [[UkraineMilitaryCommandReform]]. [[ShashankJoshi]] describes him as divisive, rigid, Soviet-style, and associated with crude tactics, while the conflict with Fedorov centers on brigade organization, resource allocation, and the treatment of technologically innovative units.

Syrskyi's removal does not resolve the source's whole Ukraine problem. Fedorov remains dismissed, air-defense shortages continue, and [[MykhailoDrapatyi]] inherits a battlefield system where command responsibility, technology adoption, and casualty sensitivity all matter.
