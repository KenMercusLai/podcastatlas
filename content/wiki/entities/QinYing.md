# 秦嬴 / Qin Ying

秦嬴 / Qin Ying appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-2-lmfzo4sl9nd5kz3mrxdl1dl9efei]] through the [[ZhushuJinian|《竹书纪年》]] account of [[JinYouGong|晋幽公]]'s death. The episode explains that "秦" marks her origin from [[QinState|秦国]], while "嬴" is the surname clue; it then uses her as evidence that a "bandit" killing can be politically directed.
