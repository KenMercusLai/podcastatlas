# Valery Legasov / 列加索夫

[[ValeryLegasov|Valery Legasov / 列加索夫]] appears in [[04-qieernuobeili-de-wuye-woyao-jin-jianyu-le-530594843]] as the scientist through whom the episode reads truth, conscience, and state pressure after the [[ChernobylNuclearPowerPlant|Chernobyl]] disaster. The hosts mention that the [[HBO]] dramatization's central Legasov figure corresponds to a real suicide, while keeping the source's focus on the broader question of what he could say publicly.

The episode says Legasov gave an international account that put more responsibility on operators than he believed was fully true. His later suicide is interpreted by the source as an extreme signal that the official report was incomplete or distorted.

Legasov extends the wiki's [[PoliticalTruthTelling]] branch into technological disaster. The issue is not only personal honesty; it is whether a scientist inside a closed system can tell enough truth about design, responsibility, and future risk when [[AdministrativeOpacityAsControl]] and [[NuclearSafetyPropaganda]] are already operating.
