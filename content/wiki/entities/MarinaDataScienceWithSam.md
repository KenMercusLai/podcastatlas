# Marina (Data Science With Sam)

Marina is the [[DataScienceWithSam]] guest in [[ep-15-unveiling-data-scientists-role-in-the-generative-ai-era]]. The source identifies her as a principal consultant and data scientist at [[BellupsConsulting]], with more than 15 years of project-management experience in telecom projects.

Her central contribution is [[DataScientistGenerativeAIFluency]]: she argues that data scientists still need machine-learning and discriminative-AI foundations, but now also need domain understanding, [[PromptAsIntentTransmission|prompting]] skill, API and web-demo fluency, resource awareness, and judgment about hallucination, bias, privacy, and use-case risk.

Marina repeatedly frames generative AI as something data scientists must evaluate before adopting. In the source, that means testing outputs, reviewing generated code, respecting security rules around generated data, and using [[GenerativeAIUseCaseTriage]] when a use case needs rules, human checks, or simpler machine-learning methods.
