# FanDuel

## Overview

FanDuel appears in [[gambling-with-michael-lewis-gambling-with-michael-lewis|Gambling with Michael Lewis]] as one of the major mobile sportsbooks whose advertising scale and customer data help define the post-legalization U.S. betting market.

## Current Profile

The source groups FanDuel with [[DraftKings]] rather than presenting a company-specific operating history. [[MichaelLewisAuthor|Michael Lewis]] says the two companies spent roughly $2 billion each on marketing across five years and argues that such spending can make sports media economically reluctant to investigate the industry. The production says it declined money from both companies while reporting the season.

## Key Characteristics
