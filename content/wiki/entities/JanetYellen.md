# Janet Yellen

Janet Yellen appears in [[ep39-feng-man-lou-xiaji-quanqiu-shuaitui-manman-bijin-yanfang-sishou-bubu-weiying-manliao-xiabannian-meigu-meizhai-huilv-lgz718un4sjvgxqbydfikyxz0hcj]] as the U.S. fiscal-policy figure attached to the episode's discussion of short-term versus long-term Treasury issuance. [[LaoMai]] interprets the short-debt emphasis as a possible attempt to avoid locking in high long-term funding costs before eventual [[FederalReserve]] cuts.

[[tsr-s5-ronconway-v5-tsr-s5-ronconway-v5]] adds Yellen through [[RonConway]]'s account of the [[SiliconValleyBank]] weekend. Conway says he watched Yellen say on Sunday morning that the government was not going to guarantee deposits, became angry because the decision deadline was hours away, and later concluded she may have been avoiding getting ahead of regulators before consensus existed.
