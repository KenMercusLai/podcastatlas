# 肖磊

肖磊 is one of the [[Banlatte|半拿铁]] hosts in [[fanwai-14-gen-lidan-liaoliao-boke-chuangzuo-ai-yu-zhongnian-972693552]]. The episode gives his background as a computer-science undergraduate who later studied broadcasting at [[ChinaCommunicationUniversity|中国传媒大学]] and worked in traditional radio before moving toward podcast production.

His source role is to make the bridge from radio to podcasting concrete. He describes how traditional radio understood the threat of podcasts but did not easily convert that awareness into institutional action, making 半拿铁 a case of individual media-worker transition rather than a simple broadcaster-to-podcast pipeline.
