# 《了凡四训》 / Liaofan Sixun

《了凡四训》 / Liaofan Sixun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-161-caiyun-he-renji-guanxi-de-liangzi-jiuchan-4-lml7lpd3u9l7esryknmhoampgx6v|Hanji 161 part 4]] as the traditional text attached to [[YuanLiaofan|袁了凡]]'s fate-change story. The host presents it as a practical manual for altering life direction through intention, virtue, good deeds, and faith-backed practice.

The source treats the text as a bridge between older self-cultivation and modern concerns about wealth, relationships, family, and career. It is said to have influenced [[ZengGuofan|曾国藩]] in修身 and official conduct, and [[InamoriKazuo|稻盛和夫]] in self-cultivation and enterprise management.

The page also repairs the earlier [[MingZiWoLi|《命自我利》]] branch. Hanji 161 part 1 preserved that title exactly as rendered in the source file; part 4 identifies the same opening story as Yuan Liaofan's story and names 《了凡四训》, so the wiki treats 《命自我利》 as a source/transcript variant rather than a separate established text unless future sources prove otherwise.
