# Charles Branas

## Overview

Charles Branas is a researcher whose Philadelphia vacant-lot study supplies the episode's non-carceral, environmental reinterpretation of broken windows.

## Current Profile

The episode describes a study led by Branas of cleaning and greening neglected vacant lots. It reports that removing waste, planting grass, and installing low fences was associated with a 29 percent reduction in gun violence.

[[MalcolmGladwell]] uses the finding to distinguish repairing disordered places from arresting people for minor disorder. The study therefore supports [[PlaceBasedViolencePrevention]] in the bounded source, while the summary does not provide its design, intervention assignment, outcomes, uncertainty, or transfer limits.

## Key Characteristics

- Studies relationships among urban environments, public health, and violence.
- Supplies a place-repair alternative to person-focused disorder enforcement.
- Is represented through an episode-reported effect requiring primary-study verification.
