# Georg Simmel / 齐美尔

Georg Simmel / 齐美尔 is cited near the end of [[162-caifu-de-benzhi-yiji-ziyou-de-zhenzheng-hanyi-chuantai-shifen-xiyin-lofyxfhiprud7zvekufexsjhtust]] through The Philosophy of Money. The source uses him to make the episode's final boundary: money is a bridge toward final values, not a place where a person can finally live.

In the episode, Simmel supports [[WealthDesireGap]] and [[WealthAsCapabilityAndRights]]. Money can help claim resources, reduce fear, and create options, but when it becomes the final object of desire, it can turn into another form of dependence rather than freedom.
