# Anta Sports / 安踏

Anta Sports / 安踏 appears in [[vol-127-nianbaoji-zhong-de-zhenshi-zhongguo-2025-lqjy2pnfy09zyewj0ljxjuc3gxqv]] as a consumer-brand and operating-system case. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] contrasts it with the older image of a Jinjiang shoe company, emphasizing that Anta has become a multi-brand sportswear group with both franchise and direct-retail capabilities.

The source's Anta point is that consumer companies can build [[ConsumerBrandMoat|brand moats]] through more than advertising. Direct stores can increase sensitivity to changing customer demand, while the broader operating system turns supply chain, retail format, and portfolio management into part of the brand's durability.

## Source Position
- Anta is used as an example of Chinese consumer-company maturation.
- The episode stresses the shift from single domestic brand to multi-brand group operation.
- Its mixed franchise/direct model is treated as a way to balance scale with consumer feedback.
- Anta sits between [[CompressedCorporateEvolution]] and [[ThreeLayerScaleEconomies]]: it grows through brand acquisition, channel learning, and domestic operating scale.
