# Anta Sports / 安踏

## Overview
Anta Sports / 安踏 is represented in the wiki as a Chinese sportswear group whose evidence spans Jinjiang-era brand comparison, listed-company operating maturity, and acquired-brand expansion into women's yoga and fitness experiences.

## Current Profile
The bounded sources move Anta from a historical Chinese footwear-brand benchmark into a multi-brand consumer operating system. [[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw]] uses Anta as the successful advertising, endorsement, and listing route that [[WoteShoes|沃特鞋企]] missed. [[vol-127-nianbaoji-zhong-de-zhenshi-zhongguo-2025-lqjy2pnfy09zyewj0ljxjuc3gxqv]] treats Anta as a multi-brand sportswear group with franchise and direct-retail capabilities. [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] adds [[MayaActive|Maya Active]] as Anta's women's yoga-apparel branch, where the group is testing offline exercise experience as a way to build brand identity.

## Key Characteristics
- Functions as a benchmark for Chinese footwear and sportswear brand maturation beyond factory production.
- Uses multi-brand portfolio management rather than relying only on the original Anta label.
- Combines franchise scale with direct-retail sensitivity to consumer demand.
- Uses acquisition to fill category gaps, with Maya Active supplying a women's yoga and athleisure branch.
- Tests [[SportswearExperienceSpace|offline fitness experience]] as a brand-identity and user-operation mechanism.

## Evidence
- Footwear benchmark: [[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw]] uses Anta as a successful Chinese footwear-brand path contrasted with Wote's missed brand cycle.
- Operating-system evidence: [[vol-127-nianbaoji-zhong-de-zhenshi-zhongguo-2025-lqjy2pnfy09zyewj0ljxjuc3gxqv]] presents Anta as a consumer company that has moved from a Jinjiang shoe image into multi-brand, franchise, and direct-retail operation.
- Category-gap evidence: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says Anta acquired 75% of Maya Active in 2023 to fill its women's yoga-sports field.
- Experience-space evidence: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says Maya Active will open a large Shanghai fitness space with Pure to build brand recognition and feed product sales.

## Qualifications
The sources do not provide Anta's full financial results, detailed Maya Active integration economics, or evidence that the fitness-space strategy will scale. Maya Active's store count and pricing remain source-scoped claims, and the episode itself notes that foreign high-end brand playbooks may not transfer cleanly to an acquired local brand.
