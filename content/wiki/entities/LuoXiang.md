## Overview

罗翔 / Luo Xiang is a legal scholar and public intellectual represented in the wiki through discussions of 《法律的悖论》 and 《刑法学讲义》. Across the current sources, his function is to make legal knowledge publicly accessible while keeping judgment constrained by uncertainty, dignity, proportionality, and the danger of state punishment.

## Current Profile

Luo's legal profile centers judgment where plausible principles conflict. Criminal law is both a response to wrongdoing and a restraint on coercive power; punishment must not collapse into moral disgust, crowd fury, or single-principle certainty. [[100-fa-lv-de-bei-lun-xie-tian-xie-di-xing-hao-wo-men-hai-you-luo-xiang-733477302|《法律的悖论》 episode]]

His public-knowledge profile extends the same restraint into learning: admitting ignorance begins the pursuit of wisdom, every discipline can point beyond its narrow branch toward truth, and knowledge should reach willing public minds rather than remain a status performance inside specialist spaces. These claims are attributed through the host's use of the 《刑法学讲义》 preface. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1139-jiemi-gudai-rouxing-yige-bi-yige-xiaren-lgnj-8smphspf8nud-2o3xh_lggk|Hanji 1139]]

## Key Characteristics

- Frames legal judgment as work under conflicting values rather than delivery of automatic answers.
- Treats criminal law as a restraint on state power as well as a means of punishment.
- Associates intellectual seriousness with acknowledged ignorance and disciplined doubt.
- Presents public accessibility as part of knowledge's purpose rather than a dilution of expertise.

## Qualifications

Both pages summarize podcasts interpreting Luo's books rather than the complete books or a direct interview. The wording and emphases should therefore be attributed to the source presentations, not treated as a comprehensive account of Luo's scholarship. Public communication does not remove the need for specialist evidence, and intellectual humility does not imply that all legal positions are equally supported.
