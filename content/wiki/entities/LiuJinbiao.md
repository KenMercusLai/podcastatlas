# Liu Jinbiao / 刘金彪

Liu Jinbiao is the founder associated with [[GiantBicycles]] in [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]]. The episode says he founded Giant's manufacturing base in Taichung in 1972 and later helped build the Giant brand that entered mainland China in the early 1990s.

His source role is to connect contract manufacturing, brand creation, and retail-channel renewal. In the episode's China branch, Giant's growth shows how a company could compete with state-era bicycle brands by pairing manufacturing capability with consumer-facing product design and service.
