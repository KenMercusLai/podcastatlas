# iGentic AI

## Overview
iGentic AI is the company co-founded and led by [[ZahraDataScienceWithSam|Dr. Zahra]] in [[ep-52-the-ai-ceo-leadership-governance-and-the-ai-gold-rush|Data Science With Sam EP52]]. The source positions it around enterprise AI governance and regulated-industry adoption.

## Current Profile
The episode says iGentic AI was founded in 2024 and initially emphasized powerful technology before shifting toward measurable customer pain and return on investment. Its named product is [[GiniIGenticAI|Gini]], described as an enterprise governance platform intended to make compliance an enabler of adoption rather than only an obstacle.

## Key Characteristics
- Enterprise AI company presented through a governance and compliance lens.
- Founded in 2024 according to the episode summary.
- Shifted from technology-first building toward problem-first customer value and ROI.
- Develops Gini as its named enterprise governance platform.
- Operates in the source's broader frame of human accountability in regulated AI use.
