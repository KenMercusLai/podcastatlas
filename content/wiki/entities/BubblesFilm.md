# Bubbles (Isaac Adamson screenplay)

## Overview

*Bubbles* is [[IsaacAdamson]]'s unproduced screenplay about [[MichaelJackson]], narrated by [[BubblesChimpanzee|Bubbles the chimpanzee]] and conceived as stop-motion animation.

## Current Profile

The screenplay treats Bubbles as an exiled heir who mistakes “King of Pop” for literal monarchy. His elevated voice, pride, hierarchical worldview, and misreading of human events produce comedy while making humiliation, displacement, and continuing loyalty emotionally serious.

The project topped the 2015 Black List and was later acquired by Netflix. After *Leaving Neverland*, revisions gave Bubbles clearer knowledge of alleged abuse and a firmer moral break with Jackson. Despite advanced pre-production, an attached director and animation team, the project ended after Taika Waititi withdrew; the source says rights later reverted to Adamson.

## Key Characteristics

- Stop-motion feature concept centered on an animal protagonist rather than a human celebrity observer.
- Uses pseudo-Shakespearean voice and chimpanzee hierarchy to reinterpret Jackson's life.
- Combines comedy, tragedy, celebrity history, abandonment, and disputed allegations.
- Changed morally and tonally after *Leaving Neverland* altered the cultural context.
- Reached advanced development and pre-production without becoming a completed film.
