# 河狸家 / Helijia

## Overview
河狸家 / Helijia is the home-beauty and artisan-service O2O company the episode uses as the marketplace-style contrast to standardized manicure operations.

## Current Profile
The source presents Helijia as Diaoye Meng Xing's 2014 attempt to "liberate artisans" and build a Taobao-like marketplace for service workers. It gained a high valuation and large financing during the O2O boom, but the episode later notes shrinkage and 2024 complaints around withdrawal and refunds, with Diaoye saying he had exited years earlier.

## Key Characteristics
- It is framed as an artisan-marketplace version of local-services O2O.
- Its slogan and positioning emphasized independent service workers rather than only standardized service operations.
- Its early financing and valuation reflect the intensity of the 2014-2015 O2O wave.
- Its later shrinkage and complaint reports make it a caution about platform trust and cash handling in local services.
