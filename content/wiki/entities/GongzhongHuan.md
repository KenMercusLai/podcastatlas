# 公中缓 / Gongzhong Huan

公中缓 / Gongzhong Huan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-2-lmnqfjmydygqnfec6xhzpixralvi]] as the rival of [[LiangHuiWang|魏罃]] after [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]] dies without clearly designating a successor. The source says their struggle throws [[WeiState|魏国]] into disorder.

His wiki role is source-scoped and tied to [[SuccessionNonDesignationRisk]]. The episode does not develop Gongzhong Huan as an independent biography; it uses him as the alternative claimant whose existence makes Wei Wuhou's failure to settle succession costly.
