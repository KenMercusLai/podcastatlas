# Prague / 布拉格

[[Prague|Prague / 布拉格]] appears in [[12-man-dang-milan-kundela-yi-ju-zhengjing-meiyou-549335675]] as more than a place name. In the source's reading of [[SlowKundera|《慢》]], Berck's public embarrassment depends on mistaking Prague for Budapest and then treating Czech, Polish, and Eastern European references as interchangeable.

The Czech scholar's corrections matter because the episode treats [[Prague|Prague]] as a marker of concrete historical identity: a city tied to western cultural self-understanding, university history, Jan Hus, and the refusal to be dissolved into careless geopolitical shorthand. The mistake exposes the gap between [[PublicMoralPerformance|performed solidarity]] and actual knowledge.
