# University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

The University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign appears in [[tech-20260107-0107-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260107-0107-mp-tech-pod-128]] through [[AndrewNelson]]'s reference to UIUC and its [[CropWizard]] project. The episode uses the university context to make agricultural AI practical: images or situation descriptions can lead a farmer toward relevant research documentation.

In the wiki, UIUC is an agricultural-research node for [[DigitalAgriculture]] rather than a broad university profile. Its contribution in this source is the research-grounding side of [[AIFarmDecisionSupport]], where AI helps surface evidence but does not replace farm judgment or agronomic expertise.
