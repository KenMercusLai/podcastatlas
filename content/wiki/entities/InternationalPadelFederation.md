# International Padel Federation

The International Padel Federation appears in [[spring-then-fall-a-weakened-muslim-brotherhood-6a7aef1b616564bf00d9b354]] as the body trying to expand [[Padel]] beyond its strongest bases in Spain, Argentina, Europe, and South America. The episode says the federation wants more growth in Asia and North America and hopes the sport could become Olympic, potentially by the 2032 Brisbane Games.

Its role in the source is ambition-setting. Participation has grown quickly, but Olympic inclusion, broadcast appeal, and wider geographic adoption still require organizational scale beyond local courts and friendly communities.
