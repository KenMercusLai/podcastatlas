# Harald Bluetooth

## Overview

Harald Bluetooth appears in the wiki as the Danish king whose Jelling claim joined territorial consolidation, dynastic commemoration, and Christian conversion into a new model of royal authority.

## Current Profile

The episode places Harald within a dynasty consolidating Denmark as England was being unified under Alfred's descendants. At Jelling, Harald commemorated his parents and claimed to have united Denmark and Norway and made the Danes Christian; the site therefore materializes continuity with a pagan royal past alongside a Christian state-building claim.

Harald's son [[SweynForkbeard|Sweyn Forkbeard]] overthrew him but inherited the more concentrated Danish power that later threatened England. The familiar Bluetooth technology name and runic logo are presented as Harald's modern cultural afterlife rather than part of the medieval political argument.

## Key Characteristics

- Danish dynast whose royal claim combines family memory, territorial rule, and Christianization.
- Jelling patron used to symbolize a fusion of pagan inheritance and Christian monarchy.
- Predecessor whose consolidation supplies institutional capacity to Sweyn's conquests.
- Medieval ruler with an unusually visible modern naming afterlife through Bluetooth technology.

## Qualifications

The episode's broad state-formation interpretation does not establish the reach, uniformity, or popular experience of Harald's rule and conversion program. His Jelling claims are royal claims, not neutral measurements of control.

## What Changed

- Established a first profile of Harald as Jelling dynast, Christianizing consolidator, and institutional predecessor to Sweyn.
