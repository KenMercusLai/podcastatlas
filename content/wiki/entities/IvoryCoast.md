# Ivory Coast

Ivory Coast appears in [[reeses-heir-vs-chocolate-skimpflation]] as one of the major cocoa-producing countries whose weather problems contributed to a [[CocoaSupplyShock]]. The episode groups it with [[Ghana]] when explaining why cocoa and cocoa-butter prices rose sharply enough to pressure chocolate companies.

The page's role is commodity geography. Consumers encounter the shock through [[Reeses|Reese's]] labels, chocolate prices, package sizes, or formulas, but the upstream pressure begins in agricultural production exposed to heat, drought, excessive rainfall, and crop disease risk.
