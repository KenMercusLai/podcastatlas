# 魏王假 / King Jia of Wei

魏王假 / King Jia of Wei enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-2-yanguo-cisha-qinshihuang-weihe-hui-xuan-jingke-lsewjrmgup1yanf8ipn-i0ykqwjx]] as the successor to [[WeiJingminWang|魏景湣王 / 魏增]]. The episode mentions his accession only briefly before turning to [[YanState|燕国]] and [[JingKe|荆轲]].

The source-scoped point is Wei's weakness, not Jia's personal policy. By this stage of the Qinji sequence, [[WeiState|魏国]] is described as unable to reverse its position under [[QinState|秦国]] pressure, making the old hezong repair option look too slow for [[YanTaiziDan|燕太子丹]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-117-1-papa-dalian-lishishang-shuangwen-de-kaishan-bizu-lr0h-kfeguvatattnj-mz8fgvnc]] gives Jia's endpoint. [[WangBenQin|王贲]] attacks [[Daliang|大梁]] by water, and after roughly three months the damaged city can no longer hold. Jia surrenders and is killed in the episode's account, making him the last Wei ruler in this source branch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-120-1-shui-shi-huangdi-zhong-de-mabaonan-lhw8vsj6ta6rjdkugcvfohaqqxc0]] brings Wei Wang Jia into Qin's post-unification defense story. Qin's version says Wei only pretended submission while secretly joining Han and Zhao against Qin, so Wei's destruction becomes part of the new empire's moralized explanation for conquest. This page keeps that as [[ConquestSelfDefenseNarrative|兼并自卫叙事]] rather than as a separate verified biography claim.
