# 徐州 / Xuzhou

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-941-shenchu-guanchang-ta-jing-xiang-ganfan-huangdi-liu-fqiz9pi7u6cltrn-cjmjz-r|Hanji 941]] adds a late-Han recurrence notice before the later Cao Cao and Liu Bei branches. The episode says Yellow Turban forces in [[QingzhouLateHan|青州]] and Xuzhou rise again in late 188 and attack commanderies and counties, making Xuzhou part of the same civilian-disorder field that later becomes a warlord base and massacre setting.

Xuzhou appears in [[105-su-dongpo-yiqiang-haomai-zhiqi-yi-dupi-buhe-shiyi-741072645]] as one of [[SuShi|苏轼]]'s consequential local posts. The episode highlights his flood-response work there, saying he led people to resist flooding and preserve the city.

The source uses Xuzhou to balance the popular Su Dongpo image of food, poetry, and leisurely scenery. Su Shi's public value also lies in emergency coordination, practical rule adjustment, and local responsibility, which connects the episode to [[LocalGovernanceAsCare]] and the wiki's broader [[DisasterResponseStateCapacity]] frame.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-27-weiguan-weihuiwang-he-qiweiwang-shi-za-shangye-hupeng-lminlxhrm5bethub9kyy-8blimrb]] adds a much earlier Warring States use of the place-name through [[XuzhouMutualKingship|徐州相王]]. In that source, [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] and [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王 / 梁惠王]] meet at Xuzhou and mutually recognize kingly titles after [[MalingBattle|马陵之战]], making the page connect local-governance Xuzhou to a distinct pre-Qin diplomacy node.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-28-hanzhaohou-de-kuaile-ni-xiangxiang-budao-ln2tbxniz4kvwogbwp32exmdokz8]] adds the sequel: Xuzhou becomes the place [[ChuWeiwang|楚威王]] surrounds or attacks after the title exchange angers other rulers. The location therefore marks both the ceremony of [[XuzhouMutualKingship|徐州相王]] and the visible cost of [[StatusOrderBacklash|名分秩序反弹]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-122-3-qinshihuang-jing-he-shenxian-si-qilai-le-ljnw5estg6dvgfllm3kfcq6ulhxu]] adds an earlier Qin-imperial use of the same place through its Pengcheng name. After leaving former Qi territory, [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] passes through Pengcheng, identified by the host with present-day Xuzhou, to search the Si River for a lost [[Jiuding|九鼎]]. The failed search makes Xuzhou/Pengcheng a site where imperial travel intersects with missing Zhou legitimacy symbols, distinct from both Xuzhou mutual kingship and Su Shi local governance.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-132-5wan-wanbao-40wan-xiangyu-cizhan-fengshen-lk87nb3fskg5zh6fcgptypixhzuw|Qinji 132]] adds Pengcheng as the new Chu political center after [[XiangLiang|项梁]] dies at [[Dingtao|定陶]]. [[XiangYu|项羽]], [[LiuBang|刘邦]], and [[LuChenQinRebel|吕臣]] move [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]] there from Xuyi. From Pengcheng, Mi Xin merges troops, distributes titles, recognizes [[WeiBao|魏豹]], and issues [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]], turning the place into the staging point for both the [[Julu|巨鹿]] rescue and Liu Bang's westward route toward [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-151-huanyuan-pengcheng-zhizhan-xiangyu-zai-fengshen-1-lspx-bqe5kczq6bmn6thlerfbrmu|Hanji 151 part 1]] makes Pengcheng the immediate object of [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s anti-[[XiangYu|项羽]] coalition. Liu Bang captures the city while Xiang Yu is tied down by [[TianHeng|田横]] in Qi, but the source's emphasis falls on the danger after capture: Pengcheng's palace wealth, women, and celebratory drinking become the setting for [[VictoryOverreachFailure|数胜必亡式胜利反噬]] just before Xiang Yu returns.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-151-huanyuan-pengcheng-zhizhan-xiangyu-zai-fengshen-2-lqy1uu29xbfipo9e0kq6obkl3oay|Hanji 151 part 2]] makes the area around Pengcheng the battle's tactical field. Xiang Yu secretly returns, takes [[XiaoCounty|萧县]] to the west rather than immediately attacking the occupied capital, then drives Liu Bang's coalition back through Pengcheng-side river terrain around Gu water, [[SiRiver|泗水]], and [[SuiRiver|睢水]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-971-luanshi-miyun-shui-sha-le-caocao-laodie-ljh3hdhssigmv78bueo-hprfqwku|Hanji 971]] supplies the darker immediate prehistory to the later Xuzhou transfer branch. After [[CaoSong|曹嵩]] is killed, [[CaoCao|曹操]] attacks [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]], defeats him near Pengcheng, and carries retaliation into the [[XuzhouMassacreLateHan|徐州屠杀]]. The episode stresses that refugees from the Luoyang disaster had already moved east into Xuzhou, so revenge warfare falls on a crowded civilian refuge rather than only on Tao Qian's army.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-973-changpingguan-zhizhan-shengsi-zhidi-zhanhou-jing-chonggui-yuhao-lgazc9dewfayb8qnkncfkfa12yet|Hanji 973]] adds the first late-Han foothold before the deathbed transfer scene. [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] seeks help from [[TianKaiLateHan|田楷]] and [[LiuBei|刘备]] against [[CaoCao|曹操]], then gives Liu Bei four thousand Danyang soldiers, recommends him as Yuzhou inspector, and stations him at [[PeiCounty|小沛]].

The same source also shows why Xuzhou remains vulnerable. Cao Cao's renewed attack damages towns and defeats Liu Bei east of Tan, but the offensive is interrupted when [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] and [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] bring [[LyuBu|吕布]] into [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]] adds the initial late-Han handoff scene. [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] dies after telling [[MiZhu|糜竺]] that only [[LiuBei|刘备]] can protect Xuzhou; Liu Bei first refuses because he has little force and the province is surrounded by local and outside risks. [[ChenDeng|陈登]] and [[KongRong|孔融]] then argue him into accepting.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-982-ba-yi-ba-liubei-ru-xu-beihou-de-liyi-zhi-zheng-ljajdc95xtylhkn5teefz26yyyz1|Hanji 982]] adds the late-Han transfer logic behind [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s initial position. [[MiZhu|糜竺]], [[ChenDeng|陈登]], and [[KongRong|孔融]] support Liu Bei because he is legitimate and reputable but not strong enough to consume the local order. Xuzhou therefore becomes the main source case for [[LocalEliteProxySelection|地方豪强代理人选择]], with [[YuanShu|袁术]] rejected as a Huainan-backed absorption risk.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-986-tianyu-qiwang-bixian-lingqi-kuang-lvlvu057-tnz3hal6oodhydvzvbd|Hanji 986]] adds the immediate late-Han succession trigger. [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] dies, [[LiuBei|刘备]] takes over Xuzhou, and [[CaoCao|曹操]] considers attacking before Liu Bei's position has settled. The source stops at the strategic question, which Hanji 987 then answers through [[RootBasePriority|根本根据地优先]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-987-dingtao-zhizhan-caocao-he-lvbu-dianfeng-duijue-lofq0pq8n9rrfvfttunahisy0ave|Hanji 987]] adds the late-Han temptation phase before the later Xuzhou transfer crisis. After [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] dies and [[LiuBei|刘备]] has not yet fully rooted himself, [[CaoCao|曹操]] sees Xuzhou as a possible eastward target. [[XunYu|荀彧]] warns that the target is less attractive than it looks: Cao Cao's earlier harsh campaign has damaged Xuzhou民心, the wheat is already harvested, and attacking it would expose [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] to [[LyuBu|吕布]] and [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1018-ta-weihe-rang-caocao-jidan-sunce-weiju-liubei-wanxi-lqccdhrjmero-gt7vsaq2ejuhhw8|Hanji 1018]] adds a late-Han administrative-status use. [[LyuBu|吕布]] wants [[ChenDeng|陈登]] to obtain the Xuzhou governor title from [[CaoCao|曹操]], but the court does not grant it. In this source, Xuzhou is not a battle site; it is the office title that makes Lü Bu feel either recognized or cheated.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1016-sanguo-luanshi-li-chengui-jiazu-de-mingyun-jueze-ltuckdcxpyxk-xmzp8rt-2gjfzcs|Hanji 1016]] adds the immediately earlier exposure risk. [[ChenGui|陈珪]] argues that if [[LyuBu|吕布]] accepts [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s marriage alliance, Xuzhou will become the forward zone for Yuan Shu's northern ambitions. The page therefore gains [[MarriageAllianceWarExposure|联姻同盟战场暴露]] as a late-Han complement to the later governor-title dispute.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu|Hanji 999]] adds the earlier transfer crisis that makes those later Xuzhou title disputes meaningful. [[YuanShu|袁术]] attacks [[LiuBei|刘备]], uses grain to draw [[LyuBu|吕布]] toward [[Xiapi|下邳]], and benefits from the disorder after [[ZhangFei|张飞]] kills [[CaoBaoLateHan|曹豹]]. Xuzhou shifts from Liu Bei's fragile base into Lü Bu's claimed position, while Liu Bei survives only by submitting and moving to [[PeiCounty|小沛]].
