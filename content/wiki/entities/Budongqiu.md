## Overview
不懂球 / Budongqiu is the football podcast context for episodes that the wiki uses to analyze Chinese football legacy, Hong Kong football history, and sportswear business. The sources show the program moving from [[Evergrande]]'s football assets and supporter memory into [[HongKong|Hong Kong]] professionalization history and a broader sports-business comparison of [[Adidas]] and [[Nike]].

## Current Profile
Within the wiki, Budongqiu functions as a sports-analysis source rather than a fully profiled media institution. One branch gives football-specific context that an Evergrande property-finance source could not supply: how [[GuangzhouFC]] is remembered, why [[EvergrandeFootballSchool]] still matters, and why successor teams such as [[GuangzhouLeopard]] and [[GuangdongChenxingJuli]] are emotionally different for former supporters.

The Hong Kong football branch adds historical range. With [[LiJunrongFootballHistorian|Li Junrong]] as guest, the show treats [[HongKongFootballProfessionalization]] as an institutional accident shaped by Olympic amateur-status rules, paid-player reality, semi-professional employment, foreign-player recruitment, and commercial-team governance. This connects football history to the same sports-business questions that appear in the Evergrande branch: who pays, who controls, what rules keep a league viable, and how club identity survives institutional change.

The Adidas-versus-Nike episode adds a consumer-brand branch. It uses football apparel, jerseys, retro shoes, channel choices, and running-shoe technology to explain why [[BrandDNAConsumerCycleFit]] can matter as much as short-term channel reform when sportswear brands move through different fashion and economic cycles.
