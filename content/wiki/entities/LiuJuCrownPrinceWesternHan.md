## Overview

卫太子刘据 / Liu Ju enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-396-2-hanwudi-zhizi-liuju-wugu-zhi-an-hou-fuhuo-le-lhj6dzjsikkmbbcjlfgnz1bbok-1|Hanji 396-2]] as the dead former crown prince whose name is used by [[ChengFangsuiWesternHan|程方遂]] in a palace-gate imposture under [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]].

## Current Profile

The episode does not give Liu Ju a full biography. His source role is posthumous and political: because he was [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s crown prince and was believed dead after the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]], the claim that he had returned carries enough charge to unsettle officials and draw a large crowd near [[WeiyangPalace|未央宫]]. The case shows that a dead heir can remain politically active as a memory, rumor, and claimant identity even when the living claimant is false.

## Key Characteristics

- Former Western Han crown prince and son of Han Wudi.
- Figure associated in the episode with the Wugu affair and presumed death before Han Zhao's reign.
- Posthumous claimant identity used by Cheng Fangsui to seek wealth and rank.
- Succession-memory figure whose possible survival makes officials hesitate.
- Legitimacy-risk figure because his name can attract public attention and destabilize a young ruler's court.

- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-396-2-hanwudi-zhizi-liuju-wugu-zhi-an-hou-fuhuo-le-lhj6dzjsikkmbbcjlfgnz1bbok-1|Hanji 396-2]] says Han Zhao orders senior officials to inspect the claimant and that the resemblance makes them hesitate.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-396-2-hanwudi-zhizi-liuju-wugu-zhi-an-hou-fuhuo-le-lhj6dzjsikkmbbcjlfgnz1bbok-1|Hanji 396-2]] says commoners gather at the palace gate after hearing the rumor of the crown prince's return.

Imposture resolution:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-396-2-hanwudi-zhizi-liuju-wugu-zhi-an-hou-fuhuo-le-lhj6dzjsikkmbbcjlfgnz1bbok-1|Hanji 396-2]] says Jun Buyi argues that even a real surviving Liu Ju would not be a valid returning claimant after offending the former emperor and fleeing.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-396-2-hanwudi-zhizi-liuju-wugu-zhi-an-hou-fuhuo-le-lhj6dzjsikkmbbcjlfgnz1bbok-1|Hanji 396-2]] says the claimant later confesses that he is Cheng Fangsui, not Liu Ju.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 396-2. It does not reconstruct Liu Ju's full life, his mother and household, the complete Wugu affair, the evidence for his death, or later historiographical debates about his responsibility.
