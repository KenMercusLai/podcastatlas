## Overview

卫太子刘据 / Liu Ju enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-396-2-hanwudi-zhizi-liuju-wugu-zhi-an-hou-fuhuo-le-lhj6dzjsikkmbbcjlfgnz1bbok-1|Hanji 396-2]] as the dead former crown prince whose name is used by [[ChengFangsuiWesternHan|程方遂]] in a palace-gate imposture under [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-391-1-wei-le-li-taizi-hanwudi-weihe-xian-sha-chongfei-gouyi-furen-lhsva8zj7n7mfy6u4vyfhrwmbwzg|Hanji 391-1]] adds the prior frame-up branch: the host says Liu Ju was destroyed by false accusation involving [[JiangChongWesternHan|江充]], [[SuWenWesternHan|苏文]], and, source-scopedly, [[LadyGouyiWesternHan|钩弋夫人]] as the hidden mover.

## Current Profile

The sources do not give Liu Ju a full biography. Hanji 391-1 uses him as the victim of a late-Wudi succession frame-up: the host says Han Wudi later knows Liu Ju was wronged and treats Lady Gouyi as the true behind-the-scenes actor, though that judgment remains source-scoped. Hanji 396-2 gives Liu Ju a posthumous political role: because he was Han Wudi's crown prince and was believed dead after the Wugu affair, the claim that he had returned carries enough charge to unsettle officials and draw a large crowd near [[WeiyangPalace|未央宫]]. The case shows that a dead heir can remain politically active as a memory, rumor, and claimant identity even when the living claimant is false.

## Key Characteristics

- Former Western Han crown prince and son of Han Wudi.
- Figure associated in the episode with the Wugu affair and presumed death before Han Zhao's reign.
- Source-scoped victim of a frame-up attributed to Jiang Chong, Su Wen, and the host's hidden Lady Gouyi explanation.
- Posthumous claimant identity used by Cheng Fangsui to seek wealth and rank.
- Succession-memory figure whose possible survival makes officials hesitate.
- Legitimacy-risk figure because his name can attract public attention and destabilize a young ruler's court.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 396-2 and Hanji 391-1. It does not reconstruct Liu Ju's full life, his mother and household, the complete Wugu affair, the evidence for his death, or later historiographical debates about his responsibility. Hanji 391-1's claim about Lady Gouyi as hidden mover is recorded as the episode's interpretation.
