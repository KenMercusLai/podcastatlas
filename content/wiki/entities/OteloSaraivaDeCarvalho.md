# Otelo Saraiva de Carvalho

## Overview

Otelo Saraiva de Carvalho was the operational strategist of Portugal's [[CarnationRevolution]] and later a radical military actor in the [[PortugueseRevolutionaryProcess]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Otelo as a figure of sharp political transformation: an African-war officer once photographed mourning Salazar who moved left, coordinated the 1974 coup, visited Cuba, and advocated revolutionary councils over party government. His failed 25 November 1975 deployment ended his immediate military power; later involvement with a violent far-left organization complicated the revolution's peaceful public memory.

## Key Characteristics

- Planned the coup's operational sequence and radio-song signals.
- Moved from service within the old imperial military toward radical left politics.
- Favored revolutionary councils rather than conventional party rule.
- Lost the November 1975 confrontation to moderate military forces.
- Later became associated with the Popular Forces of the 25th of April.

## Evidence

- Coup evidence: [[273-portugal-the-carnation-revolution-glt3297148398]] identifies Otelo as the key strategist behind the April operation.
- Radicalization evidence: [[273-portugal-the-carnation-revolution-glt3297148398]] links his Cuba visit and meeting with Fidel Castro to advocacy of council government.
- Afterlife evidence: [[273-portugal-the-carnation-revolution-glt3297148398]] records imprisonment after 25 November, weak later electoral support, paramilitary involvement, imprisonment, and eventual amnesty.

## Qualifications

The episode's later-life treatment is anecdotal and personality-driven. Exact responsibility within the Popular Forces, legal findings, and the meaning of his late comments on Salazar require fuller sourcing.
