## Overview
《三国志》 / Sanguozhi is a classical Three Kingdoms source tradition used in the wiki for attribution, literary-memory correction, and front-four-history orientation.

## Current Profile
The current synthesis treats Sanguozhi as concise but consequential. Hanji 591-2 introduces it as part of the front-four histories while noting its special structure: it is short and lacks several tables and treatises common in fuller official histories. Hanji 971 uses it for the version that makes [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] directly responsible for sending cavalry against [[CaoSong|曹嵩]]. Hanji 1041 uses it to correct the novelized "关公约三事" memory, presenting [[GuanYu|关羽]] as captured, appointed, generously treated, and still loyal to [[LiuBei|刘备]] rather than bound by a formal three-condition surrender pact.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a source tradition for politically consequential attribution claims.
- Helps separate event evidence from later literary memory around Three Kingdoms figures.
- Is introduced as concise and structurally unusual within the front-four-history group.
- Can sharpen moral judgment by changing who acted, under what status, and with what recorded wording.

## Evidence
- Front-four-history orientation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-591-2-ruhe-geng-gaoxiao-di-dudong-lishi-gudai-biannian-jianzan-juequ-qiannian-fengliu-yunyan-lruv48fgfpo7u0tjsqb264k0y7yq|Hanji 591-2]] introduces Sanguozhi as composed of Wei, Shu, and Wu books and notes its concise structure and missing tables/treatises.
- Cao Song attribution: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-971-luanshi-miyun-shui-sha-le-caocao-laodie-ljh3hdhssigmv78bueo-hprfqwku|Hanji 971]] presents the Sanguozhi line as assigning direct agency to Tao Qian in the killing of Cao Song and Cao De.
- Guan Yu source boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1041-jiemi-lishishang-zhenshi-de-guanyu-jiangcao-luisp0ndk1lfrc87byygn6r8myhx|Hanji 1041]] invokes Sanguozhi for Guan Yu's capture, 偏将军 appointment, generous treatment by Cao Cao, and stated refusal to betray Liu Bei.

## Qualifications
The wiki currently records Sanguozhi as mediated through podcast source notes. It should not treat the pages as a comprehensive source study of Chen Shou's text or Pei Songzhi's commentary. Hanji 971 preserves a genuine attribution split with [[HouHanshu|《后汉书》]], and Hanji 1041 uses Sanguozhi mainly to reject the historical status of "关公约三事."
