# 《三国志》 / Sanguozhi

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-971-luanshi-miyun-shui-sha-le-caocao-laodie-ljh3hdhssigmv78bueo-hprfqwku|Hanji 971]] introduces [[Sanguozhi|《三国志》]] as one of the source traditions for [[CaoSongMurder|曹嵩遇害]]. In the episode's presentation, this line says [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] resents [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s attack on [[Xuzhou|徐州]] and sends cavalry to kill [[CaoSong|曹嵩]] and [[CaoDeLateHan|曹德]].

The wiki treats this as a source-critical attribution, not as a final adjudication. Its political weight lies in direct agency: if Tao Qian sends the killers, Cao Cao's Xuzhou campaign reads more straightforwardly as retaliation for a political murder, even if the later [[XuzhouMassacreLateHan|徐州屠杀]] remains disproportionate civilian violence.
