## Overview

袁和平 / Yuen Woo-ping is the director of [[BiaoRenFilm2026|《镖人：风起大漠》]] in the bounded [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] source.

## Current Profile

The episode treats Yuen's command of action as the film's strongest and least disputed achievement. Its hosts value combat in which opponents exchange intelligible moves, weapons and bodies occupy readable space, and changing tactical situations create momentum. They especially praise the mounted archery and sandstorm work, fire-field combat, and extended final duel.

The profile is intentionally narrow: the source interprets an end-credit costume appearance as a gesture of intergenerational wuxia transmission, but it does not provide a verified career history or comprehensive account of Yuen's authorship within the production.

## Key Characteristics

- Directs combat around visible exchanges and tactical progression.
- Preserves physical martial readability within a commercial fantasy-scale film.
- Uses large action sequences as the adaptation's principal source of momentum.
- Is associated by the hosts with continuity in the traditional wuxia-film lineage.
