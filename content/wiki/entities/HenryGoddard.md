# Henry H. Goddard / 亨利·戈达德

Henry H. Goddard / 亨利·戈达德 appears in [[37-zhishang-ceshi-qingwen-ni-shi-zhili-poluomen-ma-608020679]] as the American psychologist who brought intelligence testing into institutions concerned with children labeled "feeble-minded." The episode connects him to school and training-institution laboratories, immigration screening, and the attempt to diagnose ability through standardized methods.

Goddard's most important source role is the [[KallikakFamily|Kallikak family]] case. The episode presents that study as a warning about how interviews, neighbor reputation, poverty, sexual judgment, criminality, and household appearance could be converted into hereditarian evidence and then used to legitimize [[EugenicGovernance|eugenic governance]].
