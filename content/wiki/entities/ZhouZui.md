# 周最 / Zhou Zui

周最 / Zhou Zui appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-56-qiaoqiao-liangqian-duo-nianqian-sha-shi-da-zhangfu-ltokbutqgsnrno-y29xmzwa3da41]] as one of the persuaders named after [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]], [[SuQin|苏秦]], and [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]]. The episode does not develop his independent biography; it uses him as evidence that court-to-court persuasion had become a wider fashion in the late [[WarringStatesPeriod|战国时期]] setting.

The page is therefore source-scoped. Zhou Zui's function here is to widen [[ZonghengjiaDiplomacy|纵横家外交]] beyond its most famous pair: Zhang Yi and Su Qin are exemplary, but the source says many others traveled the realm using speech, strategy, and deception to influence rulers.
