# Ursula K. Le Guin / 厄休拉·勒古恩

Ursula K. Le Guin / 厄休拉·勒古恩 is the science-fiction and fantasy writer discussed in [[36-heian-de-zuoshou-liufang-yu-ai-de-weilai-wangshi-605254522]] through [[TheLeftHandOfDarkness|《黑暗的左手》 / The Left Hand of Darkness]]. The episode presents her as a major corrective to the stereotype that science fiction is cold, hard, and male: her speculative worlds are politically sharp, emotionally delicate, anthropological in texture, and mythic without losing story force.

The source emphasizes Le Guin's anthropological inheritance and literary worldbuilding. Her [[HainishCycle|海恩宇宙 / Hainish Cycle]] fiction can resemble an archive or field report, while [[Earthsea|《地海传奇》 / Earthsea]] is used to show why her atmosphere, names, magic, and moral texture are hard to reduce to adaptation-ready plot points.

In this episode, Le Guin matters less as a predictor than as a designer of questions. [[AmbisexualWorldbuilding]], [[AnthropologicalScienceFictionWorldbuilding]], [[CorrectQuestionEpistemology]], and [[IntimacyBeyondGenderScript]] show how one invented planet can make readers ask what gender, nation, loyalty, betrayal, and love mean when familiar categories stop working.
