# Ryan Seiders

Ryan Seiders is the co-founder of [[YETI]] with [[RoySeiders]], as discussed in [[yeti-ron-and-ryan-seiders-how-two-brothers-turned-a-400-cooler-into-a-2-billion-brand-d7f8e0e8-51a5-4444-8c1d-f5c43c353285]]. The episode presents Ryan as the brother whose fishing, wildlife-management, rod-building, and road-sales background helped make YETI credible to the specialty outdoor retailers and serious users who first understood the product.

## Key Points
- Ryan studied wildlife management at Texas A&M and built fishing rods before starting [[WaterlooRods]].
- He sold Waterloo Rods after making roughly 1,000-1,500 rods per year, then used proceeds from that sale to help fund YETI's early container orders.
- In early YETI, Ryan joined Roy at a low wage and spent much of his time selling to independent outdoor, sporting-goods, and hardware stores.
- His selling role made [[SpecialtyRetailBeachhead]] central to the brand's early growth because stores had to understand why a cooler could cost hundreds of dollars.
- Ryan later helped broaden the product line through the [[YETIRambler]] drinkware opportunity.
- He stepped down as president in 2015 while remaining involved with the company.
