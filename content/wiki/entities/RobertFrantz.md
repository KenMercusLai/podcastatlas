# Robert Frantz

## Overview

Robert Frantz is the cardiologist and son of [[IvanFrantz]] who recovered preserved [[MinnesotaCoronaryExperiment]] records from the family basement.

## Current Profile

Robert made repeated trips to search his father's former house after [[ChristopherRamsden]] asked whether old trial material might survive. He found a deteriorating box of magnetic tapes and permitted the data to support a reanalysis that could challenge his father's dietary beliefs.

The episode makes his decision an example of principled inheritance. Robert argues that Ivan would have shared the tapes because a scientist should test hypotheses and explain results that move in an unexpected direction.

## Key Characteristics

- Cardiologist and son of Minnesota researcher [[IvanFrantz]].
- Custodian and retriever of the trial's surviving magnetic tapes.
- Cooperated with a reanalysis that challenged his father's favored hypothesis.
- Frames filial loyalty as preservation of scientific principle rather than inherited belief.
