# Electronic Frontier Foundation

The Electronic Frontier Foundation enters the wiki through [[tech-20260406-0406-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260406-0406-mp-tech-pod-128]], where [[AaronMackey]] presents its view of [[CaliforniaAB1709]] and similar state-level youth social-media restrictions. In this source, EFF's role is to defend online speech access for minors and adults while still treating platform harms as real.

The organization's policy position is narrower than a general defense of platforms. It opposes broad social-media bans, parental-permission requirements, and age-verification gates because they can burden [[YouthOnlineSpeechRights]] and adult speech access, while supporting [[ComprehensiveConsumerDataPrivacy]] rules that target surveillance-heavy data collection and opaque targeting.
