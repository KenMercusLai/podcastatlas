# Uche Agbosi

## Overview
Uche Agbosi is the guest in [[ep-20-understanding-ai-agents-from-basics-to-future-potential]], presented as an electronic-engineering graduate who has worked in software engineering since 1994 and later moved into generative-AI consulting and community leadership.

## Current Profile
The source positions Agbosi as a practitioner translating AI-agent ideas for a general technical audience. His account connects action-oriented and multi-agent systems to the less visible production work around memory, storage, [[LLMOps]], correctness, quality assurance, policy, and alignment.

## Key Characteristics
- Long software career spanning consulting, startup founding, CTO work, and organizations of different sizes.
- Early AI interest grounded in a university scheduling project for factory and job-shop resources.
- Practical definition of agents as systems that act through tools or coordinate specialized models.
- Strong emphasis on control, verification, and engineered memory rather than model capability alone.
- Community-oriented learning stance centered on experimentation, mentoring, collaboration, and local AI groups.

## Evidence
