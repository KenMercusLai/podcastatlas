# Avolon

## Overview

Avolon is an Ireland-based aircraft lessor owned by [[BohaiLeasing]] and used by the source as the entry point into the global leasing industry's expansion and concentration.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Avolon as a large lessor using long-horizon orders to secure scarce aircraft supply. Its agreements for 250 Airbus and Boeing aircraft, with options for another 100 Airbus aircraft, expand an already large fleet and order book while illustrating why manufacturer access, finance, and global airline relationships now matter together.

## Key Characteristics

- Large aircraft lessor with a reported existing fleet of 561 aircraft.
- Buyer using firm orders and options to reserve future production capacity.
- Company whose reported order book rises from 499 to 749 aircraft after the new agreements.
- Irish operating base connected to the country's leasing expertise, legal rules, tax system, and treaty network.
- Subsidiary through which [[BohaiLeasing]] shifted greater strategic weight toward aircraft leasing.
