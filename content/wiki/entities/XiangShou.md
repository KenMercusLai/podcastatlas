# 向寿 / Xiang Shou

向寿 / Xiang Shou appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-59-yichao-junwang-yichaochen-ljq-qkueix0rnzfekzuadicce1bh]] as a [[QinState|秦国]] official sent by [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] after Qin's capture of [[YiyangWarringStates|宜阳]]. His assignment is administrative: comfort the residents, settle boundaries, and regularize taxes and household registration in the newly controlled area.

The episode then makes Xiang Shou a court-politics actor. [[GanMao|甘茂]] advises returning [[WusuiWarringStates|武遂]] to [[HanState|韩国]] as a stabilizing gesture, and Qin Zhaoxiang Wang accepts the advice. Xiang Shou and the Gongsun faction resent Gan Mao after the proposal is adopted, helping turn a post-victory policy dispute into part of Gan Mao's later insecurity.
