# David Giffels

## Overview
David Giffels is the Akron writer whose interpretation of rubber-industry decline gives the Marketplace Tech episode its historical warning about AI disruption.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Giffels as the source's local-historical interpreter. His role is to separate technological and economic progress from human progress: the radial tire was better for drivers, but the transition damaged Akron's manufacturing base and the workers and public budgets attached to it. That frame lets the episode ask whether AI could repeat the pattern at larger scale.

## Key Characteristics
- Giffels supplies the progress-versus-human-progress interpretation for Akron's rubber decline.
- His source role connects industrial history to present-day AI policy rather than functioning as a general author biography.
- He anchors the episode's claim that a better product can still create concentrated community harm.
