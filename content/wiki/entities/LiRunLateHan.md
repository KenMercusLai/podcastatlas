## Overview
李润 / Li Run now appears first in the anti-Deng slander field before becoming a rewarded accession-service favorite, trusted Zhongchangshi, coerced Shun-restoration participant, and later cleanup target. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-794-yidai-nvjun-dengsui-qushi-hou-jing-quanjia-zaoyang-llmlhabdd09opca6-pr80q4mfoxi|Hanji 794]] names him with Wang Sheng and Jiang Jing against Empress Dowager Deng; Hanji 795 and Hanji 799 show reward and appointment-pressure layers; Hanji 803-805 show forced restoration alignment, limited credit, and renewed suspicion.

## Current Profile
Hanji 794 places Li Run before the post-Deng reward settlement. As Emperor An's insecurity rises, [[WangShengPalaceActorLateHan|Wang Sheng]], Li Run, and [[JiangJingLateHan|Jiang Jing]] slander [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]] around the possibility that An may be replaced. The source does not isolate Li Run's motive, but it makes him part of the palace-information field that shapes the later purge.

Hanji 795 then places Li Run in the immediate post-Deng reward field. Alongside Jiang Jing, he receives a marquis title, is promoted to Zhongchangshi, and remains near Emperor An. The source uses this pair to show how accession service, palace proximity, and post-Deng politics convert into title and office.

Hanji 799 places Li Run before the restoration crisis as a Zhongchangshi trusted by Emperor An. [[GengBaoGrandGeneralLateHan|Geng Bao]] asks [[YangZhenLateHan|Yang Zhen]] to recommend Li Run's elder brother, presenting the request as an attempt to anticipate the emperor's will. Yang refuses because a real imperial intention should come through an edict, making Li Run's favor a pressure field rather than a valid appointment warrant.

Hanji 803 then places Li Run at Zhangtai Gate when [[SunChengLateHan|Sun Cheng]] and [[WangKangLateHan|Wang Kang]] launch the restoration coup. The coup group kills Jiang Jin, Liu An, and Chen Da as Yan-side loyalists, puts a blade to Li Run's neck, and demands that he help install [[EmperorShunOfHan|Liu Bao / Emperor Shun]]. Li Run accepts and helps summon shangshu officials and bring the imperial carriage into the South Palace.

Hanji 804 and Hanji 805 show that coerced usefulness does not erase earlier ambiguity. Li Run is excluded from the first-rank restoration reward because he joins after the commitment point, and Yu Xu later impeaches him with other Yan-connected ministers and older eunuchs.

## Key Characteristics
- Palace actor named in anti-Deng slander before Empress Dowager Deng's death.
- Accession-service favorite whose early title and Zhongchangshi rise follow the Deng family's fall.
- Trusted Zhongchangshi whose closeness to Emperor An can be invoked in appointment pressure.
- Gate-side palace actor whose survival depends on accepting coerced alignment with the restoration coup.
- Operational contributor after coercion because he helps summon officials and move the imperial carriage.
- Excluded or limited in reward status because he did not commit at the initial coup threshold.
- Later cleanup target, suggesting prior Yan-side or non-restoration alignment remains politically visible.

## Qualifications
This page is bounded to Hanji 803, Hanji 804, Hanji 805, Hanji 799, Hanji 795, and Hanji 794. It does not prove Li Run's private loyalties, later punishment, full office history, exact role in anti-Deng slander, or whether he personally initiated the request for his elder brother. The current profile treats him as rewarded, trusted, coerced, and politically ambiguous because the sources show slander-field placement, accession-service favor, appointment pressure, forced participation, limited reward, and later suspicion. Hanji 794 and Hanji 795 use 李闰; the wiki keeps the existing 李润 route unless later evidence requires a split.
