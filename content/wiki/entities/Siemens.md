# Siemens / 西门子

Siemens is the German supplier in [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]] whose failed first-round bid illustrates Chinese procurement leverage. The episode says Siemens initially asked for high prices and high technology-transfer fees, won nothing in the 2004 round, and later returned under less favorable terms for the 300 km/h order.

The page's role is to show that [[ProcurementLedTechnologyTransfer]] depended on credible buyer discipline. A large order alone was not enough; the [[MinistryOfRailwaysChina]] had to be willing to exclude a major vendor when price and transfer conditions did not fit.
