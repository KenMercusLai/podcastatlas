# 胡玮炜 / Hu Weiwei

## Overview
胡玮炜 / Hu Weiwei is the founder associated with Mobike's product-led shared-bike route in this episode.

## Current Profile
The source presents Hu as the person who prepared [[Mobike]] before [[LiBinNio|李斌]] supplied early capital and backing and Wang Xiaofeng later joined operations. Her source role is tied to a more hardware- and product-intensive version of shared bikes: dockless use, smart locks, positioning, durability, weather resistance, anti-rust design, puncture avoidance, and chainless or low-maintenance mechanisms.

## Key Characteristics
- She is the founder figure behind Mobike's initial preparation.
- Her Mobike branch emphasizes hardware design and operating control rather than only cheap deployment.
- The source connects her company to smart locks, GPS/positioning, durable outdoor assets, and distinctive industrial design.
- Mobike's later sale to [[Meituan]] makes her founder story part of the shared-bike consolidation outcome.
