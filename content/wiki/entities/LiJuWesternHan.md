## Overview

李沮 / Li Ju is presented as a Western Han 强弩将军 commanding a dedicated crossbow formation under [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] during the [[FirstDingxiangCampaignWesternHan|first Dingxiang sortie]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-296-1-weiqing-guashuai-chuzheng-wei-hanchao-diaoda-xiongnu-lkr5lcogip_nx5uoycleuqzp9you|Hanji 296-1]] gives Li Ju one narrow operational role. While [[GongsunHeWesternHan|公孙贺]] and [[SuJianWesternHan|苏建]] reportedly envelop a Xiongnu rear guard, Li Ju's crossbow formation supplies dense missile fire from behind. The source uses his presence to show that the expedition's command design included specialist fire support as well as cavalry maneuver and route intelligence.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han officer rendered by the source as 强弩将军.
- Commander of a distinct strong-crossbow formation in Wei Qing's expedition.
- Specialist fire-support figure paired with flanking maneuver in the reported rear-guard engagement.
