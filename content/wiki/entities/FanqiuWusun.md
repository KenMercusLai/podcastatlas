## Overview

番丘 / Fanqiu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-550-sha-diguo-taizi-xiatui-jiqian-zhuibing-ta-zenme-zuodao-de-lvzhcjutqnu4or4elds_qg4s1gto|Hanji 550]] as [[MozhenjiangWusun|末振将]]'s son and the target of [[DuanHuizongWesternHan|段会宗]]'s punitive Wusun mission.

## Current Profile

Fanqiu matters less as an independent strategist than as the person through whom Han answers Mozhenjiang's offense after the offender himself has already died. The court regrets not killing Mozhenjiang directly, then sends Duan Huizong to punish Fanqiu. Duan leaves a larger force behind, brings a small selected party, summons Fanqiu, lists the crimes, and has hidden axemen kill him.

His death produces the episode's major confrontation. Many of Fanqiu's followers flee in fear, while [[AnlimiWusun|安犁靡]] soon surrounds Duan with thousands of riders. Fanqiu therefore functions as the punished heir whose killing tests whether Han's authority in Wusun can survive immediate local retaliation.

## Key Characteristics

- Son of Mozhenjiang and heir-line target after the killing of the large kunmi Cilim.
- Punished by Han because Mozhenjiang's offense still requires visible accountability.
- Killed by surprise after formal accusation rather than in open battle.
- His death triggers Anlimi's encirclement of Duan Huizong.
