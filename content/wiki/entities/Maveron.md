# Maveron

Maveron is the consumer-focused venture-capital firm referenced during the fundraising section of [[serena-lily-serena-dugan-and-lily-kanter-they-built-a-20m-brand-then-one-investor-almost-destroyed-it-31a4ddcd-1cc1-48d0-9369-fd85e8714e66]]. The local source text spells the name as "Mavron"; this page uses the standard spelling.

In the episode, [[LilyKanter]] says Maveron told the [[SerenaAndLily|Serena & Lily]] founders during the difficult 2008-2009 fundraising market that even Starbucks would not be fundable at that moment. The point is less about Maveron itself than about the funding environment for consumer brands after the financial crisis: strong growth signals did not guarantee available capital.

## Key Claims
- Maveron appears as a fundraising-market signal rather than as a Serena & Lily investor.
- The source uses the firm's reaction to show how hard consumer-brand fundraising became during 2008 and 2009.
- The case helps separate customer demand from fundability: a company can be growing quickly and still face a closed financing market.
