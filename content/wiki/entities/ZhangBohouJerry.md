# 张伯后 / Jerry

张伯后 / Jerry is the professional [[Padel|板式网球]] player interviewed by [[Heimai|黑麦]] for [[ep238-2025-nian-ni-wan-guo-zenyang-de-xiaozhong-yundong-lpaw-kela5vk6avl-s11h-yexwlg]]. The episode says he was born in 1997, moved from tennis into padel, gained professional points, and once reached an Asia No. 1 position in the still-developing competitive field.

The source uses Jerry to show why a new racket sport can create openings for athletes. Tennis has a crowded professional ladder and increasing physical filters, while padel's rules, tactics, doubles combinations, and global tour ecology are still less settled. His reliance on friends, students, and enthusiasts rather than large sponsors also makes him part of the episode's [[PreCommercialSportsCommunity|pre-commercial sports community]] story.
