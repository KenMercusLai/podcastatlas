# James Lawrence

James Lawrence appears in [[can-trump-make-buying-a-home-more-affordable]] as the first-time buyer whose experience makes [[HousingAffordabilitySupplyMechanics]] concrete. The source identifies him as a New Hampshire National Guard member who had been priced out of his former Portsmouth apartment, moved back in with his parents, rented a campsite, lived out of his car, and later deployed near Djibouti City partly to save down-payment money.

His story is not used as a universal housing-market explanation. It gives emotional and practical weight to the episode's policy test: if someone has to seek tax-free and hazard-pay income to buy a modest condo, then marginal mortgage-rate cuts and investor restrictions have to be judged against the deeper shortage of homes ordinary first-time buyers can afford.
