## Overview

卫右渠 / King Ugeo is the ruler of [[WimanJoseon|卫氏朝鲜]] during its final war with Western Han and is killed after senior officials conclude that continued resistance cannot survive the combined Han assault.

## Current Profile

Ugeo holds [[WangxianCity|王险城]] while the divided Han command delays decisive action. Once [[XunZhiWesternHan|荀彘]] receives the combined force and intensifies the attack, several senior officials defect but Ugeo still refuses surrender. The episode says the administrator Sam then has Ugeo killed and submits to Han. Resistance continues under Seong Gi until Ugeo's son and another defector's son help induce the city's final submission.

## Key Characteristics

- Final Wiman Joseon king in the episode's conquest sequence.
- Defender whose position initially benefits from division between Han commanders.
- Ruler who refuses surrender after senior officials judge the war unwinnable.
- King killed through internal elite defection rather than direct capture in battle.
- Political center whose death does not by itself end all resistance.
