# 白隐慧鹤 / Hakuin Ekaku

白隐慧鹤 / Hakuin Ekaku enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-155-1-lishi-jizai-shenme-qingkuang-hui-fasheng-ren-chi-ren-canju-lrwz-prnxd8hzchagyrbywkux7lh|Hanji 155-1]] as the "白隐禅师" in a side story used after the [[ChenPing|陈平]] accusation thread. The episode identifies him as a mid-Edo Rinzai Zen master and uses him as a moral analogy rather than as a subject of independent Buddhist history.

In the story as told by the host, an unmarried pregnant woman names Hakuin as the father under family pressure. Hakuin accepts public blame, raises the child amid criticism, and gives the child back calmly after the woman later reveals the real father. The repeated response "是这样吗" becomes the source's emblem of composure under false accusation.

For this wiki, Hakuin matters because the anecdote reframes the preceding Chen Ping material. [[MoralSlanderReframing|道德诬陷焦点转移]] describes Chen Ping and [[WeiWuzhi|魏无知]] returning Liu Bang to evidence and usefulness; the Hakuin story supplies a parallel ethic of not being destroyed by temporary public judgment.
