# Axio / Accio Work

## Overview
Axio, called Accio Work in E255, is the [[Alibaba]] international-business agent product described by [[ZhangKuo]]. It extends procurement search into product development, supplier coordination, logistics, multi-platform commerce, and ongoing merchant operations.

## Current Profile
The two sources present a widening product scope. E231 starts with agentic sourcing: research, design packs, supplier truth, price and landed-cost checks, negotiation, orders, logistics, after-sales, and daily small-business workflows. E255 describes the workbench managing market research, product design, factories, shipping, Amazon, eBay, Shopify, TikTok, pricing, customer feedback, reporting, and employee or customer-service signals.

The product thesis is not that one model autonomously runs a business. Axio/Accio combines models, tools, permissions, memory, business data, regional integrations, and model routing, with human confirmation for consequential actions and merchant judgment over goals and resource allocation.

## Key Characteristics
- Converts loose product ideas into structured research, specifications, design material, and supplier-ready requirements.
- Connects sourcing to transaction, logistics, storefront, customer-feedback, and operating workflows.
- Uses [[VerticalAgentArchitecture|model, harness, and context]] as complementary capability layers.
- Routes tasks across models based on difficulty, modality, quality, and cost.
- Preserves human approval and management judgment for consequential commercial decisions.
- Uses real ecommerce tasks and regression coverage to evaluate system capability.

- Sourcing and workbench scope - [[e231-cong-b2b-dao-a2a-agent-xin-jijian-ruhe-rang-yiren-qiye-zuo-quanqiu-shengyi-0f4a2ab9-d3a0-41ad-8db1-6c03c851bd70]] describes product ideation through after-sales and expands Axio Work into storefront, inventory, support, finance, payroll, and tax-partner workflows.
- Multi-platform operating expansion - [[e255-moxing-yuelaiyue-qiang-weishenme-yonghu-mei-ganjue-zaifang-aliguojizhanzongcai-zhangkuo-8fa0b58e-8359-4609-8e84-30c9a632df50]] adds cross-platform publishing, pricing, feedback, operating reports, employee and support analysis, and supplier-response continuity.
- Architecture and evaluation - [[e255-moxing-yuelaiyue-qiang-weishenme-yonghu-mei-ganjue-zaifang-aliguojizhanzongcai-zhangkuo-8fa0b58e-8359-4609-8e84-30c9a632df50]] ties the product to the three-layer architecture, model routing, and a reported 107-task ecommerce evaluation.

## Qualifications
The sources are product-leader interviews rather than independent product audits. Adoption, search-growth, workflow-speed, benchmark, and merchant-revenue claims remain source-scoped, and the supplied material does not quantify error rates, total operating cost, or long-run controlled business outcomes. The E231 spelling “Axio” and E255 spelling “Accio Work” are preserved as a naming discrepancy rather than silently normalized.

## What Changed
- Expanded the profile from sourcing and small-business setup into multi-platform ongoing operations.
- Added the model–harness–context architecture and regional integration requirement.
- Added a real-task evaluation frame and model-routing cost control.
- Clarified that persistent cloud execution is not sufficient without goal retention and verified results.
