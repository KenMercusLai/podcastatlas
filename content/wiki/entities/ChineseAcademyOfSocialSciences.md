# Chinese Academy of Social Sciences / 中国社会科学院

Chinese Academy of Social Sciences / 中国社会科学院 appears in [[baiwan-ge-jingguan-shehui-tingshuo-ni-ye-xiang-dang-zhubo-lgd0yapclwvsessg7xnhaarpyiwy]] as the institutional context for [[TianFeng|田峰]] and two 00后 student researchers studying [[TalentGroupLivestreaming|才艺团播]]. The episode uses their work to show how contemporary platform labor can be studied through backstage fieldwork, participant observation, and interviews rather than only through public livestream recordings.

The source also briefly exposes the students' academic situation: doctoral study, publication expectations, delayed graduation as both pressure and transition buffer, and the difficulty of turning fast-changing social scenes into durable scholarship.

## Key Claims
- The institution matters in the episode because it supplies a social-science fieldwork frame for a stigmatized entertainment-labor industry.
- Student researchers' bodily participation in practice rooms and livestream tests makes [[RealTimeLivestreamLabor|实时数据直播劳动]] more legible.
- The source presents contemporary Chinese social change as fast enough that recording industry details now is itself a scholarly task.
