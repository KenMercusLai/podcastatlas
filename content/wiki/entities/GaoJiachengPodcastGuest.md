# 高嘉诚 / Gao Jiacheng (Podcast Guest)

## Overview
高嘉诚 is a patient-experience guest in [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episodes on painless gastrointestinal endoscopy and everyday neck-and-back health.

## Current Profile
The episode uses his symptoms, delay in seeking examination, bowel preparation, loss and return of awareness, privacy anxiety, recovery, and hyperplastic-polyp result to translate a clinical workflow into a first-person account. His role is experiential rather than clinical: the doctors interpret eligibility, risk, pathology, and aftercare.

VOL.123 adds a musculoskeletal discussion in which he helps turn neck pressure, headache, posture, standing balance, massage, joint sounds, vehicle cushions, and lifting habits into practical questions for [[MaHaoning|马浩宁]]. As in the endoscopy episode, his examples prompt explanation but do not establish diagnosis or treatment authority.

VOL.182 adds a more reflective patient-experience role. Described there as 高阿诚, he connects breathlessness, digestive discomfort, palpitations, prior hand sensations, repeated consultations, and a turn toward mystical explanation with the pressure to give every uncomfortable sensation a definite disease name. His eventual emphasis is not to bypass care, but to combine proportionate assessment with daily-habit adjustment and tolerance of uncertainty.

## Key Characteristics
- Describes recurring digestive symptoms and the decision to proceed with gastroscopy and colonoscopy.
- Provides a first-person account of bowel preparation, intravenous anesthesia, waking, and early recovery.
- Raises common anxieties about pain, privacy, cognition, food, exercise, and procedure results.
- Serves as the patient case through which clinicians explain a hyperplastic-polyp finding and follow-up.
- Raises everyday questions about posture, unilateral weight bearing, massage, neck cushions, and safer lifting.
- Uses his own unresolved digestive, cardiopulmonary, and somatic experiences to discuss the boundary between appropriate investigation and certainty-seeking.
