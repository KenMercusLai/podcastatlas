## Overview

Wang Sheng / 王盛 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-634-huozai-nanren-yingzi-beihou-de-ta-can-can-can-lm3wub0l70yxwwyvwi15j93i9lwk|Hanji 634]] as the paired commoner appointee found to match Ai Zhang's fuming-backed Xin appointment list. This page distinguishes him from later palace actor [[WangShengPalaceActorLateHan|王圣 / 王圣君]] and unrelated modern names.

## Current Profile

The source presents Wang Sheng as a street vendor turned symbolic appointee. Ai Zhang's list had included a Wang Sheng among the Four Generals even though the name was originally invented. Wang Mang searches for a real same-named person and finds a pancake seller whose appearance can be made to fit the omen.

Wang Sheng's source role is to show the elasticity of early Xin sacred appointment logic. A low-status origin does not disqualify him; it strengthens the legend by letting Wang Mang portray an unlikely commoner appointment as Heaven's hidden selection.

## Key Characteristics

- Commoner appointee found to match a fabricated fuming-list name.
- Street-side pancake seller in the episode's account.
- Included among Xin's Four Generals through name, physiognomy, and legend logic.
- Evidence that early Xin appointment theater could make accident look destined.
