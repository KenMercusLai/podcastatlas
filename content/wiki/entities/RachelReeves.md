# Rachel Reeves

Rachel Reeves appears in [[a-firm-andy-what-are-new-british-pms-plans-6a5deeca76f8ee25bb4bbf9c]] as the chancellor associated with [[KeirStarmer]]'s economic promises. The episode says [[AndyBurnham]] will keep the Starmer-Reeves promise not to raise the main taxes, even as high taxes, high borrowing, pension costs, and sluggish growth limit the new government.

Her role in the source is mainly constraint-setting. Reeves is expected to leave the Treasury, but the tax promise she helped make continues to shape [[LabourPartyUK]]'s policy room and deepens the [[PoliticalDeliveryGap]] problem if public services do not visibly improve.

- [[KeirStarmer]] - leader whose economic promise Reeves helped anchor.
