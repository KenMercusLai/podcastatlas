# Min Aung Hlaing

Min Aung Hlaing is the leader of the [[MyanmarMilitaryJunta]] in [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]]. The episode presents him as [[AungSanSuuKyi]]'s former governing-period rival and the figure asked about her welfare by [[NarendraModi]] on June 1.

The source says people familiar with conversations involving foreign interlocutors described him as reacting angrily when Aung San Suu Kyi's name was raised. That response supports the episode's reading that the junta's international incentives may be outweighed by domestic fear of her [[NonviolentSymbolicPower]].
