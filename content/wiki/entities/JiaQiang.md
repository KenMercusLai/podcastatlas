# 贾蔷 / Jia Qiang

[[ritan-chuantai-hongloumeng-haishi-keyi-du-yidu-de-587167638]] uses 贾蔷 mainly through his relationship with [[Lingguan|龄官]]. When [[JiaBaoyu|贾宝玉]] sees that Lingguan's feeling is directed elsewhere and not toward him, the episode treats the scene as one of Baoyu's lessons in emotional limitation.

Jia Qiang therefore matters less as a fully developed page in this source than as part of [[LovePoetryModernity|love as recognition]] and [[TruthfulTemperamentInRedChamber|truthful temperament]]. Lingguan's refusal and attachment show Baoyu that affection cannot be claimed merely because he is sensitive, kind, or socially privileged.
