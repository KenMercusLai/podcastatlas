# Oliver Kim

Oliver Kim is the expert voice in [[getting-entrepreneurial-in-korea-summer-school]]. He explains [[SouthKorea|South Korea]] as a development case built from partial advantages, [[LandReformEqualityDevelopment]], [[StrategicIndustrialPolicy]], and [[ExportDiscipline]], then uses [[Bangladesh]] and [[NorthKorea|North Korea]] to test what parts of that model transfer.

His source role is comparative rather than biographical. He links the [[EastAsianTigers]] to rapid convergence, treats garment production as an early [[IndustrialLadder]] rung, introduces [[FlyingGeeseModel]], and argues that North Korea has entrepreneurial talent but suppresses growth because alternative centers of market power threaten the state.
