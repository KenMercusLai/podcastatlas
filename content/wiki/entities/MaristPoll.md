## Overview

The Marist Poll is a survey-research organization at Marist College whose election work is used by [[PlanetMoney]] to examine declining telephone response, statistical adjustment, and experimental forecasting questions.

## Current Profile

In the supplied episode, Marist combines interviewer training, long calling windows, random contact, demographic and geographic weighting, and methodological experimentation. Its conventional 2022 national House estimate was very close to the reported result, whereas adding [[CrowdWisdomPolling|crowd-wisdom questions]] would have made the forecast substantially less accurate.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses trained human interviewers and random telephone outreach to build election samples.
- Adjusts underrepresented responses by factors including race, education, and geography.
- Tests alternative contact and measurement methods rather than treating one survey design as permanent.
- Retains unsuccessful experimental questions when they may measure expectations or turnout mechanisms distinct from winner prediction.

## Qualifications

- The supplied document is an episode summary, not Marist's full methods report or questionnaire.
- One accurate national estimate does not establish accuracy across states, races, cycles, or survey modes.
- The episode does not provide the full weighting model, uncertainty intervals, text-sampling results, or validation design for turnout effects.

## What Changed

- Recorded a clear negative result for crowd-wisdom questions as a direct 2022 forecasting adjustment.
