## Overview

公孙康 / Gongsun Kang is [[GongsunDuLateHan|公孙度]]'s successor in Liaodong and a semi-independent regional ruler whose current profile moves from contested frontier title-making to self-preserving accommodation with [[CaoCao|曹操]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 1066 presents Liaodong as formally connected to Cao Cao's court-facing order but practically autonomous. Cao Cao has recommended Gongsun Du for office and sent him a marquis seal, yet Gongsun Du treats the recognition dismissively. After Gongsun Du dies, Gongsun Kang succeeds him and passes the Yongning village marquis title to his younger brother Gongsun Gong, preserving the source's picture of Liaodong as a family-held frontier base.

Gongsun Kang then sends [[HanZhongLiaodongLateHan|Han Zhong]] to Wuhuan with a chanyu seal. That act makes Liaodong an outside title-maker competing with Cao Cao's claim that court authority now runs through him after [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s defeat. [[QianZhaoLateHan|牵招]] defeats the move by declaring Liaodong too minor and remote to confer offices, publicly overpowering Han Zhong, and inducing Wuhuan leaders to send the envoy away.

Hanji 1079 adds the later reversal. After the [[BailangMountainLateHan|White Wolf Mountain]] defeat, [[YuanShang|Yuan Shang]], [[YuanXiLateHan|Yuan Xi]], and [[SupuyanLateHan|Supuyan]] seek refuge with Gongsun Kang. Cao Cao refuses immediate pursuit because pressure would give host and refugees a common enemy. Once Cao withdraws, Gongsun Kang's fear that the Yuan brothers could threaten Liaodong combines with his interest in avoiding a Cao-side invasion; he kills all three refugees and sends their heads to Cao Cao.

The two episodes together make Gongsun Kang neither a simple Cao subordinate nor a fixed anti-Cao ally. He uses autonomy when the balance permits, then changes alignment when refugee protection becomes more dangerous than compliance.

## Key Characteristics

- Successor to Gongsun Du in Liaodong after Gongsun Du's death.
- Represents a regional frontier power that accepts or uses court titles without giving up substantial autonomy.
- Uses envoy diplomacy and chanyu-seal recognition to influence Wuhuan alignment.
- Competes indirectly with Cao Cao's court-backed authority during the Yuan-family collapse.
- His move fails when Qian Zhao turns the Wuhuan meeting against Liaodong's envoy.
- Recalculates after White Wolf Mountain, treating the Yuan refugees as a threat to Liaodong autonomy.
- Preserves his regional position by killing the refugees and signaling accommodation to Cao Cao without awaiting invasion.

## Qualifications

This page remains bounded to two podcast notes rather than a full reconstruction of Gongsun Kang's career. Hanji 1079 supplies the later outcome anticipated but not developed by Hanji 1066. Its banquet trap, dialogue, gestures, winter imagery, and exact transport treatment of the heads are literaryized narration and remain source-scoped. Gongsun Gong and the exact legal meaning of the Yongning title also remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Extended Gongsun Kang from failed frontier title-maker to a regional ruler who kills the Yuan refugees when Cao Cao's withdrawal exposes their incompatible interests.
- Clarified that accommodation with Cao Cao preserves, rather than erases, Gongsun Kang's semi-independent position.
