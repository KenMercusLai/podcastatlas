# 《二十四孝》 / Twenty-Four Filial Exemplars

《二十四孝》 / Twenty-Four Filial Exemplars is the later filial-piety tradition invoked by [[06-soushenji-ershisi-xiao-zhe-kou-guo-ganbao-bu-bei-536821044]]. The episode acknowledges that stories such as Guo Ju burying his son and lying on ice for carp are often traced back to or associated with [[SouShenJi|《搜神记》]], but it argues that later moralizing reception is not the same as the source collection's full stance.

The key wiki concept is [[FilialPietyStoryAfterlife]]. In the source's reading, [[GanBao|干宝]] records filial wonders as strange events in a book that also preserves revenge, women leaving abuse, sympathetic monsters, anti-authority satire, and unexplained marvels. Treating these tales only as commands to imitate is therefore a later narrowing of a more plural archive.
