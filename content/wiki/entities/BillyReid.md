# Billy Reid

Billy Reid appears in [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] as the [[ProvisionalIRA]] member who shot several soldiers during February 1971 clashes in the New Lodge area of [[Belfast]] and killed Gunner [[RobertCurtis]]. The episode uses the killing as the first British soldier death in Northern Ireland during the Troubles.

In the source, Reid's action marks a change in the [[BritishArmy]]'s role: soldiers were no longer only a temporary buffer between communities but increasingly direct targets in an insurgent conflict.
