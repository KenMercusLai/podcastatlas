## Overview

Tang Zun / 唐尊 is a late-[[XinDynasty|新朝]] official in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-657-wangmang-fei-taizi-you-duo-erxi-lklx1rd39kgz-by4tbcpaagx4z-l|Hanji 657]] who becomes taifu after Ping Yan's death. The episode uses him as an austerity and ritual-discipline exemplar praised by [[WangMang|王莽]].

## Current Profile

Tang Zun diagnoses state poverty and commoner hardship as linked to elite extravagance and poor management. His response is performative austerity: small sleeves, short clothes, a plain carriage, straw mats, pottery food vessels, and symbolic gifts of humble goods to high officials. He also polices gender separation in public by marking people with red-earth water, turning moral order into everyday visible discipline.

## Key Characteristics

- Taifu appointed during late-Xin crisis.
- Austerity exemplar whose plain consumption is made into a court model.
- Moral-discipline actor who turns ritual separation norms into public shaming.
- Official whose behavior Wang Mang praises as a standard for ministers.
