# 《三国演义》 / Romance of the Three Kingdoms

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-923-plus-luanshi-fengyun-qi-ruiqi-jiemi-shishang-zui-zhenshi-de-sanguo-lj0ic-bhawqut5zbutbzd0orxjoe|Hanji 923 plus]] adds the broad prologue for reading the coming [[ThreeKingdomsPeriod|三国时代]] arc through historical sources rather than only through Luo Guanzhong's novelized reception. The episode does not analyze the novel in detail; it uses [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]] as the contrast that makes [[FictionAsHistoricalEvidence]] and [[ThreeKingdomsStruggleStrategyReading|三国斗争策略读法]] necessary.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-955-guanyu-zhan-huaxiong-qishi-shi-wei-lishi-lndkrj-fnepye8iu-ifgyh-vzysf|Hanji 955]] adds the Hua Xiong correction as a second literary-memory contrast. The episode treats [[GuanYu|关羽]]'s famous warm-wine killing of [[HuaXiongLateHan|华雄]] as memorable novelized history, while crediting the historical event at [[YangrenBattleLateHan|阳人之战]] to [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]].

《三国演义》 / Romance of the Three Kingdoms enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-961-neihuang-zhizhan-caocao-chulu-fengmang-ljdlt6kiu8tg0qws-jlyej-yhgxr|Hanji 961]] as the literary contrast for reassessing [[CaoCao|曹操]]. The episode says the novel's Liu Bei-centered narrative makes Cao Cao look more like a villain, while the host wants to return to chronicle sources such as [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] to evaluate Cao Cao's military and political capacity.

The page is source-scoped. Hanji 961 does not analyze the novel as a literary work; it uses the text as a reception filter that can obscure the [[IntegratedCommandCapacity|全维度统帅能力]] the host sees in [[NeihuangBattleLateHan|内黄之战]] and later Cao-side statecraft.
