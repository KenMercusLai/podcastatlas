# Center for Countering Digital Hate

The Center for Countering Digital Hate appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-under-secretary-of-state-sarah-b-rogers-on-dismantling-the-censorship-industrial-complex-39828360]] as the NGO example [[SarahBRogers|Sarah B. Rogers]] uses when explaining the [[CensorshipIndustrialComplex]]. In the episode's account, Rogers says leaked emails showed priorities including damaging [[ElonMusk]]'s [[Twitter|X]] and encouraging UK and EU regulatory pressure.

This page records the source-scoped claim rather than independently verifying the organization's conduct. Its wiki role is to make concrete the episode's broader mechanism: an NGO can publicly criticize platforms, but can also become more powerful when it gains privileged access to regulators, payment processors, advertisers, or platform moderation channels.
