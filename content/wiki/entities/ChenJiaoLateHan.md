# 陈矫 / Chen Jiao (late Han)

## Overview
陈矫 / Chen Jiao is the Cao-side senior clerk who witnesses Cao Ren's Jiangling rescue and voices the garrison's astonishment at his return.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1112 places Chen Jiao on the walls of [[JianglingLateHan|江陵]] as [[NiuJinLateHan|牛金]] and other soldiers are surrounded. He watches [[CaoRen|曹仁]] leave the defended position with a small mounted group, break into the opposing force, extract the trapped soldiers, and return. His reported “真天人也” reaction records how the action was received inside the garrison and helps connect physical rescue to morale.

## Key Characteristics
- Civil official observing a battlefield crisis from the defended position.
- Provides the episode's clearest witness reaction to Cao Ren's conduct.
- Makes defender morale visible through reported speech.

## Evidence
- Witness and reaction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1112-bei-digu-de-caowei-mingjiang-zhanchang-shang-you-duo-meng-lkqecaphucolkdcsutsvjyxounln|Hanji 1112]] attributes the “真天人也” judgment to Chen Jiao after the extraction.
