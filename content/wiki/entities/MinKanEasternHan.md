## Overview

闵堪 / Min Kan is synthesized as a narrow Eastern Han frontier actor whose current evidence now starts as an opponent protected by Xiongnu-backed rescue before he later enters Eastern Han service with [[LuFangEasternHan|卢芳]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-708-jizun-bingshi-weihe-yinfa-baiguan-jingkong-lhfuvjyiiirlaf-ugrufkaspuauq|Hanji 708]] places him with Jia Lan against [[WuHanEasternHan|Wu Han]] and [[DuMaoEasternHan|Du Mao]], while Hanji 718 shows [[LiuXiu|刘秀]] using him in the Dai settlement meant to turn Lu Fang's return into frontier stabilization.

## Current Profile

Hanji 708 supplies the earlier pressure layer. Wu Han and Du Mao attack Jia Lan and Min Kan, but Xiongnu forces rescue them, and the source records no Han victory. Min Kan therefore appears before the later surrender settlement as part of the unresolved Lu Fang/Xiongnu-facing northern field.

Hanji 718 says Min Kan surrenders with Lu Fang. Liu Xiu makes Lu Fang Dai King and appoints Min Kan as Dai Chancellor, pairing royal title with an administrative role. The source does not develop Min Kan's independent motives or later conduct.

Min Kan therefore functions as part of the reintegration architecture. The court wants Lu Fang and Min Kan to help pacify relations with [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], but the broader settlement is undercut when the Xiongnu side expects a bounty for returning Lu Fang and Han treats the matter as voluntary surrender.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han surrender actor paired with Lu Fang.
- Earlier northern opponent whose force survives a Han attack through Xiongnu-backed rescue.
- Administrative appointee made Dai Chancellor in the surrender settlement.
- Frontier-stabilization figure whose role is bounded to Xiongnu-facing reintegration.
- Source-bounded person with no current independent biography beyond Hanji 718.
