## Overview

阳阿公主 / Princess Yang'a enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-529-meiyan-jiemeihua-xihan-zuida-de-huoshui-dengchang-litxz9-kwkcm04s38y8_glpaqiop|Hanji 529]] as the elite household mediator in [[ZhaoFeiyanWesternHan|赵飞燕]]'s rise. Her residence is where Zhao Feiyan and [[ZhaoHedeWesternHan|赵合德]] are said to take refuge or serve after their difficult origin story, and it is where Zhao Feiyan's dance skill becomes visible to [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 529 presents Yang'a Princess less as a biography than as a court-culture node. The episode says the Zhao sisters came under her household, where Zhao Feiyan learned dance and became known for a light, striking performance style. When Chengdi arrives privately with [[ZhangFangWesternHan|张放]], Yang'a Princess hosts the banquet and arranges singers for entertainment.

Her decisive role is interpretive and transactional. The source says she notices Chengdi's fascination with one performer, tests or draws out his response by criticizing the dancers as ordinary, and then gives Zhao Feiyan to him after the banquet. That makes her household the bridge between trained female performance and imperial intimate access.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han princess whose household provides the setting for Zhao Feiyan's dance training and first imperial exposure.
- Elite intermediary who notices Chengdi's interest and converts it into Zhao Feiyan's palace entry.
- Source-scoped figure: the wiki currently has no broader biography beyond the Hanji 529 episode.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 529. It does not reconstruct Yang'a Princess's lineage, political role, household administration, or full historical record. The episode uses her primarily as the intermediary in Zhao Feiyan's palace-entry story.
