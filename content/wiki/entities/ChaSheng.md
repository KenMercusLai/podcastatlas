# 查晟 / Cha Sheng

Cha Sheng is the [[AmazonAGI]] research-team lead interviewed in [[174-women-hai-neng-gei-suanfa-dang-duojiu-de-pinwei-laoshi-duitan-yamaxun-agi-cha-sheng-lrs0qgmr9gy1nbdtrsvn2lx5dxza]] about foundation-model training, open and closed model competition, synthetic data, self-improvement, AI values, enterprise models, and AGI limits. In the source, he is presented as someone who has participated in multiple [[Amazon]] large-model training cycles and can speak from inside the basic model-training workflow.

His recurring frame is that current AI remains dependent on people for problem definition, taste, evaluation, and organizational direction, while also making those same human contributions easier to distill into model behavior. That makes him an important source for [[HumanTasteAsAITrainingSignal]], [[ResearchTaste]], [[AgentHarness]], and [[HumanAgencyUnderAI]].

## Key Points
- He treats [[OpenSourceAIModels]] as strategically important but structurally weaker than closed consumer products at capturing direct user data.
- He frames synthetic data through information quality, filtering, and evaluation rather than through a simple synthetic-versus-human binary.
- He argues that model values emerge from data, rewards, and training choices, connecting his comments to [[ModelValueEmbedding]] and [[AIAlignmentGovernance]].
- He sees future AGI as requiring fast learning, [[ContinualLearning]], and reduced forgetting, not only larger static language models.
