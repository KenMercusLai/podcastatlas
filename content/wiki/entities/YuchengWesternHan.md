## Overview

郁成 / Yucheng is a Western Regions polity appearing in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-375-1-zhichang-qishi-bie-rang-ziji-biancheng-yi-tou-chunlu-li-za4h0ixnq4jym8algtxzyy6os|Hanji 375-1]] during [[LiGuangliWesternHan|李广利]]'s Dayuan campaign. Its forces defeat a detached Han unit, after which [[ShangguanJieWesternHan|上官桀]] attacks and its king flees to [[Kangju|康居]].

## Current Profile

The source supplies only a narrow campaign profile. Yucheng can destroy or disperse a roughly thousand-person Han detachment, but it cannot withstand the subsequent force sent under Shangguan Jie. Its king seeks safety in Kangju, whose ruler turns him over after learning that Han has defeated [[DayuanWesternHan|大宛]] and fearing that protection will widen the conflict.

Yucheng therefore appears as a small polity exposed to cascading coercion after a larger regional victory. Its local battlefield success does not survive the shift in regional expectations created by Dayuan's defeat, and its king's fate depends on a neighboring power's risk calculation rather than only Yucheng's own military capacity.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Regions polity encountered during the Han expedition against Dayuan.
- Able in the episode to defeat a separated Han detachment under Wang Shensheng.
- Subsequently attacked by Shangguan Jie's force.
- Loses its king when he flees to Kangju and is surrendered under Han pressure.
- Illustrates how a major expedition changes the choices available to smaller neighboring powers.
