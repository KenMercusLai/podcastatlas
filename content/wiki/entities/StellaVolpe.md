# Stella Volpe

## Overview
Stella Volpe is presented in [[the-magic-wand-experiment-the-magic-wand-experiment|The Magic Wand Experiment]] as a nutritionist and exercise physiologist proposing population-scale environmental intervention for public health.

## Current Profile
Volpe imagines dividing the United States into control and treatment halves and redesigning the treatment environment to encourage movement through stairs, standing desks, exercise balls, and longer walking distances. The episode connects this proposal to her earlier portion-size intervention with university students.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats everyday physical settings as active determinants of health behavior.
- Uses control-versus-treatment comparison to test environmental intervention.
- Favors routine movement and portion design over dependence on motivation alone.

## Evidence
### Environmental intervention
- [[the-magic-wand-experiment-the-magic-wand-experiment|The Magic Wand Experiment]] reports the national proposal and a prior university portion-size comparison.

## Qualifications
The supplied note does not provide methods or full results for the university study, and the national experiment is hypothetical. Redesign can impose accessibility, safety, autonomy, labor, equity, and political costs.
