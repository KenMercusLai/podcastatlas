# 文姜 / Wen Jiang

文姜 / Wen Jiang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-75-2-qiongshe-jichi-de-zushiye-guanzhong-lo8uei2u1qwj9tszftel1b4lswoa]] as the half-sister of [[QiXiangGong|齐襄公]] and wife of [[LuHuanGong|鲁桓公]]. The episode uses her relationship with Qi Xiang Gong as part of a larger diagnosis of role disorder across family, marriage, rulership, and interstate conduct.

Her wiki role is not a standalone biography. She is the personal link through which a Qi court scandal becomes a Qi-Lu diplomatic crisis and then one step in the disorder that precedes [[QiHuanGong|齐桓公]] and [[GuanZhong|管仲]]'s rise.
