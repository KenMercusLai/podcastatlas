# 孔褒 / Kong Bao (late Han)

## Overview

Kong Bao is the late-Han Kong-family figure whose friendship with [[ZhangJianLateHan|张俭]] turns his household into a refuge during the 169 CE party-prohibition danger.

## Current Profile

The page's current evidence is narrow but central. Kong Bao is absent when Zhang Jian arrives, yet the fugitive comes because of their friendship; once the case is exposed, Kong Bao claims the guilt should fall on him rather than on his younger brother [[KongRong|孔融]]. The court ultimately accepts that line and executes Kong Bao, making him the member whose death closes the family's competing responsibility claims.

## Key Characteristics

- Friend and intended host of Zhang Jian during party-prohibition flight.
- Absent household head whose relationship still defines why Zhang Jian comes to the Kong home.
- Protective elder brother who argues that Kong Rong should not bear the charge for sheltering his friend.
- Executed figure whose death becomes the political cost behind Kong Rong's later reputation.

- Responsibility claim: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-895-xuexing-tudao-xia-de-gurou-qingshen-llsplyyrjxhqkpi2aiuqzulgm6w3|Hanji 895]] has Kong Bao argue that Zhang came for him, so the guilt belongs to him rather than Kong Rong.
