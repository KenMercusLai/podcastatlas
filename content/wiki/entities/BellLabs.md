# Bell Labs

Bell Labs appears in [[zhenzheng-gaibian-shijie-de-jishu-weishenme-yikaishi-dou-bu-bei-kanhao-s10e16-8c95b3dc-d75a-4bdd-84d4-2c06fd2d85b1]] as the research environment where [[MuhammadAtalla]] and [[DawonKahng]] developed the [[MOSFET|MOS transistor]] in the 1960s. The source says Bell Labs did not initially prioritize MOS because [[BipolarJunctionTransistor|BJT]] technology better matched [[ATT|AT&T]]'s near-term switching needs.

The episode uses Bell Labs as a case where a negative judgment can be rational within the incumbent system and still miss a future trajectory. MOS was slower and less stable by the main telecom metrics, but its low cost and ease of integration later mattered more as integrated circuits scaled.
