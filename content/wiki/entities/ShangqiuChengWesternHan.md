## Overview

商丘成 / Shangqiu Cheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-388-1-shaqi-shazi-zhihou-hanwudi-weihe-zumie-dajiuzi-liguangli-lkkwpivpwqpeg5kd7azu-wmdqmgb|Hanji 388-1]] as one of the commanders in [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s 90 BCE multi-route expedition against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]].

## Current Profile

The source gives Shangqiu Cheng a limited operational role. Wudi sends him with twenty thousand troops from Xihe while [[LiGuangliWesternHan|李广利]] leaves from Five Plains and [[MaTongWesternHan|马通]] leaves from Jiuquan. Shangqiu Cheng tries to take a shortcut in pursuit of Xiongnu but cannot find the enemy and withdraws.

His page exists mainly to anchor the expedition structure. In Hanji 388-1, the narrative contrast matters: Shangqiu Cheng's route fizzles into no-contact withdrawal, while Li Guangli's initially successful route becomes entangled with succession crisis, family arrests, and surrender.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han commander in the 90 BCE anti-Xiongnu expedition.
- Leads a Xihe route separate from Li Guangli and Ma Tong.
- Attempts a shortcut pursuit but fails to locate Xiongnu forces.
- Withdraws without becoming the main disaster branch.
- Functions as structural context for Li Guangli's more politically charged failure.
