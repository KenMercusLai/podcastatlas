# Jared Bush

## Overview
Jared Bush is a Disney animation leader and screenwriter whom [[zootopia-exposed-part-two-zootopia-exposed-part-two]] associates with Zootopia, Moana, Encanto, and especially the exclusion-and-restoration plot of [[Zootopia2Film|Zootopia 2]].

## Current Profile
The episode reads Bush's work through his family history. His father [[LesterBushJr]] challenged the historical basis of Mormon racial exclusion, while other relatives supported Black financial access and gay rights. Gladwell therefore interprets the sequel's restoration of reptiles and possible acknowledgment of [[GaryGoldman]] as a continuation of that inheritance. This is an interpretive profile, not a verified account of Bush's private motives.

## Key Characteristics
- Screenwriter of Zootopia 2 and Disney animation executive in the episode's account.
- Creator associated with recurring stories about family, outsiders, and restoration to community.
- Son of Lester Bush Jr. and inheritor of a family history of resistance to exclusion.
- Proposed author of a covert or institutionally approved critique of Disney's treatment of Goldman.
- Did not respond to the handwritten letter Goldman reportedly sent him.

## Evidence
### Creative work and social theme
- [[zootopia-exposed-part-two-zootopia-exposed-part-two]] identifies Bush with Zootopia, Moana, Encanto, and a sequel centered on reptiles' return to the animal community.
- [[zootopia-exposed-part-two-zootopia-exposed-part-two]] cites a pre-release account of Bush becoming emotional while discussing the sequel's social-justice subtext.

### Family-history interpretation
- [[zootopia-exposed-part-two-zootopia-exposed-part-two]] connects the film's inclusion plot to Lester Bush Jr.'s church-history intervention and to other relatives' work against racial and sexual exclusion.
- [[zootopia-exposed-part-two-zootopia-exposed-part-two]] attributes the Goldman acknowledgment theory to close reading and family parallel, not to a statement by Bush.

## Qualifications
The source provides no direct confirmation that Bush modeled Gary De'Snake on Gary Goldman, intended an anti-Disney message, or consciously translated his family history into the screenplay. Emotional investment in social justice supports thematic relevance but does not establish a specific hidden referent. His exact corporate title and credits also remain episode-reported here.
