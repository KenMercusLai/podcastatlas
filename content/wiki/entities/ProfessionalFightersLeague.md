# Professional Fighters League

## Overview
The Professional Fighters League, or PFL, is an MMA organization linked in the source to [[JakePaul|Jake Paul]] and [[MostValuablePromotions|MVP]].

## Current Profile
The episode uses PFL mainly as the organizational vehicle for Paul's proposed challenge to [[UFC]] fighter economics. Paul says MVP has merged with PFL and argues that better pay, sponsorship freedom, and major-fight access can attract dissatisfied fighters.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates in professional mixed martial arts.
- Is presented as a challenger to UFC's incumbent position.
- Is linked to MVP through a guest-reported merger.
- Appears in a broader strategy centered on participant economics and audience distribution.
