# 张恺 / Zhang Kai (Duan Jiong officer)

## Overview

Zhang Kai is the late-Han officer in Hanji 891 who leads an eastern mountain force during Duan Jiong's encirclement of the Qiang at Shehu Valley.

## Current Profile

The source gives Zhang Kai a tactical role rather than a biography. After Tian Yan and Xia Yu move onto the western mountain with silent night discipline, Duan Jiong sends Zhang Kai and others with three thousand soldiers up the eastern mountain, creating the east-west pressure that precedes the attack.

## Key Characteristics

- Sima-level officer under Duan Jiong in the 169 CE Qiang campaign.
- Leads roughly three thousand soldiers onto the eastern mountain.
- Helps create a two-sided attack against Qiang forces holding the valley.
- Disambiguated from [[ZhangKaiAssassin|张闿]], the later assassin/escort figure in the Cao Song and Chen-king branches.
