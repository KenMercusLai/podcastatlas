# Atlanta / 亚特兰大

## Overview
Atlanta / 亚特兰大 is the U.S. passenger-hub city used in the aviation roundtable to show how air networks can elevate a city's economic position.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Atlanta as a city whose aviation status exceeds a simple reading of regional size. Ma Xiao says it has long ranked near the top of global passenger-airport traffic and benefits from central U.S. geography, east-west domestic transfer, and links toward Latin America.

In the wiki, Atlanta currently functions as an aviation-hub case rather than a broad city profile.
