## Overview

卞夫人 / Lady Bian is [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s wife and the mother of four sons in the episode tradition. The bounded sources move her from an early household marker to the chief wife after [[LadyDingCaoCao|丁夫人]]'s departure, then emphasize her reported deference toward Lady Ding rather than treating status succession as simple displacement.

## Current Profile

Hanji 918 supplies the early marriage marker, and Hanji 1014 places Lady Bian among Cao Cao's wives before identifying her as the woman who later becomes formal wife. Hanji 1158 names [[CaoPi|曹丕]], Cao Zhang, [[CaoZhi|曹植]], and Cao Xiong as her sons and reports that, after Lady Ding's rupture with Cao Cao, Lady Bian invited her to meals, yielded the senior seat, and later arranged her funeral.

The later episode presents those acts as humility and relational judgment, but the exact date, invitations, seating order, dialogue, and funeral initiative remain source-scoped pending comparison with the historical texts.

## Key Characteristics

- Early wife of Cao Cao in the episode chronology.
- Mother of four sons, including Cao Pi and Cao Zhi.
- Becomes chief wife after Lady Ding's departure.
- Reportedly preserves Lady Ding's senior dignity despite the status change.
- Is portrayed as managing a painful household relationship through restraint rather than rivalry.

## Qualifications

The bounded evidence is episodic rather than a complete biography. It does not establish Lady Bian's full political role, her sons' complete succession history, or the exact household law behind each title. Hanji 1158's private exchanges and ceremonial details are attributed to the episode rather than treated as independently verified fact.
