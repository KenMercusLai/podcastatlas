# 卞夫人 / Lady Bian (Cao Cao)

卞夫人 / Lady Bian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1014-caocao-de-15-ge-laopo-dou-you-shui-lv-hyvprg73k8w8jspxjosqwmc69|Hanji 1014]] as one of [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s named wives. The episode identifies her as the woman who later becomes formal wife after [[LadyDingCaoCao|丁夫人]]'s break with Cao Cao.

The source says Lady Bian bears four legitimate sons. It does not use the episode to build a full succession account, so this page keeps her role source-scoped: she matters here as the household figure who follows Lady Ding in status and links the opening wife-list to Cao Cao's family order.
