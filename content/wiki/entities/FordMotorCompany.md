# Ford Motor Company

Ford Motor Company appears in [[ferrari-1]] as the mass automaker that tried and failed to acquire [[Ferrari]] in 1963. The source presents Ford's interest as a search for racing credibility after deciding it needed to compete at the top of motorsport.

After [[EnzoFerrari]] rejected the deal, Ford pursued Le Mans directly and beat Ferrari in 1966. In the wiki, Ford functions as the contrast case: a giant industrial company could buy engineering capacity, but it could not buy Ferrari's founder-controlled racing identity.
