# Yaa Asantewaa

## Overview

Yaa Asantewaa is the Asante queen mother the episode presents as a famous martial example of female political and military authority.

## Current Profile

The source places Yaa Asantewaa inside the broader [[AsanteQueenMothers]] institution. She matters because queen-mother power is not only a courtly or kinship role in the episode's account: she becomes the famous martial queen mother through resistance to British pressure during the [[WarOfTheGoldenStool]]. Her example connects matrilineal political authority to anti-colonial resistance without making all female power a battlefield story.

## Key Characteristics

- Asante queen mother used as a martial female-authority case.
- Associated in the source with resistance to British pressure.
- Linked to the [[WarOfTheGoldenStool]] as the episode's best-known queen-mother conflict.
- Evidence that institutional female authority could become military leadership under crisis.
