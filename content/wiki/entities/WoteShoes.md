# 沃特鞋企 / Wote Shoes

沃特鞋企 / Wote Shoes is the [[Putian|莆田]] shoe company used in [[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw]] to show a harder [[FactorySecondGeneration|厂二代]] succession path. [[WeiQianSanlian|魏茜]] presents the founder as someone who moved through scrap selling, railway work, restaurants, and shoelace production before building a footwear company.

The case is less about inheriting a stable asset than about returning during a difficult industrial cycle. The source says Wote had tried to take an early brand path in [[Putian]] shoes, looked toward the [[AntaSports|安踏]] and Jinjiang-style advertising-and-listing model, but failed to complete that growth route. By the time the son returned, the company faced severe pressure and had to test e-commerce, live-streaming, IP, design, foreign trade, and [[Amazon]] sales.

## Source Position
- Wote is contrasted with [[Fadior|法迪奥]]: one successor returns during a low point, while the other enters during category improvement.
- The episode uses footwear to show high SKU and inventory complexity: size, color, design, material, channel, and sales all multiply operating risk.
- Wote's founder treats the brand as emotionally close to a child, while the second generation treats it more as a responsibility and possible self-expression space.
- The case shows why [[SuccessionAsContinuousEntrepreneurship]] is not a slogan: the inherited company may need to be rebuilt around new channels and consumer language.
