# 刘循 / Liu Xun (Yizhou heir)

## Overview
刘循 / Liu Xun is [[LiuZhang|刘璋]]'s eldest son in the Hanji 1055 Yi Province branch. He is primarily important as a succession and marriage-link marker because [[PangXiLateHan|庞羲]]'s daughter is married to him, making Pang Xi look both useful and threatening inside Liu Zhang's household politics.

## Current Profile
Liu Xun does not act independently in the episode. His role is relational: as Liu Zhang's eldest son and Pang Xi's son-in-law, he helps explain why Pang Xi's old service to the Liu family can become politically uncomfortable. The page is explicitly disambiguated from [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]], the Lujiang figure already present in the wiki.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as Liu Zhang's eldest-son succession marker.
- Links Pang Xi's household to Liu Zhang's ruling family through marriage.
- Helps explain why Liu Zhang could fear Pang Xi's influence despite Pang's prior favor.
- Requires disambiguation from the separate late-Han figure 刘勋 / Liu Xun.
