## Overview

孙斌 / Sun Bin is a former subordinate of [[FifthZhongLateHan|第五种]] in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-851-hanhuandi-weihe-zhuding-yichou-wannian-ltgm1ix6cgqb-kqo9bztfrzjgeun|Hanji 851]]. He is disambiguated from [[SunBin|孙膑 / Sun Bin]] the Warring States strategist. His source-bounded role is the private rescuer who prevents Fifth Zhong's exile from becoming a likely death trap.

## Current Profile

After [[ShanChaoLateHan|单超]] frames Fifth Zhong and Fifth Zhong is sent toward Shuofang, [[DongYuanLateHan|董元]] makes the destination dangerous because he is Shan Chao's maternal grandson and holds office there. Sun Bin gathers retainers, catches Fifth Zhong's party near Taiyuan, and sends Fifth Zhong home.

The episode turns Sun Bin's act into a loyalty and survival case. The court channel has punished [[YangBingLateHan|杨秉]] and Fifth Zhong; Sun Bin acts outside it to preserve his former superior's life. Fifth Zhong then hides for years until an amnesty.
