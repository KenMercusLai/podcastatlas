# CFB Group / CFB 集团

CFB Group / CFB 集团 appears in [[145-gaijia-zhongzi-de-canyin-yangpinpai-feng-tou-quan-1-145-1]] as a private-equity-backed restaurant platform tied in the source to [[FangyuanCapital]]. The episode says the group controls or operates brands including [[DairyQueen]], [[MeetFresh]], and Papa John's-related China assets, concentrating on dessert, ice cream, pizza, and other repeatable consumer foodservice categories.

The source uses CFB Group to show that [[ForeignRestaurantBrandLocalControl]] can become a portfolio strategy. Instead of betting on one foreign-origin brand, an operator can assemble multiple assets whose operations may share leasing knowledge, franchising discipline, training, procurement, and menu-development routines.
