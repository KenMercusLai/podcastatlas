# 张勋 / Zhang Xun (Yuan Shu general)

张勋 / Zhang Xun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1018-ta-weihe-rang-caocao-jidan-sunce-weiju-liubei-wanxi-lqccdhrjmero-gt7vsaq2ejuhhw8|Hanji 1018]] as the [[YuanShu|袁术]] commander sent with [[QiaoRui|桥蕤]] and other forces to attack [[LyuBu|吕布]]. The episode presents him as the operational face of Yuan Shu's seven-direction campaign after the marriage alliance with Lü Bu breaks.

Zhang Xun's defeat demonstrates the source's coalition lesson. Once [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]] and [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] accept Lü Bu's legitimacy-and-spoils appeal, Zhang Xun's camp is caught unprepared and the larger Yuan Shu coalition rapidly comes apart.
