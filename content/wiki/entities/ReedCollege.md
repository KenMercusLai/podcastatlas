# Reed College

## Overview

Reed College is the higher-education institution whose withdrawal from U.S. News data submission and subsequent ranking decline motivated the statistical reconstruction in [[lord-of-the-rankings-lord-of-the-rankings|Lord of the Rankings]].

## Current Profile

Reed stopped supplying data because its administration no longer accepted the ranking system, but [[USNewsAndWorldReport|U.S. News and World Report]] continued to rank it. A statistics class led by [[KellyMcConville]] then combined disclosed ranking variables with public government data to estimate the hidden weighted model.

The reconstruction reportedly predicted other institutions closely while treating Reed as the exceptional anomaly. For 2019, the published rank was 90th and the reconstructed placement was around 36th. That gap raises a serious question about special treatment, but the supplied source does not reveal the official calculation or establish retaliation.

Reed's durable role in the wiki is methodological as well as institutional: the case shows how an outsider can test a consequential opaque model through public inputs, residuals, and comparison cases without claiming to have recovered every hidden rule.

## Key Characteristics

- Withdrew voluntary data submission after rejecting the ranking system.
