# 柏直 / Bo Zhi

柏直 / Bo Zhi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-155-2-zhuan-gei-ni-piqi-da-de-pengyou-weiwang-panhan-jing-shi-liubang-ai-maren-ls5nvunmhakvefd2oxn4zdyoob4l|Hanji 155-2]] as the commander [[WeiBao|魏豹]] uses when Han attacks West Wei. After [[LiShiqi|郦食其]] reports Bo Zhi's role, [[LiuBang|刘邦]] dismisses him as unable to resist [[HanXin|韩信]].

The page keeps Bo Zhi source-scoped. His importance in the episode is not a developed biography, but a personnel signal: the host contrasts him with [[ZhouShu|周叔]], the stronger commander Wei Bao does not use, making Bo Zhi part of the source's argument that Wei's defeat begins with misjudging command fit.
