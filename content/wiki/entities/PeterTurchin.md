# Peter Turchin

## Overview

Peter Turchin is the scholar invoked in the episode for the concept of [[EliteOverproduction|elite overproduction]].

## Current Profile

The episode uses Turchin's concept to interpret a recurring selection problem: education and credential seeking can create more people expecting elite status than the available offices can absorb. Chinese civil-service examinations provide the historical illustration, with candidate numbers growing faster than official posts.

## Key Characteristics

- Associated in the source with a structural account of elite competition.
- Treats educated aspiration and scarce positions as a potential source of instability.
- Used comparatively rather than as a complete explanation of Chinese history.

## Evidence

- Concept attribution: [[84-exams-glt6507641896]] attributes “elite overproduction” to Turchin.
- Historical application: [[84-exams-glt6507641896]] applies the idea to the gap between Chinese examination candidates and civil-service posts.
