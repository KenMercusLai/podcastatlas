# 申不害 / Shen Buhai

申不害 / Shen Buhai appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] as a low-status official from [[ZhengState|郑国]] whose Huang-Lao and xingming learning impress [[HanZhaohou|韩昭侯]]. The episode presents his promotion to [[HanState|韩国]]'s chief minister as a turn toward administrative and ruler-technique statecraft rather than a single military exploit.

The source's central Shen Buhai story is corrective. When Shen asks Han Zhaohou to appoint his cousin, the ruler refuses by citing Shen's own teaching on [[MeritBasedRewardPunishment|因功赏罚]]: an office cannot be granted where no matching merit exists. Shen Buhai's apology makes him less a patronage broker than the teacher whose doctrine the ruler now applies back to him.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-26-mengzi-laile-ln4twwp2frmr-xpumu0rqg-2gpyn]] closes Shen Buhai's branch with his death in 337 BCE. The episode contrasts this quiet death in office with [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s dramatic end, then follows [[ZhangKaidi|张开地]] as the next Han chief minister.
