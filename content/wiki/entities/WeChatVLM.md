# WeChat VLM / 微信 VLM

WeChat VLM is the independent large-model path inside [[WeChat]] described in [[yao-shunyu-laidao-tengxun-300tian-1-176-1]]. The source says WeChat keeps its own VLM team under [[ZhouHaoTencent]] even while cooperating with [[TencentHunyuan]] and sometimes calling Hunyuan, [[DeepSeek]], or WeChat's own model in different Xiaowei scenarios.

The source's explanation is organizational as much as technical. [[ZhangXiaolong]] is presented as prioritizing privacy, user data control, product fit, and reliability for a product serving more than a billion users. That makes WeChat VLM a concrete case of [[FederatedAIOrganization]]: model duplication may be inefficient under compute scarcity, but local product ownership can still be rational inside a high-stakes platform.

## Key Points
- The source says WeChat VLM released a 258B [[MixtureOfExperts|MoE]] model in January for WeChat-internal use cases such as Xiaowei and search.
- WeChat and Hunyuan exchange people and cooperate at the business layer, but Hunyuan compensation and rebuilding opportunities are described as more aggressive.
- WeChat's independent model path is linked to privacy and control rather than only to benchmark ambition.
- The source expects WeChat VLM and Hunyuan to coexist long term unless Tencent's centralization pressure becomes much stronger.
