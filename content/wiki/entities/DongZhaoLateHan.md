# 董昭 / Dong Zhao (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1003-sanguo-zui-bei-digu-de-moushi-dongzhao-llc7q-loennxw26v3u5cbhitikw1|Hanji 1003]] backfills Dong Zhao's role before Cao Cao reaches the court. While serving [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]] in Henei, he persuades Zhang Yang to let [[CaoCao|曹操]] communicate with the eastern Han court. After [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] reaches Anyi and Dong Zhao becomes yilang, he identifies [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] as the crucial route obstacle between Xu and [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]].

The episode makes Dong Zhao a case of [[ProactiveAdviserInitiative|主动补位式谋臣]]. Without waiting for direct instruction, he writes to Yang Feng in Cao Cao's name, praises Yang Feng's rescue-and-escort merit, and reframes Cao Cao as a grain-rich outside ally for Yang Feng's soldiers. The host reads the move as evidence that Dong Zhao recognized Cao Cao's future early and could turn that recognition into executable help.

董昭 / Dong Zhao (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1004-caocao-yingfeng-hanxiandi-shi-shui-de-zhuyi-lrrga-flmx5ajnhu1c42x7kooe94|Hanji 1004]] as the adviser who gives [[CaoCao|曹操]] the decisive relocation plan after Cao Cao reaches [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] and gains access to [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]. The source says Dong Zhao praises Cao Cao's court-assisting move as a hegemony-level achievement, but immediately narrows the practical question: Luoyang's armed actors each have their own intentions and will not necessarily obey Cao Cao.

His advice is a combined political and logistical design. Dong Zhao proposes moving the emperor to [[Xudu|许县 / 许都]], while warning that Emperor Xian has only just returned to Luoyang after long displacement. He therefore suggests making food shortage and settlement needs the stated reason, then reassuring [[YangFengLateHan|杨奉]] with thanks before Yang Feng recognizes the strategic loss.

The page is source-scoped. Hanji 1004 does not narrate Dong Zhao's full career; it uses him as the person who turns "welcoming the emperor" into an executable court-control plan through supply, timing, and actor psychology.
