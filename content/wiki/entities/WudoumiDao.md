# 五斗米道 / Wudoumi Dao

五斗米道 / Wudoumi Dao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-995-ta-zuishang-cibei-weihe-duoci-cansha-enren-llicukoezccd5iycin4uq7qun51k|Hanji 995]] as [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]]'s religiously organized late-Han system. The host mentions it with [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]]'s [[TaipingDao|太平道]] as a comparator for religion's capacity to organize people and power in the late-Han breakdown.

The page is source-scoped. Hanji 995 does not narrate Wudoumi Dao's institutional details; it uses the example to clarify why [[ZeRong|笮融]]'s Buddhist institutions should be read as a possible power structure while not equating every religious movement with Ze Rong's violence.
