# 何进 / He Jin

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-948-dongzhuo-feidi-quanchen-ruhe-gaibian-huangquan-youxi-ltekg1a-5b5u37aeekjm3dm2m6kk|Hanji 948]] backfills He Jin's central late-Han succession role before the later household-reference branch. The episode says He Jin holds power after [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s death and supports [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]] as emperor; after He Jin is killed, [[DongZhuo|董卓]] enters the power vacuum and turns that He-family-backed succession into a target.

The source matters for He Jin because Dong Zhuo's deposition is followed by removal of the broader He-family base: [[EmpressDowagerHe|何太后]] is poisoned, [[HeMiaoLateHan|何苗]] is posthumously humiliated, and [[WuyangJun|武阳君]] is killed. He Jin therefore becomes the absent pivot whose earlier power explains why the family must be neutralized after the deposition.

何进 / He Jin enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] as the family status marker for [[LadyYinCaoCao|尹夫人]], whom the source identifies as his daughter-in-law. The episode does not develop He Jin's career; it uses the tie to explain why Lady Yin and [[HeYan|何晏]] already belong to an elite late-Han household world before their connection to [[CaoCao|曹操]].
