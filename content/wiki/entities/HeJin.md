# 何进 / He Jin

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-931-rensheng-jiannan-chu-bianshi-xiuxin-shi-lpzmvxop8lmlwvxhoojzj5ok9qd6|Hanji 931]] backfills He Jin before the succession and anti-eunuch crisis. The episode names him with [[YangCiLateHan|杨赐]] and [[YuanWeiLateHan|袁隗]] as a senior figure who helps rescue [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] after Wang Yun is imprisoned through [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]]'s retaliation.

This early notice gives He Jin a narrower role than the later palace-conflict episodes. He is not yet the failed anti-eunuch planner; he appears as a high-status rescuer whose intervention confirms Wang Yun's political weight inside [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s court.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-942-yue-youxiu-de-ren-huode-yue-lei-heli-ma-lkwlrcgnt-kxxc2qqz6z6wkg7e5z|Hanji 942]] backfills He Jin before [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s death and before the later anti-eunuch plans. [[JianShuoLateHan|蹇硕]] and other attendants urge the emperor to send He Jin west against [[HanSui|韩遂]], which He Jin reads as a bid to remove him from the capital power center.

He Jin does not refuse directly. He delays by asking [[YuanShao|袁绍]] to gather troops from [[Xuzhou|徐州]] and [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] first, turning a dangerous court order into time-buying maneuvering. This episode therefore gives his later dependence on Yuan Shao and outside-force logic an earlier pre-succession root.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-943-quanshi-kaoyan-renxing-beihou-cangzhe-juda-xiongxian-lg7ia5xok-swdmhml35yv9qqkvt|Hanji 943]] backfills He Jin before the anti-eunuch planning in Hanji 944. After [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] dies without a named heir, [[JianShuoLateHan|蹇硕]] tries to lure He Jin into the palace and kill him before installing Liu Xie, later [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]. [[PanYinLateHan|潘隐]] warns He Jin, so He Jin survives long enough for [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]] and [[EmpressDowagerHe|何太后]] to become the accession center.

The same source makes He Jin the first winner and first escalator in the post-Lingdi succession field. [[GuoShengLateHan|郭胜]] gives him Jian Shuo's warning letter, He Jin has Jian Shuo killed and absorbs his palace guards, then moves against [[EmpressDowagerDongLateHan|董太后]] and [[DongZhongLateHan|董重]]. This makes his later Hanji 944 problem sharper: He Jin has already used decisive violence against one eunuch and one rival succession camp, but the broader eunuch-court conflict is still unresolved.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-944-xiang-zai-luanshi-zhong-cunhuo-xian-guan-zhu-zui-lp7omiak9mpcigrj4f1nummfwntr|Hanji 944]] backfills He Jin's earlier decision trap after [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s death. [[YuanShao|袁绍]] urges him to eliminate the [[TenAttendantsLateHan|十常侍]], but [[EmpressDowagerHe|何太后]], [[HeMiaoLateHan|何苗]], and [[WuyangJun|武阳君]] keep the He-family and eunuch-interest field tied together, while [[ChenLinLateHan|陈琳]] and [[CaoCao|曹操]] warn that broad mobilization and leaked intent will make the plan fail.

The episode makes He Jin a case of [[LethalPlanLeakageRisk|致命计划泄露风险]] before he becomes the later case of outside-force escalation. He has formal position and troops, but his partial approach exposes intent without settling the target, creating the precondition for the wider [[ExternalTroopInvitationRisk|外兵入京升级风险]] and [[ZeroSumConflictHesitationRisk|死斗局优柔寡断风险]] sequence in Hanji 945 and Hanji 946.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-945-zhinian-tai-shen-de-ren-zhihui-yuelaiyue-tongku-lm6xaiy2pcqunke04yzqwwxsxxl|Hanji 945]] backfills He Jin immediately before the fatal palace entry in Hanji 946. The episode presents him as fixed on [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s plan to summon outside troops into [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] so that military pressure can force [[EmpressDowagerHe|何太后]] to authorize action against the [[TenAttendantsLateHan|十常侍]].

The source makes He Jin's failure begin before his death. [[ZhengTaiLateHan|郑泰]] and [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]] warn him not to bring [[DongZhuo|董卓]] into the capital, while [[ChenLinLateHan|陈琳]] and [[CaoCao|曹操]] are named as earlier warning voices. He Jin still mobilizes [[WangKuangLateHan|王匡]], [[BaoXin|鲍信]], [[QiaoMaoLateHan|乔瑁]], and [[DingYuanLateHan|丁原]], turning an inner-court problem into [[ExternalTroopInvitationRisk|外兵入京升级风险]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-946-zhichang-douzheng-shi-yourouguaduan-you-duo-zhiming-lpyyweaajonzg7kxuhwm9z7ae7p5|Hanji 946]] supplies He Jin's death scene and makes him the central negative case for [[ZeroSumConflictHesitationRisk|死斗局优柔寡断风险]]. He Jin tries to persuade [[EmpressDowagerHe|何太后]] to eliminate the [[TenAttendantsLateHan|十常侍]], but his delay and reliance on palace procedure leave him exposed to [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]] and [[DuanGuiLateHan|段珪]].

The episode says He Jin is lured deeper into the palace under a claimed dowager order and killed by [[QuMuLateHan|渠穆]]. His head then becomes the signal that turns a factional struggle into [[PalaceCoupViolenceCascade|宫廷政变暴力级联]]: forged orders, gate fighting, mass eunuch killing, He-family infighting, imperial flight, and the opening for [[DongZhuo|董卓]] all follow.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-947-dongzhuo-shoufu-lvbu-kaiqi-chengba-zhilu-lvr-24l6j8f6zvpfcmqgknmgywa1|Hanji 947]] adds the immediate aftermath of He Jin's death before Hanji 948's He-family liquidation branch. The power vacuum after his killing lets [[DongZhuo|董卓]] enter the capital field, while troops formerly attached to He Jin soon go over to Dong Zhuo.

This source makes He Jin's absence active: his failed handling of the eunuch crisis opens the space in which Dong Zhuo can turn the He-family succession base around [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]] into a target.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-948-dongzhuo-feidi-quanchen-ruhe-gaibian-huangquan-youxi-ltekg1a-5b5u37aeekjm3dm2m6kk|Hanji 948]] backfills He Jin's central late-Han succession role before the later household-reference branch. The episode says He Jin holds power after [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s death and supports [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]] as emperor; after He Jin is killed, [[DongZhuo|董卓]] enters the power vacuum and turns that He-family-backed succession into a target.

The source matters for He Jin because Dong Zhuo's deposition is followed by removal of the broader He-family base: [[EmpressDowagerHe|何太后]] is poisoned, [[HeMiaoLateHan|何苗]] is posthumously humiliated, and [[WuyangJun|武阳君]] is killed. He Jin therefore becomes the absent pivot whose earlier power explains why the family must be neutralized after the deposition.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] later uses He Jin as the family status marker for [[LadyYinCaoCao|尹夫人]], whom the source identifies as his daughter-in-law. The episode does not develop He Jin's career; it uses the tie to explain why Lady Yin and [[HeYan|何晏]] already belong to an elite late-Han household world before their connection to [[CaoCao|曹操]].
