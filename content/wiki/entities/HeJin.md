# 何进 / He Jin

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-945-zhinian-tai-shen-de-ren-zhihui-yuelaiyue-tongku-lm6xaiy2pcqunke04yzqwwxsxxl|Hanji 945]] backfills He Jin immediately before the fatal palace entry in Hanji 946. The episode presents him as fixed on [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s plan to summon outside troops into [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] so that military pressure can force [[EmpressDowagerHe|何太后]] to authorize action against the [[TenAttendantsLateHan|十常侍]].

The source makes He Jin's failure begin before his death. [[ZhengTaiLateHan|郑泰]] and [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]] warn him not to bring [[DongZhuo|董卓]] into the capital, while [[ChenLinLateHan|陈琳]] and [[CaoCao|曹操]] are named as earlier warning voices. He Jin still mobilizes [[WangKuangLateHan|王匡]], [[BaoXin|鲍信]], [[QiaoMaoLateHan|乔瑁]], and [[DingYuanLateHan|丁原]], turning an inner-court problem into [[ExternalTroopInvitationRisk|外兵入京升级风险]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-946-zhichang-douzheng-shi-yourouguaduan-you-duo-zhiming-lpyyweaajonzg7kxuhwm9z7ae7p5|Hanji 946]] supplies He Jin's death scene and makes him the central negative case for [[ZeroSumConflictHesitationRisk|死斗局优柔寡断风险]]. He Jin tries to persuade [[EmpressDowagerHe|何太后]] to eliminate the [[TenAttendantsLateHan|十常侍]], but his delay and reliance on palace procedure leave him exposed to [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]] and [[DuanGuiLateHan|段珪]].

The episode says He Jin is lured deeper into the palace under a claimed dowager order and killed by [[QuMuLateHan|渠穆]]. His head then becomes the signal that turns a factional struggle into [[PalaceCoupViolenceCascade|宫廷政变暴力级联]]: forged orders, gate fighting, mass eunuch killing, He-family infighting, imperial flight, and the opening for [[DongZhuo|董卓]] all follow.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-947-dongzhuo-shoufu-lvbu-kaiqi-chengba-zhilu-lvr-24l6j8f6zvpfcmqgknmgywa1|Hanji 947]] adds the immediate aftermath of He Jin's death before Hanji 948's He-family liquidation branch. The power vacuum after his killing lets [[DongZhuo|董卓]] enter the capital field, while troops formerly attached to He Jin soon go over to Dong Zhuo.

This source makes He Jin's absence active: his failed handling of the eunuch crisis opens the space in which Dong Zhuo can turn the He-family succession base around [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]] into a target.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-948-dongzhuo-feidi-quanchen-ruhe-gaibian-huangquan-youxi-ltekg1a-5b5u37aeekjm3dm2m6kk|Hanji 948]] backfills He Jin's central late-Han succession role before the later household-reference branch. The episode says He Jin holds power after [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s death and supports [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩 / 弘农王]] as emperor; after He Jin is killed, [[DongZhuo|董卓]] enters the power vacuum and turns that He-family-backed succession into a target.

The source matters for He Jin because Dong Zhuo's deposition is followed by removal of the broader He-family base: [[EmpressDowagerHe|何太后]] is poisoned, [[HeMiaoLateHan|何苗]] is posthumously humiliated, and [[WuyangJun|武阳君]] is killed. He Jin therefore becomes the absent pivot whose earlier power explains why the family must be neutralized after the deposition.

何进 / He Jin enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] as the family status marker for [[LadyYinCaoCao|尹夫人]], whom the source identifies as his daughter-in-law. The episode does not develop He Jin's career; it uses the tie to explain why Lady Yin and [[HeYan|何晏]] already belong to an elite late-Han household world before their connection to [[CaoCao|曹操]].
