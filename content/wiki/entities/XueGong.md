# 薛公 / Xue Gong

薛公 / Xue Gong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-2-jiemi-xinlingjun-ping-shenme-shi-si-gongzi-zhi-shou-lodqntthbil9o8ezppt66lywzd-f]] as a Zhao worthy whom [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] seeks after the Handan rescue. The episode describes him as doing small trade by selling wine slurry, placing him outside the normal noble setting of carriage, guest hall, and formal invitation.

The point of Xue Gong's scene is the same as [[MaoGong|毛公]]'s: Xinlingjun's reputation depends on crossing status boundaries without treating low status as disgrace. By entering Xue Gong's working environment on foot, Xinlingjun makes [[TalentAttractionThroughRespect|礼贤下士]] concrete rather than ceremonial.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-103-1-simaqian-kanzhong-de-xinlingjun-suan-shi-xiaoren-ma-lvty1pdnibvqpk0m16kkxzw4t-ck]] adds the return-to-Wei consequence of that relationship. Xue Gong and [[MaoGong|毛公]] tell [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] that the lords honor him because he remains tied to [[WeiState|魏国]], so letting Qin destroy [[Daliang|大梁]] would leave him without the political ground that made his reputation possible.
