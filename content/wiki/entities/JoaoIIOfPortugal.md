# João II of Portugal

## Overview

João II of Portugal was the monarch who rejected [[ChristopherColumbus|Christopher Columbus's]] westward proposal while Portugal pursued the African route to Asia.

## Current Profile

[[306-columbus-the-adventure-begins-part-1-glt6089901915]] presents João's decision as informed rather than obscurantist. His experts correctly judged Asia much farther west than Columbus claimed, and Portuguese success around Africa made the speculative alternative strategically unnecessary.

## Key Characteristics

- Patron of an established Portuguese maritime program.
- Recipient of Columbus's first state-sponsorship proposal.
- Ruler whose experts identified the proposal's distance error.
- Strategic decision-maker favoring the advancing African route.

## Evidence

- Technical review: [[306-columbus-the-adventure-begins-part-1-glt6089901915]] says João's experts rejected Columbus's underestimated ocean distance.
- Strategic context: [[306-columbus-the-adventure-begins-part-1-glt6089901915]] links the rejection to Portuguese progress around Africa, strengthened by Dias rounding the Cape.

## Qualifications

The source does not reconstruct João's court or policy comprehensively. The review procedure, chronology, and balance between technical and strategic objections remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Established João as the informed first royal rejecter of Columbus's proposal.
