# RAF Fairford

## Overview
RAF Fairford is a sovereign British military air base used by United States forces under bilateral and NATO arrangements. It appears in [[foiled-plot-did-iran-plan-british-base-attack-6aba32b170b66274d96fb345]] as the site near an alleged explosives plot whose sponsor had not been established.

## Current Profile
The source describes the base as supporting large American transport aircraft and bombers and as strategically relevant to operations in Europe, the Middle East, and the 2026 conflict involving [[Iran]]. That military role makes the alleged incident consequential: a confirmed state-backed kinetic attack against the base would be more direct and harder to deny than many forms of grey-zone pressure. The wiki does not infer the plot's perpetrator from the base's operational relevance.

## Key Characteristics
- A sovereign British base with substantial use by United States forces.
- Supports transport aircraft and bombers for operations extending beyond Britain.
- Holds strategic relevance to American operations in Europe and the Middle East.
- Became the focus of an unresolved suspected explosives plot in September 2026.
