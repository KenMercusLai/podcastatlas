# 李闯 / Li Chuang

李闯 / Li Chuang is the guest in [[shan-shang-shan-xia-jie-renjian-wushi-xiaoyao-ji-shenxian-lhjiguq74flhatdxnbrnzhjg-acp]] and the author of [[CizhiShangShan|《辞职上山》]]. The episode presents him through a chain of roles: anthropology student, fieldworker in [[Xishuangbanna|西双版纳]], soldier, publishing editor, hutong shop owner, [[WudangMountain|武当山]] volunteer, and later Chinese-medicine student and county-hospital intern.

His importance in the wiki is methodological. The episode treats these changes less as romantic wandering than as repeated testing of how to live, observe, write, and regain bodily and mental room under constraint. Li Chuang's strongest contribution is to connect [[FieldworkAsKnowledgeMethod]], [[DaoistTempleEverydayLabor]], [[MedicineAsWaySeeking|以医入道]], and [[XiaoyaoAsNonDependence|逍遥作为不被外物左右]] without turning any of them into a simple escape plan.

## Key Claims
- Li Chuang's life choices are exploratory, but the source emphasizes calculation, poverty experience, bodily discomfort, and institutional limits as much as spontaneity.
- His Wudang experience is used to demystify spiritual retreat through weather, shifts, chores, scenic-area pressure, and interpersonal reality.
- His fieldnote method separates event record from subjective response before turning lived material into publishable nonfiction.
- His current medical study is framed as a search for life and body understanding rather than a settled professional-doctor path.
