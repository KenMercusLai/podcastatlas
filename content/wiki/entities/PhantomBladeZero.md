## Overview

《影之刃零》 is a large single-player action game developed by [[SGame|灵游坊]] and produced by [[LiangQiwei|梁其伟]], extending the fictional lineage of [[RainBlood|《雨血》]].

## Current Profile

The source positions the game as a modern wuxia work built around dark beauty, damaged personalities, martial-arts roots, and “kung-fu punk” modernization. Its character contrast between [[SoulPhantomBlade|魂]] and [[ZuoShang|左殇]] turns a personal-versus-totalizing moral tension into narrative structure, while self-publishing is intended to preserve the project's international and cultural positioning.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines dark wuxia atmosphere with modern visual and action design.
- Uses traceable martial arts, architecture, sound, and weapon traditions as cultural roots.
- Avoids imposing an abstract patriotic or sacrificial slogan that conflicts with its revenge and conspiracy world.
- Contrasts self-rescue with grand world-saving ambition through its central characters.
- Keeps artistic creation human-led while reportedly using AI in technical testing, logic, and optimization work.
