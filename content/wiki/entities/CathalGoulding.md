# Cathal Goulding

Cathal Goulding appears in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as the [[IrishRepublicanArmy|IRA]] chief of staff who pushed the organization away from a conventional armed rising fantasy and toward socialist activism. The episode says Marxist academics and British Communist Party figures influenced this turn.

For the source, Goulding helps explain the IRA's mid-1960s quietness and internal tension. Older conservative veterans disliked the move, while loyalist fear could still treat the IRA as a latent enemy even when the organization was not yet campaigning at Troubles scale.
