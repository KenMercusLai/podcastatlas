# 《江村经济》 / Peasant Life in China

## Overview

[[JiangcunEconomy|《江村经济》]] is [[FeiXiaotong|费孝通]]'s ethnographic study of a Jiangnan village economy, used in the episode to distinguish intensive community research from both nationwide social theory and literary synthesis.

## Current Profile

The work makes village institutions, household livelihood, land pressure, and sideline production empirically visible. Its methodological importance lies in the bounded case: close observation can correct abstract claims, but [[RegionalVariationInRuralChina]] prevents one community from representing the whole country without comparison.

The episode also separates the book from [[CocoonFeiXiaotong|《茧》]]. The novel draws on a wider mixture of factories, biographies, hometown experience, and Jiangnan reform, whereas *Peasant Life in China* remains tied to a particular field site and ethnographic problem.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses intensive community study to examine a village economy in concrete institutional detail.
- Connects household livelihood to land pressure, sideline industry, and wider markets.
- Offers a qualitative ethnographic perspective distinct from large statistical rural surveys.
- Supports grounded theory testing while retaining a strong regional scope boundary.
- Informs but does not exhaust Fei's wider literary and reform thinking.

## Qualifications

The episode summary does not reproduce the book's data, field notes, or full argument. Its use here establishes methodological position and relation to *Cocoon*, not a comprehensive interpretation. The village's Jiangnan setting is evidence for regional comparison, not an automatic national model.

## What Changed

- Created a bounded profile distinguishing the ethnography from Fei's broader fictional synthesis.
- Added an explicit warning against national generalization from one Jiangnan community.
