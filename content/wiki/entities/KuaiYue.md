# 蒯越 / Kuai Yue

蒯越 / Kuai Yue enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1011-miheng-zhisi-zuiju-beiju-secai-de-jiedao-sharen-ltrzpchwdfcyp4ntmum9giu-5mva|Hanji 1011]] as one of the Jingzhou elite figures around [[LiuBiao|刘表]] whom [[MiHengLateHan|祢衡]] mocks after arriving from [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s side. The transcript appears as "款越/款月"; the wiki normalizes it to Kuai Yue in this late-Han context.

The source does not give Kuai Yue a standalone policy role. His function is social and political: Mi Heng's sharp speech offends Liu Biao's surrounding elite, and that offended circle becomes the environment in which rumors against Mi Heng can circulate. The episode therefore uses Kuai Yue as part of the local pressure that lets Liu Biao move from hospitality to delegated removal.
