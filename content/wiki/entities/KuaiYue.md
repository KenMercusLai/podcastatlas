# 蒯越 / Kuai Yue

## Overview
蒯越 / Kuai Yue is a Jingzhou elite adviser whose profile runs from hard-edged provincial consolidation under [[LiuBiao|刘表]] to senior appointment under [[CaoCao|曹操]] after the province changes hands.

## Current Profile
Hanji 953 makes Kuai Yue the coercive complement to [[KuaiLiang|蒯良]]: local armed clan heads should be induced, dangerous leaders removed, followers absorbed, and [[XiangyangLateHan|襄阳]] joined with [[JianglingLateHan|江陵]] in a geographic control design. Hanji 1010 places him within the elite alliance that stabilizes northern [[Jingzhou|荆州]]. Hanji 1011 shows the darker court-circle side, where [[MiHengLateHan|祢衡]]'s insults help produce an environment of slander and delegated removal.

Hanji 1095 adds the regime-transfer endpoint. After Jingzhou submits, Cao Cao appoints Kuai Yue minister of the household, converting a locally embedded adviser into senior new-regime capacity.

## Key Characteristics
- Supplies Liu Biao with a force-absorption strategy for initial Jingzhou consolidation.
- Treats Xiangyang and Jiangling as paired control points.
- Represents the local elite layer supporting Liu Biao's rule.
- Appears in the offended court circle around Mi Heng's transfer.
- Receives senior office from Cao Cao after Jingzhou changes hands.
