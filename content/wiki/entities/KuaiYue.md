# 蒯越 / Kuai Yue

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-953-luanshi-zhizhong-yaobuyao-jiangjiu-renyi-lrzfimgqatjxyw9zx9ry2kiwxkon|Hanji 953]] backfills Kuai Yue's decisive role in [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s first [[Jingzhou|Jingzhou]] consolidation. Paired with [[KuaiLiang|蒯良]]'s people-support advice, Kuai Yue gives the operationally harsher counsel: [[YuanShu|袁术]] can be discounted as arrogant and strategically shallow, while Jingzhou's armed clan heads should be induced into Liu Biao's orbit, stripped of their most dangerous leaders, and converted into usable troops. His north-south emphasis on [[XiangyangLateHan|襄阳]] and [[JianglingLateHan|江陵]] makes local elite removal part of a geographic control design, not mere retaliation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1010-sanguo-liubiao-jiujing-shibushi-piaoliang-de-caobao-lo58hzfo-qv0soauogtgfrkourrd|Hanji 1010]] adds Kuai Yue to [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s earlier [[Jingzhou|荆州]] consolidation. The transcript renders the name as "款越"; the wiki keeps the canonical late-Han form. In this source, Kuai Yue helps mark the local elite layer through which Liu Biao enters, stabilizes, and controls northern Jingzhou.

蒯越 / Kuai Yue enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1011-miheng-zhisi-zuiju-beiju-secai-de-jiedao-sharen-ltrzpchwdfcyp4ntmum9giu-5mva|Hanji 1011]] as one of the Jingzhou elite figures around [[LiuBiao|刘表]] whom [[MiHengLateHan|祢衡]] mocks after arriving from [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s side. The transcript appears as "款越/款月"; the wiki normalizes it to Kuai Yue in this late-Han context.

The source does not give Kuai Yue a standalone policy role. His function is social and political: Mi Heng's sharp speech offends Liu Biao's surrounding elite, and that offended circle becomes the environment in which rumors against Mi Heng can circulate. The episode therefore uses Kuai Yue as part of the local pressure that lets Liu Biao move from hospitality to delegated removal.
