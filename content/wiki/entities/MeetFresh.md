# Meet Fresh / 鲜芋仙

Meet Fresh / 鲜芋仙 appears in [[145-gaijia-zhongzi-de-canyin-yangpinpai-feng-tou-quan-1-145-1]] as a Taiwan-founded dessert brand later sold to the private-equity-backed [[CFBGroup]]. The source places the brand in a dessert and "happiness" lane alongside [[DairyQueen]], treating sweet products as part of [[EmotionEconomy]] and [[ExperientialRetail]] rather than only food calories.

The episode's main use of Meet Fresh is platform logic. A private-equity-backed operator can buy or operate multiple dessert, ice-cream, and pizza assets, then try to share site selection, franchise management, product cadence, and back-office capabilities across brands.
