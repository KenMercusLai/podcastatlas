## Overview

刘縯 / Liu Yan is synthesized as [[LiuXiu|刘秀]]'s elder brother and an early anti-Xin organizer whose coalition-building strength, strategic caution, and restoration merit later become politically dangerous. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-661-cong-shusheng-dao-kaiguo-huangdi-liuxiu-shoudeng-lishi-wutai-lmw-rgyoraks9loeouuxk5lyxfs5|Hanji 661]] now supplies the first layer: Liu Yan answers Li Tong's planned Nanyang seizure by calling local豪杰 to restore Han against [[WangMang|王莽]] and by raising Chongling youths with Liu Xiu's stabilizing presence. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-662-liuxiu-shi-ruhe-huajie-tuandui-neidou-de-lsuin9kf1epqfo2yjlk-q1-zwx2|Hanji 662]] then shows him gathering clan and youth forces, connecting with New Market and Pinglin troops, suffering the Xiaochang'an defeat, and rebuilding morale by seeking Xiajiang cooperation through [[WangChangXiajiangGengshi|王常]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-663-gengshidi-liuxuan-shi-tan-fu-bushangqiang-de-lanni-lott3pdjjunwlv3wgksn4r5tiqyz|Hanji 663]] follows with the leadership-selection problem: Nanyang and Xiajiang actors prefer Liu Yan as leader, but New Market and Pinglin commanders fear he will be hard to control and use [[LiuXuanGengshiEmperor|刘玄]] instead. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-664-kunyang-zhizhan-jueding-guoyun-de-lishi-zhuanzhedian-lihrwawp4x1w1pn4aibdohq4-l-4|Hanji 664]] then shows him through the Wan surrender and Cen Peng clemency branch. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-665-ta-busi-liuxiu-ye-nandang-huangdi-liauc3k3omnzqu09dqicxifjxy1t|Hanji 665]] supplies the detailed killing: Gengshi-side commanders fear Liu Yan and Liu Xiu's rising prestige, Liu Xuan accepts the danger frame, and Liu Yan is executed with Liu Ji after Zhu Wei and Li Yi press for quick action.

## Current Profile

The sources give Liu Yan five bounded roles. First, Hanji 661 presents him as a local restoration initiator before the wider rebel coalition forms. In response to Li Tong's proposed coordination, Liu Yan summons local豪杰, condemns Wang Mang as a ruler who has harmed the people, reads drought and disorder as signs of Wang Mang's fall, and calls for restoring Gaozu's enterprise. Chongling youths initially hide from the danger, but Liu Xiu's commander-like appearance reassures them that the cautious brother is also committed; the group eventually gathers seven or eight thousand people.

Second, Hanji 662 presents him as a pre-Gengshi coalition organizer. He gathers seven or eight thousand clan and youth fighters, sends kin to persuade New Market and Pinglin troops, and continues expanding after Liu Xiu settles a spoils crisis. When Xiaochang'an badly damages morale and New Market/Pinglin troops consider flight, Liu Yan goes personally to the Xiajiang camp with Liu Xiu and Li Tong. Wang Chang accepts the restoration argument and persuades Cheng Dan and Zhang Ang, after which Liu Yan hosts the allied troops, makes an oath, gives them rest, and sends six routes to capture Lanxiang.

Third, Hanji 663 presents Liu Yan as the stronger but more threatening leadership alternative during the Gengshi elevation debate. Liu Yan argues that claiming emperor too soon will create suspicion and internal Liu-family conflict while Wang Mang is still alive and Chimei remains unsettled; a royal title would be enough for command until the anti-Xin field is settled.

Fourth, after Wan surrenders, some Gengshi-side commanders want to kill [[CenPengEasternHan|岑彭]] because he had defended the city. Liu Yan argues that Cen Peng's defense showed integrity and should be recognized rather than punished, leading to Cen Peng's survival and title.

Fifth, Hanji 665 explains the death that Hanji 664 only previewed. Liu Xiu warns Liu Yan that Liu Xuan already treats the brothers as enemies, but Liu Yan does not withdraw or reduce exposure. Shentu Jian's jade-ring signal, Fan Hong's warning, Li Yi's shift toward new-power circles, and Liu Ji's refusal of appointment all precede the final pretext. When Liu Yan objects to Liu Ji's execution, Zhu Wei and Li Yi advise Liu Xuan to bind and kill Liu Yan as well, and both are executed the same day.

Liu Yan's profile therefore moves from coalition organizer to strategic caution voice, clemency judge at Wan, and case of [[PostRestorationMeritThreat|post-restoration merit threat]]. His merit, prestige, and follower network make him useful against Xin, but after victory they make the Gengshi center treat him as a rival pole.

## Key Characteristics

- Elder brother of Liu Xiu and early anti-Xin organizer who connects clan troops with New Market, Pinglin, and Xiajiang forces.
- Chongling initiator who frames drought, disorder, and Wang Mang's rule as reasons to restore Han.
- Post-defeat coalition repair actor who seeks Xiajiang cooperation after Xiaochang'an and turns agreement into the Lanxiang operation.
- Preferred by Nanyang and Xiajiang actors but rejected by New Market and Pinglin commanders as too hard to control.
- Strategic caution voice who argues against immediate imperial title before Wang Mang and Chimei are defeated.
- Persuades the Gengshi side to spare Cen Peng after Wan surrenders by treating loyal defense as reusable integrity rather than an offense.
- Killed after Liu Ji's refusal of appointment gives Liu Xuan's faction a usable pretext.

## Qualifications

The current page still does not reconstruct Liu Yan's full career before the 22 CE coalition scenes or every faction around the Gengshi court. Hanji 661 supplies the Chongling mobilization and restoration appeal but leaves Liu family genealogy, Liu Liang, Liu Zhong, Liu Yuan, Li Tong, Li Yi, Deng Chen, Cai Shaogong, Liu Shou, Li Shou, Zhen Fu, Liangqiu Ci, exact troop counts, and local geography source-scoped. Hanji 662 supplies the initial army-gathering, Xiaochang'an defeat, Xiajiang cooperation, and Lanxiang attack layers but keeps Liu Zhong, Liu Yuan, Liu Boji, Li Tong, Li Yi, Deng Chen, Wang Feng, Chen Mu, Cheng Dan, Xiaochang'an, Lanxiang, exact troop counts, and the astronomy insertion source-scoped. Hanji 663 supplies the leadership alternative and delay advice but leaves New Market/Pinglin/Xiajiang faction boundaries and the exact decision mechanics source-scoped. Hanji 665 supplies the direct killing sequence but leaves Shentu Jian, Fan Hong, Liu Ji, exact command titles, and the internal New Market/Pinglin faction map source-scoped. Zhu Wei and Li Yi are linked through existing Guangwu-Gengshi normalization, but the source's exact name forms remain ASR-sensitive.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 661 as the earliest layer: Liu Yan joins Li Tong's coordination, frames Wang Mang's fall through drought and disorder, and raises Chongling youths with Liu Xiu's stabilizing participation.
- Preserved Hanji 662 as the next layer: Liu Yan gathers anti-Xin forces, recovers from Xiaochang'an by seeking Xiajiang cooperation, and turns the alliance into the Lanxiang operation.
- Preserved Hanji 663 as the leadership-selection layer: Liu Yan is the stronger but less controllable alternative and argues for delaying imperial title.
- Preserved Hanji 665 as the detailed Gengshi-court suspicion, Liu Ji pretext, Zhu Wei/Li Yi advice, and same-day execution sequence.
- Reframed Liu Yan as a coalition organizer, strategic caution voice, clemency actor at Wan, and high-merit restorer whose prestige becomes lethal after victory.
