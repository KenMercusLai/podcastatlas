## Overview

刘縯 / Liu Yan is synthesized as [[LiuXiu|刘秀]]'s elder brother and an early Gengshi-side figure whose restoration merit becomes politically dangerous. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-664-kunyang-zhizhan-jueding-guoyun-de-lishi-zhuanzhedian-lihrwawp4x1w1pn4aibdohq4-l-4|Hanji 664]] introduces him through the Wan surrender and Cen Peng clemency branch. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-665-ta-busi-liuxiu-ye-nandang-huangdi-liauc3k3omnzqu09dqicxifjxy1t|Hanji 665]] then supplies the detailed killing: Gengshi-side commanders fear Liu Yan and Liu Xiu's rising prestige, [[LiuXuanGengshiEmperor|刘玄]] accepts the danger frame, and Liu Yan is executed with Liu Ji after Zhu Wei and Li Yi press for quick action.

This page is deliberately disambiguated from [[LiuYanDongCommandery|刘延 / Liu Yan (Dong Commandery)]], [[LiuYanFulingKingEasternHan|阜陵王刘延]], and [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉 / Liu Yan (late Han)]].

## Current Profile

The sources give Liu Yan two bounded roles. First, after Wan surrenders, some Gengshi-side commanders want to kill [[CenPengEasternHan|岑彭]] because he had defended the city. Liu Yan argues that Cen Peng's defense showed integrity and should be recognized rather than punished, leading to Cen Peng's survival and title.

Second, Hanji 665 explains the death that Hanji 664 only previewed. Liu Xiu warns Liu Yan that Liu Xuan already treats the brothers as enemies, but Liu Yan does not withdraw or reduce exposure. Shentu Jian's jade-ring signal, Fan Hong's warning, Li Yi's shift toward new-power circles, and Liu Ji's refusal of appointment all precede the final pretext. When Liu Yan objects to Liu Ji's execution, Zhu Wei and Li Yi advise Liu Xuan to bind and kill Liu Yan as well, and both are executed the same day.

Liu Yan's profile therefore moves from a clemency judge at Wan to a case of [[PostRestorationMeritThreat|post-restoration merit threat]]. His merit, prestige, and follower network make him useful against Xin, but after victory they make the Gengshi center treat him as a rival pole.

## Key Characteristics

- Elder brother of Liu Xiu in the early anti-Xin and Gengshi setting.
- Persuades the Gengshi side to spare Cen Peng after Wan surrenders.
- Treats loyal defense and personal integrity as reasons for reuse rather than revenge.
- High-prestige restorer whose merit becomes threatening to the Gengshi court after Kunyang.
- Killed after Liu Ji's refusal of appointment gives Liu Xuan's faction a usable pretext.
- Disambiguated from several unrelated Liu Yan/Liu Yan pages already in the wiki.

## Qualifications

The current page still does not reconstruct Liu Yan's full career before Wan or every faction around the Gengshi court. Hanji 665 supplies the direct killing sequence but leaves Shentu Jian, Fan Hong, Liu Ji, exact command titles, and the internal New Market/Pinglin faction map source-scoped. Zhu Wei and Li Yi are linked through existing Guangwu-Gengshi normalization, but the source's exact name forms remain ASR-sensitive.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 665 to replace the earlier death preview with the detailed Gengshi-court suspicion, Liu Ji pretext, Zhu Wei/Li Yi advice, and same-day execution sequence.
- Reframed Liu Yan as both a clemency actor at Wan and a high-merit restorer whose prestige becomes lethal after victory.
