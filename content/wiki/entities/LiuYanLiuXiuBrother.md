This page is deliberately disambiguated from [[LiuYanDongCommandery|刘延 / Liu Yan (Dong Commandery)]], [[LiuYanFulingKingEasternHan|阜陵王刘延]], and [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉 / Liu Yan (late Han)]].

## Current Profile

The source gives Liu Yan two bounded roles. First, after Wan surrenders, some Gengshi-side commanders want to kill [[CenPengEasternHan|岑彭]] because he had defended the city. Liu Yan argues that Cen Peng's defense showed integrity and should be recognized rather than punished, leading to Cen Peng's survival and title.

Second, the episode closes by making Liu Yan's impending death the next conflict. Liu Xiu's power is rising after Kunyang and Feng Yi's surrender, but the source says Liu Yan will be killed by his own side. The detailed cause is not in this source, so the page records only the cliffhanger and keeps the later political mechanics open for a future ingest.

## Key Characteristics
