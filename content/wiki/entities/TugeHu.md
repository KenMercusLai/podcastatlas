# 屠各胡 / Tuge Hu

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-939-donghan-luanju-de-fengkuang-sanguo-xuqu-lthmvw8ihk96fuez9vamlryzcngy|Hanji 939]] introduces 屠各胡 / Tuge Hu as a frontier group involved in two linked 188 CE crises. The source first says the Tuge Hu kill [[ZhangYiLateHan|张懿]], inspector of [[BingzhouLateHan|并州]], making the province a sign of exposed late-Han office authority.

The same episode then places the Tuge Hu inside the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] revolt. [[XiluoSouthernXiongnu|醯落]] joins with them, kills [[QiangquChanyu|羌渠]], and helps create the conditions for [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]]'s succession. The page therefore links provincial breakdown and frontier-conscription backlash rather than treating the group as a standalone ethnographic entry.
