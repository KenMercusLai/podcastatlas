## Overview

刘瑜 / Liu Yu (late Han astrologer) is a disambiguated late-Han omen interpreter in the 168 CE Dou Wu crisis sequence. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-884-zhichang-chujian-yao-dui-shui-xian-kaidao-lvtc3uqlso242olkyin3fvz5a7ys|Hanji 884]] first places him among renowned figures entering the court center, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-885-shengsi-guantou-ta-qingjia-xiuxi-luydnh9oqdyyfpbeztooi2e7lvca|Hanji 885]] gives his actionable role: he warns [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦妙 / 窦皇太后]], [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]], and [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] that an abnormal Venus position signals palace danger, closed gates, and hidden enemies near the court.

## Current Profile

Liu Yu's profile is narrow but important because it joins reputation and omen interpretation. Hanji 884 places him with [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]], [[DuMiLateHan|杜密]], and [[YinXunLateHan|尹勋]] in the group of renowned figures entering the power core. Hanji 885 then shows why his role matters: he translates a celestial event into immediate security advice. The episode says Venus intrudes into the Fang mansion's "upper general" position and enters the Taiwei star field; Liu Yu reads this as a warning that ministers are endangered and that enemies are already close to the palace center. His warning helps push Dou Wu and Chen Fan into capital-defense appointments and the replacement of Huangmenling Wei Biao with [[ShanBingLateHan|山冰]].

The page keeps Liu Yu separate from [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞 / Liu Yu (Late Han)]], the Youzhou governor and later alternative-emperor refusal figure. The identical pinyin would otherwise create an identity collision.

## Key Characteristics

- Liu Yu is a renowned court-entry figure and omen interpreter rather than a military or faction leader in the current evidence.
- His warning makes sky-sign interpretation actionable as palace-security advice.
- He addresses both the dowager side and the Dou/Chen minister side, making the warning a bridge between omen reading and operational preparation.
- His role sharpens the pre-leak timing problem: danger is identified, but the political response remains slow and partial.
- His page is intentionally disambiguated from Liu Yu / 刘虞 of Youzhou.

## Evidence

- Reform-cohort entry: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-884-zhichang-chujian-yao-dui-shui-xian-kaidao-lvtc3uqlso242olkyin3fvz5a7ys|Hanji 884]] places Liu Yu among renowned figures entering the power center with Li Ying, Du Mi, and Yin Xun.
- Omen reading: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-885-shengsi-guantou-ta-qingjia-xiuxi-luydnh9oqdyyfpbeztooi2e7lvca|Hanji 885]] says Liu Yu fears an abnormal Venus movement into Fang and Taiwei star fields.
- Warning to the court center: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-885-shengsi-guantou-ta-qingjia-xiuxi-luydnh9oqdyyfpbeztooi2e7lvca|Hanji 885]] says he writes to Dou Miao that the omen means palace gates should be shut and that hidden enemies are near.
- Warning to Dou and Chen: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-885-shengsi-guantou-ta-qingjia-xiuxi-luydnh9oqdyyfpbeztooi2e7lvca|Hanji 885]] says Liu Yu also warns Dou Wu and Chen Fan to prepare countermeasures.
- Resulting preparation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-885-shengsi-guantou-ta-qingjia-xiuxi-luydnh9oqdyyfpbeztooi2e7lvca|Hanji 885]] places the warning immediately before capital-defense appointments and Shan Bing's installation.

## Qualifications

- The source records omen reasoning as historical political reasoning, not as proof of celestial causation.
- Liu Yu's office, biography, and later fate are not established by this source note.
- The page uses `LiuYuAstrologerLateHan` to avoid collision with [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞 / Liu Yu (Late Han)]], whose chronology and role are different.

## What Changed

- The profile now separates Liu Yu's reform-cohort entrance from his later omen-to-security warning role.
- The page keeps the astrologer route distinct from Liu Yu / 刘虞 while accepting Hanji 884's transcript variant 刘渝 as the same crisis actor.
