# 曹德 / Cao De (Late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-971-luanshi-miyun-shui-sha-le-caocao-laodie-ljh3hdhssigmv78bueo-hprfqwku|Hanji 971]] introduces Cao De as [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s younger brother and a victim killed with [[CaoSong|曹嵩]] in the [[CaoSongMurder|曹嵩遇害]] branch. The episode uses his death to make the disaster a family catastrophe, not only the killing of Cao Cao's father.

The source also preserves a dramatic [[HouHanshu|《后汉书》]]-line detail that Cao De was holding his father's seal and that the seal later entered a Xuzhou black-market story. The wiki keeps that detail source-scoped because the episode uses it narratively, while the durable connection is Cao De's death alongside Cao Song and the revenge campaign that follows.

## Connections
- [[CaoSong|曹嵩]] - father killed in the same event.
- [[CaoCao|曹操]] - elder brother whose Xuzhou retaliation follows.
- [[CaoSongMurder|曹嵩遇害]] - event in which Cao De dies.
- [[HouHanshu|《后汉书》]] and [[TextualVariantPoliticalStakes|史书异文政治重量]] - source-variant context around the killing.
