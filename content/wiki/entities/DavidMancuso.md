## Overview

David Mancuso was a New York party host and curator presented as a foundational [[Disco]] figure through his 1970 Love Saves the Day party and recurring Loft gatherings.

## Current Profile

[[439-disco-sex-and-race-in-seventies-america-glt8206137792|The source]] describes Mancuso as a gay civil-rights and anti-Vietnam War activist who held a Valentine's Day 1970 party at 647 Broadway. It brought together people across race, sexuality, and class. The Loft developed into a members-oriented party built around sound quality, eclectic records, continuity, and an immersive crowd.

## Key Characteristics

- Hosted Love Saves the Day at 647 Broadway in 1970.
- Connected early disco to gay, civil-rights, and antiwar social worlds.
- Built the Loft as a recurring members-oriented party.
- Used eclectic records and continuous sound for collective dancing.
