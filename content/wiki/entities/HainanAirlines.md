# Hainan Airlines / 海南航空

## Overview
Hainan Airlines / 海南航空 is a Chinese airline used in the wiki as a peer case in the 2026 in-flight meal and passenger-service upgrade branch.

## Current Profile
Hainan Airlines is not analyzed as a standalone company in the current sources. Its role is to show that meal upgrades were framed as a broader Chinese airline pattern rather than an isolated move by one carrier.

The new source deepens the context around that pattern. Even when Hainan is not singled out for paid meal figures, the episode explains why the whole peer group might keep investing in meals: food is remembered, relatively controllable, and potentially tied to passenger loyalty even when fare and baggage allowance dominate booking choice.

## Key Characteristics
- Peer carrier in Chinese in-flight meal upgrade coverage.
- Group-level evidence for visible passenger-service investment under airline cost pressure.
- Example of an airline whose relevance is currently comparative rather than company-specific.
