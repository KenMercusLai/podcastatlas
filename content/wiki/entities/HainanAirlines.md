# Hainan Airlines / 海南航空

## Overview
Hainan Airlines / 海南航空 is a Chinese airline used in the wiki as a peer case in the 2026 in-flight meal and passenger-service upgrade branch.

## Current Profile
Hainan Airlines has two source-scoped roles. The 2026 coverage places it in a broader Chinese airline meal-upgrade pattern rather than analyzing standalone economics. A 2020 frequent-traveler account adds a more specific product case: a pass whose usefulness depended on Hainan's strong Beijing network but whose flight-level redemption quotas could constrain popular destinations even when ordinary tickets remained available.

## Key Characteristics
- Peer carrier in Chinese in-flight meal upgrade coverage.
- Group-level evidence for visible passenger-service investment under airline cost pressure.
- Historical flight-pass case where home-city network fit could coexist with tighter redemption inventory.
- Example of how passenger value depends on both nominal access and operational availability.

## Qualifications
The sources do not provide Hainan-specific meal costs, paid-meal launches, route-level performance, loyalty outcomes, or flight-pass unit economics. Pass rules and usage are historical observations from one traveler and should not be read as current terms.

- Expanded the profile from comparative meal service to a company-specific, historically bounded access product.
