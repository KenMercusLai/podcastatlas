## Overview

Mephistopheles / 梅菲斯特 is the demon who serves [[FaustusMarlowe|Faustus]] for twenty-four years under the bargain in [[DoctorFaustusMarlowe|Marlowe's tragedy]].

## Current Profile

The episode uses Mephistopheles to reverse the apparent master-servant relationship. Faustus believes magical skill compels a powerful servant, while the demon explains that Faustus's turn away from God made him a prospective soul for hell before any contract was signed.

Mephistopheles supplies travel, spectacle, illusion, and distraction but restricts questions about creation. His service therefore expands Faustus's immediate actions while helping narrow inquiry, delay repentance, and secure the contract's final transfer of control.

## Key Characteristics

- Apparent servant operating inside a larger structure of capture.
- Early warning voice about hell and lost freedom that Faustus discounts.
- Provider of capability whose answers remain selectively restricted.
- Producer of diversion and illusion rather than restored life or complete knowledge.
- Agent through whom temporary service becomes permanent dependency.

## Qualifications

The page represents the character through one episode's account of Marlowe's play, not the many later versions of Mephistopheles. The technology-dependency comparison is analogical and does not imply that present-day tools literally possess demonic intention.
