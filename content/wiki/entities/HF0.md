# HF0

## Overview
HF0 is an equity-investing residential startup program that combines a small founder cohort, shared living, operational support, and concentrated company execution.

## Current Profile
The current source set moves HF0 from an opaque hacker-house comparison into a participant-described operating model. The earlier documentary source places it in the equity-driven end of [[HackerHouseStartupInfrastructure]], reporting a roughly 12-week cycle and investment of up to about $1 million for company equity. [[GuoJingACEStudio|郭靖]] adds a ten-company cohort, extensive food and lifestyle support, one company-specific metric, and weekly Demo Dinners where progress is publicly compressed and questioned.

Its distinctive claim is [[StrategicFocusBySubtraction|strategic focus by subtraction]]. Comfort and services are used to remove attention switching, while peer proximity and public comparison increase pressure to move the current constraint. The model therefore joins hospitality, selection, capital, accountability, and temporary total immersion rather than treating hardship itself as the source of effort.

## Key Characteristics
- Runs small, time-bounded residential cohorts for startup teams.
- Combines capital and equity ownership with housing and operating support.
- Uses a single company-specific metric rather than forcing a common revenue measure.
- Makes progress visible through short weekly reports and peer questioning.
- Removes daily-life friction to concentrate attention on one current company objective.
- Relies on selection, peer comparison, and high-pressure immersion as motivational mechanisms.

## Qualifications
Terms varied across cohorts, and investment, selection, company growth, participant behavior, and program effects remain source-scoped. Public review and residential intensity can create useful accountability but may also increase stress, suppress candor, or exclude founders with caregiving, health, privacy, or accessibility needs. The sources do not provide comparative evidence that HF0 causes stronger outcomes than other accelerators.

## What Changed
- Added a participant account of HF0's single metric, Demo Dinner cadence, and daily-life support.
- Reframed the residence as an attention-management system rather than austere founder hardship.
- Added transfer evidence through ACE Studio's internal accelerator adaptation.
