# HF0

HF0 is the low-profile, equity-driven hacker-house example in [[tanfang-hacker-house-guigu-nianqingren-zhengzai-banjin-ai-chuangye-sushe-s10e10-f0870843-fd16-49f5-8f24-80281f9fff77]]. The source describes it as a top-tier but opaque program with a roughly 12-week cycle, no open public recruiting path, and access through friends-of-friends.

The episode says public information describes HF0 as offering up to roughly $1 million in investment in exchange for 5% equity. That makes it a clean comparison case for [[EquityHackerHouseModel]]: the house is not just a rental community but an attempt to underwrite and own a very early slice of founder-company upside.
