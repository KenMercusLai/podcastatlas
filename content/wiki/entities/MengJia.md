# 蒙嘉 / Meng Jia

蒙嘉 / Meng Jia enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-115-1-jingke-ciqin-sihu-you-dianer-caoshuai-le-lgz1adsn1t8jjiaobmtfedpr-ixn]] as the [[QinState|秦国]] court figure whom [[JingKe|荆轲]] and [[QinWuyang|秦武阳]] bribe after reaching [[Xianyang|咸阳]]. The episode calls him a favored official and glosses his 中庶子 role as part of the ruler-adjacent private staff.

His function is access brokerage. Meng Jia presents [[YanState|燕国]]'s claim that it fears [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]], will submit to real Qin administrative control, and asks only to preserve royal descent and ancestral sacrifices. The accompanying [[FanWuji|樊无忌]] head and [[DuKangMap|督亢地图]] make that claim concrete enough to win a formal palace audience.

Meng Jia is therefore not the designer of the assassination, but he is the human channel through which [[AssassinationAccessCredential|assassination access credentials]] become actionable. The episode makes him a late-Warring-States example of how ruler proximity, personal favor, and gift-mediated access can matter even inside a highly institutionalized Qin court.
