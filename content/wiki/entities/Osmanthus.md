# 桂花 / Osmanthus

桂花 / Osmanthus enters the wiki through [[ep263-xianxiang-jueqi-faxian-zhongshi-xiangqi-de-meili-lq1lsqcprqyr7auo5gslwyymuiv]] as a concrete case for [[ChineseFragrance|中式香气]]. The source says Western perfumers may treat osmanthus as a fruity floral raw material, while Chinese fragrance brands and consumers attach additional memory layers: autumn season, hometown, 桂花糕, 桂花酒酿, 满陇桂雨, and a quieter form of sweetness.

The point is not that osmanthus has an essence that only Chinese consumers can smell. The episode uses it to show how scent becomes culturally legible when a material connects to shared food, place, and seasonal memory.
