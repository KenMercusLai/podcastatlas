# American Independent Party

## Overview

The American Independent Party was George Wallace's vehicle for the 1968 presidential campaign.

## Current Profile

The party enabled Wallace to turn an Alabama segregationist base into a national third-party effort, but had to overcome state-by-state ballot-access rules. Its campaign drew on direct mail, conservative evangelical and anti-communist networks, small television stations, country-music events, and individual donors while remaining dependent on segregationist and far-right supporters.

## Key Characteristics

- Supplied the ballot line for Wallace's independent presidential bid.
- Built access state by state under varying signature requirements.
- Relied on distributed conservative networks, individual fundraising, and culturally marked rallies.
- Supported a regional Electoral College strategy aimed at leverage as well as victory.
