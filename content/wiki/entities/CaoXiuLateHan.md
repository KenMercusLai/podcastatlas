## Overview

[[CaoXiuLateHan|曹休]] is a Cao-side officer presented here as diagnosing Zhang Fei's conspicuous rear-cutting threat as a feint during the opening Hanzhong fighting.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1162 gives Cao Xiu a narrow information-analysis role. He reasons that a real covert movement against [[CaoHong|曹洪]]'s rear would normally be concealed, so [[ZhangFei|张飞]]'s loud public claim is evidence against literal intent. Cao Hong accepts the judgment and attacks the weaker Wu Lan wing.

## Key Characteristics

- Officer who tests a threat against the operational requirements of the claimed action.
- Adviser who converts signal interpretation into target selection.
- Successful analyst in this episode, without establishing an infallible general rule.

## Evidence

Intent inference:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1162-jinyi-zhisi-xiang-ying-jiu-bie-xinji-lpjdh6wohap6bqbghnvltu2oey5i|Hanji 1162]] attributes to Cao Xiu the argument that a genuine rear attack would be hidden rather than announced.

Operational consequence:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1162-jinyi-zhisi-xiang-ying-jiu-bie-xinji-lpjdh6wohap6bqbghnvltu2oey5i|Hanji 1162]] says Cao Hong accepts the inference, defeats Wu Lan, and helps force Zhang Fei and [[MaChao|马超]] to withdraw.

## Qualifications

The episode supplies one successful inference, not a universal deception test. A conspicuous threat can accompany real action, and the summary does not fully establish troop dispositions, alternative intelligence, or Cao Xiu's precise formal authority.

## What Changed

- Created Cao Xiu's bounded Hanzhong profile around signal-to-intent reasoning.
