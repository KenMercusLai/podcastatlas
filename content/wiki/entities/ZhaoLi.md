# 赵利 / Zhao Li

赵利 / Zhao Li enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-182-weishenme-shuo-chengye-xiaohe-baiye-xiaohe-1-lhe3h27wdbju3cgsvukdvgkri1a9|Hanji 182]] as a northern rebel figure linked to [[ChenXi|陈豨]], [[WangHuang|王黄]], [[ManqiuChen|曼丘臣]], and [[HanWangXin|韩王信]]. The episode names him among the forces that join the anti-Han pressure after Chen Xi declares himself Dai king.

This page distinguishes Zhao Li from other Zhao-named figures in the wiki. In this source, his importance is network position rather than biography: he helps show that [[ChenXiRebellion|陈豨之乱]] spreads through existing frontier and old-rebel channels rather than remaining a single official's local mutiny.
