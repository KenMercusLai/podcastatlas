# Vincent Chin / 陈果仁

## Overview
Vincent Chin / 陈果仁 is represented as a Chinese American man whose fatal attack after being mistaken for Japanese illustrates how anti-Asian hostility collapses nationality and citizenship into racialized appearance.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Chin's case as a historical bridge between economic or geopolitical hostility and violence against people inside the United States. His American birth and Chinese heritage did not protect him from anti-Japanese misidentification, making the case evidence for both [[GeopoliticalAntiAsianSpillover]] and the [[PerpetualForeignerStereotype]].

## Key Characteristics
- Chinese American victim of fatal anti-Asian violence.
- Misidentified as Japanese amid hostility associated with Japan.
- American citizenship and upbringing did not prevent racialized foreignness.
- Remembered as an example of attackers disregarding distinctions within Asian communities.
