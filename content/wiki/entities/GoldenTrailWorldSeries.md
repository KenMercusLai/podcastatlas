# Golden Trail World Series

## Overview
Golden Trail World Series / GTWS is presented as a Salomon-founded trail-running series that turns a niche sport into a repeatable competition, media, athlete, and community system.

## Current Profile
[[vol-274-chengwei-saluomeng-1013268300]] uses GTWS to show why a specialist sports brand may operate beyond equipment sales. Race organization supplies visible competition, athlete narratives, technical education, community participation, and media-ready events; the episode says GTWS has become prominent enough to appear in mainstream European sports coverage.

## Key Characteristics
- Packages separate trail races into a recognizable series and media property.
- Connects brand sponsorship with athlete and community development.
- Makes event operation part of category creation rather than only promotion.

## Evidence
- **Series ownership:** [[vol-274-chengwei-saluomeng-1013268300]] identifies Salomon as the initiator of GTWS.
- **Media reach:** [[vol-274-chengwei-saluomeng-1013268300]] says the series has entered mainstream European sports-media coverage.
- **Ecosystem role:** [[vol-274-chengwei-saluomeng-1013268300]] places GTWS alongside product development, athlete sponsorship, community cultivation, and technical education.

## Qualifications
The source does not provide governance detail, audience measurement, sponsorship economics, or an independent comparison with other trail-running series. Media prominence and commercial effects remain source-scoped.
