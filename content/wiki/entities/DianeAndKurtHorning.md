# Diane and Kurt Horning

## Overview

Diane and Kurt Horning are the parents of Matthew Horning, a 26-year-old database administrator killed on the North Tower's 95th floor on September 11, 2001. Their advocacy centers on the possibility that material connected to Matthew and other victims remains within the fines at Fresh Kills.

## Current Profile

The Hornings received and buried several small fragments identified as Matthew's, and some of his possessions were recovered at Fresh Kills. Those recoveries did not resolve their concern: visible objects during visits, later discoveries of missed remains around Ground Zero, and the physical limits of quarter-inch screening made the official endpoint feel evidentially and morally incomplete.

Through [[WTCFamiliesForAProperBurial]], they sought relocation of the fines to Ground Zero, Governors Island, or at least a site not above garbage. Their position makes family agency central: an official's personal willingness to donate a body to science could not decide what treatment other families considered respectful.

## Key Characteristics

- Parents pursuing a dignified disposition for possible remains of their son and other victims.
- Founding advocates associated with [[WTCFamiliesForAProperBurial]].
- Treat recovered possessions and fragments as evidence of a continuing personal relationship to Fresh Kills.
- Distinguish scientific uncertainty from the obligation to care for possible human remains.
- Reject a memorial at Fresh Kills while the disputed material remains there.
