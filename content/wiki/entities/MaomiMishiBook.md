# 《猫咪秘史：从史前时期到太空时代》

[[MaomiMishiBook|《猫咪秘史：从史前时期到太空时代》]] is the book discussed in [[34-maomi-mishi-he-yuchun-you-keai-de-renlei-601590607]]. The episode says the book uses a playful cat-author frame, with "小猫芭芭" and a human assistant, to shift cultural history away from a purely human-centered account.

The book functions in the episode less as a single verified chronology than as a cat-centered narrative device. It lets [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] move from [[CatDomesticationHistory]] and ancient Egyptian worship through Asian soul beliefs, European demonization, ship cats, war cats, and [[Felicette]] in the space age.

## Key Claims
- The book's cat-centered voice is the source of the episode's comic reversal: humans become the dependent, confused, and occasionally cruel species under observation.
- Its historical span makes [[Cat|猫]] a continuity device across agriculture, religion, literature, trade, war, and modern science.
- The episode's use of the book needs source-scoping because many of its most memorable stories are legends or anecdotal cultural memory.
