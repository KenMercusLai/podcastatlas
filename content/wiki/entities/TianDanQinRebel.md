# 田儋 / Tian Dan (Qin Rebel)

田儋 / Tian Dan (Qin Rebel) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-6-dingji-tianshilun-touziren-lvgong-lhqqja27zxdiypgf6wuv1abgkw3j|Qinji 128-6]] as one of the parallel rebel leaders who rise after [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] and [[WuGuang|吴广]]. The source says Tian Dan raises troops in old [[QiState|齐国]] territory during the same broader wave that includes [[LiuBang|刘邦]] in [[PeiCounty|沛县]] and [[XiangLiang|项梁]] in Wu County.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-8-yi-ming-zhaoguo-chuishibing-nixi-chengwei-waijiao-dachen-lhfwk-hjge5iuqet6astmbclhyri|Qinji 128-8]] gives that earlier notice its story. Tian Dan is in Di county with kinsmen Tian Rong and Tian Heng when [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]] forces arrive outside the city. He uses a staged request about killing a household slave to bring the county magistrate out, kills him, gathers local youth, and claims Qi royal identity as the basis for becoming king.

This page is deliberately disambiguated from [[TianDan|田单 / Tian Dan]], the Warring States Qi restoration general. The two names share pinyin in common transcription, but the source's 田儋 belongs to the Qin-collapse rebellion context, not the earlier Jimo restoration branch.
