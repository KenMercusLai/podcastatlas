# 田儋 / Tian Dan (Qin Rebel)

田儋 / Tian Dan (Qin Rebel) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-6-dingji-tianshilun-touziren-lvgong-lhqqja27zxdiypgf6wuv1abgkw3j|Qinji 128-6]] as one of the parallel rebel leaders who rise after [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] and [[WuGuang|吴广]]. The source says Tian Dan raises troops in old [[QiState|齐国]] territory during the same broader wave that includes [[LiuBang|刘邦]] in [[PeiCounty|沛县]] and [[XiangLiang|项梁]] in Wu County.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-8-yi-ming-zhaoguo-chuishibing-nixi-chengwei-waijiao-dachen-lhfwk-hjge5iuqet6astmbclhyri|Qinji 128-8]] gives that earlier notice its story. Tian Dan is in Di county with kinsmen Tian Rong and Tian Heng when [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]] forces arrive outside the city. He uses a staged request about killing a household slave to bring the county magistrate out, kills him, gathers local youth, and claims Qi royal identity as the basis for becoming king.

This page is deliberately disambiguated from [[TianDan|田单 / Tian Dan]], the Warring States Qi restoration general. The two names share pinyin in common transcription, but the source's 田儋 belongs to the Qin-collapse rebellion context, not the earlier Jimo restoration branch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-4-jiuquangui-zhangliang-ruhe-zai-liubang-shouxia-anshen-lp6qwg3ma5hdy15g4box7pe23jav|Qinji 129-4]] adds Tian Dan's failed coordination with the Chu-side [[JingJu|景居 / 景驹]] branch. [[QinJiaRebel|秦嘉]] sends [[GongsunQing|公孙庆]] to ask Qi to join an attack on Qin, but Tian Dan asks why Zhang Chu installed Jing Ju without first consulting Qi. When Gongsun Qing answers that Qi also proclaimed a king without consulting Zhang Chu, Tian Dan kills him.

The episode therefore sharpens Tian Dan's role inside [[AntiQinCoalitionFragmentation|反秦阵营诸侯化分裂]]. Qi restoration is anti-Qin in origin, but Tian Dan still defends Qi's status and authorization claims against another anti-Qin claimant.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-3-weihe-li-mutong-wei-chuwang-limian-dayou-wenzhang-lmcuvrxer-cou6gmlppqwhb3n4vz|Qinji 130-3]] gives Tian Dan's endpoint in this rebellion sequence. When [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] besieges [[WeiJiu|魏咎]] at Linji, Tian Dan leads Qi aid while [[XiangLiang|项梁]]'s Chu-side force also comes to help. Zhang Han defeats the relief army by surprise and kills Tian Dan. His death creates the Qi succession opening in which officials install [[TianJia|田假]], [[TianRong|田荣]] returns to contest the decision, and Qi later refuses Xiang Liang's anti-Qin coalition request.
