# 吴夫人 / Lady Wu (Sun Jian)

吴夫人 / Lady Wu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-982-ba-yi-ba-liubei-ru-xu-beihou-de-liyi-zhi-zheng-ljajdc95xtylhkn5teefz26yyyz1|Hanji 982]] as [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]'s wife from the [[QiantangWuClan|钱塘吴氏]]. The source names her as mother of [[SunCe|孙策]], [[SunQuan|孙权]], [[SunYiLateHan|孙翊 / Sun Yi]], Sun Kuang, and a daughter, then treats the marriage as a political resource connection rather than only a family notice.

The episode's core claim is that Lady Wu's family gives the Sun household access to Jiangdong gentry, Qiantang water-route knowledge, salt-and-iron trade networks, and [[DanyangCommandery|丹阳郡]] military resources through [[WuJingLateHan|吴景]]. This makes her page a source-scoped part of [[KinshipResourceStateBuilding|姻亲资源建构]]: family ties help turn a寒门 military household into a more durable regional project.
