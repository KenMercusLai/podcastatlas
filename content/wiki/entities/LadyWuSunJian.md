# 吴夫人 / Lady Wu (Sun Jian)

## Overview
吴夫人 / Lady Wu is Sun Jian's Qiantang Wu-clan wife and a Sun-family political actor whose current wiki role now spans resource linkage and direct restraint of [[SunCe|孙策]]'s severity.

## Current Profile
The evidence no longer treats Lady Wu only as a marriage network. Hanji 982 makes her the bridge into Jiangdong gentry and military resources; Hanji 1039 shows her as an emergency moral and political counsellor who saves [[WeiTengLateHan|魏腾]] and warns Sun Ce that killing frank local staff will alienate talent.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects the Sun household to [[QiantangWuClan|钱塘吴氏]] prestige, routes, trade resources, and Jiangdong society.
- Anchors Sun-family succession and household continuity after [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]'s death.
- Receives and legitimates early support from [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] in Sun Ce's base-building phase.
- Uses personal authority and a threatened well-side suicide to stop Sun Ce from killing Wei Teng.
- Frames talent retention and popular attachment as practical constraints on Sun Ce's punishment power.

## Evidence
- Resource bridge: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-982-ba-yi-ba-liubei-ru-xu-beihou-de-liyi-zhi-zheng-ljajdc95xtylhkn5teefz26yyyz1|Hanji 982]] links Lady Wu to Qiantang Wu-clan resources, Jiangdong gentry, water routes, and Danyang military access through Wu Jing.
- Household continuity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-982-ba-yi-ba-liubei-ru-xu-beihou-de-liyi-zhi-zheng-ljajdc95xtylhkn5teefz26yyyz1|Hanji 982]] names her as mother of Sun Ce, Sun Quan, Sun Yi, and other Sun-family children.
- Zhou Yu connection: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-982-ba-yi-ba-liubei-ru-xu-beihou-de-liyi-zhi-zheng-ljajdc95xtylhkn5teefz26yyyz1|Hanji 982]] says Zhou Yu visits and honors Lady Wu during Sun Ce's early support-network formation.
- Restraint of severity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] has Lady Wu stop Sun Ce from executing Wei Teng and warn that Jiangnan is not yet stable.
- Talent-retention logic: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] makes her intervention a lesson about preserving people willing to speak frankly.

## Qualifications
The page is based on episodes that use Lady Wu selectively. It supports her importance as resource bridge and counsellor, but it does not yet supply a continuous biography or independent account of her household governance.
