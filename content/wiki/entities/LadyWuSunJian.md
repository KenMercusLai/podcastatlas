# 吴夫人 / Lady Wu (Sun Jian)

## Overview
吴夫人 / Lady Wu is Sun Jian's Qiantang Wu-clan wife and a Sun-family political actor whose current wiki role spans resource linkage, restraint of [[SunCe|孙策]]'s severity, and direct succession-stabilizing action after Sun Ce's death.

## Current Profile
The evidence no longer treats Lady Wu only as a marriage network. Hanji 982 makes her the bridge into Jiangdong gentry and military resources; Hanji 1039 shows her as an emergency moral and political counsellor who saves [[WeiTengLateHan|魏腾]] and warns Sun Ce that killing frank local staff will alienate talent.

Hanji 1052 adds her succession role. After Sun Ce dies and [[SunQuan|孙权]] succeeds while still under 19, Lady Wu entrusts [[ZhangHongLateHan|张纮]] and [[ZhangZhaoLateHan|张昭]] with assisting him and asks [[DongXiLateHan|董袭]] whether Jiangdong can hold. The episode therefore makes her a visible stabilizer of the household and adviser network at the moment when private grief could become political collapse.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects the Sun household to [[QiantangWuClan|钱塘吴氏]] prestige, routes, trade resources, and Jiangdong society.
- Anchors Sun-family succession and household continuity after [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]'s death.
- Receives and legitimates early support from [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] in Sun Ce's base-building phase.
- Uses personal authority and a threatened well-side suicide to stop Sun Ce from killing Wei Teng.
- Frames talent retention and popular attachment as practical constraints on Sun Ce's punishment power.
- After Sun Ce's death, entrusts Zhang Hong and Zhang Zhao with assisting young Sun Quan and seeks Dong Xi's military confidence assessment.

## Qualifications
The page is based on episodes that use Lady Wu selectively. It supports her importance as resource bridge, counsellor, and succession stabilizer, but it does not yet supply a continuous biography or independent account of her household governance.
