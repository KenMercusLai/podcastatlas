# Greg Marshall

## Overview

Greg Marshall is the marine biologist credited by Episode 230 with developing Crittercam, an animal-borne camera system intended to move observation with an animal into its own environment.

## Current Profile

The episode says Marshall drew inspiration from a remora attached to a shark. Crittercam changes nature imaging by relocating the viewpoint: rather than waiting for an animal to enter a human-framed scene, the camera travels with the subject into spaces and behaviors the observer cannot directly follow.

## Key Characteristics

- Marine biologist associated with Crittercam.
- Used the remora-shark relation as the source's design inspiration.
- Places cameras on animals to record moving, animal-centered viewpoints.
- Exemplifies imaging technology aimed at discovery rather than only image polish.
