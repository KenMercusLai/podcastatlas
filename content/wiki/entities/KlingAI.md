# Kling AI / 可灵 AI

Kling AI, called 可灵 AI in the source, is [[Kuaishou]]'s AI video product in [[kafeidou-chuantong-meishi-guangchang-jielian-bidian-dashidaimen-yudao-naxie-fazhan-zuai-1007530222]]. The episode says its revenue grew more than 200%, making it a visible commercialization signal for [[VideoModels|video models]].

The same source treats the signal cautiously. Model training and R&D spending rose enough to weigh on Kuaishou's profitability, while [[ByteDance]] and [[MiniMax]] were also releasing new video-generation models. Kling therefore represents both a revenue lane and an [[AIInferenceCostStructure|inference and training cost]] pressure point.
