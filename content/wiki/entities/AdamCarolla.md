[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-adam-carolla-on-californias-collapse-fires-failed-leadership-and-gyno-fascism-39710360]] adds [[AdamCarolla|Adam Carolla]] as a [[Malibu]] and [[LosAngeles|Los Angeles]]-based commentator using personal experience with construction, owner-building, evacuation, and city permitting to criticize [[California]] governance after the Palisades fire.

His role in the source is not neutral policy analysis. He supplies an anti-regulatory and anti-DEI worldview in which rebuilding delay, school closures, media distrust, Hollywood hiring, wealth taxes, and anti-tech politics share a common pattern: institutions claim protection or fairness while weakening speed, merit, ownership, and responsibility.
