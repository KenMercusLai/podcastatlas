# Letterboxd

## Overview
Letterboxd is a film-interest community where users rate films, publish reviews, and organize watchlists.

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents Letterboxd as a 2011-founded platform with roughly 30 million users and as a potential acquisition target for the [[NewYorkTimes|New York Times]]. Its strategic value in that report is not only film content: a participatory interest graph, recurring user activity, and community identity could add a specialized entry point to a broader subscription bundle.

The reported discussion was not final. The source does not establish Letterboxd's revenue, paying-user share, retention, moderation quality, data portability, acquisition terms, or whether its community would accept integration into a larger media portfolio.

## Key Characteristics
- Organizes film discovery around user ratings, reviews, lists, and social participation.
- Combines a specialized interest community with behavioral and preference data.
- Is reported as having about 30 million users, a source-scoped figure.
- Could serve as a non-news subscription and engagement surface for a larger media owner.

## Qualifications
All transaction, valuation, and user figures come from one short news roundup. A large registered-user base does not establish active use, monetization, community health, or subscription conversion, and ownership integration could weaken the culture that makes the platform valuable.
