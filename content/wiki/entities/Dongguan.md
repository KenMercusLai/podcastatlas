# Dongguan / 东莞

Dongguan / 东莞 appears in [[ruci-dagong-30-nian-liushuixian-shang-de-miankong-yu-shijian-lodsuktwtcfxnhmzwum5iesbzbme]] as the city setting for [[ZhanTeacher|占老师]]'s long worker-photography practice and for the episode's account of [[PearlRiverDeltaManufacturingTransformation|珠三角制造业变迁]]. The source treats Dongguan less as a generic city and more as an industrial landscape where factories, dormitories, canteens, roads, suppliers, and migrant-worker services organize daily life.

The episode's Dongguan branch connects early reform-era capital, export orders, global demand shocks, [[FlexibleManufacturing|flexible manufacturing]], automation, and small-factory specialization to the lived time of workers. It also gives the wiki a labor-side complement to other Dongguan references such as [[ChinaSpallationNeutronSource]], where the city appears through large-science infrastructure.

## Key Claims
- Dongguan's manufacturing role is made legible through worker routines, not only through company or macro data.
- The source connects Dongguan to [[TayloristFactoryTime|factory time]], [[ManufacturingJobQuality]], and [[MigrantWorkerSocialInsuranceLegacy]].
- [[ChanganTown|长安镇]] functions as the source's more specific industrial district inside the Dongguan story.
