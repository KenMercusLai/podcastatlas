# Wolf Warrior 2 / 战狼2

## Overview
Wolf Warrior 2 / 《战狼2》 appears here as a comparison film for [[WelcomeToLailongRestaurant|《欢迎来龙餐馆》]] and the question of Chinese cinema in overseas conflict settings.

## Current Profile
The source uses 《战狼2》 as a contrast case rather than as its main object. In the episode's account, many earlier Chinese overseas-action films place Chinese protagonists in foreign settings, but the foreign political world often remains a background for action, rescue, and national self-display. [[WelcomeToLailongRestaurant|《欢迎来龙餐馆》]] is said to differ because it tries to judge the other society's war and ideology, even if the source finds that attempt incomplete.

The comparison also clarifies [[WenMuye|文牧野]]'s perceived ambition. The host says Wen does not appear to want a simple 《战狼2》-style good-versus-evil confrontation; the film instead tries a universal-humanitarian route centered on ordinary people, food, children, and survival.

## Key Characteristics
