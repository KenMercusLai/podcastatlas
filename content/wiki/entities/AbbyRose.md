# Abby Rose

Abby Rose is the online custom phone-case business founded by [[AbbyRosalier]] and discussed in [[advice-line-with-bobbi-brown-of-jones-road-beauty-september-2025-427590c2-11bc-43fa-9c92-d1eccf05842a]]. The source says the business has more than $2 million in total sales, grew 50% in the prior year, is family-run, and depends heavily on TikTok and Instagram audience engagement.

The episode frames Abby Rose as a [[FounderLedLiveSelling]] case. Customers want to see orders made live and interact with Rosalier, which creates strong community and conversion but also slows sales when she is not online. The advice is to test new faces, user-generated design contests, collaborations, YouTube, Substack, and giveaways before committing to expensive wholesale or paid advertising.
