# Doubao Work / 豆包工作

## Overview

Doubao Work / 豆包工作 is the ByteDance AI-office product named in [[tengxun-ali-zijie-zhengduo-dagongren-hulianwang-dachang-weihe-jiti-jiama-ai-bangong-1008598460]] as part of the 2026 Chinese big-tech move from consumer chatbot promotion toward workplace productivity.

## Current Profile

The source positions Doubao Work inside ByteDance's broader pivot from [[Doubao]] consumer-assistant scale to enterprise and office workflows. It is mentioned alongside [[Feishu]] and [[Volcengine]] as ByteDance tries to connect model capability, work surfaces, customer sales, and cloud or token consumption instead of relying only on C-end DAU.

Doubao Work is not yet treated in the wiki as identical to [[DoubaoEnterpriseEdition|Doubao enterprise edition]]. The earlier enterprise-edition source describes AI sold through a Feishu-like carrier, while this source names Doubao Work as a product in the newer AI-office push. The relationship should stay source-scoped until a source gives a product lineage.

## Key Characteristics

- Part of ByteDance's move from consumer chatbot traffic toward office and enterprise workflows.
- Depends on [[Feishu]]-style workplace context if it is to act on company documents, permissions, and collaboration history.
- Sits inside the same commercial pressure that affects [[Doubao]]: heavy token and compute use needs higher-value payment or enterprise demand.
- Competes in the same AI-office field as [[DingTalk]], [[Qwen]], and [[TencentWorkBuddy]].
- Its product identity relative to [[DoubaoEnterpriseEdition|Doubao enterprise edition]] remains unresolved in the wiki.

- **ByteDance office pivot:** [[tengxun-ali-zijie-zhengduo-dagongren-hulianwang-dachang-weihe-jiti-jiama-ai-bangong-1008598460]] names Doubao Work in the episode opening as ByteDance's AI-office product and later ties ByteDance's AI resource allocation back toward enterprise services.
