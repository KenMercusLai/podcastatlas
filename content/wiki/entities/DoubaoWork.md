# Doubao Work / 豆包工作

## Overview

Doubao Work / 豆包工作 is ByteDance's AI-office product line in the Chinese office-agent competition. [[tengxun-ali-zijie-zhengduo-dagongren-hulianwang-dachang-weihe-jiti-jiama-ai-bangong-1008598460]] names it inside the 2026 big-tech move from consumer chatbot promotion toward workplace productivity, while [[272-cong-feishu-jizuo-dao-agent-youxian-doubao-gongzuo-all-in-one-jinzhui-workbuddy-lqmfcnfkkhoxt440qy26vwpnvswp]] treats it as ByteDance's attempt to connect [[Doubao]], [[Feishu]], [[Trae]], [[Coze|扣子]], and Flow-era agent work into one visible work product.

## Current Profile

The page now treats Doubao Work as more than a source-scoped product name. The newer source gives it a concrete product and organization role: it is the work-facing layer where ByteDance tries to turn consumer model scale, Feishu collaboration context, developer-agent tooling, and agent workflows into a paid office product.

Its strongest advantage is context, not a generic skill list. Feishu already holds documents, members, meetings, permissions, web documents, and collaboration history, so Doubao Work can potentially act with richer first-party context than a standalone chatbot. The same source also keeps the product in a transitional state: entrances, pricing, model choice, personal and enterprise packaging, and multi-agent harness behavior are still uneven.

## Key Characteristics

- ByteDance AI-office product for work tasks, files, meetings, documents, and enterprise context.
- Connects the [[Doubao]] brand with [[Feishu]] workplace context and ByteDance's developer-agent products.
- Depends on [[DoubaoWorkPartner]] as a Feishu-to-Doubao Work bridge in the newest source.
- Competes against [[TencentWorkBuddy|Tencent WorkBuddy]] and [[QwenOffice|Qwen Office]] as visible office-agent features converge.
- Exposes harness-design questions around skills, tool loading, context compression, multi-agent delegation, and result review.
- Carries ByteDance's broader monetization pressure to move model use from high-cost consumer traffic toward work payment.

## Qualifications
