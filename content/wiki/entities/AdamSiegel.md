# Adam Siegel

Adam Siegel is the [[CouncilOnForeignRelations|Council on Foreign Relations]] expert interviewed in [[tech-20260804-0803-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260804-0803-mp-tech-pod-128]] about Chinese open-weight AI models and U.S. national-security concerns. His analysis separates Chinese companies' market incentives from later state messaging: open weights began as a competitive response to proprietary U.S. models, then fit China's global accessibility narrative.

Siegel's contribution to the wiki is a risk tradeoff around [[ChineseOpenWeightAIStrategy]]. He names [[AIModelCensorship]], data access, espionage, dependence, and coercion as U.S. concerns, but also argues that downloaded open-weight models can reduce some server-side data and cutoff risks because users can run and adapt them locally.
