# Sony Pictures

## Overview

Sony Pictures is a film and media company represented in the wiki primarily through the [[SonyPicturesHack2014|2014 hack]] and the governance decisions surrounding [[TheInterviewFilm|The Interview]].

## Current Profile

[[the-sony-hack-the-mistakes-series-the-sony-hack-the-mistakes-series]] describes Sony Pictures as a creative organization with a formal greenlight process intended to join artistic enthusiasm to finance, legal, marketing, public-policy, audience, and parent-company review. [[MichaelLynton]] says this process ordinarily contained failures within tolerable bounds, but he bypassed it when approving *The Interview*. The source treats that exception as a missed opportunity to identify geopolitical exposure before commitment.

The later breach produced both operational disruption and irreversible disclosure. [[dang-heike-gongpo-le-riben-de-guomin-pijiu-chule-jugong-daoqian-tamen-hai-neng-zuo-shenme-feat-top-of-japan-keji-luandun]] uses Sony Pictures as a contrast case for [[AsahiGroup]]: backups may restore systems, but leaked films, employee information, and private communications cannot be made confidential again. Together the sources distinguish business continuity, information confidentiality, governance review, and crisis distribution as separate risk domains.

## Key Characteristics

- Creative studio using cross-functional greenlight review to test proposed films.
- Subsidiary whose Japanese parent-company context mattered to North Korea–related political risk.
- Target of a destructive attack and mass disclosure in 2014.
- Case where system recovery could not reverse publication of confidential material.
- Organization whose post-attack release options depended on technology partners and independent cinemas after major chains withdrew.
