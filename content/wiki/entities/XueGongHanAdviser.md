# 薛公 / Xue Gong (Han adviser)

薛公 / Xue Gong (Han adviser) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-187-yingbu-panluan-liubang-weihe-tuobingti-qinzheng-lvjafotlnvmyqmwaeav3novz1dox|Hanji 187]] as the former Chu chancellor/adviser whom [[XiahouYing|夏侯婴]] consults after [[YingBu|英布 / 黥布]] rebels. This page is intentionally distinct from [[XueGong|薛公 / Xue Gong]], the Warring States Zhao worthy connected to [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]].

Xue Gong's contribution is diagnostic. He tells Xiahou Ying that Ying Bu's rebellion is predictable because [[HanXin|韩信]], [[PengYue|彭越]], and Ying Bu had comparable merit, while the first two are already dead. The answer turns the revolt into a structural reaction inside [[DifferentSurnameKingSuspicion|异姓诸侯王猜忌]] rather than an isolated personality event.

When [[LiuBang|刘邦]] summons him, Xue Gong maps upper, middle, and lower strategies for Ying Bu. The upper path would seize 吴郡, old Chu territory, Qi and Lu, then call Yan and Zhao; the middle path would combine eastern and western expansion with [[Aocang|敖仓]] and [[Chenggao|成皋]] control; the lower path protects supply and seeks [[ChangshaKingdom|长沙国]] support without creating a broader anti-Han field.

Xue Gong predicts the lower path because he reads Ying Bu as a former [[Lishan|骊山]] convict who has reached kingship but still thinks in short-range self-preservation terms. Liu Bang accepts the forecast, rewards Xue Gong with a thousand households, abolishes Ying Bu's title, and appoints [[LiuChangHuainanKing|刘长]] to replace him in [[HuainanKingdomEarlyHan|淮南国]].
