# Emmerson Mnangagwa

## Overview

Emmerson Mnangagwa is Zimbabwe's president in the source's account of attempts to repair the title and compensation legacy of fast-track land reform.

## Current Profile

After taking power in 2017, Mnangagwa's government reached a nominal $3.5 billion compensation agreement with former farm owners and began a new program to issue agricultural title deeds. The episode presents both as potentially constructive but incomplete: most compensation is reportedly unpaid, and banks remain unsure whether new deeds can provide reliable collateral while earlier claims remain unresolved.

## Key Characteristics

- Inherited the unresolved legal and financial aftermath of fast-track land reform.
- Reached a large nominal compensation agreement with former farm owners.
- Supports a new agricultural deed-issuance program.
- Has not yet resolved banks' doubts about claim priority and collateral value.

## Evidence

- Compensation initiative: [[disorder-of-magnitude-frances-riots-454c603849c78e6a57bfa1c8bf33de7c]] reports a $3.5 billion agreement with farm-owner representatives after 2017.
- Implementation gap: [[disorder-of-magnitude-frances-riots-454c603849c78e6a57bfa1c8bf33de7c]] says most agreed compensation remains unpaid.
- Title initiative and banking doubt: [[disorder-of-magnitude-frances-riots-454c603849c78e6a57bfa1c8bf33de7c]] describes new deeds alongside continuing lender concern about former-owner claims.

## Qualifications

The agreement value, payment status, deed program, lender response, and expected effects are source-reported and time-bounded. Formal issuance does not establish clear priority, enforceability, liquidity, or actual credit access.
