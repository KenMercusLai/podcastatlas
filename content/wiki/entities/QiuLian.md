# 邱连 / Qiu Lian

[[ep263-xianxiang-jueqi-faxian-zhongshi-xiangqi-de-meili-lq1lsqcprqyr7auo5gslwyymuiv]] adds Qiu Lian's fragrance and incense-reporting role. She explains how [[LineIncenseDailyLife|线香日常化]] differs from earlier high-threshold 香席 practice, and how [[IncenseFormulaReconstruction|古方香复刻]] and named scents such as "鹅梨帐中香" help contemporary consumers imagine [[ChineseFragrance|中式香气]].

[[ep260-yikou-feimei-zhifang-cangzhe-zhongguo-yangzhi-de-xin-gushi-linjq2fkzhqvzjcgrmhudq12vs4j]] extends Qiu Lian's [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] food-reporting role from restaurant creativity into farmed high-value ingredients. The episode places her in a conversation about [[ChineseNewSpecialtyAquaculture|中国新特产养殖]], where "new specialty" means production, labeling, processing, and consumption formats rather than only a new dish trend.

邱连 / Qiu Lian is a [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] food-issue speaker in [[ep245-yinian-yidu-xunwei-zhilv-jinnian-qu-chi-chuangyi-cai-li9wtavemokcckialbbj7unpmqsz]]. Her main source role is to explain [[GuizhouCuisineFlavorTransfer|贵州酸辣风味迁移]] and the restaurant/drink side of [[ChineseBistroLocalization|中式小酒馆本土化]].

The episode uses her reporting on [[Guizhou|贵州]] to show why acid, chili, fermentation, 木姜子, red sour soup, rice wine, and restaurant cases such as [[OhaGuizhouBistro|欧哈]] can move through contemporary Chinese dining. She also links the bistro trend to [[LocalAlcoholPairing|地方酒饮餐配]], especially rice-wine tasting sets and dishes designed around local grain, microbes, and seasoning.
