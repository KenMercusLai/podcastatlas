## Overview

马丁 is the local guide who traveled with [[QilinPhotographer|麒麟]] and [[QipeishiCamel|齐佩石]] during the Kenya journey recorded in [[CamelDiary|《骆驼日记》]].

## Current Profile

The source makes Martin's expertise operational rather than decorative. He reads routes, water and food availability, plants, insects, conflict, camps, and community contact, allowing the expedition to adapt across changing terrain and social conditions. His presence is central evidence that an apparently individual adventure rests on distributed and local knowledge.

## Key Characteristics

- Provides route-finding and environmental knowledge.
- Mediates communication with residents along the route.
- Helps distinguish safe plants, camps, water, and threat conditions.
- Shares the daily physical labor and uncertainty of the journey.
- Represents local expertise that a visitor's equipment cannot replace.
