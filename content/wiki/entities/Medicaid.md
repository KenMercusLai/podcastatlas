# Medicaid

Medicaid appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-howard-lutnick-how-america-can-hit-6-gdp-growth-in-2026-39668255]] as both a drug-access program and a fraud-detection target. [[HowardLutnick|Howard Lutnick]] says [[Ozempic]] and [[Mounjaro]] would be available through Medicaid and [[Medicare]] at $149, and later says federal benefit programs should be compared against income to identify fraud.

The source makes Medicaid part of a broader fiscal-administration story: drug prices, welfare-state immigration costs, and benefit-integrity checks are all framed as ways to change government spending outcomes. The page keeps those claims source-attributed rather than treating the savings or eligibility effects as verified facts.
