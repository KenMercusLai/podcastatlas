# Anna Trapnel

## Overview

Anna Trapnel was a Fifth Monarchist visionary whose trances and political prophecies made her a public religious voice and later an opponent of [[OliverCromwell]].

## Current Profile

The daughter of an East London shipwright, Trapnel gained attention through visions interpreted as anticipating political events, including Cromwell's expulsion of MPs. Anti-Cromwell parliamentarians later brought her to Cornwall, where authorities treated her performances as disruptive.

Imprisonment in Bridewell sharpened her political judgment that Cromwell's regime was corrupt and doomed. Her trajectory shows visionary religion becoming a vehicle of public opposition when institutional channels were unstable and contested.

## Key Characteristics

- Fifth Monarchist visionary from a non-elite London background.
- Known for trances interpreted as politically predictive.
- Used by anti-Cromwell MPs as an oppositional public voice.
- Became more explicitly hostile to the regime after imprisonment.
