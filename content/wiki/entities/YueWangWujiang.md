# 越王无疆 / Yue Wang Wujiang

越王无疆 / Yue Wang Wujiang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-28-hanzhaohou-de-kuaile-ni-xiangxiang-budao-ln2tbxniz4kvwogbwp32exmdokz8]] as the [[YueState|越国]] ruler who attacks [[QiState|齐国]] in the 334 BCE cluster. The episode says Qi diplomacy redirects him and that the resulting sequence ends badly for Yue.

The source is careful about chronology. It says [[ChuState|楚国]] does defeat Yue in this period, but Yue still has strength afterward and is not finally destroyed until more than twenty years later. Wujiang's page therefore marks the beginning of a decline branch rather than a single-event collapse.
