# Richard Titmuss / 蒂特马斯

Richard Titmuss appears in [[79-gewei-lingdao-danfan-zan-xuedian-boyilun-690004262]] through the episode's blood-donation example. The source uses his work to explain why monetary compensation can sometimes reduce participation or quality when it replaces civic, moral, or identity-based reasons for action.

His wiki role is the motivation boundary inside incentive design: not every desired behavior improves when priced, and a low external reward can make a previously meaningful action feel cheap.
