# Tomorrow Today

## Overview
Tomorrow Today is a podcast hosted in the current sources by [[ShekharNatarajan]]. Its represented episodes pair long-form biography with questions about business identity, resilience, spirituality, public institutions, technology, leadership, and human meaning.

## Current Profile
The wiki knows Tomorrow Today through four interviews. [[AndyStalman]] connects branding to whole-company identity, values, and AI. [[AraceliSegarra]] connects climbing and Everest to fear, team trust, resilience, and AI-era judgment. [[ShyalpaRinpoche]] connects Tibetan Buddhist practice to peace, meaning, and compassionate AI. [[NataliaOlson]] connects planning, logistics, zoning, federal service, and open government to institutional innovation, policy leverage, shared infrastructure, and global AI governance.
