# Tomorrow Today

## Overview
Tomorrow Today is a podcast hosted in the current sources by [[ShekharNatarajan]]. Its represented episodes pair long-form biography with questions about business identity, resilience, spirituality, survival, public institutions, creative careers, inequality, technology, leadership, and human meaning.

## Current Profile
The wiki knows Tomorrow Today through seven interviews. [[AndyStalman]] connects branding to whole-company identity, values, and AI. [[AraceliSegarra]] connects climbing and Everest to fear, team trust, resilience, and AI-era judgment. [[ShyalpaRinpoche]] connects Tibetan Buddhist practice to peace, meaning, and compassionate AI. [[NataliaOlson]] connects planning, logistics, government, and open data to institutional innovation and AI governance. [[AdahSharma]] connects acting, typecasting, controversial visibility, stress, and rejection to AI's expansion of creative access and threat to livelihoods. [[ChetanBhagat]] connects family adversity, education, writing, platform inequality, AI safety, cognition, and the search for meaning after fame. [[AlvaroVizcaino|Álvaro Vizcaíno]] adds an acute survival account that distinguishes accepting uncontrollable conditions from giving up action and treats fear as information rather than a command.

## Key Characteristics
- Identified in source metadata as Tomorrow, Today and hosted by [[ShekharNatarajan]].
- Uses biography to move from personal formation into practical, social, and institutional questions.
- Connects leadership and technology to values, trust, judgment, inequality, and purpose.
- Returns to AI as a human, moral, cognitive, labor, and governance problem rather than only a technical tool.
- Moves across business, extreme environments, spirituality, logistics, government, and creative labor without abandoning implementation detail.
- Uses guest-specific expertise and experience to test broad claims against concrete mechanisms and tradeoffs.
- Preserves plural interpretations when guests connect practical events to spiritual or existential meaning.
