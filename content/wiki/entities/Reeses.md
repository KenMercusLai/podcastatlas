# Reese's

Reese's is the peanut-butter-and-chocolate candy brand discussed in [[reeses-heir-vs-chocolate-skimpflation]]. In the episode, [[BradReese]] contrasts the classic Reese's Peanut Butter Cup with some newer or shaped products whose labels no longer say milk chocolate or peanut butter.

The brand's wiki role is a [[ConsumerBrandMoat]] stress test. Its value rests partly on a simple product memory - chocolate plus peanut butter - but the episode shows how [[IngredientReformulationStrategy]] can split a brand into a classic formula and cheaper, more flexible shaped variants. When labels switch to [[ChocolateCompound|chocolate compound]] or peanut butter cream, the brand promise becomes harder to separate from [[Skimpflation]].
