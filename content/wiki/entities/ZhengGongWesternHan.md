## Overview

[[ZhengGongWesternHan|郑公 / Zheng Gong]] enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-532-xihan-zuida-de-nongmin-qiyi-lingdaozhe-shi-ta-lsjb4yu-36ef9lolcbml5ztypr_z|Hanji 532]] as the Guanghan man who leads a Hongjia-period Western Han uprising and calls himself "山君."

## Current Profile

Hanji 532 presents Zheng Gong less as a full biography than as the visible leader of a survival revolt. In the episode, he gathers people after local pressure and livelihood collapse become unbearable, attacks government offices, frees prisoners, seizes weapons from the local arsenal, and uses the title "山君." The host uses that self-title to explain the later "山大王" idiom.

The uprising then expands in 17 BCE. Zheng Gong personally leads followers into four counties, and the force grows toward ten thousand people. Local prefectural defense is shown as unable to contain the movement until the court appoints [[ZhaoHuWesternHan|赵护]] to Guanghan and mobilizes more than thirty thousand soldiers from Guanghan and Shu.

Zheng Gong's profile therefore remains event-centered. The source treats him as a rebellion leader produced by "官逼民反" rather than as a claimant seeking the throne; his personal origin, program, death, and organization are not independently developed.

## Key Characteristics

- Guanghan man who leads a Western Han local uprising under Han Chengdi.
- Uses the title "山君," giving the episode its "山大王" origin thread.
- Mobilizes through attacks on official offices, prison release, and seizure of weapons.
- Becomes powerful enough to attack four counties and approach ten thousand followers.
- Source-scoped as a survival-rebellion leader rather than a dynastic claimant.
