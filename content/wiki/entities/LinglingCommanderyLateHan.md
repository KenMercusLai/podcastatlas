# 零陵郡 / Lingling Commandery (late Han)

零陵郡 / Lingling Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1027-jingzhou-zhizhu-liubiao-weihe-nan-cheng-bazhu-ll-ebguqllsf-5ycvpdogihzn3lj|Hanji 1027]] as one of the three [[SouthernJingzhouLateHan|southern Jingzhou]] commanderies that [[HuanJieLateHan|桓阶]] proposes combining against [[LiuBiao|刘表]]. The episode treats it together with [[ChangshaCommanderyLateHan|长沙郡]] and [[GuiyangCommanderyLateHan|桂阳郡]] rather than as an independent administrative case.

Its significance is therefore structural. Lingling helps show that Liu Biao's [[Jingzhou|荆州]] control is not evenly integrated: the southern commanderies can become a collective bargaining and defection field when local elites see a better external alignment through [[CaoCao|曹操]].
