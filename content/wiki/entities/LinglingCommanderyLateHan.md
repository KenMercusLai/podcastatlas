# 零陵郡 / Lingling Commandery (late Han)

## Overview

零陵郡 / Lingling Commandery is a southern Jingzhou commandery whose current evidence moves from local pacification and reporting vulnerability to regional defection politics and the 215 CE Sun–Liu territorial conflict.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-919-xiang-huode-hao-xian-tikai-shanliang-de-ziji-lrqxfzir4bqxt0v3ccvfef8faade|Hanji 919]] gives Lingling an administrative-security branch through [[YangXuanLateHan|Yang Xuan]], who serves as its commandery administrator when bandits from [[CangwuCommanderyLateHan|Cangwu]] and [[GuiyangCommanderyLateHan|Guiyang]] attack. Yang's lime, fire-horse, and crossbow-wagon tactic pacifies the commandery, but [[ZhaoKaiLateHan|Zhao Kai]]'s false accusation shows that successful local defense still depends on access to court reporting.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1027-jingzhou-zhizhu-liubiao-weihe-nan-cheng-bazhu-ll-ebguqllsf-5ycvpdogihzn3lj|Hanji 1027]] makes Lingling one of the three [[SouthernJingzhouLateHan|southern Jingzhou]] commanderies that [[HuanJieLateHan|桓阶]] proposes combining against [[LiuBiao|刘表]]. Alongside [[ChangshaCommanderyLateHan|长沙郡]] and [[GuiyangCommanderyLateHan|桂阳郡]], it shows that Jingzhou control is not evenly integrated and can be repriced through local elites and external patrons.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1152-sunquan-weishenme-tongma-liubei-shi-pianzi-lvyngihgs-obrqasnub4_lptryop|Hanji 1152]] moves the commandery into the 215 CE Sun–Liu dispute. Changsha and Guiyang submit, but [[HaoPuLateHan|郝普]] initially holds Lingling for [[LiuBei|刘备]]. [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] then induces surrender by falsely denying the prospect of relief from Liu Bei and [[GuanYu|关羽]]. Lingling thus becomes both a territorial bargaining unit and a case where control changes through manipulated expectations rather than immediate assault alone.

## Key Characteristics

- Appears as a southern commandery requiring local military-administrative defense.
- Is vulnerable to both external attack and distorted reporting through higher political channels.
- Forms part of a recurring Changsha–Guiyang–Lingling southern alignment field.
- Changes hands in the 215 CE dispute after the defender's relief estimate is manipulated.

## Evidence

- Local pacification and reporting risk: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-919-xiang-huode-hao-xian-tikai-shanliang-de-ziji-lrqxfzir4bqxt0v3ccvfef8faade|Hanji 919]] presents Yang Xuan's suppression operation and Zhao Kai's later accusation.
- Southern Jingzhou coalition field: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1027-jingzhou-zhizhu-liubiao-weihe-nan-cheng-bazhu-ll-ebguqllsf-5ycvpdogihzn3lj|Hanji 1027]] places Lingling with Changsha and Guiyang in Huan Jie's breakaway proposal.
- Sun–Liu conflict and surrender: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1152-sunquan-weishenme-tongma-liubei-shi-pianzi-lvyngihgs-obrqasnub4_lptryop|Hanji 1152]] has Hao Pu hold Lingling until Lyu Meng's false no-relief message changes his decision.

## Qualifications

The sources span different periods and use Lingling mainly as a setting within broader narratives rather than as a complete administrative history. Hanji 919's chronology, names, tactics, and accusation require textual normalization; Hanji 1027's taxation and regional-coalition details remain episode-presented; and Hanji 1152's force sequence, surrender dialogue, relief timing, and counterfactual defense outcome require primary-text comparison.
