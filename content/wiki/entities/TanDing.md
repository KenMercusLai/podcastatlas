# 谭丁 / Tan Ding

## Overview
谭丁 is the travel and hospitality operator interviewed in [[vol-272-2026-nian-le-zenme-hai-you-ren-yao-zuo-zhongguo-ban-airbnb-1008880863]] about building [[YijianCiwo|一间次卧]], a China-focused bedroom-sharing marketplace.

## Current Profile
His role in the wiki is a practitioner source on how lodging marketplaces depend on macro timing, household cash flow, trust operations, and non-standard hospitality judgment. He brings experience from outbound travel entrepreneurship, [[Meituan]] homestay work, investment, [[Anaya]]-related homestay operations, and field research with hundreds of hosts and guests.

## Key Characteristics
- Hospitality-trained operator who treats lodging as service design, not only online inventory.
- Marketplace founder using [[YijianCiwo|一间次卧]] to test whether spare-bedroom supply can become a paid lodging category in China.
- Field-research-heavy builder who relies on host visits, guest surveys, and social-platform demand signals.
- AI-assisted solo operator who sees [[OnePersonCompany]] leverage as useful but subordinate to compliance, data, and fulfillment logic.
