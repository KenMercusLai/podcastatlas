# Hyper Agent

Hyper Agent is the AI-agent business that [[DavidSacks|David Sacks]] says [[Airtable]] spun out before its sale to [[BendingSpoons|Bending Spoons]] in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-googles-ai-brain-drain-spacexs-huge-quarter-airtables-90-collapse-us-data-fuels-china-ai-42362555]]. The episode treats the spinout as evidence that the founders and AI talent may have wanted to pursue the new agent opportunity while selling the older no-code product.

The wiki should treat Hyper Agent as a source-scoped AI application branch rather than as proof that the spinout will become a durable company. Its significance is strategic: it separates venture-style AI upside from the legacy [[Airtable]] asset, making [[SaaSCapitalStructureReset]], [[NoCodeAIDisruption]], and [[AIApplicationLayerMoat]] easier to analyze separately.
