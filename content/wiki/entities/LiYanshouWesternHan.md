## Overview

李延寿 / Li Yanshou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-495-xihan-mingjiang-pk-yanren-chongchen-yingpengying-shui-neng-ying-lk9qa3yd8l17wyadfsi6cgubzfyo|Hanji 495]] as the deceased Yushi Dafu whose vacancy creates the personnel contest around [[FengYewangWesternHan|冯野王]], [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]], and [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]].

## Current Profile

Li Yanshou is not developed as an independent character in the source. His death matters because it opens a senior court post. The vacancy lets many officials recommend Feng Yewang, gives Yuan occasion to ask Shi Xian for judgment, and gives Shi Xian a chance to block a hostile family through reputation-risk framing.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Yushi Dafu named in Hanji 495.
- His death creates the vacancy at the center of the episode.
- Source-scoped structural figure rather than a developed biography.
