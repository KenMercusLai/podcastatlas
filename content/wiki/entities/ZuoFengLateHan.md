# 左丰 / Zuo Feng (late Han)

左丰 / Zuo Feng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-928-zhichang-buke-mingshuo-song-dui-li-banshi-geng-shunli-lgup21lsa-u1ykaiyabcko2vhp0o|Hanji 928]] as the small yellow gate sent by [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] to inspect [[LuZhiLateHan|卢植]]'s camp during the 184 CE campaign against [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]].

The source makes Zuo Feng important less as a full biography than as an access channel. Lu Zhi's subordinates suggest giving him a gift because his report will reach the emperor; Lu Zhi refuses, and Zuo Feng later says Guangzong is easy to take while Lu Zhi keeps the army resting. Emperor Ling believes the report and recalls Lu Zhi in a prison cart, after which [[DongZhuo|董卓]] is sent to replace him.

This page is source-scoped to the Lu Zhi removal branch. Zuo Feng's role is a compact example of [[InspectionGiftGatekeeping|巡查送礼关口]] and [[PalaceAccessMonopoly|宫廷接触垄断]]: a court-side inspector can convert front-line military facts into a palace interpretation that changes command.
