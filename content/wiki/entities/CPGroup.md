# CP Group / 正大集团

CP Group, or Charoen Pokphand / 正大集团, appears in [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046]] through [[XieGuomin]] and reform-era overseas Chinese return investment. The episode says CP Group received early foreign-investment approvals in [[Shenzhen]], [[Zhuhai]], and [[ShantouSpecialEconomicZone]], making it a concrete example of diaspora-linked capital entering China after 1979.

The source uses CP Group to complicate the idea that [[Chaoshan]] lacked overseas support. The stronger claim is about conversion: overseas Chinese investment and hometown ties mattered, but [[DiasporaCapitalReturnLimits]] and [[ChinaSpecialEconomicZoneAsymmetry]] shaped whether that capital could become a local manufacturing ecosystem.
