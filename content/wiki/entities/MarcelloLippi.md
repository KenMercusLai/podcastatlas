## Overview
Marcello Lippi / 里皮 appears in the source as the coach whose move from [[GuangzhouFC]]'s Evergrande-era success into China's national-team setup marks a boundary problem. The episode recognizes his national-team results while treating the process as institutionally damaging.

## Current Profile
Lippi's role in this wiki branch is not primarily tactical. He represents the moment when a dominant club model became entangled with national-team demands, "为国养士" logic, and later naturalized-player spending. The source frames this as a shift from club success toward football institutions serving higher political or administrative needs.

## Key Characteristics
- High-profile coach connected to Guangzhou FC's dominant era and the later China national-team project.
- Performance-positive but governance-problematic figure in the source's interpretation.
- Marker of [[ClubNationalTeamBoundaryBlur]] because club resources and national-team objectives became harder to separate.
