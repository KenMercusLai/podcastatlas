# Celsius Holdings

Celsius Holdings appears in [[hard-cedar-lebanon-seeks-trumps-help-6a5f41e97bddc0bea5542de5]] as an American energy-drink challenger. The episode says Celsius has become number three in America after [[RedBull]] and [[MonsterBeverage|Monster]] by using health, wellness, and women-focused positioning.

The source treats Celsius as a real competitive signal without making it a near-term Red Bull replacement. Its role is to show that energy-drink competition can move from young male party and extreme-sports associations toward a wider wellness-coded consumer market.
