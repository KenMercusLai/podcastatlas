# Angel Oak Smokehouse

## Overview

Angel Oak Smokehouse is a Charleston, South Carolina smoked-salmon company co-founded by Jade Taylor and informed by her father's long experience smoking fish in northern England.

## Current Profile

The company is presented as an early retail success deciding whether to add direct shipping and specialty wholesale. Its premium product is unusually exposed to delivery delay and mishandling, so the episode recommends preserving retail momentum while using narrow experiments to learn about repeat demand, geography, order size, logistics, and capacity.

## Key Characteristics
- Produces cold-smoked and hot-smoked salmon.
- Entered dozens of Whole Foods stores and reported additional regional retail commitments soon after launch.
- Faces a cold-chain risk in which channel failure can become product and brand failure.
- Has plausible DTC and gourmet-retail opportunities but limited evidence about repeat national demand.
- Must align salmon supply, smoking capacity, shipping economics, and quality control before accelerating growth.

## Evidence
- Retail traction: [[advice-line-with-danny-meyer-of-shake-shack-c7199e93-5dba-43ab-8fb0-91403917c3ac]] records the caller's Whole Foods, Harris Teeter, and Fresh Market distribution claims.
- Product risk: [[advice-line-with-danny-meyer-of-shake-shack-c7199e93-5dba-43ab-8fb0-91403917c3ac]] identifies spoilage and mishandling as threats to the premium experience.
- Test design: [[advice-line-with-danny-meyer-of-shake-shack-c7199e93-5dba-43ab-8fb0-91403917c3ac]] recommends geographically or temporally limited DTC tests, repeat-order tracking, selected prestigious retailers, and meaningful wholesale minimums.
- Capacity gate: [[advice-line-with-danny-meyer-of-shake-shack-c7199e93-5dba-43ab-8fb0-91403917c3ac]] asks what would happen if the new channel worked exceptionally well.

## Qualifications

Retail counts and commitments are caller-reported snapshots. The advice does not supply cold-chain failure rates, margins, customer-acquisition costs, or results from a completed channel test.

## What Changed
- Established Angel Oak as a perishable-CPG case where channel learning must be gated by logistics, supply, capacity, and quality.
