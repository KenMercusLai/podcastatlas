## Overview

梁盈 / Empress Liang Ying enters the wiki as [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]'s first empress and as the marital face of Liang-family power before the 159 CE anti-[[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] turn. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-845-cong-dangdai-hunyin-guan-liao-gudai-de-hunjian-zhidu-lr0i25twkoxm2wen7pnfbwt3qdsn|Hanji 845]] presents her as a woman whose empress title comes from her family position, whose body is inspected before marriage under imperial order, and whose formal rank never becomes affection, fertility, or security.

## Current Profile

Hanji 845 gives Liang Ying a compact but structurally important profile. She is introduced as the daughter of Liang Shang, sister of Liang Ji, and younger sister of the earlier Liang empress dowager. Her marriage to Huan is therefore not presented as a private match; it is a Liang-family status arrangement inside an already constrained imperial household.

Before the wedding, Huan sends a female official to Liang's home to inspect Liang Ying. The source lists gait, body odor, body proportions, facial features, hair, limbs, hands, feet, fingers, toes, and voice. Liang Ying resists out of shame, but the command is imperial and the Liang household has to accept. The episode treats this as [[ImperialMarriageInspection|皇室婚前身体检视]] rather than modern medical screening.

After becoming empress, Liang Ying is portrayed negatively: extravagant, overbearing, jealous of other women, and determined to monopolize Huan's access. The source also says she has no child and that Huan increasingly avoids her once the Liang dowager dies. Her autumn 159 death ends in burial at Yiling with the posthumous title Yixian Empress, but the episode's political hinge is that her death clears space for Huan to move against Liang Ji.

## Key Characteristics

- Liang-family empress whose title depends on outer-relative backing rather than Huan's affection.
- Prospective imperial spouse subjected to a palace-authorized body and voice inspection before marriage.
- Huan-era harem actor portrayed as jealous, controlling, and unable to produce an heir.
- Formal queen whose rank cannot resolve the emotional and political distance from the emperor.
- Household link between Liang-family power and Huan's later decision to act against Liang Ji.

## Qualifications

The page is bounded to one podcast source note and does not reconstruct Liang Ying's full biography, exact palace chronology, or independent factional base. The source's negative claims about her appearance, talent, virtue, jealousy, and emotional state are episode characterizations and should not be expanded beyond the supplied evidence. The "first recorded inspected empress" claim remains source-scoped until another source independently supports it.
