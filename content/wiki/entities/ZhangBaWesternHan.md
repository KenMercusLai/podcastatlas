## Overview

张霸 / Zhang Ba enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-430-chigua-qunzhong-qianren-yu-xianren-de-gongdou-daxi-lkfcjewfhmhfgw39vqfqkpa9umzq|Hanji 430]] as a young descendant in [[ZhangHeWesternHan|张贺]]'s benefactor-family reward branch.

## Current Profile

The episode says Xuan rewards Zhang Ba despite his youth, appointing him Sanqi Zhonglangjiang and granting Guannei Hou status. His role is to show that Xuan's repayment of old kindness can reach descendants who did not personally perform the original favor.

## Key Characteristics

- Child descendant in Zhang He's line.
- Rewarded because of Xuan's gratitude to earlier benefactors.
- Illustrates intergenerational transfer of favor.
