# Solon

## Overview

Solon is the Athenian lawgiver presented as an early constitutional mediator who limits exploitation and makes law more public without creating full democracy.

## Current Profile

The source places Solon in a divided, aristocrat-dominated Athens humiliated by conflict with Megara over Salamis. As archon in 594 BC, he responds to the conflict between rich and poor by giving ordinary Athenians stronger legal protection and making rich and poor more equal before written law.

His settlement is deliberately mixed. It restrains elite abuse but keeps the highest magistracies under elite control. Solon therefore matters less as a complete democratic founder than as a balancing reformer whose written-law settlement opens space for later political development without ending social hierarchy.

## Key Characteristics

- Solon responds to debt, exploitation, faction, and military humiliation through constitutional mediation.
- He strengthens the standing of ordinary Athenians before written law.
- He preserves elite control of major magistracies rather than instituting full popular rule.
- The episode treats his settlement as a necessary but incomplete stage before tyranny and Cleisthenic reform.

## Evidence

- Crisis setting: [[334-athens-and-the-birth-of-democracy-glt2496707123]] places Solon's archonship in 594 BC after division and humiliation over Salamis.
- Legal reform: [[334-athens-and-the-birth-of-democracy-glt2496707123]] says he makes rich and poor equal before written law and protects ordinary Athenians from exploitation.
- Elite continuity: [[334-athens-and-the-birth-of-democracy-glt2496707123]] says the highest offices remain controlled by the elite.

## Qualifications

The source offers a compressed constitutional interpretation rather than a full reconstruction of Solon's laws, poetry, social classes, debt reforms, or later legendary reception.

## What Changed

- Created Solon as the mixed-law and anti-exploitation precursor to Athenian democracy.

- [[Cleisthenes]] - later reformer who moves beyond Solon's elite-popular balance.
- [[Peisistratus]] - tyrant arising after the Solonian settlement fails to end factional conflict.
