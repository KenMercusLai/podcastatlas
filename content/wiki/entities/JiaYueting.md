# Jia Yueting / 贾跃亭

## Overview
Jia Yueting / 贾跃亭 is a Chinese entrepreneur whose path runs from early trading and telecom-equipment ventures through [[LeEco|乐视]] and [[FaradayFuture|法拉第未来]]. The wiki now treats him as both a substantive operator who anticipated several technology shifts and a central case of founder ambition becoming inseparable from financial opacity, regulatory violations, and uncontrolled expansion.

## Current Profile
The bounded evidence does not support a simple visionary-versus-fraud verdict. Jia repeatedly converted early judgment into actual companies and products, including telecom systems, licensed online video, smart televisions, smartphones, and an electric-vehicle program. The same record also includes overexpansion, extensive financing engineering, Le.com's regulator-determined historical financial misstatement, founder share pledges and sell-downs, and an ecosystem whose obligations became mutually contagious.

His earlier use as a modern [[WilliamDurant]] analogy remains valid but is now better grounded. Genuine industrial insight and prior success can reinforce founder self-proof, making continued financing feel like a solution even after operating scope, governance, and cash needs exceed organizational capacity.

## Key Characteristics
- Built real operating capability before the LeEco crisis, beginning with varied ventures and a telecom-equipment business that reached public markets.
- Identified mobile video, licensed content, internet-connected televisions, service-subsidized hardware, and electric vehicles early enough to turn several ideas into products.
- Used vision, recruiting, financing, and rapid execution to open many fronts faster than the organization could validate and fund them.
- Became associated with opaque related-company flows, heavy share pledging, large sell-downs, disputed disclosure, and regulatory sanction.
- Continued the EV ambition through [[FaradayFuture|Faraday Future]] after leaving China in 2017, but the source's delivery evidence remains far below mass-production scale.

## Qualifications
- The episode's biographical, sales, financing, debt, and delivery figures are secondary-source claims and should not be treated as audited reconstruction.
- Employee recollections of Jia's work ethic and manner help explain loyalty but do not offset regulatory findings or establish intent.
- Calling Jia either a sincere dreamer or a deliberate fraud would flatten a record in which belief, execution, overconfidence, and misconduct can coexist.
