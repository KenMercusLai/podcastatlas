## Current Profile

The source gives Ma Tong a compact but important role in the causal chain of the palace assassination case. During Liu Ju's uprising, Ma Tong fights vigorously and is rewarded as Chonghe Hou. That reward initially marks loyal service to Han Wudi, but after Wudi reverses against Jiang Chong's faction, the same history becomes dangerous because Ma Tong's family is connected to the side that helped destroy the crown prince.

Hanji 390-1 therefore uses Ma Tong to show how a prior reward can become later exposure when court judgment changes. His role is not the dramatic capture scene; it is the political background that explains why the Ma brothers fear being swept into the post-Wugu reckoning.

## Key Characteristics

- Brother of Ma Heluo in the Lingguang Palace assassination-plot story.
- Former anti-Liu-Ju combatant rewarded after the Wugu crisis.
- Example of court-credit reversal, where service in one political moment becomes liability in another.
- Source-scoped conspirator whose profile is mainly background for Ma Heluo's attempted assassination.
