# Hasdrubal the Handsome / 美男子哈斯德鲁巴

## Overview

Hasdrubal the Handsome / 美男子哈斯德鲁巴 is [[HamilcarBarca]]'s son-in-law and successor in Iberia, where he expands Carthaginian control, founds [[NewCarthage]], and negotiates the Ebro agreement before his assassination in 221 BC.

## Current Profile

Hasdrubal inherits command because [[HannibalBarca]] is still young when Hamilcar dies. The episode presents him as a political and military consolidator: he marries into Iberian royalty, suppresses resistance, expands the territory, and creates New Carthage as its administrative center.

His agreement with Rome not to cross the Ebro recognizes a Carthaginian sphere south of the river but does not settle the status of [[Saguntum]]. That ambiguity survives his assassination and becomes central under Hannibal.

## Key Characteristics

- Continues Hamilcar's expansion rather than beginning a separate project.
- Uses marriage, urban foundation, coercion, and diplomacy alongside military power.
- Makes New Carthage the center of the Spanish settlement.
- Leaves an Ebro settlement whose relation to Saguntum remains disputed.

## Evidence

- Consolidation: [[568-hannibal-romes-greatest-enemy-part-1-glt7606824410]] connects Hasdrubal to Iberian marriage, territorial expansion, rebellion suppression, and New Carthage.
- Diplomacy: [[568-hannibal-romes-greatest-enemy-part-1-glt7606824410]] attributes the Ebro agreement to Hasdrubal and Rome.
- Succession: [[568-hannibal-romes-greatest-enemy-part-1-glt7606824410]] says the army selects Hannibal after Hasdrubal's assassination and Carthage accepts the choice.

## Qualifications

The degree to which Hasdrubal and the Barcids acted independently of Carthage is interpretive. Marriage details, coercion accounts, treaty meaning, and the assassin's motive depend on mediated ancient evidence.

## What Changed

- Added the institutional bridge between Hamilcar's conquest and Hannibal's command.
- Located the Ebro agreement inside the unresolved Saguntum dispute.

## Relationships

- [[HamilcarBarca]] - predecessor and father-in-law whose Iberian project he inherits.
- [[HannibalBarca]] - younger successor selected after his assassination.
- [[NewCarthage]] - capital and administrative center associated with his rule.
- [[SaguntumWarTrigger]] - later crisis shaped by the territorial agreement he negotiated.
