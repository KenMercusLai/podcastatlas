# 吾之才 / Wu Zhicai

吾之才 / Wu Zhicai appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-29-suqin-lizhi-shuangwen-zhishi-gaibian-mingyun-lrf5cqvoriyep2lkbozju-lyjaxv]] as the addressee of [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s poem "赠吾之才." The episode says Sima Guang uses [[SuQin|苏秦]] and [[ZhuFuYan|主父偃]] to encourage him through frustration and social disregard.

The page is source-scoped. Its function is to anchor the reception evidence: by Sima Guang's time, Su Qin's rise from humiliation to "six-state minister" status could already be used as a literary consolation pattern.
