# 功夫熊 / Gongfu Xiong

## Overview
功夫熊 / Gongfu Xiong is the home-massage O2O company the episode uses to explain why skilled local services are hard to platformize.

## Current Profile
The source presents Gongfu Xiong as a 2014 service platform founded by Wang Run with cofounders from Baidu. The app showed technician photos, service items, distance, reviews, and prices, and it developed enterprise group-buying demand. Its limits were the classic local-service O2O constraints: hard-to-standardize skill, technician travel time, low turnover, and subsidy pressure.

## Key Characteristics
- It is the episode's main home-massage case.
- Its app interface made provider discovery, distance, ratings, and pricing more visible to users.
- It found both consumer and enterprise group-buying demand.
- Its economics were constrained by skilled-labor variation, travel time, utilization, and subsidies.
