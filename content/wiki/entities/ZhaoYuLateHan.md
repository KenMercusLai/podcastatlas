# 赵昱 / Zhao Yu (late Han)

赵昱 / Zhao Yu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-995-ta-zuishang-cibei-weihe-duoci-cansha-enren-llicukoezccd5iycin4uq7qun51k|Hanji 995]] as the [[GuanglingCommandery|广陵]] administrator who receives [[ZeRong|笮融]] after Ze Rong flees [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]]'s defeated sphere with a large following. Zhao Yu treats him courteously, but Ze Rong kills him during the reception setting and plunders Guangling.

The page is source-scoped as a victim and trust-boundary case. Zhao Yu's hospitality becomes dangerous because Ze Rong does not behave within ordinary reciprocity norms, making the episode's moral lesson less about naivete alone than about the need to detect actors who weaponize reception.
