# 陈寿 / Chen Shou

陈寿 / Chen Shou enters this wiki branch through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1026-jiemi-sanguo-di-yi-mengjiang-lvbu-de-luomu-lhjf-agujuliglqchumtff3tftxg1|Hanji 1026]], where the host cites his evaluation of [[LyuBu|吕布]] from the [[Sanguozhi|《三国志》]] tradition. In this source use, Chen Shou is not a narrative actor in the 198 CE Xiapi campaign; he is the historian whose retrospective judgment helps frame Lü Bu as militarily fierce but strategically and politically unsound.

The page keeps that role source-scoped. Chen Shou's citation supports the episode's closing contrast between raw martial force and the durable trust needed for survival after surrender, which connects Lü Bu's execution to [[QingYuQuJiu|轻于去就]] and [[XingshiqingStrategicAssessment|行势情战略评估]].
