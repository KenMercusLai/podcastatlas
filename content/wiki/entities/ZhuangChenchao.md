# Zhuang Chenchao

Zhuang Chenchao appears in [[ep91-dingfang-dingpiao-dingjiangshan-xiecheng-51-yi-wei-aoman-maidan-lovhfkz4rklv1ik-uqyeswrdf3uw]] as the founder of [[Qunar]]. The episode presents him as a technical entrepreneur who pushed travel price-comparison search and mobile growth against [[Ctrip]]'s heavier full-service OTA model.

His role in the source is competitive rather than biographical. [[Qunar]] matters because it made prices from multiple travel channels visible in one place, turning search, comparison, mobile traffic, public accusations, and later [[Baidu]]-linked capital integration into part of the [[OTAPlatformConcentration]] story.
