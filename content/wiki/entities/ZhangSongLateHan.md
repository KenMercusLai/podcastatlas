# 张松 / Zhang Song (Late Han)

## Overview
张松 / Zhang Song is the [[LiuZhang|刘璋]] emissary whose failed reception by [[CaoCao|曹操]] becomes a pivot in Hanji 1109's account of Yizhou moving toward [[LiuBei|刘备]].

## Current Profile
Liu Zhang sends Zhang Song to Cao Cao after Cao's occupation of Jingzhou. The episode portrays Zhang Song as sharp and decisive but says Cao Cao receives him coldly and declines [[YangXiu|杨修]]'s proposal to recruit him. Zhang Song returns resentful, persuades Liu Zhang to sever the Cao relationship, and recommends alliance with Liu Bei.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as an emissary whose treatment changes the information and alliance channel available to his ruler.
- Converts personal rejection into a strategic recommendation against Cao Cao.
- Helps make Yizhou's internal uncertainty consequential beyond the province.

## Qualifications
The episode compresses the diplomatic sequence and strongly moralizes Cao Cao's reception. Zhang Song's physical description and inner resentment are source-scoped, and the page does not claim that this encounter alone explains Yizhou's later fate.
