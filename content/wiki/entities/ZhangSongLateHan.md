# 张松 / Zhang Song (Late Han)

## Overview
张松 / Zhang Song is the [[LiuZhang|刘璋]] official whose failed reception by [[CaoCao|曹操]], recommendation of [[FaZheng|法正]], and covert support for [[LiuBei|刘备]] turn Yizhou's diplomatic pivot into an internal transfer-of-power project.

## Current Profile
Zhang Song's role begins in diplomacy but does not end there. Liu Zhang sends him to Cao Cao after Cao's occupation of Jingzhou; Cao reportedly receives him coldly and declines [[YangXiu|杨修]]'s proposal to recruit him. Zhang Song returns resentful, persuades Liu Zhang to sever the Cao relationship, and recommends alliance with Liu Bei. When Liu Zhang later asks for an envoy to Liu Bei, Zhang Song recommends Fa Zheng. After Fa Zheng returns favorable to Liu Bei, the two reportedly plan to receive Liu Bei into Yizhou as its future ruler.

The current profile therefore moves from failed reception and alliance advice into sponsorship and covert regime change, while keeping private motive and exact planning qualified. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1109-xizaochi-nu-pi-caocao-duonian-jiye-hui-yu-yidan-lukll8jb60nx2brhwhsw7gfrd-kt|Hanji 1109]] grounds the Cao-side rupture, and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1123-ta-bu-shang-zhanchang-ruhe-li-xia-xianhe-zhangong-losloy1puogq4o9azo2x_ombrbyz|Hanji 1123]] grounds the Fa Zheng channel and Yizhou plan.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as an emissary whose treatment changes the information and alliance channel available to his ruler.
- Converts personal rejection into a strategic recommendation against Cao Cao.
- Helps make Yizhou's internal uncertainty consequential beyond the province.
- Recognizes and sponsors Fa Zheng despite the latter's weak standing under Liu Zhang.
- Joins Fa Zheng in converting an external alliance channel into a plan for Liu Bei's entry and rule.

- Missed recruitment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1109-xizaochi-nu-pi-caocao-duonian-jiye-hui-yu-yidan-lukll8jb60nx2brhwhsw7gfrd-kt|Hanji 1109]] reports that Cao Cao does not accept Yang Xiu's recruitment suggestion.
- Alliance reversal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1109-xizaochi-nu-pi-caocao-duonian-jiye-hui-yu-yidan-lukll8jb60nx2brhwhsw7gfrd-kt|Hanji 1109]] has Zhang Song persuade Liu Zhang to break with Cao Cao and approach Liu Bei.
- Fa Zheng channel: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1123-ta-bu-shang-zhanchang-ruhe-li-xia-xianhe-zhangong-losloy1puogq4o9azo2x_ombrbyz|Hanji 1123]] says Zhang Song recommends Fa Zheng as envoy and then joins him in planning Liu Bei's entry into Yizhou.

## Qualifications
The episodes compress the diplomatic sequence and strongly interpret private motive. Zhang Song's physical description, pride, resentment, judgment of Liu Zhang, exact conversations with Fa Zheng, and premeditation before the embassy are source-scoped. Cao Cao's reception does not alone explain Yizhou's later fate, and the current evidence does not establish that Zhang Song and Fa Zheng entered every stage with an identical plan.

## What Changed
- Added Zhang Song's friendship with and sponsorship of Fa Zheng.
- Extended the profile from external alliance advice to a qualified internal transfer-of-power project.
