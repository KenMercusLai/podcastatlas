# Jair Bolsonaro

## Overview
Jair Bolsonaro is Brazil's former president and the imprisoned political figure whose family and electorate remain central to the 2026 contest in [[brazil-rut-lula-v-bolsonaro-again-6abb8653ed4e198024b38eb5]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Bolsonaro as serving a 27-year sentence for plotting a coup after losing the 2022 election, yet still able to structure national politics through a loyal voter base and his son [[FlavioBolsonaro]]. It also places his government inside the same fiscal pattern criticized under Lula: breaking spending limits and expanding welfare in pursuit of re-election.

## Key Characteristics
- Former president whose conviction does not erase his political influence.
- Center of a family succession strategy led in the source by Flavio Bolsonaro.
- Beneficiary of a proposed presidential pardon.
- Part of the cross-camp pattern of electorally motivated public spending.
