# Jair Bolsonaro

## Overview
Jair Bolsonaro is Brazil's former president and the imprisoned political figure whose family and electorate remain central to the 2026 contest. The newer source treats his son's first-round lead and multiple relatives' legislative victories as evidence that imprisonment did not dissolve the family movement.

## Current Profile
Both episodes present Bolsonaro as serving a 27-year sentence for plotting a coup after losing the 2022 election, yet still structuring national politics through a loyal voter base and [[FlavioBolsonaro]]. The newer source adds a broader family comeback through Michelle, Carlos, Jair Renan, and Bolsonaro's brother, while the earlier source places his government inside the same electorally motivated spending pattern criticized under Lula.

## Key Characteristics
- Former president whose conviction does not erase his political influence.
- Center of a family succession strategy led by Flavio Bolsonaro and reinforced by other relatives' reported electoral wins.
- Beneficiary of a proposed presidential pardon.
- Part of the cross-camp pattern of electorally motivated public spending.

## Evidence

- Succession strategy: [[brazil-rut-lula-v-bolsonaro-again-6abb8653ed4e198024b38eb5]] describes Flavio's candidacy and pardon pledge as the main vehicle; [[here-comes-the-son-brazils-election-tips-rightward-4ae9c94fbc2460c76c6e93fcf359497b]] adds Flavio's first-round lead and reported wins by several other relatives.
- Fiscal continuity: [[brazil-rut-lula-v-bolsonaro-again-6abb8653ed4e198024b38eb5]] says Bolsonaro broke spending limits and expanded welfare before his failed re-election attempt.
