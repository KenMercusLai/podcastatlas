# 郭图 / Guo Tu

## Overview
郭图 / Guo Tu is a Yuan Shao adviser whose bounded wiki role moves from Jizhou acquisition support to decision-point opposition against [[JuShou|沮授]]. The sources show him as part of [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s adviser-rich camp, but his most developed function is to shape choices away from constraint-bearing legitimacy and toward Yuan Shao-pleasing confidence.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] places Guo Tu in the persuasion-and-pressure channel that helps Yuan Shao obtain [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] from [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-960-caowei-dingji-zhinang-xunyu-weihe-qi-yuan-tou-cao-lmor1txqpm88itt4incalusconjr|Hanji 960]] keeps him in Yuan Shao's camp while [[XunYu|荀彧]] leaves for [[CaoCao|曹操]], marking how attractive Yuan's roster could appear before the talent-use critique hardens. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] then gives Guo Tu his main strategic profile: he opposes Ju Shou's emperor-welcome plan because hosting [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] could restrict Yuan Shao's freedom. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] extends that profile into the Guandu prewar council, where Guo Tu and [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] flatter Yuan Shao's martial prestige and push confidence in an immediate campaign against Cao Cao.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan Shao-side adviser active in persuasion, elite-camp formation, and high-stakes strategic debate.
- Constraint-avoidant voice in the emperor-welcome decision, preferring Yuan Shao's freedom of action over imperial-hosting leverage.
- Pro-war and flattering voice in Hanji 1034's pre-Guandu meeting.
- Marker of Yuan Shao's adviser abundance failing to become unified decision quality.
- Counterpart to Ju Shou, whose strategic caution and timing arguments Guo Tu repeatedly opposes.

## Evidence
- Jizhou pressure channel: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] names Guo Tu with [[GaoGanLateHan|高干]], [[XinPingLateHan|辛评]], and [[XunChenLateHan|荀谌]] in the mission that helps turn Han Fu's fear into surrender of Jizhou.
- Camp-choice contrast: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-960-caowei-dingji-zhinang-xunyu-weihe-qi-yuan-tou-cao-lmor1txqpm88itt4incalusconjr|Hanji 960]] makes Guo Tu one of the figures remaining with Yuan Shao while Xun Yu judges Cao Cao the better political host.
- Legitimacy constraint: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] pairs Guo Tu with [[ChunyuQiong|淳于琼]] against Ju Shou's plan to welcome Emperor Xian, arguing from the risk that imperial commands would constrain Yuan Shao.
- Flattering war counsel: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] pairs Guo Tu with Shen Pei in praising Yuan Shao's divine martial strength and Hebei resources against Ju Shou's recovery-first plan.

## Qualifications
This is not a full biography of Guo Tu. The sources make him legible through selected decision scenes and do not independently audit his whole career. Hanji 1034 criticizes the flattering strategy, but the page should keep that judgment source-scoped rather than making Guo Tu a generic villain.

## What Changed
- Hanji 1034 extends Guo Tu from the emperor-welcome opposition role into a second high-stakes Yuan-camp debate before Guandu.
- The page now treats his advice as part of Yuan Shao's factional meeting dynamics, not just as a single missed legitimacy-window objection.
- The profile is migrated to synthesis-v1 while preserving the existing source inventory order.
