# 郭图 / Guo Tu

## Overview
郭图 / Guo Tu is a [[YuanShao|Yuan Shao]] adviser whose bounded wiki role moves from Jizhou acquisition support to repeated opposition against [[JuShou|Ju Shou]] at major decision points. The sources show him as part of Yuan Shao's adviser-rich camp, but his most developed function is to shape choices away from constraint-bearing caution and toward Yuan-pleasing confidence, culminating in the [[WuchaoRaidLateHan|Wuchao]] blame shift that helps drive [[ZhangHeLateHan|Zhang He]] and [[GaoLanLateHan|Gao Lan]] to [[CaoCao|Cao Cao]].

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] places Guo Tu in the persuasion-and-pressure channel that helps Yuan Shao obtain [[JizhouLateHan|Jizhou]] from [[HanFuLateHan|Han Fu]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-960-caowei-dingji-zhinang-xunyu-weihe-qi-yuan-tou-cao-lmor1txqpm88itt4incalusconjr|Hanji 960]] keeps him in Yuan Shao's camp while [[XunYu|Xun Yu]] leaves for Cao Cao, marking how attractive Yuan's roster could appear before the talent-use critique hardens. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] then gives Guo Tu a strategic profile: he opposes Ju Shou's emperor-welcome plan because hosting [[EmperorXianOfHan|Emperor Xian]] could restrict Yuan Shao's freedom.

The Guandu-preparation sources extend that profile. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] pairs Guo Tu with [[ShenPeiLateHan|Shen Pei]] against Ju Shou's rest-and-harassment plan, then has Guo Tu's side warn that Ju Shou's combined military and administrative authority is too high. Yuan Shao responds by splitting Ju Shou's troops among Ju Shou, Guo Tu, and [[ChunyuQiong|Chunyu Qiong]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] sharpens the meeting interpretation: Guo Tu and Shen Pei win by flattering Yuan Shao's martial prestige and confidence in Hebei strength.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1049-guandu-zhizhan-jieju-jiemu-caocao-zhongyu-yi-ruo-sheng-qiang-lvtutwyhrix4rhiypa9dknz3da1m|Hanji 1049]] makes Guo Tu's risk visible at the campaign climax. When Cao raids Wuchao, Zhang He advises full rescue of the grain depot, but Guo Tu urges a heavy attack on Cao's camp instead. Yuan Shao follows that balance, Wuchao falls, the camp attack fails, and Guo Tu then tells Yuan Shao that Zhang He celebrated the defeat. This self-protective slander turns a failed decision into a command rupture when Zhang He and Gao Lan surrender to Cao.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan Shao-side adviser active in persuasion, elite-camp formation, and high-stakes strategic debate.
- Constraint-avoidant voice in the emperor-welcome decision, preferring Yuan Shao's freedom of action over imperial-hosting leverage.
- Pro-war and flattering voice in the pre-Guandu meeting.
- Participant in the power-allocation attack that reduces Ju Shou's command authority.
- Wuchao response voice whose attack-Cao-camp advice misreads the decisive logistics point.
- Self-protective blame shifter whose slander helps push Zhang He and Gao Lan into surrender.
- Counterpart to Ju Shou and marker of Yuan Shao's adviser abundance failing to become unified decision quality.

- Legitimacy constraint: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] pairs Guo Tu with Chunyu Qiong against Ju Shou's plan to welcome Emperor Xian, arguing from the risk that imperial commands would constrain Yuan Shao.
- Pro-war meeting role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] pair Guo Tu with Shen Pei in opposing Ju Shou's recovery-first strategy and praising Yuan Shao's strength.
- Command authority shift: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] says Yuan Shao divides Ju Shou's troops among Ju Shou, Guo Tu, and Chunyu Qiong after Guo Tu's side attacks Ju Shou's accumulated authority.
- Wuchao misallocation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1049-guandu-zhizhan-jieju-jiemu-caocao-zhongyu-yi-ruo-sheng-qiang-lvtutwyhrix4rhiypa9dknz3da1m|Hanji 1049]] contrasts Zhang He's rescue-Wuchao advice with Guo Tu's proposal to attack Cao Cao's camp, after which Yuan Shao sends insufficient force to the decisive depot.
- Blame-driven defection: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1049-guandu-zhizhan-jieju-jiemu-caocao-zhongyu-yi-ruo-sheng-qiang-lvtutwyhrix4rhiypa9dknz3da1m|Hanji 1049]] says Guo Tu slanders Zhang He after the failed camp attack, prompting Zhang He and Gao Lan to burn equipment and surrender to Cao.

## Qualifications
This is not a full biography of Guo Tu. The sources make him legible through selected decision scenes and do not independently audit his whole career. The Guandu sources criticize his flattering and self-protective choices, but the page keeps that judgment source-scoped rather than making Guo Tu a generic villain.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1049's Wuchao-response decision and post-failure slander.
- Extended Guo Tu's profile from prewar flattering counsel into battlefield misallocation and blame-shifting collapse.
- Linked Guo Tu directly to Zhang He and Gao Lan's surrender cascade.
