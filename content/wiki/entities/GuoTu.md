# 郭图 / Guo Tu

## Overview
郭图 / Guo Tu is a [[YuanShao|袁绍]] adviser whose bounded wiki role moves from Jizhou acquisition support to repeated opposition against [[JuShou|沮授]] at major decision points, then into blame-shifting and factional provocation after Yuan Shao's defeat and death. The sources show him as part of Yuan Shao's adviser-rich camp, but his most developed function is to steer decisions toward confidence, constraint avoidance, and self-protective factional pressure.

## Current Profile
Hanji 956 places Guo Tu in the persuasion-and-pressure channel that helps Yuan Shao obtain [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] from [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]]. Hanji 960 keeps him in Yuan Shao's camp while [[XunYu|荀彧]] leaves for [[CaoCao|曹操]], marking how attractive Yuan's roster could appear before the talent-use critique hardens. Hanji 992 then gives Guo Tu a strategic profile: he opposes Ju Shou's emperor-welcome plan because hosting [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] could restrict Yuan Shao's freedom.

The Guandu-preparation sources extend that profile. Hanji 1033 pairs Guo Tu with [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] against Ju Shou's rest-and-harassment plan, then has Guo Tu's side warn that Ju Shou's combined military and administrative authority is too high. Yuan Shao responds by splitting Ju Shou's troops among Ju Shou, Guo Tu, and [[ChunyuQiong|淳于琼]]. Hanji 1034 sharpens the meeting interpretation: Guo Tu and Shen Pei win by flattering Yuan Shao's martial prestige and confidence in Hebei strength.

Hanji 1049 makes Guo Tu's risk visible at the campaign climax. When Cao Cao raids [[WuchaoRaidLateHan|乌巢]], [[ZhangHeLateHan|张郃]] advises full rescue of the grain depot, but Guo Tu urges a heavy attack on Cao Cao's camp instead. Yuan Shao follows that balance, Wuchao falls, the camp attack fails, and Guo Tu then tells Yuan Shao that Zhang He celebrated the defeat. This self-protective slander turns a failed decision into a command rupture when Zhang He and [[GaoLanLateHan|高览]] surrender to Cao Cao.

Hanji 1058 adds Guo Tu's post-Guandu factional role. After Yuan Shao's death leaves [[YuanTan|袁谭]] and [[YuanShang|袁尚]] in rivalry, Guo Tu and [[XinPingLateHan|辛评]] tell Yuan Tan that his adoption out to an elder line and succession disadvantage were Shen Pei's doing. In the episode's sequence, that counsel helps convert the armor dispute and suspicion into direct attack on Yuan Shang.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan Shao-side adviser active in persuasion, elite-camp formation, and high-stakes strategic debate.
- Constraint-avoidant voice in the emperor-welcome decision, preferring Yuan Shao's freedom of action over imperial-hosting leverage.
- Pro-war and flattering voice in the pre-Guandu meeting.
- Participant in the power-allocation attack that reduces Ju Shou's command authority.
- Wuchao response voice whose attack-Cao-camp advice misreads the decisive logistics point.
- Self-protective blame shifter whose slander helps push Zhang He and Gao Lan into surrender.
- Post-Yuan Shao factional provoker who deepens Yuan Tan's resentment toward Yuan Shang and Shen Pei.

## Qualifications
This is not a full biography of Guo Tu. The sources make him legible through selected decision scenes and do not independently audit his whole career. The Guandu and post-Guandu sources criticize his flattering, self-protective, and provocation-oriented choices, but the page keeps that judgment source-scoped rather than making Guo Tu a generic villain.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1058's Yuan Tan-Yuan Shang provocation scene.
- Extended Guo Tu's profile from Wuchao blame-shifting into post-Yuan Shao faction hardening.
- Preserved the existing judgment that Guo Tu is most visible where adviser competition fails to improve Yuan Shao-side decision quality.
