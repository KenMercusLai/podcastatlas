# 郭图 / Guo Tu

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] backfills Guo Tu as part of Yuan Shao's Jizhou persuasion mission. Alongside [[GaoGanLateHan|高干]], [[XinPingLateHan|辛评]], and [[XunChenLateHan|荀谌]], he belongs to the pressure channel that turns [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]]'s movement and [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]]'s fear into the surrender of [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-960-caowei-dingji-zhinang-xunyu-weihe-qi-yuan-tou-cao-lmor1txqpm88itt4incalusconjr|Hanji 960]] backfills Guo Tu as one of the Yingchuan-linked figures who remain with [[YuanShao|袁绍]] when [[XunYu|荀彧]] rejects Yuan Shao and turns toward [[CaoCao|曹操]]. The source does not develop Guo Tu's own advice here, but it helps explain why Yuan Shao's camp could look like the natural destination for mobile Yingchuan elites before Xun Yu's negative talent-use judgment.

郭图 / Guo Tu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] as one of [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s advisers opposing [[JuShou|沮授]]'s proposal to welcome [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]. The source presents him with [[ChunyuQiong|淳于琼]] as the cautionary side of the debate: if Yuan Shao hosts the emperor, obedience may constrain him, but disobedience would look like defying imperial command.

The page is source-scoped. Guo Tu's current wiki role is not a full biography but a decision-point marker inside Yuan Shao's missed [[LegitimacyWindowSeizure|合法性窗口抢占]]: fear of constraint outweighs the chance to turn imperial recognition into command over rival lords.
