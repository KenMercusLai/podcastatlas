# 郭图 / Guo Tu

郭图 / Guo Tu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] as one of [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s advisers opposing [[JuShou|沮授]]'s proposal to welcome [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]. The source presents him with [[ChunyuQiong|淳于琼]] as the cautionary side of the debate: if Yuan Shao hosts the emperor, obedience may constrain him, but disobedience would look like defying imperial command.

The page is source-scoped. Guo Tu's current wiki role is not a full biography but a decision-point marker inside Yuan Shao's missed [[LegitimacyWindowSeizure|合法性窗口抢占]]: fear of constraint outweighs the chance to turn imperial recognition into command over rival lords.
