# 郭图 / Guo Tu

## Overview
郭图 / Guo Tu is a Yuan Shao adviser whose bounded wiki role moves from Jizhou acquisition support to repeated opposition against [[JuShou|沮授]] at major decision points. The sources show him as part of [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s adviser-rich camp, but his most developed function is to shape choices away from constraint-bearing legitimacy and toward Yuan Shao-pleasing confidence.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] places Guo Tu in the persuasion-and-pressure channel that helps Yuan Shao obtain [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] from [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-960-caowei-dingji-zhinang-xunyu-weihe-qi-yuan-tou-cao-lmor1txqpm88itt4incalusconjr|Hanji 960]] keeps him in Yuan Shao's camp while [[XunYu|荀彧]] leaves for [[CaoCao|曹操]], marking how attractive Yuan's roster could appear before the talent-use critique hardens. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] then gives Guo Tu a strategic profile: he opposes Ju Shou's emperor-welcome plan because hosting [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] could restrict Yuan Shao's freedom.

The Guandu-preparation sources extend that profile. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] pairs Guo Tu with [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] against Ju Shou's rest-and-harassment plan, then has Guo Tu's side warn that Ju Shou's combined military and administrative authority is too high. Yuan Shao responds by splitting Ju Shou's troops among Ju Shou, Guo Tu, and [[ChunyuQiong|淳于琼]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] sharpens the meeting interpretation: Guo Tu and Shen Pei win by flattering Yuan Shao's martial prestige and confidence in Hebei strength.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan Shao-side adviser active in persuasion, elite-camp formation, and high-stakes strategic debate.
- Constraint-avoidant voice in the emperor-welcome decision, preferring Yuan Shao's freedom of action over imperial-hosting leverage.
- Pro-war and flattering voice in the pre-Guandu meeting.
- Participant in the power-allocation attack that reduces Ju Shou's command authority.
- Marker of Yuan Shao's adviser abundance failing to become unified decision quality.
- Counterpart to Ju Shou, whose strategic caution and timing arguments Guo Tu repeatedly opposes.

## Evidence
- Jizhou pressure channel: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] names Guo Tu with [[GaoGanLateHan|高干]], [[XinPingLateHan|辛评]], and [[XunChenLateHan|荀谌]] in the mission that helps turn Han Fu's fear into surrender of Jizhou.
- Camp-choice contrast: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-960-caowei-dingji-zhinang-xunyu-weihe-qi-yuan-tou-cao-lmor1txqpm88itt4incalusconjr|Hanji 960]] makes Guo Tu one of the figures remaining with Yuan Shao while Xun Yu judges Cao Cao the better political host.
- Legitimacy constraint: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] pairs Guo Tu with Chunyu Qiong against Ju Shou's plan to welcome Emperor Xian, arguing from the risk that imperial commands would constrain Yuan Shao.
- Pro-war meeting role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] pair Guo Tu with Shen Pei in opposing Ju Shou's recovery-first strategy and praising Yuan Shao's strength.
- Command authority shift: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] says Yuan Shao divides Ju Shou's troops among Ju Shou, Guo Tu, and Chunyu Qiong after Guo Tu's side attacks Ju Shou's accumulated authority.

## Qualifications
This is not a full biography of Guo Tu. The sources make him legible through selected decision scenes and do not independently audit his whole career. The Guandu sources criticize the flattering strategy, but the page keeps that judgment source-scoped rather than making Guo Tu a generic villain.

## What Changed
- Hanji 1033 adds the command-authority consequence of Guo Tu's anti-Ju-Shou line before Guandu.
- Hanji 1034 remains the sharper source for the meeting's flattery and advice-framing interpretation.

## Relationships
- [[YuanShao|袁绍]] - leader whose preferences Guo Tu's advice often fits.
- [[JuShou|沮授]] - opposing adviser in both legitimacy-window and pre-Guandu strategic-caution patterns.
- [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] - paired pro-war/flattery voice in the Guandu-preparation sources.
- [[ChunyuQiong|淳于琼]] - paired constraint-warning voice in Hanji 992 and co-recipient of divided command in Hanji 1033.
- [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] - sovereign whose possible hosting creates Guo Tu's constraint objection.
- [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] - campaign setting where Guo Tu's confidence-oriented counsel enters Yuan Shao's prewar decision process.
- [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]] - broader pattern in which advice competition does not produce better ruler judgment.
