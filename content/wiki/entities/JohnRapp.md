# John Rapp

John Rapp is the lawyer in [[a-trip-to-the-magic-mushroom-megachurch]] who advises psychedelic churches on formation, defense, counseling, warning, and legal risk. The source contrasts his conventional legal background with his current work helping [[PsychedelicChurches]] understand [[ReligiousControlledSubstanceExemption]], the [[ControlledSubstancesAct]], and the [[DEAExemptionProcess]].

The episode also gives Rapp a personal motivation. His son struggled with opioid addiction for more than a decade and died in 2020; Rapp later tried ayahuasca after learning that his son had found relief in such ceremonies, and he says the experience changed his view of psychedelics and grief.
