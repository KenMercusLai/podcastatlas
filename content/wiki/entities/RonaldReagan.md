# Ronald Reagan / 罗纳德·里根

Ronald Reagan / 罗纳德·里根 appears in [[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] as the [[UnitedStates|U.S.]] president whose hard line toward the [[SovietUnion]] became part of the Soviet war-scare environment. The episode links his rhetoric, the Strategic Defense Initiative, and Pershing II missiles to Soviet fear that the West might be preparing or enabling a first strike.

The episode's Reagan branch is less about U.S. intention than about adversary perception. Through [[OlegGordievsky|Oleg Gordievsky]] and [[OperationRYAN]], Britain came to see that Soviet leaders were frightened as well as aggressive, making [[ColdWarNuclearMisperception]] a strategic problem.
