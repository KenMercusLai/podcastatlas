# 郴县 / Chen County (Changsha)

郴县 / Chen County (Changsha) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-143-xiangyu-shi-ruhe-feijin-xinji-nongsi-chuhuaiwang-mixin-lstfzfvbd2h9i39f7y5dxrbxxwv2|Hanji 143]] as the place where [[XiangYu|项羽]] sends [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]] after virtually honoring him as 義帝. The page uses a Changsha suffix to avoid collision with other Chen/Cheng place names in the wiki.

In the episode's reading, the relocation is not neutral geography. Xiang Yu argues that ancient emperors should possess broad land and live upstream, but the move to [[Changsha|长沙]] commandery's Chen County actually strips Mi Xin of the immediate political field. The title rises while practical power falls.
