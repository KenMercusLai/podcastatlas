# 孙小红（古龙人物） / Sun Xiaohong (Gu Long character)

## Overview

孙小红 is 天机老人的 granddaughter and [[LiXunhuan|李寻欢]]'s later romantic counterpart in [[SentimentalSwordsmanRuthlessSword|《多情剑客无情剑》]]. The bounded episode values her as a bright, decisive presence who offers the novel and Li a path beyond prolonged guilt.

## Current Profile

She is characterized through courage, optimism, loyalty, clarity of desire, and an ability to act without turning every loss into self-punishment. After her grandfather dies opposing [[ShangguanJinhongGuLong|上官金虹]], she grieves without assuming total responsibility for the outcome.

That stance makes her a deliberate contrast with both [[LinShiyin|林诗音]] and Li Xunhuan. Where their earlier relationship becomes trapped in silence, sacrifice, and retrospective guilt, Sun Xiaohong speaks and chooses more directly. The hosts infer from [[AFeiGuLong|阿飞]]'s wedding-drink remark that she and Li eventually form a couple, but the implication remains source-interpreted rather than exhaustively demonstrated here.

## Key Characteristics

- Expresses attachment directly and acts toward a chosen future.
- Combines optimism with courage and loyalty rather than naivety alone.
- Grieves family loss without converting it into limitless personal blame.
- Contrasts with Li Xunhuan's habitual guilt and self-neglect.
- Brings warmth and forward movement into the novel's oppressive later atmosphere.

## Qualifications

This profile reflects one episode's affirmative interpretation. The exact ending of the relationship, the comparison with Lin Shiyin, and the claim that Sun Xiaohong “heals” Li are literary judgments; they should not reduce either woman to a therapeutic function or erase grief after her grandfather's death.

## What Changed

- Created the profile around direct attachment, resilient grief, and movement beyond guilt.
