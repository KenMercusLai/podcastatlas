# Bain Capital

Bain Capital appears in [[fuzhuang-pinpai-a-f-xunzhao-zhongguo-hezuo-huoban-fufei-tiqian-kan-telangpu-tiewen-fuwu-shangxian-1004810677]] through the source's cited Nikkei report that it would acquire [[GongCha|Gong Cha / 共茶]] for more than $635 million. The episode says the transaction was expected to close before year-end.

In this wiki branch, Bain Capital's role is not a full private-equity profile. It is a buyer whose restaurant and consumer-chain experience is presented as potentially useful for Gong Cha's further expansion in markets such as [[Japan]] and [[SouthKorea|South Korea]].

## Source Position
- Bain Capital is treated as an operating and capital partner for a franchised beverage chain.
- The source connects the deal to chain expansion and regional store-network growth rather than to financial engineering in detail.
- The acquisition extends [[FranchiseLedConsumerChainExpansion]] by showing how private-equity ownership can sit above local operators and store networks.
