# Haley Stevens

Haley Stevens appears in [[socialist-mobility-a-telling-michigan-vote-6a74600ec3e1347e8b5bf216]] as the establishment-backed Democrat defeated by [[AbdulElSayed]] in Michigan's Senate primary. The episode describes her as a four-term member of the House of Representatives.

Her role in the wiki is comparative: Stevens marks the party-establishment side of the [[DemocraticParty]] primary, while El-Sayed's win becomes evidence for [[StatewideLeftPrimaryTest]] and possible [[PrimaryElectorateDistortion]].
