# Order of the Golden Fleece

## Overview

The Order of the Golden Fleece is the chivalric order founded by Philip the Good to bind elite status to Burgundian court identity and project standing comparable to kings.

## Current Profile

The episode interprets the order as more than ceremonial decoration. Founding a prestigious chivalric body allowed [[PhilipTheGood|Philip the Good]], who lacked a royal crown, to stage himself beside the kings of England and France while organizing loyalty around his court.

The order outlived the independent Burgundian project. It passed through the [[HabsburgDynasty|Habsburg inheritance]], demonstrating how Burgundian symbols survived the territorial division that followed Charles the Bold's death.

## Key Characteristics

- Burgundian chivalric order founded by Philip the Good.
- Instrument of elite affiliation and court hierarchy.
- Symbolic claim to status normally associated with kingship.
- Element of Burgundian spectacle and dynastic representation.
- Institution transmitted into the Habsburg inheritance.

## Qualifications

The source emphasizes symbolic politics rather than the order's statutes, membership rules, religious functions, or later institutional divisions. Contemporary membership examples mentioned in the episode are not independently audited here.
