# 《切尔诺贝利的午夜》 / Midnight in Chernobyl

[[MidnightInChernobyl|《切尔诺贝利的午夜》]] is the nonfiction work discussed in [[04-qieernuobeili-de-wuye-woyao-jin-jianyu-le-530594843]]. The [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] episode values it for its restrained tone, technical reconstruction, survivor interviews, and ability to make the [[ChernobylNuclearPowerPlant|Chernobyl nuclear disaster]] feel like a system rather than a morality play about one bad operator.

The book's role in the source is explanatory. It helps the hosts connect [[Pripyat]]'s construction, [[ViktorBryukhanov]]'s managerial position, [[AnatolyDyatlov]]'s control-room authority, [[ValeryLegasov]]'s later truth burden, reactor design details, rescue improvisation, and public secrecy into [[TechnologicalDisasterAsInstitutionalFailure]].

The episode contrasts it with [[VoicesFromChernobyl|《切尔诺贝利的悲鸣》]]. That comparison makes the book a case for technical nonfiction that remains human-centered without relying mainly on grief testimony.
