# Feuillant Club

## Overview

The Feuillant Club was the constitutional-monarchist faction formed when [[AntoineBarnave|Barnave]] and allied deputies left the [[JacobinClub|Jacobin Club]] during the July 1791 crisis.

## Current Profile

The source presents the Feuillants as revolutionaries who believed the Revolution should now be completed, contained, and stabilized through a limited monarchy. They supported retaining [[LouisXVI|Louis XVI]], restricting political participation through property thresholds, and treating the [[ChampDeMarsMassacre|Champ de Mars massacre]] as evidence that radical disorder required repression. Their project remained dependent on a royal family secretly seeking foreign aid and planning to escape the settlement.

## Key Characteristics

- Constitutional-monarchist secession from the Jacobins on 16 July 1791.
- Led by Barnave and Assembly allies seeking an end to revolutionary escalation.
- Supportive of meaningful royal executive and veto powers.
- Associated with property-weighted citizenship and economic liberalism.
- Short-term beneficiary of repression after the Champ de Mars gathering.
- Politically exposed by the crown's continuing duplicity.

- [[503-the-french-revolution-bloodbath-in-paris-part-1-glt8447202194]] shows the Feuillants interpreting the massacre as restored order while the royal couple privately undermined the intended settlement.

## Qualifications

- The source emphasizes Barnave and does not provide a complete institutional history or membership map.
- Constitutional moderation did not mean restoration of absolutism, but neither did it offer universal political participation.
- The faction's confidence after Champ de Mars did not translate into durable legitimacy.

## What Changed

- Establishes the Feuillants as the organized constitutional-monarchist response to Varennes and republican mobilization.

## Relationships

- [[AntoineBarnave]] - leading strategist of the faction's settlement.
- [[JacobinClub]] - parent club from which Feuillant deputies seceded.
- [[CordeliersClub]] - radical republican opponent in the July crisis.
- [[LouisXVI]] - monarch whose constitutional role the faction preserved.
- [[ChampDeMarsMassacre]] - repression the faction treated as defeat of disorder.
