# 毛遂 / Mao Sui

毛遂 / Mao Sui enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-1-lvqbmaabsvbdz2zibgmseidx5phe]] as a [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] retainer during [[ZhaoState|赵国]]'s post-[[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] emergency. With [[QinState|秦国]] pressing [[Handan|邯郸]], [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] sends Pingyuanjun to seek aid from [[ChuState|楚国]].

The episode says Pingyuanjun wants twenty retainers with both literary and martial ability, but can only select nineteen. Mao Sui volunteers himself for the mission. Pingyuanjun initially doubts him because Mao Sui has been in the household for three years without visible reputation.

Mao Sui answers with the awl-in-a-bag image: if he had already been placed in the bag, the point would not merely show but pierce through. In this source, Mao Sui is therefore less a completed hero than a latent retainer demanding the chance to be tested, and his exchange becomes the source for [[MaoSuiSelfRecommendation|毛遂自荐]] and "脱颖而出."
