# Kevin Rudd

## Overview

Kevin Rudd was an Australian Labor prime minister and former diplomat whose financial-crisis response and two nonconsecutive periods in office place him at the beginning of the modern leadership-spill cycle.

## Current Profile

The source presents Rudd as an intellectually impressive Mandarin speaker with diplomatic experience and specialist knowledge of [[China]]. His government used fiscal stimulus during the 2008 financial crisis, and the episode credits [[Australia]] with avoiding the recession experienced by many industrialized economies. Yet his political profile is dominated by internal removal: deputy [[JuliaGillard]] displaced him in 2010, and Rudd later returned to displace Gillard before losing the general election to [[TonyAbbott]].

## Key Characteristics

- Former diplomat and Mandarin speaker with China expertise.
- Led Labor to power after John Howard's long government.
- Associated with fiscal stimulus during the global financial crisis.
- Removed by his deputy Julia Gillard in 2010.
- Returned to the premiership by defeating Gillard in 2013.
- Lost the subsequent election to Tony Abbott.

The episode offers little detail on the stimulus design, Australia's commodity exposure, monetary policy, or the internal causes of Rudd's removal. Causal credit for avoiding recession and judgments about his personal impressiveness therefore remain source-scoped.
