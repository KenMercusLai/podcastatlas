# George Monck

## Overview

George Monck was the general commanding the army in Scotland whose march toward London reopened Parliament and helped create the path to the Restoration.

## Current Profile

Monck initially acted against an army junta after troops had again expelled Parliament. The episode stresses that he did not begin as a straightforward royalist restorer: he stated a parliamentary aim, refused to open messages from [[CharlesIIOfEngland|Charles II]], and believed the old royal settlement had ended.

His later movement toward restoration emerged through unfolding choices rather than a fixed original plan. [[AnneMonck]] is presented as a close political partner who first urged intervention for Parliament and later encouraged consideration of the king's return.

## Key Characteristics

- General commanding forces in Scotland during the republican collapse.
- Marched on London to reverse army-junta rule.
- Initially framed his intervention as restoration of Parliament.
- Did not begin with an openly royalist program.
- Worked in close political partnership with Anne Monck.
