# Jay Bhattacharya

## Overview
Jay Bhattacharya appears in the All-In corpus as a public-health and NIH reform figure. [[MartyMakary|Marty Makary]] names him in a COVID-era school-reopening and NIH-priority context, while [[MichaelKratsios|Michael Kratsios]] later cites him as an NIH director source during the discussion of researcher age and institutional stagnation.

## Current Profile
Bhattacharya's wiki profile is source-scoped and reform-oriented. He is used as a contrast to COVID-era institutional dogma and as a figure tied to changing NIH priorities, but the corpus does not yet provide an independent biography or a full account of his policy record.

## Key Characteristics
- Bhattacharya is associated with COVID-era dissent and school-reopening arguments in Makary's account.
- He is linked to NIH reform, especially a shift toward root-cause public-health research.
- The Kratsios source uses him as an internal reform reference for a claim about the age profile of NIH intramural researchers.
- His page connects medical trust repair to federal science-funding reform.
