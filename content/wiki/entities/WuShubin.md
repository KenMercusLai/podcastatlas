# 吴淑彬 / Wu Shubin

吴淑彬 / Wu Shubin is a [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] speaker in [[ep238-2025-nian-ni-wan-guo-zenyang-de-xiaozhong-yundong-lpaw-kela5vk6avl-s11h-yexwlg]]. The source spells the name 吴淑彬, so this page keeps it separate from [[WuShubing|吴淑冰]] unless a later source resolves the spelling as the same person.

Her parts of the episode explain water-adjacent [[UrbanNicheSports|城市小众运动]], especially [[MermaidFreediving|美人鱼潜水]] and [[StandUpPaddleboarding|桨板]]. The mermaid segment shows how a freediving branch can become a course, certificate, photography, and equipment market; the paddleboarding segment shows how portable gear and open urban waterways can make a water sport feel less remote than sailing.

[[ep244-jizhe-yanzhong-de-hao-gongzuo-shenme-yang-lk8iz9cllzx2jou5-sw9yt2ogpyb]] adds her journalism-career reflection. 吴淑彬 describes leaving an institutionally stable first media job because hierarchy, miscellaneous work, and meaningless content were draining; she also says three years of social news were her most exhausting period, while Sanlian's relative respect for rest keeps [[MediaWorkAsLifestyle|media work as lifestyle]] viable.
