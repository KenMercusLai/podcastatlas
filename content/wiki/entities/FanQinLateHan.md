# 繁钦 / Fan Qin (late Han)

## Overview
繁钦 / Fan Qin is the talented Yingchuan refugee in Hanji 1022 whose early visibility before Liu Biao becomes the episode's warning case for premature self-display.

## Current Profile
Fan Qin appears with [[DuXiLateHan|杜袭]] and [[ZhaoYanLateHan|赵俨]] after they flee to Liu Biao's Jingzhou. His talent earns Liu Biao's appreciation, but that success is exactly what Du Xi treats as dangerous: a refugee preserving himself should not keep displaying ability before a ruler the group has not chosen as its final patron. The source later sketches Fan Qin as a literary figure in Cao Cao's orbit, but the current wiki role is mainly as the foil that makes concealed timing visible.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents conspicuous talent inside a politically uncertain refuge.
- Shows how hospitality and praise can tempt a displaced actor into premature alignment.
- Accepts Du Xi's warning, making restraint a chosen discipline rather than a lack of ability.
- Carries a source-scoped later literary identity tied to Cao-side service and writing.
