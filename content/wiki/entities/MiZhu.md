# 糜竺 / Mi Zhu

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]] adds the first Xuzhou handoff scene. [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] tells Mi Zhu that only [[LiuBei|刘备]] can protect [[Xuzhou|徐州]], and after Tao Qian dies Mi Zhu brings officials and people to invite Liu Bei. The episode later says Mi Zhu and [[ChenDeng|陈登]] provide substantial money after Liu Bei accepts.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-982-ba-yi-ba-liubei-ru-xu-beihou-de-liyi-zhi-zheng-ljajdc95xtylhkn5teefz26yyyz1|Hanji 982]] adds Mi Zhu's earlier role in supporting [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s entry into [[Xuzhou|徐州]]. The episode reads him as a Donghai economic actor whose family wealth, salt-and-iron interests, and local status make Liu Bei attractive: a governor who needs Mi Zhu's funding can protect Mi-family influence rather than absorb it like [[YuanShu|袁术]] might.

糜竺 / Mi Zhu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu|Hanji 999]] as the Donghai figure who uses family wealth to support [[LiuBei|刘备]] after the loss of [[Xiapi|下邳]]. The episode places his assistance amid famine, army dispersal, and Liu Bei's defeat by [[YuanShu|袁术]], so the money is not decorative patronage but emergency survival capital.

Mi Zhu's source role contrasts with [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s turn against Liu Bei under [[GrainLeverageAllianceBetrayal|粮食诱导式同盟背叛]]. One actor exploits scarcity to seize a city; Mi Zhu absorbs personal cost to keep Liu Bei's damaged organization alive long enough to submit, regroup, and later operate from [[PeiCounty|小沛]].
