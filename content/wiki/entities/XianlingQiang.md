# 先零羌 / Xianling Qiang

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] gives the Xianling Qiang an earlier uprising role before the Ji County branch. The source says Xianling Qiang and related groups kill [[LingZhengLateHan|伶征]], elect [[BeigongBoyuLateHan|北宫伯玉]] and [[LiWenhouLateHan|李文侯]], and pull [[BianZhangLateHan|边章]] plus [[HanSui|韩遂]] into military-political management.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-930-ruhe-pochu-tanxin-he-buganxin-lkyysh5jbgxwjq8ayrfar4gsjdcd|Hanji 930]] uses the Xianling Qiang as part of the western disorder surrounding [[BianZhangLateHan|边章]] and [[GaiXunLateHan|盖勋]]. The source says Bian Zhang had been coerced and was handling military-political affairs for the Xianling Qiang side when Gai Xun rebuked him at Ji County.

The page is narrow because the source does not provide an independent ethnic or frontier history of the Xianling Qiang. Its wiki role here is to mark the Qiang-front environment in which Bian Zhang's rebellion and Gai Xun's reputation become visible.
