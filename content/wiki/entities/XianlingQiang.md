# 先零羌 / Xianling Qiang

## Overview

The Xianling Qiang are a late-Han Qiang group whose current wiki evidence now spans a 160-161 CE attack and surrender field, a 168 CE battlefield defeat at [[FengyishanBattleLateHan|冯翊山之战]], and a later 184 CE western uprising role around [[BianZhangLateHan|边章]], [[HanSui|韩遂]], [[BeigongBoyuLateHan|北宫伯玉]], and [[LiWenhouLateHan|李文侯]].

## Current Profile

The page stays source-bounded rather than treating the Xianling Qiang as a full ethnic history. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-855-ni-you-duo-wuqing-jiu-you-duo-qiangda-lulbpnufi8xfijrviufo6biuy-h1|Hanji 855]] gives the earliest current layer: Xianling Qiang, [[ShendiQiang|沈氐羌]], and other Qiang groups attack Bingzhou and [[LiangzhouLateHan|凉州]], prompting the court to send [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]] with the Five Camps. After [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]] is appointed zhonglangjiang and defeats Qiang forces, Xianling and other Qiang groups surrender in large numbers because of his prestige and trustworthiness.

Hanji 881 places Xianling Qiang forces at Fengyishan, where Duan Jiong defeats them during the wider Eastern Qiang campaign context and the source reports more than 8,000 killed. This layer links the name to Duan's hard-line frontier profile after the earlier Hanji 855 command-failure and surrender episode.

Hanji 929 and Hanji 930 then show a later western disorder role. Hanji 929 says Xianling Qiang and related groups kill [[LingZhengLateHan|伶征]], elect Beigong Boyu and Li Wenhou, and pull Bian Zhang plus Han Sui into military-political management. Hanji 930 uses the Xianling Qiang as part of the Ji County field: Bian Zhang had been coerced and was handling affairs for the Xianling Qiang side when [[GaiXunLateHan|盖勋]] rebuked him.

## Key Characteristics

- Named Qiang group appearing in multiple late-Han frontier episodes.
- Earliest current layer joins Shendi Qiang and other Qiang groups attacking Bingzhou and Liangzhou.
- Appears in Huangfu Gui's 160-161 CE victory and mass-surrender field.
- Later battlefield opponent of Duan Jiong at Fengyishan in 168 CE.
- Later participant in the western Liangzhou uprising field by 184 CE.
- Able in the later sources to draw Han-side figures such as Bian Zhang and Han Sui into rebel military-political management.

## Evidence

- Initial Huan-era attack field: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-855-ni-you-duo-wuqing-jiu-you-duo-qiangda-lulbpnufi8xfijrviufo6biuy-h1|Hanji 855]] says Xianling Qiang, Shendi Qiang, and other Qiang groups attack Bingzhou and Liangzhou, prompting Duan Jiong's deployment.
- Huangfu Gui surrender field: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-855-ni-you-duo-wuqing-jiu-you-duo-qiangda-lulbpnufi8xfijrviufo6biuy-h1|Hanji 855]] says Xianling and other Qiang groups are moved by Huangfu Gui's prestige and trustworthiness, leading to 110,000 people surrendering to him.
- Early battlefield appearance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-881-shi-shui-zai-fa-zhanzheng-hengcai-lipglslijnbp0cuqfpps88q5wit6|Hanji 881]] says Duan Jiong meets Xianling Qiang forces at Fengyishan, arranges his army, attacks, and reports more than 8,000 Qiang killed.
- Later uprising formation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] says Xianling Qiang and related groups kill Ling Zheng, elect Beigong Boyu and Li Wenhou, and coerce or pull Bian Zhang plus Han Sui into their command field.
- Ji County political-military field: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-930-ruhe-pochu-tanxin-he-buganxin-lkyysh5jbgxwjq8ayrfar4gsjdcd|Hanji 930]] says Bian Zhang had been coerced and was handling affairs for the Xianling Qiang side when Gai Xun rebuked him.

## Qualifications

Hanji 855's attack and surrender sequence, Hanji 881's Fengyishan battle, Hanji 929's uprising formation, and Hanji 930's Ji County branch are related by the Xianling Qiang name but should not be compressed into a single continuous organization unless later sources supply that continuity. The page also keeps [[DongqiangLateHan|东羌]] and [[ShendiQiang|沈氐羌]] as related but distinct pages: the wiki should avoid merging all Qiang-frontier labels into one undifferentiated actor.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 855 as the earliest current layer: attack with Shendi Qiang, then surrender field under Huangfu Gui.
- Reframed Hanji 881's Fengyishan battle as a later Duan Jiong layer rather than the first appearance.
- Clarified that Xianling Qiang, Shendi Qiang, Eastern Qiang, and later western Liangzhou rebel fields should remain analytically separated unless a source explicitly connects them.
