# 先零羌 / Xianling Qiang

## Overview

The Xianling Qiang are a late-Han Qiang group whose current wiki evidence now spans an earlier 168 CE battlefield defeat at [[FengyishanBattleLateHan|冯翊山之战]] and a later 184 CE western uprising role around [[BianZhangLateHan|边章]], [[HanSui|韩遂]], [[BeigongBoyuLateHan|北宫伯玉]], and [[LiWenhouLateHan|李文侯]].

## Current Profile

The page stays source-bounded rather than treating the Xianling Qiang as a full ethnic history. Hanji 881 places Xianling Qiang forces at Fengyishan, where [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]] defeats them during the wider Eastern Qiang campaign context and the source reports more than 8,000 killed. That appearance is earlier than the Liangzhou uprising branch and links the name to Duan's hard-line frontier profile.

Hanji 929 and Hanji 930 then show a later western disorder role. Hanji 929 says Xianling Qiang and related groups kill [[LingZhengLateHan|伶征]], elect Beigong Boyu and Li Wenhou, and pull Bian Zhang plus Han Sui into military-political management. Hanji 930 uses the Xianling Qiang as part of the Ji County field: Bian Zhang had been coerced and was handling affairs for the Xianling Qiang side when [[GaiXunLateHan|盖勋]] rebuked him.

## Key Characteristics

- Named Qiang group appearing in multiple late-Han frontier episodes.
- Early battlefield opponent of Duan Jiong at Fengyishan in 168 CE.
- Later participant in the western Liangzhou uprising field by 184 CE.
- Able in the later sources to draw Han-side figures such as Bian Zhang and Han Sui into rebel military-political management.
- Evidence should remain separated by episode and date because the sources do not provide a continuous Xianling Qiang institutional history.

## Evidence

- Early battlefield appearance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-881-shi-shui-zai-fa-zhanzheng-hengcai-lipglslijnbp0cuqfpps88q5wit6|Hanji 881]] says Duan Jiong meets Xianling Qiang forces at Fengyishan, arranges his army, attacks, and reports more than 8,000 Qiang killed.
- Later uprising formation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] says Xianling Qiang and related groups kill Ling Zheng, elect Beigong Boyu and Li Wenhou, and coerce or pull Bian Zhang plus Han Sui into their command field.
- Ji County political-military field: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-930-ruhe-pochu-tanxin-he-buganxin-lkyysh5jbgxwjq8ayrfar4gsjdcd|Hanji 930]] says Bian Zhang had been coerced and was handling affairs for the Xianling Qiang side when Gai Xun rebuked him.

## Qualifications

Hanji 881's Fengyishan battle, Hanji 929's uprising formation, and Hanji 930's Ji County branch are related by the Xianling Qiang name but should not be compressed into a single continuous organization unless later sources supply that continuity. The page also keeps [[DongqiangLateHan|东羌]] as a related but distinct page: Hanji 881 links the Fengyishan episode to Eastern Qiang pacification, but the wiki should avoid merging all Qiang-frontier labels into one undifferentiated actor.
