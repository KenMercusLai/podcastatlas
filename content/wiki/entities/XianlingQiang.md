## Overview

先零羌 / Xianling Qiang is a late-Han Qiang group whose current wiki evidence now stretches from a brief pre-Hanji 786 Baozhong attack through a 113 CE Anding campaign notice, a 116 CE Dingxi defeat, and later Huan- and Ling-era appearances in 160-161, 168, and 184 CE. The page remains source-bounded and does not treat every appearance as proof of one uninterrupted command organization.

## Current Profile

Hanji 784 now supplies the earliest current layer. The source says Xianling Qiang forces again attack Baozhong, and Hanzhong administrator Zheng Qin ignores Duan Chong's advice to hold defensively until the enemy's momentum weakens. Zheng Qin attacks, the Han force is defeated, and Wang Zong plus Yuan Zhan die trying to protect him. The notice makes the group part of the same early Deng-regency frontier field as the Southern Xiongnu surrender and Yu Xu's Liangzhou argument.

Hanji 786 gives the next current layer. In 113 CE, Hou Ba and [[MaXianLateHan|Ma Xian]] attack a Xianling Qiang branch in [[AndingCommandery|Anding]]. The notice is brief, but it places the group inside the early An-era Deng-regency war field before the better-developed 116 CE Dingxi layer.

Hanji 789 adds the next campaign layer. During the Deng-regency Qiang crisis, [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]] defeats Xianling Qiang forces near Dingxi while other Han commanders attack [[LinChangQiang|Lingchang / Lin Chang]] and [[DuJigongLateHan|Du Jigong]]. This places the name inside the long northwest-war background before the later Huan/Ling-era material.

Hanji 855 gives the next major layer: Xianling Qiang, [[ShendiQiang|Shendi Qiang]], and other Qiang groups attack Bingzhou and [[LiangzhouLateHan|Liangzhou]], prompting the court to send [[DuanJiongLateHan|Duan Jiong]] with the Five Camps. After [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|Huangfu Gui]] is appointed zhonglangjiang and defeats Qiang forces, Xianling and other Qiang groups surrender in large numbers because of his prestige and trustworthiness.

Hanji 881 places Xianling Qiang forces at [[FengyishanBattleLateHan|Fengyishan]], where Duan Jiong defeats them during the wider Eastern Qiang campaign context and the source reports more than 8,000 killed. Hanji 929 and Hanji 930 then show a later western disorder role: Xianling Qiang and related groups kill Ling Zheng, elect Beigong Boyu and Li Wenhou, and pull [[BianZhangLateHan|Bian Zhang]] plus [[HanSui|Han Sui]] into military-political management around Ji County.

## Key Characteristics

- Recurrent Qiang group name across several late-Han frontier crises rather than a fully reconstructed continuous polity.
- Earliest current appearance is a brief Baozhong attack and Han defeat notice.
- Appears in both battlefield defeat contexts and surrender or leadership-selection contexts.
- In the early Deng-regency layers, functions as one branch of wider Qiang pressure that also affects Liangzhou policy and Southern Xiongnu captive movement.
- In 160-168 CE, appears in the Huan-era command-failure, surrender, and Duan Jiong hard-line suppression field.
- In 184 CE, helps form the western uprising environment that draws in Bian Zhang and Han Sui.

## Qualifications

The sources are separated by decades and political contexts. The page treats "Xianling Qiang" as a recurring group label in the wiki evidence, not as a fully continuous leadership biography. Hanji 784, Hanji 786, and Hanji 789 also arrive through podcast summaries with name, place, and chronology caution, so the early An-regency layers should be corroborated by future notes before becoming more detailed.

## What Changed
