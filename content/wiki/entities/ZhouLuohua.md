# Zhou Luohua / 周洛华

Zhou Luohua / 周洛华 is the guest in [[jifa-dongwu-jingshen-chuangzao-gengduo-jihui-lndyfdmtgo-9l5cr8oy82t1xnlfe]], where he discusses the revised [[BitStandardBook|《比特本位》]] and the unfinished or revisable ambitions behind [[CurrencyOriginBook|《货币起源》]]. The episode presents him as a finance and monetary-history thinker using Bitcoin, gold, fiat money, AI, capital markets, health care, and Marxist production relations as one connected argument about risk and opportunity.

His source role is not ordinary investment advice. He explicitly avoids giving trading recommendations and instead argues that investors and societies should look at asset-side opportunity, risk structure, and governance before treating debt, deflation, or market losses as only balance-sheet or price problems.

## Key Claims
- Zhou reframes [[Bitcoin]] as a monetary-system and production-relations problem rather than only a digital asset.
- He argues that [[RiskCostSeparation|risk and cost must be separated]], because lowering cost without lowering risk may reduce the willingness to invest or experiment.
- He treats [[OpportunityGeneratingDecentralization|decentralization]] as an opportunity-creating mechanism and centralization as an order-creating mechanism, not as simple good versus bad.
- He argues that AI raises the question of human value: useful deployment depends on institutions that make protecting, explaining to, and caring for people valuable work.
