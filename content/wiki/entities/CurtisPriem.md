# Curtis Priem

## Overview
Curtis Priem is a co-founder of [[Nvidia]] who, with [[ChrisMalachowsky|Chris Malachowsky]], approached [[JensenHuang|Jensen Huang]] about building affordable three-dimensional graphics for personal computers.

## Current Profile
The bounded source places Priem in Nvidia's 1993 founding team and Huang's collective account of the company's achievements. It does not provide enough evidence to characterize Priem's later work independently.

## Key Characteristics
- Co-founded Nvidia with Huang and Malachowsky.
- Helped originate the personal-computer 3D-graphics opportunity described in the episode.
- Appears as part of the founding team rather than as a separately interviewed participant.
