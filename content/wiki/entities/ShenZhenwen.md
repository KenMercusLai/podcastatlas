# 沈真文 / Shen Zhenwen

沈真文 / Shen Zhenwen is the second-generation successor at [[Fadior|法迪奥]] in [[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw]]. [[LiXiaojie|李小杰]] describes her as having studied through a dual-degree program involving Sun Yat-sen University and a British school before returning to the family company in 2018.

Her path makes the source's [[SecondGenerationBusinessEntryRoutes]] concrete. She begins with after-sales customer service, then works as chairman assistant, then takes over marketing after the 2020 pandemic. The episode presents her as more interested in expression, brand, and dealer-facing work than in workshop technology, which fits the company's division of labor with her brother.

## Source Position
- Shen Zhenwen is used to challenge the idea that [[FactorySecondGeneration|厂二代]] simply step into power without apprenticeship.
- Her marketing role shows why [[ManufacturingSalesShift]] gives successors a plausible entry point: she can work on dealers, live-streaming, exhibitions, and brand communication.
- Her later willingness to stay depends on finding a role that feels effective and personally meaningful.
- Her comments about not forcing her daughter to inherit the business make [[FamilyBusinessWorkLifeBoundary]] and softer third-generation expectations visible.
