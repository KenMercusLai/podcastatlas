# Georgetown University

## Overview

Georgetown University is the institution whose tennis recruitment, admissions process, and fundraising research formed the setting for [[USVKhoury|U.S. v. Khoury]].

## Current Profile

In the bounded episodes, Georgetown appears both as the institution allegedly deceived by an admissions scheme and as an organization whose athletics development staff discussed prospective recruits' wealth and later researched admitted students' donation potential. Smith's emails were used at trial; a later Salesforce report reportedly placed the Khoury family's giving capacity at $1–5 million but was not shown to the jury because no witness authenticated it.

## Key Characteristics

- Admitted [[CatherineKhoury]] through a process connected to tennis coach Gordie Ernst.
- Employed athletics fundraiser [[BrendaSmithGeorgetown]], whose emails discussed property values, affluent prospects, and donor potential.
- Maintained Salesforce records on wealth, donation potential, institutional ties, and fundraiser contacts.
- Treated athletic connections and having a child at Georgetown as indicators of possible future giving.
- Assessed the Khoury family's giving capacity after Catherine's admission.

## Qualifications

Wealth-focused emails and donor research do not by themselves establish that Georgetown authorized the coach's payment, traded admission for donations, or knew of the alleged scheme. The episodes give limited space to Georgetown's complete response and are not a university policy archive or trial record.
