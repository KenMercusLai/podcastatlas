# Georgetown University

## Overview

Georgetown University is the institution whose tennis recruitment, admissions process, and fundraising research formed the setting for [[USVKhoury|U.S. v. Khoury]].

## Current Profile

In the bounded episode, Georgetown appears both as the institution allegedly deceived by an admissions scheme and as an organization that researched admitted students' family wealth and donation potential. Its Salesforce report reportedly placed the Khoury family's giving capacity at $1–5 million, but the document was not shown to the jury because no witness authenticated it.

## Key Characteristics

- Admitted [[CatherineKhoury]] through a process connected to tennis coach Gordie Ernst.
- Maintained Salesforce records on wealth, donation potential, institutional ties, and fundraiser contacts.
- Treated athletic connections and having a child at Georgetown as indicators of possible future giving.
- Assessed the Khoury family's giving capacity after Catherine's admission.
- Claimed confidentiality over the report after the acquittal, according to the episode.

## Qualifications

Donor research does not by itself establish that Georgetown authorized the coach's payment, traded admission for donations, or knew of the alleged scheme. The episode gives limited space to Georgetown's complete response and is not a university policy archive or trial record.
