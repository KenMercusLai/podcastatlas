孔伷 / Kong Zhou (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-950-budong-quanli-beihou-de-renxing-bi-bai-wuyi-lu8-u78yqvvwwarsz8dlevvzchxr|Hanji 950]] as the coalition participant stationed at [[YingchuanCommandery|颍川]] during the first anti-[[DongZhuo|董卓]] deployment. The page remains narrow. Kong Zhou's value in this source is structural: he helps show that the [[AntiDongZhuoCoalition|反董卓联盟]] has many named posts but uneven military credibility and uncertain coordination.
