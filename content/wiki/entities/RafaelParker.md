# Rafael Parker

Rafael Parker appears in [[peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted-peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted]] as [[Segment]]'s first sales hire. The episode says he pushed [[PeterReinhardt]] to hire [[ChrisSurdean]] as the company's second sales representative even though Peter initially doubted whether Chris could sell to developers.

Rafael's wiki role is small but useful: he marks the point where founder-led selling became an early sales organization at Segment. The source connects him to [[FounderLedSales]] and [[SalesAsLearnableSkill]], because Peter learned from the sales team that awkward but persistent discovery could expose customer pain better than a clean feature pitch.
