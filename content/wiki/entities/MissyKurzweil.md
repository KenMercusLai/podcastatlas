# Missy Kurzweil

## Overview

Missy Kurzweil is a writer and friend of [[JJGoode]] whose experience in [[face-value-face-value|Face Value]] shows why a recognition failure can reasonably feel like rejection before an invisible perceptual difference is known.

## Current Profile

Kurzweil and Goode formed a quick friendship after sharing a crowded coffee-shop table and discovering multiple coincidences in their family lives. When she later sat elsewhere in the café, Goode appeared to look toward her and leave on two consecutive days. She wondered whether she had offended him or made his wife uncomfortable.

The misunderstanding ended when she returned to their usual table and Goode warmly asked where she had been. A later incident in which he conversed with a woman he could not identify helped make his prosopagnosia legible. Kurzweil and Goode remained close, and their families also became friends.

## Key Characteristics

- Supplies the observer's side of the episode's central recognition failure.
- Interprets repeated apparent avoidance through ordinary relationship expectations before learning about prosopagnosia.
- Updates her judgment after seeing evidence inconsistent with deliberate rejection.
- Maintains a close friendship with Goode after the explanation.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one structured summary and preserves Kurzweil's interpretation as understandable rather than irrational. The episode does not establish every detail of the encounters or imply that all apparent rejection is caused by perceptual difference.
