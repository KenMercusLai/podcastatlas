# 英布 / 黥布 / Ying Bu

英布 / 黥布 / Ying Bu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-4-jiuquangui-zhangliang-ruhe-zai-liubang-shouxia-anshen-lp6qwg3ma5hdy15g4box7pe23jav|Qinji 129-4]] as a punished Qin convict who becomes an anti-Qin military actor. The source says he is from the Liu'an region, is sentenced under Qin law, and is sent as a convict laborer to [[Lishan|骊山]].

At Lishan, Ying Bu builds ties with other convict heads and forceful men, then escapes with a group toward the Yangtze River area and becomes a bandit leader. When [[LuChenQinRebel|吕臣]] is trying to recover the Chu banner after [[ChenSheng|陈胜]]'s death, Ying Bu joins him and helps defeat Qin forces around Chen County.

The next step is local alliance. Ying Bu brings several thousand followers to [[WuRui|吴芮]], the Panyang magistrate known as Lord Pan. Wu Rui marries his daughter to Ying Bu and orders him to attack Qin, turning Ying Bu's punished-and-bandit following into a politically connected anti-Qin force.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-2-lishi-jilu-shouci-tucheng-lm4kuzdpnqn-hicyuvvb4p2wdudm|Qinji 130-2]] folds Ying Bu into [[XiangLiang|项梁]]'s larger army. After defeating Qin troops and moving east, Ying Bu hears that Xiang Liang has crossed the Huai and brings his force under Xiang Liang. This makes Ying Bu one of the armed actors whose attachment helps Xiang Liang's camp grow to roughly sixty or seventy thousand people.

The episode also preserves a fate-reading story. A physiognomist reportedly says Ying Bu will become king after being punished; after the punishment occurs, Ying Bu adopts the surname or name 黥, hoping the second half of the prediction will also come true. This page records that as the episode's legend-colored framing, not as a verified causal explanation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-134-ni-buzhidao-de-pofu-chenzhou-banben-ltnry1fva2hzplw4ft8nrfpoiolw|Qinji 134]] gives Ying Bu a decisive operational role before [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s main [[JuluBattle|巨鹿之战]] attack. Ying Bu and [[PuGeneral|蒲将军]] lead twenty thousand elite Chu troops across the Yellow River first, strike Qin support units, and break the protected corridor carrying grain from [[Aocang|敖仓]] toward [[Julu|巨鹿]].

In the episode's reading, Ying Bu's corridor attack is what makes the later [[BreakingCauldronsAndSinkingBoats|破釜沉舟]] charge coherent. It separates [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] from [[WangLiQin|王离]] and shifts Qin from a well-fed siege posture into a divided battlefield problem.
