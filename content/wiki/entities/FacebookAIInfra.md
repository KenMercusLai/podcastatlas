# Facebook AI Infra

Facebook AI Infra is the [[Meta|Facebook]] infrastructure organization [[JiaYangqing|Jia Yangqing]] discusses in [[jia-yangqing-wo-suo-jingli-de-rengongzhineng-yisi-dao-ai-dianfu-shijie-de-shunian-jubian-chuantai-shengdongjixi-s10e24-a3884ade-4669-4d5c-ab2e-f98aa580f429]]. In his account, the team turned AI from research capability into production systems supporting recommendation, NLP, computer vision, and large-scale deployment constraints.

The source makes Facebook AI Infra a production-reliability page: model quality is only part of the work when a change can create regressions, compatibility problems, or infrastructure cost at platform scale. That links it to [[AIVerification]], [[AICodingVerification]], [[AgentHarness]], and [[AIInfrastructureAsProduct]].

## Source Position
- Facebook AI Infra is framed as a bridge from research frameworks to large-platform production.
- Production AI required careful handling of regressions, compatibility, and workload diversity.
- Jia connects the organization to the Caffe2 and PyTorch era of framework evolution.
- The source treats Facebook's experience as evidence that infrastructure teams shape what AI can safely become inside products.
