## Overview

Zhang Feng / 张丰 is the Zhuo Commandery rebel in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-690-jiaoyou-bushen-you-duo-kepa-kankan-tianrong-de-jingli-lpbms8pebzdo2ldiwho1cba81njk|Hanji 690]] whose rebellion is tied to occult fraud. The source says he believes a Daoist's claim that he will become emperor, wears a stone wrapped in a multicolored bag as if it contains an imperial seal, is captured by [[JiZunEasternHan|祭遵]], and discovers only after defeat that the promised seal is not real.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Zhang Feng as a negative legitimacy case. Unlike Tian Rong's divination reversal, Zhang Feng's problem is a fraudulent object attached to imperial destiny. The Daoist's claim gives rebellion a symbolic center: if the stone contains a seal, Zhang Feng's ambition seems cosmically authorized.

Capture destroys the sign. Ji Zun attacks first and seizes Zhang Feng; when the stone is broken open, there is no seal. Zhang Feng's final recognition that he had been deceived makes the episode's lesson explicit: mystical legitimacy can redirect political action before reality testing happens.

## Key Characteristics

- Zhuo Commandery rebel in the Jianwu 4 source branch.
- Political actor whose rebellion is enabled by a Daoist's imperial-prophecy claim.
- Bearer of a fake seal object that performs legitimacy without substance.
- Captive of Ji Zun after Han forces attack.
- Negative example of occult promise replacing political and military judgment.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to the Zhuo Commandery episode and does not reconstruct Zhang Feng's earlier career, follower base, or all commanders involved in the attack. The source supports the occult-fraud reading, but the exact social spread of Zhang Feng's claim remains source-scoped.
