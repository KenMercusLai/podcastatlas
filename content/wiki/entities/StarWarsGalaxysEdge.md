## Overview

Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge is a Disney theme-park land based on [[StarWars|Star Wars]] and used by [[BobIger]] as a leading example of technology and storytelling combined in physical space.

## Current Profile

[[rihc-disneys-legacy-with-bob-iger-glt6989183827]] says Galaxy's Edge exists at Disneyland and in Florida and is the contemporary park environment Iger would especially want to show [[WaltDisney]]. Its significance in the source is not a single ride but the conversion of an established screen mythology into an inhabitable place.

The case makes [[ThemeParkAsMediaPlatform]] concrete: narrative, design, technology, movement, and visitor participation can extend franchise attachment beyond filmed media. The interview does not establish visitor satisfaction, financial return, operating costs, or critical consensus.

## Key Characteristics

- A physical extension of the Star Wars story world.
- Combines themed design and technology to support immersion.
- Presented by Iger as evidence that Disney continues Walt-era experimentation rather than only preserving old attractions.
- Functions as a park node in the wider Disney and Lucasfilm IP system.
