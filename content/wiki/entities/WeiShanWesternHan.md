## Overview

卫山 / Wei Shan is the Western Han envoy whose near-successful negotiation with [[UgeoWimanJoseon|卫右渠]] collapses when reciprocal fear prevents the Joseon crown prince and his armed following from crossing the Pei River.

## Current Profile

Han Wudi sends Wei Shan after both field commands fail to secure a quick victory. Ugeo offers horses, provisions, and his son as part of submission, but Wei Shan and [[XunZhiWesternHan|荀彘]] fear the prince's large armed escort and demand disarmament. The prince fears a trap in turn and withdraws. Wei Shan returns without completing the submission and is executed, a punishment Xun Zhi interprets as a signal for immediate offensive action.

## Key Characteristics

- Imperial envoy inserted into a stalled military campaign to obtain submission.
- Negotiator who secures an offer involving material tribute, supplies, and the ruler's son.
- Participant in a security demand that converts near-agreement into renewed resistance.
- Agent caught inside reciprocal fear rather than a simple absence of willingness to surrender.
- Executed after failure, with the punishment altering the field commander's reading of imperial intent.

## Qualifications

The source does not establish that either side's fear was fabricated or irrational: an armed escort posed a security problem, while the preceding killing of a Joseon officer gave the prince reason to fear a trap. The exact legal basis for Wei Shan's execution and the private reasoning of Han Wudi are not supplied.

## What Changed

- Created Wei Shan's profile as a failed-surrender intermediary whose punishment changes field behavior.
