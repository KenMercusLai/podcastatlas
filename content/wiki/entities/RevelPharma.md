# Revel Pharma

## Overview
Revel Pharma is an aging-focused biotechnology company named in the latest All-In source with Calico in work on an AlphaFold-enabled enzyme intended to degrade CML glycation damage.

## Current Profile
Within the current wiki evidence, Revel Pharma is a narrow AI-for-science entity. The All-In source links the company to extracellular matrix aging, CML as an advanced glycation end product, protein binding, directed evolution, and experimental testing. Its importance is as a participant in a concrete anti-aging enzyme story rather than as a full company profile.

The current profile remains source-scoped: Revel Pharma is treated as part of an early research and commercialization path where a biological mechanism, AI-assisted candidate search, and validation results still leave delivery and market endpoint uncertainty.
