# MI6 / British Secret Intelligence Service / 军情六处

[[MI6|MI6 / British Secret Intelligence Service / 军情六处]] enters the wiki through [[80-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-shang-691866649]] as the [[UnitedKingdom|British]] intelligence service that identified, cultivated, and eventually recruited [[OlegGordievsky|Oleg Gordievsky]]. The episode contrasts MI6's patient handling style with the KGB's harsher institutional discipline.

MI6's work in the source is less cinematic than procedural. Officers read defector files, compared Danish observations, used Kaplan as a trust test, held back from rushed recruitment, and then built [[OperationSunbeam]] around a human source whose value depended on not being exposed. Later, [[OperationPimlico]] made escape planning part of source protection rather than a separate afterthought.
