# Gerhard Schröder

## Overview

Gerhard Schröder appears as the German chancellor whose Third Way turn and later Russian corporate ties connect domestic economic reform to the controversy over [[NordStream]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Schröder as moving from a harder-left background toward the market-oriented Third Way associated with Blair and Clinton. Its central retrospective judgment concerns his post-office work linked to Gazprom and Rosneft, his relationship with [[VladimirPutin]], and his association with a pipeline strategy that deepened direct Russian-German energy ties while bypassing transit states.

- Social Democratic leader associated with Third Way economic politics.
- Chancellor whose economic program is described by the hosts as relatively right-wing.
