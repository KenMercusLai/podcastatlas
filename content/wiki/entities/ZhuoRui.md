# 卓瑞 / Zhuo Rui

Zhuo Rui is the [[Nvidia]] speaker in [[acc532947b65-acc532947b65]], described as working on product landing for autonomous driving, robotics, and smart-city scenarios. In the episode, he argues that Nvidia's automotive role should be understood as software, AI, and platform infrastructure rather than only graphics cards or chips.

His explanation connects [[CUDA]] and CUDA-X to the automotive stack: CUDA is the low-level way developers access GPU capability, while CUDA-X packages domain-specific SDKs and solution layers above it. For Robotaxi, he presents the platform as a three-computer system: training, simulation, and vehicle-side inference all have to work together before L4 service can scale.
