# 《缝纫机乐队》 / City of Rock

## Overview
《缝纫机乐队》 is represented as [[DapengDirector|大鹏]]'s music- and hometown-rooted second feature and the project he treats as the more deliberate beginning of his film-author identity.

## Current Profile
Unlike the opportunity-led origin of [[JianbingManFilm|《煎饼侠》]], this film is described as deliberately developed from Dapeng's band experience, musical longing, and Ji'an memory. Its lower-than-hoped box office made it a durable case of greater personal investment and perceived craft growth failing to produce a larger visible reward.

## Key Characteristics
- Film built from personal music experience and hometown feeling.
- Stronger authorial intention than the source attributes to Dapeng's debut.
- Evaluation-pressure case where improvement and effort did not scale with commercial return.

## Evidence
- Authorship - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] says Dapeng and screenwriter Su Biao actively conceived the film from music and hometown material.
- Reception gap - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] says the film was profitable at roughly RMB 460 million but did not meet Dapeng's expectation.
- Local memory - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] connects the film's large-guitar image to a later Ji'an landmark and Dapeng's company ident.

## Qualifications
The page records Dapeng's retrospective judgment and episode-attributed box-office figure; it does not establish a neutral ranking against his other films.
