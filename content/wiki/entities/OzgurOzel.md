# Ozgur Ozel

Ozgur Ozel is the former [[RepublicanPeoplesParty|CHP]] leader who founds the [[NewPartyTurkey|New Party]] in [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]]. The source says he took over the CHP in 2023 after disappointing general-election results and then led the party to its strongest local-election result in about half a century in 2024.

The episode presents Ozel's removal as the turning point in [[TurkishOppositionRealignment]]. A court ruled that the 2023 CHP congress had been rigged, returned [[KemalKilicdaroglu]] to the leadership, and prompted Ozel's supporters to reject the ruling before police stormed party headquarters. Ozel's response was to resign and build a new opposition vehicle.
