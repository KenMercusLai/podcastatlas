# Lucky Coffee / 幸运咖

Lucky Coffee / 幸运咖 appears in [[lanjian-hangtian-wancheng-zhongguo-shouci-ludi-huojian-huishou-yushu-keji-shizhi-chaoguo-3000-yi-1007302506]] as [[MixueBingcheng|蜜雪集团]]'s coffee brand moving into higher-tier city locations. The source says Lucky Coffee is preparing a larger Shenzhen store near DJI's headquarters, close to a [[LuckinCoffee|Luckin Coffee / 瑞幸咖啡]] location, with island-counter and seating design.

The episode frames Lucky Coffee's shift as a search for new growth after low-tier-city quality sites became more saturated. It also notes that Lucky Coffee's plan to keep 2026 new-store additions below 2,000 suggests that the brand is trying to reduce cannibalization while moving toward stronger urban business districts.
