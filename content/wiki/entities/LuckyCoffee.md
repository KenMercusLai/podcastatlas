# Lucky Coffee / 幸运咖

## Overview
Lucky Coffee / 幸运咖 is [[MixueBingcheng|蜜雪集团]]'s franchise-led coffee brand, built through a broad low-price network and now testing higher-tier locations and a higher price-image position.

## Current Profile
The bounded sources show a chain moving from footprint growth toward network and positioning repair. The earlier source reports more than 10,000 stores, fewer planned 2026 additions, and larger office-area formats in higher-tier cities. The new source adds why this is difficult: weaker demand after delivery-subsidy retreat, dense-store cannibalization, a 10–16 RMB price move, celebrity marketing, franchisee upgrade costs, and fresh-coffee overlap from the parent brand.

The current judgment is that slower openings and higher-tier experiments may protect store economics, but image upgrading alone cannot resolve franchisee returns or portfolio overlap. The chain must make price, product, location, and group-brand roles coherent.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates a large franchise-led coffee network under Mixue Bingcheng.
- Built its footprint around low prices and lower-tier markets.
- Is reducing opening pace as strong sites become scarcer and cannibalization risk rises.
- Is testing larger higher-tier office and industrial-park formats.
- Faces a repositioning conflict among higher prices, franchisee costs, and parent-brand coffee overlap.

## Evidence
- Scale and site shift - [[lanjian-hangtian-wancheng-zhongguo-shouci-ludi-huojian-huishou-yushu-keji-shizhi-chaoguo-3000-yi-1007302506]] reports more than 10,000 stores, fewer than 2,000 planned additions for 2026, and a larger Shenzhen office-area store near a [[LuckinCoffee|Luckin Coffee / 瑞幸咖啡]].
- Demand and density pressure - [[yingweida-qidong-dui-huangrenxun-de-diaocha-xiaofei-pinpai-jiti-kuajie-chongwu-shengyi-gkwrijiox3zoafmb9at4mvyn]] says some stores weakened after delivery subsidies receded and demand normalized, prompting tighter opening control.
- Repositioning burden - [[yingweida-qidong-dui-huangrenxun-de-diaocha-xiaofei-pinpai-jiti-kuajie-chongwu-shengyi-gkwrijiox3zoafmb9at4mvyn]] reports a 10–16 RMB price range, celebrity endorsement, and franchisee reluctance around higher upgrade costs.
- Group overlap - [[yingweida-qidong-dui-huangrenxun-de-diaocha-xiaofei-pinpai-jiti-kuajie-chongwu-shengyi-gkwrijiox3zoafmb9at4mvyn]] says Mixue Bingcheng is accelerating fresh coffee whose positioning overlaps with Lucky Coffee's earlier promise.

## Qualifications
Store count, opening plans, sales pressure, price-band timing, franchisee response, and parent-brand rollout are source-reported. The sources provide no same-store sales, closures, franchisee return distribution, cohort economics, refurbishment costs, product quality measures, or proof that group coffee is a direct substitute across markets.
