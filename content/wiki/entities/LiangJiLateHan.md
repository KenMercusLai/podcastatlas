## Overview

梁冀 / Liang Ji enters the wiki through Hanji 853 as the powerful late-Han figure whose conflict with [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] produces the Li-family disaster and whose later execution opens a narrow path for [[LiXieLateHan|李燮]] to return. The page remains source-bounded and does not attempt a full Liang-clan biography.

## Current Profile

Hanji 853 uses Liang Ji as the structural danger behind Li Gu's fall. Li Gu anticipates household catastrophe after offending Liang Ji and sends his sons away; the subsequent arrests and prison deaths make Liang Ji's power the condition against which [[LiWenjiLateHan|李文姬]] and [[WangChengLiGuStudent|王成]] act.

The same source uses Liang Ji's later death as an incomplete release valve. Li Xie can disclose his identity and return home only after Liang Ji is killed, yet Li Wenji still warns him not to criticize the Liang family because the political meaning of the earlier purge can still touch [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]].

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Han power holder whose conflict with Li Gu creates a family-extermination danger.
- Background actor whose fall changes the risk environment without making it fully safe.
- Political reference point that Li Wenji treats as still dangerous even after death.
- Source-bounded bridge from Liang-clan dominance to the Huan-era eunuch-marquis field.

## Qualifications

Hanji 853 gives Liang Ji mainly as the persecution background for Li Gu and Li Xie. The page should not be treated as a complete account of Liang Ji's offices, household, or role in Emperor Huan's accession. The raw note writes 梁继; this page normalizes the route to 梁冀 because the Li Gu and Huan-era chronology match the received late-Han sequence.
