# 闽越 / Minyue

闽越 / Minyue enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-169-pandian-liubang-dengjiqian-buweirenzhi-de-gushi-2-lqq8qhramfzii5o5icesd-uagwkc|Hanji 169 part 2]] as the southeastern polity [[LiuBang|刘邦]] recognizes when he makes [[WuzhuMinyueKing|无诸 / Wuzhu]] its king. The source places Minyue in the Minzhong region and ties it to the wider post-Yue dispersal after [[ChuState|楚国]] destroyed [[YueState|越国]].

In the episode, Minyue matters less as administrative geography than as founding recognition. Wuzhu's support for Liu Bang during the Chu-Han struggle is rewarded with royal title, so the polity becomes part of the same early [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]] settlement field as [[ChangshaKingdom|长沙国]].

The page also links Minyue to [[Baiyue|百越]] without collapsing the terms. Baiyue remains the broad southern label used across earlier Qin sources; Minyue is the more specific Yue-descended polity recognized in this Hanji 169 part 2 episode.

## Key Claims
- Minyue is introduced as a southeastern Yue-descended polity recognized by Liu Bang through Wuzhu's kingship.
- The source ties Minyue to post-Yue dispersal and to the Chu-Han incentive structure created by Xiang Yu's unrewarding settlement.
- Minyue extends the wiki's southern China branch from Qin conquest and Baiyue campaigns into early Western Han recognition politics.
