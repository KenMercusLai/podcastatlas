## Overview

杨兴 / Yang Xing enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-479-zhichang-qingshang-qianfei-kankan-xihan-jiajuanzhi-de-xiachang-lpsopbj1dtftnsn-kjjeyftst7el|Hanji 479]] as [[JiaJuanzhiWesternHan|贾捐之]]'s friend and co-schemer in a failed attempt to use praise of [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]] to gain senior office under [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]].

## Current Profile

The source gives Yang Xing a narrower profile than Jia Juanzhi. He has just met Yuan before the drinking conversation, and he encourages Jia's ambition by saying Jia would be stronger than [[WuluChongzongWesternHan|五鹿充宗]] as Shangshu Ling. Jia then promises to recommend Yang for Jingzhao Yin, turning friendly praise into a political exchange.

Yang survives where Jia dies, but the episode does not make him innocent. He participates in the joint praise of Shi Xian, in the proposal to honor Shi and place Shi's brothers in office, and in the reciprocal recommendation design. His lesser punishment reflects a weaker prior political contradiction, not a clean record.

## Key Characteristics

- Friend of Jia Juanzhi who joins the drinking-table office discussion after seeing Han Yuandi.
- Aspiring Jingzhao Yin candidate in Jia's proposed recommendation.
- Encourages Jia's Shangshu Ling ambition by comparing him favorably with Wulu Chongzong.
- Co-signer of the memorial praising Shi Xian and proposing rewards for Shi's side.
- Punished as a convict rather than executed, because the episode treats his credibility reversal as less severe than Jia's.
