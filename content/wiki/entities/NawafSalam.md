# Nawaf Salam

Nawaf Salam appears in [[hard-cedar-lebanon-seeks-trumps-help-6a5f41e97bddc0bea5542de5]] as [[Lebanon]]'s prime minister after years of caretaker government and political vacancy. The episode identifies him as a former head of the International Court of Justice and former ambassador to the [[UnitedNations|United Nations]].

The source treats Salam's outsider profile as useful but insufficient. Alongside [[JosephAoun]], he inherits the same linked problems: [[Hezbollah]]'s armed role, the ceasefire and withdrawal dispute with [[Israel]], the need for an [[InternationalMonetaryFund|IMF]] deal, and the unresolved allocation of banking-sector losses.
