## Overview

丙吉 / Bing Ji enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-454-yi-zhi-niao-ganfan-yige-chengxiang-huangba-you-duo-can-lrjgft-s8oi3yt3lrnsh0_m20sxj|Hanji 454]] as the Western Han chancellor whose death precedes [[HuangBaWesternHan|黄霸]]'s appointment. The episode uses him as a contrast figure: a minister tied to [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]'s survival and the Xiaoxuan revival who does not turn merit into public self-advertisement. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-452-xiaowangzhi-bei-jiangzhi-ni-zaizhichang-fanguo-zhezhong-cuo-ma-likmpw-97b-o3p5qaxgyyourkldn|Hanji 452]] makes that tie politically active: [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]]'s contempt toward Bing Ji offends Xuan because Bing Ji had protected and cared for the future emperor as an infant.

## Current Profile

The sources give Bing Ji a compact evaluative role rather than a full biography. They identify him as a figure who served from censorial office into chancellorship, became one of the Qilin Pavilion merit figures, and helped enable Emperor Xuan's rule without publicly boasting that he had once protected the future emperor. Hanji 452 adds why his status cannot be read only from old age or current office: he carries Xuan's survival memory, so disrespect toward him can become disrespect toward the emperor's gratitude order.

Within Hanji 454, Bing Ji matters because he sets the standard Huang Ba fails to meet. Bing Ji's quiet merit, restraint, and reputation are used to frame what a chancellor's interior steadiness can look like; Huang Ba's later search for auspicious proof and visible recognition becomes more conspicuous against that background.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han chancellor whose death opens the episode's court transition.
- Meritorious official connected to Emperor Xuan's rescue and reign legitimacy in the host's account.
- Quiet-credit contrast who does not advertise his role in Xuan's survival.
- Protected symbolic elder whose hidden relationship weight makes Xiao Wangzhi's contempt politically costly.
- Benchmark for chancellor-level steadiness before Huang Ba's central-office mismatch.

## Qualifications
