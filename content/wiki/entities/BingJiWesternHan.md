## Overview

丙吉 / Bing Ji enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-454-yi-zhi-niao-ganfan-yige-chengxiang-huangba-you-duo-can-lrjgft-s8oi3yt3lrnsh0_m20sxj|Hanji 454]] as the Western Han chancellor whose death precedes [[HuangBaWesternHan|黄霸]]'s appointment. The episode uses him as a contrast figure: a minister tied to [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]'s survival and the Xiaoxuan revival who does not turn merit into public self-advertisement.

## Current Profile

The source gives Bing Ji a compact evaluative role rather than a full biography. It identifies him as a figure who served from censorial office into chancellorship, became one of the Qilin Pavilion merit figures, and helped enable Emperor Xuan's rule without publicly boasting that he had once protected the future emperor.

Within Hanji 454, Bing Ji matters because he sets the standard Huang Ba fails to meet. Bing Ji's quiet merit, restraint, and reputation are used to frame what a chancellor's interior steadiness can look like; Huang Ba's later search for auspicious proof and visible recognition becomes more conspicuous against that background.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han chancellor whose death opens the episode's court transition.
- Meritorious official connected to Emperor Xuan's rescue and reign legitimacy in the host's account.
- Quiet-credit contrast who does not advertise his role in Xuan's survival.
- Benchmark for chancellor-level steadiness before Huang Ba's central-office mismatch.
