# 冯贵人 / Feng Guiren (late Han)

冯贵人 / Feng Guiren enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-901-jixingzi-de-ren-hui-shi-shenme-xiachang-lh1yfyjzqptxvtnv9taiqms9yjud|Hanji 901]] as the proposed alternative to [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦皇太后]] in the posthumous burial dispute around [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]. The source does not give her an independent political career; she matters because opposing officials use her as a way to deny Dou the full empress-dowager burial position.

[[ChenQiuLateHan|陈球 / 陈求]] argues that Feng Guiren lacks the same ritual claim because she did not have Dou's accession-related contribution and because her tomb had reportedly been robbed, leaving the body mixed with those of thieves. The episode's purpose is not to build a biography of Feng, but to show how alternative consort status can become a tool in [[ImperialBurialLegitimacy|帝后合葬名分]] and [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] disputes.
