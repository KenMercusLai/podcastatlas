# Cock Lane Ghost

## Overview

The Cock Lane Ghost is an eighteenth-century London haunting hoax in which mysterious knocks were interpreted as an accusation of murder.

## Current Profile

[[66-ghosts-glt2309926019]] presents the case as a scandal that escaped its domestic setting through crowds, newspapers, elite interest, and an investigation involving Dr. Johnson. What began around drink, conflict, and a joke acquired the structure of public supernatural testimony before collapsing as fraud.

Its importance is therefore less paranormal than institutional: it shows how an ambiguous sound can be converted into a message, an accusation, an attraction, and a media event.

## Key Characteristics

- Poltergeist-style knocks treated as communicative evidence.
- Murder allegation embedded in domestic scandal.
- Amplified by newspapers, crowds, and socially prominent visitors.
- Investigated rather than accepted without challenge.
- Ultimately framed by the source as a hoax that grew beyond its makers.

## Evidence

- Narrative formation - [[66-ghosts-glt2309926019]] describes knocks being interpreted as a dead woman's murder accusation.
- Public circulation - [[66-ghosts-glt2309926019]] records crowds, press attention, royal interest, and Dr. Johnson's involvement.
- Hoax boundary - [[66-ghosts-glt2309926019]] presents the affair as a fraud linked to drink, scandal, and escalation.

## Qualifications

The episode gives a compressed account rather than a documentary reconstruction. Motives, individual responsibility, press chronology, and the precise mechanics of the knocks remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Created a profile treating the case as a media-amplified hoax rather than supernatural proof.
