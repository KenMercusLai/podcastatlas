# John Jacob Astor (fur trader)

## Overview

John Jacob Astor was a fur trader and financier whose fortune illustrates how Canadian beaver pelts could be routed through Manhattan and European markets into exceptional private wealth.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Astor as the commercial counterpart to chartered-company and mapping power. He accumulated wealth by moving pelts from Canadian supply networks to New York and Europe, showing that the [[BeaverFurTrade|beaver fur trade]] linked frontier extraction to Atlantic finance and fashion demand.

## Key Characteristics

- Fur trader connected to Canadian, Manhattan, and European markets.
- Beneficiary of high demand for beaver-felt hats.
- Example of private wealth built from long-distance animal-commodity chains.
- Distinct from his descendant [[JohnJacobAstor|John Jacob Astor IV]].

## Evidence

- Trade route: [[285-canada-beaver-wars-glt6084067740]] describes Astor trading Canadian pelts through Manhattan to Europe.
- Wealth accumulation: [[285-canada-beaver-wars-glt6084067740]] presents him as the richest man in the United States at death.
- Market basis: [[285-canada-beaver-wars-glt6084067740]] links the fortune to beaver felt's valued stiffness and water resistance in hats.

## Qualifications

The modern wealth comparison and the scale of Astor's fortune remain source-scoped. The episode does not provide a full business history, distinguish every company and route, or fully trace the Indigenous labor and ecological loss underlying the trade.
