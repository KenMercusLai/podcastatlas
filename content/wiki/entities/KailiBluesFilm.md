# Kaili Blues / 《路边野餐》

## Overview
《路边野餐》 / Kaili Blues is represented as [[BiGan|毕赣]]'s first feature and the clearest source case for turning limited resources, real locations, fragmented information, music, and extended duration into a coherent authored experience.

## Current Profile
The film emerged from a small Kaili-based team without mature budgeting or production management. In Bi's account, original music licensing, real locations, route planning, nonprofessional performers, and a roughly 42-minute shot were not detachable flourishes; they carried period, place, and emotional structure. Luo Yonghao's experience of confusion followed by emotional impact and immediate rewatch supplies one favorable audience example for [[ArtFilmDeferredLegibility]], not general reception evidence.

## Key Characteristics
- Small-team first feature rooted in Kaili locations, memory, atmosphere, and improvised capacity.
- Film whose early obstacles are described as information for later emotional recombination.
- Work using an extended real-location shot to sustain movement, time, and experiential continuity.
- Production in which original recorded music is treated as part of the represented sound world.

## Evidence
- Formation and production: [[daoyan-bigan-luoyonghao-qingxing-shenke-duyiwuer-de-zaomengzhe-lhsut9wu8n73dbdyhps50ymyf9wa]] describes family financing, a small team, real locations, music licensing, route planning, and limited takes.
- Viewing method: [[daoyan-bigan-luoyonghao-qingxing-shenke-duyiwuer-de-zaomengzhe-lhsut9wu8n73dbdyhps50ymyf9wa]] records Luo's confusion-and-recombination response and Bi's defense of obstacles as film language.
- Long-take function: [[daoyan-bigan-luoyonghao-qingxing-shenke-duyiwuer-de-zaomengzhe-lhsut9wu8n73dbdyhps50ymyf9wa]] presents the extended shot as designed experience rather than a detachable technical stunt.

## Qualifications
Budget, licensing cost, shot length, number of takes, box office, festival-driven discovery, and reception claims come from the interview and remain source-scoped. One enthusiastic viewer response cannot establish how typical audiences encounter the film.

## What Changed
- Created a bounded project profile centered on resource constraint, deferred legibility, sound, and extended duration.
