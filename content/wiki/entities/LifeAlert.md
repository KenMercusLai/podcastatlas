# Life Alert

Life Alert is used in [[advice-line-with-chris-riccobono-of-untuckit-c087247f-1f85-4c15-b4aa-4ffea0368c4c]] as the positioning comparison for [[SnugSafety]]. The source frames Life Alert as an emergency-button product, while Snug Safety is described as daily reassurance and escalation when a person does not check in.

The comparison matters because it keeps Snug from treating the market as a simple head-to-head replacement problem. [[GuyRaz]] suggests Snug may be complementary, while [[ChrisRiccobono]] notes that an emergency button depends on the user being able to press it.
