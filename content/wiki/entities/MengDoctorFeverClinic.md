## Current Profile
The guest translates a high-pressure fever-clinic shift into practical public questions: how abruptly influenza can present, why an early antigen test may be negative, how blood tests and influenza screening fit the local workflow, when home observation becomes less appropriate, and why prescription antivirals, multi-ingredient cold medicines, and antibiotics should not be chosen casually. Her account is useful as situated clinical experience, not as population surveillance or a universal protocol.

## Key Characteristics

- Frames care-seeking as a balance among symptoms, duration, vulnerability, waiting-room exposure, and treatment timing.
- Emphasizes prescription boundaries and ingredient checking rather than medicine stockpiling.
- Rejects reassurance-driven antibiotic self-treatment for presumed viral illness.
