## Overview

张献忠 / Zhang Xianzhong enters the wiki through Hanji 388-4 as one named actor in the episode's explanation of late-Ming and early-Qing Sichuan devastation.

## Current Profile

The current profile is intentionally narrow. Hanji 388-4 does not give a full biography of Zhang Xianzhong; it uses "张献忠屠蜀" as part of a broader causal cluster that also includes other armies, famine, epidemic, and tiger disaster. The page therefore records his role as a symbol and source-scoped contributor in the episode's [[SichuanWarDevastation|四川战乱人口毁伤]] argument.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Ming rebellion figure named in the episode's Sichuan catastrophe narrative.
- Associated by the source with "屠蜀" and extreme civilian suffering.
- Not treated as the sole cause of Sichuan's demographic collapse in this source.
- Used as part of a multi-cause war-disaster explanation involving armies, famine, epidemic, and social breakdown.
