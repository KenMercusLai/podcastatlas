# 何森堡 / He Senbao

何森堡 / He Senbao appears in [[271-tangchao-douyao-bucunzai-le-weishenme-weishilun-hui-zai-jinnian-da-baofa-lg7-wdrdw1b9h7lwa4vwkvmyb4un]] as the science writer and debunker whose recent public arguments against [[PseudoHistoryConspiracy|伪史论]] anchor the episode. He treats pseudohistory as a self-expanding denial system: to protect one false claim, believers must reject more sources, evidence chains, and expert communities.

His source-specific contribution is methodological. He uses [[RosettaStone|罗塞塔石碑]], [[JeanFrancoisChampollion|商博良]], [[Aristotle|亚里士多德]], the [[TangDynasty|唐朝]], and broader cross-civilization contact to argue that history is not a stack of isolated texts. It is an interlocking field of documents, material traces, institutions, scripts, names, archaeology, and foreign contact.
