## Overview

于阗国 / Khotan Kingdom is a Western Regions polity whose current wiki profile now has two contrasting late-Han layers. Hanji 810 shows Khotan under Fang Qian as an aggressor against [[JumiKingdomLateHan|居弥 / 拘弥国]] and a tester of Han response; Hanji 838 shows Khotan destabilized by [[WangJingXiyuLateHan|王敬]]'s banquet killing after a Jumi accusation.

## Current Profile

The earlier layer comes from Hanji 810. Khotan king Fang Qian kills Jumi king Xing, installs his own son, and sends tribute to test the Han court's response. [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] offers pardon if Khotan returns Jumi, but Fang Qian refuses. This makes Khotan a case where local force outruns Han's recognition-backed order.

The later layer comes from Hanji 838. Former Western Regions chief clerk Zhao Ping dies in Khotan; [[JumiKingChengguoLateHan|居弥王成国]] turns that death into an accusation against [[KhotanKingJianLateHan|于阗王建]]. Wang Jing accepts the charge as an intervention opportunity and kills Jian at a banquet. Khotanese actors then reject Wang Jing's imperial-command claim, [[ShuboKhotanLateHan|输僰]] kills Wang Jing and briefly seizes kingship, the people kill Shubo, and Jian's son [[AnGuoKhotanLateHan|安国]] is installed.

The current synthesis is therefore not simply pro- or anti-Khotan. Khotan can be the coercive neighbor in one source and the victim of unverified accusation and reckless Han envoy action in another. The durable pattern is Western Regions volatility when local rivalry, king-making, and Han recognition authority are weakly aligned.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Regions kingdom whose local king can act independently against a neighboring polity.
- Polity that tests Han response by pairing tribute with refusal to reverse a seizure.
- Later target of a Han envoy's failed intervention after a Jumi accusation.
- Internal political community able to reject both an external killer and a self-enthroning general.
- Case for frontier instability produced by weak recognition, local rivalry, and coercive intervention.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 810 and Hanji 838 and does not reconstruct Khotan's broader history, geography, or later Han relations. Fang Qian remains source-scoped because the wiki has only one current note for him. The charge that Jian caused Zhao Ping's death remains an accusation from a hostile ruler unless later source notes verify it.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 810's earlier Fang Qian layer, making Khotan both a local aggressor in one source and a later destabilized polity in another.
- Preserved Hanji 838's Wang Jing intervention as the main evidence for rumor-driven frontier intervention and Khotanese internal succession repair.
