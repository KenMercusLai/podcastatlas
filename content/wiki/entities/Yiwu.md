# Yiwu

Yiwu is the Chinese open-market toy ecosystem named in [[what-makes-a-toy-go-viral]]. [[ReallyMagicalStuff|RMS]] first found the [[SquishyDumplings]] concept there before adapting it into a mystery toy and later viral retail hit.

The source uses Yiwu narrowly as a sourcing and product-discovery context rather than as a full city history. Its wiki role is to add a toy-market counterpart to [[ChenghaiToyCluster]]: instead of only asking where toys are manufactured, the episode shows how a company can browse an open product ecosystem, identify a form with potential, and then apply [[SmallIInnovation]] and [[ViralToySupplyChain]] discipline around it.
