# Carlsberg

Carlsberg appears in [[pump-and-circumstance-is-china-the-new-opec-6a7c37287cf3c58886773a66]] as the Danish beer company partnered with the Lao state in [[LaoBreweryCompany]]. The episode uses the partnership to explain how [[BeerLao]] became both a domestic staple and a growing Asian export.

The source presents the company as part of a possible foreign-investment model for [[Laos]], but the lesson is limited: Beer Lao is successful enough to be a national champion, yet the broader Lao economy still lacks many comparable job-creating industries.

[[kafeidou-chuantong-meishi-guangchang-jielian-bidian-dashidaimen-yudao-naxie-fazhan-zuai-1007530222]] adds a portfolio-shift view. The source says Carlsberg's first-half revenue grew 2.6% even as traditional beer weakened because soft drinks and alcohol-free beer offset pressure, connecting the company to [[NonAlcoholicBeerGrowth]] as a response to health-oriented lifestyles and cost-of-living pressure.
