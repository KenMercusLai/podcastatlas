# 《四库全书》 / Siku Quanshu

## Overview
《四库全书》 / Siku Quanshu appears in the [[BowuzhiPodcast|博物志]] Hangzhou episode through the discussion of [[WenlanPavilion|文澜阁]] and other imperial library pavilions.

## Current Profile
The source treats Siku Quanshu mainly as the collection and institutional background for Wenlan Pavilion. It is not analyzed as a full intellectual or political project here; its role is to make Hangzhou's book-heritage landscape more legible.

## Key Characteristics
- Connected to a named set of Qing imperial library pavilions.
- Gives [[WenlanPavilion]] its book-heritage significance in the source.
- Functions as a bridge between ancient-book exhibition discussion and Hangzhou site travel.
