# University of Science and Technology of China / 中国科学技术大学

University of Science and Technology of China / 中国科学技术大学 enters the wiki through [[no-211-huishang-wangshi-cong-qianshi-buxiu-dao-zuiniu-fengtou-1002275189]] as a key scientific base for [[Hefei]]. The episode says USTC was founded in Beijing in 1958 under the Chinese Academy of Sciences, then moved to Anhui amid the late-1960s security environment before settling in Hefei.

The source treats the relocation as painful in the short run and transformative in the long run. Equipment, books, and staff were difficult to move; faculty losses and local shortages were real. But the school's presence later gave Hefei a talent and research anchor that helped the city position itself for hard-tech industry rather than ordinary招商 alone.
