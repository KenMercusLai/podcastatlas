# Vasily Golitsyn / 瓦西里·戈利岑

## Overview

Vasily Golitsyn was [[SophiaAlekseyevna|Sophia Alekseyevna]]'s principal minister and a pre-Petrine Westernizer whose diplomatic and reform ambitions were undone politically by failed Crimean campaigns.

## Current Profile

The episode portrays Golitsyn as an educated, multilingual aristocrat interested in Western art, maps, court culture, religious toleration, military reform, and even serf emancipation. His diplomacy secured recognition of Russian control over Smolensk, Kiev, and eastern Ukraine in exchange for joining a coalition against the Ottoman Empire.

The resulting campaigns against the Crimean Khanate failed in 1687 and 1689. Golitsyn's attempt to frame the second retreat as victory sharpened conflict with [[PeterTheGreat|Peter]], weakened Sophia's regency, and ended with his loss of property and long Arctic exile after Peter's faction prevailed.

## Key Characteristics

- He represents Westernizing reform inside Sophia's government rather than only under Peter.
- His agenda joined culture, military change, religious policy, and social reform.
- Diplomacy expanded Russia's recognized western holdings while creating a costly anti-Ottoman obligation.
- Campaign failure converted foreign policy into a domestic legitimacy crisis.
- His fall followed coalition collapse rather than a direct military overthrow of the regency.

## Qualifications

The episode does not establish how close Golitsyn came to implementing religious toleration or serf emancipation, and its territorial, casualty, motive, and campaign-performance claims remain source-scoped. His Westernization should not be treated as identical to Peter's later program.
