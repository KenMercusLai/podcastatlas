# 巨鹿之战 / Battle of Julu

巨鹿之战 / Battle of Julu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-134-ni-buzhidao-de-pofu-chenzhou-banben-ltnry1fva2hzplw4ft8nrfpoiolw|Qinji 134]] as the decisive battlefield after [[XiangYu|项羽]] kills [[SongYi|宋义]] and takes practical command of the Chu relief army. [[WangLiQin|王离]] surrounds [[Julu|巨鹿]], where [[ZhaoXie|赵歇]] and [[ZhangEr|张耳]] are trapped, while [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] maintains the supply link that keeps Wang Li's force fed.

The source's main operational point is that Xiang Yu does not simply rush a stronger Qin army. He first sends [[YingBu|英布]] and [[PuGeneral|蒲将军]] with twenty thousand Chu troops to attack the Qin support corridor, whose food flow depends on [[Aocang|敖仓]] and protected yongdao works between the Yellow River/Zhang River area and Julu. Once that corridor is broken, Wang Li's besieging army is separated from Zhang Han and no longer has the same logistical advantage.

The famous [[BreakingCauldronsAndSinkingBoats|破釜沉舟]] move belongs to the second stage. After the Chu main force crosses the [[ZhangRiver|漳水]] near Julu, Xiang Yu sinks boats, destroys cooking vessels, burns the camp, and gives the troops only three days of rations. The episode reads this as a death-ground morale device that follows logistics preparation rather than replacing it.

Julu is also a coalition test. [[ChenYu|陈馀]] remains outside the city with troops but hesitates, Zhang Yan and Chen Ze's five-thousand-person attack fails completely, Zhang Ao's Dai troops do not press in, and the other lords watch from their camps while Chu fights. After Xiang Yu wins nine engagements against Qin, those same lords submit to him as coalition commander.

The battle therefore marks both a military and political turn. Militarily, Qin loses the immediate siege force around Julu; politically, Xiang Yu's personal command authority begins to outrun ordinary restored-state hierarchy. The source treats this as a decisive blow to Qin's remaining war capacity and morale.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-135-mingyun-tai-shenqi-cong-yufu-zuodao-wang-lrh1lhuxxikx4m6prpi6qm0jcafb|Qinji 135]] adds the immediate Zhao-side aftermath. Once the siege is lifted, the unresolved conflict between [[ZhangEr|张耳]] inside Julu and [[ChenYu|陈馀]] outside it does not disappear. Zhang Er's accusation, Chen Yu's thrown seal, and Zhang Er's seizure of the troops turn the battle's relief into [[CommandSealTrustRupture|将印信任破裂]] between the former allies.
