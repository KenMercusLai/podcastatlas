## Current Profile

The source's evidence for Jie Guang is concise. He appears as part of the access route through which Aidi asks for solutions, and he later becomes one of the figures [[XiaHeliangWesternHan|夏贺良]]'s group wants elevated after the ritual cure fails. Because [[LiXunWesternHan|李寻]] is the more developed technical figure in the episode, Jie Guang's current profile is source-scoped around recommendation, proximity, and later exile.

## Key Characteristics

- Belongs to Aidi's solution-seeking court environment during illness and disorder.
- Is tied to the access channel that brings Xia Heliang into imperial view.
- Becomes part of the proposed replacement governing arrangement after the renewed-mandate cure fails.
- Avoids execution but is exiled to Dunhuang with Li Xun.
