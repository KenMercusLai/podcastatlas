# 郭元 / Guo Yuan (Late Han)

## Overview
郭元 / Guo Yuan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1057-da-bu-liao-zhang-de-jiangjun-bushi-youxiu-de-shufajia-ljhy16leevjd8i-7vmuxtqm96ytg|Hanji 1057]] as a Yuan-family western commander and Zhong Yao's nephew. The source uses him to show how a proud attacking commander can become vulnerable when a regional defender has already disrupted the supporting alliance field.

## Current Profile
Guo Yuan is presented as the force sent west by [[YuanShang|袁尚]] after Guandu-era Yuan pressure shifts toward [[HedongCommandery|河东郡]] and [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]]. He links with the [[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]] and attacks [[PingyangDefenseLateHan|平阳]], but [[ZhongYao|钟繇]] predicts that his pride will make him careless and refuses to retreat. When Guo Yuan's troops are only partly across the Fen River, Zhang Ji urges an immediate strike; Guo Yuan is defeated and [[PangDe|庞德]] presents his head.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan-family western pressure actor rather than an independent long-term regional power.
- Kinship tie to Zhong Yao complicates the aftermath but does not protect him in the source's public-duty framing.
- Proud and careless commander in Zhong Yao's assessment.
- Defeated because crossing-state vulnerability turns his attack into a timing trap.
- Functions as a case where alliance disruption precedes battlefield success.

## Evidence
- Western-pressure role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1057-da-bu-liao-zhang-de-jiangjun-bushi-youxiu-de-shufajia-ljhy16leevjd8i-7vmuxtqm96ytg|Hanji 1057]] says Yuan Shang sends Guo Yuan west, links him with the Southern Xiongnu, and hopes to attach Ma Teng to the Yuan-family pressure line.
- Command judgment and defeat: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1057-da-bu-liao-zhang-de-jiangjun-bushi-youxiu-de-shufajia-ljhy16leevjd8i-7vmuxtqm96ytg|Hanji 1057]] frames Zhong Yao's refusal to abandon Pingyang around Guo Yuan's pride, then records Zhang Ji's advice to strike while the enemy is not fully across the river.
- Kinship and public duty: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1057-da-bu-liao-zhang-de-jiangjun-bushi-youxiu-de-shufajia-ljhy16leevjd8i-7vmuxtqm96ytg|Hanji 1057]] says Pang De did not know the severed head belonged to Zhong Yao's nephew, and Zhong Yao answers that Guo Yuan was a rebel against the court.
