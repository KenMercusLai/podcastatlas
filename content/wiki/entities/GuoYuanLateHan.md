# 郭援 / 郭元 / Guo Yuan (Late Han)

## Overview
郭援 / 郭元 / Guo Yuan (Late Han) is a Yuan-family western commander whose bounded wiki profile now includes both the setup of his Hedong campaign in Hanji 1056 and the defeat at [[PingyangDefenseLateHan|平阳]] in Hanji 1057. The sources use him to show how a Yuan Shang pressure line could look dangerous militarily while still depending on fragile alliance work, brittle coercion, and timing vulnerabilities.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1056 presents Guo Yuan as the force sent west by [[YuanShang|袁尚]] while the Yuan brothers are trying to withstand [[CaoCao|曹操]]. He joins [[GaoGanLateHan|高干]] and the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]], attacks [[HedongCommandery|河东郡]], and overruns the Jiang County branch guarded by [[JiaKuiLateHan|贾逵]]. His treatment of Jia Kui becomes politically costly: local elders agree to surrender only if Jia Kui is protected, officials and people resist Guo Yuan's attempt to kill or coerce him, and Jia Kui is eventually hidden in confinement until [[ZhuGongdaoLateHan|祝公道]] secretly rescues him.

Hanji 1057 completes the same western branch at Pingyang. Guo Yuan is also identified as [[ZhongYao|钟繇]]'s nephew, but the kinship does not change Zhong Yao's public-duty stance. Zhong Yao predicts that Guo Yuan's pride will make him careless and refuses to retreat. When Guo Yuan's troops are only partly across the Fen River, Zhang Ji urges an immediate strike; Guo Yuan is defeated and [[PangDe|庞德]] presents his head.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan-family western pressure actor rather than an independent long-term regional power.
- Yuan Shang-side commander whose attack depends on coordination with Gao Gan, the Southern Xiongnu, and attempted Ma Teng alignment.
- Coercive occupier in the Jiang County/Jia Kui branch, where military success fails to secure local trust.
- Kinship tie to Zhong Yao complicates the aftermath but does not protect him in the source's public-duty framing.
- Proud and careless commander in Zhong Yao's assessment.
- Defeated because crossing-state vulnerability turns his attack into a timing trap.

## Evidence
- Western-pressure setup: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1056-yuanshao-sihou-tade-erzimen-zhijie-fanmu-chengchou-lqxhhayal-srq4fm4yy-hnfu15ye|Hanji 1056]] says Yuan Shang sends Guo Yuan west to connect with Gao Gan and the Southern Xiongnu while also trying to pull Ma Teng into the pressure line.
- Hedong and Jia Kui branch: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1056-yuanshao-sihou-tade-erzimen-zhijie-fanmu-chengchou-lqxhhayal-srq4fm4yy-hnfu15ye|Hanji 1056]] says Guo Yuan attacks Hedong, takes the Jiang County position, and tries to kill or coerce Jia Kui before local people protect Jia Kui and Zhu Gongdao rescues him.
- Pingyang command judgment and defeat: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1057-da-bu-liao-zhang-de-jiangjun-bushi-youxiu-de-shufajia-ljhy16leevjd8i-7vmuxtqm96ytg|Hanji 1057]] frames Zhong Yao's refusal to abandon Pingyang around Guo Yuan's pride, then records Zhang Ji's advice to strike while the enemy is not fully across the river.
- Kinship and public duty: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1057-da-bu-liao-zhang-de-jiangjun-bushi-youxiu-de-shufajia-ljhy16leevjd8i-7vmuxtqm96ytg|Hanji 1057]] says Pang De did not know the severed head belonged to Zhong Yao's nephew, and Zhong Yao answers that Guo Yuan was a rebel against the court.

## Qualifications
The page treats 郭援 and 郭元 as the same late-Han Yuan-family western commander because Hanji 1056 and Hanji 1057 describe the same Yuan Shang-Gao Gan-Southern Xiongnu-Hedong/Pingyang campaign branch. The name-variant normalization remains source-scoped. Guo Yuan's motives, exact force scale, and the source's judgment of his pride should not be generalized beyond these notes without further evidence.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1056's setup before the Hanji 1057 Pingyang defeat.
- Renamed the page title to include both 郭援 and 郭元 as source-attested variants.
- Added Jia Kui and Zhu Gongdao as the local-defense branch that exposes the political weakness of Guo Yuan's occupation.
