# Booking Holdings

Booking Holdings appears in [[kunzai-xitong-li-de-jiudian-ni-buzhidao-de-xiecheng-longduan-lianchengshi-keji-luandun]] as one of the global OTA giants used to contextualize [[Ctrip]] and Trip.com Group. The episode does not analyze Booking in detail; it uses the company as a scale comparison.

The connection matters because the source treats OTA economics as structurally global: search, booking, inventory allocation, hotel relationships, and user trust can produce large travel platforms in multiple regions.

[[140-jiudian-jituan-weishenme-dou-rezhongyu-dazao-huiyuan-he-jifen-tixi-chuantai-yuanxingzhe-yu-suibingjiang-lj-xa3wxzjog6-8zm9nfakh6ulew]] adds Booking as the European comparison case: when hotel supply is more fragmented, an OTA can gain more bargaining power because fewer large hotel groups can pull users into [[HotelDirectBookingChannels]] at scale.
