# 惠文后 / Queen Huiwen of Qin

惠文后 / Queen Huiwen of Qin appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-60-zhongguo-di-yi-wei-bachi-chaozheng-de-taihou-jingshi-ta-ltazis9qo0dwvwmzxikk8mmpvosc]] as [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]]'s formal mother in the Qin succession purge after [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] dies. The episode says [[WeiRan|魏冉]] secretly executes her after suppressing [[YingZhuang|嬴壮]] and the associated rebellion.

The page should remain source-scoped. Her importance here is not a developed biography but the severity of the post-Wuwang purge: the new [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]] and Wei Ran power configuration removes not only rebel princes but also a senior palace figure tied to the previous order.
