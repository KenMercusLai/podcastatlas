# ByHealth / 汤臣倍健

## Overview
ByHealth / 汤臣倍健 is the Chinese supplement company the source uses as the domestic comparison point for marketing intensity, arguing that heavy selling expense is the category's real competitive axis.

## Current Profile
The episode cites the company's 2026 half-year report as showing a selling-expense ratio of 42%, at a time when revenue and profit growth are said to lag the growth of that spending. The figure is used to support the source's argument that the supplement industry has little hard technical or regulatory differentiation, so companies compete mainly through distribution and advertising, and that this competition is becoming less efficient for everyone.
