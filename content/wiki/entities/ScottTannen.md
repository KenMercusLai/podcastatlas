# Scott Tannen

## Overview
Scott Tannen is the co-founder of [[BollAndBranch|Boll & Branch]] and an adviser in [[advice-line-with-scott-tannen-of-boll-branch-and-jamie-siminoff-of-ring-2025-e64c6623-10d1-4222-85d6-22eeab736221]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Tannen as a consumer-brand operator who values founder vision, disciplined capital, clear positioning, and simple problem solving. His own experience complicates a rigid bootstrap rule: after personal debt created severe pressure, outside investment reduced family risk and made growth decisions easier to evaluate.

## Key Characteristics
- Bootstrap-first adviser who treats control as protection for a founder's product and mission.
- Pragmatic about capital when it reduces dangerous personal exposure or funds a clear growth plan.
- Brand operator who emphasizes customer language over technical product explanation.
- Warns that market education consumes margin.
- Uses simplicity as an operating discipline rather than a claim that business operations stay easy.
