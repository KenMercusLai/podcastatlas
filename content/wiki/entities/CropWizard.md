# Crop Wizard

Crop Wizard is the [[UniversityOfIllinoisUrbanaChampaign|UIUC]] project named by [[AndrewNelson]] in [[tech-20260107-0107-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260107-0107-mp-tech-pod-128]]. In the source, Nelson describes using images or descriptions of a farm situation to retrieve university research documentation from the United States.

The wiki role of Crop Wizard is narrower than a general autonomous-farming claim. It grounds [[AIFarmDecisionSupport]] as an information-retrieval and research-access layer: AI can help a farmer find relevant agronomic documentation faster, while [[HumanJudgmentUnderAI]] still decides how that information applies to a live field, crop, season, and budget.
