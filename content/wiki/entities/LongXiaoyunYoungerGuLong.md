# 龙小云（古龙人物） / Long Xiaoyun (son)

## Overview

龙小云 is the son of [[LongXiaoyunElderGuLong|龙啸云]] and [[LinShiyin|林诗音]] in [[SentimentalSwordsmanRuthlessSword|《多情剑客无情剑》]]. The route is disambiguated because his name and his father's are both commonly romanized “Long Xiaoyun.”

## Current Profile

The bounded episode describes a highly capable child who acts with cruelty while protected by household servants and parents who do not adequately recognize or constrain him. It rejects a single-cause explanation: temperament and low empathy may matter, but so do paternal moral compromise, maternal indulgence, family secrecy, and the knowledge that his mother never fully loved his father.

His wish that Li Xunhuan were his father is interpreted less as simple admiration of power than as fantasy of a father his mother could love completely. The source also reports a later-life tradition in 《圆月弯刀》 in which he removes harmful material from the 《怜花宝鉴》 and preserves healing knowledge, but this later remorse is not developed in depth.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines childhood status with significant martial ability and calculated cruelty.
- Benefits from adult protection and household participation rather than acting in isolation.
- Absorbs conflict generated by his parents' emotionally unstable marriage.
- Directs longing toward an imagined family configuration in which paternal worth and maternal love align.
- Retains the possibility of later remorse without having early harm minimized.

## Qualifications

The episode's division among temperament, empathy, parenting, household structure, and marital conflict is interpretive, not developmental evidence. It does not establish that family dysfunction caused every act, and it preserves the child's own agency. The later-life account is reported briefly through the hosts and remains source-scoped pending direct comparison with 《圆月弯刀》.
