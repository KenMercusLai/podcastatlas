# Sporting Lisbon

Sporting Lisbon appears in [[ep-13-soccer-analytics-through-the-lens-of-coaching]] as part of [[BrunoSoccerCoach|Bruno]]'s personal soccer background and as an example of Portuguese football culture and player development. Bruno says he played for Sporting Lisbon and other teams when younger before injury pushed him toward coaching and teaching.

The club also appears through the episode's discussion of [[RubenAmorim]], whom Bruno cites as a younger coach using data, staff support, and live information effectively during matches. In the source, Sporting Lisbon helps connect [[Portugal]]'s dense soccer culture with [[FootballAnalyticsModernization]] and [[CoachingIntegratedSoccerAnalytics]].
