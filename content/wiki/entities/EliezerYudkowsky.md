# Eliezer Yudkowsky

## Overview
Eliezer Yudkowsky is presented as an AI-safety writer and foundational internet-rationalist figure.

## Current Profile
The episode connects rationalist belief revision with AI alignment through Yudkowsky. It says he moved from favoring faster development of powerful AI toward warning about superintelligence after judging alignment much harder than expected. Value fragility and the orthogonality of intelligence and goals summarize that risk frame.

## Key Characteristics
- Foundational writer in online rationalist culture.
- Early contributor to AI alignment and catastrophic-risk discourse.
- Presented as changing position after revising his view of alignment difficulty.
- Emphasizes that intelligence does not guarantee benevolent goals.
