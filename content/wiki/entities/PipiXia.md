# 皮皮虾 / PipiXia

PipiXia / 皮皮虾 appears in [[no-214-xunzhao-tonglei-xiaohongshu-bilibili-yiji-wuhuabamen-de-naxie-shequ-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-26-1005977305]] as the later [[ByteDance]] humor product launched after [[NeihanDuanzi]] was permanently shut down. The source says it did not recreate Neihan Duanzi's influence.

For the wiki, PipiXia is useful as a negative community-transfer case: a company can launch a successor product, but the jokes, rituals, user identity, and trust that made the earlier community alive may not move with the app shell.
