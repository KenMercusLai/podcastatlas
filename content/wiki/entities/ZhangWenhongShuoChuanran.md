# 《张文宏说传染》

《张文宏说传染》 is the first book recommended in [[yi-fen-laizi-bingchuang-shang-de-xiangguan-shudan-598284093]]. The hosts describe it as a low-threshold, illustrated infectious-disease popularization book that can be read by ordinary families and even upper-primary-school students.

In the episode, the book covers common pathogens, disease prevention, diagnosis, treatment, flu, COVID-era concerns, skin infections, and digestive-system infections. Its wiki role is to anchor [[InfectiousDiseasePublicLiteracy]] before the episode moves into historical, political, and literary plague material.
