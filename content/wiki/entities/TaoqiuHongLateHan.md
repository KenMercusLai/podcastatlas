# 陶丘洪 / Taoqiu Hong (late Han)

陶丘洪 / Taoqiu Hong (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-941-shenchu-guanchang-ta-jing-xiang-ganfan-huangdi-liu-fqiz9pi7u6cltrn-cjmjz-r|Hanji 941]] as a possible recruit into [[WangFenLateHan|王芬]]'s failed coup. After [[CaoCao|曹操]] refuses Wang Fen, the plot is brought to [[HuaXin|华歆]] and Taoqiu Hong.

Taoqiu Hong initially wants to participate, but Hua Xin persuades him not to join. His source role is therefore the counterexample to Wang Fen's rush: he is close enough to danger to be tempted, but still removable from the plot by a better risk reading.
