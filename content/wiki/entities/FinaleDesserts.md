# Finale Desserts

Finale Desserts is the restaurant group in [[toast-aman-narang-how-a-long-wait-for-the-dinner-check-launched-a-2-billion-business-6eca81bc-b0e1-47e2-a87d-f3b243e76a97]] that helped [[Toast]] understand the breadth of restaurant operating software. [[ChrisKane]] showed the founders how many separate systems were needed for POS, payments, loans, accounting, inventory, online ordering, gift cards, payroll, and scheduling.

The source's memorable detail is that online orders were still arriving by fax in 2013. Finale Desserts therefore anchors the [[RestaurantOperatingSystem]] idea: the problem was not only checkout speed but a disconnected operating stack that vendors were not eager to integrate with a young startup.
