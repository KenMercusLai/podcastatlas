# Zanny Minton Beddoes

Zanny Minton Beddoes appears in [[the-elon-game-musks-vision-of-the-future-6a633594d19896314260e5c4]] as the interviewer who meets [[ElonMusk]] at [[Tesla]]'s Texas Gigafactory. Her stated reason for the interview is that Musk's companies sit across rockets, AI models, humanoid robots, social platforms, and other systems that could shape the future.

The source presents her stance as double-edged. She describes Musk's technological achievements as extraordinary and says he has done more to build the future than almost anyone else, but she also challenges his claims about Europe, migration, Islam, [[DepartmentOfGovernmentEfficiency|DOGE]], and [[USAID]] as inaccurate, irresponsible, or morally evasive.
