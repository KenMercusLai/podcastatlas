# 成侯邹忌 / Chenghou Zou Ji

成侯邹忌 / Chenghou Zou Ji appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-19-maling-zhi-zhan-lhbapt09x2ptdqx0uopkp8apbada]] during [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]]'s debate over whether and when to rescue [[HanState|韩国]] from [[WeiState|魏国]]. The raw transcript writes the name as "成侯周济," but the received passage is usually 成侯邹忌, so the wiki keeps Zou Ji as the indexed figure and treats the transcript wording as a source caveat.

His role in this source is compact but useful. He argues that early rescue and late rescue are both inferior to not rescuing at all, creating the negative pole in a three-way policy debate. [[TianJi|田忌]] then argues for early rescue, while [[SunBin|孙膑]] argues for [[DelayedRescueStatecraft|delayed rescue]].
