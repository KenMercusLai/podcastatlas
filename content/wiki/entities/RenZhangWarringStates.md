# 任章 / Ren Zhang (Warring States)

## Overview
Ren Zhang is the Wei adviser who persuades [[WeiJu|Wei Ju]] to satisfy [[ZhiYao|Zhi Yao]]'s unjustified territorial demand so that easy gains will deepen Zhi Yao's arrogance and align the threatened houses.

## Current Profile
Ren Zhang answers Wei Ju's instinct to refuse with an inversion: the absence of justification is why Wei should concede. A gain without resistance can make Zhi Yao feel invulnerable, while the houses he pressures acquire a common enemy. His advice is the episode's clearest formulation of “将欲败之，必姑辅之.”

## Key Characteristics
- Reframes an unjust demand through long-term adversary incentives.
- Uses tactical compliance to encourage overconfidence and carelessness.
- Anticipates coalition formation among separately coerced actors.
- Accepts immediate material loss for a possible later strategic opening.

## Evidence
- Deliberate-overconfidence advice: the episode has Ren Zhang argue that an unjustified grant will make Zhi Yao more arrogant and the threatened houses more united; Wei Ju then concedes a large city. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-02-jiangyu-baizhi-bi-gufuzhi-lglk2_eu1uvegdac8sj6bfuwv1dw|Zhouji 02]]
