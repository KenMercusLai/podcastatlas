# Robin Rombach

Robin Rombach appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-open-source-wins-agi-is-here-and-scorseses-ai-toolkit-with-ceos-of-cerebras-black-forest-labs-42029880]] as CEO of [[BlackForestLabs|Black Forest Labs]]. He explains the company's roots in [[StableDiffusion|Stable Diffusion]] and [[LatentDiffusion|latent diffusion]], then uses [[Flux]] to ground a broader argument about visual and multimodal AI.

Rombach's main synthesis is that generative media and robotics are converging through action prediction. The same model family that learns from images, video, and audio can generate media, understand scenes, predict plausible next actions, and eventually support physical-world agents.

He also presents AI as a creative medium rather than a substitute director. In the [[MartinScorsese|Scorsese]] example, the value is rapid visual communication and iteration around a mental image; in the IP-owner discussion, the value is controlled creation under rights-holder constraints.
