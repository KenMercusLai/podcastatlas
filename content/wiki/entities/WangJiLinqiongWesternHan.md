## Overview

王吉 / Wang Ji appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-358-2-simaxiangru-diao-fengliu-guafu-lphrip8prodrc46x__y6elbrkigz|Hanji 358-2]] as the Linqiong magistrate and friend who helps [[SimaXiangru|司马相如]] become publicly legible as an important guest before the meeting with [[ZhuoWenjun|卓文君]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Wang Ji as the institutional half of a reputation-making performance. He invites Sima to the area, repeatedly visits his lodging as county magistrate, and continues even when Sima sometimes refuses to see him. The visible deference encourages local elites to infer that Sima must be exceptional.

That inference helps move Sima into [[ZhuoWangsunWesternHan|卓王孙]]'s banquet. The host calls the arrangement a deception aimed at Zhuo Wenjun, but the exact plan, intent, and division of responsibility remain source-scoped.

## Key Characteristics

- Linqiong magistrate whose public office lends weight to his attention toward Sima Xiangru.
- Friend and host who connects Sima to the local elite social field.
- Uses repeated visible visits to create a status signal around Sima.
- Kept distinct from other historical figures named Wang Ji.

## Evidence

- Local office and friendship: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-358-2-simaxiangru-diao-fengliu-guafu-lphrip8prodrc46x__y6elbrkigz|Hanji 358-2]] identifies Wang Ji as Sima's friend and a county magistrate.
- Reputation staging: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-358-2-simaxiangru-diao-fengliu-guafu-lphrip8prodrc46x__y6elbrkigz|Hanji 358-2]] says he visits Sima's lodging three times a day and persists despite occasional refusal.
- Banquet pathway: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-358-2-simaxiangru-diao-fengliu-guafu-lphrip8prodrc46x__y6elbrkigz|Hanji 358-2]] says this display makes local elites take Sima seriously and contributes to Zhuo Wangsun inviting him.

## Qualifications

The profile rests on the episode's compressed retelling. The claim that Wang Ji and Sima explicitly conspired to deceive Zhuo Wenjun is the host's framing, not a settled reconstruction of intention. Office details, chronology, and the precise sequence of visits and invitations require comparison with fuller historical texts.

## What Changed

- Created a semantically suffixed page for the Linqiong magistrate in the Sima Xiangru tradition.
- Preserved the staged-reputation mechanism while qualifying claims of deliberate fraud.
