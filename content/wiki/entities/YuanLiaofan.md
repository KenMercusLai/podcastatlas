# 袁了凡 / Yuan Liaofan

袁了凡 / Yuan Liaofan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-161-caiyun-he-renji-guanxi-de-liangzi-jiuchan-4-lml7lpd3u9l7esryknmhoampgx6v|Hanji 161 part 4]] as the figure behind the episode's fate-change story. The host says Yuan was once given a detailed life prediction, then later met [[YunguChanshi|云谷禅师]] and changed the apparently fixed pattern through intention, virtue practice, and accumulated good deeds.

In the source, Yuan Liaofan is not a broad biography subject. He functions as the narrative proof case for [[FateChangeThroughVirtuePractice|积善修心式改命]]: seeking wealth, children, office, and longevity becomes possible only when desire is paired with修心, 发愿, and actual conduct.

The episode ties Yuan directly to [[LiaofanSixun|《了凡四训》]], treating the text as a method left for descendants and later readers rather than as an ornamental classic.
