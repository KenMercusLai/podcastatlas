## Current Profile
The guest distinguishes an insurer-appointed agent from a buyer-oriented broker who compares products across companies. She applies that distinction to temporary workers, older adults, and ordinary employees, discussing public medical coverage, medical reimbursement, accident protection, flexible policy duration, consultation, and policy review as different parts of household risk planning.

## Key Characteristics
- Presents broker advice as cross-insurer comparison on behalf of the prospective buyer.
- Organizes coverage questions around age, health, work, income, budget, and existing protection.
- Separates medical-expense protection from accident-related needs.
- Describes shorter-duration and monthly-payment options as potentially relevant to temporary work.
- Appears inside a sponsored segment, requiring explicit incentive and verification boundaries.
