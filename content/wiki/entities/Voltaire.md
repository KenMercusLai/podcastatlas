# Voltaire / 伏尔泰

## Overview

Voltaire appears across the wiki as an Enlightenment reference point, a satirical workplace character, and a major literary amplifier of the [[ManInTheIronMask]] legend.

## Current Profile

The algorithm source places Voltaire within an Enlightenment commitment to public knowledge and active reason, then asks how recommendation systems can turn that inheritance into [[AlgorithmicReasonOutsourcing]]. The workplace satire instead converts his disagreement with [[JeanJacquesRousseau]] into a sales fight over orders, credit, responsibility, and territory, while [[FriedrichNietzsche]] challenges rationality when it becomes another object of worship.

His historical-memory role comes from the iron-mask case. After hearing prison stories during his own Bastille confinement, Voltaire described an iron mask with a feeding mechanism, exceptional deference toward the prisoner, and later a royal-brother identity. Those claims helped transform a documented but obscure masked prisoner into a dynastic mystery even though the contemporary evidence supports [[EustacheDege]], a valet, more strongly.

## Key Characteristics

- Enlightenment figure used to represent the democratization of knowledge and the demand for active reason.
- Philosophical rival translated into comic workplace competition.
- Writer whose authority and narrative detail enlarged a sparse prison mystery.
- Historical actor whose later claims must be separated from contemporary documentation.
