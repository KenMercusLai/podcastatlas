# South China Morning Post / 南华早报

South China Morning Post / 南华早报 is the historical newspaper source emphasized in [[vol-269-xiao-lishi-bu-yao-pa-shi-jishu-xing-tiaozheng-1004793119]]. The episode says its archive of reports, advertisements, and market data helps reconstruct the [[HongkongLandDairyFarmTakeover|置地饮牛奶]] takeover and the atmosphere before the [[HongKong1973StockMarketCrash|1973 Hong Kong stock market crash]].

The newspaper matters in the source because the takeover was fought partly on its pages. [[HongkongLand|香港置地]] and [[DairyFarmInternational|牛奶公司]] placed repeated ads, but the source argues Hongkong Land's frequency, placement, charts, property photographs, and shareholder instructions gave it a stronger [[TenderOfferMediaCampaign]].
