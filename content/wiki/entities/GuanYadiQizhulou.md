# 关亚迪 / Guan Yadi

关亚迪 appears in [[vol-128-guanshui-zhan-xiayibu-zouxiang-hefang-meiguo-renmin-ruhe-kandai-telangpu-bairi-xinzheng-kuangxi-bokejie-duihua-zhongshu-talich-ltivoqhue1t-q2ejjlzlenormkhb]] as the live-session moderator for the 狂喜播客节 video link with [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]], [[Zhongshu|众数 / 仲树]], and [[Talich]]. His role is mainly to frame the discussion around the China-U.S. tariff war and Trump first-hundred-days politics, move through audience questions, and close the session by reflecting on how U.S. politics has shifted from distant spectacle to direct material pressure for Chinese listeners.

The source uses his closing comment to mark a change in media distance. U.S. elections and policy drama may once have felt like entertainment from across the ocean, but by 2025 tariffs, supply chains, research funding, prices, and household planning make that distance much smaller.
