# 袁征征 / Yuan Zhengzheng

## Overview

袁征征 is a Chinese video editor and rural documentary creator who co-founded [[YuzhenJishi|玉真记事]] with [[ZhaoYushun|赵玉顺]].

## Current Profile

The bounded source presents Yuan as a Chinese-language graduate who learned filming and editing at university, rejected entertainment-oriented short-video aggregation, and later learned long-form television editing while questioning staged production. After unstable work in Beijing and Guangzhou, she helped turn weekend field trips into a full-time practice documenting village life, agriculture, children, and elderly residents.

Her childhood experience of parental absence and grandparent care informs the project's attention to left-behind children without making that experience representative of every rural family.

## Key Characteristics

- Video editor and documentary maker with experience in short-form and television production environments.
- Co-creator of field reporting centered on villages and towns outside dominant media attention.
- Brings personal experience of grandparent care to reporting on children and intergenerational communication.
- Works through open-ended village visits rather than primarily filming tourist destinations.
- Balances public-interest expression with the financial and platform constraints of independent creation.
