# Mao Lixiang / 毛李翔

## Overview

Mao Lixiang / 毛李翔 is the first-generation Ningbo entrepreneur in [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]]. The episode uses his lighter business, Guangzhou Fair order-seeking, and later handoff to [[MaoZhongqun|毛中群]] to explain how one private manufacturer moved from opportunistic export production toward [[FangtaiGroup|方太]].

## Current Profile

Mao is portrayed as an action-oriented founder from the township-enterprise era. His lighter business reached global scale, but the episode stresses that low entry barriers quickly pulled the product into price competition.

His later importance is the succession environment he created. He wanted his son to join, debated business direction, then eventually allowed a new company with a new name, new team boundary, and phased handoff.

## Key Characteristics

- Mao represents the first-generation export-manufacturing entrepreneur who won orders through persistence and improvisation.
- His lighter success also reveals the weakness of low-barrier production when suppliers and competitors can copy the process.
- He initially preferred a microwave-oven direction before accepting the range-hood route.
- His Fangtai handoff is framed as "带三年、帮三年、看三年" in the source.
- He later institutionalized succession education through a Ningbo family-business successor school.

## Qualifications

- The episode's Mao profile is built around Fangtai's origin story; it is not a complete biography.
- The Guangzhou Fair story is used as entrepreneurial evidence, not as a general rule for export-market entry.
