# Baby's Only

Baby's Only is the toddler-formula brand that [[Bobbie]] kept after acquiring [[NaturesOne]] in [[bobbie-laura-modi-how-a-baby-formula-startup-took-market-share-from-two-industry-giants-4488842b-74d3-40ed-8776-5831c5934e69]]. The episode treats it as part of the acquired manufacturing and brand base rather than as the main Bobbie formula product.

Its wiki role is to connect Bobbie's move toward owned production with a broader portfolio question: in regulated baby CPG, acquiring capacity may also bring an existing brand, customer base, facility history, and separate approval path.
