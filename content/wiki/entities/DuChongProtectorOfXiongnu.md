## Overview
杜崇 / Du Chong was the Eastern Han Protector of the Xiongnu whose current wiki profile spans the An Guo-Shizi crisis and the later Southern Xiongnu false-report punishment. He is paired with [[ZhuHuiHeEraLateHan|Zhu Hui]] in both intervention and accountability.

## Current Profile
In Hanji 768, An Guo was at odds with Du Chong and submitted a complaint against him, but Du Chong had it intercepted by the Xihe governor. Du Chong and Zhu Hui then countercharged An Guo with rebellion and moved troops into the crisis. Hanji 769 later shows Du Chong and Zhu Hui executed after their false reporting in the next phase was exposed.

## Key Characteristics
- Protector official embedded in a tense relationship with An Guo.
- Used information control when An Guo's complaint against him was intercepted.
- Joined Zhu Hui in escalating the case into a rebellion charge and military intervention.
- Later became a cautionary figure for false reporting in frontier command.
- Connects the internal Southern Xiongnu succession crisis to Han court oversight.

- Countercharge and movement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-768-lishi-gaosu-ni-jidu-shi-shaxin-lgk8qoxvs5ax8oikgecrxqvispna|Hanji 768]] says Du Chong and Zhu Hui charged An Guo with rebellion and advanced with troops.

## Qualifications
The sources give enough to show Du Chong's role in complaint control, intervention, and later punishment, but not enough to settle whether his initial countercharge against An Guo was entirely accurate, self-protective, or both. His page should therefore keep Hanji 768 and Hanji 769 as adjacent but analytically separate phases.
