# International Maritime Organization

The International Maritime Organization appears in [[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]] through 2020 rules reducing sulphur from cargo ships. [[OliverMorton]] says those shipping rules had a measurable effect on reduced reflectivity.

The source makes the IMO part of [[AerosolCoolingMasking]]. Shipping sulphur cuts improve air quality, but they also reduce reflective aerosols that had been masking some greenhouse-gas warming. That turns a maritime pollution rule into an example of how public-health regulation can expose [[EarthEnergyImbalance]] more clearly.
