# 亿万富翁回归

《亿万富翁回归》 is the overseas [[AIShortDrama]] case used in [[cong-yangshi-jilupian-dao-baokuan-ai-duanju-di-yi-pi-zhuanshen-de-daoyan-s10e11-3c05e3d5-d8f6-44c1-97ca-698261d7b2bc]] to argue that story fit can beat visible AI production flaws. [[Chouxiangzai]] says the drama ranked first overseas in November and made money, while also acknowledging consistency problems such as lightweight objects and shifting side profiles.

The source's story lesson is that the work moved beyond a simple billionaire-and-Cinderella structure. A writer named Guo Meng reportedly suggested making the female lead and female antagonist ally, making the male lead more complex or negative, and ending with the female lead secretly drugging him back into a loving, simple state while controlling the family herself.

For the wiki, the work sharpens [[ShortDramaEconomics]] and [[AIShortDramaOverseasLocalization]]: the important variable is not whether every shot is perfect, but whether genre, gender psychology, reversal, and emotional payoff fit the target audience.

## Key Points
- The source treats it as a real monetization proof point for AI short drama overseas.
- Its production flaws are acknowledged rather than hidden, making it useful evidence for the "story won" claim.
- Its revised gender-power structure is used to explain North American female-audience resonance in the source.
