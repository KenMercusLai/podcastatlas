# Hearsay Brewing and Theater

Hearsay Brewing and Theater is [[TonyDeRosa]]'s Ann Arbor business in [[advice-line-strategy-sessions-4962a0d6-36bc-4797-8002-3bd8b748a89a]]. The source describes it as combining a microbrewery, bar, comedy theater, food truck, and corporate improv workshops, with about 40 events a month and $667,000 in revenue in its first full year.

The Advice Line diagnosis is that Hearsay should clarify the business's strongest role before adding partners. [[JeffreyHollender]] and [[GuyRaz]] point Tony toward the higher-margin workshop/class business, stronger community-and-laughter positioning, sharper hiring evaluation, and [[ControlPreservingIncentives]] for operators who can earn trust over time.
