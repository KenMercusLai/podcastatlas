# Songguo Shike / 松果时刻

## Overview
Songguo Shike / 松果时刻 is a consumer AI creation product from [[DongnianYinxian|动念引线]] discussed as an exploration of an “imagination camera.”

## Current Profile
The product aims to lower the amount of input needed for ordinary users to turn an imagined scene or personal idea into shareable content. The company uses it as a general observation surface, then considers retaining promising behaviors in the product or separating them into more focused products.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats generative AI as an expression interface analogous to a camera for imagination.
- Seeks low-input creation that remains recognizably shaped by the user's intent.
- Observes unexpected use cases before narrowing audience and content form.
- Connects creation to consumption, sharing, feedback, and possible new product branches.

## Evidence
- **Low-friction expression** - [[duihua-liangchenqi-douyin-maoxiang-chuangye-tamen-dou-gao-shengchanli-wo-xiang-yong-ai-chuangzao-kaixin-1-182-1]] says Songguo Shike reduces the initiative and imagination burden of open-ended roleplay by letting small inputs generate richer expression.
- **Discovery surface** - [[duihua-liangchenqi-douyin-maoxiang-chuangye-tamen-dou-gao-shengchanli-wo-xiang-yong-ai-chuangzao-kaixin-1-182-1]] reports use by varied groups and says the team starts broadly to observe behavior before selecting narrower opportunities.
- **Product constraint** - [[duihua-liangchenqi-douyin-maoxiang-chuangye-tamen-dou-gao-shengchanli-wo-xiang-yong-ai-chuangzao-kaixin-1-182-1]] records Liang's statement that low input is useful only when it has decisive influence and produces a large enough increase in richness, playability, or surprise.

## Qualifications
- User examples are anecdotal and do not establish audience distribution, retention, or market scale.
- The source does not provide verified product metrics, monetization, or a stable long-term scope.
- AI-generated richness can dilute personal specificity when the user's contribution is too weak.

## What Changed
- Added Songguo Shike as a canonical consumer-creation product.
- Defined its role as both an expression tool and a behavior-discovery surface.
- Added the low-input but consequential-control constraint.
