# 秦简公 / Duke Jian of Qin

秦简公 / Duke Jian of Qin appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-1-lvkp-ju7pf5z5cz3thojv3s2fkyx]] as the [[QinState|秦国]] ruler whose death is followed by his son's accession as [[QinHuiGong|秦惠公]]. The same source also places Qin in active military conflict with [[WeiState|魏国]] in [[ZhouAnWang|周安王]]'s first year.
