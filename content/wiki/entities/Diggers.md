# Diggers

## Overview

The Diggers were a small radical group led by [[GerardWinstanley]] that cultivated common land and challenged exclusive ownership during the English republican crisis.

## Current Profile

About fifty people from Cobham established a cultivation experiment at St George's Hill, planting crops including beans and peas. Their practical scale was small, but Winstanley's writing gave the movement a wider intellectual afterlife as a critique of private landholding and social hierarchy.

The central republican authorities, including Thomas Fairfax, did not treat them as a major military threat. Local hostility proved decisive, showing how radical possibilities could be closed by neighborhood property power even when the central state was comparatively restrained.

## Key Characteristics

- Small communal land movement active during the English Republic.
- Associated with common cultivation and opposition to exclusive ownership.
- Led and publicized by Gerard Winstanley.
- More influential through writing and memory than through numbers.
- Removed primarily through local resistance rather than national mass repression.
