# 任隗 / Ren Kui (Eastern Han)

## Overview

任隗 / Ren Kui appears as a senior minister who joins [[YuanAnEasternHan|Yuan An]] in resisting [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] before the Dou-family cleanup. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-760-lishi-jiemi-huanghou-chugui-bei-zhua-xiachang-can-bucan-lgz2sc9xt8ulltvwemiz2uzfghqi|Hanji 760]] now backfills his earliest current layer: after [[SongYouEasternHan|Song You]] and other high officials retreat from the anti-expedition memorial, Ren and Yuan repeatedly object to attacking [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]] without a fresh frontier offense. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-764-ta-weile-huoming-jing-jianjie-mie-le-luoma-diguo-lpk0c6muneabhk1oonzyjvawwf4|Hanji 764]] later shows Ren and Yuan impeaching dozens of Dou-linked officials and remaining hard to attack because of their reputation. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-766-lishi-jiaogei-ni-weishenme-yao-guyi-fa-piqi-lrs-mguyfmnxe970hdwjvmevl8oc|Hanji 766]] then shows Ren joining Yuan in resisting Dou over the Northern Xiongnu settlement. His role is compact but structurally important: he shows that anti-Dou opposition is not merely one official's private courage.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-765-donghan-fengyun-nengchen-nengbuneng-gandiao-bahu-waiqi-ltnvdqnyofuelaklssbquguwivan|Hanji 765]] now supplies the earlier policy layer for that opposition. Ren Kui stands with Yuan against installing [[YuChuNorthernXiongnu|Yu Chu / 于除]] as a new Northern Xiongnu chanyu, arguing that the settlement would distort the original [[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]] strategy and add public burdens.

## Current Profile

Hanji 760 supplies the earliest current Ren Kui layer. Three Excellencies and Nine Ministers object that the Northern Xiongnu has not invaded the frontier and that an unprovoked expedition is poor state policy. Song You becomes afraid and does not sign, while Ren Kui and Yuan An continue resisting, removing their caps and submitting objections repeatedly.

Hanji 764 presents Ren Kui as part of a small senior-minister pair that can still impose bureaucratic cost on Dou Xian's bloc. With Yuan An, he impeaches more than forty Dou-linked officials and helps check the spread of Dou's recommendation network. The source says Dou's brothers hate Yuan and Ren but hesitate because of their public reputation.

Ren Kui's current profile is that of a coalition-confirming dissenter. Hanji 765 does not give him a long individual speech, but it places him beside Yuan An at the first dispute over the Northern Xiongnu arrangement. That matters because the objection is substantive: the ministers read Southern Xiongnu incorporation as a contingent frontier tool, not as a precedent for multiplying costly chanyu arrangements.

This makes Ren Kui part of the pre-coup institutional resistance layer. He helps establish that the later palace action against the Dou bloc follows visible court conflict rather than sudden personal rivalry alone.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior minister aligned with Yuan An from the pre-campaign objection layer through later Northern Xiongnu policy disputes.
- Impeachment partner whose public reputation constrains immediate Dou retaliation.
- Helps make opposition to Dou Xian appear collective rather than isolated.
- Shares a policy objection that links precedent, frontier commitments, and fiscal cost.
- Belongs to the visible remonstrance layer before Emperor He's secret operational planning.
- Signals that outer-relative dominance faces principled resistance inside the bureaucracy.

## Qualifications

The sources do not present Ren Kui as drafting the memorials, facing the death threat, or joining Emperor He's secret consultation. Those roles belong respectively to [[ZhouRongEasternHan|Zhou Rong]], the Dou retainer confrontation, and the [[ZhengZhongEasternHan|Zheng Zhong]] / [[LiuQingQingheKing|Liu Qing]] planning channel. Ren's importance is collective, repeated, impeachment-based, and policy-confirming rather than biographically expansive.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 760 as Ren's earliest current anti-Dou layer: repeated opposition to the Northern Xiongnu expedition after Song You retreats.
- Reframed Ren as a sustained co-dissenter with Yuan An before both the impeachment and Yu Chu recognition layers.
- Preserved Hanji 764 as the impeachment and reputation-shield layer and Hanji 765 as the later policy substance.
