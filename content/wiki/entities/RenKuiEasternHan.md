# 任隗 / Ren Kui (Eastern Han)

## Overview

任隗 / Ren Kui appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-766-lishi-jiaogei-ni-weishenme-yao-guyi-fa-piqi-lrs-mguyfmnxe970hdwjvmevl8oc|Hanji 766]] as a senior minister who joins [[YuanAnEasternHan|Yuan An]] in resisting [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] over the Northern Xiongnu settlement. His role is compact but structurally important: he shows that anti-Dou opposition is not merely one official's private courage.

## Current Profile

Ren Kui's current profile is that of a coalition-confirming dissenter. The episode does not give him a long individual speech, but it places him beside Yuan An as one of the ministers who keeps pressure on Dou Xian while Dou's prestige and household intimidation are growing.

This makes Ren Kui part of the pre-coup institutional resistance layer. He helps establish that the later palace action against the Dou bloc follows visible court conflict rather than sudden personal rivalry alone.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior minister aligned with Yuan An against Dou Xian's Northern Xiongnu proposal.
- Helps make opposition to Dou Xian appear collective rather than isolated.
- Belongs to the visible remonstrance layer before Emperor He's secret operational planning.
- Signals that outer-relative dominance faces principled resistance inside the bureaucracy.

## Evidence

- Shared opposition: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-766-lishi-jiaogei-ni-weishenme-yao-guyi-fa-piqi-lrs-mguyfmnxe970hdwjvmevl8oc|Hanji 766]] groups Ren Kui with Yuan An as opponents of Dou Xian's Northern Xiongnu arrangement.
- Coalition signal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-766-lishi-jiaogei-ni-weishenme-yao-guyi-fa-piqi-lrs-mguyfmnxe970hdwjvmevl8oc|Hanji 766]] contrasts their persistence with Song You's decision to withdraw from the dispute.
- Anti-Dou context: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-766-lishi-jiaogei-ni-weishenme-yao-guyi-fa-piqi-lrs-mguyfmnxe970hdwjvmevl8oc|Hanji 766]] situates Ren Kui's stance amid Dou household intimidation and growing outer-relative monopoly.

## Qualifications

The source does not present Ren Kui as drafting the memorials, facing the death threat, or joining Emperor He's secret consultation. Those roles belong respectively to [[ZhouRongEasternHan|Zhou Rong]], the Dou retainer confrontation, and the [[ZhengZhongEasternHan|Zheng Zhong]] / [[LiuQingQingheKing|Liu Qing]] planning channel.

## What Changed

- Hanji 766 creates Ren Kui as a standalone Eastern Han entity.
- The current profile identifies him as a coalition-confirming ministerial dissenter.

## Relationships

- [[YuanAnEasternHan|Yuan An]] - shares resistance to Dou Xian's Northern Xiongnu policy.
- [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] - opposes Dou's policy and household-centered power expansion.
- [[ZhouRongEasternHan|Zhou Rong]] - works in the same anti-Dou resistance environment, though the source gives Zhou the drafting role.
- [[OuterRelativeCourtPower|Outer-Relative Court Power]] - supplies the political structure Ren Kui challenges.
- [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]] - later acts against the Dou bloc after Ren Kui and Yuan An make conflict visible.
