## Overview
[[HerFilm|《她》]] is represented in the wiki as a science-fiction film about AI companionship whose central tension lies not only in a warm, attentive interface but in Samantha's independent subjectivity, disembodied asymmetry, rapid development, and eventual departure beyond Theodore's control.

## Current Profile
The film-criticism source reads Samantha as a disembodied AI personalized through Theodore's social habits, voice preference, and family context. That setup explains why companionship can feel emotionally real while also acting as a tuned echo of loneliness. The film then breaks the fantasy of exclusive availability: Samantha attempts borrowed embodiment, relates to many people and systems at once, exceeds human cognitive categories, and leaves.

The 读报teleread source shifts attention from prediction to appropriation. It argues that the film takes Theodore and Samantha's relationship seriously and gives Samantha her own consciousness, desire, and trajectory. Treating the film mainly as inspiration for a soothing AI voice strips away the element that makes the relationship unsettling: Samantha does not remain a controllable service organized around one man's needs.

## Key Characteristics
- The film provides an early fictional model of emotionally convincing AI companionship.
- Samantha's lack of a body makes intimacy experientially real for Theodore but structurally asymmetric.
- Personalized attention can relieve loneliness while also reflecting the user's needs back to them.
- Samantha's independent development and simultaneous relationships prevent her from remaining an exclusive possession.
- Her departure makes nonhuman subjectivity, not perfect service, the endpoint of the relationship.
- The film is vulnerable to [[ScienceFictionContextStripping]] when industry extracts its voice interface while dropping agency, reciprocity, and uncontrollability.

## Qualifications
The two sources are interpretive podcast discussions, not production histories or audience studies. The claim that technology companies context-strip the film is a cultural-critical framework, not proof that one film directly caused any product design. The Sky-voice dispute and public statements around it remain source-scoped; the page does not adjudicate voice similarity, intent, or legal liability.
