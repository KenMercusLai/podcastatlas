# Mitzi Kaspar

## Overview

Mitzi Kaspar is [[CrownPrinceRudolf]]'s regular mistress in the Mayerling episode and an earlier recipient of Rudolf's death-pact language.

## Current Profile

Mitzi matters because she qualifies the Rudolf-Mary story. The episode says Rudolf had already floated a death pact to her and she laughed it off, which makes his later pact with [[MaryVetsera]] look less like a uniquely mutual romantic destiny and more like a recurring morbid proposal that a vulnerable partner accepted or could not resist.

## Key Characteristics

- Mitzi is still in Rudolf's life while his affair with Mary begins.
- Her rejection of Rudolf's death-pact talk provides a contrast to Mary's involvement.
- Rudolf leaves her a farewell letter, showing that she remains inside his emotional and logistical world.
