# 刘天昭 / Liu Tianzhao

## Overview
刘天昭 / Liu Tianzhao is the author of [[UnwatchedNovel|《无人观看》]], discussed in [[vol-352-wuren-guankan-ziwo-chayixing-jida-waibu-xiangsixing-jiqiang-de-women-gkwrijiofxaxaq8boatxoohj]] as a writer of contemporary urban interiority.

## Current Profile
The current wiki evidence is limited to one [[CultureLimited|文化有限]] episode. In that source, the hosts describe Liu as born in Jilin in 1977, educated in architecture at Tsinghua University, formerly an editorial writer at Southern Metropolis Daily, and later a freelance writer. They connect [[UnwatchedNovel|《无人观看》]] with his stated view that literature is not first a service to technique, structure, or reader convenience, but a way for a person to approach truth and self-recognition.

## Key Characteristics
- He is presented as a writer whose work privileges interior truth-seeking over plot efficiency.
- His background is described as crossing architecture, journalism, and independent writing.
- [[UnwatchedNovel|《无人观看》]] is treated as his major current wiki case for contemporary urban middle-class life.
- The source associates his fiction with philosophical questions about selfhood, recognition, aging, AI, and social performance.
