# Vibe Trading

## Overview
Vibe Trading is an open-source AI-assisted secondary-market research project created and maintained by [[WuHaozhe|吴浩哲]]. The source describes it as evolving from a professional quantitative toolkit into a broader workspace spanning macro research, company fundamentals, quantitative validation, and risk review.

## Current Profile
The project's defining product move is from specialist metrics toward an integrated, inspectable research process. Users can express an investment idea in natural language, after which the system can organize evidence across domains, separate source material from hypotheses, run selected calculations, test risks, and present reasons a person can examine before acting.

Its stated objective is not fully autonomous trading. The platform is presented as an implementation of [[AITrading]] in which cheap candidate decisions pass through data, validation, and constraint layers before limited action remains subject to human risk rules and acceptance.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines fundamental, macro, quantitative, and risk research in one workflow.
- Began with hundreds of factors, multi-market backtesting, and statistical tests before broadening beyond professional quant users.
- Uses natural language to lower the interface barrier while preserving inspectable sources and reasoning.
- Treats deterministic calculation, data alignment, temporal validity, and contrary evidence as constraints on generative output.
- Keeps execution bounded and human-controlled rather than turning every model judgment into a trade.

## Qualifications
- Project capabilities, usage, contributor geography, star count, factor count, and market coverage are reported by its author in one source and have not been independently audited here.
- An inspectable workflow can reduce but not eliminate hallucination, bad data, dependency errors, overfitting, transaction costs, manipulation, or misuse by an underqualified user.
- The source does not establish that the platform generates persistent alpha or improves realized risk-adjusted returns.
