# Smile Action / 微笑行动

## Overview
Smile Action / 微笑行动 is the source's name for a volunteer medical program providing free surgery and follow-up support to people in remote or underserved areas with cleft lip, cleft palate, and other facial differences.

## Current Profile
[[vol-115-mao-manfen-fumu-ni-zuo-60-fen-yi-buyi-13-wei-yisheng-10-xueke-chuzhao-shenxinxing-jiankang-jiaoyu-gonglue-ertongjie-zhuanti-ljlyg0kuqvuj2r89k6o5hhuefjt9|VOL.115]] presents the program as more than a short surgical event. Teams travel, screen candidates, operate for several days, support anesthesia and recovery, and confront cases delayed by age, nutrition, distance, or access. Cleft care itself can span feeding, appearance, speech, hearing, oral health, psychology, learning, and social participation, making timing and multidisciplinary follow-up important.

The source also foregrounds volunteer cost. Clinicians may use annual leave, save days off, travel overnight, and work long operating days. Patient families may spend one or two days reaching the site or walk for hours, so “free surgery” does not erase travel, time, nutrition, or continuity barriers.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides source-described free surgical and follow-up care for cleft and other facial differences in underserved regions.
- Uses multidisciplinary screening, surgery, anesthesia, recovery, and longer-term care rather than treating repair as a single isolated procedure.
- Depends on clinicians who contribute travel time, leave, physical labor, and repeated participation.
- Serves families whose distance, nutrition, age, and transport constraints can delay or prevent treatment.
- Frames treatment as affecting confidence, speech, learning, relationships, and social participation as well as anatomy.
