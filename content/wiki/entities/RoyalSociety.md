# Royal Society / 皇家学会

The Royal Society appears in [[26-niudun-yu-weibi-zhizaozhe-keneng-shi-sineipu-de-yuanxing-580065317]] as the institutional setting where early modern science becomes public, social, and evidentiary. [[EdmondHalley|Edmond Halley]] brings [[IsaacNewton|Newton]]'s work into its orbit, while figures such as [[RobertHooke|Robert Hooke]] and [[RobertBoyle|Robert Boyle]] show the experimental and instrument-building side of the same world.

The episode uses the Society to resist a lone-genius story. Newton's results matter, but they became durable through questions, demonstrations, records, instruments, peer judgment, publication priority, and people who trusted evidence enough to circulate it.
