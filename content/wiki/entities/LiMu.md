# 李牧 / Li Mu

李牧 / Li Mu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-105-zhanzheng-huayang-zhaoguo-limu-zui-hui-shua-ljtqdg46a8o13zzsjv-p-fpol5oh]] as the [[ZhaoState|赵国]] commander who follows [[LianPo|廉颇]] as Zhao's next major military asset. In the 244 BCE annalistic frame, [[ZhaoDaoxiangwang|赵道襄王]] appoints him to attack [[YanState|燕国]], and he takes 五遂 and 方城.

The episode's main Li Mu material is a retrospective northern-frontier command story. Stationed around Dai and Yanmen against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], he receives unusual operating autonomy: he can appoint local officials according to need, direct local tax revenue into military funds, reward troops with meat and drink, train riding and archery, watch beacon signals, and use spies.

Li Mu's controversial rule is to avoid battle. When Xiongnu forces enter, people, livestock, and supplies withdraw into fortified positions, and anyone who captures prisoners or fights without authorization is executed. The source says this prevents serious frontier losses for years, but it also makes both Xiongnu observers and Zhao insiders call him timid.

The episode treats his temporary removal as the proof of his method. After [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] replaces him, the new commander fights every raid and repeatedly loses, leaving frontier people unable to farm or herd normally. Li Mu returns only after the ruler agrees not to interfere with the old policy.

Once soldiers are rested, rewarded, and eager for battle, Li Mu changes posture. He exposes people and livestock as bait, feigns retreat, deliberately lets a small party be captured, draws the Xiongnu chanyu's main force deep, then attacks with frontal fixing, flanking, and route-cutting formations. The episode says the result destroys more than 100,000 Xiongnu cavalry, forces the chanyu to flee, and keeps Xiongnu forces away from the Zhao frontier for more than ten years.

The page is source-scoped. Li Mu's wiki role is not only "famous general," but a case where reputation damage, patience, soldier morale, intelligence, and resource limits are held together until the commander can choose one decisive fight.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-112-2-qinshihuang-yeshi-tade-shumi-llkoy-zvhjjr6af9tflcw1xvogwz]] moves Li Mu from the frontier retrospective into the direct Qin-Zhao front. After [[HuanYiQin|桓齮]] kills Zhao general Hu Zhe and the episode reports a 100,000-person slaughter in 234 BCE, [[ZhaoYoumiuWang|赵迁 / 赵幽缪王]] appoints Li Mu. Li Mu defeats Huan Yi around Yi'an and Feixia, Huan Yi flees back to Qin, and Zhao grants Li Mu the title 武安君.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-112-3-hanfei-yu-yaojia-lunzhan-qinwang-dang-caipan-ljxxlok4uh-ajjirmolybodlspv9]] returns to Li Mu as the next unresolved obstacle after [[HanFei|韩非]]'s death. The source says Qin's conquest tempo continues and that, among the remaining six-state problems, Zhao's Li Mu remains the serious military opponent to watch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-113-limu-ruhe-yong-yiji-zhili-zu-qinguo-tongyi-luymqpcolsvyz5miodit3wdlx0ob]] completes that obstacle branch. In 232 BCE, the source says Qin withdraws after meeting Li Mu on the Zhao front, reinforcing the host's claim that Qin is not simply invincible in every field encounter. In 229 BCE, Qin avoids letting Li Mu define the campaign by bribing [[GuoKai|郭开]] into accusing Li Mu and [[SimaShang|司马尚]] of rebellion, after which [[ZhaoYoumiuWang|赵迁 / 赵幽缪王]] replaces them with [[ZhaoCong|赵葱]] and [[YanJuZhao|颜聚]]. Li Mu refuses the order and is killed or arrested, turning his final wiki role into the starkest case of Zhao discarding the commander Qin most feared.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-156-bingxian-hanxin-de-fengshen-zhizhan-1-ltkz7bwqgkcqessdfbn9unf5e05l|Hanji 156 part 1]] adds Li Mu's family afterlife through [[LiZuoChe|李左车]], identified as his grandson and as Zhao's Guangwu Jun. The source uses that lineage to strengthen Li Zuoche's credibility when he proposes cutting [[HanXin|韩信]] and [[ZhangEr|张耳]]'s supply line at [[JingxingPass|井陉口]].
