# Spot Robot

## Overview
Spot is Boston Dynamics' mobile robot platform in the All-In robotics special. The episode presents it as the strongest commercial evidence that Boston Dynamics has moved from research spectacle into industrial deployment.

## Current Profile
Spot is framed as an agile industrial inspection robot used for sensing, security, and asset monitoring in environments where consistent human inspection may be dangerous, expensive, or incomplete. Its product story in the episode turns on customer count, pricing, docking, battery life, mean time between intervention, and sub-two-year ROI expectations.

## Key Characteristics
- Spot is used for industrial inspection tasks such as acoustic inspection, gauge reading, vibration detection, and asset monitoring.
- Security perimeter work is part of its deployment surface in the episode.
- Pricing is described as roughly $100,000 for the base robot and up to about $300,000 when fully loaded with services and integration.
- Spot's battery is described as about 90 minutes, with docking for recharge.
- Mean time between intervention is claimed to be above 3,000 hours.
- Boston Dynamics frames Spot as labor augmentation and dangerous-work removal more than simple headcount replacement.
