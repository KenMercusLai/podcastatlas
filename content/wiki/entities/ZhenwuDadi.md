# 真武大帝 / Zhenwu Dadi

真武大帝 / Zhenwu Dadi appears in [[shan-shang-shan-xia-jie-renjian-wushi-xiaoyao-ji-shenxian-lhjiguq74flhatdxnbrnzhjg-acp]] through [[LiChuang|李闯]]'s account of Daoist hierarchy and advice received at [[WudangMountain|武当山]]. When Li Chuang considers seeking help from looser folk or occult channels, a Daoist master uses Zhenwu's system to redirect him back toward the Daoist frame already available at Wudang.

The page is source-scoped. The episode uses Zhenwu Dadi less as a complete theological subject than as evidence that religious practice can have internal order, rank, and jurisdiction. That matters for [[ChineseFolkReligionLayering]] because folk and Daoist systems can overlap in lived use while still drawing boundaries around which gods, rituals, and requests are appropriate.
