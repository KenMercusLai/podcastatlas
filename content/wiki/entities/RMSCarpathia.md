# RMS Carpathia

## Overview

RMS Carpathia was the Cunard liner that responded to [[RMSTitanic|Titanic]]'s distress and received its survivors, as described in [[432-titanic-the-survivors-part-6-glt4613169046]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Carpathia as the operational rescue counterpoint to Titanic's failure. After its wireless operator learned of the collision, Captain Arthur Rostron redirected the ship, rapidly prepared crew and facilities, navigated toward the reported position, and arrived in roughly three and a half hours. The survivor reception then shifted the story from urgent seamanship to shock, cold, silence, grief, and the management of prominent survivor [[BruceIsmay]].

## Key Characteristics

- Cunard passenger ship traveling from New York toward Gibraltar and Mediterranean ports.
- Wireless-equipped vessel that received and confirmed Titanic's distress information.
- Rescue ship prepared rapidly under Captain Arthur Rostron.
- Vessel that reached a dangerous ice field and recovered Titanic survivors.
- First contained setting for the survivors' trauma, separation, and medical care.

## Evidence

- Rescue response: [[432-titanic-the-survivors-part-6-glt4613169046]] describes the wireless exchange, Rostron's preparations, rapid diversion, rockets, lifeboat readiness, and iceberg danger.
- Survivor reception: [[432-titanic-the-survivors-part-6-glt4613169046]] describes survivors as cold, silent, and traumatized and records Ismay's sedation and separation in the doctor's cabin.

## Qualifications

The page is source-bounded to an aftermath episode and does not provide a complete operational history of Carpathia, Rostron, or every rescue decision. The reported timing and scene details remain attributed to that account.
