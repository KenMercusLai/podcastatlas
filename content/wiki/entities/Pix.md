# Pix

Pix is the [[CentralBankOfBrazil]] instant digital payment system discussed in [[working-memory-the-surprising-decline-of-dementia-6a6b0eeca585d3be78cd9d1b]]. The source says it launched in 2020, lets people send money instantly around the clock, usually charges no fee, and works through phones and QR codes.

The episode presents Pix as a [[PublicInstantPaymentRail]] and a financial-inclusion case. Around 80% of Brazilians are described as using it, more than half of payment transactions run through it, and Brazil's central bank estimates that about 70 million people entered the formal financial system after launch.

Pix also becomes a trade-policy test. [[DonaldTrump]]'s administration framed it as harming American payment companies, but the source says foreign and domestic licensed financial institutions can connect on the same terms. The segment therefore treats the dispute as [[PaymentInfrastructureTradeDispute]] rather than straightforward discrimination against [[Visa]] or [[Mastercard]].
