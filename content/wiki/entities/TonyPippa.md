# Tony Pippa

Tony Pippa appears in [[tech-20260423-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260423-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the [[BrookingsInstitution]] expert interviewed by [[StephanieHughes]] about data-center development becoming a political issue. His role in the wiki is to shift [[DataCenterBacklash]] from a generic anti-buildout mood into a local-capacity problem: communities are reacting not only to power and water demands, but also to the speed and asymmetry of negotiations with large technology companies.

Pippa's central frame is [[DataCenterCommunityConsent]]. He says towns need time, information, and negotiating position to decide whether data centers fit their desired future, what jobs or benefits they can secure, and how to handle uncertainty about whether today's facilities will still be valuable decades later.
