# Busia, Kenya

Busia is the western [[Kenya]] town in [[piles-of-cash-and-a-town-of-solutions-in-kenya-nigeria-summer-school]] where [[PlanetMoney]] locates a key chapter in modern [[DevelopmentEconomics]]. The episode describes it as a place where [[MichaelKremer]] and later economists used [[RandomizedControlledTrials]] to test education, health, and poverty interventions.

Busia's importance is not only academic. The source follows [[CarolNkesa]] from survey-data work into research leadership, making the town a case in how local staff can become institutional builders rather than only data collectors for visiting economists.
