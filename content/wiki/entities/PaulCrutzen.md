# Paul Crutzen

Paul Crutzen appears in [[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]] as the reference point for a 2006 [[SolarGeoengineering]] proposal. [[OliverMorton]] says Crutzen suggested preserving the cooling effect of sulphate particles while reducing direct health harms by putting reflective particles higher in the atmosphere.

In the source, Crutzen's role is not to settle the policy question. He functions as the scientist whose proposal makes the albedo lever explicit: if lower-atmosphere sulphur pollution cools by reflecting sunlight, a deliberate high-atmosphere intervention could in principle imitate part of that effect while remaining politically and ethically controversial.
