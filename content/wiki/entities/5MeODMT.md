# 5-MeO-DMT

## Overview
5-MeO-DMT is a high-intensity psychedelic described through three different personal accounts: [[BryanJohnson|Bryan Johnson]] treats it as a measured longevity self-experiment, [[DJShipley|DJ Shipley]] describes it as the second part of an ibogaine-centered trauma and life-restructuring intervention, and [[ChristofKoch|Christof Koch]] uses it to examine whether experience can persist without an ordinary self, body, space, or time.

## Current Profile
All three accounts emphasize rapid ego disruption, fear or terror, surrender, and an intense positive or ecstatic state, but their goals differ. Johnson connects the experience to [[DefaultModeNetwork|default-mode-network]] disruption, [[Neuroplasticity|neuroplasticity]], and speculative biological rejuvenation; he also says his objective 5-MeO measurements were not yet available. Shipley connects it to compassion, spirituality, responsibility, and a supervised treatment setting, then attributes durability to [[PsychedelicIntegration|integration]] through relationship, environment, medication, and routine changes. Koch treats the persistence of experience during loss of ordinary selfhood as personally important evidence about [[ConsciousnessBehaviorDissociation|consciousness and self]], then extends it toward idealism and reduced fear of death.

The combined profile therefore remains experience- and risk-centered rather than efficacy- or metaphysics-centered. Three compelling narratives do not establish a general treatment effect, a longevity intervention, a compound-specific mechanism, a theory of consciousness, an afterlife claim, or safe self-use. Screening, dose control, monitoring, support, legality, and post-session follow-up remain central.

## Key Characteristics
- Described as fast, overwhelming, and organized more around ego dissolution or surrender than extended visual content.
- Appears in supervised settings in both sources, with explicit concern about dose, set, setting, screening, and psychological risk.
- Serves different source goals: longevity measurement for Johnson and trauma-related responsibility and life change for Shipley.
- Serves a third philosophical role for Koch, who treats selfless experience as evidence that consciousness and the ordinary self can dissociate.
- Produces strong immediate subjective claims while objective, comparative, and durable evidence remains incomplete in these notes.
- Raises continuity questions because intense experiences may alter values, relationships, obligations, or self-understanding.

## Evidence
- Longevity self-experiment - [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bryan-johnson-i-just-took-the-most-powerful-dose-of-dmt-in-the-world-heres-what-it-was-like-40626165]] describes Johnson's supervised dosing, immediate subjective aftereffects, measurement program, and still-pending objective 5-MeO results.
- Treatment testimony - [[how-to-make-yourself-unbreakable-dj-shipley-scim4854936060]] describes Shipley's fear, surrender, bliss, compassion, and later life restructuring after ibogaine followed by 5-MeO-DMT in a medically supported setting.
- Consciousness testimony - [[scim8616450810-scim8616450810]] describes Koch's loss of ordinary self, body, space, and time alongside retained terror, ecstasy, and light, followed by a personal turn toward idealism and reduced fear of death.
- Risk boundary - [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bryan-johnson-i-just-took-the-most-powerful-dose-of-dmt-in-the-world-heres-what-it-was-like-40626165]], [[how-to-make-yourself-unbreakable-dj-shipley-scim4854936060]], and [[scim8616450810-scim8616450810]] distinguish subjective intensity and meaning from objective evidence and raise psychiatric, identity, medical, or interpretive risks.

## Qualifications
These are three self-reports, not controlled comparative evidence. The sources do not isolate 5-MeO-DMT from preparation, ibogaine, support, expectation, setting, other interventions, or later behavior. They do not establish clinical indications, contraindications, optimal dose, long-term efficacy, neurobiological mechanism, medication-discontinuation safety, legal status, idealism, survival after death, or a universal consciousness model. Intensity and personal meaning are not proof of therapeutic benefit, biological rejuvenation, or metaphysical truth.
