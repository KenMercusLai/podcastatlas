# Alien (film)

Alien is the original film case in [[the-real-horror-of-alien-and-how-it-explains-why-were-not-paid-enough]]. [[PlanetMoney]] and [[ArinDube|Arin Dube]] read the Nostromo crew as blue-collar space truckers working for [[WeylandYutani]], with the alien threat made possible by a contract clause, hidden risk, and the workers' inability to refuse without losing their pay.

The episode uses the film to teach [[NegativeJobAmenities]], [[CompensatingDifferentials]], [[ShroudedJobAttributes]], and [[LaborMonopsony]]. Its argument is that Alien's horror depends not only on the creature, but on the weak worker power that lets the company convert a dangerous discovery into an employee obligation.
