# 《命自我利》 / Ming Zi Wo Li

《命自我利》 / Ming Zi Wo Li is the source-scoped text title named in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-161-caiyun-he-renji-guanxi-de-liangzi-jiuchan-1-lhkqrhbrejnhuxoouem8x8-1aujj|Hanji 161]]. The episode says an ancient figure, after testing whether people could seek wealth, longevity, sons, and fate change through action, wrote this text so descendants could follow the method.

The title is preserved exactly as it appears in the provided part 1 source markdown. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-161-caiyun-he-renji-guanxi-de-liangzi-jiuchan-4-lml7lpd3u9l7esryknmhoampgx6v|Hanji 161 part 4]] later identifies the same opening story as [[YuanLiaofan|袁了凡]]'s story and names [[LiaofanSixun|《了凡四训》]]. The wiki therefore treats 《命自我利》 as a source/transcript title variant rather than as a separate established text unless a future source supports that separation.

Its function in the Hanji 161 sequence is to anchor the fate-change question before the host turns to quantum entanglement, relationship networks, consciousness, and virtue practice.
