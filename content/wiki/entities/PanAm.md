# Pan Am

Pan Am appears in [[rolex-1]] as the airline partner for the [[RolexGMTMaster]]. The source says the GMT-Master was developed with Pan Am for jet-age pilots who needed to track two time zones.

The Pan Am case makes [[ProfessionalToolBranding]] concrete because the product's original use case was attached to glamorous, technical, global aviation rather than only to consumer fashion.
