# 稻草人旅行 / Daocaoren Travel

## Overview
稻草人旅行 / Daocaoren Travel enters the wiki as the small-group travel operator behind [[LaobaoTravelLeader|老宝]]'s National Day travel-planning and route-operations account.

## Current Profile
In the source, Daocaoren is less a generic booking platform than an operator built around route development, small-group experience design, local suppliers, and trained trip leaders. Its National Day capacity is constrained by leaders as well as hotels, restaurants, flights, and in-destination resources.

The profile is source-scoped. The episode describes Daocaoren through one leader's account of its route research, leader matching, first-route launch, and emergency backup practices; it does not provide a full company history, ownership profile, financial data, or complete product map.

## Key Characteristics
- Small-group travel operator using self-developed routes rather than simply reselling local itineraries.
- National Day capacity depends on available trip leaders as well as travel demand.
- Route development relies on product research, supplier negotiation, local experience selection, and field scouting.
- New routes require heavy leader preparation and sometimes dual-leader support with product staff.
- Leader matching uses route difficulty, leader level, and style tags.
- Holiday operations include backup leaders who can cover sudden leader absence or route disruption.
