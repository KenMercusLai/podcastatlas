# Gavrilo Princip

## Overview

Gavrilo Princip was the nineteen-year-old assassin who fatally shot [[FranzFerdinand]] and [[SophieChotek|Sophie Chotek]] in Sarajevo on 28 June 1914.

## Current Profile

In the episode, Princip is one participant in a plot that initially appears to have failed. After other conspirators did not act and Nedeljko Čabrinović's bomb missed the royal car, Princip waited near Moritz Schiller's delicatessen. A route-communication failure brought the returning motorcade to his position, where the car stopped while correcting a wrong turn. He fired twice at close range, was prevented from killing himself, and later stated that Franz Ferdinand threatened his goal of a Serb-dominated Yugoslavia. His age spared him execution, but he died during a twenty-year prison sentence.

## Key Characteristics

- Young Bosnian Serb conspirator motivated by a Yugoslav political project.
- Beneficiary of an unplanned second opportunity after the plot's first phase failed.
- Used a pistol at close range when the royal car stopped beside his position.
- Captured immediately after the shooting and tried with his associates.
- Legally ineligible for execution because he was nineteen and died in prison in 1918.

## Qualifications

The episode is a narrative reconstruction, not a complete biography or forensic case file. It leaves uncertainty over the intended victim of the second shot, Princip's immediate knowledge of the result, and the authenticity of a famous cell-wall verse. His political motive does not make every later Yugoslav commemoration or myth a direct expression of his own views.
