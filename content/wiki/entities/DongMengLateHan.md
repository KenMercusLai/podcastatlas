# 董萌 / Dong Meng (late Han)

董萌 / Dong Meng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-898-renzei-zuofu-kelian-zhiren-neng-you-duo-huangtang-lgfup6tyv85zhvnmzf6xlesmblcj|Hanji 898]] as a huangmenling who repeatedly speaks for [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦皇太后]]. The episode says [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] finds Dong Meng's argument reasonable and increases Dou Miao's treatment, because Dou Miao and [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] had supported his accession.

Dong Meng's danger comes from the older anti-Dou grievance. [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] and [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]] resent Dou Wu because Dou had tried to remove them, so they accuse Dong Meng of slandering Emperor Ling's mother, [[EmpressDowagerDongLateHan|董太后]]. Dong Meng is imprisoned and killed.

This page is scoped to one palace-accusation case, not a broad biography. Dong Meng matters as a small pro-Dou intermediary whose speech briefly reaches the ruler, then becomes a target when eunuch power recodes the issue through maternal slander and old revenge.
