# Internet Archive

The Internet Archive enters the wiki through [[tech-20260421-0421-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260421-0421-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the nonprofit institution behind the [[WaybackMachine|Wayback Machine]]. The episode treats it as a public-interest archive whose crawling and snapshots help preserve internet history, but whose role becomes contested when news publishers fear archived content could become an AI training-data backdoor.

The source does not include a direct Internet Archive response. Its contribution is instead to frame the archive as caught between [[PublicWebArchiving]], publisher copyright concerns, [[AIProxyScrapingRisk]], and the wider [[OpenWebSocialContractErosion]] created by aggressive AI crawling.
