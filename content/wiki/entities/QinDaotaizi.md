# 秦悼太子 / Qin Daotaizi

秦悼太子 / Qin Daotaizi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-89-qinwang-jing-bei-ta-dui-mabaonan-ljugedx8qxhtuzd1ja-xiwxgse19]] through the notice that he was held as a Qin hostage in [[WeiState|魏国]] and died there in 267 BCE. The episode says he had been sent to Wei in 269 BCE and was later returned for burial at Zhiyang.

The page is source-scoped because the episode uses him less as an independent biography than as a strategic puzzle. If [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] already had a crown prince in Wei, Fan Ju's advice to attack Han and Wei under [[YuanjiaoJingongStrategy|远交近攻]] raises the question of how much the king valued that hostage's safety.

The host cautiously suggests that the crown prince may not have been favored, especially because [[QinXiaowenwang|安国君柱 / 秦孝文王]] is later established as crown prince. The wiki records that as an inference from the episode, not as a settled fact.
