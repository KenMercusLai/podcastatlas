# 临武君 / Linwu Jun

临武君 / Linwu Jun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-2-mingming-yilian-baixiang-de-caize-ping-shenme-baixiang-lhfyb6hzcqkpbzdrwsxajb7hhmhe]] as the figure named in the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] notice that [[Xunzi|荀子]] once discussed military affairs before [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]]. That episode does not develop his biography; it uses him mainly to raise a chronology and setting question after describing Xunzi's appointment in [[LanlingWarringStates|兰陵]] under [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-3-xunzi-bingfa-li-cangzhe-renxing-de-mimi-lgfcp4hkruyhhrjguqhjbhllvwp43]] turns Linwu Jun from a placeholder into Xunzi's direct debate counterpart. Linwu Jun argues that military success depends on天时, 地利, speed, enemy-movement reading, and deceptive technique, citing the record of [[SunziBingfa|《孙子兵法》]]-style strategy and [[WuQi|吴起]]-style command. His role is still source-scoped, but now he anchors the tactical side of the episode's contrast with [[XunzianMilitaryLegitimacy|荀子仁义之师]].
