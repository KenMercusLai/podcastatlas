# 周赧王 / Zhou Nan Wang

周赧王 / Zhou Nan Wang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-45-yi-shi-wei-jian-junwang-daodi-gai-bu-gai-sharen-chengxing-lo3cok7cfwzrxesp-i8miz1fc1db]] as part of the show's 周纪三 frame. The episode says this section covers twenty-three years from 320 to 298 BCE, including six years of [[ZhouShenliangwang|周慎靓王]] and the first seventeen years of Zhou Nan Wang.

The source does not yet develop his reign as a personal story. His wiki role is chronological: he marks the next stage of the reduced [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]] annalistic frame while the real political action continues among Qin, Wei, Qi, Chu, Han, Zhao, Yan, and Song.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-49-mengzi-yue-wo-jiao-ni-zhiguo-ni-que-ba-wo-dang-huaping-lhy14pbf9thcfdiaulcmf8dwcef8]] locates the episode's opening in Zhou Nan Wang's first year, 314 BCE. As in Zhouji 45, the Zhou king is a chronological frame while the substance is the [[QiState|齐国]] conquest of [[YanState|燕国]] and [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]]'s advice to [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-50-zhangyi-shi-zenyang-ba-chuhuaiwang-huyou-que-de-loxg3ylhmskvdm-axtco5y-acege]] locates the next episode in Zhou Nan Wang's second year, 313 BCE. The Zhou king again functions as annalistic frame while [[QinState|秦国]] acts through [[Chulizi|樗里子]] against [[ZhaoState|赵国]] and through [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] against the [[QiState|齐国]]-[[ChuState|楚国]] alliance.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-51-chuhuaiwang-qin-xiao-er-kan-jiujiu-ruhe-mie-le-ni-ltfkdlivomnvux5xodh2rlfqf6ac]] continues into Zhou Nan Wang's third-year neighborhood, centered on 312 BCE. The Zhou king remains chronological frame while [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] turns Zhang Yi's six-li reversal into war, loses at [[DanyangBattle|丹阳之战]], loses [[Hanzhong|汉中]], and is defeated again near [[LantianBattle|蓝田之战]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2]] extends the same frame into Zhou Nan Wang's fourth year. The Zhou king remains annalistic backdrop while the episode follows [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]]'s recovery politics, records [[HanXuanhuiwang|韩宣惠王 / 韩惠王]]'s death and [[HanXiangwang|韩襄王]]'s succession, and turns [[ChenZhuangWarringStates|陈庄]]'s killing of [[ShuHouNingTong|蜀侯宁通]] into a Qin southwestern governance problem.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-53-qinhuiwang-yong-zhangyi-huan-dipi-lvmo2cwbafcg2gnxliuo18fewimk]] remains in the same 311 BCE neighborhood. The Zhou king again functions as chronological frame while [[YingSi|秦惠文王]], [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]], and [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] turn the Qin-Chu conflict from battlefield aftermath into a land-for-person bargaining and envoy-risk problem around [[Qianzhong|黔中]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-54-wo-zun-ni-shi-wang-ni-dang-wo-shi-pi-lvhcz0ban9frlmwshd9vch-q7-ie]] continues Zhou Nan Wang's fourth-year frame in 311 BCE. The Zhou king remains annalistic backdrop while Zhang Yi tours Han, Qi, Zhao, and Yan for Qin, and while [[YingSi|秦惠文王]]'s death shifts the branch toward [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] and Zhang Yi's [[PatronSuccessionExposure|靠山更替暴露风险]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-56-qiaoqiao-liangqian-duo-nianqian-sha-shi-da-zhangfu-ltokbutqgsnrno-y29xmzwa3da41]] places the ethical reassessment of persuaders in Zhou Nan Wang's fifth year, 310 BCE. The Zhou king again remains chronological frame while the episode moves from [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s completed career to [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]]'s [[MencianGreatManCriterion|大丈夫标准]] and [[YangXiong|扬雄]]'s critique of zonghengjia motive.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-57-gudai-fa-dushi-haiyou-zhe-zuoyong-lj6wi61osaytowgmdp8dtkrwb-si]] continues that fifth-year frame and then moves into the sixth and seventh years, 309-308 BCE. The Zhou king remains background while [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] suppresses [[ChenZhuangWarringStates|陈庄]], meets [[WeiXiangwang|魏襄王]], sets [[Chulizi|樗里疾 / 樗里子]] and [[GanMao|甘茂]] as left/right chancellors, and prepares the [[YiyangWarringStates|宜阳]] campaign through an oath with Gan Mao.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-58-zhaowulingwang-ruhe-dazao-qiangguo-lq9mgpib0whh0w-jkv0kxcafbnnz]] locates its main notices in Zhou Nan Wang's eighth year, 307 BCE. The Zhou king remains the annalistic frame while Gan Mao takes [[YiyangWarringStates|宜阳]], [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] dies in the Zhou capital setting after the ding-lifting episode, and [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] begins [[HufuQishe|胡服骑射]] against elite resistance.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-60-zhongguo-di-yi-wei-bachi-chaozheng-de-taihou-jingshi-ta-ltazis9qo0dwvwmzxikk8mmpvosc]] locates its notices in Zhou Nan Wang's tenth through thirteenth years, 305-302 BCE. The Zhou king again remains chronological frame while [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] presses [[ZhongshanState|中山国]], [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]] and [[WeiRan|魏冉]] consolidate [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]]'s court, Qin and Chu repair through marriage and the [[Huangji|黄棘]] meeting, and the [[MiHeng|芈横]] hostage incident flips Qin back toward Han and Wei.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-61-shui-shi-lishishang-daner-zui-fei-de-diwang-lvpoaszanascnlrmu2atinlxzmk1]] moves the frame into Zhou Nan Wang's fourteenth through sixteenth years, 301-299 BCE. The Zhou king remains annalistic backdrop while Qin, Han, Wei, and Qi attack Chu, [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]] dies and [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]] succeeds, the Qin chancellor sequence after [[Chulizi|樗里疾 / 樗里子]] is corrected through [[ChronicleChronologyDrift|编年错位]], and [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] gives the throne to [[ZhaoHe|赵何]] before personally scouting [[QinState|秦国]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-62-ji-er-kuang-ni-jiujiu-de-tui-a-lumch-ibj8m94abjh5nvr3qhiqff]] stays in the 299 BCE portion of that frame. Zhou Nan Wang again remains a date marker while [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] traps [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] at [[Wuguan|武关]], [[ChuState|楚国]] negotiates the return of [[MiHeng|芈横 / 楚顷襄王]] from [[QiState|齐国]], and [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]] is set up to enter Qin as chancellor.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-63-degao-wangzhong-mengchangjun-shi-weijunzi-lmd0owe3rq-faponbwzhkaigprin]] gives the seventeenth-year, 298 BCE frame. Zhou Nan Wang remains the chronological marker while Mengchangjun is suspected, replaced by [[LouHuan|楼缓]], imprisoned, and rescued by retainers in [[QinState|秦国]], and while [[ChuState|楚国]]'s new-king announcement leads Qin to attack through [[Wuguan|武关]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-64-gudai-gangjing-gongsunlong-yangchengji-lkrchesllfi9ipgfapft7av2mnh5]] completes the same seventeenth-year frame and the show's 周纪三 sequence. The Zhou king remains a date marker while [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] enters as a [[ZhaoState|赵国]] patron, [[GongsunLong|公孙龙]] displays [[MingjiaDisputation|名辩]], and [[KongChuan|孔川]] plus [[ZouYan|邹衍]] turn the passage into a lesson about [[TruthSeekingDebateNorm|truth-seeking debate]]. The episode then previews 周纪四, covering 297-273 BCE.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-65-wo-chuanyue-qu-gei-langman-de-chuwang-nian-shou-shi-lvyd7pve-tfu4ixsqrbdvhm6g64j]] opens that 周纪四 frame with Zhou Nan Wang's eighteenth and nineteenth years, 297-296 BCE. The Zhou king remains annalistic backdrop while [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] fails to escape Qin and dies in captivity, five states attack Qin to [[EzhiWarringStates|阏氏]], [[QinState|秦国]] returns [[WusuiWarringStates|武遂]] and [[FenglingWarringStates|封陵]] to split the pressure, [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] recruits [[Loufan|楼烦]] fighters, and Lu, Wei, and Han move through succession notices.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-66-zhongguo-lishishang-naxie-diwang-shi-bei-huohuo-e-si-de-1-lvl1uxjadiedqwad-skxrwf-usnq]] continues into Zhou Nan Wang's twentieth year, 295 BCE. The Zhou king remains the date marker while [[SimaCuo|司马错]] attacks [[WeiState|魏国]], [[QinState|秦国]] replaces [[LouHuan|楼缓]] with [[WeiRan|魏冉]], [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] finishes [[ZhongshanState|中山国]], and Zhao's post-abdication succession arrangement moves toward [[ShaqiuCrisis|沙丘之变]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-67-zhanguo-mieba-baiqi-de-junshi-caineng-jiujing-ruhe-ljw0lltowxd4qz75v-wcnlkwqskv]] covers Zhou Nan Wang's twenty-first through twenty-fifth years, 294-290 BCE. The Zhou king remains annalistic frame while Qin defeats Wei at Xiecheng, [[BaiQi|白起]] wins the [[YiqueBattle|伊阙之战]], Qin threatens Chu into a renewed marriage settlement, [[WeiRan|魏冉]] resigns and returns, [[YuanjunWarringStates|垣郡]] and [[Dingtao|陶 / 定陶]] require source-critical territorial timing, and [[MangMao|芒卯]] enters as an ambiguous Wei persuader.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-69-bikan-gudai-diwang-qipa-sifa-da-pandian-lukvjsktg-pgfjhizvypwmdmw-13]] jumps to Zhou Nan Wang's thirtieth year, 285 BCE. Zhou Nan Wang remains the date frame while [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] meets Chu and Zhao, [[MengAo|蒙敖]] attacks [[QiState|齐国]], [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]] kills remonstrants and overreaches after destroying [[SongState|宋国]], and [[YanZhaowang|燕昭王]] plus [[LeYi|乐毅]] prepare the anti-Qi coalition.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-70-xihuan-huayang-zuosi-de-qiguo-junzhu-luuyasp5-nhnikdcozzaueosb-md]] moves to Zhou Nan Wang's thirty-first year, 284 BCE. The Zhou king again remains the annalistic frame while Le Yi commands the coalition against Qi, [[Linzi|临淄]] falls, Qi Min Wang flees, and [[ZhuoChi|卓齿]] kills him near Ju. The episode closes by turning the event back into [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s "资治" purpose through [[Xunzi|荀子]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-71-xunzide-xingbenelun-lnyfxoxauexhwbrwkdevs8m4mr2j]] remains in the same thirty-first-year, 284 BCE aftermath. Zhou Nan Wang is again only the chronological frame while the source unpacks [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s [[Xunzi|荀子]] quotation into [[XunzianWangbaThreePaths|荀子王霸三分法]] and then turns toward [[LeYi|乐毅]]'s search for a worthy person in the devastated Qi field.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-72-shenme-yang-de-ren-cai-shi-zhongzhen-zhishi-logxegnqexgc7z-lodw6tr6-8f28]] continues that same thirty-first-year, 284 BCE aftermath. Zhou Nan Wang remains the annalistic frame while [[WangZhu|王蠋]] refuses [[YanState|燕国]] service and dies, [[LeYi|乐毅]] turns conquest into occupation governance in [[QiState|齐国]], [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]], [[WeiZhaowang|魏昭王]], and [[HanXiwang|韩釐王]] meet at [[Luoyi|洛邑]], and [[TianFazhang|田法章]] begins a hidden flight after [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]]'s death.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-73-lishishang-ziyou-lianai-de-kaichuangzhe-shi-shui-lkumfsfialqgdl6qpb3c-hnz7h9u]] stays in the same thirty-first-year aftermath. Zhou Nan Wang remains the date frame while [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] meets [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] at Rangcheng, Qin attacks [[WeiState|魏国]] and takes [[AnchengWarringStates|安城]], [[WangSunGu|王孙古]] kills [[ZhuoChi|淖齿 / 卓齿]], and [[TianFazhang|田法章]] is restored at [[JuWarringStates|莒城]] as [[QiXiangWang|齐襄王]] with [[JunWangHou|君王后]] as queen.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-76-shui-geile-chuqingxiangwang-de-zixin-lsut4cfic7xbbrmbmimrruf7n6y0]] moves Zhou Nan Wang into his thirty-third and thirty-fourth years, 282-281 BCE. He remains the annalistic frame for Qin's attacks on Zhao, but becomes more active when he hears that [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]] may attack the [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]]; he sends [[DongzhouWugong|东周武公]] to persuade Chu that taking Zhou land, people, or [[Jiuding|九鼎]] is not worth the symbolic cost.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-77-lianpo-yu-linxiangru-shishang-zui-zhuming-de-yidui-hao-jiyou-lmelnhhtfuzezvmmsuw4yf10plsb]] moves the frame into Zhou Nan Wang's thirty-fifth and thirty-sixth years, 280-279 BCE. Zhou Nan Wang remains the date marker while [[QinState|秦国]] attacks [[ZhaoState|赵国]] through [[BaiQi|白起]], pressures [[ChuState|楚国]] through [[SimaCuo|司马错]] and Bai Qi, and then turns the Qin-Zhao relationship into the [[Mianchi|渑池]] meeting and the [[LianPo|廉颇]]-[[LinXiangru|蔺相如]] "将相和" aftermath.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-82-daqin-de-hewuqi-baiqi-laile-lo0w8mifkijhzmx7fdwcimmv7ade]] continues through Zhou Nan Wang's thirty-seventh to fortieth years, 278-275 BCE. Zhou Nan Wang remains the chronological frame while [[BaiQi|白起]] captures the Chu capital region, Qin creates [[NanCommandery|南郡]], [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]] moves toward Chen and recalls [[ZhuangXin|庄辛]], [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] succeeds in Wei, [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] receives his title, and [[WeiRan|魏冉]] pounds Wei in two campaigns.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-83-zhuhou-bailan-jing-chengle-jingdian-ji-zhong-ji-ljp7nbqnrctr5mw6i9u982abmx-1]] moves the frame into Zhou Nan Wang's forty-first and forty-second years. The king remains the annalistic date marker while [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] tries a weak [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] posture with [[QiState|齐国]], [[WeiState|魏国]] and [[ZhaoState|赵国]] attack [[HanState|韩国]] at [[HuayangBattle|华阳之战]], Han draws in Qin through [[ThreatenedDefectionLeverage|投敌威胁式求援]], and [[HanXiwang|韩釐王]] dies before [[HanHuanhuiwang|韩桓惠王]] succeeds.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-85-xuantaihou-weihe-sitong-30-nian-hou-ansha-le-laoqingren-lg7zy6vqtdjcufou4-mox5qkg2ct5]] opens the 周纪五 frame in Zhou Nan Wang's forty-third year, 272 BCE, and previews the section's final seventeen-year span. Zhou Nan Wang remains the chronological marker while [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] accompanies the Chu crown prince into Qin, [[YanState|燕国]] suffers coup and attack, and [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]]'s killing of the [[YiquState|义渠]] king leads to Qin's [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-86-lishishang-kao-shouzu-de-jiangjun-jingran-shi-ta-lmmmfz4ii-hx7trbxv2rcpkllcq]] moves the frame to Zhou Nan Wang's forty-fourth year, around 271 BCE. Zhou Nan Wang remains the annalistic marker while [[ZhaoState|赵国]]'s narrative shifts from [[LinXiangru|蔺相如]] and [[LianPo|廉颇]] war notices toward [[ZhaoShe|赵奢]]'s tax-enforcement story and [[EliteFiscalLawCompliance|贵族税法同遵]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-87-xialu-xiangfeng-yongzhe-sheng-zhongyu-zhaodao-chuchu-le-loppr83sdq5hhesmqihckwmhpl2h]] moves the frame to Zhou Nan Wang's forty-fifth year, 270 BCE. Zhou Nan Wang again remains the date marker while [[QinState|秦国]] attacks [[ZhaoState|赵国]] at [[EyuBattle|阏与 / 燕宇之战]], [[ZhaoShe|赵奢]] wins the rescue campaign, and the episode pivots toward [[FanJu|范雎]]'s coming humiliation-and-rise branch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-89-qinwang-jing-bei-ta-dui-mabaonan-ljugedx8qxhtuzd1ja-xiwxgse19]] moves the frame through Zhou Nan Wang's forty-sixth to forty-ninth years, 269-266 BCE. The king remains the annalistic marker while [[QinState|秦国]] attacks Zhao and Wei, [[QinDaotaizi|秦悼太子]] dies in [[WeiState|魏国]], [[QinXiaowenwang|安国君柱]] later becomes crown prince, and [[FanJu|范雎]] shifts from [[YuanjiaoJingongStrategy|远交近攻]] to a direct attack on [[QinFourNoblesCourtPower|秦国四贵权力结构]].
