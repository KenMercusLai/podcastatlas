## Overview

孙会宗 / Sun Huizong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-457-hanxuandi-xinxiong-xiaai-shiji-gongchen-yangyun-yinhe-er-si-lpcfrhewqmmexb3ofg18iqbdsslm|Hanji 457]] as the correspondent who warns [[YangYunWesternHan|杨恽]] that a disgraced former official should show fear, humility, and withdrawal rather than visible resentment.

## Current Profile

Sun Huizong's role is small but structurally important. He sees that Yang Yun's social display, drinking, and complaints can be read as unrepentance in [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]'s court. His letter tries to move Yang Yun into a safer performance of submission. Yang Yun's refusal and mocking reply turn Sun from adviser into the addressee of a dangerous document.

The source later says Yang Yun regrets not listening to Sun before execution. Sun is also harmed by association: after the case expands, he is among the people removed from office for close contact with Yang Yun.

## Key Characteristics

- Warning correspondent who recognizes the danger of post-disgrace visibility.
- Practical adviser whose counsel emphasizes posture, fear, and self-protection more than moral vindication.
- Addressee whose warning triggers Yang Yun's mocking reply.
- Association casualty in the case's wider cleanup.
