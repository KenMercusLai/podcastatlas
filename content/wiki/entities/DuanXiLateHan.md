## Overview

段禧 / Duan Xi enters the wiki as the Western Regions protector appointed after [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]] is removed for misgoverning the Western Regions, with Hanji 778 and Hanji 779 both placing him inside the Qiuci relocation-and-withdrawal crisis.

## Current Profile

Hanji 779 gives Duan Xi a narrow but important role in the 107 CE Western Regions collapse. Ren Shang's harsh approach triggers rebellion and an attack on the protectorate position at Shule. After Ren temporarily defeats Pan Tuo but loses court confidence, the Deng regency removes him and appoints Duan Xi as the new protector.

Duan Xi's tenure is immediately constrained by the inherited crisis. Together with [[LiangJinLateHan|Liang Jin]], he moves the protectorate seat to [[QiuciKingdom|Qiuci]], a still pro-Han polity under the king Ba Ba. The relocation provokes Qiuci nobles and their Gumo and Wensu allies. Liang Jin wins the emergency battle, but the court soon recalls Duan Xi, Liang Jin, Zhao Bo, and the tuntian troops as part of the decision to abandon the Western Regions.

Hanji 778 backfills this sequence as part of [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]]'s initial regency burden. Duan Xi therefore marks the limit of replacement appointment: changing the protector can stabilize the office briefly, but it cannot by itself repair local rebellion, regional alliance pressure, and central withdrawal logic.

## Key Characteristics

- Replacement protector appointed after Ren Shang's governance failure.
- Crisis manager whose office begins after local trust and regional balance have already been damaged.
- Linked to the protectorate relocation into Qiuci, which stabilizes access only briefly before provoking local opposition.
- Politically subordinate to the Deng court's final cost decision to withdraw from the Western Regions despite temporary Qiuci stabilization.

## Qualifications

Duan Xi is currently bounded to Hanji 778 and Hanji 779. The page does not infer his earlier career, personal motives, or independent policy views. His importance is institutional: he marks the failed handoff from Ren Shang's protectorate to the final withdrawal decision.
