# 楼敬 / Lou Jing

楼敬 / Lou Jing enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-170-liubang-weisha-cong-luoyang-banjia-dao-changan-2-lpqtexvxjrbrpjgbw5hrgmhl9apk|Hanji 170 part 2]] as the Qi man who reaches [[Luoyi|洛阳 / 洛邑]], refuses to change into fancier clothes before audience, and directly asks [[LiuBang|刘邦]] whether he plans to make Luoyang the new capital in imitation of Zhou.

His argument is historical and strategic. Zhou could use Luoyang because its authority rested on generations of accumulated virtue, ritual prestige, and a settled lordly order. Liu Bang's [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]] is different: it has just been made through violent campaigns, many deaths, and unsettled eastern territories. Lou Jing therefore argues for entering [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]], where old Qin territory supplies fertile land, defensive passes, and a base that can survive eastern rebellion.

The episode treats Lou Jing as the first adviser who breaks the superficial Zhou analogy. [[ZhangLiang|张良]] later confirms the argument with a fuller geography-and-transport analysis, after which Liu Bang moves west and fixes the capital at [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]]. Liu Bang rewards Lou Jing as langzhong and names him Fengchun Jun.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-177-beikun-baidengshan-liubang-mingxuan-yixian-ljdmxf6bej8ju0gmvm9cp48gy0on|Hanji 177]] uses him under the Liu Jing / 刘敬 name in the run-up to the [[BaidengEncirclement|白登之围]]. After other envoys report Xiongnu weakness, Liu Jing argues that an enemy should normally display strength before battle; because [[MaoDunChanyu|冒顿单于]] is showing only weak horses, sick livestock, and tired people, the display is probably deliberate bait. [[LiuBang|刘邦]] rejects the warning, imprisons him, and continues the northern advance, making Liu Jing a source case for ignored strategic reconnaissance.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-178-maodun-weihe-yao-fangguo-liubang-zhe-cai-shi-zhenxiang-1-lumszkbr-klnlodxmxdf-n8p5wym|Hanji 178]] gives Liu Jing the post-crisis vindication. After escaping Baideng, Liu Bang apologizes for rejecting the warning, releases Liu Jing, punishes envoys who had underestimated the Xiongnu, and grants Liu Jing the Jianxin Hou title. The same episode then makes Liu Jing the proposer of [[HanXiongnuHeqinPolicy|汉匈和亲政策]], arguing that marriage may restrain Modu where immediate military solution has failed.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-178-maodun-weihe-yao-fangguo-liubang-zhe-cai-shi-zhenxiang-2-loluh53iyrzo4ucf3ttzmn3-otkv|Hanji 178 part 2]] confirms that Liu Jing's proposal has become the post-Baideng policy direction. After the return through Zhao and the move into Weiyang Palace, the episode says Liu Bang's Xiongnu stance has shifted from conquest toward heqin and compromise.
