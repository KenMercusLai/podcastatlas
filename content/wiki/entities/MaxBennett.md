# Max Bennett

## Overview
Max Bennett appears in the wiki through [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] as the author of [[ABriefHistoryOfIntelligence]]. The episode uses his book as a scaffold for explaining why minds evolved to survive rather than to see other people directly.

## Current Profile
Bennett's source role is conceptual rather than biographical. Meng Yan takes the book's sequence of intelligence breakthroughs and rereads it as a history of the mechanisms that generate valuation, wanting, simulation, interpersonal projection, and language labels. In the wiki, Bennett therefore anchors a bridge between neuroscience, evolution, AI-era cognition, and relationship misrecognition.

## Key Characteristics
- Authorial anchor for [[ABriefHistoryOfIntelligence]] in the E46 source.
- Supplies the five-breakthrough sequence Meng Yan adapts into [[EvolutionaryMisrecognition]].
- Connects biological intelligence to contemporary questions about AI, world models, and human self-understanding.
- Functions as a source-mediated figure; the current wiki evidence does not support a broader independent biography.
