# 李璧 / Li Bi

李璧 / Li Bi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-153-xiayi-huace-canbai-hou-liubang-ruhe-qisi-huisheng-2-ll-rvx07pyw-qg1jofkjro-qfllu|Hanji 153 part 2]] as a former Qin cavalry specialist recommended when [[LiuBang|刘邦]] wants to build a mounted force after the [[PengchengBattle|彭城之战]]. The source groups him with [[LuoJiaQinCavalry|骆甲]] as someone with the technical background needed for cavalry organization.

Li Bi's most important action is refusing to be the top commander. He argues that because he is originally a Qin person, Han troops may not submit to him easily, so the main command should go to a trusted Liu-side figure who is skilled at riding. He accepts a supporting role under [[GuanYing|灌婴]], becoming one of the specialists behind [[HanCavalryFormation|汉军骑兵建设]].
