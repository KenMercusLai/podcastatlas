# 乌巢夜袭 / Wuchao Raid (late Han)

## Overview
The Wuchao Raid is the decisive supply-depot strike inside the [[GuanduCampaign|官渡 Campaign]]. The current evidence now covers both the pre-raid trigger and the raid's result: [[XuYouLateHan|Xu You]] defects after Yuan-camp pressure, identifies the vulnerable Gushi-Wuchao supply zone, and [[CaoCao|Cao Cao]] converts that intelligence into a commander-led night attack that burns [[YuanShao|Yuan Shao]]'s grain reserve.

## Current Profile
Cao's choice to move on Wuchao begins before the fire. At Guandu, his own supplies are nearly exhausted and he has considered retreating to [[Xudu|许都]]. [[XunYu|荀彧]] persuades him to hold, [[XunYou|荀攸]] helps turn supply pressure outward by targeting [[HanMengLateHan|韩猛]]'s convoy, and [[JuShou|沮授]] separately warns Yuan Shao to strengthen the outer protection around [[ChunyuQiong|淳于琼]]'s later grain convoy through [[JiangQiLateHan|蒋奇]].

The decisive intelligence arrives because Yuan Shao's adviser system breaks internally. Xu You's proposed Xudu raid is rejected, [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] arrests his family at Ye, and Xu You defects to Cao Cao with information that Yuan Shao's supplies around Gushi and Wuchao are lightly guarded. Cao then moves personally with a small disguised force, using Yuan banners, silence discipline, night movement, and carried fuel.

The raid succeeds because Cao concentrates instead of splitting his small force. When Yuan reinforcements approach, Cao refuses to detach troops for defense and drives the assault harder, making the burning of Wuchao the priority that overwhelms the local defenders.

The aftermath turns a logistics loss into a command-system failure. [[ChunyuQiong|Chunyu Qiong]] is captured and executed, [[GuoTu|Guo Tu]] blames [[ZhangHeLateHan|Zhang He]], Zhang He and [[GaoLanLateHan|Gao Lan]] defect, Yuan Shao flees with [[YuanTan|Yuan Tan]], and [[JuShou|Ju Shou]] becomes a captured loyalist rather than a transferable adviser.

## Key Characteristics
- A high-risk commander-led raid against the enemy's grain depot.
- A pre-raid intelligence event produced by Yuan-camp rejected advice, family arrest, and Xu You's defection.
- A logistics strike that becomes psychologically decisive once Yuan troops learn the supplies are gone.
- A test of Cao Cao's willingness to act on defector intelligence through silent, disguised night movement under extreme supply pressure.
- A battlefield decision point where Yuan Shao misallocates force between rescuing Wuchao and attacking Cao's camp.
- A catalyst for elite defection, especially Zhang He and Gao Lan's surrender after Guo Tu's slander.
- A campaign hinge that connects White Horse/Yanjin shocks, pre-Wuchao logistics warnings, the raid itself, and the post-victory settlement in Hanji 1050.

## Qualifications
This page is grounded in two adjacent podcast source notes and should not be read as a full military monograph on Wuchao. Hanji 1048 stops at the launch of the disguised movement; Hanji 1049 supplies the burning, rescue-failure, and collapse sequence. Casualty figures, mutilation details, Xu You's inner motive, and the exact sequence of camp panic remain source-scoped.

## What Changed
- Added the pre-raid setup from Hanji 1048: Cao's depleted supplies, Xun Yu's hold counsel, Ju Shou's ignored protection warning, and Xu You's defection.
- Reframed Wuchao from only a raid outcome into a sequence of supply pressure, adviser-system failure, intelligence transfer, and concentrated night attack.
- Added Jiang Qi, Han Meng, Xu Huang, Shi Huan, Xun Yu, Xun You, and Shen Pei to the event's surrounding evidence and relationships.
