# 金尚 / Jin Shang

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-964-bie-jiaoao-bie-jiaoao-bie-jiaoao-lo2-edzffu0v9ixoabc06rx-oqoq|Hanji 964]] backfills Jin Shang's earlier Yanzhou title conflict before the Yuan Shu refusal branch. After [[LiuDaiLateHan|刘岱]] dies and [[CaoCao|曹操]] enters [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] through local invitation, the court appoints Jin Shang as Yanzhou inspector. Cao Cao blocks him, and Jin Shang is forced to attach to [[YuanShu|袁术]].

金尚 / Jin Shang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1016-sanguo-luanshi-li-chengui-jiazu-de-mingyun-jueze-ltuckdcxpyxk-xmzp8rt-2gjfzcs|Hanji 1016]] as a former Yan province inspector whom [[YuanShu|袁术]] tries to make taiwei after claiming imperial status. Jin Shang refuses the office and tries to flee, but Yuan Shu kills him.

The source uses Jin Shang as a compact caution rather than a full biography.
