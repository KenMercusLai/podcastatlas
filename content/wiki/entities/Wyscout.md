# Wyscout

Wyscout appears in [[ep-13-soccer-analytics-through-the-lens-of-coaching]] as a football scouting and video/data platform example inside [[BrunoSoccerCoach|Bruno]]'s discussion of player recruitment. The source does not analyze the product in depth, but uses it to show how clubs and scouts can review players beyond their local networks before deciding whether deeper evaluation is worth the cost.

In the wiki, Wyscout belongs to the [[DataDrivenFootballScouting]] and [[SoccerScoutingDueDiligence]] branch. Its source-scoped role is not that software replaces scouts, but that platforms can make the first filtering step wider, faster, and cheaper before live viewing and human fit checks.
