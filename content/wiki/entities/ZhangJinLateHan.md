# 张津 / Zhang Jin (late Han)

张津 / Zhang Jin (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-943-quanshi-kaoyan-renxing-beihou-cangzhe-juda-xiongxian-lg7ia5xok-swdmhml35yv9qqkvt|Hanji 943]] as a close aide of [[HeJin|何进]] through whom [[YuanShao|袁绍]] advances the proposal to eliminate the eunuchs. The source does not develop him as an independent strategist; his importance is as an access channel into He Jin's decision circle.

The episode uses Zhang Jin to show how Yuan Shao's advice begins moving before Hanji 944's broader anti-eunuch planning. After [[JianShuoLateHan|蹇硕]] is killed, Yuan Shao's recommendation reaches He Jin through a trusted intermediary, helping shift He Jin from eliminating one rival eunuch toward considering a wider purge of palace eunuch power.
