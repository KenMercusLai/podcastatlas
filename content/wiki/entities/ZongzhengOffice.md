# 宗正 / Zongzheng Office

宗正 / Zongzheng Office enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-178-maodun-weihe-yao-fangguo-liubang-zhe-cai-shi-zhenxiang-2-loluh53iyrzo4ucf3ttzmn3-otkv|Hanji 178 part 2]] as the office set up to manage the imperial Liu clan after [[LiuBang|刘邦]] moves into [[WeiyangPalace|未央宫]]. The episode says the office began under Qin and was one of the Qin-Han nine ministers.

Its importance here is institutional. Early Western Han has been expanding Liu-family titles through [[SameSurnameKingEnfeoffment|同姓王分封]], but the clan also requires central administration. Zongzheng therefore marks the point where kinship becomes part of state bureaucracy rather than only household relation.
