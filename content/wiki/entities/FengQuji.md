# 冯去疾 / Feng Quji

冯去疾 / Feng Quji enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-127-3-haiwai-huigui-yipi-zhujian-jiekai-huhai-lidi-zhi-mi-lja0pankcx4ugtylbxxqkwuavdnw|Qinji 127-3]] as a Qin minister named in the [[ZhaoZhengShu|《赵正书》]] account of [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]]'s final succession discussion. In that version, he appears with [[LiSi|李斯]] near the dying emperor and helps argue that the court should establish the nearby [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] rather than summon a broader discussion from far away.

The page is source-scoped because the episode itself flags Feng Quji as a difficulty. The host says his presence in the deathbed scene is hard to reconcile with the expectation that he was at [[Xianyang|咸阳]], and leaves the contradiction unresolved rather than forcing a harmonized narrative.

For the wiki, Feng Quji matters as a marker of the [[ZhaoZhengShu|《赵正书》]] variant. If he is present, Hu Hai's establishment looks more like [[EmergencySuccessionLegitimation|临终就近立储]] by senior ministers; if he is not, the alternate account loses one of the voices that makes the decision look procedurally collective.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-4-ruhe-dinglun-qinxiang-lisi-de-xiachang-ljnz0vflgr62hyktrg5cigz7c-7x|Qinji 130-4]] gives Feng Quji a later, less source-problematic court role. As right chancellor, he joins left chancellor [[LiSi|李斯]] and general [[FengJieQin|冯劫 / 冯杰]] in warning [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] that rebellion is worsening while [[AfangPalace|阿房宫]] construction, taxes, and frontier rotations keep burdening the population.

The episode presents the advice as loyal burden reduction, but Hu Hai reads it as a failure of the three senior officials and orders them jailed. Feng Quji therefore shifts from a contested accession-witness in Qinji 127-3 to a concrete example of high ministerial remonstrance failing under Hu Hai's captured court.

## Key Claims
- Feng Quji is named in the alternate source tradition, not in the previously ingested forged-edict account.
- His function in the episode is ministerial validation for a nearby-heir emergency choice.
- His contested location makes him a source-critical warning, not a settled biographical anchor.
- Qinji 130-4 makes him a right chancellor who joins Li Si and Feng Jie in a practical remonstrance against construction and corvee burdens, then is imprisoned.
