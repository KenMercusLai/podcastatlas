# Lauren Fleshman

## Overview

Lauren Fleshman is an elite runner, coach, parent, and author who argues that girls' and women's sport should be designed around female development rather than an unchanged male performance model.

## Current Profile

In the episode, Fleshman connects adolescent performance plateaus, scholarship timing, body ideals, revealing uniforms, early competition, and adult incentives. Her recurring standard is whether a system preserves health, autonomy, honest body knowledge, and long-term love of sport while still allowing ambition.

She rejects simple gender substitution in coaching. Women can reproduce harmful norms, while men can coach well if they learn about female bodies and development. Education, accountability, and athlete voice therefore matter more than assuming identity alone guarantees safe practice.

## Key Characteristics

- Interprets girls' performance through developmental waves rather than linear progress.
- Connects body restriction and appearance pressure to institutional incentives, not only individual psychology.
- Defends ambitious sport while opposing systems that make short-term results the price of belonging.
- Treats athlete autonomy and unconditional parental love as protections against identity capture.
- Uses personal experience as an athlete, coach, and parent while acknowledging the need for structural reform.
