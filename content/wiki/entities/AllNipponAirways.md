# All Nippon Airways / 全日空

All Nippon Airways / 全日空 appears in [[vol-268-liang-ge-lao-si-lai-si-1003563933]] as the Japanese airline whose [[LockheedL1011TriStar|L-1011 TriStar]] purchase became central to the [[LockheedBriberyScandal]]. The episode says the airline ultimately bought 21 TriStar aircraft, giving [[LockheedCorporation|Lockheed]] an important overseas order after the aircraft lagged behind the [[McDonnellDouglasDC10|DC-10]] in the U.S. market.

The airline's source role is to show why sales pressure intensified. Once the TriStar's launch was delayed by the [[RollsRoyceRB211|RB211]] engine, overseas buyers became more important to Lockheed's survival story, and aircraft procurement became entangled with intermediaries, politicians, and later anti-bribery law.
