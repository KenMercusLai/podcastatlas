# National Transportation Safety Board

The National Transportation Safety Board appears in [[omission-accomplished-why-the-iran-war-cycle-spins-on-6a6c7475aa1642a30f17f1cb]] as a later institution in [[WallyFunk]]'s aviation career. The episode says Funk investigated air crashes for the NTSB, sometimes reaching difficult crash sites by mule or by abseiling.

The page is source-scoped: the episode uses the NTSB to show that Funk's blocked astronaut path did not end her technical and operational aviation work.
