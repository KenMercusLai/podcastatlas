# 庞煖 / Pang Nuan

庞煖 / Pang Nuan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-106-jingren-faxian-xinlingjun-de-feizhengchang-siwang-lsj3kd2tb4tqtuvhwvsdwkh-h5el]] as the [[ZhaoState|赵国]] commander whom [[YanWangXi|燕王喜]] underestimates after consulting [[JuXin|剧辛]]. Ju Xin had once been in Zhao and knew Pang Nuan, but the episode says he tells Yan that Pang Nuan is easy to handle.

That judgment fails in battle. Yan Wang Xi sends Ju Xin to attack Zhao, Pang Nuan meets the invasion, kills Ju Xin, and captures 20,000 Yan soldiers. The source uses the episode to show that Zhao remains dangerous even after losing [[LianPo|廉颇]] and suffering earlier disasters.

Pang Nuan therefore extends [[PostDisasterPredationMisread|灾后趁虚误判]] beyond the earlier Li Fu/Lian Po case. The error is not only reading population loss after [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] as helplessness; it is also treating personal familiarity with an enemy commander as enough to price his actual battlefield capacity.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-107-zuihou-yici-hezong-faqin-fa-le-ge-jimo-lrzq1qks7z-oromd-5f-dgqjwjhw]] gives Pang Nuan a different role through the [[Shiji|《史记》]] Zhao-house account. In that tradition, he commands a Zhao-Chu-Wei-Yan anti-Qin coalition in 241 BCE, gains no Qin territory, and then turns toward [[QiState|齐国]] to take Raoan. The page keeps this beside, not over, the Chunshenjun-led version because the episode treats the two accounts as unresolved variants.
