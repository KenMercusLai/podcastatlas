# Nike

Nike is a global sportswear company discussed in [[e241-paoxie-jishu-diedaishi-malasong-paojin-2-xiaoshi-kao-ren-haishi-kao-xie]] as the brand that reshaped modern marathon racing through [[NikeVaporfly]] and Alphafly-style super shoes. The episode contrasts Nike's earlier lead in carbon-plate racing shoes with [[Adidas]]' reported sub-2 breakthrough through [[AdidasAdiosProEvo3]].

[[138-angpao-zhongguo-zhong-zhiying-chaoji-xingxing-bu-banka-feng-tou-quan-1-138-1]] uses Nike as the DTC caution case. The episode says Nike's strong turn toward direct channels reduced some wholesale reach and helped create shelf opportunity for challenger running brands such as [[HOKA]] and [[OnRunning]].

[[16-shishang-shouhaizhe-jing-luse-huo-wei-zui-du-yanse-559889282]] adds a safety-history angle through [[BillBowerman|Bill Bowerman / 鲍尔曼]]. The source uses Bowerman's reported long-term harm from shoe-glue chemicals to show that modern sportswear can also involve [[ToxicFashionMaterialRisk]] and [[FashionSupplyChainExternality]], even when the consumer-facing brand is associated with health and performance.

[[xingbake-huiying-mixue-bingcheng-daigong-deng-chuanwen-li-ning-fouren-yu-mubapei-qianyue-1006054195]] adds Nike as the incumbent sponsorship context for [[KylianMbappe|Kylian Mbappe / 姆巴佩]]. The source says Mbappe had cooperated with Nike for years and that reports about that relationship changing made the denied [[LiNing|Li-Ning]] rumor more plausible.

## Key Claims
- The episode says Nike's breakthrough was not merely adding a carbon plate, but combining plate structure with lightweight, soft, high-rebound foam.
- [[NikeVaporfly]] and Alphafly gave Nike a period of elite-race and mass-consumer influence that competitors challenged as "spring shoe" style advantage.
- Nike's later risk is framed as organizational and commercial as much as technical: business decline, DTC channel controversy, leadership change, and slower racing-shoe innovation can weaken a technology lead.
- The FengTouQuan source adds that DTC can become overextended when it weakens channel coverage and lets competitors occupy wholesale shelf space.
- Episode 16 adds that footwear innovation also has an exposure history when glues, solvents, and prototyping work are included.
- The 声动早咖啡 source adds that athlete-sponsorship uncertainty can create public rumor even before a new brand deal exists.
