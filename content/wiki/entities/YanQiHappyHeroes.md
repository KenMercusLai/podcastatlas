# 燕七（《欢乐英雄》） / Yan Qi

## Overview

燕七 is one of the four protagonists of [[HappyHeroesGuLong|《欢乐英雄》]]. She enters 富贵山庄 while fleeing danger and presents herself as male, making gender disguise one of the group's concealed identities.

## Current Profile

The bounded source treats the disguise less as a fair-play puzzle for readers than as a comic test of [[GuoDalu|郭大路]]'s assumptions. 王动 and later 林太平 understand the situation before he does, while Guo first recognizes that his attachment to Yan Qi exceeds ordinary friendship.

Her second major secret is being the daughter of the notorious 南宫丑. Gender presentation and family origin therefore place romance, shame, danger, and trust inside the same relationship.

## Key Characteristics

- Enters the ensemble in male disguise while evading pursuit.
- Combines socially masculine-coded ease with a concealed female identity.
- Receives Guo Dalu's clearest preferential attachment.
- Carries a second identity secret as 南宫丑's daughter.
- Helps make secrecy compatible with intimacy rather than proof that friendship is false.
