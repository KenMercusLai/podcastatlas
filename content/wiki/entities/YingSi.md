# 嬴驷 / Ying Si

嬴驷 / Ying Si appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-13-gongsunyang-keke-bianfa-re-shashen-1-lgmjapc2eecefbdshnpenzfcv6ga]] as [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]]'s crown prince. The episode introduces him at the endpoint, after [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]'s new law has been active for more than a year and public complaints have accumulated.

The source uses Ying Si as a suspense point rather than a full biography. His violation of the new law turns [[ShangYangReforms|商鞅变法]] from a general policy program into a legitimacy test: if the law does not reach the ruler's heir, the reform loses credibility; if it does, it threatens the court's highest-status people.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-13-gongsunyang-keke-bianfa-re-shashen-2-lk-d9uibhneerfol6z2rrle-6ypm]] resolves the suspense by making Ying Si's tutors suffer in his stead. The episode stresses that the heir's status prevents direct punishment, but the tutor punishments still make the case a [[HighStatusLawEnforcementSignal|high-status law-enforcement signal]]. That makes Ying Si the hinge between Qin's new legal credibility and the later resentment carried by figures such as [[GongziQian|公子虔]] and [[GongsunJia|公孙贾]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-23-weihuiwang-hui-buyong-gongshu-zhi-yan-lveaqklo7oncmqudgmdk7l03qhsf]] returns to Ying Si as successor after Qin Xiao Gong's death in 338 BCE. The episode does not develop his independent rule, but his accession is the political turn that lets [[GongziQian|公子虔]]'s faction attack Shang Yang and makes the earlier prince-law conflict part of the larger [[WarringStatesReformBacklash]] sequence.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-30-suqin-de-kaigua-rensheng-lkmn-fruukdxgkddx7zb4lc3o9bw]] refers to him as [[QinState|秦国]]'s Qin Huiwen Wang in [[SuQin|苏秦]]'s failed lobbying itinerary. The episode says Qin had just executed [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] and was therefore unfriendly to persuader politics, so Su Qin's renewed learning and [[ChuaimoTechnique|揣摩]] do not win access there.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-31-hezong-lianheng-weiou-qin-lncpgmxrojc0y1meg9a3ul-o9nih]] returns to him as the Qin ruler who trusts [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] after Zhang Yi reaches Qin. The episode makes Ying Si's court the place where Su Qin's hidden manipulation of Zhang Yi turns into a diplomatic buffer for the still-forming [[HezongAlliance|合纵]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] shows Ying Si using that court talent. After Qin captures [[PuyangWarringStates|蒲阳]], Zhang Yi advises him to return it to [[WeiState|魏国]] and send a Qin prince as hostage. Ying Si's acceptance lets Zhang Yi turn a captured city into [[Shangjun|上郡]]'s fifteen counties and elevates Zhang Yi inside Qin.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-35-lishishang-diyi-qibing-jiaofu-shi-ta-lnxgnu1czkl-ftfb3gq9kzxwnxdk]] keeps him as Qin Huiwen Wang while Qin balances two fronts. Qin defeats [[YiquState|义渠]] without fully ending the frontier problem, then returns [[Jiaoyi|焦邑]] and [[QuwoWarringStates|曲沃]] to [[WeiState|魏国]]. The episode reads the returned-land move as Zhang Yi's way to improve the diplomatic setting before Ying Si claims kingly status.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-36-weisijun-de-diwang-zhishu-lt5cpdm9yqdj4erwykd0kubfpucz]] gives the title claim itself. In 324 BCE Ying Si becomes Qin Huiwen Wang in a fuller status sense by claiming kingly title; the episode says the move is calmer than [[XuzhouMutualKingship|徐州相王]] because Qi and Wei have already set a precedent and because [[QinState|秦国]] is strong enough that other rulers do not respond as sharply.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-39-lishishang-zhangyi-shi-zenyang-nudui-qiangdi-de-lv2gc2wtcxuhugfgixk7rbbhpa6x]] keeps Ying Si active behind [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s Wei posting. The episode says Zhang Yi has not yet delivered the desired Wei submission to Qin, but Qin Huiwen Wang secretly treats him better instead of punishing him. The source reads this as [[GraceDebtLoyaltyPressure|恩赏负债式忠诚压力]]: favor creates shame and repayment pressure, helping explain why Zhang Yi remains Qin-oriented while serving in [[WeiState|魏国]]. This should be kept source-scoped alongside [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-38-gao-jianzhi-de-zhangyi-bu-yiban-lobhcpz679psy54k0iduccqkfcrn|Zhouji 38]]'s different [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] supplement.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-47-yanwangkuai-de-lixiangzhuyi-lhgl6rqkv9nonzxc9xqquzzlz5pr]] gives Ying Si's Ba-Shu decision directly. He considers attacking [[ShuStateWarringStates|蜀国]], hears [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] propose the higher-status eastern route against [[HanState|韩国]] and the [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]], and then follows [[SimaCuo|司马错]]'s lower-risk argument for gaining land, wealth, and people through Shu.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-50-zhangyi-shi-zenyang-ba-chuhuaiwang-huyou-que-de-loxg3ylhmskvdm-axtco5y-acege]] shows Ying Si using Zhang Yi against the [[QiState|齐国]]-[[ChuState|楚国]] relationship directly. Qin Huiwen Wang wants to attack Qi but worries about Qi's alliance with Chu, so he sends Zhang Yi to promise [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] six hundred li of [[Shangyu|商於]]. The resulting [[UnverifiedTerritorialPromiseTrap|未验地先断交陷阱]] isolates Chu and pushes Qi toward Qin before Chu attacks in anger.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-51-chuhuaiwang-qin-xiao-er-kan-jiujiu-ruhe-mie-le-ni-ltfkdlivomnvux5xodh2rlfqf6ac]] shows the payoff of that move for Qin Huiwen Wang's state. Once Chu attacks in anger, Qin defeats Chu at [[DanyangBattle|丹阳之战]], captures [[QuGai|屈匄]], takes [[Hanzhong|汉中]], and later defeats Chu again near [[LantianBattle|蓝田之战]]. The episode therefore makes Ying Si's use of Zhang Yi a bridge from diplomatic separation into territorial and battlefield gain.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2]] looks back to Ying Si's earlier strategic choice to take [[ShuStateWarringStates|蜀国]]. The episode says he follows [[SimaCuo|司马错]]'s advice over the competing eastern-expansion impulse, conquers Ba-Shu, and creates a resource base that later becomes difficult to govern when [[ChenZhuangWarringStates|陈庄]] rebels.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-53-qinhuiwang-yong-zhangyi-huan-dipi-lvmo2cwbafcg2gnxliuo18fewimk]] returns Ying Si to the center of Qin-Chu bargaining. He asks to exchange Qin land around [[Wuguan|武关]] and [[Shangyu|商於]] for Chu's [[Qianzhong|黔中]], then faces Chu Huaiwang's counter-demand for [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]. The host's reading of [[Shiji|《史记》]] makes Ying Si's calculation cold: Zhang Yi might win territory if spared and useful, while his death could give Qin a war pretext under the [[EnvoyKillingTaboo|不斩来使]] norm. That makes Ying Si a case of [[MinisterAsExchangeableAsset|可交换功臣]] as well as ruler-backed zongheng use.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-54-wo-zun-ni-shi-wang-ni-dang-wo-shi-pi-lvhcz0ban9frlmwshd9vch-q7-ie]] shows the final phase of Ying Si's patronage of Zhang Yi. After Zhang Yi persuades [[HanState|韩国]], Ying Si rewards him with six cities and the title Wuxin Jun, then sends him to continue the [[LianhengStrategy|连横]] tour through Qi, Zhao, and Yan. The episode closes with Ying Si's death before Zhang Yi reaches [[Xianyang|咸阳]], converting his ruler-backed success into [[PatronSuccessionExposure|靠山更替暴露风险]] under [[QinWuwang|秦武王]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-55-zhangyi-tongshi-guiren-qinhuiwang-lsclfbwn06fgzooipf5up9sdkedy]] makes his death the decisive break in Zhang Yi's final career. Once Ying Si is gone, Qin courtiers attack Zhang Yi, the lords abandon [[LianhengStrategy|连横]] for renewed [[HezongAlliance|合纵]], and Zhang Yi must turn his own removal from Qin into [[SelfPreservationAsStateStrategy|以自保包装国策]] under Qin Wuwang.
