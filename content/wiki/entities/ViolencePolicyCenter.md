# Violence Policy Center

## Overview

The Violence Policy Center is the gun-control advocacy organization whose assault-weapon publications and arguments are examined in [[guns-part-3-a-shooting-lesson]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents the organization as an influential proponent of focusing gun-control advocacy on civilian assault weapons. Its publications reportedly argue that civilian and military assault weapons have few meaningful differences in killing power and that civilian designs retain features relevant to spray firing.

[[MalcolmGladwell]] challenges those formulations by distinguishing one-shot-per-trigger-pull operation from automatic fire and by questioning whether pistol grips and hip firing provide the asserted practical advantage. The resulting profile is not a general judgment on the organization's work; it is a bounded dispute over technical language, legal categories, and policy targeting.

## Key Characteristics

- Advocates regulation of civilian assault weapons.
- Uses comparisons between civilian and military designs to explain the policy target.
- Treats design features and firing practices as relevant to lethality.
- Is criticized in the episode for blurring semi-automatic and automatic capabilities.
- Shows how advocacy categories can combine technical, symbolic, and strategic considerations.
