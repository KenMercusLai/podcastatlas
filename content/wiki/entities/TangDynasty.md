# 唐朝 / Tang Dynasty

唐朝 / Tang Dynasty enters the wiki through [[271-tangchao-douyao-bucunzai-le-weishenme-weishilun-hui-zai-jinnian-da-baofa-lg7-wdrdw1b9h7lwa4vwkvmyb4un]] as the shocking target of a 2026 [[PseudoHistoryConspiracy|伪史论]] claim: the episode says some online accounts moved from denying Western antiquity to claiming that the Tang did not exist. The source uses that claim to show how pseudohistory can turn from flattering national comparison into denial of China's own historical memory.

The episode does not develop a full Tang history page. Its contribution is evidentiary: a dynasty such as Tang is not supported by one isolated text, but by documents, monuments such as the [[GreatWildGoosePagoda|大雁塔]], poetry, institutional continuities, material traces, and relations with neighboring histories.
