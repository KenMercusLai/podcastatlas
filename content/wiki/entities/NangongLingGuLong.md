# 南宫灵（古龙人物） / Nangong Ling (Gu Long character)

## Overview

南宫灵 / Nangong Ling is [[ChuLiuxiang|楚留香]]'s friend, the young leader of the Beggars' Gang, and [[WuhuaGuLong|无花]]'s brother in [[BloodSeaFragranceGuLong|《血海飘香》]].

## Current Profile

The bounded source refuses to reduce 南宫灵 to a manipulated avenger. His birth history and 仁慈's role in his father's death provide a revenge motive, but prolonged poisoning of his adoptive father, eagerness to secure leadership, and use of 白玉魔 as a dirty intermediary also reveal ambition and image management.

That mixed profile is constrained by plot logic. The hosts question the slow poison, failure to neutralize [[QiuLingsuGuLong|秋灵素]], and other decisions that leave the plan vulnerable. His character therefore joins genuine grievance, adopted loyalty, status desire, and instrumental violence without making their exact proportions certain.

## Key Characteristics

- Holds public legitimacy as a young Beggars' Gang leader and 楚留香's friend.
- Carries a revenge claim tied to his birth father and adoptive father.
- Pursues power as well as revenge rather than serving only as his brother's instrument.
- Uses intermediaries to preserve a respectable public image.
- Makes strategically weak choices that expose the conspiracy.
