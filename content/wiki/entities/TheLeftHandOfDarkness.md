# 《黑暗的左手》 / The Left Hand of Darkness

[[TheLeftHandOfDarkness|《黑暗的左手》 / The Left Hand of Darkness]] is the [[UrsulaKLeGuin|Ursula K. Le Guin / 厄休拉·勒古恩]] novel read in [[36-heian-de-zuoshou-liufang-yu-ai-de-weilai-wangshi-605254522]]. The episode follows [[GenlyAi|金利·艾 / Genly Ai]], envoy of the [[Ekumen|艾库曼 / Ekumen]], as he tries to persuade [[Gethen|格森星 / Gethen]] to join a wider interstellar alliance.

The source reads the novel through two linked movements. Politically, Genly's mission passes through [[Karhide|卡海德 / Karhide]]'s honor-bound court politics and [[Orgoreyn|欧格瑞恩 / Orgoreyn]]'s bureaucratic police state before [[Estraven|伊斯特拉凡 / Estraven]]'s rescue and sacrifice force both systems to face the mission's truth. Anthropologically and emotionally, Gethen's ambisexual society makes Genly confront assumptions he did not know he carried about sex, protection, weakness, intimacy, and trust.

The episode's title emphasis on exile and love is important. Estraven remains officially a traitor, but the source treats him as the person whose loyalty reaches beyond ruler, border, and reputation into service to a larger human future.
