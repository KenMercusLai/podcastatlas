# 韩廆 / Han Gui

韩廆 / Han Gui appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-2-lmnqfjmydygqnfec6xhzpixralvi]] as the [[HanState|韩国]] chancellor appointed by [[HanAihou|韩哀侯]]. The source says Han Gui and [[YanZhongzi|严遂]] become enemies after competing for the ruler's favor.

His source role is the target rather than the architect of the crisis. Yan Sui hires an assassin to kill Han Gui, but Han Aihou is killed while protecting him, turning a ministerial feud into a ruler-death and succession event.
