# 许褚 / Xu Chu

## Overview
许褚 / Xu Chu is a late-Han military protector whose current profile runs from local fortified defense to trusted close guard and battlefield rescuer for [[CaoCao|曹操]].

## Current Profile
The evidence portrays Xu Chu as force disciplined into security capacity. Hanji 1020 shows him protecting local households and then attaching himself to Cao Cao's larger power; Hanji 1039 shows the close-guard role functioning under insider threat when he detects and kills Xu Ta's assassination group. Hanji 1121 extends that role into open battle: Xu Chu shields Cao Cao during a contested Yellow River crossing and helps move the boat after its operator is killed.

## Key Characteristics
- Builds local protection capacity in disorder by gathering warriors and kin.
- Reads the larger power field and joins Cao Cao when isolated defense has reached its limit.
- Combines physical force with quiet discipline, making him suitable for close protection.
- Detects insider risk in the guard environment and kills Xu Ta's conspirators.
- Maintains direct protection during a river-crossing crisis under cavalry and missile attack.
- Becomes even more trusted after the incident, with Cao Cao keeping him near day and night.

## Evidence
- Local defense origin: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1020-sanguo-baijiazi-yuanshu-ruhe-ba-hao-pai-da-de-xilan-lhwr9hfmanr-zktleufy3kvl38u0|Hanji 1020]] says Xu Chu gathers warriors and kin to protect thousands of households after Yellow Turban disruption.
- Strategic attachment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1020-sanguo-baijiazi-yuanshu-ruhe-ba-hao-pai-da-de-xilan-lhwr9hfmanr-zktleufy3kvl38u0|Hanji 1020]] frames Xu Chu's move to Cao Cao as a correct reading of勢.
- Guard suitability: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1020-sanguo-baijiazi-yuanshu-ruhe-ba-hao-pai-da-de-xilan-lhwr9hfmanr-zktleufy3kvl38u0|Hanji 1020]] stresses both ferocity and discipline, including Cao Cao's Fan Kuai comparison.
- Assassination prevention: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] says Xu Chu unexpectedly returns, notices Xu Ta's group looking suspicious, and kills them.
- Increased trust: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] says Cao Cao then relies on Xu Chu even more and keeps him by his side day and night.
- Battlefield extraction: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1121-tongguan-shengsi-jie-caocao-weile-ying-yishen-fanxian-lvfxitrmo0wq9hl1fmzzuagsobbl|Hanji 1121]] says Xu Chu uses a saddle to shield Cao Cao from arrows and rows during the escape from Ma Chao's cavalry.

## Qualifications
The current sources build Xu Chu as a protection figure, not as a complete campaign biography. The Xu Ta episode also shows that strict guard discipline can generate resentment elsewhere in the unit, so Xu Chu's success does not remove the broader close-guard risk. Hanji 1121's exact boat, saddle, and arrow details remain source-scoped pending comparison with primary records.

## What Changed
- Extended Xu Chu's protection profile from local defense and insider-threat detection to battlefield extraction.
- Added the Yellow River crossing as evidence of physical protection under direct attack.
