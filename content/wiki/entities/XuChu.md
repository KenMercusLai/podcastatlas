# 许褚 / Xu Chu

许褚 / Xu Chu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1020-sanguo-baijiazi-yuanshu-ruhe-ba-hao-pai-da-de-xilan-lhwr9hfmanr-zktleufy3kvl38u0|Hanji 1020]] as the local strongman who turns disorder-era self-protection into service under [[CaoCao|曹操]]. The source says that after the Yellow Turban disruption, Xu Chu gathered warriors and kin, fortified his locality, and protected thousands of households from raiders.

His move to Cao Cao is presented as a successful reading of "势." A local armed group can preserve order for a time, but when Cao Cao consolidates Yan and Yu regions, joining the larger power gives Xu Chu and his followers a future beyond isolated defense. Cao Cao immediately values him as a guard figure, compares him to [[FanKuai|樊哙]], and assigns him to close protection.

The episode stresses Xu Chu's combination of force and discipline. He is fierce in combat but cautious, law-abiding, and quiet, which lets him become part of Cao Cao's core security rather than merely another violent local actor.
