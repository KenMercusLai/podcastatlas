## Overview

苏武 / Su Wu is synthesized as the Western Han chief envoy whose mission crisis, coerced detention, long exile, and return form one extended loyalty case. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-376-1-lishishang-zhenshi-de-suwu-muyang-shi-shenmeyang-lidxs1d66m5q2hegjteq1lgra5fp|Hanji 376-1]] supplies the opening: [[ZhangShengSuWuMissionWesternHan|张胜]]'s unauthorized involvement in [[YuChangWesternHan|虞常]]'s plot exposes the mission, after which Su Wu twice seeks death rather than let coercion publicly degrade his office. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-376-2-cong-liulanger-nixi-cheng-jiangjun-jiemi-hanjiang-zhaoponu-lszmnylwk8zxpjhelkd1gd6jsfsd|Hanji 376-2]] carries the sequence through failed inducement, starvation, secret North Sea transfer, an impossible ram-herding condition, and continued south-facing ritual. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-398-ruguo-ni-shi-liling-hui-ti-xiongnu-quanjiang-suwu-ma-lrvkyakuzpnv77uoq4wkrt7vcuzy|Hanji 398]] supplies the later persuasion, mourning, rescue stratagem, and return under [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]].

## Current Profile

The current evidence presents Su Wu less as a complete biographical subject than as a test of office identity under escalating coercion. Before exile, he has not joined or known about Zhang Sheng's private agreement, yet he recognizes that the chief envoy and entire mission will bear the consequences. His first response is to seek death before hostile treatment can dishonor the mission; after the chanyu moves from contemplated execution to coerced surrender, he stabs himself and later remains unmoved by Wei Lü's sword threat.

The later exile turns that immediate crisis response into durable identity. Hanji 376-2 makes the transition concrete: after inducement fails, Su Wu is denied food, survives through snow and felt, is secretly moved to the North Sea, and is told he may return only if rams give birth. He keeps the Han envoy staff and faces south despite isolation; Hanji 398 then adds Li Ling's emotionally and politically realistic surrender argument and Su Wu's filial-duty refusal.

The return branch complicates a simple glory story. Su Wu's rescue depends on Chang Hui's tactical proof-of-life story to the Han envoy, not on Xiongnu generosity alone. When he returns, he has survived nineteen years and receives honor, office, and reward, but he also leaves behind a Xiongnu wife and child. The episode therefore frames his loyalty as costly, embodied, and publicly honored only after long private suffering.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han chief envoy whose completed peace mission is compromised by his deputy's unauthorized plot support.
- Treats bodily survival after coerced humiliation as a possible betrayal of the mission and twice seeks death before yielding.
- Rejects collective guilt for Zhang Sheng's conduct while refusing Wei Lü's sword threat and surrender demand.
- Keeps the Han envoy staff and south-facing ritual through starvation, cold, concealment, and an impossible release condition, making office identity physically visible.
- Refuses Li Ling's surrender argument despite family disaster, political danger under Han Wudi, and the apparent uselessness of unseen loyalty.
- Returns through Chang Hui's diplomatic ruse and Han pressure but carries private cost because his Xiongnu wife and child remain behind.

## Evidence

- Mission-crisis origin: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-376-1-lishishang-zhenshi-de-suwu-muyang-shi-shenmeyang-lidxs1d66m5q2hegjteq1lgra5fp|Hanji 376-1]] says Zhang Sheng reveals his role only after Yu Chang's capture, leading Su Wu to expect collective exposure and seek death before the mission can be humiliated.
- Coerced refusal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-376-1-lishishang-zhenshi-de-suwu-muyang-shi-shenmeyang-lidxs1d66m5q2hegjteq1lgra5fp|Hanji 376-1]] says Su Wu stabs himself when surrender pressure begins, survives, denies guilt by association, and remains unmoved when Wei Lü raises a sword.
- Exile transition: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-376-2-cong-liulanger-nixi-cheng-jiangjun-jiemi-hanjiang-zhaoponu-lszmnylwk8zxpjhelkd1gd6jsfsd|Hanji 376-2]] says inducement gives way to starvation, a false death report, secret North Sea transfer, rams with an impossible release condition, and dispersion of non-surrendering mission members.
- Directional ritual: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-376-2-cong-liulanger-nixi-cheng-jiangjun-jiemi-hanjiang-zhaoponu-lszmnylwk8zxpjhelkd1gd6jsfsd|Hanji 376-2]] says Su Wu continues holding the envoy staff and facing south even when food and ordinary institutional contact are absent.
- Exile identity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-398-ruguo-ni-shi-liling-hui-ti-xiongnu-quanjiang-suwu-ma-lrvkyakuzpnv77uoq4wkrt7vcuzy|Hanji 398]] says Su Wu is sent to the North Sea, suffers intermittent supplies, survives through desperate food sources, and continues holding the Han envoy staff until its yak-tail ornaments fall away.
- Refusal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-398-ruguo-ni-shi-liling-hui-ti-xiongnu-quanjiang-suwu-ma-lrvkyakuzpnv77uoq4wkrt7vcuzy|Hanji 398]] says Li Ling argues from hopeless return prospects, family disasters, and Han Wudi's late unpredictability, but Su Wu answers that service to ruler is like service to father and threatens death if surrender is pressed.
- Return and cost: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-398-ruguo-ni-shi-liling-hui-ti-xiongnu-quanjiang-suwu-ma-lrvkyakuzpnv77uoq4wkrt7vcuzy|Hanji 398]] says Chang Hui's wild-goose message story forces acknowledgement that Su Wu is alive, after which Su Wu returns to Chang'an in 81 BCE and leaves a Xiongnu wife and newborn child behind.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to three compact podcast sources. Together they join the mission crisis, transition into North Sea exile, later persuasion, and return, but they do not reconstruct Su Wu's complete chronology, every companion, later office-holding, the textual history of the wild-goose letter, or the historicity of every quoted line. The survival details, exact North Sea geography, false-death report, and impossible release condition remain source-scoped rather than independently verified here.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 376-2's starvation, secret transfer, false-death report, impossible release condition, and south-facing ritual bridge into North Sea exile.
- Strengthened the continuity between immediate defense of mission dignity and later endurance through ritual token, mourning, and refusal.
