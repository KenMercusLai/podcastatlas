## Overview

沈景 / Shen Jing is a Shun-era official whose current wiki role is the disciplined kingdom minister sent to repair [[LiuZhengHejianKing|河间王刘政]]'s Hejian fief. Hanji 810 uses him as a compact example of appointment fit: protocol, coercive authority, and local cleanup work together.

## Current Profile

Hanji 810 says Liu Zheng inherits the Hejian kingdom after Liu Kai's death and behaves arrogantly and lawlessly. [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] appoints Shen Jing as Hejian xiang. Shen's first move is not a policy memo but a status confrontation: when Liu Zheng appears drunk and improperly dressed, Shen refuses ordinary obeisance and insists on proper royal comportment before acknowledging him.

After establishing that boundary, Shen Jing rebukes the royal tutor, arrests abusive local actors, executes dozens of bullies, and releases more than one hundred people the source describes as wrongfully imprisoned. Liu Zheng then reforms. The page therefore treats Shen as a case where a correctly matched official turns royal-fief discipline into visible state capacity.

## Key Characteristics

- Kingdom minister appointed to discipline a same-surname royal fief.
- Uses ritual protocol to establish that royal status does not excuse disorderly conduct.
- Converts symbolic correction into concrete law enforcement against local bullies.
- Repairs wrongful imprisonment as well as punishing evildoers.
- Source-bounded appointment-quality example, not yet a full Shen Jing biography.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 810. It does not reconstruct Shen Jing's whole career, the exact legal counts behind the executions and releases, or independent Hejian administrative records. The source's appointment-quality lesson should be read as a synthesis of the episode's narrative, not as proof that one official can always reform a royal fief.
