## Overview

赵食其 / Zhao Shiqi is the Western Han right general paired with [[LiGuangHanGeneral|李广]] on the eastern route during Li Guang's final campaign against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]]. The current source develops him only through the shared navigation failure and his later punishment.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-325-2-liguang-weihe-hui-zisha-ta-fanle-shenme-cuo-lsrkaep1np1bugqae8ujie2ttqpe|Hanji 325-2]] says Zhao Shiqi and Li Guang march from the east without a guide, become lost in the desert, and miss the main battle under [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]]. Unlike Li Guang, who kills himself before questioning, Zhao is tried, redeems the capital sentence with payment, loses office, and becomes a commoner.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han right general assigned to the same eastern route as Li Guang.
- Co-commander in a guide and navigation failure that causes the force to miss the main engagement.
- Surviving defendant who loses military and official status after redeeming the reported capital sentence.

## Qualifications

The current profile comes from one short podcast note and is not a complete biography. The episode does not establish the division of responsibility between Zhao Shiqi and Li Guang, reproduce the legal record, or independently verify the sentence, payment, titles, route, and guide failure. The supplied transcript's “赵一鸡 / 赵易基” forms are treated as ASR errors rather than separate people.
