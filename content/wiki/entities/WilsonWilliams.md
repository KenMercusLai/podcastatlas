# Wilson Williams

Wilson Williams appears in [[the-skyscrapers-that-nimbys-and-zoning-couldnt-stop]] as a [[SquamishNation|Squamish]] council figure whose great-grandfather had lived on [[SinakVillageSite|the village site]]. The source uses his account to show why a modest apartment plan did not feel adequate to some Squamish members after the land was returned.

Williams brings the "seven generations" standard into the housing decision. In the episode, that standard shifts the project from ordinary real estate into [[SovereignHousingDevelopment]]: the question is whether the land can generate durable wealth, visibility, and a built form worthy of the place.
