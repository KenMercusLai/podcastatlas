# 甘宁 / Gan Ning

## Overview
甘宁 / Gan Ning moves from an Yizhou talent-integration failure and Liu Biao-era comparator into an Eastern Wu commander whose local knowledge, small-force initiative, and visible composure matter in the post-Red-Cliffs Jiangling campaign.

## Current Profile
Hanji 981 presents Gan Ning as a Ba-region elite who rebels with Shen Mi and Lou Fa under [[LiuZhang|刘璋]], loses to Dongzhou troops, and leaves east. The episode uses his later Wu career to argue that Liu Zhang could suppress but not integrate important local capacity. Hanji 993 invokes him as another capable figure underused in the [[LiuBiao|刘表]] field.

Hanji 1108 supplies his first direct offensive command. During the standoff around [[JianglingLateHan|江陵]], Gan Ning proposes taking [[YilingLateHan|夷陵]] with several hundred selected troops. The source attributes the seizure to familiarity with waterways, roads, and defenses, plus troops suited to mountain and river fighting. Hanji 1109 continues the operation after he is besieged: Gan Ning asks [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] for aid and is portrayed drinking calmly under missile fire until a [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]]-backed relief force arrives.

## Key Characteristics
- Begins as a local-capacity loss from Liu Zhang's fragmented Yizhou order.
- Serves as a comparison for talent recognized but not made usable.
- Identifies Yiling as a flank opportunity during the Jiangling stalemate.
- Matches selected troops and local knowledge to a fast seizure.
- Uses visible calm to keep siege pressure from becoming morale collapse.
- Becomes operationally useful in another regime after earlier patron failure.

## Qualifications
Gan Ning's Yizhou rebellion cannot be reduced to victimhood, and the “lost talent” reading is retrospective. The 1108-1109 sequence comes from short popular-history retellings; exact numbers, “top special forces,” 解烦兵 genealogy, drinking details, and the horse capture remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Joined the 1108 seizure and 1109 relief into one initiative-exposure-composure sequence.
- Added terrain knowledge and force-task fit to the profile.
