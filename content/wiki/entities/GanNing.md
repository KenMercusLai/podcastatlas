# 甘宁 / Gan Ning

## Overview
甘宁 / Gan Ning moves in the current wiki from an Yizhou talent-integration failure and Liu Biao-era comparator into an Eastern Wu field commander whose composure under siege and successful relief make his military value concrete.

## Current Profile
Hanji 981 presents Gan Ning as a Ba-region elite who rebels with Shen Mi and Lou Fa after pressure under [[LiuZhang|刘璋]], loses to Dongzhou troops, and leaves east. The episode uses his later Wu career to argue that Liu Zhang could suppress but not integrate significant local capacity. Hanji 993 then invokes Gan Ning comparatively as another capable figure underused in the [[LiuBiao|刘表]] field.

Hanji 1109 supplies the first direct Wu command scene. Besieged by Cao Ren's force, Gan Ning requests aid from [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]]. The episode portrays him drinking calmly on the tower even as missiles fall nearby, treating visible composure as a way to keep troops steady until the [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]]-backed relief arrives.

## Key Characteristics
- Begins as a local-capacity loss from Liu Zhang's fragmented Yizhou order.
- Serves as a comparative example of recognized ability not made usable by a patron.
- Reappears as an Eastern Wu commander entrusted with a vulnerable forward position.
- Uses visible calm to prevent siege pressure from becoming morale collapse.
- Makes earlier talent-loss claims concrete by becoming useful in another regime.

## Qualifications
Gan Ning's early Yizhou rebellion cannot be reduced to pure victimhood, and Hanji 981's “lost talent” reading is retrospective. Hanji 993 uses him only as a comparison, while Hanji 1109's drinking and missile scene is vivid source-scoped storytelling rather than a complete command evaluation.
