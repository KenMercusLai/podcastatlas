# 甘宁 / Gan Ning

甘宁 / Gan Ning enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-993-shentingling-zhizhan-sunce-dazhan-taishici-lrivpk7col2l7k-s4dqzxxp2kqp1|Hanji 993]] as a comparator in the episode's late-Han talent discussion. The host mentions him alongside the idea that some leaders distrust or underuse capable people because background, style, or reputation makes the talent uncomfortable.

In that source-scoped role, Gan Ning is linked to [[LiuBiao|刘表]] as a parallel to [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]]'s failure to authorize [[TaishiCi|太史慈]]. The page does not narrate Gan Ning's wider career; it marks his place in the wiki's [[TalentAsStateTreasure|以贤臣为宝]] branch as another sign that recognizing talent is not the same as making it usable.
