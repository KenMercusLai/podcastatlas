# 甘宁 / Gan Ning

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]] gives Gan Ning a direct [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] origin branch before the later comparator use. The episode presents him as a Ba-region elite who is pushed out under [[LiuZhang|刘璋]]'s order, rebels with Shen Mi and Lou Fa around Jiangzhou, is defeated by Dongzhou troops, and then leaves eastward.

The source treats this as a talent-integration failure. Gan Ning is not simply a rebellious subordinate in the host's reading; his later career as a Wu naval commander makes his exit evidence that Liu Zhang's regime could suppress local challengers but could not reliably turn them into capacity. This gives [[ImportedTroopsLocalEliteCleavage|外来武装与本土豪族裂缝]] a concrete personnel case.

甘宁 / Gan Ning enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-993-shentingling-zhizhan-sunce-dazhan-taishici-lrivpk7col2l7k-s4dqzxxp2kqp1|Hanji 993]] as a comparator in the episode's late-Han talent discussion. The host mentions him alongside the idea that some leaders distrust or underuse capable people because background, style, or reputation makes the talent uncomfortable.

In that source-scoped role, Gan Ning is linked to [[LiuBiao|刘表]] as a parallel to [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]]'s failure to authorize [[TaishiCi|太史慈]]. The page does not narrate Gan Ning's wider career; it marks his place in the wiki's [[TalentAsStateTreasure|以贤臣为宝]] branch as another sign that recognizing talent is not the same as making it usable.
