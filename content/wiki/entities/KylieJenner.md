# Kylie Jenner

## Overview

Kylie Jenner appears in the wiki through a [[MarketplaceTech]] discussion of [[Meta]]'s smart-glasses promotion. The episode says Jenner promoted a Starfire model of [[RayBanSmartGlasses|Ray-Ban smart glasses]] through a first-person-style campaign video.

## Current Profile

In this source, Jenner is a celebrity-marketing signal. Her campaign made the glasses more visible, but [[CarrieBatten]] treats it as a mixed move because the same visibility also intensified awareness of possible misuse and bystander privacy.

The page's wiki role is therefore not a broad celebrity profile. It records how a high-visibility influencer campaign can normalize AI hardware while making the hardware's social risks harder to ignore.
