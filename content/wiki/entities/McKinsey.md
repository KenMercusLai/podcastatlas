# McKinsey

McKinsey is mentioned in [[e240-openai-lianshou-pe-zaxia-40-yi-meiyuan-liaoliao-guigu-zuihuo-xin-zhiwei-fde]] through [[Oliver]]'s prior private-equity consulting background. The episode uses that background to contrast strategy advice with AI deployment work that changes workflows and leaves systems behind, while still arguing that consulting demand may grow as companies rethink business models under AI.

[[tsr-s5-tomblomfield-v2-audio-tsr-s5-tomblomfield-v2-audio]] adds [[TomBlomfield]]'s brief intended path into McKinsey after Oxford. Tom says he accepted a role but had gardening leave before starting, during which the [[GoCardless]] opportunity emerged, and he later treats consulting as evidence that conventional employment did not fit him well.

[[burning-questions-a-more-fire-prone-world-6a687ea64207b2a60119df8b]] adds McKinsey as part of the survey context for [[PromotionAmbitionGap]]. The episode says McKinsey and Lean In tracked a reversal in the prior trend around women wanting promotions, linking consulting research to [[WomenWorkplaceProgressStall]] rather than to client work.
