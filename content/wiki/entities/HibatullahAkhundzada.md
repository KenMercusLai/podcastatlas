# Hibatullah Akhundzada

Hibatullah Akhundzada appears in [[afghanistan-five-years-on-our-correspondent-visits-6a7ee547049c692a6b3ee0a9]] as the Taliban amir around whom hardline power has consolidated in [[Afghanistan]]. The source places him in Kandahar and says his circle has taken control of important revenue sources, including mining contracts.

The episode uses him less as a biography than as a sign of the regime's direction. Under his leadership, the source says more moderate [[Taliban]] figures have not been able to reverse restrictions on women and girls or the ideological policing that defines [[TalibanHardlineRule]].
