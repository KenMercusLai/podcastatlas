# 2006《超级女声》 / Super Girl 2006

## Overview
2006《超级女声》 was a Chinese televised singing competition whose contestants included [[XuFeiSinger|许飞]] and [[LiNaSinger|厉娜]].

## Current Profile
In the available source, the season combined live broadcasting, comparatively unpolished contestants, paid audience voting, complex revival and advancement rules, and contestant votes that could affect one another's survival. That structure created vivid personalities and unusually high fan investment, but it also made people living as peers compete under zero-sum pressure. Its large archive later became raw material for [[NostalgicMediaRediscovery]] when former contestants returned to public attention.

## Key Characteristics
- Used paid text-message or telephone voting that converted fandom into direct monetary and emotional investment.
- Combined revival, breakthrough, and contestant-vote mechanisms with public performance.
- Placed shared living, friendship, rivalry, and elimination responsibility inside one competition system.
- Featured strongly differentiated contestant images before contemporary celebrity packaging became fully standardized.
- Produced durable footage, phrases, pairings, and fan memories available for later reinterpretation.

- **Competitive structure:** [[ep392-houhuiyouqi-dabao-women-yiran-wei-zhenshide-qinggan-huadiweilao-gkwrimaov6-qafmdiw-t1kurp]] reconstructs revival, advancement, and peer voting, including the vote between Xu Fei and Shang Wenjie.
- **Fan investment:** [[ep392-houhuiyouqi-dabao-women-yiran-wei-zhenshide-qinggan-huadiweilao-gkwrimaov6-qafmdiw-t1kurp]] describes paid votes and intense fan responses around elimination, including reciprocal apology and pleading gestures.
- **Relationship intensity:** [[ep392-houhuiyouqi-dabao-women-yiran-wei-zhenshide-qinggan-huadiweilao-gkwrimaov6-qafmdiw-t1kurp]] presents the “飞雪” pairing as a friendship formed within shared life and direct competition.

## Qualifications
This profile represents one 2026 retrospective podcast discussion rather than a complete production history. Rules, rankings, participant motives, company strategy, audience scale, and causal claims about later careers are not independently verified. The source's contrast between early “realness” and later packaging is interpretive.

## What Changed
- Created a profile emphasizing the joint production of closeness and rivalry.
- Connected paid voting and live exposure to high fan investment.
- Identified the season's archive as infrastructure for later nostalgic rediscovery.
