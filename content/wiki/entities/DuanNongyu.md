# 段农裕 / Duan Nongyu

[[ep261-mifeng-weiji-ruguo-shiqu-mifeng-shijie-jianghui-zenyang-lqp-cwcdxx1ziixb35ijmmvd6cv]] adds Duan's field-reporting role in [[Mengyin|蒙阴]]. She follows migratory beekeepers through hive placement, flower windows, transfer timing, social help among Zhejiang beekeepers, local farmer relationships, low honey prices, hail risk, and [[PesticidePollinatorRisk|pesticide exposure]], making [[MigratoryBeekeeperLivelihood|转场蜂农生计]] concrete.

段农裕 / Duan Nongyu is the [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] reporter in [[ep240-ba-zong-tian-chong-zai-haiwai-duanju-chuhai-de-chanye-mima-ltvxvfj5ko6djhsjatiphdy-dzek]] who focuses on overseas actors and localized short-drama production. She interviews performers and follows a Shenzhen crew to show how foreign actors experience, question, and adapt Chinese short-drama scripts.

Her reporting makes [[ShortDramaOverseasLocalization|短剧海外本土化]] concrete. Native-speaking actors correct unnatural lines, raise objections about plot and intimacy framing, and sometimes turn value mismatches into on-set changes, while also treating short drama as paid acting work and practice in a difficult entertainment labor market.
