# Juliette Bruce

## Overview
Juliette Bruce is the Provence-based founder of [[KulaWand]] and a caller in [[advice-line-with-kip-tindell-of-the-container-store-93508940-11b5-4b29-ba7b-eb077edee062]]. Her question centers on how to create discovery for a new consumer-product category without a large awareness budget.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Juliette as a product-origin founder who built from a real household problem and now needs low-cost, proof-led discovery. Her strongest signals are authentic social sharing, including an ADHD-focused influencer who bought the product, and simple videos that make the product's use visible.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder motivated by a concrete problem her children had with hot food.
- Quality-conscious operator who says the product is made in Portugal with strict material standards.
- Early-stage consumer founder testing DTC, independent retail, influencers, and demonstration video.
