# William Allen

William Allen appears in [[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] as an English Catholic opponent of [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]]'s religious settlement and foreign policy. The episode first mentions him as a Catholic critic of the proposed [[MarriageDiplomacyAsStrategicOption|Anjou marriage]], showing that Elizabeth's French option could anger Catholics as well as Protestants.

Allen then appears in the Paris planning around the [[ThrockmortonPlot]]. Alongside [[HenryDukeOfGuise|Henry, Duke of Guise]], the papal nuncio, [[JamesBeaton]], and other Catholic figures, he took part in invasion discussions intended to replace Elizabeth with [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]].

In the wiki, Allen anchors the English Catholic exile side of [[ConfessionalInvasionConspiracy]]. The source does not give a full biography; it uses him as evidence that religious opposition to Elizabeth had both domestic and continental channels.
