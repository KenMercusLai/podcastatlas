## Overview

赵熙 / Zhao Xi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-728-weihe-mingchen-nan-shanzhong-beihou-de-yuanyin-ni-gai-dong-ltjl2bgpyyz1bmwe-4mfcyd9e6cf|Hanji 728]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-732-donghan-guangwudi-liuxiu-jiabeng-bingsi-de-lnsu1le2x-cj75cjslysvbndeuvr|Hanji 732]] as a late-Guangwu and accession-transition minister whose visible value is procedural order: first through royal fief-return advice, then through funeral-order repair.

## Current Profile

Hanji 728 shows Zhao Xi before [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]'s death. In 51 CE, Liu Xiu asks how to preserve the imperial enterprise permanently, and Zhao Xi advises that princes should return to their own fiefs. Liu Xiu begins carrying this out in winter by sending Lu King Liu Xing and Qi King Liu Shi to their fiefs.

Hanji 732 then shows Zhao Xi at the death-and-accession moment. After Liu Xiu dies, the crown prince and princes are unsure how to handle the funeral procedure because old ceremonial practice has been damaged by the Wang Mang disruption and later institutional discontinuity. Zhao Xi steps in to manage the funeral and make the hierarchy legible again.

The current synthesis is that Zhao Xi's remembered role is not policy authorship in the abstract. It is risk-reducing procedure: territorial distance for adult princes before crisis, and ritual procedure for the heir and princes when the old ruler dies.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han senior minister visible in late Guangwu and Mingdi accession-transition scenes.
- Advises prince-return-to-fief as a way to preserve imperial order.
- Functions as a ritual-procedure restorer after inherited Han ceremony has become uncertain.
- Stabilizes royal-family hierarchy through both territorial and ceremonial order.
- Represents ministerial memory as a practical resource when ruler, heir, and princes face unstable protocols.

## Qualifications

This page remains bounded to Hanji 728 and Hanji 732. It does not reconstruct Zhao Xi's full career, precise office chronology across both notices, or broader political program. Hanji 728 supplies the prince-distance layer; Hanji 732 supplies the funeral-order layer.
