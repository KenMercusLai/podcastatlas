## Overview

赵熙 / Zhao Xi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-732-donghan-guangwudi-liuxiu-jiabeng-bingsi-de-lnsu1le2x-cj75cjslysvbndeuvr|Hanji 732]] as the taiwei who restores ritual order at [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]'s death and [[EmperorMingOfHan|汉明帝刘庄]]'s accession.

## Current Profile

The source presents Zhao Xi through a single succession scene. After Liu Xiu dies, the crown prince and the princes are unsure how to handle the funeral procedure because the old system has been damaged by the Wang Mang disruption and later institutional discontinuity. Zhao Xi steps in to manage the funeral and make the hierarchy legible again.

His profile is therefore not a full ministerial biography. In the current wiki layer, Zhao Xi matters as the person who turns imperial death from confusion into ceremony, allowing Mingdi's accession to proceed inside recognizable court order.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han senior minister visible at the Guangwu death and Mingdi accession scene.
- Functions as a ritual-procedure restorer after inherited Han ceremony has become uncertain.
- Stabilizes the transition by clarifying conduct for the heir and princes.
- Represents ministerial memory as a practical accession resource.
