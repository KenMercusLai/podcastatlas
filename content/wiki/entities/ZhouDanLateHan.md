# 周丹 / Zhou Dan (Late Han)

周丹 / Zhou Dan (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] as the Yuyang taishou sent by [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] against the Liu Yu-aligned coalition. [[YanRou|阎柔]] defeats him, and the episode reports that Zhou Dan and more than four thousand of his men are killed.

His page is intentionally narrow. Zhou Dan matters in this source as evidence that Gongsun Zan's appointed officials could be isolated and destroyed once [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]]'s old network, [[Wuhuan|乌桓]], and Han forces began to coordinate.
