# Costco Next

Costco Next is the partner-commerce program in [[costco-1]] where [[Costco]] members shop directly on partner websites and receive Costco-related discounts after entering a membership number. The episode treats it as an e-commerce extension that gives members value without forcing Costco to absorb every operational burden of a conventional marketplace.
