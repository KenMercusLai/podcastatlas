# Omer Khan

Omer Khan is the host of [[TheSaaSPodcast]] and the interviewer in [[featherless-ai-when-your-weekend-experiment-makes-more-than-your-startup]], [[he-demoted-his-saas-to-sell-a-service-and-4xd-revenue-in-12-months]], [[50-cents-a-pool-the-pricing-model-behind-a-saas-exit]], [[stuck-at-50k-arr-for-5-years-now-1-5m-with-ai-agents]], and [[enterprise-sales-with-no-product-landing-a-big-four-customer]]. In the Responna episode, he frames [[FarzadRashidi]]'s story as a SaaS founder demoting the software product on the homepage and leading with a done-for-you service.

Omer's role in this source is to connect the Responna story to the wider market language of AI-native services and "service as software." His questions move the conversation from the customer-pricing anecdote into fulfillment, automation, productized operations, and AI visibility tactics.

In the Skimmer episode, Omer frames [[RonHash]]'s story around concrete bootstrapped SaaS execution: paper workflow replacement, customer-count pricing, support-led growth, onboarding, churn, acquisition, and the move from [[Skimmer]] to [[QuickFax]].

In the Happierleads episode, Omer frames [[GeorgeGeorgiadis]]'s story around getting unstuck from a five-year $50K ARR plateau, refusing a $1M acquisition offer, and using AI agents and internal tools without pretending that sales, channel focus, and eventual hiring disappear.

In the Featherless AI episode, Omer frames [[EugeneChia]]'s story around a weekend experiment that outperformed the original startup, then follows the pivot into [[GPUHotSwapping]], [[FlatRateAIInferencePricing]], [[LongTailModelHosting]], and simpler positioning for an open-source AI inference platform.

In the Templafy episode, Omer frames [[ChristianLund]]'s story around selling enterprise software before product maturity, qualifying small pilots, disqualifying weak prospects, and retiming AI messaging for buyer readiness.
