# 丹阳之战 / Battle of Danyang

丹阳之战 / Battle of Danyang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-51-chuhuaiwang-qin-xiao-er-kan-jiujiu-ruhe-mie-le-ni-ltfkdlivomnvux5xodh2rlfqf6ac]]. After [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] reduces the promised [[Shangyu|商於]] transfer from six hundred li to six li, [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] sends [[ChuState|楚国]] forces north from Danyang toward [[Wuguan|武关]] instead of following [[ChenZhenWarringStates|陈枕]]'s containment advice.

The episode presents the battle as the first military price of [[UnverifiedTerritorialPromiseTrap|未验地先断交陷阱]]. [[QinState|秦国]] defeats Chu, kills eighty thousand Chu armored soldiers, captures commander [[QuGai|屈匄]] and many high-ranking nobles, and takes [[Hanzhong|汉中]]. The defeat turns a diplomatic humiliation into a territorial and military disaster.
