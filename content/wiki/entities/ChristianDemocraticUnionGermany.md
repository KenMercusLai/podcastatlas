# Christian Democratic Union (Germany)

The Christian Democratic Union is the center-right party context for [[FriedrichMerz]] and [[JensSpahn]] in [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]]. The source describes Germany's governing center-right coalition through Spahn's parliamentary leadership and Merz's management of the scandal.

The party's relevance is reputational and electoral. The episode says Spahn had been useful to Merz as a negotiator, but his surrogacy scandal became too damaging to keep. The source also warns that the episode could harm the party before important eastern German state elections where [[AlternativeForGermany|AfD]] leads.
