# 练晶晶 / Lian Jingjing

## Overview
练晶晶 is presented in the source as an associate chief physician in digestive endoscopy at [[ShanghaiEastHospital|上海市东方医院]] who explains gastrointestinal screening, procedure selection, preparation, anesthesia boundaries, biopsy, and common misconceptions.

## Current Profile
Her public-education approach matches each test to the question it can answer. She distinguishes direct mucosal inspection and tissue sampling from imaging, breath, stool, blood, tumor-marker, capsule, and genetic tests, while emphasizing that early lesions may be silent and that symptoms, family history, prior findings, and examination quality change the appropriate route.

She also treats the patient's experience as part of clinical quality: bowel cleansing, fasting, anesthesia assessment, technical skill, prior reports, escort arrangements, pathology, and follow-up all affect whether endoscopy is safe and useful. Her myth correction separates biopsy from cancer diagnosis and rejects “detox,” “宿便,” and indiscriminate colon-cleansing products as substitutes for clinical care.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains gastrointestinal endoscopy through anatomy, test capability, and clinical question rather than price or prestige.
- Emphasizes screening before symptoms in risk-appropriate contexts while preserving symptom-triggered diagnostic routes.
- Connects bowel preparation and fasting to examination quality and anesthesia safety.
- Distinguishes biopsy and pathology from an automatic cancer diagnosis.
- Explains painless endoscopy through eligibility assessment, contraindications, escort, and recovery needs.
- Uses procedure details and misconception correction to reduce fear without promising zero risk.
