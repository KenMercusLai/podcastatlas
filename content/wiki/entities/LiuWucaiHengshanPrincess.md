## Overview

刘无采 / Liu Wucai is the daughter of [[LiuCiHengshanKingWesternHan|衡山王刘赐]] and 乘舒, and sister of [[LiuShuangHengshanCrownPrince|刘爽]] and [[LiuXiaoHengshanPrince|刘孝]]. Hanji 305-1 makes her personal rupture with Liu Shuang one of the grievances that [[XuLaiHengshanQueen|徐来]] converts into political pressure against the crown prince.

## Current Profile

The episode says Liu Wucai returns to her natal household after her marriage ends and has sexual relationships with household dependents and guests. Liu Shuang repeatedly rebukes her; she rejects his authority and breaks with him. Xu Lai then treats her favorably, bringing her into a temporary alliance whose members oppose Liu Shuang for different reasons.

## Key Characteristics

- Royal daughter whose private-life conflict becomes part of a succession struggle.
- Sister who resists Liu Shuang's attempts to regulate her conduct.
- Coalition participant motivated differently from the queen and Liu Xiao.
- Figure described through moralizing sexual language that requires source-boundary caution.

## Qualifications

The transcript calls her “刘五彩”; this page uses the received identity 刘无采 to avoid a duplicate. The episode's sexual characterization is moralizing and supplies neither her perspective nor independent evidence. The supplied excerpt does not narrate the attempted fratricide promised by the title.
