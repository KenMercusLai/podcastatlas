## Overview
Suo Ban is the Han long clerk sent to Yiwu with a small tuntian force after Cao Zong's Western Regions request, and his death becomes the immediate trigger for the Hanji 791 frontier-strategy debate.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-790-lishi-gaosu-ni-ruhe-he-lingdao-da-jiaodao-lulf0r1sdkod3luzwru0-kkrijxk|Hanji 790]] introduces Suo Ban as the officer sent with more than one thousand men to garrison and cultivate Yiwu after [[CaoZongLateHan|Cao Zong]] asks the court to protect the Western Regions. Cheshi Qian and Shanshan initially submit. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-791-zhichang-shang-zong-you-ren-beihou-yin-ni-yi-zhao-yingdui-llz74ebi88qmw-9050x2qaddjwqn|Hanji 791]] then records Suo Ban's killing after Northern Xiongnu and Cheshi Hou attack, turning a small re-entry force into a crisis over whether and how Han should return to the region.

## Key Characteristics
- Field implementer rather than chief strategist of the Western Regions re-entry.
- Embodies the fragility of a small Yiwu garrison placed into a contested regional order.
- His initial success in receiving submissions is quickly reversed by Xiongnu-backed attack.
- His death becomes the practical problem around which Cao Zong and Ban Yong's policy disagreement forms.

## Qualifications
The current profile does not infer Suo Ban's personal competence beyond the bounded sources. His importance lies in the structural role of his mission and death inside Han's Western Regions debate.
