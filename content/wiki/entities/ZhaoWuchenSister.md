# 武臣姐姐 / Wu Chen's Sister

武臣姐姐 / Wu Chen's Sister enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-2-lishishang-hejiu-neng-wu-duo-da-shi-zhuan-gei-jiugui-laogong-kan-zhe-ji-lvzxpoxclwdrr66bwejd-bga8dp|Qinji 129-2]] as the unnamed sister of [[WuChen|武臣]], the restored Zhao king. The episode says she is returning from a banquet near [[Handan|邯郸]] while drunk when [[LiLiangZhaoGeneral|李良]] mistakes her carriage for Wu Chen's arrival and bows by the roadside.

Her source role is a fatal ritual failure. Because she does not recognize Li Liang and only sends attendants to tell him to rise, Li Liang later treats the scene as humiliation: if even Wu Chen's sister will not return proper respect, his standing in the new Zhao court looks unsafe. The episode uses this to make drinking a political-amplification problem rather than a private vice alone.

Li Liang kills her after his followers convert the insult into a broader status argument. Her death is immediately followed by Li Liang's attack on Handan and the killing of Wu Chen, making her page part of [[StatusInsultDefectionCascade|名分羞辱触发叛变]] and a later contrast to [[LuWineHandanWarCascade|鲁酒薄而邯郸围]].
