# Liu Zhijun / 刘志军

Liu Zhijun is the railway minister figure at the center of [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]]. The episode presents him as a poor Hubei-born railway worker who rose through the system, became head of the [[MinistryOfRailwaysChina]], compressed management layers, and pushed [[ChineseHighSpeedRail]] with unusually high execution pressure.

The source's judgment is deliberately mixed. Liu's power and urgency helped create conditions for [[ProcurementLedTechnologyTransfer]], fast line construction, and record-setting [[Hexiehao]] tests, but the same concentrated authority also produced [[InfrastructureLaborBurden]], fear-driven execution, and corruption channels involving [[ZhangShuguangRailway]] and [[DingShumiao]]. His fall in 2011 marks the end of the episode's "high-speed rail strongman" phase.
