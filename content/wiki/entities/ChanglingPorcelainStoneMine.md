# Changling Porcelain Stone Mine / 长岭瓷石矿遗址

Changling Porcelain Stone Mine / 长岭瓷石矿遗址 is the porcelain-stone heritage component in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode lists it alongside old-city production remains, [[HutianKilnSite|湖田窑址]], [[GaolingVillage|高岭]] clay, and fuel-supply areas as evidence that Jingdezhen's heritage is an industrial ecology.

Porcelain stone was the early material base for [[Jingdezhen]], but the source says it could deform at high temperature when used alone. That limitation makes [[PorcelainStoneClayBinaryFormula]] important: clay and stone together let the city extend its product range and technical ambition.
