# Henry Mintzberg

## Overview
Henry Mintzberg appears in the source as the management thinker Yang Bin invokes when contrasting emergent strategy with planned innovation.

## Current Profile
Mintzberg's current wiki role is source-scoped: he supplies the management vocabulary for doubting whether original innovation can be "cultivated" through efficiency-first planning. Yang uses the reference to argue that strategy and innovation often need emergence rather than a fully preset path.

This page should be expanded only if future sources add more about Mintzberg's work.

## Key Characteristics
- Management thinker named in the episode's innovation-organization discussion.
- Used to support the contrast between emergence and planning.
- Helps connect Dartmouth-style innovation to broader organization theory.
