# Hamilcar Barca / 哈米尔卡·巴卡

## Overview

Hamilcar Barca / 哈米尔卡·巴卡 is the Carthaginian commander presented as the founder of the [[BarcidSpanishPowerBase|Barcid Spanish power base]] and the father whose military settlement made [[HannibalBarca]]'s later campaign possible.

## Current Profile

After fighting the [[RomanRepublic]] in Sicily and negotiating the end of the First Punic War, Hamilcar helps suppress Carthage's mercenary revolt. Rome's seizure of Sardinia and Corsica during Carthaginian weakness deepens the postwar grievance attributed to him.

In 237 BC he turns to Iberia, where Phoenician settlements, mines, agriculture, and recruits offer a route to recovery. The episode portrays his expansion as both a Carthaginian rebuilding program and a possible Barcid family power base. His mix of severe punishment and rich rewards expands the army before he dies fighting in the interior.

## Key Characteristics

- Veteran of the First Punic War and its settlement.
- Rebuilds power through Iberian resources after Carthage loses its island empire.
- Combines coercion, reward, recruitment, and dynastic continuity.
- Creates foundations later developed by Hasdrubal and Hannibal.
