# 《小白》 / Shiro

《小白》 / Shiro is an [[AkutagawaRyunosuke|芥川龙之介]] story summarized near the end of [[17-diyubian-zhongsheng-jie-ku-yi-nian-ru-mo-561780565]]. The episode describes a white dog who fails to save a friend, becomes black, is driven away, then keeps rescuing others until transformation and possible redemption become the story's center.

The source treats the story as gentler than [[HellScreen|《地狱变》]] but still tied to guilt, cowardice, self-punishment, rescue, and the desire to become good after failure. It helps keep Akutagawa's darkness from becoming one-note.
