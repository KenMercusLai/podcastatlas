# 童立 / Tong Li

## Overview
童立 / Tong Li appears in [[shi-di-fu-shuo-469-tongli-zuihaode-guanxi-shi-zai-airen-huaili-jinqing-yangw-zaox-lmq6et15-mluqyuuav7z4jpx4hcb]] as a sex-education communicator and sex-psychology counselor interviewed by [[SteveShiDiFuShuoHost|Steve]] on [[ShiDiFuShuo|史蒂夫说]].

## Current Profile
Tong Li's current wiki profile is source-scoped to this episode. He presents sex education as life-course exploration rather than one-time puberty instruction, and he presents sex-psychology counseling as a nonjudgmental professional space for discussing desire, shame, relationship conflict, dysfunction, infidelity, sex work, and self-acceptance. His broader position is that sexual experience cannot be separated from relationship safety, social norms, body state, emotional need, and responsibility.

## Key Characteristics
- Works at the intersection of sex education, psychology, and counseling.
- Values a safe, nonjudgmental space where people can discuss sexual questions without being treated as abnormal or immoral.
- Treats sex as exploration across body, feeling, identity, relationship, culture, law, and life stage.
- Gives particular attention to male vulnerability, loneliness, shame, and performance anxiety.
- Frames adult products and AI companionship as partial supports that cannot replace embodied human relationship.
