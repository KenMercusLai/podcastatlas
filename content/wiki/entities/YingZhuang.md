# 嬴壮 / Ying Zhuang

嬴壮 / Ying Zhuang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-60-zhongguo-di-yi-wei-bachi-chaozheng-de-taihou-jingshi-ta-ltazis9qo0dwvwmzxikk8mmpvosc]] as the shuzhang who leads or fronts a Qin revolt after [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] dies without a son. The episode places him among princes, high officials, and party followers resisting the accession order around [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]].

His role is brief but structurally important. [[WeiRan|魏冉]] crushes the revolt, and the purge helps turn the succession crisis into a new court order under [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]] and her kin. Ying Zhuang therefore functions as the named rebel through whom [[SuccessionNonDesignationRisk|未定继承人风险]] becomes visible as open Qin palace violence.
