# Rocky Mountain AI Interest Group

## Overview
The Rocky Mountain AI Interest Group is an AI community organization described by [[UcheAgbosi]] in [[ep-20-understanding-ai-agents-from-basics-to-future-potential]].

## Current Profile
In the source, the group is a regional learning and networking community with a large monthly Boulder meeting and subgroups focused on startups and engineering. It supports the episode's broader argument that hands-on experimentation and peer learning help people follow a fast-changing AI field.

## Key Characteristics
- Regional AI community serving the Rocky Mountain area.
- Monthly meeting activity centered in Boulder.
- Startup- and engineering-oriented subgroup activity.
- Mentoring and community-learning role for founders, developers, and students.

## Evidence
- Scale and meeting activity: [[ep-20-understanding-ai-agents-from-basics-to-future-potential]] records Agbosi's statement that the group has about 2,500 members and holds a major monthly Boulder meeting.
- Community structure: [[ep-20-understanding-ai-agents-from-basics-to-future-potential]] describes AI-for-startups and AI-for-engineering subgroup work plus mentoring for startups, developers, and students.

## Qualifications
The membership count, leadership role, meeting cadence, and subgroup descriptions come from one guest's interview account and were not independently verified during ingest.
