# John I Tzimiskes

## Overview

John I Tzimiskes was the Byzantine emperor who succeeded the murdered [[NikephorosIIPhokas]] and reopened marriage negotiations with [[OttoTheGreat]], sending his step-niece [[Theophano]] west.

## Current Profile

Within this episode John is a hinge between violent Byzantine succession and successful cross-imperial diplomacy. After taking power in 969, he accepted the revived proposal linking Theophano to [[OttoII]]. The resulting 972 marriage connected the courts without resolving their rival claims to Roman authority.

Theophano was not John's daughter, and disappointment over her exact status shows that kinship rank mattered within diplomatic exchange. Her later hostility toward John's successors is reported by the episode but not developed into a settled account.

## Key Characteristics

- Successor produced by a violent palace overthrow.
- Byzantine emperor willing to reopen negotiations with the Ottonians.
- Step-uncle who supplied Theophano as the marriage partner.
- Diplomatic actor who enabled alliance without settling rival Roman titles.

- Kinship status - [[460-the-empress-of-the-apocalypse-glt9103397168]] identifies Theophano as his step-niece rather than daughter.

## Qualifications

This is a source-bounded diplomatic profile, not a full account of John's reign or military record. The precise negotiations, Theophano's lineage, and her later attitude toward Byzantine successors require broader evidence.
