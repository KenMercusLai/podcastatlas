# Mori Building / 森大厦

Mori Building appears in [[131-wo-zai-riben-mai-le-yi-tao-zizhu-fang-llofkgaj-hyajksrpkoylakuocxz]] as a Japanese urban redevelopment example, especially through the source's discussion of Roppongi Hills. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] uses the company to show how leading developers in mature markets increasingly compete through district operation rather than only construction.

The episode's point is that the value of central real estate depends on continuous management: shops change, hotels and restaurants are curated, greenery and public space are maintained, and the surrounding district is kept attractive enough for residents, workers, and visitors.
