# Center for Building in North America

## Overview
The Center for Building in North America is [[StephenSmithBuildingPolicy|Stephen Smith]]'s research organization for studying building codes, construction practices, and cost differences that receive limited academic attention.

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, the center's work is represented by a comparative investigation of elevators. The analysis connects American labor organization, model-code rules, cabin dimensions, installation costs, and small-building feasibility rather than treating each as an isolated technical issue.

## Key Characteristics
- Focuses on under-researched building rules and construction practices.
- Uses international comparison to identify unusual North American costs and standards.
- Connects technical requirements to housing supply and accessibility outcomes.
