# Brad Reese

Brad Reese is presented in [[reeses-heir-vs-chocolate-skimpflation]] as a retired man in West Palm Beach, [[Florida]], a major [[Reeses|Reese's]] fan, and a grandson of [[HBReese|H.B. Reese]]. After tasting Reese's Peanut Butter Mini Hearts Unwrapped, he checked the package and objected that the product did not say real milk chocolate or real peanut butter.

His role is more than ordinary customer complaint because the source turns his family link into [[StoryLedConsumerBranding|story-led brand pressure]]. Brad writes open letters, documents ingredient lists, makes media appearances, and tries to make the label issue visible enough that [[Hershey]] cannot treat [[ChocolateCompound|compound chocolate]] as a quiet product detail.

The source does not prove that Brad caused Hershey's announced formula reversal. It does show that a family heir can turn [[Skimpflation]] into a public trust problem by connecting technical labels to a simple legacy claim about what Reese's should be.
