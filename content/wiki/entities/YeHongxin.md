# 叶洪新 / Ye Hongxin

## Overview
叶洪新 / Ye Hongxin is the source-described founder and product leader of [[Laifen|徕芬]], whose path from ecommerce through aircraft, electric scooters, and small appliances centers on self-directed technical learning, industrial design, and repeated hardware iteration.

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents Ye less as a single-breakthrough inventor than as a product-manager founder whose failed and partial projects accumulated reusable capability. Aircraft work introduced motors, structures, propellers, control, and reliability; scooters exposed production and quality gaps; Laifen combined that learning with self-developed motors, owned manufacturing, consumer communication, and multi-category brand ambition. His current leadership problem is no longer only choosing and refining a product. It is distinguishing personal taste from market evidence and making product judgment teachable enough for several teams to operate without his constant intervention.

## Key Characteristics
- Interest-led self-learner whose first sustained confidence reportedly came through computers and practical technical competence rather than formal schooling.
- Product-led hardware founder willing to spend heavily on engineering, industrial design, and custom components.
- Reuses knowledge from commercial failures instead of treating each abandoned product as disconnected.
- Combines aesthetic judgment with an explicit test of practical benefit to many users.
- Remains deeply involved in product detail while recognizing founder dependence as an organizational risk.

## Evidence
- Capability formation: [[ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli-ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli]] connects computer training, ecommerce, aircraft, scooters, motors, manufacturing, and consumer appliances into a cumulative learning path.
- Product and engineering role: [[ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli-ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli]] describes Ye as Laifen's technical and product lead during development of its first high-speed hair dryer.
- Commercial learning: [[ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli-ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli]] records weak early sales, later content-driven explanation, and continuing concern about marketing, premium pricing, and overseas operation.
- Organization boundary: [[ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli-ll4ekwtvjnpxit2gdkvcfphf0eli]] says three research teams ultimately report to Ye while the company develops internal design and R&D methods to reduce personal dependence.

## Qualifications
This profile rests on one structured interview summary and largely on Ye's retrospective account. Education, family history, spending, borrowing, ownership, sales, product performance, legal disputes, management culture, and causal links between past projects and later success have not been independently verified here. Product persistence should not be generalized into a rule that financial risk or repeated failure reliably produces useful learning.
