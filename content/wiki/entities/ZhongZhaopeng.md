# 钟照鹏 / Zhong Zhaopeng

## Overview
钟照鹏 is presented as a cardiac-surgery attending physician at Beijing Anzhen Hospital and the clinical guest in VOL.66 of [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]].

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Zhong acts as a public medical educator across symptom triage, hospital navigation, diagnostic limits, hypertension harm, cardiac operations, robotics, pacemakers, and mechanical circulatory support. His recurring decision rule is to identify the disease and treatment objective first, then choose medication, hospital level, surgical access, bypass support, or device technology according to the patient's condition rather than prestige or novelty.

## Key Characteristics
- Presents symptom relief and a normal single test as incomplete evidence rather than proof that cardiac disease is absent.
- Supports urgency-matched care: nearby stabilization for acute illness and targeted larger-center review for stable or difficult cases.
- Treats minimally invasive and robotic surgery as conditional means to a therapeutic end.
- Explains technical cardiac-surgery concepts through accessible distinctions such as sternum versus ribs and electrical pacing versus pump support.
- Frames medication and intervention through comparative risk, emphasizing the harms of untreated disease alongside treatment burdens.
