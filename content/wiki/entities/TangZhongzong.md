## Overview

Tang Zhongzong, personal name Li Xian, enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-536-3-jiemi-shangguan-waner-he-ta-beihou-de-nanrenmen-lqj7rpwwfllogrxrbav-xqwwnnnm|Hanji 536-3]] as the restored Tang emperor whose reign gives [[ShangguanWaner|上官婉儿]] renewed rank and exposure after [[WuZetian|武则天]]'s fall. The episode uses him mainly as a power center around which Shangguan Wan'er, [[WuSansi|武三思]], and [[EmpressWeiTang|韦后]] reorganize court influence.

## Current Profile

The source says that after the 705 [[ShenlongCoup|神龙政变]], Wu Zetian withdrew and Li Xian became emperor. Shangguan Wan'er gained credit for drafting edicts and was made Zhaorong, while her mother also received status. Li Xian then becomes both protector and weak center: during [[LiChongjunTang|李重俊]]'s failed coup, Shangguan Wan'er flees to his bedchamber and persuades him that the rebels will move from killing her to killing the empress and emperor.

## Key Characteristics

- Restored emperor after the Shenlong Coup in the episode's compressed political chronology.
- Grants Shangguan Wan'er Zhaorong rank after her document work.
- Serves as the court center around which Wu Sansi, Empress Wei, and Shangguan Wan'er form influence.
- Acts as immediate protector during Li Chongjun's coup when Shangguan Wan'er appeals to his fear for the throne.
- His death in 710 creates the succession opening in which Empress Wei and Shangguan Wan'er maneuver around Li Dan.

- Court alliance center: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-536-3-jiemi-shangguan-waner-he-ta-beihou-de-nanrenmen-lqj7rpwwfllogrxrbav-xqwwnnnm|Hanji 536-3]] says Shangguan Wan'er recommended Wu Sansi to Empress Wei and that the three formed an alliance inside Zhongzong's court.
- Coup response: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-536-3-jiemi-shangguan-waner-he-ta-beihou-de-nanrenmen-lqj7rpwwfllogrxrbav-xqwwnnnm|Hanji 536-3]] says Li Xian went upstairs to address the troops after Shangguan Wan'er warned him during Li Chongjun's coup.
- Succession trigger: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-536-3-jiemi-shangguan-waner-he-ta-beihou-de-nanrenmen-lqj7rpwwfllogrxrbav-xqwwnnnm|Hanji 536-3]] says his death in 710 led Empress Wei to rearrange offices and spurred Shangguan Wan'er to draft a fallback edict involving Li Dan.

## Qualifications

This page is not a full biography of Tang Zhongzong. It reflects only the episode's role for him in Shangguan Wan'er's later political career. His earlier reign, exile, restoration details, policy record, death circumstances, and full relationship with Empress Wei remain outside the current evidence.
