## Overview

丽姬 / Consort Li is presented as [[HanJingdi|汉景帝]]'s favored consort and the mother of former crown prince [[LiuRongWesternHan|刘荣]]. Her profile is bounded to one popular-history episode, which makes rejection of a marriage alliance, failure to reassure the emperor about the wider household, and a suspected push for empress status the linked political sequence preceding Liu Rong's removal.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-239-bie-beipianle-jinwu-cangjiao-beihoude-yinmou-zhenxiang-1-ljdaasdscqwie1ishma3nwvgzhqp|Hanji 239 part 1]] says [[LiuPiaoWesternHan|馆陶长公主刘嫖]] proposes marrying [[EmpressChenAjiaoWesternHan|陈阿娇]] to Crown Prince Liu Rong. Li refuses because she resents Liu Piao for introducing other women to Jingdi. The refusal does not itself remove Liu Rong, but it reportedly turns a potential ally into an advocate for [[HanWudi|刘彻]] and creates the Liu Piao-[[EmpressDowagerWangWesternHan|王娡]] marriage coalition.

The episode then joins personal conduct to a ruler-side succession test. Jingdi reportedly asks whether Li would care for the other consorts and their sons after his death; her angry refusal makes a Liu Rong succession look unsafe for the wider imperial household. Wang's alleged covert request that a minister seek Li's elevation as empress then lets Jingdi interpret the memorial as harem interference. Liu Rong is deposed, Li loses access to Jingdi, and the episode says she dies in grief.

This sequence supports a profile of coalition and trust failure, not the host's stronger claim that Li's beauty, jealousy, or lack of intelligence fully explains the outcome. Exact interior motive, speeches, coordination, and causal weight remain unverified.

## Key Characteristics

- Favored consort of Han Jingdi and mother of Liu Rong.
- Rejects Liu Piao's proposed Chen Ajiao-Liu Rong marriage in the episode's account.
- Loses a potential succession ally when Liu Piao turns toward Wang and Liu Che.
- Fails the reported request to guarantee humane treatment of rival consorts and royal sons after Jingdi's death.
- Becomes the apparent beneficiary of a ministerial empress request that Jingdi reads as improper harem pressure.
- Loses favor after Liu Rong's deposition and is said to die in grief.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on one short, moralized popular-history summary. It does not independently establish Li's rank and full biography, her responsibility for the rejected proposal, the chronology of Liu Piao's advocacy, Jingdi's exact test, the minister's identity or agency, Wang's secret instruction, the legal meaning of the memorial, the complete reason for Liu Rong's deposition, or the circumstances of Li's death. “恋爱脑,” jealousy, lack of intelligence, and refusal to learn palace rules are host judgments, not settled psychological findings. Li is distinct from the transcription error “丽姬” used for [[LiShiqi|郦食其]] in an unrelated early-Han episode.
