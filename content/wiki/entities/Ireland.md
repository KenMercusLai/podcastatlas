# Ireland

[[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] adds Ireland through [[JackLynch]]'s August 1969 intervention during the [[BattleOfTheBogside]]. The episode says Lynch called for UN peacekeepers and border hospitals for injured Catholics, while the reference to the Irish Army encouraged some Catholics and alarmed many Protestants in [[NorthernIreland]].

Ireland appears in [[vol-123-telangpu-de-duideng-guanshui-an-buzhi-shi-yi-chang-daxing-fucongxing-ceshi-lm6bkiqgyyefwompxon7lo6cnqp6]] as the low-tax jurisdiction in the episode's [[GlobalizationTaxOptimization]] explanation. The source uses the pattern of U.S. R&D, Asian production, global sales, and Irish tax booking to explain why the [[UnitedStates]] government may see border tariffs as a way to recover revenue that does not show up as domestic corporate tax.

The episode specifically mentions [[Apple]]'s Ireland-linked tax payment as evidence that corporate tax residence can materially affect both company accounting and national fiscal receipts. In the wiki, Ireland therefore becomes a tax-structure node rather than a general country profile.
