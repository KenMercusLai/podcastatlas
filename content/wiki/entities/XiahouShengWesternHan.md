## Overview

夏侯胜 / Xiahou Sheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-430-chigua-qunzhong-qianren-yu-xianren-de-gongdou-daxi-lkfcjewfhmhfgw39vqfqkpa9umzq|Hanji 430]] as the crown-prince tutor who interprets [[BingJiWesternHan|丙吉]]'s grave illness through accumulated hidden virtue. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-416-xihan-xiahousheng-ruhe-pai-hanxuandi-de-mapi-lvqbs--20kvjimwdwgfciq-n6wee|Hanji 416]] backfills his earlier prison, release, direct-speech, and teacher-scholar profile under [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]].

## Current Profile

Across the sources, Xiahou Sheng functions as a trusted scholar whose speech is forceful because it is backed by moral credibility. Hanji 416 shows the earlier basis: after being condemned and imprisoned with [[HuangBaWesternHan|黄霸]], he continues studying, is released under Xuan's disaster amnesty, and is promoted because Xuan reads his learning and uprightness as useful rather than threatening. His informality before the emperor and his disclosure of imperial words would be dangerous in another mouth, but the source says Xuan trusts him because he is direct, unselfish, and learned.

Hanji 416 also supplies the page's praise-and-memory layer. Xiahou answers Xuan's anger over leaked words by saying that good royal speech should be spread like the sayings of sage rulers. The episode reads this as [[PraiseAsPoliticalMemory|praise as political memory]]: Xiahou flatters by placing Xuan inside a transmissible sage-ruler frame, not by abandoning his role as a truth-telling adviser. Hanji 430 then shows the same moral-interpreter role in another register. When Xuan worries that Bing Ji may die before receiving his marquis seal, Xiahou argues that a person with hidden virtue will enjoy blessing before death and pass blessing to descendants. Bing Ji's recovery becomes the episode's proof scene for [[YindeRewardCausality|阴德福报因果]].

## Key Characteristics

- Scholar-official and crown-prince tutor whose authority rests on learning, uprightness, and a reputation for having no private agenda.
- Prison learner released and promoted after Xuan treats continued study with Huang Ba as evidence of character.
- Direct speaker whose informality before the emperor is tolerated because it reads as plain truth rather than contempt.
- Uses praise to frame Xuan's words as worthy of sage-ruler transmission and later political memory.
- Explains illness and recovery through hidden virtue and deferred reward.
- Helps turn Bing Ji's service into a moral-causal teaching.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 416 and Hanji 430. It preserves Xiahou Sheng's hidden-virtue claim as the episode's moral interpretation, not as a medical explanation. Hanji 416's "flattery" reading is likewise treated as the host's interpretation of a court-rhetorical move that depends on Xiahou's prior credibility; it is not generalized into a claim that praise is always safe.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 416's earlier Xiahou profile: prison study, release, trusted directness, praise as political memory, and Shangshu-teacher honor.
- Preserved Hanji 430's Bing Ji hidden-virtue interpretation as a later moral-causal branch.
