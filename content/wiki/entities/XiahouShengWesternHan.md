## Overview

夏侯胜 / Xiahou Sheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-430-chigua-qunzhong-qianren-yu-xianren-de-gongdou-daxi-lkfcjewfhmhfgw39vqfqkpa9umzq|Hanji 430]] as the crown-prince tutor who interprets [[BingJiWesternHan|丙吉]]'s grave illness through accumulated hidden virtue.

## Current Profile

In this source, Xiahou Sheng functions as a moral interpreter. When [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] worries that Bing Ji may die before receiving his marquis seal, Xiahou Sheng argues that a person with hidden virtue will enjoy blessing before death and pass blessing to descendants. Bing Ji's recovery then becomes the episode's proof scene for [[YindeRewardCausality|阴德福报因果]].

## Key Characteristics

- Scholar-official serving as taizi taifu in the episode.
- Explains illness and recovery through hidden virtue and deferred reward.
- Helps turn Bing Ji's service into a moral-causal teaching.
