# San Francisco Bay Area

The San Francisco Bay Area is the regional startup-network setting in [[tanfang-hacker-house-guigu-nianqingren-zhengzai-banjin-ai-chuangye-sushe-s10e10-f0870843-fd16-49f5-8f24-80281f9fff77]]. The source treats the Bay Area as a dense supply chain for founders, investors, media, technical peers, and risk-taking culture, making it central to [[FounderNetworkArbitrage]].

The episode says [[TheResidency]] stays focused on the Bay Area because the United States contains a large share of global unicorns and the Bay Area contains a large share of U.S. unicorns. It also mentions post-pandemic talent return after the ChatGPT moment and notes that high housing costs and California tax changes push some founders or investors toward [[Texas]] and [[Florida]].
