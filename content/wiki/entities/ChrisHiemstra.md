# Chris Hiemstra

## Overview
Chris Hiemstra is a research scientist who guides the [[HiddenBrain]] host through an Alaskan permafrost tunnel and explains the climate danger created when long-frozen organic material thaws.

## Current Profile
In [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-ce411815c1cca7882f5b93d662620544-51879597-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-ce411815c1cca7882f5b93d662620544-51879597]], Hiemstra makes [[PermafrostCarbonFeedback]] tangible through preserved plants, animals, and fungi and the changing landscape around him. His communication problem is not simply lack of evidence: the threat is remote, gradual, difficult for most audiences to experience, and filtered through misinformation and distrust of scientists.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses a physical permafrost tunnel to make a planetary process locally visible.
- Connects thawed organic material with decomposition and carbon release.
- Frames permafrost thaw as a feedback process rather than an isolated consequence of warming.
- Expresses frustration at the gap between visible scientific warning and public reception.
