# Lori Huang

## Overview
Lori Huang is [[JensenHuang|Jensen Huang]]'s wife and, in his account, the principal family support that made his long work hours and risk-taking while building [[Nvidia]] possible.

## Current Profile
The bounded source traces the relationship to Oregon State University and emphasizes an asymmetry in family labor: Huang says Lori managed the household without complaint while he completed graduate study, built Nvidia, and missed many of their children's activities. The account recognizes her contribution but comes through Huang rather than Lori's own testimony.

## Key Characteristics
- Met Huang while both were studying at Oregon State University.
- Managed much of the family's daily life during Huang's intensive work and study years.
- Provided the security Huang associates with his willingness to take strategic risks.

## Evidence
### Family support
- [[vmp8419655880-vmp8419655880]] records Huang crediting Lori with managing the family while he worked long hours and missed children's events.

### Risk capacity

## Qualifications
This profile is based on Huang's retrospective account, not Lori Huang's independent perspective. It should not convert gratitude into a complete account of her work, preferences, sacrifices, or relationship.
