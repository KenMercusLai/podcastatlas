## Overview

桓帝五侯 / Five Marquises of Emperor Huan's reign is the Huan-era eunuch-reward group whose profile now begins with the 159 CE post-[[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] settlement in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-850-huandi-weihe-zhiyi-sha-zhongchen-lokxtceq7vv-giec6iufwwk9fwk7|Hanji 850]] and continues into the 160 CE arrogance and local-predation branch in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-853-jiemi-hanchao-ban-de-zhaoshi-gu-er-lvsa2pby8iyfevfoobobpik52mij|Hanji 853]]. The page treats the group as a political ecology rather than a complete membership register.

## Current Profile

Hanji 850 supplies the founding pressure point. After [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] kills Liang Ji, the reward structure expands toward the Empress Deng family and eunuch-favorite network. [[ShanChaoLateHan|单超]] is enfeoffed with an especially large household grant, four other eunuchs receive grants above ten thousand households, and [[LiYunLateHan|李云]] argues that the rewards exceed ordinary court scale and risk alienating frontier troops who fought without comparable recognition.

Hanji 853 then shows the same group ecology after its prestige has matured. Shan Chao receives lavish imperial burial treatment, including imperial coffin and clothing gifts, Five Camps cavalry, and a construction office-led tomb. The source then says the remaining four marquises become more indulgent and arrogant.

The local consequence is the page's second main point. Relatives, guests, and servants attached to the marquises or nearby eunuch favorites appear in provincial and commandery contexts, extracting wealth and producing violence. The Zhao Qi branch makes this concrete through [[ZuoGuanLateHan|左悺]]'s brother [[ZuoShengLateHan|左胜]] and [[TangHengLateHan|唐衡]]'s brother [[TangXuanLateHan|唐玹]].

## Key Characteristics

- Huan-era eunuch reward group whose status begins in the post-Liang political settlement.
- Reward bloc whose large enfeoffments become a target of Li Yun's open remonstrance.
- Court-favored group whose mortality and honor are later linked to intensified indulgence.
- Patronage ecology whose relatives, guests, and servants can become local predatory actors.
- Background network behind Zhao Qi's refusal to serve under Zuo Sheng and Tang Xuan's retaliation.
- Source-bounded bridge between court reward and local enforcement failure.

## Evidence

- Founding reward layer: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-850-huandi-weihe-zhiyi-sha-zhongchen-lokxtceq7vv-giec6iufwwk9fwk7|Hanji 850]] says Emperor Huan rewards the eunuch-favorite side after Liang Ji's fall, with Shan Chao receiving an especially large grant and four other eunuchs receiving grants above ten thousand households.
- Remonstrance target: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-850-huandi-weihe-zhiyi-sha-zhongchen-lokxtceq7vv-giec6iufwwk9fwk7|Hanji 850]] says Li Yun treats the excessive rewards as politically dangerous because frontier troops had fought without comparable recognition.
- Court favor: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-853-jiemi-hanchao-ban-de-zhaoshi-gu-er-lvsa2pby8iyfevfoobobpik52mij|Hanji 853]] says Shan Chao receives imperial coffin and clothing gifts, Five Camps cavalry, and construction-office support for burial.
- Remaining-marquis behavior: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-853-jiemi-hanchao-ban-de-zhaoshi-gu-er-lvsa2pby8iyfevfoobobpik52mij|Hanji 853]] says the four remaining marquises become more indulgent and arrogant after Shan Chao's death.
- Local predation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-853-jiemi-hanchao-ban-de-zhaoshi-gu-er-lvsa2pby8iyfevfoobobpik52mij|Hanji 853]] says relatives and client-retainer networks tied to the powerful hold provincial and commandery offices, plunder people, and worsen public order.
- Zhao Qi branch: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-853-jiemi-hanchao-ban-de-zhaoshi-gu-er-lvsa2pby8iyfevfoobobpik52mij|Hanji 853]] says Zhao Qi resigns rather than serve under Zuo Sheng and later loses his family through Tang Xuan's retaliation.

## Qualifications

The bounded evidence now starts with Hanji 850's reward critique and continues with Hanji 853's burial and local-predation evidence. The source set still does not reconstruct the full membership register or later biographies of every marquis. Hou Lan and Duan Gui appear in the same eunuch-favorite ecology, but this page does not assume they are members of the Five Marquises unless a source says so.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 850 as the founding reward layer before the Hanji 853 death, arrogance, and local-predation evidence.
- Reframed Li Yun's memorial as an early critique of the group's reward scale and political cost.
- Kept the membership caveat in place because the source set still does not give a complete register.
