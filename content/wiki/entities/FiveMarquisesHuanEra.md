## Overview

桓帝五侯 / Five Marquises of Emperor Huan's reign is the Huan-era eunuch-reward group represented in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-853-jiemi-hanchao-ban-de-zhaoshi-gu-er-lvsa2pby8iyfevfoobobpik52mij|Hanji 853]] through [[ShanChaoLateHan|单超]]'s death, the remaining marquises' indulgence, and family-linked local pressure around [[ZuoGuanLateHan|左悺]] and [[TangHengLateHan|唐衡]]. The page treats the group as a political ecology rather than a complete membership register.

## Current Profile

Hanji 853 presents the group after the [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] crisis has already opened space for eunuch-favorite reward. Shan Chao receives lavish imperial burial treatment, including imperial coffin and clothing gifts, Five Camps cavalry, and a construction office-led tomb. The source then says the remaining four marquises become more indulgent and arrogant.

The local consequence is the page's main point. Relatives, guests, and servants attached to the marquises or nearby eunuch favorites appear in provincial and commandery contexts, extracting wealth and producing violence. The Zhao Qi branch makes this concrete through Zuo Guan's brother [[ZuoShengLateHan|左胜]] and Tang Heng's brother [[TangXuanLateHan|唐玹]].

## Key Characteristics

- Huan-era eunuch reward group whose status follows the post-Liang political settlement.
- Court-favored group whose mortality and honor are linked in the episode to intensified indulgence.
- Patronage ecology whose relatives, guests, and servants can become local predatory actors.
- Background network behind Zhao Qi's refusal to serve under Zuo Sheng and Tang Xuan's retaliation.
- Source-bounded bridge between court reward and local enforcement failure.

## Evidence

- Court favor: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-853-jiemi-hanchao-ban-de-zhaoshi-gu-er-lvsa2pby8iyfevfoobobpik52mij|Hanji 853]] says Shan Chao receives imperial coffin and clothing gifts, Five Camps cavalry, and construction-office support for burial.
- Remaining-marquis behavior: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-853-jiemi-hanchao-ban-de-zhaoshi-gu-er-lvsa2pby8iyfevfoobobpik52mij|Hanji 853]] says the four remaining marquises become more indulgent and arrogant after Shan Chao's death.
- Local predation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-853-jiemi-hanchao-ban-de-zhaoshi-gu-er-lvsa2pby8iyfevfoobobpik52mij|Hanji 853]] says relatives and client-retainer networks tied to the powerful hold provincial and commandery offices, plunder people, and worsen public order.
- Zhao Qi branch: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-853-jiemi-hanchao-ban-de-zhaoshi-gu-er-lvsa2pby8iyfevfoobobpik52mij|Hanji 853]] says Zhao Qi resigns rather than serve under Zuo Sheng and later loses his family through Tang Xuan's retaliation.

## Qualifications

The source does not list every member of the Five Marquises or reconstruct the full enfeoffment history. Hou Lan and Duan Gui appear in the same 160 CE eunuch-favorite ecology, but this page does not assume they are members of the Five Marquises. It records only the group mechanisms supported by Hanji 853.
