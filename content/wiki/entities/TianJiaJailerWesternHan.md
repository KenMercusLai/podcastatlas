## Overview

田甲 / Tian Jia is the Liang jailer in the episode's completed “死灰复燃” anecdote. This page uses a disambiguated identity because the wiki already contains [[TianJia|田假 / Tian Jia]], a different person from the Qin-Han transition.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-266-sihui-furan-yu-yi-pao-niao-de-gushi-2-ltzr_gqioz9ftrqzbs7gyced90fm|Hanji 266 part 2]] says Tian Jia had humiliated the imprisoned [[HanAnguoWesternHan|韩安国]] and answered Han's warning that dead ash might rekindle by saying he would urinate on it. When Han returns to high office, Tian Jia flees in fear of revenge.

Han reportedly threatens Tian Jia's family to force his return, repeats the urination taunt after Tian Jia apologizes, and then refuses to punish him because he considers the jailer beneath revenge. Tian Jia's role is therefore not an independent career profile; he is the vulnerable counterparty through whom the source examines abuse during another person's weakness and restraint after a reversal of power.

## Key Characteristics

- Liang jailer accused in the episode of humiliating Han Anguo during imprisonment.
- Speaker of the urination-on-rekindled-ash response associated with the “死灰复燃” story.
- Frightened fugitive who returns after Han's restoration and is ultimately spared.

## Qualifications

The page is bounded to one retelling of an anecdote. Tian Jia's office, conduct, exact words, flight, family threat, apology, and Han's reason for sparing him require comparison with the transmitted historical text. The moral lesson drawn from the story should not be mistaken for a full biography or an independent account of Liang prison administration.
