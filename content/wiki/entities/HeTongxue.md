# 何同学 / He Tongxue

## Overview
何同学 / He Tongxue is represented as a Chinese video creator whose 5G breakthrough grew into [[HeTongxueStudio|a production studio]] combining storytelling, photography, editing, animation, engineering, and large DIY builds.

## Current Profile
He describes himself as a video-maker first. Technology, product knowledge, and engineered objects are materials for entertainment and occasional science communication, not evidence that he wants to operate a hardware manufacturer. His favored large projects start from small lived frustrations, make the solution visually surprising, and remain legible enough for broad distribution.

Success has expanded what he can build while increasing quality pressure, public scrutiny, cost, and managerial load. He still participates in topic selection, writing, filming, post-production, and subtitle rhythm, but recognizes that a 32-person studio cannot sustainably depend on founder inspection of every detail. His unresolved task is to become more like a coach without losing the taste and timing associated with his personal channel.

## Key Characteristics
- Video-first creator who treats technology and DIY as expressive material rather than a manufacturing destination.
- Detail-oriented editor attentive to story, image, effects, color, animation, and even subtitle line breaks.
- High-standard creator whose viral success produced anxiety and slower publication as well as greater resources.
- Studio founder learning management without prior experience as an employee in a mature company.
- Commercially restrained but responsible for a team whose wages and project costs make revenue necessary.
- Public personal brand whose individual speech can affect employees, clients, and company finances.
