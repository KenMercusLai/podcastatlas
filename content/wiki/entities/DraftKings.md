# DraftKings

## Overview

DraftKings is a mobile sportsbook represented by two bounded sources: one uses its wager data in a sports-integrity case, while the other uses its account controls, customer data, and advertising scale to criticize the post-legalization betting market.

## Current Profile

[[tech-20260324-0324-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260324-0324-mp-tech-pod-128]] places DraftKings inside a licensed data environment that made unusual wagers around [[JontayPorter]] visible. That source emphasizes the public value of tracked bets, insider screening, integrity monitors, and reporting duties.

[[gambling-with-michael-lewis-gambling-with-michael-lewis]] presents the other side of granular platform data. It reports that DraftKings lowered [[RufusPeabody]]'s golf limits after a losing week because later line movement suggested his wagers had a durable advantage. The episode also groups DraftKings with [[FanDuel]] in a claim about extensive advertising and the media incentives that spending creates.

Together, the sources show that the same broad data infrastructure can support both [[SportsbookIntegrityMonitoring|event-integrity monitoring]] and [[MobileSportsbookCustomerSelection|commercial customer selection]]. They do not establish that the two purposes have the same rules, legitimacy, or effects.

## Key Characteristics

- Generates granular wager records that can expose suspicious event-linked betting patterns.
- Uses account-level controls capable of changing how much an individual customer may wager.
- Appears as a major advertiser in the source's account of sports-media dependence.
- Sits at the boundary between public integrity benefits and private customer-profitability decisions.

## Evidence

### Integrity data

- [[tech-20260324-0324-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260324-0324-mp-tech-pod-128]] says bettors connected to the Porter case became the day's biggest DraftKings winners on NBA bets after the first suspicious game.

### Skilled-bettor limits

- [[gambling-with-michael-lewis-gambling-with-michael-lewis]] reports that DraftKings reduced Peabody's golf limits even after a short-term loss because his bets repeatedly preceded favorable line movement.

### Advertising scale

- [[gambling-with-michael-lewis-gambling-with-michael-lewis]] groups DraftKings and FanDuel in an estimate of roughly $2 billion each in marketing over five years.

## Qualifications

Neither source provides DraftKings's complete policies, internal data, financial definitions, or a substantive company response. The Porter source uses DraftKings as one node in a wider monitoring system rather than attributing the entire detection process to the company. The Lewis source is openly critical, reports that DraftKings declined to participate, and does not establish how common Peabody's treatment is or test operator explanations for limits.

## What Changed

- Added customer selection and advertising to the earlier integrity-monitoring profile.
- Distinguished public event oversight from commercial account-level risk control.
