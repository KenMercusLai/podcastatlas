## Overview

Emperor Ai of Han / 汉哀帝 is synthesized as a late [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] emperor whose current wiki role spans pre-accession succession performance, political reputation, accession promise, birth-family pressure, banquet-status conflict, early self-image, illness-driven ritual instability, filial-duty pressure, favorite-origin politics, fabricated merit, public-armory privatization, popular-suffering warnings, favorite-driven reward risk, invited-but-filtered criticism, suspicious court governance, partial correction, abdication-language danger, diplomatic ritual, succession rupture, and posthumous burial timing. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-551-jiemi-hanchengdi-de-huangwei-chuancheng-zhimi-lkefo-pkg_n8ll57kdsofcrpad_v|Hanji 551]] supplies the pre-accession layer: as Dingtao king Liu Xin, he impresses [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] through retinue procedure, legal knowledge, and [[Shijing|《诗经》]] interpretation while [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]] works palace relationships for him. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-562-han-aidi-jiwei-shi-you-duo-biequ-lpv8_ks5b3c0ow_3o9_mvxi9n3xu|Hanji 562]] supplies the accession layer: Aidi begins with thrift and personal handling of affairs, but Fu Taihou's North Palace residence, daily access through the Zifang passage, title demand, and relative-promotion pressure quickly limit how freely the new ruler can govern. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-565-ruiqi-he-ni-liaoliao-xiaofanju-li-de-daxuewen-ljeybels0exmbtpzm-npxgxgx2lfq|Hanji 565]] adds the banquet-seat hinge: after [[WangMang|王莽]] removes Fu Taihou's seat beside [[WangZhengjun|王政君]], Aidi lets Wang Mang retire with honors, appoints [[ShiDanWesternHan|施丹]] as Da Sima, and sidelines [[FuXiWesternHan|傅喜]] despite court support. The Xiangrui source rereads him as more active than the damaged later image centered on [[DongXian|董贤]], while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-592-handai-chengxiang-shi-ruhe-pingjia-qian-boss-de-lncdt3l6lk1hfxupuxti_dsjh9u5|Hanji 592]] preserves the early image [[WangJiaWesternHan|王嘉]] uses rhetorically: Aidi as learned, frugal, and attentive to public burden before the later Dong Xian crisis. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-580-huigu-sizhou-shuishi-nide-kaoshan-liid3tymhojmajkyeqoq6vryprhz|Hanji 580]] adds an earlier patronage-and-filial-pressure layer: Aidi refuses [[ZhuBoWesternHan|朱博]] and [[ZhaoXuanWesternHan|赵璇]]'s request to strip Wang Mang because Wang Zhengjun remains Wang Mang's political shelter, accepts [[YangXuanWesternHan|杨宣]]'s reminder that Chengdi chose him to serve Wang Zhengjun, and arranges [[DingJiWesternHan|丁姬]]'s Dingtao burial with his birth father. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-581-han-aidi-zai-guishen-zhidao-you-duo-yuchun-ltok7kbzmuf-os84spseww0qjbrm|Hanji 581]] adds an earlier vulnerability layer: Aidi's illness, no-heir pressure, and disaster anxiety make him accept [[XiaHeliangWesternHan|夏贺良]]'s renewed-mandate proposal, publicly change reign title, imperial style, and timekeeping, then reverse most of the edict after the promised cure fails. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-585-ta-yu-ta-de-aiqing-keyi-duo-fuza-lltwzxxbjedik65yonehtfaeefsa|Hanji 585]] adds the relationship-origin layer: Aidi notices Dong Xian in palace service, elevates him rapidly, extends favor to his wife, sister, father, in-laws, house, treasure access, burial goods, and tomb site, and turns criticism of that affection into deadly risk for [[ZhengChongWesternHan|郑崇]] and [[SunBaoWesternHan|孙宝]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-586-weile-aiqing-gudai-huangdi-you-duo-chongdong-lizfxc8a4do88e6vyrcupyet3vs|Hanji 586]] adds the next escalation: Aidi accepts [[FuJiaWesternHan|傅嘉]]'s proposal to alter the Dongping king Liu Yun case record so Dong Xian can appear meritorious, then overrides [[WangJiaWesternHan|王嘉]] and [[JiaYanWesternHan|贾延]]'s call for public examination and enfeoffs Dong Xian as Gaoan Hou. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-587-shui-hui-ba-wuqi-dang-liwu-songgei-airen-llt5femzyvgmi2yldvvbefj-0e9t|Hanji 587]] adds the armory boundary layer: Aidi repeatedly sends officials to choose state weapons for Dong Xian and related households, while [[WuJianglongWesternHan|武将龙]] argues that publicly funded military stores cannot become private gifts. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-588-laoban-bu-chenzhi-xiashu-neng-you-duo-can-lovz7epf8qs0atyr3njy2kvblqpv|Hanji 588]] adds a harsher public-livelihood diagnosis from [[BaoXuanWesternHan|鲍宣]]: commoners face seven harms and seven deaths while the court rewards outer relatives, harmful insiders, and Dong Xian's circle. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-590-gudai-zhongguo-de-shengsi-choudi-shi-ta-lhr5pxx1-ldo6qtxibdryjtarh4a|Hanji 590]] adds one positive correction case: Aidi accepts [[YangXiong|扬雄]]'s warning, reverses an attempted refusal of a [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] chanyu visit, and repairs the immediate diplomatic signal. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-594-ni-shenbian-you-yi-ziwo-wei-zhongxin-de-ren-ma-lo27wnfpchgxpzvstea4azmxsga2|Hanji 594]] adds a sharper self-knowledge problem: Aidi requests fault statements after disasters, but [[DuYeWesternHan|杜邺]]'s memorial about dowager deference, outer-relative offices, and eclipse-linked Da Sima appointments does not visibly become correction. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-595-wangmang-shazi-jielu-quanli-de-canku-lh9nr18cllq1rrl0nz4fzipai4eq|Hanji 595]] adds a Wang-family return layer: Aidi recalls Wang Mang and Wang Ren to serve Wang Zhengjun after petition pressure for Wang Mang. ## Current Profile

Hanji 562 and the Xiangrui source keep Aidi's early profile from being flattened into later failure. Hanji 562 says he takes the throne in 7 BCE, honors Wang Zhengjun and Zhao Feiyan, amnesties the realm, practices thrift, reduces expenses, and personally handles government. The Xiangrui source says Aidi tried to constrain the Wang consort family, reduce imperial expenses, attend to government, and consider limits on land and enslaved dependents. Hanji 592 adds the source-scoped version of the same contested-reputation problem from inside Wang Jia's memorial: Wang Jia says Aidi as Dingtao king loved the classics, entered Chang'an with public hope attached to him, began rule with visible austerity, and delayed Gonghuang temple construction out of concern for expense and commoner burden. That praise is strategic rather than neutral biography, but it keeps Aidi's early image from being collapsed into his later Dong Xian favoritism.

Hanji 562 also adds the first constraint on that promise. Aidi wants better placement for his birth grandmother and mother, but Kong Guang warns against bringing Fu Taihou too close because she is forceful and politically active. He Wu recommends North Palace instead; once Fu Taihou can reach Weiyang Palace daily, she presses for title and relative advancement. Aidi first retreats when Wang Mang and Shi Dan impeach Dong Hong's proposal, but then seeks Wang Zhengjun's approval after Fu Taihou's anger, setting up the later Dingtao-line conflicts.

Hanji 565 shows that the same status pressure could erupt through protocol before formal title settlement. Aidi's banquet places Fu Taihou next to Wang Zhengjun, but Wang Mang removes the seat on ritual-rank grounds. Aidi then accepts Wang Mang's retirement rather than preserving him in active office, while cushioning the removal with gold, an anju carriage, special status, palace access, and frequent imperial meals. The source also shows Aidi not immediately installing Fu Xi despite broad support, because Fu Taihou distrusts him.

Hanji 580 adds the earlier court-balance and personal-filial branch. Aidi does not fully accept Zhu Bo and Zhao Xuan's attack on Wang Mang, leaving Wang Mang's title and fief intact because Wang Zhengjun remains too important to disregard. Yang Xuan's secret memorial then makes the restraint explicit: Aidi's succession from Chengdi carries an obligation to serve Wang Zhengjun, not humiliate her and her family. The same source also shows Aidi honoring his birth parents by returning Ding Taihou to Dingtao for burial with his father, which complicates the profile by making Aidi visibly filial in one family direction while politically constrained by another.

Hanji 581 adds the earlier ritual-instability branch. Aidi's illness and court disorder make him receptive to Xia Heliang's inherited [[Taipingjing|《太平经》]]-based claim that disaster warnings require renewed Heaven authorization. He changes the reign title from Jianping to Taichu, adopts "陈圣刘太平皇帝," and changes the water-clock scale from one hundred to one hundred twenty, but reverses most of the edict after the promised health and order improvements fail to appear.

Hanji 585 supplies the origin and escalation layer of that favoritism. Aidi notices Dong Xian during palace communication, promotes him to close-attendant status, shares bed and carriage with him, and turns the "断袖" anecdote into the symbolic memory of the relationship. The favor then expands from intimacy to institution: Dong Xian's wife enters the palace, his sister becomes Zhaoyi, his father and in-law network receive offices, his mansion and tomb complex are built at extraordinary scale, and imperial treasure and burial goods flow toward him.

Dong Xian-related correction is already dangerous before the later Bao Xuan and Wang Jia sequence. Zheng Chong criticizes the overfavor, Aidi repeatedly blames him, Zhao Chang turns private resentment into an accusation about Liu-kin contacts, and Sun Bao's investigation finds no confession despite harsh interrogation. Aidi removes Sun Bao and Zheng Chong dies in prison, showing that criticism of the favorite can become a legal and career-ending exposure.

Hanji 586 shows Aidi turning this favorite relationship into fabricated public merit. Because Dong Xian lacks a proper marquisate basis, Aidi accepts Fu Jia's proposal to revise the Dongping king Liu Yun accusation record, first granting Guannei Hou status and then using an edict to credit Dong Xian and others with discovering rebellion. Wang Jia and Jia Yan warn that the case record should be opened for ministerial and scholarly examination before any additional reward, but Aidi ultimately imposes the reward from above.

Hanji 587 adds that Aidi's Dong Xian favor also reaches the public armory. Aidi repeatedly sends officials to select weapons from the armory for Dong Xian and related households. Wu Jianglong objects from the position of Zhijinwu, distinguishing Da Sinong-funded public military resources from Shaofu-managed imperial-household spending and warning that armory weapons are command and defense instruments, not private presents.

Hanji 588 now adds the people's-burden critique before the Wang Jia sequence. Bao Xuan tells Aidi that commoners face disaster, tax and corvee pressure, official greed, land seizure by powerful households, theft, harsh punishment, wrongful cases, famine, and disease. The episode's title judgment that Aidi is an unfit "boss" rests on this contrast: the ruler's court is tolerating outer-relative and favorite privilege while ordinary households cannot secure food, clothing, family members, or property.

Hanji 593 adds the strongest current evidence for why the Dong Xian-centered image was politically damaging. Wang Jia's memorial says Aidi directs extraordinary attention and public resources toward Dong Xian's house: mansion and garden works, utensils, food and ritual provisioning for Dong Xian's mother, guest and wedding expenses, market escorts, servant rewards, and large land grants. Aidi's displeasure after reading the memorial shows that even carefully sequenced criticism of this private favor still threatens the feedback channel.

Hanji 594 adds a parallel Du Ye remonstrance branch. Aidi invites criticism after disasters, and Du Ye answers through the court's own omen and ritual vocabulary: eclipses and earthquakes signal imbalance, filial respect does not require blind obedience to parental or dowager wishes, and relatives without merit should not accumulate palace, guard, and military authority. The absence of a recorded correction, followed by Aidi's later eclipse consultation with Kong Guang and Kong's promotion, makes Aidi's fault-seeking look selective rather than fully receptive.

Hanji 597 and Hanji 598 add the suspicious-ruler layer. In Hanji 597, Aidi uses a claimed Fu Taihou instruction to seek extra households for Dong Xian and fiefs for several marquises; Wang Jia's sealed return of the edict turns that favor package into a direct challenge to Aidi's private reward will. In Hanji 598, when [[LiangXiangWesternHan|梁相]] asks to review the Dongping king Liu Yun case, Aidi reads the request as an attempt to delay execution. When Wang Jia later recommends Liang's group, Aidi treats talent preservation as a challenge to imperial judgment and sends Wang Jia toward Tingwei custody.

Hanji 599 shows Aidi's limited capacity to correct after damage is done. He is angry that Wang Jia obeys the summons alive rather than dying privately, but after Wang Jia dies in prison and his final words are reported, Aidi restores [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] and [[HeWuWesternHan|何武]] and dismisses [[FuJiaWesternHan|傅嘉]] for slandering worthy officials. The correction does not become durable reform: Aidi also dismisses [[DingMingWesternHan|丁明]] for grieving Wang Jia and soon returns attention to private favor toward Dong Xian.

Hanji 601-1 makes the succession-language danger explicit before Aidi's death. After Xiao Xian reads Dong-family appointment language as Yao-Shun abdication-coded, Aidi later says at a Qilin Hall banquet that he intends to imitate Yao by yielding to Dong Xian. [[WangHongWesternHan|王洪]] interrupts with a dynastic objection, and Aidi punishes him by removing palace access before later recalling him.

Hanji 602 adds a court-ritual and diplomacy layer from Yuanshou 2 / 1 BCE. Aidi receives a Xiongnu chanyu in Chang'an, has Dong Xian identified as a young but highly placed grand marshal, and lodges the chanyu in Shanglinyuan's Putao Palace because of a Tai Sui rationale.

Hanji 590 backfills the Jianping 4 / 3 BCE decision that made the later visit possible. Aidi initially risks damaging Han-Xiongnu relations by delaying or refusing the chanyu's audience, but he accepts Yang Xiong's memorial, recalls the already-returning Xiongnu envoy, changes the reply, agrees to the next-year visit, and rewards Yang Xiong.

Hanji 595 adds a court-balance layer immediately before the later Dong Xian crisis. After Wang Mang's fief retreat and reputation recovery, Aidi recalls Wang Mang and Wang Ren to Chang'an to serve Wang Zhengjun. The same source shows Aidi accepting Dong Xian's eclipse-framed accusation against Fu Yan and Xifu Gong, so Wang-family rehabilitation and Dong Xian's anti-Fu influence coexist in the same late-reign field.

Hanji 603 adds the immediate succession-rupture layer. Aidi dies in the sixth month of Yuanshou 2 / 1 BCE at Weiyang Palace, age twenty-five, after finalizing a three-excellency arrangement with Dong Xian as Da Sima Wei General, Kong Guang as Da Situ, and Peng Xuan as Da Sikong. His lack of a son lets [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] seize the imperial seal and command channels while [[WangMang|王莽]] removes Dong Xian.

Hanji 610-2 adds a ritual-body layer: Aidi dies in the sixth month but is buried in the tenth month at Yiling, giving the wiki a concrete example of extended imperial停灵 and [[ImperialFuneraryPreservation|帝王停灵防腐]].

## Key Characteristics

- Aidi's wiki profile is not limited to the later Dong Xian-centered reputation; Hanji 562, Hanji 565, and Wang Jia's memorial preserve an early image of thrift and personal government already constrained by birth-family residence, title, and banquet-seating pressure.
- Bao Xuan's public-suffering memorial makes Aidi's failure visible at the people-and-officials level before later Dong Xian succession risk becomes explicit.
- His conflict with Wang-family power makes Wang Mang's later return politically contingent.
- His Dong Xian favoritism makes private affection visible as a family-promotion, palace-access, public-construction, public-finance, military-resource, market-order, land, funerary, fabricated-merit, reward-procedure, and succession-boundary problem.
- His advice reception is uneven: Yang Xuan's Wang-family memorial and Yang Xiong's Xiongnu memorial convert into correction, Xia Heliang's renewed-mandate proposal gains temporary acceptance and then reversal, while Du Ye's outer-relative warning does not visibly do so.
- His Dong Xian-centered suspicion can convert legal caution, personnel advice, direct correction, and even dissenting sympathy into disloyalty, while later partial corrections remain unstable.
- His sudden death without a son creates the succession field in which Wang Zhengjun and Wang Mang become decisive, while diplomatic and burial notices show the ritual layer around his late reign and death.

## Qualifications

The wiki does not yet have a full reign narrative for Han Aidi. Hanji 562 is focused on accession, Fu Taihou's residence/title pressure, Dong Hong's proposal, and early relative rewards rather than a complete account of Aidi's first-year policy record or the full legal history of Dingtao-line elevation; Hanji 565 is focused on one banquet-seat conflict, Wang Mang's honored retirement, Fu Xi's sidelining, and early anti-Wang timing rather than the full personnel record; the Xiangrui source is interpretive and focused on Wang Mang's legitimacy problem; Hanji 592 preserves Wang Jia's strategic praise rather than a neutral reign assessment; Hanji 580 preserves a short court-balance and Dingtao-burial branch rather than a full account of the Wang purge, Yang Xuan's career, or Aidi's birth-parent ritual policy; Hanji 581 preserves the host's reading of Aidi's renewed-mandate error and does not settle the full Taipingjing textual history, water-clock procedure, or each adviser's private motive; Hanji 585 preserves the host's reading of the Dong Xian relationship and its political consequences rather than a full sexuality, legal, or institutional history; Hanji 586 preserves the source's account of fabricated Dongping-case merit and public-procedure objection rather than the complete Liu Yun case file; Hanji 587 preserves Wu Jianglong's armory and official-slave remonstrance rather than a complete military-fiscal law code; Hanji 588 preserves Bao Xuan's critical memorial and the host's unfit-ruler reading rather than a balanced reign appraisal; Hanji 590 is focused on one corrected Xiongnu reception decision rather than Aidi's broader foreign policy; Hanji 593 and Hanji 597 are focused on Wang Jia's memorials against Dong Xian-centered overfavor and reward; Hanji 594 is focused on Du Ye's omen-coded critique and the source's inference about Aidi's selective hearing; Hanji 598 is focused on Wang Jia, Liang Xiang, and Dong Xian-related court danger; Hanji 599 is focused on Wang Jia's interrogation, death, and immediate personnel aftermath; Hanji 601-1 is focused on abdication-coded language, Wang Hong's intervention, and Dong Xian's office risk; Hanji 602 is a short bridge from a court notice to Tai Sui belief; Hanji 603 is focused on the immediate succession crisis; and Hanji 610-2 is focused on funerary timing. Aidi's exact cause of death, full policy record, full Dong Xian relationship, complete Dongping case record, exact armory goods, and full succession process remain source-scoped.
