# Bandai / 万代

## Overview
Bandai / 万代 is a Japanese toy company represented in the source through licensing-led expansion of [[Tamagotchi|拓麻歌子]] in Asian markets outside Japan.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Bandai as using external brands and categories to reach people beyond traditional toy distribution. Phone cases, cosmetics, luggage, a conveyor-belt sushi chain, and K-pop collaborations let Tamagotchi operate as a fashion, lifestyle, and nostalgia IP as well as an electronic toy.

## Key Characteristics
- Owner and commercial operator of the Tamagotchi case in the source.
- Uses licensing to reach audiences traditional toy channels may miss.
- Extends a device character across fashion, beauty, travel, foodservice, and music contexts.
- Targets underpenetrated Asian markets outside Japan.
