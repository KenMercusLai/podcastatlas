# Randy Crane

## Overview

Randy Crane is identified by the episode as chief judge of the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Texas and organizer of its modern prize-rule drafting effort.

## Current Profile

Crane assembled a team after the [[USDepartmentOfJustice|U.S. Department of Justice]] warned that a prize case could arrive with little notice. The resulting 30-page rules adapt historical evidence collection and questioning to modern agencies and vessel tracking while allowing early sale of hazardous or perishable cargo. His team did not prejudge whether undeclared armed conflict satisfies a statute using the word “war.”

## Key Characteristics

- Prepared a federal district for a legal procedure that had largely fallen out of modern use.
- Combined historical prize practice with current investigative agencies and AIS vessel data.
- Preserved judicial adjudication of the threshold war question instead of embedding the executive branch's preferred answer in local rules.

## Qualifications

The profile reflects a preparatory rulemaking exercise, not a decided prize case. Publication of procedure does not settle jurisdiction, constitutionality, claimant rights, speed, or appellate review.

## What Changed

- Created the profile around Crane's role in designing modern prize procedure while leaving the war threshold unresolved.
