# Staci Whitman

## Overview
Staci Whitman is the dentist and [[HubermanLab]] guest who presents oral health as a preventive, microbial, nutritional, and airway problem connected to whole-body health.

## Current Profile
In [[how-to-improve-your-teeth-oral-microbiome-for-brain-body-health-dr-staci-whitman-scim9002750849]], Whitman argues for an ecosystem-centered approach: preserve saliva and useful oral organisms, reduce repeated acid exposure, disrupt plaque mechanically, investigate persistent symptoms, and consider breathing and craniofacial development alongside teeth and gums. Her clinical framing spans adults and children and favors prevention before restoration, but many of the episode's oral-systemic, fluoride, ozone, testing, supplement, and newer-procedure claims remain source-scoped or medically contingent.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames dentistry through prevention, nutrition, microbial ecology, airway function, and whole-body context.
- Favors gentle brushing, interdental cleaning, whole foods, hydration, and nasal breathing over indiscriminate antimicrobial product use.
- Distinguishes early mineral loss that may be reversible from cavitated disease that usually requires treatment.
- Treats bleeding, halitosis, dry mouth, mouth breathing, and altered oral function as signals for further assessment.
- Emphasizes early airway and oral-function assessment in children while recognizing structural limits in adults.
- Discusses fluoride and dental materials through risk-benefit, exposure, and consent questions rather than a single product rule.
