## Overview

张成 / Zhang Cheng is a late-Han Henan yin figure whose current wiki profile is source-bounded to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-874-zhenjing-muzi-xiangcan-tianli-nanrong-lpr3eglbnwbve1clrttapw6k5zdq|Hanji 874]]. He is presented as an expert in 占候 / astrological prediction whose anticipated amnesty and son's murder become the immediate criminal trigger for [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]]'s enforcement action and the eunuch-backed first Party Prohibition accusation.

## Current Profile

Hanji 874 makes Zhang Cheng important less as a full biographical subject than as a hinge between omen knowledge, legal manipulation, and court retaliation. He predicts that a general amnesty is imminent and lets his son commit murder under that expectation. Li Ying, serving in the capital enforcement field, treats the case as calculated abuse rather than ordinary pardon-covered crime and executes Zhang Cheng father and son despite the amnesty.

Zhang Cheng's political danger comes from his access ties. The source says he has eunuch backing and had been consulted by [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]], so Li Ying's execution creates a grievance that can travel through inner-court channels. Zhang Cheng's disciple [[LaoXiuLateHan|劳修]] then accuses Li Ying and other士人 of mutual praise, party formation, court slander, and disordering customs, turning one amnesty-gaming murder case into the first Party Prohibition arrest field.

## Key Characteristics

- Henan yin-associated late-Han figure skilled in 占候 / astrological prediction.
- Actor who treats imminent amnesty as a shield for deliberate crime.
- Criminal trigger for Li Ying's assertion that calculated abuse should not be protected by formal pardon.
- Eunuch-connected figure whose death can be converted into political retaliation.
- Upstream source of Lao Xiu's accusation channel against Li Ying and other士人.
