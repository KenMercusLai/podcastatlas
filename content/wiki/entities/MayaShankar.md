# Maya Shankar

## Overview
Maya Shankar is a researcher credited in the goal-setting episode with the “middle problem” framing for motivation that falls between a salient beginning and endpoint.

## Current Profile
In [[goals-toolkit-how-to-set-achieve-your-goals-scim1532399364]], Shankar's contribution is used to explain why motivation can sag in the middle of a work session, week, or 12-week cycle. The practical response is to acknowledge the pattern and divide the middle into smaller consecutive chunks rather than interpret it automatically as evidence that the goal is wrong.

## Key Characteristics
- Supplies a temporal account of changing motivation during pursuit.
- Connects the same middle-stage pattern across sessions, weeks, and longer cycles.
- Supports chunking as a practical response to lower-salience middle periods.

## Evidence
- Middle-problem attribution - [[goals-toolkit-how-to-set-achieve-your-goals-scim1532399364]] credits Shankar for the pattern and its application across several goal timescales.

## Qualifications
The supplied note does not give a study citation, sample, effect size, or boundary conditions. Chunking is a practical response in this source, not proof that every motivation dip has the same cause.
