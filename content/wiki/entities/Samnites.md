# Samnites / 萨莫奈人

## Overview

The Samnites / 萨莫奈人 are an Italian people whose long conflict with the [[RomanRepublic]], coerced incorporation into its alliance system, and special hostility to [[LuciusCorneliusSulla]] connect Rome's conquest of Italy to the final anti-Sullan army defeated at the Colline Gate.

## Current Profile

Episode 423 places the Samnites much earlier in Rome's conquest of Italy. A major war beginning in 326 BC includes Rome's humiliation at the Caudine Forks, rejection of the resulting settlement, and continued conflict until Samnite defeat. Roman colonies are then planted through their territory and the Samnites become allies, an example of coercion and incorporation operating together.

Episode 702 places the Samnites inside the later [[SocialWar]] and Sulla's rise. They are based in the Apennines east of the Bay of Naples, suffer heavily from Sulla's reprisals, and remain associated with Italian resistance to Rome's unequal citizenship order.

Episode 704 turns that background into the last military crisis of the Sullan civil war. Because Sulla had been especially brutal against them, the Samnites become a major anti-Sullan reserve. They first try to relieve [[GaiusMariusYounger]] at Praeneste, then see Rome itself as undefended and march on the Colline Gate. Their commander is presented as wanting to destroy Rome, not simply change the government. Sulla and [[MarcusLiciniusCrassus]] defeat them after a night battle, and the next day Sulla massacres thousands of prisoners at the Villa Publica.

## Key Characteristics

- The Samnites are an Italian military and regional actor rather than a Roman faction.
- Their forced alliance follows prolonged war, Roman humiliation, renewed fighting, and colonization.
- Their hostility to Sulla is grounded in Social War violence and remembered Roman-Samnite conflict.
- They supply the final anti-Sullan field army in Italy.
- Their attack on Rome raises the stakes from factional control to possible destruction of the city.
- Their defeat at the Colline Gate enables Sulla's terror settlement.

## Qualifications

The current synthesis follows three narrative podcast episodes rather than a full Samnite history. The early chronology, Caudine settlement, Roman response, colony sequence, political terms, later reported speeches, casualty figures, and all Samnite aims remain source-scoped. Roman allied status should not be mistaken for consensual integration.

## What Changed

- Extended the profile back to the Samnite Wars, Caudine Forks, Roman colonies, and coerced alliance.
- Connected the later Social War and Colline Gate hostility to an older history of conquest rather than only Sulla's personal reprisals.

## Relationships

- [[SocialWar]] - recent Italian conflict that shapes Samnite hostility to Rome and Sulla.
- [[LuciusCorneliusSulla]] - Roman commander whose brutality makes him the Samnites' direct enemy.
- [[GaiusMariusYounger]] - Marian figure initially aided by the Samnite relief attempt.
- [[MarcusLiciniusCrassus]] - Sullan ally whose wing helps decide the Colline Gate battle.
- [[RomanRepublic]] - Roman polity threatened by Samnite attack and then reshaped by Sulla's victory.
- [[RepublicanEmergencyViolencePrecedent]] - violence pattern extended by Sulla's massacre of Samnite prisoners.
- [[RomanCivicMilitaryIncorporation]] - system that turns Samnite defeat into allied manpower without erasing inequality or resistance.
