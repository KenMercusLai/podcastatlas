# Samnites / 萨莫奈人

## Overview

The Samnites / 萨莫奈人 are an Italian people whose conflict with Rome and special hostility to [[LuciusCorneliusSulla]] make them the final anti-Sullan army defeated at the Colline Gate.

## Current Profile

Episode 702 places the Samnites inside the [[SocialWar]] and Sulla's rise. They are based in the Apennines east of the Bay of Naples, suffer heavily from Sulla's reprisals, and remain associated with Italian resistance to Rome's citizenship order.

Episode 704 turns that background into the last military crisis of the Sullan civil war. Because Sulla had been especially brutal against them, the Samnites become a major anti-Sullan reserve. They first try to relieve [[GaiusMariusYounger]] at Praeneste, then see Rome itself as undefended and march on the Colline Gate. Their commander is presented as wanting to destroy Rome, not simply change the government. Sulla and [[MarcusLiciniusCrassus]] defeat them after a night battle, and the next day Sulla massacres thousands of prisoners at the Villa Publica.

## Key Characteristics

- The Samnites are an Italian military and regional actor rather than a Roman faction.
- Their hostility to Sulla is grounded in Social War violence and older Roman-Samnite conflict.
- They supply the final anti-Sullan field army in Italy.
- Their attack on Rome raises the stakes from factional control to possible destruction of the city.
- Their defeat at the Colline Gate enables Sulla's terror settlement.

## Qualifications

The current synthesis follows the Roman Civil War episodes. It does not separately reconstruct the earlier Samnite Wars or all Samnite political aims beyond the episode's claim that their final commander framed Rome itself as the enemy.

## What Changed

- Created the Samnites as the Italian people whose Social War hostility to Sulla becomes the last military threat at the Colline Gate.

- [[MarcusLiciniusCrassus]] - Sullan ally whose wing helps decide the Colline Gate battle.
