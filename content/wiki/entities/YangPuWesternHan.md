## Overview

杨仆 / Yang Pu is the Western Han tower-ship general whose weakened force and separate surrender channel collide with [[XunZhiWesternHan|荀彘]]'s more aggressive command during the campaign against [[WimanJoseon|卫氏朝鲜]].

## Current Profile

Yang Pu's troops have already suffered defeat, and the episode presents him as repeatedly missing agreed coordination dates while trying to secure Joseon's surrender through his own channel. [[GongsunSuiWesternHan|公孙遂]] summons him under imperial authority, has him arrested in Xun Zhi's camp, and transfers his troops to the left general. After the conquest, Yang Pu is held responsible for advancing without coordination and losing many soldiers; he avoids execution by redemption and becomes a commoner.

## Key Characteristics

- Tower-ship general leading one branch of the Han attack on Wiman Joseon.
- Commander of troops whose prior defeat reduces confidence and appetite for renewed attack.
- Holder of a separate surrender channel that conflicts with Xun Zhi's timetable and suspicions.
- General arrested through an imperial-tally summons and stripped of command.
- Postwar defendant who redeems a death sentence and loses official status.

- Divergent operational posture: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-366-2-hanwudi-henxin-zhansha-gongsunsui-ls1izjhow_j4ubgmm0lm8ruaurox|Hanji 366-2]] says Yang Pu does not meet repeated joint-attack dates and maintains a surrender channel with Joseon.
- Arrest and dispossession: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-366-2-hanwudi-henxin-zhansha-gongsunsui-ls1izjhow_j4ubgmm0lm8ruaurox|Hanji 366-2]] says he is summoned into Xun Zhi's camp, arrested, and deprived of his army.
- Punishment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-366-2-hanwudi-henxin-zhansha-gongsunsui-ls1izjhow_j4ubgmm0lm8ruaurox|Hanji 366-2]] says his unauthorized advance and heavy losses merit death, but redemption reduces him to commoner status.

## Qualifications

The episode does not make caution equivalent to treason. Yang Pu's separate diplomacy, missed coordination, and prior losses create operational risk, but the page does not adopt Xun Zhi's fear of a larger plot as proven. His broader career and earlier campaigns are outside this bounded source.

## What Changed

- Established Yang Pu's Joseon-campaign profile from separate diplomacy through arrest and postwar demotion.
