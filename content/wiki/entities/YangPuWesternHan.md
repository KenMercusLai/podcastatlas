## Overview

杨仆 / Yang Pu is the Western Han tower-ship general whose profile runs from selection for the conquest of [[Nanyue|南越]] through victory, alleged credit inflation, renewed use against Dongyue, a boundary proposal, command fracture in Wiman Joseon, and eventual punishment. His recurring career makes him a test of how [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] combines military usefulness with accountability.

## Current Profile

Hanji 352-2 now supplies the campaign opening. After [[HanQianqiuWesternHan|韩千秋]]'s force is destroyed and Wudi mobilizes convicts and southern naval forces, Yang Pu is selected as tower-ship general alongside [[LuBodeWesternHan|伏波将军路博德]]. The title and force composition suggest a waterborne role, but the episode stops before giving his route, conduct, or result.

Hanji 356-1 supplies the retrospective southern-campaign layer. After service against Nanyue, Yang Pu is accused of treating surrenderers and exhumed bodies as battlefield results, failing to pursue completely, boasting at home, lingering after recall, and failing to report or explain himself promptly. Wudi rebukes him, Yang Pu offers to fight to redeem the faults, and the emperor continues using him against [[YuShanDongyueKing|东越王余善]]. The sequence couples discipline with redeployment rather than either unconditional reward or immediate removal.

Hanji 356-2 adds a territorial-administration proposal. It attributes to Yang Pu the idea of moving [[HanguPass|函谷关]] east to Xin'an and reads the move as serving both his desire for protected inner-zone status and Wudi's desire to enlarge the directly supervised core. The confident account of private bargaining remains an interpretation.

Hanji 366-1 and 366-2 show that command problems recur during the campaign against [[WimanJoseon|卫氏朝鲜]]. Yang Pu advances with seven thousand Qi troops, is defeated, hides in the mountains, and gradually regathers survivors. At Wangxian City he favors a surrender channel after the loss, while [[XunZhiWesternHan|荀彘]] favors assault; missed joint dates and mutual suspicion culminate in [[GongsunSuiWesternHan|公孙遂]] arresting Yang Pu and transferring his force. After conquest, Yang Pu avoids execution by redemption but becomes a commoner because of unauthorized advance and heavy losses.

## Key Characteristics

- Tower-ship general first introduced as a co-commander in the large Nanyue conquest mobilization.
- High-merit commander whose achievement does not prevent scrutiny of credit, reporting, pursuit, and obedience.
- Officer repeatedly allowed to seek redemption through further service or legal commutation.
- Commander whose early Wiman Joseon defeat helps explain a later preference for negotiated surrender.
- Participant in fragmented field command whose separate channel and missed timing deepen distrust.
- Proposer, in Hanji 356-2, of moving Hangu Pass eastward to Xin'an.

## Qualifications

Hanji 352-2 establishes Yang Pu's selection but not his route or victory. Hanji 356-1's five accusations and its reading of him as arrogant remain episode-attributed; Hanji 356-2's claim that Wudi was waiting for his pass-relocation suggestion is likewise an inference. Transcripts render his name as 杨仆, 杨溥, or 杨浦; the wiki normalizes them to the received form. The Joseon episodes do not make caution equivalent to treason: premature advance, separate diplomacy, missed coordination, and prior losses create operational risk, but Hanji 366-1 says Xun Zhi lacks evidence for a larger plot.

## What Changed

- Added Yang Pu's initial selection as tower-ship general before his later Nanyue merit and accountability dispute.
- Made the source boundary explicit: command selection is established here, while routes, conquest details, and later alleged faults come from other episodes.
- Preserved redemption as a recurring career pattern that maintains usefulness without erasing accountability.
