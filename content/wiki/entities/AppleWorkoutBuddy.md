# Apple Workout Buddy

Apple Workout Buddy appears in [[tech-20260204-0204-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260204-0204-mp-tech-pod-128]] as [[Apple]]'s AI fitness feature. [[NicoleNguyen]] describes it as basic compared with [[FitbitAIHealthCoach]] and [[Peloton]], but also notes that it does not require an extra fee.
