# Gregory of Tours

## Overview

Gregory of Tours was a sixth-century bishop and historical narrator whose account is central to the surviving story of [[Fredegund]] and [[Brunhilde]] but whose unequal sympathies shape the moral and causal form of that story.

## Current Profile

[[521-warlords-of-the-west-killer-queens-part-2-glt5666966864]] uses Gregory as both indispensable witness and interpretive problem. His narrative preserves assassinations, succession struggles, epidemics, court accusations, clerical conflict, and alleged plots that make the rival queens historically visible. It also presents Fredegund through a strongly hostile frame of cruelty, murder, and witchcraft while treating Brunhilde more favorably.

The episode therefore does not discard Gregory. It separates events and outcomes he helps preserve from causal allegations that remain uncorroborated in the summary. Sigebert's death and Fredegund's political benefit are secure within the narrative; her commission of the murder is an inference. Likewise, repeated accusation can explain a reputation without proving every act attributed to its subject.

## Key Characteristics

- Bishop and near-contemporary narrator of sixth-century Frankish politics.
- Essential source for the feud between Fredegund and Brunhilde.
- Preserver of court, clerical, epidemiological, dynastic, and violent detail.
- Openly hostile interpreter of Fredegund.
- Comparatively sympathetic narrator of Brunhilde.
- Example of why source proximity and source neutrality must not be conflated.

## Evidence

- Narrative centrality - [[521-warlords-of-the-west-killer-queens-part-2-glt5666966864]] frames the entire rivalry through stories and judgments attributed to Gregory.
- Asymmetrical characterization - [[521-warlords-of-the-west-killer-queens-part-2-glt5666966864]] explicitly contrasts his hostility toward Fredegund with his greater sympathy for Brunhilde.
- Recoverable event versus accusation - [[521-warlords-of-the-west-killer-queens-part-2-glt5666966864]] uses Sigebert's assassination and its political result while qualifying who commissioned it.
- Moral vocabulary - [[521-warlords-of-the-west-killer-queens-part-2-glt5666966864]] shows cruelty and witchcraft allegations helping organize Fredegund's historical reputation.

## Qualifications

This page captures Gregory only as he appears in one episode summary. It is not a full profile of his episcopacy, works, theology, manuscript transmission, political relationships, or reliability across different subjects. Bias does not make all reported events false, and comparative sympathy toward Brunhilde does not make her portrayal neutral.
