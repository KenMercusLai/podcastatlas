# Monday Night Football

Monday Night Football is the prime-time NFL television product in [[the-nfl-1]] created by [[PeteRozelle]] and [[RooneArledge]] with [[ABC]]. The episode presents it as a major example of the league inventing new media inventory rather than only selling existing Sunday games.

The product added a weekly prime-time appointment, higher production values, more cameras, field-level shots, a three-person booth, theme music, and highlight packaging. In the wiki, it anchors [[LiveSportsStreamingTransition]]'s pre-streaming ancestor: the NFL learned to create scarce live windows for each new distribution era.
