## Overview

冯焕 / Feng Huan enters the wiki as a Youzhou inspector who survives a forged-edict revenge plot after his son Feng Gun detects suspicious wording. In Hanji 797, Feng's importance lies less in a long career profile than in showing how local obedience to imperial command can become dangerous when command authenticity is not verified.

## Current Profile

Feng Huan appears alongside [[YaoGuangLateHan|Yao Guang]]. The source says both men had repeatedly exposed bad actors, creating enemies who then forge an imperial command against them. The forged edict orders Liaodong commandery commandant Pang Fen to act.

Pang Fen executes Yao Guang and arrests Feng Huan. Feng Huan considers suicide, but his son Feng Gun notices that the command's wording seems wrong and advises petitioning the court. The court verifies that the command was forged and summons Pang Fen to the capital for punishment.

The episode makes Feng Huan a survival case within the same mechanism that kills Yao Guang. He avoids death not because the system has robust built-in verification, but because a family member questions the document before its authority becomes final.

## Key Characteristics

- Youzhou inspector targeted by enemies after exposing wrongdoing.
- Official arrested through a forged imperial command executed by a local military-administrative officer.
- Near-victim of command obedience before document authenticity is checked.
- Survival case dependent on Feng Gun's textual suspicion and petition strategy.
