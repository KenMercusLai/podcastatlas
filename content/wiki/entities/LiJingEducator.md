# 李晶 / Li Jing (therapeutic educator)

## Overview

李晶 is a former [[Alibaba]] manager and family-education entrepreneur who later studied therapeutic education in Sweden and moved into face-to-face educational work with children with additional needs in Oregon.

## Current Profile

In the source account, Li Jing's career spans retail, consulting, corporate strategy, Alibaba's high-growth years, a short period at [[Airbnb]], family-education entrepreneurship, and therapeutic education. She presents neither the internet period nor the later transition as a simple mistake-and-escape story: extreme work brought skill, responsibility, income, equity, and repeated success, while also narrowing life around company goals and carrying a forceful management style into family relationships.

Her son's school difficulties and her own punitive response became the turning point. Repeated listening led her to connect perfectionism and achievement with a long search for maternal approval, to recognize the fear and hurt created by her parenting, and to change her conduct rather than seek a new technique for controlling the child. She now describes choice as an ongoing calibration among reason, inner response, bodily health, relationships, economic reality, and explicit boundaries.

## Key Characteristics

- Experienced exponential internet growth as an operator and multi-layer team manager.
- Treats high effort as potentially chosen and rewarding while rejecting compulsory presence and boundaryless work.
- Publicly acknowledges having used pressure and punishment with employees and her son.
- Uses listening, emotional awareness, and behavioral change as the basis of relational repair.
- Reoriented entrepreneurship around normal hours and practical alignment between company values and team life.
- Chose lower-paid, face-to-face therapeutic education after concluding that scalable content products conflicted with the depth of help she wanted to provide.
- Frames self-knowledge as repeated action, observation, review, and adjustment rather than discovery of a fixed essence.
