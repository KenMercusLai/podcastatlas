## Overview

繇王居股 / Jugu is the Dongyue royal insider whom Hanji 356-2 credits with joining [[JianchengHouAo|建成侯敖]] to kill [[YuShanDongyueKing|余善]], surrender, and receive Han reward.

## Current Profile

Jugu's bounded importance lies in converting royal proximity into a successful internal overthrow. Together with Ao, he kills Yu Shan after Han pressure and earlier defections have weakened Dongyue cohesion, then brings followers to the Han side. The episode says he is rewarded afterward, showing how conquest can be completed by incorporating an internal rival rather than destroying every local elite.

## Key Characteristics

- Royal insider within the Dongyue political field.
- Co-actor in the killing of Yu Shan.
- Surrender leader whose cooperation receives Han recognition.
