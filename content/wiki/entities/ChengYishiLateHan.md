## Overview
成翊世 / Cheng Yishi is a late-Han official whose current profile is source-bounded to the post-Deng transition in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-793-lishi-zhuming-nvhuang-dengsui-zenme-si-de-lszfgj7waiuusrddzjmv5mbrp1i|Hanji 793]]. He appears as a commandery official punished for urging [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]] to return power to [[EmperorAnOfHan|Emperor An]], then restored once An begins personal rule.

## Current Profile
The episode groups Cheng Yishi with [[DuGenLateHan|Du Gen]] as a return-power remonstrant, but his biography is thinner. Cheng is described as a commandery-level official who advised Empress Dowager Deng to return power to the adult emperor and was punished for that stance. After Deng's death, Chen Zhong recommends men of integrity, Emperor An accepts, and Cheng Yishi is summoned with Du Gen to the gongche channel.

Cheng's appointment as shangshulang makes him part of An's first personal-rule personnel signal. The page does not yet treat him as an independent policy actor; the durable point is that the new regime publicly restores a figure previously punished for pressing the regency-transfer issue.

## Key Characteristics
- Return-power remonstrant associated with the demand that Empress Dowager Deng yield governing authority to the adult emperor.
- Commandery official whose punishment places him in the same remonstrance field as Du Gen.
- Post-regency restoration figure summoned after Deng's death when Emperor An begins personal rule.
- Shangshulang appointee whose reuse marks the court's early attempt to distinguish new personal rule from the regency's suppression of transfer demands.

## Qualifications
The current profile rests on one source note and does not establish Cheng Yishi's family, full career, exact punishment, later policy role, or relationship to all other officials restored after Deng's death. Yin Yi is named in the same recommendation list but remains source-scoped because the episode gives no durable profile.
