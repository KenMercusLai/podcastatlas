# Rajiv Kanhaja

## Overview
Rajiv Kanhaja is the co-founder and CEO of [[AdButler]], represented in the wiki through one [[TheSaaSPodcast]] interview about bootstrapped software, enterprise selling, pricing, modular engineering, and AI-generated software.

## Current Profile
Kanhaja's source profile joins long-horizon product accumulation to relationship-oriented enterprise execution. He began with Pollit.com as a teenager, built an ad server when an incumbent was inaccessible, survived the dot-com advertising collapse by emphasizing paid software, and later turned AdButler into the primary business. His operating view favors live product proof, fast engineering response, direct customer access, and sufficient rather than maximal contract extraction. His AI forecast is more speculative: stable capabilities may persist while agents generate and maintain customer-specific software above them.

## Key Characteristics
- Technical founder who built the initial ad-serving system in response to an unavailable and expensive incumbent.
- Long-horizon bootstrapper who let a side product accumulate customer-requested depth before deliberately pursuing the enterprise market.
- Product-led enterprise seller who relies on demonstrations, implementation credibility, and rapid delivery more than conventional sales theater.
- Relationship-oriented operator who treats retention and customer success as stronger tests than contractual lock-in.
- AI-software maximalist who expects cheap generation to shift value from fixed interfaces toward stable callable capabilities.

## Evidence
Technical origin and persistence:
- [[b765334b]] traces Pollit.com, the self-built AdButler ad server, the dot-com crash, the paid-software pivot, and more than a decade of incremental product development.

Enterprise and customer practice:
- [[b765334b]] describes Kanhaja completing Costco's RFP without prior enterprise experience, demonstrating the product live, prioritizing the deployment, and extending the model through later inbound accounts.
- [[b765334b]] reports his preference for healthy-margin but relationship-oriented pricing, month-to-month availability, direct engineer access, and delivery ahead of promised dates.

Software architecture thesis:
- [[b765334b]] connects small deployable components to parallel engineering and predicts that agents will build customer-specific interfaces over API or MCP-like platform capabilities.

## Qualifications
The profile is based on one founder interview summarized rather than transcribed in the supplied document. Company scale, customer economics, churn, historical revenue, delivery performance, and acquisition history are not independently verified here. The AI thesis does not address the governance needed to secure, test, audit, upgrade, and support thousands of divergent customer implementations.

## What Changed
- Established Kanhaja as a technical founder whose enterprise model grew from accumulated product depth rather than a planned top-down sales motion.
- Distinguished his evidence-backed operating history from his directional forecast about agent-maintained bespoke software.
