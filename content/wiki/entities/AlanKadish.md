# Alan Kadish

## Overview
Alan Kadish is presented in the bounded source as an [[ApexReplicate]] representative focused on strategy and practical applications for digital expert systems. Alongside [[DrewHarris]], he argues that named expert judgment can be made more available while the human remains responsible for unfamiliar cases.

## Current Profile
Kadish's represented position joins access, monetization, and institutional retention. Professionals could sell lower-cost digital access to their perspective, while enterprises could preserve knowledge from retiring specialists. He also supports a human-first training use: protégés should help newer workers query experienced judgment, with escalation preserving the expert's role when the system reaches its limit.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames digital expert systems as an access and knowledge-retention mechanism.
- Supports both marketplace and enterprise deployment models.
- Distinguishes represented expert judgment from generic model output and task execution.
- Retains human escalation for unknown or consequential cases.
- Treats education and faster development of newer workers as primary opportunities.
