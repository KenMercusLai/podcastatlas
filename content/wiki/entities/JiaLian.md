# 贾琏 / Jia Lian

In [[ritan-chuantai-hongloumeng-haishi-keyi-du-yidu-de-587167638]], 贾琏 is part of the episode's attempt to read [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]] male characters with moral gradation. He is not made innocent, but he is discussed beside [[XuePan|薛蟠]] as someone shaped by environment, habit, and male social norms rather than by pure evil alone.

The comparison matters because [[JiaBaoyu|贾宝玉]] can look relatively clean inside [[GrandViewGarden|大观园]], yet the episode also notes how male talk outside that space can pull him downward. [[JiaYucun|贾雨村]] then marks a more straightforwardly corrupt pole in the same social world.
