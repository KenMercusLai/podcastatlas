# 贾琏 / Jia Lian

## Overview

贾琏 is a morally compromised [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]] man whose social ease and relative gradation inside the male world coexist with infidelity, entitlement, and failure to protect women whose status depends on him.

## Current Profile

The earlier source places Jia Lian in a gray male-character comparison shaped by environment, habit, and patriarchal norms rather than pure evil. The You Sanjie episode makes the consequences more concrete. His secret marriage to [[YouErjie|尤二姐]] does not create a secure boundary at 小花枝巷, and when he encounters 贾珍 pursuing [[YouSanjie|尤三姐]], he attempts to normalize rather than stop the arrangement.

He is also the intermediary who proposes You Sanjie to [[LiuXianglian|柳湘莲]] and obtains a pledge. That helpful act does not cancel the earlier objectification; together they show a man able to facilitate a socially respectable outcome while continuing to participate in the system from which the women need protection.

## Key Characteristics

- Is best read with moral gradation, but gradation does not make his conduct harmless.
- Benefits from male norms that widen his sexual freedom and narrow women's ability to enforce boundaries.
- Gives You Erjie a new nominal position without providing durable protection or public security.
- Joins 贾珍's sexualized treatment of You Sanjie rather than recognizing her as a relative with standing.
- Acts as an effective marriage intermediary between You Sanjie and Liu Xianglian despite his own role in her vulnerability.

### Household status without safety

- [[123-honglou-jinfen-yousanjie-rousui-taohua-hongmandi-yushan-qingdao-zai-nan-fu-ll-xqierm5dlcyr80_dxmlfh97ni]] shows You Sanjie naming the men's treatment of the sisters as recreational use and seizing control of the encounter until they retreat.

### Marriage intermediation

- [[123-honglou-jinfen-yousanjie-rousui-taohua-hongmandi-yushan-qingdao-zai-nan-fu-ll-xqierm5dlcyr80_dxmlfh97ni]] presents him as the person who proposes You Sanjie to Liu Xianglian and receives the 鸳鸯剑 pledge.

## Qualifications

The sources preserve moral gradation but do not supply a comprehensive profile of Jia Lian across the novel. Environment and patriarchal permission explain opportunity and normalization, not absence of choice. His facilitation of the engagement may be helpful without functioning as reparation for the danger he helped create.

## What Changed

- Made the harms concealed by the earlier “gray male world” comparison explicit.
- Distinguished nominal provision for You Erjie from enforceable household protection.
- Added the tension between his sexual entitlement and his useful marriage intermediation.

## Relationships

- [[YouErjie|尤二姐]] - secretly married woman whose new status he does not make secure.
- [[YouSanjie|尤三姐]] - woman he objectifies before later helping arrange her chosen match.
- [[LiuXianglian|柳湘莲]] - prospective husband to whom he proposes You Sanjie.
- [[WangXifeng|王熙凤]] - wife whose delegated household power does not secure marital fidelity or equality.
- [[JiaFamilyHousehold|贾府]] - patriarchal institution that normalizes and enables his latitude.
- [[DomesticAuthorityWithoutSecurity]] - concept exposing the mismatch between household position and women's durable safety.
