# 共尉 / Gong Wei

共尉 / Gong Wei enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-162-2-liubang-ruhe-shoushi-wei-gao-quan-zhong-de-xiashu-lqvye2zmqhu3otk0uwu7lakf2prz|Hanji 162-2]] as the son and successor of [[GongAo|共敖]], the Linjiang king under the Western Chu order. The episode mentions him briefly during the seventh-month transition after the Xingyang and Chenggao crisis, alongside the turn toward the [[QiState|齐国]] front.

The page's role is mainly chronological. Gong Ao's death and Gong Wei's succession show that the Chu-Han war is unfolding inside a still-active landscape of regional kings created by [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s post-Qin settlement.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-169-pandian-liubang-dengjiqian-buweirenzhi-de-gushi-1-liwkaygjtyafxxpdwzq9pdhuzroi|Hanji 169]] closes that branch after Xiang Yu's death. Gong Wei refuses to submit to [[LiuBang|刘邦]], so [[LuWan|卢绾]] and [[LiuJia|刘贾]] besiege Jiangling for months before Han reinforcements help take the city and capture him. Linjiang therefore becomes one of the last regional holdouts folded into Liu Bang's [[BattlefieldVictoryToFoundingOrder|战场胜利到建国秩序]].
