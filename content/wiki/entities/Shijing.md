# 《诗经》 / Shijing

《诗经》 / Shijing enters this wiki branch through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]], where the episode cites "十月之交" and the line "日有蚀之" to explain why solar eclipses were politically serious in ancient China.

The page is source-scoped. It does not summarize the whole Classic of Poetry; it records the episode's use of a Shijing eclipse notice as evidence for [[CelestialOmenPoliticalResponsibility]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl]] adds a second citation route: [[Zisi|子思]] quotes a Shijing line to criticize a court where everyone claims wisdom and no one can distinguish better counsel from worse. The source uses the citation for [[CourtFeedbackCollapse]] rather than omen politics.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-4-lhjz-m12pedkfszxyzgqtjnpsn3p]] adds a much more operational use. In the episode, [[ZhaoCangtang|赵苍唐]] uses Shijing references to communicate the future [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]]'s longing and grievance to [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] without blunt accusation, and Wei Wenhou's own response depends on the son recognizing a Shijing-coded summons. The source therefore turns Shijing into [[PoetryAsElitePoliticalLanguage|elite political language]]: a shared repertoire that lets status, emotion, and command move indirectly.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-124-fenshu-kengru-wo-zai-bzhan-wei-qinshihuang-pingfan-ljnhwcouqu35rmvb-cnrzyzj2j6l]] adds the other side of that political power. Under [[LiSi|李斯]]'s [[QinBookBurningPolicy|秦代焚书令]], private possession and discussion of Shijing become dangerous because the classic can support non-Qin standards for judging the present. The episode therefore turns Shijing from elite communication resource into a target of [[OfficialLearningMonopoly|official learning control]].
