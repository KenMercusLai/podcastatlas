# 《诗经》 / Shijing

《诗经》 / Shijing enters this wiki branch through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]], where the episode cites "十月之交" and the line "日有蚀之" to explain why solar eclipses were politically serious in ancient China.

The page is source-scoped. It does not summarize the whole Classic of Poetry; it records the episode's use of a Shijing eclipse notice as evidence for [[CelestialOmenPoliticalResponsibility]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl]] adds a second citation route: [[Zisi|子思]] quotes a Shijing line to criticize a court where everyone claims wisdom and no one can distinguish better counsel from worse. The source uses the citation for [[CourtFeedbackCollapse]] rather than omen politics.
