# Bryan Johnson

Bryan Johnson is the guest in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bryan-johnson-i-just-took-the-most-powerful-dose-of-dmt-in-the-world-heres-what-it-was-like-40626165]], where [[AllIn|All-In]] presents him as a founder-turned-longevity self-experimenter. The source connects his post-[[Braintree]] and [[Venmo]] period, divorce, and exit from the Mormon Church to later therapeutic and quantified experimentation.

His main wiki role in this source is as the high-measurement edge of [[TechCultureBiohacking]]. He describes [[5MeODMT|5-MeO-DMT]] as the most powerful psychedelic he has taken, says earlier [[PsilocybinMushrooms|psilocybin]] experiments were measured through [[KernelCompany|Kernel]] and imaging tools, and treats the brain as the hardest organ to rejuvenate. The source keeps his strongest 5-MeO claims source-scoped because the objective results were not yet available.

Johnson also pushes the episode beyond psychedelics into [[MitochondrialTherapy|mitochondrial therapy]], [[PatientDerivedOrganoids|patient-derived organoids]], gene and cell therapies, [[NewLimit]], and [[CellularReprogrammingControlProblem|cellular reprogramming control risk]]. His self-description is unusually open-ended: when asked about limits on future enhancement or reprogramming, he says he does not have a fixed one.
