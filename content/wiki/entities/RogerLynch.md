# Roger Lynch

## Overview
Roger Lynch is presented as Mattel's new chief executive, bringing media and entertainment experience to the toy company's attempt to build a broader brand-and-content business.

## Current Profile
The episode frames Lynch's appointment as a strategic signal. His prior leadership at Condé Nast makes him an outsider to conventional toy manufacturing but a plausible steward for films, games, experiences, and theme parks. His test is whether Mattel can turn *Barbie* from an exceptional hit into a repeatable capability across brands.

## Key Characteristics
- Chief executive of [[Mattel]] in the source's account.
- Former Condé Nast leader with media and entertainment experience.
- Attracted by Mattel's portfolio of recognizable intellectual property.
- Charged with moving the company beyond conventional toy manufacturing.
- Will be judged on repeatable execution rather than one blockbuster.
