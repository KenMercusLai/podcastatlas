# 赵元任

赵元任 enters the wiki through [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] as a polymath whose language work belongs to the same fieldwork turn as geology, architecture, and archaeology. The episode describes him through science-society work, musical and mathematical ability, translation for Russell's China visit, and an unusual capacity to learn and reproduce local speech.

His central role in the source is dialect research. Zhao and assistants such as 杨时逢 travel south with recording equipment to capture speech samples, while also participating in debates over national language unification. The episode stresses the tension: he helped with Mandarin standardization while also treating dialect diversity as something worth documenting before war and institutional disruption destroyed the conditions for systematic survey.

## Key Claims
- Zhao Yuanren expands [[FieldworkAsKnowledgeMethod]] from geology and archaeology into linguistics.
- His work joins modern technology, trained listening, local speech communities, and institutional support.
- The source treats language diversity as knowledge, not as a defect to be erased by national standardization.
- Wartime loss of books and laboratory stability makes his branch a case of [[ScholarshipInWartime]].
