# MadeGood

MadeGood is the organic snack brand built by [[NimaFotovat]], [[SalmaFotovat]], and [[SabaFotovat]] in [[madegood-salma-and-nima-fotovat-lost-their-first-business-they-grew-their-next-one-into-a-snack-giant-cf5715bf-e9e0-45e0-a8a9-1256a7a4ed0d]]. The episode presents the brand as a second act after the family's loss of [[TasteOfNature]], with growth driven by school-safe snacking, owned manufacturing, standout packaging, and retail execution through [[Loblaw]], [[WholeFoodsMarket]], and [[Costco]].

## Key Points
- The first MadeGood product was granola bites rather than bars, even though the family already knew bar manufacturing.
- The brand targeted an intersection of organic ingredients, allergen-safe school rules, parental trust, child taste, and broader family snacking.
- The team led with taste and visible shelf presence rather than making allergen-free status the only brand message.
- [[RiversideNaturalFoods]] chose vertical manufacturing to control allergen-free claims, costs, and product development.
- Local Toronto-area work, demos, merchandising, and farmers markets supplied [[LocalMarketProof]] before MadeGood expanded across Canada and into the United States.
- The episode says MadeGood later sold in more than 40,000 stores and reached sales in the hundreds of millions of dollars.
