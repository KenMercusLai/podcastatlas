# 子顺 / Zi Shun

子顺 / Zi Shun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-101-1-zhe-wei-daomei-junwang-dengji-santian-ji-cusi-lll-bcc7basaf6-nzwqtmcb5qw6w]] in a discussion with [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] about whether the world has genuine high-minded士. When Wei Anxi Wang says [[LuZhonglian|鲁仲连]] seems too performative, Zi Shun answers that people are all somewhat performative; if they keep practicing that way, they can become gentlemen.

The episode uses Zi Shun less as an independent biographical subject than as a voice for [[VirtuePracticeInternalization|修养内化]]. His point converts "做作" from simple hypocrisy into a developmental possibility: repeated conduct can become habit, and habit can become character.
