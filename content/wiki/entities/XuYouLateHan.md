# 许攸 / Xu You (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-941-shenchu-guanchang-ta-jing-xiang-ganfan-huangdi-liu-fqiz9pi7u6cltrn-cjmjz-r|Hanji 941]] adds an earlier Xu You branch before his later Yuan Shao adviser role. In this source he appears with [[WangFenLateHan|王芬]] and [[ChenYiLateHan|陈逸]] around the claim that celestial signs suggest trouble for the eunuchs, helping open the failed 188 CE coup discussion.

The episode does not make Xu You the operational leader. His role is source-scoped to the conspiratorial circle whose anti-eunuch desire escalates into a plan to seize [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] and install [[HefeiMarquisLateHan|合肥侯]].

许攸 / Xu You (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] as part of [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s powerful adviser group. The episode names Xu You with [[JuShou|沮授]] and [[TianFengLateHan|田丰]] to make a leadership point: an impressive adviser roster does not become state capacity if the ruler cannot select, trust, and execute usable advice.

The source does not narrate Xu You's full career or the later Guandu defection story. In this ingest, Xu You's role remains source-scoped to Yuan Shao's underconverted Hebei adviser system and the broader [[TalentAsStateTreasure|以贤臣为宝]] problem.
