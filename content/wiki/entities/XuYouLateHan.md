# 许攸 / Xu You (late Han)

## Overview
许攸 / Xu You is a late-Han political operator whose bounded wiki profile now runs from a failed anti-eunuch coup circle to [[YuanShao|Yuan Shao]]'s underconverted adviser bench, then to the [[GuanduCampaign|Guandu]] defection that gives [[CaoCao|Cao Cao]] decisive [[WuchaoRaidLateHan|Wuchao Raid]] intelligence, and finally to a post-[[JizhouLateHan|Jizhou]] death caused by public credit overreach.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-941-shenchu-guanchang-ta-jing-xiang-ganfan-huangdi-liu-fqiz9pi7u6cltrn-cjmjz-r|Hanji 941]] supplies an early Xu You branch before his Yuan Shao adviser role. He appears with [[WangFenLateHan|Wang Fen]] and [[ChenYiLateHan|Chen Yi]] around a claim that celestial signs suggest trouble for the eunuchs, helping open the failed 188 CE plan to seize [[EmperorLingOfHan|Emperor Ling]] and install [[HefeiMarquisLateHan|the Hefei Marquis]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] places him in Yuan Shao's powerful adviser group with [[JuShou|Ju Shou]] and [[TianFengLateHan|Tian Feng]]. In that source, Xu You is less an independent protagonist than part of the evidence that an impressive adviser roster does not become state capacity unless the ruler can select, trust, and execute usable advice.

At Guandu, Xu You's defection is now bounded by both motive trigger and operational payoff. Before leaving Yuan Shao, he proposes a light night raid on [[Xudu|许都]] to force Cao Cao into divided attention, but Yuan rejects the plan. After [[ShenPeiLateHan|审配]] arrests Xu You's family at [[YeLateHan|邺城]], Xu You goes over to Cao Cao and forces a frank discussion of Cao's grain shortage before identifying lightly guarded Yuan supplies at Gushi and Wuchao.

The later Wuchao source keeps Xu You's intelligence under trust risk. Cao Cao faces depleted supplies and cannot be sure Xu You's information is not a trap, but he still uses the Wuchao claim to launch the decisive raid. Xu You's post-Guandu fate then reverses the value of that contribution. After Cao Cao takes Jizhou, Xu You repeatedly boasts before others that Cao Cao would not have obtained the province without him, even calling him "曹阿瞒." The bounded sources therefore present Xu You as both a decisive intelligence broker and a credit-overreach case: real service becomes dangerous when it is performed publicly as superiority over the ruler who controls recognition and punishment.

## Key Characteristics
- Adviser and plot participant whose early source branch is tied to failed anti-eunuch coup politics.
- Member of Yuan Shao's adviser-rich Hebei camp in the leadership-capacity critique.
- Adviser whose rejected Xudu raid proposal shows he can identify Cao Cao's political-military vulnerability.
- Defector-intelligence broker at Guandu, where information about Gushi and Wuchao becomes militarily decisive.
- Family-pressure defector whose move to Cao follows Shen Pei's arrest of his household.
- Trust-risk and post-merit overreach figure: Cao Cao first acts on his potentially trap-like intelligence, then later reads his public boasting as an authority challenge.
- Naming-normalization case, because Hanji 1049's transcript uses "许由" while the wiki keeps the established [[XuYouLateHan|Xu You]] route.

## Qualifications
This page remains source-scoped and does not supply a full Xu You biography outside the bounded episodes. Hanji 1048 gives the family-arrest trigger, but the family offense and Xu You's inner motive remain source-scoped. Hanji 1064 supplies a post-Guandu death narrative and workplace analogy, but Cao Cao's exact legal pretext and the full chronology of Xu You's conduct are not developed here. The Hanji 1049 source uses the wording "许由"; because the role, Guandu context, and Wuchao intelligence match the received Xu You/许攸 branch, the wiki normalizes that form to this page rather than creating a separate entity.
