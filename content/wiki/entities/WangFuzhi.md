# 王夫之 / Wang Fuzhi

王夫之 / Wang Fuzhi enters this wiki branch through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1027-jingzhou-zhizhu-liubiao-weihe-nan-cheng-bazhu-ll-ebguqllsf-5ycvpdogihzn3lj|Hanji 1027]], where the host cites his negative assessment of [[LiuBiao|刘表]]. In the source's use, Wang Fuzhi helps sharpen the distinction between genuinely preserving [[Jingzhou|荆州]] and merely occupying a regional buffer until stronger powers such as [[CaoCao|曹操]] and the Sun side contest it.

The page preserves that role as source-scoped commentary rather than a full intellectual biography. Wang Fuzhi functions here as a later evaluator whose judgment supports [[FenceSittingStrategicExposure|骑墙式战略暴露]] and qualifies Liu Biao's earlier [[DefensiveLocalGovernance|保境安民式守成治理]].
