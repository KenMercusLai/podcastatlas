# Alice Weidel

## Overview

Alice Weidel is co-leader of [[AlternativeForGermany|AfD]] and the interview subject who articulates the party's national governing ambitions across economic, European, defence, migration, and social policy.

## Current Profile

Weidel presents herself as a libertarian conservative and portrays [[Germany]] as facing a systemic crisis caused by costly energy, nuclear shutdowns, bureaucracy, taxation, debt, migration, and weak national prioritization. Her program combines restored nuclear power, tax and spending cuts, pension reform, a looser [[EuropeanUnion|EU]], greater German defence capacity, an end to support for [[Ukraine]], renewed Russian energy access, and large-scale refugee return.

The interview also shows how her positions change under specificity. She leaves euro withdrawal unresolved if EU reform fails, does not demonstrate that proposed savings cover tax and pension promises, broadens remigration beyond people already required to leave, and joins personal lifestyle freedom to party support for traditional social norms. She rejects extremist characterizations and the [[BrandmauerCordonSanitaire|firewall]], but says AfD will not compromise on the central policies discussed.

## Key Characteristics

- AfD co-leader presenting a national rather than only state-level governing program.
- Self-described libertarian conservative who supports market reform, nuclear energy, and lower taxes and spending.
- Advocate of a looser sovereign-state EU while remaining ambiguous about the euro if reform fails.
- Supporter of stronger German defence who opposes continued German military and financial aid to Ukraine.
- Proponent of remigration whose concrete categories extend to Syrians, Afghans, and postwar Ukrainians.
- Skilled-migration supporter who distinguishes qualified work migrants from asylum populations she wants returned.
- Coalition challenger who rejects the firewall while declining compromise on core policies.

## Evidence

- Economic and European program: [[an-interview-with-alice-weidel-027be4d01e889c07f016e332ffe9a77e]] records Weidel's case for nuclear restoration, deregulation, tax and spending cuts, pension reform, and an EU of sovereign states.
- Defence and Ukraine: [[an-interview-with-alice-weidel-027be4d01e889c07f016e332ffe9a77e]] records her support for German military capability alongside opposition to Ukraine aid and support for immediate talks and renewed Russian energy access.
- Migration and integration: [[an-interview-with-alice-weidel-027be4d01e889c07f016e332ffe9a77e]] records her broad refugee-return categories while preserving her stated welcome for qualified, law-abiding, integrated foreign workers.
- Social and coalition posture: [[an-interview-with-alice-weidel-027be4d01e889c07f016e332ffe9a77e]] records the tension between personal libertarianism and traditional party norms, her rejection of extremist accusations, and her refusal to compromise on core policies.

## Qualifications

This profile derives from one adversarial interview and records stated positions rather than verified effects or enacted policy. Economic, fiscal, migration, crime, war, polling, historical, and party-character claims remain source-scoped. The interview identifies ambiguities and internal tensions but does not establish Weidel's private motives or a complete AfD platform.
