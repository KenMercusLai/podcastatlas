# Ben Wilcox

## Overview
Ben Wilcox is identified in the source as CTO and CISO of [[Proarc|ProArch]], with experience spanning software engineering, enterprise architecture, cloud strategy, and cybersecurity.

## Current Profile
Wilcox's source-scoped operating position is that enterprise AI should move quickly inside explicit risk boundaries. Holding technology and security responsibilities together lets him examine innovation, identity, permissions, tools, data paths, and failure modes as one system. His practical emphasis is on minimizing rather than pretending to eliminate risk, keeping deterministic enforcement outside probabilistic models, and designing controls early enough that security provides a usable deployment path.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines CTO and CISO perspectives on enterprise AI opportunity and cybersecurity risk.
- Treats non-human identity and delegated authority as foundational agent-security questions.
- Favors least-capability connectors and continuing review of changing MCP servers.
- Defines secure by design as control across the full agent workflow rather than a pre-production assessment.
- Frames guardrails as infrastructure for safe speed rather than a blanket restriction on experimentation.

## Qualifications
- The profile comes from one interview summary and does not independently evaluate ProArch's architecture, Wilcox's deployments, or the effectiveness of the controls discussed.
- Combining CTO and CISO authority may reduce handoff friction, but the source does not compare governance structures or address concentration-of-duty risks.
- Racing and highway-barrier analogies communicate risk posture; they are not evidence that a particular control improves deployment speed or safety.
