# Leo Taxil

## Overview

Leo Taxil was the creator of a long-running anti-Masonic hoax alleging devil worship, obscene ritual, and a hidden Palladian network within [[Freemasonry]].

## Current Profile

In the episode, Taxil is a concentrated example of how existing institutional fear can make fabricated insider revelation persuasive. His story succeeded not because it supplied reliable evidence, but because it fitted Catholic anti-Masonic expectations before he publicly disclosed the deception after twelve years.

## Key Characteristics

- He presented lurid claims as revelations about concealed Masonic practice.
- His fabrication exploited an established conflict between the [[CatholicChurch]] and Freemasonry.
- Parts of the Catholic world accepted the story for twelve years according to the episode.
- His eventual confession exposed the account as a hoax without ending the wider anti-Masonic conspiracy tradition.
