# 王婉 / Wang Wan

王婉 appears in [[154-sishisui-ganyan-bu-zuo-nazhi-wenshuili-de-qingwa-lg-qj3p2lq-8sk-rn2vk209tq9x]] as the author of [[PaoWaimai|《跑外卖：一个女骑手的世界》]]. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] uses a detail from the book, going to the riverbank to pick up a stone, as an image for stepping out of life's conveyor belt and recovering attention to concrete life.

The source does not make Wang Wan a general labor-history subject. It uses her book as a practical metaphor inside [[AutonomyUnderInformationFlow]].
