# Janelle Shane

[[tech-20260806-0806-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260806-0806-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds Shane as the [[MarketplaceTech]] guest explaining why chatbots can unexpectedly insert foreign-language words into otherwise fluent English. Her explanation ties multilingual training data, mixed domains, and dialogue formatting to [[ChatbotCodeSwitching]], while her caution about a chatbot's own post-hoc explanation creates [[ChatbotSelfExplanationUncertainty]].

Janelle Shane appears in [[tech-20260722-0722-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260722-0722-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the [[MarketplaceTech]] guest explaining why chatbots can remember random personal facts in socially awkward ways. She describes how large language models can draw on chat history or a separate memory file, and why a saved detail may be treated as something to reuse even when it is incidental.

Her role in the source is to separate memory storage from memory judgment. A chatbot such as [[Claude]] may correctly retain a detail, but still lack human-like proportion, appropriateness, and sensitivity about when to mention it. That makes her explanation central to [[ChatbotMemorySalienceFailure]], [[PersonalAIMemory]], and [[PersistentAgentMemory]].
