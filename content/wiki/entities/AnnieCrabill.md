# Annie Crabill

Annie Crabill is the [[TheIntelligence]] reporter for the [[Greenmarket]] segment in [[a-firm-andy-what-are-new-british-pms-plans-6a5deeca76f8ee25bb4bbf9c]]. She uses [[UnionSquare]] to show how a farmers-market network can become part of [[NewYorkCity|New York City]]'s daily public life, restaurant culture, and regional food system.

Her reporting connects individual market rituals to institutional design. [[GrowNYC]] vets farms, manages market expansion, and supports food-benefit use across vendors, while farmers and chefs use direct relationships to shape what gets grown and cooked.
