# The Alabama Solution

## Overview

The Alabama Solution is a documentary by [[AndrewJarecki]] and [[CharlotteKaufman]] about violence, forced labor, parole, secrecy, and organizing inside [[Alabama]] prisons.

## Current Profile

The film began through limited official access but changed form after incarcerated people warned that managed visits concealed the institution's conditions. Prohibited phones then became a window for live conversation and participant-recorded evidence; Jarecki says calls comprise roughly 30 percent of the finished film.

Its central investigative branch follows [[StephenDavis]]'s death and his mother's attempt to challenge the official account. Its structural branch connects low parole, work compulsion, economic value, and retaliation. The episode presents the participants' recordings, protests, work stoppages, and hunger strikes as part of the film's authorship, not merely its subject matter.

## Key Characteristics

- Built substantially from incarcerated participants' phone recordings and calls.
- Combines a death investigation with a system-level account of prison conditions.
- Treats visibility as a counterweight to secrecy and disbelief.
- Connects forced labor and parole to institutional incentives.
- Keeps participant protection active after production because retaliation risk continues.
