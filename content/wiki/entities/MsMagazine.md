# Ms. Magazine

## Overview
*Ms.* is the national feminist magazine co-founded by [[GloriaSteinem]] and presented in [[drone-truth-russia-has-the-upper-hand-6aad03f98a1508074de8337e]] as a publication owned, managed, and written entirely by women.

## Current Profile
The episode treats *Ms.* as an answer to both editorial exclusion and narrow definitions of legitimate women's subjects. Its covers and articles placed overloaded domestic roles, body hair, pay for housework, abortion, and female power inside a glossy national format.

## Key Characteristics
- Built editorial control around women writers and managers.
- Treated private or stigmatized experience as public subject matter.
- Used popular cultural symbols to communicate feminist arguments.
- Joined journalism with movement-level institution building.
