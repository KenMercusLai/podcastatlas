## Overview

王曼 / Wang Man enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-511-taihou-tianhuaban-ta-ruhe-yitian-feng-wuge-didi-wei-hou-lhoz_ubecvc8-mgfoad2pya72uht|Hanji 511]] as the same-father brother of [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] who had already died before Chengdi's 27 BCE same-day enfeoffment of the other Wang brothers.

## Current Profile

Wang Man is an absence inside the [[ChengdiFiveMarquisesWesternHan|成帝五侯]] story. The episode notes that all of Wang Zhengjun's same-father brothers other than Wang Man receive marquis status, making his prior death the boundary of the group rather than a separate action.

## Key Characteristics

- Same-father brother of Wang Zhengjun.
- Already dead by the 27 BCE enfeoffment.
- Defines the exception to the statement that the other Wang brothers receive marquis status.
