# 孙元化 / Sun Yuanhua

Sun Yuanhua appears in [[ep259-yu-miao-shifu-mantan-xu-guangqi-sibainian-qian-ruhe-rangren-biancongming-lkirczxnx8skbbfpmz5-m-mivs4e]] as [[XuGuangqi|徐光启]]'s student in the episode's military-technology branch. The source says he applied or continued Xu's ideas around artillery and fortification in the Ningyuan and Xingcheng areas.

The source uses Sun less as a biographical subject than as evidence that Xu's learning was meant to travel into practice. Firearms, city-wall placement, multi-directional defensive platforms, Portuguese-made cannon, and training of artillery personnel make [[LateMingWesternLearning|late-Ming Western learning]] a military-operational issue as well as a mathematical or calendar issue.

Sun therefore connects [[EuclideanProofTraining|reasoning discipline]] to statecraft: the episode's point is that new knowledge became valuable when it could reshape calculation, farming, defense, and astronomical administration together.
