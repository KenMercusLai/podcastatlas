# Italian Fascism / 意大利法西斯主义

Italian Fascism / 意大利法西斯主义 is the childhood political environment in [[77-ta-shi-ziyou-de-yegou-ta-shi-falaqi-685710187]]. The episode says [[OrianaFallaci|Oriana Fallaci]] was born in 1929 Florence under Mussolini's rule, grew up in an anti-authoritarian family, encountered fascist school instruction, and learned through underground newspapers that official education could be contradicted by people trying to tell the truth.

The source uses Italian fascism as Fallaci's first lesson in [[PowerMythDeflation]]. As a child, she sees promoted leaders as ordinary rather than sacred, and her family treats fascist loyalty as a moral and political injury. That formation helps explain why later rulers, revolutionaries, religious leaders, and institutions all appear to her as possible sources of domination rather than as natural objects of reverence.
