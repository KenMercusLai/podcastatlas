# Socrates (Footballer)

## Overview

Socrates the footballer appears in the episode as a Brazilian sporting figure associated with [[DiretasJa]] democratic protest, distinct from the existing philosopher page [[Socrates]].

## Current Profile

The episode mentions Socrates as part of the cultural and sporting constellation around Diretas Ja. His role is source-scoped: he helps show that the movement was not only party politics but also a public culture in which football, mass rallies, and the [[BrazilianNationalAnthem]] helped stage democratic aspiration.

## Key Characteristics

- Brazilian footballer, disambiguated from [[Socrates]] the philosopher.
- Associated by the episode with the Diretas Ja movement.
- Part of the sporting-culture layer of Brazil's democratic-election demand.
- Helps connect anthem politics to football and mass public identity.
