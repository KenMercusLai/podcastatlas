# 欧阳修 / Ouyang Xiu

## Overview

Ouyang Xiu was a Northern Song writer, official, examiner, mentor, and historian whose influence joined literary recognition, official selection, and later historical judgment.

## Current Profile

The sources place Ouyang Xiu at two institutional gates. As a historian, his [[NewFiveDynastiesHistory|《新五代史》]] helped stabilize the later category and moral memory of the [[FiveDynastiesTenKingdoms|Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms]]. As a senior literary official and chief examiner associated with the celebrated 1057 cohort, he helped recognize writers and future officials including [[SuShi|苏轼]], [[SuZhe|苏辙]], and figures later divided by political judgment such as [[WangAnshi|王安石]].

This combination makes him more than a factional label. He is a bridge between historical canon formation and talent selection: literary judgment could open official life, while historiographical judgment could shape how earlier political lives were remembered.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior Northern Song literary official whose judgment could convert writing talent into official opportunity.
- Chief examiner associated with the unusually prominent 1057 examination cohort.
- Early recognizer of figures later separated by political and governing differences, including Su Shi and Wang Anshi.
- Historian whose *New History of the Five Dynasties* shaped period naming and moral evaluation.
- Institutional bridge among literature, examination selection, mentorship, official service, and historical memory.

## Evidence

- Literary recognition and official entry: [[105-su-dongpo-yiqiang-haomai-zhiqi-yi-dupi-buhe-shiyi-741072645]] presents Ouyang Xiu's recognition of Su Shi and Su Zhe as an entry point into Northern Song official-literary life.
- Examination role and cohort significance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-341-6-yige-lishi-bozhu-gei-jinnian-kaosheng-bixu-shuode-hua-lg2_qim2_yhkdgrl-qidz11bvlvp]] identifies Ouyang Xiu as chief examiner for the 1057 cohort and uses that cohort to separate examination rank from later prominence.
- Historiographical influence: [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]] connects Ouyang Xiu and the *New History of the Five Dynasties* to the formation of the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms category and later moral judgment of [[FengDao|冯道]].
- Cross-faction reach: [[105-su-dongpo-yiqiang-haomai-zhiqi-yi-dupi-buhe-shiyi-741072645]] notes his appreciation of both Su Shi and Wang Anshi before their governing styles and political judgments diverged.

## Qualifications

The sources are public-history and literary-biographical summaries rather than a complete study of Ouyang Xiu's career. The 1057 episode's cohort counts, later-role totals, and “千年科甲第一榜” label remain source-scoped, and recognition by an examiner should not be treated as the sole cause of any candidate's later achievement.

## What Changed

- Added Ouyang Xiu's chief-examiner role in the 1057 cohort.
- Integrated examination selection with his existing literary-mentorship and historiographical profile.
- Reframed his influence as institutional gatekeeping without making it the sole cause of candidates' later prominence.

- [[SuZhe|苏辙]] - younger writer recognized in the same Northern Song examination world.
- [[WangAnshi|王安石]] - another recognized talent whose later politics diverged from Su Shi's.
