# 刘续 / Liu Xu (Anping Wang)

刘续 / Liu Xu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] as the Anping Wang whose restoration becomes a disputed court decision. The source says Liu Xu had been captured by the [[YellowTurbansLateHan|黄巾军]] and later ransomed by people of Anping, creating the background for [[LiXieLateHan|李燮]]'s refusal to support restoring his kingdom.

Li Xie's warning is initially punished as slander against the imperial clan, but the episode says Liu Xu is executed less than a year later for great treason and the Anping state is abolished. The page therefore treats Liu Xu as a source case where princely title and clan status do not guarantee public fitness under late-Han disorder.
