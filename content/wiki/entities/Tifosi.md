# Tifosi

Tifosi is the Ferrari fan base in [[ferrari-1]]. The episode uses the term to describe the broad base of people who may never own a [[Ferrari]] but still participate emotionally in [[ScuderiaFerrari]] and [[FormulaOne]].

For the wiki, the Tifosi make Ferrari different from a purely closed luxury house. They are the inclusive layer in [[LuxuryBrandAsSportsTeam]] and the base of the [[LuxuryCommunityPyramid]] that supports the brand's cultural reach.
