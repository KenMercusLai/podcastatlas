# 襄城 / Xiangcheng (Qin)

襄城 / Xiangcheng enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-2-lishi-jilu-shouci-tucheng-lm4kuzdpnqn-hicyuvvb4p2wdudm|Qinji 130-2]] as a city in Yingchuan commandery that resists [[XiangYu|项羽]] during [[XiangLiang|项梁]]'s early anti-[[QinState|秦国]] expansion.

The episode's source role for Xiangcheng is not ordinary territorial control. After the city holds out and is taken, Xiang Yu kills the soldiers and civilians, and the host treats this as the first recorded "屠城" in the narrative frame. This page therefore keeps the claim source-scoped while linking it to the wiki's broader violence concepts.

Xiangcheng anchors a shift in the Xiang branch. Earlier sources present Xiang Yu through aristocratic Chu identity, physical force, and the [[KuaijiCommanderyCoup|会稽郡守府夺权]]. Qinji 130-2 adds a punitive mass-violence marker that later makes him a negative case beside [[LiuBang|刘邦]] in [[NonKillingUnification|不爱杀人者能一之]].
