The supplied episode identifies her with the dermatology department of Beijing Chang'an Hospital affiliated with Capital Medical University and says her clinical focus includes hair loss.

## Current Profile
In this source, Huang translates common hair anxieties into diagnostic and treatment boundaries. She distinguishes normal turnover from sustained density loss, then separates androgenetic alopecia, alopecia areata, telogen effluvium, postpartum shedding, and traction-related loss by pattern, timing, triggers, family history, and examination.

Her practical stance is conservative: rapid-growth promises are implausible against follicle-cycle timing; massage and commercial scalp care are adjuncts at most; shampoo can support cleansing or inflammation management but does not regrow hair; and transplantation does not remove the need to manage continuing loss. She also frames gray hair, washing, heat, rubbing, styling products, conditioning, and dyeing through harm reduction rather than elaborate routines.

## Key Characteristics
- Dermatologist presented by the episode as clinically focused on hair-loss care.

- Rejects rapid-cure marketing and keeps massage, shampoo, and salon care in an adjunctive role.
- Presents androgenetic hair loss as a chronic management problem rather than a one-time repair.
- Separates hair-shaft protection and scalp hygiene from follicle diagnosis and treatment.
- Uses qualification and escalation rather than universal product recommendations.
