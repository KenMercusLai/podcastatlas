# 蒙敖 / Meng Ao

蒙敖 / Meng Ao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-69-bikan-gudai-diwang-qipa-sifa-da-pandian-lukvjsktg-pgfjhizvypwmdmw-13]] as the Qin commander whom the host identifies behind the 285 BCE attack on [[QiState|齐国]]. The episode says [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] names Meng Wu, but corrects the notice through [[Shiji|《史记》]]: the commander should be Meng Ao, Meng Wu's father.

The source uses him less as a full biography than as a source-critical hinge. Under [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]], Qin attacks Qi after Qi's destruction of [[SongState|宋国]] has made Qi look too powerful. Meng Ao's campaign captures nine cities, and the episode says Qin then establishes them as counties governed by Qin-appointed officials.
