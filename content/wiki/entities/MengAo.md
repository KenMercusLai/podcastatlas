# 蒙敖 / Meng Ao

蒙敖 / Meng Ao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-69-bikan-gudai-diwang-qipa-sifa-da-pandian-lukvjsktg-pgfjhizvypwmdmw-13]] as the Qin commander whom the host identifies behind the 285 BCE attack on [[QiState|齐国]]. The episode says [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] names Meng Wu, but corrects the notice through [[Shiji|《史记》]]: the commander should be Meng Ao, Meng Wu's father.

The source uses him less as a full biography than as a source-critical hinge. Under [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]], Qin attacks Qi after Qi's destruction of [[SongState|宋国]] has made Qi look too powerful. Meng Ao's campaign captures nine cities, and the episode says Qin then establishes them as counties governed by Qin-appointed officials.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-1-lvbuwei-shi-jiangziya-houren-lveg-wsmggroaxjsnxopaevexqod]] adds a later [[QinZhuangxiangwang|秦庄襄王]] campaign. Meng Ao attacks [[HanState|韩国]], takes Chenggao, and Qin establishes [[SanchuanWarringStates|三川]] commandery. The episode uses him here as the general attached to Qin's continued eastward administrative consolidation after the Lu Buwei succession payoff.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-2-kongzi-zai-luguo-de-shenshi-zhimi-lphy12exg4u7f-30qkir96gv634r]] adds the next campaign notice. In [[QinZhuangxiangwang|秦庄襄王]]'s second year, Meng Ao attacks [[ZhaoState|赵国]] and takes thirty-seven cities, with Yuci and Langmeng named in the source. The episode uses the notice mainly as annalistic context after the Lu-destruction explanation and before the [[LushiChunqiu|《吕氏春秋》]] compilation note.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-103-1-simaqian-kanzhong-de-xinlingjun-suan-shi-xiaoren-ma-lvty1pdnibvqpk0m16kkxzw4t-ck]] adds Meng Ao's next pressure point against [[WeiState|魏国]]. He takes Gaodu and Ji city, pushing [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] to recall [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] from [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. The episode then makes Meng Ao the Qin commander defeated by Xinlingjun's five-state coalition south of the Yellow River.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-104-yinmou-shisuan-rang-qinguo-bantu-you-you-you-pengzhang-le-lgnyhbduqutegtksrjwcvhmn8-t6]] gives Meng Ao a brief post-succession stabilization notice: in 246 BCE, under young [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]], he suppresses the Jinyang disturbance. The source uses the event to show that Qin has internal disorder even as the eastern states fail to coordinate effectively against it.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-105-zhanzheng-huayang-zhaoguo-limu-zui-hui-shua-ljtqdg46a8o13zzsjv-p-fpol5oh]] adds Meng Ao's 244 BCE Han campaign. Even though [[QinState|秦国]] is experiencing famine, Meng Ao attacks [[HanState|韩国]] and captures twelve cities, making him part of the source's point that Qin's eastward pressure continues despite internal strain.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-106-jingren-faxian-xinlingjun-de-feizhengchang-siwang-lsj3kd2tb4tqtuvhwvsdwkh-h5el]] moves Meng Ao from Han back to [[WeiState|魏国]]. In 243 BCE he attacks Wei and stops in the third month; in the following year, after Qin's locust and epidemic crisis, he attacks Wei again, takes twenty cities, and the gains become [[DongCommandery|东郡]]. The source therefore makes him the military carrier of Qin's recovery from disaster into administrative expansion.
