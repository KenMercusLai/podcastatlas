# Pew Charitable Trusts

The Pew Charitable Trusts appears in [[tech-20260414-0414-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260414-0414-mp-tech-pod-128]] as [[LizFarmer]]'s organization and as the source of the episode's state-policy count: at least 19 states considered laws in 2025 allowing some state funds to be invested in digital assets.

In this wiki branch, Pew anchors a public-finance reading of crypto adoption. The episode does not treat state crypto policy as only ideology or branding; it asks how laws, reserve design, asset custody, general-fund separation, and [[PublicCryptoInvestmentRisk]] shape what state governments can actually do with volatile digital assets.
