# 白绕 / Bai Rao (late Han)

白绕 / Bai Rao (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] as one of the armed leaders attacking [[DongCommandery|东郡]] alongside [[YuDuLateHan|于毒]] and [[SuiGuLateHan|眭固]]. The source places him inside the broader [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]] pressure field.

Bai Rao's role is operational rather than biographical. [[WangGongLateHan|王肱]] cannot resist the attack, [[CaoCao|曹操]] moves into Dong Commandery, and Cao Cao defeats Bai Rao around [[PuyangLateHan|濮阳]]. That victory becomes one of the steps by which Cao Cao gains a formal foothold as Dong Commandery administrator before the later Hanji 961 Neihuang branch.
