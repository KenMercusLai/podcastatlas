# MUJI / 无印良品

MUJI / 无印良品 appears in [[142-riben-xiaofeizhe-weihe-ruci-chimi-jijie-xianding-chuantai-kuahai-dianbo-lgbjiahlutyyf4paylnyxy7hv0iq]] as a New Year lucky-bag case inside [[JapaneseSeasonalLimitedConsumption]]. The source says MUJI's lucky bags can combine zodiac themes, Japanese regional craft, reservation or lottery access, and a gift-card mechanism that makes the craft object feel like a seasonal bonus.

The example matters less as discounting than as annual brand memory. A buyer who receives local craft plus future store credit is nudged to remember MUJI again at the next New Year, turning a retail campaign into a recurring ritual.
