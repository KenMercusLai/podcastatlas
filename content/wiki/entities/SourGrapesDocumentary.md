# 《酸葡萄》 / Sour Grapes

《酸葡萄》 / Sour Grapes is the wine-fraud documentary recommended in [[sanlian-chuantai-shen-ci-gei-ren-putao-shijie-weixun-le-585125156]]. The episode uses it to show the darker side of [[Wine|wine]] culture: rare-bottle collecting, auctions, social tasting circles, labels, bottle weight, absent vintages, and expert confidence can all become part of a deception environment.

For the wiki, the documentary complements [[WineAsAgriculturalCulture]] by showing what happens when wine is pulled away from agriculture and into status, scarcity, and social performance. It also qualifies [[TasteTraining]]: knowing flavors matters, but the fraud case suggests that reading people and institutions can be as important as reading a glass.
