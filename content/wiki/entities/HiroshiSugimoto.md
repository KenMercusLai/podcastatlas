# Hiroshi Sugimoto / 杉本博司

## Overview

Hiroshi Sugimoto / 杉本博司 is the photographer used by Episode 230 to show how apparently natural images can question photographic truth, constructed display, time, and perception.

## Current Profile

The source highlights two bodies of work. Photographs of museum dioramas make fabricated ecological displays appear plausibly real and thereby expose photography's capacity to confer truth-like presence. The *Seascapes* series uses sea and horizon to shift nature photography away from species capture toward time, perception, and a repeated elemental form.

## Key Characteristics

- Photographs constructed museum dioramas as if they were natural scenes.
- Uses photographic plausibility to question the relation between image and reality.
- Uses sea-horizon repetition to examine time and perception.
- Exemplifies work that crosses out of conventional nature-photography categories.
