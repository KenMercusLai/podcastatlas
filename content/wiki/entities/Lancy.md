# 朗姿 / Lancy

## Overview

朗姿 / Lancy is a Chinese womenswear company presented in [[ep151-ep151]] as an established premium-fashion case serving affluent mature women and formal social occasions.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Lancy to show how a defined local customer and use context can support premium positioning. It reports that online business represents about 40% of the company and grew roughly 30%, suggesting that ecommerce can become material even for a brand rooted in occasion-led apparel and offline customer understanding.

## Key Characteristics

- Premium womenswear positioning.
- Focus on mature, higher-spending women.
- Formal social occasions provide a bounded use context.
- Ecommerce is reported as a substantial and growing part of the business.

## Evidence

- Customer focus - [[ep151-ep151]] describes Lancy as serving mature women with spending power who value formal social occasions.
- Channel mix - [[ep151-ep151]] reports online business at roughly 40% and online growth around 30%.
- Structural role - [[ep151-ep151]] treats the company as evidence that older domestic brands are also upgrading online.
