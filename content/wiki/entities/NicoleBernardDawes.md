# Nicole Bernard Dawes

## Overview
Nicole Bernard Dawes is the founder of [[LateJulySnacks]] and later [[Nixie]], profiled in [[late-july-snacks-nicole-bernard-dawes-crackers-and-cookies-were-failing-tortilla-chips-saved-them-97049a31-9144-42f5-856c-9590c5e6beb4]]. Her episode frames her as a second-generation natural-food entrepreneur whose taste standards, category judgment, and willingness to survive a 2009 financing crisis shaped Late July's eventual tortilla-chip breakthrough.

## Current Profile
Nicole grew up around [[CapeCodChips]], where her father [[SteveBernard]] proved that better ingredients and better taste could create a mainstream snack business. She carried that worldview into Late July, but the company had to move through slow crackers, unprofitable cookies, debt pressure, and allergy constraints before tortilla chips gave the brand a scalable category. After the [[SnydersLance]] and [[CampbellSoupCompany]] ownership sequence, she redirected the same taste-first organic mission into Nixie beverages.

## Key Characteristics
- Second-generation CPG founder shaped by family exposure to natural foods and potato chips.
- Taste-first operator who treats mission as a loyalty layer rather than a substitute for repeat purchase.
- Crisis-tested founder who kept Late July alive through grief, a loan default, and product-strategy pressure.
- Category-sensitive builder who moved from crackers and cookies into tortilla chips after seeing velocity and margin limits.
- Post-exit founder who carried the same organic positioning into beverages through Nixie.
