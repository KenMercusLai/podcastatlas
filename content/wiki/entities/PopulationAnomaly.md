# 《人口异常》 / Population Anomaly

## Overview
《人口异常》 / Population Anomaly is a later AI short film by [[WuHankun|吴瀚坤]] discussed in [[no-232-wuhankun-ai-shidai-yanyuan-diyige-diufanwan-gkwriw4oa148aw4mzwtp-xj]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Population Anomaly as a different experiment from [[PinkParadox|《粉色悖论》]]. Wu connects it to his science-fiction interests, especially 《三体》, and to his experience living in Australia, using an Australian migration premise and wasteland aesthetics influenced by works such as 《沙丘》 and 《疯狂的麦克斯》.

Its main wiki value is workflow evolution. Wu says tools such as Cdance 2.0 made multi-shot transitions and storyboard handling smoother, reducing editing work and shifting him from tight control toward more co-creative prompting and selection.

## Key Characteristics
- It is framed as a science-fiction AI short-film experiment.
- It uses an Australia-linked premise and wasteland visual references.
- It shows Wu moving from precise control toward AI co-creation and selection.
- It exposes story-development limits: Wu says the script was not fully solved and some plot transitions felt disconnected.
- It treats a five-minute short as better suited to posing one memorable question than resolving a large world.
