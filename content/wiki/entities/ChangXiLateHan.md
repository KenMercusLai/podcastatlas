# 昌豨 / Chang Xi (late Han)

## Overview
昌豨 / Chang Xi enters the wiki through Hanji 1040 as the East Sea rebel or local armed leader who joins [[LiuBei|刘备]] after Liu Bei kills [[CheZhouLateHan|车胄]] and retakes [[Xuzhou|徐州]].

## Current Profile
Chang Xi currently matters as evidence that Liu Bei's breakaway created a wider regional realignment rather than only one city seizure. His support helps the episode explain why Liu Bei's force can expand to tens of thousands and why Cao Cao's advisers were right to fear his release.

## Key Characteristics
- East Sea armed leader associated with the post-Che-Zhou shift.
- Joins Liu Bei after Xuzhou changes hands.
- Represents local-force adhesion around Liu Bei's renewed autonomy.
- Appears in the transcript as "昌熙," normalized here to Chang Xi.

## Evidence
- Alignment shift: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1040-luanshi-yingxiong-caocao-weihe-dudu-kanhao-liubei-lrazzwo4bjspvwmnkx5hoar1iq3x]] says the East Sea leader rendered as "昌熙" and other counties turn toward Liu Bei.
- Force-growth implication: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1040-luanshi-yingxiong-caocao-weihe-dudu-kanhao-liubei-lrazzwo4bjspvwmnkx5hoar1iq3x]] links those accessions to Liu Bei's rapid growth to several tens of thousands.
- Release-risk role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1040-luanshi-yingxiong-caocao-weihe-dudu-kanhao-liubei-lrazzwo4bjspvwmnkx5hoar1iq3x]] uses this support to validate Cheng Yu, Guo Jia, and Dong Zhao's warning.

## Qualifications
The episode does not provide Chang Xi's prior history, later conduct, or exact force size. The name normalization remains source-scoped because the transcript uses "昌熙."
