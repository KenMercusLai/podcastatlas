# 昌豨 / Chang Xi (late Han)

## Overview
昌豨 / Chang Xi is an East Sea / Donghai local armed leader whose wiki profile now runs from support for [[LiuBei|刘备]] after Liu Bei retakes [[Xuzhou|徐州]] to surrender under Cao-side siege after [[ZhangLiao|张辽]]'s persuasion, followed by later rebellion and execution.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1040 makes Chang Xi evidence that Liu Bei's breakaway created a wider regional realignment rather than only one city seizure. His support helps explain why Liu Bei's force can expand to tens of thousands and why Cao Cao's advisers were right to fear releasing Liu Bei.

Hanji 1054 turns Chang Xi into a more unstable local-force case. [[CaoCao|曹操]] sends [[XiahouYuan|夏侯渊]] and Zhang Liao to besiege him in Donghai; after months of resistance and Cao-side grain pressure, Zhang Liao reads his hesitation, negotiates through old ties and guarantee, and brings him to surrender. Cao appoints Chang Xi as Donghai taishou, but the same source says he later rebels again and is killed by [[YuJin|于禁]].

## Key Characteristics
- East Sea / Donghai armed leader associated first with Liu Bei's post-Che-Zhou Xuzhou shift.
- Represents local-force adhesion around Liu Bei's renewed autonomy in Hanji 1040.
- Appears later as a repeatedly shifting, opportunistic armed actor under Cao-side pressure.
- Surrenders when siege pressure, prior acquaintance, promised office, and Zhang Liao's personal guarantee make submission credible.
- Does not become a stable loyalty case; later rebellion and execution qualify the incorporation.

## Evidence
- Alignment shift to Liu Bei: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1040-luanshi-yingxiong-caocao-weihe-dudu-kanhao-liubei-lrazzwo4bjspvwmnkx5hoar1iq3x|Hanji 1040]] says the East Sea leader rendered as "昌熙" and other counties turn toward Liu Bei after Che Zhou's death.
- Force-growth implication: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1040-luanshi-yingxiong-caocao-weihe-dudu-kanhao-liubei-lrazzwo4bjspvwmnkx5hoar1iq3x|Hanji 1040]] links those accessions to Liu Bei's rapid growth to several tens of thousands.
- Cao-side siege: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1054-jingtan-caocao-yuanshao-liubei-de-mingyun-zhuanzhe-lmd2yjre-cwdbxky2ht5o0n4g6fk|Hanji 1054]] says Cao Cao sends Xiahou Yuan and Zhang Liao to Donghai to besiege Chang Xi after repeated betrayal.
- Surrender negotiation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1054-jingtan-caocao-yuanshao-liubei-de-mingyun-zhuanzhe-lmd2yjre-cwdbxky2ht5o0n4g6fk|Hanji 1054]] says Zhang Liao observes Chang Xi's wavering, offers a personal guarantee, visits his family alone, and brings him to Cao Cao.
- Appointment and failure of durable incorporation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1054-jingtan-caocao-yuanshao-liubei-de-mingyun-zhuanzhe-lmd2yjre-cwdbxky2ht5o0n4g6fk|Hanji 1054]] reports Chang Xi's appointment as Donghai taishou and later rebellion before Yu Jin kills him.

## Qualifications
The name normalization remains source-scoped because Hanji 1040 renders the name as "昌熙." Hanji 1054's claim about Chang Xi's exact kinship with Lü Bu is also kept source-bounded; the stable wiki claim is that prior acquaintance with Zhang Liao made persuasion easier. His appointment after surrender should not be read as durable pacification because the same episode records later renewed rebellion.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1054 to extend Chang Xi from Liu Bei-side local support into a Cao-side siege, surrender, appointment, and later rebellion sequence.
- Reframed the page from a single Xuzhou adhesion note into an unstable local-force profile.
- Added Zhang Liao's guarantee and Chang Xi's failed long-term incorporation as the core new evidence.
