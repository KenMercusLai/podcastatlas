# 张时 / Zhang Shi

张时 / Zhang Shi is one of the speakers in [[ep250-zoujin-shanye-shunliu-ershang-xunzhao-huwai-yundong-zhong-de-xinliu-shike-lpv4egumbxcjrxk3jypehyxcqfuo]], where he discusses trail running, hiking, skiing, breath practice, and the search for [[OutdoorFlowState|户外心流]]. His examples move from karate breathing to a 20 km trail run, downhill running, Yunnan mountain activity, wrong turns, safety equipment, and the decision to pursue enjoyable challenge rather than status escalation.

His role in the source is to keep outdoor sport tied to self-knowledge. Zhang Shi repeatedly frames mountains as a place where the body encounters natural limits: climbing is painful, altitude and weather matter, running farther is not automatically better, and even a short trail run can require headlamp, battery, GPS, warmth, and water. That makes him a source voice for [[TrailRunningEntryRisk]], [[OutdoorSafetyPreparation]], and [[OutdoorBodyMindReintegration]].
