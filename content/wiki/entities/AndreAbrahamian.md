# Andre Abrahamian

Andre Abrahamian appears in [[getting-entrepreneurial-in-korea-summer-school]] as a business and policy educator who taught North Koreans topics such as marketing, management, accounting, and trade. The source places his work inside [[KimJongUn]]'s limited economic opening, when entrepreneurs, retail operators, factory managers, software companies, and startup ideas had some room to learn business practice.

His role is source-scoped: Abrahamian shows that [[NorthKorea|North Korea]] did not lack teachable entrepreneurial capacity. The constraint was that [[DonjuMarketClass|donju]] activity remained politically tolerated rather than legally secure, so learning could strengthen growth without creating durable [[PropertyRightsAsInvestmentIncentive]].
