# Sriman Swarup

## Overview
Sriman Swarup is the practicing oncologist and [[OncoNexus]] founder interviewed in [[ep-26-the-future-of-healthcare-ai-data-and-human-touch|Data Science With Sam EP26]] about integrated, human-centered AI for cancer care.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Swarup as a clinician-builder whose AI perspective begins with workflow redesign rather than autonomous diagnosis. His earlier EHR protocol is used to show that structured software can change clinical behavior, while his current argument is that oncology needs a quiet, integrated coordination layer that makes evidence visible and leaves consequential action with clinicians and staff.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines practicing oncology with clinical-workflow and healthcare-AI product work.
- Treats fragmented coordination, documentation, and administration as patient-care problems rather than merely back-office inefficiency.
- Advocates [[HumanJudgmentUnderAI]] instead of doctor replacement or autonomous clinical action.
- Makes inspectability, consent, auditability, and bounded failure central to clinical trust.
- Broadens precision oncology from molecular matching to treatment feasibility and the patient's circumstances.

## Evidence
### Workflow redesign
- [[ep-26-the-future-of-healthcare-ai-data-and-human-touch]] describes Swarup's 2017 heparin-induced thrombocytopenia EHR protocol and his later argument for integrating notes, calls, scheduling, billing, and clinical context.

### Human-centered clinical AI
- [[ep-26-the-future-of-healthcare-ai-data-and-human-touch]] records his preference for evidence-backed flags, staff review, explicit consent, traceability, containment, and technology that feels like subtraction.

### Precision and access
- [[ep-26-the-future-of-healthcare-ai-data-and-human-touch]] connects his account of trial matching and diagnostic acceleration to patient logistics, specialist scarcity, and retained human care.

## Qualifications
- The available evidence is one interview summary and does not independently verify Swarup's reported protocol outcomes or OncoNexus's product performance.
- Product architecture, clinical-validation data, privacy controls, deployment scale, and patient outcomes are not supplied.
