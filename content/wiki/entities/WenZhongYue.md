# 文种 / Wen Zhong of Yue

文种 / Wen Zhong of Yue appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-57-gudai-fa-dushi-haiyou-zhe-zuoyong-lj6wi61osaytowgmdp8dtkrwb-si]] as the minister [[FanLi|范蠡]] warns after [[Goujian|越王勾践]] defeats [[WuState|吴国]]. The episode preserves Fan Li's warning that bows and hounds are put away once birds and hares are gone, using it to explain why a successful minister can become endangered after the enemy disappears.

Wen Zhong does not leave. The source says Goujian later gives him a sword and Wen Zhong commits suicide. His page therefore records the failed-exit side of the episode's post-victory minister-risk lesson.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-165-2-hanxin-weihe-zhisi-ye-bu-beipan-liubang-lrgtvbdyc9cwbfhaix1aabsmqhhb|Hanji 165-2]] reuses Wen Zhong as [[KuaiChe|蒯彻 / 蒯通]]'s warning to [[HanXin|韩信]]. The precedent is no longer only a Yue-state afterstory; it becomes a live Chu-Han analogy for a minister or commander whose merit will be too large once the ruler's main enemy disappears.
