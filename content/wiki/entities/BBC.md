# BBC

## Overview
The [[BBC]] appears in these sources as an international information signal, a broadcaster retiring old infrastructure and the institutional origin of a conservation project. A BBC journalist's separate materials report is a reporting connection, not a fourth institutional strategy.

## Current Profile
Its significance here lies in the contrast between outside news under censorship, broad cultural and conservation programming, and the distribution problem created when a legacy broadcast channel serves too few people to maintain but remains important to some listeners.

## Key Characteristics
- An outside news signal could challenge Soviet official accounts in one Cold War insider's experience.
- Longwave retirement exposes a cost-versus-access tradeoff in public broadcasting.
- A BBC-origin endangered-species project joined popular storytelling to conservation testimony.

## Evidence
- **Outside information:** In the [[OlegGordievsky|Gordievsky]] story, a BBC report on Britain's expulsion of Soviet intelligence personnel is contrasted with official Soviet denial within a gradual [[KGB]] officer's ideological disillusionment; this is one narrated comparison, not a general accuracy audit. [[80-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-shang-691866649]]
- **Infrastructure and access:** The Economist episode says BBC Droitwich longwave transmitters ceased on June 27, 2026 after operating since 1934; it recalls wartime coded messages, Radio 4, cricket commentary and the shipping forecast. The BBC's maintenance rationale sits beside concern for remote listeners and people without easy digital access. [[latin-lessons-the-donroe-doctrine-boost-6a4233e26b6690f46aae4e3d]]
- **Conservation project:** A book-review episode describes the BBC as the 1980s institutional origin of [[LastChanceToSee|Last Chance to See]], pairing [[DouglasAdams]] and [[MarkCarwardine]] in endangered-species field travel that later became humorous but grave nature writing. [[186-rang-ni-xiao-dao-tou-diao-reng-bu-wang-sikao-weida-de-xiaoshi-shijie-manyou-zhinan-998655210]]

## Qualifications
- The espionage and conservation links come through secondary podcast retellings, not direct BBC archives. The longwave timing, audience and access tradeoff are described by a June 2026 episode, not independently audited here.
- [[AnnaHolligan]]'s report from Alaska on mycelium packaging is a BBC journalist's participation in a [[MarketplaceTech]] segment, not evidence that the BBC developed [[MyceliumInsulation]] or adopted it as an institutional policy.
