# Michael Kratsios

## Overview
Michael Kratsios is a Trump administration science and technology policy figure in the All-In corpus. Across the bounded sources, he moves from PCAST co-chair and national AI strategy speaker into the direct [[OfficeOfScienceAndTechnologyPolicy|OSTP]] voice defending the administration's [[NewGoldenAgeScienceAgenda|New Golden Age science agenda]].

## Current Profile
Kratsios's current wiki profile is a policy operator linking AI strategy, public science funding, national missions, and science-trust politics. He frames U.S. technological leadership as a full-stack problem: AI models and infrastructure, basic research, energy, public scientific data, export strategy, talent formation, and competition with [[China]] all reinforce one another.

## Key Characteristics
- Kratsios is a White House science-and-technology coordinator, first appearing through [[PCAST]] and later through OSTP.
- He presents U.S. AI leadership as a stack strategy: out-innovate competitors, build infrastructure, and export American technology.
- He connects [[AIForScience|AI for Science]] to public scientific data and the [[GenesisMission|Genesis Mission]] rather than treating AI only as a private company race.
- He argues that federal science support should be judged by output, incentive design, and scientist autonomy, not only by budget totals.
- He uses politicization, COVID trust loss, DEI grant criteria, and climate-emergency framing to explain why the administration says science needs credibility repair.
- He treats China competition and the STEM talent pipeline as strategic constraints on U.S. science policy.
