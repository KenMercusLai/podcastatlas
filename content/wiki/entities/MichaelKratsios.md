# Michael Kratsios

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-anthropics-generational-run-openai-panics-ai-moats-meta-loses-lawsuits-40647420]] adds Kratsios through [[PCAST]]. In [[DavidSacks|Sacks]]'s account, Kratsios co-chairs the council with him, connecting the earlier [[AmericanAIStackStrategy]] and [[AIForScience]] branch to a broader presidential science-and-technology advisory structure.

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-inside-americas-ai-strategy-infrastructure-regulation-and-global-competition-39846955]] adds Michael Kratsios as the policy voice explaining the administration's AI strategy to [[MariaBartiromo|Maria Bartiromo]] and [[DavidSacks|David Sacks]]. His main frame is a three-pillar strategy: out-innovate competitors, build AI infrastructure, and export American technology so developers and governments abroad build on the U.S. stack.

Kratsios's distinctive contribution is to connect [[AIForScience|AI for Science]] with public scientific infrastructure. He describes the [[GenesisMission|Genesis Mission]] as an effort to make fragmented scientific data from chemistry, math, materials science, and Department of Energy national labs more useful for model training, simulation, and discovery.
