## Overview

鲍昱 / Bao Yu is an Eastern Han minister whose current profile now runs from Mingdi's appointment of him as Situ to Zhangdi-era frontier rescue advocacy and disaster-framed legal redress. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-743-chuo-ren-xinwo-de-lishi-shunjian-mingdi-liuzhuang-de-wenqing-meng-lgwtuqtug5bh90qohwiskccx77hy|Hanji 743]] supplies the office-entry layer; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-745-donghan-zui-xuexing-zhi-zhan-zhengjiu-dabing-genggong-lhg3pppaovl5mxawgmj-dgjgth4d|Hanji 745]] makes him the Situ who argues that rescuing [[GengGongEasternHan|Geng Gong]] and Guan Chong protects state credit and loyal-service morale; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-746-shenmeyang-de-lingdao-zhide-bei-sixintata-zhuisui-lvhbdgfbc69t6sdw92k4dipii-rx|Hanji 746]] shows his drought answer turning the Chu-king implication cases into concrete redress.

## Current Profile

Hanji 743 gives Bao Yu's appointment context. After Situ Wang Min dies, [[EmperorMingOfHan|Mingdi]] appoints Bao Yu, then Runan taishou, as Situ. The source does not explain the appointment's reasoning, but it supplies the bridge between Bao's regional office and the central-minister role assumed in the later rescue debate.

Hanji 745 gives Bao Yu's rescue layer. When Guan Chong requests aid for the isolated Western Regions defenders, [[FifthLunEasternHan|Fifth Lun]] opposes dispatching troops. Bao Yu argues the opposite: [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]] has failed to take two small Han positions for months, so it is tired, and abandoning the defenders would externally embolden frontier enemies while internally injuring those who die for Han. [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Zhangdi]] accepts the argument and orders reinforcements from Zhangye, Jiuquan, Dunhuang, and Shanshan.

Hanji 746 then presents Bao Yu as Situ when Zhangdi asks how to respond to drought. Bao does not directly demand a full reversal of the Chu-king case. Instead, he says wrongful implication is likely widespread and recommends allowing exiles to return home and removing the ban that keeps them from office.

Zhangdi accepts the recommendation, so Bao's importance in the source is both rhetorical and administrative. He identifies a human cause of political grievance under the language of drought response and converts it into a limited but concrete remedy.

The same episode keeps Bao Yu politically mixed. In the later debate over Northern Xiongnu campaigning and Western Regions garrison burdens, Bao Yu is grouped with Mou Rong against Yang Zhong's retrenchment view. The current page therefore treats Bao as a capable remonstrant in the legal-redress case, not as a blanket anti-war or anti-burden figure.

## Key Characteristics

- Mingdi-appointed Situ whose central role is first identified after service as Runan taishou.
- Zhangdi-era senior minister consulted during the 76 CE drought.
- Rescue advocate who treats isolated frontier defenders as a test of Han credit and loyal-service morale.
- Strategic reasoner who reads prolonged enemy failure as evidence that relief may be feasible.
- Uses disaster-response language to reopen wrongful-implication and exile policy.
- Favors a limited remedy that avoids a direct institutional humiliation of the ruler.
- Later appears on the non-retrenchment side of the Northern Xiongnu and Western Regions policy debate.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 743, Hanji 745, and Hanji 746 and does not reconstruct Bao Yu's full career. Hanji 743 gives the appointment but not its internal politics. Hanji 745 supports a rescue-credit argument, not a general claim that every isolated force should always be relieved. Hanji 746 supports a strong claim about his advice-framing in the Chu-king aftermath, but it does not make him uniformly lenient or uniformly anti-expedition.
