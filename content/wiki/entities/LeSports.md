# 乐视体育 / LeSports

LeSports appears in [[e237-yangshi-he-fifa-tanpan-fenzheng-beihou-tiyu-saishi-zhuanboquan-de-boyi-yu-shengyi-c3607c08-6eb6-48bd-8250-b41b5b8272e6]] as one of the clearest China-side examples of [[SportsRightsBubble]]. The episode says LeSports once tried to buy large sports-rights packages at prices far above competitors, including an NBA bid reportedly around three times [[Tencent]]'s renewal level, but rights owners also considered buyer stability rather than only headline price.

The source frames LeSports' mistake as more than overpaying for one event. It tried to justify expensive rights through an ecosystem and O2O traffic story, but the market did not yet have enough sports-payment habit, advertising recovery, or conversion capacity to carry the rights cost.
