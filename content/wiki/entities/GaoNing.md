# 高宁 / Gao Ning

高宁 / Gao Ning appears in [[keyi-gei-nide-agent-fa-yidian-linghuaqian-le-s10e22-9a652c19-ceb3-46c2-87b4-bca36e684311]] as a recurring [[WhatsNextKejiZaozhidao|What's Next｜科技早知道]] guest discussing the market meaning of [[AgentPaymentInfrastructure]].

His source-scoped contribution is demand-side framing. He argues that as users delegate longer tasks to [[ChatGPT]], [[Codex]], or similar agents, they will care less about the intermediate tool or skill and more about whether the final result is good and reasonably priced. Once that happens, repeated tasks may need standing budgets rather than per-step approval.

Gao Ning also distinguishes early product-linked payment from a later personal-agent stage. In the first stage, users bind cards or recharge a specific product or skill; in the later stage, the user's own agent can enter a marketplace, call suitable skills, and spend within [[AgentSpendControls]].
