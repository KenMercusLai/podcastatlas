# Olympias

## Overview

Olympias was a queen of Macedon, wife of Philip II, and mother of Alexander the Great, remembered in the bounded source through marital conflict, political intensity, assassination rumor, and snake imagery.

## Current Profile

[[206-historical-love-island-glt8073929093]] casts Olympias as the “angry jilted” contestant by emphasizing Philip's repeated infidelity and stories connecting her to his assassination. It also repeats traditions associating her with snakes, a vivid image whose sources and hostile political uses the episode does not investigate.

She is initially paired with [[LordByron]], then left for [[Sporus]] and recoupled with [[JimmyCarter]]. The scenario captures the episode's taste for dramatic contrast but offers no evidence of compatibility and no balanced account of Macedonian dynastic politics.

- Queen of Macedon, wife of Philip II, and mother of Alexander the Great.
