# 夏侯惇 / Xiahou Dun

## Overview
夏侯惇 / Xiahou Dun is a Cao-side commander whose profile spans mobile recruitment, emergency rear stabilization, practical caution, and a later tactical warning failure. The sources do not present him as a simple foil: he can act decisively in crisis, but Hanji 1057 adds a case where he ignores better battlefield signal-reading from [[LiDian|李典]].

## Current Profile
Hanji 953 places Xiahou Dun with [[CaoCao|曹操]] after the [[SuanzaoCamp|酸枣]] coalition begins to dissolve, when Cao turns toward Yangzhou to rebuild force. Hanji 975 gives Xiahou Dun an emergency Yanzhou role: he is recalled from [[PuyangLateHan|濮阳]] to [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]], interrogates and kills officials tied to rebellion, and helps hold Cao's rear even though the move leaves Puyang exposed. Hanji 978 shows him warning [[XunYu|荀彧]] that leaving Juancheng to meet [[GuoGongLateHan|郭贡]] risks morale and command continuity. Hanji 1057 adds the Bowang branch, where Xiahou Dun pursues [[LiuBei|刘备]] after a burned-camp withdrawal despite Li Dian's ambush warning and must be rescued.

## Key Characteristics
- Early Cao-side mobile cadre member during post-Suanzao recovery.
- Coercive rear stabilizer in the Yanzhou crisis.
- Practical caution voice in the Guo Gong/Xun Yu scene.
- Tactical commander vulnerable to bait when he discounts terrain and retreat signals.
- Profile combines command utility with source-specific judgment limits rather than a one-note evaluation.

## Qualifications
The Bowang branch should not erase Xiahou Dun's earlier practical competence. The current source set shows different roles under different pressures: recruitment companion, rear coercion, cautionary adviser, and overconfident pursuit commander.
