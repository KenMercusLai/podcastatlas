# 夏侯惇 / Xiahou Dun

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-953-luanshi-zhizhong-yaobuyao-jiangjiu-renyi-lrzfimgqatjxyw9zx9ry2kiwxkon|Hanji 953]] places Xiahou Dun in Cao Cao's post-Suanzao recovery. After the anti-[[DongZhuo|董卓]] coalition refuses [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s attack plan and the [[SuanzaoCamp|Suanzao]] camp begins to dissolve, Cao Cao goes with Xiahou Dun toward Yangzhou to recruit a new force, showing Xiahou Dun as part of the early mobile cadre around Cao.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-975-yanzhou-zhiluan-xunyu-ruhe-bang-caocao-shou-dabenying-ln0hh4jrokkwtqsmxmqhdjzcosdr|Hanji 975]] backfills Xiahou Dun's emergency role before the later [[GuoGongLateHan|郭贡]] scene. After [[XunYu|荀彧]] detects [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] and [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]]'s rebellion, he recalls Xiahou Dun from [[PuyangLateHan|濮阳]] to [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]] because [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s main force is away attacking [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]].

The source makes Xiahou Dun the coercive stabilizer in a weak rear. He finds grain-store fires and implicated officials, interrogates the city administration, and kills dozens involved in the rebellion. His movement helps hold Juancheng, but it also leaves Puyang open for [[LyuBu|吕布]] to seize.

夏侯惇 / Xiahou Dun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-978-gushen-quantui-shuwan-dijun-ta-zenme-zuodao-de-lhgd8vsvuaf5oi9aefh0wxabmzwv|Hanji 978]] as the Cao-side commander who warns [[XunYu|荀彧]] against leaving [[JuanchengLateHan|鄄城]] to meet [[GuoGongLateHan|郭贡]]. His warning is practical: Xun Yu is the main person stabilizing the city, so losing him would damage morale and command continuity.

The source uses Xiahou Dun as a counterweight rather than a failed adviser. His caution makes Xun Yu's choice legible: Xun Yu is not casually brave, but deliberately decides that Guo Gong's unsettled commitment makes direct composure more useful than staying behind the walls.
