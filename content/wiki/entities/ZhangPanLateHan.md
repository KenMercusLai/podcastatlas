# 张磐 / Zhang Pan (late Han)

## Overview
Zhang Pan is the [[JiaozhiCommandery|Jiaozhi]] inspector in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-867-wugu-bei-po-zangshui-ta-xuanze-baqi-huiji-lqenvoy7x8ic-qn3-bf93qj8tjvk|Hanji 867]] who refuses to accept amnesty as a substitute for exoneration. After suppressing [[HuLanLateHan|Hu Lan]]'s remnants in [[CangwuCommanderyLateHan|Cangwu]], he is falsely accused by [[DuShangLateHan|Du Shang]] and turns imprisonment into a demand for factual confrontation.

## Current Profile
Zhang Pan's current profile is built around procedural defiance. When Du Shang's memorial sends him to the Tingwei prison, Zhang Pan is still not finally judged before a general amnesty arrives. He refuses to leave prison, remains in restraints, and argues that a provincial governor falsely accused and tortured cannot let a pardon stand in place of proven innocence.

The confrontation vindicates him. Prison officials report his refusal to [[EmperorHuanOfHan|Emperor Huan]], Du Shang is summoned, and Zhang Pan's direct challenge exposes the false report. The episode therefore presents him as a case where reputation, legal truth, and official legitimacy require more than practical release.

## Key Characteristics
- Provincial inspector whose southern mop-up action suppresses Hu Lan's Cangwu remnants.
- Wrongly accused official who treats amnesty as inadequate when guilt would remain implied.
- Procedural challenger who demands face-to-face confrontation rather than quiet release.
- Core case for [[PardonWithoutExoneration|Pardon Without Exoneration / 赦免不等于昭雪]].

## Qualifications
The transcript gives the name as 张盘; this page normalizes the actor to 张磐 while preserving the source variant. Zhang Pan's case is not evidence that amnesty usually lacked legal force; it shows that, in this episode, reputational and factual vindication mattered more than immediate release. The source does not give a broader administrative biography beyond the Cangwu suppression and false-accusation case.
