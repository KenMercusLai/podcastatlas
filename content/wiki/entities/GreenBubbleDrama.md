# 绿泡泡短剧 / Green Bubble Drama

## Overview
绿泡泡短剧 is the official [[WeChat]] mini-program entry described by the source for aggregating free short dramas supplied by external mini-programs.

## Current Profile
The product is presented as a distribution and monetization layer rather than a studio. It gathers content already dispersed across the WeChat ecosystem, lets viewers watch without direct payment, and places advertising between episodes. The strategic value lies in converting fragmented mini-program audiences into a platform-controlled entrance that can improve discovery and bargaining power.

## Key Characteristics
- Aggregates external short-drama mini-programs into an official WeChat content pool.
- Uses free viewing and inter-episode advertising rather than direct episode payment in the source account.
- Connects mini-program discovery with [[WeChatChannels|WeChat Channels]] links that can jump into a drama.
- Seeks to consolidate a large but fragmented audience inside the WeChat ecosystem.

## Qualifications
The source does not provide audited audience definitions, retention, ad load, revenue sharing, content-governance rules, or profitability. An official entrance may concentrate discovery without eliminating competition among providers or with standalone short-drama platforms.
