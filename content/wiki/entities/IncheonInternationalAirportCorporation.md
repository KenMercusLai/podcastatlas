# Incheon International Airport Corporation / 仁川国际机场公社

## Overview
Incheon International Airport Corporation is the operator associated with [[IncheonInternationalAirport|Incheon International Airport]] and the episode's principal case of a mature hub operator building an overseas airport business.

## Current Profile
The source describes a staged internationalization path. Small consulting assignments beginning with an Iraq project in 2009 provided limited revenue and reportedly remained loss-making through 2019. A 2020 reorganization around [[AirportPPPConcession|PPP projects]] shifted the corporation toward development, equity participation, long-term operation, and maintenance in markets including Kuwait, Indonesia, the Philippines, and Uzbekistan.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses a major home-hub operating record as a qualification for foreign airport tenders and partnerships.
- Expanded from consulting toward capital-bearing, long-duration development and operating concessions.
- Reportedly holds 7.5% of the project company for the new Tashkent airport and will participate in terminal and parking construction and operation.
- Seeks geographic diversification and exposure to aviation markets growing faster than its mature home market.
- Treats route development, local positioning, and commercial operations as part of the airport product rather than merely maintaining facilities.

The episode does not provide audited segment results, full concession contracts, financing structures, or independent performance comparisons. The profitability improvement, project pipeline, 2030 target of at least ten airports, ownership percentage, and contract duration remain source-reported.
