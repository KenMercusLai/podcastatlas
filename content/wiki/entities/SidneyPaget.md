# Sidney Paget

## Overview

Sidney Paget was the illustrator whose Strand images helped turn [[SherlockHolmes|Sherlock Holmes]] into a visually standardized and globally recognizable character.

## Current Profile

[[85-sherlock-holmes-glt8342867383]] treats Paget as a co-producer of Holmes's cultural afterlife. His illustrations gave the detective a stable silhouette and familiar costume, including the deerstalker-associated image, even where that public image exceeded or simplified [[ArthurConanDoyle|Arthur Conan Doyle]]'s prose.

Paget therefore shows how a literary character can become a mass-cultural icon through interaction among text, magazine illustration, serial circulation, stage and screen adaptation, and audience memory. Holmes's appearance is not merely descriptive detail; it works like visual branding that lets the character remain identifiable across retellings.

## Key Characteristics

- Illustrated Holmes for the mass magazine environment associated with the character's success.
- Helped establish the detective's recognizable costume, posture, and silhouette.
- Added visual conventions that became canonical even when not fully specified by Doyle.
- Demonstrates that illustration can share authorship of a fictional character's public image.

## Evidence

- Visual canon: [[85-sherlock-holmes-glt8342867383]] credits Paget's illustrations with a major role in Holmes's success and recognizable appearance.
- Text-image divergence: [[85-sherlock-holmes-glt8342867383]] notes that the familiar deerstalker-and-pipe iconography does not simply reproduce Doyle's descriptions.
- Adaptation durability: [[85-sherlock-holmes-glt8342867383]] compares Holmes's distinctive look with superhero iconography and later screen recognizability.

## Qualifications

The episode summarizes Paget's importance without cataloguing individual illustrations or separating every element of Holmes iconography by artist, actor, production, and date. Paget's major role should not be treated as sole authorship of the visual tradition.

## What Changed

- Added Paget as a visual co-producer of Holmes's canonical public image.
