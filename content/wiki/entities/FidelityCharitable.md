# Fidelity Charitable

## Overview
Fidelity Charitable is a public charity that sponsors donor-advised funds and appears in the bounded source as a recipient of nearly 10% of the [[MuskFoundation]] distributions reviewed for roughly mid-2020 through 2024.

## Current Profile
Its role in this source is infrastructural rather than accusatory. A transfer to a sponsored donor-advised fund reserves assets for future charitable grants and can count as a current distribution by a private foundation, but public reporting may not reveal the eventual recipient with the same clarity. Fidelity Charitable therefore makes the difference between legal charitable commitment and publicly traceable final deployment concrete.

## Key Characteristics
- Sponsors donor-advised accounts through a public-charity structure.
- Receives charitable assets before an adviser recommends later grants.
- Can provide administrative flexibility and delayed grant selection.
- Creates a public traceability gap between the contributing foundation and eventual recipients.

## Evidence
- Musk Foundation transfer: [[effective-non-altruism-elon-musks-foundation-6ae30277305baddee189d087]] reports that nearly 10% of reviewed foundation distributions went to Fidelity Charitable.
- Transparency role: [[effective-non-altruism-elon-musks-foundation-6ae30277305baddee189d087]] says subsequent donor-advised-fund distributions are subject to limited public reporting, making final destinations harder to follow.

## Qualifications
The source does not allege misconduct by Fidelity Charitable and does not identify the particular accounts, advisers, final grants, timing, fees, or outcomes. Reduced public traceability does not mean that assets cease to be legally restricted to charitable purposes.
