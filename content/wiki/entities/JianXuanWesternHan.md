## Overview

减宣 / Jian Xuan enters the wiki as the official who investigates the disclosed [[LuYezhuWesternHan|鲁谒居]]–[[LiWenWesternHan|李文]] affair while carrying his own prior grievance against [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 338-2 portrays Jian Xuan as both a reputedly fair investigator and an interested opponent. Once Lu Yezhu's brother reveals the covert accusation against Li Wen, Jian pursues Zhang's role aggressively. The episode says he withholds an early report while gathering more material because he fears [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] may protect Zhang if the case reaches the throne too soon.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han investigating official in the Zhang Tang case.
- Presented by the episode as known for fair handling.
- Has a prior grievance against Zhang Tang.
- Pursues the Lu Yezhu–Li Wen chain intensively.
- Delays reporting while trying to build a stronger case.

## Qualifications

The raw transcript's “贤轩” is normalized to 减宣 from the transmitted Zhang Tang account. Fairness, grievance, fear of imperial interference, and the reason for delayed reporting are presented through this compact retelling; the page does not infer a complete judicial philosophy or settle whether personal resentment affected the investigation's accuracy.
