## Overview
Pink Bungalow is the Ridgewood boutique in [[bogg-bag-kim-vaccarella-the-100-million-business-she-almost-abandoned-e1aadd32-1dc4-43fb-b654-32148b3f6cee]] that placed the first known retail order for [[BoggBag]]. Its role in the wiki is a small-retailer validation node rather than a full independent company profile.

## Current Profile
The source presents Pink Bungalow as the first practical market test for [[KimVaccarella]]'s beach-bag idea. Buyer Mary Frances ordered after seeing the prototype pink bag, and later reorders indicated that the product could sell in boutique retail before Bogg Bag had mature ecommerce or marketing infrastructure.

## Key Characteristics
- Pink Bungalow acted as the first boutique channel for Bogg Bag.
- Its order forced Kim to improvise wholesale and retail prices before formal margin planning.
- Its reorders gave early evidence that the product had retailer and customer pull.

## Evidence
- **First order:** [[bogg-bag-kim-vaccarella-the-100-million-business-she-almost-abandoned-e1aadd32-1dc4-43fb-b654-32148b3f6cee]] says Kim took the pink prototype to Pink Bungalow, where buyer Mary Frances placed the first order.
- **Pricing improvisation:** [[bogg-bag-kim-vaccarella-the-100-million-business-she-almost-abandoned-e1aadd32-1dc4-43fb-b654-32148b3f6cee]] says Kim chose $30 wholesale and $60 retail without formal margin calculations.
- **Retail validation:** [[bogg-bag-kim-vaccarella-the-100-million-business-she-almost-abandoned-e1aadd32-1dc4-43fb-b654-32148b3f6cee]] says Pink Bungalow sold and reordered bags, including for another location.

## Qualifications
The episode gives only a source-scoped account of Pink Bungalow's role in Bogg Bag's origin. It does not provide a broader history of the boutique, its full customer base, or its later relationship with Bogg Bag.
