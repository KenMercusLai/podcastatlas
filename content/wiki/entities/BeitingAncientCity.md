# 北庭故城 / Beiting Ancient City

北庭故城 / Beiting Ancient City is the Xinjiang site recommended by [[ZhangXingyun|张星云]] in [[ep264-tafang-tangshi-zhilu-shengtang-qixiang-shaonianxin-loo2dfuxffizpbl4si5nwcixnngi]] for understanding [[CenShen|岑参]] and Tang frontier poetry. The episode links the site to Beiting service, the Tianshan view, Cheshi routes, and poems that came from lived western-region geography rather than from inherited frontier imagination alone.

In the source, Beiting matters as a place where literary reading and archaeological geography meet. It helps turn [[TangFrontierPoetryFieldExperience|边塞诗的实地经验]] into a site-specific branch of [[TangPoetryCulturalGeography|唐诗文化地理]].
