# 刘琦 / Liu Qi (Late Han)

## Overview
刘琦 / Liu Qi is the [[LiuBiao|刘表]] heir whose Jingzhou-inspector title provides a legitimacy channel for [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s post-Red-Cliffs expansion into southern Jingzhou.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1109 presents Liu Qi mainly through the political use of his name. Liu Bei recommends him as Jingzhou inspector and operates under that title while receiving the surrender of four southern commanderies, converting a succession-linked claim into territory, revenue, and appointments.

## Key Characteristics
- Supplies a recognized Jingzhou succession claim after Liu Biao's death.
- Makes Liu Bei's southern advance legible as provincial continuity rather than naked seizure alone.
- Functions in the current source as a legitimacy-bearing figure more than an independent commander.

- Administrative consequence: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1109-xizaochi-nu-pi-caocao-duonian-jiye-hui-yu-yidan-lukll8jb60nx2brhwhsw7gfrd-kt|Hanji 1109]] treats the acquired tax base as the start of Liu Bei's durable regional power.

## Qualifications
The episode does not provide a full Liu Qi biography or resolve how much initiative belongs to Liu Qi himself. Its emphasis is on the legitimacy value of his office in Liu Bei's expansion.
