# 刘琦 / Liu Qi (Late Han)

## Overview
刘琦 / Liu Qi is the [[LiuBiao|刘表]] heir whose Jiangxia force helps [[LiuBei|刘备]] recover after Changban and whose later Jingzhou-inspector title provides a legitimacy channel for southern expansion.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1094 supplies Liu Qi's immediate military-support layer: he leads Jiangxia troops north to meet Liu Bei around Hanjin, helping scattered forces regroup toward Xiakou after Changban. Hanji 1109 then presents the political use of his name. Liu Bei recommends him as Jingzhou inspector and operates under that title while receiving the surrender of four southern commanderies, converting a succession-linked claim into territory, revenue, and appointments.

## Key Characteristics
- Brings Jiangxia support into Liu Bei's post-Changban recovery.
- Supplies a recognized Jingzhou succession claim after Liu Biao's death.
- Makes Liu Bei's southern advance legible as provincial continuity rather than naked seizure alone.
- Functions in the current source as a legitimacy-bearing figure more than an independent commander.

## Qualifications
The sources do not provide a full Liu Qi biography or resolve how much initiative belongs to Liu Qi himself. Hanji 1094's route, force total, and command sequence remain episode-presented, while Hanji 1109 emphasizes the legitimacy value of his office more than independent rule.

## What Changed
- Extended the profile backward from post-Red-Cliffs legitimacy to post-Changban military support.
