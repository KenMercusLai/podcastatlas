# 刘琦 / Liu Qi (Late Han)

## Overview
刘琦 / Liu Qi is the [[LiuBiao|刘表]] heir who escapes a dangerous succession center through the Jiangxia command, later helps [[LiuBei|刘备]] recover after Changban, and supplies a legitimacy channel for southern expansion.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1091 supplies the origin layer. Liu Qi lacks the maternal and military backing of the pro-[[LiuCongLateHan|刘琮]] coalition and presses [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] for advice. The Shen Sheng-Chong'er analogy directs him outside the succession center, and [[HuangZu|黄祖]]'s death lets him obtain [[JiangxiaCommanderyLateHan|Jiangxia]] as a capacity-preserving refuge.

Hanji 1094 supplies the immediate military payoff: Liu Qi leads Jiangxia troops north to meet Liu Bei around Hanjin, helping scattered forces regroup toward Xiakou after Changban. Hanji 1109 then presents the political use of his name. Liu Bei recommends him as Jingzhou inspector and operates under that title while receiving the surrender of four southern commanderies, converting a succession-linked claim into territory, revenue, and appointments.

## Key Characteristics
- Leaves a hostile succession center through an external command rather than unprotected flight.
- Converts Jiangxia into personal protection and retained military relevance.
- Brings Jiangxia support into Liu Bei's post-Changban recovery.
- Supplies a recognized Jingzhou succession claim after Liu Biao's death.
- Makes Liu Bei's southern advance legible as provincial continuity rather than naked seizure alone.
- Functions in the current source as a legitimacy-bearing figure more than an independent commander.

## Qualifications
The sources do not provide a full Liu Qi biography or resolve how much initiative belongs to Liu Qi himself. Hanji 1091's household genealogy, exact dialogue, and concealed Liu Bei-plan interpretation remain source-scoped. Hanji 1094's route, force total, and command sequence remain episode-presented, while Hanji 1109 emphasizes the legitimacy value of his office more than independent rule.

## What Changed
- Extended the profile backward to the succession danger, Zhuge Liang's indirect counsel, and the Jiangxia appointment that creates Liu Qi's later capacity.
