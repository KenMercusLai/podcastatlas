# Li Auto MEGA / 理想 MEGA

## Overview

Li Auto MEGA / 理想 MEGA is a pure-electric multipurpose vehicle used in the bounded source to discuss product narrative, public-opinion crisis, correction, charging infrastructure, and an unexpectedly broad private-space use case.

## Current Profile

[[LiXiangLiAuto|李想]] presents MEGA's launch as a double failure surface. He attributes part of the reaction to organized hostile imagery and commentary, while also accepting that an attack can succeed when product expression, launch readiness, or public response is insufficient. The company's reported response included seating and accessibility changes, price and upgrade remedies for existing users, a larger communications function, and expansion of Li Auto's charging network.

MEGA also complicates a narrow family-MPV profile. Li says some single users buy it as a high-quality private space and some owners use it to receive important guests. These user shares and owner-vehicle comparisons are useful hypotheses, not independently verified market segmentation.

## Key Characteristics

- Pure-electric MPV positioned around high-quality shared or private space.
- Launch-crisis case combining hostile narratives with internal product-expression and response weaknesses.
- Product-correction case involving seating, accessibility, price, and upgrade remedies.
- Charging-network-dependent product whose viability is tied to route coverage and replenishment experience.
- Source-scoped user-profile story requiring independent sales and usage evidence.
