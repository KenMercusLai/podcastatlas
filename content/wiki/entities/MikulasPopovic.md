# Mikulas Popovic

## Overview

Mikulas Popovic is an NIH virologist presented by the bounded source as having helped make HIV cultivation in the laboratory possible. His later misconduct case provides a close comparison with [[MargitHamosh]] because investigators treated ambiguous scientific notation and editing as evidence of intentional deception.

## Current Profile

Popovic used “ND” in a table to mean that results were not determinable, while a footnote added with editorial assistance defined the initials as “not done.” The episode reports that resolving this discrepancy consumed more than 7,000 hours of interviews and deliberations, depleted his savings, and contributed to his departure from the NIH.

His case does not establish that notation disputes are always innocent. It shows why [[ScientificMisconductDueProcess]] must reconstruct authorship, editing, disciplinary usage, intent, and material effect before turning a textual inconsistency into a fraud finding.

## Key Characteristics

- Virologist whose work supported laboratory cultivation of HIV.
- NIH scientist working in a high-stakes AIDS-era research environment.
- User of “ND” to mean “not determinable” in the disputed table described by the episode.
- Subject of a prolonged misconduct inquiry centered on a conflicting editorial footnote.
- Comparative case for the professional costs of treating language ambiguity as intentional deception.
