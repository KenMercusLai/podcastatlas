## Overview

曹襄 / Cao Xiang is presented in Hanji 344-2 as the first husband of [[WeiZhangPrincessWesternHan|卫长公主]], a descendant of 曹参, a son of 平阳公主, and a military figure who accompanied [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] on campaign. The supplied file writes “曹湘”; this page uses the established “曹襄” form.

## Current Profile

The episode introduces Cao Xiang only to establish the princess's marital history before [[LuanDaWesternHan|栾大]]. It says he and the princess were cousins, reports that they had a son, and places his death when the princess was twenty-four. His military and lineage details explain why her first marriage already joined prominent service and imperial kinship, but the current evidence does not support a complete career narrative.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han elite identified as a descendant of Cao Can.
- Son of Pingyang Princess in the supplied account.
- First husband and reported cousin of Princess Wei the Elder.
- Military figure said to have campaigned under Wei Qing.
- Father of a reported son before an early death.

- **Military role:** [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-344-2-hanwudi-dangnian-weihe-yaozhan-qinnvxu-llmavvgeaaxwcsz_pxirfulbfpaa|Hanji 344-2]] says he accompanied Wei Qing on campaign.

## Qualifications

The current profile rests on one short summary and does not independently verify the exact Cao Can genealogy, parentage, cousin relationship, military office, campaign, death date, age statement, or the son's canonical name. “曹湘” is normalized to 曹襄 from the surrounding Western Han kinship and campaign context, but the original form remains recorded in the source note.
