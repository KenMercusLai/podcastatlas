# Matthew Coriotti

## Overview
Matthew Coriotti is presented as an AI-safety nonprofit fellow who favors strict regulation and company liability over either ordinary development or an undifferentiated halt.

## Current Profile
Coriotti's policy position and career choice reinforce one another. He argues that AI companies should be heavily regulated and liable for harm, and says he declined an [[Amazon]] software-engineering job paying nearly $200,000 a year to take a nonprofit fellowship at roughly one-third of that salary because he believes the world is at risk.

## Key Characteristics
- Supports strong AI regulation and legal accountability for harms.
- Occupies an intermediate position between engineering-only responses and a complete halt.
- Made a large reported income tradeoff to work in AI safety.
- Presents the career change as a response to perceived global risk.
