# 诸坊 / Zhu Fang (Zhang Chu)

诸坊 / Zhu Fang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-3-chensheng-zhisi-weishenme-dele-minxin-hanle-renxin-lnx4zs-vsi3zeoixnqskkpexmr3|Qinji 129-3]] as one of [[ChenSheng|陈胜]]'s harsh internal inspectors inside [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]]. The source says Chen Sheng appoints him as zhongzheng while [[HuWuZhangChu|胡武]] serves as si guo, making them responsible for scrutinizing officials' and commanders' faults.

The episode presents Zhu Fang and Hu Wu as a fear mechanism. Returning generals are criticized for field orders, disliked people can be handled outside ordinary judicial process, and harsh treatment of people becomes evidence of loyalty. Zhu Fang therefore belongs to [[PunitiveInspectionLoyaltyTrap|苛察忠诚陷阱]], the rebel-regime counterpart to late Qin's [[DuzeExtractionPolitics|督责式压榨政治]].
