# SMIC

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-all-ins-2026-predictions-39681260]] adds SMIC as the foundry partner in Friedberg's bullish [[Huawei]] chip-stack prediction. The source does not add technical detail about SMIC's process capability; it uses the company as part of a 2026 investor thesis around domestic Chinese AI hardware and supply-chain resilience.

SMIC appears in [[ep86-mianzi-dizi-rizi-caibao-zhi-jiang-zhe-san-jian-shi-lukmnlkkuxvrvv12frosd9teg7bj]] as the heavy-asset semiconductor contrast to [[Nvidia]]. The episode presents SMIC as a strategically important Chinese wafer foundry whose statements show the burdens of fabrication: expensive equipment, depreciation, capital expenditure, financing needs, and the tension between industrial catch-up and near-term profitability.

[[ep270-yi-mei-xinpian-de-manchang-zhengtu-women-li-suanli-ziyou-haiyou-duoyuan-lm7lxlmcnjwnawtq-9typc-fnrci]] adds SMIC as the key domestic manufacturing node for [[DomesticAIChipCatchUp|domestic AI-chip catch-up]]. The source says external observers infer SMIC may have used older lithography routes to produce 7 nm-class chips, but treats the harder question as yield, cost, capacity, and whether domestic chips can become stable commercial substitutes for [[Nvidia]] systems.

## Source Position
- SMIC is used to explain why [[AssetLightVsHeavyAssetModels]] produce very different margins, asset structures, and cash-flow profiles.
- The episode says SMIC grew revenue in 2025 but saw profit pressure because manufacturing requires fabs, depreciation, and capacity expansion.
- The source frames SMIC's spending not only as a company-level return question but also as part of a national semiconductor self-sufficiency effort.
- [[TSMC]] is used as the benchmark for global foundry share and for why a follower cannot simply stop investing without falling further behind.
- EP270 shifts the emphasis from financial statements to [[PhotolithographyBottleneck|lithography]], process complexity, [[SemiconductorSupplyChain|supply-chain closure]], and the difference between demonstration production and economical volume manufacturing.
- The All-In source is a market prediction rather than a process-node assessment, so it does not resolve the existing yield, cost, and volume-manufacturing caveats.
