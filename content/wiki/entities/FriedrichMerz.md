# Friedrich Merz

Friedrich Merz is [[Germany]]'s chancellor in [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]]. The source presents him as initially underestimating public anger over [[JensSpahn]]'s surrogacy scandal before eventually forcing Spahn out of his parliamentary leadership role.

The episode treats Merz's response as a leadership and coalition-management test. Spahn had been a rival for [[ChristianDemocraticUnionGermany|CDU]] leadership but was useful in coalition talks and reform negotiations. Merz uses the scandal for a small reshuffle, but the source warns it may still hurt his party before eastern German state elections where [[AlternativeForGermany|AfD]] leads.
