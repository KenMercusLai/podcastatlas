# Matrix Partners China / 经纬中国

Matrix Partners China, or 经纬中国, appears in [[173-duihua-yao-song-shenjian-dongfang-kongjian-zaichufa-tiancai-shaonian-shinian-hou-1-173-1]] as the venture-capital firm where [[YaoSong]] worked part-time after the [[DeePhiTech]] acquisition. Yao says the role let him observe more directions and understand what other founders and investors were doing.

In the source, Matrix is not mainly a financing actor for Yao's companies. It is part of his direction-search process after a successful exit, connecting [[PostAcquisitionFounderIdentity]] to [[StartupTimingWindows]] and [[HardTechDirectionSelection]].
