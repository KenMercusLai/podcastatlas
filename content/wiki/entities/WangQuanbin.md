# 王全斌 / Wang Quanbin

Wang Quanbin / 王全斌 is a Song general in [[170-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-xia-958158115]]'s account of the conquest of Shu. The episode says that after victory, commanders turned promised rewards into plunder, coercion, and violence in Chengdu and the surrounding region.

His treatment of [[QuanShixiong|全师雄]] and the wider suppression of Shu soldiers are used to show that early [[NorthernSong|北宋]] unification cannot be reduced to a benevolent "王师" narrative. Even a strategically rational [[SongUnificationStrategy|先南后北]] policy could create local suffering when conquest discipline failed.
