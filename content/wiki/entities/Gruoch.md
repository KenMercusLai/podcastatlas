# Gruoch

## Overview

Gruoch was Macbeth's wife and queen, a royal-line descendant whose marriage strengthened his dynastic position and whose historical profile differs sharply from Lady Macbeth.

## Current Profile

[[104-macbeth-glt3313769671]] presents Gruoch as a descendant of Kenneth MacAlpin through a rival royal line. Her marriage to [[MacbethHistoricalKing|Macbeth]] therefore joined Moray power to another dynastic claim. She accompanied Macbeth on pilgrimage to Rome and appears in the episode's account of a charter as Queen of Scotland.

The source uses these details chiefly to restore Gruoch as a historical queen rather than to project [[MacbethPlay|Lady Macbeth's]] ambition, murder pressure, or guilt onto her.

## Key Characteristics

- Descendant of a rival branch of the Scottish royal kindred.
- Dynastic partner whose marriage strengthened Macbeth's claim.
- Queen associated with a stable enough reign for pilgrimage to Rome.
- Historical figure distinct from Shakespeare's Lady Macbeth.

## Evidence

- Dynastic evidence: [[104-macbeth-glt3313769671]] identifies Gruoch's descent and explains the political value of her marriage.
- Queenly evidence: [[104-macbeth-glt3313769671]] reports the joint Roman pilgrimage and a charter naming her queen.
- Reception evidence: [[104-macbeth-glt3313769671]] contrasts the historical record with the durable ambitious-wife archetype created by the play.

## Qualifications

This is a source-bounded profile, not a complete biography. The episode supplies little about Gruoch's independent activity, the charter's precise context, her life after Macbeth, or how much historical evidence survives beyond dynastic and queenly references.
