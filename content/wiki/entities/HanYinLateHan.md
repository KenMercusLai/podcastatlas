# 韩胤 / Han Yin (late Han)

韩胤 / Han Yin enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1016-sanguo-luanshi-li-chengui-jiazu-de-mingyun-jueze-ltuckdcxpyxk-xmzp8rt-2gjfzcs|Hanji 1016]] as [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s envoy to [[LyuBu|吕布]]. He tells Lü Bu that Yuan Shu has claimed imperial status and proposes a marriage between Yuan Shu's eldest son and Lü Bu's daughter.

Han Yin's mission initially works: Lü Bu agrees and sends his daughter with him toward Yuan Shu's side. After [[ChenGui|陈珪]] persuades Lü Bu that the marriage would expose [[Xuzhou|徐州]] and attach Lü Bu to an unjust camp, Lü Bu recalls his daughter, cancels the alliance, and sends Han Yin to [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s court. The source says Han Yin is executed and displayed in the market, making him an envoy caught between marriage diplomacy, factional reversal, and anti-usurpation punishment.
