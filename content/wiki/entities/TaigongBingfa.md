# 《太公兵法》 / Taigong Bingfa

《太公兵法》 / Taigong Bingfa enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-123-2-gudai-shiqi-de-daochamen-daodi-you-duo-weixian-lgcknwubwm3uzqmzgbgdlxlvcwxy]] as the military book [[HuangshiGong|黄石公]] gives to [[ZhangLiang|张良]]. The episode says Zhang Liang studies it day and night after the bridge encounter, using it to become a deep strategic thinker.

The source does not analyze the text as a bibliographic object. Its role is narrative and formative: the gift connects Zhang Liang to the older [[JiangTaigong|姜太公 / 姜子牙]] strategy tradition and prepares his later identity as a counselor rather than only an anti-Qin avenger.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-4-jiuquangui-zhangliang-ruhe-zai-liubang-shouxia-anshen-lp6qwg3ma5hdy15g4box7pe23jav|Qinji 129-4]] gives the book's first Liu Bang payoff. Zhang Liang repeatedly explains Taigong Bingfa to [[LiuBang|刘邦]], and Liu Bang can understand and adopt the counsel. The episode therefore turns the book from Zhang Liang's private formation text into a medium of [[StrategicRecognitionAlignment|谋略知己式归附]] between strategist and future ruler.
