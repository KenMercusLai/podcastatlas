# Li Auto L9 LEVIUS / 理想 L9 LEVIUS

## Overview
Li Auto L9 LEVIUS / 理想 L9 LEVIUS is the flagship SUV discussed in [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]]. [[LiXiangLiAuto|李想]] presents it as a product bridge from the original [[LiAutoL9|理想 L9]] family-SUV positioning toward [[LiAuto|理想汽车]]'s AI and embodied-intelligence strategy.

## Current Profile
The source describes L9 LEVIUS through three major changes: full wire-control chassis and active suspension, [[LiAutoM100Chip|马赫 M100]] compute, and a new generation of range-extension plus battery capability. Li argues that these upgrades matter not only for premium comfort, but also for faster vehicle perception-to-execution loops and future L3/L4 autonomous driving.

## Key Characteristics
- Flagship SUV used to narrate Li Auto's shift from family comfort toward high-compute physical AI.
- Equipped, according to the source, with the M100 chip and 2560 TOPS compute.
- Uses full wire-control chassis and active suspension as both safety/control and comfort claims.
- Positioned as a lifecycle asset whose high compute can support future model upgrades.
- Source-scoped product whose real user experience and autonomous-driving readiness remain to be verified after delivery.

## Evidence
- Product setup: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] opens by naming L9 LEVIUS as Li Auto's new-generation flagship SUV.
- Chassis and suspension: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] says full wire-control braking can shorten electronic brake response and make wheel-level control more independent, while active suspension can improve large-vehicle comfort.
- Compute: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] ties the vehicle to the M100 chip and argues that higher compute enables larger models, higher frame rates, and longer vehicle lifecycle growth.
- Vehicle AI: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] says delivery will include a system-level agent architecture for generalized tasks, information access, vehicle control, record keeping, and personalization.
