# Christof Koch

## Overview
Christof Koch is a neuroscientist presented in the source as a researcher and public interpreter of consciousness who connects brain circuits and clinical measurement with first-person altered states and philosophical questions.

## Current Profile
Koch's most stable contribution in this source is conceptual separation: consciousness is experience itself and should not be equated with intelligence, behavior, communication, or self-consciousness. He connects that distinction to corticothalamic circuitry, perturbational complexity, and [[CovertConsciousness|covert consciousness]], where a person may retain experience and command-following brain activity without visible response.

His broader profile is deliberately mixed. He uses [[PerceptionBox|perception boxes]] to discuss constructed reality, empathy, therapy, and social conflict, while his [[5MeODMT|5-MeO-DMT]] and mystical experiences lead him toward idealism and a personal view of death. The source presents those metaphysical judgments as consequential to him, not as results established by his empirical methods.

## Key Characteristics
- Defines consciousness by lived experience rather than outward performance.
- Separates consciousness from intelligence and the experienced sense of self.
- Connects consciousness research to clinically consequential assessment of unresponsive patients.
- Uses first-person altered states as philosophical evidence while continuing to distinguish them from laboratory measurement.
- Treats curiosity, direct experience, empathy, and revisable priors as ways to loosen rigid perception.

## Evidence
- Conceptual distinctions - [[scim8616450810-scim8616450810]] records Koch's separation of consciousness, behavior, intelligence, and self-consciousness across sleep, dreams, flow, meditation, and anesthesia.
- Empirical program - [[scim8616450810-scim8616450810]] describes corticothalamic circuits, perturbation with transcranial magnetic stimulation, high-density EEG, response complexity, and covert command following.
- Perception and change - [[scim8616450810-scim8616450810]] presents the perception-box model and links revisable priors to direct experience, therapy, community, belief, and curiosity.
- Personal metaphysics - [[scim8616450810-scim8616450810]] attributes selfless psychedelic and mystical experiences, reduced fear of death, and a turn toward idealism to Koch.

## Qualifications
The supplied document summarizes a long public interview rather than providing study protocols or a verbatim transcript. Koch's psychedelic and mystical reports may inform hypotheses about selfhood, but they do not establish idealism, survival after death, treatment efficacy, or a universal model of consciousness.
