# 公子纠 / Gongzi Jiu

公子纠 / Gongzi Jiu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-75-2-qiongshe-jichi-de-zushiye-guanzhong-lo8uei2u1qwj9tszftel1b4lswoa]] as [[QiHuanGong|姜小白]]'s rival in the succession race after [[GongsunWuzhi|公孙无知]] is killed. [[GuanZhong|管仲]] serves Gongzi Jiu, while [[BaoShuya|鲍叔牙]] serves Jiang Xiaobai.

The source makes Gongzi Jiu important less as a full biographical figure than as the defeated pole of Guan Zhong's choice. Guan Zhong attacks Jiang Xiaobai on his behalf, but after Gongzi Jiu fails and is killed, Guan Zhong accepts service under the victorious Qi Huan Gong instead of dying like [[ShaoHu|召忽]].
