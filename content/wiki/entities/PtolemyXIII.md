# Ptolemy XIII

## Overview

Ptolemy XIII was the younger brother and joint ruler of [[CleopatraVII|Cleopatra VII]] whose court displaced her, killed [[Pompey]], resisted [[JuliusCaesar|Julius Caesar]] during the [[AlexandrianWar]], and collapsed after his death by drowning.

## Current Profile

[[196-julius-caesar-cleopatra-part-2-glt3216275988]] presents Ptolemy as a child king operating through powerful ministers, especially Pothinus and Achillas. Their court excluded Cleopatra from Alexandria, blocked her return near Pelusium, and chose to murder Pompey in the hope of avoiding retaliation from either Roman civil-war faction.

Caesar rejected the court's attempted settlement and summoned both royal siblings. Ptolemy became the dynastic focus of opposition to Caesar and Cleopatra; after being released from the palace, he joined the anti-Caesarian army and drowned following its defeat.

## Key Characteristics

- Child co-ruler whose authority was mediated by senior courtiers.
- Dynastic rival whose faction forced Cleopatra from Alexandria.
- Nominal ruler under whose government Pompey was murdered.
- Rallying figure for Egyptian resistance to Caesar's palace occupation.
- Defeated claimant whose death enabled Cleopatra's restoration.

## Evidence

- Court control and succession conflict: [[196-julius-caesar-cleopatra-part-2-glt3216275988]] identifies Pothinus and Achillas as dominant advisers who excluded Cleopatra and opposed her return.
- Pompey's murder: [[196-julius-caesar-cleopatra-part-2-glt3216275988]] attributes the decision to Ptolemy's council and describes the king witnessing its result.
- Alexandrian opposition: [[196-julius-caesar-cleopatra-part-2-glt3216275988]] follows Ptolemy from Caesar's custody into the anti-Caesarian force.
- Death and settlement: [[196-julius-caesar-cleopatra-part-2-glt3216275988]] reports his drowning and Cleopatra's subsequent rule with Ptolemy XIV.

## Qualifications

The source does not isolate Ptolemy's own agency from that of his ministers. His reactions, the council's exact deliberation, the circumstances of his release, and the details of his drowning depend on later narrative evidence.

## What Changed

- Established Ptolemy XIII as a constrained child monarch and dynastic rallying point rather than treating his court as a unitary personal regime.

## Relationships

- [[CleopatraVII]] - sister and co-ruler displaced by his court, then restored after his defeat.
- [[JuliusCaesar]] - Roman arbiter and military opponent during the succession crisis.
- [[Pompey]] - Roman refugee murdered by Ptolemy's advisers.
- [[ArsinoeIV]] - sister who became a separate rival claimant during the war.
- [[AlexandrianWar]] - conflict in which his faction opposed Caesar and Cleopatra.
- [[PtolemaicEgypt]] - kingdom whose succession crisis made his court dependent on Roman outcomes.
