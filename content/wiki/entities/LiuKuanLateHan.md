# 刘宽 / Liu Kuan (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-907-suoyou-de-bing-doushi-ni-de-xinbing-ltqzyohcauduwuanlvqph9l81d79|Hanji 907]] places Liu Kuan in the 176 CE senior-office churn before the failed Xianbei expedition. The source says he moves from guanglu xun to taiwei after the previous taiwei is removed, making his Hanji 909 dismissal part of a longer unstable office sequence rather than a single isolated omen event.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-909-2025-ruhe-yangcheng-yi-shen-guiqi-lomndmphrybunjbbbbn9ebafcsj|Hanji 909]] introduces Liu Kuan as the taiwei removed after the tenth-month first-day solar eclipse that follows the failed 177 CE Xianbei expedition. The source gives him an office-accountability role rather than a developed policy voice: responsibility shifts upward through omen-linked removal while the defeated commanders redeem themselves from prison.
