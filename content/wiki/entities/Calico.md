# Calico

[[Calico]] is the life-sciences company [[BillMaris|Bill Maris]] says he founded and helped incubate while at [[Google]] in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-maris-how-google-could-crush-ai-competitors-why-small-funds-win-and-ais-atari-stage-41586010]]. The source uses Calico to explain why Maris is interested in [[ComputationalBiology]] and human health, while still remaining cautious about clinical development timelines.

In the episode, Calico matters less as a complete company profile than as evidence that Maris's AI and venture views extend into biology. His caution is that drug discovery, titration, safety testing, human biology, and FDA processes keep life-sciences progress tied to validation and regulation even when computation improves.
