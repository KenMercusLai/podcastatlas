## Overview

许平君 / Xu Pingjun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-540-taihou-de-zangli-lnd6gmr_bk7dv8cbb1zzoagtbwsd|Hanji 540]] as [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]'s earlier empress and the birth mother of crown prince [[HanYuandi|刘奭 / 汉元帝]].

## Current Profile

The episode uses Xu Pingjun as background to [[QiongchengTaihouWesternHan|邛成太后]]'s elevation. After Xu was poisoned by the Huo family, Emperor Xuan sought a cautious and childless consort to serve as empress and raise Liu Shi. Xu therefore matters in this source less through her own court activity than through the succession-protection problem her death created.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han empress linked to Emperor Xuan.
- Mother of Liu Shi, the future Emperor Yuan.
- Poisoned by the Huo family according to the episode's background summary.
- Her death creates the need for a politically safer foster-empress arrangement.

## Evidence

Mother of the crown prince:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-540-taihou-de-zangli-lnd6gmr_bk7dv8cbb1zzoagtbwsd|Hanji 540]] says Xu Pingjun was the birth mother of crown prince Liu Shi.

Poisoning and aftermath:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-540-taihou-de-zangli-lnd6gmr_bk7dv8cbb1zzoagtbwsd|Hanji 540]] says Emperor Xuan pitied the crown prince after Xu Pingjun was poisoned by the Huo family and chose a cautious childless consort to raise him.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 540's background use. It does not reconstruct Xu Pingjun's full biography, the full Huo-family poisoning case, or the later political memory of her death.
