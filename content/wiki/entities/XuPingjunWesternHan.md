## Overview

许平君 / Xu Pingjun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-540-taihou-de-zangli-lnd6gmr_bk7dv8cbb1zzoagtbwsd|Hanji 540]] as [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]'s earlier empress and the birth mother of crown prince [[HanYuandi|刘奭 / 汉元帝]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-459-hanxuandi-weishenme-shuo-taizi-liushi-shi-baijiazi-lsnb6mesdpegwhihmxx06fy8fawz|Hanji 459]] adds her memory as one reason Xuan does not replace Liu Shi with [[LiuQinHuaiyangKingWesternHan|淮阳王刘钦]] despite doubting Liu Shi's fitness. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-415-xihan-zhaoguanghan-ruhe-daji-xiangba-cunba-heie-shili-ltnvaopr2hv0jx-v0oii3l9rjaur|Hanji 415]] adds a remembered conduct layer: even as empress, Xu is described as frugal in carriages and clothing.

## Current Profile

The episode set uses Xu Pingjun less through her own court activity than through the succession-protection problem her death created and the moral memory attached to her. Hanji 415 contrasts her low-background frugality with [[HuoChengjunWesternHan|霍成君]]'s elite luxury after Huo becomes empress. Hanji 540 uses Xu's poisoning by the Huo family to explain why Emperor Xuan sought a cautious and childless consort, [[QiongchengTaihouWesternHan|邛成太后]], to raise Liu Shi. Hanji 459 uses the same emotional and moral memory differently: Xu's family had supported Xuan during hardship, Xu died before enjoying long security as empress, and Liu Shi had been endangered by the Huo family, so Xuan's possible replacement of Liu Shi becomes more than a cold personnel decision.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han empress linked to Emperor Xuan.
- Mother of Liu Shi, the future Emperor Yuan.
- Remembered as frugal in palace display despite becoming empress.
- Poisoned by the Huo family according to the episode's background summary.
- Her death creates the need for a politically safer foster-empress arrangement.
- Her memory helps restrain Xuan's later impulse to replace Liu Shi with Liu Qin.

## Evidence

Mother of the crown prince:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-540-taihou-de-zangli-lnd6gmr_bk7dv8cbb1zzoagtbwsd|Hanji 540]] says Xu Pingjun was the birth mother of crown prince Liu Shi.

Frugality and memory:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-415-xihan-zhaoguanghan-ruhe-daji-xiangba-cunba-heie-shili-ltnvaopr2hv0jx-v0oii3l9rjaur|Hanji 415]] says Xu Pingjun came from a low-status commoner background and remained frugal in carriages and clothing as empress.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-415-xihan-zhaoguanghan-ruhe-daji-xiangba-cunba-heie-shili-ltnvaopr2hv0jx-v0oii3l9rjaur|Hanji 415]] uses Xu as the contrast case for Huo Chengjun's more lavish empress display.

Poisoning and aftermath:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-540-taihou-de-zangli-lnd6gmr_bk7dv8cbb1zzoagtbwsd|Hanji 540]] says Emperor Xuan pitied the crown prince after Xu Pingjun was poisoned by the Huo family and chose a cautious childless consort to raise him.

Succession restraint:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-459-hanxuandi-weishenme-shuo-taizi-liushi-shi-baijiazi-lsnb6mesdpegwhihmxx06fy8fawz|Hanji 459]] says Xuan's gratitude and guilt toward Xu Pingjun and her family weigh against replacing Liu Shi even after Xuan considers Liu Qin.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 540, Hanji 459, and Hanji 415's background uses. It does not reconstruct Xu Pingjun's full biography, the full Huo-family poisoning case, or the later political memory of her death beyond the succession-protection, replacement-restraint, and frugality-contrast roles those sources assign her.
