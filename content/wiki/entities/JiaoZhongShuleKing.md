## Overview

叫中 / Jiao Zhong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-756-weishenme-quan-ni-de-rao-ren-chu-qie-rao-ren-ltn4tqzy1d-sodhjidncv8dyqxpj|Hanji 756]] as the [[ShuleKingdom|Shule]] ruler who borrows troops from Kangju and then offers a false surrender to [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]]. His failed trap gives Ban Chao's Western Regions profile an earlier Shule counter-deception layer.

## Current Profile

The source presents Jiao Zhong as a ruler trying to hold Shule against Ban Chao by using external backing and deception together. He borrows soldiers from Kangju, keeps his position, and sends an envoy offering surrender.

Ban Chao reads the offer as false and accepts it outwardly. When Jiao Zhong arrives with young fighting men, Ban Chao turns the encounter against him and executes him. Ban then defeats Jiao Zhong's followers, after which the episode says the Western Regions southern route becomes unobstructed.

Jiao Zhong is therefore important less as an independent biography than as a tactical foil. His false surrender marks the kind of frontier situation where "forbearance" cannot mean credulous acceptance; Ban Chao's restraint is bounded by intelligence and timing.

## Key Characteristics

- Shule ruler in the source's 86 CE Western Regions layer.
- Uses Kangju military support to hold position against Han pressure.
- Attempts false surrender through an envoy.
- Executed when Ban Chao turns the surrender meeting into a counter-trap.
- Tactical foil for Ban Chao's threat reading and counter-deception.
