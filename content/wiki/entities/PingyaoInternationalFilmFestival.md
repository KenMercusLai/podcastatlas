# 平遥国际电影展 / Pingyao International Film Festival

Pingyao International Film Festival is the festival context in [[ep277-duihua-jiazhangke-xia-wo-meiyou-beipan-zhenshi-shijie-wo-zhishi-zai-xunzhao-dianying-de-xin-keneng-lqprbtgi7pkch3hj3wxa1q8wovox]]. [[JiaZhangke|贾樟柯]] says its seventh edition included AI-related elements, used AI-generated posters, and adopted a "摩登时代" theme to connect [[CharlieChaplin|卓别林]]'s industrial-line image with AI's possible entrance into film and ordinary life.

In the wiki, the festival functions as a testing ground rather than only an event listing. The source uses it to show how a film institution can make AI visible before the production norms, rights boundaries, and aesthetic standards of AI cinema have stabilized.
