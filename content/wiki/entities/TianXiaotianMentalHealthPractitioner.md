# 田小甜 / Tian Xiaotian (Mental-Health Practitioner)

## Overview
田小甜 is the mental-health practitioner interviewed in EP67 of [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]] about psychiatry, psychotherapy, clinical social work, common psychiatric conditions, client support, and the occupational challenges of counseling.

## Current Profile
The source describes Tian as having previously supported trafficking survivors with trauma-focused cognitive behavioral work and later serving a broader client population. She presents clinical social work as attentive to both psychological patterns and the person's housing, relationships, daily environment, services, and case-management needs.

Her public-education stance combines normalization with limits. She emphasizes function, collaboration with psychiatry, listening before advising, client agency, therapist self-awareness, and risk recognition, while repeatedly acknowledging uncertainty or lack of specialist authority around hypnosis, unusual perceptions, and anecdotal claims.

## Key Characteristics
- Mental-health practitioner explaining psychotherapy through clinical-social-work and practical-life context.
- Former anti-trafficking worker using trauma-focused cognitive behavioral approaches with survivors.
- Advocate for collaboration among psychiatry, psychotherapy, counseling, family support, and social services.
- Client-agency advocate who compares the therapist to a navigator rather than the driver.
- Practitioner attentive to countertransference, emotional load, supervision, continuing education, and crisis risk.
