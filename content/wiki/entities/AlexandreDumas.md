# Alexandre Dumas

## Overview

Alexandre Dumas is the novelist whose Three Musketeers sequence gave the [[ManInTheIronMask]] its best-known royal-twin form.

## Current Profile

[[411-the-man-in-the-iron-mask-glt3787137343]] uses Dumas as the entry point into the mystery and then tests his dramatic world against contemporary evidence. D'Artagnan and [[SaintMars]] were historical figures, but the identical-twin plot in *The Vicomte of Bragelonne* belongs to literary transformation rather than documented identity. Dumas's importance to the case is therefore cultural: he made one speculative royal theory feel like the default memory of a much less certain prisoner.

## Key Characteristics

- Historical novelist working with real names, institutions, and invented plot.
- Popularizer of the identical-twin version of the iron-mask story.
- Major bridge between early modern archival mystery and modern cultural memory.

## Evidence

### Historical-fiction boundary
