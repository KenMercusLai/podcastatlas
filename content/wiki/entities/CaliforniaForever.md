# California Forever

## Overview
California Forever is the investor-backed proposed city used in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-76efcd195d1b26a4faab0a64b31c1972-52966951-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-76efcd195d1b26a4faab0a64b31c1972-52966951]] as a live test of large-scale housing development under local political constraints.

## Current Profile
The project acquired tens of thousands of acres and proposed 170,000 homes between San Francisco and Sacramento. After a county-level route was blocked, its developers pursued annexation into Suisun City; the project then became a central mayoral-election issue and remained contested when the episode was produced.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines concentrated private capital, land assembly, and dense new-city planning.
- Seeks housing scale large enough to test mass-production and infrastructure coordination.
- Remains dependent on local political legitimacy and land-use authority.
- Shifted institutional strategy after its initial county-level proposal was blocked.
