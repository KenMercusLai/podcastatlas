# NFL Films

NFL Films is the football film and storytelling institution in [[the-nfl-1]] that turned game footage into mythology, emotion, and reusable league media. The episode traces it to [[EdSabol]]'s filming of the 1962 NFL Championship and [[PeteRozelle]]'s later decision to bring film and enterprise functions into the league structure.

In the source's business frame, NFL Films is not just archive footage. It is part of the NFL's [[SportsEntertainmentFlywheel]]: cinematic presentation increased fan attachment, which increased media value, which funded better presentation and league growth.
