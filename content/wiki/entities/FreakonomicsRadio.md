# Freakonomics Radio

## Overview
Freakonomics Radio is the economics and public-policy podcast that produced episode 695, "Can the U.S. Close Its Fertility Gap?"

## Current Profile
In [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-58f17fc3725ef3102277ae962324f7dd-59875813-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-58f17fc3725ef3102277ae962324f7dd-59875813]], the show uses disagreement among [[MelissaKearney]], [[LymanStone]], and [[NickBloom]] to map low fertility without presenting a single causal explanation or policy cure. Its organizing move is to separate population targets from whether people can realize their own family intentions.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects personal choices with labor, housing, and public policy.
- Preserves disagreement about financial incentives and remote-work effects.
- Distinguishes evidence, interpretation, and speculation in the supplied summary.
