# Gordie Howe Bridge

The Gordie Howe Bridge is the [[Detroit]]-[[WindsorOntario|Windsor, Ontario]] bridge profiled in [[outsider-chance-ending-sudans-proxy-war-6a75b3695d765781a2b1b132]]. [[AnnieCrabill]] presents it as a long-planned, mutually useful trade link that gives trucks a new crossing beside the older [[AmbassadorBridge]].

The episode says the Detroit-Windsor crossing handles about $94bn in annual trade and that [[Canada]] paid the C$6bn construction cost upfront, expecting to recover costs through tolls. The bridge becomes a [[CrossBorderInfrastructureTradeTension]] case because [[DonaldTrump]] attacked the project, demanded a larger U.S. stake, and a later agreement in principle would send half of net profits to an American fund for 15 years.
