# Pingtouge

Pingtouge is Alibaba's chip team, referenced in [[1-yi-token-julebu-jibaole-ai-de-ranliao-bugoule-duitan-yu-wenyuan-aliyun-bailian-jishu-fuzeren-ltn5k9jd9e04i5mfdkdo-ycoslsm]] as part of [[AliyunBailian]]'s end-to-end infrastructure stack. [[YuWenyuan]] says Bailian's experience using Pingtouge chips has been especially smooth compared with other domestic compute he had not personally used.

[[guochan-ai-suanli-neng-ping-chaojiedian-wandao-chaoche-ma-waic-shendu-guancha-s10e23-a6c6ab3e-72b2-470b-aefd-04b19679d37f]] adds Pingtouge to the domestic AI-chip order-validation frame. The source groups large-platform-linked chip teams such as Pingtouge and [[Kunlunxin]] among the vendors more likely to get workloads, engineering feedback, and major customer opportunities than smaller standalone firms.

## Source Position

- Used as evidence that [[Alibaba]] can coordinate models, infrastructure, compute scale, and chip-level work inside one platform.
- Treated positively on software-hardware integration and practical deployment experience.
- The episode still frames China's AI constraint as aggregate compute supply, not only the quality of any single domestic chip team.
- The WAIC supernode source adds that chip-team strength must become deployable [[AIAcceleratorSupernode|supernode]] capacity and major customer orders.
