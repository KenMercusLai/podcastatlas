# Julie Osk

Julie Osk appears in [[tech-20260721-0721-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260721-0721-mp-tech-pod-128]] as a technology analyst at [[OskAdvisory|Osk Advisory]] explaining why AI infrastructure demand is pushing large technology companies into the corporate bond market. She frames the issue through demand for AI tokens and compute, data-center capex outrunning free cash flow, and the fit between long-term bonds and long-lived physical infrastructure.

Her contribution to the wiki is a bond-market version of [[AIInfrastructureDebtFinancing]]. Osk does not argue that borrowing is automatically irrational; she says debt can match data-center asset life and preserve flexibility. The risk is that interest rates, uncertain AI demand, regulation, competition with China, public backlash, water and power access, and return-on-investment discipline may all change how investors price the same buildout.
