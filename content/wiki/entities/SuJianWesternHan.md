## Overview

苏建 / Su Jian is presented as a Western Han general whose defeat leads to capital review, redemption from a death sentence, loss of status, and later restoration as governor of Dai Commandery. He is also identified as the father of [[SuWuWesternHan|苏武]], but the current page keeps his own command-accountability and return-to-office trajectory central.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-299-2-xihan-caizheng-da-weiji-hanwudi-ruhe-gao-qian-fqf4vv3uhemqey3qqk6cdiht_2cc|Hanji 299-2]] places Su Jian under [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]] in an anti-[[Xiongnu|匈奴]] campaign and says he is sent back to the capital after battlefield failure. [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] reportedly spares him from death after payment of redemption money and reduces him to commoner status.

The episode then gives the punishment a nonfinal endpoint: Su Jian later becomes governor of Dai Commandery and dies in office. The sequence makes him a compact case of severe military accountability coexisting with financial redemption and later reuse, while the current evidence does not explain the legal mechanism or the reasons for restoration.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han commander serving under Wei Qing in war against Xiongnu.
- Defeated officer returned to the capital for judgment rather than executed in the field.
- Beneficiary of death-penalty redemption who is reduced to commoner status.
- Later appointee as governor of Dai Commandery, showing restoration after punishment.
- Father of Su Wu in the episode's biographical bridge.

## Qualifications

The current evidence is one compact structured summary rather than a full biography or legal study. Su Jian's precise command title, battlefield responsibility, review procedure, original sentence, redemption amount, interval before restoration, Dai Commandery record, death circumstances, and complete family remain source-scoped. Later appointment shows reuse after punishment but does not by itself establish why the court restored him or whether the earlier judgment was reconsidered.
