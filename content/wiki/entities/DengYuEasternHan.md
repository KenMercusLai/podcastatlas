# 邓禹 / Deng Yu (Eastern Han)

## Overview

Deng Yu is presented in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-1-lishi-suo-yiwang-de-diwang-zhishi-mingjun-liuzhuang-lor7vhzzpwz1rv9tijksogukjqvr|Hanji 733-1]] as the elder founding minister whom [[EmperorMingOfHan|Han Mingdi]] places in the Taifu role at accession. His page currently functions as a succession-support profile rather than a full biography.

## Current Profile

The source frames Deng Yu through Mingdi's first political self-presentation. Mingdi says he is young, lacks a previous emperor to consult, and needs a virtuous elder. Naming Deng Yu as Taifu therefore does more than distribute office: it gives the new reign a visible elder support structure after [[LiuXiu|Guangwu]]'s death.

Deng Yu's death later in the same episode closes that immediate accession layer. The note does not expand his earlier founding career, but it makes his final Mingdi-era function clear: he embodies continuity between Guangwu's founding generation and the second Eastern Han emperor.

## Key Characteristics

- Elder founding minister used to stabilize Mingdi's accession.
- Taifu appointment supplies ritual and political seniority at the start of the reign.
- His significance in this evidence layer is transitional rather than operational.
- Death in the episode marks the thinning of direct Guangwu-era elder support.
