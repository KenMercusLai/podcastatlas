# 邓禹 / Deng Yu (Eastern Han)

## Overview

Deng Yu / 邓禹 is synthesized as an Eastern Han founding minister whose current wiki role spans two ends of the founding generation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-715-shishang-zui-zhihui-de-yici-duoquan-loqkawl247maw9fn-nomaw5gzkgu|Hanji 715]] presents him as a high-merit commander who accepts post-unification withdrawal into honored status, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-1-lishi-suo-yiwang-de-diwang-zhishi-mingjun-liuzhuang-lor7vhzzpwz1rv9tijksogukjqvr|Hanji 733-1]] presents him as the elder founding minister whom [[EmperorMingOfHan|Han Mingdi]] places in the Taifu role at accession.

## Current Profile

Hanji 715 supplies the postwar Guangwu layer. After unification, [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] rewards Deng Yu as Gaomi marquis, and the episode says Deng Yu understands Liu Xiu's desire to set aside warfare and govern through ritual and civilian order. Deng Yu therefore gives up military authority while retaining rank, income, honor, and special court access. The source also uses his household as a restrained elite model: he has thirteen sons, has each learn a skill, maintains strict family discipline, and relies on fief income rather than side business.

Hanji 733-1 frames Deng Yu through Mingdi's first political self-presentation. Mingdi says he is young, lacks a previous emperor to consult, and needs a virtuous elder. Naming Deng Yu as Taifu therefore does more than distribute office: it gives the new reign a visible elder support structure after [[LiuXiu|Guangwu]]'s death.

Deng Yu's death later in Hanji 733-1 closes that immediate accession layer. The page now holds a longer arc: postwar withdrawal under Guangwu, then symbolic elder support at Mingdi's accession.

## Key Characteristics

- Founding minister rewarded as Gaomi marquis after Liu Xiu completes unification.
- High-merit actor who accepts military authority withdrawal without public disgrace.
- Representative of [[HonorsWithoutOfficeSettlement|honors without office]] through rank, fief income, and limited court access.
- Household-discipline model whose sons are trained in skills and whose estate avoids added commercial accumulation.
- Elder founding minister used to stabilize Mingdi's accession.
- Death under Mingdi marks the thinning of direct Guangwu-era elder support.

## Evidence

- Postwar reward and withdrawal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-715-shishang-zui-zhihui-de-yici-duoquan-loqkawl247maw9fn-nomaw5gzkgu|Hanji 715]] says Deng Yu is rewarded as Gaomi marquis and joins the commanders who surrender military authority after unification.
- Civilian-governance alignment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-715-shishang-zui-zhihui-de-yici-duoquan-loqkawl247maw9fn-nomaw5gzkgu|Hanji 715]] says Deng Yu and Jia Fu understand Liu Xiu's desire to put down weapons and govern through ritual and instruction.
- Household discipline: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-715-shishang-zui-zhihui-de-yici-duoquan-loqkawl247maw9fn-nomaw5gzkgu|Hanji 715]] presents Deng Yu's sons, skill training, strict family management, and reliance on fief income as part of his postwar profile.
- Accession elder support: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-1-lishi-suo-yiwang-de-diwang-zhishi-mingjun-liuzhuang-lor7vhzzpwz1rv9tijksogukjqvr|Hanji 733-1]] records Mingdi's youth-and-guidance rhetoric and the appointment of Deng Yu as Taifu.
- Founding-generation continuity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-1-lishi-suo-yiwang-de-diwang-zhishi-mingjun-liuzhuang-lor7vhzzpwz1rv9tijksogukjqvr|Hanji 733-1]] places Deng Yu beside [[LiuCangDongpingKing|Liu Cang]] in the opening personnel settlement of the new reign.
- Terminal notice: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-1-lishi-suo-yiwang-de-diwang-zhishi-mingjun-liuzhuang-lor7vhzzpwz1rv9tijksogukjqvr|Hanji 733-1]] notes Deng Yu's death, making this page's current evidence a late-life Mingdi accession layer.

## Qualifications

This page still does not reconstruct Deng Yu's full military or ministerial career. Hanji 715 gives a post-unification settlement and household-discipline layer, while Hanji 733-1 gives his late symbolic role in Mingdi's accession. Exact campaign chronology, office history before the settlement, and later family consequences remain outside the current bounded evidence.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 715, extending Deng Yu from late accession elder into an earlier Guangwu postwar withdrawal and honor-settlement case.
- Reframed the page from a narrow Mingdi succession-support profile into a two-stage founding-generation arc.
