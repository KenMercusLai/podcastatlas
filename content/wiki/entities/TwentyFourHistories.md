## Overview
二十四史 / Twenty-Four Histories is introduced in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-591-2-ruhe-geng-gaoxiao-di-dudong-lishi-gudai-biannian-jianzan-juequ-qiannian-fengliu-yunyan-lruv48fgfpo7u0tjsqb264k0y7yq|Hanji 591-2]] as the broad dynastic official-history corpus that gives listeners a map for locating [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] among older Chinese historical texts.

## Current Profile
The current wiki profile is source-scoped and introductory. The episode presents the Twenty-Four Histories as a huge archive running from legendary antiquity through the Ming, covering politics, economy, military affairs, ideas, culture, astronomy, geography, and institutions. Its function in the episode is not to analyze the corpus in detail, but to show why one cannot read Zizhi Tongjian as an isolated book: it sits inside a long tradition of official history, dynastic memory, and governance-oriented historical learning.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as the episode's macro-map for Chinese official historiography.
- Represents dynastic and civilizational continuity across regime change.
- Contains many domains beyond court narrative, including military, economy, ideas, culture, astronomy, geography, and institutions.
- Is presented as too large for casual linear completion, making selective reading and source-map awareness necessary.
