# Eugene McCarthy

## Overview

Eugene McCarthy was the anti-war Democratic senator whose 1968 New Hampshire challenge weakened [[LyndonBJohnson|Lyndon B. Johnson]], opened the nomination race, and made [[RobertFKennedy|Robert F. Kennedy]]'s later entry vulnerable to charges of opportunism.

## Current Profile

The episode casts McCarthy as both the candidate who took the first major risk against an incumbent president and Kennedy's bitter rival for the anti-war vote. His restrained, intellectual style and support among educated white liberals contrasted with Kennedy's celebrity, emotional crowds, and stronger minority and poorer-voter coalition. McCarthy lost Indiana, defeated Kennedy in Oregon, and then lost the decisive California primary, but their rivalry divided opposition to [[HubertHumphrey|Hubert Humphrey]] and made Democratic succession a contest over legitimacy and personality as well as Vietnam.

## Key Characteristics

- Anti-war senator who challenged Johnson before Kennedy entered the race.
- New Hampshire performer whose result helped make Johnson's position untenable.
- Cerebral campaigner with particular appeal among educated white liberals.
- Fierce Kennedy antagonist who framed the later entrant as opportunistic and insufficiently courageous.
- Oregon victor and California runner-up in the primary sequence covered by the source.
