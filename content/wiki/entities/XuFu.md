# Xu Fu / 徐福

Xu Fu / 徐福 appears in [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997]] as the Qin fangshi attached to sea voyages for immortal medicine. The episode uses him as a compact case of [[ImmortalityQuestPolitics|求仙政治]]: eastern-sea immortal-island lore, imperial funding, explanations for failure, and later origin legends all gather around one figure.

The source keeps the later destination stories source-scoped. Versions that place Xu Fu in Japan, the Americas, Zhoushan, or elsewhere are treated as legend history rather than established fact.

## Key Claims
- Xu Fu's story shows how immortal-island geography could mobilize imperial resources.
- Failure can be managed narratively through obstacles, distance, danger, or non-return.
- Later destination legends show how fangshi stories keep accumulating cultural functions.
- Xu Fu is more useful here as a node in the fangshi-imperial bargain than as a verifiable migration founder.
