# David Railton

## Overview

David Railton was a British army chaplain and Military Cross recipient whom the episode credits with proposing the burial of the [[UnknownWarrior]] in Westminster Abbey.

## Current Profile

[[118-end-of-the-first-world-war-remembrance-glt2743125909]] places Railton at the origin of a symbol able to hold both individual grief and national scale. His proposal transformed the problem of unidentified battlefield death into a representative burial: one unknown serviceman could stand for many missing men without privileging rank, family, or a known heroic biography.

The episode also associates Railton with later care for poor returning veterans, linking ceremonial remembrance to the material failures faced by injured, unemployed, or impoverished ex-servicemen.

## Key Characteristics

- Army chaplain with First World War service.
- Awarded the Military Cross in the episode's account.
- Proposed bringing an unknown serviceman to Westminster Abbey.
- Connected Christian pastoral experience to national public ritual.
- Later associated with care for impoverished veterans.
