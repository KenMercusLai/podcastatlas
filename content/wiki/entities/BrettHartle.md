# Brett Hartle

Brett Hartle appears in [[tech-20260818-tech-pod-128-tech-20260818-tech-pod-128]] as the [[CenterForBiologicalDiversity]] critic of the proposed [[FederalAviationAdministration|FAA]] launch-review waiver. He argues that the timing makes the proposal look like a benefit for [[SpaceX]], especially if the company moves toward a new southern [[Louisiana]] Gulf spaceport.

Hartle's source-scoped objection has two parts. First, he warns that wetlands and federally protected species could be harmed if [[SpaceLaunchEnvironmentalReview]] is weakened. Second, he argues that Congress has not given the FAA authority to waive environmental obligations so broadly, making litigation possible.
