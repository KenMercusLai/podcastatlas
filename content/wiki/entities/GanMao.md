# 甘茂 / Gan Mao

甘茂 / Gan Mao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2]] as the Qin figure sent to suppress [[ChenZhuangWarringStates|陈庄]] after Chen kills [[ShuHouNingTong|蜀侯宁通]] and rebels in [[ShuStateWarringStates|蜀国]].

The episode gives Gan Mao a compact role: he enters Shu in the following year, kills Chen Zhuang, and restores Qin control. His function is to show that Chen Zhuang's terrain-based confidence underestimated [[QinState|秦国]]'s ability to project force into [[BaShu|巴蜀]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-57-gudai-fa-dushi-haiyou-zhe-zuoyong-lj6wi61osaytowgmdp8dtkrwb-si]] turns Gan Mao from a compact suppressor into Qin's left chancellor and a campaign-risk theorist. After [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] sends him to [[WeiState|魏国]] to arrange a joint attack on [[HanState|韩国]], Gan Mao reports Wei's agreement but warns that [[YiyangWarringStates|宜阳]] will be hard to take quickly.

His argument is political as much as military. Gan Mao uses the [[ZengShen|曾参]] and [[LeYang|乐羊]] stories to explain [[RumorErosionOfTrust|谣言侵蚀信任]], predicts that [[Chulizi|樗里疾 / 樗里子]] and the Gongsun faction will attack him if the siege drags on, and gets Qin Wuwang to swear at Xirang before the campaign. In this source, Gan Mao therefore anchors [[OathBoundCommandTrust|盟誓约束下的统帅信任]]: a commander exposes predictable slander in advance and turns the ruler's backing into a public constraint.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-58-zhaowulingwang-ruhe-dazao-qiangguo-lq9mgpib0whh0w-jkv0kxcafbnnz]] confirms the value of that constraint. After five months without taking Yiyang, Gan Mao faces the predicted court pressure, invokes Xirang, receives reinforcements, takes the city, and forces [[HanState|韩国]] to seek peace. The source therefore turns his earlier risk model into a completed campaign case.
