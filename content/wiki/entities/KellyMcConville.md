# Kelly McConville

## Overview

Kelly McConville is the Reed College statistics professor who led the class reconstruction of the U.S. News college-ranking model in [[lord-of-the-rankings-lord-of-the-rankings|Lord of the Rankings]].

## Current Profile

McConville's role in the episode is to turn suspicion about Reed's declining rank into a testable statistical question. Her class uses disclosed variables and public government data to approximate the hidden weighted model, then evaluates how closely its predictions match published results.

The analysis reportedly fits nearly every tested institution but not [[ReedCollege]], whose 2019 published rank of 90th contrasts with a reconstructed placement around 36th. It also identifies peer assessment as the largest modeled influence, with a reported 6.58-point overall-score association for a one-unit assessment increase while other factors are held constant.

Her contribution is therefore an audit method rather than a final accusation: reproduce what can be inferred, identify the residual that demands explanation, and preserve the boundary between an anomaly and proof of its cause.

## Key Characteristics

- Leads a statistics class investigating a consequential opaque ranking system.
- Uses public data and disclosed variables to reconstruct a weighted model.
- Tests the model across institutions rather than reasoning from Reed alone.
- Identifies Reed as an exceptional prediction error.
- Separates statistical anomaly from proof of deliberate treatment.
