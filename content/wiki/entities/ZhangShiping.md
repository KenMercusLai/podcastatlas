# 张诗平 / 张诗坪 / Zhang Shiping

## Overview
张诗平 / 张诗坪 appears in the wiki as the [[HuzuoHuyou|忽左忽右]] guest discussing [[ZhuluQinHanBook|《逐鹿：神话与预言背后的秦亡汉兴》]], [[QinCollapseOverextension|Qin collapse]], and [[ChuHanWarDemythologizing|楚汉战争去神话化]] from a military-history angle.

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents Zhang as a historical interpreter who rereads familiar Qin-Han stories through war organization, logistics, geography, political incentives, source comparison, and Eurasian military context. He challenges simplified accounts of [[XiangYu|项羽]] as merely brave and politically stupid, [[LiuBang|刘邦]] as merely lucky, [[JuluBattle|巨鹿之战]] as a one-stroke miracle, and [[HanXin|韩信]] as a supernatural strategist. His method keeps dramatic source traditions visible while asking what troop numbers, supply routes, state capacity, and coalition incentives would make the events coherent.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses military history to de-mythologize Qin-Han narrative traditions.
- Treats battle outcomes as products of logistics, troop quality, command structure, and politics rather than bravery alone.
- Re-evaluates Xiang Yu as a violent and capable power player instead of a simple tragic aristocrat.
- Treats Liu Bang's victory as resource and organization accumulation across multiple fronts.
- Connects Qin-Han warfare to wider Eurasian military-technology questions while marking those claims as inferential.
