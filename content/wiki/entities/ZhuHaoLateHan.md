# 朱皓 / Zhu Hao (late Han)

朱皓 / Zhu Hao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-995-ta-zuishang-cibei-weihe-duoci-cansha-enren-llicukoezccd5iycin4uq7qun51k|Hanji 995]] as [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]]'s newly appointed [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]] administrator. Liu Yao sends him to attack [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s appointee [[ZhugeXuan|诸葛玄]], who retreats to Xicheng.

Zhu Hao's vulnerability is named before the disaster: [[XuShao|许劭]] warns that [[ZeRong|笮融]] lacks trust and Zhu Hao is too straightforward. Liu Yao still sends Ze Rong to support him; Ze Rong deceives and kills Zhu Hao, then occupies Yuzhang. The episode makes Zhu Hao the third major case in Ze Rong's repeated betrayal pattern.
