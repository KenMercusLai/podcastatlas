# Angela Merkel

[[AngelaMerkel|Angela Merkel]] appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-rahm-emanuel-trumps-foreign-policy-china-europes-decline-immigration-dsa-vs-democrats-42416310]] as [[RahmEmanuel|Rahm Emanuel]]'s counterexample to a simple left-versus-right explanation of Europe's problems. When the hosts argue that open borders, welfare pressure, cultural dilution, and energy policy helped weaken Europe, Emanuel points out that Merkel came from Germany's [[ChristianDemocraticUnionGermany|Christian Democratic Union]] rather than from the left.

In the source, Merkel is tied to the Syrian-refugee decision and Germany's nuclear shutdown path. Emanuel's point is not a full biography of Merkel or a complete assessment of those policies; it is a source-scoped warning that [[EuropeanUnion|European]] stagnation, immigration disorder, and energy mistakes should not be flattened into a single partisan label.
