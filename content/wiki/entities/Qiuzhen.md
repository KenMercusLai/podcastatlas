# 求珍 / Qiuzhen

## Overview
Qiuzhen is a Chinese mid- to high-end bag brand discussed as one of the "山下有松们" competing for the domestic light-luxury bag market. The available evidence frames it around functional commuter products, a rising average selling price, and a narrative of independent, steady, intellectual femininity.

## Current Profile
Qiuzhen appears in the source as a domestic brand that is no longer positioned mainly as a cheaper alternative to imported accessible luxury. Its growth is tied to the same premiumization formula as [[ShanxiaYousong|山下有松]] and [[Gele|各乐]]: higher prices, domestic supply-chain capability, story-led identity, and a shift toward offline environments that can make material and craft feel credible. The brand's clearest narrative anchor is its partnership around the film character Wang Tiemei, played by Song Jia in `好东西`, which connects a mailbag format to a stable, independent urban-woman image.

## Key Characteristics
- Qiuzhen is positioned as a Chinese mid- to high-end bag brand in the domestic light-luxury price band.
- Its online sales reportedly grew by more than 300% year over year during 2026 618.
- Its average price reportedly rose from about RMB 1,400 to about RMB 1,700.
- Its brand narrative links commuter utility to independent, steady, intellectual female identity.
- Its offline strategy is shifting from destination streets toward shopping centers and core malls.
- Its premium claim depends on whether product quality, store experience, and design distinctiveness can keep pace with rising prices.

- Offline channel: [[yue-mai-yue-gui-de-shanxia-yousong-men-ruhe-chixu-qiangzhan-qingshe-baodai-shichang-1008110631]] places Qiuzhen in Shanghai Tianzifang and Suzhou Pingjiang Road, then says the brand plans to focus more on shopping centers and core malls.
- Market context: [[yue-mai-yue-gui-de-shanxia-yousong-men-ruhe-chixu-qiangzhan-qingshe-baodai-shichang-1008110631]] groups Qiuzhen with Shanxia Yousong and Gele as domestic brands benefiting from weak imported accessible-luxury excitement and luxury price fatigue.

## Qualifications
Qiuzhen is represented here through one source, so the profile should be treated as source-scoped until additional revenue, margin, retention, and store-level evidence appears. The same source also warns that the domestic bag field is crowded, with many brands using similar low-logo, neutral, values-driven cues; that makes design originality and service execution more important as prices rise.
