# Lucian of Samosata / 刘善（卢奇安）

## Overview

Lucian of Samosata is the Roman-imperial Greek-language satirist represented here through [[ATrueStory|《真实的故事》]], an openly invented voyage that parodies false travel writing, historical authority, and inherited literary conventions.

## Current Profile

The episode places Lucian around the second century CE and presents him as a reader of Homeric, Platonic, mythic, historical, and travel traditions. His method is to declare that the coming account is impossible, then narrate it with exact distances, durations, troop numbers, customs, and political detail. The mismatch makes serious factual style itself an object of comedy.

His imagination changes the physical world more easily than the political one. Moon travel, interplanetary warfare, male pregnancy, cultivated bodies, and detachable organs coexist with monarchy, colonization, siege, tribute, hierarchy, and wealth. Lucian therefore functions in this source less as a technological prophet than as a satirist whose impossible worlds reveal durable habits of authority and power.

## Key Characteristics

- Greek-language writer situated in the Roman imperial world.
- Satirist of philosophers, religious imposture, false history, and knowledge authority.
- Combines epic, myth, travel narrative, comedy, and historiographic precision.
- Announces invention while using factual style to test the boundary between fiction and deception.
- Uses bodily and cosmic inversion to make ordinary assumptions newly visible.
- Imagines radical physical change while preserving recognizable political conflict and hierarchy.

## Qualifications

The page reflects one podcast's selective discussion of the lunar episode rather than Lucian's complete corpus or a specialist biography. The Chinese name 刘善 follows the supplied episode; other Chinese transliterations include 卢奇安. Dating, textual relationships, authorial intention, and the label “science fiction” require specialist support beyond this source.

## What Changed

- Established Lucian's profile as a declared fabulist and critic of false factual authority.
- Added the contrast between radical physical imagination and persistent political hierarchy.
