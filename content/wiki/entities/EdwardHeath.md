# Edward Heath

Edward Heath appears in [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] as the British prime minister who inherited the [[NorthernIreland]] crisis after the June 1970 election. The episode says Heath had little knowledge of or interest in Northern Ireland, even as Cabinet Secretary Burke Trend warned that the best case was a costly stalemate and the worst case could be civil war with possible Dublin intervention.

Heath is central to the source's governance failure because he tried to avoid direct rule while relying on [[Stormont]] leaders such as [[JamesChichesterClark]] and [[BrianFaulkner]]. He authorized [[InternmentWithoutTrial]] through [[OperationDemetrius]] despite warnings from the [[BritishArmy]], the [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]], and [[JackLynch]] that internment would likely fail and drive Catholics toward the IRA.
