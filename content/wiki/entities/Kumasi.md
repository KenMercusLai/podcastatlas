# Kumasi

## Overview

Kumasi is the Asante capital through which the episode makes state capacity, court display, outsider admiration, imperial destruction, and symbolic recovery spatially visible.

## Current Profile

The source links Kumasi's selection to [[OseiTutu]] and presents it as the center of an expanding militarized kingdom. British visitors describe broad or planned streets, cleanliness, waste collection, toilets, architecture, and lavish court ceremony that disrupted stereotypes of African political disorder. The same city later becomes a target: Garnet Wolseley's forces enter and destroy parts of it after the 1870s conflict, showing how admiration for sophistication could coexist with imperial violence.

## Key Characteristics

- Capital of [[Asante]].
- City whose selection is attached to Osei Tutu's founding tradition.
- Center of court ceremony, gold display, hierarchy, and administration.
- Described by British visitors as planned, clean, and architecturally impressive.
- Sacred-political capital partly destroyed during British military reprisals.

## Evidence

- Founding and centrality: [[269-ghana-the-ashanti-empire-glt3638102749]] links Kumasi's selection to Osei Tutu and the consolidation of Asante power.
- Urban profile: [[269-ghana-the-ashanti-empire-glt3638102749]] reports British praise for its streets, sanitation, architecture, palace, and court display.
- Imperial attack: [[269-ghana-the-ashanti-empire-glt3638102749]] says Wolseley admired the city but still blew up parts of it.

## Qualifications

The page reflects one podcast's synthesis of British observer accounts. Sanitation practices, palace dates and materials, legal penalties, military destruction, and observer motives require specialist corroboration and should not be generalized to every period of the city.

## What Changed

- Created Kumasi as the urban center joining Asante sophistication, sovereignty, and imperial attack.

- [[Ghana]] - modern country containing Kumasi and the continuing Asante institution.
