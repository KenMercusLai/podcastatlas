## Overview

《太平经》 / Taipingjing enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-581-han-aidi-zai-guishen-zhidao-you-duo-yuchun-ltok7kbzmuf-os84spseww0qjbrm|Hanji 581]] as the text [[GanZhongkeWesternHan|甘忠可]] uses to present renewed Heaven authorization, dynastic continuity, and social peace as a cure for imperial crisis.

## Current Profile

The source treats the Taipingjing as a politically usable sacred text rather than as a fully reconstructed religious corpus. Gan Zhongke uses it to tell [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] that childlessness signals Liu-family mandate decline and that renewed Heaven authorization can restore the line. [[XiaHeliangWesternHan|夏贺良]] later preserves and reactivates this approach under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]], where the text's promise becomes tied to illness, heirs, disaster relief, reign-title change, imperial-title change, and altered timekeeping.

## Key Characteristics

- Serves as the textual vehicle for Gan Zhongke's renewed-mandate claim.
- Connects cosmic order, dynastic continuity, heir production, and "great peace" into one political promise.
- Fails under Chengdi because Liu Xiang's skeptical counsel blocks the proposal.
- Reappears under Aidi as part of a much more damaging symbolic reset.

## Qualifications

This page is intentionally source-scoped. It does not identify the received Daoist Taipingjing corpus with the episode's described text in a settled philological sense, nor does it reconstruct authorship, dating, transmission, or doctrinal history beyond Hanji 581.
