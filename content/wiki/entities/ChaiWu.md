# 柴武 / Chai Wu

柴武 / Chai Wu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-167-jianzheng-xiangyu-zuizhong-zhizhan-2-li14pizmfrzhpg8m5bmxdx8ibw7p|Hanji 167 part 2]] as one of the commanders of the third line in [[HanXin|韩信]]'s [[GaixiaBattle|垓下之战]] formation. The source pairs him with [[ZhouBo|周勃]] over the final ten-ten-thousand-person line.

His wiki role is to mark the stopping point of [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s shock attack. Xiang Yu breaks through Han Xin's first line and [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s second line, but by the time he reaches the Zhou Bo / Chai Wu line, the Chu force is exhausted and cannot break the formation. Chai Wu therefore belongs to the final holding layer inside [[LayeredDepthDefense|纵深分层防御]].
