# 柴武 / Chai Wu

柴武 / Chai Wu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-167-jianzheng-xiangyu-zuizhong-zhizhan-2-li14pizmfrzhpg8m5bmxdx8ibw7p|Hanji 167 part 2]] as one of the commanders of the third line in [[HanXin|韩信]]'s [[GaixiaBattle|垓下之战]] formation. The source pairs him with [[ZhouBo|周勃]] over the final ten-ten-thousand-person line.

His wiki role is to mark the stopping point of [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s shock attack. Xiang Yu breaks through Han Xin's first line and [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s second line, but by the time he reaches the Zhou Bo / Chai Wu line, the Chu force is exhausted and cannot break the formation. Chai Wu therefore belongs to the final holding layer inside [[LayeredDepthDefense|纵深分层防御]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-182-weishenme-shuo-chengye-xiaohe-baiye-xiaohe-2-lqk-jifupm9lbghfzaf09igfufgb|Hanji 182 part 2]] gives Chai Wu a diplomacy-and-force role in the [[ChenXiRebellion|陈豨之乱]] cleanup. He presses [[HanWangXin|韩王信]] with [[ZhouBo|周勃]], then writes to Han Wang Xin urging surrender and arguing that [[LiuBang|刘邦]] has forgiven returnees before. Han Wang Xin rejects the appeal and is later defeated and killed at [[Canhe|参合]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-183-hanxin-zhisi-zhiyu-le-liubang-de-jingshen-neihao-lrbucdibto5zezrhl7ue0y9vhb-l|Hanji 183]] reuses that terminal action as part of the episode's same-year juxtaposition. Chai Wu's killing of Han Wang Xin at Canhe becomes one side of the host's "two Han Xins" framing, paired with the capital death of [[HanXin|韩信]].
