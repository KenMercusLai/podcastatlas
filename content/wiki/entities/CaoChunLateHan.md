# 曹淳 / Cao Chun (late Han)

## Overview
曹淳 / Cao Chun is a Cao-family elite-cavalry commander whose current evidence joins the final pursuit of [[YuanTan|袁谭]] at [[NanpiLateHan|南皮]] to the later pursuit and captures at [[ChangbanBattle|长坂坡之战]].

## Current Profile
In Hanji 1068, [[CaoCao|曹操]] struggles to finish the assault on Nanpi and considers easing the attack after heavy fighting. Cao Chun argues that Cao's force is deep in hostile territory, so a stalled assault or withdrawal would bring heavy losses and damage military prestige. Cao Cao accepts the warning, personally renews the attack, and takes the city.

After Yuan Tan flees, Cao Chun leads the Tiger and Leopard Cavalry in pursuit and kills him. The episode also identifies Cao Chun as part of Cao Cao's close kinship circle through [[CaoSong|曹嵩]]'s family line, making his role more than that of an interchangeable battlefield officer.

Hanji 1094 extends that pursuit profile to 208 CE. It associates Cao Chun's cavalry with overtaking Liu Bei's movement, capturing baggage and two daughters, and producing the family-loss layer later invoked in the Changban aftermath.

## Key Characteristics
- Cao-family commander presented as part of Cao Cao's close military circle.
- Gives tempo advice during the difficult Nanpi assault.
- Frames a deep operation's delay or retreat as a danger to force safety and military prestige.
- Leads elite cavalry pursuit after Nanpi falls.
- Kills Yuan Tan, closing the eldest-claimant branch of the Yuan-family succession conflict.
- Reappears at Changban as the pursuit commander associated with captives, baggage, and Liu Bei's family losses.

- Assault outcome: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1068-yuanshao-qushi-hou-caocao-shi-ruhe-xiaomie-yuantan-de-llebqjk-dz18ajvgcjkut7-qha1o|Hanji 1068]] links Cao Chun's advice to Cao Cao's renewed attack and capture of Nanpi.
- Pursuit and killing: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1068-yuanshao-qushi-hou-caocao-shi-ruhe-xiaomie-yuantan-de-llebqjk-dz18ajvgcjkut7-qha1o|Hanji 1068]] says Cao Chun leads the Tiger and Leopard Cavalry after the fleeing Yuan Tan and kills him.
- Kinship position: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1068-yuanshao-qushi-hou-caocao-shi-ruhe-xiaomie-yuantan-de-llebqjk-dz18ajvgcjkut7-qha1o|Hanji 1068]] identifies Cao Chun's father as a brother of Cao Song, placing Cao Chun in Cao Cao's core clan field.
- Changban captures: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1094-changbanpo-zhizhan-zhangfei-he-zhaoyun-ruhe-zai-zhanchang-fengshen-ltymdohev05pmxhwcdme9d2jymzk|Hanji 1094]] credits Cao Chun with capturing two of Liu Bei's daughters and baggage during the pursuit.

## Qualifications
This is a narrow source-scoped page, not a full Cao Chun biography. Exact title sequence, Tiger and Leopard Cavalry command structure, Changban pursuit speed, casualty claims, captive disposition, and the daughters' later fate require broader evidence; the Copper Bird Terrace story is not treated as established.

## What Changed
- Extended the pursuit profile from Nanpi to Changban.
- Added the capture of Liu Bei's daughters and baggage while rejecting unsourced later-fate claims.
