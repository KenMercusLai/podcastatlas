# 曹淳 / Cao Chun (late Han)

## Overview
曹淳 / Cao Chun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1068-yuanshao-qushi-hou-caocao-shi-ruhe-xiaomie-yuantan-de-llebqjk-dz18ajvgcjkut7-qha1o|Hanji 1068]] as a Cao-family cavalry commander in the final campaign against [[YuanTan|袁谭]] at [[NanpiLateHan|南皮]]. The source makes him both an adviser on campaign tempo and the officer who overtakes and kills Yuan Tan.

## Current Profile
In Hanji 1068, [[CaoCao|曹操]] struggles to finish the assault on Nanpi and considers easing the attack after heavy fighting. Cao Chun argues that Cao's force is deep in hostile territory, so a stalled assault or withdrawal would bring heavy losses and damage military prestige. Cao Cao accepts the warning, personally renews the attack, and takes the city.

After Yuan Tan flees, Cao Chun leads the Tiger and Leopard Cavalry in pursuit and kills him. The episode also identifies Cao Chun as part of Cao Cao's close kinship circle through [[CaoSong|曹嵩]]'s family line, making his role more than that of an interchangeable battlefield officer.

## Key Characteristics
- Cao-family commander presented as part of Cao Cao's close military circle.
- Gives tempo advice during the difficult Nanpi assault.
- Frames a deep operation's delay or retreat as a danger to force safety and military prestige.
- Leads elite cavalry pursuit after Nanpi falls.
- Kills Yuan Tan, closing the eldest-claimant branch of the Yuan-family succession conflict.
