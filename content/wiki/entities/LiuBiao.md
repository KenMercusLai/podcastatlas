# 刘表 / Liu Biao

刘表 / Liu Biao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] as the regional power with whom [[ZhangXiu|张绣]] reconnects after the Wancheng attack and subsequent defeat by [[CaoCao|曹操]]. The episode does not develop Liu Biao's own policy; it uses him as the fallback alignment that keeps Zhang Xiu from becoming simply a defeated remnant.
