# Michel de Castelnau

Michel de Castelnau appears in [[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] as the French ambassador in [[England]] whose embassy became a channel for [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]]' cipher correspondence. The episode says he had been sent after the [[StBartholomewsDayMassacre]] to improve Anglo-French relations.

Castelnau matters because formal diplomacy and clandestine politics overlap in his London embassy. [[FrancisWalsingham]] had moles at Salisbury Court, obtained copies of Mary-Castelnau correspondence, and later used the [[ThrockmortonPlot]] exposure to show how foreign embassies could become security risks.

The source keeps Castelnau's role inside the diplomatic channel rather than making him the plot's main designer. [[FrancisThrockmorton]]'s confession implicated him, but [[HenryDukeOfGuise|Guise]], [[BernardinoDeMendoza]], and Mary's claimant status carry the larger invasion logic.
