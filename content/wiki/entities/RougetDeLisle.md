# Claude Joseph Rouget de Lisle

## Overview

Claude Joseph Rouget de Lisle was the French army engineer, officer, Freemason, and amateur composer who wrote the song later known as [[LaMarseillaise|La Marseillaise]].

## Current Profile

Rouget de Lisle composed the "Song of the Army of the Rhine" overnight in Strasbourg after a civic dinner on 25 April 1792. The episode treats his authorship as the beginning rather than the whole explanation of the song's importance: military circulation, printing, Jacobin reception, and performance by Marseille fédérés converted his local wartime commission into a revolutionary and national symbol.

## Key Characteristics

- He was a serving captain and engineer as well as a part-time composer.
- He wrote the song days after France declared war on Austria.
- His original title tied it to the Army of the Rhine rather than Marseille.
- The song's later political identity was created by circulation beyond his authorship.
