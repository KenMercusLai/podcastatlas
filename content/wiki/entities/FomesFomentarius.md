# Fomes fomentarius

Fomes fomentarius is the fungus identified by [[FilipAmtislavski]] in [[tech-20251224-1224-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251224-1224-mp-tech-pod-128]] as common across Arctic and subarctic regions. The source says its physical feel and sound resemble polystyrene foam enough to make it a useful starting point for [[MyceliumInsulation]] research.

In the wiki, Fomes fomentarius is a material-species node. The source connects it to [[Alaska]], [[UniversityOfAlaska]] research, and the larger question of whether biological binding can produce [[BiodegradableFoamPackaging]] that works in real [[SeafoodColdChainPackaging]].
