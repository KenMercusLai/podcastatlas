# Afra

## Overview
Afra is the technology journalist and writer who maps effective altruism, rationalism, and AI safety in the episode.

## Current Profile
Afra is an informed participant-observer rather than a formal movement representative. She has accepted AI-safety fellowship support and values the community's ability to turn worldviews into action, while declining the effective-altruist identity and questioning frontier AI's speed, opacity, and concentrated power.

## Key Characteristics
- Traces effective altruism through measurable charity, longtermism, post-FTX crisis, and AI safety.
- Separates effective altruism's moral project from rationalism's epistemic culture.
- Treats funding, fellowships, jobs, venues, and networks as agenda-forming mechanisms.
- Supports a temporary slowdown while capability and risk remain poorly explained.
- Maintains critical distance from a community whose action orientation she values.
