# Credit Suisse / 瑞士信贷

## Overview
[[CreditSuisse|Credit Suisse / 瑞士信贷]] was the Swiss bank whose 2023 confidence crisis ended in an emergency acquisition by [[UBS|UBS / 瑞银]].

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Credit Suisse matters less as a continuing independent bank than as the failure that reshaped Swiss regulation. Losses and reputational damage linked to Greensill and Archegos accelerated client distrust, while the emergency rescue prevented a potentially wider crisis but left [[Switzerland|Switzerland]] with an even larger systemically important UBS.

## Key Characteristics
- Suffered repeated losses and trust damage before its 2023 rescue.
- Had material exposure to the Greensill and Archegos failures in the episode's account.
- Entered a self-reinforcing confidence crisis that required emergency state coordination.
- Was acquired by [[UBS|UBS]] in a transaction presented as systemic-crisis containment.
- Remains the causal reference point for stricter Swiss parent-bank capital proposals.

## Evidence
- Loss and confidence chain: [[shangye-xiaoyang-51-shenme-ruiyin-yaoba-zongbu-banli-ruishi-gkwrijiotaj1agndggtvuvds]] connects Greensill and Archegos losses to eroding client trust and an eventual emergency.
- Rescue outcome: [[shangye-xiaoyang-51-shenme-ruiyin-yaoba-zongbu-banli-ruishi-gkwrijiotaj1agndggtvuvds]] describes a roughly CHF3bn share transaction backed by urgent Swiss action and guarantees.
- Regulatory legacy: [[shangye-xiaoyang-51-shenme-ruiyin-yaoba-zongbu-banli-ruishi-gkwrijiotaj1agndggtvuvds]] presents the proposed UBS capital rule as a direct institutional response to the rescue.

## Qualifications
The source provides a compressed crisis narrative rather than a full balance-sheet, legal, or resolution history. Exposure values, losses, guarantee totals, motives, and counterfactual contagion are episode-attributed. The page does not treat every Credit Suisse business as failed or claim that the two named counterparties alone explain the bank's collapse.

## What Changed
- Established Credit Suisse as the failed institution behind the current Swiss capital-policy dispute.
- Connected named investment failures to confidence erosion without reducing the crisis to a single cause.
- Made the rescue's creation of a larger UBS part of the post-crisis risk picture.
