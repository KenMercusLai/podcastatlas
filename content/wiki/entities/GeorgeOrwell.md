# George Orwell / 乔治·奥威尔

[[socialist-mobility-a-telling-michigan-vote-6a74600ec3e1347e8b5bf216]] adds Orwell as a postcard-culture critic. [[CatherineNixie|Catherine Nixey]] cites him as criticizing comic postcards for vulgarity and hideousness, making him part of the source's [[PostcardMediaDecline]] and analog cultural-history branch.

George Orwell appears in [[51-yanshi-fanrenlei-tonghua-gushi-geliefu-youji-ke-shen-le-qu-le-632315320]] as a later reader of [[JonathanSwift|Jonathan Swift]] and [[GulliversTravels|《格列佛游记》]]. The episode says Orwell admired the third voyage's anticipations of technical domination, surveillance, and total control, especially around [[LaputaFlyingIsland|Laputa]].

At the same time, Orwell is presented as uncomfortable with the [[Houyhnhnms|慧骃]] society. The episode uses this discomfort to show why the fourth voyage cannot be read as a clean utopia: a purely rational and noble society may still erase the warmth and messiness that make human life humane.
