# Sara Gottfried

## Overview
Sara Gottfried is the physician and women's hormone-health guest in the [[HubermanLab]] Essentials episode on vitality, longevity, PCOS, contraception, perimenopause, and cardiometabolic risk.

## Current Profile
The current wiki profile is source-scoped to one episode. Gottfried presents female hormone health as a systems problem rather than a narrow sex-hormone checklist: genetics, intergenerational trauma, cortisol, thyroid function, metabolism, gut function, reproductive history, micronutrients, insulin, glucose, and symptoms all shape the phenotype. Her practical stance is early measurement plus individualized clinical interpretation, with stronger opinions around oral contraceptives, PCOS risk, perimenopause, and midlife coronary screening kept inside the source boundary.

## Key Characteristics
- She emphasizes individualized female hormone phenotyping across adolescence, reproductive adulthood, and perimenopause.
- She treats family history, trauma, cortisol, gut function, thyroid function, micronutrients, and metabolic markers as part of hormone interpretation.
- She links PCOS and insulin physiology to lifetime cardiometabolic risk rather than only fertility or reproductive-age symptoms.
- She supports reproductive choice while asking for fuller informed consent around oral contraceptive benefits, side effects, and alternatives.
- She treats perimenopause symptoms and midlife coronary calcium scoring as prevention signals, not nuisance details.
