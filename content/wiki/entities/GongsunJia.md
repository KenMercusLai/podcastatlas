# 公孙贾 / Gongsun Jia

公孙贾 / Gongsun Jia belongs to the [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] punishment branch around [[YingSi|太子嬴驷]]'s violation of the new Qin law. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-13-gongsunyang-keke-bianfa-re-shashen-2-lk-d9uibhneerfol6z2rrle-6ypm]] describes the prince's tutors being punished because the heir himself cannot be directly punished; in the standard later identification preserved by the wiki branch, Gongsun Jia is the tutor marked by facial tattooing.

The figure matters less as an independent biography than as evidence for [[HighStatusLawEnforcementSignal|高位执法信号]]. Punishing a prince's teacher makes the new law visible at the top of [[QinState|秦国]]'s hierarchy, but it also converts legal credibility into personal and elite resentment.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-25-zhaoliang-dui-shangyang-shuo-ni-bupei-lnqmwjbrc9qomwrdt5brourf-78p]] later uses Gongsun Jia's face tattoo in [[ZhaoLiang|赵良]]'s warning against Shang Yang. Alongside [[GongziQian|公子虔]]'s suffering and Zhu Huan's death, the tattoo becomes one of the named injuries showing why Shang Yang's reform success did not create [[PeopleBasedPoliticalSecurity|stable political security]] for its architect.
