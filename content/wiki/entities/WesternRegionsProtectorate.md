# Western Regions Protectorate / 西域都护府

## Overview
The Western Regions Protectorate is the Han frontier institution that Hanji 689-3 uses to explain how central authority managed oasis polities such as [[JingjueKingdom]]. In this source, it is less a bureaucratic biography than the institutional background that makes the brocade's possible gift, warning, and campaign message politically legible.

## Current Profile
The episode places the protectorate at Luntai and describes it as the Han mechanism for governing the Western Regions. Its importance lies in the contrast between Western Han capacity to project protection and later moments when Jingjue and other oasis states were exposed to Xiongnu, Qiang, and regional-polity pressure after that protection weakened.

## Key Characteristics
- The protectorate is presented as the central Han office for managing Western Regions polities.
- Its Luntai location anchors Han authority inside the region rather than only at the inner frontier.
- It provides the institutional background for a brocade interpreted as diplomatic gift or warning.
- It links frontier military success to symbolic messaging among oasis kings.
- Its later absence or non-restoration helps explain Jingjue's vulnerability.
