# 仇子龙 / Qiu Zilong

## Overview
仇子龙 / Qiu Zilong is the scientist named in the 不合时宜 episode's discussion of the Xiaomei gene-editing case.

## Current Profile
The page is strictly source-scoped. In the episode, the hosts name Qiu Zilong as the scientist leading the gene-editing work connected to Xiaomei's case and say that a later Nature publication presented successful animal-experiment material without equivalently presenting the child's death and human-experiment data. The episode uses that account to raise questions about experimental escalation, negative-result disclosure, ethics review, and scientific incentives.

This page should not be read as a full independent biography or legal finding. Its current role is to preserve the named-person link inside [[XiaoMeiGeneEditingCase]] and [[GeneEditingTreatmentEthics]].
