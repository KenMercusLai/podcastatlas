# OceanBase

## Overview
OceanBase is the Ant-origin database business discussed in [[273-guangwan-waitan-dahui-faxian-mayi-zhaodaole-xin-weizhi-lotxogfoqqwcjigxihtixvncbfc3]].

## Current Profile
The episode frames OceanBase as moving from the distributed database foundation behind Alipay and Taobao-scale transactions toward AI database infrastructure. In that frame, a database must hold structured, unstructured, and vector data while serving transaction, analytics, and agent-context workloads.

## Key Characteristics
- Originates from financial-grade, high-concurrency database needs inside the Ant/Alibaba ecosystem.
- Is presented as a reusable infrastructure asset for financial institutions and large enterprises.
- Moves toward AI database requirements where agents need governed, complete, queryable context.
- Benefits from enterprise AI, domestic substitution, and sovereignty-oriented data control in the episode's interpretation.

## Evidence
- Origin evidence: [[273-guangwan-waitan-dahui-faxian-mayi-zhaodaole-xin-weizhi-lotxogfoqqwcjigxihtixvncbfc3]] says OceanBase originally supported Alipay and Taobao's underlying data capability.
- AI-database evidence: [[273-guangwan-waitan-dahui-faxian-mayi-zhaodaole-xin-weizhi-lotxogfoqqwcjigxihtixvncbfc3]] says OceanBase is trying to unify structured, unstructured, and vector data for transaction, analysis, and AI workloads.
- Market evidence: [[273-guangwan-waitan-dahui-faxian-mayi-zhaodaole-xin-weizhi-lotxogfoqqwcjigxihtixvncbfc3]] treats OceanBase as an independently priced technology asset within Ant's organizational split.

## Qualifications
The source is a strategic discussion and does not compare OceanBase's AI database capability with TiDB, Oracle, cloud-native vector databases, or other enterprise data platforms.

## What Changed
- Added OceanBase as Ant's database-infrastructure asset.
