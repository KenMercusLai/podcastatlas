# 蔡瑁 / Cai Mao

## Overview
蔡瑁 / Cai Mao is a Jingzhou elite figure tied first to [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s consolidation and court circle, then to rank under [[CaoCao|曹操]] after the province submits.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1010 places Cai Mao in the local elite alliance through which Liu Biao suppresses armed powers and stabilizes northern [[Jingzhou|荆州]]. Hanji 1011 names him with [[KuaiYue|蒯越]] among elites offended by [[MiHengLateHan|祢衡]], making him part of the social pressure behind Mi Heng's transfer to [[HuangZu|黄祖]].

Hanji 1095 adds the post-surrender endpoint: Cao Cao grants Cai Mao marquis rank. His profile therefore links the elite infrastructure of Liu Biao's rule with the rapid absorption policy that follows regime change.

## Key Characteristics
- Member of Liu Biao's local elite alliance.
- Associated with northern Jingzhou consolidation.
- Part of the court-circle hostility surrounding Mi Heng.
- Receives noble rank from Cao Cao after Jingzhou submits.

## Qualifications
The current sources use Cai Mao mostly as an elite-network marker rather than giving a full biography. The transcript variant “蔡茂” is normalized to Cai Mao, and the exact marquisate requires primary-source verification.
