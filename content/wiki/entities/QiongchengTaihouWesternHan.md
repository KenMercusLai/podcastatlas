## Overview

邛成太后 / Qiongcheng Taihou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-540-taihou-de-zangli-lnd6gmr_bk7dv8cbb1zzoagtbwsd|Hanji 540]] as the Western Han dowager whose death in 15 BCE triggers a high-office accountability and personnel reshuffle under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-427-yiren-zhengfu-shacheguo-xihan-fengfengshi-zenme-zuodao-de-loxxi2qynbrbmfqgokos30sypsxp|Hanji 427]] backfills her selection as [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]'s childless, low-threat empress after the Huo Chengjun danger to crown prince [[HanYuandi|刘奭 / 汉元帝]].

## Current Profile

The source set presents her less as an active late-Chengdi political operator than as a long-standing dynastic role created by succession-risk control. Hanji 427 says Xuan needs a new empress after the Huo Chengjun threat to the crown prince and therefore favors a woman without sons, ambition, or strong palace danger. Hanji 540 later summarizes the same origin: after [[XuPingjunWesternHan|许平君]] was poisoned, Xuan chose a cautious, childless consort from the Wang line as empress and had her raise crown prince [[HanYuandi|刘奭 / 汉元帝]]. She was not especially favored, but she held ritual status through empress, empress dowager, and grand empress dowager phases.

Her death makes ritual duty visible. The episode says her funeral was handled hastily and that officials used collected taxes to rush the burial arrangements. [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] responded by blaming the chancellor and Yushi Dafu, dismissing [[XueXuanWesternHan|薛宣]] and demoting [[ZhaiFangjinWesternHan|翟方进]], even though Zhai was soon promoted.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han empress selected by Emperor Xuan after Xu Pingjun's poisoning and Huo Chengjun's threat because caution and lack of sons made her politically safer as foster mother.
- Foster mother of the future Emperor Yuan and therefore a bridge between Xuan, Yuan, and Cheng reigns.
- Long-held dowager figure whose rank endured for nearly half a century.
- Death and burial case that exposes how funeral ritual could become high-office accountability.
- Source-scoped transcript form appears as "穷城太后," normalized here to 邛成太后.

## Evidence

Foster-empress origin:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-427-yiren-zhengfu-shacheguo-xihan-fengfengshi-zenme-zuodao-de-loxxi2qynbrbmfqgokos30sypsxp|Hanji 427]] says Xuan wants a childless, unambitious empress who will not threaten crown prince Liu Shi after the Huo Chengjun case.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-427-yiren-zhengfu-shacheguo-xihan-fengfengshi-zenme-zuodao-de-loxxi2qynbrbmfqgokos30sypsxp|Hanji 427]] says Wang shi's lack of favor becomes protective, letting her later reach dowager status.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-540-taihou-de-zangli-lnd6gmr_bk7dv8cbb1zzoagtbwsd|Hanji 540]] says Emperor Xuan chose a cautious and childless consort as empress after Xu Pingjun's poisoning and ordered her to raise crown prince Liu Shi.

Long dowager status:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-540-taihou-de-zangli-lnd6gmr_bk7dv8cbb1zzoagtbwsd|Hanji 540]] says she passed from empress to empress dowager and grand empress dowager and held recognized status for forty-nine years.

Funeral accountability:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-540-taihou-de-zangli-lnd6gmr_bk7dv8cbb1zzoagtbwsd|Hanji 540]] says her rushed funeral angered Chengdi and led to punishment of Xue Xuan and Zhai Fangjin.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 540 and Hanji 427. It does not reconstruct Qiongcheng Taihou's full biography, her household politics, her exact burial protocol, Wang Fengguang's full genealogy, or the received-history wording behind every title. The "穷城" spelling is preserved as a transcript form; the canonical route uses 邛成太后.
