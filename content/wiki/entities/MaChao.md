## Overview

马超 / Ma Chao is a western military successor who retains [[MaTeng|马腾]]'s force after Ma Teng enters court and later becomes the principal Guanzhong coalition figure in the 211 CE [[WeinanBattleLateHan|渭南之战]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 1088 supplies the prehistory to the later campaign. When Cao Cao brings Ma Teng into court as weiwei, Ma Chao receives a subordinate-general title and continues leading the former force. The household later moves to Ye, creating a split between field command and family collateral. The episode then says Ma Chao rises three years later, with Ma Teng's household executed as a result.

Hanji 1105 compresses the 211 campaign itself. Ma Chao represents a militarily substantial but internally divisible coalition: Cao Cao is said to welcome the concentration of opponents because numbers do not guarantee cohesion, and [[JiaXu|贾诩]]'s counsel targets that weakness.

## Key Characteristics

- Principal opposing commander named in the episode's Weinan account.
- Associated with a broader Guanzhong coalition rather than an isolated force.
- Inherits continuing field command after Ma Teng enters court.
- Acts while the wider Ma household is politically exposed at Ye.
- Represents the difference between troop concentration and coalition cohesion.
- Is defeated after Cao Cao accepts Jia Xu's divisive strategy.

## Qualifications

Neither source reconstructs Ma Chao's full career, the coalition's membership, the causes of the conflict, or the complete command transition. Hanji 1088's title, tiger-tally language, household relocation timing, three-year interval, execution total, and the claim that Ma Teng never rebelled remain source-scoped. Hanji 1105's battlefield exchange, Cao Cao's private reaction, and exact mechanism of division are likewise bounded to that episode.
