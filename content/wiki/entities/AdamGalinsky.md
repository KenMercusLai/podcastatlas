## Overview
Adam Galinsky is the psychologist whose commentary structures the Hidden Brain episode's comparison of crisis leaders.

## Current Profile
In the source, Galinsky defines leadership as stewardship and protection rather than authority alone. He connects crisis performance to amplified leader signals, shared goals, hoped-for outcomes, recalled control, warmth, competence, and deliberate practice, while also using his own effort to replace explosive anger with empathy as evidence that leadership habits can change.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames leaders as protectors and stewards of a group.
- Uses the leader amplification effect to explain why small signals from authority carry disproportionate weight.
- Connects hope and shared goals to persistence and cohesion.
- Presents recalled control as a way to restore confidence under stress.
- Treats inspiring leadership as learned rather than fixed.
