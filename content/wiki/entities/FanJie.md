# 番杰 / Fan Jie

## Overview
[[FanJie|番杰 / Fan Jie]] is the founder-partner of 聪明投资者 and the guest in [[lossad1trayjdp11pbjiywrxy43o|the 面基 episode]], where he discusses his new Chinese translation of [[CharlesEllis|Charles Ellis]]'s history of [[CapitalGroup|Capital Group]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Fan Jie as a translator with finance, fund-industry, and investment-media experience who treats translation as research rather than lexical substitution. His analysis focuses on which Capital Group practices form a coherent system, why visible mechanisms cannot simply be copied into China, and how investors can infer institutional values from decisions made under conflicts of interest.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines financial, fund-industry, and investment-content experience in interpreting an asset-management history.
- Re-researched people, institutions, terminology, and historical context during translation.
- Distinguishes unrepeatable founder and era conditions from operating choices that can still be studied.
- Evaluates institutions through ownership, incentives, hiring, attribution, product policy, and client outcomes together.
- Treats AI as a translation aid that does not remove the need for full-context understanding and fact checking.
