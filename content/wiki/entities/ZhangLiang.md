# 张良 / Zhang Liang

Zhang Liang is [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s strategist in the episode's reading of [[HongmenYan|《鸿门宴》]] in [[164-guwen-de-liliang-qing-shou-xia-zhe-fen-qiannian-wei-bian-de-shenqing-dongjian-he-yongqi-940236576]]. The source treats him as the person most able to read both power etiquette and human psychology inside the banquet crisis.

In QinZong's reading, [[FanKuai|樊哙]]'s dramatic rebuke of [[XiangYu|项羽]] likely works because it is not merely spontaneous bravery: the speech recognizes Xiang Yu's status, aligns him against "暴秦", and gives him a way to admire the speaker without killing Liu Bang. Zhang Liang's value is therefore strategic composition under pressure.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-26-mengzi-laile-ln4twwp2frmr-xpumu0rqg-2gpyn]] adds Zhang Liang's family background before the Qin-Han story. The episode says his grandfather [[ZhangKaidi|张开地]] and father [[ZhangPingHan|张平]] served [[HanState|韩国]] as chief ministers across several rulers, making Zhang Liang's later anti-Qin and Han-restoration motive a family-political inheritance.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-122-3-qinshihuang-jing-he-shenxian-si-qilai-le-ljnw5estg6dvgfllm3kfcq6ulhxu]] brings Zhang Liang into the Qinji forward line. After narrating Qin Shi Huang's tour and the [[XiangshanShrine|湘山祠]] incident, the episode returns to Zhang Liang's Han noble background and says Han's destruction turns family memory into anti-Qin action: after his brother dies, Zhang Liang spends family resources seeking an assassin rather than prioritizing funeral expense, setting up the next attempt on [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-123-1-shen-zhi-yuyan-wangqin-zhe-hu-jing-shi-zhi-lg7m3vfdbcweb4joxe4jvhxjx6y8]] narrates that attempt. Zhang Liang finds a strongman through the eastern [[CanghaiJun|沧海君]] tradition, prepares a heavy iron hammer, and strikes at Qin Shi Huang's traveling column at [[Bolangsha|博浪沙]] in 218 BCE. The attack hits an accompanying carriage rather than the emperor's own vehicle, producing the "误中副车" memory; Zhang Liang escapes the ten-day search and remains a remnant-Han revenge actor before his later [[LiuBang|刘邦]] strategist role.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-123-2-gudai-shiqi-de-daochamen-daodi-you-duo-weixian-lgcknwubwm3uzqmzgbgdlxlvcwxy]] gives the next stage after escape. Zhang Liang meets [[HuangshiGong|黄石公]] / 圯上老人 at a bridge, accepts repeated humiliating tests around retrieving and putting on the old man's shoe, and receives [[TaigongBingfa|《太公兵法》]]. The episode uses this scene to move Zhang Liang from revenge-driven assassin toward the patient strategist who can later serve as a teacher of kings.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-1-guailaotou-yiqiao-san-shua-zhangliang-chuanshou-shenshu-lqwjo13zzfgtndpajzpuvrb2h4l2|Qinji 130-1]] reprises that bridge scene with more emphasis on discipline and timing. Zhang Liang passes only after arriving before midnight for the third appointment, and the host connects the story to "人生有度，过则为灾." The episode also looks forward to Zhang Liang's later ability to withdraw after success, making his formation a case not only of strategy acquisition but also of measured self-limitation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-4-jiuquangui-zhangliang-ruhe-zai-liubang-shouxia-anshen-lp6qwg3ma5hdy15g4box7pe23jav|Qinji 129-4]] gives the payoff of that strategist formation. Zhang Liang gathers a small following and is initially heading toward [[JingJu|景居 / 景驹]], but after meeting [[LiuBang|刘邦]] he joins Liu Bang instead. The episode says Zhang Liang repeatedly explains [[TaigongBingfa|《太公兵法》]] to Liu Bang, Liu Bang understands and adopts the counsel, and Zhang Liang sees him as a rare person who can actually understand his strategy. This becomes the wiki's main case for [[StrategicRecognitionAlignment|谋略知己式归附]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-136-meinv-xijiao-liubang-ye-hao-zhe-kouer-lplgzwzahepwx9p7krfc4x0cnz4o|Qinji 136]] shows Zhang Liang working as a route-risk adviser on Liu Bang's western line. After Liu Bang moves through [[YingchuanCommandery|颍川郡]], Zhang Liang reappears and is sent to recover old Han territory. At [[WanCounty|宛县]], he warns Liu Bang that bypassing the city could leave a rear enemy while Qin blocks the road ahead, causing Liu Bang to turn back and surround Wan before accepting its surrender.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-138-wudu-qiannian-de-lishi-zhenxiang-zhaogao-bushi-taijian-2-lvtievjrclb1lxxxxr6k-q7kryto|Qinji 138 part 2]] gives a late payoff to that route-risk role at [[Yaoguan|峣关]]. Zhang Liang tells [[LiuBang|刘邦]] not to underestimate Qin, uses mountain flags to create psychological pressure, and sends [[LiShiqi|郦食其]] and [[LuJia|陆贾]] to persuade Qin generals. When Liu Bang considers accepting the peace offer, Zhang Liang warns that generals may want to defect while soldiers may not follow, so the better move is to attack while the Qin side is relaxed.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-139-ta-zaici-yingxiang-hanchao-lp1zujuq0o3ohxc79xqi1phpb5zo|Hanji 139]] adds a palace-restraint version of the same adviser role. After Liu Bang enters [[Xianyang|咸阳]] and wants to stay amid palace wealth and women, Zhang Liang reinforces [[FanKuai|樊哙]]'s warning by saying Qin's violence and indulgence are precisely why Liu Bang could enter the capital. His advice helps move Liu Bang back to [[Bashang|霸上]], where occupation policy can be staged through [[YuefaSanzhang|约法三章]] rather than palace consumption.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-140-xiangyu-yu-liubang-shi-zhen-youqing-haishi-xianghu-liyong-1-lmmhoq2wo3bs6b1johpc0ixuf662|Hanji 140 part 1]] begins Zhang Liang's Hongmen-crisis role from the warning side. After [[XiangBo|项伯]] learns that [[XiangYu|项羽]] plans to attack [[LiuBang|刘邦]], the available transcript turns toward Zhang Liang rushing to notify Liu Bang.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-140-xiangyu-yu-liubang-shi-zhen-youqing-haishi-xianghu-liyong-2-lvdgirxmwxcckh2loh-yk2zzer2e|Hanji 140 part 2]] completes the scene. Xiang Bo wants to save Zhang Liang because Zhang Liang had once saved him, but Zhang Liang refuses to abandon Liu Bang secretly, arguing that leaving would be both disloyal to the mission from the Han king and faithless to his friend. That choice turns Xiang Bo's private rescue offer into Liu Bang's crisis channel.

The same source shows Zhang Liang's value as more than tactical cleverness. He knows which relationship can be activated, asks Xiang Bo to meet Liu Bang, and lets Liu Bang perform deference, kinship, and loyalty in the idiom Xiang Bo will accept. Zhang Liang is therefore the connector who converts old恩义 into a politically usable meeting before Hongmen.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-141-huanyuan-lishi-zhong-de-hongmenyan-lpmb-bcoocngtqjvtnpj0elm-siy|Hanji 141]] brings Zhang Liang into the banquet room with Liu Bang. The source stresses that [[FanKuai|樊哙]], [[XiahouYing|夏侯婴]], [[JiXin|纪信]], [[JinQiang|金强]], and the cavalry remain outside; inside the tent, Zhang Liang sits in the lowest place while Liu Bang performs apology and deference before [[XiangYu|项羽]]. His position makes the prior mediation path visible in the room's status order.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-142-zhechang-juhui-xiangyu-shudiao-le-yiqie-ln7dv2jxrk6lt1c4szylsrb41k3r|Hanji 142]] completes Zhang Liang's Hongmen management role. He brings [[FanKuai|樊哙]] into the crisis, helps convert the room away from assassination, then stays behind after Liu Bang leaves so he can present the white bi and jade dipper only after estimating Liu Bang has returned safely to [[Bashang|霸上]]. His later encounter with [[ChenPing|陈平]] becomes a quiet contest of inference and restraint: Zhang Liang preserves Liu Bang's escape without forcing open conflict in Xiang Yu's camp.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-144-xiangyu-jituan-fumie-de-yuanyin-1-lr0ft8-jupxgvutn6c89mfs8r4wb|Hanji 144]] adds Zhang Liang's post-settlement relationship work. After Liu Bang accepts the need to enter [[Hanzhong|汉中]], he gives Zhang Liang heavy gifts, and Zhang Liang passes them to [[XiangBo|项伯]] so Xiang Bo can ask [[XiangYu|项羽]] for a more favorable practical arrangement. The source therefore extends Zhang Liang's [[HongmenYan|鸿门宴]] mediation role into post-banquet territorial bargaining through personal trust.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-144-xiangyu-jituan-fumie-de-yuanyin-2-lut3jiatchla3pfxpkznsrs7wkzu|Hanji 144 part 2]] makes Zhang Liang's advice operational rather than only relational. After escorting [[LiuBang|刘邦]] toward Baozhong, he says he should return to the Han king's side to avoid deepening [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s suspicion, then tells Liu Bang to destroy the cliffside road behind him. [[BurningGalleryRoads|烧栈道]] becomes a Zhang Liang tactic: it protects against attack and makes Liu Bang's apparent acceptance of [[Hanzhong|汉中]] credible.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-148-2-peng-le-wangling-muqin-xiangyu-sharen-zhuxin-ljyv8bow4rj1l5ths3znx2evgvtm|Hanji 148-2]] extends that suspicion-management role into letter routing. Zhang Liang passes to [[XiangYu|项羽]] both [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s claim that the Guanzhong move only restores the [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]] and a copied [[TianRong|田荣]] letter calling for anti-Xiang action. The source treats the pairing as effective delay: Xiang Yu remains wary of Liu Bang, but he focuses first on Tian Rong.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-149-xichu-bawang-xiangyu-weihe-shijun-1-lsqxs2hzu0ngnrpmzsklkqhtyuvw|Hanji 149]] adds Zhang Liang's final break from the restored-Han branch. After [[XiangYu|项羽]] kills [[HanCheng|韩成]], Zhang Liang cannot continue in Korea's old restoration project, changes clothes, escapes by side roads, and goes to [[LiuBang|刘邦]]. Liu Bang makes him Chengxin Hou, and the episode emphasizes that Zhang Liang remains a strategist close to Liu Bang rather than a field commander.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-149-xichu-bawang-xiangyu-weihe-shijun-2-lpohbb3prsgvokpjipfd7i1ntiwn|Hanji 149 part 2]] keeps Zhang Liang connected to the Han-restoration line indirectly. The episode says [[HanWangXin|韩王信]] had followed Liu Bang and Zhang Liang into Guanzhong, then into Hanzhong, before Liu Bang later makes him Han king after [[ZhengChangHanKing|郑昌]] surrenders.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-150-chenping-weihe-bei-cheng-zhiwei-chenggong-zhengzhijia-yangban-3-lhfbm3twyxhyvokphf8hc9ssblqj|Hanji 150 part 3]] uses Zhang Liang as the clean strategist contrast for [[ChenPing|陈平]]. Both help [[LiuBang|刘邦]], but the source treats Zhang Liang's value as more legible adviser craft, while Chen Ping's value depends on morally uncomfortable hidden work and [[SecondInCommandRoleFit|二把手角色适配]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-150-chenping-weihe-bei-cheng-zhiwei-chenggong-zhengzhijia-yangban-4-lhhprrkjcuzijgndi2uuazaalern|Hanji 150 part 4]] repeats Zhang Liang as a comparison figure when arguing that Chen Ping's success is a different kind of political accomplishment. Zhang Liang can withdraw after achievement, while the source's Chen Ping remains inside the court field and becomes a case for [[WorldlyPoliticalCultivation|入世政治修行]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-151-huanyuan-pengcheng-zhizhan-xiangyu-zai-fengshen-1-lspx-bqe5kczq6bmn6thlerfbrmu|Hanji 151 part 1]] gives Zhang Liang a rare negative-space role. After [[LiuBang|刘邦]] captures [[Xuzhou|彭城]], the source says even Zhang Liang and [[ChenPing|陈平]] are momentarily swallowed by the sudden victory and do not interrupt the revelry. This does not overturn Zhang Liang's strategist profile, but it makes [[PengchengBattle|彭城之战]] a case where a whole command culture, not only Liu Bang's appetite, loses vigilance.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-153-xiayi-huace-canbai-hou-liubang-ruhe-qisi-huisheng-1-lgvjuxuskyyfmyeeqm9y8aeln-hu|Hanji 153]] restores Zhang Liang's role as strategic designer after the rout. At [[Xiayi|下邑]], he answers Liu Bang's question about attacking Chu by naming [[YingBu|英布]], [[PengYue|彭越]], and [[HanXin|韩信]] as the usable force centers. His [[XiayiPlan|下邑画策]] recognizes Liu Bang's direct-command limit, turns Ying Bu's damaged relationship with [[XiangYu|项羽]] into a target, and makes delegation to Han Xin part of recovery rather than a side issue.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-158-koucai-hao-de-tianhuaban-yiren-ding-baiwan-dajun-lnup-vmw-ff-nqmuh6-x8si0k8zu|Hanji 158]] validates the Ying Bu part of Zhang Liang's design. [[SuiHe|随何]]'s mission succeeds because the target Zhang Liang selected was already in a half-broken relationship with Xiang Yu: Ying Bu had a separate [[JiujiangChuHan|九江国]] base, feared Xiang Yu, and had repeatedly acted like a watcher rather than a fully loyal subordinate.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-159-1-liubang-xijiao-de-yuren-zhishu-lga3puexunjzeu3s8pvt8saaa8n3|Hanji 159-1]] adds Zhang Liang as a brake on panic strategy. After Chu takes [[Aocang|敖仓]] and pressures Han's supply corridor, [[LiShiqi|郦食其]] persuades [[LiuBang|刘邦]] to prepare seals for restoring the six kingdoms. Zhang Liang returns before the plan is executed, calls it a "馊主意," and asks to borrow chopsticks to explain why; the transcript stops before the full analysis, so the page records this as the opening of the "借箸" refutation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-159-2-zhangliang-shuole-sha-jiuxia-hanchao-lvs9elvrjp-ypz63w4czpuhbsfb|Hanji 159-2]] completes that refutation. Zhang Liang uses [[ShangTang|商汤]] and [[ZhouWuwang|周武王]] as control-condition examples: they could honor old lineages and disarm only after victory was secure, while Liu Bang still cannot control the field, stop the war, or give away his future reward pool. His core warning is organizational rather than only ideological: restoring six kingdoms would pull followers away from Liu Bang and may create new rulers who attach to [[XiangYu|项羽]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-164-1-liubang-geqiang-chaofeng-qi-de-xiangyu-shejian-touxi-lhs6miq2aa3avnny-prxtvkfxgvy|Hanji 164-1]] gives Zhang Liang a crisis-continuity role at [[GuangwuChuHan|广武]]. After [[XiangYu|项羽]] secretly shoots [[LiuBang|刘邦]], Liu Bang hides the chest wound by claiming a toe injury. Zhang Liang then patrols the camp and calms soldiers, making him the visible proxy who keeps the army from treating the wound as command collapse under [[CommandContinuityDamageControl|统帅连续性危机处置]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-165-1-liubang-zheju-hua-chadian-nongdiu-dahan-jiangshan-llxgisomsdo8qjeecgdg6frri-xq|Hanji 165-1]] gives Zhang Liang another fast crisis-reading scene. When [[HanXin|韩信]] asks to act as king of [[QiState|齐国]], Zhang Liang and [[ChenPing|陈平]] stop [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s angry first reaction by physically signaling that Han cannot restrain Han Xin under current conditions. Zhang Liang then becomes the envoy sent to [[Linzi|临淄]] to make the [[ConcededKingshipStabilization|real Qi kingship concession]] formal.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-166-jiran-xiangyu-bujiang-wude-kan-liubang-ruhe-fuchou-ltwwbn9rzeqzzimmxxfihb9-drj1|Hanji 166]] gives Zhang Liang a decisive post-Honggou role. When Liu Bang plans to return west after the settlement, Zhang Liang and Chen Ping argue that Chu is weak, grain is exhausted, and failing to pursue would be raising a future danger. The source makes their advice the visible pivot that lets Liu Bang break the agreement and chase Xiang Yu.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-167-jianzheng-xiangyu-zuizhong-zhizhan-1-lm6nwhjjuguhrmwzoz0sqawgxwuv|Hanji 167]] gives Zhang Liang the final coalition-incentive diagnosis before Gaixia. When Han Xin and Peng Yue do not arrive, he tells Liu Bang that their non-arrival is natural without clear promised land, then recommends assigning the Suiyang-to-Gucheng region to Peng Yue and the Chen-to-sea region to Han Xin. This makes Zhang Liang the bridge from Xiayi Plan's broad reward logic to Territorial Reward Mobilization.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-169-pandian-liubang-dengjiqian-buweirenzhi-de-gushi-2-lqq8qhramfzii5o5icesd-uagwkc|Hanji 169 part 2]] places Zhang Liang inside Liu Bang's South Palace explanation of Han victory. Liu Bang says he cannot match Zhang Liang in strategy, [[XiaoHe|萧何]] in supply administration, or [[HanXin|韩信]] in command, but he can use all three. Zhang Liang therefore becomes one of the named specialists behind [[SpecialistDelegationLeadership|专才分工式领导力]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-170-liubang-weisha-cong-luoyang-banjia-dao-changan-2-lpqtexvxjrbrpjgbw5hrgmhl9apk|Hanji 170 part 2]] gives Zhang Liang the decisive confirming role in the capital debate. After [[LouJing|楼敬]] argues that Liu Bang should not imitate Zhou's [[Luoyi|洛阳 / 洛邑]] choice, Zhang Liang supplies the practical geography: Luoyang has limited hinterland and is exposed from several sides, while [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] has mountain-pass defense, fertile land, [[BaShu|巴蜀]] resources, northern supply, and river transport. His endorsement turns Lou Jing's warning into immediate action: Liu Bang travels west the same day and fixes the capital at [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]].

The episode also previews Zhang Liang's next problem. Once Liu Bang has the empire, Zhang Liang's value as founding strategist becomes inseparable from [[PowerExitTrap|权力退场困境]]: the source closes by asking how he will avoid the "bird gone, bow put away" danger.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-171-zhangliang-jiao-ni-ruhe-mingli-shuangshou-1-lptgy7wxwsve8ljt79asetubin41|Hanji 171 part 1]] picks up that exact post-founding problem. After the move to Chang'an, Zhang Liang is described as ill, secluded, practicing Daoist techniques, avoiding grain, and claiming that his worldly achievements are already complete enough. His stated wish to follow [[Chisongzi|赤松子]] becomes [[TranscendentExitPoliticalCamouflage|假托神仙式权力退场]]: he makes retreat from Liu Bang's power field sound like transcendence rather than rivalry or resentment.

The source also complicates earlier praise of Zhang Liang's exit. The host accepts that Zhang Liang's withdrawal is brilliant self-protection, but argues that [[HanXin|韩信]] and [[XiaoHe|萧何]] should not be blamed for later danger as if the real problem were simply failure to know when to stop. Zhang Liang's success therefore becomes both a model of timing and a warning against turning survival advice into [[VictimBlamingAsInstitutionalDefense|victim-blaming]].
