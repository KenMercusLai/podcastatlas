# Paul Newman

Paul Newman appears in [[rolex-1]] through the [[RolexDaytona]] collector story. The episode says Newman's wife Joanne Woodward gave him a Daytona connected to his racing hobby; the watch later passed to his daughter's boyfriend and eventually sold at auction for $17.5 million.

The source uses Newman to show how a product can become more valuable after consumer mythology forms around provenance, celebrity, design details, and scarcity. That makes the Daytona branch part of [[ProfessionalToolBranding]] and [[MechanicalWatchRepositioning]] rather than only watch-collector trivia.
