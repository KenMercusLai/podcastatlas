# Effie (Data Science With Sam)

Effie appears in [[data-ai-and-scientific-research-a-coffee-chat]] as a biology-focused postdoctoral research associate working around cancer metabolism, bone biology, and pathology. The source gives only her first name, so this page keeps the identity scoped to the episode.

Her contribution is to make [[AIForScience]] concrete inside messy biological research. She emphasizes [[ExperimentalScienceDataQuality]], careful recordkeeping, quality control, standard operating procedures, protocol deviations, blinding, randomization, and the [[BioinformaticsDomainGap]] between bench scientists and computational analysts.

Effie's future-facing AI idea is not a replacement scientist. She imagines [[AIExperimentDocumentation]] and [[HumanDrivenScientificAI]] systems that remember prior experiments, observe current lab work, connect new inputs with old results, and suggest next experiments while leaving creativity and judgment with researchers.
