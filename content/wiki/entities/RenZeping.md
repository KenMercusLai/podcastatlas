# Ren Zeping / 任泽平

## Overview
Ren Zeping / 任泽平 appears in EP93 as the economist Evergrande hired during its 2017 peak. The source uses him less as a full biography and more as a marker of the boom-era housing-price narrative around [[Evergrande]] and [[ChinaRealEstateDebtCycle]].

## Current Profile
The current profile is narrow. EP93 says Evergrande hired Ren as chief economist in 2017, during the period when the company had very large assets, high reported profits, and [[XuJiayin]] had become China's richest person on the Hurun list. The episode then cites Ren's bullish housing-price view as an example of how continued appreciation expectations made Evergrande's leverage easier to overlook.

Ren's role in this wiki branch is therefore evidentiary and contextual. He represents the confidence environment in which large developers, households, investors, and commentators could treat property as a structurally rising asset class, even as debt and cash-flow fragility were building underneath.

## Key Characteristics
- Source-scoped economist figure attached to Evergrande's 2017 peak rather than a general biography.
- Boom-era confidence marker because EP93 uses his housing-price forecast to represent optimistic property expectations.
- Interpretive bridge between individual developer leverage and a broader market narrative that housing would keep appreciating.
- Cautionary example for [[InvestmentRiskManagement]] because expert optimism can coexist with hidden balance-sheet and financing risk.
