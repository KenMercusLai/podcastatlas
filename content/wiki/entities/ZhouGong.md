# 周公 / Duke of Zhou

周公 / Duke of Zhou appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-49-mengzi-yue-wo-jiao-ni-zhiguo-ni-que-ba-wo-dang-huaping-lhy14pbf9thcfdiaulcmf8dwcef8]] as [[ChenJiaWarringStates|陈甲]]'s example of a sage who could still make an understandable mistake. Chen Jia cites the episode where Zhou Gong sent Guan Shu to oversee the former Shang lands and Guan Shu later rebelled.

[[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] does not use the example to deny error. Instead, he says the mistake was understandable because Zhou Gong could not know in advance that Guan Shu would rebel, then shifts the issue toward correction: ancient gentlemen corrected faults, while later self-styled gentlemen invented excuses. Zhou Gong therefore becomes part of the episode's [[CorrectableLeadershipError|犯错能改的领导力]] frame.
