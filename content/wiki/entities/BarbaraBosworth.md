# Barbara Bosworth

## Overview

Barbara Bosworth is the photographer used by Episode 230 to model nature photography that includes human encounter and builds knowledge through long-term attention to one place.

## Current Profile

The episode emphasizes photographs in which ornithologists and held birds occupy the same frame, with titles phrased as “bird with person” so the human is part of the event rather than an invisible recorder. It also presents *The Meadow* as a project sustained for more than a decade, combining repeated photography with researchers and historical nature-observation notes to know one site across time.

## Key Characteristics

- Keeps bird, researcher, and encounter visible in the same image.
- Uses titles to resist treating the human observer as absent.
- Works through repeated attention to a single place over many years.
- Combines photography with scientific and historical records.
