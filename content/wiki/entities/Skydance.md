# Skydance

## Overview
Skydance is [[DavidEllison]]'s media company, evolving in the sources from a co-financing Hollywood entrant into the vehicle for [[Paramount]] and [[WarnerBrosDiscovery|Warner Bros. Discovery]] consolidation.

## Current Profile
The earlier source describes Skydance's risk-managed path: it partnered with Paramount, attached financing and production capacity to established franchises, and used [[TopGunMaverick]] as proof that David Ellison could operate beyond family-capital access. It then presents the Paramount combination and Warner bid as a move from production into control over studios, libraries, streaming, and news assets.

The newest episode advances that source-local chronology from acquisition proposal to claimed completion. It says a USD 110 billion Warner Bros. Discovery purchase cleared its main legal obstacle through a federal settlement, left the combined company with more than USD 50 billion of acquisition debt, and imposed theater-release and U.S.-production commitments. The current profile therefore combines expanded asset control with substantial financing and integration constraints.

## Key Characteristics
- Began with co-financing, production, Paramount distribution, and established entertainment IP.
- Uses David Ellison's leadership and Larry Ellison-linked capital as scaling resources.
- Expanded from production credibility into vertically integrated media ownership.
- Faces debt, antitrust, theatrical-output, production-spending, and integration constraints.
