# B.A.I Capital

B.A.I Capital is the venture-capital firm context for [[WangTianfan|Will Wang Tianfan / 汪天凡]] in [[reai-yige-hangye-15-nian-de-liyou-shi-shenme-duitan-wang-tianfan-woyao-tou-zhenzheng-de-kuaile-tou-zui-chun-de-yuanjing-tou-renxing-de-guanghui-gonglu-boke-lu98aa1byafbbljyjrn8oquiezk]]. The source says Wang entered B.A.I as an intern in 2011 and remained there through multiple VC cycles.

The episode uses B.A.I Capital mainly as an investment-process and portfolio lens. Wang says the firm has backed several [[AIContextMachine|context-machine]] directions, including memory glasses, wearable devices, privacy-oriented AI hardware, offline AI social products, and projects such as [[Lookie|Loki/Lookie]] and [[Mashie]].

## Key Claims
- The source presents B.A.I's AI interest as broader than model labs or productivity software.
- Its context-machine thesis treats hardware as a way to gather user context, while software and interaction design determine whether the product creates reflection, care, or joy.
- The firm's AI investing frame combines top-down belief formation with direct product use and founder judgment.
