# Qwen Office / 千问办公

## Overview

Qwen Office / 千问办公 is the Alibaba/Qwen office-agent product surface discussed in [[270-da-chang-yazhu-ai-bangong-feishu-he-dingding-que-xian-chengle-peijue-lmb4dgcgov3mr4cn7cikbghpfro4]] and [[272-cong-feishu-jizuo-dao-agent-youxian-doubao-gongzuo-all-in-one-jinzhui-workbuddy-lqmfcnfkkhoxt440qy26vwpnvswp]] as a comparator to [[DoubaoWork|Doubao Work]] and [[TencentWorkBuddy|WorkBuddy]].

## Current Profile

The bounded sources place Qwen Office inside [[Alibaba]]'s broader model-plus-work route. Episode 270 treats Qwen, DingTalk, and related Alibaba products as one of the large-company paths into AI office. Episode 272 adds that Qwen Office and Qwen appear more separated at the user level than Doubao and Doubao Work, while Qwen Office may still deepen its relationship with [[DingTalk]] over time.

The ecommerce branch adds verticalization rather than another generic feature comparison. A nearly 100-person team is reported to be developing an ecommerce edition for merchants operating across platforms; ecommerce is said to represent about 20% of current paying users, while business, data, and customer resources become the emerging competitive layer. If accurate, Qwen Office is moving from a general office-agent comparator toward an industry-shaped workflow product.

The page remains cautious on model openness. The source predicts that public-cloud Qwen Office is unlikely to support BYOK, while enterprise-targeted deployments may differ; this remains a source-scoped forecast.

## Key Characteristics

- Alibaba/Qwen office-agent comparator in the Chinese AI-office market.
- Adjacent to [[DingTalk]] as Alibaba's collaboration and enterprise-work substrate.
- Presented as less user-side seamless with Qwen than Doubao Work is with Doubao.
- Model openness and enterprise deployment options remain source-scoped.
- Developing a source-reported ecommerce edition for cross-platform merchants.
- Treats industry workflow, business data, and customer resources as differentiation beyond generic agent functions.

## Qualifications

- The wiki does not yet have direct Qwen Office product tests, pricing, customer cases, or retention data.
- BYOK and DingTalk-integration claims are participant forecasts rather than confirmed product state.
- Team size, user share, release timing, platform coverage, and the depth of merchant integrations are secondary-report claims without product tests or customer outcome data.
- Ecommerce-user concentration shows demand in the source's account but does not establish retention, revenue quality, or workflow advantage.

## What Changed

- Added the reported ecommerce edition as a move from generic office features toward industry-specific workflows.
- Added ecommerce-user concentration while keeping team, timing, and adoption claims source-scoped.
