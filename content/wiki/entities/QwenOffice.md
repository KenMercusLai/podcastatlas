# Qwen Office / 千问办公

## Overview

Qwen Office / 千问办公 is the Alibaba/Qwen office-agent product surface discussed in [[270-da-chang-yazhu-ai-bangong-feishu-he-dingding-que-xian-chengle-peijue-lmb4dgcgov3mr4cn7cikbghpfro4]] and [[272-cong-feishu-jizuo-dao-agent-youxian-doubao-gongzuo-all-in-one-jinzhui-workbuddy-lqmfcnfkkhoxt440qy26vwpnvswp]] as a comparator to [[DoubaoWork|Doubao Work]] and [[TencentWorkBuddy|WorkBuddy]].

## Current Profile

The bounded sources place Qwen Office inside [[Alibaba]]'s broader model-plus-work route. Episode 270 treats Qwen, DingTalk, and related Alibaba products as one of the large-company paths into AI office. Episode 272 adds that Qwen Office and Qwen appear more separated at the user level than Doubao and Doubao Work, while Qwen Office may still deepen its relationship with [[DingTalk]] over time.

The page remains cautious on model openness. The source predicts that public-cloud Qwen Office is unlikely to support BYOK, while enterprise-targeted deployments may differ; this remains a source-scoped forecast.

## Key Characteristics

- Alibaba/Qwen office-agent comparator in the Chinese AI-office market.
- Adjacent to [[DingTalk]] as Alibaba's collaboration and enterprise-work substrate.
- Presented as less user-side seamless with Qwen than Doubao Work is with Doubao.
- Model openness and enterprise deployment options remain source-scoped.
