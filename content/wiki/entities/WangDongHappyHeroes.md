# 王动（《欢乐英雄》） / Wang Dong

## Overview

王动 is the owner of 富贵山庄 and one of the four protagonists of [[HappyHeroesGuLong|《欢乐英雄》]]. He is introduced as a man who stays in bed because nothing seems worth standing up for.

## Current Profile

The bounded discussion treats “不动” as more than laziness: it can signal exhaustion, disillusionment, and hermit-like withdrawal. At the same time, 王动 often recognizes hidden facts early and voices the novel's general judgments, making him both participant and interpretive spokesman.

His past becomes consequential in the “四凶追王动” arc. The danger is strongest because he cannot simply overpower every enemy; survival depends on planning and the four friends' mutual support.

## Key Characteristics

- Withdrawn estate owner whose inactivity suggests both comedy and despair.
- Perceptive observer who recognizes Yan Qi's identity before Guo Dalu.
- Frequent speaker of the novel's aphoristic judgments.
- Conceals a dangerous past even from close friends.
- Depends on collective planning rather than invincible individual force.
