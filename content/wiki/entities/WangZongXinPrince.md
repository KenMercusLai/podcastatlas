## Overview

王宗 / Wang Zong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-654-jiemi-xihan-yangxiong-de-chuanqi-rensheng-lq3o-13h2in89qwh0dvchczlvrqk|Hanji 654]] as [[WangMang|王莽]]'s grandson whose private imperial imagery and seals lead to suicide. His case adds an earlier Wang-family fear-and-punishment layer before the better-developed Hanji 658 succession-collapse branch.

## Current Profile

The episode says Wang Zong privately draws or keeps an image of himself in emperor's clothing and has three seals carved. Once the matter is discovered, he kills himself. The source uses the case to show that late-Xin insecurity reaches inside Wang Mang's own clan, where symbolic signs of possible imperial claim become lethal.

Wang Zong is also linked to Wang Fang's case through family relation rather than a fully reconstructed faction. His sister Wang Fang, wife of Wang Xing, is accused of cursing family members and killing a maid to silence evidence; she and Wang Xing also die by suicide in the episode's compressed account.

## Key Characteristics
