# Sean Barassa

Sean Barassa is the founder behind [[EulogyGame|Eulogy]] in [[advice-line-with-carlton-calvin-of-razor-c0de1a84-3d35-45f3-921c-6144693ad86e]]. The episode presents him as a game inventor who has moved from a COVID-era party-game idea to a 2024 [[Kickstarter]] campaign, product delivery in 2025, almost 1,000 units sold, and roughly 500 units still in inventory.

Sean's strategic question is how to make Eulogy a household name. [[CarltonCalvin]] and [[GuyRaz]] steer him toward [[ToyInventorLicensingPath]] and [[FastProductValidation]]: a publisher or toy company may be a path eventually, but the game likely needs stronger demand proof from conventions, small stores, game nights, and visible player reactions before licensing becomes plausible.
