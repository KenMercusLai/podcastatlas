## Overview

庄青翟 / Zhuang Qingdi enters the wiki as the Western Han chancellor whose proposed joint apology over stolen burial money turns into an open responsibility conflict with [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-338-2-yinwei-yi-chang-anmo-yinfa-de-xuean-lp8mwe-z21lhqhg4lwnj-jmeotag|Hanji 338-2]] says burial money is stolen from Emperor Wen's funerary park and Zhuang arranges to apologize before [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] together with Zhang. Zhuang accepts management failure, but Zhang remains silent because he regards seasonal inspection of the park as a chancellor's duty rather than his own. Wudi then orders Zhang to investigate Zhuang's responsibility, and Zhang seeks to accuse Zhuang of knowing about the theft and failing to report it. The sequence changes attempted shared responsibility into direct ministerial conflict.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han chancellor in the Yuanding 2 court setting.
- Connected to the responsibility for inspecting Emperor Wen's funerary park.
- Tries to make a joint apology with Zhang Tang after burial money is stolen.
- Accepts fault while Zhang stays silent.
- Becomes the subject of an investigation led by Zhang.

## Qualifications

The raw transcript's “庄青宅” is normalized to 庄青翟 from the transmitted Zhang Tang account. The current source does not complete Zhuang's biography, the theft investigation, his later coordination with other officials, or his death; it only establishes the opening responsibility conflict covered by this episode.

## What Changed
