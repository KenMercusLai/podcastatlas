## Overview

庄青翟 / Zhuang Qingdi is the Western Han chancellor whose proposed joint apology over stolen burial money turns into an open responsibility conflict with [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]], prompts a self-protective alliance with three hostile chief clerks, and ends in his own forced suicide after Zhang's death.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-338-2-yinwei-yi-chang-anmo-yinfa-de-xuean-lp8mwe-z21lhqhg4lwnj-jmeotag|Hanji 338-2]] says burial money is stolen from Emperor Wen's funerary park and Zhuang arranges to apologize before [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] together with Zhang. Zhuang accepts management failure, but Zhang remains silent because he regards seasonal inspection of the park as a chancellor's duty rather than his own. Wudi then orders Zhang to investigate Zhuang's responsibility, and Zhang seeks to accuse Zhuang of knowing about the theft and failing to report it. The sequence changes attempted shared responsibility into direct ministerial conflict.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-339-1-sanrenchenghu-ruhe-bisi-xihan-diyi-kuli-lowphplbvgugdyfag-sczpgggyrv|Hanji 339-1]] says Zhang continues collecting evidence despite knowing Zhuang has limited responsibility. Zhuang responds by coordinating with [[ZhuMaichenWesternHan|朱买臣]], [[WangChaoWesternHan|王朝]], and [[BianTongWesternHan|边通]], who fear that his removal would expose them as Zhang's next targets. Zhu's proposed counterattack uses merchant [[TianXinWesternHan|田信]] to allege policy leakage and official-commercial collusion. The incomplete source shows the coalition forming but not the accusation's proof or full outcome.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-339-2-yi-si-sharen-kuli-zhangtang-shi-ruhe-zuodao-de-luq3jaa9iruhiqiqlduewbj-luca|Hanji 339-2]] supplies the endpoint. After Zhang's suicide, final letter, estate inventory, and mother's sparse funeral alter Wudi's judgment, the emperor investigates [[ZhuMaichenWesternHan|朱买臣]], [[WangChaoWesternHan|王朝]], and [[BianTongWesternHan|边通]], treats them as aligned with Zhuang, executes the three clerks, and forces the chancellor to kill himself.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han chancellor in the Yuanding 2 court setting.
- Tries to make a joint apology and accepts fault after burial money is stolen from Emperor Wen's funerary park, while Zhang Tang stays silent.
- Becomes the subject of an investigation led by Zhang.
- Coordinates with three chief clerks under shared threat, using the source-scoped Tian Xin allegation as their counterattack route.
- Is forced to suicide when Han Wudi reverses direction.

## Qualifications

The raw transcripts' “庄青宅” and “庄卿宅” are normalized to 庄青翟 from the transmitted Zhang Tang account. Hanji 339-1 now supports coordination with the three clerks, but the sources still do not reconstruct Zhuang's full biography, the legal proof against him, each participant's acts, or the truth of the incomplete Tian Xin allegation. Claims about his limited responsibility, fear, and motives remain episode-attributed.

## What Changed

- Replaced a merely terminal association with an earlier, explicit counter-coalition under shared threat.
- Added the Tian Xin allegation as the coalition's proposed attack route while keeping its truth unresolved.
