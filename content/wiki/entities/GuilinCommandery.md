# 桂林郡 / Guilin Commandery

桂林郡 / Guilin Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-123-2-gudai-shiqi-de-daochamen-daodi-you-duo-weixian-lgcknwubwm3uzqmzgbgdlxlvcwxy]] as one of the three commanderies [[QinState|秦国]] establishes after the 214 BCE southern campaign against [[Baiyue|百越]]. The episode locates it broadly in today's Guangxi and treats it as part of Qin's attempt to turn southern conquest into direct imperial administration.

The commandery matters because it moves the Baiyue branch beyond Wang Jian's earlier Jiangnan notice. Qin now pushes deeper into Lingnan, drafts marginal groups for the campaign, and follows military occupation with commandery naming and convict settlement.
