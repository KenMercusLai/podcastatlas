# 接力长江 / Jieli Changjiang

接力长江 / Jieli Changjiang is the family-business successor program at [[ChangjiangBusinessSchool|长江商学院]] named in [[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw]]. The episode says it began in 2014 to serve founders' demand for structured support as their children entered or prepared to enter family firms.

The program matters as institutional evidence that [[FactorySecondGeneration|厂二代]] succession is becoming a recognized field rather than only a private family issue. In the episode, it sits beside 基业长青, MBA, and EMBA programs as a context where [[FanXinyu|范鑫宇]] observes second-generation successors.

## Source Position
- The program shows that private-company succession has become a business-education category.
- It helps successors compare family-specific problems across sectors and regions.
- The source still treats classroom learning as partial: the key missing capacity remains [[TacitManagementKnowledge|默会知识]] gained through operating practice.
