# Tesla Optimus

Tesla Optimus appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-why-ai-will-dwarf-every-tech-revolution-before-it-robots-manufacturing-ar-glasses-from-ces-2026-39655790]] as the episode's most aggressive humanoid-robot forecast. The host says he visited Tesla's Optimus lab, saw many people working there on a Sunday morning, and predicts [[Tesla]] may eventually be remembered more for Optimus than for cars.

The page should be read as source-scoped and speculative. The episode treats Optimus as a possible bridge from [[PhysicalAI]] to broad material automation, but [[HemantTaneja|Hemant Taneja]] also warns that robotics may advance more slowly than software AI because physical deployment lacks a simple cloud-like API layer.
