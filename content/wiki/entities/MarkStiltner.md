# Mark Stiltner

## Overview
Mark Stiltner is the [[Rapid]] Senior Director of Content and Web Marketing interviewed by [[SamDataScienceWithSam|Sam]] in [[ep-39-why-the-future-of-ai-belongs-to-divergent-thinkers]] on [[DataScienceWithSam]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Mark as a marketing leader and creator who uses ADHD as a lens for understanding AI-enabled work. His central claim is not that AI simply makes him faster, but that it helps translate nonlinear cognition into finished outputs by preserving context, giving immediate feedback, and reducing friction between idea and execution.

Mark also uses side projects as AI learning labs. His tabletop role-playing publishing work, including fully illustrated books and [[SoloAIRPGs]], lets him test AI workflows outside his regular job and then bring lessons back into marketing practice.
