# 罗路 / Luo Lu (plastic surgeon)

## Overview
罗路 is presented in the source as a plastic-surgery attending physician in a breast reconstruction group at [[ShanghaiEastHospital|上海市东方医院]] who explains how disease treatment, reconstruction, appearance, safety, and psychosocial goals meet in plastic surgery.

## Current Profile
Her public-education approach replaces broad consumer labels with clinical distinctions. She separates glandular gynecomastia from fat accumulation, contouring from weight loss, hypertrophic scars from keloids, and “美容针” from wound-specific incision and closure planning. In reconstruction, she describes direct closure, grafting, and flaps as options matched to tissue loss, exposed structures, infection, durability, function, and appearance rather than to wound depth alone.

She also emphasizes expectation management. Macromastia, scars, wounds, tumors, and microtia can carry physical and psychosocial burdens, but a desired appearance does not erase procedural risk or create a guaranteed result. The episode presents medical judgment, refusal of unsuitable procedures, and psychological support as part of treatment rather than as obstacles to service.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains plastic surgery as a medical and reconstructive specialty as well as an aesthetic one.
- Uses differential assessment to separate superficially similar conditions and match treatment to cause and tissue.
- Treats incision design, tension, tissue layers, closure, and scar control as one planning sequence.
- Connects function and objective surgical indicators with subjective appearance and patient-defined goals.
- Emphasizes safety, indication, realistic expectations, and clinician-patient communication.
