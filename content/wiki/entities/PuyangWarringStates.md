# 蒲阳 / Puyang (Warring States)

蒲阳 / Puyang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] as the [[WeiState|魏国]] place captured by [[QinState|秦国]] in 328 BCE. The attacking command is associated with Gongzi Hua and [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]].

Its importance comes from what happens after the capture. Zhang Yi advises [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] to return Puyang to Wei and send a Qin prince as hostage, then uses that apparent generosity to pressure [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]] into yielding [[Shangjun|上郡]]'s fifteen counties. The page therefore anchors the source's [[StrategicConcessionDiplomacy|欲擒故纵式让利外交]] case.
