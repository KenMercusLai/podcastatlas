# Manius Aquillius / 马尼乌斯·阿奎利乌斯

## Overview

Manius Aquillius / 马尼乌斯·阿奎利乌斯 is the Roman figure whom episode 702 associates with provoking the conflict against [[MithridatesVI]] while Rome is still strained by the [[SocialWar]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Aquillius as closely associated with [[GaiusMarius]], but it keeps his motives uncertain. He helps provoke a conflict with Mithridates of Pontus, after which Mithridates invades Asia, Roman power collapses locally, and Aquillius flees.

Aquillius matters because he helps turn Asia from an exploited Roman business field into an emergency. That emergency then becomes the command prize assigned to [[LuciusCorneliusSulla]] and contested by Marius.

## Key Characteristics

- Aquillius is tied to Marius's network in the source.
- His actions help provoke the war with Mithridates, though motives remain uncertain.
- His failure to hold Asia marks the collapse of Roman authority in the province.
- His role connects provincial exploitation to the domestic command fight in Rome.

## Evidence

- Marius association: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] says Aquillius was closely associated with Marius.
- Uncertain motive: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] explicitly says his motives in provoking conflict are uncertain.
- Eastern collapse: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] says Mithridates invaded Asia, Roman power there collapsed, and Aquillius fled.
- Command consequence: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] makes the resulting eastern war the command first assigned to Sulla and then transferred toward Marius.

## Qualifications

The page preserves the source's motive uncertainty. It does not claim Aquillius alone caused the Mithridatic War or settle his wider career.

## What Changed

- Created Aquillius as the Roman-provocation and eastern-collapse node before the Sulla-Marius command fight.
