# Manius Aquillius / 马尼乌斯·阿奎利乌斯

## Overview

Manius Aquillius / 马尼乌斯·阿奎利乌斯 is the Roman figure whom the Roman Civil War sequence associates with provoking conflict against [[MithridatesVI]] and then becoming Mithridates's most vivid propaganda victim against Roman greed.

## Current Profile

Episode 702 presents Aquillius as closely associated with [[GaiusMarius]], but it keeps his motives uncertain. He helps provoke a conflict with Mithridates of Pontus, after which Mithridates invades Asia, Roman power collapses locally, and Aquillius flees. Aquillius matters because he helps turn Asia from an exploited Roman business field into an emergency, and that emergency becomes the command prize assigned to [[LuciusCorneliusSulla]] and contested by Marius.

Episode 703 adds Aquillius's capture and execution. Mithridates's agents seize him after illness, parade him through Pergamum in chains, and kill him by pouring molten gold down his throat. The episode treats the punishment as revenge and propaganda: Mithridates presents Aquillius as a symbol of Roman greed for empire and riches.

## Key Characteristics

- Aquillius is tied to Marius's network in the source.
- His actions help provoke the war with Mithridates, though motives remain uncertain.
- His failure to hold Asia marks the collapse of Roman authority in the province.
- His capture and execution turn him into a propaganda object for Mithridates.
- His role connects provincial exploitation to the domestic command fight in Rome.

## Qualifications

The page preserves the source's motive uncertainty. It does not claim Aquillius alone caused the Mithridatic War or settle his wider career beyond the provocation, collapse, capture, and execution sequence presented here.

## What Changed

- Added Aquillius's capture, chained parade through Pergamum, molten-gold execution, and use as Mithridatic anti-Roman propaganda.
