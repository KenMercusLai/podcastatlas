# 朱瑀 / Zhu Yu (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-916-ming-hao-he-yun-hao-nage-geng-zhongyao-lmzudjq5zfkfsukmhdeiumesm9qu|Hanji 916]] backfills Zhu Yu before Lyu Qiang names him. After [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] rallies the eunuch side, the episode says Cao Jie and Zhu Yu's power rises again, while [[ShenZhongLateHan|沈忠]]'s later memorial singles Zhu Yu out for fabricating rebellion charges, disturbing the palace, seizing seals, and dividing the dowager and emperor.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-917-ruhe-xunsu-zhaodao-shengzhi-jiaxin-de-pojudian-lk2zt5c3onvui9a4ppbowshy66tg|Hanji 917]] introduces 朱瑀 / Zhu Yu as a late-Han eunuch favorite named beside [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] in [[LyuQiangLateHan|吕强]]'s remonstrance. Lyu Qiang treats him as part of the group whose low character, slander, flattery, and family privilege make title grants look detached from public merit.

The closing hook also places Zhu Yu in [[YangQiuLateHan|阳球]]'s next target set after [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]]. That makes him part of the anti-eunuch escalation immediately before Hanji 918 shows how targeting Cao Jie rebounds.

The transcript's "朱羽" is normalized here to 朱瑀 and kept source-scoped unless later ingests add more detail.
