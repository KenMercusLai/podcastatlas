## Overview

李文 / Li Wen enters the wiki as a Western Han official and rival of [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]] whose attempt to find damaging material in official documents is preempted by a covert accusation.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-338-2-yinwei-yi-chang-anmo-yinfa-de-xuean-lp8mwe-z21lhqhg4lwnj-jmeotag|Hanji 338-2]] presents Li Wen inside a retaliatory court chain. After enduring conflict with Zhang Tang, Li searches documentary work for a basis to report him. Zhang's favored clerk [[LuYezhuWesternHan|鲁谒居]] secretly has someone accuse Li of grave misconduct; Han Wudi assigns the case to Zhang, who condemns and kills his rival. The later disclosure by Lu's brother makes Li's death an evidentiary route into Zhang's own investigation.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official in a long-running conflict with Zhang Tang.
- Looks for documentary evidence that could support an accusation against Zhang.
- Is preempted by a covert report arranged by Lu Yezhu.
- Is tried by Zhang Tang and executed in the episode's account.
- Becomes central after death because the concealed accusation is later disclosed.
