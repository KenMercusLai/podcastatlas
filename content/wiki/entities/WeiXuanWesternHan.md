## Overview

Wei Xuan / 卫玄 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-you-xinji-de-ren-you-duo-kepa-lnhkk70905eqg4lqwh3mp2sxzamc|Hanji 616]] as a Wei-clan relative named in the punishment wave after the [[DogBloodCase|狗血案]].

## Current Profile

The source does not make Wei Xuan an independent actor. His importance is evidentiary: by naming him with Wei Bao and the wider Wei family, the episode shows that Wang Mang uses the case to damage Pingdi's maternal clan far beyond the immediate dog-blood participants.

## Key Characteristics

- Wei-clan relative connected to Pingdi's maternal family.
- Named victim in the case's expanded punishment wave.
- Evidence for the near-destruction of the Wei consort-kin network.

## Evidence

- Punishment wave: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-you-xinji-de-ren-you-duo-kepa-lnhkk70905eqg4lqwh3mp2sxzamc|Hanji 616]] names Wei Xuan among the Wei relatives punished after the dog-blood case.
- Clan damage: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-you-xinji-de-ren-you-duo-kepa-lnhkk70905eqg4lqwh3mp2sxzamc|Hanji 616]] says the Wei clan is nearly destroyed and declines after the case.
