## Overview
Francesco Schettino is the Costa Concordia captain used by the episode as a negative case of crisis leadership displaced by self-protection.

## Current Profile
The supplied account emphasizes Schettino's delayed evacuation response, reputation management, blame shifting, departure from the ship, and resistance to orders to return. Galinsky interprets the pattern as abandonment of a leader's protective and stewardship duties when accepting reality would threaten the leader's own status.

## Key Characteristics
- Delays protective action after the ship strikes a reef.
- Prioritizes explanation and reputation over evacuation.
- Shifts responsibility despite occupying the primary command role.
- Leaves the vessel while passengers remain aboard.
