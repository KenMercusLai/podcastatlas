# Chris Bell

## Overview
Chris Bell is a former technology design leader in Mercer Island, Washington, whose [[MarketplaceTech]] interview opens the [[ClockedOut|Clocked Out]] series with a first-person account of retiring shortly before age 50.

## Current Profile
Bell's wiki significance is not a claim that technology careers generally end early. His case shows how [[RecurringLayoffManagementBurden]], family needs, and financial capacity can combine into [[EarlyRetirementWorkAutonomy]]: an exit from formal management that preserves the possibility of future self-directed work.

## Key Characteristics
- Progressed from working as a solo designer to director-level management of other managers in technology.
- Describes recurring responsibility for selecting employees for layoffs as a major reason the management role stopped fitting.
- Made the retirement decision with his wife and family amid responsibilities involving children and aging parents.
- Defines retirement as control over whether and how he works, rather than permanent withdrawal from productive activity.
- Acknowledges ongoing financial uncertainty while reporting no regret so far.

## Evidence
- Career and exit - [[tech-20260907-0907-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260907-0907-mp-tech-pod-128]] identifies Bell as a former design and management leader who retired at the end of May 2026, shortly before turning 50.
- Management burden - [[tech-20260907-0907-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260907-0907-mp-tech-pod-128]] attributes his exit partly to an almost annual cycle of deciding whom to lay off.
- Family and autonomy - [[tech-20260907-0907-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260907-0907-mp-tech-pod-128]] describes the family decision, his time with two daughters and aging parents, and his preference for work undertaken on his own terms.

## Qualifications
Bell's account is a short personal interview, not a representative study of technology managers or early retirees. The supplied source does not name his former employers, quantify his finances, or establish the relative weight of each reason for leaving.
