# 黄子翊 / Huang Ziyi

黄子翊 / Huang Ziyi is the [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] reporter and [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] speaker in [[ep266-dang-ai-zhonggou-daxue-women-gai-ruhe-dingyi-hao-zhuanye-llbzcgsefktcvkq-f37hoan79dd]]. The source spells her name 黄子翊; the wiki keeps this page separate from [[HuangZiyiSanlian|黄子逸 / Huang Ziyi]] unless later sources show they are the same person.

Her contribution in EP266 is the engineering and software-education branch of the AI-era major-choice discussion. She explains how AI pressures junior programming and other rule-execution work, why first-year foundational training can be hollowed out, how [[NewEngineeringEducation|新工科教育]] tries to move students toward real system problems, and why students need [[TShapedAITalent|T-shaped AI talent]] rather than a simple hot-major bet.
