# Mary of Burgundy

## Overview

Mary of Burgundy is Charles the Bold's daughter and heir, whose marriage to Maximilian of Habsburg redirected the northern Burgundian inheritance into the [[HabsburgDynasty|Habsburg dynasty]].

## Current Profile

Mary inherited a crisis after [[CharlesTheBold|Charles the Bold]] died at Nancy. The French crown recovered the original Duchy of Burgundy, while competing powers sought control of the remaining territories.

Her marriage to Maximilian preserved much of the Low Countries inheritance outside direct French absorption. Through their descendants, the Burgundian lands passed to [[CharlesV|Charles V]], connecting the failed ducal kingdom to later Habsburg rule, the Spanish Netherlands, and the political fragmentation of the Low Countries.

## Key Characteristics

- Sole legitimate child and dynastic heir of Charles the Bold.
- Successor facing immediate French and imperial pressure.
- Marriage partner in the decisive Burgundian-Habsburg union.
- Carrier of the northern Burgundian inheritance into a new dynasty.
- Link between the Valois dukes and Charles V's Low Countries realm.
