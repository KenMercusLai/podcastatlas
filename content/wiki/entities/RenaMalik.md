# Rena Malik

## Overview
Rena Malik is the board-certified urologist and pelvic surgeon interviewed by [[AndrewHuberman]] about male and female urinary, pelvic-floor, and sexual health.

## Current Profile
In the available source, Malik uses a mechanism- and function-oriented clinical frame. She separates desire from genital arousal, weak pelvic-floor function from excessive tension, urinary infection from mimics, and hormone effects from vascular, neural, medication, psychological, and relationship contributors. Her practical emphasis is individualized assessment, non-shaming communication, and escalation when symptoms are persistent, painful, obstructive, urgent, or medically complex.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains sexual and urinary symptoms through interacting vascular, neural, muscular, hormonal, medication, cardiometabolic, psychological, and relationship factors.
- Distinguishes pelvic-floor weakness from excessive tension or poor coordination before recommending strengthening.
- Uses functional impairment and distress, rather than moral judgment alone, to discuss pornography and masturbation.
- Treats genital arousal and lubrication as physiologic responses that do not prove desire or consent.
- Prioritizes health foundations and qualified assessment before supplements or broad self-treatment protocols.
- Frames persistent erectile dysfunction as a possible cardiovascular signal rather than simply a testosterone problem.
