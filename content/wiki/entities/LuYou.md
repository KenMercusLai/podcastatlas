# 陆游 / Lu You

陆游 / Lu You enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-150-plus-zaiye-bujian-2022-qing-duo-guanzhao-2023-fjhgtqac9r-1rpjh6oubmspqywue|Hanji 150 PLUS New Year special]] as the poet cited for New Year imagery. The episode uses a Southern Song poem that mentions peach charms and pepper wine to show how 元旦 customs survive as literary images as well as practices.

The page is source-scoped. Lu You is not being summarized as a whole poet here; he is an evidence anchor for [[YuanDanFestivalLayering|元旦节日层累]], where poetry stores the memory of seasonal objects, door symbols, and ritual drink.
