# Charlotte Howard

## Overview
Charlotte Howard is identified in the source as The Economist's U.S. editor and provides the episode's national interpretation of the 2026 midterms.

## Current Profile
Howard frames the election as more than a seat forecast. In her account, it tests [[DonaldTrump]]'s standing, affects his final two years in office, shapes whether Republicans move beyond the Trump era, and may determine whether Congress becomes a substantive check on the executive. She also stresses uncertainty: Democratic optimism depends on difficult red-state races, historical polling misses, oil and Middle East developments, and slow-counting systems that can delay a clear result.
