# 北地郡 / Beidi Commandery

北地郡 / Beidi Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-85-xuantaihou-weihe-sitong-30-nian-hou-ansha-le-laoqingren-lg7zy6vqtdjcufou4-mox5qkg2ct5]] as the administrative result of [[QinState|秦国]]'s destruction of [[YiquState|义渠]]. After [[XuanTaihou|芈八子 / 宣太后]] kills the Yiqu king at [[GanquanPalace|甘泉宫]], Qin moves against the leaderless polity and establishes Beidi Commandery in 271 BCE.

The episode makes Beidi important as more than a conquered district. Together with [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]] and [[Shangjun|上郡]], it gives Qin a connected northern frontier line. That line lets Qin turn the older problem of Yiqu pressure into [[QinFrontierBufferManagement|frontier buffer management]] by commandery administration and defensive wall-building.

The host says the commandery seat was Yiqu county and links the likely old Yiqu city area to modern Ning County in Qingyang, Gansu. The exact archaeological-geography claim remains source-scoped to this episode.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-121-yingzheng-zao-shijie-zuizao-gaosu-gonglu-2200nian-huangcao-busheng-ls1u8dmuvs5bbrzadf2dtd00js-p]] adds Beidi to Qin Shi Huang's first post-unification route in 220 BCE. Paired with [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]], Beidi marks the westward/northern old-Qin geography through which the episode interprets the journey as [[HighTempleRite|高庙礼]] rather than ordinary touring.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-149-xichu-bawang-xiangyu-weihe-shijun-2-lpohbb3prsgvokpjipfd7i1ntiwn|Hanji 149 part 2]] adds Beidi to Liu Bang's Guanzhong consolidation. After Longxi is taken, Han forces attack Beidi and defeat [[ZhangPingQinGeneral|章平]], which lets the source describe Liu Bang's northern boundary reaching the south bank of the Yellow River loop and the repair of Qin-era frontier fortifications.
