# 北地郡 / Beidi Commandery

## Overview
北地郡 is a northern frontier commandery in accounts spanning Qin's destruction of Yiqu, Qin Shi Huang's post-unification journey and Liu Bang's Chu-Han consolidation. Its recurrence shows how conquered frontier territory became an administrative and strategic inheritance across regimes.

## Current Profile
The commandery's establishment, ritual-route role and later Han occupation are related but separate episodes. The historical podcast's geographic reconstruction and ritual interpretation are attributed readings rather than direct proof from the base chronicle.

## Key Characteristics
- Qin's conquest of [[YiquState|义渠]] culminated in the establishment of Beidi in 271 BCE after the Yiqu king's killing at [[GanquanPalace|甘泉宫]].
- Together with [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]] and [[Shangjun|上郡]], it formed a northern line for commandery administration, buffer management and defenses.
- It marked Qin Shi Huang's western/northern old-Qin itinerary after unification in 220 BCE, which the host interprets as a ritual journey.
- In the Chu-Han struggle Liu Bang's capture of Beidi after defeating Zhang Ping extended his Guanzhong frontier; later administration grouped it with Zhongdi and Longxi after Feiqiu fell.

## Evidence
- **Conquest and institutionalization:** The historical episode relates [[XuanTaihou|宣太后]]'s decades-long Yiqu-king relationship, the king's killing at Ganquan, Qin's destruction of the leaderless polity, and creation of Beidi in 271 BCE, with Yiqu county as seat. It associates the old city with the modern Ning County/Qingyang, Gansu vicinity as a host-level geographic inference. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-85-xuantaihou-weihe-sitong-30-nian-hou-ansha-le-laoqingren-lg7zy6vqtdjcufou4-mox5qkg2ct5]]
- **Frontier system:** Alongside Longxi and Shangjun, Beidi joined a connected northern defensive line and a basis for wall-building against northern Hu groups; control of Qin's rear helped sustain eastward pressure. This is an interpretation of [[QinFrontierBufferManagement]], not proof that the court planned every later defensive work at the moment of conquest. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-85-xuantaihou-weihe-sitong-30-nian-hou-ansha-le-laoqingren-lg7zy6vqtdjcufou4-mox5qkg2ct5]]
- **Imperial route:** In 220 BCE the recorded itinerary runs from [[Xianyang]] through Longxi and Beidi, via Jitou mountain and Huizhong. The episode reads the journey through old Qin lands as a possible [[HighTempleRite]] toward [[YongchengQin]], rather than ordinary sightseeing; the route itself does not establish that motive. [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-121-yingzheng-zao-shijie-zuizao-gaosu-gonglu-2200nian-huangcao-busheng-ls1u8dmuvs5bbrzadf2dtd00js-p]]
- **Han takeover and reorganization:** A Chu-Han account has [[LiuBang]] take Longxi, attack Beidi and defeat [[ZhangPingQinGeneral|章平]]; the host describes the northern boundary reaching the Yellow River loop's south bank and repairs to Qin-era frontier fortifications. Another account places Beidi with [[ZhongdiCommandery|中地郡]] and Longxi in the administrative settlement after the year-long siege and flooding of [[Feiqiu|废丘]], [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]]'s suicide and the pacification of former Yong territory. ## Qualifications
- The sources describe Beidi's Qin creation and a later Han administrative setup. They should not be flattened into a single founding event; the latter is a [[ConquestCommanderyTransition]] after regime change.
- Yiqu county as seat, the precise modern site, the motivation of [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]]'s journey, and details of frontier repairs are claims of the episodes rather than independently checked archaeological or court records. Xuan Taihou's personal versus strategic motive is unresolved in the source.

## What Changed
- The commandery's Qin creation, imperial route and Han reuse are distinguished as successive frontier functions rather than source-arrival episodes.
- The geographic and ritual interpretations remain qualified rather than treated as settled fact.

## Relationships
- [[QinState]] - conqueror of Yiqu; [[XuanTaihou]]'s act at [[GanquanPalace]] precedes the commandery's formation.
- [[LongxiCommandery]] - partner in both Qin frontier organization and Han post-conquest administration; [[Shangjun]] is a northern Qin defensive neighbor.
- [[QinEastwardPressure]] - rear security made eastern campaigns more viable in the historical episode's reading.
- [[QinShiHuang]] - traveler through Beidi in the old-Qin itinerary toward the possible [[YongchengQin]] ritual setting.
- [[ThreeQinContainment]] - older frontier and Guanzhong military geography inherited by Liu Bang.
- [[Feiqiu]] - final former-Yong stronghold whose fall preceded a commandery reorganization.
