# Sasanian Empire

## Overview

The Sasanian Empire is the third major pre-Islamic Iranian imperial formation identified by [[AliAnsari]], following the [[AchaemenidEmpire]] and [[ParthianEmpire]].

## Current Profile

[[41-persia-glt6414068538]] presents the Sasanians as especially influential in defining Iran as a political territory and in compiling historical traditions that shaped later Iranian self-understanding. The episode also associates Sasanian and Parthian Iran with heavily armored cavalry and a courtly heroic culture later visible in Persian epic.

This profile is deliberately narrow. The source uses the Sasanians to explain continuity in Iranian political memory and pre-Islamic imperial succession, not to provide a full account of Sasanian institutions, religion, wars, or collapse.

## Key Characteristics

- Third major pre-Islamic Iranian empire in the episode's sequence.
- Political formation associated with applying the name Iran to a territorial state.
- Compiler and transmitter of historical traditions central to later Iranian memory.
- Courtly and military culture associated with armored cavalry and heroic epic.
- Important bridge between ancient imperial inheritance and post-conquest Persian cultural reassertion.

## Evidence

Iranian territorial identity:
- [[41-persia-glt6414068538]] attributes to the Sasanians an especially important role in naming and defining Iran as a political territory.

Historical memory and court culture:

## Qualifications

The page is bounded to one overview episode. Claims about priority, territorial continuity, historical compilation, cavalry development, and influence on later epic remain source-attributed and do not substitute for a full Sasanian political, religious, social, or military history.
