# 敖仓 / Aocang

敖仓 / Aocang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-134-ni-buzhidao-de-pofu-chenzhou-banben-ltnry1fva2hzplw4ft8nrfpoiolw|Qinji 134]] as the grain source behind [[WangLiQin|王离]]'s siege pressure at [[Julu|巨鹿]]. The episode says Qin food mainly comes from the Yellow River-side Aocang area north of Luoyang before being moved through the protected corridor toward the Julu front.

The page's importance is logistical. [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] can keep Wang Li's force large, active, and fed because the supply line still works; Julu's defenders, by contrast, are losing food and soldiers inside the siege. Once [[YingBu|英布]] and [[PuGeneral|蒲将军]] break the yongdao system, Aocang no longer translates smoothly into battlefield strength around Julu.

Aocang therefore gives [[JuluBattle|巨鹿之战]] a supply-chain layer. The episode's version of [[BreakingCauldronsAndSinkingBoats|破釜沉舟]] depends on first changing the material balance through [[TransportCorridorSeverance|交通线切割]], not only on creating desperate courage among Chu troops.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-153-xiayi-huace-canbai-hou-liubang-ruhe-qisi-huisheng-2-ll-rvx07pyw-qg1jofkjro-qfllu|Hanji 153 part 2]] reuses Aocang on the Han side after the [[PengchengBattle|彭城之战]]. Once [[LiuBang|刘邦]] begins holding the [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]] front, he orders road construction from Xingyang toward the Yellow River ferry so Aocang grain can feed the army. The granary therefore shifts from a Qin supply source attacked before Julu into a Han logistical support point for [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-159-1-liubang-xijiao-de-yuren-zhishu-lga3puexunjzeu3s8pvt8saaa8n3|Hanji 159-1]] gives the reversal. After [[LongJuChuGeneral|龙且]] restores pressure for Chu and joins [[XiangYu|项羽]], Chu captures Aocang and controls Yellow River transport. Aocang therefore becomes the source of Han's crisis rather than its stabilizer: once the granary and transport line are lost, Liu Bang faces food shortage at Xingyang and becomes receptive to [[LiShiqi|郦食其]]'s risky restoration proposal.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-162-1-ta-ti-liubang-qu-si-rujin-chengle-zhengzhou-de-shouhushen-li3su2zlmbm1ift9nibujgtcrxwm|Hanji 162-1]] uses Aocang counterfactually in the host's critique of [[XiangYu|项羽]]. After [[LiuBang|刘邦]] escapes [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]], the host argues that Xiang Yu might have pressed the central front by using Aocang as a rear logistics base, but instead follows Liu Bang south and becomes exposed to [[PengYue|彭越]]'s grain-route pressure.
