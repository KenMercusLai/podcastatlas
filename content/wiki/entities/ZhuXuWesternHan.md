## Overview

朱徐 / Zhu Xu is synthesized as a late [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] figure whose one source-backed wiki role is loyal action after [[DongXian|董贤]]'s fall. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-605-dongxian-sihou-tamen-liuxiale-eyu-de-yanlei-lladjfbixqzhxjurjs1z8ztsa1st|Hanji 605]] contrasts him with opportunistic public grief outside the Dong household: Zhu Xu had once been treated well by Dong Xian, resigns by self-impeachment after Dong-family disaster, retrieves Dong Xian's body, and buries him.

## Current Profile

Zhu Xu matters because his action is costly and poorly aligned with the new power environment. In the source's reading, most surrounding behavior after Dong Xian's fall is driven by advantage, theft, or self-protection. Zhu Xu instead treats past patronage as an obligation that survives political collapse. That makes him a counterexample inside the episode's broader human-nature frame.

The same source also makes his loyalty unsafe. [[WangMang|王莽]] later hears of Zhu Xu's act and kills him under another accusation. Zhu Xu therefore links private gratitude to political exposure: honoring a fallen patron can become dangerous when the new ruler is actively clearing the old favorite's network.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han figure remembered here through one loyalty act rather than a broad biography.
- Former beneficiary of Dong Xian's generosity.
- Resigns through self-impeachment after Dong Xian's fall.
- Retrieves and buries Dong Xian's body.
- Killed by Wang Mang under another accusation after the burial act becomes known.
