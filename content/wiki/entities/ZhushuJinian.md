# 《竹书纪年》 / Bamboo Annals

《竹书纪年》 / Bamboo Annals appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-2-lmfzo4sl9nd5kz3mrxdl1dl9efei]] as the text the episode cites for the [[JinYouGong|晋幽公]] case. In that account, Jin You Gong's wife [[QinYing|秦嬴]] directs bandits to kill him, making the record useful for thinking about [[BanditAssassinationAttribution]].

The page's wiki role is evidentiary. It gives the episode a named alternative textual source while preserving the source's caution: the goal is not to solve every assassination notice, but to notice when terse records may hide a political operator.
