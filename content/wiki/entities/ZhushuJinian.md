# 《竹书纪年》 / Bamboo Annals

《竹书纪年》 / Bamboo Annals appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-2-lmfzo4sl9nd5kz3mrxdl1dl9efei]] as the text the episode cites for the [[JinYouGong|晋幽公]] case. In that account, Jin You Gong's wife [[QinYing|秦嬴]] directs bandits to kill him, making the record useful for thinking about [[BanditAssassinationAttribution]].

The page's wiki role is evidentiary. It gives the episode a named alternative textual source while preserving the source's caution: the goal is not to solve every assassination notice, but to notice when terse records may hide a political operator.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-3-lmhj4le2mwu1yhhl-qmuoqkfmhjf]] adds another evidentiary use. The host cites the Bamboo Annals for [[GongzhongHuan|公中缓]] going to Zhao's capital to ask for help during [[WeiState|魏国]]'s succession crisis.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] uses the Bamboo Annals for the [[XianglingWarringStates|襄陵]] aftermath. In the episode's account, [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]] uses Han forces to defeat a coalition there, after which [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] seeks peace with Wei through [[Jingshe|景舍]].
