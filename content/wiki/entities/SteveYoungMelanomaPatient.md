# Steve Young (Melanoma Trial Patient)

## Overview
Steve Young is represented in the wiki as the patient case used by [[TheIntelligence]] to explain a personalized mRNA melanoma-vaccine trial.

## Current Profile
The source describes Steve Young as a musician and music teacher in Hertfordshire who was diagnosed in 2023 with T4 nodular melanoma after a long-standing lump on his head was assessed clinically. After tumor removal, he matched a clinical trial involving a newly licensed immunotherapy treatment and a personalized mRNA vaccine associated with [[Moderna]].

His case is useful because it translates [[IndividualizedCancerVaccine]] from platform language into patient workflow. Tumor tissue was sent to American labs for DNA sequencing, the vaccine was mapped to his cancer, and he received a first dose in 2024. The episode says he is in year three of a seven-year trial with good blood work and clear scans so far, but it does not treat that as proof of cure or routine access.

## Key Characteristics
- Patient example for a personalized mRNA melanoma-vaccine trial rather than a general cancer-vaccine beneficiary.
- Connects diagnosis, tumor removal, trial eligibility, sequencing, manufacturing, dosing, and monitoring into one clinical path.
- Carries the episode's cautious promise: encouraging individual follow-up without settled survival evidence.
- Makes patient risk communication concrete through recurrence-risk numbers and trial uncertainty.
