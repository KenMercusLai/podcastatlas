# Dan Pashman

## Overview
Dan Pashman appears as the food-podcast host and tasting foil in the [[PlanetMoney]] episode on branded produce.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Pashman as a consumer-sensory checkpoint. He enters as someone skeptical of grape tomatoes, then tastes [[ScarletSunrise|Scarlet Sunrise]] and gives the variety a favorable verdict. His role helps connect technical breeding and market strategy to whether consumers can actually perceive a better product.

## Key Characteristics
- Food-media figure used as a taster rather than as the episode's economic analyst.
- Provides the skeptical-consumer setup for evaluating Scarlet Sunrise.
- Helps make product differentiation concrete through flavor, texture, sweetness, and acidity.
