# 《搜神记》

《搜神记》 is the early Chinese zhiguai collection at the center of [[06-soushenji-ershisi-xiao-zhe-kou-guo-ganbao-bu-bei-536821044]]. The episode presents [[GanBao|干宝]]'s book as a mixed archive of ghosts, omens, gods, animals, disasters, historical figures, local cults, strange natural observations, and story prototypes rather than a single-purpose moral manual.

The source's main interpretive move is [[ZhiguaiArchiveValue]]. The book can contain [[TwentyFourFilialExemplars|filial-piety]] material, but it also contains [[LiJiSlaysSnake|李寄斩蛇]], revenge narratives, abused women who remarry, harmless or sympathetic nonhuman beings, and stories where rulers kill charismatic or knowledgeable figures. That range makes the book important for [[StrangeTaleMoralPluralism]] and [[ClassicReadingComplexity]].

## Source Position
- The episode treats the book's contradictions as part of its value: it records folk belief and anecdotal knowledge without reconciling every item into one doctrine.
- Its later influence appears through folk stories, opera, drama, fairy-tale-like motifs, underworld imagination, and famous filial-piety exempla.
- The source keeps belief and skepticism in tension: 干宝 may sincerely believe in omens, while modern readers can still use the record as evidence of narrative, religion, and perception.
