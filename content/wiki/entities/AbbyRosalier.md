# Abby Rosalier

Abby Rosalier is the Dallas founder of [[AbbyRose]] in [[advice-line-with-bobbi-brown-of-jones-road-beauty-september-2025-427590c2-11bc-43fa-9c92-d1eccf05842a]]. She says she started the custom phone-case business at 16, built it through COVID-era growth, and now runs it with family involvement, TikTok live selling, and a large social audience.

Her Advice Line question is whether to double down on live social selling or expand into wholesale and paid advertising. [[BobbiBrown]] and [[GuyRaz]] treat Rosalier's on-camera presence as both an asset and a bottleneck: the next experiment is to bring in her sister, family, friends, guests, or commission-based reps before assuming retail or paid acquisition is the right answer.
