# Howard Carter

## Overview

Howard Carter was the excavator who discovered Tutankhamun's tomb in the Valley of the Kings in 1922 after a career that began in archaeological illustration.

## Current Profile

[[27-tutankhamun-glt2697164979]] follows Carter from illustration and work with Flinders Petrie at Amarna into the Egyptian antiquities service and privately funded excavation. After Theodore Davis judged the Valley of the Kings exhausted and Lord Carnarvon nearly ended his support, Carter persisted in believing that [[Tutankhamun]]'s tomb remained undiscovered.

The discovery made Carter part of the tomb's public legend, especially through the reported “wonderful things” exchange. Yet his survival also works against the supernatural curse narrative that grew around Carnarvon's later death.

## Key Characteristics

- Began as an illustrator before developing an excavation career in Egypt.
- Worked at Amarna and in the Valley of the Kings before the Tutankhamun discovery.
- Persisted after other excavators declared the valley exhausted.
- Depended on Lord Carnarvon's long-running private financial support.
- Became a central character in the media mythology of the tomb while outliving the alleged curse.
