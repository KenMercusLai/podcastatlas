## Overview
小块 / Xiao Kuai appears as a central panel participant in a [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] live episode about emotional stability, online argument, blocking, road conflict, and intentionally contacting disliked people.

## Current Profile
Xiao Kuai's current wiki role is the episode's strongest advocate for widening through disagreement. He describes arguing with people on opposing-view sites as either a way to win and feel better or to learn when he cannot win. He also resists deleting and blocking people too quickly because he fears narrowing into an information cocoon.

The source makes his position concrete through offline conflict stories. In the road-rage incident and drunk-driving crash story, he frames restraint and limited claims as a "happiness-based" choice rather than pure fear or passivity. The rest of the panel repeatedly contests that interpretation, which makes Xiao Kuai important not because he settles the issue, but because he forces the difference between moral restraint, self-protection, and rationalized avoidance into the open.

## Key Characteristics
- Central dissent-contact voice in the emotional-stability panel.
- Treats argument with opposing views as both emotional release and learning opportunity.
- Resists over-cleaning his online environment because he fears becoming narrow or self-satisfied.
- Frames conflict retreat as preserving happiness, while accepting that others may read it as weakness.
- Uses performance self-reflection, including Trump-related jokes, to connect persuasive ability with self-restraint.
