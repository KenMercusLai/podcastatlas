# 刘晓漾 / Liu Xiaoyang

## Overview
刘晓漾 / Liu Xiaoyang is the rural woman at the center of the 《人物》 profile 《平原上的娜拉》 and this [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]] discussion. Her earlier statement “宁可痛苦，不要麻木” and later attempts to leave, work in cities, return home, and preserve reading, music, flowers, and solitude make her a source-scoped figure for aspiration without a clean success ending.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Liu as dissatisfied with a narrow prescribed life but unclear about one concrete destination. She had a supportive husband, children, and a home to return to, yet also faced limited urban skills, income constraints, family attachment, and the burden of public expectation after [[ZhangYueCCTV|张越]]’s interview. Her repeated departures and returns are therefore neither proof of liberation nor proof of failure. They show how a wish for spiritual recognition can remain real even when it does not become a stable occupation, literary identity, or permanent urban life.

## Key Characteristics
- Rural woman publicly associated with the refusal of numbness.
- Repeated experimenter whose departures did not yield a single final transformation.
- Reader and music lover seeking understanding and a more expansive inner life.
- Person protected and constrained by family support, attachment, and responsibility.
- Public symbol whose own life became burdened by other people’s hopes for a successful ending.

## Qualifications
This profile is mediated through the hosts’ reading of a magazine article, not Liu Xiaoyang’s complete first-person testimony. Her motives, mental and physical health, city experiences, writing aims, family relationships, and present views should not be inferred beyond the source.

## What Changed
- Established Liu Xiaoyang as a figure whose significance lies in voiced desire and repeated action rather than a conventional success outcome.
- Kept departure, return, family support, public attention, and ordinary-life meaning in one qualified profile.
