# Aman Narang

Aman Narang is the [[Toast]] co-founder and CEO interviewed by [[GuyRaz]] in [[toast-aman-narang-how-a-long-wait-for-the-dinner-check-launched-a-2-billion-business-6eca81bc-b0e1-47e2-a87d-f3b243e76a97]]. The source says he earned a master's degree in computer science from [[MIT]], joined [[Endeca]] in 2006, met [[SteveFredette]] there, and later co-founded Toast with Fredette and [[JohnGraham]] after the [[Oracle]] acquisition.

The episode makes Aman a founder-learning case. He initially hesitated to leave Oracle without a settled idea, then joined the restaurant payment app effort after [[FirebrandSaints]] exposed a slow-check problem. His account emphasizes that the first product failed, that the [[DwellTime]] outage made restaurant software's failure intolerance obvious, and that his 2024 CEO return was grounded in long company context rather than a new outsider mandate.
