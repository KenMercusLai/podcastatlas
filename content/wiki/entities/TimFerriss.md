# Tim Ferriss

## Overview
Tim Ferriss is an author, podcaster, investor, teacher, and philanthropist represented in the wiki through a founder-advice episode and a wide-ranging [[HubermanLab]] interview.

## Current Profile
Across the bounded sources, Ferriss's durable role is experimentalist-teacher: he looks for overlooked signals, translates them into questions and bounded tests, records outcomes, and turns the results into practical teaching. In founder advice, this appears as focused channel trials, made-to-order tests, and identity diversification. In the Huberman interview, it expands into [[EdgeCaseOpportunityDiscovery]], disciplined [[SelfExperimentation]], [[ExperimentalLifeDesign]], psychedelic research philanthropy, attention design, and emergent creative work through [[Cockpunch]].

His current profile also includes a movement away from optimization as an end in itself. Ferriss still values measurement, adherence, and leverage, but now pairs them with meditation, nature, awe, integration time, fewer inputs, reciprocal mentorship, and projects whose value is not reducible to scale or public status.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses questions and edge cases to locate assumptions, unmet needs, and uncrowded opportunities.
- Prefers reversible, staged experiments over permanent strategic swings.
- Treats records and scientific literacy as checks on autobiographical evidence.
- Diversifies identity through offline, artistic, philanthropic, and unfamiliar projects.
- Converts reflection into calendar, environment, and attention decisions.
- Supports psychedelic research while warning against unsupervised or contraindicated use.
- Frames carefully timed disclosure of suffering as a possible form of service.

## Qualifications
Ferriss's health, diet, supplement, cold-exposure, fasting, retreat, and psychedelic accounts are personal reports and public education, not individualized medical or psychiatric guidance. Repeated anecdotes can motivate investigation but do not establish population-level effects. The source also does not independently audit the reported Cockpunch fundraising or every research-funding outcome.

## What Changed
- Expanded the profile from founder advice into experimental learning, attention design, research philanthropy, and creative practice.
- Added the shift from optimization alone toward well-being, de-optimization, and integration.
- Added explicit evidence and supervision limits around bodily and psychedelic experimentation.
