# 峣关 / Yaoguan

峣关 / Yaoguan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-138-wudu-qiannian-de-lishi-zhenxiang-zhaogao-bushi-taijian-2-lvtievjrclb1lxxxxr6k-q7kryto|Qinji 138 part 2]] as the pass [[ZiyingQin|子婴]] tries to reinforce after killing [[ZhaoGao|赵高]]. The episode locates it around southeast [[LantianLateQin|蓝田]], making it the defensive line that follows [[Wuguan|武关]] in [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s approach toward Qin's heartland.

The Yaoguan episode is not only a battle notice. [[ZhangLiang|张良]] tells Liu Bang not to underestimate Qin troops, advises flags on nearby mountains, and sends [[LiShiqi|郦食其]] and [[LuJia|陆贾]] to persuade Qin generals. When the generals consider peace, Zhang Liang warns that their soldiers may not obey a private surrender, so Liu Bang bypasses the pass and attacks while Qin is relaxed.

## Key Claims
- Yaoguan is the pass Ziying tries to defend after Zhao Gao's death, but the defensive move comes too late to restore Qin's position.
- The source ties Yaoguan to a hybrid method: visual deception, surrender persuasion, and attack timing.
- Zhang Liang treats negotiations as militarily useful but politically uncertain because commanders and soldiers may not have the same willingness to defect.
