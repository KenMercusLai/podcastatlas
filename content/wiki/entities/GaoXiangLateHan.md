## Overview
[[GaoXiangLateHan]] is a former [[YuanShang]] subordinate whose defection to [[CaoCao]] is paired with [[LyuKuangLateHan]] and then targeted by [[YuanTan]]'s secret seal-lure.

## Current Profile
In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1061-yuanshao-zhizi-he-shafu-chouren-jiecheng-qinjia-lnsnj6ipiyscw-vopnjjgoxpnt5r|Hanji 1061]], Gao Xiang and Lyu Kuang defect from Yuan Shang to Cao Cao after Yuan Shang retreats toward Ye. Yuan Tan secretly grants them general seals, hoping to pull them to himself, and Cao Cao interprets the move as proof of Yuan Tan's unreliability.

## Key Characteristics
- Acts as a defector from Yuan Shang to Cao Cao during Cao's intervention for Yuan Tan.
- Becomes part of Yuan Tan's private attempt to capture military allegiance.
- Helps reveal the unstable trust structure beneath the anti-Yuan-Shang alignment.
