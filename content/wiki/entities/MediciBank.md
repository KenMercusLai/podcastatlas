## Overview

The Medici Bank was the financial institution associated with the [[HouseOfMedici|House of Medici]] whose weakening branches and liabilities under [[LorenzoDeMedici|Lorenzo de' Medici]] exposed the material fragility beneath the family's cultural and political prestige.

## Current Profile

The source presents the bank not as a permanently secure foundation of Medici power but as a declining network. Its London and Bruges branches collapsed, other branches struggled, and Lorenzo borrowed heavily while continuing to perform princely magnificence.

Financial and political boundaries became porous. Lorenzo used cousins' trust funds and later Florentine public money to settle debts, making bank mismanagement more than a private commercial failure. The case therefore connects credit, family control, officeholding influence, and public resources inside a formally republican order.

The bank's decline did not immediately destroy Medici authority because diplomacy, patronage, institutional control, and reputation could temporarily compensate for financial weakness. That separation between visible prestige and underlying solvency is central to [[PoliticalMagnificenceStructuralFragility]].

## Key Characteristics

- Multi-branch banking institution tied to Medici family power.
- Financial base whose London and Bruges branches collapsed in the source account.
- Institution weakened by borrowing and poor management under Lorenzo.
- Channel through which family, commercial, and public resources became entangled.
- Example of prestige persisting after underlying financial capacity had deteriorated.

## Qualifications

The source supplies an interpretive summary, not accounts, branch records, or a complete institutional chronology. The scale and timing of losses, responsibility among partners and managers, legality of transfers, creditor outcomes, and the bank's relationship to Florence's broader economy remain source-scoped.

- Connected branch failures and debt to the political use of family and public resources.
