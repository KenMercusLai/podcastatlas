# Justin Kittredge

## Overview
Justin Kittredge is the founder of [[ISlide]], a customized footwear and apparel company.

## Current Profile
Kittredge's case is not early validation but the attempt to move a thirteen-year-old, ten-person business from about $5.5 million toward $10 million in annual revenue. He values channel diversification because different channels protected the company at different times. The advice preserves that resilience while concentrating his own attention and resources on the one or two opportunities most likely to produce the next growth stage.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates across e-commerce, wholesale, bulk orders, and partner dropshipping.
- Is reluctant to abandon channels that have performed under different market conditions.
- Seeks a larger growth engine after thirteen years in business.
- Is exploring AI to improve sales-representative productivity.
- Balances concentration with selective channel continuity.
