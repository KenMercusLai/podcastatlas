# 《水浒传》 / Water Margin

[[fanwai-15-banlatte-jiang-jinpingmei-la-erqie-haishi-luse-de-991508433]] uses 《水浒传》 as the familiar narrative doorway into [[JinPingMei|《金瓶梅》]]. The episode first brings in [[WuSong|武松]] through the Jingyang Ridge tiger episode, then follows him to Qinghe, where his reunion with [[WuDalang|武大郎]] and first encounter with [[PanJinlian|潘金莲]] connect heroic fiction to domestic and urban-social fiction.

The source's contrast matters. Wu Song's branch carries physical courage, official appointment, brotherly protection, and public admiration, while [[JinPingMei|《金瓶梅》]] redirects that inherited material toward [[HouseholdAsSocialWeb]], neighbor surveillance, sexual pressure, household boundaries, and [[UrbanEverydayFiction]]. The same characters therefore feel different once the narrative center moves from outlaw heroics to market-town life.
