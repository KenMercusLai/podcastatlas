# Genesis Mission

## Overview
Genesis Mission is the government AI-for-science initiative described by [[MichaelKratsios|Michael Kratsios]] in All-In sources. It is presented as an effort to organize public scientific data, national-lab resources, and AI tools so researchers can accelerate hard scientific discovery.

## Current Profile
The mission currently links the wiki's [[AmericanAIStackStrategy|American AI stack strategy]] branch to the broader [[NewGoldenAgeScienceAgenda|New Golden Age science agenda]]. In the January AI strategy source, Genesis is about Department of Energy data and national labs; in the August science-agenda source, it becomes the flagship mission to double U.S. scientific output by applying AI to hard scientific problems.

## Key Characteristics
- Genesis Mission is an AI-for-science project, not a generic chatbot or private foundation-model deployment.
- It depends on making fragmented scientific records, simulations, and national-lab data usable for model training, simulation, and discovery.
- The mission is framed as public scientific infrastructure where government data and researcher access can complement private AI investment.
- In the New Golden Age agenda, Genesis is one of several discrete national missions beside moon, space reactor, quantum, fusion, and nuclear goals.
- Its success is still unproven and depends on data quality, verification, agency execution, and scientist adoption.

## Qualifications
Both bounded sources describe Genesis through Kratsios's policy presentation. The wiki does not yet have independent evidence on implementation, dataset access, model performance, or measured scientific output.
