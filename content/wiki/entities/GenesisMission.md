# Genesis Mission

Genesis Mission is the government AI-for-science initiative described by [[MichaelKratsios|Michael Kratsios]] in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-inside-americas-ai-strategy-infrastructure-regulation-and-global-competition-39846955]]. The source says the mission aims to accelerate AI-enabled scientific discovery by organizing fragmented scientific data and drawing on [[USDepartmentOfEnergy|U.S. Department of Energy]] national-lab research.

In the episode, the mission is not a generic chatbot deployment. It is tied to simulation and discovery loops in fusion, materials science, therapeutics, and other technical domains where scientific data is valuable but scattered across formats and institutions.
