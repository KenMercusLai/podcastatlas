# Li Xiang / 李想 (Li Auto)

## Overview
Li Xiang / 李想 is the [[LiAuto|理想汽车]] founder interviewed in [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]]. This page is disambiguated from [[LiXiang|李翔 / Li Xiang]], the football-media entrepreneur in a different source.

## Current Profile
The source presents Li as a founder trying to reposition Li Auto from a family-SUV company toward an AI and embodied-intelligence company. His core strategy joins enterprise [[AgenticWorkflow|agent workflows]], vehicle AI, autonomous driving, factory and household robotics, custom inference chips, organization redesign, talent recruitment, and overseas expansion.

Li's AI view is practical and production-oriented. He argues that casual users often see AI as search or chat, while companies can see stronger effects when agents access real workflows, data, tools, and feedback loops. His embodied-intelligence view similarly starts from deployed physical environments: cars, factories, loading and handling tasks, full wire-control chassis, local inference, and later household robots.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder-strategist tying [[LiAuto|Li Auto]]'s automotive business to [[EmbodiedAI]] and physical-world AI.
- Heavy user and promoter of AI tools inside the company, especially for real production workflows rather than casual prompting.
- Advocate of the view that AI amplifies strong professionals and changes talent evaluation rather than simply replacing expertise.
- Long-horizon hardware investor who treats inference chips, operating systems, models, simulation, and control stacks as embodied-intelligence infrastructure.
- Product narrator who reframes Li Auto's "family" positioning into a broader "home" and intelligent-service story.
- Source-scoped corporate speaker whose robot timelines, chip-moat claims, L3/L4 expectations, and overseas strategy still need external validation.

## Evidence
- Production AI: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] says Li spent more than two hundred days learning AI, pushing colleagues to use tools such as Claude Code and OpenClaude, and observing how high-token users rebuilt workflows.
- Talent and organization: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] has Li argue that AI can magnify professional workers and expose hidden "super individuals" whose old-era expression or resource-grabbing ability was weaker than their thinking.
- Embodied-intelligence strategy: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] frames autonomous driving as the first half of embodied intelligence and humanoid robots as the second half.
- Hardware moat: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] has Li describe the [[LiAutoM100Chip|马赫 M100]] and future inference chips as a strategic bet that reduces short-term profit but may build long-term capability.
- Product and brand narrative: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] positions [[LiAutoL9Levius|L9 LEVIUS]] as both an upgraded SUV and a node in Li Auto's move toward high-compute, high-control physical AI.

## Qualifications
The current evidence is one founder interview. It explains Li's strategic logic but does not independently verify L9 LEVIUS delivery outcomes, M100 chip competitiveness, L3/L4 regulatory readiness, robot commercialization timelines, overseas-market acceptance, or internal productivity gains.
