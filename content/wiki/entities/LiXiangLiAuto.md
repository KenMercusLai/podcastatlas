# Li Xiang / 李想 (Li Auto)

## Overview
Li Xiang / 李想 is the founder of [[Autohome|汽车之家]] and [[LiAuto|理想汽车]]. This page is disambiguated from [[LiXiang|李翔 / Li Xiang]], the football-media entrepreneur in a different source.

## Current Profile
The earlier interview presents a formation-to-company arc: teenage writing and web work created real user and income feedback; [[Autohome|汽车之家]] taught Li to translate specifications into user experience; and Li Auto began with complementary hiring, family scenarios, [[RangeExtendedEV|range extension]], and repeated correction under financing, quality, supply-chain, and public-opinion pressure. His stated method combines long commitment with rapid market iteration, while his 2008 shareholder crisis taught him that privately carrying every problem can deprive colleagues of context and agency.

The later interview presents Li as trying to reposition Li Auto from a family-SUV company toward an AI and embodied-intelligence company. Enterprise agents, vehicle AI, autonomous driving, factory and household robotics, inference chips, operating systems, organization redesign, recruitment, and overseas expansion become one physical-AI strategy. Across both sources, his AI view remains production-oriented: capabilities matter when they enter real workflows and feedback loops, and vehicle intelligence should be judged through recognizable driving performance rather than labels alone.

## Key Characteristics
- Serial founder whose path runs from teenage media and web work through [[Autohome|汽车之家]] to [[LiAuto|理想汽车]].
- Product strategist who starts from user jobs—readable car evaluation, family space, replenishment, comfort, and private space—before selecting a technical route.
- Organization builder who emphasizes complementary senior talent, direct high-level hiring, argument that exposes reasoning, and crisis transparency with core colleagues.
- Long-horizon iterator who joins durable market choice to frequent user and operational feedback.
- AI strategist tying enterprise workflows, vehicle intelligence, inference chips, operating systems, autonomous driving, and robotics into one physical-world stack.
- Advocate of evaluating an AI driver through route choice, speed, comfort, safety, and communication rather than autonomy labels alone.
- Source-scoped corporate narrator whose retrospective causal claims, product figures, robot timelines, chip moat, autonomy expectations, and overseas strategy require external validation.

## Qualifications
The current evidence is two interviews with the same founder and host, not independent corroboration. They explain Li's formation and strategic logic but do not independently verify remembered motives and dialogue, financing and conflict accounts, product sales and faults, charging coverage, VLA improvement, L9 LEVIUS delivery outcomes, M100 competitiveness, L3/L4 readiness, robot commercialization, overseas-market acceptance, or internal productivity gains.
