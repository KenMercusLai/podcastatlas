# Chris Surdean

Chris Surdean appears in [[peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted-peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted]] as [[Segment]]'s second sales representative, hired after [[RafaelParker]] pushed [[PeterReinhardt]] to bring him in. Peter initially doubted Chris because his style did not match Peter's assumptions about selling to developers.

The episode makes Chris important as a [[SalesAsLearnableSkill]] example. Peter says watching Chris taught him that strong sales often requires pushing through awkward discovery to understand the customer's actual pain before presenting the product. The same lesson also changed how Peter thought about product discovery, because repeated customer conversations around data warehouses pointed Segment toward another growth curve.
