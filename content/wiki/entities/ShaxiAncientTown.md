沙溪古镇 appears in [[144-nvxia-duan-sihe-he-tade-jiaxiang-mapingguan-women-ceng-yiwei-you-dian-you-lu-zhishi-meng-linmjqt-n-sa5sarpqgdxgtoeejt]] as the nearby town used to locate [[Mapingguan|马坪关]] and as the restoration context attached to [[HuangYunwu|黄云武]]. The episode does not analyze 沙溪 independently, but it makes the town part of the village's outside-contact, heritage, and tourism field.

In this source, 沙溪 is useful as a contrast point. It represents a more recognized visitor-facing place, while 马坪关 is described as less publicized, slower to open to tourism, and more protective of its local rhythm.
