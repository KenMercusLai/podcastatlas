# 张飞 / Zhang Fei

## Overview
张飞 / Zhang Fei is a [[LiuBei|刘备]] commander whose current evidence pairs a command failure at [[Xiapi|下邳]] with a successful rear-guard action at [[ChangbanBattle|长坂坡之战]].

## Current Profile
The source set prevents a one-note portrait. Qinji 131-2 compresses Zhang Fei's loss of Xuzhou into a drinking caution, while Hanji 999 gives the proximate chain: Zhang Fei kills [[CaoBaoLateHan|曹豹]], disorder follows, [[XuDanLateHan|许耽]] opens Xiapi to [[LyuBu|吕布]], and Liu Bei loses his base and family security.

Hanji 1094 adds the opposite command result. During the Changban rout, Zhang Fei takes a small mounted rear guard, blocks or destroys the bridge, identifies himself, and deters immediate pursuit. The episode distinguishes this historical-source frame from the more dramatic [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]] scene in which his shout kills an enemy officer.

## Key Characteristics
- Remains a core Liu Bei commander across both defeat and survival episodes.
- Causes or fails to contain internal breakdown at Xiapi in the Hanji 999 account.
- Is remembered through an alcohol-centered Xuzhou version that does not fully match the more detailed causal sequence.
- Uses a small rear guard, terrain obstruction, and personal intimidation to protect Liu Bei's escape at Changban.
- Illustrates why literary amplification and historical-source claims must be kept separate.

## Evidence
- Xiapi command failure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu|Hanji 999]] links Cao Bao's killing, city disorder, Xu Dan's gate-opening, and Lü Bu's takeover.
- Drinking-memory variant: [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-2-pandian-huangdimen-jiuhou-xiaopihao-livpg3zh7lsqffgca2pomiqnlvyd|Qinji 131-2]] uses Zhang Fei's drunken loss of Xuzhou as a compact example of alcohol magnifying political harm.
- Changban rear guard: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1094-changbanpo-zhizhan-zhangfei-he-zhaoyun-ruhe-zai-zhanchang-fengshen-ltymdohev05pmxhwcdme9d2jymzk|Hanji 1094]] says Zhang Fei covers Liu Bei's retreat with a small mounted group and blocks the bridge approach.
- History-literature boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1094-changbanpo-zhizhan-zhangfei-he-zhaoyun-ruhe-zai-zhanchang-fengshen-ltymdohev05pmxhwcdme9d2jymzk|Hanji 1094]] separates the deterrence account from the Romance death-by-shout embellishment.

## Qualifications
The Xuzhou sources preserve different levels of explanation rather than a settled single cause: one emphasizes drunkenness, while the other emphasizes Cao Bao's killing and internal gate-opening. Hanji 1094 does not provide a complete Changban command chronology, and its exact troop number, bridge condition, dialogue, and enemy reaction remain source-scoped.
