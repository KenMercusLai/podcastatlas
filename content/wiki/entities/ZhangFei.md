# 张飞 / Zhang Fei

张飞 / Zhang Fei appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-2-pandian-huangdimen-jiuhou-xiaopihao-livpg3zh7lsqffgca2pomiqnlvyd|Qinji 131-2]] as the compact Three Kingdoms example of "drinking causes trouble." The source says he loses Xuzhou after drunkenness, leaving [[LiuBei|刘备]] without a stable base.

The episode does not develop Zhang Fei as a full biography. It uses him as a quick contrast with the longer [[CaoCao|曹操]] and [[LiYuanTang|李渊]] stories: alcohol can produce political or military consequences even when the narrative is remembered as a personal flaw.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu|Hanji 999]] gives the event a more direct late-Han mechanism. While [[LiuBei|刘备]] is fighting [[YuanShu|袁术]], Zhang Fei guards [[Xiapi|下邳]], clashes with [[CaoBaoLateHan|曹豹]], and kills him. The source says the killing throws the city into disorder, after which [[XuDanLateHan|许耽]] opens the gates to [[LyuBu|吕布]].

This does not fully resolve the earlier compact "drunken loss of Xuzhou" memory. The wiki keeps the variation source-scoped: Qinji 131-2 uses Zhang Fei's loss as a drinking caution, while Hanji 999 emphasizes the Cao Bao killing and internal gate-opening as the proximate political-military chain.
