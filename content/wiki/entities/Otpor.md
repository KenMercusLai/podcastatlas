# Otpor

## Overview

Otpor was the Serbian resistance movement used in the episode to illustrate how humor, electoral verification, coalition breadth, and [[DilemmaActions]] can weaken authoritarian control.

## Current Profile

The movement challenged [[SlobodanMilosevic|Slobodan Milošević]] around a fraudulent election. Its “Dinar for Change” barrel action invited passersby to strike the ruler's image, leaving police to tolerate ridicule, arrest participants, or appear to arrest an object. Otpor also recruited grandparents to demonstrations, broadening participation while making public police violence against protesters more socially costly.

The case supports [[NonviolentResistanceStrategy]] because the tactic was not merely expressive. Humor undermined invincibility, diverse participation widened social protection, parallel vote verification challenged official claims, and eventual police non-defense showed a fracture in the ruler's [[PillarsOfSupport]].

## Key Characteristics

- Serbian movement opposing Milošević through organized civil resistance.
- Used humor to weaken an autocrat's image of inevitability and control.
- Designed actions that made several possible police responses costly.
- Broadened participation through socially diverse recruitment.
- Combined symbolic action with electoral verification and mass mobilization.
