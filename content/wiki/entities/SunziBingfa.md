# 《孙子兵法》 / The Art of War

《孙子兵法》 / The Art of War appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-19-maling-zhi-zhan-lhbapt09x2ptdqx0uopkp8apbada]] through the episode's discussion of the "军争" chapter. The host uses its warning about long-distance pursuit for advantage to explain why [[SunBin|孙膑]] wants [[PangJuan|庞涓]] and the Wei army to hurry after Qi.

The page is distinct from [[SunBinBingfa|《孙膑兵法》]]. Zhouji 16 used the excavated Sun Bin text to separate Sun Bin from Sunzi; this source uses the older strategic tradition as an interpretive frame for [[ForcedMarchOperationalRisk|forced-march risk]] at [[MalingBattle|马陵之战]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-22-gongsunyang-zha-wei-gongzi-ang-lss1l7lojfwgwdtcje7m5ijodpxi]] adds a brief sequel use. After [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]] captures [[GongziAng|公子卬 / 公子昂]], the episode invokes the "不战而屈人之兵" ideal as the next question: whether commander capture can be converted into negotiation leverage rather than only battlefield success.
