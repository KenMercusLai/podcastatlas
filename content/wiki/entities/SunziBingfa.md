## Overview

《孙子兵法》 / *The Art of War* appears across the wiki as a reusable strategic vocabulary rather than a single tactic manual. The sources apply it to long-distance pursuit, deception, secrecy, terrain, death ground, intelligence, regular-and-surprise action, emotional restraint, war cost, and crisis survival. Its authority is therefore strongest as conditional reasoning about advantage and system design, not as permission to copy a visible maneuver apart from its preconditions.

## Current Profile

The Warring States sources use Sunzi to diagnose movement and decision. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-19-maling-zhi-zhan-lhbapt09x2ptdqx0uopkp8apbada|Zhouji 19]] frames [[SunBin|孙膑]]'s Maling design through the risk that long pursuit for advantage breaks command, supply, and formation. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-22-gongsunyang-zha-wei-gongzi-ang-lss1l7lojfwgwdtcje7m5ijodpxi|Zhouji 22]] invokes victory without battle after [[ShangYang|商鞅]] captures [[GongziAng|公子卬]], while [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-51-chuhuaiwang-qin-xiao-er-kan-jiujiu-ruhe-mie-le-ni-ltfkdlivomnvux5xodh2rlfqf6ac|Zhouji 51]] uses the warning against anger-driven war to judge [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-87-xialu-xiangfeng-yongzhe-sheng-zhongyu-zhaodao-chuchu-le-loppr83sdq5hhesmqihckwmhpl2h|Zhouji 87]] connects secrecy and deception to [[ZhaoShe|赵奢]], and [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-3-ljwz8ebk0iyfleivkefvvoonrqb9|Zhouji 94 part 3]] uses Sunzi to limit post-victory overreach through war-cost and tempo discipline.

The Qin and Han sources broaden the same logic. [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-3-xunzi-bingfa-li-cangzhe-renxing-de-mimi-lgfcp4hkruyhhrjguqhjbhllvwp43|Qinji 99-3]] makes Sunzi-style deception [[LinwuJun|临武君]]'s tactical counterpoint to [[Xunzi|荀子]]'s people-unifying legitimacy, while [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-105-zhanzheng-huayang-zhaoguo-limu-zui-hui-shua-ljtqdg46a8o13zzsjv-p-fpol5oh|Qinji 105]] uses feigned weakness to explain [[LiMu|李牧]]'s frontier patience. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-145-junshi-qicai-hanxin-bolan-zhuangkuo-de-yisheng-2-npgife56ltayww2wt4wo-fiwo-ep|Hanji 145 part 2]] converts interest-gated action and refusal of anger into a life-conduct frame for [[HanXin|韩信]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-148-1-hanxin-yupan-le-zhanghan-de-yupan-lodhpuxpossjno87gorjkfrtitbe|Hanji 148-1]] applies hidden defense and sudden attack to the central breakthrough at [[ChenCang|陈仓]], while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-157-hanxin-beishuiyizhan-bingfa-jiedu-1-loiz8ge-a39tmigajool2spzri5i|Hanji 157 part 1]] joins death ground, intelligence, and surprise to show why the visible backwater formation cannot be copied alone. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-980-lvbu-xiansha-caocao-kankan-yi-ju-hua-ruhe-niuzhuan-lishi-lgggw3vmonu-3wvzabvg943v5qcg|Hanji 980]] applies regular-and-surprise language to [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s failed-entry crisis and later attrition around Puyang.

A boundary case comes from [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-327-2-huoqubing-bu-du-sunzi-bingfa-weihe-neng-yongbing-ru-shen-lqafy7cesohwmuwhfilt2ghicsw7|Hanji 327-2]]. It reports that [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] declined to be constrained by ancient military texts and contrasts Sunzi's long-distance pursuit warning with source-reported high-tempo cavalry operations in the 121 BCE Hexi campaigns. The source itself still concedes that some principles remain valid. The current profile therefore treats the episode as evidence for [[MilitaryDoctrineEraFit|兵法的时代适配]]: a rule may be conditionally non-transferable without making the entire work obsolete.

## Key Characteristics

- Strategic text repeatedly used to connect advantage, restraint, tempo, cost, deception, intelligence, terrain, and force disposition.
- Framework whose strongest applications reconstruct hidden preconditions rather than copy famous winning forms.
- Source of emotional discipline: anger, resentment, humiliation, and momentum should not decide whether action serves advantage.
- Conditional authority whose prescriptions must be tested against force structure, logistics, opponent, and operational range.
- Tactical counterpoint to legitimacy-centered traditions such as Xunzi's claim that unified people-support can defeat deception.

- [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-51-chuhuaiwang-qin-xiao-er-kan-jiujiu-ruhe-mie-le-ni-ltfkdlivomnvux5xodh2rlfqf6ac|Zhouji 51]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-145-junshi-qicai-hanxin-bolan-zhuangkuo-de-yisheng-2-npgife56ltayww2wt4wo-fiwo-ep|Hanji 145 part 2]] use Sunzi against action selected by anger or resentment.

## Qualifications

These pages summarize popular-history podcast readings rather than establish a textual history, translation, authorship study, or complete reception history of *The Art of War*. Several episodes attach famous formulations to a case without reproducing full chapter context, and later actors' plans are often interpreted through Sunzi rather than documented as direct application. Hanji 327-2 does not establish that Huo Qubing rejected all study or that one successful cavalry model invalidated the whole text; its march distances, daily tempo, force totals, casualties, and “obsolete” conclusion remain source-scoped. The page is distinct from [[SunBinBingfa|《孙膑兵法》]], and neither work should be silently substituted for the other.

## What Changed

- Added Huo Qubing's reported refusal of doctrinal constraint as a boundary test for direct transfer of old rules.
- Reframed the text's authority explicitly as conditional: operational mismatch can narrow a prescription without negating the whole strategic tradition.
- Migrated the page to the synthesis-first schema while preserving all prior source evidence.
