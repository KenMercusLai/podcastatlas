# British Army

The British Army appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] at the moment Westminster sends troops into [[NorthernIreland]] in August 1969. The episode says [[JamesChichesterClark]] asked London to send troops into [[Derry]] on 14 August, [[JimCallaghan]] approved the request, and the 1st Battalion of the Yorkshire Regiment entered the [[Bogside]] at 5 p.m.; troops entered [[Belfast]] the next day.

The source emphasizes the army's changing meaning. Catholics initially welcomed soldiers as protectors from loyalists and the [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]], while unionist hardliners saw their own army as an occupying force. The Hunt Report and later loyalist riots then showed the army becoming a target and actor inside [[TheTroubles]].
