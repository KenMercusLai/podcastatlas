## Overview

田广明 / Tian Guangming enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-388-2-yin-chanyan-cuosha-taizi-hanwudi-wannian-zuidade-huihen-lm9e_lrbbcc7a6kf22hbid04_u73|Hanji 388-2]] as the local official who detects Hu Qian's false official identity during the Gongsun Yong rebellion attempt.

## Current Profile

The source gives Tian Guangming a narrow crisis role. Hu Qian pretends to be a Guanglu Dafu and plans to capture the commandery head when he comes to pay respects. Tian Guangming notices signs of imposture and moves against the group, making him part of the episode's larger point that low- and mid-level officials can prevent political disorder when they do not yield automatically to intimidating titles.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han local official in the Gongsun Yong/Hu Qian rebellion case.
- Detects an impostor rather than obeying title performance.
- Helps prevent a plot built around ceremonial access and surprise detention.
- Functions as evidence for the value of local alertness under imperial high-pressure institutions.
