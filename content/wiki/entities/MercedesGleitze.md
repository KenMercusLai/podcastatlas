# Mercedes Gleitze

Mercedes Gleitze is the endurance swimmer whose 1927 English Channel publicity became central to the [[RolexOyster]] story in [[rolex-1]]. The source says she carried a Rolex Oyster during a vindication swim attempt; the watch stayed in water for many hours, and [[HansWilsdorf]] turned the event into major newspaper advertising.

For the wiki, Gleitze is the first major case of [[ProfessionalToolBranding]] in the Rolex story. The point was not only that the watch survived water, but that real human endurance made the technical claim culturally memorable.
