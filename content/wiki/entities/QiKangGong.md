# 齐康公 / Duke Kang of Qi

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-3-lsbx8l7wxeioae2lwuwkkkutpdwn]] gives Qi Kang Gong an earlier displacement scene. The source says [[TianHe|田和]] moves him to a coastal city and leaves him local tax revenue for ancestral offerings. The host treats this as effective deposition with a polite ritual surface, because Qi Kang Gong no longer controls [[QiState|齐国]] while the Tian family prepares formal recognition.

齐康公 / Duke Kang of Qi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl|Zhouji 09]] as the last Jiang-line ruler of [[QiState|齐国]]. The source says he dies without an heir during the broader early Warring States war period.

The episode uses his death as an old-order endpoint. Jiang-line Qi had already lost practical control to the Tian family; with Qi Kang Gong's death, the source treats the older lineage as fully ended while Tian-family Qi continues through [[TianQiHuanGong|Tian-family Qi Huan Gong]] and [[TianYingqi|田英齐]].
