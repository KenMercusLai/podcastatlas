# 杨广 / Emperor Yang of Sui

杨广 / Emperor Yang of Sui appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-2-pandian-huangdimen-jiuhou-xiaopihao-livpg3zh7lsqffgca2pomiqnlvyd|Qinji 131-2]] as the ruler whose palace women and imperial bed make [[LiYuanTang|李渊]]'s drunken night politically dangerous. The page uses the `YangGuangSui` slug to avoid collision with the existing [[YangGuangxian|杨光先]] page.

The source does not develop Yang Guang's reign. It uses his authority as the background condition that lets [[PeiJi|裴寂]] and [[LiShimin|李世民]] argue that Li Yuan has already crossed a line and should rebel before exposure brings punishment.
