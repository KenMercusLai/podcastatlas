# 张孟谈 / Zhang Mengtan

## Overview
Zhang Mengtan is Zhao Wuxu's envoy in the [[BattleOfJinyang|Battle of Jinyang]], credited with turning Wei and Han's latent fear of Zhi Yao into a coordinated counteralliance.

## Current Profile
With Jinyang near collapse, Zhang Mengtan leaves the city at night and meets [[WeiJu|Wei Ju]] and [[HanKangzi|Han Hu]]. His “唇亡齿寒” argument does not create a new interest; it names the sequential danger already visible to both houses. When they fear disclosure, he contains the problem by treating their own words and the private audience as the security boundary, then returns with an agreed date for action.

## Key Characteristics
- Diagnoses the listener's existing fear before making an appeal.
- Reframes Zhao's survival as Wei and Han's immediate security interest.
- Uses secrecy assurances to convert agreement into coordinated action.
- Operates under siege conditions where communication itself is dangerous.

## Evidence
- Night mission and persuasion: the source has Zhang Mengtan leave Jinyang secretly and use the lip-and-teeth analogy with Wei Ju and Han Hu. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-03-weiju-hanhu-wujiandao-lkiwf0zdnylmaqjgsix-dktxea0l|Zhouji 03]]
- Operational result: the three parties agree on a date, Zhao reverses the dike, and Wei-Han forces attack Zhi from the flanks. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-03-weiju-hanhu-wujiandao-lkiwf0zdnylmaqjgsix-dktxea0l|Zhouji 03]]

## Qualifications
This is a single-source profile. The speech, meeting choreography, and secrecy exchange are transmitted narrative as presented by the podcast; the result demonstrates aligned incentives but does not prove that rhetoric alone caused the reversal.
