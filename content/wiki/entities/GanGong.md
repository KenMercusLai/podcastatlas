# 甘公 / Gan Gong

甘公 / Gan Gong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-149-xichu-bawang-xiangyu-weihe-shijun-1-lsqxs2hzu0ngnrpmzsklkqhtyuvw|Hanji 149]] as the adviser who redirects [[ZhangEr|张耳]] after Zhang Er is defeated by [[ChenYu|陈馀]] and considers where to flee. The episode says Zhang Er initially thinks of going to [[XiangYu|项羽]], but Gan Gong tells him to join [[LiuBang|刘邦]] instead.

Gan Gong's source role is compact but important. He predicts that Liu Bang will ultimately take the empire, so his advice turns Zhang Er's defeat into a Liu-side acquisition. The page therefore belongs near [[StrategicRecognitionAlignment|谋略知己式归附]]: in this episode, Liu Bang's recovered Guanzhong position and future prospects are becoming visible enough that outside advisers can recommend him over Xiang Yu.
