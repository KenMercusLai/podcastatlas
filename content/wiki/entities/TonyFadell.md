# Tony Fadell

Tony Fadell is the builder-narrator figure in [[we-almost-had-a-smartphone-in-the-90s-why-did-it-fail]]. The episode says he joined [[GeneralMagic]] as employee number 29 at age 21 after repeatedly calling the secretive company where members of the original Macintosh orbit were working.

In the source, Fadell's importance is the learning arc. He saw General Magic try to build a whole mobile future without enough constraints, then later applied the opposite operating logic at [[Apple]]: a clear customer desire, a Christmas deadline, a limited budget, borrowed components, and many iterations of the [[IPod]] and early [[IPhone]].

The episode also mentions Fadell's later [[NestThermostat]] work, but uses that mainly as evidence that General Magic's talent and lessons traveled into later technology successes rather than disappearing with the failed company.
