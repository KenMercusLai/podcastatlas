# Boubacar Joseph Ndiaye

## Overview

Boubacar Joseph Ndiaye was the curator and public storyteller whom the episode credits with transforming the [[HouseOfSlavesGoree|House of Slaves]] into an internationally recognized site of slavery memory.

## Current Profile

Ndiaye's role is interpretive and institutional. He promoted the doorway as the exit through which one million enslaved Africans departed and claimed 20 million exports from [[Goree|Gorée]], helping create a vivid narrative for tourists and diaspora pilgrims. The episode says documentary research does not support those numbers or the building's alleged warehouse function.

His profile therefore demonstrates the power and risk of memory entrepreneurship: a compelling guide can give dispersed historical suffering a concrete sacred place while also making unsupported details feel authoritative.

## Key Characteristics

- Curator associated with the House of Slaves museum.
- Promoter of a vivid “Door of No Return” narrative.
- Important mediator between a local building and global diaspora memory.
- Source of numerical claims the episode presents as historically unsupported.

## Evidence

- Curatorial role and claims: [[272-senegal-the-door-of-no-return-glt8611353465]] identifies Ndiaye and attributes the one-million and 20-million figures to his narration.
- International memory role: [[272-senegal-the-door-of-no-return-glt8611353465]] connects that storytelling to tourism, UNESCO-era visibility, famous visitors, and African American pilgrimage.
- Evidentiary challenge: [[272-senegal-the-door-of-no-return-glt8611353465]] contrasts his account with documentary research, departure geography, building use, and the doorway's physical setting.

## Qualifications

This page records one podcast's account of Ndiaye rather than a full biography or direct study of his words, motives, museum practice, or reception in Senegal. It does not infer deliberate deception from historical inaccuracy, and it distinguishes the claims' evidentiary weakness from the memorial work the site performs.
