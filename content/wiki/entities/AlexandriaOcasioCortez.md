# Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez

## Overview
Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez / AOC enters the wiki through [[NateSilver]]'s [[AllIn|All-In]] analysis of the 2028 Democratic field. The source treats her as a left-faction figure and a serious nonzero outsider scenario rather than as an announced or settled nominee.

## Current Profile
In this source, Ocasio-Cortez represents the possibility that the [[DemocraticParty]] could produce its own anti-establishment breakthrough candidate if younger and more change-seeking voters reject continuity politics. Silver compares the opening to earlier Republican outsider cycles before [[DonaldTrump]] and argues that an effective outsider would need ideological contrast plus broader, less narrowly partisan rhetoric.

## Key Characteristics
- Left-faction Democratic figure in Silver's party map.
- Plausible outsider bet for 2028 in a party mood that may reward anti-establishment contrast.
- Candidate archetype tied to younger voters' weaker attachment to legacy party brands.
- Politically distinct from a continuity candidate such as [[GavinNewsom]].
