# 小四爷 / Xiao Siye

## Overview

## Current Profile
In the source, Xiao Siye contributes the public-figure verification layer. A fan asks him to amplify a medical fundraiser, he declines because he cannot verify it, and the later blocking shows how public visibility turns help into a trust-transfer problem. He also joins the discussion of relatives borrowing money and of awkwardness around visible consumption after income changes.
