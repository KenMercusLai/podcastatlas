## Overview
段志强 / Duan Zhiqiang is the guest in [[yu-duan-zhiqiang-tan-chongming-xiangye-bowuzhi-wo-zhu-changjiang-wei-8212963740-775078]], where he discusses farming in [[Chongming|崇明]] and the writing of [[WoZhuChangjiangWei|《我住长江尾》]].

## Current Profile
Duan is presented as a writer and knowledge worker whose rural practice lets him connect manual labor, childhood farm memory, food, classical texts, and publishing. He is not framed as a full-time farmer; that boundary matters because the episode repeatedly distinguishes chosen farming as experience from agriculture as livelihood pressure.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses small-scale farming as a way to observe weather, soil, neighbors, plants, and time with unusual concreteness.
- Converts lived experience into essays, books, podcast conversation, and historical-literary reflection.
- Reads agricultural and classical texts through bodily participation rather than only through desk study.
- Keeps a comic self-awareness about crop failure, social embarrassment, minor injuries, and amateur limits.
- Treats family and neighbors as part of the farming scene, not as background to solitary authorship.

## Evidence
- Embodied farming method: [[yu-duan-zhiqiang-tan-chongming-xiangye-bowuzhi-wo-zhu-changjiang-wei-8212963740-775078]] describes Duan's watermelon, corn, peanut, weeding, irrigation, and typhoon-loss stories as the practical base for his reflection.
- Experience-to-writing conversion: [[yu-duan-zhiqiang-tan-chongming-xiangye-bowuzhi-wo-zhu-changjiang-wei-8212963740-775078]] says Duan began from notes and later expanded the material into a book after external encouragement.
- Textual self-positioning: [[yu-duan-zhiqiang-tan-chongming-xiangye-bowuzhi-wo-zhu-changjiang-wei-8212963740-775078]] records his claim that farming gives him an entry point for reading older agricultural writers, including [[XuGuangqi|徐光启]].
- Social and family setting: [[yu-duan-zhiqiang-tan-chongming-xiangye-bowuzhi-wo-zhu-changjiang-wei-8212963740-775078]] includes his parents, wife, neighbors, landlord-neighbor help, dialect limits, and field-boundary lessons.

## Qualifications
Duan's account should not be flattened into a universal farmer profile. The episode emphasizes that he works primarily as a brain worker and can turn hardship into material partly because his household income is not determined by the harvest.
