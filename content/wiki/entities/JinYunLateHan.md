# 靳允 / Jin Yun (Late Han)

靳允 / Jin Yun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-978-gushen-quantui-shuwan-dijun-ta-zenme-zuodao-de-lhgd8vsvuaf5oi9aefh0wxabmzwv|Hanji 978]] as the [[FanCountyLateHan|范县]] magistrate whom [[ChengYu|程昱]] persuades during [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] crisis. [[LyuBu|吕布]] has seized Jin Yun's family, so the source frames his choice as both strategic alignment and family-hostage pressure.

Cheng Yu's argument asks Jin Yun to compare leaders and conditions: Lü Bu is rough, [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] is not treated as a durable ruler, and Cao Cao remains the actor with broader potential. Jin Yun then pledges that he will not have a second mind, ambushes [[FanYiLateHan|范仪]] after Fan Yi enters Fan County, and keeps the county from turning.

The page is source-scoped because the episode also records [[XuZhongJin|许众]]'s later criticism. Xu Zhong argues that Jin Yun had no settled lord-subject bond with Cao Cao and should have prioritized his captured mother, while the host resists turning that criticism into a universal rule.
