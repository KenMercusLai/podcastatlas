# Charles Schwab

Charles Schwab appears in [[vol-126-gongmu-jijin-hai-zhide-mai-ma-lojrz1qctaqzkc2rg0lqokos-o6v]] as the U.S. low-fee brokerage and wealth-management comparison case. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] uses Schwab to argue that a distributor can move beyond simple fund sales when low commissions, advice, asset allocation, and acquisitions expand the institution's role in household wealth management.

In the source, Schwab is a possible model for the kind of challenger that China lacks: an institution able to pressure fund-distribution economics, improve advisory service, and shift competition from channel possession toward customer outcome. The comparison extends [[FinancialPlatformIncentives]] rather than presenting Schwab as a flawless template.

[[145-jijin-tougu-zhide-xinren-ma-lrckug0zjqolcczni8ajikb0k5mi]] adds Schwab's tiered advisory model to the wiki. The episode uses Schwab as an example of a platform that can offer pure robo-advice, human-plus-automated service, and fuller human advisory for different client segments, making it a [[RoboAdvisorHybridService]] reference rather than only a low-fee brokerage comparison.
