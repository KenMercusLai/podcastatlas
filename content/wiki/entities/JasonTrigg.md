# Jason (Trigg)

## Overview

Jason is the founder of [[Trigg]] and a former designer whose team moved through [[SuperDesign]], [[Coolit]], and an agent-facing tool marketplace.

## Current Profile

The source presents Jason as an experiment-driven founder who uses product operation, model adoption, and distribution results to decide whether to persist or pivot. His current thesis favors building specialized data and tool infrastructure on top of general agents instead of reproducing their harness, reasoning, sandbox, and memory layers.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses short operating experiments to reduce strategic uncertainty.
- Treats open source and platform ecosystems as distribution channels.
- Prefers concentrated application-layer capability over a full self-built agent stack.
- Sees growth and innovation as judgment-heavy work that remains human-led.
- Values cross-domain product, design, and frontend experience.
