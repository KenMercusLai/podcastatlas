## Overview

[[LiuZhenLateHan|刘桢]] is a Jian'an literary figure whose Hanji 1149 role is to warn his friend Cao Zhi that congenial brilliance cannot replace strict, useful counsel.

## Current Profile

The episode places Liu Zhen among the talented companions favored by [[CaoZhi|曹植]], but does not reduce him to a flatterer. He writes to Cao Zhi in defense of [[XingYongLateHan|刑颙]], comparing visible literary ornament to spring flowers and practical advice to autumn fruit. His function is therefore corrective from inside Cao Zhi's preferred circle.

## Key Characteristics

- Literary companion associated with Cao Zhi.
- Recognizes the value of a stricter and less congenial counselor.
- Uses an ornamental-versus-useful contrast to advise political maturity.
