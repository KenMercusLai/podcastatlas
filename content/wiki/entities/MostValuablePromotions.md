# Most Valuable Promotions

## Overview
Most Valuable Promotions, or MVP, is [[JakePaul|Jake Paul]]'s combat-sports promotion and representation company as described in the source.

## Current Profile
Paul presents MVP as an attempt to use audience distribution and more favorable fighter economics to build a challenger in boxing and MMA. He says it represents roughly 400 fighters and has merged with [[ProfessionalFightersLeague|PFL]].

## Key Characteristics
- Combines promotion, athlete representation, and audience distribution.
- Claims a roster spanning boxing and MMA.
- Positions fighter pay, sponsorship freedom, and major-fight access as competitive tools.
- Is tied to Paul's broader creator and investing flywheel.
