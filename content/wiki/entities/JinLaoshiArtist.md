# 金老师 / Jin Laoshi

## Overview
金老师 / Jin Laoshi is the painter, gallery founder, and multidisciplinary designer in [[ep163-bei-hulianwang-chongji-de-yishu-ckwriaieiyu2abaaaacscgxf|EP163-被互联网冲击的艺术]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Jin Laoshi as a traditionally trained painter who later had to loosen the constraints of early training. She frames art as a route into emotion and attention that remains available beyond professional institutions, argues that childhood creativity can be reopened, and connects artistic perception to patience, sensitivity, cross-medium work, and inner quiet.

## Key Characteristics
- Early traditional painting trainee who treats unlearning as part of later artistic development.
- Advocate for broad, everyday creative agency rather than professional ownership of art.
- Emphasizes openness, emotional honesty, sensitivity, and quiet attention.
- Supports multidisciplinary exhibition practice and child-led, low-prejudice viewing.

## Evidence
- Training and unlearning - [[ep163-bei-hulianwang-chongji-de-yishu-ckwriaieiyu2abaaaacscgxf]] says she began traditional painting training around age ten and later recognized its accumulated constraints.
- Creative agency - [[ep163-bei-hulianwang-chongji-de-yishu-ckwriaieiyu2abaaaacscgxf]] uses children's unconstrained drawing to argue that adults can reopen creative expression.
- Audience and exhibition practice - [[ep163-bei-hulianwang-chongji-de-yishu-ckwriaieiyu2abaaaacscgxf]] links deeper viewing to reduced prejudice, wider experience, and attention to the state an artist explores.
- Attention - [[ep163-bei-hulianwang-chongji-de-yishu-ckwriaieiyu2abaaaacscgxf]] treats quiet as a condition for sensing materials, other people, and one's own direction.
