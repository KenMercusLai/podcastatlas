# 持械入宋 / Chixie Ru Song

## Overview
《持械入宋》 is an AI comic-drama project used in the source as an example of a high-play breakout emerging after sustained team production rather than from a first attempt.

## Current Profile
The episode reports roughly six billion cumulative plays and attributes the result first to the source novel and then to the team's high-quality adaptation. Its analytical role is to counter instant-success narratives: the team reportedly worked for about a year and made dozens of dramas before this breakout.

## Key Characteristics
- Source-reported multi-billion-play AI comic drama.
- Adaptation whose story quality is treated as prior to tool novelty.
- Team result emerging after repeated production rather than a first-work anomaly.

## Evidence
- Breakout and persistence: [[lhhrwpwmn7e-unjt7wpxdixllxgs-lhhrwpwmn7e-unjt7wpxdixllxgs]] reports about six billion plays and says the ordinary small team had produced dozens of works over roughly a year.
- Story and adaptation: [[lhhrwpwmn7e-unjt7wpxdixllxgs-lhhrwpwmn7e-unjt7wpxdixllxgs]] attributes performance to a strong original story plus high-quality restoration of that story in the finished drama.

## Qualifications
Playback, production-history, team-composition, and causal claims are participant-reported and not independently verified. The source does not provide a project ledger, platform statement, or representative comparison group.
