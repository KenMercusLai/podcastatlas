# Mohammad Reza Pahlavi / 穆罕默德·礼萨·巴列维

Mohammad Reza Pahlavi / 穆罕默德·礼萨·巴列维 appears in [[77-ta-shi-ziyou-de-yegou-ta-shi-falaqi-685710187]] as one of [[OrianaFallaci|Oriana Fallaci]]'s political interview subjects. The episode says Fallaci criticized him sharply, which later made [[RuhollahKhomeini|Khomeini]]'s side think she might be useful or sympathetic when arranging its own interview.

The source uses Pahlavi less as a full Iran-history subject than as a power-interview case. In the episode's Fallaci frame, criticizing the shah and then challenging Khomeini shows [[JournalisticIndependenceAgainstFaction]]: opposition to one ruler did not become loyalty to the next power center.
