# Andy Barber

Andy Barber is the prosecutor-father and first-person narrator in [[DefendingJacob|《捍卫雅各布》 / Defending Jacob]], discussed in [[24-hanwei-yagebu-xiangxin-ni-shi-wo-weiyi-de-zhinian-576218441]]. He begins the case as a senior prosecutor investigating Ben Rifkin's murder, then becomes personally implicated when evidence points toward his son [[JacobBarber|Jacob Barber]].

The episode reads Andy as the main carrier of [[ParentalDefenseConflict]]. His professional self knows the importance of evidence, conflict-of-interest boundaries, and truthful testimony, but his fatherly self hides family history, discards a knife, destroys a device, and refuses to test the possibility that Jacob is dangerous.

## Key Claims
- Andy makes [[LegalTruthEvidenceGap]] painful because he knows the justice system's limits from the inside.
- His love is intelligible but not clean: the source stresses that understanding his fear does not excuse his evidence destruction.
- His narration creates [[FirstPersonMoralAlignment]], pulling the reader close enough to feel his denial before stepping back to judge it.
