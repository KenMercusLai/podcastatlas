# Douyin E-commerce / 抖音电商

## Overview
Douyin E-commerce / 抖音电商 is the commerce layer of [[Douyin]] discussed in the wiki through infringement, live-selling, goods, account, and rights-governance operations.

## Current Profile
The current source profile is narrow and governance-centered. [[JiaxuDouyinEcommerce|嘉旭]] presents Douyin ecommerce as a high-volume platform where short videos, livestreams, product listings, account identities, shop decoration, and product-detail pages can all become infringement surfaces when sellers imply unauthorized celebrity, creator, or brand endorsement.

The episode's distinctive point is that AI does not create a wholly new legal problem so much as a cheaper and more ambiguous enforcement environment. Portrait, voice, name, trademark, decoration, nickname, emoji, and category cues can combine to create consumer confusion even when no single frame looks like a straightforward copy.

## Key Characteristics
- It is treated as a commerce and live-selling layer inside the broader [[Douyin]] platform context.
- Its infringement surface spans short video, livestream, product, account, shop-decoration, and product-detail signals.
- It uses rights-owner submissions and active detection to support [[PlatformRightsPreReview]].
- It must distinguish unauthorized impersonation from lawful similarity, legitimate authorization, and rights-owner silence.
- It faces an asymmetric enforcement problem because AI lowers fake-content production cost while raising detection and appeal complexity.
