# 莫子浩 / Mo Zihao

Mo Zihao is the founder of [[Anbi]] discussed in [[ai-fazhanle-4-nian-ba-yingyong-fazhan-meile-ai-nianzhong-fupan-lgtuy-eszlci5yaguocyndigwmlx]]. [[QuKai]] says he first advised Mo to present a model story during fundraising because investors liked models, but Mo questioned why the company had to make or emphasize models at that moment.

Mo's wiki role is the episode's concrete founder example for [[FounderSignalDiscipline]]. Even with model-training experience and access to algorithm talent, the source frames his point as: the company should not bend its actual product thesis around capital fashion if its harder problem is AI interaction, offline context, and user value.
