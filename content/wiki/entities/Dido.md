# Dido / Elissa

## Overview

Dido, also called Elissa, is the legendary Tyrian princess whose flight from [[Tyre]] and foundation of [[Carthage]] encode the city's remembered eastern origin.

## Current Profile

In the episode's foundation narrative, Dido flees after her brother Pygmalion kills her wealthy husband. She deceives him about the location of the couple's treasure, escapes with supporters and wealth, and establishes Carthage in North Africa. The tale makes resourcefulness, exile, dynastic violence, and Tyrian continuity parts of Carthage's origin story.

The source treats the narrative as legend rather than secure biography. Some names and the durable parent-city relationship may preserve older Tyrian elements, but the plot cannot establish Dido's life, motives, route, or date. Her importance here is therefore historical memory: she personifies the claim that Carthage was a “new city” rooted in Tyre.

## Key Characteristics

- Legendary Tyrian princess also known as Elissa.
- Exile whose story begins with dynastic murder and threatened wealth.
- Cunning founder who preserves resources through deception and escape.
- Personification of Carthage's remembered kinship with Tyre.
- Mythic figure whose cultural importance exceeds what can be established biographically.

## Qualifications

Dido is not treated as a securely recoverable historical individual. Her chronology conflicts with later literary settings, and the murder, deception, journey, foundation acts, dialogue, and motives remain legendary or source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Established Dido as a historical-memory bridge between Tyre and Carthage.
- Separated the foundation tradition's durable kinship claim from its unrecoverable biography.
