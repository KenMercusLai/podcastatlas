# 贺涵 / He Han

## Overview
贺涵 / He Han enters the wiki as the TV-drama mentor and elite-consultant figure in [[MyFirstHalfLife|《我的前半生》]], criticized in EP385 as the main force turning a female-growth story toward male guidance.

## Current Profile
The source treats He Han as a once-effective but now visibly dated fantasy of the mature elite man: toxic-tongued, resource-rich, professionally omniscient, and positioned to shape women around him. He is important less as a realistic consultant than as the adaptation device that absorbs work, female help, and heroine judgment into a [[MaleMentorFemaleGrowthNarrative]].

## Key Characteristics
- TV-added or TV-amplified male mentor whose function displaces female support.
- Elite-consultant fantasy tied to the drama's dated workplace prestige.
- "Overbearing CEO" template whose appeal depends on authority, resources, and emotional control.
- Gender-politics problem because he frames women as works to be shaped or rescued.
- Adaptation engine that makes Luo Zijun's independence read as pseudo-independent.
