# Ndongo

## Overview

Ndongo is the 17th-century polity in present-day northern Angola that the episode uses as [[QueenNjinga]]'s original royal and anti-Portuguese setting.

## Current Profile

The source presents Ndongo as a kingdom navigating Portuguese contact, military pressure, succession violence, and colonial encroachment. Its rulers initially sought Portuguese ties because neighboring Kongo appeared to gain goods, weapons, and power through contact, but relations deteriorated as Portuguese forces tried to build a colony in Angola and intervened in local conflicts. Njinga's return, diplomacy, rebellion, and later effort to recover much of Ndongo make the polity central to the episode's sovereignty story.

## Key Characteristics

- Present-day northern Angola setting for [[QueenNjinga]]'s origin and claim.
- Polity initially drawn toward Portuguese contact for goods, weapons, and regional advantage.
- Site of succession violence after Njinga's father was assassinated and her brother seized power.
- Kingdom pressured by Portuguese colonial ambition and local intervention.
- Recovered in part by Njinga after decades of diplomacy and war, according to the source.

## Evidence

- Portuguese contact: [[317-african-amazons-glt5973082485]] says Ndongo's rulers initially sought ties with the Portuguese because neighboring Kongo had gained goods, weapons, and power.
- Colonial pressure: [[317-african-amazons-glt5973082485]] says relations deteriorated as the Portuguese tried to create a colony in Angola and intervened in local conflicts.
- Succession violence: [[317-african-amazons-glt5973082485]] says Njinga's brother seized power after their father was assassinated and killed rivals, including Njinga's baby son.
- Njinga's recovery: [[317-african-amazons-glt5973082485]] says Njinga recovered much of Ndongo by the early to mid-1660s settlement phase.

## Qualifications

The source uses Ndongo to explain Njinga's career rather than to reconstruct the kingdom's full political history, internal institutions, or Kongo relations.
