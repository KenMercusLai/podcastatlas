# Roy E. Disney

Roy E. Disney is Walt Disney's nephew and the Disney family governance figure in [[disney-the-renaissance-and-the-empire-1]]. The source distinguishes him from [[RoyDisney]], Walt's brother: Roy E. acts decades later as a boardroom catalyst when [[TheWaltDisneyCompany]] appears creatively weak and strategically vulnerable.

In the episode, Roy E. Disney helps force the 1984 leadership change that brings in [[MichaelEisner]], [[FrankWells]], and [[JeffreyKatzenberg]]. He later resigns from the board with Stanley Gold and launches the Save Disney campaign against Eisner, making him a recurring guardian figure for [[CreativeCoreRenewal]] and the [[EntertainmentIPFlywheel]].
