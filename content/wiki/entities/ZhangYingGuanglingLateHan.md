## Overview

张婴 / Zhang Ying (Guangling, late Han) enters the wiki through Hanji 825 as the rebel who rises again in [[GuanglingCommandery|广陵郡]] after [[ZhangGangLateHan|张纲]] has died. Hanji 823 now supplies the missing prehistory: Zhang Ying had disturbed Yang and Xu for more than ten years, accepted Zhang Gang's direct negotiation, surrendered with more than ten thousand followers and families, and was stabilized through residence, land, and office arrangements before the settlement later frayed.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-823-bingbuxueren-shou-zeikou-ta-ruhe-zuodao-de-luai1f0nhb-ddajli99dw4bxj9m2|Hanji 823]] presents Zhang Ying as the head of a long-running Guangling rebellion. The episode frames his movement as arising from real anger at predatory two-thousand-shi officials, while still treating rebellion against the Han court as politically illegitimate and militarily doomed if he refuses settlement.

Zhang Ying's turning point comes when Zhang Gang arrives without asking for more troops and asks to meet him directly. Zhang Gang's speech gives Zhang Ying a path to surrender without denying the rebels' grievance: the state can recognize abuse, pardon the group, grant status, and use local offices to absorb descendants, while also retaining the threat of a multi-province army if Zhang Ying rejects terms.

The surrender is not merely verbal. Zhang Ying brings more than ten thousand followers and families out of rebellion, and Zhang Gang disperses them, chooses housing and farmland for Zhang Ying, and gives office routes to younger kin or descendants who want local service. Zhang Ying's group mourns Zhang Gang's death and helps carry the coffin home, which shows that the settlement created personal attachment.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-825-lishi-gaosu-ni-dang-ni-meiyong-shi-renxing-zui-zisi-luv-8z9axuejnfhlkgsuxee3uked|Hanji 825]] supplies the rebound. After Zhang Gang dies, Zhang Ying again gathers several thousand people and occupies Guangling. The profile therefore holds both facts together: Zhang Ying is a successful surrender case under Zhang Gang and also evidence that person-dependent pacification can reopen when the trusted official disappears.

## Key Characteristics

- Long-running Guangling rebel whose movement affects Yang and Xu before Zhang Gang's appointment.
- Grievance figure whose rebellion is explained through earlier official greed and violence without being treated as righteous.
- Surrender actor who responds to a negotiation that pairs dignity, pardon, and credible coercive alternative.
- Reintegration case whose followers and families are dispersed and settled rather than simply crushed.
- Person-dependent pacification test because mourning after Zhang Gang's death coexists with later renewed revolt.
- Disambiguated from [[ZhangYingLateHan|张英]], the later Liu Yao-side Jiangdong officer.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 823 and Hanji 825. It does not reconstruct Zhang Ying's full family background, exact military organization, later suppression, or the precise legal titles implied by the surrender settlement. Hanji 823's transcript form 张英 is normalized to 张婴 because Hanji 825 already uses the Guangling Zhang Ying identity and the received Tongjian passage distinguishes him from the later [[ZhangYingLateHan|张英]].

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 823 as the full prehistory of Zhang Ying's rebellion, negotiated surrender, and settlement under Zhang Gang.
- Reframed Hanji 825's renewed revolt as a rebound after documented submission rather than the first visible Zhang Ying event.
- Added the identity caution separating Guangling 张婴 from the later Jiangdong 张英.
