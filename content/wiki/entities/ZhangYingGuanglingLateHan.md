## Overview

张婴 / Zhang Ying (Guangling, late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-825-lishi-gaosu-ni-dang-ni-meiyong-shi-renxing-zui-zisi-luv-8z9axuejnfhlkgsuxee3uked|Hanji 825]] as the rebel who rises again in [[GuanglingCommandery|广陵郡]] after [[ZhangGangLateHan|张纲]] has died. The page is disambiguated from [[ZhangYingLateHan|张英]], the later Liu Yao officer in the Jiangdong branch.

## Current Profile

Hanji 825 gives Zhang Ying only a compact notice. The episode says Zhang Gang had previously recruited or pacified him, but after Zhang Gang's death, Zhang Ying again gathers several thousand people and captures Guangling.

The source uses the renewed revolt as a local-order aftershock rather than a full biography. Zhang Ying matters because his return shows that pacification can depend on a specific trusted official; once that personal authority disappears, coercion, grievance, or opportunity can reopen the rebellion.

## Key Characteristics

- Guangling rebel whose renewed uprising appears in the 145 CE succession year.
- Earlier pacification target associated with Zhang Gang's local authority.
- Actor whose return marks the fragility of settlement after a trusted official dies.
- Source-scoped local-disorder figure rather than a fully developed court actor.
