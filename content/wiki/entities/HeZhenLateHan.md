# 何真 / He Zhen (late Han)

[[HeZhenLateHan|何真]] was the father of [[EmpressDowagerHe|何皇后 / 何太后]], [[HeJin|何进]], and [[HeMiaoLateHan|何苗]] in the He-family rise branch of [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-918-shiqing-bu-shunli-shi-weishenme-yao-bizui-lh-tbohgo96qw8bjqu3x1yp6czv5|Hanji 918]].

The episode presents He Zhen as a butcher-family household head whose ambition and spending convert private access into palace opportunity: he supports his daughter's entry into the imperial selection channel and later benefits posthumously from her elevation and birth of [[LiuBianHongnongWang|刘辩]]. This makes him part of the prehistory behind later He-family and eunuch politics.
