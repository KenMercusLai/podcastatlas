# Order of the Garter

## Overview

The Order of the Garter is the English royal chivalric order through which [[EdwardIIIOfEngland|Edward III]] made [[StGeorge|St George]] a durable patron of court, military, and national identity.

## Current Profile

The episode dates the order's foundation to 23 April 1344 and emphasizes its George-centered imagery: the saint's feast day, red cross on white, and the first seal showing Edward kneeling before an armored George. The order turned an adaptable saint into an institutional patron rather than leaving him only in diffuse popular legend.

Its later history also demonstrates symbolic conflict. Radical Protestant reformers under Edward VI tried to remove George's associations, but the order retained him, making the institution a bridge between medieval chivalry, monarchy, Reformation contest, and continuing royal ritual.

## Key Characteristics

- Royal chivalric order founded by Edward III in the episode's account.
- Institution closely tied to St George's feast and patronage.
- User of the red cross on white in its visual language.
- Mechanism for joining sainthood, kingship, chivalry, and military identity.
- Site of Protestant-era contest rather than an unchanged medieval survival.
