# Quanzhen Daoism / 全真道教

Quanzhen Daoism / 全真道教 appears in [[shan-shang-shan-xia-jie-renjian-wushi-xiaoyao-ji-shenxian-lhjiguq74flhatdxnbrnzhjg-acp]] as the main Daoist institutional frame associated with [[WudangMountain|武当山]]. The episode does not attempt a full doctrinal history; it uses Quanzhen as the setting for morning and evening lessons, scripture recitation, temple duties, and varied personal cultivation methods.

The page remains source-scoped. The episode's main point is not that every Quanzhen site works like Li Chuang's Wudang experience, but that Daoist life there is organized through ordinary institutional time as well as religious time. This connects Quanzhen practice to [[DaoistTempleEverydayLabor]], [[DaoistNourishingLifeTechniques]], and [[DaoistRitualTechnology]].
