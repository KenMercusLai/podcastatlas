## Overview
AJ Fountain is [[JackHalldrup]]'s childhood friend and an early seller, investor, and business partner in the company that became [[DrSquatch]]. [[dr-squatch-jack-halldrup-from-a-closet-full-of-soap-to-a-1-5-billion-brand-vmp1750831437]] uses his path to show how informal founder help can become ownership and how legitimate but divergent ambitions can later require separation.

## Current Profile
Fountain first sold soap door to door for a reported $1 commission per bar, then invested family money and received an ownership stake. He and Halldrup pursued small retailers and early growth together, but Fountain wanted a business compatible with an outdoor-centered lifestyle while Halldrup wanted a much larger company. Their buyout resolved the strategic mismatch but strained a long childhood friendship and imposed multi-year financial obligations on a cash-constrained business.

## Key Characteristics
- Entered through commission-based field selling before becoming an investor and co-owner.
- Helped bring the early brand into small West Coast retail accounts.
- Preferred a lifestyle-compatible company rather than the larger-scale path Halldrup pursued.
- Became part of a financially and emotionally difficult partner buyout.
