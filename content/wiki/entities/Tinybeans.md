# Tinybeans

## Overview
Tinybeans is described by [[MarketplaceTech]] as a private photo-sharing and journaling app used by [[KayleeRobbins]] to share family material with a more limited audience.

## Current Profile
Within the bounded source, Tinybeans represents a middle path between public social posting and abandoning digital family sharing. Robbins entered a paid partnership with the service and continues to share extensively about her children there rather than making the same material available to the open internet.

## Key Characteristics
- Private family photo-sharing and journaling service in the episode's description.
- Offers a narrower sharing context than public social-media profiles.
- Appears through a paid partnership, making the recommendation commercially connected.
- Preserves family memory-sharing while changing audience scope.
