# Azizi Seixas

## Overview

Azizi Seixas is presented as a [[UniversityOfMiami]] health-data scientist leading work across digital transformation, sleep and circadian research, and population-health informatics.

## Current Profile

Seixas argues that care must extend beyond clinics into everyday environments and underserved communities. His team's [[TheMailboxHealthMonitoring|Mailbox]] project combines affordable connected devices, phones, APIs, a university command center, and clinical referral relationships to observe health risks between visits.

His equity thesis is design-led: systems built first for populations facing access barriers may become more scalable and broadly useful. The episode also attributes prospective digital-twin, augmented-reality, virtual-reality, and ambient-documentation work to his group.

## Key Characteristics

- Treats continuity gaps as both clinical and infrastructure problems.
- Uses home and community settings to collect data outside artificial clinical environments.
- Favors affordable interoperable devices over one expensive proprietary system.
- Builds community-health-center partnerships into research and escalation workflows.
- Makes underserved populations an initial design constituency rather than a later adaptation target.
