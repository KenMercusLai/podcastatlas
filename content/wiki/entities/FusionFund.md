# Fusion Fund

Fusion Fund is the venture firm associated with [[ZhangLu]] in [[e227-meiguo-yiliao-shichang-ai-zhengduozhan-jutou-yazhu-chuangye-gongsi-neng-ying-ma-f14f8686-a6e2-47ea-92c1-ca7e71199f67]]. The episode identifies Zhang as its founder and managing partner while she interprets healthcare AI from an investment and infrastructure perspective.

In this source, Fusion Fund is not analyzed as a portfolio case; it matters as the institutional position behind Zhang's view that regulated healthcare AI startups must solve model capability, integration, privacy, audit, compliance, legal, and enterprise sales problems together.
