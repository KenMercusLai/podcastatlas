# Wu Kai / 吴恺

Wu Kai / 吴恺 is the [[Airwallex]] chief revenue officer interviewed in [[11-nian-110-yi-meijin-ranhou-ne-duihua-airwallex-wu-kai-ai-shidai-xiayizhan-1000-yi-lr4tvdrq25by7fugoqkqojw6vwdk]]. The source says his CRO role covers revenue growth, with global marketing, sales teams, and regional general managers reporting into that remit.

Wu's narrative links three company stages: the original cross-border payment pain, the building of a [[GlobalFinancialNetwork]], and the move toward [[IntelligentFinance]]. He argues that Airwallex's durability comes from long-term global-network investment, broader banking-product coverage, local market execution, and proprietary financial workflow data that can make AI products stronger.

## Key Claims
- Frames Airwallex's first strategic choice as building globally from day one rather than optimizing for one region.
- Argues that money transfer alone is too low-level and must be joined with accounts, FX, cards, acquiring, yield, and credit.
- Treats AI as a way to lower the operational threshold for finance automation, especially for smaller companies without engineering teams.
- Presents data and talent as the main logic behind AI-era acquisitions.
- Uses local general managers and GDP-based market prioritization as Airwallex globalization lessons.
