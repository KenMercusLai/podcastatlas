# 辋川 / Wangchuan

辋川 / Wangchuan is the Lantian valley landscape tied to [[WangWei|王维]] in [[ep264-tafang-tangshi-zhilu-shengtang-qixiang-shaonianxin-loo2dfuxffizpbl4si5nwcixnngi]]. The episode describes it as a roughly eleven-kilometer valley south of Lantian, within reach of [[ChangAnTang|唐代长安]], where later readers try to connect Wang Wei and Pei Di's "辋川二十景" to actual terrain.

The source also stresses loss. Twentieth-century military-industrial construction and restricted access mean many Wang Wei-related relics are gone or difficult to view; the remaining tree, local scholarship, and approximate sites therefore become evidence fragments rather than a complete restored poetic estate.
