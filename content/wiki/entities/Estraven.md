# 伊斯特拉凡 / Estraven

伊斯特拉凡 / Estraven is the exiled [[Karhide|卡海德 / Karhide]] politician and central moral figure in [[UrsulaKLeGuin|Le Guin]]'s [[TheLeftHandOfDarkness|《黑暗的左手》]]. In [[36-heian-de-zuoshou-liufang-yu-ai-de-weilai-wangshi-605254522]], Estraven begins as prime minister, loses office after trying to avoid border conflict with [[Orgoreyn|欧格瑞恩 / Orgoreyn]], and becomes officially marked as a traitor.

The episode reads Estraven as the character who understands the scale of the problem before others do. He protects [[GenlyAi|金利·艾 / Genly Ai]], tries to use the [[Ekumen|艾库曼 / Ekumen]] contact to break Gethen's nationalist rivalry, rescues Genly from a forced-labor farm, crosses the ice with him, and finally dies in a way that makes the political truth harder to bury.

Estraven's tragedy is that the title "traitor" remains institutionally attached even after his action helps the planet join a larger human community. The source therefore links him to [[PatriotismAsHatred]], [[IntimacyBeyondGenderScript]], [[StatelessExile]], and [[LoyaltyBeyondContract]]: his loyalty is not to reputation, king, or border alone.
