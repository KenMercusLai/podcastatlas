# 亚萌 / Ya Meng

## Overview
亚萌 is the dermatology-side doctor in the VOL.218 [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode on missed diagnosis and follow-up after an uncertain first visit.

## Current Profile
The source presents 亚萌 as the voice explaining why visible skin findings still need history, hypothesis testing, and follow-up. Rashes, redness, and congestion can reflect infection, allergy, or systemic disease, so the dermatologist cannot responsibly treat every skin sign as self-evident.

Her practical emphasis is bounded observation. When a presentation is not typical, diagnostic treatment can be reasonable, but the patient should know whether the medicine should work in two or three days, what failure looks like, and when to return.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains dermatology as pattern recognition plus history and verification rather than surface inspection alone.
- Uses diagnostic treatment as a bounded test when the presentation is not yet definitive.
- Treats short follow-up windows and return conditions as part of responsible outpatient advice.
