# Betye Saar

Betye Saar is the assemblage artist profiled by [[AnneRowe]] in [[outsider-chance-ending-sudans-proxy-war-6a75b3695d765781a2b1b132]]. The episode presents her as an artist who found meaning in discarded objects from flea markets, yard sales, and market stalls, treating them as materials that carried the energy of previous owners.

The source links Saar's childhood in Watts and the influence of the [[WattsTowers]] to her later [[AssemblageArtMemoryWork]]. Objects such as rope, ships, chains, and birdcages became ways to address slavery, Black history, restricted life, and liberation.

Saar is especially connected to [[TheLiberationOfAuntJemima]], the 1968 work that transformed a racist commercial figure into an image of armed Black resistance. The episode says the piece touched a national nerve and made Saar feel she had become an artist.
