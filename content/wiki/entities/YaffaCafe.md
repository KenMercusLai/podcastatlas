# Yaffa Cafe

## Overview
Yaffa Cafe is a Brooklyn specialty cafe opened by [[HakeemSoleimani]] in 2019 near the deli operated by his father, [[TarekSoleimani]].

## Current Profile
The cafe joins specialty drinks and gathering space to Yemeni coffee heritage and direct origin sourcing. Its distinctiveness does not come from cultural story alone: the episode shows a business assembled from subway foot traffic, a relationship-enabled lease, family retail knowledge, neighborhood trust, relatives' farm contacts, and improvised logistics through a country at war. It reportedly became profitable, opened a second shop, and expanded the original location's food offering.

## Key Characteristics
- Offers specialty drinks and a cafe environment distinct from the neighboring low-price deli format.
- Uses Yemeni origin and sourcing as product identity and cultural connection.
- Occupies a high-foot-traffic location obtained through an inherited neighborhood relationship.
- Depends on local and transnational family networks for execution.
- Converted an initially fragile import route into a profitable, expanding operation according to the episode.
