## Overview
亚冠 / AFC Champions League appears in the source as the continental benchmark for judging [[GuangzhouFC]]'s Evergrande-era achievement. The episode treats Guangzhou's two Asian titles as historically high and difficult for Chinese clubs to reproduce.

## Current Profile
The competition's role here is achievement calibration. Domestic titles may eventually be matched by other Chinese clubs, but the source argues that Asian championships require a rare combination of club strength, high-level foreign players, favorable regional competition timing, and workable tournament conditions.

## Key Characteristics
- Continental club competition used to measure the peak of Evergrande-era Guangzhou football.
- Hard-to-repeat achievement context because Chinese clubs now face stronger Asian competition, especially from West Asian and Saudi clubs.
- Evidence that Guangzhou FC's legacy cannot be reduced to financial scandal or domestic spending alone.

## Evidence
- Title record: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] says Guangzhou FC won the Asian championship in 2013 and 2015.

- Regional competition: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] says current West Asian and Saudi strength makes near-term Chinese title contention difficult.
