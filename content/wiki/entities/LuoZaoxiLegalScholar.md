# 罗早熙 / Luo Zaoxi (legal scholar)

## Overview
罗早熙 is presented as a guest from Donghua University's legal-affairs office discussing identity records, education credentials, employment, and anti-discrimination law for sexual minorities.

## Current Profile
Luo distinguishes formal access to a changed household-registration or identity-card sex marker after gender-reassignment surgery from the harder problem of keeping older education records aligned. She describes school-issued explanatory proof as a possible workaround rather than a complete privacy solution because third-party verification can still expose earlier data. Her policy framing favors explicit anti-discrimination protection while acknowledging that the described system gives less support to people who have not undergone surgery.

## Key Characteristics
- Separates formal legal entitlement from administrative implementation.
- Identifies education records as a continuity and privacy problem.
- Treats school proof as a workaround rather than full record correction.
- Connects record mismatch with employment and involuntary disclosure.
- Supports explicit protection against sexual-minority and transgender discrimination.
