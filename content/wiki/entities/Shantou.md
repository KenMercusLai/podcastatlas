# Shantou / 汕头

Shantou is the port city that anchors [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046]]. The episode follows Shantou from a treaty-port and overseas-labor departure point into one of China's first four special economic zones, then asks why it did not become a [[Shenzhen]]-style industrial city.

The source's answer is cumulative rather than monocausal. Shantou inherited [[Chaoshan]]'s overseas Chinese networks and [[QiaopiRemittanceNetworks]], but the [[ShantouSpecialEconomicZone]] started small, was less strategically comprehensive than Shenzhen, sat farther from [[HongKong]], lost regional scale through [[RegionalAdministrativeFragmentation]], and later suffered a severe [[ExportTaxFraudCreditCrisis]].
