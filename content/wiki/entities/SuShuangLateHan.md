## Overview
苏双 / Su Shuang is a late-Han merchant presented as an early financial backer of [[LiuBei|刘备]].

## Current Profile
The bounded source pairs Su Shuang with [[ZhangShipingLateHan|张世平]] as merchants whose money helps Liu Bei assemble an initial group. Su Shuang therefore represents the commercial-capital layer in an early career otherwise supported by family, teacher, and peer relationships.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides early funding before Liu Bei has stable territorial power.
- Helps convert social reputation into organized personnel capacity.
- Appears as part of a paired merchant intervention rather than an isolated patron.

## Evidence
- Early finance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1097-cong-fanlu-shaonian-dao-liuhuangshu-ta-de-nixi-bi-ni-xiang-de-geng-hen-llhlpnoymxxf7y7_rkhtjb1jkkeg|Hanji 1097]] names Su Shuang with Zhang Shiping as a source of funds for gathering followers.

## Qualifications
The current evidence is one podcast source and gives no independent profile, exact amount, or proven motive for the support.

## What Changed
- Created Su Shuang as one half of Liu Bei's early merchant-finance pair.
