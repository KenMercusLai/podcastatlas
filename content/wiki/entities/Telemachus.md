# Telemachus

## Overview

Telemachus is the son of [[Odysseus]] and [[Penelope]], represented in [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] as the young heir whose maturation forms the second front of the homecoming plot.

## Current Profile

The episode begins Telemachus in a weak position: he is surrounded by suitors consuming his father's estate and cannot yet protect the household. [[Athena]]'s intervention sends him to call an assembly, travel for news of Odysseus, return safely to [[Ithaca]], recognize his father, remove the hall's weapons, and join the revenge against the suitors.

## Key Characteristics

- Telemachus' arc changes the household crisis from passive endurance into coordinated recovery.
- His journey to Pylos and Sparta supplies reliable confirmation that Odysseus is alive on Calypso's island.
- His recognition of Odysseus depends on Athena's temporary restoration of Odysseus' noble appearance.
- His practical role in the revenge is decisive because he removes weapons from the hall and cooperates with loyal servants.

## Evidence

- Initial weakness and summons to action: [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] has Athena urge Telemachus to call an assembly and seek news of his father.
- Reputation and confirmation journey: [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] recounts Nestor, Helen, Menelaus, and Proteus giving Telemachus stories and confirmation that Odysseus lives.
- Recognition scene: [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] says Athena restores Odysseus' appearance so Telemachus can recognize him.
- Tactical participation: [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] describes Telemachus removing the weapons before Odysseus strings the bow.

## Qualifications

The page is bounded to the episode's retelling of the Odyssey homecoming. Telemachus' growth is significant, but the source still presents the plan as dependent on Odysseus' return, Athena's aid, and loyal household allies.

## What Changed

- Created Telemachus as the heir-maturation actor in the Odyssey homecoming branch.
