# 陈蕃 / Chen Fan (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-901-jixingzi-de-ren-hui-shi-shenme-xiachang-lh1yfyjzqptxvtnv9taiqms9yjud|Hanji 901]] adds Chen Fan as a background grievance before his later rehabilitation-memory branch. In the debate over [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦皇太后]]'s burial with [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]], [[ChenQiuLateHan|陈球 / 陈求]] says Chen Fan and [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] were unjustly harmed, and the source treats that memory as part of why eunuch figures resist honoring Dou.

This source therefore places Chen Fan inside [[ImperialBurialLegitimacy|帝后合葬名分]] before [[PartyProhibitionRehabilitation|党人翻案合法性]]. The grievance can surface through a burial decision long before Dong Zhuo later tries to borrow legitimacy by rehabilitating old anti-eunuch victims.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-921-jinqian-ru-shui-que-ze-ke-si-tan-ze-yan-si-lj1dk4qdgcago9elr7ctuvre3zic|Hanji 921]] backfills Chen Fan as an older victim of [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]]'s eunuch-side politics. The episode names him with [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] when summarizing Cao Jie's record, giving the later anti-eunuch grievance a pre-184 memory before Chen Yi and Dong Zhuo reuse that moral capital.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-941-shenchu-guanchang-ta-jing-xiang-ganfan-huangdi-liu-fqiz9pi7u6cltrn-cjmjz-r|Hanji 941]] backfills Chen Fan through his son [[ChenYiLateHan|陈逸]]. The episode says Chen Yi's father had been harmed by the eunuchs, making Chen Fan's death a grievance that feeds the anti-eunuch mood around [[WangFenLateHan|王芬]]'s failed coup.

陈蕃 / Chen Fan (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-948-dongzhuo-feidi-quanchen-ruhe-gaibian-huangquan-youxi-ltekg1a-5b5u37aeekjm3dm2m6kk|Hanji 948]] as one of the earlier anti-eunuch and Party Prohibition victims whose case [[DongZhuo|董卓]] asks the court to rehabilitate. The episode does not give a full biography; it uses Chen Fan as a prestige-bearing name in Dong Zhuo's bid to make his violent takeover look like a correction of past injustice.

His role therefore grounds [[PartyProhibitionRehabilitation|党人翻案合法性]]. Restoring honors and appointing descendants can answer a real old grievance, but in this source it also works as political cover for Dong Zhuo after the forced deposition and He-family purge.
