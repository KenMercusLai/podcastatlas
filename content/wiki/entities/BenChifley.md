# Ben Chifley

## Overview

Ben Chifley was an Australian Labor prime minister and former engine driver who moved from trade-union politics and wartime treasury leadership into postwar reconstruction.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Chifley as an Australian counterpart to Clement Attlee: personally rooted in labor and union life, administratively associated with fiscal discipline and controls, and politically committed to social-democratic reconstruction. His failed attempt to nationalize banks marks both the ambition and limit of that program before [[RobertMenzies|Robert Menzies]] returned to office.

## Key Characteristics

- Former engine driver with strong Labor and trade-union roots.
- Wartime treasurer under [[JohnCurtin|John Curtin]].
- Postwar prime minister associated with austerity and fiscal discipline.
- Social-democratic leader whose bank-nationalization attempt failed.
