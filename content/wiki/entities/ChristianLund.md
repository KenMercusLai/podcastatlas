# Christian Lund

Christian Lund is the [[Templafy]] co-founder interviewed by [[OmerKhan]] in [[enterprise-sales-with-no-product-landing-a-big-four-customer]]. In the episode, he explains how [[Templafy]] was spun out from [[Omnidocs]] after the cloud shift made the older technology model insufficient for enterprise document automation.

Lund's main wiki role is as a source for enterprise sales discipline. He argues that early enterprise startups can sell domain expertise and a credible market-shift thesis before a full product exists, but should not accept vague small POCs without budget, timing, success criteria, and rollout consequences. His "yes, if" framing anchors [[EnterprisePOCDiscipline]].

He also supplies the AI reset lesson. Lund compares AI to the cloud shift, argues that enterprises need controllable and guarded AI behavior, and says [[Templafy]] initially overreached in messaging by talking too far ahead of what buyers were ready to evaluate.
