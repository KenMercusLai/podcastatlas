# 司马尚 / Sima Shang

司马尚 / Sima Shang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-113-limu-ruhe-yong-yiji-zhili-zu-qinguo-tongyi-luymqpcolsvyz5miodit3wdlx0ob]] as the [[ZhaoState|赵国]] commander paired with [[LiMu|李牧]] during [[QinState|秦国]]'s 229 BCE attack on Zhao. The source presents him less as an independent battlefield figure than as part of the command pair Qin chooses to remove through court intrigue.

The episode says Qin bribes [[GuoKai|郭开]] to accuse Li Mu and Sima Shang of rebellion before [[ZhaoYoumiuWang|赵迁 / 赵幽缪王]]. Zhao then replaces them with [[ZhaoCong|赵葱]] and [[YanJuZhao|颜聚]]. Sima Shang yields the command, while Li Mu refuses and is killed or arrested, making Sima Shang part of the same [[BattlefieldCommanderReplacementRisk|front-line replacement]] failure even though his personal endpoint is less dramatic in the source.
