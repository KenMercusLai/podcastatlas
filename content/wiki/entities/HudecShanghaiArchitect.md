# 乌达克 / Hudec (Shanghai architect)

## Overview
乌达克 appears as the better-known comparison point for [[LaiAnArchitect|赉安 / 赖安]] in the episode's account of modern Shanghai architecture.

## Current Profile
The source treats Hudec as a major Shanghai architect whose reputation was studied earlier and more visibly than Lai An's. He is positioned as commercially strong, socially capable, active across public and French Concession spaces, and supported by networks such as the American Raven connection. The comparison helps Wu Feipeng argue that Lai An's French academic training, Art Deco purity, and French Concession concentration deserve more attention.

## Key Characteristics
- Hudec is the familiar benchmark against which Lai An's underrecognition is measured.
- His Shanghai work is described as more commercially colored than Lai An's.
- He is associated with both public-settlement and French-Concession commissions.
- His survival record is contrasted with Lai An's more preserved building corpus.
