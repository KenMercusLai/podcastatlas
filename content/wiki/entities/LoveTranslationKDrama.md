# 《爱情怎么翻译》 / Love Translation K-Drama

## Overview
《爱情怎么翻译》 is the Korean-drama hook in a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode on intimacy, attachment, indirect speech, and the difficulty of translating one's own inner state.

## Current Profile
The ingested episode presents the drama less as an object of plot summary than as a relationship-psychology case. The title's translation metaphor lets [[Atang|阿汤]], [[CuiJiabin|崔嘉宾]], and [[NanJiHe|南基贺]] ask why people who can manage language, work, or social presentation may still fail to express need, fear, shame, and closeness directly.

The drama's key use in the source is contrast: one lead appears to flee love because closeness activates injury and undeservingness, while the other appears to manage emotion through rationalization, work boundaries, and controlled distance.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a media hook for discussing intimacy rather than as a clinically diagnosed case file.
- Makes translation a metaphor for unspoken needs, indirect speech, and self-knowledge.
- Gives the episode examples of confession flight, "you go" speech, split-off expression, and rescuer dynamics.
- Lets Chinese and Korean clinicians connect drama scenes to real work pressure, shame, and youth relationship avoidance.
