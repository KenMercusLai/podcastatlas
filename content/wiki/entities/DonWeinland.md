# Don Weinland

## Overview
Don Weinland is a [[TheIntelligence]] contributor in the wiki's China technology coverage, especially where Chinese AI and robotics move from domestic capability into deployed services, standards, and physical-world data.

## Current Profile
The bounded sources make Weinland a correspondent voice for China's applied AI edge. His earlier segment explains Chinese driverless-taxi exports as an economics and standards problem. The newer segment turns to humanoid robots, where he separates spectacle from useful capability and makes real-world movement data the central constraint.

## Key Characteristics
- He connects Chinese technology capability to deployment economics rather than only lab progress.
- He treats first-mover advantage as a standards and market-shaping problem in overseas AI services.
- He is cautious about humanoid robot usefulness despite acknowledging China's industrial lead and scale advantage.
- His robotics analysis emphasizes data infrastructure, training centers, costs, and real-world physical learning.
