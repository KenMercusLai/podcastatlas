# Don Weinland

Don Weinland appears in [[omission-accomplished-why-the-iran-war-cycle-spins-on-6a6c7475aa1642a30f17f1cb]] as the [[TheIntelligence]] contributor explaining why Chinese AI companies are moving driverless-taxi services abroad. His segment turns the wiki's existing China AI export branch from chips, hardware, and open models toward deployed services that carry operational rules into foreign cities.

Weinland's main contribution is the economics-and-standards link. He argues that [[China]] has too many taxi drivers and low domestic fares, while overseas markets such as [[Switzerland]] may offer much higher ride revenue. He also warns that early deployments can shape local technical standards before [[Waymo]] or other American entrants arrive.
