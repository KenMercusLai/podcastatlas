# Chartreuse

## Overview

Chartreuse is the liqueur whose closely guarded formula is used in [[the-formula-the-formula|The Formula]] to contrast communal, trust-based secrecy with corporate legal enforcement.

## Current Profile

The episode reports that the formula contains 130 herbs and is held within the [[CarthusianOrder]]. Former Carthusian Father Michael Halloran describes the product as supporting contemplative communal life and says the order trusted him not to disclose the formula after he left.

Chartreuse matters to the episode less as a technical recipe than as a social arrangement: confidential knowledge can bind members to a shared purpose without becoming an explicit instrument of post-employment restraint.

## Key Characteristics

- Herbal liqueur associated with a secret formula.
- Produced by the [[CarthusianOrder]] to support communal life.
- Represents confidential knowledge sustained through trust in the episode's comparison.
- Functions as a normative counterpoint rather than a technically comparable food experiment.

This page is based on one structured episode summary rather than the formula, production record, institutional archive, or independent history. The Chartreuse and Thomas cases differ in product, organization, law, scale, and purpose, so the comparison is interpretive rather than controlled.
