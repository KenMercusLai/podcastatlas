# Matt Ball (Colorado legislator)

## Overview
Matt Ball appears as a Colorado Democratic state senator and bipartisan sponsor of new sports-betting restrictions.

## Current Profile
[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-90166871d561fa45ba428e0a0fbc3e7e-20290737-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-90166871d561fa45ba428e0a0fbc3e7e-20290737]] presents Ball as a bettor and long-time fantasy commissioner who nonetheless frames problem gambling as a public-health issue. His policy theory is to add friction at the moments when users borrow, reload after losses, or receive prompts, while openly acknowledging the lack of outcome data behind the chosen limits.

## Key Characteristics
- Legislator using a public-health rather than moral-prohibition frame.
- Supports payment, deposit-frequency, and platform-communication restrictions.
- Treats the Colorado law as an experiment whose effectiveness remains unproven.
