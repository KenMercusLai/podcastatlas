## Overview

张谭 / Zhang Tan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-502-rang-laoban-bei-heiguo-shi-shui-zai-zhaosi-ls833sgrdjz9e1tupiiqillagohb|Hanji 502]] as the Yushi Dafu paired with [[KuangHengWesternHan|匡衡]] in [[WangZunWesternHan|王尊]]'s early-[[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] impeachment after [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]]'s fall.

## Current Profile

The episode does not develop Zhang Tan as an independent biography. His role is structural: Wang Zun uses him with Kuang Heng to argue that senior officials who accommodated Shi Xian while he was powerful should not later shift responsibility toward [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] after the amnesty and regime change.

Zhang Tan therefore functions as part of a top-office accountability pair. If he and Kuang Heng were removed together, Chengdi would face simultaneous senior-office disruption early in the new reign. The source uses that risk to explain why Chengdi stops the impeachment drive and treats the case as [[CourtTransitionBlameManagement|新君清算中的责任安排]] rather than simple right-or-wrong adjudication.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Yushi Dafu figure in the early Chengdi cleanup after Shi Xian's fall.
- Paired with Kuang Heng in Wang Zun's impeachment.

- Source-scoped figure whose broader career is not reconstructed here.
