## Overview

张谭 / Zhang Tan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-495-xihan-mingjiang-pk-yanren-chongchen-yingpengying-shui-neng-ying-lk9qa3yd8l17wyadfsi6cgubzfyo|Hanji 495]], [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-500-taijian-shixian-zhisi-zhan-de-yuegao-si-de-yuecan-lvu7w8xa8mjn8_mbxew7e4afupra|Hanji 500]], and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-502-rang-laoban-bei-heiguo-shi-shui-zai-zhaosi-ls833sgrdjz9e1tupiiqillagohb|Hanji 502]] as the Yushi Dafu appointed after [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]] blocks [[FengYewangWesternHan|冯野王]], then later paired with [[KuangHengWesternHan|匡衡]] in the early-[[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] cleanup after Shi Xian's fall.

## Current Profile

The episodes do not develop Zhang Tan as an independent biography. His role is structural. Hanji 495 places him at the personnel-outcome side of Shi Xian's maneuver: once Feng Yewang is blocked on consort-kin reputation grounds, Zhang Tan is appointed Yushi Dafu. Hanji 500 later places him with Kuang Heng in the first successful attack on Shi Xian after Shi Xian loses [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]]'s backing and is moved away from the inner access channel. Hanji 502 then shows the reverse exposure: [[WangZunWesternHan|王尊]] uses Zhang Tan with Kuang Heng to argue that senior officials who accommodated Shi Xian while he was powerful should not later shift responsibility toward Yuan after the amnesty and regime change.

Zhang Tan therefore functions as part of a top-office accountability pair. If he and Kuang Heng were removed together, Chengdi would face simultaneous senior-office disruption early in the new reign. The source uses that risk to explain why Chengdi stops the impeachment drive and treats the case as [[CourtTransitionBlameManagement|新君清算中的责任安排]] rather than simple right-or-wrong adjudication.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Yushi Dafu appointed after Feng Yewang is blocked under Yuan.
- Later Yushi Dafu figure in the early Chengdi cleanup after Shi Xian's fall.
- Paired with Kuang Heng first in impeaching Shi Xian, then in Wang Zun's counter-impeachment.
- Represents senior-office continuity risk if a new ruler removes too many top officials at once.
- Source-scoped figure whose broader career is not reconstructed here.
