# 营养快线 / Wahaha Nutri-Express

## Overview
营养快线 is a [[WahahaGroup|娃哈哈]] milk-and-fruit beverage used in the source as both a peak product-success case and a rumor-crisis case.

## Current Profile
The product launched in 2004 around a portable breakfast occasion. In the episode, it reached exceptional single-product scale by 2013, then suffered from internet rumors about overnight gel formation and health risks that traditional expert-led public relations could not fully neutralize.

## Key Characteristics
- Breakfast-positioned beverage combining milk, fruit flavor, portability, and convenience.
- Became one of Wahaha's most important high-scale single products.
- Exposes how internet rumor dynamics can damage a legacy consumer brand faster than old media-era crisis responses can repair it.

## Evidence
- Use occasion: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] says the product targeted office workers who wanted a quick breakfast-like drink.
- Peak scale: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] describes 2013 sales as a major single-product record in China's beverage industry.
- Rumor crisis: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] reports rumors about gel formation, botulinum, leukemia, and recall claims, and says expert demonstrations and press conferences had limited effect.

## Qualifications
The page keeps the claimed sales drop and loss amounts source-scoped. The episode explains the gel-like residue as milk-protein dehydration rather than proof of the rumors, but this page does not independently verify product-safety science.
