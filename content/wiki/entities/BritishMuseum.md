# British Museum

## Overview
The British Museum appears in this wiki branch as the institution receiving and exhibiting the [[BayeuxTapestry|Bayeux Tapestry]] in Britain.

## Current Profile
The museum's source-bounded role is logistical and symbolic. [[AlexandraSuichBass]] describes the tapestry arriving by truck with police escort from an undisclosed location in France and being unfurled at the museum, making the exhibition both a conservation event and a return of an England-stitched object to Britain.

## Key Characteristics
- Cultural institution hosting the episode's Bayeux Tapestry exhibition.
- Conservation and display site where curators carefully unfurl the medieval textile.
- Symbolic venue because the source frames the exhibition as the tapestry's first outside France and a kind of homecoming.
- Interpretive institution represented through [[NickCullinan]]'s comments on gaps, missing evidence, and meaning.
