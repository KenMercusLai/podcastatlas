# Malibu High School

Malibu High School is the project example in [[tech-20260420-0420-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260420-0420-mp-tech-pod-128]], where [[MarketplaceTech]] explains [[California]]'s [[EmbodiedCarbonBuildingCode]]. The episode describes a modern metal-and-concrete school building with solar panels and EV chargers, then uses its material review to make [[LowCarbonConstructionMaterials]] concrete.

The school matters to the source because it turns carbon accounting from an abstract rule into a construction checklist. Roofing, floors, steel, walls, paint, and insulation all become places where [[VerticalGroup]] and builders can evaluate material choices under [[ConstructionCarbonCompliance]].
