# 薄静 / Bo Jing

薄静 / Bo Jing is the [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] reporter and [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] speaker in [[ep264-tafang-tangshi-zhilu-shengtang-qixiang-shaonianxin-loo2dfuxffizpbl4si5nwcixnngi]] who explains the [[ZhedongTangPoetryRoad|浙东唐诗之路]] and [[MengHaoran|孟浩然]] branch. The source spells her name as 薄静, so this page is kept separate from [[BoJing|博靖 / Bo Jing]] unless later sources resolve the homophone as the same person.

Her reporting follows the Qiantang crossing, Shaoxing, Shengzhou, Xinchang, Tiantai, and related mountains, roads, and temple sites. The episode uses her field impressions to show how [[TangPoetryCulturalGeography|唐诗文化地理]] combines scenery, old waterways, Six Dynasties memory, Daoist recommendation networks, local slow life, and the uncertainty of reconstructing a poet's route.
