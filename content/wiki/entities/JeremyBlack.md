# Jeremy Black

Jeremy Black appears in [[elbows-up-again-a-us-canada-trade-flare-up-6a8c0fa3a89d26398ee7a5db]] as the military historian whose siege distinction anchors the episode's final segment. Prompted by [[ChristopherNolan]]'s [[TheOdyssey]], the segment uses Black's contrast between totemic and utilitarian sieges to separate later symbolic memory from direct strategic consequence.

In this source, Black's contribution is classificatory rather than a full campaign history. [[Vienna]] in 1683 and the Alamo in 1836 appear as totemic cases, while Tyre, Leningrad, and Malta appear as utilitarian cases involving heavy material and strategic stakes. The page therefore connects him primarily to [[SiegeTypology]] rather than to each siege as a separate canonical subject.
