# 徐奉 / Xu Feng (late Han)

徐奉 / Xu Feng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-924-1-de-cuowu-100-de-shibai-lmeo7vufm6ib5lni6p49rponazut|Hanji 924]] as a palace eunuch exposed with [[FengXuLateHan|封谞]] as an inside contact for [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]].
