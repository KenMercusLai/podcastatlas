# Jean-Marie Le Pen

## Overview

Jean-Marie Le Pen was the National Front co-founder and presidential candidate whose party lineage, extremist reputation, and 2002 runoff breakthrough define the inheritance that [[MarineLePen]] has tried to normalize.

## Current Profile

[[179-french-presidents-1981-2022-part-2-glt2206497694]] places Jean-Marie Le Pen in an anti-Gaullist tradition connected by the hosts to Action Française, Vichy, opposition to Algerian decolonization, and service in Algeria. His 2002 advance to the presidential runoff revealed an electoral force that had long sat in the background of the [[FrenchFifthRepublic]].

[[marine-warfare-le-pen-runs-for-president-6a4e1f2781fe1f6460b7117c]] focuses on the reputational afterlife: the xenophobic and anti-Semitic associations of the party he co-founded created the ceiling his daughter has tried to lower through renaming, distance, and changed presentation.

## Key Characteristics

- Co-founded the National Front in the 1970s.
- Represented an anti-Gaullist and nationalist far-right current.
- Opposed de Gaulle's Algeria policy after serving in Algeria.
- Reached the second round of the 2002 presidential election.
- Left a party and family reputation central to Marine Le Pen's normalization strategy.
