# Osorio Duque Estrada

## Overview

Osorio Duque Estrada appears in the episode as the lyricist whose words were eventually adopted for the [[BrazilianNationalAnthem]] around the 1922 independence centenary.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Duque Estrada to show that Brazil's anthem text was settled much later than its tune. He won a 1909 contest, but his lyrics were debated and revised for about a decade before the government bought and officially settled them as the centenary of independence approached.

## Key Characteristics

- Lyricist of the officially settled Brazilian anthem text in the episode's account.
- Won a contest for anthem lyrics in 1909.
- His words underwent years of debate and revision.
- The government bought the lyrics as the 1922 independence centenary approached.
- His role completes the pairing with [[FranciscoManuelDaSilva]]'s older tune.

## Evidence

- Contest and revision: [[681-brazil-the-emperors-anthem-part-5-glt5596929385]] says Duque Estrada won a 1909 lyrics contest and that the text was debated and revised for about a decade.
- Official settlement: [[681-brazil-the-emperors-anthem-part-5-glt5596929385]] says the government purchased the lyrics and settled the anthem text around the 1922 centenary.
