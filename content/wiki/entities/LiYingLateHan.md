# 李膺 / Li Ying (late Han)

## Overview

Li Ying is a late-Han scholar-official and party-prohibition victim whose reputation places him at the center of the 169 CE second party-prohibition disaster.

## Current Profile

The current evidence presents Li Ying as a public moral figure whose prestige becomes exposure. Hanji 892 supplies the pre-crackdown layer: even after dismissal, Li Ying remains respected by士人, and the episode places him in the honor-list environment that turns fame into a warning sign. Hanji 893 then supplies the arrest layer: [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] and [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] had briefly restored Li Ying and other士人 to office, but their deaths let the eunuch side remove him again.

When [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] frames party networks as a possible rebellion to the young [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]], Li Ying refuses flight, enters prison, and dies under torture. His profile therefore combines moral authority, reputation-network danger, and teacher-network liability. Li Ying's students and former subordinates are prohibited from office after his death, and [[JingYiLateHan|景毅]] treats his son's connection to Li Ying as an honor serious enough to self-report rather than accept omission from the punishment list.

## Key Characteristics

- Publicly respected late-Han士人 whose reputation places him inside the party-prohibition target field.
- Pre-crackdown honor-list figure whose name remains socially powerful even after dismissal.
- Reinstated under Chen Fan and Dou Wu before being removed again after their destruction.
- Death-facing official who refuses to flee and actively enters prison.
- Teacher and patron figure whose students and former subordinates are punished after his case.
- Reputation peer of [[FanPangLateHan|范滂]] and Du Mi in the episode's account of名节.

## Qualifications

This page does not yet attempt a full Li Ying biography. The current sources preserve the political meaning of his reputation, death, and student network, while details of earlier offices, the first party-prohibition disaster, and the full accusation list remain outside the bounded evidence. Transcript variants such as 李英 are normalized to 李膺.
