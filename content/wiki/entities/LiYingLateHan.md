## Overview

Li Ying is a late-Han scholar-official and party-prohibition victim whose profile now begins in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-874-zhenjing-muzi-xiangcan-tianli-nanrong-lpr3eglbnwbve1clrttapw6k5zdq|Hanji 874]] as the enforcement figure who executes [[ZhangChengAstrologerLateHan|张成]] father and son despite an amnesty. That action triggers eunuch retaliation through [[LaoXiuLateHan|劳修]]'s accusation, after which Li Ying himself becomes a first Party Prohibition prisoner. Hanji 875-876 then develop his reputation field and rescue path, Hanji 884 restores him into reform hope, and Hanji 892-893 show how the same prestige becomes lethal exposure in the second party-prohibition disaster.

## Current Profile

The current evidence presents Li Ying as a public moral figure whose prestige moves through enforcement, prison liability, reform capital, and fatal exposure. Hanji 874 supplies the earliest bounded layer: Li Ying refuses to let a predicted amnesty protect Zhang Cheng's calculated murder scheme, arrests Zhang Cheng father and son, and executes them. Because Zhang Cheng has eunuch connections, the enforcement case becomes a grievance that can be recoded as party accusation. Once Li Ying is arrested, the case expands to roughly 200 people, including [[DuMiLateHan|杜密]], [[ChenShiLateHan|陈寔]], and [[FanPangLateHan|范滂]].

Hanji 875 supplies the reputation-paired layer. Li Ying and Du Mi are arrested together because their reputations are comparable, and the surrounding [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]] case shows that imprisonment of famous worthy men can become a sign of moral belonging. Hanji 876 supplies the rescue layer: after [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]]'s removal frightens officials away from open pleading, Dou Wu's memorial argues that Li Ying and the other士人 have been accused without firm evidence. [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]]'s softened interrogation of Fan Pang then leads to Li Ying and the others having their heavy restraints removed.

Hanji 884 supplies the reform-capital layer: Li Ying, Du Mi, [[YinXunLateHan|尹勋]], and [[LiuYuAstrologerLateHan|刘瑜]] enter the political core as part of the court-repair hope around Chen Fan and [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]]. Hanji 892 then supplies the pre-crackdown reputation layer: even after dismissal, Li Ying remains respected by士人. Hanji 893 supplies the arrest-death layer. [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] frames party networks as possible rebellion to the young [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]], Li Ying refuses flight, enters prison, and dies under torture. His students and former subordinates are prohibited from office after his death.

## Key Characteristics

- Enforcement-trigger figure who executes Zhang Cheng father and son despite an amnesty-gaming defense.
- First Party Prohibition prisoner whose case expands from a criminal enforcement dispute into a士人 accusation field.
- Reputation-paired figure arrested in the same moral field as Du Mi.
- Publicly respected late-Han士人 whose imprisonment helps make punishment a moral-belonging signal.
- Prisoner important enough for Dou Wu to defend in a high-risk memorial.
- Reform-cohort figure whose entry into the 168 CE court center helps create expectations of political repair.
- Death-facing teacher and patron figure whose students and former subordinates are punished after his case.

## Qualifications

This page does not attempt a full Li Ying biography. Hanji 874 adds the enforcement trigger and first arrest expansion, while Hanji 875-876 preserve the reputation and rescue field and Hanji 893 preserves the later death-facing branch. The source shows an amnesty dispute but does not supply a complete Han legal analysis of Li Ying's execution decision. Transcript variants such as 李英 are normalized to 李膺.

## What Changed

- Hanji 874 moves Li Ying's bounded profile earlier than Hanji 875, into the Zhang Cheng enforcement case.
- The current profile now explains why eunuch retaliation targets Li Ying before he becomes a party-prisoner symbol.
- Li Ying's first Party Prohibition role now includes case expansion to roughly 200 implicated people.
- The page keeps pre-amnesty prison softening separate from later release, registry, reputation, and death-facing layers.
