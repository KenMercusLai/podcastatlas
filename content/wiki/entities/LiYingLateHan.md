## Overview

李膺 / Li Ying is a late-Han scholar-official, reputation model, and party-prohibition victim whose current wiki profile now begins in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-869-liying-weihe-bei-chengwei-tianxia-diyi-kaimo-lltckcioe1n9v91yy4c9vvwmz4mo|Hanji 869]]. That episode shows his pardon, return as Sili xiaowei, execution of [[ZhangShuoLateHan|张硕]], and "登龙门" prestige before the later Party Prohibition arc. Hanji 874-876 then develop the Zhang Cheng enforcement trigger, first arrest expansion, reputation field, and rescue path, while Hanji 884 and Hanji 892-893 show reform restoration followed by fatal exposure in the second party-prohibition disaster.

## Current Profile

Li Ying's current evidence presents him as a public moral figure whose prestige moves through restored office, high-risk enforcement, reputation gatekeeping, prison liability, reform capital, and fatal exposure. Hanji 869 supplies the earliest bounded layer: [[YingFengLateHan|应奉]] persuades [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] to remove punishment from Li Ying and other officials by making their usefulness a state-security issue. Li Ying then returns as Sili xiaowei and immediately becomes a test case for whether law can reach eunuch-connected offenders.

The Zhang Shuo branch makes that reputation concrete. [[ZhangShuoLateHan|张硕]], brother of [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]], hides in Zhang Rang's Luoyang house after abusive local conduct. Li Ying breaks into the hiding place, imprisons him, and executes him. When Zhang Rang complains to Huan, Li Ying cites the Confucius and Shao Zhengmao precedent and reframes speed as lawful severity rather than unauthorized violence. Huan accepts the defense, and palace eunuchs become visibly afraid of Li Ying. The same episode says士人 treated being received by him as "登龙门," making his personal recognition a prestige threshold.

Hanji 874 supplies the later first Party Prohibition trigger. Li Ying refuses to let a predicted amnesty protect [[ZhangChengAstrologerLateHan|张成]]'s calculated murder scheme, arrests Zhang Cheng father and son, and executes them. Because Zhang Cheng has eunuch connections, the enforcement case becomes a grievance that [[LaoXiuLateHan|劳修]] can recode as party accusation. Once Li Ying is arrested, the case expands to roughly 200 people, including [[DuMiLateHan|杜密]], [[ChenShiLateHan|陈寔]], and [[FanPangLateHan|范滂]].

Hanji 875 and Hanji 876 supply the imprisonment and rescue layers. Li Ying and Du Mi are arrested together because their reputations are comparable, and Huangfu Gui's shame at not being imprisoned shows that punishment of famous worthy men can become a moral-belonging signal. After [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]]'s removal frightens officials away from open pleading, [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]]'s memorial defends Li Ying and the other士人 as accused without firm evidence; [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]]'s softened interrogation of Fan Pang then leads to Li Ying and others having their heavy restraints removed.

Hanji 884 supplies the reform-capital layer: Li Ying, Du Mi, [[YinXunLateHan|尹勋]], and [[LiuYuAstrologerLateHan|刘瑜]] enter the political core as part of the court-repair hope around Chen Fan and Dou Wu. Hanji 892 then supplies the pre-crackdown reputation layer: even after dismissal, Li Ying remains respected by士人. Hanji 893 supplies the arrest-death layer. [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] frames party networks as possible rebellion to the young [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]], Li Ying refuses flight, enters prison, and dies under torture. His students and former subordinates are prohibited from office after his death.

## Key Characteristics

- Pardon-and-restoration beneficiary whose return follows Ying Feng's state-capacity argument to Emperor Huan.
- High-status law-enforcement figure who executes Zhang Rang's brother Zhang Shuo despite eunuch-family protection.
- Reputation gatekeeper whose reception creates a "登龙门" status marker among士人.
- Enforcement-trigger figure who later executes Zhang Cheng father and son despite an amnesty-gaming defense.
- First Party Prohibition prisoner whose case expands from criminal enforcement into a士人 accusation field.
- Reform-cohort figure whose entry into the 168 CE court center helps create expectations of political repair.
- Death-facing teacher and patron figure whose students and former subordinates are punished after his case.

## Qualifications

This page does not attempt a full Li Ying biography. Hanji 869 adds a bounded 165 CE pardon, enforcement, and reputation layer; Hanji 874 adds the later enforcement trigger and first arrest expansion; Hanji 875-876 preserve the reputation and rescue field; and Hanji 893 preserves the later death-facing branch. The source shows dramatic legal defense in the Zhang Shuo case, but it does not supply a complete Han legal analysis of Li Ying's execution decision. Transcript variants such as 李英 are normalized to 李膺.

## What Changed

- Hanji 869 moves Li Ying's bounded profile earlier than the Zhang Cheng case, into pardon, restored office, and Zhang Shuo enforcement.
- The page now explains "天下第一楷模" and "登龙门" as reputation built through visible enforcement against eunuch-connected power.
- Zhang Shuo and Ying Feng are added as distinct relationship nodes in Li Ying's earlier Emperor Huan-era branch.
- The later Zhang Cheng, Party Prohibition, reform restoration, prison death, and student-liability layers remain intact.
