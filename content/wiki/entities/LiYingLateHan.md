## Overview

Li Ying is a late-Han scholar-official and party-prohibition victim whose reputation now spans reform hope and later exposure. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-884-zhichang-chujian-yao-dui-shui-xian-kaidao-lvtc3uqlso242olkyin3fvz5a7ys|Hanji 884]] places him among renowned figures entering the court center under [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦妙 / 窦皇太后]], [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]], and [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]], while Hanji 892-893 show how that kind of public prestige becomes dangerous in the second party-prohibition disaster.

## Current Profile

The current evidence presents Li Ying as a public moral figure whose prestige first becomes reform capital and then exposure. Hanji 884 supplies the opening layer: Li Ying, [[DuMiLateHan|杜密]], [[YinXunLateHan|尹勋]], and [[LiuYuAstrologerLateHan|刘瑜]] enter the political core as part of the court-repair hope around Chen Fan and Dou Wu. Hanji 892 supplies the pre-crackdown layer: even after dismissal, Li Ying remains respected by士人, and the episode places him in the honor-list environment that turns fame into a warning sign. Hanji 893 then supplies the arrest layer: Chen Fan and Dou Wu had briefly restored Li Ying and other士人 to office, but their deaths let the eunuch side remove him again.

When [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] frames party networks as a possible rebellion to the young [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]], Li Ying refuses flight, enters prison, and dies under torture. His profile therefore combines moral authority, reputation-network danger, and teacher-network liability. Li Ying's students and former subordinates are prohibited from office after his death, and [[JingYiLateHan|景毅]] treats his son's connection to Li Ying as an honor serious enough to self-report rather than accept omission from the punishment list.

- Pre-crackdown honor-list figure whose name remains socially powerful even after dismissal.
- Reinstated under Chen Fan and Dou Wu before being removed again after their destruction.
- Death-facing official who refuses to flee and actively enters prison.
- Teacher and patron figure whose students and former subordinates are punished after his case.
- Reputation peer of [[FanPangLateHan|范滂]] and [[DuMiLateHan|杜密]] in the episode's account of名节.

## Qualifications

This page does not yet attempt a full Li Ying biography. The current sources preserve the political meaning of his reputation, death, and student network, while details of earlier offices, the first party-prohibition disaster, and the full accusation list remain outside the bounded evidence. Transcript variants such as 李英 are normalized to 李膺.

- Li Ying's public honor is now treated as a resource that first supports court-repair hope and later becomes target-legible before Cao Jie's formal treason framing.
