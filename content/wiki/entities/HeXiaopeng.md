# He Xiaopeng / 何小鹏

## Overview
He Xiaopeng is represented as the co-founder of [[UCWeb]] and founder, chair, and CEO of [[XPeng]], whose second major entrepreneurial arc moves from mobile software and financial freedom into vehicles, autonomy, robotics, chips, and [[PhysicalAI]].

## Current Profile
The evidence shows a founder whose strategy changed through contact with a harder physical system. UCWeb emerged from user behavior around an embedded browser, while XPeng forced He to replace software-style assumptions with manufacturing, quality, industrial-design, supply-chain, safety, and capital discipline. He later describes his own detached delegation as a failure: returning to frontline facts, changing first-level departments, and adding process checks became part of XPeng's recovery.

The later interview raises the technical stakes. He frames XPeng as a physical-AI company, stops a heavily funded [[StitchedAIArchitecture|lower-ceiling autonomy stack]], and joins vehicles, [[XPengIron]], [[XPengGX]], models, controls, chips, manufacturing, and globalization. The premium-brand source adds a commercial boundary: founder visibility and technical storytelling can help an upmarket move, but they do not erase product-line confusion or the low-price association created by [[XPengMONA03]].

## Key Characteristics
- Serial founder moving from mobile software and UCWeb into capital-intensive automotive and robotics systems.
- Product learner who treats user behavior and direct field facts as more reliable than fixed plans or filtered management reports.
- CEO willing to stop expensive technical or product directions before their correction cost grows further.
- Physical-AI strategist connecting autonomy, humanoids, chips, vehicles, data, controls, and manufacturing.
- Founder whose public technical narrative supports XPeng's premium ambitions while remaining constrained by product, quality, and brand execution.
- Source-scoped forecaster whose spending thresholds, autonomy timelines, consolidation outlook, and robot plans require independent evidence.

## Qualifications
The evidence consists mainly of founder interviews plus one investor-oriented brand analysis. Personal recollections, internal reorganization accounts, R&D thresholds, sales comparisons, product specifications, autonomous-driving timelines, robot and flying-car plans, and brand effects remain source-scoped. The sources illuminate He's operating model but do not provide a neutral biography or independent audit of XPeng's technology and performance.
