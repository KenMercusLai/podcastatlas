# Chen Zhi

Chen Zhi appears in [[for-bucks-sake-the-rise-of-self-made-billionaires-6a61e076562e3ad886d54c95]] as an alleged major scam boss connected to [[Cambodia]]. The source describes him as believed to lead one of Asia's most powerful criminal organizations, headquartered in Cambodia, and as an adviser to [[HunSen]].

The episode's significance is enforcement signaling. It says the [[UnitedStates]] and Britain sanctioned Chen, that he was extradited to [[China]], and that the United States seized around $15bn in cryptocurrency from him, described by the source as the largest seizure in U.S. history. In the wiki, Chen anchors the targeted-enforcement side of [[ScamCompoundPoliticalProtection]]: sanctions and extradition can frighten scam bosses, but the broader network persists if protection systems remain intact.
