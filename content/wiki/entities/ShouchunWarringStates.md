# 寿春 / Shouchun (Warring States)

寿春 / Shouchun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-107-zuihou-yici-hezong-faqin-fa-le-ge-jimo-lrzq1qks7z-oromd-5f-dgqjwjhw]] as the city to which [[ChuState|楚国]] moves its capital after the failed 241 BCE anti-[[QinState|秦国]] coalition. The episode says Chu calls the new capital Ying, preserving the old capital-name prestige while shifting the seat of rule.

Its significance is defensive geography. [[ZhuYingWarringStates|朱英]] warns [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] that if [[WeiState|魏国]] yields more central-plains points to Qin, Qin will be too close to Chu's old capital at Chen. Moving to Shouchun is therefore a late Chu attempt to buy distance from [[QinEastwardPressure|秦国东进压力]] after [[TerminalHezongFailure|末次合纵失败]].

Shouchun also sharpens the contrast between personal and state geography in Chunshenjun's late arc. Earlier the wiki recorded his move toward [[JiangdongWarringStates|江东]] as a safer fief choice; this source shows the whole Chu court facing a similar safety problem at capital scale.
