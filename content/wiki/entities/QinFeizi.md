# 非子 / Qin Feizi

非子 / Qin Feizi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] inside the source's [[QinState|秦国]] origin story. The episode says [[ZhouXiaowang|周孝王]] valued Feizi's skill with horses, assigned him land at Qin, and allowed him to continue the Ying family sacrifices.

The source stresses that this did not yet make Qin a full state. Feizi's Qin is still an attached fief or dependent status, which later makes [[QinXiangGong|秦襄公]]'s promotion by [[ZhouPingwang|周平王]] politically important.
