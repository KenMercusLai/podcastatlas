# Carlo Bonaparte

## Overview

Carlo Bonaparte was Napoleon's father, a Corsican notable whose movement from Paolist sympathy to accommodation with French rule secured noble recognition and educational access for his sons.

## Current Profile

The episode places Carlo inside the Bonaparte family's difficult adaptation to conquest. He admired [[PasqualePaoli|Pasquale Paoli]] and belonged to the Corsican political world displaced by France, but later worked within French rule. Recognition of the family's noble status allowed the young [[NapoleonBonaparte|Napoleon]] to leave [[Corsica]] for elite schooling and military training. Carlo's early death then increased Paoli's emotional importance as an imagined father figure for Napoleon.

## Key Characteristics

- Corsican notable initially associated with Paoli's political cause.
- Adapted to French rule after Paoli's defeat and exile.
- Used noble recognition as a practical route into French elite education.
- Helped direct Napoleon toward military schooling before dying during his son's youth.
