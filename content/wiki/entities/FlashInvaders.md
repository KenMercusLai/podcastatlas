# Flash Invaders

Flash Invaders appears in [[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]] as the app fans use to find and scan [[Invader]] mosaics. The source says players have logged more than 47 million flashes, turning scattered street artworks into a global scoring system.

In the wiki, Flash Invaders is a useful counterexample to phone-only attention. The app uses the phone as a scoring and recognition device, but the behavior it rewards is physical searching, looking up, walking streets, and noticing tile mosaics in public space.
