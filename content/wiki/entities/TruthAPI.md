# Truth API

Truth API is the paid data-access service launched by [[TruthSocial|Truth Social]] in [[fuzhuang-pinpai-a-f-xunzhao-zhongguo-hezuo-huoban-fufei-tiqian-kan-telangpu-tiewen-fuwu-shangxian-1004810677]]. The source says the service gives financial institutions and media groups millisecond-level access to important platform information, including posts from [[DonaldTrump]]'s account, with annual pricing as high as $1.2 million.

The product matters because it converts political posting speed into a sellable data service. In the wiki, it extends [[PolicyAnnouncementTradingRisk]] from unpredictable public statements into [[PoliticalInformationLatency]]: a platform can charge for earlier or cleaner access to posts that may affect markets, creating legal and fairness questions even when the information is public shortly afterward.

## Source Position
- Truth API is presented as a high-priced data feed, not as an ordinary social-media developer API.
- The source says several customers had signed up, but keeps the customer identities source-scoped.
- The episode notes that U.S. congressional Democrats asked regulators to examine whether the service is legal.
