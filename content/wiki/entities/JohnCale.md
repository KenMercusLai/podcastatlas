# John Cale

## Overview

John Cale is the musician whose 1991 interpretation of [[HallelujahSong|“Hallelujah”]] selected and recombined [[LeonardCohen]]'s evolving material into the principal bridge between Cohen's versions and [[JeffBuckley]]'s recording.

## Current Profile

After hearing Cohen perform a revised version, Cale asked for the lyrics and reportedly received extensive pages of material. He selected verses from different versions, changed wording and thematic balance, and recorded a sparse piano interpretation for *I'm Your Fan*. The episode credits this editorial and musical intervention with making a latent form more legible to later performers.

Cale's role complicates single-author accounts of creative completion. He did not merely reproduce a stable composition; he exercised selection, recombination, arrangement, and emphasis. The source nevertheless cannot establish that his choices alone caused the song's later recognition.

## Key Characteristics

- Encountered the song through Cohen's continued live revision rather than only the original recording.
- Selected from a larger body of supplied lyric material.
- Recombined verses and altered thematic emphasis.
- Created the sparse arrangement that Buckley later adapted.

### Transmission role

- [[encore-hallelujah-encore-hallelujah]] states that Buckley encountered and built from Cale's recording.
- [[hallelujah-hallelujah]] traces Buckley's version through the same intermediary recording.

## Qualifications

The supplied summaries do not include the lyric packet, session records, or a comparative score and recording analysis. The 2016 release and 2025 encore are overlapping versions of one episode rather than independent evidence. The claim that Cale “cracked the code” is an episode interpretation, while authorship, arrangement, reception, and influence involve contributions that cannot be reduced to one decisive intervention.

## What Changed

- Established Cale as an editor-arranger in the song's adaptation chain.
- Distinguished active recombination from a straightforward cover performance.
- Preserved causal caution around the claim that his version unlocked later success.
