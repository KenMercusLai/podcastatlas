# Karl Ludwig of Austria

## Overview

Karl Ludwig of Austria is [[FranzJosephI]]'s brother and the next succession step named after [[CrownPrinceRudolf]]'s death.

## Current Profile

The source uses Karl Ludwig as a succession bridge. He does not receive a full narrative role, but his position makes clear that Mayerling changes more than family grief: once Rudolf is gone, the imperial line has to move sideways before reaching [[FranzFerdinand]].

## Key Characteristics

- Karl Ludwig appears because Rudolf's death leaves no direct male heir for Franz Joseph.
- He represents collateral succession inside the [[HabsburgDynasty]].
- His importance is structural: he connects Mayerling to the later Franz Ferdinand branch.
