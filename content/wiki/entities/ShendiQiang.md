# 沈氐羌 / Shendi Qiang

## Overview

The Shendi Qiang are now visible in both the An-era and Huan-era Qiang-front record. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-792-dengkang-renu-taihou-dagou-weishenme-yao-kan-zhuren-lh3xkjneqfd9sqijqo3rgzsa5vwn|Hanji 792]] places them in the 120 CE Zhangye campaign cycle against [[MaXianLateHan|马贤]], while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-855-ni-you-duo-wuqing-jiu-you-duo-qiangda-lulbpnufi8xfijrviufo6biuy-h1|Hanji 855]] later names them in the Huan-era frontier crisis before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-856-lishi-gaosu-ni-ren-ruo-xinluan-zhenjiao-zi-luan-lo7m-9mz-pvstxtfgavrne4bszks|Hanji 856]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 792 supplies the earlier layer. In 120 CE, the Shendi Qiang attack Zhangye. Ma Xian leads more than ten thousand troops against them, kills and captures large numbers, and receives surrender. The same frontier field remains unstable because Jiwu attacks Jincheng while Ma is concentrated at Zhangye, and further Zhangye pressure follows from other Qiang groups after Ma returns.

Hanji 855 later names the Shendi Qiang alongside [[XianlingQiang|先零羌]] and other Qiang groups attacking Bingzhou and [[LiangzhouLateHan|凉州]], a crisis that exposes [[GuoHongLateHan|郭闳]]'s obstruction of [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]] and [[HuHongLateHan|胡闳]]'s weak replacement command.

The same source later names the Shendi Qiang again as the group attacking Zhangye and Jiuquan after [[HuangfuGuiLateHan|皇甫规]]'s initial victory and mass surrender. Their renewed action forces Huangfu Gui to mobilize surrendered Qiang groups toward Longyou, where the army finds its retreat blocked and epidemic conditions become severe.

## Key Characteristics

- Qiang group visible in a 120 CE An-era Zhangye campaign before later Huan-era frontier crisis notices.
- Initially appears with Xianling Qiang and other groups attacking Bingzhou and Liangzhou.
- Later attacks the Zhangye and Jiuquan branch of the Hexi frontier.
- Helps connect Duan Jiong's failed campaign, Hu Hong's incapacity, and Huangfu Gui's later intervention.
- Helps show that a Han tactical victory over one Qiang group does not settle wider mobile frontier pressure.
- Kept distinct from the broader [[XianlingQiang|先零羌]] and [[DongqiangLateHan|Eastern Qiang]] pages unless future sources justify merging.

- Huan-era attack field: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-855-ni-you-duo-wuqing-jiu-you-duo-qiangda-lulbpnufi8xfijrviufo6biuy-h1|Hanji 855]] names Shendi Qiang with Xianling Qiang and other groups attacking Bingzhou and Liangzhou.
- Renewed Hexi pressure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-855-ni-you-duo-wuqing-jiu-you-duo-qiangda-lulbpnufi8xfijrviufo6biuy-h1|Hanji 855]] says Shendi Qiang later attack Zhangye and Jiuquan.
- Huangfu Gui sequel: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-855-ni-you-duo-wuqing-jiu-you-duo-qiangda-lulbpnufi8xfijrviufo6biuy-h1|Hanji 855]] says Huangfu Gui mobilizes surrendered Qiang groups against the renewed Shendi Qiang crisis before his army is cut off and struck by disease.

## Qualifications

The current evidence does not provide an institutional history of the Shendi Qiang. The page records their source-bounded roles in the 120 CE Zhangye sequence and the later Huan-era Qiang-front sequence, while avoiding folding them into every later Qiang label in the wiki. Hanji 792 distinguishes the initial Shendi notice from later pressure by Dianjian and Shaodang/Shaohe groups.
