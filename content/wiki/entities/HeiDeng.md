# 黑灯 / Hei Deng

## Overview
黑灯 / Hei Deng is a comedian and podcaster who uses his experience of inherited visual impairment to explain [[StargardtDisease|Stargardt disease]], disability adaptation, urban barriers, and patient-led information work.

## Current Profile
In the current source, 黑灯 says an eye disorder was diagnosed around age thirteen and became substantially more limiting in early adulthood. He connects central-vision loss to slower reading, difficulty recognizing faces and vehicles, constrained employment, concealment and anxiety, then describes learned workarounds including very high-speed audio listening.

His public role joins comedy, podcast conversation, accessibility critique, and rare-disease organizing. He argues that comedy must preserve emotional truth even when literal reality sounds implausibly absurd, and that disability access should begin with the user's destination and complete journey rather than the presence of a nominal facility. He also helps operate [[JuvenileMacularDegenerationCareCenter|青少年黄斑变性关爱中心]].

## Key Characteristics
- Translates inherited visual loss into concrete effects on reading, recognition, work, mobility, and anxiety.
- Treats high-speed listening and other workarounds as learned adaptation to information need.
- Uses comedy to communicate structural barriers without reducing disability to inspiration or tragedy.
- Critiques accessibility facilities by whether they complete a real user task.
- Helps organize patient-facing information for a rare inherited retinal disease.
