# 起朱楼宴宾客 / Qizhulou Yan Binke

起朱楼宴宾客 is the podcast show represented in [[vol-113-cong-jiqian-ye-zhiku-wenjian-zhong-goule-telangpu-2-0-zhizheng-jihua-beihou-de-ren-jigou-sixiang-he-mailuo-lihnknc-3om5evexocb6a6zdwueu]]. In this source, the show uses a long-form reading-notes format to connect [[DonaldTrump]]'s second-term agenda with [[Project2025]], [[HeritageFoundation]], [[AmericaFirstPolicyInstitute]], [[ConservativePartnershipInstitute]], and older American trade-policy traditions.

The episode is explicitly cautious about expertise. It presents the discussion as a synthesis of policy files, articles, and books rather than as direct U.S. political reporting, and asks listeners to read the nationalist and populist claims critically.

[[vol-101-ji-anquan-shouyi-you-gao-liudongxing-hai-hao-de-touzi-daodi-cunzai-ma-lr7xt-2cgoru2gfdbatvbiv78ixg]] adds the show's personal-finance and asset-allocation side. The episode uses [[YueBao|余额宝]], household liquidity, product lockups, diversification, and scarcity to explain [[InvestmentImpossibleTriangle]], [[InvestmentLiquidityTradeoff]], [[AssetScarcityPremium]], and portfolio-level [[AssetAllocation]] for ordinary investors.

[[vol-102-aoguo-jiuye-bingheqi-de-riben-nianqingren-qu-nali-xunzhao-xingfugan-chuantai-qingdao-kuaima-lmpluij7rcfq49cxcyb65x3f7zfn]] adds the show's youth, confidence, and social-trust side through a crossover with [[QingdaoKuaima|轻刀快马]]. [[FuYu|傅宇]] connects Japanese inflation, [[NISA]], [[EmploymentIceAgeGeneration|employment ice age]] pressure, [[HouseholdBalanceSheetRepair]], and Chinese youth surveys to [[YouthHappinessAfterGrowth]], [[SocialTrustAndHappiness]], and [[HikikomoriStrategicRetreat]].

[[vol-103-wenyi-fuxing-keji-ximengsi-de-fengshen-zhilu-shi-lianghua-zhiwang-gengshi-dongcha-renxing-de-dashi-lulzvnaxdb4klqag-p2yyqlmiikl]] adds the show's financial-personage and quant-history side. It uses [[JimSimons]], [[RenaissanceTechnologies]], and the [[MedallionFund]] to connect [[QuantitativeInvesting]], [[QuantitativeDataMoat]], [[ShortTermStatisticalArbitrage]], [[AlphaDecay]], and [[HumanRiskOverride]] to a broader claim about organization, talent, and risk.

[[vol-104-putongren-ganggu-wanquan-shengcun-zhinan-chuantai-sandian-xiaban-lmzivgi6d3guv7wajyjw9cv9zz90]] adds the show's Hong Kong retail-investing survival side through a crossover with [[SanDianXiaban|三点下班]]. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] and [[Haoge|浩哥]] use personal wins, losses, AH discounts, dividend assets, IPO attention paths, and old-thousand-stock warnings to connect ordinary-investor behavior to [[HongKongMarketStructure]].

[[vol-105-ruhe-panduan-yige-touzi-zuhe-shifou-shihe-ziji-lmaowq8820pa0jyjw6z93b6hstpe]] continues the asset-allocation series by asking how ordinary investors can tell whether a portfolio fits them. It adds [[PortfolioSuitability]], [[AdaptivePortfolioDesign]], and [[InvestmentCooldownDiscipline]] while using [[HongseHuojianIndexTool|红色火箭]], [[ChinaAMC|华夏基金]], [[ShiLei|时雷]], and [[ShifenXiyin|十分吸引]] as source-scoped references around index tools and more technical allocation thinking.

[[vol-106-dibai-zhende-biandi-shi-huangjin-lpxchutxf7yomjdfykwzvvnmbaq8]] adds the show's overseas-opportunity and city-fieldwork side. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] interviews [[Ricky]] about [[Dubai]], [[AbuDhabi|Abu Dhabi]], [[DubaiBusinessHubModel]], [[DubaiGoldenVisaResidency]], [[DubaiRealEstateMarket]], and the need to separate opportunity excitement from due diligence.

[[vol-107-85hou-tiwen-95hou-ruguo-shiqing-bu-xuyao-zuo-xuanze-na-wo-jiu-zhuang-shiti-pangguan-llvntaqucnilovj19zoywrfdwzsa]] adds a generational-dialogue branch. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] asks [[SiriQizhulou|Siri]] how a 95后 thinks about "尸体" passivity, present-choice attention, work-life separation, controllable life supports, finance careers, and romance or childbirth as optional choices.

[[vol-108-riben-wuda-zonghe-shangshe-zhongfan-wutai-zhongyang-lhamp3hn14iihcicpa8po5dvh6gq]] adds the show's Japan-business-history and global-trade branch. It uses [[JapaneseSogoShosha|Japanese sogo shosha]], [[MitsubishiCorporation]], [[MitsuiAndCo]], [[Itochu]], [[SumitomoCorporation]], [[Marubeni]], and [[KeiretsuBusinessGroups|keiretsu]] to explain why trading companies can function as commercial infrastructure, and why [[DeglobalizationTradeIntermediation]] may make intermediaries more valuable again.

[[vol-109-fofpai-vs-zhishupai-guanyu-geren-yanglaojin-zhanghu-gai-pei-shenme-de-yichang-bianlun-lmwscl4ppaxveccgtvp5oi-ypv2x]] returns to the show's ordinary-investor asset-allocation branch through a debate about [[PersonalPensionAccount|个人养老金账户]] products. [[JiangHuaan|蒋华安]] helps move the question from "FOF派 vs 指数派" to pension-account suitability: index funds bring low fees and transparency, while [[FOFProductDesign|FOF]] and [[TargetDateFund|目标日期基金]] can provide allocation, glide-path, and holding-discipline support when investors need more than a single beta exposure.

[[vol-110-touzi-jiushi-dui-shijieguan-de-toupiao-maichu-zichan-peizhi-di-yi-bu-wanjiepian-lk5jkgnzvzerymcixylsdqdtn7f8]] closes the show's "迈出资产配置第一步" sequence by naming [[InvestmentWorldviewFit]] as the deeper reason allocation, stock picking, trading, cash, real estate, and crypto feel suitable or unsuitable to different people. The episode connects [[PaulSlovic]], [[DanielKahneman]], [[RayDalio]], [[JimSimons]], [[WarrenBuffett]], [[Bitcoin]], [[KenFollett]], and [[TheCenturyTrilogy]] to a practical conclusion: diversified [[AssetAllocation]] is a worldview-compatible tool for the host, but no investment school is mandatory for everyone.

[[173-dang-mianhuai-gaoshanwen-boshi-shi-women-jiujing-zai-huainian-shenme-lruwsn-me-sheuddobz13rmhwhfe]] adds the show's macro-research and public-expression branch through [[GaoShanwen|高善文]]. The episode treats Gao's memorial as a way to connect [[AssetRevaluationTheory]], [[BalanceSheetMacroAnalysis]], [[CapitalReturnRateDecline]], [[SellSideResearchIncentives]], and [[MacroResearchPublicExpression]]: finance research can be market-facing, policy-facing, and publicly valuable only when its data, logic, and uncomfortable claims remain discussable.

[[vol-112-yici-feigongshi-de-2024-fansi-he-2025-zhanwang-duihua-lanxiaokang-x-mouyiling-lkasjtodqgmnbpyambqoxl-fcsyv]] adds the show's non-consensus China-asset branch through [[LanXiaokang|蓝小康]] and [[MouYiling|牟一凌]]. The episode connects 2024 market style swings, [[DonaldTrump]]'s return, [[ChinaSupplySideClearing]], [[LaborShareConsumptionRebalancing]], [[RMBExchangeRatePolicy]], [[StateOwnedEnterpriseSocialValue]], [[BeltAndRoadExternalDemand]], and [[ActiveManagementStyleEvolution]] into [[NewOrderAssetPricing]].

[[vol-111-guanyu-2025-nian-de-si-ge-caixiang-lgaira5qw1fhic4qwihrimed6q9g]] adds the show's solo annual-prediction branch for 2025. The episode connects [[ChinaLocalDebtResolution]], [[InternalStabilityConfidenceRepair]], Trump 2.0's domestic bureaucratic focus, [[ChinaCorporateAntiInvolution]], and [[ChinaDivergentTechnologyRoute]] under the closing phrase "殊途同归".

[[vol-114-ai-de-2025-he-deepseek-men-de-weilai-duitan-fudan-zhangqi-jiaoshou-lhvhnvqtvuv4ln-cckcpedgldolo]] adds the show's academic AI annual-review branch through [[ZhangQi|张奇]] of [[FudanUniversity|复旦大学]]. The episode uses [[DeepSeek]]'s breakout to discuss current large-model limits, [[ModelPostTrainingBottleneck]], [[ScenarioSpecificAI]], [[ContactCenterAI]], AI search, and why [[AgenticWorkflow]] becomes more credible when models can reflect, revise, and recover.

[[vol-115-quanqiu-hongguan-he-ziben-shichang-2025-zhanwang-duanqi-wenti-bu-jiejue-jiu-meiyou-zhongqi-he-changqi-le-lrfl2e5nuf7-ubq-shybqytjhuhx]] returns to the show's macro and capital-market outlook format with [[Ricky]]. It connects [[ChinaPolicyEasingPivot]], [[ShortTermDemandBeforeLongTermReform]], [[ChinaFiscalExpansionChannels]], [[AHShare2025Barbell]], [[BondFundReturnExpectationReset]], and [[USMegaCapTechRightSideTrade]] into a source-dated 2025 allocation map.

[[vol-117-shengwu-yiyao-de-2025-chaodi-zhongguo-yanfa-jiaolv-he-xinwang-jiwei-lmhral0rmq6tohiqdwsgmfapnyn7]] adds the show's biotech and innovation-drug branch. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] and [[XiaoPTeacher|小P老师]] review 2024-2025 biopharma through [[ChinaBiotechAssetRepricing]], [[BiotechLicenseOutArbitrage]], [[ReverseNewCoBiotechModel]], [[MultinationalPharmaPatentCliffAnxiety]], [[ADCEngineeringOptimization]], [[GLP1Agonists]], and [[ChinaBiotechGlobalization]], while keeping company and technology discussion separate from investment advice.

[[vol-118-danshen-renshi-yanglao-zhinan-bushi-gudu-de-daimingci-shi-gengzao-zhangwo-rensheng-de-zhudongquan-lhvkuveznxd4citlwf5marzaj-wt]] adds the show's single-aging and later-life planning branch through a crossover with [[ZhiqingXiaojiuguan|知青小酒馆]]. The episode connects the show's personal-finance instincts to [[SoloAgingPlanning]], arguing that retirement security is not only a product or portfolio problem but also a residence, care, health, relationship, emotion, and death-preparation system.

[[vol-119-quanshang-yanjiu-baogao-hai-zhide-du-ma-ljpctapo-auonrn4tlqzvzv66qv3]] opens the show's "金融行业是如何运作的" sequence with a sell-side research explanation. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] treats [[BrokerageResearchReports|brokerage research reports]] as public artifacts of a buy-side service business, then connects report-reading value to [[SellSideResearchIncentives]], compliance, analyst career paths, report type, [[MacroResearchPublicExpression]], [[PassiveInvesting]], and [[ResearchReportReadingDiscipline]].

[[vol-120-riben-yiliao-tixi-de-bengkui-yu-zhongsheng-yiyuan-yaopin-yibao-sanjiao-kunju-de-yizhong-jiefa-lp-p5p1cybbldphe0qajteggsitt]] adds the show's Japan healthcare-system branch. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] uses [[JapaneseHealthcareSystem]], [[JapaneseMedicalCollapse]], [[HighCostMedicalExpenseBenefit]], [[JapaneseMedicalTriageSystem]], [[CommunityIntegratedCare]], [[DPCDRGPaymentReform]], [[JapaneseDrugPricingReform]], [[GenericDrugTrustRebuilding]], and [[PharmacistAsSecondDoctor]] to explain why hospital access, drug pricing, insurance caps, and aging care have to be analyzed together.

[[vol-121-cong-tanhua-yixian-de-fenji-jijin-dao-fengtou-zhengjing-de-ganggan-etf-yongyuan-buyao-digu-renxing-de-fengkuang-lusagcitdozvzm8wausvecvi-qmb]] adds the show's leveraged-product risk branch. The episode connects Chinese [[ChineseStructuredFund|structured funds]], [[StructuredFundDownwardConversion|downward conversion]], and 2014-2015 B-share mania with overseas [[LeveragedETF|leveraged ETFs]], [[FNGU]]-style ETNs, [[DailyLeverageReset]], [[VolatilityDecay]], [[FuturesRollCost]], and [[LeveragedProductSuitability]].

[[vol-123-telangpu-de-duideng-guanshui-an-buzhi-shi-yi-chang-daxing-fucongxing-ceshi-lm6bkiqgyyefwompxon7lo6cnqp6]] adds the show's April 2025 tariff-shock branch. The episode links [[DonaldTrump]]'s [[ReciprocalTariffFormula]] to [[TariffComplianceTest]], [[ChinaPolicyEasingPivot]], [[RMBExchangeRatePolicy]], [[GlobalizationTaxOptimization]], [[MarketRegimeShift]], and [[PatrimonialGovernance]], turning a current-events tariff story into a broader macro, tax, alliance, and governance analysis.
