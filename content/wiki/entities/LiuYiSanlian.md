# 刘仪 / Liu Yi

刘仪 / Liu Yi is the international-news guest in [[ep251-yilang-kunju-sishinian-zhanlue-xuanze-de-daijia-yu-xianshi-lp8vyonyugtd2ozymgof-mim9zkd]], where he explains the renewed [[UnitedStates]]-[[Israel]]-[[Iran]] war for [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]]. The episode presents him as a geopolitical analyst focused on how Iranian nuclear bargaining, missiles, the [[IslamicRevolutionaryGuardCorps]], proxy networks, oil routes, and Gulf confidence interact.

His strongest contribution is the distinction between tactical leverage and strategic health. Liu Yi argues that [[Iran]] repeatedly found clever leverage points, from nuclear bargaining to regional proxies and missile or drone threats, but those tools eventually produced [[ResistanceAxisBackfire]], [[IranHorizontalEscalation]], fiscal pressure, and legitimacy traps.

He also provides the episode's postwar political-economy diagnosis. If talks resume, he expects Iran to seek security and fiscal guarantees, but he is skeptical that compensation or relief would become reform-and-opening capital; in his reading, it would more likely serve [[RegimeBasicBaseStabilization]] around people dependent on the state machine.
