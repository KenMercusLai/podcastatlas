# 天真不天真

## Overview
天真不天真 is a conversation-led podcast represented through reflective interviews with [[SongJia|宋佳 / 小花老师]], [[XieyinMelody|携隐 Melody]], [[MiziCrimePodcaster|咪仔]], and [[LiRonghao|李荣浩]]. The sources connect work identity, relationships, long-form media, creator economics, emotional practice, artistic craft, and self-knowledge.

## Current Profile
The show treats conversation method as part of its subject. With Song Jia it moves through career uncertainty, speech intention, jealousy, relationship needs, apology, travel, and inward attention. With Melody it argues that long-form voice can preserve thought, relationship, and human presence even when short video is commercially faster. With Mizi it links a creator's full career history to mission recognition, paid-content ethics, team economics, grief, vulnerability, and practiced emotion regulation.

The Li Ronghao conversation adds sustained craft observation. Concrete experience, arrangement, mixing, long practice, delayed reception, concerts, solitary attention, and AI music are connected without forcing them into one productivity formula. Across all four sources, [[YangTianzhen|杨天真 / 天真]] brings operational questions about formats, conversion, organizations, tools, and public learning into conversations that tolerate uncertainty, detailed personal evidence, and revision.

## Key Characteristics
- The show uses guests' career histories and current transitions as entry points into broader questions of identity, purpose, and craft.
- Its conversations prioritize process, mutual recognition, and concrete personal examples over systematic expert instruction.
- It treats self-knowledge, relationships, emotional practice, creative work, and work design as intertwined.
- It presents podcasting as comparatively complete long-form exchange with durable relational value.
- It combines business and technology analysis with boundaries around manipulation, credibility, audience trust, and personal judgment.
- It allows specialist detail to remain intelligible through analogy without pretending that metaphor replaces practice.
