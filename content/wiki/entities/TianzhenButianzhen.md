# 天真不天真

## Overview
天真不天真 is a podcast represented in the wiki through a return conversation with [[SongJia|宋佳 / 小花老师]] on work pauses, self-knowledge, relationships, acting, travel, and podcast method.

## Current Profile
The available source presents 天真不天真 as a conversation-led show rather than a formal interview program. The host begins from a previous high-performing guest episode, then lets the return conversation move through career uncertainty, manifestation language, speech intention, jealousy, inward attention, relationship needs, apology, travel, study abroad, and future English-language podcast ambitions.

In this episode, the show matters because its method matches its subject. The host explicitly describes podcasting as a medium that can preserve a complete conversational process with relatively little editing, and she distinguishes her work from deep interview formats. That makes the episode itself a case for [[PodcastAuthenticityBoundary]]: its authority comes from visible process, pauses, shifts, mutual correction, and source-scoped personal experience rather than a tightly argued doctrine.

## Key Characteristics
- The show uses returning guests and prior audience response as entry points into updated life states.
- Its conversational format prioritizes process, mutual recognition, and personal examples over systematic expert instruction.
- The show treats self-knowledge, relationships, and work identity as intertwined rather than separate advice categories.
- It presents podcasting as a medium of comparatively complete, lightly edited, real-time exchange.
- The available source links the show's future direction to cross-language conversation and the host's desire to interview people who do not speak Chinese.
