## Overview

条支国 / Tiaozhi Kingdom enters the wiki as an additional far-western destination named in [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]]'s mission for [[GanYingEasternHan|Gan Ying]]. The current page keeps it source-scoped because Hanji 771 names it briefly and glosses it only broadly as part of a Middle Eastern route field.

## Current Profile

Hanji 771 says Ban Chao sends Gan Ying toward [[DaQin|Da Qin]] and Tiaozhi. The episode's substantive narrative follows Gan Ying to [[ParthianEmpire|Parthia / Anxi]] and then focuses on the blocked sea route toward the [[RomanEmpire|Roman Empire]], so Tiaozhi functions as a route marker rather than an independently developed polity.

The page is still useful because it prevents the Tiaozhi notice from being folded into Da Qin or Parthia. In the current evidence, Tiaozhi marks the breadth of the mission's western horizon while remaining under-described.

## Key Characteristics

- Far-western polity or destination named alongside Da Qin in Hanji 771.
- Part of Gan Ying's mission horizon under Ban Chao.
