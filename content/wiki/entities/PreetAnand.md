# Preet Anand

Preet Anand is the founder of [[SnugSafety]] and a caller in [[advice-line-with-chris-riccobono-of-untuckit-c087247f-1f85-4c15-b4aa-4ffea0368c4c]]. The source says Snug began in 2017 after a customer named Jan described the need for help if she could not respond after a seizure-like event.

Preet's Advice Line question is how to grow awareness for a trust-heavy senior-safety product without a large marketing budget. [[ChrisRiccobono]] and [[GuyRaz]] advise him to test channels separately, clarify whether the buyer is the senior or an adult child, and rely on media or intermediaries that already carry trust.
