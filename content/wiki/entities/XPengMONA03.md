# XPeng MONA 03 / 小鹏 MONA 03

XPeng MONA 03 / 小鹏 MONA 03 is the low-price volume vehicle discussed in [[143-wei-xiao-li-de-gaoduan-tansuo-feng-tou-quan-1-143-1]]. The source says it helped [[XPeng]] reach buyers outside the company's older base, especially younger women with roughly 100,000 to 150,000 RMB budgets, through lower price, design, and [[SmartDrivingDemocratization]].

The episode treats MONA 03 as both success and risk. It can solve volume and expand the intelligent-driving audience, but if roughly half of company sales come from the cheapest model, [[LowPriceBrandPerception]] may make [[XPengGX]] and other high-end attempts harder to explain. That makes MONA 03 a concrete case for [[EVProductLineClarity]].
