# 尤妈妈 / 猫猫

尤妈妈 / 猫猫 is the guest in [[132-dang-guodu-sikao-de-dagongren-yu-shang-di-yuwang-de-shidai-luaqswjqezbakxdrcshifxi0ermo]], where [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] asks how working in [[Japan]] changed her view of workplace pressure after earlier conversations about mid-career work problems. Her source-scoped role is to make the topic concrete: Japanese overtime approval, off-work boundaries, indirect conflict, AI-startup exhaustion, low desire after relocation, and the search for life practices that fit one's own temperament.

The episode uses her experience to connect [[RuleBoundOverthinking]] with daily work. She describes a setting where rules and manuals reduce some decision pressure, while hidden hierarchy, passive-aggressive communication, and fear of violating context can still intensify rumination.

She also gives the low-desire branch a practical shape. Instead of treating low desire as pure nihilism, she distinguishes material wanting from continued curiosity about language learning, gardening, running, cooking, relationships, and self-recognition, linking the episode to [[LowDesireDefensiveContraction]], [[ControllableLifeAnchors]], and [[MicroHappiness]].
