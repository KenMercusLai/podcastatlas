# Jaak Panksepp

## Overview
Jaak Panksepp appears in the bounded source as a pioneering researcher on the biology and psychology of play, especially animal play and laughter-like vocalization.

## Current Profile
[[using-play-to-rewire-improve-your-brain-scim9321743392|The play episode]] credits Panksepp with helping establish play as a biologically regulated behavior rather than optional amusement. It associates his work with tickling experiments in rodents, ultrasonic vocalizations interpreted as laughter-like, and the study of neural and chemical systems involved in play. The source uses that research as part of a broader account of low-stakes exploration, but it does not provide a full biography, publication record, or direct evaluation of individual studies.
