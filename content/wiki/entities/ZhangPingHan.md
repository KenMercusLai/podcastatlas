# 张平 / Zhang Ping

张平 / Zhang Ping appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-26-mengzi-laile-ln4twwp2frmr-xpumu0rqg-2gpyn]] as [[ZhangKaidi|张开地]]'s son and [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s father. The episode says he continues to serve [[HanState|韩国]] as chief minister, extending the family's binding to Han after Shen Buhai's generation.

The page uses `ZhangPingHan` as the filename to avoid confusing this Warring States figure with other possible Zhang Ping pages. Its source role is to make Zhang Liang's later political identity legible: Han restoration is not an abstract slogan, but the remembered cause of a family that had served Han rulers for generations.
