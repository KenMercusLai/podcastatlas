# Lebanon

[[hard-cedar-lebanon-seeks-trumps-help-6a5f41e97bddc0bea5542de5]] adds the domestic state-capacity version of the Lebanon problem. [[GregCarlstrom]] says [[JosephAoun]] and [[NawafSalam]] have a chance to rebuild state authority after years of leadership vacuum, but their strategy depends on army funding, pressure on [[Israel]] to withdraw, reconstruction money, and an [[InternationalMonetaryFund|IMF]] deal.

The episode makes Lebanon a [[StateCredibilityAgainstMilitia]] case. The government wants to show Shia citizens that [[Hezbollah]] brought war on [[Iran]]'s behalf while the state can secure and rebuild the south, but [[AmerBisat]]'s damage estimate and the unresolved banking losses show why security credibility and financial reform cannot be separated.

Lebanon appears in [[missing-peace-will-israel-imperil-iran-deal-6a3bae9fe1238e54b0e886c3]] as the location where a ceasefire clause could weaken the broader America-[[Iran]] deal. The episode says [[Israel]] doubts that the Lebanese government can disarm [[Hezbollah]] or stop it from rebuilding military capacity in the south.

That earlier source's Lebanon segment is about enforcement, not just territory. If a formal ceasefire does not change what Israel believes Hezbollah can do near the border, Lebanon remains a trigger point for renewed war even while outside powers pursue a larger diplomatic bargain.
