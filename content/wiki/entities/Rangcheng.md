# 穰城 / Rangcheng

穰城 / Rangcheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1010-sanguo-liubiao-jiujing-shibushi-piaoliang-de-caobao-lo58hzfo-qv0soauogtgfrkourrd|Hanji 1010]] as the [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]] city attacked by [[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]] after he brings troops from Guanzhong into [[Jingzhou|荆州]]. Zhang Ji dies during the fighting, and his death gives [[LiuBiao|刘表]] the opening to refuse celebration, mourn the dead commander, and absorb the remaining troops through [[MourningFramedMilitaryAbsorption|丧礼化收编]].

The place also sits behind the Wancheng branch. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1012-haose-caocao-luanshi-zhong-de-dute-qingshi-llvrucihuv2buvp1-lisz4usbprh|Hanji 1012]] uses Zhang Ji's death at Rangcheng as the reason [[ZhangXiu|张绣]] inherits the army before the later [[WanCounty|宛县]] surrender to [[CaoCao|曹操]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] then says Zhang Xiu retreats toward Rangcheng and reconnects with Liu Biao after the failed Wancheng attack sequence.
