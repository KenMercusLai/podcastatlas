## Overview

萧峰 / Xiao Feng is a central character in [[TianLongBaBu|《天龙八部》]] who enters this bounded character study as 阿朱's bereaved lover, [[Azi|阿紫]]'s protector, and the unavailable object of her possessive attachment.

## Current Profile

The episode treats Xiao Feng's grief after accidentally killing 阿朱 as the scene through which A Zi first recognizes both his emotional depth and the security represented by his strength. He accepts responsibility for A Zi because she is A Zhu's sister and needs protection, but care never becomes romantic consent.

This boundary remains explicit. A Zi invokes A Zhu's death, demands continued companionship, and seeks the love attached to her sister's place, while Xiao Feng repeatedly affirms that A Zhu is the person he loves. His suicide at Yanmen Pass therefore cannot be read as fulfillment of A Zi's romance; her leap with his body imposes a final physical union he did not choose.

## Key Characteristics

- Carries profound grief and responsibility after A Zhu's death.
- Provides A Zi with physical protection and a form of family security.
- Distinguishes care for a dependent relative from romantic reciprocity.
- Remains emotionally committed to A Zhu despite A Zi's persistence and sacrifice.
- Becomes the object onto which A Zi projects strength, safety, rivalry, and possession.

## Qualifications

This is a deliberately narrow, one-source profile of Xiao Feng inside an A Zi-centered episode. It does not yet synthesize his full biography, ethnic identity, leadership, martial career, political tragedy, relationship with A Zhu, or reasons for suicide. Claims about what attracts A Zi and what his care means remain literary interpretations.
