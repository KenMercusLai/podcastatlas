# METR

## Overview

METR is an AI evaluation and safety research organization represented in the wiki through reported studies of developer productivity and AI-assisted model research.

## Current Profile

The two sources place METR at different levels of the AI-development stack. [[ep108-vibe-coding-da-dizhen-cursor-dingjia-zhengyi-windsurf-shougou-fengbo-moxing-changshang-qin-erzi-men-you-jiang-ruhe-jinchang-lqn-icq1xqgk7xxxxzrpunj4fan]] reports a study in which experienced developers working in familiar repositories expected AI speed gains but took longer after conversation, waiting, and review overhead were included. [[tech-20261124-1124-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261124-1124-mp-tech-pod-128]] cites a different METR report as estimating that greater AI use in research and development may explain about 80% of a reported acceleration in selected [[Anthropic]] capabilities.

Together, the sources make METR a measurement boundary rather than a simple pro- or anti-AI authority: AI can add overhead in one bounded coding setting while still accelerating parts of model research. Neither podcast source supplies the underlying methods needed to reconcile populations, tasks, time horizons, or causal attribution.

## Key Characteristics

- Evaluates practical AI performance rather than relying only on model-provider capability claims.
- Measures effects that can differ across developers, tasks, repositories, and research settings.
- Makes time-to-completion and development acceleration visible as separate questions.
- Supplies evidence used in debates about [[AICodingVerification]] and [[RecursiveSelfImprovement]].
- Requires method-level context before reported percentages can be generalized.

## Evidence

### Developer productivity and review overhead

- [[ep108-vibe-coding-da-dizhen-cursor-dingjia-zhengyi-windsurf-shougou-fengbo-moxing-changshang-qin-erzi-men-you-jiang-ruhe-jinchang-lqn-icq1xqgk7xxxxzrpunj4fan]] reports a METR study in which experienced developers on familiar repositories took longer with AI despite expecting speed gains.

### AI-assisted research acceleration

- [[tech-20261124-1124-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261124-1124-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports a METR estimate linking increased AI research-and-development use to much of a selected Anthropic capability acceleration.

## Qualifications

Both records are podcast summaries rather than the METR studies themselves. The wiki therefore does not infer that AI coding is generally slower, that 80% is a universal causal estimate, or that research acceleration proves direct successor-building RSI. The two findings concern different tasks and measurement frames and are not treated as contradictory.

## What Changed

- Expanded METR from one developer-productivity result to a broader AI-development evaluation profile.
- Added the reported research-acceleration estimate while keeping its causal and methodological limits explicit.
- Distinguished task-level coding overhead from model-research acceleration.
