# Euan Roger

## Overview

Euan Roger is the [[NationalArchivesUK|National Archives]] historian who guides the medieval and Tudor half of *Treason: People, Power and Plot* in episode 248 of [[TheRestIsHistory|The Rest Is History]].

## Current Profile

Roger interprets treason through surviving legal objects and the human cases attached to them. He treats the [[TreasonAct1351|Treason Act 1351/1352]] as a durable baseline, explains how [[ParliamentaryAttainder]] and punishment extended a conviction into property and family, and uses cases from Dafydd ap Gruffydd to [[RichardRoose]] and Guy Fawkes to show doctrine changing under political pressure.

## Key Characteristics

- Archival interpreter of medieval and Tudor treason law.
- Guide to *Treason: People, Power and Plot*.
- Connects statute rolls and prosecution records to changing political authority.
- Distinguishes the 1351 legal date from the 1352 archival dating convention.
- Emphasizes both continuity in the core statute and instability in its application.

## Evidence

- Exhibition role: [[248-medieval-treason-part-1-glt9532907553]] presents Roger guiding the hosts through the National Archives exhibition.
- Legal interpretation: [[248-medieval-treason-part-1-glt9532907553]] attributes to him the dating explanation, the statute's core clauses, and the repeal of most Henrician additions.
- Case method: [[248-medieval-treason-part-1-glt9532907553]] follows his use of documents concerning punishment, magic, attainder, poisoning, religious conflict, and the Gunpowder Plot.
