# Red Star Macalline / 美凯龙

Red Star Macalline / 美凯龙 appears in [[165-nianbaoji-zhong-de-zhenshi-zhongguo-2026-lpredevu-gakn92dwutmulytmslo]] as a home-furnishing mall and commercial-property stress case. The episode links its 2025 loss to weaker home-furnishing and building-material demand, lower rent and management income, and large fair-value losses on investment property.

In wiki terms, Red Star Macalline shows how [[ChinaRealEstateDebtCycle]] spreads beyond developers. When home sales, renovation demand, retail footfall, rental income, and property marks fall together, an operating downturn can become a balance-sheet downturn, reinforcing [[ChinaIndustrialKShapedDivergence]].
