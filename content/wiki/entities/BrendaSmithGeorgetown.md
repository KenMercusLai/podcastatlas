# Brenda Smith (Georgetown fundraiser)

## Overview

Brenda Smith was a senior Georgetown athletics fundraiser whose subpoenaed testimony became the first major turning point in [[USVKhoury|U.S. v. Khoury]].

## Current Profile

Smith initially denied lobbying admissions or sharing prospective recruits' family wealth. Defense emails then showed her discussing property values, donor capacity, and affluent families with coaches, weakening Georgetown's claimed separation between development work and admissions-related athletic recruitment.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior fundraiser working around Georgetown athletics.
- Appeared as a defense witness under subpoena.
- Initially denied several links among fundraising, coaches, wealth information, and admissions.
- Exchanged emails about prospects' homes, family resources, and donation potential.
- Became central to the defense's institutional-hypocrisy argument.
