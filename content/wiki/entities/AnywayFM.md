# Anyway.FM 设计杂谈

## Overview
Anyway.FM 设计杂谈 is the design podcast context for [[anyway-195-anyway-195]] and [[anyway-194-anyway-194]], two episodes that connect designer work identity, AI pressure, independent toolmaking, and exits from purely screen-centered practice.

## Current Profile
Across the bounded sources, Anyway.FM functions as a design-media venue that can connect long-running UI/UX work conversations with broader life and industry transitions. The UX Coffee crossover uses a bookstore project to ask how designers and tech workers find outlets as AI and industry cycles change their assumptions. The MacRabbit episode uses independent Mac app memory and current AI coding tools to ask how designers can regain the ability and desire to build small software products.

The page remains source-bounded. It does not reconstruct the show's full history, host roster, publishing cadence, or earlier design-theory archive.
