# William Golding / 威廉·戈尔丁

## Overview
William Golding / 威廉·戈尔丁 appears in [[vol-351-yingwang-21shiji-de-huangdao-gushi-haineng-zenme-kai-naodong-1983-nuojiang-dezhu-gkwriueodap1a3taaattkgol]] as the author of [[LordOfTheFlies|《蝇王》 / Lord of the Flies]].

## Current Profile
The episode's profile of Golding centers on two experiences: teaching children and serving in World War II. Those backgrounds make his island story less a fantasy of innocent youth and more a compressed judgment on how quickly rule, trust, and self-knowledge can fail under fear. The source also notes the novel's difficult path to publication and Golding's later Nobel recognition.

## Key Characteristics
- He is presented as a British writer whose most important wiki role here is author of [[LordOfTheFlies]].
- His teaching background supports the episode's confidence in the children's group dynamics.
- His wartime naval experience supports the novel's severe view of civilization and violence.
- The source treats his work as classic because it keeps illuminating the condition of the modern world.
- The quoted "evil comes from human beings" frame makes him a source for human-nature pessimism, but the episode does not reduce him to a slogan.
