# 昭雎 / Zhao Ju

昭雎 / Zhao Ju appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-62-ji-er-kuang-ni-jiujiu-de-tui-a-lumch-ibj8m94abjh5nvr3qhiqff]] as the [[ChuState|楚国]] minister who gives the clearest warning against [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] going to [[Wuguan|武关]] for a meeting with [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]]. He argues that [[QinState|秦国]] is untrustworthy and that Chu should guard its borders rather than put the ruler inside Qin's chosen terrain.

The same source gives Zhao Ju a second succession role after Chu Huaiwang is detained in [[Xianyang|咸阳]] and crown prince [[MiHeng|芈横]] is still hostage in [[QiState|齐国]]. When some Chu elites consider installing another prince, Zhao Ju argues that bypassing both the trapped king and the absent crown prince would violate the ruler's intent and destabilize legitimacy.
