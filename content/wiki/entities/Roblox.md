# Roblox

[[tech-20260424-0424-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260424-0424-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds Roblox as a child-safety and state-settlement case. The episode says Roblox reached $36 million in settlements with the attorneys general of [[Alabama]], [[Nevada]], and [[WestVirginia|West Virginia]] amid allegations that it failed to protect minors from predatory behavior. [[MattKaufman]], Roblox's chief safety officer, describes a face-snapshot age estimation tool that helps decide communication access for younger users.
