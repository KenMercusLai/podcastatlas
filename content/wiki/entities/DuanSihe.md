# 段四合 / Duan Sihe

段四合 is the central speaker in [[144-nvxia-duan-sihe-he-tade-jiaxiang-mapingguan-women-ceng-yiwei-you-dian-you-lu-zhishi-meng-linmjqt-n-sa5sarpqgdxgtoeejt]], a [[QizhulouYanBinke|起朱楼宴宾客]] field conversation recorded in [[Mapingguan|马坪关]]. The source presents her as a [[BaiPeople|白族]] woman who returned home after junior high, once expected a life organized around marriage and childcare, and later became a homestay host, village communicator, and public-affairs participant.

Her importance in the wiki is not individual hero worship. The episode repeatedly has her resist the idea that one person carried the whole village; she describes change as mutual learning among many villagers, with outside support from [[HuangYunwu|黄云武]], [[KakaGugu|卡卡姑姑]], and visitors helping local capacity become more usable.

## Key Claims
- The source says 段四合 once spoke little Mandarin and was shy with outsiders, but gradually learned reception, communication, and public problem-solving by helping outside teams and villagers understand each other.
- Her homestay role is tied to [[SlowRuralTourism]] because she frames tourism as useful only if the village can absorb guests without losing its own pace.
- Her education work includes the [[AiWawaReadingGroup|爱娃娃亲子阅读小组]] and advocacy for a village parent to serve as school life administrator after school-consolidation pressure.
- Her public role connects [[MamaHui|妈妈会]], dance/performance groups, Mandarin practice, and women's confidence to [[RuralWomenPublicParticipation]].
- Her story extends [[FemaleSelfPossession]] by showing self-command formed through local work, speech practice, hosting, collective responsibility, and refusal to reduce rural life to inferiority.
