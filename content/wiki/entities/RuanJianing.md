# 阮佳宁 / Ruan Jianing

阮佳宁 / Ruan Jianing is a [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] reporter and [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] guest in [[ep236-lao-wu-kuai-qian-xinlihua-fufei-de-peiban-shi-zhende-ma-ltvuoeluoeu4-a3j3lamhc3buudx]]. Her source role is to make [[PaidListeningServices|付费树洞 / 倾听师]] concrete through user stories, worker conditions, pricing, matching, platform commissions, and safety boundaries.

The episode uses her reporting to distinguish paid listening from both friendship and psychotherapy. Users may value online anonymity, immediate feedback, and the ability to stop the relationship at any time, while listeners face [[EmotionalServicePlatformIncentives|platform renewal incentives]], blurred intimacy, gray demand, and [[MentalHealthCrisisInterventionBoundary|crisis-intervention limits]].
