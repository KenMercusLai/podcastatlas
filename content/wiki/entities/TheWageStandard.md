# The Wage Standard

The Wage Standard is [[ArinDube|Arin Dube]]'s labor-economics book in [[the-real-horror-of-alien-and-how-it-explains-why-were-not-paid-enough]]. The [[PlanetMoney]] episode uses the book as the source of its argument that [[LaborMonopsony]] is a central modern wage problem, not only a textbook curiosity.

The book's wiki role is to connect wage stagnation, minimum-wage evidence, [[NonCompeteAgreements]], [[LaborSearchFrictions]], and [[LaborMarketCounterforces]] into one worker-power branch. The episode does not deeply review the whole book; it uses Alien as an accessible demonstration of the problem the book foregrounds.
