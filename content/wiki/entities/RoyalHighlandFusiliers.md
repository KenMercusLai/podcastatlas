# Royal Highland Fusiliers

The Royal Highland Fusiliers appear in [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] through the killing of three off-duty soldiers on 10 March 1971. The episode says the soldiers were lured from a [[Belfast]] pub and shot in the back of the head, shocking both [[NorthernIreland]] and Britain.

The source uses the murders to show how violence could radicalize soldiers as well as civilians. A later paratrooper memoir is cited for the desire for revenge among troops, making the killings part of the episode's concern with discipline erosion inside the [[BritishArmy]].
