# 怪东西 Weird History

## Overview

怪东西 Weird History is the history podcast represented in the wiki by a comparative episode on the 1556 Huaxian and 1755 Lisbon earthquakes.

## Current Profile

In the bounded source, [[LuDapeng|陆大鹏]] and [[ZhangDabai|张大白]] use two catastrophes to join event narrative to buildings, communications, relief, political legitimacy, scientific inquiry, urban planning, and intellectual history. The format moves between personal earthquake memories, historical evidence, and explicit source qualifications.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses cross-regional comparison to identify shared disaster mechanisms and institutional differences.
- Treats catastrophe as an interaction among nature, built environment, public health, information, and governance.
- Connects historical records and survivor narratives to later scientific and intellectual interpretation.
