# 怪东西 Weird History

## Overview

怪东西 Weird History is a comparative history podcast represented through episodes pairing the Huaxian and Lisbon earthquakes and the reform programs of Zi Chan and Cleisthenes.

## Current Profile

In the earthquake source, [[LuDapeng|陆大鹏]] and [[ZhangDabai|张大白]] join event narrative to buildings, communications, relief, political legitimacy, scientific inquiry, urban planning, and intellectual history. The reform source compares [[ZiChan|子产]] and [[Cleisthenes]] through land, population, law, civic identity, and office design. Across both episodes, the format uses distant cases to expose shared mechanisms while preserving divergent institutions, mixed motives, and evidence limits.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses cross-regional comparison to identify shared disaster mechanisms and institutional differences.
- Treats catastrophe as an interaction among nature, built environment, public health, information, and governance.
- Connects historical records and survivor narratives to later scientific and intellectual interpretation.
- Uses paired reformers to compare how institutional change redistributes power across different political systems.

- Institutional range: [[43-dongxifang-de-dizhen-1556nian-jiajing-dadizhen-vs-1755nian-lisiben-dadizhen-lqz5aznr86rd4a8xawybzotkkaps]] links relief and reconstruction to state capacity, belief, archives, and philosophy.
- Reform comparison: [[42-dongxifang-de-bianfa-weixin-zichan-gouli-guojia-shengsiyi-beihou-de-nanren-vs-kelisitini-yadian-minzhu-zhifu-lvjn1o0aavcjhsm7gak9wpd9-drd]] compares Zheng state-building with Athenian civic redesign without collapsing their different outcomes.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to two episodes. It does not establish the show's full format, release history, creators, or editorial method. Quantitative, priority, attribution, and ancient institutional claims require specialist verification.

## What Changed
