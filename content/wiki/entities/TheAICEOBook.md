# The AI CEO

## Overview
The AI CEO is the book attributed to [[ZahraDataScienceWithSam|Dr. Zahra]] in [[ep-52-the-ai-ceo-leadership-governance-and-the-ai-gold-rush|Data Science With Sam EP52]]. The episode presents it as a research-based guide to AI-enabled leadership rather than a proposal to replace executives with machines.

## Current Profile
The book's reported framework joins practical use cases and industry examples to a leadership boundary: AI can accelerate research, analysis, communication, and execution, while humans retain judgment, courage, empathy, risk decisions, and accountability. Its adoption advice begins with real problems and everyday practice rather than hype or exhaustive technical study.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames AI as leadership leverage rather than executive replacement.
- Emphasizes better questions, critical challenge, and final human judgment.
- Includes reported use cases, analyses, frameworks, and practical examples.
- Connects adoption to pain points, KPIs, value, and return on investment.
- Treats governance and accountability as central in regulated industries.
