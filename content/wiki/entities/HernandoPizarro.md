# Hernando Pizarro

## Overview

Hernando Pizarro was a Spanish conquistador who helped extract treasure and capture Chalcuchima, carried the crown's share of Atahualpa's ransom to Spain, and later commanded the defense of Cusco during Manco Inca's siege.

## Current Profile

[[646-the-fall-of-the-incas-death-to-the-emperor-part-3-glt8131543734]] places Hernando in the ransom's expansion beyond Cajamarca. He destroys the Pachacamac shrine after finding little treasure, then encounters [[Chalcuchima]] at Xauxa and persuades or compels the general to accompany his small party to [[Atahualpa]]. Pizarro later sends Hernando to Spain with gold, ornaments, and a report intended to secure Charles V's recognition; the departure removes a Spaniard whom Atahualpa regarded as relatively friendly.

[[648-the-fall-of-the-incas-battle-for-the-sacred-city-part-5-glt6392035011]] presents Hernando as more ceremonially respectful toward [[MancoInca]] than Juan or [[GonzaloPizarro]], but still vulnerable to the same appetite for treasure. Manco wins permission to leave Cusco by promising a golden statue, then mobilizes the uprising that leaves Hernando commanding fewer than two hundred Spaniards and roughly five hundred Indigenous auxiliaries.

During [[SiegeOfCusco1536|the siege]], Hernando concludes that the defenders must retake Sacsayhuaman and continues the assault after Juan Pizarro's fatal wound. When [[DiegoDeAlmagro]] returns, Hernando warns Manco against trusting him but cannot prevent Almagro from entering Cusco, burning Pizarro properties, and imprisoning him.

## Key Characteristics

- Ransom expedition leader who violated Pachacamac and brought Chalcuchima into captivity.
- Royal envoy who carried treasure and Pizarro's claim for confirmation to Spain.
- Pizarro-family commander responsible for Spanish-held Cusco during the 1536 siege.
- More courteous toward Manco than his brothers, without abandoning the conquest hierarchy.
- Deceived by Manco's promise to retrieve a golden statue.
- Advocate and commander of the costly operation to retake Sacsayhuaman.

- Factional defeat: [[648-the-fall-of-the-incas-battle-for-the-sacred-city-part-5-glt6392035011]] follows his warning to Manco and subsequent imprisonment when Almagro takes Cusco.

## Qualifications

This profile covers selected episodes from Cajamarca through the siege of Cusco. Relative friendliness toward Atahualpa or courtesy toward Manco does not establish equal dealings or opposition to conquest. Dialogue, motives, troop totals, Pachacamac prophecy, Chalcuchima's reasons for compliance, and the golden-statue episode remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Added Pachacamac, Chalcuchima's capture, the ransom shipment, and Hernando's early rapport with Atahualpa.
- Extended the profile backward from the siege of Cusco to the extraction phase at Cajamarca.

## Relationships

- [[MancoInca]] - ruler he released before commanding the defense against Manco's uprising.
- [[GonzaloPizarro]] - brother and fellow defender later imprisoned by Almagro.
- [[DiegoDeAlmagro]] - factional rival who seized Cusco and imprisoned him.
- [[SiegeOfCusco1536]] - campaign in which he commanded the Spanish-held enclave.
- [[ConquistadorCivilWarPeru]] - intra-Spanish conflict that overtook the siege.
- [[Chalcuchima]] - Inca general whom Hernando brought into Spanish captivity.
- [[Atahualpa]] - captive ruler who feared execution after Hernando left for Spain.
