## Overview

东里衮 / Dongli Gun is the Nanyang administrator whose renewed levy mission triggers [[HouYinLateHan|侯音]]'s resistance in the current source.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1163-gaogu-ziji-shibushi-zui-da-de-yuchun-lokzo7kcz69kf_lhgskx8kie-9_c|Hanji 1163]] says Dongli Gun comes to collect further resources after an earlier large grain extraction. Hou Yin surrounds him; an attempted escape costs the life of the subordinate Ying Yu and ends with Dongli Gun recaptured. [[ZongZiqingLateHan|宗子卿]] later persuades Hou Yin to authorize his release and escapes with him to [[CaoRen|曹仁]].

## Key Characteristics

- Commandery administrator associated with wartime extraction.
- Immediate target of Hou Yin's local resistance.
- Attempts escape and is recaptured before Zong Ziqing frees him.
- Reaches Cao Ren and thereby helps expose Hou Yin's loss of control.

Release and escape:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1163-gaogu-ziji-shibushi-zui-da-de-yuchun-lokzo7kcz69kf_lhgskx8kie-9_c|Hanji 1163]] says Zong Ziqing frees Dongli Gun under Hou Yin's written order and takes him to Cao Ren.

## Qualifications

The source does not establish whether Dongli Gun designed the levy, merely implemented a superior's order, or had discretion over its scale. The grain amount, escape mechanics, Ying Yu's office and death, and Dongli Gun's later role require primary-source verification.
