## Overview

刘孝 / Liu Xiao is the younger son of [[LiuCiHengshanKingWesternHan|衡山王刘赐]] and 乘舒. Hanji 305-1 presents him as a succession aspirant who joins [[XuLaiHengshanQueen|徐来]] and [[LiuWucaiHengshanPrincess|刘无采]] against elder brother and crown prince [[LiuShuangHengshanCrownPrince|刘爽]].

## Current Profile

Liu Xiao's alliance with Xu Lai is tactical. Both want Liu Shuang removed, but Liu Xiao expects to inherit the crown-prince position himself, whereas Xu Lai wants her own son 刘广 advanced. His participation therefore demonstrates how a shared immediate target can conceal incompatible succession settlements.

## Key Characteristics

- Younger royal son who sees the established heir's weakness as an opening.
- Sibling participant in a queen-led anti-heir alignment.
- Tactical ally whose desired successor conflicts with Xu Lai's plan.
