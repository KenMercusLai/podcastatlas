# Royal Mint / 皇家造币厂

The Royal Mint appears in [[26-niudun-yu-weibi-zhizaozhe-keneng-shi-sineipu-de-yuanxing-580065317]] as the institution where [[IsaacNewton|Isaac Newton / 牛顿]] became warden and later master. The source presents it as both a production system and a state-capacity bottleneck: bad management, missing dies, coin shortages, counterfeit pressure, and the [[GreatRecoinage]] forced Newton to study machines, labor, accounts, suppliers, and factory rhythm.

The Mint also becomes a legal and intelligence platform. When [[WilliamChaloner|William Chaloner]] attacked the Mint and tried to redirect suspicion, Newton's role expanded from administration into [[CounterfeitCurrencyPolicing]], informant management, and courtroom preparation.
