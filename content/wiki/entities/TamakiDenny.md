# Tamaki Denny

## Overview
Tamaki Denny is the incumbent [[Okinawa]] governor whom [[KojaGentar]] defeated in the prefecture's September 2026 election.

## Current Profile
The source portrays Tamaki as a popular, alliance-supporting critic of the [[Henoko]] relocation who framed base resistance as a defense of Okinawan dignity. Eight years of failed lawsuits and inability to stop the project contributed to voter resignation and a turn toward economic concerns.

## Key Characteristics
- Opposed Henoko without opposing every U.S. base or the U.S.-Japan alliance.
- Used dignity and unequal burden as the moral frame for prefectural resistance.
- Pursued legal challenges against the central government.
- Lost support as voters judged the anti-relocation campaign unlikely to succeed.

## What Changed
- Tamaki's third-term bid failed as the electorate prioritized economic change over another cycle of resistance centered on Henoko.
