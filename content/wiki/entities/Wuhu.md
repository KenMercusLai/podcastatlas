# Wuhu / 芜湖

Wuhu / 芜湖 is the Anhui city used in [[no-211-huishang-wangshi-cong-qianshi-buxiu-dao-zuiniu-fengtou-1002275189]] to explain the early [[Chery]] story. The episode says Wuhu was looking for a new growth point in the 1990s as traditional light industry weakened, then pursued automobile manufacturing despite licensing constraints and scarce operating experience.

The city's Chery case is presented as a local industrial breakthrough built in a policy gap. Wuhu-backed entities funded the project, bought a secondhand Ford engine line from Britain, recruited Yin Tongyue, and used local taxi demand before Chery gained formal production-list access through its SAIC arrangement.
