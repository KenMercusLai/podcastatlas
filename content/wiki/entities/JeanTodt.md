# Jean Todt

Jean Todt is one of the Ferrari Formula One operators credited in [[ferrari-1]] with rebuilding [[ScuderiaFerrari]] under [[LucaDiMontezemolo]]. The source names him with [[RossBrawn]] and [[MichaelSchumacher]] as part of the team that won five straight drivers' and constructors' championships from 2000 to 2004.

Todt matters in the wiki because Ferrari's brand power in this source depends on operating excellence, not only nostalgia. The modern dynasty gave [[Ferrari]] fresh racing proof for [[RacingHeritageBrandMoat]].
