## Overview

彭宣 / Peng Xuan is synthesized as a Western Han senior official whose current arc runs from appointment as Yushi Dafu in [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]'s partial post-[[WangJiaWesternHan|王嘉]] correction to later resignation as Da Sikong under [[WangMang|王莽]]'s tightening court. His case supplies an early example of dignified retreat under a dangerous power field.

## Current Profile

Hanji 599 provides the earlier office layer. After Wang Jia dies and Aidi restores [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]] and [[HeWuWesternHan|何武]], Aidi transfers He Wu to Qian Jiangjun and appoints Peng Xuan as Yushi Dafu.

Hanji 606 presents Peng Xuan later as one of the Three Excellencies observing Wang Mang's concentration of power. He cannot realistically contend with Wang Mang, so he submits a resignation memorial that frames withdrawal as incapacity: the Three Excellencies are like the three legs of a ding vessel, and if one leg cannot bear weight the whole vessel overturns. He cites age, illness, mental confusion, and declining memory while asking to return the Da Sikong and Changping Hou seals and go home.

Wang Mang reports the request to Wang Zhengjun, who removes Peng Xuan from office and lets him return to his fief. The source stresses Wang Mang's resentment afterward: although retired high ministers ordinarily receive gold, a comfortable carriage, and four horses, Peng Xuan receives none. Ban Gu's later comparison treats Peng Xuan's retreat as clearer-sighted than mediocre clinging to office.

Peng Xuan therefore becomes a survival-judgment figure. He exits before direct destruction, but Wang Mang's denial of normal retirement gifts shows that even successful retreat can carry symbolic punishment.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Yushi Dafu in Aidi's post-Wang-Jia personnel sequence.
- Later Da Sikong and Changping Hou in the Wang Mang power field.
- Senior official who reads the court situation as dangerous.
- Uses illness, age, and incapacity language to justify resignation.
- Returns to his fief after Wang Zhengjun accepts the resignation.
- Punished symbolically when Wang Mang withholds normal retirement gifts.
- Later framed by Ban Gu as a clearer-sighted official who knew when to retreat.

## Qualifications

The sources do not prove Peng Xuan's private motive beyond Hanji 606's reading that resignation was survival-minded. Hanji 599 supports only his Yushi Dafu appointment in Aidi's office sequence. His health claims may have been sincere, strategic, or both. The wiki therefore treats him as a source-bounded senior-official case rather than a full biography.
