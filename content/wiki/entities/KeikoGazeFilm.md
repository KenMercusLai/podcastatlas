# 《惠子凝视》

《惠子凝视》 is the Japanese film introduced in [[88-bie-renshu-huizi-zai-ting-budao-hecai-de-shijie-li-huiquan-708510803]] as an adaptation of [[KeikoBoxer|惠子]]'s real boxing story and autobiographical source [[BieRenshuHuiziBook|《别认输，惠子》]]. The episode uses the film mainly as a doorway into the life story rather than as a sustained piece of film criticism.

Its function in the wiki is therefore bounded. It anchors the source's interest in female boxing and disability visibility, but the core material added by the episode comes from the life narrative: school fit, communication, teenage alienation, gym access, and what it means to box without hearing ordinary ring sounds.
