# 哈啰 / HelloBike

## Overview
哈啰 / HelloBike is the shared two-wheel platform the episode presents as a later survivor after the Mobike-OFO war.

## Current Profile
The source contrasts HelloBike with the first-wave duopoly. It starts from Ningbo in 2016, emphasizes lower-tier cities, merges with low-carbon technology under 永安行 context, and later changes into 哈啰出行 with bikes, e-bikes, electric vehicles, and local-life extensions.

## Key Characteristics
- It entered shared bikes after the earliest Mobike/OFO attention cycle.
- It focused on smaller and lower-tier cities rather than only top-tier urban battlefields.
- It is presented as a survivor in the current shared two-wheel market.
- Its later profile expands from bicycles into e-bikes, electric vehicles, and local life.
