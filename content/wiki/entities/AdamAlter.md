# Adam Alter

## Overview
Adam Alter is presented in [[the-magic-wand-experiment-the-magic-wand-experiment|The Magic Wand Experiment]] as an NYU psychologist proposing a lifelong randomized study of screen and social-media exposure.

## Current Profile
Alter's imagined design assigns newborns to different exposure levels and follows them for at least fifty years. It would compare happiness, meaning, social connection, facial-cue learning, and dissatisfaction associated with curated lives while separating earlier phone-and-email technology from app-centered smartphones.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames screen effects as a long-horizon exposure and dosage problem.
- Distinguishes technology generations rather than treating all screens as one intervention.
- Seeks thresholds and mechanisms, not only correlations between use and well-being.

## Evidence
### Proposed longitudinal randomization
- [[the-magic-wand-experiment-the-magic-wand-experiment|The Magic Wand Experiment]] supplies the fifty-year design and proposed outcomes.

## Qualifications
The episode reports a proposal, not trial results. Content, device, social context, developmental stage, self-selection, platform design, and historical change would remain difficult to separate, and the source gives no validated harmful threshold.

## What Changed
- Created a bounded profile of Alter's episode-reported experiment and causal questions.
