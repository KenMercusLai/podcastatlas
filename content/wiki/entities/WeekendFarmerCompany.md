# Weekend Farmer Company

## Overview

Weekend Farmer Company is the family business formed by [[GeorgeRohrbacher]] and [[AnneRohrbacher]] to produce and sell [[TheFarmingGame]].

## Current Profile

In [[board-game-season-board-game-season]], the company emerges from a farm household under severe financial pressure. Its early operating model distributes manufacturing across specialist suppliers, brings assembly and order fulfillment into the family home, and reaches customers through agricultural magazines, trade shows, small-town retailers, word of mouth, and direct telephone orders.

The source presents the company as inseparable from Breaks Ranch: the game business supplies income that protects the farm, while lived farm experience gives the product credibility and a defined audience. This is a source-reported survival narrative rather than a complete company history or current operating profile.

## Key Characteristics

- Began as a family response to agricultural financial distress.
- Coordinated specialized board, backing, money, dice, and acreage-piece production.
- Used household labor for assembly, customer service, and fulfillment.
- Built distribution around agricultural media, rural events, small retailers, and direct orders.
- Linked product authenticity and company purpose to continued life on Breaks Ranch.
