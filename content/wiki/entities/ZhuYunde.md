# Zhu Yunde / 竺韵德

## Overview

Zhu Yunde / 竺韵德 is the founder figure behind [[NingboYunsheng|宁波韵生]] in [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]]. The episode uses him to show an entrepreneur who notices a supply bottleneck at the Guangzhou Fair, builds domestic music-box movement capability, and later pushes into rare-earth permanent magnets.

## Current Profile

Zhu is portrayed as a technical-industrial founder whose strategic moves come from gaps in supply and scale. Music-box movements were a narrow product, but solving them required precision and production consistency; when the market size proved limited, he redirected the company toward sintered NdFeB magnets.

His most important source moment is refusing a technology purchase condition that would have restricted all resulting products to a German counterparty. The episode treats that refusal as the trigger for autonomous R&D rather than dependency.

## Key Characteristics

- Zhu spotted the music-box movement opportunity when Chinese exporters could not accept orders because key mechanisms depended on Japanese supply.
- He built Yunsheng under capital, equipment, and talent constraints.
- He treated equipment and process development as part of company capability.
- He renamed and repositioned the company before the rare-earth magnet second venture.
- He chose self-development after rejecting a restrictive foreign technology condition.
