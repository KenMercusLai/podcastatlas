# RPO Robot

## Overview
RPO Robot is the earlier [[LuoboPaidui|萝博派对]] humanoid robot product discussed in [[22-sui-de-jushen-ceo-5-lun-rongzi-guo-yi-meiyuan-buzhi-tian-you-duo-gao-yinian-chile-shi-nian-de-ku-duitan-huangyi-luobo-paidui-chuangshiren-ceo-ljwdmocryiblf0k3rkz8eonkqox]]. The source presents it as a zero-to-one education and research product that generated real monthly orders and customer feedback before the company pushed toward [[RP1Robot|RP1]].

## Current Profile
In the episode, RPO is described as a kit-like product with about 100 units of monthly orders, mostly for schools, startups, and similar technical users. It is important because it gives Luobo Paidui customer groups, support patterns, product pain points, and proof that an open-source humanoid platform can produce real demand before the company scales manufacturing.

## Key Characteristics
- Education/research orientation: RPO is positioned as a platform customers can assemble, study, and modify.
- Real order signal: Huang says the product has about 100 monthly orders, with schools making up roughly 30-40 percent.
- Support-community effect: customer groups sometimes answer each other's questions, reducing direct support pressure.
- Delivery constraint exposure: a large European order reportedly exceeded the company's then-current supply-chain and delivery capacity.
- Transition function: RPO's limits helped define RP1's productization agenda.
