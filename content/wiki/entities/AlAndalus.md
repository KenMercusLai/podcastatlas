# al-Andalus / 安达卢斯

## Overview
al-Andalus / 安达卢斯 appears in the Ceuta episode as the Islamic Iberian world connected to North Africa through [[Ceuta]] and the [[StraitOfGibraltar]].

## Current Profile
The source uses al-Andalus to break a simple Europe-versus-Africa or Christian-versus-Muslim geography. Muslim rule in Iberia, Berber military participation, the [[CordobaCaliphate|Cordoba Caliphate]], Taifa fragmentation, Christian kingdoms, and cross-religious alliances all make the region politically mixed and historically entangled with North Africa.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects Iberian Islamic history with North African military and political networks.
- Includes the Cordoba-centered civilization branch discussed in the episode.
- Shows that medieval alliances did not always follow religious boundaries.
