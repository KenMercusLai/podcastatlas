# 刘松明 / Liu Songming

## Overview
刘松明 (Liu Songming) is the founder of [[LiberAI]], a physical-AI company combining world-action models, robot data, hardware integration, control, and deployment.

## Current Profile
Born in 2000, Liu entered [[TsinghuaUniversity|Tsinghua University]] through chemistry competition, moved from chemical engineering into computer science, and used AI-for-science work as a bridge between the fields. His robotics research included [[RDT1B]], whose pretraining results and open technical documentation helped create a research and industry network that later supported company formation.

Liu presents entrepreneurship rather than any single technical method as his long-term commitment. His operating view joins technical leadership with capital discipline: the lead founder must understand how compute, data, experiments, hardware cycles, and hiring compete for resources, while refusing to treat a large eventual market as proof of near-term product demand.

## Key Characteristics
- Cross-disciplinary path from chemistry and chemical engineering into computer science, AI for science, and robotics.
- Early research emphasis on robot pretraining, diffusion-based continuous action modeling, scalable data, and reproducible technical disclosure.
- Founder-led technical resource allocation across compute, data, hardware, experiments, and product scenes.
- Selective full-stack thesis built around the expected expansion of general foundation models into robotics.
- Cautious commercialization posture that distinguishes technical progress from timing alignment among models, hardware, and market adoption.
- Organization-building style emphasizing taste, simplicity, debate, collaboration, and final founder accountability.
