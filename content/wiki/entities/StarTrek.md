## Overview

Star Trek is a science-fiction franchise represented here through its explicit reuse of Roman names, gladiatorial spectacle, empire, mass media, and Christianity.

## Current Profile

The episode uses the Romulans and the 1968 episode “Bread and Circuses” as direct Roman signals. Kirk, Spock, and McCoy encounter a society resembling Rome in the twentieth century; gladiatorial combat is televised in color, turning ancient spectacle into a Cold War-era media allegory before the ending introduces Christianity through the “sun” / “Son” pun.

## Key Characteristics

- Roman reference embedded directly in the Romulan name.
- Alternative-world Rome extended into modern mass media.
- Gladiatorial violence treated as televised spectacle.
- Cold War and Christian meanings layered onto antiquity.
