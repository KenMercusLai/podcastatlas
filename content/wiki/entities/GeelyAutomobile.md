# Geely Automobile / 吉利汽车

## Overview
Geely Automobile / 吉利汽车 is a Chinese automaker presented in the bounded sources through vehicle usability, governance succession, EV-brand integration, charging and battery-swap partnerships, and reported localization of production in Europe.

## Current Profile
The current evidence shows Geely managing several layers of automotive execution at once. A recall item places emergency-handle legibility inside its product-safety obligations. A governance item presents [[AnConghui|安聪慧]] succeeding founder [[LiShufu|李书福]] as chair while [[Zeekr|极氪]] creates both revenue scale and integration pressure. A reported two-way relationship with [[NIO]] adds charging-and-swapping optionality rather than proof that either route has won.

The latest source extends this portfolio logic into manufacturing geography. It says a Geely transaction involving [[FordMotorCompany|Ford]]'s Spanish subsidiary received unconditional Chinese merger-control approval and that the parties will jointly operate the Valencia plant, where Geely expects to produce two new-energy models. If executed, underused European capacity can become local supply and tariff-risk management, but approval and planned production do not establish successful launch, utilization, or economics.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates a multi-brand automotive group with significant EV integration and profitability questions around [[Zeekr]].
- Is moving from founder chairmanship toward veteran internal leadership under [[AnConghui]].
- Carries ordinary vehicle-safety and usability obligations alongside strategic technology bets.
- Is reported to be entering NIO's consumer battery-swap network while retaining its own charging capability.
- Treats charging and swapping as parallel infrastructure options under technical and demand uncertainty.
- Is pursuing reported European production through Ford's Valencia plant as a localization and trade-risk response.

## Evidence
- Safety-usability evidence: [[qishui-yinyue-chongji-tengxun-wangyi-tesila-deng-cheqi-zai-guonei-kaizhan-daguimo-zhaohui-1008396395]] names Geely in a China recall concerning how easily occupants can identify and operate emergency mechanical door handles.
- Governance evidence: [[buai-zhibo-daihuo-de-oumei-xiaofeizhe-weihe-zai-zhejia-pingtai-shang-daba-huaqian-1007104138]] reports Li Shufu's chair resignation, An Conghui's succession, and Zeekr's growing but still difficult integration role.
- Infrastructure-partnership evidence: [[jingdong-kaichu-gouwuzhongxin-weilai-yu-jili-tuijin-chongdian-huandian-hezuo-1019779523]] reports a Geely Holding stake in NIO Energy, planned Geely consumer swap vehicles using NIO service, and reciprocal NIO investment in Geely's charging affiliate.
- European-production evidence: [[gaojiesi-huoguo-liansuo-neng-kai-shang-qianjia-tongyang-zuo-jucan-shengyi-de-shaokao-pinpai-weihe-kuozhang-nan-1023993593]] reports Chinese approval for the Ford Spain transaction, joint operation around the Valencia plant, two planned Geely new-energy models, and a 2028 target for the first model.

## Qualifications
Recall scale, governance characterization, ownership terms, valuation, approval scope, factory capacity, and development plans are source-scoped podcast claims. The sources do not establish that the NIO cross-investments have closed, that Geely swap vehicles have launched, or that interoperability and utilization will make the arrangement profitable. Nor do they establish that Valencia production will start on schedule, reach efficient utilization, qualify for the expected tariff treatment, or reverse Ford's European weakness. Fast charging may reduce swapping's convenience advantage without eliminating use cases for swapping.

## What Changed
- Added the reported Ford Spain transaction and Valencia production plan.
- Extended Geely's profile from infrastructure optionality into manufacturing-location and trade-risk optionality.
- Kept approval, production timing, tariff treatment, utilization, and returns explicitly unproven.
