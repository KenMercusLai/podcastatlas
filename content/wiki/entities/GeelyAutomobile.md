# Geely Automobile / 吉利汽车

## Overview
Geely Automobile / 吉利汽车 is a Chinese automaker presented in the bounded sources through vehicle usability, governance succession, EV-brand integration, and a reported two-way charging and battery-swap partnership with [[NIO]].

## Current Profile
The current evidence shows Geely managing several layers of automotive execution at once. A recall item places emergency-handle legibility inside its product-safety obligations. A governance item presents [[AnConghui|安聪慧]] succeeding founder [[LiShufu|李书福]] as chair while [[Zeekr|极氪]] creates both revenue scale and integration pressure. The newest episode says Geely Holding will take a 30% NIO Energy stake, develop consumer swap-capable vehicles served by NIO's network, and receive reciprocal investment from NIO in Geely's VREMT Energy charging business. This is a portfolio approach to charging and swapping, not evidence that either route has already won.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates a multi-brand automotive group with significant EV integration and profitability questions around [[Zeekr]].
- Is moving from founder chairmanship toward veteran internal leadership under [[AnConghui]].
- Carries ordinary vehicle-safety and usability obligations alongside strategic technology bets.
- Is reported to be entering NIO's consumer battery-swap network while retaining its own charging capability.
- Treats charging and swapping as parallel infrastructure options under technical and demand uncertainty.

## Evidence
- Safety-usability evidence: [[qishui-yinyue-chongji-tengxun-wangyi-tesila-deng-cheqi-zai-guonei-kaizhan-daguimo-zhaohui-1008396395]] names Geely in a China recall concerning how easily occupants can identify and operate emergency mechanical door handles.
- Governance evidence: [[buai-zhibo-daihuo-de-oumei-xiaofeizhe-weihe-zai-zhejia-pingtai-shang-daba-huaqian-1007104138]] reports Li Shufu's chair resignation, An Conghui's succession, and Zeekr's growing but still difficult integration role.
- Infrastructure-partnership evidence: [[jingdong-kaichu-gouwuzhongxin-weilai-yu-jili-tuijin-chongdian-huandian-hezuo-1019779523]] reports a Geely Holding stake in NIO Energy, planned Geely consumer swap vehicles using NIO service, and reciprocal NIO investment in Geely's charging affiliate.

## Qualifications
Recall scale, governance characterization, ownership terms, valuation, and development plans are source-scoped podcast claims. The sources do not establish that the cross-investments have closed, that Geely swap vehicles have launched, or that interoperability and utilization will make the arrangement profitable. Fast charging may reduce swapping's convenience advantage without eliminating use cases for swapping.
