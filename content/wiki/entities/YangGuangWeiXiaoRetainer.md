## Overview

杨广 / Yang Guang is a source-bounded [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|Wei Xiao]] subordinate in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-703-donghan-kaiguo-hou-zui-xianhe-de-jiazu-pingsha-shi-ta-lgg-fra8ostp2xsuugph6o-ilzac|Hanji 703]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-704-weixiao-wo-da-ni-shi-bu-dui-dan-ni-ping-sha-hai-shou-lgi-cjyledgzldrie6kwpvejwsls|Hanji 704]]. His current significance is not an independent biography but a remonstrance-pressure role: [[MaYuanEasternHan|Ma Yuan]] writes to him because Yang Guang is positioned to persuade Wei Xiao, and because official obligation plus private friendship should either produce frank warning or justify leaving before collective ruin.

## Current Profile

Hanji 703 now introduces Yang Guang inside Wei Xiao's failing western field before the later apology sequence. Ma Yuan writes him to ask that he persuade Wei Xiao, rejects Wang Yuan's claim that the Han side is weak, and invokes Wei Xiao's son Wei Xun in Luoyang as a hostage whose danger makes continued rebellion a family-security problem.

Hanji 704 then sharpens the same role. Ma Yuan urges Yang Guang to join Niu Han and others in remonstrating with Wei Xiao, arguing that the balance of commanderies makes resistance untenable: Wei Xiao holds only two commanderies against the rest of the realm.

The episode then narrows Yang Guang's profile by silence. Yang Guang does not answer Ma Yuan, so the current wiki should not infer later defection, martyrdom, or policy influence. His importance is the pressure point itself: Ma Yuan treats honest counsel as a duty created by both public hierarchy and private friendship, while the absence of a reply shows how hard such counsel can be inside a collapsing camp.

## Key Characteristics

- Wei Xiao retainer whose current evidence is limited to Hanji 703 and Hanji 704 warning-letter episodes.
- Recipient of Ma Yuan's family-security pressure around Wei Xun's hostage exposure.
- Recipient of Ma Yuan's strategic arithmetic about two commanderies standing against the wider realm.
- Actor positioned between public ruler-minister obligation and private friendship obligation.
- Silent figure whose nonresponse keeps his later stance source-scoped.

## Qualifications

The page remains source-bounded and should not be merged with [[YangGuangSui|Emperor Yang of Sui]] or [[YangGuangxian|Yang Guangxian]]. Hanji 703 adds Wang Yuan and Wei Xun pressure but does not establish Yang Guang's later career, exact office, clan background, reply to Ma Yuan, or final decision. Niu Han is named only as another person to join in remonstrance in Hanji 704.
