## Overview

杨广 / Yang Guang is a source-bounded [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|Wei Xiao]] subordinate in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-704-weixiao-wo-da-ni-shi-bu-dui-dan-ni-ping-sha-hai-shou-lgi-cjyledgzldrie6kwpvejwsls|Hanji 704]]. His current significance is not an independent biography but a remonstrance-pressure role: [[MaYuanEasternHan|Ma Yuan]] writes to him because Yang Guang has both an official ruler-minister tie and a private friendship tie to Wei Xiao, and therefore should either warn Wei Xiao openly or leave before collective ruin.

## Current Profile

Hanji 704 introduces Yang Guang inside Wei Xiao's failing western field. Ma Yuan urges him to join Niu Han and others in remonstrating with Wei Xiao, arguing that the balance of commanderies makes resistance untenable: Wei Xiao holds only two commanderies against the rest of the realm.

The episode then narrows Yang Guang's profile by silence. Yang Guang does not answer Ma Yuan, so the current wiki should not infer later defection, martyrdom, or policy influence. His importance is the pressure point itself: Ma Yuan treats honest counsel as a duty created by both public hierarchy and private friendship, while the absence of a reply shows how hard such counsel can be inside a collapsing camp.

## Key Characteristics

- Wei Xiao retainer whose current evidence is limited to one Hanji 704 warning-letter episode.
- Recipient of Ma Yuan's strategic arithmetic about two commanderies standing against the wider realm.
- Actor positioned between public ruler-minister obligation and private friendship obligation.
- Silent figure whose nonresponse keeps his later stance source-scoped.
