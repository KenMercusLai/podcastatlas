# Robert Skidelsky

## Overview

Robert Skidelsky appears as a historian of [[JohnMaynardKeynes|John Maynard Keynes]], Xiang Lanxin's teacher and friend, and a descendant of a Russian-Jewish family connected to Harbin.

## Current Profile

The episode joins Skidelsky's scholarship to family memory. His long Keynes biography is described as intellectual and institutional history, while his return with [[XiangLanxin|Xiang Lanxin]] to Shanghai, Tianjin, and Harbin reconnects wartime displacement, family enterprise, schools, homes, and graves with contemporary China.

The visit is said to have softened some of Skidelsky's prior views of China. The claim is useful as firsthand recollection about intellectual change, not independent evidence for his complete position or for China's trajectory.

## Key Characteristics

- Historian and biographer of [[JohnMaynardKeynes|John Maynard Keynes]].
- Linked through family history to Russian-Jewish life and enterprise in Harbin.
- Described by Xiang as both teacher and friend.
- Returned to Chinese family sites with Xiang around 2005.
- Connected Keynesian biography to questions of international finance and later defense-led stimulus.
