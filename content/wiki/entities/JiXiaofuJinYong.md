## Overview

纪晓芙（金庸小说） / Ji Xiaofu (Jin Yong) is an Emei disciple in [[HeavenSwordDragonSaber|《倚天屠龙记》]] whose love for [[YangXiaoJinYong|杨逍]] conflicts with sect loyalty, honesty, non-harm, and self-preservation.

## Current Profile

Ji neither returns fully to Emei nor joins Yang Xiao, instead raising their daughter in concealment. The episode reads this both as difficulty establishing a priority among incompatible goods and as a principled refusal to secure safety through deceit or murder.

That second reading becomes strongest when Abbess Miejue offers restoration, succession, and the Heaven-Reliant Sword if Ji will deceive and kill Yang. Ji refuses and dies. Comparison with Zhou Zhiruo's later obedience makes the refusal a test of moral action under direct institutional pressure, not simply passive temperament.

## Key Characteristics

- Retains attachment to Yang Xiao without abandoning all loyalty to Emei.
- Raises Yang Buhui independently rather than resolving the conflict by joining either side.
- Refuses to deceive and assassinate Yang in exchange for institutional restoration.
- Accepts death rather than perform an act she judges disloyal and harmful.
- Can be read as indecisive across ordinary life but decisive at the final moral threshold.
- Leaves the nature of the relationship's initial coercion unresolved.

## Qualifications

The profile reflects a podcast debate, not a definitive account of the novel or every edition. “Indecision” and “principled refusal” are competing interpretations that can describe different phases rather than mutually exclusive verdicts. Her later attachment does not settle the earlier coercion question, and death under her master's violence should not be reframed as a freely chosen romantic sacrifice.

## What Changed

- Created the profile around divided belonging and a decisive refusal to deceive or kill.
- Preserved both the indecision and principled-integrity readings.
- Separated later attachment from proof about earlier consent.
