# Revo

## Overview

Revo is an AI-native sales platform co-founded and led by [[DavidZhu]]. It aims to unify pipeline generation, deal progression, CRM, coaching, forecasting, and reporting through shared data, semantic, memory, workflow, and agent layers.

## Current Profile

Revo is best understood in the wiki as a platform-architecture and go-to-market case. The company spent roughly two years building broad workflow depth with a large engineering-heavy founding team, then discovered that mid-market buyers would not readily replace an established Salesforce-centered stack all at once. It retained the integrated foundation but changed the adoption path: customers can start with a specific “golden workflow” and expand, making [[IntegratedPlatformModularAdoption]] the company's central strategic lesson.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses a vertically integrated foundation across multiple sales workflows rather than a loose bundle of point tools.
- Began with 14 founders, including 12 senior or staff-level engineers, and remained heavily weighted toward engineering, product, and design.
- Initially paired its broad technical architecture with an all-at-once replacement pitch that mid-market buyers largely rejected.
- Repackaged the platform around modular entry points and three customer outcomes rather than an exhaustive capability menu.
- Reports growth driven by retention, expansion, referrals, and founder-led commercial learning rather than paid marketing.

## Evidence

Integrated product foundation:
- [[2bbd2ffd]] describes Revo's shared data, semantic, and memory layers across pipeline generation, deal progression, CRM, coaching, forecasting, and reporting.

Commercial packaging and adoption:
- [[2bbd2ffd]] reports that 200-300 early prospects resisted full-stack replacement, after which Revo allowed customers to adopt individual capabilities through outcome-oriented “golden workflows.”

Early traction and organization:
- [[2bbd2ffd]] reports roughly $1 million in ARR within five or six months of the first paying customer, an engineering-heavy organization of about 105-110 people, and hundreds of customers at the later interview date.

## Qualifications

The available evidence is one interview with Revo's CEO, supplied as a structured summary rather than an independent product review. Metrics, onboarding comparisons, customer counts, architectural differentiation, and future self-improving-platform claims are source-scoped. Revo's revised packaging was itself described as an experiment rather than a settled commercial formula.
