## Overview

虞诩 / Yu Xu is a Shun-era official whose current wiki profile joins two frontier-policy judgments: a 129 CE restoration proposal that [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] accepts, and a later Wuling tax warning that Shun rejects. He now anchors a broader pattern of frontier advice that treats revenue, settlement, and commandery geography as stability problems rather than isolated administrative choices.

## Current Profile

Yu Xu's earlier current layer comes from Hanji 810. He argues that Anding, Beidi, and Shangjun should be restored because their terrain protects Chang'an and imperial tombs while also supporting pasture, irrigation, transport, and cultivation. Shun accepts the proposal and orders people moved back. This makes Yu Xu a successful advocate for strategic frontier restoration, not only a rejected warning figure.

His later current layer comes from Hanji 818. As Shangshu Ling, Yu Xu opposes taxing submitted [[WulingManLateHan|武陵蛮]] groups like ordinary Han households. Most court participants accept the revenue proposal, but Yu Xu treats the inherited lighter burden as a credibility settlement. He warns that the revenue gain will not cover rebellion costs. Shun rejects the advice, and later tribute mismatch and tax pressure help trigger Wuling killings and revolt.

## Key Characteristics

- Frontier-policy adviser who integrates geography, settlement, and state-security costs.
- Successful advocate for restoring Anding, Beidi, and Shangjun as a defensive and productive commandery belt.
- Minority dissenter against later Wuling tax uniformity in Shun's court.
- Cost-based reasoner who judges whether short-term revenue or administrative tidiness will provoke larger disorder.
- Warning figure whose later rejected advice is vindicated by the Wuling revolt.
- Source-bounded Eastern Han official, not yet a complete Yu Xu biography.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 810 and Hanji 818 and does not attempt a complete Yu Xu biography. Hanji 810 sharpens the profile by showing Shun can accept Yu Xu's advice in one frontier context and reject it in another. The Hanji 818 episode transcript writes 于许; this page preserves the durable route 虞诩 while retaining normalization caution through the source note.
