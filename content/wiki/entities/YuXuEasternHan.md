## Overview
虞诩 / Yu Xu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-818-guanchang-weishenme-yao-duo-jiaoyou-neng-jiuming-liyziudpezaoa4l6pbsq0wk2qdjdr|Hanji 818]] as the Shangshu Ling who opposes taxing submitted [[WulingManLateHan|武陵蛮]] groups like ordinary Han households. His page anchors the earliest current wiki case for frontier taxation as a stability problem rather than a simple revenue question.

## Current Profile
Yu Xu's current profile is bounded to one source note. When the Wuling administrator argues that submitted Man groups should now pay rent and tax on the same model as Han people, most court participants agree. Yu Xu dissents, treating the earlier lighter arrangement as a political settlement whose credibility matters.

His warning is explicitly cost-based. He argues that changing inherited rules and increasing burdens will provoke resentment, and that the added revenue will not cover the cost of the disorder it creates. [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] rejects the advice; the later tribute-cloth mismatch and tax pressure help trigger Wuling killings and revolt.

## Key Characteristics
- Court official who judges frontier tax policy through stability costs rather than uniformity alone.
- Minority dissenter against a majority revenue proposal in Shun's court.
- Defender of inherited settlement terms as a governance credibility problem.
- Warning figure whose rejected advice is vindicated by the Wuling revolt.
- Source-bounded Eastern Han official, not yet a full biography in the wiki.
