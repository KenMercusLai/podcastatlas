## Overview

虞诩 / Yu Xu is an Eastern Han official whose current wiki profile now begins with small-force frontier command before extending into Shun-era purge enforcement, court-risk courage, personnel recommendation, frontier restoration, and taxation dissent. Hanji 788 shows him advising cavalry mobility and rescuing [[WuduCommanderyLateHan|武都郡]] through deception and ambush; Hanji 805 and Hanji 806 show him as a hard cleanup and anti-eunuch figure under [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]]; Hanji 810 and Hanji 818 show his frontier-policy judgment being accepted in one setting and rejected in another.

## Current Profile

Hanji 788 supplies Yu Xu's earliest current layer. He argues that infantry-heavy Han forces cannot reliably chase mounted Qiang enemies, so local soldiers should return to farming while pooling money for horses. The court accepts the advice, [[RenShangLateHan|任尚]] sends light cavalry, and the result supports Yu Xu's reputation for military judgment.

That reputation makes him useful and exposed. [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]] appoints him [[WuduCommanderyLateHan|Wudu]] taishou with only a little more than two thousand troops. On the route, Yu Xu falsely claims he is waiting for reinforcements, moves when Qiang blockers disperse, and uses [[IncreasingStovesDeception|increasing stoves]] to make pursuers believe reinforcements have arrived. At [[ChitingDefenseLateHan|Chiting]], he hides strong crossbows, concentrates fire only when the enemy attacks closely, sorties, burns tents, stages fake reinforcements through gate rotations, leaks a decision to fight, and ambushes the Qiang during withdrawal.

Hanji 805 then places Yu Xu in Shun's restoration settlement as sili xiaowei. He impeaches Feng Shi and Liu Xi for attaching themselves to the Yan outer relatives, then turns against Cheng Huang, Chen Bing, Meng Sheng, and Li Run as older palace actors rather than true Shun-restoration helpers. When the Three Excellencies accuse him of overreaching after an amnesty, he defends law as public restraint and accuses office culture of rewarding compliant self-protection. Shun does not punish him.

Hanji 806 supplies the court-risk layer. Yu Xu repeatedly memorializes against [[ZhangFangEunuchLateHan|Zhang Fang]]'s bribery and power abuse, but Zhang Fang's palace access turns the accusation back on him. Sent to left-school labor and repeatedly interrogated, Yu Xu refuses quiet suicide because public punishment leaves a way for right and wrong to be distinguished. Friends, students, [[SunChengLateHan|Sun Cheng]], and [[GaoFanLateHan|Gao Fan]] reopen the channel to Shun; Zhang Fang is banished, Yu Xu is released and promoted, and Yu Xu recommends [[ZuoXiongLateHan|Zuo Xiong]] as a loyal direct official.

Hanji 810 and Hanji 818 show the policy adviser after these crisis layers. In Hanji 810, Yu Xu argues that Anding, Beidi, and Shangjun should be restored because their terrain protects Chang'an and imperial tombs while supporting pasture, irrigation, transport, and cultivation; Shun accepts and orders the commanderies restored. In Hanji 818, Yu Xu opposes taxing submitted [[WulingManLateHan|Wuling Man]] groups like ordinary Han households. He warns that added revenue will not cover rebellion costs, but Shun rejects the warning and Wuling later erupts after extraction pressure and tribute mismatch.

## Key Characteristics

- Frontier tactician who matches enemy mobility through cavalry advice and then compensates for small numbers through deception, crossbow timing, and ambush.
- Post-restoration purge enforcer who targets Yan-connected ministers and older non-restoration eunuchs.
- Hard self-defender who treats law as public restraint and accuses high officials of preemptive self-protection.
- Anti-eunuch accuser and public-witness figure who refuses to let Zhang Fang's retaliation become an unseen prison death.
- Ally-network beneficiary whose rescue requires Sun Cheng, Gao Fan, family, and students to reopen a blocked channel.
- Talent recommender who moves from personal rescue to naming Zuo Xiong as a loyal and direct official.
- Frontier-policy adviser whose judgment ranges from accepted commandery restoration to rejected Wuling tax-stability warning.

## Qualifications

This page synthesizes five source notes, not a complete Yu Xu biography. Hanji 788 gives a military-tactical Yu Xu before the later Shun-era court material; Hanji 805 and Hanji 806 show him as a powerful correction instrument who can also become a target; Hanji 810 and Hanji 818 show that the same frontier-policy mind can be accepted in one problem and ignored in another. The Hanji 788 and Hanji 818 source notes write 于许; this page preserves the durable route 虞诩 while keeping that transcript normalization explicit.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 788 as Yu Xu's earliest current layer: cavalry-mobility advice, Wudu appointment, increasing stoves, Chiting defense, and withdrawal ambush.
- Reframed Yu Xu as a frontier tactician before he becomes a Shun-era purge, access, and policy figure.
- Preserved Hanji 805-806 as the court-risk sequence and Hanji 810-818 as the accepted-versus-rejected frontier-policy contrast.
