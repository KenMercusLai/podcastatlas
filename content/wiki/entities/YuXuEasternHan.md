## Overview

虞诩 / Yu Xu is an Eastern Han official whose current wiki profile now begins with strategic frontier remonstrance and county-level pacification before extending into small-force Wudu command, Shun-era purge enforcement, court-risk courage, personnel recommendation, frontier restoration, and taxation dissent. Hanji 784 shows him blocking a plan to abandon [[LiangzhouLateHan|凉州]] and then pacifying [[ZhaogeCountyLateHan|朝歌县]] through [[InformationAsymmetricPacification|information-asymmetric methods]]; Hanji 788 makes him a Qiang-front tactician; Hanji 805-806 show his court-risk role under [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]]; Hanji 810 and Hanji 818 show accepted and rejected policy advice.

## Current Profile

Hanji 784 supplies Yu Xu's earliest current layer. When [[PangCanLateHan|庞参]] and [[DengZhiLateHan|邓骘]] support abandoning Liangzhou, Yu Xu warns Zhang Yu that the province is a western shield for [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]], [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]], and imperial tombs, and that abandoning it would alienate loyal frontier populations while strengthening Qiang and Hu pressure. His alternative is not simple generosity: he proposes bringing Liangzhou elite and official sons into capital offices, a reward that also works as quiet hostage control.

Deng Zhi then turns Yu Xu's success against him by appointing him magistrate of Zhaoge during Ning Ji's rebellion. Yu Xu treats the dangerous post as an opportunity, diagnoses the rebels as brave but strategically shallow because they have not seized [[Aocang|Aocang]], weapons, or cities, and uses local offenders as pardoned infiltrators to lure rebels down from the mountains. The follow-up colored-thread marking of rebel clothing lets officials identify targets in the market and turns local social knowledge into coercive state capacity.

Hanji 788 then gives the better-known Wudu tactical layer. Yu Xu argues that infantry-heavy Han forces cannot reliably chase mounted Qiang enemies, so local soldiers should return to farming while money is pooled for horses. [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]] implements the light-cavalry advice, and [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]] later appoints Yu Xu [[WuduCommanderyLateHan|Wudu]] taishou. With a small force, Yu Xu uses false delay, [[IncreasingStovesDeception|increasing stoves]], hidden strong crossbows, fake reinforcements, night attacks, and a withdrawal ambush at [[ChitingDefenseLateHan|Chiting]].

Hanji 805 places Yu Xu in Shun's restoration settlement as sili xiaowei. He impeaches Yan-connected ministers and older palace eunuchs, then survives Three Excellencies criticism by defending law as public restraint and accusing office culture of rewarding compliant self-protection. Hanji 806 supplies the court-access layer: Yu Xu repeatedly memorializes against [[ZhangFangEunuchLateHan|Zhang Fang]], is punished after Zhang uses palace access, refuses quiet suicide, and is rescued when friends, students, [[SunChengLateHan|Sun Cheng]], and [[GaoFanLateHan|Gao Fan]] reopen the case. After release, he recommends [[ZuoXiongLateHan|Zuo Xiong]] as a loyal direct official.

Hanji 810 and Hanji 818 show the policy adviser after these crisis layers. In Hanji 810, Yu Xu argues that Anding, Beidi, and Shangjun should be restored because their terrain protects Chang'an and imperial tombs while supporting pasture, irrigation, transport, and cultivation; Shun accepts. In Hanji 818, Yu Xu opposes taxing submitted [[WulingManLateHan|Wuling Man]] groups like ordinary Han households, warning that added revenue will not cover rebellion costs; Shun rejects the advice and Wuling later erupts.

## Key Characteristics

- Strategic frontier adviser who prices Liangzhou and later Anding/Beidi/Shangjun through shield, population, logistics, pasture, and imperial-tomb protection.
- Local pacification operator who diagnoses rebel limits and converts offenders, hidden identifiers, and markets into an information advantage.
- Frontier tactician who matches enemy mobility through cavalry advice and then compensates for small numbers through deception, crossbow timing, and ambush.
- Post-restoration purge enforcer who targets Yan-connected ministers and older non-restoration eunuchs.
- Anti-eunuch accuser and public-witness figure who refuses to let Zhang Fang's retaliation become an unseen prison death.
- Ally-network beneficiary and talent recommender whose rescue leads into Zuo Xiong's recommendation.
- Frontier-policy adviser whose judgment ranges from accepted commandery restoration to rejected Wuling tax-stability warning.

## Qualifications

This page synthesizes six source notes, not a complete Yu Xu biography. Hanji 784 and Hanji 788 give military and local-governance layers before the later Shun-era court material; Hanji 805 and Hanji 806 show him as a powerful correction instrument who can also become a target; Hanji 810 and Hanji 818 show that the same frontier-policy mind can be accepted in one problem and ignored in another. The source notes sometimes write 于许; this page preserves the durable route 虞诩 while keeping transcript normalization explicit.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 784 as Yu Xu's new earliest current layer: Liangzhou anti-abandonment counsel, elite-hostage policy, and Zhaoge pacification.
- Reframed Yu Xu as a strategic-frontier and local-information operator before his Wudu tactical profile.
- Preserved Hanji 805-806 as the court-risk sequence and Hanji 810-818 as the accepted-versus-rejected frontier-policy contrast.
