# Francis Throckmorton

Francis Throckmorton appears in [[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] as the English Catholic gentleman whose movements and correspondence make the [[ThrockmortonPlot]] visible to [[FrancisWalsingham]]. The episode presents him as well connected but financially vulnerable after his father's death and fine, which made him a plausible link between [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]], Catholic contacts in [[England]], and conspirators in [[France]].

Throckmorton was arrested on 7 November 1583 while encoding a letter to Mary. Under torture in the Tower he confessed and implicated [[HenryDukeOfGuise|Henry, Duke of Guise]], [[MichelDeCastelnau]], [[BernardinoDeMendoza]], and Mary, then retracted the confession at trial as coerced.

His execution at Tyburn on 10 July 1584 did not end the Mary problem for Elizabethan ministers. In the episode, Throckmorton's case demonstrates how [[ConfessionalInvasionConspiracy]] and intelligence work could reveal danger without yet giving [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]] a clean legal path to execute Mary.
