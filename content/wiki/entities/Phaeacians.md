# Phaeacians

## Overview

The Phaeacians are the idealized island society that receives [[Odysseus]] before his return to [[Ithaca]] in [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]].

## Current Profile

In the episode, the Phaeacians provide the positive hospitality counterpoint to Calypso's captivity and the suitors' abuse. Nausicaa first gives Odysseus a route into the community, Alcinous' court offers abundance and song, and the society's miraculous ships carry Odysseus home while he sleeps.

## Key Characteristics

- The Phaeacians are presented as an ideal island society of hospitality, abundance, wise rule, and technological marvel.
- Their court creates the self-aware narrative frame where Odysseus hears a song about Troy and then tells his own story.
- Their hospitality restores Odysseus enough for return without making them the final home.
- Their self-sailing ships make mythic technology part of the poem's fantasy texture.

- Transport home: [[688-the-odyssey-return-of-the-king-part-2-glt2882541084]] says Alcinous sends Odysseus to Ithaca on a Phaeacian ship while he sleeps.
