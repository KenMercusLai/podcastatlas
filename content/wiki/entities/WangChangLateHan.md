# 王昌 / Wang Chang (late Han)

王昌 / Wang Chang (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-986-tianyu-qiwang-bixian-lingqi-kuang-lvlvu057-tnz3hal6oodhydvzvbd|Hanji 986]] as the huben warrior [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] sends to pursue [[HuangfuLiLateHan|皇甫郦]] after Huangfu Li reports Li Jue's refusal to obey [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s mediation.

The episode makes Wang Chang a small restraint case inside a violent command chain. He recognizes Huangfu Li's loyalty and uprightness, lets him escape, and tells Li Jue he failed to catch him. His action does not repair the court crisis, but it keeps one truthful envoy alive.
