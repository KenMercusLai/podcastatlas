# Ken Rubenstein

Ken Rubenstein appears in [[the-invention-invention]] as one of the lawyers who helped create the [[MPEGPatentPool]]. The source describes him as one of the pool's founding figures and says his job was to make international standards collaboration work without violating American antitrust law.

Rubenstein's source role centers on [[EssentialPatents]] screening. He became an umpire over which patents were necessary to the [[MPEG]] standard and which were not, helping turn the pool into a structure based on complementary necessities rather than a club for market control.
