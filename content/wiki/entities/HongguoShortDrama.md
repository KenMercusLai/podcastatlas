# Hongguo Short Drama / 红果短剧

## Overview

Hongguo Short Drama / 红果短剧 is a [[ByteDance|字节跳动]] free short-drama destination that combines serialized viewing, advertising, recommendation, creator supply, and traffic distribution inside the company's consumer-app ecosystem.

## Current Profile

One episode reports 430 million monthly active users in September and describes rapid growth from roughly 10 million to 400 million users in less than three years. A later discussion explains the operating model: free viewing lowers the user payment barrier, advertising supports monetization, creator revenue sharing expands supply, and paid traffic can influence which works scale. Short video can provide discovery while Hongguo carries longer serialized viewing, but this integrated funnel also makes creator returns and content diversity dependent on platform rules.

## Key Characteristics

- Large reported monthly audience within ByteDance's consumer ecosystem.
- Destination product for serialized short-drama viewing.
- Free, advertising-supported access paired with creator revenue sharing.
- Traffic relationship with short-video platforms rather than simple audience substitution.
- Platform position spanning discovery, distribution, monetization, and increasingly AI-enabled supply.
- Scale achieved in a market where many smaller apps and mini-programs have short operating lives.

## Qualifications

The audience figures and ranking are episode-reported market-research claims, not platform disclosures reviewed here. Monthly active users do not reveal unique viewers across ByteDance products, retention, viewing time, acquisition cost, revenue, or profitability. Neither source provides contracts, payout data, ad-load data, ranking audits, or causal evidence isolating cross-promotion, paid traffic, or AI supply effects. Claims about creator surplus and content homogeneity remain reasoned concerns rather than measured outcomes.
