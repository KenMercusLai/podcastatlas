# Hongguo Short Drama / 红果短剧

## Overview

Hongguo Short Drama / 红果短剧 is a [[ByteDance|字节跳动]] short-drama application presented by the source as a very large destination-viewing product within the company's consumer-app portfolio.

## Current Profile

The episode reports 430 million monthly active users in September and says Hongguo grew from roughly 10 million to 400 million users in less than three years. Its strategic position is described as complementary to short-video platforms: short video can supply discovery and traffic, while Hongguo carries longer serialized viewing. This scale signal does not by itself establish retention, unique audience, profitability, or how much growth came from paid or internal traffic allocation.

## Key Characteristics

- Large reported monthly audience within ByteDance's consumer ecosystem.
- Destination product for serialized short-drama viewing.
- Traffic relationship with short-video platforms rather than simple audience substitution.
- Scale achieved in a market where many smaller apps and mini-programs have short operating lives.

## Qualifications

The audience figures and ranking are episode-reported market-research claims, not platform disclosures reviewed here. Monthly active users do not reveal unique viewers across ByteDance products, retention, viewing time, acquisition cost, revenue, or profitability. The source describes traffic complementarity but does not isolate causal cross-promotion effects.
