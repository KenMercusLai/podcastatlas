## Overview

魏绾 / Wei Wan is a Western Han official whose current profile moves from cautious service around the crown prince and Seven States military credit through promotion under [[HanJingdi|汉景帝]] to a later chancellorship and ideological personnel screen in the early [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] court.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-249-dongzhongshu-wo-bushi-rujia-de-zuiren-livm-uxnbsm0mkbrm-lvnozqnail|Hanji 249]] places Wei Wan at a transition in court ideology. He reportedly asks that recommended worthies who study Shen Buhai, [[HanFei|韩非]], [[SuQin|苏秦]], or [[ZhangYi|张仪]] and use those teachings to disturb government be dismissed, and Wudi approves the proposal.

The episode then places this act beside [[DongZhongshu|董仲舒]]'s policy answers and later popular memory of “罢黜百家，独尊儒术.” The current profile keeps their roles distinct: Wei Wan supplies the explicit personnel-screening proposal in this source, while Dong supplies a broader Confucian policy argument. Wei Wan is removed from the chancellorship later in the same episode, but no causal link between the two events is established.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-244-beiwu-yu-sao-sitong-zhibuyi-weisha-bubianjie-1-lpqwsteakynxl2ms1vypqay1w5r5|Hanji 244]] supplies the earlier office transition: after Liu She is removed, Jingdi promotes Wei Wan from 御史大夫 to chancellor. The source does not describe his policy role in that appointment scene, so it establishes chronology rather than an explanation for selection.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-236-qiguo-zhiluan-pingding-hanjingdi-shafengle-2-lisudlhjt_yawpyv4i8chvxl37cr|Hanji 236 part 2]] backfills the earlier career. It says Wei Wan had served Emperor Wen as 中郎将, repeatedly declined the crown prince's private banquets by claiming illness, received Wen's deathbed recommendation, and was trusted by Jingdi. His appointment as 中尉 after the rebellion is linked in the episode to service against the Wu-Chu force, restrained conduct, and inherited recommendation; the page preserves that three-part explanation as source-attributed rather than treating it as a complete appointment record.

## Key Characteristics

- Promoted from 御史大夫 to chancellor under Han Jingdi and retained into the early Wudi court.
- Proposes an ideological screen for recommended candidates.
- Names Shen Buhai, Han Fei, Su Qin, and Zhang Yi traditions as grounds for exclusion when used to disrupt government.
- Receives imperial approval for the proposal in the episode's account.
- Is later removed from office without an established connection to the approved measure.
- Earlier court profile joins refusal of private crown-prince access, Emperor Wen's recommendation, Jingdi's trust, and Seven States military service.

## Qualifications

All three sources are short popular-history episodes. They do not independently establish the appointments' exact dates and titles or the exclusion proposal's exact text, scope, implementation, and relationship to Dong Zhongshu's policy answers. Exclusion from recommendation is not equivalent to the disappearance of the named traditions. Hanji 236 part 2's “魏婉” is normalized to the existing Wei Wan identity, but its banquet refusals, deathbed recommendation, military role, and three-part appointment explanation remain episode-attributed.

## What Changed

- Backfilled Wei Wan's pre-chancellorship reputation through restrained access to the crown prince and Emperor Wen's recommendation.
- Added Seven States service and appointment as 中尉 while keeping the episode's causal explanation bounded.
- Preserved the existing separation between Wei Wan's exclusion proposal and Dong Zhongshu's broader ideological role.

## Relationships

- [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] - ruler who approves Wei Wan's proposal in the episode's account.
- [[HanJingdi|汉景帝]] - predecessor who promotes Wei Wan to the chancellorship in Hanji 244.
- [[RebellionOfTheSevenStatesWesternHan|七国之乱]] - military crisis in which the episode credits Wei Wan with service before his appointment as 中尉.
- [[DongZhongshu|董仲舒]] - neighboring but distinct contributor to the source's Confucian-turn narrative.
- [[HanFei|韩非]] - named intellectual tradition targeted by the proposed personnel screen.
