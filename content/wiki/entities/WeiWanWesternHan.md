## Overview

魏绾 / Wei Wan is the Western Han chancellor to whom Hanji 249 attributes a proposal excluding candidates associated with selected Legalist and diplomatic traditions from the early [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] court.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-249-dongzhongshu-wo-bushi-rujia-de-zuiren-livm-uxnbsm0mkbrm-lvnozqnail|Hanji 249]] places Wei Wan at a transition in court ideology. He reportedly asks that recommended worthies who study Shen Buhai, [[HanFei|韩非]], [[SuQin|苏秦]], or [[ZhangYi|张仪]] and use those teachings to disturb government be dismissed, and Wudi approves the proposal.

The episode then places this act beside [[DongZhongshu|董仲舒]]'s policy answers and later popular memory of “罢黜百家，独尊儒术.” The current profile keeps their roles distinct: Wei Wan supplies the explicit personnel-screening proposal in this source, while Dong supplies a broader Confucian policy argument. Wei Wan is removed from the chancellorship later in the same episode, but no causal link between the two events is established.

## Key Characteristics

- Chancellor in the early Wudi court.
- Proposes an ideological screen for recommended candidates.
- Names Shen Buhai, Han Fei, Su Qin, and Zhang Yi traditions as grounds for exclusion when used to disrupt government.
- Receives imperial approval for the proposal in the episode's account.
- Is later removed from office without an established connection to the approved measure.

## Evidence

Ideological personnel screen:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-249-dongzhongshu-wo-bushi-rujia-de-zuiren-livm-uxnbsm0mkbrm-lvnozqnail|Hanji 249]] attributes the exclusion proposal to Wei Wan and says Wudi approves it.

Office transition:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-249-dongzhongshu-wo-bushi-rujia-de-zuiren-livm-uxnbsm0mkbrm-lvnozqnail|Hanji 249]] later records Wei Wan's removal before Dou Ying becomes chancellor.

## Qualifications

The sole source is a short popular-history episode. It does not independently establish the proposal's exact text, scope, implementation, chronology, or relationship to Dong Zhongshu's policy answers. Exclusion from recommendation is not equivalent to the disappearance of the named traditions, and the source does not show that Wei Wan's later removal resulted from this policy.

## What Changed

- Created a bounded profile separating Wei Wan's exclusion proposal from Dong Zhongshu's broader ideological role.

## Relationships

- [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] - ruler who approves Wei Wan's proposal in the episode's account.
- [[DongZhongshu|董仲舒]] - neighboring but distinct contributor to the source's Confucian-turn narrative.
- [[HanFei|韩非]] - named intellectual tradition targeted by the proposed personnel screen.
- [[OuterConfucianInnerLegalistGovernance]] - later governance pattern that qualifies any claim of complete Legalist disappearance.
