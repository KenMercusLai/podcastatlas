# Cambodia / 柬埔寨

Cambodia / 柬埔寨 enters [[ep267-tafang-meigonghe-yuji-hunfeiyi-yu-shuili-diguo-lkq2vxaatn4wxue7y5q1fc3-8yfk]] through [[Angkor|吴哥]], [[TonleSapLake|洞里萨湖]], and the later discussion of water-management fragility. The source treats Cambodia as the clearest case for how a tropical landscape can support a large imperial center when hydrology, disease adaptation, agriculture, labor, and temple politics line up.

The episode also uses Cambodia as a warning against simple water-control confidence. It mentions [[KhmerEmpire|吴哥王朝]]'s water-managed abundance, but also later social rupture and poorly grounded canal-building under the Khmer Rouge period as examples of how agricultural ideology can fail when hydraulic knowledge and ecological judgment are damaged.

[[for-bucks-sake-the-rise-of-self-made-billionaires-6a61e076562e3ad886d54c95]] adds a contemporary organized-fraud branch. [[SuLinWong]] describes Cambodia as a hub for scam compounds where casino-linked sites, political protection, sanctions, extradition pressure, tourism damage, and stranded lower-level workers all interact. The source turns Cambodia into the country case for [[ScamCompoundPoliticalProtection]].
