# 现场！现场！ / Xianchang Xianchang

现场！现场！ / Xianchang Xianchang is the reporting-focused [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] series context for [[xianchang-xianchang-2025-nianzhong-teji-sanlian-nvjizhe-jiemi-xinwen-beihou-de-ku-yu-guang-loz-sggelhx166n2me8v6d8k9a3c]]. In this source, [[WuLiwei|吴立伟]] describes the series as having updated 22 episodes over the year and as experimenting with synchronized audio and video podcast production through 三联 channels.

The year-end episode defines the series less by format than by a reporting promise: it brings [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] reporters back to the scene behind published work. The discussion turns "现场" into a method across physical fieldwork, remote interviews, translation, source trust, local volunteers, embodied emotion, and audience feedback.
