# George Shiras

## Overview

George Shiras is the early wildlife photographer used by Episode 230 as an example of technology revealing nocturnal animal life and allowing animals to trigger photographs without a photographer at the camera.

## Current Profile

The source places Shiras in the 1890s and credits him with flash and trigger arrangements that let wildlife activate the shutter. His importance in the episode is methodological: the camera becomes a remote observing system capable of recording night and animal presence beyond ordinary human viewing.

## Key Characteristics

- Associated with 1890s wildlife photography.
- Used flash to make nocturnal animals photographically visible.
- Used triggers that allowed animals to initiate exposure.
- Represents discovery-oriented rather than merely optimizing camera technology.
