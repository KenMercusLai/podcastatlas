# Edgar Cayce

## Overview

Edgar Cayce is presented in this source as the "sleeping prophet" who moved [[Atlantis]] into psychic, technological, and esoteric territory. His role is not archaeological evidence; it is a case of Atlantis becoming hidden-knowledge spirituality.

## Current Profile

The episode's Cayce is a bridge between Atlantis and modern occult technology claims. He is said to have given visions and diagnoses while asleep, then to have described Atlanteans through racial categories, crystal energy, the Bermuda Triangle, and a failed rediscovery prediction.

For the wiki, Cayce matters because his Atlantis branch shows how pseudohistory can shift from origin stories into speculative technology and prophecy. Atlantis becomes not only a lost place but an imagined storehouse of future recoverable power.

## Key Characteristics

- Cayce's authority in the source comes from trance and prophecy claims rather than documents, excavation, or textual criticism.
- His Atlantis story makes crystals and hidden energy central to the myth.
- The source treats his Bermuda Triangle and 1958 rediscovery claims as examples of failed esoteric specificity.
- Cayce extends Atlantis reception from racial-origin and diffusion stories into New Age-style technology and healing imagination.

## Evidence

- Esoteric authority style: [[315-atlantis-legacy-of-the-lost-empire-part-2-glt2807981945]] describes Cayce as the sleeping prophet who made claims from sleep-like states.
- Atlantis technology claims: [[315-atlantis-legacy-of-the-lost-empire-part-2-glt2807981945]] reports his crystal-energy, Bermuda Triangle, and future rediscovery claims as part of Atlantis reception.
- Pseudohistory function: [[315-atlantis-legacy-of-the-lost-empire-part-2-glt2807981945]] places Cayce after nationalist and racial Atlantis variants, showing a shift toward hidden technology and prophecy.

## Qualifications

The page records the episode's account of Cayce, not an independent biography or validation of psychic claims. The source's use is cultural: Cayce shows Atlantis becoming an esoteric technology myth.
