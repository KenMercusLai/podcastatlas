## Overview

邓鸿 / Deng Hong is a Deng-family figure in Hanji 788 whose death becomes a test of posthumous honor management under [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]]. The source presents him as frugal, interested in the Ouyang interpretation of the Shangshu, and once involved in teaching [[EmperorAnOfHan|汉安帝]] inside the palace.

## Current Profile

Hanji 788 identifies Deng Hong as a former huben zhonglangjiang. His profile in the source is brief but pointed: he is remembered for frugality, classical study, and teaching Emperor An. After he dies, officials propose high posthumous military honor and an added Xiping marquisate.

Empress Dowager Deng does not follow the maximal honor proposal. The source says she respects Deng Hong's usual preference and refuses added office, title, and clothing, while still granting substantial money and cloth. [[DengZhiLateHan|Deng Zhi]] and other brothers refuse the grant, and Deng Hong's son is later enfeoffed as Xiping Hou with a more restrained funeral arrangement than officials had sought.

## Key Characteristics

- Deng-family member whose source profile emphasizes frugality rather than conspicuous privilege.
- Former palace military official connected to Emperor An's education.
- Classical-study figure associated with the Ouyang interpretation of the Shangshu.
- Posthumous honor case where Deng-family status, restraint, grants, and refusal appear together.
- Source-bounded figure whose son-enfeoffment is recorded without creating a separate durable child page.
