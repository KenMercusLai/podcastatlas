# Zhou Xinian / 周希年

Zhou Xinian / 周希年 appears in [[vol-269-xiao-lishi-bu-yao-pa-shi-jishu-xing-tiaozheng-1004793119]] as [[DairyFarmInternational|牛奶公司]]'s board chair during the [[HongkongLandDairyFarmTakeover|置地饮牛奶]] fight. The source says he and [[PhilOram]] were alerted shortly before [[HongkongLand|香港置地]] announced its offer.

The episode uses Zhou's position to show Dairy Farm's governance weakness. Even with a chair and prominent board network, Dairy Farm had dispersed shareholders and no obvious controlling block that could stop paper-shareholders from accepting Hongkong Land's exchange offer.
