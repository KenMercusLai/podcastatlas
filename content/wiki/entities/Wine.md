# Wine / 葡萄酒

[[sanlian-chuantai-shen-ci-gei-ren-putao-shijie-weixun-le-585125156]] adds wine / 葡萄酒 as an agricultural drink, sensory system, and cultural object. The episode's central move is to pull wine away from pure luxury signaling: one bottle can contain grape variety, soil, climate, weather, farming, fermentation, winemaker decisions, market fashion, memory, and changing taste. That makes wine a case for [[WineAsAgriculturalCulture]], [[WineTerroir]], [[TasteTraining]], [[NaturalWine]], and [[FlavorAsSelfKnowledge]].

The wiki already touched wine commercially through [[CultureWineCompany]], where premium South African wine becomes a [[CategoryCreation]] and relationship-led distribution problem. This episode adds the consumer and cultural side: people often approach wine through brands, gifts, restaurant pairing, comics, films, blind tasting, or relaxed low-intervention styles, but the source insists that wine knowledge should eventually serve the drinker's own palate.

## Key Claims
- Wine can be read as a material history object because agriculture, transport, production, vocabulary, and social status are all present in the bottle.
- Wine education becomes less intimidating when it starts from comparison and memory rather than from memorizing prestige hierarchies.
- The same wine region or estate can change over time because climate, technique, market preference, and human succession are not fixed.
- Wine's cultural works, including [[DropsOfGod|《神之水滴》]], [[SidewaysFilm|《杯酒人生》]], [[SOMDocumentary|SOM]], and [[SourGrapesDocumentary|《酸葡萄》]], show that wine operates through stories, self-projection, training, and authenticity anxiety.
