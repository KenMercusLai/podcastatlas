# Parti Québécois

## Overview
The Parti Québécois is presented as the separatist party elected to lead Quebec’s regional government in the episode’s Canada unity test.

## Current Profile
The party represents Quebec’s culturally grounded independence movement, centered on French language, identity, and civil-law difference rather than Alberta-style resource grievance. Because the source places current independence support near 30%, it argues that the party would need to expand support before a referendum, potentially by escalating disputes with Ottawa.

## Key Characteristics
- Advocates Quebec independence in the episode’s account.
- Draws on French-language, cultural, and legal distinctiveness.
- Governs in a context where reported independence support remains a minority position.
- May use conflict with the federal government to change referendum conditions.
