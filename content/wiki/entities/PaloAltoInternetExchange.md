# Palo Alto Internet Exchange

Palo Alto Internet Exchange, or PAIX, appears in [[tech-20260127-0127-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260127-0127-mp-tech-pod-128]] as an early internet exchange point located in the Palo Alto building now operated by [[Equinix]]. The episode identifies a first PAIX cage from 1996 and says the model helped pioneer the modern colocation business.

The source's key claim is that PAIX made internet growth more direct by letting many networks connect in a neutral location. For the wiki, PAIX is therefore a historical bridge from telecom switchboards and early search to [[ColocationDataCenter]], [[NeutralInternetExchange]], cloud infrastructure, and AI data traffic.

[[tech-20260128-0128-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260128-0128-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds [[PaulVixie]] as the human bridge between PAIX and the dot-com fiber boom. The episode uses his view of [[DarkFiber]] to show that exchange points and fiber capacity both belong to a longer [[TechnologyInstallationCycle]]: infrastructure may be overbuilt for immediate demand while still enabling later networked services.
