# American Society of Travel Advisers

The American Society of Travel Advisers appears in [[its-not-easy-being-green-zack-polanski-6a82d1a0ff328abd84724cf0]] as a membership signal for the survival of human travel advice. [[CaitlinTalbot]] says the association has more than 310,000 agent members, up from nearly 200,000 in 2024.

The source uses the organization to qualify replacement narratives around [[AITravelPlanning]]. Travel advisers may use AI for administrative work, but the segment argues that human advisers still sell destination knowledge, taste, supplier relationships, crisis support, and confidence for high-stakes trips.
