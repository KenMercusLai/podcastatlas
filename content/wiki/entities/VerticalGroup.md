# Vertical Group

Vertical Group is the sustainability consulting firm named in [[tech-20260420-0420-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260420-0420-mp-tech-pod-128]]. The source says its founder, identified as Shula, helped ensure that [[MalibuHighSchool]]'s materials met sustainability requirements.

The firm's role is to make [[EmbodiedCarbonBuildingCode]] operational: instead of treating climate impact as a general aspiration, it reviews specific building components such as roof, floor, steel, walls, paint, and insulation. That links consulting work to [[ConstructionCarbonCompliance]] and to the availability of [[LowCarbonConstructionMaterials]].
