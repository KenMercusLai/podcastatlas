# Valois Dynasty

## Overview

The Valois Dynasty appears as the French royal line selected after the direct [[CapetianDynasty|Capetian]] male line ends, making [[PhilipVIOfFrance|Philip VI]] the French alternative to [[EdwardIIIOfEngland|Edward III]]'s female-line claim.

## Current Profile

The current source treats Valois less as a full dynastic profile than as the chosen political solution to the 1328 succession crisis. When Charles IV dies and his widow gives birth to a daughter, the French reject Edward III's claim through [[IsabellaOfFrance|Isabella]] and choose Philip of Valois.

This choice creates a durable legitimacy dispute. The episode stresses that female-line exclusion is not yet settled law in the way later Salic-law language can imply, so Valois accession becomes one source of the [[HundredYearsWar|Hundred Years' War]] rather than a neutral procedural outcome.

## Key Characteristics

- Collateral French royal line chosen after direct Capetian male succession fails.
- Represented in the episode through Philip VI's accession.
- Political answer to Edward III's maternal Capetian claim.
- Bound to the claim that French royal succession cannot pass through women.
- Part of the legitimacy field that makes Anglo-French war possible.
- Source-bounded to the 1328 succession and 1337 war-opening context.

## Evidence

- Accession context: [[318-hundred-years-war-a-game-of-thrones-part-1-glt5584156008]] says Philip of Valois becomes Philip VI after Charles IV's widow bears a daughter.
- Claim rejection: [[318-hundred-years-war-a-game-of-thrones-part-1-glt5584156008]] says Edward III's claim is rejected because it comes through a woman.
- Legal uncertainty: [[318-hundred-years-war-a-game-of-thrones-part-1-glt5584156008]] says that exclusion was not yet fully settled law.
- War connection: [[318-hundred-years-war-a-game-of-thrones-part-1-glt5584156008]] presents the Valois choice as one cause among several behind the Hundred Years' War.
- French continuity: [[318-hundred-years-war-a-game-of-thrones-part-1-glt5584156008]] places Philip VI inside a powerful French monarchy that cannot easily concede Edward's claim.
