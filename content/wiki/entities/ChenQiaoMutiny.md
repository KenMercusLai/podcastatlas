# 陈桥兵变 / Chenqiao Mutiny

Chenqiao Mutiny / 陈桥兵变 is the founding coup of the [[NorthernSong|北宋]] in [[169-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-shang-957052909]]. The episode describes a reported northern border emergency, [[ZhaoKuangyin|赵匡胤]] leading troops out from [[Kaifeng|开封]], omen talk around two suns, soldiers demanding a new ruler, and the famous 黄袍加身 scene.

The source treats the event as staged or at least heavily prepared. [[ShiShouxin|石守信]]'s control of the capital gate, the ready abdication text, and the immediate pressure on Later Zhou ministers make "being forced by the army" look like a necessary political script rather than a full explanation.

Chenqiao also becomes the root problem that [[SongMilitaryCentralization|宋初军事集权]] tries to solve. Zhao Kuangyin gained power through military command; after gaining it, he had to prevent other commanders from repeating the same move.
