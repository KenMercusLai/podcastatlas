# Sarah LaFleur

Sarah LaFleur is the founder of [[MMLaFleur]] and a returning [[HowIBuiltThis]] Advice Line advisor in [[advice-line-strategy-sessions-4962a0d6-36bc-4797-8002-3bd8b748a89a]]. In the mashup episode, she advises [[MonicaStoney]] of [[TressLondon]] on turning a premium kidswear product into a more legible lifestyle and community proposition.

Her advice emphasizes that a small premium brand should not treat social-media exposure as the same thing as trust. She pushes Monica toward [[NeighborhoodFirstWordOfMouth]], putting clothes on stylish connected children, and using content to make slower consumption, garment care, and capsule wardrobes feel desirable rather than austere.
