# 韩广 / Han Guang

韩广 / Han Guang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-8-yi-ming-zhaoguo-chuishibing-nixi-chengwei-waijiao-dachen-lhfwk-hjge5iuqet6astmbclhyri|Qinji 128-8]] as [[WuChen|武臣]]'s general sent to take old [[YanState|燕国]] territory after Wu Chen has become king of [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. Yan-region notables instead make Han Guang their own king.

The source frames his kingship as both opportunistic and hostage-constrained. Han Guang initially refuses because his mother remains in [[Handan|邯郸]], but local advisers argue that Zhao is boxed in by [[QinState|秦国]] to the west and [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]] to the south and therefore will not risk harming her. Zhao later sends his mother and family safely to Yan.

Han Guang's second source role is the captive crisis around Wu Chen. After Yan captures Wu Chen and demands land, several Zhao envoys are killed. [[ZhaoServantEnvoy|赵厮养卒]] then persuades Yan that killing Wu Chen would give [[ZhangEr|张耳]] and [[ChenYu|陈馀]] a righteous pretext to attack Yan, and Han Guang releases him.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-143-xiangyu-shi-ruhe-feijin-xinji-nongsi-chuhuaiwang-mixin-lstfzfvbd2h9i39f7y5dxrbxxwv2|Hanji 143]] gives Han Guang's settlement demotion. [[XiangYu|项羽]] moves him from Yan king to Liaodong king, while [[ZangTu|臧荼]] becomes Yan king because he followed the Chu rescue-Zhao and westward-entry line. Han Guang's page therefore now shows how the anti-Qin title gained in Qinji 128-8 can be overridden by Xiang Yu's military-participation standard.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-144-xiangyu-jituan-fumie-de-yuanyin-1-lr0ft8-jupxgvutn6c89mfs8r4wb|Hanji 144]] uses Han Guang as an explicit resentment case. The episode argues that rewarding Zang Tu by giving him Yan while pushing Han Guang to remote Liaodong shows why Xiang Yu's settlement produced [[RewardAllocationBacklash|分配反噬]]: the earlier king sees not just a smaller share, but a humiliation by one of his own campaign generals.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-148-2-peng-le-wangling-muqin-xiangyu-sharen-zhuxin-ljyv8bow4rj1l5ths3znx2evgvtm|Hanji 148-2]] gives the violent endpoint of that Yan resentment. Han Guang refuses to leave Ji for Liaodong, while [[ZangTu|臧荼]] no longer preserves the old ruler relationship; Zang Tu attacks, kills Han Guang, and settles the Liaodong branch by force. The source uses the event as one of several fires that keep [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s new order unstable while [[LiuBang|刘邦]] consolidates in Guanzhong.
