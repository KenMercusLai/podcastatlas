# Daewoo

Daewoo appears in [[getting-entrepreneurial-in-korea-summer-school]] as the South Korean company that partnered with [[AbdulMajidChowdhury]] to train Bangladeshi workers and managers for export-oriented garment production. The source links Daewoo's incentives to the [[MultiFiberArrangement]]: because Korean textile exports faced quota limits, production through [[Bangladesh]] created a way to keep serving export demand.

The source's Daewoo branch is important because it turns development transfer into a concrete firm-to-firm mechanism. [[TextileKnowledgeTransfer]] moves through training, factory routines, and cross-cultural adjustment, not only through abstract capital or policy advice.
