## Current Profile
The episode identifies 孙老师's subspecialty as pacing and electrophysiology, including pacemakers, [[ImplantableCardioverterDefibrillator|ICDs]], cardiac resynchronization therapy, and ablation for arrhythmias. The guest uses clinical cases and pump, engine, house, plumbing, door, wall, and electrical-circuit analogies to explain why different cardiac failures require different interventions.

The guest's practical position is selective rather than device-promotional: healthy people do not need an ICD simply to insure themselves against sudden death, but patients assessed as high risk should not infer safety from feeling well at a particular moment. That position anchors [[SuddenCardiacDeathRiskStratification]] and keeps implantation, upgrades, programming, monitoring, and replacement within specialist care.
