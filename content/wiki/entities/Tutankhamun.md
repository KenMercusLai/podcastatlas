# Tutankhamun

## Overview

Tutankhamun was a short-lived Eighteenth Dynasty pharaoh whose modern fame rests chiefly on the survival and 1922 discovery of his richly furnished tomb.

## Current Profile

[[27-tutankhamun-glt2697164979]] presents Tutankhamun as a boy ruler who came to the throne around eight or nine and died in his late teens. Initially named Tutankhaten, he became Tutankhamun during the restoration of Amun and reversal of [[Akhenaten]]'s [[Atenism]]. His later removal from king lists may have contributed to the tomb's obscurity and exceptional survival, although that causal explanation remains inferential.

The episode distinguishes the ruler's sparse political biography from his enormous archaeological and emotional afterlife. Youth, early death, gold burial goods, [[HowardCarter]]'s discovery, press competition, and the invented curse narrative made him globally recognizable; changing readings of his mummy have not settled his parentage or cause of death.

## Key Characteristics

- Boy pharaoh associated with the transition away from Akhenaten's religious program.
- Renamed from Tutankhaten to Tutankhamun as Amun worship was restored.
- Historically obscure ruler whose intact burial created exceptional modern fame.
- Subject of unresolved arguments about family relationships, health, injury, murder, and accidental death.
- Cultural figure whose appeal joins youthful vulnerability to extraordinary royal display.

## Evidence

- Restoration and renaming - [[27-tutankhamun-glt2697164979]] connects the name change to the counter-revolution against Atenism.
- Tomb and modern fame - [[27-tutankhamun-glt2697164979]] follows Carter, Carnarvon, the Valley of the Kings search, the burial goods, and the curse story.
- Evidentiary limits - [[27-tutankhamun-glt2697164979]] explicitly leaves Tutankhamun's death and several Amarna family relationships unresolved.

## Qualifications

This profile derives from one popular-history podcast rather than a specialist Egyptological review. Ages, genealogies, medical readings, tomb-survival causes, and death theories remain source-scoped; the absence of a secure biography should not be filled with the certainty created by modern fame.

## What Changed

- Created a profile separating Tutankhamun's limited recoverable reign from his unusually large archaeological and media afterlife.

## Relationships

- [[Akhenaten]] - predecessor whose Aten-centered religious changes were reversed during Tutankhamun's reign.
- [[Nefertiti]] - major Amarna royal figure within the same uncertain dynastic field.
- [[HowardCarter]] - excavator whose discovery made Tutankhamun globally famous.
- [[Atenism]] - religious program reflected in Tutankhamun's original name and later renaming.
- [[Egyptology]] - discipline through which the tomb, mummy, chronology, and family relationships continue to be interpreted.
