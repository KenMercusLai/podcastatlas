# Stanislas Hutin

## Overview

Stanislas Hutin was a French reservist and former Jesuit seminarian whose encounter with torture during the Algerian War turned him into a long-term witness against French colonial abuses.

## Current Profile

Hutin had already rejected aspects of French colonial policy during earlier service in Madagascar, where the source says he refused to fire his machine gun, befriended prisoners, and secretly brought them bread. In north-eastern Algeria he encountered 14-year-old shepherd [[SaidBoutout|Saïd Boutout]] being tortured with electricity and protested to his lieutenant.

The short posting redirected his life. He abandoned plans for the priesthood, sought to support developing countries, preserved written and photographic evidence, joined a group of anti-war reservists formed in 2004, and spoke in schools about French atrocities. His importance in the source is not senior command or policymaking but sustained [[ColonialAtrocityWitnessing]].

## Key Characteristics

- French reservist whose Algeria service confronted him with torture by his own army.
- Former Jesuit seminarian whose religious vocation changed after the experience.
- Earlier critic of colonial conduct during service in Madagascar.
- Immediate protester when he encountered Saïd Boutout's torture.
- Photographer and writer who preserved evidence of abused prisoners.
- Anti-war speaker who carried the testimony into schools and public memory.

- Direct-witness evidence: [[coffer-stop-ai-may-shrink-tax-base-6abf81fdb06169dc7218d384]] says he found Boutout being electrically tortured and objected to his lieutenant.
- Life-change evidence: [[coffer-stop-ai-may-shrink-tax-base-6abf81fdb06169dc7218d384]] says the Algeria posting led him away from the priesthood and toward work supporting developing countries.
- Documentary evidence: [[coffer-stop-ai-may-shrink-tax-base-6abf81fdb06169dc7218d384]] attributes written testimony and photographs of harmed prisoners to Hutin.
- Public-witness evidence: [[coffer-stop-ai-may-shrink-tax-base-6abf81fdb06169dc7218d384]] connects him to anti-war reservists and repeated school talks.
