# RSF Finance

## Overview
RSF Finance appears in [[late-july-snacks-nicole-bernard-dawes-crackers-and-cookies-were-failing-tortilla-chips-saved-them-97049a31-9144-42f5-856c-9590c5e6beb4]] as the lender that agreed to take over [[LateJulySnacks]]' debt during its 2009 financing crisis.

## Current Profile
After the bank called a roughly $3.5 million equipment loan into default under a death-of-a-member clause, Nicole Bernard Dawes needed time and debt relief. The episode says Gary Hirschberg introduced her to RSF Finance, which agreed to take over the debt as a loan. That makes RSF a concrete actor in the mission-aligned rescue-capital branch.

## Key Characteristics

- Debt replacement path that helped Late July survive until the tortilla-chip pivot.
