# Sergio Marchionne

Sergio Marchionne is the executive in [[ferrari-1]] who pushed the [[Ferrari]] IPO and spinout from [[FiatChryslerAutomobiles|FCA]]. The episode frames this as part of a broader effort to reduce FCA debt after the Fiat-Chrysler combination.

Marchionne matters because his public-market plan created direct tension with [[LucaDiMontezemolo]]. The source treats that conflict as a governance problem: Ferrari's long-term value came from scarcity, but public investors and a leveraged parent could pressure growth.
