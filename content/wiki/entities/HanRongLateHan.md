# 韩融 / Han Rong (late Han)

韩融 / Han Rong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-949-lishishang-de-dongzhuo-daodi-duo-canbao-lk6idogwmc54nk1sdfc5wonyvgbb|Hanji 949]] as one of the respected figures [[DongZhuo|董卓]] brings into the court after receiving xiangguo status. The source says Han Rong is appointed da honglu alongside [[XunShuangLateHan|荀爽]]'s rapid elevation and [[ChenJiLateHan|陈纪]]'s appointment as wuguan zhonglangjiang.

The page is source-scoped because the episode does not give a full biography. Han Rong matters here as part of Dong Zhuo's attempt to borrow scholar reputation for a rule already marked by military terror in [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]]. His appointment therefore belongs to [[CoercedTalentLegitimacy|胁迫征士合法性]] rather than ordinary [[TalentAttractionThroughRespect|礼贤下士式人才吸附]].
