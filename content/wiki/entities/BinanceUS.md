## Overview
Binance.US is the U.S.-market exchange structure that [[ChangpengZhao|CZ]] says was created in 2019 as American authorities increased scrutiny of crypto companies.

## Current Profile
In the bounded account, Binance.US was intended to separate the U.S. market from global [[Binance]] through a distinct entity, deployment, matching engine, and equity structure. The episode uses that architecture as evidence that Binance recognized jurisdictional risk, but it does not independently establish how complete the operational separation was or resolve the later government allegations and settlements.

## Key Characteristics
- It was created as a U.S.-specific response to increasing regulatory scrutiny.
- CZ describes it as legally and technically separate from global Binance.
- The claimed separation included its entity, deployment, matching engine, and ownership structure.
- Its history remains intertwined with questions about U.S.-user boundaries and regulatory trust.
