# AerCap

## Overview

AerCap is presented as the world's largest aircraft lessor and as the modern company carrying forward part of [[GuinnessPeatAviation|GPA]]'s Irish leasing lineage.

## Current Profile

In the episode, AerCap's fleet of more than 1,700 aircraft demonstrates that a lessor can control a civil-aircraft portfolio larger than the operating fleets of major airlines. Its relevance is not only fleet size: a global customer network helps place aircraft after leases expire and spread exposure across regions.

## Key Characteristics

- Source-described global leader in aircraft leasing.
- Reported portfolio of more than 1,700 aircraft.
- Fleet scale used to contrast lessors with major operating airlines.
- Global customer network that supports aircraft remarketing between leases.
- Irish lineage connected to the earlier GPA industry cluster.
