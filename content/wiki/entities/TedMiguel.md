# Ted Miguel

Ted Miguel appears in [[piles-of-cash-and-a-town-of-solutions-in-kenya-nigeria-summer-school]] as the economist who evaluated free deworming medicine for schoolchildren near [[BusiaKenya|Busia]]. The episode says the study found treated children attended school more often, and a follow-up linked treatment to better jobs and higher incomes later.

His study anchors [[DewormingAsHumanCapitalInvestment]]. The case connects health treatment, school attendance, later labor-market outcomes, and [[PositiveExternality]] because one child's treatment can reduce disease transmission and improve conditions for others.
