## Overview
Nick Green is the co-founder and CEO of [[ThriveMarket]] and an adviser on a [[HowIBuiltThis]] Advice Line episode.

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Green presents company building as continuous adaptation rather than a transition to a finished operating model. His advice joins customer data, product and channel focus, repeatable economics, mission fit, and persistent execution. He treats intuition as insufficient for predicting consumer-brand success and prefers behavioral evidence such as sales, search gaps, subscriptions, retention, and referrals.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses customer behavior and search data to identify demand rather than relying primarily on founder intuition.
- Advises founders to simplify around a scalable core before broadening product, capital, or awareness.
- Treats mission and economics as potentially reinforcing rather than inherently opposed.
- Distinguishes debt, aligned equity, and organic growth by risk, dilution, discipline, and strategic support.
- Favors advocates who already use a product over generic paid reach.
- Describes entrepreneurial persistence as conviction combined with continual problem solving.
