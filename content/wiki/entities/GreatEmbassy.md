# Great Embassy / 大使团出访

## Overview

The Great Embassy was [[PeterTheGreat|Peter the Great]]'s 1697-1698 European mission combining diplomacy, recruitment, observation, and practical technical study under the nominal disguise of Peter Mikhailov.

## Current Profile

The mission sought officers, sailors, shipwrights, technical specialists, and allies against the [[OttomanEmpire]]. Peter traveled through Riga, Brandenburg, the Dutch Republic, England, Dresden, and Vienna, working in shipyards and visiting factories, laboratories, collections, Parliament, and the English Mint. His failed anonymity made the embassy theatrical, but its practical purpose was real: he wanted transferable maritime and administrative capability rather than ceremonial exposure alone.

The Streltsy revolt cut short the planned continuation to Venice. The embassy did not secure the anti-Ottoman coalition Peter wanted, yet it supplied personnel, tools, techniques, and models later folded into [[CoerciveWesternization|coercive Westernization]] and [[ModernizingAutocracy]].

## Key Characteristics

- It joined state diplomacy to a ruler's personal technical apprenticeship.
- Shipbuilding and maritime power were its strongest practical focus.
- Peter compared tacit Dutch craft knowledge with more systematic English methods.
- Recruitment converted observation into movement of specialists back to Russia.
- Political admiration abroad did not translate into limits on Russian monarchy.
- The Streltsy crisis ended the journey and linked foreign learning directly to repression at home.

## Qualifications

The page rests on one narrative podcast episode. Exact participant counts, reported remarks, incidents, recruitment totals, and the causal weight of particular visits remain source-scoped; the embassy should not be reduced either to a disguised holiday or to a complete blueprint for Peter's later reforms.

## Relationships

- [[PeterTheGreat]] - ruler who traveled as Peter Mikhailov and used the mission as technical apprenticeship.
- [[ModernizingAutocracy]] - state-building pattern into which imported knowledge and personnel were incorporated.
- [[CoerciveWesternization]] - domestic enforcement program that followed Peter's return.
- [[Streltsy]] - revolt that ended the journey and became the target of mass repression.
- [[OttomanEmpire]] - intended target of the alliance diplomacy that produced limited results.
- [[GreatNorthernWar]] - Baltic conflict toward which Peter pivoted soon after the embassy.
