# 欧哈 / Oha Guizhou Bistro

欧哈 / Oha is the Guizhou-style bistro example in [[ep245-yinian-yidu-xunwei-zhilv-jinnian-qu-chi-chuangyi-cai-li9wtavemokcckialbbj7unpmqsz]]. The episode treats it as an early visible case for [[GuizhouCuisineFlavorTransfer|贵州酸辣风味迁移]] and [[ChineseBistroLocalization|中式小酒馆本土化]] rather than as a conventional regional-restaurant chain.

Its examples show how source-place flavors can enter modern dish structures. A 包谷粑 dessert borrows the idea of a cheese-filled sweet pastry but rebuilds it around corn, sticky corn, brown-sugar cream, and soybean powder. Red sour soup moves beyond sour-soup fish or beef into wontons and meatball-like forms, showing [[RegionalFlavorTransfer|regional flavor transfer]] through a strong acid base.
