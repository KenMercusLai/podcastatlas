## Overview

韩伯 / Han Bo enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-656-zhichang-buhui-shuohua-de-ren-hen-nan-hunchutou-lpn15smz7r1glekugzwtm2tpw-tl|Hanji 656]] as a late-[[XinDynasty|新朝]] official whose report about a giant named "巨无霸" functions as satire against [[WangMang|王莽]]. His case shows that indirect ridicule can still be decoded as political attack and punished lethally.

## Current Profile

Han Bo reports that an enormous man has appeared from a mythical geography and claims to have been sent by Heaven to assist the Xin dynasty. He then asks the court to prepare oversized armor, vehicle, generals, guards, and city gates. The episode reads the absurd exactness of the report as deliberate mockery of Wang Mang's fascination with omens, prodigies, and grand symbolic staging.

The strategy fails as protection. Wang Mang outwardly renames the man from "巨无霸" to "巨无士," but privately rages, imprisons Han Bo, and has him killed on a pretext. Han Bo therefore sits between [[AllusiveRemonstrance|隐语进谏]] and [[DirectRemonstranceCriminalization|直谏入罪]]: the message is indirect, but the ruler experiences it as ridicule and makes it fatal.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Xin official whose main wiki role is a single satirical memorial.
- Allusive critic who attacks Wang Mang's omen/prodigy politics through absurd bureaucratic seriousness.
- Failed self-protector whose indirection is too legible to shield him.
- Victim of ruler retaliation after symbolic criticism is decoded as personal insult.
- Contrast case to [[FanShengXinOfficial|范生]], whose indirect route fails as policy but avoids lethal punishment.

## Qualifications
