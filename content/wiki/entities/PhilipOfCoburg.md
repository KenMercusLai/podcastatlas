# Philip of Coburg

## Overview

Philip of Coburg is the prince who arrives at Mayerling with [[JosefHoyos]] during the hunting-party setup before Rudolf and Mary die.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Philip as a supporting witness in the surface-normality phase. His presence helps show that [[CrownPrinceRudolf]] continues ordinary aristocratic hunting routines and manages appearances even while [[MaryVetsera]] is hidden in the lodge.

## Key Characteristics

- He is not presented as part of the affair's origin, death pact, or suppression strategy.
