# 蓝盈莹 / Lan Yingying

## Overview
蓝盈莹 is an actor and participant in the first season of [[SistersWhoMakeWaves|《乘风破浪的姐姐》]]. The available source uses her conduct as a case of direct competition meeting relationship-sensitive group norms.

## Current Profile
The hosts interpret Lan as openly pursuing opportunities, including stating group preferences that others handled through implicit loyalty conventions. They do not treat that conduct as inherently wrong; instead, they locate the backlash in a mismatch between explicit individual competition and expectations around tact, continuity, and collective feeling. They also argue that sustained collaboration softened her initially instrumental stance, though that reading remains interpretive.

## Key Characteristics
- Directly states preferences and competes for opportunities.
- Challenges implicit expectations that an existing team should remain intact.
- Attracts criticism partly because assertiveness is judged through relationship norms.
- Is portrayed as adapting when repeated teamwork creates genuine attachment.

## Evidence
- **Direct competition:** [[ep136-er-liao-lang-jie-zhichang-yiran-you-hen-duo-zhi-de-jiyi-de-shanguangdian-wkg5hf9t5qetspydbxyk6to0ea4442]] describes Lan openly choosing preferred group arrangements rather than following an unspoken non-separation norm.
- **Norm conflict:** [[ep136-er-liao-lang-jie-zhichang-yiran-you-hen-duo-zhi-de-jiyi-de-shanguangdian-wkg5hf9t5qetspydbxyk6to0ea4442]] explicitly frames audience discomfort as partly arising from Chinese expectations of social tact and interpersonal obligation.
- **Adaptation:** [[ep136-er-liao-lang-jie-zhichang-yiran-you-hen-duo-zhi-de-jiyi-de-shanguangdian-wkg5hf9t5qetspydbxyk6to0ea4442]] records the hosts' view that her behavior became more reflective and emotionally invested after intensive shared training and elimination pressure.

## Qualifications
The source is cultural commentary, not direct testimony from Lan or a systematic behavioral record. Claims about her initial motives, emotional change, or degree of calculation are the hosts' interpretations. Public editing may have selected the moments on which those interpretations rely.

## What Changed

- Preserved both the legitimacy of direct opportunity-seeking and the relational costs attributed to it.
