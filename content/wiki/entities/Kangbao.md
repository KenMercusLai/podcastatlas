# Kangbao

## Overview
Kangbao is the Chinese county used in [[working-through-it-the-ai-jobs-boom-dawns-6aa27a3fa6e9aef4cc7741b8]] as the setting for a cabbage-formaldehyde scandal.

## Current Profile
The source says videos from Kangbao showed truckers dipping freshly harvested cabbages into formaldehyde to keep them looking fresh. The scandal prompted panic, criminal detentions, and emergency checkpoints for cabbages leaving the county.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as the concrete case for [[ChinaFoodSafetyFragmentation]].
- Shows how transport-stage behavior can become a food-safety risk after harvest.
- Illustrates how local governments may respond quickly to visible scandals without necessarily solving deeper monitoring problems.
