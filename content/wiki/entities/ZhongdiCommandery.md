# 中地郡 / Zhongdi Commandery

中地郡 / Zhongdi Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-155-1-lishi-jizai-shenme-qingkuang-hui-fasheng-ren-chi-ren-canju-lrwz-prnxd8hzchagyrbywkux7lh|Hanji 155-1]] as one of the administrative units named after [[Feiqiu|废丘]] falls and [[LiuBang|刘邦]] fully pacifies the Yong territory.

The source lists Zhongdi together with [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]] and [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]]. Its wiki role is narrow but useful: it marks the moment when the remaining [[ThreeQinContainment|三秦压制]] space stops being only a battlefield and becomes territory that the Han side can classify and govern.

## Connections
- [[Feiqiu|废丘]] and [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] - siege endpoint that opens the administrative conversion.
- [[LiuBang|刘邦]] and [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] - ruler and regional base in the source.
- [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]] and [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]] - commanderies named beside Zhongdi.
- [[ConquestCommanderyTransition|灭国后的郡县化过渡]] - concept for converting defeated or pacified territory into commandery control.
