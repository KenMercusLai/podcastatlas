# Battle of Sekigahara / 关原合战

The Battle of Sekigahara / 关原合战 is the power-transition battle that frames [[WilliamAdams|William Adams / 三浦按针]]'s arrival in [[14-wushi-weilian-dahanghai-shidai-de-riben-he-xifang-555771285]]. The episode says Adams reaches Japan in 1600, just before [[TokugawaIeyasu|Tokugawa Ieyasu]] fights the coalition supporting Toyotomi succession.

The source mentions a possible story that European cannon from Adams's damaged ship may have helped Ieyasu, but it explicitly treats this as uncertain. The battle matters more securely as timing: Adams arrives when Ieyasu is about to become the dominant ruler, which makes access to the Tokugawa center historically consequential.

## Key Claims
- Sekigahara makes Adams's arrival politically meaningful because he reaches Japan at a regime-formation moment.
- [[KonishiYukinaga|Konishi Yukinaga]]'s defeat, execution, and later European reception show that the battle also entered Christian and European imagination.
- The source avoids making the cannon story a firm causal claim.
