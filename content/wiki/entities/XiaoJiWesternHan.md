## Overview

萧吉 / Xiao Ji enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-470-xiao-wangzhi-shasi-wode-chule-taijian-haiyou-wode-yufu-lof1c5nctali_zsy0hotqyocjgoe|Hanji 470]] as [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]]'s son, whose petition to vindicate his father becomes the immediate trigger for a renewed attack on Xiao Wangzhi.

## Current Profile

Hanji 470 presents Xiao Ji as a filial but politically incautious petitioner. His request asks the court to clear Xiao Wangzhi's earlier case, but the episode stresses that this is not a neutral appeal. Because Yuan himself ultimately authorized the earlier handling of Xiao, full vindication would make [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] face his own error and gives [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]] and [[HongGongWesternHan|弘恭]] a new opening.

## Key Characteristics

- Son of Xiao Wangzhi.
- Acts from filial vindication rather than from an effective reading of court risk.
- Triggers renewed legal and reputational pressure against his father.
- Functions in the source as a caution about petitions that corner the ruler.
