# 张承 / Zhang Cheng (late Han)

张承 / Zhang Cheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1001-weishenme-quan-ni-buyao-fanzhongnu-lolurkcjpie14nnczk0tu7iqerke|Hanji 1001]] as [[ZhangFanLateHan|张范]]'s younger brother and the person who answers [[YuanShu|袁术]] most directly. Yuan Shu lists his land, population, and soldiers, then asks whether he can emulate Qi Huan Gong's hegemony or Han Gaozu's founding.

Zhang Cheng rejects the force-first premise. His answer is that ruling the realm is "in virtue, not in strength": benevolent action can make even a small base viable, while usurpation against Heaven and the people's wishes will produce abandonment. The source makes him the clearest voice for the episode title's warning against provoking public anger.
