# Mark (Little Water Distillery)

Mark is the [[LittleWaterDistillery]] caller in [[advice-line-with-carlton-calvin-of-razor-c0de1a84-3d35-45f3-921c-6144693ad86e]]. The source does not give his surname, so this page uses a stable semantic suffix rather than the ambiguous canonical key "Mark."

His call centers on how to move from a small local reputation to broader demand for [[48BlocksEspressoMartini]]. [[GuyRaz]] and [[CarltonCalvin]] interpret his problem as a mix of [[SKUFocusUnderCashConstraint]], [[CPGDistribution]], and [[FounderVisibilityMarketing]]: tell the product story, show ingredients and process, consider trend-aligned formats such as cans, and stop letting a broad tasting-room assortment consume the products with the strongest pull.
