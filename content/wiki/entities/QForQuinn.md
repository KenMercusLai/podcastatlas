# Q for Quinn

## Overview
Q for Quinn is [[MelitaCyril]]'s bootstrapped organic-cotton and merino-wool apparel company.

## Current Profile
The company makes socks and underwear in Portugal and India and is moving toward natural-fiber athleisure. Its strategic question is not whether growth is desirable, but whether faster growth from outside capital can preserve the material mission and founder control that distinguish the brand.

## Key Characteristics
- Natural-fiber socks and underwear rooted in a family eczema need.
- Profitable, bootstrapped, mid-seven-figure-revenue business in the source's account.
- Family-operated after Cyril's husband joined full-time.
- Expanding into athleisure after promising sports-bra performance.
- Potential investment case where investor fit may matter as much as capital amount.
