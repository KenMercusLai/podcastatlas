# Anders' Army

## Overview

Anders' Army was the Polish force organized under [[WladyslawAnders|Władysław Anders]] from prisoners and displaced people in the Soviet Union, evacuated through Iran in 1942, and developed into the Second Polish Corps that fought with the Allies in Italy.

## Current Profile

The force emerged from the reversal created by Germany's June 1941 invasion of the Soviet Union. Britain pressed the Polish government-in-exile and its new Soviet ally toward agreement, Polish captives were released, and Anders organized an army amid the wider population of deported and displaced Poles. [[533-wojtek-the-bear-who-beat-the-nazis-glt8956421015]] says more than 100,000 soldiers and civilians crossed the Caspian Sea to Iran in 1942.

Reorganized as the Second Polish Corps, the force served in the [[ItalianCampaign1943|Italian campaign]] and played a decisive role in the [[BattleOfMonteCassino|Battle of Monte Cassino]]. The episode uses [[Wojtek]] and the 22nd Artillery Supply Company to show the army at human scale: a force held together not only by command and logistics but also by shared displacement, companionship, and symbols of a homeland its members might never recover.

## Key Characteristics

- Formed from Polish military captives and a wider displaced population released from Soviet control.
- Evacuated soldiers and civilians through Iran rather than remaining dependent on the Soviet war effort.
- Reorganized into the Second Polish Corps for service with the Western Allies.
- Suffered heavy losses while helping capture Monte Cassino in May 1944.
- Combined military identity with the unresolved politics and grief of national exile.

## Evidence

- Formation and evacuation: [[533-wojtek-the-bear-who-beat-the-nazis-glt8956421015]] connects Anders's release, Polish-Soviet wartime agreement, recruitment, and the 1942 movement through Iran.
- Italian service: [[533-wojtek-the-bear-who-beat-the-nazis-glt8956421015]] follows the Second Polish Corps through Monte Cassino and the later advance to Bologna.
- Unit culture and exile: [[533-wojtek-the-bear-who-beat-the-nazis-glt8956421015]] uses Wojtek, the 22nd Company's badge, and postwar settlement in Scotland to connect morale and identity to displacement.

## Qualifications

“Anders' Army” changed organization, location, and formal designation over time; it should not be treated as a single unchanged formation from Soviet territory through the entire Italian campaign. The episode supplies a narrative overview rather than an order of battle, administrative history, or complete account of the civilians evacuated with it.

## What Changed

- Established the force as a joined evacuation, combat, community, and exile history.
- Distinguished the broad Anders' Army label from its later Second Polish Corps form.
