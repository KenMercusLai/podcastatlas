# 刘禅 / Liu Chan

## Overview
刘禅 / Liu Chan enters the current wiki evidence as [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s young son rescued by [[ZhaoYun|赵云]] during the [[ChangbanBattle|长坂坡之战]].

## Current Profile
Hanji 1094 gives Liu Chan a narrow but dynastically important role. Liu Bei's movement collapses under pursuit and family members are separated or captured, but Zhao Yun returns carrying Liu Chan. The source treats the rescue as preservation of Liu Bei's family continuity rather than only as an individual martial feat.

## Key Characteristics
- Young son of Liu Bei in the episode's Changban sequence.
- Becomes separated from the main fleeing group during battlefield disorder.
- Is carried back by Zhao Yun.
