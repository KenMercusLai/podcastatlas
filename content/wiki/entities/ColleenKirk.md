# Colleen Kirk

Colleen Kirk is the [[NewYorkInstituteOfTechnology]] professor interviewed by [[StephanieHughes]] in [[tech-20260413-0413-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260413-0413-mp-tech-pod-128]]. In the episode, Kirk explains research on how consumers respond when products, advertising, communications, or artwork are described as AI-generated.

Her main contribution to the wiki is the consumer-behavior side of AI trust. She argues that AI-authorship labels can reduce trust, perceived authenticity, purchase intent, and word of mouth, especially where a product or message is emotionally meaningful or identity-linked. She also distinguishes AI as author from AI as tool: when humans lead and AI assists or edits, the negative response can disappear.

## Key Claims
- AI-generated labels can reduce consumer trust and perceived authenticity.
- Emotional, identity, and self-expression categories are more exposed than utilitarian categories.
- Consumers may pay more for human-made products because they infer care and personal expression.
- Human-led AI assistance is received differently from AI-led work that humans only edit.
- Gen Z consumers may be more skeptical of AI-generated ads than older consumers in the research Kirk cites.
