# Monster Beverage

Monster Beverage appears in [[hard-cedar-lebanon-seeks-trumps-help-6a5f41e97bddc0bea5542de5]] as [[RedBull]]'s main global energy-drink rival. The episode says Monster has about 17% of the global market compared with Red Bull's roughly 28%.

The source uses Monster mainly as a scale comparison. It also contrasts Red Bull's unusually high marketing spend with Monster's lower spend as a share of sales, making Monster a reference point for [[AssetLightBrandMachine]] rather than a full case study.
