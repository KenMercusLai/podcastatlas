# Oded Rechavi

## Overview
Oded Rechavi is the guest scientist in the [[HubermanLab]] Essentials source on genes, RNA, epigenetic inheritance, and whether acquired information can pass across generations.

## Current Profile
In the current wiki evidence, Rechavi functions as a careful boundary-setter for popular claims about inherited memories and acquired traits. He explains ordinary inheritance through DNA, RNA, proteins, somatic cells, and germ cells, then identifies the [[WeismannBarrier]] and [[EpigeneticReprogrammingBoundary]] as major reasons learned knowledge, muscle gain, and most other acquired states do not become hereditary.

His distinctive contribution in this source is the C. elegans evidence. Rechavi presents worms as the place where acquired-trait inheritance can be shown cleanly: small RNAs can cross generations, carry antiviral sequence information, and in his group's work connect brain-produced RNAs with descendant behavior. He repeatedly limits the inference to worms and treats mammal or human intergenerational-memory claims as unresolved.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains inheritance through molecular flow from DNA to RNA to protein rather than through vague family resemblance.
- Separates somatic experience from germline transmission using the [[WeismannBarrier]].
- Uses epigenetic reprogramming to explain why many inherited molecular marks are reset before development.
- Treats RNA, especially small RNAs, as a plausible carrier for intergenerational information in specific organisms.
- Grounds the strongest acquired-trait inheritance claims in [[CElegansModelOrganism]] experiments.
- Explicitly cautions against extending worm evidence into claims that human memories or learned skills are inherited.

## Evidence
- Inheritance boundary - [[essentials-genes-inheritance-memories-across-generations-dr-oded-rechavi-scim1624813352]] has Rechavi contrast inherited eye color with non-inherited learned architecture or gym-built muscle.
- Two-barrier explanation - [[essentials-genes-inheritance-memories-across-generations-dr-oded-rechavi-scim1624813352]] identifies soma-germline separation and epigenetic reprogramming as core barriers.
- Worm evidence - [[essentials-genes-inheritance-memories-across-generations-dr-oded-rechavi-scim1624813352]] describes inherited antiviral resistance and brain-derived small RNA effects in C. elegans.
- Mammal qualification - [[essentials-genes-inheritance-memories-across-generations-dr-oded-rechavi-scim1624813352]] repeatedly says mammalian and human mechanisms remain much less settled than worm mechanisms.
