## Overview

贾捐之 / Jia Juanzhi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-479-zhichang-qingshang-qianfei-kankan-xihan-jiajuanzhi-de-xiachang-lpsopbj1dtftnsn-kjjeyftst7el|Hanji 479]] as a Western Han writer-official with [[JiaYi|贾谊]] lineage prestige, repeated anti-[[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]] speech, and a fatal attempt to convert praise of Shi Xian into promotion opportunity under [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-471-qizhuya-yi-beihou-hanchao-weihe-fangqi-zhengfuyu-lqf0rzjmdzvcjvbtrnrrjmenoy6l|Hanji 471]] backfills his earlier successful policy role as the author of the "弃珠崖议."

## Current Profile

The source set now gives Jia Juanzhi a two-sided profile. Hanji 471 shows him as a capable remonstrant: when Yuan considers a large expedition against Zhuya and Dan'er, Jia argues from classical precedent, Qin-Han overextension examples, famine relief, troop attrition, and island logistics that direct suppression is not worth the burden. [[YuDingguoWesternHan|于定国]] supports the recommendation, and Yuan adopts it.

Hanji 479 then shows a later credibility collapse. Jia is talented enough to have literary and family status, but politically unstable in the court field. Before the case, he has attacked Shi Xian repeatedly and therefore lacks promotion prospects. After drinking with [[YangXingWesternHan|杨兴]], he moves abruptly from anti-Shi-Xian criticism to memorial praise of Shi Xian, while also preparing a separate recommendation for Yang Xing as Jingzhao Yin.

The combined profile is not simply "wise adviser" or "failed court schemer." Jia can formulate a serious capacity-bound policy argument, yet later loses political credibility by making his speech look transactional. Hanji 479 does not treat Jia's death as simply the result of offending a powerful eunuch. It reads the fatal problem as credibility collapse and unauthorized intervention in senior appointments: Jia looks willing to abandon a public stance for office, and his scheme makes Yuan's personnel authority appear manipulable through Shi Xian.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han court actor with source-scoped Jia Yi lineage prestige and literary ability.
- Author of the "弃珠崖议" argument against an expensive Zhuya and Dan'er punitive expedition.
- Remonstrant who grounds frontier retrenchment in classical precedent, famine relief, campaign cost, and administrative durability.
- Repeated critic of Shi Xian before the case, which makes later praise especially suspect.
- Ambitious office-seeker who privately discusses Shangshu Ling and Jingzhao Yin advancement with Yang Xing.
- Participant in a memorial strategy praising Shi Xian and recommending benefits for Shi's brothers.
- More severely punished than Yang Xing because his prior anti-Shi-Xian stance makes the reversal look like blatant self-serving inconsistency.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 471 and Hanji 479. It does not settle Jia Juanzhi's full biography, exact offices, the full received "弃珠崖" memorial tradition, his relationship to Jia Yi beyond the episode's claim, the received legal wording of the later case, or whether Shi Xian's report should be treated primarily as retaliation, self-protection, or loyal warning to Yuan.
