# All-In

Cuban treats AI as real but says the likely wipeout is concentrated in high-entry private capital, private equity, [[PrivateCreditMarket|private credit]], and data-center financing; he then connects that risk to early AI IPOs, employee collars, hard enterprise implementation, [[AgentMaintenanceBurden]], [[Lovable]]-enabled entrepreneurship, [[OpenEvidence]], [[WorldModels]], Texas operating culture, and sports streaming valuations.
