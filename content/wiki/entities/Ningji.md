# Ningji / 宁记

Ningji / 宁记 appears in [[145-gaijia-zhongzi-de-canyin-yangpinpai-feng-tou-quan-1-145-1]] as the Chinese lemon-tea brand that acquired [[HaagenDazs]] China store operations in 2026. The source says the deal covers stores rather than packaged ice-cream channels, which remain with [[GeneralMills]].

The hosts frame Ningji's move as a difficult local-turnaround bet. If the source's diagnosis is right, the challenge is not only buying a famous brand but redesigning price, product freshness, store format, health perception, and space economics against competitors such as [[YerenXiansheng]], [[MixueBingcheng]], [[Chagee]], and [[HeyTea]].
