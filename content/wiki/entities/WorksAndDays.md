# Works and Days / 《工作与时日》

## Overview

*Works and Days* is the Hesiodic poem that joins advice about honest labor and justice to the Prometheus-Pandora account of scarcity, technical power, suffering, and ambiguous hope.

## Current Profile

The episode contrasts the poem's concern with ordinary subsistence, property, agriculture, and justice with heroic epic's emphasis on war and honor. Addressed as counsel to [[Hesiod]]'s brother, it asks how people should live when the means of life are hidden and sustained provision requires work.

Its mythic explanation makes fire and tools genuinely civilizing without turning progress into guaranteed happiness. [[Prometheus]] restores fire, [[Zeus]] commissions [[Pandora]], disasters leave her jar, and *elpis* remains. The resulting world contains technique and labor alongside disease, scarcity, uncertainty, and death.

## Key Characteristics

- Didactic poem concerned with labor, property, agriculture, justice, and finite resources.
- Source for a Prometheus-Pandora account connecting technical capacity to the burdens of civilization.
- Text in which divine concealment of livelihood explains recurring human work.
- Source of the disputed image of *elpis* remaining inside Pandora's storage jar.
- Ancient text used by the episode to question simple narratives of inevitable progress.

## Qualifications
