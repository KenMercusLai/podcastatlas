# 秦武王 / King Wu of Qin

秦武王 / King Wu of Qin appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-38-gao-jianzhi-de-zhangyi-bu-yiban-lobhcpz679psy54k0iduccqkfcrn|Zhouji 38]] through the episode's [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] supplement explaining [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s move from [[QinState|秦国]] to [[WeiState|魏国]]. The source says that after [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] dies and Qin Wuwang succeeds, Zhang Yi's enemies begin attacking him at court.

In Zhang Yi's pitch, Qin Wuwang is not merely pushing out an unwanted minister. Zhang Yi persuades him that sending Zhang Yi to Wei can exploit [[QiState|齐国]]'s hatred: Qi will attack Wei, Qin can move against [[HanState|韩国]], and disorder among the eastern states will help Qin. The king accepts enough to send Zhang Yi out with a formal escort.

The wiki should keep this page source-scoped because the episode uses the Qin Wuwang strand inside a 322 BCE Zhou Xian Wang frame that sits uneasily with the immediately preceding [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] branch. That makes him part of the episode's [[ChronicleChronologyDrift|编年错位]] and source-layer problem as well as part of its Zhang Yi narrative.
