# Justin Kan

Justin Kan appears in [[socialradarspod-emmettshear-v2]] as [[EmmettShear]]'s cofounder through [[Kiko]], [[JustinTV|Justin.tv]], and the path that later produced [[Twitch]]. In the Kiko period, he and Emmett applied to [[YCombinator]] as Yale seniors with a working browser calendar demo.

Justin's role is especially visible in the Justin.tv origin. The live-streaming idea centered on Justin wearing a camera and broadcasting his life continuously, partly because he was more willing than Emmett to be famous on camera. That lifecasting product was often boring, but audience conversation and gaming moments created the usage signals that later pointed toward Twitch.

[[tsr-s5-justinkan-v2-audio-tsr-s5-justinkan-v2-audio]] turns Justin from an adjacent cofounder into a direct source. He says the first YC batch created pressure through dinners, demos, and peers; Kiko's failure still produced learning and cash optionality through its eBay sale; and Justin.tv moved from a viral 24/7 lifecasting stunt into an open streaming platform after viewers asked to make their own streams.

The same source makes Justin a [[ServiceProductization]] and [[FounderMotivationEvolution]] case. [[Exec]] and [[Atrium]] show him repeatedly trying to turn human services into scalable company systems, while the post-[[Twitch]] reflection section has him name external approval as a major driver and test work by whether it would still feel worthwhile without money or public recognition.
