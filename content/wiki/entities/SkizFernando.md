# Skiz Fernando

## Overview

Skiz Fernando is a hip-hop journalist whom the bounded source uses as an early observer of [[WuTangClan|Wu-Tang Clan]] and an expert on [[RZA]]'s artistic and business plan.

## Current Profile

Because the host could not interview Wu-Tang directly, Fernando supplies historical interpretation based on covering the group from its beginning. He emphasizes RZA's intelligence, kung-fu knowledge, coordination of distinct MCs, and negotiation of a label arrangement that permitted independent solo deals.

Fernando also supplies a critical boundary on the episode's central analogy. He had not previously connected Wu-Tang's formation with [[StatenIslandSecession1993|Staten Island's secession movement]] and did not know whether the group followed it, supporting a comparison of simultaneous aspirations rather than a claim of influence.

## Key Characteristics

- Hip-hop journalist and early observer of Wu-Tang Clan.
- Expert source used when direct group interviews were unavailable.
- Interpreter of RZA's intelligence, musical coordination, and business strategy.
- Describes the group's contract structure as unprecedented and revolutionary.
- Preserves uncertainty around any link to the secession campaign.
