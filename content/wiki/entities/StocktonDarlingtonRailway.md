# Stockton and Darlington Railway

## Overview

The Stockton and Darlington Railway is presented in [[140-the-birth-of-the-railways-glt2177757841]] as the 1825 line that joined coal transport from South West Durham toward the River Tees with an early public passenger service.

## Current Profile

The line occupies a transitional position in the episode. Like the [[HettonRailway]], its commercial foundation was moving coal toward a navigable river, but it also carried passengers in a carriage called *Experiment*. [[GeorgeStephenson]] built *Locomotion* for the route, making the railway a visible step from colliery infrastructure toward mixed public transport.

Its importance is not that freight suddenly disappeared. Instead, the same coal-centered engineering and economic system acquired a passenger layer, preparing the way for the more deliberately intercity and spectacular [[LiverpoolManchesterRailway]].

## Key Characteristics

- It opened in 1825.
- Its primary economic task was moving coal toward the River Tees.
- George Stephenson's *Locomotion* served the line.
- It carried passengers as well as freight.
- Its *Experiment* carriage symbolizes the still-provisional character of early passenger rail.

## Evidence

- Coal purpose: [[140-the-birth-of-the-railways-glt2177757841]] says the route moved coal from South West Durham toward the Tees.
- Stephenson locomotive: [[140-the-birth-of-the-railways-glt2177757841]] associates George Stephenson and *Locomotion* with the line's opening.
- Passenger transition: [[140-the-birth-of-the-railways-glt2177757841]] contrasts it with freight-only colliery railways and names the passenger carriage *Experiment*.
- Sequence: [[140-the-birth-of-the-railways-glt2177757841]] places it between all-locomotive Hetton and the Rainhill-tested Liverpool-Manchester line.

## Qualifications

The profile follows the episode's selective origin narrative rather than a full company or operating history. Opening details, priority claims, the balance of horse and locomotive operation, and the story that sunlight through a driver's pipe glass lit *Locomotion*'s fire remain source-scoped.
