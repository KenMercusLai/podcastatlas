# 3M

3M appears in [[a-pro-worker-experiment-in-private-equity]] as the buyer of [[CapitalSafety]] in 2015. The sale was the event that revealed [[KKR]]'s broad employee-equity grant to workers such as [[CindyCordes]].

The source does not treat 3M as the main subject. Its role is transaction-trigger: the acquisition converted an otherwise opaque ownership promise into cash payouts for Capital Safety workers and exposed the weakness of KKR's earlier [[EmployeeEquityCommunication]].
