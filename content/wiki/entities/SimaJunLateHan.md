## Overview

司马钧 / Sima Jun is the Hanji 789 Qiang-front commander whose brief Dingxi success collapses after subordinate disobedience. His profile is a compact case of command discipline, logistics denial, and punishment fear in the 116 CE campaign.

## Current Profile

Hanji 789 says Sima Jun and [[PangCanLateHan|Pang Can]] split attacks against the Qiang field around [[LinChangQiang|Lin Chang]] and [[DuJigongLateHan|Du Jigong]]. Sima Jun defeats Du Jigong and captures Dingxi, creating what looks like a useful battlefield opening.

The success fails because the follow-through order is not obeyed. Sima Jun sends a subordinate to cut or destroy enemy grain, but the subordinate does not execute the order and is ambushed. Du Jigong retakes Dingxi. Sima Jun, fearing punishment, kills himself. The episode also notes his later genealogical importance as an ancestor of Sima Yi, but that lineage point remains secondary to the command-failure case.

## Key Characteristics

- Field commander in the 116 CE Qiang campaign under the Deng regency.
- Achieves a short-lived victory over Du Jigong and takes Dingxi.
- Depends on grain-denial follow-through to make the victory durable.
- Loses the result after subordinate disobedience and ambush.
- Ends by suicide from fear of punishment, showing how command accountability becomes lethal under frontier failure.
