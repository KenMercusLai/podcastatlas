## Overview

13th Floor Entertainment is a U.S. haunted-attraction operator described through its technical production work at the [[HollywoodHauntedHayride]].

## Current Profile

[[KipPolly]] says the company owns roughly 18–20 attractions around the United States, producing a continuing stream of projects for its technical team. The episode emphasizes temporary California productions that must be installed and removed, and a production model that combines live scare performers with lighting, audio, animatronics, sensors, controllers, video, and tactile effects.

## Key Characteristics

- Operates a multi-attraction U.S. portfolio according to its technical director.
- Combines live performance with centrally coordinated technical effects.
- Builds temporary productions whose setup and teardown create seasonal travel and labor demands.
- Uses several pacing formats, including moving mazes and hayrides and longer seated shows.
- Searches continually for new attraction ideas while treating guest memory as the operating purpose.
