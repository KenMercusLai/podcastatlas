## Overview

建宁公主 / Princess Jianning is a royal woman in [[LuDingJi|《鹿鼎记》]] whose combative play, sexuality, and temperamental similarity with [[WeiXiaobao|韦小宝]] make her his most conventionally developed everyday spouse in the bounded episode.

## Current Profile

Jianning and Wei begin through rough play and sadomasochistic interaction, then develop sustained intimacy during the journey toward Yunnan. The episode considers that interval unusually close to an on-page courtship because teasing, proximity, sexuality, and mutual entertainment accumulate before marriage.

She becomes both Wei's sexual initiator and Kangxi's designated principal wife. Frequent marital quarrels do not simply mark incompatibility; for the hosts they also show shared language, similar energy, and the capacity to keep engaging one another in ordinary life.

## Key Characteristics

- Shares Wei's appetite for rough play, teasing, and conflict.
- Acts as his sexual initiator in the episode's account.
- Receives the clearest sustained courtship-like development among the seven relationships.
- Combines bodily compatibility with similar temperament.
- Functions as a lived spouse and playmate rather than a purified romantic ideal.
