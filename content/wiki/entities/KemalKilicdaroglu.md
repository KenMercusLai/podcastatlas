# Kemal Kilicdaroglu

Kemal Kilicdaroglu is the former [[RepublicanPeoplesParty|CHP]] leader whom a court returns to the party leadership in [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]]. The source says the ruling removed [[OzgurOzel]] by finding that the 2023 party congress had been rigged.

The episode frames Kilicdaroglu as a politically convenient opposition figure for [[RecepTayyipErdogan]]. He is described as having led the CHP through repeated defeats, including his own 2023 presidential loss, which makes his return part of [[JudicializedOppositionSuppression]] and [[OppositionPartyHollowing]] rather than a normal leadership change.
