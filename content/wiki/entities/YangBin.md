# 杨彬 / Yang Bin

## Overview
Yang Bin is the Tsinghua professor and former university administrator interviewed in the source about the Dartmouth AI workshop, innovation fields, and AI-era education.

## Current Profile
Within the wiki, Yang Bin currently matters as an interpreter of innovation environments. He uses the 1956 Dartmouth AI workshop to argue that original innovation needs a field where participants can stay with ambiguity, suspend premature judgment, and let different routes coexist before efficiency logic narrows them.

His education argument extends the same idea into the AI era. If AI reduces the scarcity of lectures, homework, and routine knowledge transmission, Yang argues that universities need to foreground campus interaction, leadership, entrepreneurship, friendship, apprenticeship, oral defense, and face-to-face learning.

## Key Characteristics
- Tsinghua University professor introduced by Xu Tao as a former vice president and provost.
- Uses the phrase [[SlowGatheringImprovisation|慢聚慢奏]] to describe the Dartmouth-style innovation mode.
- Treats psychological safety as concrete freedom from excessive hierarchy, correctness policing, and status anxiety.
- Argues that organizations should tolerate difference before they can know whether an idea is right or wrong.
- Connects AI history to university reform, campus design, and apprenticeship-like education.
