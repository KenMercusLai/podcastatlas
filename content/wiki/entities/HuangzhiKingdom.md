## Overview

黄支国 / Huangzhi Kingdom enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-610-1-jiemi-gudai-huangdi-niangjiaren-de-quanli-douzheng-lplz8f3wg1p0m5kmlh-w32jwnlzq|Hanji 610-1]] as a distant southern polity whose rhinoceros tribute is used by [[WangMang|王莽]] to display prestige during [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]]'s early reign.

## Current Profile

The episode places Huangzhi in the far southern or South Sea direction, while noting expert speculation that it may have been in southeastern India. Its narrative role is not a developed foreign-policy profile but a prestige signal: Wang Mang sends valuable gifts first, Huangzhi then sends envoys with a rhinoceros, and the court can treat the exchange as evidence that moral authority reaches distant lands.

This makes Huangzhi a source-bounded example of tribute as staged recognition. The episode's emphasis falls on Wang Mang's management of the signal, not on Huangzhi's internal politics or independent motive.

## Key Characteristics

- Distant southern polity appearing in a 2 CE tribute notice.
- Sends a rhinoceros to the Han court in the episode's account.
- Located broadly in the South Sea direction, with southeastern India preserved as source-scoped speculation.
- Functions mainly as a prestige display for Wang Mang's claimed virtue.
- Tribute is preceded by Wang Mang's generous gifts, making the recognition managed rather than spontaneous.

## Qualifications

This page is source-bounded to Hanji 610-1. It does not settle Huangzhi's exact geography, independent political structure, diplomatic motive, trade route, or whether the tribute should be interpreted as voluntary allegiance outside Wang Mang's gift-backed staging.
