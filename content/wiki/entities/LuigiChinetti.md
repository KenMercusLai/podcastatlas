# Luigi Chinetti

Luigi Chinetti is the Ferrari-linked racer and commercial connector who, in [[ferrari-1]], convinced [[EnzoFerrari]] that wealthy buyers in the [[UnitedStates]] could support Ferrari road-car demand. The source presents him as crucial to the early U.S. market path.

Chinetti matters because [[Ferrari]] needed customers who understood racing, beauty, and status before the company had mass distribution. His role connects early customer cars such as the [[Ferrari166Barchetta]] to [[RacingHeritageBrandMoat]] and later collector demand.
