# 杭州师范大学附属医院 / Hangzhou Normal University Affiliated Hospital

## Overview
杭州师范大学附属医院 is the hospital affiliation stated for otolaryngologist [[XieDaoyu|谢道宇]] in two [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episodes.

## Current Profile
Within the bounded wiki evidence, the hospital is the clinical setting behind Xie's public explanations of tinnitus, middle-ear disease, external-canal inflammation, hearing change, fish-bone foreign bodies, acute epiglottitis, pharyngitis, and tonsil decisions. The throat episode also attributes to the hospital a dedicated evening fish-bone clinic supported by endoscopic evaluation and referral from surrounding services.

The sources use clinical cases to illustrate assessment and escalation, including foreign-body localization, possible perforation, airway monitoring, deep infection, and surgery. They do not provide a broader institutional history, independently audited outcomes, or a complete service inventory.

## Key Characteristics
- Hospital named as the guest's clinical affiliation in both source episodes.
- Institutional context for source-scoped otolaryngology public education.
- Associated in the sources with ear, hearing, throat, foreign-body, airway, and surgical assessment.
- Described as operating a dedicated evening fish-bone clinic with endoscopic capability.
