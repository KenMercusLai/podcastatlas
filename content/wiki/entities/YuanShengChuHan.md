# 袁先生 / Yuan Sheng (Chu-Han)

袁先生 / Yuan Sheng (Chu-Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-162-1-ta-ti-liubang-qu-si-rujin-chengle-zhengzhou-de-shouhushen-li3su2zlmbm1ift9nibujgtcrxwm|Hanji 162-1]] as the adviser who redirects [[LiuBang|刘邦]] after Liu Bang escapes [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]]. The episode calls him 袁先生 and presents his advice as a way to avoid a direct return into the same siege pressure.

His proposal is operationally indirect: Liu Bang should move through [[Wuguan|武关]] toward the Wan, Ye, and Chou area, fortify, refuse battle with [[XiangYu|项羽]], and draw Chu forces south. This creates rest time for [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]] and [[Chenggao|成皋]], while also giving [[HanXin|韩信]] room to digest Zhao and pressure Yan and Qi.
