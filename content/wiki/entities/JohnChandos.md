# John Chandos

## Overview

John Chandos is the English knight and commander portrayed as the [[EdwardTheBlackPrince|Black Prince's]] close friend, right-hand man, and military adviser from Crécy through the Poitiers campaign.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Chandos as a younger son whose ability creates a career at the center of English war making. He reconnoiters the French fleet before Sluys, stands beside the prince at Crécy, enters the [[OrderOfTheGarter]], and accompanies the 1355 expedition to Gascony.

During the following campaign, Chandos leads the attempt to find a Loire crossing so the prince can join Lancaster. He drives off French troops at a possible ford but cannot overcome the swollen river. At [[BattleOfPoitiers|Poitiers]], he helps direct the mounted flanking attack that turns French confusion into encirclement and royal capture.

## Key Characteristics

- Younger son whose career rests on military service.
- Early reconnaissance role before Sluys.
- Companion of the Black Prince at Crécy.
- Founding-era knight of the Order of the Garter.
- Operational adviser during the Gascon and Loire campaigns.
- Commander associated with the decisive Poitiers counterstroke.
