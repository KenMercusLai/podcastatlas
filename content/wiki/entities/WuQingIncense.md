# 吴清 / Wu Qing (incense)

吴清 / Wu Qing is the ancient-formula reconstruction practitioner discussed in [[ep263-xianxiang-jueqi-faxian-zhongshi-xiangqi-de-meili-lq1lsqcprqyr7auo5gslwyymuiv]]. The episode uses his work on "鹅梨帐中香" to show that reconstructing traditional fragrance is not simply copying an old recipe: the maker has to compare textual variants, test whether ingredients and procedures are workable, and decide whether the final smell matches the intended cultural scene.

The source places Wu Qing at the boundary between scholarship and product. His reconstruction work helps answer "what did Chinese fragrance smell like?" while also feeding contemporary consumer curiosity triggered by drama references and named ancient formulas.
