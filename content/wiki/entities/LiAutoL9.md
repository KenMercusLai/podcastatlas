# Li Auto L9 / 理想 L9

## Overview
Li Auto L9 / 理想 L9 is the flagship family SUV line used in the wiki to explain both [[LiAuto|理想汽车]]'s early premium positioning and its later AI-platform upgrade through [[LiAutoL9Levius|L9 LEVIUS]].

## Current Profile
[[143-wei-xiao-li-de-gaoduan-tansuo-feng-tou-quan-1-143-1]] treats L9 as Li Auto's clearest early proof of [[HighEndEVBranding]]. The "500万以内最好的SUV" claim, family-comfort scenario, and "fridge, TV, sofa" experience helped make a Chinese new-energy vehicle feel like a credible alternative to foreign luxury SUVs.

The same source uses L9 as a renewal stress test: an update can satisfy existing Li Auto users without recreating the original breakout effect among non-users. The L9 line now also has a more explicit technology-platform extension through [[LiAutoL9Levius|L9 LEVIUS]], tying it to full wire-control chassis, active suspension, high inference compute, and vehicle-agent architecture in [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]].

## Key Characteristics
- Flagship family SUV line associated with Li Auto's early premium differentiation.
- Product proof point for family comfort, household use, and bold Chinese EV branding.
- Renewal-pressure case because copied family-SUV formulas force new premium reasons.
- Technology-platform branch through L9 LEVIUS, M100 compute, full wire-control chassis, and vehicle AI.
- Source-scoped evidence base that does not independently verify post-update market response or L9 LEVIUS performance.

## Evidence
- Premium positioning: [[143-wei-xiao-li-de-gaoduan-tansuo-feng-tou-quan-1-143-1]] uses L9 to explain Li Auto's high-end family-SUV story.
- Stress test: [[143-wei-xiao-li-de-gaoduan-tansuo-feng-tou-quan-1-143-1]] says the L9 update has not recreated the original breakout effect among non-users.
- Technology-platform extension: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] says L9 LEVIUS adds full wire-control chassis, active suspension, M100 compute, and a system-level agent architecture.

## Qualifications
The current sources treat L9 as a brand and strategy case. They do not provide independent sales, user-satisfaction, safety, autonomous-driving, or hardware benchmark evidence.

## What Changed
- Added L9 LEVIUS as the AI and embodied-intelligence extension of the earlier L9 family-SUV story.
- Reframed L9 from a static premium proof point into a product line facing continuous renewal pressure.
