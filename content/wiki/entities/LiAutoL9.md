# Li Auto L9 / 理想 L9

Li Auto L9 / 理想 L9 is the flagship family SUV case in [[143-wei-xiao-li-de-gaoduan-tansuo-feng-tou-quan-1-143-1]]. The episode treats it as [[LiAuto]]'s clearest early proof of [[HighEndEVBranding]]: the "500万以内最好的SUV" claim, family-comfort scenario, and "fridge, TV, sofa" experience helped make a Chinese new-energy vehicle feel like a credible alternative to foreign luxury SUVs.

The source also uses L9 as a stress test. A major update still satisfies many existing Li Auto users, but the hosts argue it has not recreated the original breakout effect among non-users. That makes L9 important for [[ProductLedWillingnessToPay]] and [[QualityLowPriceReasonedPremium]]: the premium reason must keep renewing once competitors copy the visible family-SUV formula.
