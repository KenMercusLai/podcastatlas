# 360 Canine Group

## Overview

360 Canine Group is presented as an Alabama working-dog organization that evaluates detection aptitude and develops operational screening scenarios.

## Current Profile

[[the-dog-will-see-you-now-the-dog-will-see-you-now]] uses the organization's assessment of Cooley, a Belgian Malinois, to show that working-dog selection combines odor search with persistence, confidence, focus, and composure around unfamiliar surfaces and sudden obstacles. A second anecdote about a retired Labrador detecting residual weapon odor illustrates trace sensitivity without functioning as a controlled comparison.

Jerry Johnson then outlines a school-entry COVID-screening workflow using rotating dogs, brief hand or foot sniffs, and secondary rapid tests after an alert. The proposal demonstrates an operational concept, not a proven program: the source supplies no field trial, throughput audit, false-result analysis, child-safeguarding protocol, or comparative outcome data.

## Key Characteristics

- Evaluates working dogs for search drive, persistence, confidence, focus, and environmental stability.
- Treats detection performance as a dog-handler-system capability rather than nose sensitivity alone.
- Uses obstacle and unfamiliar-surface exposure to test working composure.
- Contributes an operational design for rapid school-entry screening.
- Is represented through demonstrations and proposals rather than an independently reviewed organizational record.
