# Fiat Chrysler Automobiles

Fiat Chrysler Automobiles, or FCA, is the parent-company context for [[Ferrari]]'s public-market separation in [[ferrari-1]]. The episode says Fiat's Chrysler deal left the combined company with heavy debt, which [[SergioMarchionne]] sought to reduce by floating Ferrari.

The source treats FCA as the pressure source behind [[PublicMarketLuxuryGrowthTension]]. Ferrari's brand value depended on scarcity and independence, while FCA's balance sheet made Ferrari's hidden valuation and cash-raising potential hard to ignore.
