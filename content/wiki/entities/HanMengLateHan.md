# 韩猛 / Han Meng (late Han)

## Overview
韩猛 / Han Meng is a [[YuanShao|袁绍]] commander whose current wiki role is narrowly tied to the pre-[[WuchaoRaidLateHan|乌巢]] logistics struggle in the [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]]. He appears as the grain-convoy commander whom [[XunYou|荀攸]] identifies as brave but careless enough for [[CaoCao|曹操]] to attack.

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents Han Meng as a vulnerability inside Yuan Shao's stronger material position. Yuan's army has grain convoys moving toward Guandu, but Xun You judges Han Meng's temperament as exploitable: he is courageous, yet prone to underestimating danger.

Cao Cao acts on that judgment by sending [[XuHuangCaoGeneral|徐晃]] and [[ShiHuanLateHan|史涣]] to intercept Han Meng's convoy. The successful attack burns Yuan Shao's baggage and shows Cao's side shifting from passive endurance at Guandu toward strikes on Yuan logistics before Xu You exposes the larger Wuchao target.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan Shao-side convoy commander in the Guandu supply struggle.
- Brave but careless figure in Xun You's tactical assessment.
- Target of Cao Cao's pre-Wuchao logistics interception.
- Source-bounded minor commander whose importance comes from the convoy he escorts rather than a full biography.
