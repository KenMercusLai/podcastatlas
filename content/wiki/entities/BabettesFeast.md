# 《巴贝特之宴》 / Babette's Feast

## Overview
《巴贝特之宴》 is a 1987 film discussed by [[DaYiCultureLimited|大一]] as a story about food, faith, artistic generosity, mourning, and communal repair.

## Current Profile
In the episode's account, Babette is a French refugee who spends fourteen years serving an austere Danish religious community, then uses her winnings to prepare an elaborate French meal rather than simply depart. The guests initially refuse to acknowledge taste, but shared eating gradually loosens resentment and separation. The film anchors [[SharedSensoryCommunityRepair]] and qualifies the assumption that bodily pleasure necessarily corrupts spiritual life.

## Key Characteristics
- Places culinary artistry inside an ascetic religious community.
- Treats a feast as gift, performance, memorial, and social intervention.
- Uses sensory pleasure to reopen connection without relying on argument.
- Joins death imagery, mourning, food, faith, and artistic vocation.

## Evidence
- Refuge and gift: [[vol-353-qiuri-teji-pingguo-de-xiangwei-zhong-wo-jianjian-hunhun-yushui-gkwriaiohmv_a7ndcatastow]] recounts Babette's long service and decision to spend her winnings on the feast.
- Communal effect: [[vol-353-qiuri-teji-pingguo-de-xiangwei-zhong-wo-jianjian-hunhun-yushui-gkwriaiohmv_a7ndcatastow]] says the meal gradually reconnects people who had agreed not to comment on its pleasures.
- Spiritual qualification: [[vol-353-qiuri-teji-pingguo-de-xiangwei-zhong-wo-jianjian-hunhun-yushui-gkwriaiohmv_a7ndcatastow]] reads the dinner as artistic and spiritually reparative rather than merely luxurious.
