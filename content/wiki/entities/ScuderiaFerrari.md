# Scuderia Ferrari

Scuderia Ferrari is the racing organization that preceded [[Ferrari]] as a road-car company. In [[ferrari-1]], [[EnzoFerrari]] creates it as a stable that runs races for [[AlfaRomeo]] with its own drivers, mechanics, and operating responsibility.

The source treats Scuderia Ferrari as the bridge between motorsport and luxury brand power. It gave Ferrari legitimacy inside [[FormulaOne]], a visible fan base through the [[Tifosi]], and a history that later road-car buyers could treat as more than ordinary performance marketing.

## Source Position
- Scuderia Ferrari began as a racing operator before Ferrari manufactured customer cars.
- Continuous F1 participation made it central to [[FormulaOne]]'s legitimacy.
- Its wins, deaths, technical rivalries, and driver mythology became part of Ferrari's broader [[RacingHeritageBrandMoat]].
