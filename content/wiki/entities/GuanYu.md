# 关羽 / Guan Yu

## Overview
关羽 / Guan Yu is currently represented less as a full biography than as a source-critical and loyalty-under-pressure figure in the late-Han arc. The available evidence separates famous [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]] memory from chronicle-source claims, then places Guan Yu inside [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s camp after [[Xiapi|下邳]] as both a desired military talent and a [[LiuBei|刘备]] loyalist.

## Current Profile
The current synthesis is that Guan Yu's wiki role has two layers. First, he is a test case for literary memory: the "warm wine and Hua Xiong" scene is treated as a novelized attribution, while the historical-source frame credits [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] at [[YangrenBattleLateHan|阳人之战]]. Second, Hanji 1041 turns "关羽降曹" into a constrained-captivity case rather than a simple betrayal or the formal "关公约三事" contract. Cao Cao captures him, appoints him 偏将军, and treats him well, but Guan Yu tells [[ZhangLiao|张辽]] that he cannot betray Liu Bei and will leave after repaying Cao Cao by merit.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a prominent boundary case between Three Kingdoms literary memory and source-grounded event attribution.
- Remains Liu Bei-aligned even when temporarily inside Cao Cao's camp after capture.
- Is valued by Cao Cao as a high-capacity and loyal military talent, not only as a defeated enemy.
- Shows how captivity can create temporary service and repayment obligations without erasing prior allegiance.
- Also appears in the Xiapi household branch, where his request for [[LadyDuCaoCao|杜夫人]] inadvertently draws Cao Cao's attention to her.

## Evidence
- Literary-memory boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-955-guanyu-zhan-huaxiong-qishi-shi-wei-lishi-lndkrj-fnepye8iu-ifgyh-vzysf|Hanji 955]] contrasts the novelized Guan Yu killing of Hua Xiong with the episode's historical-source attribution to Sun Jian at Yangren.
- Xiapi household branch: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] says Guan Yu repeatedly asks Cao Cao for Lady Du after Xiapi, but the reminders prompt Cao Cao to inspect and take her himself.
- Captive service and rank: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1041-jiemi-lishishang-zhenshi-de-guanyu-jiangcao-luisp0ndk1lfrc87byygn6r8myhx|Hanji 1041]] records Cao Cao capturing Guan Yu, appointing him 偏将军, and treating him generously after the attack on Liu Bei.
- Residual loyalty and repayment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1041-jiemi-lishishang-zhenshi-de-guanyu-jiangcao-luisp0ndk1lfrc87byygn6r8myhx|Hanji 1041]] has Guan Yu tell Zhang Liao that Liu Bei's恩义 prevents betrayal and that he will repay Cao Cao's treatment with merit before leaving.

## Qualifications
The page should not infer Guan Yu's full later career from the current bounded sources. Hanji 1041 rejects the historical status of "关公约三事" but does not deny Guan Yu's loyalty; it relocates that loyalty from a formal contract into a recorded statement and later intended departure. Hanji 1013's Lady Du branch is a household and desire-pattern case, not a complete account of Guan Yu's family life.
