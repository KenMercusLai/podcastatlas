# 关羽 / Guan Yu

关羽 / Guan Yu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] as a [[LiuBei|刘备]]-aligned general temporarily in [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s camp before the fall of [[Xiapi|下邳]]. The source says he repeatedly asks Cao Cao to give him [[LadyDuCaoCao|杜夫人]] after the city is taken, explaining that his own wife has not borne a son.

The request has the opposite effect. Cao Cao initially agrees, but Guan Yu's repeated reminders make him curious; after seeing Lady Du himself, Cao Cao takes her into his own household. In this source, Guan Yu's role is not a full biography but a contrast case: another man's desire first signals Lady Du's value to Cao Cao.
