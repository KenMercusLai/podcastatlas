# 关羽 / Guan Yu

## Overview
关羽 / Guan Yu is a source-critical, loyalty-under-pressure, status-sensitive, and late-strategic-threat figure in the current late-Han arc. The evidence separates famous [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]] memory from chronicle-source claims, follows his constrained service under [[CaoCao|曹操]] through repayment and return to [[LiuBei|刘备]], and connects his appointment dispute and flood-assisted northern high point to the rear-defense exposure exploited by Eastern Wu.

## Current Profile
The current synthesis has four layers. First, Guan Yu is a test case for literary memory: the "warm wine and Hua Xiong" scene is treated as a novelized attribution, while the historical-source frame credits [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] at [[YangrenBattleLateHan|阳人之战]]. Second, Hanji 1041, Hanji 1043, and Hanji 1044 turn "关羽降曹" into constrained captivity rather than simple betrayal or a formal "关公约三事" contract: Guan Yu accepts temporary rank, repays Cao Cao by killing Yan Liang at White Horse, seals the gifts, and returns toward Liu Bei.

Third, Hanji 1166 adds a status-sensitive command relationship. Guan Yu reportedly rejects appointment as 前将军 when [[HuangZhong|黄忠]] receives comparable standing. [[FeiShiLateHan|费诗]] waits for the outburst to subside, restores the ruler-minister and collective-mission frame, and makes refusal and future regret visible; Guan Yu then accepts. A parallel anecdote has [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] confirm Guan Yu's singular standing to prevent a contest with [[MaChao|马超]]. These scenes support a pattern of pride and recognition sensitivity, while the source's deeper insecurity diagnosis remains interpretation.

Fourth, Hanji 1166 supplies the missing operational high point before Hanji 1105's compressed endpoint. At the [[FanchengBattle219LateHan|Battle of Fancheng]], rain and Han River flooding destroy [[YuJin|于禁]]'s field position; Guan Yu accepts Yu Jin's surrender, executes [[PangDe|庞德]] after refusal, and pressures the damaged city until [[CaoRen|曹仁]], [[ManChong|满宠]], and later [[XuHuangCaoGeneral|徐晃]] preserve the defense. Hanji 1105 then jumps to Cao's alignment with [[SunQuan|孙权]], Guan Yu's death, and the reported respectful burial order.

Fifth, Hanji 1168 supplies part of that missing transition. Cao-side advisers identify Guan Yu's rise as a threat likely to reprice the Sun–Liu alliance, while [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] and [[LuXunDongWu|陆逊]] combine apparent illness, a low-profile succession, compartmentalized planning, and deferential praise. The episode says Guan Yu consequently moves rear-defense troops toward Fancheng. This supports [[AdversaryTraitExploitation|对手性格弱点利用]], but pride remains an amplifying vulnerability rather than a complete explanation for a defeat also shaped by Jingzhou interests, geography, alliance change, and force allocation.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a prominent boundary case between Three Kingdoms literary memory and source-grounded event attribution.
- Remains Liu Bei-aligned even when temporarily inside Cao Cao's camp after capture.
- Is valued by Cao Cao as a high-capacity military talent, repays temporary patronage by killing Yan Liang at White Horse, and then returns toward Liu Bei.
- Later becomes a northern strategic threat whose pressure helps drive Cao Cao toward coordination with Sun Quan and makes his rear allocation decisive.
- Reacts sharply to perceived status equivalence, but can accept authority after recognition, relationship framing, and consequences are made explicit.
- Converts the Han River flood into the surrender of Yu Jin's field army and the high point of the Fancheng campaign.
- His reported pride and contempt for lesser-known officers become inputs to Wu deception, but do not alone explain his final defeat.

## Evidence
- Literary-memory boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-955-guanyu-zhan-huaxiong-qishi-shi-wei-lishi-lndkrj-fnepye8iu-ifgyh-vzysf|Hanji 955]] contrasts the novelized Guan Yu killing of Hua Xiong with the episode's historical-source attribution to Sun Jian at Yangren.
- Xiapi household branch: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] says Guan Yu repeatedly asks Cao Cao for Lady Du after Xiapi, but the reminders prompt Cao Cao to inspect and take her himself.
- Captive service and rank: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1041-jiemi-lishishang-zhenshi-de-guanyu-jiangcao-luisp0ndk1lfrc87byygn6r8myhx|Hanji 1041]] records Cao Cao capturing Guan Yu, appointing him 偏将军, and treating him generously after the attack on Liu Bei.
- Residual loyalty and repayment promise: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1041-jiemi-lishishang-zhenshi-de-guanyu-jiangcao-luisp0ndk1lfrc87byygn6r8myhx|Hanji 1041]] has Guan Yu tell Zhang Liao that Liu Bei's恩义 prevents betrayal and that he will repay Cao Cao's treatment with merit before leaving.
- White Horse repayment act: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1043-yuanshao-tongma-caocao-jing-zhihaole-tade-piantouteng-ltibjj6xizsrynpgr9oyf-q3ydna|Hanji 1043]] says Guan Yu sees Yan Liang's banner, rides into the enemy formation, kills Yan Liang, takes the head, and lifts the White Horse siege.
- Departure endpoint: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1044-guanyu-qianli-zou-danqi-xu-ganxie-caocao-busha-enqing-lpptlnc-e4zu4lcjr49bquxdw0pm|Hanji 1044]] completes the arc by having Guan Yu seal Cao Cao's rewards, leave a letter, and return toward Liu Bei while Cao prevents pursuit.
- Northern-campaign endpoint: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1105-guanyu-zhisi-maizang-le-caocao-zenyang-de-mengxiang-lgmk_dlknjpzz7fvzasno6tu7fs3|Hanji 1105]] says Guan Yu's northern advance threatens Cao Cao, Sun Quan's pressure contributes to his death, and Cao Cao orders a respectful burial after receiving his head.
- Appointment dispute and status sensitivity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1166-ruhe-yu-piqi-da-nengli-qiang-de-ren-xiangchu-lhm17dmeln7dmj1czmeq7omxgtmh|Hanji 1166]] says Guan Yu angrily rejects ranking alongside Huang Zhong before Fei Shi's delayed, relationship-based warning leads him to accept.
- Fancheng high point: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1166-ruhe-yu-piqi-da-nengli-qiang-de-ren-xiangchu-lhm17dmeln7dmj1czmeq7omxgtmh|Hanji 1166]] attributes Yu Jin's flooded defeat and surrender, Pang De's execution, and severe pressure on Fancheng to Guan Yu's 219 offensive.
- Rear-defense exposure and trait-matched deception: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1168-zuoren-tai-ao-zhen-de-bi-zhaohuo-ma-lqufnweysvpas7ahznr3tpb9ajad|Hanji 1168]] says Lyu Meng's apparent illness and Lu Xun's deferential letter reinforce Guan Yu's confidence and precede his shifting more forces toward Fancheng.

## Qualifications
The sources still do not provide a full account of Guan Yu's middle career or the complete operational sequence of his final campaign. Hanji 1041 rejects the historical status of "关公约三事" but not Guan Yu's loyalty; Hanji 1044 treats "千里走单骑" and "过五关斩六将" as Romance embellishments. Hanji 1166's status insecurity, vanity, and emotional mechanism are presenter interpretations, while its dialogue, force totals, flood sequence, and execution details require broader textual comparison. Hanji 1168's marriage insult, arrogance diagnosis, motives, letters, secrecy, and redeployment sequence remain episode-attributed. Pride can magnify exposure without replacing Jingzhou claims, Wu strategy, Cao diplomacy, geography, or force pressure. Hanji 1105 remains compressed, so the reported words to Guan Yu's head and symbolic burial interpretation also remain source-scoped.

## What Changed
- Added the Wu deception and rear-force transfer between the Fancheng high point and compressed death endpoint.
- Treated pride as an exploited vulnerability rather than a single-cause explanation.
- Preserved uncertainty around letters, motives, alliance mechanics, and troop movement.
