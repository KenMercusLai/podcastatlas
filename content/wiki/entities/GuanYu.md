# 关羽 / Guan Yu

## Overview
关羽 / Guan Yu is a source-critical, loyalty-under-pressure, and late-strategic-threat figure in the current late-Han arc. The evidence separates famous [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]] memory from chronicle-source claims, follows his constrained service under [[CaoCao|曹操]] through repayment and return to [[LiuBei|刘备]], and now adds his final northern campaign and death as the endpoint of Cao and Sun Quan's converging pressure.

## Current Profile
The current synthesis has three layers. First, Guan Yu is a test case for literary memory: the "warm wine and Hua Xiong" scene is treated as a novelized attribution, while the historical-source frame credits [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] at [[YangrenBattleLateHan|阳人之战]]. Second, Hanji 1041, Hanji 1043, and Hanji 1044 turn "关羽降曹" into constrained captivity rather than simple betrayal or a formal "关公约三事" contract: Guan Yu accepts temporary rank, repays Cao Cao by killing Yan Liang at White Horse, seals the gifts, and returns toward Liu Bei. Third, Hanji 1105 jumps to the endpoint: Guan Yu's northern campaign threatens Cao's center, Cao aligns with [[SunQuan|孙权]], and Guan Yu's death is followed by a respectful burial order in the episode's account.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a prominent boundary case between Three Kingdoms literary memory and source-grounded event attribution.
- Remains Liu Bei-aligned even when temporarily inside Cao Cao's camp after capture.
- Is valued by Cao Cao as a high-capacity military talent whose temporary service, repayment, and gift refusal do not erase prior allegiance.
- Provides the decisive vanguard action at White Horse by killing Yan Liang in the Yuan-side formation.
- Later becomes a northern strategic threat whose pressure helps drive Cao Cao toward coordination with Sun Quan.
- His death closes the current source arc with an episode-level account of Cao Cao ordering respectful burial.
- Also appears in the Xiapi household branch, where his request for [[LadyDuCaoCao|杜夫人]] inadvertently draws Cao Cao's attention to her.

## Evidence
- Literary-memory boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-955-guanyu-zhan-huaxiong-qishi-shi-wei-lishi-lndkrj-fnepye8iu-ifgyh-vzysf|Hanji 955]] contrasts the novelized Guan Yu killing of Hua Xiong with the episode's historical-source attribution to Sun Jian at Yangren.
- Xiapi household branch: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] says Guan Yu repeatedly asks Cao Cao for Lady Du after Xiapi, but the reminders prompt Cao Cao to inspect and take her himself.
- Captive service and rank: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1041-jiemi-lishishang-zhenshi-de-guanyu-jiangcao-luisp0ndk1lfrc87byygn6r8myhx|Hanji 1041]] records Cao Cao capturing Guan Yu, appointing him 偏将军, and treating him generously after the attack on Liu Bei.
- Residual loyalty and repayment promise: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1041-jiemi-lishishang-zhenshi-de-guanyu-jiangcao-luisp0ndk1lfrc87byygn6r8myhx|Hanji 1041]] has Guan Yu tell Zhang Liao that Liu Bei's恩义 prevents betrayal and that he will repay Cao Cao's treatment with merit before leaving.
- White Horse repayment act: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1043-yuanshao-tongma-caocao-jing-zhihaole-tade-piantouteng-ltibjj6xizsrynpgr9oyf-q3ydna|Hanji 1043]] says Guan Yu sees Yan Liang's banner, rides into the enemy formation, kills Yan Liang, takes the head, and lifts the White Horse siege.
- Departure endpoint: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1044-guanyu-qianli-zou-danqi-xu-ganxie-caocao-busha-enqing-lpptlnc-e4zu4lcjr49bquxdw0pm|Hanji 1044]] completes the arc by having Guan Yu seal Cao Cao's rewards, leave a letter, and return toward Liu Bei while Cao prevents pursuit.
- Northern-campaign endpoint: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1105-guanyu-zhisi-maizang-le-caocao-zenyang-de-mengxiang-lgmk_dlknjpzz7fvzasno6tu7fs3|Hanji 1105]] says Guan Yu's northern advance threatens Cao Cao, Sun Quan's pressure contributes to his death, and Cao Cao orders a respectful burial after receiving his head.

## Qualifications
The sources still do not provide a full account of Guan Yu's middle career or the operational sequence of his final campaign. Hanji 1041 rejects the historical status of "关公约三事" but not Guan Yu's loyalty; Hanji 1044 treats "千里走单骑" and "过五关斩六将" as Romance embellishments. Hanji 1105 is highly compressed, so the reported words to Guan Yu's head, the exact alliance mechanics, and the claim that the burial symbolically ended Cao Cao's youthful dream remain source-scoped.

## What Changed
- Extended the profile from captive service and departure to the final northern-campaign endpoint.
- Added Cao-Sun coordination and respectful burial as source-scoped closing claims.
- Preserved the gap between the early service arc and the later campaign rather than inventing a complete biography.

## Relationships
- [[LiuBei|刘备]] - prior lord whose恩义 remains Guan Yu's loyalty anchor under Cao Cao.
- [[CaoCao|曹操]] - captor and temporary patron who values and rewards Guan Yu.
- [[ZhangLiao|张辽]] - intermediary through whom Cao Cao tests Guan Yu's long-term intention and vanguard peer at White Horse.
- [[BaimaReliefLateHan|白马之围]] - event in which Guan Yu kills Yan Liang and lifts the siege.
- [[YanLiangLateHan|颜良]] - Yuan Shao general whose killing functions as Guan Yu's repayment merit before departure.
- [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]] - literary tradition whose famous Guan Yu scenes require source-critical separation.
- [[Sanguozhi|《三国志》]] - source tradition invoked for Guan Yu's capture, rank, treatment, and loyalty statement.
- [[CaptiveSubmissionResidualLoyalty|被俘归顺与旧主忠义]] - concept describing Guan Yu's temporary service under captivity without erased prior allegiance.
- [[LadyDuCaoCao|杜夫人]] - Xiapi household figure whose desirability is signaled by Guan Yu's repeated request.
- [[SunQuan|孙权]] - southern ruler whose pressure joins Cao Cao's response to Guan Yu's northern campaign.
