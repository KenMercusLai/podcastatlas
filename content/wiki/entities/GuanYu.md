# 关羽 / Guan Yu

## Overview
关羽 / Guan Yu is currently represented less as a full biography than as a source-critical and loyalty-under-pressure figure in the late-Han arc. The available evidence separates famous [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]] memory from chronicle-source claims, then places Guan Yu inside [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s camp after [[Xiapi|下邳]] as a captured military talent who repays favor by killing [[YanLiangLateHan|颜良]] and then returns toward [[LiuBei|刘备]].

## Current Profile
The current synthesis is that Guan Yu's wiki role has two layers. First, he is a test case for literary memory: the "warm wine and Hua Xiong" scene is treated as a novelized attribution, while the historical-source frame credits [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] at [[YangrenBattleLateHan|阳人之战]]. Second, Hanji 1041 and Hanji 1044 turn "关羽降曹" into a constrained-captivity case rather than a simple betrayal or the formal "关公约三事" contract. Cao Cao captures him, appoints him 偏将军, and treats him well; Guan Yu tells [[ZhangLiao|张辽]] that he cannot betray Liu Bei, then repays Cao Cao by killing Yan Liang before sealing Cao's gifts, leaving a farewell letter, and departing.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a prominent boundary case between Three Kingdoms literary memory and source-grounded event attribution.
- Remains Liu Bei-aligned even when temporarily inside Cao Cao's camp after capture.
- Is valued by Cao Cao as a high-capacity and loyal military talent, not only as a defeated enemy.
- Shows how captivity can create temporary service, repayment obligations, and gift refusal without erasing prior allegiance.
- Also appears in the Xiapi household branch, where his request for [[LadyDuCaoCao|杜夫人]] inadvertently draws Cao Cao's attention to her.

## Evidence
- Literary-memory boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-955-guanyu-zhan-huaxiong-qishi-shi-wei-lishi-lndkrj-fnepye8iu-ifgyh-vzysf|Hanji 955]] contrasts the novelized Guan Yu killing of Hua Xiong with the episode's historical-source attribution to Sun Jian at Yangren.
- Xiapi household branch: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] says Guan Yu repeatedly asks Cao Cao for Lady Du after Xiapi, but the reminders prompt Cao Cao to inspect and take her himself.
- Captive service and rank: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1041-jiemi-lishishang-zhenshi-de-guanyu-jiangcao-luisp0ndk1lfrc87byygn6r8myhx|Hanji 1041]] records Cao Cao capturing Guan Yu, appointing him 偏将军, and treating him generously after the attack on Liu Bei.
- Residual loyalty and repayment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1041-jiemi-lishishang-zhenshi-de-guanyu-jiangcao-luisp0ndk1lfrc87byygn6r8myhx|Hanji 1041]] has Guan Yu tell Zhang Liao that Liu Bei's恩义 prevents betrayal and that he will repay Cao Cao's treatment with merit before leaving.
- Repayment and departure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1044-guanyu-qianli-zou-danqi-xu-ganxie-caocao-busha-enqing-lpptlnc-e4zu4lcjr49bquxdw0pm|Hanji 1044]] completes that arc by having Guan Yu kill Yan Liang, seal Cao Cao's rewards, leave a letter, and return toward Liu Bei while Cao prevents pursuit.

## Qualifications
The page should not infer Guan Yu's full later career from the current bounded sources. Hanji 1041 rejects the historical status of "关公约三事" but does not deny Guan Yu's loyalty; Hanji 1044 relocates the departure into repayment, gift sealing, and Cao Cao's restraint. Hanji 1044 also treats "千里走单骑" and "过五关斩六将" as Romance-level embellishments rather than chronicle proof. Hanji 1013's Lady Du branch is a household and desire-pattern case, not a complete account of Guan Yu's family life.
