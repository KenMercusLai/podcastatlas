# Kérastase / 卡诗

Kérastase / 卡诗 appears in [[meizhuang-jutou-jiti-dingshang-toufa-xihu-shengyi-weihe-you-re-qilai-1005860109]] as one of [[LOreal|L'Oreal / 欧莱雅]]'s hair-care brands and as a high-end professional foreign brand in the cited 618 hair-care sales discussion. The episode uses it to show that category growth is not coming mainly from basic shampoo volume, but from premium, professional, and specific-need products.

Its wiki role is the professional-haircare example in [[PremiumHaircareMarket]] and [[SalonChannelTrust]]. The brand helps show why technical language, stylist recommendation, salon origin, and high-priced routines can matter in a category that mass consumers once treated as basic cleaning.

[[lvdian-xiaoyang-yao-tuiguang-tihuanxin-de-ba-meizhuang-chongzuo-yibian-1006757937]] adds Kérastase as part of [[LOreal|L'Oreal / 欧莱雅]]'s refillable beauty portfolio, mentioning hair oil as one refill example. That extends the brand from premium hair-care trust into [[RefillableBeautyPackaging]], where a high-value package has to remain useful across repeat purchases.
