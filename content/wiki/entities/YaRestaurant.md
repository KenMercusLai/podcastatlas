# 芽 / Ya Restaurant

芽 / Ya is the Yunnan-flavored vegetarian fine-dining restaurant discussed in [[ep245-yinian-yidu-xunwei-zhilv-jinnian-qu-chi-chuangyi-cai-li9wtavemokcckialbbj7unpmqsz]]. The episode presents it as a case of [[YunnanCuisineMenuNarrative|云南菜路线叙事]]: the menu uses the [[TeaHorseRoad|茶马古道]] and a route from highland areas toward the Lancang/Mekong line to organize ingredients, techniques, and flavor memory.

The restaurant matters because it does not simply reproduce village dishes. The source describes Qingke pastry, cashew-fermented plant butter, black-truffle buckwheat risotto, butter-tea soup, fermentation, and pickling as ways to translate [[Yunnan|云南]] into a modern menu while keeping the region's geography and food systems visible.
