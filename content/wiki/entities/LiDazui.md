# 李大嘴 / Li Dazui

## Overview

李大嘴 is the cook at 同福客栈 in [[WulinWaizhuan|《武林外传》]]. The bounded sources place him inside two stories shaped by his relationship with his mother: a martial-champion lie that becomes an ensemble deception, and a gambling episode in which an early win pulls him past a prior promise not to participate.

## Current Profile

In the martial-champion story, Li Dazui's filial motive is inseparable from dishonesty. His claim immediately revives his ill mother, but preserving it requires recruiting the whole inn, impersonating public institutions, and inventing new lies. His friends' cooperation becomes comic leverage because 郭芙蓉's cooking is the alternative.

The gambling story reverses who is enforcing reality. Dazui begins with a promise to his mother not to gamble, but [[BaiZhantang|白展堂]] provokes him into staking food. His first win becomes the bridge to another game, after which he loses a large inventory of food. [[DuanzhiXuanyuan|断指轩辕]] refuses to let him avoid punishment as a witness, then recovers what he lost through her own staged lesson. Maternal care again appears alongside accountability rather than replacing it.

## Key Characteristics

- A cook whose role and food stores become recurring sources of both plot leverage and comedy.
- Lies about becoming the martial-examination champion to comfort his reportedly dying mother.
- Converts one filial deception into a collective impersonation of capital-city institutions.
- Lacks the official vocabulary required by his claimed status, exposing the gap between role and knowledge.
- Breaks a promise not to gamble after provocation and an initially successful wager.
- Experiences maternal protection together with punishment, moral correction, and demands for responsibility.

## Qualifications

This profile covers two stories and one podcast's interpretations, not Li Dazui's complete series arc. The first source focuses on comic construction, while the second uses him mainly as the initially reluctant participant and object of maternal discipline; neither establishes a persistent gambling pattern across the wider series.

## What Changed

- Added the gambling story as a second case of maternal protection combined with accountability.
- Distinguished one early-reward-driven lapse from Bai Zhantang's more developed escalation pattern.
- Connected Dazui's food and labor world to the episode's argument that noncash stakes retain practical value.
