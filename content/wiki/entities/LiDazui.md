# 李大嘴 / Li Dazui

## Overview

李大嘴 is the cook at 同福客栈 in [[WulinWaizhuan|《武林外传》]]. In the bounded episode, he tells his ill mother that he has become the martial-examination champion, then asks his friends to construct a false Beijing so that the comforting lie will survive her visit.

## Current Profile

The story makes Li Dazui's filial motive inseparable from dishonesty. His claim immediately revives his mother, but preserving it requires recruiting the whole inn, impersonating public institutions, and repeatedly inventing new lies. His friends' cooperation is itself comic leverage: once 郭芙蓉's cooking becomes the alternative, helping him seems less unbearable.

Li Dazui is also the object of his mother's contradictory hopes. She wants him to avoid corruption, stop deceiving people, and accept the public duty attached to the status he claims; she simultaneously advises concealment, worries that he might kill someone powerful, and marks his back with “好汉饶命.” The tattoo turns apparent martial honor into a plea for survival while remaining consistent with her attachment to him.

## Key Characteristics

- A cook whose absence exposes the inn to 郭芙蓉's disastrous substitute cooking.
- Lies about becoming the martial-examination champion to comfort his reportedly dying mother.
- Converts one filial deception into a collective impersonation of capital-city and imperial institutions.
- Lacks the official vocabulary required by his claimed status, repeatedly choosing the wrong self-address before being corrected.
- Receives both moral correction and protective favoritism from his mother.
- Becomes the center of the final reversal when his tattoo reads “好汉饶命” rather than the expected patriotic motto.

## Qualifications

This profile covers one story and a podcast's interpretation of it, not Li Dazui's complete series arc. The supplied source focuses more on comic construction than on continuity across the show, and whether his mother detects the fraud before the porridge scene remains debated.
