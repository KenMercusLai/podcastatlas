# 诸葛亮 / Zhuge Liang

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] adds a positive state-building portrait before the darker Hanji 977 reading. The episode frames Zhuge Liang as the adviser who upgrades [[LiuBei|刘备]] from mobile survival politics into a state operating system, making him a key example of [[AdviserStateArchitecture|谋士政权架构]] rather than only a brilliant tactician.

This remains source-scoped because Hanji 976 is thematic and compressed. Its useful contribution is the contrast with [[YuanShao|袁绍]]: Zhuge Liang's advice matters because Liu Bei's camp is portrayed as able to institutionalize it.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-977-yi-zheng-yi-xie-luanshi-dushi-de-guijue-fengyun-lgtpgxl5anxial8ym2nt8dmuc3ee|Hanji 977]] adds a critical, source-scoped reading of Zhuge Liang as a user of moral pressure and information filtering. The episode says he could mobilize inherited mission language and loyal-minister vocabulary as political leverage, while also shaping what [[LiuBei|刘备]] saw about Jing-Yi personnel.

This does not replace the page's older, thinner reception-marker role. It adds Zhuge Liang to [[DarkAdviserCraft|毒士灰度谋略]] as the example where morality and information management become tools of rule. Because the source is thematic and fast-moving, claims about Liu Shan, Li Yan, and personnel filtering are kept as episode interpretation rather than settled biography.

诸葛亮 / Zhuge Liang enters this wiki branch through the title of [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-80-zhugeliang-de-ouxiang-daodi-you-duo-lihai-lse6qmqqhoyqdzqjl4qfuvioeiw0]], which frames [[LeYi|乐毅]] as "诸葛亮的偶像." The episode itself does not develop Zhuge Liang's biography; it uses his later reputation to signal Le Yi's standing among commanders and political actors.

For this source, Zhuge Liang is therefore a reception marker rather than an actor in the Warring States narrative. The substantive argument remains about Le Yi's military merit, post-exile restraint, and ability to survive after achievements that could frighten rulers.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-148-1-hanxin-yupan-le-zhanghan-de-yupan-lodhpuxpossjno87gorjkfrtitbe|Hanji 148-1]] adds Zhuge Liang as a comparative limit on route imitation. The host contrasts [[HanXin|韩信]]'s successful return from [[Hanzhong|汉中]] to [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] with Zhuge Liang's later northern expeditions from a similar geographic base. In this use, Zhuge Liang is not judged simply by personal ability; he marks the fact that terrain and historical goal cannot be copied apart from timing, opponent, army condition, and broader situation.
