# 诸葛亮 / Zhuge Liang

诸葛亮 / Zhuge Liang enters this wiki branch through the title of [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-80-zhugeliang-de-ouxiang-daodi-you-duo-lihai-lse6qmqqhoyqdzqjl4qfuvioeiw0]], which frames [[LeYi|乐毅]] as "诸葛亮的偶像." The episode itself does not develop Zhuge Liang's biography; it uses his later reputation to signal Le Yi's standing among commanders and political actors.

For this source, Zhuge Liang is therefore a reception marker rather than an actor in the Warring States narrative. The substantive argument remains about Le Yi's military merit, post-exile restraint, and ability to survive after achievements that could frighten rulers.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-148-1-hanxin-yupan-le-zhanghan-de-yupan-lodhpuxpossjno87gorjkfrtitbe|Hanji 148-1]] adds Zhuge Liang as a comparative limit on route imitation. The host contrasts [[HanXin|韩信]]'s successful return from [[Hanzhong|汉中]] to [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] with Zhuge Liang's later northern expeditions from a similar geographic base. In this use, Zhuge Liang is not judged simply by personal ability; he marks the fact that terrain and historical goal cannot be copied apart from timing, opponent, army condition, and broader situation.
