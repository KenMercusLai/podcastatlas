# 《中世纪的身体》

《中世纪的身体》 is recommended in [[yi-fen-laizi-bingchuang-shang-de-xiangguan-shudan-598284093]] as a way to see how medieval people understood the human body. The hosts mention hand-drawn body images, Hippocrates, humoral theory, skin, heart, hands, feet, blood, bathing, and manuscript images of saints holding heads or skins.

The book adds [[HistoricalBodyConcept]] to the wiki: bodies are biological, but every era also reads them through inherited theories, religious images, hygiene rules, and social practices. It therefore pairs naturally with [[BingMeigui|《病玫瑰》]], where visual knowledge also shapes medical imagination.
