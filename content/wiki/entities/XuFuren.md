# 徐夫人 / Xu Furen

徐夫人 / Xu Furen enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-3-jingke-ciqin-weihe-jiujiu-bu-xingdong-zai-deng-shui-liihe-kgwigu3bggxmyofgvotged]] as the [[ZhaoState|赵国]] figure associated with the dagger prepared for [[JingKe|荆轲]]'s assassination mission. The episode follows [[SimaZhen|司马贞]]'s note that "徐夫人" here is a man, not a woman, and that his name is attached to an especially sharp dagger.

The source makes the dagger part of the mission's operational design. After [[FanWuji|樊无忌]]'s head and the [[DuKangMap|督亢地图]] are prepared as access credentials, Xu Furen's dagger is poisoned and tested so that even a slight blood-drawing wound can be fatal.
