## Overview

西门吹雪 / Ximen Chuixue is the supreme swordsman in [[DuelBeforeAndAfterGuLong|《决战前后》]] whose marriage to 孙秀青, impending fatherhood, and duel with [[YeGucheng|叶孤城]] test whether humanity weakens, completes, or ethically reorients mastery.

## Current Profile

The bounded source distinguishes the earlier, deathlike sword devotee from a husband and prospective father who smiles, talks more, saves people, and prepares for his family's future. These changes are not treated as a simple decline. Attachment makes him more recognizably human and enables compassion, but it also creates fear of loss and possible hesitation in a contest previously governed by total concentration.

His conduct at the duel joins technical seriousness to respect. He refuses to exploit Ye before the latter regains composure, argues that a person without sincerity cannot truly discuss the sword, and reportedly tries to withdraw at the end. His survival may therefore reflect Ye's chosen death rather than conclusive superiority, leaving victory, mercy, and fulfillment inseparable.

## Key Characteristics

- Begins from an ideal of life wholly devoted to the sword.
- Gains warmth, restraint, and responsibility through marriage and impending fatherhood.
- Experiences attachment as both moral enlargement and potential technical vulnerability.
- Seeks a complete opponent rather than advantage over an unsettled one.
- Treats sincerity to people as inseparable from sincerity in swordsmanship.
- Understands and completes Ye's possible choice of death while losing the chance for an uncontested victory.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one novel as interpreted in one structured podcast summary. Whether affection lowers his skill, whether he would have lost without Ye's deviation, whether he tries to withdraw, and why later works move him back toward technical purity remain disputed or source-scoped. His marriage, sword philosophy, dialogue, and combat sequence have not been independently collated against the novel.
