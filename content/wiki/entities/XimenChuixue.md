## Overview

西门吹雪 / Ximen Chuixue is [[GuLong|古龙]]'s ritualized supreme swordsman, introduced in [[GoldenRocDynastyGuLong|《金鹏王朝》]] through stylized vengeance and later tested in [[DuelBeforeAndAfterGuLong|《决战前后》]] by marriage, impending fatherhood, and his duel with [[YeGucheng|叶孤城]].

## Current Profile

《金鹏王朝》 establishes the earlier figure through incense, bathing, fasting, white clothing, sparse speech, and a long journey to kill 洪涛 for the murder of a righteous man he never knew. The entrance makes justice, lethal skill, and ritual purity immediately legible, but the hosts question a moral certainty that authorizes killing without adequate investigation. His attraction therefore lies partly in stylized ethical fantasy rather than fully realistic judgment.

《决战前后》 distinguishes that deathlike sword devotee from a husband and prospective father who smiles, talks more, saves people, and prepares for his family's future. These changes are not treated as a simple decline. Attachment makes him more recognizably human and enables compassion, but it also creates fear of loss and possible hesitation in a contest previously governed by total concentration.

His conduct at the duel joins technical seriousness to respect. He refuses to exploit Ye before the latter regains composure, argues that a person without sincerity cannot truly discuss the sword, and reportedly tries to withdraw at the end. His survival may therefore reflect Ye's chosen death rather than conclusive superiority, leaving victory, mercy, and fulfillment inseparable.

## Key Characteristics

- Begins from ritual purity, lethal mastery, and self-authorized chivalric justice.
- Creates immediate aura through white clothing, silence, preparation, travel, and compressed language.
- Gains warmth, restraint, and responsibility through marriage and impending fatherhood.
- Experiences attachment as both moral enlargement and potential technical vulnerability.
- Seeks a complete opponent rather than advantage over an unsettled one.
- Treats sincerity to people as inseparable from sincerity in swordsmanship.
- Understands and completes Ye's possible choice of death while losing the chance for an uncontested victory.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to two novels as interpreted in two structured podcast summaries. Whether his first act is justice or reckless private execution, whether affection lowers his skill, whether he would have lost without Ye's deviation, whether he tries to withdraw, and why later works move him back toward technical purity remain disputed or source-scoped. His vengeance, marriage, sword philosophy, dialogue, and combat sequences have not been independently collated against the novels.

## What Changed

- Added ritualized vengeance as the foundation of his aura and ethical ambiguity.
- Reframed later family attachment as a change from self-authorized lethal certainty rather than only abstract technical purity.
