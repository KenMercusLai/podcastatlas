# 侯公 / Hou Gong (Chu-Han)

Hou Gong / 侯公 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-166-jiran-xiangyu-bujiang-wude-kan-liubang-ruhe-fuchou-ltwwbn9rzeqzzimmxxfihb9-drj1|Hanji 166]] as the Liu Bang-side envoy who succeeds where [[LuJia|陆贾]] fails. After [[XiangYu|项羽]] rejects Lu Jia's first peace approach, Liu Bang sends Hou Gong, who helps secure the [[HonggouSettlement|鸿沟议和]].

The episode does not give Hou Gong an independent biography. His importance is functional: he becomes the successful negotiation channel through which [[LiuBang|刘邦]] recovers [[LiuTaigong|刘太公]] and [[LuZhi|吕雉]] before turning back toward pursuit. This page uses the disambiguated title "Hou Gong (Chu-Han)" because 侯公 is a generic address-like name and the source gives no stable fuller identity.
