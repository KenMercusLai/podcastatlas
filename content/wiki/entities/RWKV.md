# RWKV

RWKV is the open-source model project around which [[Recursor]] originally built its product in [[featherless-ai-when-your-weekend-experiment-makes-more-than-your-startup]]. [[EugeneChia]] describes it as a next-generation foundation model direction with the potential for much cheaper inference.

The episode's main RWKV lesson is not a technical evaluation of the architecture. It is a pivot lesson: a model can fit a mission of cheaper and broader AI access, while the market still pulls harder toward already demanded model families such as [[Llama]], [[MistralAI|Mistral]], [[Qwen]], and [[DeepSeek]].

## Connections
- [[Recursor]] - company/product direction built around RWKV before the pivot.
