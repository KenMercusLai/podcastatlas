# 桂阳郡 / Guiyang Commandery (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-919-xiang-huode-hao-xian-tikai-shanliang-de-ziji-lrqxfzir4bqxt0v3ccvfef8faade|Hanji 919]] adds an earlier Guiyang security branch: bandits from Guiyang join bandits from [[CangwuCommanderyLateHan|Cangwu]] in attacking commanderies and counties until [[YangXuanLateHan|Yang Xuan]] of [[LinglingCommanderyLateHan|Lingling]] suppresses them with an improvised lime, fire-horse, and crossbow-wagon tactic. The note ties Guiyang to cross-commandery disorder rather than a single contained local uprising.

桂阳郡 / Guiyang Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1027-jingzhou-zhizhu-liubiao-weihe-nan-cheng-bazhu-ll-ebguqllsf-5ycvpdogihzn3lj|Hanji 1027]] as the third commandery in the [[SouthernJingzhouLateHan|southern Jingzhou]] alignment that [[HuanJieLateHan|桓阶]] recommends to [[ZhangXianLateHan|张献]]. It appears as part of a regional structure rather than as a separately narrated commandery.

In the source, Guiyang helps make the breakaway large enough to matter. Together with [[ChangshaCommanderyLateHan|长沙郡]] and [[LinglingCommanderyLateHan|零陵郡]], it represents nearly half the source's stated Jingzhou commandery field, so its movement toward [[CaoCao|曹操]] turns southern local discontent into a constraint on [[LiuBiao|刘表]].
