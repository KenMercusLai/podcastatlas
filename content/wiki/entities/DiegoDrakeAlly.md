# Diego (Drake Ally)

Diego appears in [[693-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-englands-greatest-hero-part-3-glt5069845329]] as an African man enslaved by the Spanish who defects to [[FrancisDrake]] during the [[NombreDeDios]] raid. The episode presents him as a close ally who introduces Drake to the [[Cimarrones]], escaped African fighters in Panama.

Diego makes the source's moral contradiction concrete. Drake had participated in slaving with [[JohnHawkins]], yet his most important Panama alliance depends on an escaped enslaved man and on black military partners who want revenge on [[Spain]].

The source later places Diego on the 1577 expedition with Drake. After Indigenous islanders off Chile mistake the English for Spanish, both Drake and Diego are wounded, and Diego later dies.
