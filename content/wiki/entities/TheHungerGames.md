## Overview

The Hunger Games is a future dystopian franchise whose Panem, Capitol, tributes, classical names, and death spectacle make Roman political symbolism unusually explicit.

## Current Profile

The episode treats the franchise as futuristic Rome without a space setting. A collapsed North America sends district children as tributes to fight to the death, while names such as Coriolanus, Plutarch, and Cato and the labels Panem and Capitol turn classical reference into immediate political shorthand for center–periphery domination and spectacle.

## Key Characteristics

- Future North American polity organized around a dominating capital.
- Children sent as district tributes into lethal public spectacle.
- Overt Roman vocabulary in Panem, the Capitol, and character names.
- Gladiatorial imagery used to give young-adult dystopia political and historical weight.
