## Overview

袁盎 / Yuan Ang is a Western Han official whose current profile centers on a succession remonstrance to [[EmpressDowagerDouWesternHan|窦太后]] and the episode's opening notice that his later assassination was connected to [[LiuWuLiangKingWesternHan|梁孝王刘武]].

## Current Profile

In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-240-hanjingdi-kusu-xiongnu-he-taihou-sha-le-wode-ying-lvl4t-2etswvi3ts9gbvor5dh8mi|Hanji 240]], Yuan Ang opposes the dowager's wish to make Liu Wu successor to [[HanJingdi|汉景帝]]. He uses the story of Song Xuangong: placing a brother ahead of the ruler's son does not settle succession but transfers the conflict into the next generation, where competing descendant lines help produce a five-generation disorder.

The intervention works by reframing maternal affection as a state-risk problem. Yuan does not merely tell the dowager to suppress feeling; he asks whether preserving a “small love” is worth exposing the dynasty to repeated civil conflict. The episode says she accepts the warning and sends Liu Wu back to Liang, making historical analogy a practical form of court remonstrance.

The source opens with Yuan's assassination in 148 BCE and says an investigation connects it to Liu Wu, but the supplied body stops before narrating the killing plot or adjudication. His current profile therefore records the earlier political reason for Liang hostility without filling the later evidentiary gap.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official presented as an opponent of collateral succession in favor of the emperor's established line.
- Remonstrant who uses a historical analogy rather than a direct personal accusation against the dowager or Liang king.
- Advocate of larger dynastic duty over a senior royal's immediate family preference.
- Court actor whose warning reportedly helps send Liu Wu back to his fief.
- Later assassination victim whose connection to Liang is announced but not fully narrated in the supplied episode.

## Qualifications

This profile is based on one short source note rather than a full biography. The exact succession proposal, Song precedent, wording, audience procedure, dowager's reasoning, timing of Liu Wu's return, assassination participants, investigative evidence, and final adjudication require broader textual comparison. The episode establishes an asserted Liang connection, not a complete murder case.

## What Changed

- Kept the announced assassination connection distinct from a fully narrated or proven Liang murder mechanism.
