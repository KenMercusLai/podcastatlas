## Overview

刘不亥 / Liu Buhai is presented as the elder non-heir son of [[LiuAn|淮南王刘安]] whose marginal position within the Huainan household becomes a grievance for his own son and a source of conflict with crown prince [[LiuQianHuainanCrownPrinceWesternHan|刘迁]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-302-1-xihan-shuzhi-neidou-jieguo-kengdie-ye-kengye-ln6rfk8nzoyeqxduik_edlqqiyug|Hanji 302-1]] says Liu An dislikes Liu Buhai, the queen does not treat him as a son, and Liu Qian does not treat him as an elder brother. Liu Buhai is characterized as enduring this unequal treatment rather than openly resisting it.

The conflict moves to the next generation. Liu Buhai's son reportedly resents his father's humiliation, discusses removing his crown-prince uncle, and is arrested and tortured after the plan leaks. The episode therefore makes Liu Buhai important less as an active conspirator than as the marginalized branch around which inherited resentment forms.

## Key Characteristics

- Elder son of Liu An but not the designated crown prince.
- Member of a marginalized branch within the Huainan royal household.
- Son reportedly rejected by his father, the queen, and his crown-prince brother.
- Figure characterized as enduring unequal treatment rather than retaliating directly.
- Father whose treatment motivates his son to challenge the favored heir branch.

## Qualifications

The only current source is a compact podcast summary. It gives no independent account of Liu Buhai's mother, age, offices, later fate, or own political intentions. The transcript also alternates between 刘不亥 and 刘不怀; this page uses 刘不亥 because that is the summary's primary form, while preserving the uncertainty. The son's name is not normalized because the transcript appears to duplicate 刘谦, already the crown prince's name.

## What Changed

- Created the page to distinguish the marginalized elder-son branch from the established crown-prince identity.
- Preserved the transcript uncertainty around Liu Buhai's name and left his son's identity unresolved.
- Framed the durable contribution as inherited household grievance rather than proved participation by Liu Buhai.
