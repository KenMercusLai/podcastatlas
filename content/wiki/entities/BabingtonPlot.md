# Babington Plot

The Babington Plot appears in [[696-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-killing-the-queen-of-scots-part-6-glt2334035496]] as the conspiracy that gives [[FrancisWalsingham]] and [[WilliamCecil|William Cecil / Lord Burleigh]] the evidence path for trying [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]]. [[AnthonyBabington]]'s plan links assassination of [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]], Mary's release, Catholic domestic rising, foreign invasion, and restoration of Catholicism in [[England]].

The plot is exposed through [[SpyNetworkEntrapment]]. [[GilbertGifford]]'s beer-barrel courier channel appears secure to Mary's supporters, but it is supervised by Walsingham and decrypted by [[ThomasPhillips]].

In the episode's synthesis, the plot matters because it turns Mary from dangerous claimant into written conspirator under the [[ActForTheQueensSafety|Act for the Queen's Safety]]. It therefore connects underground conspiracy to formal trial, public execution, and later [[ConfessionalWarEscalation]].
