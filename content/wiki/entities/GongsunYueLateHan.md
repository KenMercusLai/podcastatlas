# 公孙越 / Gongsun Yue (late Han)

公孙越 / Gongsun Yue (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-958-jiemi-shishang-zui-duanming-de-tongchoudikai-lve41q9gyijbop8wk-b7e6shogju|Hanji 958]] as [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]]'s kinsman and envoy to [[YuanShu|袁术]]. Gongsun Zan sends him with one thousand cavalry after warning [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] not to trust Yuan Shu's request for troops.

The source then turns Gongsun Yue into the human fuse for the [[YuanShao|袁绍]]-Gongsun Zan rupture. Yuan Shu sends him to assist [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] against [[ZhouAngLateHan|周昂]], Yuan Shao's appointee in the Yuzhou office fight, and Gongsun Yue is killed by a stray arrow. Gongsun Zan treats the death as Yuan Shao's responsibility, even though the episode keeps the deeper cause in the struggle over territory and offices.
