# 秋灵素（古龙人物） / Qiu Lingsu (Gu Long character)

## Overview

秋灵素 / Qiu Lingsu is a former celebrated beauty in [[BloodSeaFragranceGuLong|《血海飘香》]] whose disfigurement, marriage to 仁慈, failed appeal for help, and prior cruelty prevent a simple movement from “femme fatale” to purified victim.

## Current Profile

She first appears through a portrait and a painter's memory, including the disturbing claim that she blinded him after the work. The later account of [[ShiGuanyin|石观音]] coercing her to choose death or disfigurement creates real sympathy, while 仁慈's acceptance separates care from restored beauty.

The episode resists retrospective cleansing. Suffering does not explain the painter's blinding, and the source's fairy-tale-like beauty competition narrows both women around appearance. Her request for aid also draws former admirers into the conspiracy's violence, leaving her as victim, prior aggressor, wife, and endangered witness at once.

## Key Characteristics

- Enters indirectly through portrait, testimony, and remembered beauty.
- Suffers coercive disfigurement driven by another woman's jealousy.
- Receives care from 仁慈 after the loss of conventional beauty.
- Seeks help against 南宫灵's poisoning and confinement of 仁慈.
- Retains responsibility for an earlier act of cruelty that victimization does not erase.
