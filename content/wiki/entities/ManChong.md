## Overview

满宠 / Man Chong is a Cao-side official whose current profile joins severe legal enforcement, reputation-sensitive political prosecution, and an anti-withdrawal judgment during the flood-damaged defense of Fancheng.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1006-guojia-buman-yuanshao-weihe-que-neng-guishun-caocao-lup-p-inz1sdlhpnaggqjp0eavar|Hanji 1006]] presents Man Chong as the Xu official who turns [[CaoHong|曹洪]]'s lawbreaking clients into a public-authority test. Cao Hong first writes to him and then seeks [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s help, but Man Chong executes the offenders before kinship access can become exemption. Cao Cao's approval makes the case [[RuleOverKinshipPower|法度压过亲缘特权]], although it remains ruler-backed governance rather than a modern rule-of-law equivalence.

The [[YangBiao|杨彪]] prosecution supplies a second legal context. [[KongRong|孔融]] and [[XunYu|荀彧]] urge Man Chong not to torture Yang Biao; the source says he proceeds, then reports that no incriminating statement has been produced and warns that punishing a famous old minister without evidence would damage public confidence. This makes him part of [[PrestigeConstrainedPoliticalProsecution|名望约束下的政治追诉]], while the source's benign-intention reading does not excuse or independently validate coercive procedure. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1020-sanguo-baijiazi-yuanshu-ruhe-ba-hao-pai-da-de-xilan-lhwr9hfmanr-zktleufy3kvl38u0|Hanji 1020]]

The [[FanchengBattle219LateHan|Battle of Fancheng]] moves Man Chong's profile from legal administration into military crisis judgment. After flooding destroys Yu Jin's field position and damages the city, he opposes evacuation, argues that the water will recede and that abandonment could expose the region south of the Yellow River, and helps stabilize [[CaoRen|曹仁]]'s decision to hold for relief. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1166-ruhe-yu-piqi-da-nengli-qiang-de-ren-xiangchu-lhm17dmeln7dmj1czmeq7omxgtmh|Hanji 1166]]

## Key Characteristics

- Enforces local authority against clients protected by Cao-clan access.
- Uses severe procedure and evidence absence together in the Yang Biao case.
- Treats political reputation and public confidence as constraints on punishment.
- Reads temporary flood conditions against the enduring regional cost of evacuation.
- Influences a pressured commander by connecting immediate survival to wider strategic exposure.

## Evidence

## Qualifications

The three source notes do not provide a full biography. Hanji 1006's execution sequence, authority, and political signaling remain source-scoped. Hanji 1020 does not establish the accusation, exact interrogation method, private intention, or whether the final report was planned from the beginning; a release outcome does not erase coercive harm. Hanji 1166's flood timing, wall condition, dialogue, regional forecast, and causal contribution to the defense require broader textual comparison.
