# 满宠 / Man Chong

满宠 / Man Chong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1020-sanguo-baijiazi-yuanshu-ruhe-ba-hao-pai-da-de-xilan-lhwr9hfmanr-zktleufy3kvl38u0|Hanji 1020]] as the Xudu official assigned by [[CaoCao|曹操]] to interrogate [[YangBiao|杨彪]]. [[KongRong|孔融]] and [[XunYu|荀彧]] urge him not to use torture, but the source says he proceeds and then reports that Yang Biao has provided no incriminating statement.

The episode reads Man Chong's role as more complex than simple cruelty. His final report gives Cao Cao a path to release Yang Biao: without firm evidence, punishing a famous old minister would lose public confidence. That makes Man Chong part of [[PrestigeConstrainedPoliticalProsecution|名望约束下的政治追诉]], where process, reputation, and political trust interact.

After Yang Biao is freed, Kong Rong and Xun Yu are said to understand Man Chong's intention and respect him more. The page keeps that as the source's interpretation, not as a general defense of torture.
