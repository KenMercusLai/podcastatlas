# Daniel Kahneman

## Overview

Daniel Kahneman appears in the Wiki as a behavioral-economics bridge between rational-choice models and the context-sensitive behavior of actual consumers and investors. His source role is practical rather than biographical.

## Current Profile

Across the represented sources, Kahneman anchors three connected claims. Losses and reference points can shape choice asymmetrically; felt risk is filtered through worldview and experience before it becomes portfolio math; and learning bias vocabulary does not by itself correct behavior. The newest source therefore shifts his practical relevance from diagnosis toward process design: identify repeat-error situations, record reasons before outcomes are known, and evaluate decision quality separately from luck.

## Key Characteristics

- **Model boundary:** the rational-person assumption is useful for analysis but incomplete as a description or life norm.
- **Reference-dependent choice:** ownership, loss, framing, and context can change economic and investment behavior.
- **Risk is felt as well as calculated:** personality, upbringing, worldview, and social models shape what an investor can hold.
- **Bias literacy is insufficient:** naming a bias does not guarantee that the person can suppress it in real time.
- **Process can compensate for unreliable intuition:** rules, records, and institutional checks can target repeated error situations.

## Evidence

- **Loss aversion and ordinary choice:** [[155-meimao-neng-dang-fan-chi-ma-xiang-zhuanqian-gai-zuo-dian-sha-jieju-shi-ying-bimian-shenme-xingwei-jingjixue-siwei-you-shenme-yong-920167210]] uses prospect-theory-style loss aversion to connect real-money ownership, consumption, and investment behavior.
- **Risk perception and worldview:** [[vol-110-touzi-jiushi-dui-shijieguan-de-toupiao-maichu-zichan-peizhi-di-yi-bu-wanjiepian-lk5jkgnzvzerymcixylsdqdtn7f8]] routes [[PaulSlovic]]'s risk-perception argument through Kahneman and into portfolio suitability.
- **Bias knowledge versus behavioral correction:** [[touzizhe-de-diren-wo-yu-wo-zhouxuan-jiu-ljhveb4f95emokkbltnvlmbxvbmd]] invokes Kahneman to argue that knowing many biases does not automatically improve real decisions and recommends situation-specific process controls.

## Qualifications

- These sources use Kahneman as an interpretive authority; they do not provide a complete account of his research program, collaborations, or later debates.
- The newest source's practical recommendations are episode-attributed and should not be treated as a verbatim or comprehensive statement of Kahneman's own prescriptions.
- Behavioral explanations do not establish that every loss, disagreement, or strategy failure is caused by bias.

## What Changed

- The profile now separates bias literacy from successful behavior change.
- Decision logging and process evaluation are added as practical controls against memory rewriting and outcome bias.
