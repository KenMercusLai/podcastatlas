# 张大明 / Zhang Daming (Mental-Health Guest)

## Overview

## Current Profile
His public-education approach combines accessible Freudian history with a functional clinical boundary. Dreams can be explored through wishes, metaphor, memory, stress, bodily sensation, and personal context, but they should not be treated as prophecies or fixed diagnostic signs. What matters clinically is whether sleep and dream experiences create persistent distress or daytime impairment, and whether a distinct parasomnia such as sleepwalking is present.

The supplied source identifies him by name and role in the discussion but does not establish an institutional affiliation, specialty credential, or complete professional biography. Those details therefore remain open rather than inferred.

- Connects dream recall to sleep-stage timing without using dream quantity alone as a measure of sleep quality.
- Uses daytime impairment and subjective distress as practical escalation thresholds.
- Distinguishes sleepwalking from ordinary dreaming and routes it toward qualified clinical assessment.
