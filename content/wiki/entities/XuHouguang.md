# Xu Houguang / 徐厚广

Xu Houguang is the high-speed-rail commentator cited by [[Banlatte]] in [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]]. The episode says Xu wrote under the name "高铁见闻", had worked inside the high-speed-rail system doing publicity, and became known as an active public explainer of Chinese high-speed rail.

The page matters as source-provenance context. Banlatte explicitly says it draws heavily on Xu's narrative frame, so claims about [[ChineseHighSpeedRail]], the [[WenzhouTrainCollision]], and post-accident public discussion should be read as filtered through a railway-informed but not neutral vantage point.
