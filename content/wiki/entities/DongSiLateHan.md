# 董祀 / Dong Si (late Han)

## Overview

[[DongSiLateHan|董祀]] enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1116-bei-xiongnu-luzou-12-nian-caiwenji-ruhe-qizi-guihan-lgqrifny2iyuohlal306ezntwbc6|Hanji 1116]] as the official whom [[CaiWenji|蔡文姬]] marries after [[CaoCao|曹操]] ransoms her back from the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]]. His profile is limited to the episode's marriage-and-pardon narrative.

## Current Profile

The source calls Dong Si a young tuntian commandant and says he initially resists the marriage. His main narrative function comes later: after he commits a capital offence, Cai Wenji risks entering Cao Cao's gathering to plead for him and offers a practical way to recall the execution order. The episode interprets the rescue as changing his understanding of her, but that psychological development is not independently established.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Han official identified by the episode as a tuntian commandant.
- Post-return husband of Cai Wenji under an arrangement attributed to Cao Cao.
- Condemned offender whose life is spared after Cai Wenji's intervention.

## Qualifications

The page records one podcast's retelling. Dong Si's exact office, offence, marriage attitude, the legal and messenger sequence, Cao Cao's gathering, and the claimed transformation in his feelings remain source-scoped until checked against primary historical material.

## What Changed

- Kept the episode's psychological account separate from the narrower event claim.
