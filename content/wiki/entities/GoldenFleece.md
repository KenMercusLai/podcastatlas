# Golden Fleece / 金羊毛

## Overview

The Golden Fleece is the guarded Colchian object whose promised recovery launches [[Jason]]'s voyage, brings him into dependence on [[MedeaCharacter|Medea]], and supplies the heroic prehistory later dismantled by [[Euripides]]' [[MedeaPlay|Medea]].

## Current Profile

[[605-greek-myths-jason-the-quest-for-the-golden-fleece-part-4-glt3067620137]] gives the fleece a layered origin. Phrixus and Helle escape on a flying golden ram; Helle falls into the Hellespont, while Phrixus reaches Colchis. The fleece then becomes associated with the prosperity of King Aetes's realm and is guarded by a dragon in a sacred grove.

Pelias sends Jason to recover it under an oracle- and succession-laden pretext. In the [[Argonautica]], the object organizes a series of trials and alliances, but it does not explain success by itself: Medea's magic and counsel make possession possible. [[67-meidiya-gu-xila-qinxianglian-de-fuchou-ji-qi-xiandaixing-662400556]] then uses the quest as the moral background to the later tragedy, because Jason's prestige rests on sacrifices he subsequently discounts.

## Key Characteristics

- Sacred and prosperity-bearing object located in Colchis.
- Remnant of the golden ram that carried Phrixus and Helle from danger.
- Quest goal used by Pelias to send Jason away on a near-impossible mission.
- Narrative bridge between heroic adventure and the later tragedy of Jason and Medea.
- Object whose recovery reveals collective help and obligation beneath individual heroic fame.

## Evidence

- Origin and location: [[605-greek-myths-jason-the-quest-for-the-golden-fleece-part-4-glt3067620137]] connects the ram, Helle's fall, Phrixus's arrival, Aetes, prosperity, and the guarding dragon.
- Quest function: [[605-greek-myths-jason-the-quest-for-the-golden-fleece-part-4-glt3067620137]] connects Pelias's throne promise, the ghost of Phrixus, oracle language, and Jason's Colchian trials.
- Moral afterlife: [[67-meidiya-gu-xila-qinxianglian-de-fuchou-ji-qi-xiandaixing-662400556]] uses the fleece to establish the debt to Medea that Jason later minimizes.
- Layered tradition: [[67-meidiya-gu-xila-qinxianglian-de-fuchou-ji-qi-xiandaixing-662400556]] and [[605-greek-myths-jason-the-quest-for-the-golden-fleece-part-4-glt3067620137]] treat the fleece as part of a connected but variable mythic system rather than an isolated object story.

## Qualifications

The sources summarize Pindaric, Euripidean, and Apollonian material rather than establishing a single original fleece narrative. Prosperity, royal succession, ghostly command, and heroic testing are overlapping literary functions, not necessarily one consistent ancient theology. Calling the fleece a MacGuffin clarifies plot function but understates its dynastic and sacred meanings.

## What Changed

- Added the Phrixus-Helle origin, Colchian prosperity claim, sacred grove, and guarded-object setting.
- Distinguished the fleece's plot function from the obligations created during its recovery.
