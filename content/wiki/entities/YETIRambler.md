# YETI Rambler

YETI Rambler is the drinkware line discussed in [[yeti-ron-and-ryan-seiders-how-two-brothers-turned-a-400-cooler-into-a-2-billion-brand-d7f8e0e8-51a5-4444-8c1d-f5c43c353285]] as the product that broadened [[YETI]] beyond high-priced hard coolers. The episode says [[RoySeiders]] saw Rambler as the first mass-appeal YETI product because a roughly $30 cup let more customers participate in the brand.

## Key Points
- Ryan brought Roy a vacuum-insulated bottle, helping point the company toward drinkware.
- The 20 oz and 30 oz cups lowered the purchase threshold relative to a $300-$400 cooler.
- The line reportedly helped YETI grow from roughly $100 million to about $400 million in sales over about 18 months, with supply constrained by demand.
- Rambler extends YETI's brand moat by turning a rugged cooler reputation into an everyday-carry product.
