# 《疑孟》 / Yi Meng

《疑孟》 / Yi Meng appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-26-mengzi-laile-ln4twwp2frmr-xpumu0rqg-2gpyn]] as [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s text questioning [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]]. The episode uses it to complicate the simple idea that Sima Guang's sympathetic reconciliation of the renyi-benefit passage means he personally admired Mencius.

Its wiki role is source-scoped. The page records that the episode places Yi Meng inside [[MenciusSongReception|宋代孟子接受史]], where [[WangAnshi|王安石]]'s enthusiasm for Mencius and Song reform politics made Mencius's status intellectually and politically charged.
