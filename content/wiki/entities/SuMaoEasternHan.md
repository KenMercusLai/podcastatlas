## Current Profile

Hanji 686 places Su Mao in the unsettled former-Gengshi and Liu Yong field. He rebels, kills the Huaiyang administrator Pan Jian, occupies Gangle/Guangle, and then submits to Liu Yong. Liu Yong's appointment of Su Mao as Da Sima makes him part of the rival military structure that Liu Xiu's side still has to clear during early consolidation.

Hanji 692 gives Su Mao a more developed battlefield role. At Chuihui, he comes with Wuxiao troops to rescue [[ZhouJianEasternHan|周建]]. Together they defeat Ma Wu, creating the crisis that sends Ma Wu to Wang Ba's camp for help.

Wang Ba's reading of Su Mao drives the episode's military lesson. Su Mao's troops are described as elite and numerous enough that immediate rescue would be dangerous. Once Wang Ba refuses battle, Su Mao and Zhou Jian attack Ma Wu for a long time, which exhausts them and opens the window for Wang Ba's rear cavalry.

Su Mao later returns with Zhou Jian to challenge Wang Ba, but Wang Ba infers that Su Mao's force has poor provisions and needs a quick battle. When Wang Ba refuses to leave his fortified camp, Su Mao withdraws; after Zhou Song closes Chuihui's gate against Zhou Jian, Su Mao flees to Xiapi and joins Dong Xian.

## Qualifications

The current page should not infer Su Mao's full factional biography from two compact source notes. Hanji 686 gives his early rebellion, killing, occupation, and Liu Yong alignment, but Liu Yong, Pan Jian, and Gangle/Guangle remain source-scoped here. Hanji 692 supplies the Wang Ba-facing battlefield profile, while Dong Xian, Xiapi, exact Wuxiao composition, Chuihui's location, and the campaign sequence after flight remain source-scoped until additional source notes require expansion.
