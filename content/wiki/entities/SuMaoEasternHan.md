## Overview
苏茂 / Su Mao is currently a source-scoped Guangwu-era opponent in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-692-donghan-kaiguo-dajiang-wangba-de-rensheng-you-duo-baqi-lmqqtsxwemetqqae-qdierska9pl|Hanji 692]]. He appears as the commander whose Wuxiao force helps defeat [[MaWuEasternHan|马武]], overpresses after [[WangBaEasternHan|王霸]] refuses immediate rescue, loses to Wang Ba's delayed rear-cavalry strike, and later fails to draw Wang Ba into a quick battle under supply pressure.

## Current Profile
Hanji 692 places Su Mao at Chuihui, coming with Wuxiao troops to rescue [[ZhouJianEasternHan|周建]]. Together they defeat Ma Wu, creating the crisis that sends Ma Wu to Wang Ba's camp for help.

Wang Ba's reading of Su Mao drives the episode's military lesson. Su Mao's troops are described as elite and numerous enough that immediate rescue would be dangerous. Once Wang Ba refuses battle, Su Mao and Zhou Jian attack Ma Wu for a long time, which exhausts them and opens the window for Wang Ba's rear cavalry.

Su Mao later returns with Zhou Jian to challenge Wang Ba, but Wang Ba infers that Su Mao's force has poor provisions and needs a quick battle. When Wang Ba refuses to leave his fortified camp, Su Mao withdraws; after Zhou Song closes Chuihui's gate against Zhou Jian, Su Mao flees to Xiapi and joins Dong Xian.

## Key Characteristics
- Opposing commander whose initial strength makes immediate rescue risky.
- Tactical overpresser whose long attack on Ma Wu creates the exhaustion Wang Ba waits for.
- Supply-constrained challenger whose second attempt depends on drawing Wang Ba into quick battle.
- Source-scoped figure whose broader career is not developed in the current wiki.
