# Republican People's Party

The Republican People's Party, or CHP, appears in [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]] as Turkey's old secular social-democratic opposition party. The episode says [[OzgurOzel]] led it to a strong 2024 local-election result before a court removed him as leader and returned [[KemalKilicdaroglu]] to the post.

The source presents the CHP as a party being stripped of political function. At least 91 MPs defect to [[NewPartyTurkey|the New Party]], polling falls into single digits, and a July 25 ceremony for new members is described as relying on paid extras. The page therefore becomes the institutional counterpart to [[OppositionPartyHollowing]].

## Connections
- [[OzgurOzel]] - former leader whose removal triggers the split.
- [[KemalKilicdaroglu]] - former leader returned by the court.
- [[NewPartyTurkey]] - party that receives many defecting CHP MPs.
- [[EkremImamoglu]] and [[MansurYavas]] - CHP-associated opposition figures under pressure.
- [[RecepTayyipErdogan]] - incumbent whose government the party opposes.
