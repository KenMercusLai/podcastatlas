# 赵武侯 / Marquis Wu of Zhao

赵武侯 / Marquis Wu of Zhao appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-1-lvkp-ju7pf5z5cz3thojv3s2fkyx]] as the younger brother installed after [[ZhaoLiehou|赵烈侯]] dies. The source uses this as one of several early [[WarringStatesPeriod|战国时期]] accession notices rather than as a detailed biography.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-2-lvaqlgyccrugfo0l-wuwf1mt1kcm]] records Zhao Wuhou's death in 387 BCE. Zhao people then install Zhao Liehou's son [[ZhaoJinghou|赵靖侯]], shifting the Zhao branch from Wuhou's accession to the next succession.
