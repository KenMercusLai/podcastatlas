# Lori Gottlieb / 洛莉·戈特利布

## Overview
[[LoriGottlieb|Lori Gottlieb / 洛莉·戈特利布]] appears in the wiki as the therapist-author of [[MaybeYouShouldTalkToSomeone|《也许你该找个人聊聊》 / Maybe You Should Talk to Someone]].

## Current Profile
The Culture Limited source profiles Gottlieb through movement across storytelling and care work. Her early Hollywood and writing background helps explain why the book reads like a character ensemble, while her later clinical work gives the stories their psychotherapy frame. The episode's most important point is her double position: she treats clients while also entering therapy herself after a sudden breakup, making vulnerability part of the book's authority rather than a contradiction of it.

## Key Characteristics
- She is presented as both therapist and client, not only as an observing expert.
- Her Hollywood/story-development background is used to explain the book's narrative strength.
- Her clinical identity is associated with marriage and family therapy, public psychology writing, podcasting, and broad integrative practice.
- Her own breakup story exposes uncertainty, anger, future loss, and the limits of professional self-knowledge.
- Her public-facing advice in the episode stresses preparation for sessions and action after insight.
