# 王肱 / Wang Gong (late Han)

王肱 / Wang Gong (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] as the [[DongCommandery|东郡]] administrator who cannot withstand the attack by [[YuDuLateHan|于毒]], [[BaiRaoLateHan|白绕]], [[SuiGuLateHan|眭固]], and their large armed following.

The source uses Wang Gong mainly to explain the office opening for [[CaoCao|曹操]]. Once Wang Gong cannot hold Dong Commandery, Cao Cao enters the field, defeats Bai Rao near [[PuyangLateHan|濮阳]], and is then recommended by [[YuanShao|袁绍]] for Wang Gong's commandery position.
