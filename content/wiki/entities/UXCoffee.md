# UX Coffee

## Overview
UX Coffee is the guest podcast and practitioner context in [[anyway-195-anyway-195]], where its hosts discuss moving from UI/UX-centered media work toward an offline Chinese-language bookstore and broader maker/craft interests.

## Current Profile
The source presents UX Coffee as a design podcast whose original mission was to share UI/UX work methods, interview designers, and help listeners understand product-design practice. By the time of this episode, the guests say that mission has largely been completed for them personally, while their current energy has shifted toward a bookstore, markets, physical objects, and interviews with makers or craftspeople.

The bookstore project changes the profile from a purely digital-product and podcast entity into a mixed media, retail, and cultural-translation project. The hosts' U.S.-based Chinese bookstore is both a personal answer to missing Chinese-language bookstore access and a public-facing way to present contemporary Chinese books, design, art, ceramics, glassware, and everyday goods.

## Key Characteristics
- It originated around UI/UX enthusiasm, design interviews, work methods, and software skills.
- Its current source profile includes a move toward offline bookstore and cultural-retail work.
- The bookstore work requires logistics, supplier coordination, inventory, markets, packing, and weekend labor.
- The guests treat the project as a personal outlet as AI and tech-industry cycles weaken old work assumptions.
- The podcast's future direction may move toward makers, craftspeople, and Chinese culture/design/art explanations for Western audiences.
