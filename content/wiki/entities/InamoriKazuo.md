# 稻盛和夫 / Inamori Kazuo

稻盛和夫 / Inamori Kazuo enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-161-caiyun-he-renji-guanxi-de-liangzi-jiuchan-4-lml7lpd3u9l7esryknmhoampgx6v|Hanji 161 part 4]] as the business-side reception case for [[LiaofanSixun|《了凡四训》]]. The host says Inamori read the text, took it as guidance for self-cultivation and business operation, and used similar moral-practice principles in building enterprises.

This page keeps that use source-scoped. Inamori is not introduced here as a full biography subject; he is a management example used to argue that traditional self-cultivation can travel from personal fate change into organizational life.
