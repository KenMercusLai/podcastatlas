# 皮牢 / Pilao

皮牢 / Pilao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-1-lq4wvkd8qx3q7zji6fnnkxi3qcl1]] as one of the [[ZhaoState|赵国]] places attacked by [[WangHe|王龁]] after the [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]]. It is named with [[WuanWarringStates|武安]] in the episode's two-route [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] account.

The episode's point is strategic rather than local: Pilao helps show how [[QinState|秦国]] tried to keep pressure on Zhao's remaining positions before the campaign could move toward [[Handan|邯郸]].
