# Harris Alterman

Harris Alterman is a New York City-based comedian who appears in [[tech-20260714-tech-pod-128-tech-20260714-tech-pod-128]] as one of the creators of [[FakeAISubwayAds]]. In the [[MarketplaceTech]] episode, he says the project began from the everyday disorientation of riding the subway and seeing polished AI ads that did not clearly explain what they sold.

Alterman's role in the source is to turn private confusion into public satire. He frames vague AI ads as a reality-distortion problem for ordinary riders: the ads look official and expensive, but the missing product explanation makes the viewer feel outside the intended audience.
