# The Jungle / 《屠场》

## Overview

*The Jungle* is [[UptonSinclair|Upton Sinclair]]'s novel about a Lithuanian immigrant family confronting exploitation, poverty, injury, abuse, corruption, and contaminated meat in industrial Chicago.

## Current Profile

In [[14-zheyang-de-rou-ni-gan-chi-ma-dongxifang-de-shipin-anquan-chouwen-315-tebie-jiemu-lkagyyokjnnfxtovnqpfg4cxsjov]], the novel has two distinct public lives. Sinclair's intended argument concerns class oppression and socialist remedy, while readers, investigators, and lawmakers concentrate on meatpacking sanitation. The gap makes the book a case of [[InvestigativeNarrativeRegulatoryChange|investigative narrative producing regulatory change]] through an audience response narrower than its author's program.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses an immigrant family's losses to connect workplace exploitation with housing, law, politics, and poverty.
- Mixes investigation-based description with fictional construction rather than functioning as a government report.
- Makes food contamination emotionally and bodily immediate to consumers distant from factory production.
- Had a public effect that diverged from its intended socialist emphasis.
- Remains useful only when verified findings are separated from scenes the source itself identifies as fictional.

## Evidence

- Labor and migration narrative: [[14-zheyang-de-rou-ni-gan-chi-ma-dongxifang-de-shipin-anquan-chouwen-315-tebie-jiemu-lkagyyokjnnfxtovnqpfg4cxsjov]] describes an immigrant family facing fraud, child labor, injury, sexual abuse, poverty, and corruption.
- Consumer reception: [[14-zheyang-de-rou-ni-gan-chi-ma-dongxifang-de-shipin-anquan-chouwen-315-tebie-jiemu-lkagyyokjnnfxtovnqpfg4cxsjov]] says readers fixated on dirty meat processing and prompted political investigation.
- Evidence boundary: [[14-zheyang-de-rou-ni-gan-chi-ma-dongxifang-de-shipin-anquan-chouwen-315-tebie-jiemu-lkagyyokjnnfxtovnqpfg4cxsjov]] says the worker-rendering-vat episode was considered fictional even though investigators reportedly confirmed many sanitation allegations.

## Qualifications

The bounded source does not provide a textual edition, publication archive, sales history, or independent analysis of the investigation and 1906 legislative record. The novel's descriptions should not be treated uniformly as verified reportage, and its public impact should not be reduced to a single-cause account of federal regulation.
