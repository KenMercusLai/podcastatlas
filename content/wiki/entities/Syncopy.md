# Syncopy

## Overview

Syncopy is the small production company associated with [[ChristopherNolan]] and [[EmmaThomas]] in the episode's account of Nolan's bargaining position inside Hollywood.

## Current Profile

The episode treats Syncopy as a lean control vehicle rather than a broad studio. Its importance is structural: Nolan can negotiate project by project, protect theatrical and technical priorities, and make large films with unusually strong authorial control while relying on major-studio distribution capacity.

## Key Characteristics

- Syncopy is described as small and closely tied to Nolan's own films.
- Its project-by-project posture supports Nolan's leverage over studio terms, release timing, format, and creative control.
- The company helps explain why Nolan can combine auteur identity with blockbuster-scale production.
