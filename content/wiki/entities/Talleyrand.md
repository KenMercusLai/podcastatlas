# Charles Maurice de Talleyrand-Périgord

## Overview

Charles Maurice de Talleyrand-Périgord was the bishop and revolutionary statesman presented in this episode as the advocate for nationalizing Church land to address France's fiscal crisis.

## Current Profile

The source places Talleyrand at the hinge between fiscal rescue and religious transformation. His proposal treated the Church's large property holdings as national resources and paired confiscation with state payment of clergy, redefining priests as public functionaries. The plan therefore promised financial relief while weakening the Church's independent corporate position and preparing the ground for deeper conflict over national and ecclesiastical authority.

## Key Characteristics

- Clerical insider advancing revolutionary Church-property reform.
- Connected fiscal stabilization to nationalization of ecclesiastical wealth.
- Proposed state support for clergy after confiscation.
- Helped shift the clergy from an autonomous corporate order toward public employment.
