# 大庭叶藏 / Oba Yozo

大庭叶藏 / Oba Yozo is the protagonist of [[DazaiOsamu|太宰治]]'s [[NoLongerHuman|《人间失格》]] as read in [[33-renjian-shige-danxiaogui-de-zhuanglie-xianji-599924624]]. The episode treats him as a frighteningly sensitive person who cannot understand, trust, or inhabit ordinary human rules, rather than as a simple emblem of stylish despair.

Yozo's basic survival strategy is [[ComicMaskSurvival|搞笑面具式生存]]. In childhood he uses jokes, falls, and staged foolishness to keep family members, servants, classmates, and teachers from turning hostile or seeing his fear. The source repeatedly stresses that this is self-erasure under pressure, not lighthearted humor.

The mask begins to break when [[TakeichiNoLongerHuman|竹一]] sees through it. It later collapses across [[HorikiNoLongerHuman|堀木]]'s urban drift, the failed suicide with [[TsunekoNoLongerHuman|常子]], temporary family-like life with [[ShizukoNoLongerHuman|静子]], and the damaged marriage with [[YoshikoNoLongerHuman|豪子]]. The Yoshiko episode produces the central [[TrustAsMoralRisk|trust-as-risk]] question.

## Key Claims
- Yozo is morally hard to defend, but the episode asks readers to understand his fear before condemning him.
- His "human disqualification" is an identity wound: he experiences ordinary life as something he cannot qualify for.
- His self-destruction is tied to social pressure, family authority, shame, dependence, and inability to trust, not only to private temperament.
- The source-scoped [[CowardlySacrificeWitness]] reading treats Yozo as a witness for failures and outsiders without making his behavior exemplary.
