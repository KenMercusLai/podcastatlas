## Overview

鲍圣之 / Bao Shengzhi enters the wiki as a [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]-era official whose profile now spans two pressure points. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-379-2-jiemi-jinyiwei-zuzong-shenmi-de-hanchao-xiuyi-shizhe-lj7naddqh_zcdvolio3u7bjvvotf|Hanji 379-2]] presents him as Bao Gongzi, a direct envoy with power to suppress banditry, inspect commanderies and kingdoms, and execute disobedient lower officials. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-386-2-diwang-wuqing-huanghou-weizifu-beipo-zisha-lrvdfib62oczcy7loqss4-prkgl|Hanji 386-2]] later places him inside the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]] aftermath as an official who tries to slow summary punishment and then dies under Wudi's harsh response.

## Current Profile

Hanji 379-2 makes Bao Shengzhi a concrete face of emperor-direct coercive authority before the later Wugu story. He is sent as zhizhi envoy in bright clothing and with axe authority, tasked with suppressing thieves and inspecting local government. His mandate is extraordinary: officials below two-thousand-stone rank who refuse orders can be executed without first asking Wudi.

The same episode complicates Bao's severity. In Bohai, he hears of [[JunBuyiWesternHan|隽不疑]], receives him after Jun refuses to remove his sword, and accepts Jun's counsel that effective authority must avoid both brittle harshness and useless softness. Bao recommends Jun, leading to Jun's Qingzhou inspector appointment. Bao therefore becomes not only a killing-power figure but a severe official capable of recognizing moderation when it appears in the right ritual and intellectual form.

Hanji 386-2 supplies the later risk of that moderation. During the Wugu armed crisis, Bao tries to slow summary punishment after Liu Ju's defeat, keeping alive a father-son distinction that Wudi's rebellion frame does not tolerate. The source note says his attempt ends in suicide, marking how dangerous restraint could become when imperial suspicion demanded visible punishment.

## Key Characteristics

- Han Wudi-era direct envoy associated with bandit suppression and commandery-kingdom inspection.
- Holder of exceptional punitive authority over disobedient lower-ranked officials.
- Severe official whose authority helps define early [[XiuyiEnvoyImperialSurveillance|绣衣使者皇权监察]].
- Receptive to Jun Buyi's advice that威严 must be balanced with恩惠.
- Wugu-era official who tries to slow summary punishment and is destroyed by the harsh treason frame.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 379-2 and Hanji 386-2. It does not reconstruct Bao Shengzhi's complete title history, exact legal authority, whether every direct envoy in this period shared his powers, or the full circumstances of his Wugu-era death. Hanji 379-2's comparison to later锦衣卫 is recorded as an explanatory analogy, not as institutional identity.
