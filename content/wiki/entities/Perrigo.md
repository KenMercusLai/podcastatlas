# Perrigo

Perrigo is the U.S. contract manufacturer discussed in [[bobbie-laura-modi-how-a-baby-formula-startup-took-market-share-from-two-industry-giants-4488842b-74d3-40ed-8776-5831c5934e69]]. The episode's transcript spells the name as "Perigo"; this page records the standard spelling while keeping the role source-scoped.

In the source, Perrigo initially declined to manufacture [[Bobbie]], but contacted [[LauraModi]] after the [[FoodAndDrugAdministration|FDA]] recall because Bobbie handled the crisis with an apology and compliance posture. An internal Perrigo R&D champion is described as already wanting changes similar to Bobbie's product thesis, including grass-fed milk, European-style nutritional standards, and DHA.
