# Liam McMillen

Liam McMillen appears in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as the independent Republican candidate whose West Belfast campaign office displayed an Irish tricolor during the 1964 Westminster election. Under pressure from [[IanPaisley]], the [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]] removed the flag on 28 September 1964.

The resulting riot gave Paisley publicity and made flag symbolism a street-level case of [[HistoricalMemoryContest]]. McMillen's role is therefore less about electoral success than about how symbols, policing, and publicity could turn ordinary political campaigning into sectarian confrontation.
