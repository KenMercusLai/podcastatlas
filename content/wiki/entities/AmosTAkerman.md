# Amos T. Akerman

## Overview

Amos T. Akerman is Grant's attorney general presented as a central legal architect of the federal campaign against the first Ku Klux Klan in South Carolina.

## Current Profile

The bounded source shows Akerman converting reports of terror into national enforcement. He investigated South Carolina, backed [[LewisMerrill|Lewis Merrill]]'s evidence gathering, supported the use of Enforcement Act powers, and treated white local indifference to Klan violence as a profound moral and institutional danger.

## Key Characteristics

- Attorney general under [[UlyssesSGrant|Ulysses S. Grant]].
- Strong advocate of federal action against Klan conspiracies.
- Sponsor of systematic evidence gathering rather than indiscriminate military response.
- Observer of the local moral and institutional normalization that protected perpetrators.
