# 山音 / Shanyin

## Overview
山音 is a screenwriting-trained AI director interviewed in [[ai-wuxian-rensheng-youxian-duitan-kk-shanyin-yixian-ai-chuangzuozhe-lmk4luckjgn-cpivd-7dy9vmsl0u|AI 无限，人生有限]]. The source uses [[AMingDeYiTianFilm|《阿明的一天》]] as the clearest example of his iterative creative method.

## Current Profile
Shanyin's workflow starts from a sharp premise rather than necessarily from a finished screenplay. Text, generated characters, images, editing gaps, and emotional intention can feed back into one another, with the timeline acting as a place where the work is continuously rewritten.

He defines human creative value through selection and refusal. The “眉头一皱的能力” is the capacity to notice when a fluent answer is false, generic, or emotionally unfit, then use that reaction to discover the creator's own version. AI also helped him rediscover a film vocation he had set aside after formal arts education and career doubt.

## Key Characteristics
- Screenwriting-trained director who permits text, image, and editing decisions to co-evolve.
- Starts quickly from a compact premise and learns through making rather than waiting for complete tool mastery.
- Treats rejection, selection, and discomfort with generic output as central creative capabilities.
- Draws emotionally important work from personal experience, including family illness and fragmented work life.
- Explores AI video beyond finished clips toward games, livestreams, real-time generation, and new interactive media.
