# 触龙 / Chu Long

触龙 / Chu Long enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-91-chulong-ruhe-qiaokai-shougua-zhaotaihou-de-men-1-lmgd7kdl1nrfwcrtkqttumtlpqok]], which identifies him as the Zhao left master in the classic [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] story often known as "触龙说赵太后." He appears when [[ZhaoWeihou|赵威后]] refuses to send [[ChangAnJunZhao|长安君]] to [[QiState|齐国]] as hostage despite [[QinState|秦国]]'s attack on [[ZhaoState|赵国]].

The episode's first-half account makes Chu Long a persuasion case rather than a general biographical subject. He enters slowly, presents himself as old and physically weak, talks about Zhao Weihou's health, and asks for a palace-guard post for his own youngest son before raising the logic of parental long-term care. This makes him the source case for [[EmotionalDeescalationPersuasion|情绪降温式说服]]: the policy argument becomes possible only after the target no longer experiences the conversation as another direct attack.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-91-chulong-ruhe-qiaokai-shougua-zhaotaihou-de-men-2-liy5yulg2ysop-jwfigvdkub1pk7]] completes the scene. Chu Long asks whether earlier Zhao royal descendants with noble title and wealth still hold their positions, then argues that Zhao Weihou has given Chang'an Jun land and treasure without giving him a chance to earn standing for after her death. The argument persuades her, but the episode treats his explanation as rhetorically effective rather than fully accurate, because hereditary-aristocratic decline is better explained through [[WarringStatesReformBacklash|Warring States centralizing reforms]] than through merit alone.
