## Overview

陈阿娇 / Empress Chen is presented as [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s early empress and [[LiuPiaoWesternHan|馆陶长公主刘嫖]]'s daughter. Hanji 239 places her childhood match inside Liu Piao's shift from Liu Rong to Liu Che and therefore separates the “金屋藏娇” memory from the marriage's coalition function. Hanji 251 adds childlessness, possessiveness, and escalating protest after [[EmpressWeiZifuWesternHan|卫子夫]] gains favor; Hanji 270 then carries lost favor, weak coalition support, reliance on [[ChuFuWesternHan|楚服]], and a wugu case into deposition and residence at Changmen Palace.

## Current Profile

The sources interpret Chen's position and fall through overlapping frames. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-239-bie-beipianle-jinwu-cangjiao-beihoude-yinmou-zhenxiang-1-ljdaasdscqwie1ishma3nwvgzhqp|Hanji 239 part 1]] supplies the formation layer: Liu Piao first offers Chen to Crown Prince Liu Rong, then turns to Wang and Liu Che after Li's refusal. The “金屋” promise gives that change a romantic memory, but the durable addition is that Chen's match is part of [[PalaceMarriageSuccessionCoalition|宫廷婚姻—储位联盟]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-251-mengdongshaonian-huncheng-sichao-yuanlao-shifen-de-dingji-jiafeng-2-ltxndq1kxzb6lf-cmh3okvjvxutr|Hanji 251]] continues the alliance and household layer: Liu Piao's support helps place her daughter beside Liu Che, but the episode says eleven childless years, attempted medical spending, jealousy, and repeated self-harm threats deepen the marriage crisis after Wei Zifu's arrival. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-270-chenajiao-mishi-hougong-zhizhong-ta-weihe-duchong-nvguan-1-lqyk8xtyqwmbmytg9pq17flesyyf|Hanji 270-1]] then supplies the wugu event sequence: Chen allegedly tries to harm Wei, turns to Chu Fu's sacrifice, curse, and “魅术” practices, and is removed after [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]] investigates. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-270-chenajiao-mishi-hougong-zhizhong-ta-weihe-duchong-nvguan-2-lllzlb2knjj0ovmuc8tiid34m0gp|Hanji 270-2]] adds Wudi's moral explanation and promise of continuing material support.

Politically, Hanji 270-2 argues that the case gave Wudi a low-disruption opportunity to replace Chen after he had already wanted a change. It also contrasts Chen's weak coalition with Wei Zifu's connection to Wei Qing and Huo Qubing and with Lü Zhi's broader political network. Personally, both episodes treat jealousy, harmful tactics, and loss of emotional proportion as failures of palace judgment. These motive claims remain the host's interpretation rather than direct access to Chen's inner life.

Hanji 270-1 adds a distinct intimacy layer. Through a passage attributed to 《汉武故事》, it describes Chu Fu wearing male clothing, sharing Chen's sleeping quarters, and being “相爱若夫妇” with her. This supports a transmitted record of spouse-like intimacy, but the host's first-case claim, “对食” terminology history, supernatural manipulation explanation, and modern identity label remain source-scoped under [[PalaceFemaleSameSexIntimacy|宫廷女性同性亲密关系]].

The final literary and emotional layer links Chen to the reported commissioning of [[ChangmenFu|《长门赋》]] from [[SimaXiangru|司马相如]], then uses Changmen residence as a modern lesson about one-sided attachment and self-worth. Those psychological and literary claims remain source-scoped.

## Key Characteristics

- Early Western Han empress proposed first as Liu Rong's marriage partner and then matched to Liu Che, with the political coalition later remembered through “金屋藏娇.”
- Daughter of Liu Piao, whose earlier imperial leverage does not prevent the deposition.
- Childless and displaced in affection by Wei Zifu in the episodes' account.
- Relies on Chu Fu's promised ritual solution after losing favor and is implicated in a wugu case that becomes both the formal reason and, in the host's interpretation, the political opportunity for removal.
- Described through 《汉武故事》 as sharing spouse-like intimacy with Chu Fu, without enough evidence here to settle modern identity, coercion, or priority claims.
- Lacks the durable coalition, experience, and self-command that Hanji 270-2 attributes to more effective palace actors.
- Remembered through Changmen Palace and the 《长门赋》 tradition, although authorship, commission details, and emotional effect are not established here.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on four short popular-history summaries, not a full biography or primary-source study. The sources do not establish the first proposal, marriage bargain, historical status or wording of “金屋藏娇,” exact childless interval, medical-spending figure, self-harm sequence, Wudi's private motive, exact wugu evidence, Chen's responsibility, investigation scope, or the authorship and commissioning record of 《长门赋》. Hanji 251's title-body mismatch also means its Chen material appears under a misleading Shi Fen title. Hanji 270-1 does not verify the textual status of 《汉武故事》, the first-case claim, the semantic history of “对食,” supernatural efficacy, coercion, or either woman's modern identity. Its comparisons with Lü Zhi, Qi Furen, Wei Zifu, and Zhang Ailing are interpretive. This person is distinct from [[ChenJiaoWesternHan|陈蟜 / 陈娇]], the husband of Longlü Princess.

## What Changed

- Added the earlier Liu Rong proposal and Liu Che rematch as a marriage-coalition sequence.
- Reframed “金屋藏娇” as a source-scoped romantic memory attached to that political match, not proof of motive or causation.
