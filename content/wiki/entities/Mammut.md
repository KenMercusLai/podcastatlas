# Mammut / 猛犸象

Mammut / 猛犸象 appears in [[mai-xiangzao-qijia-de-bainian-huawang-weishenme-chengle-bandaoti-zhizao-de-guanjiang-qiye-1004213131]] as the Swiss outdoor brand acquired by [[CPEYuanfeng|CPE源峰]]. The source treats Mammut as a professional outdoor brand positioned by Chinese outdoor consumers near Arc'teryx and Klattermusen in the shorthand "一鸟二象三鼠."

The side item's business point is that acquisition value depends on both brand asset and market execution. The episode says Mammut's China pricing is below Arc'teryx and Klattermusen and closer to Kailas, while many stores remain authorized rather than directly operated, making channel control and brand strengthening central tasks for CPE after acquisition.
