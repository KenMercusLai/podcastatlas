# 邓通 / Deng Tong

邓通 / Deng Tong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-135-fanwaipian-2-nvxiangshi-yin-kanming-nengli-bei-liubang-fenghou-lgu1fr1zrzmc0o72wtbpxmajb7s|Qinji 135 番外篇2]] as a minor figure whom [[LiuHeng|刘恒 / Emperor Wen of Han]] favors after he resembles a helpful young man from the emperor's dream. The episode uses him to test whether imperial favor can override a fate-reading prediction.

In the source, [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负]] says Deng Tong's face shows wealth and honor but that he will ultimately starve in poverty. Emperor Wen responds by giving him a copper mountain and minting privileges, trying to make poverty impossible through direct patronage.

The prediction is fulfilled after [[HanJingdi|汉景帝 / Emperor Jing of Han]] succeeds Emperor Wen, dislikes Deng Tong, and confiscates his wealth under the charge of private minting. The episode says Deng Tong becomes destitute and dies hungry, making him a case where favor-generated wealth proves non-durable after a patron's death.
