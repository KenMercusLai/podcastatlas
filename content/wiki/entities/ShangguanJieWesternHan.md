## Overview

上官桀 / Shangguan Jie first appears chronologically in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-375-1-zhichang-qishi-bie-rang-ziji-biancheng-yi-tou-chunlu-li-za4h0ixnq4jym8algtxzyy6os|Hanji 375-1]] as the 搜粟都尉 sent by [[LiGuangliWesternHan|李广利]] to attack [[YuchengWesternHan|郁成]] and pursue its king to [[Kangju|康居]]. Later sources move him from frontier officer to [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s entrusted minister, Shangguan family strategist, and high-ranking anti-[[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] conspirator under [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]].

## Current Profile

The combined episodes present a much longer arc than the final conspiracy alone. Hanji 375-1 gives Shangguan Jie an early operational role in the Dayuan-war aftermath: after Wang Shensheng's detached force is defeated, Li Guangli assigns Shangguan Jie to break Yucheng; the king flees, and Kangju hands him over under the changed regional pressure created by Han's victory. The episode does not independently establish how much this success caused Shangguan Jie's later rise, but it adds real frontier command experience before his court career.

Hanji 391-2 makes him part of the original entrusted-minister design around young Han Zhao. Hanji 396-1 shows the family move to convert a granddaughter's palace entry into Shangguan leverage even over Huo Guang's objection. Hanji 399 then shows a slander-and-procedure strategy: use Liu Dan's name in a forged memorial, make Huo look like a potential rebel, and wait for an investigation that would let opponents strip him of power. After that fails, Hanji 400-1 shows Shangguan Jie, [[ShangguanAnWesternHan|上官安]], [[GaiChanggongzhuWesternHan|盖长公主]], [[SangHongyangWesternHan|桑弘羊]], and others moving toward a banquet assassination plan: kill Huo, depose Han Zhao, and initially use [[LiuDanYanKingWesternHan|燕王刘旦]] as the replacement claimant.

His profile darkens because Shangguan An's later private plan would discard Liu Dan and install Shangguan Jie himself as emperor. The episode therefore frames Shangguan Jie as both a participant in anti-regent rebellion and the intended beneficiary of a second betrayal inside the conspiracy.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han frontier officer whose Yucheng campaign precedes his rise as Han Wudi's entrusted sickbed appointee and later anti-Huo conspirator.
- Family strategist who seeks palace status for his young granddaughter before the open anti-Huo alignment.
- Father of Shangguan An and part of the Shangguan outer-relative network.
- Participant in the forged memorial attempt to turn Han Zhao against Huo Guang.
- Participant in a plan to use Gai Chang Gongzhu's banquet access to assassinate Huo Guang.
- Intended imperial beneficiary of Shangguan An's later plan to kill Liu Dan after using him.
- Destroyed with the main conspiracy group after the plot leaks.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to five podcast sources. Hanji 375-1 supplies one early frontier campaign; Hanji 391-2, Hanji 396-1, Hanji 399, and Hanji 400-1 supply the entrusted-minister, family-mobility, slander, and coup sequence. It does not reconstruct Shangguan Jie's complete offices, full career, kinship network, or every prior anti-Huo episode.
