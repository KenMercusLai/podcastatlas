# Constitution Hill Johannesburg

## Overview
Constitution Hill Johannesburg is the former prison site in the episode where apartheid's racial hierarchy becomes visible through imprisonment conditions, pass-law enforcement, and guide interpretation.

## Current Profile
The site matters because the episode links it to both [[NelsonMandela]] and Gandhi, while focusing less on famous names than on everyday carceral inequality. 王妈妈 recounts guide explanations of different food, bedding, bathing, space, and treatment for white and black prisoners, as well as imprisonment for curfew or pass-document violations.

Constitution Hill also introduces the episode's contested-memory mode. The local guide's interpretation of Mandela and Gandhi is not purely reverential; it brings a black South African perspective that complicates inherited heroic stories.

## Key Characteristics
- Former Johannesburg prison site attached to apartheid and constitutional memory.
- Site where racially unequal prison conditions make [[Apartheid]] concrete.
- Historical stop associated in the episode with [[NelsonMandela]] and Gandhi.
- Place where curfew and pass-law enforcement show how ordinary movement could become criminalized.
- Memory site where guide interpretation complicates simple hero-worship.
