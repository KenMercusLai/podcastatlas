# Lena Curtis

## Overview
Lena Curtis is the guest in [[ep-37-neurons-future-of-ai-processing]], introduced as a neuroscientist and strategic advisor at [[FinalSpark]].

## Current Profile
In this source, Curtis represents the bridge between neuroscience, medical imaging, AI, and early biocomputing. She explains FinalSpark's work without presenting it as a mature replacement for silicon: her core role is to clarify what living-neuron processors are, what researchers can already measure, and which scientific and ethical questions remain open.

## Key Characteristics
- Neuroscientist whose academic work focused on brain and medical imaging.
- Industry practitioner who encountered AI through medical-imaging startups and large imaging datasets.
- Strategic advisor explaining [[FinalSpark]]'s remote neuro platform and biocomputing thesis.
- Careful communicator who distinguishes neurons as processors from neurons as power sources.
- Early-field realist who labels generative-AI use cases, cost reductions, and commercial scale as assumptions rather than proven deployments.

## Evidence
### Neuroscience and AI background
- [[ep-37-neurons-future-of-ai-processing]] says Curtis did a PhD in medical brain imaging, worked with medical-imaging startups in London, learned Python, and moved into commercial AI applications.

### FinalSpark and biocomputing explanation
- [[ep-37-neurons-future-of-ai-processing]] has Curtis explain electrode-based stimulation and measurement, analog-to-digital conversion, and the remote platform model.

### Scientific caution
- [[ep-37-neurons-future-of-ai-processing]] records her view that researchers can observe activity but still do not know exactly how in vitro neurons encode information.

## Qualifications
