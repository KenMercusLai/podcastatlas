## Overview
丰臻 / Feng Zhen is the football commentator invited by [[Budongqiu|不懂球]] to discuss why Chinese national teams and clubs should take Asian competition seriously while protecting the continuity of the professional league.

## Current Profile
Within the bounded source, Feng Zhen connects competitive ambition to institutional conditions. He supports using stronger squads in Asian events because Chinese teams cannot assume superiority, but rejects prolonged league suspension as the price of that commitment. At club level, he frames [[AFCChampionsLeague|AFC Champions League]] effort as responsibility to the club and its supporters, while treating league reputation as a downstream benefit.

His reform position favors more stable high-quality match supply through [[ChineseFootballLeagueExpansion]], greater foreign-player roster depth, and pragmatic consideration of [[NaturalizedPlayerPolicyInChineseFootball|naturalization]] when the national team's short-term ceiling is low.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats Asian competition as a necessary confidence, experience, and capability test for Chinese teams.
- Separates serious national-team preparation from repeated disruption of professional-league operations.
- Evaluates club continental participation through supporter responsibility and realistic incentives rather than abstract league honor alone.
- Favors expansion, deeper foreign-player rosters, and naturalization as pragmatic but bounded tools.
