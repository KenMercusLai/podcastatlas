## Overview

安国少季 / An Guo Shaoji is a Western Han envoy sent to [[Nanyue|南越]] after [[ZhaoXingNanyue|赵兴]] succeeds. His earlier intimate relationship with [[QueenDowagerJiuNanyue|樛太后]] makes personal access part of the diplomatic channel behind the royal party's request for closer Han incorporation.

## Current Profile

Hanji 351 identifies Shaoji as a man known intimately to Lady Jiu before she became Nanyue queen dowager. [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] later includes him in the mission charged with advancing incorporation after Zhao Xing's accession. The queen dowager and king then seek interior-vassal-style court attendance, removal of border barriers, Han official seals, legal convergence, and continued envoy presence.

The episode calls Shaoji a long-planted “spy” whose task is to persuade the queen dowager. The evidence supplied here securely supports prior intimacy, envoy status, and a role in a pro-incorporation mission; it does not independently document a formal intelligence commission, the exact content of his persuasion, or his private motives.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han envoy in the mission to Zhao Xing's Nanyue court.
- Earlier intimate associate of the future Queen Dowager Jiu.
- Personal-access channel linked to the royal party's incorporation request.
- Figure whose private relationship becomes politically sensitive inside Nanyue.
- “Long-planted spy” in the episode's framing, with formal agent status unverified by the bounded source.

## Qualifications

The supplied summary writes his name as “少纪”; the wiki uses the received form 安国少季. The episode's “spy” language is an interpretive modernization, not enough by itself to establish a formal intelligence post. This source does not reconstruct his office, origin, complete mission roster, later fate, direct speech, or independent policy influence. It also does not separate consensual intimacy, later rumor, and hostile political use through multiple perspectives.
