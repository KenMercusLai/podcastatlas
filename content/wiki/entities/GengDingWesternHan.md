## Overview

耿定 / Geng Ding enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-515-shi-shui-lan-xia-le-xihan-chengxiang-de-jubaoxin-lshhivowk9jpigkr7ssjiysefm6r|Hanji 515]] as the Pingyang man who accuses [[WangShangChancellorWesternHan|乐昌侯王商]] after Wang Shang refuses [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]]'s request to drop the Yang Rong impeachment.

## Current Profile

The source uses Geng Ding as the accusation channel in Wang Feng's counterattack. His claims concern alleged sexual misconduct and possible involvement in a killing connected to Wang Shang's sister, but the episode explicitly says the matters cannot be verified.

## Key Characteristics

- Counter-accuser in the Wang Feng-Wang Shang struggle.
- Carrier of private-conduct allegations that displace the original official-accountability question.
- Source-scoped figure whose independent motive and evidentiary access are unknown.

## Evidence

- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-515-shi-shui-lan-xia-le-xihan-chengxiang-de-jubaoxin-lshhivowk9jpigkr7ssjiysefm6r|Hanji 515]] says Wang Feng directs Geng Ding to accuse Wang Shang.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-515-shi-shui-lan-xia-le-xihan-chengxiang-de-jubaoxin-lshhivowk9jpigkr7ssjiysefm6r|Hanji 515]] says the underlying allegations cannot be verified and that Chengdi initially does not accept them as sufficient.

## Qualifications

The episode does not supply an independent dossier, later biography, or proof of Geng Ding's claims. His role is therefore preserved as an attributed accusation, not as confirmation of Wang Shang's guilt.
