# Benjamin Alarie

Benjamin Alarie is the [[UniversityOfToronto|University of Toronto]] law professor interviewed in [[tech-20260805-0805-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260805-0805-mp-tech-pod-128]] about AI and accessible legal help. The episode identifies him as co-author of *Super Justice: Law in the Age of Artificial Intelligence* and uses his argument to frame AI as possible legal infrastructure rather than simply a lawyer-replacement tool.

Alarie's wiki role is to connect optimism about [[AIAccessToJustice]] with a strong accountability boundary. He argues that [[SuperJustice|Super Justice]] could make legal reasoning and legal help more abundant, but he repeatedly returns to [[LegalAIVerificationAuditability]], [[HumanInTheLoopLegalAI]], and professional responsibility when AI produces wrong or invented legal material.
