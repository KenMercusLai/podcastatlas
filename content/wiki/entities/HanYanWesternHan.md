## Overview

韩嫣 / Han Yan is presented as a childhood companion and later highly favored intimate of [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] who introduces the politically frustrated young emperor to hunting; his access, office, wealth, protocol overreach, and eventual death make him a compact case of companionship becoming both recreation and political exposure.

## Current Profile

Hanji 252 identifies Han Yan as Wudi's similarly aged childhood companion and grandson of a contributor to suppressing the Seven States rebellion. It credits him with proposing hunting as relief after Wudi's early political agenda is checked, after which the emperor adopts increasingly wide-ranging incognito hunts. The episode thus adds recreational influence to Han Yan's profile without proving that he alone caused Wudi's continuing practice.

Hanji 372-4 identifies Han Yan as a descendant of the Han royal house, raised in the palace and close to Liu Che from youth. It links his appeal to martial skill, riding and archery, and knowledge useful for war against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], while also describing him through the host's modern lover language.

After Wudi's accession, Han Yan receives the office of shang dafu, lavish rewards, and unusually close daily access. The story's turning point comes when he rides ahead in imperial vehicles and [[LiuFeiJiangduKingWesternHan|江都王刘非]] mistakenly bows, then complains to the empress dowager. The episode says she has Han Yan killed despite Wudi's plea, showing that intimacy with the ruler did not defeat superior palace-family authority.

## Key Characteristics

- Childhood palace companion of the future Han Wudi in the episode's account.
- Companion credited with introducing hunting as an escape from early political frustration.
- Royal-descended, martial, and riding-and-archery figure whose skills align with Wudi's anti-Xiongnu interests.
- Court favorite whose personal access becomes office, wealth, and conspicuous consumption.
- Status-overreach case because use of imperial vehicles creates a humiliating protocol mistake for Liu Fei.
- Favorite whose protection fails when the empress dowager intervenes.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to two popular-history episodes. Their descriptions of Han Yan's ancestry, body, influence over Wudi's recreation, the transition from childhood friendship to romance, the exact sexual content of the relationship, and the empress dowager's motive are not independently established here. The page therefore treats companionship, favorite status, and intimate access as grounded within the sources while leaving sole causation and modern orientation labels unresolved.
