## Overview

韩嫣 / Han Yan is presented in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-372-4-xihan-huangdi-duo-shi-shuangxinglian-de-yuanyin-lqinxoxbu6dhvmdiiowocjaahkwr|Hanji 372-4]] as a childhood companion and later highly favored intimate of [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] whose access, office, wealth, and eventual death make him a compact case of court favor becoming political exposure.

## Current Profile

The episode identifies Han Yan as a descendant of the Han royal house, raised in the palace and close to Liu Che from youth. It links his appeal to martial skill, riding and archery, and knowledge useful for war against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], while also describing him through the host's modern lover language.

After Wudi's accession, Han Yan receives the office of shang dafu, lavish rewards, and unusually close daily access. The story's turning point comes when he rides ahead in imperial vehicles and [[LiuFeiJiangduKingWesternHan|江都王刘非]] mistakenly bows, then complains to the empress dowager. The episode says she has Han Yan killed despite Wudi's plea, showing that intimacy with the ruler did not defeat superior palace-family authority.

## Key Characteristics

- Childhood palace companion of the future Han Wudi in the episode's account.
- Royal-descended, martial, and riding-and-archery figure whose skills align with Wudi's anti-Xiongnu interests.
- Court favorite whose personal access becomes office, wealth, and conspicuous consumption.
- Status-overreach case because use of imperial vehicles creates a humiliating protocol mistake for Liu Fei.
- Favorite whose protection fails when the empress dowager intervenes.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one popular-history episode. Its descriptions of Han Yan's body, the transition from childhood friendship to romance, the exact sexual content of the relationship, and the empress dowager's motive are host interpretations rather than independently established facts here. The page therefore treats favorite status and intimate access as grounded within the source while leaving modern orientation labels unresolved.
