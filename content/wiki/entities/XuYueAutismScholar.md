# 许悦 / Xu Yue

## Overview
许悦 / Xu Yue is the autism, disability, and intervention scholar interviewed in the 不合时宜 episode on the Xiaomei gene-editing case and autism support.

## Current Profile
In the source, Xu Yue is introduced as a clinical assistant professor at the University of Illinois Chicago College of Medicine Rockford who studies autism, disability, and intervention treatment. Her role in the episode is both explanatory and normative: she explains autism as a neurodevelopmental spectrum while criticizing cure-first assumptions that turn disabled children into risky experimental subjects.

Her current wiki profile is therefore source-scoped to autism and disability-care ethics. She supports evidence-based intervention, family participation, early developmental observation, inclusive education, and public rights advocacy, while rejecting the fantasy that a "mystery pill" should erase autistic difference.

## Key Characteristics
- Presents autism as a clinical, developmental, and sensory-communication spectrum rather than a single-gene repair target.
- Critiques [[AbleistCurePressure]] in families, medical science, and achievement-oriented social expectations.
- Distinguishes useful intervention for a person's suffering from normalization driven by stigma or parental panic.
- Emphasizes practical, evidence-based [[AutismEarlyIntervention]] and family interaction over miracle cures.
- Uses U.S. early-intervention, school, Medicaid, and community-support examples to show how [[AutismSupportSystems]] can share family burden.
