## Overview
金牛道 / Jinniu Road is a major branch of the [[ShuRoadNetwork|古蜀道网络]] linking [[Hanzhong|汉中]] through Guangyuan, Zhaohua, Jianmen, Zitong, Mianyang, and Deyang toward [[Chengdu|成都]].

## Current Profile
Among the episode's three roads, Jinniu Road has the longest continuous official importance and the richest combination of visible remains and walkable sections. Its political memory is often traced to Qin's 316 BCE conquest of Shu and the “stone ox excreting gold” legend, although the source allows that earlier movement may predate that event.

The route is not materially frozen in antiquity. The stone paving and cypress-lined sections around Cuiyun Corridor largely present Ming-Qing road forms, and the northern line shifted eastward more than once as political centers and preferred connections changed.

## Key Characteristics
- Long-lived official corridor between Hanzhong and the Chengdu Plain.
- Route whose best-known origin story joins road building to Qin conquest.
- Surviving and walkable remains concentrated especially around Cuiyun Corridor.
- Northern alignment changed more than the relatively stable southern approach.
- Corridor later overlapped with modern highway construction and suffered partial covering or destruction.

## Evidence
- Duration and alignment: [[ep285-tafang-gushudao-ganwen-lu-zai-hefang-li20senvn3oelioproznnykdajvt|EP285]] describes long official use, northern eastward rerouting, and southern branching.
- Material form: [[ep285-tafang-gushudao-ganwen-lu-zai-hefang-li20senvn3oelioproznnykdajvt|EP285]] identifies much of the visible paving around Cuiyun Corridor as Ming-Qing rather than pristine Qin-Han fabric.
- Terrain transition: [[ep285-tafang-gushudao-ganwen-lu-zai-hefang-li20senvn3oelioproznnykdajvt|EP285]] uses Qiqu Mountain's “坡去平来” inscription and view southward to mark the change from mountains to the Chengdu Plain.
- Transport layering: [[ep285-tafang-gushudao-ganwen-lu-zai-hefang-li20senvn3oelioproznnykdajvt|EP285]] shows old route, Sichuan-Shaanxi highway, rail, expressway, and high-speed rail overlapping around Mingyue Gorge.

## Qualifications
The road's legendary opening by a deceptive stone-ox gift is cultural memory, not a secure construction record. Early origins and exact ancient alignments remain less certain than the later visible road fabric.
