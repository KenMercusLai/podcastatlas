## Overview

符融 / Fu Rong is a late-Han reputation actor whose current wiki profile has two sides: he can introduce real talent to [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]], and he can warn Li Ying when fame outruns proof. Hanji 860 has him recommend [[GuoTaiLateHan|郭泰]] to Li Ying, while Hanji 862 has him warn that [[HuangYunLateHan|黄允]] and [[JinWenjingLateHan|晋文经]] are being treated as great men before their conduct and achievements have been publicly established.

## Current Profile

Hanji 860 gives Fu Rong a positive recommendation role. He meets Guo Tai, is deeply impressed, and introduces him to Li Ying. Li Ying's subsequent praise and Guo Tai's Luoyang fame make Fu Rong part of an endorsement chain where a recognized intermediary helps real ability reach a more powerful reputation gatekeeper.

Hanji 862 uses Fu Rong as a corrective observer inside the same prestige-heavy field. Huang Yun and Jin Wenjing have made themselves scarce while officials and disciples orbit them; appointment decisions are affected by their praise or criticism. Fu Rong's warning identifies the risk: an empty reputation can distort public values and administrative judgment when it is treated as evidence.

Fu Rong's significance is therefore diagnostic as well as connective. He does not create reputation by force, and he does not punish false reputation directly. He routes attention toward Guo Tai when praise is grounded, then makes Huang Yun and Jin Wenjing's reputation field visible to Li Ying when praise has become detached from proof.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Han scholar-observer whose source roles are recommendation and reputation correction.
- Intermediary who introduces Guo Tai to Li Ying before Guo Tai's Luoyang fame.
- Critic of fame that lacks visible conduct and achievement.
- Intermediary who brings the Huang Yun / Jin Wenjing concern to Li Ying.
- Counterweight to Huang Yun and Jin Wenjing's scarcity-based prestige.
- Source case for identifying empty reputation before it damages selection norms further.

## Qualifications

This page is not a full biography of Fu Rong. The current source evidence is limited to his recommendation of Guo Tai and his warning about Huang Yun and Jin Wenjing. The source notes normalize the transcript's "芙蓉" to 符融.

## What Changed

- Fu Rong's profile now includes both positive recommendation of Guo Tai and negative correction of empty reputation.
- The current judgment treats Fu Rong as a reputation intermediary, not only a critic.
