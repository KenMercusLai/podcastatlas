# No More Marking

## Overview

No More Marking is the assessment company introduced by [[DaisyChristodoulou|Daisy Christodoulou]] in the episode's discussion of comparative judgment.

## Current Profile

The company is presented as using repeated comparisons between two pieces of extended writing. Teachers choose the better response in each pair, and the aggregated judgments produce a scale. The model aims to avoid some inconsistency and workload associated with detailed rubric marking while retaining human judgment about overall quality.

## Key Characteristics

- Applies pairwise comparison to essays and extended writing.
- Aggregates many simple judgments into a measurement scale.
- Seeks faster and more consistent assessment than independent rubric scoring.
- Retains human evaluative judgment rather than replacing it with a fixed-answer test.

## Evidence

- Method: [[84-exams-glt6507641896]] describes teachers comparing pairs of work and the judgments being combined into a scale.
- Claimed benefits: [[84-exams-glt6507641896]] reports Christodoulou's claims of improved reliability, validity, and speed.
