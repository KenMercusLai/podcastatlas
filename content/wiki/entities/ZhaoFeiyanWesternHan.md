## Overview

赵飞燕 / Zhao Feiyan is synthesized as a Western Han palace figure whose political meaning shifts by source layer. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-529-meiyan-jiemeihua-xihan-zuida-de-huoshui-dengchang-litxz9-kwkcm04s38y8_glpaqiop|Hanji 529]] now supplies the origin and palace-entry layer: Zhao Feiyan is narrated through abandoned-child traditions, trained as a dancer in [[YangAPrincessWesternHan|阳阿公主]]'s household, noticed by [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] at a banquet, and soon made jieyu. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-530-zhaofeiyan-yanguan-hougong-chengdi-weihe-gengchong-meimei-lra2oyg6pl3gmcn_khkby9kuuk0j|Hanji 530]] gives the pre-elevation favorite layer: Zhao Feiyan is already Chengdi's exclusive favorite, but Zhao Hede's beauty forces Chengdi to manage her jealousy with gifts and palace arrangements before bringing the younger sister in. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-531-mian-dui-zao-huangyao-ta-de-banfa-zhide-ni-xuexi-lsi7k-67uel46ea024kaubj2l_qh|Hanji 531]] gives the next advancement layer: after [[EmpressXuWesternHan|废后许氏]] is deposed, Zhao Feiyan still moves against [[BanJieyuWesternHan|班婕妤]] because Ban Jieyu remains a plausible rival for higher palace status. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-534-zhichang-li-weishenme-yao-tuichong-zhanan-lpaup86hls0kp7hc3vd2msjfuyw3|Hanji 534]] supplies the formal elevation layer: Chengdi wants to make Zhao Feiyan empress, [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] resists her low origin, [[ChunyuZhangWesternHan|淳于长]] mediates the approval, and Zhao Lin's enfeoffment repairs the public status problem. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-536-1-renxing-huanghou-zhaofeiyan-ruhe-mingmu-zhangdan-gaowaiyu-llxo0my5ngp-mnbvjbl92yod1x2n|Hanji 536-1]] then gives the scandal-and-insecurity layer: Zhao Feiyan becomes empress but remains childless, allegedly hides men in palace rooms to seek a child, and benefits from Chengdi's refusal to pursue informers. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-573-zhaofeiyan-jiemei-shi-hongyanhuoshui-haishi-tizuigaoyang-lkacfndvi8t1opep67xmquyzr_ww|Hanji 573]] later challenges the simple "red-beauty disaster" explanation for Chengdi's heir failure, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-536-4-jiemi-zhaofeiyan-jiemei-wuming-yiyun-lo557jbq9ni-xissplybtcuraelm|Hanji 536-4]] makes that reputation question explicit by comparing Zhao Feiyan's later "千古荡妇" image with older beauty-blame cases. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-574-gudai-de-manfen-zuowen-shi-shenme-yangzi-lotqprwr3rk5lke0rjn94riljuvh|Hanji 574]] shows how [[GengYuWesternHan|耿育]]'s memorial lets [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] stop further investigation without appearing to suppress truth for private gratitude.

## Current Profile

Hanji 529 presents Zhao Feiyan's rise as a movement from precarious origin legend into elite household performance. The episode quotes the abandoned-infant story from `Han Shu`, then adds a variant twin-sister version involving Zhao Hede. Its more concrete mobility layer places Zhao Feiyan in Yang'a Princess's household, where lightness, dance training, and banquet staging make Chengdi notice her. That origin layer is valuable for the wiki, but its birth variants and "祸水" label remain source-scoped popular narration.

Hanji 530 presents Zhao Feiyan as the already-favored woman whose own sister's entry must be made politically and emotionally acceptable. Chengdi hears of Zhao Hede, reads Hede's hesitation as fear of Zhao Feiyan's jealousy, and compensates Zhao Feiyan before bringing Hede in. The same source makes Zhao Feiyan's favor culturally visible through the "环肥燕瘦" beauty frame and says palace women imitate her thinness through extreme dieting.

Hanji 531 presents Zhao Feiyan's ambition as strategic rather than satisfied by Empress Xu's removal alone. In the host's reading, Empress Xu is the obvious obstacle, but Ban Jieyu is the latent one: senior, reputable, well-born, and skilled enough in palace relationships that she could benefit if formal status were allocated by qualification rather than current favor. Zhao Feiyan's attack on Ban Jieyu therefore becomes preventive rival removal.

Hanji 534 adds the conversion from favored woman to formally recognized empress. Chengdi's preference cannot directly overcome Wang Zhengjun's status objection, so Chunyu Zhang spends more than a year moving between the palace centers to secure approval. The episode also says Chengdi first ennobles Zhao Feiyan's adoptive father Zhao Lin, making her elevation depend on status repair as well as personal favor.

Hanji 536-1 adds the immediate post-elevation vulnerability. Zhao Feiyan receives the empress title in 16 BCE, but [[ZhaoHedeWesternHan|赵合德]] receives stronger practical favor through the lavish Zhaoyang Palace. The episode's scandal narrative says Zhao Feiyan hides male favorites because she has no child and hopes to secure her position through pregnancy. It also says Chengdi's trust, mediated by Zhao Hede's emotional defense, turns later accusations into capital danger for informers.

Hanji 571 does not add a direct action by Zhao Feiyan in the [[CaoGongWesternHan|曹宫]] case, but it adds proximity and accusation context. Cao Gong is said to have taught Zhao Feiyan, and before dying she accuses the Zhao sisters of wanting to dominate the realm. That makes Zhao Feiyan part of the reported palace network around the case while leaving the exact responsibility question source-scoped.

Hanji 573 presents Zhao Feiyan and [[ZhaoHedeWesternHan|赵合德]] as accused favorites whose guilt story deserves skepticism rather than automatic acceptance. The episode stresses that Chengdi had children who died young before the Zhao sisters explain the pattern, that the investigation is testimony-heavy, and that [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] and the Wang family had strong reasons to monitor heir production. Zhao Feiyan is therefore treated as a plausible target of [[GenderedConsortScapegoating|红颜祸水式替罪叙事]], not as a cleared innocent or settled culprit.

Hanji 536-4 adds an explicit literary-memory layer. The episode says Zhao Feiyan's received image as a uniquely licentious woman may be less secure than later moralizing makes it appear, and it compares her reputation with [[XiaJiSpringAutumn|夏姬]], [[BaoSi|褒姒]], and [[HelenOfTroy|海伦]]. This does not erase the earlier scandal layer; it explains how scandal becomes durable public memory through gendered blame and moral performance.

Hanji 574 presents Zhao Feiyan as a politically sensitive beneficiary-protector figure for Aidi. When Aidi was selected as crown prince, Zhao Feiyan is said to have helped him; [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]] was grateful to her and maintained a friendly relationship with her. Geng Yu's memorial changes the frame around the case from palace jealousy and favored-woman crime to Chengdi's posthumous reputation and succession legitimacy.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han palace figure whose rise begins with abandoned-child and Yang'a-household performance narratives, then becomes exclusive favor, requires jealousy management when Zhao Hede enters, and includes preventive action against both the sitting empress and a credible senior consort.
- Empress whose formal rank is made insecure by childlessness and by Zhao Hede's stronger practical favor.
- Accused in Hanji 536-1 of hiding men in palace rooms to seek pregnancy.
- Paired with Zhao Hede as an accused favorite in Chengdi's heir-loss narrative.
- Connected to Cao Gong through palace literary instruction before Cao Gong's infant becomes the reported missing-son case.
- Possible object of gendered scapegoating whose "red-beauty disaster" reputation is explicitly questioned rather than treated as a settled single-cause explanation.
- Helped Aidi's earlier designation as crown prince according to Hanji 574.

## Qualifications

This page does not reconstruct Zhao Feiyan's full life, legal case record, or every stage of her rise. Hanji 529 supplies an origin and palace-entry narrative with variant birth traditions and popular reputation language; it should not be treated as strict source adjudication. Hanji 530 supplies a host interpretation of Chengdi managing her jealousy and palace beauty culture, not direct access to Zhao Feiyan's inner state. Hanji 531 supplies a host interpretation of her preventive attack on Ban Jieyu, not direct access to Zhao Feiyan's inner motive. Hanji 534 supplies the elevation procedure and status-repair frame, but not Zhao Feiyan's own strategic account. Hanji 536-1 supplies an early scandal narrative around adultery, childlessness, and alleged pregnancy obstruction, but parts of that field remain rumor-shaped and must be held beside later evidentiary caution. Hanji 571 supplies proximity to Cao Gong and a dying accusation against the Zhao sisters, but not complete proof of Zhao Feiyan's personal action. Hanji 573 challenges the evidentiary and narrative simplicity of the accusation but does not prove innocence. Hanji 574 supplies the Aidi-era closure frame and the memorial's political reading of the case.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 530's pre-elevation favorite layer: Zhao Feiyan is already the exclusive favorite, but Zhao Hede's entry requires Chengdi to manage jealousy and marks the start of Zhao-sister concentrated favor.
- Added Hanji 529's origin and palace-entry layer: abandoned-child traditions, Yang'a Princess's household, dance performance, and jieyu promotion.
- Preserved Hanji 536-4's reputation-history layer and Hanji 573's evidentiary caution so the scandal layer does not become a settled explanation for every Chengdi heir-loss claim.
