# The Gracchi / 格拉古兄弟

## Overview

The Gracchi / 格拉古兄弟 are the reforming brothers whose deaths mark the wiki's key pre-Marius precedent for republican political violence and dangerous popular office.

## Current Profile

The Caesar source treats the Gracchi aftermath as one of the signs that the [[RomanRepublic|Roman Republic]] was already damaged before Caesar. Episode 701 explains why that aftermath mattered for [[GaiusMarius]]: Tiberius Gracchus and Gaius Gracchus had used the tribunate to push reforms favoring the poor, both were killed, and about 3,000 followers of Gaius Gracchus were executed without trial. When Marius runs for tribune in 119 BC, he therefore enters an office whose formal powers still exist but whose protections have already been violated.

The Gracchi are stored here as a collective because the current evidence uses them as a paired precedent rather than as two separate biographies.

## Key Characteristics

- The Gracchi make the tribunate a reform office with high personal danger.
- Their deaths show elite and emergency violence entering republican politics before Marius, Sulla, and Caesar.
- Their poor-favoring reform memory helps define the terrain for later popularis positioning.
- Their aftermath is one of the earliest crisis markers linking social inequality to constitutional breakdown.

## Evidence

- Pre-Caesar crisis marker: [[124-kaisa-zhisi-lishi-de-yunjiao-shifou-yi-shixiao-781894361]] lists the Gracchi aftermath among the pressures that weakened republican norms before Caesar.
- Tribune danger: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] says Marius's 119 BC tribunate was dangerous because the Gracchi had used that office to push reforms favoring the poor.
- Violence precedent: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] records that both Gracchi were killed and that 3,000 followers of Gaius Gracchus were executed without trial.
- Popularis context: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] places Marius's voting-pen reform and reputation for defending the common man after this Gracchan precedent.

## Qualifications

The page does not reconstruct the brothers' full reform programs or chronology. It records the current wiki role: their deaths and followers' executions made reform through republican office look vulnerable to political emergency and violence.
