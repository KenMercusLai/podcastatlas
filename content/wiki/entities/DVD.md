# DVD

DVD appears in [[the-invention-invention]] as a later media standard that followed the [[MPEGPatentPool]] template. The source uses DVDs to show that patent pools were not only a sewing-machine historical curiosity or a single video-compression episode; they became a recurring structure for consumer technology standards.
