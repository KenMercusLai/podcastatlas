# Anne Koedt / 安妮·科特

## Overview
Anne Koedt is presented as an American radical feminist whose 1968 essay *The Myth of the Vaginal Orgasm* challenged the hierarchy that treated vaginal orgasm as mature and clitoral pleasure as inferior.

## Current Profile
[[af3491ca3aca3a270aadb44a7f572e93-af3491ca3aca3a270aadb44a7f572e93]] places Koedt within the feminist pressure that made women's sexual pleasure and bodily self-knowledge legitimate questions independent of reproduction or male participation. Her intervention is framed against a Freudian sexual script in which autonomous clitoral pleasure was treated as immature and heterosexual penetration supplied the standard of adult sexuality.

## Key Characteristics
- Radical feminist writer associated with a foundational critique of the vaginal-orgasm hierarchy.
- Connects anatomical knowledge to the political legitimacy of women's independent pleasure.
- Challenges theories that define sexual maturity through male participation.
- Represents social movements as active contributors to changes in research questions, not merely recipients of later science.

## Evidence
- **Critique of sexual hierarchy:** [[af3491ca3aca3a270aadb44a7f572e93-af3491ca3aca3a270aadb44a7f572e93]] identifies Koedt's 1968 essay as an attack on the patriarchal construction of vaginal orgasm as the adult norm.
- **Movement-level effect:** [[af3491ca3aca3a270aadb44a7f572e93-af3491ca3aca3a270aadb44a7f572e93]] places this critique beside contraception and wider feminist thought as forces that separated sexual pleasure from inevitable pregnancy and challenged which bodily questions deserved study.
