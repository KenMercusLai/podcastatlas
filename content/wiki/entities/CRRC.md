# CRRC / 中国中车

CRRC is the Chinese rolling-stock manufacturer context at the end of [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]]. The episode's earlier sections discuss predecessor firms such as Sifang and Changchun Railway Vehicles through the [[Hexiehao]] technology-transfer period, while the closing section names China Railway Rolling Stock Corporation in connection with high-speed maglev development.

In wiki terms, CRRC connects [[ProcurementLedTechnologyTransfer]] to [[PrecisionManufacturingAsStrategy]] and [[ChineseStandardEMU]]. The episode's story is not that a single company invented high-speed rail alone; it is that domestic firms learned through imported platforms, assembly, component substitution, redesign, and system-level integration until [[Fuxinghao]] and [[CR450]] became plausible.
