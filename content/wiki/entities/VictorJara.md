# Victor Jara

## Overview
Victor Jara was a Chilean folk singer and leading cultural figure of the left who was detained and killed after the September 1973 coup.

## Current Profile
[[371-the-1973-chilean-coup-general-pinochet-seizes-power-part-2-glt3740766328]] places Jara within the mass detention system that followed the coup. His killing in Estadio Chile five days later makes cultural repression concrete within the wider movement from military seizure to organized state terror.

## Key Characteristics
- He was a prominent folk singer associated with Chile's political left.
- His cultural visibility made his detention and killing symbolically consequential.
- His death occurred within the coup regime's early mass-arrest apparatus.
