## Current Profile

The current source does not discuss the text's authorship, commentary tradition, or broader intellectual history. It uses Chunqiu as a shared scholarly field where reputation matters: Zhai Fangjin and Hu Chang both lecture on it, and Hu's envy of Zhai's stronger learning becomes the interpersonal conflict that Zhai later de-escalates through visible respect.
