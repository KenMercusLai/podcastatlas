# Maya Active

## Overview
Maya Active is the yoga-apparel brand in the source, owned by [[AntaSports|Anta Sports / 安踏]] and used to test fitness-space-based brand building.

## Current Profile
The source presents Maya Active as Anta's acquired women's yoga and athleisure branch. Its product positioning sits below [[Lululemon]] pricing, but the source says that advantage is narrowing as Lululemon discounts and other brands enter China. Opening a large Shanghai fitness space with [[PureFitness|Pure]] is therefore framed as a brand-identity and user-operation move rather than only a store-expansion tactic.

## Key Characteristics
- Anta-owned yoga-apparel brand acquired to fill a women's yoga-sports gap.
- Core product is yoga pants in a mid-premium price band below Lululemon in the source account.
- Store expansion appears slower than a simple acquisition-growth story would imply.
- Faces competition from Lululemon discounts, [[AloYoga|Alo Yoga]], [[Nike]], and other entrants.
- Uses a fitness-space partnership as an offline brand-experience strategy.

## Evidence
- Ownership evidence: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says Anta acquired 75% of Maya Active in 2023.
- Expansion evidence: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says the brand had 36 stores at acquisition and still had only more than 50 by the current midyear point.
- Fitness-space evidence: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says Maya Active and Pure plan a more than 2,000-square-meter Shanghai fitness facility opening in November.

## Qualifications
The source does not prove the fitness-space strategy will improve apparel sales or brand retention. Pricing, store count, and competitor dynamics remain source-scoped, and the episode itself cautions that overseas high-end sportswear playbooks may not fit Anta's first acquired local brand.

## What Changed
- Created Maya Active as Anta's yoga-apparel and offline experience-space branch.
