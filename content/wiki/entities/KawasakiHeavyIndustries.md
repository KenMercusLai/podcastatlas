# Kawasaki Heavy Industries / 川崎重工

Kawasaki Heavy Industries is one of the foreign railway vendors in [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]]. The episode links the company to the Japanese technology lineage behind early [[Hexiehao]] CRH2 trainsets and to the first large 2004 procurement round under the [[MinistryOfRailwaysChina]].

The source treats Kawasaki as both a technology supplier and a bargaining counterparty. It says the Japanese side initially resisted broad transfer, then conceded enough to win part of the order, while later reluctance around 300 km/h technology pushed Chinese firms toward more aggressive domestic redesign.
