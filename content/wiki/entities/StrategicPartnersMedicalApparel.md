## Overview
Strategic Partners is the incumbent medical-apparel company identified in [[figs-trina-spear-and-heather-hasson-the-2-5-billion-scrubs-brand-that-investors-said-would-never-work-vmp6448511514]] as the plaintiff in a major lawsuit against [[FIGS]]. The semantic suffix distinguishes this company from unrelated organizations with the generic name Strategic Partners.

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents Strategic Partners only through the founders' account of litigation. It says the company alleged false product statements and misuse of trade secrets, sought approximately $800 million, and lost after a jury trial that concluded in early 2022.

## Key Characteristics
- It operated as an incumbent in medical apparel when FIGS entered the category.
- It brought claims concerning product statements and alleged trade-secret misuse.
- Its lawsuit became a large financial and organizational burden for FIGS.
