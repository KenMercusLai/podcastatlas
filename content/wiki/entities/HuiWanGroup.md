# Hui Wan Group

Hui Wan Group appears in [[for-bucks-sake-the-rise-of-self-made-billionaires-6a61e076562e3ad886d54c95]] as part of the financial-pressure campaign against scam networks linked to [[Cambodia]]. The source describes it as the world's largest illicit marketplace and says the [[UnitedStates]] blocked subsidiaries of the group from America's financial system in June.

In the wiki, Hui Wan Group is a concrete marketplace node between consumer-facing [[SocialEngineeringFraud]] and enforcement tools such as sanctions, financial-system blocking, and crypto seizures. The page keeps the "world's largest" description source-scoped to this episode.
