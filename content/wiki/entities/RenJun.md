# 任峻 / Ren Jun

任峻 / Ren Jun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1008-ruhe-pingjia-caocao-de-tuntianzhi-ls8wvh9ox-pcjmt0z8w82opbha-k|Hanji 1008]] as the official [[CaoCao|曹操]] appoints as典农中郎将 after [[ZaoZhi|枣祗]] proposes [[TuntianSystem|屯田制]]. The source places him inside the execution layer rather than the idea layer: the policy needs named offices and administrators before it can become grain.

Ren Jun's page is source-scoped. In this episode, his significance is that曹操's response is not only approval of good advice but rapid institutionalization. Ren Jun helps make屯田 an operating system around [[Xudu|许都]], connecting agricultural recovery to military supply.
