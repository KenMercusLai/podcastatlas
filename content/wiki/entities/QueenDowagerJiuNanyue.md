## Overview

樛太后 / Queen Dowager Jiu is the Han-born mother of Nanyue king [[ZhaoXingNanyue|赵兴]], presented as the strongest royal advocate of closer incorporation into Han and the intended organizer of a banquet move against [[LyuJiaNanyue|吕嘉]]. Lü Jia converts her origin, envoy relationship, and policy into hostile mobilization material before killing her in the coup.

## Current Profile

Hanji 352-1 portrays Queen Dowager Jiu as more willing than her son and the Han envoys to remove Lü Jia when he appears at a royal banquet. The attempt fails: the envoys hesitate, Zhao Xing restrains her, and Lü Jia leaves. The episode uses this scene to make her an emblem of forceful decision, while the actual balance of palace power remains unclear.

Once the Han mission under [[HanQianqiuWesternHan|韩千秋]] and her brother [[JiuLeNanyue|樛乐]] enters Nanyue, Lü Jia alleges that her Han origin, sexual relationship with an envoy, and wish to transfer treasure and people prove betrayal. His faction kills her alongside Zhao Xing and the Han envoys. The sexual and enslavement claims are preserved as hostile rhetoric rather than accepted as neutral biography.

## Key Characteristics

- Han-born Nanyue queen dowager and mother of Zhao Xing.
- Royal advocate of closer submission to Han.
- Intended initiator of a direct move against Lü Jia at the banquet.
- Political target whose origin and intimate life are weaponized in mobilization rhetoric.
- Coup victim killed with the king and Han envoys.

## Qualifications

The supplied summary writes her name as “纠太后”; the wiki normalizes it to the received form 樛太后. This source does not supply her personal name, full marriage history, independent court network, or perspective beyond the crisis. The host's sympathy and decisiveness framing should not convert the failed banquet counterfactual into certainty. Lü Jia's sexual and enslavement allegations belong to partisan mobilization and require independent evidence before use as fact.
