# 侯生 / Hou Sheng (Fangshi)

侯生 / Hou Sheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-125-2-lishishang-qinshihuang-weihe-yao-fenshu-kengru-lulaivzp1xqs71nbaltr-ygpjihw|Qinji 125-2]] as a fangshi around [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]]'s failed search for immortals and deathless medicine. He is distinct from [[HouSheng|后胜]], the late-[[QiState|齐国]] chancellor; this page preserves the source's separate 侯生 figure.

In the episode, Hou Sheng and [[LuSheng|卢生]] criticize Qin Shi Huang behind his back and then flee. Their critique is political as much as personal: Qin Shi Huang is portrayed as self-willed and punitive, doctoral officials are treated as decoration, and all affairs depend on the emperor's own decision. The role makes Hou Sheng part of the transition from [[FangshiAccountabilityTrap|方士问责陷阱]] to the [[QinKengruIncident|秦代坑儒事件]].

## Key Claims
- Hou Sheng represents the fangshi who must escape once miracle promises become undeliverable.
- His criticism of Qin Shi Huang highlights centralized decision-making, punitive rule, and fear-driven silence.
- The source leaves his later fate uncertain.
- This page disambiguates 侯生 from the existing [[HouSheng|后胜]] page.
