# 周源 / Zhou Yuan

周源 / Zhou Yuan is the [[Zhihu|知乎]] founder in [[no-212-cengjing-de-jingshen-jiaoluo-douban-zhihu-tieba-hupu-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-25-1003510980]]. The episode traces his path from programmer to IT journalist and then to entrepreneurship, including earlier failed startup attempts before the idea for Zhihu formed.

In the source, Zhou's journalism period matters because he wanted to "participate in things happening" rather than only observe them. After a failed meta-search project and a trip to Tibet, he and early collaborators discussed a Q&A community inspired by serious technology-blog conversation. Zhihu launched in January 2011 as an invitation-based knowledge community.

Zhou Yuan's source role is not just founder biography. He anchors the wiki's [[KnowledgeCommunityCreatorIncentives]] problem: the early room's credibility came from invitation, expertise, and peer recognition, but scale, monetization, creator compensation, and competitor subsidies made it harder to keep the room's original social contract intact.
