## Overview

东郭咸阳 / Dongguo Xianyang enters the wiki as a Qi-region salt merchant whom [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s court recruits into the agricultural-finance administration to help establish official salt operation.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-321-huangdi-gen-shangren-qiang-fanwan-ni-jianguo-ma-luab_ikpgy_tmykmw-6e6voi-ghv|Hanji 321]] presents Dongguo Xianyang as a wealthy specialist brought into government because existing officials lacked practical knowledge of salt production and trade. Paired with iron merchant [[KongJinWesternHan|孔仅]] and placed beside [[SangHongyangWesternHan|桑弘羊]], he represents the court's conversion of private commercial expertise into [[StateCommercialMonopolyExtraction|state fiscal capacity]].

The episode associates him with a hybrid salt system: recruited households perform the boiling, officials provide the iron vessel described as a 牢盆, the product is delivered to government, and government controls sale. The supplied evidence does not isolate which details Dongguo personally designed or administered.

## Key Characteristics

- Qi-region merchant associated with private salt production and substantial wealth.
- Recruited because the fiscal administration lacked salt-industry expertise.
- Appointed with Kong Jin to a salt-and-iron administrative role.
- Associated with a hybrid system of private production, official equipment, exclusive official purchase, and monopoly sale.
- Illustrates how a state can absorb merchant knowledge while displacing private control of the same profit field.
