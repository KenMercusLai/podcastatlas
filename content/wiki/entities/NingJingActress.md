# 宁静 / Ning Jing

## Overview
宁静 is an actor and participant in the first season of [[SistersWhoMakeWaves|《乘风破浪的姐姐》]]. The source treats her team as a case of effective coordination intertwined with seniority-based informal power.

## Current Profile
The hosts credit Ning with observing members' strengths, assigning work effectively, and leading teams toward results. They also object to the durable “big sister” structure around her: other members often accommodated her views, and nominal leaders could still need senior approval. Her profile in this evidence therefore separates practical leadership capability from the costs of concentrated informal authority.

## Key Characteristics
- Identifies team members' strengths and allocates roles around them.
- Commands substantial informal authority through seniority and persona.
- Can improve execution while narrowing room for other leaders to develop.
- Attracts teammates partly because affiliation may improve competitive survival.

- **Coordination skill:** [[ep136-er-liao-lang-jie-zhichang-yiran-you-hen-duo-zhi-de-jiyi-de-shanguangdian-wkg5hf9t5qetspydbxyk6to0ea4442]] credits Ning with reading members' advantages, dividing work, and producing team results.
- **Informal hierarchy:** [[ep136-er-liao-lang-jie-zhichang-yiran-you-hen-duo-zhi-de-jiyi-de-shanguangdian-wkg5hf9t5qetspydbxyk6to0ea4442]] says participants repeatedly centered her preferences even when someone else formally held responsibility.
- **Strategic affiliation:** [[ep136-er-liao-lang-jie-zhichang-yiran-you-hen-duo-zhi-de-jiyi-de-shanguangdian-wkg5hf9t5qetspydbxyk6to0ea4442]] records the hosts' inference that some participants chose her group partly because reputation and prior results appeared to improve survival odds.

## Qualifications
The account is based on edited television and host reactions. It does not establish how decisions were made off camera or whether deference arose from coercion, respect, strategy, or production framing. The survival motive is explicitly an inference.
