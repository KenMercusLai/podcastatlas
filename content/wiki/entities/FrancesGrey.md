# Frances Grey

## Overview

Frances Grey is Henry VIII's niece and [[LadyJaneGrey|Lady Jane Grey]]'s mother. Her place in the hereditary sequence makes her omission from [[EdwardVI|Edward VI]]'s device an important unresolved feature of the 1553 succession plan.

## Current Profile

Frances descends from Henry VIII's younger sister Mary and therefore stands genealogically before her daughters. Edward's early draft nevertheless directs the crown toward male heirs of Frances's daughters, and his revision names Jane rather than Frances herself.

The episode reports contemporary or later impressions that Frances was unreliable, flighty, or difficult, but does not establish that personality alone explains her exclusion. Jane's account of severe parental treatment adds family context without permitting a confident reconstruction of either parent's individual conduct.

## Key Characteristics

- Frances connects Jane to the Tudor line through Henry VIII's younger sister Mary.
- Her hereditary position is closer than Jane's, making her omission analytically significant.
- Edward's device directs succession through her daughters rather than to Frances herself.
- Negative character descriptions are reported but not independently established.
- Jane's account places her within a severe parental household without isolating maternal responsibility.
