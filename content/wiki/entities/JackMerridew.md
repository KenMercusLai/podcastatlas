# Jack Merridew / 杰克（《蝇王》）

## Overview
Jack Merridew / 杰克 is the hunting-faction leader in [[LordOfTheFlies|《蝇王》]] as discussed in [[vol-351-yingwang-21shiji-de-huangdao-gushi-haineng-zenme-kai-naodong-1983-nuojiang-dezhu-gkwriueodap1a3taaattkgol]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Jack as the boy who converts choir discipline and hunting energy into rival sovereignty. His appeal comes from meat, excitement, masks, and freedom from Ralph's rescue-oriented rules. He is not treated as the only source of evil, but he is the political organizer of the island's turn from assembly and signal fire toward ritual, faction, and pursuit.

## Key Characteristics
- He begins with organized choir authority and shifts it toward hunting.
- He turns meat, pleasure, and action into a competing basis of legitimacy.
- His faction lets fear of the beast become ritual control.
- He opposes Ralph's rescue-and-rule project by prioritizing hunting and destruction.
- His final pursuit of Ralph makes factional power openly murderous.

## Qualifications
The page follows the episode's symbolic and political reading. Jack is not treated as a complete explanation of the novel's evil; the source also gives important roles to fear, group frenzy, technology, and Roger's more direct violence.
