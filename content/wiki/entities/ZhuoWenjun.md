## Overview

卓文君 / Zhuo Wenjun is [[ZhuoWangsunWesternHan|卓王孙]]'s daughter and the central woman in the “琴挑文君,” elopement, tavern, and [[BaiTouYin|《白头吟》]] traditions preserved by Hanji 358-2 and 359-1.

## Current Profile

Hanji 358-2 places Zhuo Wenjun inside a courtship shaped by both personal choice and deliberate staging. She hears [[SimaXiangru|司马相如]] perform [[FengQiuHuang|《凤求凰》]] from behind a screen, receives a message through a maid in the program's account, and elopes with him to Chengdu.

Hanji 359-1 extends her agency beyond the initial departure. It says she endures poverty after her father withholds support, proposes a small business, sells wine at the couple's tavern, and later answers Sima's reported wish for a concubine through 《白头吟》. The host reads this sequence as commitment under hardship followed by a restrained defense of fidelity, but the private motives and strategic “以退为进” interpretation remain unverified.

Together the episodes complicate a simple romance. Sima and [[WangJiLinqiongWesternHan|王吉]] are said to have engineered access before the elopement; Zhuo Wenjun nevertheless makes consequential choices in leaving, working, and responding to a later relationship crisis. Her actions are the strongest source-backed layer, while inner feeling, poem authorship, and the effect of the poem on the marriage remain less secure.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han woman presented as the widowed daughter of a wealthy Linqiong household.
- Central listener and decision-maker in the “琴挑文君” and elopement retelling.
- Presented as accepting poverty and participating directly in the couple's tavern livelihood.
- Literary memory connects her to 《白头吟》 and a boundary-setting response to threatened abandonment.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to two short episodes from the same popular-history series. The banquet choreography, maid's role, message, tavern details, property transfer, Maoling woman, Zhuo Wenjun's private feelings, 《白头吟》 authorship and transmission, and later relationship chronology are not independently established here. Calling the courtship a “局” or the poem “以退为进” records the host's interpretation and should not erase Zhuo Wenjun's decision-making or turn strategic silence into universal marriage advice.

## What Changed

- Extended Zhuo Wenjun's agency from courtship and elopement into shared work and response to a fidelity crisis.
- Added the 《白头吟》 tradition while keeping authorship, textual wording, motive, and marital effect qualified.
- Reframed willingness to endure poverty as source evidence against the host's rejected wealth-seeking interpretation, not proof of private feeling.
