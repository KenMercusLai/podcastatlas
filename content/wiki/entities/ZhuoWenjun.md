## Overview

卓文君 / Zhuo Wenjun appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-358-2-simaxiangru-diao-fengliu-guafu-lphrip8prodrc46x__y6elbrkigz|Hanji 358-2]] as [[ZhuoWangsunWesternHan|卓王孙]]'s widowed daughter and the listener at the center of the “琴挑文君” tradition.

## Current Profile

The episode places Zhuo Wenjun inside a courtship shaped by both personal choice and deliberate staging. She hears [[SimaXiangru|司马相如]] perform [[FengQiuHuang|《凤求凰》]] from behind a screen, receives a message through a maid in the program's account, and elopes with him to Chengdu.

The program celebrates the pair as a famous love story but also complicates that romance by saying Sima and [[WangJiLinqiongWesternHan|王吉]] had engineered access and reputation in advance. Zhuo Wenjun's agency is therefore present in the decision to leave, while her inner feelings and the exact means of persuasion remain narrated rather than independently established.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han woman presented as the widowed daughter of a wealthy Linqiong household.
- Central listener and decision-maker in the episode's “琴挑文君” retelling.
- Elopes with Sima Xiangru to Chengdu in the source's account.
- Later literary memory is associated by the episode with fidelity, abandonment, and “愿得一人心，白头不相离.”

- Musical encounter: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-358-2-simaxiangru-diao-fengliu-guafu-lphrip8prodrc46x__y6elbrkigz|Hanji 358-2]] says she hears Sima perform 《凤求凰》 from behind a screen at Zhuo Wangsun's banquet.
- Elopement decision: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-358-2-simaxiangru-diao-fengliu-guafu-lphrip8prodrc46x__y6elbrkigz|Hanji 358-2]] says a maid carries a private message before Zhuo Wenjun and Sima leave for Chengdu at night.
- Literary afterlife: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-358-2-simaxiangru-diao-fengliu-guafu-lphrip8prodrc46x__y6elbrkigz|Hanji 358-2]] associates her with a celebrated fidelity line and previews her response to Sima's later attachment elsewhere.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one short popular-history episode. The exact banquet choreography, maid's role, message, Zhuo Wenjun's private emotional response, authorship of works attributed to her, and later relationship chronology are not independently established here. Calling the approach a “局” is the host's interpretation and should not erase Zhuo Wenjun's own decision-making.

## What Changed

- Created a source-bounded profile separating Zhuo Wenjun's reported actions from the episode's interpretation of her feelings.
- Preserved disputed literary attribution and courtship details as qualifications.
