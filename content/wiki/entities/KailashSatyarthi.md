# Kailash Satyarthi

## Overview
Kailash Satyarthi is a child-rights activist and [[TomorrowToday]] guest whose represented account connects direct rescue, consumer campaigning, [[GoodWeave]], [[TransformativeCompassion]], and [[CompassionateAI]].

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents Satyarthi as an activist who moved from rescuing individual trafficked children toward changing the market and institutional conditions that reproduced exploitation. He attributes the courage to confront criminal networks, commercial interests, and discriminatory traditions to compassion understood as responsibility and action, then extends that ethic to leadership and artificial intelligence.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats child exploitation as a structural system sustained by employer incentives and vulnerable communities, not only as isolated wrongdoing.
- Converts field experience into institution-building through consumer pressure and [[EthicalSupplyChainCertification]].
- Defines compassion as disruptive, dynamic, and transformative rather than as pity, kindness, or temporary charity alone.
- Places dignity, freedom, relationships, and moral accountability above efficiency-only optimization.
- Proposes [[CompassionQuotient]] as a trainable personal and institutional measure.
