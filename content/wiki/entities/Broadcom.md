# Broadcom

## Overview
Broadcom appears in the sources as a specialist implementation and connectivity partner for custom AI accelerators. It contributes the physical, mixed-signal, network-topology, and back-end capabilities that turn a model company's architecture into a deployable chip and multi-chip system.

## Current Profile
The current profile spans two custom-silicon relationships. In Google's TPU system, Broadcom is tied to ICI, physical links, topology details, and back-end implementation. In [[e251-tuili-xinpian-zhi-zhan-liaoliao-groq-cerebras-yu-openai-sanda-lujing-yu-bill-dally-de-sheji-zhexue-786d63c4-d46c-4ff3-80f8-fc895b2a57f0|E251]], it is named as OpenAI's partner on [[HanaPino]]. Both cases show that custom accelerator strategy depends on implementation and system integration, while also creating supplier bargaining and execution dependencies.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides back-end and physical implementation capability for custom AI chips.
- Contributes mixed-signal, connectivity, and topology expertise to multi-chip systems.
- Helps model and cloud companies convert workload requirements into deployable silicon.
- Acts as both a capability multiplier and a strategic supplier dependency.
