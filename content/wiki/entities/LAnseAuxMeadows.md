## Overview

L'Anse aux Meadows is a Norse archaeological site in Newfoundland that provides material evidence for pre-Columbian Scandinavian presence in North America.

## Current Profile

[[103-the-norse-sagas-glt8280648151]] presents Helge and Anne Ingstad's 1960s identification of the site as the decisive archaeological counterpart to the Vinland traditions. Its remains fit a temporary base used for overwintering, ship repair, and perhaps further travel better than a permanent colony.

## Key Characteristics

- Materially establishes a Norse presence in Newfoundland.
- Appears temporary rather than a permanent settler community.
- Supports the broad Vinland voyage tradition without mapping every saga location.
- May have served as a repair and staging base for travel farther south.

## Evidence

- Identification: [[103-the-norse-sagas-glt8280648151]] credits Helge and Anne Ingstad with recognizing Norse remains in the 1960s.
- Site function: [[103-the-norse-sagas-glt8280648151]] describes evidence for overwintering, ship repair, and possible onward journeys.
- Historical boundary: [[103-the-norse-sagas-glt8280648151]] uses the site to corroborate a broad travel outline while retaining uncertainty around saga geography.

## Qualifications

The site does not by itself prove that it was the whole of Vinland, identify every traveler named in the sagas, or establish the exact duration and reach of Norse activity in North America.

## What Changed

- Initial profile created as the archaeological anchor and evidentiary limit for the Vinland tradition.
