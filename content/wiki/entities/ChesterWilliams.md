# Chester Williams

## Overview

Chester Williams is presented as the only non-white player in the 1995 [[Springboks]] squad, making him a visible but limited sign of racial change inside a still overwhelmingly white rugby symbol.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Williams to qualify the reconciliation story. Mandela's embrace of the Springboks works as political theater, but the team itself is not presented as already transformed. Williams, nicknamed the Black Pearl in the source, shows that the 1995 symbol had power precisely because inclusion remained thin and contested.

## Key Characteristics

- Only non-white player in the 1995 Springboks squad in the episode's account.
- Nicknamed the Black Pearl by the source.
- Figure whose presence made racial representation visible but limited.
- Part of the team Mandela asked black South Africans to support despite its apartheid-era associations.
- Qualification on reading the 1995 rugby moment as complete transformation.
