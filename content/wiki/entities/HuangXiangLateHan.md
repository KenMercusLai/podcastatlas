## Overview

黄香 / Huang Xiang appears in the current wiki as the [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]] court official whose refusal of an outwardly higher [[DongCommandery|Dong Commandery]] appointment turns the [[ShangshuTaiImperialAccess|Shangshu Tai]] into a concrete case of office value exceeding formal rank.

## Current Profile

Hanji 769 presents Huang Xiang as shangshu ling when Emperor He plans to transfer him to Dong Commandery as taishou. The episode explains the move as a nominal promotion from a lower-ranking court post to a two-thousand-shi commandery office with substantial local authority.

Huang Xiang petitions that he is not suited to governing a commandery and asks instead to remain in court handling the smaller business of the Shangshu desk. Emperor He accepts the request, reappoints him as shangshu ling, and raises the office's rank to two thousand shi, making the case a clear signal of trust rather than failed promotion.

The host reads Huang Xiang's decision as both self-knowledge and rational positioning. Remaining near the emperor, senior ministers, court documents, and information flows gives a court official visibility, network access, and future opportunity that a more powerful but distant local post may not provide.

## Key Characteristics

- Court official whose current profile is defined by choosing Shangshu proximity over a commandery-level promotion.
- Presented as humble, diligent, loyal to duty, and careful about the work he is suited to perform.
- Career case for distinguishing formal rank and real office value.
- Carrier of the episode's argument that information, visibility, and high-level access are political resources.
- Evidence point for Emperor He's willingness to retain and elevate a trusted court operator.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 769 and is not a full Huang Xiang biography. The source gives a strong office-choice case, but the host's rational-positioning explanation remains an interpretation layered on top of Huang Xiang's stated self-fit claim.
