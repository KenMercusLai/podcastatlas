# Marco Rubio

Marco Rubio appears in [[caracas-under-pressure-democracy-in-venezuela-6a7d8bbe7d85cbdf96e00822]] as the U.S. secretary of state driving a three-phase transition plan for [[Venezuela]]. The source presents him as personally invested in negotiations that are supposed to move from institutional reform toward free and fair elections.

The episode makes Rubio important because the [[UnitedStates]] has leverage over Venezuelan oil revenue. Royalties and taxes are described as requiring State Department approval, so Rubio's office can affect whether money reaches the [[DelcyRodriguez]] administration. That turns him into a practical actor in [[OilRevenueSanctionsLeverage]], not only a public commentator.
