# 北宫伯玉 / Beigong Boyu (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] backfills Beigong Boyu's first rebel-command layer. The source says [[XianlingQiang|先零羌]] and related groups kill [[LingZhengLateHan|伶征]], then elect Beigong Boyu and [[LiWenhouLateHan|李文侯]] as commanders while coercing [[BianZhangLateHan|边章]] and [[HanSui|韩遂]] to handle military affairs.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-934-sha-yiren-ze-tianxia-an-ni-sha-busha-ltar8hvdkqct8oizm3k22z38b1v8|Hanji 934]] backfills Beigong Boyu before the Han Sui consolidation branch. The episode says Beigong Boyu and related rebels again attack the Sanfu / [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] region in 185 CE, prompting [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] to send [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]] to guard [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]].

北宫伯玉 / Beigong Boyu (late Han) enters through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-937-zhanshen-zhisi-haoren-zongshi-duozai-duonan-lp7przsmag9ookq7eoisvsmnm5aw|Hanji 937]] as one of the Liang州 rebel figures whose force is absorbed by [[HanSui|韩遂]] before Han Sui attacks [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]].

The page is source-scoped to that consolidation layer. Beigong Boyu is important here because his removal from the rebel leadership field helps explain why Han Sui can become the more visible western power before the later [[WangGuoLateHan|王国]] and [[MaTeng|马腾]] sequence.
