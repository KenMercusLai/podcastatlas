# Ryan Litt

## Overview

Ryan Litt is presented as co-founder and COO of [[ThreeAMInnovations|3AM Innovations]], where he works on situational-awareness technology for firefighters and incident commanders.

## Current Profile

Litt frames emergency AI as purpose-built decision support for unpredictable conditions. His design stance begins with observing responders, minimizing interaction burden, and converting noisy radio, sensor, and location inputs into information a human commander can assess.

He resists broad claims about minutes or lives saved and instead points to bounded operational examples, such as routing the closest resources at a marathon and reconstructing incident history afterward.

## Key Characteristics

- Connects product design to direct observation of emergency work.
- Prioritizes low-friction and unobtrusive tools over additional screens or controls.
- Treats AI as information retrieval and prioritization, not autonomous command.
- Uses multi-sensor location and radio context to reduce incident uncertainty.
- Avoids universal impact estimates where incident conditions differ.
