# Psilocybin Mushrooms

Psilocybin mushrooms are the central sacramental substance in [[a-trip-to-the-magic-mushroom-megachurch]]. [[ZydorChurch]] and [[DaveHodges]] describe mushrooms as giving direct access to the divine, while the episode treats them as federally illegal controlled substances whose religious use depends on [[ReligiousControlledSubstanceExemption]] arguments and legal risk tolerance.

The source emphasizes dose and setting. Zydor offers multiple strains and potencies, including edibles, and Dave's theology emphasizes doses much higher than ordinary recreational use. That makes [[HighDosePsychedelicPractice]] central to the church's claim that its work is religious rather than casual consumption.
