# Chang'e / 嫦娥

## Overview

Chang'e / 嫦娥 is the lunar figure at the center of this source's Mid-Autumn discussion. She is not assigned one fixed biography: her flight to the moon is alternately described as selfish pursuit of immortality, protection of an elixir, punishment for marital betrayal, sacrifice, or escape from an unsatisfying relationship.

## Current Profile

[[ep34-zhongqiu-teji-linghun-kaowen-biepa-jinye-kongxu-jimo-leng-wkgkk1unb1oxo9ngcvx-ivoggiw069|EP34]] treats disagreement about Chang'e as evidence of [[MythAsSocialMirror|myth's social reinterpretability]]. A version can condemn female desire, praise self-sacrifice, preserve marital fidelity, or recover personal agency depending on which action, motive, and consequence a teller emphasizes.

The source also places her inside [[MidAutumnFestivalLayering|Mid-Autumn layering]]. Her cold, solitary moon palace contrasts with a festival of reunion, while shared moonlight and poetry turn that separation into a language for longing across households, countries, and generations. This is a reception profile, not a settled genealogy of lunar religion.

## Key Characteristics

- Occupies the moon after taking an immortality elixir in several incompatible story versions.
- Carries competing moral profiles of betrayer, protector, sacrifice, self-seeker, and marital escapee.
- Makes female desire and autonomy unusually visible because her decisive action changes the relationship.
- Is kept tied to Hou Yi by many later ethical readings even when other lunar figures share her setting.
- Connects lunar solitude to a festival centered on family reunion and distant attachment.

## Evidence

- **Competing motives:** [[ep34-zhongqiu-teji-linghun-kaowen-biepa-jinye-kongxu-jimo-leng-wkgkk1unb1oxo9ngcvx-ivoggiw069|EP34]] contrasts deliberate elixir theft with protective swallowing and later marital-escape readings.
- **Gender and marriage:** [[ep34-zhongqiu-teji-linghun-kaowen-biepa-jinye-kongxu-jimo-leng-wkgkk1unb1oxo9ngcvx-ivoggiw069|EP34]] asks why male ambition can be ennobled while female desire becomes warning, and reframes lunar departure through unpaid domestic labor and constrained divorce.
- **Festival reception:** [[ep34-zhongqiu-teji-linghun-kaowen-biepa-jinye-kongxu-jimo-leng-wkgkk1unb1oxo9ngcvx-ivoggiw069|EP34]] connects Chang'e's solitude with moon poetry, reunion, overseas family practice, and cultural memory.

## Qualifications

The source is a multi-guest cultural conversation whose participants openly doubt some versions and improvise others. It does not establish which Chang'e narrative is earliest, how variants moved among texts and rituals, or whether a particular political order deliberately created them. Reproductive-symbol, moral-education, marriage, and feminist readings remain interpretive and source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Created a profile that preserves incompatible motives rather than selecting one canonical Chang'e.
- Made gender, marriage, agency, punishment, and festival reception explicit interpretive layers.
