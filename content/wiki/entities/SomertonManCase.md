# Somerton Man Case

## Overview

The Somerton Man case is the investigation of an unidentified man found dead on Somerton Park Beach near Adelaide on December 1, 1948. The source says later hair-DNA and genealogical work identified him as [[CarlWebb|Carl “Charles” Webb]], but the cause and circumstances of his death remain unresolved.

## Current Profile

Witnesses saw the man against the seawall on November 30 and two jockeys found him dead the following morning. He carried tickets, a U.S.-made comb, gum, and cigarettes, but no identification; labels had been removed from the clothes he wore. Autopsy findings were treated as compatible with poison, though no poison was detected and no violence was established.

A suitcase at Adelaide station was linked through thread matching a trouser repair. A rolled fragment reading “Tammam Shad,” cut from the *Rubaiyat of Omar Khayyam*, was later found in a pocket. A matching book reportedly contained uncracked letter sequences and [[JessicaThompson]]'s phone number, helping turn the death into an espionage and doomed-romance story.

[[DerekAbbott]] pursued both a paternity hypothesis involving Jessica's son Robin and genetic identification. The episode says hair recovered from the death mask, combined with genealogical research by Colleen Fitzpatrick, identified [[CarlWebb]] and excluded Robin as his son. The identification makes the “T Keane” clothing markings less anomalous through Webb's brother-in-law Thomas Keane, but it does not explain Webb's death.

## Key Characteristics

- Unidentified-death investigation beginning at Somerton Park Beach in 1948.
- Case shaped by removed labels, uncertain toxicology, tickets, a suitcase, altered cigarettes, and a literary fragment.
- Historical mystery repeatedly interpreted through Cold War espionage and secret-romance narratives.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one narrative podcast published soon after the announced identification. The source does not reproduce laboratory data, the genealogical proof chain, police files, autopsy records, or an official finding, so [[CarlWebb]]'s identification is recorded as the episode's conclusion rather than independently verified here. Ballet inference from feet and leg muscles, poisoning, espionage, coded messages, horse-betting notation, and a relationship with [[JessicaThompson]] remain hypotheses.
