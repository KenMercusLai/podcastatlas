# Midea Group

Midea Group appears in [[vol-123-telangpu-de-duideng-guanshui-an-buzhi-shi-yi-chang-daxing-fucongxing-ceshi-lm6bkiqgyyefwompxon7lo6cnqp6]] through the episode's closing discussion of a shareholder letter. The host uses Midea as a practical business coda after the tariff and deglobalization analysis: companies have to survive a more volatile environment rather than assume the previous globalization logic still holds.

In the wiki, Midea Group links the macro tariff branch back to operating-company behavior. The source's point is not a company deep dive; it is that global growth pressure, geopolitical conflict, technology change, and industry competition make adaptation capacity a business necessity.

[[vol-127-nianbaoji-zhong-de-zhenshi-zhongguo-2025-lqjy2pnfy09zyewj0ljxjuc3gxqv]] makes Midea a fuller example of [[TechnologyInnovationAsScaleEconomy]]. The episode emphasizes Midea's global operating footprint, large workforce, many subsidiaries, R&D centers, manufacturing bases, and multi-country business as evidence that Chinese home-appliance leaders are becoming global platform companies rather than low-cost assemblers.
