# Crested Myna in Taiwan / 冠八哥

## Overview
[[CrestedMynaTaiwan]] is the myna species the Duanwen episode identifies as Taiwan's currently recognized native black myna and a protected-species reference point in public education.

## Current Profile
The episode says Taiwan ornithological consensus currently treats crested myna as native, while acknowledging that past debate existed because distribution boundaries, historical records, and possible Han-migrant introduction histories made the classification less self-evident.

Crested myna matters in the episode as the native counterpart to introduced mynas. It also shows why native/alien labels require historical and spatial boundaries: even a currently native-labeled species may have had earlier classification uncertainty.

## Key Characteristics
- Current Taiwan ornithological consensus treats it as native.
- Used in public education as the native contrast to common and white-vented mynas.
- Its past classification debate illustrates that species origin categories are not always simple.

## Evidence
- Current status: [[wailai-bage-yeying-zhibo-yu-taiwan-wailai-wuzhong-nanti-db02e7aa04c129b49b1f36f750155b42]] says crested myna is treated by Taiwan bird experts as native and as a protected species.
- Boundary debate: [[wailai-bage-yeying-zhibo-yu-taiwan-wailai-wuzhong-nanti-db02e7aa04c129b49b1f36f750155b42]] notes earlier discussion around distribution, history, and possible human introduction.
- Education role: [[wailai-bage-yeying-zhibo-yu-taiwan-wailai-wuzhong-nanti-db02e7aa04c129b49b1f36f750155b42]] places crested myna in common identification materials alongside introduced black mynas.
