# 应劭 / Ying Shao (Late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-971-luanshi-miyun-shui-sha-le-caocao-laodie-ljh3hdhssigmv78bueo-hprfqwku|Hanji 971]] introduces Ying Shao as the Taishan official whom [[CaoCao|曹操]] orders to receive and escort [[CaoSong|曹嵩]] from Langya toward [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]]. His role is procedural but consequential: he is responsible for the official reception channel that fails before [[CaoSongMurder|曹嵩遇害]].

After the killing, Ying Shao flees because he cannot answer to Cao Cao. The episode therefore uses him as a marker of command responsibility in a fractured late-Han landscape: even when an escort order is formally issued, route security, local force, and accountability can collapse.
