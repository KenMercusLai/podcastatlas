# Julia Gillard

## Overview

Julia Gillard was Australia's first woman prime minister, presented through her Welsh and Baptist background, minority government, rivalry with Kevin Rudd, and parliamentary confrontation with Tony Abbott over sexism and misogyny.

## Current Profile

Born in Wales and raised in [[Australia]], Gillard became a lawyer, Labor politician, deputy prime minister, and education minister before replacing [[KevinRudd|Kevin Rudd]]. She then governed through a hung parliament with Greens and independent support. The episode emphasizes the gendered hostility she faced, including attacks connected to being unmarried and childless, and treats her misogyny speech against [[TonyAbbott]] as the defining public answer. Rudd later returned and removed her before the election.

## Key Characteristics

- Welsh-born Australian lawyer and Labor politician.
- First woman to serve as Australian prime minister.
- Replaced Kevin Rudd after serving as his deputy.
- Governed a hung parliament through negotiated support.
- Faced sustained gendered and personal attacks.
- Delivered a widely remembered parliamentary speech on sexism and misogyny.
- Was later displaced by Rudd's return.
