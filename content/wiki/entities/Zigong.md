# 子贡 / Zigong / Duanmu Ci

子贡 / Zigong, also named 端木赐 / Duanmu Ci, enters this wiki branch through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-56-qiaoqiao-liangqian-duo-nianqian-sha-shi-da-zhangfu-ltokbutqgsnrno-y29xmzwa3da41]] as the contrast case in [[YangXiong|扬雄]]'s [[Fayan|《法言》]] critique of [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] and [[SuQin|苏秦]].

The episode says a questioner asks whether Zigong also traveled among rulers in a way that resembles zonghengjia persuasion. Yang Xiong's answer, as presented by the source, separates outward activity from inner motive: Zigong's purpose was to avoid disorder and save his own state, while Zhang Yi and Su Qin are judged as pursuing wealth and rank.

The host adds that Zigong was already wealthy, so the episode does not read his diplomacy as a money-seeking project. Zigong therefore supports [[MotiveBasedTalentEvaluation|动机导向的才干评价]]: the same diplomatic craft can be morally different when it serves rescue rather than self-advancement.
