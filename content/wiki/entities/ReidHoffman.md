## Overview
Reid Hoffman appears in the current wiki as a former [[OpenAI]] board member and as a subject of disputed file-based claims about the extent and framing of his relationship with [[JeffreyEpstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].

## Current Profile
The two sources illuminate different parts of Hoffman's public role. [[SamAltman|Sam Altman]] treats Hoffman's board departure as one factor that weakened continuity before the [[OpenAIBoardCrisis]]. In the All-In special, [[KevinBass|Kevin Bass]] argues that released Epstein records contradict Hoffman's public description of a few fundraising-related interactions ending in 2015. The episode's participants distinguish that alleged minimization from proof of sexual misconduct, which the source does not provide.

## Key Characteristics
- Hoffman is presented as an experienced technology investor and former OpenAI board member whose presence may have strengthened board continuity.
- His Epstein relationship is described publicly as limited and connected to MIT Media Lab fundraising.
- Bass alleges that released records show substantially more contact, meetings, financial activity, and travel than Hoffman's account conveyed.
- The current evidence set supports scrutiny of disclosure consistency but not an inference of sexual criminality from association alone.

## Qualifications
Bass's counts and interpretations are claims made in the episode; the supplied source does not reproduce the underlying records or an independent audit. The discussion also occurs in an openly partisan context involving Hoffman's criticism of political figures. Nothing in the current evidence inventory establishes sexual misconduct by Hoffman.
