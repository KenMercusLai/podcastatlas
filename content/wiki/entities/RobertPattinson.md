# Robert Pattinson

## Overview
Robert Pattinson is a British actor whom the source reports has been selected to play [[JamesBond]] in the 26th official Bond film.

## Current Profile
The episode treats Pattinson as a more established star than the franchise usually chooses, but sees his experience with heroic, morally ambiguous, and villainous roles as compatible with Bond's mixture of glamour, danger, and damage. His casting is presented as a cultural choice about how Britain represents itself, not only as a commercial film decision.

## Key Characteristics
- Established British film actor selected, according to the episode, as the next James Bond.
- Brings experience playing both heroic and antagonistic characters.
- Enters a role whose audience expectations combine continuity, reinvention, masculinity, and British identity.
- Becomes the public face of a franchise now described as under Amazon MGM creative control.
