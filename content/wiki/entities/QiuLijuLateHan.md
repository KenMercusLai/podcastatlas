# 丘力居 / Qiu Liju (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-938-lishi-de-jingxing-ruanruo-zhihui-dailai-quru-lnlnrketgfbadhfjbzdjjtrmqfsh|Hanji 938]] introduces Qiu Liju as the Wuhuan leader whose forces align with [[ZhangChunLateHan|张纯]] and [[ZhangJuLateHan|张举]] after the failed Wuhuan cavalry mobilization. In this origin layer, the problem begins with auxiliary desertion and resentment before later sources show Liu Yu's pacification.

[[QiuLijuLateHan|丘力居]] is the [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] leader who appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-942-yue-youxiu-de-ren-huode-yue-lei-heli-ma-lkwlrcgnt-kxxc2qqz6z6wkg7e5z|Hanji 942]] as both a military threat and a political actor open to inducement. He is associated with the late-Han raids around [[ZhangChunLateHan|张纯]] and helps turn [[YouzhouLateHan|幽州]] into a multi-front frontier crisis.

After [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] defeats Zhang Chun at Shimen, Gongsun Zan pursues too deeply and is surrounded by Qiu Liju at Guanzi City for more than two hundred days. Later, [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] changes the situation through envoys, incentives, and surrender signaling; Qiu Liju sends envoys to submit and the rebellion around him disperses.
