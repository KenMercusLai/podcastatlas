# Sudan

Sudan is the main conflict setting in [[outsider-chance-ending-sudans-proxy-war-6a75b3695d765781a2b1b132]]. The episode treats the civil war as both a humanitarian catastrophe and a [[ForeignBackedCivilWar]], where domestic armed camps are sustained by outside patrons interested in Sudan's [[RedSea]] coastline, gold, arable land, and future political alignment.

The source says the [[SudaneseArmedForces|SAF]] retook [[Khartoum]] and controls much of the north and east, while the [[RapidSupportForces|RSF]] still controls much of western Sudan, especially [[Darfur]]. That battlefield map does not amount to recovery: Khartoum remains devastated, millions are displaced, drone strikes continue, and [[ElObeid]] is presented as a decisive hotspot.

The episode's peace logic is ambivalent. Outside powers such as the [[UnitedStates]], [[Egypt]], [[SaudiArabia]], and the [[UnitedArabEmirates]] may be needed to push local partners toward a deal, but both the SAF and RSF are fragmented coalitions, making [[FragmentedCeasefireEnforcement]] a central obstacle.
