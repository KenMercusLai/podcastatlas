# Mitsubishi Estate / 三菱地所

Mitsubishi Estate appears in [[131-wo-zai-riben-mai-le-yi-tao-zizhu-fang-llofkgaj-hyajksrpkoylakuocxz]] as one of the large Japanese developers used to explain the shift from building property to operating urban districts. The episode points to Marunouchi-style renewal as evidence that mature real-estate markets reward long-term tenant mix, hotel, retail, food, greenery, and place-management capability.

The company matters here less as a financial security than as a city-operator example. In the source's frame, central land can remain valuable when developers maintain surrounding urban functions, while ordinary buildings themselves age and often behave more like depreciating consumption goods.
