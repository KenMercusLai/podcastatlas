# Recursion Pharma

## Overview
Recursion Pharma is an AI-assisted drug-discovery company used by two [[DataScienceWithSam]] sources to connect molecular prediction, experimental data, and end-to-end biological workflows.

## Current Profile
The 2021 Coffee Chat names Recursion as a commercial example of using AI to predict drugs and small molecules, while keeping heterogeneous biology, missing negative results, and experimental verification in view. EP32 adds an operating-system frame: Recursion treats drug discovery as an end-to-end data problem and is named among organizations associated with candidates moving toward mid- or late-stage testing.

The combined evidence supports a profile of Recursion as a data-centric platform example, not a verified claim about any particular drug. Neither source note supplies a candidate name, trial registration, endpoint, clinical result, or independent platform comparison.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses AI-driven drug and small-molecule prediction as its core role in the available corpus.
- Is framed in EP32 as an end-to-end biological data operating system rather than a single prediction model.
- Illustrates how machine-learning infrastructure thinking can be applied across a drug-discovery workflow.
- Remains bounded by experimental data quality, negative-result visibility, wet-lab verification, toxicity, and clinical outcomes.
