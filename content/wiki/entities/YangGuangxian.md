# 杨光先 / Yang Guangxian

Yang Guangxian enters the wiki through [[ep259-yu-miao-shifu-mantan-xu-guangqi-sibainian-qian-ruhe-rangren-biancongming-lkirczxnx8skbbfpmz5-m-mivs4e]] as the Qing critic who attacked [[JohannAdamSchallVonBell|汤若望]] and the foreign calendar network. The episode places him in the Kangxi-era controversy around burial dates, astronomical administration, and suspicion of Jesuit influence.

The source uses Yang as the hard case for [[ScienceValueTransmission|science value transmission]] and [[LateMingWesternLearning|晚明西学东渐]]. He is not presented only as ignorant; the episode notes that many intellectuals could see him as defending Chinese cultural purity. But his stance also shows how cultural-sovereignty arguments can block technical accuracy, destroy learning communities, and turn institutions against people trained in new methods.

Yang therefore functions as an anti-example to [[XuGuangqi|徐光启]]. Where Xu's story centers curiosity, translation, and disciplined learning, Yang's story centers closure, purity, and suspicion when foreignness and state legitimacy collide.
