# RP1 Robot

## Overview
RP1 Robot is the next productized humanoid robot platform discussed by [[HuangYiRobotics|Huang Yi]] for [[LuoboPaidui|萝博派对]] in [[22-sui-de-jushen-ceo-5-lun-rongzi-guo-yi-meiyuan-buzhi-tian-you-duo-gao-yinian-chile-shi-nian-de-ku-duitan-huangyi-luobo-paidui-chuangshiren-ceo-ljwdmocryiblf0k3rkz8eonkqox]]. In the source, it is the company's transition point from the more kit-like [[RPORobot|RPO]] product toward a platform that can support larger-scale research, education, and selected industrial exploration.

## Current Profile
The episode positions RP1 as a research-education-oriented humanoid platform planned for public launch around the fourth quarter after the source date. Compared with RPO, it adds productization work such as head degrees of freedom, integrated body architecture, internal wiring, and fixes for known customer pain points including夹手, shoulder overheating, wrist support, fragile arm-swinging parts, and packaging difficulty.

## Key Characteristics
- Productized successor: RP1 is treated as the next step after RPO rather than as a pure lab prototype.
- Research-education fit: the main target customers are labs, schools, and developers who need a usable humanoid body for experimentation.
- Pain-point-driven design: the team interviewed users and studied competitors to address practical reliability and usability issues.
- Platform co-design: customer requests include hand interfaces, EtherCAT, hard synchronization, head design, and egocentric-data support.
- Strategic milestone: Huang frames RP1 release and mass production as an A-round-level company milestone, with later rounds tied more to commercialization or business closure.
