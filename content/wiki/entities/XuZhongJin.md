# 许众 / Xu Zhong (Jin commentator)

许众 / Xu Zhong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-978-gushen-quantui-shuwan-dijun-ta-zenme-zuodao-de-lhgd8vsvuaf5oi9aefh0wxabmzwv|Hanji 978]] as the later commentator cited from "三国评" on [[JinYunLateHan|靳允]]'s Fan County decision. He argues that Jin Yun should not be praised without qualification because Jin Yun's family, especially his mother, had been seized by [[LyuBu|吕布]] and Jin Yun had no settled lord-subject bond with [[CaoCao|曹操]].

The page records Xu Zhong's argument as a source-scoped moral reading, not as a change to the event sequence. His criticism sharpens the [[LoyaltyCollateralDilemma|忠义牵连困境]] branch: holding [[FanCountyLateHan|范县]] may preserve Cao Cao's base, but it also forces a hierarchy between political alignment and kinship obligation.
