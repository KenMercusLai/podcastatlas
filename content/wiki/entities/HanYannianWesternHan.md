## Overview

韩延年 / Han Yannian is presented across two episodes as [[HanQianqiuWesternHan|韩千秋]]'s son and marquis of Cheng'an, then as a Western Han officer commanding a forward element in [[LiLingWesternHan|李陵]]'s isolated infantry force against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]]. The shared name and Cheng'an association make a continuous identity plausible, but the supplied episodes do not independently narrate the career bridge.

## Current Profile

Hanji 352-2 supplies the family and title layer. After Han Qianqiu's force is destroyed in [[Nanyue|南越]], [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] distinguishes failed outcome from vanguard courage and makes Han Qianqiu's son Han Yannian marquis of Cheng'an. In this episode, Han Yannian is the heir through whom the court turns casualty recognition into household status.

Hanji 377-2 supplies the later battlefield layer. Li Ling and Han Yannian each have eight hundred soldiers opening the route, distinguished by yellow and white flags. After [[GuanGanWesternHan|管敢]] defects, this arrangement becomes actionable enemy intelligence because Guan Gan advises Xiongnu elite cavalry to target the two commanders. When the Han force is trapped, out of arrows, and unable to sound its broken drums, Han Yannian mounts with Li Ling and a small group for a midnight breakout. Thousands of Xiongnu cavalry pursue them, and Han Yannian is killed.

## Key Characteristics

- Son of Han Qianqiu and recipient of the Cheng'an marquisate after the failed Nanyue expedition.
- Western Han officer serving in Li Ling's deep-frontier infantry force in the later episode.
- Forward-element commander associated with an eight-hundred-man formation and flag marker.
- Target exposed when Guan Gan reveals command and identification details to Xiongnu.
- Final breakout companion who dies under mounted pursuit.

## Qualifications

Both profiles come from short popular-history episodes. Hanji 352-2 establishes parentage and title but gives no later office history; Hanji 377-2 establishes the forward command and death but does not repeat his parentage or title. The wiki preserves one canonical identity because the evidence aligns in name, period, and Cheng'an context, while explicitly retaining the missing career bridge. The sources do not settle his exact rank progression, which flag belonged to him, or the detailed circumstances of his death.

## What Changed

- Added the earlier identity layer as Han Qianqiu's son and recipient of the Cheng'an marquisate.
- Connected heir reward after Nanyue to later frontline command while retaining the source gap in the continuous biography.
- Preserved the existing account of formation exposure and death during Li Ling's breakout.
