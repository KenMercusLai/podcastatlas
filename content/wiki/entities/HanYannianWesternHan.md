## Overview

韩延年 / Han Yannian is a Western Han officer in [[LiLingWesternHan|李陵]]'s isolated infantry force against [[Xiongnu|匈奴]]. Hanji 377-2 gives him a compact but consequential role: he commands one of the forward eight-hundred-man elements and dies beside Li Ling's attempted night breakout.

## Current Profile

The source first identifies Han Yannian through battlefield organization. Li Ling and Han Yannian each have eight hundred soldiers opening the route, distinguished by yellow and white flags. After [[GuanGanWesternHan|管敢]] defects, this arrangement becomes actionable enemy intelligence because Guan Gan advises Xiongnu elite cavalry to target the two commanders.

When the Han force is trapped, out of arrows, and unable to sound its broken drums, Han Yannian mounts with Li Ling and a small group for a midnight breakout. Thousands of Xiongnu cavalry pursue them, and Han Yannian is killed.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han officer serving in Li Ling's deep-frontier infantry force.
- Forward-element commander associated with an eight-hundred-man formation and flag marker.
- Target exposed when Guan Gan reveals command and identification details to Xiongnu.
- Final breakout companion who dies under mounted pursuit.
