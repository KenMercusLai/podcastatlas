# CITIC Capital / 中信资本

CITIC Capital / 中信资本 appears in [[145-gaijia-zhongzi-de-canyin-yangpinpai-feng-tou-quan-1-145-1]] as the controlling investor in [[McDonalds]] China after the 2017 transaction that made "Golden Arches" widely visible as the China company name. The source says CITIC held 52%, while [[CarlyleGroup]] and McDonald's global held minority stakes.

The episode treats CITIC's role as the earliest large proof point for [[ForeignRestaurantBrandLocalControl]]. McDonald's global later increased its China stake and Carlyle exited, but the source says CITIC still retained 52% control, making the case a template for later China-control deals around [[Starbucks]], [[BurgerKing]], and [[PizzaHutChina]].
