# Lisa Feldman Barrett

## Overview
Lisa Feldman Barrett is the psychologist and neuroscientist interviewed by [[AndrewHuberman]] about constructed emotion, affect, prediction, facial-expression inference, emotional granularity, and allostatic bodily regulation.

## Current Profile
In [[how-to-understand-emotions-dr-lisa-feldman-barrett-scim3919749252]], Barrett argues against fixed emotion packages with universal facial, physiological, or neural signatures. Her account instead connects [[TheoryOfConstructedEmotion]] to a predictive brain that categorizes sensory and bodily signals using prior experience, language, culture, and context, then prepares actions and constructs an emotional interpretation. Her practical contribution joins [[EmotionalGranularity]] to [[AllostaticBodyBudget]]: more precise concepts can widen action options, while sleep, nutrition, movement, substances, uncertainty, illness, trust, and relationships can change the affective substrate being interpreted.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats facial movements as context-dependent signals rather than universal direct readouts of emotion.
- Distinguishes low-dimensional affect from context-sensitive emotion categories.
- Explains the brain as predictive, action-oriented, and continuously engaged in bodily regulation.
- Connects emotional vocabulary and granularity to flexible action rather than perfect labeling.
- Prioritizes bodily foundations and social conditions before assuming every unpleasant feeling identifies an external problem.

## Evidence
- Constructed-emotion account - [[how-to-understand-emotions-dr-lisa-feldman-barrett-scim3919749252]] links emotion to prediction, categorization, past experience, bodily input, language, culture, and context.
- Face-reading boundary - [[how-to-understand-emotions-dr-lisa-feldman-barrett-scim3919749252]] reports Barrett's critique of universal facial-expression templates and her emphasis on interpretation.
- Affect and allostasis - [[how-to-understand-emotions-dr-lisa-feldman-barrett-scim3919749252]] distinguishes affect from emotion and uses body budget as a metaphor for predictive resource regulation.
- Practical regulation - [[how-to-understand-emotions-dr-lisa-feldman-barrett-scim3919749252]] connects sleep, food, exercise, attention, precise labels, uncertainty reframing, trust, and kindness to affective flexibility.

## Qualifications
This profile is based on one public podcast summary rather than Barrett's complete scholarly record. Cross-cultural expression findings, numerical estimates, depression claims, synchrony research, and treatment remarks remain source-scoped; the body-budget metaphor is not a standalone diagnostic or treatment protocol.
