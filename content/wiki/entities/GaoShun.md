# 高顺 / Gao Shun

高顺 / Gao Shun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1019-jiemi-chenminwang-liuchong-de-chuanqi-yisheng-ll2fma7wupp5gczudysfett5lhio|Hanji 1019]] as the loyal subordinate whom [[LyuBu|吕布]] does not properly value. The episode uses him as the positive side of Lü Bu's personnel failure: loyalty exists, but the leader fails to recognize and secure it.

The source contrasts Gao Shun with [[WeiXuLateHan|魏续]], whom Lü Bu trusts more and who later joins the action against Gao Shun. Gao Shun's page is source-scoped to that leadership lesson rather than a full military biography.
