# 高顺 / Gao Shun

高顺 / Gao Shun enters the wiki through the Hanji 1018-1019 late-Han branch as the loyal subordinate whom [[LyuBu|吕布]] does not properly value. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1018-ta-weihe-rang-caocao-jidan-sunce-weiju-liubei-wanxi-lqccdhrjmero-gt7vsaq2ejuhhw8|Hanji 1018]] gives the fuller version: Gao Shun is described as clean, respected, and disciplined, with a seven-hundred-plus soldier unit known as "陷阵营."

The source contrasts Gao Shun with [[WeiXuLateHan|魏续]] in practical command terms. Lü Bu gives Gao Shun's troops to Wei Xu, then still calls Gao Shun back when a hard assault is needed. Gao Shun does not complain, and he continues warning Lü Bu against errors such as the failed personal demand for supplies from [[ZhangBaLateHan|张霸]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1019-jiemi-chenminwang-liuchong-de-chuanqi-yisheng-ll2fma7wupp5gczudysfett5lhio|Hanji 1019]] keeps that personnel lesson active, using Gao Shun as the positive side of Lü Bu's judgment failure. The page remains source-scoped rather than a full military biography.
