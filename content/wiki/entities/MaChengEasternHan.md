## Overview

马成 / Ma Cheng is currently synthesized as an Eastern Han frontier commander promoted into a corrective border-defense role after [[DuMaoEasternHan|Du Mao]]'s removal. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-717-13-ge-zi-yinfa-de-diwang-zhinu-liz3jsbvw3snfapzsrqw917fqv7v|Hanji 717]] identifies him as Yangwu general and emphasizes his strengthening of northern defenses with beacon stations roughly every ten li.

## Current Profile

Ma Cheng's current profile is narrow but useful. He appears in a personnel-correction sequence: Du Mao is removed because a subordinate commander kills someone, Ma Cheng replaces him, and border defense is made more systematic through beacon infrastructure.

The source presents beacon stations as a practical frontier-warning system using smoke and fire. Ma Cheng therefore belongs to the administrative-military side of Guangwu-era frontier stabilization, where leadership replacement, warning infrastructure, and later Zhang Kan's Gaoliu/Yuyang branch all reinforce each other.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han Yangwu general placed into frontier command after Du Mao's removal.
- Border-defense organizer associated with beacon stations at regular intervals.
- Military-administrative actor in the same 39 CE frontier branch as Lu Fang's Gaoliu return and Zhang Kan's later Yuyang tenure.
- Evidence point for the court correcting frontier personnel and infrastructure after command failure.
