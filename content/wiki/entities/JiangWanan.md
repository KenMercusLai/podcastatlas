# 蒋万安 / Chiang Wan-an

## Overview
[[JiangWanan|蒋万安]] is the incumbent Taipei mayor and [[Kuomintang|国民党]] politician whose unexpectedly close reelection contest is analyzed by [[b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26-b6c8b008370523ce9ee300c46a29db26mp3aid6af3219e1f5126ac85b049f0chid66b07190af99592]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Chiang as a durable winner with unusually strong entry advantages but an unresolved personal-vote ceiling. His Chiang-family identity, [[JiangXiaoyan|蒋孝严]]'s constituency network, early media attention, and support from multiple party systems helped turn a novice candidacy into legislative and internal-party success. Yet the episode argues that divided opposition in his first legislative race and a three-way 2022 mayoral contest make those wins weak tests of [[CrossCampElectoralAppeal]].

His current campaign is portrayed as strategically reactive. Attempts to define [[ShenBoyang|沈伯洋]] through recall polarization and drug policy did not clearly broaden Chiang's support; the drug attack instead required him to reconcile absolute anti-drug language with existing harm-reduction policy.

## Key Characteristics
- Benefits from high inherited name recognition and political-network access.
- Has repeatedly won office but has not, in this source, demonstrated a vote beyond the party baseline.
- Entered the current contest as an incumbent without a major scandal yet remained in a close race.
- Uses opponent-definition attacks centered on social division and drug policy.
- Faces a turnout contest over voters previously aligned with [[HuangShanshan|黄珊珊]].
