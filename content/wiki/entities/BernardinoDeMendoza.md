# Bernardino de Mendoza

Bernardino de Mendoza appears in [[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] as [[PhilipII|Philip II]]'s ambassador in [[England]] and a hard-edged diplomatic voice for [[Spain]]. After [[FrancisDrake]]'s return, Mendoza demanded action against Drake and warned [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]] that war was easier to begin than to end.

The episode then places Mendoza inside the [[ThrockmortonPlot]] network. He supported Catholic invasion planning from London, helped connect Spanish backing to [[HenryDukeOfGuise|Guise]]'s project, and became one of the figures named in [[FrancisThrockmorton]]'s tortured confession.

On 19 January 1584, [[FrancisWalsingham]] presented charges of conspiracy against Mendoza before the Privy Council. Mendoza denied them, but his expulsion broke normal Anglo-Spanish diplomacy and pushed the crisis toward [[ConfessionalWarEscalation]] and the [[SpanishArmada]].
