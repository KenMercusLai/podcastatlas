# 见手青 / Jianshouqing

## Overview
见手青 / Jianshouqing is a broad food-and-risk label for blue-staining boletes discussed in two Yunnan-facing podcast sources. The wiki treats it as a culturally valued but medically risky mushroom group, not as a single-species identification category or a cooking guide.

## Current Profile
[[150-chi-jun-he-acuo-liao-yunnan-de-jizong-songrong-jianshouqing-ca-koushui-897563165|episode 150]] places 见手青 inside [[YunnanWildMushroomCulture]]: it can be desirable food, yet local knowledge includes refusal, careful sourcing, preparation boundaries, and severe poisoning stories. [[vol-124-gu-du-huan-zhe-bing-bu-gu-du-zhong-xi-xin-li-7-zhen-shi-9-yisheng-yi-yi-fenxi-lnqswz3nim_8767094yiysgmef1g|VOL.124]] adds a clinical case in which a few bites preceded gastrointestinal, sensory, and visual symptoms, followed by prolonged uncertainty and multidisciplinary follow-up.

Together the sources reject the internet shorthand that 见手青 poisoning is mainly an amusing hallucination. Outcomes may vary within one meal, apparent improvement may be misleading, and persistent visual or systemic symptoms require qualified assessment rather than folk certainty.

## Key Characteristics
- Blue-staining bolete group embedded in Yunnan food culture rather than one safely identifiable species.
- Valued for eating in some local contexts while carrying real poisoning risk.
- Associated with variable gastrointestinal, neurologic, perceptual, and possibly systemic presentations.
- Often trivialized through “seeing little people” jokes that obscure organ injury, prolonged symptoms, and death risk.
- Unsuitable for field-identification, preparation, reheating, or self-treatment instructions on this wiki.

## Evidence
- Cultural value and local caution - [[150-chi-jun-he-acuo-liao-yunnan-de-jizong-songrong-jianshouqing-ca-koushui-897563165]] presents 见手青 as both desirable food and a reason for strict household refusal and preparation boundaries.
- Variable poisoning experience - [[vol-124-gu-du-huan-zhe-bing-bu-gu-du-zhong-xi-xin-li-7-zhen-shi-9-yisheng-yi-yi-fenxi-lnqswz3nim_8767094yiysgmef1g]] describes different symptoms and severity among people sharing a meal, including illness after a small reported portion.
- Clinical uncertainty and follow-up - [[vol-124-gu-du-huan-zhe-bing-bu-gu-du-zhong-xi-xin-li-7-zhen-shi-9-yisheng-yi-yi-fenxi-lnqswz3nim_8767094yiysgmef1g]] records persistent blurred vision, specialist referral, abnormal visual testing, and no definitive final causal diagnosis.
- Anti-romanticization boundary - both [[150-chi-jun-he-acuo-liao-yunnan-de-jizong-songrong-jianshouqing-ca-koushui-897563165]] and [[vol-124-gu-du-huan-zhe-bing-bu-gu-du-zhong-xi-xin-li-7-zhen-shi-9-yisheng-yi-yi-fenxi-lnqswz3nim_8767094yiysgmef1g]] oppose treating mushroom poisoning as harmless entertainment.

## Qualifications
“见手青” is a vernacular group label and does not identify a precise specimen, toxin, or risk level. The case source does not prove that every later symptom was caused by the meal, and its clinicians express uncertainty about the visual mechanism and final diagnosis. Restaurant procedures and local expertise can reduce some process failures but do not make uncertain wild-mushroom consumption safe.
