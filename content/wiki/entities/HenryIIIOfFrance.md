# Henry III of France

Henry III of France appears in [[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] as the childless French king whose succession problem destabilizes [[France]] during [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]]'s crisis with [[Spain]]. The episode frames him as one of the three Henrys alongside [[HenryOfNavarre]] and [[HenryDukeOfGuise|Henry, Duke of Guise]].

Henry's younger brother [[FrancoisDukeOfAnjou|Francois, Duke of Anjou]] is useful to Elizabeth because he offers a French diplomatic option and a potential non-Spanish solution in the [[LowCountries|Low Countries]]. When Anjou dies in 1584, Henry III is left without a direct heir.

That succession gap makes the Protestant Henry of Navarre next in line and helps push France into the [[WarOfTheThreeHenrys]]. For England, the result is that France becomes a less reliable counterweight to [[PhilipII|Philip II]] just as the [[ThrockmortonPlot]] and Anglo-Spanish break sharpen.
