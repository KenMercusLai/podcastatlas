# Max Demian / 德米安

[[MaxDemian|Max Demian / 德米安]] is the mysterious schoolmate and mentor figure in [[DemianNovel|《德米安》]] as read by [[20-demian-gaiyin-de-etou-shang-na-shenmi-de-yinji-567302734]]. The episode presents him as unusually mature, observant, and intellectually dangerous: he challenges classroom orthodoxy, offers [[CainMarkReinterpretation|a new reading of Cain's mark]], sees through [[EmilSinclair|辛克莱尔]]'s fear, and later becomes part of Sinclair's inner world.

The source treats Demian as deliberately ambiguous. He can be a real friend who intervenes against [[FranzKromer|克罗默]], a spiritual guide who points Sinclair toward [[Abraxas|Abraxas]], or a [[SpiritualMentorAsSelfProjection|self-projection]] that externalizes Sinclair's own courage and forbidden knowledge.

## Key Claims
- Demian's role is to make inherited moral interpretations feel unstable.
- He rescues Sinclair once, but the novel's ending requires Sinclair to live without him as an external guide.
- The character's power comes from observation, attention, and symbolic authority rather than conventional school achievement.
