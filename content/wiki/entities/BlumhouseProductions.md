# Blumhouse Productions

## Overview

Blumhouse Productions is a horror-focused film company used by the episode to illustrate a low-budget, portfolio-based production model.

## Current Profile

The hosts describe Blumhouse as controlling downside through strict budgets while preserving enough creative freedom for occasional breakout successes. The model does not require every film to be excellent or profitable; a small number of hits can support a larger slate and make horror a viable entry point for filmmakers.

The episode also qualifies company-label assumptions around [[ObsessionCurryBarkerFilm|《痴迷》]]. It says Blumhouse did not originate or initially finance the film and joined later primarily for marketing, so brand presence should not be mistaken for one uniform development, risk, or ownership model.

## Key Characteristics

- Horror-centered company associated with tightly controlled production budgets.
- Portfolio logic accepts uneven outcomes in exchange for breakout upside.
- Low financial exposure can coexist with greater creator latitude.
- Brand participation can occur late and need not imply original development or financing.
