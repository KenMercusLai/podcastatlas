# David Stirling

## Overview
David Stirling appears as the founder of the SAS and organizer of GB75, a proposed volunteer body for maintaining essential services during a general strike.

## Current Profile
[[419-britain-in-1974-countdown-to-a-coup-part-3-glt2033018212]] places Stirling inside the gray zone between civil-emergency preparation and coup fear. He described GB75 as "apprehensive patriots" ready to keep services operating; once the plan leaked to the press, he reduced its public profile. The episode treats the organization as one reason Wilson's fears felt plausible, not as proof that Stirling had assembled an operational coup.

## Key Characteristics
- Military veteran and founder of the SAS.
- Organizer of GB75 around continuity of essential services.
- Used patriotic and emergency language rather than an explicit coup program.
- Became part of Wilson-era conspiracy fear after press exposure.

## Evidence
- GB75 purpose: [[419-britain-in-1974-countdown-to-a-coup-part-3-glt2033018212]] says Stirling proposed volunteers to maintain essential services during a general strike.
- Political effect: [[419-britain-in-1974-countdown-to-a-coup-part-3-glt2033018212]] links the leaked plan to Wilson's fear that ex-servicemen might serve as shock troops.

## Qualifications
The source does not establish that GB75 planned to depose the government, possessed serving-military support, or could seize state institutions. Its civil-continuity description and its place in coup anxiety must both remain visible.

## What Changed
- Created a qualified profile separating GB75's stated continuity purpose from the coup fears it generated.
