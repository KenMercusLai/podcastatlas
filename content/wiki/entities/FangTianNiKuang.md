# 方天 / Fang Tian (Ni Kuang)

## Overview

方天 is the blue-blooded Saturn scientist at the center of [[BlueBloodManNiKuangNovel|《蓝血人》]]. Stranded on Earth for roughly two centuries, he appears first as a dangerous and secretive former classmate of [[WesleyNiKuang|卫斯理]], then as a displaced person trying to recover the navigation technology needed to return home.

## Current Profile

Fang Tian is most coherent as a figure of threatened exposure and failed belonging. His fear that humans will discover, capture, or dissect an outsider makes his secrecy psychologically credible, while his scientific role and long displacement give the navigation-device search a practical goal. Once he trusts Wesley, the narrative recasts him from mystery object and antagonist into collaborator.

That sympathy remains morally qualified. He uses mental influence, causes serious physical harm, and reportedly dismisses the loss of several human years because human lives are short by his standards. Difference in lifespan can explain his perspective without justifying coercion or killing.

His homecoming completes the character's tragic function. He reaches Saturn only to find that secret weapons research and environmental contamination have destroyed the civilization preserved in his memory. The question “where is my home?” therefore concerns continuity of community rather than mere arrival at a place.

## Key Characteristics

- Saturn scientist stranded on Earth for approximately two centuries.
- Conceals blue blood, nonhuman origins, and advanced technical capability.
- Uses mind-affecting and injuring technology to protect his identity.
- Needs a lost navigation device to correct the route back to Saturn.
- Changes from threatening unknown to Wesley's collaborator.
- Treats human longevity and moral worth in a way the hosts reject.
- Embodies homesickness after the remembered home has ceased to exist socially.
