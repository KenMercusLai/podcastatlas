# 右北平 / Youbeiping Commandery

右北平 / Youbeiping Commandery enters through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-972-sanguo-liuyu-zhisi-taisha-taitianzhen-lvawrcandbh628rckukosgarh7v2|Hanji 972]] as the home commandery associated with [[TianChou|田筹]]. The episode introduces Tian Chou as a young local talent from this frontier setting before [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] sends him toward the court.

In this source, Youbeiping is mainly a background node for frontier credibility. Tian Chou's later movement through [[JuyongPass|居庸关]] and his retreat from [[WuzhongCountyLateHan|无终县]] into [[XuWuMountain|徐无山]] make his authority local and border-facing rather than only court-derived.

## Connections
- [[TianChou|田筹]] - local figure tied to the commandery.

- [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] - frontier group later connected to Tian Chou's mountain order.
