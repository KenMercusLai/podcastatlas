## Overview

申屠刚 / Shentu Gang is currently the Wei Xiao-side adviser who urges [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|隗嚣]] to submit to [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-698-wangchao-xingshuai-qishilu-weiao-de-yihan-yu-jinchao-zhi-jing-lqim2hbjtdzb0wwmsaxcyzj7u8ff|Hanji 698]]. His source role is to name the opposite judgment from [[WangYuanWeiXiaoRetainer|王元]]: the field has already turned toward Liu Xiu, so continued fear and desire will only damage Wei Xiao's loyalty and reputation.

## Current Profile

Shentu Gang's advice is framed through mandate and public alignment. He says that when human support gathers, Heaven grants rule, and when human support departs, Heaven removes it. In the source's reading, Liu Xiu's repeated edicts and promises show a ruler offering trust and shared wealth rather than immediate destruction.

The remonstrance asks Wei Xiao to identify the real objects of his fear and desire. That wording matters because it makes continued hesitation look less like prudent statecraft and more like a failure to read the changed political field.

Wei Xiao does not accept the advice. Shentu Gang therefore functions as an ignored internal warning before the later rebellion sequence rather than as evidence that Wei Xiao lacked good counsel.

## Key Characteristics

- Wei Xiao-side adviser whose current source role is pro-submission remonstrance.
- Uses mandate and human-support language to describe Liu Xiu's advantage.
- Treats Liu Xiu's repeated edicts as trust signals rather than traps.
- Challenges Wei Xiao's fear and desire as the motives behind continued hesitation.
- Negative contrast to Wang Yuan's terrain-backed holdout strategy.

## Qualifications

This page is source-bounded to Hanji 698. It does not reconstruct Shentu Gang's full office history, later career, or exact wording beyond the episode's summary. The source supports his role as an internal remonstrant, not as proof that submission carried no danger.

## What Changed

- Created a canonical page for Shentu Gang because Hanji 698 makes his ignored submission warning central to Wei Xiao's failed alignment choice.
