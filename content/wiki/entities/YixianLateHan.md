# 易县 / Yi County (Late Han)

易县 / Yi County (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] as the place [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] chooses after hearing a children's song about a refuge near the Yan-Zhao border. He interprets the song as pointing to Yi County and moves his headquarters there.

The episode's Yi County matters because it becomes the setting for [[YijingFortress|易京]], Gongsun Zan's deep-trenched and towered stronghold. The move represents a strategic and psychological retreat from campaigning into defensive enclosure.
