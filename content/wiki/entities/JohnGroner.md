# John Groner

## Overview
John Groner is the [[BexleyNaturalMarket]] board member and caller in [[advice-line-with-ben-goodwin-of-olipop-f9d3fe17-eb2c-440d-b223-990fed970727]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Groner as a retired physician turned co-op board operator trying to help a financially stressed community grocery store recover. His initial e-commerce solution becomes the setup for advice that the co-op should lean harder into in-store curation and community experience.

## Key Characteristics
- Retired in 2024 as a pediatric trauma surgeon.
- Joined the board of [[BexleyNaturalMarket]] when the store was facing financial trouble.
- Built a GraceCart website to list inventory, allow ordering, and accept payment.
- Sought advice after the website failed to create meaningful traction.
- Frames the store as a natural, organic, local-vendor cooperative with deep community history.
