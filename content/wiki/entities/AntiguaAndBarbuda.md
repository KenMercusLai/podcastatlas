# Antigua and Barbuda

[[AntiguaAndBarbuda]] is the state context for the Barbuda land dispute in [[sand-heists-and-property-rights-in-the-caribbean-summer-school]]. The episode focuses on [[Barbuda]] rather than Antigua, but the national government matters because [[GastonBrowne]] uses post-[[HurricaneIrma]] recovery to push individual title and development access.

In the source, Antigua and Barbuda is also a small-island political economy case: recovery money, bank collateral, resort development, environmental review, and national constitutional arguments all intersect with [[SmallOpenEconomyVulnerability]] and [[TourismDependentSmallEconomy]].
