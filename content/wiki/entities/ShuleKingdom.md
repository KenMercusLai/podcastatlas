# 疏勒 / Shule Kingdom

疏勒 / Shule Kingdom enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-897-zhichang-bidu-shenme-cai-jiao-lingdaoli-lvrrmksykazlsb7w8abbdmnqfzqq|Hanji 897]] as the Western Regions target whose defense exposes [[MengTuoLateHan|孟陀]]'s command limits. The episode says Meng Tuo sends a force built from Dunhuang troops, Han frontier offices, and allied Western Regions kingdoms against Shule, but after more than forty days the army cannot take Zhenzhong city and withdraws.

The page is source-scoped to this military and governance test. Shule matters here less as a full regional history than as the case where an office obtained through [[ManufacturedAccessBrokerage|假势通道套利]] fails against operational reality.
