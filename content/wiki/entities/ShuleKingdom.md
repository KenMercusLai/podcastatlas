## Overview
Shule Kingdom is a Western Regions polity whose wiki profile now has both an early succession-and-alignment layer under Northern Xiongnu pressure and a later military-resistance layer against Meng Tuo's failed attack.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-790-lishi-gaosu-ni-ruhe-he-lingdao-da-jiaodao-lulf0r1sdkod3luzwru0-kkrijxk|Hanji 790]] shows Shule as an active regional actor before Han's renewed Western Regions debate. After King Anguo dies without a son, local actors first install Yifu, the son of Anguo's maternal uncle. Later, because the nearby Yuezhi/Yueshi-associated state is close to Chen Pan, Chen Pan is installed as king. The episode also says Shache defects from [[KhotanKingdomLateHan|Khotan]] to Shule, making Shule strong enough to balance Qiuci and Khotan.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-897-zhichang-bidu-shenme-cai-jiao-lingdaoli-lvrrmksykazlsb7w8abbdmnqfzqq|Hanji 897]] then presents Shule as the Western Regions target whose defense exposes [[MengTuoLateHan|Meng Tuo]]'s command limits. Meng Tuo sends a force built from Dunhuang troops, Han frontier offices, and allied Western Regions kingdoms against Shule, but after more than forty days the army cannot take Zhenzhong city and withdraws.

## Key Characteristics
- Western Regions kingdom whose succession politics can reshape regional alignment.
- Regional balancer after Shache moves away from Khotan and toward Shule.
- Actor inside the wider field created by Northern Xiongnu suzerainty and Han's uncertain re-entry.
- Later military target whose fortified resistance exposes the difference between borrowed office and operational competence.

## Qualifications
The page remains source-scoped to two episodes rather than a complete Shule history. The minor succession figures Yifu and Chen Pan and the Yuezhi/Yueshi-associated state are preserved as Hanji 790 data points without inferring a broader genealogy. The page also does not merge this Shule material into Khotan or Qiuci pages beyond the explicit alignment relationships.
