## Overview

Shule Kingdom is a Western Regions polity whose current wiki profile now begins with the 76-77 CE crisis when [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]]'s recall makes local dependency and internal fracture visible. Later layers show Ban Chao's intervention after [[WangZhongShuleKing|Wang Zhong]]'s defection, succession and regional balancing under Northern Xiongnu pressure, and eventual military resistance against [[MengTuoLateHan|Meng Tuo]]'s failed attack.

## Current Profile

Hanji 747 supplies the earliest current layer. When the court recalls Ban Chao and abolishes Western Regions offices, Shule is frightened enough that its Han-side duwei Li kills himself. After Ban Chao leaves, two Shule cities defect toward [[QiuciKingdom|Qiuci]] and Wutou. Ban Chao returns to Shule, kills the rebels, defeats Wutou, and restores stability, making Shule the first clear theater for [[MissionDrivenFrontierResponsibility|mission-driven frontier responsibility]].

Hanji 754 then shows Shule as a recoverable but internally divisible local base. Ban Chao raises Shule troops for a campaign against Shache, but Shache bribes the Shule king Wang Zhong, who defects and holds Wujicheng. Ban Chao installs a Shule deputy as king, attacks Wang Zhong with Shule troops that had not defected, and has Kangju seize and return Wang Zhong before Wujicheng surrenders.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-790-lishi-gaosu-ni-ruhe-he-lingdao-da-jiaodao-lulf0r1sdkod3luzwru0-kkrijxk|Hanji 790]] later shows Shule as an active regional actor before Han's renewed Western Regions debate. After King Anguo dies without a son, local actors first install Yifu, the son of Anguo's maternal uncle. Later, because the nearby Yuezhi/Yueshi-associated state is close to Chen Pan, Chen Pan is installed as king. The episode also says Shache defects from [[KhotanKingdomLateHan|Khotan]] to Shule, making Shule strong enough to balance Qiuci and Khotan.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-897-zhichang-bidu-shenme-cai-jiao-lingdaoli-lvrrmksykazlsb7w8abbdmnqfzqq|Hanji 897]] then presents Shule as the Western Regions target whose defense exposes Meng Tuo's command limits. Meng Tuo sends a force built from Dunhuang troops, Han frontier offices, and allied Western Regions kingdoms against Shule, but after more than forty days the army cannot take Zhenzhong city and withdraws.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Regions kingdom whose security can depend directly on Han envoy presence.
- Polity whose cities can defect under external pressure and weak central protection.
- Local base where Ban Chao uses return, punishment, replacement kingship, and loyal local troops to recover control.
- Kingdom whose succession politics can reshape regional alignment.
- Regional balancer after Shache later moves away from Khotan and toward Shule.
- Actor inside the wider field created by Northern Xiongnu suzerainty and Han's uncertain re-entry.
- Later military target whose fortified resistance exposes the difference between borrowed office and operational competence.

## Qualifications

The page remains source-scoped rather than a complete Shule history. Hanji 747 does not promote duwei Li, Wutou, or the two defecting cities into standalone pages. The Hanji 754 Wujicheng, Kangju, and Shache details are kept source-scoped; the minor Hanji 790 succession figures Yifu and Chen Pan are preserved without inferring a broader genealogy. The page also does not merge Shule into Khotan or Qiuci pages beyond explicit alignment relationships.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 747 as the earliest current layer: Ban Chao's recall, Shule panic, city defections, return, and stabilization.
- Reframed Shule as a dependent and internally divisible frontier base before the later Wang Zhong branch.
- Preserved Hanji 754's replacement-kingship layer, Hanji 790's succession layer, and Hanji 897's operational test against Meng Tuo.
