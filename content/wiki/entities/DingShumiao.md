# Ding Shumiao / 丁书苗

Ding Shumiao is the Shanxi businessperson in [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]] used to show the contracting and brokerage side of the high-speed-rail boom. The episode says she entered high-speed-rail-related businesses through relationships around [[LiuZhijun]], then built access to rail projects, parts, media, and sound-barrier work.

Her wiki role is not technical. She is a corruption and intermediation node: the episode uses her case to argue that [[StrongmanInfrastructureGovernance]] can move fast partly because decision rights are concentrated, but that the same concentration creates opportunities for brokers to extract fees from public infrastructure procurement.
