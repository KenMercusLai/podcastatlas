## Overview

糜芳 / Mi Fang is the Jiangling-side commander in the current 219 CE evidence whose supply failure, accumulated grievance against [[GuanYu|关羽]], fear of punishment, and isolation after [[FuShirenLateHan|傅士仁]]'s surrender culminate in opening [[JianglingLateHan|江陵]] to [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]].

## Current Profile

The bounded source places Mi Fang behind Guan Yu's [[FanchengBattle219LateHan|Fancheng]] front. Guan Yu's capture of Yu Jin's large force increases provisioning pressure, Mi Fang and Fu Shiren fail to supply enough materiel, and Guan Yu threatens to punish them after returning. The episode layers that immediate fear onto an older claim that Guan Yu has repeatedly treated both men with contempt.

Lyu Meng then brings the already surrendered Fu Shiren to Jiangling. Mi Fang opens the city rather than attempt an isolated defense. This makes him part of a command-cohesion failure, but not a one-cause morality tale: interpersonal disrespect, logistical strain, surprise, enemy coercion, and self-preservation converge.

## Key Characteristics

- Rear-area commander tied to Jiangling during Guan Yu's northern campaign.
- Faces exceptional provisioning pressure after the capture of Yu Jin's force.
- Is reportedly alienated by Guan Yu's repeated contempt and threatened punishment.
- Surrenders after Fu Shiren has already yielded and Lyu Meng reaches the city.
- Illustrates how rear-base security depends on subordinate cohesion as well as fortification.
