# 冯燕飞 / Feng Yanfei (Vascular Surgery)

## Overview
冯燕飞 / Feng Yanfei is a vascular-surgery doctor named in the VOL.204 [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode on [[EconomyClassSyndromeTravelThrombosis|economy-class syndrome]], thrombosis risk factors, ordinary prevention, and layperson triage.

## Current Profile
In this source, Feng's role is to make vascular-risk categories concrete for ordinary travelers. She names higher-risk contexts such as pregnancy, tumors, thrombophilia, surgery, older age, hormonal medication, and prior risk factors, then returns the advice to daily prevention: drink a reasonable amount of water, interrupt sitting, move briefly when possible, and use ankle-pump movement when standing up is not practical.

Her triage contribution is comparative and practical. She helps distinguish sudden unilateral swelling and pain from bilateral dependent swelling that may improve after rest, while still keeping the final judgment inside clinical assessment when thrombosis is plausible.

## Key Characteristics
- Names common high-risk contexts for venous thrombosis, including pregnancy, tumors, surgery, thrombophilia, hormones, older age, and prior clot history.
- Explains ordinary [[TravelThrombosisPrevention|travel thrombosis prevention]] through hydration, movement breaks, and ankle-pump exercise.
- Helps distinguish unilateral concerning swelling from more symmetric, rest-improving dependent swelling.
- Reinforces the need for professional evaluation when symptoms suggest thrombosis.
