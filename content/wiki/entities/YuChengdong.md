# Yu Chengdong

Yu Chengdong is the Huawei executive through whom [[no-210-zhongguo-shouji-jianghu-xia-cong-meizu-xiaomi-chuizi-dao-ov-huawei-de-xin-shinian-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-24-1000932027]] explains [[Huawei]]'s smartphone turn. The episode says Yu took over Huawei phones in 2011, cut many operator-label and low-value models, and pushed the company toward self-owned brands, high-end devices, and consumer-facing competition.

In the source, Yu's importance is organizational: he represents the decision to stop treating phones mainly as carrier customization and to build product identity, flagship confidence, offline channels, and chip-backed differentiation around P-series, Mate-series, and Kirin.

[[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] extends Yu's role from phones into cars. The episode presents him as the executive who believed intelligent EVs could absorb Huawei's missing consumer-terminal energy after smartphone supply restrictions, and who pushed high targets, product definition, brand launches, channel use, and delivery pressure around [[AITO|问界]] and [[HongmengZhixing|鸿蒙智行]].
