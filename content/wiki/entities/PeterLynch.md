# Peter Lynch

Peter Lynch appears in [[ep88-chuanyue-lianghua-zhifu-ximengsi-ai-hui-rang-putongren-geng-rongyi-zhuanqian-haishi-geng-nan-lhvigzza2ugmayezkbrxufkmp4l1]] as a comparison point for [[JimSimons]]. The episode uses Lynch to represent a more retail-intuitive style of investing in understandable businesses, then contrasts that with Simons's attempt to remove subjective business stories from trading decisions.

[[134-touzi-dashi-xilie-xiandao-pian-tamen-bu-zhi-zhuan-le-henduo-qian-geng-chuangzao-le-lijie-shijie-de-fangfa-lqkjpfqc0ymgvnboek5jmjycwtyg]] adds the key caveat around Lynch's accessibility. [[Yubai|雨白]] recommends his episode precisely because "buy what you know" can look easy, while [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] stresses the hidden research intensity, company visits, executive conversations, and work rhythm behind the simple retail-facing image.

[[143-ruhe-panduan-yiduan-hangqing-shi-huitiao-haishi-jieshu-sanjidu-touzi-zhang-fupan-lnmkuiw9mfdi5tqojzi07vnaorqz]] uses Lynch's stock-category habit as a practical tool inside [[MarketPullbackVsTrendEnd]]. The source does not add a Lynch biography; it uses the growth, slow-growth/value, and turnaround distinction to ask whether a falling stock's original investment reason has actually been damaged.
