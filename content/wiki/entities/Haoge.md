# 浩哥 / Haoge

[[160-ruhe-yingdui-zhongguo-zichan-niushi-de-tiaozhengqi-xinshu-fenxianghui-chengdu-chang-shilu-lm8degdbgmgi6nnwspjy-gdzwqfz]] adds HaoGe as the source's industry-selection and stock-picker counterpart to [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]]'s allocation frame. He argues that whether a bull market exists for a given investor depends partly on choosing the right industries and avoiding weaker choices when better opportunities are visible; his examples include comparing simple high-dividend oil-style opportunities with more complex AI, optical-module, real-estate, and consumer-sector decisions.

浩哥 is the [[SanDianXiaban|三点下班]] speaker in [[vol-104-putongren-ganggu-wanquan-shengcun-zhinan-chuantai-sandian-xiaban-lmzivgi6d3guv7wajyjw9cv9zz90]]. His contribution is a retail-investor and long-running Hong Kong stock participant's view: early wins in Alibaba B2B and Meiji Mask, a ten-bagger property-stock experience, later losses in illiquid small caps, and a strong emphasis on cutting mistakes quickly.
