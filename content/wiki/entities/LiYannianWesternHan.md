## Overview

李延年 / Li Yannian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-372-4-xihan-huangdi-duo-shi-shuangxinglian-de-yuanyin-lqinxoxbu6dhvmdiiowocjaahkwr|Hanji 372-4]] as a Western Han musician and performer whose talent, palace access, family connection to [[LadyLiWesternHan|李夫人]], and favor from [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] produce a sharp rise in status.

## Current Profile

The episode places Li Yannian in an entertainer family and credits him with music, dance, and the “倾国倾城” song associated with his sister's presentation to Han Wudi. It says he earlier underwent castration after an offense, entered palace service, and then gained recognition through performance before Lady Li's favor further strengthened his position.

Li Yannian becomes xielü duwei with a two-thousand-shi salary in the episode's account. The phrase “与上卧起” and a comparison with [[HanYanWesternHan|韩嫣]] establish unusual personal proximity, but the podcast goes further by reading that access as a sexual relationship and direct replacement of Han Yan. This page preserves the access-and-patronage claim while treating the modern lover label as interpretive.

## Key Characteristics

- Musician and performer from an entertainer family.
- Palace servant whose artistic skill becomes a route to imperial recognition.
- Brother of Lady Li, whose favor reinforces his own court position.
- Xielü duwei and high-salary beneficiary in the episode's account.
- Intimate favorite whose “与上卧起” access is stronger evidence of proximity than of a settled modern identity label.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one popular-history episode and does not reconstruct Li Yannian's complete musical career, punishment, offices, family prosecution, or the textual history of “倾国倾城.” Shared daily life and favorite status do not alone establish a modern sexual orientation, and the claim that he simply replaced Han Yan as a lover remains the host's interpretation.
