## Overview

李延年 / Li Yannian is a Western Han musician and performer whose talent, palace access, family connection to [[LadyLiWesternHan|李夫人]], and favor from [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] produce a sharp rise in status. Hanji 372-2 places that personal ascent beside Wudi's effort to create a military-merit path for Li Yannian's brother [[LiGuangliWesternHan|李广利]], making the Li household a case of favor spreading through different public channels.

## Current Profile

The episode places Li Yannian in an entertainer family and credits him with music, dance, and the “倾国倾城” song associated with his sister's presentation to Han Wudi. It says he earlier underwent castration after an offense, entered palace service, and then gained recognition through performance before Lady Li's favor further strengthened his position.

Li Yannian becomes xielü duwei with a two-thousand-shi salary in the episode's account. The phrase “与上卧起” and a comparison with [[HanYanWesternHan|韩嫣]] establish unusual personal proximity, but the podcast goes further by reading that access as a sexual relationship and direct replacement of Han Yan. This page preserves the access-and-patronage claim while treating the modern lover label as interpretive.

Hanji 372-2 adds a family comparison rather than a new office. It says Wudi's affection for Lady Li also extends to Li Yannian and places that favor beside the planned advancement of Li Guangli. Li Yannian's artistic office and intimate access, however, should not be treated as proof that he designed or benefited from the Dayuan command decision.

## Key Characteristics

- Musician and performer from an entertainer family.
- Palace servant whose artistic skill becomes a route to imperial recognition.
- Brother of Lady Li and Li Guangli, inside a household whose imperial favor converts through artistic office, intimate access, and military opportunity by different routes.
- Xielü duwei and high-salary beneficiary in the episode's account.
- Intimate favorite whose “与上卧起” access is stronger evidence of proximity than of a settled modern identity label.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to two popular-history episodes and does not reconstruct Li Yannian's complete musical career, punishment, offices, family prosecution, or the textual history of “倾国倾城.” Shared daily life and favorite status do not alone establish a modern sexual orientation, and the claim that he simply replaced Han Yan as a lover remains the host's interpretation. Hanji 372-2 establishes only a favored-household comparison; it does not show Li Yannian participating in Li Guangli's appointment or the Dayuan decision.

## What Changed

- Added the Li-family comparison: imperial favor reaches Li Yannian through artistic and intimate access while Li Guangli receives a military-merit opportunity.
- Preserved the boundary between household-wide favor and any unsupported claim that Li Yannian influenced the Dayuan appointment.
