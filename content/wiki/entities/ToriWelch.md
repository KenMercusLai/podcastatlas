# Tori Welch

Tori Welch appears in [[who-decides-what-big-box-sells-our-game-got-us-answers]] as [[ExplodingKittens]]' global sourcing person, speaking with [[KennyMalone]] from a factory floor in Shanghai while [[SellMeASasquatch]] is being manufactured. Her role makes the episode's retail win operational rather than abstract.

The source uses Welch to connect [[MassRetailProductionDeadline]] with actual production control: thousands of cards and boxes have to be printed, color has to match the selected Pantone purple, and quality checks have to preserve the retail promise that [[PackagingAsProductExperience]] created before manufacturing began.
