# 袁玉梅 / Yuan Yumei

## Overview
袁玉梅 / Yuan Yumei is the director and producer interviewed in [[ep281-duihua-wuzhenyu-kun-zai-chuangshang-li-de-women-jueding-zhongxin-zhaohui-ziji-lvvreapoykpj5xtlryaaupu1xo7r]] about [[ChanDrama|《蝉》]]. The source presents her as the creator who protects the drama's title, emotional structure, location texture, and trauma-centered reading.

## Current Profile
In this episode, Yuan frames [[ChanDrama|《蝉》]] as a psychological suspense story whose central question is not only "who is the culprit" but "how do wounded people find themselves." She repeatedly turns production choices into emotional architecture: cicadas, plum-like marks, birdcages, Guangxi humidity, water, bridges, green landscapes, original voices, and temporary family bonds all become part of how the drama makes trauma and connection visible.

Her production identity is also described through care for stories. She calls herself a guardian of stories and images, says long-term commitment to film and television depends on sustained love for projects, and values scenes that emerge from live conditions rather than only from pre-planned control.

## Key Characteristics
- Story-guardian identity - she describes production and directing as protecting story, image, and each day's shooting opportunity.
- Self-search suspense - she insists that [[ChanDrama|《蝉》]] is about finding oneself, not only finding a murderer.
- Symbolic production thinking - cicadas, plum marks, birdcages, water, bridges, and green humidity are used to externalize character states.
- Casting by contrast - she casts [[WuZhenyu|吴镇宇]] against familiar villain images while preserving the explosive power those images carry.
- Relational healing focus - she emphasizes Awu's role as Yu Songfan's responsibility, hope, and late-arriving family feeling.
- Human-centered AI boundary - she accepts AI help for difficult effects while keeping emotion, aesthetics, imperfection, and natural texture on the human side of creation.
