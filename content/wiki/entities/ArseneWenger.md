# Arsene Wenger / 温格

Arsene Wenger / 温格 appears in [[22-zuqiu-jingjixue-duzhe-bubi-shi-qiumi-570874427]] as the episode's clearest example of [[FootballAnalyticsModernization]]. [[QinZong|秦总]] and [[DengLaoban|邓老板]] present him as a coach whose economics background and openness to knowledge helped [[ArsenalFC]] use diet, transfer-market information gaps, data, and professional management before those practices became industry norms.

The source treats Wenger as both revolutionary and historically bounded. His early advantage came from seeing undervalued players, especially from French, African, and non-English markets, and from using physical and performance indicators to make disciplined contract and transfer decisions. The later problem is that a small edge in knowledge and efficiency could be overwhelmed once larger capital and broader [[PremierLeague]] professionalization arrived.

## Key Claims
- Wenger stands for the shift from charismatic old football toward evidence, nutrition, cross-border scouting, and management systems.
- His use of undervalued foreign players links him to [[OpenFootballTalentMarkets]] and to the episode's argument that prejudice creates economic waste.
- His later decline in relative advantage shows that innovation can become ordinary infrastructure once the whole field catches up.
