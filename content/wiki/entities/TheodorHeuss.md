# Theodor Heuss

## Overview

Theodor Heuss appears as the West German president who resisted restoring *Das Lied der Deutschen* before accepting the third-verse compromise.

## Current Profile

The episode frames Heuss as worried that the old anthem had become too closely associated with Nazism. He proposed a rival "Hymn to Germany," but it failed to catch on, and he eventually approved the restoration of [[GermanNationalAnthem|Das Lied der Deutschen]] on the understanding that only the third verse would be sung.

## Key Characteristics

- West German president concerned about Nazi-tainted symbolic continuity.
- Sponsor of an alternative anthem that did not gain public traction.
- Actor who publicly reemphasized the third verse after the 1954 World Cup controversy.
- Counterpart to [[KonradAdenauer]] in the postwar anthem compromise.

- Compromise: [[679-germany-the-song-hitler-stole-part-3-glt6217148052]] says Heuss accepted the anthem with the third verse as the proper sung version.
- Public correction: [[679-germany-the-song-hitler-stole-part-3-glt6217148052]] says Heuss later emphasized the third verse after German fans reportedly sang the first verse at the 1954 World Cup final.
