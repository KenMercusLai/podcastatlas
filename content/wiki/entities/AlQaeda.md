# Al-Qaeda

## Overview

Al-Qaeda is the transnational jihadist organization founded by [[OsamaBinLaden|Osama bin Laden]] and identified in this source as the organization behind the September 11 attacks.

## Current Profile

[[95-9-11-glt8208243510]] translates the name as “the base” or “the foundation” and presents the organization as a globalized militant network rather than a simple survival of local religious traditionalism. Its recruits could be uprooted, educated, affluent, multilingual, and familiar with Western popular culture while remaining committed to violent rejection of American power and Western modernity.

The episode treats al-Qaeda's September 11 operation as symbolic communication as well as physical attack. American financial, military, and governmental targets were selected for globally legible meaning, and television transformed destruction into a worldwide spectacle. This differentiates al-Qaeda from the [[Taliban]], whose priorities the hosts characterize as more locally Afghan.

## Key Characteristics

- Transnational jihadist network organized around a global rather than primarily local struggle.
- Movement capable of recruiting mobile and partly Westernized militants.
- Organization that targeted symbols of American finance, military power, and government.
- User of global television and cinematic imagery as force multipliers for terrorism.
- Actor whose violence helped trigger a much larger American military and security response.

- [[95-9-11-glt8208243510]] links the attacks to the American war frame, Afghanistan, Iraq, and the broader [[WarOnTerrorOverreach]] argument.

## Qualifications

The source is a historical interpretation, not a complete organizational history, intelligence assessment, or account of al-Qaeda's changing affiliates. The recruits described in the episode cannot be treated as a demographic profile of all members, and the global-media interpretation does not substitute for operational evidence about every planner's motive.
