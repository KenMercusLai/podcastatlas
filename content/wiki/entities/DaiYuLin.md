# Dai Yu Lin

## Overview
Dai Yu Lin is the researcher named in the [[HubermanLab]] aggression Essentials episode for optogenetic experiments activating estrogen-receptor neurons in the [[VentromedialHypothalamus]] of mice.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Dai Yu Lin's work as a vivid modern demonstration that activating a selected VMH neuron population can switch behavior into attack. In the source account, light stimulation of estrogen-receptor-containing VMH neurons caused a male mouse to stop mating and attack, and in another setup to attack an inanimate glove-like target until stimulation ended.

The page is narrowly source-bounded: Dai Yu Lin appears as part of the episode's aggression-circuit evidence, not as a full career profile.

## Key Characteristics
- Associated in the source with optogenetic control of VMH estrogen-receptor neurons.
- Provides evidence that a small selected neural population can rapidly alter aggressive action.
- Helps connect hormone receptor biology with circuit-triggered behavior.
- Appears as part of the modern experimental branch following older stimulation studies.

## Evidence
- Optogenetic stimulation - [[essentials-understanding-controlling-aggression-scim4757138073]] names Dai Yu Lin's experiments using light to activate estrogen-receptor-containing VMH neurons in mice.
- Behavioral switching - [[essentials-understanding-controlling-aggression-scim4757138073]] says stimulation could interrupt mating and trigger attack, and could also make a mouse attack an inanimate target.
