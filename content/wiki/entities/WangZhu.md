# 王蠋 / Wang Zhu

王蠋 / Wang Zhu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-72-shenme-yang-de-ren-cai-shi-zhongzhen-zhishi-logxegnqexgc7z-lodw6tr6-8f28]] as a worthy man from [[QiState|齐国]] after [[LeYi|乐毅]] and [[YanState|燕国]] have broken Qi in 284 BCE. The episode says he had once served in office but withdrew because [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]] did not accept remonstrance.

Yan forces first signal respect by keeping troops away from the area around him and offering wealth plus a ten-thousand-household fief. When he refuses, the approach turns coercive: Yan soldiers threaten to slaughter his city if he does not come.

Wang Zhu's answer is the episode's central ethical case. He says a loyal minister does not serve two rulers, judges himself already unable to save the broken state and dead ruler, and refuses to live by submitting under force. His death becomes the source case for [[LoyaltyCollateralDilemma|忠义牵连困境]]: he will not serve Yan, but he also will not make his neighbors the price of his refusal.
