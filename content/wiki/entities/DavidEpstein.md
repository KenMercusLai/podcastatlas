# David Epstein

David Epstein appears in [[we-almost-had-a-smartphone-in-the-90s-why-did-it-fail]] as the journalist and constraints analyst who interprets [[GeneralMagic]]'s failure. The episode attributes to him the argument that the company failed in part because it had too much: too much talent, time, money, freedom, and partner complexity.

His role is conceptual rather than biographical. Epstein turns the [[SonyMagicLink]] from a simple flop into a case for [[ConstraintDrivenProductDiscipline]], [[ClearCustomerDefinition]], [[FeatureCreep]], and [[DesirableDifficulty]].

## Connections
- [[GeneralMagic]] and [[SonyMagicLink]] - source case he interprets.
- [[TonyFadell]] - builder whose later Apple work becomes the contrast case.
