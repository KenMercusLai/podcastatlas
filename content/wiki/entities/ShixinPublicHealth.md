# 《失信：公共卫生体系的崩溃》

《失信：公共卫生体系的崩溃》 is the public-health-system book recommended in [[yi-fen-laizi-bingchuang-shang-de-xiangguan-shudan-598284093]]. The source transcript recognizes the title as "《诗信》", but the subtitle and author description point to [[LaurieGarrett|劳丽·加勒特 / Laurie Garrett]]'s book on the collapse of global public health systems.

The episode uses the book to explain [[PublicHealthInvisibility]]. Clean water, sewage systems, safe food, school-canteen hygiene, surveillance, and emergency preparedness often work by making disasters not happen; that success can make them look politically less urgent than visible hospitals, heroic treatments, or prestigious projects.
