# 粉墨 / Fenmo

## Overview
粉墨 / Fenmo is the creative team represented by director [[LiChenFenmo|李晨]] in the 三五环 episode on AI short-film production.

## Current Profile
The source presents 粉墨 as a small-team AI short-film practice rather than a one-off prompt account. Alongside [[NvwaZhiSi|《女娲之死》]], the team is described as submitting multiple works from a developing AI short anthology, with 李晨 sometimes directing and sometimes supervising. The format is compared to short speculative series such as *Love, Death & Robots* or *Black Mirror*.

Its relevance to the wiki is that it turns AI video from individual experimentation into small-team production. The team still needs story development, visual style choice, storyboards, image generation, video generation, editing, sound, color, and output selection. AI changes the size and cost of the crew, but not the need for direction and taste.

## Key Characteristics
- Small creative team in the source's AI short-film contest context.
- Uses AI video for multiple short works rather than only a single demo.
- Organizes work through conventional creative roles, including directing and supervision, adapted to AI tools.
- Treats AI short films as a possible anthology format where speculative stories can be produced at lower cost.
- Shows that AI video can support small-team production while preserving craft bottlenecks around script, image, motion, editing, and finishing.
