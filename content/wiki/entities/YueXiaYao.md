# 月下瑶 / Yue XiaYao

## Overview
月下瑶 / Yue XiaYao is a Chinese podcaster and loyal [[DuBaoTeleread|读报teleread / 独报]] listener who appears in the show's fifth-anniversary upper-half special.

## Current Profile
In the source, 月下瑶 functions less as an external expert than as a peer creator-listener. She asks how the show sustains itself, how the host thinks about tipping, community, release rhythm, topic selection, English writing, audience response, and data, and she repeatedly contrasts creator pleasure with productization.

Her role is important because she gives the episode a listener's memory and a creator's practical pressure at the same time. She identifies the show's stable quality and values from the outside, while also pressing the host on what can or cannot be turned into an explicit method.
