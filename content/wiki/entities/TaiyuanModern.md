## Overview
太原 / Taiyuan is Shanxi's provincial capital and one of the three northern morning cases in the source, disambiguated here from [[TaiyuanWarringStates|the Warring States place and commandery]].

## Current Profile
The source presents a narrow everyday-life profile: Taiyuan reuses parking lots and an antique-market compound for morning trade, after which the spaces return to their primary functions. Regular customers often buy directly from familiar stalls, while closing-time discounting and seasonal produce show how timing organizes both price and habit. Breakfast examples include beef-head soup, ma ye with old tofu, meatball soup, flatbread, and liang da tang with steamed bread.

## Key Characteristics
- Morning markets can occupy multi-use compounds rather than only public streets.
- Regular-customer knowledge shortens browsing but can create requests for credit, extra quantity, or queue exceptions.
- Closing time changes prices as vendors try to clear perishable stock.
- Seasonal produce remains central to the market profile described by the episode.
- Local breakfast is represented through soup, offal, tofu, fried dough, and breads.

## Evidence
- Space reuse: [[ep287-yiqi-guang-zaoshi-xunzhao-beifang-zui-xianhuo-de-zaochen-lpuox4b-b02k-awt-hsjuph-froi]] reports morning markets in a parking lot and antique-market space that resume their other uses after closing.
- Regular-customer trade: [[ep287-yiqi-guang-zaoshi-xunzhao-beifang-zui-xianhuo-de-zaochen-lpuox4b-b02k-awt-hsjuph-froi]] describes direct visits to known vendors, closing discounts, and the operating costs of familiar social obligations.
- Breakfast profile: [[ep287-yiqi-guang-zaoshi-xunzhao-beifang-zui-xianhuo-de-zaochen-lpuox4b-b02k-awt-hsjuph-froi]] names beef-head soup and several other local morning foods while noting that Taiyuan receives less detailed treatment than Shenyang.

## Qualifications
The episode gives Taiyuan substantially less historical and institutional context than Shenyang. It does not establish how representative the visited markets or breakfast shops are across the city, nor does it provide a current citywide count.
