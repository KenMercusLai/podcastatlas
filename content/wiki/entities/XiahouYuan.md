# 夏侯渊 / Xiahou Yuan

## Overview
夏侯渊 / Xiahou Yuan enters the wiki through Hanji 1054 as a Cao-side commander sent with [[ZhangLiao|张辽]] to besiege [[ChangXiLateHan|昌豨]] in Donghai.

## Current Profile
The current evidence is narrow. Xiahou Yuan functions as the co-commander and operational frame for the siege, while Zhang Liao receives the episode's detailed attention as the officer who reads Chang Xi's wavering and conducts the surrender persuasion. Xiahou Yuan therefore anchors Cao Cao's continued eastern pacification pressure rather than a full independent biography.

## Key Characteristics
- Cao-side military commander in the Donghai operation against Chang Xi.
- Paired with Zhang Liao in the siege force.
- Provides the command setting in which Zhang Liao's psychological observation and negotiation become possible.
- Currently source-bounded to the Chang Xi operation, not yet developed as a wider career page.

## Evidence
- Siege assignment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1054-jingtan-caocao-yuanshao-liubei-de-mingyun-zhuanzhe-lmd2yjre-cwdbxky2ht5o0n4g6fk|Hanji 1054]] says Cao Cao sends Xiahou Yuan and Zhang Liao with troops to Donghai to besiege Chang Xi.
- Counsel scene: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1054-jingtan-caocao-yuanshao-liubei-de-mingyun-zhuanzhe-lmd2yjre-cwdbxky2ht5o0n4g6fk|Hanji 1054]] has Zhang Liao explain to Xiahou Yuan why Chang Xi's behavior suggests hesitation and why persuasion may work.

## Qualifications
Hanji 1054 does not provide Xiahou Yuan's full career, kinship placement, title history, or later campaigns. This page should remain compact until later sources add independent evidence.
