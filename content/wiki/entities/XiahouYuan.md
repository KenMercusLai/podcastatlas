# 夏侯渊 / Xiahou Yuan

## Overview
夏侯渊 / Xiahou Yuan is a Cao-side commander whose current profile now spans an early Donghai pacification role and his late death in the struggle for [[Hanzhong|汉中]].

## Current Profile
Hanji 1054 makes Xiahou Yuan the co-commander and operational frame for Zhang Liao's surrender persuasion of Chang Xi. Hanji 1105 adds the far later endpoint: the episode identifies Xiahou Yuan as Cao Cao's long-serving kinsman and says his death during Liu Bei's Hanzhong campaign helped leave Cao Cao unable to reverse the theater's momentum.

## Key Characteristics
- Cao-side military commander in the Donghai operation against Chang Xi.
- Paired with Zhang Liao in the siege force.
- Provides the command setting in which Zhang Liao's psychological observation and negotiation become possible.
- Is presented by Hanji 1105 as Cao Cao's kinsman and long-serving companion in arms.
- Dies in the Hanzhong campaign, becoming a marker of Cao Cao's late western reversal.
- Still remains a partial profile rather than a complete career reconstruction.

## Evidence
- Siege assignment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1054-jingtan-caocao-yuanshao-liubei-de-mingyun-zhuanzhe-lmd2yjre-cwdbxky2ht5o0n4g6fk|Hanji 1054]] says Cao Cao sends Xiahou Yuan and Zhang Liao with troops to Donghai to besiege Chang Xi.
- Counsel scene: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1054-jingtan-caocao-yuanshao-liubei-de-mingyun-zhuanzhe-lmd2yjre-cwdbxky2ht5o0n4g6fk|Hanji 1054]] has Zhang Liao explain to Xiahou Yuan why Chang Xi's behavior suggests hesitation and why persuasion may work.
- Hanzhong endpoint: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1105-guanyu-zhisi-maizang-le-caocao-zenyang-de-mengxiang-lgmk_dlknjpzz7fvzasno6tu7fs3|Hanji 1105]] says Xiahou Yuan is killed during Liu Bei's Hanzhong offensive and presents the loss as both strategically and personally significant to Cao Cao.

## Qualifications
The two sources leave a large chronological gap and do not provide Xiahou Yuan's full career, precise kinship genealogy, title history, death circumstances, or the full Hanzhong operation. Hanji 1105's "thirty years" companionship language is retained as source framing rather than independently verified duration.

## What Changed
- Added the Hanzhong death endpoint and its strategic-personal significance for Cao Cao.
- Expanded the page beyond Donghai while preserving the unresolved career gap.
