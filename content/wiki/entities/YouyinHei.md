# 右尹黑 / Youyin Hei

右尹黑 / Youyin Hei appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-14-qiweiwang-buming-zeyi-yiming-jingren-lrs6rbifyiqrojxg0mccpg7sf9rv]] as the [[ChuState|楚国]] high official sent to [[QinState|秦国]] to receive a bride. The episode explains 右尹 as a senior Chu office and argues that the unnamed Qin woman was probably intended for [[ChuXuanwang|楚宣王]], not for Youyin Hei himself.

The page is source-scoped. Youyin Hei matters because he makes [[WarringStatesMarriageDiplomacy|战国联姻外交]] visible as a state-level act: a minister performs the marriage protocol, but the political relationship belongs to the ruling house and interstate strategy.
