# Huberman Lab

## Overview
Huberman Lab is the podcast/show context for [[essentials-diet-nutrition-for-mental-health-dr-chris-palmer-scim2465421786]], an Essentials episode on nutrition, ketogenic diets, and mental health.

## Current Profile
In this source, [[AndrewHuberman]] introduces the episode and frames nutrition as a variable that can interact closely with mental health over time. The show gives [[ChrisPalmer]] space to connect clinical stories, epilepsy history, and mitochondrial mechanisms while repeatedly marking professional-supervision boundaries for serious psychiatric disorders.

## Key Characteristics
- The show presents health-science topics through host framing, guest clinical experience, and biological mechanism discussion.
- The Essentials format is used here to concentrate on a single practical topic: diet, ketosis, metabolism, and mental health.
- The episode treats nutrition as clinically relevant without turning it into a replacement for psychiatric care.
- The discussion style moves between patient stories, historical evidence from epilepsy, and mechanistic claims about mitochondria.
