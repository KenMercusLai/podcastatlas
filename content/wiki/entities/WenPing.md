# 文聘 / Wen Ping

## Overview
文聘 / Wen Ping is a Jingzhou commander whose shame over failing the old regime is reclassified by [[CaoCao|曹操]] as evidence of loyalty and future reliability.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1095 presents Wen Ping as refusing to hurry toward the victorious camp after [[LiuCongLateHan|刘琮]] submits [[Jingzhou|荆州]]. He remains by the Han River until Cao Cao has crossed, then explains through tears that he failed to help [[LiuBiao|刘表]] serve the court, could not preserve the territory after Liu Biao's death, and felt unable to face either Liu Cong or the deceased ruler.

Cao Cao treats this grief as a recommendation rather than a disqualification. He praises Wen Ping as a loyal official, rewards him, preserves his military authority, and appoints him administrator of Jiangxia. The episode later presents his long defense there as retrospective validation of Cao Cao's judgment.

## Key Characteristics
- Jingzhou military commander tied to the Liu Biao-Liu Cong regime.
- Delays submission from shame rather than bargaining for office in the episode's account.
- Publicly expresses grief and responsibility for failing to preserve the old territory.
- Has old-lord loyalty reinterpreted as proof of character by Cao Cao.
- Retains command and receives the Jiangxia administrator appointment.
- Functions as the episode's clearest case of loyalty-based former-enemy incorporation.

## Qualifications
The page reflects one popular-history episode. The exact dialogue, emotional reconstruction, title wording, duration of the Jiangxia command, and later military record require primary-source verification. Loyalty to an old regime does not by itself prove future reliability; the episode interprets the later career as confirmation.
