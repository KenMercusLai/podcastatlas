# Desh Garments

Desh Garments is the export-oriented garment factory in [[Bangladesh]] created through the [[AbdulMajidChowdhury]] and [[Daewoo]] transfer story in [[getting-entrepreneurial-in-korea-summer-school]]. The episode says it opened in Chittagong in 1980 after a group of Bangladeshis trained in Korea for six months.

Its wiki role is as a first-rung [[IndustrialLadder]] case. Desh Garments shows how [[TextileKnowledgeTransfer]] can seed a much larger export sector, while the later discussion of [[RanaPlaza]] keeps the safety and institutional costs of fast garment industrialization visible.
