## Overview

Salomon's House is the scientific institution at the center of [[FrancisBacon|Francis Bacon]]'s [[NewAtlantis|*New Atlantis*]], responsible for gathering knowledge, organizing experiments, producing inventions, and advising Bensalem.

## Current Profile

The institution divides inquiry into recurring roles: agents collect foreign discoveries; others gather craft knowledge and literature; experimenters test nature; analysts organize results; theorists form explanations; and inventors translate findings into applications. Its caves, towers, lakes, farms, health rooms, pharmacies, and optical and acoustic facilities give that labor a durable material base.

This capability also creates autonomy. Members decide which discoveries to publish or conceal and may not always tell the government what they know. They can authenticate wonders, forecast hazards, manufacture convincing sights and sounds, and then promise not to misuse those powers. The same institution is therefore public benefactor, expert authority, information gatekeeper, and potential unaccountable power center.

## Key Characteristics

- Specialized research organization rather than a gathering of isolated savants.
- Global collector of books, instruments, inventions, and practical knowledge.
- Operator of diverse experimental facilities and long-term research programs.
- Translator of discovery into medicine, agriculture, instruments, transport, and public forecasts.
- Gatekeeper that chooses what to disclose, conceal, or communicate to government.
- Source of scientific, civic, and quasi-religious authority in Bensalem.
- Institution whose safeguards depend heavily on internal discipline and moral commitment.

## Qualifications

Salomon's House is a fictional institution described through an unfinished work and one podcast interpretation. It should not be treated as a complete constitutional design or direct blueprint for a modern university, laboratory, technology company, or intelligence agency. The source does not settle whether Bacon intended its secrecy and autonomy as praise, warning, or productive ambiguity.

## What Changed

- Established the institution's linked research, infrastructure, disclosure, and advisory roles.
- Distinguished its public-service capacity from the accountability risk created by expert autonomy.
