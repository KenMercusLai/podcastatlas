# 微子启 / Weizi Qi

微子启 / Weizi Qi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]] as the Shang royal relative through whom [[SongState|宋国]] is founded after the Zhou conquest. The source says he comes to the Zhou side with ritual vessels, and that Zhou rule allows the Shang survivors to continue living, reproducing, and worshiping ancestors through Song.

His wiki role is to ground [[ShangRemnantStateLegitimacy]]. The episode presents him as a conquered-dynasty lineage figure turned into a ritually honored political guest rather than a defeated enemy to be erased.
