# Carl von Clausewitz / 克劳塞维茨

Carl von Clausewitz / 克劳塞维茨 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-4-shui-shi-kongzi-hou-zui-weidade-sixiangjia-lr4phoq9chc4tz0yb8y95gy21qdd]], where the host cites 《战争论》 as a contrast to [[Xunzi|荀子]] and [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]] on righteous war. The source uses him for one purpose: to insist that war is coercive violence aimed at compelling the enemy, not a moralized encounter that can be imagined away as nearly bloodless.

In this source, Clausewitz is not developed biographically. His wiki role is a realist foil that sharpens [[ConfucianMilitaryIdealismLimits|儒家军事理想的限度]]: a theory of renyi warfare may restrain cruelty and clarify legitimate purpose, but it still has to face resistance, fear, killing, and the practical mechanics of force.
