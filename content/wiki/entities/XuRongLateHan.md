# 徐荣 / Xu Rong (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-953-luanshi-zhizhong-yaobuyao-jiangjiu-renyi-lrzfimgqatjxyw9zx9ry2kiwxkon|Hanji 953]] shows Xu Rong before the Sun Jian branch: he intercepts [[CaoCao|曹操]] near Xingyang/Bianshui during Cao Cao's first westward anti-[[DongZhuo|董卓]] move and defeats him badly. Yet the source stresses that Xu Rong withdraws after seeing Cao Cao's small force resist through the day, reading the larger [[SuanzaoCamp|Suanzao]] coalition as potentially dangerous if attacked directly.

徐荣 / Xu Rong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-955-guanyu-zhan-huaxiong-qishi-shi-wei-lishi-lndkrj-fnepye8iu-ifgyh-vzysf|Hanji 955]] as the [[DongZhuo|董卓]]-side commander who defeats [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] near Liang before Sun Jian's Yangren recovery. The source uses Xu Rong's victory to show that Sun Jian's later momentum is not a straight-line success story: it begins with loss, pursuit, and near exposure.

The aftermath gives Xu Rong's branch its practical meaning. [[ZuMaoLateHan|祖茂]] diverts pursuers by taking Sun Jian's headgear, and Sun Jian later gathers remaining troops and moves to [[YangrenBattleLateHan|阳人之战]]. Xu Rong therefore functions as the pressure point that makes Sun Jian's resilience and retainer loyalty visible.
