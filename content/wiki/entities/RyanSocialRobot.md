# Ryan Social Robot

## Overview
Ryan is the social companion robot discussed in [[ep-42-when-ai-meets-robotics-building-machines-that-care]]. The source presents Ryan as an embodied AI care companion for older adults living with loneliness, depression, cognitive impairment, and dementia.

## Current Profile
Ryan's profile in the episode combines companionship, affect sensing, conversation, and cognitive engagement. The robot is described as recognizing names and faces, reading facial expressions, remembering prior conversations, playing cognitive games, and participating in clinical pilots with nearly 900 hours of interaction.

The source's strongest profile claim is that Ryan is not just a technical demo. It is a test of whether [[ArtificialEmotionalIntelligence]], LLM conversation, privacy controls, consent, and respectful user experience can work together in [[SocialRoboticsElderCare]].

## Key Characteristics
- Designed as a companion for older adults, especially those facing loneliness, depression, cognitive impairment, or dementia.
- Uses social cues such as face, gaze, voice, and sentiment to support appropriate interaction.
- Shifted from scripted chatbot dialogue toward broader LLM-enabled conversation.
- Has been used in clinical-pilot contexts where residents formed visible emotional bonds.
- Requires careful consent, privacy, transparency, and departure planning because users may be vulnerable.
- Represents care augmentation rather than a replacement for caregivers or family members.

## Evidence
- Purpose and users: [[ep-42-when-ai-meets-robotics-building-machines-that-care]] describes Ryan as a social companion robot for older adults with depression, cognitive impairment, and dementia.
- Capabilities: [[ep-42-when-ai-meets-robotics-building-machines-that-care]] says Ryan can read facial expressions, hold conversations, play cognitive games, remember interactions, and recognize people.
- LLM shift: [[ep-42-when-ai-meets-robotics-building-machines-that-care]] reports that Ryan moved beyond a scripted dialogue system limited to roughly 90 minutes of non-repetitive conversation.
- Pilot learning: [[ep-42-when-ai-meets-robotics-building-machines-that-care]] reports nearly 900 hours of clinical-pilot interaction and strong resident attachment when Ryan was removed.
- Care boundary: [[ep-42-when-ai-meets-robotics-building-machines-that-care]] ends by framing Ryan as a tool to empower caregivers, families, and humans rather than take over care.

## Qualifications
The available source does not give a full technical architecture, independent clinical outcome measures, deployment economics, safety incident history, or long-term retention data. Emotional attachment is treated as important field evidence, but also creates a duty to manage expectations, withdrawal, and honesty about what the machine understands.
