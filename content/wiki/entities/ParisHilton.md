# Paris Hilton

## Overview

Paris Hilton is a media personality presented in the bounded source as the formative modern example of a celebrity vocal persona combining youthfulness, submission, sexuality, and recognizability.

## Current Profile

[[LakeBell]] treats Hilton's voice as central to her fame rather than incidental to it. In Bell's account, high pitch and other features of [[SexyBabyVoice]] helped make Hilton instantly recognizable while joining a childlike sound to overt sexuality.

The episode places Hilton at a media transition in the 2000s. Reality television, constant celebrity exposure, and the public circulation of sexual intimacy turned a private-sounding “bedroom” register into a public-facing identity. Hilton therefore anchors [[BroadcastIntimacyAsPersonalBrand]]: a repeatable voice can operate as both persona and attention asset.
