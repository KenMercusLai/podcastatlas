# Bexley Natural Market

## Overview
Bexley Natural Market is a cooperative grocery store in Central Ohio and the third caller case in [[advice-line-with-ben-goodwin-of-olipop-f9d3fe17-eb2c-440d-b223-990fed970727]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Bexley Natural Market as a long-running local grocery co-op trying to recover sales after a financial shock. Guy Raz and Ben Goodwin argue that its advantage is not matching large grocery e-commerce, but giving shoppers a curated, local, pleasurable store experience that larger chains cannot easily copy.

## Key Characteristics
- Described by [[JohnGroner]] as Central Ohio's only cooperative grocery store.
- Has existed for about 50 years.
- Sells natural and organic products and buys from local vendors and farmers.
- Pre-pandemic sales were about $1.2 million to $1.4 million per year, while current sales are about halfway back in the source account.
- Needs about 20% more sales to feel safe.
- Built a GraceCart website with inventory, ordering, and payment, but saw little traction.

## Qualifications
