# 秦文公 / Duke Wen of Qin

秦文公 / Duke Wen of Qin appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] at the end of the source's [[QinState|秦国]] origin digression. The host says Qin had to recover the land granted by [[ZhouPingwang|周平王]] from the Quanrong and only drove them out by Qin Wen Gong's generation.

His source role is to show that recognition and actual control are different. Qin may receive status and land claims through [[QinXiangGong|秦襄公]], but territorial possession still requires later effort.
