# 徐庶 / Xu Shu (late Han)

## Overview

徐庶 / Xu Shu is the adviser who gives [[LiuBei|刘备]] a direct referral to [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] and insists that Liu Bei approach Zhuge Liang personally.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1082-zhugeliang-yiduan-hua-dianpo-luanshi-fengyun-tai-gan-shuo-le-llflnkdaqiub4_dgujugkdzkl3qh|Hanji 1082]] places Xu Shu after [[SimaHuiLateHan|司马徽]] in a repeated recommendation chain. His distinctive contribution is not another memorable nickname but a recruitment condition: Zhuge Liang should not be casually summoned, so Liu Bei must demonstrate seriousness through a personal visit.

## Key Characteristics

- Serves as a direct talent referrer inside Liu Bei's Jingzhou search.
- Independently reinforces Sima Hui's positive assessment of Zhuge Liang.
- Treats recruitment posture as part of whether exceptional talent will engage.
- Converts reputation into a concrete next action for the prospective patron.

## Evidence

- Repeated recommendation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1082-zhugeliang-yiduan-hua-dianpo-luanshi-fengyun-tai-gan-shuo-le-llflnkdaqiub4_dgujugkdzkl3qh|Hanji 1082]] has Xu Shu identify Zhuge Liang as the “卧龙” after Sima Hui's earlier appraisal.
- Recruitment condition: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1082-zhugeliang-yiduan-hua-dianpo-luanshi-fengyun-tai-gan-shuo-le-llflnkdaqiub4_dgujugkdzkl3qh|Hanji 1082]] has Xu Shu reject Liu Bei's idea of having Zhuge Liang brought to him and recommend a personal visit instead.

## Qualifications

The current profile rests on one popular-history episode and covers only Xu Shu's referral role. Exact wording, chronology, the relationship between the two recommendations, and the causal path from referral to the three visits require verification against underlying historical texts.

## What Changed

- Created a bounded profile for Xu Shu's referral and recruitment-posture role.
