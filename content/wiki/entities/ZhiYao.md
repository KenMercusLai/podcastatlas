# 智瑶 / Zhi Yao

智瑶 / Zhi Yao appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-04-yurang-wei-zhiyao-fuchou-ln34tydha6iqwlrgmw7iugqfgfik]] as the defeated Jin power-holder whose failure lets [[SimaGuang|司马光]] state the episode's [[TalentVirtueDistinction|才德之分]] lesson. The source says Zhi Yao did not lose because he lacked ability; he lost because his talent exceeded his virtue.

After the Battle of Jinyang, Han, Zhao, and Wei divide Zhi territory. The episode uses Zhi Yao's death and [[ZhaoXiangzi|赵无恤]]'s use of his lacquered skull as a drinking vessel to make [[YuRang|豫让]]'s revenge both a political aftermath and a personal honor story.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-84-fangzhi-qinguo-miechu-jing-yong-zhe-zhao-llgh12eistejijsl6yildzkixocm]] brings Zhi Yao back as a warning example inside [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]]'s letter. Huang Xie argues that if Qin trusts [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]] while marching against [[ChuState|楚国]], it risks repeating Zhi Yao's mistake of relying on partners whose incentives can flip once the campaign creates an opening.
