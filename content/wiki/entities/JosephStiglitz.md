# Joseph Stiglitz

## Overview
Joseph Stiglitz is the Columbia economist quoted by Marketplace Tech as favoring corporate-profit taxation over more elaborate AI-specific tax designs.

## Current Profile
In the source, Stiglitz functions as the simple-tax-base counterpoint. While other proposals look for AI-specific units or public equity claims, he argues that governments can close loopholes and tax profits more effectively. His role is to shift the AI public-finance debate from taxing model activity directly toward taxing the corporate gains that may come from automation.

## Key Characteristics
- Stiglitz supplies the corporate-profit tax alternative in the episode's policy menu.
- His argument reduces design complexity by focusing on existing business-income tax channels.
- He treats loophole closure as central to recapturing revenue from AI-era corporate gains.
- His source role is explicitly redistributive but does not require proving a taxable token unit.
