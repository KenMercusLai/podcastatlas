# Alexander the Great / 亚历山大大帝

## Overview
Alexander the Great / 亚历山大大帝 enters the wiki through a tactical comparison between his reported assault on [[SogdianRock|粟特岩堡]] and [[GanNing|甘宁]]'s seizure of [[YilingLateHan|夷陵]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Alexander in Central Asia in 327 BCE. Faced with a fortress whose defenders consider its cliffs impassable, he reportedly selects three hundred experienced climbers for a night ascent. Their dawn appearance above the position creates the impression that the inaccessible height has been breached, and the garrison surrenders.

The profile is deliberately narrow: it establishes one example of [[UnexpectedApproachFortressAssault|意外进路式要塞突袭]], not a complete biography.

## Key Characteristics
- Adapts to an apparently inaccessible fortress.
- Selects personnel for specialized climbing ability.
- Uses night movement and an unexpected approach.
- Converts small-scale penetration into psychological pressure.
