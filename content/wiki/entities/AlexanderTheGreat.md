# Alexander the Great / 亚历山大大帝

## Overview
Alexander the Great / 亚历山大大帝 enters the wiki through both a tactical comparison at [[SogdianRock|粟特岩堡]] and a cultural-history account of his clean-shaven ruling image.

## Current Profile
The Central Asian source presents Alexander in 327 BCE. Faced with a fortress whose defenders consider its cliffs impassable, he reportedly selects three hundred experienced climbers for a night ascent. Their dawn appearance above the position creates the impression that the inaccessible height has been breached, and the garrison surrenders.

A separate cultural-history account adds another mode of influence. By presenting himself clean shaven rather than in his father Philip II's mature, Zeus-like style, Alexander made the smooth-chinned ruler youthful, powerful, and prestigious; later Roman shaving culture repeatedly negotiated that model. The profile remains selective rather than biographical.

## Key Characteristics
- Adapts to an apparently inaccessible fortress.
- Selects personnel for specialized climbing ability.
- Uses night movement and an unexpected approach.
- Converts small-scale penetration into psychological pressure.
- Made clean-shaven rulership an influential elite visual model.

- Specialized selection: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1108-ganning-qixi-yiling-zhanshu-jing-he-yalishanda-zhuangche-lia4azq39ja4pt6yfpjggjl-g3vc|Hanji 1108]] says Alexander chooses three hundred experienced climbers.
- Unexpected approach: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1108-ganning-qixi-yiling-zhanshu-jing-he-yalishanda-zhuangche-lia4azq39ja4pt6yfpjggjl-g3vc|Hanji 1108]] describes a night ascent on the discounted cliff.
- Psychological result: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1108-ganning-qixi-yiling-zhanshu-jing-he-yalishanda-zhuangche-lia4azq39ja4pt6yfpjggjl-g3vc|Hanji 1108]] links their dawn appearance to surrender.
- Ruler image: [[491-historys-greatest-beards-from-egyptian-queens-to-medieval-conquerors-part-1-glt6058938613]] contrasts Alexander's youthful smooth face with Philip II's mature bearded kingship and traces the former's later prestige at Rome.

## Qualifications
This profile relies on two popular-history retellings. Force size, equipment, casualties, captive totals, surrender mechanism, and the Roxana sequence are not independently verified here. The beard episode rejects Plutarch's anti-grabbing explanation for military shaving and treats Alexander's precise image-making motive as interpretation rather than settled fact.

## What Changed
- Added Alexander's clean-shaven image as a distinct form of long-run cultural influence.
