## Overview

王肃 / Wang Su enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-728-weihe-mingchen-nan-shanzhong-beihou-de-yuanyin-ni-gai-dong-ltjl2bgpyyz1bmwe-4mfcyd9e6cf|Hanji 728]] as [[WangPanEasternHan|王磐]]'s son, whose continued movement through princely mansions after his father's execution helps turn elite guest access into a court-security problem.

## Current Profile

Hanji 728 presents Wang Su with very little independent biography. His importance is relational and diagnostic. After Wang Pan is killed for a crime, Wang Su still frequents the residences of kings and marquises. Later, a memorial accuses Wang Su and others of being descendants of destroyed families who have become honored guests of princes and may create disorder.

The episode places Wang Su inside the larger prince-guest disaster. His continued access does not by itself prove a rebellion in the source summary, but it gives court critics a visible association pattern: punished-family descendants, princely households, private guests, and fear of coordinated violence.

## Key Characteristics

- Son of Wang Pan and continuation of the Wang family exposure after Wang Pan's death.
- Visible in princely mansions despite the warning and punishment around his father.
- Named in an accusation that treats punished-family descendants as dangerous princely guests.
- Functions as evidence of association risk more than as a fully profiled actor.
