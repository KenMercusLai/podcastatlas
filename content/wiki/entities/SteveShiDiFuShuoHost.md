# Steve (史蒂夫说 host)

## Overview
Steve is the host of [[ShiDiFuShuo|史蒂夫说]] in [[shi-di-fu-shuo-472-zhao-jinzhao-huajie-jiaolv-zuijia-fangfa-chaijie-wenti-wenrou-xiangdai-lqw9h-jqfzrsrveagfslnakhcgjh]], where he interviews [[ZhaoJinzhao|赵金昭 / Dr. Claire]] about anxiety, and in [[shi-di-fu-shuo-471-zixunshi-huixin-wanshi-zhi-jiang-luoji-de-ren-dou-chengzhang-yu-lixing-wei-ming-de-yapo-lrsk-0knooxxa-twm1ci5dfhldxi]], where he answers listener letters about trauma, overthinking, therapy, and body-based self-trust.

## Current Profile
Across the current sources, Steve functions as a reflective psychology host who moves between clinical language and ordinary life. In the Claire interview, he brings personal examples of anxiety signals, body tension, shame sensitivity, family dynamics, and relationship repair. In the counselor-letter episode, he interprets listener experiences while repeatedly warning against turning psychological insight into self-surveillance or replacing feeling and body with endless reasoning.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses self-observation as interview material, including facial tension, urgency, shallow breathing, and bodily release.
- Repeatedly asks how anxiety appears in family, eldercare, death anxiety, intimate relationships, and social uncertainty.
- Frames the episode around making anxiety concrete enough to name, de-escalate, and approach gently.
- Uses listener letters to distinguish trauma-linked safety adaptations from moral weakness or personality defect.
- Treats body, feeling, anger, touch, and self-care as necessary counterparts to rational explanation.
- Keeps therapy and psychological vocabulary bounded by comfort, safety, and actual care.
