# 柴荣 / Emperor Shizong of Later Zhou

Chai Rong / 柴荣 is the Later Zhou ruler whose reign makes [[ZhaoKuangyin|赵匡胤]] politically consequential in [[169-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-shang-957052909]]. The episode says Chai Rong appreciates Zhao Kuangyin, uses him in battle, and later has him reform the禁军 after the Battle of Gaoping.

The source also makes Chai Rong a tragic succession figure. He has to balance [[ZhangYongde|张永德]], [[LiChongjin|李重进]], and Zhao Kuangyin, responds to the "点检做天子" rumor by removing Zhang Yongde, and dies leaving a young son. That combination turns capable rule into a fragile inheritance problem.

[[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]] adds Chai Rong to the wider [[FiveDynastiesTenKingdoms|五代十国]] frame. Wang Hongjie presents him as an unusually capable second-generation ruler in a period defined by [[WudaiSuccessionCrisis|王二代]] failures, and also notes his later folk-memory connection to wealth and财神 worship.
