# Louis Moreau Gottschalk

## Overview

Louis Moreau Gottschalk appears in the episode as an American composer whose 1869 Rio visit and anthem-inspired works gave the [[BrazilianNationalAnthem]] a wider musical afterlife.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Gottschalk as evidence that the anthem's tune became more than ceremonial state music. His "Grand Triumphal Fantasy" based on the anthem entered Brazilian musical memory and later acquired political resonance on the left, extending the anthem's afterlife through concert performance and reinterpretation.

## Key Characteristics

- American composer who visited Rio in 1869 in the episode's account.
- Wrote music inspired by Brazil's anthem.
- Associated with "Grand Triumphal Fantasy" as a lasting Brazilian musical reference.
- Shows the anthem's movement from state ceremony into broader performance culture.
- Later political resonance makes him part of [[NationalAnthemPoliticalPlasticity]].
