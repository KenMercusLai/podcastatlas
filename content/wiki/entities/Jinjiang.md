# Jinjiang / 晋江

## Overview
Jinjiang / 晋江 is the Quanzhou-region manufacturing cluster represented in the wiki through Minnan maritime-diaspora history and the Chendai sportswear branch that produced major Chinese footwear and sportswear brands.

## Current Profile
The current bounded sources treat Jinjiang as accumulated regional capacity rather than a sudden reform-era exception. [[no-207-minnan-wangshi-zhongshen-renjian-banshichu-zhanghai-shengzhong-wanguo-shang-992579918]] connects Jinjiang's shoe, apparel, ceramics, plumbing, building-materials, bathroom-fixture, and food industries to [[MinnanMaritimeCommercialCulture]], overseas Chinese houses, returned capital, labor, and local division of work.

[[500-jinjiang-chendaizhen-wangshi-huangziyi-tan-gaikai-jincheng-zhong-de-zhongguo-yundong-pinpai-lswtplovdbo4pl52yoxu6rgzyal2]] makes the sportswear branch more specific. It follows [[ChendaiTown|陈埭镇]] from "three idle" resources, Ding-clan networks, informal finance, Nike-linked process learning, and quality crises into [[AntaSports|Anta]], [[Xtep]], [[361Degrees]], and [[QiaodanSports]] as brand outcomes of the [[JinjiangModel]].

## Key Characteristics
- Links older Minnan maritime and diaspora networks to modern private manufacturing.
- Uses overseas Chinese remittances, houses, and hometown ties as industrial seed resources.
- Contains Chendai's footwear cluster as its most developed sportswear case.
- Relies on kinship, acquaintance trust, and informal finance during early firm formation.
- Combines local-government tolerance with later quality-control correction after reputation shocks.
- Moves from workshop production and subcontracting toward domestic brand building and listed-company scale.

## Evidence
- Diaspora-continuity evidence: [[no-207-minnan-wangshi-zhongshen-renjian-banshichu-zhanghai-shengzhong-wanguo-shang-992579918]] frames Jinjiang as the modern manufacturing continuation of Minnan migration, qiaopi, fanzi buildings, and overseas Chinese institutions.
- Cluster-breadth evidence: [[no-207-minnan-wangshi-zhongshen-renjian-banshichu-zhanghai-shengzhong-wanguo-shang-992579918]] broadens Jinjiang beyond shoes to apparel, ceramics, plumbing, building materials, bathroom fixtures, and food.
- Chendai sportswear evidence: [[500-jinjiang-chendaizhen-wangshi-huangziyi-tan-gaikai-jincheng-zhong-de-zhongguo-yundong-pinpai-lswtplovdbo4pl52yoxu6rgzyal2]] traces the shoe cluster through three idle resources, clan halls, Nike's early China manufacturing, and reform-era township enterprise tolerance.
- Brand-evolution evidence: [[500-jinjiang-chendaizhen-wangshi-huangziyi-tan-gaikai-jincheng-zhong-de-zhongguo-yundong-pinpai-lswtplovdbo4pl52yoxu6rgzyal2]] connects Jinjiang to Anta, Xtep, 361 Degrees, Qiaodan Sports, quality scandals, inventory cycles, and later performance-running upgrades.

## Qualifications
The sources do not provide a complete administrative, ethnic, religious, or firm-by-firm history of Jinjiang. The Chendai episode centers sportswear and may overrepresent footwear relative to Jinjiang's wider industrial base. Nike timing, firm counts, and local-government slogans remain source-scoped.
