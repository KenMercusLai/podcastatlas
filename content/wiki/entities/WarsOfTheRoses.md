# Wars of the Roses / 玫瑰战争

The [[WarsOfTheRoses|Wars of the Roses / 玫瑰战争]] enter the wiki through [[31-bing-yu-huo-zhi-ge-suiran-cong-bu-zicheng-lishi-594509848]] as the strongest historical analogy for the political core of [[ASongOfIceAndFire|《冰与火之歌》 / A Song of Ice and Fire]]. The source describes the conflict as an English succession struggle around Lancaster, York, weak kingship, factional nobles, marriage politics, and dynastic settlement.

The episode uses the War of the Roses cluster to explain why Martin's noble-family conflict feels historically plausible without being a direct copy. It points to Lancaster/Lannister name resemblance, Henry VI and Margaret of Anjou, Richard of York's protector role and death, Edward IV's youthful military charisma, Warwick as "kingmaker," the [[PrincesInTheTower|Princes in the Tower / 塔中王子案]], and Henry Tudor's red-white rose settlement.

The page is useful mainly as a comparison engine. The source repeatedly warns that [[HistoricalFantasySourceLayering]] works by splitting traits, scenes, and political structures across fictional families and characters rather than by mapping one historical house onto one fantasy house.
