## Overview

冯信 / Feng Xin enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-542-zhichang-xinlixue-shengzhi-hou-weihe-geng-rongyi-zao-paiji-lua_jsiusjojpwlwlroehw7jebgm|Hanji 542]] as a Western Han official paired with [[ChenXianWesternHan|陈贤]] in the episode's account of friendship, promotion rivalry, and later removal around [[ZhaiFangjinWesternHan|翟方进]]. He is less individually developed than Chen Xian, but he matters as the second former friend whose conduct convinces Zhai that old relationships have become hostile.

## Current Profile

Feng Xin is introduced as a senior official who, like Chen Xian, stands above Zhai Fangjin in earlier official chronology but loses the Yushi Dafu opportunity to Zhai. The source treats this shared disappointment as part of the relationship imbalance after Zhai's promotion.

During Zhai's danger, Feng does not function as the supportive old friend Zhai expects. The episode groups Feng with Chen in the failure to help and in the later self-protection logic after Zhai survives. Zhai's counterattack then targets both men through their association with Chen Tang and the claim that they gained rank through improper recommendation.

## Key Characteristics

- Feng Xin is a Western Han official presented as senior to Zhai Fangjin before Zhai's rise.
- He belongs to the same close-relationship field as Chen Xian and Zhai before the promotion rupture.
- His role is mostly paired: the episode treats him with Chen as an older rival disappointed by Zhai's ascent.
- He fails to protect Zhai during the inquiry, helping turn old friendship into open hostility.
- After Zhai survives, Feng shares Chen's self-protection exposure because retaliation becomes predictable.
- His removal is tied to the accusation that Chen Tang's praise helped him obtain office improperly.
