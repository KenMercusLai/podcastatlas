# Pershing Square

## Overview
Pershing Square is [[BillAckman]]'s investment firm, presented in the All-In interview as an activist and long-term owner whose current portfolio and vehicles express Ackman's focus on quality, AI-aware risk, and permanent-capital compounding.

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Pershing Square has moved from needing public campaigns to gain management attention toward a reputation-backed model where companies often welcome it as a shareholder. The firm is described as invested in AI-adjacent public incumbents, able to engage constructively when needed, and connected to three ways for outsiders to align with Ackman's strategy.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates as an activist investor, but activism can mean board support and constructive shareholder pressure rather than only public confrontation.
- Holds AI-exposed public companies including [[Microsoft]], [[Meta]], and [[Amazon]] in the source discussion.
- Uses business quality, moat durability, and disruption risk as core filters.
- Manages or benefits from permanent-capital vehicles that can compound fees or capital over long horizons.
- Uses [[HowardHughesHoldings]] as a vehicle for a Berkshire-style compounding strategy.

## Qualifications
The source is Ackman's own public explanation of Pershing Square's strategy, not a third-party performance audit. Claims about historical compounding, discounts, future AUM growth, and vehicle economics remain source-scoped and investment-opinion claims.
