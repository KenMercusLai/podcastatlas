# Harold Holt

## Overview

Harold Holt was an Australian Liberal prime minister whose short tenure is presented through continuity with Menzies, strong Vietnam alignment, and his unexplained disappearance while swimming in December 1967.

## Current Profile

[[188-australian-prime-ministers-harold-holt-malcolm-fraser-part-2-glt4635125602]] describes Holt as a Menzies protege, former labour minister and treasurer, and pragmatic politician formed during Australia's postwar boom. As prime minister he tripled Australia's Vietnam commitment and publicly aligned himself with [[LyndonBJohnson|Lyndon B. Johnson]], before vanishing at Cheviot Beach. The source treats accidental drowning as the credible explanation and later submarine, Viet Cong, and murder stories as implausible conspiracy theories.

## Key Characteristics

- Liberal successor to Robert Menzies after long ministerial service.
- Pragmatic economic administrator associated with a boom period.
- Strong supporter of the [[UnitedStates|United States]] and the Vietnam War.
- Disappeared while swimming at Cheviot Beach in December 1967.
- Became the subject of conspiracy theories and unusual memorial culture.

- Disappearance and afterlife: [[188-australian-prime-ministers-harold-holt-malcolm-fraser-part-2-glt4635125602]] contrasts drowning with unsupported abduction and assassination theories and notes the Harold Holt Memorial Swimming Centre.

## Qualifications

This is a selective one-source profile, not a full assessment of Holt's domestic policy or government. No body was recovered, but absence of recovery does not make the episode's conspiracy theories evidentially equal to accidental drowning; the exact intentions behind the swimming-centre memorial also remain lightly treated.

## What Changed

- Established Holt as the alliance-focused bridge from Menzies to the unstable succession of the late 1960s.
- Separated the likely drowning from the more memorable but unsupported conspiracy theories.
