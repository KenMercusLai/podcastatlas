# Fabletics

## Overview
Fabletics appears in the source as an apparel brand using existing best-selling silhouettes as the base for licensed U.S. university logo products.

## Current Profile
The episode makes Fabletics the clearest example of [[LogoSwapMerchandiseStandardization]]. Instead of building a new product line for every school, the company can reuse fabrics, fits, sizes, production processes, and warehousing, then change the university name, color, and logo for each licensed program.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses existing apparel patterns as the operating base for college-logo products.
- Benefits from standardized T-shirts, hoodies, and related sportswear categories.
- Scales school-by-school assortment by changing marks and colors more than product architecture.
- Depends on university approval and licensing terms before marks can be used.
- Still faces demand concentration because nationally recognized sports schools drive much of the volume.
