## Overview

灌夫 / Guan Fu is a Western Han court figure whose denunciation of [[TianFenWesternHan|田蚡]] at a banquet becomes, in the current bounded evidence, the trigger for a larger conflict that also destroys his defender [[DouYingWesternHan|窦婴]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-265-dongchaotingbian-ruhe-yanbian-cheng-jieduandazhan-2-lvu23osgv46nn3rbgque1mbhmpgw|Hanji 265]] does not supply a full biography of Guan Fu. It treats his insult of Tian Fen at Empress Dowager Wang's marriage banquet as an offense that [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] could have regarded as limited, but which becomes lethal once Dou Ying's rescue attempt enters a court struggle between declining Dou-family influence and Wang-family protection.

The episode says Guan Fu receives clan extermination. His role is therefore less a standalone legal case in this source than the initial node in a chain linking public insult, factional defense, maternal pressure, and unequal access to sovereign protection.

## Key Characteristics

- Ally of Dou Ying in the episode's court-conflict sequence.
- Publicly attacks or insults Tian Fen at a politically sensitive banquet.
- Serves as the immediate object of Dou Ying's unsuccessful defense.
- Receives collective punishment extending beyond the individual.

## Evidence

- Banquet conflict: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-265-dongchaotingbian-ruhe-yanbian-cheng-jieduandazhan-2-lvu23osgv46nn3rbgque1mbhmpgw|Hanji 265]] identifies Guan Fu's attack on Tian Fen as the opening offense in the closing phase of the case.
- Alliance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-265-dongchaotingbian-ruhe-yanbian-cheng-jieduandazhan-2-lvu23osgv46nn3rbgque1mbhmpgw|Hanji 265]] says Dou Ying risks his own position in an effort to save Guan Fu.
- Punishment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-265-dongchaotingbian-ruhe-yanbian-cheng-jieduandazhan-2-lvu23osgv46nn3rbgque1mbhmpgw|Hanji 265]] reports that Han Wudi orders Guan Fu's clan exterminated.

## Qualifications

The supplied source gives only the end of the dispute. It does not establish Guan Fu's broader career, the exact wording and legal classification of his conduct, the membership covered by the reported clan punishment, or the complete earlier causes of his conflict with Tian Fen. The episode's claim that the insult alone was a small matter is an interpretive judgment, not a legal finding reproduced here as fact.

## What Changed

- Created the canonical entity around Guan Fu's banquet confrontation, alliance with Dou Ying, and collective punishment.
