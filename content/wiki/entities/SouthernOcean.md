## Overview
The Southern Ocean appears in [[the-continent-nobody-owns-everyone-benefits-from-summer-school]] as the ocean system around [[Antarctica]] that helps make the continent economically valuable even without ordinary markets.

## Current Profile
In the source, the Southern Ocean matters through both market and non-market channels. It supports Antarctic fisheries, but its larger role is climate regulation: absorbing carbon dioxide and helping stabilize planetary conditions alongside Antarctica's reflective ice and stored land ice.

## Key Characteristics
- It is part of the Antarctic value system rather than a separate commercial setting in the episode.
- Its carbon absorption makes it central to [[AntarcticClimateServices]].
- Its fisheries are one of the smaller direct market values in the source's Antarctic valuation.
- Its protection is tied to [[TrustBasedCommonsGovernance]] because benefits and damage cross national boundaries.

## Evidence
- Climate role: [[the-continent-nobody-owns-everyone-benefits-from-summer-school]] names Southern Ocean carbon absorption as a major climate-regulating service.
- Fisheries role: [[the-continent-nobody-owns-everyone-benefits-from-summer-school]] estimates Antarctic fisheries value at about $370 million per year.
- Governance role: [[the-continent-nobody-owns-everyone-benefits-from-summer-school]] links protection of Antarctica and the Southern Ocean to a proposed trust-like approach.

## Qualifications
The source does not provide a full ecological account of Southern Ocean species, circulation, or legal regimes. Its evidence is narrowed to the economic-valuation frame of the episode.
