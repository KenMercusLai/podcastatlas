# 聂英 / Nie Ying

聂英 / Nie Ying appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]] as [[NieZheng|聂政]]'s sister. After Han authorities display the unidentified assassin's body in the market, she publicly recognizes him and then dies beside him.

The source interprets her action through [[AristocraticHonorOverLife]]. Nie Zheng had destroyed his face to protect her from implication, but Nie Ying refuses to let his name disappear because she values his reputation and family honor above her own survival. The page is source-scoped and records the episode's honor reading rather than a full biography.
