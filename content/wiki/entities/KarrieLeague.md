# Karrie League

## Overview
Karrie League is the co-founder of [[AlamoDrafthouseCinema]] and the former microbiology graduate student who helped build its theaters, restaurant operations, financial survival, and locally rooted company model.

## Current Profile
The source presents League as a full operating partner rather than a secondary participant in Tim League's founder story. She reupholstered most seats in the first theater, learned restaurant service and management before the Austin launch, helped run the business without staff in Bakersfield, and articulated the distinction between short-term profit and long-term brand investment during the franchise dispute.

Her account emphasizes that sustained labor did not act alone: family financing, suppliers, distributors, Austin supporters, and luck made the second attempt possible. The source says she sold her stake in the later Sony transaction and now manages the Baker Center creative center in Austin.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines physical startup labor with restaurant and management learning.
- Frames unusual cinema events as long-horizon brand investment, not mere inefficiency.
- Supports local autonomy inside a connected cinema network.
- Credits community assistance and luck alongside years of intensive work.
- Helps interpret the first theater's failure as evidence about location, licensing, and demand.
