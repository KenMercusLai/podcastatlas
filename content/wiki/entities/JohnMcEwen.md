# John McEwen

## Overview

John McEwen was Australia's short-term caretaker prime minister after Harold Holt's disappearance and before John Gorton became Liberal leader.

## Current Profile

[[188-australian-prime-ministers-harold-holt-malcolm-fraser-part-2-glt4635125602]] uses McEwen mainly as a constitutional and coalition bridge. He held office for only a few weeks while the Liberal Party chose a successor, placing continuity between [[HaroldHolt|Holt's]] disappearance and [[JohnGorton|Gorton's]] accession above any independent governing program.

## Key Characteristics

- Country Party leader within the governing coalition.
- Caretaker prime minister after Holt disappeared.
- Served only for the interval needed to settle Liberal succession.
- Receives little policy treatment in the episode.
