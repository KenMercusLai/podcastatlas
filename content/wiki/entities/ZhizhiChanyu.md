## Overview

郅支单于 / Zhizhi Chanyu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-570-xinyang-buke-queshi-yingxiong-burong-dihui-lrrcbw5rrurytgjxasbp12buf82l|Hanji 570]] as the Xiongnu ruler killed by [[ChenTangWesternHan|陈汤]] and [[GanYanshouWesternHan|甘延寿]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-491-zhongguo-waijiao-zui-qiangying-de-shike-jing-shi-zai-xihan-lpgajp_vky9dx7jvnoahaxdfjflk|Hanji 491]] supplies the immediate public aftermath: his head is sent to Chang'an, the commanders ask that it be displayed as a warning, and [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] orders a ten-day display before burial. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-496-qiong-pa-le-jiemi-tanguan-zouxiang-tanfu-de-xinli-youyin-lnskbcqvjhtpfi4ikq44kpbbfnol|Hanji 496]] adds the immediate reward-dispute layer: [[LiuXiang|刘向]] argues that killing Zhizhi Chanyu repaired Han prestige and affected frontier diplomacy enough that the commanders' faults should not block recognition.

## Current Profile

The sources present Zhizhi Chanyu as a defiant ruler whose death restored Han prestige and ended a humiliation burden in memorial rhetoric. Hanji 491 adds that the death is turned into state communication: the head display, court announcement, amnesty, and victory banquet make him the object through which Han advertises deterrence across distance. His role is therefore mostly relational: he is the opponent whose defeat made Chen Tang and Gan Yanshou's service extraordinary enough that both the initial reward dispute and later under-reward/disgrace argument became politically meaningful.

The source summary uses the form "置之单于"; this page normalizes the route to the historically standard 郅支单于 while preserving the exact naming issue as a source-scoped qualification.

## Key Characteristics

- Xiongnu ruler used in the source as the defeated opponent in a major Western Han frontier victory.
- Defeated opponent whose head display makes the victory a public warning in Chang'an.
- His killing anchors Geng Yu's argument that Chen Tang and Gan Yanshou performed extraordinary service.
- His killing anchors Liu Xiang's earlier argument that large frontier merit should outweigh smaller faults.
- Functions as a prestige and deterrence marker in the episode rather than as a fully developed biography.
- Normalized here from a likely transcription or summary variant to the standard historical name.

## Qualifications

This page is source-bounded and does not reconstruct Zhizhi Chanyu's full career, Xiongnu succession politics, western movement, or the complete campaign record. Hanji 491 uses the defeat as victory announcement and deterrence theater; Hanji 496 and Hanji 570 use the defeat primarily for Han court reward and memory arguments. The route normalization should be revisited if a future source preserves a genuinely distinct "置之单于" identity.
