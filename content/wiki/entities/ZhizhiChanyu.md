## Overview

郅支单于 / Zhizhi Chanyu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-570-xinyang-buke-queshi-yingxiong-burong-dihui-lrrcbw5rrurytgjxasbp12buf82l|Hanji 570]] as the Xiongnu ruler killed by [[ChenTangWesternHan|陈汤]] and [[GanYanshouWesternHan|甘延寿]]. The episode uses his defeat less as an independent Xiongnu biography than as the military event behind a later Han court argument over heroic merit.

## Current Profile

The source presents Zhizhi Chanyu as a defiant ruler whose death restored Han prestige and ended a humiliation burden in the memorial's rhetoric. His role is therefore mostly relational: he is the opponent whose defeat made Chen Tang and Gan Yanshou's service extraordinary enough that later under-reward and disgrace became politically dangerous.

The source summary uses the form "置之单于"; this page normalizes the route to the historically standard 郅支单于 while preserving the exact naming issue as a source-scoped qualification.

## Key Characteristics

- Xiongnu ruler used in the source as the defeated opponent in a major Western Han frontier victory.
- His killing anchors Geng Yu's argument that Chen Tang and Gan Yanshou performed extraordinary service.
- Functions as a prestige and deterrence marker in the episode rather than as a fully developed biography.
- Normalized here from a likely transcription or summary variant to the standard historical name.
