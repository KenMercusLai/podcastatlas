# EVO2

EVO2 is the research project [[AmyWebb]] names in [[tech-20260106-0106-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260106-0106-mp-tech-pod-128]] while explaining [[GenerativeBiology]]. In the source, it represents a workflow where data can be used to generate a new molecule or genome, analogous to how generative AI can produce text, code, or media.

The page is source-scoped: EVO2 is used as an example of biological design becoming more programmable, not as a full technical dossier. Its main wiki role is to connect [[GenerativeBiology]] to [[AIForScience]], [[AIDrugDiscoveryPlatform]], and [[ScientificDiscoveryAutomation]].
