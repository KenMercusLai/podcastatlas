# Guo Lanying

## Overview
Guo Lanying is synthesized here as a Chinese opera singer and national-treasure performer whose life connected poverty, harsh training, revolutionary opera, and state cultural memory.

## Current Profile
In [[out-numbered-ais-contentious-maths-milestone-6aa3ce622e8bb8424c920cfb]], [[AnneRowe]] presents Guo as a "People's Artist" honored by China in 2019 and remembered above all for "My Motherland." The episode emphasizes both the brutality of her childhood training and her identification with revolutionary opera, especially [[TheWhiteHairedGirl]], where she saw performance as participation in revolution and as a way to sing about women's repression.

## Key Characteristics
- Opera singer treated by the source as a Chinese national treasure.
- Recipient of the 2019 "People's Artist" honor in the episode's account.
- Associated with [[TheWhiteHairedGirl]], revolutionary opera, and the signature song [[MyMotherlandSong|"My Motherland"]].
- Her early training is described as coercive and physically brutal.
- Her public legacy joins artistic achievement, revolutionary identity, and mass audience recognition.

## Evidence
- State honor and signature song - [[out-numbered-ais-contentious-maths-milestone-6aa3ce622e8bb8424c920cfb]] says Guo was given the People's Artist title in 2019 and that "My Motherland" became her signature song.
- Revolutionary opera identity - [[out-numbered-ais-contentious-maths-milestone-6aa3ce622e8bb8424c920cfb]] says Guo loved [[TheWhiteHairedGirl]] and valued the heroine's role because it let her sing about women's repression.
- Harsh apprenticeship - [[out-numbered-ais-contentious-maths-milestone-6aa3ce622e8bb8424c920cfb]] describes sale into opera training at age four, beatings, and winter vocal training on frozen lakes.
