# 魏兹 / Wei Zi (late Han)

## Overview

魏兹 / Wei Zi is an early [[CaoCao|曹操]] supporter in [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]]'s [[Chenliu|陈留]] orbit. The current sources place him on both sides of Cao Cao's first independent mobilization: Hanji 1104 credits his private resources with helping form the initial force, while Hanji 953 places him in the failed westward attack where he dies.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1104 presents Wei Zi as more than an officer dispatched at the moment of battle. It says he exhausted his wealth to support Cao Cao, supplied money and people, and helped recruit roughly five thousand soldiers at Chenliu. That makes his backing a bridge between Zhang Miao's political shelter and Cao Cao's first usable military organization.

Hanji 953 then shows Zhang Miao sending Wei Zi to reinforce Cao Cao when the wider [[AntiDongZhuoCoalition|反董卓联盟]] hesitates. Their small force fights [[XuRongLateHan|徐荣]] at [[BianshuiBattleLateHan|汴水之战]] and is defeated; Hanji 1104 adds that Wei Zi is killed there. His role therefore combines early investor-like support, field participation, and personal exposure to the project's first major defeat.

## Key Characteristics

- Early financial and manpower supporter of Cao Cao's Chenliu mobilization.
- Actor embedded in Zhang Miao's political and military network.
- Participant in Cao Cao's first direct westward action against Dong Zhuo's forces.
- Supporter whose risk extends from private resources to battlefield presence.
- Casualty of the Bianshui defeat whose son is said to continue the family alignment.

## Evidence

- Initial mobilization: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1104-caocao-banbu-qiuxianling-xiyin-le-duoshao-dingji-rencai-lvrsdp13uv-80kzmwawnqwonkyny|Hanji 1104]] credits Wei Zi with money, people, and support for recruiting roughly five thousand soldiers.
- Coalition exception: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-953-luanshi-zhizhong-yaobuyao-jiangjiu-renyi-lrzfimgqatjxyw9zx9ry2kiwxkon|Hanji 953]] shows Zhang Miao sending Wei Zi when most coalition leaders refuse Cao Cao's call to advance.
- Death and continuity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1104-caocao-banbu-qiuxianling-xiyin-le-duoshao-dingji-rencai-lvrsdp13uv-80kzmwawnqwonkyny|Hanji 1104]] says Wei Zi dies at Bianshui and that his son later continues to follow Cao Cao.

## Qualifications

The source set is small and popular-historical. The exact size of the recruited force, the claim that Wei Zi exhausted all his wealth, and the son's later service require narrower textual verification. Hanji 953 calls him an officer under Zhang Miao, while Hanji 1104 foregrounds private investment; these roles can coexist, but the sources do not fully reconstruct their chronology.

## What Changed

- Added Wei Zi's pre-battle role in financing and recruiting Cao Cao's initial Chenliu force.
- Added his death at Bianshui and the reported continuation of support through his son.
- Reframed him from a small reinforcement figure into an early resource-and-risk partner.

## Relationships

- [[ZhangMiaoLateHan]] - Chenliu patron and superior/network node who sends Wei Zi west.
- [[Chenliu]] - mobilization environment in which Wei Zi's resources become an army.
- [[BianshuiBattleLateHan]] - battle where Wei Zi's support ends in defeat and death.
- [[XuRongLateHan]] - opposing commander whose force defeats the Cao-Wei contingent.
- [[AntiDongZhuoCoalition]] - hesitant alliance field that makes Wei Zi's participation exceptional.
