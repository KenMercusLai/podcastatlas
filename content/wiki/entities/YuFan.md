# 虞翻 / Yu Fan

## Overview
虞翻 / Yu Fan is a Kuaiji merit officer and adviser whose source role is unusually consistent: he speaks from a clear reading of conditions, whether warning Wang Lang, remonstrating with Sun Ce, or persuading Hua Xin.

## Current Profile
The current evidence makes Yu Fan a practical speech actor rather than only a local official. He sees asymmetry early, survives the transfer from Wang Lang to [[SunCe|孙策]], warns against leader exposure, and in Hanji 1039 becomes the messenger who turns Sun Ce's military advantage into [[HuaXin|华歆]]'s surrender.

## Key Characteristics
- Reads battlefield and political asymmetry before senior actors accept it.
- Moves from Wang Lang's Kuaiji office network into Sun Ce's retained staff.
- Uses remonstrance to translate Sun Ce's personal habits into political-security risk.
- Executes force-backed persuasion by giving Hua Xin face while making refusal materially irrational.
- Appears in Hanji 1039 through the transcript form "于藩," normalized here to 虞翻.

## Evidence
- Asymmetry warning: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1004-caocao-yingfeng-hanxiandi-shi-shui-de-zhuyi-lrrga-flmx5ajnhu1c42x7kooe94|Hanji 1004]] has Yu Fan warn Wang Lang that Sun Ce is too strong to meet frontally.
- Retained adviser role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1005-sunce-zhuding-zaoshi-shi-xingge-jueding-mingyun-lpqnere2cwcrngggyezd-bsqahx4|Hanji 1005]] says Sun Ce keeps Yu Fan after taking Kuaiji and treats him familiarly.
- Personal-security remonstrance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1005-sunce-zhuding-zaoshi-shi-xingge-jueding-mingyun-lpqnere2cwcrngggyezd-bsqahx4|Hanji 1005]] uses Yu Fan's warning about solo hunting and light clothing to build the leader-exposure lesson.
- Surrender persuasion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] has Yu Fan pressure Hua Xin by comparing Yuzhang's resources with Kuaiji and invoking Sun Ce's record.
