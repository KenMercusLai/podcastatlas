NRA v. Vullo appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-under-secretary-of-state-sarah-b-rogers-on-dismantling-the-censorship-industrial-complex-39828360]] through [[SarahBRogers|Sarah B. The source uses the case as the legal anchor for [[ViewpointDebanking]] and [[IndirectRegulatoryCoercion]].
