# Madagascar / 马达加斯加

## Overview

Madagascar appears in the wiki as both an ecological island-refuge case and the political setting for [[RanavalonaI]]'s contested 19th-century rule.

## Current Profile

The ecological branch comes from the [[LastChanceToSee|Last Chance to See]] episode: [[DouglasAdams]] and [[MarkCarwardine]] travel to Madagascar to find the [[AyeAye|aye-aye]], making the island a former no-human refuge that later needs smaller human-made refuges after habitat pressure and human arrival change the baseline.

The historical branch comes from [[TheRestIsHistory]]. [[RanavalonaI]] turns Madagascar into a monarchy and anti-European resistance case. The episode qualifies hostile European and missionary depictions of her as mad or purely evil, but still links her reign to Christian persecution, tangena ordeal, executions, overwork, and harsh royal projects. Madagascar now therefore carries two different wiki problems: ecological refuge after human disruption, and the difficulty of reading anti-colonial resistance together with domestic coercion.

## Key Characteristics

- Island refuge case in the conservation-writing branch.
- Setting for the [[AyeAye|aye-aye]] encounter in [[LastChanceToSee|Last Chance to See]].
- Example of [[IslandExtinctionAfterHumanArrival]] because a former no-human refuge becomes vulnerable after human arrival.
- Monarchy and anti-European resistance setting through [[RanavalonaI]].
- Historical-memory case where hostile European sources must be qualified without erasing harsh rule.

- Ecological refuge: [[186-rang-ni-xiao-dao-tou-diao-reng-bu-wang-sikao-weida-de-xiaoshi-shijie-manyou-zhinan-998655210]] presents Madagascar as a former human-free refuge damaged after human arrival and habitat pressure.
- Focal species: [[186-rang-ni-xiao-dao-tou-diao-reng-bu-wang-sikao-weida-de-xiaoshi-shijie-manyou-zhinan-998655210]] makes the [[AyeAye|aye-aye]] the island's focal endangered-species case.
- Conservation intervention: [[186-rang-ni-xiao-dao-tou-diao-reng-bu-wang-sikao-weida-de-xiaoshi-shijie-manyou-zhinan-998655210]] uses Madagascar to show that humans can both damage habitats and build smaller protected refuges.
- Ranavalona setting: [[317-african-amazons-glt5973082485]] introduces [[RanavalonaI]] as a ruler of Madagascar whose reputation is shaped by European and missionary sources.
- Resistance and harsh rule: [[317-african-amazons-glt5973082485]] says British and French forces were pushed back from Madagascar's coast, while Ranavalona's reign involved persecution, ordeal, executions, overwork, and harsh royal projects.

## Qualifications

The wiki's Madagascar profile is source-bounded and uneven: one source is literary conservation travel, the other is a podcast segment on a ruler. It does not yet synthesize Madagascar's full ecological history, human settlement, monarchy, colonial history, language, or modern politics.
