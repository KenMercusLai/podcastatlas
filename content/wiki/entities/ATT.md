# AT&T

[[we-almost-had-a-smartphone-in-the-90s-why-did-it-fail]] adds AT&T as one of the major partners around [[GeneralMagic]]. The source uses AT&T mainly as evidence that the company had heavyweight corporate backing, while still failing to turn broad resources into a focused, demanded [[SonyMagicLink]] product.

AT&T appears in [[zhenzheng-gaibian-shijie-de-jishu-weishenme-yikaishi-dou-bu-bei-kanhao-s10e16-8c95b3dc-d75a-4bdd-84d4-2c06fd2d85b1]] as the business context behind [[BellLabs]]' early preference for [[BipolarJunctionTransistor|BJT]] devices over [[MOSFET|MOS transistors]]. The source says AT&T's communication-switching needs made speed and stability especially important.

The episode uses AT&T to explain why incumbents can reject a future-defining technology for reasons that make sense locally. MOS did not initially fit the telecom performance target, but it later fit the scaling logic of integrated circuits.
