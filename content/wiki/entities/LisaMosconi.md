# Lisa Mosconi

## Overview
Lisa Mosconi is the researcher cited in the Gottfried [[HubermanLab]] episode for work on women's brain metabolism across premenopause, perimenopause, and postmenopause.

## Current Profile
The current wiki profile is source-scoped to Gottfried's citation of Mosconi's work. Mosconi appears as an evidence reference rather than as an interview guest: the episode uses her research to support the claim that cerebral metabolism can decline across the menopause transition and that brain-fog symptoms should not be dismissed as merely subjective complaints.

## Key Characteristics
- She is used as the named research reference for menopause-transition brain metabolism.
- Her cited work supports the episode's claim that midlife hormone change can involve measurable brain-energy change.
- In this source, she functions as an evidentiary link between women's health, neuroscience, and perimenopause care.
