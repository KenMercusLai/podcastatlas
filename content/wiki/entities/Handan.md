# 邯郸 / Handan

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-15-jiu-bu-chun-yinfa-de-shijie-dazhan-li-iojxthq8komhlhyumho3yvtem]] adds the immediate prehistory of Handan's siege. [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]] attacks [[ZhaoState|赵国]] and surrounds Handan after reading [[ChuState|楚国]]'s attack on [[LuState|鲁国]] as a distraction that may keep [[ChuXuanwang|楚宣王]] from rescuing Zhao. This makes Handan the endpoint of the [[LuWineHandanWarCascade|鲁酒薄而邯郸围]] causal chain.

邯郸 / Handan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]] as the [[ZhaoState|赵国]] city under [[WeiState|魏国]] siege. The source uses the siege as the immediate reason Zhao seeks help from [[QiState|齐国]].

Handan's source role is to define the obvious rescue target that [[SunBin|孙膑]] refuses. A direct march to Handan would put Qi into the battlefield chosen by Wei; threatening [[Daliang|大梁]] instead forces Wei to move, making [[GuilingBattle|桂陵之战]] possible.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] adds the diplomatic afterlife of that siege. [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]] returns Handan to Zhao as the credibility move that lets [[WeiState|魏国]] and [[ZhaoState|赵国]] form the [[ZhangshuiAlliance|漳水之盟]] under [[QinEastwardPressure|秦国东进压力]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-18-zhongguo-lishishang-di-yi-li-rentoushui-lgqnequk0rxejqgihwdqxqyqnsvq]] adds Handan as a succession-struggle target. [[ZhaoFan|赵范]] tries to take it by surprise in 347 BCE, but fails and dies, giving the source its case for [[MilitaryActionTerminology|the "袭" category]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-74-huanyuan-wanbi-guizhao-zhenxiang-lg1irvbbc1wp8oph2kmpatfoxebk]] adds Handan as the place where [[HeShiBi|和氏璧]] appears in [[ZhaoState|赵国]] before the Qin exchange crisis. The episode says [[MiuXian|缪贤]] buys the jade there and [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] then forces it into royal possession, making Handan the court setting before [[LinXiangru|蔺相如]]'s mission to Qin.
