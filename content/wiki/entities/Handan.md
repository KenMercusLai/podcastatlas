# 邯郸 / Handan

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-15-jiu-bu-chun-yinfa-de-shijie-dazhan-li-iojxthq8komhlhyumho3yvtem]] adds the immediate prehistory of Handan's siege. [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]] attacks [[ZhaoState|赵国]] and surrounds Handan after reading [[ChuState|楚国]]'s attack on [[LuState|鲁国]] as a distraction that may keep [[ChuXuanwang|楚宣王]] from rescuing Zhao. This makes Handan the endpoint of the [[LuWineHandanWarCascade|鲁酒薄而邯郸围]] causal chain.

邯郸 / Handan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]] as the [[ZhaoState|赵国]] city under [[WeiState|魏国]] siege. The source uses the siege as the immediate reason Zhao seeks help from [[QiState|齐国]].

Handan's source role is to define the obvious rescue target that [[SunBin|孙膑]] refuses. A direct march to Handan would put Qi into the battlefield chosen by Wei; threatening [[Daliang|大梁]] instead forces Wei to move, making [[GuilingBattle|桂陵之战]] possible.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] adds the diplomatic afterlife of that siege. [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]] returns Handan to Zhao as the credibility move that lets [[WeiState|魏国]] and [[ZhaoState|赵国]] form the [[ZhangshuiAlliance|漳水之盟]] under [[QinEastwardPressure|秦国东进压力]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-18-zhongguo-lishishang-di-yi-li-rentoushui-lgqnequk0rxejqgihwdqxqyqnsvq]] adds Handan as a succession-struggle target. [[ZhaoFan|赵范]] tries to take it by surprise in 347 BCE, but fails and dies, giving the source its case for [[MilitaryActionTerminology|the "袭" category]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-74-huanyuan-wanbi-guizhao-zhenxiang-lg1irvbbc1wp8oph2kmpatfoxebk]] adds Handan as the place where [[HeShiBi|和氏璧]] appears in [[ZhaoState|赵国]] before the Qin exchange crisis. The episode says [[MiuXian|缪贤]] buys the jade there and [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] then forces it into royal possession, making Handan the court setting before [[LinXiangru|蔺相如]]'s mission to Qin.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-3-ljwz8ebk0iyfleivkefvvoonrqb9]] adds Handan as the next Qin target after the [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]]. [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] sends [[WangLing|王陵]] to attack the Zhao capital, but the campaign goes poorly enough that he wants [[BaiQi|白起]] to replace him. Bai Qi's refusal, grounded in Qin's post-Changping exhaustion, makes Handan the test case for [[WarCostTempoDiscipline|战争成本与速决纪律]] after a huge victory.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-1-lq4wvkd8qx3q7zji6fnnkxi3qcl1]] rewinds slightly and explains why Handan is both tempting and hard. The episode describes it as Zhao's high-walled, deeply defended capital, which means Qin's depleted army cannot assume an easy assault even after Changping. [[SuDai|苏代]] then turns the possible siege of Handan into [[FanJu|范雎]]'s personal worry: if [[BaiQi|白起]] takes it, Bai Qi's rank and merit could eclipse the Qin chancellor.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-1-lvqbmaabsvbdz2zibgmseidx5phe]] makes Handan the immediate survival line for Zhao in 258 BCE. After Changping, Zhao has little western buffer left, but the capital's will is hardened by mass death and common anger. [[WangLing|王陵]] fails against the city, Qin reinforcements raise casualties without creating a breakthrough, and Bai Qi treats a long-distance assault across terrain against a fortified capital as a campaign Qin should avoid.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-2-ltlobdujy-hdipimfdupvwod2ech]] keeps Handan as the endpoint that alliance politics must actually relieve. [[MaoSui|毛遂]] wins [[ChuState|楚国]]'s agreement to aid Zhao, but Chu's army would need [[WeiState|魏国]] transit, and Wei's own [[JinBi|晋鄙]] stops at [[YeWarringStates|邺城]]. Handan therefore remains endangered even after a diplomatic victory.

The same source makes Handan the setting for [[XinyuanYan|新垣衍]]'s status-submission proposal. He enters the city by a side route and tries to persuade Zhao through [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] to honor [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] as western emperor. Handan is thus not only a siege target but the place where Zhao must choose between continuing resistance and a surrender-like diplomatic exit.
