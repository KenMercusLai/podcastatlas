## Overview

燕王刘定国 / Liu Dingguo is the Western Han Yan king whose alleged sexual crimes and retaliatory killing are reported to [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] through [[ZhuFuYan|主父偃]] in Hanji 284.

## Current Profile

The episode says the brother of Feiru magistrate Yin Ren approaches Zhufu Yan after Liu Dingguo has Yin Ren killed on a fabricated charge. His report accuses Liu Dingguo of relations with his father's consort, his brother's wife, and his own daughters. Zhufu Yan forwards the case; after court deliberation, Han Wudi orders Liu Dingguo to commit suicide and abolishes the Yan kingdom.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han king of Yan and member of the imperial Liu house.
- Accused of multiple incestuous or otherwise prohibited sexual relationships.
- Accused of trying to bribe Yin Ren and then killing him through a fabricated charge.
- Dies by ordered suicide after central-court deliberation.
- His case becomes evidence of Zhufu Yan's ability to threaten princely houses.
