# Aima Technology / 爱玛

## Overview
Aima Technology / 爱玛 is a traditional Chinese electric two-wheeler maker used in the source set to represent the scale-and-value side of the market before smart-feature challengers expanded higher price bands.

## Current Profile
Aima is grouped with [[Yadea|雅迪]] as an incumbent whose historical competition centered on broad distribution, volume, and lower prices. Its recent revenue and profit decline sits inside a weakening category affected by demand pull-forward, new safety standards, and input costs, while challenger growth shows that the market is segmenting rather than moving uniformly.

## Key Characteristics
- Aima is positioned as a traditional electric two-wheeler scale manufacturer.
- Its historical market context emphasized low-price competition and broad consumer reach.
- Recent first-half revenue and profit are described as declining.
- Its operating environment includes weaker demand, regulatory redesign, and higher manufacturing costs.
- The growth of premium challengers increases pressure to differentiate beyond price and distribution.
