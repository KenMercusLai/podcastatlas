# Lucas Simon

Lucas Simon is the [[DataScienceWithSam]] guest in [[ep-8-implementation-of-ai-in-scientific-research]]. The source identifies him as leading a small data-science group at the [[TherapeuticInnovationCenter]] at [[BaylorCollegeOfMedicine]] in Houston, Texas, where the group applies data science to molecular data for early cancer therapeutics.

His main wiki contribution is a practical map of [[AIForScience]] inside biomedical research. He separates [[Bioinformatics]] from [[ComputationalBiology]], explains why [[SequencingDataPipeline|sequencing pipelines]] and [[GeneExpressionMatrix|gene expression matrices]] are difficult infrastructure objects, and argues that [[SingleCellRNASequencing]] gives [[BiomedicalDeepLearning]] a stronger data shape than many bulk RNA-seq settings.

Lucas also gives the source's tool-practice boundary. His team uses a mix of R and Python, commonly uses [[Keras]] on [[TensorFlow]], and treats modeling as only one part of the workflow alongside visualization, output analysis, and biological interpretation.
