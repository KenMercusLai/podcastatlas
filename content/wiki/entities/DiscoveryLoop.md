# DiscoveryLoop

## Overview
DiscoveryLoop is a reported Jeff Dean-linked startup interpreted in the wiki as a small-team AI-for-science and self-improving-systems bet emerging from Google AI talent movement.

## Current Profile
The entity enters the wiki as part of a Google AI brain-drain story: senior Google and Google DeepMind-adjacent talent leaving to build systems outside the hyperscaler structure. The older source frames the move as evidence that model companies, cloud platforms, and AI researchers may not remain aligned when frontier work becomes commercially valuable.

The current bounded evidence gives the startup more shape while keeping the uncertainty explicit. Zhou Jiangong describes [[JeffDean]] and long-term Google colleagues leaving after Dean's 27-year Google career, and interprets Discovery Loop as a scientific-discovery platform, likely in healthcare or life sciences, where a smaller team can pursue focus that a large organization makes harder.

## Key Characteristics
- Reported startup, not a mature public product in the current evidence.
- Strongly associated with Jeff Dean and senior Google AI talent movement.
- Interpreted as AI-for-science or scientific-discovery infrastructure rather than a generic chatbot/model company.
- Used by sources as a signal of [[GoogleDeepMindOrganizationalFriction]] and the appeal of smaller execution teams.
