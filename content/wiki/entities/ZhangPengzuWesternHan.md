## Overview

张彭祖 / Zhang Pengzu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-430-chigua-qunzhong-qianren-yu-xianren-de-gongdou-daxi-lkfcjewfhmhfgw39vqfqkpa9umzq|Hanji 430]] as [[ZhangAnshiWesternHan|张安世]]'s younger son, [[ZhangHeWesternHan|张贺]]'s adopted son, and childhood study companion of [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Zhang Pengzu as the person through whom Xuan repays Zhang He's old kindness. Xuan first grants him Guannei Hou status, then makes him Yangdu Hou as Zhang He's adopted heir.

## Key Characteristics

- Adopted heir of Zhang He.
- Childhood study companion of the future Emperor Xuan in the episode.
- Reward recipient because of Zhang He's old favor to Xuan.
- Example of gratitude being transferred across family lines.
