# Hidden Potential

## Overview
*Hidden Potential* is [[AdamGrant]]'s book framing personal development around learning capacity, motivation, opportunity, character skills, and progress through obstacles rather than initial talent alone.

## Current Profile
In the interview, the book supplies the umbrella for three character skills: embracing discomfort, absorbing useful knowledge like a sponge, and becoming an imperfectionist. These skills matter because a person's starting point does not reveal the quality of their future learning environment, feedback, persistence, or rate of improvement.

The book's frame is contextual rather than purely individual. [[GrowthMindset]] needs scaffolding and a culture where experimentation is credible, while jobs and environments may themselves be redesigned through task changes, swaps, or subtraction.

## Key Characteristics
- Measures promise through developmental trajectory rather than early performance.
- Joins motivation and opportunity to skill rather than reducing achievement to talent.
- Treats discomfort as a learning condition when support and a viable path exist.
- Uses sponge-like learning to emphasize receptivity to advice and outside perspective.
- Recasts imperfectionism as selective standards and willingness to complete or experiment.

## Evidence
- Core premise - [[how-to-unlock-your-potential-motivation-unique-abilities-dr-adam-grant-scim2670765142]] says people underestimate potential when they overvalue starting ability.
- Character skills - [[how-to-unlock-your-potential-motivation-unique-abilities-dr-adam-grant-scim2670765142]] identifies discomfort, sponge-like learning, and imperfectionism as development tools.
- Context - [[how-to-unlock-your-potential-motivation-unique-abilities-dr-adam-grant-scim2670765142]] connects mindset to scaffolding, culture, job malleability, feedback, and opportunity.

## Qualifications
The source is an interview summary rather than the book's full text. It does not establish a general metric for potential or show that motivation and opportunity erase biological, structural, material, health, or domain-specific constraints. Grant's diving story illustrates the thesis but is not population-level evidence.
