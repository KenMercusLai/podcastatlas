# Johannesburg

## Overview
Johannesburg enters the wiki as the South African arrival city where travel safety, urban unease, and apartheid-memory institutions become immediately visible.

## Current Profile
In the episode, Johannesburg is not treated as a relaxed strolling destination. It is the place where 王妈妈 first notices high-security hotels, airport weapon checks, electric fences, downtown homelessness, and the value of end-to-end guided transport. At the same time, it anchors the historical itinerary through [[Soweto]], [[ConstitutionHillJohannesburg|Constitution Hill]], and [[ApartheidMuseumJohannesburg|the Apartheid Museum]].

The city therefore functions as a concentrated entry point into [[ComplexDestinationTravelRisk]] and [[PostApartheidStructuralInequality]]. Safety routines and museum routes are not separate from history; they are the traveler's first way of sensing how inequality, memory, urban form, and current governance problems sit together.

## Key Characteristics
- Safety-bounded urban gateway for the source's South Africa trip.
- Arrival point where security infrastructure makes crime risk emotionally concrete.
- Base for guided historical visits to [[Soweto]], [[ConstitutionHillJohannesburg|Constitution Hill]], and [[ApartheidMuseumJohannesburg|the Apartheid Museum]].
- City where downtown travel is presented as requiring route discipline rather than casual wandering.
- Urban setting where post-apartheid inequality appears through adjacent neighborhoods, homelessness, and tourist movement boundaries.
