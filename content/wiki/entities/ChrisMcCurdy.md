# Chris McCurdy

## Overview
Chris McCurdy is a medicinal chemist and [[HubermanLab]] guest whose work focuses on natural products, opioid chemistry, and especially the pharmacology and public-health risks of kratom.

## Current Profile
In [[scim3176341770-scim3176341770]], McCurdy presents kratom neither as one medicine nor one hazard. He distinguishes fresh leaf and tea from powder, extracts, concentrates, isolates, and chemically modified derivatives; explains that mitragynine and 7-hydroxymitragynine have different pharmacologic roles; and repeatedly separates traditional or anecdotal use from controlled human evidence. His practical stance combines product-specific labeling and age restrictions with research on dependence, respiratory effects, interactions, and possible therapeutic uses, while directing serious withdrawal and polypharmacy concerns toward medical care.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses medicinal chemistry to connect plant compounds, metabolites, receptors, processing, and product formulation.
- Treats kratom risk as conditional on product identity, concentration, serving size, onset, frequency, age, purpose, and co-use.
- Distinguishes whole-plant pharmacology from isolated or chemically produced compounds, especially 7-hydroxymitragynine.
- Supports science-based labeling, serving-size limits, alkaloid guardrails, and age restrictions rather than a binary good-or-bad classification.
- Marks antidepressant, alcohol-harm-reduction, aphrodisiac, respiratory-safety, and withdrawal-treatment claims as open research questions when evidence is inadequate.

## Evidence
- Product and chemistry framework - [[scim3176341770-scim3176341770]] has McCurdy distinguish fresh leaf, dried powder, extracts, concentrates, isolates, and semisynthetic products while explaining opioid, serotonergic, and adrenergic pathways.
- Safety and dependence - [[scim3176341770-scim3176341770]] has him discuss tolerance, physical dependence, delayed effects, serving-size confusion, drug interactions, developing-brain caution, respiratory depression, and naloxone reversal in animal work.
- Research discipline - [[scim3176341770-scim3176341770]] has him separate anecdote, case reports, surveys, animal findings, chemical analysis, and controlled human trials rather than treating them as interchangeable proof.
