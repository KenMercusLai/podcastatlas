[[ep236-lao-wu-kuai-qian-xinlihua-fufei-de-peiban-shi-zhende-ma-ltvuoeluoeu4-a3j3lamhc3buudx]] adds the emotion-economy and mental-health boundary branch. With [[WeiQianSanlian|魏茜]] and [[RuanJianing|阮佳宁]], the show connects post-pandemic [[EmotionEconomy|情绪经济]], [[PaidListeningServices|付费树洞 / 倾听师]], [[TactileComfortConsumption]], [[ImmediateReliefWellnessConsumption]], [[RitualHopeConsumption]], and [[AIFriendProducts|AI companionship]] to a practical question: when does paid comfort help, and when does it blur into dependency or nonprofessional crisis care?

The episode positions industrial reporting as a translation layer. Instead of treating chips only as investment symbols or geopolitical slogans, it asks what has to happen across [[SemiconductorSupplyChain|design, manufacturing, packaging, testing]], software ecosystems, and user-facing token prices before [[ComputeFreedom|算力自由]] becomes practical.
