# DARPA

DARPA appears in [[yushu-shangshi-baozhang-dan-renxing-jiqiren-de-qian-daodi-cong-nali-zhuan-s10e26-4a50d4a3-a6ff-4c89-b754-367b73ce924b]] through the source's discussion of [[BostonDynamics]]. The episode uses DARPA-linked demand to explain why early quadruped robots had a clearer initial scenario: carrying loads across difficult terrain for military or field use.

In the source, DARPA is therefore a demand-shaping institution rather than the episode's main subject. It helps show why a robot body can begin from a specific customer and terrain problem, while humanoid robots still need their own [[RobotRepurchaseDemand|repeat-demand]] proof.
