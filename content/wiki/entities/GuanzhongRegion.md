# 关中 / Guanzhong Region

关中 / Guanzhong Region first appears in the wiki as Qin's strategic grain and irrigation base around [[ZhengGuoCanal|郑国渠]] in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-104-yinmou-shisuan-rang-qinguo-bantu-you-you-you-pengzhang-le-lgnyhbduqutegtksrjwcvhmn8-t6|Qinji 104]]. In that branch, [[HanState|韩国]] tries to exhaust [[QinState|秦国]] through canal construction, but Qin's interest in Guanzhong agriculture turns the supposed burden into durable state capacity.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-132-5wan-wanbao-40wan-xiangyu-cizhan-fengshen-lk87nb3fskg5zh6fcgptypixhzuw|Qinji 132]] turns Guanzhong from Qin's capacity base into the political prize of [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]]. [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]] promises that whoever first enters Guanzhong and destroys Qin will be made king there. The source therefore makes the region not only a military target but a legitimacy object for the coming Chu-Han settlement.

Guanzhong also separates the two mission tracks in this episode. [[LiuBang|刘邦]] is sent west toward Guanzhong because older Chu generals present him as more suitable for pacifying Qin's exhausted population, while [[XiangYu|项羽]] is assigned to the [[Julu|巨鹿]] rescue under [[SongYi|宋义]] because his earlier violence makes him politically risky as a pacifier.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-139-ta-zaici-yingxiang-hanchao-lp1zujuq0o3ohxc79xqi1phpb5zo|Hanji 139]] gives the first concrete result of that assignment. Liu Bang reaches Qin's center first, spares [[ZiyingQin|子婴]], preserves local officials, returns to [[Bashang|霸上]], and announces [[YuefaSanzhang|约法三章]], causing Guanzhong people to hope he will become their king. The same episode makes [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s [[XinanMassacre|新安坑降]] the opposite route: killing surrendered Qin troops makes support inside Qin's old heartland far harder to recover.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-140-xiangyu-yu-liubang-shi-zhen-youqing-haishi-xianghu-liyong-1-lmmhoq2wo3bs6b1johpc0ixuf662|Hanji 140 part 1]] turns Guanzhong from prize and support base into immediate conflict object. Liu Bang closes the entry routes after occupying the region, while [[CaoWushang|曹无伤]] reports to [[XiangYu|项羽]] that Liu Bang intends to rule Guanzhong, install [[ZiyingQin|子婴]] as chancellor, and keep the treasures. The episode therefore makes Guanzhong the hinge between public reward promise and military confrontation before [[HongmenYan|鸿门宴]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-140-xiangyu-yu-liubang-shi-zhen-youqing-haishi-xianghu-liyong-2-lvdgirxmwxcckh2loh-yk2zzer2e|Hanji 140 part 2]] adds the counter-framing through [[XiangBo|项伯]]. For Liu Bang's side, Guanzhong is the fulfilled reward and future base; for Xiang Yu's angry camp, it looks like seizure. Xiang Bo's successful argument reframes Liu Bang's first entry as coalition-enabling merit, making an immediate attack look morally wrong even before the final settlement is decided.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-142-zhechang-juhui-xiangyu-shudiao-le-yiqie-ln7dv2jxrk6lt1c4szylsrb41k3r|Hanji 142]] adds the strategic alternative after Xiang Yu has effectively taken control. [[HanSheng|韩生]] argues that Guanzhong's terrain and fertility make it the right base for durable rule, but [[XiangYu|项羽]] rejects the advice because he wants to return east in glory. The region therefore becomes not only Liu Bang's promised prize and support base, but also the statecraft option Xiang Yu refuses after winning the immediate confrontation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-143-xiangyu-shi-ruhe-feijin-xinji-nongsi-chuhuaiwang-mixin-lstfzfvbd2h9i39f7y5dxrbxxwv2|Hanji 143]] turns Guanzhong into the territory Xiang Yu deliberately prevents [[LiuBang|刘邦]] from holding directly. Liu Bang is made Han king at [[Nanzheng|南郑]] over [[BaShu|巴蜀]] and [[Hanzhong|汉中]], while [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]], [[SimaXin|司马欣]], and [[DongYiQin|董翳]] are placed across the old Qin core as [[ThreeQinContainment|三秦压制]]. Guanzhong therefore shifts from promised reward and rejected capital-base into a managed exclusion zone inside [[ZhouQinHybridOrder|周秦混合制]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-147-pimei-zhugeliang-longzhongdui-de-hanzhongdui-lqucctzhipgtcvtjlixvj5qbdeai|Hanji 147]] turns Guanzhong from excluded prize into Han Xin's first recoverable target. In [[HanzhongDui|汉中对]], [[HanXin|韩信]] argues that Guanzhong people remember Liu Bang's non-plunder entry and [[YuefaSanzhang|约法三章]], know the [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]] should have made him king there, and resent the Three Qin rulers because of Qin wartime losses and [[XinanMassacre|新安坑降]]. The region therefore becomes a people-support and legitimacy opening, not only a geographic objective.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-148-1-hanxin-yupan-le-zhanghan-de-yupan-lodhpuxpossjno87gorjkfrtitbe|Hanji 148-1]] records the move from recoverable target to recovered operating space. [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s army breaks back toward Guanzhong through [[ChenCang|陈仓]], defeats [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]], and receives the surrender of [[SimaXin|司马欣]] and [[DongYiQin|董翳]]. The episode's emphasis on [[HanInheritsQinSystem|military-system inheritance]] keeps the recovery from being only a legitimacy story: Guanzhong opens because local resentment, institutional preparation, and operational deception converge.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-149-xichu-bawang-xiangyu-weihe-shijun-2-lpohbb3prsgvokpjipfd7i1ntiwn|Hanji 149 part 2]] shows Guanzhong becoming a working rear base rather than only a recovered prize. Liu Bang moves the capital to [[LiyangQinHan|栎阳]], takes [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]] and [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]], repairs Qin frontier works against Xiongnu pressure, and leaves [[XiaoHe|萧何]] and [[HanXin|韩信]] as the civil-military anchors of the rear.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-155-1-lishi-jizai-shenme-qingkuang-hui-fasheng-ren-chi-ren-canju-lrwz-prnxd8hzchagyrbywkux7lh|Hanji 155-1]] adds the rear base's disaster side. While Han finishes [[Feiqiu|废丘]] and turns former Yong territory into commanderies, Guanzhong suffers a catastrophic famine. The source explains the famine through [[WartimeAgriculturalLaborCollapse|战争抽空农业劳动力]]: Chu-Han warfare removes young labor from fields, grain prices explode, Liu Bang lets people move toward [[BaShu|蜀郡]] and [[Hanzhong|汉中]], and [[RenShiQinMerchant|任氏]]'s grain fortune becomes a case for [[PowerBackedGrainHoarding|权力保护下的乱世囤粮]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-170-liubang-weisha-cong-luoyang-banjia-dao-changan-2-lpqtexvxjrbrpjgbw5hrgmhl9apk|Hanji 170 part 2]] makes Guanzhong the formal capital base for [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]]. [[LouJing|楼敬]] argues that if eastern territories rebel, holding the Qin heartland still preserves a defensible and fertile core. [[ZhangLiang|张良]] adds the full logistical picture: passes and mountains guard the region, Ba-Shu and northern resources feed it, and the Yellow River / Wei River network can move tribute, grain, troops, and supplies between [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] and the eastern field.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-180-xihan-diyi-yinghan-guangao-de-gushi-1-li5u-dmsmb1lp4ny3rk02kaiyu4k|Hanji 180]] adds the population and elite-control follow-through to that capital choice. After reporting that Xiongnu-linked groups can raid toward Guanzhong quickly, [[LouJing|刘敬 / 楼敬]] proposes moving six-state royal descendants, local magnates, and famous people into the region. Guanzhong is therefore not only selected as a defensive capital base; it must be populated and politically managed through [[GuanzhongEliteRelocation|关中豪强迁徙]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] adds a much later late-Han use of Guanzhong as a rear-stability problem for [[CaoCao|曹操]]. [[XunYu|荀彧]] argues that the western generals are fragmented and likely to preserve themselves, then recommends [[ZhongYao|钟繇]] to soothe the region. Zhong Yao's letters persuade [[MaTeng|马腾]], [[HanSui|韩遂]], and related forces to submit and send sons to the court, making Guanzhong calm enough for Cao Cao to face the eastern and northern sequence around [[LyuBu|吕布]] and [[YuanShao|袁绍]].

This page uses the `GuanzhongRegion` key to avoid colliding with [[GuanZhong|管仲 / Guan Zhong]], the Spring and Autumn minister.
