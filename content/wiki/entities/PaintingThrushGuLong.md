# 《画眉鸟》 / The Thrush

## Overview

《画眉鸟》 is a [[GuLong|古龙]] novel in the [[ChuLiuxiang|楚留香]] sequence. The bounded episode treats it as a linked hostage plot, assassination trail, formation puzzle, poisoning-belief mystery, and tragic love story culminating in conflict with [[ShuiMuYinJi|水母阴姬]].

## Current Profile

李玉函 and 柳无眉 draw 楚留香 toward 拥翠山庄 by controlling information about 苏蓉蓉、李红袖 and 宋甜儿, while repeated ambushes and a supposedly ceremonial sword test gradually reveal an intended killing. The novel's strongest solution does not grant the hero a sudden increase in force. He alters who enters the formation, directs 胡铁花 toward the hostages, and offers an extra sword to a fighter whose two-sword habit disrupts the six-person system.

The second half turns from tactical design to uncertain knowledge. 柳无眉 sincerely attributes her suffering to 石观音's poison and accepts 水母阴姬's demand for 楚留香's head; 水母阴姬 says no poison exists. The episode reads both statements as potentially honest but differently informed, making fear, suggestion, dependence, love, and responsibility coexist. The final underwater duel retains emotional symmetry and mercy but is judged mechanically weaker because skin breathing and total airway blockage through a kiss lack adequate physiological or narrative support.

## Key Characteristics

- Converts a search for missing companions into a controlled journey toward a prepared killing ground.
- Builds its most admired combat solution from formation structure, participant substitution, habit, and timing rather than superior force.
- Lets hostage rescue and direct combat proceed as coordinated but distinct tasks.
- Uses incompatible sincere testimony to separate subjective suffering from objective poisoning.
- Treats love as both genuine attachment and a rationale for enabling serious harm.
- Humanizes 柳无眉、李玉函 and 水母阴姬 without canceling responsibility for coercion and violence.
- Ends with a spectacular underwater mechanism that the source finds less credible than the earlier sword puzzle.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on one conversational podcast's retelling and criticism rather than a collation of novel editions or adaptations. The title's “series best” claim is source-attributed. Poisoning, psychological suggestion, opium dependence, relationships, combat capacity, physiological claims, the assassin organization, and the cause of 柳无眉's death remain fictional, interpretive, or unresolved within the supplied account.
