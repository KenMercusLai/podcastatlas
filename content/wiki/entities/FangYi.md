## Overview

方怡 / Fang Yi is a woman in [[LuDingJi|《鹿鼎记》]] whose relationship with [[WeiXiaobao|韦小宝]] joins attraction and sexual awakening to deception under coercion.

## Current Profile

The episode describes Fang as older and more experienced than Wei and treats her as part of his movement from childhood toward adolescent desire. Her initial warmth is connected to his willingness to save Liu Yizhou, but later conduct repeatedly damages trust.

That damage is qualified by circumstance. The hosts place her deception inside coercion by the Divine Dragon Cult, poison, and survival pressure rather than presenting betrayal as an essential trait. Wei's capacity to recognize those pressures becomes part of the relationship's significance.

## Key Characteristics

- Older and more experienced than Wei in the bounded comparison.
- Associated with his early sexual and emotional awakening.
- Capable of gratitude and attraction after he risks himself for another person.
- Deceives him while under cult coercion and survival pressure.
- Tests whether contextual understanding can coexist with real injury and damaged trust.
