# 弘历 / Hongli (中国认知症好朋友)

## Overview
弘历 is presented as a co-initiator of [[ChinaDementiaFriends|中国认知症好朋友]] and the dementia-care and social-inclusion guest in [[vol-133-nin-haijide-wo-shi-shui-ma-qing-tingzhi-zheyang-de-tiwen-nineng-zuo-de-haiyou-henduo-lmnn6rq16uvpkpmnimxfd5xqbr4a|VOL.133]].

## Current Profile
Her contribution moves the episode from disease knowledge into everyday practice. She advocates the non-clinical term “认知症” to reduce stigma, explains how members of the public can help people who appear lost or confused, and asks families to preserve personhood through communication, choice, useful participation, and safer environments.

She also treats diagnosis as a family transition. Early acceptance can create time for the person to join future-care decisions, while relatives need to mobilize family, community, professional, and peer-support resources for a long course of care.

## Key Characteristics
- Advocates less stigmatizing public language without denying clinical reality.
- Treats dementia inclusion as a community and public-service responsibility.
- Centers remaining ability, emotion, biography, dignity, and choice.
- Converts care principles into concrete communication and home-design practices.
- Recognizes caregiver anxiety, depression, isolation, and need for peer support.
