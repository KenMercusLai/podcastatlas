## Overview
应翔 / Ying Xiang is represented as a film researcher and curator specializing in Central and Eastern European cinema and using circulation institutions to connect film history with political and cultural history.

## Current Profile
The bounded episode traces Ying from television film programs and a university world-cinema course into graduate research on Romanian and wider Central and Eastern European film. His method of "film exchange history" asks how works enter another country, which audiences and institutions receive them, and how that circulation shapes later creation and canon formation. As a Wajda retrospective curator, he balances canonical works, less familiar films, literary adaptations, and career phases while stating where anecdotes or historical examples remain unverified.

## Key Characteristics
- Studies films together with production, funding, festival, restoration, and distribution mechanisms.
- Uses cross-national circulation to question apparently neutral world-film canons.
- Connects Central and Eastern European cases without assuming identical national histories.
- Treats retrospective selection as a historical and interpretive argument.
- Distinguishes documented claims from examples still needing verification.
