# Ningbo Yunsheng / 宁波韵生

## Overview

Ningbo Yunsheng / 宁波韵生 is the Ningbo precision-manufacturing and magnetic-materials company in [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]]. The episode follows [[ZhuYunde|竺韵德]] from music-box movements into sintered NdFeB rare-earth magnets and later succession to [[ZhuXiaodongYunsheng|竺晓冬]].

## Current Profile

Yunsheng is presented as a company that repeatedly uses precision process capability to enter deeper industrial positions. It first solved music-box movement manufacturing that had been dominated by Japanese suppliers, then used self-developed equipment and process know-how as a bridge toward rare-earth permanent magnets.

The magnetic-materials branch matters because the episode ties NdFeB magnets to motors, electric vehicles, consumer electronics, and robots. Yunsheng's story therefore shows the "one meter wide, ten thousand meters deep" pattern moving from a small consumer mechanism into advanced materials.

## Key Characteristics

- Yunsheng's first major capability was high-consistency music-box movement production.
- The company built process and equipment knowledge internally, including automated tuning and flexible assembly systems.
- Its second venture into sintered NdFeB magnets moved the company into a larger industrial-materials market.
- The episode frames Yunsheng's refusal of a restrictive German technology deal as a key autonomous R&D moment.
- Its succession from Zhu Yunde to Zhu Xiaodong is described as gradual, board-based, and decade-long.
