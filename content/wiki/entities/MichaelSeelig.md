# Michael Seelig

## Overview
Michael Seelig appears as chair of the [[CommodityFuturesTradingCommission|CFTC]] discussing purpose-fit oversight for crypto, prediction markets, AI, futures, swaps, and cross-agency products.

## Current Profile
Seelig presents a pro-innovation regulatory posture that rejects both enforcement-led ambiguity and guardrail-free markets. He favors technology-aware rules, primary-regulator coordination, exchange surveillance, simpler systemic-risk reporting, and wider market participation while retaining controls against insider trading, manipulation, fraud, and customer harm.

## Key Characteristics
- Advocates rules designed for crypto networks, on-chain software, autonomous agents, and other emerging financial technologies.
- Supports a coordinated primary-regulator model for products spanning CFTC and SEC jurisdiction.
- Treats exchanges as first-line gatekeepers through contract certification and market surveillance.
- Favors simplifying swap reporting while preserving regulators' systemic-risk visibility.
- Frames onshore innovation and investor protection as complementary goals rather than substitutes.
