# Nicolas Sarkozy

## Overview

Nicolas Sarkozy was the French president from 2007 whose hard-line security politics and conspicuous personal style are presented as a forceful but diminished version of Gaullist presidential authority.

## Current Profile

[[179-french-presidents-1981-2022-part-2-glt2206497694]] connects Sarkozy's rise to his performance as interior minister during the 2005 banlieue unrest and to the growing salience of immigration, Islam, security, and urban disorder. The source treats his rhetoric as politically effective but judges his “bling bling” image, salary increase, wiretapping, and Libya-related allegations as incompatible with the disinterested grandeur associated with [[CharlesDeGaulle]].

## Key Characteristics

- Built political authority through a hard-man security persona.
- Used confrontational language during urban unrest to signal order and control.
- Made immigration, Islam, and security central to his public position.
- Cultivated a conspicuous, celebrity-inflected presidential image.
- Faced corruption and wiretapping cases after leaving office.
