## Overview
宋念申 is a historian represented in the wiki through a conversation on Northeast Asian borders, ordinary border-crossing lives, colonial modernity, and the limits of rigid categories.

## Current Profile
In [[405-cong-dili-bianjie-dao-siwei-bianjie-women-ruhe-bei-huajie-suzao-16a80abc]], Song Nianshen moves between field observation, archival border history, political economy, and epistemology. His method begins from places such as Xita and the Tumen River, asks how law and everyday movement diverge, and then connects those local cases to identity documents, colonial partition, international law, capitalism, and disciplinary boundaries.

## Key Characteristics
- Historian of borders and Northeast Asian modernity in the episode's framing.
- Reads places and borders as changing relationship networks rather than fixed containers.
- Uses ordinary lives and administrative cases to test abstract geopolitical categories.
- Historicizes legal and political concepts instead of treating present rules as timeless.
- Connects border hardening to imperialism, colonialism, capitalism, and modern state classification.
- Advocates question-led movement across academic disciplines.
