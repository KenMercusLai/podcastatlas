# David Fishman

## Overview
David Fishman is an American energy-sector professional and English-language writer whose personal study of Chinese cities combines rankings, administrative research, local documents, travel, and informal interviews.

## Current Profile
[[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-22-zhongguo-de-zhongweishu-chengshi-shi-nazuo-1021674577|城市就是这样22]] presents Fishman as having lived in Shanghai for about fifteen years while working around power assets, generation, and electricity purchasing. His city research begins from a representativeness problem: Shanghai experience cannot support claims about "what Chinese people think." His revised method uses quantitative frames to select places, then tests categories through conversations and observation while stating what a small sample cannot establish.

## Key Characteristics
- Starts unfamiliar topics by constructing a systematic data and administrative frame.
- Uses city rankings as case-selection tools rather than accepting rank as a complete explanation.
- Interviews ordinary residents about concrete choices before inferring attitudes or local conditions.
- Revises public conclusions when field observation conflicts with the original category.
- Writes for foreign readers and builds background explainers for Chinese administrative concepts.
- Uses AI as a retrieval assistant for large or difficult text collections while retaining a verification boundary.
