# 迈奇公寓 / Majestic Apartments (Shanghai)

## Overview
迈奇公寓 appears as a Lai An-designed Shanghai apartment building and as one of the examples where the episode identifies his rounded balcony vocabulary.

## Current Profile
The source uses Majestic Apartments alongside [[GascoigneApartments|盖斯康公寓]] and other former French Concession buildings to show Lai An's recognizable design signature. The building matters less as an isolated monument than as part of a cluster of apartments whose semi-circular balconies, curved corners, and technical difficulty make Lai An legible on the street.

## Key Characteristics
- Majestic Apartments is one of the emblematic Lai An apartment works named in the episode.
- Its rounded or semi-circular balcony vocabulary helps identify Lai An's style.
- It belongs to the former French Concession apartment cluster discussed through Hengfu streets.
