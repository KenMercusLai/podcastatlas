# Ryan Soave

## Overview
Ryan Soave appears in the wiki as the addiction-treatment and trauma-recovery specialist interviewed in [[tools-for-overcoming-substance-behavioral-addictions-ryan-soave-scim5476071523]].

## Current Profile
Soave presents recovery as a staged process: stabilize dangerous or overwhelming physical conditions first, then examine the biological, psychological, relational, and social functions that substance use or compulsive behavior has served. His central clinical frame is that addiction is often an attempted solution before it becomes a larger problem. He therefore emphasizes both [[RecoveryDistressTolerance|tolerating difficult states]] and constructing a life organized around connection, purpose, pleasure, responsibility, and practice.

His practical approach combines professional care with accessible supports. He discusses treatment programs, therapy, 12-step and alternative peer groups, emotional inventory, yoga nidra or NSDR, breathwork, exercise, and trusted relationships, while marking severe withdrawal and psychedelic work as matters for appropriate medical or clinical oversight.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames addiction as an attempted relief strategy whose later costs can exceed the original problem.
- Prioritizes medical assessment and stabilization before trauma exploration in acute cases.
- Treats distress tolerance as a learnable recovery capacity rather than demanding constant comfort.
- Combines proactive daily practice with reactive tools for moments of dysregulation.
- Emphasizes community, disclosure, accountability, and family-system context.
- Defines recovery positively as building a meaningful life, not merely removing a substance or behavior.

## Evidence
- Staged care - [[tools-for-overcoming-substance-behavioral-addictions-ryan-soave-scim5476071523]] places assessment, detoxification when indicated, and physical stabilization before deeper psychological work.
- Functional addiction model - [[tools-for-overcoming-substance-behavioral-addictions-ryan-soave-scim5476071523]] repeatedly asks what relief the substance or behavior supplies and what costs it creates.
- Emotional practice - [[tools-for-overcoming-substance-behavioral-addictions-ryan-soave-scim5476071523]] presents the emotional weather forecast, scheduled state-regulation practices, and in-the-moment tools.
- Community model - [[tools-for-overcoming-substance-behavioral-addictions-ryan-soave-scim5476071523]] recommends repeated exposure to peer groups, trusted disclosure, and support for affected families.
- Positive recovery - [[tools-for-overcoming-substance-behavioral-addictions-ryan-soave-scim5476071523]] argues that abstinence must develop into purpose, connection, pleasure, and a life worth sustaining.
