# 孙明 / Sun Ming (Qing governance scholar)

## Overview
孙明 is presented as a Peking University School of Government professor whose work joins Qing local-administration archives, social history, governance research, and intellectual history.

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Sun uses long-term work with Sichuan local archives to read mid-Qing thought against administrative practice. His book 《积弊：清朝的中叶困境与周期感知》 asks how a still-stable dynasty accumulated problems, how officials and thinkers located themselves within a historical cycle, and why reform remained difficult even when diagnosis existed.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects local archives and administrative practice to intellectual history.
- Treats ordinary or apparently quiet historical periods as analytically important.
- Explains institutional failure through layered rules, interests, staffing, and information channels.
- Preserves disagreement among historical actors rather than assigning them one shared forecast.
- Treats period perception as a cause of action, not merely retrospective commentary.
