# Ralph / 拉尔夫（《蝇王》）

## Overview
Ralph / 拉尔夫 is the central order-seeking boy in [[LordOfTheFlies|《蝇王》]] as discussed in [[vol-351-yingwang-21shiji-de-huangdao-gushi-haineng-zenme-kai-naodong-1983-nuojiang-dezhu-gkwriueodap1a3taaattkgol]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Ralph as the boy who tries to keep rescue, rules, speech order, fire, and shelter in view after the crash. He is not idealized: he mocks [[PiggyLordOfTheFlies|Piggy]] and lacks enough force to hold the group together. His final collapse after rescue makes him the figure who survives long enough to recognize the loss of innocence and the darkness revealed by the island.

## Key Characteristics
- He is associated with the conch, assembly, rule, rescue, and signal fire.
- His leadership depends on symbolic legitimacy but weak execution.
- He is morally better aligned with rescue than Jack's hunting faction, but not flawless.
- His isolation at the end marks the collapse of the island's civil order.
- His tears after the naval officer arrives carry the novel's recognition of lost innocence.
