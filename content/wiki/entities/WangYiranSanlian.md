# 王依然 / Wang Yiran (Sanlian)

[[ep262-gaokao-guohou-zhishi-guiling-women-xuyao-zenyang-de-kexue-jiaoyu-ls8ecqcdpe9d5ioytyca3jqdf-3r]] adds Wang Yiran's science-education reporting role. She contributes the policy and classroom side: the rise of primary-school science, the difference between science and traditional science-subject education, the risk of [[ExamDrivenScienceEducation|刷题化]], and the need for [[ProjectBasedScienceLearning|project learning]] to remain tied to understanding rather than pure play.

[[ep253-baohuo-de-ai-haowu-daodi-shi-zhenxiang-haishi-zhishangshui-lgt0cdkotgnzjl0mu2tx41p9fw-4]] adds Wang Yiran's AI-toy reporting role. She frames AI toys as emotional products that need memory, tone, and companion design rather than only voice access to a large model, while also warning that child-facing virtual companionship can blur parent-child communication, real peer conflict, and attachment boundaries.

Wang Yiran / 王依然 is the [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] reporter and [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] guest in [[ep242-duli-youxi-shi-yitiao-gaoqian-hao-saidao-ma-lrqbzrceumbuggzuero5qb4jv3zu]]. Her reporting starts from a "young people making money" assignment and moves away from generic side-hustle examples toward communities where interests, internet identity, and production skills can become income.

In the episode, she connects [[IndieGameCommercialization|independent games]] to [[OriginalCharacterCommissionEconomy|设圈约稿经济]], [[Mihuashi|米画师]], [[BJDDollMakeupCommission|BJD妆面]], and AIGC content attempts. Her contribution is to place Wu Jun's indie-game case inside a broader creator-economy question rather than treating it as only a game-industry interview.
