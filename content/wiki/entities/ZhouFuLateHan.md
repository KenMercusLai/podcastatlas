## Overview

Zhou Fu is a late-Han Ganling teacher-official whose source role is to show how personal instruction, imperial memory, and office promotion could become factional capital. [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] had studied with him before accession, and Zhou Fu's later promotion helps turn the rivalry with [[FangZhiLateHan|房植]] into an early local party-line story.

## Current Profile

The source does not develop Zhou Fu as a full biographical actor. His importance lies in the institutional reading of reputation. A former teacher of the future emperor becomes a promoted official, and local critics fold that promotion into the rhyme contrasting Zhou Fu's teacher-backed advancement with Fang Zhi's reputation for rules and standards.

The Ganling split matters because the episode treats it as an early origin point for party talk. Zhou Fu's household or follower side is not presented as a formal treasonous organization, but the two families' guests and supporters begin attacking one another until north-south local groupings harden. That makes Zhou Fu a source case for reputation turning into factional identity before later court accusations make "party" language more dangerous.

## Key Characteristics

- Former teacher of Emperor Huan before Huan's accession.
- Promoted official whose advancement becomes legible as teacherly favor.
- One side of the Ganling Zhou-Fang rivalry that hardens into north-south follower blocs.
- Source-bounded origin figure for later party-language escalation.

## Qualifications

This page is limited to Zhou Fu's Hanji 871 function and does not reconstruct his wider career. The evidence describes a local rivalry and reputation mechanism, not proof that Zhou Fu himself directed a later criminal party.
