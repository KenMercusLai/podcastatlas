# 董宠 / Dong Chong (late Han)

董宠 / Dong Chong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-897-zhichang-bidu-shenme-cai-jiao-lingdaoli-lvrrmksykazlsb7w8abbdmnqfzqq|Hanji 897]] as an imperial relative whose access becomes political danger. The episode says he serves as zhijinwu after entering the capital, but then falsely invokes instructions from [[EmpressDowagerDongLateHan|董太后]] to make private requests.

The case matters because kinship and guard office do not protect him once private advantage threatens the ruler's legitimacy surface. The host reads the false-instruction behavior as giving opponents a handle against [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]: a relative who exploits the empress dowager's name for favors makes the court look captured by family interest.
