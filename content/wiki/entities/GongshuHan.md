# 公叔 / Gongshu of Han

公叔 / Gongshu of Han appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-44-yi-ge-gongsi-weishenme-buneng-you-liang-wei-ceo-lsz0xk6-0wwznkc62k-xkoxgz-r]] as one of the two likely brothers [[HanXuanhuiwang|韩宣惠王]] wants to appoint as joint chief minister of [[HanState|韩国]].

The page is deliberately source-scoped and distinct from [[GongshuWeiChancellor|公叔痤]], the Wei chancellor in earlier Zhouji episodes. Here "公叔" is treated by the host as kinship/rank language inside the Han ruling house, not as the same Wei figure.

Gongshu's role is mainly structural. Together with [[GongzhongHan|公众]], he creates the two-executive arrangement that [[MiuLiu|谬刘]] criticizes as [[DualExecutivePowerRisk|双首长权力风险]].
