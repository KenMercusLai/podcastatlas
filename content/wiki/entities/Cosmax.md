# COSMAX / 科丝美诗

## Overview

COSMAX is presented as a Korean beauty research and manufacturing company whose services help brands develop, test, produce, and localize products for multiple markets.

## Current Profile

[[e261-biaoyan-youxiao-zutuan-chuhai-hanguo-meizhuang-ruhe-qiangzhan-meiguo-shichang-9753c5f7-03dd-4cfa-8d4a-40531f7c4b79]] uses COSMAX with [[KolmarKorea|Kolmar Korea]] to explain [[BeautyODMInfrastructure]]. The episode says the company combines stored formulations, ingredients, formats, packaging, production, stability work, market research, and compliance support so that even small brands can access capabilities that would otherwise require multiple specialist suppliers.

## Key Characteristics

- Beauty OEM/ODM and broader brand-development capability.
- Pre-developed formulation, format, ingredient, and packaging options that support fast iteration.
- Global research and consumer-insight network used to adapt products across markets.
- Technical experience in complex categories such as base makeup and sunscreen.
- Infrastructure role that benefits many brands rather than depending on one label's longevity.
