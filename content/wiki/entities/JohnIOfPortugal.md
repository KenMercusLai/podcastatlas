# John I of Portugal / 若昂一世

## Overview
John I of Portugal / 若昂一世 appears in the Ceuta source as the Aviz monarch under whom Portugal captured [[Ceuta]] in 1415.

## Current Profile
The episode presents John I's court as newly ambitious after Portugal's mainland Reconquista and the establishment of the Aviz dynasty. The 1415 campaign required large-scale preparation, taxation, requisition, and secrecy; its result became a founding episode in later Portuguese overseas-expansion memory.

## Key Characteristics
- Aviz monarch attached to the 1415 conquest of Ceuta.
- Represents Portugal's shift from mainland consolidation toward North African and Atlantic horizons.
- Provides dynastic context for the knighting of his sons after the Ceuta victory.

- Victory evidence: [[496-yiminchao-beihou-de-xiuda-cong-gudian-shidai-manzu-duhai-yibiliya-shoufu-shidi-yundong-dao-putaoya-dahanghai-lu8pawnkiselmmrgubrey4pq-4fw]] says Portuguese forces captured Ceuta on 21 August 1415.
- Memory evidence: [[496-yiminchao-beihou-de-xiuda-cong-gudian-shidai-manzu-duhai-yibiliya-shoufu-shidi-yundong-dao-putaoya-dahanghai-lu8pawnkiselmmrgubrey4pq-4fw]] links the campaign to later Portuguese Age of Discovery narratives.

## Qualifications
This source does not provide a full reign profile; it records John I mainly through the Ceuta expedition.
