## Overview

隗恂 / Wei Xun is the eldest son of [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|隗嚣]] whose current wiki role is as a hostage and court-service signal in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-698-wangchao-xingshuai-qishilu-weiao-de-yihan-yu-jinchao-zhi-jing-lqim2hbjtdzb0wwmsaxcyzj7u8ff|Hanji 698]]. After Wei Xiao refuses [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]]'s pressure to attack [[GongsunShuEasternHan|公孙述]] and avoids going to Luoyang himself, Wei Xun is sent east with [[LaiXiEasternHan|来歙]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 698 presents Wei Xun less as an independent political actor than as collateral in an unresolved authority relationship. Wei Xiao sends him after hearing that Liu Yong and [[PengChongEasternHan|彭宠]] have been killed, which makes visible submission more urgent while also making direct court attendance feel dangerous to Wei Xiao.

Liu Xiu incorporates Wei Xun through office and title, naming him Huqi Xiaowei and Juanqiang Hou in the source's transcript wording. The arrangement temporarily signals compliance, but it does not solve Wei Xiao's larger problem because Wei Xiao still keeps his own body, army, and decision-making outside Luoyang.

Wei Xun's role therefore sits between protection and leverage. He helps keep communication open between Wei Xiao and Liu Xiu, but his position inside Liu Xiu's court also makes family security part of Wei Xiao's unresolved alignment problem.

## Key Characteristics

- Eldest son of Wei Xiao whose current source role is hostage and court-service signal.
- Sent to Luoyang because Wei Xiao will not personally enter Liu Xiu's court.
- Incorporated by Liu Xiu through military office and marquis title.
- Evidence that Wei Xiao makes a partial concession without surrendering autonomy.
- Family-security lever inside Wei Xiao's unresolved alignment crisis.

## Qualifications

The page is source-bounded to Hanji 698. Wei Xun's independent intentions, exact court duties, later treatment, and final fate are not reconstructed here. The transcript form 韦巡 is normalized to 隗恂 because the eldest-son hostage role in Wei Xiao's Jianwu 5 alignment crisis matches the established Guangwu chronology.

## What Changed

- Created a canonical page for Wei Xun because Hanji 698 promotes him from source-scoped hostage detail into a causal signal in Wei Xiao's submission crisis.
