# Natalia Olson

## Overview
Natalia Olson is presented in the bounded [[TomorrowToday]] source as a planner, entrepreneur, former Philadelphia zoning official, and Obama-administration innovation adviser whose work spans transport, logistics, small-business policy, open government, development, and AI governance.

## Current Profile
Olson's profile joins cross-cultural upbringing with an unusually broad operating path through economics, diplomacy, urban planning, finance, logistics, and public administration. Her consistent method is to find a system's leverage point, attach measurable incentives or constraints, open information to wider participation, and build change through the officials and institutions that remain after political appointees leave.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines planning, policy, finance, logistics, and entrepreneurship rather than treating them as separate domains.
- Treats government innovation as long-term institutional capacity built through career staff.
- Uses data, procurement, funding conditions, and regulation as practical levers for behavior change.
- Supports open information and wider financial access while acknowledging privacy and regulatory tradeoffs.
- Frames AI as a durable tool that needs global, multi-stakeholder governance rather than fear or denial.
- Treats friction, failure, and disagreement as inputs to learning and institutional change.
