# 春申君 / 黄歇 / Lord Chunshen

春申君 / 黄歇 / Lord Chunshen first appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-63-degao-wangzhong-mengchangjun-shi-weijunzi-lmd0owe3rq-faponbwzhkaigprin]] as the [[ChuState|楚国]] member of the [[WarringStatesFourLords|战国四公子]]. That earlier episode mentions him only in comparison with [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]], [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]], and [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-84-fangzhi-qinguo-miechu-jing-yong-zhe-zhao-llgh12eistejijsl6yildzkixocm]] makes Huang Xie the central actor. While he is in [[QinState|秦国]] as a Chu envoy, he hears that [[BaiQi|白起]] is preparing to move with [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]] against Chu again. He writes to [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] and argues from Qin's advantage rather than from Chu's weakness.

The letter's craft is [[InterestReframingPersuasion|利益重构式说服]]. Huang Xie praises Qin's power, warns that extreme success can reverse, invokes [[Fuchai|吴王夫差]] and [[ZhiYao|智瑶]] as examples of rulers destroyed after trusting temporary partners, and reframes Han and Wei as resentful corridor states that could endanger Qin's rear. He then offers Qin-Chu alliance as the more useful route for Qin to press Han, Wei, [[QiState|齐国]], and [[ZhaoState|赵国]].

The episode also makes Huang Xie a case of elite rhetorical competence. His use of [[Shijing|《诗经》]], Yijing, and historical precedent belongs with [[PoetryAsElitePoliticalLanguage|诗经作为贵族政治语言]] and [[ZonghengjiaDiplomacy|纵横家外交]]: classical citation gives the argument status and shared language, while the strategic map gives it operational force.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-85-xuantaihou-weihe-sitong-30-nian-hou-ansha-le-laoqingren-lg7zy6vqtdjcufou4-mox5qkg2ct5]] follows the practical sequel to that success. After Qin accepts friendship with [[ChuState|楚国]], Chu sends its crown prince to [[QinState|秦国]] as hostage and Huang Xie accompanies him as left official. The episode treats this as the opening of Huang Xie's later rise: a hostage prince in a foreign court depends heavily on trusted attendants, so Huang Xie's Qin service becomes a future power base.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-92-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhuming-tiangou-shi-shui-1-lmwf0q0-3hxb6d4hro8fdmc3zdxp]] shows Huang Xie cashing in that nine-year attachment to the hostage prince. When [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]] is gravely ill and [[MiWan|太子完]] remains in Xianyang, Huang Xie argues to [[FanJu|范雎]] that Qin should let the prince return and inherit rather than let another Chu prince make him valueless. When [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] hesitates, Huang Xie proposes a risk-transfer plan: Mi Wan flees disguised as a Chu envoy's driver, while Huang Xie remains behind to conceal the escape.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-92-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhuming-tiangou-shi-shui-2-lt1nwraiuuvpy2tzo-zwaoqsajzx]] completes that risk-transfer plan. Huang Xie waits until Mi Wan is safely back in Chu, then admits the escape and accepts whatever punishment [[QinState|秦国]] chooses. [[FanJu|范雎]] persuades Qin Zhaoxiang Wang that killing Huang Xie would only fight a sunk cost, while sending him back may preserve future Qin influence over the new Chu ruler.

After [[MiWan|芈完 / 楚考烈王]] succeeds, the episode makes Huang Xie's reward the beginning of his Chunshenjun identity: he becomes chancellor, receives Huai River-region territory, and is compared against the other [[WarringStatesFourLords|战国四公子]] as the one whose rise is framed most directly through personal merit, courage, and long companionship with the future ruler. The source also records the Shanghai/申城 and Huangpu River associations as cultural memory around Huang Xie, kept source-scoped rather than treated as settled geography.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-1-ljshdryvf9qgabtn-siontthzsok]] uses that survival and promotion as the handoff into the next annalistic year. Huang Xie is now safely back in [[ChuState|楚国]] and elevated, but the episode's immediate Chu point is sober: [[MiWan|楚考烈王]]'s first year still brings land cession to [[QinState|秦国]], so Chunshenjun's personal rise sits inside continued Chu weakness.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-2-lutsvwvnwzsezoorhe-vu7yjthyx]] gives Chunshenjun a post-[[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] coalition signal rather than a full action scene. [[ZhaoState|赵国]] grants him Lingqiu as an overture toward [[ChuState|楚国]], suggesting that Zhao is trying to reopen [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] after refusing or failing to complete its promised land cession to Qin.
