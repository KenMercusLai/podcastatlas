# 春申君 / 黄歇 / Lord Chunshen

春申君 / 黄歇 / Lord Chunshen first appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-63-degao-wangzhong-mengchangjun-shi-weijunzi-lmd0owe3rq-faponbwzhkaigprin]] as the [[ChuState|楚国]] member of the [[WarringStatesFourLords|战国四公子]]. That earlier episode mentions him only in comparison with [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]], [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]], and [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-84-fangzhi-qinguo-miechu-jing-yong-zhe-zhao-llgh12eistejijsl6yildzkixocm]] makes Huang Xie the central actor. While he is in [[QinState|秦国]] as a Chu envoy, he hears that [[BaiQi|白起]] is preparing to move with [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]] against Chu again. He writes to [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] and argues from Qin's advantage rather than from Chu's weakness.

The letter's craft is [[InterestReframingPersuasion|利益重构式说服]]. Huang Xie praises Qin's power, warns that extreme success can reverse, invokes [[Fuchai|吴王夫差]] and [[ZhiYao|智瑶]] as examples of rulers destroyed after trusting temporary partners, and reframes Han and Wei as resentful corridor states that could endanger Qin's rear. He then offers Qin-Chu alliance as the more useful route for Qin to press Han, Wei, [[QiState|齐国]], and [[ZhaoState|赵国]].

The episode also makes Huang Xie a case of elite rhetorical competence. His use of [[Shijing|《诗经》]], Yijing, and historical precedent belongs with [[PoetryAsElitePoliticalLanguage|诗经作为贵族政治语言]] and [[ZonghengjiaDiplomacy|纵横家外交]]: classical citation gives the argument status and shared language, while the strategic map gives it operational force.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-85-xuantaihou-weihe-sitong-30-nian-hou-ansha-le-laoqingren-lg7zy6vqtdjcufou4-mox5qkg2ct5]] follows the practical sequel to that success. After Qin accepts friendship with [[ChuState|楚国]], Chu sends its crown prince to [[QinState|秦国]] as hostage and Huang Xie accompanies him as left official. The episode treats this as the opening of Huang Xie's later rise: a hostage prince in a foreign court depends heavily on trusted attendants, so Huang Xie's Qin service becomes a future power base.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-92-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhuming-tiangou-shi-shui-1-lmwf0q0-3hxb6d4hro8fdmc3zdxp]] shows Huang Xie cashing in that nine-year attachment to the hostage prince. When [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]] is gravely ill and [[MiWan|太子完]] remains in Xianyang, Huang Xie argues to [[FanJu|范雎]] that Qin should let the prince return and inherit rather than let another Chu prince make him valueless. When [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] hesitates, Huang Xie proposes a risk-transfer plan: Mi Wan flees disguised as a Chu envoy's driver, while Huang Xie remains behind to conceal the escape.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-92-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhuming-tiangou-shi-shui-2-lt1nwraiuuvpy2tzo-zwaoqsajzx]] completes that risk-transfer plan. Huang Xie waits until Mi Wan is safely back in Chu, then admits the escape and accepts whatever punishment [[QinState|秦国]] chooses. [[FanJu|范雎]] persuades Qin Zhaoxiang Wang that killing Huang Xie would only fight a sunk cost, while sending him back may preserve future Qin influence over the new Chu ruler.

After [[MiWan|芈完 / 楚考烈王]] succeeds, the episode makes Huang Xie's reward the beginning of his Chunshenjun identity: he becomes chancellor, receives Huai River-region territory, and is compared against the other [[WarringStatesFourLords|战国四公子]] as the one whose rise is framed most directly through personal merit, courage, and long companionship with the future ruler. The source also records the Shanghai/申城 and Huangpu River associations as cultural memory around Huang Xie, kept source-scoped rather than treated as settled geography.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-1-ljshdryvf9qgabtn-siontthzsok]] uses that survival and promotion as the handoff into the next annalistic year. Huang Xie is now safely back in [[ChuState|楚国]] and elevated, but the episode's immediate Chu point is sober: [[MiWan|楚考烈王]]'s first year still brings land cession to [[QinState|秦国]], so Chunshenjun's personal rise sits inside continued Chu weakness.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-2-lutsvwvnwzsezoorhe-vu7yjthyx]] gives Chunshenjun a post-[[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] coalition signal rather than a full action scene. [[ZhaoState|赵国]] grants him Lingqiu as an overture toward [[ChuState|楚国]], suggesting that Zhao is trying to reopen [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] after refusing or failing to complete its promised land cession to Qin.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-2-ltlobdujy-hdipimfdupvwod2ech]] notes Chunshenjun's absence from the narrated [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] mission to [[MiWan|楚考烈王]]. The episode says Zhao had previously worked through him and that he had anti-Qin inclination, but this scene has Pingyuanjun negotiate directly with the king until [[MaoSui|毛遂]] intervenes. For the wiki, his role here is source-scoped context: he would have been an obvious intermediary, but the episode does not give him action in the Chu court.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-98-lishishang-shouge-laolai-dengchang-le-luxsxepdedsxd-zprezfg3u3bsnu]] makes Chunshenjun the planner behind a new anti-Qin move after the Handan relief. He advises [[MiWan|楚考烈王]] to call the lords under [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s Son of Heaven name, trying to turn residual [[ZhouRoyalHouse|Zhou]] prestige into coalition leverage. The source's outcome is negative for his plan: the lords do not bear enough cost, Chu withdraws, and Zhou Nan Wang is left with the [[ZhaitaiGaozhu|debt]] and Qin punishment.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-2-mingming-yilian-baixiang-de-caize-ping-shenme-baixiang-lhfyb6hzcqkpbzdrwsxajb7hhmhe]] adds Chunshenjun as a talent-allocator inside [[ChuState|楚国]] after those anti-Qin coalition attempts. He appoints [[Xunzi|荀子]] as magistrate of [[LanlingWarringStates|兰陵 / Lanling]], a newly held and culturally important former [[LuState|鲁国]] zone. The episode reads this as fitting the person to the place: a major Confucian scholar can govern, reassure local elites, and give Chu's occupation more cultural legitimacy than force alone.

The same source also gives the limit of Chunshenjun's judgment environment. Xunzi governs successfully, but a retainer's warning that Xunzi controls a border county near [[QiState|齐国]] and has long Qi ties makes Chunshenjun dismiss him. The episode therefore adds a vulnerability beside Chunshenjun's diplomatic skill: he can use talent well and still be moved by suspicion inside his retainer circle.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-4-shui-shi-kongzi-hou-zui-weidade-sixiangjia-lr4phoq9chc4tz0yb8y95gy21qdd]] adds Chunshenjun's attempt to correct that mistake. He regrets dismissing [[Xunzi|荀子]], receives Xunzi's writings through an envoy, and then personally goes to [[Handan|邯郸]] to urge him back after [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] dies. The source presents the 251 BCE return to [[LanlingWarringStates|兰陵 / Lanling]] as Chunshenjun recognizing that Xunzi's governing technique should not be wasted. It also records the endpoint: Chunshenjun is killed in 238 BCE during Chu turmoil, after which Xunzi can no longer remain Lanling magistrate.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-2-kongzi-zai-luguo-de-shenshi-zhimi-lphy12exg4u7f-30qkir96gv634r]] returns Chunshenjun to the late Chu-Lu frame. The episode credits him with helping [[ChuState|楚国]] recover enough great-power posture for the state to destroy [[LuState|鲁国]], but then questions his strategic judgment when he asks [[MiWan|楚考烈王]] to shift his fief from exposed Huai north to [[JiangdongWarringStates|江东]]. Chunshenjun justifies the move by saying the Huai north border with [[QiState|齐国]] needs commandery-county administration, while the host reads Jiangdong as a much safer former [[WuState|吴国]] base protected by the Yangtze and distance from [[QinState|秦国]].

The same source sharpens the [[WarringStatesFourLords|战国四公子]] comparison around Chunshenjun. It says [[SimaGuang|司马光]] ranks him last, below [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]], [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]], and [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]], and frames the later death of Chunshenjun and his household as the unresolved question for the next arc.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-106-jingren-faxian-xinlingjun-de-feizhengchang-siwang-lsj3kd2tb4tqtuvhwvsdwkh-h5el]] uses Chunshenjun as a forward hook after [[QinState|秦国]] creates [[DongCommandery|东郡]] and [[YanState|燕国]] wastes strength attacking [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. The episode says Chunshenjun worries about Qin's continuing attacks and begins carrying the [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] banner again, making him the next figure through whom the eastern states may try to coordinate.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-107-zuihou-yici-hezong-faqin-fa-le-ge-jimo-lrzq1qks7z-oromd-5f-dgqjwjhw]] completes that hook as failure. In the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] / [[Shiji|《史记》]] Chunshenjun tradition, [[MiWan|楚考烈王]] is the nominal leader and Chunshenjun commands a five-state anti-Qin coalition. The army reaches the [[HanguPass|函谷关]] area but wins no real advantage, making the episode a [[TerminalHezongFailure|末次合纵失败]] case.

The aftermath damages Chunshenjun inside Chu. The source says Mi Wan begins to distance himself from Chunshenjun after the failed campaign, and [[ZhuYingWarringStates|朱英]] then warns that Qin's pressure through weakened Wei makes Chu's capital geography dangerous. Chunshenjun accepts the advice and [[ChuState|楚国]] moves to [[ShouchunWarringStates|寿春]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-109-1-lishishang-zui-zhuming-de-huangdi-bei-lv-le-liawtywj4c9n0zpii-rxl7cguzyy]] turns that late vulnerability inward. With [[MiWan|楚考烈王]] still childless, Chunshenjun is drawn into [[LiYuanWarringStates|李源]]'s plan: after [[LiYuanSister|李源妹妹]] becomes pregnant by him, she persuades him to present her to Mi Wan so the child can be recognized as heir. The source reads this as a fall from earlier loyal courage into fear, greed, and [[PregnancySuccessionSubstitution|孕身继承替换]], and it makes [[ZhuYingWarringStates|朱英]] reappear as the adviser who sees that Li Yuan may become Chunshenjun's unexpected disaster.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-109-2-chunshenjun-bei-huangdi-dajiuge-quanzu-miemen-locma2plu7v-vsssm5dr5frc3szw]] completes the fall. [[ZhuYingWarringStates|朱英]] warns that Chunshenjun will have the chance to assist a young ruler after [[MiWan|楚考烈王]] dies, but that [[LiYuanWarringStates|李源 / 李元]] will enter the palace first and kill him unless stopped. Chunshenjun refuses because he believes Li Yuan is weak, grateful, and dependent on him. When Mi Wan dies seventeen days later, Li Yuan ambushes him at the palace gate, kills him and his household, and leaves [[ChuYouwang|楚幽王]] under Li-family control. The episode then makes this death the final exit of the [[WarringStatesFourLords|战国四公子]] and the source of the lesson "当断不断，反受其乱."
