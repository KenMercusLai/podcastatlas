# 《快乐王子》 / The Happy Prince

[[TheHappyPrince|《快乐王子》 / The Happy Prince]] appears in [[10-wang-erde-qiyi-gushiji-yong-zui-hui-sahuang-de-zuichun-qinwen-tianshi-545332195]] as one of the familiar [[OscarWilde|Wilde]] tales that shaped readers' early impression of his sadness and sacrificial beauty. [[FeiBi|菲比]] recalls liking it since childhood, and the episode places it beside [[TheNightingaleAndTheRose|《夜莺与玫瑰》]] and [[HansChristianAndersen|安徒生]]'s [[TheLittleMermaid|《海的女儿》]] as a cluster of beautiful, painful fairy-tale reading.

The source uses the story mainly as contrast. [[WildeStrangeStories|《王尔德奇异故事集》]] contains gentler endings, satire, and black comedy, so returning to The Happy Prince helps prevent a single "sad Wilde" image from flattening the wider body of work.
