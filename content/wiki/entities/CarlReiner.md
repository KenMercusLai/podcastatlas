# Carl Reiner

## Overview

Carl Reiner is a comedian, actor, writer, and director represented here as the director of [[SteveMartin]]'s first film, *The Jerk*, and as the source of the [[RefrigeratorLaugh]] heuristic for durable screen comedy.

## Current Profile

The bounded preview places Reiner between an older American comedy tradition and Martin's emerging film career. [[AdamGopnik]] recalls his work with Sid Caesar and as Mel Brooks's straight man, then emphasizes his comic knowledge and generosity. Martin's recollection adds a compact craft principle: a movie should contain moments that return to viewers later and make them laugh again.

This makes Reiner's role both practical and transmissive. He directed Martin's film debut while passing along a criterion for memorable comedy that Martin connects to preserving odd, not fully explainable ideas.

## Key Characteristics

- Directed Martin's first film, *The Jerk*.
- Connects mid-century American comedy experience to Martin's early film work.
- Is described by Gopnik as knowledgeable, generous, and trusted for comic instinct.
- Frames delayed audience recall as a meaningful test of a movie's comic durability.
