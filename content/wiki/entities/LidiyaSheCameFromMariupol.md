# 莉蒂娅 / Lidiya

莉蒂娅 / Lidiya is [[YevgeniaSheCameFromMariupol|叶弗根尼亚]]'s older sister and one of the brightest figures in [[05-ta-laizi-maliwuboer-ruguo-ni-kanjian-guo-wo-ceng-jiandao-de-533375925]]. The episode describes her childhood in the family's prosperous white house, her later humiliation over class background, her arrest after anti-Stalinist activity, and her exile to Siberia under the [[SovietUnion]].

The source treats Lidiya as more than a survivor. Her notebooks become a "book within the book" inside [[SheCameFromMariupol|《她来自马里乌波尔》]], giving voice, sequence, humor, fear, guilt, and dignity to a family otherwise recoverable only through fragments. Her teaching of juvenile prisoners in a labor-camp setting becomes the episode's main example of literature and education preserving human dignity inside violence.
