## Overview

甄寻 / Zhen Xun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-642-kao-xinji-shangwei-de-ren-zui-taoyan-shenme-lnc6e3dgxxnrylkfqzgl2ro9gpbj|Hanji 642]] as the Xin official whose fuming texts expose the danger of copying [[WangMang|王莽]]'s own legitimacy method after Wang Mang has become emperor.

## Current Profile

Zhen Xun's first fuming text works inside Wang Mang's tolerable range. It invokes old Zhou models to divide authority near the capital and makes [[ZhenFengXinOfficial|甄丰]] right bo. Wang Mang accepts that proposal, showing that fuming has not disappeared from court politics.

His second fuming text crosses into family and dynastic status by proposing that Wang Mang's daughter, the former [[EmpressWangPingdi|汉平帝王皇后]], become Zhen Xun's wife. Wang Mang reads the proposal as an intolerable violation of the "national mother" position, orders Zhen Xun arrested, and the affair expands into flight, confession, exile, execution, and hundreds of implicated deaths.

## Key Characteristics

- Fuming user who shows that mandate-sign tactics remain available after Wang Mang's accession.
- Initially succeeds by using fuming to support a court-territorial arrangement for his father.
- Triggers crisis by extending fuming into an imperial-family marriage claim.
- Becomes the immediate actor through whom fuming monopoly backfire turns into purge.

- Punitive expansion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-642-kao-xinji-shangwei-de-ren-zui-taoyan-shenme-lnc6e3dgxxnrylkfqzgl2ro9gpbj|Hanji 642]] says Zhen Xun flees, is eventually arrested after hiding near Huashan, and his confession implicates many people.

## Qualifications

This page is source-bounded to Hanji 642. It does not reconstruct Zhen Xun's full office history, precise motive, all implicated people, or the exact legal procedure behind the purge.
