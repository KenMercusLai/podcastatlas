# Baopuzi / 《抱朴子》

[[Baopuzi|《抱朴子》]] is the [[GeHong|葛洪]] text discussed in [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]]. The episode uses it to explain gold elixirs, ranked elixir systems, apotropaic objects, moral accounting for immortality, and the original context of "我命由我不由天".

The text matters because it shows [[FangshuSystematization|方术系统化]] at a high level of explicitness. Immortality is not only a wonder story; it becomes a written program combining elixir technique, body discipline, moral conduct, and religious authority.

## Key Claims
- Baopuzi turns immortality practice into a system of claims, classifications, and ethical requirements.
- The episode treats its famous slogan as source-bound to longevity and elixir practice.
- The text links [[DaoistAlchemy]] with [[DaoistRitualTechnology]] and moral self-cultivation.
