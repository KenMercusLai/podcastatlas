# Kula Wand

## Overview
Kula Wand is the children's cooling-wand product introduced by [[JulietteBruce]] in [[advice-line-with-kip-tindell-of-the-container-store-93508940-11b5-4b29-ba7b-eb077edee062]]. It is a reusable magic wand with a detachable frozen star designed to cool hot food and drinks for children.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Kula Wand as a novel physical product that has early direct-to-consumer sales, limited independent-retail placement in France and Malta, and strong need for repeated demonstration. Guy and Kip treat the product less as an awareness-campaign problem and more as an [[IndependentRetailDiscovery]] and [[DemonstrationLedProductProof]] problem.

## Key Characteristics
- Children's mealtime product created from a founder's own family need.
- Novel category that requires demonstration before parents quickly understand the use case.
- Early channel mix includes DTC plus a small number of independent retailers.
- Quality and safety claims matter because parents must trust the product's materials and construction.
