# Campion's Brag

Campion's Brag appears in [[692-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-massacre-in-paris-part-2-glt4665799025]] as [[EdmundCampion]]'s letter denying treason after entering [[England]] with [[RobertPersons]]. The episode says Catholics could read the text as a pastoral and apologetic declaration, while Protestants treated it as provocative enough to become known by that label.

The text matters because it exposes [[PastoralMissionSubversionAmbiguity]]. Campion asserts a non-treasonous religious purpose, but his underground preaching, Jesuit identity, and the broader [[SeminaryPriestMission|seminary priest mission]] make the same document look threatening inside the [[ElizabethanRecusancySecurityState]].
