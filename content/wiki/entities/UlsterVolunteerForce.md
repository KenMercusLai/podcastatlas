# Ulster Volunteer Force

The Ulster Volunteer Force appears in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as the loyalist paramilitary organization tied to the first 1966 killings in the episode's account. The source links [[GustySpence]] and associates to attacks that killed [[MatildaGould]], [[JohnPatrickScullion]], and [[PeterWard]], presenting them as early choices that helped bring [[TheTroubles]] into being.

[[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] then connects the UVF to later 1969 infrastructure bombings that were publicly blamed on the [[IrishRepublicanArmy|IRA]]. The source says Gusty Spence later described those bombings as a plan to expose [[TerenceONeill|Terence O'Neill]]'s weakness and bring him down. The UVF therefore anchors [[ParamilitarySpoilerViolence]]: violence used against Catholics, against infrastructure, and against a moderate political settlement.

[[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] extends the UVF branch into the 1971 bombing cycle. The episode identifies the 4 December 1971 [[McGurksBarBombing|McGurk's Bar bombing]] as a UVF attack that killed 15 people, making loyalist retaliation part of the wider [[AtrocityRetaliationCycle]] alongside IRA attacks.
