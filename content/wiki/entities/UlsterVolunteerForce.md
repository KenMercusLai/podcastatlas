# Ulster Volunteer Force

The Ulster Volunteer Force appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] as the loyalist paramilitary organization tied to early escalation in [[TheTroubles]]. The episode connects the UVF to the first killings in 1966 and to later 1969 infrastructure bombings that were publicly blamed on the [[IrishRepublicanArmy|IRA]].

The source says Gusty Spence later described those bombings as a plan to expose [[TerenceONeill|Terence O'Neill]]'s weakness and bring him down. The UVF therefore anchors [[ParamilitarySpoilerViolence]]: violence used not only against the other community, but also to destroy a moderate political settlement.
