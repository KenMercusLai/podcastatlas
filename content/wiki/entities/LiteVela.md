# LiteVela

## Overview
LiteVela is a [[Tencent]] personal-agent product that the source says can be accessed through [[WeChat]] and remain continuously available for everyday requests.

## Current Profile
LiteVela is presented as Tencent's response to the emerging consumer personal-agent category represented by [[MusePersonalAgent|Muse]]. Its proposed distinction from ordinary chat is persistence and action: an agent stays available, gathers context across tasks, notices needs, and helps execute them. The source does not yet establish the product's actual integrations, reliability, or permission model.

## Key Characteristics
- Accessible through WeChat in the source account.
- Described as continuously online for varied everyday requests.
- Competes in a category moving from conversation toward proactive, cross-application action.
- Potentially benefits from WeChat's existing communication and service context.
