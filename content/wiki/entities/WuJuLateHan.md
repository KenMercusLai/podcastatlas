# 吴巨 / Wu Ju (late Han)

## Overview
吴巨 / Wu Ju is the Cangwu administrator whom [[LiuBei|刘备]] names as a possible refuge after the Changban defeat, before [[LuSu|鲁肃]] redirects him toward [[SunQuan|孙权]].

## Current Profile
Hanji 1099 presents Wu Ju as the inferior alternative inside Lu Su's alliance pitch. Liu Bei calls him an old acquaintance, but Lu Su describes him as ordinary, geographically remote, and vulnerable to absorption. In the episode's rhetoric, Wu Ju matters less as an independently developed actor than as the fallback whose weakness makes Jiangdong cooperation look like an upgrade rather than humiliation.

## Key Characteristics
- Administrator of Cangwu in the episode's late-Han setting.
- Named by Liu Bei as an old acquaintance and possible destination after Changban.
- Used by Lu Su as the weak comparison case against Sun Quan's Jiangdong capacity.

## Evidence
- Refuge option: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1099-liubei-kuai-pochan-shi-ruhe-nixi-panshang-sunquan-llnev7pzbraogd00u0h7i7ba65eq|Hanji 1099]] has Liu Bei say he intends to join Wu Ju after his defeat.
- Negative contrast: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1099-liubei-kuai-pochan-shi-ruhe-nixi-panshang-sunquan-llnev7pzbraogd00u0h7i7ba65eq|Hanji 1099]] has Lu Su contrast Wu Ju's limited position with Sun Quan's territory, troops, supplies, and talent base.

## Qualifications
The source does not establish whether Liu Bei's stated plan was sincere, give Wu Ju's own view, or provide a full account of Cangwu's capacity. Lu Su's description belongs to a persuasive encounter and should not be treated as a neutral biography.
