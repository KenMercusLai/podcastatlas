# 豫章郡 / Yuzhang Commandery

豫章郡 / Yuzhang Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-995-ta-zuishang-cibei-weihe-duoci-cansha-enren-llicukoezccd5iycin4uq7qun51k|Hanji 995]] as the safer alternative [[XuShao|许劭]] recommends to [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]]. Unlike [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]], which is rich but coastal, close to the sea, and attractive to [[SunCe|孙策]], Yuzhang gives Liu Yao routes north toward Yu and west toward Jing and a place to settle displaced officials and people.

The commandery is also contested. [[YuanShu|袁术]] has appointed [[ZhugeXuan|诸葛玄]], while Liu Yao sends [[ZhuHaoLateHan|朱皓]] as a competing administrator. [[ZeRong|笮融]] is sent to support Zhu Hao but kills him and occupies Yuzhang; Liu Yao then attacks Ze Rong, who flees into the mountains and is killed by locals. The court later appoints [[HuaXin|华歆]] as Yuzhang administrator.

Yuzhang is therefore both a survival route and a risk site. Its geography fits [[PoliticalSurvivalFirst|安全第一政治生存]], but the personnel chain shows why [[XingshiqingStrategicAssessment|行势情战略评估]] must include character and trust, not just routes.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1029-guoshi-wushuang-taishici-yinuo-qianjin-yi-dangxian-lvckwlhz5g-oappy89thye9oyrto|Hanji 1029]] continues the commandery after Liu Yao's death. [[TaishiCi|太史慈]]'s report to [[SunCe|孙策]] shows Yuzhang as fragmented: [[HuaXin|华歆]] has virtue but cannot control [[TongZhiLateHan|僮芝]] in [[LulingLateHan|庐陵]] or [[PanyangLocalForces|番阳民帅]] around [[HaihunLateHan|海昏]]. The commandery thus shifts from Liu Yao's refuge route into Sun Ce's intelligence target and expansion opportunity.
