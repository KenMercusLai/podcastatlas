# 《天注定》 / A Touch of Sin

[[TianZhuDing|《天注定》]] enters the wiki through [[ep276-duihua-jiazhangke-shang-yige-shidai-zouyuan-yiding-shi-yinwei-lingyige-shidai-zai-kaojin-ni-lqzsj-ujv-gpem-796cvncekdewc]], where [[JiaZhangke|贾樟柯]] recalls meeting highly mobile young workers while making the film. In the episode, they may work in one place for only two or three months before moving again.

The film is not summarized as a whole here. Its source role is to ground [[MobilityFarewellGeneration]] in labor mobility: repeated movement is not only a student, city, or study-abroad experience, but also part of short-term work and factory life.
