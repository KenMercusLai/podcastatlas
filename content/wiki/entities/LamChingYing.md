## Overview

林正英 (Lam Ching-ying) was a Hong Kong actor and action practitioner represented here through his performance as 九叔 in [[MrVampire1985|《僵尸先生》]] and the episode's account of his later jiangshi-television legacy.

## Current Profile

The bounded source treats Lam's screen persona as the stabilizing center of a volatile genre mixture.九叔 combines ritual authority, physical precision, restraint, and comic timing, allowing fallible apprentices and fantastic devices to remain credible inside the film. The episode also places Lam's earlier action experience and later television work around this performance, culminating in a source-scoped account of his 1997 death and the dedication associated with [[MyDateWithAVampire|《我和僵尸有个约会》]].

## Key Characteristics

- Gives 九叔 both ritual authority and readable martial competence.
- Serves as the serious center against which apprentice comedy can operate.
