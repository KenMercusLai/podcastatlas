# 《游褒禅山记》

《游褒禅山记》 is the [[WangAnshi|王安石]] prose text used in [[164-guwen-de-liliang-qing-shou-xia-zhe-fen-qiannian-wei-bian-de-shenqing-dongjian-he-yongqi-940236576]] to discuss ideal, effort, and failure. The episode uses the travel-record frame less as scenic writing than as a way to think about whether a person can still count a failed pursuit as meaningful when they have truly tried.

The source connects the text to Wang Anshi's later political fate without making a simple verdict on [[WangAnshiReforms|王安石变法]]. QinZong's reading holds two claims together: Wang Anshi may not have resolved the contradiction between imperial power and reform ideals, but his private moral standard of "尽吾志" still gives modern listeners a model of [[SubjectivityThroughFailure]].
