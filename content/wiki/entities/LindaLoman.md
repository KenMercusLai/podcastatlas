# Linda Loman / 林达·洛曼

## Overview
Linda Loman is [[WillyLoman|Willy Loman]]'s wife in [[DeathOfASalesman|《推销员之死》]], represented as a caregiver who sees the family's financial and suicidal danger while protecting Willy's dignity and the household's fragile story.

## Current Profile
Linda knows that Willy is borrowing money, no longer earning normally, and showing signs of suicidal intent. Her care is therefore neither simple ignorance nor passive sentiment. She uses practical explanation and emotional protection to preserve his place in the family, but that same protection can delay direct confrontation with the beliefs and deceptions sustaining the crisis.

The episode's imagined Linda-centered adaptation asks what remains when wife and mother cease to be sufficient identities. That proposal opens a possible post-funeral movement toward independent self-definition, while remaining a host-created extension rather than part of the play's settled ending.

## Key Characteristics
- Caregiver who recognizes financial collapse and suicide risk.
- Defender of Willy's dignity against humiliation and abandonment.
- Participant in maintaining a family narrative she knows is unstable.
- Figure whose care and complicity cannot be cleanly separated.
- Character used by the hosts to ask about identity beyond wife and mother.

## Qualifications
Linda's interior life is reconstructed through the play and the hosts' adaptation proposal. The idea that Willy's death could begin an independent life for her is speculative and should not be mistaken for a canonical continuation.
