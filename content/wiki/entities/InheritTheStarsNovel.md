# 《星之继承者》 / Inherit the Stars

## Overview
《星之继承者》 is a science-fiction mystery by James P. Hogan recommended by [[XingguangCultureLimited|星光]] for its interdisciplinary investigation of a human-looking body found on the Moon.

## Current Profile
The episode describes a 2027 discovery: a spacesuited body that appears modern human but is dated to fifty thousand years earlier. Linguistics, biology, physics, and astronomy successively reshape the problem, joining hard science fiction to fair-play mystery structure. The work anchors [[HumanisticExplorationOptimism]] because it treats reason, courage, curiosity, and continued inquiry as a counterweight to technological anxiety and end-times imagination.

## Key Characteristics
- Begins with a lunar corpse that creates a human-origins contradiction.
- Builds explanation across several scientific disciplines.
- Uses mystery pacing, successive hypotheses, and late clue convergence.
- Presents exploration and revisable reason as human strengths.

- Interdisciplinary method: [[vol-353-qiuri-teji-pingguo-de-xiangwei-zhong-wo-jianjian-hunhun-yushui-gkwriaiohmv_a7ndcatastow]] lists linguistic, biological, physical, and astronomical investigation.
