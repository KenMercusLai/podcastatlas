# 岑参 / Cen Shen

岑参 / Cen Shen is the Tang frontier poet used in [[ep264-tafang-tangshi-zhilu-shengtang-qixiang-shaonianxin-loo2dfuxffizpbl4si5nwcixnngi]] to ground [[TangFrontierPoetryFieldExperience|边塞诗的实地经验]]. The episode distinguishes him from poets who wrote frontier imagery without going there: Cen Shen twice served beyond the passes and spent years in Anxi and Beiting, making places such as Luntai, Hanhai, Huoshan, [[BeitingAncientCity|北庭故城]], and western-region routes part of his poetic evidence.

The source reads his career through both ambition and historical rupture. After limited success through ordinary Chang'an routes, he joined frontier military offices; later the fates of Gao Xianzhi and Feng Changqing, the An Lushan Rebellion, and repeated send-offs gave his famous farewell poems a concrete institutional and emotional background.
