# Huang Yi / 黄一 (robotics founder)

## Overview
Huang Yi / 黄一 is the founder and CEO of [[LuoboPaidui|萝博派对]] in [[22-sui-de-jushen-ceo-5-lun-rongzi-guo-yi-meiyuan-buzhi-tian-you-duo-gao-yinian-chile-shi-nian-de-ku-duitan-huangyi-luobo-paidui-chuangshiren-ceo-ljwdmocryiblf0k3rkz8eonkqox]]. The episode presents him as a 22-year-old embodied-AI hardware founder whose route combines early prototype proof, aggressive financing, open-source distribution, and a focus on research and education customers.

## Current Profile
Huang is profiled less as a pure technologist than as a young founder learning to convert robot prototypes into capital, team growth, product focus, and customer feedback. His current judgment is that [[LuoboPaidui]] should keep its base in humanoid body hardware and platforms such as [[RPORobot|RPO]] and [[RP1Robot|RP1]], while exploring lower-level control and foundation-model directions without prematurely committing to heavy pretraining or broad home/entertainment markets.

## Key Characteristics
- Prototype-led fundraising: he emphasizes that investors funded the company after the team had already built robot prototypes and made personal early investment.
- Financing terms literacy: he treats first-round agreements, buyback clauses, lawyers, FA filtering, deep-pocket investors, and industrial capital as strategic founder responsibilities.
- Open-source pragmatism: he argues that open source is valuable only when the product is good enough to attract real users and ecosystem feedback.
- Product focus under uncertainty: he uses RP1, research-education demand, and customer iteration to discipline broader ambitions around brain models and home robots.
- High-density founder learning: he describes entrepreneurship as compressed experience that is difficult physically and mentally but unusually dense for growth.
- Direct management style: he sees youth as both a constraint and a negotiating shield, while acknowledging the need for sharper process and managers as the company scales.
