# Black Kettle

## Overview

Black Kettle was a Cheyenne leader associated with negotiation and peace who was killed when [[GeorgeArmstrongCuster]] attacked his village on the Washita River in 1868.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Black Kettle as the principal alternative to the militant Dog Soldiers during intensifying conflict over roads, railroads, settlement, bison, and Cheyenne mobility. His commitment to negotiation did not protect his community from collective military retaliation.

At the [[WashitaMassacre]], Custer's Seventh Cavalry attacked his unprepared village at dawn, killed Black Kettle and other residents, destroyed the settlement, and slaughtered its pony herd. His death makes the episode's central policy problem concrete: U.S. forces treated a peace-associated village as a permissible target within a broader campaign against Cheyenne resistance.

## Key Characteristics

- He represented negotiation within a politically diverse Cheyenne response to expansion.
- His peace association did not give his village effective protection from U.S. military attack.
- His death exposes the gap between identifying hostile war parties and imposing collective punishment.
