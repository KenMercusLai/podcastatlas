# Joanna Stern

Joanna Stern is the technology columnist interviewed in [[tech-20260410-0410-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260410-0410-mp-tech-pod-128]] and [[tech-20251212-1212-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251212-1212-mp-tech-pod-128]]. In the April 10 [[MarketplaceTech]] Bytes episode, she connects [[Anthropic]]'s restricted [[ClaudeMethosPreview|Claude-Methos Preview]] rollout, [[PortWashingtonWisconsin|Port Washington]]'s data-center incentive vote, and [[NASA]]'s [[Artemis2|Artemis 2]] livestreaming into a shared question of how powerful technology earns public trust.

In the December 12 Marketplace Tech Bytes episode, she interprets [[Apple]]'s leadership departures, the unresolved search for a new AI device, the [[WarnerBrosDiscovery]] streaming bids, and the backlash to [[McDonaldsNetherlands]] using AI-generated advertising.

In the August 7, 2026 Marketplace Tech Bytes episode, Stern connects [[SpaceX]]'s first public earnings report, [[HouseholdRobotTrainingData]], and [[MySpace]] nostalgia. Her robotics segment is the strongest addition: it shows how humanoid-robot progress depends on people recording ordinary chores, while also raising the question of whether workers are training possible future competitors.

Her role in the wiki is a cross-domain commentator: she connects AI competition to leadership succession, hardware form factor, streaming consumer friction, synthetic-media disclosure, robotics data labor, and social-platform nostalgia.
