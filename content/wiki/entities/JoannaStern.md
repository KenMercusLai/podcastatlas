# Joanna Stern

## Overview
Joanna Stern is the technology journalist and recurring [[MarketplaceTech]] commentator the wiki uses as a cross-domain interpreter of consumer technology, AI products, platform power, robotics, media, and school technology policy.

## Current Profile
Stern's Marketplace Tech appearances work as practical technology synthesis. She moves between product launches, AI capability claims, public trust, household use, and institutional adoption rather than treating technology as isolated company news. The September 2026 episode extends that role by connecting [[Apple]] succession, [[Google]] antitrust remedies, [[NewYorkCityPublicSchools|New York City public schools]] AI limits, companion-chatbot safety, and agentic shopping tests.

## Key Characteristics
- She translates fast-moving technology news into consumer, institutional, and trust questions.
- Her Apple commentary links leadership succession to hardware form factor, AI assistant readiness, and device value.
- Her AI-policy commentary distinguishes ordinary utility from emotional or educational risk, especially for minors.
- Her product evaluations emphasize real workflows, including shopping-agent follow-through and clarifying questions.
- Her recurring role creates bridges across AI competition, robotics data labor, media consolidation, synthetic advertising, and platform nostalgia.
