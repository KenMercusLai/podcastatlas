# 王立新 / Wang Lixin

Wang Lixin / 王立新 is the gold-industry expert in [[ep239-he-daka-liaoliao-jinjia-you-shuang-ruo-zhuo-biao-le-putongren-hai-neng-shangche-ma-lgcy2trgzt7expvr5bcybybrummq]]. The episode presents him as having joined the [[WorldGoldCouncil|世界黄金协会]] in 1995 and having roughly three decades of experience observing China's gold market.

His contribution is to move the conversation away from a pure price-chasing question. He explains gold as a financial product, commodity, and industrial raw material, then argues that its investment role should be understood through independence, safety, liquidity, and long-term return. That makes him the source's main bridge between [[GoldMonetaryAnchor]], [[GoldInvestmentProducts]], and [[StrategicGoldAllocation]].

Wang also supplies the product-suitability frame around [[GoldPortfolioProducts|黄金家组合产品]]. He treats professional portfolios as a possible answer to ordinary investors' recognition, entry, and holding difficulties, while still requiring scrutiny of provider, manager record, underlying assets, and personal risk preference.
