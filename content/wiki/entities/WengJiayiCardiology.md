# 翁家毅 / Weng Jiayi

## Overview
翁家毅, spelled 翁嘉义 in VOL.43, is a source-scoped cardiovascular clinician appearing in [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]]. The supplied episodes identify him as a cardiology physician and, in VOL.43, associate him with the cardiovascular center of Suzhou Municipal Hospital.

## Current Profile
Across the two episodes, Weng explains cardiovascular risk through baseline vulnerability and longitudinal control rather than one trigger or one number. VOL.43 covers primary and secondary hypertension, reliable home measurement, younger-adult and metabolic patterns, individualized medication, gradual exercise, and preventable dosing errors. VOL.116 applies the same logic to intense laughter: temporary activation is usually tolerable, but coronary disease, impaired cardiac function, poorly controlled hypertension, or the acute period after myocardial infarction changes the risk boundary.

## Key Characteristics
- Cardiovascular medical voice on hypertension and trigger-related cardiac risk.
- Separates a blood-pressure reading from sustained disease and primary from potentially secondary hypertension.
- Treats measurement technique, medication continuity, monitoring, lifestyle, and follow-up as one control system.
- Connects younger-adult hypertension to possible diastolic and metabolic patterns without reducing it to weight alone.
- Separates ordinary physiological activation from risk in people with coronary disease, impaired function, or unstable pressure.
- Keeps medication, exercise, potassium, and post-infarction advice inside a cautious clinical boundary.

## Qualifications
The supplied files vary between 翁家毅 and 翁嘉义. Matching romanization, specialty, and show context suggest one guest, but the wiki has not independently verified the legal-name spelling, institutional biography, clinical record, or guideline basis of the claims. The fatal anecdote in VOL.116 does not establish population risk or prove laughter as the sole cause. Both episodes are public education rather than individualized cardiology advice.
