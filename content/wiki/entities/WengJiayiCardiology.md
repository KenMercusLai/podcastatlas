# 翁家毅 / Weng Jiayi

## Overview
翁家毅 is the source-scoped cardiovascular clinician in [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] VOL.116 who explains how intense laughter interacts with underlying heart disease.

## Current Profile
翁家毅 describes faster heart rate, breathing, and higher blood pressure during laughter as usually tolerable physiological activation for healthy people. His risk boundary shifts with coronary disease, impaired cardiac function, hypertension, or the acute period after myocardial infarction, when emotional stability and clinical context matter more.

## Key Characteristics
- Cardiovascular medical voice in the episode.
- Separates ordinary physiological activation from risk in vulnerable patients.
- Connects emotional intensity to underlying coronary, functional, and blood-pressure status.
- Keeps post-infarction advice inside a cautious clinical boundary.
