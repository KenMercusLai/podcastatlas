## Overview

梁王刘立 / Liu Li enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-624-du-lishi-wu-xingfu-ni-shi-rensheng-yingjia-haishi-yongren-zirao-daibiao-lpavicltc7nducbxiqgpr6t7iczd|Hanji 624]] as a Western Han Liu-line king removed during [[WangMang|王莽]]'s late pre-usurpation administrative tightening, where the source note rendered the name as 刘丽. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-545-xihan-huangzu-jingtian-dagua-lvdplebjdtlav7kywaxvevmbtx6x|Hanji 545]] supplies the earlier Chengdi-era scandal layer and gives the clearer name form 刘立: Liu Li is accused of lawlessness, hostile speech against [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s outer-relative family, and an alleged "禽兽行" relationship with his aunt [[LiuYuanziWesternHan|刘原子]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 545 now supplies the earlier current profile. Liu Li is reported by the Liang chancellor as lawless and as speaking resentfully against the empress dowager's family. The investigation then expands into the alleged relationship with Liu Yuanzi, and officials ask Chengdi to execute him. [[GuYongWesternHan|谷永]] challenges the case by arguing that the original speech accusation was not proved, the sexual accusation is hard to verify, Liu Li denies it, and the age, incentive, and shame structure all make the accusation doubtful. Chengdi ultimately suppresses the case and forbids punishment.

Hanji 624 supplies the later endpoint. Liu Li is accused of colluding with members of Pingdi's Wei-surname maternal or in-law network. The court strips him of the Liang kingship and exiles him to Nanzheng, after which he kills himself. In this later episode, his death is not developed as an independent biography; it functions as part of a wider sequence of legal, territorial, and office rearrangements that the host reads through the "庸人自扰" frame.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Liu-line royal titled King of Liang.
- Earlier accused of lawlessness, hostile speech, and a "禽兽行" scandal with Liu Yuanzi.
- Protected in that case when Gu Yong's procedural defense leads Chengdi to suppress punishment.
- Target of an accusation involving Pingdi's Wei-family remnants.
- Removed from royal status and exiled to Nanzheng.
- Suicide case embedded in Wang Mang-era royal-house pressure.

## Qualifications

The source notes do not settle the truth of the Chengdi-era incest accusation, the later collusion accusation, Liu Li's full genealogy, the legal procedure behind the demotion, or the political role of the Wei-family remnants. The page therefore treats his cases as source-scoped evidence of royal-house exposure, not as proof of actual conspiracy or sexual misconduct. The name form is normalized to 刘立 after Hanji 545; earlier 刘丽 remains treated as a source-note rendering.
