## Overview

梁王刘丽 / Liu Li enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-624-du-lishi-wu-xingfu-ni-shi-rensheng-yingjia-haishi-yongren-zirao-daibiao-lpavicltc7nducbxiqgpr6t7iczd|Hanji 624]] as a Western Han Liu-line king removed during [[WangMang|王莽]]'s late pre-usurpation administrative tightening. His case shows how royal status could become vulnerable once court politics linked a Liu relative to alleged remnants around [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]].

## Current Profile

The source says Liu Li was accused of colluding with members of Pingdi's Wei-surname maternal or in-law network. The court strips him of the Liang kingship and exiles him to Nanzheng, after which he kills himself. In this episode, his death is not developed as an independent biography; it functions as part of a wider sequence of legal, territorial, and office rearrangements that the host reads through the "庸人自扰" frame.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Liu-line royal titled King of Liang.
- Target of an accusation involving Pingdi's Wei-family remnants.
- Removed from royal status and exiled to Nanzheng.
- Suicide case embedded in Wang Mang-era royal-house pressure.

## Qualifications

The source note does not settle the truth of the collusion accusation, Liu Li's genealogy, the legal procedure behind the demotion, or the political role of the Wei-family remnants. The page therefore treats his case as source-scoped evidence of royal-house exposure, not as proof of actual conspiracy.
