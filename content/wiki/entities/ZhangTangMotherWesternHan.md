## Overview

张汤母亲 / Mother of Zhang Tang enters the wiki through her refusal to give [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]] an elaborate funeral after his suicide, turning burial restraint into a public claim that a high minister had died under false accusation.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-339-2-yi-si-sharen-kuli-zhangtang-shi-ruhe-zuodao-de-luq3jaa9iruhiqiqlduewbj-luca|Hanji 339-2]] says Zhang's family initially wants a costly burial, but his mother refuses because a minister who has died under hostile words is not entitled to such treatment. The family therefore uses only a coffin and an ox cart. In the episode's causal account, that decision joins Zhang's final letter and modest documented estate in persuading [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] that Zhang was wronged.

## Key Characteristics

- Mother of Zhang Tang in the episode's account.
- Rejects an elaborate funeral after her son's suicide.
- Uses sparse burial as a judgment on the injustice of his death.
- Helps alter the emperor's posthumous reading of the case.

## Evidence

Funeral refusal:

Political effect:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-339-2-yi-si-sharen-kuli-zhangtang-shi-ruhe-zuodao-de-luq3jaa9iruhiqiqlduewbj-luca|Hanji 339-2]] presents Han Wudi's admiration of her response as part of his turn toward investigating Zhang's accusers.

## Qualifications

The source does not preserve her personal name or an independent biography. Her exact words, control over the funeral, the sequence of imperial reconsideration, and the relative weight of funeral conduct versus the will and estate remain episode-attributed.
