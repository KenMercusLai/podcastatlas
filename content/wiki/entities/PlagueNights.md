# 《瘟疫之夜》 / Nights of Plague

《瘟疫之夜》 / Nights of Plague is the [[OrhanPamuk|奥尔罕·帕慕克 / Orhan Pamuk]] novel recommended in [[yi-fen-laizi-bingchuang-shang-de-xiangguan-shudan-598284093]]. The source notes that the transcript recognized the title as "《文艺之夜》"; because the episode describes Pamuk, plague, an island, 1901, the Ottoman Empire, foreign powers, and murder, the wiki treats this as a transcript variant of 《瘟疫之夜》.

In the episode, the novel echoes [[DongbeiBoyi|《东北博弈》]]: plague spreads through a politically fragile imperial borderland, while quarantine, foreign influence, murder, investigation, and public order become inseparable. Its function is to show that [[PublicHealthGeopolitics]] can be carried by fiction as well as by history.
