## Current Profile

The page is intentionally narrow. Hanji 627-1 uses 《汉书》 to show that the Pingdi death tradition is not textually flat: the episode says 《汉书·平帝纪》 records the emperor's illness and death without directly narrating poisoning, while 《汉书·王莽传》 describes illness and Wang Mang's Zhougong-like prayer performance. The poisoning details enter through later annotation rather than the episode's account of the base annalistic notice.

That makes 《汉书》 useful here as a source-criticism node. It contrasts with [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]], which the episode says adopts the pepper-wine poisoning account more directly.

## Key Characteristics

- Historical text used in the episode as a comparison point for Pingdi's death.
- Preserves illness-centered notices in the episode's account.
- Contains Wang Mang's Zhougong-like prayer performance in the source comparison.
- Functions as a historiographical caution against flattening the poisoning claim.
