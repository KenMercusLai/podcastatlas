# Hexiehao / 和谐号

Hexiehao is the first major Chinese high-speed EMU family in [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]]. The episode says it emerged after the [[MinistryOfRailwaysChina]] imported and absorbed foreign technology from suppliers including [[KawasakiHeavyIndustries]], [[Alstom]], [[Bombardier]], and [[Siemens]].

The source treats Hexiehao as a transitional system, not a fully unified domestic standard. It carried multiple foreign lineages under one Chinese brand, made the 2007 sixth railway speed-up visible to passengers, and later fed into CRH380 redesign work. Its limitations also explain why [[ChinaRailwayCorporation]] later pursued [[ChineseStandardEMU]] and [[Fuxinghao]].
