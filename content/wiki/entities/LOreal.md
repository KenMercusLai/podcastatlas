# L'Oreal

## Overview
L'Oreal is represented in the wiki as a global beauty company whose source-backed profile spans refillable packaging, hair-care category renewal, strategic-buyer activity, and mini beauty product strategy.

## Current Profile
The bounded sources place L'Oreal at several beauty-market frontiers. [[lvdian-xiaoyang-yao-tuiguang-tihuanxin-de-ba-meizhuang-chongzuo-yibian-1006757937]] uses L'Oreal's refill campaign to show how beauty packaging, reuse durability, price gap, and channel explanation must align before sustainability becomes a consumer habit. [[meizhuang-jutou-jiti-dingshang-toufa-xihu-shengyi-weihe-you-re-qilai-1005860109]] uses L'Oreal's hair-care growth to show beauty companies moving into scalp care, hair oils, premium salon trust, and ingredient-led routines. [[e-l-f-cosmetics-joey-shamah-the-dollar-store-formula-that-built-a-cosmetics-giant-bbc71e65-9e47-41cb-8dd4-c61fcfc2572e]] adds a historical acquisition-process role through the failed e.l.f. Cosmetics transaction.

The mini-beauty source places L'Oreal in a small-format strategy branch. L'Oreal is one of the beauty groups named as brands convert small formats from samples and travel sizes into independent mini products, connecting the company to [[MiniBeautyStandaloneProduct]], [[TrialSizeProduct]], and [[BeautyAccessorization]].

## Key Characteristics
- Uses packaging as strategic product infrastructure in refillable beauty, not only as outer decoration.
- Participates in hair-care category renewal through premium, scalp, ingredient, and salon-trust frames.
- Appears as a strategic buyer in beauty consolidation, including the failed e.l.f. Cosmetics process.
- Serves as a named participant in the mini-beauty shift from samples toward standalone small-format products.
- Links multinational beauty strategy to channel explanation, packaging complexity, consumer habit formation, and category-specific willingness to pay.

## Evidence
- Refillable-packaging evidence: [[lvdian-xiaoyang-yao-tuiguang-tihuanxin-de-ba-meizhuang-chongzuo-yibian-1006757937]] says L'Oreal's refill options span divisions, brands, and products such as Lancome, YSL Beauty, Kerastase, and Paris L'Oreal in the source account.
- Hair-care evidence: [[meizhuang-jutou-jiti-dingshang-toufa-xihu-shengyi-weihe-you-re-qilai-1005860109]] treats L'Oreal's hair-care growth and brand portfolio as a category signal for scalp care, oils, premium products, and professional trust.
- Strategic-buyer evidence: [[e-l-f-cosmetics-joey-shamah-the-dollar-store-formula-that-built-a-cosmetics-giant-bbc71e65-9e47-41cb-8dd4-c61fcfc2572e]] names L'Oreal as the buyer that offered roughly $225 million for e.l.f. Cosmetics before the deal collapsed.
- Mini-beauty evidence: [[meizhuang-xiaoyang-bianshen-duli-shangpin-pinpai-weihe-dou-zai-fenfen-buju-mini-chanpin-1011859226]] names L'Oreal among beauty groups laying out mini makeup and small-format fragrance products.
