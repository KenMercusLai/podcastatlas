# L'Oreal

[[meizhuang-jutou-jiti-dingshang-toufa-xihu-shengyi-weihe-you-re-qilai-1005860109]] adds L'Oreal as the central hair-care-market case. The [[ShengdongZaokafei|声动早咖啡]] episode says the company's hair-care products grew faster than fragrance, color cosmetics, and skincare in the cited first-half results, and that L'Oreal, [[Kerastase|Kérastase / 卡诗]], [[Vichy|薇姿]], and [[CeraVe|适乐肤]] hair-related businesses all posted double-digit growth in the source's account. The episode uses L'Oreal to connect beauty-company portfolio strategy to [[HaircareSkincareization]], [[PremiumHaircareMarket]], and [[SalonChannelTrust]].

L'Oreal appears in [[e-l-f-cosmetics-joey-shamah-the-dollar-store-formula-that-built-a-cosmetics-giant-bbc71e65-9e47-41cb-8dd4-c61fcfc2572e]] as the strategic buyer that offered roughly $225 million for [[ELFCosmetics]] before the deal collapsed. [[JoeyShamah]] says L'Oreal cited discomfort with e.l.f.'s low-cost supply chain, while he believed [[UrbanDecay]] was the real focus at the time.

The failed process matters because it left e.l.f. available for the later [[TPG]] deal at a higher valuation.
