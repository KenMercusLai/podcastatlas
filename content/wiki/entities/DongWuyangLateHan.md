# 东武阳 / Dong Wuyang (Late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] first brings Dong Wuyang into Cao Cao's East Commandery branch as the place where [[CaoCao|曹操]] sets the commandery office after defeating [[BaiRaoLateHan|白绕]] and receiving [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s recommendation as [[DongCommandery|东郡]] administrator.

东武阳 / Dong Wuyang (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-961-neihuang-zhizhan-caocao-chulu-fengmang-ljdlt6kiu8tg0qws-jlyej-yhgxr|Hanji 961]] as the place [[YuDuLateHan|于毒]] attacks while [[CaoCao|曹操]] is at [[DunqiuLateHan|顿丘]]. The episode's military problem is whether Cao Cao should rescue the city directly or shift pressure somewhere Yu Du cannot ignore.

Cao Cao's answer makes Dong Wuyang important as the visible pressure point rather than the final battlefield. By marching west toward Yu Du's base, he forces Yu Du to lift the siege without first fighting at the city. Dong Wuyang therefore anchors the episode's late-Han application of [[EncirclingWeiToRescueZhao|围魏救赵]] before the fighting moves on to [[NeihuangBattleLateHan|内黄之战]].
