## Overview
刘蒜 / Liu Suan is the late-Han imperial-clan alternative whose rejected candidacy matters before and after [[EmperorHuanOfHan]]'s accession. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-828-da-jiangjun-liangji-shanquan-feili-de-donghan-jianchen-llqnqijagtmdlgitulddxspncu0e|Hanji 828]] shows why [[CaoTengLateHan]] and [[LiangJiLateHan]] see him as dangerous; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-829-junzi-weishenme-doubuguo-xiaoren-lthvnstkfu8n9thx1sz6yezl4yqq|Hanji 829]] shows his unused legitimacy becoming politically lethal; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] preserves the later memory of [[LiGuLateHan]]'s succession judgment.

## Current Profile
The first current layer is the 146 CE succession crisis. Hanji 828 presents Liu Suan, the Qinghe king, as a candidate whom Li Gu and [[DuQiaoLateHan]] are willing to defend but whom Cao Teng warns against because Liu Suan is strict and clear enough to threaten Liang Ji's guests and eunuch fortunes.

Hanji 829 adds the immediate political consequence. Li Gu's preference for Liu Suan touches [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan]] and Liang-family interests, helping explain Li Gu's removal. [[TangHengLateHan]] and [[ZuoGuanLateHan]] later tell Huan that Li Gu and Du Qiao opposed his accession, converting Liu Suan's candidacy into personal imperial resentment.

The same source supplies Liu Suan's final branch. In the eleventh month of 147 CE, [[LiuWenQingheRebelLateHan]] and [[LiuWeiNanjunRebelLateHan]] claim Liu Suan should rule the realm and attempt to install him. After the plot leaks, [[XieHaoQingheLateHan]] refuses to cooperate and is killed; officials impeach Liu Suan, who is demoted to Weishi Hou, exiled to Guiyang, and later dies by suicide.

Hanji 929 then works as a memory layer. It recalls Liu Suan as the older and more capable candidate Li Gu preferred over the later Huan, and links that judgment to the saying that Li Gu would not establish an emperor and [[LiXieLateHan]] would not establish a king.

## Key Characteristics
- Qinghe king and imperial-clan alternative in the 146 CE succession debate.
- Candidate rejected by Liang Ji and Cao Teng because independent severity would threaten incumbent interests.
- Figure whose name becomes a resentment channel against Li Gu and Du Qiao after Huan's accession.
- Target of a failed 147 CE enthronement plot by Liu Wen and Liu Wei.
- Demoted, exiled, and suicide-ending figure after the plot is suppressed.

## Qualifications
This page remains bounded to three podcast source notes and is not a full Liu Suan biography. Hanji 828 explains why powerful insiders rejected him; Hanji 829 shows his name being used in accusation and rebellion; Hanji 929 gives a later moral-political memory of Li Gu's judgment. The evidence still does not prove how much agency Liu Suan himself had in Liu Wen and Liu Wei's attempt.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 828 as the direct 146 CE accession-debate layer.
- Clarified that Liu Suan is politically dangerous to Liang Ji and Cao Teng because he is portrayed as strict and clear, not merely because he is an alternative claimant.
- Preserved Hanji 829 as the failed-plot and punishment layer and Hanji 929 as the later reputation-memory layer.
