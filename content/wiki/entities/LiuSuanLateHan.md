## Overview
刘蒜 / Liu Suan is the late-Han imperial-clan alternative whose rejected candidacy matters twice before [[EmperorHuanOfHan]]'s accession and after it. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-825-lishi-gaosu-ni-dang-ni-meiyong-shi-renxing-zui-zisi-luv-8z9axuejnfhlkgsuxee3uked|Hanji 825]] now shows [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] recommending him after [[EmperorChongOfHan|汉冲帝]]'s death, only for [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] and [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan|梁妠]] to choose the child Liu Zuan. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-827-yi-ju-hua-yinfa-de-diwang-zhisi-lnf6rz1i2vmgsclgiejbh0qevzc2|Hanji 827]] shows senior officials recommending him again after [[EmperorZhiOfHan]]'s death and [[CaoTengLateHan]] opposing him from personal resentment; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-828-da-jiangjun-liangji-shanquan-feili-de-donghan-jianchen-llqnqijagtmdlgitulddxspncu0e|Hanji 828]] shows why Cao Teng and Liang Ji see him as institutionally dangerous; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-829-junzi-weishenme-doubuguo-xiaoren-lthvnstkfu8n9thx1sz6yezl4yqq|Hanji 829]] shows his unused legitimacy becoming politically lethal; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] preserves the later memory of Li Gu's succession judgment.

## Current Profile
The first current layer is the 145 CE succession crisis in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-825-lishi-gaosu-ni-dang-ni-meiyong-shi-renxing-zui-zisi-luv-8z9axuejnfhlkgsuxee3uked|Hanji 825]]. After Emperor Chong dies, Liu Suan reaches Luoyang and is described as solemn, careful, and ritually proper enough to win admiration from senior officials. Li Gu privately tells Liang Ji that the new ruler should be older, wise, virtuous, and able to govern personally, but Liang Ji and Liang Na select the eight-year-old Liu Zuan instead and send Liu Suan back to his fief.

The second current layer is the 146 CE succession crisis in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-827-yi-ju-hua-yinfa-de-diwang-zhisi-lnf6rz1i2vmgsclgiejbh0qevzc2|Hanji 827]]. After Emperor Zhi dies, Li Gu, [[HuGuangLateHan|胡广]], Zhao Jie, and [[DuQiaoLateHan|杜乔]] urge broad consultation and recommend Liu Suan, the Qinghe king. Cao Teng then opposes him because Liu Suan had once failed to show him deference.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-828-da-jiangjun-liangji-shanquan-feili-de-donghan-jianchen-llqnqijagtmdlgitulddxspncu0e|Hanji 828]] presents the strategic rejection layer. Liu Suan remains the candidate Li Gu and Du Qiao are willing to defend, but Cao Teng warns Liang Ji that Liu Suan is strict and clear enough to threaten Liang Ji's guests and eunuch fortunes.

Hanji 829 adds the immediate political consequence. Li Gu's preference for Liu Suan touches [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan]] and Liang-family interests, helping explain Li Gu's removal. [[TangHengLateHan]] and [[ZuoGuanLateHan]] later tell Huan that Li Gu and Du Qiao opposed his accession, converting Liu Suan's candidacy into personal imperial resentment.

The same source supplies Liu Suan's final branch. In the eleventh month of 147 CE, [[LiuWenQingheRebelLateHan]] and [[LiuWeiNanjunRebelLateHan]] claim Liu Suan should rule the realm and attempt to install him. After the plot leaks, [[XieHaoQingheLateHan]] refuses to cooperate and is killed; officials impeach Liu Suan, who is demoted to Weishi Hou, exiled to Guiyang, and later dies by suicide.

Hanji 929 then works as a memory layer. It recalls Liu Suan as the older and more capable candidate Li Gu preferred over the later Huan, and links that judgment to the saying that Li Gu would not establish an emperor and [[LiXieLateHan]] would not establish a king.

## Key Characteristics
- Qinghe king and imperial-clan alternative recommended after both Emperor Chong's death and Emperor Zhi's death.
- Candidate rejected by Cao Teng first through personal resentment and then by Liang Ji and Cao Teng because independent severity would threaten incumbent interests.
- Figure whose name becomes a resentment channel against Li Gu and Du Qiao after Huan's accession.
- Target of a failed 147 CE enthronement plot by Liu Wen and Liu Wei.
- Demoted, exiled, and suicide-ending figure after the plot is suppressed.

## Evidence
- First recommendation and rejection: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-825-lishi-gaosu-ni-dang-ni-meiyong-shi-renxing-zui-zisi-luv-8z9axuejnfhlkgsuxee3uked|Hanji 825]] says Liu Suan is admired by senior officials after Emperor Chong's death, Li Gu urges Liang Ji to choose an older worthy ruler, and Liang Ji plus Liang Na choose Liu Zuan instead.
- Second senior-official recommendation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-827-yi-ju-hua-yinfa-de-diwang-zhisi-lnf6rz1i2vmgsclgiejbh0qevzc2|Hanji 827]] says Li Gu, Hu Guang, Zhao Jie, and Du Qiao recommend Liu Suan after Emperor Zhi's death.
- Cao Teng's personal opposition: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-827-yi-ju-hua-yinfa-de-diwang-zhisi-lnf6rz1i2vmgsclgiejbh0qevzc2|Hanji 827]] says Cao Teng objects because Liu Suan had not shown him proper deference.
- Succession threat profile: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-828-da-jiangjun-liangji-shanquan-feili-de-donghan-jianchen-llqnqijagtmdlgitulddxspncu0e|Hanji 828]] says Cao Teng warns Liang Ji that Liu Suan's strictness and clarity would threaten Liang's guests and eunuch wealth.
- Ministerial support: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-828-da-jiangjun-liangji-shanquan-feili-de-donghan-jianchen-llqnqijagtmdlgitulddxspncu0e|Hanji 828]] shows Li Gu and Du Qiao resisting Liu Zhi's accession, which preserves Liu Suan's alternative claim in the debate.
- Interest conflict: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-829-junzi-weishenme-doubuguo-xiaoren-lthvnstkfu8n9thx1sz6yezl4yqq|Hanji 829]] says Li Gu's desire to establish Liu Suan touches Liang dowager and Liang-family interests.
- Imperial resentment channel: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-829-junzi-weishenme-doubuguo-xiaoren-lthvnstkfu8n9thx1sz6yezl4yqq|Hanji 829]] says Tang Heng and Zuo Guan tell Huan that Li Gu and Du Qiao opposed his accession.
- Failed plot and punishment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-829-junzi-weishenme-doubuguo-xiaoren-lthvnstkfu8n9thx1sz6yezl4yqq|Hanji 829]] says Liu Wen and Liu Wei try to install Liu Suan, after which Liu Suan is demoted, exiled, and later dies by suicide.
- Later succession memory: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] recalls Liu Suan as the candidate Li Gu preferred and connects that judgment to Li Xie's later title refusal.

## Qualifications
This page remains bounded to five podcast source notes and is not a full Liu Suan biography. Hanji 825 supplies the first recommendation after Emperor Chong's death; Hanji 827 supplies the second recommendation and Cao Teng grievance layer after Emperor Zhi's death; Hanji 828 explains why powerful insiders rejected him; Hanji 829 shows his name being used in accusation and rebellion; Hanji 929 gives a later moral-political memory of Li Gu's judgment. The evidence still does not prove how much agency Liu Suan himself had in Liu Wen and Liu Wei's attempt.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 825 as the first rejected Liu Suan candidacy after Emperor Chong's death.
- Reframed Hanji 827 as a second recommendation after Emperor Zhi's death rather than the beginning of Liu Suan's succession role.
- Preserved Hanji 828 as the strategic explanation for why Liu Suan is politically dangerous to Liang Ji and Cao Teng.
- Preserved Hanji 829 as the failed-plot and punishment layer and Hanji 929 as the later reputation-memory layer.
