# 刘蒜 / Liu Suan (late Han)

刘蒜 / Liu Suan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] as the imperial-clan alternative whom [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] was said to prefer over the later Emperor Huan. The episode uses Liu Suan only as background for the saying that Li Gu would not establish an emperor and [[LiXieLateHan|李燮]] would not establish a king.

The page is intentionally narrow. Liu Suan's role here is not a full biography but a succession-quality marker: the source presents him as the more mature and capable option in Li Gu's earlier judgment.
