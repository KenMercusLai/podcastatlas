# 刘蒜 / Liu Suan (late Han)

## Overview

刘蒜 / Liu Suan is the late-Han imperial-clan alternative whose current wiki profile links [[LiGuLateHan|李固]]'s earlier succession judgment, [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]'s later resentment, and a failed 147 CE enthronement plot. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] preserves him as the older and more capable candidate Li Gu preferred; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-829-junzi-weishenme-doubuguo-xiaoren-lthvnstkfu8n9thx1sz6yezl4yqq|Hanji 829]] shows that this unused legitimacy can still become politically lethal when [[LiuWenQingheRebelLateHan|刘文]] and [[LiuWeiNanjunRebelLateHan|刘伟]] try to install him.

## Current Profile

The first layer is succession judgment. Hanji 929 recalls Liu Suan as the imperial-clan candidate whom Li Gu preferred over the later Huan because Liu Suan was older and more capable in that source's framing. The saying that Li Gu would not establish an emperor and [[LiXieLateHan|李燮]] would not establish a king later links father and son through public-order judgment about dynastic titles.

Hanji 829 adds the immediate political consequence. The episode says Li Gu's preference for Liu Suan touches [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan|梁妠 / 梁太后]] and Liang-family interests, helping explain Li Gu's removal. [[TangHengLateHan|唐衡]] and [[ZuoGuanLateHan|左悺]] later tell Huan that Li Gu and [[DuQiaoLateHan|杜乔]] opposed his accession, converting Liu Suan's candidacy into personal imperial resentment.

The same source supplies Liu Suan's own final branch. In the eleventh month of 147 CE, Liu Wen and Liu Wei claim Qinghe King Liu Suan should rule the realm and attempt to install him. After the plot leaks, [[XieHaoQingheLateHan|谢浩]] refuses to cooperate and is killed; officials impeach Liu Suan, who is demoted to Weishi Hou, exiled to Guiyang, and later dies by suicide.

## Key Characteristics

- Imperial-clan alternative to Emperor Huan in Li Gu's earlier succession judgment.
- Qinghe king whose unused candidacy remains politically active after Huan's accession.
- Figure whose name becomes a resentment channel against Li Gu and Du Qiao.
- Target of a failed 147 CE enthronement plot by Liu Wen and Liu Wei.
- Demoted, exiled, and suicide-ending figure after the plot is suppressed.

## Qualifications

This page remains bounded to two podcast source notes and is not a full Liu Suan biography. Hanji 929 gives a later moral-political memory of Li Gu's succession judgment rather than a complete accession debate. Hanji 829 shows Liu Suan as the focus of a plot and punishment outcome, but it does not prove how much agency Liu Suan himself had in Liu Wen and Liu Wei's attempt.
