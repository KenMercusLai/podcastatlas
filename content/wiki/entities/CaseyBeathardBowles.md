# Casey Beathard Bowles

## Overview

Casey Beathard Bowles is a singer-songwriter whose memory lapse during a [[RevisionistHistory]] interview became an unplanned demonstration of the emotional phenomenon the episode was investigating.

## Current Profile

While discussing a song about her mother, Bowles described repeatedly forgetting a line about never being alone and connected the lapse to her mother's selflessness and her own contrasting experience. When she performed the song, she again lost her place. The recording therefore moved from explanation to observation: a failed original interview plan led to a live instance of parapraxis with personal meaning.

Her role is bounded to this retrospective example; the source does not supply a broader musical biography or establish a clinical explanation for the lapse.
