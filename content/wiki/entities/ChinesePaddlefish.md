# Chinese paddlefish

The Chinese paddlefish appears in [[socialist-mobility-a-telling-michigan-vote-6a74600ec3e1347e8b5bf216]] as an already extinct Yangtze species. The source uses it alongside the probably extinct [[Baiji]] to limit the optimism around [[YangtzeFishingBanRecovery]].

Its wiki role is a loss marker: fish biomass may rise after the commercial fishing ban, but extinct species show why recovery cannot be reduced to one aggregate number.
