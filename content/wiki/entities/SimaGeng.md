# 司马梗 / Sima Geng

司马梗 / Sima Geng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-1-lq4wvkd8qx3q7zji6fnnkxi3qcl1]] as one of the [[QinState|秦国]] commanders used after the [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]]. In the episode's [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] account, [[BaiQi|白起]] orders [[WangHe|王龁]] toward [[WuanWarringStates|武安]] and [[Pilao|皮牢]], while Sima Geng leads another route toward [[TaiyuanWarringStates|太原]] and [[Shangdang|上党郡]].

His role is source-scoped and operational. The episode uses him to show that Qin's post-Changping pressure was not merely a threat against [[Handan|邯郸]] in the abstract; it was a set of converging movements intended to strip [[ZhaoState|赵国]] of surrounding positions before the capital became the likely next target.
