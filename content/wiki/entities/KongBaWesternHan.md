## Overview

孔霸 / Kong Ba enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-476-kongzi-de-12-shisun-kongba-jielu-zhichang-yinmi-ltjlhphlrqnazeqh2cc2hgsvrvwc|Hanji 476]] as a twelfth-generation descendant of [[Confucius|孔子]], a `Shangshu` teacher of [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]], and a Yuan-era honored scholar who refuses repeated high-office opportunities.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Kong Ba as a prestige holder whose best move is not to cash prestige into maximum office. Yuan had studied with him as crown prince and later rewarded him with Guannei Hou and Jishizhong status, keeping him near the ruler. Yet Kong Ba repeatedly says his virtue and talent cannot bear such wealth and rank, and Yuan eventually stops pressing him while respecting him more.

The host interprets this refusal as strategic preservation of the Kong family's standing ground. Kong Ba's durable asset is not military force or factional court skill, but Confucian orthodox lineage. If he became a top minister, he would be pulled into unresolved court struggles among Confucian ideals, Legalist aristocratic interests, emperor-teacher intimacy, and eunuch power. The page therefore treats Kong Ba as a source-bounded example of refusing a visible promotion because it would exchange long-run symbolic capital for near-term political exposure.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Confucian lineage figure identified as a twelfth-generation descendant of Confucius.
- Former `Shangshu` teacher of Han Yuandi before Yuan's accession.
- Receives honor and proximity through Guannei Hou and Jishizhong status.
- Repeatedly refuses high office by citing insufficient virtue and ability.
- Interpreted by the host as preserving Confucian lineage legitimacy rather than entering a dangerous court struggle.
- Exposed in principle to aristocratic, Legalist, and eunuch-linked risks because imperial-teacher status would mark him as the ruler's intimate.
