# 楚悼王 / King Dao of Chu

楚悼王 / King Dao of Chu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-2-lmfzo4sl9nd5kz3mrxdl1dl9efei]] as [[ChuShengWang|楚声王]]'s son and successor after the latter is killed. The source uses him mainly as part of the succession notice.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-2-lvaqlgyccrugfo0l-wuwf1mt1kcm]] develops his reign by making him the ruler who immediately recognizes and employs [[WuQi|吴起]] after Wu Qi flees [[WeiState|魏国]]. Under Chu Dao Wang's backing, Wu Qi pursues centralizing reforms that strengthen [[ChuState|楚国]] but anger nobles, royal relatives, and idle officials, making Chu Dao Wang's patronage central to [[WarringStatesReformBacklash]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-3-ltca-ikuzo9ej7a0lfrgwv2-wulb]] turns his death into the trigger for Wu Qi's fall. The episode says Wu Qi can survive only while Chu Dao Wang protects him; once the ruler dies, the nobles immediately attack. Chu Dao Wang's corpse then becomes the final political shield in the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] account, because arrows that kill Wu Qi also damage the dead king's body and expose the attackers to punishment under [[ChuSuwang|楚肃王]].
