# Charles Sled

Charles Sled appears in [[692-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-massacre-in-paris-part-2-glt4665799025]] as a courier and fixer attached to [[WilliamAllen]] and the Catholic priest network. The episode says he listened to priest conversations and then appeared before [[FrancisWalsingham]] in London in May 1580.

Sled's importance is that he converts diffuse suspicion into actionable intelligence. He gives Walsingham around 300 English Catholic names, pensions, and physical descriptions, making the [[SeminaryPriestMission|seminary priest mission]] legible as a dossier inside the [[ElizabethanRecusancySecurityState]]. The episode presents this as the kind of information that confirmed Walsingham's darkest reading of Catholic mission networks.
