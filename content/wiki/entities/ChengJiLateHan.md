# 程畿 / Cheng Ji (late Han)

## Overview
程畿 / Cheng Ji is a late-Han Yi Province official whose wiki role is defined by his refusal to help [[PangXiLateHan|庞羲]] turn fear of [[LiuZhang|刘璋]] into armed self-protection. He becomes the source case for [[OfficialDutyOverPatronCommand|守职胜私命]]: a subordinate can remain willing to serve a superior in office while rejecting an unrighteous mobilization order.

## Current Profile
Cheng Ji appears after [[ZhaoWeiLateHan|赵韪]]'s revolt has been put down and Pang Xi worries that Liu Zhang will move against him. Pang Xi sends for Cheng Ji and wants him to gather Cong forces, but Cheng Ji argues that slander and suspicion should be met through loyal explanation, not two-heartedness. When threatened, he invokes [[LeYang|乐羊]] eating his son during the Zhongshan campaign to say that great public duty can override private attachment. Pang Xi abandons the armed path, apologizes with gifts, and Liu Zhang later promotes Cheng Ji to Jiangyang administrator.

## Key Characteristics
- Refuses to treat a superior's fear as permission for disloyal mobilization.
- Separates legitimate service under Pang Xi from participating in an unrighteous act.
- Uses classical precedent to make his refusal intelligible inside elite moral language.
- De-escalates a local militarization path and is recognized by Liu Zhang afterward.

## Qualifications
The page is bounded to the Hanji 1055 episode. It does not reconstruct Cheng Ji's full career outside this incident, and it treats the Le Yang comparison as Cheng Ji's rhetoric rather than proof that every private tie must yield to political office.
