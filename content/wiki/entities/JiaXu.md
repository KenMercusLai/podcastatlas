# 贾诩 / Jia Xu

贾诩 / Jia Xu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1013-dianwei-zhisi-jingshi-caocao-haose-haileta-lhe6jrpjp3cfbdhvjtdiezijfnoh|Hanji 1013]] as the adviser to [[ZhangXiu|张绣]] during the Wancheng crisis. After [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s secret plan to kill Zhang Xiu leaks, the source says Jia Xu helps turn Zhang Xiu's exposed position into a pre-emptive attack.

The plan is built on permission and timing. Zhang Xiu asks to change camp, says his route must pass through Cao Cao's camp, and has his troops move in armor. Cao Cao accepts the explanation, allowing Zhang Xiu's force to enter the camp posture needed for the night attack.

In this source, Jia Xu is not yet a full biographical figure. He is the strategist who sees that Cao Cao's desire, confidence, and loose guard posture have created a usable tactical opening.
