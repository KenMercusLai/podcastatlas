# Hongmeng Zhixing G9 / 鸿蒙智行 G9

## Overview

Hongmeng Zhixing G9 / 鸿蒙智行 G9 is the hard-SUV and outdoor-lifestyle product case discussed in [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]]. The episode positions it with the phrase "让硬派更有生活，让豪华更有远方" and uses it to show how the [[HongmengZhixing|鸿蒙智行]] portfolio tries to combine rugged travel, comfort, camping, oxygen, and scenario-specific controls.

## Current Profile

The current wiki profile treats G9 as a source-scoped product-definition case, not as an independently evaluated vehicle. Its importance is that it makes [[OutdoorVehicleLifestyleDesign]] visible: hard-SUV capability is framed together with tent access, tailgate cooking, side awning, cabin oxygen, comfortable chassis behavior, and physical buttons for outdoor or low-attention scenarios.

## Key Characteristics

- Positioned as a differentiated Hongmeng Zhixing product rather than another M9-style luxury SUV.
- Combines hard-SUV imagery with camping and daily-comfort claims.
- Uses outdoor accessories such as a roof tent, tailgate kitchen, one-touch tent control, and side awning in the episode's account.
- Adds all-terrain and chassis claims through Huawei's Turing platform, disconnectable active anti-roll bars, and mechanical differential locks.
- Includes oxygen and physical-control features aimed at high-altitude, outdoor, or recovery scenarios.
