# Jonathan Ross

Jonathan Ross appears in [[e228-guge-tpu-neng-handong-yingweida-ma-qian-tpu-gongchengshi-shouci-jiemi-fd17090c-0d72-4c0d-aa3e-9b00bc062149]] through the episode's origin story for [[Google]] [[TPU|TPUs]]. The source says a 2013-era speech-recognition demonstration showed that deep-learning workloads needed [[GPU]]-style acceleration but that serving all users with GPUs would make data-center costs prohibitive, helping motivate a dedicated inference accelerator.

His role in this wiki is source-scoped: he anchors the episode's claim that TPU began as a response to internal product-scale cost pressure, not merely as an abstract research-chip project.
