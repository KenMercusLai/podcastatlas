# Unilever

[[meizhuang-jutou-jiti-dingshang-toufa-xihu-shengyi-weihe-you-re-qilai-1005860109]] adds Unilever as one of the resource-rich daily-chemical players moving technology and function claims into mainstream shampoo and conditioner. The episode uses Unilever alongside [[LOreal|L'Oreal / 欧莱雅]] to show how large CPG companies can push [[HaircareSkincareization]] down-market while keeping value pricing.

Unilever is the consumer-goods company that acquired [[SeventhGeneration]] in 2016, according to [[advice-line-with-jeffrey-hollender-of-seventh-generation-f8ce2410-1176-47d9-be44-03f9fa945714]]. The episode uses the acquisition as background for [[JeffreyHollender]]'s lessons about purpose-driven business, growth, and the responsibilities of companies with social or environmental missions.
