# Unilever

## Overview

Unilever is a global consumer-goods company that the bounded sources connect to purpose-driven brand acquisition, mass-market hair care, and a reported portfolio shift toward beauty, personal care, and home care.

## Current Profile

The sources show Unilever operating at two levels. It can acquire mission-led consumer brands such as [[SeventhGeneration]], creating the governance question of how purpose survives inside a large owner, while its scale lets it move technology and functional claims into mainstream categories such as hair care. The latest episode adds portfolio concentration: after separating ice cream and agreeing to combine its food business with a U.S. condiment maker, the remaining company is reportedly orienting around care categories.

## Key Characteristics

- Large consumer-goods owner spanning brands, channels, and mass-market product categories.
- Acquirer of mission-led businesses whose purpose must coexist with financial and operating scale.
- Participant in the functional and premium reshaping of mainstream hair care.
- Company reportedly narrowing its remaining portfolio toward beauty, personal care, and home care.

Investor concerns that food brands may be marginalized are risks, not established outcomes.
