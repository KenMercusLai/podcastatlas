# Li Auto / 理想汽车

## Overview
Li Auto / 理想汽车 is represented as a Chinese new-energy-vehicle company moving through four connected frames: family-use and range-extension origin, premium-SUV competition, vocational and manufacturing partnership, and an AI/embodied-intelligence strategy.

## Current Profile
The founder-origin source says Li Auto began by combining a six-seat family job, a roughly middle-premium price, and [[RangeExtendedEV|range extension]] when pure EV charging and battery constraints made household travel harder. [[LiAutoONE|理想 ONE]] validated the positioning while also exposing early engineering and quality failures. [[LiAutoMEGA|MEGA]] later exposed the difference between a coherent internal product thesis and successful external launch, communication, and infrastructure readiness.

In [[ep241-xiaoqi-hezuo-shi-xinyidai-de-tiefanwan-ma-lnmpsbvoqbexeardxyx88hywufig]], Li Auto is the enterprise partner in a [[SchoolEnterpriseCooperation|校企合作]] case, with an [[IndustrialCollege|产业学院]] inside [[ChangzhouIndustrialVocationalTechnicalCollege|常州工业职业技术学院]]. That source ties Li Auto to [[Changzhou|常州]]'s new-energy-vehicle and battery cluster.

In [[143-wei-xiao-li-de-gaoduan-tansuo-feng-tou-quan-1-143-1]], Li Auto becomes the premium family-SUV case. [[LiAutoL9]] and the "dad car" positioning helped define a high-end Chinese EV reason to buy, but the source says competitors copied the formula and pressured Li Auto toward value-for-money volume defense.

The founder-strategy layer now describes Li Auto as moving toward AI and embodied intelligence through [[LiAutoL9Levius|L9 LEVIUS]], the [[LiAutoM100Chip|马赫 M100]] chip, system-level vehicle agents, autonomous driving, factory robots, humanoid robots, AI organization design, and overseas expansion, as explained by [[LiXiangLiAuto|李想]] in [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]].

## Key Characteristics
- New-energy-vehicle company founded around family scenarios, six-seat packaging, and a range-extension response to early charging constraints.
- Product organization whose ONE and MEGA cases join strong user definition to engineering, quality, launch, and public-communication correction.
- Manufacturing company connected to Changzhou vocational training and local industrial clusters.
- Company under pricing and margin pressure as competitors copy the visible family-SUV formula.
- Founder-led AI and embodied-intelligence strategy that treats vehicles, robots, chips, models, operating systems, and production workflows as one long-term stack.
- Source-scoped company case whose historical motives, product figures, M100 chip, robot commercialization, L3/L4 roadmap, and overseas outcomes remain incompletely verified.

## Qualifications
The vocational and brand sources analyze Li Auto from outside the company, while the origin and AI/embodied-intelligence sources are interviews with its founder. Claims about founding motives, investor actions, internal conflict, product faults and remedies, public-relations attacks, user profiles, charging coverage, internal AI productivity, L9 LEVIUS capability, M100 advantage, robot timing, autonomy readiness, and overseas fit remain source-scoped.
