# Li Auto / 理想汽车

Li Auto / 理想汽车 is the new-energy-vehicle company case in [[ep241-xiaoqi-hezuo-shi-xinyidai-de-tiefanwan-ma-lnmpsbvoqbexeardxyx88hywufig]]. The episode describes a Li Auto [[IndustrialCollege|产业学院]] inside [[ChangzhouIndustrialVocationalTechnicalCollege|常州工业职业技术学院]], with vehicles, training space, and company employee training also placed on campus.

The source uses Li Auto to show how [[SchoolEnterpriseCooperation|校企合作]] changes when a local industry cluster is strong. In [[Changzhou|常州]], the surrounding new-energy-vehicle and battery chain makes it easier for the school to adjust from weaker traditional majors toward training that fits nearby companies.
