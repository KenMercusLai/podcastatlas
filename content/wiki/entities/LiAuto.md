# Li Auto / 理想汽车

## Overview
Li Auto / 理想汽车 is represented in the wiki as a Chinese new-energy-vehicle company moving through three frames: vocational-training partner, premium family-SUV brand, and now AI/embodied-intelligence company.

## Current Profile
In [[ep241-xiaoqi-hezuo-shi-xinyidai-de-tiefanwan-ma-lnmpsbvoqbexeardxyx88hywufig]], Li Auto is the enterprise partner in a [[SchoolEnterpriseCooperation|校企合作]] case, with an [[IndustrialCollege|产业学院]] inside [[ChangzhouIndustrialVocationalTechnicalCollege|常州工业职业技术学院]]. That source ties Li Auto to [[Changzhou|常州]]'s new-energy-vehicle and battery cluster.

In [[143-wei-xiao-li-de-gaoduan-tansuo-feng-tou-quan-1-143-1]], Li Auto becomes the premium family-SUV case. [[LiAutoL9]] and the "dad car" positioning helped define a high-end Chinese EV reason to buy, but the source says competitors copied the formula and pressured Li Auto toward value-for-money volume defense.

The founder-strategy layer now describes Li Auto as moving toward AI and embodied intelligence through [[LiAutoL9Levius|L9 LEVIUS]], the [[LiAutoM100Chip|马赫 M100]] chip, system-level vehicle agents, autonomous driving, factory robots, humanoid robots, AI organization design, and overseas expansion, as explained by [[LiXiangLiAuto|李想]] in [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]].

## Key Characteristics
- New-energy-vehicle company connected to Changzhou vocational training and local industrial clusters.
- Family-SUV premium brand whose early differentiation was comfort, household scenarios, and clear user positioning.
- Company under pricing and margin pressure as competitors copy the visible family-SUV formula.
- Founder-led AI and embodied-intelligence strategy that treats vehicles, robots, chips, models, operating systems, and production workflows as one long-term stack.
- Source-scoped strategy case whose M100 chip, robot commercialization, L3/L4 roadmap, and overseas outcomes remain unproven in the current evidence inventory.

Claims about internal AI productivity, L9 LEVIUS capability, M100 chip advantage, robot shipment timing, L3/L4 readiness, and overseas market fit remain source-scoped.
