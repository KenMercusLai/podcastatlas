# 赵括 / Zhao Kuo

赵括 / Zhao Kuo enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-2-lrlulw0b6v0fb2xpr2i5ifem1h4n]] as the later [[ZhaoState|赵国]] commander associated with [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]]. This episode does not yet narrate his command decisions; it only mentions him in the tradition that [[FengTing|冯亭]] later followed Zhao Kuo in the battle and died there.

The page should therefore remain source-scoped until later Changping material is ingested. Its current function is to preserve the link between the Shangdang decision, Feng Ting's uncertain later fate, and the coming Changping branch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-1-loxyybm3njrtnudydafwzu9jp9jz]] makes Zhao Kuo central rather than merely prospective. After [[FanJu|范雎]] spreads the claim that Qin does not fear [[LianPo|廉颇]] but does fear Zhao Kuo, [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] appoints Zhao Kuo to replace Lian Po at the front. The episode treats that appointment as enemy-aided wishful thinking, not as evidence that Qin truly fears him.

The source also gives three independent warnings about Zhao Kuo. [[LinXiangru|蔺相如]] says appointing him by reputation is like "胶柱鼓瑟"; [[ZhaoShe|赵奢]] had earlier warned that his son treats war too lightly despite being able to debate military theory; and [[ZhaoKuoMother|赵母]] argues from command character, noting his arrogance, private accumulation, and failure to share rewards and hardship with soldiers. Zhao Kuo therefore becomes the main case for [[PaperWarCommandTrap|纸上谈兵式统帅陷阱]] and for [[BattlefieldCommanderReplacementRisk|阵前换将风险]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-2-lvqctrazn8ddkcnp-lhspzzmzmns]] gives the battlefield consequence. Zhao Kuo changes the army's dispositions after taking command and attacks across the Dan River, but because he does not know Bai Qi is the true opposing commander, he reads Qin's retreat as opportunity rather than lure. Once Qin's barriers stop his forward movement and the side forces cut his rear, Zhao Kuo's main force is trapped without food or support.
