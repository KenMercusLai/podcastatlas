# 天线宝宝猞猁 / Tianxian Baobao Lynx

天线宝宝 is the rescued [[Lynx|lynx]] individual at the center of [[163-jiao-yi-zhi-sheli-wangji-dongwuyuan-xiye-de-gushi-bi-fengkuang-dongwucheng-2-geng-jingcai-939887179]] and [[JiaoYiZhiSheliWangjiDongwuyuan|《教一只猞猁忘记动物园》]]. The source describes it as an under-one-year-old Eurasian lynx rescued on 2021-04-06 after entering a sheep pen, killing five lambs, and being caught by a herder while weak and right-eye blind.

After treatment at [[XiningWildlifePark|西宁野生动物园]], 天线宝宝 recovered sight and showed live-prey hunting ability. The team's harder task was [[WildReleaseHumanAvoidance]]: keeping it from seeing visitors, from treating people as food sources, or from turning the zoo into a social environment it would seek after release.

## Key Claims
- 天线宝宝's original conflict with a sheep pen is read as a failed juvenile survival situation rather than as simple aggression.
- Its rehabilitation required both health recovery and behavior testing, because vision and hunting mattered to release viability.
- Its 2022-04 release used a satellite collar and a temporary shelter in a protected area, but the animal left the shelter quickly and did not return.
- Post-release movement, signal gaps, river crossings, and later settlement near a village made the case an example of monitoring under uncertainty.
- Yuan Zhang's later visual encounter with 天线宝宝 gives the source its emotional center, but the episode keeps his interpretation of the animal's motive source-scoped and cautious.
