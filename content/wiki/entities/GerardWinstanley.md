# Gerard Winstanley

## Overview

Gerard Winstanley was the writer and leader associated with the [[Diggers]], whose communal cultivation experiment made radical land criticism visible during the English Republic.

## Current Profile

Winstanley argued that exclusive land ownership should dissolve and that people should cultivate the earth together. He joined theory to a small practical settlement at St George's Hill, but his durable importance in the episode comes from writing that carried the movement's claims far beyond its limited membership.

His case also distinguishes central and local repression. Republican commanders largely regarded the group as harmless, while nearby property holders successfully forced it out.

## Key Characteristics

- Radical writer challenging exclusive land ownership.
- Leader associated with the Diggers' communal cultivation.
- Connected political theology to a practical land experiment.
- Achieved influence disproportionate to the movement's size through publication.
