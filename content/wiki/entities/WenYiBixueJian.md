## Overview

温仪（《碧血剑》） / Wen Yi is [[WenQingqing|温青青]]'s mother and [[XiaXueyi|夏雪宜]]'s beloved, whose relationship with her family's enemy briefly opens a route out of revenge.

## Current Profile

Wen Yi begins as Xia Xueyi's captive but comes to understand the Wen family's wrongdoing and experiences respect and gentleness where she expected violence. The episode proposes sympathy, guilt, admiration, dependence, contrast, and safety as possible contributors to love while refusing a confident clinical explanation.

Her family then uses her unwittingly to incapacitate Xia. Years later, learning from [[YuanChengzhi|袁承志]] that Xia died rather than abandoned her and continued to remember her removes two central uncertainties: whether he had left voluntarily and whether he blamed her for the betrayal.

## Key Characteristics

- Occupies a weak position inside the Wen family and knows something of its wrongdoing.
- Develops attachment to a captor who behaves more respectfully than expected.
- Becomes an unwitting instrument in her family's betrayal of Xia Xueyi.
- Lives with uncertainty about abandonment and blame until Yuan supplies the death message.
- Receives emotional clarification rather than material repair before death.

## Qualifications

The page does not adopt the episode's tentative Stockholm-syndrome analogy. A fictional captivity romance cannot be clinically classified from conversational plot summary, and respect under coercive conditions does not by itself establish free consent or a general model of real relationships.
