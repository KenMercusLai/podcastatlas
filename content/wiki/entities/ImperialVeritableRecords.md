# Imperial Veritable Records / 帝王实录宝训

## Overview

Imperial veritable records and exemplary instructions are dynastic memory projects discussed through the First Historical Archives of China and Huangshicheng in [[244-shuxie-gei-guoqu-weilai-de-ren-yu-shenming-zhongguo-wenming-zhong-de-shuxie-chuantong-yu-changqi-zhuyi-8225426843-437457]]. They turn court documentation into authoritative records for a dynasty and evidence for later historians.

## Current Profile

The episode emphasizes both monumental custody and source criticism. Large gilt-covered nanmu cabinets and purpose-built archival space gave imperial records near-sacral status, but official compilation did not make them neutral: later rulers and compilers could recast predecessors, enemies, and political legitimacy. Their historical value lies in the density and survival of information that can still be compared and criticized, not in an assumption of transparent truth.

## Key Characteristics

- Consolidate court records into official narratives of a reign and its governing lessons.
- Receive unusually high material and architectural protection as dynastic memory.
- Preserve political and institutional information even when the narrative is shaped after events.
- Allow later public and scholarly reuse beyond the narrow audience and purpose of their creation.

## Evidence

- Archival custody - [[244-shuxie-gei-guoqu-weilai-de-ren-yu-shenming-zhongguo-wenming-zhong-de-shuxie-chuantong-yu-changqi-zhuyi-8225426843-437457]] describes Huangshicheng and large decorated cabinets used to store records and instructions.
- Compilation chain - [[244-shuxie-gei-guoqu-weilai-de-ren-yu-shenming-zhongguo-wenming-zhong-de-shuxie-chuantong-yu-changqi-zhuyi-8225426843-437457]] presents daily court notes and other records as inputs to veritable records and, ideally, later dynastic history.
- Political editing - [[244-shuxie-gei-guoqu-weilai-de-ren-yu-shenming-zhongguo-wenming-zhong-de-shuxie-chuantong-yu-changqi-zhuyi-8225426843-437457]] cites repeated revision of the Ming founder's record and compiler influence on reputations as reasons for caution.
- Later utility - [[244-shuxie-gei-guoqu-weilai-de-ren-yu-shenming-zhongguo-wenming-zhong-de-shuxie-chuantong-yu-changqi-zhuyi-8225426843-437457]] still treats Ming veritable records as indispensable because comparatively little Ming archival material survives.

## Qualifications

The page groups veritable records and *baoxun* because the episode discusses their shared custody and memory function; they are distinct genres and should not be treated as interchangeable. Examples of editing illustrate bias but do not make every entry false.

## What Changed

- Created a source-critical profile joining official memory, monumental storage, political editing, and later historical reuse.
