# Dom Cotton

## Overview
Dom Cotton is a co-founder of [[Newlane]], a UK-made folding bicycle-helmet company.

## Current Profile
Cotton's case concerns the transition from a long development and certification cycle into repeatable consumer growth. He presents a product with early revenue and a distinctive physical form, but also with pressure from domestic manufacturing cost, paid media, and channel margins. The advice redirects his growth question toward buyer influence, habitual non-use, premium positioning, and physical word of mouth.

## Key Characteristics
- Spent six years developing and certifying Newlane's folding helmet.
- Prioritizes UK manufacturing for quality control.
- Faces a margin tradeoff across production, advertising, distributors, and retailers.
- Recognizes gifting as a meaningful purchase context.
- Is evaluating how product design can generate visible conversation and adoption.
