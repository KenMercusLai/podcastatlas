## Overview

楼兰 / Loulan Kingdom enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-403-1-xisha-xiongnu-shizhe-xihan-zui-tiexue-waijiaoguan-jingshi-ta-lum-xolrqlwjgmv5xscssenogyx|Hanji 403-1]] as a Western Han Western Regions polity caught between [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] and [[Xiongnu|Xiongnu]] pressure. The source presents it as both an object of Han rebuke and an intelligence channel that helps [[FuJieziWesternHan|傅介子]] locate Xiongnu envoys.

## Current Profile

The source's Loulan is a small frontier polity whose survival strategy is double-sided. Because Xiongnu power is closer and forceful, Loulan and similar Western Regions states lean toward Xiongnu, sometimes cooperate against Han envoys, and fail to report Xiongnu envoy movement. Han Zhao's court responds by sending Fu Jiezi to rebuke and frighten Loulan rather than by launching a large campaign.

Loulan's immediate behavior shifts under pressure. After Fu threatens that Han troops can arrive and asks why Loulan did not report Xiongnu envoys passing westward, the king apologizes and supplies intelligence that the envoys have recently moved toward Wusun by way of Qiuci. After Fu kills the envoys, Loulan sends representatives with him to the Han capital and promises to break with Xiongnu and submit to Han.

The episode also limits the conclusion. Loulan's submission is effective as an immediate signal, but later renewed Western Regions disorder and the closing "Loulan king" cliffhanger show that the settlement is not durable.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Regions polity positioned between Han and Xiongnu power.
- Presented as leaning toward Xiongnu because Xiongnu pressure is nearer and more immediate.
- Rebuked by Fu Jiezi for not reporting Xiongnu envoy movement.
- Supplies the intelligence that lets Fu pursue the Xiongnu envoys through Qiuci.
- Sends a submission signal to Han after Fu's violent deterrent action.
- Remains unstable enough that Fu's later second mission targets the Loulan king.
