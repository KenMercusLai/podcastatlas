# Drownproof

## Overview
[[Drownproof]] is [[AndyStumpf]]'s book as discussed in the [[the-mental-frame-specific-daily-actions-to-succeed-andy-stumpf-scim2498586099]] Huberman Lab source note. The episode treats it as the source of Stumpf's practical tools for agency, attention, adversity, discipline, vulnerability, and defining enough.

## Current Profile
Within the wiki, [[Drownproof]] functions as a self-regulation text rather than a biography page. [[AndrewHuberman]] says the episode was prompted less by Stumpf's military or wingsuiting career than by the tools and life lessons in the book. The source presents those tools through [[ConcernInfluenceSorting]], [[SlightlyHarderChoice]], adversity time-frame shortening, self-talk management, open pain communication, and success-boundary reflection.

## Key Characteristics
- Supplies the episode's practical distinction between concern and influence.
- Frames agency as repeated control of thoughts, words, time, planning, and responses.
- Treats small disciplined actions as consequential even when they are not visible to others.
- Applies tools to divorce, parenting strain, pain, suicide discussion, and life after elite military service.
- Keeps the success question bounded by relationships, family, fulfillment, and knowing enough.
