# Lucas Babic

Lucas Babic is the freelance video editor in [[currency-chaos-in-argentina-summer-school]] whose 2022 household behavior illustrates Argentina's loss of monetary trust. Originally from [[Brazil]] and living in [[Argentina]] for 30 years, he keeps U.S. dollars in a shoebox because he does not trust the peso to preserve purchasing power.

The episode uses Babic to make [[CurrencyRisk]] and [[MonetaryVolatility]] tangible. His comparison between a stable $10 bill and rapidly devaluing pesos shows how inflation turns saving, grocery planning, and ordinary price memory into active work.
