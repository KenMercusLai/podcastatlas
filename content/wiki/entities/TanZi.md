# 檀子 / Tan Zi

檀子 / Tan Zi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-15-jiu-bu-chun-yinfa-de-shijie-dazhan-li-iojxthq8komhlhyumho3yvtem]] as one of the officials [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] names when [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]] asks whether [[QiState|齐国]] has treasures. The source does not give a full biography; it uses Tan Zi as evidence that useful officials are more valuable than luminous pearls.

In the episode's reading, Tan Zi belongs to [[TalentAsStateTreasure|以贤臣为宝]] because his value is measured by border security, public order, and state attraction rather than rarity or display. The page is therefore source-scoped: Tan Zi is preserved as a named personnel example inside Qi Wei Wang's governance rhetoric.
