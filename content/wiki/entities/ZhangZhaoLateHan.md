# 张昭 / Zhang Zhao (Late Han)

张昭 / Zhang Zhao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-994-genzhe-dongwu-zhongchen-lvfan-xuexi-zhichang-zhihui-lgqp2vqmh1mss96gcwmflp-ccpfu|Hanji 994]] as the Jiangdong adviser whom [[SunCe|孙策]] appoints as `长史` and treats with the manners of a teacher and friend. The episode says Sun Ce entrusts major administrative and military affairs to Zhang Zhao while building a bench that also includes [[ZhangHongLateHan|张纮]], [[QinSongLateHan|秦松]], and [[ChenDuanLateHan|陈端]].

Zhang Zhao becomes the episode's main case for [[SubordinateSuccessNonJealousy|下属成就不嫉妒]]. Northern士大夫 send him letters that attribute Jiangdong's achievements to him, but Sun Ce is pleased rather than threatened. The host reads this as a leader understanding that subordinate reputation can increase the organization's and leader's reputation when the underlying work is real.

The source uses Zhang Zhao alongside [[QiHuanGong|齐桓公]] and [[GuanZhong|管仲]] to make a broader talent-use point: true talent should compensate for the leader's limits, not merely perform low-level tasks inside the leader's existing cognition. Zhang Zhao should therefore connect to the wiki's talent and leadership concepts more than to a full biography until more sources develop his later career.
