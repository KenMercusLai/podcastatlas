# Fremont Unified School District

## Overview
Fremont Unified School District is the school-board setting in [[404-shaoyang-yiwei-huaren-shiyiyuan-qinlide-meiguo-difang-zhengzhi-f96bdc7a-c667-4594-8719-2070d24f8231]], where [[ShaoYang|邵阳 / Shao Yang]] says he moved from parent and church youth concerns into elected office.

## Current Profile
The source presents the district as a high-pressure Bay Area public-school environment with strong Chinese American parent interest in education. Shao says student mental health and parent representation brought him into school politics, and he describes budget choices around mental-health counselors as a concrete board responsibility.

The district is also the source's main arena for [[SchoolParentalRightsConflict]]. Shao criticizes parts of California school policy and curriculum around gender identity, sex education, teacher speech, and parent notification. The page records that as his source-scoped account, not as an independent finding about the district or statewide policy.

## Key Characteristics
- Local public-school district in [[FremontCalifornia|Fremont]] where Shao served after winning a school-board seat in 2014.
- High academic-pressure setting in the source account, with Chinese American parent concerns and student mental health central to Shao's campaign motive.
- Board-governance case where budget priorities, superintendent hiring, and district policy are the elected board's major levers.
- Mental-health resource case where Shao says counselor ratios improved and elementary counseling was expanded.
- Education-culture conflict setting for sex education, gender-identity policy, parent notice, teacher political speech, and union influence.

## Evidence
- Campaign motive: [[404-shaoyang-yiwei-huaren-shiyiyuan-qinlide-meiguo-difang-zhengzhi-f96bdc7a-c667-4594-8719-2070d24f8231]] says Shao became concerned after hearing church youth repeatedly ask for prayer about grades and after seeing student mental-health pressure in competitive Fremont schools.
- Board role: [[404-shaoyang-yiwei-huaren-shiyiyuan-qinlide-meiguo-difang-zhengzhi-f96bdc7a-c667-4594-8719-2070d24f8231]] describes a school board as handling budget, major appointments, and policy while hiring a superintendent for daily operation.
- Counseling resources: [[404-shaoyang-yiwei-huaren-shiyiyuan-qinlide-meiguo-difang-zhengzhi-f96bdc7a-c667-4594-8719-2070d24f8231]] has Shao say the district moved from roughly one counselor per 800-1000 students toward one per 400 students and expanded counseling into elementary school.
- Curriculum dispute: [[404-shaoyang-yiwei-huaren-shiyiyuan-qinlide-meiguo-difang-zhengzhi-f96bdc7a-c667-4594-8719-2070d24f8231]] describes a 2018 sex-education curriculum review that ended in rejection of the proposed materials after a long meeting.
- Union and teacher conflict: [[404-shaoyang-yiwei-huaren-shiyiyuan-qinlide-meiguo-difang-zhengzhi-f96bdc7a-c667-4594-8719-2070d24f8231]] records Shao's claims about teacher political speech, teacher discipline limits, high labor-cost share, tenure protection, and union opposition to his later city race.

## Qualifications
The page is based on Shao's interview account. It does not verify the named depression survey, internal district records, curriculum text, teacher-discipline details, union mailers, or current district policy. Opposing parent, teacher, union, student, and administrator perspectives are not represented in the source.
