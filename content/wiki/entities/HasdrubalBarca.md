# Hasdrubal Barca / 哈斯德鲁巴·巴卡

## Overview

Hasdrubal Barca / 哈斯德鲁巴·巴卡 is [[HannibalBarca]]'s brother and a Carthaginian commander whose armies defend the [[BarcidSpanishPowerBase|Barcid Spanish power base]] before his attempt to reinforce Italy ends at the [[BattleOfMetaurus]].

## Current Profile

Hasdrubal controls southern Iberian forces and the strategic center at [[NewCarthage]] while Hannibal campaigns in Italy. His first attempted march north after Cannae is stopped at the [[BattleOfIbera]], preventing an early concentration of the brothers' armies.

He later helps defeat and kill Scipio's father and uncle, but the younger [[ScipioAfricanus]] captures New Carthage while the Carthaginian field armies are dispersed. Hasdrubal survives a later defeat by Scipio, withdraws, rebuilds, crosses the Pyrenees and Alps, and reaches northern Italy with a reinforcement army.

The plan fails at the communication and concentration stage. Roman forces intercept his message to Hannibal, Gaius Claudius Nero secretly joins Marcus Livius Salinator, and the combined consular army defeats Hasdrubal at the Metaurus before the brothers unite.

## Key Characteristics

- Commands a major branch of Carthaginian power in Iberia.
- Repeatedly seeks to transfer Spanish resources and troops into Hannibal's Italian war.
- Helps destroy the older Scipios' armies in 211 BC.
- Preserves enough force after Scipio's attack to cross into Italy.
- Dies before completing the operational concentration with Hannibal.
