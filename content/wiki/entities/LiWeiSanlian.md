# 李维 / Li Wei (Sanlian)

李维 / Li Wei is the [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] speaker in [[ep275-token-tongzhang-shidai-shui-hai-neng-bu-ke-tidai-renzai-zhongliu-tebie-cehua-01-lgnagvell4jyvv7eejrcgno2xjsr]]. He frames the episode through workplace AI use, team adoption of tools such as [[ChatGPT]], [[Codex]], and [[Claude]], and the gap between visible token consumption and a "qualitative" productivity shift.

His contribution is strongest on the operator side of the discussion. He keeps asking how AI changes actual work: whether translation, interviews, sales, and agent supervision create better outcomes, or simply make employees busier under a new tool regime.
