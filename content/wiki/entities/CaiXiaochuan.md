# 蔡小川 / Cai Xiaochuan

蔡小川 / Cai Xiaochuan is the [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] photographer and [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] guest in [[ep249-minnan-gucheng-youshenji-chuantong-yishi-beihou-de-xiangtu-wangluo-lpk1vb9-zmgmuous1nyzefgapgww]]. Her role is to make the [[YongningAncientCity|永宁古城]] ritual case tangible through visual and bodily field observation rather than only through institutional analysis.

She contrasts highly visible processional images from the Fuzhou/Changle side with Yongning's sedan-chair deity form, where the god may be hidden from the camera. Her strongest contribution is sensory: late-night [[JitongSpiritMedium|鸡童/乩童]] possession scenes, weapon self-striking, blood talismans, gold-leaf temple decoration, rain, smoke, firecrackers, gongs, and crowd density all show why ritual cannot be fully understood from short videos.

The episode also uses Cai's outsider-positioned reactions to make [[GenderedLineageRitualOrder]] legible. She describes feeling unexpected surname-based welcome around a Cai ancestral hall in [[Quanzhou|泉州]], while also noting that women are more visible in worship, offerings, music, dance, and preparation than in the formal core of sedan-chair carrying, spirit mediumship, or main ancestor worship.
