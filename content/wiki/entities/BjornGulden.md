# Bjørn Gulden / 古尔登

Bjørn Gulden / 古尔登 is the executive named in [[vol-271-a-di-da-si-ruhe-chudi-fantan-1007333155]] as the [[Adidas]] CEO brought in from [[Puma]] in 2023. The episode frames his importance through operating repair: he did not solve Adidas' problems by one product launch alone, but by handling [[Yeezy]] inventory, reducing pressure from an aggressive direct-channel push, rebuilding dealer relationships, and restarting retro products such as [[AdidasSamba|Samba]].

## Key Claims
- Gulden is presented as a repair operator rather than a pure brand storyteller.
- His first-stage choices in the source combine inventory disposition, channel repair, and product-portfolio reactivation.
- The episode treats his decision to sell part of the Yeezy inventory in batches as a way to reduce the damage from an abrupt collaboration shutdown.
- His regional-autonomy push is linked to [[GlobalProductLocalization]] and Adidas' China recovery.
