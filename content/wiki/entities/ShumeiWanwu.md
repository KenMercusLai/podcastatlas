# 树美万物 / Shumei Wanwu

## Overview

树美万物 / Shumei Wanwu is the AI 3D company founded by [[RenLifeng|任立峰 / 卷卷]]. The source describes it as building a self-developed AI-generated 3D general model and connecting that capability to physical manufacturing through [[Hi3D]], [[Zaohaowu|造好物]], suppliers, devices, and Maker workflows.

## Current Profile

The company is positioned between model lab, creator platform, and manufacturing pipeline. Its distinctive claim is not only generating 3D assets from images, but making those assets usable in downstream production through post-processing, splitting, connectors, arranging, material parameters, and equipment-compatible formats.

The source says the company serves global users, has raised over $100 million historically, recently closed a roughly $50 million round, and has a team in the high hundreds. Those figures remain source-scoped founder statements.

## Key Characteristics

- Builds AI-generated 3D models with physical manufacturing as the main application direction.
- Operates [[Hi3D]] and [[Zaohaowu]] as consumer-facing or Maker-facing product surfaces.
- Runs or ran a small experimental factory in Guangzhou Panyu to learn equipment, materials, and production constraints directly.
- Treats model quality, post-processing, file formats, devices, suppliers, and delivery as one system.
- Targets users such as Makers, illustrators, artists, pet owners, toy creators, and people with concepts they cannot otherwise manufacture.
- Sees 3D printing as the current strongest AI 3D application while exploring flexible-fabric processes such as embroidery and plush patterning.
- Frames its staged ambition as first a Maker-industry OS and later a broader manufacturing OS.

## Qualifications

The source is a founder interview, so reported financing, team size, model precision, usage, delivery rate, and market-growth claims should be treated as claims in the source rather than independently verified facts. The company may have stronger or weaker positions across games, film, ecommerce, printing, and industrial production than this single conversation can establish.
