# 《苍白的骑士》 / Pale Rider

《苍白的骑士》 / Pale Rider is recommended in [[yi-fen-laizi-bingchuang-shang-de-xiangguan-shudan-598284093]] as a history of the 1918 influenza pandemic and its global consequences. The hosts say its newer preface and afterword add reflections on twenty-first-century pandemics, making it especially useful beside COVID-era experience.

The episode uses the book to make [[PandemicAsHistoricalForce]] concrete. Quarantine, isolation, masks, handwashing, global transport, expert uncertainty, xenophobia, war, independence movements, and social fracture all become part of pandemic history rather than background noise.
