# Derry Citizens Defence Association

The Derry Citizens Defence Association appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] as the Catholic local defense body formed before the August 1969 Apprentice Boys parade. The episode says it operated under IRA veteran Sean Keenan and prepared barricades and petrol bombs in [[Derry]].

During the [[BattleOfTheBogside]], the association appealed to every able-bodied man in Ireland to help. In the source, this defensive mobilization becomes hard to separate from escalation because each community reads the other's preparation as aggression.
