## Overview

Emperor Xuan of Han / 汉宣帝 / 刘询 / 刘病已 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-689-3-wuxing-chu-dongfang-li-zhongguo-yige-gulao-yuyan-de-xiandai-jiedu-lqccxzbvg-jziurfxm5tr5k4jlvq|Hanji 689-3]] as the Western Han ruler whose reign supplies the five-star omen command formula behind [[ZhaoChongguoWesternHan|赵充国]]'s Southern Qiang campaign setting. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-465-cong-qiutu-dao-mingjun-hanxuandi-liubingyi-shi-zenme-si-de-lguhdrq8lfw2wfykuteib-sucwyt|Hanji 465]] adds the broader reign-and-death layer: Xuan is presented as a witchcraft-disaster survivor, prison child, cautious post-Huo-Guang ruler, restorer of Western Han administration, and frontier strategist whose death marks a Western Han turning point.

## Current Profile

The current profile has two source layers. Hanji 689-3 is narrow and artifact-linked: Xuan appears through an omen-worded command context where "five stars rising in the east" signals advantage for China and defeat for non-Han enemies during a crisis threatening the Han-Western-Regions route. That layer makes him a bridge between [[FiveStarsOutOfTheEastBrocade]], astrological-political language, and Zhao Chongguo's frontier campaign.

Hanji 465 broadens the profile from a campaign setting into a reign evaluation. It says Xuan's early life matters to his later rule: rescue by Bing Ji, prison childhood, release under Han Wudi, and time among common people are used to explain why he understands ordinary suffering. After Huo Guang raises him to the throne, Xuan waits cautiously, consolidates after Huo Guang's death, repairs internal administration, disciplines officials, and stabilizes livelihood. The same source credits him with using Xiongnu internal division, supporting the submitted Huhanye side, weakening Zhizhi's position, reducing the Xiongnu threat, incorporating the Western Regions, and establishing the [[WesternRegionsProtectorate|西域都护府]]. His death is therefore framed as a dynastic transition into [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]]'s accession and visible Western Han decline pressure.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han emperor attached to the five-star omen formula that links celestial language, the Niya brocade, and Zhao Chongguo's frontier campaign.
- Former witchcraft-disaster survivor whose prison and commoner experience is used by Hanji 465 to explain later attention to livelihood.
- Cautious post-Huo-Guang ruler who consolidates authority before repairing internal governance.
- Reward-punishment and personnel-assessment ruler praised through Ban Gu's evaluation in Hanji 465.
- Frontier strategist credited with exploiting Xiongnu division and formalizing Western Regions control.
- Deathbed ruler who entrusts affairs to Shi Gao, Xiao Wangzhi, and Zhou Kan before Han Yuandi's accession.
- Turning-point figure whose death leads into the Yuan-era decline and court-politics branch.

## Qualifications

This page is not a full biography of Emperor Xuan. Hanji 689-3 remains narrow to the brocade, omen formula, and Zhao Chongguo campaign interpretation; the brocade's direct relationship to Xuan's court remains interpretive because the source does not prove production date, sender, or recipient. Hanji 465 is a compact reign evaluation and does not reconstruct Xuan's full biography, the Huo-family purge, legal reforms, exact Western Regions administration, or the full original evaluations by Ban Gu and Liu Xiang. Its death-cause claim is deliberately narrow: the episode reports grave illness, entrustment, and death, but not a specific disease or immediate medical cause.
