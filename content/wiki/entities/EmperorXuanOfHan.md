# Emperor Xuan of Han / 汉宣帝

## Overview
Emperor Xuan of Han is the Western Han ruler whose reign supplies the five-star omen command formula in Hanji 689-3. The source uses him to connect celestial language, Zhao Chongguo's frontier campaign, and the possible political meaning of the [[FiveStarsOutOfTheEastBrocade]].

## Current Profile
The episode profiles Emperor Xuan only through the Southern Qiang campaign setting. He appears as the ruler who gives [[ZhaoChongguoWesternHan]] an omen-worded command context, where "five stars rising in the east" signals advantage for China and defeat for non-Han enemies during a crisis threatening the Han-Western-Regions route.

## Key Characteristics
- Emperor Xuan is the ruler attached to the episode's quoted five-star omen formula.
- His court faces a frontier crisis involving Qiang groups, Xiongnu influence, Shanshan, Dunhuang, and Western Regions communications.
- He authorizes or frames Zhao Chongguo's campaign through auspicious celestial language.
- His reign provides the strongest textual bridge between the brocade phrase and Western Han military action.
- The source makes him a node between omen politics and practical frontier defense.

## Qualifications
This page is a narrow source-scoped profile, not a general biography of Emperor Xuan. The brocade's direct relationship to his court remains interpretive because the source does not prove production date, sender, or recipient.
