# 胖东来 / Pang Dong Lai

## Overview
胖东来 / Pang Dong Lai is a Chinese retailer represented as a benchmark for product quality, service, customer orientation, and unusually open operational assistance to other supermarkets.

## Current Profile
In the source, Pang Dong Lai matters less as a complete company history than as proof-of-concept for a different supermarket logic. Ye Guofu interprets its stores as selling chosen products and service rather than monetizing supplier access, and credits its assistance on a Yonghui prototype with making a broader turnaround appear feasible.

The episode also emphasizes institutional generosity: Pang Dong Lai is said to help multiple retailers without charging and to send substantial teams into Yonghui's remodels. That account makes knowledge transfer and customer-centered norms part of the benchmark, but the evidence remains an admiring outside description rather than a neutral operating study.

## Key Characteristics
- Quality-retail benchmark centered on products, service, and customer need.
- Contrast case to fee-driven traditional hypermarket economics.
- Hands-on reform partner providing teams and operating knowledge to Yonghui.
- Example of knowledge sharing presented as altruistic rather than licensing-led.
- Source-scoped institution whose economics and transferability are not established by the interview.

## Evidence
- Product and service benchmark: [[ltdplf6zr1agmhhbopqptutvr-ng-ltdplf6zr1agmhhbopqptutvr-ng]] groups Pang Dong Lai with quality retailers whose success is attributed to product, service, and users rather than low price alone.
- Yonghui prototype: [[ltdplf6zr1agmhhbopqptutvr-ng-ltdplf6zr1agmhhbopqptutvr-ng]] says its team supported a first remodel whose reported performance triggered Ye's investment thesis.
- Knowledge transfer: [[ltdplf6zr1agmhhbopqptutvr-ng-ltdplf6zr1agmhhbopqptutvr-ng]] describes unpaid assistance to several supermarkets and continuing personnel support at Yonghui.

## Qualifications
This page is bounded to Ye Guofu's highly favorable account. It does not independently establish store performance, labor conditions, economics, the exact scope of unpaid support, or whether Pang Dong Lai's culture can transfer across a much larger network.

## What Changed
- Created Pang Dong Lai as the product, service, and knowledge-transfer benchmark in the Yonghui reform account.
