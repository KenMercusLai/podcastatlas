# 子夏 / Zi Xia

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-908-nijing-chu-qiangzhe-xialu-chu-yongzhe-gudu-chu-zhizhe-lqih1dradhzi0mipxsaiedd52dlj|Hanji 908]] adds Zi Xia through a [[Analects|《论语》]] citation on "small ways." The host uses the line to distinguish skills that may still have something worth seeing from the danger of treating them as final goals, matching [[CaiYong|蔡邕]]'s warning against making literary and artistic accomplishment the center of appointment judgment.

子夏 / Zi Xia appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-05-guangcai-duomu-de-weiwenhou-1-lmkxzbmwhw3nhurape-eebydqcdl]] as one of the Confucian scholars whom [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] honors as a teacher. The source identifies him as Bu Shang, courtesy name Zixia, a major disciple of [[Confucius|孔子]] whose learning is associated with classics, ritual, and music rather than "literature" in the modern narrow sense.

The episode places Zi Xia's later teaching activity in the West River region inside [[WeiState|魏国]]'s orbit. His role is less biographical than political: Wei Wenhou's willingness to learn from him turns scholar prestige into part of [[TalentAttractionThroughRespect|礼贤下士式人才吸附]] and [[ConfucianIdealGovernance|儒家理想治理]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-1-lk-6ejgmjhvukqgtlz2lowu8qx-v]] reuses Zi Xia in the chancellor-selection comparison. [[LiKe|李克]] says [[WeiCheng|魏成]]'s ability to attract Zi Xia, [[TianZifang|田子方]], and [[DuanGanmu|段干木]] helps explain why Wei Cheng outranks [[DiHuang|翟黄]], making Zi Xia part of [[TalentReferralQuality]] as well as rulerly respect.
