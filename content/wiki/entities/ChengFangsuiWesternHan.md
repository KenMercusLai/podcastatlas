## Overview

程方遂 / Cheng Fangsui enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-396-2-hanwudi-zhizi-liuju-wugu-zhi-an-hou-fuhuo-le-lhj6dzjsikkmbbcjlfgnz1bbok-1|Hanji 396-2]] as the diviner who impersonates [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]] at [[WeiyangPalace|未央宫]] and is executed after confession.

## Current Profile

The source presents Cheng Fangsui as a small actor whose fraud becomes large because of the identity he borrows. He is not dangerous because he has troops or a faction; he is dangerous because resemblance, the unresolved emotional charge of the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]], palace location, and crowd attention can briefly turn a private scheme into a public legitimacy crisis under [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]].

## Key Characteristics

- Xiagui man living in Huxian, described by the episode as originally working as a diviner.
- Impostor who claims to be Liu Ju at Weiyang Palace's north gate.
- Opportunist whose scheme begins after a former Liu Ju attendant says he resembles the dead crown prince.
- Fraud figure whose desire for wealth and rank turns resemblance into a capital crime.
- Executed by waist-cutting after confession.
