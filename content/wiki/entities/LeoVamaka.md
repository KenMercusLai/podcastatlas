## Overview
Leo Vamaka is a 17-year-old high school senior used by [[PlanetMoney]] as the episode's case for inflation-shaped youth consumer habits.

## Current Profile
The source presents Leo as already highly price-conscious before full adult financial responsibility. He has worked since age 13, holds two jobs, uses coupons with friends, notices gasoline prices, pays for food outside the home, and budgets camping-trip meals for his scout troop.

## Key Characteristics
- He represents a young consumer whose baseline expectations are being formed during a high-price period.
- His thrift is practical rather than total austerity: he hunts deals but still buys some discretionary music.
- His scout-troop budgeting shows inflation as a repeated group-planning problem, not only a private household issue.
- His story anchors [[InflationShapedConsumerHabits]] and connects to [[GenerationalEconomicTiming]].

## Qualifications
Leo lives with his parents and does not yet face rent or household bills, so his case is not adult financial hardship. Its importance is formative: prices, coupons, and budgeting become part of early money habits.
