## Overview

周举 / Zhou Ju is a Shun-era late-Han official whose current profile combines recommendation accountability and remonstrance. Hanji 819 shows him impeaching [[ZuoXiongLateHan|左雄]] after Zuo recommends a previously corrupt official, while Hanji 821 places him in [[LiangShangLateHan|梁商]]'s staff environment, reading an ominous banquet song before Liang Shang recommends him to [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-819-2-ren-gong-bi-4-wanren-tade-quanmou-lingren-paianjuejue-lsslj3y-krnd8h-c8x5pgsqeinff|Hanji 819]] supplies Zhou Ju's recommendation-accountability layer. Zuo Xiong had earlier recommended Zhou Ju for office, but Zhou Ju later impeaches Zuo when Zuo recommends Feng Zhi despite Feng's corruption record. Zhou Ju's reply distinguishes a bravery-focused edict from permission to recommend a corrupt man.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-821-budong-laoban-xinsi-haixiang-jinsheng-meimen-lmjxr8fdxgw-colrlinmujba74fh|Hanji 821]] later places Zhou Ju near Liang Shang, where he serves in Liang's staff environment. During a wine gathering, he hears a song associated with Xie Lu funeral sound and reads the scene as an omen pointing toward death. Before dying, Liang Shang recommends him, and Shun appoints Zhou Ju as jianyi dafu, an office for counsel and remonstrance.

The combined profile makes Zhou Ju a feedback actor in two directions. He corrects a recommender to protect public appointment standards, then enters a remonstrance office through Liang Shang's final recommendation.

## Key Characteristics

- Public-duty official willing to impeach the person who had previously recommended him.
- Recommendation-quality critic who rejects the idea that "brave" means corruption can be ignored.
- Omen-reading actor who interprets a banquet song as a death warning.
- Recommended by Liang Shang before death and appointed by Emperor Shun.
- Remonstrance-office figure whose value is tied to court feedback rather than field command.
