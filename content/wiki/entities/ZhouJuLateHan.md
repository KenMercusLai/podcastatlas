## Overview

周举 / Zhou Ju is a Shun-era late-Han official introduced through Liang Shang's final recommendation and an omen-reading scene. Hanji 821 presents him as a court-adjacent staff figure who hears a mourning-like song during Liang Shang's banquet, interprets it as politically ominous, and is later appointed remonstrance adviser by [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-821-budong-laoban-xinsi-haixiang-jinsheng-meimen-lmjxr8fdxgw-colrlinmujba74fh|Hanji 821]] first places Zhou Ju near Liang Shang, where he serves in Liang's staff environment. During a wine gathering, he hears a song associated with Xie Lu funeral sound and reads the scene as an omen pointing toward death.

The source then makes Zhou Ju part of Liang Shang's final personnel legacy. Before dying, Liang Shang recommends him, and Shun appoints Zhou Ju as jianyi dafu, an office for counsel and remonstrance. His current profile therefore sits at the intersection of omen interpretation, elite recommendation, and court feedback.

## Key Characteristics

- Shun-era official whose current profile begins in Liang Shang's staff environment.
- Omen-reading actor who interprets a banquet song as a death warning.
- Recommended by Liang Shang before death and appointed by Emperor Shun.
- Remonstrance-office figure whose value is tied to court feedback rather than field command.
