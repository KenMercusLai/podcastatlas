# 苏不韦 / Su Buwei (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-903-weishenme-quan-ni-yuanyuan-xiangbao-heshi-le-lokpt93muc8kasvn44vgsjki8bom|Hanji 903]] introduces [[SuBuweiLateHan|苏不韦]] as a late-Han avenger whose feud with [[LiHaoLateHan|李昊]] spirals beyond direct retaliation. After his attempt to kill Li fails, Su digs up Li's father's grave, cuts off the corpse's head, and hangs it in the market, turning private vengeance into [[TombDesecrationPoliticalViolence|tomb desecration]] and [[PublicCorpseHumiliation|public corpse humiliation]].

The same episode treats Su's return home after an amnesty as the point where exposure replaces pardon. [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]], tied to Li Hao's side of the conflict, offers Su a private post that functions as a loyalty test; when Su refuses, Duan coerces a subordinate by threatening the subordinate's father and sends him to kill Su's entire household. Su's case is therefore less a closed vengeance anecdote than a compact example of [[AtrocityRetaliationCycle|retaliatory escalation]] and [[PrivatePunishmentWithoutProcedure|private punishment without procedure]].
