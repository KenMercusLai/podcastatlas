# 苏不韦 / Su Buwei (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-902-kongzhi-qingxu-shi-rensheng-bixiuke-ljynylvdlsdtjf4es9jmilav863b|Hanji 902]] introduces [[SuBuweiLateHan|苏不韦]] as the son of [[SuQianLateHan|苏谦]], whose killing by [[LiHaoLateHan|李昊]] becomes the source of a long revenge plan. The episode says Su Buwei delays full burial for his father, changes name, hides his identity, builds a friendship network, and digs a tunnel from Li Hao's fodder store toward Li's bedroom. His first attack misses Li but kills Li's concubine and young son, leaving Li terrified.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-903-weishenme-quan-ni-yuanyuan-xiangbao-heshi-le-lokpt93muc8kasvn44vgsjki8bom|Hanji 903]] continues the revenge branch beyond direct assassination. Su digs up Li Hao's father's grave, cuts off the corpse's head, and hangs it in the market, turning private vengeance into [[TombDesecrationPoliticalViolence|tomb desecration]] and [[PublicCorpseHumiliation|public corpse humiliation]].

The same Hanji 903 episode treats Su's return home after an amnesty as the point where exposure replaces pardon. [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]], tied to Li Hao's side of the conflict, offers Su a private post that functions as a loyalty test; when Su refuses, Duan coerces a subordinate by threatening the subordinate's father and sends him to kill Su's entire household. Su's case is therefore less a closed vengeance anecdote than a compact example of [[AtrocityRetaliationCycle|retaliatory escalation]] and [[PrivatePunishmentWithoutProcedure|private punishment without procedure]].

## Connections

- [[SuQianLateHan]] - father whose killing creates Su's filial revenge motive.
- [[LiHaoLateHan]] - Su's target in the failed bedroom attack and later grave-desecration spectacle.
- [[DuanJiongLateHan]] - transforms the feud into a mass household killing.
- [[TombDesecrationPoliticalViolence]], [[PublicCorpseHumiliation]], [[AtrocityRetaliationCycle]], and [[PrivatePunishmentWithoutProcedure]] - the main conceptual lenses for the episode's revenge sequence.
