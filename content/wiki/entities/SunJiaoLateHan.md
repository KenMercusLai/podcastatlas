## Overview

孙皎 / Sun Jiao is an Eastern Wu commander whose current bounded role is the senior supporting appointment in [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s planned operation against [[GuanYu|关羽]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1169-2026-gai-zenme-shou-zhu-zishen-de-fuqi-lu-3dpvj0nbqhise2aqx-a3zi3kp|Hanji 1169]] says Sun Quan initially considers giving Sun Jiao and [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] equal command. Lyu Meng objects that two commanders of equal standing can reproduce the earlier Zhou Yu-Cheng Pu coordination conflict. Sun Quan therefore gives Lyu Meng final command and assigns Sun Jiao to assist.

The evidence establishes a role in command design, not a full biography or independent operational profile. Sun Jiao matters here because a capable senior participant can retain a meaningful supporting position without sharing ambiguous final authority.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Wu commander considered for joint authority in the operation against Guan Yu.
- Reassigned from prospective equal command to a supporting role under Lyu Meng.
- Illustrates how senior participation can be preserved while final authority is clarified.
