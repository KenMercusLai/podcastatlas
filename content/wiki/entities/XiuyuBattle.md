# 修鱼之战 / Battle of Xiuyu

修鱼之战 / Battle of Xiuyu is the 317 BCE battle foregrounded in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-46-ma-ni-shi-ge-erbaiwu-de-lishi-laiyuan-lkz5hiamaojtvanllbunecy3g3ff]]. The episode places Xiuyu near today's Yuanyang County in Henan, in the central plains between the Zhengzhou and Kaifeng areas, and treats that geography as part of why [[HanState|韩国]] and [[WeiState|魏国]] could no longer simply retreat from [[QinState|秦国]].

The battle is framed as the sequel to the failed 318 BCE anti-Qin coalition at [[HanguPass|函谷关]]. After [[ChuState|楚国]], [[ZhaoState|赵国]], Wei, Han, and [[YanState|燕国]] withdraw, Qin follows east under [[Chulizi|樗里子]] and forces a decisive fight at Xiuyu.

The source reports that Qin cuts down eighty thousand and then pursues to [[ZhuozeBattle|浊泽]], where Han generals rendered as Wei Sou and Shen Chai are captured. For the wiki, the key point is not only casualty scale but alliance consequence: [[CoalitionSelfPreservationFailure|联盟自保失灵]] leaves the coalition's front-line members exposed to Qin's retaliatory reach.

## Key Claims
- Xiuyu is presented as a central-plains battle rather than a Qin border skirmish.
- The fight follows the failed Hangu Pass campaign and shows Qin moving from defense to pursuit.
- The source treats geography as operational: Han and Wei face pressure because the Qin chase reaches a zone close to Wei's capital space.
- The reported result weakens the [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] side and forces a fresh reading of the post-Hangu power map.
