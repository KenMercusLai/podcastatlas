# Obsession / 《痴迷》 (Curry Barker film)

## Overview

Obsession is a horror film by [[CurryBarker]] in which Bear wishes that Nikki would love him and the imposed love becomes destructive obsession.

## Current Profile

The episode reads the film as a modern malicious-wish story whose horror begins before the supernatural consequences: Bear's wish attempts to replace Nikki's will rather than risk asking for her consent. The resulting possession and body horror convert the “nice guy,” romantic persistence, and woman-as-reward conventions into [[ConsentViolationHorror]].

The ending remains morally unstable. Bear's attempted self-sacrifice can recode an aggressor as a tragic hero, while Nikki's restored autonomy leaves her with death and trauma. The hosts disagree on whether a harsher or more open ending would sharpen the critique, so the film's closure remains contested within the source.

## Key Characteristics

- Malicious-wish structure in which fulfilled desire becomes punishment.
- Consent violation is the initiating harm rather than a secondary theme.
- Possession reframed as male control presented in the language of love.
- Reversal of romantic-comedy persistence and woman-as-prize conventions.
- Ending that restores agency without restoring safety or innocence.
