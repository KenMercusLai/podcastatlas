# 雷波 / Lei Bo (Yuan Shu retainer)

## Overview
雷波 / Lei Bo (Yuan Shu retainer) enters the wiki as one of the old Yuan Shu-associated figures who refuses to receive [[YuanShu|袁术]] during his final flight.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] gives Lei Bo a narrow but important role. Yuan Shu expects old subordinates to be fallback capacity after his palace and supply system fail, but Lei Bo and [[ChenJianYuanShuRetainer|陈简]] refuse him. The source uses the refusal as a terminal sign that Yuan Shu's old dependency network has become unusable.

## Key Characteristics
- Yuan Shu-side old subordinate named in the final-collapse branch.
- Appears as a possible refuge rather than a developed independent commander.
- Refuses to accept Yuan Shu once Yuan Shu's supplies and authority have collapsed.
- Helps mark the difference between nominal old attachment and usable protection.

## Evidence
- Refused refuge: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] says Yuan Shu tries to go to Chen Jian and Lei Bo, but they do not receive him.
- Network failure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] places the refusal alongside soldier flight and later retreat toward Shouchun, showing that Yuan Shu lacks reliable subordinates when crisis arrives.
