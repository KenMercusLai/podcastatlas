# Charles Lyell

## Overview

Charles Lyell is represented as the geologist whose *Principles of Geology* helped [[CharlesDarwin|Charles Darwin]] interpret large natural changes through slow processes.

## Current Profile

[[12-days-port-wine-and-darwin-sets-sail-glt6423200478]] introduces Lyell through the book Darwin obtained while preparing for the [[HMSBeagle|Beagle]] voyage. Its account of slow but profound geological change becomes a conceptual resource for Darwin's later thinking about change in natural history. The source establishes influence, not a complete account of Lyell's geology or Darwin's intellectual debts.

## Key Characteristics

- Geologist associated with *Principles of Geology*.
- Advocate, in the episode's summary, of profound change through slow processes.
- Pre-voyage intellectual influence on Darwin.
- Bridge between geological observation and later biological theory formation.

## Evidence

- Reading evidence - [[12-days-port-wine-and-darwin-sets-sail-glt6423200478]] says Darwin acquired *Principles of Geology* before sailing.
- Conceptual influence - [[12-days-port-wine-and-darwin-sets-sail-glt6423200478]] connects Lyell's slow-change geology to Darwin's later thinking about natural history.

## Qualifications

The episode gives only a concise influence claim. It does not reconstruct Lyell's arguments, their predecessors, Darwin's agreements and departures, or the full history of geological gradualism.

## What Changed

- Created a source-bounded profile of Lyell's pre-voyage influence on Darwin.
