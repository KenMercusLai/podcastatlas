# Felix Hoffman

## Overview
Felix Hoffman is presented in the source as the co-founder and CEO of [[SevenLearnings]], a SaaS company that helps retailers automate pricing, marketing, and ordering decisions.

## Current Profile
The episode frames Felix as a domain-led founder whose credibility came from pricing consulting at [[Kearney]] and exposure to predictive retail decision-making at [[Zalando]]. His main contribution to the wiki is an enterprise-SaaS operating case: he used consulting to secure data, founder-led selling to win early enterprise customers, and A/B testing to turn an abstract AI promise into measurable profit evidence.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines pricing-consulting experience with operational exposure to advanced retail decision systems.
- Treats early enterprise sales as founder work that cannot be cleanly delegated before the first customers trust the product.
- Uses customer data access and paid pilots as product-development inputs, not only as revenue.
- Emphasizes measurable ROI and explainability when selling automated business decisions.
- Draws a sharp technology-fit boundary between deterministic predictive systems and LLM-first products.

## Evidence
Founder-market fit:
- [[founder-led-sales-to-1m-arr-with-just-10-customers-aharo8420023047]] says Felix spent six years at [[Kearney]] and two years at [[Zalando]], where he saw predictive decision-making in practice.

Enterprise sales and validation:
- [[founder-led-sales-to-1m-arr-with-just-10-customers-aharo8420023047]] says Felix sold the first customers through founder-led sales, events, referrals, and customer champions, with Seven Learnings reaching about $1M ARR from its first 10 customers.

Technology judgment:
- [[founder-led-sales-to-1m-arr-with-just-10-customers-aharo8420023047]] records Felix's view that pricing and marketing optimization need deterministic, cheap, accurate, explainable systems and should not default to LLMs.
