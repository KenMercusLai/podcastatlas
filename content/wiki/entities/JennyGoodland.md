# Jenny Goodland

## Overview
Jenny Goodland is a recovery organizer associated with the [[RebuildParadiseFoundation]], where she connected wildfire mitigation and insurance availability to the wider reconstruction of [[ParadiseCalifornia|Paradise]].

## Current Profile
[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-043ffe0bc2c7516bac64132229755e93-43026861-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-043ffe0bc2c7516bac64132229755e93-43026861]] presents Goodland as an institutional translator between residents and insurers. She organized mitigation, challenged weak insurance recognition, brought executives into the town, and later took a [[MercuryInsurance]] role helping other communities pursue risk reduction and private coverage.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames insurance availability as a condition for home sales, population recovery, business recruitment, and the local tax base.
- Organized Firewise communities with [[HeidiLang]].
- Pressed insurers to compare remote classifications with actual hardened construction.
- Invited Mercury executives to inspect Paradise directly.
- Extended the Paradise approach to other California communities through a later insurer role.
