# Qishilin / 起士林

## Overview

Qishilin / 起士林 is a Tianjin legacy food brand associated in the bounded source with chocolate, bread, Russian- and German-style Western food, and several generations of local taste memory.

## Current Profile

[[fengwei-chihe-tianjin-weishenme-shi-qiaokeli-tongcheng-gkwriueoo7cnayah8atndka]] uses Qishilin as one bridge between imported food and everyday [[Tianjin]] life. Guests remember large chocolate bars as holiday treats or rewards, while the brand's bread, borscht, steak, salad, and pickles connect restaurant-style Western food with meals that could also be taken home.

The episode reports that a current classic chocolate product declares compound fat. That creates a strategic tension rather than a simple quality verdict: changing the formulation could alter the remembered flavor, while leaving it unchanged may fit poorly with newer health, ingredient, and premium expectations. A two-track portfolio—preserving a classic line while developing new products—is proposed by the speakers, not reported as company strategy.

## Key Characteristics

- A legacy Tianjin brand spanning chocolate, bread, and Western-style foods.
- Strong association with childhood rewards, holiday treats, and intergenerational memory.
- An example of foreign-origin food becoming locally ordinary and portable into household meals.
- A classic chocolate formulation reported to use compound fat.
- A potential tension between flavor continuity and contemporary ingredient expectations.

## Evidence

- Product memory: [[fengwei-chihe-tianjin-weishenme-shi-qiaokeli-tongcheng-gkwriueoo7cnayah8atndka]] reports memories of large Qishilin chocolate bars shared slowly and their foil wrappers reused for play.
- Broader food role: [[fengwei-chihe-tianjin-weishenme-shi-qiaokeli-tongcheng-gkwriueoo7cnayah8atndka]] associates the brand with bread, borscht, steak, salad, pickles, and take-home Western-style meals.
- Formulation: [[fengwei-chihe-tianjin-weishenme-shi-qiaokeli-tongcheng-gkwriueoo7cnayah8atndka]] says a current product package identifies compound fat, connecting the brand to [[ChocolateCompound]].
- Strategic tension: [[fengwei-chihe-tianjin-weishenme-shi-qiaokeli-tongcheng-gkwriueoo7cnayah8atndka]] records the speakers' argument that reformulation could change a classic taste even as lower-sugar and craft expectations rise.

## Qualifications

The source does not independently establish the brand's founding date, corporate history, manufacturing ownership, current product portfolio, sales, or whether it introduced chocolate to Tianjin around 1907. Product formulation is episode-reported and may vary by item or over time. The proposed classic-plus-new portfolio is commentary, not a verified company plan.
