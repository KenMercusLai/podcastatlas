# HMS Endeavour

## Overview

HMS Endeavour was the vessel commanded by [[JamesCook]] on the voyage that observed the transit of Venus at Tahiti, charted New Zealand, reached eastern Australia, and returned to Britain in 1771.

## Current Profile

The ship functions as mobile observatory, collecting platform, armed authority, trading community, and vector of disease risk. Its crew carries scientific instruments and sealed imperial instructions, but also nails, muskets, sexual demand, punishment, and internal theft. The episode's first-contact frame depends on this dual character: the Endeavour brings observation and exchange in a form backed by superior firepower and a distant Crown.

Its near-loss on the Great Barrier Reef also keeps contingency visible. British claims and later historical consequences depended on a vessel that could easily have been wrecked before completing the voyage.

## Key Characteristics

- Mobile platform for astronomy, natural history, cartography, language study, and collecting.
- Hierarchical shipboard society governed through Cook's strict discipline.
- Armed contact environment whose muskets and Marines shape negotiation.
- Trading system in which iron and nails become high-value exchange goods and sources of theft.
- Vulnerable wooden vessel nearly destroyed on the Great Barrier Reef and later hit by severe disease losses.
