# Mathieu Favasse

Mathieu Favasse appears in [[pump-and-circumstance-is-china-the-new-opec-6a7c37287cf3c58886773a66]] as the oil-market analyst explaining why [[China]]'s response to the [[StraitOfHormuz]] shock mattered. The source uses his view to frame China as a consumer-side stabilizer: by cutting imports, drawing down stocks, reducing exports of refined products, and adapting domestic fuel use, China could reduce upward pressure on oil prices without acting like a producer cartel.

His role in the wiki is source-scoped. The episode links him to [[DemandSideOilMarketPower]] rather than to a broad biography or institutional profile.
