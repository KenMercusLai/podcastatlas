# CyberCab

CyberCab appears in [[lanjian-hangtian-wancheng-zhongguo-shouci-ludi-huojian-huishou-yushu-keji-shizhi-chaoguo-3000-yi-1007302506]] as [[Tesla]]'s dedicated robotaxi vehicle. The source says The Information reported Tesla could launch the steering-wheel-free, pedal-free model in Austin, first for employee rides on public roads and then for commercial operation.

The episode treats CyberCab as a cost-control and operational-standardization move. A dedicated model could reduce the retrofit work needed on [[Tesla]] Model Y vehicles, but broad deployment still depends on production, regulation, fleet operations, and public-road safety acceptance.
