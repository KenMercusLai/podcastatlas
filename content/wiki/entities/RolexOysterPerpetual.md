# Rolex Oyster Perpetual

Rolex Oyster Perpetual is the product line in [[rolex-1]] that combines the [[RolexOyster]] waterproof case logic with automatic winding. The source says a self-winding mechanism mattered because manual winding required unscrewing the crown, weakening the sealed-watch proposition.

In the episode's product logic, "Perpetual" completes the early Rolex promise: accurate, sealed, and convenient. That makes the line central to [[VerticalIntegrationForQualityControl]] and later [[MechanicalWatchRepositioning]].
