## Overview

城阳郡 / Chengyang Commandery is a territorial unit of the early-Han [[QiKingdomEarlyHan|齐国]] in this wiki's current evidence. Its main significance here is not a complete administrative history, but its use as the material concession in [[LiuFeiQiKing|刘肥]]'s reported settlement with [[LuZhi|吕雉]].

## Current Profile

After a Chang'an banquet allegedly exposes Liu Fei to a poison threat, a Qi official advises him to surrender one commandery to [[LuYuanPrincess|鲁元公主]] as a [[TangmuYi|汤沐邑]]. Liu Fei chooses Chengyang and combines that transfer with honoring his younger half-sister as queen mother. The commandery thus gives material credibility to a [[StatusReversalSurvivalBargain|名分倒置式求生]]: the concession is not only verbal submission but a reduction of the grantor's territorial resources in favor of Lu Zhi's daughter. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-195-lvhou-zhangquan-liubang-zhangzi-jimang-renmei-wei-niang-lokajlf0bhft1b7fo-hwazyqretn|Hanji 195]]

## Key Characteristics

- Commandery presented as part of Liu Fei's early-Han Qi kingdom.
- Territorial resource large enough to make political submission materially costly.
- Assigned to Princess Lu Yuan as a bathing estate in the episode's account.
- Paired with a queen-mother title rather than transferred as an isolated fiscal measure.

## Qualifications

The supplied structured summary does not establish Chengyang's exact borders, population, revenue, transfer procedure, later administration, or whether “giving the commandery” meant full jurisdiction or a bounded revenue assignment. The political purpose and causal effect of the concession remain the episode's interpretation pending comparison with transmitted histories.
