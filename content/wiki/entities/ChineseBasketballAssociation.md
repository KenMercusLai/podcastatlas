# 中国男子篮球职业联赛 / Chinese Basketball Association

The Chinese Basketball Association appears in [[e237-yangshi-he-fifa-tanpan-fenzheng-beihou-tiyu-saishi-zhuanboquan-de-boyi-yu-shengyi-c3607c08-6eb6-48bd-8250-b41b5b8272e6]] as an example of Chinese-style sports-rights monetization on short-video platforms. [[ZhangBinSportsMedia|Zhang Bin]] describes a model where [[Douyin]] can pay a base rights fee and then share revenue once commerce sales pass a threshold.

The source uses the case to show why sports rights are no longer only about full-match broadcast ads or memberships. In a short-video setting, a stream can become commentary, shopping, clips, fan conversation, and traffic conversion at the same time, which changes how rights owners and platforms split risk.
