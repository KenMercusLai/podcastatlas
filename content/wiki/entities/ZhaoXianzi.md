# 赵献子 / Zhao Xianzi

赵献子 / Zhao Xianzi, personal name 赵浣, appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-04-yurang-wei-zhiyao-fuchou-ln34tydha6iqwlrgmw7iugqfgfik]] as [[DaiChengjun|代成君]]'s son and [[ZhaoBolu|赵伯鲁]]'s grandson. [[ZhaoXiangzi|赵襄子]] designates him as successor instead of naming one of his own sons.

The source says Zhao Xianzi succeeds Zhao Xiangzi in 425 BCE, is soon expelled by [[ZhaoHuanzi|赵桓子]], and is restored in 424 BCE after Zhao Huanzi dies. After Zhao Xianzi's own death, his son Zhao Ji succeeds; the episode identifies Zhao Ji as [[ZhaoLiehou|赵烈侯]], one of the three figures later formally recognized by [[ZhouWeilieWang|周威烈王]].
