# Rolex GMT-Master

Rolex GMT-Master is the pilot watch discussed in [[rolex-1]] as a product developed with [[PanAm]] for the jet age. The source says pilots needed to track two time zones, and Rolex used visual bezel design to make day/night and time-zone functions legible.

The GMT-Master is a clean example of [[ProfessionalToolBranding]] because a specific operating need became a long-running identity product. Its continued cultural demand also supports [[MechanicalWatchRepositioning]]: the original tool job becomes part of the symbolic value.
