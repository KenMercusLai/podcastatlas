# 袁胤 / Yuan Yin (late Han)

## Overview
袁胤 / Yuan Yin is the Yuan Shu kinsman whose flight with remnants, household dependents, and seals turns [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s collapse into a logistical burden for [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]].

## Current Profile
The current sources use Yuan Yin less as an independent actor than as a pressure carrier after Yuan Shu's defeat and death. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] supplies the immediate route: fearing [[CaoCao|曹操]], Yuan Yin leaves [[Shouchun|寿春]] with Yuan Shu's seals, family, and troops and goes to Liu Xun at Lujiang. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1037-zhongyu-caocao-or-liubiao-jiemi-hansong-de-zhongyi-jueze-lomrbxffcu6euantozh2fjvrykva|Hanji 1037]] interprets the same arrival as food pressure: Yuan Yin's remnant group adds mouths and soldiers to Liu Xun's already strained base before the Shangliao lure.

## Key Characteristics
- Belongs to the Yuan Shu family network after Yuan Shu's defeat and death.
- Moves with seals, troops, family, and household dependents rather than only as an individual refugee.
- Seeks protection from Liu Xun in Lujiang because Cao Cao's pressure makes Shouchun unsafe.
- Intensifies Liu Xun's grain-consumption problem before the Shangliao branch.
- Serves as a link between Yuan Shu's failed title project and Liu Xun's later resource vulnerability.

## Evidence
- Immediate flight route: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] says Yuan Yin fears Cao Cao, leaves Shouchun, and takes Yuan Shu's seals, family, and troops to Liu Xun.
- Food-pressure effect: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1037-zhongyu-caocao-or-liubiao-jiemi-hansong-de-zhongyi-jueze-lomrbxffcu6euantozh2fjvrykva|Hanji 1037]] links Yuan Yin's arrival with Yuan Shu remnants and households to Liu Xun's rapid grain depletion and later search for supplies.

## Qualifications
This page is bounded to Yuan Yin's role as a post-Yuan-Shu remnant carrier in the Liu Xun logistics branch. It does not reconstruct his full genealogy or later life.

## What Changed
- Hanji 1033 adds the immediate post-Yuan-Shu flight route, including seals, family, troops, and Cao Cao pressure.
- Hanji 1037 remains the source for interpreting Yuan Yin's arrival as a Lujiang food burden.

## Relationships
- [[YuanShu|袁术]] - kinsman and collapsed patron whose remnants Yuan Yin carries.
- [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]] - protector whose food pressure increases after Yuan Yin arrives.
- [[Shouchun|寿春]] - starting point Yuan Yin leaves because Cao Cao's pressure makes it unsafe.
- [[LujiangCommandery|庐江郡]] - refuge and supply setting.
- [[HeirloomImperialSeal|传国玉玺]] - adjacent symbolic object branch in Yuan Shu's collapse, returned through Xu Qiu rather than carried forward as Yuan Yin's new claim.
- [[ArmedFollowerIntegrationCost|武装部众整合成本]] - broader cost pattern opened by the arrival of armed and dependent followers.
