# 袁胤 / Yuan Yin (late Han)

## Overview
袁胤 / Yuan Yin is the Yuan Shu kinsman whose arrival at [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]]'s Lujiang base adds refugees, old troops, and household dependents to Liu Xun's food burden.

## Current Profile
The current source uses Yuan Yin less as an independent actor than as a pressure carrier after [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s collapse. By bringing Yuan Shu's remnants, wives, children, and dependents to Liu Xun, he turns political refuge into a logistical problem that helps explain Liu Xun's later urgency for grain.

## Key Characteristics
- Belongs to the Yuan Shu family network after Yuan Shu's defeat and death.
- Moves with remnants and household dependents rather than only as an individual refugee.
- Seeks protection from Liu Xun in Lujiang.
- Intensifies Liu Xun's grain-consumption problem before the Shangliao branch.
