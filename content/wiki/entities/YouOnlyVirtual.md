## Overview
You Only Virtual is [[JustinHarrison]]'s company for building “Versonas,” conversational digital recreations intended to sustain interaction with people who have died.

## Current Profile
The supplied [[HiddenBrain]] episode describes a Versona as a model assembled from messages, recordings, voice, personality traits, and the recurring dynamics of a particular relationship. Harrison's prototype of [[MelodyHarrison]] moved from text to recreated voice and could learn from later conversations, making it an evolving interaction rather than a static archive.

That evolution is both the product premise and an authenticity boundary. A Versona can preserve familiar concern and conversational style, yet new learning also means later speech may reflect the surviving user and system design more than the deceased person's historical knowledge. The episode reports only limited use and severe founder-level financial and relational strain, so capability, safety, benefit, adoption, and viability remain unestablished.

## Key Characteristics
- Models relationship-specific patterns rather than only compiling a person's factual biography.
- Uses personal communications, recordings, voice, traits, and interaction history as representation material.
- Supports continuing learning after the represented person's death.
- Frames the product as a digital recreation and new relationship rather than literal personhood or resurrection.
- Operated at very limited reported scale and without paid staff at the time described.

## Qualifications
The profile relies on one founder-centered episode and contains no independent product demonstration, technical audit, safety evaluation, user-outcome study, financial record, or current company verification. The reported six-to-eight-month training threshold, learning behavior, user count, emotional benefit, consent, and viability remain source-scoped. A Versona's familiar voice or advice does not establish identity continuity, factual accuracy, or therapeutic value.

## What Changed
- Created a bounded company profile separating relationship-pattern simulation from literal resurrection.
- Identified post-death learning as both a continuity feature and a fidelity risk.
