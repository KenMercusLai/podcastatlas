## Overview

《骆驼日记》 is [[QilinPhotographer|麒麟]]'s book about walking roughly 700 kilometers across [[Kenya]] with a guide and one camel.

## Current Profile

The source presents the book as more than a travel feat. Its journey is built from water, food, direction, heat, camp selection, animal health, armed conflict, photography, and changing relationships among Qilin, [[MartinKenyaGuide|马丁]], and [[QipeishiCamel|齐佩石]]. The route ends when the conflict environment changes the acceptable downside, making incompletion part of the book's risk judgment rather than evidence that the journey lacked meaning.

## Key Characteristics

- Long-distance walking journey rather than vehicle-supported sightseeing.
- Shared human-animal labor with one camel carrying equipment.
- Dependence on local environmental and social knowledge.
- Photographic record shaped by bodily strain and uncertainty.
- Narrative arc in which attachment and withdrawal matter alongside distance.
