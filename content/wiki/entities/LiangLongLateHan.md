# 梁龙 / Liang Long (late Han)

梁龙 / Liang Long enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-920-mingwu-zhege-daoli-caineng-zhuan-daqian-lqmabg3bpyz6ct2v4arx-iun57ws|Hanji 920]] as a [[JiaozhiCommandery|交趾郡]] rebel figure in 181 CE. The source says that after [[WuhuManJiaozhi|乌浒蛮]] rebellion had persisted for a long time, Liang Long and others rose as well, broke commanderies and counties, and made the southern situation severe.

Liang Long's page is source-scoped. In this ingest he matters less as a full biography than as the named leader whose killing makes [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]]'s quick Jiaozhi pacification visible before Zhu Jun's later [[YellowTurbansLateHan|Yellow Turban]] career.
