# ChatGPT 6 / Astra

## Overview
[[ChatGPT6Astra]] is the sources' label for a limited-release frontier AI model or ChatGPT experience associated with [[OpenAI]]. Five bounded sources describe rollout, professional computer use, robotics use, scientific and mathematical capability, and hands-on workflow fit, but the name, release mechanics, benchmark standing, and commercial tiers remain source-scoped until corroborated by primary documentation.

## Current Profile
The first source presents Astra as a limited rollout that triggered near-AGI, market, governance, and education-policy debate. The robotics sources report grasping, spatial reasoning, planning, error correction, end-effector target generation, and separate source-reported evaluation figures: roughly 22% average success in one simulation evaluation, and roughly 26% direct control versus 48% when supervising a specialized action model in another experiment. These figures are not directly comparable because the sources do not establish common tasks or protocols. They preserve the deployment boundary: slow inference, fine manipulation, long action sequences, dynamic recovery, and real-time control still require stronger physical-system evidence and may need a lower action layer.

The two workflow sources broaden Astra beyond robot demonstrations. Vol. 175 treats it as one capable option in quota- and cost-aware model routing. LateTalk episode 183 reports stronger computer use across Blender, Unity, KiCad, Excel, and Power BI plus scientific and mathematical work. Episode 184 adds a research-tool role: Astra can call simulation and content tools to construct environments and revise policies, a source-defined [[PhysicalRecursiveSelfImprovement|Physical RSI]] loop that remains unproven across sim-to-real transfer.
