# ChatGPT 6 / Astra

## Overview
[[ChatGPT6Astra]] is the episode's label for a limited-release frontier AI model or product experience associated with [[OpenAI]] and ChatGPT. The wiki now has two bounded sources, one on the rollout and market reaction and one on robotics use, so its name, rollout status, and capability level should still be treated as source-scoped until corroborated by primary documentation.

## Current Profile
The entity is presented as a limited rollout to selected organizations and paid ChatGPT users that prompts the hosts to discuss whether AGI has effectively arrived. In the episode, [[GregBrockman]] is cited as saying an AGI era has begun, [[SamAltman]] is cited as warning that future models will be sobering, and [[ChamathPalihapitiya]] argues that the threshold is already functionally crossed. The durable significance for the wiki is less the product name itself than the way frontier-model releases become market, governance, and education-policy triggers before their details are broadly settled.

A second source adds a robotics branch. The Shizilukou Crossing interview with [[WangJiawei]] describes Astra directly driving a robot arm with surprising grasp success and spatial, scene, task-planning, and trial-and-error ability, including a multi-attempt pen drawing of the Golden Gate Bridge. The same account cautions that the video is sped up, that a single inference is slow, that static-scene success does not establish dynamic recovery such as catching a tilting cup, and that the upper model may still need an action layer integrated below it.

## Key Characteristics
- Limited availability is central: the product is described as reaching selected organizations and paid ChatGPT users, not as a fully public baseline.
- It is used as evidence in near-AGI discourse, with hosts and cited OpenAI figures treating the capability jump as institutionally important.
- Its rollout is tied to the broader [[FrontierModelDuopoly]] debate because expensive frontier models remain concentrated in a small set of closed labs.
- The entity remains uncertain: the wiki has not yet independently corroborated the naming, release mechanics, or benchmark performance.
- Its robotics branch is double-edged: the same second source that reports surprising manipulation and planning ability also reports sped-up video, slow single-pass inference, and no guarantee of real-time recovery from physical disturbance.
