# ChatGPT 6 / Astra

## Overview
[[ChatGPT6Astra]] is the sources' label for a limited-release frontier AI model or ChatGPT experience associated with [[OpenAI]]. Three bounded sources now describe rollout, robotics use, and hands-on workflow fit, but the name, release mechanics, benchmark standing, and commercial tiers remain source-scoped until corroborated by primary documentation.

## Current Profile
The first source presents Astra as a limited rollout to selected organizations and paid ChatGPT users that triggered near-AGI, market, governance, and education-policy debate. A robotics interview reports surprising grasping, spatial reasoning, planning, and trial-and-error behavior, while emphasizing slow inference, sped-up video, static scenes, and the continuing need for a lower action layer.

Vol. 175 adds a practical user view rather than a benchmark claim. The hosts describe Astra as a capable frontier option used alongside other models when preferred quota is constrained. In their workflow, model choice depends on planning quality, execution role, price, speed, and remaining allowance; Astra's importance is therefore as one component in [[ModelRoutingCostControl]], not proof that one frontier model should handle every task.
