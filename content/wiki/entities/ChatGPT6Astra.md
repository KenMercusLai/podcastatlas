# ChatGPT 6 / Astra

## Overview
[[ChatGPT6Astra]] is the sources' label for a limited-release frontier AI model or ChatGPT experience associated with [[OpenAI]]. Four bounded sources describe rollout, professional computer use, robotics use, scientific and mathematical capability, and hands-on workflow fit, but the name, release mechanics, benchmark standing, and commercial tiers remain source-scoped until corroborated by primary documentation.

## Current Profile
The first source presents Astra as a limited rollout that triggered near-AGI, market, governance, and education-policy debate. The robotics sources report grasping, spatial reasoning, planning, error correction, and a source-reported 22% average success rate across one simulation evaluation. They also preserve the deployment boundary: slow inference, fine manipulation, long action sequences, dynamic recovery, and real-time control still require stronger physical-system evidence and may need a lower action layer.

The two workflow sources broaden Astra beyond robot demonstrations. Vol. 175 treats it as one capable option in quota- and cost-aware model routing. LateTalk reports stronger computer use across Blender, Unity, KiCad, Excel, and Power BI plus scientific and mathematical work, but these examples do not establish that one frontier model should handle every task or that professional-software demonstrations transfer reliably to production.
