## Overview

杜延年 / Du Yannian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-455-zhichang-renshi-wenti-bie-ti-yijian-kankan-huangba-de-xiachang-lhaztu17ulzaartaa6y0ehaqvnzx|Hanji 455]] in a closing annalistic notice: [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] appoints him, then Xihe taishou, as yushi dafu. The episode identifies him as a son of Du Zhou, as one of the Qilin Pavilion meritorious ministers, and as a lenient, legally knowledgeable official trusted by Xuan.

## Current Profile

The source gives only a compact profile. Du Yannian is not part of the Huang Ba-Shi Gao recommendation conflict; he appears in the later chronology after Xuan's Hedong tour, tax reduction, amnesty, and dependent-state arrangements for submitted Xiongnu. His appointment to yushi dafu functions as a legitimate personnel notice after the episode's warning about improper personnel advice.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official appointed yushi dafu by Emperor Xuan in the episode's closing chronology.
- Former Xihe taishou in the source account.
- Identified by the host as Du Zhou's son.
- Remembered in the episode as one of the Qilin Pavilion meritorious ministers.
- Described as lenient, legally knowledgeable, long involved in court affairs, and trusted by Xuan.
