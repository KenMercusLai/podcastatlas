## Current Profile

The sources give a compact six-stage profile. Hanji 400-1 and Hanji 401 place Du Yannian inside Huo Guang's Han Zhao-era governing style: Huo promotes him to Taipu for loyalty, uses his more lenient judgment as a policy-review buffer, and the court can rely on him to report an existential conspiracy when Yang Chang will not act. Hanji 402-1 shows what that tact looks like under maximum pressure: Du does not openly overturn Huo's line in the Hou Shiwu case, but he warns that overbroad treason labeling will look legally excessive and damage Huo's reputation, while also preserving Tian Qianqiu from being discarded over Xu Ren. Hanji 402-2 reads the interpersonal side of that role: Du can speak fairly for Tian Qianqiu and maintain official harmony, and the host infers that his father Du Zhou's harsh career gave the family unusually clear knowledge of court survival. Hanji 410-1 places Du in the succession machinery after Liu He's deposition: he joins the push to make Liu Bingyi legible to Huo Guang and Zhang Anshi, then drives the light carriage that brings Liu Bingyi into the ritual preparation sequence. Hanji 455 later shows him as an already trusted senior official, appointed yushi dafu after Xuan's Hedong tour, tax reduction, amnesty, and dependent-state arrangements for submitted Xiongnu.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official later appointed yushi dafu by Emperor Xuan and remembered in the episode as one of the Qilin Pavilion meritorious ministers.
- Huo Guang-backed Han Zhao official promoted to Taipu and used as a lenient policy-review counterpart.
- Crisis reporter who brings the leaked anti-Huo conspiracy to Han Zhao after Yang Chang hesitates.
- Tactful remonstrator who can warn Huo Guang about excessive legal classification without directly challenging his authority.
- Conciliatory court actor identified as Du Zhou's son, with tact linked by the host to family experience with harsh official survival.
- Accession supporter who helps move Liu Bingyi from recommended candidate into formal enthronement procedure.
- Former Xihe taishou in the source account.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 400-1, Hanji 401, Hanji 402-1, Hanji 402-2, Hanji 410-1, and a brief closing notice in Hanji 455. It does not reconstruct Du Yannian's complete family, legal career, offices, Qilin Pavilion role, full participation in the anti-Huo conspiracy reporting chain, full participation in the succession deliberations, or exact legal position in the Hou Shiwu case. Hanji 402-2's father-to-son explanation is preserved as the host's inference rather than proof of direct training.
