# 赵新 / Zhao Xin (ZTE)

## Overview

赵新 is presented as [[ZTE]]'s China-market portable Wi-Fi product director in the No.216 [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] interview. The semantic suffix distinguishes him from [[ZhaoXinWesternHan|赵信]], the Western Han-Xiongnu figure.

## Current Profile

The source says Zhao joined ZTE in 2008 and is responsible for the consumer mobile-connectivity chain from planning and design through development and delivery. His account combines radio and antenna engineering with market segmentation: portable Wi-Fi is valuable only where a dedicated connection device materially improves duration, sharing, reception, safety, mobility, or operating cost over a phone hotspot.

His product method begins with a user and scene, decomposes the job into measurable requirements, selects a form factor and network tier, and returns to users after launch. He uses this method to explain battery-powered, plug-in, vehicle, RedCap, full-5G, multi-network, and indoor/outdoor CPE products as separate answers to different constraints.

## Key Characteristics

- He frames portable Wi-Fi as mobile-broadband infrastructure extended to an individual user.
- He uses concrete student, rental, travel, driver, livestream, ship, farm, workshop, business, and gaming scenes to define product requirements.
- He emphasizes antenna clearance, modem capability, radio-band coverage, thermal behavior, battery safety, and independent Wi-Fi hardware.
- He treats product families as segmented solutions rather than a single best device.
- He presents post-launch feedback and 12-to-18-month iteration as part of hardware product work.
- He warns against low-price and implausible-data marketing while acknowledging that ZTE products also receive criticism and need improvement.
