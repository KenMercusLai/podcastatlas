## Overview

路博德 / Lu Bode enters the wiki through Hanji 380-1 as the Qiangnu Duwei assigned to join [[LiGuangliWesternHan|李广利]]'s large anti-[[Xiongnu|匈奴]] expedition under [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]. In this source, he is a supporting commander in the broad campaign environment that makes [[LiLingWesternHan|李陵]]'s smaller infantry action stand out.

## Current Profile

The episode gives Lu Bode a narrow operational role. He leads more than ten thousand men to rendezvous with Li Guangli while other Han forces depart from Wuyuan and Yanmen. The source does not describe his independent decision-making or later outcomes.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han commander titled Qiangnu Duwei in the episode.
- Assigned to join Li Guangli during the 97 BCE anti-Xiongnu campaign.
- Functions as part of the multi-route military scale before Li Ling's isolated defeat.
