## Overview

路博德 / Lu Bode is a Western Han commander whose profile spans an earlier northern service record, leadership of a principal route against [[Nanyue|南越]], Juyan frontier construction, support for [[LiGuangliWesternHan|李广利]], and intended support for [[LiLingWesternHan|李陵]]. Across these episodes, he moves among expedition leadership, fixed defense, coalition support, and contested subordinate duty.

## Current Profile

Hanji 353-1 adds the background and route missing from Hanji 352-2. It presents Lu Bode as a former Youbeiping administrator who had joined [[HuoQubing|霍去病]] in a northern campaign, taken or killed more than two thousand opponents, and received a marquisate. In the Nanyue expedition he serves as Fubo general, departing from the Guiyang direction while [[YangPuWesternHan|杨仆]] leads the tower-ship route from [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]]. Hanji 354 then places Lu northwest of Panyu, where he receives captives leniently, issues credentials, and uses surrenderers to induce more defections while Yang Pu attacks from the southeast. The resulting collapse makes Lu's role one of political disintegration as well as battlefield pressure.

Hanji 374-1 gives Lu Bode a fixed-defense role in 102 BCE, building and holding at Juyan within a wider frontier program. Hanji 380-1 places him with more than ten thousand men joining Li Guangli in a 97 BCE anti-[[Xiongnu|匈奴]] campaign.

Hanji 377-1 supplies a separate 99 BCE support dispute. After Li Ling volunteers to lead five thousand infantry, Wudi orders Lu to support him. Lu resists the subordinate role and argues that autumn favors Xiongnu horses, recommending a joint spring campaign. Wudi misattributes the memorial to Li Ling's fear, redirects Lu toward Xihe, and sends Li Ling on a separate route. The seasonal caution can be coherent even though role resistance and court interpretation damage coordination.

## Key Characteristics

- Experienced northern officer presented as having administrative, battlefield, and enfeoffment credentials before Nanyue.
- Fubo general leading a principal land route in the multi-route Nanyue campaign.
- Commander whose lenient prisoner policy and recursive recruitment of surrenderers help collapse Panyu's defense.
- Builder and holder of the Juyan position during broader frontier reinforcement.
- Commander assigned to join Li Guangli during a later anti-Xiongnu campaign.
- Intended support commander for Li Ling whose seasonally cautious memorial alters the deployment.

## Qualifications

The sources do not reconstruct a continuous office history. Hanji 353-1 supplies a compact popular account rather than a documented dossier; the Youbeiping post, casualty or capture count, marquisate, and route remain episode-attributed. Hanji 354's surrender mechanism and reward likewise do not settle exact conduct, authorship of policy, or division of credit. Hanji 374-1's “陆博德” and Hanji 353-1's “路伯德” are normalized to 路博德. Hanji 377-1 does not settle whether delay was operationally preferable, and its 99 BCE deployment should not be merged with Hanji 380-1's 97 BCE notice without chronology checking.

## What Changed

- Added Lu Bode's lenient prisoner policy and surrender cascade at Panyu.
- Recast his Nanyue role as political-demobilization work complementing Yang Pu's direct assault.
- Kept later fixed-defense and support roles separate from the earlier expedition command.
