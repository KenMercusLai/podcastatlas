## Overview

路博德 / Lu Bode is a Western Han commander whose profile now spans selection as Fubo general for the conquest of [[Nanyue|南越]], Juyan frontier construction, support for [[LiGuangliWesternHan|李广利]] in a large anti-[[Xiongnu|匈奴]] campaign, and intended support for [[LiLingWesternHan|李陵]]. Across these episodes, he appears as a reusable commander whose roles range from shared expedition leadership to fixed defense and contested support duty.

## Current Profile

Hanji 352-2 supplies the earliest layer now in the evidence inventory. After [[HanQianqiuWesternHan|韩千秋]]'s force is destroyed and the southern theater is framed as historically difficult, [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] selects Lu Bode as Fubo general alongside [[YangPuWesternHan|楼船将军杨仆]] for the coming conquest. The episode establishes selection and strategic importance, but stops before routes, division of labor, or outcome.

Hanji 374-1 gives Lu Bode a later fixed-defense role in 102 BCE. As Han prepares for risk after a Xiongnu succession, Wudi orders the Qiangnu Duwei to build at Juyan and hold the position within a wider wall, fort, beacon, and garrison program. Hanji 380-1 then gives him a narrow operational role in a 97 BCE campaign: he leads more than ten thousand men to rendezvous with Li Guangli while other Han forces depart from Wuyuan and Yanmen.

Hanji 377-1 supplies an earlier and more independent decision in 99 BCE. After Li Ling volunteers to lead five thousand infantry, Wudi orders Lu to support him en route. The episode says Lu does not want the subordinate role and memorializes that autumn is a poor time to fight because Xiongnu horses are fat, recommending a joint spring campaign instead. Wudi reads this as a message engineered by a frightened Li Ling, becomes angry at Li Ling rather than Lu, sends Lu toward Xihe, and orders Li Ling onto a separate September route. Lu's advice can be militarily coherent even though his role resistance and the court's interpretation damage coordination.

## Key Characteristics

- Fubo general selected for shared responsibility in the large campaign against Nanyue.
- Western Han commander titled Qiangnu Duwei in the Juyan episode.
- Builder and holder of the Juyan position during broader frontier reinforcement.
- Commander assigned to join Li Guangli during the 97 BCE anti-Xiongnu campaign.
- Intended support commander for Li Ling's five-thousand-man infantry operation in 99 BCE.
- Seasonally cautious memorialist whose role objection becomes entangled with command misattribution.

## Qualifications

The sources do not reconstruct Lu Bode's full office history or a continuous chronology. Hanji 352-2 announces the Nanyue command but does not establish the later campaign result. Hanji 374-1's “陆博德” is normalized to the established 路博德 identity, while the exact installation type and force size remain source-scoped. Hanji 377-1 attributes both role aversion and seasonal reasoning to his memorial but does not settle whether delay was operationally preferable. Its 99 BCE deployment should not be merged with Hanji 380-1's 97 BCE campaign notice without chronology checking.

## What Changed

- Added Lu Bode's selection as Fubo general for the Nanyue campaign, extending his profile earlier than the northern frontier roles.
- Reframed the profile around varied command assignments while preserving the difference between shared command, fixed defense, and subordinate support.
- Preserved the distinction between plausible seasonal caution and the source-scoped claim that he resisted subordinate status.
