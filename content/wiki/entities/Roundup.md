# Roundup

## Overview
Roundup appears in this episode as the pesticide product at the center of [[Monsanto]]/[[Bayer]] litigation and Supreme Court argument over federal label preemption.

## Current Profile
The source frames Roundup through the tension between EPA labeling and state product-liability claims. [[DavidFriedberg]] says the [[EnvironmentalProtectionAgency]] determined Roundup does not cause cancer for labeling purposes, while plaintiffs continue to bring state failure-to-warn suits. The product therefore becomes a test case for how much federal regulatory approval shields manufacturers from state-law liability.

## Key Characteristics
- Pesticide product linked to Monsanto and Bayer.
- Discussed through failure-to-warn litigation.
- Central to an EPA-labeling versus state-law preemption dispute.
- Used as an example of how scientific, regulatory, and legal determinations can diverge.
