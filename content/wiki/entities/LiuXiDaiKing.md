# 刘喜 / Liu Xi (Dai king)

刘喜 / Liu Xi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-174-liubang-weihe-dafeng-zijide-chouren-1-lnzzvpieqtgatmvgogz3msyhs60u|Hanji 174]] as [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s elder brother. In the episode's founding settlement, Liu Bang makes him king of [[DaiKingdomEarlyHan|代国]], a northern kingdom assembled from Yunzhong, Yanmen, Dai, and related counties.

The page uses the suffix "Dai king" because "Liu Xi" is a common enough romanized name to need a stable semantic route. In this source, Liu Xi matters as a same-surname royal placeholder in the northern security map rather than as a fully developed character.
