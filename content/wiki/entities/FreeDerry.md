# Free Derry

## Overview
Free Derry is the episode's name for the barricaded no-go area around the [[Bogside]] in [[Derry]]. It appears as the local territorial expression of collapsed trust in [[Stormont]], the [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]], and the [[BritishArmy]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Free Derry as more than a slogan. By early 1972, barricades and masked vigilantes made the area a symbolic challenge to state authority, while Protestant business owners complained that arson and inadequate protection had become unsustainable. The British Army's desire to reassert control over this no-go area helped shape the decision to use the Parachute Regiment as an arrest force on 30 January.

## Key Characteristics
- Barricaded Catholic no-go area in Derry around the Bogside.
- Symbolic test of whether British and Stormont authority still functioned.
- Local security zone guarded by masked vigilantes, including some openly armed people.
- Pressure point for army commanders who believed passive patrols had become humiliating.
- Setting that linked civil-rights protest, riot control, and counterinsurgency thinking before [[BloodySunday]].
