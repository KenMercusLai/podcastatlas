# 《蓝血人》 / Blue Blood Man (Ni Kuang novel)

## Overview

《蓝血人》 is an early [[NiKuang|倪匡]] Wesley novel represented here through a [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] retelling. It begins with [[FangTianNiKuang|方天]]'s blue blood and dangerous technology, widens into an international struggle over an extraterrestrial navigation device, and ends with his return to a Saturn civilization destroyed by its own weapons competition.

## Current Profile

The novel's strongest movement is from bodily mystery to civilizational tragedy. [[WesleyNiKuang|卫斯理]] first encounters Fang Tian as an uncanny former classmate who can affect thought, inflict burns, and disappear amid a widening contest involving diplomats, police, a religious organization, and a hidden device. Once Fang Tian discloses that he is a stranded Saturn scientist, the pursuit changes into a homecoming mission and the apparent antagonist becomes a companion.

That shift supports two distinct achievements. The opening converts blue blood, unknown writing, advanced objects, and fear of exposure into fast suspense. The ending converts personal homesickness into [[WeaponsRaceCivilizationalSelfDestruction|a civilization-scale warning]]: formal arms prohibitions fail under mutual secrecy, repeated testing contaminates air and water, and the returning exile finds no intact home.

The same breadth creates instability. [[WuxiaScienceFictionHybrid|Wuxia–science-fiction hybridity]] gives the story action and accessibility, but miraculous healing, exceptional movement, mind control, alien parasites, positrons, longevity by orbital period, and repeated reversals do not form a rigorous scientific system. Ethical problems—whether a controlled body remains the same person, whether illegal violence against a cult is justified, and whether long-lived outsiders may discount shorter lives—are memorable but incompletely developed.

## Key Characteristics

- Opens with a strong bodily and identity mystery built from blue blood, disappearance, mental influence, and unfamiliar technology.
- Expands a personal investigation into espionage, secret-society, spacecraft, and extraterrestrial conflict.
- Changes Fang Tian from threatening unknown to displaced scientist and homesick ally.
- Combines modern-wuxia action grammar with science-fiction language and devices.
- Uses speculative science for narrative possibility rather than engineering consistency.
- Turns the return-home plot into a Cold War-coded warning about arms competition, pollution, and civilizational self-destruction.
- Raises identity and violence questions more effectively than it resolves them.
