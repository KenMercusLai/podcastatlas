# Marine Le Pen

## Overview

Marine Le Pen is the [[NationalRally]] leader whose rebranding of an older far-right inheritance, restored legal eligibility, stable polling base, and second-round viability define the wiki's current French presidential-election branch.

## Current Profile

[[179-french-presidents-1981-2022-part-2-glt2206497694]] supplies the historical interpretation: Le Pen's expulsion of [[JeanMarieLePen|her father]], party renaming, and softer public presentation are attempts to clean up a movement the hosts connect to Vichy, Action Française, anti-republican nationalism, and the Algerian conflict. The episode also stresses that her protectionist and social policies do not fit a simple British left-right map.

[[marine-warfare-le-pen-runs-for-president-6a4e1f2781fe1f6460b7117c]] presents restored practical eligibility as another stage in that [[FarRightNormalization]] project. [[national-front-runner-who-can-beat-le-pen-6ab3960493749e4c0ba24e60]] then shifts the question from whether she can run to whether anyone can beat her, reporting unusually stable first-round support and tested runoff leads amid [[CentristFragmentationRunoffRisk]].

## Key Characteristics

- Leads National Rally after a deliberate effort to reduce its inherited reputational ceiling.
- Distanced the party from her father without erasing its older ideological lineage.
- Combines hard-right immigration politics with protectionist and social-policy appeals.
- Uses energetic anti-establishment campaigning while seeking presidential legitimacy.
- Benefits from a stable base and division among center, center-right, and left opponents.
- Remains subject to legal, polling, coalition, and governing-capacity uncertainty.

## Evidence

- Historical lineage and rebranding: [[179-french-presidents-1981-2022-part-2-glt2206497694]] links her break with Jean-Marie Le Pen and party renaming to a deeper anti-republican tradition.
- Eligibility and legitimacy: [[marine-warfare-le-pen-runs-for-president-6a4e1f2781fe1f6460b7117c]] says the appeal ruling restored her practical campaign path while she continued to contest the underlying conviction.
- Class and party positioning: [[marine-warfare-le-pen-runs-for-president-6a4e1f2781fe1f6460b7117c]] contrasts her working-class social-protection appeal with [[JordanBardella]]'s business-friendly pitch.
- Polling and fragmentation: [[national-front-runner-who-can-beat-le-pen-6ab3960493749e4c0ba24e60]] reports durable first-round support, a tested runoff lead over [[EdouardPhilippe]], and a fragmented opposition field.

## Qualifications

The historical lineage is the hosts' interpretive framing, not proof that every current voter, policy, or party institution directly continues older movements. The legal and polling sources are time-bounded snapshots; early polls, candidate declarations, coordination, transfers, and governing capacity can change before an election.

## What Changed

- Added the older anti-republican lineage used to interpret the rebranding project.
- Distinguished image softening from a complete ideological break.
- Added protectionist and social-policy positioning to the class-appeal profile.

## Relationships

- [[NationalRally]] - party she leads and has tried to normalize.
- [[JeanMarieLePen]] - father and political inheritance from whom she has publicly distanced herself.
- [[JordanBardella]] - possible successor with a more business-friendly appeal.
- [[EmmanuelMacron]] - president whose party-system disruption shapes the field she contests.
- [[FrenchFifthRepublic]] - presidency whose authority she seeks to capture.
- [[TwoRoundPresidentialElection]] - structure translating plurality, fragmentation, and transfer behavior into victory risk.
- [[CentristFragmentationRunoffRisk]] - opposition weakness reinforcing her first-round advantage.
