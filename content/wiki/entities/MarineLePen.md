# Marine Le Pen

## Overview

Marine Le Pen is the [[NationalRally]] leader whose legal eligibility, party-normalization strategy, stable polling base, and second-round viability define the wiki's current 2027 [[France|French]] presidential-election branch.

## Current Profile

[[marine-warfare-le-pen-runs-for-president-6a4e1f2781fe1f6460b7117c]] presents Le Pen's restored practical ability to run after an appeal ruling as the latest stage in a long [[FarRightNormalization]] project. [[national-front-runner-who-can-beat-le-pen-6ab3960493749e4c0ba24e60]] shifts the question from whether she can stand to whether anyone can beat her: it reports a low-to-mid-30s first-round position, unusual stability across three years, and victories in every runoff pairing tested.

Her advantage is both personal and structural. She already uses energetic, anti-establishment campaigning, while the broad center faces [[CentristFragmentationRunoffRisk]] and former Macron officials struggle to present themselves as a break from the incumbent era.

## Key Characteristics

- Leads National Rally after a long attempt to reduce the party's inherited reputational ceiling.
- Combines anti-immigration politics with social-protection appeals aimed at working-class voters.
- Has a more stable polling base than earlier far-right candidates described in the sources.
- Benefits when center, center-right, and left candidates divide first-round support.
- Remains subject to polling uncertainty even though the newer source treats a presidency as plausible.

## Evidence

- Eligibility and legitimacy: [[marine-warfare-le-pen-runs-for-president-6a4e1f2781fe1f6460b7117c]] says the appeal ruling restored her practical campaign path while she continued to contest the underlying conviction.
- Normalization and party positioning: [[marine-warfare-le-pen-runs-for-president-6a4e1f2781fe1f6460b7117c]] links her candidacy to distance from [[JeanMarieLePen]] and contrasts her working-class appeal with [[JordanBardella]]'s business-friendly positioning.
- Polling and fragmentation: [[national-front-runner-who-can-beat-le-pen-6ab3960493749e4c0ba24e60]] reports durable first-round support, a 55%-45% tested runoff lead over [[EdouardPhilippe]], and a fragmented opposition field.

## Qualifications

Both sources are episode-based snapshots. The legal story is source-attributed, and early election polls, candidate declarations, coordination, and transfer behavior can change before the vote. A stable plurality does not itself prove a majority coalition or governing capacity.
