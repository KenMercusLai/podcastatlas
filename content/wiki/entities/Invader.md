# Invader

Invader appears in [[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]] as the pseudonymous French street artist whose tile mosaics are tracked by fans through [[FlashInvaders]]. The source says his pixel-like mosaics have appeared in more than 80 cities and even on highly visible landmarks and unusual sites.

The episode's wiki contribution is not only art-market or street-art biography. Invader becomes a case where low-resolution visual language, urban placement, phone scanning, and fan competition create a [[StreetArtGameLayer]] over the city.
