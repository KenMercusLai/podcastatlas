# Nestle Pure Life / 雀巢优活

Nestle Pure Life / 雀巢优活 appears in [[bali-shui-he-shengpeilu-hai-neng-zhuanqian-quechao-weihe-yao-boli-shui-yewu-1005452453]] as [[Nestle]]'s mass-market bottled-water line. The source contrasts this mass line with premium brands such as [[Perrier]] and [[SanPellegrino]], showing how Nestle once operated both broad water volume and high-end water status.

The episode says Nestle's China mainland water business was sold to [[TsingtaoBreweryGroup|青岛啤酒集团]] in 2020, and that Nestle Pure Life production and sales in China became part of that handoff. This makes the brand a useful marker for the shift away from regional and mass-market water operations before the later premium-water joint venture.
