## Overview

张卬 / Zhang Ang is a Gengshi-era actor whose current wiki role is bounded to three moments in [[LiuXuanGengshiEmperor|更始帝刘玄]]'s rise and collapse. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-663-gengshidi-liuxuan-shi-tan-fu-bushangqiang-de-lanni-lott3pdjjunwlv3wgksn4r5tiqyz|Hanji 663]] shows him forcing the coalition toward Liu Xuan's enthronement after broader discussion had tilted toward [[LiuYanLiuXiuBrother|刘縯]]'s caution. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-677-liuxuandi-dangnian-zhen-shi-yishou-haopai-da-xilan-lu8hizigjkqeps6ulqfqw9gfxnkv|Hanji 677]] then shows Zhang Ang's group treating [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] as no longer defensible under [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]] pressure, and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-680-ming-sang-chimei-zhe-wei-qiyi-huangdi-maigu-hechu-lurtvfqerb0vf3a33msmd-txr9pv|Hanji 680]] shows his group arranging Liu Xuan's killing when public sympathy makes the captive former ruler dangerous.

## Current Profile

Hanji 663 supplies the accession-pressure layer. When anti-Xin commanders agree that a Liu-family royal descendant is needed to bind popular sentiment, Liu Yan argues that the coalition should first call its leader king and delay imperial title until [[WangMang|王莽]] and Chimei are defeated. Many commanders agree, but Zhang Ang throws down his sword and insists that a great decision must not be doubted. The source makes him a pressure actor who can push an improvised coalition from debate into action.

Hanji 677 supplies the later rupture layer. With Chimei near Zheng and Huayin, Zhang Ang's group proposes plundering Chang'an and retreating toward Nanyang or bandit life. Liu Xuan refuses, later summons suspected dissenting commanders into the palace, and the confrontation turns into flight, palace-gate burning, and open rupture. Zhang Ang is therefore not just an enthronement enforcer; he later becomes part of the same coalition's breakdown under military pressure.

Hanji 680 supplies the terminal danger layer. After Chimei occupation, Sanfu officials and civilians sympathize with the deposed Liu Xuan and hope to rescue him. Zhang Ang's group treats that sympathy as a danger and arranges Liu Xuan's night strangling. The source does not make Zhang Ang a complete Chimei commander biography, but it does show him as a recurring actor around Liu Xuan's symbolic value: first using it, then fearing it.

## Key Characteristics

- Gengshi-era pressure actor who helps force Liu Xuan's imperial elevation.
- Coalition hard-liner whose sword gesture turns debate into commitment.
- Later Chang'an actor whose group prefers plunder and retreat when Chimei pressure makes defense look untenable.
- Participant in the suspicion spiral where Liu Xuan's summons converts disagreement into palace rupture.
- Actor whose group later treats Liu Xuan's residual public sympathy as a lethal risk.
- Name-normalized page for Zhang 卬 / Zhang Ang / 张昂 forms in the Gengshi collapse sequence.

## Qualifications

This page is a bounded Gengshi sequence page, not a full biography. Hanji 663 supports Zhang Ang's role in forcing accession, but it keeps his prior affiliation, office, and factional base source-scoped. Hanji 677 supports the plunder-and-retreat and palace-rupture layer, but Liao Zhan, Hu Yin, Shentu Jian, Wei Xiao, Wang Kuang, Chen Mu, Cheng Dan, Zhao Meng, and exact force counts remain source-scoped. Hanji 680 supports the killing-risk layer, but the exact individual responsibility inside Zhang Ang's group remains source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Created Zhang Ang as a bounded Gengshi actor because Hanji 663 and Hanji 677 together show him at both Liu Xuan's elevation and Chang'an rupture.
- Added Hanji 680 as the terminal layer where Zhang Ang's group treats Liu Xuan's residual sympathy as a lethal risk.
