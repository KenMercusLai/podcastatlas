## Overview

丁外人 / Ding Wairen enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-396-1-12-sui-huangdi-yu-6-sui-huanghou-shui-ganchu-de-huangtang-shier-lnq-puxt0zdxhfm7pwilxellccdr|Hanji 396-1]] as [[GaiChanggongzhuWesternHan|鄂邑 / 盖长公主]]'s favored man and the private-access broker who helps [[ShangguanJieWesternHan|上官桀]] and [[ShangguanAnWesternHan|上官安]] bypass [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] after Huo refuses to support six-year-old [[ShangguanTaihouWesternHan|上官氏]]'s palace entry.

## Current Profile

The source presents Ding Wairen less as an independent policy actor than as a palace-intimacy channel. His relationship with the princess is public enough that Han Zhao and Huo Guang have already issued language telling him to serve her well. Shangguan An uses that access by promising that if Ding helps Shangguan Shi become imperial spouse, Ding might gain a marquisate and therefore a more legitimate path toward the princess.

Ding's role is structurally important because the episode shows private favor replacing formal regency consent. Huo Guang says no, but Ding can persuade the princess, and the princess can move Han Zhao's household selection. That makes Ding an early access-broker figure in the conflict that later hardens into the Shangguan-princess anti-Huo alignment.

## Key Characteristics

- Favored man of the Western Han princess known here as Eyi / Gai Chang Gongzhu.
- Inner-court access broker who can influence the princess's palace decision.
- Status seeker because a possible marquisate would improve his relationship to the princess under Han marriage norms.
- Workaround channel used after Huo Guang refuses Shangguan Shi's entry.
- Early figure in the grievance chain that later links the Shangguan family, the princess, and anti-Huo politics.
