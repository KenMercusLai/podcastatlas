# 韵达快递

## Overview
韵达快递 is a major Chinese franchise carrier founded in Shanghai by 聂腾云 after the split following [[NieTengfei|聂腾飞]]'s death.

## Current Profile
The source places 韵达 inside the family branch of the [[TongluExpressCluster|桐庐快递网络]]. It joined Taobao logistics in 2007, later listed through a reverse merger, received Alibaba investment, and remained one of the four Tonglu carriers through repeated price wars.

## Key Characteristics
- Family-genealogy branch from the early 申通 network.
- Franchise carrier tied to ecommerce volume.
- Public company exposed to price-share tradeoffs.
