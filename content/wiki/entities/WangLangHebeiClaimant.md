## Overview
Wang Lang / 王郎 is treated as a Hebei claimant whose pressure first drives [[LiuXiu|刘秀 / 光武帝]] into bodily flight, then redirects Xindu, Zhending, Shanggu, Yuyang, Julu, and Handan actors toward Liu Xiu before Wang Lang's defeat creates Liu Xiu's clearest early [[PostVictoryAmnestySignal|post-victory amnesty signal]]. This page exists to keep that Wang Lang distinct from [[WangLangLateHan|王朗 / Wang Lang (Late Han)]].

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-670-juejing-qiusheng-liuxiu-xianru-shengsi-da-weiji-ltz1nciofev4cqjkod-p8mmwovhe|Hanji 670]] supplies the earliest current Wang Lang layer. Wang Lang's pursuit turns Liu Xiu's Hebei mission into a survival crisis: Liu Xiu crosses the Hutuo under danger, shelters through a storm at Nangong, and reaches Xindu only because [[RenGuangEasternHan|Ren Guang]] and [[PiTongEasternHan|Pi Tong]] remain outside Wang Lang's submission field. The same source shows Wang Lang's pressure creating a strategic opening, because Pi Tong urges Liu Xiu to stay in Hebei and attack Wang Lang while the claimant's Liu Ziyu identity remains unstable.

Geng Yan initially tries to reach the Gengshi regime, but the Wang Lang crisis disrupts movement through Hebei. That disruption becomes historically productive for Liu Xiu: Geng Yan encounters Liu Xiu, judges him a more durable center, and then helps connect Liu Xiu to Youzhou cavalry resources.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-671-nide-lingdao-shifou-zhide-zhuisui-jinlai-duibi-xia-lrjwus-s14y1x2y4r3f-dvri1nv|Hanji 671]] supplies Wang Lang's active pre-defeat layer. Wang Lang sends commanders to contest Yuyang and Shanggu and urgently draw northern border troops, but the Shanggu-Yuyang coalition instead moves toward Liu Xiu. His appointed officials and generals are attacked along the route. At Julu, Wang Lang sends Ni Hong and Liu Feng with a large relief force, but [[JingDanEasternHan|Jing Dan]] and other cavalry raiders defeat them near Nanluan.

The same source gives the surrender-negotiation boundary before Handan falls. Wang Lang sends Du Wei to ask for surrender terms and argues Wang Lang's claim as Emperor Cheng's son, but Liu Xiu denies the legitimacy claim and refuses a wanhu-hou settlement, offering only life. The talks break down, setting up the Handan defeat recorded in Hanji 672.

The defeat layer is now more concrete. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-672-shenchu-zhichang-hui-zhuang-cai-shi-gaoji-zhihui-ls35j8plj-cqnblgufc4plbzpnby|Hanji 672]] says Li Li opens Handan after heavy fighting, Wang Lang flees by night and is killed, and Liu Xiu finds letters from his own officials and acquaintances to Wang Lang. Instead of turning the archive into a purge list, Liu Xiu publicly burns it and announces non-pursuit so wavering insiders can feel safe.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-699-plus-jipin-huangdi-liuxiu-wu-neng-da-tianxia-wen-neng-zhi-guojia-lntw7hm3afitzt7revomgqs-qvph|Hanji 699 PLUS]] preserves the same archive scene as a compact ruler-character example. In the current synthesis, Wang Lang matters less as a developed biography than as the defeated rival whose paperwork becomes Liu Xiu's test of whether victory will produce confidence or retrospective terror.

## Key Characteristics
- Hebei claimant whose pursuit turns Liu Xiu's mission into a survival crisis before it becomes a counteroffensive.
- Active rival whose pressure over Xindu, Zhending, Shanggu, Yuyang, Julu, and Handan triggers Liu Xiu's northern coalition building.
- Political-disruption figure whose own biography remains thinner than the alignment effects around him.
- Defeated Handan claimant whose correspondence archive lets Liu Xiu demonstrate post-victory trust.
- Source node for Liu Xiu's early distinction between fear-driven wavering and purge-worthy hostile continuation.
- Identity-disambiguation node distinct from the later-Han/Three Kingdoms Wang Lang.

## Qualifications
This page is intentionally narrow. It records the Wang Lang claimant only as required by the Geng Yan, Liu Xiu, and northern-coalition episodes and should not absorb content about [[WangLangLateHan|王朗 / Wang Lang (Late Han)]]. Hanji 670 develops the pursuit, Xindu holdout, and anti-Wang-Lang mobilization layer, but the exact Hutuo crossing details, proclamation wording, and force counts remain source-scoped. Hanji 671 develops Wang Lang's active pressure and failed negotiation but keeps Ni Hong, Liu Feng, Du Wei, Wang Lang's exact claim text, and force counts source-scoped. Hanji 672 develops Wang Lang's defeat and archive more than his prior rule; Li Li, exact fighting duration, and minor Handan details remain source-scoped. Hanji 699 PLUS uses the same defeated archive as evidence for Liu Xiu's style of trust repair rather than as an independent Wang Lang biography.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 670 as Wang Lang's earliest current layer: pursuit of Liu Xiu, Xindu holdout context, and Pi Tong's attack-rather-than-retreat reading.
- Preserved Hanji 671 as Wang Lang's pre-defeat pressure, Julu-relief, failed surrender-negotiation, and Liu Xiu legitimacy-rejection layer.
- Preserved Hanji 672 as the concrete Handan defeat, archive discovery, and letter-burning amnesty layer.
- Preserved Hanji 699 PLUS as the compact evaluative version of the same archive scene.
- Preserved Hanji 733-2 as the Geng Yan alignment-redirection layer while continuing to prevent merger with the later-Han Wang Lang page.
