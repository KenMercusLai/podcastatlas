## Overview
Wang Lang / 王郎 is treated in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-2-gengyan-youzhizhe-shijingcheng-jiangmen-huzi-yongguan-sanjun-lnlfumtdzrigby9ox4v6pzbeogeh|Hanji 733-2]] as a Hebei claimant whose uprising redirects [[GengYanEasternHan|耿弇]] toward [[LiuXiu|刘秀 / 光武帝]]. This page exists to keep that Wang Lang distinct from [[WangLangLateHan|王朗 / Wang Lang (Late Han)]].

## Current Profile
In the episode's sequence, Geng Yan initially tries to reach the Gengshi regime, but the Wang Lang crisis disrupts movement through Hebei. That disruption becomes historically productive for Liu Xiu: Geng Yan encounters Liu Xiu, judges him a more durable center, and then helps connect Liu Xiu to Youzhou cavalry resources.

## Key Characteristics
- Hebei claimant whose uprising creates the turning point in Geng Yan's alignment.
- Political-disruption figure rather than a sustained subject of the current source.
- Identity-disambiguation node distinct from the later-Han/Three Kingdoms Wang Lang.
