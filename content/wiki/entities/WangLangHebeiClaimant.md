## Overview
Wang Lang / 王郎 is treated as a Hebei claimant whose uprising redirects [[GengYanEasternHan|耿弇]] toward [[LiuXiu|刘秀 / 光武帝]] and whose later defeat creates a trust-management test for Liu Xiu. This page exists to keep that Wang Lang distinct from [[WangLangLateHan|王朗 / Wang Lang (Late Han)]].

## Current Profile
Geng Yan initially tries to reach the Gengshi regime, but the Wang Lang crisis disrupts movement through Hebei. That disruption becomes historically productive for Liu Xiu: Geng Yan encounters Liu Xiu, judges him a more durable center, and then helps connect Liu Xiu to Youzhou cavalry resources.

The aftermath layer makes Wang Lang a test of victorious trust. After Liu Xiu defeats Wang Lang at Handan, he finds letters between many subordinates and Wang Lang but burns the correspondence unopened. The scene works as a public trust signal: victory does not have to become retrospective exposure of everyone who once hedged under uncertainty.

## Key Characteristics
- Hebei claimant whose uprising creates the turning point in Geng Yan's alignment.
- Political-disruption figure rather than a sustained subject of the current source.
- Defeated claimant whose correspondence archive lets Liu Xiu demonstrate post-victory trust.
- Identity-disambiguation node distinct from the later-Han/Three Kingdoms Wang Lang.

## Qualifications
This page is intentionally narrow. It records the Wang Lang claimant only as required by the Geng Yan and Liu Xiu trust episodes and should not absorb content about [[WangLangLateHan|王朗 / Wang Lang (Late Han)]]. Hanji 699 PLUS does not reconstruct Wang Lang's biography; it uses his defeated archive as evidence for Liu Xiu's style of trust repair.
