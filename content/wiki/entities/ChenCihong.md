# Chen Cihong / 陈慈黉

Chen Cihong is the overseas Chaoshan merchant figure used in [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046]] to make [[QiaopiRemittanceNetworks]] concrete. The episode traces the broader Chen family route from red-head-ship work and the Shantou-Bangkok line into Hong Kong trading houses and Bangkok rice businesses.

Chen's importance in the source is institutional rather than only biographical. The family's shops across Bangkok, [[HongKong]], and [[Shantou]] show how hometown trust, family credit, trade, and remittance could approximate a private exchange network before qiaopi business was eventually absorbed into formal banking channels such as [[BankOfChina]].
