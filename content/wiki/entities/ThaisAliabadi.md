# Thaïs Aliabadi

## Overview
Thaïs Aliabadi is the obstetrician, gynecologist, and surgeon featured in a [[HubermanLab]] episode about PCOS, endometriosis, fertility, perimenopause, and breast-cancer risk.

## Current Profile
In the current wiki evidence, Aliabadi is a clinician and public educator focused on recognizing women's-health patterns that routine care can miss. She connects cycle history, androgen-related symptoms, metabolic context, pain, imaging, ovarian reserve, fertility, and cancer risk while encouraging patients to bring persistent or function-limiting symptoms into qualified clinical evaluation.

Her discussion is strongly practice-based. It supplies symptom clusters and decision prompts, but prevalence estimates, overlap claims, medication strategies, supplements, surgery thresholds, and screening recommendations remain bounded to one public interview rather than independently verified guidance.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames delayed PCOS and endometriosis diagnosis as a symptom-recognition and care-pathway problem.
- Connects reproductive symptoms with insulin resistance, inflammation, ovarian reserve, and fertility.
- Emphasizes that one lab value or normal scan cannot settle every gynecological diagnosis.
- Uses functional impairment and longitudinal symptom patterns to distinguish ordinary variation from evaluation-worthy change.
- Advocates individualized breast-cancer risk assessment rather than reliance on age alone.
