## Overview

杜密 / Du Mi is a late-Han scholar-official figure whose current wiki evidence places him at two points in the party-prohibition arc. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-884-zhichang-chujian-yao-dui-shui-xian-kaidao-lvtc3uqlso242olkyin3fvz5a7ys|Hanji 884]] lists him among renowned worthy figures entering the court center under [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦妙 / 窦皇太后]], [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]], and [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]]; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-893-rongyu-bang-ruhe-biancheng-siwang-mingdan-lmxzqoauf79-swwnk-0cevav0gq|Hanji 893]] leaves him as a source-scoped reputation/accusation-list name in the later crackdown environment.
