# 韩当 / Han Dang

韩当 / Han Dang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-993-shentingling-zhizhan-sunce-dazhan-taishici-lrivpk7col2l7k-s4dqzxxp2kqp1|Hanji 993]] as one of the thirteen riders with [[SunCe|孙策]] during the [[ShentinglingBattle|神亭岭之战]] encounter. The episode names him with [[HuangGai|黄盖]] as a strong veteran in Sun Ce's immediate retinue.

The page is intentionally narrow. Hanji 993 uses Han Dang mainly to show the odds around [[TaishiCi|太史慈]]: Taishi Ci is not facing an isolated scout but Sun Ce accompanied by a small group that includes capable fighting men.
