# Tarek Soleimani

## Overview
Tarek Soleimani is a Yemeni American Brooklyn deli operator and the father of [[YaffaCafe]] founder [[HakeemSoleimani]].

## Current Profile
The episode uses Tarek's path to show how an immigrant business cluster transfers work, language learning, operating knowledge, and capital. He reportedly left Yemen during 1990s conflict, entered corner-store work through a brother, and opened his own store with interest-free family loans after conventional credit proved inaccessible. His long-hours retail base later supplied relationships and practical support for his son's different cafe model.

## Key Characteristics
- Entered U.S. retail through a family member who provided an immediate job and training path.
- Learned English and corner-store operations through work.
- Used interest-free loans from relatives when he lacked conventional credit history.
- Sustained the deli through long, task-dense working days.
- Supplied local relationships, location knowledge, and initial import help to his son's cafe.
