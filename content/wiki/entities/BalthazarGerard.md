# Balthazar Gerard

## Overview

Balthazar Gerard appears in the wiki as the Catholic assassin who killed [[WilliamOfOrange|William of Orange]] in Delft on 10 July 1584 during the [[DutchRevolt]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Gerard through the political consequences of the assassination rather than as a full biography. [[PhilipII|Philip II of Spain]] had placed a bounty on William's head in 1580, and Gerard's killing of William removed the revolt's most important rallying figure. In the anthem-centered narrative, the assassination gives [[HetWilhelmus|Het Wilhelmus]] and the Dutch founding story a sacrificial close: William is remembered as a David-like or Moses-like figure who leads toward freedom but dies before the promised land.

## Key Characteristics

- Catholic assassin tied to the violent escalation of the Dutch Revolt.
- Kills William of Orange in Delft on 10 July 1584.
- Acts after Philip II has placed a bounty on William.
- Functions in the source mainly as the agent of William's martyr-like political memory.
