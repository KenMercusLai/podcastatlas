# Hongkong Land / 香港置地

Hongkong Land / 香港置地 is the acquirer in [[vol-269-xiao-lishi-bu-yao-pa-shi-jishu-xing-tiaozheng-1004793119]]. The episode presents it as a [[JardineMatheson|怡和]]-backed company founded in 1889 to develop [[HongKong|Hong Kong]] land and later concentrated around Central offices, hotels, retail properties, and connected commercial space.

In the [[HongkongLandDairyFarmTakeover|置地饮牛奶]] case, Hongkong Land offered two of its shares for each [[DairyFarmInternational|Dairy Farm]] share, then used [[SouthChinaMorningPost|南华早报]] ads, charts, property images, criticism of Dairy Farm management, and explicit shareholder instructions to make the offer feel operationally easy and financially attractive.

The episode treats Hongkong Land's advantage as organizational and narrative as much as financial: unified control, the [[HenryKeswick|亨利·凯瑟克]] leadership line, stronger advertising execution, and a promise of one-for-five bonus shares let it mobilize shareholders faster than Dairy Farm could defend itself.
