# Cyberport / 數碼港

## Overview
Cyberport / 數碼港 appears in the Tung source as one of the signature controversies of early post-handover Hong Kong governance.

## Current Profile
The source frames Cyberport as a public technology-development project whose allocation to [[LiTzarKai|李澤楷]] without open tender became a symbol of official-business collusion concerns. In the episode, it belongs to a broader [[BusinessEliteRuleHongKong]] and [[PostHandoverHongKongGovernance]] critique rather than a technology-policy success story.

## Key Characteristics
- Tung-era technology and development project.
- Associated in the source with non-open-tender allocation.
- Used as evidence of business-government trust deficits.
- Connected to the broader legitimacy crisis around early post-handover administration.
