# Jogye Order

## Overview

The Jogye Order is the Korean Buddhist organization that the source names as organizer of a Seoul Buddhist expo aimed partly at renewing public interest in Buddhism.

## Current Profile

The source presents the Jogye Order through an event rather than a full institutional history. Its expo gathers stalls selling Buddhist-themed art, prayer beads, singing bowls, funeral services, and modernized religious products, making the order a vehicle for [[CommercializedReligiousAdaptation]] as well as traditional practice.

The order's challenge is durability. The source says South Koreans, especially young people, are becoming less religious and that practicing Buddhist numbers are shrinking, so Buddhist leaders are trying to create lasting engagement without coercion.

## Key Characteristics

- Korean Buddhist organization behind the Seoul expo in the source.
- Institutional bridge between traditional Buddhist ritual and modern visitor-facing formats.
- Actor trying to reach young secular Koreans amid declining formal religious practice.
- Case where religious outreach has to balance accessibility, products, and doctrinal accuracy.

## Evidence

- Event organizer: [[all-right-on-the-night-afds-victory-6a9e8573fe3b7e34fd2d258f]] says the Buddhist expo in Seoul is organized by the Jogye Order.
- Expo contents: [[all-right-on-the-night-afds-victory-6a9e8573fe3b7e34fd2d258f]] describes stalls for art, prayer beads, singing bowls, and Buddhist funeral services.
- Outreach challenge: [[all-right-on-the-night-afds-victory-6a9e8573fe3b7e34fd2d258f]] says Buddhist groups hope such events can revive interest while religious practice is shrinking.
- Engagement boundary: [[all-right-on-the-night-afds-victory-6a9e8573fe3b7e34fd2d258f]] says leaders discuss lasting engagement without coercion.
