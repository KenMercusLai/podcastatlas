# Mapping Police Violence

Mapping Police Violence appears in [[kyiv-brawl-ukraines-top-general-fired-6a609aec4fac21e67f1be165]] as the data source behind [[DanielKnowles]]'s account of police killings. The episode uses its figures to argue that police killings can rise nationally even when murder rates fall.

In the wiki, the project anchors [[PoliceKillingRateDivergence]] as a data problem. It supports comparisons across [[Albuquerque]], [[NewMexico]], and larger cities, and it helps the source separate police killings from broader violent-crime trends.
