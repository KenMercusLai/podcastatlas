# General Mills

General Mills appears in [[132-xuegao-jianghu-fengtouquan-1-132-1]] as the food company behind [[HaagenDazs]] in the episode's China ice-cream discussion. The hosts use General Mills reporting on China store traffic to argue that Haagen-Dazs's premium store model is under pressure.

[[145-gaijia-zhongzi-de-canyin-yangpinpai-feng-tou-quan-1-145-1]] adds that General Mills kept the packaged [[HaagenDazs]] China business after selling the China store business to [[Ningji]]. The source uses this split to show the tension between a packaged-food company and a restaurant-store operation: the same brand can fit supermarket and hotel channels while its dine-in or mall-store model needs different local operating capabilities.
