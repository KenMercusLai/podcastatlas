# 舞阴 / Wuyin (late Han)

## Overview
舞阴 / Wuyin is a late-Han campaign location in Cao Cao's winter 197 CE movement against the [[ZhangXiu|张绣]]-[[LiuBiao|刘表]] line. In the episode, Cao Cao attacks Wuyin after taking [[HuyangLateHan|胡阳]] and capturing [[DengJiLateHan|邓济]], making it part of the Nanyang pressure sequence rather than a stand-alone battle story.

## Current Profile
Wuyin's current wiki role is to mark the second position in Cao Cao's Huyang-Wuyin operation. The episode treats the move as a way to cut or strain the connection between Zhang Xiu's Wan base and Liu Biao's Jingzhou support. That keeps Wuyin connected to [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]], post-Wancheng revenge, and strategic isolation of an allied opponent.

## Key Characteristics
- Late-Han Nanyang-front place tied to Cao Cao's 197 CE southern operation.
- Paired with Huyang in the same campaign sequence.
- Used to explain pressure on the Zhang Xiu-Liu Biao support line.
