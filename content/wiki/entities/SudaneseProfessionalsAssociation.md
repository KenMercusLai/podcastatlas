# Sudanese Professionals Association

## Overview

The Sudanese Professionals Association is the organizing network the episode associates with strikes, demonstrations, sit-ins, stay-at-home actions, and mass noncooperation during Sudan's resistance campaign.

## Current Profile

The episode presents the association as a coordinator able to move beyond street demonstration. After [[OmarAlBashir|Omar al-Bashir]] was removed, protesters continued pressing the military council for civilian representation. Following lethal repression in Khartoum, organizers called a general strike and widespread noncooperation that disrupted ordinary life while reducing the need for millions of participants to confront security forces directly.

Its role illustrates tactical adaptation within [[NonviolentResistanceStrategy]]: a campaign can change the form of participation under repression while continuing to pressure the opponent's [[PillarsOfSupport]].

## Key Characteristics

- Professional organizing network in Sudan's civil-resistance campaign.
- Coordinated multiple tactics rather than relying on demonstrations alone.
- Continued mobilization after the ruler's removal to demand civilian representation.
- Used general strike and mass noncooperation after severe repression.
