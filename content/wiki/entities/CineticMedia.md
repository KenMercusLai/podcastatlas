## Overview

Cinetic Media is [[JohnSloss|John Sloss]]'s independent-film sales company and the principal organizational case in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-20c3269bc63dde599e9e1898d7da833b-33908266-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-20c3269bc63dde599e9e1898d7da833b-33908266|Anatomy of a movie market]].

## Current Profile

At the 2026 [[SundanceFilmFestival|Sundance Film Festival]], Cinetic represents ten films and tries to convert screenings into distribution deals. Its work begins with film review and competition for representation, then continues through buyer attendance tracking, review monitoring, publicity, events, relationship building, private screenings, and negotiation. A weaker post-pandemic market and competition from larger talent agencies have pushed the company toward documentaries, which make up six of its ten films in the episode's festival slate.

## Key Characteristics

- Selects a small slate after reviewing a large annual pipeline.
- Coordinates screenings, publicity, events, and negotiations.
- Monitors buyers, reviews, rival films, budgets, and attention in real time.
- Uses long-standing social and physical festival infrastructure to support deals.
- Has shifted toward documentaries as scripted-film representation became harder to secure.
