# Biba

## Overview

Biba is Barbara Hulanicki's London fashion boutique, used by the episode to trace alternative 1960s styling from intimate glamour through mass retail expansion and financial overreach.

## Current Profile

Opening in Kensington in 1964, Biba combined dark colors, tie-dye, rich decoration, and an Eastern-souk atmosphere with celebrity appeal and high customer volume. Its 1969 expansion showed that alternative aesthetics could scale without immediately losing glamour. The 1973 move into the former Derry and Toms department store as Big Biba then pushed the model beyond sustainable economics; closure around 1975 makes it a bridge from late-1960s boutique confidence to early-1970s excess and punk reaction.

## Key Characteristics

- London boutique founded and run by Barbara Hulanicki.
- Combined alternative visual styling with accessible mass appeal.
- Attracted prominent actors, models, and musicians.
- Expanded from boutique scale to the department-store-sized Big Biba.
- Became a case of glamour, growth, and financial unsustainability.
