# 冷自泉 / Leng Ziquan

## Overview

冷自泉 is the human protagonist of [[BaoHuNiKuangNovel|《宝狐》]], whose lifelong devotion to [[BaoHuCharacter|宝狐]] is framed between transcendent love, resistance to assigned status, and uncertain autonomy.

## Current Profile

The episode presents the young Leng as a privileged military-political prospect who abandons appointment, family expectation, and inherited authority after meeting Bao Hu. He continues searching after extraterrestrial pursuers remove her and, more than four decades later, accepts the claim that joining her requires leaving his body.

His final plane crash can therefore signify unwavering choice and rejection of a life designed by his family. It can also signify the completion of a dependency whose origin he cannot audit. Because Bao Hu may alter perception and desire, [[RomanticAutonomyUnderManipulation]] prevents sacrifice alone from proving that his decision is free.

## Key Characteristics

- Begins inside an elite family and anticipated political career.
- Rejects office and kinship expectations for an extraordinary relationship.
- Sustains the search across more than four decades in the episode's account.
- Makes irreversible bodily sacrifice the final expression of devotion.
- Remains ambiguous between autonomous refusal and manipulated dependence.

## Qualifications

Leng is fictional, and the source's links to Zhang Xueliang, Edward VIII, class politics, addiction, or mind control are interpretive comparisons rather than established prototypes or diagnoses. The episode does not settle whether his death is romantic transcendence, suicide under influence, political refusal, or some combination.
