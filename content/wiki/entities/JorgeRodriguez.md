# Jorge Rodriguez

Jorge Rodriguez appears in [[caracas-under-pressure-democracy-in-venezuela-6a7d8bbe7d85cbdf96e00822]] as the regime-side leader in Venezuela's negotiations with opposition parties. The source describes him as president of the current National Assembly and brother of [[DelcyRodriguez]].

His role matters because the regime presents the talks as a way to cool a two-sided political conflict, while the opposition presents them as a negotiation to end dictatorship. That gap makes Rodriguez a central actor in how institutional reform, sanctions relief, and election timing are framed.
