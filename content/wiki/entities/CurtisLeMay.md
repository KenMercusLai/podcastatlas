# Curtis LeMay

## Overview

Curtis LeMay was the former head of Strategic Air Command selected as George Wallace's vice-presidential running mate in 1968.

## Current Profile

LeMay brought military stature and an aggressive bombing reputation to the [[AmericanIndependentParty]] ticket, but his unveiling became a campaign failure. Despite aides' attempts to keep him away from the topic, he discussed possible nuclear-weapons utility and called public attitudes a phobia, making the ticket appear dangerous and poorly controlled.

## Key Characteristics

- Former Strategic Air Command leader with an aggressive air-power reputation.
- Selected after far-right backers rejected a more moderate running-mate option.
- Publicly entertained nuclear-weapons use at his campaign unveiling.
- Turned the running-mate announcement into a credibility and message-control crisis.
