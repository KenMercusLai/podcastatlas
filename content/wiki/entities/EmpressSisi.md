# Empress Sisi

## Overview

Empress Sisi is [[CrownPrinceRudolf]]'s mother and [[FranzJosephI]]'s wife in the Mayerling episode.

## Current Profile

The source presents Sisi as the family conduit for catastrophe rather than as a complete imperial biography. News of Rudolf's death reaches her before Franz Joseph, and after Mayerling she becomes increasingly solitary. Her later murder in Geneva by [[LuigiLucheni]] is used as one of the later Habsburg tragedies that deepens the episode's sorrowful dynastic frame.

## Key Characteristics

- Sisi is named as Rudolf's mother in the family structure of the Habsburg court.
- She is the person through whom Franz Joseph first learns that something terrible has happened.
- Her grief after Mayerling is linked to withdrawal and isolation.
- Her 1898 murder is used as part of the episode's later Habsburg tragedy sequence.
