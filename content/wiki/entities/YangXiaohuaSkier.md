# 杨小华 / Yang Xiaohua (Skier)

杨小华 / Yang Xiaohua is the skier discussed in [[ep250-zoujin-shanye-shunliu-ershang-xunzhao-huwai-yundong-zhong-de-xinliu-shike-lpv4egumbxcjrxk3jypehyxcqfuo]] through [[ShunliuErshangDocumentary|《顺流而上》]]. The speakers use her example to separate long-term devotion from constant peak experience: even a person who spends large amounts of time skiing will not necessarily reach an exceptional flow state every day.

In the episode, Yang Xiaohua functions as an athlete case for [[OutdoorFlowState]] and [[WomenOutdoorVisibility]]. Her relevance is not only gender representation, but also the discipline of building life around a demanding sport without reducing the practice to daily measurable goals or gendered proof.
