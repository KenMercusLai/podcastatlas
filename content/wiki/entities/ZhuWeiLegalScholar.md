# Zhu Wei / 朱威 (legal scholar)

## Overview
Zhu Wei / 朱威 is the communication-law and internet-law scholar in the No.224 [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] episode on AI-enabled infringement in [[DouyinEcommerce|抖音电商]].

## Current Profile
In the source, Zhu's role is to translate platform examples into legal categories. He distinguishes rights from protected interests, places personality rights, trademark rights, unfair competition, consumer fairness, and authorization contracts into one analytic frame, and repeatedly returns practical examples to the standard of whether consumers are likely to be confused.

His contribution is not a full doctrinal treatise. It is a public-explainer layer for how AI-generated or AI-assisted imitation changes evidence and enforcement without erasing older legal questions around consent, endorsement, platform scope, time limits, product categories, public order, and rights-holder choice.

## Key Characteristics
- He is presented as a university teacher researching communication law and internet law.
- He distinguishes explicit rights from protected interests when analyzing platform infringement.
- He treats consumer confusion as a key standard for account, decoration, trademark, and endorsement imitation.
- He frames authorization as contract-bounded rather than automatically valid across every platform, duration, category, or AI use.
- He treats AI as a tool whose legal relevance depends on infringement, deception, illegality, or platform-rule violation.
