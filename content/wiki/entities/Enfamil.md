# Enfamil

Enfamil is one of the two incumbent infant-formula brands used as the market-concentration backdrop in [[bobbie-laura-modi-how-a-baby-formula-startup-took-market-share-from-two-industry-giants-4488842b-74d3-40ed-8776-5831c5934e69]]. The episode describes the U.S. market as dominated by Enfamil and [[Similac]], making [[Bobbie]]'s entry a challenge against entrenched scale, distribution, and regulatory capacity.

For the wiki, Enfamil is source-scoped as an incumbent reference point rather than a full company history. Its importance is that concentration made [[InfantFormulaRegulatoryMoat]], [[WICFormulaContracting]], and supply-chain dependence central to the startup story.
