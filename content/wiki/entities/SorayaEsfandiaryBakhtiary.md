# Soraya Esfandiary-Bakhtiary / 索拉雅

Soraya Esfandiary-Bakhtiary / 索拉雅 appears in [[77-ta-shi-ziyou-de-yegou-ta-shi-falaqi-685710187]] through [[OrianaFallaci|Oriana Fallaci]]'s interview with the young queen of [[MohammadRezaPahlavi|Mohammad Reza Pahlavi]]. The episode says Soraya lived in a palace but seemed tired of that life, and that questions around her childlessness had become a dynastic problem.

The source uses Soraya as a gendered-power case. Even royal status does not free her from reproductive expectation; her body and fertility become political instruments. That makes her a source example for [[FemaleSelfPossession]] under constraint and for the broader problem of [[ProtectionAsControl]] when status, family, and state power define women's roles.
