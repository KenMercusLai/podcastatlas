# Ruth Porat

Ruth Porat appears in [[tsr-s5-ronconway-v5-tsr-s5-ronconway-v5]] as Google's CFO and a former Morgan Stanley executive who helped [[RonConway]] during the [[SiliconValleyBank]] weekend. Conway says Porat offered to gather precedent from people involved in the 2008 financial crisis.

The episode makes Porat a bridge between private technology networks and financial-crisis expertise. Her work is tied to [[SystemicRiskException]], [[MoralHazardContagionTradeoff]], and [[DepositGuaranteeCrisisResponse]] because the group needed a credible legal and policy argument for guaranteeing deposits without making it look like an ordinary bailout.
