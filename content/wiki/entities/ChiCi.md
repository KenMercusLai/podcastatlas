# 絺疵 / Chi Ci

## Overview
Chi Ci is Zhi Yao's adviser during the [[BattleOfJinyang|Battle of Jinyang]], presented as correctly detecting Wei-Han defection before withdrawing when Zhi Yao compromises the warning.

## Current Profile
Chi Ci infers disloyalty from an incentive mismatch: Zhao is close to collapse, yet [[WeiJu|Wei Ju]] and [[HanKangzi|Han Hu]] appear worried rather than pleased. He warns that Zhao's destruction would leave Wei and Han next in line. [[ZhiYao|Zhi Yao]] rejects the diagnosis and repeats it to the suspected allies; after reading their changed demeanor, Chi Ci concludes that his own position is unsafe and leaves under the pretext of a mission to Qi.

## Key Characteristics
- Reads behavior against expected incentives rather than accepting formal alliance labels.
- Identifies sequential elimination as the allies' reason to defect.
- Recognizes that a disclosed warning can endanger the adviser as well as the plan.
- Chooses exit when the principal will neither use nor protect accurate counsel.

## Evidence
- Defection diagnosis: Chi Ci cites Wei and Han's anxiety at the moment they should expect victory and predicts that they fear being next. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-03-weiju-hanhu-wujiandao-lkiwf0zdnylmaqjgsix-dktxea0l|Zhouji 03]]
- Exit judgment: after Zhi Yao reveals the warning, Chi Ci reads the allies' behavior and leaves on a nominal Qi mission. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-03-weiju-hanhu-wujiandao-lkiwf0zdnylmaqjgsix-dktxea0l|Zhouji 03]]
