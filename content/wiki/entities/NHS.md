# NHS

The NHS appears indirectly in [[a-firm-andy-what-are-new-british-pms-plans-6a5deeca76f8ee25bb4bbf9c]] through the episode's claim that ordinary people are more concerned with seeing a doctor or dentist than with slogans about devolution, AI, or foreign policy. It extends the wiki's [[PoliticalDeliveryGap]] branch by making public-service access the everyday test of national authority.

In this source, the NHS is not analyzed as a health system in detail. It functions as a visible service benchmark for [[AndyBurnham]]'s government: fiscal constraint, tax promises, and [[LabourPartyUK]] unity matter politically because voters judge whether basic care becomes easier to obtain.
