# Bogside

The Bogside appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] as the Catholic district of [[Derry]] where the August 1969 street confrontation became the [[BattleOfTheBogside]]. The episode describes barricades, petrol bombs, first aid stations, water cannon, tear gas, burning shops, and clashing interpretations of whether the violence showed police aggression or insurrection.

The Bogside also becomes the place where [[BritishArmy]] troops were initially welcomed by many Catholics and where [[JimCallaghan]] was cheered after visiting. That welcome is source-scoped as temporary because troop deployment did not resolve the political crisis.
