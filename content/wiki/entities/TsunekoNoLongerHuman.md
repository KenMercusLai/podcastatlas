# 常子 / Tsuneko

常子 / Tsuneko is the lonely bar hostess in [[NoLongerHuman|《人间失格》]] whose relationship with [[ObaYozo|大庭叶藏]] leads to the novel's first failed double suicide as summarized in [[33-renjian-shige-danxiaogui-de-zhuanglie-xianji-599924624]]. The episode presents her as the first woman toward whom Yozo feels an active, almost positive attraction.

Their decision to die together comes from poverty, drink, loneliness, and Yozo's failure to imagine a livable ordinary future. Tsuneko dies and Yozo survives, turning the episode's earlier shame into a sharper guilt: even suicide becomes something he cannot complete cleanly.

## Key Claims
- Tsuneko is not only a plot device; she marks the point where Yozo's attraction to loneliness becomes fatal.
- Her death deepens Yozo's sense that survival itself can become shame.
- The episode keeps the event source-scoped as tragedy and guilt rather than romanticizing double suicide.
