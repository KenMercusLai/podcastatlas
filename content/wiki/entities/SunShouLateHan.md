## Overview

孙寿 / Sun Shou is the wife of [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] in the wiki's current Hanji 848 evidence. She matters as part of the Liang household's final collapse: after [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] seizes the palace and military initiative against Liang, the episode says Liang and Sun Shou die by suicide on the same day, followed by a wider Liang-Sun family purge.

## Current Profile

Hanji 848 gives Sun Shou one concentrated role rather than a full biography. She appears at the moment Liang Ji's apparent power suddenly fails: palace access has been controlled, imperial symbols and troops are in Huan's hands, Liang's general-in-chief seal is removed, and Liang is demoted. The immediate result is that Liang and Sun Shou both kill themselves.

The source then makes Sun Shou relevant beyond her death by naming the Sun family in the collective purge. Huan arrests Liang and Sun relatives and their court and local attachments, executes family members publicly, removes senior officials who had depended on Liang, and dismisses hundreds of former clerks and guests. Sun Shou therefore marks the household and kinship dimension of [[OuterRelativeCourtPower|outer-relative court power]] rather than an independent policy profile.

## Key Characteristics

- Liang Ji's wife whose death is tied directly to the 159 CE coup's success.
- Household actor whose suicide shows how quickly Liang's protection failed once palace and command symbols were seized.
- Sun-family node in the wider Liang-Sun purge after the coup.
- Source-bounded figure whose current significance is kinship, household collapse, and purge scope.

## Qualifications

The current page is intentionally narrow. Hanji 848 does not give Sun Shou's earlier career, family strategy, or independent factional role. It only makes her relevant to the Liang household's collapse and to the breadth of the Liang-Sun punishment after Huan's coup.
