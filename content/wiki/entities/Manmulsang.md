# Manmulsang

Manmulsang is the Amazon-like company named in [[getting-entrepreneurial-in-korea-summer-school]] as part of [[NorthKorea|North Korea]]'s limited domestic market activity. The source says North Korea developed a domestic intranet and an Amazon-like company called Manmulsang while local businesses, foreign investment, and business training were expanding.

Its wiki role is narrow but useful: Manmulsang shows that digital commerce-like structures can appear even inside a repressive economy. The source keeps that emergence bounded by [[KimJongUn]]'s regime priorities and the absence of secure [[PropertyRightsAsInvestmentIncentive]].
