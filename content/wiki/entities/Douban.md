# 豆瓣 / Douban

豆瓣 / Douban appears in [[140-haikeyi-de-jin-nvshi-suoyi-ren-weishenme-yao-nuli-a-854173655]] as the public book-rating and review surface that affects [[Jinzi|金子]] after publishing [[HaikeyiDeJinNvshi|《还可以的金女士》]]. The source treats Douban less as a technology object than as a scoring environment: even when a creator knows that reviews are partial, one low score or hostile comment can dominate attention.

This makes Douban part of the wiki's [[CreatorEvaluationPressure]] branch. In this source, platform scoring revives [[RedPenLogic]] because book reception begins to feel like being graded again, not only like hearing from readers.

[[167-bailatu-lusuo-hayeke-alunte-sida-zhexuejia-hui-ruhe-jieshi-suanfa-shidai-chuantai-dushu-buchenglin-lmbsdbfeqqfz5vm3g67b-wjgdjfja]] adds Douban as a comparison point in the episode's platform-regime discussion. The source contrasts platform structures by asking how different public spaces shape creator behavior, reputation, and [[AlgorithmicPublicAppearance|public appearance]].

[[no-212-cengjing-de-jingshen-jiaoluo-douban-zhihu-tieba-hupu-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-25-1003510980]] adds Douban as a full platform-history case inside [[ChinesePCInternetCommunities]]. The source traces [[Abei|阿北]] from reading, science fiction, programming, and a failed travel-guide project to Douban's 2005 launch around books, groups, movies, music, same-city activity, musicians, and Douban FM. It frames Douban's restrained design and taste community as a rare cultural asset, while also treating its many separate mobile apps as a source case for [[MobileCommunityFragmentationRisk]].

The same source sharpens Douban's business-model tension. Abei's dislike of copying, points-driven spam, excessive ads, and algorithmic content-farm logic helped protect Douban's atmosphere, but also contributed to missed opportunities in movie ticketing, ecommerce, algorithmic distribution, and broader social products. Douban therefore becomes one of the clearest examples of [[OnlineCommunityCommercializationFriction]].
