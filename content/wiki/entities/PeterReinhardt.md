# Peter Reinhardt

Peter Reinhardt is the founder interviewed in [[peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted-peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted]] about [[ClassMetric]], [[Segment]], and [[CharmIndustrial|Charm Industrial]]. The episode presents his career as a series of evidence-forced pivots: a classroom feedback product failed in real classrooms, a planned analytics product failed to produce buying intent, and a small open-source library, [[AnalyticsJS|analytics.js]], unexpectedly became the seed for Segment.

His wiki role is not only successful founder retrospective. The source makes Peter a case in [[FounderProductFit]] that changes over time: software entrepreneurship came through roommates, [[YCombinator]], and customer-data infrastructure, while Charm aligned more directly with his hard-tech and hardware interests. The episode also turns him into a bridge between [[FounderLedSales]], [[ProductLedWillingnessToPay]], and [[CarbonRemovalPermitting]] because he describes both enterprise software pricing and real-world climate-tech permitting as founder-level problems.
