# Jonathan Rottenberg

## Overview
Jonathan Rottenberg is a psychologist represented through a contextual mood-system account of depression and a pluralistic stance toward care.

## Current Profile
In [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361|The Story of Stories]], Rottenberg argues that low mood can sometimes signal unresolved problems, conserve effort, or prompt reevaluation, while severe depression can become chronic, destructive, and disconnected from any benefit. His position is neither medication rejection nor compulsory meaning-making: medication, psychotherapy, activity, relationships, self-understanding, and time may all matter.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames mood through interacting biological, environmental, relational, temperamental, and behavioral conditions.
- Separates ordinary low mood and possible signaling functions from severe depression.
- Rejects both a universal defect story and a universal “silver lining” requirement.
- Supports plural care routes and leaves medication decisions with patients and treating professionals.
