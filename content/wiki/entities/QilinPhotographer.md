## Overview

麒麟 is a photographer and explorer whose work links long-distance travel, documentary practice, conflict exposure, visual impairment, and ethical reflection.

## Current Profile

The source presents Qilin through a refusal of simple heroic identity. He crossed roughly 700 kilometers of [[Kenya]] with [[MartinKenyaGuide|马丁]] and [[QipeishiCamel|齐佩石]], worked in high-risk settings including [[Haiti]], and continued photographing after losing sight in one eye and facing deterioration in the other. These experiences support an operating profile built around local knowledge, explicit downside assessment, withdrawal when risk crosses capacity, and skepticism about professional prestige derived from other people's suffering.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses travel and photography to replace distant stereotypes with situated contact.
- Treats local guidance, animal care, logistics, and retreat as parts of adventure competence.
- Uses the camera as a perceptual, relational, and expressive tool under changing vision.
- Questions whether photographic publication and awards produce material benefit for depicted people.
- Distinguishes courage from ignorance, survivorship, and manageable exposure.
- Discusses trauma and mortality directly without presenting fearlessness as the goal.
