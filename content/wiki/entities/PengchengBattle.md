# 彭城之战 / Battle of Pengcheng

彭城之战 / Battle of Pengcheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-151-huanyuan-pengcheng-zhizhan-xiangyu-zai-fengshen-1-lspx-bqe5kczq6bmn6thlerfbrmu|Hanji 151 part 1]] as the coming first major battle of the Chu-Han struggle. The source introduces the famous scale contrast, with [[XiangYu|项羽]] later defeating [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s large allied army, but the available episode stops before the tactical battle sequence.

The setup begins with legitimacy. After Xiang Yu kills Yi Emperor [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]], [[DongGongXincheng|新城董公]] tells Liu Bang to mourn publicly and declare Xiang Yu a rebellious regicide. The battle is therefore not introduced as a pure clash of armies; it is preceded by [[MoralizedWarPretext|道德化战争借口]], public mourning, and the attempt to make anti-Xiang war acceptable to other lords.

The coalition side is deliberately opportunistic. Liu Bang uses the accusation over Mi Xin to summon lords, deceives [[ChenYu|陈馀]] with a substitute head so Zhao will participate without killing [[ZhangEr|张耳]], and benefits from the earlier collapse of Xiang Yu's northern settlement after [[SimaAng|司马卬]] is captured and [[HeneiCommandery|河内郡]] is organized under Han control.

The Xiang Yu side is constrained by Qi. [[TianHeng|田横]] gathers remnants after [[TianRong|田荣]]'s death, installs [[TianGuangQiKing|田广]] as Qi king, and holds around [[JuWarringStates|莒城 / 城阳]]. Xiang Yu remains in Qi trying to suppress the revolt while Liu Bang moves on [[Xuzhou|彭城]], making the battle's opening condition a product of [[CoalitionSettlementFailure|联军战后安排失败]] as much as battlefield timing.

The episode's final setup is Liu Bang's post-capture relaxation. After taking Pengcheng, Liu Bang and the army enjoy treasure, women, wine, and feasts; even [[ZhangLiang|张良]] and [[ChenPing|陈平]] are described as momentarily silent. The battle page therefore begins as a case where successful political mobilization and strategic timing are immediately endangered by [[VictoryOverreachFailure|数胜必亡式胜利反噬]].
