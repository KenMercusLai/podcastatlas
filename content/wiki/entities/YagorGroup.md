# Yagor Group / 雅戈尔

Yagor Group / 雅戈尔 appears in [[ep271-tanfang-rouxing-zhizao-gongchang-shi-shui-rang-ni-xiangyao-jiu-neng-maidao-lqyrioptggnbb8hl-llej0-5ow0v]] as the apparel and menswear case for [[FlexibleManufacturing|柔性制造]]. [[WuLiwei|吴立伟]] emphasizes that Yagor differs from many apparel brands because it remains relatively heavy-asset, with more of its raw material, fabric, production, sales property, and sales team inside its own system.

The source uses Yagor to show how a traditional formalwear company adapts when post-pandemic suit demand softens and outdoor or casual clothing grows faster. Its flexibility is less about endless color or style variation than about fabric function, anti-wrinkle and non-iron expectations, custom suit data, scientific scheduling, insertable small batches, computerized pattern layout, and laser cutting. The episode also treats Yagor as a constraint case: upgrading to higher-level smart factories can require more capital than current sales momentum easily supports.
