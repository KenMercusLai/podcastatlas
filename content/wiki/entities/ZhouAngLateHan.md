# 周昂 / Zhou Ang (late Han)

周昂 / Zhou Ang (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-958-jiemi-shishang-zui-duanming-de-tongchoudikai-lve41q9gyijbop8wk-b7e6shogju|Hanji 958]] as [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s appointed Yuzhou inspector during the breakdown of the [[AntiDongZhuoCoalition|反董卓联盟]]. While [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] is fighting [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s forces, Zhou Ang takes Sun Jian's base at Yangcheng.

The source uses Zhou Ang less as a developed biography than as the proxy through whom allied sabotage becomes visible. Sun Jian can claim he is risking himself for public duty against Dong Zhuo, but a nominal coalition partner's appointee seizes his rear. [[YuanShu|袁术]] then sends [[GongsunYueLateHan|公孙越]] to help Sun Jian counterattack, turning Zhou Ang's occupation into the battlefield where Gongsun Yue dies.
