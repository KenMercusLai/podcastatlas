# 徐晓楠 / 曲晓楠

## Overview
徐晓楠 / 曲晓楠 is the [[Didi]] ride-hailing safety lead interviewed by [[LiuFei|刘飞]] in the [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] episode on platform safety systems.

## Current Profile
The source presents Xu as an operator responsible for explaining how [[Didi]] rebuilt ride-hailing safety after the 2018 Hitch safety incidents. Her account is process-heavy: safety is not one feature but a chain of admission controls, driver verification, risk-aware dispatch, model screening, human warning specialists, phone intervention, police escalation, and after-the-fact responsibility handling.

Her strongest conceptual contribution is a conservative safety posture. She argues that [[HighRecallSafetyIntervention]] should tolerate many false alarms because missed severe incidents are more costly than user annoyance, while also warning that platform safety is incomplete when users move rides offline, close recording, send minors alone, or let drunk friends travel without support.

## Key Characteristics
- She is represented in the source as [[Didi]]'s ride-hailing safety负责人.
- She frames 2018 as the starting point of [[Didi]]'s modern safety rebuild.
- She defines safety boundaries through safe driver-passenger separation rather than payment or trip completion alone.
- She prioritizes recall over precision in safety warning models.
- She treats ride-hailing safety as a shared system involving platform, driver, and passenger behavior.
- She keeps privacy and safety linked through optional recording, model-first scanning, and restricted human review.
