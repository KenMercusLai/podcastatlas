## Overview
Qianling Mountain macaques / 黔灵山猕猴 are the urban-park macaque population discussed in [[ep282-bu-wanmei-de-gongcun-dang-yesheng-dongwu-chengwei-chengshi-xin-jumin-lg31-8lajmed4k4mhg5iz7hh8r1a]] as one of the episode's highest-conflict examples of wild animals becoming city "new residents."

## Current Profile
The source portrays the macaques as neither simply wild nor domesticated. They remain protected wild animals, but long tourism exposure, abundant human food, lack of predators, and repeated feeding have made them unusually comfortable around people. The population is described as much larger than estimated park carrying capacity, and the animals now create management costs through grabbing, biting, vaccination needs, and movement from the park into surrounding residential areas.

## Key Characteristics
- Long-term visitor contact has reduced fear of people and made close encounters routine.
- Unregulated feeding comes from multiple groups, including tourists, volunteer feeders, and short-video creators.
- The reported population has exceeded the estimated ecological carrying capacity of the park.
- Human safety and animal protection are linked because bites, robbery, and disease anxiety can trigger harsher control pressure.
- Relocation is discussed as a management idea but remains underspecified in the source.

## Evidence
- Habituation: [[ep282-bu-wanmei-de-gongcun-dang-yesheng-dongwu-chengwei-chengshi-xin-jumin-lg31-8lajmed4k4mhg5iz7hh8r1a]] describes macaques approaching visitors, grabbing items, and drinking a reporter's stolen water in front of her.
- Feeding pressure: [[ep282-bu-wanmei-de-gongcun-dang-yesheng-dongwu-chengwei-chengshi-xin-jumin-lg31-8lajmed4k4mhg5iz7hh8r1a]] names tourists, volunteer monkey feeders, and short-video creators as distinct food sources.
- Capacity and conflict: [[ep282-bu-wanmei-de-gongcun-dang-yesheng-dongwu-chengwei-chengshi-xin-jumin-lg31-8lajmed4k4mhg5iz7hh8r1a]] reports more than one thousand macaques against an expert-estimated carrying capacity of roughly three hundred to five hundred, plus bites requiring vaccination.
- Management uncertainty: [[ep282-bu-wanmei-de-gongcun-dang-yesheng-dongwu-chengwei-chengshi-xin-jumin-lg31-8lajmed4k4mhg5iz7hh8r1a]] mentions "diversion" or relocation while saying the destination problem is unclear.

## Qualifications
The source gives reported figures and expert estimates rather than a full census or management plan. It also does not establish whether all conflict is caused by feeding alone; missing predators, park design, greenbelts, reproduction, and tourist density all matter.
