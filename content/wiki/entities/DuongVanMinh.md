# Dương Văn Minh

## Overview

Dương Văn Minh, called “Big Minh” in the episode, was South Vietnam's last leader at the moment Saigon fell in April 1975.

## Current Profile

His source role is concentrated in the Presidential Palace surrender scene. He waited for North Vietnamese forces and offered to transfer power, only to be told that no power remained to transfer. The exchange makes him the human symbol of a state whose legal office survived briefly after its practical sovereignty had disappeared.

The episode says he was not executed, later lived under house arrest, and devoted time to orchids. This restrained ending contrasts with expectations of immediate bloodshed while not resolving the wider repression and displacement that followed.

## Key Characteristics

- He was the final leader of South Vietnam.
- He remained at the Presidential Palace as North Vietnamese forces entered Saigon.
- His attempted surrender dramatized the gap between formal office and vanished state power.
- He survived the regime's collapse rather than being summarily executed.
- His later house arrest separates personal survival from political freedom.
