# 《讲了一百万次的故事》 / Stories Told a Million Times

[[StoriesToldAMillionTimes|《讲了一百万次的故事》]] is the oral-literature anthology frame connecting two [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] mythology episodes. [[147-feizhou-shenhua-yemao-chuangshi-tulang-xiadan-ji-nezha-de-lingyi-banben-889573004]] uses it for collected African tales, while [[32-yindianren-jiang-le-yi-bai-wan-ci-de-gushi-shi-maerkesi-de-laoshi-596386714]] uses it for Indigenous American stories.

The book matters in the wiki less as a single canon than as a mediation point. Oral stories reach distant readers through collectors, translators, editors, hosts, and podcast retellings, so the anthology makes [[StoryMotifTransmission]], [[MythicSourceLayering]], and source caution visible. Its stories can feel strange, abrupt, comic, or cruel because they preserve local explanation, performance memory, and social worlds rather than modern literary smoothness.
