# Sri Narayanan

Sri Narayanan is a [[UniversityOfSouthernCalifornia|USC]] professor featured in [[tech-20260730-0730-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260730-0730-mp-tech-pod-128]] for human-centered AI research at the [[SignalAnalysisAndInterpretationLab]]. The episode uses his real-time MRI and machine-learning work on beatboxing to make [[BehavioralSignalProcessing]] concrete: AI can map tongue, lip, airway, and trachea movement to study how vocalization works.

Narayanan's larger role in the source is to keep AI research anchored in human signals and human consequences. His lab's work extends beyond vocal performance into neurodevelopment, autism-related research, and early biomarkers for depression, while his caution centers on privacy, bias, and agency when systems can infer sensitive identity-linked traits.
