# 即墨大夫 / Jimo Dafu

即墨大夫 / Jimo Dafu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-2-lmnqfjmydygqnfec6xhzpixralvi]] as the local [[QiState|齐国]] official whose court reputation is bad but whose district performs well when [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] secretly investigates it. The source says Jimo has opened land, has full fields, a prosperous population, and stable eastern borders.

His source role is the positive half of [[IndependentOfficialAudit]]. Qi Wei Wang concludes that Jimo Dafu is attacked at court because he has not bribed or flattered nearby courtiers, then rewards him with additional households.
