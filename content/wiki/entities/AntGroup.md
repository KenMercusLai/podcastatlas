# 蚂蚁集团 / Ant Group

## Overview
Ant Group / 蚂蚁集团 is the Chinese fintech and AI company that the wiki now tracks across [[Alipay|支付宝]], agentic payment, health, enterprise infrastructure, globalization, and embodied intelligence.

## Current Profile
Ant is presented as seeking an agent-era position broader than its old financial-technology label: personal life agents, health agents, AI payment, offline touchpoints, databases, enterprise technology, world models, and global wallet networks reinforce one another. The roundtable adds an operating role for Ant as commerce catalyst and trust provider, using merchant incentives, authorization, KYA, capability assessment, anti-fraud, traceability, and recourse to help a thin two-sided market start moving.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses [[Alipay]] as a payment, service, order, bill, mini-program, and offline touchpoint base for agentic workflows.
- Reframes payment trust, risk control, authorization, and liability as foundations for [[AgentPaymentInfrastructure|AI payment]].
- Treats health through [[AntAfu|阿福]] as a high-priority intent and data domain rather than a simple medical chatbot.
- Builds or incubates technology assets such as [[OceanBase]], [[DigitalAnt|蚂蚁数科]], and [[AntLingbo|蚂蚁灵波]] beyond consumer finance.
- Pushes global payment and risk-control capacity through [[AntInternational|蚂蚁国际]] and wallet-network infrastructure.
- Uses organizational separation and financing as a way to price, incentivize, and develop technology businesses independently.
- Seeks to catalyze merchant-agent supply while extending payment trust into agent identity, authorization, audit, and remedies.

## Qualifications
The wiki still has no independent performance or financial verification for the new Ant thesis. Investment amounts, financing rounds, valuation ranges, touchpoint counts, wallet counts, WorldFirst volume, shopping growth, merchant demand, launch timing, and adoption forecasts remain source-scoped. Ant's new profile also does not make it a robotics hardware company; Lingbo remains a robot-brain and physical-world AI branch within a wider Ant strategy.

## What Changed
- Added Ant's proposed role as a two-sided agent-commerce catalyst.
- Expanded payment trust to include KYA, capability assessment, audit, anti-fraud, and recourse.
