# 蚂蚁集团 / Ant Group

## Overview
Ant Group / 蚂蚁集团 is the Chinese fintech and AI company that the wiki now tracks across [[Alipay|支付宝]], agentic payment, health, enterprise infrastructure, globalization, and embodied intelligence.

## Current Profile
Ant is presented as seeking an agent-era position broader than its old financial-technology label: personal life agents, health agents, AI payment, offline touchpoints, databases, enterprise technology, world models, and global wallet networks reinforce one another. The roundtable adds an operating role for Ant as commerce catalyst and trust provider, using merchant incentives, authorization, KYA, capability assessment, anti-fraud, traceability, and recourse to help a thin two-sided market start moving. The LingGuang interview adds a consumer-product and model-development route: use Alipay-linked service supply, matter-level product feedback, and close application/model coordination to pursue a restrained personal agent.

A historical labor-supply-chain branch now complements the product profile. [[YongheAidoTechnology|永和爱豆科技]] reportedly received annotation orders internally from Ant or through Ant-linked connections to Alipay, Amap, Alibaba Cloud, and other applications. The case ties apparently automated products to county women's [[DataAnnotationLabor]] while leaving contract structure, customer attribution, and Ant's responsibility unverified.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses [[Alipay]] as a payment, service, order, bill, mini-program, and offline touchpoint base for agentic workflows.
- Reframes payment trust, risk control, authorization, and liability as foundations for [[AgentPaymentInfrastructure|AI payment]].
- Treats health through [[AntAfu|阿福]] as a high-priority intent and data domain rather than a simple medical chatbot.
- Builds or incubates technology assets such as [[OceanBase]], [[DigitalAnt|蚂蚁数科]], and [[AntLingbo|蚂蚁灵波]] beyond consumer finance, using organizational separation and financing to develop some businesses independently.
- Pushes global payment and risk-control capacity through [[AntInternational|蚂蚁国际]] and wallet-network infrastructure.
- Is reported to have connected outsourced county annotation labor to AI use cases across its own or affiliated business network.
- Seeks to catalyze merchant-agent supply while extending payment trust into agent identity, authorization, audit, and remedies, including through [[AntLingGuang|LingGuang]]'s consumer personal-agent route.

## Qualifications
The wiki still has no independent performance or financial verification for the new Ant thesis. Investment amounts, financing rounds, valuation ranges, touchpoint counts, wallet counts, WorldFirst volume, shopping growth, merchant demand, launch timing, adoption forecasts, LingGuang product behavior, and retention claims remain source-scoped. Ant's new profile also does not make it a robotics hardware company; Lingbo remains a robot-brain and physical-world AI branch within a wider Ant strategy. LingGuang and 阿宝 remain separate entities because the supplied sources do not establish their organizational or product relationship. The Yonghe episode provides no contract, purchase order, customer confirmation, or Ant response, so it supports a reported supply-chain connection rather than a verified allocation of responsibility for local employment conditions.
