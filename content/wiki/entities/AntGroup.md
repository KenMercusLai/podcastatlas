# 蚂蚁集团 / Ant Group

## Overview
Ant Group / 蚂蚁集团 is the Chinese fintech and AI company that the wiki now tracks across [[Alipay|支付宝]], agentic payment, health, enterprise infrastructure, globalization, and embodied intelligence.

## Current Profile
The earlier Ant Group page was narrow: it appeared mainly as the parent behind [[AntLingbo|蚂蚁灵波]] and an "AI first" physical-world AI context. The new Bund Conference source broadens that profile. Ant is presented as seeking a new position after the old financial-technology label became too small: personal life agents, health agents, AI payment, offline touchpoints, databases, enterprise technology, world models, and global wallet networks are all part of the current synthesis.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses [[Alipay]] as a payment, service, order, bill, mini-program, and offline touchpoint base for agentic workflows.
- Reframes payment trust, risk control, authorization, and liability as foundations for [[AgentPaymentInfrastructure|AI payment]].
- Treats health through [[AntAfu|阿福]] as a high-priority intent and data domain rather than a simple medical chatbot.
- Builds or incubates technology assets such as [[OceanBase]], [[DigitalAnt|蚂蚁数科]], and [[AntLingbo|蚂蚁灵波]] beyond consumer finance.
- Pushes global payment and risk-control capacity through [[AntInternational|蚂蚁国际]] and wallet-network infrastructure.
- Uses organizational separation and financing as a way to price, incentivize, and develop technology businesses independently.

## Qualifications
The wiki still has no independent performance or financial verification for the new Ant thesis. Investment amounts, financing rounds, valuation ranges, touchpoint counts, wallet counts, WorldFirst volume, and priority claims remain source-scoped until corroborated. Ant's new profile also does not make it a robotics hardware company; Lingbo remains a robot-brain and physical-world AI branch within a wider Ant strategy.
