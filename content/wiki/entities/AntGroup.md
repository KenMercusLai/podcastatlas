# 蚂蚁集团 / Ant Group

Ant Group / 蚂蚁集团 appears in [[147-he-mayi-lingbo-shenyujun-liao-jiqiren-yuansheng-jichu-moxing-danao-he-benti-de-guanxi-yuxunlian-yu-shuju-scale-up-laoshi-tangxiaoou-luxtyuafi-2onim15fw6lpypo2ga]] as the parent organization behind [[AntLingbo|蚂蚁灵波]]. [[ShenYujun|沈宇军]] says Ant Group is overall "AI first" and that Lingbo carries part of the group's exploration of physical-world AI and the upper bound of embodied intelligence.

The source does not turn Ant Group into a robotics hardware company. Its role is institutional support: Ant provides the context for a startup-like company that can pursue a long-horizon robot-brain bet while still facing the risk that a key technical path may fail.
