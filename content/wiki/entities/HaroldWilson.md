# Harold Wilson

Harold Wilson appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] as the British prime minister whose government moved from pressure on [[Stormont]] toward direct military intervention in [[NorthernIreland]]. The episode says Wilson and [[JimCallaghan]] pushed [[TerenceONeill|Terence O'Neill]] for reforms after the Derry march violence, including possible financial pressure from London.

Wilson's government still appears reactive rather than strategically prepared. The episode emphasizes British cabinet ignorance of Northern Ireland, later treating the [[BritishArmy]] deployment as probably unavoidable by August 1969 but not backed by a coherent political settlement.
