# Consuelo de Saint-Exupery / 康苏爱萝

[[ConsueloDeSaintExupery|Consuelo de Saint-Exupery / 康苏爱萝]] enters the wiki through [[07-yong-rongge-lilun-fenxi-xiaowangzi-yongheng-shaonian-ji-qita-539091165]], where [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] discusses her as [[AntoineDeSaintExupery|Antoine de Saint-Exupery / 圣埃克苏佩里]]'s wife and as the main biographical source behind the rose in [[TheLittlePrince|《小王子》]]. The episode notes her nickname Rosa, her earlier widowhood, the difficult marriage, and her later account that waiting became a major theme of her life.

In the source, Consuelo matters less as an independent biography than as the person who makes the rose hard to romanticize. The rose can be beautiful, vulnerable, vain, demanding, and beloved, but the little prince's departure also leaves her with the cost of his avoidance. This turns the rose from a decorative symbol into a test of [[ResponsibilityAgainstRomanticEscape]].

## Key Claims
- The source treats the rose as strongly tied to Consuelo while keeping the claim source-scoped.
- The rose's mixture of purity, desire, vulnerability, and irritation becomes a way to read conflicted intimacy rather than a simple love emblem.
- Consuelo's waiting makes the little prince's departure morally heavier; the story's sadness is relational, not only metaphysical.
