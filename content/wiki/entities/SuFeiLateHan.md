# 苏飞 / Su Fei (late Han)

## Overview

苏飞 / Su Fei enters the bounded wiki as [[HuangZu|黄祖]]'s former subordinate whose earlier help to [[GanNing|甘宁]] later becomes the basis for a life-backed plea and pardon from [[SunQuan|孙权]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1086-sunquan-beihao-zhuang-rentou-de-muhe-weihe-pianpian-fangguo-sufei-lt7xfjunv42n9532eqog-8k64odf|Hanji 1086]] says Sun Quan originally intends to kill Su Fei after defeating Huang Zu. At the victory banquet, Gan Ning says Su Fei's earlier aid kept him from dying and made later service under Sun Quan possible. Gan Ning accepts liability for any escape, and Sun Quan spares Su Fei.

The source therefore establishes Su Fei mainly through relational consequence: an earlier favor survives regime change because its beneficiary has acquired enough standing to convert gratitude into political intercession.

## Key Characteristics

- Serves in Huang Zu's defeated political-military field.
- Previously helps Gan Ning when Gan Ning is exposed under Huang Zu.
- Becomes an intended execution target after Sun Quan's victory.
- Survives because Gan Ning offers a personal guarantee for his conduct.

- Earlier aid: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1086-sunquan-beihao-zhuang-rentou-de-muhe-weihe-pianpian-fangguo-sufei-lt7xfjunv42n9532eqog-8k64odf|Hanji 1086]] attributes Gan Ning's survival and eventual Sun-side service partly to Su Fei's help.
- Intended punishment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1086-sunquan-beihao-zhuang-rentou-de-muhe-weihe-pianpian-fangguo-sufei-lt7xfjunv42n9532eqog-8k64odf|Hanji 1086]] says Sun Quan prepares a box for Su Fei's head.
- Pardon: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1086-sunquan-beihao-zhuang-rentou-de-muhe-weihe-pianpian-fangguo-sufei-lt7xfjunv42n9532eqog-8k64odf|Hanji 1086]] makes Gan Ning's plea and offered liability the immediate reason Su Fei is spared.

## Qualifications

The bounded source supplies no independent biography, exact offense, later office, or later conduct for Su Fei. The prepared-box detail, dialogue, and claim that he would not flee remain source-scoped.
