# Michael Mattioli

Michael Mattioli is the law professor in [[the-invention-invention]] who explains the historical and legal mechanics of [[PatentPool|patent pools]]. The source uses his commentary to move from [[IsaacMerrittSinger]] and the [[SewingMachineCombination]] to the broader spread, abuse risk, and revival of patent pooling.

His source role is interpretive: Mattioli helps distinguish [[ComplementaryPatents]] from competing substitutes and explains why antitrust policy can either discipline pools or chill them so severely that useful cooperation stops.
