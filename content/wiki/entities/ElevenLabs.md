# ElevenLabs

## Overview
ElevenLabs is represented in this wiki as an AI audio and communication company whose voice products sit between base models, enterprise workflows, media licensing, and identity safeguards.

## Current Profile
The current profile is no longer just a model-routing example. Across the bounded All-In sources, ElevenLabs is used as evidence that AI-native application companies can build value in the interaction layer: text-to-speech, speech-to-text, turn-taking, voice agents, localization, templates, authentication, integrations, and domain workflows. The July 13 source also presents the company as an AI-native organization, with small teams, no product managers, engineers embedded in business functions, and source-scoped claims of rapid revenue growth.

## Key Characteristics
- Builds an AI audio and communication platform rather than a single speech-generation feature.
- Treats enterprise voice-agent adoption as a reliability, knowledge, integration, and orchestration problem.
- Uses a model-agnostic architecture while keeping its own specialization around voice, interaction, and workflow packaging.
- Frames organization design as AI-native, with small teams and technical operators embedded across non-engineering functions.
- Makes voice identity, detection, licensing, and abuse prevention central to the product opportunity.

## Qualifications
Revenue, employee-count, marketplace-payout, and adoption-speed claims are source-scoped to the July 13 All-In interview and are not independently audited in this wiki. The safety profile is also vendor-reported: detection, moderation, and marketplace authentication reduce misuse risk only if they work reliably across impersonation, scams, unauthorized archives, and downstream redistribution.
