## Overview
武汉三镇 / Wuhan Three Towns is referenced in the source as part of Wuhan's recent professional-football continuity and as a caution that continuity alone does not guarantee a fully cultivated city football market.

## Current Profile
Within the wiki, Wuhan Three Towns is a source-scoped club example. The episode uses Wuhan's run from Zall to Three Towns to argue that a city can avoid complete professional-football interruption while still underdeveloping its market, especially when stadium location and professional-stadium quality do not support a stronger matchday habit.

## Key Characteristics
- Wuhan professional-football continuity example.
- Caution that continuity without stronger stadium infrastructure may not produce a full city market.
- Club case used to qualify optimistic expansion arguments.
