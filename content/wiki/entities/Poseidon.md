# Poseidon

## Overview

Poseidon is the sea god invoked by [[Polyphemus]] to turn Odysseus' successful escape into a cursed and delayed homecoming.

## Current Profile

In [[687-the-odyssey-hero-of-the-trojan-horse-part-1-glt2393125423]], Poseidon enters through kinship and curse. After [[Odysseus]] blinds Polyphemus and then reveals his identity, Polyphemus prays to his father Poseidon that Odysseus either never reach home or return late, alone, on another ship, and find trouble there. The later warnings and losses make that curse part of the voyage's governing pressure.

## Key Characteristics

- Poseidon's hostility is triggered through Odysseus' public naming of himself after escape.
- The curse specifies delayed, lonely, troubled return rather than simple death.
- The sea god's role turns geography and weather into moral consequence inside the voyage.
- Poseidon links Polyphemus' local cave episode to the whole arc of Odysseus' suffering.

## Evidence

- Curse origin: [[687-the-odyssey-hero-of-the-trojan-horse-part-1-glt2393125423]] says Polyphemus prays to Poseidon after Odysseus reveals his name, lineage, and home.
- Return conditions: [[687-the-odyssey-hero-of-the-trojan-horse-part-1-glt2393125423]] gives the curse as no return, or late solitary return on another ship with trouble at home.
- Arc pressure: [[687-the-odyssey-hero-of-the-trojan-horse-part-1-glt2393125423]] treats Tiresias' later prophecy and ship destruction as echoes of the curse's terms.

This page covers Poseidon only as he functions in the episode's first-half Odyssey retelling. It does not cover wider Poseidon mythology or cult.
