# Taiwan

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-rahm-emanuel-trumps-foreign-policy-china-europes-decline-immigration-dsa-vs-democrats-42416310]] adds Taiwan as a central security-planning case. [[RahmEmanuel|Rahm Emanuel]] says U.S. policy can keep formal political ambiguity while still being non-ambiguous about opposing a forceful change to the status quo. He identifies three scenarios to prepare for: quarantine, invasion, and seizure of small Taiwan-associated islands near China's coast.

The same source places Taiwan inside [[AlliedEconomicBlocAgainstChina]]. Emanuel includes Taiwan among the partners whose economic and strategic weight should be aggregated with [[Japan]], [[SouthKorea|South Korea]], [[Australia]], the [[EuropeanUnion|EU]], and other U.S.-aligned countries to counter [[China]]'s coercive leverage.

Earlier [[AllIn|All-In]] material mentioned Taiwan through energy vulnerability: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-more-trillion-dollar-ipos-anthropic-3t-zucks-price-war-china-ends-open-source-trump-accounts-42041390]] says Taiwan's reliance on LNG could matter if China blockades it. Other wiki sources use Taiwan as a semiconductor and historical-migration node, including [[JapanGeopoliticalSupplyChainDividend]] around [[TSMC]]'s Kumamoto investment and [[MinnanMaritimeCommercialCulture]] through Taiwan migration and Zheng Chenggong's maritime base.
