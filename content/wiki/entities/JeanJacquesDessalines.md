# Jean-Jacques Dessalines

## Overview

Jean-Jacques Dessalines was the revolutionary leader who led [[Haiti]] to independence and whose government also carried out systematic killing of some remaining white inhabitants.

## Current Profile

The episode places Dessalines at the final transition from emancipated resistance to independent statehood. Napoleon's attempt to restore French control made re-enslavement an immediate danger, and Dessalines framed resistance through freedom from oppression and reprisal for colonial violence.

His profile remains morally divided. Leadership in defeating colonial restoration and founding Haiti is not erased by later violence, but neither does the history of slavery and war excuse the massacre conducted under his government. He therefore embodies the episode's refusal to turn revolutionary emancipation into a morally purified story.

## Key Characteristics

- Leader of the final Haitian independence struggle.
- Opponent of renewed French colonial control and re-enslavement.
- Founder associated with Haiti's transition to independent rule.
- Political actor using liberation and reprisal as justifications.
- Ruler responsible for systematic post-independence killing.
- Figure requiring simultaneous recognition of emancipation and atrocity.
