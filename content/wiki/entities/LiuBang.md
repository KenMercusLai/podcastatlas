# Liu Bang / 刘邦

[[164-guwen-de-liliang-qing-shou-xia-zhe-fen-qiannian-wei-bian-de-shenqing-dongjian-he-yongqi-940236576]] adds Liu Bang's [[HongmenYan|《鸿门宴》]] role. The episode reads him through [[PowerEtiquetteReading]]: his endangered position at the banquet is made visible by seating, humiliation, [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s management of speech and gifts, and [[FanKuai|樊哙]]'s status-recognizing intervention.

Liu Bang / 刘邦 appears in [[126-wangmang-zhisi-shensheng-diguo-de-huixuanbiao-he-huixuanbiao-de-huixuanbiao-786255780]] as the founder whose sacred narratives helped establish [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] legitimacy. The episode points to stories such as the white-snake killing and red-emperor descent as examples of how the Han explained why the Liu house was Heaven's chosen ruler.

The episode's interest in Liu Bang is structural. His myths helped build [[MandateOfHeavenLegitimacy]], but the same logic could later become [[DynasticLegitimacyBackfire]]: if Heaven once selected Liu Bang's line, bad omens and broken succession could suggest Heaven might select someone else.

[[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-jie-shangji-704051550]] adds Liu Bang's sword-and-white-snake story to a broader discussion of ritual objects and divine sanction. In that source, the founding myth helps explain why swords can become political-theological signs inside [[DaoistRitualTechnology]] and [[AuspiciousOmenPolitics]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-45-yi-shi-wei-jian-junwang-daodi-gai-bu-gai-sharen-chengxing-lo3cok7cfwzrxesp-i8miz1fc1db]] uses Liu Bang as the later-history answer to [[Mengzi|孟子 / Mencius]]'s claim that the ruler able to unify is one who does not love killing. The host contrasts Liu Bang with [[XiangYu|项羽]], presenting Liu Bang as the less-killing figure who ultimately founds [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]], so his branch now also supports [[NonKillingUnification|不爱杀人者能一之]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-5-lp6yfcxoysxgcnmg8v1uljfyptof]] adds Liu Bang as a later admirer of [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]]. The episode says that after becoming emperor, Liu Bang repeatedly sacrificed to Xinlingjun when passing [[Daliang|大梁]], and in 195 BCE assigned households to maintain the Wei prince's tomb rites. Here Liu Bang is used less as founder and more as evidence of Xinlingjun's long afterlife as an admired political model.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-103-2-simaqiande-idol-weihe-siyu-jiuse-lvmqg1ojmka9pqagfhgvo4albpjx]] makes that admiration biographical rather than only ritual. The source says young Liu Bang, already aware of Xinlingjun as the hero who defeated Qin through a five-state coalition, went to [[Daliang|大梁]] hoping to join his retainer world. Xinlingjun had just died, so Liu Bang turned to [[ZhangEr|张耳]], a former Xinlingjun retainer.

The episode then reads Liu Bang's later generosity and reputation-building as partly inherited from the Xinlingjun model. Before he had won the empire, Liu Bang was already willing to promise land and status broadly, and the source treats that open-handed attraction of followers as a practical adaptation of [[WarringStatesRetainerPatronage|战国养士]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-106-jingren-faxian-xinlingjun-de-feizhengchang-siwang-lsj3kd2tb4tqtuvhwvsdwkh-h5el]] repeats the afterlife branch with a more ritual emphasis. The episode says Liu Bang heard of [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]]'s worth in youth, sacrificed to him whenever he passed [[Daliang|大梁]] after becoming emperor, and in 195 BCE, after suppressing Ying Bu, assigned five households to guard and perform seasonal rites at Xinlingjun's tomb.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-117-1-papa-dalian-lishishang-shuangwen-de-kaishan-bizu-lr0h-kfeguvatattnj-mz8fgvnc]] adds Liu Bang as the actual father behind [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负]]'s prediction about [[BaoJi|薄姬]]. [[WeiBao|魏豹]] hears that Bao Ji will bear a future royal or imperial son and reads the prediction through himself, but the episode's payoff is that Bao Ji later bears [[LiuHeng|刘恒]] to Liu Bang. Liu Bang's role here is less active biography than a pivot from fallen Wei hopes to [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] succession.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-5-wo-you-zhengju-zanmen-buneng-shuo-qinershi-sha-ye-bushi-ljuome-xea6sjv4hhzmrr0b0-1hg|Qinji 128-5]] adds Liu Bang only as a transition signal at the end of the episode. After the source describes [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]]'s western setback and [[AntiQinCoalitionFragmentation|反秦阵营诸侯化分裂]], it says the real later protagonists are [[XiangYu|项羽]] and Liu Bang, then introduces Liu Bang as a forty-eight-year-old Pei County man who starts without grand background and with only a small follower group. The source does not yet narrate his uprising in detail.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-6-dingji-tianshilun-touziren-lvgong-lhqqja27zxdiypgf6wuv1abgkw3j|Qinji 128-6]] develops that transition into Liu Bang's early social and performative profile. The source presents him as Liu Ji, son of [[LiuTaigong|刘太公]], from [[PeiCounty|沛县]], with a mixed reputation for drink, women, non-farming idleness, generosity, boldness, and youxia-style sociability. His Sishui亭长 period becomes [[GrassrootsOfficialNetwork|基层官吏网络政治资本]] because low-level county officials and companions later become the first Han founding-merit circle.

The same source makes [[LuGongLiuBangFatherInLaw|吕公]]'s banquet the first concrete scene of Liu Bang's status-making ability. Liu Bang arrives without invitation or money but announces "贺钱一万" and sits like an honored guest; [[XiaoHe|萧何]] tries to contain the boast, while Lu Gong reads the bearing through [[PhysiognomicInvestmentJudgment|看相式政治投资]] and offers [[LuZhi|吕雉]] in marriage. The episode therefore turns Liu Bang's early shamelessness into [[CharismaticPoliticalPerformance|造势型政治表演]] rather than a mere flaw.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-7-liubang-he-xiangyu-youhe-butong-llwcqatndck4zita2piufwhjpxp1|Qinji 128-7]] turns that social-capital branch into action. While escorting Pei County laborers toward [[Lishan|骊山]], Liu Bang faces mass flight and releases the remaining men, becoming a fugitive around [[MangdangMountain|芒砀山]]. [[FanKuai|樊哙]] and others follow him out of gratitude, and the white-snake/red-emperor story begins to give his outlaw group a sacred-reputation layer under [[MandateOfHeavenLegitimacy|天命合法性]] and [[AuspiciousOmenPolitics|祥瑞政治]].

The same source then makes Liu Bang's return to [[PeiCounty|沛县]] a case of [[PeiCountyUprisingMobilization|沛县起义动员]]. [[XiaoHe|萧何]] and [[CaoShen|曹参]] first advise the magistrate to call Liu Bang's men back after the [[DazexiangUprising|大泽乡起义]], then defect when the magistrate reverses and targets them. Liu Bang's letter into the city helps local elders kill the magistrate, open the gates, name him Pei Gong, and gather more than three thousand troops.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-1-jiemi-wuguang-bei-miekou-de-lishi-zhenxiang-lk1bkhqkwf9upbbj08pjkn8cstyp|Qinji 129-1]] gives Liu Bang a parallel local-success line while Zhang Chu's central armies are breaking. Qin's Sishui commandery supervisor Ping besieges Liu Bang, but Liu Bang counterattacks successfully; [[XiahouYing|夏侯婴]] and [[XiaoHe|萧何]] then persuade Ping to surrender Huling. Liu Bang leaves [[YongChi|雍齿]] to hold [[FengCounty|丰邑 / 丰县]], moves north toward Xue County, and his left sima [[CaoWushang|曹无伤]] captures and kills the Sishui commandery governor Zhuang.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-3-chensheng-zhisi-weishenme-dele-minxin-hanle-renxin-lnx4zs-vsi3zeoixnqskkpexmr3|Qinji 129-3]] adds Liu Bang as a later keeper of [[ChenSheng|陈胜]]'s memory rather than as the episode's main battlefield actor. After [[LuChenQinRebel|吕臣]] avenges and reburies Chen Sheng, the source says Liu Bang, after gaining the empire, assigns thirty households to guard Chen Sheng's tomb and maintain hereditary sacrifices. The episode uses this to separate Chen Sheng's failed personal rule from his continuing symbolic status as the initiator of the anti-Qin wave.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-4-jiuquangui-zhangliang-ruhe-zai-liubang-shouxia-anshen-lp6qwg3ma5hdy15g4box7pe23jav|Qinji 129-4]] gives Liu Bang both a setback and a new strategic asset. [[YongChi|雍齿]], whom Liu Bang had left at [[FengCounty|丰邑 / 丰县]], defects to [[ZhouShiRebel|周市]]'s Wei-side force because he looks down on Liu Bang, and Liu Bang fails to recover Feng immediately. The same episode then has Liu Bang meet [[ZhangLiang|张良]] while both are moving near the [[JingJu|景居 / 景驹]] branch; Zhang Liang joins Liu Bang because Liu Bang can understand and adopt his [[TaigongBingfa|《太公兵法》]]-based counsel, making this a source case for [[StrategicRecognitionAlignment|谋略知己式归附]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-1-guailaotou-yiqiao-san-shua-zhangliang-chuanshou-shenshu-lqwjo13zzfgtndpajzpuvrb2h4l2|Qinji 130-1]] continues that unsettled phase. Liu Bang and Zhang Liang formally join [[JingJu|景居 / 景驹]] at Liu County and hope to get help against Feng, but [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]]'s Qin pressure forces Liu Bang into immediate fighting. He is slightly checked near Xiao County, then takes Dang County, absorbs nearly six thousand troops, captures Xiayi, and returns against Feng without success. The episode therefore keeps Liu Bang's rise uneven: strategic talent is arriving, but local recovery and command consolidation are still incomplete.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-2-lishi-jilu-shouci-tucheng-lm4kuzdpnqn-hicyuvvb4p2wdudm|Qinji 130-2]] gives Liu Bang the missing force behind that recovery. He goes to [[XiangLiang|项梁]] at Xue with a little over one hundred cavalry, receives five thousand men and ten fifth-rank commanders, and finally retakes [[FengCounty|丰邑 / 丰县]] on the fourth attempt. [[YongChi|雍齿]] flees to Wei, so the episode marks Liu Bang's shift from repeated local failure to recovery through temporary attachment to the Xiang camp.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-132-5wan-wanbao-40wan-xiangyu-cizhan-fengshen-lk87nb3fskg5zh6fcgptypixhzuw|Qinji 132]] moves Liu Bang from Xiang Liang attachment into an officially assigned westward route. After [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] kills Xiang Liang at [[Dingtao|定陶]], Liu Bang joins [[XiangYu|项羽]] and [[LuChenQinRebel|吕臣]] in moving [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]] to [[Xuzhou|彭城]]. Mi Xin then makes Liu Bang Dang commandery governor and Wu'an Hou.

Under [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]], Liu Bang is selected to lead the smaller westward campaign toward [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]]. The older Chu generals prefer him over Xiang Yu because they read Liu Bang as broader and more generous, while Xiang Yu's [[XiangchengQin|襄城]] massacre makes him a bad fit for pacifying Qin's long-burdened population. The episode therefore turns Liu Bang's less-killing profile from later historical comparison into immediate coalition strategy.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-134-ni-buzhidao-de-pofu-chenzhou-banben-ltnry1fva2hzplw4ft8nrfpoiolw|Qinji 134]] keeps Liu Bang on that separate line while the main narrative follows [[XiangYu|项羽]] at [[JuluBattle|巨鹿之战]]. In the episode's brief update, Liu Bang reaches Li County, defeats a Qin force, gathers or joins forces with Huang Xin and Wu Man, and defeats Qin again. This preserves the split created in Qinji 132: Liu Bang's westward route advances in parallel while Xiang Yu wins coalition command at Julu.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-135-mingyun-tai-shenqi-cong-yufu-zuodao-wang-lrh1lhuxxikx4m6prpi6qm0jcafb|Qinji 135]] continues Liu Bang's parallel line after Julu. He moves north against Changyi and meets [[PengYue|彭越]], a former fisher and bandit leader whose disciplined local force now follows him. The episode makes Peng Yue's attachment a new manpower and organization gain for Liu Bang, not just a colorful biography.

The same source also introduces [[LiShiqi|郦食其]] as a pending persuasion case. Li Shiqi wants to approach Liu Bang because he hears that Liu Bang, despite arrogance, can listen to large strategy. A cavalryman warns that Liu Bang dislikes scholars, citing the insulting Confucian-cap story and Liu Bang's habit of cursing, but Li Shiqi insists on being introduced. The source ends before the meeting, so Liu Bang's recognition of Li Shiqi remains unresolved here.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-136-meinv-xijiao-liubang-ye-hao-zhe-kouer-lplgzwzahepwx9p7krfc4x0cnz4o|Qinji 136]] completes that recognition scene. Liu Bang receives Li Shiqi while two women wash his feet, is rebuked for claiming to oppose Qin's "无道" while behaving without礼, then stops, apologizes, and seats Li Shiqi properly. The episode uses the scene to show Liu Bang's mix of coarseness and correctability: he may begin arrogantly, but he can change posture when an argument names political legitimacy and practical value.

Li Shiqi's advice leads Liu Bang to [[Chenliu|陈留]], where grain, weapons, and surrendered troops make the westward campaign materially stronger. Liu Bang later fails to take [[Kaifeng|开封]] quickly, defeats [[YangXiongQinGeneral|杨熊]], massacres [[YingchuanCommandery|颍川郡]], and blocks [[SimaAng|司马卬]] from moving toward [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]]. The Yingchuan massacre is important because it keeps Liu Bang's [[NonKillingUnification|不爱杀人者能一之]] profile comparative, not innocent.

The same episode then gives Liu Bang a cleaner pacification turn at [[WanCounty|宛县]]. After [[ZhangLiang|张良]] warns that bypassing Wan would leave a rear threat, [[ChenHuiNanyang|陈恢]] persuades Liu Bang to accept [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]]'s surrender, reward the governor, and continue west. The episode reads this as a shift from hating Qin people to treating them as future subjects, making Wan a major source case for [[PoliticalSurrenderCascade|招降示范级联]] and [[OccupationPacificationStrategy|占领区怀柔治理]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-137-shendu-jiedu-zhiluweima-zhong-de-quanli-boyi-lgkjmddvrcelw2ltdwt92e4poguj|Qinji 137]] uses Liu Bang mainly as the military fact that breaks Zhao Gao's information screen. By August in the source's chronology, Liu Bang has captured [[Wuguan|武关]] with tens of thousands of troops, putting pressure on a gateway into Qin's core. The episode does not dwell on Liu Bang's tactics here; it treats Wuguan as the moment when the [[CrisisInformationBlockade|危机信息封锁]] around Hu Hai becomes untenable.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-135-fanwaipian-1-zhengshi-jizai-kan-nvxiangshi-ruhe-gaiming-ls8lfj4pq-ncsk7x3er7nlb0ltkk|Qinji 135 番外篇1]] mentions Liu Bang at the cutoff point of the available transcript. After refusing [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]]'s summons, [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负]] sees Liu Bang, brings him home, and tells her father to give up the county seal.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-135-fanwaipian-2-nvxiangshi-yin-kanming-nengli-bei-liubang-fenghou-lgu1fr1zrzmc0o72wtbpxmajb7s|Qinji 135 番外篇2]] completes that recognition scene. In the episode's account, Xu Fu's face-reading turns into immediate local transfer: her father hands over Wen County's seal and Liu Bang gains the county nearly bloodlessly. The sequel also says Liu Bang later remembers and rewards Xu Fu after founding Han, making her case a higher-stakes parallel to [[LuGongLiuBangFatherInLaw|吕公]]'s earlier [[PhysiognomicInvestmentJudgment|看相式政治投资]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-138-wudu-qiannian-de-lishi-zhenxiang-zhaogao-bushi-taijian-2-lvtievjrclb1lxxxxr6k-q7kryto|Qinji 138 part 2]] carries Liu Bang from the [[Wuguan|武关]] pressure point into Qin's final defensive line. After [[ZiyingQin|子婴]] sends troops to [[Yaoguan|峣关]], [[ZhangLiang|张良]] advises Liu Bang to use flags, persuasion through [[LiShiqi|郦食其]] and [[LuJia|陆贾]], and then attack while Qin is relaxed rather than accept a premature peace. Liu Bang bypasses Yaoguan, crosses mountain terrain, and defeats Qin forces south and north of [[LantianLateQin|蓝田]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-139-ta-zaici-yingxiang-hanchao-lp1zujuq0o3ohxc79xqi1phpb5zo|Hanji 139]] moves Liu Bang from breaking Qin's last defense to occupying Qin's center. After [[ZiyingQin|子婴]] surrenders, Liu Bang refuses to kill him, pardons old Qin officials, keeps offices functioning, and withdraws from the Qin palace to [[Bashang|霸上]] after [[FanKuai|樊哙]] and [[ZhangLiang|张良]] warn against immediate indulgence. At Bashang, [[XiaoHe|萧何]] helps organize [[YuefaSanzhang|约法三章]], envoys carry the policy through Guanzhong, and Liu Bang refuses local gifts, turning his less-killing comparative profile into a concrete [[OccupationPacificationStrategy|occupation pacification]] case.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-140-xiangyu-yu-liubang-shi-zhen-youqing-haishi-xianghu-liyong-1-lmmhoq2wo3bs6b1johpc0ixuf662|Hanji 140 part 1]] turns that success into danger. Liu Bang has reached [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] first under the logic of the [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]], and Guanzhong people hope he can become their ruler after [[YuefaSanzhang|约法三章]]. When he then seals the entry routes against the arriving coalition, [[XiangYu|项羽]] reads the move through [[CaoWushang|曹无伤]]'s leak as a bid to rule Guanzhong, making Liu Bang's restraint and popularity the trigger for the [[HongmenYan|鸿门宴]] crisis.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-140-xiangyu-yu-liubang-shi-zhen-youqing-haishi-xianghu-liyong-2-lvdgirxmwxcckh2loh-yk2zzer2e|Hanji 140 part 2]] shows Liu Bang's emergency adaptation after [[ZhangLiang|张良]] brings [[XiangBo|项伯]]'s warning to [[Bashang|霸上]]. Liu Bang is frightened because he cannot withstand Xiang Yu militarily, but he rapidly shifts into performance: he treats Xiang Bo as an elder brother, offers wine and a marriage tie, and recasts his own pass closure as anti-bandit security while presenting his sealed archives and warehouses as loyal waiting for Xiang Yu.

The source treats this as morally mixed but politically effective. Liu Bang's "不敢有二心" language is not recorded as pure sincerity; it is a crisis move that fits Xiang Bo's concern for personal义, status, and old Chu-camp comradeship. Liu Bang's advantage here is the ability to read a mediator's emotional code and turn military inferiority into a face-to-face explanation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-141-huanyuan-lishi-zhong-de-hongmenyan-lpmb-bcoocngtqjvtnpj0elm-siy|Hanji 141]] moves Liu Bang from that mediated opportunity into [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s tent at [[HongmenYan|鸿门宴]]. He enters with [[ZhangLiang|张良]] while [[FanKuai|樊哙]], [[XiahouYing|夏侯婴]], [[JiXin|纪信]], [[JinQiang|金强]], and cavalry remain outside, then apologizes by stressing shared anti-Qin work and blaming "small people" for the split. The episode reads this as high-grade plausible performance: Liu Bang hides ambition in ordinary, reasonable speech while exploiting Xiang Yu's wish to settle emotionally without naming the conflict as irreconcilable.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-142-zhechang-juhui-xiangyu-shudiao-le-yiqie-ln7dv2jxrk6lt1c4szylsrb41k3r|Hanji 142]] completes Liu Bang's Hongmen survival as a deliberate surrender-and-exit sequence. [[FanKuai|樊哙]] breaks the immediate assassination pressure, but the episode argues that Xiang Yu lets Liu Bang live because Liu Bang has effectively accepted Xiang Yu's terms: yielding [[Xianyang|咸阳]], staying at [[Bashang|霸上]], and submitting to Xiang Yu's command for the moment. Liu Bang then leaves under a bathroom pretext, returns by small roads with Fan Kuai, [[XiahouYing|夏侯婴]], and [[JiXin|纪信]], and kills [[CaoWushang|曹无伤]] after reaching safety, completing the informer branch that Xiang Yu exposed.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-143-xiangyu-shi-ruhe-feijin-xinji-nongsi-chuhuaiwang-mixin-lstfzfvbd2h9i39f7y5dxrbxxwv2|Hanji 143]] gives the formal cost of that short-term survival. Liu Bang is made Han king, but not king of open [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] under the [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]] as originally expected. [[XiangYu|项羽]] and [[FanZeng|范增]] assign him [[BaShu|巴蜀]], [[Hanzhong|汉中]], and a capital at [[Nanzheng|南郑]], while [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]], [[SimaXin|司马欣]], and [[DongYiQin|董翳]] form [[ThreeQinContainment|三秦压制]] between him and the Qin heartland. The page therefore now treats Liu Bang's Han kingship as both a title and a strategic confinement.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-144-xiangyu-jituan-fumie-de-yuanyin-1-lr0ft8-jupxgvutn6c89mfs8r4wb|Hanji 144]] follows Liu Bang's response to that confinement. He is said to rage and order preparations to attack Xiang Yu, with [[ZhouBo|周勃]], [[GuanYing|灌婴]], and [[FanKuai|樊哙]] supporting the impulse. [[XiaoHe|萧何]] then argues that direct battle would mean repeated defeat, and reframes Hanzhong and Ba-Shu as a place to govern people, gather resources, recruit talent, and wait for a return toward Guanzhong. The host further suggests that Liu Bang's anger and Xiao He's restraint may be a staged "双簧" to let the generals accept [[StrategicRetreatBaseBuilding|退让式根据地经营]] without reading it as cowardice.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-144-xiangyu-jituan-fumie-de-yuanyin-2-lut3jiatchla3pfxpkznsrs7wkzu|Hanji 144 part 2]] gives that retreat its first practical mechanics. [[ZhangLiang|张良]] sends Liu Bang's gifts through [[XiangBo|项伯]] to gain more room around Hanzhong, then advises [[BurningGalleryRoads|burning the gallery roads]] so Xiang Yu will believe Liu Bang has no near-term return plan. Liu Bang enters Hanzhong with his assigned troops plus disappointed followers from the wider coalition, including [[HanXin|韩信]], while eastern revolts by [[TianRong|田荣]], [[PengYue|彭越]], and [[ChenYu|陈馀]] begin to pull Xiang Yu away from the western front.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-146-qiannian-jingdian-xiaohe-yuexia-zhui-hanxin-lq0oc3vqr-tsr0r-iwztifxeezl|Hanji 146]] turns Liu Bang's Hanzhong base from a strategic idea into a personnel test. Liu Bang initially fails to see [[HanXin|韩信]] as more than an unusual lower officer: [[XiahouYing|夏侯婴]]'s recommendation earns Han Xin a grain-supply commandant role, but not decisive authority. Only after [[XiaoHe|萧何]] chases Han Xin back and frames him as necessary for contesting the empire does Liu Bang agree to appoint him as chief commander.

The source makes Liu Bang's ambition the hinge. Xiao He says Han Xin is unnecessary if Liu Bang only wants to remain king in Hanzhong, but indispensable if Liu Bang means to return east and fight for all under Heaven. Liu Bang's acceptance of full public ceremony therefore becomes more than personnel administration: it is a visible commitment to [[RitualizedCommandAppointment|仪式化拜将留才]] and to the larger strategy behind [[StrategicRetreatBaseBuilding|退让式根据地经营]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-147-pimei-zhugeliang-longzhongdui-de-hanzhongdui-lqucctzhipgtcvtjlixvj5qbdeai|Hanji 147]] gives Liu Bang the first strategic return on that commitment. In Han Xin's audience after the appointment, Liu Bang first admits that he is weaker than [[XiangYu|项羽]] in bravery, courtesy, and present strength; he then hears why Xiang Yu's apparent strengths have become defects and why [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] remains politically open. Liu Bang's decision to follow Han Xin's deployment makes [[HanzhongDui|汉中对]] the point where his Hanzhong ambition becomes an actionable plan to attack the [[ThreeQinContainment|Three Qin]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-148-1-hanxin-yupan-le-zhanghan-de-yupan-lodhpuxpossjno87gorjkfrtitbe|Hanji 148-1]] gives Liu Bang the operational payoff of accepting Han Xin's command. His army is reorganized through [[HanInheritsQinSystem|汉承秦制式军法]], exits the confined Hanzhong position through a coordinated route plan, defeats [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] around [[ChenCang|陈仓]] and Haozhi, and then sees [[SimaXin|司马欣]] plus [[DongYiQin|董翳]] surrender. Liu Bang's Hanzhong base is therefore no longer only preserved ambition; it becomes recovered access to the old Qin heartland.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-148-2-peng-le-wangling-muqin-xiangyu-sharen-zhuxin-ljyv8bow4rj1l5ths3znx2evgvtm|Hanji 148-2]] shows Liu Bang using that recovered position cautiously. He sends people to recover [[LiuTaigong|刘太公]], [[LuZhi|吕雉]], and his son, and to contact [[WangLingHanMinister|王陵]] around [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]]. At the same time, he publicly frames the Guanzhong campaign as restoring the [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]] rather than declaring direct war on [[XiangYu|项羽]], buying time while Xiang Yu is distracted by [[TianRong|田荣]], [[PengYue|彭越]], Yan unrest, and [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]]'s relocation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-149-xichu-bawang-xiangyu-weihe-shijun-1-lsqxs2hzu0ngnrpmzsklkqhtyuvw|Hanji 149]] gives Liu Bang the public moral charge that Hanji 148-2 had only prepared. After [[XiangYu|项羽]] kills Yi Emperor [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]], Liu Bang can call lords against Xiang Yu under the charge of murdering the nominal senior ruler. The same episode brings [[ZhangEr|张耳]] into Liu Bang's camp after [[GanGong|甘公]] advises him that Liu Bang has the future, and it has [[ZhangLiang|张良]] return after [[HanCheng|韩成]]'s death.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-149-xichu-bawang-xiangyu-weihe-shijun-2-lpohbb3prsgvokpjipfd7i1ntiwn|Hanji 149 part 2]] turns that mobilization into territorial absorption. Liu Bang receives [[ShenYang|申阳]]'s surrender and converts the Henan kingdom into [[HenanCommanderyChuHan|河南郡]], replaces [[ZhengChangHanKing|郑昌]] with [[HanWangXin|韩王信]] after Zheng Chang surrenders, moves the capital to [[LiyangQinHan|栎阳]], and takes [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]], [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]], and [[HeneiCommandery|河内郡]]. The source therefore shows Liu Bang moving from legal-political accusation into commandery-building and alliance capture.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-150-chenping-weihe-bei-cheng-zhiwei-chenggong-zhengzhijia-yangban-2-lkbo2kwkn6aexyyygv-m84fnqx2h|Hanji 150 part 2]] adds Liu Bang's internal personnel logic after the territorial branch. At [[Xiuwu|修武]], [[WeiWuzhi|魏无知]] introduces [[ChenPing|陈平]], and Liu Bang quickly makes him a duwei/canche and later護軍-style figure despite veteran complaints. The episode reads this as both [[VeteranMeritCliqueSupervision|功臣旧将监督]] over old generals and [[OutsiderTalentSignaling|厚待外来人才信号]] after Liu Bang has already benefited from empowering [[HanXin|韩信]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-150-chenping-weihe-bei-cheng-zhiwei-chenggong-zhengzhijia-yangban-3-lhfbm3twyxhyvokphf8hc9ssblqj|Hanji 150 part 3]] sharpens that personnel logic into bounded trust. Liu Bang listens to Chen Ping's blunt defense against slander, then gives him large funds for covert inducement work without requiring accounts. The source reads this as practical [[CovertPoliticalTechnology|阴谋式政治技术]] use: Liu Bang can exploit Chen Ping's hidden skills while still judging through [[SecondInCommandRoleFit|二把手角色适配]] that Chen Ping is not suited to independent sole responsibility.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-150-chenping-weihe-bei-cheng-zhiwei-chenggong-zhengzhijia-yangban-4-lhhprrkjcuzijgndi2uuazaalern|Hanji 150 part 4]] adds two Liu Bang-side danger cases around Chen Ping. At the [[BaidengEncirclement|白登之围]], Liu Bang is the trapped ruler whose escape depends on Chen Ping's indirect channel toward [[MaoDunChanyu|冒顿单于]]'s household. Later, Liu Bang's order to kill [[FanKuai|樊哙]] creates a succession-sensitive dilemma that Chen Ping and [[ZhouBo|周勃]] defuse through [[TacticalDelayRiskTransfer|延时转责式政治化解]] rather than immediate execution.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-151-huanyuan-pengcheng-zhizhan-xiangyu-zai-fengshen-1-lspx-bqe5kczq6bmn6thlerfbrmu|Hanji 151 part 1]] turns Liu Bang's anti-Xiang charge into the opening of the [[PengchengBattle|彭城之战]] branch. At Xincheng, [[DongGongXincheng|董公]] gives him the public sequence for using [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]]'s murder: mourn the Yi Emperor, put the army into grief, and call the lords to punish [[XiangYu|项羽]] as a regicidal rebel. Liu Bang then secures [[ChenYu|陈馀]]'s participation by sending a substitute head instead of killing [[ZhangEr|张耳]], takes advantage of Xiang Yu being tied down by [[TianHeng|田横]] in Qi, captures [[Xuzhou|彭城]], and immediately falls into treasure, women, wine, and celebration. The source therefore adds both Liu Bang's strongest mobilizing skill and a dangerous [[VictoryOverreachFailure|victory-overreach]] moment before Xiang Yu's counterattack is narrated.

## Key Claims
- Liu Bang's origin stories helped sacralize Han rule.
- Founding mythology became a later vulnerability when legitimacy was read through signs.
- The source uses Liu Bang as the starting point for understanding why Wang Mang's claim became thinkable.
- Episode 87 adds his white-snake story to the weapon-symbol branch of omen politics.
- Zhouji 96 part 5 adds his sacrifices to Xinlingjun as a later reception signal for Warring States patronage reputation.
- Qinji 103-2 adds a youthful attempt to join Xinlingjun and a Zhang Er connection, making Liu Bang's admiration part of his pre-imperial network formation.
- Qinji 106 reinforces the tomb-rite branch and supplies the five-household arrangement as a sign of Xinlingjun's continued prestige in Liu Bang's political memory.
- Qinji 117-1 adds Bao Ji and Liu Heng as a later Han payoff to Wei Bao's misread fate prediction.
- Qinji 128-5 adds Liu Bang as the closing transition toward the next Chu-Han line, not yet as a developed actor in this episode.
- Qinji 128-6 develops Liu Bang as Liu Ji of Pei County: a low-status but socially connected亭长 whose local official network, youxia habits, Lu Gong marriage, and "贺钱一万" performance become early political capital.
- Qinji 128-7 develops Liu Bang's first uprising route: corvee escort failure, release of the laborers, Mangdang Mountain exile, the white-snake legitimacy rumor, return through Xiao He and Cao Shen, and Pei County's killing of the magistrate to make him Pei Gong.
- Qinji 129-1 adds Liu Bang's first post-Pei County battlefield expansion: Huling surrender through Xiahou Ying and Xiao He, then the Xue County branch where Cao Wushang kills the Sishui commandery governor.
- Qinji 129-3 adds Liu Bang's later Chen Sheng tomb-rite order as evidence that Chen Sheng's symbolic initiating role survived his personal collapse.
- Qinji 129-4 adds Yong Chi's Feng defection as an early setback and Zhang Liang's decision to join Liu Bang as a strategic-recognition turning point.
- Qinji 130-1 adds Liu Bang's post-Jing-Ju fighting sequence: joining Jing Ju with Zhang Liang, responding to Zhang Han's pressure, absorbing Dang County troops, taking Xiayi, and failing again to retake Feng.
- Qinji 130-2 adds Liu Bang's successful fourth attempt at Feng after Xiang Liang grants him five thousand troops and ten fifth-rank commanders.
- Qinji 132 adds Liu Bang's official westward assignment under the Huaiwang agreement after Xiang Liang's death, with his more generous profile used to justify sending him toward Guanzhong instead of Xiang Yu.
- Qinji 134 adds Liu Bang's brief parallel victories around Li County while Xiang Yu's Julu branch becomes the main battlefield.
- Qinji 135 adds Peng Yue's attachment during the Changyi campaign and introduces Li Shiqi as a low-status persuader trying to reach Liu Bang despite Liu Bang's anti-scholar reputation.
- Qinji 136 completes Li Shiqi's recognition scene, turns Chenliu into Liu Bang's preparation base, adds the Yingchuan massacre as a terror-signaling complication, and makes Wan/Nanyang surrender the turn toward pacifying Qin people as future subjects.
- Qinji 137 adds Liu Bang's capture of Wuguan as the pressure point that exposes Zhao Gao's hidden war crisis.
- Qinji 138 part 2 adds Liu Bang's Yaoguan-Lantian breakthrough: persuasion creates an opening, but final progress comes from bypassing the pass and attacking while Qin is lax.
- Hanji 139 adds Liu Bang's first post-Qin occupation policy: sparing Ziying, preserving Qin administration, accepting Xiao He's archive capture, leaving palace luxury, announcing Yuefa Sanzhang at Bashang, and refusing local gifts to secure Guanzhong people-support.
- Hanji 140 part 1 adds the immediate backlash to that policy: Liu Bang's first-entry Guanzhong advantage, pass closure, and perceived kingship ambition provoke Xiang Yu's decision to attack before Hongmen.
- Hanji 140 part 2 adds Liu Bang's crisis de-escalation through Xiang Bo: ritual deference, claimed loyalty, marriage-alliance language, and a security explanation convert an unwinnable attack into a meeting with Xiang Yu.
- Hanji 141 adds Liu Bang's first banquet-room performance: he enters under a humiliating seat order, keeps trusted retainers outside, apologizes in a way Xiang Yu can accept, and survives the first phase of Fan Zeng's assassination pressure through Xiang Bo's protection.
- Hanji 142 adds that Liu Bang's survival depends on accepting short-term subordination, using Fan Kuai and Zhang Liang to manage danger and timing, escaping by small roads to Bashang, and then executing Cao Wushang once Xiang Yu has exposed the informant.
- Hanji 144 adds Liu Bang's post-settlement morale management: anger over Hanzhong may be partly a performance, while Xiao He's counsel turns forced retreat into base-building.
- Hanji 144 part 2 adds Liu Bang's post-retreat signaling: Zhang Liang and Xiang Bo secure practical room, burning the gallery roads protects and deceives, and disappointed followers including Han Xin make Hanzhong a talent-absorption field.
- Hanji 146 adds Liu Bang's first major Hanzhong talent-retention test: Xiahou Ying notices Han Xin, but Xiao He's pursuit and argument push Liu Bang to make a public command appointment.
- Qinji 135 番外篇1 adds the initial Xu Fu recognition scene, and 番外篇2 completes it by having Wen County's seal handed over to Liu Bang and later linking Liu Bang's imperial reward to Xu Fu's early support.
- Hanji 148-2 adds Liu Bang's first post-Guanzhong consolidation moves: family recovery, contact with Wang Ling, and public use of the Huaiwang promise as a limited explanation before open conflict with Xiang Yu.
- Hanji 149 adds Liu Bang's use of Mi Xin's killing as an anti-Xiang mobilization charge, while Zhang Er and Zhang Liang both move toward him after Xiang Yu's settlement and coercive revisions alienate them.
- Hanji 149 part 2 adds Liu Bang's conversion of surrendered or conquered settlement territories into commanderies while using Han Wang Xin to absorb old Han legitimacy.
- Hanji 150 part 2 adds Liu Bang's rapid use of Chen Ping as an outsider supervisor and recruitment signal, showing that talent policy also manages old founding followers.
- Hanji 150 part 3 adds Liu Bang's bounded trust in Chen Ping: he can fund and use a morally ambiguous covert operator while still recognizing that such talent needs role limits.
- Hanji 150 part 4 adds Liu Bang as both the ruler rescued at Baideng through Chen Ping's indirect leverage and the order-giver whose Fan Kuai command becomes dangerous once succession timing changes.
- Hanji 151 part 1 adds Liu Bang's Pengcheng setup: public mourning for Yi Emperor Mi Xin, coalition-building through a moralized anti-Xiang charge, deception to satisfy Chen Yu without killing Zhang Er, capture of Pengcheng, and immediate victory indulgence before Xiang Yu's return.
