# 中欧瑞博 / Zhongou Ruibo

中欧瑞博 is the investment firm context in [[a-gu-de-chun-xia-qiu-dong-zhongshu-zhongliang-zhongcai-ljzicmfbl9guxoqmcdd8ikdn6jbx]], where [[WuWeizhi|吴伟志]] explains its internal A-share investing and research process. The source presents the firm less through product marketing than through its operating system: investment-committee discussion, hedging, quant support, AI-assisted research, sector teams, and research-index construction.

The firm's process is built around matching market state and company type to portfolio action. [[AShareMarketSeasons|A 股四季框架]] informs total exposure and buying speed, [[ASharePlantingCompanyTaxonomy|种树、种粮、种菜]] separates return profiles, and [[MAPERInvestmentResearchFramework|MAPER]] organizes company research for growth and value candidates.

The episode also describes 中欧瑞博 as using both discretionary and systematic tools. Quant is treated as engineering machinery for breadth, speed, product lines, and support; AI is encouraged as a research assistant but bounded by analyst verification; and [[ResearchIndexPortfolioConstruction|research indexes]] are used to connect analyst work with portfolio construction.
