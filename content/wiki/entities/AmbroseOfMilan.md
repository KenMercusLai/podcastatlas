# Ambrose of Milan

## Overview

Ambrose of Milan was a late Roman Christian bishop represented in the source through his opposition to restoring the Altar of Victory.

## Current Profile

In [[46-culture-wars-glt4237848862]], Ambrose supplies the moral-purification side of a proto-culture-war dispute. Where [[Symmachus]] argues for toleration of inherited civic religion, Ambrose treats pagan sacrifice as demonic and therefore not open to compromise. The episode uses the clash to illuminate, without equating it with, modern arguments over whether morally tainted public symbols may remain.

## Key Characteristics

- Late Roman Christian bishop and public religious authority.
- Opponent of restoring the Altar of Victory.
- Represents refusal to compromise with practices defined as morally intolerable.

## Evidence

- Altar dispute: [[46-culture-wars-glt4237848862]] contrasts his anti-sacrifice position with Symmachus's toleration appeal.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one conversational episode and its culture-war analogy. It does not reconstruct Ambrose's wider theology, episcopal career, imperial relationships, writings, or the full legal and religious history of the altar dispute.
