## Overview
广东陈兴聚力 / Guangdong Chenxing Juli is the lower-tier Guangdong football team used by the source to show how [[GuangzhouFC]] and [[EvergrandeFootballSchool]] identities can continue outside the original club. The episode describes a Qingyuan derby where former Evergrande-school players and Guangzhou-team chants made a low-level match carry legacy significance.

## Current Profile
The team's current profile is as a bridge rather than an established successor. It matters because academy graduates need a professional outlet and because some supporters treat it as closer to the old Guangzhou football lineage than a separately created top-flight contender. The source says the school wants at least a China League Two-level professional team to reassure students and parents about future pathways.

## Key Characteristics
- Lower-tier team connected to former Evergrande-school player flows.
- Possible professional outlet for [[EvergrandeFootballSchool]] students and graduates.
- Emotional continuity carrier for some former [[GuangzhouFC]] supporters.
- Currently constrained by registration and venue realities in the source's account.
- Future-path uncertainty case, with relocation or merger possibilities explicitly treated as speculative.

- Match context: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] says Pan Weili watched a Qingyuan Guangdong derby involving Chenxing Juli and another youth-heavy side.
- Player continuity: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] says both teams had many players from Evergrande's football school, making the match a "senior-junior" derby.
- Supporter identity: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] says Chenxing Juli supporters continued chanting "广州队."
- Professional outlet: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] says the school strongly wants a professional team and treats China League Two as the bottom-line platform.

## Qualifications
The source explicitly marks possible relocation to Guangzhou or merger with Guangdong Mingtú as hearsay. Those possibilities should not be treated as settled plans.
