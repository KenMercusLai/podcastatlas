# Regal Cinemas

Regal Cinemas appears in [[zhongguo-xiaofeizhe-daidong-lafu-laolun-zengzhang-donghang-youhua-jipiao-tuigaiqian-zhengce-1005631805]] as one of the U.S. cinema chains approached by [[Paramount]] in a reported three-year release commitment. The source pairs Regal with [[AMCTheatres|AMC]] as theaters whose support could help Paramount answer concerns about a possible [[WarnerBrosDiscovery|Warner Bros. Discovery]] acquisition.

The episode makes Regal part of [[TheatricalWindowBargaining]]. The reported offer promised 30 theatrical releases per year after a merger, a minimum 45-day exclusive theatrical window, and streaming availability only after 90 days. That makes theaters a stakeholder in [[StreamingConsolidation]], not only downstream exhibitors.
