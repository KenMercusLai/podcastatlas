## Overview

魂 is the protagonist of [[PhantomBladeZero|《影之刃零》]] and one side of [[LiangQiwei|梁其伟]]'s contrast between two extreme approaches to action.

## Current Profile

Facing limited remaining life, Soul acts first to save himself and break the situation around him. The source treats the possibility that his local, personally grounded struggle could also save others as a modern heroic route: larger good emerges from credible self-completion rather than from declaring an abstract identity as a savior.

## Key Characteristics

- Begins from self-rescue under a severe time constraint.
- Acts rather than accepting a fatal script.
- Represents a grounded, local motive rather than a total plan for the world.
- May produce public good without making public virtue his announced identity.
- Carries a rough, damaged, grass-roots visual language.

## Qualifications

This profile reflects a creator's pre-release thematic explanation. The final game's plot, player interpretation, and character consequences may qualify or complicate the stated contrast.
