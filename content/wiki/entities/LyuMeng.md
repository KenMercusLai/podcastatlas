# 吕蒙 / Lyu Meng

## Overview
吕蒙 / Lyu Meng develops from an early Jiangdong commander noticed through disciplined troops into a field adviser who combines trusted surrender incorporation with practical relief planning after Red Cliffs.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1053 presents Lyu Meng as a Biesima from [[RunanCommanderyLateHan|汝南]] whose orderly, trained unit makes him visible during [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s inspection. Sun Quan praises him, adds troops, and begins to trust him more.

Hanji 1109 shows that trust becoming judgment in action. Lyu Meng opposes absorbing [[XiSuLateHan|袭肃]]'s surrendered force into his own command, arguing that a sincere and capable defector should retain and strengthen his troops. He then proposes leaving [[LingTong|凌统]] to hold the rear while he joins [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] in relieving [[GanNing|甘宁]], converting limited manpower into a successful rescue and counterattack.

## Key Characteristics
- Becomes visible through unit discipline rather than lineage or rhetoric.
- Serves as an early test case for Sun Quan's ability to identify usable command talent.
- Protects a defector's command autonomy when trust can attract further submission.
- Designs a rear-guard and relief division that turns constrained manpower into action.
- Combines personnel judgment with operational initiative.

## Evidence
- Unit discipline and promotion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1053-genzhe-sunquan-xuexi-ruhe-zhengdun-jituan-gaoguan-lq9hy25xonjzhj1sfisvauu3bwmi|Hanji 1053]] says Sun Quan sees Lyu Meng's troops as orderly, praises him, adds soldiers, and increases trust.
- Defector incorporation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1109-xizaochi-nu-pi-caocao-duonian-jiye-hui-yu-yidan-lukll8jb60nx2brhwhsw7gfrd-kt|Hanji 1109]] has Lyu Meng reject Zhou Yu's proposed transfer of Xi Su's troops and argue for strengthening Xi Su instead.
- Relief planning: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1109-xizaochi-nu-pi-caocao-duonian-jiye-hui-yu-yidan-lukll8jb60nx2brhwhsw7gfrd-kt|Hanji 1109]] records his proposal to leave Ling Tong at the rear and accompany Zhou Yu to relieve Gan Ning.
- Battlefield result: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1109-xizaochi-nu-pi-caocao-duonian-jiye-hui-yu-yidan-lukll8jb60nx2brhwhsw7gfrd-kt|Hanji 1109]] attributes victory and a three-hundred-horse capture to the relief action.

## Qualifications
The evidence still covers only two early windows, not Lyu Meng's full Eastern Wu career. Hanji 1109's exact troop and horse details remain source-scoped, and Xi Su's incorporation should not be generalized into a universal rule that every surrenderer should retain armed autonomy.

## What Changed
- Extended Lyu Meng from inspected unit disciplinarian into a commander making personnel and relief decisions.
- Added Xi Su's command-retention case and the Gan Ning relief plan.

## Relationships
- [[SunQuan|孙权]] - ruler who recognizes Lyu Meng's discipline and later accepts his incorporation advice.
- [[XiSuLateHan|袭肃]] - surrendering commander whose troop autonomy Lyu Meng protects.
- [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] - senior commander whose transfer proposal Lyu Meng revises and whose relief force he joins.
- [[GanNing|甘宁]] - besieged commander relieved through Lyu Meng's plan.
- [[LingTong|凌统]] - officer assigned to hold the rear during the relief operation.
- [[SuccessionAuthorityConsolidation|继位权威整合]] - earlier framework for Sun Quan's recognition of Lyu Meng.
- [[CharacterReadingDelegationTrust|识人授权信任]] - relationship between observed conduct and delegated command.
