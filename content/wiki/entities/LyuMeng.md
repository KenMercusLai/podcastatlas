# 吕蒙 / Lyu Meng

## Overview
吕蒙 / Lyu Meng enters the wiki as an early Jiangdong commander noticed by [[SunQuan|孙权]] during the young ruler's inspection of inherited military leaders. The page currently covers only this first source-scoped profile: disciplined troops make Lyu Meng visible as usable command talent.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1053 presents Lyu Meng as a Biesima from [[RunanCommanderyLateHan|汝南]] in Sun Quan's command field. When Sun Quan reviews generals and their troops, Lyu Meng's unit stands out because the soldiers are well ordered and trained. Sun Quan praises him, adds troops to his command, and begins to trust him more.

## Key Characteristics
- Becomes visible through unit discipline rather than lineage or rhetoric.
- Serves as an early test case for Sun Quan's promised strength in selecting worthy people.
- Shows how a successor can inspect inherited forces and identify underused capacity.

- Promotion and trust: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1053-genzhe-sunquan-xuexi-ruhe-zhengdun-jituan-gaoguan-lq9hy25xonjzhj1sfisvauu3bwmi|Hanji 1053]] reports that Sun Quan praises Lyu Meng, increases his troops, and treats him with greater trust.

## Qualifications
This page should not yet project Lyu Meng's later Eastern Wu career backward. The current evidence is narrow: it records one early promotion scene and its relevance to Sun Quan's post-succession personnel judgment.
