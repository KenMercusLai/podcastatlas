# 吕蒙 / Lyu Meng

## Overview
吕蒙 / Lyu Meng develops from an early commander noticed through disciplined troops into a combat leader and field adviser who joins personal battlefield action with personnel judgment and relief planning.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1053 presents Lyu Meng as a Biesima from [[RunanCommanderyLateHan|汝南]] whose orderly, trained unit makes him visible during [[SunQuan|孙权]]'s inspection. Sun Quan praises him, adds troops, and increases his trust.

Hanji 1085 supplies direct combat evidence: after the barrier at [[MianKouLateHan|沔口]] is broken, Lyu Meng fights [[HuangZu|黄祖]]'s naval commander [[ChenJiuLateHan|陈就]] and kills him, helping turn the breach into the collapse of the Jiangxia defense. Hanji 1109 then shows judgment beyond personal combat. Lyu Meng protects [[XiSuLateHan|袭肃]]'s surrendered command autonomy and proposes leaving [[LingTong|凌统]] to hold the rear while he joins [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] in relieving [[GanNing|甘宁]].

## Key Characteristics
- Becomes visible through unit discipline rather than lineage or rhetoric.
- Serves as an early test of Sun Quan's ability to identify usable command talent.
- Is credited with killing Chen Jiu during the Jiangxia naval fighting.
- Protects a sincere defector's command autonomy when trust can attract further submission.
- Designs a rear-guard and relief division that turns constrained manpower into action.
- Combines personal combat, personnel judgment, and operational initiative.

## Evidence
- Unit discipline and promotion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1053-genzhe-sunquan-xuexi-ruhe-zhengdun-jituan-gaoguan-lq9hy25xonjzhj1sfisvauu3bwmi|Hanji 1053]] says Sun Quan sees Lyu Meng's troops as orderly, adds soldiers, and increases trust.
- Jiangxia combat: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1085-dongwu-naxia-jiangxia-dengyu-wan-le-liubiao-de-xin-lrnhjns-qu0d6tsit3j_re96zk2u|Hanji 1085]] credits Lyu Meng with killing Chen Jiu after the Mian Kou barrier breach.
- Defector incorporation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1109-xizaochi-nu-pi-caocao-duonian-jiye-hui-yu-yidan-lukll8jb60nx2brhwhsw7gfrd-kt|Hanji 1109]] has Lyu Meng reject the transfer of Xi Su's troops and argue for strengthening him instead.
- Relief planning: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1109-xizaochi-nu-pi-caocao-duonian-jiye-hui-yu-yidan-lukll8jb60nx2brhwhsw7gfrd-kt|Hanji 1109]] records his proposal to leave Ling Tong at the rear and accompany Zhou Yu to relieve Gan Ning.

## Qualifications
The evidence covers only three early windows, not Lyu Meng's full Eastern Wu career. Chen Jiu's killing and display, exact combat details, relief troop and horse figures, and Xi Su's incorporation remain source-scoped. The Xi Su case should not be generalized into a universal rule that every surrenderer should retain armed autonomy.

## What Changed
- Added Lyu Meng's direct combat role and killing of Chen Jiu in the Jiangxia campaign.
- Connected early unit discipline, personal battlefield action, and later command judgment into one developmental profile.

## Relationships
- [[SunQuan|孙权]] - ruler who recognizes Lyu Meng's discipline and later accepts his advice.
- [[ChenJiuLateHan|陈就]] - opposing naval commander Lyu Meng is credited with killing.
- [[MianKouLateHan|沔口]] - battle setting where the barrier breach precedes Lyu Meng's combat.
- [[XiSuLateHan|袭肃]] - surrendering commander whose troop autonomy Lyu Meng protects.
- [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] - senior commander whose transfer proposal Lyu Meng revises and whose relief force he joins.
- [[GanNing|甘宁]] - besieged commander relieved through Lyu Meng's plan.
- [[LingTong|凌统]] - officer assigned to hold the rear during the relief operation.
- [[CharacterReadingDelegationTrust|识人授权信任]] - relationship between observed conduct and delegated command.
