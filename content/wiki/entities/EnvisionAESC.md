# Envision AESC / 远景动力

Envision AESC / 远景动力 appears in [[chengshi-jiushi-zheyang-21-shiyan-yizuo-yin-qiche-erqi-you-bugan-bei-qiche-paoxia-de-chengshi-1001770066]] as one of [[Shiyan]]'s battery-industry diversification anchors. The source says Envision AESC has a core battery-production base in Shiyan, has historical background connected to Nissan's battery business, and serves customers including European automakers.

In the episode's argument, Envision AESC matters because Shiyan is trying to extend from [[CommercialVehicleIndustrialBase|commercial vehicles]] into battery and new-energy chains. That makes the company part of a legacy-city version of [[LocalBatteryIndustrialClusters]] rather than a purely battery-first city story.
