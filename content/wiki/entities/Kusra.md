# Kusra

Kusra is the Palestinian village in [[settler-bet-a-west-bank-flashpoint-6a842bb539e73ea8f3c13a2d]] where the episode locates its recent [[WestBank|West Bank]] flashpoint. The source says settlers from the new [[TelTelPiyot]] outpost tried to occupy several outlying houses, including one owned by an American citizen, and that residents' video footage helped push the incident into wider public and diplomatic view.

The village matters in the wiki because it turns [[WestBankOutpostEscalation]] from an abstract settlements issue into a specific land, house, and video-evidence case. It also shows how local Palestinian documentation can sometimes reach the [[UnitedStates]] administration through [[MikeHuckabee]] and embassy intervention.
