# 渡边美树 / Miki Watanabe

## Overview

[[MikiWatanabe|渡边美树]] is the founder of [[WatamiGroup|和民集团]], a former Japanese legislator, and the central leadership figure in the current source's account of Watami's dream-centered corporate ideology and response to [[MoriMina|森美菜]]'s death.

## Current Profile

Watanabe promoted dreams, gratitude, social change, and a founder-authored learning system as employee-development principles. In Mina's case, those principles were embedded in assessment and off-shift obligations, making the distinction between inspiration and control material. He wrote after her death that the misfortune was his responsibility, but later publicly denied a labor-management problem and resisted direct engagement and legal responsibility until the 2015 settlement.

## Key Characteristics

- Built Watami's public identity around dreams, social contribution, agriculture, and people-centered management.
- Made founder-authored philosophy study a significant part of new-employee evaluation.
- Used responsibility language immediately after Mina's death while later disputing organizational and legal fault.
- Entered electoral politics during the accountability campaign and later returned to lead Watami.
- Became a focal point for public debate over whether corporate inspiration can conceal labor coercion.
