# Vera Kundera / 薇拉·昆德拉

[[VeraKundera|Vera Kundera / 薇拉·昆德拉]] appears in [[12-man-dang-milan-kundela-yi-ju-zhengjing-meiyou-549335675]] in both the biographical and fictional orbit around [[MilanKundera|Milan Kundera]]. The source describes Kundera and his wife as having lived under post-1968 surveillance, while [[SlowKundera|《慢》]] also opens with the narrator and wife Vera traveling to a castle hotel.

The episode uses Vera less as an independent biographical subject than as part of Kundera's privacy and narrative frame. In the novel, her dream and final comment that the narrator is writing a book with no serious sentences help mark the layered relation among reality, dream, fiction, and authorial self-parody.
