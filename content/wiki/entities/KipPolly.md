## Overview

Kip Polly is a haunted-attraction technical director whose work combines manufacturing engineering, theatrical effects, maintenance, show control, and creative design.

## Current Profile

In the supplied [[tech-20261028-1028-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261028-1028-mp-tech-pod-128|Marketplace Tech episode]], Polly leads technical work for [[ThirteenthFloorEntertainment|13th Floor Entertainment]]. His career moved from an early ambition to design roller coasters through engineering education, special-effects makeup and attraction maintenance, a prop-and-effects business, and technical direction. He treats implementation and artistic conception as one coupled practice: every creative idea is evaluated through how it can be built, timed, operated, and kept unobtrusive to guests.

## Key Characteristics

- Integrates creative design with practical engineering rather than separating artist and implementer.
- Uses sensors, controllers, lighting, audio, animatronics, video, and tactile effects to support live performance.
- Values technical sophistication behind the scenes and experiential simplicity for the audience.
- Built his career through formal education, self-teaching, maintenance work, and entrepreneurial risk.
- Accepts travel and long production hours in exchange for creating memorable shared experiences.
