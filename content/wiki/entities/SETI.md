# SETI / Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence

SETI enters the wiki through [[sp-01-yuzhou-kaer-sagen-de-xingchen-dahai-he-renlei-mengxiang-543901074]] as the organized search for extraterrestrial intelligence using radio telescopes and pattern-seeking across cosmic signals. The source emphasizes that [[CarlSagan|Carl Sagan]] did not merely enjoy alien-life speculation; he treated the possibility as a serious scientific question worth listening, modeling, messaging, and arguing about.

The episode links SETI to Sagan's wider project: Mars and Jupiter-life speculation, Arecibo-style messages, the [[VoyagerGoldenRecord|Voyager golden record]], and [[ContactNovel|《接触》 / Contact]] all turn the question of extraterrestrial life into a blend of evidence, imagination, engineering, and civilization self-description.

## Key Claims
- SETI is not UFO credulity in this source; it is a disciplined search for signals and structure.
- The project keeps extraterrestrial life possible while requiring evidence and interpretation before belief.
- Sagan's SETI work shows how [[ScientificSkepticism]] and wonder can share the same project.
- The source treats SETI as both a scientific question and a narrative resource for science fiction, especially Contact.
