# AITO / 问界

## Overview
AITO / 问界 is the [[Seres]] and [[Huawei]] cooperation brand that serves as the first proof case for [[HuaweiSmartSelectionCarModel|华为智选车模式]]. Its profile now spans China product-market validation, owner use of the M9, and a source-reported plan to enter Europe with [[Bosch]]-supported vehicle systems.

## Current Profile
AITO remains the clearest case where Huawei product definition, intelligent systems, channel influence, and service meet Seres manufacturing, supply-chain, and quality responsibility. The M5 introduced Huawei-linked range-extension and cockpit capability, the new M7 showed product-market repair after upgrades, and the M9 became both a high-end validation case and a lived-use example for assisted-driving trust, long-distance travel, and mobile work or family space.

Internationalization is now part of the profile. AITO plans, in the source account, to enter Europe in autumn 2027 with overseas models jointly developed with Bosch in advanced driver assistance, cockpit, and chassis systems after Huawei shifted more overseas-brand leadership to the automaker. This is a localization plan, not evidence that European regulatory approval, launch timing, distribution, or demand have already been secured.

## Key Characteristics
- First deep-cooperation template inside [[HongmengZhixing|鸿蒙智行]].
- Combines Seres manufacturing responsibility with Huawei product, technology, retail, launch, and service influence.
- Uses M5, new M7, and M9 as staged evidence for product-market fit, repair, and high-end positioning.
- Adds owner evidence that M9 assisted driving can move from distrust to routine use after concrete road experience.
- Extends the M9 into long-distance travel and mobile living-space use.
- Pursues source-reported European entry through Bosch-supported localization of driver assistance, cockpit, and chassis systems.

## Evidence
- **Cooperation model:** [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] says Seres supplies qualification, factories, supply chain, manufacturing, and quality management, while Huawei supplies product definition, configuration, pricing, launches, marketing, intelligent cockpit, assisted driving, electric drive, and retail channels.
- **Model sequence:** [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] treats M5 as the early Drive One / HarmonyOS cockpit case, new M7 as product-market repair, and M9 as high-end validation.
- **Assisted-driving adoption and living space:** [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]] describes an M9 owner moving from avoidance to routine assisted-driving use and includes Tibet, Xinjiang, high-pass, meeting, rest, and family-space stories.
- **European localization:** [[gkwrijiorooxafjycwtsvi3u-gkwrijiorooxafjycwtsvi3u]] reports a planned autumn 2027 Europe entry and Bosch cooperation across driver assistance, cockpit, and chassis systems.

## Qualifications
AITO's strong Huawei influence does not erase Seres's manufacturing and quality responsibility. EP279's owner stories are positive anecdotes, not comparative tests of assisted driving, reliability, or off-road capability. The Europe plan, Bosch work split, regulatory advantage, launch date, and overseas leadership allocation are source-reported; the evidence does not yet establish homologation, sales channels, price positioning, or market acceptance.

## What Changed
- Added the source-reported Europe launch plan and Bosch localization partnership.
- Qualified European entry as a regulatory and operating plan rather than an achieved market position.
