# AITO / 问界

## Overview

AITO / 问界 is the [[Seres]] and [[Huawei]] cooperation brand that [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] treats as the first proof case for [[HuaweiSmartSelectionCarModel|华为智选车模式]]. [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]] adds an owner-experience branch through M9 use in assisted driving, long-distance travel, high-altitude routes, and mobile living-space scenarios.

## Current Profile

The brand remains the clearest case where Huawei product definition and Seres manufacturing capacity meet. The M5 introduced Huawei-linked range-extension and cockpit capability, the new M7 showed product-market repair after upgrades, and the M9 became the high-end validation case. EP279 turns the M9 from a portfolio proof point into a lived-use case: owners use it to test assisted-driving trust, reduce long-trip fatigue, travel through Tibet/Xinjiang, and experience the car as a mobile meeting or family space.

## Key Characteristics

- Serves as the first deep-cooperation template inside [[HongmengZhixing|鸿蒙智行]].
- Combines Seres manufacturing responsibility with Huawei product, technology, retail, launch, and service influence.
- Uses M5, new M7, and M9 as staged evidence for product-market fit, repair, and high-end positioning.
- EP279 adds owner evidence that M9-assisted driving can move from distrust to routine use after concrete road experience.
- M9 is also used as a long-distance and lifestyle case, not only as a high-end sales proof point.

## Evidence

- **Cooperation model:** [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] says Seres supplies qualification, factories, supply chain, manufacturing, and quality management, while Huawei supplies product definition, configuration, pricing, launches, marketing, intelligent cockpit, assisted driving, electric drive, and retail channels.
- **Model sequence:** [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] treats M5 as the early Drive One / HarmonyOS cockpit case, new M7 as product-market repair, and M9 as high-end validation.
- **Assisted-driving adoption:** [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]] describes an M9 owner who avoided assisted driving for thousands of kilometers before a mountain-road return made the feature feel useful and safer.
- **Long-distance living space:** [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]] includes M9 stories from Tibet, Xinjiang, high passes, no-man's-land crossings, and mobile meeting/rest use.

## Qualifications

- AITO's strong Huawei influence does not erase Seres's manufacturing and quality responsibility.
- EP279's owner stories are positive anecdotes, not comparative tests of M9 assisted driving, reliability, or off-road capability.
- High-end validation and delivery claims remain source-scoped to the episode or prior source.
