# AITO / 问界

AITO / 问界 is the [[Seres]] and [[Huawei]] cooperation brand that [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] treats as the proof case for [[HuaweiSmartSelectionCarModel|华为智选车模式]]. Before AITO, the source says Seres SF5 was used to test Huawei technology, store traffic, and sales-channel fit.

The source describes the division of work clearly: Seres supplied qualification, factories, supply chain, manufacturing, and quality management, while Huawei supplied product definition, target users, configuration, pricing, launches, marketing, intelligent cockpit, assisted driving, electric drive, and retail channels. The M5 introduced the Drive One range-extended platform and HarmonyOS cockpit, the new M7 showed a turnaround after price, body, chassis, and assisted-driving upgrades, and the M9 validated a higher-end route.

## Source Position
- AITO is the first deep-cooperation template inside [[HongmengZhixing]].
- The new M7 case is treated as product-market repair, not only as Huawei store traffic.
- The M9 case is the source's strongest [[HighEndEVBranding|high-end EV branding]] evidence for the Huawei-Seres route.
- AITO also shows why [[HuaweiNoCarManufacturingBoundary|not formally making cars]] can still leave Huawei with major influence over the customer-facing product.
