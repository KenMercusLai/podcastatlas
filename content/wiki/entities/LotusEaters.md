# Lotus Eaters

## Overview

The Lotus Eaters are the Odyssey people whose food threatens homecoming by making travelers forget the desire to return.

## Current Profile

In [[687-the-odyssey-hero-of-the-trojan-horse-part-1-glt2393125423]], scouts sent inland after a storm eat lotus and no longer want to send word or return home. [[Odysseus]] has to bring them back by force. The hosts mention identification theories involving North African plants, opium, or psychotropic blue lotus, but the wiki value of the episode is symbolic: pleasure can erase [[HomericNostos]] before violence is even needed.

## Key Characteristics

- The Lotus Eaters threaten memory and desire rather than the body directly.
- Their episode makes homecoming vulnerable to forgetting, comfort, and passivity.
- Odysseus preserves the voyage by forcing affected men back to the ships.
- The source keeps possible botanical identifications speculative rather than settled.

## Evidence

- Loss of return desire: [[687-the-odyssey-hero-of-the-trojan-horse-part-1-glt2393125423]] says lotus eaters lose the desire to send word or return.
- Forced rescue: [[687-the-odyssey-hero-of-the-trojan-horse-part-1-glt2393125423]] has Odysseus retrieve the affected scouts because they are unwilling to leave.
- Speculative localization: [[687-the-odyssey-hero-of-the-trojan-horse-part-1-glt2393125423]] mentions theories around North African plants, opium, and psychotropic blue lotus without settling the question.

## Qualifications

This page treats the Lotus Eaters through the episode's literary and interpretive use. It does not identify a real plant or historical people.
