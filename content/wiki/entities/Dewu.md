# 得物 / Dewu

得物 / Dewu appears in [[no-212-cengjing-de-jingshen-jiaoluo-douban-zhihu-tieba-hupu-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-25-1003510980]] as the commercially stronger derivative path from [[Hupu|虎扑]]'s equipment community. The episode says [[YangBing|杨冰]] developed ShiHuo and Du App from gear-section demand before Du App was renamed Dewu and separated from Hupu through later financing.

For the wiki, Dewu is useful because it qualifies the source's "communities are hard to monetize" claim. A pure relationship forum faces [[OnlineCommunityCommercializationFriction]], but a community can sometimes create a transaction business when the shared interest contains verifiable products, price comparison, authenticity concerns, and repeat purchase behavior.
