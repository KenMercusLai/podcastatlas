# The Indicator

## Overview
The Indicator is an economics show in the [[PlanetMoney]] orbit that uses short stories, statistics, and concrete examples to make wider economic mechanisms legible.

## Current Profile
[[indicators-of-2025-and-what-to-watch-in-2026]] shows the format at its most macro: six compact indicators stand for household mood, tariffs, valuation, rates, electricity costs, and consumer concentration. [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-90166871d561fa45ba428e0a0fbc3e7e-20290737-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-90166871d561fa45ba428e0a0fbc3e7e-20290737]] applies the same compression to two household-risk stories, pairing margin-driven liquidation with sports betting treated as investment.

## Key Characteristics
- Short-form economic storytelling built around a small number of revealing signals.
- Crossover relationship with [[PlanetMoney]].
- Moves between macro indicators and behavioral or market-structure risks.

## Evidence
### Macro indicator format
- [[indicators-of-2025-and-what-to-watch-in-2026]] uses six participants and indicators to map different layers of the economy without treating any one signal as a full forecast.

### Explanatory compression
- [[indicators-of-2025-and-what-to-watch-in-2026|Indicators of 2025]] and [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-90166871d561fa45ba428e0a0fbc3e7e-20290737-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-90166871d561fa45ba428e0a0fbc3e7e-20290737|How investing is getting riskier]] use accessible examples to lead into institutional questions involving the [[FederalReserve]], brokerage rules, and state gambling policy.

## Qualifications
- The show format favors concise synthesis; individual statistics and expert claims remain source-dated and may require independent verification.
- Pairing two stories under one theme does not establish that margin trading and sports betting have identical economics or regulation.
