# Tanaka Kakuei / 田中角荣

Tanaka Kakuei / 田中角荣 appears in [[vol-268-liang-ge-lao-si-lai-si-1003563933]] first as the Japanese prime minister whose 1972 visit to [[China]] normalized Sino-Japanese relations, and then as the politician drawn into the [[LockheedBriberyScandal]]. The episode presents his rise from relatively modest origins, infrastructure and business networks, and rapid China diplomacy as part of his political significance.

In the Lockheed segment, the source says Tanaka was implicated in payments connected to [[AllNipponAirways|All Nippon Airways]] buying [[LockheedL1011TriStar|L-1011 TriStar]] aircraft with [[RollsRoyceRB211|RB211]] engines. It records his 1976 arrest and later first-instance conviction as the Japanese political endpoint of a U.S. foreign-bribery investigation.

The source also gives a more interpretive political reading: it suggests that U.S. political dissatisfaction with Tanaka may have related to China normalization and oil-crisis positioning. That claim is treated here as source-scoped interpretation, not as a settled causal account.
