# Beer Lao

Beer Lao is the national lager used in [[pump-and-circumstance-is-china-the-new-opec-6a7c37287cf3c58886773a66]] to read [[Laos]]'s economy. The episode describes it as culturally ubiquitous, widely branded across Laos, and unusually dominant: it accounts for nearly 90% of domestic beer consumption while also growing as an export across Asia.

The product's formula matters to the segment's national-brand story. Beer Lao is described as combining European malt and hops with locally grown jasmine rice, producing a light, faintly sweet lager that locals often drink with ice and travelers praise for taste and price.

The source's economic point is double-edged. Beer Lao gives Laos a rare successful branded export and tax base, but its importance also exposes [[SingleProductNationalChampionDependence]] because few other Lao industries have reached comparable scale.
