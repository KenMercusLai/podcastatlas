# NORAID

NORAID appears in [[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] as the Irish-American fundraising channel the hosts name in the [[ProvisionalIRA]] weapons and money story. The episode says Irish-American fundraising helped finance weapons, including Armalite rifles, some of which were smuggled across the Atlantic.

The page is source-scoped to this episode's 1970-1971 support-network account. It links the [[UnitedStates]] and [[Ireland]] to the material escalation of [[TheTroubles]] without treating all Irish-American politics as support for violence.
