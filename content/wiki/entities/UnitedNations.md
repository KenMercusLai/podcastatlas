# United Nations

The United Nations appears in [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]] as a recognition arena for [[Myanmar]]. The episode says Myanmar is represented at the UN by an ambassador from the civilian government rather than by the [[MyanmarMilitaryJunta]].

That representation makes Aung San Suu Kyi's status diplomatically relevant. The source says releasing her, or even allowing foreign diplomats to verify her condition, could reduce resistance to normalizing relations with the junta. The UN therefore becomes one stage for [[ProofOfLifeDiplomacy]] and not only a background institution.

[[sand-heists-and-property-rights-in-the-caribbean-summer-school]] adds the United Nations as an environmental knowledge source through [[PascalPeduzzi]] and the cited report "Sand Rarer Than One Thinks." The episode uses the UN sand work to connect a [[Jamaica]] stolen-beach case to global [[SandScarcity]], [[IllegalSandMining]], and land-reclamation demand from places such as [[Singapore]].
