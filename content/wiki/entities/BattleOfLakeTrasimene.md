# Battle of Lake Trasimene / 特拉西梅诺湖战役

## Overview

The Battle of Lake Trasimene / 特拉西梅诺湖战役 is Hannibal's 217 BC destruction of Flaminius's Roman army in a fog-covered ambush along a road constrained by the lake and surrounding hills.

## Current Profile

The episode makes Trasimene the culmination of an operational lure. [[HannibalBarca]] crosses the Apennines and marshes by an unexpected route, ravages Etruria to provoke Gaius Flaminius, and lets a visible force appear to move away. Flaminius follows, then advances into a lakeside corridor where Hannibal has concealed roughly 30,000 men on the hills.

Mist and terrain prevent the Roman column from seeing or coordinating against attacks from the front, flanks, and rear. Flaminius is killed, escape routes collapse into the lake or cavalry, and the episode reports the destruction of about 25,000 men. A second ambush of 4,000 cavalry intensifies the shock and prompts Rome to appoint [[QuintusFabiusMaximus]] dictator.

## Key Characteristics

- Emerges from an unexpected route, devastation, and pursuit.
- Uses fog and lakeside geography to hide the scale and direction of attack.
- Strikes a marching column before officers can form a coherent picture.
- Destroys an army and its consul rather than merely forcing retreat.
- Triggers a Roman emergency response and the [[FabianStrategy]].

- Command collapse: [[570-hannibal-the-invasion-of-italy-part-3-glt2952414067]] says attacks from several directions leave Roman officers unable to control the engagement.
- Consequence: [[570-hannibal-the-invasion-of-italy-part-3-glt2952414067]] links the defeat and subsequent cavalry loss to Fabius's dictatorship.

## Qualifications

The exact date, totals, duration, death count, scouting information, and individual death scenes derive from ancient narratives and remain source-scoped. Flaminius is presented through Polybius's hostile portrait, which may reflect elite and Scipionic bias.

## What Changed

- Established Trasimene as the endpoint of an operational pursuit trap.
- Added command blindness from fog, terrain, column formation, and multidirectional attack.
- Connected the defeat directly to emergency dictatorship and strategic change.
