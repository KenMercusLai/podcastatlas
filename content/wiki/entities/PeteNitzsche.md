# Pete Nitzsche

## Overview
Pete Nitzsche is the Rutgers agriculture and extension figure who guides the episode through the [[ScarletSunrise|Scarlet Sunrise]] tomato case.

## Current Profile
Nitzsche appears as both field guide and practical breeder. He helps explain why a tomato must work for growers as well as eaters: fruit quality matters, but plant size, disease pressure, insect pressure, firmness, and harvest labor shape whether a variety can become economically useful. His comments move the episode from branding alone into farmer profitability and crop-management economics.

## Key Characteristics
- Rutgers-linked agriculture practitioner associated with the Scarlet Sunrise breeding effort.
- Explains the practical crop traits that sit behind a marketable produce brand.
- Treats grower economics as central to whether a variety succeeds.
- Links Scarlet Sunrise to a compact tomato-plant experiment with labor-saving potential.
