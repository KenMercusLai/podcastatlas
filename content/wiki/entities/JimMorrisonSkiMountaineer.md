# 吉姆·莫里森 / Jim Morrison (ski mountaineer)

## Overview
Jim Morrison is a ski mountaineer and the principal adventure subject in [[EverestBeyondTheCloudsFilm|《珠穆朗玛：云中彼岸》]], represented here through his Everest north-face project, partnership with [[HilareeNelson|希拉里·尼尔森]], bereavement, and decision-making after her death.

## Current Profile
Morrison describes the Everest project as the hardest, boldest, and highest-risk undertaking of his professional and personal life. His account of Nelson holds together her devotion to mountain sport and to her children rather than reducing either side. After her death in 2022, he portrays grief as a collapse of the known world and the film as a possible way to preserve her full character while showing that people can continue through devastating change.

## Key Characteristics
- Pursues technically and personally consequential ski-mountaineering objectives.
- Frames Nelson's identity as simultaneously maternal, professional, adventurous, and self-directed.
- Does not deny that extreme pursuit transfers consequences to children, partners, and friends.
- Understands loss as enduring absence rather than a problem that must be completely filled.
- Uses the documentary to carry Nelson's story and a possibility of continuing after catastrophe.
