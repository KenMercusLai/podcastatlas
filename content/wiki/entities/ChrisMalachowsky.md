# Chris Malachowsky

## Overview
Chris Malachowsky is a co-founder of [[Nvidia]] who, with [[CurtisPriem|Curtis Priem]], approached [[JensenHuang|Jensen Huang]] about building affordable three-dimensional graphics for personal computers.

## Current Profile
The bounded source places Malachowsky in Nvidia's 1993 founding team and Huang's broader insistence that the company's history is a collective achievement. It does not provide enough evidence to profile Malachowsky's later operating roles independently.

## Key Characteristics
- Co-founded Nvidia with Huang and Priem.
- Helped frame the original opportunity around bringing high-end 3D graphics to personal computers.
- Appears in the source as part of the team behind Nvidia rather than as a separately profiled executive.
