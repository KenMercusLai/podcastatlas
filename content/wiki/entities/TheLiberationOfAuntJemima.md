# The Liberation of Aunt Jemima

The Liberation of Aunt Jemima is the 1968 [[BetyeSaar]] assemblage discussed in [[outsider-chance-ending-sudans-proxy-war-6a75b3695d765781a2b1b132]]. The episode says Saar transformed an Aunt Jemima notebook-holder figurine by adding a rifle, a hand grenade, and a Black Power fist.

The source treats the work as a turning point. It reworked the racist mammy figure into an image of agency and resistance, touched a national nerve, and made Saar feel she had become an artist.
