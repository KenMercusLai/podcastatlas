## Overview
大连英博 / Dalian Yingbo is used in the episode as a Chinese football-club example where city support, commercial activation, supporters, and matchday consumption can turn league participation into local economic activity.

## Current Profile
The source treats Dalian Yingbo less as a sporting case than as a city-market example. Public facilitation, sponsors, state-owned shareholder participation, jersey demand, and all-day matchday scenes are cited as signs that top-flight or high-attention football can operate as a local consumption platform.
