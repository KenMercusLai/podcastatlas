# Théroigne de Méricourt

## Overview

Théroigne de Méricourt was a revolutionary activist associated with the Women's March on Versailles, political clubs, and advocacy for an armed women's battalion.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Méricourt as a visible challenge to the Revolution's gendered public boundary. Her Austrian imprisonment increased her prestige and helped her gain access to the Cordeliers and Jacobin clubs, but her attempts to build women's or mixed clubs and an armed female battalion did not secure durable institutional support.

## Key Characteristics

- She came from Liège and became a flamboyant revolutionary public figure.
- She was associated with the Women's March on Versailles.
- Austrian agents arrested her in 1790 and imprisoned her until late 1791.
- Her imprisonment increased rather than destroyed her revolutionary prestige.
- She pursued women-only and mixed political clubs.
- She advocated a women's battalion to defend Paris.
