# Ferrari 166 Barchetta

The Ferrari 166 Barchetta is the early road-car model that [[ferrari-1]] treats as the foundation of the Ferrari formula. The source describes it as beautiful, powerful, elegant, and capable of racing.

The car matters because it made [[Ferrari]] legible to paying customers without separating road use from racing proof. Through [[LuigiChinetti]] and early elite buyers, the 166 Barchetta shows how [[RacingHeritageBrandMoat]] and [[ProductLedWillingnessToPay]] began to reinforce each other.
