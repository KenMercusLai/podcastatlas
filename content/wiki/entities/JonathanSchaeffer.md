# Jonathan Schaeffer

[[ep-47-the-ai-pioneer-who-decided-privacy-matters-more-than-hype]] identifies the guest as Dr. Jonathan and describes the work associated with [[JonathanSchaeffer]]: a distinguished professor at the [[UniversityOfAlberta]], a co-founder of the [[AlbertaMachineIntelligenceInstitute]], and the AI researcher behind the checkers program tracked here as [[ChinookCheckers]]. The page keeps that identity source-scoped to the episode description and uses the source's biographical details rather than adding outside claims.

In the episode, Schaeffer connects three AI eras: expert systems that struggled under handcrafted rules, search-heavy game AI that became verifiable in constrained domains, and modern LLMs that are powerful but probabilistic. His current product frame is [[KindPrivateAI]] from [[Synsira]], which turns privacy, local files, [[RetrievalAugmentedGeneration]], and [[DigitalSovereignty]] into an AI design problem rather than only a model-quality problem.

## Key Claims
- Schaeffer's checkers work is used as a contrast case for modern LLMs because [[ChinookCheckers]] could target zero-error game-theoretic verification.
- He treats current LLMs as useful assistants that still need [[HumanJudgmentUnderAI]], [[AIVerification]], and [[AugmentedIntelligence]] boundaries.
- His local-AI argument ties individual privacy to larger [[DigitalSovereignty]] questions about who controls data, infrastructure, and jurisdiction.
