# Jessica Thompson

## Overview

Jessica Thompson was a nurse living near Somerton Park Beach whose phone number appeared in the *Rubaiyat* connected to the [[SomertonManCase]]. She denied knowing the dead man, while her reported reaction, literary interests, family circumstances, and Cold War associations became the basis for unproven romance and espionage theories.

## Current Profile

Thompson lived about 400 meters from where the body was found. Police connected her to the case after finding her number in the recovered *Rubaiyat*. She said the book was a favorite and that she had once given a copy to Australian Army lieutenant Alf Boxel; police found Boxel alive, excluding an early identification theory.

A policeman later described Thompson as extremely shaken when shown the body or plaster cast, but she denied acquaintance. She had a young son, Robin, and was involved with Prosper Thompson before marrying him. [[DerekAbbott]] later proposed that Robin was the Somerton Man's son, using physical resemblance and Robin's later ballet career, but the episode says DNA excluded that paternity claim.

Claims that Thompson spoke Russian, had communist interests, or participated in intelligence activity come through later family recollection and Cold War interpretation in the source. They do not establish that she knew [[CarlWebb]] or explain why he died nearby.

## Key Characteristics

- Local nurse linked to the case by a phone number in the recovered book.
- Owner or admirer of the *Rubaiyat* who had given a copy to Alf Boxel.
- Subject of a disputed emotional-reaction account despite denying acquaintance.
- Mother of Robin Thompson, whose proposed paternity link was reportedly excluded by DNA.
- Focus of romance and espionage theories that exceed the episode's hard evidence.

## Evidence

- Geographic and documentary link: [[257-australia-the-mystery-of-the-somerton-man-glt2531949703]] reports Thompson's nearby home and phone number in the book.
- Statement and reaction: [[257-australia-the-mystery-of-the-somerton-man-glt2531949703]] records her denial, the policeman's reaction account, and the Alf Boxel explanation.
- Family hypothesis: [[257-australia-the-mystery-of-the-somerton-man-glt2531949703]] presents Abbott's Robin theory and the DNA result that reportedly rejected it.
- Cold War overlay: [[257-australia-the-mystery-of-the-somerton-man-glt2531949703]] attributes Russian-language and communist-interest claims to Thompson's daughter and treats espionage as unsupported.

## Qualifications

The source does not establish that Thompson knew [[CarlWebb]], owned the exact recovered book, wrote its letter sequences, had an affair with the deceased, or engaged in espionage. A reported emotional reaction is not reliable proof of acquaintance, and a relative's later account of language ability or politics is not proof of intelligence work. The DNA result addresses paternity, not every possible relationship.

## What Changed

- Established the first canonical Thompson profile.
- Separated direct links—location and phone number—from inferred romance or espionage.
- Recorded the reported rejection of the Robin paternity theory.
- Preserved her denial and evidentiary limits alongside the policeman's reaction account.
