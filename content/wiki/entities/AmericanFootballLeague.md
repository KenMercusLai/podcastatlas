# American Football League

The American Football League is the rival football league in [[the-nfl-1]] founded with [[LamarHunt]] as a key organizer. The episode presents the AFL as a counter-positioned challenger that treated national television as a league-level asset from the start.

The AFL signed a league-wide television deal before playing a game, later landed a larger NBC deal, signed [[JoeNamath]], and escalated the salary war with the [[NationalFootballLeague|NFL]]. Its merger with the NFL and validation through the [[SuperBowl]] are central to the episode's explanation of how competition pushed professional football toward centralized scale.
