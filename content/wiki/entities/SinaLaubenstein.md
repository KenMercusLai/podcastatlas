# Sina Laubenstein

Sina Laubenstein appears in [[tech-20260727-0727-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260727-0727-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the [[InstituteForStrategicDialogue]] researcher explaining how AI nudify tools spread through mainstream online infrastructure. She says her team found 181 websites offering tools that generate nudified images or videos from real people's photos, and she links their visibility to [[YouTube]], [[Twitter|X]], [[Facebook]], [[Instagram]], app stores, and payment channels.

Her role in the wiki is to make [[AINonConsensualIntimateImageAbuse]] an ecosystem problem rather than only a content-moderation problem. She argues that enforcement should look beyond posts and ads to app-store distribution, [[AbusePaymentInfrastructure]], crypto intermediaries, banking oversight, and [[California]] legal pressure on [[Apple]] and [[Google]].
