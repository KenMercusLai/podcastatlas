招商基金 appears in [[vol-121-cong-tanhua-yixian-de-fenji-jijin-dao-fengtou-zhengjing-de-ganggan-etf-yongyuan-buyao-digu-renxing-de-fengkuang-lusagcitdozvzm8wausvecvi-qmb]] through 招商中证白酒分级. The episode uses 白酒 B as a structured-fund case that survived the 2015 crash window and later rode a strong liquor-stock cycle before structured funds exited the market.

The source does not use 招商基金 as a general company profile. Its role is to show how a leveraged product can look successful when the underlying theme later delivers a powerful bull market, even though the category-level suitability problem remains.
