# Nagase Tomiro / 长濑富郎

Nagase Tomiro / 长濑富郎 is the founder figure in [[mai-xiangzao-qijia-de-bainian-huawang-weishenme-chengle-bandaoti-zhizao-de-guanjiang-qiye-1004213131]]. The episode says he operated Nagase Shoten in Tokyo in the late nineteenth century, selling imported soap, cosmetics, and stationery during [[Japan]]'s Meiji-era westernization.

His source role is to anchor [[Kao|花王]] in a practical product gap. Imported American soap was useful but expensive, while cheaper domestic soap was not suitable for washing the face, so Nagase developed an affordable local facial soap and launched it in 1890 under the Kao name.

The episode uses Nagase less as a full biography than as the starting point for a long continuity: solving one cleaning problem eventually led Kao into detergents, household products, industrial chemicals, [[CleanThrough]], and [[SemiconductorPrecisionCleaning|semiconductor precision cleaning]].
