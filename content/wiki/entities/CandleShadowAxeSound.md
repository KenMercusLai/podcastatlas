# 烛影斧声 / Candle Shadow Axe Sound

Candle Shadow Axe Sound / 烛影斧声 is the death-night story around [[ZhaoKuangyin|赵匡胤 / 宋太祖]] in [[170-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-xia-958158115]]. The episode's version has Taizu drinking with [[ZhaoGuangyi|赵光义 / 宋太宗]], attendants withdrawn, distant observers seeing odd movement in candlelight, Taizu striking the ground with an axe-like object, and Taizu dying later that night.

The source does not make the phrase proof of murder by itself. Its importance comes from the surrounding chain: terse official death records, [[SongEmpress|宋皇后]] expecting [[ZhaoDefang|赵德芳]], [[WangJien|王继恩]] going instead to Zhao Guangyi, [[ChengDexuan|程德玄]]'s strange door-call story, and the later need for [[GoldenCabinetPledge|金匮之盟]] to stabilize succession.

For the wiki, the event sits between [[EvidenceBoundHistoricalRevision]] and [[ConspiracyTheoryPatternSeeking]]. The episode treats suspicion as reasonable only when attached to chronology, source motive, political beneficiaries, and later consequences such as the deaths of [[ZhaoDezhao|赵德昭]], [[ZhaoDefang|赵德芳]], and the fall of [[ZhaoTingmei|赵廷美]].
