# Cotti Coffee / 库迪咖啡

## Overview
Cotti Coffee / 库迪咖啡 is a Chinese coffee chain represented in the source through a limited test of freshly fried food in selected stores.

## Current Profile
The source uses Cotti as a second case, alongside [[LuckinCoffee|Luckin]], of beverage chains searching for more revenue per store by widening the menu. Cotti's test remains below national rollout and therefore signals exploration rather than a settled restaurant-format shift.

## Key Characteristics
- Chinese coffee-chain context in the episode.
- Testing a freshly prepared food category in selected stores.
- Seeking store-output growth beyond the core beverage menu.
- Exposed to preparation, equipment, service-speed, waste, and supply-chain complexity.

## Evidence
- **Menu experiment:** [[gaojiesi-doubao-zengjia-shuidian-jiaofei-gongneng-ruixing-yinru-naicha-pinlei-gkwrirwopt2saflztgtozfrl]] says selected Cotti stores are testing freshly fried food and that the category has not rolled out nationally.
- **Operating interpretation:** [[gaojiesi-doubao-zengjia-shuidian-jiaofei-gongneng-ruixing-yinru-naicha-pinlei-gkwrirwopt2saflztgtozfrl]] pairs Cotti with Luckin to argue that new categories can add demand while increasing store and supply-chain burden.

## Qualifications
The source gives no store sample, menu details, sales lift, waste rate, labor requirement, equipment cost, test duration, or rollout decision. It does not establish that the experiment is profitable or representative of Cotti's whole network.
