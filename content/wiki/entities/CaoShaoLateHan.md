## Overview

曹绍 / Cao Shao is introduced by [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-890-xiang-dangguan-xian-xue-duifu-xiaoren-ltnvh6qu-mr8flsdlxf-e6rknpis|Hanji 890]] as a [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]] clan relative serving as Dong Commandery administrator. His role in the source is narrow but consequential: he becomes the local official who arrests and tortures [[XieBiLateHan|谢弼]] after Xie Bi's dangerous memorial.

## Current Profile

Cao Shao is not developed as an independent political thinker in the episode. He functions as an enforcement extension of the eunuch-side retaliation field: resentment generated by court remonstrance can be carried into local administration, where charges are fabricated and punishment becomes lethal.

## Key Characteristics

- Cao Shao is source-scoped as a Cao Jie-linked local administrator, not yet as a full biographical subject.
- His action against Xie Bi shows how central factional danger can become local criminal prosecution.
- The page mainly clarifies identity and avoids leaving Cao Shao as an unindexed name inside the Xie Bi sequence.

## Qualifications

- The source gives Cao Shao only through the Xie Bi retaliation sequence; later sources may require more precise family and office detail.
- His page should not be generalized into a complete Cao-clan or eunuch-family history without additional evidence.
