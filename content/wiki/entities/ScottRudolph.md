# Scott Rudolph

## Overview
Scott Rudolph is [[BrianRudolph]]'s older brother and the co-founder of [[Banza]]. Brian initially approached him for advice before asking him to join the company.

## Current Profile
Scott appears in the source as the co-founder who helped convert Brian's home experiment into a funded, packaged, manufactured, and distributed food business. His role is clearest during the early television, fundraising, retail, and manufacturing period, including the high-risk decision to fulfill Banza's first large order with emergency cooking instructions.

## Key Characteristics
- Joined after first serving as an informal adviser to his younger brother.
- Shared responsibility for early funding, media exposure, retail entry, and manufacturing survival.
- Operated through extreme uncertainty before the product or production process was stable.
