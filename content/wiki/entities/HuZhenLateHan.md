# 胡轸 / Hu Zhen (late Han)

胡轸 / Hu Zhen enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-955-guanyu-zhan-huaxiong-qishi-shi-wei-lishi-lndkrj-fnepye8iu-ifgyh-vzysf|Hanji 955]] as the Liangzhou-side commander [[DongZhuo|董卓]] sends against [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] after Sun Jian regroups at Yangren. The source pairs him with [[LyuBu|吕布]], whose Bingzhou troops and personal resentment make the operation unstable from inside.

The episode's Hu Zhen branch is a compact case of command fracture. Lü Bu first spreads a false report that Yangren is empty, then raises a false alarm that Sun Jian is attacking; the resulting disorder lets Sun Jian defeat Hu Zhen's force at [[YangrenBattleLateHan|阳人之战]] and kill [[HuaXiongLateHan|华雄]]. Hu Zhen therefore matters less as a full biography than as the commander whose army reveals Dong Zhuo-side factional weakness.
