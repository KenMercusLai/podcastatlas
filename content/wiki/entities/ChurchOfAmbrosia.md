# Church of Ambrosia

Church of Ambrosia is the broader organization named in [[a-trip-to-the-magic-mushroom-megachurch]] as the context from which [[ZydorChurch]] developed. The episode says the organization began as a marijuana church in 2019 before [[DaveHodges]] shifted the center of gravity toward mushrooms and the protection of the psychedelic movement.

Its wiki role is organizational continuity. It links Zydor's current [[SacramentDonationModel]], [[HighDosePsychedelicPractice]], and [[ReligiousControlledSubstanceExemption]] claims to an earlier cannabis-oriented church form rather than to a conventional congregation with a long institutional history.
