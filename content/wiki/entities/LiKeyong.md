# 李克用 / Li Keyong

Li Keyong / 李克用 is the Shatuo military leader used in [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]] to explain late Tang and Five Dynasties military politics. The episode links him to broad use of adoptive sons or假子 as a way to expand armed force and bind loyalty.

In the source, Li Keyong's world helps explain why [[LaterTang|后唐]] cannot be treated only as a dynastic title. It grows out of military households, private ties, ethnic frontier elements, and command networks.
