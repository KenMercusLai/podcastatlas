# Houston Dynamo

Houston Dynamo appears in [[ep-13-soccer-analytics-through-the-lens-of-coaching]] as [[BrunoSoccerCoach|Bruno]]'s recurring example for applying [[SportsAnalytics]] to real team questions. He uses the team to explain why poor attacking output should not be reduced to finishing alone: weak chance creation, final-third service, and playmaking can lower expected goals before a striker ever shoots.

The source also uses Houston Dynamo examples for tactical micro-adjustments, including player positioning, uncomfortable defenders, build-up errors, crossing areas, and the role of Hector Herrera as a possible attacking organizer. In the wiki, Houston Dynamo becomes a concrete case for [[ExpectedGoalsProcessMetric]], [[LiveMatchAnalytics]], and [[CoachingIntegratedSoccerAnalytics]].
