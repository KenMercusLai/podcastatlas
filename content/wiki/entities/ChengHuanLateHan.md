# 程奂 / Cheng Huan (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] introduces Cheng Huan as a [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] commander paired with [[ZhaoFuLateHan|赵浮]] in the last military warning before the Jizhou surrender. The two return from Mengjin with a large crossbow force and physically show that Han Fu still has organized troops.

Cheng Huan's source value is therefore not separate biography but evidence quality. His presence reinforces Zhao Fu's claim that [[YuanShao|袁绍]] is not yet secure enough to force surrender if Han Fu uses [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]]'s resources coherently.
