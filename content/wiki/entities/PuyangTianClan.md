# 濮阳田氏 / Puyang Tian Clan

濮阳田氏 / Puyang Tian Clan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-980-lvbu-xiansha-caocao-kankan-yi-ju-hua-ruhe-niuzhuan-lishi-lgggw3vmonu-3wvzabvg943v5qcg|Hanji 980]] as the local household whose false inside-support signal draws [[CaoCao|曹操]] into [[PuyangLateHan|濮阳]]. The episode does not develop a full family history; the clan's wiki role is functional, showing how local access and city information can become part of a battlefield trap.

In the source's reading, the danger is not only that Cao Cao is deceived. Once he enters the city and burns the east gate, the false signal turns into a command-exposure problem: [[LyuBu|吕布]] can press the attack, [[DianWei|典韦]] must stabilize the front, and Cao Cao survives only through [[CrisisDeceptionSurvival|临危欺敌求生]].
