## Overview

严助 / Yan Zhu enters the wiki as [[ZhuMaichenWesternHan|朱买臣]]'s early court benefactor and as the execution victim whose death gives Zhu's later opposition to [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]] a personal foundation.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-339-1-sanrenchenghu-ruhe-bisi-xihan-diyi-kuli-lowphplbvgugdyfag-sczpgggyrv|Hanji 339-1]] says Yan recommends the struggling Zhu Maichen to [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], after which Zhu receives a court appointment. The episode later places Yan in the political fallout from his friendship with Huainan king Liu An: Wudi reportedly regards the offense as insufficient for death, while Zhang Tang insists on execution. Zhu thereafter watches Zhang for actionable misconduct.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han court figure in the episode's account.
- Early patron who recommends Zhu Maichen to Han Wudi.
- Friend of Huainan king Liu An.
- Executed after the Huainan case in the episode's reconstruction.
- Becomes the personal grievance linking Zhu Maichen to Zhang Tang's downfall.

## Evidence

Patronage and execution:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-339-1-sanrenchenghu-ruhe-bisi-xihan-diyi-kuli-lowphplbvgugdyfag-sczpgggyrv|Hanji 339-1]] says Yan recommends Zhu Maichen at court and that Zhang Tang later insists on Yan's execution after the Huainan case.

Political afterlife:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-339-1-sanrenchenghu-ruhe-bisi-xihan-diyi-kuli-lowphplbvgugdyfag-sczpgggyrv|Hanji 339-1]] presents Yan's death as the decisive personal reason Zhu begins monitoring Zhang for wrongdoing.

## Qualifications

The raw transcript renders the name as “严柱”; this page normalizes it to 严助 because the Zhu Maichen recommendation and Huainan Liu An context identify that figure. The source does not reconstruct Yan's full career, the complete case against him, Zhang Tang's formal authority, Han Wudi's exact decision process, or whether Zhu's later conduct can be reduced to this grievance alone.

## What Changed

- Created the entity and bounded his role to patronage, Huainan-case implication, and Zhu Maichen's grievance.
