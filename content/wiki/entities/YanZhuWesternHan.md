## Overview

严助 / Yan Zhu enters the wiki as [[ZhuMaichenWesternHan|朱买臣]]'s early court benefactor and as the execution victim whose death gives Zhu's later opposition to [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]] a personal foundation.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-339-1-sanrenchenghu-ruhe-bisi-xihan-diyi-kuli-lowphplbvgugdyfag-sczpgggyrv|Hanji 339-1]] says Yan recommends the struggling Zhu Maichen to [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], after which Zhu receives a court appointment. The episode later places Yan in the political fallout from his friendship with Huainan king Liu An: Wudi reportedly regards the offense as insufficient for death, while Zhang Tang insists on execution. Zhu thereafter watches Zhang for actionable misconduct.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-286-canbei-wujie-qiannian-zhumaichen-xiuqi-de-fanzhuan-zhenxiang-2-lrgdpir3ez-hk_eptcq8q3-pzozr|Hanji 286 part 2]] supplies the earlier access mechanism. It calls Yan a favored fellow townsman already close to Wudi and says he obtains an audience for Zhu after Zhu has exhausted his provisions while waiting in Chang'an. Zhu's learning then earns appointment in the episode's account, making Yan an access broker rather than a substitute for demonstrated ability.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han court figure in the episode's account.
- Early patron who recommends Zhu Maichen to Han Wudi.
- Court insider and fellow townsman who reportedly secures Zhu's first imperial audience.
- Friend of Huainan king Liu An.
- Executed after the Huainan case in the episode's reconstruction.
- Becomes the personal grievance linking Zhu Maichen to Zhang Tang's downfall.

## Evidence

Patronage and execution:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-286-canbei-wujie-qiannian-zhumaichen-xiuqi-de-fanzhuan-zhenxiang-2-lrgdpir3ez-hk_eptcq8q3-pzozr|Hanji 286 part 2]] says Yan uses his access to secure Zhu an audience after the latter's prolonged wait in Chang'an.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-339-1-sanrenchenghu-ruhe-bisi-xihan-diyi-kuli-lowphplbvgugdyfag-sczpgggyrv|Hanji 339-1]] says Yan recommends Zhu Maichen at court and that Zhang Tang later insists on Yan's execution after the Huainan case.

Political afterlife:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-339-1-sanrenchenghu-ruhe-bisi-xihan-diyi-kuli-lowphplbvgugdyfag-sczpgggyrv|Hanji 339-1]] presents Yan's death as the decisive personal reason Zhu begins monitoring Zhang for wrongdoing.

## Qualifications

Hanji 339-1 renders the name as “严柱”; this page normalizes it to 严助 because the Zhu Maichen recommendation and Huainan Liu An context identify that figure, while Hanji 286 part 2 independently uses 严助 in the same patronage sequence. Neither source reconstructs Yan's full career, the exact audience mechanism, the complete case against him, Zhang Tang's formal authority, Han Wudi's decision process, or whether Zhu's later conduct can be reduced to this grievance alone.

## What Changed

- Added the Chang'an access sequence and distinguished Yan's brokerage from Zhu's displayed learning.

## Relationships

- [[ZhuMaichenWesternHan]] - beneficiary whose hostility to Zhang Tang is partly rooted in Yan's execution.
- [[ZhangTangWesternHan]] - official said to have insisted on Yan's execution.
- [[HanWudi]] - ruler who appoints Zhu after Yan's recommendation and reportedly hesitates to execute Yan.
- [[AccumulatedEnmityDefaultSuspicion]] - Yan's death supplies a stored grievance activated in the later coalition against Zhang.
