# Maoxiang / 猫箱

## Overview
Maoxiang / 猫箱 is an AI interactive-story product developed within [[ByteDanceFLOW]] and discussed by [[LiangChenqi|梁琛奇]] as part of his path toward AI-native entertainment.

## Current Profile
The product is presented as a creator-and-user system rather than only a romantic companion chat. Creators define characters or story frames, and users can interact across adventure, science-fiction, historical, romantic, and other settings for experiences that branch at runtime.

## Key Characteristics
- Grew from experiments with scripts, multiple roles, and interactive story demonstrations.
- Was conceived for multiple content genres rather than only romantic interaction.
- Initially focused on young women aged roughly 18 to 30 in the source's account.
- Uses creator-authored character and story structure as the anchor for AI-generated interaction.
- Produces subjective user-feedback data whose interpretation varies by audience, culture, and desired character experience.

## Evidence
- **Format and audience** - [[duihua-liangchenqi-douyin-maoxiang-chuangye-tamen-dou-gao-shengchanli-wo-xiang-yong-ai-chuangzao-kaixin-1-182-1]] describes Maoxiang's multi-genre intent, early audience choice, and movement from internal story demos toward a product category with visible market demand.
- **Creator leverage** - [[duihua-liangchenqi-douyin-maoxiang-chuangye-tamen-dou-gao-shengchanli-wo-xiang-yong-ai-chuangzao-kaixin-1-182-1]] uses Maoxiang as the clearest example of [[EmergentCreation]]: a creator may define a character or story framework that users then explore for hours through repeated model calls.
- **Model and data loop** - [[duihua-liangchenqi-douyin-maoxiang-chuangye-tamen-dou-gao-shengchanli-wo-xiang-yong-ai-chuangzao-kaixin-1-182-1]] records Liang's argument that character quality is subjective and therefore must be improved through diverse real-user feedback rather than a single objective answer key.

## Qualifications
- The page reflects one former team leader's retrospective account and not current product documentation or operating metrics.
- Audience, cost, training, retention, and data-moat claims remain source-scoped.
- Broad genre intent does not establish that every genre achieved comparable supply quality or user demand.

## What Changed

- Distinguished it from romance-only companion chat.
- Connected its creator framework to emergent creation and subjective-feedback training.
