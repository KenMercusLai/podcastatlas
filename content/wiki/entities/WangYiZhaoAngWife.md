## Overview

王异 / Wang Yi is the wife of [[ZhaoAngLateHan|赵昂]] in the Ji resistance sequence against [[MaChao|马超]]. She is presented as endorsing the decision to send her son Zhao Yue as a hostage because resistance to Ma Chao and redress for political wrongs outrank family safety in the episode's moral frame.

## Current Profile

Wang Yi's current profile is narrow but consequential. When Ma Chao demands Zhao Yue as security for Zhao Ang, she argues that even life itself would not be too high a price for the cause, and therefore supports sending the child. The episode praises her resolve alongside Jiang Xu's mother, but the durable synthesis retains both sides: her decision resists coercive rule, and it also accepts lethal risk on behalf of someone else.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Han woman associated with the resistance to Ma Chao around Ji.
- Active participant in the family decision rather than a passive figure in Zhao Ang's biography.
- Places public or political loyalty above the safety of her hostage son.
- Later remembered by the episode through a tradition of exemplary women, with the exact textual attribution requiring verification.
