## Overview
Nanjing wild boars / 南京野猪 are the urban and peri-urban boar case in [[ep282-bu-wanmei-de-gongcun-dang-yesheng-dongwu-chengwei-chengshi-xin-jumin-lg31-8lajmed4k4mhg5iz7hh8r1a]], used to complicate the media image of a "wild boar city" with field observation, crop conflict, campus traces, and culling controversy.

## Current Profile
The source portrays Nanjing's boars as highly present in public imagination but unevenly visible in daily life. Because they are crepuscular or nocturnal, many residents may never see them directly, while signs such as tracks, mud, flattened grass, crop damage, and campus movement still make them a practical management issue. Their case shifts the episode from cute urban wildlife toward bodily danger, rural loss, and contested lethal control.

## Key Characteristics
- Media visibility exceeds ordinary visibility because boars often move at dawn, dusk, and night.
- Feeding can teach dangerous approach behavior, including attention to food bags or bag sounds.
- The same species is experienced differently by urban residents, campus observers, and farmers facing crop loss.
- Control methods range from cages and fencing to hunting with dogs and stabbing, raising welfare and secondary-risk concerns.
- Ecological value remains part of the profile because boars can create water pits and movement paths for other animals.

## Evidence
- Visibility gap: [[ep282-bu-wanmei-de-gongcun-dang-yesheng-dongwu-chengwei-chengshi-xin-jumin-lg31-8lajmed4k4mhg5iz7hh8r1a]] says Qiu Lian found traces and eventually saw two boars after repeated night visits, while many residents may not see them.
- Feeding risk: [[ep282-bu-wanmei-de-gongcun-dang-yesheng-dongwu-chengwei-chengshi-xin-jumin-lg31-8lajmed4k4mhg5iz7hh8r1a]] discusses bread feeding and a possible conditioned response to plastic-bag sounds.
- Rural and urban conflict: [[ep282-bu-wanmei-de-gongcun-dang-yesheng-dongwu-chengwei-chengshi-xin-jumin-lg31-8lajmed4k4mhg5iz7hh8r1a]] connects boars to crop damage such as peanuts and corn as well as university and mountain sightings.
- Control controversy: [[ep282-bu-wanmei-de-gongcun-dang-yesheng-dongwu-chengwei-chengshi-xin-jumin-lg31-8lajmed4k4mhg5iz7hh8r1a]] describes trapping with corn, honey, and juice, plus dog-assisted hunting and stabbing as contested methods.
- Ecological role: [[ep282-bu-wanmei-de-gongcun-dang-yesheng-dongwu-chengwei-chengshi-xin-jumin-lg31-8lajmed4k4mhg5iz7hh8r1a]] notes that boar wallows can supply water and snow paths can help other animals move.

## Qualifications
The episode questions whether some density-based killing standards are scientifically grounded, but it does not provide enough evidence to settle the proper local carrying capacity. It also separates Nanjing's urban perception from broader rural conflict, where economic damage can justify different urgency.
