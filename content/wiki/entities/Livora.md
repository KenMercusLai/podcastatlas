# Livora

Livora is the lending-as-a-service platform described by [[SharminDataScienceWithSam|Sharmin]] in [[ep-28-the-ai-revolution-redefining-healthcare-financing]]. The source presents Livora as a funding-access layer for independent healthcare clinics, especially women-owned practices, that need capital for growth but face slow bank processes, personal guarantees, documentation burden, or underwriting criteria that miss current demand.

Livora's claimed product role is to combine [[AIEnabledLoanDocumentAnalysis]], [[ClinicLenderMatching]], [[BorrowerReadinessFinancing]], and [[ConsentBasedLoanDataSharing]]. Clinics provide basic information and financial documents, the platform compares the request with lender criteria, shares masked snapshots for soft quotes, and connects the clinic with a chosen lender after consent.

The source should be treated as founder-perspective evidence. It explains the product thesis and operating model but does not independently validate Livora's security architecture, lender obligations, funding terms, approval rates, or borrower outcomes.
