## Overview

公孙禄 / Gongsun Lu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-604-xihan-wangmang-duoquan-hou-ruhe-qingxi-quanli-geju-ltwscyyiobc1ocvgti3fd-deajru|Hanji 604]] as the Western Han left general who joins [[HeWuWesternHan|何武]] in trying to keep the Da Sima office from becoming another outer-relative monopoly after [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]'s death. He is distinct from [[GongsunLuXinOfficial|公孙禄 / Gongsun Lu (Xin official)]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 604 gives Gongsun Lu a narrow but clear political role. He and He Wu argue that earlier Lu, Huo, and Shangguan outer-relative precedents nearly endangered the Liu house, so the post-Aidi court should not again let consort kin monopolize top command. Their mutual recommendation plan makes Gongsun Lu both recommender and recommended candidate.

The plan fails once Wang Zhengjun appoints Wang Mang as Da Sima. In the episode's interpretation, the failed recommendation matters because it records Gongsun Lu as someone who saw and resisted Wang-family concentration before Wang Mang's cleanup cycle began.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han left general involved in the post-Aidi Da Sima contest.
- Diagnoses outer-relative monopoly as a dynastic danger.
- Mutually recommends He Wu as part of an office-balancing attempt.
- Loses to Wang Mang's Wang-family and reputation advantage.
- Source-scoped as an early exposure figure in Wang Mang's return.
