## Overview

公孙禄 / Gongsun Lu is synthesized as a Western Han left general whose current wiki role now spans two late-[[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] decision points. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-591-1-yichang-bingjia-ruhe-xianxie-yinbao-liangguo-zhizhan-lrq-xdhvrnuarzwgrykp7j-x8j-a|Hanji 591-1]], he objects to turning a [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] chanyu's illness-delayed visit into deceptive frontier escalation. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-604-xihan-wangmang-duoquan-hou-ruhe-qingxi-quanli-geju-ltwscyyiobc1ocvgti3fd-deajru|Hanji 604]], he joins [[HeWuWesternHan|何武]] in trying to keep the Da Sima office from becoming another outer-relative monopoly after Aidi's death. He is distinct from [[GongsunLuXinOfficial|公孙禄 / Gongsun Lu (Xin official)]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 591-1 gives Gongsun Lu the first current layer. When [[XiFuGongWesternHan|息夫躬]] proposes using false envoys, intentional leakage, and Wusun-hostage pressure against Xiongnu, Gongsun Lu objects that Han should rely on visible authority, credible relations, and trust-preserving conduct rather than speculation and covert trickery. His argument treats the chanyu's illness report and explanatory envoy as diplomatically sufficient unless firmer evidence appears.

Hanji 604 gives Gongsun Lu the second current layer. He and He Wu argue that earlier Lu, Huo, and Shangguan outer-relative precedents nearly endangered the Liu house, so the post-Aidi court should not again let consort kin monopolize top command. Their mutual recommendation plan makes Gongsun Lu both recommender and recommended candidate.

The plan fails once Wang Zhengjun appoints Wang Mang as Da Sima. In the episode's interpretation, the failed recommendation matters because it records Gongsun Lu as someone who saw and resisted Wang-family concentration before Wang Mang's cleanup cycle began.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han left general involved in both a pre-death frontier debate and the post-Aidi Da Sima contest.
- Objects to deceptive escalation when a Xiongnu illness report is treated as hidden threat.
- Frames credible diplomacy as preferable to speculative manipulation.
- Diagnoses outer-relative monopoly as a dynastic danger after Aidi's death.
- Mutually recommends He Wu as part of an office-balancing attempt.
- Loses to Wang Mang's Wang-family and reputation advantage.
- Source-scoped as an early exposure figure in both late-Aidi remonstrance and Wang Mang's return.
