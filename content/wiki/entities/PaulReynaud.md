# Paul Reynaud

## Overview

Paul Reynaud was the French prime minister during the final phase of the [[FallOfFrance1940]], who opposed a separate armistice and sought to continue the war from abroad.

## Current Profile

[[622-the-nazis-at-war-the-fall-of-france-part-3-glt6447940829]] presents Reynaud as constrained by a cabinet and military leadership increasingly committed to ending the fighting. He supported continued resistance from North Africa, maintained France's undertaking not to seek a separate peace, and accepted the proposed Franco-British union as an alternative to capitulation. When ministers rejected that course on 16 June, he resigned; he then enabled [[CharlesDeGaulle]] to leave for London with money from secret funds.

## Key Characteristics

- Opposed a separate French armistice with Germany.
- Sought to preserve French belligerence from overseas territory.
- Supported the proposed Franco-British union during the Bordeaux crisis.
- Lost the cabinet struggle to the armistice faction.
- Helped de Gaulle continue resistance from London after his own resignation.
