# Asante Queen Mothers

## Overview

Asante queen mothers are the political institution the episode uses to show female authority as a formal part of Asante kingship and counsel.

## Current Profile

The episode presents the Asante queen mother as a major political figure in a matrilineal setting. She can select or veto a king, instruct the Asantehene before councillors, and stand near him in council as a protected and authoritative figure. The role is not only symbolic in the source: it is part of how kingship is made, checked, and advised. [[YaaAsantewaa]] provides the best-known martial example through the [[WarOfTheGoldenStool]].

## Key Characteristics

- Political institution inside [[Asante]] kingship rather than a one-off female exception.
- Authority to select or veto a king in the source's account.
- Capacity to advise or instruct the Asantehene.
- Protected public standing near the king in council.
- Still present today in the episode's account, though less publicly visible and more flexible in kinship terms.

- Counsel role: [[317-african-amazons-glt5973082485]] says she can instruct the Asantehene even before his councillors.
- Council standing: [[317-african-amazons-glt5973082485]] says she stands near the king in council as a protected and authoritative figure.
- Contemporary qualification: [[317-african-amazons-glt5973082485]] says there is still a queen mother, though the role is less publicly visible and more flexible in kinship terms.
- Martial example: [[317-african-amazons-glt5973082485]] presents [[YaaAsantewaa]] as the most famous martial queen mother.

## Qualifications

The source gives a podcast-level account and does not define every local variation, lineage rule, office-holder responsibility, or modern constitutional boundary. The role should be read as a specific institutional case, not proof that all matrilineal systems are matriarchies.
