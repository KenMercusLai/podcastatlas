# Bond of Association

The Bond of Association appears in [[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] as the political security instrument [[WilliamCecil|William Cecil / Lord Burleigh]] and [[FrancisWalsingham]] drew up after [[FrancisThrockmorton]]'s execution. It stated that anyone responsible for [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]]'s death would be ineligible to succeed her.

The episode says a later provision allowed such a person to be put to death even if they did not know about the assassination plot. [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]] was not named, but the hosts treat the bond as clearly aimed at her because her succession claim made every assassination or invasion scheme politically explosive.

Thousands of loyal subjects signed the bond, and Mary herself signed it on 5 January 1585. In the wiki, the bond bridges the [[ThrockmortonPlot]] and the later [[ActForTheQueensSafety|Act for the Queen's Safety]] by turning claimant danger into a broader loyalty and exclusion framework.
