# 冯喜 / Feng Xi (Warring States)

冯喜 / Feng Xi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-38-gao-jianzhi-de-zhangyi-bu-yiban-lobhcpz679psy54k0iduccqkfcrn]] as [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s retainer. After [[QiState|齐国]] attacks [[WeiState|魏国]], Zhang Yi sends Feng Xi under a [[ChuState|楚国]] diplomatic identity to tell the Qi ruler how Zhang Yi had earlier persuaded [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] to let him go to Wei.

His role is small but structurally important. Feng Xi is the messenger who turns a hidden design into an open signal, making Qi ask whether continuing the war would merely fulfill Zhang Yi's plan. For the wiki, he belongs mainly to [[YinmouYangmouConversion|阴谋阳谋转换]] and [[ZonghengjiaDiplomacy|纵横家外交]] rather than to a standalone biography.
