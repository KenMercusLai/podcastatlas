# 李旎 / Li Ni

Li Ni / 李旎 appears in [[no-214-xunzhao-tonglei-xiaohongshu-bilibili-yiji-wuhuabamen-de-naxie-shequ-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-26-1005977305]] as one of the key executives brought into [[Bilibili]] as the company moved beyond its early hobbyist stage. The episode groups [[XuYiBilibili|徐逸]], [[ChenRuiBilibili|陈睿]], and Li Ni as Bilibili's early "三剑客", then describes Chen and Li as later dual management anchors.

Her source role is most visible in the ad-format discussion: the episode cites her 2022 earnings-call argument that traditional pre-roll inventory was a small part of the ad market and not worth damaging Bilibili's experience for. That makes Li relevant to [[CreatorTrustAdvertising]].
