## Overview

范仲淹 / Fan Zhongyan enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-467-guanchang-li-jiang-zhenhua-shuo-shihua-you-duo-nan-ls_zlyqolvpfttspah8wery4e3fs|Hanji 467]] as a second contrast to [[GongYuWesternHan|贡禹]]'s selective truth-telling. The episode presents him as an official who preserves public-duty speech despite age, demotion, and political cost.

## Current Profile

The source uses Fan Zhongyan as a moral vocabulary for official responsibility rather than as a full Song political profile. His cited stance is that an official should prefer speaking and risking punishment to living through silence when public harm is visible. The episode connects that stance to the broader ideal of worrying before the realm worries and rejoicing only after the realm rejoices.

## Key Characteristics

- Song official used as a model of official conscience and direct warning.
- Cited for the stance that speaking under risk is preferable to silent survival.
- Connected to long-term public-duty identity rather than one-time emotional bluntness.
- Functions as a contrast case to safe half-truths and "为官不为."
