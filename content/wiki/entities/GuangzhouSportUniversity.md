## Overview
广州体育学院 / Guangzhou Sport University appears in the source as the public sports-education institution that acquired [[EvergrandeFootballSchool]]. The episode treats this as a comparatively good outcome because the football-school assets remain inside an education and sports-training system rather than being dispersed through real-estate or local-financing vehicles.

## Current Profile
The university's current wiki role is institutional stabilizer. It is not presented as simply buying land; it is described as preserving students, staff, teams, coaching, and campus life while potentially giving academy students a clearer educational ladder from basic schooling to undergraduate sports pathways.

## Key Characteristics
- Sports-education institution that acquired the former Evergrande football school.
- Stabilizing owner in the source because it can keep football and education functions together.
- Potential pathway provider for academy students whose culture grades or football-specialty performance may later support university admission.

## Evidence
- Acquisition role: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] says Guangzhou Sport University bought the school for 600 million yuan.
- Campus transition: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] reports that old Evergrande signage and mottos had been replaced by university markings and that renovation was underway.
- Continuity logic: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] says the source's guest viewed the university takeover as preferable to a city-investment, developer, or non-education owner.

## Qualifications
The source gives a positive near-term assessment but does not document the university's detailed financing, governance plan, admissions rules, or long-term academy budget.
