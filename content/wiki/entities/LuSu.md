# 鲁肃 / Lu Su

## Overview
鲁肃 / Lu Su is a Jiangdong adviser whose current profile joins resource-backed independence, long-range Yangtze strategy, post-Changban alliance brokerage, and role-sensitive resistance to surrender.

## Current Profile
Lu Su first appears as someone willing to move household and future away from [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s failing path toward Jiangdong. Hanji 1052 shows that this choice rests on an independent base: he is wealthy, organizes followers, trains men, and reaches [[SunQuan|孙权]] through [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]]'s recommendation. His [[TaShangCeBipartiteStrategy|榻上策二分天下]] rejects immediate Han restoration, centers Jiangdong and the Yangtze, and sequences expansion through Huang Zu and Liu Biao.

Hanji 1099 makes Lu Su an alliance broker after Changban. He does not ask defeated [[LiuBei|刘备]] to admit dependence. He contrasts Sun Quan's capacity with [[WuJuLateHan|吴巨]]'s weakness, presents cooperation as shared enterprise, and tells [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] that he is [[ZhugeJin|诸葛瑾]]'s friend. The pitch combines material comparison, dignity protection, and relationship signaling.

Hanji 1101 shows the same attention to audience-specific incentives under Cao Cao's threat. Lu Su privately distinguishes the portable career of an ordinary minister from Sun Quan's non-portable ruler status. His counsel therefore spans both sides of coalition choice: he can make alliance acceptable to a weakened external actor and make surrender unacceptable to an exposed ruler.

## Key Characteristics
- Rejects a failing patron path and relocates resources toward a more viable political base.
- Converts private wealth into organized people and military preparedness.
- Offers long-range strategy grounded in Jiangdong and Yangtze control.
- Brokers alliance by pairing material comparison with face-preserving language.
- Uses personal networks as diplomatic infrastructure through the Zhuge Jin connection.
- Reframes decisions around the specific exposure of the person who must choose.
- Retains Sun Quan's trust despite criticism from Zhang Zhao.

## Qualifications
The current profile is still bounded to four popular-history episodes rather than a complete career. The two-part天下 plan is early strategic advice, not a final prediction. Hanji 1099 attributes detailed rhetorical intent and emotional effect to Lu Su, while Hanji 1101 stops before his complete answer and Sun Quan's decision. The Zhuge Jin friendship is evidence of a relationship signal, not proof that personal ties alone secured the coalition.

## What Changed
- Added Lu Su's post-Changban role as the broker who first redirects Liu Bei toward Sun Quan.
- Connected his alliance pitch to his later role-specific surrender analysis.
- Added dignity protection and personal-network signaling to his persuasion profile.
