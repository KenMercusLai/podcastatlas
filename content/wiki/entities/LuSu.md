# 鲁肃 / Lu Su

## Overview
鲁肃 / Lu Su is a future Eastern Wu adviser whose current wiki profile now runs from leaving [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s failing appointment path with [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] to presenting [[SunQuan|孙权]] with the [[TaShangCeBipartiteStrategy|榻上策二分天下]].

## Current Profile
Lu Su first enters the wiki as an alignment marker: capable people are not only judging Yuan Shu negatively, but moving households and futures toward Sun Ce's Jiangdong base. Hanji 1052 turns him into a strategic actor. It depicts him as wealthy, organized, and militarily prepared, then shows Zhou Yu recommending him to Sun Quan.

In Sun Quan's first rulerly conversations, Lu Su refuses a simple Han-restoration premise. He argues that the Han house cannot be revived and Cao Cao cannot be quickly destroyed, so Sun Quan should hold Jiangdong, watch for northern distraction, take Huang Zu and Liu Biao's line, control the Yangtze, and only then pursue imperial enterprise. The source frames this as an earlier two-part天下 plan that later events qualify.

## Key Characteristics
- Rejects Yuan Shu's appointment path and relocates with Zhou Yu toward Sun Ce's Jiangdong base.
- Has independent resources rather than appearing only as a dependent client.
- Turns wealth into organization by selling assets, recruiting men, and training soldiers.
- Becomes visible to Sun Quan through Zhou Yu's recommendation.
- Offers a strategic program that treats Han restoration as no longer practical and centers Jiangdong plus the Yangtze.
- Is valued by Sun Quan despite Zhang Zhao's criticism that he is young and careless.

## Qualifications
The current Lu Su profile is still source-bounded. Hanji 1052 gives a strong strategic entrance, but it is not a full career synthesis. The episode also notes that later events, especially the Red Cliffs branch and the formation of three-kingdom order, qualify Lu Su's two-part天下 plan; the page therefore preserves the plan as early strategic advice rather than a final map of the period.
