# 鲁肃 / Lu Su

鲁肃 / Lu Su enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1028-xiaobawang-sunce-liwei-chengba-jiangdong-lgur-3fmpr55wy3aji-0rm38y7x|Hanji 1028]] as one of the future Eastern Wu figures who leaves [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s orbit after Yuan Shu's imperial project starts visibly failing. Yuan Shu tries to appoint him as Dongcheng county chief, while [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] is assigned to Juchao, but both judge Yuan Shu as unable to succeed.

The source's Lu Su material is compact. It says Lu Su crosses the Yangtze with Zhou Yu toward [[SunCe|孙策]], and then moves his household to [[QuALateHan|曲阿]]. His current wiki role is therefore an early alignment marker: capable local actors are no longer only rejecting Yuan Shu in words; they are relocating themselves and their households toward Sun Ce's Jiangdong base.
