# Adrian (The Residency founder)

Adrian is one of the resident founders interviewed in [[tanfang-hacker-house-guigu-nianqingren-zhengzai-banjin-ai-chuangye-sushe-s10e10-f0870843-fd16-49f5-8f24-80281f9fff77]]. The source says he has a Berkeley Haas MBA background, has worked across music, banking, and finance, and is building a P2P payment-network protocol and AI cross-border payment idea.

In the episode, Adrian's value case is solo-founder time pressure. Because he has many company responsibilities, compressing fundraising into a few weeks through [[TheResidency]] is attractive. That makes him a source-scoped example of [[FounderLifestyleInfrastructure]], [[FounderNetworkArbitrage]], and [[StartupPitchCompression]].
