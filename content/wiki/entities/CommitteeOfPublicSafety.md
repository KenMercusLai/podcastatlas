# Committee of Public Safety

## Overview

The Committee of Public Safety was the emergency executive body created by the National Convention on 6 April 1793 amid military defeat, revolt, scarcity, and fear of internal betrayal.

## Current Profile

The source presents the committee as one part of an improvised emergency architecture rather than the execution of a pre-existing Terror blueprint. [[GeorgesDanton|Danton]] initially dominated its nine members, but the political climate after [[JeanPaulMarat|Marat]]'s assassination displaced his pragmatism; [[MaximilienRobespierre|Robespierre]] joined seventeen days after Danton's removal and became its defining later figure.

## Key Characteristics

- It was created to coordinate military and political emergency response.
- Its initial form had nine members, with Danton as the dominant figure.
- Its meaning changed as compromise became suspect and revolutionary fear intensified.
- It operated alongside the [[RevolutionaryTribunal]], surveillance committees, and deputies on mission.
- It illustrates how temporary crisis powers can combine into durable coercive capacity.

## Qualifications

- This source covers the committee's formation and immediate leadership shift, not its full later record during the Reign of Terror.
- The episode interprets institutional accumulation as a pathway to terror without claiming that every emergency measure was designed from the outset for that end.

## What Changed

- The wiki now distinguishes the committee's emergency origin from its later identification with Robespierre and the Terror.
- Danton-to-Robespierre leadership change is located inside the post-assassination radicalization sequence.
