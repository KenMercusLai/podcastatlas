# 东尼 / Tony (content engineer)

东尼 / Tony is the former senior media worker interviewed in [[e245-cangzai-damoxing-beihoude-xinwenren-gptmen-de-huifu-shi-zheyang-xie-chulaide-5aeaeb64-9165-4271-9884-23329b511e11]] about [[ContentEngineering]]. The episode says he worked on model-content internationalization at [[Meta]]'s superintelligence lab and later became a senior model designer at [[GoogleDeepMind]].

His main source role is to translate media practice into AI design. Tony argues that journalism trains audience awareness, context setup, what can and cannot be said, objectivity under constraint, and flexible follow-up questioning. He treats those skills as almost directly portable to [[AIAnswerEvaluation]], [[AIInteractionInternationalization]], and model dialogue design rather than as soft skills adjacent to engineering.

The episode also uses Tony's AI host experiment as a practical case. He tried a voice agent for podcast-like conversation, found generic [[ChatGPT]] and [[Gemini]] voice behavior too assistant-like, and improved the result by decomposing past podcast questions and using retrieved examples instead of only adding more rules. That makes his work a bridge between [[ContextEngineering]], [[VoiceInteraction]], and [[HumanAgentCollaboration]].
