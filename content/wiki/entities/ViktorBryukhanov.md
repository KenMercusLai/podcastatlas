# Viktor Bryukhanov / 布留哈诺夫

[[ViktorBryukhanov|Viktor Bryukhanov / 布留哈诺夫]] appears in [[04-qieernuobeili-de-wuye-woyao-jin-jianyu-le-530594843]] as the director of the [[ChernobylNuclearPowerPlant]]. The episode presents him as a young, politically reliable, capable engineering cadre whose background was electrical rather than nuclear, making him a useful figure for reading the plant as both a technical and bureaucratic project.

His page matters because the episode resists treating him as merely a guilty individual. Bryukhanov is responsible as the plant leader, but he also stands inside [[SovietUnion|Soviet]] construction pressure, material shortages, propaganda, and the expectation that visible targets would be met despite hidden safety compromises.

The title phrase "我要进监狱了" is attached to his recognition that the accident would become a criminal and political responsibility case. That places him inside [[PostDisasterTruthAndResponsibility]] rather than only operational error.
