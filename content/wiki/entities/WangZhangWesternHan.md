## Overview

王章 / Wang Zhang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-544-yuangong-yue-zhongcheng-laoban-yue-huangkong-lk52zm46585z2noqxmtfn9kpwek2|Hanji 544]] as the Jingzhao Yin remembered as plain and upright, then killed after criticizing [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-520-daji-duishou-weishenme-yao-xian-faqi-yulunzhan-lh1ibhc9xsihtrh6mhzznqq9ioso|Hanji 520]] backfills the immediate aftermath: his charges are kept secret, the public suspects wrongful death for direct speech, and [[DuQingWesternHan|杜清]] uses that suspicion to argue for reopening remonstrance.

## Current Profile

The sources use Wang Zhang as the concrete case behind two related warnings. Wang Zhang had a reputation for frugality and uprightness, and his criticism of Wang Feng made him politically exposed. Once Wang Feng has him killed, other officials fall silent.

Hanji 520 makes the reputational problem more explicit. Because the formal charges are not publicly disclosed, people can read Wang Zhang as a man punished for direct remonstrance rather than as someone transparently convicted of a known crime. Wang Zhang therefore functions as a negative remonstrance precedent. His death teaches everyone nearby that honest correction may be fatal, gives Du Qing grounds to call for public speech repair, and gives Mei Fu grounds to warn Chengdi that blocking speech lets outer-relative power grow without correction.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official presented as plain, loyal, and upright.
- Criticizes Wang Feng and becomes the episode's punished-speaker case.
- His death produces a wider silence among officials.
- Secret charges make his case a public-trust problem.
- Becomes the reason Du Qing recommends visibly reopening remonstrance channels.
- Serves as the immediate trigger for Mei Fu's warning to Chengdi.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 544 and Hanji 520 and does not resolve Wang Zhang's full prosecution record, precise charges, biography, or the complete Wang-family factional context. Hanji 520 specifically preserves the gap between secret formal charges and public interpretation; the wiki therefore treats wrongful-death belief as politically important without independently settling legal guilt.
