## Overview

王章 / Wang Zhang is the Jingzhao Yin remembered as plain and upright, then killed after criticizing [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-518-jiemi-xihan-zuihou-de-zhengchen-wangzhang-lg8zeudpelxedglsyqp_slo9piva|Hanji 518]] supplies the biographical and institutional prelude: poverty under an ox blanket, an earlier fall after confronting [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]], return under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]], refusal to become Wang Feng's dependent, and a plan to bypass the controlled memorial route with a sealed submission. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-519-aiguozhe-wangzhang-de-si-yuanyugen-cuo-le-zhuzi-lt5hzlh9t-gryds1lpn0ieqw3cvn|Hanji 519]] supplies the confrontation and reversal, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-520-daji-duishou-weishenme-yao-xian-faqi-yulunzhan-lh1ibhc9xsihtrh6mhzznqq9ioso|Hanji 520]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-544-yuangong-yue-zhongcheng-laoban-yue-huangkong-lk52zm46585z2noqxmtfn9kpwek2|Hanji 544]] trace the public suspicion and court silence that follow.

## Current Profile

The four sources make Wang Zhang both an upright remonstrant and a political-risk failure case. Hanji 518 shows that his directness is durable rather than episodic: he survives poverty, resists Shi Xian, accepts Wang Feng's recommendations without accepting dependence, and seeks a sealed route around Wang Feng's document control. It also makes [[WangZhangWifeWesternHan|his wife]] a repeated counterweight, first restoring his ambition and later warning that principle without a viable path can destroy the household. Hanji 519 says his diagnosis is strong enough to move Chengdi, but his vulnerability lies in treating private imperial agreement as durable authority. Once the plan leaks, Wang Feng's resignation and [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]'s pressure cause Chengdi to protect the family alliance and sacrifice his adviser.

Hanji 520 makes the reputational aftermath explicit. Because the formal charges are not publicly disclosed, people read Wang Zhang as a man punished for direct remonstrance rather than transparently convicted of a known crime. Hanji 544 carries that interpretation forward through Mei Fu. Wang Zhang therefore functions as a negative remonstrance precedent: his death teaches officials that correct advice may be fatal when the ruler cannot protect the adviser, gives Du Qing grounds to call for visible speech repair, and gives Mei Fu grounds to warn that blocking correction lets outer-relative power grow unchecked.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official whose poverty, Confucian learning, and earlier clash with Shi Xian frame a durable commitment to direct speech.
- Diagnoses Wang Feng's concentrated power and asks Chengdi to replace him.
- Uses a sealed memorial to bypass an ordinary document route controlled by Wang Feng.
- Recommends Feng Yewang after Chengdi privately accepts the plan.
- Misjudges Chengdi's ability to withstand Wang-family and maternal pressure.
- Dies in prison while his wife and children are exiled.
- Becomes a punished-speaker precedent that produces public suspicion, official silence, and later demands for protected remonstrance.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 544, Hanji 520, Hanji 519, and Hanji 518 and does not resolve Wang Zhang's full prosecution record, precise charges, biography, or the complete Wang-family factional context. Hanji 518 and Hanji 519 supply a strongly moralized account that admires his integrity while criticizing his odds; neither independently establishes every motive, office detail, or legal fact. Hanji 520 preserves the gap between secret formal charges and public interpretation, so the wiki treats wrongful-death belief as politically important without independently settling legal guilt.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 518's poverty, Shi Xian conflict, Wang Feng patronage, Sili Xiaowei service, and sealed-memorial strategy.
- Reframed his wife as a repeated source of both ambition and political-risk judgment.
- Sharpened the distinction between Wang Zhang's integrity and the effectiveness of acting through weak authority against a controlled information route.
