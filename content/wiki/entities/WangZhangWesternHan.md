## Overview

王章 / Wang Zhang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-544-yuangong-yue-zhongcheng-laoban-yue-huangkong-lk52zm46585z2noqxmtfn9kpwek2|Hanji 544]] as the Jingzhao Yin remembered as plain and upright, then killed after criticizing [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]]. In this source, his death is less a full biography than a signal event: punished direct speech makes the whole court afraid to speak.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Wang Zhang as the concrete case behind [[MeiFuWesternHan|梅福]]'s memorial. Wang Zhang had a reputation for frugality and uprightness, and his criticism of Wang Feng made him politically exposed. Once Wang Feng has him killed, other officials fall silent.

Wang Zhang therefore functions as a negative remonstrance precedent. His death shows how the removal of one critic can damage more than one career: it teaches everyone nearby that honest correction may be fatal, and it gives Mei Fu grounds to warn Chengdi that blocking speech lets outer-relative power grow without correction.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official presented as plain, loyal, and upright.
- Criticizes Wang Feng and becomes the episode's punished-speaker case.
- His death produces a wider silence among officials.
- Serves as the immediate trigger for Mei Fu's warning to Chengdi.
