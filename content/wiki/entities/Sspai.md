# 少数派 / Sspai

## Overview
少数派 / Sspai is the digital-life media and community platform described by [[LaoMaiSspai|老麦 / Michael]] in the No.229 [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] source.

## Current Profile
The source presents 少数派 as an evolution from early mobile-app recommendation into a broader "digital life guide." It began with carrier content demand and the mobile-app boom, using app selection, hands-on testing, and usage explanation to help users navigate iOS and Android software.

Its distinctive position in the episode is the conversion of content trust into a creator and product loop. The platform pays authors, supports deeper columns and courses, sells memberships, works with brands, participates in product co-creation, and experiments with offline spaces, while still defining its value through a relatively dense community rather than mass-market feed scale.

## Key Characteristics
- It began as a high-quality application recommendation and usage media product.
- It expanded from apps into hardware, workflows, productivity, life services, macOS, iOS, shortcuts, and digital-life practices.
- It established author systems and payment early, treating contributor compensation as normal rather than optional.
- It uses paid columns, courses, memberships, and advertising as part of a content business loop.
- It treats creative users, authors, brands, and products as a community asset for co-creation.
- It positions "niche" as high-density user quality rather than smallness for its own sake.
