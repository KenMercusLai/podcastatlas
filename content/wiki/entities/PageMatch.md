# PageMatch

PageMatch is the [[Spotify]] feature described in [[tech-20260417-0417-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260417-0417-mp-tech-pod-128]] as matching a reader's place between an audiobook and a physical book. The feature matters because it makes the physical book part of a platform workflow rather than treating print and audio as separate habits.

In the wiki, PageMatch supports [[PhysicalBookPlatformBridge]]: Spotify can encourage offline reading while keeping the purchase path and audiobook continuity inside its app ecosystem.

## Connections
- [[Spotify]] - platform offering the feature.
- [[BookshopOrg|Bookshop.org]] - physical-book purchase partner in the same source.
- [[PhysicalBookPlatformBridge]] - concept that PageMatch exemplifies.
- [[ReadingAsLifeExperience]] - adjacent reading concept because the feature preserves a physical reading path rather than replacing it with audio alone.
