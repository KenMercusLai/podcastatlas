# 胡文才 / Hu Wencai (late Han)

胡文才 / Hu Wencai enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-966-meiyou-yexin-budong-quanmou-jiu-bie-dang-lingdao-lldn3hi1gl5v3axvklwakgf6vx3u|Hanji 966]] as a Liangzhou-linked figure whom [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] tries to use during the crisis created by [[JiaXu|贾诩]]'s advice to [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and other [[DongZhuo|董卓]] remnant commanders.

The episode's claim is narrow but important: Wang Yun has the right instinct that Liangzhou influence could matter, but his arrogant reception of Hu Wencai and [[YangZhengxiuLateHan|杨整修]] turns a possible mediation channel into a push toward the Li-Guo advance on [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]]. Hu Wencai therefore anchors the episode's warning that political networks do not work when status performance contradicts the practical need to ask for help.
