# Hugh Parsons

## Overview

Hugh Parsons is the Springfield brickmaker accused of witchcraft by neighbors and by his wife, [[MaryParsons]], in the early 1650s.

## Current Profile

Hugh's case connects material dependence to reputational danger. Brick chimneys made his labor valuable for safety and status, but disputes over work, threats, silence, and a brooding public image made neighbors who depended on him more willing to interpret misfortune as occult retaliation.

## Key Characteristics

- He entered [[SpringfieldMassachusetts|Springfield]] as a skilled brickmaker.
- His work on bricks and chimneys made households materially dependent on him.
- Quarrels and threats during transactions contributed to a dangerous-neighbor reputation.
- Marital breakdown and the death of a child preceded Mary's accusation against him.
- Boston authorities did not find the evidence sufficient to execute him for witchcraft.
