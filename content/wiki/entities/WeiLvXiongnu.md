## Overview

魏律 / Wei Lü enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-389-2-lishi-jiemi-shui-shi-xie-jiantaoshu-zuiduo-de-wang-lvnamm5dza3s9ao8ofcshhiynsu1|Hanji 389-2]] as the Xiongnu-aligned figure who becomes jealous of captured [[LiGuangliWesternHan|李广利]]'s honor among [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] and helps bring about Li Guangli's ritual killing. In this source, he functions as a court-jealousy and ritual-manipulation actor.

## Current Profile

The episode does not give Wei Lü's full biography. It introduces him after Li Guangli's capture, when Li Guangli is allegedly respected in Xiongnu territory. Wei Lü's jealousy is paired with the illness of the chanyu's mother, the Great Yanzhi. He instructs a Hu shaman to say that the dead old chanyu demands Li Guangli as a sacrifice to the land spirit, creating a ritual pretext for political elimination.

## Key Characteristics

- Xiongnu-aligned actor in the source's Li Guangli death scene.
- Jealous of Li Guangli's honored status after capture.
- Uses illness and shamanic speech as a political weapon.
- Helps convert court rivalry into sacrificial killing.
- Appears here as a source-scoped episode figure, not as a full biographical subject.
