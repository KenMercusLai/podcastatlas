# Tania Lombrozo

## Overview
Tania Lombrozo is a psychologist represented through research and public explanation of how people build, prefer, and revise explanations.

## Current Profile
In [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-d08500083dda91b814b053f30bd7b557-87666361|The Story of Stories]], Lombrozo presents explanation as model building. It can organize observations, improve learning, and guide action, but the same preference for coherence, simplicity, and breadth can protect stereotypes, political identity, and conspiratorial accounts when rival explanations are not tested.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats explanations as models that support prediction, response, and knowledge transfer.
- Uses self-explanation to connect learning with metacognitive calibration.
- Distinguishes useful explanatory virtues from seductive qualities that do not establish truth.
- Recommends detailed explanation and rival-account generation as checks on confidence.
