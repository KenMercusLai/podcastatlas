# 刘辟 / Liu Pi (Runan late Han)

## Overview
刘辟 / Liu Pi is a Runan Yellow Turban remnant leader in the Hanji 1045 branch of the pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]] Cao-Yuan contest. The episode's transcript renders the name as "刘璧"; this page normalizes the figure as Liu Pi while preserving that source-scoped caution.

## Current Profile
Liu Pi represents the afterlife of Yellow Turban forces as local military pressure during the [[CaoCao|曹操]]-[[YuanShao|袁绍]] contest. His significance in the current wiki is mainly that he aligns with Yuan Shao and helps create recruitment pressure against [[LiTongLateHan|李通]].

## Key Characteristics
- Liu Pi is presented as part of the Runan Yellow Turban remnant network.
- He rebels against Cao Cao in a moment when Yuan Shao is looking for southern pressure points.
- His branch works with Yuan Shao's attempt to pull Li Tong away from Cao Cao.
- He illustrates how old rebel formations can be reactivated as instruments in elite warlord competition.

## Qualifications
The name normalization is source-scoped: the transcript has "刘璧," while the wiki uses 刘辟 / Liu Pi for the historical Runan Yellow Turban figure. This page does not merge him with [[LiuBiWuKing|刘濞]], whose normalized key and historical context are different.
