# Karissa Bodnar

## Overview
Karissa Bodnar is the founder of [[ThriveCausemetics]], introduced through a [[HowIBuiltThis]] interview with [[GuyRaz]] about building a mission-driven beauty company.

## Current Profile
The source presents Bodnar as a product-led beauty founder whose mission came from personal loss but whose operating system depends on formulation, customer feedback, cash discipline, and control. Her path runs from childhood cosmetic experiments and retail beauty education into [[Clarisonic]] and [[LOreal]] product work, then into building [[ThriveCausemetics]] after [[ChristieLamond]]'s death from cancer.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines emotional mission with product-development discipline rather than treating purpose as a substitute for performance.
- Uses personal savings, outside employment, and scarcity to fund early product work before the company can sustain itself.
- Builds around direct customer feedback, especially when retailers do not initially validate the brand.
- Treats founder control as a way to protect mission, formulation priorities, and long-term private-company direction.
