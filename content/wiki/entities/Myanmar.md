# Myanmar

Myanmar is the country context for [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]]'s opening segment. The episode describes a post-2021 coup crisis in which the [[MyanmarMilitaryJunta]] overthrew [[AungSanSuuKyi]]'s government, detained her, and faced nationwide uprising and armed resistance.

The source insists that Myanmar should not be reduced to Aung San Suu Kyi's fate. It says the country has about 55 million people, war continues across the country, tens of thousands have been killed, and millions have been displaced. That makes the segment a case for [[OverPersonalizedCrisisDiplomacy]]: proof of life matters, but it cannot stand in for the wider crisis.
