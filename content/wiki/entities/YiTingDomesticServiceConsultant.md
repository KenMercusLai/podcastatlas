# Yi Ting / 怡婷（家政顾问）

## Overview
Yi Ting / 怡婷 is a Shanghai-based domestic-service consultant whose frontline account connects employer recruitment, domestic-worker employment, infant care, household boundaries, and service-industry professionalization.

## Current Profile
[[vol-276-zuo-yuesao-yuersao-de-jiejie-dou-you-gushi-1017890906]] says Yi Ting graduated from Shanghai University with a sociology degree in 2022 and had recruitment experience in executive search before entering domestic service through a relative's internet-brand agency project. Her first successful placement came from revisiting a previously unconverted client, and the combination of recruitment skill, immediate positive feedback, and contact with workers and higher-end urban households led her to remain in the industry.

Her account defines the consultant as a two-sided recruiter and continuing coordinator rather than a directory. She elicits budgets and hidden requirements, screens and prepares candidates, controls interview and salary timing, translates unrealistic instructions into workable communication, and intervenes when placements need adjustment or replacement. She also describes learning to overcome social distance from domestic workers by asking about food, children, family, and daily life instead of speaking only about orders.

## Key Characteristics
- Sociology-trained observer who moves between individual household cases and structural care, gender, migration, and labor questions.
- Former recruiter who applies candidate discovery, interview preparation, and fit assessment to domestic-service placement.
- Treats employers and domestic workers as participants in a bilateral choice rather than treating payment as unilateral authority.
- Emphasizes after-sales mediation, expectation management, and replacement support as the hardest part of the consultant role.
- Advocates treating domestic workers as equal people with family obligations, emotions, preferences, and professional dignity.
- Grounds her market claims primarily in Shanghai and relatively high-end household-service experience.

## Evidence
- Career route - [[vol-276-zuo-yuesao-yuersao-de-jiejie-dou-you-gushi-1017890906]] describes the 2022 sociology graduation, executive-search experience, family-linked entry, and first placement from a dormant lead.
- Matching practice - [[vol-276-zuo-yuesao-yuersao-de-jiejie-dou-you-gushi-1017890906]] details requirement discovery, screening, interview sequencing, pay negotiation, placement, adaptation, and replacement.
- Communication practice - [[vol-276-zuo-yuesao-yuersao-de-jiejie-dou-you-gushi-1017890906]] shows her translating a ten-page AI-generated job specification into humane, executable guidance rather than forwarding it without context.
- Worker relationship - [[vol-276-zuo-yuesao-yuersao-de-jiejie-dou-you-gushi-1017890906]] recounts her early difficulty building trust, the electronic-signature misunderstanding, and the later shift toward conversation about workers' families and lives.
- Professional judgment - [[vol-276-zuo-yuesao-yuersao-de-jiejie-dou-you-gushi-1017890906]] distinguishes trainable care skills from personality and fit, and treats long-term reputation and referrals as stronger signals than quick signing alone.

## Qualifications
The profile comes from one podcast interview and does not independently verify Yi Ting's education, tenure, client mix, conversion rate, or pricing observations. Her cases are valuable qualitative evidence, but they should not stand in for representative national labor-market statistics or individualized childcare advice.
