# Li Ka-shing / 李嘉诚

Li Ka-shing appears in [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046]] as one of the most prominent entrepreneurs with [[Chaoshan]] roots. The episode uses him first as part of the opening contrast between the region's merchant reputation and [[Shantou]]'s weaker growth outcome.

His more specific role is tied to [[ShantouUniversity]]. The source treats Li's support for the university as a major hometown contribution, but also uses it to clarify [[DiasporaCapitalReturnLimits]]: philanthropy and education can be valuable without being the same as large-scale local industrial investment.

## Connections
- [[Chaoshan]] and [[Shantou]] — hometown and regional frame.
- [[ShantouUniversity]] — main institution connected to Li in the source.
- [[DiasporaCapitalReturnLimits]] and [[MerchantToIndustrialCityUpgrade]] — concepts the source uses his case to sharpen.
