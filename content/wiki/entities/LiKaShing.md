## Overview
Li Ka-shing / 李嘉诚 appears in the bounded sources as a Chaoshan-rooted Hong Kong entrepreneur, philanthropist, property counterparty, advertiser, and media-power figure.

## Current Profile
The Chaoshan source uses Li to distinguish hometown philanthropy from industrial transformation. His support for [[ShantouUniversity]] is treated as a major contribution, but not as proof that diaspora capital alone could create a Shenzhen-scale manufacturing base.

Episode 504 adds a Hong Kong media relationship. It describes early personal ties with [[LinXingzhi|林行止]] and the [[HongKongEconomicJournal|《信报》]], including use of Li-controlled property during the paper's difficult early period. The relationship later became an editorial-independence test when criticism of Li reportedly prompted a threat to withdraw substantial advertising and Lin refused to retreat.

## Key Characteristics
- Prominent entrepreneur with [[Chaoshan]] roots and a central Hong Kong business position.
- Supported hometown education through [[ShantouUniversity]].
- Illustrates the limits of equating philanthropy with local industrial investment.
- Appears as both an early business relationship and a source of advertiser leverage over media.
