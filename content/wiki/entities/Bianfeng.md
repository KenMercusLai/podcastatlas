# Bianfeng / 边锋

Bianfeng / 边锋 appears in [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]] as a chess/card and casual-game platform acquired by [[Shanda|盛大]]. The episode places it beside [[Haofang|浩方]] as part of Shanda's attempt to broaden from [[LegendOfMir|《传奇》]] into more forms of digital entertainment.

The source later notes that Shanda sold Bianfeng and Haofang, making Bianfeng part of the company's asset-recycling and post-box-strategy retreat. It helps show why [[FamilyInternetPortalStrategy]] needed more than content aggregation to become a durable platform.
