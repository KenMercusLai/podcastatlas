# 艾江涛 / Ai Jiangtao

艾江涛 / Ai Jiangtao is a [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] reporter and [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] speaker in [[ep264-tafang-tangshi-zhilu-shengtang-qixiang-shaonianxin-loo2dfuxffizpbl4si5nwcixnngi]]. His role in the episode is to explain the [[ChangAnTang|唐代长安]] and [[WangWei|王维]] route of the "唐诗之路" cover report.

The source presents his reporting as a return to places he already knew from studying in Xi'an. He connects present-day plant life, Tang warm-period imagination, vanished palace architecture, surviving towers, monastery public spaces, Wang Wei's capital career, and [[Wangchuan|辋川]] site fragments into [[TangPoetryCulturalGeography|唐诗文化地理]].
