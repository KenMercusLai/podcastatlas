## Overview
艾江涛 / Ai Jiangtao is a [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] reporter and [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] speaker whose wiki profile connects literature, historical geography, material traces, and route-based field reporting.

## Current Profile
Across both sources, Ai Jiangtao reads places as layered evidence rather than as scenery. His Tang-poetry reporting reconstructs Chang'an and Wang Wei through plants, vanished architecture, surviving towers, inscriptions, monastery space, poems, and local research. His [[BaoxieRoad|褒斜道]] reporting shifts the same method toward trestle sockets, cliff roads, inscriptions, floods, military destruction, sparse settlement, and route change.

The common profile is cultural-historical fieldwork: texts become more reliable and more meaningful when checked against terrain and remains, but physical traces also need chronology and documentary interpretation before they can support a confident story.

## Key Characteristics
- Reporter specializing in site-based cultural and historical interpretation.
- Connects written records to surviving terrain, structures, inscriptions, and route fragments.
- Treats loss and absence as evidence problems rather than invitations to invent continuity.
- Uses political geography to explain why roads, capitals, and cultural centers change importance.
