# Jiddu Krishnamurti / 克里希那穆提

## Overview
Jiddu Krishnamurti / 克里希那穆提 appears in [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] as the thinker Meng Yan uses to name "image", "register", and total attention. The episode uses him to move from cognitive explanation toward a possible interruption of interpersonal misrecognition.

## Current Profile
Krishnamurti's wiki role is the attention theorist behind E46's final move. "Image" names the accumulated memory, judgment, label, and emotional residue through which people often perceive one another. "Register" names the mind's recording of experience, especially emotionally charged experience, that thickens those images. [[TotalAttentionPractice]] names the episode's non-method endpoint: when whole-body attention is present, old images may stop doing the seeing.

## Key Characteristics
- Supplies the image/register vocabulary used to describe frozen interpersonal perception.
- Shifts the episode from explanation toward direct attention rather than another technique.
- Treats ordinary communication as limited when both people only exchange existing images.
- Makes bodily and sensory presence central to seeing, not only conceptual analysis.
- Is used interpretively beside Buddhist vocabulary without being reduced to a doctrinal Buddhist source.

## Evidence
- Image/register vocabulary: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] introduces Krishnamurti through those terms and applies them to partners, memories, judgments, and labels.
- Communication limit: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] says more speech can thicken the image wall when it only adds new registrations to old judgments.
- Attention endpoint: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] explains total attention as eyes, ears, body, and mind being present rather than the mental simulator watching for the person.

## Qualifications
The page records Krishnamurti as mediated by Meng Yan's episode. It should not be treated as a full reconstruction of Krishnamurti's philosophy, nor as a claim that the episode offers an easy method.
