# 包惜弱 / Bao Xiruo

## Overview

包惜弱 is a fictional character in [[LegendOfTheCondorHeroes|《射雕英雄传》]], wife of 杨铁心 and mother of 杨康. The bounded source reads her as compassionate, vulnerable to deception, dependent under crisis, and divided between an unrecovered past and life in the Jin court.

## Current Profile

The episode refuses a simple condemnation. Bao Xiruo's rescue of a wounded enemy expresses a genuine refusal to let an injured person die, while pregnancy, presumed widowhood, isolation, and 完颜洪烈's staged protection constrain her later marriage choice. Her rebuilt 牛家村 cottage keeps attachment to 杨铁心 visible but does not resolve whether it represents love, guilt, refusal of royal identity, or a way of reassuring herself.

Her most consequential maternal failure is not lack of affection. She protects 杨康 and fears disrupting his secure life, but conceals his parentage, supplies little explicit identity education, and struggles to correct his conduct. The source therefore places real love beside [[ParentalIndulgenceBlindness|indulgent blindness]] without claiming that she alone causes her son's later choices.

## Key Characteristics

- Extends compassion even to a wounded enemy, without reliably detecting manipulation.
- Makes survival choices under pregnancy, bereavement, isolation, and dependence.
- Maintains an unresolved symbolic attachment to 牛家村 and 杨铁心.
- Protects and indulges 杨康 while withholding central facts about his identity.
- Has limited educational authority inside a court environment organized by privilege and concealment.
- Ultimately chooses reunion and death with 杨铁心 after eighteen years of divided life.

## Qualifications

The profile comes from one podcast interpretation rather than a complete textual study. Her feelings toward 完颜洪烈, the meaning of the cottage, the reasons for concealment, and her influence on 杨康 are disputed by the hosts. Court environment, 丘处机's teaching, privilege, and 杨康's own decisions prevent a single-cause maternal explanation.

## What Changed

- Created a bounded profile separating compassion from discernment and dependence.
- Identified concealment and indulgence, rather than absence of love, as the episode's central maternal criticism.
- Preserved disagreement over romance, nostalgia, guilt, and causality.
