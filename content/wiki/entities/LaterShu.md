# 后蜀 / Later Shu

Later Shu / 后蜀 is one of the southern regimes in [[FiveDynastiesTenKingdoms|五代十国]]. In [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]], it belongs with [[FormerShu|前蜀]], Wu, and [[SouthernTang|南唐]] as a regime that could interact with central-plain dynasties from a comparatively parallel position.

For the wiki's existing Song branch, Later Shu also points forward to [[SongUnificationStrategy|先南后北]] and [[SichuanPostConquestResistance|川蜀征服后反抗]]. The episode helps explain why Shu was not simply a conquest target but part of a richer southern-regime landscape.
