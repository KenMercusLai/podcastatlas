# 李杰（极兔）

## Overview
李杰 is the founder of [[JAndTExpress|极兔速递]] and a former OPPO Indonesia executive.

## Current Profile
The source presents Li as transferring a regional-agent playbook from the OPPO network into Indonesian delivery and then into China. J&T entered China by acquiring a licensed carrier, installing trusted regional operators, and using high fees, subsidies, financing, and acquisitions to build volume quickly.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder with OPPO Indonesia operating experience.
- Cross-border transfer of a regional-agent network model.
- Capital- and subsidy-backed Chinese market entrant.
