# Miniso / 名创优品

## Overview
Miniso / 名创优品 is a Chinese consumer-retail company that enters the wiki through its push into large, IP-heavy stores and a slower overseas-opening stance.

## Current Profile
The source presents Miniso as shifting attention from ordinary small-store replication toward a larger store format where exclusive, first-release, limited, and check-in-friendly IP products drive the visit. Management frames these stores as a response to weak traditional retail demand and stronger interest consumption or emotional-value consumption.

The same episode qualifies the expansion story: overseas business underperformed expectations, so Miniso is slowing overseas openings and focusing on operating efficiency in existing stores before broader replication.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses large-format stores to carry more IP goods, experience, and social-media-friendly visits.
- Treats interest consumption and emotional value as stronger demand than ordinary retail goods.
- Benchmarks large-store performance ambition against [[PopMart|Pop Mart / 泡泡玛特]] in the source.
- Plans large domestic store potential while also slowing overseas openings after weaker-than-expected international performance.
- Shifts from speed of expansion toward store-model validation and operating efficiency.

## Evidence
- Large IP-heavy store model: [[kaile-shi-zai-ouzhou-kaidian-guanggao-shouci-chengwei-b-zhan-diyi-da-yewu-1010144081]] says Miniso's large "land" stores are much larger than ordinary stores and carry IP products at more than half of assortment.
- Interest-consumption thesis: [[kaile-shi-zai-ouzhou-kaidian-guanggao-shouci-chengwei-b-zhan-diyi-da-yewu-1010144081]] reports management's view that traditional goods are weak while interest and emotional-value consumption keep growing.
- Store-count ambition: [[kaile-shi-zai-ouzhou-kaidian-guanggao-shouci-chengwei-b-zhan-diyi-da-yewu-1010144081]] cites estimates of thousands of domestic stores and roughly 1,200 large-format opportunities.
- Overseas qualification: [[kaile-shi-zai-ouzhou-kaidian-guanggao-shouci-chengwei-b-zhan-diyi-da-yewu-1010144081]] says overseas performance was below expectations and management planned to slow openings until the single-store model is stronger.

## Qualifications
The source is management- and media-report based. It does not prove that large stores can outperform Pop Mart, that IP-heavy retail demand will persist, or that overseas store optimization will restore margins.

## What Changed
- Added Miniso as an IP-heavy experiential retail and interest-consumption case.
- Kept its overseas slowdown as a qualification on the expansion story.
