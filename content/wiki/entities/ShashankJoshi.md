# Shashank Joshi

Shashank Joshi appears in [[kyiv-brawl-ukraines-top-general-fired-6a609aec4fac21e67f1be165]] as the analyst explaining the Ukraine segment for [[TheIntelligence]]. His role is to turn the firing of [[MykhailoFedorov]] and [[OleksandrSyrskyi|Oleksandr Sersky]] from personnel news into a diagnosis of [[UkraineMilitaryCommandReform]].

Joshi's interpretation stresses ethos and institutions. He reads the fight as a clash between Fedorov's technology-forward procurement and drone model and Syrskyi's more rigid command culture, while also noting that [[VolodymyrZelensky]] emerges politically bruised.
