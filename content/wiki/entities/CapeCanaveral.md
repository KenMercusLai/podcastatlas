# Cape Canaveral

Cape Canaveral appears in [[tech-20260818-tech-pod-128-tech-20260818-tech-pod-128]] as the core launch geography around the [[FloridaSpaceCoast]]. The episode describes the Cape Canaveral area as one of the busiest launch clusters in the world and as a place where crowded pads and upgrade needs reveal [[SpaceLaunchCapacityBottleneck]].

In the source, Cape Canaveral also illustrates [[SpaceportSitingConstraint]]: suitable launch locations need ocean-facing trajectories, distance from population centers, port access, and tolerance for noise, debris risk, and airspace disruption.
