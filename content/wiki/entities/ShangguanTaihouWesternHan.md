## Overview

上官太后 / Empress Dowager Shangguan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-424-huoguang-qushi-hou-huojia-shi-ruhe-fumie-de-ltisydawuqdhqpffdjv_xkwts90p|Hanji 424]] as the empress dowager whose banquet authority is to be used in the Huo family's failed plan to kill major ministers and depose [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-404-hanzhaodi-liufuling-zhendeshi-bei-huoguang-haiside-lu3adnqvomeco0tsoviuqwedjpfs|Hanji 404]] adds her earlier role as [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]]'s empress and [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]]'s granddaughter, making her childlessness part of the succession crisis before Liu He's accession. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-400-1-taidula-hanzhaodi-jing-bipo-gege-yanwang-liudan-zisha-ln0-b64ip2kwpcpen_65e4du3vqc|Hanji 400-1]] adds the earlier family-disaster layer: after [[ShangguanJieWesternHan|上官桀]] and [[ShangguanAnWesternHan|上官安]] are destroyed for rebellion, she is spared because the source presents her as young, uninvolved, and politically protected by her relation to Huo Guang.

## Current Profile

The source set gives Shangguan Taihou a procedural profile rather than a full biography. Hanji 404 shows the earlier palace structure around her: as Huo Guang's granddaughter and Han Zhao's empress, her potential to bear an heir is favored through Huo's control of the harem, including restrictions meant to prevent other palace women from producing a rival child. The episode does not present her as the decision-maker in that policy, but her position gives Huo a reason to control the emperor's reproductive environment.

Hanji 400-1 adds that Shangguan's early palace position survives a family catastrophe. The source says she is not deposed after the Shangguan plot because she is too young to have participated and remains Huo Guang's granddaughter. Hanji 424 then shows her later institutional role as empress dowager. In the Huo plan, she would host a banquet for Boping Jun Wang Ao, with chancellor Wei Xiang, Xu Guanghan, and other figures summoned as attendants. Huo-linked actors then planned to use commands in her name to kill key ministers and replace Xuan with Huo Yu.

Her importance in these sources is therefore institutional. As empress, she sits at the intersection of harem access, childbearing, and Huo-family power. As empress dowager, her space, invitation, and command language could make a coup look like palace procedure long enough for targeted violence to occur.

## Key Characteristics

- Huo Guang's granddaughter and Han Zhao's empress, whose childlessness helps create the succession vacuum after Han Zhao dies.
- Palace figure around whom Huo Guang's harem-access controls are organized, though the source does not show her directing those controls.
- Young empress spared after her father and grandfather's rebellion because the source treats her as uninvolved.
- Empress-dowager figure whose banquet setting is central to the Huo conspiracy plan.
- Palace-authority channel because commands in her name could legitimize sudden action against ministers.
- Source-scoped procedural actor rather than a fully reconstructed biography.
- Evidence that harem and dowager institutions could shape succession and be imagined as coup infrastructure.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 400-1, Hanji 404, and Hanji 424. It does not reconstruct Shangguan Taihou's full life, marriage age, family background, agency in Huo Guang's harem policy, agency in the Huo plan, or later status.
