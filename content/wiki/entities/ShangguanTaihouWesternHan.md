## Overview

上官太后 / Empress Dowager Shangguan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-424-huoguang-qushi-hou-huojia-shi-ruhe-fumie-de-ltisydawuqdhqpffdjv_xkwts90p|Hanji 424]] as the empress dowager whose banquet authority is to be used in the Huo family's failed plan to kill major ministers and depose [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]].

## Current Profile

The source gives Shangguan Taihou a narrow procedural role rather than a developed biography. In the Huo plan, she would host a banquet for Boping Jun Wang Ao, with chancellor Wei Xiang, Xu Guanghan, and other figures summoned as attendants. Huo-linked actors then planned to use commands in her name to kill key ministers and replace Xuan with Huo Yu.

Her importance in this source is therefore institutional. The plan depends on empress-dowager space, invitation, and command language to make a coup look like palace procedure long enough for targeted violence to occur.

## Key Characteristics

- Empress-dowager figure whose banquet setting is central to the Huo conspiracy plan.
- Palace-authority channel because commands in her name could legitimize sudden action against ministers.
- Source-scoped procedural actor rather than a fully reconstructed biography.
- Evidence that harem and dowager institutions could be imagined as coup infrastructure.
