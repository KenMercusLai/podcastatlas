# 《四季之后》

## Overview
《四季之后》 is a youth-romance film recommended by [[ChaoGe|超哥]] as a test of whether a relationship must become permanent to have been worthwhile.

## Current Profile
The episode contrasts Remi's planned path toward Harvard, law, and judgeship with Barnes's musical life and uncertain future. They meet in winter, love through summer, and separate in autumn without either person abandoning a core direction. Their gap-year dispute also exposes the pressure to make rest productive. The open ending supports [[RelationshipValueBeyondDuration]] as a third path between self-erasure for love and refusing attachment unless permanence is guaranteed.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses the seasons to structure approach, intimacy, and separation.
- Contrasts a highly planned future with exploratory uncertainty.
- Questions whether rest must justify itself through productivity.
- Refuses to make self-sacrifice or permanent union the only proof of love.
