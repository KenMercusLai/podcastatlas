## Overview

Kuan Shu / 宽舒 is the coastal official whom [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] assigns to continue [[LiShaojunWesternHan|李少君]]'s search method after the fangshi's death in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-259-hanwudi-wo-ba-ni-dang-dashi-ni-ba-wo-dang-shazi-1-lqczbezelhdjrtxdrgsfcnmpoklx|Hanji 259 part 1]]. The current profile is deliberately narrow because the source gives no wider biography.

## Current Profile

Kuan Shu enters the episode only after Wudi interprets Li Shaojun's death as transformation rather than failure. His assignment is to search the coast for [[Penglai|蓬莱]] and [[AnQisheng|安期生]] using Li's approach. He therefore represents administrative continuation: an unverifiable claim survives its original claimant by being transferred to an official mission.

## Key Characteristics

- Identified as a coastal official in the episode's account.
- Receives the search assignment after Li Shaojun's death.
- Carries a personal fangshi program into continuing state-supported action.

## Evidence

- **Official role and assignment:** [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-259-hanwudi-wo-ba-ni-dang-dashi-ni-ba-wo-dang-shazi-1-lqczbezelhdjrtxdrgsfcnmpoklx|Hanji 259 part 1]] says Wudi sends Kuan Shu from the coastal administration to seek Penglai and An Qisheng according to Li Shaojun's method.
