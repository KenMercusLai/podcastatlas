# 李倩 / Li Qian (brand consultant)

## Overview
Li Qian / 李倩 is the brand consultant, media creator, and entrepreneur interviewed by [[LiuFei|刘飞]] in a [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] episode about brand strategy after traffic and capital-storytelling advantages recede.

## Current Profile
The supplied source identifies Li as the creator of “李倩说品牌” and founder of 天梯品牌管理咨询. Her profile combines media editing, investment, consulting, and physical-business experience. That operating exposure informs her view that branding is not a communication department at the end of a process, but an upstream sensing and coordination function joining customer insight, product, service, price, channel, sales, and expression.

## Key Characteristics
- She frames the 2025-2026 brand shift as “landing”: returning brand work to revenue, product, service, channel, and customer relationships.
- She describes the needed brand leader as an integrator who detects social change and keeps cross-functional choices coherent.
- Her physical-business experience makes pricing, margin, channel feedback, and unsold inventory central constraints rather than afterthoughts.
- She argues that small brands should stabilize their own identity before reacting to every platform or audience signal.
- She links mature consumer demand to authenticity, emotional resonance, self-care, and deeper offline connection while retaining functional value.
