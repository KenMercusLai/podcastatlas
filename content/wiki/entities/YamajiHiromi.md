# Yamaji Hiromi

Yamaji Hiromi appears in [[wake-up-haul-an-ozempic-moment-for-the-brain-6a85798d1ad8716bb6078744]] as chief executive of the [[TokyoStockExchange]]. The source quotes him saying the exchange wants IPOs to be a starting point rather than the end goal.

His role in the wiki is tied to [[JapaneseStartupExitConstraint]]. The episode presents TSE delisting reform as an attempt to discourage tiny, stagnant public listings and give startups more reason to build for larger scale before and after listing.
