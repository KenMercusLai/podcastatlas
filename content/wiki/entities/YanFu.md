# Yan Fu / 严复

## Overview
Yan Fu / 严复 is the modern Chinese translator used by [[323-ai-shifou-keyi-qudai-fanyi-cuowu-de-fanyi-ruhe-suzao-xianshi-luw-q1sjef4uttao4ghe1yx7rkyf]] to examine how terminology competes, stabilizes, and shapes political understanding.

## Current Profile
The episode presents Yan Fu neither as an infallible authority nor simply as the loser to Japanese-coined Chinese terminology. His alternatives matter because they expose semantic distinctions obscured by later standard usage: 《群己权界论》 foregrounds boundaries between individual and collective liberty, 《原富》 asks after the source of wealth without reducing it to government wealth, and “演” describes change without necessarily building improvement into evolution.

This profile is interpretive and source-bounded. The source uses Yan Fu as evidence that a rejected rendering may remain analytically useful even after ordinary language has settled elsewhere.

## Key Characteristics
- Modern Chinese translator and terminology-maker in the episode's intellectual-history account.
- Participant in competition between indigenous renderings and Japanese-coined Chinese academic vocabulary.
- Valued for alternatives that make conceptual boundaries visible rather than for universally winning standard terms.
- Associated here with 《群己权界论》, 《原富》, and 《天演论》.
- Demonstrates that lexical uptake depends on circulation and historical conditions as well as precision.

## Evidence
- Liberty boundary: [[323-ai-shifou-keyi-qudai-fanyi-cuowu-de-fanyi-ruhe-suzao-xianshi-luw-q1sjef4uttao4ghe1yx7rkyf]] contrasts the standard “自由” with “群己权界,” which makes limits among self, others, and society explicit.
- Wealth and political economy: [[323-ai-shifou-keyi-qudai-fanyi-cuowu-de-fanyi-ruhe-suzao-xianshi-luw-q1sjef4uttao4ghe1yx7rkyf]] uses 《原富》 to resist reading Adam Smith's subject as merely state or treasury wealth.
- Evolution and directionality: [[323-ai-shifou-keyi-qudai-fanyi-cuowu-de-fanyi-ruhe-suzao-xianshi-luw-q1sjef4uttao4ghe1yx7rkyf]] treats “演” as retaining change without guaranteeing progress.
- Terminology competition: [[323-ai-shifou-keyi-qudai-fanyi-cuowu-de-fanyi-ruhe-suzao-xianshi-luw-q1sjef4uttao4ghe1yx7rkyf]] presents Yan Fu's frequent failure to displace established vocabulary as evidence for [[TranslationPathDependence]].

## Qualifications
The wiki currently knows Yan Fu only through this episode's argument. Exact etymologies, his intentions, the chronology of competing renderings, and their long-run causal influence require primary texts and specialist linguistic history; the episode's claims are not treated as settled biography or philology.
