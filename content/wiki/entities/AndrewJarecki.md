# Andrew Jarecki

## Overview

Andrew Jarecki is a documentary filmmaker and co-director of [[TheAlabamaSolution|The Alabama Solution]], represented here through a [[RevisionistHistory]] conversation with [[MalcolmGladwell]].

## Current Profile

Jarecki describes a discovery-led documentary method: he resists fixing characters and outcomes before investigation, follows unexpected evidence, and gives firsthand voices priority over explanatory experts when those voices can carry the account. In Alabama, losing official prison access shifted the project toward calls and recordings made by incarcerated people themselves.

His account also frames filmmaking as an ongoing relationship. He and [[CharlotteKaufman]] consulted participants about the film's representation, showed them the completed work on phones, assembled legal support against retaliation, and remained in contact after release.

## Key Characteristics

- Treats uncertainty and discovery as essential to documentary investigation.
- Uses firsthand recordings to challenge institutional secrecy and retrospective reconstruction.
- Balances narrative momentum with limited disclosure about participants' convictions.
- Describes incarcerated participants as collaborators, organizers, and authors.
- Extends filmmaker responsibility beyond release when participation creates continuing risk.
