# John Maynard Keynes / 凯恩斯

John Maynard Keynes / 凯恩斯 enters the wiki through [[jifa-dongwu-jingshen-chuangzao-gengduo-jihui-lndyfdmtgo-9l5cr8oy82t1xnlfe]], where [[ZhouLuohua|周洛华]] interprets Keynesian government spending as a way to restart [[AnimalSpiritsOpportunityActivation|animal spirits]] rather than as a doctrine of endless state borrowing.

The source-scoped reading matters because it ties Keynes to opportunity rather than only stimulus. In Zhou's account, spending is useful when it helps the economic engine return to a cycle of confidence, investment, consumption, and action; it is not an independent substitute for real opportunity.
