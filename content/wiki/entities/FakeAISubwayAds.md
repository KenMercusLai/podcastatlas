# Fake AI Subway Ads

Fake AI Subway Ads is the satire project by [[HarrisAlterman]] and [[DaveRoss]] discussed in [[tech-20260714-tech-pod-128-tech-20260714-tech-pod-128]]. The project placed fake AI company posters around New York City's transit system, using invented companies such as Froggle, Dennis, Cutlery.AI, Goofstump, and Ziplink to mimic the look and language of real AI marketing.

The project works because the fake ads are plausible. Their solid backgrounds, generic icons, logos, renamed-product language, cloud and safety claims, and vague transformation slogans expose [[AIMarketingJargon]] as a repeatable pattern. The episode says the video of the project spread widely on [[Instagram]], suggesting that many viewers recognized the same confusion from real public AI ads.

The source also makes the project a case in [[StartupLegitimacyTransfer]]. Even when an ad says little, appearing in public transit can make a company seem real, funded, and important. That legitimacy effect is exactly what makes the parody hard to distinguish from actual AI hype at first glance.
