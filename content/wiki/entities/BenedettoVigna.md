# Benedetto Vigna

Benedetto Vigna is the Ferrari CEO in the public-company era discussed in [[ferrari-1]]. The source says [[Ferrari]] hired him in 2021 and uses his statement that Ferrari sells a dream rather than just a car as the modern expression of the brand.

Vigna's role in the source centers on preserving emotional distinctiveness while Ferrari grows production, licensing, lifestyle activity, and electric-vehicle development. That places him inside [[PublicMarketLuxuryGrowthTension]] and the [[FerrariLuce]] EV question.
