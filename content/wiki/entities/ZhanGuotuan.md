# Zhan Guotuan / 詹国团

## Overview
Zhan Guotuan is presented by [[Banlatte]] as a Putian-origin medical operator whose career connects itinerant family practice, advertising-led expansion, public-hospital cooperation, medical-equipment revenue sharing, overseas experience, and investment in a large comprehensive hospital.

## Current Profile
His account is useful as a business-history thread rather than a representative biography for every [[PutianMedicalNetwork|Putian-linked operator]]. It combines unusually candid acknowledgment of early abusive practices with a later attempt to build a licensed, staff-intensive hospital whose losses illustrate the difficulty of replacing high-margin specialist models with long-horizon comprehensive care.

## Key Characteristics
- Entered mobile medical work through relatives and kin-based operating teams.
- Used television advertising and monthly lead-source tracking to improve acquisition efficiency.
- Expanded through contracted hospital departments and equipment-purchase revenue sharing.
- Acknowledged false or exaggerated diagnoses, prolonged treatment, and arbitrary charging in the early sexually transmitted infection business.
- Built national cooperation across many public hospitals according to his own account.
- Later invested in a large Jiaxing comprehensive hospital and recruited clinicians from public institutions.
- His reported investment and loss figures demonstrate slow payback but are not independently audited on the page.

## Evidence
- **Early model:** The episode describes relatives operating across cities, television-ad experiments, monthly attribution, department contracting, and equipment revenue sharing. [[gkwrimaorinia5ejiwtsfhox-gkwrimaorinia5ejiwtsfhox]]
- **Practice acknowledgment:** The source attributes to Zhan an admission that the early market included inventing or exaggerating illness, extending treatment, and charging arbitrarily. [[gkwrimaorinia5ejiwtsfhox-gkwrimaorinia5ejiwtsfhox]]
- **Comprehensive-hospital investment:** The Jiaxing project reportedly began construction in 2005, opened in 2009 after delay, recruited heavily, and by 2014 had absorbed about RMB 1 billion while losing more than RMB 200 million. [[gkwrimaorinia5ejiwtsfhox-gkwrimaorinia5ejiwtsfhox]]

## Qualifications
- Wealth, partner counts, investment, losses, and payback estimates are episode-reported and partly based on Zhan's own statements.
- His trajectory illustrates several industry mechanisms but does not establish control over the wider [[PutianMedicalNetwork|Putian medical network]].
- Later formal investment does not erase harms attributed to earlier business practices, and earlier abuses do not prove that every later institution used the same conduct.
