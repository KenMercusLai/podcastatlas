# 庞羲 / Pang Xi (late Han)

## Overview
庞羲 / Pang Xi is a late-Han Yi Province official whose profile now runs from Liu-family continuity broker to militarized insider under suspicion. His page shows how old favor, local force, elite ties, and household marriage can make a provincial strongman useful to [[LiuZhang|刘璋]] while also making him dangerous-looking.

## Current Profile
Pang Xi first appears as a Henan official and friend of [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]. After Liu Yan's Chang'an-side sons are killed or unavailable, Pang Xi helps send Liu Yan's grandsons into Shu, preserving a family continuity channel around [[YizhouLateHan|益州]]. That makes him part of Liu Zhang's succession support network rather than an outside enemy.

Hanji 1055 turns that same closeness into risk. Pang Xi controls force in the Baxi field, has ties to local elites and Cong groups, once showed favor to the Liu family, and has married his daughter to Liu Zhang's eldest son [[LiuXunYizhouHeirLateHan|刘循]]. He fears Liu Zhang's suspicion and tries to have [[ChengJiLateHan|程畿]] gather Cong forces, but Cheng Ji refuses the unrighteous command. Pang Xi then abandons the armed path and apologizes with gifts.

## Key Characteristics
- Acts as a Liu-family continuity broker after Liu Yan's family crisis.
- Holds enough Baxi-area force and local relationships to become politically suspect.
- Is tied to Liu Zhang's household through his daughter and Liu Zhang's eldest son Liu Xun.
- Responds to fear by considering armed self-protection, but remains persuadable by Cheng Ji's moral refusal.
- Illustrates how prior favor can become a source of anxiety when provincial succession is insecure.

## Qualifications
Pang Xi is not currently framed as a completed rebel in the wiki. Hanji 1055 shows fear and a contemplated armed response, but the branch is de-escalated after Cheng Ji's refusal. The page therefore treats him as a strong suspect insider, not as another Zhao Wei.
