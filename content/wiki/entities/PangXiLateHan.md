# 庞羲 / Pang Xi (late Han)

庞羲 / Pang Xi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]] as a Henan official and friend of [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]. After Liu Yan's Chang'an-side sons are killed or unavailable, Pang Xi sends Liu Yan's grandsons into Shu, helping preserve a family continuity channel around [[YizhouLateHan|益州]].

The source does not make Pang Xi an independent political protagonist. His significance is connective: outside personal networks still matter when a provincial regime is geographically insulated and dynastic family continuity has been damaged by failed intervention in [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]].
