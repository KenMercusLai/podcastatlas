# Nolan Williams

## Overview
Nolan Williams is the psychiatrist and neuroscience guest in [[essentials-psychedelics-neurostimulation-for-brain-rewiring-dr-nolan-williams-scim5296180263]], where he explains depression, neuromodulation, and psychedelic-assisted psychiatric research through a brain-circuit model.

## Current Profile
Within the current wiki evidence, Williams is a clinical-research voice for [[CircuitBasedPsychiatry]]. He argues that depression should not be reduced to a simple serotonin deficit and that fast-acting neuromodulation or altered-state treatments may work by changing network timing, prefrontal regulation, default-mode connectivity, or memory reconsolidation. His stance is not psychedelic boosterism: he repeatedly separates promising signals from completed evidence and keeps powerful compounds inside [[PsychedelicClinicalSupervisionBoundary]].

## Key Characteristics
- Frames depression as a severe, disabling, body-linked medical condition rather than only a mood label.
- Presents TMS and [[StanfordNeuromodulationTherapy]] as circuit-targeted psychiatric interventions.
- Treats SSRIs as useful for some patients while rejecting the simple chemical-imbalance explanation.
- Discusses MDMA, psilocybin, ketamine, ibogaine, and ayahuasca as clinically interesting but evidence- and risk-bounded.
- Uses trauma and PTSD examples to explain how maladaptive rules can persist after their original survival context.
- Emphasizes rigorous trials, screening, and medical supervision for psychedelic or intensive neuromodulation work.

## Evidence
- Depression and circuits - [[essentials-psychedelics-neurostimulation-for-brain-rewiring-dr-nolan-williams-scim5296180263]] has Williams connect depression to disabling illness, cardiovascular risk, and mood-regulatory brain networks.
- Neuromodulation - [[essentials-psychedelics-neurostimulation-for-brain-rewiring-dr-nolan-williams-scim5296180263]] presents TMS and Stanford Neuromodulation Therapy as ways to externally engage prefrontal control and compressed spaced stimulation.
- Psychedelic evidence boundary - [[essentials-psychedelics-neurostimulation-for-brain-rewiring-dr-nolan-williams-scim5296180263]] discusses MDMA, psilocybin, ketamine, ibogaine, and ayahuasca while distinguishing trial results, anecdotes, open-label signals, risks, and supervision needs.
