# Birdhouse

## Overview
Birdhouse appears in the wiki as the skateboard company [[TonyHawk]] founded in 1992 during an industry downturn.

## Current Profile
The current evidence presents Birdhouse as a necessity-driven entrepreneurial project rather than an effortless extension of Hawk’s fame. With skateboarding income shrinking and a large mortgage and young child to support, Hawk used home equity, cut expenses, took other work, and built the company on the road. He served simultaneously as professional skater, coach, tour manager, and owner while assembling a team that included Willie Santos, Jeremy Klein, Steve Berra, Ocean Howell, Andrew Reynolds, and Matt Beach.

## Key Characteristics
- Founded during an early-1990s contraction in skateboarding.
- Financed in part through equity from Hawk’s home.
- Required Hawk to combine athletic, coaching, touring, and business roles.
- Built around a traveling team of professional skaters.
- Represents entrepreneurial persistence under financial pressure rather than guaranteed celebrity success.
