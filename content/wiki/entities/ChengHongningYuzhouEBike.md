# 程泓宁 / 陈鸿宁

## Overview
程泓宁 / 陈鸿宁 is the [[YuzhouEBike|宇宙 E-Bike]] founder interviewed by [[LiuFei|刘飞]] in the No.222 [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] episode.

## Current Profile
The source presents him as a finance-trained operator whose route into entrepreneurship passed through audit, secondary-market investing, primary-market investing, listed-company board and management work, family-office capital management, and then electric-assist bicycle brand building. His central claim is not that investing was shallow, but that it left him outside product, production, cash flow, service, and customer purchase decisions.

As an operator, he treats [[YuzhouEBike|宇宙 E-Bike]] as both a category bet and a personal capability test. The episode links his earlier work around [[BafangElectric|八方股份]], manufacturing, new energy, retail, and overseas markets to the choice of electric-assist bicycles, then follows how the startup forced more direct understanding of product iteration, price positioning, user trust, overseas channels, and organizational pressure.

The source also makes him a founder-led AI transformation advocate. He argues that small manufacturing companies may be better positioned to become AI-native than larger organizations, but only if the top leader personally pushes data use, workflow redesign, training, and role changes.

## Key Characteristics
- He has a compound finance and operating background spanning audit, public markets, primary markets, listed-company management, and entrepreneurship.
- He treats entrepreneurship as a way to repair investor-side distance from real operating control.
- He chose electric-assist bicycles because the category joins manufacturing, new energy, retail, export potential, and riding experience.
- He positions [[YuzhouEBike|宇宙 E-Bike]] in a premium Chinese price band where product quality and after-sales service can be funded.
- He frames AI adoption in manufacturing as an organization-level founder responsibility rather than a delegated tool rollout.
