## Current Profile

The episodes give Gai Chang Gongzhu a palace-access and private-favor profile. Hanji 396-1 shows why her access matters before the rebellion: after Han Zhao's mother is gone, the princess is brought in as an elder caregiver and can shape palace selection around him. Hanji 399 shows her as a beneficiary and broker of Shangguan-family marriage politics, then as a resentful actor after Huo Guang refuses advancement for Ding Wairen. Hanji 400-1 shows the later violent use of her access: the conspirators plan to have her invite Huo Guang to a banquet, then use hidden armed men to kill him before deposing [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]]. Her household also becomes the source of the leak: Yan Cang, the father of a retainer near her, learns of the plan and passes it onward.

The host frames her as one of several already privileged actors ruined by intensified desire. She has high status and proximity to the ruler, yet joins a dangerous anti-Huo coalition and dies by suicide after the plot collapses.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han princess whose banquet setting is imagined as the assassination site.
- Elder princess-caregiver whose palace role lets her influence Han Zhao's early consort selection.
- Palace-influence broker whose favored Ding Wairen becomes an office-and-title grievance against Huo Guang.
- Palace-access actor in the anti-Huo conspiracy.
- Co-conspirator with Shangguan Jie, Shangguan An, Sang Hongyang, and Liu Dan.
- Household leak point because Yan Cang learns the plan through her retainer network.
- Suicide case after the conspiracy is exposed and the main group is destroyed.
