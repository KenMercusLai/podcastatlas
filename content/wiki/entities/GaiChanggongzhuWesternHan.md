## Overview

盖长公主 / Gai Chang Gongzhu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-400-1-taidula-hanzhaodi-jing-bipo-gege-yanwang-liudan-zisha-ln0-b64ip2kwpcpen_65e4du3vqc|Hanji 400-1]] as the princess whose banquet access is central to the failed plan to assassinate [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]].

## Current Profile

The episode gives Gai Chang Gongzhu a palace-access profile. The conspirators plan to have her invite Huo Guang to a banquet, then use hidden armed men to kill him before deposing [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]]. Her household also becomes the source of the leak: Yan Cang, the father of a retainer near her, learns of the plan and passes it onward.

The host frames her as one of several already privileged actors ruined by intensified desire. She has high status and proximity to the ruler, yet joins a dangerous anti-Huo coalition and dies by suicide after the plot collapses.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han princess whose banquet setting is imagined as the assassination site.
- Palace-access actor in the anti-Huo conspiracy.
- Co-conspirator with Shangguan Jie, Shangguan An, Sang Hongyang, and Liu Dan.
- Household leak point because Yan Cang learns the plan through her retainer network.
- Suicide case after the conspiracy is exposed and the main group is destroyed.
