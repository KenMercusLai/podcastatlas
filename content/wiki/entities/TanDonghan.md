# 谭东涵 / Tan Donghan

## Overview
谭东涵 is the medically trained investment guest in a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] discussion of Chinese innovative drugs, ASCO visibility, payment, licensing, and globalization.

## Current Profile
The source says 谭东涵 moved from clinical medicine into finance and investment because he wanted a form of leverage capable of affecting more patients than one-at-a-time clinical care. In the episode, he interprets Chinese innovative drugs as an industry system: unmet need, research, trials, capital, regulation, reimbursement, licensing, and global commercialization have to work together.

His assessment is optimistic but conditional. China has a large market, unified regulation and insurance structures, extensive clinical resources, rapid organizational execution, and increasingly competitive assets, yet weaker payment capacity and incomplete original-science and global-commercial capabilities still limit leadership. He treats cross-border partnership as a normal part of biotech rather than automatic surrender, while allowing that only a small number of Chinese firms may build full multinational-pharma capabilities.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines medical training with an investment and industry-structure perspective.
- Frames innovative drugs around unmet clinical need, high risk, long timelines, and value capture.
- Treats payment capacity as part of the innovation system rather than a downstream detail.
- Distinguishes ordinary biotech licensing from the rarer task of building a multinational pharmaceutical company.
- Sees Chinese execution capacity as real while keeping original discovery and globalization gaps explicit.
