# Figma

## Overview
Figma is a design-centered software company co-founded by [[DylanField]] in 2012. The bounded source presents it as both a professional instrument for product design and a broader visual-work platform spanning marketing, advertising, presentations, whiteboarding, and diagramming.

## Current Profile
Figma's AI-era position rests on combining generation with direct manipulation rather than treating model output as a finished product. Its proposed $20 billion acquisition by [[Adobe]] and continued shipping during a roughly 16-month review also make it a case in maintaining operating momentum while a transformative transaction remains uncertain.

## Key Characteristics
- Builds professional design workflows intended to feel responsive and expressive like an instrument.
- Extends beyond interface design into multiple visual collaboration and communication tasks.
- Continued independent product development while the Adobe transaction faced regulatory uncertainty.
- Uses audience knowledge, workflow detail, and interaction quality as defenses against generic AI generation.
- Seeks fluid movement between AI generation, code on canvas, and hands-on refinement.
- Uses an internal “written with AI” label to make authorship effort more legible.
