# Dean Graziosi

## Overview
Dean Graziosi appears in the wiki as the entrepreneur and educator interviewed by [[ShekharNatarajan]] on [[TomorrowToday]] about adversity, family-centered purpose, business building, partnership with [[TonyRobbins]], and practical AI use.

## Current Profile
The available source presents Graziosi as an underdog-identified entrepreneur who moved from firewood, car repair, towing, property, and infomercials into business education. He attributes persistence to an immigrant family history, childhood poverty, a volatile home, and a desire to give his parents and children more choice. His stated measure of success is control over time and presence for family rather than wealth alone.

His AI position is optimistic but conditional. He favors [[ContextRichAIPartnership]] for strategy and repetitive work, rejects unreviewed output, and uses [[HumanAIDelegationBoundary]] to retain leadership, consequential judgment, speeches, intimacy, and responsibility with people.

## Key Characteristics
- Converts an underdog identity and family responsibility into a persistent entrepreneurial narrative.
- Defines success through agency, calendar control, family presence, and contribution more than status.
- Treats disliked work as preparation when it is performed carefully and creates trust or future opportunity.
- Advocates a small number of context-rich AI systems over constant tool switching.
- Keeps review, leadership, intimate relationships, and final decisions on the human side of AI use.
