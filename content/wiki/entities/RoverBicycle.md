# Rover Bicycle / 罗福自行车

The Rover Bicycle is the safety bicycle discussed in [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]] through [[JohnKempStarley]]. The episode treats the 1885 design as the point where the bicycle's basic modern architecture became stable enough to survive for more than a century.

The product's significance lies in architecture rather than styling. Chain drive and gear ratios let riders gain speed without sitting above a huge front wheel, turning [[BicycleProductEvolution]] away from dangerous high-wheel spectacle toward mass usability.
