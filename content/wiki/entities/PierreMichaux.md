# Pierre Michaux / 皮埃尔·米肖

Pierre Michaux appears in [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]] as one of the makers associated with putting pedals on the front-wheel axle in the 1860s. In the source's history, that direct-drive step moved the bicycle beyond [[KarlDrais]]'s foot-propelled running machine while still leaving comfort, stability, and gear ratio unresolved.

The episode uses Michaux's phase to show that [[BicycleProductEvolution]] advanced by solving one constraint while exposing another. Pedals freed the rider's feet from the ground, but direct front-wheel drive later encouraged dangerous high-wheel designs before [[JohnKempStarley]]'s safety bicycle changed the architecture.
