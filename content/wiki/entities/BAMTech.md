# BAMTech

BAMTech is the Major League Baseball streaming technology platform that Disney buys into in [[disney-the-renaissance-and-the-empire-1]]. The source presents it as the technical base Disney needed to launch ESPN+ and [[DisneyPlus|Disney+]] rather than depending on [[Netflix]] or another external distribution interface.

BAMTech matters because Disney's direct-to-consumer shift is not only a content decision. It requires payments, apps, streaming reliability, subscriber management, data, and bundle execution, making technology infrastructure part of the [[StreamingContentTreadmill]] and [[LiveSportsStreamingTransition]].
