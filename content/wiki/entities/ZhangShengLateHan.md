# 张盛 / Zhang Sheng (late Han)

## Overview
张盛 / Zhang Sheng (late Han) enters the wiki through Hanji 1070 as the Henei man who gathers a large force around Xiaoshan and Mianchi while Cao Cao is dealing with northern and western instability. This page keeps him distinct from [[ZhangShengWesternHan|张胜 / Zhang Sheng (Western Han)]].

## Current Profile
Hanji 1070 places Zhang Sheng after [[GaoGanLateHan|高干]]'s renewed rebellion. He gathers more than ten thousand people around the Xiaoshan and Mianchi area and creates harassment pressure. [[ZhangYanHedongLateHan|张衍]] then rises in Hongnong in response to Zhang Sheng. The episode uses Zhang Sheng as one part of the wider chain proving that Cao Cao's northern advantage has not settled the western approaches.

## Key Characteristics
- Henei-origin armed actor in the post-Ye instability field.
- Gathers a large force around Xiaoshan and Mianchi.
- Creates western-route pressure while Gao Gan is also rebelling.
- Triggers or accompanies Zhang Yan's Hongnong response.
