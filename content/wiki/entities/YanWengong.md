# 燕文公 / Duke Wen of Yan

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-11-shijie-fengqi-yunyong-2-llzzalowv5vmbvgd6l-jujltkbyf]] records Yan Wen Gong's accession in 362 BCE after [[YanHuanGong|燕桓公]] dies. The episode keeps this as a compact succession notice within a year otherwise dominated by Three Jin infighting and [[QinXiaogong|秦孝公]]'s accession.

燕文公 / Duke Wen of Yan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-29-suqin-lizhi-shuangwen-zhishi-gaibian-mingyun-lrf5cqvoriyep2lkbozju-lyjaxv]] through a compact succession notice: he dies, and [[YanYiwang|燕易王]] succeeds.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-30-suqin-de-kaigua-rensheng-lkmn-fruukdxgkddx7zb4lc3o9bw]] gives him a more active role inside [[SuQin|苏秦]]'s biographical sequence. Su Qin persuades Yan Wen Gong that [[YanState|燕国]]'s quiet survival depends on [[ZhaoState|赵国]] acting as a geographic shield against [[QinState|秦国]]. Yan Wen Gong accepts the argument, funds Su Qin, and sends him toward Zhao to build relations.

The page is source-scoped and records the annalistic handoff rather than a full biography. Its main wiki function is to keep the Yan branch of the 333 BCE notice cluster explicit.
