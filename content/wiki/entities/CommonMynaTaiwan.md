# Common Myna in Taiwan / 加八哥

## Overview
[[CommonMynaTaiwan]] is the introduced myna species at the center of the Duanwen episode's Taiwan nightjar-livestream controversy and broader alien-species debate.

## Current Profile
In the episode, common mynas are clearly treated as alien to Taiwan because their original range is South Asia and mainland Southeast Asia and their arrival in Taiwan is human-mediated. The source also presents them as highly urban-adapted birds that use buildings, low-tree-density environments, parks, farmland, and other human-made settings.

The episode does not clear the species of ecological risk. It narrows the claim: Taiwan lacks sufficiently detailed evidence to say that common mynas directly caused native bird decline, so management should be tied to concrete risk, sensitive habitats, population distribution, and humane practice.

## Key Characteristics
- Human-introduced alien bird in Taiwan rather than a native species.
- Highly adapted to urban and human-modified environments.
- Publicly moralized after the nightjar egg livestream despite uncertain causality in that single incident.
- Management relevance depends on evidence of harm, location, and feasibility rather than origin label alone.

## Evidence
- Alien-species status: [[wailai-bage-yeying-zhibo-yu-taiwan-wailai-wuzhong-nanti-db02e7aa04c129b49b1f36f750155b42]] says common mynas are human-introduced into Taiwan from outside their original range.
- Livestream flashpoint: [[wailai-bage-yeying-zhibo-yu-taiwan-wailai-wuzhong-nanti-db02e7aa04c129b49b1f36f750155b42]] identifies a common myna as the bird that pecked the unhatched nightjar egg.
- Urban adaptation: [[wailai-bage-yeying-zhibo-yu-taiwan-wailai-wuzhong-nanti-db02e7aa04c129b49b1f36f750155b42]] describes introduced mynas spreading with buildings and human-modified habitats.
- Causal uncertainty: [[wailai-bage-yeying-zhibo-yu-taiwan-wailai-wuzhong-nanti-db02e7aa04c129b49b1f36f750155b42]] reports that current Taiwan evidence is insufficient to assert direct native-bird decline from introduced mynas.

## Qualifications
This page follows the episode's Taiwan-specific framing. It does not claim global common myna impacts are weak, nor does it deny that common mynas may be invasive or harmful in other places.
