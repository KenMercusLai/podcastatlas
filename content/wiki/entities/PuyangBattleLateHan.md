# 濮阳之战 / Puyang Battle (Late Han)

濮阳之战 / Puyang Battle (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-980-lvbu-xiansha-caocao-kankan-yi-ju-hua-ruhe-niuzhuan-lishi-lgggw3vmonu-3wvzabvg943v5qcg|Hanji 980]] as an earlier [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] crisis before [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s later base-recovery sequence in Hanji 986 and Hanji 987. [[PuyangTianClan|濮阳田氏]] make Cao Cao believe the city can be taken through inside support, but the move becomes a trap once he enters [[PuyangLateHan|濮阳]] and faces [[LyuBu|吕布]]'s counterattack.

The battle matters less as a clean victory than as a survival-and-recovery case. [[DianWei|典韦]] holds the close front under pressure, Cao Cao escapes by redirecting an enemy cavalryman toward a yellow horse, and the host reads the moment as [[CrisisDeceptionSurvival|临危欺敌求生]]. The later fighting turns into a hundred-day attrition problem shaped by locust damage, grain exhaustion, morale, and local resistance such as [[LiJinLateHan|李进]] at [[ChengshiCountyLateHan|乘氏县]].
