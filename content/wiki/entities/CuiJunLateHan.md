# 崔钧 / Cui Jun (late Han)

崔钧 / Cui Jun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-933-weiguan-zhidao-yousuo-wei-yousuo-buwei-lopulnufsagoqyx7nngvcqi2nhrj|Hanji 933]] as [[CuiLieLateHan|崔烈]]'s son and the blunt witness to Cui Lie's bought-office reputation collapse. When Cui Lie asks how outsiders view his new Three Excellencies rank, Cui Jun says people once thought Cui Lie worthy, but after the appointment they felt disappointed and described a "stink" around him.

The episode uses Cui Jun as comic moral evidence rather than as an independent political actor. His reply after Cui Lie threatens to beat him, invoking Shun's "small stick endure, big stick run" precedent, turns filial rhetoric back into survival judgment: Cui Lie has a promotion method, and Cui Jun has a method for avoiding harm.

This page is disambiguated from [[CuiJian|崔健 / Cui Jian]], the modern rock musician.
