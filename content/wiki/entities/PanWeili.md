## Overview
潘伟力 / Pan Weili is the football reporter and commentator who appears as guest in the [[Budongqiu|不懂球]] episode on Evergrande's football legacy. The source presents her as a long-time observer of [[GuangzhouFC]] and [[EvergrandeFootballSchool]] with recent first-hand visits to Qingyuan and the former Evergrande school site.

## Current Profile
Pan's current wiki role is evidence-provider and interpreter. She separates [[XuJiayin]] and [[Evergrande]]'s corporate wrongdoing from the football project, while still criticizing scale-first academy building, contract-breaking symbolism, high-salary distortions, and national-team boundary blur. Her strongest contribution is local continuity analysis: former academy players, [[GuangdongChenxingJuli]], [[GuangzhouLeopard]], and lingering "广州队" chants make the legacy question more concrete than a simple success-or-failure judgment.

## Key Characteristics
- Long-time Guangzhou football observer whose Evergrande reporting brought her national recognition.
- Field reporter in the episode, citing recent visits to Qingyuan matches and the former [[EvergrandeFootballSchool]] campus.
- Legacy analyst who argues for separating football assets and achievements from Evergrande's corporate collapse.
- Critic of the early "万人足校" scale impulse and of the later club-national-team boundary blur.
- Interpreter of supporter identity around [[GuangzhouFC]], [[GuangzhouLeopard]], and [[GuangdongChenxingJuli]].
