# 《河童》 / Kappa

《河童》 / Kappa is the [[AkutagawaRyunosuke|芥川龙之介]] work discussed late in [[17-diyubian-zhongsheng-jie-ku-yi-nian-ru-mo-561780565]]. The episode presents it as a dark social satire disguised by absurdity: a human outsider enters a kappa society whose inverted institutions reveal pressures in human society.

The source uses examples such as labor disposal and asking an unborn child whether it wants to enter the world to show why the work is not only comic fantasy. Its function in the episode is to widen Akutagawa from gothic cruelty into social diagnosis and late-life pessimism.
