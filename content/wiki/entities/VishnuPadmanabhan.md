# Vishnu Padmanabhan

## Overview
Vishnu Padmanabhan is the explainer voice in [[even-more-maximum-pressure-sanctions-on-iran-6a8d6a9b66c4a1e81a4c86a9]] introducing the distinction between worker effects and city place effects in wage gaps.

## Current Profile
Padmanabhan frames city wages as the product of who works in a city and what the city itself does to earnings. His segment prepares the ground for [[GauravKarna]]'s LinkedIn-based research by explaining why high average wages do not automatically prove that moving to a city will raise a specific worker's pay.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains labor-market evidence through a clear split between people effects and place effects.
- Uses city moves to make wage gaps measurable rather than relying only on static city averages.
- Connects individual wage gains to broader questions about housing, transport, borders, and worker-firm matching.
