# 信陵君 / Lord Xinling

信陵君 / Lord Xinling appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-63-degao-wangzhong-mengchangjun-shi-weijunzi-lmd0owe3rq-faponbwzhkaigprin]] as one of the [[WarringStatesFourLords|战国四公子]]. The episode does not yet give him an independent story; it names him to place [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]] inside the broader Warring States pattern of aristocrats known for养士.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-82-daqin-de-hewuqi-baiqi-laile-lo0w8mifkijhzmx7fdwcimmv7ade]] supplies his accession-point context. After [[WeiZhaowang|魏昭王]] dies and [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] succeeds, the new Wei king enfeoffs his younger brother Wei Wuji as Xinling Jun. The episode still does not develop his later rescue-of-Zhao story, but it now fixes his entry to a moment when [[WeiState|魏国]] is under heavy [[QinState|秦国]] pressure from [[BaiQi|白起]] and [[WeiRan|魏冉]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-3-lsrmq1-rjqnsja8g07bhaqvhxhpi]] gives Xinlingjun his first developed action in the wiki. [[YuQing|虞卿]] and [[WeiQiWarringStates|魏齐]] come to Wei after Zhao begins hunting Wei Qi, and Xinlingjun hesitates because receiving them risks [[QinState|秦国]]'s anger. He asks [[HouYing|侯嬴]], whose answer reframes the issue as a test of whether Xinlingjun recognizes Yu Qing's character and sacrifice.

Xinlingjun then goes out to receive Yu Qing and Wei Qi, but the hesitation has already become fatal in Wei Qi's honor world. Wei Qi hears that Xinlingjun initially wavered and kills himself, so the episode makes Xinlingjun's reputation depend not only on generosity but also on the timing and publicity of recognition.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-4-lphaverx7gyxntncak-lb5hsrqa]] adds the interpretive point that Xinlingjun's behavior should not be read as fixed anti-Qin ideology. Like [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]], he is caught in a reputation order where receiving a desperate guest can be required even when it is strategically dangerous.

The episode therefore makes Xinlingjun part of the same debate as [[YuQing|虞卿]]: later admiration can see a noble commitment to being worthy of trust, while [[XunYue|荀悦]] can read such commitment as [[DeathPartyLoyalty|死党之义与守职奉上]] that competes with office and state responsibility.
