# 刘可凡 / Liu Kefan

刘可凡 is the guest in [[ep119-duihua-liu-kefan-yong-try-catch-finally-gei-duli-zuo-chanpin-de-neihao-xie-ge-chuli-liucheng-ludjc3ab-jbwpci6tpaajtffsblx]], where he explains how he uses programming metaphors and engineering thinking to handle solo product work, teaching, ecommerce operations, and psychological friction.

In the source, Liu describes a background that moves from computer science, cybersecurity, and cloud ERP project management into independent work. His current work includes online AP Computer Science A teaching, math-modeling competition instruction, and ecommerce support for a Haining home-textile factory. He also mentions a TEDx talk about using programming thinking to handle internal friction.

His main contribution to the wiki is [[TryCatchFinallySelfManagement]]: use `try` to start with a tiny action, `catch` to convert vague fear into a [[FalsifiableProductHypothesis]], and `finally` to preserve shutdown rituals and long-term pace. His AI comments connect him to [[HumanAsAgentTool]], [[AIEngineeringThinking]], and [[HumanAgencyUnderAI]] because he treats AI as a task-decomposition and process tool, not a substitute for personal direction.
