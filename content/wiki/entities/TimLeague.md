# Tim League

## Overview
Tim League is the co-founder of [[AlamoDrafthouseCinema]] and the former engineer whose film-programming interests helped turn a failed Bakersfield theater experiment into an Austin cinema-and-hospitality chain.

## Current Profile
League's source profile combines creative conviction, extreme hands-on learning, and acknowledged leadership failure. He learned projection, construction, food preparation, and theater operations while building the business, then used unusual screenings and strong audience rules to articulate a recognizable cinema philosophy. He also says luck, family resources, and community support were indispensable.

The same account records limits to founder judgment. League says he over-focused on customers and made a serious error by rehiring an accused harasser, after which broader allegations forced a more explicit workplace response. The source says he sold his Alamo stake in the later Sony transaction and subsequently opened Metro Private Cinema in New York.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats cinema programming and audience focus as a designed cultural experience.
- Learns operational work directly, including projection, construction, food, and service.
- Uses high-variance events and clear brand positions to build attention and loyalty.
- Credits luck, inherited support, and other people's help alongside sustained labor.
- Acknowledges that personal loyalty and customer obsession contributed to governance failure.
