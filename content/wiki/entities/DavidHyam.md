# David Hyam

## Overview
David Hyam is the Burlington, North Carolina owner of [[HazelGroveCustoms]] and a caller in [[advice-line-with-kip-tindell-of-the-container-store-93508940-11b5-4b29-ba7b-eb077edee062]]. His question is how to choose among wholesale growth paths after wholesale becomes the majority of the business.

## Current Profile
The episode presents David as an acquisition entrepreneur operating a personalized-gifts business with Etsy as the main DTC channel and wholesale as the growth channel. Guy and Kip push him toward customer-segment analysis, independent retailers, commission-based reps, and service quality for larger corporate gifting orders.

## Key Characteristics
- Owner-operator who bought Hazel Grove Customs in 2016 rather than founding it from zero.
- Channel-aware founder comparing independent retailers, larger accounts, trade shows, sales reps, corporate gifting, and existing channels.
- Early wholesale scaler whose problem is prioritization more than basic demand proof.

## Evidence
- Acquisition path: [[advice-line-with-kip-tindell-of-the-container-store-93508940-11b5-4b29-ba7b-eb077edee062]] says David bought the business in 2016 after it began in 2012.
- Business metrics: [[advice-line-with-kip-tindell-of-the-container-store-93508940-11b5-4b29-ba7b-eb077edee062]] records expected revenue of about $350,000 this year versus just over $300,000 the prior year.
- Advisor response: [[advice-line-with-kip-tindell-of-the-container-store-93508940-11b5-4b29-ba7b-eb077edee062]] has Kip recommend independents and commission reps while Guy recommends best-customer analysis and service for inbound corporate buyers.

## Qualifications
The source does not show David's full P&L, gross margins, retention, or sales-cycle length, so the channel recommendations remain advice-context claims rather than validated operating conclusions.
