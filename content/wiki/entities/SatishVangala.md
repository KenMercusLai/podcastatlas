# Satish Vangala

Satish Vangala enters the wiki through [[tech-20260126-0126-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260126-0126-mp-tech-pod-128]] as director of network product development at [[AmazonWebServices|AWS]]. He guides [[MarketplaceTech]] through an AWS networking hardware lab and frames the network as an information highway for AI clusters.

His contribution is to make [[AIClusterNetworking]] concrete. Instead of describing AI infrastructure only through chips, data centers, or capital spending, Vangala points to fibers, connectors, transponders, deployment time, and reliability as the pieces that let large clusters of processors exchange data fast enough to be useful.

## Key Claims
- Large AI clusters need fast, reliable networking so processors can exchange information without bottlenecks.
- A redesigned AWS connector can combine 64 fibers in a smaller form factor and, according to Vangala, reduce deployment time by more than 54 percent.
- [[OpticalTransponders|Transponders]] convert electrical data into light waves for movement over fiber.
- AWS's networking challenges include meeting demand, scaling faster, and building resilient systems.
