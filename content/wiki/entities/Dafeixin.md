# 达菲辛 / Dafeixin

## Overview
达菲辛 / Dafeixin is the hair-growth brand sponsoring [[93-liaoliao-toufa-tu-le-jiu-hui-bian-qiang-ma-718794489]] and [[vol-80-zhengxingke-tutou-jiemei-buyao-huang-zijiu-ganhuo-bang-ni-mang-li5aqx1zugvobfuifx7hkhyvevox|VOL.80]]. Both episodes use its [[Minoxidil|minoxidil]] products as the commercial bridge into hair-loss education.

## Current Profile
The brand is presented as offering 2% and 5% minoxidil products. VOL.80 also promotes a follow-up service involving reminders, question support, and progress tracking as a way to reduce the adherence burden of long-term treatment.

The wiki keeps these product and service claims separate from the durable medical synthesis. The episodes can still inform [[HairLossDiagnosticTriage]] and [[HairLossFollicleCycle]], but sponsorship, retail promotion, product feel, tolerability, concentration selection, and service effectiveness are not independent evidence of clinical suitability.

## Key Characteristics
- Sponsor brand in two ingested podcast episodes about hair and hair loss.
- Uses minoxidil education to frame its product category.
- Markets two concentration options for different user profiles.
- Promotes comfort and low-irritation claims as supports for adherence.
- Adds reminder, question-answering, and progress-tracking services around product use.
- Appears alongside sales-event and listener-giveaway promotion in VOL.80.
- Requires explicit commercial-source qualification whenever its claims are reused.
