# 朱亥 / Zhu Hai

朱亥 / Zhu Hai enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-5-lp6yfcxoysxgcnmg8v1uljfyptof]] as the butcher whom [[HouYing|侯嬴]] deliberately visits after [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] has personally driven to [[Daliang|大梁]]'s Yi Gate to receive him.

In this episode, Zhu Hai is less an independent actor than a pressure point in Hou Ying's test. Hou Ying asks Xinlingjun to detour to the market, talks with Zhu Hai for a long time, and quietly watches whether Xinlingjun becomes irritated while his high-status carriage waits in a low-status public setting.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-6-lteqe-0ykjtv01cmxv1fhx6z0vdi]] gives Zhu Hai that later famous role. [[HouYing|侯嬴]] sends him with Xinlingjun because [[JinBi|晋鄙]] may refuse to surrender military command even when the [[TigerTallyCommandSystem|tiger tally]] matches. When Jin Bi questions the sudden handover, Zhu Hai takes a heavy iron hammer from his sleeve and kills him.

Zhu Hai therefore moves from social proof to coercive executor. In part 5, his market conversation helps test Xinlingjun's patience; in part 6, his violence makes [[StealingTigerTalliesToRescueZhao|窃符救赵]] work while also creating the episode's clearest [[MoralCostOfEffectiveRescue|moral cost]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-1-baiqi-zhisi-lrqoq1sbxljbvuayq3o1hxebxhu6]] adds the reception layer for Zhu Hai after the action. The host describes him as a典型游侠: low-status, emotionally controlled,恩怨分明, and able to give himself to a patron once recognition is settled. The episode also leaves his later path source-scoped, saying he may have followed Xinlingjun in Zhao, but the point for this page is the侠义 reading rather than a confirmed biography.
