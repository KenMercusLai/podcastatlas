# 秦二世 / 胡亥 / Qin Er Shi

秦二世 / 胡亥 / Qin Er Shi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-71-xunzide-xingbenelun-lnyfxoxauexhwbrwkdevs8m4mr2j]] as a comparison case in [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s Xunzi-backed ruler warning. The source groups him with [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]] and [[SongJunYan|宋康王 / 宋君偃]] as rulers whose situations became so bad that they could not simply step down and live safely as commoners.

This page is source-scoped. The episode does not narrate Hu Hai's reign; it uses him as a later dynastic example for the same political-security principle behind [[XunzianWangbaThreePaths|荀子王霸三分法]]: occupying supreme power magnifies both success and ruin, so rule by coercion or deception can make retreat impossible once support collapses.
