# Karen Nussbaum

## Overview
Karen Nussbaum is presented in the wiki as the office-worker organizer who helped build [[NineToFiveOrganization|9to5]] and connected clerical-worker grievances to the movie and anthem [[NineToFiveFilm|9 to 5]].

## Current Profile
The episode profiles Nussbaum as an organizer who moved from anti-war and women's-movement activism into clerical work, then noticed that office indignities were not isolated personality conflicts. Her practical role was to translate those complaints into shared workplace demands: visible job postings, higher pay, pregnancy protections, child care, paid leave, and dignity around harassment and boss power.

Her profile also depends on media translation. Because she already knew [[JaneFonda]] through anti-war activism, Nussbaum became a bridge between workers' stories and Hollywood production. After the film's release, she used screenings, rallies, and the song's recognition power to help expand the organization and union presence, while still stressing that the central workplace problems were not solved.

## Key Characteristics
- Organizer who converted private clerical-worker frustration into collective workplace demands.
- Founder or leading figure associated with [[NineToFiveOrganization|9to5]] and its national expansion.
- Bridge between anti-war, women's-movement, and office-worker organizing networks.
- Movement-side collaborator who helped [[JaneFonda]] understand the organizing value of a major motion picture.
- Retrospective interpreter who treats the film's success as recognition, not as proof that equal pay, child care, harassment, and leave problems were resolved.
