## Overview

杨潭 / Yang Tan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-457-hanxuandi-xinxiong-xiaai-shiji-gongchen-yangyun-yinhe-er-si-lpcfrhewqmmexb3ofg18iqbdsslm|Hanji 457]] as [[YangYunWesternHan|杨恽]]'s nephew and Anping marquis, a kin adviser who tries to reassure Yang Yun that his offense may not permanently end his career.

## Current Profile

Yang Tan's function in the source is to show that Yang Yun had more than one chance to lower the temperature. Unlike [[SunHuizongWesternHan|孙会宗]], who stresses fear and repentance, Yang Tan offers a restoration argument: Yang Yun has merit, the offense may not be too large, and future reuse by the state remains possible. Yang Yun's reply that merit is useless and service is not worth giving to the ruler becomes another sign of hardened resentment.

Yang Tan is then drawn into the consequences. The episode says he is demoted to commoner status after Yang Yun's punishment, making him part of the case's collateral damage.

## Key Characteristics

- Kin adviser who counsels Yang Yun toward patience and possible restoration.
- Anping marquis whose status makes the later demotion politically visible.
- Evidence of Yang Yun's refusal to preserve future room for office.
- Collateral victim in the expanded punishment around Yang Yun's case.
