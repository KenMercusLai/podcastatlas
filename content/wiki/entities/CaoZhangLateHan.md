## Overview

[[CaoZhangLateHan|曹彰]] is Cao Cao's son, presented here as the field commander who defeats a 218 CE Wuhuan revolt and induces Xianbei submission.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1162 emphasizes the shift from family to office: [[CaoCao|曹操]] tells Cao Zhang that expeditionary command is governed by ruler-minister law rather than father-son familiarity. Cao Zhang then fights and pursues aggressively enough that the episode treats the victory itself as a deterrent signal to nearby [[Xianbei|鲜卑]].

## Key Characteristics

- Cao-family prince assigned formal frontier command.
- Personally aggressive battlefield leader willing to accept high exposure.
- Commander whose victory is presented as producing deterrence beyond the defeated force.
- Subject to an explicit distinction between kinship and military office.
