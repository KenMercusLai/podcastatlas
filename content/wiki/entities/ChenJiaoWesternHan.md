## Overview

陈蟜 / 陈娇 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-392-1-hanwudi-shoule-meimei-de-qian-weihe-reng-yao-shasi-qinwaisheng-lhey4utgx3njqea-suiqohjybrpa|Hanji 392-1]] as the father of [[ZhaoPingjunWesternHan|赵平君]] and husband of [[LongluePrincessWesternHan|隆虑公主]].

## Current Profile

The episode uses Chen Jiao mostly as family background. It says he had earlier committed an offense during mourning for his mother Liu Piao / Princess Guantao, faced a capital charge, and killed himself before punishment. The host then treats his conduct as part of Zhao Pingjun's poor family environment.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han royal-affinal figure in the episode's Zhao Pingjun family chain.
- Remembered here through a capital-offense-and-suicide episode, not through a full career.
- Functions as part of the source's family-environment explanation for Zhao Pingjun's later arrogance.
