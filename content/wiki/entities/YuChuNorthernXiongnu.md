## Overview

俞厨 / Yu Chu enters the wiki as the [[NorthernXiongnuLateHan|Northern Xiongnu]] remnant leader whom [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] had installed and planned to escort back to the royal court. In Hanji 767, he abandons that arrangement after Dou's dismissal and is killed after [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]] sends cavalry under Wang Fu and [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]].

## Current Profile

Yu Chu's current profile is defined by a failed patronage-backed settlement. While Dou Xian remains powerful, Yu Chu is positioned as leader of a Northern Xiongnu remnant and is supposed to return north under escort. Dou's fall changes the incentive structure: Yu Chu abandons the agreement and returns north on his own.

The Han response is quick and punitive. Emperor He orders Wang Fu, described as jiangbing changshi, to lead more than one thousand cavalry with zhonglangjiang Ren Shang. Yu Chu is killed soon afterward, and his force is destroyed. The source uses the event as a sign that the Han court can still enforce frontier authority after Dou Xian's removal.

## Key Characteristics

- Northern Xiongnu remnant leader installed under Dou Xian's earlier arrangement.
- Dependent on a patronage-backed settlement that becomes unstable after Dou's fall.
- Defects by abandoning the agreed return route and moving north independently.
- Destroyed by Han cavalry under Wang Fu and Ren Shang after Emperor He's order.
- Source-bounded figure who marks post-Dou frontier credibility rather than a full biography.
