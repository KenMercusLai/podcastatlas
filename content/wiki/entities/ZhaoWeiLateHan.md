# 赵韪 / Zhao Wei (late Han)

## Overview
赵韪 / Zhao Wei is a Yi Province official whose profile moves from Liu Yan follower and Liu Zhang succession supporter to old-minister rebel. Hanji 1053 makes him a key case in [[LiuZhang|刘璋]]'s integration failure: senior advice goes unheard, Dongzhou troop abuses inflame locals, and Zhao Wei turns that resentment into revolt.

## Current Profile
Hanji 939 backfills Zhao Wei's earlier attachment to [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]. The episode identifies him as taicang ling and says he follows Liu Yan into [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] together with [[DongFuLateHan|董扶]], making him part of Liu Yan's first provincial entourage before the later succession role.

Hanji 981 then places Zhao Wei among the senior Yi Province officials who support Liu Zhang after Liu Yan dies. The episode names him among the provincial figures who prefer Liu Zhang's mildness and petition for his appointment. After Liu Zhang is recognized, Zhao Wei is appointed expeditionary general of the east and sent against [[LiuBiao|刘表]], with the army stationed near Quren.

Hanji 1053 reverses the practical outcome of that support. Zhao Wei repeatedly advises Liu Zhang but is ignored. As a senior old minister, he becomes resentful. He then uses local anger against Dongzhou troops to raise a revolt with tens of thousands of troops, sends gifts to Liu Biao, and gains responses from Shu, Guanghan, and Qianwei commanderies.

## Key Characteristics
- Enters Yi Province as part of Liu Yan's early entourage rather than as a native-only local actor.
- Helps legitimate Liu Zhang's succession because Liu Zhang appears mild and acceptable.
- Holds an eastern military assignment against Liu Biao before becoming connected to Liu Biao through revolt diplomacy.
- Turns ignored senior advice and local resentment into armed rebellion.
- Illustrates how imported-troop abuse can become a political resource for an ambitious or alienated insider.

## Qualifications
Zhao Wei's rebellion is not presented as pure local justice. The source gives real grievances against Dongzhou troops and Liu Zhang's weak control, but Zhao Wei also acts from old-minister resentment and seeks outside linkage through Liu Biao. The page therefore treats him as both symptom and exploiter of Yi Province's fracture.
