# 赵韪 / Zhao Wei (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-939-donghan-luanju-de-fengkuang-sanguo-xuqu-lthmvw8ihk96fuez9vamlryzcngy|Hanji 939]] backfills Zhao Wei's earlier attachment to [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]. The episode identifies him as taicang ling and says he follows Liu Yan into [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] together with [[DongFuLateHan|董扶]], making him part of Liu Yan's first provincial entourage before the later succession role.

赵韪 / Zhao Wei enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]] as one of the senior [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] officials who support [[LiuZhang|刘璋]] after [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]] dies. The episode names him among the provincial figures who prefer Liu Zhang's mildness and petition for his appointment.

After Liu Zhang is recognized, Zhao Wei is appointed expeditionary general of the east and sent against [[LiuBiao|刘表]], with the army stationed near Quren. In the source, this makes Zhao Wei part of the early operating layer of Liu Zhang's regime rather than only a succession broker.
