# Huawei Ascend 950DT / 华为昇腾 950DT

## Overview
Huawei Ascend 950DT / 华为昇腾 950DT is the AI chip named in the source as the planned basis for a large [[DeepZ]] inference data-center cluster.

## Current Profile
The source uses Ascend 950DT as a domestic AI infrastructure signal. The reported order size would make DeepZ's Inner Mongolia cluster a major Huawei-chip deployment if completed, but the source also emphasizes production capacity as a constraint that could stretch fulfillment beyond a year.

## Key Characteristics
- AI chip named in a reported large procurement plan by DeepZ.
- Intended for model inference and serving user AI requests in the source account.
- Associated with domestic AI compute substitution and data-center buildout.
- Capacity-limited supply is part of the source's strategic uncertainty.

## Evidence
- Procurement evidence: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says DeepZ plans to buy at least 160,000 Ascend 950DT chips.
- Deployment evidence: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says the chips are planned for an Inner Mongolia data center used for model inference and running.
- Capacity evidence: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says Huawei's limited production capacity may make the order take more than a year to complete.

## Qualifications
The source is a media-reported procurement claim. It does not independently verify the chip's specifications, delivery schedule, benchmark performance, wafer supply, or cluster completion.
