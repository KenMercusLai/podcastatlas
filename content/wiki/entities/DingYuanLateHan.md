# 丁原 / Ding Yuan (late Han)

丁原 / Ding Yuan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-947-dongzhuo-shoufu-lvbu-kaiqi-chengba-zhilu-lvr-24l6j8f6zvpfcmqgknmgywa1|Hanji 947]] as the late-Han commander whose force becomes the first major absorption target after [[DongZhuo|董卓]] enters [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]]. The source says [[LyuBu|吕布]] serves under him as a trusted clerk and subordinate, making Lü Bu's later defection a command-trust failure rather than a routine transfer.

His role is brief but structurally important. Dong Zhuo cannot rely on his initial small force alone, so turning Lü Bu against Ding Yuan lets him remove a rival commander and fold Ding Yuan's troops into the new coercive court field. Ding Yuan therefore becomes the source's hinge between [[MilitaryPresenceTheater|军力声势表演]] and actual force expansion.
