# 丁原 / Ding Yuan (late Han)

丁原 / Ding Yuan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-945-zhinian-tai-shen-de-ren-zhihui-yuelaiyue-tongku-lm6xaiy2pcqunke04yzqwwxsxxl|Hanji 945]] as one of the commanders drawn into [[HeJin|何进]]'s anti-eunuch escalation. He Jin orders Ding Yuan into [[HeneiCommandery|河内郡]], and the source says Ding Yuan burns Mengjin as part of the pressure around [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-947-dongzhuo-shoufu-lvbu-kaiqi-chengba-zhilu-lvr-24l6j8f6zvpfcmqgknmgywa1|Hanji 947]] later uses Ding Yuan as the late-Han commander whose force becomes the first major absorption target after [[DongZhuo|董卓]] enters Luoyang. The source says [[LyuBu|吕布]] serves under him as a trusted clerk and subordinate, making Lü Bu's later defection a command-trust failure rather than a routine transfer.

His role is brief but structurally important. Dong Zhuo cannot rely on his initial small force alone, so turning Lü Bu against Ding Yuan lets him remove a rival commander and fold Ding Yuan's troops into the new coercive court field. Ding Yuan therefore becomes the source's hinge between [[MilitaryPresenceTheater|军力声势表演]] and actual force expansion.
