# 马日磾 / Ma Ridi (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-963-shishang-bei-diantian-deng-de-diyiren-shi-ta-lkpkhlpkv3xpw-hur4b7sh-3druk|Hanji 963]] adds Ma Ridi's earlier court-literary role. After [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] imprisons [[CaiYong|蔡邕]], Ma Ridi pleads that Cai Yong's offense is not enough to justify death and that sparing him matters because he carries unfinished Han history.

The episode uses Ma Ridi as the failed warning voice. He argues that killing Cai Yong will damage Wang Yun's later reputation, but Wang Yun still refuses because he fears what a historian might record. This places Ma Ridi on the protective side of [[HistoricalRecordAuthority|史官记录话语权]] before his later Yuan Shu humiliation branch.

马日磾 / Ma Ridi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-982-ba-yi-ba-liubei-ru-xu-beihou-de-liyi-zhi-zheng-ljajdc95xtylhkn5teefz26yyyz1|Hanji 982]] as a court official sent to [[Shouchun|寿春]] with [[ZhaoQiLateHan|赵岐]]. The episode says [[YuanShu|袁术]] deliberately humiliates him, takes the imperial tally in his hands, pressures him to appoint several Yuan Shu-side officers, and tries to keep him as an adviser.

His source role is about [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] under coercion. Ma Ridi carries court authority, but the visible object that should authorize appointment can be seized by a regional warlord, turning title machinery into Yuan Shu's resource. The episode ends his branch with shame and fatal distress after the lost tally.
