# 马日磾 / Ma Ridi (late Han)

## Overview
马日磾 / Ma Ridi is a late-Han court official whose wiki role moves from learned high office to a failed warning voice and then to the vulnerability of imperial envoys under regional coercion. His career makes court prestige visible, but Hanji 982 and 1021 show that prestige and imperial objects can be humiliated once the court lacks force.

## Current Profile
Ma Ridi is best read as an institutional figure whose authority repeatedly depends on a weakening court setting. He can rise to taiwei and speak for the value of historical record, yet he cannot save [[CaiYong|蔡邕]] from [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] or protect the imperial tally from [[YuanShu|袁术]] at [[Shouchun|寿春]]. Hanji 1021 closes the branch by returning his body to the capital and turning his funeral treatment into a judgment about honor, shame, and the limits of envoy dignity after coercion.

## Key Characteristics
- Learned high official whose taiwei role is exposed to omen-linked office accountability.
- Court-literary warning voice who tries to protect Cai Yong and unfinished Han historical writing.
- Imperial envoy whose tally and appointment authority can be seized by Yuan Shu.
- Posthumous figure whose funeral honor is contested after the court receives his body.

## Evidence
- High office and omen vulnerability: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-941-shenchu-guanchang-ta-jing-xiang-ganfan-huangdi-liu-fqiz9pi7u6cltrn-cjmjz-r|Hanji 941]] moves Ma Ridi upward to taiwei, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-942-yue-youxiu-de-ren-huode-yue-lei-heli-ma-lkwlrcgnt-kxxc2qqz6z6wkg7e5z|Hanji 942]] removes him after an eclipse in a [[CelestialOmenPoliticalResponsibility|天象政治责任]] sequence.
- Historian-protection role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-963-shishang-bei-diantian-deng-de-diyiren-shi-ta-lkpkhlpkv3xpw-hur4b7sh-3druk|Hanji 963]] shows Ma Ridi pleading for Cai Yong's life because Cai Yong carries unfinished Han history and because killing him will damage Wang Yun's reputation.
- Envoy vulnerability at Shouchun: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-982-ba-yi-ba-liubei-ru-xu-beihou-de-liyi-zhi-zheng-ljajdc95xtylhkn5teefz26yyyz1|Hanji 982]] has Yuan Shu humiliate Ma Ridi, seize the imperial tally, extract appointments, and try to keep him as an adviser.
- Posthumous honor debate: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1021-caocao-shouwo-zhongquan-weishenme-rongbuxia-yangbiao-lr-y4xqanwjyslvf4fbwxjuymtun|Hanji 1021]] reports Ma Ridi's body returning to the capital and Kong Rong opposing a higher funeral because the Shouchun humiliation had compromised the mission.

## Qualifications
Ma Ridi's page does not judge him only as failed or dishonored. The same evidence that shows his humiliation also shows the structural weakness of late-Han court authority: an envoy can carry legitimacy and still lack the force to protect its symbols. Hanji 1021 narrows the funeral question but does not erase his earlier learned reputation or warning role.
