# Muslim Brotherhood

The Muslim Brotherhood is the political and social movement at the center of [[spring-then-fall-a-weakened-muslim-brotherhood-6a7aef1b616564bf00d9b354]]. [[GarethBrown]] describes it as grounded in Islamic values and organized through more than elections: welfare programs, businesses, community support, and social-service networks helped it survive periods of exclusion from formal power.

The source presents the Brotherhood as a post-Arab Spring loser after an initial opening. It appeared to benefit from uprisings across the region, and [[MohamedMorsi]] became [[Egypt]]'s first democratically elected president, but [[AbdelFattahAlSisi]]'s coup and the broader authoritarian backlash left the movement's leadership dead, jailed, exiled, fragmented, or caught in internal disputes.

Its weakness is not treated as permanent disappearance. The episode says economic stagnation, regression, dignity politics, and anger connected to [[Gaza]] and [[Israel]] could preserve demand for opposition movements, even if the Brotherhood itself is no longer the obvious beneficiary.
