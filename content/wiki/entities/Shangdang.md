# 上党 / Shangdang

上党 / Shangdang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-92-lishishang-di-yi-wei-zhuming-tiangou-shi-shui-1-lmwf0q0-3hxb6d4hro8fdmc3zdxp]] as the [[HanState|韩国]] northern territory isolated after [[QinState|秦国]] attacks [[HanNanyangWarringStates|韩国南阳]] and [[TaihangRoad|太行道]]. The episode says that once the route back to Xinzheng is cut, Qin conquest of Shangdang appears only a matter of time.

The source's key point is that Shangdang does not simply fall into Qin hands. Its defending commander is described as unwilling to surrender to Qin and inclined to offer the territory to [[ZhaoState|赵国]] instead. The episode explicitly treats this as the setup for a later, extremely large Qin-Zhao annihilation-war branch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-1-ljshdryvf9qgabtn-siontthzsok]] names that local actor as [[FengTing|冯亭]] and gives the offer its political logic. Shangdang's seventeen cities, land, and people are offered to [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]] because Feng Ting expects Qin to attack Zhao afterward, forcing Zhao to align with Han. The episode also explains local willingness through Three Jin affinity, geographic familiarity, and fear of Qin rather than through attachment to Zhao's ruler alone.
