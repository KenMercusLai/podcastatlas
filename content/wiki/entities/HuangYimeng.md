# 黄一孟 / Huang Yimeng

Huang Yimeng / 黄一孟 appears in [[no-214-xunzhao-tonglei-xiaohongshu-bilibili-yiji-wuhuabamen-de-naxie-shequ-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-26-1005977305]] as the founder associated with [[TapTap]]. The source presents TapTap as his attempt to build a game distribution and community surface that did not depend on the dominant high-take-rate channel model.

His wiki role is tied to [[ChineseMobileInternetCommunities]]: the episode uses him to show how a transaction-adjacent vertical category can still need community trust, reviews, and curation.
