## Overview

张任 / Zhang Ren is a Yizhou defender presented in the current source as a principal military obstacle during [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s prolonged attack on 雒城.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1144-fengchu-pangtong-shi-zenme-si-de-lpc9kyfhupts3zfjwjsv4-ugjxox|Hanji 1144]] places Zhang Ren inside the defensive campaign that delays Liu Bei's advance toward [[Chengdu|成都]] for roughly a year in the episode's chronology. The fighting forms the immediate setting for [[PangTong|庞统]]'s death by arrows during an assault.

The bounded evidence supports a narrower profile than the later legend-rich image: Zhang Ren represents organized Yizhou resistance and the material difficulty of the conquest, but the source does not establish his complete command record, personal responsibility for Pang Tong's death, or later dialogue and death traditions.

## Key Characteristics

- Serves the Yizhou defense against Liu Bei's advance.
- Is associated with prolonged resistance around 雒城.
- Helps show that the final Yizhou campaign was not an effortless takeover.
