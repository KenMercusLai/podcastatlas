# Eric Abramovich

## Overview
Eric Abramovich is the co-founder of [[Roc360]] interviewed in [[the-loan-at-the-heart-of-a-new-foreclosure-crisis]] about private real estate lending and [[DSCRLoans|DSCR loans]].

## Current Profile
Abramovich is the episode's market-builder and product-defense voice. He explains how private lenders served fix-and-flip investors after the financial crisis, how Wall Street capital entered the channel, and why DSCR loans became attractive to landlords and investors. He rejects a simple comparison to pre-crisis no-income mortgages, arguing that the country needs credit for housing repair and construction.

## Key Characteristics
- He locates DSCR-loan origins in post-crisis private lending for real estate investors.
- He explains why Wall Street capital liked the product's higher yields.
- He defends defaults as a normal part of lending rather than automatic evidence of a broken market.
- He argues that post-crisis credit became too tight.
- He frames outside capital as useful for a housing shortage and old-home repair needs.

## Evidence
Market history:
- [[the-loan-at-the-heart-of-a-new-foreclosure-crisis]] quotes Abramovich's account that DSCR lending grew out of private fix-and-flip lending after the financial crisis.

Defense of the product:
- [[the-loan-at-the-heart-of-a-new-foreclosure-crisis]] says Abramovich rejected the pre-crisis comparison and argued that credit, housing shortage, and repair needs require capital.

Baltimore exposure:
- [[the-loan-at-the-heart-of-a-new-foreclosure-crisis]] says reporters told Planet Money that about $35 million of the bad Baltimore loans came from private lenders associated with Abramovich's company.

## Qualifications
Abramovich's account is a participant defense from inside the private-lending market. The episode balances it with reporter findings, neighborhood harm, and unresolved investigation signals.
