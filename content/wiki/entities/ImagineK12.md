# Imagine K-12

Imagine K-12 appears in [[tsr-s5-johncoogan-audiofile-1-tsr-s5-johncoogan-audiofile-1]] as the education-technology accelerator that accepted [[JohnCoogan]] and his high school friend in summer 2012. The episode frames it as closely related to [[YCombinator]] and associated with [[JeffRalston]].

In the source, Imagine K-12 functions as an adjacent YC-style selection surface. Coogan's education app did not become a durable company, but the program moved him into the Silicon Valley startup environment where the later [[Soylent]] team formed after Demo Day.
