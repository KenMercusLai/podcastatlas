# Richard Neville, Earl of Warwick

## Overview

Richard Neville, Earl of Warwick, was the dominant Yorkist magnate whose failed effort to guide Edward IV's diplomacy and marriage helped turn patronage grievance into rebellion and regime reversal.

## Current Profile

Warwick initially helped the young Edward IV secure and govern a contested throne. The king's secret marriage to Elizabeth Woodville undercut Warwick's French marriage negotiations and signaled independence from his tutelage. As Woodville influence expanded, Warwick allied with Clarence, restored Henry VI, and forced Edward into exile before dying at Barnet during Edward's return.

## Key Characteristics

- Powerful Yorkist supporter who expected substantial influence over the young king.
- Advocate of a French diplomatic alignment and royal marriage.
- Personally and strategically humiliated by Edward IV's secret English marriage.
- Ally of [[GeorgeDukeOfClarence]] in rebellion against Edward.
- Political turncoat who restored the Lancastrian regime before his defeat and death at Barnet.
