# Natascha Wodin / 纳塔莎·沃丁

Natascha Wodin / 纳塔莎·沃丁 is the Ukrainian-descended German writer and translator at the center of [[05-ta-laizi-maliwuboer-ruguo-ni-kanjian-guo-wo-ceng-jiandao-de-533375925]]. The episode presents her as the daughter of [[OstarbeiterForcedLabor|Eastern forced laborers]] whose mother [[YevgeniaSheCameFromMariupol|叶弗根尼亚]] died by suicide when Wodin was ten, leaving her with little direct knowledge of her mother's family, hometown, or prewar life.

Her search in [[SheCameFromMariupol|《她来自马里乌波尔》]] begins as a personal need to find the mother she barely knew, then expands through [[KonstantinMariupolGenealogist|康斯坦丁]]'s help into a reconstruction of a larger family damaged by revolution, famine, Stalinist repression, Nazi forced labor, postwar fear, and silence. In the wiki, Wodin anchors the link between [[FamilyMemoryNonfiction]] and [[TraumaNarrativeIntegration]].
