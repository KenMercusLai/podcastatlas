# 后唐 / Later Tang

Later Tang / 后唐 is the Five Dynasties regime that defeats [[LaterLiang|后梁]] in [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]]'s periodization of [[FiveDynastiesTenKingdoms|五代十国]]. The episode treats the Later Tang moment as a serious attempt at reunification that ultimately fails.

Through [[LiCunxu|李存勖]], [[LiSiyuan|李嗣源]], and the Shatuo military world around [[LiKeyong|李克用]], Later Tang also helps the source explain adoptive-son networks, military kinship, and the difficulty of turning wartime command into stable dynasty.
