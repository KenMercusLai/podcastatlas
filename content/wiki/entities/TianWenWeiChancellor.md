# 田文 / Tian Wen (Wei chancellor)

田文 / Tian Wen is the [[WeiState|魏国]] chancellor in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-1-lq5r3xe-3y7al9nkbc8kyhntipll]]. The episode stresses that he is not the later Mengchangjun Tian Wen, and that little is known about his earlier record in this telling.

His wiki role is to make [[TransitionFitOverMerit]] concrete. When [[WuQi|吴起]] compares military command, civil administration, treasury strength, West River defense, and pressure on [[QinState|秦国]], [[HanState|韩国]], and [[ZhaoState|赵国]], Tian Wen accepts that Wu Qi's measurable achievements are greater. He wins the argument by pointing to the political moment: [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]] has newly succeeded, senior ministers distrust one another, and the people have not yet settled into confidence.

The source presents Tian Wen as a stabilizing figure whose value lies less in superior personal accomplishment than in fit for succession turbulence. Wu Qi's eventual silence and concession make Tian Wen the episode's counterweight to a purely merit-based view of office.
