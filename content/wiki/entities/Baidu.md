# Baidu

## Overview
[[Baidu]] is a Chinese search platform whose PC-era advertising and community scale faces mobile and AI-answer disruption; the same source set also places it in healthcare-search trust, cloud infrastructure, potential platform integration and overseas robotaxis. These are distinct roles, not evidence of one successful AI replacement business.

## Current Profile
The reported decline of online marketing and the monetization pause for AI search sharpen the tradeoff: a more complete answer can reduce the click path that funded classic search. The company retains other assets, but cloud exhibition, reported partnerships and export plans require their own validation.

## Key Characteristics
- Its PC-era search/advertising flywheel is squeezed by closed-app discovery and AI answers before a replacement monetization loop is proven.
- Keyword communities and travel comparison grew out of search, but user trust and platform consolidation complicate their legacy.
- Medical search exposes both ad-ranking authority risks and ordinary patient self-diagnosis anxiety.
- AI cloud and reported China-platform integrations represent infrastructure/partner roles, not proof of mass consumer model adoption.
- Driverless taxi exports are a proposed service-standard opportunity rather than a completed overseas operating record.
- Early employee-option upside is a separate historical workplace association, not evidence of current equity liquidity.

## Qualifications
- Revenue figures are source-dated reportage, not audited here. The cash-cow, product and management diagnosis belongs to the hosts; social-search substitution includes anecdote, not a measured Baidu market-share series.
- WAIC presence does not establish large orders, let alone outperformance of [[ScaleUpAIInterconnect]] alternatives. The Apple integration is a reported prospective arrangement, and the Swiss service a planned launch, not verified deployment.
- Patient anxiety is not the same as a documented ad-ranking scandal. The options recollection is not Baidu's current employee-compensation policy.

## What Changed
- Search economics now sit beside, rather than subsume, community governance, medical trust, infrastructure and export opportunities.
- Reported figures, plans and host explanations are separated from observed operating outcomes.
