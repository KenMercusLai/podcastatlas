# Chhatral Rinpoche

## Overview
Chhatral Rinpoche appears in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-97450677c47f502770248abb02f35793-163628914-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-97450677c47f502770248abb02f35793-163628914]] as [[ShyalpaRinpoche]]'s principal teacher and a highly respected Himalayan [[Dzogchen]] master.

## Current Profile
The source uses Chhatral Rinpoche as the lineage anchor for Shyalpa Rinpoche's training. He is presented as a teacher of preliminary practices, higher teachings, and Dzogchen empowerment, and as someone whose compassion took material form through large-scale fish release into the Ganges.

## Key Characteristics
- Himalayan Dzogchen master named as Shyalpa Rinpoche's principal teacher.
- Lineage figure through whom study, service, blessing, and empowerment are transmitted.
- Associated in the source with life-release charity through large annual fish releases.
- Evidence of the episode's contrast between lived lineage and technique-only spirituality.
