# 贤解羌 / Xianjie Qiang (Western Han)

## Overview

贤解羌 / Xianjie Qiang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-481-wei-feng-xihan-fengfengshi-yiju-mie-diguo-libgqzvquegllma9soju57k_jxue|Hanji 481]] as the Qiang branch or leader-associated group whose rebellion in [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]] triggers [[FengFengshiWesternHan|冯奉世]]'s force-size debate under [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]].

## Current Profile

The source gives Xianjie Qiang as a compact crisis actor rather than a developed polity. Its importance is strategic: Feng Fengshi estimates the rebel strength at more than thirty thousand, treats the group as numerous enough to punish insufficient Han force, and warns that failure to suppress it could weaken Han deterrence toward other frontier groups.

The page therefore preserves a narrow entity identity. It does not merge Xianjie Qiang with later Eastern Han Qiang groups such as Shaodang or Xianling, whose pages belong to different chronological and political contexts.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Qiang rebel actor named in a 42 BCE Longxi crisis.
- Appears during poor harvest and hunger, increasing the court's provisioning anxiety.
- Estimated by Feng Fengshi as more than thirty thousand people or fighters in the source's telling.
- Functions as a deterrence test: if Han responds weakly, other frontier groups may follow.
- Kept distinct from later Eastern Han Qiang entities unless future sources prove continuity.
