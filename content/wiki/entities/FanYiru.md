# 樊一如 / Fan Yiru

樊一如 / Fan Yiru appears in [[vol-125-riben-daodi-hai-xing-bu-xing-chuantai-dongya-guancha-ju-lsilax772olwi9xvvvfz6xcelfqw]] as the [[EastAsiaObservatory|东亚观察局]] speaker in conversation with [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]]. She steers the discussion from podcast formats and earlier Japan-life observations into Japan's stock-market narrative, foreign labor, overtourism, industrial policy, and long-term social openness.

Her role is comparative and probing. She repeatedly asks whether Japan's current advantages are durable development capacity or a comfortable use of earlier industrial and institutional achievements, helping the episode arrive at [[JapanComfortableStagnation]] rather than a simple bullish or bearish answer.
