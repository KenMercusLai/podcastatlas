# Sewing Machine Combination

The Sewing Machine Combination is the 1856 patent-pool agreement in [[the-invention-invention]]. The source says [[GroverAndBaker]], [[WheelerAndWilson]], [[IsaacMerrittSinger|Singer]], and [[EliasHoweJr|Howe]] joined the arrangement after [[OrlandoBPotter]] proposed pooling their mutually blocking sewing-machine patents.

In the episode, the combination becomes the first major American [[PatentPool]] and the historical model later revisited by the [[MPEGPatentPool]]. It solved a production blockade by turning overlapping claims into shared royalties, but it also previews the later [[CollaborationVsCollusion]] problem: cooperation among rivals can help consumers or become market coordination.
