# Iberian Peninsula / 伊比利亚半岛

## Overview
The Iberian Peninsula / 伊比利亚半岛 is the European landmass opposite North Africa that frames the Ceuta episode's discussion of [[Spain]], [[Portugal]], al-Andalus, Reconquista, and maritime expansion.

## Current Profile
In this source, Iberia is not only a European region. Its southern coast is part of a two-shore world around the [[StraitOfGibraltar]], and its internal Christian-Muslim frontier history helps explain why Portuguese expansion could move from mainland consolidation to North Africa and the Atlantic.

## Key Characteristics
- Contains the Spanish and Portuguese mainland setting behind the episode.
- Sits opposite [[Ceuta]] and [[Morocco]] across the Strait of Gibraltar.
- Holds the al-Andalus and Reconquista sequence that precedes early-modern overseas expansion.
