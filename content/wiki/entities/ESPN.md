# ESPN

ESPN appears in [[the-nfl-1]] as a modern NFL media and platform partner. The episode says the NFL agreed to sell NFL Network and its fantasy app to ESPN in exchange for a 10% equity stake in ESPN, pending regulatory review at the time of the source.

The source uses ESPN to show how the [[NationalFootballLeague|NFL]] can exchange content assets for strategic position in a future distributor. That makes ESPN part of [[LiveSportsStreamingTransition]] and [[SportsMediaRights]], not only a broadcaster.

[[disney-the-renaissance-and-the-empire-1]] adds ESPN as [[TheWaltDisneyCompany]]'s cable-era cash engine. The episode says Disney acquired ESPN through [[CapitalCitiesABC]], then used ESPN's affiliate-fee power and sports-rights leverage to fund much of the Iger-era expansion into [[Pixar]], [[MarvelEntertainment|Marvel]], and [[Lucasfilm]]. Cord-cutting later turns ESPN from an endlessly compounding crown jewel into a valuable but more exposed asset.
