# 唐周 / Tang Zhou (late Han)

唐周 / Tang Zhou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-923-chengnianrende-dingji-henjin-guzi-lide-juejue-lifzh-gohpqnmdznnfv72tndqf8h|Hanji 923]] as the [[TaipingDao|太平道]] disciple from Jinan who discloses [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]]'s uprising plan to the court in spring 184. The episode treats his report as the trigger that exposes [[MaYuanyiLateHan|马元义]], palace contacts, and the planned date of coordinated action.

Tang Zhou's function is therefore not a full biography but a timing break. His disclosure lets [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s court arrest Ma Yuanyi and begin a purge of believers, while also forcing Zhang Jiao to notify local leaders and begin the [[YellowTurbansLateHan|Yellow Turban]] uprising early.
