# Toast

Toast is the restaurant technology company at the center of [[toast-aman-narang-how-a-long-wait-for-the-dinner-check-launched-a-2-billion-business-6eca81bc-b0e1-47e2-a87d-f3b243e76a97]]. In the [[HowIBuiltThis]] episode, [[AmanNarang]], [[SteveFredette]], and [[JohnGraham]] move from a slow-check mobile payment idea into a broader cloud-based restaurant platform after discovering how fragmented restaurant point-of-sale and back-office workflows were.

The source presents Toast as a [[RestaurantOperatingSystem]] case. The company starts with payment and POS integration pain at [[FirebrandSaints]], learns reliability the hard way during the failed first live install at [[DwellTime]], expands through [[GordonFoodService]], and later adds COVID-era QR menu, ordering, and payment tools through [[OffPremiseRestaurantCommerce]]. The episode also frames Toast's 2021 listing and Aman's 2024 CEO return as a [[PublicCompanyTransition]] and [[StageAppropriateHiring]] moment rather than a clean break from the earlier growth era.
