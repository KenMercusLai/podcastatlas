# Olipop

## Overview
Olipop is the prebiotic soda company co-founded by [[BenGoodwin]] and used as the established CPG context in [[advice-line-with-ben-goodwin-of-olipop-f9d3fe17-eb2c-440d-b223-990fed970727]].

## Current Profile
The episode frames Olipop as a scaled mission-driven beverage brand still trying to balance broad consumer appeal with nutrition education. Its current source-scoped position combines a brand refresh, fiber education, remote organization, research claims, and a revenue target that Goodwin says should not override product integrity.

## Key Characteristics
- Better-for-you soda brand positioned around prebiotics, fiber education, taste, and mission impact.
- Valued by Guy Raz at more than $2 billion in the episode setup.
- Running a refresh that includes visual identity, sonic identity, and a fiber education campaign.
- Operates as a fully remote company with roughly 280 employees and possible growth toward 300.
- Goodwin says the company is already well over half a billion dollars in gross revenue and is targeting $1 billion.
- Serves as a reference case for maintaining standards despite acquisition pressure in the beverage category.
