# Duncan French

## Overview
Duncan French is an exercise physiologist and applied performance leader presented in the source as vice president of performance at the [[UFC]] Performance Institute.

## Current Profile
French's profile joins doctoral work on neuroendocrinology and resistance exercise with more than two decades of coaching and performance-science work across professional and Olympic sport. His distinctive contribution in the episode is [[AdaptationLedProgramming]]: define the desired adaptation, then coordinate training load, recovery, nutrition, environmental exposure, and measurement around that goal rather than treating any tool as inherently beneficial.

His career path moves from physical-education teaching through William Kraemer's research group, the British Olympic system, Newcastle United, Notre Dame, and the UFC. The source portrays this breadth as an applied communication skill as much as scientific expertise: he translates among athletes, coaches, researchers, and organizational leaders while retaining what he calls a white-belt learning mentality.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines exercise-physiology research with practical strength-and-conditioning leadership.
- Organizes interventions around specific adaptations, training phases, and competition demands.
- Treats recovery as the capacity to reproduce high-quality technical and physical work rather than as passive rest alone.
- Uses individual tracking and structured experimentation instead of assuming identical responses to the same workout.
- Works across scientific, coaching, athlete, and executive communication contexts.
