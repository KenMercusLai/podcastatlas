# Stephen Davis

## Overview

Stephen Davis is the incarcerated man whose death after an encounter with a guard becomes a central investigation in [[TheAlabamaSolution|The Alabama Solution]].

## Current Profile

The episode says the filmmakers learned Davis had been badly beaten and transferred to a hospital, where [[AndrewJarecki]] and [[CharlotteKaufman]] discovered that he had died. His mother, Sandy Ray, believed prison officials were misleading her and asked the production to help record their calls; an unidentified prison whistleblower later disputed the official account.

The documentary uses a small number of childhood photographs to restore individuality without making Davis's offense the condition for concern about how he was treated. [[MalcolmGladwell]] describes remaining emotionally invested despite receiving little conventional biographical backstory.

## Key Characteristics

- Central subject of the documentary's death and alleged-cover-up investigation.
- Represented through contemporaneous family contact as well as prison evidence.
- Grounds the film's distinction between human dignity and judgments about a conviction.
