## Overview

伏完 / Fu Wan is the father of [[FuShouEmpress|伏皇后]], appointed zhijinwu when she becomes empress and later named as the recipient of her secret request to remove [[CaoCao|曹操]].

## Current Profile

Fu Wan links court office, imperial marriage, and exposed kinship. His 195 CE appointment continues ordinary name order inside Li Jue-Guo Si captivity; the later letter makes him the family channel through which Fu Shou seeks action against Cao Cao. Hanji 1150 does not show him carrying out that request, so his profile remains one of potential access and inherited risk rather than demonstrated operational leadership.

## Key Characteristics

- Father of Empress Fu Shou.
- Receives zhijinwu office during the Li-Guo captivity.
- Becomes the intended family contact for anti-Cao action.
- Illustrates how an empress's natal family can be both court resource and purge exposure.

## Qualifications

The current evidence does not establish that Fu Wan acted on the letter, commanded a developed conspiracy, or was alive at every later enforcement stage implied by the episode's compressed narrative. Exact chronology and kin punishments remain source-scoped.
