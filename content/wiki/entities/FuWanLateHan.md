# 伏完 / Fu Wan (late Han)

伏完 / Fu Wan (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-985-fanmu-chengchou-liguo-zhiluan-de-xijie-tanjiu-lt-t2un0zcudkige9ucl1txa2hhk|Hanji 985]] as [[FuShouEmpress|伏寿]]'s father. When [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] appoints Fu Shou as empress in April 195 CE, Fu Wan is appointed zhijinwu.

His page is source-scoped. Fu Wan matters here because normal court household and office language continues even while [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] holds the emperor and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] holds ministers, making the appointment a small case of [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] under coercion rather than a sign that ordinary command has recovered.
