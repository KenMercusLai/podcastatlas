# Ethan Jordan

## Overview

Ethan Jordan is a young North Carolina farmer used as a field case for news consumption in the rural Border Belt.

## Current Profile

Jordan's media diet combines [[NewsBreak]], Fox News, Facebook posts and comments, television, and personal relationships. Algorithmic relevance helps him find agricultural stories, while local social posts can provide video and claimed eyewitness testimony when newspaper coverage is weak. He recognizes that these sources can be biased.

His political account shows information judgment interacting with identity and personal contact. He describes Fox News as sometimes too far right, objects more strongly to coverage he sees as anti-Trump and pro-Harris, and emphasizes that Mark Robinson recognized him personally. The episode interprets this as a case in which perceived fairness, local identity, and relationship can outweigh adverse national reporting.
