# Jodi Kantor

Jodi Kantor appears in [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]] through her career-advice book *How to Start*. The episode treats her journalism success as impressive but atypical, making it an imperfect template for Gen Z graduates trying to choose careers under AI pressure.

The source places Kantor beside [[BillGurley]] as a successful adviser whose advice can contain useful fragments while still over-weighting passion, dream pursuit, and unusual achievement paths. Her role helps define [[CareerAdviceSurvivorshipBias]].
