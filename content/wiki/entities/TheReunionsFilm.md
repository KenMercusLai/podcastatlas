# 《吉祥如意》 / The Reunions

## Overview
《吉祥如意》 is represented as [[DapengDirector|大鹏]]'s family-based experimental film, where a planned test of authorship and audience framing was overtaken by an actual family death during production.

## Current Profile
The project began with rural, dialect, short-film, and audience-label experiments and used one crew to film a story while another filmed the making of that story. Dapeng's grandmother's fatal accident changed the evidentiary and emotional status of the material, leaving the completed work both artistically important and personally difficult to revisit.

## Key Characteristics
- Hybrid construction combining a filmed family story with observation of its production.
- Experiment in how prestige labels influence audience interpretation.
- Work transformed by an unplanned death that exceeded the original formal design.

## Evidence
- Formal design - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] describes two crews and the later formation of the paired sections 《吉祥》 and 《如意》.
- Audience framing - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] describes a planned comparison between “master's new work” and “student short” labels for the same material.
- Family rupture - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] says Dapeng's grandmother fell and died during the shoot, changing the project and its later emotional accessibility.

## Qualifications
The source is Dapeng's retrospective account of a family trauma and artistic process. The page does not infer family members' views or settle the film's documentary ethics and reception.
