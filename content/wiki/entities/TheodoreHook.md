# Theodore Hook

Theodore Hook appears in [[socialist-mobility-a-telling-michigan-vote-6a74600ec3e1347e8b5bf216]] as the British novelist credited in the source with sending the first postcard in 1840 by mailing a card to himself in London.

The page keeps this as a source-scoped postcard-history detail. Hook helps [[PostcardMediaDecline]] reach back to the medium's origin rather than treating postcards only as a late tourist habit.
