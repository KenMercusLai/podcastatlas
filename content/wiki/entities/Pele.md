# Pelé

## Overview

Pelé was a Brazilian footballer presented as both an exceptional World Cup player and a politically ambiguous national symbol during Brazil's military dictatorship.

## Current Profile

The episode traces Pelé from poverty and childhood work to [[Brazil]]'s 1958 World Cup victory at age seventeen and the globally iconic 1970 team. His Black identity, professionalism, and international celebrity helped Brazil project football genius, social mobility, multiracial optimism, and modernity. That representative power also exposed him to political appropriation: the military regime used the national team for legitimacy, while the source records his later defense of the dictatorship but resists assuming that open opposition would have been safe or simple.

## Key Characteristics

- Brazilian footballer who became a global symbol through World Cup success.
- Seventeen-year-old member of Brazil's 1958 World Cup-winning team.
- Black national icon used to project a modern and multiracial Brazilian identity.
- Professional, aspirational public figure contrasted in the episode with Garrincha's wayward image.
- Politically ambiguous celebrity whose sporting symbolism was useful to military rule.
