# Birkenstock

Birkenstock appears in [[vans-kuangwei-fengguang-buzai-jingdian-fanbuxie-weishenme-maibudongle-1006742982]] as a comfort-forward footwear contrast to [[Vans]] and [[Converse]]. The source says Birkenstock-style sandals entered young consumers' everyday outfits through loose, light, comfortable wear, and cites 18% revenue growth in fiscal 2025.

In the episode's argument, Birkenstock matters less as a direct skate-shoe competitor than as evidence that consumers are letting comfort and relaxed fit define style. That makes it part of [[ComfortLedFootwearDemand]] and a pressure point against flat canvas shoes with limited cushioning or arch support.
