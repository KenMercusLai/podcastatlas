# Dino Mavroukis

Dino Mavroukis appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-saronic-founders-autonomous-warships-chinas-230x-advantage-swarms-of-robot-ships-42334230]] as a co-founder of [[Saronic]]. In the interview he makes the main strategic case for [[NavalShipbuildingThroughput]], arguing that the [[UnitedStates]] needs cheaper, faster-produced autonomous naval mass to offset [[China]]'s shipbuilding advantage.

Dino also frames Saronic's work as a procurement and industrial-base challenge. He criticizes cost-plus incentives, says Saronic used private capital and internal R&D to build [[MarauderSaronic|Marauder]], and presents [[PortAlpha|Port Alpha]] as a way to build a different kind of shipyard around autonomous vessels.
