## Current Profile

The source says Shangguan Wan'er was favored by Tang Gaozong and made a cairen, but was soon sent to Wu Zetian's side. In the episode's structure, this is not a developed biography of Gaozong; it is a first example of how Shangguan Wan'er's relationships with powerful men were constrained by palace authority and rarely translated into autonomous security.
