# 华阳君 / Huayang Jun (Qin)

华阳君 / Huayang Jun appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-89-qinwang-jing-bei-ta-dui-mabaonan-ljugedx8qxhtuzd1ja-xiwxgse19]] as one of the four Qin nobles [[FanJu|范雎]] names when warning [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] that close kin and ministers are crowding out royal authority.

The source does not give Huayang Jun an independent action in this episode. His role is structural: together with [[WeiRan|魏冉]], [[JingyangJunQin|泾阳君]], and [[GaolingJunQin|高陵君]], he helps make [[QinFourNoblesCourtPower|秦国四贵权力结构]] a named obstacle to strategy and kingship.
