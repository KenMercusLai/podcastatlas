# Frontier Tower

Frontier Tower is the vertical-tech-village case in [[tanfang-hacker-house-guigu-nianqingren-zhengzai-banjin-ai-chuangye-sushe-s10e10-f0870843-fd16-49f5-8f24-80281f9fff77]]. The source says three German founders acquired or controlled a 16-story, 90,000-plus-square-foot Market Street building in San Francisco and wanted to cluster theme floors around longevity, Ethereum, AI, deep tech, crypto, robotics, and neurotechnology.

The episode frames Frontier Tower as a high-fixed-cost physical bet on [[SanFranciscoBayArea]] startup density. Its revenue possibilities include membership fees, private offices, full-floor leases, sponsorships, and potential co-living rents. Compared with [[TheResidency]] or [[HF0]], the emphasis is less on taking equity from selected founders and more on whether real estate can become [[HackerHouseStartupInfrastructure]] for specialized technology communities.
