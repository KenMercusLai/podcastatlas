## Overview

寿光侯刘李 / Liu Li, Shouguang Marquis enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-728-weihe-mingchen-nan-shanzhong-beihou-de-yuanyin-ni-gai-dong-ltjl2bgpyyz1bmwe-4mfcyd9e6cf|Hanji 728]] as Liu Xuan's son and the guest-mobilizing killer whose action helps trigger Guangwu's purge of princely guests.

## Current Profile

Hanji 728 places Liu Li inside the most dangerous branch of the episode's prince-guest theme. He is favored by [[LiuFuPeiKingEasternHan|Pei King Liu Fu]] and resents Liu Penzi. The source says he gathers guests and kills Liu Penzi's elder brother Liu Gong.

The killing changes the political status of princely guest networks. What Ma Yuan had warned about as a structural risk becomes a concrete case of private violence tied to a royal household. Liu Xiu reacts with anger, punishes Liu Fu through brief imprisonment, and then orders a nationwide arrest of princely guests in which thousands are killed through implication.

## Key Characteristics

- Son of Liu Xuan and Shouguang Marquis in the source's Eastern Han setting.
- Favored by Pei King Liu Fu, making his private conduct politically attached to a princely household.
- Mobilizes guests to kill Liu Gong because of resentment toward Liu Penzi.
- Functions as the immediate trigger that turns guest-network suspicion into a national purge.
