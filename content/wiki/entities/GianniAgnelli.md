# Gianni Agnelli

Gianni Agnelli appears in [[ferrari-1]] as both an early elite [[Ferrari]] customer and a central figure in the [[Fiat]] industrial world that later owned Ferrari. The source uses him to connect Ferrari's European elite demand to the larger Italian automotive system.

His role is source-scoped: the episode does not make him the primary architect of Ferrari's brand, but he helps anchor the ownership and customer context around [[EnzoFerrari]], [[Fiat]], [[PieroFerrari]], and later [[Exor]] influence.
