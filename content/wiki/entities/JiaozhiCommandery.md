# 交趾郡 / Jiaozhi Commandery

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-920-mingwu-zhege-daoli-caineng-zhuan-daqian-lqmabg3bpyz6ct2v4arx-iun57ws|Hanji 920]] gives Jiaozhi an earlier 181 CE rebellion layer before the later Jia Cong episode. The source says [[WuhuManJiaozhi|乌浒蛮]] rebellion had persisted for a long time, local officials could not settle it, and [[LiangLongLateHan|梁龙]] and others then rose and broke commanderies and counties.

This branch is a suppression case rather than a root-cause repair case. [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] sends [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]] as inspector; Zhu Jun kills Liang Long and other rebel leaders and pacifies the rebellion in less than a month. That earlier coercive settlement now sits beside Hanji 927's later extraction-diagnosis and burden-relief settlement under [[JiaCongLateHan|贾从]].

Jiaozhi Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-927-zuo-shengyi-de-zuigao-jingjie-shi-shenme-lngfbjn6xui9k-9lp-ihdqkhpbpr|Hanji 927]] as a southern commandery in the region of today's northern Vietnam and the Red River valley. The source emphasizes pearls and distance from the capital to explain why earlier inspectors could treat the post as a temporary extraction opportunity.

The rebellion in the source is presented as a legitimacy failure before it is a military problem. Prior inspectors extract wealth, request transfers after enriching themselves, and leave local people and lower officials with no practical complaint route. Rebels then capture the inspector and the Hepu administrator Lai Da and choose a leader styled Zhu Tian General.

[[JiaCongLateHan|贾从]]'s pacification gives Jiaozhi its main wiki function: a case where local order returns only after the incoming official investigates root causes, relieves burdens, reassures displaced people, removes violent leaders, and installs clean county-level officials. Later pages such as [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]] and [[DongFuLateHan|董扶]] use Jiaozhi as a remote survival destination, while Hanji 927 supplies an earlier view of its local governance conditions.
