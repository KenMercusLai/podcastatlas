## Overview
交趾郡 / Jiaozhi Commandery is a southern late-Han frontier setting whose wiki profile now spans three source layers. Hanji 819 backfills the 138 CE crisis that [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] resolves by rejecting a large distant expedition; Hanji 920 adds the 181 CE Wuhu Man and [[LiangLongLateHan|梁龙]] rebellion suppressed by [[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]]; Hanji 927 adds [[JiaCongLateHan|贾从]]'s later root-cause pacification.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-819-2-ren-gong-bi-4-wanren-tade-quanmou-lingren-paianjuejue-lsslj3y-krnd8h-c8x5pgsqeinff|Hanji 819]] makes Jiaozhi a logistical and appointment-quality test under [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]]. The court majority wants to send a major general and 40,000 troops south; Li Gu warns that long-distance recruitment would damage the recruiting regions, expose soldiers to disease and exhaustion, and impose enormous grain cost. He proposes [[TemporaryFrontierRetrenchment|临时边防收缩]] for Rinan, rewards and division tactics, and the appointments of [[ZhangQiaoLateHan|张乔]] and [[ZhuLiangLateHan|祝良]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-920-mingwu-zhege-daoli-caineng-zhuan-daqian-lqmabg3bpyz6ct2v4arx-iun57ws|Hanji 920]] gives Jiaozhi an 181 CE rebellion layer. The source says [[WuhuManJiaozhi|乌浒蛮]] rebellion had persisted for a long time, local officials could not settle it, and Liang Long and others then rose and broke commanderies and counties. [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] sends Zhu Jun as inspector; Zhu Jun kills Liang Long and other rebel leaders and pacifies the rebellion in less than a month.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-927-zuo-shengyi-de-zuigao-jingjie-shi-shenme-lngfbjn6xui9k-9lp-ihdqkhpbpr|Hanji 927]] supplies the extraction-diagnosis layer. It introduces Jiaozhi as a southern commandery in the region of today's northern Vietnam and the Red River valley, with pearls and distance from the capital making earlier inspectors treat the post as a temporary enrichment opportunity. Jia Cong's repair then turns the page toward cause investigation, burden relief, reassurance, removal of violent leaders, and clean county appointments.

## Key Characteristics
- Distant southern commandery where logistics, disease, and grain cost make large expedition plans risky.
- Repeated rebellion theater, appearing in 138, 181, and 184 CE source layers.
- Place where appointment quality can matter more than troop scale when officials have local credibility and suitable methods.
- Legitimacy-failure setting where extraction and blocked complaints help explain later rebellion.
- Comparative pacification site: Li Gu's appointees, Zhu Jun's fast suppression, and Jia Cong's root-cause repair all become distinct response models.

## Qualifications
This page is a bounded synthesis from three podcast source notes, not a complete administrative history of Jiaozhi. The 138 CE branch in Hanji 819 includes transcript variants for Jiaozhi, Jiuzhen, and Rinan; the page normalizes those names cautiously. Hanji 920 emphasizes rapid suppression, while Hanji 927 emphasizes extraction and repair; those response models are complementary layers unless a later source establishes a contradiction.

## What Changed
