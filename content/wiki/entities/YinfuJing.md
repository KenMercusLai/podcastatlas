# 《阴符》 / Yinfu Jing

《阴符》 / Yinfu Jing appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-30-suqin-de-kaigua-rensheng-lkmn-fruukdxgkddx7zb4lc3o9bw]] as the text [[SuQin|苏秦]] studies after his first failures and family humiliation. The episode says Su Qin moves from broad reading to deep reading, choosing one short text as the basis for a practical breakthrough.

The source keeps the textual identity cautious. It says [[Shiji|《史记》]] calls the work 《周书阴符》, while [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] calls it 《太公阴符之谋》. The host treats the later identification with [[Liutao|《六韬》]]'s short 《阴符》 chapter as less persuasive because that version mainly concerns military information signaling, whereas Su Qin's breakthrough is [[ChuaimoTechnique|揣摩]] in persuasion and political reading.
