# 摆渡人 BDR / Baiduren BDR

## Overview
摆渡人 BDR / Baiduren BDR is the farewell-service organization founded by [[ShiHuiFuneralProfessional|石慧]] and described in [[shi-di-fu-shuo-476-shihui-women-rensheng-zuihou-de-yishi-pubian-caoshuai-cangcu-mei-zunyan-lo2evzu5zgam7jjevwovlwxr37ah|史蒂夫说476期]]. Its reported purpose is to help people leave in a way recognizable as their own while supporting families through ceremony and early bereavement.

## Current Profile
The episode portrays BDR as a team-based alternative to a single operator handling multiple families. Life planners investigate biography and relationships, ritual specialists handle embodied ceremonial practice, coordinators turn plans into logistics, and care staff continue contact after the funeral. The organization combines traditional knowledge with personalized design and treats price transparency, staff emotional capacity, and post-funeral remembrance as parts of service quality.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses multiple specialized roles for one family rather than a single all-purpose operator.
- Designs farewell details from biography, preference, relationship, and family context.
- Retains traditional practices when they serve care, meaning, or family needs.
- Continues bereavement contact through commemorative dates and activities.
- Requires emotional responsiveness while using leave or departure when staff capacity is depleted.
- Frames clear pricing as necessary to sustain labor-intensive service.
