## Overview

《不死药》 is a [[NiKuang|倪匡]] novel in the [[WesleyNiKuang|卫斯理]] series that joins a prison-break and identity-exchange mystery to a speculative drug that arrests aging while imposing infertility and permanent supply dependence.

## Current Profile

The bounded episode treats the novel's first half as a successful chain of curiosity, pursuit, concealment, and reversal. A condemned 骆之逊 asks 卫斯理 to arrange an escape but withholds his motive; the later revelation is that the survivor is actually 骆之谦, who killed his brother, assumed his identity, and sought to commercialize the island's drug with the dead man's wife.

The drug reframes immortality as infrastructure rather than a one-time gift. It enhances recovery and preserves youth, yet users remain physically killable, lose fertility, and face cognitive collapse if the geographically concentrated supply ends. 骆之谦's proposed massacre converts that dependence into monopoly power. The plot makes 卫斯理 ingest the drug under mortal threat, but the episode argues that his credulity and failure to control an armed captive are conspicuous devices for reaching that outcome.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines jailbreak, pursuit, concealed motive, and fraternal identity exchange with speculative science fiction.
- Defines “immortality” as arrested aging and enhanced healing rather than immunity from fatal injury.
- Makes infertility and lifelong dosing structural costs of the enhancement.
- Turns concentrated production and user dependence into incentives for murder and monopoly.
- Uses forced ingestion to move the ethical problem from detached investigation into the protagonist's body.
- Ends by destroying the supply through a tsunami while leaving the protagonist's treatment outcome open.

## Qualifications

This profile is based on one podcast retelling rather than direct comparison of editions or the novel's full text. Names, drug rules, exact sequence, quoted thematic statements, authorial intention, and the hosts' craft judgments remain source-scoped. Fictional medicine and island ecology are not evidence for real anti-aging mechanisms, and the title's “不死” does not imply that characters cannot be killed.
