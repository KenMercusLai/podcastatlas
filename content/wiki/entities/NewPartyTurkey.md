# New Party (Turkey)

The New Party is the opposition party [[OzgurOzel]] forms in [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]] after a court removes him from the leadership of the [[RepublicanPeoplesParty|CHP]]. The source says at least 91 CHP MPs defected to the New Party, making it the second-largest party in Turkey's Parliament.

The party matters because it turns opposition survival into a moving target. On paper, the source says polls put it above 30% and ahead of [[RecepTayyipErdogan]]'s [[JusticeAndDevelopmentParty|AK Party]]. In practice, the episode questions whether it can become more than a CHP clone and whether it can attract secular nationalists or Kurdish voters while facing [[JudicializedOppositionSuppression]].
