# 孙策遇刺 / Sun Ce Assassination

## Overview
孙策遇刺 / Sun Ce Assassination is the 200 CE hunting ambush in which retainers of [[XuGongLateHan|徐贡]] exploit [[SunCe|孙策]]'s separation from his guards and wound him fatally. In the wiki, the event connects the earlier [[LeaderPersonalExposure|领导者轻身涉险]] pattern to the immediate [[SunQuan|孙权]] succession crisis.

## Current Profile
The event is not only a death scene. It joins retainer revenge, field-access deception, and a leader's exposed mobility, then forces Sun Ce to turn personal injury into institutional transfer through seal handoff, successor designation, and reliance on [[ZhangZhaoLateHan|张昭]] and [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]].

## Key Characteristics
- The ambush begins because Sun Ce's fast horse leaves his escort behind during a hunt.
- The attackers are framed as Xu Gong's retainers seeking revenge rather than as battlefield enemies.
- The assassins use [[HanDang|韩当]]'s name as a false field cover before Sun Ce suspects them.
- The protective boundary returns too late: escorts kill the remaining attackers after Sun Ce has already been badly wounded.
- The wound immediately becomes a command-continuity problem that requires a Sun Quan handoff and staff stabilization.
