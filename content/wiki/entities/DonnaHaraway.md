# Donna Haraway / 唐娜·哈拉维

## Overview
Donna Haraway / 唐娜·哈拉维 appears in [[episode-225-yidai-yilu-8212959555-438866|Episode 225: 一袋一录]] as the guide-text writer who extends [[UrsulaKLeGuin|Le Guin]]'s carrier-bag theory toward feminist, cyborg, and more-than-human politics.

## Current Profile
The source presents Haraway as a theorist resisting pure-origin and single-salvation stories. Her guide text on Le Guin's carrier bag is linked with Colombian Mochila bags, environmental justice groups, and the practice of staying with tangled ecological and political trouble rather than replacing one grand story with another.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses carrier-bag language to connect literary form with feminist and ecological politics.
- Treats the cyborg as a figure without pure origin, innocence, or return-to-Eden fantasy.
- Opposes the authority of one universal big story.
- Connects theory to Colombian environmental and racial-justice movements.
- Turns wordplay, material bags, and activist practice into one interpretive field.
