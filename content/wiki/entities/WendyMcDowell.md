# Wendy McDowell

## Overview

Wendy McDowell is an Albany, New York school psychologist whose [[HiddenBrain]] listener story recounts being abandoned at Chicago O'Hare Airport at age eight and escorted home by an unidentified woman.

## Current Profile

McDowell presents the airport encounter as a formative contrast between adult abandonment and protective care. The woman who helped her did more than solve the travel problem: she repeatedly asked whether Wendy felt safe, contacted her mother, changed her itinerary, bought a ticket, and stayed beside her through the flight. McDowell says the experience shaped her sense that people can choose to offer security in moments of need.

At the episode's publication, McDowell reported more than 25 years of work as a school psychologist. She also tells the story to her son, making the memory an intergenerational example of [[ConsentAwareChildSafeguarding]] and morally consequential kindness.

## Key Characteristics

- Survived an episode of childhood abandonment during disrupted air travel.
- Remembers the helper's repeated safety checks and permission-seeking as central to feeling protected.
- Interprets the encounter as a lasting counterexample to the cruelty of being left alone.
- Built a long career supporting children as a school psychologist.
- Uses the story in parenting to show that ordinary people can choose protective care.
