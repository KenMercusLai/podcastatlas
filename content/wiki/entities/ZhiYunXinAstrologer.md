## Overview

Zhi Yun / 智韵 is a Runan specialist in astronomy and calendrics in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-657-wangmang-fei-taizi-you-duo-erxi-lklx1rd39kgz-by4tbcpaagx4z-l|Hanji 657]]. He tells [[WangMang|王莽]] that Han will revive and that Wang Mang should return to ministerial status, making him a direct mandate-remonstrance figure in late [[XinDynasty|新朝]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Zhi Yun as using the same Heaven-readable political language that supports omen politics, but against Wang Mang's rule. His advice is not a mild administrative correction: he says abnormal signs are Heaven's warning and that what was taken from Heaven should be returned to Heaven. Wang Mang reacts with anger, arrest, and imprisonment; Zhi Yun survives only after a later amnesty.

## Key Characteristics

- Runan astronomy and calendrical specialist.
- Han-restoration remonstrator who denies the durability of Xin legitimacy.
- Direct critic whose advice asks Wang Mang to leave imperial status.
- Political prisoner spared only after winter amnesty.

## Qualifications

This Zhi Yun is distinct from [[ZhiYunEasternHan|郅恽]], the Guangwu-era remonstrator. The source does not settle whether Zhi Yun's astronomical reading was empirically valid; the wiki records the political use of that language.

## What Changed

- Created from Hanji 657 as a late-Xin astrologer whose remonstrance turns omen language against Wang Mang.
