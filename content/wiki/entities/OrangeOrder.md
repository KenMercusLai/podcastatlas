# Orange Order

The Orange Order appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] through the July 1969 marching season in [[NorthernIreland]]. The episode says the 12 July Orange parades brought worse rioting, especially in [[Belfast]], and contributed to forced displacement of Catholic and Protestant families.

In the source, Orange marching is part of a wider ritual and street-politics field. Public commemoration, territorial memory, civil-rights protest, and policing decisions all become ways that each community reads threat.
