# Orange Order

The Orange Order appears in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as the Protestant fraternal organization founded to defend Protestantism and the union with Britain. The episode says almost every [[BSpecials|B Special]] and almost every unionist MP belonged to an Orange lodge, making the order part of the social infrastructure of unionist power.

In [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]], the Orange Order appears through the July 1969 marching season in [[NorthernIreland]]. The episode says the 12 July Orange parades brought worse rioting, especially in [[Belfast]], and contributed to forced displacement of Catholic and Protestant families. Public commemoration, territorial memory, civil-rights protest, and policing decisions all become ways that each community reads threat.

[[699-the-troubles-return-of-the-ira-part-3-glt9519811642]] continues the marching-season branch into summer 1970, when [[EdwardHeath]] decided not to ban Orange Order marches. The episode links the 27 June parades to rioting, street fighting, and the [[ShortStrand]] gun battle that helped the [[ProvisionalIRA]] claim defensive credibility.
