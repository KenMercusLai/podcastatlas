# Madame de Renal / 雷奈尔夫人

Madame de Renal is a central character in [[Stendhal|Stendhal]]'s [[RedAndBlackNovel|《红与黑》]], discussed in [[21-hong-yu-hei-ta-si-yu-zhencheng-569042001]] as more than the pious married woman in a scandal plot. The episode presents her as beautiful, sincere, religious, kind, and emotionally inexperienced, but not empty or merely passive.

Her relationship with [[JulienSorel|Julien Sorel]] begins through anxiety about her children and his tutor role, then moves through admiration, jealousy, guilt, and love. The source argues that Julien first treats the relationship as conquest and revenge against class humiliation, but gradually encounters a kind of love that is direct and uncalculating.

The episode's later reading makes Madame de Renal a key site for [[FemaleSubjectivityInClassicReading]]. Her letter to [[MathildeDeLaMole|Mathilde]]'s father injures Julien and triggers violence, but the source keeps the pressure around confession, priestly instruction, conscience, fear, and love visible rather than flattening her into saint, victim, or betrayer.
