## Overview

郭镇 / Guo Zhen appears in Hanji 803 as the shangshu who helps end the Yan-family counterattack during [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]]'s restoration. His defining scene is leaving sickbed, taking Yulin guards out through the South Stop-Carriage Gate, confronting Yan Jin at Shengde Gate, and capturing him under the new edict.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-803-han-shundi-ruhe-zai-xuefengxueyu-zhong-dengshang-huangwei-lqvno7t-gcgxtbs3phuflr4ybe2s|Hanji 803]] places Guo Zhen after [[SunChengLateHan|孙程]]'s group has already enthroned Liu Bao and after [[YanXianLateHan|阎显]]'s attempted mobilization begins to fail. Yan Xian's brother Yan Jin leaves the palace for an outside office and gathers troops at Shengde Gate. Sun Cheng transmits an edict ordering the shangshu officials to arrest Yan Jin.

Guo Zhen is ill at home, but he gets up, leads on-duty Yulin guards, exits through the South Stop-Carriage Gate, and meets Yan Jin's force. He reads the edict. Yan Jin attacks with a blade and misses; Guo Zhen strikes back, knocks him from his carriage, and the Yulin guards capture him. Yan Jin is sent to the commandant of justice and dies before dawn, which marks the collapse of Yan-side armed recovery.

## Key Characteristics

- Shangshu actor who turns the restored side's edict into immediate armed enforcement.
- Sickbed official whose action matters because he can mobilize on-duty Yulin guards quickly.
- Gate-level enforcer in the same palace-coup sequence that installs Shun.
- Counter-coup breaker whose capture of Yan Jin prevents the Yan side from regaining momentum.
