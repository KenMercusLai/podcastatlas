# Cameroon / 喀麦隆

Cameroon is the fieldwork setting of [[35-tianzhende-renleixuejia-zuo-tianye-hui-shangyin-de-603418624]]. The episode follows [[NigelBarley|Nigel Barley]] from entry bureaucracy and Douala airport confusion toward northern mountain fieldwork among the [[DowayoPeople|Dowayo people]], while [[QinZong|秦总]] adds her own West and Central Africa work experience as context.

In this source, Cameroon is not only geographic background. Its colonial history, French and English language environment, small-scale corruption, patronage obligations, schools, ID cards, voting procedures, agricultural policy, missionaries, and local development offices all shape what anthropology can see.

## Key Claims
- Entry documents, visa delays, airport treatment, and old passport suspicion make state bureaucracy part of [[EthnographicEverydayFriction]].
- The episode uses Cameroon to show why a field site is never isolated from colonial history, state administration, local officials, missions, markets, and development experts.
- Cotton promotion, vegetable-garden policy, and a water-diversion plan make the country context important for [[DevelopmentProjectUnintendedConsequences]].
- Local corruption and patronage are described with attention to moral economy, not only as an outside observer's complaint.
