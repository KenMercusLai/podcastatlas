# Treaty of Greenwich (1543)

## Overview

The Treaty of Greenwich was [[HenryVIII|Henry VIII]]'s failed settlement for marrying the infant [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]] to Prince Edward and joining the English and Scottish crowns without formally abolishing Scotland's laws.

## Current Profile

Signed with [[JamesHamiltonEarlOfArran|Arran]] in July 1543, the treaty proposed that Mary be raised with English-speaking attendants, marry Edward at ten, and produce heirs ruling a united Great Britain while [[Scotland]] retained its laws and liberties. It joined marriage diplomacy to Protestant reform and English strategic control.

The settlement failed because Arran could not carry Scottish nobles, a still largely Catholic kingdom, or [[MaryOfGuise|Mary of Guise]] with him. Guise's custody of Mary at Stirling prevented transfer of the child, and Henry's turn to the [[RoughWooing|Rough Wooing]] transformed the failed contract into an attempt at military compulsion.

## Key Characteristics

- Dynastic marriage agreement between the English crown and Arran's regency.
- Proposal for crown union with formal preservation of Scottish law.
- Settlement entangled with Protestant reform and control of Mary's upbringing.
- Treaty signed without sufficient Scottish political consent.
- Failed diplomatic prelude to the Rough Wooing.

## Qualifications

The source summarizes rather than reproduces the treaty. Its interpretation that many Scots saw subordination beneath the preserved-law formula is historically consequential but not a clause-by-clause legal analysis.

## What Changed

- Established Greenwich as a failed negotiated union whose collapse preceded coercive war.

## Relationships

- [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]] - child monarch whose marriage the treaty arranged.
- [[JamesHamiltonEarlOfArran|James Hamilton / Earl of Arran]] - Scottish regent who signed the agreement.
- [[MaryOfGuise|Mary of Guise]] - opponent who kept control of Mary.
- [[RoughWooing|Rough Wooing]] - military escalation after the treaty failed.
