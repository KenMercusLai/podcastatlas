# Adrian Alvarez

Adrian Alvarez is the founder of [[AeroShorts]] and a caller in [[advice-line-with-chris-riccobono-of-untuckit-c087247f-1f85-4c15-b4aa-4ffea0368c4c]]. The source says he started the business as a high school senior, found manufacturing partners after a Las Vegas sourcing show, and was a college sophomore at the time of the call.

His question is how to move from early youth-market traction toward a broader athletic-apparel brand. [[ChrisRiccobono]] and [[GuyRaz]] advise him to use his youth as an advantage in TikTok-native storytelling while making the brand more specific around a first customer tribe, especially volleyball.
