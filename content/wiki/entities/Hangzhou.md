# 杭州 / Hangzhou

## Overview
Hangzhou is a Chinese city that currently appears in the wiki through two cultural-memory routes: [[SuShi|苏轼]]'s Song-dynasty local service and a [[BowuzhiPodcast|博物志]] episode on ancient books, museum interpretation, and Hangzhou heritage travel.

## Current Profile
The current wiki profile treats Hangzhou as a place where literature, governance, books, and tourism overlap. In the Su Shi source, Hangzhou is a site of public works, disaster preparation, medicine, and West Lake memory. In the “意味杭之” source, the same city becomes a local publishing and heritage field: [[HangzhouMuseum]], [[YiweiHangzhiExhibition]], [[WenlanPavilion]], [[LiuhePagoda]], [[LeifengPagoda]], [[YuQianTombAndTemple]], [[HuiyinGaoliTemple]], and food stops all make Hangzhou legible through institutions, objects, sites, and everyday travel.

## Key Characteristics
- Song literary-governance site where [[SuShi|苏轼]]'s public works join writing, conscience, and local care.
- West Lake-centered cultural landscape where scenic memory, travel writing, and city identity reinforce one another.
- Ancient-book and publishing-history setting through [[HangzhouMuseum]] and [[YiweiHangzhiExhibition]].
- Heritage travel field combining book sites, towers, memorials, temples, museum objects, and food recommendations.
- Local interpretation case where city history becomes understandable through [[MuseumInterpretationLegibility]] and [[PlaceBasedExhibitionCuration]].

## Evidence
- Su Shi governance - [[105-su-dongpo-yiqiang-haomai-zhiqi-yi-dupi-buhe-shiyi-741072645]] says Su Shi dredged West Lake, built the [[SuCauseway|苏堤]], stored grain, created medical facilities, distributed medicine, and organized infant-rescue support.
- Ancient-book exhibition - [[246-hangzhou-bowuguan-yiwei-hangzhi-haojiu-mei-kan-guo-zheme-tuijian-de-zhanlan-le-8217911423-973793]] uses [[HangzhouMuseum]] and [[YiweiHangzhiExhibition]] to connect Hangzhou to Ming-Qing ancient books and publishing culture.
- Heritage route - [[246-hangzhou-bowuguan-yiwei-hangzhi-haojiu-mei-kan-guo-zheme-tuijian-de-zhanlan-le-8217911423-973793]] discusses [[WenlanPavilion]], [[LiuhePagoda]], [[LeifengPagoda]], [[YuQianTombAndTemple]], and [[HuiyinGaoliTemple]] as Hangzhou sites that extend the exhibition's book and place-memory themes.
