# Oliver North

## Overview
Oliver North was a U.S. National Security Council aide presented in the source as a central operator in the [[IranContraAffair|Iran-Contra affair]].

## Current Profile
[[312-reagan-iran-contra-and-the-cold-war-part-3-glt4042202229]] places North at the junction between covert support for Nicaragua's Contras and an arms channel to Iran intended to help free American hostages in Lebanon. The episode says he helped seek outside Contra funding, proposed diverting profits from Iranian arms sales, joined a failed mission to Iran, and later participated in document destruction or removal after exposure.

## Key Characteristics
- National Security Council operator working outside ordinary transparent foreign-policy channels.
- Participant in efforts to sustain Contra support despite congressional restrictions.
- Proponent of connecting Iranian arms-sale proceeds to Contra financing.
- Participant in the failed Iran mission and later concealment activity described by the episode.

## Evidence
### Operational integration
- [[312-reagan-iran-contra-and-the-cold-war-part-3-glt4042202229]] attributes outside fundraising, weapons support, the diversion proposal, and the Iran trip to North and associates.

### Exposure and concealment
- [[312-reagan-iran-contra-and-the-cold-war-part-3-glt4042202229]] says North and Fawn Hall destroyed or removed records after the operation began to emerge publicly.

## Qualifications
This profile rests on one compressed podcast account rather than the full documentary record, trial history, congressional investigation, or North's defense. It distinguishes the episode's description of his operational role from unresolved questions about the exact knowledge, authorization, and intent of every participant.
