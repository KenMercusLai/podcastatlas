# 郑执 / Zheng Zhi

## Overview
郑执 / Zheng Zhi enters the wiki through a [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] interview as a Chinese writer, screenwriter, and first-time feature director whose current profile is shaped by [[NortheastLiteraryFilmAuthorship]], commercial screenwriting, and a self-described late-cycle sense of arriving after several cultural booms have already peaked.

## Current Profile
The source presents Zheng as a creator who is difficult to place in a single lane. He published young, wrote youth fiction and market-oriented work, used screenwriting as survival work, gained serious-literary recognition through 《仙症》, and then became associated with adaptations such as 《生吞》 and film writing including 《刺猬》. His director debut, [[ShenzhongYoulinFilm|《身中有林》]], becomes the episode's test case for how a novelist's family, regional, and fate-driven material is compressed into theatrical film form.

Zheng's authorial identity in the episode is practical rather than purely romantic. He values story grip, local texture, and personal memory, but he also talks openly about debt, poor book sales, copyright income, casting, producer opinions, runtime constraints, roadshows, and the desire for investors to recover cost. That makes him a strong case for [[AuthorCommercialFilmBalance]] rather than for a simple opposition between art and market.

## Key Characteristics
- Writer-director whose work is grounded in Shenyang, Northeast memory, family material, violence, superstition, humor, and ordinary survival pressure.
- Cross-system creator who has moved through youth fiction, market writing, serious-literature recognition, screenwriting, adaptation, and directing.
- Practical storyteller who values readability and story pull even when discussing serious literature.
- Film practitioner whose director role emerged from screenwriting experience, control over the script, and difficulty delegating the work's intent.
- Source-scoped public figure whose broader bibliography, reception history, and non-interview biography are not fully covered by the current evidence inventory.
