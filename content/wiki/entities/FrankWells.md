# Frank Wells

Frank Wells is the operating partner brought into Disney with [[MichaelEisner]] in [[disney-the-renaissance-and-the-empire-1]]. The source presents him as a stabilizing executive who helped balance Eisner and [[JeffreyKatzenberg]] while the company rebuilt parks, live action, animation, home video, and broader studio economics.

Wells's death in a 1994 helicopter crash is treated as a turning point. The episode argues that his absence contributed to the collapse of the Eisner-Katzenberg relationship and to the later governance stress that made [[RoyEDisney]]'s Save Disney campaign possible.
