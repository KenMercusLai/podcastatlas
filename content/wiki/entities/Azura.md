# Azura

Azura is a sales-oriented internal agent at [[Kuse]] in [[women-shi-ruhe-dingyi-openclaw-for-teams-xin-chanpin-xingtai-de-duitan-kuse-junior-lianchuang-jian-cto-yuhao-lkp1a0todflxoyycyo3zhrap3ebv]]. The source says Azura held customer and sales data, built a simple internal CRM with the sales leader, scanned for upsell opportunities, and began using a company email to send outbound reach-out messages.

Azura matters because it makes the [[AINativeSaaSThreat]] concrete. The CRM case is not polished enterprise software, but it shows how an agent with [[EnterpriseAgentMemory|business memory]], tool access, and sales context can generate a tailored internal workflow faster than a traditional requirements-to-product cycle.
