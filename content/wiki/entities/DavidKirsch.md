# David Kirsch

David Kirsch is the historian and management professor interviewed in [[tech-20260130-0130-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260130-0130-mp-tech-pod-128]]. The source identifies him with the [[UniversityOfMaryland]] and with [[BubblesAndCrashes]], using his historical work on technological booms to evaluate whether AI is in a bubble.

Kirsch's role in the wiki is to connect [[AIEquityValuationRisk]] with a broader history of [[TechBubbleConditions]]. He argues that AI strongly matches uncertainty, novice-investor participation, investable access, and narrative intensity, while its lower number of pure-play AI IPOs keeps the score just short of maximum. His aviation analogy also links the source to [[TechnologyInstallationCycle]]: useful technology may need decades of infrastructure, norms, and business-model development before investors can know which early bets were justified.
