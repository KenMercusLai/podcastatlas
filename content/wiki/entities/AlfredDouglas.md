# Lord Alfred Douglas / 波西

Lord Alfred Douglas / 波西 enters the wiki through [[10-wang-erde-qiyi-gushiji-yong-zui-hui-sahuang-de-zuichun-qinwen-tianshi-545332195]] as the lover whose relationship with [[OscarWilde|Oscar Wilde / 王尔德]] becomes central to the episode's account of Wilde's downfall. The source frames Douglas through [[DeProfundis|《自深深处》]], money, illness, dependence, and the disastrous legal conflict pushed forward after Douglas's father attacked Wilde.

The page keeps the claims source-scoped. In the episode, Douglas matters not as an independently developed biography but as the person through whom Wilde's brilliance, desire, public scandal, legal vulnerability, and later prison writing become inseparable.
