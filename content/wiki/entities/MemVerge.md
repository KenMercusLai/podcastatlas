# MemVerge

## Overview
MemVerge is the AI-memory and infrastructure company represented by CEO [[CharlesFan]] in [[lsh8sdro6i9mkt5nug4zkibv95tn]].

## Current Profile
In the source, MemVerge is moving from infrastructure, memory management, and storage toward an AI-memory product stack. [[MemoryMachine]] supplies an open developer layer, while [[MemoryBox]] packages related capabilities into a personal local-and-hybrid assistant. The episode presents this as a company strategy rather than independent evidence of product performance or adoption.

## Key Characteristics
- Builds both developer infrastructure and a consumer-facing memory application.
- Treats private-data organization as a distinct layer from foundation-model capability.
- Favors local and hybrid execution to combine privacy, device access, and stronger remote models.
- Uses open source for the developer-facing memory layer and subscriptions as the planned consumer-business model.

## Evidence
### Product stack
- [[lsh8sdro6i9mkt5nug4zkibv95tn]] distinguishes open [[MemoryMachine]] infrastructure from the commercial [[MemoryBox]] application.

### Strategic position
- [[lsh8sdro6i9mkt5nug4zkibv95tn]] says the company expects private data and memory management to differentiate AI products as models become cheaper and more interchangeable.

### Deployment model
- [[lsh8sdro6i9mkt5nug4zkibv95tn]] describes local, hybrid, and claimed zero-retention execution options plus planned PC, Mac, Android, and iPhone support.
