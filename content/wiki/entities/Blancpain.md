# Blancpain

Blancpain appears in [[rolex-1]] through [[JeanClaudeBiver]]'s post-quartz mechanical-watch repositioning work. The source says Biver bought Blancpain and repositioned it as a mechanical-only luxury brand, helping revive the romance of complicated mechanical watches.

The Blancpain case gives the wiki a contrast to [[Rolex]]. Rolex survived through brand continuity and industrial discipline, while Blancpain is used as a sharper example of explicit [[MechanicalWatchRepositioning]] after the quartz shock.
