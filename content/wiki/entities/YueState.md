# 越国 / Yue State

越国 / Yue State appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-28-hanzhaohou-de-kuaile-ni-xiangxiang-budao-ln2tbxniz4kvwogbwp32exmdokz8]] through [[YueWangWujiang|越王无疆]]'s failed anti-[[QiState|齐国]] move and [[ChuState|楚国]]'s later pressure. The episode uses Yue to keep chronology precise: the 334 BCE cluster includes Yue's failed campaign and Chu's defeat of Yue, but not Yue's final extinction.

The page is source-scoped. Its role is to prevent the wiki from collapsing a damaging defeat, a later Chu victory, and the eventual destruction of Yue into one event.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-57-gudai-fa-dushi-haiyou-zhe-zuoyong-lj6wi61osaytowgmdp8dtkrwb-si]] adds the earlier Spring-and-Autumn Yue branch through [[Goujian|越王勾践]]. In [[Zigong|子贡 / 端木赐]]'s rescue chain, Yue appears submissive to [[WuState|吴国]] while waiting for [[Fuchai|吴王夫差]] to exhaust Wu against [[QiState|齐国]] and [[JinState|晋国]]. Yue then attacks Wu and destroys it, before the episode turns to the danger facing [[FanLi|范蠡]] and [[WenZhongYue|文种]] after victory.
