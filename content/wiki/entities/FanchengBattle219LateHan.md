## Overview

219年樊城之战 / Battle of Fancheng is the current wiki's event page for [[GuanYu|关羽]]'s flood-assisted defeat of [[YuJin|于禁]]'s field army, the siege pressure on [[CaoRen|曹仁]], [[XuHuangCaoGeneral|徐晃]]'s staged landward relief, and the simultaneous loss of Guan Yu's [[JianglingLateHan|江陵]] rear to Eastern Wu.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1166-ruhe-yu-piqi-da-nengli-qiang-de-ren-xiangchu-lhm17dmeln7dmj1czmeq7omxgtmh|Hanji 1166]] supplies the campaign's high point. Sustained rain and Han River flooding overwhelm Yu Jin's outside position. Guan Yu surrounds the survivors by boat; Yu Jin surrenders, while [[PangDe|庞德]] is captured and executed after refusing submission. Flood damage then creates pressure to evacuate Fancheng, but [[ManChong|满宠]] argues that the water will fall and that withdrawal could expose the wider region. Cao Ren and the defenders recommit to holding.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1169-2026-gai-zenme-shou-zhu-zishen-de-fuqi-lu-3dpvj0nbqhise2aqx-a3zi3kp|Hanji 1169]] fills the decision layer between siege and relief. Xu Huang does not immediately attack with inexperienced troops. [[ZhaoYanLateHan|赵俨]] instead argues for moving close enough to signal that aid is coming, raising defender morale while reinforcements gather for coordinated action. [[DongZhaoLateHan|董昭]] then recommends disclosing Sun Quan's planned attack to both the besieged city and Guan Yu's camp. [[HuanJieLateHan|桓阶]] persuades [[CaoCao|曹操]] that holding the rear with a large force can support Cao Ren more effectively than personal front-line intervention.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1170-zuoshi-cun-jiaoxing-zhen-de-hui-wanjiebufu-ma-lkju2wdbb-v20gf2vikkr_aibjnx|Hanji 1170]] supplies execution and reversal. Xu Huang feints toward one siege sector, redirects against another, defeats Guan Yu's returning force, and drives through successive field defenses until the land encirclement opens. Guan Yu's boats still control the Mian River, so land relief does not instantly restore every route. At the same time, [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]]'s disguised river movement removes warning posts and reaches Jiangling, where [[FuShirenLateHan|傅士仁]] and [[MiFangLateHan|糜芳]] surrender. Guan Yu retreats under coupled forward reversal and rear-base loss.

## Key Characteristics

- Northern offensive launched from Guan Yu's Jingzhou position in 219 CE.
- Battlefield balance transformed by sustained rain and Han River flooding.
- Produces Yu Jin's surrender and Pang De's refusal and execution.
- Fancheng survives through a hold decision, morale maintenance, staged relief, and later battlefield action.
- Information about Wu's attack is deliberately released to change both defender morale and Guan Yu's withdrawal calculation.
- Xu Huang's feint and concentration break the land siege while river control remains temporarily contested.
- The final reversal couples front-line relief with the covert loss of Guan Yu's rear base.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to three structured episode summaries rather than a primary-source campaign reconstruction. Rainfall, flood mechanics, army and captive totals, positions, wall damage, dialogue, force experience, relief timing, camp counts, place-name readings, feint mechanics, river control, warning-post capture, surrender timing, and precise Cao-Sun coordination require broader textual comparison. The sunk-cost reading of Guan Yu's delay is plausible but cannot replace his stated confidence in rear defenses, the value of the objective, communication limits, logistics, or alliance interests.

## What Changed

- Added the staged relief decision between the flood siege and Xu Huang's later attack.
- Added deliberate disclosure as a mechanism for morale and withdrawal pressure.
- Added rear-area leadership as part of Cao Cao's operational contribution.
- Preserved the campaign as coupled forward and rear collapse rather than one decisive exchange.
