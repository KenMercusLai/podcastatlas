# Matamba

## Overview

Matamba is the polity Queen Njinga conquers and uses as a recognized ruling base after conflict with Portuguese power and Ndongo rivals.

## Current Profile

The episode introduces Matamba after [[QueenNjinga]] suffers defeat and rebuilds power through alliance, movement, and conquest. Njinga is said to conquer Matamba, spare Queen Mwanga, brand her, and send her into exile rather than kill her. Later, Portuguese authorities recognize Njinga as queen of Matamba while she refuses treaty language that would make slave tribute a marker of vassal status.

## Key Characteristics

- Conquered polity that becomes Njinga's durable ruling base.
- Site where Njinga's rule includes both restraint and coercion toward the displaced queen.
- Recognition object in the eventual Portuguese settlement.
- Part of the episode's case that sovereignty can shift across war, exile, diplomacy, and treaty language.

## Evidence

- Conquest and treatment of prior queen: [[317-african-amazons-glt5973082485]] says Njinga conquered Matamba, spared Queen Mwanga, branded her, and sent her into exile.
- Recognition: [[317-african-amazons-glt5973082485]] says Njinga was recognized as queen of Matamba in the settlement phase.
- Treaty context: [[317-african-amazons-glt5973082485]] ties the Matamba recognition to Njinga's refusal of annual slave-tribute language.

## Qualifications

The source does not provide a full internal history of Matamba before or after Njinga. Its current wiki role is limited to Njinga's sovereignty and treaty position.
