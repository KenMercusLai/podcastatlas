# 田子方 / Tian Zifang

田子方 / Tian Zifang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-05-guangcai-duomu-de-weiwenhou-1-lmkxzbmwhw3nhurape-eebydqcdl]] as another scholar whom [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] treats as a teacher. The host infers from the Tian surname that he came from the [[QiState|齐国]] Tian lineage world before leaving to serve or advise [[WeiState|魏国]].

In the source, Tian Zifang is one part of a three-person image system: [[ZiXia|子夏]] represents Confucian scholarly prestige, Tian Zifang shows mobile talent entering Wei, and [[DuanGanmu|段干木]] shows a worthy person who remains difficult to access. Together they make Wei Wenhou's rise look rooted in [[TalentAttractionThroughRespect|respectful talent attraction]], not only military force.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-1-lk-6ejgmjhvukqgtlz2lowu8qx-v]] makes Tian Zifang part of [[WeiCheng|魏成]]'s chancellor-selection advantage. In [[LiKe|李克]]'s explanation, attracting Tian Zifang, [[ZiXia|子夏]], and [[DuanGanmu|段干木]] counts differently from [[DiHuang|翟黄]]'s operational recommendations because these figures raise [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]]'s own learning circle.
