## Overview

田子方 / Tian Zifang is a scholar whom [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] treats as a teacher. The current evidence makes him both a marker of Wei's ability to attract mobile talent and a corrective voice who insists that rulers manage people, roles, and hierarchy rather than display technical cleverness or rank-based arrogance.

## Current Profile

The earlier sources place Tian Zifang beside [[ZiXia|子夏]] and [[DuanGanmu|段干木]] in Wei Wenhou's teacher circle, and make his attraction to Wei evidence for [[WeiCheng|魏成]]'s superior referral environment. Zhouji 05 part 3 gives him direct speech and action. He rebukes Wei Wenhou for concentrating on a bell's incorrect pitch instead of the selection and supervision of music officials, then refuses to return crown prince [[WeiWuhou|魏击 / 后来的魏武侯]]'s roadside bow and argues that high status creates greater duties of restraint while a poor scholar retains the option to leave.

## Key Characteristics

- Scholar-teacher whose standing depends on advice rather than formal command.
- Represents mobile talent entering Wei's court from a wider Warring States market.
- Defines leadership as appointing, supervising, and evaluating specialists.
- Uses etiquette conflict to remind a crown prince that rank increases the cost of contempt.
- Treats exit as a scholar's bargaining power when counsel is rejected.

## Evidence

- Teacher-circle position: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-05-guangcai-duomu-de-weiwenhou-1-lmkxzbmwhw3nhurape-eebydqcdl|Zhouji 05 part 1]] places Tian Zifang among the scholars Wei Wenhou honors and source-scopes the host's inference about his Qi/Tian background.
- Referral quality: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-06-weiwenhou-xuan-shui-dang-zaixiang-ne-1-lk-6ejgmjhvukqgtlz2lowu8qx-v|Zhouji 06 part 1]] treats the ability to attract Tian Zifang, Zi Xia, and Duan Ganmu as different from recommending operational officials.
- Ruler-specialist boundary: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-05-guangcai-duomu-de-weiwenhou-3-lqbr7i9s82pi1jptizs18gidsipi|Zhouji 05 part 3]] has Tian Zifang redirect Wei Wenhou from judging bell pitch toward managing the responsible official.
- Rank and exit: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-05-guangcai-duomu-de-weiwenhou-3-lqbr7i9s82pi1jptizs18gidsipi|Zhouji 05 part 3]] says Tian Zifang refuses the crown prince's return bow and explains that the powerful risk losing state or household, while the poor can leave when ignored.

## Qualifications

The host's inference that Tian Zifang came from the Qi Tian lineage world is not independently established by the bounded evidence. The bell and roadside dialogues are transmitted anecdotes used by the episode for modern leadership interpretation; their wording, etiquette context, and Tian Zifang's private intention remain source-scoped. His refusal to bow should not be generalized into contempt for office, because the episode frames it as instruction about the obligations of office.
