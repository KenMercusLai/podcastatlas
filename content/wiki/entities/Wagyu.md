# Wagyu / 和牛

Wagyu is the premium beef category explained in [[kafeidou-he-niu-ziyou-cheng-zizhu-canting-maidian-guijia-guanghuan-cong-he-er-lai-1004978054]]. The source says the modern strict definition refers to four Japanese cattle breeds, but this definition is relatively recent compared with Japan's longer history of cattle, beef avoidance, Meiji-era meat promotion, and later specialized breeding.

The episode treats wagyu's status as historically made rather than natural. Meiji reformers recast beef as a symbol of modernization and bodily improvement; sukiyaki-style cooking made thin, fatty beef attractive; postwar institutions standardized breeds and grading; and import competition from cheaper American and Australian beef pushed Japanese producers toward higher-end black-haired wagyu.

Wagyu's premium perception also depends on [[BeefGradingAsMarketInfrastructure]] and [[WagyuPremiumBranding]]. A5 grading rewards yield and marbling, but the source cautions that grading is designed for commercial exchange and does not automatically settle personal taste. Overseas, the term can be looser because many markets use "wagyu" for crossbred cattle rather than strictly Japanese-defined beef.
