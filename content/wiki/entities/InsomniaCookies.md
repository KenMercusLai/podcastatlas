# Insomnia Cookies

## Overview

Insomnia Cookies is a late-night cookie-delivery and bakery company founded by [[SethBerkowitz]] while he was in college.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Insomnia Cookies as a mostly directly operated chain approaching 400 bakeries, with a long-term aspiration of 1,800 locations worldwide. Its growth path began in college markets and followed customers into cities and suburbs. Direct operation is treated as a quality and innovation system, while past overexpansion supplies a caution: geographic replication should follow local brand love rather than substitute for it.

## Key Characteristics

- Built around fresh cookies and late-night delivery originating in a college use case.
- Remains heavily associated with college markets while extending into cities, suburbs, transit sites, stadiums, and international locations.
- Operates nearly all bakeries directly, with only limited licensed locations.
- Uses a deliberately simple core offer after earlier experiments with adjacent desserts.
- Treats local brand awareness and customer affection as gates for expansion.

## Evidence

- Origin and lifecycle path: [[advice-line-with-seth-berkowitz-of-insomnia-cookies-vmp8144262595]] describes dorm-room delivery and later movement with former students into cities and suburbs.
- Scale and control: [[advice-line-with-seth-berkowitz-of-insomnia-cookies-vmp8144262595]] reports roughly 390 directly operated bakeries and limited airport and stadium licensing.
- Focus and expansion: [[advice-line-with-seth-berkowitz-of-insomnia-cookies-vmp8144262595]] connects assortment simplification and local awareness measurement to more disciplined growth.

## Qualifications

The reported store counts and 1,800-location target are approximate snapshots and aspirations, not independently verified current figures. The episode argues for the benefits of direct operation but does not compare its full capital requirements or returns with franchising.

## What Changed

- Initial profile created around direct operation, lifecycle expansion, and a focused core offer.
- Preserved overexpansion as a qualification to the company's growth ambition.
