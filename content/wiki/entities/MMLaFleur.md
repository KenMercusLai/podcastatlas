# M.M. LaFleur

M.M. LaFleur is the apparel company founded by [[SarahLaFleur]], used in [[advice-line-strategy-sessions-4962a0d6-36bc-4797-8002-3bd8b748a89a]] as background for advice on premium consumer-brand growth. The episode specifically mentions the brand's M-Dash blog/newsletter as an example of content that can keep customers engaged without being only product promotion.

In the [[TressLondon]] call, M.M. LaFleur functions less as a direct analogue and more as a proof point for editorial customer relationships. The lesson is that a premium brand can use content to express taste, context, and use occasions, but the product still needs tactile proof, buyer trust, and a clear community path.
