# Argo floats

Argo floats appear in [[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]] as the ocean-observation system that helps verify [[EarthEnergyImbalance]]. [[OliverMorton]] says the floats have measured ocean temperature, salinity, and depth patterns since about 2000.

The source uses Argo as independent confirmation of satellite measurements. Because most excess planetary heat goes into the oceans, [[OceanHeatMonitoring]] can check whether [[NASA]] satellite instruments are capturing the same broad change in absorbed energy.
