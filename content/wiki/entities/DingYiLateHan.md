## Overview

[[DingYiLateHan|丁仪]] is presented as a Cao Zhi-aligned political actor whose grievance against Cao Pi and possible role in the accusation of Cui Yan place him inside the late-Han succession struggle.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1156 says [[CaoCao|曹操]] once considered marrying a daughter to Ding Yi, but [[CaoPi|曹丕]] opposed the match by referring to Ding Yi's eye impairment. Ding Yi is then said to resent Cao Pi and become an important adviser to [[CaoZhi|曹植]].

The episode treats Ding Yi as the likely person who turns [[CuiYanLateHan|崔琰]]'s letter into an accusation. That identification is not conclusive: the source distinguishes the more general *Sanguozhi* notice of an accuser with a prior grievance from later or indirect material used to identify Ding Yi. His current profile therefore separates demonstrated alignment and grievance from the episode's reconstructed attribution.

## Key Characteristics

- Prospective marriage candidate rejected after Cao Pi's intervention.
- Political actor described as resentful toward Cao Pi.
- Adviser aligned with Cao Zhi in the succession contest.
- Likely but not conclusively established accuser in Cui Yan's case.
- Useful informer whom the episode says Cao Cao also regards as dangerous and untrustworthy.

Instrumental relationship with Cao Cao:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1156-caocao-weihe-bi-chu-cuiyan-zhe-mian-daode-daqi-lpmr4ep1uk9hjmwteosbusxukjgo|Hanji 1156]] interprets Cao Cao as using Ding Yi's accusations while remaining wary of his methods and character.

## Qualifications

The page rests on one structured podcast summary. It does not settle the accuser's identity, Ding Yi's private motives, the marriage proposal's full context, or Cao Cao's judgment of him. Physical impairment is part of the reported marriage objection and should not be treated as evidence of political character.

- Separated established political alignment from the episode's indirect identification of Cui Yan's accuser.
