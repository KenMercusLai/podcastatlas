# Isaac Merritt Singer

Isaac Merritt Singer appears in [[the-invention-invention]] as the inventor and entrepreneur whose improved [[SingerSewingMachine|sewing machine]] became central to the nineteenth-century patent conflict. The source says Singer improved a rudimentary machine with design changes such as a straight needle suspended from an overhead arm, then received a patent in 1851.

Singer's source role is ambivalent. His machine helped commercialize sewing technology, but its use of parts claimed by [[EliasHoweJr]] and other inventors helped create the [[PatentThicket]] that led to the [[SewingMachineCombination]].
