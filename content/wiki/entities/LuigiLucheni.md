# Luigi Lucheni

## Overview

Luigi Lucheni is named in the Mayerling episode as the man who murders [[EmpressSisi]] in Geneva in 1898.

## Current Profile

The source uses Lucheni only as a later tragedy marker. His act deepens the episode's image of [[FranzJosephI]] and the [[HabsburgDynasty]] as surrounded by loss after [[CrownPrinceRudolf]]'s death.

## Key Characteristics

- Lucheni appears in the closing aftermath rather than the Mayerling plot itself.
- His murder of Sisi is used to extend the Habsburg tragedy sequence.
- The transcript renders the name as "Luigi Lucchini"; this page normalizes to Luigi Lucheni.

## Evidence

- Later tragedy: [[690-a-murderous-affair-death-at-mayerling-part-2-glt7203783068]] says Sisi is murdered in Geneva in 1898 by Luigi Lucchini.
- Habsburg aftermath: [[690-a-murderous-affair-death-at-mayerling-part-2-glt7203783068]] places that murder after Rudolf's death and before the later Franz Ferdinand crisis.
- Normalization: [[690-a-murderous-affair-death-at-mayerling-part-2-glt7203783068]] supplies the source-scoped transcript form, while this page uses the standard Lucheni spelling.

## Qualifications

This is not a full profile of Lucheni, anarchism, or the Geneva assassination. It exists to preserve the episode's named later-tragedy link without overextending it.

## What Changed

- Created Luigi Lucheni as the normalized route for the source's Sisi-assassination reference.
