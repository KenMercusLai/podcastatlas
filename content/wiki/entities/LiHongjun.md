# 李洪军 / Li Hongjun

Li Hongjun is the Xiehe urology director referenced by [[SunRuoxi|孙若曦]] in [[ep274-ruhe-zhaodao-shihe-ziji-de-gengnianqi-daan-li2bp1rtitdnvc04rmizyfg1txfh]]. The source mentions him for community research on male menopause-like conditions, including a survey network covering more than 30 research centers and over 10,000 questionnaires.

His wiki role is to ground [[MaleMenopauseVisibility]] in clinical and public-health observation rather than only in popular talk about testosterone. EP274 uses his research reference to argue that male midlife symptoms can be real and severe for a minority of men, while also being hard for patients and families to name.
