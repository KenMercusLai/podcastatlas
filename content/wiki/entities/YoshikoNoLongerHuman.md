# 豪子 / Yoshiko

豪子 / Yoshiko is the tobacco-shop daughter whom [[ObaYozo|大庭叶藏]] marries in [[NoLongerHuman|《人间失格》]] as read by [[33-renjian-shige-danxiaogui-de-zhuanglie-xianji-599924624]]. The source presents her central quality as unpolluted trust: Yozo is moved by her innocent confidence in people and briefly imagines a happy life with her.

The episode treats the later scene with the visiting merchant as deliberately painful and somewhat ambiguous. Its strongest interpretive claim is not simple jealousy or blame. Yozo's collapse comes from the destruction of trust itself: he cannot protect Yoshiko, cannot ask directly what happened, and begins to experience trust as a possible sin.

## Key Claims
- Yoshiko is the source's main case for trust as a fragile moral condition, not merely a romantic possession.
- The source leans toward reading her as harmed rather than guilty, while noting the novel's ambiguity.
- After the event, her careful speech and Yozo's suspicion show that the damage falls on relation itself.
