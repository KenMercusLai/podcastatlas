# Clink

Clink appears in [[keyi-gei-nide-agent-fa-yidian-linghuaqian-le-s10e22-9a652c19-ceb3-46c2-87b4-bca36e684311]] as [[PatrickWu]]'s agent-payment infrastructure company. The source describes Clink as a layer connecting agents, merchants, and payment networks when a transaction includes a nonhuman task executor.

The episode's key Clink example is a [[Visa]]-related demo. An agent passes user context, product type, budget, and authorization into Clink; Clink records that as an instruction or mandate, checks the selected item against the mandate, then turns it into a one-time payment capability. That makes Clink a case for [[AgentPaymentInfrastructure]] and [[AgentSpendControls]] rather than only a checkout button.

Clink's source-scoped strategy is compatibility. Patrick says the company wants to work across multiple protocols, agent platforms, merchant systems, and local payment arrangements, including Shopify-style plugins and later Magento or WooCommerce-style merchant onboarding.
