# Articuler.ai

## Overview

Articuler.ai is the people-and-opportunity matching company founded by [[JasonArticulerAI|Jason]] and profiled in [[lnvlrnkk-mvyhiy31eq-gw4qopje]]. The source presents its current wedge as event audience acquisition and attendee introductions rather than a general-purpose social network or another ticketing system.

## Current Profile

The product serves two linked sides of an event. Organizers describe a topic and target audience so the company can identify and invite likely participants; before the event, attendees receive recommendations based on their background, intent, needs, and resources. The company aims to improve attendance and make the event useful as a relationship-discovery environment.

Its longer-term thesis combines [[EventIntentMatching]] with [[RevealedConnectionPreference]]. A single event supplies temporary density and explicit intent, while connection requests and reciprocal acceptance supply behavioral feedback for later matching. The episode also describes a Signal feature that gathers relevant developments and identifies people and contact routes, but does not independently verify current product scope or performance.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses events as bounded, intent-rich environments for introductions rather than trying to launch a dense global social graph all at once.
- Offers organizers targeted audience invitation alongside attendee-facing relationship recommendations.
- Represents backgrounds and intentions for model-based matching instead of relying only on literal keyword search.
- Treats connection choice and reciprocal response as learning signals about user preference.
- Seeks complementarity with ticketing and registration platforms rather than duplicating their full operational stack.
- Mixes product work with hands-on service and operations while moving toward remote, more scalable event support.

## Qualifications

The company profile comes from one founder interview summarized rather than transcribed verbatim. Claims about a billion-person public-data pool, roughly 70,000 registered users, email performance, attendance improvement, geographic distribution, partner events, customer outcomes, technical differentiation, and planned weekly event volume are source-scoped and not independently verified. Public-data use and personalized outreach also require consent, privacy, accuracy, deliverability, and bias safeguards not specified in the episode. The source alternates between “Articuler.ai” and “Articular AI”; the title spelling is retained here as canonical.
