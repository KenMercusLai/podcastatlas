# Boston

Boston appears in [[burning-questions-a-more-fire-prone-world-6a687ea64207b2a60119df8b]] as one of the U.S. cities with a nighttime-economy official. [[CorrineReynolds]] is described as the city's director of the nighttime economy, working across complaints, late-night crowd management, noise measurement, regulation, and policy advocacy.

The source uses Boston to make [[NightlifeMayor]] practical. A nighttime-economy role is not only branding; it is a city operating function that has to balance residents' sleep, legal entertainment, restaurant and bar economics, public safety, and [[SocializingDecline]].
