# 孙膑 / Sun Bin

孙膑 / Sun Bin enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]], where he is the strategist behind [[TianJiHorseRacing|田忌赛马]] and [[EncirclingWeiToRescueZhao|围魏救赵]]. The episode first presents him as [[PangJuan|庞涓]]'s fellow student under [[Guiguzi|鬼谷子]], then as the victim of Pang Juan's trap in [[WeiState|魏国]].

The source stresses Sun Bin's recovery of agency after punishment. A [[QiState|齐国]] envoy secretly brings him out of Wei, [[TianJi|田忌]] treats him as a guest, and his reading of the horse races reveals his method: classify available resources, accept a local loss, and arrange matchups so the total contest can be won.

In the military branch, [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] first wants him to command the rescue of [[ZhaoState|赵国]], but Sun Bin says his mutilation and disability make immediate army authority difficult. He therefore becomes Tian Ji's military adviser and redirects Qi away from a direct rescue march toward an attack on [[Daliang|大梁]], leading to [[GuilingBattle|桂陵之战]].
