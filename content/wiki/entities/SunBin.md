# 孙膑 / Sun Bin

孙膑 / Sun Bin enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]], where he is the strategist behind [[TianJiHorseRacing|田忌赛马]] and [[EncirclingWeiToRescueZhao|围魏救赵]]. The episode first presents him as [[PangJuan|庞涓]]'s fellow student under [[Guiguzi|鬼谷子]], then as the victim of Pang Juan's trap in [[WeiState|魏国]].

The source stresses Sun Bin's recovery of agency after punishment. A [[QiState|齐国]] envoy secretly brings him out of Wei, [[TianJi|田忌]] treats him as a guest, and his reading of the horse races reveals his method: classify available resources, accept a local loss, and arrange matchups so the total contest can be won.

In the military branch, [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] first wants him to command the rescue of [[ZhaoState|赵国]], but Sun Bin says his mutilation and disability make immediate army authority difficult. He therefore becomes Tian Ji's military adviser and redirects Qi away from a direct rescue march toward an attack on [[Daliang|大梁]], leading to [[GuilingBattle|桂陵之战]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-19-maling-zhi-zhan-lhbapt09x2ptdqx0uopkp8apbada]] extends Sun Bin from the Guiling pattern into the [[MalingBattle|马陵之战]] setup. He advises [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] not to rescue [[HanState|韩国]] too early, turning intervention timing into [[DelayedRescueStatecraft|delayed rescue statecraft]]. After Qi enters the war, he again targets [[Daliang|大梁]] and then designs the next move around [[ForcedMarchOperationalRisk|forced-march risk]], using Wei's contempt for Qi to lure [[PangJuan|庞涓]] into costly pursuit.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-20-pangjuan-si-ci-shu-xia-ls-8-3jhz5q1c5ca11mhdzmz4wg4]] narrates the execution of that plan. Sun Bin uses [[DecreasingStovesDeception|减灶诱敌]] to make Pang Juan believe Qi troops are deserting, then chooses Maling as the ambush site, writes the fatal message on a stripped tree, and orders crossbowmen to fire when they see torchlight. The episode also says [[SunBinBingfa|《孙膑兵法》]] gives the ambush a field-engineering layer, with chariots serving as walls and caltrops as a moat.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-21-zouji-xia-yinzhao-tianji-guowai-binan-lkvmjoxolgfgw-ig7ir-ngvjifij]] moves Sun Bin from battlefield adviser to political-crisis adviser. When [[ZouJi|成侯邹忌]]'s [[TreasonAccusationTrap|treason accusation trap]] reaches [[TianJi|田忌]], Sun Bin proposes rushing Yongmen before it closes and turning the accusation into a sudden counterattack. Tian Ji refuses and flees to [[ChuState|楚国]], so Sun Bin's final role in this arc is as a strategist whose high-risk political counterstroke is not adopted.
