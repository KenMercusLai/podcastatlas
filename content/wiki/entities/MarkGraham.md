# Mark Graham

Mark Graham is identified in [[tech-20260421-0421-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260421-0421-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the director of the [[WaybackMachine|Wayback Machine]]. The episode cites his view that when news publishers turn away from libraries, they may hurt themselves, because journalists can lose evidence, documentation, and tools they rely on for reporting.

Within the wiki, Graham anchors the archive-side warning in [[ArchiveAccessTradeoff]]. Publisher blocking may reduce perceived [[AIProxyScrapingRisk]], but the same move can weaken [[PublicWebArchiving]], [[PublicServiceJournalism]], and the ability to check how online information changed over time.
