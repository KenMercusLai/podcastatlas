# Dream Face Technologies

## Overview
Dream Face Technologies is the company connected to [[RyanSocialRobot|Ryan]] in [[ep-42-when-ai-meets-robotics-building-machines-that-care]]. The source says the company is based on research from [[MohammadMahoor]]'s lab at [[UniversityOfDenver]].

## Current Profile
In the source, Dream Face Technologies is a research-commercialization vehicle for social robotics in care. Its relevance is not only that it builds a robot, but that its product depends on integrating computer vision, affect sensing, LLM conversation, user experience, safety, privacy, and clinical-pilot learning.

The company is also used as an academic-entrepreneurship case. Mahoor describes starting with NSF I-Corps-style customer discovery and then thinking in terms of prototypes, MVPs, SBIR/STTR-style funding phases, persistence, and pivots.

## Key Characteristics
- Productizes University of Denver lab research into social companion robotics.
- Centers [[RyanSocialRobot|Ryan]] as an elder-care companion for cognitively impaired or lonely older adults.
- Integrates AI architecture shaped by computer vision, CNN-based models, foundation models, and LLM-enabled dialogue.
- Uses graduate-student research, internships, and company work as part of the research-to-product path.
- Represents academic entrepreneurship where customer discovery, grants, pilots, and product iteration must move together.
