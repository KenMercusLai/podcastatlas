# MineLab

## Overview
MineLab is presented as an AI company focused on enterprise reinforcement learning and model post-training, using customer work as both a delivery setting and a source of grounded research problems.

## Current Profile
The source says MineLab moved from consumer applications toward enterprise post-training without changing its deeper aim of improving model capability through reinforcement learning. Its proposed advantage is access to private tasks, dynamic feedback, and real environments, combined with infrastructure for efficient iteration and LoRA-based specialization.

## Key Characteristics
- Enterprise post-training provider and research organization.
- Uses customer environments to obtain proprietary data, failures, and evaluation signals.
- Requires customer benchmarks before claiming capability or cost improvement.
- Treats commercial delivery as a test of research usefulness rather than as detached outsourcing.
- Explores low-cost specialization and multi-model diversity through [[MineLabMint|Mint]].
