## Overview

樊准 / Fan Zhun appears in the current wiki as an early Eastern Han official whose advice helps [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]] convert crisis pressure into institutional repair through education, relief, and field administration.

## Current Profile

Hanji 779 adds the earliest current Fan Zhun layer. Presented as a Nanyang shangshulang, he warns that learning has weakened: doctors are not teaching, students are fewer, and scholars are drifting toward flattery rather than loyal and upright speech. He urges the court to seek hidden scholars and learned Confucians so the young ruler's future education has capable instructors. Deng accepts the advice and orders senior officials to recommend people of high conduct and broad learning who can become doctors.

Hanji 781 then shows Fan Zhun in a disaster-relief role as yushi zhongcheng. Facing earthquakes, floods, wind, hail, drought, hunger, and refugee distress, he memorializes that central spending and construction labor should be reduced, envoys should investigate afflicted regions, and the extremely poor should be relocated so they do not starve. Deng again accepts the memorial, releases public fields to civilians, and sends Fan Zhun to Jizhou to comfort victims.

## Key Characteristics

- Education memorialist who treats scholar recruitment and imperial instruction as state repair.
- Relief memorialist who frames disaster response as administrative action rather than only omen interpretation.
- Adviser whose proposals are accepted quickly by Empress Dowager Deng in both education and relief contexts.
- Field official sent to Jizhou after the relief memorial, linking advice to implementation.
- Positive early-regency channel beside the same period's coup, frontier, and fiscal crises.

## Qualifications

The current evidence is still narrow. Hanji 779 and Hanji 781 show Fan Zhun through successful memorials and assignments, not through a full career, the actual performance of nominated doctors, or measured relief results in Jizhou. The page treats him as an institutional-repair figure within Deng's early regency, not as a complete biography.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 779 as Fan Zhun's education-repair layer before the existing Hanji 781 relief layer.
- Reframed Fan Zhun as an early regency adviser across learning and relief rather than only a disaster memorialist.
