## Overview

樊准 / Fan Zhun is an Eastern Han official in Hanji 781 whose relief memorial shapes the early administrative profile of [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]]'s regency. He links disaster interpretation to spending restraint, field investigation, migration relief, and public-field release.

## Current Profile

Hanji 781 presents Fan Zhun as yushi zhongcheng during the Yongchu disaster field. Facing earthquakes, floods, wind, hail, drought, hunger, and refugee distress, he memorializes that central spending and construction labor should be reduced, envoys should be sent to afflicted regions, and the extremely poor should be relocated so they do not starve.

Deng accepts the memorial immediately and gives all public fields to civilians. Fan Zhun is then sent to Jizhou, while Lu Cang is sent to Yanzhou, to comfort disaster victims. His page therefore anchors the relief-administration side of the regency's opening, balancing the same source's coup and coercion material.

## Key Characteristics

- Relief memorialist who frames disaster response as administrative action rather than only ritual blame.
- Advocates spending cuts, construction-labor reduction, disaster-zone envoys, and relocation of the extremely poor.
- Helps trigger Deng's immediate public-field release to civilians.
- Represents the humane state-capacity side of a regency otherwise marked by coup suppression and frontier war.
- Serves as a concrete official channel between disaster-omen pressure and relief execution.
