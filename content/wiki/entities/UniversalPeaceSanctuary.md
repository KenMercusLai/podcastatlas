# Universal Peace Sanctuary

## Overview
Universal Peace Sanctuary is the peace project described by [[ShyalpaRinpoche]] in [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-97450677c47f502770248abb02f35793-163628914-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-97450677c47f502770248abb02f35793-163628914]]. The source places it in Lumbini, [[Nepal]], and frames it as a sanctuary, conference venue, peace museum, library, institute, and global movement for dialogue, trust, and unconditional love.

## Current Profile
In the episode, the sanctuary is both a physical construction project and a peace-building institution. Shyalpa Rinpoche connects its location to Maya Devi and motherly love, says the project is partly built, describes planned halls and cultural facilities, and imagines a network of peace ambassadors, peace clubs, donors, technologists, and transparent blockchain-based accounting.

## Key Characteristics
- Lumbini-based project presented as belonging to the world rather than only to Nepal.
- Symbolically grounded in motherly unconditional love and the birth context of the Buddha.
- Designed in the source as a physical venue for dialogue, conferences, culture, education, and peace research.
- Intended to scale through ambassadors, members, peace clubs, donors, and cross-border stakeholder participation.
- Connected to [[PeaceInfrastructure]] because it treats peace as something needing institutions, facilities, trust, and operating systems.
- Uses technology language, including blockchain and AI, to argue for transparent and modern peace-building.
