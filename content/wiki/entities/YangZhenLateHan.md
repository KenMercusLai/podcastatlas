# 杨震 / Yang Zhen (late Han)

杨震 / Yang Zhen enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-914-xinren-de-ren-weihe-zong-shang-ni-zui-shen-lvwit-rzrkscn5a3h6wblntxppso|Hanji 914]] as the family-reputation figure behind [[YangQiLateHan|杨琦]]'s exchange with [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]. When Yang Qi answers Ling's comparison with Emperor Huan too directly for the ruler's taste, Ling calls him a stubborn descendant of Yang Zhen.

The episode also recounts the omen-like burial story in which a large bird appears at Yang Zhen's coffin and cries until burial is complete. In this source, the story functions less as a full Yang Zhen biography than as a moral-family marker: Ling can mock Yang Qi through the same reputation that makes Yang Qi's direct answer legible.
