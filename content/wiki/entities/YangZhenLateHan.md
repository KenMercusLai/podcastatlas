## Overview

杨震 / Yang Zhen now appears in this wiki as a late-Han moral official whose death, posthumous repair, and later family reputation form one sequence. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]] supplies the death layer under [[EmperorAnOfHan|汉安帝]]; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-805-tongtou-renxing-de-nvren-jing-neng-yuce-jixiong-ll3bzoyrkbtnvt-aqu9nqelqonew|Hanji 805]] supplies [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]]'s vindication; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-914-xinren-de-ren-weihe-zong-shang-ni-zui-shen-lvwit-rzrkscn5a3h6wblntxppso|Hanji 914]] shows Yang-family reputation still usable under [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]] gives Yang Zhen his active biography in the current wiki. The source presents him as a classical scholar who studied deeply, taught thousands of students, gained the reputation label "Guanxi Kongzi," entered office around age fifty, and rose to taiwei while keeping an upright and unbending public style.

The same source makes his death a court-feedback failure. [[FanFengEunuchLateHan|Fan Feng]], [[ZhouGuangLateHan|Zhou Guang]], [[XieYunLateHan|Xie Yun]], and other favorites forge imperial instructions to use public resources and labor for private estates. Yang Zhen repeatedly memorializes against eunuch power and luxury construction, using earthquake, no winter snow, and spring drought as [[OmenCodedRemonstrance|omen-coded]] evidence that the court must remove corrupt favorites.

Yang Zhen also tries to save [[ZhaoTengLateHan|Zhao Teng]] after Zhao's direct criticism is treated as grave disloyalty. Emperor An ignores the mitigation plea and executes Zhao Teng. When Yang Zhen later obtains the forged orders and prepares to report them after An returns, Fan Feng's group uses a taishi star report and the Zhao Teng case to frame him as resentful, factional, and tied to the old Deng family.

The result is removal and self-destruction. On 124-03-29, Emperor An strips Yang Zhen of the taiwei seal. Fan Feng's side then has [[GengBaoGrandGeneralLateHan|Geng Bao]] accuse Yang of resentful noncompliance, and An orders Yang sent back to his home commandery. At Xiyang Pavilion, Yang tells his sons that he failed to punish corrupt ministers and stop disorder, instructs them to use a plain coffin and burial, and kills himself by poison. The later humiliation of his funeral procession makes the public sympathy in the source explicit.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-805-tongtou-renxing-de-nvren-jing-neng-yuce-jixiong-ll3bzoyrkbtnvt-aqu9nqelqonew|Hanji 805]] supplies the repair layer. After Shun's restoration, Yang Zhen's students Yu Fang and Chen Yi come to the palace gate seeking redress. Shun appoints two of Yang's sons as lang, awards money, reburies Yang with Three Excellency rites, and sacrifices to him after the reported large-bird omen at the coffin.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-914-xinren-de-ren-weihe-zong-shang-ni-zui-shen-lvwit-rzrkscn5a3h6wblntxppso|Hanji 914]] supplies the later reputation layer. Yang Qi's direct answer to Emperor Ling's comparison with Emperor Huan irritates Ling, who calls Yang Qi a stubborn descendant of Yang Zhen. The moral name that makes Yang Qi's answer legible also gives Ling a way to mock it.

## Key Characteristics

- Upright scholar-official remembered through deep classical learning, teaching, and the "Guanxi Kongzi" reputation label.
- Taiwei who uses disaster signs and public-resource abuse to attack eunuch and favorite power under Emperor An.
- Clemency advocate who tries and fails to save Zhao Teng from execution for direct criticism.
- Political martyr figure whose evidence against forged orders is neutralized by slander before reaching the emperor.
- Posthumously vindicated moral official whose sons, burial status, and sacrifices become part of Shun's legitimacy repair.
- Yang-family reputation marker later invoked when Yang Qi speaks too directly to Emperor Ling.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 800, Hanji 805, and Hanji 914, not a complete Yang Zhen biography. Hanji 800 supplies the death narrative and the host's clear moral judgment, but the wiki keeps motive, legal accusation, and public sympathy as source-bounded claims. Hanji 805's large-bird event is preserved as a source-reported omen story, not independent natural evidence. Yu Fang, Chen Yi, Yang Zhen's sons, Yi Liang, and the exact procedure behind the forged documents remain source-scoped.
