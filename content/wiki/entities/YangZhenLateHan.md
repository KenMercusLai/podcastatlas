## Overview

杨震 / Yang Zhen now appears in this wiki as a late-Han moral official whose confrontation with court favorites begins before the fatal forged-edict case. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-799-donghan-mingchen-yangzhen-ruhe-jujue-zou-houmen-lmufb7lvb-kzsvjtn6iu-zrpiptf|Hanji 799]] adds the 123 CE appointment-refusal and Wang Sheng mansion memorial layer; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]] supplies his 124 CE death under [[EmperorAnOfHan|汉安帝]]; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-805-tongtou-renxing-de-nvren-jing-neng-yuce-jixiong-ll3bzoyrkbtnvt-aqu9nqelqonew|Hanji 805]] supplies [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]]'s vindication; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-914-xinren-de-ren-weihe-zong-shang-ni-zui-shen-lvwit-rzrkscn5a3h6wblntxppso|Hanji 914]] shows Yang-family reputation still usable under [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-799-donghan-mingchen-yangzhen-ruhe-jujue-zou-houmen-lmufb7lvb-kzsvjtn6iu-zrpiptf|Hanji 799]] supplies Yang Zhen's earliest current court layer. After Liu Kai is removed, Yang moves from situ to taiwei. [[GengBaoGrandGeneralLateHan|Geng Bao]] personally asks him to recommend [[LiRunLateHan|Li Run]]'s elder brother for Three Excellencies recruitment, saying Li Run is trusted by the emperor and that Geng is only guessing imperial intent. Yang answers that if the emperor wants the office to summon someone, there should be an imperial instruction. He also refuses [[YanXianLateHan|Yan Xian]]'s request for a close associate, making procedural authorization rather than private access the decisive line.

The same source shows Yang's first current memorial against favorite privilege. Emperor An enfeoffs his wet nurse [[WangShengPalaceActorLateHan|Wang Sheng]] and orders a mansion built for her. Yang argues that disasters, poverty, frontier war, and an empty treasury make such construction improper, then criticizes [[ZhouGuangLateHan|Zhou Guang]], [[XieYunLateHan|Xie Yun]], and favored palace actors for bribery, solicitation, and placing people barred for corruption into important posts. An ignores the memorial, and Yang's refusal increases hostility from outer relatives and eunuch networks.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-800-yangzhen-zhisi-yi-chang-jianchen-dui-qingguan-de-jiaosha-lk9qcfs4xl-rjd-zpwdtsblybxa8|Hanji 800]] then gives Yang his fatal active biography. The source presents him as a classical scholar who studied deeply, taught thousands of students, gained the reputation label "Guanxi Kongzi," entered office around age fifty, and rose to taiwei while keeping an upright and unbending public style.

Hanji 800 also turns the earlier tension into a court-feedback collapse. [[FanFengEunuchLateHan|Fan Feng]], Zhou Guang, Xie Yun, and other favorites forge imperial instructions to use public resources and labor for private estates. Yang Zhen memorializes against eunuch power and luxury construction through earthquake, no winter snow, and spring drought as [[OmenCodedRemonstrance|omen-coded]] evidence that the court must remove corrupt favorites.

Yang also tries to save [[ZhaoTengLateHan|Zhao Teng]] after Zhao's direct criticism is treated as grave disloyalty. Emperor An ignores the mitigation plea and executes Zhao Teng. When Yang later obtains the forged orders and prepares to report them after An returns, Fan Feng's group uses a taishi star report and the Zhao Teng case to frame him as resentful, factional, and tied to the old Deng family.

The result is removal and self-destruction. On 124-03-29, Emperor An strips Yang Zhen of the taiwei seal. Fan Feng's side then has Geng Bao accuse Yang of resentful noncompliance, and An orders Yang sent back to his home commandery. At Xiyang Pavilion, Yang tells his sons that he failed to punish corrupt ministers and stop disorder, instructs them to use a plain coffin and burial, and kills himself by poison. The later humiliation of his funeral procession makes the public sympathy in the source explicit.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-805-tongtou-renxing-de-nvren-jing-neng-yuce-jixiong-ll3bzoyrkbtnvt-aqu9nqelqonew|Hanji 805]] supplies the repair layer. After Shun's restoration, Yang Zhen's students Yu Fang and Chen Yi come to the palace gate seeking redress. Shun appoints two of Yang's sons as lang, awards money, reburies Yang with Three Excellency rites, and sacrifices to him after the reported large-bird omen at the coffin.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-914-xinren-de-ren-weihe-zong-shang-ni-zui-shen-lvwit-rzrkscn5a3h6wblntxppso|Hanji 914]] supplies the later reputation layer. Yang Qi's direct answer to Emperor Ling's comparison with Emperor Huan irritates Ling, who calls Yang Qi a stubborn descendant of Yang Zhen. The moral name that makes Yang Qi's answer legible also gives Ling a way to mock it.

## Key Characteristics

- Upright scholar-official remembered through deep classical learning, teaching, and the "Guanxi Kongzi" reputation label.
- Taiwei who treats appointment requests without edict authority as improper private access.
- Remonstrant who attacks wet-nurse luxury construction, favorite solicitation, bribery, and corrupt office placement before the forged-edict scandal.
- Court critic who uses disaster signs and public-resource abuse to attack eunuch and favorite power under Emperor An.
- Clemency advocate who tries and fails to save Zhao Teng from execution for direct criticism.
- Political martyr figure whose evidence against forged orders is neutralized by slander before reaching the emperor.
- Posthumously vindicated moral official whose family reputation remains politically legible generations later.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 799, Hanji 800, Hanji 805, and Hanji 914, not a complete Yang Zhen biography. Hanji 799 supplies the first current appointment and luxury-construction conflict; Hanji 800 supplies the death narrative and the host's clear moral judgment. Hanji 805's large-bird event is preserved as a source-reported omen story, not independent natural evidence. Li Run's elder brother, Yu Fang, Chen Yi, Yang Zhen's sons, Yi Liang, and the exact procedure behind the forged documents remain source-scoped.
