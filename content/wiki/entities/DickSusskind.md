# Dick Susskind

## Overview

Dick Susskind was [[CliffordIrving]]'s collaborator in constructing the fraudulent [[HowardHughes]] autobiography.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Susskind as part of the hoax's creative and evidentiary machinery. He and Irving researched Hughes's past and speech, traveled to alleged interview locations, improvised recorded conversations in alternating roles, transcribed the performances, and shaped them into a more persuasive narrative.

An invented episode in which Hughes offers Susskind a prune rather than a handshake illustrates the method: a small, unverifiable, eccentric detail felt credible because it matched expectations of the reclusive billionaire.

## Key Characteristics

- Collaborated in research, travel, interview performance, transcription, and narrative editing.
- Helped create an apparently spontaneous Hughes voice through rehearsed improvisation.
- Functioned inside a system that joined factual scaffolding to invented intimate detail.
