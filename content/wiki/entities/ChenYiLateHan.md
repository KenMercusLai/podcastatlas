# 陈逸 / Chen Yi (late Han)

陈逸 / Chen Yi (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-941-shenchu-guanchang-ta-jing-xiang-ganfan-huangdi-liu-fqiz9pi7u6cltrn-cjmjz-r|Hanji 941]] as a participant in [[WangFenLateHan|王芬]]'s failed 188 CE coup plan. The source identifies him as a son of [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]], whose death under eunuch pressure gives Chen Yi a revenge motive.

His role is mainly emotional and factional rather than operational. The episode uses Chen Yi to show how old [[PartyProhibitionRehabilitation|党锢/党人]] grievances can feed a new anti-eunuch plot, but the actual plan still fails because the group cannot solve the risk of seizing and replacing the emperor.
