# 鲜于辅 / Xianyu Fu

鲜于辅 / Xianyu Fu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] as a former subordinate of [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] from Yuyang. After [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] kills Liu Yu, Xianyu Fu raises troops to avenge him.

The episode makes Xianyu Fu part of the coalition mechanism rather than a lone avenger. He works alongside [[YanRou|阎柔]], receives support from [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] and [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] cavalry, welcomes [[LiuHeLateHan|刘和]], and links with [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s side to attack Gongsun Zan.
