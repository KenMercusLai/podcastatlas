# 鲜于辅 / Xianyu Fu

## Overview
鲜于辅 / Xianyu Fu is a Youzhou actor who moves from Liu Yu loyalist retaliation to court-recognized regional settlement. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] presents him as a former [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] subordinate from Yuyang who raises troops against [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] after Liu Yu's killing. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1032-duidai-pantu-caocao-weihe-renci-lmrr3c2f2dayectlwndt4p-katuy|Hanji 1032]] shows him accepting [[TianYuLateHan|田豫]]'s advice to submit to [[CaoCao|曹操]] and the emperor-backed court, and Hanji 1044 adds his personal visit to Guandu and later Youzhou-facing appointment.

## Current Profile
Xianyu Fu currently functions as a hinge between local revenge politics and post-Gongsun-Zan institutional settlement. His first appearance is coalition-building against Gongsun Zan with [[YanRou|阎柔]], [[Wuhuan|乌桓]], [[Xianbei|鲜卑]], [[LiuHeLateHan|刘和]], and [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s side. His later appearance turns that local force into a court-facing arrangement when [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] appoints him Jianzhong general and lets him oversee six Youzhou commanderies. Hanji 1044 confirms the Cao-facing trajectory by placing Xianyu Fu at Guandu and recording Cao Cao's appointment of him as Right Duliaojun.

## Key Characteristics
- Former Liu Yu subordinate whose initial mobilization is framed as revenge for Liu Yu's death.
- Coalition actor rather than lone avenger, working with Yan Rou and frontier cavalry support.
- Youzhou local leader whose position survives Gongsun Zan's fall.
- Recipient of Tian Yu's legitimacy argument for submitting to Cao Cao's emperor-backed order.
- Court-recognized regional authority after Emperor Xian's appointment.
- Later appears personally at Guandu and receives a Youzhou-facing military appointment from Cao Cao.

## Evidence
- Liu Yu revenge coalition: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] has Xianyu Fu raise troops after Liu Yu's death and join a larger anti-Gongsun Zan coalition with Yan Rou, Wuhuan, Xianbei, Liu He, and Yuan Shao-linked support.
- Coalition context: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] makes Xianyu Fu part of the Youzhou backlash that weakens Gongsun Zan's people-support and regional control.
- Submission advice: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1032-duidai-pantu-caocao-weihe-renci-lmrr3c2f2dayectlwndt4p-katuy|Hanji 1032]] has Tian Yu advise Xianyu Fu that Cao Cao's emperor-backed position gives him the better submission path.
- Formal settlement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1032-duidai-pantu-caocao-weihe-renci-lmrr3c2f2dayectlwndt4p-katuy|Hanji 1032]] says Emperor Xian appoints Xianyu Fu Jianzhong general and leaves him over six Youzhou commanderies.
- Guandu visit and appointment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1044-guanyu-qianli-zou-danqi-xu-ganxie-caocao-busha-enqing-lpptlnc-e4zu4lcjr49bquxdw0pm|Hanji 1044]] says Xianyu Fu visits Guandu and Cao Cao appoints him Right Duliaojun.

## Qualifications
The current evidence does not make Xianyu Fu an independent claimant or a fully profiled long-term governor. His role is source-bounded to anti-Gongsun coalition mobilization and the later Cao-court submission settlement. The administrative details of the six commanderies and Right Duliaojun appointment remain compact here unless later sources expand them.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1032, shifting the page from a single revenge-coalition note to a two-stage profile: Liu Yu loyalist mobilization followed by court-backed Youzhou settlement.
- Added Tian Yu and Cao Cao as direct relationships in Xianyu Fu's alignment change.
- Added Hanji 1044's Guandu visit and Right Duliaojun appointment.

- [[YanRou|阎柔]] - Wuhuan-backed organizer in the same anti-Gongsun coalition and later frontier settlement.
- [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] - opponent whose killing of Liu Yu and later fall frame Xianyu Fu's rise.
- [[TianYuLateHan|田豫]] - adviser who urges Xianyu Fu to submit to Cao Cao's emperor-backed order.
- [[CaoCao|曹操]] - power to whom Xianyu Fu submits through the court-facing route and who later appoints him Right Duliaojun.
- [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] - formal appointing authority for Xianyu Fu's Jianzhong general role.
- [[Wuhuan|乌桓]], [[Xianbei|鲜卑]], [[LiuHeLateHan|刘和]], and [[YuanShao|袁绍]] - coalition and regional forces around Xianyu Fu's anti-Gongsun phase.
- [[YouzhouLateHan|幽州]] - regional setting for both the revenge coalition and later settlement.
