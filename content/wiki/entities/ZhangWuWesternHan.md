## Overview

张武 / Zhang Wu is presented as a Dai official who initially warns [[LiuHeng|刘恒]] that the invitation to take the throne may be a trap, then accompanies him to Chang'an and receives responsibility for inner-palace security after the accession.

## Current Profile

Zhang Wu's initial judgment starts from the ministers rather than from Liu-family legitimacy. The senior officials are experienced founding-era soldiers and strategists who have just destroyed the Lü group and taken control of the capital; because their ambition and purpose are uncertain, he advises Liu Heng to plead illness and observe rather than enter their field immediately.

The episode does not portray that caution as disloyalty or permanent opposition. After [[SongChangWesternHan|宋昌]] wins the argument and further checks support the invitation, Zhang Wu travels with the Dai party. Liu Heng's post-accession appointment of him as chamberlain responsible for palace gates and internal security shows that the skeptic remains trusted and becomes part of the new ruler's command structure. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-207-liubang-xiaoyizi-lvxu-bei-gunsha-2-luij-woqemjhmopkgrgnl9bclfwd|Hanji 207 part 2]]

## Key Characteristics

- Adviser who treats accession-maker control of the capital as a direct threat to the invited candidate.
- Advocate for delay, illness as a pretext, and observation before commitment.
- Member of the Dai entourage that proceeds after multiple confirmations.
- Trusted skeptic whose caution does not exclude later service.
- Appointee responsible for palace gates and internal protection after Liu Heng enters the palace.

## Qualifications

The current profile rests on one structured podcast summary. The collective phrase “Zhang Wu and others” does not identify which parts of the warning are uniquely his, and the exact office, entourage, dialogue, and security remit require comparison with transmitted histories. Later appointment shows continued trust but does not prove that the original warning was merely performative.
