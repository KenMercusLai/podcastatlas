# Peugeot / 标志

Peugeot appears in [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]] as one of the source's bridges from bicycle manufacturing into automobile manufacturing. The episode uses the Peugeot family trajectory from bicycles toward steam tricycles and fuel-powered cars as evidence that bicycle capabilities could migrate into adjacent vehicle industries.

The page's main wiki role is therefore [[BicycleIndustrialSpillovers]]. Lightweight structures, wheels, bearings, tires, and vehicle assembly knowledge made the bicycle industry more than a consumer-product fad; it helped prepare firms and workers for later mobility categories.
