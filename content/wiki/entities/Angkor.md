# Angkor / 吴哥

Angkor / 吴哥 is the monumental site through which [[ep267-tafang-meigonghe-yuji-hunfeiyi-yu-shuili-diguo-lkq2vxaatn4wxue7y5q1fc3-8yfk]] explains the [[KhmerEmpire|吴哥王朝]]. [[XingHaiyang|邢海洋]] presents his first visit to Angkor as a turning point: the temples, sculptures, abandoned-stone atmosphere, and later forest overgrowth make the scale of mainland Southeast Asian civilization visible.

The source treats Angkor as a combined religious, political, and hydraulic city. Its temples express kingly sacrality, but [[AngkorHydraulicEmpire]] explains how water storage, dry-season irrigation, rice production, and [[TonleSapLake|洞里萨湖]] resources could support dense population and imperial building over centuries.
