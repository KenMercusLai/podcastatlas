## Overview

渭南之战 / Battle of Weinan is the 211 CE Guanzhong campaign in which [[CaoCao|曹操]] defeats a coalition represented in the source by [[MaChao|马超]], with [[JiaXu|贾诩]]'s divisive counsel presented as the decision hinge.

## Current Profile

The current wiki evidence is compact and retrospective. Hanji 1105 uses the campaign to demonstrate Cao Cao's continuing personal command, opponent assessment, and adviser use at age fifty-seven. The page therefore records the battle as a late-career capacity marker, not as a complete operational reconstruction of the Guanzhong war.

## Key Characteristics

- Located in the 211 CE Guanzhong conflict field.
- Pits Cao Cao against Ma Chao and a coalition the episode portrays as numerous but internally fragile.
- Turns coalition cohesion into the key strategic vulnerability.
- Uses Jia Xu's divisive counsel as the episode's decisive mechanism.
- Serves as a bridge between Cao Cao's rise and the later reversals at Hanzhong and Guan Yu's northern campaign.

- Campaign and outcome: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1105-guanyu-zhisi-maizang-le-caocao-zenyang-de-mengxiang-lgmk_dlknjpzz7fvzasno6tu7fs3|Hanji 1105]] places Cao Cao and Ma Chao at Tong Pass / Weinan in 211 and says Cao Cao wins.
- Coalition assessment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1105-guanyu-zhisi-maizang-le-caocao-zenyang-de-mengxiang-lgmk_dlknjpzz7fvzasno6tu7fs3|Hanji 1105]] has Cao Cao judge that a large opposing force cannot remain united.

## Qualifications

Hanji 1105 summarizes rather than reconstructs the campaign. Exact force composition, maneuver sequence, correspondence, casualties, the circumstances of Ma Chao's revolt, and the episode's battlefield dialogue remain source-scoped and need broader historical-source comparison.
