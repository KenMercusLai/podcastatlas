# Lovable

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-more-trillion-dollar-ipos-anthropic-3t-zucks-price-war-china-ends-open-source-trump-accounts-42041390]] adds Lovable as an application company that may move toward its own models. The episode uses Lovable to show why [[ModelFungibility]] is not simple interchangeability: vertical workflow, prompt history, product harnesses, and cost curves can make a specialized model more attractive than a generic frontier API.

Lovable appears in [[youhua-shenglv-erfei-peilv-ba-yi-jian-shi-zuodao-lilun-shang-gaiyou-de-yangzi-duitan-lianxu-chuangyezhe-albert-lu0vamaawctwva3qblnsf99esar2]] as one of [[Albert]]'s examples for [[CodingDemocratization]]. He contrasts possible AI coding containers by audience: [[Cursor]] for programmers, Lovable as a designer-oriented route, and [[Replit]] as a product-manager or builder-oriented route.

The source does not analyze Lovable's company history. Its wiki role is narrower: it is evidence for Albert's claim that AI coding can be handed to different user groups when the product container matches their existing strengths, vocabulary, and workflow.
