# Aaron Burr / 阿伦·伯尔

Aaron Burr appears in [[78-xifang-juedou-shi-chujian-ba-weile-rongyu-687731000]] as the U.S. vice president who fought the fatal duel with [[AlexanderHamilton|Alexander Hamilton]]. The episode uses the duel as a political-honor case where conflict over reputation and public standing moved outside ordinary institutional channels.

The source's interest is not Burr's full career, but the way his duel with Hamilton became an American example of [[HonorDuelCulture|honor duel culture]] colliding with republican politics. It helps show why private combat could be both socially intelligible and politically destructive.
