# 《保你平安》 / Post Truth

## Overview
《保你平安》 is represented as [[DapengDirector|大鹏]]'s comedy about a man pursuing the source of a rumor to restore the reputation of a deceased client with whom he has no kinship obligation.

## Current Profile
The film is the source's clearest expression of Dapeng's faith in “热血,” kindness, and stubborn help for a stranger. When viewers question whether such a person exists, he treats his own belief as a production constraint: disbelief at the creator level would push the story toward a different moral world.

## Key Characteristics
- Rumor-and-reputation story organized around justice for a dead stranger.
- Comedy grounded in character and situation rather than detachable sketch performance.
- Film whose plausibility depends on the creator's sincere belief in uncommon altruistic persistence.

## Evidence
- Story premise - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] describes Wei Ping'an tracing a rumor to clear Han Lu's name.
- Moral belief - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] records Dapeng's direct insistence that people like Wei Ping'an exist.
- Comedy method - [[daoyan-dapeng-luoyonghao-wo-zhe-liang-beizi-liytv2s219yn3ox5dhr4z4mrovyt]] says jokes should arise from character and situation rather than pause the film for isolated skits.
