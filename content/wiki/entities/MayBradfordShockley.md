## Overview

May Bradford Shockley was William Shockley's mother and the episode's proposed private link between his biography and the location of Silicon Valley's early semiconductor industry.

## Current Profile

[[silicon-valley-on-the-couch]] describes May as mathematically educated, intellectually formidable, professionally and financially capable, and emotionally restrained. She raised William largely as an only child after his father's early death and encouraged in him an expansive sense of power and responsibility.

Her residence at 261 Waverly Street in Palo Alto provides the episode's central geographic clue: it was close to the future [[ShockleySemiconductorLaboratory]] site. Letters and Shockley's immediate Nobel telegram show a durable bond, but the claim that he chose Mountain View to remain near her is an interpretive hypothesis rather than documented intent.

## Key Characteristics

- Held a mathematics degree and worked as a mining surveyor.
- Pursued art and reportedly traded stocks successfully.
- Was portrayed as intellectually exceptional and emotionally withholding.
- Raised William largely alone after his father's death.
- Encouraged an unusually expansive sense of power and responsibility in her son.
- Lived in Palo Alto near the future Shockley laboratory.
