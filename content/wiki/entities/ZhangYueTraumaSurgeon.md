# 张悦 / Zhang Yue (Trauma Surgeon)

## Overview
张悦 is presented in VOL.183 as a trauma surgeon at [[ShanghaiEastHospital|上海市东方医院]] who explains emergency trauma work, life-before-limb decisions, risk communication, conservative first aid, and her own exercise injury.

## Current Profile
Within the bounded source, Zhang Yue's clinical profile combines rapid injury assessment with scene and expectation management. She describes a department covering road crashes, falls, fractures, crush injuries, and multi-trauma while also handling wards and operations. Her central decision frame is [[TraumaDamageControlPrioritization]]: first stabilize threats to life, then consider limb preservation, reconstruction, function, rehabilitation, and longer-term quality of life.

Her public-education role emphasizes limits as well as action. Serious internal injury may be visually subtle; amputation and healing decisions remain uncertain; consent should include risks and plans; and bystanders can worsen injury by pulling embedded objects, forcing reductions, discarding exposed bone, or bending a potentially injured spine. Her account of a fatigue-state lifting injury also makes professional knowledge compatible with ordinary human error and vulnerability.

## Key Characteristics
- Trauma surgeon working across emergency assessment, inpatient care, and operative treatment in the source account.
- Prioritizes physiological survival before limb salvage or definitive reconstruction in severe trauma.
- Treats calm communication and control of a chaotic scene as part of emergency clinical competence.
- Explains operative uncertainty without treating consent as transfer of responsibility.
- Advocates conservative bystander handling and early entry into qualified emergency care.
- Uses her own training injury to connect fatigue, inadequate preparation, and load judgment to prevention.
