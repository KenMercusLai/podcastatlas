# Ivan Frantz

## Overview

Ivan Frantz Jr. was a University of Minnesota physician-researcher whose work on cholesterol and heart disease culminated in the [[MinnesotaCoronaryExperiment]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Frantz as both a committed diet-heart researcher and a meticulous experimentalist. He moved from observational uncertainty to an unusually controlled institutional feeding trial, even helping change Minnesota law so margarine could be served in visually indistinguishable squares. The trial lowered cholesterol but did not demonstrate longer life, and its full implications remained underexamined for decades.

His son [[RobertFrantz]] interprets the preserved raw data as evidence of Ivan's deeper commitment to testable science: challenging his preferred hypothesis would honor that principle more faithfully than protecting the belief itself.

## Key Characteristics

- University of Minnesota physician and teacher focused on cholesterol, blood lipids, and heart disease.
- Principal designer of the large, double-blind [[MinnesotaCoronaryExperiment]].
- Personally adopted and promoted a low-saturated-fat diet within his family.
- Preserved raw trial materials despite limited publication of the unexpected outcome.
- Remembered by his son as humble and committed to evidence over personal credit.
