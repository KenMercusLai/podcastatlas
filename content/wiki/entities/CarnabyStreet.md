# Carnaby Street

## Overview

Carnaby Street is the London retail street that became an international symbol of Swinging London through youth boutiques, men's fashion, pop culture, tourism, and media attention.

## Current Profile

The episode describes Carnaby Street's rise from a cheap, rundown backwater rather than from an established luxury district. John Stephen's bright, affordable menswear shops helped build its boutique concentration, while British pop and overseas publicity turned the street into a destination. Its fame was therefore both materially grounded and myth-amplified.

## Key Characteristics

- Benefited from low-cost, centrally located urban space.
- Became important through men's as well as women's fashion.
- Joined inexpensive colorful clothing to dense boutique retail.
- Drew foreign visitors seeking the Swinging London image.
- Lost authenticity as its style became widely marketed and imitated.

## Qualifications

The episode offers a selective cultural history, not a complete business census or urban redevelopment account. Exact shop numbers, timing, causal weights, and ordinary shoppers' experiences remain source-scoped.
