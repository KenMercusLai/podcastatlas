# 陈平嫂 / Sister-in-law of Chen Ping

陈平嫂 / Sister-in-law of Chen Ping is the unnamed woman at the center of [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-154-diangu-chenping-daosao-lingyou-yinqing-li-c6ezwfpejcvbrpzojg-xv2ge8|Hanji 154]]'s source-critical discussion of the "陈平盗嫂" charge. The episode cites [[Shiji|《史记》]] to say she disliked [[ChenPing|陈平]] because he did not work the fields and appeared to consume household support without producing.

The source uses that hostility as evidence against the later sexual-scandal rumor. If the sister-in-law openly resented Chen Ping and [[ChenPingBrother|his elder brother]] responded by expelling her, the host argues that the family scene looks more like conflict than intimacy. The page therefore does not prove Chen Ping's innocence absolutely; it records the episode's reasoning that the famous stain is probably a weak political smear.

Her role is source-scoped and unnamed. She matters because the accusation was not raised by her in the episode's account, but by old generals far from Chen Ping's family circle, making her a test case for [[MoralSlanderReframing|道德诬陷焦点转移]] and [[RumorErosionOfTrust|谣言侵蚀信任]].
