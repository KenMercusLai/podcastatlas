# 平州 / Pingzhou (Warring States)

平州 / Pingzhou appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-38-gao-jianzhi-de-zhangyi-bu-yiban-lobhcpz679psy54k0iduccqkfcrn]] as a place [[QinState|秦国]] takes from [[WeiState|魏国]] after [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] becomes Wei chief minister and urges Wei to lead the lords in serving Qin. When [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王 / 梁惠王]] refuses, Qin attacks and takes [[QuwoWarringStates|曲沃]] and Pingzhou.

The source gives Pingzhou only as part of this military notice, so the page is source-scoped. Its wiki value is to extend [[QinEastwardPressure|秦国东进压力]] from the earlier Jiaoyi/Quwo/Shangjun sequence into the next Zhang Yi-in-Wei episode.
