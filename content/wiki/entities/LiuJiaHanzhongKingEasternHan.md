## Overview

汉中王刘嘉 / Liu Jia, Hanzhong King is a source-scoped Eastern Han actor whose current profile combines a compressed Hanji 670 talent-transfer branch with the more detailed Hanji 686 Hanzhong/Wudu conflict. The page is deliberately disambiguated from [[LiuJia|刘贾]], the Chu-Han and early Western Han figure, because this Liu Jia belongs to the Guangwu-era Hanzhong field around [[YanCenEasternHan|延岑]], [[GongsunShuEasternHan|公孙述]], [[JiaFuEasternHan|贾复]], and [[ChenJunEasternHan|陈俊]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-670-juejing-qiusheng-liuxiu-xianru-shengsi-da-weiji-ltz1nciofev4cqjkod-p8mmwovhe|Hanji 670]] gives Liu Jia a short but important side role. In the source's compressed Hanzhong notice, Yan Cen rises and occupies Hanzhong; Liu Jia resists and the episode then uses him to explain how Jia Fu and the Nanyang man Chen Jun are recommended to Liu Xiu. Liu Jia judges Jia Fu to have broad ambition and sends both men toward Liu Xiu.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-686-liuxiu-fashi-zhi-ai-yinlihua-weihe-li-guoshi-wei-hou-lnqlqgq54srb1zzzgemfwuwujhvc|Hanji 686]] presents Liu Jia in a fuller military sequence. After Yan Cen rebels and surrounds [[Nanzheng|南郑]], Liu Jia is defeated and flees, while Yan Cen occupies [[Hanzhong|汉中]] and attacks [[Wudu|武都]].

The source then gives Liu Jia a recovery but not a stable victory. He gathers tens of thousands of scattered troops, appoints Li Bao as chancellor, and moves from Wudu toward Nanzheng after Gongsun Shu's commander Hou Dan has taken the city. That attack fails, forcing Liu Jia back toward the Hechi area.

The final branch restores limited initiative. Liu Jia later fights Yan Cen again and defeats him after Yan Cen enters the Sanguan route. The current evidence therefore makes Liu Jia a regional survivor and counterattacker, not a fully developed founding minister or independent ruler profile.

## Key Characteristics

- Source-scoped Hanzhong king in the Jianwu 2 / 26 CE Guangwu-era conflict.
- Talent recommender who sends Jia Fu and Chen Jun toward Liu Xiu in the earlier Hanji 670 branch.
- Initial loser to Yan Cen around Nanzheng and Hanzhong.
- Recovery actor who regathers scattered troops and tries to retake Nanzheng from Hou Dan.
- Regional military figure whose comeback remains limited by failed operations and retreat.
- Later counterattacker who defeats Yan Cen near the Sanguan route.
- Disambiguated from the unrelated [[LiuJia|刘贾]] of the Chu-Han and early Western Han corpus.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to the listed source notes. It does not reconstruct Liu Jia's lineage, title basis, later career, or exact territorial authority beyond the Hanzhong/Wudu/Nanzheng sequence. Hanji 670 is compressed and should not override Hanji 686's more detailed account of defeat, regathering, failed counterattack, and later victory; its durable new contribution is the Jia Fu and Chen Jun recommendation. Li Bao, Hou Dan, Hechi, Sanguan, and exact Hanzhong chronology remain source-scoped until additional evidence makes separate canonical pages useful.
