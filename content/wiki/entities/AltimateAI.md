# Altimate AI

## Overview
Altimate AI is the company context for [[PradmeshPatil|Pradmesh Patil]] in the EP45 [[DataScienceWithSam]] source. The episode positions it as focused on agentic AI for data engineering and uses [[AltimateCode|Altimate Code]] as its main open-source harness example.

## Current Profile
Altimate AI is represented in the source as a data-agent infrastructure company. Its claimed differentiation is not a new base model, but a harness that supplies data agents with context, validation, governance, tools, and execution infrastructure.

The company profile is therefore source-scoped around [[AgenticDataEngineeringHarness|agentic data engineering harnesses]]. The episode does not provide a broader company history, financing profile, customer list, revenue, or audited adoption data.

## Key Characteristics
- Builds around agentic data engineering rather than generic chatbot interaction.
- Uses [[AltimateCode|Altimate Code]] as an open-source route into data-agent harness adoption.
- Emphasizes context delivery and validation as the product layer between LLMs and production data work.
- Presents governance, permissions, cost controls, and PII rules as configurable enterprise requirements.
- Uses benchmark performance as part of its argument that harness quality can outperform raw model choice.

## Evidence
- Company identity: [[ep-45-why-ai-agents-break-in-production-the-missing-harness-in-your-data-stack]] introduces Pradmesh as co-founder and CEO of Ultimate/Altimate AI, focused on agentic AI for data engineering.
- Product layer: [[ep-45-why-ai-agents-break-in-production-the-missing-harness-in-your-data-stack]] describes Altimate Code as trying to solve context delivery and validation-layer problems for agents.
- Adoption claims: [[ep-45-why-ai-agents-break-in-production-the-missing-harness-in-your-data-stack]] says Altimate's open-source products have source-scoped claims of more than one million downloads and users in over 200 countries.
- Governance stance: [[ep-45-why-ai-agents-break-in-production-the-missing-harness-in-your-data-stack]] says Altimate's harness includes configurable rules, permissions, and guardrails around cost, governance, and PII access.
- Benchmark positioning: [[ep-45-why-ai-agents-break-in-production-the-missing-harness-in-your-data-stack]] says Altimate topped ADE Bench and DAB, using those claims to argue for harness quality.

## Qualifications
The page records the company's role as presented in one interview. Download counts, geographic adoption, benchmark results, and governance capabilities are not independently audited by the wiki. The source includes a naming ambiguity between "Ultimate" and "Altimate"; this page uses Altimate because the product and repeated company references use that spelling.
