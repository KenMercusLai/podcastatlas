# John Kemp Starley / 约翰·坎普·斯塔利

John Kemp Starley appears in [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]] as the source's key safety-bicycle figure. His [[RoverBicycle]] is presented as the design that made the modern bicycle form legible: two more similar-sized wheels, chain drive, gearing, and a safer riding posture.

Starley's importance is that he solved the high-wheel tradeoff inside [[BicycleProductEvolution]]. Earlier designs could gain speed by enlarging the driven front wheel, but that made the product dangerous and socially narrow; the safety bicycle separated speed from wheel height.
