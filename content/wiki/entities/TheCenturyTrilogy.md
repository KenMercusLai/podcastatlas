# The Century Trilogy / 世纪三部曲

The Century Trilogy is the [[KenFollett]] historical-fiction series recommended in [[vol-110-touzi-jiushi-dui-shijieguan-de-toupiao-maichu-zichan-peizhi-di-yi-bu-wanjiepian-lk5jkgnzvzerymcixylsdqdtn7f8]]. The host emphasizes the first two books, 《巨人的陨落》 and 《世界的凛冬》, because they show families and ordinary people moving through World War I, World War II, and later historical turbulence.

The source uses the trilogy as an investing metaphor rather than as market evidence. In calm periods, people, families, companies, and institutions can use agency; at historical turning points, the scale of change can overwhelm individual plans. That reading supports cash buffers, global perspective, and diversified [[AssetAllocation]] as ordinary-person survival tools.

## Key Claims
- Historical fiction can train attention to periods where individual agency matters and periods where macro forces dominate.
- The trilogy helps the host explain why [[MarketRegimeShift]] should produce humility rather than all-in conviction.
- The source's lesson is not withdrawal from risk; it is [[AdaptivePortfolioDesign]], cash, and broader geographic and asset-class perspective.
