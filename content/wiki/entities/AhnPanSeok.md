# 安盼熙 / Ahn Pan-seok

## Overview
安盼熙 / Ahn Pan-seok enters the wiki through 展开讲讲's memorial discussion of his Korean television directing and the hosts' early podcast memory.

## Current Profile
The source presents Ahn as a director whose dramas made intimacy, female desire, class, workplace hierarchy, and social structure feel inseparable. The hosts divide his work into a "现实三部曲" around adultery, family, and class, and a later "爱情三部曲" more familiar to Chinese audiences, while stressing that his television retained a literary and observational quality they find increasingly rare.

## Key Characteristics
- Korean television director associated with social observation and relationship realism.
- Repeatedly linked by the source to women's desire, subjectivity, marriage, family, and social structure.
- Remembered by the hosts as formative for their own early podcasting and viewing language.
- Treated as a director whose workplace and class details keep powerful people connected to the workers around them.
- Valued for a literary conception of television rather than only plot efficiency or romance.
