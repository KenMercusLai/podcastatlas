# 沙丘 / Shaqiu

## Overview
沙丘 / Shaqiu is the place where [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] dies during his final tour. It is also associated with the earlier death of [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]], so the place links two distinct ruler-and-succession disasters without making them the same event.

## Current Profile
In the Qin branch, Shaqiu moves from a prophetic payoff in the Xu Fu physiognomist legend to the concrete death site at the end of Qin Shi Huang's final tour. The new source emphasizes the historical irony that Zhao Wuling Wang had earlier starved during the Zhao [[ShaqiuCrisis|沙丘之变]], while Qin Shi Huang later died there before a vulnerable imperial transition. The place therefore operates as both geography and a narrative marker for failed succession control.

## Key Characteristics
- Shaqiu is Qin Shi Huang's death site in the late-Qin transition narrative.
- The place is separately tied to Zhao Wuling Wang's fatal succession crisis.
- Its repeated association with ruler death gives it narrative weight beyond ordinary itinerary geography.
- The Qin event and Zhao Shaqiu Crisis must remain distinct despite sharing the place name.

## Evidence
- Fate-reading payoff: [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-135-fanwaipian-1-zhengshi-jizai-kan-nvxiangshi-ruhe-gaiming-ls8lfj4pq-ncsk7x3er7nlb0ltkk|Qinji 135 番外篇1]] uses Qin Shi Huang's death at Shaqiu to validate the source's legend-colored avoidance story about [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负]].
- Final-tour death site and historical parallel: [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-127-1-jiemi-qinshihuang-weili-chujun-shi-shenme-yuanyin-lniz32t2r2d3thgqnbfarrqeq2a2|Qinji 127-1]] places Qin Shi Huang's death at Shaqiu and explicitly recalls Zhao Wuling Wang's earlier death there.

## Qualifications
The place-name recurrence is historically and narratively meaningful, but it does not establish that the two deaths share one causal pattern. Qinji 135's Xu Fu material is legend-colored and incomplete, so its prophetic framing should not be treated as independent confirmation of supernatural foresight.

## What Changed
- Added the direct final-tour account of Qin Shi Huang's death at Shaqiu.
- Made the distinction between the Qin death site and the Zhao Shaqiu Crisis explicit within the current profile.
- Reframed the shared location as a narrative parallel rather than a merged event.
