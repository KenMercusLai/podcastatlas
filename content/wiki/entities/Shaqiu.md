# 沙丘 / Shaqiu

沙丘 / Shaqiu enters this ingest through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-135-fanwaipian-1-zhengshi-jizai-kan-nvxiangshi-ruhe-gaiming-ls8lfj4pq-ncsk7x3er7nlb0ltkk|Qinji 135 番外篇1]] as the place where [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] dies after [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负]] refuses his summons and predicts Qin's collapse. In this source, the place functions as the payoff to the fate-reading branch: avoiding a meeting with the emperor becomes understandable once his death at Shaqiu confirms the danger of the late-Qin court.

This page is a place node, not the event node [[ShaqiuCrisis|沙丘之变]]. The older Shaqiu Crisis branch belongs to [[ZhaoWulingwang|赵武灵王]] and Zhao succession politics; the Qinji 135 branch uses Shaqiu as the late-Qin death-site reference.
