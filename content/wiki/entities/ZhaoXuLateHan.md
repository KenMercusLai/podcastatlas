## Overview
赵序 / Zhao Xu is a 145 CE late-Han military official attached to the Xu-Yang anti-bandit campaign. His profile combines appointment to assist [[FengGunLateHan|冯绲]] with later execution for cowardice and inflated kill reporting.

## Current Profile
Zhao Xu appears when [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan|梁妠]] seeks commanders for unrest in Xu and Yang provinces. He is appointed zhonglang jiang to assist Feng Gun while [[TengFuLateHan|滕抚]] is placed over the Jiujiang commandery branch.

His later end is negative. After Xianbei and southeast unrest continue, Zhao Xu is punished because the source says he behaved fearfully and falsely enlarged his reported victories. He therefore shows the punishment side of anti-bandit and frontier accountability.

## Key Characteristics
- Campaign assistant assigned to support Feng Gun in Xu-Yang suppression.
- Military official operating beside Teng Fu's reward-backed campaign structure.
- Negative accountability case for cowardice during continued disorder.
- False-reporting case where inflated enemy-kill claims become punishable.
