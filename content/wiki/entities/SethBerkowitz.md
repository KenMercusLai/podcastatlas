# Seth Berkowitz

## Overview

Seth Berkowitz is the founder and CEO of [[InsomniaCookies]], which began as a college dorm-room cookie-delivery business and developed into a directly operated bakery chain.

## Current Profile

In this Advice Line episode, Berkowitz is profiled primarily as an operator who links growth permission to customer love. His experience with overexpansion leads him to favor local awareness and demonstrated demand before store rollout, while his preference for direct operation reflects a desire to protect product quality and move innovations through the network without franchise coordination. He applies the same discipline to founders choosing products, channels, and licenses.

## Key Characteristics

- Treats product quality as the non-negotiable center of the Insomnia Cookies system.
- Favors customer evidence over founder instinct when deciding what and where to expand.
- Uses direct operation to retain control over testing, deployment, and execution.
- Recommends narrowing to a simple, repeatable core before adding adjacent offerings.
- Prefers staged experiments in channels, locations, products, and licenses over broad commitment.

## Evidence

- Operating model: [[advice-line-with-seth-berkowitz-of-insomnia-cookies-vmp8144262595]] reports that Insomnia Cookies directly operates roughly 390 bakeries and uses only limited airport and stadium licensing.
- Expansion lesson: [[advice-line-with-seth-berkowitz-of-insomnia-cookies-vmp8144262595]] says earlier openings outran local brand awareness in some markets.
- Advice pattern: [[advice-line-with-seth-berkowitz-of-insomnia-cookies-vmp8144262595]] repeatedly steers Cahindo, Sauna Société, and Desden toward focus, observable customer behavior, and bounded tests.

## Qualifications

The page reflects Berkowitz's episode role and stated operating philosophy, not a complete biography or independent audit of Insomnia Cookies. Store counts, licensing arrangements, expansion targets, and causal claims about direct operation remain episode-scoped.

## What Changed

- Initial profile created around customer-earned expansion and operating control.
- Recorded the qualification that previous rollout sometimes outran local awareness.
