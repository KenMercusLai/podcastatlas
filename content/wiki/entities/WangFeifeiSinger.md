# 王霏霏 / Wang Feifei

## Overview
王霏霏 is a singer and dancer whose participation in [[SistersWhoMakeWaves|《乘风破浪的姐姐》]] is paired with [[MengJiaSinger|孟佳]] in the source's account of mid-career persistence.

## Current Profile
The hosts initially saw prior girl-group training as an unfair advantage, then revised that judgment after learning about reduced work and recognition following her return to China. In this bounded evidence, Wang represents specialist ability maintained through a loss of platform and made newly legible by a major program. Whether that visibility became durable career recovery is not established.

## Key Characteristics
- Brings prior professional group-performance training.
- Continued developing singing-and-dancing ability during reduced opportunity.
- Experienced a return-and-restart phase after an earlier group career.
- Became newly visible when a large program reconnected skill with audience attention.
