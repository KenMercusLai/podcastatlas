# Aung San Suu Kyi

Aung San Suu Kyi is the central political figure in [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]]. The episode says she has been jailed since the 2021 coup by the [[MyanmarMilitaryJunta]], and that her last confirmed sighting was December 30, 2022, at the end of her trial.

The source presents her as a [[DemocraticIconComplexLegacy]] case. Her earlier life includes long detention, the [[NationalLeagueForDemocracy]]'s 1990 election win, the 1991 Nobel Peace Prize, and state-counsellor authority after the 2015 election. The same segment also stresses her defense of the army over atrocities against the [[Rohingya]], her narrowing of debate, and the jailing of journalists.

Her current political importance is symbolic and diplomatic. The episode says [[KimEris]] is pressing for proof of life, while [[MinAungHlaing]]'s government might gain international breathing room by allowing access to her. The junta's refusal is presented as evidence that it may still fear her [[NonviolentSymbolicPower]] inside [[Myanmar]].
