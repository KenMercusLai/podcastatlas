# 刘良佑 / Liu Liangyou

刘良佑 / Liu Liangyou appears in [[ep263-xianxiang-jueqi-faxian-zhongshi-xiangqi-de-meili-lq1lsqcprqyr7auo5gslwyymuiv]] as the figure associated with Taiwan incense-culture revival. The episode says his work drew on ancient incense implements and systems in the Taipei Palace Museum, helping rebuild 香席, 品香, 香笺, and literati-style incense gatherings.

His role in the source is comparative. By presenting earlier 香席 practice as elegant but high-threshold, the episode can explain why today's [[LineIncenseDailyLife|line incense daily-life]] movement matters: incense is shifting from specialized aesthetic training toward more ordinary home scenes.
