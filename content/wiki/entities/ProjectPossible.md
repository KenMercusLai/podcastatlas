# Project Possible

Project Possible is [[NirmalPurja]]'s high-altitude climbing project discussed in [[afghanistan-five-years-on-our-correspondent-visits-6a7ee547049c692a6b3ee0a9]]. The source says its goal was to climb all fourteen mountains above 8,000 metres as quickly as possible.

The project matters because it changed the time scale of elite Himalayan climbing. The episode says Purja completed the fourteen in six months and six days, compared with a previous record of more than seven years, making the feat a case of extreme record compression rather than only another summit list.

It also anchors [[HighAltitudeClimbingEthics]]. The same speed was enabled by methods that drew criticism - bottled oxygen, fixed ropes and helicopters between base camps - while Purja argued that oxygen also helped him rescue stranded climbers.
