# 和连 / He Lian (Xianbei)

和连 / He Lian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-920-mingwu-zhege-daoli-caineng-zhuan-daqian-lqmabg3bpyz6ct2v4arx-iun57ws|Hanji 920]] as [[TanshihuaiXianbei|檀石槐]]'s son and immediate [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] successor. The transcript renders him as "何莲"; this page normalizes him to He Lian because the father-son succession and later [[KuitouXianbei|魁头]] branch match the Xianbei leadership sequence.

The source gives He Lian a negative leadership profile. He is said to lack his father's ability and to be greedy and lustful; when he attacks Beidi, he is killed by local people. Because his son is still too young, power passes to Kuitou, and the later contest between He Lian's son and Kuitou disperses the group.
