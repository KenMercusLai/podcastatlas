# 鲜卑 / Xianbei

鲜卑 / Xianbei enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] as a northern cavalry group joining the anti-[[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] coalition. The episode says several thousand Xianbei riders follow [[XianyuFu|鲜于辅]] south, help welcome [[LiuHeLateHan|刘和]], and coordinate with [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] and [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s side.

The source uses Xianbei mainly to show that Gongsun Zan's political failure has widened beyond a narrow Han official dispute. Frontier groups, Liu Yu loyalists, and Yuan Shao's network all find common cause against him.
