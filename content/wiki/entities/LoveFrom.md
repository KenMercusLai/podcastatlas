# LoveFrom

LoveFrom is the design firm named in [[ferrari-1]] as part of the [[FerrariLuce]] electric-vehicle development. The source links LoveFrom with [[JonyIve]] and [[MarcNewson]], treating the EV as a design problem as much as a drivetrain problem.

The source-scoped point is that [[Ferrari]] cannot rely on raw acceleration alone in an electric world. It needs tactile, emotional, and brand-specific experience design to preserve [[ProductLedWillingnessToPay]].
