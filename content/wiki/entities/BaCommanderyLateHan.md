## Overview
巴郡 / Ba Commandery is a late-Han Yizhou commandery whose current wiki profile links repeated unrest to governance repair, religiously framed revolt, and later regional rebellion. The 145 CE layer now shows [[FuZhiLateHan|服直]]'s small Heavenly King movement becoming politically significant because failed suppression exposes [[ZhongHaoLateHan|种暠]] and [[YingChengLateHan|应承]] to [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]]'s retaliation.

## Current Profile
The earliest current layer is the Fu Zhi episode in 145 CE. Fu Zhi gathers several hundred people in Ba Commandery and calls himself Heavenly King. Zhong Hao and Ying Cheng fail to suppress him, and Liang Ji uses the failure to imprison both men until [[LiGuLateHan|李固]]'s memorial and [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan|梁妠]]'s order reduce the punishment to dismissal.

The later Bandun Man layer makes Ba a clean example of governance-caused rebellion and repair. [[ChenBaoLateHan|陈鲍]] diagnoses the revolt as a response to illegal taxation, coercive treatment, ignored complaints, and blocked access to the capital. The court sends [[CaoQianLateHan|曹谦]] as administrator and issues amnesty, after which the Bandun people quickly submit.

The Five-Pecks and Yizhou revolt layers then widen the commandery's role. [[ZhangXiuFivePecks|张修]]'s religiously framed healing and rice contribution appear in Ba before attacks on commandery and county offices, while [[MaXiangLateHan|马相]] and [[ZhaoZhiLateHan|赵祗]] later spread revolt into Ba and nearby commanderies after rising at [[MianzhuLateHan|绵竹]].

## Key Characteristics
- Yizhou commandery where small-scale revolt can create high-stakes court consequences.
- Repeated unrest setting across Fu Zhi, Bandun Man, Five-Pecks, and Ma Xiang-Zhao Zhi layers.
- Governance-diagnosis case where illegal extraction and blocked complaints explain later Bandun Man rebellion.
- Religious and status-claim setting where rebel leaders use titles, healing, and rice contribution to organize followers.
- Regional node tied to [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] and [[BaShu|巴蜀]] rather than a generic geography label.

## Qualifications
This page is a source-bounded synthesis of Ba Commandery as it appears in the listed podcast notes. It does not reconstruct the commandery's complete administrative geography or every local lineage. Fu Zhi, Bandun Man, Zhang Xiu, and Ma Xiang-Zhao Zhi are kept as distinct episodes unless a source explicitly connects them.
