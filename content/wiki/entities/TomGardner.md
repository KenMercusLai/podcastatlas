# Tom Gardner

Tom Gardner is the [[TheIntelligence]] correspondent for the [[Sudan]] segment in [[outsider-chance-ending-sudans-proxy-war-6a75b3695d765781a2b1b132]]. His reporting frames the war as a [[ForeignBackedCivilWar]] rather than as a conflict nearing conclusion after the [[SudaneseArmedForces|SAF]] retook [[Khartoum]].

Gardner's key contribution is to connect battlefield observation with regional sponsorship. He describes Khartoum as devastated and largely empty, tracks [[LowCostDroneWarfare]] around [[ElObeid]], and argues that outside sponsors' interests in the [[RedSea]], gold, land, and influence make both outright victory and enforceable ceasefire harder.
