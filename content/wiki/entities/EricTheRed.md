## Overview

Eric the Red is the saga-associated Norse explorer and settler linked to the late-10th-century establishment of Greenlandic communities.

## Current Profile

[[103-the-norse-sagas-glt8280648151]] presents Eric through the sagas as an outlaw driven successively from Norway and Iceland after killings, then as an explorer and promoter of Greenland around 985. The famous claim that he chose an attractive name to draw settlers is memorable evidence of saga characterization, while the broad Greenland settlement belongs to a historical outline stronger than the precise dialogue and motives attached to him.

## Key Characteristics

- Associated with Norse settlement of Greenland around 985.
- Characterized by the sagas through outlawry, violence, exploration, and persuasion.
- Father of figures connected to Vinland expeditions, including Leif and Freydis.
- Illustrates how plausible migration history and vivid literary motive coexist.

## Evidence

- Settlement role: [[103-the-norse-sagas-glt8280648151]] connects Eric to the exploration and promotion of Greenland after Icelandic outlawry.
- Family and westward movement: [[103-the-norse-sagas-glt8280648151]] places his children in later Vinland voyages.
- Literary characterization: [[103-the-norse-sagas-glt8280648151]] preserves the attractive-name explanation as a saga claim rather than independently verified speech.

## Qualifications

The episode does not independently authenticate the killings, motives, promotional wording, or every family expedition detail. Eric's profile is therefore a graded reconstruction from saga tradition and broader settlement history.
