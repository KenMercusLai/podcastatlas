## Overview

薛方 / Xue Fang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-644-shui-gan-he-huangdi-shizhe-taojiahuanjia-lkgn0idimbu6twz-symr5012f8sn|Hanji 644]] as a famous scholar who refuses [[WangMang|王莽]]'s summons without dying for the refusal. The episode uses him as a tactically elegant counterpart to [[GongShengXinScholar|龚胜]]: he cites the Xu You recluse model in a way that lets Wang Mang accept nonservice without losing face.

## Current Profile

Xue Fang's importance is his refusal technique. Wang Mang sends a carriage to bring him to court, but Xue Fang declines by saying he wishes to keep the integrity of Mount Ji rather than become Tang Yao's minister. Because Wang Mang presents himself through archaizing and classical models, the allusion gives the ruler a culturally honorable way to stop pressing the summons.

The page therefore treats Xue Fang as a source-scoped case of principle joined to survival. He refuses the same regime that Gong Sheng refuses, but his classical citation avoids escalation and keeps reclusion legible within Wang Mang's own ideological language.

## Key Characteristics

- Famous scholar whose reputation makes him a target of Wang Mang's recruitment.
- Refuses Xin service through a classical recluse allusion rather than open confrontation.
- Uses Wang Mang's own archaizing preference to make refusal face-saving for the ruler.
- Represents the nonfatal, rhetorically skilled end of Xin-era office refusal.
