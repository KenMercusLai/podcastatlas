# 吴立伟 / Wu Liwei

[[xianchang-xianchang-2025-nianzhong-teji-sanlian-nvjizhe-jiemi-xinwen-beihou-de-ku-yu-guang-loz-sggelhx166n2me8v6d8k9a3c]] adds Wu Liwei's reporting-host role. In this [[XianchangXianchang|现场！现场！]] year-end episode, he convenes [[QinSi|秦思]], [[ChengJing|程静]], and [[WeiZhaoyang|魏昭阳]] to discuss women reporters' work, international reporting, source trust, emotional pressure, and audience feedback.

吴立伟 / Wu Liwei is the [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] host in [[ep245-yinian-yidu-xunwei-zhilv-jinnian-qu-chi-chuangyi-cai-li9wtavemokcckialbbj7unpmqsz]]. His role is to frame the annual [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] food issue around "new flavor" and to move the conversation between regional cuisines, restaurant cases, and broader dining trends.

The source uses his questions and summaries to make [[CreativeChineseCuisine|创意中餐]] legible as more than novelty. He pushes especially on [[GuangxiCuisineLegibility|广西菜可识别化]], asking why Guangxi remains less visible than Guizhou, Yunnan, or Sichuan, and on [[ChineseBistroLocalization|中式小酒馆本土化]], where consumers want eating and drinking to happen in the same scene.
