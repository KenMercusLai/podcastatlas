# Alexander Pushkin / 普希金

Alexander Pushkin appears in [[78-xifang-juedou-shi-chujian-ba-weile-rongyu-687731000]] as one of the episode's literary examples of reputation conflict turning deadly. The hosts place his fatal duel in a cluster of cases where love, rumor, and insult made [[HonorDuelCulture|honor duel culture]] feel compulsory even when the underlying dispute was avoidable.

The source uses Pushkin to show the cost of treating public reputation as inseparable from personal worth. His case sits beside [[EvaristeGalois|Evariste Galois / 伽罗瓦]] and [[RichardBrinsleySheridan|Richard Brinsley Sheridan / 谢里丹]] as an example of intellectual or artistic lives redirected by duel logic.
