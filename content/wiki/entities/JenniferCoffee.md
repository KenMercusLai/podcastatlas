# 珍妮花 / Jennifer

## Overview
珍妮花 is the [[HeyCoffee]] operator and guest host who launches the “咖啡小课堂” column within [[DaxiaoDianbo|大小电波]].

## Current Profile
The source presents her as a coffee practitioner whose path runs from instant coffee through company work, espresso and hand-brew experience, brand creation, and roasting. Her consumer guidance prioritizes freshness, traceability, practical observation, and fit between equipment and daily behavior, while remaining explicitly grounded in personal industry experience rather than controlled product testing.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects professional coffee experience to entry-level consumer guidance.
- Treats roasted coffee as a perishable ingredient whose freshness window matters.
- Uses baseline drinks, packaging details, cleanliness, and staff explanations as practical quality clues.
- Favors budget, mature products, maintenance, and after-sales support in equipment decisions.
- States direct preferences and price heuristics while acknowledging differences in sensory ability and convenience needs.
