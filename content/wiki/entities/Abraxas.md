# Abraxas / 阿布拉克萨斯

[[Abraxas|Abraxas / 阿布拉克萨斯]] is the religious-symbolic name that enters [[DemianNovel|《德米安》]] through the bird-breaking-out-of-the-egg note in [[20-demian-gaiyin-de-etou-shang-na-shenmi-de-yinji-567302734]]. The episode says a teacher later explains Abraxas as a figure combining divine and demonic qualities.

The source treats Abraxas as the symbol that lets [[HermannHesse|Hesse]] move beyond a moral universe split between clean good and exiled evil. It connects the name to [[Gnosticism]], taboo, desire, and the need for a god-image large enough to contain the world that [[EmilSinclair|辛克莱尔]] actually experiences.

## Key Claims
- Abraxas links the bird-egg image of self-birth to [[AbraxasWholeGodSymbolism|whole-god symbolism]].
- The symbol does not authorize ordinary cruelty; in the source it asks readers to examine why inherited categories hide half the world.
- Its power in the novel comes from being difficult to define: it points toward wholeness rather than a neat doctrine.
