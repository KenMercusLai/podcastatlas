# Tamagotchi / 拓麻歌子

## Overview
Tamagotchi / 拓麻歌子 is Bandai's electronic-pet product and character IP, approaching its thirtieth anniversary in the episode's account.

## Current Profile
The source presents Tamagotchi as a globally proven but regionally uneven franchise. It reports more than 100 million cumulative units shipped by the end of the prior July, while Asia outside Japan represented only 2% versus 33% in the Americas. [[Bandai]] is therefore using collaborations to lower the access barrier and reconnect the device with fashion, lifestyle, music, and nostalgia demand.

## Key Characteristics
- Portable electronic-pet product with a long-lived character identity.
- Combines device sales with licensable visual and nostalgic value.
- Globally distributed but reported as underpenetrated in Asia outside Japan.
- Adaptable to cross-category collaborations that do not require consumers to begin in a toy aisle.

## Evidence
- **Scale and regional gap:** [[gaojiesi-doubao-zengjia-shuidian-jiaofei-gongneng-ruixing-yinru-naicha-pinlei-gkwrirwopt2saflztgtozfrl]] reports cumulative shipments above 100 million and a small non-Japan Asian share.
- **Lifestyle extension:** [[gaojiesi-doubao-zengjia-shuidian-jiaofei-gongneng-ruixing-yinru-naicha-pinlei-gkwrirwopt2saflztgtozfrl]] lists collaborations across accessories, cosmetics, luggage, foodservice, and K-pop.

## Qualifications
Shipment totals and regional shares are episode-reported and do not provide active users, current sell-through, revenue, licensing income, or anniversary-period retention. Nostalgia can create attention without guaranteeing durable device demand.
