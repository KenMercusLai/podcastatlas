# Koch Disruptive Technologies

In the episode, Koch Disruptive Technologies extends [[CapabilityBoundedGrowth]] beyond legacy industrial categories. Its logic is not simply to buy technology exposure; it is to test where Koch's capabilities, networks, capital, and operating principles can create value in disruptive markets.
