# 晋静公 / Duke Jing of Jin

晋静公 / Duke Jing of Jin appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl|Zhouji 09]] first as [[JinXiaogong|晋孝公]]'s son and successor. The source then says [[HanState|韩国]], [[ZhaoState|赵国]], and [[WeiState|魏国]] depose him, reduce him to commoner status, and divide the last remnant of [[JinState|晋国]].

His source role is terminal. Jin Jing Gong marks the point where the old Jin polity stops being a residual title behind the three successor states and becomes a completed historical extinction in the episode's account.
