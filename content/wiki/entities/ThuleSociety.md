# Thule Society

## Overview

The Thule Society appears in this source as part of the German nationalist and esoteric appropriation of [[Atlantis]]. Its role is to show how Atlantis could be moved from Plato's Atlantic setting into a northern Aryan homeland story.

## Current Profile

The Atlantis episode places the Thule Society after earlier attempts to make Atlantis serve national pride, including Olof Rudbeck's Swedish Atlantis. In the German nationalist version, an ordinary Atlantic location is less useful than a northern origin, so Atlantis shifts toward Thule, the North Sea, and Aryan prehistory.

The source also distinguishes levels of Nazi interest. Hitler is said to have dismissed this Atlantis idea, while Rudolf Hess, Alfred Rosenberg, and Heinrich Himmler are described as interested in it. For the wiki, the key point is not institutional policy but the way racial-national mythmaking can relocate evidence around identity desire.

A narrower organizational role appears in postwar Munich, where the German Workers' Party belonged to hard-right, pan-German, antisemitic circles linked to the Thule Society. This supplies context for the milieu from which the [[NaziParty]] emerged without making esotericism the party's sole origin or treating every later Nazi as committed to Thule mythology.

## Key Characteristics

- The Thule Society represents the northern and Aryan-homeland branch of Atlantis reception in this source.
- Its Atlantis use depends on relocation: Plato's Atlantic island becomes less important than a mythically useful northern origin.
- The source presents the branch as radical nationalist and esoteric rather than archaeological.
- The Nazi-interest details remain differentiated rather than treated as a single uniform position.
- The society also appears as part of the hard-right Munich network around the German Workers' Party.

## Evidence

- Northern relocation: [[315-atlantis-legacy-of-the-lost-empire-part-2-glt2807981945]] says radical German nationalist thinkers shifted Atlantis toward the North Sea because a normal Atlantic location was less useful for their mythology.
- Nationalist-esoteric function: [[315-atlantis-legacy-of-the-lost-empire-part-2-glt2807981945]] places the Thule Society inside a broader sequence where Atlantis is used for national and racial origin stories.
- Differentiated Nazi interest: [[315-atlantis-legacy-of-the-lost-empire-part-2-glt2807981945]] reports Hitler dismissing the idea while Hess, Rosenberg, and Himmler are described as interested.
- Munich party milieu: [[295-the-rise-of-the-nazis-part-1-glt8045984312]] links the German Workers' Party's hard-right, pan-German, and antisemitic origins to circles around the society.

## Qualifications

This page records only the Thule Society's roles in two podcast accounts of Atlantis reception and early Nazi-party milieu. It does not attempt a full organizational history or history of Nazi occultism. The precise institutional link to the German Workers' Party and the beliefs of named Nazis remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Added the society's place in the postwar Munich milieu around the German Workers' Party.
- Preserved the distinction between organizational proximity and uniform Nazi commitment to esoteric mythology.
