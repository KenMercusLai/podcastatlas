# Ministry of Finance of China / 中国财政部

The Ministry of Finance of China appears in [[shangye-xiaoyang-46-mai-jipiao-shi-weishenme-zongyao-duojiao-liang-bi-qian-1005487014]] as the fiscal recipient of the [[CivilAviationDevelopmentFund|civil aviation development fund]]. The episode says passenger and airline payments under this fund ultimately enter the ministry's government-fund system rather than becoming direct airline revenue.

This role makes the ministry part of the wiki's aviation-infrastructure-finance branch. The same passenger checkout line can include airline revenue, a regulated fuel-cost surcharge, and a fiscal transfer used for airports, air-traffic-control systems, routes, and subsidies.
