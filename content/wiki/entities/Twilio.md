# Twilio

Twilio appears in [[peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted-peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted]] as the company that acquired [[Segment]]. [[JeffLawson]] argued over several years that Twilio and Segment would be stronger together because Twilio supplied the communications layer while Segment supplied the customer-data layer.

The source keeps the acquisition logic customer-grounded. [[PeterReinhardt]] says he initially did not accept Lawson's strategic argument, but around 2019 and early 2020 customer requests made the combination more credible. In the wiki, Twilio therefore extends [[CustomerEvidenceStrategy]] and [[VisionToRealityExecution]] into acquisition reasoning: a plausible platform story became actionable only after customer behavior pointed in the same direction.
