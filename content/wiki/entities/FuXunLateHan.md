# 傅巽 / Fu Xun (Late Han)

## Overview
傅巽 / Fu Xun (Late Han) is the Jingzhou official whose surrender argument to [[LiuCongLateHan|刘琮]] treats reliance on [[LiuBei|刘备]] as a no-win delegation of power.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1092 identifies Fu Xun as an East Bureau clerk and one of the officials urging surrender to [[CaoCao|曹操]]. His argument is structural: resisting the court-backed army is dangerous, Liu Bei's defeat would leave Jingzhou exposed, and Liu Bei's victory would make him too strong to defer to Liu Cong. The episode therefore uses Fu Xun as an early Jingzhou case of [[RoleAsymmetricSurrenderIncentives|role-asymmetric surrender incentives]], though it does not supply his full biography.

## Key Characteristics
- Advises Liu Cong during the 208 CE Jingzhou crisis.
- Frames Cao Cao's advance through court legitimacy as well as force.
- Treats reliance on Liu Bei as dangerous under both victory and defeat.
- Represents elite risk calculation favoring surrender.

## Evidence
- Court-and-force argument: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1092-fu-ke-di-guo-de-lougui-yin-yiju-gankai-bei-caocao-cisi-lutwkvmtn_7cv4r5l-mwknmgyjhs|Hanji 1092]] says Fu Xun warns against resisting the court army immediately after Liu Cong's accession.
- Liu Bei dilemma: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1092-fu-ke-di-guo-de-lougui-yin-yiju-gankai-bei-caocao-cisi-lutwkvmtn_7cv4r5l-mwknmgyjhs|Hanji 1092]] says Liu Bei's defeat cannot save Jingzhou, while his victory would eclipse Liu Cong.

## Qualifications
The source does not settle Fu Xun's exact influence relative to Cai Mao, Zhang Yun, the Cai family, and other Jingzhou elites. His office translation, exact words, later career, and private motive remain source-scoped.
