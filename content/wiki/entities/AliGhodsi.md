# Ali Ghodsi

## Overview
Ali Ghodsi is the co-founder and CEO of [[Databricks]], presented in the source as a former UC Berkeley researcher and an operator focused on enterprise data, AI deployment, organizational analytics, and the practical boundary between current AI and superintelligence.

## Current Profile
Ghodsi's central operating thesis is that model intelligence is no longer the main constraint on enterprise transformation. Firms first need to expose governed organizational context, control access and spending, preserve strategic ownership, and redesign work around evidence. His Databricks examples apply that thesis internally through [[DatabricksGenie]], while his AI-risk position distinguishes present cybersecurity and harmful deployment from an unproven resource-efficient recursive takeoff.

## Key Characteristics

- Treats organizational context and governance as prerequisites for enterprise agents rather than secondary integration work.
- Uses internal management analytics to test hypotheses about feedback, hierarchy, productivity, meetings, culture, and decision delay.
- Defines runaway recursive self-improvement more strictly than ordinary AI-assisted software or research automation.
