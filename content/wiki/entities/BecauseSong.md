# "Because" / Beatles Song

## Overview
"Because" is the [[TheBeatles|Beatles]] song that [[RenNing]] reads as the earlier half of the episode's because/so comparison with [[LuoDayou|罗大佑]]'s [[SuoyiSong|《所以》]].

## Current Profile
In the source, "Because" matters because it borrows a logical word while refusing ordinary logical proof. The song links the world's roundness, wind, sky, and human feeling, but the episode argues that these relations fail as deduction and succeed as poetry.

The song also becomes a historical hinge. Recorded in 1969, it sits close to spaceflight's new view of Earth while also inside the Vietnam War, Cold War, and Beatles breakup context. That tension lets the episode compare a sparse self-world relation with 《所以》's denser world of AI, satellites, GPT, data, chips, and system-mediated agency.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses "because" as a poetic connector rather than a proof relation.
- Presents a comparatively cleared world where nature and feeling are directly adjacent.
- Lets love suspend the need for explanatory reason in the episode's reading.
- Becomes newly audible after Earth photography, environmental consciousness, and later technological mediation.
- Functions as the classic that 《所以》 both honors and repositions.

## Evidence
- Logical-word reading: [[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] says "Because" is not really asking for reasons.
- Sparse world: [[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] contrasts its world, wind, and sky with the dense modern inventory of 《所以》.
- Historical context: [[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] places the song near Apollo-era Earth imagery without treating 1969 as politically innocent.
- Later response: [[episode-228-suoyi-aodesai-8211241331-711602]] reports Luo Dayou's tribute and uses the two songs to define [[PoeticCausality]].
