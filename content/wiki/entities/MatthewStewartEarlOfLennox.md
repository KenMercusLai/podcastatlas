# Matthew Stewart / Earl of Lennox

## Overview

Matthew Stewart, Earl of Lennox, is a rival Scottish claimant whose move into English service and Tudor marriage turned factional exile into a later cross-border dynastic line.

## Current Profile

Lennox stood behind [[JamesHamiltonEarlOfArran|Arran]] in the succession because his Stuart claim ran through a female line. He nevertheless remained important enough to carry the sceptre at [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary]]'s coronation and to appear in [[MaryOfGuise|Mary of Guise]]'s efforts to balance the regency factions.

The Reformation and Anglo-French struggle then pulled him toward [[England]]. He assigned his Scottish claim to [[HenryVIII|Henry VIII]], received English lands, and married one of Henry's nieces. The episode treats that marriage as a long-term dynastic consequence because it joined Tudor and Stuart descent in a family later central to Mary's marriage politics.

## Key Characteristics

- Secondary Stuart claimant during Mary's minority.
- Noble participant in the infant queen's coronation.
- Scottish exile who entered English allegiance.
- Dynastic bridge between Stuart and Tudor bloodlines.

## Qualifications

The episode compresses Lennox's changing allegiance and does not independently establish his religious conviction, private motive, or the legal strength of the transferred claim.

## What Changed

- Established Lennox as the early dynastic bridge behind a later Stuart-Tudor marriage claim.
