# Aesop / 伊索

## Overview
Aesop / 伊索 is the skincare and beauty brand in the source's China retail segment, presented as closing some symbolic mainland stores while still expanding its broader city network.

## Current Profile
The source frames Aesop's mainland China movement as retail reallocation rather than simple withdrawal. It closed the Shanghai Hongqiao airport T2 store, its first mainland airport store, after also closing earlier symbolic stores. At the same time, the source says Aesop entered Chongqing and Wuhan, planned additional stores, and had about 30 mainland stores after acceleration under [[LOreal|L'Oreal / 欧莱雅]] ownership.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses high-visibility stores and airports as early brand-recognition surfaces.
- Is now reallocating physical retail away from some symbolic locations.
- Continues to expand into additional mainland Chinese cities in the source account.
- Has an online-channel layer through [[Tmall]].
- Is linked to L'Oreal ownership and North Asia strength in the source.

## Evidence
- Closure evidence: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says Aesop closed its Shanghai Hongqiao airport T2 store and had closed two earlier landmark mainland stores within less than a year and a half.
- Expansion evidence: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says Aesop entered Chongqing and Wuhan and was preparing multiple stores.
- Channel evidence: [[yibao-chunjingshui-fene-xiahua-anta-qixia-yujiafu-pinpai-kaishe-jianshenfang-1012409578]] says Aesop has expanded after L'Oreal's 2023 acquisition and entered Tmall and other online channels.

## Qualifications
The source does not prove whether the store closures were only lease-driven or whether Aesop's China economics improved. The strongest current claim is retail-network reallocation under continued expansion, not a settled growth or retreat diagnosis.
