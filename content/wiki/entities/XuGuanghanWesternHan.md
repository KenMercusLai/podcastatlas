## Overview

徐广汉 / Xu Guanghan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-411-hanxuandi-liubingyi-dengji-hou-de-diyichang-huangwei-daweiji-lks_pia3lxwv_qao02pm4mv-xype|Hanji 411]] as [[XuPingjunWesternHan|许平君]]'s father and [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]]'s father-in-law, whose hoped-for honor is constrained by [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] immediately after Xuan's accession. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-419-nongxu-zuojia-de-hanchen-wangcheng-ruhe-ba-hanxuandi-pian-de-tuantuanzhuan-lim7jqyvestgoqb68pxhyuwiuhyd|Hanji 419]] later presents him as [[HanYuandi|刘奭]]'s maternal grandfather. After Liu Shi is installed as crown prince, Xuan honors Xu Guanghan as Ping'en Hou, but Xu's later request to place [[XuShunWesternHan|徐顺]] near the crown prince triggers the episode's public-interest tutor boundary.

## Current Profile

Hanji 411 presents Xu Guanghan first as a gratitude problem under regency pressure. Xuan wants to reward the father of the wife who had treated him well before accession, but Huo Guang objects that Xu Guanghan had suffered punishment and is unsuitable as a marquis. Xuan therefore delays and later settles for the lesser title Changcheng Jun, making Xu Guanghan's first wiki layer a case of affection and repayment limited by Huo's practical authority.

Hanji 419 presents Xu Guanghan later as a valued relative whose political request becomes delicate because of prior personal kindness and plausible family concern. The episode says Xuan cannot easily reject him face to face: Liu Shi is young, Xu Guanghan is the crown prince's maternal grandfather, and the Xu family had treated Xuan well before he became emperor.

The same source treats the proposal as more than household help. Xu Guanghan asks that his younger brother Xu Shun oversee the crown prince's household, which the host reads as family positioning around the next generation. Xuan answers indirectly by asking [[ShuGuangShuShouWesternHan|疏广]], and Shu Guang reframes the issue: the crown prince is the state's heir, so his teachers and associates should be worthy people of the realm rather than a narrow circle of maternal relatives.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han outer relative linked first to Xu Pingjun and then to Liu Shi through maternal kinship.
- Father-in-law whom Emperor Xuan wants to honor after accession but cannot fully reward while Huo Guang still controls practical authority.
- Later crown-prince maternal grandfather whom Xuan is reluctant to reject bluntly because of past kindness and plausible concern.
- Recipient of Ping'en Hou status after Liu Shi is made crown prince.
- Actor whose request for Xu Shun's crown-prince household role tests the boundary between kinship care and political access.
- Source case showing why outer-relative requests can require mediated refusal rather than direct confrontation.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 411 and Hanji 419. It does not reconstruct Xu Guanghan's full biography, exact title history, complete punishment background, full relationship with Xu Pingjun, or the complete court procedure behind the crown-prince household request. The claim that the request was next-generation political positioning is preserved as the host's interpretation.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 411's accession-stage honor constraint: Xuan wants to reward Xu Guanghan, but Huo Guang blocks immediate marquisate treatment and Xuan delays.
- Preserved Hanji 419's later Xu-family outer-relative request, where crown-prince household access triggers Shu Guang's public-interest refusal.
