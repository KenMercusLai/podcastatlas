# 瓴羊 / Lingyang

瓴羊 is the [[Alibaba]]-linked enterprise AI and data company discussed in [[e248-yi-ge-cui-fahuo-ai-yao-paotong-260-bu-he-ali-lingyang-pengxinyu-liaoliao-zhongguoshi-fde-9e923c4c-1c87-499b-90a4-9a21cc83e4b1]]. In the source, CEO [[PengXinyu|彭新宇]] describes 瓴羊 as building [[EnterpriseGrowthAgent|enterprise growth agents]] for consumer circulation, with agent work concentrated around marketing, sales, customer service, and operations.

The company's relevance to the wiki is its China-side deployment account. 瓴羊's examples make [[ChineseStyleFDE]] less a job-title import and more a full deployment organization: business analysts define outcomes and evaluation standards, AI architects translate business needs into model and handoff architecture, and customer-side experts teach agents the tacit work of strong customer-service, sales, or marketing employees.

## Key Claims
- 瓴羊's "4+X" approach starts with four preset agent roles, marketing, sales, customer service, and operations, then adds the customer's own data assets, context, approvals, and authority boundaries.
- The "催发货" case shows why [[ContactCenterAI]] requires order systems, warehouses, platforms, dispatching, support libraries, and exception handling, not only fluent conversation.
- 瓴羊's route tries to avoid pure person-day customization by standardizing work areas, data flows, and contexts while letting agents adapt to customer-specific workflows.
- Its commercial logic can use labor-substitution math in customer service or effect-linked math in growth scenes such as marketing and投手 work.
