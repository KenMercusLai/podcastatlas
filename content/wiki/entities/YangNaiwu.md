## Overview

杨乃武 / Yang Naiwu was a late-Qing degree-holder wrongfully accused with [[XiaoBaicai|Xiao Baicai]] of adultery and poisoning in the [[YangNaiwuXiaoBaicaiCase|杨乃武与小白菜案]].

## Current Profile

[[29-dongxifang-de-yuanan-deleifusi-yangnaiwu-he-xiaobaicai-lmmhqfl-idiuetsvufgrmpso2l-8|episode 29]] presents Yang as a new juren who wrote legal petitions for neighbors, participated in disputes, and had an antagonistic relationship with the local magistrate. Xiao Baicai had lived nearby, eaten with Yang's household, and received literacy instruction there; rumor turned that ordinary contact into an alleged sexual relationship even after they reduced contact.

When Xiao Baicai's husband died, Yang was accused of supplying poison despite the absence of reliable purchase or transfer evidence. The magistrate allegedly tortured him before the legally required removal of his degree status, and successive reviews preserved the conviction. His family, especially his sister's Beijing petitioning, eventually obtained outside support and a retrial. Acquittal spared his life but did not restore his degree; the episode says torture left him disabled and that he later supported himself through sericulture.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Qing juren whose legal petition work contributed to conflict with a local magistrate.
- Neighbor and literacy instructor whose contact with Xiao Baicai was transformed into sexual rumor.
- Wrongfully accused of supplying poison without reliable procurement or transfer evidence.
- Tortured defendant whose procedural status did not protect him from coercive investigation.
- Beneficiary of sustained family petitioning, elite assistance, press attention, and capital review.
- Formally cleared of murder but not fully restored in rank, health, livelihood, or reputation.
