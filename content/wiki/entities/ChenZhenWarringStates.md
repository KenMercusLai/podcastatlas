# 陈枕 / Chen Zhen (Warring States strategist)

陈枕 / Chen Zhen appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-39-lishishang-zhangyi-shi-zenyang-nudui-qiangdi-de-lv2gc2wtcxuhugfgixk7rbbhpa6x]] as a capable Warring States strategist who arrives in [[WeiState|魏国]] after [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] has already defeated [[HuiShi|惠施 / 惠师]] inside the Wei court. The page keeps the source spelling 陈枕 and uses a source-scoped filename to avoid colliding with other Chen pages.

The episode treats Chen Zhen as someone whose power-political skill is comparable to Zhang Yi and [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]]. That is why Zhang Yi acts first: before Chen Zhen can settle into Wei, Zhang Yi tells [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王 / 梁惠王]] that Chen Zhen's loyalties point toward [[ChuState|楚国]] and that his advice would serve Chu interests.

Chen Zhen's recovery depends on [[ZuoShuang|左爽]]'s advice. Instead of fighting Zhang Yi for a Wei position under a hostile frame, he lets Zhang Yi's private words reach Chu and restores his standing with [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]. In the source, this makes him the main example of [[SituationalPoliticalPivot|因事立道式政治转身]]: a blocked career path can become usable if the actor changes the target court and turns the accusation into a credential elsewhere.
