# 陈寔 / Chen Shi (late Han)

## Overview

Chen Shi is a late-Han moral authority and former Taiqiu magistrate whose wiki profile now combines local moral reputation with practical survival in the party-prohibition crisis.

## Current Profile

Chen Shi's reputation works on two registers. In the later [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-938-lishi-de-jingxing-ruanruo-zhihui-dailai-quru-lnlnrketgfbadhfjbzdjjtrmqfsh|Hanji 938]] layer, he appears as a trusted local judge whose death draws mass mourning and whose "梁上君子" story turns shame into reform. In the earlier [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-896-jiaru-luanshi-jianglin-ni-gai-ruhe-zibao-lsuvt-ksp-gfggc9riqqovjswkg|Hanji 896]] layer, his attendance at Zhang Rang's father's funeral, against the social avoidance of other worthies, later becomes protection for himself and other innocents when party-prohibition pursuit intensifies.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses moral instruction and material help to repair local order rather than relying on fear.
- Carries reputation that makes others trust his dispute judgments and mourn him at scale.
- Shows that courtesy across factional contempt can create later protection without endorsing the powerholder.
- Bridges Confucian moral authority and disorder-era self-protection.

## Evidence

- Moral repair and local trust: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-938-lishi-de-jingxing-ruanruo-zhihui-dailai-quru-lnlnrketgfbadhfjbzdjjtrmqfsh|Hanji 938]] records the "梁上君子" story, fair dispute resolution, and public mourning.
- Courtesy as protection: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-896-jiaru-luanshi-jianglin-ni-gai-ruhe-zibao-lsuvt-ksp-gfggc9riqqovjswkg|Hanji 896]] says Chen Shi attended Zhang Rang's father's funeral when other worthies stayed away, and Zhang later protected him and many connected innocents.
- Moral authority under coercive disorder: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-938-lishi-de-jingxing-ruanruo-zhihui-dailai-quru-lnlnrketgfbadhfjbzdjjtrmqfsh|Hanji 938]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-896-jiaru-luanshi-jianglin-ni-gai-ruhe-zibao-lsuvt-ksp-gfggc9riqqovjswkg|Hanji 896]] together place his reputation against rebellions, eunuch power, and party-prohibition danger.

## Qualifications

Chen Shi's attendance at Zhang Rang's family funeral should not be treated as approval of eunuch politics. The source presents it as a socially risky act of courtesy whose later consequences were protective. The page remains limited to the episodes summarized in the named source notes rather than a complete biography.
