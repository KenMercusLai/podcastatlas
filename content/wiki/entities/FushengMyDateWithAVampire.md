## Overview

复生 is a child transformed into a vampire in the 1938 opening of [[MyDateWithAVampire|《我和僵尸有个约会》]], leaving him with decades of experience inside an unchanging school-age body.

## Current Profile

Shot during the 红溪村 violence and then bitten by Jiangchen, Fusheng survives into 1998 while publicly posing as [[KuangTianyou|况天佑]]'s son. He and Kuang avoid killing and rely on blood-bank supplies or substitutes, but ethical feeding does not make their condition ordinary: exposure repeatedly forces them to move, and Fusheng must return to primary school rather than progress through a visible adult life.

His birthday wish gives the episode its sharpest account of immortality's cost. He wants to grow, marry, have children, age, die, and be buried like other people. The hosts nevertheless qualify the characterization: his dialogue and conduct often seem younger than a person with roughly sixty years of accumulated experience, leaving the premise more fully developed than the psychology.

## Key Characteristics

- Possesses an aging consciousness in a permanently childlike body.
- Avoids killing and tries to live on nonviolent blood supplies.
- Protects others even when using his abilities risks exposure.
- Experiences school repetition, relocation, and blocked adulthood as costs of immortality.
- Desires a complete finite life rather than indefinite biological survival.
- Exposes a gap between the character's stated age and his sometimes childlike writing.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one podcast retelling of the series opening. The exact age, abilities, later development, and continuity are story-world matters, while the mismatch between chronological age and characterization is the hosts' critical judgment.

## What Changed

- Established Fusheng as the series' clearest embodiment of immortality as arrested development rather than fulfilled longevity.
