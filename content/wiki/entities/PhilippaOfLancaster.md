# Philippa of Lancaster

## Overview

Philippa of Lancaster was John of Gaunt's daughter and the English queen consort whose marriage to [[JohnIOfPortugal|João I of Portugal]] gave the [[AngloPortugueseAlliance]] a dynastic foundation.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Philippa as an educated Lancastrian princess whose marriage linked English diplomacy to the new Aviz monarchy. Her nine children, remembered as the “illustrious generation,” connected the alliance to Portugal's next turn, especially through [[HenryTheNavigator|Henry the Navigator]].

Philippa therefore appears less as a private marital figure than as a bridge among learning, dynasty, alliance, and overseas ambition. The source's deathbed north-wind story reinforces that memory but remains anecdotal.

## Key Characteristics

- Daughter of John of Gaunt and member of the English Lancastrian dynasty.
- Dynastic link between England and João I's Portugal.
- Educated royal figure associated in the episode with Chaucer, Froissart, and Wycliffe.
- Mother of the “illustrious generation,” including Henry the Navigator.

## Evidence

- Alliance evidence: [[227-portugal-on-the-edge-of-the-world-part-1-glt1834337589]] places Philippa's marriage after the Treaty of Windsor as a dynastic sealing of the alliance.
- Education evidence: [[227-portugal-on-the-edge-of-the-world-part-1-glt1834337589]] describes major English intellectual figures as part of her educational world.
- Dynastic evidence: [[227-portugal-on-the-edge-of-the-world-part-1-glt1834337589]] links her children to the 1415 Ceuta expedition and Portugal's outward turn.

## Qualifications

The episode supplies a compressed, admiring portrait rather than a full political biography. Its claims about her teachers and the north-wind deathbed story remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Created Philippa as the dynastic and educational bridge in the Anglo-Portuguese alliance.

## Relationships

- [[AngloPortugueseAlliance]] - interstate relationship her marriage reinforced.
- [[HenryTheNavigator]] - son whose career connects the dynasty to expansion.
- [[Portugal]] - kingdom whose royal and diplomatic history her marriage shaped.
