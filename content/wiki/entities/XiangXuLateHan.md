# 项诩 / Xiang Xu (late Han)

项诩 / Xiang Xu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-924-1-de-cuowu-100-de-shibai-lmeo7vufm6ib5lni6p49rponazut|Hanji 924]] as an attendant official from Henei who memorializes against the unlawful conduct of [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]'s eunuch favorites during the [[YellowTurbansLateHan|Yellow Turban]] crisis.

The source shows his warning being recoded into the most convenient emergency accusation. [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]] frames Xiang Xu as connected to [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]], and Xiang Xu is imprisoned in the Huangmen northern prison. The case sharpens [[PalaceAccessMonopoly|宫廷接触垄断]] because the palace side does not need to answer the substance of the memorial once it can label the speaker a rebel insider.
