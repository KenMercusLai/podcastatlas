# 左昌 / Zuo Chang (late Han)

左昌 / Zuo Chang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] as a Liangzhou inspector who steals a large amount of military grain. [[GaiXunLateHan|盖勋]] discovers the theft and warns him, which turns Zuo Chang's corruption into a personal resentment against Gai.

The episode then shows Zuo Chang trying to convert office power into revenge. He sends Gai Xun, [[XinZengLateHan|辛曾]], and Kong Chang to defend Ahyang against rebels, apparently hoping battlefield failure will give him a reason to punish Gai; instead Gai wins repeated victories.

When [[ChenYiJinchengLateHan|金城太守陈懿]] is killed and rebels besiege Zuo Chang at Ji County, Zuo asks Gai Xun for help. Gai still argues for rescue, making Zuo Chang the negative counterpart in [[PublicDutyOverPrivateGrievance|对事不对人式公义]]: private resentment drives Zuo's earlier choices, while Gai refuses to answer private injury with dereliction of duty.
