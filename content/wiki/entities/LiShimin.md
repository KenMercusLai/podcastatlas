# 李世民 / Li Shimin

李世民 / Li Shimin appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-2-pandian-huangdimen-jiuhou-xiaopihao-livpg3zh7lsqffgca2pomiqnlvyd|Qinji 131-2]] as one of the actors who pushes [[LiYuanTang|李渊]] toward rebellion against [[YangGuangSui|杨广]]. The episode presents him beside [[PeiJi|裴寂]] rather than as the later emperor in his own right.

His source role is to make the drinking story strategic. Li Yuan's drunken exposure is not enough by itself; Li Shimin and Pei Ji interpret it as an irreversible danger and convert that danger into a founding-rebellion argument.
