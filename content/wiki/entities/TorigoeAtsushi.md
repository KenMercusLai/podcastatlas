# Torigoe Atsushi

Torigoe Atsushi appears in [[elbows-up-again-a-us-canada-trade-flare-up-6a8c0fa3a89d26398ee7a5db]] as [[MoekaIida]]'s concrete example of [[Japan]]'s [[EmploymentIceAgeGeneration|employment ice age generation]]. The source describes him as a 50-year-old Tokyo worker whose career was shaped by graduating into weak post-bubble job conditions and spending much of his work life in stock-room roles at a luxury fashion firm.

His case gives [[EntryCohortLaborMarketScarring]] a personal form. The episode says his income barely increased over the years, fell after bonus and payment-scheme changes, and felt especially unfair when younger recruits were later advertised at higher base salaries. The source connects that pattern to long-term earnings, pension, and housing risk rather than to individual motivation alone.
