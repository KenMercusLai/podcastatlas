# 艾库曼 / Ekumen

艾库曼 / Ekumen is the interstellar alliance or wider human community represented by [[GenlyAi|金利·艾 / Genly Ai]] in [[TheLeftHandOfDarkness|《黑暗的左手》]]. In [[36-heian-de-zuoshou-liufang-yu-ai-de-weilai-wangshi-605254522]], the source stresses that the Ekumen sends one envoy rather than a fleet because it trusts recognition, truthfulness, and patient contact more than coercive first-contact power.

The Ekumen matters as a political imagination. It gives [[Estraven|伊斯特拉凡 / Estraven]] a way to think beyond the [[Karhide|卡海德 / Karhide]] versus [[Orgoreyn|欧格瑞恩 / Orgoreyn]] border conflict, and it makes loyalty to "all humanity" possible even when local institutions still call that loyalty treason.
