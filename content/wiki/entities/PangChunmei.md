# 庞春梅 / Pang Chunmei

庞春梅 is one of the three title-name figures of [[JinPingMei|《金瓶梅》]]. [[fanwai-15-banlatte-jiang-jinpingmei-la-erqie-haishi-luse-de-991508433]] introduces her chiefly through title interpretation, explaining that "金瓶梅" can refer directly to [[PanJinlian|潘金莲]], [[LiPinger|李瓶儿]], and Pang Chunmei while also carrying symbolic readings around desire and wealth.

The source keeps her as a future structural node rather than a fully narrated character. That matters because the episode presents the novel as a long [[HouseholdAsSocialWeb]] story, where later women, servants, and domestic hierarchies will matter as much as the first [[WaterMargin|《水浒传》]]-derived branch.
