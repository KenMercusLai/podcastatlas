# Xining Wildlife Park / 西宁野生动物园

Xining Wildlife Park / 西宁野生动物园 enters the wiki through [[163-jiao-yi-zhi-sheli-wangji-dongwuyuan-xiye-de-gushi-bi-fengkuang-dongwucheng-2-geng-jingcai-939887179]], where [[YuanZhang|袁掌]] discusses its wildlife rescue, rehabilitation, and public-science work around [[TianxianBaobaoLynx|天线宝宝]]. The episode presents the institution less as a display venue than as a place where care, quarantine-like distance, release preparation, public education, and public attention are all entangled.

The source gives the zoo a paradoxical role. It saved and treated a wild [[Lynx|lynx]], but it also had to become something the animal should not treat as home. Later, Yuan Zhang reflects that 天线宝宝's chosen wild slope, with village activity and road sounds nearby, may have resembled the zoo's own sound environment more than the team expected.

## Key Claims
- The zoo's rescue work includes medical treatment, feeding, behavioral observation, release preparation, and post-release interpretation.
- When the goal is release, good zoo care may mean blocking visitor exposure and reducing comfortable human contact.
- The institution's public role is double-edged: attention can make local wildlife widely known, but it can also bring complaint, fan-style pressure, and operational disruption.
- The source frames Xining's animal-protection identity as a long-term public project, not only as an individual rescue case.
