# CPE 源峰

CPE 源峰 appears in [[mai-xiangzao-qijia-de-bainian-huawang-weishenme-chengle-bandaoti-zhizao-de-guanjiang-qiye-1004213131]] as the Chinese private-equity investor that announced the acquisition of [[Mammut|猛犸象]] on July 30, 2026. The source says financial details were not disclosed, while earlier market discussion placed the possible valuation around 4 billion yuan.

In the episode, CPE's challenge is not framed as financial engineering alone. It inherits an outdoor brand with professional credibility but also questions around China-market channel control, direct-store share, brand asset reinforcement, and positioning against higher-priced global competitors.

[[145-gaijia-zhongzi-de-canyin-yangpinpai-feng-tou-quan-1-145-1]] adds a restaurant-chain control case. The episode says CPE Yuanfeng signed a February 2026 strategic cooperation with [[BurgerKing]], injecting 350 million USD for 83% of Burger King China while the global brand retained 17%. This extends CPE's page from outdoor-brand acquisition into [[ForeignRestaurantBrandLocalControl]].
