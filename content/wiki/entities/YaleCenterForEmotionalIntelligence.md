# Yale Center for Emotional Intelligence

## Overview
The Yale Center for Emotional Intelligence is the institutional context named in two Huberman Lab source notes for [[MarcBrackett]]'s emotional-intelligence work.

## Current Profile
Within these wiki sources, the center matters because Brackett's claims are not presented as only personal advice. The episodes associate his work with a structured emotional-intelligence program, school implementation, shared emotion language, emotional check-ins, and the claim that emotion skills can be taught and measured.

The sources do not provide a detailed institutional history, governance structure, or independent program evaluation. Its wiki role is therefore narrow: it anchors Brackett's school-facing emotional-intelligence framework and connects [[RULERFramework]], [[MoodMeterEmotionalCheckIn]], and [[BullyingPreventionThroughEmotionalSkills]] to system-level education practice.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as the institutional home named for Brackett's emotional-intelligence work.
- Connects emotion regulation to school culture and shared language rather than only individual coping.
- Supports the source's claim that emotional intelligence can be defined, taught, and measured.
- Anchors the episode's discussion of leaders, teachers, students, and parents using a common emotional vocabulary.
- Connects emotional check-ins and bullying prevention to adult responsibility and school climate.

## Evidence
- Institutional identification - [[how-to-better-regulate-your-emotions-dr-marc-brackett-scim6338474733]] identifies Brackett as director of the Yale Center for Emotional Intelligence.
- School-system frame - [[how-to-better-regulate-your-emotions-dr-marc-brackett-scim6338474733]] says emotional-intelligence work in schools must involve leaders, teachers, students, and parents.
- Shared-language role - [[how-to-better-regulate-your-emotions-dr-marc-brackett-scim6338474733]] argues that emotion work requires a shared vocabulary and scientific attitude toward emotional life.
- School-climate extension - [[how-to-increase-your-emotional-intelligence-dr-marc-brackett-scim6059113217]] connects emotion check-ins, task-supporting states, empathy, perspective-taking, role play, and regulation to classroom and bullying-prevention practice.
