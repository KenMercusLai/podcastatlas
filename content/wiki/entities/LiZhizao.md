# 李之藻 / Li Zhizao

Li Zhizao appears in [[ep259-yu-miao-shifu-mantan-xu-guangqi-sibainian-qian-ruhe-rangren-biancongming-lkirczxnx8skbbfpmz5-m-mivs4e]] as one of the late-Ming Chinese literati who took Western learning seriously rather than treating it only as novelty or foreign technique. [[MiaoWeiSanlian|苗伟]] groups him with [[XuGuangqi|徐光启]] as someone whose first move was to make himself more capable of understanding.

The episode's use of Li is mostly conceptual. He helps make [[LateMingWesternLearning|晚明西学东渐]] a story of active learning rather than passive import. That matters because the source's core claim is not simply that Jesuits brought knowledge, but that Chinese scholars had to decide whether to learn the underlying reasoning disciplines.

Li also connects to [[TongwenSuanzhi|《同文算指》]], the arithmetic text discussed near the end of the episode as an example of practical mathematical education that might have changed learning efficiency if institutionalized.
