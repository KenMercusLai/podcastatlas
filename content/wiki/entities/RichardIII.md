# Richard III / 理查三世

## Overview
Richard III / 理查三世 is the English king whose inherited villain image lets the wiki track how evidence, literature, dynastic winners, and public memory compete.

## Current Profile
The first bounded source uses Richard III through [[TheDaughterOfTime|《时间的女儿》]] and historical-detective reasoning: accusations around usurpation, deformity, cruelty, and the [[PrincesInTheTower|Princes in the Tower]] become less stable when evidence is separated from later hostile narratives. The Shakespeare source adds the political-theatre mechanism behind that memory. [[RichardIIIPlay|Shakespeare's Richard III]] is treated as a Tudor-facing drama that darkens Richard so Henry VII and the Tudor settlement look providential, peaceful, and legitimate.

## Key Characteristics
- His public image is a prime wiki case of literary and dynastic memory overpowering uncertain evidence.
- Responsible revision of his reputation depends on source dating, political incentive, material evidence, and rival explanations.
- Shakespeare's play is treated as a major transmitter of the hostile Richard image, not as neutral biography.
- The 2012 Leicester remains discovery complicates caricature by adding material evidence, including wounds and spinal curvature.
