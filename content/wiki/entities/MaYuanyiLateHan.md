# 马元义 / Ma Yuanyi (late Han)

马元义 / Ma Yuanyi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-923-chengnianrende-dingji-henjin-guzi-lide-juejue-lifzh-gohpqnmdznnfv72tndqf8h|Hanji 923]] as a [[TaipingDao|太平道]] `大方` figure in [[ZhangJiaoLateHan|张角]]'s pre-184 uprising plan. The episode says he gathers believers from [[Jingzhou|荆州]] and [[YangzhouLateHan|扬州]], coordinates around [[YeLateHan|邺城]], and repeatedly enters [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] to connect with palace contacts including [[FengXuLateHan|封谞]] and [[XuFengLateHan|徐奉]].

His role matters because the episode uses him to show that the [[YellowTurbansLateHan|Yellow Turban]] outbreak was not only desperation turning into disorder. By the time [[TangZhouLateHan|唐周]] informs on the plot, the movement already has regional believers, a date, inner-court links, and enough organization that arresting Ma Yuanyi forces Zhang Jiao to accelerate rather than prevents mobilization.
