# Ross Brawn

Ross Brawn appears in [[ferrari-1]] as part of the Ferrari Formula One rebuild under [[LucaDiMontezemolo]]. The source names him with [[JeanTodt]] and [[MichaelSchumacher]] in the 2000-2004 championship run.

His role links Ferrari's luxury value back to technical and operational competence. In the source, [[Ferrari]]'s brand does not float free of racing; it is refreshed by periods when [[ScuderiaFerrari]] wins through [[EngineeringCompetition]].
