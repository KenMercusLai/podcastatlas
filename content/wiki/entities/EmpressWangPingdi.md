## Overview

王皇后（汉平帝皇后） / Empress Wang of Han Pingdi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-666-xihan-zuihou-yiwei-huanghou-weihe-touhuo-zifen-lrjurgjzvwezkppvgeupu6ormmzw|Hanji 666]] as [[WangMang|王莽]]'s daughter and the former empress of [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]]. The episode centers her last act during the burning of [[WeiyangPalace|未央宫]]: she does not flee, says she has no face to see the Han house, and enters the fire as the [[XinDynasty|新朝]] collapses.

## Current Profile

The current evidence is narrow but important. Hanji 666 presents her as a woman whose political identity has become unlivable at the end of Wang Mang's regime. Her father is the Xin usurper whose forces are losing Chang'an, while her former title binds her to the Western Han house that anti-Xin troops claim to restore.

The episode does not give an independent biography, court role, or policy position. Its focus is the final palace-fire scene. The host reads her self-immolation through shame, widowhood, family destruction, and the impossibility of facing the restored Han order after having been Wang Mang's daughter and Han Pingdi's empress.
