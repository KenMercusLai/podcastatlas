## Overview

王皇后（汉平帝皇后） / Empress Wang of Han Pingdi is [[WangMang|王莽]]'s daughter and the former empress of [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]], now synthesized as a palace figure whose Han marriage identity resists Xin recoding before ending in self-immolation. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-640-qipa-wangmang-ruhe-chedi-bifan-xiongnu-lhxbx6keibmfe8z6bcsmlpnr6eht|Hanji 640]] presents her as Ding'an Taihou / Huanghuang Shizhu, refusing Wang Mang's remarriage plan. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-666-xihan-zuihou-yiwei-huanghou-weihe-touhuo-zifen-lrjurgjzvwezkppvgeupu6ormmzw|Hanji 666]] later centers her last act during the burning of [[WeiyangPalace|未央宫]].

## Current Profile

The current evidence is narrow but now covers both coercive recoding and terminal collapse. Hanji 640 says that after Western Han has fallen, she often claims illness and avoids court attendance. Wang Mang wants to use remarriage to link her to [[SunJianXinGeneral|孙建]]'s family, but she refuses to become a political instrument and does not want to betray the Liu house through her father's arrangement.

Hanji 640 also shows Wang Mang changing her title from Ding'an Taihou to Huanghuang Shizhu, then sending Sun Jian's son in a staged visit with doctors. Her anger and illness make refusal a bodily and palace-bound form of opposition. Hanji 666 presents the endpoint: as Xin collapses and Weiyang Palace burns, she says she has no face to see the Han house and enters the fire.

The page therefore reads her not as a policy actor but as a dynastic-identity rupture case. Her father uses Xin authority to rename, remarry, and position her, while her former Han empress status makes full assimilation impossible.

## Key Characteristics

- Wang-family daughter whose status was tied directly to Wang Mang's political project.
- Former Western Han empress whose identity remains bound to the Han house after Wang Mang's usurpation.
- Refusal figure who resists Wang Mang's attempt to remarry her as a political instrument.
- Title-recoded palace woman moved from Ding'an Taihou to Huanghuang Shizhu.
- Palace woman whose end is narrated inside Weiyang Palace's burning rather than battlefield combat.
- Figure of dynastic identity rupture: family loyalty, marriage status, and restored-Han legitimacy become mutually unbearable.

## Qualifications

This page should not be expanded into a full biography without more source notes. Hanji 640 supports the Ding'an Taihou/Huanghuang Shizhu title branch and refusal of Wang Mang's remarriage plan, but her exact legal title mechanics, daily court position, and inner motives beyond the host's interpretation remain source-scoped. Hanji 666 supports her identity as Wang Mang's daughter and Han Pingdi's former empress, her residence inside the palace-fire field, and the episode's shame-centered interpretation of her death.
