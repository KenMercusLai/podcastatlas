# Michael Li (Texas Political Demographer)

## Overview

Michael Li is the political-demographics expert who maps a hypothetical five-state division of [[Texas]] in [[divide-and-conquer-divide-and-conquer|Divide and Conquer]].

## Current Profile

Li proposes regions centered on Dallas, Houston, Austin, West Texas, and the San Antonio-to-El Paso corridor. His analysis turns the legal possibility of subdivision into a time-sensitive electoral exercise: a plan once imagined as a Republican advantage could, after demographic change, create safely Democratic, safely Republican, and competitive Senate seats.

## Key Characteristics

- Translates a constitutional possibility into a regional political map.
- Centers the five proposed regions on major Texas population and geographic areas.
- Treats demographic change as capable of reversing the proposal's expected partisan benefit.
- Separates safe seats from competitive outcomes rather than assigning one statewide lean to every successor state.

## Qualifications

The supplied summary does not include boundary files, data, year-specific vote estimates, uncertainty intervals, or alternative maps. The reported allocation is therefore an episode-scoped illustration, not a current electoral forecast or proof of how subdivision would affect party control.
