# Rodo Sayagues

Rodo Sayagues appears in [[the-real-horror-of-alien-and-how-it-explains-why-were-not-paid-enough]] as [[FedeAlvarez]]'s co-writer on [[AlienRomulus|Alien: Romulus]] and on a future sequel mentioned in the episode. His wiki role is source-scoped: the episode names him as part of the creative team behind the franchise's renewed labor themes.

Through the source, Sayagues connects to [[AlienFranchise]], [[WeylandYutani]], and [[CompanyTownLaborControl]], but the episode does not give him a separate extended argument apart from Alvarez's interview.

- [[FedeAlvarez]] - co-writer and interview subject in the source.
