## Overview

陈崇 / Chen Chong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-642-kao-xinji-shangwei-de-ren-zui-taoyan-shenme-lnc6e3dgxxnrylkfqzgl2ro9gpbj|Hanji 642]] as the Xin official who warns [[WangMang|王莽]] that uncontrolled fuming reports will corrupt office incentives and confuse mandate language.

## Current Profile

Chen Chong's current profile is narrow but structurally important. In the episode, he identifies a backfire in Wang Mang's legitimacy machinery: a device that helped Wang Mang gain the throne now gives opportunists a way to seek rank and favor. His memorial supplies the explicit rationale for Wang Mang's later restriction of fuming claims.

The page therefore treats Chen Chong as a diagnostic official inside early [[XinDynasty|新朝]] sign politics, not as an independently reconstructed biographical figure.

## Key Characteristics

- Xin official associated with fuming regulation.
- Diagnoses uncontrolled mandate-sign claims as a source of opportunism.
- Supplies the court rationale for suppressing unauthorized fuming reports.
- Remains source-bounded until further episodes add office history or later conduct.
