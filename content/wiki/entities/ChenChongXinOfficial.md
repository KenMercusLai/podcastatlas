## Overview

陈崇 / Chen Chong is synthesized as a Wang Mang-era official whose source-bounded profile now has two sides: in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-611-lishi-wuming-de-sunbao-you-duo-yemen-lhlc5v3ndnivv9j_cb9w65v4xutw|Hanji 611]], he impeaches [[SunBaoWesternHan|孙宝]] after Sun Bao publicly resists yellow-dragon praise of [[WangMang|王莽]]; in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-642-kao-xinji-shangwei-de-ren-zui-taoyan-shenme-lnc6e3dgxxnrylkfqzgl2ro9gpbj|Hanji 642]], he warns Wang Mang that uncontrolled fuming reports will corrupt office incentives and confuse mandate language.

## Current Profile

The page no longer treats Chen Chong only as a narrow Xin-era diagnostic official. Hanji 611 places him earlier, in the late-Western-Han court under [[HanPingdi|汉平帝]], as the official who turns Sun Bao's family circumstance into an impeachment after Sun Bao exposes the gap between court praise and commoner distress. The episode presents the charge as filial-duty failure: Sun Bao's mother becomes ill on the route to Chang'an and stays at Sun Bao's younger brother's home while Sun Bao's wife and children reach the capital. Sun Bao reads the political context as unwinnable, admits decline in filial support, and is dismissed.

Hanji 642 later gives Chen Chong a different function inside [[XinDynasty|新朝]] sign politics. He identifies a backfire in Wang Mang's legitimacy machinery: fuming claims helped Wang Mang gain the throne, but once rewards attach to such reports, opportunists can fabricate signs to seek rank and favor. His memorial supplies the explicit rationale for Wang Mang's restriction of unauthorized fuming.

The combined profile is therefore not morally simple. Chen Chong can appear as a procedural instrument against a direct-speaking official in Hanji 611 and as a rational diagnostician of sign-politics disorder in Hanji 642. Both roles fit a court where legitimacy language and bureaucratic accusation can shape survival.

## Key Characteristics

- Wang Mang-era official whose profile spans late Western Han and early Xin source notes.
- Impeaches Sun Bao after Sun Bao publicly rejects yellow-dragon praise of Wang Mang.
- Uses filial-duty procedure in a politically charged setting.
- Diagnoses uncontrolled mandate-sign claims as a source of opportunism.
- Supplies the court rationale for suppressing unauthorized fuming reports.
- Remains source-bounded outside these two focused episodes.

## Qualifications

The sources do not prove Chen Chong's private motive in Sun Bao's removal, reconstruct his full office history, or settle whether the Hanji 611 impeachment was centrally directed by Wang Mang. Hanji 611 supports the politically charged sequence and the host's retaliation reading, while the formal charge remains filial support. Hanji 642 supports his fuming diagnosis and its policy effect but does not establish his broader factional position or later career.
