# Riverside Natural Foods

Riverside Natural Foods is the operating company behind [[MadeGood]] in [[madegood-salma-and-nima-fotovat-lost-their-first-business-they-grew-their-next-one-into-a-snack-giant-cf5715bf-e9e0-45e0-a8a9-1256a7a4ed0d]]. The episode presents Riverside as the family's post-[[TasteOfNature]] reset: a new food-manufacturing company built around owned production, allergen-free credibility, retailer responsiveness, and a portfolio that later included [[GoodToGo]] and [[CookiePal]].

## Key Points
- Riverside was financed through family retirement funds, bank debt, home-secured loans, and a pre-revenue bank loan supported by the family's prior track record.
- The company built a 20,000-square-foot factory because the family believed manufacturing control was central to cost, quality, and allergen-free trust.
- Its first order was a large contract-manufacturing run for a Costco supplier, which tested hiring, training, process setup, certifications, and date-code correction under pressure.
- MadeGood became the core brand, while Good To Go became a discontinued focus lesson and Cookie Pal became a more separated adjacent brand.
