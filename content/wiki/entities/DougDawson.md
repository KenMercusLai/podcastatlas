# Doug Dawson

[[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]] adds Dawson in a second marriage-data segment. The local source text spells the name "Doug Dowson", but this page keeps the existing wiki spelling for continuity. In the episode, he uses [[TaylorSwift]] and [[TravisKelce]] as a celebrity hook for [[FemaleBreadwinnerNorms]], then contrasts egalitarian survey attitudes with [[ReportedIncomeGenderNorms]] and [[DomesticLaborDoubleBurden]] in American couples.

Doug Dawson appears in [[hard-cedar-lebanon-seeks-trumps-help-6a5f41e97bddc0bea5542de5]] as the analyst explaining census-based evidence on age gaps in U.S. heterosexual marriages. The episode says he expected some popular patterns, including "trophy husbands," to show up more strongly than they did.

His key contribution is [[AgeGapMarriageDecline]]. Dawson says that in 1980 top-earning men were more likely than typical earners to marry women at least a decade younger, but by 2024 the gap had closed. He offers higher education and age-peer socialization as one possible explanation.
