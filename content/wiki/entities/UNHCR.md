# UNHCR / 联合国难民署

## Overview
UNHCR / 联合国难民署 appears in [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] as the [[UnitedNations]] refugee agency whose registration work helps determine when newly arrived families in [[WedweilRefugeeCamp|维德维尔难民营]] can begin receiving [[WorldFoodProgramme|WFP]] support.

## Current Profile
The episode presents UNHCR less as a standalone institution than as a key gate in the refugee-aid sequence. Families arriving from [[Sudan]] must have nationality and identity checked before they become eligible for organized assistance.

That procedure is necessary for accountable aid delivery, but the source makes its humanitarian cost visible: even a one-day registration process can matter when children arrive hungry and families have no immediate food, shelter, or cash reserve.

## Key Characteristics
- UN refugee agency represented through registration and identity verification.
- Operational partner to WFP in the South Sudan camp setting.
- Gatekeeping actor whose verification process precedes emergency support.
- Institution caught between accountability requirements and urgent hunger.

## Evidence
- Registration role: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] says UNHCR must verify identity and confirm Sudanese nationality before newly arrived refugees are registered.
- Aid sequencing: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] says WFP cash and biscuit support follows registration rather than arriving instantly at camp entry.
- Field constraint: [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] shows a family waiting under a tree before registration while a child eats salt for temporary relief.

## Qualifications
The source gives only a narrow view of UNHCR's registration function at one camp. It does not evaluate UNHCR's broader protection mandate, resettlement work, or camp-management responsibilities.
