# Ed Sabol

Ed Sabol appears in [[the-nfl-1]] as the founder figure behind [[NFLFilms]]. The episode says he won the right to film the 1962 NFL Championship and created a cinematic sports film that later became part of the league's storytelling infrastructure.

In the wiki, Sabol connects the [[NationalFootballLeague|NFL]]'s media strategy to production craft. [[PeteRozelle]] could centralize rights and league narratives, but NFL Films made the sport emotionally legible through slow motion, music, narration, and archival footage.
