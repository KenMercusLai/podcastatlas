# 尸佼 / Shi Jiao

尸佼 / Shi Jiao appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-25-zhaoliang-dui-shangyang-shuo-ni-bupei-lnqmwjbrc9qomwrdt5brourf-78p]] as an important adviser associated with [[ShangYang|公孙鞅 / 商鞅]]. The episode mentions him at the end rather than making him the main subject: after Shang Yang's death, Shi Jiao withdraws toward the [[Chengdu|成都]] area and writes Shizi.

The source uses him as a bridge out of the Shang Yang death sequence. His retreat marks the intellectual afterlife of the reform circle after [[QinState|秦国]] turns against its chief reformer, suggesting that the political failure of one minister does not end the circulation of his associated ideas.
