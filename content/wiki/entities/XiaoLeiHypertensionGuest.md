# 小磊 (hypertension guest)

## Overview
小磊 is the source-scoped young patient guest whose incidental hypertension diagnosis anchors a [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] discussion of chronic-disease management.

## Current Profile
The episode says 小磊 learned of elevated blood pressure during a household-registration medical examination, was initially considered for white-coat hypertension, and was later diagnosed after 24-hour monitoring. He reports no obvious symptoms, a paternal family history, daily medication with occasional weekend uncertainty, inconsistent home measurement, and limited follow-up. During the conversation, he reinterprets bedtime project planning and persistent mental activation as possible normalized stress rather than proof that he is pressure-free.

## Key Characteristics
- Grounds the episode in an asymptomatic younger-adult diagnosis found through screening rather than symptom-driven care.
- Illustrates the gap between taking medicine and verifying whether treatment is controlling blood pressure.
- Makes missed-dose, weekend schedule, side-effect, monitoring, and follow-up questions concrete.
- Revises his own interpretation of work stress during the discussion.
