# What's Going Right?

## Overview
What's Going Right? is [[PaulConti]]'s mental-health book used as the organizing object in the Huberman Lab interview on agency, confidence, self-understanding, and trauma.

## Current Profile
The source presents the book as a strength-first method for optimizing mental health. Its title becomes the episode's governing question: start by seeing what is already functioning, then examine self-talk, repeated choices, old patterns, trauma, relationships, and action from a position of compassionate curiosity rather than defect-hunting.

The book's episode role is practical rather than bibliographic. It supplies a frame for linking self-structure, self-function, observing self, intentional action, positive-memory cues, and real happiness as peace, contentment, and delight that can coexist with suffering.

## Key Characteristics
- Gives the episode its strength-first mental-health starting point.
- Treats self-examination as more truthful when it includes resilience and functioning, not only symptoms.
- Connects mental health to repeated questions, action steps, and ongoing process.
- Frames the mind-body parallel: mental health, like physical health, requires structure and repeated practice.
- Supports agency without denying trauma, stress, anxiety, or painful childhood patterns.
