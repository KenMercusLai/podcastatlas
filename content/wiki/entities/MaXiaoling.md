## Overview

马小玲 is the exorcist who coordinates the response to living corpses, mass haunting, and [[APingMyDateWithAVampire|阿平]]'s returning ghost in the bounded 佳佳大厦 arc of [[MyDateWithAVampire|《我和僵尸有个约会》]].

## Current Profile

Ma Xiaoling combines specialist knowledge with improvised group coordination. She diagnoses [[PingMaMyDateWithAVampire|平妈]] as a corpse animated by a trapped soul, fights the ghosts released during 三破日, prepares the reincarnation ritual, distributes protective candles, and uses jealousy to expose the possessed person.

Her work also carries the arc's distinctive urban-supernatural style: ritual equipment travels in a professional case, cinnabar resembles lipstick, glasses permit ghost vision, and occult action is treated like a hidden service occupation. The hosts admire that design while questioning why she conceals the profession from a close friend and how consistently the ritual rules work.

## Key Characteristics

- Serves as investigator, exorcist, ritual designer, and group coordinator.
- Prefers redemption or reincarnation when force alone would destroy a soul.
- Converts traditional ritual imagery into portable urban work practice.
- Uses tactical psychology as well as supernatural equipment.
- Operates within rules that the hosts sometimes find narratively effective but logically unstable.
