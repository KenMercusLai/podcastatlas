## Overview

马小玲 is the modern professional exorcist whose paid ghost work, family mission, friendships, and ritual coordination organize the opening and 佳佳大厦 arcs of [[MyDateWithAVampire|《我和僵尸有个约会》]].

## Current Profile

Ma Xiaoling descends from a family charged with eliminating Jiangchen, but the series presents that inheritance through contemporary work and style. She runs a “cleaning company” for supernatural problems, negotiates fees, travels to a Japanese hotel for a client, carries ritual equipment as professional gear, folds spell papers into lucky stars, and invokes a divine dragon with the nine-character formula. Short skirts, boots, commercial pragmatism, and friendship with 王珍珍 sharply distinguish her from a conventional male Daoist-master image.

Duty also limits intimacy. The rule that women of the Ma family lose power if they cry for a man makes romance appear incompatible with the family mission, while the identical tarot draw for Ma and 王珍珍 foreshadows conflict around [[KuangTianyou|况天佑]]. The hosts treat this as effective melodramatic pressure but also note its emotional paradox and obviousness as setup.

In the 佳佳大厦 continuation, she moves from solo specialist to group coordinator. She diagnoses [[PingMaMyDateWithAVampire|平妈]] as a corpse animated by a trapped soul, fights released ghosts, prepares reincarnation procedure, distributes protective candles, and uses jealousy to expose possession. Across both arcs she prefers containment or redemption when possible, although the hosts question secrecy, rule consistency, and some tactical choices.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines inherited exorcist duty with fee-based modern professional work.
- Uses fashion, commerce, travel, and portable ritual tools to modernize a traditional role.
- Serves as investigator, combatant, ritual designer, and group coordinator.
- Prefers containment, truth recovery, or reincarnation when destruction is not necessary.
- Uses tactical psychology as well as supernatural equipment.
- Faces a family rule that places romantic emotion in conflict with magical duty.
- Operates within rules the hosts sometimes find effective but logically unstable.

## Qualifications

This profile covers two consecutive arcs through podcast accounts rather than Ma Xiaoling's complete series history. The origin and religious status of the nine-character formula, the family's tear rule, divine-dragon mechanics, tarot symbolism, romantic compatibility, and tactical judgments remain story-world or host-interpreted material. Modern visual design should not be mistaken for a complete account of real religious practice.

## What Changed

- Added her professional origin, Japanese commission, family mission, fee logic, and visual break from the conventional exorcist image.
- Added the nine-character formula and lucky-star spells to her portable ritual practice.
- Added the tear prohibition as the mechanism joining romantic restraint to inherited duty.
- Connected the opening's solo specialist to the continuation's group coordinator and redemption-oriented tactician.
