# 《不可承受的生命之轻》 / The Unbearable Lightness of Being

[[TheUnbearableLightnessOfBeing|《不可承受的生命之轻》 / The Unbearable Lightness of Being]] appears in [[12-man-dang-milan-kundela-yi-ju-zhengjing-meiyou-549335675]] as one of [[MilanKundera|Kundera]]'s more familiar and politically weighted works. The episode invokes it to connect [[SlowKundera|《慢》 / Slowness]] back to Kundera's anti-kitsch vocabulary.

The key source-scoped contribution is [[KitschAsWasteDenial]]. The episode says Kundera's critique of kitsch involves refusing a clean, sentimental picture of existence that denies waste, excretion, and bodily awkwardness. That makes the coarse body comedy in [[SlowKundera|《慢》]] continuous with the better-known novel rather than a mere loss of seriousness.
