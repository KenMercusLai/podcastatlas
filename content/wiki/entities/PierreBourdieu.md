# Pierre Bourdieu / 皮埃尔·布迪厄

Pierre Bourdieu / 皮埃尔·布迪厄 is the sociologist used by [[162-caifu-de-benzhi-yiji-ziyou-de-zhenzheng-hanyi-chuantai-shifen-xiyin-lofyxfhiprud7zvekufexsjhtust]] to expand wealth beyond money and assets. The episode introduces [[BourdieuCapitalForms]] through economic, cultural, social, and symbolic capital, then uses the framework to explain why taste, credentials, networks, and reputation can function as convertible wealth.

In the source, Bourdieu is not treated as a standalone academic subject. His framework becomes a practical bridge from [[WealthAsCapabilityAndRights]] to [[CapitalConversionLoop]], showing why social recognition and symbolic legitimacy can either support real wealth or temporarily disguise the absence of it.
