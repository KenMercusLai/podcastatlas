# Nvidia Vera Rubin Platform

## Overview
Nvidia Vera Rubin is the post-Blackwell rack-scale platform in [[Nvidia]]'s roadmap. The sources use it to show that future AI capacity is delivered by coordinated CPUs, GPUs, networking, memory, switches, data-center systems, and software rather than by a single accelerator specification.

## Current Profile
The platform is framed as an inference- and agent-oriented AI supercomputer that also supports training. It sits inside Nvidia's $1 trillion cumulative Blackwell-and-Vera-Rubin order narrative and makes [[AIInfrastructureFullStackMoat]] visible. At the same time, rising rack density makes the roadmap dependent on packaging, memory, networking, power, cooling, construction, and customer deployment before projected orders can become useful token capacity.

## Key Characteristics
- Post-Blackwell rack-scale platform composed of multiple coordinated chip and networking types.
- Designed in the source accounts for training, production inference, and agentic workloads.
- Uses system integration, not an isolated GPU, as the relevant unit of performance and delivery.
- Raises power and cooling density, turning facility design into part of platform execution.
- Anchors Nvidia's forward order narrative without proving that projected demand will be delivered or utilized.

## Evidence
### Roadmap and order narrative
- [[e230-1-wan-yi-shouru-yuqi-beihou-yingweida-de-dianfeng-yu-ruanlei-d97446f1-d6e3-4894-89d1-dca0a362b10b]] places Vera Rubin beside Blackwell in the claimed $1 trillion cumulative-order outlook and emphasizes coordinated chips, software, packaging, networking, and deployment.
- [[ep-36-nvidia-gtc-2026-everything-that-matters-recapped]] describes Vera CPUs, Rubin GPUs, NVLink 6 switches, ConnectX-9 NICs, BlueField DPUs, and other components as one inference- and agent-oriented rack-scale system.

### Physical deployment
- [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-the-future-of-everything-what-ceos-of-circle-crowdstrike-more-see-coming-in-2026-39870920]] reports a move toward roughly 250 kW rack density and a possible higher-density Ultra path, tying platform rollout to power, cooling, batteries, labor, and data-center construction.

## Qualifications
- Order projections are demand claims, not proof of manufacturing, energization, customer acceptance, utilization, or revenue recognition.
- Component lists, launch schedules, rack density, and performance claims are source-scoped and not independently benchmarked here.
- Higher density can improve locality while intensifying power delivery, thermal management, grid, and facility-design constraints.
