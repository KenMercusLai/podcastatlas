# Nvidia Vera Rubin Platform

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-the-future-of-everything-what-ceos-of-circle-crowdstrike-more-see-coming-in-2026-39870920]] adds [[Crusoe]]'s rack-density view. The source says Vera Rubin can push rack density to about 250 KW, with a possible Ultra path toward 600 KW, turning future platform deployment into a [[DataCenterPowerBottleneck]] and thermal-design problem.

The Nvidia Vera Rubin Platform appears in [[e230-1-wan-yi-shouru-yuqi-beihou-yingweida-de-dianfeng-yu-ruanlei-d97446f1-d6e3-4894-89d1-dca0a362b10b]] as the post-Blackwell platform in [[Nvidia]]'s GTC roadmap. The episode notes the claimed one-shot release of seven new chips and places Vera Rubin inside the same $1 trillion cumulative order narrative as [[NvidiaBlackwellPlatform|Blackwell]].

The source's key point is not only roadmap speed. Vera Rubin makes [[AIInfrastructureFullStackMoat]] visible because a future platform has to coordinate chip design, [[AdvancedPackaging]], [[HighBandwidthMemory]], networking, data-center power, software, and customer deployment before it can become real token capacity.
