# 熊昊 / 熊犹 / 楚哀王 / King Ai of Chu

熊昊 / 熊犹 / 楚哀王 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-1-jintian-liaoliao-yingzheng-dou-you-naxie-canren-shouduan-lmqky6g3qi9nklpdnye4lk5rrbz6]] as the briefly installed successor after [[ChuYouwang|楚幽王]] dies. The episode says the Six States chronological table gives the name as 熊昊, while the Chu lineage account gives 熊犹; this page treats them as the same source-scoped succession figure unless later sources require separation.

His role is a compressed succession failure. [[ChuState|楚国]] installs him as Chu Youwang's younger brother, but he has not stabilized the throne before the faction of his older half-brother [[ChuFuchu|熊负刍 / 楚王负刍]] kills him and installs Fuchu. The page therefore continues the Chu branch from hidden-paternity succession into open palace violence.
