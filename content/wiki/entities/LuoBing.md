## Overview

骆冰 / Luo Bing is a 红花会 member and 文泰来's wife in [[BookAndSword|《书剑恩仇录》]], represented here through active loyalty, reciprocal marriage, and an unambiguous refusal to convert 余鱼同's devotion into romantic obligation.

## Current Profile

The episode begins from the mutual attentiveness of Luo Bing and 文泰来. She acts urgently to rescue him, while he adjusts decisions in response to her fear and values her safety and feelings. Their marriage is therefore presented through conduct and trust rather than a declaration of ideal love.

Her relationship with 余鱼同 establishes a second boundary. She invokes her husband's goodness against his advance, remains angry at his impropriety, and interprets his later sacrifice as fraternal duty rather than a private romantic claim. The hosts disagree about whether one ambiguous smile shows pleasure at admiration or prior awareness of his feelings, but agree that she never reciprocates them.

## Key Characteristics

- Acts with urgency and courage to protect her husband.
- Participates in a marriage marked by mutual attention, trust, and influence.
- Rejects an advance that violates her marital boundary.
- Does not treat another man's sacrifice as a debt requiring emotional return.
- Remains subject to disputed interpretation around one ambiguous reaction.
