# 章平 / Zhang Ping (Qin General)

章平 / Zhang Ping (Qin General) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-149-xichu-bawang-xiangyu-weihe-shijun-2-lpohbb3prsgvokpjipfd7i1ntiwn|Hanji 149 part 2]] as [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]]'s younger brother. The page is disambiguated from [[ZhangPingHan|张平]], [[ZhangLiang|张良]]'s father.

The source gives him a narrow military role. After [[LiuBang|刘邦]] moves his capital to [[LiyangQinHan|栎阳]] and takes [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]], Han forces attack [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]] and defeat Zhang Ping there. His defeat helps clear Liu Bang's northern rear and pushes the Han frontier toward the Yellow River loop.
