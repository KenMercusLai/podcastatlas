# Monica Stoney

Monica Stoney is the founder of [[TressLondon]] and a caller in [[advice-line-strategy-sessions-4962a0d6-36bc-4797-8002-3bd8b748a89a]]. The source presents her as having a luxury womenswear background and Parsons training, then applying that taste and craft orientation to British-made kidswear.

Her Advice Line question is how to create brand heat and word of mouth among style-conscious parents without a large marketing budget. [[SarahLaFleur]] and [[GuyRaz]] advise her to build from physical product experience, neighborhood trust, pop-ups, trunk shows, and buyer relationships rather than relying on broad influencer reach alone.
