# Sonamji

Sonamji appears in [[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]] as a veteran activist on a hunger strike supporting the demand that [[DharmendraPradhan]] resign. The source says plainclothes police removed him from a [[Delhi]] protest site to a government hospital on the twenty-first day of the strike.

The episode treats the removal as a catalytic event. Protesters described it as a kidnapping, and the source links the aftermath to a much larger mobilization around the [[CockroachMovement]], [[EducationAccountabilityProtest]], and [[DissentSpaceCompression]].
