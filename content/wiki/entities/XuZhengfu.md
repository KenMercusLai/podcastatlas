# 許正甫 / Xu Zhengfu

## Overview
[[XuZhengfu]] is introduced in the Duanwen episode as a freelance writer who follows ecological and environmental issues through fiction, essays, and reporting.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Xu as a public-discourse and reporting voice rather than as a technical management authority. His role is to frame why Taiwan's introduced myna debate matters: the same bird can be an ecological-risk topic, a media story, a school-education example, and an object of public hatred.

Xu's contribution is strongest where the episode shifts from species identification to language and responsibility. He treats "alien species" as a classification that requires ecological context, not a shortcut to moral condemnation.

## Key Characteristics
- Works across ecological writing, environmental reporting, essays, and fiction.
- Frames introduced mynas as a public-discourse problem as well as an ecological-management problem.
- Distinguishes ecological risk assessment from hatred-based moral labeling.
