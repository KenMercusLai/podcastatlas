# Blue Seattle

## Overview

Blue Seattle is [[CameronCrowe]]'s unproduced screenplay and accompanying ten-song cycle for an imagined final [[ElvisPresley]] movie.

## Current Profile

The story follows Linda Parnell and Louis Zicks, a struggling married songwriting duo who create a complete song package after hearing Elvis might make one more film. Their proposal casts him as a Seattle cab driver and deliberately includes the occupational pride, romance, child, fight, animal encounter, novelty song, and upbeat finale Crowe identified as recurring Elvis-film ingredients.

The fictional pair eventually meet Elvis during a break from *Change of Habit*. He warns that he may never make another movie, performs “My People” with Louis, and takes the sheet music without accepting their proposal. This brief realization lets recognition and rejection coexist. Outside the screenplay, Crowe, [[NancyWilson]], and [[AnnWilson]] completed the songs, but Crowe never pitched the film.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines an unproduced screenplay with a completed ten-song demo cycle.
- Reconstructs the Elvis musical formula with affection, comic precision, and critical distance.
- Mirrors its fictional married songwriting partnership in the real collaboration between Crowe and Nancy Wilson.
- Withholds conventional production success while granting the characters a brief moment of artistic recognition.
- Exists most concretely through recordings, memory, and retrospective narration.

## Qualifications

The screenplay itself was not supplied, and the demos are described rather than subjected to formal musical analysis. Plot, dialogue, intent, and emotional meaning come through Crowe's retrospective account. The source cannot show whether the project would have been producible, how Elvis or his estate would have responded, or whether filming would have enriched or diminished it.
