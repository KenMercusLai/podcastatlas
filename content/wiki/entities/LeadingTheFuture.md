# Leading the Future

## Overview

Leading the Future is an AI-linked political spending group described by [[MarketplaceTech]] as connected to [[OpenAI]] and oriented toward less AI regulation.

## Current Profile

The source presents the group as part of a multi-PAC network that supports or opposes individual candidates across party lines. Its New York primary intervention against [[AlexBores]] and the reported donation decisions of [[GregBrockman]] make it the episode's main case for direct industry influence on regulatory politics.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses outside spending to intervene in candidate races.
- Is described as connected to OpenAI and less regulation-oriented.
- Operates through affiliated committees whose names may not disclose an AI-policy purpose.
- Can create reputational risk for donors, companies, and candidates as AI politics becomes more contentious.

## Evidence

- **Regulatory and affiliation profile:** [[tech-20261007-1007-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261007-1007-mp-tech-pod-128]] supplies the OpenAI connection and less-regulatory characterization.
- **Candidate intervention:** [[tech-20261007-1007-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261007-1007-mp-tech-pod-128]] says Leading the Future-linked groups opposed Alex Bores in a New York primary.
- **Donor boundary:** [[tech-20261007-1007-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261007-1007-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports that Greg Brockman and his wife withdrew a planned second $25 million donation after an earlier contribution.

## Qualifications

The affiliation, organizational network, donation history, spending amounts, and policy label are episode-attributed. A connection to OpenAI does not by itself establish company control, endorsement, or responsibility for every affiliated committee's action.
