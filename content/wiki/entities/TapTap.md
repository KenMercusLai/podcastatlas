# TapTap

TapTap appears in [[no-214-xunzhao-tonglei-xiaohongshu-bilibili-yiji-wuhuabamen-de-naxie-shequ-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-26-1005977305]] as a game-distribution and game-community case founded by [[HuangYimeng|黄一孟]] in 2016. The episode contrasts TapTap with high-commission mobile-game channel models by saying it refused revenue share on game distribution and relied more on advertising.

The wiki relevance is trust. TapTap's editor recommendations, player reviews, scores, and community discussion made it a more credible discovery surface for mobile games than a pure app-store channel.
