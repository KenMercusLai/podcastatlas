## Overview

王密 / Wang Mi is a late-Han Changyi county magistrate whose nighttime gold gift to [[YangZhenLateHan|杨震]] becomes the concrete foil for Yang's Four Knows reputation.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-783-renxing-qian-guize-nide-jiazhi-jueding-yiqie-lq5qbki6crhieafbv7g5kc07unqe|Hanji 783]] presents Wang Mi as a former maocai recommended by Yang Zhen. When Yang passes through Changyi on his way to Donglai, Wang visits at night with ten jin of gold and says that no one will know.

Yang refuses the gold by answering that Heaven knows, spirits know, Yang knows, and Wang knows. The episode treats Wang's gift as probably gratitude and continuing investment in someone who once helped him, but the moral weight of the scene comes from Yang turning a hidden private transaction into public self-knowledge.

## Key Characteristics

- County magistrate at Changyi when Yang Zhen passes through on the way to Donglai.
- Former maocai whose career had been helped by Yang Zhen's recommendation.
- Offers a large gold gift at night, framed by the source as gratitude and relationship investment.
- Functions mainly as Yang Zhen's foil in the Four Knows integrity story.

## Qualifications

The current evidence is limited to the Four Knows scene in Hanji 783. The source does not supply Wang Mi's wider career, factional position, later conduct, or a proven corrupt request attached to the gold; the host interprets the gift as gratitude and relationship maintenance rather than documenting a specific demanded favor.
