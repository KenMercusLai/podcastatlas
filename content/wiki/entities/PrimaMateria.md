# Prima Materia

## Overview
Prima Materia is an organization created by [[DanielEk|Daniel Ek]] with Martin Lorentzon to support founders through active company-building involvement.

## Current Profile
Ek describes Prima Materia as an alternative to passive investing: it aims to contribute like a highly capable co-founder rather than only supply capital. In this source, the organization mainly clarifies Ek's post-Spotify operating identity; no portfolio, governance, or performance detail is supplied.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder-support organization created by Daniel Ek and Martin Lorentzon.
- Positioned as active company building rather than conventional passive investing.
- Intended to provide co-founder-like capability to supported companies.
