# Houthis

The Houthis appear in [[omission-accomplished-why-the-iran-war-cycle-spins-on-6a6c7475aa1642a30f17f1cb]] as one example in [[GregCarlstrom]]'s broader critique of thin Middle East diplomacy. The episode says a U.S.-linked agreement involving [[SaudiArabia]] and the Houthis left major questions unresolved, after which the Houthis announced a blockade of Saudi shipping in the Red Sea and attacked Saudi airports and energy infrastructure.

The source uses the Houthis less as a full Yemen analysis than as a warning about agreement text without enforcement detail. In the wiki, they connect the Iran MOU branch to [[ThinDiplomaticFrameworks]] and [[ProxyConflictSpoilerRisk]].

## Connections
- [[Yemen]] and [[SaudiArabia]] - conflict setting and target state in the source.
- [[ThinDiplomaticFrameworks]] - broader diplomatic pattern Carlstrom identifies.
