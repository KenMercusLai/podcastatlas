# Barry Benepe

Barry Benepe appears in [[a-firm-andy-what-are-new-british-pms-plans-6a5deeca76f8ee25bb4bbf9c]] as one of the urban planners who created the original [[Greenmarket]] idea. The episode presents him alongside [[BobLewis]] as designing a market that would connect city residents directly with regional farmers.

In the wiki, Benepe anchors the planning side of [[FarmersMarketUrbanism]]. The idea was not only to sell produce, but to build a repeatable public-space and food-access institution in [[NewYorkCity|New York City]].

## Connections
- [[BobLewis]] - co-founder figure in the source.
- [[Greenmarket]] and [[GrowNYC]] - institution that grew from the founding idea.
