## Overview

袁闳 / Yuan Hong (Chenliu, late Han) is the Huan-era attendant and remonstrant introduced in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-852-dang-meinv-ruyun-shi-fu-haishi-huo-luml30ytbc-awpvk-7-23ehzbqpx|Hanji 852]]. He gives [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] a blunt but initially accepted "middle-grade ruler" assessment, then later uses a sealed omen memorial to criticize Deng Wanshi's excessive favor before withdrawing when the emperor gives no response.

## Current Profile

Hanji 852 presents Yuan Hong in two stages. First, Huan asks him what kind of ruler he is. Yuan answers that Huan is in the middle range of Han rulers because [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]]'s governance improves the realm while eunuch and yellow-gate participation disorders it. The assessment is sharp but not rejected: Huan invokes an old strong-remonstrance precedent, acknowledges the criticism, and appoints Yuan as wuguan zhonglangjiang before Yuan later rises to dahonglu.

The second stage is less successful. After a strange star crosses the Dazuo field, Huan privately asks Yuan for an explanation. Yuan submits a sealed memorial that treats the sign as a political warning: [[DengWanshiLateHan|邓万世]] receives honor beyond senior ministers and imperial kin because of old friendship, is summoned into the palace for games, and becomes intimate enough to blur ruler-minister propriety. Yuan argues that a ruler's close companions shape daily moral direction; worthy companions teach good, while flatterers multiply evil thoughts. Huan gives no answer, and Yuan leaves office by claiming illness.

## Key Characteristics

- Chenliu remonstrant whose source-supported role is direct assessment of Emperor Huan.
- Diagnoses Huan as mixed rather than simply incapable: he can follow worthy people or corrupt attendants.
- Uses omen language to turn an astronomical anomaly into criticism of Deng Wanshi's excessive intimacy and favor.
- Treats companion choice as a daily governance mechanism, not only a private moral preference.
- Withdraws after nonresponse, making his case a bounded example of counsel exit after ignored advice.

## Evidence

- Middle-ruler assessment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-852-dang-meinv-ruyun-shi-fu-haishi-huo-luml30ytbc-awpvk-7-23ehzbqpx|Hanji 852]] says Yuan tells Huan he is a middle-grade ruler because he can do good with Chen Fan and disorder the court with eunuch and yellow-gate actors.
- Initial acceptance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-852-dang-meinv-ruyun-shi-fu-haishi-huo-luml30ytbc-awpvk-7-23ehzbqpx|Hanji 852]] says Huan accepts the direct criticism enough to appoint Yuan as wuguan zhonglangjiang and that Yuan later reaches dahonglu.
- Omen memorial: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-852-dang-meinv-ruyun-shi-fu-haishi-huo-luml30ytbc-awpvk-7-23ehzbqpx|Hanji 852]] says Yuan uses a strange-star inquiry to criticize Deng Wanshi's exceptional favor, palace gambling, and collapsed ruler-minister distance.
- Companion-choice argument: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-852-dang-meinv-ruyun-shi-fu-haishi-huo-luml30ytbc-awpvk-7-23ehzbqpx|Hanji 852]] says Yuan urges Huan to distance flattering small men and draw near loyal, upright people.
- Withdrawal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-852-dang-meinv-ruyun-shi-fu-haishi-huo-luml30ytbc-awpvk-7-23ehzbqpx|Hanji 852]] says Yuan receives no response to the sealed memorial and claims illness to return home.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to one source note and is not a full biography. The source's Chenliu official/remonstrant should not be merged with [[YuanHongLateHan|袁闳 / Yuan Hong (late Han)]], the Yuan-family recluse whose Hanji 896 profile centers on refusing clan wealth and withdrawing into a doorless hut. The episode does not establish Yuan Hong's later career beyond the dahonglu notice or the consequences after his resignation.
