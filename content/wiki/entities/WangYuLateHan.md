# 王寓 / Wang Yu (late Han)

[[WangYuLateHan|王寓]] enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-902-kongzhi-qingxu-shi-rensheng-bixiuke-ljynylvdlsdtjf4es9jmilav863b|Hanji 902]] as a former sili xiaowei whose request for recommendation becomes a personal-grudge route into late-Han party politics. The episode says Wang relies on his eunuch connections and asks [[ZhangHuanLateHan|张奂]], then taichang, to recommend him. Zhang refuses.

Wang later uses the political environment against Zhang by accusing him of being a party figure. In the episode's structure, this makes Wang an early example of how a refused favor can be converted into political accusation once [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|feedback channels]] and party-prohibition pressure make reputation dangerous.
