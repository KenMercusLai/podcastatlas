# Tom Gibson

## Overview
Tom Gibson is presented as the chief flavorist at [[Flavorman]], where he explains how aroma components are assembled into beverage flavors.

## Current Profile
Gibson's source role is to make flavor engineering legible. He describes flavor as substantially aroma-driven, shows that a natural profile may contain hundreds of aroma chemicals, and treats “cherry” or “strawberry” as design briefs requiring choices about ripeness, floral notes, darkness, and depth rather than as single standardized targets.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames aroma as a primary driver of perceived flavor.
- Builds recognizable profiles from essential oils and aroma chemicals.
- Uses small dissonant or unpleasant-smelling components to create depth in a balanced mixture.
- Interprets flavor requests as multidimensional design problems rather than fixed recipes.
