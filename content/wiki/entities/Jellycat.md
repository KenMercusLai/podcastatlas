# Jellycat

Jellycat appears in [[ep236-lao-wu-kuai-qian-xinlihua-fufei-de-peiban-shi-zhende-ma-ltvuoeluoeu4-a3j3lamhc3buudx]] as an example of plush-toy consumption inside the [[EmotionEconomy|情绪经济]]. The source does not analyze the company strategy directly; it uses the brand as a recognizable case where softness, touch, cute design, and companion-like presence can make a toy worth more to buyers than its materials.

In the wiki, Jellycat belongs to [[TactileComfortConsumption]] rather than to a generic collectibles branch. The episode's point is that a plush object can soothe through bodily sensation, weight, familiarity, and quiet presence, which makes it adjacent to [[CompanionAnimalHealth]] while remaining a manufactured consumer product.
