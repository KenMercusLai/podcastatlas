# 秦出公 / Qin Chugong

秦出公 / Qin Chugong appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-2-lvaqlgyccrugfo0l-wuwf1mt1kcm]] as [[QinHuiGong|秦惠公]]'s son and successor after Qin Hui Gong dies in 387 BCE. The episode presents him as a tragic accession figure: he inherits the throne, but the host says he will be killed in a coup a few years later, along with his mother.

The page is source-scoped. It records the episode's Qin succession notice without expanding into a full biography.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-3-ltca-ikuzo9ej7a0lfrgwv2-wulb]] supplies that coup. In 385 BCE, shuzhang Gai brings [[QinXianGong|秦献公]] back from exile, installs him as ruler, and kills Qin Chugong and his mother. The episode explains the coup through the instability of a child ruler, palace regency, eunuch influence, and a returning royal with a plausible succession claim.
