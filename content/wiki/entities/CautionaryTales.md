# Cautionary Tales

## Overview

Cautionary Tales is [[TimHarford]]'s narrative podcast about historical mistakes and the lessons people draw from them, represented here by a reassessment of [[WilliamMcGonagall]].

## Current Profile

The bounded episode combines narrated history, literary criticism, behavioral interpretation, and a contemporary technology analogy. Its caution is not simply that grand self-belief produces bad art. By reconsidering McGonagall as a persistent maker and possible knowing clown, the show turns confidence in the audience's own interpretation into the object of scrutiny.

The episode preserves tension between instruction and uncertainty. It offers a usable lesson about fear of failure and [[EverydayCreativeAgency]] while refusing to settle the historical subject's intention beyond the supplied evidence.

## Key Characteristics

- Narrative history organized around mistakes, judgment, and practical lessons.
- Uses biography and vivid scenes to make abstract behavioral questions concrete.
- Revises its apparent moral as new interpretations accumulate.
- Connects historical material to current creative and technological questions.
- Preserves uncertainty when the available anecdotes cannot establish motive.
