# Founder School

## Overview
Founder School is the Alpha-associated high-school entrepreneurship program described in the source.

## Current Profile
The source presents Founder School as an extension of [[AlphaSchool]]'s builder logic into high school: students continue academics while starting businesses, and the program is described with a source-scoped profit-or-tuition-back guarantee. Its role in the wiki is to show how Alpha's nonacademic day becomes a concrete entrepreneurship pathway rather than only enrichment.

## Key Characteristics
- Extends [[BuilderBasedSchoolDay]] into business creation during high school.
- Links entrepreneurship, financial literacy, public communication, and applied responsibility to the school model.
- Is described with a strong source-scoped guarantee around student profit or tuition return.
