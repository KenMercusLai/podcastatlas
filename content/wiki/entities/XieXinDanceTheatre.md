# 谢欣舞蹈剧场 / Xie Xin Dance Theatre

## Overview
谢欣舞蹈剧场 / Xie Xin Dance Theatre is the contemporary-dance company founded and artistically directed by [[XieXinChoreographer|谢欣]].

## Current Profile
The company appears in EP286 as one of the Chinese contemporary-dance participants in the Shanghai edition of [[DanceReflections|Dance Reflections]]. The episode uses Xie Xin's working methods rather than a company chronology to make its artistic context legible: trained bodily discipline, inward breath and release, relational choreography, and an independent bodily aesthetic within international exchange.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder-led by choreographer and performer [[XieXinChoreographer|谢欣]].
- Identified with Chinese contemporary-dance creation.
- Participates in an international program joining performance, circulation, and education.
- Represented through a practice of bodily attention and relational choreography.
