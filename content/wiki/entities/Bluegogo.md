# 小蓝单车 / Bluegogo

## Overview
小蓝单车 / Bluegogo is the shared-bike player the episode uses to show how a well-liked product could still fail when financing and cash flow broke.

## Current Profile
The source presents Bluegogo as a third-line contender founded by Li Gang after Beast Ride. Its bikes were remembered as comfortable and easy to ride, and the company reported large user and order numbers. But financing failure, acquisition missteps, wage and supplier arrears, and deposit delays pushed it out of the independent shared-bike race.

## Key Characteristics
- It competed on product comfort and ride quality.
- It grew to substantial claimed users, bikes, and daily orders during the 2017 boom.
- It failed despite product reputation because financing and cash discipline collapsed.
- Its case qualifies any simple reading that better product experience alone could win the shared-bike war.
