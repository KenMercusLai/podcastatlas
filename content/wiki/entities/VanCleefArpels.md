# Van Cleef & Arpels / 梵克雅宝

## Overview
Van Cleef & Arpels / 梵克雅宝 is the luxury jewelry maison presented in this source as the founder and supporter of [[DanceReflections|Dance Reflections]].

## Current Profile
EP286 focuses narrowly on the maison's dance patronage. It traces an asserted relationship with dance to the 1920s and presents the 2020 Dance Reflections program as an institutional commitment to contemporary creation, international circulation, education, and broader public access.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses a branded cultural program to support contemporary dance.
- Connects funding with curatorial, educational, and international-partner activity.
- Presents public workshops and masterclasses alongside professional performance.
- Links its China activity to a longer source-reported relationship with dance.

## Evidence
- Program sponsorship - [[ep286-zai-yuelaiyue-shuzihua-de-shidai-ruhe-tongguo-wudao-zhongxin-zhaohui-yong-shenti-ganzhi-shijie-de-nengli-ltbnmxye2gnqzs4oubhe3mtmx0q9]] identifies the episode as supported by Dance Reflections and names the maison as its founder.
- Institutional framing - [[ep286-zai-yuelaiyue-shuzihua-de-shidai-ruhe-tongguo-wudao-zhongxin-zhaohui-yong-shenti-ganzhi-shijie-de-nengli-ltbnmxye2gnqzs4oubhe3mtmx0q9]] describes creation, circulation, and education as the program's organizing commitments.

## Qualifications
The episode is sponsored by the featured program and does not independently assess the maison's motives, total spending, governance, artist-selection process, or long-term audience impact. Historical continuity claims remain source-scoped.
