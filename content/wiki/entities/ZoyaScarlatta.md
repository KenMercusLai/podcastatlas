# Zoya Scarlatta

## Overview
Zoya Scarlatta is the digital-marketing practitioner interviewed in [[ep-23-ai-in-marketing-strategies]] about AI strategy, operations, measurement, and responsibility in B2B marketing.

## Current Profile
The source identifies Scarlatta as Director of Digital Marketing at [[InterraInformationTechnologies]] with nearly twelve years of B2B marketing experience. Her approach is problem-led: use AI to predict and coordinate customer action, reduce manual work, and improve decisions, but keep human interpretation, brand judgment, privacy, and data guardrails in the operating model.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames AI as an assistant to marketing strategy rather than a reason to adopt every new tool.
- Connects personalization to predictive intent and orchestration across customer-data sources.
- Measures AI through efficiency, business impact, and team adoption rather than a single headline ROI number.
- Treats AI literacy as knowledge of limitations, hallucinations, data quality, privacy, ethics, and misinformation.
- Keeps human review central to public content, creative distinctiveness, and brand trust.
- Proposes a guarded client-growth and retention agent as a practical application of account intelligence.
