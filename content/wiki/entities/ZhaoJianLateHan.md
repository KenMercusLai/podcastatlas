# 赵戬 / Zhao Jian (late Han)

赵戬 / Zhao Jian (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-966-meiyou-yexin-budong-quanmou-jiu-bie-dang-lingdao-lldn3hi1gl5v3axvklwakgf6vx3u|Hanji 966]] as a former subordinate of [[WangYunLateHan|王允]]. The `ZhaoJianLateHan` key avoids collision with [[ZhaoJianzi|赵简子]].

After [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] kill Wang Yun and leave his body exposed in the marketplace, Zhao Jian gives up office and risks collecting and burying the corpse. The episode presents him as a small but morally clear loyalty case after the larger political failure: he cannot save Wang Yun or restore the court, but he preserves a human obligation when fear prevents others from acting.
