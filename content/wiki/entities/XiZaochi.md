# 习凿齿 / Xi Zaochi

## Overview
习凿齿 / Xi Zaochi enters the wiki as the Eastern Jin historian whose criticism of [[CaoCao|曹操]] supplies Hanji 1109's interpretive frame for the post-Red-Cliffs balance.

## Current Profile
The episode compares Xi Zaochi's judgment with the story of Duke Huan of Qi losing support after displaying achievement. Xi Zaochi argues that Cao Cao's pride after accumulated success damaged personnel reception and strategic opportunity, helping [[LiuBei|刘备]] and [[SunQuan|孙权]] consolidate rival positions.

## Key Characteristics
- Functions in the source as a later historian evaluating political character through strategic consequence.
- Connects Cao Cao's treatment of [[ZhangSongLateHan|张松]] with the broader post-Red-Cliffs reversal.
- Treats pride as a repeated decision distortion rather than one isolated etiquette failure.
