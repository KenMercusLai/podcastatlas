# Ring (Kuse)

Ring is a top-level internal [[Junior]] at [[Kuse]] in [[women-shi-ruhe-dingyi-openclaw-for-teams-xin-chanpin-xingtai-de-duitan-kuse-junior-lianchuang-jian-cto-yuhao-lkp1a0todflxoyycyo3zhrap3ebv]]. This page is named `KuseRing` to avoid colliding with [[Ring]], the Amazon-owned camera company.

The source says Ring participates in Junior's PRD, PR, code, marketing material, and sales material, and that the team sometimes asks Ring first when a project problem appears. Ring began with meeting notes and project summaries, then grew into a persistent project participant that could message [[Yuhao]], assign work, and preserve much of the product context.
