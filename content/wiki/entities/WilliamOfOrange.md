# William of Orange

William of Orange appears in [[695-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-the-shadow-war-part-5-glt5637968455]] as the central rebel leader whose assassination on 10 July 1584 pushes the [[DutchRevolt]] toward collapse. The episode says [[PhilipII|Philip II]] had placed a bounty on him, making his death part of the wider Catholic-Protestant struggle rather than an isolated murder.

William's death matters because it removes a rallying figure just as [[AlexanderFarnese|Alexander Farnese / Duke of Parma]] is recovering the southern [[LowCountries|Low Countries]] for [[Spain]]. The source uses that sequence to explain why [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]] and English leaders could no longer treat the Dutch theatre as a distant problem.

In the wiki, William anchors the pre-[[TreatyOfNonsuch]] crisis. His assassination, Ghent's surrender, and the siege of [[Antwerp]] together create the pressure that moves England toward direct intervention.
