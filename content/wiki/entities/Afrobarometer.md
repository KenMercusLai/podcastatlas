# Afrobarometer

Afrobarometer appears in [[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]] as the survey source for African radio-listening habits. The episode says around 60% of people in both cities and the countryside listen to radio multiple times a week.

Its source role is to keep [[AfricanRadioResilience]] from being only anecdotal. The episode uses survey evidence to argue that radio remains a mass medium even as social media and streaming grow.
