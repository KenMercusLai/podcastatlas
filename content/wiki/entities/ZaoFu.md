# 造父 / Zaofu

造父 / Zaofu enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-3-xunzi-bingfa-li-cangzhe-renxing-de-mimi-lgfcp4hkruyhhrjguqhjbhllvwp43]], where the host pauses inside the [[Xunzi|荀子]] and [[LinwuJun|临武君]] military debate to explain why Zhao and Qin share an origin-memory around horses and service to Zhou rulers.

The episode identifies Zaofu as a famous royal charioteer who receives land at Zhao and becomes an ancestor-marker for [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. It then links him to [[QinFeizi|非子]], described in the source as a later kinsman whose horse-raising service leads to the Qin grant. Zaofu's role here is not a developed biography; he is the bridge that lets the debate setting carry both Zhao and Qin lineage memory.
