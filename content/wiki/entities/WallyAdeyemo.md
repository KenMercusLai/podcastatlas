# Wally Adeyemo

Wally Adeyemo appears in [[tsr-s5-ronconway-v5-tsr-s5-ronconway-v5]] as [[USTreasury|U.S. Treasury]] Deputy Secretary and one of [[RonConway]]'s first calls during the [[SiliconValleyBank]] weekend. Conway says Adeyemo directed him to [[GrahamSteele]], the Treasury point person on the crisis, and later took Conway's calls directly as the process developed.

In the episode, Adeyemo matters because he turns [[CivicRelationshipsAsCrisisInfrastructure]] into an operational pathway: a preexisting relationship gave Conway a route into Treasury before the decision window closed.
