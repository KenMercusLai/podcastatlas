# Amazon Leo

## Overview
Amazon Leo is [[Amazon]]'s low-earth-orbit satellite internet service as described in the episode's in-flight-connectivity market comparison.

## Current Profile
The current bounded evidence positions Amazon Leo as the most visible alternative to [[Starlink]] for airline connectivity. The source says [[DeltaAirLines|Delta Air Lines]] and JetBlue selected it and reports Delta's view that it may cost less than Starlink.

## Key Characteristics
- Low-earth-orbit satellite connectivity competitor backed by [[Amazon]].
- Airline-market alternative to [[Starlink]].
- Presented as a possible cost competitor rather than a proven universal replacement.
