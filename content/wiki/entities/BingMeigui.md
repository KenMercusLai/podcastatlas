# 《病玫瑰》

《病玫瑰》 is one of the medical-history books recommended in [[yi-fen-laizi-bingchuang-shang-de-xiangguan-shudan-598284093]]. The hosts place it inside the ideal-country "手术剧场" style of medical visual culture: eighteenth- and nineteenth-century disease illustrations, anatomical images, and bodily records that are visually striking but also unsettling.

The book's function in the wiki is not ordinary health advice. It makes [[MedicalVisualHistory]] visible: medicine has often needed images, specimens, drawings, and visual comparison before disease categories and surgical practice could become transmissible knowledge.
