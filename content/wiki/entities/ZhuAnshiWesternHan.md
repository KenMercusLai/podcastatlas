## Overview

朱安世 / Zhu Anshi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-383-2-chengxiang-gongsunhe-shi-ruhe-bei-erzi-kengsi-de-lt1yhocymjtgwjb7537yotbwoikz|Hanji 383-2]] as the wanted outlaw whom [[GongsunHeWesternHan|公孙贺]] tries to capture in exchange for leniency toward [[GongsunJingshengWesternHan|公孙敬声]]. His role matters because his retaliatory accusation converts a corruption rescue attempt into a lethal wugu case.

## Current Profile

The page is currently source-scoped to Hanji 383-2. The episode portrays Zhu Anshi as a violent and well-connected wanted man who has evaded arrest because friends and informants help him avoid officials. When Gongsun He pressures Zhu's relatives to force his capture, Zhu surrenders but retaliates by accusing Gongsun Jingsheng of adultery with Yangshi Princess and curse activity against [[HanWudi|汉武帝]].

The source's synthesis is that Zhu Anshi's accusation functions as a revenge mechanism inside a court already primed to fear hidden ritual harm. The episode does not make him a mastermind of the later [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]], but it makes him the immediate accuser whose charges destroy the Gongsun family.

## Key Characteristics

- Wanted Western Han outlaw used by Gongsun He as a proposed pardon bargain.
- Socially connected fugitive who can evade ordinary arrest before Gongsun He pressures his kin network.
- Retaliatory accuser who turns coercive arrest tactics back against the Gongsun family.
- Wugu-case trigger figure whose claims about adultery and curse objects become politically lethal.
- Executed before the Gongsun father and son die in prison, according to the episode.

## Qualifications

This page is not a full biography of Zhu Anshi. It preserves the episode's account of his role in the Gongsun case and does not independently reconstruct his criminal record, the full arrest process, or whether every element of the accusation was fabricated, staged, or partially grounded.
