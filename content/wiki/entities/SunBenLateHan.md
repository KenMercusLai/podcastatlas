# 孙贲 / Sun Ben (late Han)

## Overview
孙贲 / Sun Ben is a Sun-family military and administrative actor whose current source profile connects Danyang recruitment infrastructure to Sun Ce's later Yuzhang settlement.

## Current Profile
The evidence gives Sun Ben a support role rather than an independent arc. Yuan Shu first uses him with [[WuJingLateHan|吴景]] to extend Sun-family access into [[DanyangCommandery|丹阳郡]]; Hanji 1039 later has [[SunCe|孙策]] install him as [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]] administrator after Hua Xin's surrender.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates as a Sun-family channel inside Yuan Shu's Danyang arrangement.
- Helps form the recruitment and resource base Sun Ce uses before independent expansion.
- Is later assigned Yuzhang after Sun Ce's takeover, linking family personnel to territorial administration.
- Functions mainly as institutional infrastructure around Sun Ce, not as a fully narrated protagonist.

## Evidence
- Danyang channel: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] places Sun Ben with Wu Jing in Yuan Shu's Danyang arrangement.
- Sun Ce launch support: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] says Sun Ce is sent to rely on Wu Jing and Sun Ben rather than receiving all of Sun Jian's old troops.
- Yuzhang appointment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] says Sun Ce makes Sun Ben administrator of Yuzhang after Hua Xin's surrender.
- Family administration pattern: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1039-dongwu-diyi-zuipao-qie-kan-ta-ruhe-quanjiang-huaxin-lhre-ih73ynzfwujo0lnxyjdguyd|Hanji 1039]] pairs Sun Ben's Yuzhang appointment with Sun Fu's Luling appointment.

## Qualifications
The sources do not yet provide Sun Ben's independent motives or command style. His current profile should stay anchored to Sun-family logistics and administration.
