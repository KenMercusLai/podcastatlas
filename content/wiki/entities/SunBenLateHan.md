# 孙贲 / Sun Ben (late Han)

孙贲 / Sun Ben (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] as a Sun-family military actor whom [[YuanShu|袁术]] places in [[DanyangCommandery|丹阳郡]] alongside [[WuJingLateHan|吴景]]. The source says Yuan Shu uses Wu Jing's governorship and Sun Ben's military appointment to put a Sun-family channel into Danyang, a valuable troop and supply base.

Sun Ben's main source role is launch infrastructure for [[SunCe|孙策]]. Yuan Shu sends Sun Ce to rely on Wu Jing and Sun Ben for recruitment rather than immediately handing over all of [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]'s old troops. Later in the same episode, [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]] first receives Wu Jing and Sun Ben at [[QuALateHan|曲阿]], then expels them when he fears Yuan Shu and Sun Ce will absorb him.
