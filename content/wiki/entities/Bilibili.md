# Bilibili / 哔哩哔哩

## Overview
Bilibili / B站 is a Chinese video and community platform whose wiki profile combines its hobbyist bullet-comment origin, advertising-led monetization milestone, and current AI-creator contest surface.

## Current Profile
Bilibili began in the wiki as a community-platform case. The Banlatte source traces it from [[XuYiBilibili|徐逸]]'s MikuFans project and the [[AcFun]] / [[Niconico]] bullet-comment lineage into a public platform shaped by [[ChenRuiBilibili|陈睿]], [[LiNi|李旎]], licensing, games, membership, creator culture, and an explicit no-forced-pre-roll identity.

The current revenue-mix turn is that advertising became Bilibili's largest business segment for the first time, overtaking live streaming, big membership, and other value-added services. That does not erase the community thesis. It sharpens it: Bilibili's monetization now depends on making advertising work through young consumer users, AI-learning attention, and creator recommendation trust without losing the browsing rhythm and community attachment that made the platform valuable.

Bilibili is also a creator-platform venue for new tool categories. The [[BilibiliAICreationContest|B站 AI 创作大赛]] frames [[NvwaZhiSi|《女娲之死》]] and other AI shorts as evidence that AI video is moving from demo novelty into platform-visible creator practice, while story, emotion, and audience response remain harder than visual spectacle.

## Key Characteristics
- Descendant of the [[AcFun]] and [[Niconico]] bullet-comment lineage with strong anime, remix, meme, knowledge, and youth-culture roots.
- Community identity depends on [[BulletCommentCommunity]], UP creator rituals, membership culture, shared events, and creator-viewer trust.
- Professionalization under Chen Rui and Li Ni centered on licensing, games, memberships, advertising, IP, and creator commercialization.
- The no-forced-pre-roll stance makes Bilibili a key example of [[CreatorTrustAdvertising]] and [[OnlineCommunityCommercializationFriction]].
- Advertising has now become the largest reported business segment in the latest source, making Bilibili a case for [[PlatformAdvertisingMonetization]].
- Its AI creation contest makes Bilibili a current venue for platform-visible [[AIVideoProductionWorkflow]] experimentation.

## Qualifications
Bilibili's ad growth and contest scale are source-reported and time-specific. The sources do not disclose ad-format mix, creator-income distribution, retention effects, event rules, full participant counts, or whether AI advertising and AI-creation demand will persist after the current category heat. The older community evidence also means the platform should not be reduced to a generic video ad inventory business.
