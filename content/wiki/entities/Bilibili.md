# Bilibili / 哔哩哔哩

Bilibili / B站 is one of the two main cases in [[no-214-xunzhao-tonglei-xiaohongshu-bilibili-yiji-wuhuabamen-de-naxie-shequ-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-26-1005977305]]. The episode traces it from [[XuYiBilibili|徐逸]]'s MikuFans project and the [[AcFun]] backup-user migration into a bullet-comment video community with strong anime, remix, meme, and creator culture.

The source frames Bilibili as a company built from a hobbyist community into a public platform. [[ChenRuiBilibili|陈睿]] and [[LiNi|李旎]] professionalized the company through licensing, games, membership, advertising, IP, and creator commercialization, while still trying to preserve the community's distinctive trust and browsing rhythm.

For the wiki, Bilibili extends [[ChineseMobileInternetCommunities]] and [[CommunityVsContentPlatform]]. It is not just a video container: [[BulletCommentCommunity]], creator-viewer rituals, membership exams, BML, New Year's events, UP creator identity, and shared youth culture all make the platform partly a relationship system.

## Key Points
- The episode presents Bilibili as the successful descendant of the [[AcFun]] and [[Niconico]] bullet-comment lineage.
- Its early community survived partly through user contribution, including server support, app development, and informal help.
- The company route under Chen Rui centered on legalization, licensed content, and commercialization.
- Games, especially source-described early mobile-game revenue, carried Bilibili financially before advertising and value-added services became stronger.
- The no-forced-pre-roll debate shows why Bilibili is a case for [[CreatorTrustAdvertising]] and [[OnlineCommunityCommercializationFriction]].
