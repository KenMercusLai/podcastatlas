## Overview

傅商 / Fu Shang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-584-xihan-hushan-lishi-moufan-shijian-shimo-lmpoeruya1v3qmixnvi8ewaffqfo|Hanji 584]] as [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]]'s cousin and a late [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] court favorite-relative whose proposed enfeoffment becomes a test of outer-relative privilege.

## Current Profile

Hanji 584 presents Fu Shang as an attendant and Guanglu Daifu whom Aidi wants to enfeoff. [[ZhengChongWesternHan|郑崇]] opposes the reward by comparing it to earlier Chengdi-era enfeoffments that had at least family or office grounds and were still accompanied by ominous signs. Zheng argues that Fu Shang's case lacks proper basis and violates Han institutions and public feeling.

Fu Shang himself is not the active political speaker in the source. His importance is positional: his kinship to Fu Taihou turns an apparently routine reward into evidence that Aidi remains constrained by elder consort-side power even when he resents ministerial constraint.

## Key Characteristics

- Fu-family relative and cousin of Fu Taihou.
- Late-Aidi court figure holding attendant and Guanglu Daifu status.
- Proposed recipient of a marquisate from Han Aidi.
- Becomes the object of Zheng Chong's institutional and omen-coded remonstrance.
- Enfeoffed as Luchang Hou despite the objection.
- Functions as evidence of Fu-side outer-relative pressure rather than as an independently profiled policymaker.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 584. It does not establish Fu Shang's full biography, merit record, exact offices, later fate, or personal role in securing the title. The page treats him as a node in Fu-side outer-relative politics because that is how the source uses him.
