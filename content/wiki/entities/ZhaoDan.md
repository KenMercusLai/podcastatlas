# 赵丹 / 赵孝成王 / King Xiaocheng of Zhao

赵丹 / 赵孝成王 / King Xiaocheng of Zhao enters the wiki in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-90-jiemi-xianqin-zui-wanmei-de-fuchou-liu7gbl-bffknkw6dx5udqcq4tcm]] when [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] dies and Zhao Dan succeeds in [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. The episode does not yet develop his own decisions; it introduces him through the immediate political environment around his uncle [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]].

That succession timing matters because [[WeiQiWarringStates|魏齐]] flees to Pingyuanjun while [[FanJu|范雎]] is using [[QinState|秦国]] pressure against [[WeiState|魏国]] to demand Wei Qi's head. Zhao Dan is therefore born into the episode as a new ruler whose court can be pulled into another state's revenge and coercion through aristocratic refuge.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-91-chulong-ruhe-qiaokai-shougua-zhaotaihou-de-men-1-lmgd7kdl1nrfwcrtkqttumtlpqok]] shows the practical crisis around that accession. Because Zhao Dan is young, [[ZhaoWeihou|赵威后]] governs in a [[QueenMotherRegency|queen-mother regency]] while Qin attacks Zhao. The state asks [[QiState|齐国]] for help, but Qi demands [[ChangAnJunZhao|长安君]] as hostage, creating the setting for [[ChuLong|触龙]]'s persuasion.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-91-chulong-ruhe-qiaokai-shougua-zhaotaihou-de-men-2-liy5yulg2ysop-jwfigvdkub1pk7]] resolves that first accession crisis: Zhao Weihou sends Chang'an Jun to Qi, Qi sends troops, and Qin withdraws. The episode also compares Zhao Dan's youth with [[QiWangJian|齐王建 / 田建]]'s young accession under [[JunWangHou|君王后]], making queen-mother regency a cross-state pattern in this annalistic year.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-1-ljshdryvf9qgabtn-siontthzsok]] makes Zhao Dan an active decision-maker after that regency phase. With [[ZhaoWeihou|赵威后]] gone and Zhao Dan newly governing in his own right, [[FengTing|冯亭]] offers [[Shangdang|上党]]'s seventeen cities to Zhao. Zhao Dan consults [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] and [[ZhaoBao|赵豹 / 平阳君]], but Zhao Bao's warning against [[WindfallDecisionTrap|无故之利]] does not overcome Zhao Dan's belief that Shangdang's people are drawn by his 德. The episode treats this as the beginning of Zhao's [[WindfallDecisionTrap|利令智昏]] exposure.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-2-lrlulw0b6v0fb2xpr2i5ifem1h4n]] completes that decision. Zhao Dan rejects Zhao Bao's risk pricing, follows [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]], sends him to receive Shangdang, and rewards [[FengTing|冯亭]] and Shangdang's officials lavishly. The source also adds the [[Shiji|《史记》]] dream layer omitted by [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]]: Zhao Dan dreams of mixed clothing, riding a flying dragon, falling midway, and landing among gold and jade. The episode reads this as [[HistoricalDreamForeshadowing|dream foreshadowing]] for the coming [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-1-loxyybm3njrtnudydafwzu9jp9jz]] turns the foreshadowed consequence into Zhao Dan's serial decision failure. He consults [[LouChang|娄昌]] and [[YuQing|虞卿]] after repeated front-line losses, but follows the direct-peace path by sending [[ZhengZhu|郑朱]] to [[QinState|秦国]]. The episode presents this as a diplomatic mistake: Qin receives Zheng Zhu publicly, isolates Zhao from possible [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] help, and does not actually make peace.

The same source then makes Zhao Dan responsible for the command break at Changping. He pressures [[LianPo|廉颇]] for staying defensive, accepts [[FanJu|范雎]]'s rumor that Qin fears [[ZhaoKuo|赵括]], and appoints Zhao Kuo despite warnings from [[LinXiangru|蔺相如]] and [[ZhaoKuoMother|赵母]]. Zhao Dan's page therefore now links the earlier [[WindfallDecisionTrap|Shangdang windfall trap]] to [[BattlefieldCommanderReplacementRisk|阵前换将风险]] and [[PaperWarCommandTrap|纸上谈兵式统帅陷阱]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-2-lvqctrazn8ddkcnp-lhspzzmzmns]] gives the battlefield consequence of Zhao Dan's replacement decision. Zhao Kuo abandons the defensive attrition line, attacks across the Dan River, and falls into Bai Qi's encirclement-and-supply-cutting system. The later grain crisis also exposes the earlier diplomatic error: after Qin seals the route, Zhao asks Qi for food but receives no relief.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-3-ljwz8ebk0iyfleivkefvvoonrqb9]] adds the final consequence and retrospective blame. Zhao Kuo's failed breakout and death lead to the Zhao army's surrender, after which [[BaiQi|白起]] kills the prisoners and returns only 240 young soldiers as messengers. The host names Zhao Dan's greed for [[Shangdang|上党]] as one link in the causal chain, placing the ruler's earlier [[WindfallDecisionTrap|利令智昏]] alongside [[FengTing|冯亭]]'s transfer and Zhao Kuo's paper-war command.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-1-lq4wvkd8qx3q7zji6fnnkxi3qcl1]] shows Zhao Dan after the catastrophe as a ruler trying to preserve the state through direct risk. The episode says the [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] layer has Zhao Dan personally go to [[QinState|秦国]] for peace despite the remembered danger of [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]]'s captivity. Qin detains him, reaches a settlement, then [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] releases him while keeping [[ZhaoHao|赵郝]] to negotiate the details of the six-city cession.

This episode softens the page's earlier blame frame without erasing it. Zhao Dan's acceptance of [[Shangdang|上党]] and appointment of [[ZhaoKuo|赵括]] remain catastrophic decisions, but the source now also presents him as accepting personal danger and the political cost of peace after Changping.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-2-lutsvwvnwzsezoorhe-vu7yjthyx]] immediately complicates that peace settlement. Once Zhao Dan returns, [[YuQing|虞卿]] argues against completing the six-city cession because it would help an exhausted Qin recover, while [[LouHuan|楼缓]] argues that non-cession exposes Zhao to Qin and opportunistic neighbors. Zhao Dan's choice is now not only personal risk at Qin, but a post-defeat credibility trap: either cede land and advertise weakness or break the promise and invite Qin's retaliation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-3-lsrmq1-rjqnsja8g07bhaqvhxhpi]] adds a second post-defeat choice. When [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] detains [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] and demands [[WeiQiWarringStates|魏齐]]'s head, Zhao Dan sends troops to surround Pingyuanjun's house and pursue Wei Qi. The decision protects a royal relative and reduces immediate Qin leverage, but it also means Zhao's ruler turns against the guest-protection norm that Pingyuanjun and [[YuQing|虞卿]] are trying to uphold.

The exchange succeeds only narrowly. Zhao receives Pingyuanjun back after Wei Qi's suicide, but [[QinState|秦国]] still attacks under [[WangLing|王陵]]. Zhao Dan's page therefore adds [[AristocraticFriendshipStateConflict|贵族义气与国家秩序冲突]] to the post-Changping survival branch: state rescue and legal command win the immediate round without buying peace.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-1-lvqbmaabsvbdz2zibgmseidx5phe]] shows Zhao Dan's next survival move under siege. With [[WangLing|王陵]] failing but Qin still reinforcing and [[BaiQi|白起]] refusing to command, Zhao cannot simply wait for Qin exhaustion; the state also needs external help. Because Wei and Qi are unreliable, Zhao Dan sends [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] to seek [[ChuState|楚国]] aid, opening the [[MaoSui|毛遂]] branch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-2-ltlobdujy-hdipimfdupvwod2ech]] shows that Zhao Dan's external-help path only half succeeds. [[MaoSui|毛遂]] secures [[MiWan|楚考烈王]]'s alliance commitment, but Chu cannot easily reach [[Handan|邯郸]] without [[WeiState|魏国]] transit, and [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]] halts [[JinBi|晋鄙]] at [[YeWarringStates|邺城]]. Zhao Dan's crisis therefore shifts from "find an ally" to "make allied help physically and politically arrive."

The episode then brings [[XinyuanYan|新垣衍]]'s proposal to Zhao Dan through [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]]: honor [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] as western emperor to induce Qin withdrawal. The source frames this as a near-submission choice forced by failed relief, putting Zhao Dan between resistance at annihilation risk and [[SubmissionThroughStatusElevation|status concession]] at legitimacy cost.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-3-lj-cr4vg4e1qdfjs6yez85-3zuiv]] keeps Zhao Dan in that dilemma but adds [[LuZhonglian|鲁仲连]] as the counterweight to status submission. Lu Zhonglian's rebuttal gives Zhao's side an argument for refusing the western-emperor plan even before rescue has actually arrived: recognizing Qin's superior order may save the moment while exposing Zhao and Wei to deeper dependency.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-2-jiemi-xinlingjun-ping-shenme-shi-si-gongzi-zhi-shou-lodqntthbil9o8ezppt66lywzd-f]] shows Zhao Dan after survival as a grateful ruler trying not to mis-handle the rescuer. He consults [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] about rewarding [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] with five cities, receives him through a high guest ritual, and later considers giving Haocheng as a [[TangmuYi|汤沐邑]]. Xinlingjun's refusal of excessive status lets Zhao Dan express gratitude without pushing the Wei prince into open parity with the Zhao throne.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-3-xunzi-bingfa-li-cangzhe-renxing-de-mimi-lgfcp4hkruyhhrjguqhjbhllvwp43]] adds Zhao Dan as the listening ruler in [[Xunzi|荀子]]'s debate with [[LinwuJun|临武君]]. The episode does not make Zhao Dan the decision-maker in the argument; his function is to host a military-theory contrast after Zhao has already lived through Changping, Handan, and rescue politics. The source therefore makes his court a stage for asking whether Zhao's survival problem should be understood through tactical skill, incentive systems, or deeper [[PeopleBasedPoliticalSecurity|people-based alignment]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-100-2-liuguo-jingran-tongshi-wei-ta-diaosang-ln6-5v-b-ybqmf5ucepusdo3lf68]] returns Zhao Dan to active crisis management after [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] dies. [[YanWangXi|燕王喜]] and [[LiFuWarringStates|栗腹]] treat the losses from [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] as proof that Zhao can be attacked, but Zhao Dan appoints [[LianPo|廉颇]], whose victory in the [[HaodaiBattle|鄗代之战]] shows that Zhao's post-disaster military capacity is not exhausted.
