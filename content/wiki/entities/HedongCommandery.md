# 河东郡 / Hedong Commandery

## Overview

河东郡 / Hedong Commandery is a Yellow River commandery whose wiki profile spans conquest administration, late-Han rebellion and court flight, and [[DuJiLateHan|杜畿]]'s political entry, defense, and conversion of a contested transport and resource hub into a prosperous Cao-side base.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-155-2-zhuan-gei-ni-piqi-da-de-pengyou-weiwang-panhan-jing-shi-liubang-ai-maren-ls5nvunmhakvefd2oxn4zdyoob4l|Hanji 155 part 2]] gives the early administrative layer. After [[HanXin|韩信]] defeats [[WeiBao|魏豹]], Han converts former Wei territory into Hedong, [[Shangdang|上党郡]], and [[TaiyuanWarringStates|太原郡]]. Hedong therefore begins in this source set as a case of converting military conquest into commandery government.

The late-Han sources show recurring exposure to disorder. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-939-donghan-luanju-de-fengkuang-sanguo-xuqu-lthmvw8ihk96fuez9vamlryzcngy|Hanji 939]] has [[GuoDaLateHan|郭大]] and the [[BaiboArmy|白波军]] attack Hedong and Taiyuan in 188 CE. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-948-dongzhuo-feidi-quanchen-ruhe-gaibian-huangquan-youxi-ltekg1a-5b5u37aeekjm3dm2m6kk|Hanji 948]] returns to Baibo pressure after Dong Zhuo's court seizure and sends [[NiuFuLateHan|牛辅]] against it. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] then makes [[Anyi|安邑]] a refuge for [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]: the governor supplies cloth to an impoverished court whose ritual titles survive after material state capacity has collapsed.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1071-danqiang-pima-chuang-hedong-zhe-wei-mingchen-you-duo-niu-lnfo6i2lx7nmc1mrojfkqic7zlfk|Hanji 1071]] makes the commandery a problem of civilian alignment as well as geography. Du Ji argues that a punitive army could drive undecided households into [[WeiGuLateHan|卫固]]'s camp. His solo entry, concessions to Wei Gu and [[FanXianLateHan|范先]], and recruitment-plus-leave rules disperse local armed capacity while creating the support network later visible outside the commandery seat.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1072-duji-danqi-shou-hedong-jing-shi-baixing-yuan-wei-ta-fusi-ltiuwt7v-rjcjpjpovqf9ffl88hu|Hanji 1072]] supplies the mature strategic profile. The episode presents Hedong as a salt-and-iron region with Yellow River crossings between Guanzhong and the Central Plains and military value against threats from Bingzhou and the north. Du Ji's county defenses and grain denial keep a rebel coalition from consolidating; his long governorship then combines farming, sericulture, schooling, fortification, arms, and training to make the commandery both wealthy and defensible.

## Key Characteristics

- Administrative unit created from conquered former Wei territory in the Chu-Han transition.
- Repeated late-Han target of Baibo and wider frontier-adjacent disorder.
- Refuge and material support point for Emperor Xian's impoverished mobile court.
- Yellow River crossing and Guanzhong-Central Plains transport hub in Hanji 1072's account.
- Salt-and-iron resource base whose value extends beyond local taxation.
- Contested local field entered through civilian-risk restraint and local-network dispersal, then held through county defense, food denial, and outside reinforcement.
- Long-term governance case joining livelihood, education, fortification, and military preparation.

## Evidence

- Administrative origin: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-155-2-zhuan-gei-ni-piqi-da-de-pengyou-weiwang-panhan-jing-shi-liubang-ai-maren-ls5nvunmhakvefd2oxn4zdyoob4l|Hanji 155 part 2]] says Han creates Hedong after Han Xin defeats Wei Bao.
- Recurring insurgent pressure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-939-donghan-luanju-de-fengkuang-sanguo-xuqu-lthmvw8ihk96fuez9vamlryzcngy|Hanji 939]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-948-dongzhuo-feidi-quanchen-ruhe-gaibian-huangquan-youxi-ltekg1a-5b5u37aeekjm3dm2m6kk|Hanji 948]] place Baibo attacks and Niu Fu's response in the commandery.
- Court-refuge function: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] says the Hedong governor supplies the emperor's Anyi court with cloth amid severe material collapse.
- Political entry and local alignment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1071-danqiang-pima-chuang-hedong-zhe-wei-mingchen-you-duo-niu-lnfo6i2lx7nmc1mrojfkqic7zlfk|Hanji 1071]] says Du Ji avoids mass attack, enters alone, and uses office grants, recruitment, and leave to separate neutral or reliable households from the armed local bloc.
- Strategic geography and resources: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1072-duji-danqi-shou-hedong-jing-shi-baixing-yuan-wei-ta-fusi-ltiuwt7v-rjcjpjpovqf9ffl88hu|Hanji 1072]] identifies salt, iron, river crossings, interregional connection, and frontier defense as the commandery's strategic value.
- Resilience and settlement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1072-duji-danqi-shou-hedong-jing-shi-baixing-yuan-wei-ta-fusi-ltiuwt7v-rjcjpjpovqf9ffl88hu|Hanji 1072]] attributes county defense, grain denial, prosperity, education, fortification, arms production, and training to Du Ji's rule.

## Qualifications

This page combines episodes separated by centuries and does not present a continuous institutional history of the commandery. Hanji 1071's household count, routes, offices, force numbers, and personnel-sorting intent remain source-scoped, as do Hanji 1072's salt-and-iron scope, crossing geography, strategic superlatives, and performance rankings. The source set shows recurring value and vulnerability but does not prove unchanged boundaries or resource regimes.

## What Changed

- Added Du Ji's political-entry and pre-revolt capacity-dispersal phase.
- Connected avoidance of mass coercion to the county support and defense visible in the later outcome.

## Relationships

- [[WeiBao|魏豹]] and [[HanXin|韩信]] - defeated ruler and conquering commander behind the commandery's early administrative origin.
- [[BaiboArmy|白波军]] and [[NiuFuLateHan|牛辅]] - recurring late-Han insurgent pressure and response.
- [[Anyi|安邑]] and [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] - refuge city and mobile court supported from Hedong.
- [[DuJiLateHan|杜畿]] - governor who holds and develops the commandery in Hanji 1072.
- [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]], [[Shangdang|上党]], and [[BingzhouLateHan|并州]] - neighboring strategic fields connected by Hedong's routes and defenses.
- [[TransitHubCommanderyControl|交通枢纽郡控制]] - concept explaining why Hedong's administration has interregional consequences.
- [[DistributedLocalResilience|分布式地方韧性]] - mechanism through which county defense and food denial preserve the wider region.
- [[LayeredLocalEliteDemobilization|分层式地方武力拆解]] - mechanism through which armed local capacity is dispersed before open revolt.
