# 河东郡 / Hedong Commandery

河东郡 / Hedong Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-155-2-zhuan-gei-ni-piqi-da-de-pengyou-weiwang-panhan-jing-shi-liubang-ai-maren-ls5nvunmhakvefd2oxn4zdyoob4l|Hanji 155-2]] as one of the commanderies created after [[HanXin|韩信]] defeats [[WeiBao|魏豹]]. The source groups it with [[Shangdang|上党郡]] and [[TaiyuanWarringStates|太原郡]] as the administrative result of pacifying Wei territory.

The page keeps Hedong in the Chu-Han transition rather than treating it as a full institutional history. Its relevance is that Liu Bang's side does not merely capture Wei Bao; it converts the conquered territory into governable commandery units under [[ConquestCommanderyTransition|灭国后的郡县化过渡]].
