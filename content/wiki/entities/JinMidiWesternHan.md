## Overview

金日磾 / Jin Midi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-394-1-guren-ruhe-jiao-haizi-kankan-junbuyi-de-chengzhang-gushi-lrlotzt34bbp3jahjwmzygxuzb_z|Hanji 394-1]] as a Xiongnu-born Western Han minister whose death, marquisate, funeral honors, and descendants are used to extend the episode's family-education theme from [[JunBuyiWesternHan|隽不疑]] to household discipline and political caution.

## Current Profile

The source presents Jin Midi as a rare case of foreign-origin service being fully honored inside the Western Han court. [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] had ordered that he be enfeoffed as Du Hou, but Jin initially refused because [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]] was still young and because standing out too much was dangerous for a Xiongnu-born official. When he became seriously ill, [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] requested the title again and Jin accepted, which the episode reads as a choice for his sons' inheritance rather than a sudden appetite for rank.

His profile is therefore defined by restraint, family continuity, and institutionally bounded favor. Han Zhao personally wants both Jin sons to receive special marquis-level treatment, but Huo Guang answers that only one son can inherit and that marquisate should follow prior rules and state merit. Jin's posthumous honors remain extraordinary: the episode says he receives military-style funeral treatment and burial honor near the imperial mausoleum, placing him with a very small group of highly honored Han Wudi-era ministers. The closing link to Jin's mother and her strict discipline makes family education part of the source's explanation for the Jin household's later caution.

## Key Characteristics

- Xiongnu-born Western Han minister whose Han-court service is presented as unusually successful and honored.
- Cautious official who refuses an early marquisate because accepting high rank under a young emperor could make him too conspicuous.
- Father who accepts sickbed enfeoffment so the title can pass to his sons.
- Posthumously honored figure whose funeral treatment marks exceptional court recognition.
- Household model whose descendants are said to preserve cautious, low-profile conduct into the Wang Mang period.
- Family-discipline example paired with Jun Buyi's mother inside the episode's parenting frame.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 394-1. It does not reconstruct Jin Midi's full early life, the complete story of his Xiongnu origin, the full legal form of Du Hou inheritance, the exact funeral protocol, the complete record of his sons, or the later Jin lineage under Wang Mang. The source's claim about motives is interpretive: Jin's refusal and later acceptance are read through caution and family continuity rather than proven from private statements.
