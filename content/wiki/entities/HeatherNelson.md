# Heather Nelson

## Overview

Heather Nelson enters the wiki as the [[BostonChildrensHospital|Boston Children's Hospital]] technology leader who connects patient access, mobile clinical reliability, cybersecurity, home care, and the changing CIO role.

## Current Profile

Nelson argues that healthcare IT should function as a strategic business partner rather than an order-taking support unit. Her examples translate infrastructure into service consequences: dropped calls during clinical movement, thousands of connected endpoints, patient-data exposure, and the dependence of visible clinical equipment on less visible technical foundations. She also describes patient self-service, home-based care, and ambient documentation as directions for reducing friction, while the sponsored source does not independently establish their outcomes.

## Key Characteristics

- Frames technology strategy around patient access, control, continuity, and care experience.
- Treats dependable connectivity as clinical infrastructure.
- Connects private-network design to latency, capacity, reliability, and security requirements.
- Presents CIO leadership as organizational influence and financial storytelling as well as technical expertise.
- Uses concrete service failures to explain the value of infrastructure investment.
