# Yangtze Memory Technologies / 长江存储

## Overview
Yangtze Memory Technologies is a Chinese NAND supplier whose domestic storage role is now being tested by reported movement between historically separate NAND and DRAM competitors.

## Current Profile
Yangtze Memory remains the better-established NAND-side company in the available sources, with products described as widely sold domestically. The latest episode says [[ChangXinMemory|ChangXin Memory]] is preparing to enter NAND while Yangtze Memory is also exploring the other's historical segment, turning a complementary domestic division into potential direct competition.

## Key Characteristics
- Is primarily identified with NAND storage rather than DRAM.
- Supplies the domestic Chinese storage market.
- Participates in an AI-era market where NAND supports persistent and recoverable workloads.
- Faces reported cross-segment competition from ChangXin Memory.

## Evidence
- NAND role and market context: [[cunchu-sanjutou-po-wanyi-shizhi-cunchu-chaoji-zhouqi-heshi-neng-jianding-s10e13-c47ff830-8cb5-4e58-b7d7-1a04e4e5a4c1]] identifies Yangtze Memory as the domestic NAND-side supplier within the broader memory hierarchy.
- Competitive shift: [[jinghu-gaotie-zhongqiujie-qian-chuxian-jiangjia-xinbailun-qisu-dikanong-qinquan-1017386449]] reports ChangXin's NAND plan and says both companies have begun exploring the other's established segment.

## Qualifications
The sources do not provide detailed capacity, process, yield, market share, or cross-segment production evidence. Reported exploration should not be treated as successful commercial entry.

## What Changed
- Added direct competitive pressure from ChangXin's reported NAND expansion.
- Qualified the earlier clean division between domestic DRAM and NAND specialists.
