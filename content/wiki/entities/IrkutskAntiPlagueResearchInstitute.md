# Irkutsk Anti-Plague Research Institute

## Overview
The Irkutsk Anti-Plague Research Institute is a Russian pathogen-research institution founded in 1934 and represented here through uncertainty surrounding the death of researcher [[DariaShepolova]].

## Current Profile
The institute studies plague and other pathogens. Its public significance in the episode comes not from a confirmed outbreak or weapons role but from the mismatch between sparse official information and reported precautionary measures after Shepolova's death.

The episode states that there is no current evidence tying the institute to Russia's military biological-weapons program, while noting suggestions of a Cold War connection. Russia's broader history of secret biological-weapons work makes transparency especially important, but institutional history cannot substitute for evidence about this case.

## Key Characteristics
- Specialized Russian institute researching plague and other pathogens.
- Founded in 1934.
- Workplace of researcher Daria Shepolova before her unexplained death.
- Subject to heightened scrutiny because official disclosure did not fully explain reported precautions.
- Not currently shown by the supplied source to be part of a military biological-weapons program.
