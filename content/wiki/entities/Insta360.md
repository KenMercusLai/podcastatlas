# Insta360 / 影石

## Overview
Insta360 / 影石 is a Chinese consumer-imaging company represented through 360-degree cameras, action cameras, creator software, and a move toward panoramic drones. Its distinct category contribution is reducing capture-time framing through [[CaptureNowReframeLater|capture now, reframe later]].

## Current Profile
The earlier market sources position Insta360 as [[DJI]]'s strongest portable-camera competitor and the leading 360-degree camera company. Its workflow captures a broad scene first and moves framing into editing, trading more post-production for less on-site composition pressure. A later business-news episode adds the X6 release, a lower-priced DJI response, faster reported panoramic-camera growth, and a source-scoped claim that Insta360 held close to 70% of first-quarter global shipments.

The Liu Jingkang interview adds the company's internal development arc. The business moved from a VR-content thesis toward action and creator workflows, learned [[HardwarePrototypeToProductionGap|the gap between demos and production]], built market education through creators and communities, and adopted AI in automatic editing. Its proposed panoramic-drone expansion is framed through [[MissionLedCategoryAdjacency]]: solve another recording-and-sharing problem while accepting competition, long development cycles, litigation, capital intensity, and public-company obligations.

## Key Characteristics
- Category leader associated with consumer 360-degree cameras and deferred software reframing.
- Hardware-software company whose value depends on capture, stitching, stabilization, editing, export, and sharing as one workflow.
- Global consumer-hardware operator shaped by market education, creators, agencies, distributors, communities, and supply chains.
- Competitor to DJI and GoPro across panoramic, action, and portable creator-camera categories.
- Company expanding from handheld imaging toward panoramic drones and more automated capture.
- Public company whose founder frames creative ambition as bounded by commercialization, reliability, survival, and stakeholder duties.
