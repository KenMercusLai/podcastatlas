# Vingeru Makandewiri

Vingeru Makandewiri appears in [[burning-questions-a-more-fire-prone-world-6a687ea64207b2a60119df8b]] as the [[TheIntelligence]] speaker explaining why women's workplace progress may be stalling. Her segment connects lower female executive appointments, fewer board seats, lower promotion ambition, full-time participation decline, widening gender pay gaps, childcare problems, and DEI rollback into [[WomenWorkplaceProgressStall]].

The source uses her less as a profile subject than as an interpreter of a labor-market reversal. Her key contribution is to make [[PromotionAmbitionGap]] and [[ChildcareInfrastructureConstraint]] part of the same story: women can remain educated and capable while finding the available promotion ladder less worth climbing.
