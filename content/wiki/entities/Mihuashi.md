# 米画师 / Mihuashi

米画师 / Mihuashi is the art-commission platform named by [[WangYiranSanlian|王依然]] in [[ep242-duli-youxi-shi-yitiao-gaoqian-hao-saidao-ma-lrqbzrceumbuggzuero5qb4jv3zu]]. The episode places it inside [[OriginalCharacterCommissionEconomy|设圈约稿经济]], where people commission character settings, independent-game art, commercial marks, and identity-linked visual assets.

Mihuashi matters because it makes the creator-economy branch more concrete. The source is not only about selling finished media products on [[Steam]]; it also shows a services marketplace where artists monetize craft, reputation, and style one commission at a time.
