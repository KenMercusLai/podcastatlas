## Overview

王忠 / Wang Zhong is treated in the wiki as the Shule royal figure whose arc begins with [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]]'s 74 CE installation of a locally legitimate Shule successor and later turns into a Shache-backed defection crisis. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-743-chuo-ren-xinwo-de-lishi-shunjian-mingdi-liuzhuang-de-wenqing-meng-lgwtuqtug5bh90qohwiskccx77hy|Hanji 743]] names the installed figure as Zhong, the previous Shule king's brother's son; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-754-gudai-ban-qieting-fengyun-jieju-shuangwen-ltvymh-2r-ic5diqfh-2ic9cudq2|Hanji 754]] later names Wang Zhong as the Shule king whose defection forces Ban Chao to manage succession, coercion, and external mediation.

## Current Profile

Hanji 743 provides the origin layer. Qiuci king Jian, backed by Xiongnu power, kills or removes the Shule king and installs Duti, a non-Shule subject, in the city of Pantuo. Ban Chao sends Tian Lu to arrest Duti, gathers Shule officials, denounces Qiuci's interference, and installs Zhong from the previous Shule royal line. He then releases Duti instead of killing him, using mercy to display Han authority to Qiuci.

Hanji 754 supplies the later defection layer. Emperor Zhang sends an officer with reinforcements to Ban Chao, who raises Shule troops to attack Shache. Shache bribes Wang Zhong, and Wang Zhong defects, occupying Wujicheng.

Ban Chao responds by installing a Shule `fu-cheng` as king, attacking Wang Zhong with Shule troops that had not defected, and arranging for the Kangju king to seize and return Wang Zhong. Wujicheng then surrenders. Wang Zhong's profile is therefore a local ruler whose legitimacy helps Ban Chao reverse Qiuci-backed intrusion, but whose later defection becomes a test of Ban Chao's ability to split local alignments and restore control without relying only on direct Han force.

## Key Characteristics

- Shule royal-line figure installed by Ban Chao after a Qiuci-backed usurpation.
- Local-legitimacy counterweight to Duti, who is presented as a non-Shule Qiuci appointee.
- Later Shule king whose loyalty is broken by Shache bribery during Ban Chao's campaign.
- Occupier of Wujicheng after defecting from Ban Chao's side.
- Defection target neutralized through replacement kingship, loyal Shule troops, and Kangju-mediated seizure.
- Kept distinct from other Wang Zhong figures by the `ShuleKing` route.

## Qualifications

This page cautiously connects Hanji 743's Zhong and Hanji 754's Wang Zhong through the Shule-king route and source sequence, but does not reconstruct a full genealogy beyond each source's wording. It does not create standalone Shache, Kangju, Duti, Tian Lu, Pantuo, or Wujicheng pages from these episodes, and it does not merge Wang Zhong with [[WangZhongLateHan|王忠 / Wang Zhong (late Han)]], a different later figure.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 743 as the origin layer for Ban Chao's installation of Zhong after Qiuci-backed Duti.
- Reframed Wang Zhong's later defection as a reversal of a legitimacy-restoration arrangement rather than an isolated local betrayal.
- Preserved disambiguation from the later Wang Zhong route.
