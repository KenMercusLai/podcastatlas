## Overview

王忠 / Wang Zhong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-754-gudai-ban-qieting-fengyun-jieju-shuangwen-ltvymh-2r-ic5diqfh-2ic9cudq2|Hanji 754]] as the [[ShuleKingdom|Shule]] king whose Shache-backed defection forces [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]] to manage succession, coercion, and external mediation in the Western Regions.

## Current Profile

The episode says Emperor Zhang sends an officer with reinforcements to Ban Chao, who raises Shule troops to attack Shache. Shache then bribes Wang Zhong, and Wang Zhong defects, occupying Wujicheng.

Ban Chao responds by installing a Shule `fu-cheng` as king, attacking Wang Zhong with Shule troops that had not defected, and arranging for the Kangju king to seize and return Wang Zhong. Wujicheng then surrenders. Wang Zhong's profile is therefore a local ruler whose defection becomes a test of Ban Chao's ability to split local alignments and restore control without relying only on direct Han force.

## Key Characteristics

- Shule king in the early Ban Chao Western Regions layer.
- Local ruler whose loyalty is broken by Shache bribery during Ban Chao's campaign.
- Occupier of Wujicheng after defecting from Ban Chao's side.
- Defection target neutralized through replacement kingship, loyal Shule troops, and Kangju-mediated seizure.
- Kept distinct from other Wang Zhong figures by the `ShuleKing` route.
