# Amer Sports / 亚玛芬体育

## Overview
Amer Sports / 亚玛芬体育 is represented as a specialist sports-brand group whose China growth and portfolio logic are visible through [[Arcteryx|Arc'teryx]] and [[Salomon]].

## Current Profile
[[lanjian-hangtian-wancheng-zhongguo-shouci-ludi-huojian-huishou-yushu-keji-shizhi-chaoguo-3000-yi-1007302506]] supplies the current premium-outdoor growth snapshot: Amer Sports' reported results and Arc'teryx store expansion suggest continued China demand after the fastest breakout stage. [[vol-274-chengwei-saluomeng-1013268300]] adds corporate history and a second operating case. It describes Salomon as the group's largest revenue contributor before the Anta-led acquisition and shows how a scaled European specialist brand can still need recognition, localization, direct retail, and lifestyle translation in China and North America.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates a portfolio of distinct specialist sports and outdoor brands.
- Uses Arc'teryx and Salomon to address different premium outdoor, technical, and lifestyle positions.
- Treats China as both a growth market and a brand-education problem.
- Combines group ownership with continued brand-specific product and market narratives.
- Sits inside the investor consortium led by [[AntaSports|Anta]] in the source's ownership account.

## Evidence
- **Premium-outdoor growth:** [[lanjian-hangtian-wancheng-zhongguo-shouci-ludi-huojian-huishou-yushu-keji-shizhi-chaoguo-3000-yi-1007302506]] reports strong quarterly growth and continued Arc'teryx store expansion in Greater China.
- **Salomon scale:** [[vol-274-chengwei-saluomeng-1013268300]] says Salomon generated about EUR 900 million in 2017 and more than one-third of Amer Sports revenue before the Anta-led acquisition.
- **Portfolio history:** [[vol-274-chengwei-saluomeng-1013268300]] traces Salomon and related assets from [[Adidas]] into Amer Sports in 2005.
- **Localization:** [[vol-274-chengwei-saluomeng-1013268300]] presents China and North America as recognition markets requiring direct retail, local expression, and lifestyle translation.

## Qualifications
Both sources are podcast summaries rather than full corporate filings. Revenue, margin, growth, ownership contribution, store-count, and market-recognition claims remain source-scoped. Arc'teryx and Salomon do not establish the performance of every Amer Sports brand, and strong China retail expansion does not by itself prove durable portfolio economics.
