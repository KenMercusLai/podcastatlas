# Wheeler and Wilson

Wheeler and Wilson appears in [[the-invention-invention]] as another sewing-machine company involved in the overlapping patent conflict. The source names it alongside [[GroverAndBaker]], [[IsaacMerrittSinger|Singer]], and [[EliasHoweJr|Howe]] as part of the dispute that made normal production difficult.

The company became a participant in the [[SewingMachineCombination]], the episode's first major [[PatentPool]] example.
