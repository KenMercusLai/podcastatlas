## Overview

孔光 / Kong Guang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-624-du-lishi-wu-xingfu-ni-shi-rensheng-yingjia-haishi-yongren-zirao-daibiao-lpavicltc7nducbxiqgpr6t7iczd|Hanji 624]] as the Taishi and Fushan marquis whose death marks a high-office transition in [[HanPingdi|Han Pingdi]]'s 5 CE court. The episode uses him mainly as a chronological hinge before [[MaGongWesternHan|Ma Gong]] succeeds to the Taishi post.

## Current Profile

The source says Kong Guang dies on the first day of the fourth lunar month in 5 CE. The court grants him a rich funeral package, and the episode emphasizes the large burial procession by noting that more than ten thousand vehicles attend. Soon afterward, Ma Gong is appointed Taishi to replace him.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Taishi active in Han Pingdi's late court.
- Holder of the Fushan marquis title in this source.
- Receives unusually grand funeral treatment after death.
- Office-transition figure before Ma Gong's appointment as Taishi.
