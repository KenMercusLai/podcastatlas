## Overview

孔光 / Kong Guang is synthesized as a Western Han senior official whose late-career posture shows both participation in and fear of [[WangMang|王莽]]'s ascent. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-606-duan-diao-qin-shushu-wangmang-de-xinji-you-duo-shen-lswummzfg74d-_dm_k4vc7fg8eut|Hanji 606]] backfills his earlier role as the formal impeachment proxy against [[WangLiWesternHan|王立]], while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-621-diwang-xinshu-gongxin-zui-zhongyao-lgo01rnxby9ynyyzanljhtrawjck|Hanji 621]] later presents him as a frightened senior official trying to withdraw. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-624-du-lishi-wu-xingfu-ni-shi-rensheng-yingjia-haishi-yongren-zirao-daibiao-lpavicltc7nducbxiqgpr6t7iczd|Hanji 624]] records his 5 CE death and the high-office transition to [[MaGongWesternHan|Ma Gong]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 606 gives Kong Guang an earlier and more compromised role. Wang Mang does not directly attack Wang Li because Wang Li is his uncle and because direct accusation would damage Wang Mang's upright persona. Kong Guang therefore becomes the formal channel: he impeaches Wang Li over old ties to Chunyuzhang and over the Yang Ji heir proposal, giving Wang Mang a procedurally respectable opening before Wang Mang persuades Wang Zhengjun to send Wang Li away. The same source closes by saying Kong Guang recognizes Wang Mang's ruthlessness and understands that continued office leaves him acting like a puppet.

Hanji 621 says Kong Guang later has already tried unsuccessfully to resign, and that the court's praise-heavy Wang Mang atmosphere makes him increasingly afraid. He uses illness to insist on leaving regular office duties. [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] cannot keep him fully in office, so the settlement reduces his obligations: he no longer has to attend ordinary court meetings, enters the palace every ten days, receives retirement implements and food gifts, and leaves Taishi-subordinate business running.

Hanji 624 then supplies the endpoint. Kong Guang dies on the first day of the fourth lunar month in 5 CE. The court grants him a rich funeral package, and the episode emphasizes the large burial procession by noting that more than ten thousand vehicles attend. Soon afterward, Ma Gong is appointed Taishi to replace him.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Taishi active in Han Pingdi's late court.
- Holder of the Fushan marquis title in this source.
- Formal impeachment proxy used in Wang Mang's removal of Wang Li.
- Senior official who becomes aware that continued service means instrumentalization by Wang Mang.
- Senior official whose illness-based withdrawal reflects fear under Wang Mang's praise-heavy power environment.
- Semi-retired court figure whose status is honored while ordinary attendance is reduced.
- Receives unusually grand funeral treatment after death.

## Qualifications

The sources do not provide Kong Guang's full earlier career or a direct statement of his policy position toward Wang Mang. Hanji 606 supports his impeachment role and the episode's puppet-like interpretation, but his degree of consent, private motive, and independent judgment of Wang Li's guilt remain source-scoped. Hanji 621 supports fear, illness-based withdrawal, and reduced attendance, but Kong Guang's inner calculation, the exact legal status of his semi-retirement, and the operation of the Taishi office after withdrawal remain source-scoped. Hanji 624 supports death, funeral scale, and succession by Ma Gong but does not explain why Ma Gong is selected.
