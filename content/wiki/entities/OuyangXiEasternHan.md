## Overview

欧阳歙 / Ouyang Xi is currently synthesized as a high-status Eastern Han official whose appointment and fall make [[DutianAccountabilityEasternHan|degree-field accountability]] concrete. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-716-luanshi-zhizhong-lvfa-you-duo-zhongyao-lqxuinbrl-rop8d9yxgwx9z4fr4i|Hanji 716]] records Liu Xiu appointing him as Da Situ in 39 CE after Han Xin's removal, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-717-13-ge-zi-yinfa-de-diwang-zhinu-liz3jsbvw3snfapzsrqw917fqv7v|Hanji 717]] presents him as a Shangshu-lineage scholar-official, former Runan taishou, and Situ who is jailed for inaccurate land measurement and large embezzlement, then dies in prison despite mass petitioning.

## Current Profile

Ouyang Xi's profile combines high appointment, cultural prestige, and administrative liability. Hanji 716 shows his appointment as Da Situ after an anomalous-star notice and after Han Xin's removal. Hanji 717 then says his family transmitted Shangshu learning for generations and had many disciples, giving him unusual social and scholarly capital. He also held major offices under the late Xin/Gengshi/Eastern Han transition before reaching Situ.

His fall is framed through earlier Runan service. The charge is not merely vague corruption: it ties inaccurate degree-field reporting to embezzlement of a very large sum. More than a thousand people petition for him, and Li Zhen offers to die in his place, but [[LiuXiu|Liu Xiu]] does not relent. The durable wiki claim is that Ouyang Xi becomes a visible high-status punishment signal inside Guangwu's fiscal-administrative cleanup.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han Situ appointed in the 39 CE sequence after Han Xin's removal.
- High official whose previous local service becomes the basis for a later accountability case.
- Shangshu-lineage official with many disciples and enough prestige to draw large-scale petitions.
- Former Runan taishou accused of inaccurate degree-field reporting and major embezzlement.
- High-status punishment target whom Liu Xiu refuses to spare despite emotional and scholarly pressure.
- Evidence point for the claim that fiscal enforcement could override classical reputation and office rank.

## Qualifications

The current page is bounded to Hanji 716 and Hanji 717. Hanji 716 supplies the appointment notice but does not explain why Ouyang Xi was selected for Situ. Hanji 717 does not yet reconstruct his full earlier career, the legal record behind the embezzlement amount, or whether petitioners disputed guilt or pleaded for mercy despite guilt. The host's harsh moral judgment is kept as source interpretation; the canonical claim is narrower, concerning appointment, office rank, scholarly prestige, degree-field falsification, and punishment.
