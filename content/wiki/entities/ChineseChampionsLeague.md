## Overview
中国足球协会会员协会冠军联赛 / Chinese Champions League is the lower-tier entry layer discussed in the episode as a weak link in Chinese football's competition pyramid because many clubs play too few meaningful matches.

## Current Profile
The source frames the Chinese Champions League as part of China's unusual football base, alongside city leagues and lower professional tiers. Its key problem is match scarcity: teams may organize for a season but play too few games to build players, community habit, operations, or durable promotion credentials.

## Key Characteristics
- Lower-tier route connecting city or amateur football with the professional pyramid.
- Criticized for insufficient annual match supply and excessive randomness.
- Needs home-ground and weekly-competition structures to build club-city connection.
