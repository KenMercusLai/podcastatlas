# National Healthcare Security Administration / 国家医保局

The National Healthcare Security Administration / 国家医保局 appears in [[130-shui-cai-shi-shengwu-yiyao-hangye-fazhan-de-hexin-qudongli-jiantan-chuangxinyao-chanyelian-de-jiaose-fengong-lnau0sacx1jm1t-2locdc5nszakh]] as the payer-side actor in China's innovation-drug ecosystem.
