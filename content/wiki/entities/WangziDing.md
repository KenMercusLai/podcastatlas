# 王子定 / Prince Ding

王子定 / Prince Ding appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-1-lvkp-ju7pf5z5cz3thojv3s2fkyx]] as a [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]] prince who flees to [[JinState|晋国]] in 399 BCE. The episode uses his flight to explain the difference between 太子 and 王子 and to show how aristocratic families handled internal power struggle through outside refuge.

The source says royal and princely exiles often brought retainers with them and could be treated well by host states because noble blood, kinship ties, and the possibility of future restoration still mattered.
