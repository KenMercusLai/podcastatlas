# Battle of Ilipa / 伊利帕战役

## Overview

The Battle of Ilipa / 伊利帕战役 is [[ScipioAfricanus]]'s decisive 206 BC victory over the remaining Carthaginian field army in Iberia, commanded by [[MagoBarca]] and supported by [[Massinissa]].

## Current Profile

The episode treats Ilipa as the culmination of Scipio's Iberian campaign. Repeated Roman drilling improves cavalry performance and supports a battle plan in which Scipio eventually outflanks and envelops the Carthaginian army.

The result removes the last effective Carthaginian field force from Iberia. Mago survives and continues fighting elsewhere, but Carthage can no longer restore the Spanish resource system on which Hannibal's war depends. Massinissa's subsequent approach to Scipio shows how battlefield outcomes alter coalition choices as well as territory.

## Key Characteristics

- Culminates Scipio's campaign against Carthaginian power in Iberia.
- Reflects prior drilling and improved Roman cavalry performance.
- Uses outflanking and envelopment against Mago's army.
- Removes effective Carthaginian field capacity from Spain.
- Precedes Massinissa's transfer of allegiance to Rome.

## Qualifications

The episode compresses the battle and attributes the surviving Carthaginian force primarily to Mago. Exact command structure, army composition, drilling effects, tactical sequence, losses, chronology, and Massinissa's motives remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Created the decisive-battle node for Rome's removal of Carthaginian field power from Iberia.
- Connected tactical preparation to the postbattle realignment of local allies.
