# 苏见信（信） / Shin

## Overview
苏见信, known professionally as 信, is a singer represented in the wiki through one [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] interview about cooking, exercise, music, travel, art, optimism, and preparation for the “尽兴而活” concert tour.

## Current Profile
The source presents him less as a health authority than as a working musician who has built personal routines around food, training, recovery, and curiosity. He describes reducing sugar, oil, and salt while preserving taste; beginning exercise at home with deliberately tiny sets; progressing into a six-day gym rhythm; and limiting pre-concert overload because injury and fatigue would undermine performance.

His non-musical interests also feed his professional identity. Cooking gives daily life an artistic ritual, extended travel favors local markets and street food over brief sightseeing, and exhibitions or architecture can suggest stage ideas. Optimistic reframing and quickly releasing negative comments are described as personal coping tendencies, not clinical mental-health methods.

## Key Characteristics
- Singer who treats his person and lived style as part of his music identity.
- Uses low-resistance exercise starts and gradual progression to reduce early aversion.
- Prefers lighter seasoning and less added sugar while keeping food pleasurable.
- Connects travel, cooking, exhibitions, architecture, and ordinary listening to creative replenishment.
- Frames optimism, perspective shifts, and reduced dwelling on criticism as personal state-management habits.
- Prioritizes performance readiness and singing over adding spectacle for its own sake.
