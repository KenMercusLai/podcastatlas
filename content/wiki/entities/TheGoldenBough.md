# 《金枝》 / The Golden Bough

[[TheGoldenBough|《金枝》 / The Golden Bough]] is the [[JamesGeorgeFrazer|James George Frazer / 弗雷泽]] book invoked in [[29-qianmian-yingxiong-edipusi-zhengzai-xiaban-ji-ditie-590112891]]. The episode uses it for the idea that ancient kings, priests, or sacred figures can become ritual embodiments of fertility, seasonal force, taboo separation, sacrifice, death, and renewal.

The source treats the book as influential but methodologically risky. Its material helps connect [[HeroJourneyNarrative]] to sacrifice and rebirth, but the episode repeatedly notes that Frazer's collected examples should not be accepted as clean evidence without modern historical and anthropological caution.
