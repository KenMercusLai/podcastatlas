# Bobbie for Change

Bobbie for Change is the advocacy group mentioned near the end of [[bobbie-laura-modi-how-a-baby-formula-startup-took-market-share-from-two-industry-giants-4488842b-74d3-40ed-8776-5831c5934e69]]. [[GuyRaz]] describes it as working to influence industry policy around infant feeding.

The source names one effort: a bill that would allow women who have had breast cancer to get insurance coverage for [[InfantFormula]]. In the wiki, Bobbie for Change connects [[Bobbie]]'s brand mission to policy work around [[FormulaFeedingStigma]], healthcare coverage, and parent support.
