# 杨修 / Yang Xiu

杨修 / Yang Xiu appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1020-sanguo-baijiazi-yuanshu-ruhe-ba-hao-pai-da-de-xilan-lhwr9hfmanr-zktleufy3kvl38u0|Hanji 1020]] as the son of [[YangBiao|杨彪]] and the marriage link between the Yang family and [[YuanShu|袁术]]. The episode mentions him narrowly: Yang Xiu is said to have married Yuan Shu's daughter, making Yang Biao vulnerable when Cao Cao treats Yuan Shu's imperial claim as a court-security problem.

This page is source-scoped. It does not use the episode to narrate Yang Xiu's later career; it records only the kinship role that helps explain why Yang Biao's old-Han prestige becomes suspicious under [[CaoCao|曹操]].
