## Overview

[[YangXiu|杨修]] appears in the bounded wiki as both a politically consequential kinship link and an adviser associated with Cao Zhi's succession camp.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1020 identifies Yang Xiu as [[YangBiao|杨彪]]'s son and the husband of [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s daughter. That marriage tie helps explain why Yang Biao's old-Han prestige becomes dangerous when [[CaoCao|曹操]] treats Yuan Shu's imperial claim as a court-security problem; it does not establish wrongdoing by Yang Xiu.

Hanji 1159 places Yang Xiu with [[DingYiLateHan|丁仪]] among the people praising and supporting [[CaoZhi|曹植]] during the succession contest. The episode characterizes Cao Zhi's advisers as better at factional maneuver and literary advocacy than governing, but the supplied summary gives little direct evidence about Yang Xiu's specific conduct or coordination.

## Key Characteristics

- Son of Yang Biao and marriage link between the Yang and Yuan families.
- Kinship connection that increases Yang Biao's political exposure after Yuan Shu's imperial claim.
- Adviser associated with Cao Zhi during the Cao-family succession contest.
- Figure whose political capability is judged negatively by Hanji 1159 without detailed supporting cases.
