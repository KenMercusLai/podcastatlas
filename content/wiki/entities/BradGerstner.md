# Brad Gerstner

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-more-trillion-dollar-ipos-anthropic-3t-zucks-price-war-china-ends-open-source-trump-accounts-42041390]] adds Gerstner as the All-In guest connecting public-market access, AI company disclosure, and policy product design. He presents [[SpaceX]] as the template for a wave of trillion-dollar listings, treats possible [[Anthropic]] and [[OpenAI]] filings as revenue and margin truth tests, and promotes [[TrumpAccounts|Trump accounts]] as a way to extend equity ownership beyond already-wealthy investors.

Brad Gerstner appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-inside-the-private-stock-market-boom-spacex-anthropic-openai-the-rise-of-secondaries-41564170]] as the [[AltimeterCapital|Altimeter Capital]] investor voice presenting data on the boom in [[PrivateCompanySecondaries|private-company secondaries]]. His frame is that secondaries have moved from workaround liquidity into a major exit channel competing with IPOs and acquisitions.

In the episode, Gerstner also supplies the risk warning. He says his firm is selling into the market, argues that VCs should think about both buying and selling, and warns retail investors not to chase famous private names through high-fee vehicles or one-shot deployment. His position links democratized access to [[InvestmentRiskManagement]] rather than treating access as enough.
