## Overview

[[JinYiLateHan|金祎]] is presented as a Han loyalist involved in the failed 218 CE [[XuduRebellion218LateHan|许都之乱]] against Cao Cao's control of the imperial court.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1162 places Jin Yi among conspirators who aim to attack Wang Bi, free [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], and connect with outside military support. The profile is narrow: the source title names his death, but the supplied summary does not clearly distinguish all of his actions from those of Geng Ji, Wei Huang, Ji Ben, and Ji Ben's sons.

## Key Characteristics

- Han-loyalist conspirator opposing Cao Cao's control of the court.
- Participant in a surprise-centered plan with weak contingency depth.
- Figure whose individual role is partly obscured by collective and dramatized narration.
