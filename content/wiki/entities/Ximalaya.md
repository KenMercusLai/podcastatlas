## Overview
喜马拉雅 / Ximalaya is a Chinese audio and podcast distribution platform represented both as source provenance and as the incumbent channel in [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]]'s distribution retrospective.

## Current Profile
The platform appears as the host or distribution channel for episode audio. In the Budongqiu source, its role is mainly provenance. In the Wushicha anniversary source, it becomes part of a [[PodcastPlatformPortfolio]] case: the hosts say they relied on their existing Ximalaya subscriber advantage, were slow to join [[Xiaoyuzhou|小宇宙]], and later saw Ximalaya's emphasis shift toward knowledge payment and audiobooks while podcast discovery strengthened elsewhere.

## Key Characteristics
- Chinese audio platform used as the source's distribution context.
- Provenance marker for the episode's original audio URL.
- Incumbent platform whose accumulated audience may not transfer automatically to a newer podcast-focused surface.

## Qualifications
The sources do not establish Ximalaya's ownership, market share, full business model, recommendation system, or current strategic priorities. The knowledge-payment and audiobook comparison is a host's February 2021 retrospective and should not be treated as a current platform assessment.
