# 王洪瑞 / Wang Hongrui

## Overview
王洪瑞 / Wang Hongrui is the [[WorldFoodProgramme|WFP]] staff member interviewed in [[402-jijiang-zhongduan-de-liangshi-yuanzhu-yige-zhongguoren-zai-nansudan-kandaode-jiee-fa63b0de]] about field work, hunger, and aid cuts in [[SouthSudan|南苏丹]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Wang as a humanitarian worker moving from headquarters logistics toward field proximity. After joining WFP in September 2024 and working on supply-chain tasks in Rome, he applies for harder field postings because he wants to see recipients and program effects more directly.

His profile is also defined by the limits of personal response. In Wedweil, refugees ask him for food, money, and help retrieving a pawned WFP card, but he can mostly explain rules, listen, and carry stories outward. The source uses his discomfort to show the gap between institutional aid rules and immediate hunger.

## Key Characteristics
- WFP staff member whose role moves from headquarters supply chain to South Sudan partnership and field exposure.
- Chinese humanitarian worker whose first African field experience structures the episode's perspective.
- Witness to refugee hunger, aid-card pawning, nutrition triage, and school-meal effects.
- Reflective narrator of personal helplessness inside institutional humanitarian boundaries.
