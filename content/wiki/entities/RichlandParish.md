# Richland Parish, Louisiana

## Overview

Richland Parish is the Louisiana community used in the bounded [[AllIn|All-In]] source as the principal positive case for a negotiated [[Meta]] data-center development.

## Current Profile

Local officials describe a parish that had lost population and had average wages around $36,000-$38,000 before the project. The episode says construction activity drove unusually large sales-tax gains, increased teacher supplements, supported new local catering demand, and encouraged some residents to return. Officials also say the site uses less water than its former agricultural use and that Meta funds its own generation, grid upgrades, resilience, and storm costs.

The case matters as a counterexample to undifferentiated national backlash, but it is not a general verdict on data centers. The episode is a produced advocacy segment featuring Meta and supportive officials, with no independent budget, utility, water, employment, or opposition evidence.

## Key Characteristics

- Presented as a population-losing rural community seeking a large new economic-development project.
- Reports large construction-period sales-tax gains and teacher salary supplements.
- Uses former agricultural land, enabling a relative water-use comparison with farming.
- Is described as having project-specific generation, grid, resilience, and storm-cost protections.
- Serves as Meta's model for informed local opt-in and continuing communication.

## Evidence

- **Local fiscal effect:** [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-metas-dina-powell-mccormick-the-case-for-data-centers-backlash-ai-job-boom-metas-future-42943275]] attributes sharp sales-tax increases and large teacher checks to the local superintendent.
- **Resource and utility bargain:** [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-metas-dina-powell-mccormick-the-case-for-data-centers-backlash-ai-job-boom-metas-future-42943275]] attributes lower water use than prior farming and Meta-funded energy and grid costs to participating officials.
- **Community opportunity:** [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-metas-dina-powell-mccormick-the-case-for-data-centers-backlash-ai-job-boom-metas-future-42943275]] describes local business growth, returning residents, and new training or job paths in its produced video and official discussion.

## Qualifications

All measured benefits in the bounded input are participant-reported. Construction-period sales taxes may not predict permanent operating employment or revenue. Lower water use than former agriculture does not establish low absolute use or negligible regional stress. Project-specific cost commitments need utility and contract evidence before being generalized.

## What Changed

- Established Richland Parish as a canonical data-center community case.
- Preserved the positive local claims alongside their source and measurement limits.
