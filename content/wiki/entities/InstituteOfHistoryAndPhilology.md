# 历史语言研究所 / 史语所

历史语言研究所, or 史语所, enters the wiki through [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] as one of the four institutional peaks in [[HuangyeShangDeDashi|《荒野上的大师》]]. The episode connects it to [[FuSinian|傅斯年]], the [[Yinxu|殷墟]] excavations, and the language-fieldwork branch associated with [[ZhaoYuanren|赵元任]].

The source presents 史语所 as a place where history, archaeology, philology, and linguistics became institutionally organized. Its excavations at Anyang moved the evidentiary basis for Chinese civilization history, while its language ambitions depended on laboratories, field surveys, trained assistants, and the short windows of stability before war.

## Key Claims
- 史语所 is the episode's strongest example of [[ModernChineseAcademicInstitutionBuilding]] in humanities and social sciences.
- Its [[Yinxu|殷墟]] work turns ancient history into field evidence rather than inherited textual assertion alone.
- Its language branch shows how speech diversity can become a research object through recording and survey.
- War makes the institute's ambitions fragile, especially around laboratories, books, and provincial surveys.
