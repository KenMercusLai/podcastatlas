# Gravity-1 Rocket / 引力一号

Gravity-1, or 引力一号, is the [[Orienspace]] launch vehicle highlighted in [[173-duihua-yao-song-shenjian-dongfang-kongjian-zaichufa-tiancai-shaonian-shinian-hou-1-173-1]]. [[YaoSong]] says its successful first flight in early 2024 was the happiest moment of his Orienspace period.

The episode emphasizes both engineering and public visibility. The rocket used an unusual solid-core plus solid-booster configuration, launched at sea, and required engineering around waves, flame, ship structure, and launch-site operations. The livestream, naming, and live-performance elements make it a case for [[SpaceMissionLivestreaming]] as well as technical proof.
