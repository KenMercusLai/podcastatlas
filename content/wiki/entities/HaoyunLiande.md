# 好运连得 / Haoyun Liande

好运连得 appears in [[buai-zhibo-daihuo-de-oumei-xiaofeizhe-weihe-zai-zhejia-pingtai-shang-daba-huaqian-1007104138]] as [[Damai]] Entertainment's designer-toy brand. The source says it opened stores in Shanghai, Shenzhen, and Guangzhou, planned eight stores across six cities in the year, and aimed to make all store IP exclusive or self-developed within three to five years.

The episode frames the brand as [[Alibaba]]'s move into an IP-led designer-toy market, but it also warns that distribution, capital, and supply chain do not guarantee a hit character or durable fandom.
