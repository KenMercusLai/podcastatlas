## Overview
Smartisan is the smartphone company founded by [[LuoYonghao]] and represented in the wiki as a design- and expression-heavy route into China's smartphone war. The current sources make it both a supply-chain execution case and a later value-judgment problem for Luo.

## Current Profile
Smartisan's public force came from interaction taste, typography, industrial design, Smartisan OS, the T1, the Nut line, TNT workstation ideas, and Luo's launch-event performance. The company also functions as a hard execution lesson: product ideals did not remove yield problems, supply-chain weakness, delayed delivery, quality issues, or the difficulty of converting a strong public persona into durable handset scale.

A retrospective layer separates commercial outcome from value judgment. Luo describes Smartisan as commercially failed, but says whether it was a mistake is more complicated. His example is that Smartisan did not simply copy iPhone-like exterior trends and kept some product and aesthetic principles, while he remains unsure whether that was the right choice for investors and employees.

## Key Characteristics
- Smartphone company founded by [[LuoYonghao]].
- Design-led and launch-event-visible entrant in China's post-feature-phone handset market.
- Case where product taste and public attention did not overcome supply-chain, yield, delivery, and quality-control limits.
- Retrospective value-choice case where commercial failure is easier to state than whether the project was wrong.
