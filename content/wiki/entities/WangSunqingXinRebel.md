## Overview

王孙卿 / Wang Sunqing enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-652-wangmang-de-waike-shoushu-ltdiqldskazy-cb6igdmrv5huxm9|Hanji 652]] as a captured [[ZhaiYiRebellion|翟义起义]] leader whose body becomes the object of [[WangMang|王莽]]'s punitive anatomical experiment.

## Current Profile

Hanji 652 presents Wang Sunqing primarily through the aftermath of rebellion. He is described as one of the leaders tied to Zhai Yi's anti-Wang-Mang uprising, then as a fugitive who escapes for nine years before being captured by the [[XinDynasty|新朝]].

The episode's profile is less biographical than symbolic. Wang Mang has palace medical personnel, medical-store officials, and a skilled butcher dissect Wang Sunqing to observe organs and channels, with the stated claim that records could aid later healing. The host rejects a simple scientific-progress reading and treats the event as punishment, revenge, and intimidation aimed at living witnesses.

## Key Characteristics

- Anti-Xin rebel leader associated with the Zhai Yi uprising.
- Long-term fugitive whose nine-year escape makes his capture politically charged.
- Victim of Wang Mang's medicalized punitive violence.
- Source figure through whom anatomy, punishment, and political terror are linked.
- Biographical detail remains narrow outside the capture and dissection episode.

- Dissection scene: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-652-wangmang-de-waike-shoushu-ltdiqldskazy-cb6igdmrv5huxm9|Hanji 652]] says Wang Mang ordered medical officials and a butcher to dissect him, inspect organs and channels, and record findings.

## Qualifications

The episode preserves but does not confirm the conjecture that Wang Sunqing may have been dissected alive. The current wiki profile should therefore avoid treating live dissection as settled fact. His full family background, offices, capture route, and role inside Zhai Yi's command remain source-scoped.
