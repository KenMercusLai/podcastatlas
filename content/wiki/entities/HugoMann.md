# Hugo Mann

Hugo Mann is the German retailer and capital partner in [[costco-1]] whose deal with [[SolPrice]] and [[RobertPrice]] led to the end of the Price family's control of [[FedMart]]. The hosts present the conflict as a mismatch between Price's operating-growth ambition and Mann's apparent interest in FedMart's real estate value.

In the source, Mann matters less as an operating model builder than as the shock that pushed Sol and Robert Price to found [[PriceClub]], preserving and recombining FedMart's warehouse learning.
