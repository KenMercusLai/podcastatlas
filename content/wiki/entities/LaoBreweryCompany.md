# Lao Brewery Company

Lao Brewery Company appears in [[pump-and-circumstance-is-china-the-new-opec-6a7c37287cf3c58886773a66]] as the producer of [[BeerLao]]. The source says the company traces the beer to a 1973 joint venture between French and Lao businessmen and is now part of a joint venture between the Lao state and [[Carlsberg]].

The company is economically central in the episode. Its 2024 sales are reported at $600 million, equivalent to about 3.5% of [[Laos]]'s GDP, and it is described as the country's largest taxpayer that year.
