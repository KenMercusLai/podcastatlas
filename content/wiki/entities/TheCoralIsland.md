# The Coral Island / 《珊瑚岛》

## Overview
The Coral Island / 《珊瑚岛》 is the 1857 children's adventure novel used in [[vol-351-yingwang-21shiji-de-huangdao-gushi-haineng-zenme-kai-naodong-1983-nuojiang-dezhu-gkwriueodap1a3taaattkgol]] as the tradition that [[LordOfTheFlies|《蝇王》]] overturns.

## Current Profile
The episode treats The Coral Island less as an independently analyzed text than as the optimistic island-adventure template Golding rewrites. Its boys, names, and adventure structure stand for a worldview in which children can carry civilization outward and overcome danger. Lord of the Flies keeps the island setup but reverses the moral expectation.

## Key Characteristics
- It represents the older child-adventure model behind the Lord of the Flies comparison.
- The episode associates it with civilization defeating savagery and truth defeating evil.
- Its character names and island setup are presented as direct intertextual signals in Golding's novel.
- It helps define [[IslandAdventureInversion]] by giving the later book something specific to reverse.
