# Ferrero

## Overview

Ferrero appears in the wiki as one of the foreign mass-market chocolate companies that dominate chocolate consumption in [[China]].

## Current Profile

The current profile is narrow and source-scoped. Ferrero is not analyzed as a full company. It is grouped with [[Mars]], [[Nestle]], and [[Hershey]] as part of the incumbent foreign-brand baseline that Chinese premium chocolate makers such as [[ChocRevive]], [[Nibbo]], and [[SaturnBird]] are trying to move beyond.

## Key Characteristics
