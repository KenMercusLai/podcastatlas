# 于莺 / Yu Ying

## Overview
于莺 is presented in the source as a physician, former Peking Union Medical College Hospital emergency clinician, early medical social-media writer known as “急诊科女超人,” and guest discussing weight management from both clinical and personal experience.

## Current Profile
In VOL.176, 于莺 uses repeated attempts at dieting, exercise, time-restricted eating, and clinical weight management to explain why plateaus and regain should not be reduced to willpower. She connects weight change to water balance, body fat, muscle, basal expenditure, thyroid context, stress, social eating, appetite signals, and longer-term maintenance, then routes medication, surgery, and more complex cases toward qualified assessment.

Her public-literacy role in this source is safety-oriented. She discourages copying internet diets, using unauthorized slimming products, or forcing severe restriction and excessive exercise, while distinguishing endogenous gut-hormone physiology from a personalized treatment decision.

## Key Characteristics
- Physician voice combining former emergency-care experience with public medical communication.
- Uses personal weight-management experience to make plateaus, rebound, and food cues concrete.
- Frames obesity care as individualized assessment rather than a universal diet or exercise template.
- Emphasizes body composition, muscle preservation, metabolic context, and health over rapid scale loss or a narrow appearance ideal.
- Keeps medicines, bariatric procedures, and endocrine questions inside professional evaluation and follow-up.
