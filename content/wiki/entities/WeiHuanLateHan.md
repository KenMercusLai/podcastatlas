## Overview

魏桓 / Wei Huan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-850-huandi-weihe-zhiyi-sha-zhongchen-lokxtceq7vv-giec6iufwwk9fwk7|Hanji 850]] as an Anyang recluse whom [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] repeatedly summons. His refusal is not presented as apolitical withdrawal; it is a risk assessment that the Huan court's palace luxury, horse-stable scale, and powerful-family corruption cannot be corrected from inside office.

## Current Profile

Hanji 850 uses Wei Huan as the opening diagnostic outsider before the Li Yun case. When fellow townspeople urge him to accept office, Wei Huan says official salary and promotion should serve one's political aspiration. He then asks whether the court can reduce the thousands of palace women, the ten-thousand-horse stable, and the corruption of powerful imperial intimates.

When the answer is effectively no, Wei Huan concludes that entering the capital would mean going alive and returning dead. His role therefore frames the episode's later direct-remonstrance disaster: before [[LiYunLateHan|李云]] is killed for speaking, Wei Huan has already judged that frank reform under Huan is likely to be futile and dangerous.

## Key Characteristics

- Recluse whose refusal of office is grounded in court diagnosis rather than simple quietism.
- Political realist who treats salary and promotion as meaningful only if office can advance a public program.
- Early Huan-era observer who identifies palace women, imperial horses, and powerful-family corruption as unreformable pressures.
- Foreshadowing figure for the episode's later claim that direct correction under Huan can become lethal.

## Evidence

- Summons refusal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-850-huandi-weihe-zhiyi-sha-zhongchen-lokxtceq7vv-giec6iufwwk9fwk7|Hanji 850]] says Huan again summons Wei Huan and that local people urge him to serve.
- Office-purpose standard: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-850-huandi-weihe-zhiyi-sha-zhongchen-lokxtceq7vv-giec6iufwwk9fwk7|Hanji 850]] says Wei Huan treats official pay and higher rank as justified only when they support one's governing aspiration.
- Court diagnosis: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-850-huandi-weihe-zhiyi-sha-zhongchen-lokxtceq7vv-giec6iufwwk9fwk7|Hanji 850]] says he asks whether palace women, stable horses, and powerful-family corruption can be reduced or corrected.
- Lethal-risk judgment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-850-huandi-weihe-zhiyi-sha-zhongchen-lokxtceq7vv-giec6iufwwk9fwk7|Hanji 850]] says he expects to go to Luoyang alive and be sent back dead if he accepts office.

## Qualifications

This page is source-bounded to Hanji 850. It does not establish Wei Huan's full biography, later activity, or exact relationship to other Eastern Han reclusion traditions. The episode's diagnostic use of Wei Huan should be read as a prelude to the Li Yun case, not as proof that all office-holding under Huan was impossible.
