## Overview

赵平 / Zhao Ping is presented as the Qi chancellor who resisted [[LiuXiangQiKingWesternHan|刘襄]]'s anti-Lü mobilization, briefly surrounded the royal palace, then lost his troops and died by suicide.

## Current Profile

In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-205-nvhuangdi-lvzhi-jingran-yin-gou-er-si-2-ltgwfdhn9cdwphudy0blyh36fge1|Hanji 205 part 2]], Zhao Ping is the only named senior Qi official who refuses the mobilization plan. After learning that Liu Xiang intends to kill him, he calls troops around the palace, but [[WeiBoWesternHan|魏勃]] gains control of that force and turns it against the chancellor's residence. Zhao Ping then dies by suicide and is associated with the warning that failure to decide invites disorder.

## Key Characteristics

- Qi chancellor opposing the king's anti-Lü mobilization.
- Holder of enough administrative authority to surround the royal palace.
- Loser of a rapid coercive-power contest with Wei Bo.
- Cautionary figure for hesitation and misjudgment in the episode's interpretation.

Defeat and death:

## Qualifications

This is a one-source profile. Zhao Ping's reasons, legal authority, exact sequence, attributed maxim, and death require comparison with transmitted histories. The episode's lesson about indecision is interpretation rather than a complete causal account.
