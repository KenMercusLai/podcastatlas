# Heiligenkreuz Abbey

## Overview

Heiligenkreuz Abbey is the burial destination for [[MaryVetsera]] after her body is removed from Mayerling.

## Current Profile

The episode uses Heiligenkreuz Abbey to show the asymmetry between Mary and Rudolf after death. Rudolf receives a public imperial funeral once Catholic burial is secured; Mary's relatives retrieve her body and take it to the abbey while the court suppresses her public role.

## Key Characteristics

- Heiligenkreuz is Mary's burial site in the source.
- It marks the private, family-handled side of the Mayerling aftermath.
- Its role contrasts with Rudolf's managed Capuchin burial and public funeral.
