## Overview

班昭 / Ban Zhao is presented in the current source as an Eastern Han court intellectual and adviser whose counsel shapes how [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]] manages the Deng family's public legitimacy after her mother's death.

## Current Profile

The source places Ban Zhao inside the Deng regency's legitimacy problem rather than only in literary or historiographical memory. After Empress Dowager Deng is constrained from staying with her gravely ill mother and the mother dies, [[DengZhiLateHan|Deng Zhi]] and other Deng brothers repeatedly request permission to leave office and observe mourning. The court problem is double-edged: keeping them at the center risks confirming accusations of natal-family dominance, while releasing them could weaken Deng's governing network.

Ban Zhao advises acceptance of the mourning withdrawal as a public signal that the Han court praises non-monopolization of power. Her counsel helps convert a potentially damaging family request into a reputation-management decision: Deng can appear restrained while preserving later consultation through fengchaoqing status. Ban Zhao's role is therefore advisory, rhetorical, and institutional, tying family mourning, gendered court proximity, and outer-relative power into one practical recommendation.

## Key Characteristics

- Court adviser who can speak into high-stakes Deng-regency personnel decisions.
- Reputation strategist who frames Deng-family resignation as evidence against monopolizing power.
- Mediator between kinship obligation and state legitimacy after the death of Empress Dowager Deng's mother.
- Participant in a women-centered advisory channel around [[EmpressDowagerDengLateHan|Empress Dowager Deng]] rather than a battlefield or officeholding actor in this source.
- Her counsel enables restraint without complete Deng-family exclusion, because fengchaoqing later restores formal deliberative access.

## Evidence

- Family-power legitimacy: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-785-donghan-dengsui-zhangkong-yuwang-caineng-xiaodao-zuihou-llfg4kjaautqlfewhplikvu3ry1u|Hanji 785]] reports Ban Zhao advising Deng to let the Deng brothers depart for mourning so the court can display praise for non-exclusive power.
- Mourning and regency balance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-785-donghan-dengsui-zhangkong-yuwang-caineng-xiaodao-zuihou-llfg4kjaautqlfewhplikvu3ry1u|Hanji 785]] places the advice immediately after Deng's constrained response to her mother's illness and death.
- Institutional aftermath: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-785-donghan-dengsui-zhangkong-yuwang-caineng-xiaodao-zuihou-llfg4kjaautqlfewhplikvu3ry1u|Hanji 785]] pairs acceptance of resignation with later fengchaoqing honors, showing that the advice preserved legitimacy without ending Deng-family consultation.

## Qualifications

The current profile is source-bounded to one episode. It does not yet cover Ban Zhao's broader authorship, family background, role in Han historiography, or any independent political program outside the Deng-family mourning episode.
