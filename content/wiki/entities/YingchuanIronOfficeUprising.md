## Overview

[[YingchuanIronOfficeUprising|颍川铁官徒起义 / Yingchuan Iron-Office Uprising]] enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-523-gudai-nongmin-qiyi-zong-shibai-meilixiang-or-jianshiduan-ltwfeupz2zzzvo76fhm7-6txjiup|Hanji 523]] as a Yangshuo 3 / 22 BCE Western Han revolt led by [[ShentuShengWesternHan|申屠圣]] among iron-office convicts in [[YingchuanCommandery|颍川郡]].

## Current Profile

The source frames the uprising as a coercive-labor variant of [[SurvivalPressureRebellion|生存压力型起义]]. The episode links the revolt to Western Han salt-and-iron state control, where iron offices use penal laborers, corvee workers, and craftsmen to produce agricultural tools and weapons, while officials allegedly neglect subsistence, impose harsh work, and punish discontent.

The event begins small but becomes politically visible quickly. About 180 iron-office convicts led by Shentu Sheng seize weapons, kill the iron official and commandery clerk, leave the city, and move through several places with farmer support. The source says the revolt passes through nine commanderies in under a month and alarms the capital before being defeated by state forces.

The episode uses the failure to separate grievance from governing capacity. It treats the uprising as evidence of serious Western Han stress, but also argues that rebel forces need organization, discipline, legitimacy, strategic judgment, and civilian support if they are to move beyond armed outbreak.

## Key Characteristics

- Yangshuo 3 / 22 BCE Western Han uprising rooted in iron-office coercive labor.
- Led by [[ShentuShengWesternHan|申屠圣]] and initially involving about 180 iron-office convicts.
- Begins with arsenal seizure and the killing of local iron-office and commandery officials.
- Gains local farmer support and moves through multiple commanderies quickly.
- Suppressed by the Han state despite visible spread and capital alarm.
- Used by the source to distinguish justified grievance from durable rebel governance capacity.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 523 and does not reconstruct the full administrative geography, campaign route, legal categories of every participant, or independent archaeology of Western Han iron offices. The source transcript says "尹川," but this wiki normalizes the setting to [[YingchuanCommandery|颍川郡]] based on the historical event and existing route. The episode's wider claims about peasant-rebel failure are treated as source interpretation rather than a complete comparative history.
