# Ecover

## Overview

Ecover is a detergent company defended by [[BrendanFrancisNewnam]] in a [[RevisionistHistory]] teaser about laundry choices and internal criticism at [[PushkinIndustries]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Ecover through Newnam's favorable comparison with [[ProcterGamble|Procter & Gamble]]. He cites wages, a long carbon-neutral record, highly rated buildings, a smaller supply-chain footprint, scent, and cleaning effectiveness, but the teaser supplies none of the underlying measurements or company records needed to verify those claims.
