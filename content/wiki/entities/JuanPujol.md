# Juan Pujol / Garbo

## Overview

Juan Pujol, codenamed Garbo, was a Spanish double agent whose invented network of sub-agents became part of British deception against Germany during the Second World War.

## Current Profile

[[37-spies-with-ben-macintyre-glt4382644548]] presents Pujol as the clearest example of [[EspionageFictionWorldbuilding|fiction operating as intelligence]]. He made an imaginary organization appear credible through accumulated detail, then helped the Allies use that credibility to support the false expectation that the principal D-Day landing would come at Calais rather than Normandy.

## Key Characteristics

- Built a convincing but fictitious network of sub-agents.
- Worked as a double agent in the Allied deception system.
- Used narrative consistency and operational detail to make false reporting believable.
- Helped reinforce German expectations about Calais after the Normandy landings.
- Connects literary invention to measurable wartime behavior in the episode's argument.
