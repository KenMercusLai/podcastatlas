# Koja Gentar

## Overview
Koja Gentar is the 42-year-old governor-elect of [[Okinawa]] reported by the source as the prefecture's youngest-ever governor and its first born after the end of direct American administration.

## Current Profile
Koja won a record-vote landslide by prioritizing economic improvement, promising to double incomes, and supporting the [[Henoko]] base-relocation project. His [[LiberalDemocraticPartyJapan|LDP]]-backed victory strengthens Japan's conservative government, but future accidents or disputes may test whether he sides with Okinawan residents against Tokyo.

## Key Characteristics
- Campaigned on economic advancement rather than renewed anti-base confrontation.
- Supports Henoko and received strong conservative-party backing.
- Used his experience raising five children as part of his appeal.
- Represents a post-occupation generation without direct memory of war or American rule.
- Faces a credibility test if base burdens produce a new local crisis.
