# Joy FM

Joy FM appears in [[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]] as a major [[Ghana|Ghanaian]] radio station and national-news example. The episode uses it to show that radio in Africa is not only legacy infrastructure; prominent stations can still shape public information environments.

In the wiki, Joy FM anchors the concrete media side of [[AfricanRadioResilience]]. Its role complements the broader [[Afrobarometer]] listening data and the technology branch around [[DABRadioCapacityExpansion]].
