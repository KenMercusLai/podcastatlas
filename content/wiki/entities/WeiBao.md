# 魏豹 / Wei Bao

魏豹 / Wei Bao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-117-1-papa-dalian-lishishang-shuangwen-de-kaishan-bizu-lr0h-kfeguvatattnj-mz8fgvnc]] as a surviving Wei royal figure after [[WangBenQin|王贲]] floods [[Daliang|大梁]] and [[WeiWangJia|魏王假]] surrenders. The episode says Wei Bao and [[WeiJiu|魏咎]] fall into commoner status after [[WeiState|魏国]] is destroyed, then later reappear around the Chen Sheng and Chu-Han transition.

His source role is mostly ironic. Wei Bao worries about his fate and asks [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负]] to read [[BaoJi|薄姬]], his favored woman. When Xu Fu predicts that Bao Ji will bear a future royal or imperial son, Wei Bao reads the prediction through himself and imagines that his own fortunes may rise.

The prediction is fulfilled in a way that empties his self-centered reading. The episode says Wei Bao dies without seeing that Bao Ji's future child is not his successor but [[LiuHeng|刘恒]], son of [[LiuBang|刘邦]] and later Emperor Wen of Han.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-135-fanwaipian-2-nvxiangshi-yin-kanming-nengli-bei-liubang-fenghou-lgu1fr1zrzmc0o72wtbpxmajb7s|Qinji 135 番外篇2]] retells the same Bao Ji prediction inside [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负]]'s full legend. It adds the source's stronger causal framing: Wei Bao treats the prophecy as a reason to leave Liu Bang's side for [[XiangYu|项羽]], but the promise of an emperor-child is displaced away from Wei Bao and fulfilled only after Bao Ji enters Liu Bang's household.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-3-weihe-li-mutong-wei-chuwang-limian-dayou-wenzhang-lmcuvrxer-cou6gmlppqwhb3n4vz|Qinji 130-3]] moves Wei Bao back into the Qin-collapse restoration field. After [[WeiJiu|魏咎]] dies at Linji under [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]]'s pressure, Wei Bao escapes to [[ChuState|楚国]]. [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]] gives him several thousand soldiers so he can try to recover old Wei territory.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-132-5wan-wanbao-40wan-xiangyu-cizhan-fengshen-lk87nb3fskg5zh6fcgptypixhzuw|Qinji 132]] records the payoff of that backing. Wei Bao recovers more than twenty old Wei cities, and Mi Xin recognizes him as Wei king. The source therefore turns his earlier Chu-backed restoration attempt into a partial success inside the broader [[SixStateRestorationAntiQinStrategy|six-state restoration]] field.
