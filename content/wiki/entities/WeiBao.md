# 魏豹 / Wei Bao

魏豹 / Wei Bao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-117-1-papa-dalian-lishishang-shuangwen-de-kaishan-bizu-lr0h-kfeguvatattnj-mz8fgvnc]] as a surviving Wei royal figure after [[WangBenQin|王贲]] floods [[Daliang|大梁]] and [[WeiWangJia|魏王假]] surrenders. The episode says Wei Bao and [[WeiJiu|魏咎]] fall into commoner status after [[WeiState|魏国]] is destroyed, then later reappear around the Chen Sheng and Chu-Han transition.

His source role is mostly ironic. Wei Bao worries about his fate and asks [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负]] to read [[BaoJi|薄姬]], his favored woman. When Xu Fu predicts that Bao Ji will bear a future royal or imperial son, Wei Bao reads the prediction through himself and imagines that his own fortunes may rise.

The prediction is fulfilled in a way that empties his self-centered reading. The episode says Wei Bao dies without seeing that Bao Ji's future child is not his successor but [[LiuHeng|刘恒]], son of [[LiuBang|刘邦]] and later Emperor Wen of Han.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-135-fanwaipian-2-nvxiangshi-yin-kanming-nengli-bei-liubang-fenghou-lgu1fr1zrzmc0o72wtbpxmajb7s|Qinji 135 番外篇2]] retells the same Bao Ji prediction inside [[XuFuPhysiognomist|许负]]'s full legend. It adds the source's stronger causal framing: Wei Bao treats the prophecy as a reason to leave Liu Bang's side for [[XiangYu|项羽]], but the promise of an emperor-child is displaced away from Wei Bao and fulfilled only after Bao Ji enters Liu Bang's household.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-3-weihe-li-mutong-wei-chuwang-limian-dayou-wenzhang-lmcuvrxer-cou6gmlppqwhb3n4vz|Qinji 130-3]] moves Wei Bao back into the Qin-collapse restoration field. After [[WeiJiu|魏咎]] dies at Linji under [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]]'s pressure, Wei Bao escapes to [[ChuState|楚国]]. [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]] gives him several thousand soldiers so he can try to recover old Wei territory.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-132-5wan-wanbao-40wan-xiangyu-cizhan-fengshen-lk87nb3fskg5zh6fcgptypixhzuw|Qinji 132]] records the payoff of that backing. Wei Bao recovers more than twenty old Wei cities, and Mi Xin recognizes him as Wei king. The source therefore turns his earlier Chu-backed restoration attempt into a partial success inside the broader [[SixStateRestorationAntiQinStrategy|six-state restoration]] field.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-143-xiangyu-shi-ruhe-feijin-xinji-nongsi-chuhuaiwang-mixin-lstfzfvbd2h9i39f7y5dxrbxxwv2|Hanji 143]] gives Wei Bao's Xiang Yu settlement adjustment. [[XiangYu|项羽]] moves him from Wei king to West Wei king, with Pingyang as capital and Hedong as territory. The change shows how old restored royal titles can survive but be re-ranked and relocated inside [[ZhouQinHybridOrder|周秦混合制]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-149-xichu-bawang-xiangyu-weihe-shijun-2-lpohbb3prsgvokpjipfd7i1ntiwn|Hanji 149 part 2]] shows that demotion becoming defection. Wei Bao surrenders to [[LiuBang|刘邦]] without major resistance after Liu Bang crosses the Yellow River from Linjin. The episode explains the quick turn through [[RewardAllocationBacklash|分配反噬]]: Xiang Yu's settlement had cut Wei Bao down from a larger old-Wei commandery base and reassigned pieces to West Chu and [[SimaAng|司马卬]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-152-xiangyu-3wanren-ruhe-wansheng-liubang-56wan-dajun-2-lq9ecpjb4r-ij0-e-iu-ezfavowj|Hanji 152 part 2]] reverses that short-lived Liu-side attachment after the [[PengchengBattle|彭城之战]]. Once Liu Bang is routed, Wei Bao returns to West Wei and rebels from Han back toward Chu. The page now treats Wei Bao as a two-stage alliance case: [[RewardAllocationBacklash|分配反噬]] helps explain his first move toward Liu Bang, while [[PowerBasedAllianceRepricing|实力重估式阵营转向]] explains why Pengcheng changes the calculation again.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-155-1-lishi-jizai-shenme-qingkuang-hui-fasheng-ren-chi-ren-canju-lrwz-prnxd8hzchagyrbywkux7lh|Hanji 155-1]] gives that renewed break a concrete scene and stated motive. Wei Bao asks [[LiuBang|刘邦]] for permission to return to West Wei to visit his sick mother, then closes Yellow River crossings and submits to [[XiangYu|项羽]]. The source records his explanation as personal humiliation: Liu Bang's coarse insults toward lords and ministers make Wei Bao unwilling to return, adding status injury to the existing power-repricing and settlement-grievance explanations.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-155-2-zhuan-gei-ni-piqi-da-de-pengyou-weiwang-panhan-jing-shi-liubang-ai-maren-ls5nvunmhakvefd2oxn4zdyoob4l|Hanji 155-2]] completes the consequences of that break. Wei Bao concentrates at [[PubanWarringStates|蒲阪]] against the expected Han crossing from [[LinjinWarringStates|临晋]], but [[HanXin|韩信]] crosses instead at [[XiayangChuHan|下阳]] and strikes [[Anyi|安邑]]. After [[CaoShen|曹参]] and [[GuanYing|灌婴]] press the pursuit, Wei Bao is captured and West Wei territory is converted into [[HedongCommandery|河东郡]], [[Shangdang|上党郡]], and [[TaiyuanWarringStates|太原郡]].
