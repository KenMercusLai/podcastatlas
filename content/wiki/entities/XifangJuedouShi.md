# 《西方决斗史》 / The History of Duelling

《西方决斗史》 is the book frame used by [[78-xifang-juedou-shi-chujian-ba-weile-rongyu-687731000]] to discuss Western dueling. The episode presents it as a nineteenth-century account by [[JohnGideonMillingen|John Gideon Millingen / 约翰·基甸·米林根]], useful for stories, rules, and period attitudes, but not treated as a fully neutral modern academic history.

The source uses the book to separate [[JudicialDuel|judicial duel]], [[ChivalricTournamentCulture|chivalric tournament culture]], and [[HonorDuelCulture|honor duel culture]]. Its most reusable contribution is the tension between denunciation and fascination: Millingen condemns dueling as bloody and absurd, while still preserving the honor language that made duelists admire courage, procedure, and personal reputation.
