# Phil Oram

Phil Oram appears in [[vol-269-xiao-lishi-bu-yao-pa-shi-jishu-xing-tiaozheng-1004793119]] as an executive director of [[DairyFarmInternational|牛奶公司]] during [[HongkongLand|香港置地]]'s takeover bid. The episode says he was in New Zealand after a minor operation when the bid emerged and had to return to [[HongKong|Hong Kong]] quickly.

The source uses Oram's absence to underline timing and preparedness. Hongkong Land's offer arrived when Dairy Farm's leadership was not fully positioned for a fast public fight, while the acquirer was already ready with a newspaper and shareholder-operation campaign.
