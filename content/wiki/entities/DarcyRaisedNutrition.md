# Darcy (Raised Nutrition)

## Overview
Darcy is the Raised Nutrition founder-caller in the Daymond John Advice Line episode, where he asks whether the brand should keep its jiu-jitsu origin story after seeing stronger uptake from broader health-and-wellness buyers.

## Current Profile
The source identifies Darcy as calling from Ballina, New South Wales, Australia, and as co-founding [[RaisedNutrition]] with his wife Jess. He presents the company as a whole-food protein bar brand shaped by ingredient scrutiny, jiu-jitsu training, RXBar inspiration, and Australian sourcing.

His decision point is not whether the product can appeal beyond jiu-jitsu; the source already says many customers do. The question is whether to erase the niche origin to avoid alienating a larger market. [[DaymondJohn]] and [[GuyRaz]] argue the opposite: keep the origin because it explains the product's purpose, then broaden the packaging and marketing around collagen, recovery, and clean ingredients.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder-caller with a specific first-community origin in Brazilian jiu-jitsu.
- Frames the product around ingredient integrity and avoidance of artificial additives.
- Notices that demand is coming from broader wellness customers, creating a positioning tension.
- Receives advice to keep the jiu-jitsu story while strengthening ingredient and recovery language.
