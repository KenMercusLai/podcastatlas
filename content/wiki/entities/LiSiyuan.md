# 李嗣源 / Li Siyuan

Li Siyuan / 李嗣源 is one of the Later Tang figures named in [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]]'s discussion of adoptive sons, name changes, and military loyalty. The episode treats such ties as practical substitutes for weak formal institutions in a violent age.

His importance here is structural. Li Siyuan shows how [[WudaiAdoptiveSonSystem]] could create real military capacity while also making personal bonds, names, and regime changes politically charged.
