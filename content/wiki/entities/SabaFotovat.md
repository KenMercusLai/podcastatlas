# Saba Fotovat

Saba Fotovat is the third sibling in the [[MadeGood]] founder/operator story in [[madegood-salma-and-nima-fotovat-lost-their-first-business-they-grew-their-next-one-into-a-snack-giant-cf5715bf-e9e0-45e0-a8a9-1256a7a4ed0d]]. The source gives less direct detail about Saba than about [[NimaFotovat]] and [[SalmaFotovat]], but it includes her in the sibling group that rebuilt after the family's loss of [[TasteOfNature]].

## Key Points
- Saba worked in the family food business before the MadeGood reset, including early factory-floor work alongside Salma.
- She was at the office with Salma when the 2012 buyout displaced the family from the prior business.
- The episode positions her as part of the sibling team that moved from co-packing and [[TasteOfNature]] experience into [[RiversideNaturalFoods]] and [[MadeGood]].
