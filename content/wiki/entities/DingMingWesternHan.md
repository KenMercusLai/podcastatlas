## Overview

丁明 / Ding Ming enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-599-gan-dui-qianguize-shuo-bu-chengxiang-wangjia-you-duo-yong-lpptx-fzls9xbakw52b8n1n6xow0|Hanji 599]] as a Western Han Da Sima whose grief over [[WangJiaWesternHan|王嘉]] becomes politically punishable under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 599 presents Ding Ming narrowly but sharply. He is identified as a high official who had long respected Wang Jia. After Wang Jia dies in prison, Ding Ming grieves deeply. Aidi learns of this grief and issues an order dismissing Ding Ming and sending him home.

The page therefore treats Ding Ming as a political-sympathy case rather than a full biography. His removal shows that Wang Jia's death continues to divide the court after the formal case is over.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Da Sima in the immediate Wang Jia aftermath.
- Longtime admirer or respecter of Wang Jia in the episode's account.
- Publicly or visibly grieves Wang Jia's prison death.
- Removed by Han Aidi because that grief itself becomes politically charged.
