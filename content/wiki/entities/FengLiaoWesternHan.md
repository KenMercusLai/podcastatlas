## Overview

冯嫽 / Feng Liao is synthesized as a Western Han Wusun-network diplomat whose authority comes from literacy, local knowledge, marriage ties, reputation, and state-backed credentials. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-461-lishi-shouwei-nvwaijiaojia-fengliao-ruhe-woxuan-xiyu-weiji-lpxkawzol3526yztjyxxmz26phcq|Hanji 461]] supplies the earlier crisis role: after [[NimiWusun|狂王泥靡]] is killed and Wujiutu claims the Wusun title, [[ZhengJiWesternHan|郑吉]] sends Feng Liao to persuade Wujiutu before war begins. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-463-caicaikan-zai-hanxuandi-yanzhong-shui-shi-zuidade-gongchen-lqblzdg7sdkzln4vxhtnnye92292|Hanji 463]] supplies the later return role: after [[XieyouPrincessWesternHan|解忧公主]] comes back to Chang'an, Feng Liao voluntarily returns in old age to help stabilize the young ruler Xingmi.

## Current Profile

Hanji 461 now gives Feng Liao's active diplomatic profile before her old-age return. She begins in the episode as [[XieyouPrincessWesternHan|Xieyou Princess]]'s attendant, but the source emphasizes that she can write official documents, understands Han institutions, knows Western Regions affairs, has carried Han credentials for missions to regional states, and is respected as "Feng Furen." Her marriage to the Wusun right general also gives her a channel close to Wujiutu, making her unusually suited to speak where a normal envoy or army might harden resistance.

Her mediation works because it joins personal credibility to Han force. Zheng Ji sends her after Han has mobilized Western Regions forces and Dunhuang support, so her warning to Wujiutu is not empty. Wujiutu accepts submission but asks for a small-king title and a secure place. [[EmperorXuanOfHan|Xuan]] then summons Feng Liao, questions her directly about Wusun, and sends her back with imperial credentials and escorts to install Yuanguimi as large kunmi and Wujiutu as small kunmi.

Hanji 463 adds the late sequel. After Yuanguimi and Chimi die, Xingmi succeeds while still too young and weak to command obedience. Feng Liao, already over seventy in the episode's telling, asks Xuan for permission to go back to Wusun and help settle the situation. Xuan agrees; Wusun and Western Regions people welcome her return; and, with her support, Xingmi suppresses rebellion and the Han-Wusun relationship remains friendly through the later succession to Cili Mi.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han woman whose influence belongs to the Wusun frontier-diplomacy field.
- Moves from Xieyou Princess's attendant into a recognized diplomatic actor because she can handle documents and Western Regions affairs.
- Uses local marriage and reputation as practical diplomatic channels inside Wusun elite politics.
- Persuades Wujiutu to accept a Han-backed settlement when Han military pressure is already credible.
- Receives direct imperial trust from Xuan, who recalls, questions, and re-commissions her with credentials.
- Remains active after Xieyou's return to Han and later volunteers despite old age to stabilize Xingmi.
- Trusted by Wusun and Western Regions people enough that her arrival has political effect across multiple crises.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 461 and Hanji 463. It does not reconstruct Feng Liao's full biography, exact titles, original mission documents, the legal form of her credentials, or the full Wusun genealogy around Yuanguimi, Wujiutu, Chimi, Xingmi, and Cili Mi. Hanji 461's "first female diplomat" framing and Hanji 463's age statement are preserved as source claims rather than settled comparative historiography.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 461's earlier crisis role: Feng Liao as the decisive intermediary who helps convert Han military pressure into a split-kunmi settlement.
- Reframed Hanji 463's old-age return as a later instance of the same Wusun-network diplomatic authority.
