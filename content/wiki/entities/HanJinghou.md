# 韩景侯 / Marquis Jing of Han

韩景侯 / Marquis Jing of Han appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-1-lvkp-ju7pf5z5cz3thojv3s2fkyx]] as the [[HanState|韩国]] ruler whose death in 400 BCE is followed by [[HanLiehou|韩烈侯]]'s accession. The episode uses the timing to explain why [[ZhengState|郑国]] may have attacked Han-held Yangdi during a vulnerable mourning and succession window.
