# 韩景侯 / Marquis Jing of Han

韩景侯 / Marquis Jing of Han appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-1-lvkp-ju7pf5z5cz3thojv3s2fkyx]] as the [[HanState|韩国]] ruler whose death in 400 BCE is followed by [[HanLiehou|韩烈侯]]'s accession. The episode uses the timing to explain why [[ZhengState|郑国]] may have attacked Han-held Yangdi during a vulnerable mourning and succession window.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] looks back to Han Jinghou as a key relay point in [[GenerationalFamilyStrategy|Han's multi-generation strategy]] against Zheng. The source says he accepted [[ZhouWeilieWang|周威烈王]]'s recognition as a vassal and moved the Han capital to Yangdi, continuing the direction set by [[HanKangzi|韩康子]] and [[HanWuzi|韩武子]].
