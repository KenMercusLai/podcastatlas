## Overview

黄忠 / Huang Zhong is represented in the current wiki by two Hanzhong operations and by the later promotion that triggers Guan Yu's appointment dispute.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1164-jingti-zhichang-tai-nenggan-de-xianjing-lrw8byuza9fzasd0ewwszkoj3mu|Hanji 1164]] says Huang Zhong learns that a Cao-side grain convoy will pass North Mountain and leaves to attack it before seeking further authorization, while [[ZhaoYun|赵云]] remains at camp. When Huang Zhong does not promptly return, Zhao Yun rides out with a small group and encounters a much larger Cao-side formation.

Hanji 1163 supplies the earlier and strategically larger role. After Liu Bei's pressure induces Xiahou Yuan to divide his force and a fire draws the commander into defensive repair, [[FaZheng|法正]] identifies the attack window and Huang Zhong leads the assault that kills Xiahou Yuan. Together the notes show rapid execution in two operations, while only the Dingjun action has a stated decisive result.

The political afterlife of that success appears when Liu Bei elevates Huang Zhong to a rank presented as comparable to [[GuanYu|关羽]], prompting Guan Yu's angry refusal of his own appointment. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1166-ruhe-yu-piqi-da-nengli-qiang-de-ren-xiangchu-lhm17dmeln7dmj1czmeq7omxgtmh|Hanji 1166]] uses the dispute to analyze Guan Yu's status sensitivity; it does not diminish Huang Zhong's campaign merit or establish the exact institutional equivalence of the ranks.

## Key Characteristics

- Liu-side commander in the Hanzhong campaign.
- Executes the decisive assault after Fa Zheng identifies Xiahou Yuan's exposure.
- Acts quickly on intelligence about an enemy grain movement.
- Leaves Zhao Yun responsible for the camp.
- Creates or encounters an unplanned support problem when he does not promptly return.
- Becomes the comparison figure in Guan Yu's later promotion dispute after Hanzhong.

## Qualifications

The current profile rests on three structured popular-history summaries. The dates, locations, command relationships, force strengths, Dingjun signal and route, identity of casualties, convoy intelligence, authorization, convoy outcome, rank equivalence, and reason for delayed return require broader textual comparison. The page does not yet attempt a full biography or import later literary characterization.

- Preserved campaign merit separately from the episode's interpretation of Guan Yu's reaction.
