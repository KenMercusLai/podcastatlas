# 乘氏县 / Chengshi County (Late Han)

乘氏县 / Chengshi County (Late Han) appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-980-lvbu-xiansha-caocao-kankan-yi-ju-hua-ruhe-niuzhuan-lishi-lgggw3vmonu-3wvzabvg943v5qcg|Hanji 980]] as the place [[LyuBu|吕布]] attacks after the prolonged Puyang fight breaks under locust and grain pressure. [[LiJinLateHan|李进]] defeats him there, after which Lü Bu moves eastward toward Shanyang.

The county is useful as a local-resistance marker in the [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] branch. It shows that the campaign's outcome is not decided only by the dramatic city escape at [[PuyangBattleLateHan|濮阳之战]], but also by food, morale, and smaller local actors who make further operations harder for Lü Bu.
