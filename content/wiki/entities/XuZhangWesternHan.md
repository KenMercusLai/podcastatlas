## Overview

许章 / Xu Zhang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-469-weishenme-junzi-dou-buguo-xiaoren-kankan-xiaowangzhi-de-xiachang-lj8iji-c3scj6vznntavm6awolho|Hanji 469]] as a Western Han court actor on the [[ShiGaoWesternHan|史高]] side of the first [[XiaoWangzhiWesternHan|萧望之]] conflict.

## Current Profile

Hanji 469 gives Xu Zhang a narrow role. [[ZhengPengWesternHan|郑朋]] had earlier attacked Shi Gao and Xu Zhang in memorials while close to Xiao Wangzhi and [[ZhouKanWesternHan|周堪]]. After Xiao cuts him off, Zheng turns toward Shi Gao and Xu Zhang and claims that the earlier attacks were directed by Zhou Kan and [[LiuXiang|刘向 / 刘更生]].

## Key Characteristics

- Court actor named with Shi Gao as a prior target of Zheng Peng's memorial attacks.
- Receives Zheng Peng's defection after Zheng is rejected by Xiao Wangzhi.
- Functions mainly as part of the hostile recipient side rather than as the episode's principal operator.
