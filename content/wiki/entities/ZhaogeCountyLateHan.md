## Overview

朝歌县 / Zhaoge County is the Eastern Han county setting where [[YuXuEasternHan|虞诩]] turns a dangerous anti-bandit posting into a local-governance success. The current wiki evidence is source-bounded to Hanji 784 and treats the county primarily as the terrain, supply, and population environment behind Ning Ji's rebellion and Yu Xu's pacification method.

## Current Profile

Hanji 784 presents Zhaoge as a county under rebel pressure after Ning Ji kills officials and gathers followers. [[DengZhiLateHan|Deng Zhi]], resentful after Yu Xu blocks the plan to abandon [[LiangzhouLateHan|Liangzhou]], appoints Yu Xu as Zhaoge magistrate and orders him to take office immediately.

Yu Xu's first move is diagnosis. The episode says Zhaoge sits near Taihang, the Yellow River, and [[Aocang|Aocang]], with many refugees and fugitives around it. But Ning Ji's group does not seize the grain store, armories, or city defenses. Yu Xu therefore reads the rebels as brave but strategically weak, which lets him choose inducement and information control rather than treating them as a disciplined insurgent army.

The pacification sequence depends on local social knowledge. Yu Xu classifies violent offenders, thieves, and idle men, pardons and feeds more than one hundred of them, and sends them to infiltrate the rebels and draw them down from the mountains. After an ambush, he adds hidden colored-thread marks to rebel clothing through local sewing work so officials can identify them in the market. The source says Zhaoge is quickly pacified and that the rebels do not understand how they are being recognized.

## Key Characteristics

- County-level rebellion setting rather than a fully reconstructed administrative history.
- Dangerous appointment used by Deng Zhi to expose Yu Xu after a court policy defeat.
- Geography matters because nearby mountains, river routes, and Aocang make rebel non-seizure strategically revealing.
- Local population includes fugitives and idle or criminalized men who can become either disorder infrastructure or state informants.
- Pacification turns on hidden identification, infiltration, lure, and market arrest rather than large-force conquest.

## Evidence

- Dangerous appointment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-784-donghan-diaoyu-zhifa-diyiren-yuxu-ls6zezfw2pvaytoo94igdscfwkpt|Hanji 784]] says Deng Zhi sends Yu Xu to Zhaoge after Ning Ji's rebellion kills officials and overwhelms local suppression.
- Terrain and rebel diagnosis: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-784-donghan-diaoyu-zhifa-diyiren-yuxu-ls6zezfw2pvaytoo94igdscfwkpt|Hanji 784]] says Yu Xu judges the rebels by what they fail to seize despite access to Taihang, the Yellow River, Aocang, weapons, and city defenses.
- Pacification method: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-784-donghan-diaoyu-zhifa-diyiren-yuxu-ls6zezfw2pvaytoo94igdscfwkpt|Hanji 784]] records Yu Xu recruiting offenders as infiltrators, using them to lure rebels down, and marking clothing with hidden colored threads for later identification.
