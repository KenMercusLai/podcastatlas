# Incheon International Airport / 仁川国际机场

Incheon International Airport / 仁川国际机场 appears in [[zhongguo-yinglai-hanbao-kaidianchao-guochan-huangningmeng-jiage-huiluo-1006920543]] as an international travel-routing case. The source cites preliminary Airports Council International data saying Incheon handled more than 38 million international passengers in the first half of the year and became the world's largest international-passenger airport for that period.

The episode explains the change as a mix of rerouting and tourism demand. Middle East conflict reportedly shifted some Europe-Asia transfer traffic away from Dubai toward Incheon, while more Chinese and Japanese travelers lifted the foreign-passenger share to about 40%.
