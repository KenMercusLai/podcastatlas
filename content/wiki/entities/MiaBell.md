# Mia Bell

## Overview

Mia Bell is a podcast collaborator whom [[MalcolmGladwell]] credits with teaching him about podcasting and helping reshape the proposed first season of [[RevisionistHistory]].

## Current Profile

In the anniversary account, Bell responds to strong story ideas with a structural criticism: the proposed season relied heavily on sports, cars, and male sources and therefore risked excluding women listeners. Gladwell treats the directness of that judgment as evidence of the editorial relationship he wanted, making Bell an early example of [[EditorialCandorAsRevision]].

The source establishes the significance of her intervention but does not reconstruct the revised lineup, her full production role, or the degree to which the eventual audience changed.

## Key Characteristics

- Early podcast guide and collaborator for Malcolm Gladwell.
- Identified a gender and subject-matter imbalance in the proposed first season.
- Delivered criticism directly enough to change the working relationship.
- Represents candid editorial challenge as part of creative revision.
