# 祝公道 / Zhu Gongdao (late Han)

## Overview
祝公道 / Zhu Gongdao (late Han) enters the wiki through Hanji 1056 as the figure who secretly rescues [[JiaKuiLateHan|贾逵]] from [[GuoYuanLateHan|郭援 / 郭元]]'s confinement. The source gives him a narrow but important role: he turns public sympathy for Jia Kui into an actual escape path.

## Current Profile
After Guo Yuan takes the Jiang County position in [[HedongCommandery|河东郡]], Jia Kui refuses to cooperate with him and is protected by local elders, officials, and common people. Guo Yuan confines Jia Kui at Huguan in a pit or cellar. Zhu Gongdao then rescues Jia Kui secretly at night, completing the episode's local-protection chain from communal pleading to concrete action.

## Key Characteristics
- Single-source late-Han figure currently known only through Hanji 1056.
- Rescue actor who intervenes after Guo Yuan's coercion fails to erase Jia Kui's local support.
- Operates through stealth rather than formal office or battlefield command in the bounded source.
- Connects Jia Kui's moral standing to practical survival.
