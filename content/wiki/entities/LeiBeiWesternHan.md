## Overview

雷被 / Lei Bei is a skilled Huainan swordsman and retainer whose accidental injury of crown prince [[LiuQianHuainanCrownPrinceWesternHan|刘迁]] develops into blocked exit, flight to Chang'an, and an early disclosure channel for the alleged conspiracy of [[LiuAn|刘安]]. His survival becomes the comparison for [[WuBeiWesternHan|伍被]]'s later, unsuccessful confession.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-294-2-yi-chang-bijian-yinbao-de-jingtian-daan-lpqd6by5nn9k8p28m30yrzxanxql|Hanji 294-2]] supplies the personal prehistory. Lei Bei is presented as a skilled swordsman who accidentally wounds Liu Qian during a bout. Liu Qian treats the injury as deliberate, making advancement inside Huainan appear closed. When Lei Bei seeks to answer the central call for anti-Xiongnu service, Liu Qian denounces him and Liu An blocks the departure; Lei Bei then flees by night toward Chang'an because he fears retaliation and wants central protection.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-295-doufu-de-famingzhe-jing-shi-zaofan-de-fanwang-libnwsqalvpzymlnbaewua4s6miw|Hanji 295]] continues the grievance into procedure. Lei Bei petitions the court about his treatment, and the廷尉 inquiry detects a larger rebellion concern, although the episode does not reproduce a complete confession by Lei Bei.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-304-1-gudai-ruhe-zuo-shuangmian-jiandie-lu05uaxp5b_buvl5go2bn7iy9q_x|Hanji 304-1]] looks back on that survival and treats Lei Bei as an early denouncer of father and son. It further argues that the court handled Liu An cautiously because an unresolved Xiongnu war made premature confrontation with a vassal king dangerous.

Lei Bei matters here less as a complete biography than as a timing comparison. His alleged early disclosure remained useful while the center wanted intelligence without immediate escalation; Wu Bei's later confession arrived after arrests and exposure had changed the court's priorities.

## Key Characteristics

- Huainan insider associated by the episode with Liu An's intimate retainer circle.
- Skilled swordsman whose accidental injury of Liu Qian becomes a source-scoped origin for hostility.
- Retainer who is blocked from seeking central military service, escapes Huainan, appeals at the capital, and survives the case.
- Alleged source of information about Liu An and Liu Qian's conspiracy.
- Comparison case showing how timing and central political need can alter the value of defection.

## Evidence

Early denunciation:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-294-2-yi-chang-bijian-yinbao-de-jingtian-daan-lpqd6by5nn9k8p28m30yrzxanxql|Hanji 294-2]] supplies the sword-bout injury, Liu Qian's hostile interpretation, blocked enlistment, nighttime flight, and intention to seek central protection.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-295-doufu-de-famingzhe-jing-shi-zaofan-de-fanwang-libnwsqalvpzymlnbaewua4s6miw|Hanji 295]] links Lei Bei's departure to obstructed military service and says his petition triggers a廷尉 investigation that discovers a larger risk.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-304-1-gudai-ruhe-zuo-shuangmian-jiandie-lu05uaxp5b_buvl5go2bn7iy9q_x|Hanji 304-1]] says Lei Bei reported Liu An and Liu Qian early enough to escape the executions that reached other members of the Huainan circle.

Political timing:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-304-1-gudai-ruhe-zuo-shuangmian-jiandie-lu05uaxp5b_buvl5go2bn7iy9q_x|Hanji 304-1]] attributes the court's restraint to fear that open action during the Xiongnu war could trigger princely revolt.

## Qualifications

None of the sources reproduces a complete Lei Bei confession or independently establishes exactly what he knew, what the court learned from him rather than later inquiry, or why he survived. Hanji 294-2's sword injury, deliberate-harm suspicion, blocked career judgment, assassination fear, and intended explanation remain episode-attributed. Hanji 295 calls him “雷备”; the identity is normalized from the shared Huainan service dispute and later Lei Bei comparison. His membership in the 淮南八公 and the claim that he directly inspired Wu Bei also remain episode-attributed.

## What Changed

- Added the sword-bout injury and Liu Qian's hostile interpretation as the source-scoped origin of Lei Bei's unsafe position.
- Extended the sequence backward from petition to blocked enlistment, nighttime flight, and intended appeal for central protection.
