## Overview

雷被 / Lei Bei is a Huainan retainer whose blocked attempt to seek central military service becomes an early disclosure channel for the alleged conspiracy of [[LiuAn|刘安]] and [[LiuQianHuainanCrownPrinceWesternHan|刘迁]]. His survival becomes the comparison for [[WuBeiWesternHan|伍被]]'s later, unsuccessful confession.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-295-doufu-de-famingzhe-jing-shi-zaofan-de-fanwang-libnwsqalvpzymlnbaewua4s6miw|Hanji 295]] gives the grievance concrete form. Lei Bei wants to leave Huainan, enlist at the capital, and serve in the war, but Liu An and Liu Qian prevent him. He escapes and petitions the court about his treatment. The廷尉 inquiry then detects a larger rebellion concern, although the episode does not reproduce a complete confession by Lei Bei.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-304-1-gudai-ruhe-zuo-shuangmian-jiandie-lu05uaxp5b_buvl5go2bn7iy9q_x|Hanji 304-1]] looks back on that survival and treats Lei Bei as an early denouncer of father and son. It further argues that the court handled Liu An cautiously because an unresolved Xiongnu war made premature confrontation with a vassal king dangerous.

Lei Bei matters here less as a complete biography than as a timing comparison. His alleged early disclosure remained useful while the center wanted intelligence without immediate escalation; Wu Bei's later confession arrived after arrests and exposure had changed the court's priorities.

## Key Characteristics

- Huainan insider associated by the episode with Liu An's intimate retainer circle.
- Retainer who is blocked from seeking central military service, escapes Huainan, appeals at the capital, and survives the case.
- Alleged source of information about Liu An and Liu Qian's conspiracy.
- Comparison case showing how timing and central political need can alter the value of defection.

## Evidence

Early denunciation:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-295-doufu-de-famingzhe-jing-shi-zaofan-de-fanwang-libnwsqalvpzymlnbaewua4s6miw|Hanji 295]] links Lei Bei's departure to obstructed military service and says his petition triggers a廷尉 investigation that discovers a larger risk.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-304-1-gudai-ruhe-zuo-shuangmian-jiandie-lu05uaxp5b_buvl5go2bn7iy9q_x|Hanji 304-1]] says Lei Bei reported Liu An and Liu Qian early enough to escape the executions that reached other members of the Huainan circle.

Political timing:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-304-1-gudai-ruhe-zuo-shuangmian-jiandie-lu05uaxp5b_buvl5go2bn7iy9q_x|Hanji 304-1]] attributes the court's restraint to fear that open action during the Xiongnu war could trigger princely revolt.

## Qualifications

Neither source reproduces a complete Lei Bei confession or independently establishes exactly what he knew, what the court learned from him rather than later inquiry, or why he survived. Hanji 295 calls him “雷备”; the identity is normalized from the shared Huainan service dispute and later Lei Bei comparison. His membership in the 淮南八公 and the claim that he directly inspired Wu Bei also remain episode-attributed.

## What Changed

- The profile now distinguishes Lei Bei's documented grievance narrative from the later episode's inference that he fully denounced the conspiracy.
- His appeal now has a concrete trigger: obstruction of his attempt to seek central military service.
