## Overview

王元 / Wang Yuan is currently the Wei Xiao-side adviser whose [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-698-wangchao-xingshuai-qishilu-weiao-de-yihan-yu-jinchao-zhi-jing-lqim2hbjtdzb0wwmsaxcyzj7u8ff|Hanji 698]] role is to defend continued autonomy after [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|隗嚣]] has sent his son to Luoyang. He argues that the world is still unsettled enough for Wei Xiao to hold terrain, preserve forces, and wait rather than submit fully to [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]].

## Current Profile

Wang Yuan's advice is the strongest internal statement of the holdout line in the source. He recalls the earlier collapse of Liu Xuan's apparently broad support to warn that current momentum can reverse, then points to [[GongsunShuEasternHan|公孙述]], [[LuFangEasternHan|卢芳]], and other claimants as evidence that Liu Xiu has not yet made the realm inevitable.

The practical plan is defensive and geographic. Wang Yuan tells Wei Xiao to use intact Tianshui resources, strong troops, Hangu Pass closure, and local terrain to protect the base and watch for changes. The advice turns non-submission into a strategy rather than mere fear.

The page keeps Wang Yuan distinct from later or unrelated Wang Yuan names by anchoring him to Wei Xiao's camp. Later Hanji source notes mention Wang Yuan on the Wei Xiao/Gongsun Shu side, but this page is currently grounded only where Hanji 698 makes his reasoning explicit.

## Key Characteristics

- Wei Xiao-side adviser who gives the clearest source-backed holdout argument.
- Reads the wider field as still unsettled because Gongsun Shu, Lu Fang, and other claimants remain.
- Uses Liu Xuan's collapse as a warning against overreading early momentum.
- Treats Tianshui troops, terrain, and Hangu Pass closure as autonomy tools.
- Negative contrast to Shentu Gang's submission advice in the same episode.

## Qualifications

The current page is source-bounded to Hanji 698. Later source notes mention Wang Yuan in the Wei Xiao and Gongsun Shu field, but this page does not yet reconstruct his later command, titles, or final outcome. The semantic suffix prevents collision with other possible Wang Yuan figures.

## What Changed

- Created a canonical page for Wang Yuan because Hanji 698 makes his advice causal in Wei Xiao's continued fence-sitting.
