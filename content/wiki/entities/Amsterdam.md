# Amsterdam

Amsterdam appears in [[695-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-the-shadow-war-part-5-glt5637968455]] as the northern Dutch city strengthened by [[Antwerp]]'s fall. The episode says many Protestant bankers and merchants left Antwerp after its 1585 surrender and moved north into Holland, helping Amsterdam become a major centre of 17th-century capitalism.

In the wiki, Amsterdam matters as a consequence of war-driven migration and capital relocation. [[AlexanderFarnese|Alexander Farnese / Duke of Parma]] wins a southern victory for [[Spain]], but the permitted Protestant exodus changes the economic geography of the [[LowCountries|Low Countries]].

The page remains source-scoped: the episode uses Amsterdam as the outcome of the Antwerp branch, not as a full city history.
