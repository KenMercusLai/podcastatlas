# 韩釐王 / King Xi of Han

韩釐王 / King Xi of Han appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-65-wo-chuanyue-qu-gei-langman-de-chuwang-nian-shou-shi-lvyd7pve-tfu4ixsqrbdvhm6g64j]] as Han Ji, the son who succeeds after [[HanXiangwang|韩襄王]] dies in 296 BCE.

The same episode records Qin returning [[WusuiWarringStates|武遂]] to Han after the five-state anti-Qin coalition reaches [[EzhiWarringStates|阏氏]]. The source keeps the succession compact, so this page treats Han Xi Wang as a ruler-line marker inside a larger coalition-fragility and returned-land diplomacy scene.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-72-shenme-yang-de-ren-cai-shi-zhongzhen-zhishi-logxegnqexgc7z-lodw6tr6-8f28]] adds another compact notice. In 284 BCE, Han Xi Wang meets [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] and [[WeiZhaowang|魏昭王]] at [[Luoyi|洛邑]], keeping Han visible in the post-Qi-collapse diplomatic field.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-83-zhuhou-bailan-jing-chengle-jingdian-ji-zhong-ji-ljp7nbqnrctr5mw6i9u982abmx-1]] closes his reign in the aftermath of [[HuayangBattle|华阳之战]]. The episode's main Han story is not the king's personal decision-making but the state's survival maneuver: Han turns a Wei-Zhao attack into a Qin rescue through [[ThreatenedDefectionLeverage|投敌威胁式求援]]. Han Xi Wang then dies, and [[HanHuanhuiwang|韩桓惠王]] succeeds into a Qin-dominated alignment setting.
