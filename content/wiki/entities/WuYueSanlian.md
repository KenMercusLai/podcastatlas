# 伍玥 / Wu Yue (Sanlian)

伍玥 / Wu Yue is a [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] speaker in [[ep272-shui-de-qingchun-li-meiyou-yi-ben-bei-chuanyue-dao-juanbian-de-zazhi-lmwyj84wfdgnlgsovkihulfailu]], where she reconstructs magazine memory through family subscriptions, classroom bans, youth literature, and Hallyu fan culture. Her earliest examples include her father's 《辽宁青年》 and later school-era magazines that were bought, hidden, confiscated, and passed among classmates.

Her contribution is strongest on the social and affective side of magazine reading. She treats teen magazines, fantasy literature, and idol magazines not only as information sources, but as objects that made aesthetics, friendship, language interest, and cross-cultural curiosity feel available in ordinary school life.
