# 蒋永亮 / Jiang Yongliang

## Overview
蒋永亮 is a gastroenterologist who explains common digestive symptoms, gastrointestinal-cancer risk and warning signs, endoscopy, pathology, and follow-up in two podcast sources.

## Current Profile
Across the sources, his approach joins everyday symptom literacy to structured evaluation. He explains gas, constipation, diarrhea, hydration, bleeding, longitudinal bowel change, diet and Helicobacter pylori without treating a single food or symptom as a diagnosis. When risk rises, he connects those observations to endoscopy, pathology, polyp treatment, cancer screening, or contextual interpretation of imaging findings.

His endoscopy account treats the procedure as a diagnostic and follow-up pathway rather than a completed event once the scope is removed. Image and pathology reports complement one another, while diet and activity restrictions depend on what actually occurred during the procedure.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains common digestive symptoms through pattern, mechanism, persistence, and warning signs rather than one-cause shortcuts.
- Frames gastrointestinal-cancer prevention through long-term risk context, screening, endoscopy, and polyp detection rather than fear of one food.
- Explains endoscopy findings through pathology, lesion type, size, and treatment context.
- Emphasizes that image and pathology reports should be interpreted together.
- Connects post-procedure diet and activity restrictions to biopsy, polyp removal, clips, and bleeding risk.
- Uses patient cases and his own unsedated gastroscopy experience to make mechanisms and care pathways understandable.
