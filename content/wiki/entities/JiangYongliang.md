# 蒋永亮 / Jiang Yongliang

## Overview
蒋永亮 is the gastroenterologist in the source who explains gastroscopy, colonoscopy, biopsy, polyp treatment, pathology, recovery, and follow-up.

## Current Profile
His account treats gastrointestinal endoscopy as a diagnostic and follow-up pathway rather than a completed event once the scope is removed. He interprets the patient case, distinguishes hyperplastic from adenomatous polyps, explains why image and pathology reports complement one another, and ties diet and activity restrictions to what actually occurred during the procedure.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains endoscopy findings through pathology, lesion type, size, and treatment context.
- Emphasizes that image and pathology reports should be interpreted together.
- Connects post-procedure diet and activity restrictions to biopsy, polyp removal, clips, and bleeding risk.
- Uses his own unsedated gastroscopy experience to explain gagging, saliva, breathing, and patient communication.
