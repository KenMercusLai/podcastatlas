# 马陵之战 / Battle of Maling

马陵之战 / Battle of Maling appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-18-zhongguo-lishishang-di-yi-li-rentoushui-lgqnequk0rxejqgihwdqxqyqnsvq]] as the event previewed for the year after [[ZhouHegemonRecognition|Zhou recognition of Qin hegemon status]]. The host frames it as a major [[QiState|齐国]]-[[WeiState|魏国]] confrontation and the final duel between [[SunBin|孙膑]] and [[PangJuan|庞涓]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-19-maling-zhi-zhan-lhbapt09x2ptdqx0uopkp8apbada]] begins the actual campaign setup. [[WeiState|魏国]] attacks [[HanState|韩国]], Han asks [[QiState|齐国]] for help, and [[SunBin|孙膑]] persuades [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] to practice [[DelayedRescueStatecraft|delayed rescue]]: reassure Han privately, wait until Han is desperate and Wei is tired, then intervene.

The battle branch repeats but changes the [[EncirclingWeiToRescueZhao|围魏救赵]] logic from [[GuilingBattle|桂陵之战]]. Qi again moves toward [[Daliang|大梁]] rather than the ally's battlefield, forcing Wei to react. Wei recalls [[PangJuan|庞涓]] from the Han front and appoints [[PrinceShenWei|太子申]] to command another army, which the source uses to explain [[CrownPrinceCommandRisk|太子挂帅风险]].

The episode stops before narrating the final ambush in detail, but it gives Sun Bin's operational premise. He reads Wei troops as contemptuous of Qi and designs a plan around [[ForcedMarchOperationalRisk|forced-march risk]]: lure Pang Juan into urgent pursuit, make speed consume the Wei army's strength, and fight with rested Qi forces.
