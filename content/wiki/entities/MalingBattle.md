# 马陵之战 / Battle of Maling

马陵之战 / Battle of Maling appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-18-zhongguo-lishishang-di-yi-li-rentoushui-lgqnequk0rxejqgihwdqxqyqnsvq]] as the event previewed for the year after [[ZhouHegemonRecognition|Zhou recognition of Qin hegemon status]]. The host frames it as a major [[QiState|齐国]]-[[WeiState|魏国]] confrontation and the final duel between [[SunBin|孙膑]] and [[PangJuan|庞涓]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-19-maling-zhi-zhan-lhbapt09x2ptdqx0uopkp8apbada]] begins the actual campaign setup. [[WeiState|魏国]] attacks [[HanState|韩国]], Han asks [[QiState|齐国]] for help, and [[SunBin|孙膑]] persuades [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] to practice [[DelayedRescueStatecraft|delayed rescue]]: reassure Han privately, wait until Han is desperate and Wei is tired, then intervene.

The battle branch repeats but changes the [[EncirclingWeiToRescueZhao|围魏救赵]] logic from [[GuilingBattle|桂陵之战]]. Qi again moves toward [[Daliang|大梁]] rather than the ally's battlefield, forcing Wei to react. Wei recalls [[PangJuan|庞涓]] from the Han front and appoints [[PrinceShenWei|太子申]] to command another army, which the source uses to explain [[CrownPrinceCommandRisk|太子挂帅风险]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-20-pangjuan-si-ci-shu-xia-ls-8-3jhz5q1c5ca11mhdzmz4wg4]] completes the battle outcome. [[SunBin|孙膑]] turns camp logistics into [[DecreasingStovesDeception|减灶诱敌]], reducing the visible stove count from ten wan to five wan to two wan so [[PangJuan|庞涓]] reads Qi's movement as desertion. Pang Juan leaves the slower main force, pursues at doubled pace, reaches Maling at night, lights a torch to read the "庞涓死此树下" inscription, and triggers the prepared volley.

The same source makes Maling a turning point rather than only a famous trick. Pang Juan kills himself, Wei's army collapses, Qi attacks through the disorder, and [[PrinceShenWei|太子申]] is captured. The host also gives a present-day traditional location near Beimaling in Dazhangjia Town, Shen County, Liaocheng, Shandong, but keeps the dramatic story and location note source-scoped.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-21-zouji-xia-yinzhao-tianji-guowai-binan-lkvmjoxolgfgw-ig7ir-ngvjifij]] gives Maling a court-political afterlife. The same victory that damages Wei raises [[TianJi|田忌]]'s prestige enough for [[ZouJi|成侯邹忌]] to treat him as a rival, culminating in the staged divination and treason accusation that sends Tian Ji and [[SunBin|孙膑]] out of Qi.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-23-weihuiwang-hui-buyong-gongshu-zhi-yan-lveaqklo7oncmqudgmdk7l03qhsf]] gives Maling a methodological afterlife. The episode compares Sun Bin's reduced-stove deception with Shang Yang's capture of Gongzi Ang, arguing that Maling's tactic is more reusable and less personally trust-destroying even though Shang Yang's immediate payoff is larger.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-27-weiguan-weihuiwang-he-qiweiwang-shi-za-shangye-hupeng-lminlxhrm5bethub9kyy-8blimrb]] gives Maling a diplomatic afterlife. Qi's victory prestige makes [[TianYing|田婴]] push for a Qi-centered order, while Wei's defeat makes [[HuiShi|惠施]] advise [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王 / 梁惠王]] to accept [[LowPostureStatecraft|low-posture]] diplomacy. The result is [[XuzhouMutualKingship|徐州相王]], where the battle's power shift becomes title recognition rather than another immediate campaign.
