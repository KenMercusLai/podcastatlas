## Overview

济北王刘志 / Liu Zhi is the Western Han prince whose ambiguous exposure during the Seven States crisis is reclassified through [[GongsunJueWesternHan|公孙爵]]'s advocacy, allowing him to avoid collective punishment and become king of Zichuan.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-236-qiguo-zhiluan-pingding-hanjingdi-shafengle-2-lisudlhjt_yawpyv4i8chvxl37cr|Hanji 236 part 2]] says Liu Zhi fears implication strongly enough to consider suicide while seeking protection for his wife and children. Gongsun Jue argues that Jibei's weakness made open early refusal dangerous, but its continued defense and refusal to surrender materially deprived Wu of support. [[LiuWuLiangKingWesternHan|梁孝王刘武]] carries the claim to [[HanJingdi|汉景帝]], after which Liu Zhi avoids collective punishment and is reassigned to Zichuan.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han king of Jibei during the Seven States crisis.
- Prince exposed to suspicion despite not surrendering his city in the episode's account.
- Ruler whose fear of collective punishment nearly produces suicide.
- Beneficiary of Gongsun Jue's strategic-conduct defense and Liu Wu's mediation.
- Reassigned as king of Zichuan rather than destroyed with the leading rebels.
