# Ahmad al-Sharah

Ahmad al-Sharah is the Syrian leader figure in [[a-firm-andy-what-are-new-british-pms-plans-6a5deeca76f8ee25bb4bbf9c]]. The episode says [[Syria]] has been more peaceful since he gained power and presents him as the right person by default because no other obvious national figure can hold the country together.

The source keeps al-Sharah's position fragile. It mentions assassination attempts, militia threats, [[ISIS]] risk, and investor-confidence problems, while also saying Gulf states and the [[UnitedStates]] do not want Syria's new state project to fail. That makes him central to [[PostwarReconstructionFinance]] because reconstruction money depends on political survival as well as project economics.
