# Bob Lewis

Bob Lewis appears in [[a-firm-andy-what-are-new-british-pms-plans-6a5deeca76f8ee25bb4bbf9c]] as one of the planners behind [[Greenmarket]]. The episode says he described French-style outdoor markets where food could be picked in the morning and sold directly to the public.

The source uses Lewis to make the direct-producer logic explicit. Cutting out intermediaries helped [[NewYorkCity|New York City]] residents access fresh regional produce while giving farmers a city customer base large enough to change planting and sales decisions.
