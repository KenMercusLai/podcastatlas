# 射犬 / Shequan (Late Han)

## Overview
射犬 / Shequan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1032-duidai-pantu-caocao-weihe-renci-lmrr3c2f2dayectlwndt4p-katuy|Hanji 1032]] as a late-Han Yellow River north-bank site tied to [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s operation against [[SuiGuLateHan|眭固]]. The episode places Shequan in the transition from local northern fighting to the direct Cao-Yuan border before [[GuanduCampaign|官渡]].

## Current Profile
Shequan functions as a tactical waypoint with strategic consequences. Sui Gu attempts to connect with [[YuanShao|袁绍]] but is killed at Quan City, Shequan then surrenders, and Cao Cao's capture of [[WeiZhongLateHan|魏种]] there becomes a visible talent-clemency episode before Cao camps at [[Aocang|敖仓]].

## Key Characteristics
- Yellow River north-bank campaign setting in Jian'an 4 / 199 CE.
- Linked to Cao Cao's move against Sui Gu after the Henei and northern-frontier disturbances.
- Surrender point after Sui Gu is killed while trying to reach Yuan Shao.
- Capture site for Wei Zhong and therefore part of Cao's talent-use story.
- Bridge between local pacification and the Guandu approach.

## Evidence
- Campaign setting: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1032-duidai-pantu-caocao-weihe-renci-lmrr3c2f2dayectlwndt4p-katuy|Hanji 1032]] has Cao Cao cross the Yellow River and send Shi Huan and [[CaoRen|曹仁]] against Sui Gu before the Shequan surrender.
- Yuan connection attempt: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1032-duidai-pantu-caocao-weihe-renci-lmrr3c2f2dayectlwndt4p-katuy|Hanji 1032]] says Sui Gu tries to go north to Yuan Shao but is killed at Quan City.
- Talent-clemency setting: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1032-duidai-pantu-caocao-weihe-renci-lmrr3c2f2dayectlwndt4p-katuy|Hanji 1032]] makes Shequan the place where Wei Zhong is captured and then spared.
