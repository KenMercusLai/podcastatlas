# Tel Tel Piyot

Tel Tel Piyot is the new settler outpost in [[settler-bet-a-west-bank-flashpoint-6a842bb539e73ea8f3c13a2d]] that the episode places near [[Kusra]] in the [[WestBank|West Bank]]. The source treats it as a concrete example of how an outpost can begin as a small physical foothold and then reshape Palestinian access to nearby houses, fields, grazing land, or olive groves.

The outpost is therefore a node in [[WestBankOutpostEscalation]]. Its importance is not only the local clash but the broader political reading supplied by [[AnshelPfeffer]]: if such incidents provoke larger unrest, they can feed [[SettlerProvocationStrategy]] and pull the Israeli army deeper into West Bank cities.
