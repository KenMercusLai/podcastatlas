# 赵独 / Zhao Du (late Han)

## Overview
赵独 / Zhao Du (late Han) enters the wiki through Hanji 1070 as a Youzhou rebel active after Cao Cao receives a wave of northern submissions. His role shows that formal surrender does not automatically stabilize local order.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1070 pairs Zhao Du with [[HuoNuLateHan|霍奴]]. They rebel in Youzhou and kill the Youzhou inspector and Zhuo commandery administrator. Cao Cao later personally campaigns against Zhao Du, kills the rebel leaders, and then crosses the Lu River to relieve [[XianyuFu|鲜于辅]] at Kuangping from Wuhuan pressure.

## Key Characteristics
- Youzhou rebel active after Yuan-family collapse and Cao-side surrender waves.
- Paired with Huo Nu in killing provincial and commandery officials.
- Target of Cao Cao's personal punitive expedition.
- Evidence that Cao Cao's northern settlement still faces armed local disorder.
