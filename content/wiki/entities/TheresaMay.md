# Theresa May

## Overview

Theresa May was the British prime minister whose prolonged 2017-2019 loss of authority is used as the episode's clearest modern example of political survival continuing long after leadership strength has visibly drained away.

## Current Profile

[[193-how-prime-ministers-fall-glt7608869237]] traces May from a strong accession honeymoon and the slogan "Brexit means Brexit" to the damaging 2017 general-election campaign, loss of her Commons majority, repeated Brexit defeats, and eventual resignation in May 2019. She survived a December 2018 Conservative confidence vote by 200 to 117 and a January 2019 Commons confidence vote by 325 to 306. Those victories kept her formally in office but did not restore the authority needed to settle [[Brexit]] or reunify her party.

## Key Characteristics

- Began with strong party and public expectations after becoming prime minister.
- Made delivering Brexit the defining task of her premiership.
- Lost her parliamentary majority in the June 2017 general election.
- Survived both party and Commons confidence votes after her authority had sharply weakened.
- Remained in office through repeated Brexit defeats before resigning in May 2019.
