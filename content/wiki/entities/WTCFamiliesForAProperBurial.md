# WTC Families for a Proper Burial

## Overview

WTC Families for a Proper Burial is a family advocacy organization seeking dignified relocation and disposition of the fines produced by the World Trade Center recovery operation at Fresh Kills.

## Current Profile

The organization treats the fines as materially uncertain rather than as proved human remains or ordinary refuse. Its claim is that possible commingled remains deserve a burial or memorial setting even when no family can prove that a named relative is present.

Members pursued public meetings, protest, legislation, negotiation, and litigation. Their preferred outcome was incorporation at Ground Zero, with Governors Island or a non-garbage Fresh Kills location presented as alternatives. The city's 2004 decision to leave the material in place and the dismissal of the 2005 suit left the principal demand unresolved.

## Key Characteristics

- Family-led organization focused on the disposition of the fines.
- Frames relocation as dignity, burial, and family-agency questions.
- Uses political, legislative, public, and legal channels.
- Accepts uncertainty about individual identity while rejecting uncertainty as a reason for inaction.
- Remains in conflict with the city's site and classification decisions.
