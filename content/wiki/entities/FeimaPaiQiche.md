# 《飞马牌汽车》

《飞马牌汽车》 is the [[ZhengYuanjie|郑渊洁]] story at the center of [[23-feima-pai-qiche-anquan-shi-di-yi-wei-de-572699943]]. The episode presents it as a children's story that becomes more unsettling when reread as adult satire: a fast new bus is trapped in traffic, but every concrete request is blocked by the rule that a public bus may not open its doors before reaching a stop.

The story's force comes from escalation. A blocked door becomes associations, police guarding, a traffic-jam bureau, remote services, childbirth by video, corpse preservation, fees, hotels, research products, promotions, foreign exchange, and finally a bus that no longer wants to move. In the wiki, it anchors [[SafetyAsControl]], [[RuleIdolatry]], [[BureaucraticAbsurdityEscalation]], and [[ProblemIndustrialization]].

## Key Claims
- The story's satire depends on people obeying rules that sound reasonable until the situation changes.
- "Safety first" becomes absurd when it protects a procedure while endangering or humiliating actual passengers.
- The story criticizes systems rather than a single bad actor: drivers, police, officials, media, researchers, vendors, and passengers all adapt to the same stuck situation.
- The ending makes stagnation psychological, not only logistical; the bus is trained out of its original desire to run.
