# Bruce Taylor

Bruce Taylor is named in [[big-lettuce-meets-small-intestine]] as the founder of [[TaylorFarms]]. The source uses his 1995 company as the clearest business case for the shift from whole-head lettuce as a commodity to bagged, chopped, and mixed lettuce as a value-added manufactured product.

The page is intentionally narrow: the episode does not provide a full biography. Taylor's wiki role is to anchor the founding point for [[TaylorFarms]] and the broader [[BaggedSaladIndustrialization]] branch.
