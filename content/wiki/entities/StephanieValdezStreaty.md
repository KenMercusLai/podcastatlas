# Stephanie Valdez Streaty

Stephanie Valdez Streaty appears in [[tech-20260407-0407-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260407-0407-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the [[CoxAutomotive]] voice explaining why American consumers remain slower to buy EVs. Her contribution is the price diagnosis: the source frames U.S. EV adoption around sticker price and the remaining premium over gasoline vehicles.

In the episode, her comments help separate interest in EVs from actual purchase conversion. That makes her part of the wiki's [[ElectricVehiclePriceParity]] branch, where buyer economics, not only climate preference or gas-price anxiety, drives adoption.
