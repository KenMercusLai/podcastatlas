# The Second Mountain / 第二座山

[[TheSecondMountain|《第二座山》]] appears in [[154-sishisui-ganyan-bu-zuo-nazhi-wenshuili-de-qingwa-lg-qj3p2lq-8sk-rn2vk209tq9x]] as the source of the [[TelosCrisis|telos crisis]] frame. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] uses the term to explain why a successful finance role can still feel empty when the person no longer knows what the continued climbing is for.

The source does not analyze the book as literature or biography. Its wiki role is narrower: it supplies language for the moment when external success, income, and title stop answering the question of purpose.
