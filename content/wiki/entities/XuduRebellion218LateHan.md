## Overview

The [[XuduRebellion218LateHan|218 CE Xu rebellion]] is presented as a failed Han-loyalist surprise attack against Cao Cao's court-control apparatus in [[Xudu|许都]].

## Current Profile

The conspirators plan to exploit festival security, kill Wang Bi, free [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]], and obtain outside support. They mortally wound Wang Bi but do not disable the wider command system. Yan Kuang reaches organized troops, suppresses dispersed attackers, and leaves [[CaoCao|曹操]] able to turn the failure into a broad political purge.

## Key Characteristics

- Surprise and decapitation plan dependent on multiple unverified conditions.
- Tactical injury to a commander without strategic destruction of command continuity.
- Weak unified command and insufficient preparation for sustained street fighting.
- Failure followed by collective punishment and tighter court control.

## Qualifications

The episode compresses chronology and dramatizes dialogue. The exact date, the attackers' command relationships, Wang Bi's death, the scale and legal mechanism of the purge, and the claimed Guan Yu connection require verification against the historical texts.
