## Overview
博物志 / Bowuzhi Podcast is the podcast show that hosts [[yu-duan-zhiqiang-tan-chongming-xiangye-bowuzhi-wo-zhu-changjiang-wei-8212963740-775078]] and [[246-hangzhou-bowuguan-yiwei-hangzhi-haojiu-mei-kan-guo-zheme-tuijian-de-zhanlan-le-8217911423-973793]]. The route is disambiguated from [[Bowuzhi|《博物志》]], the older strange-knowledge text already tracked elsewhere in the wiki.

## Current Profile
Across the current sources, the show functions as a long conversational venue for material culture and lived knowledge. One episode moves through rural life, farming practice, writing, and physical book design; the Hangzhou episode moves through museum interpretation, ancient-book materiality, local publishing history, heritage sites, and food recommendations.

## Key Characteristics
- Allows detailed, slow discussion of rural life and material practice.
- Treats books through lived context, not only summary or promotion.
- Links natural history, ordinary labor, memory, cultural reading, and museum interpretation in one conversation.
- Uses place-based routes such as [[Chongming]] and [[Hangzhou]] to make knowledge concrete.
- Gives room to digress into platform behavior, publishing, childhood, and family work.
