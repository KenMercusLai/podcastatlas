## Overview
博物志 / Bowuzhi Podcast is the podcast show that hosts [[yu-duan-zhiqiang-tan-chongming-xiangye-bowuzhi-wo-zhu-changjiang-wei-8212963740-775078]]. The route is disambiguated from [[Bowuzhi|《博物志》]], the older strange-knowledge text already tracked elsewhere in the wiki.

## Current Profile
In this source, the show functions as a long conversational venue for rural life, natural observation, book discussion, and everyday knowledge. The episode lets the conversation move through typhoon damage, crops, village reuse, property boundaries, writing process, classical reading, and physical publishing without forcing a narrow interview agenda.

## Key Characteristics
- Allows detailed, slow discussion of rural life and material practice.
- Treats books through lived context, not only summary or promotion.
- Links natural history, ordinary labor, memory, and cultural reading in one conversation.
- Gives room to digress into platform behavior, publishing, childhood, and family work.
