# University of Southern California

University of Southern California appears in [[tech-20260209-0209-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260209-0209-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the institution running the [[UnderstandingAmericaStudy]]. The Marketplace Tech episode uses that panel as the experimental setting for testing whether smartphones change survey performance on knowledge questions.

Within this source, USC matters as research infrastructure rather than as a broad institutional profile. The panel makes it possible to compare smartphone and non-smartphone survey modes under assignment rather than treating all device differences as respondent self-selection.

[[can-the-trump-administration-make-college-cheaper]] adds a separate graduate-affordability context. The [[PlanetMoney]] episode cites analysis saying USC is among the institutions with many borrowers potentially affected by [[FederalStudentLoanCaps]], linking the university to the [[BennettHypothesis]], [[GraduateSchoolDebt]], and [[LoanCapAccessRisk]] debate.

[[tech-20260730-0730-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260730-0730-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds USC's own AI education and research strategy. The [[MarketplaceTech]] episode links [[SriNarayanan]]'s [[SignalAnalysisAndInterpretationLab]] work on [[BehavioralSignalProcessing]] to USC's $200 million [[USCStevensSchoolForComputingAndArtificialIntelligence]] investment, new AI major, non-STEM AI minors, and [[HumanCenteredAIEducation]] framing.
