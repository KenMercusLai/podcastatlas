# John Mussington

[[JohnMussington]] is a Barbudan voice in [[sand-heists-and-property-rights-in-the-caribbean-summer-school]] who argues against reducing [[Barbuda]]'s land system to small titled household plots. He describes the island's collective ownership as including land, farming resources, animal resources, real estate value, food security, and clean air.

His role in the source is to make [[CommunalLandTenure]] an economic asset rather than only a cultural attachment. The episode uses his argument to show why [[PropertyRightsAsInvestmentIncentive]] can conflict with [[PropertyRightsCommunityObligations]] when title reform changes who controls the residual value of a place.
