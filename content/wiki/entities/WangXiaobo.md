## Overview
王小波 / Wang Xiaobo appears in the bounded source as a writer mentored by Xu Zhuoyun while studying at the University of Pittsburgh.

## Current Profile
The episode describes a weekly relationship built around editing, discussion, and literary development. Xu reportedly asked Wang to bring his best work, cut roughly one-third of it, and won Wang's agreement with the revision. Their conversations ranged across Chinese social change, freedom, democracy, Western society, Tang tales, and vernacular story traditions, while Xu later recommended *The Golden Age* to a literary-award channel where the work was judged on its own merits.

## Key Characteristics
- Accepted demanding editorial intervention when the revised work persuaded him.
- Used regular conversation with Xu to connect literature, social change, freedom, and comparative society.
- Explored how older Chinese narrative traditions could inform modern fiction.
- Benefited from mentorship and opportunity without having his literary achievement reduced to patronage.
