# NEOM

## Overview

NEOM is a Saudi megaproject used in the current source as both an instrument of [[SaudiVision2030]] and a visible test of the gap between transformation ambition and delivery.

## Current Profile

The episode places NEOM among the large state projects intended to attract capital, create non-oil industries, and announce a new Saudi economic identity. It also says NEOM and The Line were being rescaled, making them evidence in its broader argument that the economic side of Vision 2030 was under pressure.

## Key Characteristics

- Flagship Saudi state-backed megaproject.
- Instrument for tourism, technology, logistics, and non-oil diversification.
- Symbolic announcement of future-oriented national transformation.
- Delivery-risk case when scope, finance, and expectations are reduced.

## Evidence

- Diversification role: [[304-shate-alabo-guoqu-shinian-zai-jinxing-yichang-zenyang-de-zhengzhi-gaige-lsm0nzvie2-h_sgbmhavg7xbv74t]] includes NEOM among Vision 2030’s major projects.
- Retrenchment role: [[304-shate-alabo-guoqu-shinian-zai-jinxing-yichang-zenyang-de-zhengzhi-gaige-lsm0nzvie2-h_sgbmhavg7xbv74t]] reports significant rescaling of NEOM and The Line as evidence of program strain.
- Political meaning: [[304-shate-alabo-guoqu-shinian-zai-jinxing-yichang-zenyang-de-zhengzhi-gaige-lsm0nzvie2-h_sgbmhavg7xbv74t]] situates the project inside [[DevelopmentalAuthoritarianLegitimacy]], where delivery affects the reform project’s credibility.

## Qualifications

The reported reduction percentages and current project status are not independently verified by the supplied source. The episode does not provide a complete engineering, financing, environmental, labor, or construction audit.

## What Changed

- Kept project-scale claims explicitly source-scoped.
