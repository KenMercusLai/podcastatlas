# 李相如 / Li Xiangru (late Han)

李相如 / Li Xiangru (late Han) enters through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-937-zhanshen-zhisi-haoren-zongshi-duozai-duonan-lp7przsmag9ookq7eoisvsmnm5aw|Hanji 937]] as the [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]] administrator who joins rebellion after [[HanSui|韩遂]]'s enlarged force attacks Longxi.

The source uses Li Xiangru as a marker of official-order breakdown. Once the commandery administrator himself follows the rebels, the Liangzhou crisis is no longer only a bandit or frontier-tribal uprising; formal local administration is folding into the revolt.
