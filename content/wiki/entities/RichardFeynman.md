## Overview

Richard Feynman appears in the bounded sources as both a model of independent catastrophic-failure investigation and an example of continuing attachment after bereavement.

## Current Profile

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-gurley-searching-for-feynman-42956628|Bill Gurley's presentation]] emphasizes Feynman's direct interviews, cold-temperature O-ring demonstration, and refusal to let a softened commission report obscure his conclusion. [[502-yuzhou-chaoji-gongcheng-shi-ruhe-kua-diao-de-liuyi-tan-tiaozhanzhehao-shigu-sishi-zhounian-loylfmnxyc-cuajgcn12-v7-ybd|The Challenger episode]] places that intervention inside a wider account of budget pressure, cadence promises, warning suppression, and “go fever.” [[the-science-process-of-healing-from-grief-scim7724129089|The grief episode]] uses his letter to his deceased wife Arline to show that explicit knowledge of death can coexist with love, address, and an internal relationship. Together these appearances concern legible evidence and lived attachment, not a full account of Feynman's life or physics.

## Key Characteristics

- Pursues evidence independently of institutional choreography.
- Makes a technical failure publicly legible through a simple demonstration.
- Treats disagreement inside a commission as something to disclose rather than smooth over.
- Represents personal credibility joined to persistence and inspectable evidence.
- Illustrates how a continuing bond can remain emotionally active after explicit acceptance of a death.

## Evidence

- Direct inquiry: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-gurley-searching-for-feynman-42956628|Bill Gurley's presentation]] says Feynman visited NASA facilities and interviewed personnel independently.
- Public demonstration: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-gurley-searching-for-feynman-42956628|Bill Gurley's presentation]] recounts his ice-water demonstration of cold-stiffened O-ring material.
- Report independence: [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-bill-gurley-searching-for-feynman-42956628|Bill Gurley's presentation]] says he threatened to remove his name when the commission report softened his findings, after which his notes appeared as an appendix.
- Organizational context: [[502-yuzhou-chaoji-gongcheng-shi-ruhe-kua-diao-de-liuyi-tan-tiaozhanzhehao-shigu-sishi-zhounian-loylfmnxyc-cuajgcn12-v7-ybd]] uses Feynman's warning that reality must outrank public relations to connect the seal mechanism with the institutional decision system.
- Continuing attachment: [[the-science-process-of-healing-from-grief-scim7724129089|The grief episode]] reads Feynman's letter to Arline as an example of knowing a person has died while continuing to address and love them.

## Qualifications

The sources compress distinct parts of Feynman's life into illustrative examples. They do not provide a complete biography, physics profile, Challenger chronology, or account of his marriage and bereavement. The two Challenger sources overlap substantially and are not independent confirmation of every technical or institutional detail. The letter is used interpretively to explain continuing attachment, not to diagnose Feynman's grief.

## What Changed

- Added the grief-letter example as a bounded illustration of continuing attachment after loss.
- Added the wider “reality versus public relations” organizational context for the Challenger investigation.
