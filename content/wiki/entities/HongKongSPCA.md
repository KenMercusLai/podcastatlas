# Hong Kong SPCA / 香港爱护动物协会

## Overview
[[HongKongSPCA|Hong Kong SPCA / 香港爱护动物协会]] is the mainstream Hong Kong animal-protection institution discussed in the 旺旺 episode through its statement on the case and the backlash over calls to remove cruelty footage.

## Current Profile
The source presents the organization as a long-standing and mainstream animal-protection body in [[HongKong|Hong Kong]]. In the 旺旺 case, it expressed grief and anger, reported the video to Hong Kong police, and asked platforms such as [[YouTube]] and Threads to remove the footage. Critics treated this as too close to suppression, while the organization explained the request as a harm-reduction step to protect young viewers and reduce imitation risk. Its wiki significance is the institutional side of [[CrueltyFootageVisibilityDilemma]].

## Key Characteristics
- The source identifies it as a historically established, mainstream animal-protection institution in Hong Kong.
- Its response combined condemnation of animal cruelty with a request to remove graphic footage.
- The request created public backlash because visibility and evidence were already contested in mainland spaces.
- Its explanation emphasized youth distress and imitation risk, not indifference to the case.

## Evidence
- Institutional response: [[liulangquan-wangwang-zhisi-he-yichang-kuajing-dongbao-shengyuan]] reports the July 15 statement, police report, platform takedown request, later criticism, and cancellation of a planned flag-selling activity.
- Visibility dilemma: [[liulangquan-wangwang-zhisi-he-yichang-kuajing-dongbao-shengyuan]] frames the dispute as a genuine tension between preventing secondary harm and keeping a suppressed case visible.
