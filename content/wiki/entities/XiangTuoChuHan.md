# 项佗 / Xiang Tuo (Chu-Han)

项佗 / Xiang Tuo enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-155-2-zhuan-gei-ni-piqi-da-de-pengyou-weiwang-panhan-jing-shi-liubang-ai-maren-ls5nvunmhakvefd2oxn4zdyoob4l|Hanji 155-2]] as the infantry commander named in [[WeiBao|魏豹]]'s army. [[LiShiqi|郦食其]] reports him to [[LiuBang|刘邦]] alongside [[BoZhi|柏直]] and [[FengJingChuHan|冯敬]].

The source reads Xiang Tuo's presence as evidence that Wei Bao's renewed submission to [[XiangYu|项羽]] has concrete military support rather than being only a diplomatic posture. Because the episode does not develop Xiang Tuo beyond this command role, the page keeps the identification limited to the Hanji 155-2 campaign.
