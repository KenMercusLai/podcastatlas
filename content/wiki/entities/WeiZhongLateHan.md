# 魏种 / Wei Zhong (Late Han)

## Overview
魏种 / Wei Zhong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1032-duidai-pantu-caocao-weihe-renci-lmrr3c2f2dayectlwndt4p-katuy|Hanji 1032]] as a former [[CaoCao|曹操]] recommendee who later fled during the [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] crisis. After Cao captures him at [[ShequanLateHan|射犬]], Cao pardons him, personally unbinds him, and appoints him as a commandery governor because he still judges Wei Zhong talented.

## Current Profile
Wei Zhong is a narrow but useful case of talent-based clemency. He is not presented as an independent armed bloc like Zhang Xiu or a dangerous warlord like Lü Bu; the episode instead uses him to show Cao Cao overriding private anger and prior disappointment when an able administrator can still serve the pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] state-building problem.

## Key Characteristics
- Former recipient of Cao Cao's recommendation as xiaolian.
- Prior defector in the Yanzhou-crisis memory that made Cao treat his later capture as personal.
- Captured in the Shequan sequence after Cao's forces move north of the Yellow River against Sui Gu.
- Pardoned and appointed because Cao treats talent as more valuable than revenge.
- Evidence point for selective, strategic clemency rather than blanket forgiveness.

## Evidence
- Prior bond and betrayal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1032-duidai-pantu-caocao-weihe-renci-lmrr3c2f2dayectlwndt4p-katuy|Hanji 1032]] says Cao Cao had recommended Wei Zhong and had expected him not to betray, which makes Wei Zhong's earlier flight matter.
- Shequan capture: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1032-duidai-pantu-caocao-weihe-renci-lmrr3c2f2dayectlwndt4p-katuy|Hanji 1032]] places Wei Zhong's capture in Cao Cao's campaign against Sui Gu and Shequan.
- Talent-based settlement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1032-duidai-pantu-caocao-weihe-renci-lmrr3c2f2dayectlwndt4p-katuy|Hanji 1032]] records Cao's explanation as "for his talent" and pairs pardon with office.

## Qualifications
The current bounded source gives Wei Zhong's role as a short illustrative episode, not a full biography. "Traitor" is the episode-title frame around prior flight and should not be expanded into a general moral judgment beyond the source. The page also does not yet document Wei Zhong's later administrative record.
