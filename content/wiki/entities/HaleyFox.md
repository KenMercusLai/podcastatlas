# Haley Fox

## Overview

Haley Fox is a journalist investigating [[JohnAjay]]'s disappearance and the institutional response for [[ValleyOfShadowsPodcast|Valley of Shadows]].

## Current Profile

In [[from-valley-of-shadows-the-devil-s-punchbowl-from-valley-of-shadows-the-devil-s-punchbowl|The Devil's Punchbowl]], Fox works with [[BetsyShepherd]] to reconstruct Ajay's timeline, examine the search landscape, interview former colleagues and park personnel, and seek records from the [[LosAngelesCountySheriffsDepartment]]. Their reporting treats institutional accountability as part of the mystery without adopting the episode's unverified murder and cover-up allegations as conclusions.

## Key Characteristics

- Investigative journalist and co-reporter on the Ajay case.
- Combines place-based reporting with witness and insider interviews.
- Examines both the missing-person evidence and the department's case handling.
- Maintains open hypotheses in an episode built around unresolved allegations.
