# 苏正和 / Su Zhenghe (late Han)

苏正和 / Su Zhenghe enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] as a Liangzhou subordinate official who prosecutes corruption in the Wuwei commandery field and reports it to the court. His action threatens to implicate [[LiangHuLateHan|梁鹄]], who considers killing him to suppress the problem.

The episode uses Su Zhenghe mainly to reveal [[GaiXunLateHan|盖勋]]'s public-duty boundary. Gai Xun dislikes Su Zhenghe, but still blocks Liang Hu's murder plan; when Su later tries to thank him, Gai avoids the meeting and says the intervention was for Liang Hu's office, not for Su personally.

Su Zhenghe is therefore a source case for [[PublicDutyOverPrivateGrievance|对事不对人式公义]]: his personal relationship with Gai Xun remains bad, but the legality of killing him is judged separately from private feeling.
