# Gordon “Gordie” Ernst

## Overview

Gordon “Gordie” Ernst was the Georgetown tennis coach whom prosecutors placed at the center of the recruiting-slot payment alleged in [[USVKhoury|U.S. v. Khoury]].

## Current Profile

The bounded episode portrays Ernst as having broad discretion over tennis recruits while also operating a summer camp on Georgetown property. Prosecutors alleged that he accepted millions of dollars for recruiting slots, including the Khoury payment, while the defense used Georgetown's accepted wealth cultivation and Ernst's camp discretion to question where institutionally tolerated influence ended and bribery began.

## Key Characteristics

- Former Brown tennis teammate of [[AymanKhoury]] who reconnected with him at a 2014 reunion dinner.
- Georgetown tennis coach with substantial influence over athletic recruiting slots.
- Described by prosecutors as having accepted roughly $3.5 million for nearly two dozen slots.
- Operated a summer tennis camp on Georgetown property and retained its proceeds, according to the episode.
- Discussed prospective recruits and family resources with Georgetown fundraiser [[BrendaSmithGeorgetown]].
