# Gilded Coach Teas

Gilded Coach Teas is the fairy-tale-inspired tea brand founded by [[MarissaValenzuela]] in [[advice-line-with-curt-richardson-of-otterbox-e94bf774-de11-4434-843b-a551309a6773]]. The source says its blends connect to original fairy-tale details such as Cinderella's hazel tree and Snow White's apple, and that the company sells canisters and bags while remaining in two local stores after a one-year founder hiatus.

The Advice Line diagnosis is that the brand's strongest differentiation is the story and experience around the tea, not tea alone. [[CurtRichardson]] and [[GuyRaz]] advise Marissa to restart with consistent direct-to-consumer action, reactivate earlier customers, and test bundles such as tea-party experiences before pursuing broad retail growth.
