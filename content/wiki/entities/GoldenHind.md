# Golden Hind

[[693-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-englands-greatest-hero-part-3-glt5069845329]] adds the ship's voyage history before the public ceremony in episode 694. The ship begins as the Pelican, a small vessel built to [[FrancisDrake]]'s requirements, before Drake renames it the Golden Hind at the Strait of Magellan.

The renaming carries a political sting because the golden hind is [[ChristopherHatton]]'s crest, and Drake has just executed Hatton's secretary [[ThomasDoughty]]. The source then follows the ship through storms, the Pacific capture of the [[NuestraSenoraDeLaConcepcion]], the [[NewAlbion]] claim, the Spice Islands, the Cape of Good Hope, and its September 1580 return to Plymouth.

The Golden Hind appears in [[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] as [[FrancisDrake]]'s ship from his circumnavigation and Spanish treasure raid. By the time Drake returned to [[England]] in September 1580, the ship had become a symbol of global reach, Protestant defiance, and diplomatic danger.

On 1 April 1581, [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]] met Drake aboard the Golden Hind at Deptford and had him knighted. The episode treats the scene as political theatre: crowds gather, the ship becomes a public stage, and Elizabeth has a French envoy perform the dubbing rather than doing it herself.

For the wiki, the Golden Hind is the object through which [[PrivateeringPlausibleDeniability]] becomes visible. Elizabeth can enjoy Drake's glamour and treasure while still using ceremony, delegation, and denial to manage [[Spain]] and [[France]].
