# 黄帝 / Huangdi

黄帝 / Huangdi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-122-3-qinshihuang-jing-he-shenxian-si-qilai-le-ljnw5estg6dvgfllm3kfcq6ulhxu]] as a mythic route model for [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]]'s post-unification touring. The episode suggests, source-scopingly, that Qin Shi Huang's repeated movement across the empire may imitate Huangdi's legendary pattern of traveling broadly after ordering the realm.

The page records Huangdi's role in this source narrowly: not as a general mythology page, but as a legitimating precedent invoked to explain why imperial movement could matter after military conquest was already complete. In that reading, travel makes the ruler resemble a culture-ordering ancestor rather than only a new monarch inspecting territory.
