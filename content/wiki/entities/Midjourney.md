# Midjourney

Midjourney is an AI image-generation tool discussed in two different application contexts in the wiki.

In [[e234-weilai-shipai-dianying-hai-cunzai-ma-yu-daoyan-luchuan-liaoliao-ai-gei-yingshiren-de-kongju-yu-ziyou-b2be7093-3366-4ee2-8a7a-625f06206ae5]], [[KESA]] uses Midjourney to make images while iterating the AI short 《加班夜》, and [[LuChuan]] discusses Midjourney-like workflows as a way to create many character, scene, and action keyframes quickly. The source treats it as a previsualization and ideation tool more than a final-film system: stable style, professional delivery, and film-level aesthetic control still require [[IndustrialGradeFilmModels]].

[[ai-jibao-26q2-cong-coding-dao-rsi-qiangzhe-yu-qiang-de-weilai-1-171-1]] adds a source-reported move into medical-imaging hardware. The episode says Midjourney Medical and Midjourney Scanner use ultrasound transducers and compute-heavy reconstruction to create 3D images of muscles, fat, bones, and organs. It uses this as an example of an AI company reinvesting software cash flow in an unexpected physical-world hardware direction.
