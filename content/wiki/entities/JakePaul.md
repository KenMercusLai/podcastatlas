# Jake Paul

## Overview
Jake Paul is a creator, professional boxer, promoter, investor, and philanthropist who describes audience attention as an asset that can be converted into new businesses and public influence.

## Current Profile
Paul's account links daily internet content, [[Team10]], boxing, [[MostValuablePromotions]], [[AntiFund]], and philanthropy into a [[CreatorBusinessFlywheel]]. His claimed edge is not fame alone but the ability to bring distribution, brand judgment, and audience activation to a new field. The episode also records a possible future move into politics, but this is an aspiration rather than a declared campaign.

## Key Characteristics
- Built an audience through early Vine and YouTube entertainment and sustained publishing.
- Used an existing audience to accelerate a professional boxing and promotion career.
- Frames attention, distribution, and branding as operational inputs rather than endorsements alone.
- Invests through [[AntiFund]] and argues that performance should be judged through realized fund returns.
- Presents philanthropy and athlete opportunity as part of the wider business flywheel.
- Expresses interest in entering politics later in life.
