# 秦松 / Qin Song (Late Han)

秦松 / Qin Song enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-994-genzhe-dongwu-zhongchen-lvfan-xuexi-zhichang-zhihui-lgqp2vqmh1mss96gcwmflp-ccpfu|Hanji 994]] as one of the people [[SunCe|孙策]] brings into deliberation while building his Jiangdong organization. The source names him with [[ChenDuanLateHan|陈端]] after introducing the higher-profile appointments of [[ZhangZhaoLateHan|张昭]] and [[ZhangHongLateHan|张纮]].

The episode does not develop Qin Song's biography. His source-scoped role is to show that Sun Ce's camp is gaining a wider advisory field rather than depending only on personal military charisma. In that sense he belongs to the same organizational-building branch as Zhang Zhao, Zhang Hong, and [[LuFan|吕范]].
