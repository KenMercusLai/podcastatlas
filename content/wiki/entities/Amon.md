# 阿蒙 / Amon

阿蒙 / Amon is the outdoor-sports speaker in [[ep250-zoujin-shanye-shunliu-ershang-xunzhao-huwai-yundong-zhong-de-xinliu-shike-lpv4egumbxcjrxk3jypehyxcqfuo]] who grounds the episode in years of hiking, skiing, and especially climbing practice. She contrasts indoor bouldering with touching real rock at Beijing Baihe, describes breath control at hard climbing moves, and treats fear, falling, frustration, and repeated route attempts as part of the sport rather than as side effects.

Her strongest contribution is [[RockClimbingFailurePractice]]. Amon frames climbing as a practice where bodily skill, fear, emotion, route difficulty, and daily condition are exposed quickly; this makes failure both painful and useful. She also gives the source its most explicit safety boundary by rejecting unprotected climbing for ordinary participants and emphasizing controlled risk, equipment judgment, and realistic ability assessment.
