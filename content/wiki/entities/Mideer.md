# Mideer / 迷鹿

## Overview

Mideer / 迷鹿 is the Ningbo children's toy and puzzle brand in [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]]. The episode uses it to show how Ningbo manufacturing can move from OEM and supply-chain strength into original design, international packaging, illustration, safety standards, and higher-end retail channels.

## Current Profile

Mideer is presented as a design-led brand built by [[QinQiangfeng|秦强峰]] after an earlier children's offline-space business. Its first products centered on large paper puzzles for young children, but the episode stresses details such as illustration, cut lines, piece fit, thickness, burrs, safety testing, and international retail positioning.

The company also makes the city-level argument concrete: design-led brands need artists, designers, display spaces, international buyers, and young people willing to remain in [[Ningbo|宁波]], not only factories.

## Key Characteristics

- Mideer turns Ningbo's toy and printing supply-chain base into original, design-led products.
- The brand is international from the beginning in packaging and market posture.
- Product quality depends on many physical details, not only attractive illustration.
- Toy safety standards add a regulatory and material-control layer to design.
- The source positions Mideer as evidence that Ningbo wants to add aesthetics and brand to manufacturing.
