## Overview

康敏 / Kang Min is a lover of [[DuanZhengchun|段正淳]] in [[TianLongBaBu|《天龙八部》]] who tests romantic language through formal status, material placement, and personal advantage rather than asking the prince to become a wandering Jianghu companion.

## Current Profile

Where several women imagine Duan leaving royal life, Kang Min asks to enter it: Will he take her to Dali, recognize her position, and eventually make her queen? The episode finds this demand more practically aligned with who he is, even while treating her as self-centered, dangerous, and less exclusively attached to him.

Her questions also expose his evasiveness. Instead of giving a direct account of the obstacles, he invokes heat and miasma, making her a stress test for the difference between affectionate language and implementable commitment. The episode does not romanticize her realism; status bargaining can clarify expectations while remaining instrumental, coercive, or lethal.

## Key Characteristics

- Seeks incorporation into princely life rather than renunciation of it.
- Converts love into questions of recognized place, title, and future advantage.
- Places her own interest ahead of exclusive devotion to Duan Zhengchun.
- Forces romantic language to confront practical implementation.
- Combines relative realism about status with manipulation and danger.
