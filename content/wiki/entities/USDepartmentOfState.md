# U.S. Department of State

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-under-secretary-of-state-sarah-b-rogers-on-dismantling-the-censorship-industrial-complex-39828360]] adds the department's public-diplomacy and free-speech branch through [[SarahBRogers|Sarah B. Rogers]]. The episode frames public diplomacy as government-to-foreign-public communication and treats foreign platform regulation as a State Department issue when the [[UKOnlineSafetyAct|UK Online Safety Act]] or [[EuropeanUnionDigitalServicesAct|EU Digital Services Act]] pressure American companies and American speech norms.

The U.S. Department of State appears in [[caracas-under-pressure-democracy-in-venezuela-6a7d8bbe7d85cbdf96e00822]] as the approval point for royalties and taxes from Venezuelan oil revenue. The episode says that approval power gives [[MarcoRubio]] and the [[UnitedStates]] leverage over the [[DelcyRodriguez]] administration during transition talks.

In the wiki, this makes the department a concrete mechanism inside [[OilRevenueSanctionsLeverage]]. The source's claim is not only that sanctions pressure exists, but that permission over revenue flows can be used to bargain over institutional reforms and a possible [[DemocraticTransitionElection]].
