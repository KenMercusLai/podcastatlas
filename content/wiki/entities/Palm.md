# Palm

## Overview
Palm appears in the No.229 [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] source as the PDA and early smartphone ecosystem that shaped [[LaoMaiSspai|老麦 / Michael]]'s device enthusiasm and the initial identity of [[ZhiJiWang|主机网]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Palm through user experience rather than corporate history. Palm's PDA lineage, pocketable size, fast Palm OS feel, square-screen devices, physical keyboards, and rich software ecosystem made it attractive to technically curious users before iPhone-style touch smartphones became dominant.

For the wiki, Palm matters here because it shows how an operating-system ecosystem can produce an enthusiast community. 老麦's Palm 650 use, ROM packaging, blog posts, and user downloads become the direct path into 主机网.

## Key Characteristics
- It is framed as a PDA-to-smartphone lineage rather than only a phone brand.
- Palm OS is described as fast and lightweight because of limited multitasking and embedded-system constraints.
- Palm devices supported a hands-on user culture around software, ROMs, input, and device customization.
- Its ecosystem helped create Chinese enthusiast communities before mainstream mobile-app stores dominated.
- Its webOS successor created a short-lived sense that Palm could re-enter the smartphone platform contest.

## Qualifications
The source is a participant recollection and does not provide a complete Palm corporate, product, or market-share history. Its Newton, PDA, iPhone, and Palm comparisons are used as context for the community story.
