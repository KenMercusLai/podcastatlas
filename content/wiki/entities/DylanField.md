# Dylan Field

## Overview
Dylan Field is the co-founder and chief executive of [[Figma]], represented here through an [[AllIn|All-In]] interview about design, professional software, the failed [[Adobe]] acquisition, and human creativity under AI.

## Current Profile
Field's distinctive position is that AI changes who can produce digital work without eliminating the value of demanding product craft. He treats design as creativity joined to problem-solving and argues that easier implementation raises the relative importance of judgment, point of view, brand, workflow knowledge, and emotional effect.

## Key Characteristics
- Leads Figma as a design-centered software company serving both product design and broader visual work.
- Distinguishes model capability progress from the interaction and workflow work required for product progress.
- Argues that AI lowers the floor for creation but does not automatically raise its ceiling.
- Favors rapid movement between generated material and hands-on manipulation rather than one-way automation.
- Treats visible human attention and authorship as a trust signal in AI-assisted work.
- Frames AI adoption as a choice between surrendering thought and expanding one's capacity to learn and make.
