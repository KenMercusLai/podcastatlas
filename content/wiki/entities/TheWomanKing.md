# The Woman King

## Overview

The Woman King is the film that the episode uses as the modern controversy and popular-history entry point into [[Dahomey]]'s women warriors.

## Current Profile

In this source, the film matters less as a film review than as a trigger for historical interpretation. Dominic Sandbrook introduces the topic through the arguments generated by its portrayal of [[Agojie|Dahomey's women warriors]], especially controversy over slavery and historical distortion. The episode then broadens beyond the film into [[EuropeanAmazonProjection]], [[AfricanFemaleAuthorityInstitutions]], and the difficulty of telling stories about female military power without erasing coercion.

## Key Characteristics

- Modern popular-history frame that brings [[Dahomey]] and the [[Agojie]] into public debate.
- Controversial because the source says its portrayal prompted arguments about slavery and historical distortion.
- Narrative trigger rather than the episode's full subject.
- Helps expose the pressure to convert complex African histories into simple empowerment or condemnation stories.
