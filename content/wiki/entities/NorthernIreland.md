# Northern Ireland

Northern Ireland is the central setting of [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] and [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]]. Part 1 presents it as a statelet created through partition with a two-thirds Protestant majority and a large Catholic minority, producing [[PartitionMinorityTrap]] and [[UnionistSiegeMentality]] before the 1968-1969 crisis.

The sources read the crisis through [[Stormont]], [[Derry]], [[Belfast]], and the [[Bogside]], showing how institutional discrimination, policing distrust, rival commemorations, civil rights protest, paramilitary action, and British military deployment combined. Northern Ireland therefore anchors the wiki's [[TheTroubles]] branch as a case of [[InstitutionalSectarianDiscrimination]], [[ReformTimingTrap|late reform]], [[StatePolicingLegitimacyCrisis|policing legitimacy collapse]], and [[SectarianSecurityDilemma|mutual communal fear]].
