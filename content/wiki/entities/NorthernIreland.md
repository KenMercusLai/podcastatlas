# Northern Ireland

Northern Ireland is the central setting of [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]]. The episode presents it in 1968-1969 as a statelet where Catholic grievances over housing, local voting rights, policing, and political exclusion collided with Protestant fears about security, constitutional identity, and possible absorption into [[Ireland]].

The source reads the crisis through [[Stormont]], [[Derry]], [[Belfast]], and the [[Bogside]], showing how events that began as civil rights protest became street fighting, paramilitary action, and British military deployment. Northern Ireland therefore anchors the wiki's [[TheTroubles]] branch as a case of [[ReformTimingTrap|late reform]], [[StatePolicingLegitimacyCrisis|policing legitimacy collapse]], and [[SectarianSecurityDilemma|mutual communal fear]].
