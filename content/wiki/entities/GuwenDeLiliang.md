# 《古文的力量》

《古文的力量》 is [[DuRuo|杜若]]'s book discussed in [[164-guwen-de-liliang-qing-shou-xia-zhe-fen-qiannian-wei-bian-de-shenqing-dongjian-he-yongqi-940236576]]. The episode says the book collects 17 familiar classical-prose pieces and reads them as essays about human situations, not as a textbook of function words, sentence patterns, or exam answers.

The book's role in the wiki is to make [[ClassicalProseReading]] explicit. It routes classical texts from memorized school passages back into emotional, ethical, and political attention: [[XiangJiXuanZhi|《项脊轩志》]] for intimate grief, [[YouBaochanShanJi|《游褒禅山记》]] for failed ideals, [[ShangShumiHanTaiweiShu|《上枢密韩太尉书》]] for youthful "养气", and [[HongmenYan|《鸿门宴》]] for power details.

## Key Claims
- Classical prose can carry narrative, reasoning, political judgment, and daily detail in ways short poems often cannot.
- A familiar school text may remain unread if the reader only remembers it as recitation material.
- Editorial framing can lower the entry barrier while preserving complexity.
