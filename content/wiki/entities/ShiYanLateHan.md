# 施延 / Shi Yan (late Han)

## Overview

施延 / Shi Yan now appears in the wiki as a recurring Shun-era official. Hanji 801 places him among the Hongdu Gate remonstrants against [[EmperorAnOfHan|汉安帝]]'s demotion of [[EmperorShunOfHan|刘保]], while Hanji 815 later records him as the dahonglu appointed taiwei after [[PangCanLateHan|Pang Can]] is dismissed in 133 CE.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-801-han-andi-weishenme-henxin-fei-taizi-lgnhbsqnp6vdigsmqfkxiqmx2vdy|Hanji 801]] gives Shi Yan an earlier political-speech role. After Liu Bao is deposed as Jiyin Wang on 124-09-07, Shi Yan joins [[LaiLiLateHan|Lai Li]], [[ZhuChangLateHan|Zhu Chang]], and other officials at Hongdu Gate to argue that the prince is blameless.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-815-kebei-gudai-zhongchen-de-jiafeng-zhishang-llkhbxo-63pjw-gk9pihnbfr94v|Hanji 815]] then makes Shi Yan an appointment marker rather than a full policy actor. His promotion closes the Pang Can dismissal sequence: after Pang is removed in the seventh month, the eighth-month first-day appointment makes Shi the new taiwei.

## Key Characteristics

- Hongdu Gate remonstrant who joins the collective defense of Liu Bao in 124 CE.
- Later serves as dahonglu before promotion.
- Becomes taiwei after Pang Can's dismissal in 133 CE.
- Functions in the current evidence as both a remonstrance participant and office-continuity marker.

## Evidence

- Hongdu Gate remonstrance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-801-han-andi-weishenme-henxin-fei-taizi-lgnhbsqnp6vdigsmqfkxiqmx2vdy|Hanji 801]] names Shi Yan among the officials who remonstrate that Liu Bao is blameless after the crown prince is demoted.
- Office transition: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-815-kebei-gudai-zhongchen-de-jiafeng-zhishang-llkhbxo-63pjw-gk9pihnbfr94v|Hanji 815]] says Shi Yan is appointed taiwei on the first day of the eighth month after Pang Can's seventh-month dismissal.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 801 and Hanji 815. The sources do not provide Shi Yan's full policy profile, factional position, exact Hongdu Gate speech, or later actions. The page should remain sparse until later sources add substance.
