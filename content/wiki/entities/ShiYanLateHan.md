## Overview
施延 / Shi Yan now appears as a recurring late-Han official whose current record begins with Emperor An's worthy-and-upright selection process. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-794-yidai-nvjun-dengsui-qushi-hou-jing-quanjia-zaoyang-llmlhabdd09opca6-pr80q4mfoxi|Hanji 794]] records his exceptional examination result and shizhong appointment; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-801-han-andi-weishenme-henxin-fei-taizi-lgnhbsqnp6vdigsmqfkxiqmx2vdy|Hanji 801]] names him among Hongdu Gate remonstrants for Liu Bao; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-815-kebei-gudai-zhongchen-de-jiafeng-zhishang-llkhbxo-63pjw-gk9pihnbfr94v|Hanji 815]] later records his appointment as taiwei.

## Current Profile
Hanji 794 gives Shi Yan's first current layer. After [[ChenZhongLateHan|Chen Zhong]] warns [[EmperorAnOfHan|Emperor An]] to tolerate direct criticism from recommended worthy men, Shi Yan performs especially well and is appointed shizhong. The source does not yet show his policy voice, but it places him inside a court process where criticism and selection are linked.

Hanji 801 gives Shi Yan an explicit remonstrance role. After [[EmperorShunOfHan|Liu Bao]] is deposed as Jiyin Wang on 124-09-07, Shi Yan joins [[LaiLiLateHan|Lai Li]], [[ZhuChangLateHan|Zhu Chang]], and other officials at Hongdu Gate to argue that the prince is blameless.

Hanji 815 then makes Shi Yan an office-continuity marker. His promotion closes the Pang Can dismissal sequence: after [[PangCanLateHan|Pang Can]] is removed in the seventh month, the eighth-month first-day appointment makes Shi the new taiwei.

## Key Characteristics
- Worthy-and-upright examinee appointed shizhong after an unusually strong result.
- Early example of Emperor An accepting a personnel outcome after Chen Zhong's warning to tolerate criticism.
- Hongdu Gate remonstrant who joins the collective defense of Liu Bao in 124 CE.
- Later serves as dahonglu before promotion.
- Becomes taiwei after Pang Can's dismissal in 133 CE.
- Functions in the current evidence as a selection, remonstrance, and office-continuity marker more than a fully developed policy actor.

## Qualifications
This page is bounded to Hanji 815, Hanji 801, and Hanji 794. The sources do not provide Shi Yan's full policy profile, factional position, exact examination text, exact Hongdu Gate speech, or later actions. The page should remain sparse until later sources add substance.
