# 施延 / Shi Yan (late Han)

## Overview

Shi Yan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-815-kebei-gudai-zhongchen-de-jiafeng-zhishang-llkhbxo-63pjw-gk9pihnbfr94v|Hanji 815]] as the dahonglu appointed taiwei after [[PangCanLateHan|Pang Can]] is dismissed in 133 CE.

## Current Profile

Shi Yan's profile is an appointment marker rather than a full biography. His promotion closes the Pang Can dismissal sequence: after Pang is removed in the seventh month, the eighth month first-day appointment makes Shi the new taiwei.

## Key Characteristics

- Serves as dahonglu before the appointment.
- Becomes taiwei after Pang Can's dismissal.
- Functions in the current evidence as a succession and office-continuity marker.

## Evidence

- Office transition: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-815-kebei-gudai-zhongchen-de-jiafeng-zhishang-llkhbxo-63pjw-gk9pihnbfr94v|Hanji 815]] says Shi Yan is appointed taiwei on the first day of the eighth month after Pang Can's seventh-month dismissal.

## Qualifications

The source does not provide Shi Yan's policy profile, factional position, or later actions. The page should remain sparse until later sources add substance.
