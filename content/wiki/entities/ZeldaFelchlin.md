# Zelda Felchlin

## Overview

Zelda Felchlin was the Welby Way Elementary School PTA newsletter editor whose 1962 message supplies the episode's clearest defense of fair inquiry, intellectual honesty, and respect under political pressure.

## Current Profile

[[john-birch-vs-the-pta-john-birch-vs-the-pta]] introduces Felchlin through an otherwise routine April 1962 newsletter containing school notices about parking, safety, a fire-station visit, and a council luncheon. Her closing message responded to a local [[JohnBirchSociety]] takeover attempt and accusations that participating women were dishonest and un-American.

The episode paraphrases her standard as seeking truth justly, rationally, kindly, and without twisting it to serve personal purposes. It also emphasizes willingness to accept truth when it conflicts with prior belief. Felchlin therefore functions less as a comprehensive biographical subject than as the local voice for [[EvidenceBasedCivicCooperation]].

## Key Characteristics

- Edited a local elementary-school PTA newsletter in 1962.
- Responded to political accusation through a defense of inquiry rather than counter-denunciation.
- Joined rational judgment to kindness and procedural fairness.
- Treated belief revision as a democratic virtue rather than a defeat.
- Represents everyday civic writing with consequences beyond its mundane setting.
