# 刘飞

刘飞 is one of the [[Banlatte|半拿铁]] hosts in [[fanwai-14-gen-lidan-liaoliao-boke-chuangzuo-ai-yu-zhongnian-972693552]]. The episode presents him as a former internet product manager with experience around companies such as [[Smartisan]] and [[Didi]], and as the host who also created [[SanWuHuan|三五环]] before 半拿铁 settled into its business-story form.

His source role is to connect product thinking, business storytelling, and podcast operations. He explains how the show handles brand communication, audience trust, and the choice to stay closer to an "individual household" than a scaled media company. He also helps frame podcasts as a horizontal ecosystem where hosts, guests, listeners, and brands form repeated relationships.
