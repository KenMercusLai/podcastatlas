# 《明智的孩子》 / Wise Children

《明智的孩子》 / Wise Children appears near the end of [[38-anjila-kate-jingguai-gushiji-o-mirende-hanfu-609607139]] as a late [[AngelaCarter|Angela Carter / 安吉拉·卡特]] work that the hosts compare in feeling to a female version of [[GabrielGarciaMarquez|Gabriel Garcia Marquez / 加西亚·马尔克斯]]'s family-world scale. The source especially preserves the formulation that fatherhood is a hypothesis while motherhood is real.

The page's wiki role is small but useful: it connects the episode's grandmotherly and maternal storytelling frame to Carter's wider literary system. Mothers, grandmothers, daughters, and female narrators are not only biographical background; they become a way to imagine lineage, story authority, and bodily certainty against patriarchal naming.

## Key Claims
- The source uses the book to connect Carter's fairy-tale feminism to maternal and family narrative.
- Its quoted father/mother contrast supports [[FemaleOralStorytelling]] by making maternal reality a source of story authority.
- The episode treats the novel as part of Carter's broader myth-making system rather than as a separate realist family novel.
