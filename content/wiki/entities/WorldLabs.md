# World Labs

## Overview
World Labs is the Fei-Fei Li-associated AI company that the wiki currently understands through spatial intelligence, generated worlds, robotics simulation, and creator-supporting use cases.

## Current Profile
The current evidence frames World Labs as a move from visual recognition toward spatial and physical intelligence. The What's Next source uses it to discuss simulated-world data for robots and the unresolved question of whether virtual environments transfer to real robotics. The Huberman Lab interview adds Li's own broader description: WorldLabs was founded in early 2024 to generate 3D and 4D worlds useful for creators, robot training, architecture, healthcare, education, robotics, and industry.

## Key Characteristics
- Pursues spatial and physical intelligence beyond language-only AI systems.
- Treats generated 3D and 4D worlds as useful infrastructure for creators and physical-world applications.
- Connects AI video and world generation to robot training and simulation, not only entertainment.
- Keeps creator empowerment central, with human storytelling, camera choice, emotion, and technique still important.
- Leaves real-world transfer, physical accuracy, and robotics evaluation as unresolved constraints.

## Qualifications
The evidence is directional rather than evaluative. It does not establish product maturity, customer adoption, model quality, or whether generated environments can reliably substitute for real robot data.
