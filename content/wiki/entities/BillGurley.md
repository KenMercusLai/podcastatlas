# Bill Gurley

Bill Gurley appears in [[brave-new-whirl-turkeys-opposition-overhaul-6a69c7766043035f89eb097f]] through his career-advice book *Running Down a Dream*. The episode groups the book with [[JodiKantor]]'s *How to Start* as advice that encourages people to pursue passions and dreams.

The source's critique is not that Gurley's advice is useless. It is that highly successful people can become unreliable guides for ordinary graduates when their examples depend on rare paths, exceptional timing, or unusual talent. Gurley therefore becomes part of [[CareerAdviceSurvivorshipBias]] and the episode's challenge to [[PassionTrapCareerAdvice]].
