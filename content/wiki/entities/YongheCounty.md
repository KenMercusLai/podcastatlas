# 山西永和县 / Yonghe County

## Overview
[[YongheCounty]] is the Shanxi county used by the source to examine how outsourced [[DataAnnotationLabor]] briefly connected a small, formerly nationally designated poor locality to the AI supply chain.

## Current Profile
The episode describes Yonghe as more than 300 kilometers from Taiyuan, without a direct high-speed railway connection and with fewer than 50,000 permanent residents. Public employment and low-paid retail or food service form much of the visible local labor market, while many young people migrate and some men take better-paid but hazardous natural-gas work.

This structure made the [[YongheAidoTechnology]] annotation base unusually valuable to mothers who needed to remain near children. It also limited the base's ability to follow the market into projects requiring at least 20 university graduates or specialist language, medical, historical, and 3D skills.

## Key Characteristics
- Small, remote county labor market with limited formal private-sector alternatives.
- Significant youth out-migration and a narrow pool of university-educated local recruits.
- Care responsibilities make regular local hours especially valuable to mothers.
- Brief integration into the AI supply chain through basic data annotation.
- Difficulty retaining upgraded annotation demand once projects required specialized cohorts.
