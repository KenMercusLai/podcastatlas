# 许相 / Xu Xiang (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-938-lishi-de-jingxing-ruanruo-zhihui-dailai-quru-lnlnrketgfbadhfjbzdjjtrmqfsh|Hanji 938]] backfills Xu Xiang's 187 CE office movement before the 189 forged-appointment crisis. The episode records him moving from sikong to situ during a Three Excellencies turnover that also brings [[DingGongLateHan|丁宫]] into the sikong office.

许相 / Xu Xiang (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-946-zhichang-douzheng-shi-yourouguaduan-you-duo-zhiming-lpyyweaajonzg7kxuhwm9z7ae7p5|Hanji 946]] as the official whom the eunuch faction's forged order appoints as Henan yin after killing [[HeJin|何进]]. He is paired with [[FanLingLateHan|樊陵]], who is appointed sili xiaowei in the same attempted reset.

The episode treats Xu Xiang's appointment and death as part of the same institutional collapse. [[ZhangRangLateHan|张让]] and [[DuanGuiLateHan|段珪]] use a false order to name him, then [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and [[YuanWeiLateHan|袁隗]] use another false order to summon and kill him. The office still matters, but the process around it has become weaponized by armed factions.
