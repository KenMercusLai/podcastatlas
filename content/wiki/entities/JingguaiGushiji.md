# 《精怪故事集》

《精怪故事集》 is the [[AngelaCarter|Angela Carter / 安吉拉·卡特]] folk- and fairy-tale collection discussed in [[38-anjila-kate-jingguai-gushiji-o-mirende-hanfu-609607139]]. The episode presents it less as a polite children's anthology than as a rough story world where women act, desire, cheat, escape, punish, suffer, laugh, and survive.

In the source, the book's importance comes from selection and framing. Carter gathers familiar and unfamiliar variants, including Cinderella-like, Snow White-like, Rapunzel-like, animal-spouse, trickster-wife, mother-wit, and gender-reversed sleeping-prince stories, but the episode emphasizes that the women are not cleaned into virtue. They can be selfish, violent, lustful, funny, practical, tender, or cruel without losing narrative force.

## Key Claims
- The book is treated as an antidote to overly sanitized fairy-tale memory.
- Its female-centered stories expand [[FemaleSelfPossession]] beyond good-girl agency into comic, hungry, desiring, and morally impure action.
- Carter's editorial posture connects [[FemaleOralStorytelling]] with [[FeministFairyTaleRewriting]]: old-wife and grandmotherly story energy becomes literary method.
- The collection makes [[CrossCulturalFairyTaleFlow]] visible by placing variants from many regions beside one another.
