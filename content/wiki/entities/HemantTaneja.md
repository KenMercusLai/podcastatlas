# Hemant Taneja

Hemant Taneja appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-why-ai-will-dwarf-every-tech-revolution-before-it-robots-manufacturing-ar-glasses-from-ces-2026-39655790]] as the [[GeneralCatalyst|General Catalyst]] guest. He frames the current environment as "peak ambiguity" and uses [[Anthropic]]'s rapid revenue and valuation growth to argue that AI company-building timelines are compressing.

Taneja's second contribution is more operational than financial. He argues that AI transformation inside incumbents requires data infrastructure, adapted models, and a new workforce model where humans and agents work together. In the physical-AI section, he also warns that robotics may diffuse more slowly than software because robot deployment lacks a cloud-like API layer.
