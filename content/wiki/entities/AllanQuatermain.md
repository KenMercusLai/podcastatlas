# Allan Quatermain

## Overview

Allan Quatermain is the hunter, explorer, adventurer, and narrator at the center of [[KingSolomonsMines|King Solomon's Mines]], later reused as a model and character across adventure fiction.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Quatermain as a practical guide through Haggard's fantastical Africa and as a prototype for later expedition heroes. He joins a small male fellowship seeking treasure and a missing brother, survives Kukuana dynastic conflict and underground entrapment, and returns wealthy.

His conclusion complicates a simple reward story: diamonds do not satisfy him, and wealth leaves him restless. The hosts connect the character to real explorers and hunters Frederick Selous and Joseph Thomson, showing how the fictional adventurer condensed hunting, science, settler expansion, and imperial travel.

## Key Characteristics

- Experienced hunter and guide who makes the expedition plot operationally plausible.
- Narrator linking the reader to an imagined African frontier.
- Member of a homosocial fellowship organized around danger, loyalty, and masculine testing.
- Treasure seeker whose later dissatisfaction weakens the fantasy that wealth completes the quest.
- Composite adventure figure associated by the episode with Selous and Thomson.

## Evidence

- Narrative role: [[332-king-solomons-mines-glt4960691587]] identifies Quatermain as hunter, explorer, narrator, and expedition guide.
- Quest outcome: [[332-king-solomons-mines-glt4960691587]] says he survives the treasure chamber and becomes wealthy but remains unhappy and restless.
- Historical models: [[332-king-solomons-mines-glt4960691587]] names Frederick Selous and Joseph Thomson as inspirations joining hunting, geology, exploration, and empire.

## Qualifications

The source does not establish a one-to-one model for the character, and its treatment is thematic rather than a complete account across every Quatermain novel. Calling him a prototype identifies a broad genre lineage, not direct influence on every later adventurer.

## What Changed

- Created a profile of Quatermain as quest narrator, imperial composite, and qualified treasure hero.
