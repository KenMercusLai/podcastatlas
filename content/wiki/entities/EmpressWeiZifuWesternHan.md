## Overview

卫子夫 / Empress Wei Zifu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-387-1-taizi-liuju-taowang-hou-can-can-can-lodlsf5wtqtvoejjhe4apec7trku|Hanji 387-1]] as the empress whose suicide marks the depth of the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]] catastrophe before [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]]'s final flight and death.

## Current Profile

The source does not provide Wei Zifu's full biography. It uses her death as an index of the crisis's severity: after she kills herself, [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] still does not stop the punitive response against Liu Ju's circle. Her presence therefore works as a household and succession marker in this bounded evidence, linking the Wugu accusation field to the collapse of the empress-crown-prince branch.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han empress named in the Wugu aftermath.
