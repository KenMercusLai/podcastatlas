## Overview

卫子夫 / Empress Wei Zifu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-387-1-taizi-liuju-taowang-hou-can-can-can-lodlsf5wtqtvoejjhe4apec7trku|Hanji 387-1]] as the empress whose suicide marks the depth of the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]] catastrophe before [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]]'s final flight and death. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-386-2-diwang-wuqing-huanghou-weizifu-beipo-zisha-lrvdfib62oczcy7loqss4-prkgl|Hanji 386-2]] now supplies the immediate sequence: after Liu Ju loses the Chang'an fighting, [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] sends officials to collect Wei Zifu's empress seal, but she has already killed herself before the envoys arrive.

## Current Profile

The sources do not provide Wei Zifu's full biography. Hanji 386-2 uses her death as the immediate household consequence of Liu Ju's defeat: the order to collect her empress seal means her political identity is being stripped away, but the envoys arrive only after her suicide. Hanji 387-1 then shows that her death does not stop the punitive response against Liu Ju's circle. Her presence therefore works as a household and succession marker in this bounded evidence, linking the Wugu accusation field to the collapse of the empress-crown-prince branch.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han empress named in the Wugu aftermath.
- Mother/household-side marker for Liu Ju's destroyed succession branch.
- Suicide victim whose death follows Liu Ju's defeat and Wudi's order to collect her empress seal.
- Figure whose death does not halt Han Wudi's continuing punishments.
- Source-scoped evidence of how palace accusation becomes dynastic-family destruction.
