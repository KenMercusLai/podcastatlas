## Overview

张贺 / Zhang He enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-430-chigua-qunzhong-qianren-yu-xianren-de-gongdou-daxi-lkfcjewfhmhfgw39vqfqkpa9umzq|Hanji 430]] as a deceased Ye Tingling remembered by [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] for recognizing, praising, and helping him before accession. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-410-1-yuzhong-zhangdade-hanxuandi-liubingyi-ruhe-chengjiu-baye-loprh4dmeqsexuii4p0j4vuk7tle|Hanji 410-1]] supplies the concrete pre-accession layer: Zhang He cares for Liu Bingyi because of old loyalty to crown prince Liu Ju, funds his study, and brokers his marriage to [[XuPingjunWesternHan|许平君]] through [[XuGuanghanWesternHan|徐广汉]].

## Current Profile

Zhang He matters because his kindness becomes both pre-accession infrastructure and posthumous repayment. Hanji 410-1 shows him using office access and personal wealth while Liu Bingyi is still marginal: he supports the young man, first tries to arrange a Zhang-family marriage, accepts [[ZhangAnshiWesternHan|张安世]]'s objection, and then makes the Xu marriage possible by persuading Xu Guanghan and supplying betrothal resources. Hanji 430 then shows the later reward branch: Xuan says Zhang He had shown him educational and personal favor, rewards Zhang He's adopted line through [[ZhangPengzuWesternHan|张彭祖]], and grants posthumous honor. The combined profile makes Zhang He the clearest case of Xuan's gratitude crossing death and family boundaries.

## Key Characteristics

- Deceased Western Han official remembered as a pre-accession benefactor of Xuan.
- Associated with education, recognition, marriage brokerage, and praise of the future emperor.
- Benefactor whose personal resources helped turn Liu Bingyi's marginal status into a household and marriage network.
- Receives posthumous honor through his adopted heir.
- Central example for Xuan's repayment of old kindness.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 410-1 and Hanji 430 and does not reconstruct Zhang He's full office career, family history, complete relationship with Liu Ju's household, or every detail of the Xu marriage.
