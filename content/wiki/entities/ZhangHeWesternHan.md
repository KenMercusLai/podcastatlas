## Overview

张贺 / Zhang He enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-430-chigua-qunzhong-qianren-yu-xianren-de-gongdou-daxi-lkfcjewfhmhfgw39vqfqkpa9umzq|Hanji 430]] as a deceased Ye Tingling remembered by [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] for recognizing, praising, and helping him before accession.

## Current Profile

Zhang He matters in the source because his kindness is repaid posthumously. Xuan says Zhang He had shown him educational and personal favor, then rewards Zhang He's adopted line through [[ZhangPengzuWesternHan|张彭祖]] and grants posthumous honor. The episode uses him as the clearest case of Xuan's gratitude crossing death and family boundaries.

## Key Characteristics

- Deceased Western Han official remembered as a pre-accession benefactor of Xuan.
- Associated with education, recognition, and praise of the future emperor.
- Receives posthumous honor through his adopted heir.
- Central example for Xuan's repayment of old kindness.
