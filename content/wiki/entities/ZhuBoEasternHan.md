## Overview

朱勃 / Zhu Bo enters the wiki as the former Yunyang magistrate who defends [[MaYuanEasternHan|马援]] after Ma dies in camp and is accused by [[LiangSongLinxiangMarquis|梁松]]. His importance is not high office but posthumous correction: he asks [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] to let senior ministers weigh Ma Yuan's lifetime service, battlefield conditions, epidemic death, and family humiliation before leaving the accusation to define Ma's name.

## Current Profile

Hanji 726 gives Zhu Bo a compact relationship history with Ma Yuan. As a child, Zhu is presented as exceptionally quick with the classics; Ma Yuan once feels inferior after meeting him, while Ma Yuan's elder brother Ma Kuang predicts Zhu will later still take Ma Yuan as teacher. That prediction is fulfilled socially when Ma Yuan rises to general and marquis while Zhu remains at county level.

The episode then makes Zhu Bo's character visible after Ma Yuan's fall. Ma Yuan had remembered old ties and looked after Zhu, but also showed some condescension. Zhu does not hold the slight against him. When Ma Yuan is dishonored after death, Zhu submits the memorial that shifts Liu Xiu away from pure anger toward a merit-and-fault review.

## Key Characteristics

- Former Yunyang magistrate whose current profile is bounded to Ma Yuan's posthumous case.
- Learned early talent remembered through childhood mastery of the classics.
- Old associate of Ma Yuan whose relationship includes both favor and condescension.
- Defender who does not let personal slight override public judgment of Ma Yuan's service.
- Memorial writer who frames reputation repair through complete evidence rather than emotional loyalty alone.

## Qualifications

This page does not yet reconstruct Zhu Bo's broader offices, family, or later career. The current evidence is narrow and episodic. It supports Zhu Bo as a posthumous defender of Ma Yuan and a case of public-minded memorial writing after slander, not as a complete Eastern Han biography.
