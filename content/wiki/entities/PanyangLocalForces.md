# 番阳民帅 / Panyang Local Forces

番阳民帅 / Panyang local forces enter the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1029-guoshi-wushuang-taishici-yinuo-qianjin-yi-dangxian-lvckwlhz5g-oappy89thye9oyrto|Hanji 1029]] as a local group reported by [[TaishiCi|太史慈]] after his mission to inspect [[YuzhangCommandery|豫章郡]]. The source says local forces around Panyang establish their own office or organization near [[HaihunLateHan|海昏]] and no longer obey the Yuzhang commandery center.

The page is source-scoped. The transcript renders the place as "攀扬," which this ingest normalizes cautiously to 番阳 / Panyang in the late-Han Yuzhang context. The group's role is not a full political history; it is evidence that [[HuaXin|华歆]] faces local autonomy he cannot suppress.
