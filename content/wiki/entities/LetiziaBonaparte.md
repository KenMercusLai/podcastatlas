# Letizia Bonaparte

## Overview

Letizia Bonaparte, born Letizia Ramolino, was Napoleon's mother and a central formative influence within the large, aspirational Bonaparte household in Ajaccio.

## Current Profile

The episode introduces Letizia through Napoleon's retrospective insistence that she strongly shaped him. She married [[CarloBonaparte|Carlo Bonaparte]] within the Paolist and conquest-era world of [[Corsica]], raised a large family with limited wealth and strong ambitions, and remained part of the domestic formation behind [[NapoleonBonaparte|Napoleon]]'s toughness, family attachment, and drive. The source does not isolate her influence from the violence, instability, and social aspiration of the wider environment.

## Key Characteristics

- Corsican mother situated within the Paolist and French-conquest generation.
- Managed life in a large, ambitious household that was noble but not especially wealthy.
- Remembered by Napoleon as a major formative influence.
- Represents the family and domestic layer behind his later political and military formation.
