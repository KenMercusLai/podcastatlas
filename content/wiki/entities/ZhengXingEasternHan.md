## Overview

郑兴 / Zheng Xing enters the wiki as the Eastern Han official used in Hanji 731 to contrast with [[HuanTanEasternHan|桓谭]] on how to speak when [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] wants chenwei support. His role is not developed as a full biography; it is a focused case of face-saving ambiguity under imperial suspicion.

## Current Profile

The episode says that in 31 CE Liu Xiu asked Zheng Xing about sacrifice and wanted to judge the matter through chenwei language. Zheng Xing answered that he did not understand such materials. Liu Xiu became angry because "not understanding" could be heard as rejection, especially where chenwei touched the emperor's own legitimacy story.

Zheng Xing then repairs the situation by clarifying that he does not understand chenwei but does not oppose it. The answer gives Liu Xiu room to withdraw anger without requiring Zheng Xing to endorse the prophetic framework substantively. In the episode's comparison, this makes Zheng Xing the survival-through-ambiguity case between Huan Tan's direct refusal and Jia Kui's accommodating interpretation.

## Key Characteristics

- Used as a source-scoped example of cautious speech under Guangwu.
- Avoids direct contradiction by distinguishing ignorance from opposition.
- Preserves imperial face without making a strong chenwei endorsement.
- Contrasts with Huan Tan's direct anti-chenwei answer and Jia Kui's adaptive interpretation.

## Qualifications

This page is limited to Hanji 731's comparison. It does not establish Zheng Xing's full career, broader scholarship, or private view of chenwei. The source shows a speech tactic under risk, not proof that Zheng Xing substantively accepted the chenwei system.
