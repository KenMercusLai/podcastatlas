## Overview

郑兴 / Zheng Xing is synthesized as a Guangwu-era adviser whose current evidence now spans exit from [[WeiXiaoEasternHan|隗嚣]]'s suspicious camp, policy remonstrance under [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]], and survival under chenwei speech risk. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-698-wangchao-xingshuai-qishilu-weiao-de-yihan-yu-jinchao-zhi-jing-lqim2hbjtdzb0wwmsaxcyzj7u8ff|Hanji 698]] shows him leaving Wei Xiao through a filial-burial request despite attempted retention. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-705-1-diwang-moulue-liuxiu-ruhe-yi-sheling-nizhuan-caiyuan-fengyun-lq2m06p7eczj9ebysxpl84bkzzi|Hanji 705-1]] then shows him using a solar eclipse and sealed-memorial opening to push Liu Xiu toward public reassurance, better appointment timing, and less rushed strictness. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-731-jinhu-wanmei-de-liuxiu-zui-da-quedian-shi-ta-lrhwupjmjm-hch3usx26jfxkhoma|Hanji 731]] preserves the same chenwei exchange as a comparison in safe speech under imperial preference.

## Current Profile

Hanji 698 gives Zheng Xing an earlier alignment-risk layer. He asks to leave Wei Xiao's side to bury his parents, while Wei Xiao tries to hold him through material support and a military-adviser role. Zheng Xing does not accept retention; he offers his wife and children as reassurance, and Wei Xiao eventually lets the whole family leave.

That exit matters because it is not staged as an open betrayal. Zheng Xing uses filial obligation and family-risk reassurance to make departure survivable, but the episode still places him among the people who stop trusting Wei Xiao's undecided future.

In Hanji 705-1, Liu Xiu responds to a solar eclipse by asking officials for sealed criticism. Zheng Xing's answer treats omen anxiety as a governance problem: the ruler should attend to popular feeling, reduce unnecessary political pressure, and make high appointments in a way that calms rather than fuels public rumor.

The Da Sikong question makes the advice concrete. Zheng Xing says public opinion favors [[GuoJiEasternHan|Guo Ji]], and that Liu Xiu's delay has itself become damaging because people have begun speculating about why a widely trusted official is not being used. The source then presents Liu Xiu's amnesty and the later appointment of [[LiTongEasternHan|Li Tong]] as practical corrections after the memorial, even though Guo Ji himself is not appointed to the post.

Zheng Xing's second current role is chenwei ambiguity. When Liu Xiu later consults him on prophetic materials, Zheng Xing first says he does not understand them and triggers imperial anger; he then clarifies that ignorance does not mean opposition. Hanji 731 uses this answer as the middle path between [[HuanTanEasternHan|Huan Tan]]'s direct anti-chenwei refusal and [[JiaKuiEasternHan|Jia Kui]]'s accommodating interpretation.

## Key Characteristics

- Wei Xiao-side adviser who leaves through filial framing instead of direct factional rupture.
- Uses family-risk reassurance to reduce a patron's suspicion during departure.
- Guangwu-era remonstrant who can turn disaster consultation into policy and personnel advice.
- Uses public reputation as evidence, especially in the Guo Ji appointment discussion.
- Counsels Liu Xiu against excessive strictness and political haste without rejecting imperial authority.
- Demonstrates face-saving ambiguity when speaking around Liu Xiu's chenwei preference.
- Source-bounded official whose current importance is advisory technique rather than a full career biography.

## Qualifications

This page remains source-bounded to Hanji 698, Hanji 705-1, and Hanji 731. Hanji 698 supports Zheng Xing's departure from Wei Xiao's camp but does not reconstruct his full prior service, later route to Liu Xiu's administration, or private motive beyond filial framing and family reassurance. The evidence does not reconstruct Zheng Xing's full office chronology, scholarship, factional ties, or private view of chenwei. Hanji 705-1 supports the claim that his remonstrance helped reframe Liu Xiu's policy mood, but it does not prove that every subsequent amnesty or appointment was caused only by Zheng Xing. The transcript form 郑新 is normalized to 郑兴 because the Guangwu chenwei, eclipse-remonstrance, and Wei Xiao-camp contexts match the established route.
