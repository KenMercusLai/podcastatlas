# 陈温 / Chen Wen (late Han)

陈温 / Chen Wen (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-969-yuanshi-xiongdi-neidou-shui-neng-jianlou-lsgj6sewo35jpsgfm-koivijtr6r|Hanji 969]] as the Yangzhou inspector whose death opens a contested provincial office. The episode treats his death as the trigger after which [[YuanShao|袁绍]] sends [[YuanYiLateHan|袁遗]] to lead [[YangzhouLateHan|扬州]], while [[YuanShu|袁术]] moves to defeat the Yuan Shao-side appointment.

Chen Wen's source role is therefore an office-vacancy role. The page does not try to settle every variant tradition about whether he died naturally or was killed; it records how the episode uses his death to show a late-Han appointment field where formal succession, family rivalry, and actual armed control diverge.
