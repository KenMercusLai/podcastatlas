# Antonio Mays Jr.

Antonio Mays Jr. is the 16-year-old whose death is the central unresolved case in [[20260721-pmoney-new-npr-series-we-keep-us-safe-from-the-embedded-podcast-trailer-20260721-pmoney-new-npr-series-we-keep-us-safe-from-the-embedded-podcast-trailer]]. The trailer says Antonio was a passenger in a Jeep that entered [[CapitolHillOrganizedProtest|CHOP]] and that protesters fired on the Jeep because they believed they were under attack.

The source says protesters saw Antonio as a threat that night, while Antonio's father challenges that narrative by saying his son was there for the cause. That tension makes Antonio's death the human center of [[WeKeepUsSafe]] and the clearest case for [[SafetyIdealFatalContradiction]] in the trailer.
