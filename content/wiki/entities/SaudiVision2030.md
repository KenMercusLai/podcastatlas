# Saudi Vision 2030 / 沙特2030愿景

## Overview

Saudi Vision 2030 is the reform program through which [[MohammedBinSalman|MBS]] seeks to change [[SaudiArabia]]’s oil economy, gender order, and religion-state settlement without democratizing the monarchy.

## Current Profile

[[304-shate-alabo-guoqu-shinian-zai-jinxing-yichang-zenyang-de-zhengzhi-gaige-lsm0nzvie2-h_sgbmhavg7xbv74t|episode 304]] treats Vision 2030 as a whole-state project rather than an investment plan. Economic diversification, sovereign capital, tourism, entertainment, logistics, women’s employment, reduced religious-police power, and [[NEOM]] are parts of one [[AuthoritarianModernization]] strategy.

The source records partial gains but judges the original ambition underfulfilled. Non-oil activity and women’s public participation grew, while oil-price exposure, debt, foreign-investment shortfalls, and megaproject retrenchment remained. That mixed record matters politically because performance is the project’s substitute for wider participation.

## Key Characteristics

- Seeks to reduce dependence on the [[RentierState|oil rentier-state model]].
- Uses sovereign investment and new sectors to create a broader productive economy.
- Expands women’s mobility and employment for economic as well as social reasons.
- Reduces autonomous [[Wahhabism|Wahhabi control]] over public life.
- Includes high-visibility megaprojects such as [[NEOM]].
- Preserves centralized monarchical power rather than opening democratic competition.

## Evidence

- Three-part design: [[304-shate-alabo-guoqu-shinian-zai-jinxing-yichang-zenyang-de-zhengzhi-gaige-lsm0nzvie2-h_sgbmhavg7xbv74t]] organizes the project around oil, gender, and religious authority.
- Instruments: [[304-shate-alabo-guoqu-shinian-zai-jinxing-yichang-zenyang-de-zhengzhi-gaige-lsm0nzvie2-h_sgbmhavg7xbv74t]] names sovereign investment, technology, tourism, logistics, entertainment, private-sector growth, and megaprojects.
- Mixed outcome: [[304-shate-alabo-guoqu-shinian-zai-jinxing-yichang-zenyang-de-zhengzhi-gaige-lsm0nzvie2-h_sgbmhavg7xbv74t]] pairs women’s driving, labor participation, entertainment, and non-oil growth with continued oil exposure, debt, weak FDI, and project reductions.

## Qualifications

The page reflects one critical episode and does not independently audit every official Vision 2030 target. “Failure” in the source means a large gap between original ambition and observed delivery, not the absence of all reform or non-oil growth.

## What Changed

- Added Vision 2030 as a whole-state political project rather than only an economic policy.
- Made partial social gains and economic underperformance explicit in the same synthesis.
- Connected program delivery to authoritarian legitimacy and Gulf rivalry.

## Relationships

- [[MohammedBinSalman]] - principal political sponsor and executor.
- [[SaudiArabia]] - state whose economy and social order the program seeks to remake.
- [[NEOM]] - flagship megaproject and delivery-risk case.
- [[Wahhabism]] - historic religious authority constrained by the program.
- [[AuthoritarianModernization]] - governing model that captures reform without political opening.
- [[DevelopmentalAuthoritarianLegitimacy]] - performance-based legitimacy exposed when targets are missed.
