# 杨雍 / Yang Yong (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-930-ruhe-pochu-tanxin-he-buganxin-lkyysh5jbgxwjq8ayrfar4gsjdcd|Hanji 930]] names Yang Yong as the Liangzhou inspector who recommends [[GaiXunLateHan|盖勋]] after Gai Xun survives capture by the Qiang. His memorial leads to Gai Xun concurrently holding [[HanyangCommanderyLateHan|汉阳郡]].

Yang Yong's role is brief but useful: it shows that Gai Xun's battlefield reputation and clean-official image can still be converted into appointment after defeat. The page remains source-scoped until later late-Han sources add a fuller Yang Yong biography.
