# Paula Mejia

## Overview
Paula Mejia is the writer interviewed by [[MarketplaceTech]] about luxury digital-detox services and her own attempt to reduce phone use without disconnecting completely.

## Current Profile
Mejia functions in the source as both participant and critic. She experienced [[GoldenDoor]]'s phone-light environment, then tried a more accessible experiment suited to freelance work: changing her morning routine, reading, spending time with her dog, and accepting social invitations. The temporary reduction and quick rebound led her to question why users must buy products or exercise exceptional discipline to counter attention-seeking technology.

## Key Characteristics
- Writer examining the commercialization and social meaning of digital disconnection.
- Participant in both a high-priced retreat and a self-directed phone-use experiment.
- Pragmatic about the difficulty of total disconnection for a freelancer who monitors email.
- Focused on replacing automatic phone checking with reading, companionship, and social contact.
- Critical of placing the full responsibility for technology overuse on individual consumers.
- Advocate for cultural and workplace norms that protect work-life balance.

## Evidence
- Retreat perspective: [[tech-20260922-0922-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260922-0922-mp-tech-pod-128]] uses Mejia's visit to [[GoldenDoor]] to examine device-light luxury and younger demand for disconnection.
- Behavioral experiment: [[tech-20260922-0922-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260922-0922-mp-tech-pod-128]] describes her morning substitutions, greater acceptance of social invitations, reduced screen time, and rapid rebound.
- Structural critique: [[tech-20260922-0922-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260922-0922-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports her argument that culture and workplace expectations should reduce constant phone use instead of leaving the problem entirely to consumers.

## Qualifications
The source supplies a short interview and one personal experiment, not a complete biography or controlled evaluation of digital-detox methods. The durability and generalizability of Mejia's approach remain untested.
