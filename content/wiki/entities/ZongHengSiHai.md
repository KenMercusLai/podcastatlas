# 纵横四海 / Zong Heng Si Hai

## Overview
纵横四海 / Zong Heng Si Hai is represented in this wiki by an extended Mini MBA episode on marketing management. The show uses business-school frameworks, consumer-brand examples, and personal-development applications to make marketing useful beyond formal marketing departments.

## Current Profile
The episode positions the show as a long-form explanatory format that connects management theory to everyday judgment. Its marketing-management lesson is not a list of tactics; it is a disciplined route from identity and capability to customer understanding, positioning, product value, pricing, channel choice, and ethical responsibility.

## Key Characteristics
- The show uses structured business frameworks while keeping the discussion tied to concrete consumer and company cases.
- It treats business tools as transferable to individual careers, creator positioning, and personal brand decisions.
- Its Mini MBA frame emphasizes foundational concepts before execution details.
- The episode gives ethics a central role rather than treating consumer persuasion as neutral technique.
- It uses brand, core competence, STP, and 4P as connected decisions instead of separate vocabulary items.
