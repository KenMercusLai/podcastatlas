# 赵德芳 / Zhao Defang

Zhao Defang / 赵德芳 is [[ZhaoKuangyin|赵匡胤 / 宋太祖]]'s son in [[170-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-xia-958158115]]. He matters most in the immediate palace sequence after Taizu's death: [[SongEmpress|宋皇后]] reportedly sends [[WangJien|王继恩]] to summon Defang, but Wang goes instead to [[ZhaoGuangyi|赵光义 / 宋太宗]].

The episode uses that substitution as one of the suspicious details in the succession story. Defang is not shown as an active plotter, but his expected arrival would have supported a son-based succession path rather than the brother-based path that Taizong ultimately took.

Defang's later sudden death at age twenty-three is paired with [[ZhaoDezhao|赵德昭]]'s death as part of the source's broader [[SongSuccessionLegitimacyCrisis|宋初继承合法性危机]] reading.
