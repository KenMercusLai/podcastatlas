# 朱家 / Zhu Jia (Lu)

朱家 / Zhu Jia enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-170-liubang-weisha-cong-luoyang-banjia-dao-changan-1-lpioq8izitba7uhbdybp7i03djdy|Hanji 170 part 1]] as the Lu-region figure who shelters [[JiBu|季布]] after [[LiuBang|刘邦]] orders Ji Bu's arrest. The episode says Zhu Jia recognizes Ji Bu even after Ji Bu shaves his head, wears chains, and is sold as a slave.

Zhu Jia's main role is persuasion. He approaches [[XiahouYing|夏侯婴]] in [[Luoyi|洛阳 / 洛邑]] and argues that former [[XiangYu|项羽]] officers were fulfilling duty when they fought for their lord. If Liu Bang kills a talented defeated officer over personal resentment, he will look narrow-minded and may drive such people toward [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] or [[Nanyue|南越]].

After Ji Bu is pardoned and appointed, Zhu Jia no longer meets him. The episode uses that withdrawal to keep Zhu Jia's action from looking like client-building; he acts as a rescuer and advocate for [[FormerEnemyLoyaltyReframing|旧敌忠义重释]], not as a broker seeking repayment.
