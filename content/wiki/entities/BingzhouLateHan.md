# 并州 / Bingzhou (Late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-939-donghan-luanju-de-fengkuang-sanguo-xuqu-lthmvw8ihk96fuez9vamlryzcngy|Hanji 939]] adds 并州 / Bingzhou (Late Han) as the northern provincial field where inspector [[ZhangYiLateHan|张懿]] is killed by the [[TugeHu|屠各胡]]. The episode places that killing beside renewed [[BaiboArmy|白波军]] attacks and the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] revolt, making Bingzhou part of the same 188 CE frontier breakdown.

The page is scoped to the late-Han provincial crisis. Its value is not a full geography of Bingzhou, but a place marker for how provincial office, frontier groups, and insurgent forces intersect before [[ProvincialShepherdAutonomy|州牧集权与地方割据]] becomes a central institutional answer.
