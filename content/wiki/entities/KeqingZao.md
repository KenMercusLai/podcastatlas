# 客卿灶 / Keqing Zao

客卿灶 / Keqing Zao appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-88-ni-zenme-kandai-fenghuangnan-1-lpuxkdq-rvnifuoneq212g53w5z5]] as the Qin guest minister recommended by [[WeiRan|魏冉]] to attack [[QiState|齐国]]. The source transcript renders the name as 客卿造; this page normalizes the figure as 客卿灶 while keeping the variant visible.

His role is brief but structurally important. The episode treats his campaign against Gang and Shou as a tool for Wei Ran's [[PrivateFiefExpansionThroughStateWar|private fief expansion through state war]], because the gains lie near [[Dingtao|陶 / 定陶]] and do not obviously solve Qin's core route or frontier problems.
