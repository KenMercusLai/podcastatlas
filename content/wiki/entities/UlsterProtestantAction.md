# Ulster Protestant Action

Ulster Protestant Action appears in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as [[IanPaisley]]'s political wing during the 1960s. The episode says it recruited more people amid economic anxiety and sectarian tension.

The organization links Paisley's preaching and publicity tactics to organized loyalist politics. In the source's sequence, it sits beside [[FreePresbyterianChurchOfUlster]] and before [[NoelDoherty]]'s [[UlsterProtestantVolunteers]], showing how anti-Catholic rhetoric could become a recruiting structure.
