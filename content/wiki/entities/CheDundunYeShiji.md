# 《车墩墩野事记》

《车墩墩野事记》 is the essay collection by [[YinQiCheDundun|尹琪]] discussed in [[85-chedundun-yeshiji-zou-a-bandao-xiangxia-zuan-xiaoshuliner-qu-700935571]]. The title comes from [[ChedunTown|车墩镇]], while "野事" refers both to wild animals and to the less polished human activity found around rural and suburban edges.

The source says the book has qualities of essay, nature literature, and popular science, but 尹琪 prefers to frame it as a book about a different way of living. Its hundred short pieces make bats, swallows, pheasants, moths, fireflies, raccoon dogs, markets, small shops, abandoned objects, local oddities, and a partner nicknamed "小狮子" part of one lived ecology.

## Key Claims
- The book's strength is not systematic species instruction but attention to how ordinary places become readable through repeated watching.
- It extends [[NatureWriting]] by keeping human traces, humor, awkwardness, and community life inside the natural scene.
- It supports [[UrbanEdgeNature]] because its setting is neither deep wilderness nor sealed city, but a mixed habitat of transport, shops, woods, fields, houses, animals, and waste.
- It turns [[ObservationBeforeInference]] into an everyday literary ethic: leave space for what the scene might mean before using it as a ready-made moral.
- Its movement from animals back to people makes the book part of the wiki's [[NatureContactSelfPerception]] branch.
