# 蜀侯宁通 / Shu Hou Ning Tong

蜀侯宁通 / Shu Hou Ning Tong appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2]] as the reduced Shu ruler after [[QinState|秦国]] conquers [[ShuStateWarringStates|蜀国]]. The episode says Qin leaves Shu in a lowered marquisate form while appointing [[ChenZhuangWarringStates|陈庄]] as chancellor.

His death is the trigger event. Chen Zhuang kills him, which marks the move from delegated Qin governance to open rebellion in [[BaShu|巴蜀]].
