# 《尚书》 / Shangshu

《尚书》 / Shangshu enters this wiki branch through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-124-fenshu-kengru-wo-zai-bzhan-wei-qinshihuang-pingfan-ljnhwcouqu35rmvb-cnrzyzj2j6l]], where it is named with [[Shijing|《诗经》]] as one of the classics targeted by the [[QinBookBurningPolicy|秦代焚书令]]. The source does not summarize Shangshu as a text; it records its political role in Qin's ban on private classical study and private criticism of current institutions through ancient models.

The page should therefore treat Shangshu here as a controlled classic rather than as an independent source under analysis. Its significance is that discussion, private possession, and use as an "以古非今" resource become part of Qin's knowledge-control problem.
