# 荀攸 / Xun You

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1005-sunce-zhuding-zaoshi-shi-xingge-jueding-mingyun-lpqnere2cwcrngggyezd-bsqahx4|Hanji 1005]] backfills Xun You as one of the advisers [[XunYu|荀彧]] recommends to [[CaoCao|曹操]] after Cao Cao's court settles at [[Xudu|许都]]. The transcript appears to render his name as "徐悠"; the wiki normalizes cautiously to Xun You because the surrounding sequence matches the Xun Yu recommendation branch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1006-guojia-buman-yuanshao-weihe-que-neng-guishun-caocao-lup-p-inz1sdlhpnaggqjp0eavar|Hanji 1006]] then develops Xun You as the low-profile strategist paired with [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] in the episode's comparison of Cao Cao advisers. The source presents him as an Yingchuan elite whose reputation is quieter than Guo Jia's and partly overshadowed by Xun Yu, but whose value lies in cautious, exacting, secret-keeping counsel.

The host uses the phrase "外愚内智" to explain the style. Xun You is not portrayed as less intelligent because he appears plain or restrained; rather, he survives by keeping his brilliance controlled, avoiding theatrical exposure, and knowing when to withdraw from the visible center of power.

In the episode's contrast, Guo Jia supplies bold, disruptive strategic judgment, while Xun You supplies discipline, concealment, and self-preservation. The page therefore connects Xun You to [[PoliticalSurvivalFirst|安全第一政治生存]] as much as to battlefield planning: in this source, successful advisers are judged not only by good ideas but also by their ability to remain alive and trusted in a dangerous court.
