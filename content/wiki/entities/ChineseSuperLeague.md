## Overview
中国足球超级联赛 / Chinese Super League is the Chinese top football league used by the wiki as a sports-rights bubble example, the competitive arena shaped by [[Evergrande]]'s football project, and now a live policy-design case for expansion, financial discipline, calendar reform, and foreign-player rules.

## Current Profile
The league's current profile has three branches. In the media-rights branch, it shows that a local rights market can be overvalued when platforms pay more than audience, advertising, membership, and sublicensing economics can support. In the football-legacy branch, it is where [[GuangzhouFC]] rose rapidly after 2010, where the [[ChineseFootballGoldenYuanEra]] later became a multi-club arms race, and where successor identity now matters because Guangzhou football may return before Guangzhou FC's original institutional lineage does. In the policy branch, the league is treated as ready for possible expansion only if [[ChineseLeagueOne]], [[ChineseFootballFinancialDiscipline]], [[ChineseFootballCalendarPolicy]], and [[ChineseFootballForeignPlayerPolicy]] move together.

## Key Characteristics
- Local sports-rights case showing that bubble conditions depend on monetization capacity, not only absolute rights prices.
- Competitive stage for Evergrande-era [[GuangzhouFC]]'s rapid rise, eight domestic titles, and two Asian titles.
- Spending-arms-race arena where the source dates the true golden-yuan era to the broader 2016 multi-club escalation.
- Salary-expectation reset environment for domestic players after other clubs began competing for Guangzhou's player base.
- Current identity target because [[GuangzhouLeopard]]'s promotion could mean Guangzhou football returns to the top league without resolving [[GuangzhouFC]] supporter continuity.
- Expansion candidate whose move from 16 to 18 clubs is argued to require advance rules, second-tier linkage, and playoff-style transition design.
- Policy testbed where calendar quality, foreign-player roster depth, and club financial discipline interact with market recovery.

## Qualifications
The rights-market source is about media economics, not on-field quality. The Evergrande source is an interpretive football conversation, not a complete league history or audited salary dataset. The expansion source is a policy discussion, not an adopted regulation. League-wide golden-yuan timing, supporter adoption forecasts, expansion timing, and roster-rule changes remain source-scoped.
