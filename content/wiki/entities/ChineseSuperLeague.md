# 中国足球超级联赛 / Chinese Super League

The Chinese Super League appears in [[e237-yangshi-he-fifa-tanpan-fenzheng-beihou-tiyu-saishi-zhuanboquan-de-boyi-yu-shengyi-c3607c08-6eb6-48bd-8250-b41b5b8272e6]] as a local example for defining [[SportsRightsBubble]]. The episode says a bubble does not require a price to exceed the [[PremierLeague]] in absolute terms; it is enough that the price exceeds what the local audience, advertisers, membership model, and platform ecosystem can monetize.

The source uses the league to distinguish China-market rights inflation from durable global rights demand. A local league can become expensive during a platform race while still lacking the product quality, paying habit, and bargaining power needed to make those rights sustainable.
