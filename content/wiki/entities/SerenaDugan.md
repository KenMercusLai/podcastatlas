# Serena Dugan

Serena Dugan is the designer and creative co-founder of [[SerenaAndLily|Serena & Lily]] in [[serena-lily-serena-dugan-and-lily-kanter-they-built-a-20m-brand-then-one-investor-almost-destroyed-it-31a4ddcd-1cc1-48d0-9369-fd85e8714e66]]. The source traces her path from psychology plans into painted furniture, art school, decorative painting for interiors, and textile work that became the aesthetic basis for the company's first nursery products.

Serena's contribution was not only pattern design. She brought a room-level vision: nurseries and kids' rooms could feel considered, elevated, and respectful rather than limited to generic baby motifs. That visual system helped [[LilyKanter]] sell retailers and customers on a broader home environment before the company had a mature manufacturing operation.

After the majority-shareholder restructuring, Serena initially stayed in her creative role, then shifted to an external part-time role and later fully stepped away from the company. The source preserves her pride in the brand while noting that she recognizes the current aesthetic has changed.

## Key Claims
- Serena began painting furniture after college and built a design practice through decorative painting and interior-designer relationships.
- Her hand-painted and block-print work supplied the early Serena & Lily design language.
- She saw nursery design as a real interior-design problem, not a category that had to default to pastel baby themes.
- Her creative work helped the catalog imagery generate demand for adjacent products such as kids' bedding, furniture, paint, rugs, and lamps.
- She later left the company and no longer owns shares, but describes the overriding feeling as pride.
