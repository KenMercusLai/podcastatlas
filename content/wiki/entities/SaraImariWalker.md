# Sara Imari Walker

## Overview
Sara Imari Walker is presented as the astrobiology and origins-of-life voice who uses assembly theory to distinguish appearance or output from the causal histories that produce them.

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Walker questions the label of AI as an alien mind because language models emerge from human language, infrastructure, culture, and the terrestrial history of life. She argues that repeated complex objects embody accumulated lineage, so detecting truly alien life requires instruments and concepts sensitive to independently assembled causal histories rather than familiar shapes alone.

## Key Characteristics
- Challenges inherited categories such as alien, organism, intelligence, and consciousness.
- Uses construction steps and copy number to investigate embodied causal history.
- Distinguishes resemblance from shared process or ontology.
- Places terrestrial AI inside Earth's biological, cultural, and industrial lineage.
- Reframes the great filter as potentially perceptual rather than only evolutionary.
