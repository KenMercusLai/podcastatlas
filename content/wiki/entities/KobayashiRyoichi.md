# 小林亮一 / Kobayashi Ryoichi

小林亮一 appears in [[88-bie-renshu-huizi-zai-ting-budao-hecai-de-shijie-li-huiquan-708510803]] as the coach at [[ZhendouBoxingGym|真斗拳馆]] who recognizes [[KeikoBoxer|惠子]]'s professional potential. After she tells him that other gyms and competitions have rejected her because of deafness, he insists that she can become a professional boxer.

His role becomes sharper after 惠子's second fight. When she admits that she hates being hit, he teaches defensive technique rather than reducing the problem to willpower. The source therefore treats him as part of a support system that combines belief, instruction, and risk discipline.
