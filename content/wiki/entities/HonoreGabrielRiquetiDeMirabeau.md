# Honore-Gabriel Riqueti, comte de Mirabeau

## Overview

Honoré-Gabriel Riqueti, comte de Mirabeau was a nobleman elected for the Third Estate who became a dramatic defender of the [[NationalAssembly1789|National Assembly]] during the opening constitutional confrontation of the [[FrenchRevolution|French Revolution]].

## Current Profile

The source emphasizes the political force of Mirabeau's voice, appearance, reputation, and timing. He opposed separate credential verification because it would concede separate chambers, then answered the attempt to clear the hall after the royal session of 23 June with a declaration that the deputies would leave only under armed force. The episode treats the line as theatre with constitutional effect: [[LouisXVI|Louis XVI]] declined to enforce his order, leaving the Assembly in place.

## Key Characteristics

- Noble by birth but elected for the Third Estate at Aix-en-Provence.
- Forceful orator with a scandalous biography and popular political presence.
- Opponent of separate verification by the three estates.
- Defender of the Assembly against royal dispersal.
- Example of political theatre becoming practical authority.
