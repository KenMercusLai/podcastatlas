# Abhijit Tipke

## Overview
Abhijit Tipke is presented by the sources as a founder and organizer whose online response helped convert a “cockroach” insult against unemployed Indian youth into the shared identity of the [[CockroachMovement]].

## Current Profile
The first source emphasizes symbolic entrepreneurship: Tipke's question about what would happen if the “cockroaches” came together helped a joke carry grievances about exam leaks, unemployment, and education scarcity. The later source adds an operational role. It describes him and two co-leaders being removed from a plane at [[Delhi]] airport and held separately without phones before an election-administration protest, leaving the central demonstration without its intended leadership.

The combined evidence supports a profile that moves from meme originator to offline organizer. It does not establish his formal title, total authority over the movement, detention's legality, or his longer-term political plans.

## Key Characteristics
- Helped turn a stigmatizing label into a shared protest identity.
- Connected online satire to material grievances rather than treating humor as the movement's endpoint.
- Appears in the later source as one of several organizers coordinating offline protest.
- Became a target of pre-emptive detention before a planned Delhi demonstration.

## Evidence
- Meme formation: [[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]] attributes the catalytic online question to Tipke and embeds it in education and employment anger.
- Organizer role and detention: [[kicking-kyiv-trumps-doubtful-deals-64ee795d3a366335cf4a682ed4b0f0d5]] reports that police detained Tipke and two co-leaders on arrival and denied them phone access for roughly 24 hours.

## Qualifications
The sources do not independently verify Tipke's exact organizational office, the authorities' legal basis, the undisclosed detention locations, or a direct causal line from his absence to every feature of the scattered protest. Treating him as important does not imply that a decentralized youth movement depends on one person.

## What Changed
- Added Tipke's later role as an offline protest organizer.
- Added his reported pre-emptive detention before an election-administration demonstration.
- Distinguished symbolic influence from complete operational control over the movement.
