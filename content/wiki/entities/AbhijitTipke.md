# Abhijit Tipke

Abhijit Tipke appears in [[cockroaches-1-modi-0-indias-remarkable-protests-6a6729a33e2f0b6f92d26ab8]] as the person whose tweet helped turn a "cockroach" insult into a collective protest meme. The source says his question about what would happen if all cockroaches came together helped the [[CockroachMovement]] move from online satire toward a shared identity.

His role is a compact example of meme-to-movement conversion. The episode does not present the tweet as sufficient by itself; it mattered because the insult already resonated with anger over [[NationalEligibilityCumEntranceTest|NEET]], unemployment, exam leaks, and education scarcity.
