# 钟离春 / 钟无艳 / Zhongli Chun

钟离春 / Zhongli Chun, also known in later legend as 钟无艳, appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-49-mengzi-yue-wo-jiao-ni-zhiguo-ni-que-ba-wo-dang-huaping-lhy14pbf9thcfdiaulcmf8dwcef8]] in the episode's closing side note on [[QiXuanWang|齐宣王]]. The host says the tradition has Qi Xuan Wang marrying the famously unattractive but capable Zhongli Chun, and links her to the saying "有事钟无艳，无事夏迎春."

The page is source-scoped. The episode uses Zhongli Chun less as a historical proof point than as part of Qi Xuan Wang's cultural afterlife, contrasting the legend with the more central political evaluation: Qi Xuan Wang is not treated as a model ruler, but his reign still coincides with Qi's development and [[JixiaAcademy|稷下学宫]] flourishing.
