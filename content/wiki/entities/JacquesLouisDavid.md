# Jacques-Louis David / 雅克-路易·大卫

## Overview

Jacques-Louis David was the revolutionary painter and political organizer who shaped [[JeanPaulMarat|Jean-Paul Marat]]'s posthumous image through [[DeathOfMaratPainting|The Death of Marat]] and an elaborate public funeral.

## Current Profile

David's role extended beyond painting a retrospective icon. The sources show him selecting the visual signs of purity, charity, sacrifice, and absence in the painting while also organizing a Roman-style funeral that helped transform Marat's decomposing body, heart, hair, and memory into objects of revolutionary devotion.

## Key Characteristics

- He made Marat clean, calm, charitable, and saint-like while excluding Corday from the painting.
- He organized Marat's public funeral as political spectacle modeled on Rome.
- His memorial work borrowed Christian forms even within an anti-clerical revolutionary movement.
- His art determined which body received pity and which political motive disappeared from view.
- His earlier portrait connection to Antoine Lavoisier places him between revolutionary sainthood and threatened scientific elite culture.

### Political range

- [[125-mala-zhisi-beibi-de-ansha-huo-zhengyi-de-chujue-783972516]] connects David to Lavoisier as well as Marat, showing how one artist linked revolutionary memory and scientific culture.

## Qualifications

- The sources analyze David primarily through Marat and do not offer a comprehensive account of his career.
- Calling the painting propaganda does not exhaust its artistic power or settle how later viewers interpret it.

## What Changed

- David's current profile now includes funeral organization and relic-making alongside the painting.
- Christian and Roman memorial forms are integrated into the judgment of his political image-making.

## Relationships

- [[JeanPaulMarat]] - political subject whom he transformed into a martyr.
- [[CharlotteCorday]] - absent assassin whose exclusion narrowed the painting's moral field.
- [[RevolutionaryMartyrdomImageMaking]] - process joining his painting, funeral, and cult design.
- [[HistoricalMemoryContest]] - later struggle over the meaning of the assassination.
- [[AntoineLavoisier]] - scientific-elite subject connecting another side of David's revolutionary-era work.
