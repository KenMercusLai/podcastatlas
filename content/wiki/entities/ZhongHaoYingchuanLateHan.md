## Overview

钟皓 / Zhong Hao is a late-Han Yingchuan county-magistrate figure named in the current wiki through the [[YingchuanFourMagistrates|颍川四长]] cohort and now given a separate prudence-counsel branch in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-832-wei-lingdao-beiguo-ta-shi-sha-haishi-zhi-lmfpuixmi4odrf6lu31rf6-66-lq|Hanji 832]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-842-shenme-shi-haoguan-ta-wei-baixing-gan-pinming-lj2okcbcvcmrfqrj3d-k1ylqdi2si|Hanji 842]] groups him with [[HanShaoLateHan|韩韶]], [[XunShuLateHan|荀淑]], and [[ChenShiLateHan|陈寔]] as magistrates remembered for virtuous local governance and benefit to the people.

## Current Profile

Zhong Hao's current page is no longer only a cohort placeholder. Hanji 842 does not provide an individual anecdote comparable to Han Shao's granary decision, but it names Zhong Hao as one member of a four-person Yingchuan magistrate reputation field, where local office, 德政, and public benefit become the basis for praise.

Hanji 832 adds an individual reputation and counsel layer. It says Zhong Hao and Xun Shu enjoyed comparable reputation, then places Zhong Hao as the elder who explains [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]]'s criticism of [[ZhongJinLateHan|钟瑾]]. Zhong Hao does not deny the value of right-wrong judgment, but he argues that sharp public distinction can create resentment and danger when a person lacks Li Ying's family backing. The main profile value is therefore identity, cohort placement, and a prudence teaching that feeds [[PrudentAmbiguitySelfProtection|审慎含混式自保]].

Zhong Hao should still be kept distinct from [[ZhongHaoLateHan|种暠]], the differently written frontier official attached to Hanji 844 and Duliao governance. This page preserves the 钟皓 route for the Yingchuan Four Magistrates figure.

## Key Characteristics

- Yingchuan county-magistrate figure named as one of the Yingchuan Four Magistrates.
- Associated with 德政 and people-benefiting local administration in the source's cohort description.
- Reputation peer of Xun Shu in Hanji 832's famous-worthy field.
- Prudence mentor who counsels Zhong Jin on the danger of excessive public right-wrong sorting.
- Canonically distinct from the existing 种暠 route because the Chinese character and source context differ.

## Qualifications

The source notes still do not provide Zhong Hao's exact county posting, full biography, or a detailed administrative episode comparable to Han Shao's granary relief. Hanji 832 adds reputation and counsel evidence, not a complete career. Claims about his prudence teaching should remain source-bounded to Zhong Jin's case.

## What Changed

- Hanji 832 adds Zhong Hao's reputation parity with Xun Shu and his counsel to Zhong Jin.
- The page now connects Zhong Hao to prudent ambiguity and self-protection, not only cohort membership.
