## Overview

钟皓 / Zhong Hao is a late-Han Yingchuan county-magistrate figure named in the current wiki through the [[YingchuanFourMagistrates|颍川四长]] cohort. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-842-shenme-shi-haoguan-ta-wei-baixing-gan-pinming-lj2okcbcvcmrfqrj3d-k1ylqdi2si|Hanji 842]] groups him with [[HanShaoLateHan|韩韶]], [[XunShuLateHan|荀淑]], and [[ChenShiLateHan|陈寔]] as magistrates remembered for virtuous local governance and benefit to the people.

## Current Profile

Zhong Hao's current page is intentionally narrow. Hanji 842 does not provide an individual anecdote comparable to Han Shao's granary decision. It names Zhong Hao as one member of a four-person Yingchuan magistrate reputation field, where local office, 德政, and public benefit become the basis for praise.

The main profile value is therefore identity and cohort placement. Zhong Hao should be kept distinct from [[ZhongHaoLateHan|种暠]], the differently written frontier official attached to Hanji 844 and Duliao governance. This page preserves the 钟皓 route for the Yingchuan Four Magistrates figure until more source notes add individual detail.

## Key Characteristics

- Yingchuan county-magistrate figure named as one of the Yingchuan Four Magistrates.
- Associated with 德政 and people-benefiting local administration in the source's cohort description.
- Currently source-bounded to cohort evidence rather than a separate detailed biography.
- Canonically distinct from the existing 种暠 route because the Chinese character and source context differ.
