# 赵奢 / Zhao She

赵奢 / Zhao She enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-86-lishishang-kao-shouzu-de-jiangjun-jingran-shi-ta-lmmmfz4ii-hx7trbxv2rcpkllcq]] before his famous later identity as a Zhao general. The episode first treats him as a possible former [[YanState|燕国]] frontier official at 上谷, using [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] as a suggestive but source-limited basis.

His developed role in this source is fiscal. After returning to [[ZhaoState|赵国]], Zhao She serves as a 田部吏 responsible for tax collection. When [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]]'s household refuses to pay, he enforces the law by executing nine household personnel, turning a tax dispute into a test of whether powerful aristocrats can stand outside state law.

Zhao She's argument to Pingyuanjun reframes enforcement as self-preservation. If Pingyuanjun violates law, Zhao's law collapses, the state weakens, and outside powers can destroy the very privilege Pingyuanjun wants to protect. If Pingyuanjun leads in public law observance, Zhao grows stronger and his household remains secure.

Pingyuanjun accepts the argument and recommends Zhao She to [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]], who appoints him to manage national taxation. The episode says Zhao She's administration fills the treasury and enriches the people, making him a case where non-battlefield state capacity precedes military fame.
