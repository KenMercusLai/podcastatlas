# GoCyber Academy

GoCyber Academy is the education venture founded by [[JosephStrader]] and discussed in [[ep-9-chatgpt-and-education-systems]]. The source describes it as a K-12 computer-science education program teaching foundational skills such as block coding, typing, and computer literacy.

In the episode, GoCyber Academy makes [[K12ComputerScienceAccess]] practical rather than abstract. Joseph says the program tries to bridge older and newer education approaches by letting students create technology projects and by showing parents what students have built.

## Key Claims
- GoCyber Academy is source-scoped as a K-12 computer-science and technology-literacy program.
- Its curriculum examples include block coding, typing, and basic computer literacy rather than only advanced AI use.
- The episode treats parent visibility and student projects as ways to make technology education more trusted and less abstract.
- GoCyber Academy's role supports the source's equity claim that students from different communities need access to technical opportunity.
