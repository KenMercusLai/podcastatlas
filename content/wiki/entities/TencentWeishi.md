# Tencent Weishi / 腾讯微视

## Overview
Tencent Weishi / 腾讯微视 is [[Tencent]]'s short-video product represented in the wiki through platform-competition analysis and [[MengYangStandup|梦阳 / 孟阳]]'s operator recollection.

## Current Profile
The platform-competition account in [[touteng-dazhan-ba-nian-hou-zai-ba-zijie-he-tengxun-zai-gege-zhanchang-shang-de-jingzheng-zhuyi-chaikai-zijie-tiaodong-di-6-ji-lvglr-jws7o7utjauxnoqqasj91d]] presents Weishi as Tencent's restarted answer after [[Douyin]]'s rapid rise. Traffic buying, MCN imports, subsidies, and Tencent distribution could produce scale, but could not quickly recreate creator supply, creation tools, content ecology, retention, and network effects.

An internal operating perspective in [[no-218-mengyang-dachang-xunlian-ni-xian-suan-shouyi-er-ziyou-zhiye-jiao-ni-yongbao-suiji-gkwrijenemmyatrz5grxxlq6]] does not overturn that account. Mengyang recalls growth from several million to roughly 40 million DAU, continuing management pressure rather than celebration, and teams across rivals confronting similar shortages and using similar tactics. Her account shifts the judgment from one obvious mistake toward the combined effects of entry timing, algorithmic capability, product inheritance, ecosystem depth, and organizational response.

## Key Characteristics
- It was a Tencent short-video response operating under strong comparison with Douyin.
- It could draw on Tencent traffic, spending, creator recruitment, and organizational resources.
- Its operating work included content ecology, creator management, review standards, incentives, and content acquisition.
- Reported user growth did not close the competitive gap or remove management pressure.
- Its history illustrates that similar tactics can yield different results when timing, recommendation systems, ecosystem, and retention differ.

## Evidence
- Competitive-system account - [[touteng-dazhan-ba-nian-hou-zai-ba-zijie-he-tengxun-zai-gege-zhanchang-shang-de-jingzheng-zhuyi-chaikai-zijie-tiaodong-di-6-ji-lvglr-jws7o7utjauxnoqqasj91d]] describes traffic buying, MCN import, Tencent distribution, and weaker UGC ecology and retention relative to Douyin.
- Operator account - [[no-218-mengyang-dachang-xunlian-ni-xian-suan-shouyi-er-ziyou-zhiye-jiao-ni-yongbao-suiji-gkwrijenemmyatrz5grxxlq6]] records Mengyang's work on content ecology, creators, review standards, ecosystem design, and incentive budgets.
- Scale without convergence - [[no-218-mengyang-dachang-xunlian-ni-xian-suan-shouyi-er-ziyou-zhiye-jiao-ni-yongbao-suiji-gkwrijenemmyatrz5grxxlq6]] reports growth to about 40 million DAU while the organization still understood itself as behind a stronger rival.

## Qualifications
The first source is interpretive platform history and the second is one former employee's retrospective account. The DAU figure, internal mood, cross-company interview comparisons, and causal explanations remain source-scoped; neither source provides a controlled comparison that isolates one reason for the outcome.

## What Changed
- Added an operator-side account of substantial growth, similar cross-rival tactics, and continued competitive lag.
- Reframed Weishi's outcome as a multi-factor ecosystem and timing problem rather than evidence of one obvious execution error.
