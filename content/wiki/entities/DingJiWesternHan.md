## Overview

丁姬 / Ding Ji enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-555-ta-weile-dang-huangdi-weisha-yao-xisheng-qindie-lq3kkkmk9nh4yhxuovmy_uktalrv|Hanji 555]] as [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]'s birth mother whose access to the new crown prince is denied because she did not raise him. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-580-huigu-sizhou-shuishi-nide-kaoshan-liid3tymhojmajkyeqoq6vryprhz|Hanji 580]] adds the later death-and-burial branch: Aidi sends her back to [[Dingtao|定陶]] for burial with his birth father, making her part of Aidi's personal-filial ritual before [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-626-waren-fenmu-wangmang-jing-ruci-sangxinbingkuang-lotwk6rbtl5pvbrnlfrysu5ol6rz|Hanji 626]]'s posthumous vulnerability under [[WangMang|王莽]].

## Current Profile

Hanji 555 says Ding Ji is Liu Xin's birth mother, but not the person who raised him. When [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] asks that Fu Taihou and Ding Ji be allowed to visit the crown prince, Chengdi allows Fu Taihou's visits on foster-care grounds but keeps Ding Ji excluded. Ding Ji therefore appears first as a birth-mother tie subordinated to the adoptive succession boundary.

Hanji 580 says Ding Ji dies while Aidi is still ruling and that Aidi invokes husband-wife burial logic to have her returned to Dingtao and buried with his birth father. The later Hanji 626 episode says Ding Ji's burial treatment and posthumous honors are judged by Wang Mang as above the rank proper to a vassal consort-side woman. After Wang Zhengjun first resists the grave-opening proposal, Wang Mang presses again and gains approval for stronger demotion measures, including lower-status burial arrangements and removal of luxury burial symbols.

Ding Ji's current wiki role is narrow. She is not developed as a full lifetime political actor in this source; she marks how Wang Mang's ritual correction reaches backward into the dead, using tombs, titles, and material signs as evidence for political punishment.

## Key Characteristics

- Mother of Han Aidi whose posthumous honor remains politically exposed after death.
- Birth mother whose access to Liu Xin is blocked after he becomes Chengdi's heir because she did not raise him.
- Birth mother whose Dingtao burial is treated by Aidi as personal filial duty.
- Parallel target with Fu Taihou in Wang Mang's 5 CE grave-opening and demotion campaign.
- Burial-status case in which luxury goods and rank symbols become grounds for punishment.
- Figure through whom the episode shows dead consort women being used for live court politics.

## Qualifications

The page does not settle Ding Ji's full lifetime status, exact title wording, Dingtao tomb labor mechanics, or the legal validity of Wang Mang's rank argument. Hanji 555 supplies the pre-accession access distinction, but not Ding Ji's private relationship with Liu Xin. Hanji 580 supplies the burial branch from Aidi's perspective; Hanji 626 supplies a compact narrative of her later posthumous treatment, and the wiki keeps broader Ai-era factional history source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 555's pre-accession exclusion branch before Hanji 580's Dingtao burial and Hanji 626's posthumous demotion branch.
