## Overview

丁姬 / Ding Ji enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-626-waren-fenmu-wangmang-jing-ruci-sangxinbingkuang-lotwk6rbtl5pvbrnlfrysu5ol6rz|Hanji 626]] as [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]'s mother and one of the two deceased women whom [[WangMang|王莽]] targets in 5 CE. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-580-huigu-sizhou-shuishi-nide-kaoshan-liid3tymhojmajkyeqoq6vryprhz|Hanji 580]] adds the earlier death-and-burial branch: Aidi sends her back to [[Dingtao|定陶]] for burial with his birth father, making her part of Aidi's personal-filial ritual before her later posthumous vulnerability.

## Current Profile

Hanji 580 says Ding Ji dies while Aidi is still ruling and that Aidi invokes husband-wife burial logic to have her returned to Dingtao and buried with his birth father. The later Hanji 626 episode says Ding Ji's burial treatment and posthumous honors are judged by Wang Mang as above the rank proper to a vassal consort-side woman. After [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] first resists the grave-opening proposal, Wang Mang presses again and gains approval for stronger demotion measures, including lower-status burial arrangements and removal of luxury burial symbols.

Ding Ji's current wiki role is narrow. She is not developed as a full lifetime political actor in this source; she marks how Wang Mang's ritual correction reaches backward into the dead, using tombs, titles, and material signs as evidence for political punishment.

## Key Characteristics

- Mother of Han Aidi whose posthumous honor remains politically exposed after death.
- Birth mother whose Dingtao burial is treated by Aidi as personal filial duty.
- Parallel target with Fu Taihou in Wang Mang's 5 CE grave-opening and demotion campaign.
- Burial-status case in which luxury goods and rank symbols become grounds for punishment.
- Figure through whom the episode shows dead consort women being used for live court politics.

- Identity and target status: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-626-waren-fenmu-wangmang-jing-ruci-sangxinbingkuang-lotwk6rbtl5pvbrnlfrysu5ol6rz|Hanji 626]] identifies Ding Ji as Han Aidi's mother and says Wang Mang includes her in the posthumous prosecution.
- Burial demotion: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-626-waren-fenmu-wangmang-jing-ruci-sangxinbingkuang-lotwk6rbtl5pvbrnlfrysu5ol6rz|Hanji 626]] says Wang Mang requests lower-grade treatment for her coffin, garments, and burial placement.
- Tomb destruction sequence: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-626-waren-fenmu-wangmang-jing-ruci-sangxinbingkuang-lotwk6rbtl5pvbrnlfrysu5ol6rz|Hanji 626]] says her tomb is part of the large-scale leveling campaign.

## Qualifications

The page does not settle Ding Ji's full lifetime status, exact title wording, Dingtao tomb labor mechanics, or the legal validity of Wang Mang's rank argument. Hanji 580 supplies the burial branch from Aidi's perspective; Hanji 626 supplies a compact narrative of her later posthumous treatment, and the wiki keeps broader Ai-era factional history source-scoped.
