## Overview

丁姬 / Ding Ji enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-626-waren-fenmu-wangmang-jing-ruci-sangxinbingkuang-lotwk6rbtl5pvbrnlfrysu5ol6rz|Hanji 626]] as [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]'s mother and one of the two deceased women whom [[WangMang|王莽]] targets in 5 CE. The source groups her with [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]] as a posthumous-rank and burial-status case.

## Current Profile

The episode says Ding Ji's burial treatment and posthumous honors are judged by Wang Mang as above the rank proper to a vassal consort-side woman. After [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] first resists the grave-opening proposal, Wang Mang presses again and gains approval for stronger demotion measures, including lower-status burial arrangements and removal of luxury burial symbols.

Ding Ji's current wiki role is narrow. She is not developed as a full lifetime political actor in this source; she marks how Wang Mang's ritual correction reaches backward into the dead, using tombs, titles, and material signs as evidence for political punishment.

## Key Characteristics

- Mother of Han Aidi whose posthumous honor remains politically exposed after death.
- Parallel target with Fu Taihou in Wang Mang's 5 CE grave-opening and demotion campaign.
- Burial-status case in which luxury goods and rank symbols become grounds for punishment.
- Figure through whom the episode shows dead consort women being used for live court politics.
