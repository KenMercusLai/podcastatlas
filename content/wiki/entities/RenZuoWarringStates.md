## Overview

任作 / Ren Zuo is a [[WeiState|魏国]] remonstrant who challenges [[WeiWenhou|魏文侯]] over the proposed assignment of newly conquered [[ZhongshanState|中山国]] to the ruler's son.

## Current Profile

Ren Zuo's importance lies less in a developed biography than in the feedback relationship his speech exposes. He answers a ruler's request for moral evaluation with an unwelcome distributional critique, leaves when Wei Wenhou becomes angry, and returns only after [[DiHuang|翟黄]] recasts his directness as evidence that the ruler is capable of sustaining an honest minister. His case therefore distinguishes asking for praise from maintaining a court in which a negative answer can survive.

## Key Characteristics

- Direct critic who refuses the court's consensus praise of Wei Wenhou.
- Treats the disposition of conquered territory as evidence about ruler virtue and family favoritism.
- Speaker placed at risk by the ruler's immediate anger.
- Minister whose recall becomes a visible test of whether criticism remains institutionally survivable.

## Evidence

- Direct distributive criticism: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-05-guangcai-duomu-de-weiwenhou-2-lrpjtxkpfccebs-0phxiozjsv5rn|Zhouji 05 part 2]] says Ren Zuo objects that Zhongshan is going to the ruler's son rather than a brother.
- Anger and withdrawal: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-05-guangcai-duomu-de-weiwenhou-2-lrpjtxkpfccebs-0phxiozjsv5rn|Zhouji 05 part 2]] says Wei Wenhou becomes angry and Ren Zuo leaves.
- Restored access: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-05-guangcai-duomu-de-weiwenhou-2-lrpjtxkpfccebs-0phxiozjsv5rn|Zhouji 05 part 2]] says Di Huang's reframing leads Wei Wenhou to recall Ren Zuo and welcome him personally.

## Qualifications

The current bounded evidence is a single short podcast episode. It does not establish Ren Zuo's office, broader career, exact wording, or the long-run effect of his criticism. The source reports a repaired audience and respectful recall, not an explicit reversal of the Zhongshan appointment.
