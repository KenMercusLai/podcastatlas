# Stellantis

## Overview
Stellantis appears in the source as the international automotive group that invested in [[Leapmotor|零跑汽车]] and formed [[LeapmotorInternational|零跑国际]] to sell Leapmotor vehicles outside Greater China.

## Current Profile
The source presents Stellantis less as a standalone automaker than as the overseas-market operating partner for a Chinese EV company. Its value in the episode is channel, brand, service, finance, insurance, and local-market infrastructure, while Leapmotor contributes electric-vehicle technology and products. This makes Stellantis part of a reverse-joint-venture globalization case where an older overseas automaker helps commercialize Chinese EVs abroad.

## Key Characteristics
- Industrial investor in Leapmotor after the company's 2022 listing raised less capital than hoped.
- Majority partner in Leapmotor International, according to the source's 51/49 ownership description.
- Provides overseas channels, service systems, finance, insurance, and local operating knowledge.
- Represents a slower-process, older automaker culture compared with Leapmotor's fast private-company decision style.
- Creates an overseas route that may accelerate entry while making execution depend on partner commitment.
