## Overview

戚继光 appears as a technically capable military reformer who builds local effectiveness under weak standards but cannot detach his work from court patronage.

## Current Profile

The episode credits Qi with detailed reform of recruitment, organization, formation, weapons, training, pay, and logistics, represented through 《纪效新书》. The detail of the manual is interpreted as evidence that the wider military system lacked consistent technical standards.

[[ZhangJuzheng|张居正]]'s support gives Qi resources and protection. The same relationship later exposes him to reversal, making private patronage both an enabling mechanism and a durability limit.

## Key Characteristics

- Military practitioner focused on recruitment, training, arms, formation, and logistics.
- Builds detailed local standards where system-wide standards are weak.
- Converts technical knowledge into a documented operating method.
- Depends on civilian political sponsorship for resources and protection.
- Loses position when the patronage network collapses.

- Technical system-building: [[vol-362-wanli-shiwunian-da-shibai-shidai-de-ren-hai-neng-nadao-da-jieguo-ma-gkwriw4o2at-a-vjhw-t-4dvr|Vol.362]] describes 《纪效新书》 as covering recruitment, formation, weapons, and supply in detail.
- Organizational diagnosis: [[vol-362-wanli-shiwunian-da-shibai-shidai-de-ren-hai-neng-nadao-da-jieguo-ma-gkwriw4o2at-a-vjhw-t-4dvr|Vol.362]] interprets that detail as a response to absent common standards.
- Patronage dependency: [[vol-362-wanli-shiwunian-da-shibai-shidai-de-ren-hai-neng-nadao-da-jieguo-ma-gkwriw4o2at-a-vjhw-t-4dvr|Vol.362]] ties his resources and later fall to Zhang Juzheng's political fortunes.

## Qualifications

This profile does not independently evaluate Qi's campaigns, manual, innovations, or political conduct. The claim that patronage explains both success and failure is a source-scoped institutional interpretation rather than a complete causal account.

## What Changed

- Established Qi as the episode's technical capacity without durable institutional protection case.
