# Christie Lamond

## Overview
Christie Lamond is [[KarissaBodnar]]'s close friend whose cancer diagnosis and death shaped the mission behind [[ThriveCausemetics]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Lamond less as a business operator than as the moral and emotional origin of Thrive Causemetics. Her philanthropy work, cancer experience, blog language, and approach to living with limited time became the reason Bodnar reconsidered her career and built giving into a beauty company.

## Key Characteristics
- Functions as the mission-origin figure for the Thrive Causemetics story.
- Is associated in the source with philanthropy, including work with girls through international education settings.
- Influenced Bodnar's thinking about time, purpose, and professional work after her cancer diagnosis and death.
