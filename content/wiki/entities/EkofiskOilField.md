# Ekofisk Oil Field

The Ekofisk oil field appears in [[how-to-beat-the-resource-curse-in-norway-summer-school]] as the 1969 discovery that confirmed [[Norway]]'s oil era. The source uses the discovery as the turning point after [[FaroukAlQassem]] had warned that the country needed to prepare for oil wealth.

In the episode, Ekofisk matters less as a geological object than as an institutional test. Norway responded with regulation, licensing restraint, taxation, technical reinvestment, and eventually the [[NorwegianOilFund]], making the field part of a [[ResourceCurseGovernance]] branch.
