## Overview

田明 / Tian Ming is the Jiangxia commandery official paired with [[MaRongLateHan|马融]] in Hanji 836. His brief profile matters because he shows the lethal edge of Liang Ji's retaliation: the same monitored visit that Ma Rong survives leads Tian Ming into exile and death.

## Current Profile

The source introduces Tian Ming as a newly appointed Jiangxia taishou traveling to his post. He and Ma Rong pass [[LiangBuyiLateHan|梁不疑]]'s household and visit him, apparently as ordinary elite social contact.

After [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]] learns of the visit, Tian Ming is given a separate framed accusation from Ma Rong's corruption charge. He receives hair-removal punishment and flogging and is exiled to Shuofang, where the episode says he dies.

Tian Ming's current wiki role is therefore source-bounded: he is not a full biography yet, but a case in which outer-relative suspicion turns a social visit into irreversible career and life damage.

## Key Characteristics

- Jiangxia taishou whose known wiki role is tied to the Liang Buyi visit.
- Paired victim with Ma Rong in Liang Ji's retaliation against scholar-official contact.
- Exile casualty whose punishment shows that source-scoped framed charges could become fatal.
