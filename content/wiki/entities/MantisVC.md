# Mantis

## Overview
Mantis is the venture firm founded by [[DrewTaggart|Drew Taggart]] and [[AlexPall|Alex Pall]] of [[TheChainsmokers|The Chainsmokers]].

## Current Profile
The founders describe Mantis as a seed and Series A investor across cybersecurity, AI, infrastructure, deep technology, and health technology. It does not seek lead positions and instead claims a collaborative “sixth man” role in relationships, go-to-market work, and brand building. The interview emphasizes that this positioning must still produce realized returns.

## Key Characteristics
- Focuses primarily on seed and Series A investments.
- Invests across several technology and health categories.
- Avoids lead positions in favor of a supporting portfolio role.
- Uses founder access, relationships, distribution, and brand experience as claimed value-adds.
- Treats follow-on concentration and liquidity as capabilities still being developed.
