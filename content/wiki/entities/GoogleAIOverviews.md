# Google AI Overviews

## Overview
Google AI Overviews are the AI-generated search summaries that the wiki uses to track AI search attribution, publisher traffic pressure, search-ad disruption, and now community-platform referral risk.

## Current Profile
The original Marketplace Tech source presents AI Overviews as a product whose links are becoming more prominent under pressure from publishers, users, and regulators. The new Intelligence source adds [[Reddit]] as a community-platform case: investors worry that Google can use Reddit-like information in AI-generated answers while reducing referral traffic to Reddit itself.

## Key Characteristics
- Google search product that gives generated summaries above or alongside traditional web links.
- Source-attribution problem because visible links may improve trust without restoring meaningful outbound clicks.
- Publisher-economics pressure point when sites such as [[DailyMail]] lose search traffic.
- Platform-power and regulatory issue because Google controls a dominant search surface.
- Community-platform referral risk because Reddit can be both a licensed data source and a site affected by fewer clicks.

## Evidence
- Link visibility and publisher pressure - [[tech-20260220-0220-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260220-0220-mp-tech-pod-128]] says Google is making links more prominent in AI Overviews while publishers and regulators question traffic and content use.
- User-click uncertainty - [[tech-20260220-0220-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260220-0220-mp-tech-pod-128]] cites [[PewResearchCenter]] click-behavior evidence and the [[DailyMail]] traffic-loss example to show that attribution may not restore the older click path.
- Reddit referral risk - [[ire-and-ice-the-toll-of-americas-deportations-6a993426809ae7c2f4293df9]] says investors worry Google's AI-generated search box may reduce traffic to Reddit links even as Google reportedly licenses Reddit data.

## Qualifications
The source-link interface change, publisher traffic estimates, Reddit referral effect, and investor reaction remain source-scoped. The page should not assume that every AI Overview reduces traffic equally across all source types.
