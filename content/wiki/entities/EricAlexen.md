# Eric Alexen

## Overview
Eric Alexen is the founder representing [[LCubeLifestyle|L-Cube Lifestyle]] in the episode's second caller segment.

## Current Profile
Alexen is trying to choose between scaling consumer retail and wholesale co-branding for UV-protective apparel. The advice redirects that channel question toward a prior positioning problem: customers first need a simple reason to understand the product as sun protection rather than generic athleisure.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates a Florida apparel company manufacturing in Brazil.
- Sells through a website and Amazon while pursuing wholesale and co-branded events.
- Faces tariff pressure on already narrow margins.
- Holds a technical product advantage that is not yet translated into obvious customer language.
