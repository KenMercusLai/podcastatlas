# 韩烈侯 / Marquis Lie of Han

韩烈侯 / Marquis Lie of Han appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-1-lvkp-ju7pf5z5cz3thojv3s2fkyx]] as [[HanJinghou|韩景侯]]'s son and successor in [[HanState|韩国]]. The episode treats the accession as part of a clustered succession moment for early [[WarringStatesPeriod|战国时期]] states.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-2-lvaqlgyccrugfo0l-wuwf1mt1kcm]] records Han Lie Hou's death in 387 BCE and the accession of his son [[HanWenhou|韩文侯]], continuing Han's role as a compact succession node.
