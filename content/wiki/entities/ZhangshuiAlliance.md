# 漳水之盟 / Zhangshui Alliance

漳水之盟 / Zhangshui Alliance appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-17-hanzhaohou-he-shenbuhai-lghjmjhq9qs4se96c5ofgz6caqof]] as the 351 BCE reconciliation between [[WeiState|魏国]] and [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. The episode emphasizes that this peace was harder than Wei-Qi accommodation because Wei had previously taken [[Handan|邯郸]], Zhao's capital.

The source reads [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王]]'s return of Handan as the credibility move that makes the alliance possible. The deeper cause is [[QinEastwardPressure|秦国东进压力]]: Qin's attacks on Wei and Zhao make former enemies more willing to reduce their mutual exposure.
