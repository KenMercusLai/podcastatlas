# 手工耿 / Shougong Geng

## Overview
手工耿 / Shougong Geng enters the wiki as a Chinese maker and short-video creator whose welded inventions are discussed in [[shougonggeng-luoyonghao-cunkou-de-wujindian-li-chuxianle-yige-dada-zhuyi-yishujia-lgbdj-juqslyga0ulvvrqwnedga1]] as useful, useless, comic, functional, and art-adjacent at the same time.

## Current Profile
The source presents 手工耿 as a self-taught craft creator shaped by rural life, construction work, and welding rather than by formal art or product-design training. He moved into self-media around 2017 after making stainless-steel slippers, wallets, slingshots, toys, and later larger devices, finding that short video could turn making, testing, danger, awkward function, and audience comments into the value of the work itself.

His public profile is therefore not just "useless inventor." He insists many works begin from usefulness, while [[LuoYonghao]] reframes usefulness into entertainment value, playful functional value, and serious practical value.

## Key Characteristics
- Rural and manual-labor background, including farming memories, Beijing construction work, and welding experience learned partly from his father.
- Material-centered maker style built around stainless steel, scrap materials, visible handwork, and repeated physical testing.
- Short-video creator whose objects gain value through making process, performance, risk, comedy, and audience participation.
- Commercially cautious figure who dislikes arbitrary brand deals, felt early pricing shame, and did not strongly pursue MCN expansion.
- Art-adjacent public figure interpreted through modern art and Dadaist language while personally staying closer to practical maker identity.
