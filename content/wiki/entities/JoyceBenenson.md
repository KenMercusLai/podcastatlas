# Joyce Benenson

## Overview
Joyce Benenson is presented in [[the-magic-wand-experiment-the-magic-wand-experiment|The Magic Wand Experiment]] as a child psychologist proposing an impossible study of biological and parental influences on childhood behavior.

## Current Profile
Benenson imagines changing the apparent sex of newborn children, misleading parents, isolating the children from outside social influence, and observing interests through about age five. She predicts persistent sex-linked differences and frames the result as potentially persuasive to parents.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses an extreme imagined control to separate parental perception from chromosomal sex.
- Makes strong directional predictions about play, competition, caregiving, objects, and interests.
- Treats experimental evidence as a tool of public persuasion as well as discovery.

## Evidence
### Proposed developmental experiment
- [[the-magic-wand-experiment-the-magic-wand-experiment|The Magic Wand Experiment]] supplies the design, predictions, and persuasive aim.

## Qualifications
The source reports a hypothetical, unethical experiment rather than completed evidence. Isolation, deception, binary sex assignment, parental treatment, development, and social context cannot be cleanly reduced to one variable.
