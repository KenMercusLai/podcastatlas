# 和田幸子 / Wada Sachiko

和田幸子 appears in [[88-bie-renshu-huizi-zai-ting-budao-hecai-de-shijie-li-huiquan-708510803]] as the teacher connected to [[KeikoBoxer|惠子]]'s language and hearing classroom. When 惠子 cannot tolerate the ordinary classroom, 和田老师 gives her a place where she can come and go without being forced into immediate compliance.

The episode makes her important because support does not arrive as a lecture. She gives 惠子 enough safety to stay connected to school at all, and later becomes the person through whom 惠子 can try to reconnect with another teacher after many years.
