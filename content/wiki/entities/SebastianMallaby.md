# Sebastian Mallaby

Sebastian Mallaby is the biography author behind the [[DemisHassabis]] book discussed in [[e226-liaoliao-deepmind-chuangshiren-hasabisi-yige-kexuejia-yu-shikong-de-ai-jingsai-7abda28b-99c6-4ebc-8c0d-37bcc77f6a73]]. The episode uses his reporting and private conversations with Hassabis as the narrative base for the DeepMind history.

The source also treats Mallaby's biography as interpretively tilted. The host says the book may be somewhat favorable toward Hassabis, and [[ZhouJiangong]] suggests that an English author writing a British "national treasure" figure may naturally emphasize his virtues. That makes Mallaby important not only as a source, but as part of the episode's evidence-quality boundary.
