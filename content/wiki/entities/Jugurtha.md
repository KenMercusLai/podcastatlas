# Jugurtha / 朱古达

## Overview

Jugurtha / 朱古达 is the Numidian king whose war with Rome gives [[GaiusMarius]] the command opportunity that turns outsider ambition into consular and military dominance.

## Current Profile

Episode 701 first places Jugurtha at Numantia as a Numidian ally under [[ScipioAemilianus]], where Marius also serves and builds soldierly reputation. Jugurtha later becomes Rome's African problem: he kills rivals for the Numidian throne, uses bribery to manage Roman politics, attacks Cirta against Roman instructions, and forces Rome into a difficult cavalry-favored war. [[QuintusCaeciliusMetellusNumidicus|Metellus]] restores discipline and fights effectively, but Marius uses dissatisfaction with Metellus to win the 107 BC consulship and take over the command.

Jugurtha's surrender also creates the early [[LuciusCorneliusSulla|Sulla]] problem. Marius holds the command and triumph, but Sulla negotiates the surrender through the king of Mauritania and later makes that role visible as personal credit.

## Key Characteristics

- Jugurtha is both early witness to Marius's military rise and later opponent in Marius's African command.
- His use of bribery makes Roman corruption part of the war's domestic meaning.
- Numidia's terrain and cavalry advantage expose limits in Roman heavy-infantry assumptions.
- His surrender produces a command-credit dispute between Marius and Sulla.

## Evidence

- Numantia prehistory: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] places Jugurtha with Numidian cavalry and elephants under Scipio Aemilianus while Marius is building his soldierly reputation.
- Corruption and war trigger: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] says Jugurtha kills cousins, bribes Roman support, attacks Cirta, and kills Romans there, forcing Senate action.
- Military difficulty: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] presents Numidia's open terrain as cavalry-friendly and Metellus's discipline restoration as a serious but incomplete Roman response.
- Surrender and credit: [[701-roman-civil-war-rise-of-the-general-part-1-glt1262857909]] credits Sulla's negotiation with the king of Mauritania for Jugurtha's surrender while keeping Marius as the commander who celebrates the triumph.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to episode 701 and does not reconstruct Jugurtha's full biography, Numidian succession politics, or the complete chronology of the Jugurthine War. The source uses him mainly to show how African war, corruption, and command credit shaped Marius's rise.

## What Changed

- Created Jugurtha as the African-war actor linking Marius, Metellus, Sulla, and Roman corruption.

## Relationships

- [[GaiusMarius]] - Roman commander whose consulship and triumph are made possible by the Jugurthine War.
- [[LuciusCorneliusSulla]] - subordinate negotiator whose surrender credit later irritates Marius.
- [[QuintusCaeciliusMetellusNumidicus]] - Roman commander who restores discipline before Marius takes the African command.
- [[ScipioAemilianus]] - earlier Roman commander under whom Jugurtha and Marius both appear at Numantia.
- [[RomanRepublic]] - political system whose corruption and command allocation are exposed by the war.
- [[LateRomanRepublicCrisis]] - broader crisis field to which Jugurtha's war contributes.
