# 胡阳 / Huyang (late Han)

## Overview
胡阳 / Huyang is a late-Han campaign location in the 197 CE branch where [[CaoCao|曹操]] moves against [[ZhangXiu|张绣]] and [[LiuBiao|刘表]] after the Wancheng disaster. The episode treats Huyang as part of the outer defensive line linking Liu Biao's Jingzhou support to Zhang Xiu's Wan/Nanyang position.

## Current Profile
Huyang is useful in the wiki as a source-bounded place node, not as a fully reconstructed county history. In Hanji 1021, taking Huyang and capturing [[DengJiLateHan|邓济]] gives Cao Cao a practical way to pressure the [[ZhangXiu|张绣]]-[[LiuBiao|刘表]] connection before continuing toward [[WuyinLateHan|舞阴]] and the broader [[NanyangCommandery|南阳郡]] field.

## Key Characteristics
- Late-Han Nanyang/Jingzhou-front location, distinct from the Warring States name page [[HuShangHuyang|胡伤 / 胡阳]].
- Captured by Cao Cao during the winter 197 CE campaign.
- Setting for Deng Ji's capture.
- Paired with Wuyin as a position that helps explain Cao Cao's pressure on Liu Biao's northern screen.

## Qualifications
The page does not settle Huyang's full administrative history. It records only the episode's late-Han military use and the disambiguation needed to prevent collision with another Huyang/Hu Yang name in the wiki.
