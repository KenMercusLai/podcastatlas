# Tencent TEG

Tencent TEG is the technical and engineering group described in [[yao-shunyu-laidao-tengxun-300tian-1-176-1]] as the birthplace of [[TencentHunyuan]]. The source says Tencent's early large-model work included internal races among AI Lab, data-platform, and other technical teams before resources were merged into Hunyuan.

The episode uses TEG to show the mixed strength of a large technical platform: it can pool engineering, infrastructure, and business support, but it can also preserve older search, advertising, and recommendation habits that are not enough for frontier-model research without new leadership and talent.
