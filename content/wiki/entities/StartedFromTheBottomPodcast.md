# Started From the Bottom

## Overview

*Started From the Bottom* is a podcast created and hosted by [[JustinRichmond]] about how successful people of color began their careers, encountered obstacles, learned unfamiliar systems, and advanced.

## Current Profile

The show's origin lies in a failed opportunity rather than a completed success. Richmond gained unusual access to Quincy Jones and proposed a podcast, but he could not sell the project and realized that he lacked basic business knowledge and a trusted roadmap for the meetings he had entered.

He designed *Started From the Bottom* to make the guidance he wanted from figures such as Magic Johnson or Byron Allen available to listeners without comparable networks. The format presents Richmond as a co-learner rather than a final authority and joins origin stories to practical questions about risk, motivation, mentors, institutional conventions, and career mobility.

## Key Characteristics

- Centers the career beginnings and advancement of successful people of color.
- Converts private access and interview relationships into a public guidance resource.
- Seeks practical explanations of institutional language, business conventions, and major decisions.
- Uses origin stories to examine obstacles without reducing a career to triumph over adversity.
- Positions Richmond and the audience as learners who can test different sources of motivation and advice together.

## Qualifications

This profile describes the show's purpose through its creator's launch conversation, not through a systematic review of its episodes, guests, reception, or listener outcomes. Shared identity can support recognition and trust, but it does not guarantee relevant advice, transferable experience, or successful mentorship. Interview access is also not equivalent to an ongoing advising relationship.
