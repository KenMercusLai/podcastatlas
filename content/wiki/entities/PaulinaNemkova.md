# Paulina Nemkova

Paulina Nemkova appears in [[ep-6-data-science-ai-talk]] as a second-year PhD student at [[UniversityOfNorthTexas|University of North Texas]] researching AI and machine learning. The episode presents her as a [[NontraditionalAIResearchPath]] example: she moved from an economics bachelor's degree in Belarus into U.S. graduate AI research by combining statistics, programming, coursework, professor outreach, and early research exposure.

Her main PhD project is [[EEGBrainReading]], a brain-signal classification task using EEG data to infer the category of object a person may be thinking about. She frames the original motivation through [[LockedInSyndromeAssistiveCommunication]], while repeatedly keeping the claim narrower than full thought prediction.

Paulina also gives the source its academic-practice frame. She contrasts [[AcademicAIResearchRole|academic AI research]] with industry application, emphasizes [[AIResearchLiteratureCurrency]], and treats [[ResearchReplicationIntegrity]] as central because non-replicable work and statistical misuse can damage scientific progress.

The episode also mentions her [[CryptoTimeSeriesAnalysis]] paper and interest in cryptocurrency and decentralized finance, which connects her economics background back into data-science research. Her startup club at [[UniversityOfNorthTexas]] is presented as an attempt to increase collaboration between computer science and business students.
