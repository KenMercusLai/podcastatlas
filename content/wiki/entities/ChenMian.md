# Chen Mian / 陈冕

## Overview
Chen Mian is the founder of [[Evoken]], the company associated in the evidence set with [[Liblib]], [[Lovart]], [[Lavod]], and [[LibTV]]. His profile joins experience across several Chinese internet businesses to a founder thesis about directing consequential work, surviving model-layer change, and building AI applications around creative workflows.

## Current Profile
Chen presents his repeated moves through Tencent, 360, Baidu, Didi, Mobike, MissFresh, and ByteDance as a search for decision authority and product leverage. The earlier interview emphasizes how mobility, O2O, shared bikes, grocery delivery, education, and video tools taught him to distinguish product-led opportunities from businesses governed mainly by supply chain, operations, or existing organizational ceilings. He says the success of MissFresh growth work and his business role in ByteDance education supplied the confidence to lead, while the education-policy shock and later CapCut commercialization sharpened his sensitivity to external change.

His AI application thesis begins with speed but does not end there. The January interview says model breakthroughs persuaded him to leave ByteDance before funding and staffing were complete, launch [[Liblib]] for professional creators, and then build [[Lovart]] as an AI designer usable by non-design professionals. The later interview shows the cost of that pace: cash scarcity, moving model capabilities, low margins, product-priority tradeoffs, and organizational mistakes. Across both sources, survival means converting speed and user demand into workflow value, healthy-enough economics, accumulated context, and a thicker [[AIApplicationLayerMoat]].

## Key Characteristics
- Product founder motivated by decision authority and the ability to shape outcomes rather than by tenure inside one company.
- Operator whose career crossed consumer growth, B-side operations, education, video creation, and multimodal AI products.
- Advocate of fast entry and hourly learning when a technical window is compounding.
- Application-layer strategist who favors new users, new needs, and upstream workflow ownership over replaceable downstream features.
- Commercial realist willing to accept low positive margins while insisting that usage, payment, runway, and financing timing still matter.
- Creative-technology founder who expects extensive automation but treats frontier taste and new expression as a temporary human advantage.
- Self-critical manager who acknowledges gaps in technical depth, hiring, titles, feedback, and treatment of departing colleagues.

## Qualifications
Both sources are interviews centered on Chen's own account. Job scope, promotion, growth impact, financing structure, cash balances, revenue, margin, team size, product originality, and replacement forecasts are not independently verified here. The supplied January transcript alternates between “陈冕” and “陈勉”; this page retains the established canonical form “陈冕.” The January product-level cost claim and the later company cash-flow claim refer to different dates and scopes, so they are not treated as contradictory.
