# Chen Mian / 陈冕

Chen Mian is the founder of [[Evoken]], the company behind [[Liblib]], [[Lavod]], and [[LibTV]], as discussed in [[duihua-liblib-chenmian-guanyu-huoxialai-yiji-suoyou-jiejin-siwang-de-shike-1-175-1]]. The [[LateTalk]] source presents him as an unusually direct AI application founder trying to defend the company's growth, pricing, and originality while also admitting organizational weaknesses.

His source-scoped founder thesis is [[AIApplicationSurvivalStrategy]]: an independent application company has to move faster than model shifts, maintain enough cash flow, accept low positive margins when needed, and convert user scale into a thicker [[AIApplicationLayerMoat]]. At the same time, the interview shows a personal transition from strong individual drive toward more deliberate [[AIOrganizationDesign]].

## Key Points
- He says [[Evoken]] has been cash-flow positive since May 2026 and rejects the "about to explode" criticism.
- He argues that low gross margin can be a deliberate early application-layer strategy when model access is not proprietary and user scale is still the scarce asset.
- He admits that [[LibTV]] was not the first product to discover its market signal, but argues that first discovery and final market ownership are different problems.
- He describes model releases, [[DeepSeek]] demand acceleration, and [[Manus]]-style agent consensus as both opportunity and existential pressure.
- He identifies his own gaps around technical depth, management, hiring, title discipline, and how departing colleagues were treated.
- His deeper product bet is that AI should amplify human creativity rather than only replace human production.
