## Overview

陵昌 / 林昌 / Lin Chang is the young Qiang successor after Dianling in the early An-era Qiang war field. Hanji 786 backfills his succession and Langmo-assisted leadership before Hanji 789 develops the later Han campaign, household-targeting violence, and proxy assassination layers.

## Current Profile

Hanji 786 supplies the earliest current succession layer. After Dianling dies, his son Lingchang succeeds, Langmo becomes a key adviser, and [[DuJigongLateHan|Du Jigong]] remains attached to the Qiang-side command field. The source uses this succession to connect Hanyang-area rebellion to the wider Qiang war rather than treating Du's branch as isolated local disorder.

Hanji 789 says Dianling dies after the Qiang side pushes toward the Luoyang-facing route, and Lin Chang, still young, succeeds him with Langmo assisting. The host reads this succession as reducing immediate pressure on Luoyang because a new and young leader has to consolidate before continuing major advances.

The Han response attacks Lin Chang's field from several directions. [[SimaJunLateHan|Sima Jun]] and [[PangCanLateHan|Pang Can]] split forces, [[DengZunLateHan|Deng Zun]] uses Southern Xiongnu cavalry against Lin Chang at Linzhou, and [[RenShangLateHan|Ren Shang]] attacks the Beidi branch. Ren Shang kills Lin Chang's wife and children, burns his residence, and seizes livestock. Lin Chang is later assassinated by Hao Feng, who is rewarded with a Qiang royal title.

## Key Characteristics

- Young successor whose accession changes the Qiang campaign tempo after Dianling's death.
- Rendered as Lingchang or Lin Chang across bounded notes, with the route retained as `LinChangQiang`.
- Dependent on Langmo's assistance, suggesting a leadership field rather than settled one-man control.
- Target of Han split operations by Sima Jun, Pang Can, Deng Zun, and Ren Shang.
- Exposed to household-targeting coercion as Ren Shang attacks family, residence, and livestock.
- Removed by proxy assassination, turning leadership elimination into a frontier pacification tool.
