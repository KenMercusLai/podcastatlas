# Jacob Sims

## Overview
Jacob Sims is identified by the episode as an expert affiliated with Harvard's Asia Center who analyzes Cambodia's scam industry as a transnational organized-crime technology ecosystem.

## Current Profile
Sims argues that large compounds are only the visible layer of a system joining criminal syndicates, protected elites, operational expertise, stablecoins, laundering mixers, and cross-border relocation. His central warning is that site closures can disperse capacity without imposing consequences on leadership or dismantling the underlying infrastructure.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats scam compounds as parts of a broader technology and organized-crime ecosystem.
- Emphasizes financial tools, expertise, and leadership beyond physical sites.
- Warns that enforcement can displace operations geographically rather than end them.
