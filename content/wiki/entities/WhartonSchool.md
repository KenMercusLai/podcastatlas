# Wharton School

The Wharton School appears in [[tech-20260316-0316-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260316-0316-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the academic institution behind the [[CryptoConsumerConfidence|Consumer Cryptocurrency Confidence Index]]. The episode uses Wharton's monthly survey to ask whether consumer confidence in crypto can help explain or anticipate crypto-market moves.

In the wiki, Wharton anchors the research-method side of the crypto branch. The source does not provide survey-method details such as sample size or weighting, so the page records Wharton as the institutional context for the index rather than treating the index as a fully validated forecasting tool.

[[tech-20260408-0408-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260408-0408-mp-tech-pod-128]] adds an AI decision-making branch through [[SteveShaw]]. The Marketplace Tech episode links Wharton research to [[CognitiveSurrender]], where users defer to AI answers even when those answers are wrong, and to [[ArtificialCognition]], Shaw's "system three" frame for AI entering fast/slow human judgment.
