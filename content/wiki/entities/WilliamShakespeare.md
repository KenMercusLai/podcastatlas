# William Shakespeare / 威廉·莎士比亚

## Overview
William Shakespeare / 威廉·莎士比亚 appears in the wiki as a dramatist whose work reshapes literary interpretation, public memory, political analogy, English-language culture, and live theatre practice.

## Current Profile
The current evidence has moved Shakespeare from a passing literary-memory reference into a fuller cultural figure. Earlier sources used [[JuliusCaesarPlay|Julius Caesar]], sonnet interpretation, and a Macbeth analogy to show how Shakespearean writing can change historical feeling, seduce readers into theories, or clarify political-risk patterns. The new [[HuzuoHuyou|忽左忽右]] source makes Shakespeare himself the center: his plays are presented as rereadable, ambiguous, musically and rhetorically dense, grounded in the mixed audience of early-modern theatre, tied to [[GlobeTheatre|the Globe Theatre]], and influential through [[ShakespeareHistoryPlayPoliticalMemory]], global adaptation, and modern performance culture.

## Key Characteristics
- Shakespeare's work can become stronger than historical record in public memory, especially when scenes and phrases make politics emotionally memorable.
- His writing invites interpretation because ambiguity, wordplay, rhythm, allusion, and performance remain active across rereadings.
- His theatre belongs to a commercial and popular stage ecology as much as to high literary culture.
- His originality is presented as transformative recombination of inherited stories rather than pure plot invention.
- His influence travels through plays, sonnets, school reading, film, theatre training, idiom, and cross-cultural adaptation.

## Evidence
- Classical-memory evidence: [[124-kaisa-zhisi-lishi-de-yunjiao-shifou-yi-shixiao-781894361]] uses [[JuliusCaesarPlay]] to show Shakespeare turning uncertain ancient reports into memorable betrayal and republican-ideals scenes.
- Interpretive-seduction evidence: [[10-wang-erde-qiyi-gushiji-yong-zui-hui-sahuang-de-zuichun-qinwen-tianshi-545332195]] uses [[ThePortraitOfMrWH|《W.H.先生的画像》]] to show how a theory about Shakespeare's sonnets can become aesthetically convincing before evidence is secure.
- Political-analogy evidence: [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-19-maling-zhi-zhan-lhbapt09x2ptdqx0uopkp8apbada]] invokes Macbeth through Shakespeare as a comparison for [[CrownPrinceCommandRisk]].
- Literary-value evidence: [[495-lu-da-peng-tan-sha-shi-bi-ya-xi-ju-yu-ying-guo-li-shi-de-hu-xiang-ying-xiang-lthqtli5t6jsfn38mjtitzpoq3oo]] says Shakespeare remains central because his plays withstand rereading, preserve ambiguity, and combine story, structure, language, and performance.
- Theatre evidence: [[495-lu-da-peng-tan-sha-shi-bi-ya-xi-ju-yu-ying-guo-li-shi-de-hu-xiang-ying-xiang-lthqtli5t6jsfn38mjtitzpoq3oo]] grounds Shakespeare in the [[GlobeTheatre]], mixed audiences, acting, company management, shareholding, and live staging.
- History-play evidence: [[495-lu-da-peng-tan-sha-shi-bi-ya-xi-ju-yu-ying-guo-li-shi-de-hu-xiang-ying-xiang-lthqtli5t6jsfn38mjtitzpoq3oo]] develops [[ShakespeareHistoryPlayPoliticalMemory]] through [[HenryVPlay]] and [[RichardIIIPlay]].
