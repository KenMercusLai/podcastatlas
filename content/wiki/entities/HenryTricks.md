# Henry Tricks

## Overview

Henry Tricks is the technology editor appearing in the smart-glasses segment of [[trial-and-terror-russia-escalates-kyiv-campaign-6abe39c382177b9ab8a8904c]].

## Current Profile

Tricks uses his experience of being suspected of covert recording while wearing non-functioning [[RayBanSmartGlasses|Meta Ray-Ban smart glasses]] to show that face-worn cameras create social ambiguity even when they are inactive. He balances that privacy concern against continued interest in [[SnapSpecs|Snap Specs]] and the possibility that glasses, pins, pendants, and related hardware will become interfaces to AI agents.

## Key Characteristics

- Reports on consumer technology and smart-glasses adoption for The Economist.
- Treats bystander suspicion as a product constraint independent of actual recording.
- Distinguishes present phone-dependent eyewear from possible future independent devices.
- Remains interested in useful smart-glasses applications despite planning to return to ordinary glasses.

## Evidence

- **Social ambiguity:** [[trial-and-terror-russia-escalates-kyiv-campaign-6abe39c382177b9ab8a8904c]] recounts Tricks being accused of filming a performance while his glasses' recording functions were not working.
- **Privacy and misuse:** [[trial-and-terror-russia-escalates-kyiv-campaign-6abe39c382177b9ab8a8904c]] has him discuss covert harassment, disabled indicator lights, and Bluetooth-based detection tools.
- **Interface thesis:** [[trial-and-terror-russia-escalates-kyiv-campaign-6abe39c382177b9ab8a8904c]] attributes to Tricks the view that glasses will join a broader set of devices connecting people to AI agents.
