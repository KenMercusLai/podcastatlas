# LeEco / 乐视

[[170-1929-de-paomo-zhixia-sange-daibiao-renwu-he-tamen-zai-dangxia-zhouqi-de-yingzi-lo7uv7umozr0jrqfjzyhdbzicx1j]] adds LeEco through [[JiaYueting]] as a Chinese-context [[WilliamDurant]] analogy. The source reads LeEco not only as a failed stock story but as a case where real ambition, ecosystem narrative, capital-market optimism, and car-making escalation can outrun financing and execution.

LeEco / 乐视 is used in [[157-ruhe-daizou-niushi-de-shengli-guoshi-lory40ilowkjfe-lt-hiwjdsdbq2]] as a Chinese bull-market cautionary example. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] recalls the appeal of LeEco's ecosystem story during an earlier market cycle to show how a concept can contain some strategic logic while the actual company, financing, execution, and valuation fail to justify investor dreams.

In this wiki branch, LeEco belongs to [[BullMarketBezzleTrap]] and [[SpeculativeBubblePsychology]]. Its role is not to prove that every ambitious platform narrative is false, but to warn that investors must separate a seductive market label from company-level evidence such as cash flow, governance, financing resilience, and credible execution.
