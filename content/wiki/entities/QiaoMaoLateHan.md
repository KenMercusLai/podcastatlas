# 乔瑁 / Qiao Mao (late Han)

乔瑁 / Qiao Mao (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-945-zhinian-tai-shen-de-ren-zhihui-yuelaiyue-tongku-lm6xaiy2pcqunke04yzqwwxsxxl|Hanji 945]] as one of the outside commanders [[HeJin|何进]] pulls toward the capital crisis. He Jin calls Qiao Mao to garrison [[Chenggao|成皋]], placing him in the military pressure ring around [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] before the palace collapse.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-950-budong-quanli-beihou-de-renxing-bi-bai-wuyi-lu8-u78yqvvwwarsz8dlevvzchxr|Hanji 950]] later uses Qiao Mao as one of the commanders gathered with [[LiuDaiLateHan|刘岱]], [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]], [[ZhangChaoLateHan|张超]], [[YuanYiLateHan|袁遗]], [[BaoXin|鲍信]], and [[CaoCao|曹操]] at [[SuanzaoCamp|酸枣大营]]. The episode uses him as a coalition participant rather than an individually developed actor.

His source role is part of the crowded Suanzao picture: many names and posts create visible anti-Dong-Zhuo momentum, but the later coalition problem is whether those actors can convert gathering into common command.
