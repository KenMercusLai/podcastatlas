# Llama

Llama is the open model family named in [[featherless-ai-when-your-weekend-experiment-makes-more-than-your-startup]] as one of the mainstream alternatives users wanted [[FeatherlessAI|Featherless AI]] to serve. The episode contrasts demand for Llama and [[MistralAI|Mistral]] access with the company's earlier focus on [[RWKV]].

In this source, Llama matters less as a model-architecture topic than as evidence for [[CustomerPull]] inside [[OpenSourceAIModels]]. The weekend experiment showed that broad access to already popular model families could produce more immediate revenue than a technically ambitious fine-tuning product around a less demanded model.
