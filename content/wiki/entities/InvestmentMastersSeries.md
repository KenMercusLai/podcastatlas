# 投资大师系列 / Investment Masters Series

投资大师系列 is the paid podcast project introduced in [[134-touzi-dashi-xilie-xiandao-pian-tamen-bu-zhi-zhuan-le-henduo-qian-geng-chuangzao-le-lijie-shijie-de-fangfa-lqkjpfqc0ymgvnboek5jmjycwtyg]] by [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]], [[XiaoXiaoPao|小小跑]], and [[Yubai|雨白]]. The preview says the series builds on earlier episodes about [[RayDalio]] and [[JimSimons]], then adds figures such as [[HowardMarks]], [[PeterLynch]], [[StanleyDruckenmiller]], and [[PaulTudorJones]].

The project is framed as market history and method history, not investment advice. Its value is to show how famous investors created ways to understand cycles, uncertainty, companies, data, macro trades, and portfolio risk; its boundary is that no listener should treat those methods as directly copyable without matching capital, temperament, role, and time horizon.

## Key Claims
- The series is denser and more professionally prepared than an ordinary free conversation, which is one reason the speakers justify the paid format.
- It is intended for both outsiders and insiders: outsiders can follow biographies and stories, while insiders can compare market methods.
- The investor set is meant to function as an [[InvestmentStyleMap]], not a leaderboard.
- The project repeatedly warns that biography can mislead if it becomes [[InvestorIdolRisk]] or a search for simple secrets.
