## Overview

王昭君 / Wang Zhaojun enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-504-nanren-haose-zenmeban-kexi-guren-zhezhao-ni-xuebuhui-lsevoh__1vxdtzqhlzq6_an3xdq6|Hanji 504]] as the Han woman who, after [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]]'s death, marries [[FuzhuleiRuodiChanyu|复株累若鞮单于]] according to Xiongnu custom.

## Current Profile

The source uses Wang Zhaojun only at the end of the succession branch. Her remarriage marks how Han-Xiongnu marriage diplomacy and Xiongnu household custom continue across chanyu succession. The episode says she bears two daughters with the new chanyu, including Yun Princess.

## Key Characteristics

- Han woman located in the Xiongnu chanyu household.
- Linked in this source to remarriage under Xiongnu custom after Huhanye's death.
- Mother of two daughters by Fuzhulei Ruodi Chanyu in the episode's account.
- Frontier-marriage figure connected to Han-Xiongnu relations.
