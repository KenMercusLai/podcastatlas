## Overview

王昭君 / Wang Zhaojun enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-504-nanren-haose-zenmeban-kexi-guren-zhezhao-ni-xuebuhui-lsevoh__1vxdtzqhlzq6_an3xdq6|Hanji 504]] as the Han woman who, after [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]]'s death, marries [[FuzhuleiRuodiChanyu|复株累若鞮单于]] according to Xiongnu custom. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-494-ruiqi-fansi-riji-wo-fanguo-zuidade-cuo-shi-ta-ltsk3rul1e3hwxariqrdjwhetnoc|Hanji 494]] adds the prior Huhanye-side child notice: Wang Zhaojun bears a son named 伊屠智牙师, who is made You Rizhu King.

## Current Profile

The source set still uses Wang Zhaojun as a compact Han-Xiongnu marriage-diplomacy figure rather than a full biography. Hanji 494 places her inside Huhanye's household as Ninghu Yanzhi and mother of a son who receives the You Rizhu King title. Hanji 504 then continues the household sequence after Huhanye's death: her remarriage marks how Han-Xiongnu marriage diplomacy and Xiongnu household custom continue across chanyu succession. That later episode says she bears two daughters with the new chanyu, including Yun Princess.

## Key Characteristics

- Han woman located in the Xiongnu chanyu household.
- Ninghu Yanzhi in Hanji 494's Huhanye household notice.
- Mother of a son by Huhanye Chanyu, 伊屠智牙师, titled You Rizhu King in the episode's account.
- Linked in this source to remarriage under Xiongnu custom after Huhanye's death.
- Mother of two daughters by Fuzhulei Ruodi Chanyu in the episode's account.
