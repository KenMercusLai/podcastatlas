## Overview

王昭君 / Wang Zhaojun enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-504-nanren-haose-zenmeban-kexi-guren-zhezhao-ni-xuebuhui-lsevoh__1vxdtzqhlzq6_an3xdq6|Hanji 504]] as the Han woman who, after [[HuhanyeChanyuWesternHan|呼韩邪单于]]'s death, marries [[FuzhuleiRuodiChanyu|复株累若鞮单于]] according to Xiongnu custom. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-491-zhongguo-waijiao-zui-qiangying-de-shike-jing-shi-zai-xihan-lpgajp_vky9dx7jvnoahaxdfjflk|Hanji 491]] supplies the setup before her named household role: after [[ZhizhiChanyu|郅支单于]] is killed, Huhanye asks to attend Han court, and Yuan is expected to send him a beauty. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-492-fuhao-xihuan-shenme-nvren-qiaoqiao-wangzhaojun-ltxliplj2l-kvmey3imgklzgm1jt|Hanji 492]] fills in the departure story, presenting Wang Zhaojun as an overlooked palace woman who actively asks to marry Huhanye once she judges the diplomatic moment and her own palace prospects. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-494-ruiqi-fansi-riji-wo-fanguo-zuidade-cuo-shi-ta-ltsk3rul1e3hwxariqrdjwhetnoc|Hanji 494]] adds the Huhanye-side child notice: Wang Zhaojun bears a son named 伊屠智牙师, who is made You Rizhu King.

## Current Profile

The source set now gives Wang Zhaojun a fuller arc across four compact podcast notes. Hanji 491 introduces her as the coming beauty in Huhanye's post-Zhizhi court request, so her story begins as diplomatic aftermath. Hanji 492 then interprets her original departure as a constrained but active choice: she has entered the palace as a good-family woman, remains unseen under the image-painter selection system, refuses to bribe the painter in the episode's account, and asks to go to Xiongnu when Huhanye's marriage request creates a way out of palace stagnation.

The same source makes her beauty inseparable from judgment and presentation. The host treats the court-banquet scene as prepared social performance: Wang Zhaojun appears with beauty, bearing, speech, and calm intelligence, causing Yuan's regret but also making reversal politically impossible. Hanji 494 places her inside Huhanye's household as Ninghu Yanzhi and mother of a son who receives the You Rizhu King title. Hanji 504 then continues the household sequence after Huhanye's death: her remarriage marks how Han-Xiongnu marriage diplomacy and Xiongnu household custom continue across chanyu succession. That later episode says she bears two daughters with the new chanyu, including Yun Princess.

- Mother of a son by Huhanye Chanyu, 伊屠智牙师, titled You Rizhu King in the episode's account.
- Linked in this source set to remarriage under Xiongnu custom after Huhanye's death.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 491, Hanji 492, Hanji 494, and Hanji 504 and does not reconstruct Wang Zhaojun's full biography, literary reception, complete children list, or full role in Han-Xiongnu diplomacy. Hanji 492 preserves the image-painter and banquet-regret narrative as the episode's source tradition and interpretation, not as an independently adjudicated historiographical verdict. The host's beauty-as-resource reading is a modern interpretive frame layered over the historical story.
