# Food Junction

Food Junction appears in [[vol-270-da-shi-dai-liu-zai-le-ta-de-shi-dai-1006063835]] as the Singapore food-court company founded by [[GuoMingzhong|郭明忠]] in 1993 before the model was copied to Shanghai as [[FoodRepublic|大食代 / Food Republic]] in 1997. The source uses Food Junction to show the step between public [[HawkerCentre|hawker-centre]] infrastructure and a privately operated shopping-mall food court.

The episode's interpretation is that Food Junction and Food Republic share a commercialization logic: turn low-cost small-vendor dining into a repeatable managed space with design, tenancy control, shared seating, and centralized operations.
