# 文淇 / Wen Qi

文淇 is the host of [[ep254-you-xiaoying-x-wenqi-x-du-sujuan-nvxing-zhe-yisheng-dou-zai-fanfu-xuexi-xuke-ziji-lm-w7ryj4zay6vldsrkadohj997d]] and the actor associated with the "许可" perspective in [[WoXukeFilm|《我许可》]]. The episode frames her as a younger woman who can already self-reflect in public, but who still speaks honestly about hesitation, people-pleasing, and the uneven ability to say no.

Her stories add concrete material to [[SelfPermission]]: first menstruation without preparation, trying to get a role by saying "I can", not wanting to trouble a driver even when physically uncomfortable, gaining strength through Tiger Leaping Gorge hiking, and finding courage through online women's comments. That makes her page a bridge between [[MenstrualStigma]], [[SelfFeelingTrust]], [[FemaleBodyFreedom]], and [[FemaleFriendshipRecognition]].

[[144-guohuo-chuhai-xinqushi-shanxia-yousong-guanxia-pane-feng-tou-quan-1-144-1]] adds Wen Qi as a brand-collaboration figure for [[ShanxiaYousong]]. The episode reads her Nepal short film as a fit with the brand's literary, cool, stubborn, non-influencer image, turning her public persona into part of [[AspirationalEmotionalPremium]] and [[StoryLedConsumerBranding]].
