# 卫嗣君 / Wey Sijun

卫嗣君 / Wey Sijun appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-36-weisijun-de-diwang-zhishu-lt5cpdm9yqdj4erwykd0kubfpucz]] as the successor after [[WeyPinghou|卫平侯]] dies in [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]]'s forty-fourth year, 324 BCE. The episode frames him as a weak-state ruler whose political weight is shrinking while [[YingSi|秦惠文王]] claims kingly title.

His main story concerns a [[WeyState|卫国]] prisoner who escapes to [[WeiState|魏国]] and enters [[LiangHuiWang|魏惠王 / 梁惠王]]'s palace because of medical skill. Wey Sijun first sends envoys with fifty gold and repeats the request five times, then says he is willing to exchange Zuoshi/Left-shi city for the fugitive. Advisers object that a city is too much for one criminal, but Wey Sijun replies that governance has no small matters and disorder has no harmless scale.

The episode reads his choice through [[NoSmallMattersInGovernance|治无小乱无大]] rather than simple stubbornness. For Wey Sijun, the fugitive case tests whether a ruler can make law credible across borders; if law can be ignored in a small case, territory alone cannot preserve order. When his words reach Wei Hui Wang, the prisoner is returned without ransom or city transfer.
