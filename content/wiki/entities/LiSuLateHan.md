# 李肃 / Li Su (late Han)

李肃 / Li Su enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-963-shishang-bei-diantian-deng-de-diyiren-shi-ta-lkpkhlpkv3xpw-hur4b7sh-3druk|Hanji 963]] as the officer [[LyuBu|吕布]] places among disguised guards for the anti-[[DongZhuo|董卓]] ambush. He makes the first spear strike at the palace gate, but Dong Zhuo's armor prevents it from being fatal, leaving Lü Bu to finish the killing.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-965-bangguo-bieren-de-mang-weishenme-yao-shao-ti-ltjd-cv59hnm-n8ar9jxdogp9q0q|Hanji 965]] adds Li Su's post-assassination mission against [[NiuFuLateHan|牛辅]]. His mission fails: Niu Fu defeats him at Shan County, Li Su flees back toward Hongnong, and Lü Bu executes him.

The page is source-scoped because the episode uses Li Su primarily as a transition point. His failed attack shows that Dong Zhuo's remnant forces are still dangerous after the assassination, while his execution adds to the coercive and fearful atmosphere that makes later [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] decisions legible.
