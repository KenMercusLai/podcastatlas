# Good Friends / 好朋友们

## Overview
*Good Friends* / 《好朋友们》 is the Korean film case at the center of [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]]. The episode uses its friendship tragedy to ask whether familiar people are seen directly or through old fear, guilt, memory, and labels.

## Current Profile
The film is presented through three childhood friends whose relationship is fixed by an early snow-mountain accident and destroyed years later by a staged insurance robbery. Meng Yan focuses less on crime plotting than on Hyun-tae's belief that In-chul once stole a Walkman and abandoned the others. The later reveal that this belief was false makes the film a concrete case of [[InterpersonalImageProjection]]: the person judged as a betrayer may partly be a projection of the judge's own hidden actions and fears.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses a childhood friendship structure to make later betrayal, suspicion, and sacrifice emotionally legible.
- Turns a missing Walkman and snowstorm into a seventeen-year misrecognition rather than a simple mystery clue.
- Links insurance fraud, accidental death, suicide, and airport violence into a relationship-collapse plot.
- Serves E46 as the narrative case for "the other person I see may be made from myself."
- Keeps several final motives ambiguous, including what Hyun-tae knows or intends at the airport.

## Evidence
- Friendship setup: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] describes Hyun-tae, In-chul, and Min-soo as boys whose roles mix loyalty, dependency, protection, and small betrayals.
- Misrecognition evidence: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] says the film later shows the Walkman slipped into the snow rather than being stolen by In-chul.
- Projection reading: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] connects Hyun-tae's suspicion of In-chul to Hyun-tae's own moment of abandoning Min-soo and possibly to the airport ending.
- Ambiguity and collapse: [[e46-women-congwei-kanjian-bici-lmliksaiwxakmjbn-p2voaaoenxh]] preserves uncertainty around the final stabbing while treating the friends' silence and labels as already destructive.
