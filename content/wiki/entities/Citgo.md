# Citgo

## Overview
Citgo is the Venezuelan state-linked oil company that lets the wiki see [[Venezuela]]'s oil politics inside the [[UnitedStates]]. It appears first as an oil-diplomacy vehicle under [[HugoChavez]], then as the company whose executives became exposed to geopolitical detention risk under [[NicolasMaduro]].

## Current Profile
[[venezuelas-recent-economic-history-update]] presents Citgo as the company Chavez used to offer discounted heating oil to poor people in the United States, turning oil revenue into international symbolism and political outreach. [[trump-drinks-venezuelas-milkshake]] adds [[JoseAngelPereira|José Ángel Pereira]]'s later Citgo presidency and imprisonment after a 2017 trip to Caracas with other executives, making Citgo a bridge between corporate management and U.S.-Venezuela conflict.

The current synthesis is that Citgo is not only an overseas asset. It is a political interface: Venezuelan oil can buy influence, provoke U.S. attention, and expose managers to risks that standard investment analysis would miss.

## Key Characteristics
- Venezuelan state-linked oil company operating in the United States.
- Oil-diplomacy vehicle used by Chávez for discounted U.S. heating-oil outreach.
- Corporate bridge between Venezuelan resource politics and U.S. legal, political, and public opinion arenas.
- Executive-risk case through Pereira's arrest, imprisonment, and prisoner-swap release.
