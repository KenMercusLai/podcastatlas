## Overview

Domino's Pizza is a restaurant and delivery company that turned order-state data into the customer-facing [[DominosPizzaTracker|Pizza Tracker]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Domino's as a company whose service promise moved from a risky speed guarantee toward visibility and reassurance. Store computers already recorded operational events, and internal dashboards used those events to compare nearby stores; the same infrastructure then supported a consumer interface that made the order journey visible.

## Key Characteristics

- Its former 30-minute delivery guarantee combined speed with a firm boundary on uncertainty, but encouraged unsafe driving and was discontinued after deaths and lawsuits.
- Mid-2000s store systems captured order arrival, oven entry, and departure events that could be centralized.
- Competitive internal dashboards improved “out-the-door time” before the data was exposed to customers.
- The company converted operational telemetry into a widely imitated customer-experience pattern.

## Evidence

### Service-promise transition

- [[the-dominos-pizza-tracker-theory-of-everything]] links the end of the 30-minute guarantee to the search for a safer way to reduce delivery anxiety.

### Operational data foundation

- [[the-dominos-pizza-tracker-theory-of-everything]] describes store event capture and performance dashboards as the infrastructure behind the tracker.

### Customer-facing visibility

- [[the-dominos-pizza-tracker-theory-of-everything]] reports that Domino's launched the staged tracker around the Super Bowl and that the interface became a cultural and design reference point.

## Qualifications

- The episode is a historical and design account, not a comprehensive assessment of Domino's current operations.
- A field test found the tracker broadly accurate on major events but not a literal representation of every worker action or quality-control step.

## What Changed

- Added Domino's as the operating company behind a shift from delivery-time guarantee to progress visibility.
- Established the internal performance dashboard as the precursor to its consumer tracker.
