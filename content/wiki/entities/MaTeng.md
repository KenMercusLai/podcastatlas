# 马腾 / Ma Teng

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-938-lishi-de-jingxing-ruanruo-zhihui-dailai-quru-lnlnrketgfbadhfjbzdjjtrmqfsh|Hanji 938]] backfills Ma Teng's 187 CE origin before the Guanzhong branches. The source presents him as a Fufeng man, claimed descendant of Ma Yuan, mixed Qiang lineage through his mother, and low-resource soldier who rises through military merit under [[GengBiLateHan|耿鄙]]. After Geng Bi's command collapses, Ma Teng joins [[HanSui|韩遂]] and [[WangGuoLateHan|王国]], making his earliest wiki layer a frontier-governance failure and rebel-coalition case rather than a settled western warlord profile.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-973-changpingguan-zhizhan-shengsi-zhidi-zhanhou-jing-chonggui-yuhao-lgazc9dewfayb8qnkncfkfa12yet|Hanji 973]] supplies the more immediate 194 CE version of Ma Teng's conflict with [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]]. The source gives layered triggers: Li Jue refuses to lend western troops, a 《九州春秋》 branch adds a pasture seizure and killing tied to Li Jue's household network, and Ma Teng ignores [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s attempt at mediation.

Ma Teng then joins with [[HanSui|韩遂]] but loses at [[ChangpingguanBattleLateHan|长平观之战]] against [[FanChouLateHan|樊稠]], [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]], and [[LiLiLateHan|李利]]. The court later pardons him while demoting his title to 安狄将军, making the episode a failed intervention rather than a decisive western realignment.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]] supplies the setup before the later Fan Chou pursuit. Ma Teng clashes with [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] over grain and joins [[HanSui|韩遂]] under a "clear the ruler's side" banner. The host reads the practical target as [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] supply-route control rather than pure court rescue.

The same source adds [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s hidden support: five thousand Shu troops and Chang'an-side assistance through [[LiuFanLateHan|刘范]] and [[LiuDanLateHan|刘诞]]. Li Jue detects the plan, kills the Liu sons, and the failed intervention becomes part of the wider western instability that Hanji 984 continues.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-984-liangzhouxi-junfa-neidou-ta-weihe-di-yige-daoxia-lurb2qfzsa7e-owh-dl-m-sccze5|Hanji 984]] backfills an earlier military-contact branch. [[FanChouLateHan|樊稠]] attacks Ma Teng and [[HanSui|韩遂]], defeats them, and pursues them to [[ChenCang|陈仓]]. In this source Ma Teng functions as part of the western pressure that helps expose the fragility inside [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]]'s own [[LiangzhouWarlordClique|凉州系军阀]] camp.

马腾 / Ma Teng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] as one of the strongest military leaders in [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]]. [[XunYu|荀彧]] tells [[CaoCao|曹操]] that the western generals are numerous but fragmented, with Ma Teng and [[HanSui|韩遂]] the most important among them.

After [[ZhongYao|钟繇]] sends letters from [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] explaining the interests at stake, Ma Teng is grouped with Han Sui among those who accept the Han court's side and send sons to the court. The source treats this as rear stabilization: Cao Cao does not need Guanzhong permanently settled before acting elsewhere, but he does need it calm enough not to become Yuan Shao's western route.
