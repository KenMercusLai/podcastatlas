# 马腾 / Ma Teng

## Overview
马腾 / Ma Teng is a western late-Han military leader whose profile moves from frontier-rebel formation through Guanzhong conflict into conditional cooperation with Cao-side stabilization. The source sequence presents him as powerful enough to matter in every western calculation, but not as a fixed loyalist, a settled rebel, or an independent hegemon immune to persuasion.

## Current Profile
Hanji 938 backfills Ma Teng's 187 CE rise from a low-resource Fufeng soldier with claimed Ma Yuan descent and mixed Qiang lineage into the Liangzhou rebel-coalition field around [[HanSui|韩遂]] and [[WangGuoLateHan|王国]]. Hanji 973, Hanji 981, and Hanji 984 then place him in failed Guanzhong interventions and clashes with [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]]'s Liangzhou clique, including the [[ChangpingguanBattleLateHan|长平观之战]] and pursuit toward [[ChenCang|陈仓]].

Hanji 1015, Hanji 1056, and Hanji 1057 shift Ma Teng into Cao-side rear-stabilization. [[ZhongYao|钟繇]]'s letters and reassurance make Ma Teng less available to Yuan-family western pressure. Hanji 1056 sharpens the contingency: Ma Teng has secretly agreed with [[YuanShang|袁尚]]'s side, but [[FuGan|傅干]], as a subordinate in Ma Teng's circle, reframes the choice around court legitimacy, the danger of waiting between stronger powers, and the risk of joining a Yuan-Shang-[[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]] attack. Ma Teng then sends troops to Zhong Yao's side, and Hanji 1057 shows that cooperation becoming operational through [[PangDe|庞德]]'s role in defeating [[GuoYuanLateHan|郭援 / 郭元]].

## Key Characteristics
- Frontier-origin military entrepreneur shaped by Liangzhou governance collapse.
- Repeatedly paired with Han Sui, but the pairing is unstable and context-dependent.
- Major Guanzhong actor whose alignment can threaten or secure Cao Cao's western rear.
- Vulnerable to supply-road, pasture, and title/mediation conflicts with the Li Jue camp.
- Persuaded by interest and legitimacy framing rather than simply conquered.
- Hanji 1056 makes his cooperation contingent by showing an initial Yuan-side opening before Fu Gan redirects him.
- Hanji 1057 makes his cooperation operational by detaching him from Guo Yuan's Yuan-family pressure line.

## Evidence
- Origin and rebel-coalition formation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-938-lishi-de-jingxing-ruanruo-zhihui-dailai-quru-lnlnrketgfbadhfjbzdjjtrmqfsh|Hanji 938]] presents Ma Teng's Fufeng background, claimed Ma Yuan descent, Qiang maternal lineage, military rise under [[GengBiLateHan|耿鄙]], and later joining with Han Sui and Wang Guo.
- Failed Guanzhong intervention: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-973-changpingguan-zhizhan-shengsi-zhidi-zhanhou-jing-chonggui-yuhao-lgazc9dewfayb8qnkncfkfa12yet|Hanji 973]] supplies the 194 CE conflict with Li Jue, the ignored imperial mediation attempt, defeat at Changpingguan, and later pardon/title demotion.
- Grain-road and Liu Yan branch: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]] frames Ma Teng's Li Jue conflict around grain and Guanzhong supply-route control, with [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s hidden assistance collapsing after detection.
- Liangzhou-clique pressure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-984-liangzhouxi-junfa-neidou-ta-weihe-di-yige-daoxia-lurb2qfzsa7e-owh-dl-m-sccze5|Hanji 984]] shows [[FanChouLateHan|樊稠]] defeating Ma Teng and Han Sui and pursuing them to Chen Cang, later feeding internal suspicion inside Li Jue's camp.
- Persuaded stabilization role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] treats Ma Teng and Han Sui as the strongest western leaders whom Zhong Yao must persuade to keep Yuan Shao from gaining a western route.
- Yuan-side opening and Fu Gan redirection: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1056-yuanshao-sihou-tade-erzimen-zhijie-fanmu-chengchou-lqxhhayal-srq4fm4yy-hnfu15ye|Hanji 1056]] says Yuan Shang secretly contacts Ma Teng, but Fu Gan persuades Ma Teng to side with Zhong Yao and attack Guo Yuan instead.
- Operational cooperation after reassurance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1057-da-bu-liao-zhang-de-jiangjun-bushi-youxiu-de-shufajia-ljhy16leevjd8i-7vmuxtqm96ytg|Hanji 1057]] says Zhong Yao calms Ma Teng before defeating Guo Yuan and the Southern Xiongnu, with Pang De from Ma Teng's side killing Guo Yuan.

## Qualifications
Ma Teng's alignment is contingent across the sources. Earlier pages show revolt, failed intervention, and unstable coalition behavior; later pages show submission/cooperation under Zhong Yao's persuasion and Fu Gan's advice. Hanji 1056's references to Ma Chao's troop command and Zhang Ji's envoy role remain source-scoped because no canonical pages have yet been created for those distinct late-Han identities in this wiki. Hanji 1057's mention of Pang De's exact place in Ma Teng's line also remains source-scoped.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1056's Yuan-side opening: Ma Teng is contacted by Yuan Shang before Fu Gan redirects him.
- Clarified that Ma Teng's cooperation is contingent persuasion, not permanent default loyalty.
- Connected Fu Gan's argument to the later Hanji 1057 operational result against Guo Yuan.

## Relationships
- [[HanSui|韩遂]] - recurring western partner/rival in Liangzhou and Guanzhong military politics.
- [[WangGuoLateHan|王国]] - rebel-coalition counterpart in Ma Teng's early profile.
- [[GengBiLateHan|耿鄙]] - Liangzhou official under whose command Ma Teng first rises in Hanji 938.
- [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] - Guanzhong power whose grain, pasture, and command conflicts draw Ma Teng into failed intervention.
- [[FanChouLateHan|樊稠]] - Li Jue-side commander who defeats and pursues Ma Teng and Han Sui.
- [[ZhongYao|钟繇]] - Cao-side official whose persuasion and defensive campaign make Ma Teng's cooperation useful.
- [[FuGan|傅干]] - Ma Teng-side subordinate who reframes the Yuan-versus-Cao choice and persuades Ma Teng to support Zhong Yao.
- [[PangDe|庞德]] - Ma Teng-side officer whose killing of Guo Yuan makes the cooperation visible in battle.
- [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] - strategic region around Ma Teng's later bargaining and threat value.
- [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and [[YuanShang|袁尚]] - Yuan-family powers for whom Ma Teng could have become a western pressure component.
- [[GuoYuanLateHan|郭援 / 郭元]] - Yuan Shang-side commander whom Ma Teng's eventual choice helps oppose.
- [[InterestStructuredAllianceFragility|利益结构式联盟脆弱性]] - concept explaining why Ma Teng's coalitions and alignments are unstable.
