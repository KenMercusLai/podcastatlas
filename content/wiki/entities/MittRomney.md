# Mitt Romney

## Overview

Mitt Romney appears in this episode as the later Republican negotiating partner through whom [[TedKennedy]] received a second major opportunity to pursue broad health coverage.

## Current Profile

[[from-the-big-dig-mitt-ted]] introduces Romney mainly as a bridge from Kennedy's failed 1974 negotiations with [[RichardNixon]] to Massachusetts health reform. The source says their state-level collaboration produced a law that became an important template and inspiration for the [[AffordableCareAct]], but it reserves the detailed account of that partnership for later episodes in the season.

## Key Characteristics

- Republican political partner in Kennedy's later health-reform work.
- Associated with the Massachusetts coverage law that preceded the Affordable Care Act.
- Presented as an ideologically unlikely but potentially productive negotiating counterpart.

## Evidence

### Kennedy partnership and reform lineage

- [[from-the-big-dig-mitt-ted]] frames Romney as Kennedy's second chance after the lost Nixon negotiations and links their Massachusetts law to the later ACA.

## Qualifications

This opening episode does not detail Romney's policy design, negotiating choices, governorship, later presidential campaigns, or the exact provisions carried from Massachusetts into federal law. The relationship and lineage are therefore introductory and source-scoped.
