# George Santos

## Overview

George Santos appears as the satirical namesake of the episode's inaugural prize for “egregiously deceptive self-promotion.”

## Current Profile

The episode uses Santos's reported fabrications about ancestry, education, employment, personal history, and volleyball as a comic standard rather than as a complete political biography. His volleyball account supplies the episode's model of gratuitous deception because its claimed scholarship, college attendance, team history, opponents, athletic performance, and resulting surgery are presented as false while offering little obvious benefit in the interview where they were told.

## Key Characteristics

- Functions as the episode's comparison case for elaborate self-promotional falsehood.
- Supplies the “gratuitousness” criterion through a low-benefit volleyball story.
- Is used rhetorically rather than examined through a complete evidentiary or political record.
