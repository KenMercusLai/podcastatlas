# 冯亭 / Feng Ting

冯亭 / Feng Ting enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-1-ljshdryvf9qgabtn-siontthzsok]] as the [[HanState|韩国]] official responsible for the isolated [[Shangdang|上党]] crisis. After [[QinState|秦国]] cuts Shangdang's route back to Han, Feng Ting faces a choice between likely failed defense and surrender to Qin.

The episode presents his answer as political design under desperation. Feng Ting consults the local population and proposes giving Shangdang's seventeen cities, land, and people to [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. If Zhao accepts, Qin will be forced to attack Zhao, and Zhao will have reason to combine with Han against Qin.

Feng Ting's role is therefore not simple defection. He turns a lost Han frontier into a coercive invitation for Zhao, converting [[TransportCorridorSeverance|交通线切割]] into a wider interstate crisis and making Shangdang look like a gift while carrying Qin's retaliation inside it.
