# 臧霸 / Zang Ba (late Han)

## Overview
臧霸 / Zang Ba (late Han) enters the wiki through Hanji 1034 as a [[CaoCao|曹操]]-side commander used in the pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] deployment chain. The page is deliberately disambiguated from [[ZhangBaLateHan|张霸 / Zhang Ba (late Han)]], a different Taishan-area figure.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] says Cao Cao enters [[LiyangYellowRiverLateHan|黎阳]], then sends Zang Ba with elite troops into [[QingzhouLateHan|青州]] to secure the eastern side before returning to [[Xudu|许都]] and stationing troops at Guandu. Zang Ba's current wiki role is therefore operational: he anchors the Qingzhou security move that lets Cao prepare against Yuan Shao without leaving the eastern flank exposed.

## Key Characteristics
- Cao-side commander in the immediate pre-Guandu deployment sequence.
- Associated with elite troops sent to Qingzhou.
- Source-scoped eastern-flank security node rather than a complete biography.
- Disambiguated from Zhang Ba / 张霸 because the names are visually and phonetically close in English transliteration.

- Operational sequencing: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] places that move between Cao's Liyang advance and his return to Xudu and Guandu placement.

## Qualifications
The current page records only Hanji 1034's deployment function. It should not import Zang Ba's broader career without additional source notes in the frontmatter inventory.
