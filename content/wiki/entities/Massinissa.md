# Massinissa / 马西尼萨

## Overview

Massinissa / 马西尼萨 is the Roman-allied Numidian ruler whose repeated encroachments on [[Carthage]] helped turn its postwar dependence into the treaty breach that preceded the [[ThirdPunicWar]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Massinissa as more than a local antagonist. The 201 BC settlement prevented Carthage from making war without Roman permission, while Roman sympathy allowed its Numidian ally to press territorial claims with little effective restraint. Carthaginian embassies failed to secure relief, making the treaty asymmetrical in practice: Rome could deny Carthage self-defense while tolerating pressure from its ally.

In 151 BC Carthage finally attacked without permission. Massinissa defeated its army near Oroscopa and imposed humiliating terms, but the unauthorized war also gave Rome the formal breach it needed. Massinissa therefore acts as both territorial beneficiary and strategic trigger, although the episode does not prove that he and Rome coordinated a plan to manufacture the war.

## Key Characteristics

- Roman-allied Numidian ruler operating beside a legally constrained Carthage.
- Repeated territorial encroacher whose claims Rome generally favored.
- Victor over Carthage near Oroscopa in 151 BC.
- Immediate catalyst for the Carthaginian treaty breach used by Rome.
