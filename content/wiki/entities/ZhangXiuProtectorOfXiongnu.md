# 张脩 / Zhang Xiu (Protector of Xiongnu)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-917-ruhe-xunsu-zhaodao-shengzhi-jiaxin-de-pojudian-lk2zt5c3onvui9a4ppbowshy66tg|Hanji 917]] introduces 张脩 / Zhang Xiu as the 护匈奴中郎将 who clashes with [[HuzhengChanyu|呼征]], the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] chanyu. The source says Zhang Xiu kills Huzheng without authorization and installs the right wise king [[QiangquChanyu|羌渠]] as chanyu.

The Han court later sends Zhang Xiu to [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] for廷尉 judgment and executes him. The page is therefore a frontier-command authority case: a Han official can alter allied succession on the ground, but the court treats that move as punishable unauthorized intervention.

This page is deliberately disambiguated from [[ZhangXiu|张绣]], the Wancheng commander, and [[ZhangXiuFivePecks|张修]], the Five Pecks actor. The transcript's "张须" is normalized here to the historical 张脩 tied to the Huzheng-Qiangqu succession episode.
