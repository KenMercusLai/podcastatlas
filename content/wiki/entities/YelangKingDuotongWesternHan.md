## Overview

夜郎王多同 / Ye Lang King Duo Tong is the ruler whom [[TangMengWesternHan|唐蒙]] meets during the early Han Wudi attempt to open a southwestern route through [[YelangStateWesternHan|夜郎]]. He is distinct from the much later [[YelangKingXingWesternHan|夜郎王兴]].

## Current Profile

The episode introduces Duo Tong through the “夜郎孰与汉大” question, interpreting it as a product of geographic isolation and limited knowledge rather than evidence about Ye Lang's actual scale. His attitude changes after Tang Meng displays silk, art, precious metals, and other goods and describes Han power and generosity.

Tang Meng asks to establish officials and offers a place in the new arrangement to Duo Tong's son. Duo Tong does not decide alone: he consults the major chiefs, who also receive gifts. They accept the agreement while believing that difficult terrain and distance will prevent Han from fully possessing the region.

Duo Tong therefore enters the wiki as a participant in a conditional frontier bargain, not simply as a comic emblem of arrogance or as a ruler whose polity immediately loses all autonomy.

## Key Characteristics

- Ye Lang ruler encountered by Tang Meng during Han Wudi's early southwestern mission.
- Figure attached by the episode to the “Ye Lang or Han—which is larger?” question.
- Ruler whose response changes after material display and persuasion.
- Decision-maker who consults major chiefs rather than acting as an unconstrained sole authority.
- Participant in a bargain involving official appointment and a proposed office for his son.
- Local ruler whose acceptance remains conditioned by an expectation of continuing distance-based autonomy.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one retelling. It does not establish Duo Tong's reign dates, capital, genealogy, territorial reach, the precise title or office proposed for his son, or whether every saying and inner motive belongs to the underlying transmitted text. The “夜郎自大” moral label can obscure the rational collective calculation also described by the episode.
