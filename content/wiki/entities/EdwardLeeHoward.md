# Edward Lee Howard / 爱德华·李·霍华德

## Overview

Edward Lee Howard / 爱德华·李·霍华德 was a former [[CIA]] officer presented as having disclosed Moscow operations and the existence and technical domain of a high-value Soviet source to the [[KGB]].

## Current Profile

[[506-yiwan-meiyuan-jiandie-tuokaqiefu-yu-lengzhan-wanqi-meisu-qingbao-daan-lvqmgp0k-6r7o0whwbakbuct8kvn]] uses Howard to show that damaging insider access need not include a target's name. Training for a Moscow posting reportedly exposed him to source characteristics and surveillance-evasion methods; after dismissal, he approached the KGB and supplied enough context to focus the search that ended with [[AdolfTolkachev|Adolf Tolkachev]]'s arrest. He later escaped FBI surveillance and reached the Soviet Union, making his case both an intelligence-compartmentation and counterintelligence failure.

## Key Characteristics

- CIA trainee prepared for a Moscow assignment but dismissed before deployment.
- Retained operational knowledge acquired during training despite never taking the intended post.
- Presented as motivated by resentment, money, and a desire for renewed significance.
- Disclosed source descriptors and CIA Moscow tradecraft rather than only named identities.
- Evaded FBI surveillance and escaped through Europe to the Soviet Union.
