# John Elkann

John Elkann appears in [[ferrari-1]] as part of the post-Agnelli ownership and governance context around [[Fiat]], [[FiatChryslerAutomobiles|FCA]], [[Exor]], and [[Ferrari]]. The source places him with [[SergioMarchionne]] in the period when Fiat stabilized, combined with Chrysler, and separated Ferrari.

His role is source-scoped as governance context rather than the episode's main character. He helps explain why Ferrari's public-company structure still retains concentrated voting influence.
