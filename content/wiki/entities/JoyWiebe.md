# Joy "Joey" Wiebe / Joey

Joy "Joey" Wiebe / Joey is the ice-road trucker at the center of [[84-binglu-kuanghua-hao-guniang-ni-zhishi-wangle-yonggan-699443676]]. The episode presents her through [[Mothertrucker|《冰路狂花》 / Mothertrucker]] as "卡车妈妈": a woman whose [[Instagram]] images of [[Alaska]], snow, trucks, and road life made [[AmyButcher|Amy Butcher / 艾米]] imagine a life of fearlessness and freedom.

The source complicates that image. Joey's authority comes from real competence on the [[DaltonHighway|Dalton Highway]], including road knowledge, weather judgment, supplies, radio warnings, and a habit of carrying extra food and blankets for people in trouble. It also comes from history: marriage pressure, domestic violence, alcohol-related danger, and the painful recognition that trying to save a man through love can destroy a woman's life.

Joey becomes a witness rather than a perfect heroine. She can be religious, politically different from Amy, blunt, funny, wounded, generous, and sometimes angry. Her death after the trip turns the book into an act of memory work as well as self-rescue.
