# 许生 / Xu Sheng (late-Han rebel)

许生 / Xu Sheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-905-fugui-ruhe-xianzhong-qiu-lklxx5nquka2mtbllxivk0a7b-fu|Hanji 905]] as the rebel described by the episode as a "妖贼" in the 174 CE Yangzhou-Danyang suppression campaign. The source does not develop Xu Sheng's organization or program; his wiki role is to mark the local disorder that gives [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] an early opportunity to recruit more than one thousand fighters and become visible to local authority.

The page is source-scoped to avoid overbuilding from a brief notice. Xu Sheng matters here less as a biography than as the crisis setting for [[CrisisOpportunityConversion|危机机会转化]] and Sun Jian's first armed-reputation branch.
