# Haitian Flavoring / 海天味业

## Overview
Haitian Flavoring / 海天味业 appears in the source as a Chinese condiment leader acquiring [[AmoyFood|淘大]] to accelerate overseas expansion.

## Current Profile
The episode frames Haitian's Amoy acquisition as an outbound capability purchase. Haitian contributes R&D, supply chain, and channels, while Amoy contributes brand history, overseas distribution, and local operating experience.

## Key Characteristics
- Chinese condiment leader in the source's account.
- Uses acquisition rather than only organic export to support overseas growth.
- Seeks overseas revenue expansion while facing integration and brand-positioning questions.
- Source-scoped case for [[CondimentOutboundAcquisition]].

## Evidence
- Transaction evidence - [[tulasi-zishengtang-qisu-pingjia-shangchao-aoleqi-alo-qianyue-wangyibo-1014854463]] says Haitian completed its acquisition of Amoy.
- Capability evidence - [[tulasi-zishengtang-qisu-pingjia-shangchao-aoleqi-alo-qianyue-wangyibo-1014854463]] says Haitian will open R&D, supply-chain, and channel resources to Amoy while Amoy contributes overseas brand assets and local operation experience.
- Strategic target - [[tulasi-zishengtang-qisu-pingjia-shangchao-aoleqi-alo-qianyue-wangyibo-1014854463]] says Haitian's Hong Kong prospectus set a goal of lifting overseas revenue share to 15% within three years.

## Qualifications
The source does not prove integration success. Brand division, team coordination, supply-chain synergy, and overseas channel execution remain open.

## What Changed
- Created Haitian as the acquirer in a condiment-globalization case.
