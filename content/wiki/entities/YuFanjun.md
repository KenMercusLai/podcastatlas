# 余樊君 / Yu Fanjun

余樊君 / Yu Fanjun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-2-lishi-jilu-shouci-tucheng-lm4kuzdpnqn-hicyuvvb4p2wdudm|Qinji 130-2]] as a subordinate commander used by [[XiangLiang|项梁]] after Xiang Liang absorbs multiple anti-[[QinState|秦国]] forces.

The episode says Xiang Liang sends Yu Fanjun and [[ZhuJishi|朱鸡石 / 朱吉石]] to fight [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] near Li County. Zhu Jishi is defeated and flees to Huling, while Yu Fanjun is killed on the battlefield.

Yu Fanjun's wiki role is small but structurally useful. His death marks that Xiang Liang's rapidly enlarged coalition is immediately being tested by Zhang Han's Qin counteroffensive, and that absorbed or subordinate units may be used before Xiang Liang commits his core strength.
