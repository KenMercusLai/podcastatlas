## Overview

赵过 / Zhao Guo enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-389-2-lishi-jiemi-shui-shi-xie-jiantaoshu-zuiduo-de-wang-lvnamm5dza3s9ao8ofcshhiynsu1|Hanji 389-2]] as the Western Han agricultural official whom [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] appoints as sousu duwei after the [[LuntaiSelfCriticismPolicyTurn|Luntai self-criticism policy turn]]. The episode uses him as evidence that Han Wudi's late court began valuing practical livelihood talent after decades of war and extraction.

## Current Profile

The source presents Zhao Guo as a farming specialist rather than a court faction actor. He understands cultivation, daitian-style methods, land-use technique, and toolmaking, then teaches those methods widely so common people can use less labor for better harvests.

In the episode's synthesis, Zhao Guo is paired with [[SimaQian|司马迁]]'s comment that the world is not short of talent; a ruler's interests decide which talent is discovered and used. When Han Wudi wanted conquest, bold military figures appeared. When he turned toward recovery, Zhao Guo-like agricultural talent became visible and useful.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han agricultural official appointed as sousu duwei in the source's post-Luntai recovery branch.
- Associated with practical cultivation technique, daitian methods, and farming tools.
- Teaches methods to common people, improving labor efficiency and harvest.
- Functions as an example of livelihood talent becoming valuable after a ruler's policy preference changes.
- Serves the episode's recovery argument rather than an independent full biography.
