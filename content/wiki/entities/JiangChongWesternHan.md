## Current Profile

The source does not give Jiang Chong a full biography. Its narrow claim is that he helped frame Liu Ju, while the host argues that Jiang Chong was not the true ultimate mover: [[LadyGouyiWesternHan|钩弋夫人]] is presented as a hidden figure behind him. Jiang Chong therefore functions here as the visible operational actor in a source-scoped explanation of why [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] later kills Lady Gouyi.
