# Isaac Adamson

## Overview

Isaac Adamson is the screenwriter represented here through [[BubblesFilm|Bubbles]], his unproduced stop-motion screenplay about [[MichaelJackson]] narrated by [[BubblesChimpanzee|Bubbles the chimpanzee]].

## Current Profile

Adamson turned an apparently comic celebrity premise into a protagonist-centered tragedy about hierarchy, abandonment, loyalty, and denial. His imagined Bubbles treats Jackson as a literal king and himself as heir, using pseudo-Shakespearean language and mistaken interpretation to preserve pride and innocence while the audience recognizes darker meanings.

The project also places Adamson inside a changing development environment. After *Leaving Neverland*, he and the producers revised Bubbles toward clearer recognition and rejection of alleged abuse, even though he believed the change weakened the unreliable narration. He describes the eventual production collapse as a conflict between creative interest and the reputational, marketing, and financial fear attached to the subject.

## Key Characteristics

- Screenwriter who makes a celebrity chimpanzee the emotional and dramatic protagonist.
- Uses hierarchy, succession, elevated language, and misunderstanding to join comedy to tragedy.
- Treats research and reported history as material requiring legal annotation and selective fictionalization.
- Revised the screenplay when public interpretation of Jackson changed after *Leaving Neverland*.
- Recognizes a tradeoff between moral clarity and the pathos of sustained unreliable narration.
