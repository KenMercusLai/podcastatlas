# 雍丘 / Yongqiu (Late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-989-ningwei-taipingquan-buzuo-luanshi-ren-lkoycygmjnltuvpq-6wjv-ls61s6|Hanji 989]] adds Yongqiu's earlier Cao Cao-Zhang Miao branch. While [[CaoCao|曹操]] surrounds the city, [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] tries to leave for [[YuanShu|袁术]], but [[LiuYiLateHan|刘翊]] kills him before the move can turn into a new anti-Cao alignment.

雍丘 / Yongqiu (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-996-luanshi-zanghong-jiangyiqi-de-ren-xiachang-zui-biequ-lgpa-m6h6bvhyintnuj6zztopui76|Hanji 996]] as the fortified place where [[ZhangChaoLateHan|张超]] is trapped by [[CaoCao|曹操]]. Zhang Chao expects [[ZangHong|臧洪]] to rescue him because of their old superior-subordinate bond, but Zang Hong cannot move [[YuanShao|袁绍]] to send troops.

The fall of Yongqiu is the trigger for the later [[DongCommandery|东郡]] rebellion branch. Once Cao Cao captures the city and Zhang Chao's family is destroyed, Zang Hong treats Yuan Shao's refusal as an unforgivable failure of old-friend and old-superior obligation.
