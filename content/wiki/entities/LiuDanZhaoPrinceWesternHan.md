## Overview

赵太子刘丹 / Liu Dan, Zhao Crown Prince enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-382-1-bazhan-shumu-hanwudi-zhizi-liudan-you-duo-huangtang-loi-6-s1z2okmskqvbfwryz-qjto|Hanji 382-1]] as the heir of [[LiuPengzuZhaoKingWesternHan|赵敬肃王刘彭祖]] and nephew of [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]. The episode makes him important because his misconduct and attempted killing of [[JiangChongWesternHan|江充]] give Jiang Chong the accusation route that brings him into Wudi's favor before the later [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Liu Dan as a royal heir whose private disorder becomes a central-court succession problem. Jiang Chong, then Jiang Qi, is connected to Liu Dan through marriage because Jiang's sister is married to Liu Dan. Liu Dan suspects Jiang Qi of speaking against him to Liu Pengzu and knowing too much about his household conduct, so he tries to have Jiang killed.

After Jiang flees to Chang'an and changes his name, he accuses Liu Dan of incest and adultery with his father's consorts. Han Wudi treats the charge as proof that Liu Dan cannot inherit Zhao, rejects Liu Pengzu's counterclaim that Jiang is an unreliable fugitive, and initially wants Liu Dan executed. Liu Pengzu's plea and offer to serve on the frontier save Liu Dan from death. Hanji 237 part 2 adds that Liu Dan is later pardoned; the received sequence does not show restoration, and a later request to make him crown prince again is refused.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Zhao heir and Han Wudi's nephew in the episode's royal-kin frame.
- Married to Jiang Chong/Jiang Qi's sister, making Jiang initially part of the Zhao palace network.
- Accused of sexual misconduct with daughters, sisters, and Liu Pengzu's consorts.
- Attempts to kill Jiang Qi after suspecting him of reporting or knowing damaging information.
- Loses heir legitimacy when Jiang Chong reports him to Han Wudi.
- Spared death through Liu Pengzu's plea and later pardoned, without demonstrated restoration as heir.
- Functions mainly as the scandal source that launches Jiang Chong's central-court career.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to two compact episodes. It does not reconstruct Liu Dan's full genealogy, the full legal case, the exact court sentence, the primary-source wording for each sexual accusation, or his life after release. Pardon means release from punishment here, not proven restoration to the Zhao succession; the received account explicitly records a later failed restoration request. The sources' titles and transcripts use popular-history framing, so the scandal details remain source-scoped unless checked against primary histories.

## What Changed

- Created from Hanji 382-1 as the Zhao heir whose scandal and attempted killing of Jiang Chong launch Jiang Chong's Han Wudi-side career.
- Distinguished eventual pardon from restoration to the Zhao heirship.
