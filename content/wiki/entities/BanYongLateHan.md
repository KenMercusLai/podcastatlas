## Overview

班勇 / Ban Yong appears in this wiki as the Eastern Han Western Regions commander whose successful frontier reconstruction is followed by a damaging court-information failure. Hanji 803 now adds a 125 CE campaign against [[CheshiHouKingdomLateHan|车师后王国]] before Hanji 807 presents him as Ban Chao's son and the main operator behind renewed Han alignment in Cheshi and the wider Western Regions.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-803-han-shundi-ruhe-zai-xuefengxueyu-zhong-dengshang-huangwei-lqvno7t-gcgxtbs3phuflr4ybe2s|Hanji 803]] supplies Ban Yong's earlier 125 CE campaign layer. As Western Regions chief clerk, he mobilizes cavalry and allied forces from Dunhuang, Zhangye, Jiuquan, Shule, Shanshan, and Cheshi Qian against Cheshi Hou. The source says the campaign kills thousands, captures King Junjiu and a [[Xiongnu|Xiongnu]] envoy, and executes them at Suo Ban's death site before sending the heads to [[LuoyangLateHan|Luoyang]].

In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-807-banchao-zhizi-weihe-hui-hanyuan-ruyu-liiwgi77g2on1z2vnwaf1-wvph2d|Hanji 807]], Ban Yong then rebuilds Han leverage through local kingship decisions. He installs Jiatenu as king of [[CheshiHouKingdomLateHan|车师后王国]], replaces another local ruler with a same-clan alternative, and helps bring Cheshi and five other Western Regions polities back under Han alignment.

His anti-[[Xiongnu|Northern Xiongnu]] operation is both military and political. Ban Yong organizes Western Regions troops, receives a large surrender after the Huyan king flees, captures a Xiongnu royal kinsman, and has Jiatenu execute him personally. The source reads that move as a way to make Cheshi's reconciliation with Northern Xiongnu harder and therefore bind the dependent kingdom more tightly to Han.

The title case turns that competence into vulnerability. Ban Yong asks to attack [[YanqiKingdomLateHan|Yanqi]], coordinates with [[ZhangLangLateHan|Zhang Lang]] for a two-route meeting date, and mobilizes more than 40,000 Western Regions troops. Zhang Lang, trying to offset his own prior offense, advances early, attacks first, accepts Yanqi's surrender, and returns. The court then receives Ban Yong's non-arrival as lateness, recalls him to [[LuoyangLateHan|Luoyang]], imprisons him, and removes him from office.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Regions operator whose authority works through both Han command and local royal appointment.
- Campaign commander whose 125 CE victory against Cheshi Hou combines Han commandery cavalry and allied Western Regions forces.
- Uses dependent-polity commitment as strategy, especially by making Cheshi materially participate against Northern Xiongnu.
- Coordinates larger multi-force plans rather than only single-column attack.
- Vulnerable to credit and blame distortion when a cooperating commander changes timing.
- Presented by the source as wronged because the reported failure hides Zhang Lang's premature action.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 807 and Hanji 803. It does not attempt a full Ban-family biography or a full reconstruction of Eastern Han Western Regions administration. Hanji 803 supplies the 125 CE Cheshi Hou campaign but leaves Suo Ban, King Junjiu, Shanshan, commandery troop details, and the exact official report path source-scoped. Hanji 807 supplies Jiatenu, the Huyan king, Gudu Hou, Cao Jun, Yanqi king-name form, troop numbers, and court-reporting distortion, which remain source-scoped until additional notes require firmer canonical modeling.
