# Bombardier / 庞巴迪

Bombardier is the Canadian railway supplier in [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]] that won part of the first large Chinese EMU order through a local joint-venture structure. The episode links Bombardier technology to [[Hexiehao]] CRH1.

Its source role is comparative. Alongside [[KawasakiHeavyIndustries]], [[Alstom]], and [[Siemens]], Bombardier helps show how China used [[GovernmentEnterpriseProcurement]] to force foreign firms into a bargaining structure where technology access, price, local assembly, and Chinese branding were connected.
