# 西宋风云

《西宋风云》 is the later AI work mentioned in [[cong-yangshi-jilupian-dao-baokuan-ai-duanju-di-yi-pi-zhuanshen-de-daoyan-s10e11-3c05e3d5-d8f6-44c1-97ca-698261d7b2bc]] when [[Chouxiangzai]] discusses whether AI performance lacks "human feeling." He says a grief scene resembling Lin Chong seeing his wife harmed produced many different forms of sadness after repeated generation, making the result depend heavily on director selection.

The work is important to the wiki because it locates [[LiveHumanFeeling]] inside [[AIVideoProductionWorkflow]] rather than outside it. The source does not claim AI fully replaces strong actors; instead it argues that short-drama-style emotion can be generated and selected effectively, while restrained film-grade feeling remains harder and may still favor live performers.

## Key Points
- It is used as a performance example rather than a business case.
- The source frames AI performance as director-dependent: repeated draws create options, but the human chooses which sadness works.
- It reinforces the distinction between short-drama emotional intensity and film-grade restrained performance.
