# 袁遗 / Yuan Yi (late Han)

袁遗 / Yuan Yi (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-969-yuanshi-xiongdi-neidou-shui-neng-jianlou-lsgj6sewo35jpsgfm-koivijtr6r|Hanji 969]] as the [[YuanShao|袁绍]]-side appointee in the early fight over [[YangzhouLateHan|扬州]]. After [[ChenWenLateHan|陈温]] dies, Yuan Shao sends Yuan Yi to lead Yangzhou, but [[YuanShu|袁术]] defeats him; Yuan Yi flees toward Pei and is killed by soldiers.

The page keeps Yuan Yi narrow because the episode does not develop a full biography. His role is structural: he shows that in late-Han provincial politics, an appointment can be real enough to matter and still fail immediately if the appointee cannot convert title into protection, troops, and local control.
