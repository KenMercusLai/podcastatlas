# USC Stevens School for Computing and Artificial Intelligence

USC Stevens School for Computing and Artificial Intelligence is the new [[UniversityOfSouthernCalifornia|USC]] school discussed in [[tech-20260730-0730-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260730-0730-mp-tech-pod-128]]. The episode frames it as part of a $200 million university bet on embedding AI across campus.

The school is launching a new AI major and minors for non-STEM students who want to apply AI in fields such as history and art. [[GauravSukhatme]] describes its curriculum as project-driven and quickly refreshable, while the episode keeps ethics central to the school rather than treating AI as only a technical skill stack.
