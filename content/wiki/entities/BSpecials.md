# B Specials

The B Specials appear in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] as the auxiliary police force that civil-rights activists wanted abolished. The episode says off-duty B Specials were among loyalists who attacked [[PeoplesDemocracyNorthernIreland|People's Democracy]] marchers at [[BurntolletBridgeAttack|Burntollet]] while [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]] officers stood by.

The Hunt Report's recommendation that the B Specials be disbanded became a loyalist flashpoint, sparking rioting on the Shankill Road. For the wiki, the B Specials are part of [[StatePolicingLegitimacyCrisis]] because their existence and conduct made policing appear sectarian to many Catholics.
