# B Specials

The B Specials appear in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as the overwhelmingly Protestant auxiliary police force within [[NorthernIreland]]'s security structure. The episode says almost every B Special belonged to an [[OrangeOrder|Orange lodge]], making the force a visible part of [[InstitutionalSectarianDiscrimination]] for many Catholics.

In [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]], civil-rights activists demand their abolition, and the episode says off-duty B Specials were among loyalists who attacked [[PeoplesDemocracyNorthernIreland|People's Democracy]] marchers at [[BurntolletBridgeAttack|Burntollet]] while [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]] officers stood by. The Hunt Report's recommendation that the B Specials be disbanded became a loyalist flashpoint, sparking rioting on the Shankill Road.
