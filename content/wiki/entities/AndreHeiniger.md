# Andre Heiniger

Andre Heiniger is the postwar Rolex executive discussed in [[rolex-1]] as the leader who helped move [[Rolex]] from technical proof toward modern luxury identity. The source says he joined after World War II, led South America, became director of marketing, and later became CEO.

Heiniger's source role is tied to Rolex's professional-watch and lifestyle-advertising turn. Product lines such as the [[RolexSubmariner]], [[RolexGMTMaster]], and [[RolexDaytona]] made technical use cases commercially legible, while advertising increasingly emphasized achievement and status.
