## Overview

Emperor Cheng of Han / 汉成帝 enters this wiki branch through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-654-jiemi-xihan-yangxiong-de-chuanqi-rensheng-lq3o-13h2in89qwh0dvchczlvrqk|Hanji 654]] as the Western Han ruler who summons [[YangXiong|扬雄]] after Yang Xiong's literary reputation becomes visible.

## Current Profile

The source's use of Emperor Cheng is narrow. It says Yang Xiong receives a summons in the Chengdi period and serves as a palace gentleman, but despite passing through several reigns his career does not rise much.

Emperor Cheng therefore functions as part of Yang Xiong's official-life background. The episode's main point is not Chengdi's rule, but the contrast between literary notice and limited promotion.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han emperor in the source's Yang Xiong career frame.
- Summons Yang Xiong after his literary ability becomes visible.
- Marks the beginning of Yang Xiong's court-service layer in this episode.
- Serves as background for the contrast between cultural talent and limited official advancement.
