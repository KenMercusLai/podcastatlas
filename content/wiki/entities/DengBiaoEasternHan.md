## Overview

邓彪 / Deng Biao enters the wiki as the formal Taifu figure used to give [[DouXianEasternHan|Dou Xian]] a court-procedure channel after [[EmperorHeOfHan|Emperor He]]'s child accession. Hanji 759 presents him less as an independent check than as a prestigious shell through which Dou's initiatives can pass into Shangshu writing and [[EmpressDowagerDouHeLateHan|Empress Dowager Dou]]'s approval.

## Current Profile

Hanji 759 places Deng Biao in the accession aftermath. The court needs a formal senior figure around the young emperor, and [[CuiYinEasternHan|Cui Yin]] recommends Deng as Taifu. The episode emphasizes the role's procedural value: Deng writes, submits, and handles memorial machinery, while Dou Xian reports to the dowager.

That arrangement matters because it turns a private outer-relative powerholder into someone with a usable administrative interface. Deng's page therefore tracks a narrow but important mechanism: a respectable office can become a relay for another actor's power when the actual initiative and risk are held elsewhere.

## Key Characteristics

- Eastern Han Taifu figure introduced in the child-emperor accession layer.
- Recommended by Cui Yin as part of Dou Xian's governing arrangement.
- Provides writing, memorial, and Shangshu-facing procedure for Dou Xian.
- Functions as a formal shell rather than the source's main decision maker.
- Helps show how outer-relative power becomes administrative rather than merely familial.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 759 and does not reconstruct Deng Biao's full official career. The source presents him mainly through function in Dou Xian's arrangement, so the page avoids treating him as either a full co-regent or a proven resistor.
