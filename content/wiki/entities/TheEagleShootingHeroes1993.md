## Overview

《东成西就》 / *The Eagle Shooting Heroes* (1993) is a [[JeffreyLau|刘镇伟]]-directed Hong Kong ensemble comedy that repurposes names and motifs from [[JinYong|金庸]]'s Condor Heroes world.

## Current Profile

The bounded source treats the film as a production-rescue legend and an extreme adaptation case. Its reported use of the 《东邪西毒》 cast and resources, short schedule, loose writing, bodily exaggeration, contemporary references, and actor contribution locate its achievement in comic energy and ensemble execution rather than fidelity to [[LegendOfTheCondorHeroes|《射雕英雄传》]].

Its critical value is double. As comedy, it is remembered for escalating physical routines, live-action-manga deformation, character-specific performances, and a director able to keep stars within a shared tonal field. As adaptation, it largely abandons source plot and relationships; its success therefore demonstrates that autonomous artistic value and [[FunctionalAdaptationFidelity|functional fidelity]] must be judged separately.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses the young Five Greats and gaps in their backstories as a permissive frame for an almost wholly new comic plot.
- Builds humor through bodies, props, repetition, escalation, misunderstanding, anachronism, and broken wuxia logic.
- Depends on ensemble performance to give specificity to a loose, rapidly assembled narrative.
- Exemplifies the speed, shared resources, star system, and practical flexibility associated with the period's [[HongKongFilmIndustry|Hong Kong film industry]].
- Succeeds as an autonomous comedy while remaining only weakly faithful to its Jin Yong source world.
