# 红盛系 / Hongsheng System

## Overview
红盛系 / Hongsheng System is the family-linked operating network around [[WahahaGroup|娃哈哈]] that current sources associate with production, supply chain, channel control, and succession opacity.

## Current Profile
The wiki evidence treats Hongsheng less as one simple entity than as a relationship system. The 大小电波 source adds that [[ZongFuli|宗馥莉]] took over Hongsheng Beverage Group before the formal Wahaha succession stage, making it part of her operational credibility. The 不熄灯 source emphasizes the later controversy: when affiliates, distributors, trademarks, state/employee interests, and family authority are not legible to the public, Hongsheng becomes a concrete example of [[FamilyBusinessSuccessionOpacity]].

## Key Characteristics
- Family-linked production and supply-chain network associated with Wahaha.
- Important to Zong Fuli's operating background before full group succession.
- Makes the gap between consumer-facing brand unity and legal/operating control visible.
- Can be interpreted differently by insiders, employees, state-linked shareholders, distributors, and the public.

## Evidence
- Successor operating base: [[fengwei-chihe-wahaha-zongqinghou-de-sanci-duiju-gkwrijiouqosa60omatfv8yx]] says Zong Fuli took over Hongsheng Beverage Group in 2007 and built operating experience there.
- Affiliate complexity: [[bu-xi-deng-e02-bi-quan-shan-beng-mei-guo-zheng-fu-guan-men-first-brands-po-chan-wa-ha-ha-feng-bo-lt-ghzgmiroo9a-odaih4xkhsulu]] associates Hongsheng relationships with supply-chain, manufacturing, distributor control, and trademark controversy.
- Succession stakes: [[bu-xi-deng-e02-bi-quan-shan-beng-mei-guo-zheng-fu-guan-men-first-brands-po-chan-wa-ha-ha-feng-bo-lt-ghzgmiroo9a-odaih4xkhsulu]] treats Hongsheng as one reason Wahaha succession cannot be reduced to ordinary family inheritance.

## Qualifications
The exact corporate map, ownership percentages, contracts, and asset transfers are not fully established by the current source notes. The page should preserve Hongsheng as a network label until stronger documentary evidence distinguishes its entities.
