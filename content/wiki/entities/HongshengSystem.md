The source does not treat it as a single simple legal entity; it treats it as a relationship system that complicates where the brand, operating assets, and family interests actually sit.

For the wiki, 红盛系 matters because it makes [[FamilyBusinessSuccessionOpacity]] concrete. A consumer brand can look unified to the public while its factories, distributors, trademarks, and legal rights are spread across affiliated or historically accumulated structures.
