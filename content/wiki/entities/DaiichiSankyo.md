# Daiichi Sankyo / 第一三共

Daiichi Sankyo appears in [[vol-120-riben-yiliao-tixi-de-bengkui-yu-zhongsheng-yiyuan-yaopin-yibao-sanjiao-kunju-de-yizhong-jiefa-lp-p5p1cybbldphe0qajteggsitt]] as one of the Japanese pharma companies used to show that Japan retained globally meaningful pharmaceutical capability after the crisis and reform period.

In the source, the company is less a standalone case than a marker for [[JapaneseDrugPricingReform]]. Japan's later approach tries to preserve affordable public insurance while leaving enough reward for quality, added value, and innovation that firms such as Daiichi Sankyo can still matter.
