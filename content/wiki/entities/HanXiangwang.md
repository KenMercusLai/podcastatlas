# 韩襄王 / King Xiang of Han

韩襄王 / King Xiang of Han enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2]] as Han Cang, the son who succeeds after [[HanXuanhuiwang|韩宣惠王 / 韩惠王]] dies. The episode gives this as a compact succession notice before turning to the [[ShuStateWarringStates|蜀国]] rebellion.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-54-wo-zun-ni-shi-wang-ni-dang-wo-shi-pi-lvhcz0ban9frlmwshd9vch-q7-ie]] gives Han Xiang Wang a direct role in [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]]'s [[LianhengStrategy|连横]] tour. Zhang Yi tells him that Han's terrain, food supply, and soldier count make it vulnerable to [[QinState|秦国]], especially if Qin takes [[YiyangWarringStates|宜阳]] and controls Chenggao. The speech turns Han into the first example of [[EncirclementThreatPersuasion|包围威胁式说服]] in the episode: serving Qin and attacking [[ChuState|楚国]] are presented as avoiding destruction rather than as voluntary submission.

The page is source-scoped. Its role is chronological: Han's transition sits inside [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s fourth-year frame while the episode's explanatory weight moves to Yan talent recruitment and Qin's Ba-Shu problem.
