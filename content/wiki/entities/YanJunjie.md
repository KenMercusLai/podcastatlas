# 严俊杰 / Yan Junjie

## Overview

严俊杰 / Yan Junjie, also referred to as I.O. and spelled 闫俊杰 in some source titles, is the founder and CEO of [[MiniMax]]. The bounded sources present him as a self-directed mathematics and AI researcher turned industrial research leader and founder whose recurring concerns are multimodal AGI, scaling, technical organization, useful products, and the economic and social distribution of intelligence.

## Current Profile

Yan describes growing up in a small Henan county, learning mathematics beyond the school curriculum, studying mathematics at Central South University, and moving into pattern recognition and AI at the Chinese Academy of Sciences. A Baidu internship during the deep-learning expansion exposed him to scarce GPU clusters and industrial value; at SenseTime he later led a team reported as reaching several hundred people and learned to prefer directions with a high ceiling and cumulative technical advantage.

He says reading GPT-3 and watching OpenAI's earlier game work helped crystallize the idea that one model could solve many problems. MiniMax was then founded before the ChatGPT breakout around direct user service, internationalization, and foundation-driven development. His current technical thesis joins [[MultimodalAGIStrategy]], [[FrontierModelScaling]], [[ModelHarnessCoEvolution]], and [[AIInterpretabilityByAI]]; his operating thesis joins young talent, cross-functional teams, internal capability growth, user payment, and the conversion of technical lead into sustainable revenue.

His social view is neither purely accelerationist nor catastrophic. He expects rapid progress on tasks that can be defined and evaluated, sees code and content creation as early forms of intelligence democratization, and argues that the harder long-run question is how AI-created value is distributed. At the same time, he treats near-term systems as human-controlled and presents longer-horizon safety conclusions as uncertain rather than settled.

## Key Characteristics

- Self-directed technical learner whose biography moves from county-town mathematics to academic and industrial AI.
- First-principles model builder who treats algorithms, data, infrastructure, evaluation, and organization as decomposable systems.
- Multimodal AGI advocate who defines general intelligence through both multimodal input and multimodal output.
- Founder-operator who prefers direct users, international markets, and foundational capability over project delivery.
- Organization designer who values intelligence, passion, cooperation, internal growth, and complementary team profiles.
- Qualified AI optimist who emphasizes productivity and access while retaining uncertainty about safety, monopoly, and value distribution.
