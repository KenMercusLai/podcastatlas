# Sean Lemass

Sean Lemass appears in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as the Irish Taoiseach whose 1965 visit to [[TerenceONeill|Terence O'Neill]] in Belfast symbolized cautious contact between Dublin and [[Stormont]]. The episode says Lemass privately accepted that partition would continue for a long time, even though the Irish constitution still claimed the whole island.

The visit becomes politically important because [[IanPaisley]] used it to attack O'Neill as a traitor to Protestant Ulster. Lemass therefore functions as a bridge between practical normalization and the [[UnionistSiegeMentality]] that made normalization appear threatening.
