# Paul Offit

## Overview

Paul Offit is a pediatrician and vaccine researcher presented as a co-developer of [[RotaTeq]].

## Current Profile

In [[the-rfk-jr-problem-the-rfk-jr-problem|The RFK Jr. Problem]], Offit describes 26 years of rotavirus-vaccine development, from animal models and viral components through the eventual 2006 release of RotaTeq. He reports that rotavirus hospitalizations became rare enough that many pediatric residents at his hospital had not seen an inpatient case.

The episode also places him in a sustained public dispute with [[RobertFKennedyJr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]]. Offit says Kennedy attacked him as fraudulent and industry-aligned, contributing to death threats and hate mail, while never publicly acknowledging the vaccine's value.

## Key Characteristics

- Pediatrician and vaccine researcher associated with RotaTeq's development.
- Describes a 26-year path from laboratory research to vaccine release.
- Uses hospitalization disappearance as an observed marker of public-health change.
- Estimates that rotavirus vaccination saves about 165,000 lives globally each year.
- Public target of Kennedy's criticism and related harassment, according to the episode.
