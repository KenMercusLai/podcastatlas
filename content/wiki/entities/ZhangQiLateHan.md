# 张岐 / Zhang Qi (Late Han)

张岐 / Zhang Qi (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-954-renxingbenshan-haishi-renxingbene-lkurz9dbb25jrjyl5c-rrahda39h|Hanji 954]] as the former Lelang commandery administrator sent by [[YuanShao|袁绍]] and [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] to [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]] in Youzhou. His mission is to offer Liu Yu the imperial title after coalition actors decide that the [[DongZhuo|董卓]]-controlled [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] leaves them politically constrained.

The source does not develop Zhang Qi's biography. His function is procedural: he turns the [[AlternativeSovereignLegitimacyTrap|另立君主合法性陷阱]] from private coalition discussion into a formal approach to Liu Yu, whose refusal then preserves the distinction between others' title project and Liu Yu's own position.
