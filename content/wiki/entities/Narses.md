# Narses

## Overview

Narses was an Armenian eunuch who rose through the eastern Roman treasury under [[JustinianI|Justinian I]], helped defeat the [[NikaRiots|Nika riots]], commanded successful campaigns in Italy, and later administered conquered territory.

## Current Profile

[[45-top-ten-eunuchs-glt5221212176]] makes Narses its strongest example of eunuch status becoming a route to extraordinary political and military trust. His treasury career gave him court access, and during the Nika crisis he reportedly split the Blues from the Greens before imperial troops crushed the remaining revolt.

He later commanded in Italy against Gothic and Frankish enemies and celebrated what the hosts call the last Roman triumph. His long administration complicates any assumption that court eunuchs were confined to domestic or ceremonial work: in this account, fiscal competence, factional intervention, battlefield command, and provincial rule formed one career.

## Key Characteristics

- Armenian eunuch who rose through the Constantinopolitan treasury.
- Court operator credited with helping divide the Nika factions.
- Successful commander in Justinian's Italian wars.
- Long-lived administrator associated with the last Roman triumph tradition.
- Example of ruler trust enabling authority across civil and military domains.

## Qualifications

This single episode compresses a long career and uses the “last Roman triumph” as a memorable label. It does not independently establish battle chronology, the relative contribution of Narses and other commanders, the exact mechanics of factional bribery, or the quality of his Italian government.
