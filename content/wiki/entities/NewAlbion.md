# New Albion

New Albion appears in [[693-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-englands-greatest-hero-part-3-glt5069845329]] as the land [[FrancisDrake]] claims for [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]] after sailing north along the Pacific coast and returning south toward the Bay Area. The episode says Drake meets the [[Miwok]], receives a feathered crown, and names the claim New Albion.

The source uses New Albion less as a settled colony than as a symbolic extension of the [[GoldenHind]] voyage. Drake has entered the Pacific, evaded Spanish expectations, and turned a raiding expedition into a claim of English global reach.

## Connections
- [[FrancisDrake]], [[GoldenHind]], [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]], and [[England]] - claimant, ship, ruler, and state.
- [[Miwok]] - Indigenous people encountered in the source's account.
- [[Spain]] and [[SpanishImperialMonopolyVulnerability]] - strategic context for reaching the Pacific coast.
