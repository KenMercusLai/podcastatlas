# Putian / 莆田

Putian / 莆田 appears in [[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw]] as the [[Fujian|福建]] footwear-manufacturing setting for [[WoteShoes|沃特鞋企]]. [[WeiQianSanlian|魏茜]] uses the place to explain why a successor can grow up physically close to a factory yet still not understand how a shoe is produced until formally entering the business.

The episode treats Putian less as a tourist or city-profile subject than as an industrial cluster with family, gender, and brand-cycle consequences. Its shoe industry context makes [[ManufacturingSalesShift]] concrete: firms that once lived from production and OEM work now face brand, e-commerce, design, inventory, and consumer-attention pressures.
