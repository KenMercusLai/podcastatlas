# Native American Church

The Native American Church appears in [[a-trip-to-the-magic-mushroom-megachurch]] as the older religious-exemption precedent for ceremonial peyote use. The episode says the church began receiving exemptions as early as the 1960s, making it part of the legal background that later [[PsychedelicChurches]] invoke when seeking protection for controlled-substance sacrament.

In the source, the Native American Church is not treated as equivalent to [[ZydorChurch]]. Its function is precedent: it shows that U.S. law has already recognized some religious uses of otherwise controlled substances, while the [[DEAExemptionProcess]] and [[MyersTest]] still leave newer churches needing to prove sincerity and reduce diversion risk.
