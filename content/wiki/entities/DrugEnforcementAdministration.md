# Drug Enforcement Administration

The Drug Enforcement Administration appears in [[a-trip-to-the-magic-mushroom-megachurch]] as the federal agency whose exemption process matters for psychedelic churches using controlled substances as sacrament. The source says there is no full legal protection unless the government grants an exemption, so [[PsychedelicChurches]] remain exposed under the [[ControlledSubstancesAct]] even when they describe their practice as religious.

The episode emphasizes the practical trap in the [[DEAExemptionProcess]]. Applicants may have to pause controlled-substance use while the application is pending, and a denial may leave the government with detailed information about conduct the applicant disclosed.
