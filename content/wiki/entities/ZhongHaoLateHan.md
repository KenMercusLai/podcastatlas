## Overview
[[ZhongHaoLateHan|种暠]] is a late-Han official whose current profile now has two layers: a 145 CE Yizhou inspection case where corruption reporting turns into Liang Ji resentment, and a later Duliao frontier-governance case where trust, grace, and selective punishment stabilize northern relations.

## Current Profile
The 145 CE layer places Zhong Hao as Yizhou inspector. He reports that Yongchang administrator [[LiuJunshiLateHan|刘君世]] has cast a gold patterned snake for [[LiangJiLateHan|梁冀]], exposing a local-office gift to an overmighty court actor. Liang resents Zhong Hao, and when [[FuZhiLateHan|服直]] later rebels in [[BaCommanderyLateHan|巴郡]], Liang uses Zhong Hao and [[YingChengLateHan|应承]]'s failed suppression as an arrest channel.

Zhong Hao survives because [[LiGuLateHan|李固]] argues that executing officials who took real risks but failed would make future officials stop exerting themselves. [[EmpressDowagerLiangNaLateHan|梁妠]] reduces the punishment, so Zhong Hao loses office rather than dying.

The later Hanji 844 layer makes Zhong Hao a Duliao general after [[ChenGuiDuliaoGeneralLateHan|陈龟]] is recalled. He releases captured Qiang back to their tribes, persuades groups through court benevolence and sincerity, strikes those who refuse order, and produces enough local calm that warning beacons and watch posts lose practical use before he is recalled as dasinong.

## Key Characteristics
- Anti-corruption inspector whose report against Liu Junshi's gold-snake gift angers Liang Ji.
- Failed-service survivor whose punishment is reduced after Li Gu warns against discouraging official effort.
- Frontier governor whose method mixes benevolence, sincerity, prisoner release, and force against groups that continue resisting.
- Official who treats captives as future relationship infrastructure rather than only trophies or leverage.
- Success case measured by local trust and reduced raids, not simply by battlefield kills.
- Name-normalization case where transcript forms such as 崇浩 are linked to received Tongjian 种暠.

## Qualifications
The page remains bounded to two source notes, not a full biography. Hanji 826 shows Zhong Hao as a vulnerable anti-corruption inspector; Hanji 844 shows him as a later model frontier official. The exact legal charge, post-dismissal interval, and path from Yizhou dismissal to Duliao appointment require additional evidence.

## What Changed
- Added Zhong Hao's earlier Yizhou inspector layer through the gold-snake corruption report.
- Connected Liang Ji's resentment to the later arrest over failed Ba Commandery suppression.
- Preserved the Duliao trust-plus-punishment profile as a later governance layer rather than the whole profile.
- Normalized transcript forms such as 崇浩 to 种暠.
