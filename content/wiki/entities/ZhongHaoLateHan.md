## Overview

[[ZhongHaoLateHan|种暠]] is a Huan-era Duliao general whose wiki profile is currently source-bounded to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-844-ruhe-liyu-bubaizhidi-lsp49uw8atyiufrf-ryo-9ctos5z|Hanji 844]]. He enters the narrative after [[ChenGuiDuliaoGeneralLateHan|陈龟]] is recalled and becomes the frontier-governance counterpoint to both Liang Ji's coercive court politics and Zhang Huan's campaign diplomacy.

## Current Profile

Zhong Hao is described as stabilizing the Duliao frontier through trust, grace, credibility, and punishment. He releases captured Qiang back to their tribes, persuades groups through court benevolence and sincerity, strikes those who refuse order, and produces enough local calm that warning beacons and watch posts lose practical use before he is recalled as dasinong.

## Key Characteristics

- Zhong Hao's frontier method is explicitly mixed: benevolence and sincerity are paired with force against groups that continue resisting.
- He treats captives as future relationship infrastructure by releasing Qiang prisoners rather than only holding them as trophies or leverage.
- His success is measured by local trust and reduced raids, not simply by battlefield kills.
- His page supplies a governance counterpart to [[ZhangHuanLateHan|张奂]]'s campaign-level coalition fracture strategy.
- His name is normalized from the transcript's 崇浩 to the received Tongjian form 种暠.

- Appointment after Chen Gui: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-844-ruhe-liyu-bubaizhidi-lsp49uw8atyiufrf-ryo-9ctos5z|Hanji 844]] says Zhong Hao replaces Chen Gui as Duliao general after Chen is recalled.
- Trust-building governance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-844-ruhe-liyu-bubaizhidi-lsp49uw8atyiufrf-ryo-9ctos5z|Hanji 844]] emphasizes benevolence, sincerity, and release of captured Qiang.
- Selective punishment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-844-ruhe-liyu-bubaizhidi-lsp49uw8atyiufrf-ryo-9ctos5z|Hanji 844]] also says Zhong Hao attacks groups that refuse obedience, keeping the profile from becoming pure conciliation.
- Stabilization outcome: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-844-ruhe-liyu-bubaizhidi-lsp49uw8atyiufrf-ryo-9ctos5z|Hanji 844]] reports enough frontier calm that beacons and watch posts are dismantled or rendered unnecessary before his recall as dasinong.

## Qualifications

The page is a source-bounded profile, not a full biography. The episode presents Zhong Hao as a model local and frontier official, but exact dates beyond the Hanji 844 context and the full administrative record require additional sources.
