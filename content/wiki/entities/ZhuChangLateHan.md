## Overview

朱伥 / Zhu Chang is a source-bounded Shun-era Situ whose current wiki role is to show how a qualified officeholder can become the necessary remonstrance channel when a lower staffer sees the danger but lacks the same direct standing. Hanji 806 presents him through [[ZhouJuLateHan|周举]]'s pressure to speak for [[SunChengLateHan|孙程]] and the other marquises after [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]] sends them away from Luoyang.

## Current Profile

Zhu Chang initially hesitates. Zhou Ju tells him that Shun owed accession to Sun Cheng and others, and that treating them harshly for smaller faults could leave the emperor with the reputation of killing contributors if anything happened to them on the road to their fiefs. Zhu Chang worries that Shun is angry and that a lone memorial may bring punishment.

Zhou Ju then reframes the situation as Zhu Chang's own office-duty test. Because Zhu Chang is old and holds the chancellorial rank of Situ, seeking only to preserve office would expose him to the charge of being a flatterer or bad minister, while punishment for remonstrance would at least leave a loyal name. Zhu Chang submits the memorial, and Shun quickly agrees.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior officeholder whose rank makes a dangerous memorial actionable.
- Hesitant remonstrant who needs pressure from a subordinate before acting.
- Case of office duty overriding self-protective silence under ruler anger.
- Mediating actor between Zhou Ju's judgment and Shun's capacity to reverse course.
