# 《马王登基》

《马王登基》 is the [[ZhengYuanjie|郑渊洁]] [[ChineseZodiac|十二生肖]]-series volume that [[23-feima-pai-qiche-anquan-shi-di-yi-wei-de-572699943]] names as the source context for [[FeimaPaiQiche|《飞马牌汽车》]]. The episode does not give a full account of the whole book; it uses the volume mainly to place the bus story inside Zheng's larger children's-literature universe.

The page is therefore source-scoped. Its wiki role is to connect [[FeimaPaiQiche|《飞马牌汽车》]] to Zheng's animal-symbol and childhood-reading branch without claiming a broader interpretation of the complete volume.
