## Overview

王奂 / Wang Huan is the Kaocheng county magistrate in Hanji 862 who recognizes [[QiuXiangLateHan|仇香]] after the [[ChenYuanLateHan|陈元]] unfiliality case. His source role is to contrast stern local governance with Qiu Xiang's higher-status moral transformation.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Wang Huan as an official whose own governance leans severe. That makes his response to Qiu Xiang significant: he does not dismiss non-punitive correction as weakness. Instead, after hearing that Qiu Xiang transformed Chen Yuan without punishment, he appoints Qiu Xiang as a subordinate and asks whether Qiu lacks the fierce spirit associated with punitive enforcement.

Qiu Xiang answers that he prefers the image of an auspicious bird to the predatory bird, and Wang Huan accepts the distinction. He then funds Qiu Xiang's study at [[Taixue|太学]], treating the county office as too small for Qiu's talent. Wang Huan therefore functions as a recognizing official: his own severity is qualified by the ability to see a better governance style when it appears.

## Key Characteristics

- Kaocheng county magistrate in the source's Qiu Xiang story.
- Official associated with stern local governance.
- Recognizes Qiu Xiang's transformation of Chen Yuan as meaningful rather than weak.
- Uses questioning to contrast punitive force with moral teaching.
- Funds Qiu Xiang's Taixue study, helping move him beyond local office.
