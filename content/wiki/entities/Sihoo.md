# 西昊 / Sihoo

## Overview

西昊 / Sihoo is the Chinese office-chair brand used in [[shangye-xiaoyang-51-wei-shenme-bangongyi-yue-gui-dui-laoban-faner-yue-hesuan-gkwrimaoqq8naeo0kgtrbnkp|商业小样51]] as the lower-priced side of an approximate lifecycle-cost comparison.

## Current Profile

The episode uses 西昊 to show why a cheaper purchase can still require a time-horizon check. It attributes a three-year warranty to enterprise purchases and compares that period with a five-year office-equipment depreciation frame and [[Steelcase]]'s reported longer warranty. The case does not establish that 西昊 products fail after three years or are economically inferior; it exposes the limits of comparing chairs by sticker price alone.

## Key Characteristics

- Chinese ergonomic and office-chair brand in the episode's employer-purchase example.
- Represents the lower-upfront-price option in the comparison.
- Reportedly provides a three-year warranty for enterprise procurement in the source.
- Shows why warranty, realized lifespan, repair, and replacement need to be separated in lifecycle accounting.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one podcast source. The episode does not identify model numbers, exact procurement quotes, coverage exclusions, repair experience, actual service life, or comparative comfort testing. A shorter warranty does not prove shorter useful life, and the source does not establish that 西昊 is a poor purchase.
