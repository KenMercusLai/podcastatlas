# 兔咚咚 / Tu Dongdong

## Overview
兔咚咚 / Tu Dongdong is [[YuHong|于红]]'s online children's [[SocialEmotionalLearning|social-emotional learning]] product in the source.

## Current Profile
The episode presents 兔咚咚 as an attempt to turn broad social-emotional aims into repeatable child-facing practice. Its curriculum is described as six dimensions and twelve abilities spanning self-understanding and management, social interaction and conflict resolution, and adaptation to the world through responsible decisions.

The teaching method joins stories, concrete scenes, application tasks, and repeated rehearsal. An example teaches breathing through a conflict story, then uses multiple situations and the memorable actions of smelling a flower and blowing out a candle. The product therefore treats SEL as skill and attitude formation, not one-time knowledge delivery.

## Key Characteristics
- Organizes SEL beyond the narrow goal of making children appear emotionally stable.
- Uses stories to introduce values and scenarios to rehearse behavior.
- Favors short, engaging, repeated learning over purely didactic instruction.
- Treats parent understanding and observable progress as major adoption constraints.
- Measures improvement through recognition, recovery time, frequency, and application rather than the total disappearance of difficult emotion.
