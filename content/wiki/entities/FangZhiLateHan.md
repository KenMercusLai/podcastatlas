## Overview

Fang Zhi is a late-Han Ganling official used by Hanji 871 as the prestige counterpart to [[ZhouFuLateHan|周福]]. The source presents him as a named, reputable court figure whose local followers become one side of a Ganling north-south divide.

## Current Profile

Fang Zhi's profile is source-bounded to the Ganling rivalry. He is not presented as a later Party Prohibition defendant in this episode; his role is to make the local factionalization concrete. The rhyme's praise of Fang Zhi as "rules" or "standards" implies a public reputation independent of Zhou Fu's imperial-teacher channel.

That contrast becomes politically important because reputation poles attract people. Fang Zhi and Zhou Fu's supporters do not merely disagree privately; their guests exchange ridicule and organize into opposing camps. The episode therefore uses Fang Zhi as a mechanism case for how local standing can become a party-like social structure before formal court accusation.

## Key Characteristics

- Ganling-origin official with a reputation for public standards.
- Prestige counterpart to Zhou Fu's imperial-teacher advancement.
- One pole of a follower rivalry that turns local honor into social division.
- Source-bounded prelude actor for later party-language politics.
