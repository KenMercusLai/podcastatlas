# Franco-American Alliance of 1778

## Overview

The Franco-American Alliance of 1778 is the treaty relationship the Franklin episode treats as the decisive foreign-support achievement of Benjamin Franklin's mission to France.

## Current Profile

The source presents the alliance as unlikely but transformative. France was indebted after the Seven Years War, not initially at war with Britain, and many in Paris expected Britain to win. Franklin's fame, social performance, and symbolic image gave American independence a powerful public advocate until the war shifted enough for French military and financial backing to become available. Once France entered the war, the episode says the balance tipped in America's favor.

## Key Characteristics

- Diplomatic objective of Franklin's mission to France.
- Military and financial backing relationship for American independence.
- Product of strategic opportunity as well as Franklin's personal popularity.
- Internationalized the [[AmericanRevolution|American Revolution]] and weakened Britain's position.
