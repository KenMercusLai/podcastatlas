## Overview

傅昭仪 / Fu Zhaoyi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-485-yi-shen-dang-xiong-jiu-huangdi-gudai-zui-yong-feizi-jing-shi-ta-lqn6f6h7zuvdr62ja3mgufy0anty|Hanji 485]] as a [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] consort made jealous by [[FengYuanWesternHan|冯媛 / 冯昭仪]]'s bear-rescue promotion. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-484-gudai-huangdi-de-hunyin-shenghuo-you-duo-mafan-lv7yfmymwhqdejvr0hxntjszlqwe|Hanji 484]] supplies the earlier succession-warning frame: Yuan's favor toward Fu Zhaoyi and [[LiuKangDingtaoKingWesternHan|刘康]] cools his household relationship with [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] and crown prince [[HanChengdi|刘骜 / 后来的汉成帝]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-498-taizi-liu-ao-huangyin-wudu-han-yuandi-weihe-bu-fei-le-ta-lnnuw3h3c8tqlf5ystx58gi7vvjo|Hanji 498]] later presents her as Yuan's favored consort and the mother-linked court presence around Liu Kang, helping explain why Yuan considered replacing Liu Ao with Liu Kang late in life.

## Current Profile

The current evidence gives relational scenes rather than a full biography. Hanji 484 presents Fu Zhaoyi's favor as already politically legible before the deathbed crisis: because Yuan's affection tilts toward Fu Zhaoyi and Liu Kang, Kuang Heng treats the household field as dangerous for empress, consort, legitimate-son, and secondary-son order. Hanji 485 then presents Fu Zhaoyi as a peer consort whose relationship with Feng Yuan changes after Feng Yuan protects Yuan from a bear and is promoted. Hanji 498 returns to the succession version through bedside proximity and favor. As Yuan becomes ill and less engaged in state business, Fu Zhaoyi and Liu Kang are often at his side, while Wang Zhengjun and Liu Ao have less access. The source set therefore treats Fu Zhaoyi's importance as situational: favor and proximity make Liu Kang's branch feel emotionally closer to the ruler, while jealousy marks the harem-status field.

## Key Characteristics

- Han Yuandi consort whose jealousy is linked to Feng Yuan's bear-rescue promotion in Hanji 485.
- Favored consort whose relationship with Liu Kang becomes a succession-order warning in Hanji 484.
- Favored consort of Han Yuandi in the Hanji 498 succession-crisis account.
- Mother-linked presence around Liu Kang, the alternative heir candidate.
- Bedside companion whose proximity helps shift Yuan's late-life emotional field.
- Source-scoped figure rather than a complete biographical subject in the current wiki.

## Qualifications

Hanji 484, Hanji 485, and Hanji 498 give compact podcast accounts. Fu Zhaoyi's full title history, family background, later relationship to the Dingtao line, and independent political agency are not settled by these sources. The exact continuity between the household-order warning, the early jealousy scene, and the later deathbed succession-pressure role remains source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 484's earlier household-order warning, where Fu Zhaoyi and Liu Kang's favor makes the crown-prince branch politically exposed.
- Preserved Hanji 485's earlier jealousy scene after Feng Yuan's bear-rescue promotion.
- Preserved Hanji 498's favored-consort and bedside-proximity role in the late-Yuan succession crisis.
