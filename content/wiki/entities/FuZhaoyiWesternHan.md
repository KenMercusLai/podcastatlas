## Overview

傅昭仪 / Fu Zhaoyi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-498-taizi-liu-ao-huangyin-wudu-han-yuandi-weihe-bu-fei-le-ta-lnnuw3h3c8tqlf5ystx58gi7vvjo|Hanji 498]] as [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]]'s favored consort and the mother-linked court presence around [[LiuKangDingtaoKingWesternHan|刘康]]. The episode uses her as part of the explanation for why Yuan considered replacing crown prince [[HanChengdi|刘骜 / 后来的汉成帝]] with Liu Kang late in life.

## Current Profile

The current evidence is narrow. Hanji 498 does not provide Fu Zhaoyi's full biography; it presents her through bedside proximity and favor. As Yuan becomes ill and less engaged in state business, Fu Zhaoyi and Liu Kang are often at his side, while [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] and Liu Ao have less access. The source therefore treats Fu Zhaoyi's importance as relational and situational: her presence helps make Liu Kang feel like the child and household branch closest to the dying ruler.

## Key Characteristics

- Favored consort of Han Yuandi in the Hanji 498 succession-crisis account.
- Mother-linked presence around Liu Kang, the alternative heir candidate.
- Bedside companion whose proximity helps shift Yuan's late-life emotional field.
- Source-scoped figure rather than a complete biographical subject in the current wiki.

## Evidence

- Favor and maternal link: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-498-taizi-liu-ao-huangyin-wudu-han-yuandi-weihe-bu-fei-le-ta-lnnuw3h3c8tqlf5ystx58gi7vvjo|Hanji 498]] says Liu Kang's mother Fu Zhaoyi was deeply favored by Han Yuandi.
- Bedside proximity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-498-taizi-liu-ao-huangyin-wudu-han-yuandi-weihe-bu-fei-le-ta-lnnuw3h3c8tqlf5ystx58gi7vvjo|Hanji 498]] says Fu Zhaoyi and Liu Kang frequently attended Yuan during illness.
- Succession pressure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-498-taizi-liu-ao-huangyin-wudu-han-yuandi-weihe-bu-fei-le-ta-lnnuw3h3c8tqlf5ystx58gi7vvjo|Hanji 498]] links that intimacy to Yuan's temptation to favor Liu Kang over Liu Ao.

## Qualifications

Hanji 498 gives only a compact podcast account. Fu Zhaoyi's full title history, family background, later relationship to the Dingtao line, and independent political agency are not settled by this source.
