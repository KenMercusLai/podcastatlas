## Overview

傅昭仪 / Fu Zhaoyi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-485-yi-shen-dang-xiong-jiu-huangdi-gudai-zui-yong-feizi-jing-shi-ta-lqn6f6h7zuvdr62ja3mgufy0anty|Hanji 485]] as a [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] consort made jealous by [[FengYuanWesternHan|冯媛 / 冯昭仪]]'s bear-rescue promotion. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-498-taizi-liu-ao-huangyin-wudu-han-yuandi-weihe-bu-fei-le-ta-lnnuw3h3c8tqlf5ystx58gi7vvjo|Hanji 498]] later presents her as Yuan's favored consort and the mother-linked court presence around [[LiuKangDingtaoKingWesternHan|刘康]], helping explain why Yuan considered replacing crown prince [[HanChengdi|刘骜 / 后来的汉成帝]] with Liu Kang late in life.

## Current Profile

The current evidence gives two relational scenes rather than a full biography. Hanji 485 presents Fu Zhaoyi as a peer consort whose relationship with Feng Yuan changes after Feng Yuan protects Yuan from a bear and is promoted. Hanji 498 then presents Fu Zhaoyi through bedside proximity and favor. As Yuan becomes ill and less engaged in state business, Fu Zhaoyi and Liu Kang are often at his side, while [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] and Liu Ao have less access. The source therefore treats Fu Zhaoyi's importance as situational: jealousy marks the harem-status field early, and later proximity makes Liu Kang feel like the child and household branch closest to the dying ruler.

## Key Characteristics

- Han Yuandi consort whose jealousy is linked to Feng Yuan's bear-rescue promotion in Hanji 485.
- Favored consort of Han Yuandi in the Hanji 498 succession-crisis account.
- Mother-linked presence around Liu Kang, the alternative heir candidate.
- Bedside companion whose proximity helps shift Yuan's late-life emotional field.
- Source-scoped figure rather than a complete biographical subject in the current wiki.

## Qualifications

Hanji 485 and Hanji 498 give compact podcast accounts. Fu Zhaoyi's full title history, family background, later relationship to the Dingtao line, and independent political agency are not settled by these sources. The exact continuity between the early jealousy scene and the later succession-pressure role remains source-scoped.
