# Alexei Nikolaevich

## Overview

Alexei Nikolaevich was the son and heir of [[NicholasII|Nicholas II]] and [[AlexandraFeodorovna|Alexandra Feodorovna]]. His hemophilia created repeated family crises and made [[GrigoryRasputin|Grigory Rasputin]]'s apparent ability to reassure the household central to late Romanov court politics.

## Current Profile

[[128-rasputin-glt1290733613]] centers Alexei's political significance on a private medical vulnerability. Born in 1904, he inherited hemophilia and could suffer life-threatening internal bleeding after ordinary injury. At Spala in 1912, bleeding after a coach journey appeared likely to kill him. Alexandra appealed to Rasputin, who replied that Alexei would live and that doctors should not disturb him excessively; the boy recovered.

The episode does not claim a demonstrated miraculous cure. It suggests that reassurance may have calmed Alexandra and Alexei or reduced harmful medical interference, while leaving the mechanism unresolved. Whatever the cause, the recovery strengthened Alexandra's trust and thereby connected the heir's illness to Rasputin's access, court secrecy, and the political legends surrounding the monarchy.

## Key Characteristics

- Only son and heir of Nicholas II and Alexandra Feodorovna.
- Lived with hemophilia, making ordinary injuries potentially fatal and producing intense parental fear.
- Survived a severe 1912 bleeding crisis after Rasputin sent reassurance and advised less medical disturbance.
- Became the private medical reason for Rasputin's unusual standing with the imperial family.
- Was later killed with the Romanov family after the monarchy's collapse.
