# 宜阳 / Yiyang (Warring States)

宜阳 / Yiyang appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-27-weiguan-weihuiwang-he-qiweiwang-shi-za-shangye-hupeng-lminlxhrm5bethub9kyy-8blimrb]] as the [[HanState|韩国]] place taken by [[QinState|秦国]] in 335 BCE. The episode says Qin had already attacked around Yiyang more than fifty years earlier, but had then taken only several large outlying settlements rather than the city itself.

The source treats Yiyang as a long-coveted strategic target for Qin, comparable in function to [[WeiState|魏国]]'s Hexi lands. On this page, Yiyang is kept as a Warring States place node rather than merged with later or modern Yiyang references.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-28-hanzhaohou-de-kuaile-ni-xiangxiang-budao-ln2tbxniz4kvwogbwp32exmdokz8]] uses the same loss as background for [[HanZhaohou|韩昭侯]]'s high-gate criticism. Because Han has recently suffered Qin's attack and drought, the place becomes part of the circumstance that makes the gate project [[UntimelyExtravagance|时绌举赢]].
