## Overview

严从 / Yan Cong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-555-ta-weile-dang-huangdi-weisha-yao-xisheng-qindie-lq3kkkmk9nh4yhxuovmy_uktalrv|Hanji 555]] as the crown prince's shaofu who correctly reads the danger in [[HanAidi|刘欣]] thanking [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] for preserving the Dingtao birth-father line. His role is narrow but important: he treats adoption-line succession as a public status conversion rather than a private family compromise.

## Current Profile

Hanji 555 says Liu Xin, newly made Chengdi's son and heir, wants to submit thanks after Chengdi arranges for Liu Jing to continue the Dingtao line. Yan Cong advises against the memorial. In his reading, Liu Xin's new identity as Chengdi's son means he cannot openly care for his former bone-and-flesh relatives as if their benefit remains his own.

The episode contrasts Yan Cong with [[ZhaoXuanWesternHan|赵璇]]. Zhao Xuan recommends gratitude and Liu Xin follows him, but Chengdi's anger proves Yan Cong had seen the political structure more clearly. Yan Cong therefore functions as a minor but sharp case of ritual-political literacy: the correct advice is emotionally harsh because the succession order needs Liu Xin to have no visible retreat into his birth family.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han crown-prince shaofu in the Hanji 555 succession episode.
- Advises Liu Xin not to thank Chengdi for preserving the Dingtao birth-father line.
- Reads adoptive succession as a complete public status shift.
- Treats birth-family gratitude as a political signal, not merely private feeling.
- Serves as the contrast case to Zhao Xuan's sincere but politically mistaken advice.
