# Charles Johnson

Charles Johnson appears in [[ep-10-a-thought-provoking-chat-with-an-actuary-and-tedx-speaker]] as a seasoned actuary, TEDx speaker, and actuarial-development leader interviewed by [[SamDataScienceWithSam|Sam]] on [[DataScienceWithSam]]. The source uses his career path to show how [[ActuarialScience]] can attract people through job prospects and structured exams, then demand long-term self-study, business judgment, and professional accountability.

Charles's main argument is that actuaries and data scientists should collaborate rather than compete as insurance becomes more data-rich. He sees data scientists contributing modeling, programming, automation, and deployment strength, while actuaries contribute finance, accounting, risk, regulatory, and insurance-domain judgment through [[ActuaryDataScientistPartnership]].

The AI discussion makes Charles a practical [[ActuarialAIAugmentation]] voice. He encourages students and employees to use tools such as [[ChatGPT]] for business problems, but the same episode keeps validation, citations, traceability, and hallucination risk inside [[HumanJudgmentUnderAI]].
