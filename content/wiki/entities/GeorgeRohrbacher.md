# George Rohrbacher

## Overview

George Rohrbacher is an American farmer, game designer, and co-founder of [[WeekendFarmerCompany]] whose experience at Breaks Ranch became [[TheFarmingGame]].

## Current Profile

In [[board-game-season-board-game-season]], Rohrbacher leaves a conventional career path with [[AnneRohrbacher]], learns farming in Washington's Yakima Valley, and expands from a 27-acre farm to the thousand-acre Breaks Ranch during an agricultural boom. Drought and leverage later make the operation financially untenable, and in July 1979 he sketches a board game that converts the family's predicament into rules.

The episode presents him as an optimistic, high-variance builder. He designs a deliberately punishing but winnable model, bets scarce capital on 10,000 manufactured copies, sells through agricultural media and rural retail, and keeps farming after the game begins producing revenue. His own quick bankruptcy while playing the game decades later reinforces the source's portrait without establishing a general personality assessment.

## Key Characteristics

- Learned farming through local practice after joining the 1970s back-to-the-land movement.
- Expanded aggressively during favorable land, credit, and commodity conditions.
- Turned debt, weather, harvests, operating costs, and bankruptcy into game mechanics.
- Combined agricultural storytelling with direct sales to farm audiences.
- Described farming as requiring optimism despite uncontrollable outcomes.
