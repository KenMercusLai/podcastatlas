# 陈庄 / Chen Zhuang (Warring States)

陈庄 / Chen Zhuang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-52-chenzhuang-panqin-duanqi-wan-chifan-fangxia-wan-ma-niang-ls13ixmbhflepjgpelxgu6idjqa2]] as the [[QinState|秦国]]-appointed chancellor governing conquered [[ShuStateWarringStates|蜀国]]. After Qin reduces the former Shu ruler to [[ShuHouNingTong|蜀侯宁通]], Chen Zhuang holds the administrative position that links Qin authority to local control.

The episode says Chen Zhuang kills Shu Hou Ning Tong and rebels against Qin in [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s fourth-year frame. The host interprets his motive through [[AppointedGovernorSecessionRisk|appointed-governor secession risk]]: [[BaShu|巴蜀]] is hard to enter, agriculturally valuable, and politically tempting for a local governor whose power has grown.

His rebellion fails quickly in the source. The following year Qin sends [[GanMao|甘茂]] into Shu, Chen Zhuang is killed, and his attempt to turn delegated authority into independent rule ends.
