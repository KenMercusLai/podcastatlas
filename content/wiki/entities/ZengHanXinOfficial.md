## Overview

曾涵 / Zeng Han enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-624-du-lishi-wu-xingfu-ni-shi-rensheng-yingjia-haishi-yongren-zirao-daibiao-lpavicltc7nducbxiqgpr6t7iczd|Hanji 624]] as a Wang Mang-aligned right general who reports [[WangMang|王莽]]'s refusal-and-retirement language to [[WangZhengjun|王政君]]. His role is small but structurally important: he helps convert Wang Mang's apparent humility into renewed dependence on Wang Mang and preparation for Nine Bestowments.

## Current Profile

The source says Zeng Han and other Wang Mang confidants present Wang Mang's words to the empress dowager. Wang Mang asks that petitions for reward be shelved and claims he will complete rites and institutions before retiring. Zeng Han's report leads to Wang Zhengjun's moved response: she says major matters still require Wang Mang and orders the Nine Bestowments rites to be drawn up.

## Key Characteristics

- Right general and Wang Mang confidant in the Hanji 624 account.
- Court messenger who carries Wang Mang's public refusal language upward.
- Participant in the conversion of humility rhetoric into higher ritual status.
- Source-scoped figure with no broader biography yet in the wiki.
