# Min Kao / 高明环

Min Kao / 高明环 is one of the two [[Garmin]] founders named in [[kafeidou-liangci-zaoyu-pingguo-chongji-yundong-shoubiao-jiaming-weihe-hai-neng-zengzhang-1006272684]]. The source presents him as a navigation-background founder and explains Garmin's name as the combination of the first-name fragments of [[GaryBurrell|Gary Burrell]] and Min Kao.

In the source's Garmin history, Kao helps mark why the company began with high-reliability [[GlobalPositioningSystem|GPS]] equipment for aviation, marine, surveying, and other professional users before moving into consumer car navigation and then sports wearables.
