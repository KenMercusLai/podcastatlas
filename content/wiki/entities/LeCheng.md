# 乐乘 / Le Cheng

乐乘 / Le Cheng enters the wiki more directly through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-104-yinmou-shisuan-rang-qinguo-bantu-you-you-you-pengzhang-le-lgnyhbduqutegtksrjwcvhmn8-t6]]. The source describes him as a former [[YanState|燕国]] general who had been defeated or captured by [[LianPo|廉颇]] and then came into [[ZhaoState|赵国]] service.

When [[ZhaoDaoxiangwang|赵道襄王]] succeeds, he orders Le Cheng to replace Lian Po. Lian Po refuses the command transfer, attacks Le Cheng, and then flees Zhao. Le Cheng's role is therefore less an independent achievement than the trigger point for Zhao's late command rupture.
