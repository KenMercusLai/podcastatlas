## Overview

旧世代 is a games-and-culture podcast that combines industry analysis, player experience, preservation concerns, criticism, and community programming.

## Current Profile

Across the bounded episodes, the show treats games as products, technical artifacts, aesthetic wholes, and memory-bearing cultural objects. Its physical-media discussion examines platform economics and the insecurity of digital licenses; the 揽月周刊 episode adds a shorter recurring format for news, play impressions, AI, productivity, and community questions. Together they connect ownership and preservation to both distribution systems and the technical ability to keep software running.

## Key Characteristics

- Links game-industry incentives to player ownership, resale, access, and preservation.
- Uses personal play and technical experiments as evidence while marking speculation and uncertainty.
- Treats AI as both a preservation tool and a threat to creator attention, credit, and openness.
- Evaluates games through integrated mechanics, progression, art, music, and atmosphere.
- Uses recurring audio and community formats to sustain a regular creative practice.
