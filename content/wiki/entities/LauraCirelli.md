# Laura Cirelli

## Overview
Laura Cirelli is a researcher represented here through an infant synchrony experiment discussed by [[HiddenBrain]].

## Current Profile
Cirelli studies how music and coordinated movement provide social information to very young children. In the episode's experiment, fourteen-month-olds were bounced while another adult moved either in or out of sync; infants in the synchronized condition later helped that adult more often.

The result contributes an early-development case to [[RitualSynchronyAndProsociality]], but the supplied summary does not separate affiliation, familiarity, shared stimulus, arousal, or experimenter effects.

## Key Characteristics
- Studies music, movement, and early social behavior.
- Uses behavioral experiments to infer infant social cognition.
- Treats synchrony as a possible affiliation cue.

## Evidence
- Infant helping - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-63970084fa0b4353e8e3de25a37c3714-52798272-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-63970084fa0b4353e8e3de25a37c3714-52798272]] reports higher helping after synchronized than out-of-sync bouncing.
- Social cue interpretation - [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-63970084fa0b4353e8e3de25a37c3714-52798272-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-63970084fa0b4353e8e3de25a37c3714-52798272]] attributes to Cirelli the view that music and coordinated movement influence infant and adult social behavior.

## Qualifications
The profile rests on one episode summary and one described paradigm. The supplied document does not provide sample size, statistical analysis, replication record, or boundary conditions, and helping in a laboratory task should not be treated as a general measure of character.
