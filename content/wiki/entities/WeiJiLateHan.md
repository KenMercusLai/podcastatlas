# 魏觊 / Wei Ji (late Han)

## Overview
魏觊 / Wei Ji is introduced in the wiki as a Hedong official assigned to Guanzhong affairs under [[CaoCao|曹操]] before the [[GuanduCampaign|官渡之战]]. His significance lies in a rear-governance proposal: stop returning refugees from being absorbed into private military forces by using state salt revenue to rebuild farming capacity.

## Current Profile
Wei Ji appears as an administrator who reads population movement as both a food problem and a security problem. Refugees returning to [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] can restore production, but if local generals absorb them first, those generals gain autonomous manpower. His plan therefore couples fiscal control, agricultural recovery, and institutional presence: restore salt offices, sell salt, buy tools and oxen, support returning farmers, and keep the Sili Xiaowei apparatus in Guanzhong.

## Key Characteristics
- Rear-area administrator whose policy field is Guanzhong before Guandu.
- Treats returning refugees as productive capacity that can become either state-aligned farming households or private military manpower.
- Uses salt-office revenue as the financing channel for farming tools, oxen, and agricultural recovery.
- Links fiscal administration and institutional placement to the weakening of local generals' independent military base.
- Communicates policy through [[XunYu|荀彧]], whose forwarding of the letter helps turn the proposal into adopted Cao-side policy.

## Qualifications
The current wiki evidence covers Wei Ji only through this Guanzhong policy proposal. It does not yet verify implementation outcomes beyond the source's statement that Cao Cao accepted the plan. The title and office details are normalized from the episode summary and should remain source-scoped until a fuller biographical source is ingested.

## What Changed
- Established Wei Ji as the administrative author of the Guanzhong salt-revenue agricultural recovery plan.
- Connected his refugee policy to Guandu preparation, local-general containment, and state fiscal control.
