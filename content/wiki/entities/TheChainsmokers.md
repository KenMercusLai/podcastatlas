# The Chainsmokers

## Overview
The Chainsmokers are the music partnership of [[DrewTaggart|Drew Taggart]] and [[AlexPall|Alex Pall]], presented in the source as an internet-native act that later extended its relationships and distribution experience into [[MantisVC|Mantis]].

## Current Profile
Their account joins creative identity with deliberate distribution. Early remixes and personalized blogger outreach helped them become visible while they improved their production, and live performance remains economically important. In venture capital they describe a supporting role rather than a lead-investor identity, arguing that fame creates access but must be converted into repeatable help and credible returns.

## Key Characteristics
- Balances a recognizable musical identity with continued experimentation.
- Used personalized internet outreach and platform literacy in early music distribution.
- Treats direct fan reach and live performance as distinct parts of music economics.
- Founded [[MantisVC|Mantis]] to invest primarily at seed and Series A.
- Positions celebrity access as useful only when paired with work, judgment, and operating support.
