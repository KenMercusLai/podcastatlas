# Adidas Samba

Adidas Samba is the retro Adidas shoe line discussed in [[vol-271-a-di-da-si-ruhe-chudi-fantan-1007333155]] as a key product in [[Adidas]]' post-2023 recovery. The episode says [[BjornGulden|Bjørn Gulden / 古尔登]] quickly pushed Samba and other retro lines after becoming CEO, and that celebrity street photos plus TikTok-style circulation helped make the line visible to younger consumers.

## Key Claims
- Samba is treated as a [[RetroProductRevival]] case: an old product line can be made newly salient through styling, social circulation, and brand portfolio focus.
- The source says Samba helped offset part of the [[Yeezy]] gap, but does not claim it could fully replace Yeezy's North American revenue contribution.
- The product's revival connects [[SportsLifestyleConsumption]] with [[ConsumerBrandMoat]] because a sports-rooted silhouette becomes everyday fashion and identity demand.
- The episode uses Samba to contrast archive-based brand heat with celebrity-dependent product risk.

## Connections
- [[Adidas]] — parent brand.
- [[BjornGulden|Bjørn Gulden / 古尔登]] — executive associated with the revival strategy.
