# Adidas Samba

## Overview

Adidas Samba is a retro [[Adidas]] shoe line that the wiki treats as both a turnaround product and a thin-sole fashion signal. It appears first as part of Adidas' post-2023 recovery and then as one of the reference points for the broader move from bulky sneakers toward slimmer, terrace-style shoes.

## Current Profile

Samba functions as Adidas' clearest archive-revival case in this corpus. [[BjornGulden|Bjørn Gulden / 古尔登]] is associated with quickly pushing Samba and related retro lines after becoming CEO, while celebrity street photos and short-video circulation helped younger consumers read the shoe as current rather than merely old.

The ballet-footwear episode adds a lifecycle qualification. Samba-like thin-sole heat created a powerful silhouette shift, but as restocks reduced scarcity and copycat products spread, brands needed new variations. Ballet-coded hybrids are one answer to that saturation pressure.

## Key Characteristics

- Samba is an [[Adidas]] archive product reactivated through styling, social circulation, and portfolio focus.
- The line helped offset part of the [[Yeezy]] gap without fully replacing Yeezy's revenue and profit role.
- Its low-profile silhouette links [[SportsLifestyleConsumption]] with [[ThinSoleSneakerRevival]].
- Samba's popularity also creates saturation risk once scarcity fades and competitors imitate the look.
- The ballet-sneaker branch uses Samba-era thin soles as a base that can be refreshed with softer, more refined upper details.

## Evidence

- **Turnaround product:** [[vol-271-a-di-da-si-ruhe-chudi-fantan-1007333155]] presents Samba as a visible [[RetroProductRevival]] tool in Adidas' recovery after the Yeezy break.
- **Social diffusion:** [[vol-271-a-di-da-si-ruhe-chudi-fantan-1007333155]] ties the revival to celebrity street styling and short-video circulation that made the old silhouette legible to younger buyers.
- **Thin-sole context:** [[kafeidou-yundongxie-xishang-chouduan-sidai-xielu-pinpai-weishenme-jiti-mishang-baleifeng-1011206166]] frames Samba, Gazelle, Spezial, and terrace shoes as part of the slim-sneaker revival that replaced bulky dad-shoe fatigue.
- **Refresh pressure:** [[kafeidou-yundongxie-xishang-chouduan-sidai-xielu-pinpai-weishenme-jiti-mishang-baleifeng-1011206166]] says brands use color, materials, collaborations, restocks, classic revivals, and ballet-derived hybrids to extend the cycle after scarcity fades.

## Qualifications

- Samba's revival is not treated as a complete replacement for [[Yeezy]] or as proof of permanent Adidas momentum.
- The sources emphasize demand heat and product-cycle logic, not a full product-line financial breakdown.
- Ballet-coded sneakers are adjacent to Samba's silhouette logic; the page does not claim Samba itself is a ballet shoe.
