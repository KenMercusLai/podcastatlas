# Christopher Hatton

[[693-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-englands-greatest-hero-part-3-glt5069845329]] adds Hatton before [[FrancisDrake]]'s return through [[ThomasDoughty]], Hatton's secretary and a gentleman passenger on Drake's 1577 expedition. The episode notes that Drake renames the Pelican as the [[GoldenHind]], Hatton's crest, after having executed Doughty at San Julien.

Christopher Hatton appears in [[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] as one of the Privy Council figures who supported [[FrancisDrake]] after Drake returned with captured Spanish treasure. The episode groups him with [[FrancisWalsingham]] and [[RobertDudley|Robert Dudley / Earl of Leicester]] as part of the more pro-Drake side of the council.

Hatton's support is notable because Drake had executed Hatton's secretary Thomas Doughty during the voyage. The source uses that detail to show how financial interest, Protestant politics, and anti-[[Spain]] statecraft could override personal grievance.

In the wiki, Hatton is a bounded Elizabethan court node. The episode does not provide a full biography; it uses him to map the factional choice between [[WilliamCecil|Burleigh]]'s caution and a more confrontational privateering policy.
