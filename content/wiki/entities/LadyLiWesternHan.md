## Overview

李夫人 / Lady Li enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-388-1-shaqi-shazi-zhihou-hanwudi-weihe-zumie-dajiuzi-liguangli-lkkwpivpwqpeg5kd7azu-wmdqmgb|Hanji 388-1]] as [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s consort, [[LiGuangliWesternHan|李广利]]'s sister, and the mother of [[LiuBoChangyiKingWesternHan|昌邑王刘髆]].

## Current Profile

The source does not give Lady Li an independent narrative arc. Her importance is genealogical and political: because Liu Bo is her son, Li Guangli has a maternal-family reason to favor Liu Bo's elevation as crown prince. That preference links Li Guangli to [[LiuQuliWesternHan|刘屈氂]], whose family is joined to Li's through marriage.

Lady Li therefore anchors the episode's succession-risk chain without being shown as an actor in it. Her son becomes the desired heir, her brother becomes the exposed general, and the Li family connection helps explain why a battlefield crisis can become a family extermination story.
