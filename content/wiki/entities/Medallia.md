# Medallia

## Overview
Medallia is presented in the source as a customer-experience SaaS company whose leveraged buyout became a warning case for AI-era software repricing.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Medallia less as a full company history than as a concrete example of what happens when debt-backed SaaS assumptions meet slower growth, rising debt service, and AI workflow substitution. The hosts say Medallia's survey-and-insight software sits in a category where companies may increasingly build internal AI-agent workflows instead of paying for a full vertical SaaS stack.

## Key Characteristics
- Medallia is treated as a customer-experience software company built around surveys, feedback, and management insight.
- Its capital structure is central to the source's interest because the hosts discuss large acquisition debt and higher debt service.
- It represents the risk that useful SaaS products can still become overleveraged if growth and retention assumptions weaken.
- The source links Medallia's exposure to cheaper, more customized internal AI workflows.
- The company remains a source-scoped case rather than a complete independent valuation history.
