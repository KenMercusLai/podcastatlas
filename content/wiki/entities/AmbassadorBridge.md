# Ambassador Bridge

The Ambassador Bridge is the older privately owned [[Detroit]]-[[WindsorOntario|Windsor, Ontario]] crossing used as the comparison point in [[outsider-chance-ending-sudans-proxy-war-6a75b3695d765781a2b1b132]]. The episode says trucks had long relied mainly on this bridge before the [[GordieHoweBridge]] opened.

[[AnnieCrabill]] says the Ambassador Bridge charges high tolls and does not integrate directly with Canada's highway system. That contrast makes the new bridge a practical logistics improvement, not only a symbol in [[CrossBorderInfrastructureTradeTension]].
