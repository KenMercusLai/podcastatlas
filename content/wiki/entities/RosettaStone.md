# Rosetta Stone / 罗塞塔石碑

Rosetta Stone / 罗塞塔石碑 appears in [[271-tangchao-douyao-bucunzai-le-weishenme-weishilun-hui-zai-jinnian-da-baofa-lg7-wdrdw1b9h7lwa4vwkvmyb4un]] as [[HeSenbao|何森堡]]'s example of cross-checked historical evidence. The source explains the decipherment logic: Greek text, Egyptian scripts, the Ptolemaic political context, and royal names made it possible for [[JeanFrancoisChampollion|商博良]] to connect known and unknown writing systems.

For the wiki, the stone is a compact case for [[HistoricalDetectiveReasoning]] and against [[PseudoHistoryConspiracy|伪史论]]. Its force is not that one artifact proves all of Egyptian history, but that the artifact sits inside a wider network of language, regime history, inscriptions, and later scholarship.
