# Digit Robot

## Overview
Digit is Agility Robotics' humanoid warehouse robot in the All-In robotics special. The episode treats it as a bounded, work-focused humanoid rather than a general home robot.

## Current Profile
Digit's current wiki profile is a practical warehouse humanoid that justifies human-like elements only where they help with human spaces, two-arm work, balance, and whole-body control. Its business case in the source depends on repeatable material-handling workflows, safe coexistence with people, high utilization, and flexible commercial models.

## Key Characteristics
- Digit currently handles scoped warehouse workflows such as moving bins and totes.
- The humanoid form is justified by tasks requiring two arms, balance, narrow-space movement, and whole-body control.
- Digit's broader promise is versatility across each picking, bin filling, carrying, palletizing, and depalletizing.
- Digit V5 is described as a safety-focused redesign meant to operate outside a work cell without a physical barrier.
- Fast charging is presented as enabling roughly 20 operating hours out of 24.
- Commercial access can come through CapEx or robot-as-a-service depending on customer preference.

## Qualifications
