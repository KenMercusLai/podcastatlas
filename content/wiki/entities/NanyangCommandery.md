# 南阳郡 / Nanyang Commandery

南阳郡 / Nanyang Commandery appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-85-xuantaihou-weihe-sitong-30-nian-hou-ansha-le-laoqingren-lg7zy6vqtdjcufou4-mox5qkg2ct5]] as the Qin commandery organized after [[WeiState|魏国]]'s previous cession of Nanyang to [[QinState|秦国]]. The host identifies the likely region as the northern [[ChuState|楚国]] territory Qin had recently occupied, with the administrative center at Wan, near today's Nanyang in Henan.

The page is source-scoped. Its importance is administrative rather than biographical: like [[NanCommandery|南郡]] and [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]], it shows Qin converting campaign or diplomatic gains into commandery structure rather than leaving them as loose conquest.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-4-jiuquangui-zhangliang-ruhe-zai-liubang-shouxia-anshen-lp6qwg3ma5hdy15g4box7pe23jav|Qinji 129-4]] adds a Qin-collapse use of the same commandery. [[SongLiu|宋刘]], acting for [[ChenSheng|陈胜]], had taken Nanyang and moved toward Wu Pass, but after Chen Sheng's death the commandery returns to Qin and Song Liu surrenders. [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]]'s execution of Song Liu makes the place a source case for [[PunitiveSurrenderDeterrence|杀降纳降反噬]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-136-meinv-xijiao-liubang-ye-hao-zhe-kouer-lplgzwzahepwx9p7krfc4x0cnz4o|Qinji 136]] returns to Nanyang on [[LiuBang|刘邦]]'s westward route. After Liu Bang defeats the commandery governor, the governor retreats to [[WanCounty|宛县]]. Liu Bang first wants to bypass the city, but [[ZhangLiang|张良]] warns that Wan could strike from the rear while Qin blocks the road ahead.

The negotiated surrender at Wan reverses the earlier Song Liu lesson. Instead of punishing surrender, Liu Bang accepts [[ChenHuiNanyang|陈恢]]'s proposal, rewards the governor, rewards Chen Hui, and keeps moving west with a safer rear. This makes Nanyang a turning point from anti-Qin vengeance toward [[OccupationPacificationStrategy|占领区怀柔治理]] and [[PoliticalSurrenderCascade|招降示范级联]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-936-luanshi-xian-sha-shengmu-xin-zhehua-dui-ma-lml-kuyc7yys2ijh2kt46pwnugar|Hanji 936]] adds an earlier late-Han crisis marker. Qin Jie, the Nanyang administrator, is killed during [[ZhaoCiLateHan|赵慈]]'s Jiangxia revolt before Jingzhou inspector Wang Min suppresses Zhao Ci. The commandery's role here is brief but shows Nanyang appearing again in a wider late-Han breakdown field.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1010-sanguo-liubiao-jiujing-shibushi-piaoliang-de-caobao-lo58hzfo-qv0soauogtgfrkourrd|Hanji 1010]] adds a late-Han use of Nanyang. [[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]] enters [[Jingzhou|荆州]] and attacks [[Rangcheng|穰城]] in Nanyang while looking for a base, then dies during the fighting. The episode says [[LiuBiao|刘表]] had broad control in northern Jingzhou but Nanyang still sat in a contested field, making Zhang Ji's death and the absorption of his remnants a regional-stability problem rather than only a battlefield notice.
