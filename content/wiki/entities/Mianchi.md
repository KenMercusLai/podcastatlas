# 渑池 / Mianchi

渑池 / Mianchi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-76-shui-geile-chuqingxiangwang-de-zixin-lsut4cfic7xbbrmbmimrruf7n6y0]] as the meeting place [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] proposes to [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] after consecutive Qin attacks on [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. The episode only previews the meeting, framing it as a suspense point about whether Zhao Huiwen Wang will dare to attend.

The preview matters because the invitation resembles an earlier Qin diplomatic trap against [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] at [[Wuguan|武关]]. In this source, Mianchi therefore sits between [[WanbiGuizhaoDiplomacy|完璧归赵式外交]] and future Qin-Zhao negotiation, rather than yet receiving a full event treatment.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-77-lianpo-yu-linxiangru-shishang-zui-zhuming-de-yidui-hao-jiyou-lmelnhhtfuzezvmmsuw4yf10plsb]] completes the event. [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] attends after [[LianPo|廉颇]] and [[LinXiangru|蔺相如]] argue that nonattendance would show weakness, while Lian Po prepares a succession contingency in case Qin detains the ruler. At the banquet, [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] makes Zhao He play music, but Lin Xiangru forces Qin into a matching gesture by demanding that Qin strike a fou for Zhao.

Mianchi therefore becomes a diplomatic-parity scene rather than a successful hostage trap. Qin has recent military advantage, but Zhao's military preparation and Lin Xiangru's immediate pressure keep the meeting from becoming one-sided disgrace.
