## Overview

虞常 / Yu Chang is a Han-connected participant in the failed Xiongnu-court plot that exposes [[ZhangShengSuWuMissionWesternHan|张胜]] and turns [[SuWuWesternHan|苏武]]'s completed diplomatic mission into a detention and surrender crisis.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-376-1-lishishang-zhenshi-de-suwu-muyang-shi-shenmeyang-lidxs1d66m5q2hegjteq1lgra5fp|Hanji 376-1]] says Yu Chang approaches Zhang Sheng privately, offers to arrange the killing of [[WeiLuWesternHan|卫律]], and asks that his mother and younger brother in Han be rewarded. Zhang agrees and gives him valuables.

The wider plan fails after one participant informs. Yu Chang is captured alive, interrogated, and implicates Zhang Sheng. Wei Lü later has him executed in front of the Han envoys as part of the coercive surrender scene.

## Key Characteristics

- Han-connected plot participant operating inside Xiongnu territory.
- Uses his prior acquaintance with Zhang Sheng to seek sponsorship and family rewards.
- Joins a wider plan involving court abduction and the killing of Wei Lü.
- Is captured after the plan is disclosed and the plotters are attacked.
- Implicates Zhang Sheng under investigation and is later executed before the mission.
