# Imbangala

## Overview

The Imbangala are the warrior group the episode uses to explain a hard turn in [[QueenNjinga]]'s military survival after Portuguese pressure.

## Current Profile

In the source, Njinga allies with the Imbangala leader Kasanje after a major defeat in the late 1620s and adopts Imbangala rituals to gain military support. The episode treats reports of blood rituals and baby-related rites as uncertain: they may reflect real practices, hostile propaganda, metaphor, or deliberate fear-making performance. The Imbangala therefore matter as both military capacity and source-critical problem.

## Key Characteristics

- Military partner in Njinga's post-defeat strategy.
- Associated in the source with Kasanje as a named leader.
- Linked to reported rituals whose factual status is uncertain.
- Example of how survival alliances can deepen [[HeroicResistanceViolenceAmbiguity]].
