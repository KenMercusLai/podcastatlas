# Okinawa

## Overview
Okinawa is a strategically located Japanese prefecture in the First Island Chain whose concentration of U.S. military facilities makes local government central to the [[UnitedStates]]-[[Japan]] security relationship.

## Current Profile
The source presents Okinawa as carrying an unusually heavy base burden while remaining one of Japan's most economically marginalized prefectures. [[KojaGentar]]'s 2026 landslide suggests that income, child poverty, and fatigue with unsuccessful legal resistance can outweigh base opposition electorally without erasing mixed local feelings.

## Key Characteristics
- Strategic proximity to [[Taiwan]] makes Okinawa important to deterrence planning toward [[China]].
- Base politics are shaped by noise, pollution, accidents, crime, and limits on local oversight.
- Ryukyuan annexation, the Battle of Okinawa, and postwar American occupation reinforce a sense of unequal sacrifice.
- Low incomes and high child poverty make economic delivery a competing political priority.
- The [[Henoko]] dispute remains the clearest test of local resistance to Tokyo-backed base policy.

### Strategic position and base burden
- Source grounding - [[rise-and-shine-warshs-fed-rate-test-6aabb60b964bc344092c48cd]] links the prefecture's geography to U.S.-Japan deterrence plans and describes persistent local costs from the bases.

### Electoral reprioritization
- Source grounding - [[rise-and-shine-warshs-fed-rate-test-6aabb60b964bc344092c48cd]] reports that Koja defeated [[TamakiDenny]] after campaigning on incomes and supporting Henoko.

## Qualifications
- One election does not establish that Okinawans have become uniformly pro-base.
- Historical, legal, and socioeconomic claims are summarized from one reporting segment rather than a complete history of Okinawa.

## What Changed
- The 2026 election moved economic performance ahead of base resistance as the decisive campaign issue in this source.
- A pro-Henoko governor may lower local political resistance to Japan's southwest defense build-up.
