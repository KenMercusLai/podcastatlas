# Hardcore History

## Overview

Hardcore History is [[DanCarlin]]'s history podcast, represented here through a crossover with [[TheRestIsHistory]].

## Current Profile

The crossover associates Hardcore History with large comparative questions rather than a single chronological narrative: whether history improves life, how to judge compromised figures, how contingent the Cold War was, why leaders miscalculate, and what source mediation permits historians to know.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses broad historical comparison and counterfactual questions to test present assumptions.
- Emphasizes contingency, uncertainty, political psychology, and the lived force of historical belief.
- Connects ancient source problems to modern propaganda and intelligence failure.
