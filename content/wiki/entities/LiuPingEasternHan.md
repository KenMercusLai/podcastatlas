## Overview

刘平 / Liu Ping is an Eastern Han local official whose current wiki profile comes from his Quanjiao county governance in Hanji 735. The episode treats him as a compact example of beneficial administration producing voluntary trust rather than merely coerced compliance.

## Current Profile

Liu Ping governs Quanjiao in a way that leaves unusually strong civic trust signals. The account emphasizes people voluntarily increasing their reported property in order to pay more tax, lowering their declared age so they can serve, and leaving prisons nearly empty. These details make him a local-government exemplar rather than just a bureaucratic name.

His route upward also matters. Zhongli Yi and another official recommend him, and he is appointed as a Lang. In the current evidence, Liu Ping therefore stands at the hinge between county-level humane governance and the central court's ability to identify administrative talent.

## Key Characteristics

- Local administrator remembered through voluntary tax and service behavior by the people under his rule.
- Low-coercion governor whose prisons are described as nearly empty.
- Beneficial official whose local record is legible enough to be recommended for central service.
- Evidence point for trust-based administration in early Yongping politics.
