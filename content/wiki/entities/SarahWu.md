# Sarah Wu

Sarah Wu appears in [[bombs-and-bombshells-zelensky-under-pressure-6a86d3561ad8716bb64646f0]] as the [[TheIntelligence]] contributor explaining [[China]]'s mental-health treatment gap. Her segment connects rising need, lower stigma after pandemic lockdowns, shortages of qualified professionals, high therapy costs, and weak regulation of counseling services.

Wu's main wiki role is to turn mental health into both a public-health and governance story. She describes a government plan for nationwide psychological support and crisis intervention by 2030, while also noting that the push reflects social-stability concerns after violent attacks and other public incidents.
