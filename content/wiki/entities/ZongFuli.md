# 宗馥莉 / Zong Fuli

## Overview
宗馥莉 is the successor figure at [[WahahaGroup|娃哈哈]], covered by current sources as both a reforming operator and a focal point for post-founder governance conflict.

## Current Profile
Her profile now includes operating background before the public controversy: studying abroad, taking over [[HongshengSystem|宏胜/红盛系]] production work, joining Wahaha's brand and management transition, replacing a long-time spokesperson, and attempting youth-facing products, e-commerce, product-line cuts, and sugar-free variants. The 不熄灯 source then frames her resignation and trademark disputes as signs that successor control does not automatically inherit [[ZongQinghou|宗庆后]]'s legitimacy or resolve old ownership and affiliate structures.

## Key Characteristics
- Second-generation successor linked to Hongsheng operations before formal group-level succession.
- Reform-oriented manager attempting brand renewal, product pruning, professionalization, e-commerce, and sugar-free product adaptation.
- Public legitimacy is constrained by her father's founder aura and by old Wahaha stakeholder structures.
- Her disputes are best read as governance and channel-control problems, not only family drama.
- Rumors and investigation claims around her position remain uncertain in the source record.
