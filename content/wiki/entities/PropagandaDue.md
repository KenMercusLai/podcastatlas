# Propaganda Due / P2

## Overview

Propaganda Due, commonly called P2, was an Italian Masonic lodge presented in the episode as a corrupt network organized under Licio Gelli.

## Current Profile

P2 is the episode's limiting case against treating every conspiracy allegation as fantasy. Dickey describes Gelli converting a lodge into a system of patronage and blackmail linked to right-wing terrorism, mafia money laundering, Vatican-connected finance, and the Banco Ambrosiano collapse. The case shows that secrecy and fraternal access can provide infrastructure for genuine covert coordination without validating unrelated totalizing theories.

## Key Characteristics

- It used a lodge structure as a cross-institutional patronage and influence network.
- The episode attributes leadership of the corrupt system to former fascist Licio Gelli.
- It connected political extremism, organized-crime finance, banking, and elite access in the source's account.
- Its reality gives evidence-bound inquiry a reason to test specific conspiracy claims rather than dismissing or universalizing them.

## Evidence

### Network capture

- [[335-the-freemasons-historys-greatest-conspiracy-theory-glt4122245847]] describes Gelli's use of P2 for patronage, blackmail, corrupt coordination, and the funding of right-wing terrorism.

### Financial and criminal entanglement

- [[335-the-freemasons-historys-greatest-conspiracy-theory-glt4122245847]] connects P2 to mafia and Vatican money, Roberto Calvi, and the collapse of Banco Ambrosiano.

## Qualifications

The episode's account is compressed and does not independently establish each allegation, legal finding, financial path, or participant's responsibility. It calls mafia retaliation the most likely explanation for Calvi's death but explicitly does not present that conclusion as certain.
