# Laurie Barber

Laurie Barber is [[AndyBarber|Andy Barber]]'s wife and [[JacobBarber|Jacob Barber]]'s mother in [[DefendingJacob|《捍卫雅各布》 / Defending Jacob]], discussed in [[24-hanwei-yagebu-xiangxin-ni-shi-wo-weiyi-de-zhinian-576218441]]. The episode reads her movement from ordinary maternal concern into terror, self-blame, isolation, and finally a fatal car crash as one of the novel's hardest ethical arcs.

Laurie's role complicates [[ParentalDefenseConflict]]. Andy's love becomes belief and cover-up; Laurie's love becomes fear that her child may harm others. The source does not present her final action as a clean answer, but it uses her to ask what responsibility means when legal certainty, parental knowledge, and future danger do not align.
