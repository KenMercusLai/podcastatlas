# Tencent CSIG

Tencent CSIG is the Tencent business group that [[yao-shunyu-laidao-tengxun-300tian-1-176-1]] presents as the main concentration point for Tencent's AI applications outside WeChat. The source lists [[Yuanbao]], [[TencentWorkBuddy]], CodeBuddy, IMA, Marvis, and design-agent work as examples of AI products tied to this side of [[Tencent]].

In the source, CSIG matters because model capability has to connect to products and data loops. [[TencentHunyuan]] can become more useful when CSIG products provide seed users, feedback, work traces, and post-training tasks; the same structure also shows why [[WeChat]] remains exceptional, because WeChat's AI path is not simply folded into the CSIG-Hunyuan stack.
