# Vandal Kingdom / 汪达尔王国

## Overview
The Vandal Kingdom / 汪达尔王国 appears in the Ceuta source as a late-antique North African power that disrupted Roman networks and briefly controlled [[Ceuta]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents the Vandals as a Germanic group that crossed Gaul and Iberia before entering North Africa in 429 under Geiseric. Their seizure of Carthage, naval activity, and 455 sack of Rome make them part of the wider breakdown of western Roman Mediterranean order.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents late-antique migration and military reordering in North Africa.
- Damaged Roman grain and trade networks by taking Carthage and North African coastlines.
- Adds one control layer to Ceuta's borderland history before Byzantine reconquest.

- Mediterranean-disruption evidence: [[496-yiminchao-beihou-de-xiuda-cong-gudian-shidai-manzu-duhai-yibiliya-shoufu-shidi-yundong-dao-putaoya-dahanghai-lu8pawnkiselmmrgubrey4pq-4fw]] connects Vandal control of Carthage with pressure on Roman grain, trade, islands, and Rome itself.

## Qualifications
The page records the source's Vandal role in Ceuta's history and does not resolve debates over Bonifacius's alleged invitation or broader Vandal governance.
