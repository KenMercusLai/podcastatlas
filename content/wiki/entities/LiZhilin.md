# Li Zhilin / 李志林

## Overview

Li Zhilin / 李志林 is the guest in a [[ZhixingXiaojiuguan|知行小酒馆]] discussion of AI, inequality, effective giving, and the practical delivery of health, education, and employment support in underserved regions.

## Current Profile

The available source presents Li as a philanthropy practitioner who works between technology entrepreneurs and donors on one side and older people, children, teachers, and families in less-developed regions on the other. Her argument is neither anti-technology nor automatically optimistic: AI can extend scarce expertise, but public benefit depends on infrastructure, human trust, local execution, and deliberate distribution.

She describes committing 10% of her personal income to giving since 2019 and helping promote a recurring 1% income pledge. Her approach emphasizes evidence, cost effectiveness, continuing action despite imperfect delivery, and honest public discussion that makes ordinary participation feel possible.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses frontline philanthropic work to test broad claims about who benefits from technical progress.
- Favors outcome-oriented donation decisions over donor satisfaction or symbolic goods alone.
- Treats human relationships, trust, transport, training, and supervision as core delivery infrastructure.
- Supports recurring personal giving and founder commitments as practical third-distribution mechanisms.
- Holds that technological potential and distributional criticism can both be valid at once.
