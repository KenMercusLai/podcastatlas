# 庐江郡 / Lujiang Commandery

庐江郡 / Lujiang Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] as the commandery held by [[LuKangLateHan|陆康]] when [[YuanShu|袁术]] demands grain. When Lu Kang refuses, Yuan Shu orders [[SunCe|孙策]] to attack and promises that Lujiang will become Sun Ce's reward if he wins.

The source makes Lujiang a credibility test. Sun Ce does win the commandery, but Yuan Shu appoints [[LiuXunLateHan|刘勋]] instead. Lujiang therefore becomes the second concrete office in Yuan Shu's broken-promise chain after [[JiujiangCommandery|九江郡]], and it helps explain why Sun Ce's later departure from Yuan Shu is not only ambition but accumulated distrust.
