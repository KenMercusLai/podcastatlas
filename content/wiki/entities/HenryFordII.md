# Henry Ford II

Henry Ford II is the [[FordMotorCompany]] executive whose failed attempt to buy [[Ferrari]] becomes a major turning point in [[ferrari-1]]. The source says Ford pursued Ferrari to gain racing credibility, negotiated a proposed deal, and then lost it when [[EnzoFerrari]] realized Ford would control racing budgets.

The failed acquisition matters because it clarified Ferrari's priority order. Enzo would consider industrial backing, but not at the cost of [[ScuderiaFerrari]]'s racing autonomy.
