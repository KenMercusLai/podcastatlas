# 子西 / Zi Xi (Chu)

子西 / Zi Xi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-2-zanmen-dou-yuanwang-le-beifu-2000-duonian-heiguo-de-yegong-haolong-lh-h-gezpqtvnyq40zlqac8s-5e]] as the Chu lingyin whose judgment about [[BaiGongSheng|白公胜]] fails. He sees Bai as trustworthy, brave, and useful because Bai knows [[WuState|吴国]] and can serve near the frontier. [[YeGong|叶公]] warns him that those traits are dangerous if they are not restrained by 仁义, but Zi Xi recalls Bai anyway and grants him Bai county.

Zi Xi's later policy reversal exposes that mistake. Bai wants Chu to attack [[ZhengState|郑国]] to avenge [[TaiziJianChu|太子建]], but Zi Xi first delays the campaign and then rescues Zheng when [[JinState|晋国]] attacks it. In the episode's reading, this turns Bai's apparent trust and courage into open resentment; Bai eventually kills Zi Xi during the [[BaiGongShengRebellion|白公胜之乱]].
