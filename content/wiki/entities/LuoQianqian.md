# Luo Qianqian / 雒芊芊

Luo Qianqian / 雒芊芊 appears in [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]] as [[ChenTianqiao]]'s spouse and early partner. The episode places their relationship in the Jin Xin Trust period and notes that family capital and support are part of the background to [[Shanda|盛大]]'s early startup path.

She later reappears in the source through the Chen Tianqiao and Luo Qianqian research institute, the neuroscience philanthropy vehicle that connects Chen's health crisis, wealth, and second act after Shanda. That makes Luo a bridge between the early [[ChineseOnlineGameCommercialization]] story and the later [[FounderHealthStopSign]]/brain-science branch.
