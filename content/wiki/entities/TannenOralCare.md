# Tannen Oral Care

Tannen Oral Care is the natural mouth-rinse company introduced by [[HenryDavis]] in [[advice-line-with-bobbi-brown-of-jones-road-beauty-september-2025-427590c2-11bc-43fa-9c92-d1eccf05842a]]. The source says the product began as a post-coffee rinse, with the brand name referring to tannins in coffee, wine, tea, plants, and trees as well as natural ingredients.

The episode treats Tannen as a [[NicheOriginStoryRepositioning]] case. [[BobbiBrown]] likes the brand's Instagram and vibe but worries coffee-specific packaging may repel non-coffee drinkers. The suggested path is to keep coffee as an origin story while clarifying broader use cases, ingredients, oral microbiome benefits, taste, and initial communities such as gyms, dentist offices, cold plunges, saunas, and wellness circles.
