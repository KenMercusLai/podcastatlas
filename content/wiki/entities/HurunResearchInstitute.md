# Hurun Research Institute / 胡润研究院

## Overview
Hurun Research Institute is the ranking organization attributed by the bounded source with the 2026 Hurun China Rich List and its interpretation of AI as a major wealth-growth engine.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Hurun's ranking to connect founder wealth with company valuation and upstream AI infrastructure. It reports more than 1,500 entrepreneurs above the ranking threshold, places [[ZhangYiming|张一鸣]] first, and attributes more than 160 listed fortunes to AI-related activity. These are ranking estimates and classifications, not audited personal balance sheets or a complete measure of how AI gains are distributed across society.

## Key Characteristics
- Produces entrepreneur wealth rankings and accompanying sector interpretations.
- Uses estimated personal wealth rather than audited household accounts.
- In the bounded source, classifies AI as a leading driver of wealth growth.
- Extends the AI category upstream to chips, optical modules, and compute infrastructure.

- **Ranking role:** [[dafeixin-pingguo-xin-xitong-keyi-jinzhi-yaoyiyao-guanggao-zhangyiming-zai-deng-hurun-baifu-bangshou-gkwridoot6kmafx6cwtxdrfn]] attributes the 2026 rich-list counts and Zhang Yiming ranking to Hurun.

## Qualifications
The supplied episode does not include Hurun's methodology, valuation date, ownership adjustments, uncertainty ranges, category definitions, or full dataset. Ranking wealth can move with private-company estimates and public-market prices and should not be read as liquid cash or a population-wide distribution measure.

## What Changed
- Created a bounded profile distinguishing ranking estimates from audited wealth.
- Captured Hurun's source-attributed extension of AI wealth into upstream compute hardware.
