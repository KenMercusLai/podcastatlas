# Nazi Party

## Overview

The Nazi Party was the National Socialist German Workers' Party, formed from the small German Workers' Party and increasingly controlled by [[AdolfHitler]] after his entry into Munich politics.

## Current Profile

The episode locates the party's beginnings in postwar Munich's hard-right, pan-German, antisemitic environment. Army superiors sent Hitler to observe the German Workers' Party; his intervention at a meeting impressed founder Anton Drexler, and he joined the group before becoming its star speaker. Renamed in February 1920, the organization fused racial antisemitism, living-space claims, Versailles grievance, hatred of the “November criminals,” political violence, and mass rhetoric.

The movement was not yet the national electoral machine described in later episodes. By 1921 Hitler controlled it, but he still sometimes imagined himself as the propagandist or “drummer” for another leader. The source therefore captures an early organizational transition from a small circle to a leader-centered movement without making later success inevitable.

## Key Characteristics

- Originated in the German Workers' Party founded by Anton Drexler in 1919.
- Emerged from Munich's hard-right pan-German and antisemitic milieu.
- Used simple, emotional speaking and scapegoating to organize diffuse postwar grievance.
- Renamed the National Socialist German Workers' Party in February 1920.
- Came under Hitler's control by 1921.
- Joined ideology, propaganda, organization, and willingness to use violence.
