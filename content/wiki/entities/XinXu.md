# 《新序》 / Xin Xu

《新序》 / Xin Xu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-2-zanmen-dou-yuanwang-le-beifu-2000-duonian-heiguo-de-yegong-haolong-lh-h-gezpqtvnyq40zlqac8s-5e]] as the [[LiuXiang|刘向]]-compiled Western Han story collection that preserves the [[YeGongHaolong|叶公好龙]] anecdote. The episode describes the work as drawing on transmitted stories from Zhou through Western Han and using them to promote humane governance.

In this ingest, Xin Xu matters as a source layer rather than as a neutral container of fact. The host treats the Ye Gong story as a moralizing anecdote whose later idiom life damaged [[YeGong|叶公]]'s reputation, so the page belongs with the wiki's source-critical treatment of memorable but suspect origin stories.
