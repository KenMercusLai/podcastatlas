# 芒卯 / Mang Mao

芒卯 / Mang Mao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-67-zhanguo-mieba-baiqi-de-junshi-caineng-jiujing-ruhe-ljw0lltowxd4qz75v-wcnlkwqskv]] as a [[WeiState|魏国]] military and persuasion figure who becomes visible after [[YiqueBattle|伊阙之战]]. The episode says Han and Wei are terrified after the defeat, cede land to [[QinState|秦国]], and that Mang Mao begins to be valued by different states because he is known for "诈说."

The source deliberately keeps his image complex. [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] presents him as skilled at securing benefits for himself; [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] later names him with [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君 / 田文]] as a worthy figure; [[Huainanzi|《淮南子》]] is used to argue that judgment should focus on major integrity rather than small blemishes; and [[HanFei|韩非]] preserves a story in which Mang Mao talks back Qin, Han, Qi, and Wei forces attacking Wei, then bargains when Wei rewards him.

For this wiki branch, Mang Mao is a case where Warring States mobility and persuasive skill cannot be reduced to clean moral categories. He belongs beside [[WarringStatesCareeristMobility|乱世职业经理人式流动]] and [[ZonghengjiaDiplomacy|纵横家外交]], but with more emphasis on the gray zone between trickery, self-interest, and practical state rescue.
