# Keep / Keep Fitness

Keep appears in [[no-214-xunzhao-tonglei-xiaohongshu-bilibili-yiji-wuhuabamen-de-naxie-shequ-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-26-1005977305]] as a fitness-tool-to-community case founded by [[WangNingKeep|王宁]] in 2014. The episode says Keep began as a mobile fitness coach, but quickly used training data, check-ins, and community interaction rather than only static workout instruction.

The source uses Keep to show a different community attachment point from Xiaohongshu or Bilibili: exercise repetition, visible progress, medals, virtual races, apparel, equipment, food, and offline spaces can turn a tool into a lifestyle and identity loop.
