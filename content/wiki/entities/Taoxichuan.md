# Taoxichuan / 陶溪川

Taoxichuan / 陶溪川 is the former factory-area cultural district in [[Jingdezhen]] discussed in [[no-213-jingdezhen-wangshi-qiannian-shijie-cidu-de-qianshi-jinsheng-1004755625]]. The episode says the old Cosmos porcelain factory area was restored and opened formally in 2016, combining museums, galleries, studios, shops, and markets.

Taoxichuan matters because it turns state-factory decline into a contemporary [[HeritageTourismCommercialization]] and [[LongTermPlaceOperation]] case. The source also keeps the risk visible: creative market traffic, copyright pressure, product homogeneity, and [[CeramicLivestreamCommerceRisk]] can grow alongside tourism and creator density.
