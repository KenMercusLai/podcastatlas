## Overview

吕媭 / Lü Xu is presented as [[LuZhi|吕雉]]'s sister, [[FanKuai|樊哙]]'s widow, a rare early-Western-Han female marquis, and a politically active member of the Lü family who is killed in the post-Lü purge.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-207-liubang-xiaoyizi-lvxu-bei-gunsha-1-lsrs42rb8-qref1eevqdnhe3mufv|Hanji 207 part 1]] says Lü Xu resented [[ChenPing|陈平]] because of his earlier arrest of Fan Kuai and repeatedly accused him before Lü Zhi of neglecting government for drinking and women. Chen Ping's reported response was to intensify the visible behavior, which the episode interprets as political self-protection rather than proof that the accusations were accurate.

The same source says Lü Xu was made Lingguang Hou in 184 BCE and exercised power during Lü Zhi's rule. After the anti-Lü coalition won, she was beaten to death and her son Fan Kang was also killed. Her case therefore joins personal enmity, unusual female rank, outer-relative participation, and collective political liquidation without establishing a complete record of her offices or conduct.

## Key Characteristics

- Sister of Lü Zhi and widow of Fan Kuai inside the founding imperial kin network.
- Female marquis whose title is presented as unusual but not unique in early Western Han.
- Political accuser of Chen Ping after the Fan Kuai arrest.
- Lü-family power participant in the episode's account.
- Victim of lethal beating during the broader post-Lü purge.

## Evidence

Kinship, title, and political conflict:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-207-liubang-xiaoyizi-lvxu-bei-gunsha-1-lsrs42rb8-qref1eevqdnhe3mufv|Hanji 207 part 1]] identifies Lü Xu as Lü Zhi's sister and Fan Kuai's widow, describes her accusation against Chen Ping, and reports her enfeoffment as Lingguang Hou.

Post-coup death:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-207-liubang-xiaoyizi-lvxu-bei-gunsha-1-lsrs42rb8-qref1eevqdnhe3mufv|Hanji 207 part 1]] says Lü Xu is beaten to death and Fan Kang is killed during the winning coalition's destruction of the Lü network.

## Qualifications

This one-source profile does not establish Lü Xu's full title history, formal governing authority, the truth of her claims about Chen Ping, the method and procedure of her death, or Fan Kang's exact role. “专权” and the claim of broad public resentment remain the episode's compression pending comparison with transmitted histories.

## What Changed

- Created a bounded profile separating Lü Xu's reported title and political activity from the post-coup collective punishment that ended her life.
