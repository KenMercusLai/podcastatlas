# Matilda Gould

Matilda Gould appears in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as a 77-year-old Protestant grandmother fatally injured in May 1966 when loyalists targeted what they thought was a Catholic-owned pub but hit the house next door. The episode uses her death to show how sectarian violence could misfire even on the community it claimed to defend.

For the wiki, Gould is a casualty marker in the early [[UlsterVolunteerForce|UVF]] violence branch. Her death precedes the later attacks on [[JohnPatrickScullion]] and [[PeterWard]], underlining the source's claim that violence was chosen into being before the better-known 1968-1969 escalation.
