# 魏源 / Wei Yuan (Qing thinker)

## Overview
魏源 appears as a mid-Qing statecraft thinker whose administrative experience, New Text learning, Daoist resources, and historical comparison supported a reformist diagnosis of decline.

## Current Profile
The source presents Wei as both a long-serving private secretary and a thinker connected to the 《皇朝经世文编》. His “简易之道” seeks to simplify overgrown institutions so that rules become inspectable and usable again; his reformism is neither passive non-action nor a claim that dynastic collapse is inevitable.

## Key Characteristics
- Joined practical administrative exposure to classical and historical interpretation.
- Used reform within a low-coercion or “non-action” frame rather than pure passivity.
- Favored institutional simplification, usability, and restored vitality.
- Treated failures at the center and in local implementation as strategic weaknesses before foreign war.
- Remained hopeful that policy adjustment could produce revival.
