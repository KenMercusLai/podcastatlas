# Sina / 新浪

Sina / 新浪 appears in [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]] through [[Shanda|盛大]]'s 2005 purchase of a large ordinary-share stake. The episode frames it as a landmark attempted hostile acquisition in Chinese internet history and as part of [[ChenTianqiao]]'s search for a stronger entry point beyond games.

The source links the Sina move to [[FamilyInternetPortalStrategy]]: Shanda needed distribution, users, media reach, and bargaining power to make the home-entertainment ecosystem plausible. Sina's poison-pill response and Shanda's later sale of the shares made the episode's point that capital-market boldness did not solve the product and infrastructure problems around [[ShandaBox|盛大盒子]].
