# 中国西部科学院 / Chinese Western Academy of Sciences

## Overview

[[ChineseWesternAcademyOfSciences|中国西部科学院]] is presented as a [[LuZuofu|卢作孚]]-founded research institution in [[BeibeiDistrict|重庆北碚]] focused on western Chinese resources and practical regional production problems.

## Current Profile

The bounded source places agricultural, forestry, mining, and biological work inside a broader “scientific revolution in the countryside.” Its research orientation was practical, resource-focused, and close to daily production: surveys of southwestern minerals and life, cultivation, mining, agriculture, and local living conditions. The institution therefore represents [[AppliedScienceForRegionalDevelopment]], where scientific capacity is valued for making a region more legible and workable as well as for discovery.

This local base also preceded wartime migration of universities and research institutes into Beibei. The source suggests continuity between prewar institution building and the later education-and-science cluster, but does not quantify the academy's independent scientific output or causal contribution.

## Key Characteristics

- Research institution embedded in a wider place-building program.
- Focused on agriculture, forestry, mining, biology, and western resources.
- Treated surveys and practical technology as scientifically and socially valuable.
- Linked knowledge production to local industry and ordinary rural life.
- Contributed to the institutional base later used by wartime academic migration.
