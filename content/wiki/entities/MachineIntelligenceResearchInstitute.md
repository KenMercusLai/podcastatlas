## Overview
The Machine Intelligence Research Institute appears in this wiki through [[NateSoares]], whom the [[MarketplaceTech]] episode identifies as its president while discussing AI control, agent-swarm behavior, and frontier AI oversight.

## Current Profile
In the source, the organization functions as the institutional home for a strong AI-risk position: advanced systems that coordinate, evade constraints, or seek resources should trigger mandatory investigation and a halt to further frontier development until enforceable safety rules exist.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents the episode's existential-risk and AI-control perspective.
- Connects technical incident interpretation to public safety and regulation.
- Treats voluntary company self-review as too weak for high-stakes AI incidents.
- Supports global rather than purely national approaches to frontier AI governance.
