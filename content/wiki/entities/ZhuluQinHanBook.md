# 《逐鹿：神话与预言背后的秦亡汉兴》

## Overview
《逐鹿：神话与预言背后的秦亡汉兴》 enters the wiki through a [[HuzuoHuyou|忽左忽右]] interview with [[ZhangShiping|张诗平 / 张诗坪]]. The episode uses the book as a launch point for re-reading the fall of [[QinState|Qin]], the anti-Qin wars, and the Chu-Han struggle.

## Current Profile
The source presents the book as a military-history and source-critical interpretation of Qin collapse and Han rise. Its public-facing value in the episode is not a full chapter-by-chapter summary, but a set of arguments: familiar stories about prophecy, heroic battle, noble tragedy, and miraculous strategy need to be checked against state capacity, political legitimacy, supply lines, troop composition, and source transmission. The book therefore anchors [[ChuHanWarDemythologizing|楚汉战争去神话化]] and [[QinCollapseOverextension|秦亡多因素解释]] in the wiki.

## Key Characteristics
- Focuses on the Qin-to-Han transition, especially anti-Qin war and Chu-Han conflict.
- Uses military history to reinterpret familiar personalities and battles.
- Treats myth, prophecy, and literary drama as materials to be explained, not simply repeated.
- Links local Chinese military developments to wider Eurasian ancient-war comparisons.
- Keeps several force-number and cavalry-technology claims qualified rather than closed.
