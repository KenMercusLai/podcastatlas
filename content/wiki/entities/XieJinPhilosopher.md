# 谢金 / Xie Jin

## Overview
谢金 / Xie Jin appears in [[shi-di-fu-shuo-464-xiejin-women-zhichi-pingdeng-dan-moren-butongderen-peide-butongdeshenghuo-lsYpOsseeWaQIMMSv6Q2lyGsOCSw]] as a Fudan University philosophy associate professor interviewed by [[SteveShiDiFuShuoHost|Steve]] on [[ShiDiFuShuo|史蒂夫说]]. The source identifies her research areas as social philosophy, political philosophy, structural anthropology, modern critique, and gender studies, and connects her public work to [[Xiaohongshu]]'s RedX knowledge project.

## Current Profile
In the episode, Xie Jin functions as a public philosopher who translates feminist and social-philosophical concepts into everyday examples: psychological help-seeking, emotional labor, puberty and menopause, pregnancy, public seating, disability access, relationship specialization, gossip, online criticism, and parenting. Her core posture is anti-preachy but rigorous: philosophy should not hand down a single correct life script; it should give people tools for naming experience, seeing structural patterns, and noticing when equality claims hide practical hierarchy.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats philosophy as portable thinking equipment rather than paternalistic instruction.
- Uses feminism as a method for seeing many forms of exclusion, not only topics explicitly about women.
- Connects body experience, public space, and language to power and recognition.
- Reads psychological counseling partly as a speech channel for people whose ordinary environments do not hear them.
- Treats vulnerability as bodily, social, environmental, and emotional rather than only a private feeling.
- Frames good life as relational and non-extractive: being able to be happy together with others.
