# Stelato / 享界

Stelato / 享界 is the [[BAICGroup|北汽]] and [[Huawei]] brand branch in [[HongmengZhixing]]. [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] presents the S9 as a high-end sedan case, with launch pricing around RMB 399,800 and 449,800 in the source's account.

The source uses 享界 to show the importance of product-position repair inside a crowded Chinese EV market. It says the S9 later led the 400,000-yuan-plus pure-electric sedan segment for consecutive weeks, and that adding a range-extended version helped the brand reach a stronger monthly delivery position in the 300,000-yuan-plus new-energy sedan field.
