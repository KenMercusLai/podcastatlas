# Anjoy Foods / 安井食品

## Overview

Anjoy Foods / 安井食品 is a Chinese frozen-food company described in the source as expanding from products such as shrimp paste and fish balls into frozen bakery.

## Current Profile

The episode presents bakery as Anjoy's fastest-growing reported business and says the company has used acquisitions to expand into bagels, toast, frozen cakes, and desserts. This moves the company closer to the upstream supply needs of supermarkets, cafés, and convenience stores that want differentiated bakery without operating a full on-site production system. The evidence is a short business-news summary rather than audited segment reporting or an acquisition review.

## Key Characteristics

- Established frozen-food supplier associated with savory hot-pot products.
- Reported high-growth entrant in frozen bakery.
- Uses acquisition to broaden category capability and customer access.
- Business-to-business supplier whose buyers value price, consistency, recipes, differentiation, and margin.
- Exposed to channel questions as some upstream suppliers open low-price consumer-facing factory stores.
