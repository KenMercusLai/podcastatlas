# 鹿毛寿 / Lu Maoshou

鹿毛寿 / Lu Maoshou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-47-yanwangkuai-de-lixiangzhuyi-lhgl6rqkv9nonzxc9xqquzzlz5pr]] as the persuader who turns [[YanWangKuai|燕王哙]]'s trust in [[ZiZhi|子之]] into an abdication-style power transfer. The source presents him as part of the same manipulation chain as [[SuDai|苏代]], but his specialty is ancient precedent rather than interstate comparison.

Lu Maoshou first invokes Yao and Xu You to make yielding authority look like a reputation win: the ruler can appear humble and worthy while still expecting not to lose the state. He then invokes Yu, Boyi, and Qi to argue that a nominal successor gains nothing unless he also receives the offices and people who make succession real.

In the episode's interpretation, this is [[ChuaimoTechnique|揣摩]] aimed at Yan Wang Kuai's weakest motives: self-doubt after failed politics, desire to become strong, and appetite for the moral image of ancient abdication. Lu Maoshou therefore belongs to the wiki's [[RuleBreakingAbdication|破坏规则式禅让]] branch.
