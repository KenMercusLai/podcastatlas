# 居武 / Ju Wu

居武 / Ju Wu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-2-yanguo-cisha-qinshihuang-weihe-hui-xuan-jingke-lsewjrmgup1yanf8ipn-i0ykqwjx]] as [[YanTaiziDan|燕太子丹]]'s teacher or adviser in [[YanState|燕国]]. When Dan asks how to answer [[QinState|秦国]] pressure, Ju Wu proposes the ordinary strategic route: invite [[WeiState|魏国]], [[ChuState|楚国]], and [[QiState|齐国]] back into [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] and also seek support from [[Xiongnu|匈奴]].

The episode makes Ju Wu the voice of slow-statecraft caution. He also warns that sheltering the Qin fugitive [[FanWuji|樊无忌]] is dangerous and recommends sending Fan Wuji into Xiongnu territory rather than keeping him in Yan. Taizi Dan refuses, choosing an honor claim toward a surrendered guest over Ju Wu's security logic.

Ju Wu does not become the architect of the assassination plan. After Taizi Dan dismisses the coalition and fugitive-removal answers as too slow or dishonorable, Ju Wu recommends [[TianGuang|田光]]. The source therefore presents him as an adviser whose preferred strategy fails politically, but who still routes the prince toward the social world that produces [[JingKe|荆轲]].
