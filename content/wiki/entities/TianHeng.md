# 田横 / Tian Heng

田横 / Tian Heng first appears in the wiki background through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-8-yi-ming-zhaoguo-chuishibing-nixi-chengwei-waijiao-dachen-lhfwk-hjge5iuqet6astmbclhyri|Qinji 128-8]] as one of [[TianDanQinRebel|田儋]]'s Qi kinsmen at Di County, alongside [[TianRong|田荣]]. That source uses him to show that Tian Dan's anti-Qin restoration begins from a family network as well as a local uprising.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-3-weihe-li-mutong-wei-chuwang-limian-dayou-wenzhang-lmcuvrxer-cou6gmlppqwhb3n4vz|Qinji 130-3]] gives Tian Heng a more concrete office. After [[TianRong|田荣]] returns to [[QiState|齐国]], drives out [[TianJia|田假]], and installs [[TianDanQinRebel|田儋]]'s son as king, Tian Rong makes Tian Heng the Qi general.

The page therefore keeps Tian Heng tied to restored Qi's internal consolidation. In this source he is not the actor who blocks [[XiangLiang|项梁]]'s coalition request; that refusal is attributed to Tian Rong.
