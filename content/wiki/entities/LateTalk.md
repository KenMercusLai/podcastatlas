# LateTalk

LateTalk, or 晚点聊, is the podcast/show context for [[jushen-jibao-26q2-shijie-moxing-dafeng-buting-he-buxiang-bei-tie-biaoqian-de-ren-1-170-1]], [[ai-jibao-26q2-cong-coding-dao-rsi-qiangzhe-yu-qiang-de-weilai-1-171-1]], and [[167-yangcong-xueyuan-yang-lingfeng-yong-ai-zhizao-jiejing-shi-zai-shasi-zhenxuexi-1-167-1]]. The first source hosts a quarterly embodied-intelligence review with [[ChenZhePeter]], using industry events, robot demos, model releases, and commercialization signals to map the state of [[PhysicalAI]].

The Q2 2026 AI-quarter source adds [[HenryYin]] as a guest and shifts from embodied systems to frontier-model competition, [[Codex]] versus [[ClaudeCode]], [[AutoResearch]], [[RecursiveSelfImprovement]], enterprise-owned models, open-model substitution, computer use, and real-time voice.

[[166-xu-huazhe-zaici-jushen-chuangye-buxiang-cuoguo-zuida-de-xigua-1-166-1]] adds a founder-interview version of the same embodied-AI thread through [[XuHuazhe]] and [[PokeRobotics]]. The episode focuses less on quarterly industry mapping and more on why Xu believes [[PhysicalAGI]], household robots, [[AINativeRobotics]], and [[RobotActiveUseMetrics]] should define the next phase.

[[167-yangcong-xueyuan-yang-lingfeng-yong-ai-zhizao-jiejing-shi-zai-shasi-zhenxuexi-1-167-1]] adds an education and AI-learning branch through [[YangLingfeng]] and [[YangcongXueyuan]]. The episode connects [[SelfDirectedLearning]], [[LearningExperienceDesign]], [[AIAsTutor]], and [[AIShortcutRisk]] around the question of whether AI helps students think or merely gives them faster answers.

[[hanyang-wakeng-meijie-jiasuo-hao-neirong-duitan-weishenme-zuo-fuyou-tiandi-59b766f5-ee1d-4040-a650-a96b7b4b70d5]] mentions LateTalk as one of [[HanYang]]'s earlier content practices. In that source, the show functions less as a technical subject and more as part of the backstory for why [[FuyouTiandi]] tries to loosen [[MediaFormConstraint]] through [[LongFormConversation]].

[[momenta-ipo-hou-zai-fang-cao-xu-dong-jiu-shi-xiang-zuo-mei-you-jin-tou-de-ai-1-172-1]] adds an IPO-era founder interview with [[CaoXudong]] of [[Momenta]]. It connects LateTalk's embodied-AI thread to [[AutonomousDrivingDataFlywheel]], [[WorldModels]], [[PhysicalAI]], and [[LowCostShortCycleValidation]], using autonomous-driving commercialization as the bridge toward robotics.

[[173-duihua-yao-song-shenjian-dongfang-kongjian-zaichufa-tiancai-shaonian-shinian-hou-1-173-1]] adds [[YaoSong]] as a continuous hard-tech founder case. The episode ties LateTalk's AI and embodied-intelligence coverage back to [[DeePhiTech]], [[Xilinx]], [[Orienspace]], [[Gravity1Rocket]], and [[StridingAI]], making the show a place where Chinese AI-chip, commercial-space, and physical-intelligence routes can be compared through one founder's operating history.

[[ai-chongji-qiye-ruanjian-jutou-yu-sap-yuanxin-liao-damoxing-to-b-de-dianfu-yu-bianjie-1-174-1]] adds [[YuanXin]] of [[SAP]] and moves LateTalk's AI thread from frontier-model and embodied-intelligence reviews into enterprise software. The episode connects [[EnterpriseResourcePlanning]], [[ERPTrustMoat]], [[ForwardDeployedEngineer]], [[AutonomousEnterprise]], [[ChinaEnterpriseAISystemDebt]], and SAP's [[AlibabaCloud|Alibaba Cloud]] / [[Qwen]] / [[DingTalk]] China ecosystem route.

[[duihua-liblib-chenmian-guanyu-huoxialai-yiji-suoyou-jiejin-siwang-de-shike-1-175-1]] adds [[ChenMian]] of [[Evoken]] as an AI application-founder case. The episode connects [[Liblib]], [[Lavod]], and [[LibTV]] to [[AIApplicationSurvivalStrategy]], [[AIStartupUnitEconomics]], [[AISubscriptionEconomics]], [[ModelProviderToolCompetition]], and [[AIOrganizationDesign]], making LateTalk's AI coverage more directly about application-layer survival under model pressure.

[[yao-shunyu-laidao-tengxun-300tian-1-176-1]] adds [[GaoHonghao]]'s reporting-based discussion of [[YaoShunyu]]'s first 300 days at [[TencentHunyuan]]. It extends LateTalk's AI coverage from application survival and quarterly model reviews into large-company model-team reform, [[FederatedAIOrganization]], [[WeChatVLM]], and Tencent's tension between product-unit autonomy and centralized AI infrastructure.

[[xiangjie-kimi-k3-qiangdao-chongji-anthropic-guzhi-de-moxing-shenmeyang-1-177-1]] adds a technical-report deep dive on [[KimiK3|Kimi K3]] with [[ZhaoChenyang|赵晨阳]] and [[ZengZhiyuan|曾志远]]. The episode pushes LateTalk's AI branch from organization and product strategy into [[KimiDeltaAttention|KDA]], [[AttentionResidues]], [[QuantileBalancing]], [[PerHeadMuon]], [[KernelDevelopmentAgents]], [[AgentIn]], and the boundary between open weights and a repeatable model-development pipeline.
