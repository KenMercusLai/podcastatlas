## Overview

刘康 / Liu Kang, Dingtao King, enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-551-jiemi-hanchengdi-de-huangwei-chuancheng-zhimi-lkefo-pkg_n8ll57kdsofcrpad_v|Hanji 551]] as [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s brother and the father of the Dingtao king [[HanAidi|刘欣 / 后来的汉哀帝]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-498-taizi-liu-ao-huangyin-wudu-han-yuandi-weihe-bu-fei-le-ta-lnnuw3h3c8tqlf5ystx58gi7vvjo|Hanji 498]] adds an earlier layer: before Liu Ao becomes Chengdi, Liu Kang is the alternative son whom [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] considers favoring because of talent, music, [[FuZhaoyiWesternHan|傅昭仪]]'s favor, and illness-bed proximity.

## Current Profile

The current evidence is narrow but now spans two succession moments. Hanji 498 shows Liu Kang as a live alternative to Liu Ao in Yuan's final illness. Yuan praises his talent partly through musical performance, feels attended by him and Fu Zhaoyi, and considers the Emperor Jing precedent for replacing an heir. Liu Kang's candidacy fails not because the source proves him unfit, but because [[ShiDanWesternHan|史丹 / 施丹]] makes replacing the long-established crown prince politically dangerous.

Hanji 551 then shows Liu Kang posthumously shaping the next succession. The host says Chengdi and Liu Kang had a good relationship and suggests that Chengdi's earlier wish to use Liu Kang as a counterweight to Wang-family outer relatives may have ended with Liu Kang's unclear death. That makes Liu Xin's candidacy emotionally and politically legible: Chengdi can prefer his dead brother's son while also avoiding the ritual awkwardness of making a brother into a son.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Dingtao king and father of Liu Xin, later Han Aidi.
- Yuan-era alternative heir candidate whose branch nearly displaces Liu Ao before Chengdi's accession.
- Brother of Han Chengdi whose remembered closeness helps explain Chengdi's affection for Liu Xin.
- Source-scoped possible counterweight to Wang-family power before his death.
- Succession-background figure rather than a full biographical subject in the current wiki.

## Qualifications

This page is intentionally narrow. Hanji 498 does not settle Liu Kang's formal title chronology, full talents, or whether he would have governed better than Liu Ao. Hanji 551 does not provide Liu Kang's full life, death record, mother, household, or exact political role. The claim that Chengdi felt guilt or used Liu Kang against the Wang side remains the episode's interpretation.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 498's earlier alternative-heir layer, connecting Liu Kang's later Dingtao-line importance to his earlier role in Yuan's threatened replacement of Liu Ao.
