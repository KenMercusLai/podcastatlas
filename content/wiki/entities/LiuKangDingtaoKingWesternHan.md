## Overview

刘康 / Liu Kang, Dingtao King, enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-551-jiemi-hanchengdi-de-huangwei-chuancheng-zhimi-lkefo-pkg_n8ll57kdsofcrpad_v|Hanji 551]] as [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s brother and the father of the Dingtao king [[HanAidi|刘欣 / 后来的汉哀帝]]. The episode uses him to explain why Chengdi may have favored Liu Xin over [[LiuXingZhongshanKingWesternHan|中山王刘兴]]: Liu Kang had been close to Chengdi, his death is described as suspicious in the host's interpretation, and Liu Xin as nephew is ritually easier to adopt as son than a brother of Chengdi would be.

## Current Profile

The current evidence is narrow. Hanji 551 does not provide a full biography of Liu Kang; it uses him as succession background. The host says Chengdi and Liu Kang had a good relationship and suggests that Chengdi's earlier wish to use Liu Kang as a counterweight to Wang-family outer relatives may have ended with Liu Kang's unclear death. That makes Liu Xin's candidacy emotionally and politically legible: Chengdi can prefer his dead brother's son while also avoiding the ritual awkwardness of making a brother into a son.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Dingtao king and father of Liu Xin, later Han Aidi.
- Brother of Han Chengdi whose remembered closeness helps explain Chengdi's affection for Liu Xin.
- Source-scoped possible counterweight to Wang-family power before his death.
- Succession-background figure rather than a full biographical subject in the current wiki.
