# How Life Works

[[HowLifeWorks|How Life Works]] is the [[PhilipBall|Philip Ball]] book named in [[ep256-ai-shidai-ziyou-yizhi-hai-cunzai-ma-lk9aci8oqnwerk26jy683nbdddcx]] as part of [[TuMotuo|土摩托]]'s path into [[FreeWill|free will]]. The episode uses the book less as a standalone reading summary than as a bridge from life science to meaning and agency.

Its source-scoped role is to support the claim that [[MeaningAsEvolvedFunction|meaning]] can be understood as something life does, not only as a metaphysical supplement added after biology. That makes it adjacent to [[BiologicalAgency]] and [[EmbodiedIntelligence]].
