## Overview

季布 / Ji Bu is a former [[XiangYu|项羽]] general whose hostile service to [[LiuBang|刘邦]] is later reframed as loyal duty, enabling pardon and Han office. A later source places him as Hedong governor near [[ZhouBo|周勃]]'s fief but offers only a speculative theory that he received a covert order against Zhou Bo.

## Current Profile

The two Hanji 170 notes establish Ji Bu's secure current profile. Liu Bang first seeks him under severe bounty after Chu's defeat, but [[ZhuJiaLu|朱家]] shelters him and persuades [[XiahouYing|夏侯婴]] to distinguish faithful service to a former ruler from personal evil. Liu Bang pardons and appoints Ji Bu. Paired with [[DingGong|丁公]], who is executed after helping Liu Bang by betraying his old duty, Ji Bu becomes the positive case in [[FormerEnemyLoyaltyReframing|旧敌忠义重释]].

Hanji 215 places Ji Bu as Hedong commandery governor when Zhou Bo fears visits from local officials. The host infers from Ji Bu's courage, independence, and an earlier summons by [[LiuHeng|汉文帝刘恒]] that he might have been considered for covert violence against Zhou Bo. That inference is not supported by a direct order, attempt, witness, or independent corroboration and therefore does not alter Ji Bu's established profile as fact.

## Key Characteristics

- Former Chu commander whose attacks on Liu Bang are reclassified as office-bound loyalty.
- Fugitive protected by Zhu Jia and defended through Xiahou Ying before receiving pardon and office.
- Positive counterpart to Ding Gong in the new dynasty's public definition of loyalty.
- Later Hedong governor whose proximity to Zhou Bo becomes material for a speculative covert-action theory.

## Qualifications

The current evidence consists of three popular-history episode notes rather than a full biography. The rescue route, dialogue, appointment details, and Ding Gong comparison remain source-scoped. Most importantly, office, temperament, and temporal proximity do not establish an assassination instruction; the Hanji 215 theory must not be used as factual evidence that Ji Bu planned or attempted to kill Zhou Bo.
