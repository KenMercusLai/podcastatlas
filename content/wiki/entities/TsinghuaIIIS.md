# Tsinghua Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences / 清华大学交叉信息研究院

## Overview
The Tsinghua Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences, referred to as 清华叉院 in the source, is the [[TsinghuaUniversity|Tsinghua University]] institute where [[XuMengdi|徐梦迪]] serves as an assistant professor and builds a robot-learning group.

## Current Profile
In the bounded interview, the institute is presented as an academic setting that combines strong students, interdisciplinary work, research freedom, and the possibility of integrating hardware, models, data, and real deployment into complete robotic systems. This is Xu's reason for choosing the institute over a better-resourced industrial route: it supports long-horizon questions and gives students early access to real-machine research and lab building.

## Key Characteristics
- Supports interdisciplinary work across robot hardware, machine learning, data, and deployment.
- Offers academic freedom for long-horizon research questions.
- Provides access to strong students and an environment for building a new laboratory.
- Is presented as suitable for system-level robotics rather than algorithm work in isolation.
