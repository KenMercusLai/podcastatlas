# 张南 / Zhang Nan (late Han)

## Overview
张南 / Zhang Nan (late Han) enters the wiki through Hanji 1070 as the actor paired with [[JiaoChuLateHan|焦触]] in the Youzhou reversal against [[YuanXiLateHan|袁熙]]. This page keeps him distinct from [[ZhangNan|张楠]], the modern ByteDance product figure.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1070 gives Zhang Nan a narrow but important role. After Yuan Xi and [[YuanShang|袁尚]] try to preserve Yuan-family power through Youzhou and Wuhuan refuge, Zhang Nan joins Jiao Chu in attacking Yuan Xi. The source then lets Jiao Chu take the visible lead in the blood-oath surrender to [[CaoCao|曹操]], so Zhang Nan's current profile remains the paired military defection rather than the ritual organizer.

## Key Characteristics
- Late-Han Yuan-side subordinate in the Youzhou field.
- Paired with Jiao Chu in attacking Yuan Xi.
- Part of the immediate cause forcing Yuan Xi and Yuan Shang toward Wuhuan refuge.
- Distinct from the modern Zhang Nan page by period and role.
