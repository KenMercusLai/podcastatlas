## Overview

龚遂 / Gong Sui enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-425-huoguang-sihou-canzao-miemen-simaguang-weisha-fenfenbuping-lmz7bdfy8anhkuq24l-f2ulkolbu|Hanji 425]] as a Western Han official whose Bohai commandery governance becomes an exemplar of [[LivelihoodFirstPacification|民生优先安抚治理]] and [[HumaneLocalGovernance|仁厚地方治理]].

## Current Profile

The source gives Gong Sui a late-blooming official profile. He had earlier served [[LiuHeHaihunHou|刘贺]], remonstrated tearfully, and was spared by [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]] when Liu He fell, but still endured shaved-headed convict labor before later release. By the time [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] considered him for Bohai, he was old, thin, and unimpressive in appearance.

His governing value appears in diagnosis and method. Gong Sui tells Xuan that Bohai's "robbers" are fundamentally hungry commoners neglected by officials, then asks whether the emperor wants suppression or pacification. After securing room to act, he removes catch-bandit officials, distinguishes farmers' tools from weapons, opens granaries, models thrift, restores agricultural and livestock production, and personally inspects and persuades households. The episode presents his later Water-Balance Commandant appointment as a central-office honor and retirement-style reward rather than the best use of his local talents.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han clean-official figure whose early service under Liu He included direct remonstrance and later punishment.
- Elderly local-governance specialist whose value is initially hidden by unimpressive appearance.
- Diagnostic official who treats banditry as hunger plus official neglect before treating it as criminal identity.
- Pacification practitioner who asks for operational autonomy and uses classification rules to reduce confrontation.
- Relief-and-production administrator who combines granary release, thrift, agriculture, livestock, inspection, and persuasion.
- Late-recognized official recalled to central service as 水衡都尉 after Bohai's recovery.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 425. It does not reconstruct Gong Sui's full biography, exact Bohai tenure dates, the original Han Shu wording beyond the episode's retelling, or quantitative measures of crime reduction. The source treats 水衡都尉 as an honor and pension-like central role; that interpretation should remain source-scoped until supported elsewhere.
