# Phoebe Bridgers

## Overview
Phoebe Bridgers is a musician used in [[brazil-rut-lula-v-bolsonaro-again-6abb8653ed4e198024b38eb5]] as the lead example of an artist requiring phone-free concerts.

## Current Profile
The source says Bridgers has banned phones on her current and previous tours by requiring attendees to seal devices in [[Yondr]] pouches. Her example anchors the episode's argument that removing screens can restore audience attention, performer intimacy, and the fleeting quality of a live event.

## Key Characteristics
- Musician using a formal phone-sealing policy at concerts.
- Repeat adopter rather than a one-show experiment in the source's account.
- Artist-side example of controlling audience recording and attention.

## Evidence
- Concert policy: [[brazil-rut-lula-v-bolsonaro-again-6abb8653ed4e198024b38eb5]] says current and previous tours used magnetically locked Yondr pouches.
- Experience rationale: [[brazil-rut-lula-v-bolsonaro-again-6abb8653ed4e198024b38eb5]] links the policy to fewer screens between performer and audience and to preserving ephemerality.
- Broader trend: [[brazil-rut-lula-v-bolsonaro-again-6abb8653ed4e198024b38eb5]] places Bridgers alongside several other major artists using bans or softer phone-away requests.

## Qualifications
The episode largely presents artist rationales and does not assess audience satisfaction, accessibility, emergency communication, enforcement problems, or effects on unauthorized recording.

## What Changed
- Created a profile of Bridgers as the episode's central phone-free concert example.
