# Wikimedia Foundation

## Overview
The Wikimedia Foundation is the nonprofit institutional layer associated in the episode with sustaining [[Wikipedia]] as a durable, freely accessible public-knowledge project.

## Current Profile
The founder account frames nonprofit status as governance, not merely tax form. After [[Bomis]] funding disappeared, Wikipedia needed infrastructure without allowing the most lucrative subjects, markets, or page views to define editorial priorities. The volunteer community favored nonprofit status, [[JimmyWales]] rejected advertising, and an emergency server campaign demonstrated that many small donors could finance the project without concentrating dependence on a few commercial patrons.

## Key Characteristics
- Nonprofit steward aligned with free access to human knowledge.
- Institutional response to the loss of Wikipedia's original commercial subsidy.
- Funding model centered on broadly distributed small donations.
- Advertising refusal intended to reduce commercial distortion of editorial priorities.
- Financially conservative approach described as maintaining reserves for stability.

## Evidence
- Institutional choice - [[vmp5702291377-vmp5702291377]] says Wales created a nonprofit around 2003 with strong volunteer-community support.
- Revenue design - [[vmp5702291377-vmp5702291377]] links advertising refusal to concern that revenue sources shape organizational priorities.
- Donor validation - [[vmp5702291377-vmp5702291377]] reports that a first server campaign raised nearly $30,000 in roughly two weeks against a $20,000 monthly target.
- Stability - [[vmp5702291377-vmp5702291377]] describes broad small-donor support and reserve-building as protection for institutional independence and continuity.

## Qualifications
The source provides Wales's strategic rationale rather than an audited institutional history. Donation revenue, reserves, campaign timing, governance effects, and independence claims remain episode-scoped, and nonprofit status alone does not eliminate incentive, representation, labor, or accountability problems.
