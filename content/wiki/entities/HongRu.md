## Overview

Hong Ru / 闳孺 is presented as a favored young male companion of [[LiuYing|汉惠帝刘盈]] whose palace proximity gives him leverage in a dispute involving [[ShenYiji|审食其]] and [[LuZhi|吕雉]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-372-3-xihan-huangdi-dou-you-naxie-nanpengyou-lkfxzzsl2dc-vnaxupjlfn14j6kt|Hanji 372-3]] treats Hong Ru's favor as a public court fact and a political channel. When Liu Ying threatens Shen Yiji, [[PingyuanjunZhuJian|平原君朱建]] approaches Hong Ru and argues that the killing would be blamed on Hong Ru's private influence, provoking Lu Zhi's retaliation. Hong Ru then persuades Liu Ying to desist.

The episode's strongest evidence concerns access and mediation, not formal authority. Its “boyfriend” language and psychological explanation for Liu Ying's relationships remain interpretations rather than established identity diagnoses.

## Key Characteristics

- He is described as Liu Ying's publicly recognized favorite and close companion.
- His access lets an outside intermediary reach the emperor when direct approach is ineffective.
- He can influence a life-and-death decision, but the episode does not show durable control over policy.
- His leverage is inseparable from danger because Lu Zhi could treat him as responsible for harm to her favorite.
- The source describes his rise as favor-based rather than grounded in demonstrated administrative ability.

## Qualifications
