# Act of Abjuration

The Act of Abjuration appears in [[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] as the June 1581 act by which the States General formally renounced [[PhilipII|Philip II]]'s rule. The episode places it inside the [[DutchRevolt]] and the search for a replacement sovereign in the [[LowCountries|Low Countries]].

The act matters because Dutch rebels had asked [[FrancoisDukeOfAnjou|Francois, Duke of Anjou]] to accept sovereignty in Philip's place. Anjou's Catholic identity and constrained authority made the solution fragile, and his failed [[FrenchFury]] coup later damaged the French option.

For [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]], the Act of Abjuration and Anjou's Dutch role are part of the diplomatic background before direct English intervention. They show that the rebels had tried a non-English patron before [[TreatyOfNonsuch]] made England's commitment more explicit.
