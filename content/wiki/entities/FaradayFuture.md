# Faraday Future / 法拉第未来

## Overview
Faraday Future / 法拉第未来 is the U.S.-based electric-vehicle company that became [[JiaYueting|贾跃亭]]'s principal surviving project after the collapse of the wider [[LeEco|乐视]] ecosystem.

## Current Profile
Faraday Future represents both the technically serious and financially unresolved sides of Jia's automotive ambition. The company recruited engineers from established automakers, unveiled the premium FF 91, obtained financing from multiple sources, and listed through a SPAC before beginning deliveries. Yet the episode's 2024 and 2025 delivery counts remained in single digits, so product existence has not become evidence of repeatable mass production.

Its history is inseparable from repeated financing and control disputes. A proposed U.S. factory, LeEco's domestic auto plans, an investment arrangement with [[Evergrande|恒大]], later settlement, public listing, and Jia's personal-bankruptcy restructuring all extended the project's life without resolving the scale question.

## Key Characteristics
- Pursued a high-end electric vehicle through experienced engineering recruitment and an ambitious technology proposition.
- Survived the wider LeEco collapse and became the main continuing corporate vehicle for Jia's auto vision.
- Relied on repeated external financing, strategic investors, restructuring, and public-market access.
- Delivered the FF 91 only at extremely small scale in the source's 2024-2025 figures.
- Continued to announce additional brands and models while mass-production capability remained unproven.
