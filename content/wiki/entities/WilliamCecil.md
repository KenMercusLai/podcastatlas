# William Cecil / Lord Burleigh

William Cecil, Lord Burleigh, appears in [[696-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-killing-the-queen-of-scots-part-6-glt2334035496]] as [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]]'s senior minister and one of the strongest advocates for executing [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]]. The episode pairs him with [[FrancisWalsingham]] as a figure who wants Mary's removal framed as law, not private vengeance.

Burleigh's strategy is to make the [[ActForTheQueensSafety|Act for the Queen's Safety]] do political work. By pushing Parliament, emphasizing additional threats, and joining supportive councillors after Elizabeth signs the warrant, he helps move the state from hesitation to execution at [[FotheringhayCastle]].

At Mary's trial, Burleigh embodies hostile commission politics. When Mary confronts him, he answers that he is enemy to Elizabeth's enemies, turning personal accusation into a statement of state-security duty.
