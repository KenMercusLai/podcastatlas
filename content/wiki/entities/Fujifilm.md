# Fujifilm / 富士胶片

## Overview
Fujifilm is a Japanese imaging and materials company that appears in the wiki as a reported supplier expanding magnetic-tape capacity for long-duration data retention.

## Current Profile
The bounded source presents Fujifilm as a beneficiary of AI's physical data footprint outside accelerators and data centers' hottest storage tiers. Its reported plan to expand magnetic-tape capacity fivefold rests on tape's low cost, durability, and fit for infrequently read information. This is a capacity-plan signal, not proof of realized demand, market share, or project return.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates at the intersection of imaging materials and enterprise data storage.
- Supplies magnetic tape suited to durable, low-access-frequency archives.
- Is reportedly making its largest data-storage manufacturing investment in roughly two decades.
- Treats AI-driven data growth as a demand signal for archival media rather than only online storage.
