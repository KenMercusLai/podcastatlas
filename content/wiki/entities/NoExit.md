# No Exit / 《禁闭》

## Overview
No Exit / 《禁闭》 is a [[JeanPaulSartre|Jean-Paul Sartre / 萨特]] play that the wiki now holds in two frames: author-context evidence for Sartre's cultural influence and a dramatic case for [[OtherAsHell|他人即地狱]].

## Current Profile
[[135-xianliao-weida-zuojiamen-de-bagua-di-san-dan-823746670]] names [[NoExit|No Exit / 《禁闭》]] as one of Sartre's major works in the occupation-era Paris context. That source uses the play to establish Sartre's philosophical-literary importance while complicating his later public-hero image.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-395-2-sanren-beihou-de-renxing-gushi-lnxghfd6esj7jbir-m6qflth2ckp]] reads the play more directly. Its focus is the hell-room structure: Garcin, Ines, and Estelle are not tortured by devices or guards, but by the fact that they cannot stop needing, judging, desiring, exposing, and rejecting one another. The play therefore becomes a compact literary explanation of why bad relations and dependence on external judgment can become spiritual confinement.

## Key Characteristics
- Sartre work connected to [[Existentialism|existentialism]] and public-intellectual memory.
- Drama of relational punishment rather than physical punishment.
- Source for the phrase [[OtherAsHell|他人即地狱]] in the wiki's relationship-and-judgment vocabulary.
- Reading case where a famous slogan needs the play's specific situation to remain intelligible.

## Evidence
- Cultural-importance frame: [[135-xianliao-weida-zuojiamen-de-bagua-di-san-dan-823746670]] names the play beside [[BeingAndNothingness|Being and Nothingness / 《存在与虚无》]] as part of Sartre's occupation-era success.
- Relational-punishment frame: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-395-2-sanren-beihou-de-renxing-gushi-lnxghfd6esj7jbir-m6qflth2ckp]] says the hell has no instruments or executioner, but the three people become one another's torment.
- Slogan discipline: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-395-2-sanren-beihou-de-renxing-gushi-lnxghfd6esj7jbir-m6qflth2ckp]] explicitly rejects reading "他人就是地狱" as a universal condemnation of all relationships.
