# 孔熙 / Kong Xi

孔熙 / Kong Xi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-167-jianzheng-xiangyu-zuizhong-zhizhan-2-li14pizmfrzhpg8m5bmxdx8ibw7p|Hanji 167 part 2]] as one of [[HanXin|韩信]]'s named subordinate commanders in the [[GaixiaBattle|垓下之战]] formation. The source places him on the left side of Han Xin's first line, paired with [[ChenHe|陈贺]] on the right.

His role is tactical rather than biographical. As Han Xin's center yields under [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s assault, Kong Xi and Chen He keep pressing into the depth and threaten the Chu army's rear. The page therefore treats Kong Xi as part of [[LayeredDepthDefense|纵深分层防御]]: the wings make the apparent retreat dangerous instead of allowing it to become simple collapse.
