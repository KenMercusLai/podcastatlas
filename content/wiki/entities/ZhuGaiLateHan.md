# 朱丐 / Zhu Gai (late Han)

## Overview
Zhu Gai is the rebel soldier in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-867-wugu-bei-po-zangshui-ta-xuanze-baqi-huiji-lqenvoy7x8ic-qn3-bf93qj8tjvk|Hanji 867]] whose uprising helps trigger the 165 CE Jingzhou crisis. He joins the Guiyang bandit leader [[HuLanLateHan|Hu Lan]], attacks [[GuiyangCommanderyLateHan|Guiyang]], and then becomes part of the threat to [[LinglingCommanderyLateHan|Lingling]].

## Current Profile
Zhu Gai is currently a source-scoped rebellion actor rather than a developed political figure. The episode treats him as the soldier-rebel whose alliance with Hu Lan turns scattered local disorder into a multi-commandery emergency requiring central intervention. His defeat by [[DuShangLateHan|Du Shang]] anchors the campaign that later lets Du Shang claim renewed provincial authority.

## Key Characteristics
- Jingzhou soldier who initiates open rebellion in 165 CE.
- Rebel ally of [[HuLanLateHan|Hu Lan]] in the Guiyang-Lingling crisis.
- Military opponent whose defeat strengthens [[DuShangLateHan|Du Shang]]'s merit claim.
