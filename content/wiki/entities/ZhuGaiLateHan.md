## Overview
Zhu Gai is the rebel soldier whose uprising helps trigger the 165 CE Jingzhou crisis. The current evidence introduces him as joining the Guiyang bandit leader [[HuLanLateHan|Hu Lan]] to attack [[GuiyangCommanderyLateHan|Guiyang]], then follows the rebellion into [[LinglingCommanderyLateHan|Lingling]] and the later suppression campaign.

## Current Profile
Zhu Gai is currently a source-scoped rebellion actor rather than a developed political figure. The 165 CE prelude shows his alliance with Hu Lan already pressuring Guiyang and forcing Ren Yin's flight before the Lingling defense sequence. The follow-on evidence treats him as the soldier-rebel whose alliance with Hu Lan turns scattered local disorder into a multi-commandery emergency requiring central intervention.

His defeat by [[DuShangLateHan|Du Shang]] anchors the campaign that later lets Du Shang claim renewed provincial authority. The profile should therefore track both the immediate rebellion setup and the later political use of suppression merit.

## Key Characteristics
- Jingzhou soldier who initiates open rebellion in 165 CE.
- Rebel ally of [[HuLanLateHan|Hu Lan]] in the Guiyang-Lingling crisis.
- Attack participant whose pressure on Guiyang precedes Ren Yin's flight and the Lingling defense.
- Military opponent whose defeat strengthens [[DuShangLateHan|Du Shang]]'s merit claim.
