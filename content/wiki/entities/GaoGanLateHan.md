# 高干 / Gao Gan (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] introduces Gao Gan as [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s nephew in the persuasion mission sent to [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]]. His value in the source is not independent policy advice; he marks Yuan Shao's direct family stake in the effort to turn pressure on [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] into surrender.

Gao Gan travels in the same pressure-and-persuasion field as [[XinPingLateHan|辛评]], [[XunChenLateHan|荀谌]], and [[GuoTu|郭图]]. Together they help make Han Fu experience yielding Jizhou as safer and more reputable than resisting, even though Han Fu's own subordinates argue that the province still has troops and grain.
