# 去卑 / Qubei (Southern Xiongnu)

去卑 / Qubei (Southern Xiongnu) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-991-zhengduo-xiandi-zhizhan-shui-neng-zuizhong-qusheng-lkbsl5uzqxouzm6mlewrnuns0x6p|Hanji 991]] as the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] right wise king invited into the emergency escort coalition around [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]. The transcript renders the name as "去碑"; this page keeps the canonical Qubei form and disambiguates him from broader [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] pages.

Qubei's source role is narrow: his cavalry joins [[LiLeLateHan|李乐]], [[HanXianLateHan|韩暹]], and [[HuCaiLateHan|胡才]] in temporarily defeating [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]]'s side. The victory does not stabilize the court, so Qubei functions here as proof that frontier and irregular forces can preserve the emperor's body without restoring imperial command.
