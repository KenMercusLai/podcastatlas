# Moonshot AI / 月之暗面

Moonshot AI / 月之暗面 is the company behind [[Kimi]] and [[KimiK3|Kimi K3]] in [[e246-hewei-zhengliu-liaoliao-guigu-ruhe-kan-zhongguo-kaifang-moxing-bijin-qianyan-5fd236d7-9a72-4b15-9e84-e83ceadd1b41]]. The episode treats Moonshot's Kimi K3 full-weight release as a major reason Silicon Valley restarted debates about Chinese open-weight models, [[ModelDistillation]], token-price pressure, enterprise [[ModelSovereignty]], and open-model safety.

The source's Moonshot-specific contribution is not just model performance. It frames Kimi K3's license as an [[OpenWeightCommercialLicensing]] experiment: open weights can create developer adoption and model diversity while asking high-revenue model-as-service providers to enter a paid or certified relationship.

[[xiangjie-kimi-k3-qiangdao-chongji-anthropic-guzhi-de-moxing-shenmeyang-1-177-1]] adds the model-factory boundary. The source says Moonshot opened K3 weights and some infrastructure such as [[AgentIn]] and Flash KDA, but not the full environment, verifier, data, RL workflow, original expert checkpoints, or task system that would make the release fully reproducible as a next-model pipeline.
