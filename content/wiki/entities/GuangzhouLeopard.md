## Overview
广州豹 / Guangzhou Leopard appears in the source as the new Guangzhou football team whose likely promotion raises the question of whether city football can replace [[GuangzhouFC]] in supporter memory. The episode treats the team as sporting hope and legitimacy problem at the same time.

## Current Profile
Guangzhou Leopard is framed as a practical route for Guangzhou to return to the [[ChineseSuperLeague|Chinese Super League]], but not as an automatic continuation of Guangzhou FC. [[PanWeili]] argues that "广州足球冲回中超" is a better public phrasing than "广州队冲回中超" because many old supporters distinguish city representation from the original club's institutional lineage.

## Key Characteristics
- New Guangzhou club discussed as close to promotion in the source.
- City-football recovery vehicle rather than uncontested Guangzhou FC successor.
- Supporter-legitimacy challenge because some fans link it to the failure to save the original club.
- Case where performance, transparency, and time may gradually build support.

## Evidence
- Promotion context: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] says Guangzhou Leopard was leading China League One and promotion looked only a matter of time.
- Language boundary: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] says the safer framing is Guangzhou football returning to the top league, not Guangzhou FC returning.
- Fan resistance: [[vol-225-hengda-de-zuqiu-yichan-ft-panweili-gkwrijeozrpkafcpbatn-ivb]] says many former Guangzhou FC supporters resent that state-owned groups did not rescue the old club but formed a new one.

## Qualifications
The source's support-share projection and future legitimacy judgment are speculative. The page records the current conflict rather than predicting final supporter adoption.
