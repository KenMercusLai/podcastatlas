# Dali, Yunnan / 大理

Dali, Yunnan / 大理 appears in [[150-chi-jun-he-acuo-liao-yunnan-de-jizong-songrong-jianshouqing-ca-koushui-897563165]] as part of [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]]'s lived Yunnan context: 李飘 mentions eating local mushrooms, seeing plant abundance around Erhai, and comparing city friends' scenic astonishment with daily access to blue sky, sunsets, mountains, and plants.

The place also matters to an earlier show-practice branch. In [[jiaqi-moyu-geng-jiankang-574391976]], the show's move to Dali is linked to healthier rhythm, reading, home organization, and [[RestorativeCreativePacing]]. The mushroom episode extends that from production life into food, ecology, and [[NatureContactSelfPerception]].

This makes Dali a condition for the show's body, mood, and future reading interests, not only a later mushroom or travel setting.
