# Emma of Normandy

## Overview

Emma of Normandy appears in the wiki as the Norman-born English queen and dynastic broker whose marriages joined the displaced West Saxon monarchy to its Danish conqueror and whose competing commitments to her sons later shaped the post-Cnut succession struggle.

## Current Profile

The earlier source places Emma inside the conquest itself. [[AethelredTheUnready|Æthelred]] sent her, [[EdwardTheConfessor|Edward]], Alfred, and Godgifu to Normandy in 1013 as [[SweynForkbeard|Sweyn]] took England. In 1017, after [[CnutTheGreat|Cnut]] became sole king, Emma married the conqueror. The church-blessed union linked Cnut to the old royal house and strengthened the future claim of Emma's son by Cnut over his sons by Ælfgifu of Northampton.

The later source presents Emma as mother of Edward and Alfred by Æthelred and of Harthacnut by Cnut. After Cnut died in 1035, she defended the absent Harthacnut's claim from Winchester against Harold Harefoot and treated her older sons in Norman exile as secondary options. Once Harold prevailed, she recalled Edward and Alfred; Edward withdrew, while Alfred was captured, blinded, and died.

Emma then fled to Flanders, returned with Harthacnut after Harold's death, and remained politically active after Edward succeeded in 1042. Edward confiscated her treasure and removed her from court in 1043; the episode later associates her with a failed Magnus of Norway intrigue. Her career therefore connects Norman kinship, Danish royal marriage, treasury control, exile, and maternal strategy without reducing her actions to a single stable family loyalty.

## Key Characteristics

- Norman-born queen whose exile and remarriage link the houses of Æthelred, Cnut, and the Norman ducal court.
- Dynastic spouse whose church-blessed marriage helps a conqueror appropriate continuity and privileges her new line of succession.
- Mother who prioritized Harthacnut before turning again to Edward and Alfred.
- Political operator who used Winchester, treasure, rumor, summons, exile, and return in succession contests.
- Figure whose recall of Alfred ended disastrously and whose later relationship with Edward became openly hostile.

## Evidence

- Rival claim management - [[550-the-road-to-1066-rise-of-the-normans-part-3-glt9316865693]] describes Emma's defense of Harthacnut's claim against Harold Harefoot from Winchester.
- Recall and disaster - [[550-the-road-to-1066-rise-of-the-normans-part-3-glt9316865693]] follows her summons to Edward and Alfred, Edward's withdrawal, and Alfred's capture and death.
- Return and fall - [[550-the-road-to-1066-rise-of-the-normans-part-3-glt9316865693]] connects her return with Harthacnut to Edward's later confiscation of her wealth and exclusion from court.
- Exile and remarriage - [[549-the-road-to-1066-revenge-of-the-vikings-part-2-glt2312975985]] follows Emma's 1013 flight and presents her 1017 marriage to Cnut as a dynastic settlement.

## Qualifications

The sources do not independently settle Emma's agency in the 1013 exile, the marriage bargain with Cnut, Ælfgifu's status, or the legal weight given to different sons. The later source rejects Emma's claim that Harold forged her summons but does not settle the letters, her knowledge of the danger, or responsibility for Alfred's fate. Her alleged intrigue with Magnus and judgments about her maternal priorities remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Extended the profile backward through the 1013 exile and 1017 marriage, making conquest settlement central to her later succession role.
