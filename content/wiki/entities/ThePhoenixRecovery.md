# The Phoenix

The Phoenix is the addiction-recovery community example [[ChaseKoch|Chase Koch]] gives in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-charles-chase-koch-on-how-they-quietly-built-a-150b-empire-41270895]]. Chase names Scott Strode and The Phoenix as an example of backing a person close to a problem who already had a working model.

The source uses The Phoenix to illustrate [[BarrierRemovalSocialChange]] by showing rather than telling. The point is not only that philanthropy funds services, but that effective social change may come from people with lived proximity and a model others can observe.
