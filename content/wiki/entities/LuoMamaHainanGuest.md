# 罗妈妈 / Hainan Local Guest

## Overview

罗妈妈 / Hainan Local Guest is the Hainan-born guest whose family history and memories ground [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]] EP167.

## Current Profile

She presents herself through an intergenerational migration story: her grandfather left a Guangzhou opportunity to join forestry construction in Hainan, and later generations grew up on the island. Her testimony moves between personal memory and regional interpretation, covering speech, school, food, climate, housing, tourism, prices, work, public services, and the choice of whether young people return. She values Hainan's safety, health, sociability, and family life while declining to romanticize its career depth or familiar social environment.

## Key Characteristics

- Locally raised witness connecting family migration to Hainan identity.
- Corrective voice against visitor assumptions about food, weather, housing, and leisure.
- Qualified supporter of Hainan's policy opportunity who distinguishes visibility from implementation.
- Young migrant who personally prefers a broader and more anonymous social environment outside the island.
