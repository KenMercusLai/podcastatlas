# Michelle Hanlon

Michelle Hanlon appears in [[tech-20260818-tech-pod-128-tech-20260818-tech-pod-128]] as a space-policy expert explaining why launch capacity has become a practical bottleneck for the commercial space industry. She describes the shift from a few expensive satellites toward [[CommercialSatelliteConstellations]], where many smaller satellites make individual failures less damaging but require more frequent access to orbit.

Hanlon also frames the [[FederalAviationAdministration|FAA]] proposal as an "alarm bell" showing that [[SpaceLaunchCapacityBottleneck|launch capacity]] matters for keeping more of the space industry in the [[UnitedStates]]. Her role in the source is not to settle the environmental-law dispute, but to make the infrastructure constraint visible.
