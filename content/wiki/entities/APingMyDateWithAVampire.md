## Overview

阿平 is the adult tailor whose relationship with [[PingMaMyDateWithAVampire|平妈]] drives the 佳佳大厦 arc of [[MyDateWithAVampire|《我和僵尸有个约会》]].

## Current Profile

A Ping initially appears unusually gentle: he helps strangers, forgives 小倩's theft, and treats [[PPMyDateWithAVampire|P.P.]] without contempt. That concrete empathy contrasts with his mother's suspicion and rule enforcement. Yet his kindness is not a stable moral safeguard. He has built no durable self outside obedience, and after Ping Ma returns as a living corpse he hides her murders, participates in coercing 王珍珍, and converts her second death into a grievance against the neighbors.

His transformation into 厄修罗 makes suppressed dependence and resentment supernatural threats. The final reconciliation does not prove him innocent; it shows that his rage can subside once Ping Ma acknowledges the structure that formed it. His decision to bear the cost of her crimes then redirects filial attachment away from denial, though within an explicitly supernatural resolution.

## Key Characteristics

- Begins as a generous and situationally empathetic neighbor.
- Has little practiced autonomy after decades of maternal control.
- Confuses obedience and protection of his mother with moral duty.
- Crosses from passivity into concealment, coercion, and revenge.
- Directs romantic longing toward an idealized woman while appearing more at ease with P.P.
- Releases resentment only after the controlling relationship is named and apologized for.

## Qualifications

This profile comes from one podcast's recap and interpretation. Diagnoses of repression, pathology, romance, or abuse are not independently established, and the supernatural ending should not be generalized into ordinary family repair. Explaining the effects of control does not excuse A Ping's concealment, coercion, or threats.
