# Prague Spring / 布拉格之春

[[PragueSpring|Prague Spring / 布拉格之春]] appears in [[12-man-dang-milan-kundela-yi-ju-zhengjing-meiyou-549335675]] as the rupture after which [[MilanKundera|Milan Kundera]] and [[VeraKundera|Vera Kundera]] were described as living under surveillance, and after which the fictional Czech entomologist in [[SlowKundera|《慢》]] lost his research position and was forced into construction work.

The source uses the episode's post-1968 material to show how political repression enters private memory. The entomologist later remembers compromised action as heroic destiny, while Kundera's own biography becomes difficult to separate from exile, privacy, and the public hunger to classify him politically.
