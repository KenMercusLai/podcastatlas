# Charles E. Mitchell / 查理·米切尔

Charles E. Mitchell is one of the three 1929 role portraits in [[170-1929-de-paomo-zhixia-sange-daibiao-renwu-he-tamen-zai-dangxia-zhouqi-de-yingzi-lo7uv7umozr0jrqfjzyhdbzicx1j]]. The episode presents him as the chairman of [[NationalCityBank]] and a [[FederalReserve]]-adjacent banking figure whose institution used a retail bank network to distribute stocks, bonds, and Latin American sovereign debt to middle-class savers.

The episode does not reduce Mitchell to a simple villain. It notes both his market-stabilizing posture during the 1929 stress and the later hearings over executive loans, bonuses, tax behavior, and securities distribution. In the wiki, his main role is to anchor [[FinancialInstitutionTrustTransmission]]: trusted banking infrastructure can make risky assets feel suitable to households even when the underlying research, leverage, liquidity, or borrower quality is weak.
