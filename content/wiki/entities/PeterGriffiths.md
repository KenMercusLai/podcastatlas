# Peter Griffiths

## Overview

Peter Griffiths appears as the Conservative candidate who won Smethwick in the 1964 general election after an openly anti-immigration campaign.

## Current Profile

[[606-enoch-powell-rivers-of-blood-glt7520958596]] uses Griffiths's victory as crucial prehistory to [[EnochPowell|Enoch Powell]]'s later turn. The campaign demonstrated that local racial and immigration anxiety could win a parliamentary seat and exposed a perceived divide between voters and institutions such as Parliament, churches, newspapers, and the intelligentsia. [[HaroldWilson|Harold Wilson]]'s description of Griffiths as a “parliamentary leper” expressed Westminster revulsion but also helped dramatize the conflict between elite condemnation and electoral support.

## Key Characteristics

- Conservative candidate in the industrial West Midlands constituency of Smethwick.
- Won the seat in 1964 through a campaign the episode describes as openly anti-immigration.
- Became a national symbol of the electoral potency and moral toxicity of racialized campaigning.
- Supplied a nearby precedent for Powell's later hardening in Wolverhampton.
