# Curt Richardson

Curt Richardson is the founder of [[OtterBox]] and [[BlueOcean]], and the [[HowIBuiltThis]] Advice Line guest in [[advice-line-with-curt-richardson-of-otterbox-e94bf774-de11-4434-843b-a551309a6773]]. The episode presents him as a startup-oriented maker and operator who later stepped back from day-to-day work as OtterBox became a larger, more mature organization.

His advice centers on disciplined focus, testing before scaling, and improving the business model rather than treating product extension as the only form of innovation. With [[GuyRaz]], he applies that lens to [[MrGameShowFlorida]], [[GildedCoachTeas]], and [[Everloop]]: test whether founder charisma can be replicated, restart a story-led consumer brand through direct customer relationships, and measure marketing channels before expanding spend.
