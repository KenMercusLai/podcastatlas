# 赵祗 / Zhao Zhi (late Han)

赵祗 / Zhao Zhi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-940-de-minxin-zhe-de-tianxia-ni-xin-bu-xin-loipf7b0p5a-yh0rj2njnsml8a1j|Hanji 940]] as the partner of [[MaXiangLateHan|马相]] in the [[MianzhuLateHan|绵竹]] uprising. The source places him inside the Yellow Turban-banner revolt that kills [[XiJianLateHan|郗俭]] and spreads through parts of [[YizhouLateHan|益州]].

The page is source-scoped because the episode uses Zhao Zhi mainly as part of the rebellion's initial leadership rather than as a developed biography. His role matters for the wiki as a marker of how quickly local resentment, rebel symbolism, and official failure can combine before a durable command structure exists.
