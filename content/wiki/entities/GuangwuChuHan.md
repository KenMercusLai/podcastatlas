# 广武 / Guangwu (Chu-Han)

Guangwu is the standoff position where [[LiuBang]] and [[XiangYu]] face each other after Han retakes [[Chenggao]] and [[Aocang]] in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-163-liubang-shouxia-moushi-ruhe-bei-hanxin-neijuan-cansi-lubinu6hoihu59knmlixmaryhnq7|Hanji 163]]. The episode treats Guangwu as the setting where Xiang Yu's strategic pressure is weakening because Chu has lost access to grain, but his personal intimidation still remains intense.

At Guangwu, Xiang Yu threatens to cook [[LiuTaigong]], only for Liu Bang to refuse the hostage frame and for [[XiangBo]] to argue that killing the hostage would add harm without solving Chu's problem. Xiang Yu then challenges Liu Bang to a duel; Liu Bang rejects the contest as a matter of strength rather than strategy, while the intimidation of the [[Loufan]] archers shows Xiang Yu's personal force still shapes local battlefield behavior.
