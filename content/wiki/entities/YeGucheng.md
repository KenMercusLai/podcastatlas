## Overview

叶孤城 / Ye Gucheng is the white-clad sword master and lord of Baiyun City in [[DuelBeforeAndAfterGuLong|《决战前后》]], where his duel with [[XimenChuixue|西门吹雪]] conceals an unsuccessful plot to assassinate the emperor.

## Current Profile

The bounded source constructs Ye through resemblance and divergence. Like Ximen, he is isolated, proud, and technically supreme; unlike him, he accepts display, title, followers, political power, and worldly entanglement. The hosts read his flower-strewn entrance and status as early signs that the sword master is not separate from public identity and ambition.

His claim that a swordsman need only be sincere to the sword gives technical purity an internally coherent voice while exposing its moral limit. After the coup fails, his blade deviates by one or two inches in a duel he may otherwise have won. The source prefers, without settling, the interpretation that he chooses death by the only opponent worthy of granting it. That possibility preserves dignity but prevents the duel from conclusively ranking the two men.

## Key Characteristics

- Mirrors Ximen in white clothing, isolation, pride, and elite swordsmanship.
- Differs through lordship, display, retinue, political ambition, and proximity to power.
- Separates sincerity to technique from sincerity to other people.
- Uses the public duel as cover for a palace assassination plot.
- May be technically faster in the final exchange without becoming the acknowledged victor.
- Possibly chooses death after political failure, converting defeat into an act of agency and leaving combat superiority unresolved.
