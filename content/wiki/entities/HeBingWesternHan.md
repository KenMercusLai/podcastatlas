## Overview

何病 / He Bing is the Western Han official who replaces [[YanXuWesternHan|严徐]] as governor of [[YingchuanCommandery|颍川郡]] in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-619-fumuguan-zhege-ci-shi-zenme-laide-lmluscbtl3faczssz5didybawa1r|Hanji 619]]. The source uses his immediate arrests and killings to show how a new local official can change the felt safety of an entire commandery.

## Current Profile

He Bing appears as a contrast figure rather than a fully developed official biography. After Yan Xu is moved to the capital and then made Longxi governor, He Bing takes Yingchuan. The episode says he soon arrests and kills source-scoped 钟威, 赵继, 李款, and others, causing fear across the commandery.

His function in the source is interpretive: he proves why Yan Xu's farewell grief matters. A "父母官" ideal is not only praise for kindness; it is a judgment that local office controls whether people experience government as protection or as lethal exposure.

## Key Characteristics

- Replacement governor whose arrival follows Yan Xu's transfer.
- Severe local administrator remembered in this source for arrests and executions.
- Contrast figure for Yan Xu's restrained "父母官" image.
- Evidence that personnel changes can quickly alter local fear and security.

## Evidence

- Succession in office: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-619-fumuguan-zhege-ci-shi-zenme-laide-lmluscbtl3faczssz5didybawa1r|Hanji 619]] says He Bing soon replaced Yan Xu as Yingchuan governor.
- Punitive opening: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-619-fumuguan-zhege-ci-shi-zenme-laide-lmluscbtl3faczssz5didybawa1r|Hanji 619]] says he arrested and killed Zhong Wei, Zhao Ji, Li Kuan, and others.
- Public fear: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-619-fumuguan-zhege-ci-shi-zenme-laide-lmluscbtl3faczssz5didybawa1r|Hanji 619]] says the whole commandery feared his severe methods.

## Qualifications

The source does not give He Bing's full career, the charges against the people he killed, or the court's intended policy for Yingchuan. His page therefore records his role in this episode's local-governance contrast rather than treating him as a complete moral or legal case.
