# 周苛 / Zhou Ke

周苛 / Zhou Ke enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-162-1-ta-ti-liubang-qu-si-rujin-chengle-zhengzhou-de-shouhushen-li3su2zlmbm1ift9nibujgtcrxwm|Hanji 162-1]] as the 御史大夫 whom [[LiuBang|刘邦]] leaves in charge of the [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]] garrison after escaping through [[JiXin|纪信]]'s substitution. The source presents Zhou Ke as a core Liu Bang-side figure trusted enough to hold the city during the worst phase of the [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]].

His main action in this episode is the decision, together with [[CongGong|枞公]], to execute [[WeiBao|魏豹]]. The host frames the act as both a trust judgment and a garrison-commitment device: Wei Bao's repeated shifts between Chu and Han make him dangerous inside a doomed or nearly doomed defense, so killing him signals that compromise with [[XiangYu|项羽]] is no longer available.
