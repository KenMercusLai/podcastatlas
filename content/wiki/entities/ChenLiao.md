# 陈聊 / Chen Liao

陈聊 / Chen Liao appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-14-qiweiwang-buming-zeyi-yiming-jingren-lrs6rbifyiqrojxg0mccpg7sf9rv]] as the person to whom [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]] delegates state affairs while he drinks and neglects government. The source does not develop Chen Liao as a full biography; his value is to show how a ruler's withdrawal can leave the state running through a narrow surrogate channel.

This page is source-scoped. Chen Liao matters because the episode frames Qi's danger as more than personal vice: when the ruler is absent, ministers fear direct counsel, neighboring states take territory, and the court needs [[AllusiveRemonstrance|indirect remonstrance]] before [[QiWeiwangPoliticalTurnaround|political turnaround]] becomes possible.
