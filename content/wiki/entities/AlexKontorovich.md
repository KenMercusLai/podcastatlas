# Alex Kontorovich

## Overview
Alex Kontorovich is the Rutgers mathematician interviewed by [[MarketplaceTech]] about reported [[OpenAI]] progress on difficult mathematical problems and its consequences for research, education, and hiring.

## Current Profile
Kontorovich presents AI mathematics as a "two-sided gift": generated results can reduce the prospect of a celebrated human discovery while still adding valuable mathematical ideas. His core boundary is between verification and understanding. He expects mathematicians to remain necessary because formally checked output still requires interpretation, explanation, significance judgment, teaching, and integration into the field.

## Key Characteristics
- Distinguishes formally verified correctness from human explanation and understanding.
- Treats AI mathematical progress as both a genuine contribution and a challenge to inherited ideas of achievement.
- Expects oral defense, discussion, and lecture to become stronger evidence of personal mastery.
- Raises research-ethics concerns about unpublished work, training data, and prompt consent without treating them as settled findings.
- Prefers a collaborative equilibrium in which AI supplies direction while people retain judgment and intellectual responsibility.

## Evidence
- Verification and understanding - [[tech-20261102-1102-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261102-1102-mp-tech-pod-128]] has Kontorovich distinguish machine-checked truth from the work of explaining why a proof works and absorbing it into mathematics.
- Institutional response - [[tech-20261102-1102-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261102-1102-mp-tech-pod-128]] connects AI-generated papers to oral assessment, graduate education, and uncertain hiring criteria.
- Human-AI partnership - [[tech-20261102-1102-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20261102-1102-mp-tech-pod-128]] uses his compass analogy to keep AI as a research tool rather than a complete substitute for mathematicians.
