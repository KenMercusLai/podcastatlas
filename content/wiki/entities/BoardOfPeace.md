# Board of Peace

## Overview

The Board of Peace is a proposed Gaza-plan body whose formal authority and reconstruction promises have not yet produced visible implementation inside [[Gaza]].

## Current Profile

[[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]] introduces the board as part of an architecture meant to clarify [[Hamas]] disarmament and [[Israel|Israeli]] withdrawal. [[unrest-in-peace-misery-in-gaza-145c0cf22e24027ee45e02b7352ac088]] adds an institutional profile: it describes a $1 billion membership price, [[DonaldTrump]] as chair with veto power, no Palestinians on the executive board, and a $2.5 billion recovery plan presented by [[JaredKushner]]. The later source also supplies the outcome test: after extensive announcements, reconstruction machinery, materials, services, and the proposed technocratic authority remain largely absent.

## Key Characteristics

- High-level body attached to Gaza reconstruction, withdrawal, disarmament, and governance plans.
- Chairmanship and veto power concentrated in Donald Trump according to the later source.
- Executive governance described as lacking Palestinian representation.
- Business-heavy leadership paired with limited demonstrated humanitarian implementation.
- Formal plans depend on border access, territorial control, verification, security, and local administrative authority.

## Evidence

- Implementation architecture: [[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]] connects the board to a proposed timetable and companion governance, stabilization, and verification bodies.
- Institutional design: [[unrest-in-peace-misery-in-gaza-145c0cf22e24027ee45e02b7352ac088]] reports the membership price, chairmanship, veto, executive-board composition, and Kushner recovery plan.
- Outcome gap: [[unrest-in-peace-misery-in-gaza-145c0cf22e24027ee45e02b7352ac088]] says Gazans see little practical reconstruction and that the technocratic body remains in Egypt.

## Qualifications

The sources do not provide the board's charter, audited finances, complete membership, legal authority, procurement record, or operational reporting. Dollar figures, composition, and progress assessments remain source-reported, while continued violence and access restrictions make independent verification difficult.

## What Changed

- Added the board's reported membership, veto, representation, and recovery-plan design.
- Changed the current judgment from underspecified architecture to an architecture with a visible implementation failure.
- Added local access and representation as conditions of credibility.
