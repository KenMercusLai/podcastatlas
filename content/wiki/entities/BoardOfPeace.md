# Board of Peace

Board of Peace appears in [[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]] as the body that clarifies the supposed timetable for [[Hamas]] disarmament and [[Israel|Israeli]] withdrawal under [[DonaldTrump]]'s Gaza plan. The source treats it as part of the implementation architecture rather than as proof that implementation has been solved.

Its role is important because the episode's core problem is authority. A board can announce or clarify a timetable, but [[GazaDealImplementationGap]] remains if the plan does not specify who controls territory, who verifies arms, who protects technocrats, and which side moves first.
