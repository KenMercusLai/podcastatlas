# ProArch

## Overview
ProArch is the consulting and technology company associated in the available sources with [[JimSpignardo]] and [[BenWilcox]]. The stable wiki key remains `Proarc`, while the displayed company name is normalized to the capitalization used by the latest source.

## Current Profile
The two [[DataScienceWithSam]] episodes present complementary sides of ProArch's enterprise AI work. EP48 covers business enablement through use cases, data readiness, Microsoft 365 Copilot rollout, baselines, governance, champions, and accountable ownership. EP54 covers the security architecture required when agents gain identities, tools, data access, network reach, and autonomy. Together they frame adoption and security as one operating problem: organizations move faster when useful workflows, measurement, identity, permissions, deterministic controls, and monitoring are designed together.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides an enterprise context spanning cloud strategy, cybersecurity, AI adoption, and governance.
- Connects Microsoft 365 Copilot enablement to readiness, ownership, baselines, and business measurement.
- Connects agent deployment to identity, least privilege, MCP scope, external enforcement, and defense in depth.
- Is represented through practitioner interviews rather than independent company or customer evidence.
