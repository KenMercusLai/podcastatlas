# Crispus Attucks

## Overview

Crispus Attucks is presented as a Black and Native dockworker killed in the [[BostonMassacre|Boston Massacre]].

## Current Profile

The episode places Attucks at the center of the crowd confrontation while emphasizing how representation changed his place in memory. It describes him as physically imposing and active in the incident, notes his omission from Paul Revere's famous image, and records that [[JohnAdams|John Adams]] portrayed him in racialized terms while defending the soldiers.

## Key Characteristics

- Boston dockworker identified by the source as having Black and Native ancestry.
- Participant and victim in the Boston Massacre.
- Figure whose physical presence was emphasized in the defense case.
- Omitted from a famous contemporary visual representation.
- Example of race shaping revolutionary law and memory.
