# 张楚 / Zhang Chu (musician)

## Overview
张楚 is presented as a Chinese rock musician whose songs expressed social differentiation, shame, and difficult ordinary experience during the early rock breakthrough.

## Current Profile
The episode positions his work between personal truth and an era's social experience. Rather than reducing him to the later 1994 concert symbol, it treats his earlier recordings as part of the creative achievement that made that concert possible.

## Key Characteristics
- Songwriter associated in the source with social division and shame.
- Example of private emotional language becoming an account of shared historical experience.
- Participant in the creative wave preceding the 1994 public high point.
