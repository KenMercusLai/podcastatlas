## Overview
Wen Qi is the Yizhou commandery administrator in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-713-liuxiu-nu-le-mingjiang-wuhan-jing-xuexi-chengdu-lv6555l99it33gw34uydsz4tkbje|Hanji 713]] who resists Gongsun Shu's pressure, secretly submits to Liu Xiu, and is later rewarded after Shu is pacified.

## Current Profile
The episode says Wang Mang had appointed Wen Qi as Yizhou commandery taishou. Rather than ruling extractively, he farms, trains troops, settles local groups, and holds strategic ground, giving him a durable local base during the transition.

Gongsun Shu tries to win or coerce Wen Qi by taking his family hostage and offering a marquis title, but Wen Qi refuses and secretly sends a submission memorial to Liu Xiu. After Shu is pacified, Liu Xiu summons him as Zhenyuan Jiangjun and enfeoffs him as Chengyi Hou, making Wen Qi a local-loyalist bridge from resistant frontier administration into Guangwu's order.

## Key Characteristics
- Yizhou commandery administrator with a practical base in farming, troop training, and local settlement.
- Strategic local holder whose value comes from durable governance, not only office title.
- Refuser of Gongsun Shu's hostage pressure and marquis inducement.
- Secret submitter to Liu Xiu before Shu's final pacification.
- Post-conquest reward case through appointment as Zhenyuan Jiangjun and Chengyi Hou.
- Evidence point for Liu Xiu converting local integrity into imperial incorporation.
