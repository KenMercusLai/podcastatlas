# 张健 / Zhang Jian (cardiology)

## Overview
张健 is the cardiology guest identified as a committee chair in a 2026 [[ZheBingShuoLaiHuaChang|这病说来话长]] episode about sleep deprivation, stimulants, palpitations, exercise load, and cardiovascular risk. This page uses a specialty suffix to distinguish him from [[ZhangJianLateHan|张俭 / Zhang Jian (late Han)]].

## Current Profile
In [[vol-226-banye-baofuxing-aoye-baitian-nongkafei-xuming-duihua-zhangjian-zhuwei-bei-boduo-de-shuimian-shi-ruhe-yibubu-zhagan-xinzang-de-lkc710h5gqsce735jwmp9mhwewsy|VOL.226]], Zhang Jian presents cardiac symptoms and screening findings as context-dependent signals rather than automatic diagnoses. He joins sleep, stimulant use, hydration, emotion, infection recovery, exercise progression, medical history, and family history into one risk-assessment frame, while keeping consumer wearables in a warning role.

## Key Characteristics
- Cardiology educator who distinguishes sensitive screening signals from specific diagnosis.
- Advocate for normal sleep and recovery rhythms rather than caffeine-supported persistence through fatigue.
- Risk interpreter who combines symptoms, medical and family history, exercise context, and selected testing.
- Supporter of regular progressive exercise within cardiovascular, metabolic, infectious, and recovery boundaries.
