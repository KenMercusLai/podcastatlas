# 黄云武 / Huang Yunwu

黄云武 appears in [[144-nvxia-duan-sihe-he-tade-jiaxiang-mapingguan-women-ceng-yiwei-you-dian-you-lu-zhishi-meng-linmjqt-n-sa5sarpqgdxgtoeejt]] as the "黄老师" thanked by [[DuanSihe|段四合]] for long-term work with [[Mapingguan|马坪关]]. The episode identifies him as an architect connected to [[ShaxiAncientTown|沙溪古镇]] restoration and to [[KakaGugu|卡卡姑姑]].

His wiki role is as a facilitator rather than a top-down planner. The source says he encouraged residents to grow first, helped introduce meeting methods, guided practical skills such as WeChat use and visitor reception, and resisted easy online monetization when village absorptive capacity was not ready.

## Key Claims
- The source says 黄云武 entered 马坪关 in 2014 and spent time talking with villagers rather than only delivering a built project.
- His approach is summarized by the episode as "change people first": toilet renovation, reception work, meetings, and travel-study opportunities all become capacity-building tools.
- His warning about live-streaming and fast online promotion connects him to [[SlowRuralTourism]] and [[LongTermPlaceOperation]].
- His role should remain source-scoped because the episode gives a participant memory, not an independently verified project history.
