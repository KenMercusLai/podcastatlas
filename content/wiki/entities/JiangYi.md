# 江乙 / Jiang Yi

江乙 / Jiang Yi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]] as a [[ChuState|楚国]] court figure who repeatedly works against [[ZhaoXixu|昭奚恤]]. The episode says the "狐假虎威" story is also presented as his way of indirectly attacking Zhao Xixu, and then focuses on another parable about a household dog blocking a neighbor from reporting danger.

The source's judgment is sharp: Jiang Yi's danger lies in making manipulation sound like good counsel. He tells the ruler that if only praise is welcomed and defect-reporting is blocked, disaster will never be heard. The argument itself resembles a useful warning against [[CourtFeedbackCollapse]], but in context it also serves [[PoliticalParableWeaponization|political parable weaponization]] against a rival chancellor.
