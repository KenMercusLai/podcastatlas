# 何夔 / He Kui

何夔 / He Kui enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1020-sanguo-baijiazi-yuanshu-ruhe-ba-hao-pai-da-de-xilan-lhwr9hfmanr-zktleufy3kvl38u0|Hanji 1020]] as the late-Han official whose experience with [[YuanShu|袁术]] gives [[CaoCao|曹操]] a diagnosis of Yuan Shu's coming failure. The episode says He Kui had avoided disorder in Huainan, refused Yuan Shu's forced recruitment, and later served Cao Cao.

His judgment matters because it is both personal and structural. He Kui says political success depends on matching the age's direction and gaining trust, while Yuan Shu has neither. The host then uses He Kui himself as evidence: a regime that cannot accommodate a capable and upright official has already lost part of its usable capacity.

The episode also presents He Kui as cautious under Cao Cao. Cao Cao is severe enough that officials fear punishment, but He Kui's combination of principle, restraint, and careful conduct lets him keep integrity without becoming a target.
