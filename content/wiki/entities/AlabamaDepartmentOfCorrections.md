# Alabama Department of Corrections

## Overview

The Alabama Department of Corrections is the state prison institution examined in [[TheAlabamaSolution|The Alabama Solution]] through managed access, participant recordings, deaths, labor, parole, and alleged retaliation and concealment.

## Current Profile

The episode describes American prisons as unusually difficult for journalists to inspect and presents Alabama's system as an extreme case. Officially managed visits showed selected areas, while incarcerated people said solitary confinement, guard violence, rats, drugs, and other conditions remained hidden. When access ended, prohibited phones supplied an alternative evidentiary channel.

The institutional account also connects compelled work and parole. The episode says prisoners can face punishment for refusing work and alleges that people trusted for community jobs may remain less likely to receive parole than higher-custody prisoners, creating a possible retention incentive around reliable labor.

## Key Characteristics

- Controls access to a prison system the episode describes as highly secretive.
- Is examined through participant-generated footage and family recordings.
- Faces episode-reported allegations involving violence, deaths, contraband, retaliation, labor, and parole.
- Provides the institutional setting for a claimed conflict between worker reliability and release.
