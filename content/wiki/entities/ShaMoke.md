# 沙摩柯 / Sha Moke

沙摩柯 / Sha Moke enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1027-jingzhou-zhizhu-liubiao-weihe-nan-cheng-bazhu-ll-ebguqllsf-5ycvpdogihzn3lj|Hanji 1027]] as the [[WuxiMan|五溪蛮]] leader whom [[LiuBiao|刘表]] tries to court after the [[ZhangXianLateHan|张献]] and [[HuanJieLateHan|桓阶]] breakaway creates pressure in [[SouthernJingzhouLateHan|southern Jingzhou]].

His role in this source is narrow. Sha Moke is a sign that Liu Biao's strategic response has shifted toward defensive reinforcement: after [[CaoCao|曹操]] gains the southern-commandery attachment, Liu Biao looks for frontier and tribal support rather than shaping the main contest directly.
