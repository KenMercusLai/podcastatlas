## Overview

霍去病首次八百骑兵突袭 / Huo Qubing's First 800-Cavalry Raid is the source-scoped first independent operation that joins [[HuoQubing|霍去病]]'s physical and opponent-specific preparation to a reported battlefield result. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-298-shaonian-zhanshen-huoqubing-dengchang-lhwmfj24jl0ywwpcknytor9ujksh|Hanji 298]] supplies the preparation and detachment-selection stage; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-299-1-chudao-ji-dianfeng-huoqubing-17sui-yizhan-fenghou-lqi9lubm9ogxcrcpm837g8m6fq35|Hanji 299-1]] supplies the deep raid, intelligence-led target selection, result, and Champion Marquis reward. The event remains distinct from Huo's later Hexi and Mobei campaigns.

## Current Profile

The setup begins with the source's seventeen-year-old Huo seeking independent command under [[WeiQingHanGeneral|卫青]]. His claimed readiness combines combat, archery, and riding practice with questioning surrendered [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] about migration, camps, pasture, water, logistics, and likely behavior, followed by map and route rehearsal. Wei Qing then allows him to choose eight hundred cavalry, although preparation, kinship, imperial favor, and campaign need remain possible simultaneous explanations for that trust.

The execution layer says the detachment advances several hundred li away from the main force, locates camps, sustains itself partly from local livestock, and uses a captive report to identify a gathering of Xiongnu elites. The raid is therefore synthesized as a small-force intelligence-to-targeting loop: prior adversary study aids penetration, new information identifies a valuable target, and speed preserves surprise long enough to strike it.

The episode reports more than two thousand killed or captured, including senior figures and chanyu relatives, at very small Han loss, after which [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] grants Huo the Champion Marquis title. These figures, identities, losses, and mechanics are not independently established by the two compact podcast notes. The enduring judgment is narrower: the source sequence presents Huo's first command as an apparent validation of preparation, mobility, target value, and surprise, while the dramatic speeches and modern special-forces analogy remain framing.

## Key Characteristics

- First independent detachment in the current Huo Qubing source sequence, linking preparation and delegated trust to a reported result.
- Small elite cavalry raid that trades mass for mobility, penetration, surprise, and selection of a high-value target.
- Intelligence cycle combining prior questioning of surrendered personnel with new captive information gathered during the operation.
- Source-presented use of discipline and shared risk to convert initial troop skepticism into operational compliance.
- Political turning point in which a claimed battlefield success becomes the Champion Marquis title and public proof of command.

## Qualifications

The current profile rests on two consecutive popular-history podcast summaries rather than a primary-text dossier. They do not independently verify Huo's age, date and location, unrestricted troop selection, the quality or initial resentment of all eight hundred riders, maps, route, distance, supply conditions, camp sequence, captive testimony, target identity, casualty and capture totals, Han losses, or exact reward chronology. The full speeches, “铁骑特战队,” special-forces comparison, and claim that Huo could attack however he wished are dramatic or modern framing. The event page records the source sequence without treating the later Hexi and Mobei method as already proven in complete form.
