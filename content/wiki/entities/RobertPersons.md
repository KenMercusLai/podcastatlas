# Robert Persons

Robert Persons appears in [[692-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-massacre-in-paris-part-2-glt4665799025]] as the English [[Jesuits|Jesuit]] who presses for a mission to [[England]] with [[EdmundCampion]]. In the source, Persons stands beside [[WilliamAllen]] as part of the Catholic effort to preserve English Catholicism through priests trained abroad and sent home undercover.

The episode makes Persons central to [[PastoralMissionSubversionAmbiguity]]. His mission can be described as pastoral conversion, but it also sits inside papal loyalty, exile planning, foreign funding, and lists of Catholics who might aid [[Spain]]. Walsingham therefore reads Persons and the Jesuit mission through [[ElizabethanRecusancySecurityState|recusancy security politics]] rather than as only religious care.

Persons escapes after Campion is captured and executed. That survival matters because the source says priests continue entering England through Elizabeth's reign, keeping the security problem alive after the state makes an example of Campion.
