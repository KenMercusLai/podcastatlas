## Current Profile
Baojie's account joins independent care seeking with bounded technology use. After noticing a breast abnormality, she moved from nearby assessment to tertiary care and surgery. During recovery she used [[AntAfu|阿福]] for symptom questions, reports, food, activity, sleep, and risk explanations, while continuing to prefer clinicians when AI and medical advice differed.

Her experience also makes the family boundary concrete. She values being able to ask repeated low-pressure questions without burdening her son, but describes older parents as still needing affection, companionship, and emotional reassurance. The resulting profile is neither technological dependence nor refusal: digital help absorbs some explanation work while professional care and family presence remain separate supports.
