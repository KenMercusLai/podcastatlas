# 谷崎润一郎 / Tanizaki Junichiro

谷崎润一郎 / Tanizaki Junichiro appears in [[08-laojichao-laoji-busi-shenzhi-buzeng-diaoling-541092994]] as part of [[OkamotoKanoko|冈本加乃子]]'s early literary circle. The source says literary figures such as Tanizaki came through her family environment, and that he once looked down on her.

The page is currently limited to that source role. Tanizaki helps show that Okamoto's later literary recognition did not begin from an obvious consensus around her genius; the episode uses early dismissal, family literary access, and later praise from [[KawabataYasunari|川端康成]] to make her career path more uneven.

## Key Claims
- The source uses Tanizaki as a context figure, not as the central object of discussion.
- His early dismissive stance is part of the episode's [[LiteraryGossipAsContext]] branch around status, circles, and delayed recognition.
- The Okamoto episode connects him to modern Japanese literature through biography and literary networks rather than close reading of his own work.
