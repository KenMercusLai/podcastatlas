# Lockheed Bribery Scandal

The Lockheed Bribery Scandal appears in [[vol-268-liang-ge-lao-si-lai-si-1003563933]] as the political and legal tail of the [[LockheedL1011TriStar|L-1011 TriStar]] story. The episode says U.S. Senate investigations after Watergate uncovered overseas payments by U.S. companies, including [[LockheedCorporation|Lockheed]] payments connected to aircraft sales in [[Japan]].

The source's main Japanese case involves payments through [[Marubeni]] and [[KodamaYoshio|Kodama Yoshio]] around [[AllNipponAirways|All Nippon Airways]] purchasing TriStar aircraft powered by [[RollsRoyceRB211|RB211]] engines. [[TanakaKakuei|Tanaka Kakuei]] is the central politician named in the episode's Japanese aftermath.

The scandal matters to the wiki because it connects capital-intensive export sales, defense contractors, state-linked buyers, and domestic political accountability. The episode treats it as one path into the [[ForeignCorruptPracticesAct|Foreign Corrupt Practices Act]], while keeping broader claims about U.S. political motives toward Tanaka source-scoped.
