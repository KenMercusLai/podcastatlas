# Weyland-Yutani

Weyland-Yutani is the fictional corporation in [[the-real-horror-of-alien-and-how-it-explains-why-were-not-paid-enough]] that lets [[PlanetMoney]] translate the Alien franchise into labor economics. The company is presented as an exaggerated [[LaborMonopsony]]: a dominant buyer of labor that can underpay, overexpose, and trap workers because practical alternatives are weak.

In [[AlienFilm|Alien]], Weyland-Yutani's power works through a hidden mission clause and forfeiture penalty. In [[AlienRomulus|Alien: Romulus]], it becomes [[CompanyTownLaborControl]] through colony quotas and paperwork barriers that keep workers from leaving.
