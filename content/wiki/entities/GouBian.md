# 苟变 / Gou Bian

苟变 / Gou Bian appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl|Zhouji 09]] as the talented military figure whom [[Zisi|子思]] recommends to the ruler of [[WeiState|魏国]]. The source says Zisi considers him capable of commanding five hundred chariots.

The ruler hesitates because Gou Bian had once taken two eggs from a household while serving in a low-level tax role. Zisi argues that a state in wartime should use a person's strengths and discard lesser faults, turning Gou Bian into the episode's concrete case for [[UseStrengthsOverFaults]].

## Connections
- [[Zisi|子思]] - recommender and interpreter of his usefulness.
- [[WeiState|魏国]] and [[WeiWuhou|魏武侯]] - political-military setting.
