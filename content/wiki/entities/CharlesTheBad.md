# Charles the Bad

## Overview

Charles the Bad is the king of Navarre and French political rival whose dynastic claim, factional murder, shifting Anglo-French alliances, prison escape, and estate politics deepen France's crisis before and after Poitiers.

## Current Profile

The earlier episode presents Charles as a dynastically connected but opportunistic challenger. After [[JohnIIOfFrance|John II]] favors Charles of Spain, Charles arranges the favorite's murder, negotiates with the English when his Norman lands are confiscated, reconciles with John, and then reopens English alignment. His changing position repeatedly disrupts peace efforts and English campaign plans.

Charles wins over the debt-ridden Dauphin and Norman nobles before John arrests him at Rouen in 1356 and executes several conspirators. The House of Navarre then repudiates the French crown, Norman disorder opens ports and alliances to England, and Lancaster's landing becomes part of the campaign that ends at [[BattleOfPoitiers|Poitiers]].

After escaping prison in 1357, Charles returns to Paris as a hero, pressures the Dauphin over seized lands and executed allies, empties prisons, raises an army in Normandy, and enters civil war.

He suppresses the [[Jacquerie]] and gains noble support, but loses it when he tries to rule Paris through the third estate. [[CharlesVOfFrance|Charles V]] eventually outmaneuvers him, strips away his French holdings, and leaves him confined politically to Navarre.

## Key Characteristics

- Navarrese king and rival participant in French dynastic politics.
- Sponsor of Charles of Spain's murder in the episode's account.
- Repeated Anglo-French coalition-switcher before Poitiers.
- Popular prison escapee able to mobilize Paris and Normandy.
- Suppressor of the Jacquerie and temporary beneficiary of noble fear.
- Coalition-switcher whose estate politics erode his support.
- Ultimately defeated by Charles V's consolidation.
