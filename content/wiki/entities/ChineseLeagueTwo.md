## Overview
中国足球乙级联赛 / China League Two is the third professional tier in the Chinese football system, discussed in the source as necessary but still constrained by format, geography, and competitive-balance problems.

## Current Profile
The source supports keeping three professional tiers in a country of China's size, but treats China League Two as a fragile tier. Its current format is criticized for possible imbalance between regional groups and later championship or relegation groups, while the source suggests that a stable 24-club scale may be more realistic than rapid growth to 36 clubs.

## Key Characteristics
- Third professional tier whose existence is defended as necessary for a large country.
- Format concerns include regional grouping, uneven strength distribution, and fairness of later points.
- Scale is treated cautiously: 24 clubs may be workable even if a larger tier is theoretically possible.
