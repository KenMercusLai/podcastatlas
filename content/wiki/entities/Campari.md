# Campari

## Overview
Campari is the drinks company used in [[drone-truth-russia-has-the-upper-hand-6aad03f98a1508074de8337e]] to illustrate spirits makers competing for occasions traditionally dominated by beer.

## Current Profile
The episode's case is Aperol spritz on tap in Italy. It treats venue format, speed of service, and consumer willingness to try a cocktail at concerts or sporting events as category-expansion mechanisms during a wider [[AlcoholDemandReset]].

## Key Characteristics
- Competes beyond conventional spirits-serving occasions.
- Uses draft Aperol spritz as a venue-friendly format.
- Tests spirits-based drinks in beer-dominated channels.
- Illustrates occasion expansion rather than simple category growth.

## Evidence
### Venue expansion
- [[drone-truth-russia-has-the-upper-hand-6aad03f98a1508074de8337e]] says Aperol spritz on tap took an estimated share of Italy's premium beer market and sold strongly at a Bad Bunny concert in Milan.

## Qualifications
- Market-share and concert-volume figures are source-scoped and not independently verified here.
- One successful format does not establish an industry-wide reversal of declining alcohol demand.

## What Changed
- Added Campari as a case of spirits companies contesting beer occasions through format innovation.

## Relationships
- [[AlcoholDemandReset]] - demand backdrop motivating occasion expansion.
- [[PremiumReadyToDrinkAlcohol]] - adjacent format strategy for convenient cocktails.
- [[NonAlcoholicBeerGrowth]] - competing response to weaker conventional alcohol demand.
