# 冀州 / Jizhou (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] supplies the transfer mechanics behind Jizhou's later aftermath. The province is presented as militarily and materially strong enough to resist: Han Fu's advisers claim it can gather a very large army and has grain for years. Its loss comes because [[YuanShao|袁绍]] makes [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] misread the threat field, not because Jizhou is already exhausted.

冀州 / Jizhou (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] as the northern province [[YuanShao|袁绍]] takes from [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]]. The episode treats Jizhou as more than territory: it is the base whose loss strips Han Fu of office, team, and safety while giving Yuan Shao a stronger personnel and military platform.

The page also anchors the adjacent branch in which [[XunYu|荀彧]] later arrives expecting Han Fu's protection and finds Yuan Shao already in control. Jizhou therefore connects Han Fu's collapse, Yuan Shao's adviser expansion, and the later northern rivalry with [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]].
