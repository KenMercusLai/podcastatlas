# 乐叔 / Le Shu

乐叔 / Le Shu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-182-weishenme-shuo-chengye-xiaohe-baiye-xiaohe-1-lhe3h27wdbju3cgsvukdvgkri1a9|Hanji 182]] as the grandson of [[LeYi|乐毅]] whom [[LiuBang|刘邦]] finds during the suppression of [[ChenXiRebellion|陈豨之乱]]. Liu Bang grants him the title Lecheng Jun and enfeoffs him at Yuexiang.

The episode uses Le Shu less as an independent political actor than as a symbolic pacification tool. By honoring a descendant of a famous Zhao-linked Warring States general, Liu Bang signals respect for Zhao's inherited memory while trying to stabilize local sentiment around [[Handan|邯郸]] and the wider [[ZhaoState|赵国]] region.
