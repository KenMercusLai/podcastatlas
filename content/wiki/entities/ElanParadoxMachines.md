# Elan (Paradox Machines)

## Overview
Elan is the founder and CEO of [[ParadoxMachines]] in a [[DataScienceWithSam]] episode on data foundations before AI. The source identifies him by first name and role, and presents him as a practitioner who has built and scaled data functions across high-growth companies.

## Current Profile
Elan's source role is to argue that AI projects fail when organizations lack a coherent data foundation. He says his decision to build Paradox Machines came after leaving a company, consulting as a free agent, seeing demand for AI, and noticing that many AI conversations lacked a serious data story underneath them.

His operating view combines executive strategy with implementation discipline. He argues that data teams should understand business context, enable controlled bottom-up exploration, and maintain production data systems that survive customer variation, latency requirements, pipeline errors, and changing business models.

## Key Characteristics
- Presents data expertise as the practical prerequisite for useful enterprise AI.
- Frames mid-market data work as an economic and organizational talent problem, not only a tooling problem.
- Treats dashboards and analytics as weak unless they change business decisions.
- Advocates a top-down plus bottom-up data culture with executive conviction and business-user exploration.
- Values practitioners who can move between strategy, architecture, and implementation.
