## Overview

Roland Baines / 罗兰·贝恩斯 is the fictional protagonist of [[LessonsNovel|《钢琴课》 / Lessons]], whose long life joins adolescent abuse, abandoned ambitions, single fatherhood, historical accident, aging, and family attachment.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Roland neither as a hidden genius nor a simple failure. [[MiriamCornell|Miriam Cornell / 米里亚姆·康奈尔]]'s abuse alters his relation to desire, intimacy, music, and self-worth; [[AlissaEberhardt|Alissa Eberhardt / 阿丽莎·艾伯哈特]]'s departure leaves him raising their infant son; and his late family life supplies warmth that conventional achievement measures overlook.

## Key Characteristics

- Reinterprets a pleasurable and damaging adolescent experience as adult abuse of a minor.
- Carries multiple artistic and athletic aspirations without sustaining them into public achievement.
- Raises his son after Alissa leaves the family.
- Searches Alissa's fiction for versions of himself and fears loss of control over shared truth.
- Confronts both Alissa and Miriam without receiving complete explanation or repair.
- Finds late-life value in children, grandchildren, stories, and ordinary presence.

## Qualifications

Roland is a fictional character, and the source offers a podcast interpretation rather than direct psychological or legal assessment. His remembered pleasure does not erase the adult's responsibility, while his later care work does not erase self-pity, disorder, or the effects of his behavior on Alissa.
