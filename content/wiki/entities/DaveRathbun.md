# Dave Rathbun

## Overview

Dave Rathbun is a former member of the [[LosAngelesCountySheriffsDepartment|LASD]] Emergency Services Detail who searched for [[JohnAjay]] and later questioned both the reported suicide explanation and access restrictions around the case.

## Current Profile

[[from-valley-of-shadows-the-devil-s-punchbowl-from-valley-of-shadows-the-devil-s-punchbowl|The Devil's Punchbowl]] uses Rathbun's account to connect field search experience with later institutional concerns. He describes the difficult Antelope Valley terrain, the possibility that Ajay encountered criminal activity, physically exhausting search work, and the department's decision to end the operation on its sixth day.

Rathbun recalls Ajay as dependable, quiet, severe, and difficult to read. He also reports that access to the case file required unusual permission, discussion was discouraged, and a retired cold-case detective declined review because of perceived career risk. These recollections make him an important insider witness but do not independently establish Ajay's fate or deliberate suppression.

## Key Characteristics

- Former LASD Emergency Services Detail member.
- Participant in the 1998 search for Ajay.
- Source on the terrain, armed-search posture, and search termination.
- Skeptic of a conclusive suicide attribution without recovered remains.
- Source of unresolved claims about unusual file controls and professional deterrence.

## Qualifications

Rathbun's direct participation gives his account practical weight, but the source is a retrospective structured summary and does not provide contemporaneous logs, access rules, or the named officials' responses. His recollection of Ajay's remarks is ambiguous, and his file-access claims support further review rather than proving a cover-up.
