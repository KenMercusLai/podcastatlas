## Current Profile
The episode presents Xiao Yu as a teacher who returned to work at her former high school after entering a teacher-training pathway partly for stable employment. She teaches senior-high physics and also participates in student work and campus activities. Her account is valuable as practitioner testimony about how formal score expectations interact with class management, psychological-risk recognition, parent demands, non-teaching duties, physical strain, and reform implementation.

Her profile is explicitly bounded. The episode does not provide a full legal name, school identity, independently verified employment record, or evidence that her observations represent every Chinese school. No broader identity is inferred from “小雨.”
