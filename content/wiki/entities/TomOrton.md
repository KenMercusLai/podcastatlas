# Tom Orton

## Overview
Tom Orton is the retired Rutgers agriculture professor identified as a co-breeder of [[ScarletSunrise|Scarlet Sunrise]].

## Current Profile
Orton's role in the episode is to make the long experimental path behind a branded tomato visible. He represents the scientific breeding side of the case: choosing parent plants, moving through generations, and selecting for a combination of flavor, appearance, durability, and agronomic performance before commercialization can begin.

## Key Characteristics
- Retired [[RutgersUniversity|Rutgers University]] agriculture professor.
- Co-breeder of Scarlet Sunrise with [[PeteNitzsche]].
- Represents the slow plant-selection work behind a simple consumer-facing produce name.
- Connects academic crop science to market-facing variety protection.

## Evidence
