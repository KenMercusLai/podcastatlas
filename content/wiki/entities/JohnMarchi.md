# John Marchi

## Overview

John Marchi was a Staten Island legislator who designed the two-stage state-authorized process that moved borough secession from grievance toward a charter and referendum.

## Current Profile

Marchi developed a sequence in which Staten Islanders would first vote on whether to study separation and draft a charter, then vote on the completed plan in 1993. New York State approved that route, giving organizers a formal political process around which to build a campaign.

The design separated preliminary authorization from the final choice. It allowed institutional details to be developed before voters decided whether to leave, while preserving the state's decisive legal role. [[DanSingletary]] and other organizers then supplied the grassroots petitioning and party work needed to turn Marchi's framework into electoral activity.

## Key Characteristics

- Staten Island legislator and institutional architect of the secession process.
- Designed separate study-and-charter and final-referendum stages.
- Secured state approval for the process described by the episode.
- Connected local grievance to a legally structured political pathway.
