# Zhiyuan Robotics

## Overview
Zhiyuan Robotics is a Chinese robotics company used in the wiki as a body-company and commercialization example inside the embodied-intelligence value chain.

## Current Profile
The current bounded evidence portrays Zhiyuan as a commercialization- and mass-production-oriented robot company rather than only a model-brain lab. [[XieChen]] uses it as a body-company example in an ecosystem where robot hardware, data/simulation, model brains, and scenario owners may remain partly separate. The LateTalk episode adds company-specific business signals: source-reported R&D spend around 500 million RMB, staff scale around 1,300 people, top-tier domestic sales placement, and a structured partner channel that includes manufacturing suppliers as sales and scene-expansion partners.

## Key Characteristics
- Zhiyuan is treated as a Chinese robot-body and commercialization example in the embodied-AI ecosystem.
- The company appears among the reported top two domestic humanoid sellers in the LateTalk sales snapshot, though the figure is not an industry-audited statistic.
- Its source-reported R&D spend is higher than some peers because of staff scale and broader organization buildout.
- Its partner-channel structure suggests a deliberate attempt to convert supplier and manufacturing relationships into robot sales, scenarios, and support capacity.
- The channel model can help commercialization but also needs revenue-quality scrutiny when partners are also investors, suppliers, or capital-market beneficiaries.

## Evidence
- Ecosystem-position evidence: [[134-shuju-de-zongshu-he-xiechen-liao-xinshidai-de-shiyou-lishi-bantu-shuju-jinzita-dingjia-yu-recipe]] names Zhiyuan as a relatively strong commercialization and mass-production body-company example.
- Sales-ranking evidence: [[jushen-zhineng-de-jinqian-youxi-jinzhan-nance-shouru-cuishu-yu-ipo-jingsu-1-180-1]] reports that the top domestic humanoid sales names are Unitree, Zhiyuan, and UBTECH, with the first two materially ahead of later companies.
- R&D-scale evidence: [[jushen-zhineng-de-jinqian-youxi-jinzhan-nance-shouru-cuishu-yu-ipo-jingsu-1-180-1]] says Zhiyuan's R&D investment may have been around 500 million RMB, partly because the organization had roughly 1,300 people.
- Channel evidence: [[jushen-zhineng-de-jinqian-youxi-jinzhan-nance-shouru-cuishu-yu-ipo-jingsu-1-180-1]] describes a graded annual-sales partner system with 20 million RMB, 10 million RMB, 5 million RMB, and 2 million RMB levels.
- Supplier-partner evidence: [[jushen-zhineng-de-jinqian-youxi-jinzhan-nance-shouru-cuishu-yu-ipo-jingsu-1-180-1]] says higher-tier partners include manufacturing firms that can also explore robot deployment scenarios.

## Qualifications
The page should not treat source-reported sales rank, spending, staff count, or channel tiers as audited facts. The partner system may produce real sales, support, and scene discovery, but it can also blur final-customer demand if manufacturers are simultaneously suppliers, customers, investors, and public-market narrative beneficiaries.
