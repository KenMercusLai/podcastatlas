# 吴导 / Wu Dao (late Han)

## Overview

Wu Dao is the Runan commandery postal official whose grief over the order to arrest Fan Pang reveals the moral pressure inside the party-prohibition enforcement chain.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-893-rongyu-bang-ruhe-biancheng-siwang-mingdan-lmxzqoauf79-swwnk-0cevav0gq|Hanji 893]] gives Wu Dao a brief but legible role. As Runan duyou, he receives the order to arrest [[FanPangLateHan|范滂]], travels to Zhengqiang, closes the post-station lodging, and weeps in bed. Fan Pang reads Wu Dao's response as evidence that the order targets him and voluntarily reports to prison.

Wu Dao therefore marks the local-administration layer of the disaster. The source does not show him overturning the order, but his grief makes visible that enforcement can continue even when the official charged with enforcement recognizes the victim's moral standing.

## Key Characteristics

- Runan duyou responsible for carrying the arrest order against Fan Pang.
- Emotionally conflicted official whose grief signals the injustice of the order.
- Indirect trigger for Fan Pang's voluntary prison entry.
- Example of local enforcement friction under a centrally authorized purge.
