# Leopold II

## Overview

Leopold II was the Belgian king who personally controlled the [[CongoFreeState|Congo Free State]], the extractive colonial regime behind [[JosephConrad]]'s Congo journey and [[HeartOfDarkness|Heart of Darkness]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Leopold less through court biography than through the system operating in his name. Claims of progress and civilization accompanied a concessionary economy enforced through coercion, violence, and population flight. Conrad later described Leopold and his agents in monstrous terms, making the king's regime a specific target of the novella's attack even though the book never names him.

## Key Characteristics

- Held the Congo Free State as a personal possession during Conrad's 1890 journey.
- Used civilising and developmental claims to justify an extractive colonial project.
- Presided over a system associated in the episode with forced labor, armed violence, and ivory and rubber exports.
- Became a target of Conrad's later denunciation and of reform campaigns connected to [[RogerCasement]].

## Evidence

- Political control: [[541-heart-of-darkness-fear-and-loathing-in-the-congo-glt5482142786]] identifies the Congo Free State as Leopold's regime during Conrad's service.
- Justification and practice: [[541-heart-of-darkness-fear-and-loathing-in-the-congo-glt5482142786]] contrasts claims of progress with forced labor, beatings, starvation, and extraction.
- Conrad's verdict: [[541-heart-of-darkness-fear-and-loathing-in-the-congo-glt5482142786]] reports Conrad's later description of Leopold and his agents as an obscene predatory system.

## Qualifications

This profile is limited to Leopold's role in the episode's Conrad and Congo argument. It does not provide a full Belgian political biography, quantify the regime's mortality, or independently adjudicate every atrocity account.

## What Changed

- Created Leopold's profile as the sovereign authority behind the episode's colonial system.
