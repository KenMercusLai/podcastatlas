# Fremont, California / 福利蒙

## Overview
Fremont, California / 福利蒙 is the Bay Area city used in [[404-shaoyang-yiwei-huaren-shiyiyuan-qinlide-meiguo-difang-zhengzhi-f96bdc7a-c667-4594-8719-2070d24f8231]] as a concrete setting for Chinese American local politics, technology-industry geography, school-board conflict, homelessness-service siting, and city council transparency.

## Current Profile
The source presents Fremont as an East Bay city with a technology and manufacturing base. [[ShaoYang|邵阳 / Shao Yang]] describes Tesla's factory, technology-company connections, and Chinese battery or solar companies as part of the city's industrial importance.

Fremont's governance role is more important than its industrial profile in this source. Shao uses the city to explain council-manager government, part-time elected officials, public council materials, 72-hour agenda disclosure, livestreamed meetings, archives, and restrictions on private coordination among officials. The homelessness navigation-center debate and 2020 curfew response make the city a case for [[MunicipalTransparencyCompromise]] and [[PublicSafetyFirstUrbanGovernance]] rather than only a Bay Area technology node.

## Key Characteristics
- East Bay city in [[California]] with technology, electric-vehicle, battery, and solar supply-chain significance in the source account.
- Local Chinese American political setting where voter participation, representation, and school politics are foregrounded.
- Council-manager municipality where elected officials set policy while professional management runs daily administration.
- Example of public-meeting transparency through agenda publication, public materials, broadcast meetings, and archived records.
- Homelessness navigation-center case where resident opposition was handled through data, siting, policing, and operating transparency.
- 2020 protest-response case where Shao describes a curfew and police deployment to protect commercial areas.
