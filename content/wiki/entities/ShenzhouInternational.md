# Shenzhou International / 申洲国际

## Overview

Shenzhou International / 申洲国际 is a Ningbo textile and apparel manufacturer whose wiki profile now spans flexible manufacturing, exporter pressure, and vertically integrated OEM capability. The newest source turns Shenzhou from a supporting annual-report and quick-response example into a full business-history case built around [[MaBaoxing|马宝兴]], [[MaJianrong|马建荣]], quality discipline, environmental investment, international customers, and [[ApparelOEMVerticalIntegration|apparel OEM vertical integration]].

## Current Profile

Shenzhou is best understood in this corpus as an OEM supplier that deepened the manufacturing role rather than escaping it through a consumer brand. [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]] presents the company as able to meet demanding Japanese orders, absorb quality crises, invest early in wastewater treatment, serve core customers such as [[Uniqlo]], [[Nike]], [[Adidas]], [[Puma]], and [[Lululemon]], and compress lead times by colocating knitting, dyeing, printing, embroidery, garment making, inspection, and shipment.

That profile complements the earlier sources. [[ep271-tanfang-rouxing-zhizao-gongchang-shi-shui-rang-ni-xiangyao-jiu-neng-maidao-lqyrioptggnbb8hl-llej0-5ow0v]] uses Shenzhou as a rapid-response apparel case, while [[165-nianbaoji-zhong-de-zhenshi-zhongguo-2026-lpredevu-gakn92dwutmulytmslo]] uses it to show exporter margin and globalization pressure.

## Key Characteristics

- Shenzhou's moat is manufacturing-system depth: quality, speed, vertical process control, environmental compliance, and customer-specific coordination.
- The company shows that OEM success can be an upgrade path when the supplier controls enough process and reliability to become hard to replace.
- Its international customer base gives scale and learning, but the source also shows high customer concentration risk.
- Its exporter profile is exposed to tariffs, labor costs, logistics, exchange rates, weak demand, and overseas-capacity friction.
- Its flexible-manufacturing role is strongest when market windows are short and apparel orders need fast, reliable execution.

- **Environmental and customer trust:** [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]] says Ma Jianrong invested a year's profit into wastewater treatment and then met a short-deadline Uniqlo order, helping build a long customer relationship.
- **Vertical integration and speed:** [[no-217-ningbo-wangshi-cong-fangtai-dao-shenzhou-cong-youyanji-dao-zhongguo-zui-da-fuzhuang-daigong-jutou-1010906666]] describes colocated process stages and lead-time compression as the core Shenzhou capability.
- **Flexible surge response:** [[ep271-tanfang-rouxing-zhizao-gongchang-shi-shui-rang-ni-xiangyao-jiu-neng-maidao-lqyrioptggnbb8hl-llej0-5ow0v]] uses championship football jerseys for [[Adidas]], [[Nike]], and [[France]] to show how speed converts a short-lived sports event into product, production, and shipment.

## Qualifications

- Reported 2025 revenue, profit, customer concentration, employee count, capacity, lead time, and market figures are source-scoped claims from the episode.
- Shenzhou is not treated as a universal proof that OEM beats branding; the source contrasts it with Fangtai's brand route to show that different manufacturing strategies can work.
- High customer concentration is both an efficiency source and a risk; the page should preserve both sides.
- The annual-report source warns that moving production overseas does not automatically remove tariff, labor, ramp-up, or compliance pressure.
