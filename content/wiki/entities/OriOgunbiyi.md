# Ori Ogunbiyi

Ori Ogunbiyi appears in [[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]] as the [[TheIntelligence]] participant explaining cocaine trafficking through [[WestAfrica]]. Her segment links geography, European enforcement pressure, weak local enforcement capacity, corruption, and high European cocaine demand.

Ogunbiyi's main contribution is [[WestAfricaCocaineTransit]]. She says traffickers bulk-ship cocaine to West Africa, repackage it, use mother vessels, and transfer it to fast boats headed for Europe, while officials often lack the technology, pay, and port or maritime capacity to keep up.
