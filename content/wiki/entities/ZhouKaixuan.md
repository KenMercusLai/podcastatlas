# 周凯旋 / Zhou Kaixuan

Zhou Kaixuan appears in [[e226-liaoliao-deepmind-chuangshiren-hasabisi-yige-kexuejia-yu-shikong-de-ai-jingsai-7abda28b-99c6-4ebc-8c0d-37bcc77f6a73]] as a key financing figure for [[DeepMind]]. The episode says she helped DeepMind complete a roughly $25 million B round at a moment when the company still needed unusual investors willing to fund an AGI thesis.

Her page is source-scoped. In the episode, Zhou's importance is less a full investor profile than a signal that [[DeepMindAcquisitionChoice]] depended on patient, nonstandard capital before [[Google]], [[Facebook]], or [[ElonMusk]] became central to the acquisition path.
