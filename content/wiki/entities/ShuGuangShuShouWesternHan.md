## Overview

疏广与疏受 / Shu Guang and Shu Shou enter the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-431-zhichang-bidu-kuli-zhizi-zhanganshi-de-zhichang-zhihui-lmxey8uoliwynimxcnh1khjefenl|Hanji 431]] as Western Han crown-prince teachers who retire together after reaching rank, reputation, and reward sufficiency. The source transcript writes their names as 叔广 and 叔寿; this canonical page uses the standard historical names 疏广 and 疏受.

## Current Profile

Their profile has two linked lessons. First, Shu Guang tells Shu Shou that satisfaction prevents humiliation and knowing where to stop prevents danger. They decide that reaching two-thousand-shi rank, honor, and wealth is enough, so they ask to retire by claiming illness. Xuan approves and grants money; the crown prince and public send-off turn the departure into a praised example rather than disgrace.

Second, the pair's retirement is not private hoarding. Back home, they host neighbors, kin, friends, and guests, dispersing reward wealth instead of buying more property for descendants. Shu Guang argues that existing land and houses are enough if descendants are diligent, while too much wealth can blunt worthy children and empower foolish ones. The page therefore treats them as a joint case of [[TimelyRetirementRiskAvoidance|知足知止式退场]] and [[WealthTransmissionRestraint|传财有度]].

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han crown-prince teachers whose timing makes retirement appear wise rather than evasive.
- Treat sufficient rank, merit, and wealth as the signal to leave office.
- Receive imperial and crown-prince gifts plus public honor at departure.
- Spend reward wealth socially after returning home rather than concentrating it as descendant property.
- Frame excess inheritance as a risk to both worthy and foolish descendants.

## Qualifications

The page is source-scoped to Hanji 431's retirement and wealth-transmission lesson. It does not reconstruct Shu Guang's or Shu Shou's complete biographies, their educational record with the crown prince, or the full original wording of their retirement exchange.
