# 雅兰 / Yalan (Luanfanshu)

雅兰 / Yalan is a speaker in [[270-da-chang-yazhu-ai-bangong-feishu-he-dingding-que-xian-chengle-peijue-lmb4dgcgov3mr4cn7cikbghpfro4]], a [[Luanfanshu]] episode on [[Feishu]], [[DingTalk]], [[Doubao]], and AI office competition. Her main contribution is the C-end commercialization critique of Doubao.

Yalan summarizes three possible consumer monetization paths for [[Doubao]]: GMV or product-card routing, advertising, and direct subscription. The episode presents all three as constrained: product-card volume is small relative to ByteDance's ad business, ads can damage answer trust, and consumer subscription conversion is low.
