# Xiao Zhan / 肖战

## Overview
Xiao Zhan / 肖战 is the Chinese entertainer whose fandom and commercial relationships anchor EP113's discussion of coordinated fan action, reporting, and accountability.

## Current Profile
The source treats Xiao Zhan less as a biographical subject than as the symbolic center of the 2020 AO3 controversy. Fans reportedly interpreted a feminized fictional portrayal as an attack, reporting activity was followed by the archive becoming inaccessible in China, and opponents redirected pressure toward Xiao Zhan and brands associated with him. The episode does not establish that Xiao Zhan directed the reports or controlled the participants, but argues that a traffic idol cannot be understood as wholly separate from the data, consumption, visibility, and commercial value produced in his name.

## Key Characteristics
- Symbolic center around which heterogeneous fan identities and collective tasks are organized.
- Commercial beneficiary of fan attention, purchasing, promotion, and reputation work in the episode's account.
- Target of backlash that expanded from disputed fan conduct to endorsements and brand value.
- Accountability case in which benefit, influence, control, and responsibility do not fully coincide.
