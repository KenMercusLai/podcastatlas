# 张懿 / Zhang Yi (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-939-donghan-luanju-de-fengkuang-sanguo-xuqu-lthmvw8ihk96fuez9vamlryzcngy|Hanji 939]] introduces 张懿 / Zhang Yi as the inspector of [[BingzhouLateHan|并州]] killed by the [[TugeHu|屠各胡]]. The episode uses his death as evidence that formal provincial office can no longer guarantee command, protection, or local obedience in the late-Han frontier field.

Zhang Yi's killing also forms part of the setup for [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s proposal to replace weaker inspectors with stronger provincial shepherds. In this source, the reform is not an abstract administrative preference; it follows concrete cases where inspectors are exposed to rebels, frontier groups, and local collapse.
