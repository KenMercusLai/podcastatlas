# 犍为郡 / Qianwei Commandery (late Han)

犍为郡 / Qianwei Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-940-de-minxin-zhe-de-tianxia-ni-xin-bu-xin-loipf7b0p5a-yh0rj2njnsml8a1j|Hanji 940]] as one of the [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] commanderies reached by the [[MaXiangLateHan|马相]] and [[ZhaoZhiLateHan|赵祗]] revolt. The episode pairs it with [[BaCommanderyLateHan|巴郡]] to mark the rebellion's rapid spread from [[MianzhuLateHan|绵竹]].

In the source's logic, Qianwei matters as evidence that provincial disorder can scale quickly when the first official target has already lost legitimacy. The commandery is therefore a geography node for the transition from local uprising to province-wide insecurity.
