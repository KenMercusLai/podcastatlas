# Michael Levin

## Overview
Michael Levin is presented as a developmental biologist whose contribution connects collective cellular intelligence, bioelectric coordination, regeneration, cognitive scale, and future freedom of embodiment.

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Levin treats bodies as nested ecosystems of molecular, cellular, organ-level, and organism-level agency. His [[CognitiveLightCone]] framework shifts attention from biological material to the scale of goals and compassion a being can sustain, while his warning about mind blindness asks science to identify goals in unfamiliar cyborgs, hybrids, and collectives.

## Key Characteristics
- Interprets development and regeneration as goal-directed coordination among cells.
- Uses cognitive light cones to compare minds across spatial and temporal scales.
- Separates morally relevant capacities from chromosomes or familiar embodiment.
- Anticipates regenerative medicine enabling radically diverse bodies and senses.
- Treats art and poetry as complements to scientific mind-recognition tools.
