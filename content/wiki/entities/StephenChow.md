# 周星驰 / Stephen Chow

## Overview

## Current Profile

The source treats Chow's film style as capable of carrying tragedy under comic surface. It does not provide a full biography or career analysis; its relevant claim is that the film's jokes, monster scenes, romance, and Buddhist props can be read as one coherent cultivation allegory.
