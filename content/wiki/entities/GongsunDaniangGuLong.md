## Overview

公孙大娘（古龙人物） / Gongsun Daniang is a martial artist, master of disguise, and leader within 红鞋子 in [[EmbroideredBanditGuLong|《绣花大盗》]]. She is presented as a descendant of the Tang-dynasty sword-dance figure rather than the historical performer herself.

## Current Profile

The bounded episode treats Gongsun as the novel's most distinctive woman and [[LuXiaofeng|陆小凤]]'s strongest equal in intelligence, martial ability, stage presence, and strategic play. Their three contests allow both to set traps, recognize weakness, change the terms of pursuit, and eventually cooperate. Her sword work also converts the imagery of sword dance into dangerous action rather than decoration.

Her moral position is deliberately unstable. She and 红鞋子 use lethal or extralegal punishment, yet she does not betray her companions and ultimately aligns with Lu against [[JinJiulingGuLong|金九龄]]. The episode therefore resists a simple hero-villain classification. It also distinguishes her mature, forceful independence from [[XueBingGuLong|薛冰]]'s warmer romantic role and argues that adaptations lose this contrast when they merge the two.

## Key Characteristics

- Matches Lu Xiaofeng through force, intelligence, disguise, and strategic confidence.
- Uses multiple identities to control how other characters classify and pursue her.
- Turns sword-dance imagery into a union of art, danger, and bodily command.
- Moves from apparent suspect and opponent to covert collaborator.
- Combines loyalty to companions with killing and private punishment.
- Resists both simple villain classification and reduction to a romantic match.
