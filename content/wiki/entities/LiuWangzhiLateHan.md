# 刘望之 / Liu Wangzhi (late Han)

## Overview
刘望之 / Liu Wangzhi is a Nanyang scholar-official whose failed withdrawal from [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s service becomes a late-Han warning that resignation does not itself end political exposure.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1096 presents Liu Wangzhi after two friends have already been killed through accusation under Liu Biao. When his own advice is rejected, he resigns in anger and returns home. His younger brother urges him to leave the region entirely, arguing that remaining nearby is not the same as escaping the ruler's reach. Liu Wangzhi refuses, is arrested, and is killed; his brother then flees toward Yangzhou.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves Liu Biao as a recruited subordinate in Jingzhou.
- Resigns after his advice is rejected rather than accommodating the court.
- Underestimates the difference between leaving office and leaving a ruler's coercive reach.
- Becomes the negative case in a group of Jingzhou survival choices.

## Qualifications
This profile rests on one short popular-history episode and does not reconstruct Liu Wangzhi's complete career, the accusations against his friends, or the legal process behind his death.
