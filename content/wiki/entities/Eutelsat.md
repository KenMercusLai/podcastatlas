# Eutelsat

## Overview
Eutelsat is a French satellite company whose wiki profile now spans commercial connectivity in Africa and ownership of [[OneWeb]], a strategically important but incomplete European alternative to [[Starlink]].

## Current Profile
Eutelsat operates as one provider in a mixed fibre, mobile, and satellite future, particularly through business-facing infrastructure that extends mobile access into remote areas. This role matters because satellite connectivity is not a single-company or purely direct-to-consumer market.

The European security dimension comes through OneWeb ownership: its existing low-earth-orbit constellation gives Europe a base to expand if access to American systems becomes uncertain. Its much smaller scale and less favorable terminal economics mean ownership creates strategic optionality rather than current equivalence with Starlink.

## Key Characteristics
- French satellite operator participating in commercial and strategic connectivity markets.
- Business-facing contributor to remote mobile connectivity and satellite backhaul in Africa.
- Owner of the OneWeb low-earth-orbit constellation.
- European-controlled alternative whose current scale remains well below Starlink.

## Qualifications
The sources do not establish current coverage, capacity, pricing, profitability, military readiness, or contractual control. OneWeb's strategic value does not make it a complete Starlink substitute, and satellite backhaul does not eliminate terrestrial infrastructure needs.
