# 北宋 / Northern Song

The Northern Song is the historical setting for [[105-su-dongpo-yiqiang-haomai-zhiqi-yi-dupi-buhe-shiyi-741072645]] and the founding outcome of [[169-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-shang-957052909]]. The Su Shi episode places [[SuShi|苏轼]] inside a world where literary talent, examination success, memorial-writing, local office, censorial accusation, and court faction all belonged to the same scholar-official career.

The source's Northern Song is neither a quiet literary golden age nor a simple villainous bureaucracy. Fiscal strain and security concerns give the [[WangAnshiReforms|王安石变法]] a serious motive; [[SongFactionalPolitics|宋代党争]] gives Su Shi's criticism real consequences; and offices in [[Hangzhou|杭州]], [[Xuzhou|徐州]], [[Huangzhou|黄州]], [[Huizhou|惠州]], and [[Danzhou|儋州]] show how central politics and local governance constantly intersect.

The Zhao Kuangyin episode adds the dynasty's founding layer. [[ZhaoKuangyin|赵匡胤 / 宋太祖]] emerges from [[LaterZhou|后周]] military politics through [[ChenQiaoMutiny|陈桥兵变]], then tries to prevent repeat coups through [[CupWineDisarmsGenerals|杯酒释兵权]], [[SongMilitaryCentralization|宋初军事集权]], and the [[KaifengCapitalTradeoff|开封定都取舍]]. This makes later civil-official Song politics easier to understand as an institutional answer to a military-founder problem.

The second Taizu episode adds the dynasty's early unification and succession crisis. [[SongUnificationStrategy|先南后北]] presents southern conquest as a rational state-building sequence, while [[SichuanPostConquestResistance|川蜀征服后反抗]] shows how conquest violence and monopoly taxation created local resistance. [[SongSuccessionLegitimacyCrisis|宋初继承合法性危机]] then connects Taizu's death, [[ZhaoGuangyi|宋太宗]]'s accession, and the later treatment of alternative heirs to the formation of a civil-official order under legitimacy pressure.

[[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]] adds the predecessor-side frame. It treats Northern Song not as a sudden reset after chaos, but as the political outcome of [[FiveDynastiesTenKingdoms|五代十国]], [[TangSongTransition|唐宋转型]], southern-regime accumulation, military turnover, and later moral-historiographical ordering.
