# Kashmir Hill

Her source role is investigative and interpretive. Hill discusses [[AlanBrooks]]' chat history, severe mental-health crisis cases reported by the Times, OpenAI's statements about long-conversation guardrail degradation, and the broader question of whether human-like assistants can themselves harm users.
