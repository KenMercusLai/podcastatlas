# MDMA

## Overview
MDMA appears in [[essentials-using-meditation-to-focus-view-consciousness-expand-your-mind-dr-sam-harris-scim7293799235]] as the early substance experience [[SamHarris]] says made meditation and first-person inquiry into the mind seem worthwhile.

## Current Profile
The current wiki evidence does not treat MDMA as a general medical, legal, or pharmacological topic. Its role in this source is narrative and epistemic: Harris says the experience helped convince him that unexpectedly healthy psychological states and unconditional love were possible. The episode then qualifies that role by arguing that drugs wear off and that the lasting aim is [[PsychedelicOrdinaryFreedom]], not repeated pursuit of peak states.

## Key Characteristics
- Functions in the source as Harris's gateway into meditation and consciousness inquiry.
- Is linked to the claim that radically different first-person states can reveal what ordinary consciousness might later integrate.
- Is not presented as a complete path, a protocol, or a substitute for stable practice.

## Evidence
- Entry-point role: [[essentials-using-meditation-to-focus-view-consciousness-expand-your-mind-dr-sam-harris-scim7293799235]] records Harris saying meditation meant little to him before his first MDMA experience.
- Possibility proof: [[essentials-using-meditation-to-focus-view-consciousness-expand-your-mind-dr-sam-harris-scim7293799235]] says psychedelics or MDMA can show that unexpectedly healthy psychological states are possible.
- Integration boundary: [[essentials-using-meditation-to-focus-view-consciousness-expand-your-mind-dr-sam-harris-scim7293799235]] argues that drug experiences fade and need translation into ordinary waking consciousness.

## Qualifications
The page does not provide medical, legal, dosage, or safety advice. It records the source's account of Harris's past experience and keeps psychedelic claims bounded by context, age appropriateness, setting, guidance, and broader [[MedicalRiskManagement]] concerns.
