# 宋辟公 / Duke Pi of Song

宋辟公 / Duke Pi of Song appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] as the successor after [[SongXiugong|宋休公]] dies in 373 BCE. The source gives Song Xiugong's personal name as Song Tian before the succession.

His source role is a compact annalistic notice inside [[ZhouLiewang|周烈王]]'s early reign. It continues the [[SongState|宋国]] branch already tied to [[ShangRemnantStateLegitimacy|Shang-remnant legitimacy]].
