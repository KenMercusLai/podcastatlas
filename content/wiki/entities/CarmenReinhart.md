# Carmen Reinhart

## Overview
Carmen Reinhart is an economist presented by [[PlanetMoney|Planet Money]] as the coauthor, with [[KennethRogoff]], of influential 2010 research on public debt and economic growth.

## Current Profile
The source uses Reinhart's work as a case in how an empirical association can become a policy threshold stronger than the underlying evidence. The paper grouped advanced economies with debt at or above 90% of GDP and found lower average growth, but it did not establish a sudden universal cliff at that exact ratio.

## Key Characteristics
- Coauthor of the high-debt and growth study at the center of the episode.
- Associated with a cross-country historical approach rather than a single U.S. forecast.
- Part of a research controversy involving a spreadsheet error, robustness, and causal direction.

## Qualifications
This page reflects one retrospective podcast account, not a complete profile of Reinhart's scholarship. The source does not support treating 90% as a causal deadline, and it leaves reverse causality unresolved.
