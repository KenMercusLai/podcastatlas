# Shabana Mahmood

Shabana Mahmood appears in [[a-firm-andy-what-are-new-british-pms-plans-6a5deeca76f8ee25bb4bbf9c]] as the rumoured chancellor choice for [[AndyBurnham]]'s first cabinet. The episode presents the chancellor post as Burnham's most important appointment because economic policy is the least specified part of his governing programme.

The source frames Mahmood as a possible compromise figure. She is presented as less divisive than [[EdMiliband]] or [[WesStreeting]], which matters because the same episode warns that [[LabourPartyUK]] unity may weaken once Burnham starts making concrete choices.
