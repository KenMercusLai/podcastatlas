# Marauder (Saronic)

Marauder is [[Saronic]]'s larger autonomous vessel discussed in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-saronic-founders-autonomous-warships-chinas-230x-advantage-swarms-of-robot-ships-42334230]]. [[DinoMavroukis]] describes it as a 180-foot autonomous vessel and uses it to compare yearly firepower output against slower, more expensive crewed ships.

In the source, Marauder is the clearest example of [[NavalShipbuildingThroughput]]. Dino says one Marauder can carry the equivalent of 16 VLS tubes, so producing 20 per year would field 320 VLS-equivalent tubes annually; the wiki records this as a source-scoped Saronic framing of cost, time, and firepower economics.
