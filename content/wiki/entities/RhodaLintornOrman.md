# Rhoda Lintorn-Orman

## Overview

Rhoda Lintorn-Orman was the founder of the [[BritishFascisti]], whose scouting and First World War service background fed an early British fascist politics of discipline, patriotism, anti-Bolshevism, and fear of socialists and foreigners.

## Current Profile

The episode places Lintorn-Orman at the beginning of organized British fascism. She had participated in the Girl Scouts and served with distinction during the First World War before founding the British Fascisti in May 1923.

Her political turn is presented through alarm at Bolshevism, socialism, and foreigners. The resulting organization borrowed the language and practices of service, loyalty, uniforms, and discipline while connecting women organizers to military, aristocratic, and Conservative networks.

Her role also qualifies a male-only picture of fascist politics. Women were prominent in the British movement from its beginning, even though the episode does not provide a complete account of their membership, authority, or motives.

## Key Characteristics

- Founder of the British Fascisti in May 1923.
- Former Girl Scout participant associated with discipline and organized service.
- Decorated or distinguished First World War service participant in the episode's account.
- Anti-Bolshevik activist alarmed by socialism and foreigners.
- Example of women's early leadership within British fascism.
