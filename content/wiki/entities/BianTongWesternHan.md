## Overview

边通 / Bian Tong enters the wiki as one of three chief clerks named by [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]] in his final accusation and later executed after [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] reverses his judgment of Zhang's case.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-339-2-yi-si-sharen-kuli-zhangtang-shi-ruhe-zuodao-de-luq3jaa9iruhiqiqlduewbj-luca|Hanji 339-2]] places Bian with [[ZhuMaichenWesternHan|朱买臣]] and [[WangChaoWesternHan|王朝]] as three chief clerks hostile to Zhang Tang. Zhang's final letter identifies them as those who caused his death. After Zhang's modest documented estate and his mother's sparse funeral alter the emperor's reading, all three are executed and associated with Chancellor [[ZhuangQingdiWesternHan|庄青翟]].

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official identified as one of three chief clerks in the episode.
- Presented as an opponent of Zhang Tang.
- Named in Zhang's final written accusation.
- Executed after Han Wudi reopens the case.

## Evidence

Final accusation and punishment:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-339-2-yi-si-sharen-kuli-zhangtang-shi-ruhe-zuodao-de-luq3jaa9iruhiqiqlduewbj-luca|Hanji 339-2]] names Bian Tong with Zhu Maichen and Wang Chao in Zhang's final letter and says the emperor later has the three executed.

## Qualifications

The source gives only Bian's role in the terminal Zhang Tang case. It does not reconstruct his wider career, exact acts, legal charges, coordination with the other clerks or Zhuang Qingdi, or independent evidence of guilt.
