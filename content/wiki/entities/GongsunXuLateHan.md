# Gongsun Xu (Late Han)

## Overview
公孙续 / Gongsun Xu is Gongsun Zan's son in the late-Han Yijing endgame. His role is small but structurally important: he is the messenger and outside-relief node linking Gongsun Zan's trapped fortress, the Black Mountain forces under Zhang Yan, and the failed fire-signal coordination that Yuan Shao exploits.

## Current Profile
The bounded sources first show Gongsun Xu being sent from Yijing to seek help from the [[HeishanArmy|Black Mountain Army]]. The final source then places him with [[ZhangYanLateHan|张燕]] as a relief force approaches, while Gongsun Zan secretly instructs him to bring 5,000 cavalry to a northern lowland and wait for a fire signal. Yuan Shao's interception of that letter turns Gongsun Xu's intended rescue role into the hinge of a deception, and the source closes his arc by noting his later death at the hands of Xiongnu Tuge forces.

## Key Characteristics
- Familial relief agent for Gongsun Zan rather than an independently developed political actor.
- Link between Yijing's trapped center and Black Mountain outside assistance.
- Participant in a failed fire-signal coordination plan that Yuan Shao converts into a trap.
- Disambiguated late-Han figure distinct from the Warring States [[GongsunXu|公孙续]].

## Evidence
- Relief mission from Yijing: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1030-shouwo-hao-pai-gongsunzan-weihe-da-de-xilan-lpkntyov4viqyzgxgcujn36qhv8|Hanji 1030]] identifies Gongsun Xu as Gongsun Zan's son and the person sent to seek Black Mountain support.
- Failed outside coordination and death notice: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1031-gongsunzan-zhisi-cong-bianjiang-zhanshen-dao-kunshou-zifen-lmqoechccfwmfpmbdx8-tjmnm3jz|Hanji 1031]] places him with Zhang Yan's relief force, records the intercepted fire-signal instructions, and notes his later killing by Xiongnu Tuge forces.

## Qualifications
The sources do not develop Gongsun Xu's motives, factional base, or command competence in detail. His importance here is relational: he marks how Gongsun Zan's attempted external rescue depends on communication and trust channels already vulnerable to Yuan Shao.

## What Changed
- Hanji 1031 adds Gongsun Xu's role in the failed 5,000-cavalry fire-signal plan.
- His page now includes the later Xiongnu Tuge death notice.
- The profile now links him more tightly to Yijing's outside-relief failure rather than only to the earlier request for help.

## Relationships
- [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] - father and trapped commander whose rescue plan defines Gongsun Xu's late-Han role.
- [[YijingFortress|易京]] - besieged center from which the relief request and fire-signal plan originate.
- [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]] - outside force sought for relief in the Yijing crisis.
- [[ZhangYanLateHan|张燕]] - Black Mountain leader associated with the approaching relief force.
- [[YuanShao|袁绍]] - opponent whose interception of Gongsun Zan's letter turns Gongsun Xu's mission into a trap.
- [[GongsunXu|公孙续]] - separate Warring States figure sharing the same romanized name.
