## Overview

樊崇 / Fan Chong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-660-2-yi-ji-kan-dong-chimei-jun-de-dansheng-lgqk1gkvh-0cwder6g5-wryi6mce|Hanji 660-2]] as the early leader around whom the [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]] movement becomes visible in 22 CE. His current role is the origin layer: he is tied to the killing of Jing Shang, the growth of an organized eastern rebel force, and the cinnabar-eyebrow identification practice that gives Chimei its name.

## Current Profile

Hanji 660-2 presents Fan Chong's group as more organized than a spontaneous crowd. The movement reportedly uses rules such as compensating for killing or wounding, and it has internal grades such as sanlao, congshi, and zushi. Those details make Fan Chong's Chimei a disciplined rebel formation in embryo, even before later sources show its Chang'an, Liu Penzi, famine, and surrender phases.

The same source keeps Fan Chong inside a wider collapse field. Eastern famine and cannibalism, abusive Xin armies, relief corruption, and Wang Mang's ritual overreach all help explain why a rebel army can gain legitimacy by comparison with official forces.

## Key Characteristics

- Early Chimei leader whose group becomes prominent during late-Xin eastern disorder.
- Associated with killing Jing Shang and scaling an organized rebel force.
- Uses battlefield identification through cinnabar-painted eyebrows.
- Connected to internal rules and offices that distinguish Chimei from a pure mob.
- Origin-stage actor whose full later career remains mostly source-scoped here.

## Qualifications

This page is source-bounded to Hanji 660-2 and should not be read as Fan Chong's full biography. Later Chimei source notes mention Fan Chong in route decisions, Liu Penzi's selection, surrender, and renewed plotting, but those earlier wiki notes had kept him source-scoped; this page only promotes the origin-layer role now directly grounded by the Chimei-birth episode. Jing Shang, precise offices, troop counts, and Fan Chong's later motives remain source-scoped.
