## Overview

樊崇 / Fan Chong is the early leader around whom the [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]] movement becomes visible. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-655-wanzhong-guixin-dao-minyuan-feiteng-wangmang-zuocuo-le-na-yibu-lseopbzgmas0hyjrliikzgp5oxko|Hanji 655]] now supplies the earliest current layer: his Langya/Taishan group grows from roughly one hundred followers into a much larger rebel force by 18-19 CE. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-659-youxie-hexin-rencai-buyao-qingyi-dong-lg1nzal53ihdw-krici2thek-ij|Hanji 659]] adds the Yiping deterrence notice, and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-660-2-yi-ji-kan-dong-chimei-jun-de-dansheng-lgqk1gkvh-0cwder6g5-wryi6mce|Hanji 660-2]] supplies the organized eastern rebel force and cinnabar-eyebrow naming layer.

## Current Profile

Hanji 655 presents Fan Chong's group before the Chimei name becomes central. The group begins small, moves into Taishan, attracts other armed people through Fan Chong's reputation for bravery, and then joins a wider Qing-Xu rebel field. The page treats this as an early scaling layer, not yet as proof of the later Chimei institution.

Hanji 660-2 presents Fan Chong's group as more organized than a spontaneous crowd. The movement reportedly uses rules such as compensating for killing or wounding, and it has internal grades such as sanlao, congshi, and zushi. Those details make Fan Chong's Chimei a disciplined rebel formation in embryo, even before later sources show its Chang'an, Liu Penzi, famine, and surrender phases.

The same source keeps Fan Chong inside a wider collapse field. Eastern famine and cannibalism, abusive Xin armies, relief corruption, and Wang Mang's ritual overreach all help explain why a rebel army can gain legitimacy by comparison with official forces.

Hanji 659 adds that before the Chimei naming layer, Fan Chong's group could still be deterred by local force when an effective field official acted quickly. The page treats this as a limited early notice, not as a full biography or proof that the later Chimei movement was easily containable.

## Key Characteristics

- Early Chimei leader whose group begins as a Langya/Taishan rebel force during late-Xin eastern disorder.
- Pre-name rebel leader whose group is deterred from Yiping by Tian Kuang's mobilization.
- Associated with killing Jing Shang and scaling an organized rebel force.
- Uses battlefield identification through cinnabar-painted eyebrows.
- Connected to internal rules and offices that distinguish Chimei from a pure mob.
- Origin-stage actor whose full later career remains mostly source-scoped here.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 655, Hanji 659, and Hanji 660-2 and should not be read as Fan Chong's full biography. Hanji 655 and Hanji 659's 樊重 form is treated as likely Fan Chong because of the late-Xin rebel context, but the transcript-sensitive form remains source-scoped. Later Chimei source notes mention Fan Chong in route decisions, Liu Penzi's selection, surrender, and renewed plotting, but those earlier wiki notes had kept him source-scoped; this page promotes only the early scaling, pre-name, and origin-layer roles now directly grounded by the 655/659/660-2 sequence. Jing Shang, precise offices, troop counts, and Fan Chong's later motives remain source-scoped.
