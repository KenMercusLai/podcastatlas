## Overview

樊崇 / Fan Chong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-660-2-yi-ji-kan-dong-chimei-jun-de-dansheng-lgqk1gkvh-0cwder6g5-wryi6mce|Hanji 660-2]] as the early leader around whom the [[ChimeiRebellion|赤眉]] movement becomes visible in 22 CE. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-659-youxie-hexin-rencai-buyao-qingyi-dong-lg1nzal53ihdw-krici2thek-ij|Hanji 659]] now adds a cautious pre-name notice in which his group avoids Yiping after [[TianKuangXinOfficial|田邝]] arms local men. His current role is the origin layer: he is tied to the pre-Chimei armed field, the killing of Jing Shang, the growth of an organized eastern rebel force, and the cinnabar-eyebrow identification practice that gives Chimei its name.

## Current Profile

Hanji 660-2 presents Fan Chong's group as more organized than a spontaneous crowd. The movement reportedly uses rules such as compensating for killing or wounding, and it has internal grades such as sanlao, congshi, and zushi. Those details make Fan Chong's Chimei a disciplined rebel formation in embryo, even before later sources show its Chang'an, Liu Penzi, famine, and surrender phases.

The same source keeps Fan Chong inside a wider collapse field. Eastern famine and cannibalism, abusive Xin armies, relief corruption, and Wang Mang's ritual overreach all help explain why a rebel army can gain legitimacy by comparison with official forces.

Hanji 659 adds that before the Chimei naming layer, Fan Chong's group could still be deterred by local force when an effective field official acted quickly. The page treats this as a limited early notice, not as a full biography or proof that the later Chimei movement was easily containable.

## Key Characteristics

- Early Chimei leader whose group becomes prominent during late-Xin eastern disorder.
- Pre-name rebel leader whose group is deterred from Yiping by Tian Kuang's mobilization.
- Associated with killing Jing Shang and scaling an organized rebel force.
- Uses battlefield identification through cinnabar-painted eyebrows.
- Connected to internal rules and offices that distinguish Chimei from a pure mob.
- Origin-stage actor whose full later career remains mostly source-scoped here.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 659 and Hanji 660-2 and should not be read as Fan Chong's full biography. Hanji 659's 樊重 form is treated as likely Fan Chong because of the late-Xin rebel context, but the transcript-sensitive form remains source-scoped. Later Chimei source notes mention Fan Chong in route decisions, Liu Penzi's selection, surrender, and renewed plotting, but those earlier wiki notes had kept him source-scoped; this page promotes only the pre-name and origin-layer roles now directly grounded by the 659-660-2 sequence. Jing Shang, precise offices, troop counts, and Fan Chong's later motives remain source-scoped.
