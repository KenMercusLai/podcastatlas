# Oleg Gordievsky / 奥列格·戈尔杰夫斯基

Oleg Gordievsky / 奥列格·戈尔杰夫斯基 is the central figure in [[80-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-shang-691866649]], which follows [[BenMacintyre|Ben Macintyre]]'s [[TheSpyAndTheTraitor|《间谍与叛徒》]]. The episode presents him as a high-ranking [[KGB]] officer who became an ideological source for [[MI6]] after years of accumulating disgust toward the [[SovietUnion|Soviet Union]].

His path is framed as [[IdeologicalDefection|ideological defection]] rather than simple opportunism. Family disagreement, religious memory, Khrushchev-era thaw, the 1956 Hungarian crackdown, the Berlin Wall, and [[PragueSpring|Prague Spring]] all changed how he understood the Soviet system. [[Denmark]] then gave him a lived contrast through libraries, music, ordinary abundance, and foreign publications.

The episode's first half ends before the most famous escape. By then Gordievsky has worked under [[OperationSunbeam]], returned to Moscow, received the [[OperationPimlico]] escape protocol, and begun learning English in hope of a later posting to the [[UnitedKingdom]].
