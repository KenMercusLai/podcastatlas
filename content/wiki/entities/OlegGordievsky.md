# Oleg Gordievsky / 奥列格·戈尔杰夫斯基

## Overview

Oleg Gordievsky was a career [[KGB]] officer who became an ideologically motivated [[MI6]] source, provided strategic interpretation of Soviet thinking, and escaped the Soviet Union through [[OperationPimlico]].

## Current Profile

[[80-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-shang-691866649]] traces Gordievsky's [[IdeologicalDefection|ideological defection]] through family doubt, Soviet repression, foreign culture, [[PragueSpring|Prague Spring]], and lived contrast in [[Denmark]]. [[MI6]] then cultivated him through [[OperationSunbeam|patient trust-building]], respecting his initial refusal of money and his demand that reporting not harm colleagues. Source protection limited how Britain could use his information, while [[OperationPimlico]] made a rescue promise part of the relationship from the beginning.

[[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] follows his London reporting, [[OperationRYAN]], exposure by [[AldrichAmes|Aldrich Ames]], interrogation, signaling, and escape through [[Finland]]. Its central synthesis is that his highest value was [[StrategicIntelligenceInterpretation|interpretive]]: names and files mattered, but explaining Soviet fear, KGB incentives, and how leaders might read Western action helped Britain and the United States recognize [[ColdWarNuclearMisperception|nuclear misperception]].

[[37-spies-with-ben-macintyre-glt4382644548]] independently reinforces that strategic profile in a broader comparison of espionage. It says his reporting reached MI6, the CIA, Downing Street, and Reagan, helped Thatcher prepare for Gorbachev's 1984 visit, and made Soviet first-strike fear around [[AbleArcher83|Able Archer 83]] legible. The episode also preserves the moral asymmetry claimed by Macintyre: Gordievsky sought to avoid bloodshed, although the labels spy and traitor remain perspective-dependent.

## Key Characteristics

- Became an MI6 source from inside the KGB primarily through ideological rejection of the Soviet system.
- Combined document access with interpretation of Soviet institutional habits, fear, and leadership perception.
- Reported on Operation RYAN and the possibility that NATO actions were being read as first-strike preparation.
- Required patient handling, protected use of reporting, and a credible extraction plan.
- Survived exposure and interrogation before escaping through Operation Pimlico.
- Imposed severe costs on his wife, daughters, and later family life despite the operation's success.

## Qualifications

The sources rely heavily on [[BenMacintyre|Ben Macintyre's]] narrative and podcast retellings rather than open MI6 files. The exact causal weight of Gordievsky's reporting in Able Archer, Reagan's rhetorical change, Thatcher's preparation for Gorbachev, and wider Cold War policy remains source-scoped. Ideological motive does not remove the perspective-dependent fact that he betrayed his employing state or the family risks created by his secret work.
