# 全师雄 / Quan Shixiong

Quan Shixiong / 全师雄 is the leader pushed forward by Shu soldiers during the unrest discussed in [[170-songtaizu-zhisi-zhuying-fusheng-yinmoulun-zhong-de-renxing-mituan-xia-958158115]]. The episode presents his case as a flashpoint in [[SichuanPostConquestResistance|川蜀征服后反抗]] after Song commanders abused power following conquest.

The source says [[WangQuanbin|王全斌]] destroyed Quan Shixiong's family and used coercive sexual violence against his household, intensifying resistance rather than restoring order. The point is not to turn Quan into a full heroic portrait, but to show how conquest violence can make rebellion politically intelligible.
