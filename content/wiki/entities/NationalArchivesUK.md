# The National Archives (United Kingdom)

## Overview

The National Archives is the United Kingdom archival institution that hosted the exhibition *Treason: People, Power and Plot* and the two-episode document-based tour of British treason history.

## Current Profile

The paired episodes present the institution as a public-history setting where legal change becomes visible through material records. The medieval and Tudor half uses Parliament rolls, statute rolls, attainders, indictments, and execution records to connect the [[TreasonAct1351|Treason Act 1351/1352]] with changing prosecution and punishment. The modern half adds trial volumes, declarations, annotated prosecution papers, proclamations, and court-martial records, allowing visitors to compare how governments documented and enforced changing claims of allegiance.

## Key Characteristics

- Custodian and exhibitor of British state and legal records.
- Host of *Treason: People, Power and Plot*.
- Connects medieval legislation, Tudor expansion, regime conflict, republican revolution, and modern wartime allegiance through archival objects.
- Makes the production, survival, and later reuse of official records part of historical interpretation.

## Evidence

- Exhibition scope: [[249-treason-in-modern-britain-part-2-glt2159531504]] says the exhibition follows treason records from the 1352 act to modern cases.
- Medieval and Tudor foundation: [[248-medieval-treason-part-1-glt9532907553]] uses the statute rolls, attainders, Anne Boleyn records, poisoning legislation, and the Monteagle letter to reconstruct changes before the modern sequence.
- Archival method: [[249-treason-in-modern-britain-part-2-glt2159531504]] examines retrospective trial compilations, Restoration papers, annotated prosecution material, proclamations, and courts-martial.
- Public-history role: [[248-medieval-treason-part-1-glt9532907553]] and [[249-treason-in-modern-britain-part-2-glt2159531504]] were recorded at the exhibition with institutional guides [[EuanRoger]] and [[NeilJohnston]].

## Qualifications

This page represents the institution only through one temporary exhibition and two podcast recordings. It is not a general institutional history, collections guide, or statement of current exhibition availability. The episodes' readings of individual records remain source-scoped rather than complete archival descriptions.
