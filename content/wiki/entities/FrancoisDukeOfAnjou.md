# Francois, Duke of Anjou

Francois, Duke of Anjou, appears in [[694-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-a-treacherous-conspiracy-part-4-glt1627515087]] as [[HenryIIIOfFrance|Henry III]]'s younger brother, [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]]'s late-stage suitor, and a Catholic prince willing to work with Protestants. The episode treats him as a politique whose value to Elizabeth lies in [[MarriageDiplomacyAsStrategicOption]] against [[Spain]] more than romance.

Anjou's courtship was controversial inside [[England]]. Elizabeth was fond of him and used the possibility of marriage diplomatically, but Protestants such as [[JohnStubbs]] and Catholic opponents such as [[WilliamAllen]] attacked the match from different directions.

In the [[LowCountries|Low Countries]], Anjou accepted Dutch sovereignty in place of [[PhilipII|Philip II]] and was proclaimed Prince and Lord of the Netherlands in January 1581 after the [[ActOfAbjuration]] process. His rule failed because Holland and Zeeland distrusted a Catholic prince, his authority was constrained, and his January 1583 Antwerp coup collapsed in the [[FrenchFury]].

Anjou died of malaria on 10 June 1584. His death left Henry III without an heir, made [[HenryOfNavarre]] next in line, and helped drive the [[WarOfTheThreeHenrys]].
