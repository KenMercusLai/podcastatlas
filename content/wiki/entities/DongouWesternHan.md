## Overview

东瓯 / Dongou is a Yue polity presented in Hanji 252 as the target of a Minyue attack whose request for Han aid leads first to a contested relief mobilization and then to a large voluntary relocation into the Yangtze-Huai region.

## Current Profile

The episode's Dongou sequence has two stages. First, [[Minyue|闽越]] surrounds the polity and its ruler requests aid from [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]. [[YanZhuWesternHan|严助]] wins the policy argument for intervention, although the absence of a tiger tally initially blocks mobilization in Kuaiji; Minyue withdraws before the relief force arrives.

Second, continued fear of attack leads Dongou to request relocation. The source says the court moves the ruler and forty thousand residents into the Yangtze-Huai region. This makes Dongou a case of protection through population transfer rather than durable defense in place, but the source does not establish how voluntary the move was for every resident or what political identity survived afterward.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han-era Yue polity threatened by Minyue in the episode's account.
- Small-polity petitioner whose aid request produces a central-court intervention debate.
- Beneficiary of a relief mobilization that becomes unnecessary after Minyue withdraws.
- Community said to have requested collective relocation because future defense looked insecure.
- Population transferred into the Yangtze-Huai region under Han authority.
