# Sogdian Rock / 粟特岩堡

## Overview
Sogdian Rock / 粟特岩堡 is the high Central Asian fortress used to compare [[AlexanderTheGreat|亚历山大大帝]]'s cliff ascent with [[GanNing|甘宁]]'s seizure of [[YilingLateHan|夷陵]].

## Current Profile
The source describes a position cliff-bound on three sides with one narrow defended route. Its defenders treat the other approaches as impossible. Alexander's selected climbers reportedly ascend one such cliff at night and appear above the fortress at dawn, making the defensive assumption itself the vulnerability.

Its analytical value lies in the gap between physical occupation and perceived encirclement: a small force need not defeat every defender if its appearance proves that the supposedly safe side is no longer safe.

## Key Characteristics
- Fortress relying on the presumed impossibility of cliff access.
- Narrow conventional approach that encourages route innovation.
- Small-climber penetration represented as more important than frontal mass.
- Surrender case where perceived collapse exceeds attacker numbers.
