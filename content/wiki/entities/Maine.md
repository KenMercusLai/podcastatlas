# Maine

Maine enters the wiki through [[tech-20260423-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260423-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the state Marketplace Tech says passed the country's first statewide data-center ban, pausing new construction until late 2027. The episode uses Maine as evidence that [[DataCenterBacklash]] is moving beyond local protest into state-level infrastructure governance.

The Maine example connects most directly to [[HyperscaleDataCenterMoratorium]], though the source describes a statewide data-center construction pause rather than only a hyperscale threshold. In the wiki's AI infrastructure branch, Maine stands for a stronger form of [[DataCenterCommunityConsent]]: the state creates time for lawmakers and communities to reassess power, water, land-use, job, and local-benefit terms before additional facilities proceed.
