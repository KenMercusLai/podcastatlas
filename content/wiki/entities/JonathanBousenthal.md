# Jonathan Bousenthal

Jonathan Bousenthal is the [[TheIntelligence]] speaker in [[working-memory-the-surprising-decline-of-dementia-6a6b0eeca585d3be78cd9d1b]] who explains why dementia trends are more encouraging than raw population projections suggest. His segment distinguishes total case growth from [[AgeAdjustedDementiaDecline]], then walks through genetics, public health, lifestyle, vaccines, and drug-development limits.

His role is to turn dementia from a fate story into a prevention and evidence story. The source keeps causality uncertain, but uses Bousenthal's account to connect cardiovascular health, hearing loss, depression, childhood education, and vaccination to dementia risk.
