# GoPro

## Overview
GoPro is an action-camera pioneer whose wiki profile now combines category disruption by [[DJI]] with a later source-scoped optical-asset transaction involving [[StormOptical|Storm & Optical]].

## Current Profile
The earlier source uses GoPro as the incumbent that DJI pressures through product innovation and ecosystem breadth in action cameras. [[daisen-jinru-diandong-yashua-lingyu-chuantong-jindian-jiameng-chixu-shousuo-1010976825]] adds a later lifecycle stage: GoPro is reported to have agreed to a USD 285 million cash deal with Storm & Optical after losing almost all of its 2014 public-market value, making its patents, optical technology, and brand more important than its standalone consumer-camera growth story.

## Key Characteristics
- Defines the original action-camera category for many consumers.
- Faces hardware commoditization and ecosystem competition from DJI's newer camera products.
- Illustrates how consumer-electronics leaders can shift from growth companies into strategic-asset targets.
- Retains potential value through optics, patents, technical know-how, and brand recognition.
- Has a reported Storm & Optical transaction that remains subject to approval in the source.

## Qualifications
