# Kweichow Moutai / 贵州茅台

Kweichow Moutai / 贵州茅台 appears in [[zhongguo-yinglai-hanbao-kaidianchao-guochan-huangningmeng-jiage-huiluo-1006920543]] as a premium liquor brand facing a downcycle and channel adjustment. The source says its latest quarterly revenue and profit both declined year over year, while Huachuang Securities described the baijiu industry as still in a downturn without obvious demand recovery.

The episode frames Moutai's response as marketized supply and channel management. It describes more supply during peak season, price stabilization during off season, controlled group supply in the second-quarter low season, price increases for Feitian Moutai, price cuts for some high-end or series products, and stronger direct sales through i茅台, whose revenue contribution was said to be close to half of total company revenue.
