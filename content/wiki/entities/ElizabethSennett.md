# Elizabeth Sennett

## Overview

Elizabeth Sennett is the murder victim whose death begins the case examined by [[TheAlabamaMurders]] and whose family's long grief remains part of its final episode.

## Current Profile

The episode says Sennett's husband arranged her murder and recruited two troubled young men, including [[KennethSmith]], to carry it out. Its focus is the later punishment process rather than a biography of Sennett, but it repeatedly preserves her death and her sons' decades-long wait as essential context. The series' criticism of Alabama does not erase the murder or treat the losses of victim and condemned families as interchangeable.

## Key Characteristics

- Murder victim at the origin of the Alabama case.
- Wife and mother whose sons waited roughly thirty-five years for Smith's execution.
- Essential moral context for a series otherwise focused on punishment institutions and execution trauma.
- Represented only sparsely in the supplied final-episode summary.
