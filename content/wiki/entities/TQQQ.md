# TQQQ

TQQQ appears in [[vol-121-cong-tanhua-yixian-de-fenji-jijin-dao-fengtou-zhengjing-de-ganggan-etf-yongyuan-buyao-digu-renxing-de-fengkuang-lusagcitdozvzm8wausvecvi-qmb]] as the canonical three-times technology-index leveraged ETF example. The episode uses TQQQ's product-disclosure style to explain why a fund that targets daily multiples can perform very differently from three times the underlying index's long-period return.

The source uses TQQQ to make [[VolatilityDecay|volatility decay]] concrete: large up-and-down moves can reduce the leveraged fund's NAV even when the underlying index is near flat. That makes TQQQ a tool for tactical exposure rather than a default replacement for [[PassiveInvesting|passive index investing]].
