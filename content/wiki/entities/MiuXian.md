# 缪贤 / Miu Xian

缪贤 / Miu Xian appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-74-huanyuan-wanbi-guizhao-zhenxiang-lg1irvbbc1wp8oph2kmpatfoxebk]] as the Zhao palace insider who first buys [[HeShiBi|和氏璧]] for five hundred gold. The episode says [[ZhaoHe|赵惠文王赵何]] repeatedly hints that Miu Xian should offer the jade, and then sends troops to seize it when Miu Xian refuses.

His page is source-scoped. Miu Xian's role is to show that the jade becomes politically dangerous even before [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] asks for it: a court possession can become a royal object once the ruler's desire and coercive power are engaged.
