# Gerry Fitt

Gerry Fitt appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] as one of the Catholic civil-rights political figures present at the banned Derry march on 5 October 1968. The episode says the [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]] beat and arrested him, and that television footage of the police violence helped internationalize the crisis.

Fitt is later named among the civil-rights figures who urged Westminster to send troops during the August 1969 crisis, showing how Catholic leaders initially saw the [[BritishArmy]] as protection from loyalist violence and RUC hostility.
