## Overview

Liu An / 刘安 is a Western Han prince whose current wiki profile holds together an intellectual afterlife as patron of the [[Huainanzi|《淮南子》]] circle and a political memory as a defeated royal conspirator. The rebellion case first appears through its downstream investigation and now gains a source-scoped prehistory through rivalry with his brother [[LiuCiHengshanKingWesternHan|刘赐]].

## Current Profile

[[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997|Mihuan Chishu 87]] presents Liu An's fangshu interest as textual and encyclopedic rather than only miracle seeking. His circle gathers philosophy, astronomy, calendars, technical lore, and alchemical recipes, while later legend turns the politically defeated prince into an immortal figure.

The political layer remains distinct from that intellectual legacy. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-304-2-yin-tiaocao-yinfa-de-zhuhouwang-moufan-lnav5gprm-mmsbbugeflghixplf2|Hanji 304-2]] says Liu An and Liu Ci are hostile brothers and that news of Liu An's preparations leads Liu Ci to recruit independently rather than report him. Once Liu An's plot is exposed, investigation and testimony spread to [[LiuJiJiaodongKingWesternHan|胶东王刘寄]], showing how one royal rebellion case widens through knowledge, non-reporting, and suspected preparation. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-316-1-ta-gong-yue-bai-wang-weihe-zhi-xia-baixing-yuelaiyue-qiong-lu_tr4m36rmz9sx38j4nvlmof5am|Hanji 316-1]]

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han prince associated with a large knowledge-compilation circle.
- Patron figure linked to philosophy, astronomy, calendars, technical lore, fangshu, and alchemy.
- Political loser under a rebellion charge whose case spread risk to other royal relatives.
- Rival elder brother whose alleged preparations prompt parallel recruitment by Liu Ci in Hanji 304-2.
- Later immortalization figure in religious and folk storytelling.

## Evidence

Knowledge compilation and legend:
- [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997|Mihuan Chishu 87]] connects Liu An's circle to the Huainanzi's mixed philosophical, technical, astronomical, and alchemical material and to later ascent legends.

Rebellion-case aftershock:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-304-2-yin-tiaocao-yinfa-de-zhuhouwang-moufan-lnav5gprm-mmsbbugeflghixplf2|Hanji 304-2]] says Liu Ci learns of Liu An's preparations and begins recruiting because he fears absorption rather than reporting the plan.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-316-1-ta-gong-yue-bai-wang-weihe-zhi-xia-baixing-yuelaiyue-qiong-lu_tr4m36rmz9sx38j4nvlmof5am|Hanji 316-1]] says exposure of Liu An's conspiracy led to investigation and testimony implicating Liu Ji.

## Qualifications

The current sources do not reconstruct Liu An's full biography, the composition and authorship layers of the Huainanzi, the complete rebellion case, or the evidentiary and legal procedure used against implicated kings. The immortality material is later legend; Hanji 304-2 supplies only Liu Ci's perspective on the alleged preparations and brotherly hostility, while Hanji 316-1 supplies only the case's downstream effect on Liu Ji.

## What Changed

- Added the pre-exposure sibling-rivalry layer in which Liu An's alleged preparations prompt Liu Ci's parallel recruitment.
- Connected that local contagion mechanism to the already recorded downstream investigation of Liu Ji.
