# Liu An / 刘安

Liu An / 刘安 appears in [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997]] as a Western Han prince whose fangshu interest takes a more textual and encyclopedic form than [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s imperial求仙. The episode presents him as gathering guests and fangshu specialists, sponsoring large compilations, and becoming attached to later immortalization legends such as "one person attains the Dao, even chickens and dogs ascend."

The source keeps two Liu An images in tension: the historical prince punished under a rebellion charge, and the later immortal figure preserved by religious and folk storytelling.

## Key Claims
- Liu An's importance in the source comes from knowledge compilation rather than only from miracle seeking.
- His circle connects fangshu, philosophy, astronomy, calendars, technical lore, and alchemical recipes.
- Later legend turns a politically defeated prince into an immortalization figure.
- The episode treats this as evidence that fangshu material can preserve both speculative claims and concrete observations.
