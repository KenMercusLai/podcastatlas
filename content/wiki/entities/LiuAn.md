## Overview

Liu An / 刘安 is a Western Han prince whose current wiki profile holds together two later memories: patron of an encyclopedic knowledge circle associated with [[Huainanzi|《淮南子》]], and defeated royal conspirator whose exposed case implicated other kings.

## Current Profile

[[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997|Mihuan Chishu 87]] presents Liu An's fangshu interest as textual and encyclopedic rather than only miracle seeking. His circle gathers philosophy, astronomy, calendars, technical lore, and alchemical recipes, while later legend turns the politically defeated prince into an immortal figure.

The political aftershock remains distinct from that intellectual legacy. Once Liu An's plot is exposed, investigation and testimony spread to [[LiuJiJiaodongKingWesternHan|胶东王刘寄]], showing that a royal rebellion case can widen through knowledge, non-reporting, and suspected preparation. This sharpens the tension between Liu An's intellectual afterlife and the dynastic danger attached to his political defeat without reconstructing the conspiracy itself. ## Key Characteristics

- Western Han prince associated with a large knowledge-compilation circle.
- Patron figure linked to philosophy, astronomy, calendars, technical lore, fangshu, and alchemy.
- Political loser under a rebellion charge whose case spread risk to other royal relatives.
- Later immortalization figure in religious and folk storytelling.

## Evidence

Knowledge compilation and legend:
- [[87-guonian-zui-shihe-liao-xuanxue-tantao-shenxian-fangshu-fazhanshi-wei-wan-daixu-704051997|Mihuan Chishu 87]] connects Liu An's circle to the Huainanzi's mixed philosophical, technical, astronomical, and alchemical material and to later ascent legends.

Rebellion-case aftershock:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-316-1-ta-gong-yue-bai-wang-weihe-zhi-xia-baixing-yuelaiyue-qiong-lu_tr4m36rmz9sx38j4nvlmof5am|Hanji 316-1]] says exposure of Liu An's conspiracy led to investigation and testimony implicating Liu Ji.

## Qualifications

The current sources do not reconstruct Liu An's full biography, the composition and authorship layers of the Huainanzi, the complete rebellion case, or the evidentiary and legal procedure used against implicated kings. The immortality material is later legend, while Hanji 316-1 supplies only the case's downstream effect on Liu Ji.

## What Changed

- Added the downstream royal-investigation layer created by exposure of Liu An's rebellion case.
- Preserved the distinction between Liu An's knowledge-compilation legacy, political defeat, and later immortal legend.
