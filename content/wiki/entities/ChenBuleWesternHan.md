## Overview

陈步乐 / Chen Bule is the cavalry messenger sent by [[LiLingWesternHan|李陵]] to carry route and terrain drawings back to Chang'an. Hanji 377-2 says his report that Li Ling could inspire soldiers to fight to the death pleased [[HanWudi|汉武帝]], who appointed Chen Bule as a palace gentleman.

## Current Profile

The source places Chen Bule at the information link between a deep-frontier force and the imperial court. Li Ling has mountains, rivers, and traveled terrain mapped during the thirty-day advance, then sends Chen Bule to deliver the material and report. Chen Bule does not take a developed combat role in the episode; his importance is that reconnaissance becomes a transmitted court record before the army is encircled.

## Key Characteristics

- Cavalry messenger attached to Li Ling's expedition.
- Carrier of mapped route, mountain, and river information to Chang'an.
- Court witness who reports Li Ling's ability to command sacrificial loyalty.
- Recipient of appointment as a lang official from Han Wudi.
