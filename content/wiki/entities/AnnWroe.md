# Ann Wroe

Ann Wroe appears in [[omission-accomplished-why-the-iran-war-cycle-spins-on-6a6c7475aa1642a30f17f1cb]] as the [[TheIntelligence]] contributor giving the closing obituary-style profile of [[WallyFunk]]. Her segment presents Funk's life through persistence, early aviation skill, exclusion from the astronaut corps, and late spaceflight with [[BlueOrigin]].

The source uses Wroe's profile to connect biography with institutional gatekeeping. Funk's test performance did not translate into astronaut selection, so the episode treats the barrier as gendered exclusion rather than lack of ability.
