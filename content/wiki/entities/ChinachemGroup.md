# Chinachem Group / 华懋

Chinachem Group / 华懋 appears in [[vol-269-xiao-lishi-bu-yao-pa-shi-jishu-xing-tiaozheng-1004793119]] as the late local real-estate partner [[DairyFarmInternational|牛奶公司]] tried to use against [[HongkongLand|香港置地]]. The episode says Chinachem had moved from chemical-material trading into property after wartime and postwar business opportunities.

In the takeover story, Chinachem functions as a potential white knight rather than as the central subject. Dairy Farm announced a plan to cooperate with Chinachem on managing property assets, but the source argues that this counterproposal did not overcome Hongkong Land's stronger offer, media campaign, and shareholder mobilization.
