# 杨晖 / Yang Hui (late Han)

杨晖 / Yang Hui (late Han) enters through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-937-zhanshen-zhisi-haoren-zongshi-duozai-duonan-lp7przsmag9ookq7eoisvsmnm5aw|Hanji 937]] as the trusted figure to whom [[FuXie|傅燮]] entrusts [[FuGan|傅干]] before choosing to die at [[HanyangCommanderyLateHan|汉阳郡]].

The source gives Yang Hui meaning through analogy. Fu Xie calls him his "Cheng Ying", invoking the orphan-rescue story to define Yang Hui as a guardian who preserves the family line while Fu Xie completes his official duty. Yang Hui's page is source-scoped to this protection role.
