# 吴月娘 / Wu Yueniang

吴月娘 is [[XimenQing|西门庆]]'s main wife in [[fanwai-15-banlatte-jiang-jinpingmei-la-erqie-haishi-luse-de-991508433]]. The episode presents her as the household's 大房 and practical manager: she understands Ximen Qing's friend circle, warns that the would-be sworn brothers mostly exploit him, and evaluates their gift money without romanticizing brotherhood.

Her function in this source is structural. Wu Yueniang makes [[HouseholdAsSocialWeb]] visible because the household is not only Ximen Qing's property or desire field; it is also a managed institution with hierarchy, money, servants, illness, rituals, and reputational risk. Her clearer judgment gives the opening a domestic counterpoint to Ximen Qing's public face-making.
