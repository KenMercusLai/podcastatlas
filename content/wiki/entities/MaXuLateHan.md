## Overview

[[MaXuLateHan|马续 / Ma Xu]] is a Shun-era frontier commander whose current profile runs from a 134 CE Qiang-front operation against Liang Feng to the 140 CE Southern Xiongnu crisis. In the current wiki, he is important less as a simple offensive hero than as a commander whose role moves between attack, frontier familiarity, fortified defense, reward signaling, and surrender policy.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-816-xiangyao-naxia-nide-lingdao-qishi-hen-jiandan-lva3y4hgu7kn1z4jmhy6jn4-oyvc|Hanji 816]] gives Ma Xu's earliest current layer. After Liang Feng attacks Longxi and Hanyang and Shun appoints [[MaXianLateHan|Ma Xian]], the source later records Ma Xu attacking Liang Feng. This places Ma Xu in the Qiang-front response before his better-developed Southern Xiongnu role.

Hanji 820 places Ma Xu in the middle of the [[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]] revolt after [[WusiSouthernXiongnu|Wusi]] and [[CheNiuChanyu|Che Niu]] besiege Meiji and kill frontier officials. Ma Xu and [[LiangBingLateHan|Liang Bing]] defeat the rebels with a mixed frontier force, but the revolt regroups, showing that an early battlefield win does not settle the political problem.

When [[ChenGuiDuliaoGeneralLateHan|Chen Gui]]'s unauthorized coercion unsettles surrender prospects, Liang Shang argues that Ma Xu understands the frontier and that his letters already match the court's best strategy. [[EmperorShunOfHan|Emperor Shun]] orders Ma Xu to dig deep ditches, build high walls, show grace and trust, post reward deadlines, and avoid small victories that would damage the larger plan. More than 13,000 people under the Right Wise King's camp submit under this approach.

## Key Characteristics

- Frontier commander whose known roles include both Qiang-front attack and Southern Xiongnu stabilization.
- Effective in Hanji 820 through discipline, posture, and surrender signaling more than pursuit.
- Works as Liang Shang's operational instrument during the Southern Xiongnu revolt.
- Demonstrates that winning an encounter and resolving a frontier rebellion are different tasks.
- Sits between failed coercion by Chen Gui and later military cleanup by [[ZhangDanProtectorOfXiongnu|Zhang Dan]].

- Defensive-surrender command: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-820-zhichang-jingshi-xiaoxin-lingdao-rang-ni-bei-heiguo-lqkejieiihmupfifuk8sxp3oadap|Hanji 820]] says Ma Xu's campaign becomes tied to Liang Shang's advice to fortify, reassure, reward, and wait rather than pursue small wins.
- Limited meaning of early victory: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-820-zhichang-jingshi-xiaoxin-lingdao-rang-ni-bei-heiguo-lqkejieiihmupfifuk8sxp3oadap|Hanji 820]] first shows Ma Xu and Liang Bing beating rebel forces, then shows Wusi and Che Niu able to revive the revolt.
- Court trust through expert correspondence: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-820-zhichang-jingshi-xiaoxin-lingdao-rang-ni-bei-heiguo-lqkejieiihmupfifuk8sxp3oadap|Hanji 820]] has Liang Shang cite Ma Xu's frontier familiarity and letters as reasons to keep him in command.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 820 and Hanji 816. Ma Xu's lineage, wider career, and later service are not yet synthesized in this wiki. Hanji 816 establishes a Qiang-front action but does not provide the same strategic detail that Hanji 820 gives for the Southern Xiongnu crisis.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 816 as Ma Xu's earliest current layer, placing him in a 134 CE Qiang-front action against Liang Feng.
- Preserved Hanji 820 as the main strategic layer because it explains his fortified-surrender role in the Southern Xiongnu crisis.

## Relationships

- [[LiangShangLateHan|Liang Shang]] - supplies the strategy that Ma Xu is ordered to execute.
- [[MaXianLateHan|Ma Xian]] - fellow Ma-family frontier commander tied to the earlier Liang Feng response and later appointment-fit contrast.
- [[SouthernXiongnu|Southern Xiongnu]] - provides the rebellion theater in which Ma Xu's command is tested.
- [[LiangBingLateHan|Liang Bing]] - shares the early military response before Liang Bing is recalled for illness.
- [[ChenGuiDuliaoGeneralLateHan|Chen Gui]] - creates the coercive legitimacy problem that Ma Xu's later posture must repair.
- [[FortifiedAttritionPatience|Fortified attrition patience]] - names the strategic pattern Ma Xu is told to practice.
