# 《东北博弈》

《东北博弈》 is the book in [[yi-fen-laizi-bingchuang-shang-de-xiangguan-shudan-598284093]] that brings the episode's disease-history reading into Northeast Asia. The hosts describe it as covering the 1910-1911 Northeast plague, marmot hunting, railway networks, Qing weakness, and the way Japan and Russia treated public-health management as a route into regional power competition.

The book's central wiki role is [[PublicHealthGeopolitics]]. It shows that outbreak response is not only medical technique: who can inspect, quarantine, command, build legitimacy, and claim competence can become part of sovereignty and empire.
