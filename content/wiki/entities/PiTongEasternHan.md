## Overview

邳彤 / Pi Tong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-670-juejing-qiusheng-liuxiu-xianru-shengsi-da-weiji-ltz1nciofev4cqjkod-p8mmwovhe|Hanji 670]] as the Xindu-era adviser who argues that [[LiuXiu|刘秀]] should not simply retreat to Chang'an after escaping [[WangLangHebeiClaimant|王郎]]'s pursuit. The source form is 匹荣, but the role and Guangwu-era context are normalized here to Pi Tong / 邳彤.

## Current Profile

Hanji 670 places Pi Tong at the strategic hinge between survival and counterattack. Liu Xiu's group has reached Xindu after the Hutuo crossing and Nangong storm, but many people advise using local troops to escort Liu Xiu back to Chang'an. Pi Tong argues the opposite: the people still remember Han, Wang Lang's Liu Ziyu claim is false and unstable, and Xindu-side forces should be used to fight Wang Lang in Hebei.

The source treats that advice as a real turning point. Liu Xiu accepts the direction, begins gathering troops, appoints Pi Tong among the commanders, and later uses proclamations and torch displays to create a sense of overwhelming force. Pi Tong therefore functions as the adviser who turns refuge into a forward base.
