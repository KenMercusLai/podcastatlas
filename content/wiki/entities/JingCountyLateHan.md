# 泾县 / Jing County (late Han)

泾县 / Jing County (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] as the [[DanyangCommandery|丹阳郡]] county where local strongman [[ZuLangLateHan|祖郎]] attacks [[SunCe|孙策]]. The source emphasizes the surrounding mountain terrain and the strength of Danyang's local population.

Jing County's role is to localize the resistance problem. Sun Ce's early recruitment channel depends on Danyang, but Danyang is not passive territory; it includes local armed actors whose interests do not automatically align with [[YuanShu|袁术]] or the Sun family.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1028-xiaobawang-sunce-liwei-chengba-jiangdong-lgur-3fmpr55wy3aji-0rm38y7x|Hanji 1028]] adds Jing County as the center of the remaining western Danyang problem. The source says the six counties west of Jing are not yet settled, and [[TaishiCi|太史慈]] stations at Jing while drawing support from [[Shanyue|山越]]. Jing County therefore links Zu Lang's earlier attack and Taishi Ci's later resistance into one local-control branch.
