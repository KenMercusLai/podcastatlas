# Bosch / 博世

Bosch / 博世 is the manufacturing-company case in [[ep241-xiaoqi-hezuo-shi-xinyidai-de-tiefanwan-ma-lnmpsbvoqbexeardxyx88hywufig]]. [[ZhangCongzhi|张从志]] describes Bosch bringing a German-style [[DualSystemVocationalEducation|双元制职业教育]] program into China from around 2007 as the company expanded local manufacturing and needed skilled workers trained to its process standards.

The source presents Bosch as the heavy-investment version of [[SchoolEnterpriseCooperation|校企合作]]. Students split time between school and an enterprise training center, learn modules and workflows close to real factory processes, and are managed partly like quasi-employees, but the episode says they are not forced to work for Bosch after graduation.
