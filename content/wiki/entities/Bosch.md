# Bosch / 博世

## Overview
Bosch is represented in the bounded sources through two capability-transfer roles: a high-investment dual-system vocational-training operator in China and a reported engineering partner for [[AITO|问界]] vehicles planned for Europe.

## Current Profile
The vocational-education source presents Bosch as a manufacturer willing to embed training centers, modular factory processes, and quasi-employee management in a long-running [[DualSystemVocationalEducation|dual-system]] program. The newest source adds an automotive localization role: Bosch's knowledge of overseas rules, systems, and market expectations is presented as useful to AITO's planned European entry, with cooperation reportedly covering advanced driver assistance, cockpit, and chassis.

Together, the sources describe Bosch less as a component vendor in isolation than as an institution whose value can include process transfer, compliance familiarity, systems integration, and local credibility. They do not establish the commercial terms or technical boundary of the AITO partnership.

## Key Characteristics
- Manufacturing company with source-described long-term investment in vocational training.
- Uses enterprise training centers and work-process modules to connect schooling with factory practice.
- Reported partner for AITO's European driver-assistance, cockpit, and chassis development.
- Brings overseas regulatory familiarity and established market recognition in the episode's account.
- Provides capability transfer without eliminating the automaker's responsibility for final product and market execution.

## Evidence
- **Training investment:** [[ep241-xiaoqi-hezuo-shi-xinyidai-de-tiefanwan-ma-lnmpsbvoqbexeardxyx88hywufig]] describes Bosch bringing a German-style dual-system program into China from around 2007, using enterprise training centers and modular work processes.
- **Student pathway:** [[ep241-xiaoqi-hezuo-shi-xinyidai-de-tiefanwan-ma-lnmpsbvoqbexeardxyx88hywufig]] says students are managed partly like quasi-employees but are not forced to work for Bosch after graduation.
- **Vehicle localization:** [[gkwrijiorooxafjycwtsvi3u-gkwrijiorooxafjycwtsvi3u]] reports joint AITO development across advanced driver assistance, cockpit, and chassis for a planned European launch.

## Qualifications
The vocational source does not prove that Bosch's model is easily scalable across schools or industries, and it explicitly rejects treating school-enterprise cooperation as guaranteed lifelong employment. The AITO source is a short business-news account and does not define ownership of software, validation responsibility, contract economics, homologation status, or launch certainty.
