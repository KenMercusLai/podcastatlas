# 钟泰 / Zhong Tai

## Overview

钟泰 / Zhong Tai is a speaker in [[272-cong-feishu-jizuo-dao-agent-youxian-doubao-gongzuo-all-in-one-jinzhui-workbuddy-lqmfcnfkkhoxt440qy26vwpnvswp]] identified by the episode as an AI entrepreneur.

## Current Profile

His source role is to compare AI-office products from the user, founder, and ecosystem perspectives. He argues that [[Coze|扣子]] and [[Trae]] started from developer-centered audiences, that [[TencentWorkBuddy|WorkBuddy]] benefited from a lighter AI-native shape, and that teams already using [[Feishu]] may still choose [[DoubaoWork|Doubao Work]] or Trae Work because first-party context and team data matter.

He also offers the startup-competition caution. In his view, general AI-office application shells can be copied quickly as AI coding improves, so defensibility shifts toward vertical scenes, distribution, channel access, data loops, and existing ecosystems.

## Key Characteristics

- Compares WorkBuddy, Trae Work, and Doubao Work through adoption context rather than feature lists alone.
- Separates the route where enterprise IAM grows an agent from the route where an agent connects enterprise data afterward.
- Emphasizes that first-party Feishu context can outweigh a competing product's stronger standalone experience.
- Warns that generic office-agent shells face fast imitation and large-platform pressure.
