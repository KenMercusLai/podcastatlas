## Overview

窦固 / Dou Gu is a Mingdi-era frontier commander whose current profile now begins with the 57 CE [[ShaodangQiang|Shaodang Qiang]] crisis and then continues into the 73-74 CE Western Regions sequence. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-733-1-lishi-suo-yiwang-de-diwang-zhishi-mingjun-liuzhuang-lor7vhzzpwz1rv9tijksogukjqvr|Hanji 733-1]] sends him beside [[MaWuEasternHan|Ma Wu]] after the first Qiang response fails; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-741-weishenme-gudai-xiongnu-zongyao-ruqin-zhongyuan-lllpgozgi6yd5pscw8z5ofidk5rp|Hanji 741]] makes him the column commander whose route reaches Tianshan and takes [[YiwuGarrisonLateHan|Yiwu]]; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-743-chuo-ren-xinwo-de-lishi-shunjian-mingdi-liuzhuang-de-wenqing-meng-lgwtuqtug5bh90qohwiskccx77hy|Hanji 743]] makes him the senior authority who integrates Geng Bing and Liu Zhang before restoring formal Han offices in the Western Regions.

## Current Profile

Hanji 733-1 now supplies Dou Gu's earliest current layer. After Dian Wu's Shaodang Qiang forces move through Xianling and invade Longxi, Zhang Hong's first response is defeated. Mingdi then sends Dou Gu and Ma Wu with a large force. The same source gives Ma Wu the clearer later suppression credit, so Dou's role here is an escalated-command presence rather than a fully specified victory claim.

Hanji 741 then supplies Dou Gu's Western Regions campaign layer. The source says he has Hexi familiarity through the Dou family's older military and regional background, and [[EmperorMingOfHan|Mingdi]] appoints him to lead one of four anti-Northern-Xiongnu routes. Dou and Geng Zhong lead a force out from the Jiuquan side, including commandery soldiers and Lushui Qiang-Hu. This is the route that produces the clearest result: it reaches Tianshan, attacks the Northern Xiongnu Huyan king, kills more than one thousand, takes Yiwu, and stations soldiers there.

Hanji 743 then turns Dou's frontier role from raiding response into institution-building. In the 74 CE Dunhuang/Kunlun Barrier expedition against Cheshi, [[GengBingEasternHan|Geng Bing]] and Liu Zhang submit their command tallies to Dou. Dou initially wants to attack the Cheshi front king first, but Geng argues that the rear king is the strategic hinge and leads the vanguard. After the campaign succeeds, Dou petitions to restore the Western Regions Protector and Wuji Colonel structure, and Mingdi approves.

Dou's current profile is therefore command-and-restoration rather than pure tactical genius. Hanji 741 credits his route with the main tangible 73 CE result; Hanji 743 credits him with senior command integration and institutional follow-through, while reserving the key Cheshi target judgment for Geng Bing.

## Key Characteristics

- Mingdi-era frontier commander first visible here in the escalated 57 CE Shaodang Qiang response.
- Later chosen for a major 73 CE anti-Northern-Xiongnu route.
- Hexi-familiar operator whose family and regional background make him legible for Western Regions campaigning.
- Commander of the route that reaches Tianshan, defeats the Huyan king's force, takes Yiwu, and stations soldiers there.
- Senior 74 CE expedition authority who receives command tallies from Geng Bing and Liu Zhang.
- Institutional restorer who converts Cheshi success into a petition to revive Western Regions offices.
- Commander whose profile must distinguish senior authority from Geng Bing's tactical target judgment.

## Qualifications

The current page should not collapse all frontier success into Dou personally. Hanji 733-1 places him in the early Qiang response but gives Ma Wu the clearer final suppression credit. Hanji 741 gives Dou's 73 CE route the clearest concrete victory, but Hanji 743 explicitly credits Geng Bing with the key 74 CE target judgment and vanguard action. Geng Zhong, Liu Zhang, Qin Peng, route gates, and exact force counts remain source-scoped unless later evidence makes them durable.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 733-1 as the earliest current layer: Dou Gu's deployment beside Ma Wu during the first Mingdi Shaodang Qiang crisis.
- Reframed Hanji 741 and Hanji 743 as the later Western Regions campaign and office-restoration sequence.
- Clarified that Dou's Qiang-response presence, 73 CE route success, senior command, and Geng Bing's tactical judgment are separate contributions.
