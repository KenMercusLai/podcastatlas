## Overview

窦固 is documented in the wiki as the senior Han commander in the 74 CE expedition from Dunhuang against the Northern Xiongnu and Cheshi. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-743-chuo-ren-xinwo-de-lishi-shunjian-mingdi-liuzhuang-de-wenqing-meng-lgwtuqtug5bh90qohwiskccx77hy|Hanji 743]] presents him as the campaign authority to whom Geng Bing and Liu Zhang submit their command tallies, and as the official who petitions after victory to restore the Western Regions Protector and Wuji Colonel offices.

## Current Profile

Dou Gu's current profile is institutional more than tactical. The episode records his original intent to attack the Cheshi front king first, but the decisive tactical adjustment comes from [[GengBingEasternHan|Geng Bing]], who argues for striking the rear king and leads the vanguard. Dou Gu's enduring significance is that the successful expedition under his command reopens formal Han administration in the Western Regions.

## Key Characteristics

- Senior expedition commander for the 74 CE Dunhuang/Kunlun Barrier operation.
- Receives unified command symbols from Geng Bing and Liu Zhang before the campaign proceeds.
- Oversees the campaign environment in which Geng Bing's rear-king strategy defeats Cheshi resistance.
- Converts battlefield success into institutional restoration by petitioning for the Western Regions Protector and Wuji Colonel offices.

## Qualifications

This page should not overstate Dou Gu's tactical authorship. The same source credits Geng Bing with the key target judgment and vanguard action, while crediting Dou Gu with senior command and institutional follow-through.

## What Changed

- Defined his wiki role around command integration and restoration of Western Regions institutions.
