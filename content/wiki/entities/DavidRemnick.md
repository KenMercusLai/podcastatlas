# David Remnick

## Overview

David Remnick is presented as a New Yorker editor, [[MalcolmGladwell]]'s former boss, and the interviewer in a live [[RevisionistHistory]] conversation about Gladwell's development as a journalist and storyteller.

## Current Profile

Remnick structures the event as a professional self-portrait rather than a conventional book-tour interview. His questions move from early newsroom experiments and prose voice to intellectual responsibility, subject treatment, audio form, family history, identity, and sport. He is a friendly interlocutor with enough shared history to elicit candid stories, although that familiarity also limits adversarial scrutiny of several claims.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses career chronology to recover how a distinctive journalistic method developed.
- Presses Gladwell on whether playful ideas remain responsible in safety and medical contexts.
- Moves between craft, biography, ethics, family history, and cultural criticism.
- Provides a collegial live setting that encourages candor but offers limited critical distance.
