# 鲁桓公 / Duke Huan of Lu

鲁桓公 / Duke Huan of Lu enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-75-2-qiongshe-jichi-de-zushiye-guanzhong-lo8uei2u1qwj9tszftel1b4lswoa]] as the Lu ruler killed during a visit to [[QiState|齐国]]. In the episode's compressed retelling, [[QiXiangGong|齐襄公]]'s scandal with [[WenJiang|文姜]] leads to Lu Huan Gong's death and then to Qi killing the immediate perpetrator.

The page's role is to connect a personal court scandal to interstate disorder. Lu Huan Gong's death is one of the signs that the old Spring-Autumn role structure has failed badly enough that [[QiState|齐国]] must later be reorganized under [[GuanZhong|管仲]].
