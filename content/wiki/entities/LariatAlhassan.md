# Lariat Alhassan

Lariat Alhassan is the Nigerian entrepreneur whose [[LarkluxPaint]] business grounds the [[YouWinProgram|YouWin Program]] segment of [[piles-of-cash-and-a-town-of-solutions-in-kenya-nigeria-summer-school]]. In 2011, the source says she was producing, marketing, selling, and delivering paint herself from a very small operation in Abuja.

Her case illustrates [[SmallBusinessFinancingGap]] and [[MissingMiddle]]. Customers liked the paint, but the lack of a professional office or showroom made larger clients hesitate; the YouWin grant let her hire sales and marketing staff and rent a showroom, moving the business beyond a one-person credibility trap.
