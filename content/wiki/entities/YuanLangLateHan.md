## Overview

袁朗 / Yuan Lang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-798-donghan-shiqi-de-baixing-you-duo-ku-loolveumdtv4eo-omubfi5z2p2r5|Hanji 798]] as a Runan reputation figure appointed gongcao by [[WangGongTaiweiLateHan|王龚]]. The episode uses him both as a respected local official and as the clearer, more measurable contrast to [[HuangXianLateHan|黄宪]].

## Current Profile

The source says Yuan Lang does not seek novelty or eccentric distinction, yet holds strong local reputation. Wang Gong appoints him as gongcao and treats him respectfully, which helps define Wang's local talent-attraction style.

Yuan Lang also functions as a witness to Huang Xian's reputation. [[XunShuLateHan|荀淑]] tells Yuan that Runan has a Yan Hui-like figure, and Yuan immediately understands the reference as Huang Xian. Later, [[GuoTaiLateHan|郭泰]] visits Yuan but does not stay overnight, then stays several days with Huang and explains the difference through the clear-stream and deep-water comparison.

## Key Characteristics

- Runan reputation figure appointed gongcao by Wang Gong.
- Respected for steady standing rather than performative novelty.
- Local witness who recognizes Xun Shu's praise as pointing to Huang Xian.
- Contrast case whose clarity makes Huang Xian's unmeasurable depth more legible.
