# Capital Cities/ABC

Capital Cities/ABC is the media company Disney buys in 1995 in [[disney-the-renaissance-and-the-empire-1]]. The source frames the deal as a vertical-integration move after regulatory change, but its most important result is that [[TheWaltDisneyCompany]] acquires [[ESPN]].

The deal complicates Disney's identity. It gives the company network scale and the [[ESPNAffiliateFeeModel]] cash engine, but it also ties Disney to cable-bundle economics that later become fragile under cord-cutting and direct-to-consumer streaming.
