# Switzerland

## Overview
Switzerland appears in the bounded wiki as a legally and institutionally distinctive European state whose local experiments and national responsibilities span assisted death, municipal Bitcoin adoption, and the systemic-risk consequences of hosting a global bank.

## Current Profile
The sources do not support a complete national profile. They show three narrow but revealing roles: a jurisdiction where assisted suicide separates medical provision from the patient's final act; the home of [[Lugano]]'s contested [[LuganoPlanB|Plan B]] Bitcoin initiative; and the state whose 2023 rescue of [[CreditSuisse|Credit Suisse]] produced an even larger [[UBS|UBS]] and a new argument over how much parent-level capital that bank should hold. Across these domains, Swiss stability and legal credibility operate as assets, while regulation defines the terms on which institutions may use them.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides an assisted-suicide model in which the patient performs the final act and post-death official review may follow.
- Permits city-level payment experimentation without making municipal Bitcoin adoption a national monetary settlement.
- Hosts a globally important banking sector whose largest institution is unusually large relative to the domestic economy.
- Combines a valuable stability and banking reputation with home-state responsibility for crisis containment.
- Faces a distributional choice between stronger bank resilience and the costs borne by shareholders, the institution, and national competitiveness.

## Evidence
- Assisted-death law and institutions: [[119-anlesi-xianchang-zhengyin-shengming-ruci-zhengui-770771087]] presents [[LifeCircle]] and [[Dignitas]] inside a model of patient self-administration and official review.
- Municipal crypto experimentation: [[tech-20260114-0114-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260114-0114-mp-tech-pod-128]] shows [[Lugano]] accepting Bitcoin in many private and municipal settings while retaining Swiss francs and practical payment gaps.
- Banking-system responsibility: [[shangye-xiaoyang-51-shenme-ruiyin-yaoba-zongbu-banli-ruishi-gkwrijiotaj1agndggtvuvds]] connects the Credit Suisse rescue, enlarged UBS, proposed foreign-subsidiary capital coverage, and possible headquarters relocation.

## Qualifications
Each source is narrow. The assisted-death episode is a cross-national ethical account, not a complete statement of current Swiss law. The Lugano episode does not show that Switzerland as a whole uses Bitcoin or accepts its mayor's money-laundering comparison. The UBS episode relies partly on anonymous reporting and a draft policy, so it does not prove a headquarters move or final capital requirement. Legal rules, adoption counts, asset values, capital figures, and government positions remain source-scoped and time-sensitive.

## What Changed
- Added Switzerland's home-state exposure after the Credit Suisse rescue created a larger combined UBS.
- Added the tension between Swiss banking advantages and the capital cost of protecting national financial stability.
- Distinguished a possible group-headquarters move from withdrawal of Swiss banking operations.
