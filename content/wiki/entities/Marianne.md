# Marianne

## Overview

Marianne is the female personification of the French Republic and its ideals of liberty, equality, and fraternity.

## Current Profile

The episode locates her emergence around the Republic's foundation in 1792. Before “Marianne” became the settled name, a classical female Liberty appeared on the republican seal with the liberty cap, fasces, and axe. Her abstraction made her politically capacious: she was neither queen, saint, aristocrat, nor ordinary citizen, so the Republic could feminize liberty without admitting women to equal political power.

## Key Characteristics

- She personifies the Republic and republican liberty.
- Her visual language draws on classical goddess forms and civic emblems.
- Her name is linked in the episode to Guillaume Lavabre's Occitan poem *La Guérison de Marianne*.
- The name became broadly established only in the nineteenth century.
- Her abstract femininity allowed symbolic inclusion to coexist with women's civic exclusion.
