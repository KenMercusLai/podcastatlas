# Institute for Strategic Dialogue

In the episode, the institute's team found 181 websites offering tools that create nudified images or videos from real people's photos.

The source uses the Institute for Strategic Dialogue to ground [[NudifyAppEcosystem]] in evidence about traffic, ads, app stores, and payment routes. Its contribution is not a technical model explanation; it is an infrastructure map showing how [[AINonConsensualIntimateImageAbuse]] can be sustained by platform discovery, advertising, mobile distribution, and [[AbusePaymentInfrastructure]].
