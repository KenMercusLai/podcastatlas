# Future of Life Institute

## Overview
The Future of Life Institute is an existential-risk and AI-safety organization represented through lab report cards and a donor's retrospective account of its changing focus.

## Current Profile
FLI evaluates major AI labs, criticizes weakened voluntary pause commitments, favors human-controlled tool AI, and supports public accountability. A second source adds [[VitalikButerin|Vitalik Buterin]]'s donor-side qualification: he says the institute received assets when its remit covered broader existential risks, then concentrated on AI and moved from research toward legal and political action. The wiki preserves both its safety work and possible divergence from donor intent.

## Key Characteristics
- Focuses on existential risk, especially advanced AI in the current evidence.
- Uses public report cards to compare frontier-lab safety practices.
- Critiques conditional or weakened pause promises under competition.
- Favors tool AI under human control over a race to superintelligence.
- Illustrates how a grantee's cause mix and methods can change.

- **Position:** [[tech-20260720-0720-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260720-0720-mp-tech-pod-128]] attributes to [[SabinaNong]] a preference for public safety-setting and [[ToolAIHumanControl|tool AI]].

## Qualifications
The report-card account comes from an FLI representative and does not validate every grade. Buterin's view is not an audited history of programs, spending, or governance. Changed focus can reflect new risk assessment rather than mission failure, and disagreement does not establish misuse.
