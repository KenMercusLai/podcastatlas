# Future of Life Institute

The Future of Life Institute appears in [[tech-20260720-0720-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260720-0720-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the organization behind a semi-annual [[AILabSafetyReportCards|AI lab safety report card]] evaluating major frontier AI companies. The [[MarketplaceTech]] episode describes the institute as focused on reducing existential risk, including the risk that rogue superintelligent AI could threaten civilization.

Through [[SabinaNong]], the institute argues that voluntary lab safety systems are proving too flexible. The report card grades [[Anthropic]], [[OpenAI]], [[Google]], [[Meta]], and [[XAI|xAI]] and tracks model testing, whistleblower policies, current harms, military-use posture, and whether companies preserve strong [[UnilateralAIPauseCommitments|unilateral pause commitments]].
