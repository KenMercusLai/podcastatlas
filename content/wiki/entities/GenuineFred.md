# Genuine Fred

Genuine Fred is the consumer-products company mentioned in [[tech-20260413-0413-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260413-0413-mp-tech-pod-128]] through a tea infuser label saying the product was designed in Rhode Island by people. [[StephanieHughes]] uses the label as the episode's concrete example of how ordinary product language can acquire new meaning in the AI era.

The source says the company adopted the human-designed wording more than a decade earlier, before AI was a major public conversation. In the episode, that history makes the label useful precisely because it was not invented as an AI backlash slogan; it now reads as a [[HumanAuthorshipPremium]] signal in a market where consumers may suspect design, images, or copy of being AI-generated.
