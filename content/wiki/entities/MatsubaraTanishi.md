# Matsubara Tanishi

Matsubara Tanishi appears in [[kyiv-brawl-ukraines-top-general-fired-6a609aec4fac21e67f1be165]] as the comedian who has lived in [[JikobukkenIncidentProperty|jiko bukken]] for more than a decade and written memoirs about the experience. The episode uses him as the bridge between death-associated housing, entertainment, and everyday housing economics in [[Japan]].

His role is not only sensational. By repeatedly living in stigmatized homes, Matsubara helps make [[HousingStigmaDiscount]] visible as a market and cultural practice rather than a purely private fear.
