# 王楷 / Wang Kai (Late Han)

王楷 / Wang Kai (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-974-caocao-xiang-ta-tuo-qi-xian-zi-weihe-zhuanshen-ai-daozi-lhfabab7xv2vfsgnhr9ozwqhmhd|Hanji 974]] as one of the participants in the secret Yanzhou betrayal discussion. The episode names him with [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]], [[XuSiLateHan|许汜]], and [[ZhangChaoLateHan|张超]] before the group persuades [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] to invite [[LyuBu|吕布]] into [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]].

The source keeps Wang Kai source-scoped and thin. His importance is collective: he marks the rebellion as a networked local-adviser move, not merely a spontaneous break between Cao Cao and Zhang Miao.
