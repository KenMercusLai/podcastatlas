# 阏氏 / Yanzhi (Xiongnu Consort)

阏氏 / Yanzhi (Xiongnu Consort) enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-178-maodun-weihe-yao-fangguo-liubang-zhe-cai-shi-zhenxiang-1-lumszkbr-klnlodxmxdf-n8p5wym|Hanji 178]] as [[MaoDunChanyu|冒顿单于]]'s chief consort or favored woman during the [[BaidengEncirclement|白登之围]]. This page is disambiguated from [[EzhiWarringStates|阏氏 / Ezhi]], the Warring States place reached by an anti-Qin coalition.

In the episode's reconstruction, [[ChenPing|陈平]] approaches Yanzhi before trying to move Modu directly. He gives her valuables and shows her an image of a beautiful Han woman, making a possible Han submission feel like a personal threat: if Modu wins and receives Han beauties, Yanzhi may lose status inside the chanyu's household.

Yanzhi then advises Modu to accept Liu Bang's offer and open the encirclement. Her argument is not only jealousy. She also says Xiongnu and Han each have their own ruler, that Modu may not be able to govern Han territory even if he seizes it, and that the Han ruler's survival can be treated as Heaven-protected.

The source is careful about limits. Yanzhi is a pressure channel inside [[CovertPoliticalTechnology|阴谋式政治技术]], but the episode does not treat Modu as a ruler controlled by her words alone. Her intervention works because it joins Modu's wider uncertainty about missing allies, approaching Han reinforcements, and the cost of holding Liu Bang.
