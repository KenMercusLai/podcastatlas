# An Conghui / 安聪慧

An Conghui appears in [[buai-zhibo-daihuo-de-oumei-xiaofeizhe-weihe-zai-zhejia-pingtai-shang-daba-huaqian-1007104138]] as the executive succeeding [[LiShufu|李书福]] as chair of [[GeelyAutomobile|Geely Automobile]]. The source describes him as a long-running Geely manager involved in Emgrand, Lynk & Co, and [[Zeekr|极氪]] work.

The episode frames the appointment as operationally meaningful because Geely must keep integrating EV assets and meeting sales pressure while presenting a more professionalized governance structure.
