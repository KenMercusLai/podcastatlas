# 《悲伤的力量》 / Grief Works

《悲伤的力量》 / *Grief Works* is the book at the center of [[13-beishang-de-liliang-tantan-sangqin-zhitong-551507792]], a [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] episode on bereavement and grief counseling. The episode presents [[JuliaSamuel|Julia Samuel / 朱利亚·萨米尔]] as a therapist whose cases cover losing a partner, parent, child, sibling, and facing one's own death.

The book's wiki role is to make grief practical rather than abstract. It gives the hosts a way to discuss [[BereavementGriefWork]], [[ChildDeathEducation]], [[GriefCompanionSupport]], and [[TraumaNarrativeIntegration]] through specific cases: terminal cancer, sudden car death, overdose, stillbirth, drowning, childhood secrecy, anger, guilt, and rituals of farewell.

## Key Claims
- The episode treats the book as a clinical-story source, not as a quick self-help formula.
- The book's cases show that grief can include love, rage, relief, shame, envy, sexual desire, numbness, and self-blame without any one feeling cancelling the others.
- The source uses the book to argue that children can be told the truth about death and included in mourning when adults provide stable support.
- The book connects grief care to language, objects, bodies, memory, and time rather than to simple advice about "moving on."
