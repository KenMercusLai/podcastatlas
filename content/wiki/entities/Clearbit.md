# Clearbit

Clearbit appears in [[stuck-at-50k-arr-for-5-years-now-1-5m-with-ai-agents]] as the expensive reference point that helped push [[GeorgeGeorgiadis]] toward building [[Happierleads]]. George says he wanted to understand anonymous visitors to his website, found Clearbit, and judged a roughly $20,000 annual quote too expensive for his situation.

The source uses Clearbit mainly to explain product opportunity by price and access gap.
