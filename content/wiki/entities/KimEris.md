# Kim Eris

Kim Eris is [[AungSanSuuKyi]]'s son in [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]]. The episode says he has been urging foreign leaders to demand proof of life from the [[MyanmarMilitaryJunta]].

His role in the source is advocacy under uncertainty. He doubts the authenticity of a photo that the junta released and says that if his mother is under house arrest, it is not at her Yangon home. That makes him a source-linked actor inside [[ProofOfLifeDiplomacy]], not a general analyst of [[Myanmar]]'s wider war.
