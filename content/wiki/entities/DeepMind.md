# DeepMind

DeepMind is the London AI research company discussed in [[e226-liaoliao-deepmind-chuangshiren-hasabisi-yige-kexuejia-yu-shikong-de-ai-jingsai-7abda28b-99c6-4ebc-8c0d-37bcc77f6a73]]. The episode presents it as [[DemisHassabis]]' attempt to build general AI through games, neuroscience, and [[ReinforcementLearningAGIPath]], with [[ShaneLegg]] and [[MustafaSuleyman]] helping turn the research ambition into a company.

In this source, DeepMind's history runs from hard-to-explain AGI fundraising to [[Google]] acquisition, [[AlphaGo]] public proof, and [[AlphaFold]] scientific payoff. Its internal tension is that scientific ambition required outside compute and corporate resources, while those same resources pulled the lab into [[ScientificIdealVsAIArmsRace]] and later [[GoogleDeepMind]]'s Gemini-focused competition with [[OpenAI]].
