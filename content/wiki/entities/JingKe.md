# 荆轲 / Jing Ke

荆轲 / Jing Ke enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-2-yanguo-cisha-qinshihuang-weihe-hui-xuan-jingke-lsewjrmgup1yanf8ipn-i0ykqwjx]] as the person [[TianGuang|田光]] recommends to [[YanTaiziDan|燕太子丹]] for the anti-Qin assassination plan. The episode stresses that Jing Ke is not introduced as the obvious best professional swordsman. He is a [[WeyState|卫国]]-origin wandering shi, reader, and侠士 whose mixed social identity makes him unusual and less predictable.

The source spends time on apparent contradictions in his image. Jing Ke seeks visibility around sword and chess disputes but withdraws when challenged by Ge Nie and Lu Goujian; later, in [[YanState|燕国]], he drinks in the market with [[GaoJianli|高渐离]] and a dog butcher, laughing, crying, singing, and acting "as if nobody else were there." Tian Guang nevertheless reads him as deep, bookish, and connected to worthy elders rather than as a mere market eccentric.

Jing Ke's selection therefore sits at the intersection of [[MoralizedShiIdentity|moralized shi identity]], [[PoliticalAssassinationEthics|political assassination ethics]], and [[HonorBindingBySelfSacrifice|honor binding by self-sacrifice]]. He accepts Tian Guang's referral without bargaining in the episode's account, but that acceptance comes after a friend has recommended him into a death mission and then killed himself to prove secrecy and moral seriousness.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-3-jingke-ciqin-weihe-jiujiu-bu-xingdong-zai-deng-shui-liihe-kgwigu3bggxmyofgvotged]] turns Jing Ke from selected assassin into mission planner. He tells [[YanTaiziDan|燕太子丹]] that he cannot approach [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] without [[FanWuji|樊无忌]]'s head and the [[DuKangMap|督亢地图]], then privately secures Fan Wuji's suicide. The episode also makes his delay operational rather than simple hesitation: even after [[XuFuren|徐夫人]]'s dagger and [[QinWuyang|秦武阳]] are ready, Jing Ke waits for a distant friend he appears to trust more.

The same episode gives Jing Ke's departure a failure signal. Taizi Dan's suspicion angers him into leaving before that friend arrives, so the mission begins through [[MistrustTriggeredMissionLaunch|mistrust-triggered mission launch]] rather than fully trusted readiness. The [[YiShuiFarewell|易水送别]] then fixes him in memory as a figure of tragic resolve: he leaves in mourning colors and song, without turning back.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-115-1-jingke-ciqin-sihu-you-dianer-caoshuai-le-lgz1adsn1t8jjiaobmtfedpr-ixn]] follows Jing Ke into [[Xianyang|咸阳]]. He and [[QinWuyang|秦武阳]] bribe [[MengJia|蒙嘉]], package [[YanState|燕国]]'s surrender script around [[FanWuji|樊无忌]]'s head and the [[DuKangMap|督亢地图]], and win a maximum-ceremony audience before [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]. The episode also puts Jing Ke under a structural limit: even if he had killed Ying Zheng, Qin's already-built state capacity might still have carried unification forward.

The same source makes Jing Ke's solo burden concrete. When Qin Wuyang panics at the palace steps, Jing Ke explains the failure as rustic overawe, takes the map himself, and proceeds alone. The earlier doubts about assistant fit therefore become an immediate execution problem before the actual attack begins.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-115-2-qinwang-danan-busi-zhihou-ganle-jian-ni-buzhidao-de-shi-lidfjb6-nggubw8vpz5fvnfacrqu]] completes the attempt. As the [[DuKangMap|督亢地图]] opens into [[TuqiongBijian|图穷匕见]], Jing Ke grabs Ying Zheng's sleeve and strikes with the dagger, but the first attack misses and the sleeve tears. Ying Zheng cannot immediately draw his long sword, runs around a pillar, then draws after the court reminder "王负剑" and wounds Jing Ke; Jing Ke's thrown dagger also misses.

The same source gives Jing Ke's death and afterlife. Badly wounded and disarmed, he sits in a "箕踞" posture, curses Ying Zheng, and says the mission failed because he wanted to take the Qin king alive and force a pact for [[YanTaiziDan|燕太子丹]]. The episode records the [[Shiji|《史记》]] / [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] punitive wording that he was dismembered, then turns to [[TaoYuanming|陶渊明]] and [[GongZizhen|龚自珍]] as later readers who preserve him as a tragic youxia figure under [[YouxiaSuppressionUnderUnification|大一统下的游侠压制]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-1-chaijie-jingke-ciqin-bi-bai-de-mimi-lnonhw9tlsu5dudu8750zvgeaz1p]] adds the harsh evaluation after Yan's final destruction. [[SimaGuang|司马光]] and [[YangXiong|扬雄]] deny that Jing Ke's death should be called义: the episode says private repayment to Taizi Dan and courage with a dagger do not answer the political problem of saving [[YanState|燕国]]. This creates a reception tension with the later poetic memory, because Jing Ke can remain a tragic侠士 in literature while still failing the source's [[RenyiGroundedVirtue|renyi-grounded virtue]] test.
