# 赵范（东汉末） / Zhao Fan (Late Han)

## Overview
赵范 / Zhao Fan is the late-Han Guiyang administrator whose surrender and proposed marriage alliance with [[ZhaoYun|赵云]] appear in Hanji 1147. He is distinct from the Warring States prince [[ZhaoFan|赵范]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Zhao Fan's submission as coerced and therefore politically uncertain. After Zhao Yun takes responsibility for Guiyang, Zhao Fan reportedly proposes that his widowed sister-in-law marry Zhao Yun. Zhao Yun refuses because the shared surname does not create trust and the offer could bind him to an actor whose allegiance remains unclear. Zhao Fan's later flight is treated by the host as confirmation of that risk judgment.

## Key Characteristics
- Enters the current record as a surrendered local administrator rather than a secure coalition partner.
- Attempts to convert political proximity into kinship through a marriage proposal.
- Remains sufficiently mistrusted that Zhao Yun refuses the proposed alliance.
- Later flight supports a cautious reading of his submission without proving every inferred motive.

## Qualifications

The woman's identity and wishes, Zhao Fan's precise office, the proposal's wording, Zhao Yun's reasoning, the chronology and cause of Zhao Fan's flight, and the host's inference that the flight confirms prior disloyalty all require broader textual comparison.
