# Ben Goodwin

## Overview
Ben Goodwin is the co-founder of [[Olipop]] and the guest advisor in [[advice-line-with-ben-goodwin-of-olipop-f9d3fe17-eb2c-440d-b223-990fed970727]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Goodwin as a mission-driven CPG founder whose advice is unusually close to shelf behavior, product standards, and founder psychology. He treats taste and value as the first buying triggers, while preserving deeper ingredient, health, and mission narratives for customers who want to engage further.

## Key Characteristics
- Co-founder of [[Olipop]], a prebiotic soda brand used as the mature-company reference point in the episode.
- Advises food and retail founders to start from real customer behavior rather than from abstract education campaigns.
- Rejects market pressure when it means compromising standards, integrity, quality, or mission.
- Connects CPG growth to taste, comparative value, packaging clarity, and repeat purchase.
- Frames founder judgment as calibrated instinct supported by a craftsman mentality.

## Evidence
- Olipop role - [[advice-line-with-ben-goodwin-of-olipop-f9d3fe17-eb2c-440d-b223-990fed970727]] introduces Goodwin as Olipop's co-founder and uses Olipop's brand refresh, growth, research claims, remote team, and mission to set context.
- Customer-behavior advice - [[advice-line-with-ben-goodwin-of-olipop-f9d3fe17-eb2c-440d-b223-990fed970727]] records his guidance to Gheelish that taste, price-to-value, fewer calories, less fat, and real ingredients should precede deeper ghee education.
- Standards and mission - [[advice-line-with-ben-goodwin-of-olipop-f9d3fe17-eb2c-440d-b223-990fed970727]] has Goodwin reject "market" as a reason to weaken standards and describe Olipop's mission impact as the company's north star.
- Founder judgment - [[advice-line-with-ben-goodwin-of-olipop-f9d3fe17-eb2c-440d-b223-990fed970727]] closes with Goodwin's advice that founders should learn to listen to, calibrate, and trust their instincts while staying grounded in craft.
