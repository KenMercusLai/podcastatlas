# John Hume

John Hume appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] as part of the younger Catholic civil-rights generation in [[NorthernIreland]]. The episode places him alongside [[AustinCurrie]] and [[GerryFitt]] in demands for fair housing, fair electoral boundaries, an end to the [[BSpecials|B Specials]], and local electoral reform.

During the August 1969 crisis, Hume is one of the civil-rights figures who urged Westminster to send troops. In the source, that demand reflects a moment when many Catholics viewed the [[BritishArmy]] as a possible protector from loyalist violence and the [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]], before the army's role later changed.
