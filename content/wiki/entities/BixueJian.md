## Overview

《碧血剑》 / Sword Stained with Royal Blood is a [[JinYong|金庸]] wuxia novel represented here through a [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] reading of love, jealousy, responsibility, revenge, and revision.

## Current Profile

The bounded episode treats the novel's relationships as a system of mismatched feeling and constrained action. [[YuanChengzhi|袁承志]] protects more readily than he declares; [[WenQingqing|温青青]] acts more readily than she trusts; [[AjiuBixueJian|阿九]] and Yuan share feeling without converting it into a future; and [[HeHongyao|何红药]] turns total sacrifice into destructive possession.

The previous generation makes romance inseparable from revenge. [[XiaXueyi|夏雪宜]] and [[WenYiBixueJian|温仪]] nearly interrupt a family vendetta, but greed and suspicion destroy the exit. Because Xia dies before the main action yet continues to shape treasure, martial inheritance, love, and retaliation, the novel also supplies a strong case of [[AbsentPresenceCharacterDesign]].

## Key Characteristics

- Romantic choice is divided among passion, responsibility, gratitude, promise, security, and viable action.
- Family status and violent institutions shape attachment without eliminating individual responsibility.
- Love may interrupt revenge temporarily but cannot by itself overcome organized greed and coercion.
- A dead predecessor continues to govern the living plot through memory, objects, skills, and unfinished relationships.
- Edition changes alter emotional explicitness and the credibility of character motivation.

- [[liiidmjxhrngcffnld51y-l7_oan]] contrasts Yuan Chengzhi's responsibility toward Wen Qingqing with his more intense but less actionable feeling for A Jiu.

### Romance inside revenge

- [[liiidmjxhrngcffnld51y-l7_oan]] presents Xia Xueyi and Wen Yi's relationship as a possible end to revenge that the Wen elders destroy for treasure and control.

### Posthumous plot control and revision

- [[liiidmjxhrngcffnld51y-l7_oan]] shows Xia Xueyi governing later action after death and compares editions that make some emotional lines clearer while thinning others.

## Qualifications

The current profile derives from one podcast's plot summary and literary interpretation rather than a complete edition-by-edition textual study. Claims about psychology, authorial intention, and which relationship constitutes the deepest love remain interpretive and disputed within the episode.
