# 清华简《系年》 / Qinghua Bamboo Slips Xinian

清华简《系年》 / Qinghua Bamboo Slips Xinian appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-1-lvkp-ju7pf5z5cz3thojv3s2fkyx]] as supplementary evidence for events that [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] leaves terse. The episode says [[SimaGuang|司马光]] does not explain why [[ChuState|楚国]] besieged [[ZhengState|郑国]] or why Zheng killed [[ZiyangZheng|子阳]], then turns to Xinian to reconstruct a plausible background.

The source uses Xinian as an evidence-bound supplement rather than a free license to invent missing motives. Its wiki role is to connect excavated texts with [[HistoricalDetectiveReasoning]] and [[ClassicReadingComplexity]].
