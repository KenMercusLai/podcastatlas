## Overview

仪琳 is a Hengshan nun in [[XiaoAoJiangHu|《笑傲江湖》]] whose unrequited love for [[LinghuChongJinYong|令狐冲]] is represented through care, inhibited expression, and severe moral self-scrutiny.

## Current Profile

The episode reads Yilin's love through actions that do not demand reciprocation: staying beside an injured Linghu Chong, praying while institutional conflict unfolds, and withdrawing when she recognizes his attachment elsewhere. Even jealousy becomes an occasion for immediate self-correction rather than entitlement.

Her restraint has a tragic edge. Religious rules, concern for others' happiness, and an unusually demanding conscience prevent open pursuit or even ordinary emotional expression. The melon episode is treated as the condensed case: she weighs prohibition carefully and is prepared to bear spiritual consequences herself in order to help him. Whether this life is more or less fortunate than Yue Lingshan's remains unresolved because romantic fulfillment and enduring family care point in different directions.

## Key Characteristics

- Expresses love through care and presence rather than possession.
- Treats another person's existing attachment as a boundary.
- Subjects jealousy and desire to unusually strict moral review.
- Accepts personal spiritual cost when concrete care requires action.
- Retains family and community belonging despite romantic loss.

## Qualifications

The claim that Yilin was prepared to leave Hengshan and the reconstruction of her inner motives are episode interpretations rather than verified textual statements. The comparison with Yue Lingshan uses incompatible goods and should not be resolved as a simple happiness ranking.

## What Changed

- Created a profile centered on non-entitled care and the personal cost of moral self-restraint.
- Preserved disagreement over whether family belonging compensates for unrequited love.
