# dotMeow Foundation

## Overview
dotMeow Foundation is the Belgian nonprofit presented in the episode as applying to operate `.meow` for the queer community.

## Current Profile
The foundation is a community-oriented alternative to corporate and speculative registry applicants. Its proposed model joins a recognizable online namespace with revenue recycling: sell `.meow` registrations and direct proceeds toward queer projects and businesses. The application remains prospective, so the profile describes an intended governance and funding model rather than an operating registry.

## Key Characteristics
- Belgian nonprofit applying for the `.meow` generic top-level domain.
- Frames a domain registry as community identity infrastructure.
- Proposes using registration proceeds to support queer projects and businesses.
- Depends on ICANN approval, delegation, technical operation, registrar distribution, and sustained demand.

## Qualifications
The episode does not establish that `.meow` will be approved, delegated, widely distributed, profitable, or able to fund projects at meaningful scale. Community designation can improve priority without removing technical, financial, governance, abuse-prevention, and demand risks.

- Added registry revenue recycling as a possible community-funding model.
