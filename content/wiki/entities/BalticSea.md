# Baltic Sea

The Baltic Sea appears in [[tech-20260820-tech-pod-128-tech-20260820-tech-pod-128]] as an example region where subsea cable incidents have raised questions about whether damage is accidental, natural, or deliberate. The episode uses the Baltic example to show why [[UnderseaDataCables]] can sit inside [[DigitalInfrastructureWarRisk]] even when attribution remains uncertain.

For the wiki, the Baltic Sea is a geography of ambiguity rather than a settled sabotage claim in this source. Its role is to connect physical cable damage, maritime chokepoints, and gray-zone risk to [[AsymmetricInfrastructureAttack]] and [[CableNetworkResilience]].
