# Termite (Baltimore outreach worker)

## Overview

Termite is the name used for a Baltimore outreach worker in [[wolfgang-s-law-part-one|Wolfgang's Law - Part One]], where her mediation and long-term material care illustrate [[TrustedCommunityViolenceInterruption]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Termite as a locally known mediator who connects older and younger residents and who, with her husband, routinely paid for food and clothing for children. When an armed young man sought revenge after his aunt was injured, Termite verified the aunt's condition and asked him not to retaliate. He accepted her appeal because he remembered care she had provided years earlier, even though she no longer remembered the particular assistance.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses familiarity and local standing rather than formal coercive authority.
- Builds trust through repeated practical care before a crisis occurs.
- Verifies the immediate situation before intervening in a retaliation pathway.
- Converts accumulated moral credibility into a direct request for restraint.

## Evidence

### Long-term care and standing

- [[wolfgang-s-law-part-one]] describes food, clothing, and community support that made Termite a trusted figure across generations.

### Crisis interruption

- [[wolfgang-s-law-part-one]] recounts her hospital verification and successful appeal to a young man who was reportedly armed and seeking retaliation.

## Qualifications

The source supplies a single success story, not a representative sample or independent evaluation. It does not establish how often outreach fails, what risks workers bear, whether participants consent to information sharing, or which institutional supports make relationship-based intervention durable.
