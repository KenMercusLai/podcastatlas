# Long Day's Journey into Night / 《地球最后的夜晚》

## Overview
《地球最后的夜晚》 / Long Day's Journey into Night is represented as [[BiGan|毕赣]]'s transition from the small-team conditions of [[KailiBluesFilm|《路边野餐》]] into a formal film-industrial workflow.

## Current Profile
Bi describes the film less through a complete textual reading than as a difficult learning stage. The production system could save and coordinate time, but its default tendency was to convert saved time into more shooting, whereas he wanted protected time for reflection. His self-rating places the film below his first feature while still treating it as necessary training for becoming an art-film director able to work at larger scale.

## Key Characteristics
- Rapidly initiated successor to [[KailiBluesFilm|《路边野餐》]].
- Entry point into more formal scheduling, coordination, and industrial production.
- Learning project exposing tension between production throughput and directorial reflection.
- Source-scoped case in which the director values training and process despite judging the result less favorably than his first feature.
