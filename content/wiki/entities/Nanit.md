# Nanit

Nanit appears in [[tech-20260811-0811-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260811-0811-mp-tech-pod-128]] as a smart baby-monitor system used by [[SapnaMaheshwari]] with both of her children. In the source, Nanit is not only a camera or sensor product: it suggests sleep timing and put-down routines, offers a baby sleep score, and is described as part of a broader move toward [[AIBabyMonitors]] and subscription-based [[AIParentingAdvice]].

The episode also makes Nanit central to [[ChildBedroomDataPrivacy]]. The company is described as saying it does not sell or market against the data and instead earns through hardware, app, and subscription fees, but the source still raises the privacy stakes because Nanit wants to extend tracking and health interpretation beyond infancy and into childhood.

## Key Claims
- Nanit is used in the source as the clearest example of baby monitoring moving from observation to analysis and advice.
- Its sleep-score feature links the baby-tech market to [[QuantifiedParenting]] and adult wearable language.
- The source says Nanit has raised money and plans to expand tracking and health interpretation for children.
- Nanit's stated business model reduces one privacy concern but does not eliminate questions about retention, interpretation, and child consent as children get older.
