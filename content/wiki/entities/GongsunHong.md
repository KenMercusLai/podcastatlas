## Overview

公孙弘 / Gongsun Hong is a Western Han official whose current profile combines tactful policy influence with political rivalry. He can preserve room to advise [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] by narrowing rather than directly rejecting imperial priorities, but the Jiaoxi appointment episode also presents him as willing to route a scholarly rival into a dangerous office.

## Current Profile

Hanji 925 makes Gongsun Hong a positive case for [[StrategicCompromiseAsAgency|策略性妥协保全机会]]. When Han Wudi wants both pressure against the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] and southwest development, Gongsun Hong avoids flat contradiction and instead narrows the priority problem. His compromise preserves access and future influence under a ruler with strong preferences.

Hanji 367-1 adds a darker use of institutional positioning. The episode says Gongsun Hong resents [[DongZhongshu|董仲舒]] and recommends him as chancellor to [[LiuDuanWesternHan|胶西王刘端]], whose violence toward senior officials makes the post dangerous. The appointment does not kill Dong because Liu Duan respects his scholarship, but Dong still withdraws through illness rather than trust that protection.

Together, the sources make Gongsun Hong politically adaptive but morally ambivalent. The same skill in reading incentives that protects his own agency can also be used to expose a rival through formally legitimate appointment.

## Key Characteristics

- Tactful adviser who reframes conflicts instead of opposing imperial desire head-on.
- Resource-priority thinker who argues against dividing attention across simultaneous strategic fronts.
- Survival-aware court actor who protects access and influence through calibrated speech.
- Politically ambivalent rival who can use appointment as an indirect exposure mechanism.

## Qualifications

Both sources are popular-history interpretations. Hanji 925 does not reconstruct the full policy debate over Xiongnu and southwest development, while Hanji 367-1 does not independently establish every motive behind Gongsun Hong's recommendation. The rivalry and attempted-removal reading is strongly tied to the episode and transmitted biographical tradition, but the page does not infer a failed murder plan from the appointment alone.

## What Changed

- Added the Jiaoxi appointment as a darker counterpart to Gongsun Hong's tactful compromise.
- Reframed his political skill as morally ambivalent: it can preserve advice or expose a rival.
- Added Dong Zhongshu and Liu Duan to his active court relationship field.
