# 公孙弘 / Gongsun Hong

公孙弘 / Gongsun Hong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-925-tuoxie-bushi-rensong-xuehui-tuoxie-lu-geng-changliang-limafqskgblrwetestyh39w-hzm4|Hanji 925]] as the Western Han official used to define compromise as strategy rather than surrender. When [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] wants both pressure against the [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] and southwest development, Gongsun Hong does not confront the emperor as simply wrong; he narrows the priority problem and advises that the court should not divide attention across both tasks at once.

The episode's lesson is about political survivability. Gongsun Hong's position depends on reading imperial desire, resource limits, and his own room for speech; his compromise therefore belongs to [[StrategicCompromiseAsAgency|策略性妥协保全机会]] rather than to passive compliance.
