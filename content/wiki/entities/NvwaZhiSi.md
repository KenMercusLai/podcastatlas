# 《女娲之死》

## Overview
《女娲之死》 is the AI short film discussed by [[JiaoshouYiXiaoxing|叫兽易小星]] and [[LiChenFenmo|李晨]] in the 三五环 episode on AI filmmaking.

## Current Profile
The source describes 《女娲之死》 as a mythic AI short asking how and why Nüwa dies. Its claymation-like stop-motion style was chosen because clay fits the Nüwa-creates-humans motif and because the rough, handmade texture helped the piece avoid looking like a generic AI video.

The film is useful to the wiki as a concrete production case. 易小星 supplied the overall idea and script, while 李晨 and the [[FenmoTeam|粉墨]] collaborators translated text into visual plans and generated shots. The project won third prize in the [[BilibiliAICreationContest|B站 AI 创作大赛]] according to the source, and its production timeline illustrates a core [[AIVideoProductionWorkflow]] boundary: AI can compress a ten-minute stop-motion-like work dramatically, but final quality still requires script work, repeated generation, shot deletion, editing, and finishing.

## Key Characteristics
- AI short film centered on a Nüwa myth premise about creation, death, and human forgetfulness.
- Uses a claymation-like stop-motion visual style to connect form with theme.
- Built through collaboration between 易小星's idea/script role and 粉墨's AI-video execution workflow.
- Serves as a practical case where AI lowers production time compared with traditional stop-motion animation.
- Shows that high-quality AI film still requires polishing beyond first-generation output.
