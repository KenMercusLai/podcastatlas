# 青州兵 / Qingzhou Soldiers

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-964-bie-jiaoao-bie-jiaoao-bie-jiaoao-lo2-edzffu0v9ixoabc06rx-oqoq|Hanji 964]] backfills the hostile stage before the Qingzhou Soldiers exist as a Cao-side corps. The [[QingzhouYellowTurbans|青州黄巾军]] first appear as a hungry armed force attacking [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]], killing [[LiuDaiLateHan|刘岱]], and forcing [[CaoCao|曹操]] into costly fighting in which [[BaoXin|鲍信]] dies.

青州兵 / Qingzhou Soldiers enter the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-968-jiemi-sanguo-zui-lihai-de-yangmou-lg4qfkdgw-stixrebhjdoshil6yj|Hanji 968]] as the elite military layer [[CaoCao|曹操]] forms after a large Yellow Turban surrender associated with [[QingzhouLateHan|青州]]. The episode says Cao Cao does not only defeat or disperse the group; he selects the strongest fighters into a named corps while placing the wider dependent population into farming and supply work.

The source treats the Qingzhou Soldiers as a foundation of Cao Cao's early military capacity. Their significance is inseparable from logistics: the same surrender produces fighting men, families, labor, and an agricultural base, connecting the soldiers to [[TuntianSystem|屯田制]] and [[DefeatedPopulationConversion|降众资源化]] rather than to battlefield manpower alone.

This page is source-scoped. It records how Hanji 968 explains Cao Cao's early rise; it does not yet narrate the Qingzhou Soldiers' later campaign history.
