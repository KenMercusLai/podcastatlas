# Hugh Gravitt

## Overview

Hugh Gravitt was the off-duty Atlanta taxi driver who struck [[MargaretMitchell]] in August 1949; she died five days later. [[star-struck]] uses his conduct and sympathetic public reception to examine how intoxicated driving was treated as unintended tragedy rather than foreseeable wrongdoing.

## Current Profile

The episode says Gravitt had been drinking, approached at excessive speed, had more than twenty prior traffic citations, and was not given a breath test despite alcohol being detected on his breath. He served two years for manslaughter and later expressed a wish that he had died instead, but did not treat alcohol as part of the collision decades afterward.

Contemporary commentary often represented him as another victim because he had not intended to kill. That distinction between intent and responsibility is the profile's durable significance; the page does not independently adjudicate his impairment, precise speed, criminal case, or later memory.
