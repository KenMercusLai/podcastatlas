# 巨鹿 / Julu

巨鹿 / Julu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-132-5wan-wanbao-40wan-xiangyu-cizhan-fengshen-lk87nb3fskg5zh6fcgptypixhzuw|Qinji 132]] as the besieged [[ZhaoState|赵国]] refuge after [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] breaks [[Handan|邯郸]]. [[ZhaoXie|赵歇]] and [[ZhangEr|张耳]] flee there, while [[WangLiQin|王离]] surrounds the city and [[ChenYu|陈馀]] camps to the north with several tens of thousands of men.

The source makes Julu the crisis that forces [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]] to choose between old Warring States non-rescue failure and active coalition duty. Chu therefore sends [[SongYi|宋义]] as chief general, [[XiangYu|项羽]] as second general, and [[FanZeng|范增]] as last general to rescue Zhao.

In this episode, Julu is mostly the destination rather than the completed battlefield. The title points toward Xiang Yu's later fame, but the transcript itself ends with the command setup and Liu Bang's separate westward movement under [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-133-songyi-zhan-xiangyu-weihe-bei-fansha-lms7noyrm5omedv77gqfhjeu53zx|Qinji 133]] keeps Julu offstage but makes it the pressure point behind the Chu command crisis. Song Yi's halt at [[AnyangQinRebellion|安阳]] depends on the idea that Qin and Zhao should first exhaust each other; Xiang Yu counters that if Qin destroys Zhao and absorbs its troops and supplies, Julu's fall could strengthen Qin instead of weakening it.

Julu therefore becomes more than a besieged city. It is the test of whether the anti-Qin coalition can rescue a restored state before Qin defeats it separately, and it is the strategic emergency Xiang Yu uses to justify killing Song Yi.
