# 《鬼吹灯》 / Ghost Blows Out the Light

## Overview

《鬼吹灯》 is an adventure-fiction series by 天下霸唱 centered on 胡八一, 胖子, Shirley 杨, tomb exploration, folklore, survival, and the identity of the 摸金校尉. The current page is bounded to two [[ZhiZuiJinMiFM|纸醉金迷FM]] episodes approaching and concluding the first four books' final ritual.

## Current Profile

Across the supplied discussions, the first four books form a phase in which the protagonists' search for wealth becomes a test of loyalty, practical interpretation, sacrifice, and life direction. The approach to [[KunlunShrineGhostBlowsOutTheLight|《昆仑神宫》]]'s ritual chamber turns sensory puzzles into moral pressure: uncertain rules, a timer, and apparent human sacrifice expose differences among 胡八一、胖子、Shirley 杨、明叔、阿香 over autonomy, kinship, and whether collective survival can justify selecting a victim.

The group eventually breaks the curse, survives the retreat, and returns to Beijing; 胡八一 and 胖子 then decide that the danger and moral cost of taking objects from graves outweigh the fantasy of enrichment.

The phase closes symbolically when they remove their 摸金符 and prepare to join Shirley 杨 in the United States. This is both an ending and a reversible franchise device: the characters renounce a profession without making their acquired competence, relationships, or unfinished obligations disappear, while prequels and later emergencies can reopen the world.

## Key Characteristics

- Joins tomb-adventure plotting to Chinese folklore, geomancy, cosmology, and invented ritual systems.
- Treats survival puzzles as tests of loyalty, interpretation, practical skill, and the distinction between bearing a cost oneself and imposing it on someone else.
- Gives 胡八一 and 胖子 a field in which disrupted education, military experience, and specialist knowledge become useful.
- Converts the pursuit of treasure into an eventual critique of private extraction and the desire surrounding valuable objects.
- Uses removal of the 摸金符 as a visible transition from occupational identity toward another possible life.
