# 杜袭 / Du Xi (late Han)

## Overview
杜袭 / Du Xi is a late-Han Yingchuan figure whose bounded profile moves from advising concealed capacity during refugee uncertainty to restoring command after defeat and redirecting a ruler's anger before it becomes a low-value campaign.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1022 has Du Xi flee to [[Jingzhou|荆州]] with [[ZhaoYanLateHan|赵俨]] and [[FanQinLateHan|繁钦]], receive [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s hospitality, but keep the group's original purpose clear: survival first, then action when the right ruler and timing become visible. His warning to Fan Qin separates useful talent from premature display and turns the episode's “藏拙” theme into a concrete political choice.

Hanji 1163 adds a much later military-administrative role. After [[XiahouYuan|夏侯渊]] is killed at Dingjun Mountain, Du Xi and [[GuoHuaiLateHan|郭淮]] gather the soldiers and support [[ZhangHeLateHan|张郃]] as commander. Zhang He's inspection restores morale, making Du Xi part of a command-continuity response rather than only a voice for personal concealment.

Hanji 1167 adds a second kind of command repair. When [[CaoCao|曹操]] wants to attack the insubordinate Guanzhong commander [[XuYouGuanzhongCommander|许攸]], Du Xi first asks whether an erroneous decision remains correctable. He then works through Cao Cao's self-conception, arguing that a minor subordinate's insult is too small to justify the ruler's military and emotional attention. Gifts and rank replace the proposed campaign, extending Du Xi's profile from preserving latent capacity to preserving a leader's strategic judgment.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats refugee status as temporary self-preservation rather than as proof of allegiance to the first generous host.
- Judges Liu Biao's hospitality as insufficient evidence that Liu can become a restoration ruler.
- Uses concealment as strategic timing, not as denial of talent or permanent withdrawal.
- Functions as a corrective peer whose advice keeps Fan Qin and Zhao Yan aligned with their original survival goal.
- Helps turn a respected field commander into an emergency authority after battlefield decapitation.
- Sequences remonstrance by securing openness to correction before challenging an anger-driven military response.

- Host assessment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1022-luanshi-sanguo-sanwei-mingshi-de-shengcun-jueze-lgmbekomxwiu7hjnpxkxggyhvwaf|Hanji 1022]] has Du Xi warn that Liu Biao should not be treated as the decisive restoration patron.

## Qualifications
Hanji 1022 renders the name as “杜席”; the wiki normalizes to 杜袭 / Du Xi because the surrounding sequence matches the known late-Han story with Zhao Yan and Fan Qin. Hanji 1163's appointment process, Du Xi's authority, and the speed and cause of morale recovery remain source-scoped. Hanji 1167's dialogue, sword-stage detail, metaphors, Cao Cao's internal shift, and Xu You's emotional response are reconstructed by a structured summary. The large chronological gaps prevent the three episodes from serving as a full biography.

## What Changed
- Added Du Xi's intervention against Cao Cao's proposed anger-driven campaign.
- Extended his corrective role from peer advice and emergency succession into ruler-facing persuasion.
- Preserved the identity normalization and large gaps among the three evidence points.
