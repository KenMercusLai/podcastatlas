# 杜袭 / Du Xi (late Han)

## Overview
杜袭 / Du Xi is a late-Han Yingchuan figure whose bounded profile moves from advising concealed capacity during refugee uncertainty to helping restore battlefield command after Xiahou Yuan's death.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1022 has Du Xi flee to [[Jingzhou|荆州]] with [[ZhaoYanLateHan|赵俨]] and [[FanQinLateHan|繁钦]], receive [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s hospitality, but keep the group's original purpose clear: survival first, then action when the right ruler and timing become visible. His warning to Fan Qin separates useful talent from premature display and turns the episode's “藏拙” theme into a concrete political choice.

Hanji 1163 adds a much later military-administrative role. After [[XiahouYuan|夏侯渊]] is killed at Dingjun Mountain, Du Xi and [[GuoHuaiLateHan|郭淮]] gather the soldiers and support [[ZhangHeLateHan|张郃]] as commander. Zhang He's inspection restores morale, making Du Xi part of a command-continuity response rather than only a voice for personal concealment.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats refugee status as temporary self-preservation rather than as proof of allegiance to the first generous host.
- Judges Liu Biao's hospitality as insufficient evidence that Liu can become a restoration ruler.
- Uses concealment as strategic timing, not as denial of talent or permanent withdrawal.
- Functions as a corrective peer whose advice keeps Fan Qin and Zhao Yan aligned with their original survival goal.
- Helps turn a respected field commander into an emergency authority after battlefield decapitation.

## Evidence
- Refuge rather than final service: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1022-luanshi-sanguo-sanwei-mingshi-de-shengcun-jueze-lgmbekomxwiu7hjnpxkxggyhvwaf|Hanji 1022]] presents Du Xi, Zhao Yan, and Fan Qin as Yingchuan refugees in Jingzhou who are preserving themselves during war.
- Host assessment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1022-luanshi-sanguo-sanwei-mingshi-de-shengcun-jueze-lgmbekomxwiu7hjnpxkxggyhvwaf|Hanji 1022]] has Du Xi warn that Liu Biao should not be treated as the decisive restoration patron.
- Concealed capability: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1022-luanshi-sanguo-sanwei-mingshi-de-shengcun-jueze-lgmbekomxwiu7hjnpxkxggyhvwaf|Hanji 1022]] uses the submerged-dragon image to show capacity waiting for timing rather than advertising itself.
- Command recovery: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1163-gaogu-ziji-shibushi-zui-da-de-yuchun-lokzo7kcz69kf_lhgskx8kie-9_c|Hanji 1163]] says Du Xi and Guo Huai rally the army and support Zhang He after Xiahou Yuan's death.

## Qualifications
Hanji 1022 renders the name as “杜席”; the wiki normalizes to 杜袭 / Du Xi because the surrounding sequence matches the known late-Han story with Zhao Yan and Fan Qin. Hanji 1163's appointment process, Du Xi's authority, and the speed and cause of morale recovery remain source-scoped. The large chronological gap prevents these two episodes from serving as a full biography.

## What Changed
- Extended Du Xi from refugee survival counsel into emergency command continuity.
- Preserved the earlier identity normalization and the gap between the two evidence points.
