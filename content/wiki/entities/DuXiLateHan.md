# 杜袭 / Du Xi (late Han)

## Overview
杜袭 / Du Xi is a late-Han Yingchuan figure who appears in the wiki as the clearest voice for hiding capacity while disorder is unresolved.

## Current Profile
The current profile is source-scoped to Hanji 1022. Du Xi flees to [[Jingzhou|荆州]] with [[ZhaoYanLateHan|赵俨]] and [[FanQinLateHan|繁钦]], receives [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s hospitality, but keeps the group's original purpose clear: survival first, then action when the right ruler and timing become visible. His warning to Fan Qin separates useful talent from premature display and turns the episode's "藏拙" theme into a concrete political choice.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats refugee status as temporary self-preservation rather than as proof of allegiance to the first generous host.
- Judges Liu Biao's hospitality as insufficient evidence that Liu can become a restoration ruler.
- Uses concealment as strategic timing, not as denial of talent or permanent withdrawal.
- Functions as a corrective peer whose advice keeps Fan Qin and Zhao Yan aligned with their original survival goal.

- Refuge rather than final service: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1022-luanshi-sanguo-sanwei-mingshi-de-shengcun-jueze-lgmbekomxwiu7hjnpxkxggyhvwaf|Hanji 1022]] presents Du Xi, Zhao Yan, and Fan Qin as Yingchuan refugees in Jingzhou who are preserving themselves during war.
- Host assessment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1022-luanshi-sanguo-sanwei-mingshi-de-shengcun-jueze-lgmbekomxwiu7hjnpxkxggyhvwaf|Hanji 1022]] has Du Xi warn that Liu Biao should not be treated as the decisive restoration patron.

## Qualifications
The transcript renders the name as "杜席"; the wiki normalizes to 杜袭 / Du Xi because the surrounding sequence matches the known late-Han story with Zhao Yan and Fan Qin. Later career details in the episode are retained as source-scoped and are not yet a full biography.
