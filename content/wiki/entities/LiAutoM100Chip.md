# Li Auto M100 Chip / 马赫 M100

## Overview
Li Auto M100 Chip / 马赫 M100 is the inference chip named by [[LiXiangLiAuto|李想]] in [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] as a key component of [[LiAuto|理想汽车]]'s AI and embodied-intelligence strategy.

## Current Profile
The source positions M100 as both a vehicle compute upgrade for [[LiAutoL9Levius|L9 LEVIUS]] and a longer-term platform bet. Li argues that after using third-party assisted-driving chips, Li Auto saw a need for its own inference architecture, toolchain, stability, and cost profile.

## Key Characteristics
- Automotive inference chip presented as part of Li Auto's self-developed physical-AI stack.
- Connected to L9 LEVIUS compute, model size, frame rate, vehicle lifecycle upgrades, and future L3/L4 readiness.
- Framed as a dynamic-data-flow direction rather than a generic GPU-style endpoint.
- Treated by Li as a long-term moat even at the cost of near-term profit.
- Source-scoped product whose performance and adoption need independent proof.

## Evidence
- Vehicle integration: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] says L9 LEVIUS uses M100 and reaches 2560 TOPS compute.
- Investment rationale: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] has Li say the chip effort followed prior experience with Mobileye IQ4, Horizon Journey chips, and Nvidia Orin X.
- Moat thesis: [[lixiang-luoyonghao-lixiang-de-lixiang-ai-jishu-fuhao-shenghuo-lify6z4xnd4-vt9qqvxv-pccs7fp]] says future embodied intelligence will make inference chips central, while training-chip advantage is already strongly associated with Nvidia.

## Qualifications
The source is a founder interview. It does not benchmark M100 against competing automotive inference chips, verify the claimed architecture advantage, or prove that the chip will become a durable moat.
