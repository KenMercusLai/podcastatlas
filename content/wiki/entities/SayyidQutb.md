# Sayyid Qutb / Sayed Qutb

Sayyid Qutb, spelled Sayed Qutb in [[spring-then-fall-a-weakened-muslim-brotherhood-6a7aef1b616564bf00d9b354]], is the ideological reference point [[GarethBrown]] uses to explain the [[MuslimBrotherhood]]'s worldview. The episode links him to a reaction against Western civilization and to a call to restore Arab and Islamic dignity.

The page is source-scoped for now. The episode does not give a full intellectual biography; it uses Qutb to show why the Brotherhood should be read as a social and political project rooted in religious values, dignity politics, and community organization rather than only as a party machine.
