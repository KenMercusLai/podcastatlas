# David Anderson

## Overview
David Anderson is named in [[HubermanLab]] sources as both a collaborator in functional emotion framing and a modern aggression-circuit researcher whose lab is credited with ventromedial-hypothalamus evidence.

## Current Profile
The current evidence places Anderson in two related neuroscience contexts. In the [[RalphAdolphs]] emotion episode, he functions as a collaborator reference for the argument that emotions can be studied by functional criteria such as priority, valence, scalability, and persistence. In the aggression Essentials episode, [[AndrewHuberman]] credits Anderson's lab with showing that the [[VentromedialHypothalamus]] is necessary and sufficient for aggressive behavior.

The profile remains source-limited. Anderson is not developed biographically; the wiki currently uses him as a research-lineage marker for functional emotion science and aggression-circuit biology.
