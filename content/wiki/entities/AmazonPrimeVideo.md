# Amazon Prime Video

## Overview
Amazon Prime Video is [[Amazon]]'s video-streaming service, represented here through the source's report that it will become the global streaming home of the [[EmmyAwards|Emmy Awards]] ceremony from 2027.

## Current Profile
The episode frames the rights move as a shift from decades of rotating U.S. broadcast coverage across ABC, CBS, Fox, and NBC toward one globally accessible streaming platform. It says the ceremony will be viewable without a Prime subscription, suggesting that Amazon may use the event for reach, advertising, and ecosystem entry rather than subscriber exclusivity alone.

## Key Characteristics
- Amazon-operated streaming service.
- Reported future global home of the Emmy ceremony.
- Uses free access to broaden reach beyond paying Prime members.
- Connects prestige live programming with advertising and ecosystem acquisition.
