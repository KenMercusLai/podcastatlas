# 许昌 / Xu Chang (late-Han rebel)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-930-ruhe-pochu-tanxin-he-buganxin-lkyysh5jbgxwjq8ayrfar4gsjdcd|Hanji 930]] mentions Xu Chang as a rebel described by the host as a "妖贼" whom [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] had personally killed before Zhu Jun recruited Sun Jian for the Nanyang Yellow Turban campaign.

The page is source-scoped to avoid confusing this person with the Xu Chang place name used elsewhere in Three Kingdoms contexts. In this episode, Xu Chang functions only as background evidence for Sun Jian's preexisting martial record.
