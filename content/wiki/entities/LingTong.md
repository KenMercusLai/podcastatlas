# 凌统 / Ling Tong

## Overview
凌统 / Ling Tong enters the wiki as the officer left to hold the rear while [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] and [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] move to relieve [[GanNing|甘宁]].

## Current Profile
Hanji 1109 gives Ling Tong a narrow but structurally important role. Lyu Meng estimates that he can hold the existing position for ten days, making Ling Tong's rear-guard capacity the condition that permits a temporary concentration of force at the besieged forward point.

## Key Characteristics
- Holds a defensive assignment while senior commanders shift force elsewhere.
- Makes a relief operation possible by preserving the rear position.
- Appears as trusted capacity inside Lyu Meng's division-of-force plan.

## Evidence
- Rear-guard assignment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1109-xizaochi-nu-pi-caocao-duonian-jiye-hui-yu-yidan-lukll8jb60nx2brhwhsw7gfrd-kt|Hanji 1109]] says Lyu Meng proposes leaving Ling Tong behind for ten days.
- Operational dependency: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1109-xizaochi-nu-pi-caocao-duonian-jiye-hui-yu-yidan-lukll8jb60nx2brhwhsw7gfrd-kt|Hanji 1109]] makes that holding role the precondition for Zhou Yu and Lyu Meng's relief movement.
- Campaign result: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1109-xizaochi-nu-pi-caocao-duonian-jiye-hui-yu-yidan-lukll8jb60nx2brhwhsw7gfrd-kt|Hanji 1109]] reports that the relief force defeats Cao Ren's troops and returns successfully.

## Qualifications
The source gives no wider biography or independent account of Ling Tong's conduct during the ten-day assignment. His current profile is therefore limited to the confidence placed in him within Lyu Meng's plan.
