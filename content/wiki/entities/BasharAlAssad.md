# Bashar al-Assad

Bashar al-Assad appears in [[a-firm-andy-what-are-new-british-pms-plans-6a5deeca76f8ee25bb4bbf9c]] as the ruler whose repression forms one part of the episode's explanation for [[Syria]]'s destruction. The source groups Assad's rule with civil war, [[ISIS]], and foreign intervention as causes of the country's postwar reconstruction burden.

The page is source-scoped: this episode is not a full Assad biography. Its contribution is to tie Assad-era repression to [[PostwarReconstructionFinance]], because destroyed infrastructure, damaged housing, investor distrust, and refugee-return decisions all shape whether Syria can recover.
