# 殷晋 / Yinjin

殷晋 / Yinjin appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-34-qinguo-zongli-ruhe-wanzhuan-liuguo-lsenzwp0kr5wscb0btvuzmkibriq]] in two related roles. It is named as [[GongsunYan|犀首公孙衍]]'s home place in [[WeiState|魏国]], and it is the territory Wei cedes to [[QinState|秦国]] when seeking peace in 332 BCE.

The page is source-scoped. Yinjin matters less as geography by itself than as a hinge between personal origin and territorial transfer: a Wei-born strategist helps Qin damage the eastern alliance environment, and Wei then loses his home-region place to Qin in the same episode's pressure sequence.
