## Overview

孙宠 / Sun Chong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-584-xihan-hushan-lishi-moufan-shijian-shimo-lmpoeruya1v3qmixnvi8ewaffqfo|Hanji 584]] as a Chang'an man who joins [[XiFuGongWesternHan|息夫躬]] in accusing [[LiuYunDongpingKingWesternHan|东平王刘云]] of treason and is rewarded after the case.

## Current Profile

Hanji 584 makes Sun Chong a reward-seeking accusation actor. He and Xi Fu Gong learn of Liu Yun's worship of the standing stone, discuss the chance to gain noble rank, and decide to report the case through court intermediaries. The accusation reaches Aidi through You Shi Tan and the eunuch Song Hong, triggering formal investigation.

After Liu Yun is deposed, relatives are executed, and the case expands, Sun Chong receives promotion as Nanyang Taishou. His page therefore records him as part of the Dongping-case information channel rather than as a general statesman.

## Key Characteristics

- Chang'an man involved in the Dongping king accusation.
- Works with Xi Fu Gong after learning of Liu Yun's stone worship.
- Explicitly framed by the source as seeking reward through accusation.
- Uses intermediaries to bring the case to Han Aidi's court.
- Rewarded as Nanyang Taishou after the prosecution succeeds.
- Helps anchor the later Hanji 586 issue of Dongping-case merit attribution.
