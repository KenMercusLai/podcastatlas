## Overview

刘泽 / Liu Ze enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-393-2-yanwang-liudan-moufan-shibai-dui-hunzhichang-yousha-qishi-lqwzkgdj9ozmwb7r97l4ts2tjx_s|Hanji 393-2]] as a grandson of the Qi Xiao king and an operational partner in [[LiuDanYanKingWesternHan|燕王刘旦]]'s first exposed rebellion against [[EmperorZhaoOfHan|汉昭帝刘弗陵]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Liu Ze as the Qi-side executor of a wider royal conspiracy. He joins Liu Dan and Liu Chang in claiming false authorization from [[HanWudi|汉武帝]] and in circulating documents that deny Han Zhao's biological legitimacy. His assigned move is concrete: return to Qi, raise troops from Linzi, and first kill Qingzhou inspector [[JunBuyiWesternHan|隽不疑]].

The plan never reaches execution. Pinghou Liu Cheng reports it, Jun Buyi arrests Liu Ze's group in advance, and the case is sent to Han Zhao and [[HuoGuangWesternHan|霍光]]. Although the investigation identifies Liu Dan as the principal, Han Zhao exempts his elder brother from punishment and Liu Ze and the other participants are executed.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Qi royal descendant identified as a grandson of the Qi Xiao king.
- Co-conspirator who helps convert Liu Dan's succession grievance into a regional rebellion plan.
- Legitimacy propagandist associated with false authorization and denial of Han Zhao's paternity.
- Intended Linzi mobilizer whose first target is Qingzhou inspector Jun Buyi.
- Exposed conspirator arrested before action and executed while Liu Dan is spared.
