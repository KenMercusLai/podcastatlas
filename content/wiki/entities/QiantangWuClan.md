# 钱塘吴氏 / Qiantang Wu Clan

钱塘吴氏 / Qiantang Wu Clan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-982-ba-yi-ba-liubei-ru-xu-beihou-de-liyi-zhi-zheng-ljajdc95xtylhkn5teefz26yyyz1|Hanji 982]] as the family behind [[LadyWuSunJian|吴夫人]]. The episode says the family is not one of the standard Jiangdong four great families, but it is deeply rooted around Qiantang, the Qiantang River transport system, and salt-and-iron trade.

The source treats the clan as an enabling network for the Sun household. [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]'s marriage gives a lower-background military actor access to local gentry and trade routes; [[WuJingLateHan|吴景]] then carries the military-resource side by supplying [[SunCe|孙策]] with Danyang soldiers. Later, [[SunQuan|孙权]] is said to rely on Qiantang water-route knowledge and old Wu-family standing, making the clan part of [[KinshipResourceStateBuilding|姻亲资源建构]].
