# Moshe Porat

## Overview

Moshe Porat is the former [[TempleUniversity]] business-school dean used in the episode's comparison between ranking-data misconduct and institutional response after discovery.

## Current Profile

The source says Temple's online business school falsely reported that all incoming students had taken the GMAT, rose to first place, and generated almost $40 million in additional tuition. It reports that Porat was convicted of fraud and sentenced to 14 months, while Temple commissioned an outside investigation, compensated affected students, and referred him to authorities.

Porat's case is not presented as equivalent to Columbia's. It supplies a contrast in adjudication and remediation: the episode has a named convicted actor and concrete institutional actions in the Temple case, while responsibility and intent remain unresolved in the Columbia case.
