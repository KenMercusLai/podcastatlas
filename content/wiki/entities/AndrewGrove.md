# Andrew Grove

Andrew Grove appears in [[zhenzheng-gaibian-shijie-de-jishu-weishenme-yikaishi-dou-bu-bei-kanhao-s10e16-8c95b3dc-d75a-4bdd-84d4-2c06fd2d85b1]] through the episode's discussion of [[Intel]] and the management problem later associated with [[DisruptiveInnovation]]. The source recounts the story that the author of The Innovator's Dilemma presented the research to Grove and that the conversation expanded far beyond the planned short meeting.

In this source, Grove is less a biographical subject than a marker for the organizational lesson: strong incumbents can understand disruption intellectually while still having to build structures that protect new work from core-business metrics and near-term incentives.
