## Overview

《凤求凰》 / *Feng Qiu Huang* appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-358-2-simaxiangru-diao-fengliu-guafu-lphrip8prodrc46x__y6elbrkigz|Hanji 358-2]] as the musical medium through which [[SimaXiangru|司马相如]] signals desire to [[ZhuoWenjun|卓文君]] at [[ZhuoWangsunWesternHan|卓王孙]]'s banquet.

## Current Profile

The episode treats the work as an instrumental performance with a legible courtship meaning. [[WangJiLinqiongWesternHan|王吉]] asks Sima to play, Zhuo Wenjun listens from behind a screen, and the music crosses a social boundary that direct conversation has not yet crossed.

Its role in the current wiki is therefore narrative and relational: it is the pivot between staged public prestige and private romantic response. This source alone does not establish the work's authorship, original text, musical form, or transmission history.

## Key Characteristics

- Courtship work associated in the episode with Sima Xiangru and Zhuo Wenjun.
- Performed at Zhuo Wangsun's banquet after Wang Ji prompts Sima to play.
- Functions as indirect communication across a screened social setting.
- Survives in the episode as a symbol of intense love complicated by later betrayal.
