# Maextro / 尊界

Maextro / 尊界 is the [[JACGroup|江淮]] and [[Huawei]] ultra-luxury branch of [[HongmengZhixing]]. [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] presents the S800 as a 700,000-yuan to million-yuan-level vehicle aimed at Chinese-brand entry into a segment associated with Maybach, Bentley, and Rolls-Royce.

The source emphasizes manufacturing discipline as much as price. It cites a large dedicated R&D and delivery team, a Shanghai R&D center, a Hefei factory, body-in-white cleanliness checks, quality gates, and many quality-control points to argue that the brand is trying to translate Huawei's product ambition into a luxury manufacturing standard.

## Source Position
- Maextro is the episode's most aggressive [[HighEndEVBranding|high-end EV branding]] case.
- The source treats the S800 as a Chinese luxury-perception experiment, not only as another expensive EV.
- Delivery and ranking claims are kept source-scoped to the episode's cited figures.
