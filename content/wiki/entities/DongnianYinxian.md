# Dongnian Yinxian / 动念引线

## Overview
Dongnian Yinxian / 动念引线 is [[LiangChenqi|梁琛奇]]'s AI-entertainment startup, founded after he left [[ByteDance]] in 2025.

## Current Profile
The company is exploring consumer products where AI helps people express imagination and enter interactive worlds rather than only complete productivity tasks. Its reported operating model combines shared model and technical infrastructure with several small product teams that test narrower audiences and content formats.

## Key Characteristics
- Pursues AI-native leisure, expression, and interactive experience as its company direction.
- Uses multiple bounded product experiments to discover promising behavior before committing to a single broad platform.
- Centralizes model and technical capabilities while keeping product teams small and fast-moving.
- Treats serving cost, content quality, audience focus, and creator supply as one product system.
- Uses periodic review to revisit founder judgments, hiring pace, and product assumptions.

- The portfolio strategy is exploratory; the source does not establish durable retention, revenue, or product-market fit.
