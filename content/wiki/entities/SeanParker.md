# Sean Parker

## Overview
Sean Parker is a technology founder and network-builder represented in the wiki through Napster's financing crisis, later companies and relationships, and his early patronage of artist [[DavidChoe]].

## Current Profile
In [[tsr-ronconway-part4-v2-tsr-ronconway-part4-v2]], Parker is [[SeanFanning]]'s more outward-facing [[Napster]] counterpart and a central actor in the weekend intervention needed to close [[HummerWinblad]] financing. After Napster, he built [[Plaxo]], introduced [[RonConway]] to [[MarkZuckerberg]], pursued cancer-immunotherapy philanthropy, and remained connected to Fanning through later ventures.

Choe's account presents Parker as a creative patron and loyal ally. He says Parker sought his outlaw visual energy for Napster-era work and later commissioned Facebook murals intended to signal that the company was unlike conventional technology firms. Choe also credits Parker with defending him in a Warner dispute and with keeping him informed as his Facebook equity rose in value in [[transform-pain-trauma-into-creative-expression-david-choe-scim4316318313]].

## Key Characteristics
- Combines company formation with relationship brokerage across founders, investors, artists, and later ventures.
- Appears as an outward-facing operator during Napster's financing and governance crisis.
- Carries relationships beyond one company, including continued collaboration and introductions after Napster.
- Uses art and cultural signaling as part of early-company identity formation.
- Is portrayed by Choe as unusually loyal when an artist was treated dismissively.

## Evidence
- Napster financing and governance - [[tsr-ronconway-part4-v2-tsr-ronconway-part4-v2]] places Parker and Fanning inside the weekend board-resolution effort required for Hummer Winblad's financing.
- Post-Napster network - [[tsr-ronconway-part4-v2-tsr-ronconway-part4-v2]] connects Parker to Plaxo, Zuckerberg, Facebook, philanthropy, and later work with Fanning.
- Creative patronage - [[transform-pain-trauma-into-creative-expression-david-choe-scim4316318313]] says Parker commissioned Choe around Napster and Facebook because of his art and outlaw energy.
- Loyalty and equity relationship - [[transform-pain-trauma-into-creative-expression-david-choe-scim4316318313]] reports Parker's support during a Warner dispute and his updates to Choe about the changing value of Facebook shares.

## Qualifications
The Napster account is investor Ron Conway's participant recollection, while the art and loyalty account is Choe's personal recollection. The current sources do not independently verify private conversations, motives, deal terms, or the precise causal role Parker played in Choe's Facebook equity decision.
