# 燕武成王 / King Wucheng of Yan

燕武成王 / King Wucheng of Yan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-85-xuantaihou-weihe-sitong-30-nian-hou-ansha-le-laoqingren-lg7zy6vqtdjcufou4-mox5qkg2ct5]] as the successor installed after [[GongsunCao|公孙操]] kills [[YanHuiwang|燕惠王]]. The episode says his succession happens in the same political environment as the Qin-Wei-Chu attack on [[YanState|燕国]].

The page is source-scoped. Its current function is to mark that Yan's collapse after the loss of Qi did not end with military defeat: the weakened royal authority of Yan Huiwang also becomes a court coup and succession event.
