# 支付宝 / Alipay

## Overview
Alipay / 支付宝 is the Ant Group app and service network that [[273-guangwan-waitan-dahui-faxian-mayi-zhaodaole-xin-weizhi-lotxogfoqqwcjigxihtixvncbfc3]] treats as the base for Ant's agent-era repositioning.

## Current Profile
The source presents Alipay less as a standalone payment wallet than as a service graph containing payment, orders, bills, mini-programs, insurance, medical access, merchant touchpoints, and other daily-life workflows. That makes it a plausible base for [[AntAbao|阿宝]], [[AntAfu|阿福]], [[AlipayTapNetwork|碰一下]], and [[AgentPaymentInfrastructure|AI payment]], but also creates a challenge: many desires and discovery moments still happen in external attention platforms rather than inside Alipay itself.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as Ant Group's main consumer-facing app and daily-life service shelf.
- Provides payment, order, bill, mini-program, and account context that can support agentic task routing.
- Anchors Ant's move from traffic competition toward intent recognition and fulfillment.
- Connects AI payment to established trust, risk-control, and dispute-handling infrastructure.
- Extends into health and offline merchant scenes through 阿福 and 碰一下.

## Evidence
- Service-shelf evidence: [[273-guangwan-waitan-dahui-faxian-mayi-zhaodaole-xin-weizhi-lotxogfoqqwcjigxihtixvncbfc3]] describes 阿宝 as Alipay's existing service shelf made more agentic through natural-language intent.
- Trust evidence: [[273-guangwan-waitan-dahui-faxian-mayi-zhaodaole-xin-weizhi-lotxogfoqqwcjigxihtixvncbfc3]] links AI payment to Alipay's accumulated payment, risk-control, authorization, refund, and liability boundary experience.
- Offline evidence: [[273-guangwan-waitan-dahui-faxian-mayi-zhaodaole-xin-weizhi-lotxogfoqqwcjigxihtixvncbfc3]] says 碰一下 gives Alipay a dense offline interaction network that can support merchant-facing intelligence.

## Qualifications
The source is a strategic interpretation rather than an adoption study. It does not prove that users will prefer Alipay as a personal agent entrance, especially when discovery and unfulfilled desire may start in WeChat, Xiaohongshu, Douyin, or other platforms.

## What Changed
- Added Alipay as a distinct entity rather than using Ant Group as a loose alias for all Alipay-related claims.
