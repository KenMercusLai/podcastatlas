# 郦食其 / Li Shiqi

郦食其 / Li Shiqi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-135-mingyun-tai-shenqi-cong-yufu-zuodao-wang-lrh1lhuxxikx4m6prpi6qm0jcafb|Qinji 135]] as a low-status persuader preparing to approach [[LiuBang|刘邦]]. The episode introduces him after citing [[LiBai|李白]]'s 《梁甫吟》 and presents him as a poor older Gaoyang man who works as a gatekeeper and is locally called a "狂生."

The source does not yet give his successful persuasion scene. It focuses on access. Li Shiqi hears that many commanders passing through Gaoyang are too narrow-minded for his advice, but that Liu Bang is arrogant while still approachable and interested in large strategy. He asks a cavalryman who knows Liu Bang to introduce him.

The cavalryman warns that Liu Bang dislikes scholars: he has reportedly taken a Confucian cap and urinated in it, curses people in conversation, and does not like being lectured by educated men. Li Shiqi still insists that the message be delivered: he is over sixty, tall, called mad by others, but says he is not actually mad.

The episode leaves the meeting as a cliffhanger. For this source, Li Shiqi's role is therefore a pending case of [[LowStatusStrategicPersuasion|低位者战略说服]] and possible [[StrategicRecognitionAlignment|谋略知己式归附]], not yet a completed Liu Bang recruitment branch.
