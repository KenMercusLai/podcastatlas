## Overview

楚服 / Chu Fu is presented in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-270-chenajiao-mishi-hougong-zhizhong-ta-weihe-duchong-nvguan-1-lqyk8xtyqwmbmytg9pq17flesyyf|Hanji 270-1]] as a female ritual specialist who enters [[EmpressChenAjiaoWesternHan|陈阿娇]]'s confidence after the empress loses [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s favor.

## Current Profile

The episode gives Chu Fu three overlapping roles. She promises that ritual action can restore Wudi's affection, teaches sacrifice and curse practices allegedly aimed at [[EmpressWeiZifuWesternHan|卫子夫]], and becomes Chen's intimate companion. Through a passage attributed to 《汉武故事》, the source says Chu Fu wore male clothing, lived and slept with Chen, and was “相爱若夫妇” with her.

After Wudi learns of the alleged wugu activity, [[ZhangTangWesternHan|张汤]] investigates and Chu Fu is publicly executed. The episode further claims that Chu manipulated Chen into loving her, but it supplies no independent evidence for coercion, magic efficacy, or private motive. The most defensible profile is therefore a source-reported ritual intermediary and intimate whose relationship became inseparable from a politically lethal accusation.

## Key Characteristics

- Female ritual specialist approached during Chen's loss of imperial favor.
- Promises to alter Wudi's affection through ritual means.
- Allegedly teaches sacrifice, cursing, and “魅术” directed toward palace rivalry.
- Described in a passage attributed to 《汉武故事》 as wearing male clothing and sharing intimate domestic space with Chen.
- Executed after Zhang Tang's investigation turns the private relationship and ritual practice into a state case.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on one short popular-history summary. It does not establish the textual history or reliability of 《汉武故事》, the legal record against Chu Fu, whether rituals occurred as described, or whether Chen and Chu understood their relationship through any modern sexual-identity category. The host's claim that Chu made Chen love her is interpretation, not demonstrated coercion or supernatural efficacy.

## What Changed

- Created a bounded profile separating Chu Fu's reported ritual, intimate, and punishment roles from claims about magic efficacy, coercion, or modern identity.
