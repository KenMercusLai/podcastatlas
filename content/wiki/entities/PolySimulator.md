# PolySimulator

## Overview
PolySimulator is the simulation tool used in the [[DuanwenNewsPodcast|端闻]] Polymarket episode. The source says it connects to [[Polymarket]] live data and gives users virtual funds for practice trading.

## Current Profile
In this source, PolySimulator is not treated as a full trading platform or investment product. Its wiki role is methodological: it lets [[XiaoweiDuanwenReporter|小薇]] test whether news judgment translates into event-market gains while avoiding real-money loss.

The simulator makes the experiment safer, but it also limits what the result can prove. The episode can observe decision habits, timing mistakes, and attention changes, but it cannot fully capture real liquidity, withdrawal, emotional pressure, or cryptocurrency custody risk.

## Key Characteristics
- Simulation layer connected to Polymarket live market data.
- Provides virtual trading funds rather than requiring real-money exposure.
- Enables a bounded reporter experiment around information, timing, and settlement rules.
- Limits financial risk while also reducing evidence about real trading pressure.
