# 宋义 / Song Yi

宋义 / Song Yi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-131-1-qinxiang-lisi-yisheng-jingming-que-zao-yaozhan-liupvalcghtas2ibfyjrtdz6ctg7|Qinji 131-1]] as a next-story hook after [[LiSi|李斯]]'s death. The source says Song Yi had once served as [[ChuState|楚国]] lingyin and later joined [[XiangLiang|项梁]] when anti-Qin forces rose across the former Chu region.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-132-5wan-wanbao-40wan-xiangyu-cizhan-fengshen-lk87nb3fskg5zh6fcgptypixhzuw|Qinji 132]] develops that hook into Song Yi's main early role. After Xiang Liang wins repeatedly at Dong'e and Dingtao, Song Yi warns that a victorious army can still fail if the commander becomes arrogant and the soldiers slack. Xiang Liang ignores him and sends him to [[QiState|齐国]].

On the road, Song Yi meets a Qi envoy named Gaoling Jun and tells him not to hurry toward Xiang Liang because Xiang Liang will soon be defeated. [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]]'s reinforced night attack at [[Dingtao|定陶]] then kills Xiang Liang, so the episode treats Song Yi as someone who read the army's morale and risk correctly.

After [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]] moves to [[Xuzhou|彭城]] and issues [[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]], the same Gaoling Jun recommends Song Yi to Mi Xin. Mi Xin appoints Song Yi as chief general for the [[Julu|巨鹿]] relief army, with [[XiangYu|项羽]] as second general and [[FanZeng|范增]] as last general.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-133-songyi-zhan-xiangyu-weihe-bei-fansha-lms7noyrm5omedv77gqfhjeu53zx|Qinji 133]] narrates that short-lived command. On the way to Julu, Song Yi stops the army at [[AnyangQinRebellion|安阳]] for forty-six days. His strategic argument is that Qin and Zhao should be allowed to exhaust each other before Chu intervenes, a source case for [[DelayedRescueStatecraft|晚救权谋]].

The episode then turns Song Yi's authority into a failure sequence. He humiliates [[XiangYu|项羽]] by saying Xiang Yu is better at fighting in armor while Song Yi is better at planning; then he issues a disciplinary order threatening death to fierce, stubborn, greedy, and disobedient soldiers, which the host reads as aimed at Xiang Yu. This converts strategic disagreement into a personal and command-status confrontation.

Song Yi's trip to [[WuyanCounty|无盐县]] is the fatal morale marker. While soldiers at Anyang are cold, hungry, wet, and short of provisions, Song Yi sends his son toward an office in [[QiState|齐国]] and drinks with Qi officials. The episode reads this as failure to practice [[MilitaryMoraleThroughSharedHardship|同甘共苦式士气]], leaving Xiang Yu able to frame Song Yi as selfish and not state-serving.

After Song Yi returns, Xiang Yu enters the command tent with other generals and kills him. Song Yi's page therefore becomes a second source case for [[WartimeCommandDecapitation|战时统帅斩首]], distinct from [[TianZang|田臧]] killing [[WuGuang|吴广]] because Xiang Yu relies less on forged superior authorization than on emergency, soldier hardship, and the claim that the chief general has failed [[ChuState|楚国]].
