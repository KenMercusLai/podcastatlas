# Burntollet Bridge Attack

The Burntollet Bridge attack appears in [[698-the-troubles-the-brink-of-civil-war-part-2-glt2308408988]] as the 4 January 1969 loyalist ambush of [[PeoplesDemocracyNorthernIreland|People's Democracy]] marchers on the way to [[Derry]]. The episode says loyalists, including off-duty [[BSpecials|B Specials]], attacked while [[RoyalUlsterConstabulary|RUC]] officers stood by.

The source treats Burntollet as a point where both activist provocation and loyalist violence deepened the crisis after [[TerenceONeill|Terence O'Neill]]'s December appeal. Catholics increasingly saw the state as unwilling to protect them, while loyalists increasingly saw civil-rights activism as intentional disorder.
