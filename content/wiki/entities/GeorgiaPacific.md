# Georgia-Pacific

Georgia-Pacific appears in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-charles-chase-koch-on-how-they-quietly-built-a-150b-empire-41270895]] as a major [[KochIndustries|Koch Industries]] acquisition and culture-integration case. The source says Koch bought Georgia-Pacific for $20 billion in 2005.

[[CharlesKoch|Charles Koch]] describes Georgia-Pacific as having a top-down bureaucratic culture, including status signals around executive offices and restricted access. In the source's account, Koch removed bureaucratic signals and reorganized leadership to integrate the company into [[KochOperatingPrinciples]] and [[ValuesAsOperationalAsset|values as operational assets]].
