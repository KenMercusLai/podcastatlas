# Mikhail Gorbachev / 米哈伊尔·戈尔巴乔夫

Mikhail Gorbachev / 米哈伊尔·戈尔巴乔夫 enters the wiki through [[81-jiandie-yu-pantu-shuwozhiyan-007-de-gushi-ruobaole-xia-693711209]] as the rising [[SovietUnion|Soviet]] leader whose British visit was interpreted and indirectly shaped through [[OlegGordievsky|Oleg Gordievsky]]'s intelligence. The episode says [[MargaretThatcher|Margaret Thatcher]] trusted Gordievsky's advice after earlier Soviet political reporting, and that British briefings passed back through KGB channels helped make the visit work.

Gorbachev also belongs to the episode's nuclear-risk branch. After Gordievsky reached the West, Americans sought his judgment before Gorbachev's U.S. visit, especially around how the Soviet side might read nuclear-disarmament trust and the Strategic Defense Initiative.
