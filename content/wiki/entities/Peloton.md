# Peloton

Peloton appears in [[tech-20260204-0204-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260204-0204-mp-tech-pod-128]] as the connected-fitness company whose AI-enabled camera and strength-training feedback most impressed [[NicoleNguyen]]. The source says Peloton's [[ComputerVisionFormCorrection]] and rep-counting made workouts more motivating, but also tied the strongest AI fitness experience to expensive hardware and a roughly $50 monthly subscription.

The episode treats Peloton as the premium end of [[AIFitnessCoaching]]. Its camera feedback can address the form problem that generic chatbots cannot see, while its cost and occasional rep-counting errors show that AI fitness remains a hardware, subscription, and reliability tradeoff.
