## Overview

濡须口之战（217年） / Ruxukou Campaign (217 CE) is the later [[CaoCao|曹操]]–[[SunQuan|孙权]] confrontation that Hanji 1157 distinguishes from the [[RuxukouCampaign213LateHan|213 CE Ruxukou campaign]].

## Current Profile

The episode says Cao Cao moves to Juchao and gathers information before committing fully, while Sun Quan defends at Ruxu. Limited contact produces no decisive advantage. Cao Cao prepares to withdraw and leaves Xiahou Dun over a continuing force; Sun Quan sends Xu Xiang to express nominal submission, and Cao Cao reciprocates with a temporary friendly posture.

The episode interprets the settlement as reciprocal risk management. Sun Quan avoids simultaneous pressure from Cao Cao and Liu Bei, while Cao Cao accepts a face-preserving outcome from a campaign in which further fighting offers limited expected return. The relationship remains tactical rather than a durable alliance.

## Key Characteristics

- Later Ruxu confrontation distinct from the 213 CE campaign.
- Begins with Cao-side deployment and intelligence gathering rather than immediate decisive attack.
- Produces no clear battlefield decision in the supplied account.
- Ends with Cao Cao's withdrawal and a retained northern garrison structure.
- Includes Sun Quan's nominal submission and a temporary diplomatic accommodation.
- Leaves the underlying Cao-Sun rivalry unresolved.
