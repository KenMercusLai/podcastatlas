# Gnaeus Pompeius Strabo / 格奈乌斯·庞培乌斯·斯特拉波

## Overview

Gnaeus Pompeius Strabo / 格奈乌斯·庞培乌斯·斯特拉波 is a Roman commander whose [[SocialWar]] reputation turns in episode 703 into independent legion control and bargaining power during the first aftermath of [[LuciusCorneliusSulla]]'s march on Rome.

## Current Profile

Episode 702 places Pompeius Strabo in Picenum and at Asculum during the Social War. Rome initially suffers defeats, but Strabo is effective in his theater and besieges Asculum, one of the revolt's symbolic starting points. That role made the Social War a competitive proving ground for generals beyond [[GaiusMarius]] and Sulla.

Episode 703 changes his profile from capable commander to unstable military broker. Sulla tries to transfer Strabo's legions to Pompeius Rufus, but Strabo's soldiers kill the replacement commander during a sacrifice. Strabo returns, claims shock, does nothing to punish the killers, and keeps the army. Later he camps outside Rome near the Colline Gate while [[LuciusCorneliusCinna]] and Marius threaten the city, waiting to see which side needs him most. His death by plague and lightning removes the major military obstacle to Cinna and Marius entering Rome and leaves behind the young [[Pompey]].

## Key Characteristics

- Pompeius Strabo appears first as a capable Roman commander during the Social War.
- His theater is Picenum and his major named action is the siege of Asculum.
- Episode 703 turns his command into a personal bargaining asset that Sulla cannot simply reassign.
- His failure to punish his soldiers' killing of Pompeius Rufus shows command discipline subordinated to personal army control.
- His ambiguous posture outside Rome makes him a power broker rather than a loyal institutional servant.
- His death opens the way for Cinna and Marius while introducing Pompey as his nineteen-year-old son.
