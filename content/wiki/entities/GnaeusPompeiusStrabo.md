# Gnaeus Pompeius Strabo / 格奈乌斯·庞培乌斯·斯特拉波

## Overview

Gnaeus Pompeius Strabo / 格奈乌斯·庞培乌斯·斯特拉波 is a Roman commander whom episode 702 uses to show that the [[SocialWar]] became a competitive proving ground for generals beyond [[GaiusMarius]] and [[LuciusCorneliusSulla]].

## Current Profile

The source places Pompeius Strabo in Picenum and at Asculum during the Social War. Rome initially suffers defeats, but Strabo is effective in his theater and besieges Asculum, one of the revolt's symbolic starting points.

His page therefore holds a narrow function: he shows that the Social War produced multiple Roman command reputations. The episode still reserves the strongest career consequences for Sulla, who emerges as the standout commander and then wins the consulship.

## Key Characteristics

- Pompeius Strabo appears as a capable Roman commander during the Social War.
- His theater is Picenum and his major named action is the siege of Asculum.
- His role helps make the Social War a command-competition field, not only an allied-rights dispute.
- The source contrasts his effectiveness with Sulla's still greater rise.

## Evidence

- Social War theater: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] says Pompeius Strabo was effective in Picenum.
- Asculum action: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] says he besieged Asculum after the revolt began there violently.
- Command comparison: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] groups him with Marius and Sulla in the Roman military response.
- Sulla contrast: [[702-roman-civil-war-the-barbarian-invasion-part-2-glt3336491089]] treats Sulla, rather than Strabo, as the commander whose Social War performance most directly leads to the 88 BC consulship.
