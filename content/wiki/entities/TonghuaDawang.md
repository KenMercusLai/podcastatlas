# 《童话大王》

《童话大王》 is the [[ZhengYuanjie|郑渊洁]]-associated children's magazine and reading-memory object discussed in the wiki's [[177-xiaoshihou-de-shu-zenme-jiu-name-haokan-971996036]] branch and expanded by [[23-feima-pai-qiche-anquan-shi-di-yi-wei-de-572699943]]. It matters less as a generic magazine title than as a distribution form through which [[Pipilu]], [[Luxixi]], [[ShukeBeta|舒克贝塔]], [[GreyWolfRock|大灰狼罗克]], and other Zheng stories entered childhood reading.

The current source uses the magazine memory to explain why Zheng could feel formative to readers: the works made children meet school discipline, adult hypocrisy, copyright disputes, social rules, money, and institutional absurdity through funny or adventurous stories rather than through adult lectures.
