# Apple TV+

## Overview
Apple TV+ is [[Apple]]'s subscription streaming service, introduced in 2019 after the company began buying film and television projects in 2017. In the wiki it is treated as a media-strategy case rather than a full streaming business profile.

## Current Profile
The current source uses Apple TV+ to show a content-spending divergence. Apple won 28 [[EmmyAwards|Emmy Awards]] in 2026 and became the first company outside [[HBO]], [[Netflix]], and Disney's FX to lead the awards since 2003, at a moment when the rest of the industry cut output and eligible titles fell about 40% in four years to 212. The episode reads the new chief executive's appearance at the ceremony as a signal that Apple intends to keep funding large-budget projects, while separating that awards position from audience scale: Nielsen data put Apple TV at 0.5% of total U.S. television watch time in July against close to 8% for Netflix.

## Key Characteristics
- Subscription streaming service inside a hardware-and-services company, funded by Apple's balance sheet rather than by streaming economics alone.
- Originals-first catalog with a comparatively thin library of older films and series.
- Counter-cyclical content investor while competitors reduce output.
- Awards success used as a quality, talent, and brand signal.
- Audience share that remains small relative to Netflix even after awards leadership.
- Strategic direction tied to Apple leadership willingness to keep spending on prestige content.

## Evidence
- Awards position - [[tulasi-pingguo-cheng-jinnian-aimeijiang-de-zuida-yingjia-xiecheng-erjidu-youying-zhuankui-1015756412]] reports 28 Emmy wins and the first non-HBO, non-Netflix, non-FX leadership since 2003.
- Content spending - [[tulasi-pingguo-cheng-jinnian-aimeijiang-de-zuida-yingjia-xiecheng-erjidu-youying-zhuankui-1015756412]] says eligible titles across the industry dropped to 212, about 40% below four years earlier, while Apple kept investing in bigger productions.
- Leadership signal - [[tulasi-pingguo-cheng-jinnian-aimeijiang-de-zuida-yingjia-xiecheng-erjidu-youying-zhuankui-1015756412]] treats the new chief executive's Emmy attendance as evidence of continued streaming commitment.
- Audience scale - [[tulasi-pingguo-cheng-jinnian-aimeijiang-de-zuida-yingjia-xiecheng-erjidu-youying-zhuankui-1015756412]] cites Nielsen data showing Apple TV at 0.5% of U.S. television watch time in July and a missing library of older content.

## Qualifications
The source gives award counts, eligibility counts, and a watch-share figure from cited media and measurement reporting, not Apple financials. It does not provide subscribers, revenue, per-title costs, or retention, so awards leadership should not be read as proof of streaming profitability.
