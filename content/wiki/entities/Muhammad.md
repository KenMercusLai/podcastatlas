# Muhammad

## Overview

Muhammad appears in this bounded source as Islam's prophet and as the figure whose revelation, migration, return to [[Mecca]], and reconsecration of the [[Kaaba]] restore Abrahamic monotheism in the traditional Muslim account.

## Current Profile

The episode says Muhammad began receiving revelation at forty, opposed the idols of Mecca, migrated to Yathrib in 622, and returned in 630. His cleansing of the Kaaba makes the sanctuary's meaning part of a prophetic restoration narrative rather than merely a new political foundation.

The source also treats the Quran as the earliest textual starting point available for the Kaaba's history. Later claims about the shrine must therefore be separated among Quranic reference, Muslim tradition, external attestation, and modern historical inference.

## Key Characteristics

- Prophet at the center of the Islamic account presented by the episode.
- Recipient of Quranic revelation used as the earliest textual basis for the Kaaba discussion.
- Opponent of Meccan idolatry and advocate of submission to one God.
- Migrant from Mecca to Yathrib and returning victor in the episode's chronology.
- Reconsecrator of the Kaaba to monotheistic worship in Muslim tradition.

## Evidence

- **Revelation and conflict:** [[265-saudi-arabia-the-mystery-of-the-kaaba-glt1528147434]] narrates Muhammad's preaching against idols and departure from Mecca.
- **Return and reconsecration:** [[265-saudi-arabia-the-mystery-of-the-kaaba-glt1528147434]] dates his return to 630 and connects it to destruction of the shrine's idols.
- **Textual role:** [[265-saudi-arabia-the-mystery-of-the-kaaba-glt1528147434]] treats Quranic references as the starting point for the sanctuary's early history.

## Qualifications

This page is intentionally source-bounded and does not attempt a comprehensive biography, theology, chronology of revelation, or review of early Islamic scholarship. The episode's purpose is the Kaaba, so Muhammad's wider life and legacy remain outside its evidence inventory.
