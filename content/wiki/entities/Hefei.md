# Hefei / 合肥

Hefei / 合肥 is the modern city anchor in [[no-211-huishang-wangshi-cong-qianshi-buxiu-dao-zuiniu-fengtou-1002275189]]. The episode starts from the source-cited claim that Hefei became the top Chinese city by 2025 new-energy-vehicle output, then works backward into [[Anhui]] commercial history and forward into the city's public-capital industrial strategy.

The source gives Hefei three main foundations. [[UniversityOfScienceAndTechnologyOfChina]] supplied a long-run scientific and talent base after relocating from Beijing. [[BOETechnology]] made the city's high-risk display-panel financing famous. [[ChangXinMemory]] and [[NIO]] extended the model into DRAM and electric vehicles, while [[BYD]] and Volkswagen added more manufacturing density.

The episode's core caution is that Hefei is not a frictionless "venture capital city." It is closer to a [[ProductionInvestmentCity]] using [[HefeiIndustrialInvestmentModel|industrial investment]]: public capital, land, credit support, infrastructure, and city strategy are combined to build clusters, but wrong technology routes and weak company economics can still produce large losses.
