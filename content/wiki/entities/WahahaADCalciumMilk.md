# 娃哈哈AD钙奶 / Wahaha AD Calcium Milk

## Overview
娃哈哈AD钙奶 is a [[WahahaGroup|娃哈哈]] children's beverage product in the 大小电波 Wahaha history source.

## Current Profile
The product represents Wahaha's 1996 move from nutrition products into a broader beverage portfolio. It combined milk, fruit-juice flavoring, and vitamins A and D around children's calcium needs, then used packaging, school-adjacent retail, and the company's distributor system to become one of the group's long-running cash products.

## Key Characteristics
- Children's beverage positioned around calcium and vitamin supplementation.
- Closely tied to school-adjacent channels and childhood product memory.
- Long-lived core SKU that later required sugar-free and youth-facing renewal attempts.
