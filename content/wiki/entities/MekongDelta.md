# Mekong Delta / 湄公河三角洲

The Mekong Delta / 湄公河三角洲 is the downstream case in [[ep267-tafang-meigonghe-yuji-hunfeiyi-yu-shuili-diguo-lkq2vxaatn4wxue7y5q1fc3-8yfk]]. The episode contrasts it with upper-river travel and Angkor history: the delta becomes a rice-rich region through later migration, Vietnamese expansion, Chinese settler involvement, and especially French colonial canal and reclamation work.

For the wiki, the delta matters as a [[MekongDeltaColonialCanalAgriculture]] case. Canal grids and rice fields made large-scale production possible, but the same engineered landscape is vulnerable when freshwater flow weakens, sea water pushes inland, climate changes, and natural river meanders or mangrove buffers are simplified.
