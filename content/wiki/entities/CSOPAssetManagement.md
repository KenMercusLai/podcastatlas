# CSOP Asset Management / 南方东英

CSOP Asset Management / 南方东英 appears in [[vol-266-yi-ci-xing-gao-dong-etf-1002344828]] as the issuer of [[ETF7709HK|7709.HK]], the Hong Kong-listed two-times daily long [[SKHynix|SK Hynix]] ETF discussed in the episode's risk case.

The source uses CSOP less as a company-history subject than as an example of how Hong Kong can package cross-border exposure for investors. In this case, the package links [[HongKongExchangesAndClearing|Hong Kong's exchange market]] to a Korean semiconductor stock and a global AI-memory narrative.

## Key Claims
- CSOP is the issuer context for the source's most important ETF risk example.
- The source treats its 7709.HK product as evidence that ETF wrappers can turn hard-to-access overseas exposure into retail-accessible trading products.
- Product convenience does not remove the issuer-side and dealer-side need to manage swaps, hedges, and market timing.
