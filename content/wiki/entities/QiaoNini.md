# 乔妮妮 / Qiao Nini

## Overview
乔妮妮 / Qiao Nini is a soprano and vocal-performance doctoral student whose [[WushichaYanjiusuo|无时差研究所]] conversation connects Bel Canto technique, operatic acting, language study, artistic education, career uncertainty, and cross-cultural creation.

## Current Profile
In [[ep193-bel-canto-duihua-meisheng-gechangjia-ting-geju-daodi-zai-ting-shenme-ckwrijifn4vdabaaaadrcjk0]], Qiao describes beginning piano at five, discovering her singing ability through a school competition, and shifting toward vocal study when conservatory-level piano competition exposed a technical gap. She later studied at Mannes School of Music and, at recording time, was in the second year of a vocal opera-performance doctorate at Ohio State University.

Her account treats singing as a compound practice. Absolute pitch and vocal endowment helped, but [[OperaticPerformanceTraining]] also required teachers suited to her technical needs, sung-language diction, textual understanding, role analysis, movement, acting, stylistic knowledge, and adaptation to travel or changing performance time. Her planned career combines performance and teaching, with longer-term interests in Chinese-Western concert programming, cultural exchange, and music-themed food experiences.

## Key Characteristics
- Soprano trained first in piano and later in classical voice.
- Singer with absolute pitch who nevertheless emphasizes systematic technique over raw aptitude.
- International conservatory and doctoral student navigating repertoire through language and style.
- Performer who treats character psychology and truthful reaction as part of singing.
- Aspiring teacher who values visible student progress alongside stage work.
- Cross-cultural creator interested in joining Chinese language and melody with Western compositional structures.
