# 《资产配置行动指南》 / Asset Allocation Action Guide

## Overview

《资产配置行动指南》 is [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]]'s book adaptation of the [[QizhulouYanBinke|起朱楼宴宾客]] asset-allocation series. The wiki treats it as a bridge from podcast-based public education to a written, reader-stage-aware framework for ordinary investors who need to connect worldview, life phase, household balance sheets, and action.

## Current Profile

The book is not presented as a transcript, product list, or single model portfolio. Across the bounded source set it functions as an organizing artifact: episode 158 explains why the podcast series needed a durable written guide, episode 166 uses it to translate institutional discipline for ordinary investors, and episode 180 shows it working as a conversation starter for a finance-avoidant beginner who has mostly held cash.

## Key Characteristics

- Converts a multi-year podcast asset-allocation series into a durable action guide for ordinary investors.
- Starts from life goals, human capital, worldview, temperament, and risk perception before product selection.
- Treats household decisions about work, housing, insurance, retirement, emergency cash, and consumption as asset-allocation questions.
- Bridges institutional-investor process ideas into personal practice without asking individuals to copy institutional information advantages.
- Accepts reader-stage differences, including beginners who feel shame, regret, or resistance around financial planning.
- Lowers the threshold for action through self-knowledge, review cadence, small experiments, and portfolio suitability rather than a universal answer.

## Qualifications

The current evidence comes from David Weng's own podcast ecosystem and crossover conversations, so it does not yet include independent book reviews or reader outcomes. The sources do not claim the book supplies exact product picks or a universal portfolio. Its usefulness depends on whether a reader can translate self-knowledge, human-capital risk, and household obligations into actual behavior.
