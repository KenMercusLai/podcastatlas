# 《资产配置行动指南》 / Asset Allocation Action Guide

《资产配置行动指南》 is [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]]'s book explained in [[158-boke-li-liao-le-si-nian-de-zichan-peizhi-wo-ba-ta-zuocheng-le-yiben-xingdong-zhinan-ljorcjal-gpuhmwodo1l3efr7k29]]. The source presents it as a book-length continuation of the [[QizhulouYanBinke|起朱楼宴宾客]] "迈出资产配置第一步" series, especially the worldview-fit close in [[vol-110-touzi-jiushi-dui-shijieguan-de-toupiao-maichu-zichan-peizhi-di-yi-bu-wanjiepian-lk5jkgnzvzerymcixylsdqdtn7f8]].

The book is not described as a cleaned-up transcript. It keeps the podcast series' rough chapter skeleton but adds new examples, metaphors, editorial shaping, review, and reader tools through [[PodcastToBookSystematization]]. The source says the title came from the [[CITICPress|中信出版社]] side, while the author wanted "资产配置" to remain visible even if the term raised the reader-entry threshold.

Its wiki role is to turn [[AssetAllocation]] from a recurring podcast theme into a durable reference object. The source says the book is organized into preparation, psychology, and practical sections so readers at different stages can use it as a first step, a behavior mirror, or an implementation checklist.

## Key Claims
- The book's purpose is to lower the threshold for understanding asset allocation without reducing it to a list of products or a market forecast.
- The source says the book should help readers "understand and want to act," so its value depends on action design as well as explanation.
- The book's core investment position is [[InvestmentWorldviewFit]]: an allocation plan should fit how a person sees risk, uncertainty, life goals, and holdability.
- The project converts a long podcast archive into a physical, revisitable object that can be browsed, reread, and used alongside extension listening or reading.
