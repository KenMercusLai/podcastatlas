# Good Inside

## Overview
Good Inside is the parenting education and membership platform founded by clinical psychologist [[BeckyKennedy]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Good Inside as both a premise and an educational platform. The premise separates identity from behavior: a child or parent can remain fundamentally worthy while behaving badly or struggling with skills. The platform is described as translating that stance into resources, scripts, expert access, and parent community.

Its practical model centers [[ParentingSturdiness]], [[BoundaryEmpathyParenting]], repair after rupture, and building frustration tolerance. This framing rejects the forced choice between harsh control and permissiveness: adults remain responsible for decisions and safety while treating children's feelings as real.

## Key Characteristics
- Organizes parenting around the separation of a person's worth from a difficult behavior.
- Provides actionable language for boundaries, validation, apology, and collaborative problem-solving.
- Frames parents as sturdy leaders responsible for enforcement and safety.
- Treats skill-building and relationship repair as more informative than character judgment.
- Extends parenting tools into adult relationships and self-regulation.
