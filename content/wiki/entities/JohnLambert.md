# John Lambert

## Overview

John Lambert was a senior army figure and principal architect of the [[InstrumentOfGovernment1653|Instrument of Government]], the written framework that established the Protectorate under [[OliverCromwell]].

## Current Profile

The episode presents Lambert at the transition from failed nominated assembly to formal Protectoral government. His constitutional work replaced the traditional king, Lords, and Commons arrangement with a Lord Protector, Council of State, and Parliament.

That design was consequential without being conclusive. It gave the [[EnglishCommonwealth]] a written institutional structure, but army power, representation, constitutional legitimacy, and succession remained unsettled, and the regime could not transfer Cromwell's personal authority securely to [[RichardCromwell]].

## Key Characteristics

- Senior political and military figure in the English republic.
- Associated with drafting the Instrument of Government.
- Helped define the offices of Protector, council, and Parliament.
- Represents the attempt to turn army-backed revolution into written constitutional order.
- Designed within a regime that remained dependent on personal and military authority.

## Qualifications

The source gives only a compressed account of Lambert's career and constitutional role. Calling the Instrument of Government the world's first written constitution is episode-attributed and definition-dependent; the profile does not establish sole authorship, trace every provision, or cover Lambert's later politics.
