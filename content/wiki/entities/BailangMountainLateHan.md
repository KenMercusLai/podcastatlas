## Overview

白狼山 / White Wolf Mountain is the Jian'an 12 / 207 CE campaign setting where [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s expedition unexpectedly encounters [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] before the Cao force is fully equipped for battle.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1079 presents White Wolf Mountain as a tactical crisis that becomes a decisive northern victory. Cao Cao observes that the numerically intimidating Wuhuan formation is poorly ordered and sends [[ZhangLiao|张辽]] forward before the enemy can stabilize. [[TadunLateHan|蹋顿]] is killed, the Wuhuan field collapses, and the surviving Yuan-family refugees flee toward Liaodong.

The place therefore links three stages of the episode's strategy: risk acceptance during an unexpected encounter, conversion of victory into population and cavalry settlement, and the later choice not to pursue [[YuanShang|袁尚]] and [[YuanXiLateHan|袁熙]] into [[GongsunKangLateHan|公孙康]]'s territory.

## Key Characteristics

- Northern battle setting in Cao Cao's 207 CE Wuhuan expedition.
- Surprise encounter before Cao-side baggage and armor are fully available.
- Site where Cao Cao reads formation quality rather than judging by visible numbers alone.
- Setting for Zhang Liao's forward attack and Tadun's death.
- Turning point that sends the surviving Yuan-family branch toward Liaodong.
