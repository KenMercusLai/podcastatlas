# Tony Hawk's Pro Skater

## Overview
Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater appears in the wiki as the video-game franchise through which [[TonyHawk]] helped translate skateboarding movement, tricks, music, and culture to a mass audience.

## Current Profile
The current evidence frames the franchise as an uncertain project that followed failed earlier attempts to interest companies in a skateboarding game. Activision later showed Hawk a prototype built from an existing game engine; he found the movement and controls intuitive, spent roughly a year and a half reviewing builds and providing notes, and chose future royalties over a $500,000 buyout offer. Strong reception led quickly to a sequel and, in Hawk’s telling, roughly ten games.

## Key Characteristics
- Followed earlier rejected efforts to develop a commercially viable skateboarding game.
- Used an existing game-engine prototype as its technical starting point.
- Included sustained feedback from Hawk on movement, controls, and skate authenticity.
- Paired substantial commercial uncertainty with royalty upside.
- Expanded skateboarding’s cultural visibility beyond parks, contests, and specialist media.

## Qualifications
