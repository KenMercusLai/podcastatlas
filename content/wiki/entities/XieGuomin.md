# Xie Guomin / 谢国民

Xie Guomin appears in [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046]] as the CP Group leader connected to early reform-era investment in China. The episode places him in the broader [[Chaoshan]] and [[Thailand]] overseas Chinese business world.

His role is to show that overseas Chinese capital did return quickly after opening. [[CPGroup]]'s early approvals in [[Shenzhen]], [[Zhuhai]], and [[ShantouSpecialEconomicZone]] make the contrast sharper: Shantou did receive diaspora-linked capital, but the source argues that capital alone did not overcome weaker policy capacity, distance from [[HongKong]], and [[RegionalAdministrativeFragmentation]].

[[173-duihua-yao-song-shenjian-dongfang-kongjian-zaichufa-tiancai-shaonian-shinian-hou-1-173-1]] adds Xie as part of [[StridingAI]]'s partner context. [[YaoSong]] recalls Xie repeatedly listening to Chinese technology founders and presents his curiosity and [[CPGroup]] network as useful for future physical-intelligence internationalization.
