# Salesforce

Salesforce is mentioned in [[ai-chongji-qiye-ruanjian-jutou-yu-sap-yuanxin-liao-damoxing-to-b-de-dianfu-yu-bianjie-1-174-1]] as a major enterprise SaaS reference point alongside [[Workday]] and [[SAP]]. The source uses Salesforce to distinguish AI pressure on domain SaaS from the deeper problem of replacing governed enterprise systems that coordinate finance, orders, procurement, suppliers, and audit-critical data.

[[ep-16-data-decoded-navigating-the-ai-revolution]] adds Salesforce as the operating surface for [[CustomerChurnPrediction]] in a B2B SaaS case study. [[VishalDataScienceWithSam|Vishal]] says churn scores and [[ExplainableAIBusinessDecisions|explanations]] were pushed into Salesforce so account executives, sales, marketing, and customer-success teams could see both the likelihood of churn and the reasons behind it.
