# 杨瓒 / Yang Zan (late Han)

杨瓒 / Yang Zan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-962-dongzhuo-yu-lvbu-daodi-weishenme-juelie-lgp3kxfqnhokibf6kpli6p4j8iti|Hanji 962]] as one of the officials named in [[WangYunLateHan|王允]]'s anti-[[DongZhuo|董卓]] planning circle. The episode lists him with [[HuangWanLateHan|黄琬]], [[ShiSunRuiLateHan|士孙瑞]], and others as conspirators who discuss how to remove Dong Zhuo.

The page is intentionally narrow. Yang Zan is not developed beyond the planning circle in this source, but his inclusion helps show that the anti-Dong-Zhuo move is not a solo act by Wang Yun or [[LyuBu|吕布]]; it depends on court officials who can plan, authorize, and later convert Lü Bu's access into political violence.
