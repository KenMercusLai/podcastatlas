# Lee Lockwood

## Overview
Lee Lockwood is the University of Virginia economist quoted by Marketplace Tech on AI, labor income, tax revenue, and the need for advance fiscal planning.

## Current Profile
Lockwood's source role is to translate AI labor displacement into a tax-system problem. He warns that fewer taxable paychecks can reduce income-tax revenue and consumer spending, creating knock-on macroeconomic pressure. His Brookings-linked argument is precautionary: uncertainty over AI's timing and scale is not a reason for governments to wait until revenue loss is visible.

## Key Characteristics
- Lockwood frames AI disruption as a public-finance issue, not only a labor-market issue.
- He connects income-tax loss to reduced consumer spending and possible recessionary pressure.
- His policy stance is planning under uncertainty rather than claiming a precise AI job-loss forecast.
- His affiliation connects the episode to university and think-tank public-policy analysis.
