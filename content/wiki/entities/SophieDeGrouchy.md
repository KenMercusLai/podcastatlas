# Sophie de Grouchy

## Overview

Sophie de Grouchy was a salon organizer and political intellectual connected by the episode to Condorcet's support for women's suffrage.

## Current Profile

The episode distinguishes de Grouchy's salon from [[MadameRoland|Madame Roland]]'s by emphasizing women's inclusion. Her role supplies a social setting in which women could participate in political and intellectual discussion even while the Republic denied them formal citizenship.

## Key Characteristics

- She organized a politically significant salon.
- Her salon included women as participants.
- She is linked to Condorcet's support for female suffrage.
- She represents a more publicly inclusive form of salon politics than Roland's domestic-influence ideal.
