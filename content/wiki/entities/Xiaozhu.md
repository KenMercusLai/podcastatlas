# 小猪 / Xiaozhu

## Overview
小猪 appears in [[vol-272-2026-nian-le-zenme-hai-you-ren-yao-zuo-zhongguo-ban-airbnb-1008880863]] as another early Chinese platform that tried to learn from [[Airbnb]] but faced pressure away from pure peer-to-peer spare-room hosting.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Xiaozhu as a supply-side caution. When ordinary homeowners lack enough incentive to host strangers, a platform may drift toward managed supply, self-operation, or more professionalized host structures even if the original story resembles home sharing.

## Key Characteristics
- Early Chinese Airbnb-style homestay reference point in the episode.
- Illustrates the difficulty of sustaining ordinary homeowner supply during a rising property and income-expectation period.
- Functions as a contrast to [[YijianCiwo|一间次卧]]'s claim that the supply motivation may now be different.

## Evidence
- Historical contrast: [[vol-272-2026-nian-le-zenme-hai-you-ren-yao-zuo-zhongguo-ban-airbnb-1008880863]] groups Xiaozhu with early Chinese Airbnb-learning platforms that later had to adjust their business direction.
- Supply pressure: [[vol-272-2026-nian-le-zenme-hai-you-ren-yao-zuo-zhongguo-ban-airbnb-1008880863]] says the earlier China market lacked enough ordinary homeowner motivation because expected property gains and income growth made small monthly hosting income less compelling.
