# Nature's One

Nature's One is the infant-formula company and Ohio manufacturing facility acquired by [[Bobbie]] in [[bobbie-laura-modi-how-a-baby-formula-startup-took-market-share-from-two-industry-giants-4488842b-74d3-40ed-8776-5831c5934e69]]. The episode says [[LauraModi]] pursued the acquisition after the 2022 shortage and later growth made dependence on one outside manufacturer feel too fragile.

The source uses Nature's One to show that buying a plant does not instantly solve a regulated supply problem. Bobbie still needed FDA and formulation approvals before producing there, continued relying on its contract manufacturer for about two more years, and went out of stock again in 2024 for nearly seven months.
