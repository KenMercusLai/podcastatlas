# 安阳 / Anyang (Qin Rebellion)

安阳 / Anyang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-133-songyi-zhan-xiangyu-weihe-bei-fansha-lms7noyrm5omedv77gqfhjeu53zx|Qinji 133]] as the stopping point where [[SongYi|宋义]] halts the Chu rescue army on the way to [[Julu|巨鹿]]. The episode says he remains there for forty-six days while [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] and [[WangLiQin|王离]] continue pressure around restored [[ZhaoState|赵国]].

The place matters because it turns strategy into command crisis. Song Yi presents the pause as waiting for [[QinState|秦国]] and Zhao to exhaust each other, but [[XiangYu|项羽]] argues that delay risks letting Qin absorb Zhao's troops and provisions. In the source's reading, Anyang becomes the camp where disagreement over timing hardens into personal hostility.

The conditions at Anyang also weaken Song Yi's authority. Cold rain, mud, supply shortage, hunger, and lack of fuel make the soldiers' hardship visible, while Song Yi's later trip to [[WuyanCounty|无盐县]] makes him look insulated from the army he commands.
