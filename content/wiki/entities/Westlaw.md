# Westlaw

## Overview
Westlaw appears in the All-In legal AI discussion as one of the legacy legal-research incumbents in the U.S. market.

## Current Profile
The source uses Westlaw together with [[LexisNexis]] to mark the legal-data foundation that AI legal systems must confront. The episode's key point is not a standalone Westlaw product review, but the idea that complete and current legal research data remains crucial even when AI agents begin combining cases, witness statements, contracts, and strategy material.
