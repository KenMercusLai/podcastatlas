# Stephen Maddis

## Overview
Stephen Maddis is presented in [[ep-18-insurance-transformed-an-actuarys-take-on-ai]] as an actuary discussing AI implementation and the future of insurance; he explicitly limits the interview to his own views rather than those of an employer or professional body.

## Current Profile
His source-scoped position is that AI will change how insurers classify risk, validate assumptions, administer products, and serve customers more than it changes insurance's purpose. He advocates [[ActuarialWorkflowInversion]] so automation releases actuaries from preparatory data work while people retain creativity, interpretation, and responsibility for trade-offs.

## Key Characteristics
- Grounds insurance in pooled protection against rare, severe financial losses.
- Treats AI as an operational and analytical accelerator rather than a replacement for actuarial mathematics.
- Frames fairness as a governance problem spanning insurers, actuaries, customers, and regulators.
- Distinguishes long-duration trust relationships from commoditized short-term coverage.
- Encourages cross-domain learning as preparation for professional change.
