# 田丰 / Tian Feng (late Han)

## Overview
田丰 / Tian Feng (late Han) is a [[YuanShao|袁绍]] adviser whose wiki profile centers on high-quality warnings that Yuan Shao does not reliably convert into action and ultimately recodes as intolerable humiliation after defeat.

## Current Profile
Tian Feng is a diagnostic adviser rather than a full biographical subject in the current bounded evidence. Hanji 975 shows him identifying [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]]'s grain request as practical support for [[LyuBu|吕布]] during the [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] rebellion. Hanji 976 places him among Yuan Shao's underused Hebei advisers with [[JuShou|沮授]] and [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]]. Hanji 1023 and Hanji 1041 make the missed [[Xudu|许都]] attack opportunity a repeated pattern: Tian Feng sees that control of [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] and Cao Cao's absence can change the field, but [[YuanShao|袁绍]] does not convert the warning into action.

Hanji 1042 turns that pattern into direct punishment. After [[LiuBei|刘备]] reaches Yuan Shao and Cao Cao returns to [[GuanduCampaign|官渡]], Tian Feng advises Yuan Shao to avoid immediate decisive battle, hold defensible terrain, cultivate people and agriculture, keep building arms, connect with outside heroes, and send elite cavalry to harass Cao Cao south of the Yellow River. The episode treats the plan as practical [[FortifiedAttritionPatience|fortified attrition patience]], but Tian Feng presses it in a hard, direct way just as Yuan Shao wants mobilization, so Yuan reads the warning as morale damage and imprisons him.

Hanji 1050 closes the consequence. After Yuan Shao loses at Guandu, Tian Feng predicts that defeat makes him less, not more, safe: Yuan's shame and suspicion will make correct prior advice intolerable. [[FengJiLateHan|逢纪]] then says Tian Feng rejoiced over Yuan's defeat, and Yuan orders Tian Feng killed. Cao Cao's reported comment that Yuan would have been difficult to beat had he followed Tian Feng gives external validation to Tian's strategic value while reinforcing the Yuan-camp failure to preserve usable correction.

## Key Characteristics
- Reads political meaning behind resource, alignment, and court-control moves rather than treating them as isolated requests.
- Supplies urgent strategic warnings that require fast ruler-side action to matter.
- Advocates concrete attritional strategy when Yuan Shao's larger force would be better used through defense, agriculture, and harassment than through a single battle.
- Functions as a mirror to [[XunYu|荀彧]] because both detect hidden danger, but only Cao Cao's side reliably converts such detection.
- Belongs to Yuan Shao's Hebei adviser group, making Yuan's failure a decision-conversion problem rather than a talent-supply problem.
- Exposes the danger of blunt remonstrance under a proud ruler: correct advice can be recoded as disloyal speech when it wounds authority before battle.
- Becomes the post-defeat casualty of the same system: after being proved right, he is more vulnerable to shame, slander, and punitive face-saving.

## Qualifications
The sources do not provide a full biography of Tian Feng. They use him as an adviser-case inside Yuan Shao's decision system, so the page should not infer the full range of his later career from these episodes alone. Hanji 1023, Hanji 1041, and Hanji 1042 show that Tian Feng's advice could have pressured Cao Cao, but they do not prove that Yuan Shao would certainly have captured Xudu, defeated Cao Cao, or won the Guandu contest if he had acted. Hanji 1050's report of Feng Ji's slander explains the immediate narrative mechanism for Tian's death, but the page keeps Yuan Shao's underlying shame-and-suspicion profile source-bounded rather than treating it as complete psychology.

## What Changed
- Extended Tian Feng's arc from imprisonment before Guandu to execution after Yuan Shao's defeat.
- Added Tian Feng's own prediction that being proved right would make him more dangerous under an ashamed and suspicious ruler.
- Added Cao Cao's external validation that Tian Feng's advice would have made Yuan Shao harder to beat.
- Reframed the page's current judgment from repeated ignored warning to warning that becomes punishable before and after the campaign.
