# 田丰 / Tian Feng (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-975-yanzhou-zhiluan-xunyu-ruhe-bang-caocao-shou-dabenying-ln0hh4jrokkwtqsmxmqhdjzcosdr|Hanji 975]] adds Tian Feng's first active adviser case before Hanji 976's broader Hebei roster summary. The episode says [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] asks [[YuanShao|袁绍]] for grain during the [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] rebellion, and Tian Feng identifies the request as practical support for [[LyuBu|吕布]].

This makes Tian Feng a mirror to [[XunYu|荀彧]] in the same episode. Xun Yu detects the rebellion from wording and route clues, while Tian Feng detects the grain request's true alignment effect; both cases make adviser intelligence operational only if the leader acts on it.

田丰 / Tian Feng (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] as one of [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s underused Hebei advisers. The source names him alongside [[JuShou|沮授]] and [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]] to argue that Yuan Shao did not lose because he lacked strategic talent; he lost because he could not consistently convert that talent into decisions.

The page is disambiguated from [[TianFeng|田峰]], the modern sociology researcher already present in the wiki. Hanji 976 does not provide a full biography of 田丰; it uses him as a high-value adviser wasted by a ruler-side "decision black hole," linking the case to [[TalentAsStateTreasure|以贤臣为宝]] and [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]].
