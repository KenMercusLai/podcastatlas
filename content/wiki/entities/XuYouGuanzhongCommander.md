## Overview

[[XuYouGuanzhongCommander|许攸]] is a Guanzhong camp commander in Hanji 1167 whose refusal and contemptuous language provoke [[CaoCao|曹操]] toward a punitive campaign before [[DuXiLateHan|杜袭]] redirects the response.

## Current Profile

This Xu You is defined by a single command dispute. The episode says he keeps his troops outside Cao Cao's effective control and speaks dismissively of Cao's order. Cao Cao initially prepares to attack, but Du Xi reframes the insult as strategically beneath the ruler's attention. Rank and gifts then replace military punishment, and the episode reports restored submission.

## Key Characteristics

- Guanzhong military commander with an armed following.
- Subordinate who resists incorporation and challenges superior authority through contemptuous speech.
- Trigger for an anger-driven campaign proposal rather than a demonstrated major strategic threat.
- Recipient of a face-saving accommodation that reportedly restores obedience.

## Evidence

- Insubordination and status injury: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1167-budong-shenshi-duoshi-zhihui-ziqu-miewang-llr4vejetnx3dxjngwauadp-oznb|Hanji 1167]] says Xu You rejects Cao Cao's order and provokes an angry decision to campaign.
- Alternative settlement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1167-budong-shenshi-duoshi-zhihui-ziqu-miewang-llr4vejetnx3dxjngwauadp-oznb|Hanji 1167]] says Cao Cao sends gifts and rank after Du Xi's counsel and that Xu You submits.

## Qualifications

This profile rests on one structured summary. The commander's exact title, force, words, degree of autonomy, terms of settlement, and emotional response remain source-scoped. He is not merged with [[XuYouLateHan|许攸, Yuan Shao's adviser and Guandu defector]]: that better-known figure's established chronology ends before this 219-era command dispute.

## What Changed

- Established a separate identity for the Guanzhong commander named Xu You.
- Prevented a chronology and role collision with the famous Guandu adviser.

## Relationships

- [[CaoCao|曹操]] - superior whose order Xu You resists and whose accommodation reportedly restores submission.
- [[DuXiLateHan|杜袭]] - adviser who reframes the insult and prevents immediate punitive force.
- [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]] - distinct Yuan Shao adviser and Guandu defector with the same written name.
- [[AngerDrivenWarDecision|因怒兴师]] - concept illustrated by the proposed campaign and its interruption.
