# 余文杰 / Yu Wenjie

## Overview
Yu Wenjie / 余文杰 is the [[BabbittCoffee|巴比特咖啡]] practitioner interviewed in [[kafei-shijian-genzhe-xundoushi-zhaodao-neng-daibiao-zhongguo-yunnan-de-kafeidou-gkwridooy4eqafaa-wtmhv8e]] about Yunnan coffee.

## Current Profile
The source presents him as a coffee operator turned sourcing and product person. His authority in the episode comes from repeated origin visits, a long stay in [[Mangshi|芒市]], direct contact with farmers and small lots, and routine work in roasting, cupping, sample comparison, purchasing, and trade judgment.

## Key Characteristics
- Works close to the upstream coffee chain: samples, roasting, cupping, sourcing, purchasing, and direct-trade assessment.
- Uses blind cupping to counter inherited origin labels and reduce expectation bias.
- Treats [[Yunnan|云南]] coffee as an origin with its own terroir, varieties, limits, and commercial route.
- Gives strategic weight to [[SarchimorYunnanCoffee|Sarchimor]] because it can connect local flavor, yield, disease resistance, and farmer income.
- Keeps quality claims bounded when he lacks direct cup evidence, such as some high-altitude plots he has heard of but not tasted.
