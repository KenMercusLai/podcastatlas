# Adam Kelly

## Overview

Adam Kelly is a researcher represented in the source through work on relative-age bias in English basketball and its implications for talent identification and educational assessment.

## Current Profile

In [[outliers-revisited-outliers-revisited|Outliers, Revisited]], Kelly reports that players born in the quarter nearest the favorable side of the eligibility cutoff were ten times more likely to enter regional basketball talent centers. He uses the disparity to question systems that treat same-cohort children as developmentally identical.

Kelly also extends the question to education: students sitting an examination on the same date can be at different developmental points. The episode's birth-month testing proposal builds on that observation, but it does not establish that Kelly designed or validated a specific academic correction algorithm.

## Key Characteristics

- Studies relative-age effects in youth-sport selection.
- Treats talent detection as a developmental and institutional problem, not only a measure of present performance.
- Raises an educational analogy while leaving the design of academic correction unresolved.
