# 郑渊洁

郑渊洁 is the central childhood-author figure in [[177-xiaoshihou-de-shu-zenme-jiu-name-haokan-971996036]] and the direct focus of [[23-feima-pai-qiche-anquan-shi-di-yi-wei-de-572699943]]. [[QinZong|秦总]] and [[Beimin|北明]] present him as important not only because his stories were imaginative, but because they carried anti-school, anti-authoritarian, socially observant, and child-respecting energy.

The earlier episode uses works and figures such as [[Pipilu]], [[Luxixi]], [[ShukeBeta|Shuke and Beta]], [[FeimaPaiQiche|《飞马牌汽车》]], 《罐头小人》, and [[TonghuaDawang|《童话大王》]] columns to show how children's literature could expose readers to debate, copyright claims, adult social rules, independent thought, and the possibility that a good child might resist authority to protect something vulnerable.

[[23-feima-pai-qiche-anquan-shi-di-yi-wei-de-572699943]] narrows that general childhood-memory branch into a close case. [[XunTuJi|《训兔记》]] makes obedience training frightening, while [[FeimaPaiQiche|《飞马牌汽车》]] turns a stuck bus into a full satire of [[SafetyAsControl]], [[RuleIdolatry]], [[BureaucraticAbsurdityEscalation]], and [[ProblemIndustrialization]].

## Key Claims
- Zheng's value in the source is not just fantasy invention; it is the refusal to treat children as readers who need only safety, obedience, and simplified morals.
- The episode treats his stories as a bridge between [[AntiAuthoritarianEducation]] and [[ChildrensLiteratureComplexity]].
- QinZong's memory of Luxixi in 《罐头小人》 makes "乖孩子" compatible with courage, secrecy, care, and justified refusal.
- Zheng's public copyright disputes and self-narration are remembered as an early source of intellectual-property awareness for young readers.
- Episode 23 makes the institutional-satire side of Zheng more explicit: his children's fiction can criticize rule worship, safety rhetoric, formalism, and adaptation to absurd systems without leaving the children's-literature field.
