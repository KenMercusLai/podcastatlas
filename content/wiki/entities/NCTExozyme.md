# NCT (Exozyme)

## Overview
NCT is the abbreviated name used in one episode for an [[Exozyme]] natural-molecule program intended first for a dietary-supplement route; the supplied file does not expand or independently identify the abbreviation.

## Current Profile
The source presents NCT as a molecule found at very low concentration in black pepper and as an early product intended to demonstrate Exozyme's manufacturing platform. It attributes metabolic potential to the molecule and discusses possible future weight-maintenance, [[GLP1Agonists|GLP-1 combination]], and fatty-liver research, while explicitly distinguishing those ideas from established treatment claims.

## Key Characteristics
- Is presented as naturally occurring rather than as a wholly novel molecule.
- Is selected partly because direct extraction is described as impractical at the source-reported concentration.
- Serves as a proposed product proof for [[CellFreeEnzymeEngineering]].
- Is being discussed first as a supplement rather than as an approved medicine.
- Has source-described therapeutic research directions that would require stronger evidence and a different regulatory route.
