# Habiba Fayed

Habiba Fayed appears in [[the-elon-game-musks-vision-of-the-future-6a633594d19896314260e5c4]] as [[MonaKhalil]]'s collaborator in restoring the family beach house on Mansouri Beach in southern [[Lebanon]]. The source says Khalil and Fayed turned the site into the [[OrangeHouseLebanon|Orange House]], a B&B and conservation center for training, education, volunteer work, and turtle-nesting protection.

In the wiki, Fayed anchors the shared-labor side of the Mona Khalil story. The episode centers Khalil's life, but the conservation hub is presented as something built and sustained with Fayed rather than as a purely individual project.
