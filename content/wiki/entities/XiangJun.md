# 湘君 / Xiangjun

湘君 / Xiangjun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-122-3-qinshihuang-jing-he-shenxian-si-qilai-le-ljnw5estg6dvgfllm3kfcq6ulhxu]] as the local sacred figure attached to [[XiangshanShrine|湘山祠]]. When [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] encounters strong wind while trying to cross the river near the shrine, court scholars explain Xiangjun as a daughter of Yao and wife of Shun who is buried in the area.

The source treats Xiangjun less as a biography to settle than as a local sacred authority in a conquered Chu landscape. The storm is read as obstruction by a place-based god, and Qin Shi Huang's anger turns that sacred interpretation into [[ImperialLocalGodConflict|皇权与地方神冲突]].
