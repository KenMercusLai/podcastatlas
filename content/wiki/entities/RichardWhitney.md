# Richard Whitney / 理查德·惠特尼

Richard Whitney is one of the three 1929 role portraits in [[170-1929-de-paomo-zhixia-sange-daibiao-renwu-he-tamen-zai-dangxia-zhouqi-de-yingzi-lo7uv7umozr0jrqfjzyhdbzicx1j]]. The episode presents him as a senior [[NewYorkStockExchange|New York Stock Exchange]] figure who acted as a market-stabilizing "white knight" on Black Thursday by buying blue-chip stocks on behalf of Wall Street bankers before later being exposed for misuse of funds and imprisoned.

The source uses Whitney as a gatekeeper warning. Before the [[SEC]] existed, the exchange itself carried quasi-regulatory trust, so Whitney's fall mattered because he represented market order and elite credibility. In [[BubbleRoleAnalogy]], he becomes the type of figure who looks reassuring during the boom but can only be fully understood after a downturn and audit expose leverage, conflicts, or concealed losses.
