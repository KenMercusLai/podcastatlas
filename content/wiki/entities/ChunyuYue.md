# 淳于越 / Chunyu Yue

淳于越 / Chunyu Yue enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-124-fenshu-kengru-wo-zai-bzhan-wei-qinshihuang-pingfan-ljnhwcouqu35rmvb-cnrzyzj2j6l]], where he appears as a Qi man and Qin court博士 who speaks at [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]'s banquet. His central claim is that Qin's direct [[CommanderyCountyAntiEnfeoffment|commandery-county settlement]] is wrong and that the empire should follow Xia, Shang, and Zhou by restoring enfeoffment.

The source uses him less as a full biography than as the trigger for the book-burning debate. In the host's summary, Chunyu Yue's appeal to antiquity gives [[LiSi|李斯]] the opening to treat private classical argument as a threat to present rule: if scholars use old models to attack current institutions, intellectual disagreement becomes political disorder.
