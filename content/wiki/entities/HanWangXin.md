# 韩王信 / Han Wang Xin

韩王信 / Han Wang Xin enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-149-xichu-bawang-xiangyu-weihe-shijun-2-lpohbb3prsgvokpjipfd7i1ntiwn|Hanji 149 part 2]] as an old [[HanState|韩国]] royal descendant used by [[LiuBang|刘邦]] to restore a Han title under Liu Bang's authority. The source explicitly distinguishes him from the general [[HanXin|韩信]].

The episode says Han Wang Xin had followed Liu Bang and [[ZhangLiang|张良]] into [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] and then into Hanzhong. After Liu Bang defeats the Three Qin, he appoints Han Wang Xin as Han taiwei and promises to make him Han king.

That promise becomes useful after [[ZhengChangHanKing|郑昌]], the Han king installed by [[XiangYu|项羽]], surrenders. Liu Bang then makes Han Wang Xin king of Han and places [[YingchuanCommandery|颍川郡]] under his authority, turning old Han legitimacy into a tool for absorbing the central-plains territory around [[HenanCommanderyChuHan|河南郡]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-152-xiangyu-3wanren-ruhe-wansheng-liubang-56wan-dajun-2-lq9ecpjb4r-ij0-e-iu-ezfavowj|Hanji 152 part 2]] shows Han Wang Xin staying with Liu Bang after the [[PengchengBattle|彭城之战]] rather than joining the post-defeat defections. The source groups him with [[ZhangEr|张耳]] as one of the remaining Liu-side lords who withdraws toward [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]], making his loyalty a contrast case inside [[PowerBasedAllianceRepricing|实力重估式阵营转向]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-162-1-ta-ti-liubang-qu-si-rujin-chengle-zhengzhou-de-shouhushen-li3su2zlmbm1ift9nibujgtcrxwm|Hanji 162-1]] places Han Wang Xin inside the emergency garrison list after [[JiXin|纪信]]'s substitution lets [[LiuBang|刘邦]] escape [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]]. He is named with [[ZhouKe|周苛]], [[WeiBao|魏豹]], and [[CongGong|枞公]] among those left behind to hold the city during the [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-169-pandian-liubang-dengjiqian-buweirenzhi-de-gushi-1-liwkaygjtyafxxpdwzq9pdhuzroi|Hanji 169]] moves Han Wang Xin from loyal regional actor into the accession-petition group. He is named with [[HanXin|韩信]], [[YingBu|英布]], [[WuRui|吴芮]], [[ZhangAo|张敖]], and [[ZangTu|臧荼]] among the kings urging Liu Bang to accept the imperial title, making old Han legitimacy part of the public recognition of [[WesternHanDynasty|西汉]]'s founding.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-174-liubang-weihe-dafeng-zijide-chouren-1-lnzzvpieqtgatmvgogz3msyhs60u|Hanji 174]] changes Han Wang Xin's territorial role. Liu Bang moves him away from the old Han interior and gives him the Taiyuan / Han kingdom, initially around [[Jinyang|晋阳]]; because Xiongnu attacks make Jinyang too distant from the frontier, Han Wang Xin asks to shift the capital to [[Mayi|马邑]], and Liu Bang approves. The source frames the move as frontier defense while the wiki also records its containment effect: a capable non-Liu king is placed on the edge rather than in the central-plains heartland.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-175-bikan-liubang-de-huangjia-liyi-ke-los9hsomndnhyqpxafstgyquomzf|Hanji 175]] shows that edge placement becoming a crisis. When [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] forces surround Mayi, Han Wang Xin sends envoys to negotiate while also asking Western Han for rescue. [[LiuBang|刘邦]] hears that he is repeatedly communicating with Xiongnu and sends a rebuke, which the source presents as enough to make Han Wang Xin fear punishment. He then surrenders Mayi in September, and [[MaoDunChanyu|冒顿单于]] uses the opening to push past Yanmen toward Taiyuan and Jinyang.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-177-beikun-baidengshan-liubang-mingxuan-yixian-ljdmxf6bej8ju0gmvm9cp48gy0on|Hanji 177]] follows the consequences of that surrender. Han Wang Xin is defeated by Liu Bang at 铜鞮 in [[Shangdang|上党]], flees into the Xiongnu camp, and remains part of the anti-Han pressure field when 曼丘臣 and 王黄 gather escaped Han troops and elevate 赵利. The source therefore shifts him from a trapped frontier king into an active breach point between Xiongnu force and early Han different-surname king mistrust.

The episode therefore turns Han Wang Xin from a frontier-containment example into a failure case. A ruler placed at the edge can defend the border only if the center can distinguish tactical negotiation under siege from betrayal; suspicion makes the external threat and internal trust problem reinforce each other.
