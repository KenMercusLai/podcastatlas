# Cassandra (mythology)

## Overview
Cassandra is the Trojan prophet of Greek mythology whom the [[HiddenBrain]] episode uses as an archetype of the accurate but unsuccessful warning.

## Current Profile
[[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-ce411815c1cca7882f5b93d662620544-51879597-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-ce411815c1cca7882f5b93d662620544-51879597]] looks beyond Cassandra's curse to the practical structure of her failed warnings. She speaks cryptically, lacks recognized authority, sees consequences several steps beyond her audience, and asks listeners to accept realities that threaten identity and status. Her warnings about the Trojan horse and the deaths awaiting Agamemnon and herself therefore become cases for [[WarningReceptionFramework]], not proof that evidence alone determines reception.

## Key Characteristics
- Foresees catastrophe but cannot secure belief or action.
- Communicates through riddles that listeners struggle to follow.
- Lacks the formal authority and in-group credibility of an institutional insider.
- Warns across causal and temporal distances that others cannot yet imagine.
- Delivers messages whose acceptance would overturn a listener's self-conception.

## Qualifications
- This page records the episode's interpretive use of mythology, not a comparative account of ancient literary versions.
- A listener's disbelief can be rational when a warning is unsupported or opaque; Cassandra's supernatural accuracy should not be generalized to real-world claimants.
