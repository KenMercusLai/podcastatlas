# Chery / 奇瑞

Chery / 奇瑞 is the [[Wuhu]] automotive company discussed in [[no-211-huishang-wangshi-cong-qianshi-buxiu-dao-zuiniu-fengtou-1002275189]]. The episode treats Chery as an early Anhui industrial-upgrading case before the later [[Hefei]] investment stories around display panels, memory chips, and new-energy vehicles.

The source emphasizes improvised execution under constraint. The project initially used an auto-parts company name because it lacked car-production qualifications, bought a secondhand Ford engine line, recruited Yin Tongyue from FAW, installed the production line itself after delays, and used local taxi demand before gaining formal production-list access through SAIC.

Chery's low-price Fengyun launch and early export record make it a bridge between local-government industrial ambition and a real manufacturing firm. In the episode, it helps explain why modern [[Anhui]] industrial identity is not only about [[Hefei]]'s capital allocation, but also about earlier carmaking capability in Wuhu.
