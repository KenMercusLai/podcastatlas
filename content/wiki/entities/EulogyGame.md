# Eulogy

Eulogy is [[SeanBarassa]]'s party game in [[advice-line-with-carlton-calvin-of-razor-c0de1a84-3d35-45f3-921c-6144693ad86e]]. Players create a character's life story and then deliver a eulogy, giving the game an unusual premise that [[CarltonCalvin]] and [[GuyRaz]] see as potentially media-friendly if live play produces shareable reactions.

The source says Eulogy began during COVID, was funded on [[Kickstarter]] in 2024, arrived as product in 2025, and had sold just under 1,000 copies by the call. The Advice Line diagnosis is that the concept may be distinctive, but [[ToyInventorLicensingPath]] still requires stronger [[CustomerPull]] through game nights, conventions, small stores, and retail or publisher-visible proof.
