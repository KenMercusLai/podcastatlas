# 严白虎 / Yan Baihu

严白虎 / Yan Baihu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-996-luanshi-zanghong-jiangyiqi-de-ren-xiachang-zui-biequ-lgpa-m6h6bvhyintnuj6zztopui76|Hanji 996]] as a local armed strongman in the Wu region. After [[ZhuZhiLateHan|朱治]] drives out the Wu commandery administrator, the displaced official turns toward Yan Baihu for protection, showing how disorder pushes formal officeholders toward non-state force.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1004-caocao-yingfeng-hanxiandi-shi-shui-de-zhuyi-lrrga-flmx5ajnhu1c42x7kooe94|Hanji 1004]] also names Yan Baihu as a lower-priority Jiangdong threat when [[SunCe|孙策]] chooses to strike [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]] rather than clear every local strongman first. Across the two source notes, Yan Baihu functions less as a narrated protagonist than as a sign of fragmented local power.
