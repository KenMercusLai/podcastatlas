# 严白虎 / Yan Baihu

## Overview
严白虎 / Yan Baihu is a Wu-region local strongman whose page tracks how non-state armed power remains available as refuge, threat, and defeated remnant during Sun Ce's Jiangdong consolidation.

## Current Profile
The evidence makes Yan Baihu a structural marker more than a developed biography. Earlier he shelters displaced formal authority; later Sun Ce treats him as a secondary threat before eventually defeating him, after which Yan Baihu flees to Yuhang and joins [[XuZhaoLateHan|徐昭]].

## Key Characteristics
- Represents Wu-region local armed power outside regular commandery office.
- Serves as refuge for displaced officeholders or defeated actors.
- Is initially lower priority than Kuaiji in Sun Ce's campaign sequencing.
- Is later defeated by Sun Ce and retreats into another local protection network.
