# 邯郸商 / Handan Shang (late Han)

邯郸商 / Handan Shang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-979-zaoyu-tongliao-beici-ta-ruhe-wei-caocao-liwan-kuanglan-luu58a2ww11g-radh-5g4l7fcis|Hanji 979]] as the Chenliu official appointed inspector of the newly created [[YongzhouLateHan|雍州]]. The page uses the `HandanShangLateHan` key to avoid collision with [[Handan|邯郸]] as a place.

In the source, his appointment is less a biography than an administrative response to distance and road disorder. The [[HexiFourCommanderiesLateHan|河西四郡]] can no longer be governed effectively through Liangzhou's existing office channel, so the court creates a separate provincial structure and names Handan Shang to it.
