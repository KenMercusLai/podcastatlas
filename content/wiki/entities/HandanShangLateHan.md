# 邯郸商 / Handan Shang (late Han)

## Overview
邯郸商 / Handan Shang is a Chenliu official appointed inspector of the newly created [[YongzhouLateHan|雍州]], later killed by [[ZhangMengLateHan|张猛]] and publicly mourned by [[PangYuLateHan|庞淯]].

## Current Profile
Hanji 979 introduces Handan Shang through administrative reorganization. The [[HexiFourCommanderiesLateHan|河西四郡]] are too distant and road-disrupted for effective governance through Liangzhou, so the court creates a separate provincial structure and appoints him inspector.

Hanji 1076 provides the violent endpoint without reconstructing the intervening career. Zhang Meng kills Handan Shang and threatens anyone who mourns him. Pang Yu resigns, travels to Wuwei, mourns openly, and prepares revenge; Zhang Meng's eventual permission for the funeral shows that loyalty to Handan Shang remains publicly powerful after his death.

Hanji 1075 offers a dramatic friendship-to-murder prequel: it makes Handan Shang and Zhang Meng Taixue friends, has Handan Shang recommend Zhang Meng for Wuwei, and turns grain, messaging, and military control into their path to conflict. Because the episode's 204 CE killing date conflicts with Hanji 1076's 206 CE frame and its detailed scenes lack checkable citations, this material changes the profile only as an account of how the podcast interprets the relationship, not as settled biography.

## Key Characteristics
- Chenliu official selected for the newly created Yongzhou inspectorate.
- Appointment responds to distance and road disorder across the Hexi commanderies.
- Later killed by Zhang Meng in the episode's Wuwei branch.
- Inspires Pang Yu to resign, mourn publicly under threat, and seek revenge.
- Becomes the subject of a source-scoped intimacy-and-power narrative in Hanji 1075, not a verified Taixue-friend biography.
- Uses the `HandanShangLateHan` key to avoid collision with [[Handan|邯郸]] as a place.

## Qualifications
The three notes leave the intervening chronology, precise relationship with Zhang Meng, circumstances of the killing, and Handan Shang's wider career unresolved. Hanji 1075's Taixue friendship, family intimacy, bureaucratic retaliation, bridge ambush, and 204 CE date are source-scoped dramatic claims; its date conflicts with Hanji 1076's Jian'an 11 / 206 CE frame. Hanji 1076's description of Zhang Meng killing a former patron or “elder brother” likewise remains source framing rather than settled biography.
