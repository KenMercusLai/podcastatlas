# Zydor Church

Zydor Church is the Oakland psychedelic church at the center of [[a-trip-to-the-magic-mushroom-megachurch]]. [[DaveHodges]] presents it as a religious institution where [[PsilocybinMushrooms]] and [[DMT]] are sacrament, while the episode shows why its size, security, cash donations, and broad product range make it look partly like a large gray-market retail operation.

The source says Zydor has had more than 135,000 people physically come through and that more than 300 members may enter the sacrament room on busy weekend days. That scale makes Zydor a test case for [[PsychedelicChurches]], [[ReligiousControlledSubstanceExemption]], and the limits of [[LocalPsychedelicDecriminalization]] in [[OaklandCalifornia]].
