# 小果哥哥 / XiaoGuoGege

## Overview
XiaoGuoGege is an independent AI comic-drama creator whose [[AnhuiXiaoMujiang|《安徽小木匠》]] breakout and six-month follow-up provide a source-scoped case of solo production inside the [[FanqieNovel|番茄小说]]–[[Hongguo|红果]]–[[Douyin|抖音]] ecosystem.

## Current Profile
The first source presents a creator learning AI production while already carrying years of video editing, public-account, and internet-content experience. He combined [[Doubao|豆包]] script work, generated video, manual editing, platform submission, and distributor amplification to make 《安徽小木匠》 at very low cash cost.

Six months later he reports ten completed solo works, more than 1.6 billion aggregate plays, under RMB 150,000 total spending, and seven-figure income. That result did not remove volatility: 《安徽小木匠》 remained his strongest work, several projects failed review, and peers described in the episode lost money or needed dozens of attempts before a breakout. His current operating style favors small initial tests, personal story interest, cross-genre experimentation, detailed review, and continued solo control rather than rapid team expansion.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines long-standing content intuition and editing experience with newly learned AI tools.
- Runs a solo end-to-end workflow spanning story selection, rights, adaptation, generation, editing, review, and quality control.
- Scales spending only after small tests demonstrate ability and some return.
- Treats AI output as material to inspect and correct rather than as an autonomous whole-book production system.
- Experiments across historical, regional, supernatural, and other genres instead of copying one current hit.
- Works at unusually high intensity, with source-reported overnight schedules from roughly 8 p.m. to 10 a.m.

## Qualifications
Costs, plays, income, payout rates, work schedules, and peer outcomes are participant-reported rather than audited. This is an exceptional case with prior internet, filming, and editing experience, not a representative beginner outcome. Platform acceptance, distribution, rights access, quality grading, timing, and luck remain outside the creator's full control.
