## Overview

白盈 / Bai Ying is an associate of [[LiuShuangHengshanCrownPrince|衡山太子刘爽]] in Hanji 305-2. He first proposes the failed plan to compromise [[XuLaiHengshanQueen|徐来]] sexually and later carries Liu Shuang's rebellion accusation to the imperial court after the crown prince is confined.

## Current Profile

Bai Ying functions as both reckless strategist and essential intermediary. His seduction advice treats access to Xu Lai as a substitute for institutional support and worsens Liu Shuang's crisis when she refuses and reports the advance. Yet Liu Shuang's detention later makes Bai Ying the channel through which the alleged weapons preparations reach central authority.

## Key Characteristics

- Personal adviser operating outside formal succession procedure.
- Author of a high-risk sexual-compromise plan that backfires.
- Trusted intermediary able to act after Liu Shuang is confined.
- Carrier of an accusation that helps move a household struggle into imperial jurisdiction.
