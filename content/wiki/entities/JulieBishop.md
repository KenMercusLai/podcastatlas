# Julie Bishop

Julie Bishop appears in [[the-case-of-the-missing-totem-aung-san-suu-kyi-6a560fc4f96471dde4821d2f]] as another outside figure who raised [[AungSanSuuKyi]]'s welfare in May. The episode groups her intervention with [[NarendraModi]]'s question to show that the issue has become a recurring diplomatic pressure point.

The source does not make Bishop a central actor in [[Myanmar]] policy. Her function is evidentiary: foreign inquiries about proof of life appear to provoke anger from [[MinAungHlaing]], strengthening the segment's claim that access to Aung San Suu Kyi remains politically sensitive.
