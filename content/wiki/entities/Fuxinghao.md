# Fuxinghao / 复兴号

Fuxinghao is the Chinese standard high-speed train family in [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]]. The episode presents it as the post-[[Hexiehao]] answer to mixed imported platforms, non-unified parts, differing driver consoles, and rescue compatibility problems.

The source says the [[ChinaRailwayCorporation]]-led [[ChineseStandardEMU]] effort produced Fuxinghao CR400 in 2015, with high shares of Chinese standards, domestic design, improved interoperability, lower aerodynamic drag, and lower per-passenger energy use. In the episode's arc, Fuxinghao is evidence that [[ChineseHighSpeedRail]] moved from technology import toward standard-definition capacity.
