# Jackie Fisher / Admiral Lord Fisher

## Overview

Jackie Fisher / Admiral Lord Fisher enters the wiki through [[675-the-first-world-war-slaughter-at-gallipoli-part-5-glt4394132715]] as First Sea Lord and the senior naval opponent of [[WinstonChurchill|Winston Churchill]]'s Dardanelles scheme.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Fisher as substantively right about the Dardanelles risk but politically ineffective in stopping the operation. He warned that forcing the straits would require a large land commitment, while Churchill and the War Council treated the plan as a more limited naval experiment.

His importance is institutional. Fisher makes visible that Gallipoli was not launched in ignorance of all risk; objections existed inside the Admiralty, but they were overridden, softened, or failed to carry the room.

## Key Characteristics

- Fisher was First Sea Lord during the approval of the Dardanelles plan.
- He strongly opposed Churchill's naval scheme.
- He argued that securing the coastline would require about 200,000 troops.
- The episode treats him as right on the substance of risk but unable to win the political argument.
- His warnings make Gallipoli a case of ignored institutional objection rather than simple absence of information.
