# Algeria

Algeria enters the wiki through [[taken-littorally-spains-sudden-crisis-in-ceuta-6a70712c034f16a52ebfaed7]] as Morocco's long-running rival in the [[WesternSahara]] dispute. [[MichaelReid]] says [[PedroSanchez]]'s shift from backing a UN referendum toward Morocco's autonomy plan angered Algeria and created political backlash inside [[Spain]].

The source uses Algeria to show why the [[Ceuta]] crisis is not only border management. Spain's later attempt to restore relations with Algeria may have irritated [[Morocco]], making the enclave crossing part of a triangular diplomacy problem.
