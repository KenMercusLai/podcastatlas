# 太史旦 / Taishi Dan

太史旦 / Taishi Dan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] through a [[Shiji|《史记》]] story used to supplement a blank [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] year. The episode identifies him as a Zhou grand historian or archivist who would know court records, genealogies, and political origin stories.

In the source, Taishi Dan visits [[QinXianGong|秦献公]] and says Zhou and Qin once had a shared origin, later separated, would reunite after five hundred years, and would produce a hegemon after seventeen more years. The host reads this as [[KinshipLegitimacyDiplomacy|kinship legitimacy diplomacy]] by the [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]], not only as supernatural prophecy.

The episode also raises the possibility that Taishi Dan might be [[Laozi|老子]] or at least a Laozi-like wise official. That link remains source speculation.
