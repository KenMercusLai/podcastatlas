## Overview

梁其伟 is the founder of [[SGame|灵游坊]], creator of [[RainBlood|《雨血》]], and producer of [[PhantomBladeZero|《影之刃零》]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Liang as an architecture-trained creator whose writing, drawing, reading, and structural habits became inputs to game making. His career moves from independent PC creation through mobile-game commercialization and capital ambition, then back toward a large author-led premium game. He now describes company management, financing, and technology as supports for improving creative capability rather than ends in themselves.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines visual, literary, architectural, and systems-oriented approaches to game creation.
- Treats damaged, extreme, or internally divided personalities as sources of character specificity.
- Acknowledges tension between uncompromising creator and pragmatic operator roles within himself.
- Uses business setbacks and mobile-game experience as management training rather than erasing them as a failed period.
- Defines long-term success through stronger products and craft rather than company scale or capital status alone.
