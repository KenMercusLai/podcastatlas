# Pat Farah

## Overview

Pat Farah is the [[HomeDepot|Home Depot]] cofounder in [[home-depot-1]] who supplied the missing merchandising and retail-theater instinct after the other founders discovered his Home Co warehouse home-improvement store.

## Current Profile

Farah's role is the category and merchandising complement to the finance, operations, and capital-market strengths of [[BernieMarcus]], [[ArthurBlank]], and [[KenLangone]]. The episode treats him as essential because the Home Depot model had to feel full, active, and project-rich from the start.

## Key Characteristics
- Merchandising founder with practical warehouse home-improvement experience.
- Helped shape the theatrical, job-site feel of early stores.
- Supplied category selection instincts that the founding group otherwise lacked.

## Evidence
- [[home-depot-1]] says Marcus and Blank found Home Co, a Los Angeles warehouse home-improvement store run by Farah.
- [[home-depot-1]] says the team did not buy Home Co because of insolvency issues, but brought Farah into the founding group.
- [[home-depot-1]] credits Farah with objecting to polished floors and helping make early stores feel like working project spaces.

## Qualifications

The source emphasizes Farah's merchandising genius but gives less detail on his later management role, so this page stays focused on the founding model.
