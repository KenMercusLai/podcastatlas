# Karen Stahl

## Overview

Karen Stahl is an airline captain and fear-of-flying instructor whose account in [[this-is-your-captain-speaking]] shows how passenger reassurance, professional identity, and gendered expectations meet in cockpit announcements.

## Current Profile

Stahl describes being comfortable flying while initially fearing passenger announcements because her voice did not match the expected male pilot stereotype. She treats a natural, comfortable, and reassuring tone as especially important during turbulence.

Her decision not to announce her surname because “Stahl” can sound like “stall” illustrates the unusually small cues on which anxious passengers may build judgments about an unseen cockpit crew. The case supports thoughtful audience awareness without establishing that pilots should conform to one demographic or vocal norm.

## Key Characteristics

- Airline captain with experience teaching at a fear-of-flying clinic.
- Treats announcements as part of passenger reassurance rather than a purely technical task.
- Experienced tension between her own voice and a historically male occupational stereotype.
- Adjusts a potentially alarming surname cue while favoring natural rather than imitative delivery.
