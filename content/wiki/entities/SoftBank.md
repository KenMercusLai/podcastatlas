# SoftBank

[[170-1929-de-paomo-zhixia-sange-daibiao-renwu-he-tamen-zai-dangxia-zhouqi-de-yingzi-lo7uv7umozr0jrqfjzyhdbzicx1j]] adds SoftBank through [[MasayoshiSon]] as the source's main contemporary [[WilliamDurant]] analogy. The episode grants that Son's earlier industrial and platform bets created real achievement, then asks whether the search for another Alibaba-scale win can push SoftBank toward larger, more identity-loaded [[OpenAI]], [[Oracle]], and [[StargateAIInfrastructure]] commitments.

SoftBank appears in [[152-guanyu-2026-nian-de-si-ge-caixiang-nhx-c5xjwaovpaqjpua24diohsaz]] as an example in the private-market AI risk discussion. [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] mentions SoftBank selling [[Nvidia]] exposure and increasing its [[OpenAI]]-linked bet as part of a broader pattern where private AI optimism can redirect large capital pools outside ordinary public-market visibility.

The source does not treat SoftBank as proof that AI is fraudulent or that a crash is imminent. It uses the company as a signal inside [[PrivateMarketBubbleOpacity]]: late-stage AI financing, private-company valuation, and capital rotation can become important even when investors mostly discuss public mega-cap technology stocks.
