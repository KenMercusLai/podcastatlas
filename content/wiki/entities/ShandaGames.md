# Shanda Games / 盛大游戏

Shanda Games / 盛大游戏 is the game-business entity discussed in [[no-202-shengdade-shengda-chuanqide-chuanqi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-20-977833518]]. The source follows it from [[Shanda|盛大]]'s core game operations through a 2009 U.S. listing, later separation from Chen Tianqiao's Shanda system, and eventual restructuring into [[CenturyHuatong|世纪华通]] before the Shengqu Games name.

The episode treats Shanda Games as the institutional carrier of [[LegendOfMir|《传奇》]] after the founder-led Shanda era. It also shows the limits of old PC-era advantages: [[InternetCafePointCardDistribution]] and early game operations mattered less once mobile distribution, social platforms, and app stores became central.
