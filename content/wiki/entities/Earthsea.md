# 《地海传奇》 / Earthsea

[[Earthsea|《地海传奇》 / Earthsea]] appears in [[36-heian-de-zuoshou-liufang-yu-ai-de-weilai-wangshi-605254522]] as the fantasy-side example of [[UrsulaKLeGuin|Le Guin]]'s worldbuilding. The episode uses it to explain why Le Guin's work can be influential yet difficult to adapt: the force is not only plot or magical apparatus, but atmosphere, names, moral rhythm, and the feeling that a large world exists beyond the visible story.

The source also places Earthsea in an influence chain around later fantasy. It treats this as broad reception context rather than a direct proof claim: Le Guin's school, magic, and naming systems help explain her stature beside [[JRRTolkien|J.R.R. Tolkien / 托尔金]] and later fantasy writers.
