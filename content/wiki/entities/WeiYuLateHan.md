## Overview

魏宇 / Wei Yu is [[FifthZhongLateHan|第五种]]'s subordinate investigator in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-851-hanhuandi-weihe-zhuding-yichou-wannian-ltgm1ix6cgqb-kqo9bztfrzjgeun|Hanji 851]]. His importance is procedural: he converts [[ShanKuangLateHan|单匡]]'s corruption into reportable evidence and then becomes the witness whom Shan Kuang tries to remove.

## Current Profile

Fifth Zhong sends Wei Yu to investigate Shan Kuang. Wei Yu's report brings Shan Kuang and [[ShanChaoLateHan|单超]] under accusation, making him dangerous to the protected family network. Shan Kuang hires [[RenFangLateHan|任芳]] to kill Wei Yu, but Wei Yu detects the plan and captures Ren Fang.

The episode then moves Wei Yu's case from local investigation to central confrontation. Ren Fang is imprisoned in [[LuoyangLateHan|Luoyang]], and Shan Kuang tries to arrange a jailbreak. [[YangBingLateHan|杨秉]] asks to bring Shan Kuang to Luoyang for confrontation, but the court punishes Yang Bing instead.

## Key Characteristics

- Subordinate investigator whose work turns Shan Kuang's conduct into formal evidence.
- Targeted witness in a protected-family corruption case.
- Procedurally alert actor who detects and captures his would-be assassin.
- Link between local investigation and the failed Luoyang confrontation request.
