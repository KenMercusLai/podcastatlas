# 方舟 / Fangzhou (Photographer)

## Overview
方舟 is a photographer who plans story-led portrait and short-video shoots around historical, literary, cinematic, and martial-arts themes while keeping photography alongside another primary job.

## Current Profile
In EP388, 方舟 presents photography as a combined research, production, service, and direction practice. She prefers agreed, prepared collaboration to unconsented street capture; studies period, text, costume, prop, and action details; scouts locations; directs subjects through roles and environmental interaction; and treats weather or equipment failure as production problems to solve. Her current business position is deliberately unsettled: client work must prioritize the buyer's appearance and expectations, while self-directed work offers stronger authorship but consumes more preparation and is currently supported by primary-job income.

## Key Characteristics
- Plans photographs around a theme and story rather than treating portraiture as visual attractiveness alone.
- Uses historical and textual research to bound costume, prop, weapon, movement, and setting choices.
- Directs inexperienced subjects through actions, role language, environmental contact, and affirmative feedback.
- Treats scouting, team communication, backup power and storage, and weather adaptation as part of the craft.
- Prefers self-directed work for expressive control while acknowledging the obligations of paid client delivery.
- Approaches full-time commercialization as a future possibility requiring stronger skill, demand understanding, and repeatable products.
