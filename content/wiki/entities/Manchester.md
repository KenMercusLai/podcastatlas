# Manchester

Manchester appears in [[a-firm-andy-what-are-new-british-pms-plans-6a5deeca76f8ee25bb4bbf9c]] as the planned location of [[Number10North]], [[AndyBurnham]]'s proposed northern Downing Street office. The city matters because Burnham's mayoral identity and devolution politics are tied to [[GreaterManchester]].

The source treats Manchester as symbolically powerful but not sufficient on its own. Moving part of Number 10 north can signal a break from Westminster centralization, but [[DevolutionAsGoverningProject]] still has to change authority, services, and visible outcomes across the [[UnitedKingdom]].
