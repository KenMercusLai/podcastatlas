# 刘亏 / Liu Kui (Bohai king)

[[LiuKuiBohaiKing|刘亏]] enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-903-weishenme-quan-ni-yuanyuan-xiangbao-heshi-le-lokpt93muc8kasvn44vgsjki8bom|Hanji 903]] as a brother of Emperor Huan who had been demoted to a lower title and then sought restoration as king of [[BohaiKingdomLateHan|Bohai]]. The source transcript preserves the spelling 刘亏; this page keeps that spelling until later evidence requires normalization.

His restoration depends on an access bargain with [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]]: Liu promises fifty million cash if Wang can persuade the throne to restore him. After the restoration succeeds, Liu refuses to pay, saying the decision came from imperial kinship rather than Wang's work. The refusal turns [[InnerCourtAccessBrokerageRisk|inner-court access brokerage]] into a trap: the same intermediary who delivered restoration can monitor Liu's network and recode the debt dispute as a security threat.

Wang Fu then uses Liu's resentment and his links to [[ZhengSaLateHan|郑萨]] and [[DongTengLateHan|董腾]] to construct a treason frame, with [[LianZhongLateHan|廉中]] forging evidence that Zheng and others planned to install Liu as emperor. [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] accepts the accusation, sends a [[JizhouLateHan|Jizhou]] inspector to Bohai, and Liu is forced into suicide. The episode emphasizes the collateral toll: Liu's concubines, children, dancers, and fief officials are destroyed along with him, while Wang Fu and other accusers are rewarded.
