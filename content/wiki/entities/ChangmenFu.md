## Overview

《长门赋》 / Changmen Fu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-270-chenajiao-mishi-hougong-zhizhong-ta-weihe-duchong-nvguan-2-lllzlb2knjj0ovmuc8tiid34m0gp|Hanji 270]] as the fu traditionally connected to [[EmpressChenAjiaoWesternHan|陈阿娇]]'s confinement at Changmen Palace and attributed to [[SimaXiangru|司马相如]].

## Current Profile

The episode says Chen sends an intermediary with a large payment to ask Sima Xiangru to write the work, which voices the sorrow of a woman isolated at Changmen Palace. The host praises its emotional power, then uses the story to criticize a life consumed by romantic attachment. The source supplies reception and a commission tradition, not a textual analysis or authorship study.

## Key Characteristics

- Fu associated with a deposed empress's confinement and longing.
- Traditionally attributed in this episode to Sima Xiangru.
- Presented as a commissioned work purchased through an intermediary.
- Used by the host as a bridge from Western Han court history to modern relationship advice.

## Qualifications

The source neither quotes nor analyzes the text in detail and does not examine the attribution tradition, manuscript history, commission payment, intermediary's identity, or the work's actual effect on Chen's position. Its emotional lesson is modern reception rather than evidence about Chen's private psychology.
