# Exozyme

## Overview
Exozyme is a biotechnology company described by [[MichaelHeltzen]] as engineering enzymes to run biochemical pathways outside living cells and manufacture scarce natural molecules.

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Exozyme's platform combines [[CellFreeEnzymeEngineering]], [[ProteinLanguageModels]], rapid experimental validation, and an [[ExperimentGroundedProteinModelLoop]]. The company aims to turn that platform into products, beginning with the source-described [[NCTExozyme|NCT]] program and a later rare non-intoxicating cannabinoid line, while keeping supplement and drug regulatory routes distinct.

## Key Characteristics
- Re-engineers enzymes for stable operation outside the living cells in which they evolved.
- Targets naturally occurring molecules whose low abundance makes direct extraction difficult or uneconomic.
- Uses a controlled cell-free environment to reduce cellular variability and unwanted reaction products.
- Couples computational enzyme proposals to faster and cheaper source-described wet-lab testing.
- Treats experimental results as proprietary data for improving later predictions.
- Sequences commercial products and therapeutic research through different evidence and regulatory pathways.
