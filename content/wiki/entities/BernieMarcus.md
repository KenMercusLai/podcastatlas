# Bernie Marcus

Bernie Marcus is the [[HomeDepot|Home Depot]] cofounder who appears in [[costco-1]] as another retailer who studied [[PriceClub]]. The episode uses him as evidence that [[SolPrice]]'s warehouse-club model shaped more than Costco and Sam's Club.
