## Overview

公孙绿萼 / Gongsun Lü'e is a [[ReturnOfTheCondorHeroes|《神雕侠侣》]] character whose deprivation of familial care, rapid attachment to [[YangGuoJinYong|杨过]], and fatal sacrifice concentrate the costs of profoundly unequal love.

## Current Profile

The episode links her rapid attachment to scarcity of care. Brief praise, teasing, rescue, and Yang Guo's willingness to jump into danger acquire exceptional weight because her father instrumentalizes her and her mother's love is controlling and violent. Once she understands Yang Guo's bond with [[XiaolongnuJinYong|小龙女]], she does not demand displacement and instead hopes they can remain together.

Her death is therefore not reduced to rejected romance. The hosts connect it to a family system in which neither parent offers a viable home, while still reading her exchange of life for half an 绝情丹 as the episode's starkest imbalance between gift and response. Yang Guo's later disposal of the antidote can symbolize how little her sacrifice governs his choice, but it can also be understood through his decision to share Xiaolongnü's fate.

## Key Characteristics

- Receives little reliable care despite being the daughter of 绝情谷's rulers.
- Treats direct praise and rescue as unusually precious because attention has been scarce.
- Recognizes Yang Guo and Xiaolongnü's bond without trying to displace it.
- Gives her life and the antidote without gaining romantic reciprocity.
- Combines romantic despair with a broader collapse of safety and belonging inside her family.

## Qualifications

The profile derives from a structured conversational summary. The effects of parental neglect, the reasons for suicide, the meaning of her name, the discarded antidote, and Yang Guo's internal valuation remain interpretations. Familial injury and unreturned love make the tragedy intelligible but do not make self-destruction inevitable or exemplary.

## What Changed

- Established a profile joining romantic nonreciprocity to severe familial deprivation.
- Distinguished recognition of the central pair from entitlement or rivalry.
- Qualified the discarded-antidote symbol with Yang Guo's own fatal commitment.
