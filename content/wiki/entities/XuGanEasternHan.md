## Overview
Xu Gan is a Pingling man who volunteers to reinforce [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]] in the Western Regions under [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Emperor Zhang]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-750-jiemi-donghan-wanmei-nvxing-beihou-manshi-xisheng-lu-96lbdh7r0tsmiopbqqrj0q1gt|Hanji 750]] makes him a small-force support figure in Ban Chao's proxy strategy, [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-753-banchao-beigao-heizhuang-lt-sksxrz30sawit81eqbeonhzv|Hanji 753]] preserves him as a Western Regions associate, and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-705-4-banchao-donghan-zui-weida-de-zhanlueji-waijiaojia-luohuafhlmnbws52bya4vjoevrg7|Hanji 705-4]] adds the later promotion and Wusun-contact setting.

## Current Profile
Xu Gan enters the wiki as a volunteer rather than as an already powerful frontier commander. After [[BanChaoEasternHan|Ban Chao]] argues that Qiuci and its dependents can be handled through hostage kingship, local polities, and limited Han support, Xu Gan offers himself for the mission. [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Emperor Zhang]] appoints him acting sima and sends him west with about one thousand released prisoners and volunteer soldiers.

His first clear action is operational support at Shule. When Shache has shifted toward Qiuci and Shule commandant Pan Chen rebels, Xu Gan reaches the region and joins Ban Chao in attacking Pan Chen, killing more than one thousand rebels in the source account. Hanji 705-4 then keeps Xu Gan inside the continuing Wusun-and-Western-Regions policy sequence, saying Zhangdi raises him to military sima when Ban Chao is made general-command chief clerk. His importance is therefore tactical and connective: he gives Ban Chao enough outside support to stabilize a local crisis without turning the campaign into a large imperial army deployment.

## Key Characteristics
- He is a volunteer reinforcement from Pingling rather than a court-heavy frontier magnate.
- His appointment translates [[EmperorZhangOfHan|Zhangdi]]'s approval of Ban Chao's memorial into a limited expeditionary support package.
- He leads a mixed force of released prisoners and volunteers, showing low-cost mobilization.
- He joins Ban Chao in suppressing Pan Chen's Shule rebellion.
- He remains part of the Wusun-contact and Western Regions support network after Ban Chao's strategy receives court approval.
- His profile is still narrow and source-bound, with little evidence beyond the Western Regions mission.
