# 新野 / Xinye (late Han)

## Overview
新野 / Xinye enters the wiki through Hanji 1054 as the northern [[Jingzhou|Jingzhou]] post where [[LiuBiao|刘表]] stations [[LiuBei|刘备]] after Liu Bei's defeat by [[CaoCao|曹操]].

## Current Profile
The sources frame Xinye as a small but strategically charged refuge. It is described as Jingzhou's northern edge, close to Cao-controlled Wan and roughly a hundred-plus li north of [[XiangyangLateHan|襄阳]]. That placement makes Liu Bei a frontier buffer for Liu Biao while also giving Liu Bei room to preserve himself, hide sharpness, and accumulate Jingzhou elite and refugee support during several years of low visible motion. Hanji 1098 calls this a seven-year stay and says Liu Bei grows a force of more than six thousand while gathering people, family resources, and talent, sharpening Xinye's role as an incubator inside dependency.

## Key Characteristics
- Northern Jingzhou county used as Liu Bei's post-defeat station.
- Functions as both shelter and frontline buffer between Liu Biao's center and Cao Cao's northern pressure.
- Supports the episode's reading of Liu Bei's "蛰伏" as active preservation rather than passive comfort.
- Echoes Liu Bei's earlier small-base pattern at [[PeiCounty|小沛]] by giving him limited space under a stronger patron.
- Converts reciprocal dependence into accumulation: Liu Biao gains a northern buffer while Liu Bei gains time and organizational room.

## Evidence
- Reception and stationing: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1054-jingtan-caocao-yuanshao-liubei-de-mingyun-zhuanzhe-lmd2yjre-cwdbxky2ht5o0n4g6fk|Hanji 1054]] says Liu Biao personally welcomes Liu Bei, treats him with guest ritual, adds troops, and stations him at Xinye.
- Frontier placement: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1054-jingtan-caocao-yuanshao-liubei-de-mingyun-zhuanzhe-lmd2yjre-cwdbxky2ht5o0n4g6fk|Hanji 1054]] describes Xinye as Jingzhou's northern small county near Cao's Wan line and north of Xiangyang.
- Dormancy role: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1054-jingtan-caocao-yuanshao-liubei-de-mingyun-zhuanzhe-lmd2yjre-cwdbxky2ht5o0n4g6fk|Hanji 1054]] treats Liu Bei's Xinye years as a key accumulation period, with apparent submission masking ongoing political preparation.
- Duration and force-building: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1098-liubei-de-tiaocao-miji-daizhe-ziyuan-yue-tiao-yue-zhiqian-ltasucqzwrono0qayzx8sonsqc8k|Hanji 1098]] describes seven years at Xinye and a force of more than six thousand, alongside follower, family, and talent accumulation.

## Qualifications
The page keeps Xinye source-bounded to these episodes' Liu Bei branch. It does not reconstruct the county's full administrative history or later military events. Hanji 1098's seven-year duration, local population framing, and force count remain source-presented rather than independently verified.

## What Changed
- Added the reciprocal buffer-and-shelter relationship between Liu Biao and Liu Bei.
- Added the source-scoped seven-year duration and force-building estimate.
- Reframed Xinye more explicitly as an incubator for portable capacity under dependency.
