# 高梵 / Gao Fan (late Han)

## Overview

高梵 / Gao Fan is a source-bounded palace eunuch whose current profile spans two Shun-era access moments. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-806-xiangyao-chengshi-zhaodui-ren-hen-guanjian-luswh-p-oyutysxue6shweqcwqxs|Hanji 806]] shows him usefully reporting Yu Xu's family-and-student petition to [[EmperorShunOfHan|汉顺帝]], while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-824-weishenme-women-yao-xue-shouzhuo-lpssy8ltykugdpvmgrzwcpf6-9v9|Hanji 824]] shows his undocumented crown-prince transfer order becoming a succession-procedure risk.

## Current Profile

Hanji 806 gives Gao Fan a report-channel role. Yu Xu's son and more than one hundred students wait outside the palace, bloody themselves by repeated kowtow, and ask that Yu Xu be spared after [[ZhangFangEunuchLateHan|张防]]'s retaliation. Gao Fan reports this pressure to Shun, helping reopen a case that had been distorted through Zhang Fang's access.

Hanji 824 gives Gao Fan a later procedural-risk role. During the Chengguang Palace episode, Gao Fan comes from the inner palace to fetch the crown prince without producing an imperial edict or credential. [[DuQiaoLateHan|Du Qiao]] and others hesitate, while [[ZhongHaoLateHan|Zhong Hao]] blocks the crown-prince cart and demands proof. Gao Fan's role ends when he returns to obtain the required authorization.

Together, the sources make Gao Fan less a stable factional type than an access-channel case: palace proximity can transmit blocked grievance upward, but the same proximity requires credential checks when succession assets are moved.

## Key Characteristics

- Palace eunuch whose access can carry blocked public pressure to the ruler.
- Report channel in Yu Xu's rescue after family and students make the case visible.
- Palace envoy associated with crown-prince movement inside the late Han court.
- Trigger figure for Zhong Hao's credential-checking refusal.
- Ambiguous actor: the sources record channel function and procedural irregularity, not a proven plot.

## Evidence

- Yu Xu petition report: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-806-xiangyao-chengshi-zhaodui-ren-hen-guanjian-luswh-p-oyutysxue6shweqcwqxs|Hanji 806]] says Yu Xu's son and more than one hundred students petition Gao Fan, who reports the matter to Shun.
- Case reversal through access: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-806-xiangyao-chengshi-zhaodui-ren-hen-guanjian-luswh-p-oyutysxue6shweqcwqxs|Hanji 806]] says Shun releases Yu Xu and punishes Zhang Fang's side after the petition reaches him.
- Unauthorized arrival: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-824-weishenme-women-yao-xue-shouzhuo-lpssy8ltykugdpvmgrzwcpf6-9v9|Hanji 824]] says Gao Fan comes to fetch Crown Prince Liu Bing without edict or信符.
- Zhong Hao's refusal: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-824-weishenme-women-yao-xue-shouzhuo-lpssy8ltykugdpvmgrzwcpf6-9v9|Hanji 824]] records Zhong Hao blocking the cart with a sword and demanding authorization.
- Procedural resolution: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-824-weishenme-women-yao-xue-shouzhuo-lpssy8ltykugdpvmgrzwcpf6-9v9|Hanji 824]] has Gao Fan return and bring the edict before the crown prince is allowed to depart.

## Qualifications

The sources do not establish Gao Fan's intent. Hanji 806 presents him as a useful palace report channel in Yu Xu's case, while Hanji 824 presents an undocumented mission that Zhong Hao rightly refuses to trust until authorization appears. This page should therefore treat him as a palace-access and procedure actor unless later sources show a factional or conspiratorial role. The podcast transcript form 高泛 is normalized to the received Tongjian form 高梵.
