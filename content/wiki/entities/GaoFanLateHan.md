# 高梵 / Gao Fan (late Han)

## Overview

Gao Fan is a source-bounded palace eunuch envoy in the 144 CE crown-prince transfer involving Liu Bing, later [[EmperorChongOfHan|Emperor Chong of Han]]. His significance is not that the source proves malicious intent, but that his undocumented arrival exposes how much succession security depends on verifiable edict authority.

## Current Profile

During the Chengguang Palace episode, Gao Fan comes from the inner palace to fetch the crown prince without producing an imperial edict or credential. [[DuQiaoLateHan|Du Qiao]] and others hesitate, while [[ZhongHaoLateHan|Zhong Hao]] blocks the crown-prince cart and demands proof.

Gao Fan's role ends when he returns to obtain the required authorization. The episode therefore marks him as a trigger for a palace-access control problem rather than as an independently interpreted conspirator.

## Key Characteristics

- Palace eunuch envoy associated with crown-prince movement inside the late Han court.
- Trigger figure for Zhong Hao's credential-checking refusal.
- Ambiguous actor: the source records procedural irregularity, not a proven plot.
- Case study in how informal palace access could collide with succession safeguards.

## Evidence

- Unauthorized arrival - [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-824-weishenme-women-yao-xue-shouzhuo-lpssy8ltykugdpvmgrzwcpf6-9v9|Hanji 824]] says Gao Fan comes to fetch Crown Prince Liu Bing without edict or信符.
- Zhong Hao's refusal - [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-824-weishenme-women-yao-xue-shouzhuo-lpssy8ltykugdpvmgrzwcpf6-9v9|Hanji 824]] records Zhong Hao blocking the cart with a sword and demanding authorization.
- Procedural resolution - [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-824-weishenme-women-yao-xue-shouzhuo-lpssy8ltykugdpvmgrzwcpf6-9v9|Hanji 824]] has Gao Fan return and bring the edict before the crown prince is allowed to depart.

## Qualifications

The source does not establish Gao Fan's intent. This page should therefore treat him as a palace-procedure actor unless later sources show a factional or conspiratorial role. The podcast transcript form 高泛 is normalized to the received Tongjian form 高梵.
