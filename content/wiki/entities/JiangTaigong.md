# 姜太公 / 姜子牙 / Jiang Taigong

姜太公 / Jiang Taigong enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-75-2-qiongshe-jichi-de-zushiye-guanzhong-lo8uei2u1qwj9tszftel1b4lswoa]] as the founding ruler of [[QiState|齐国]] in the episode's quick setup. The source uses him to remind listeners that Qi begins as an old [[ZhouRoyalHouse|Zhou]]-era enfeoffed state before later court disorder and [[GuanZhong|管仲]]'s reforms reshape its Spring-Autumn role.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-1-lvbuwei-shi-jiangziya-houren-lveg-wsmggroaxjsnxopaevexqod]] turns that marker into a fuller source-scoped biography. The episode names him 姜姓、吕氏、名尚、字子牙, explaining why he can also be called Lü Shang and why [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] can be linked to him through a Jiang-surname/Lu-clan lineage claim.

The source presents Jiang Taigong as the episode's strongest case of [[LateBloomingTalent|大器晚成式人才显现]]. It says he came from a declined household, kept studying astronomy, geography, military strategy, and governance, and remained publicly unsuccessful until around seventy. His recognition comes when [[ZhouWenwang|周文王]] meets him fishing by the Wei River, reads him as the long-awaited adviser, and brings him back as 太师.

Under [[ZhouWuwang|周武王]], Jiang Taigong becomes 师尚父 and helps defeat Shang and establish Zhou. The episode then makes him the founding ruler of [[QiState|齐国]], crediting him with appointing worthy people, caring for the people, and developing fishing, salt, and commerce so Qi can grow from poor and small into a strong state.

The same source extends his later Zhou role beyond the conquest. After Zhou Wuwang's death, Jiang Taigong assists [[ZhouGong|周公]] in suppressing rebellion and is later described as a four-generation elder who helps secure Zhou Kang Wang's succession. The episode records his later reputation as 兵家鼻祖, while the wiki keeps the age and genealogy details source-scoped.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-112-3-hanfei-yu-yaojia-lunzhan-qinwang-dang-caipan-ljxxlok4uh-ajjirmolybodlspv9]] adds Jiang Taigong as one of [[YaoJia|姚贾]]'s courtroom precedents. Yao Jia invokes him to argue that a ruler who rejects talent because of humble or difficult origins would also reject the old examples later celebrated as founding ministers.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-123-2-gudai-shiqi-de-daochamen-daodi-you-duo-weixian-lgcknwubwm3uzqmzgbgdlxlvcwxy]] adds a later reception branch through [[TaigongBingfa|《太公兵法》]]. In the source's Zhang Liang story, [[HuangshiGong|黄石公]] gives the book to [[ZhangLiang|张良]], making Jiang Taigong's strategic authority part of Zhang Liang's transformation from anti-Qin avenger to future counselor.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-145-junshi-qicai-hanxin-bolan-zhuangkuo-de-yisheng-2-npgife56ltayww2wt4wo-fiwo-ep|Hanji 145 part 2]] adds a Han Xin reception branch. The episode presents Jiang Taigong as one of [[HanXin|韩信]]'s admired models, linking Han Xin's future military imagination to an older founder-strategist lineage rather than only to innate battlefield talent.
