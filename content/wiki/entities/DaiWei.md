# 戴威 / Dai Wei

## Overview
戴威 / Dai Wei is the OFO founder figure in the episode's shared-bike boom and deposit-crisis account.

## Current Profile
The source presents Dai as a Peking University graduate whose early cycling-tourism idea became [[Ofo|OFO]] after a campus-bike sharing pivot. His profile is shaped by the move from a low-cost, high-frequency campus model to a city-scale capital war in which OFO had to manage cheap hardware, weak locks, subsidies, strategic investors, SoftBank targets, Didi involvement, and merger pressure.

The episode's final view is tragic rather than simply celebratory. Dai's insistence on independent control appears in the failed Didi alignment and failed Mobike merger, while the later deposit-refund crisis makes OFO a central case in [[SharedBikeCapitalWar]] and [[O2OPlatformEconomics]].

## Key Characteristics
- He is the founder associated with OFO's campus-to-city shared-bike pivot.
- His early model relied on low-cost bikes, high campus frequency, and fast payback assumptions.
- His later company faced mechanical-lock abuse, subsidy burn, order-volume pressure, and strategic-investor conflict.
- He resisted arrangements that would have reduced founder control through Didi involvement or a Mobike merger.
- The source links his aftermath to OFO's unresolved deposit memory and later presence in the United States.
