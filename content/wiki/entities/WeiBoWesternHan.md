## Overview

魏勃 / Wei Bo is presented as the Qi official who helps [[LiuXiangQiKingWesternHan|刘襄]] overcome internal resistance and convert an anti-Lü court plan into an armed regional mobilization.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-205-nvhuangdi-lvzhi-jingran-yin-gou-er-si-2-ltgwfdhn9cdwphudy0blyh36fge1|Hanji 205 part 2]] places Wei Bo among Liu Xiang's advisers. When chancellor [[ZhaoPingQiChancellor|赵平]] surrounds the royal palace, Wei Bo reportedly gains control of Zhao Ping's soldiers through deception and redirects them against the chancellor's residence. After Zhao Ping's suicide, Liu Xiang appoints Wei Bo as general.

## Key Characteristics

- Qi adviser aligned with Liu Xiang's anti-Lü mobilization.
- Tactical deceiver who obtains control of the chancellor's forces in the episode's account.
- Military appointee after the internal Qi power struggle.
- Enabler of regional mobilization rather than an independent claimant.
