# 汉桓帝 / Emperor Huan of Han

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-901-jixingzi-de-ren-hui-shi-shenme-xiachang-lh1yfyjzqptxvtnv9taiqms9yjud|Hanji 901]] gives Emperor Huan a posthumous court-ritual role. The episode centers on whether [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦皇太后]], his empress and later dowager, should be buried with him and share the proper sacrificial honor at Xuanling. [[LiXianTaiweiLateHan|李贤]] and [[ChenQiuLateHan|陈球 / 陈求]] argue that her role beside Huan should not be displaced by [[FengGuirenLateHan|冯贵人]] or by family-guilt logic.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-914-xinren-de-ren-weihe-zong-shang-ni-zui-shen-lvwit-rzrkscn5a3h6wblntxppso|Hanji 914]] uses Emperor Huan as a comparison point when [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]] asks [[YangQiLateHan|杨琦]] how Ling compares with his predecessor. Yang Qi's answer does not satisfy Ling's desire for validation, making Huan part of Ling's self-image and advice-reception problem.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-929-duishi-buduiren-ni-xin-le-ma-lgaxj0jzopddmt3c5hibnexqlhb3|Hanji 929]] recalls an earlier succession dispute through [[LiGuLateHan|李固]], who had opposed establishing the later Emperor Huan and preferred [[LiuSuanLateHan|刘蒜]]. The page therefore holds Huan mostly as a reference node for later reign memory, succession evaluation, and the burial legitimacy of Dou Miao.
