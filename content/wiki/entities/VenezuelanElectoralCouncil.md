# Venezuelan Electoral Council

The Venezuelan Electoral Council appears in [[caracas-under-pressure-democracy-in-venezuela-6a7d8bbe7d85cbdf96e00822]] as one of the institutions opposition negotiators want reformed before a credible election. The source says the existing electoral council, together with the Venezuelan Supreme Court, helped enable [[NicolasMaduro]] to steal the 2024 presidential election.

The page is source-scoped to the episode's account. In the wiki's transition frame, the council matters because election timing is not enough: a [[DemocraticTransitionElection]] also needs an electoral body that defeated parties and voters can recognize as legitimate.
