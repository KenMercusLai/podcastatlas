# 台台 / Taitai

台台 / Taitai is a traditional film/TV practitioner and [[ChinaCommunicationUniversity|中国传媒大学]] graduate interviewed in [[cong-yangshi-jilupian-dao-baokuan-ai-duanju-di-yi-pi-zhuanshen-de-daoyan-s10e11-3c05e3d5-d8f6-44c1-97ca-698261d7b2bc]] about moving into [[AIShortDrama]] production. In the source, she complements [[Chouxiangzai]]'s director perspective with a producer and business view of cost, platform pricing, paid traffic, team structure, and content-quality strategy.

Her clearest contribution is the commercial frame. She divides the short-drama ecology into production/commissioning parties, traffic-buying parties, and platforms, and argues that platform policies and price systems can change company survival. She also estimates that AI short dramas can cut production cost to roughly one-third of comparable live-action modern romance short dramas, while still leaving labor as the main expense.

Taitai also frames the team as deliberately choosing a smaller, author-centered route rather than pure production scale. The source says the team considered hiring many generation workers and negotiating on capacity with [[Hongguo]] or [[YuewenGroup]], but chose to build director and writer teams around higher-quality work instead of only chasing ROI and volume.

## Key Points
- She treats "speaking to AI" as an extension of directing actors: the creator must express scene, performance, and emotion clearly.
- Her short-drama business map links [[ShortDramaEconomics]] to [[ShortDramaPaidTrafficDistribution]] and [[PlatformizedDramaProduction]].
- She warns that platform policy changes can directly threaten AI comic-drama and AI short-drama companies that depend on guaranteed fees plus traffic share.
- She argues the AI-content industry needs a more rational respect for content logic rather than only wind-chasing and cost reduction.
