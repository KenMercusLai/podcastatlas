# Sarah Pinsker / 莎拉·平斯克

Sarah Pinsker / 莎拉·平斯克 is the speculative-fiction writer and musician discussed in [[166-youyou-changfeng-wenrou-de-huanxiang-zhi-ge-fuping-jiaolv-de-gouhe-948043059]] through [[YouyouChangfeng|《悠游长风》 / 《悠悠长风》]]. The episode presents her as a writer whose stories can feel gentle, sincere, dreamlike, and darkly funny at once.

For the wiki, Pinsker matters as a different science-fiction mode from the cleaner conceptual architecture associated with [[TedChiang|Ted Chiang]] or the anthropological worldbuilding associated with [[UrsulaKLeGuin|Ursula K. Le Guin]]. The source emphasizes [[GentleSpeculativeAmbiguity]]: speculative premises around prosthetics, dream children, memory tools, generation ships, parallel selves, and AI reconstructions stay emotionally legible without being mechanically closed.

Her musician identity also matters in the source because the episode uses music from her work and treats rhythm, aftertone, and artistic transmission as part of the collection's atmosphere.
