# Laura Chase / 劳拉

## Overview
Laura Chase is [[IrisChase|Iris Chase]]'s younger sister in [[TheBlindAssassinNovel|《盲刺客》]], whose death, concealed abuse, silence, and attributed authorship organize the novel's mysteries.

## Current Profile
Laura is publicly fixed by an early death and by the book issued under her name. Iris's later account complicates both records. Laura had been sexually abused and coerced by Richard, who reportedly used Alex's safety as leverage; she became pregnant; and the novel attributed to her was actually written by Iris. Her apparent identity therefore combines experience she could not safely disclose with authorship she did not possess.

The episode resists a single-cause explanation for Laura's death. Abuse, protection of Alex, the news of his death, Iris's disclosure of their relationship, and accumulated misunderstanding all remain relevant. Laura's story embodies the cost of not being seen by a loving sister and the danger of public narratives that preserve a name while obscuring a life.

## Key Characteristics
- Younger sister shaped by maternal instruction that Iris must care for her.
- Victim of concealed sexual abuse, coercion, and pregnancy within the family household.
- Protector of Alex whose safety is used against her.
- Woman whose silence is partly translated through the novel's sacrificed and voiceless figures.
- Dead subject around whom public reports, memory, and false authorship accumulate.
- Figure whose motives and final act remain multiply caused rather than fully recoverable.

## Qualifications
Laura is a fictional character represented through one secondary discussion. The exact sequence of abuse, pregnancy, disclosure, departure, and death, as well as motive and symbol interpretation, remain source-scoped. A literary account cannot support clinical conclusions about suicide or trauma.
