## Overview

薛冰（古龙人物） / Xue Bing is [[LuXiaofeng|陆小凤]]'s principal romantic partner in [[EmbroideredBanditGuLong|《绣花大盗》]]. She accompanies his investigation after he consults the Divine Needle Xue household and becomes the episode's main case for both domestic romantic warmth and uneven female characterization.

## Current Profile

The bounded episode finds Lu and Xue most persuasive in teasing, food, disgust, anger, physical play, and his anxiety after her disappearance. Those scenes give their relationship ordinary texture and a stronger sense of canonical pairing than the hero's more overtly sexual encounters elsewhere.

The same discussion questions whether Xue's shyness, quietness, volatility, jealousy, and sudden changes emerge from stable conduct or accumulate as assigned traits. Similarities with another woman in the story weaken distinctiveness for some hosts. Her contrast with [[GongsunDaniangGuLong|公孙大娘]] is therefore double-edged: Xue supplies warmth and youthful intimacy, while Gongsun receives more force, command, and strategic individuality.

## Key Characteristics

- Creates romantic warmth through teasing, food, anger, and embodied everyday interaction.
- Receives unusual emotional importance from Lu's response to her disappearance.
- Is positioned as a principal or canonical partner within this bounded story.
- Combines shyness and quietness with volatility and jealousy.
- Exposes a gap between named traits and behaviorally prepared characterization.
- Contrasts with Gongsun Daniang's mature strategic parity and ambiguous authority.
