## Overview

杨辅 / Yang Fu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-511-taihou-tianhuaban-ta-ruhe-yitian-feng-wuge-didi-wei-hou-lhoz_ubecvc8-mgfoad2pya72uht|Hanji 511]] as the Yushi Cheng whose private resentment against [[WangZunWesternHan|王尊]] is named by [[GongchengXingWesternHan|公乘兴]] as the source of a possibly manufactured impeachment.

## Current Profile

The page treats Yang Fu as an accusation-channel actor. Hanji 511 does not give his side of the case, but Gongcheng Xing's memorial makes him important because it shows how official procedure can be contaminated when a subordinate with personal grievance supplies charge material to a censorial superior.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Yushi Cheng in Hanji 511.
- Said by Gongcheng Xing to have private resentment against Wang Zun.
- Alleged to have used office access to assemble or manufacture charges.

## Evidence

- Office and grievance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-511-taihou-tianhuaban-ta-ruhe-yitian-feng-wuge-didi-wei-hou-lhoz_ubecvc8-mgfoad2pya72uht|Hanji 511]] says Gongcheng Xing names Yang Fu as a Yushi Cheng with private resentment toward Wang Zun.
- Accusation-channel claim: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-511-taihou-tianhuaban-ta-ruhe-yitian-feng-wuge-didi-wei-hou-lhoz_ubecvc8-mgfoad2pya72uht|Hanji 511]] says Gongcheng Xing accuses Yang Fu of using his role to plan charges and frame Wang Zun.

## Qualifications

This page preserves Yang Fu as a source-scoped accusation target, not as a settled guilty party. Hanji 511 gives Gongcheng Xing's claim and Chengdi's partial restoration of Wang Zun, but not Yang Fu's defense, final penalty, or complete evidentiary record.

## What Changed

- Created Yang Fu from Hanji 511's private-grievance impeachment claim.
