# 庞涓 / Pang Juan

庞涓 / Pang Juan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-16-sunbin-pangjuan-enyuan-jiuge-guiling-zhi-zhan-lryhzkc1-xsvfjhvicu8yxa9d2jr]] as [[SunBin|孙膑]]'s fellow student and betrayer. The episode says both men studied under [[Guiguzi|鬼谷子]], but Pang Juan later becomes a [[WeiState|魏国]] general and fears that his own ability is below Sun Bin's.

Pang Juan's source role is moral and narrative as much as military. He tricks Sun Bin into coming to Wei, has him falsely punished, and turns a talent rivalry into mutilation, humiliation, and confinement. The later [[GuilingBattle|桂陵之战]] branch is therefore presented not only as a Qi-Wei-Zhao campaign but also as the return of the strategist whom Wei had tried to break.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-19-maling-zhi-zhan-lhbapt09x2ptdqx0uopkp8apbada]] moves Pang Juan into the [[MalingBattle|马陵之战]] campaign. He attacks [[HanState|韩国]] for [[WeiState|魏国]], is recalled when [[QiState|齐国]] threatens [[Daliang|大梁]], and becomes the target of [[SunBin|孙膑]]'s plan to exploit Wei arrogance through rapid pursuit and [[ForcedMarchOperationalRisk|forced-march exhaustion]].
