# LM-SYS

[[e247-duihua-shengying-xai-infra-de-langman-sglang-kaiyuan-pingquan-yu-zhenhuanchuan-6c9d13b1-ac9a-4a7a-a35b-99bfb8374668]] frames LM-SYS as the community institution [[ShengYing|盛颖]] and [[LianMin|连敏]] wanted to build so researchers and builders outside the most established schools or labs could still participate in AI work. In the source, LM-SYS is both the environment around [[SGLang]] and the values root of Sheng's [[OpenSourceAIDemocratization]] argument.

The episode says LM-SYS also produced [[LMArena|LM Arena]], an initially unexpected project that later attracted much wider attention and moved toward commercial-scale operation under a new team. That makes LM-SYS a bridge between open research, open infrastructure, model evaluation, and institution-building.
