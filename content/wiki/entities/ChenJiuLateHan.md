# 陈就 / Chen Jiu (late Han)

## Overview
陈就 / Chen Jiu is [[HuangZu|黄祖]]'s naval commander in the 208 CE defense of [[JiangxiaCommanderyLateHan|江夏郡]], killed by [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] after Wu breaches the barrier at [[MianKouLateHan|沔口]].

## Current Profile
The current evidence supplies one campaign role rather than a full biography. Hanji 1085 says Huang Zu sends Chen Jiu's naval force after [[DongXiLateHan|董袭]] cuts the anchored warships loose and opens the river approach. Lyu Meng then kills Chen Jiu, turning the broken barrier into wider collapse and clearing the way for the attack on Xiakou.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as Huang Zu's naval commander in the Jiangxia defense.
- Enters the battle after Wu breaks the fixed Mian Kou ship barrier.
- Is killed by Lyu Meng in the ensuing naval action.
- Marks the transition from obstacle breach to the collapse of organized resistance in the episode's account.
