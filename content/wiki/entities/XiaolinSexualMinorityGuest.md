# 小林 / Xiaolin (sexual-minority guest)

## Overview
小林 is a lived-experience guest who describes accepting his attraction to men, coming out to his family, and encountering gendered assumptions about male intimacy.

## Current Profile
Xiaolin reports relatively little conflict about his own orientation and locates the stronger pressure in family marriage and childbirth expectations. His account of coming out in 2023 shows disclosure as a family transition: relatives initially sought medical correction and rejected a clinician's statement that homosexuality is not an illness. He also argues that sexual minorities must first be visible before institutions and communities can respond to their practical needs.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames his own orientation as accepted rather than pathological.
- Locates major pressure in family and social expectations.
- Describes gradual disclosure followed by direct coming out.
- Uses unequal readings of male and female closeness to illustrate gender norms.
- Treats visibility as a prerequisite for practical support.
