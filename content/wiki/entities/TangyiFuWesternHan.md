## Overview

堂邑父 / Tangyi Fu is the Xiongnu-origin guide and interpreter assigned to [[ZhangQianWesternHan|张骞]]'s first mission toward [[Yuezhi|月氏]]. The current evidence gives him a direct sustainment role when provisions fail and identifies him as the only named companion who returns to Han with Zhang after the mission's repeated captivity and reported attrition.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-287-zhangqian-chushi-xiyu-huiguo-zhilu-you-duo-jianxian-1-lqvmfmtdhgtprigwri3ivpsf3hyb|Hanji 287]] places Tangyi Fu inside an expedition that must combine language, enemy-territory knowledge, navigation, and improvised subsistence. His presence shows that the mission is not carried by imperial authorization alone: it depends on portable local knowledge and survival skill before formal diplomacy can begin.

The party nevertheless suffers losses and is captured by Xiongnu cavalry. The source therefore supports an enabling rather than outcome-determinative profile: Tangyi Fu reduces some uncertainty and scarcity but cannot erase terrain, distance, surveillance, or military interception.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-287-zhangqian-chushi-xiyu-huiguo-zhilu-you-duo-jianxian-2-ll6_f8bzf3uoxglvgsetsenyuvnq|Hanji 287 part 2]] says that after the failed Yuezhi alliance and a second Xiongnu capture, only Tangyi Fu and Zhang return from the original party of more than one hundred. Han Wudi then grants Tangyi Fu the title 奉使君. The episode does not explain his separate actions during the later stages, so survival and recognition extend his profile without making him co-designer of the diplomacy.

## Key Characteristics

- Xiongnu-origin member of a Han diplomatic expedition.
- Guide and interpreter for Zhang Qian's first western mission.
- Hunter whose skill supplies food after carried provisions run out.
- Operational intermediary who links language, route knowledge, and field survival.
- Reported co-survivor of the thirteen-year mission and recipient of imperial recognition on return.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to two podcast installments. Tangyi Fu's status, name rendering, language ability, route expertise, precise hunting contribution, conduct during captivity, survival sequence, title, and subsequent life are not independently established here. His assistance does not prove that the mission had reliable maps, adequate provisions, or control over its route, while being one of two reported returnees does not by itself establish an equal diplomatic role.

## What Changed

- Added Tangyi Fu's reported return as the mission's only other named survivor and his reward as 奉使君.
- Preserved the distinction between his operational support and Zhang Qian's diplomatic leadership.
