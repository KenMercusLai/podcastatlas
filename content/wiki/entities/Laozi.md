# 老子 / Laozi

老子 / Laozi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] as a speculative comparison for [[TaishiDan|太史旦]]. The host notes that another historian with a similar-sounding name was said to have gone to [[QinState|秦国]], written roughly five thousand characters, and then disappeared.

The page records only this source-scoped association. The episode does not prove that Taishi Dan was Laozi; it uses the possibility to explain why a Zhou archivist could be imagined as a wise figure with deep historical knowledge.
