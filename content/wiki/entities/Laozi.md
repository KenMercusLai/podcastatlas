# 老子 / Laozi

## Overview
老子 / Laozi is the wiki's Daoist thinker node for a set of Ruiqi episodes that use him as speculative archivist, ethical restraint frame, strategic-adaptive metaphor, Daoist cosmology anchor, and direct teacher of [[Confucius|孔子]] in the Hanji 456 asking-the-Way sequence.

## Current Profile
Across the current sources, Laozi is less a single biographical subject than a flexible Daoist authority. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d|Zhouji 10 part 1]] keeps his possible identity with [[TaishiDan|太史旦]] tentative. Later Hanji episodes use him to judge killing, explain adaptive form, support high-dimensional consciousness language through the [[Daodejing|《道德经》]], and articulate a fuller teaching of water virtue, natural wuwei, observing sameness, and value equanimity.

## Key Characteristics
- Source-scoped identity caution: the wiki keeps Laozi's possible link to Taishi Dan as speculation rather than settled biography.
- Ethical restraint: Laozi's compassion, frugality, and not-daring-to-be-first frame is used to criticize victory without restraint.
- Adaptive strategy: water-like responsiveness becomes a metaphor for changing form with terrain, timing, and opponent behavior.
- Daoist cosmology: Laozi and the Daodejing support source-scoped claims about天人合一, high-dimensional consciousness, and perception beyond surface appearance.
- Direct teacher role: Hanji 456-2 and 456-3 present Laozi instructing Confucius beyond proud self-display and forced renyi into water virtue, natural wuwei, observing sameness, and equanimity about value pairs.

## Evidence
Identity caution:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d|Zhouji 10 part 1]] says [[TaishiDan|太史旦]] might be Laozi or a Laozi-like archivist, but the source itself treats the identification as tentative.

Ethical restraint:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-139-ta-zaici-yingxiang-hanchao-lp1zujuq0o3ohxc79xqi1phpb5zo|Hanji 139]] invokes Laozi's three treasures to read [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s [[XinanMassacre|新安坑降]] as a negative case where military boldness without compassion and restraint destroys political safety.

Adaptive strategy:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-148-1-hanxin-yupan-le-zhanghan-de-yupan-lodhpuxpossjno87gorjkfrtitbe|Hanji 148-1]] uses Laozi's water-like lack of fixed form to interpret [[HanXin|韩信]] adapting route, feint, and attack timing against [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]].

Daoist cosmology:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-161-caiyun-he-renji-guanxi-de-liangzi-jiuchan-3-ll74kq2hg2vp-zx-cqxkadxahamd|Hanji 161 part 3]] connects Laozi and the [[Daodejing|《道德经》]] to天人合一 and [[HighDimensionalConsciousnessEntanglement|高维意识纠缠]], while keeping the physics-like language source-scoped.

Direct teacher role:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-2-zhe-liangwei-shengren-de-lundao-cangzhe-renlei-de-zhongji-mimi-lsjl6awahc0gyhtwh3tagh1utqx4|Hanji 456-2]] has Laozi warn Confucius against attacking faults and exposing evils, then teach natural life and death, [[DaoistNaturalWuwei|道家自然无为]], and [[DaoistWaterVirtue|道家水德]] through the Yellow River.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-456-3-rang-ni-chushi-gengshang-yige-jingjie-laozi-jiao-ni-qiangu-zhihui-llaynmqfl8wfwo7ckkacdxommu9|Hanji 456-3]] has Laozi critique Confucius's attachment to renyi and ritual transmission, then teach [[DaoistNaturalWuwei|道家自然无为]], [[DaoistObservingSameness|观同入道]], and [[DaoistValueEquanimity|道家价值齐观]].

## Qualifications
The wiki does not treat every Laozi invocation as the same kind of evidence. Taishi Dan identity is historical speculation; Hanji 139 and 148-1 are ethical and strategic applications; Hanji 161 part 3 is a source-scoped spiritual-cosmological synthesis; Hanji 456-2 and 456-3 are instructive traditions about Confucius learning from Laozi, not verified transcripts of historical conversation.

- [[Confucius|孔子]] - learner and interlocutor in Hanji 456-3's Laozi teaching scene.
- [[TaishiDan|太史旦]] - speculative identity comparison from Zhouji 10 part 1.
- [[DaoistNaturalWuwei|道家自然无为]] - conduct teaching attributed to Laozi in Hanji 456-3.
- [[DaoistObservingSameness|观同入道]] - perception practice taught by Laozi in Hanji 456-3.
- [[DaoistValueEquanimity|道家价值齐观]] - value-pair non-attachment taught by Laozi in Hanji 456-3.
- [[DaoistWaterVirtue|道家水德]] - water-centered humility and non-contention teaching attributed to Laozi in Hanji 456-2.
