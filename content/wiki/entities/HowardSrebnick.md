# Howard Srebnick

## Overview

Howard Srebnick was a defense lawyer for [[AymanKhoury]] in [[USVKhoury|U.S. v. Khoury]].

## Current Profile

The bounded episode presents Srebnick as part of the team that accepted the risk of trying the final Varsity Blues case rather than taking a plea. He described Khoury as convinced that he had not committed a crime and helped persuade him not to testify.

## Key Characteristics

- Served alongside [[RoyBlack]] on Khoury's defense team.
- Represented a client who rejected a plea and wanted a jury trial.
- Helped persuade Khoury not to testify despite the client's stated wish to do so.
- Participated in a defense that shifted attention from the alleged exchange to Georgetown's own financial practices.
