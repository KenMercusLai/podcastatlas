# 张泉

张泉 first entered the wiki through [[kaitianchuang-moyu-ganxie-chui-dali-553650149]] as the author connected to [[HuangyeShangDeDashi|《荒野上的大师》]], though that earlier preview rendered the name as 张全. [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] upgrades the preview into a full [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] live conversation and establishes 张泉 as the corrected form used by the episode metadata and transcript.

In the full source, Zhang explains how media reporting, a story on the 鉴真纪念堂, and "重走梁思成之路" led him toward the book's larger project. His interpretation of modern Chinese scholarship emphasizes [[FieldworkAsKnowledgeMethod]], [[ScholarshipInWartime]], [[ModernChineseAcademicInstitutionBuilding]], and [[CosmopolitanChineseScholarship]] rather than monument-making around isolated masters.

## Key Claims
- 张泉 is the author of [[HuangyeShangDeDashi|《荒野上的大师》]] and the guest in episode 19.
- His account begins from concrete reporting and site visits, then scales into modern Chinese academic history.
- The earlier 张全 spelling is treated as a preview/transcription variant, not a separate person.
- He reads the modern scholarly generation through method, institution, war, and spiritual freedom rather than pure nostalgia.
