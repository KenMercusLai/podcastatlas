# 剧无霸 / Juwuba AI Studio

## Overview
剧无霸 / Juwuba AI Studio is the [[AIShortDrama|AI漫剧]] production company founded by [[MiaAIShortDrama|Mia]] in the supplied episode. It is described alongside Mia文化 and 小尾巴影视 but is the label most directly associated with the guest's AI-production work.

## Current Profile
The studio is a small-team boutique producer whose domestic economics depend disproportionately on a few hits. It develops scripts, character and scene assets, model-generated shots, animation, and post-production through specialized collaboration, while testing advertising and overseas distribution as platform revenue sharing weakens.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates a roughly 20-person AI comic-drama production team.
- Uses boutique project groups rather than a single one-click workflow for domestic work.
- Selects among models and tools based on final visual effect.
- Reports labor as the largest boutique-project cost and compute as roughly one quarter.
- Depends primarily on domestic platform revenue sharing while exploring new channels.
