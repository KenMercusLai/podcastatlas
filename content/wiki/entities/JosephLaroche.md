# Joseph Laroche

## Overview

Joseph Laroche was a Haitian engineer, described by the episode as the only Black man aboard [[RMSTitanic|RMS Titanic]], who traveled in second class with his pregnant French wife and their daughters.

## Current Profile

[[429-titanic-countdown-to-disaster-part-3-glt1273657377]] places Laroche at the intersection of education, engineering, family migration, racial discrimination, and Haitian political connection. Educated in Paris, he was returning to Haiti after encountering discrimination in France and after receiving a job opportunity connected to his uncle, the Haitian president. His family makes Titanic's second class visibly transnational and shows that movement across the Atlantic could be a return toward opportunity rather than only emigration to the United States.

## Key Characteristics

- Haitian engineer educated in Paris.
- Described as Titanic's only Black male passenger.
- Second-class traveler with his pregnant French wife and two daughters.
- Returning to Haiti after experiencing racial discrimination in France.
- Linked to a job opportunity through his uncle, the Haitian president.
- Case that widens Titanic history beyond an Anglo-American and one-directional migration frame.
