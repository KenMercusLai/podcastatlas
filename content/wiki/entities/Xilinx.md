# Xilinx / 赛灵思

Xilinx, or 赛灵思, appears in [[173-duihua-yao-song-shenjian-dongfang-kongjian-zaichufa-tiancai-shaonian-shinian-hou-1-173-1]] as the strategic acquirer of [[DeePhiTech]]. [[YaoSong]] says he deliberately favored Xilinx over other possible strategic investors because the company could become a deeper long-term business partner rather than only a brand signal.

The source frames Xilinx as a company trying to move from a device-company position toward platform-company capability. DeePhi's software, algorithms, and solution work mattered because they could fit inside that strategic change, making the acquisition a case of [[StrategicAcquirerFit]].
