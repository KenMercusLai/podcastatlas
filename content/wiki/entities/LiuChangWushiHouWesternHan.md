## Current Profile

Liu Chang's source profile is deliberately plain. When Han Wudi asks Nuo Ji's brother about him, the report says Liu Chang has no fault and also no special merit worth praising. In the source's succession logic, that very ordinariness becomes an advantage.

The episode contrasts Liu Chang with Liu Naozi. Liu Naozi's "too many desires" makes him unfit to govern, while Liu Chang's lack of obvious fault makes him acceptable as a safer Zhao successor. He therefore represents low-risk succession selection under imperial review rather than a charismatic or high-achievement candidate.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Wushi marquis selected as Zhao king in the episode.
- Chosen after Han Wudi rejects Liu Naozi's succession candidacy.
- Characterized through an external report as having no fault and no standout merit.
- Functions as a low-risk, ordinary candidate in a succession field shaped by desire control.
