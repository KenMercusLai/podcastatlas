# Nat Friedman

Nat Friedman appears in [[peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted-peter-tsr-v3-audio-converted]] through [[Segment]]'s first major sales negotiation with [[Xamarin]]. [[PeterReinhardt]] says the deal ended around $18,000 per year, a dramatic change from Segment's early $10 monthly pricing instinct.

In the wiki, Nat's source role is mainly pricing evidence. The negotiation helped Peter understand [[ProductLedWillingnessToPay]] and value-based enterprise pricing: an integration product that looked tiny from the code side could remove enough customer pain to justify a much higher annual contract.
