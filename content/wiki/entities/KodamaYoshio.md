# Kodama Yoshio / 儿玉誉士夫

Kodama Yoshio / 儿玉誉士夫 appears in [[vol-268-liang-ge-lao-si-lai-si-1003563933]] as one of the Japanese intermediaries named in the [[LockheedBriberyScandal]]. The episode groups him with [[Marubeni]] as a channel through which [[LockheedCorporation|Lockheed]] allegedly tried to influence Japanese aircraft purchasing decisions.

His source role is not a full biography. He functions as a political-brokerage node connecting U.S. aircraft exports, Japanese elite politics, and the difficulty of separating legitimate commercial representation from bribery when defense and aircraft contractors pursue overseas state-linked customers.
