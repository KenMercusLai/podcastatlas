# Brahim Ghali

Brahim Ghali enters the wiki through [[taken-littorally-spains-sudden-crisis-in-ceuta-6a70712c034f16a52ebfaed7]] as the [[PolisarioFront]] leader whose 2021 medical treatment in [[Spain]] triggered a diplomatic dispute with [[Morocco]]. The episode says Spain allowed him in without telling Morocco, after which Morocco let around 8,000 or more people arrive in [[Ceuta]].

The source uses Ghali as a hinge between [[WesternSahara]] diplomacy and [[MigrationWeaponization]]. He is not analyzed biographically; his relevance is the way his treatment made Ceuta a retaliatory border-pressure site.
