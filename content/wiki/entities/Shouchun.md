# 寿春 / Shouchun

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-982-ba-yi-ba-liubei-ru-xu-beihou-de-liyi-zhi-zheng-ljajdc95xtylhkn5teefz26yyyz1|Hanji 982]] adds the pre-coronation strategic geography and court-mission layer. The episode describes Shouchun as a Jiang-Huai node controlling north-south water routes, then places [[MaRidiLateHan|马日磾]] and [[ZhaoQiLateHan|赵岐]] there under [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s pressure before Yuan Shu's later imperial staging.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-983-zhi-hui-pianren-de-laoban-yongyuan-bushi-hao-laoban-lknr2zydwjytjo7od-rt3zi-4k-s|Hanji 983]] first places Shouchun in Yuan Shu's Jiangdong setup. [[SunCe|孙策]] goes there after entrusting his family to [[ZhangHongLateHan|张纮]], and [[YuanShu|袁术]] decides to send him back through the [[DanyangCommandery|丹阳郡]] network rather than hand over all of [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]'s old troops. The same source notes that [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]] cannot use Shouchun as the Yangzhou seat because Yuan Shu occupies it.

寿春 / Shouchun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] as the place where [[YuanShu|袁术]] declares himself emperor in 197 CE. The episode treats the location as the ritual and administrative center of Yuan Shu's attempted "仲家" regime.

At Shouchun, Yuan Shu changes offices, sets up公卿百官, and performs imperial-style sacrifices. The source uses these moves as visible proof of a title claim that outruns recognition and capacity, linking the place to [[MingqiLegitimacy|名器合法性]] and [[AntiUsurpationCoalitionPressure|讨逆同盟压力]].
