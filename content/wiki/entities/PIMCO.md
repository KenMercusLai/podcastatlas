# PIMCO

PIMCO appears in [[151-simu-xindai-private-credit-jiasu-ai-jianshe-de-tianshi-haishi-youfa-jinrong-weiji-de-emo-nlahybjwcrimit8dff7yhzpbupm1]] through the [[Meta]] data-center financing case. The source says PIMCO bought a large share of long-dated project-company debt while private-credit funds absorbed another part, making PIMCO part of the episode's [[AIDataCenterPrivateCreditFinancing]] branch.

The episode uses PIMCO less as a company profile than as a sign that AI infrastructure finance can mix private equity, private credit, and investment-grade-style fixed income. That blending matters because it spreads AI-buildout assumptions across different pools of capital and different investor risk models.
