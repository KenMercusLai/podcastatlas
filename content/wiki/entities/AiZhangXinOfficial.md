## Overview

哀章 / Ai Zhang is synthesized as a late-Western-Han opportunist who converts [[WangMang|王莽]]'s accession-stage sign politics into personal office. In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-632-shishang-zui-neng-huyou-de-dachen-lrghg2l792t4u_d3jc8ne0ngzemi|Hanji 632]], he manufactures a copper casket and Heaven-backed transfer texts, names Wang Mang's expected ministers, and inserts himself into the government he claims the signs have already authorized.

## Current Profile

Ai Zhang's importance is less his later career than the mechanism his act exposes. Once reports of new wells, stone animals, copper signs, and fuming are rewarded, an ambitious person can imitate the accepted form, attach his own name, and present self-promotion as revelation. He dresses in yellow, delivers the casket to Gaozu's temple, and succeeds because his fabrication is useful to Wang Mang at the moment when acting-emperor authority is becoming full dynastic rule.

The case also anticipates [[FumingMonopolyBackfire|符命垄断反噬]]. A sign system open enough to authorize Wang Mang can authorize its producer too; after accession, the regime will have reason to restrict the same imitative machinery.

## Key Characteristics

- Opportunistic fuming producer active at Wang Mang's 8 CE accession.
- Manufacturer of the copper casket and its two Heaven-transfer labels.
- Self-promoter who inserts his own name, office, and title into a purportedly sacred personnel list.
- Political operator whose success depends on matching an already rewarded sign-and-omen environment.
- Early example of the career-allocation risk later captured by [[FumingMonopolyBackfire|符命垄断反噬]].

## Evidence

- Casket fabrication: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-632-shishang-zui-neng-huyou-de-dachen-lrghg2l792t4u_d3jc8ne0ngzemi|Hanji 632]] says Ai Zhang makes a copper casket and labels it with Heaven-transfer and gold-casket language.
- Personnel-list manipulation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-632-shishang-zui-neng-huyou-de-dachen-lrghg2l792t4u_d3jc8ne0ngzemi|Hanji 632]] says he lists eight Wang Mang ministers, Wang Xing, Wang Sheng, and himself, with future offices and ranks.
- Temple delivery: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-632-shishang-zui-neng-huyou-de-dachen-lrghg2l792t4u_d3jc8ne0ngzemi|Hanji 632]] says he wears yellow and delivers the casket at Gaozu's temple after hearing of the new-well and stone-ox reports.
- Accession usefulness: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-632-shishang-zui-neng-huyou-de-dachen-lrghg2l792t4u_d3jc8ne0ngzemi|Hanji 632]] says Wang Mang receives the object and then proclaims that Heaven and Gaozu have transferred rule to him.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to Hanji 632. The source characterizes Ai Zhang as ill-behaved and skilled at social maneuvering, but it does not provide a complete biography, independent evidence of his inner belief, or a full account of his later Xin career. The casket and its texts are treated as fabricated political devices in the episode; exact wording, title assignments, and the identities or careers of every listed person remain source-scoped.
