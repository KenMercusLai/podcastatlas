# Ueno Chizuko / 上野千鹤子

Ueno Chizuko / 上野千鹤子 enters the wiki through [[147-zaitan-riben-yiliao-yu-zhaohu-hangye-zhi-wo-ceng-zai-beihaidao-de-yiyuan-dang-hushi-lgokb-l-nvnqsstfzlqwhtnkqzhp]] as the author of [[CareSociology|《照护社会学》]], which [[QiongQiong|琼琼]] encountered while studying and working around Japanese healthcare and care research. The episode says 琼琼 contacted Ueno by email, received permission to translate related lecture material, and later participated in pushing the book's Chinese translation and rights process.

The source uses Ueno less as a full biographical subject than as an intellectual anchor for [[CareSocialization]], [[SubjectLedCare]], and care labor recognition. Her relevance here is that care is treated as a social and political problem, not only a private family duty or a set of hospital tasks.
