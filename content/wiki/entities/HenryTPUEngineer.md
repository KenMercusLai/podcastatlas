# Henry (TPU engineer)

Henry is the former [[Google]] [[TPU]] engineer interviewed in [[e228-guge-tpu-neng-handong-yingweida-ma-qian-tpu-gongchengshi-shouci-jiemi-fd17090c-0d72-4c0d-aa3e-9b00bc062149]]. The source says he worked on several TPU generations, including [[IronwoodTPU|Ironwood]] V7 and V8-era work, and uses his engineering background to explain TPU architecture, [[XLACompiler|XLA]], [[TPUPodSystemOptimization|TPU Pod]] design, [[HighBandwidthMemory]] constraints, [[Broadcom]]'s role, and the limits of comparing a single TPU chip against a single [[Nvidia]] [[GPU]].

His source position is deliberately bounded. Henry repeatedly distinguishes what he learned from TPU engineering and colleague conversations from high-level commercial strategy, so the page should treat his comments as a practitioner model of how TPUs work rather than as a definitive [[Google]] market-share forecast.
