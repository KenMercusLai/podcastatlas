# 度尚 / Du Shang (late Han)

## Overview
Du Shang is presented in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-867-wugu-bei-po-zangshui-ta-xuanze-baqi-huiji-lqenvoy7x8ic-qn3-bf93qj8tjvk|Hanji 867]] as a late-Han commander whose useful military service does not erase his willingness to shift blame. He suppresses the [[ZhuGaiLateHan|Zhu Gai]] and [[HuLanLateHan|Hu Lan]] rebellion, but then falsely reports that Cangwu bandits entered Jingzhou in order to avoid responsibility for unfinished pacification.

## Current Profile
Du Shang's profile is mixed rather than simply villainous. [[EmperorHuanOfHan|Emperor Huan]] recalls him to [[LuoyangLateHan|Luoyang]], appoints him as 中郎将, and sends him south with more than 20,000 infantry and cavalry plus local troops. That campaign defeats Zhu Gai and Hu Lan, kills thousands of rebels, and restores Du Shang to the [[Jingzhou|Jingzhou]] inspectorate.

The same source then makes Du Shang a case of merit-protected misconduct. When [[ZhangPanLateHan|Zhang Pan]] has already suppressed Hu Lan's remnants in [[CangwuCommanderyLateHan|Cangwu]], Du Shang memorializes a false claim that Cangwu bandits entered Jingzhou. Zhang Pan forces a court confrontation, Du Shang cannot sustain the charge, and the truth becomes clear, but the court spares Du Shang because his rebellion-suppression merit and future usefulness still matter.

## Key Characteristics
- Emergency commander deployed from the center to restore order in [[Jingzhou]].
- Victory-reward beneficiary whose suppression of [[ZhuGaiLateHan|Zhu Gai]] and [[HuLanLateHan|Hu Lan]] earns renewed provincial authority.
- False accuser who turns a remaining security problem into a charge against [[ZhangPanLateHan|Zhang Pan]].
- Test case for [[MeritShieldedAccountability|Merit-Shielded Accountability / 因功免责]], where proven misconduct is softened by military value.

## Evidence
- Military deployment and victory: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-867-wugu-bei-po-zangshui-ta-xuanze-baqi-huiji-lqenvoy7x8ic-qn3-bf93qj8tjvk|Hanji 867]] reports Du Shang's appointment as 中郎将, his southern expedition, his defeat of Zhu Gai and Hu Lan, and his restoration as Jingzhou inspector.
- False report against Zhang Pan: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-867-wugu-bei-po-zangshui-ta-xuanze-baqi-huiji-lqenvoy7x8ic-qn3-bf93qj8tjvk|Hanji 867]] states that Du Shang feared blame for not eliminating all rebels and therefore misreported the Cangwu-Jingzhou movement.
- Failed confrontation: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-867-wugu-bei-po-zangshui-ta-xuanze-baqi-huiji-lqenvoy7x8ic-qn3-bf93qj8tjvk|Hanji 867]] has Du Shang summoned before Emperor Huan, unable to defeat Zhang Pan's account.
- Merit shield: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-867-wugu-bei-po-zangshui-ta-xuanze-baqi-huiji-lqenvoy7x8ic-qn3-bf93qj8tjvk|Hanji 867]] explains the court's leniency as a product of prior pacification merit and the continuing need for such talent.

## Qualifications
The transcript gives the name as 杜尚; this page normalizes the actor to 度尚 while preserving the source variant. The page is source-scoped to the Hanji 867 episode and does not claim a full biography outside the Jingzhou-Cangwu case. The court's leniency is recorded as an interpretive political outcome, not as proof that Du Shang's false report was legally excused.

## Relationships
- [[EmperorHuanOfHan|Emperor Huan of Han]] - appoints Du Shang to suppress the Jingzhou rebellion and later decides not to punish him after the false accusation is exposed.
- [[Jingzhou]] - provincial arena where Du Shang gains restored authority after suppressing Zhu Gai and Hu Lan.
- [[ZhuGaiLateHan|Zhu Gai]] - rebel opponent defeated by Du Shang's southern campaign.
- [[HuLanLateHan|Hu Lan]] - rebel opponent whose remnants create the Cangwu-Jingzhou accusation problem.
- [[ZhangPanLateHan|Zhang Pan]] - falsely accused official who forces Du Shang into a face-to-face confrontation.
- [[MeritShieldedAccountability|Merit-Shielded Accountability / 因功免责]] - concept modeled by Du Shang's spared misconduct.
- [[PardonWithoutExoneration|Pardon Without Exoneration / 赦免不等于昭雪]] - paired legal concept because Du Shang's accusation is what Zhang Pan refuses to leave unresolved.
