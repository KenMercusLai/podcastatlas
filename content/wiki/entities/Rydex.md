# Rydex

Rydex appears in [[vol-121-cong-tanhua-yixian-de-fenji-jijin-dao-fengtou-zhengjing-de-ganggan-etf-yongyuan-buyao-digu-renxing-de-fengkuang-lusagcitdozvzm8wausvecvi-qmb]] as the early leveraged-mutual-fund issuer in the episode's global history. The source says Rydex launched an early leveraged public fund tied to the S&P 500 and then an inverse public fund, before leveraged ETFs became the better-known format.

The entity matters in the wiki because it shows that retail-accessible leverage did not begin with the recent single-stock ETF boom. The same suitability problem existed once public funds started offering amplified daily exposure to ordinary investors.
