# 王龁 / Wang He

王龁 / Wang He enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-1-loxyybm3njrtnudydafwzu9jp9jz]] as the [[QinState|秦国]] general pressing [[ZhaoState|赵国]] at the opening of the 260 BCE [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] branch. The episode says Wang He continues attacking Zhao, and Zhao suffers repeated defeats, including the death of a deputy commander and several officers.

His role changes after [[ZhaoKuo|赵括]] replaces [[LianPo|廉颇]]. When [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] learns of Zhao's command change, he secretly appoints [[BaiQi|白起]] as main commander and makes Wang He the deputy. Wang He therefore marks the visible Qin front before the more dangerous hidden command upgrade.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-1-lq4wvkd8qx3q7zji6fnnkxi3qcl1]] gives Wang He the first named follow-on role after Changping. In Zhou Nan Wang's fifty-sixth year, Bai Qi orders Wang He to attack [[WuanWarringStates|武安]] and [[Pilao|皮牢]], while [[SimaGeng|司马梗]] attacks [[TaiyuanWarringStates|太原]] and [[Shangdang|上党郡]]. Wang He therefore remains part of the Qin pressure sequence even after Bai Qi's decisive command is established.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-1-lvqbmaabsvbdz2zibgmseidx5phe]] makes Wang He the practical substitute after Bai Qi refuses to take over the failing [[Handan|邯郸]] campaign from [[WangLing|王陵]]. The host reminds listeners that Wang He is already a Zhao-facing Qin commander: he fought around Shangdang, pressed [[LianPo|廉颇]], and served under Bai Qi at Changping. His appointment keeps Qin pressure on Zhao alive even without Bai Qi's consent.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-6-lteqe-0ykjtv01cmxv1fhx6z0vdi]] records the reversal of that pressure. After [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] takes [[JinBi|晋鄙]]'s Wei army and coordinates with Chu and Zhao, [[ZhengAnping|郑安平]] surrenders with 20,000 troops and Wang He retreats. Wang He therefore becomes the Qin commander whose Handan pressure survives Bai Qi's refusal but fails once the stalled coalition is forcibly activated.
