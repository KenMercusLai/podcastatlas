# 王龁 / Wang He

王龁 / Wang He enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-1-loxyybm3njrtnudydafwzu9jp9jz]] as the [[QinState|秦国]] general pressing [[ZhaoState|赵国]] at the opening of the 260 BCE [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] branch. The episode says Wang He continues attacking Zhao, and Zhao suffers repeated defeats, including the death of a deputy commander and several officers.

His role changes after [[ZhaoKuo|赵括]] replaces [[LianPo|廉颇]]. When [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] learns of Zhao's command change, he secretly appoints [[BaiQi|白起]] as main commander and makes Wang He the deputy. Wang He therefore marks the visible Qin front before the more dangerous hidden command upgrade.
