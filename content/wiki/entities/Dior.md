# Dior

## Overview
Dior is the luxury fashion house used in the source as a case of luxury brands seeking cultural relevance through film and younger audiences.

## Current Profile
The episode says Dior, under [[JonathanAnderson|Jonathan Anderson]], entered a two-year partnership with [[A24]]. The source reads the partnership against weaker luxury demand and lower attention to fashion, making Dior a case in [[LuxuryCulturalPartnership]] rather than a standalone fashion-business history.

## Key Characteristics
- Luxury fashion brand operating under the broader [[LVMH]] context in the source.
- Uses film partnership as a way to enter current culture rather than only fashion media.
- Seeks younger and aspirational consumers after luxury demand cooled.
- Source-scoped case for cultural positioning, not a full financial profile.

## Evidence
- Partnership evidence - [[tulasi-zishengtang-qisu-pingjia-shangchao-aoleqi-alo-qianyue-wangyibo-1014854463]] says Dior and A24 reached a two-year cooperation under Jonathan Anderson.
