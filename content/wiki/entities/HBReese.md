# H.B. Reese

H.B. Reese is the inventor of the Reese's Peanut Butter Cup as described in [[reeses-heir-vs-chocolate-skimpflation]]. The episode says he was born in 1879, had 16 children, worked for [[MiltonHershey]], and founded the H.B. Reese Candy Company in 1923.

The source uses Reese as the origin point for [[Reeses|Reese's]] brand identity. [[BradReese]] argues that his grandfather's product was built around milk chocolate plus peanut butter, making later labels such as [[ChocolateCompound|chocolate compound]] and peanut butter cream feel like a breach of the original product promise.

In 1963, according to the source, Reese's sons sold or merged the company with [[Hershey]] in exchange for stock and a large payout. That makes H.B. Reese a founder-history page rather than the actor directly responsible for current [[IngredientReformulationStrategy]].
