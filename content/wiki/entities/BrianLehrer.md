# Brian Lehrer

## Overview

Brian Lehrer is the interviewer who questions [[MalcolmGladwell]] about policing, gun violence, institutional trust, and the argument of [[TheAmericanWayOfKilling]].

## Current Profile

In the bounded source, Lehrer acts as a critical interlocutor rather than simply a promoter. He draws out Gladwell's claim that the United States may need more police officers while pressing it against police brutality, under-enforcement in high-crime communities, slavery's institutional legacy, and a caller's account of militarized crisis response.

## Key Characteristics

- Frames the book's central argument for a general public-radio audience.
- Tests a provocative staffing claim against brutality and community mistrust.
- Connects contemporary gun policy to historical and constitutional questions.
- Incorporates callers' experiences into the policy discussion.
- Pushes the conversation from diagnosis toward practical prevention.
