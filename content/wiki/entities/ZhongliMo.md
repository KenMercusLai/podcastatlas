# 钟离眜 / Zhongli Mo

钟离眜 / Zhongli Mo enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-160-chenping-gongxinji-bi-zhenhuanchuan-jingcai-1-lujcothn8aunydmznta1tkglqt-q|Hanji 160 part 1]] as one of the few upright, loyal figures [[ChenPing|陈平]] says remain close to [[XiangYu|项羽]]. The episode groups him with [[FanZeng|范增]], [[LongJuChuGeneral|龙且]], and [[ZhouYin|周殷]] as the trusted Chu core that Han must alienate rather than defeat only by open attack.

The source makes Zhongli Mo the first named target of Chen Ping's [[CounterintelligenceRumorWedge|反间流言楔入]]. Chen Ping's agents spread the claim that Zhongli Mo has long commanded for Xiang Yu and made great contributions without receiving a kingship, so he and other Chu generals want to join [[LiuBang|刘邦]], destroy the Xiang clan, divide the land, and become kings themselves. Xiang Yu does not immediately kill him in the episode, but the source says trust is damaged.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-160-chenping-gongxinji-bi-zhenhuanchuan-jingcai-2-lmjiydb2weahtauisouw5mdiwtoo|Hanji 160 part 2]] broadens Zhongli Mo's significance from first rumor target to member of the old Chu merit-command side of [[XiangCampFactionalCleavage|项羽阵营派系裂缝]]. The episode groups him with Fan Zeng, [[ChenYing|陈婴]], [[LongJuChuGeneral|龙且]], and [[ZhouYin|周殷]] as people whose competence keeps Chu dangerous but whose non-kin status makes them vulnerable once Xiang Yu's suspicion is activated.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-171-zhangliang-jiao-ni-ruhe-mingli-shuangshou-2-lm1iff0cadjapwf2mggwyo8wcvxn|Hanji 171 part 2]] gives Zhongli Mo a post-Xiang-Yu sequel. After Xiang Yu's death, he seeks refuge with [[HanXin|韩信]], and [[LiuBang|刘邦]] orders Han Xin to arrest him. The host reads Han Xin's sheltering as both friendship and practical Chu governance: Zhongli Mo's old Chu ties could help Han Xin manage a hostile kingdom, but the same network makes Han Xin look more dangerous to the Han center.
