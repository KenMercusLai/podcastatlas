# 颍川郡 / Yingchuan Commandery

颍川郡 / Yingchuan Commandery enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-136-meinv-xijiao-liubang-ye-hao-zhe-kouer-lplgzwzahepwx9p7krfc4x0cnz4o|Qinji 136]] as the place where [[LiuBang|刘邦]] breaks a city and massacres it during his westward campaign. The episode places the action after Liu Bang's inability to take [[Kaifeng|开封]] and his victory over [[YangXiongQinGeneral|杨熊]].

The source treats the massacre as a deliberate coercive signal. It compares Liu Bang's killing with [[XiangYu|项羽]]'s earlier massacre at [[XiangchengQin|襄城]] and argues that Liu Bang's version is more calculated: terror is used to frighten other cities into surrender and to protect campaign tempo.

For the wiki, Yingchuan therefore becomes a correction to simplified Liu Bang memory. The same line that later accepts [[WanCounty|宛县]]'s surrender and moves toward pacification also contains a [[PostBattleTerrorSignaling|战后恐怖震慑]] episode, so [[NonKillingUnification|不爱杀人者能一之]] remains comparative rather than literal innocence.
