# 秦灵公 / Duke Ling of Qin

秦灵公 / Duke Ling of Qin appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-3-ltca-ikuzo9ej7a0lfrgwv2-wulb]] as the father of [[QinXianGong|秦献公]]. The episode uses him only to explain why Qin Xian Gong has a plausible royal claim when shuzhang Gai brings him back from exile to replace [[QinChugong|秦出公]].

The page is intentionally source-scoped. It records the genealogical role needed for the Qin coup branch without expanding into a full Qin ruler biography.
