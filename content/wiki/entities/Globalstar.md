# Globalstar

Globalstar is the satellite operator [[Amazon]] is described as buying in [[tech-20260417-0417-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260417-0417-mp-tech-pod-128]]. The episode says the stock-and-cash deal is valued at about $11.6 billion and will help Amazon expand its low-earth-orbit satellite network.

Globalstar also matters because it already partners with [[Apple]] to provide satellite service for some iPhones and Apple Watches, including emergency texting when terrestrial service is unavailable. In the wiki, that makes Globalstar a bridge between consumer-device safety features, [[DirectToDeviceSatelliteConnectivity]], and [[SatelliteConnectivityCompetition]] against [[SpaceX]].
