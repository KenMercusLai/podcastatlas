# 吕臣 / Lu Chen (Qin Rebel)

吕臣 / Lu Chen enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-3-chensheng-zhisi-weishenme-dele-minxin-hanle-renxin-lnx4zs-vsi3zeoixnqskkpexmr3|Qinji 129-3]] as a former close attendant of [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] who has become a general by the time Chen Sheng dies. After [[ZhuangGu|庄谷]] kills Chen Sheng and surrenders to [[QinState|秦国]], Lu Chen raises the Cangtou Army from Xinyang.

Lu Chen retakes Chen County, kills Zhuang Gu, and reopens the Chu banner after [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]] has suffered a near-terminal collapse. He then reburies Chen Sheng at Dang County and honors him as Yin Wang. The host treats this as a modest restoration of dignity rather than a full undoing of Chen Sheng's political failure.

Lu Chen's role complicates the "everyone abandoned Chen Sheng" frame. Chen Sheng's late conduct created [[RebelFounderAlienation|起义首领人心疏离]], but Lu Chen's revenge shows that his anti-Qin symbolic value still had residual force among some followers.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-4-jiuquangui-zhangliang-ruhe-zai-liubang-shouxia-anshen-lp6qwg3ma5hdy15g4box7pe23jav|Qinji 129-4]] adds that Lu Chen retakes Chen County through coalition rather than by his own remnant force alone. He joins with Panyang and [[YingBu|英布 / 黥布]], defeats Qin troops, and again raises a Chu political line after Chen Sheng's death.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-132-5wan-wanbao-40wan-xiangyu-cizhan-fengshen-lk87nb3fskg5zh6fcgptypixhzuw|Qinji 132]] places Lu Chen in the post-[[XiangLiang|项梁]] Chu reorganization. After Xiang Liang dies at [[Dingtao|定陶]], Lu Chen joins [[XiangYu|项羽]] and [[LiuBang|刘邦]] in moving [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]] from Xuyi to [[Xuzhou|彭城]]. He is stationed east of Pengcheng while Xiang Yu is west and Liu Bang is at Dang.

Mi Xin then merges Lu Chen's army with Xiang Yu's under royal command, appoints Lu Chen as situ, and appoints Lu Chen's father Lu Qing as lingyin. The episode therefore moves Lu Chen from Chen Sheng's avenger into a visible component of the new Chu court order.
