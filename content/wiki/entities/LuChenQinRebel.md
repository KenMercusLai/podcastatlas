# 吕臣 / Lu Chen (Qin Rebel)

吕臣 / Lu Chen enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-3-chensheng-zhisi-weishenme-dele-minxin-hanle-renxin-lnx4zs-vsi3zeoixnqskkpexmr3|Qinji 129-3]] as a former close attendant of [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] who has become a general by the time Chen Sheng dies. After [[ZhuangGu|庄谷]] kills Chen Sheng and surrenders to [[QinState|秦国]], Lu Chen raises the Cangtou Army from Xinyang.

Lu Chen retakes Chen County, kills Zhuang Gu, and reopens the Chu banner after [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]] has suffered a near-terminal collapse. He then reburies Chen Sheng at Dang County and honors him as Yin Wang. The host treats this as a modest restoration of dignity rather than a full undoing of Chen Sheng's political failure.

Lu Chen's role complicates the "everyone abandoned Chen Sheng" frame. Chen Sheng's late conduct created [[RebelFounderAlienation|起义首领人心疏离]], but Lu Chen's revenge shows that his anti-Qin symbolic value still had residual force among some followers.
