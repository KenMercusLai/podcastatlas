# 孙翊 / Sun Yi (late Han)

## Overview
孙翊 / Sun Yi is a Sun-family son whose profile moves from family-network marker to victim of the Jian'an 9 [[DanyangCoupLateHan|丹阳郡变]]. Earlier sources place him inside [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]] and [[LadyWuSunJian|吴夫人]]'s household and [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s hostage-like court-service leverage over [[SunCe|孙策]]; Hanji 1067 adds his murder in [[DanyangCommandery|丹阳郡]] and [[LadyXuSunYi|徐夫人]]'s revenge.

## Current Profile
Sun Yi first appears as part of the Sun household. Hanji 982 names him among Sun Jian and Lady Wu's children, while Hanji 1028 places him with [[SunQuan|孙权]] in Cao Cao's request that younger Sun-family members serve at court, a move the source reads as a recognition package with hostage-like pressure.

Hanji 1067 gives him an independent late-Han event role. The source says Sun Yi is serving in Danyang when [[GuiLanLateHan|妫览]] and [[DaiYuanLateHan|戴员]] buy off his intimate [[BianHongLateHan|边鸿]]. During a banquet for county officials, Bian Hong kills him from behind as Sun Yi rises to see guests off. The conspirators' attempted madness cover story fails before Lady Xu's reading of the plot, and his death becomes the trigger for the revenge ambush that kills Gui Lan and Dai Yuan.

## Key Characteristics
- Belongs to the Sun Jian-Lady Wu family network that becomes the basis of Jiangdong power.
- Is named with Sun Quan in Cao Cao's court-service summons environment, showing how younger Sun-family members could become leverage against Sun Ce.
- Holds enough Danyang authority for his murder to become a local political crisis rather than only a household tragedy.
- Is killed through intimate access at a public banquet, showing vulnerability inside his own circle.
- Becomes the posthumous focus of Lady Xu's ritualized revenge against Gui Lan and Dai Yuan.

## Qualifications
Hanji 1067's supplied transcript writes the name as 孙逸, but the surrounding event, relationships, and established index route identify this as Sun Yi / 孙翊. The page remains bounded to the sources' limited profile: family position, hostage-like leverage, Danyang office exposure, murder, and posthumous revenge.
