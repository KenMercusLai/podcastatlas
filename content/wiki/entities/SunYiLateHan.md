# 孙翊 / Sun Yi (late Han)

孙翊 / Sun Yi enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-982-ba-yi-ba-liubei-ru-xu-beihou-de-liyi-zhi-zheng-ljajdc95xtylhkn5teefz26yyyz1|Hanji 982]] as one of [[LadyWuSunJian|吴夫人]] and [[SunJianLateHan|孙坚]]'s sons, named alongside [[SunCe|孙策]], [[SunQuan|孙权]], Sun Kuang, and a daughter. The source uses him as part of the Sun family network rather than as an active actor.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1028-xiaobawang-sunce-liwei-chengba-jiangdong-lgur-3fmpr55wy3aji-0rm38y7x|Hanji 1028]] adds Sun Yi to [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s leverage over Sun Ce. After Cao Cao recommends Sun Ce for titles and marriage ties, he asks that Sun Quan and Sun Yi come to the capital as officeholders; the episode reads this as carrying a hostage-like function inside the Han-court recognition package.
