苏文 / Su Wen enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-391-1-wei-le-li-taizi-hanwudi-weihe-xian-sha-chongfei-gouyi-furen-lhsva8zj7n7mfy6u4vyfhrwmbwzg|Hanji 391-1]] as a named front-line actor in the [[WuguIncidentWesternHan|巫蛊之祸]] accusation field against [[LiuJuCrownPrinceWesternHan|卫太子刘据]]. ## Current Profile

The sources give Su Wen only a narrow role. He is named alongside [[JiangChongWesternHan|江充]] as one of the visible actors involved in the frame-up of Liu Ju, while the host argues that [[LadyGouyiWesternHan|钩弋夫人]] was the deeper hidden mover. Hanji 388-2 then makes Su Wen a visible target of Wudi's remorse-driven punishment. Su Wen therefore functions here as part of the operational and punitive layer of the Wugu explanation, not as a fully profiled independent figure.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han palace/court actor named in the episode's account of the Wugu affair.
- Paired with Jiang Chong as a visible participant in the alleged frame-up of Liu Ju.
- Punished in Hanji 388-2 after Han Wudi accepts Liu Ju's grievance.
- Source-scoped actor whose full biography is not developed by this episode.
