## Current Profile

The sources give Su Wen only a narrow but now earlier-anchored role. Hanji 384-2 shows him already using palace access to feed damaging moral suspicion about Liu Ju before the formal Wugu machinery appears; Liu Ju and Wei Zifu know the danger, but Liu Ju refuses to preemptively remove him because he trusts Wudi's discernment. Hanji 391-1 then names Su Wen alongside [[JiangChongWesternHan|江充]] as one of the visible actors involved in the frame-up of Liu Ju, while the host argues that [[LadyGouyiWesternHan|钩弋夫人]] was the deeper hidden mover. Hanji 388-2 finally makes Su Wen a visible target of Wudi's remorse-driven punishment. Su Wen therefore functions here as part of the pre-crisis slander, operational accusation, and punitive aftermath layers of the Wugu explanation, not as a fully profiled independent figure.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han palace/court actor named in the episode's account of the Wugu affair.
- Early palace-access slanderer whose report about Liu Ju's late visit to Wei Zifu tests Han Wudi's perception of the heir before the Wugu case.
- Paired with Jiang Chong as a visible participant in the alleged frame-up of Liu Ju and as the survivor whose report helps the rebellion frame reach Wudi.
- Punished in Hanji 388-2 after Han Wudi accepts Liu Ju's grievance.
- Source-scoped actor whose full biography is not developed by this episode.
