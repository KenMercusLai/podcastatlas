# Henry VI of England

## Overview

Henry VI was the child king whose inherited claim to both [[England]] and [[France]] made [[JoanOfArc|Joan of Arc]] a direct threat to Lancastrian legitimacy.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Henry as nine years old and politically unimpressive when brought to France. His government needed a French coronation to answer [[CharlesVIIOfFrance|Charles VII]]'s anointing at Reims, but Reims was unavailable and Paris unstable; Joan's condemnation therefore became a substitute symbolic victory for a regime unable to erase Charles's rival kingship by ceremony or conquest.

Henry was crowned at Notre-Dame six and a half months after Joan's execution. The chronology joins his title to the English-sponsored prosecution without making the child king the operational author of the proceedings conducted in his name.

## Key Characteristics

- Child inheritor of the unfinished [[AngloFrenchDualMonarchy|Anglo-French dual monarchy]].
- Rival claimant whose French authority suffered beside Charles VII's Reims coronation.
- Monarch in whose name the proclamation against Joan was issued.
- King crowned in Paris only after Joan's execution.

## Evidence

- Weak French position: [[635-joan-of-arc-for-fear-of-the-flames-part-4-glt8113477235]] describes Henry's youth, muted Rouen reception, and the difficulty of arranging a coronation equal to Charles's.
- Trial connection: [[635-joan-of-arc-for-fear-of-the-flames-part-4-glt8113477235]] records the January 1431 proclamation in Henry's name and the Lancastrian interest in discrediting Joan.
- Delayed coronation: [[635-joan-of-arc-for-fear-of-the-flames-part-4-glt8113477235]] places Henry's Notre-Dame coronation six and a half months after her death.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to Henry's place in Joan's prosecution and the contested French succession. It does not cover his later English reign, incapacity, deposition, restoration, or death. Measures issued in a child king's name should not be treated as proof of his personal initiative or settled private beliefs.
