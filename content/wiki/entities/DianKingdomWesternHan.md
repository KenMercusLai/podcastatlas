## Overview

滇国 / Dian Kingdom is the southwestern polity reached by Han envoys while they are trying to discover a route from Shu and Qianwei toward [[ShenduWesternHan|身毒]].

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-306-2-hanchao-diyiwei-zouxiang-shijie-de-zhongguoren-lhm5be2xdxbj0v5zmkbqdoguf1z|Hanji 306-2]] presents Dian as an unintended intelligence gain from a failed long-distance route search. The ruler receives Wang Ranyu and other Han envoys, asks about Dian's size relative to Han, and sends assistance to look for a westward passage. Resistance associated with Kunming prevents the party from reaching its intended destination.

The episode consequently makes Dian strategically visible to the Han court. Envoys report it as a significant Southwestern Yi polity worth drawing into submission, shifting an unsuccessful search for a distant route into a new regional policy interest. Reception and assistance do not themselves demonstrate submission, territorial control, or accurate mutual knowledge.

## Key Characteristics

- Southwestern polity reached during Han reconnaissance from Shu and Qianwei.
- Court that receives and hosts Han envoys rather than simply blocking them.
- Participant in an attempted westward route search that fails beyond Dian.
- Information-limited ruler whose reported comparison question is framed by geographic isolation.
- Unintended strategic focus produced by a mission aimed farther west.

## Evidence

Reception and route assistance:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-306-2-hanchao-diyiwei-zouxiang-shijie-de-zhongguoren-lhm5be2xdxbj0v5zmkbqdoguf1z|Hanji 306-2]] says Wang Ranyu, Bo Shichang, Lü Yueren, and other envoys reach Dian, are retained as guests, and receive help searching for a westward road.

Limits and strategic redirection:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-306-2-hanchao-diyiwei-zouxiang-shijie-de-zhongguoren-lhm5be2xdxbj0v5zmkbqdoguf1z|Hanji 306-2]] says Kunming resistance prevents onward travel and that the returned envoys encourage Han Wudi to seek Dian's submission.

## Qualifications

This page rests on one compact podcast summary and does not reconstruct Dian's chronology, archaeology, political institutions, borders, ruler sequence, relationship to other Southwestern Yi groups, or eventual incorporation into Han administration. The title “滇王长羌,” the named envoys, exact itinerary, royal dialogue, size comparison, assistance, Kunming obstruction, and court recommendation remain episode-attributed. The ruler's question demonstrates the episode's information-limit theme but does not by itself prove arrogance or ignorance beyond the encounter as narrated.

- Distinguished diplomatic reception and route assistance from submission or Han control.
