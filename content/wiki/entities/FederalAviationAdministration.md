# Federal Aviation Administration

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-the-future-of-everything-what-ceos-of-circle-crowdstrike-more-see-coming-in-2026-39870920]] adds the Federal Aviation Administration as the implied regulator in [[ArcherAviation|Archer Aviation]]'s eVTOL certification path. [[AdamGoldsteinArcher|Adam Goldstein]] says certification is the hardest commercialization step, and that autonomy depends on regulation and air-traffic infrastructure.

The Federal Aviation Administration appears in [[omission-accomplished-why-the-iran-war-cycle-spins-on-6a6c7475aa1642a30f17f1cb]] through [[WallyFunk]]'s post-testing aviation career. The episode says Funk worked for the agency by instructing pilots and inspecting airplanes.

In this source, the FAA is not analyzed as a regulator. It is evidence that Funk remained a professional aviation pioneer even after gender barriers blocked her from the official astronaut corps.
