# Kao Hsinchu Precision Cleaning Center / 花王新竹精密洗净中心

Kao Hsinchu Precision Cleaning Center / 花王新竹精密洗净中心 is the Taiwan facility described in [[mai-xiangzao-qijia-de-bainian-huawang-weishenme-chengle-bandaoti-zhizao-de-guanjiang-qiye-1004213131]]. The episode presents it as [[Kao|花王]]'s second global precision-cleaning base after the Wakayama factory in [[Japan]], and its first such overseas base.

The center matters because Hsinchu sits near major semiconductor actors such as [[TSMC]] and [[MediaTek]]. According to the source, Kao can use the facility to simulate customer production environments, diagnose yield-affecting residue or particle problems, and tune cleaning formulas before or alongside fab validation.

The page is a concrete case of [[ChemicalControlAsManufacturingCapability|chemical control as manufacturing capability]]. Precision cleaning is not only a catalog chemical sale; it becomes embedded in customer process flows, where changing suppliers can require revalidation and raise switching costs.
