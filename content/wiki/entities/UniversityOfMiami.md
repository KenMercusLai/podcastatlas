# University of Miami

## Overview

The University of Miami appears as the institutional base for [[AziziSeixas]]'s digital-health, sleep, population-health, and decentralized monitoring work.

## Current Profile

In the episode, the university receives data from [[TheMailboxHealthMonitoring|The Mailbox]] devices through provided phones and a command center, then connects concerning findings to clinical follow-up. Its pilot is described as working with urban and rural participants, community health centers, and federally qualified health centers.

The account presents an integrated research-and-response system, but the supplied source does not establish university-wide practice or independently evaluate the program.

## Key Characteristics

- Hosts interdisciplinary health-data and digital-transformation work.
- Operates the command-center layer described for the Mailbox pilot.
- Links home sensor data to physician and specialist escalation.
- Partners with community and federally qualified health centers.
- Uses community-based research to address South Florida and broader access gaps.
