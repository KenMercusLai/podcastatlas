## Overview

谷永 / Gu Yong (Western Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-552-tongtou-renxing-de-ren-zhichang-pingbuqingyun-lnnhr8e-67qdqwhzi1h0qav-valw|Hanji 552]] as a Chengdi-era official whose career is read through patronage stigma and ruler distrust. The source says he had been recommended through the Wang-family side, repeatedly memorialized against [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]] and his inner-palace conduct, then was allowed to retire during illness and died a few months later. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-543-citou-yuangong-tai-nan-guan-yonghao-zhezhao-qingsong-rang-ta-furuan-lm9_0r8mnwcrpqukmsmoxwndzwy0|Hanji 543]] adds an earlier anti-fangshu layer: Gu Yong warns Chengdi that claims about immortals, elixirs, and gold-making are deceptive and costly when ritual anxiety is rising.

## Current Profile

Hanji 552 uses Gu Yong as the opening example before the Kong Guang succession case. In the episode's workplace language, relying on a visible backer is unsafe if the real decision-maker distrusts that patronage line. Gu Yong's link to the Wang family and his unwelcome memorials make Chengdi disinclined to trust him, even if the office form remains ordinary.

The source also stresses reputation memory. It says later historians judge Gu Yong harshly because his earlier path upward involved attaching himself to [[WangFengWesternHan|王凤]] and reversing moral language for advancement. Gu Yong therefore functions less as a full biography here than as a caution about how early court alignment shapes later credibility.

Hanji 543 shows Gu Yong in a different advisory register. After Chengdi restores major sacrifices following an eclipse and violent wind at Ganquan, fangshu petitioners increase around the emperor's childlessness. Gu Yong's memorial rejects the authority of those who promise immortals, deathlessness, and transmutation, placing him beside [[FangshiFraudAndAuthority|方士骗术与权威]] as a court skeptic rather than only a patronage-stigma example.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official under [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]].
- Recommended through Wang-family influence, making him suspect to Chengdi.
- Memorializes repeatedly against Chengdi and palace conduct, which the source says Chengdi dislikes.
- Warns Chengdi against fangshi claims about immortals, elixirs, and gold-making after sacrifice restoration increases ritual and specialist petitions.
- Allowed to retire during illness and dies soon after.
- Used by the episode as a patronage and reputation case: earlier attachment to power can make later criticism easier to dismiss.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 552 and Hanji 543 and does not reconstruct Gu Yong's full biography, offices, memorial texts, or standard-history evaluation. The source transcript writes the name as "古勇"; this page normalizes to 谷永 because the Chengdi-era official, Wang Feng association, and anti-fangshu Chengdi memorial fit the known Western Han figure. The existing [[GuYongLateHan|Gu Yong (late Han)]] page is a separate person and should not be merged.
