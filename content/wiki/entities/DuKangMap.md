# 督亢地图 / Du Kang Map

督亢地图 / Du Kang Map enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-3-jingke-ciqin-weihe-jiujiu-bu-xingdong-zai-deng-shui-liihe-kgwigu3bggxmyofgvotged]] as one of the two gifts [[JingKe|荆轲]] says he needs before approaching [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]. Together with [[FanWuji|樊无忌]]'s head, the map is meant to make Jing Ke's envoy role credible and valuable enough to bring him physically close to the Qin king.

The episode treats the map differently from Fan Wuji's head. [[YanTaiziDan|燕太子丹]] can provide the map without the same moral crisis, while Fan Wuji's death forces the plan to consume a protected guest. The pair still operates together as a single access design: territorial submission plus enemy fugitive trophy.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-115-1-jingke-ciqin-sihu-you-dianer-caoshuai-le-lgz1adsn1t8jjiaobmtfedpr-ixn]] shows the map inside the Qin audience ritual. In the staged surrender script sent through [[MengJia|蒙嘉]], the map signals that [[YanState|燕国]] is willing to accept Qin's real administrative control. [[QinWuyang|秦武阳]] initially carries it behind [[JingKe|荆轲]], but after he panics at the palace steps, Jing Ke takes the map and proceeds alone.
