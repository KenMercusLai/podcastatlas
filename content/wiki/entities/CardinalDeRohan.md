# Cardinal de Rohan

## Overview

Louis de Rohan was the aristocratic cardinal whose desire to regain [[MarieAntoinette|Marie Antoinette]]'s favor made him the essential deceived intermediary in the [[DiamondNecklaceAffair|Diamond Necklace Affair]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Rohan as high-born, generous, extravagant, proud, credulous, and politically careless. Marie Antoinette disliked him because of his earlier conduct toward her mother and his gossip about the royal family. [[JeanneDeLaMotte|Jeanne de la Motte]] converted that exclusion into leverage: a staged garden audience and forged letters persuaded him that the queen secretly wanted reconciliation and an enormous necklace. His acceptance of an implausible royal signature and unverified courier shows motivated belief rather than a technically sophisticated forgery. After public arrest and imprisonment in the Bastille, his acquittal allowed him to appear as a victim of arbitrary royal power and deepened the monarchy's reputational loss.

## Key Characteristics

- Member of a powerful family whose status did not protect him from court exclusion or manipulation.
- Desired restored access to the queen strongly enough to suspend ordinary scrutiny.
- Believed in occult and alchemical claims associated with Cagliostro, reinforcing the episode's broader atmosphere of credulity.
- Served as the respectable intermediary who made the necklace transfer appear royal to the jewelers.
- Became a sympathetic public victim after arrest, Bastille confinement, and acquittal.

## Qualifications

Rohan's gullibility does not make him an author of de la Motte's fraud, and acquittal did not prove the queen participated. The source's personality judgments, claims about his affair with de la Motte, and stories about Cagliostro's séances remain source-scoped. Public sympathy also reflected hostility to the crown and the Bastille, not a neutral audit of his judgment.

## What Changed

- Created a profile centered on motivated credulity, intermediary authority, and post-arrest political symbolism.
- Distinguished legal acquittal from validation of the false royal story.
