# 安国君柱 / 秦孝文王 / King Xiaowen of Qin

安国君柱 / 秦孝文王 / King Xiaowen of Qin appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-89-qinwang-jing-bei-ta-dui-mabaonan-ljugedx8qxhtuzd1ja-xiwxgse19]] as the son [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] establishes as crown prince in 265 BCE after [[QinDaotaizi|秦悼太子]] has died in [[WeiState|魏国]].

The source uses him mainly to make the prior hostage death legible. His appointment suggests that Qin's succession line is being reset while [[FanJu|范雎]] is pushing Qin toward nearby attacks and while court power remains tangled with [[XuanTaihou|宣太后]], [[WeiRan|魏冉]], and the [[QinFourNoblesCourtPower|秦国四贵权力结构]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-91-chulong-ruhe-qiaokai-shougua-zhaotaihou-de-men-1-lmgd7kdl1nrfwcrtkqttumtlpqok]] repeats the 265 BCE appointment after [[XuanTaihou|宣太后]]'s death and [[WeiRan|魏冉]]'s removal. It adds the biographical hint that Anguo Jun is already an adult with more than twenty sons, letting the host foreshadow [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] and the coming Qin succession chain.
