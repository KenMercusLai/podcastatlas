# Buzz Aldrin

## Overview
Buzz Aldrin was the lunar module pilot of [[Apollo11]] and the second person to step onto the [[Moon]].

## Current Profile
[[438-the-moonwalkers-with-tom-hanks-glt1422432857]] places Aldrin at the meeting point of technical work, personal meaning, and astronaut competition. His private bread-and-wine observance, the ambition shared across the astronaut corps to be first, and his position inside Eagle complicate a story often reduced to a ranking of footsteps.

## Key Characteristics
- Served as Apollo 11 lunar module pilot.
- Landed and worked on the lunar surface with [[NeilArmstrong]].
- Marked the landing privately with bread and wine.
- Participated in a culture combining intense competition with respect for demonstrated ability.
- Returned through an ascent-and-rendezvous chain dependent on [[MichaelCollins]].
