# Jozef Tiso

## Overview

Jozef Tiso was a Catholic priest and Slovak political leader whom Hitler pressured into declaring Slovak independence under German protection during the March 1939 destruction of Czechoslovakia.

## Current Profile

The episode characterizes Tiso as ultra-conservative and antisemitic, serving as prime minister of autonomous Slovakia after Munich. Hitler brought him to Berlin on 13 March and presented independence under German protection as the alternative to possible Hungarian territorial seizure.

Tiso did not immediately request protection. The episode says German military pressure against Bratislava followed, after which he asked for it. His role therefore combines ideological affinity and leadership of a dependent clerico-fascist state with coercion inside Germany's plan to dismantle Czechoslovakia.

## Key Characteristics

- Catholic priest and leader of autonomous Slovakia.
- Ultra-conservative and antisemitic politics in the episode's characterization.
- Pressured to choose German-protected independence under threat of Hungarian gains.
- Initially stopped short of requesting German protection.
- Later leader within a dependent Slovak state created through Czechoslovakia's destruction.
