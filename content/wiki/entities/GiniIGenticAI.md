# Gini (iGentic AI)

## Overview
Gini is the enterprise governance platform attributed to [[IGenticAI|iGentic AI]] in [[ep-52-the-ai-ceo-leadership-governance-and-the-ai-gold-rush|Data Science With Sam EP52]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Gini at the level of product purpose rather than architecture: it is intended to help enterprises make compliance an enabler of AI adoption. That places the product between leadership demand for faster deployment and the need for controls, accountability, and regulated-industry trust.

## Key Characteristics
- Enterprise governance platform described by iGentic AI's co-founder.
- Intended to connect compliance with practical AI adoption.
- Positioned for organizations where regulation and accountability shape deployment.
- Presented as part of a problem-first, measurable-value company strategy.
