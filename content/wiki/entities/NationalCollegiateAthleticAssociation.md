# National Collegiate Athletic Association / NCAA

## Overview
The National Collegiate Athletic Association / NCAA appears in this source as the U.S. college-sports structure that helps convert school identity into year-round attention, local fandom, and licensed merchandise demand.

## Current Profile
The episode does not profile the NCAA as a governance body in depth. It uses NCAA scale to explain why U.S. university logos travel far beyond current students: thousands of teams, many sports, national broadcasts, local loyalty, alumni identity, and family affiliation all make school names wearable signals.

## Key Characteristics
- Supplies the sports calendar and team visibility behind much of U.S. collegiate merchandise demand.
- Connects universities to community identity in places without comparable professional sports teams.
- Makes school marks socially legible through games, broadcasts, rankings, rituals, and rivalries.
- Turns students, alumni, families, and local supporters into overlapping buyer groups.
- Concentrates demand around highly visible sports programs, leaving smaller schools with thinner merchandise economics.

## Evidence
- Scale evidence: [[tulasi-ba-xiaohui-yinshang-weiyi-t-xu-meiguo-daxue-ruhe-ba-shouquan-xiaoming-zuocheng-yimen-shengyi-1014464166]] says roughly 1,100 of more than 3,600 U.S. colleges are NCAA members.
- Calendar evidence: [[tulasi-ba-xiaohui-yinshang-weiyi-t-xu-meiguo-daxue-ruhe-ba-shouquan-xiaoming-zuocheng-yimen-shengyi-1014464166]] cites more than 550,000 student athletes and nearly 20,000 teams in the 2024-2025 academic year.
- Demand evidence: [[tulasi-ba-xiaohui-yinshang-weiyi-t-xu-meiguo-daxue-ruhe-ba-shouquan-xiaoming-zuocheng-yimen-shengyi-1014464166]] connects college football viewership and claimed fan counts to annual purchases of team merchandise.

## Qualifications
The source uses NCAA figures to explain retail demand and does not analyze NCAA governance, athlete compensation, conference realignment, legal disputes, or academic-sport tensions. The fan and purchase-rate figures remain source-scoped.
