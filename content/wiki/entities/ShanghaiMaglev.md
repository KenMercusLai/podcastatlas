# Shanghai Maglev / 上海磁悬浮

Shanghai Maglev is the short commercial maglev line used in [[no-201-zhongguo-gaotie-jianshi-976314772]] as the concrete demonstration of China's early magnetic-levitation route. The episode says the line began construction in 2001, entered commercial service in 2003, ran at roughly 430 km/h in operation, and was technologically impressive but costly.

The source's point is not that maglev had no value. It uses Shanghai Maglev to explain [[WheelRailVsMaglev]]: a high-speed isolated line could prove speed but still fail to solve national railway interoperability, trunk-line financing, and network rollout. That makes the project a useful case for [[InfrastructureMalinvestment]] and opportunity cost without reducing it to simple failure.
