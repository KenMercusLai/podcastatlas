# Sudanese Armed Forces

The Sudanese Armed Forces, or SAF, are one of the two main armed camps in [[Sudan]]'s war as described in [[outsider-chance-ending-sudans-proxy-war-6a75b3695d765781a2b1b132]]. The episode says the SAF retook [[Khartoum]] from the [[RapidSupportForces|RSF]] and controls much of northern and eastern Sudan.

The source links recent SAF battlefield gains to outside support, especially weapons and drones from [[Turkey]], while also naming [[SaudiArabia]] and [[Egypt]] as supporters. That makes the SAF part of [[ForeignBackedCivilWar]] as well as a possible participant in any externally brokered ceasefire.

## Connections
- [[Sudan]], [[Khartoum]], and [[ElObeid]] - core theaters in the source.
- [[RapidSupportForces]] - opposing armed camp.
- [[Turkey]], [[SaudiArabia]], and [[Egypt]] - states described as supporting the army.
- [[ForeignBackedCivilWar]], [[LowCostDroneWarfare]], and [[FragmentedCeasefireEnforcement]] - concepts linked to the SAF branch.
