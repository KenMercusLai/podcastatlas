# al-Idrisi / 伊德里希

## Overview
al-Idrisi / 伊德里希 is a Ceuta-born medieval geographer whose work for Roger II of Sicily supports both the wiki's Mediterranean knowledge-network history and its earliest documentary evidence for dried pasta on Italian soil.

## Current Profile
The Ceuta source emphasizes al-Idrisi's geographic and cultural-translation role. Working in Norman Sicily, he gathered world geographic knowledge for Roger II, produced a silver world map that no longer survives, and completed the Book of Roger. The Italian-food source adds a concrete economic observation from 1154: large-scale production near Trabia of durum-wheat dried pasta called *ytria*, which was exported beyond Sicily.

## Key Characteristics
- Ceuta-born geographer tied to medieval Mediterranean knowledge circulation.
- Worked in Sicily, a contact zone among Catholic, Byzantine Orthodox, and Islamic cultures in the source.
- Produced geographic work that preserves commercial and food-production evidence as well as maps and place knowledge.
- Represents Ceuta's cultural and scholarly importance beyond military value.

- Knowledge-network evidence: [[496-yiminchao-beihou-de-xiuda-cong-gudian-shidai-manzu-duhai-yibiliya-shoufu-shidi-yundong-dao-putaoya-dahanghai-lu8pawnkiselmmrgubrey4pq-4fw]] places him in Norman Sicily and the Roger II project.
- Textual evidence: [[496-yiminchao-beihou-de-xiuda-cong-gudian-shidai-manzu-duhai-yibiliya-shoufu-shidi-yundong-dao-putaoya-dahanghai-lu8pawnkiselmmrgubrey4pq-4fw]] says the Book of Roger survives and opens with a spherical-earth description.
- Food-production evidence: [[484-the-food-that-changed-the-world-glt2872429496]] attributes to al-Idrisi a 1154 report of dried-pasta production and export near Palermo.

## Qualifications
This page is source-bounded and does not attempt a full biography or textual history of al-Idrisi. The pasta claim is reported through a modern interview rather than a direct reading of the relevant manuscript passage, and “first documentary evidence” does not mean the product was invented at that moment.
