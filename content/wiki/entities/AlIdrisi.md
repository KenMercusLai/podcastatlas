# al-Idrisi / 伊德里希

## Overview
al-Idrisi / 伊德里希 appears in the Ceuta episode as the Ceuta-born medieval geographer whose work for Roger II of Sicily becomes evidence that [[Ceuta]] belonged to a wider Mediterranean knowledge network.

## Current Profile
The source emphasizes al-Idrisi's geographic and cultural-translation role. Working in Norman Sicily, he gathered world geographic knowledge for Roger II, produced a silver world map that no longer survives, and completed the Book of Roger, which the episode uses to show medieval geographic sophistication and cross-cultural knowledge exchange.

## Key Characteristics
- Ceuta-born geographer tied to medieval Mediterranean knowledge circulation.
- Worked in Sicily, a contact zone among Catholic, Byzantine Orthodox, and Islamic cultures in the source.
- Represents Ceuta's cultural and scholarly importance beyond military value.
