# Palestinians

## Overview
Palestinians are a people whose wiki profile connects 1948 displacement and refugee claims to differentiated citizenship, occupation, Gaza governance, West Bank land access, and the unresolved Palestinian question in Israeli politics.

## Current Profile
The sources reject treating Palestinians as a single legal or political bloc. Refugees, Palestinian citizens of Israel, East Jerusalem residents, West Bank communities, and Gazans occupy different regimes, while Fatah-Hamas rivalry and displacement complicate collective representation. Across the sources, limited autonomy and diplomatic formulas remain constrained by Israeli territorial control, settlement growth, disarmament sequencing, and election incentives.

## Key Characteristics
- Shared national identity is shaped by the Nakba, exile, occupation, and return claims.
- Legal rights vary across Israeli citizenship, East Jerusalem residency, West Bank administration, Gaza blockade, and refugee status.
- West Bank communities face settlement outposts, land-access loss, settler violence, and inconsistent enforcement.
- Gazans face displacement, territorial division, war damage, and unresolved postwar governance.
- Political representation is divided between Hamas, Fatah, technocratic proposals, and wider civil society.

## Evidence
- Historical displacement and status: [[ep177-ba-yi-chongtu-women-gai-ruhe-lijie-yaoyuan-de-kusheng-ckwriaiepeo1abaaaac8efxz]] links the Nakba and right of return to four differentiated present-day status groups.
- West Bank land conflict: [[settler-bet-a-west-bank-flashpoint-6a842bb539e73ea8f3c13a2d]] describes villagers losing access after outposts and using video to attract outside attention.
- Gaza governance and displacement: [[in-arms-way-gaza-deal-sticking-points-6a71b49647235c354d3c9b74]] makes Palestinian residence, territorial control, technocratic administration, and Hamas disarmament implementation questions.
- Israeli political avoidance: [[youve-come-a-long-way-bibi-israels-crucial-election-6a59f9ae461a6a41901ae410]] calls the Palestinian issue central while describing campaign reluctance to address it.

## Qualifications
The sources emphasize structures of domination and implementation but do not represent the full diversity of Palestinian political preferences, class, religion, diaspora experience, local governance, or attitudes toward one-state and two-state outcomes. Claims about 1948 and apartheid analogy are especially source-scoped.

## Relationships
- [[Israel]] - dominant state actor governing or constraining major dimensions of Palestinian land, movement, residence, and security.
- [[Hamas]] - armed and political movement governing part of Gaza and contesting representation.
- [[Fatah]] - rival political movement criticized by the source for corruption and limited resistance legitimacy.
- [[Gaza]] - territory where blockade, war, displacement, and governance converge.
- [[WestBank]] - territory where settlements and fragmented control shape daily access.
- [[Nakba]] - foundational displacement memory and refugee history.
- [[PalestinianRightOfReturn]] - continuing remedy and demographic dispute.
