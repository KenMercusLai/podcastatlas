# Jeffrey Katzenberg

Jeffrey Katzenberg is the studio leader [[MichaelEisner]] brings from Paramount to Disney in [[disney-the-renaissance-and-the-empire-1]]. The source credits him with pushing Disney animation toward sharper process, debate, and story structure during the period that becomes the [[DisneyRenaissance]].

The episode does not make Katzenberg the sole creative source of the renaissance. It also emphasizes the creative roles of artists, directors, and musical-theater collaborators. His departure after [[FrankWells]]'s death, lawsuit against Disney, and role in founding DreamWorks mark the point where Disney's revived animation engine also begins producing a serious competitor.
