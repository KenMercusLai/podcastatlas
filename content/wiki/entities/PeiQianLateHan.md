# 裴潜 / Pei Qian (late Han)

## Overview
裴潜 / Pei Qian is a Hedong scholar whose judgment that [[LiuBiao|刘表]] lacks the capacity for hegemony leads him away from the Jingzhou court and eventually into [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s administration.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1096 presents Pei Qian as respected by Liu Biao but privately skeptical of the ruler's ambitions. He judges that Liu Biao compares himself to a hegemonic founder without possessing the capacity to sustain that role, and moves south toward Changsha rather than remaining close to the court. After Cao Cao takes Jingzhou, the episode places Pei Qian in the Cao-side administrative field.

## Key Characteristics
- Receives respectful treatment without treating patron favor as proof of long-term safety or success.
- Separates a ruler's self-image from demonstrated governing and strategic capacity.
- Uses geographic withdrawal to reduce exposure before regime collapse.
- Re-enters service after the political field changes.

- Later service: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1096-chibi-zhizhan-qian-gefang-shili-ruhe-anzhong-boyi-licxjg9iqlwbxa23luxbopaqeqyn|Hanji 1096]] says Pei Qian enters Cao Cao's administration after the conquest of Jingzhou.

## Qualifications
The profile is bounded to one short episode. The private conversation, exact timing, office title, and the host's capacity judgment are not treated as a complete biography.
