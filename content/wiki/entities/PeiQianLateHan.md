## Overview

[[PeiQianLateHan|裴潜]] is a Hedong scholar and Cao-side administrator whose profile combines early patron-capacity judgment with later locally embedded frontier governance.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1096 presents Pei Qian as respected by [[LiuBiao|刘表]] but privately skeptical that the ruler can convert ambition into durable power. He moves south toward Changsha rather than remaining close to the court and later enters [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s administration after the conquest of Jingzhou.

Hanji 1162 moves that career into northern governance. The episode credits Pei Qian with stabilizing Dai and says that, when recalled after Wang Bi's death, he warns local [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] will reject his successor. The subsequent revolt makes his value relationship-specific as well as generally administrative, although the source does not prove that the transfer alone caused the uprising.

## Key Characteristics

- Receives respectful treatment without treating patron favor as proof of long-term safety or success.
- Separates a ruler's self-image from demonstrated governing and strategic capacity.
- Uses geographic withdrawal to reduce exposure before regime collapse.
- Re-enters service after the political field changes.
- Builds locally embedded authority strong enough that succession becomes a governance risk.
- Warns about the downstream cost of his own transfer rather than treating promotion as an isolated gain.

## Qualifications

The profile remains bounded to two podcast summaries. The Liu Biao conversation, exact career sequence and offices, scope of Pei Qian's northern authority, Wuhuan motives, and the causal connection between recall and revolt require verification against the underlying historical texts.

## What Changed

- Added Pei Qian's Dai administration and warning about successor legitimacy.
- Qualified the episode's implied causal link between his recall and Wuhuan revolt.

## Relationships

- [[LiuBiao|刘表]] - respected patron whom Pei Qian judges unable to realize his ambitions.
- [[CaoCao|曹操]] - later ruler under whom Pei Qian serves.
- [[Jingzhou|荆州]] - regional setting for Pei Qian's assessment and withdrawal.
- [[StrategicPatronExitJudgment|主公前途与安全退出判断]] - concept exemplified by leaving before Liu Biao's regime is absorbed.
- [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] - frontier population whose acceptance of replacement authority Pei Qian doubts.
- [[CaoZhangLateHan|曹彰]] - commander sent after the predicted frontier instability becomes revolt.
- [[CrossRegionalTalentReallocationRisk|跨区域人才调配风险]] - concept illustrated by moving embedded administrative capacity to fill another vacancy.
