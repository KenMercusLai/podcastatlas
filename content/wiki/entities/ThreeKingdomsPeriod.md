# 三国时代 / Three Kingdoms Period

三国时代 / Three Kingdoms Period enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-923-plus-luanshi-fengyun-qi-ruiqi-jiemi-shishang-zui-zhenshi-de-sanguo-lj0ic-bhawqut5zbutbzd0orxjoe|Hanji 923 plus]] as the next horizon for [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] after its long [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] setup. The source presents the period less as a fixed set of famous scenes than as a world of disorder, ambition, fear, strategy, and shifting alliances.

For the wiki, this page is a period node and a reading-frame anchor. Hanji 923 plus does not yet narrate a specific battle, court move, or succession crisis. Its contribution is to define why later late-Han and Three Kingdoms episodes should be read through [[ThreeKingdomsStruggleStrategyReading|三国斗争策略读法]] while keeping [[RomanceOfTheThreeKingdoms|《三国演义》]] as powerful reception memory rather than direct historical evidence.
