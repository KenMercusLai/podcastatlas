# 阎象 / Yan Xiang (late Han)

阎象 / Yan Xiang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1001-weishenme-quan-ni-buyao-fanzhongnu-lolurkcjpie14nnczk0tu7iqerke|Hanji 1001]] as [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s chief clerk and the first named internal remonstrant against Yuan Shu's plan to claim the imperial title. When Yuan Shu asks his generals about enthronement and receives no support, Yan Xiang answers through the precedent of [[ZhouWenwang|周文王]].

His point is a title-reality warning. Zhou had accumulated virtue and merit over generations and had wide popular attachment, yet Wenwang still served Shang; Yuan Shu's family prestige and military resources are weaker than that, while the Han house has not yet become a tyranny that would make replacement publicly legitimate. The source therefore makes Yan Xiang an early voice for [[TitleRealityRestraint|名实相符式名号节制]] before the later [[AntiUsurpationCoalitionPressure|讨逆同盟压力]] forms around Yuan Shu.
