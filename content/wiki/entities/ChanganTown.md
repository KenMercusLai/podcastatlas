# Chang'an Town / 长安镇

Chang'an Town / 长安镇 is the [[Dongguan|东莞]] manufacturing setting emphasized in [[ruci-dagong-30-nian-liushuixian-shang-de-miankong-yu-shijian-lodsuktwtcfxnhmzwum5iesbzbme]]. [[ZhanTeacher|占老师]] describes it as a town of roughly 1.2 million people where most residents are industrial workers and agriculture has largely disappeared from the visible landscape.

In the source, Chang'an Town makes [[PearlRiverDeltaManufacturingTransformation|珠三角制造业变迁]] concrete. Taiwanese and Hong Kong capital, export orders, worker recruitment, dormitory life, two-shift factories, automation, and [[ManufacturingMicroFragmentation|小微化]] all appear as town-level experience rather than distant macro history.

## Key Claims
- Chang'an Town is used as a concentrated case of factory-centered urbanization.
- Its industrial system creates both opportunity and constraint: wages and remittances changed lives, while factory time, social-insurance gaps, and repeated technical shifts shaped worker vulnerability.
- The town is a key setting for [[WorkerPhotographicArchive|worker photographic archive]] because ordinary streets, shops, dorms, and factory gates hold the long record of labor life.
