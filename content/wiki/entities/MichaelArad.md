# Michael Arad

## Overview

Michael Arad is the architect whose “Reflecting Absence” proposal won the competition for the [[NationalSeptember11Memorial]].

## Current Profile

The episode traces Arad's design to his experience of silent public gathering after September 11. His initial image of two unfillable voids in the Hudson River became a Ground Zero design using the towers' footprints, falling water, and surrounding names to express absence at monumental scale.

## Key Characteristics

- He developed the core two-void concept after observing collective mourning in Washington Square Park.
- He adapted the idea to the Ground Zero site and won an international competition with more than 5,000 submissions.
- His design treats absence, exact location, scale, sound, and touch as carriers of memory.
- His principle of meaningful adjacency made relationships part of the memorial's name layout.
