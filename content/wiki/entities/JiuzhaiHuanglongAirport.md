# Jiuzhai Huanglong Airport / 九寨黄龙机场

## Overview
Jiuzhai Huanglong Airport is presented as a tourism-oriented regional airport whose reported loss of scheduled flights exposes the tension between commercial viability and public transport value.

## Current Profile
The episode says the airport, opened in 2003 to serve the Jiuzhaigou and Huanglong tourism areas, had no scheduled flights from 25 October 2026 after direct routes were withdrawn. Its traffic is described as vulnerable to tourism seasonality, a thin local business and family-visit base, and substitution by improved rail and roads. The same source argues that airports can still provide disproportionate time-saving value in large, mountainous, or border regions even when their standalone operating economics are weak.

## Key Characteristics
- Regional airport strongly dependent on tourism demand.
- Exposed to sharp peak-season and off-season traffic variation.
- Has limited everyday local demand to stabilize route economics.
- Competes with improving high-speed rail and road access.
- Illustrates the public-service case for time-saving transport infrastructure.

- **Demand structure:** [[dafeixin-pingguo-xin-xitong-keyi-jinzhi-yaoyiyao-guanggao-zhangyiming-zai-deng-hurun-baifu-bangshou-gkwridoot6kmafx6cwtxdrfn]] links the airport's vulnerability to tourism seasonality, weak routine local travel, and substitution from rail and roads.
- **Public value:** [[dafeixin-pingguo-xin-xitong-keyi-jinzhi-yaoyiyao-guanggao-zhangyiming-zai-deng-hurun-baifu-bangshou-gkwridoot6kmafx6cwtxdrfn]] uses regional construction-cost comparisons and travel-time access to argue that short-run airport profit is not the only relevant criterion.

## Qualifications
The source does not supply route-level load factors, subsidies, airport accounts, weather and altitude constraints, reopening plans, or a comparable cost-benefit model for air, road, and rail. The reported absence of flights is time-sensitive and does not establish permanent closure.

- Preserved the difference between no current scheduled flights and permanent closure.
