# 李典 / Li Dian

## Overview
李典 / Li Dian is a Cao-side officer whose current profile combines group-scale recruitment capacity with tactical warning and rescue. Hanji 1104 presents him as joining with thousands of clan members and retainers, while Hanji 1057 shows him correctly reading [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s burned-camp withdrawal before the [[BowangSlopeBattleLateHan|Bowang]] ambush.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1104 uses Li Dian to show that talent transfer can also be organization transfer: he is said to bring his clan and several thousand retainers to [[CaoCao|曹操]], effectively adding an existing force rather than arriving alone. Hanji 1057 then places him with [[XiahouDun|夏侯惇]] and [[YuJin|于禁]] against Liu Bei's northward movement from [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s Jingzhou base. After Liu Bei burns his own camp and retreats, Li Dian argues that an unexplained retreat plus narrow, wooded terrain signals an ambush. Xiahou Dun continues the pursuit, falls into the trap, and Li Dian's reserve movement rescues him.

## Key Characteristics
- Cao-side officer with better terrain and enemy-signal reading than the immediate commander in this episode.
- Group-scale recruit presented as bringing clan members and retainers into Cao Cao's organization.
- Identifies burned-camp retreat as bait rather than simple weakness.
- Preserves force continuity by rescuing Xiahou Dun after the ambush begins.
- Functions as the successful counterpart to Xiahou Dun's overconfident pursuit.
