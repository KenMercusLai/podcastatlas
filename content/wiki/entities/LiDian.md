# 李典 / Li Dian

## Overview
李典 / Li Dian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1057-da-bu-liao-zhang-de-jiangjun-bushi-youxiu-de-shufajia-ljhy16leevjd8i-7vmuxtqm96ytg|Hanji 1057]] as the Cao-side officer who correctly reads [[LiuBei|刘备]]'s burned-camp withdrawal before the [[BowangSlopeBattleLateHan|Bowang]] ambush. The episode uses him as a warning-reader whose caution is ignored before becoming operationally useful in rescue.

## Current Profile
Li Dian accompanies [[XiahouDun|夏侯惇]] and [[YuJin|于禁]] when [[CaoCao|曹操]] sends them against Liu Bei's northward movement from [[LiuBiao|刘表]]'s Jingzhou base. After Liu Bei burns his own camp and retreats, Li Dian argues that an unexplained retreat plus narrow, wooded terrain signals an ambush. Xiahou Dun continues the pursuit, falls into the trap, and Li Dian's reserve movement rescues him.

## Key Characteristics
- Cao-side officer with better terrain and enemy-signal reading than the immediate commander in this episode.
- Identifies burned-camp retreat as bait rather than simple weakness.
- Preserves force continuity by rescuing Xiahou Dun after the ambush begins.
- Functions as the successful counterpart to Xiahou Dun's overconfident pursuit.
