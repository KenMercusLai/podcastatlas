# Michael Pillsbury / 白邦瑞

## Overview

Michael Pillsbury / 白邦瑞 appears as a U.S. China strategist known to [[XiangLanxin|Xiang Lanxin]] and connected to [[AndrewMarshall]] and U.S. defense-policy networks.

## Current Profile

Xiang offers a mixed assessment. He credits Pillsbury with early advocacy of U.S.-China military exchange but rejects much of *The Hundred-Year Marathon* as academically weak and conspiratorial. The resulting profile separates network access and a potentially useful exchange initiative from acceptance of the book's strategic thesis.

## Key Characteristics

- U.S. strategist focused on China.
- Connected to defense and neoconservative policy networks in Xiang's account.
- Credited with early support for bilateral military exchange.
- Criticized by Xiang for the thesis and academic quality of *The Hundred-Year Marathon*.
