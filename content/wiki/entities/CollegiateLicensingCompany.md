# Collegiate Licensing Company / CLC

## Overview
Collegiate Licensing Company / CLC is the U.S. collegiate trademark-licensing agent described in the source as a key intermediary between universities and brands that want to use school names, logos, mascots, colors, and related marks.

## Current Profile
In the episode, CLC is the clearest example of the infrastructure that turns university identity into scalable retail. The source says CLC emerged in the early 1980s after unlicensed and uneven campus merchandise pushed schools to manage marks more systematically. CLC receives license applications, reviews brand capability and product plans, and lets universities keep final control over whether and how their marks appear.

## Key Characteristics
- Acts as a transaction-cost reducer between many universities and many apparel or consumer-product brands.
- Helps schools turn names, abbreviations, mascots, and logos into managed trademark assets.
- Screens licensee product proposals, operating capacity, supply chain, marketing plans, and sales channels before university approval.
- Leaves final mark-use and design approval with the university rather than replacing the school as rights owner.
- Makes cross-school licensing more scalable because brands can approach a common professional layer.
