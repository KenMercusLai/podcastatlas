## Overview

杨不悔（金庸小说） / Yang Buhui (Jin Yong) is the daughter of [[YangXiaoJinYong|杨逍]] and [[JiXiaofuJinYong|纪晓芙]] whose care for the injured [[YinLitingJinYong|殷梨亭]] develops into a relationship that challenges age, inherited betrothal, and repayment-marriage expectations.

## Current Profile

The episode treats Yang Buhui as an active chooser rather than an instrument for settling her mother's unfinished obligations. Long-term care during Yin Liting's weakness supplies a concrete basis for affection, even though her absent father, admiration of Yang Xiao, resemblance to Ji Xiaofu, and possible wish to repair inherited harm remain plausible influences.

Her explicit choice of Yin rather than Zhang Wuji is equally important. Childhood rescue and shared danger do not obligate marriage, and their sibling-like familiarity may inhibit romance. The source uses this refusal of repayment logic to distinguish gratitude and kinship from desire.

## Key Characteristics

- Grows up under the meaning attached to Ji Xiaofu's declaration of “no regret.”
- Provides sustained care to Yin Liting during injury and vulnerability.
- Chooses an older partner despite age and unusual family-history objections.
- Rejects the idea that rescue by Zhang Wuji creates a duty to marry him.
- May be influenced by father absence, admiration, resemblance, and compensation without being reducible to them.
- Exercises relational agency within a plot that also uses the marriage as generational repair.

## Qualifications

The relationship is fictional and ethically disputed because of age and Yin's former betrothal to Ji Xiaofu. The episode's claims about father longing, the Westermarck effect, compensation, and authorial convenience are interpretive rather than diagnoses or textual facts. Mutual care and stated choice support agency but do not make every power or kinship concern disappear.
