# HYROX World GmbH

HYROX World GmbH appears in [[shangye-xiaoyang-47-jianshen-zuotijia-de-xinhuan-hyrox-daodi-you-shenme-huchenghe-1008179335]] as the company behind [[HYROX]]'s registered marks and official commercial system. The episode uses the company to separate unowned exercise rules from protectable event-brand assets.

The source says the protectable layer includes the HYROX name, related marks such as HYROX 365 and HYROX World, slogans, venue visual elements, official document typography, apparel, fitness equipment, courses, coaching, information services, software, and training systems. That turns HYROX World GmbH into the legal and commercial holder behind [[TrademarkBasedEventProtection]] and [[AuthorizedFitnessTrainingNetwork]].
