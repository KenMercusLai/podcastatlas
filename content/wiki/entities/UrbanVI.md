# Urban VI

## Overview

Urban VI was the Rome-based pope supported by Catherine of Siena when rival elections divided the western church in 1378.

## Current Profile

In [[356-the-blood-drinking-bride-of-christ-glt8496536394]], Urban's disputed pontificate shows [[CatherineOfSiena|Catherine]] moving from advocacy for papal return to active partisanship during the [[GreatWesternSchism|Great Western Schism]]. Catherine defended his legitimacy, and in November 1378 Urban had cardinals hear her speak, using her public sanctity and rhetorical authority to strengthen his side.

## Key Characteristics

- Rome-based claimant in the opening Great Western Schism.
- Supported publicly by Catherine of Siena.
- Used Catherine's spiritual authority in a meeting with cardinals.
- Represents the political use of a mystic's reputation during institutional crisis.

## Evidence

- Schism alignment: [[356-the-blood-drinking-bride-of-christ-glt8496536394]] identifies Catherine as Urban's partisan against the Avignon-backed rival.
- Catherine's address: [[356-the-blood-drinking-bride-of-christ-glt8496536394]] reports that Urban had cardinals listen to her in November 1378.

## Qualifications

This profile covers Urban only through Catherine's episode and does not adjudicate the election's canon-law questions, rival obediences, his broader rule, personality, or responsibility for the schism. The effect of Catherine's intervention is not quantified.
