# 李叔 / Li Shu (日谈公园)

## Overview

李叔 is the [[Ritan|日谈公园 / 日谈]] speaker who appears in the 2026 [[QizhulouYanBinke|起朱楼宴宾客]] crossover on [[AssetAllocationActionGuide|《资产配置行动指南》]]. In this wiki he is treated as a distinct 日谈公园 interlocutor, separate from unrelated pages with similar Romanization such as [[LiShuLateHan]], [[LiShufu]], and [[LiShui]].

## Current Profile

The source presents Li Shu as a financially avoidant beginner rather than an investment expert. He keeps almost all available money in demand deposits, nearly canceled the recording because of resistance to investing, and uses his own housing, fund, stock, and Bitcoin memories to ask what asset allocation means for someone who has avoided the topic.

## Key Characteristics

- Acts as a listener proxy for people who feel fear, shame, or resistance around financial planning.
- Grounds the episode in a concrete missed-housing-gain story from Beijing rather than abstract market theory.
- Raises beginner questions about whether investing is necessary, when cash is insufficient, and how much emergency money is needed before changing work or life plans.
- Makes [[AssetAllocationActionGuide|《资产配置行动指南》]] function as a reader-facing entry point rather than only an authorial summary of prior podcast material.
- Accepts a practice-first path: account setup, cash products, funds, small risky positions, and gradual self-knowledge before large allocation changes.
