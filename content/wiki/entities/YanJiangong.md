# 燕简公 / Duke Jian of Yan

燕简公 / Duke Jian of Yan appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl|Zhouji 09]] as the elderly ruler of [[YanState|燕国]] during the early Warring States melee. The source names Yan as the state attacked by [[QiState|齐国]] when Qi occupies Sangqiu.

His source role is contextual rather than biographical. Yan's vulnerability draws in [[HanState|韩国]], [[ZhaoState|赵国]], and [[WeiState|魏国]], whose rescue attack on Qi becomes one starting point for the episode's [[EarlyWarringStatesInterstateWar|interstate-war]] frame.
