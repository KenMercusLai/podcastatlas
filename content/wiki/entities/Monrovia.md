# Monrovia

Monrovia appears in [[taking-the-shine-off-albedo-and-global-temperatures-6a730fdaca067b295bab9760]] as the capital setting for [[Liberia]]'s public crackdown message. The episode opens the drug-trafficking segment with a march in Monrovia aimed at showing action against drug abuse and narcotics trafficking.

The city also anchors the seizure geography: the reported raid occurred between Liberia's main airport and the capital. That makes Monrovia a visible public-trust and enforcement node inside the larger [[WestAfricaCocaineTransit]] route.
