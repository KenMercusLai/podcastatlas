# 沮授 / Ju Shou

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] adds a later thematic recall of Ju Shou inside [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s adviser roster. The episode says Yuan Shao's Hebei advisers could have overwhelmed Cao's side if their counsel had become decisions, and it names Ju Shou's long-horizon plan to wear Cao Cao down as a wasted strategic option.

This does not override the more concrete Hanji 992 welcome-the-emperor branch. It generalizes the same pattern: Ju Shou sees a strategic window, but Yuan Shao's decision process cannot convert that warning into durable action.

沮授 / Ju Shou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] as the adviser who urges [[YuanShao|袁绍]] to welcome [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] to Ye. His argument is that Yuan Shao's family prestige, the emperor's displacement, and the damaged ancestral temples create a short [[LegitimacyWindowSeizure|合法性窗口抢占]]: by holding the emperor, Yuan Shao can command lords in the Han name and punish those who refuse.

The source contrasts Ju Shou with [[GuoTu|郭图]] and [[ChunyuQiong|淳于琼]], who warn that hosting the emperor will constrain Yuan Shao. Ju Shou's reply is a timing argument: if Yuan Shao delays, another actor will seize the advantage first. The host reads Yuan Shao's refusal as ceding the "挟天子以令诸侯" opportunity to [[CaoCao|曹操]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-993-shentingling-zhizhan-sunce-dazhan-taishici-lrivpk7col2l7k-s4dqzxxp2kqp1|Hanji 993]] then invokes Ju Shou as a comparator rather than the episode's main subject. The host uses him while discussing how late-Han leaders often fail not because they lack access to talent, but because they do not properly use it.

In that comparison, Ju Shou belongs to the [[YuanShao|袁绍]] side of the talent-use failure branch. The page therefore links Hanji 993's [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]] and [[TaishiCi|太史慈]] story to the broader wiki pattern in [[TalentAsStateTreasure|以贤臣为宝]]: available people do not become state capacity unless authority, trust, and decision processes let their judgment matter.
