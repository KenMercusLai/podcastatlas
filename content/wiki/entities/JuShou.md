# 沮授 / Ju Shou

## Overview
沮授 / Ju Shou is a [[YuanShao|袁绍]] adviser whose current wiki profile centers on correct long-horizon counsel that Yuan Shao's organization fails to convert into durable decision. The bounded sources use him for the missed emperor-welcome window, the wasted Hebei adviser advantage, and the pre-[[GuanduCampaign|官渡]] debate over rest, agriculture, harassment, and immediate war.

## Current Profile
Ju Shou's strongest pattern is strategic timing. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] makes him the voice urging Yuan Shao to welcome [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] to Ye before another power seizes the legitimacy window. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-993-shentingling-zhizhan-sunce-dazhan-taishici-lrivpk7col2l7k-s4dqzxxp2kqp1|Hanji 993]] generalize him as evidence that Yuan Shao had advisers but did not turn their judgment into state capacity. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] makes that failure concrete before Guandu: Ju Shou recommends recovery after the Gongsun Zan war and pressure on Cao Cao through delay and harassment, but his blunt framing gives Yuan Shao little status-preserving path to accept the advice.

## Key Characteristics
- Long-horizon strategist who emphasizes timing, legitimacy, exhaustion, agriculture, and attritional pressure.
- Yuan-camp adviser whose value is repeatedly visible before Yuan Shao's decision process fails.
- Counterweight to flatterers and constraint-avoidant advisers such as Guo Tu in the emperor-welcome and Guandu-preparation debates.
- Case of unused talent, where access to good advice does not become organizational advantage.
- Hierarchy-framing caution: Hanji 1034 adds that accurate advice can be weakened by status-wounding phrasing.

## Evidence
- Legitimacy timing: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] has Ju Shou argue that Yuan Shao should receive Emperor Xian, command lords in the Han name, and avoid letting Cao Cao seize the "holding the emperor" advantage first.
- Long-war recovery: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] says Ju Shou reads the post-Gongsun Zan moment as a time for rest, agriculture, and harassment rather than immediate decisive battle with Cao Cao.
- Talent-use failure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-993-shentingling-zhizhan-sunce-dazhan-taishici-lrivpk7col2l7k-s4dqzxxp2kqp1|Hanji 993]] use Ju Shou as part of the broader Yuan Shao pattern in which adviser resources are present but not operationalized.
- Advice framing: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] qualifies Ju Shou's correctness by stressing that blunt moral language about Cao Cao's legitimacy and Yuan Shao's lack of just cause made acceptance harder inside the hierarchy.

## Qualifications
The page is source-scoped and should not turn Ju Shou into a complete biography. Hanji 1034 does not say his wording alone caused Yuan Shao's defeat; it treats phrasing as one mechanism alongside factional infighting, Yuan Shao's preferences, and Cao-side organization. Earlier sources still make Ju Shou primarily a missed strategic-timing adviser, not merely a failed communicator.

## What Changed
- Hanji 1034 makes Ju Shou's wasted value concrete as a pre-Guandu rest, agriculture, delay, and harassment plan.
- The page now treats his advice failure as both a ruler-decision problem and a hierarchy-framing problem.
- The profile is migrated to synthesis-v1 while preserving the existing source inventory order.
