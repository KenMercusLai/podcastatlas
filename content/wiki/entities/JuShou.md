# 沮授 / Ju Shou

## Overview
沮授 / Ju Shou is a [[YuanShao|Yuan Shao]] adviser whose current wiki profile centers on correct long-horizon counsel that Yuan Shao's organization fails to convert into durable decision. The bounded sources use him for the missed emperor-welcome window, the wasted Hebei adviser advantage, the pre-[[GuanduCampaign|Guandu]] debate over recovery and harassment, White Horse/Yanjin warnings that Yuan ignores, and a captured-loyalist ending after Yuan's Wuchao defeat.

## Current Profile
Ju Shou's strongest pattern is strategic timing. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] makes him the voice urging Yuan Shao to welcome [[EmperorXianOfHan|Emperor Xian]] to Ye before another power seizes the legitimacy window. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-993-shentingling-zhizhan-sunce-dazhan-taishici-lrivpk7col2l7k-s4dqzxxp2kqp1|Hanji 993]] generalize him as evidence that Yuan Shao had advisers but did not turn their judgment into state capacity.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] supplies the operational pre-Guandu version: after the long Gongsun Zan war, Ju Shou recommends agricultural recovery, reporting victory to the court, moving gradually toward [[LiyangYellowRiverLateHan|Liyang]], building ships and arms, and harassing Cao Cao rather than forcing an immediate decisive battle. The same source shows the cost of losing the debate when [[GuoTu|Guo Tu]] and [[ShenPeiLateHan|Shen Pei]] attack his authority and Yuan Shao divides his troops among Ju Shou, Guo Tu, and [[ChunyuQiong|Chunyu Qiong]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] then adds the communication diagnosis: Ju Shou's content is strong, but his blunt legitimacy framing gives Yuan Shao little status-preserving path to accept it.

The White Horse source shows Ju Shou moving from advice to personal risk management. Before departure he distributes family property because a defeated powerholder may no longer protect even life itself. His diagnosis is political and operational: Cao Cao has superior intelligence and the emperor as leverage, Yuan's troops are tired from defeating [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]], Yuan Shao is proud, and the generals are indulgent. He also warns that [[YanLiangLateHan|颜良]] is brave but too impulsive and narrow for independent command at White Horse.

The following Yanjin source gives the same pattern an urgent battlefield version. After Yan Liang is killed, Ju Shou warns Yuan Shao not to rush the whole army south across the Yellow River and instead to hold the crossing while probing Guandu. Yuan Shao treats the warning as obstruction, strips Ju Shou's remaining command, and transfers those troops to Guo Tu before the [[YanjinBattleLateHan|Yanjin]] loss.

The newer pre-Wuchao source makes Ju Shou's warning even more concrete. When Yuan Shao sends [[ChunyuQiong|淳于琼]] with a large grain convoy, Ju Shou recommends assigning [[JiangQiLateHan|蒋奇]] as an outer protective force to patrol and guard against a Cao-side attack. Yuan Shao again refuses, leaving a logistics vulnerability that [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]] soon exposes to Cao Cao.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1049-guandu-zhizhan-jieju-jiemu-caocao-zhongyu-yi-ruo-sheng-qiang-lvtutwyhrix4rhiypa9dknz3da1m|Hanji 1049]] gives Ju Shou an endpoint that contrasts with Zhang He and Gao Lan. After Cao burns Wuchao and Yuan Shao flees across the Yellow River with [[YuanTan|Yuan Tan]], Ju Shou is captured by Cao Cao. Cao respects him and initially treats him well, but Ju Shou insists that his family is in the north and repeatedly tries to return to Yuan Shao, so Cao eventually kills him.

## Key Characteristics
- Long-horizon strategist who emphasizes timing, legitimacy, exhaustion, agriculture, and attritional pressure.
- Yuan-camp adviser whose value is repeatedly visible before Yuan Shao's decision process fails.
- Counterweight to flatterers and constraint-avoidant advisers such as Guo Tu in the emperor-welcome and Guandu-preparation debates.
- Source of concrete pre-Guandu operational plans: rest, farming, court-facing legitimacy, Liyang positioning, ships, arms, harassment, and crossing caution.
- Target of factional authority reduction after Guo Tu and Shen Pei argue that his military and administrative power is too concentrated.
- Commander-fit and logistics-protection critic who warns against Yan Liang's independent White Horse assignment, Yuan Shao's full Yellow River crossing, and underprotected grain movement before Wuchao.
- Captured loyalist whose refusal to transfer allegiance makes him honorable to Cao Cao but not safely incorporable.

- Adviser abundance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-993-shentingling-zhizhan-sunce-dazhan-taishici-lrivpk7col2l7k-s4dqzxxp2kqp1|Hanji 993]] use Ju Shou as part of the broader Yuan Shao pattern in which adviser resources are present but not operationalized.
- Long-war recovery plan: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] says Ju Shou reads the post-Gongsun Zan moment as a time for rest, agriculture, legitimacy positioning, and harassment rather than immediate attack on Xudu.
- Advice framing: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1034-yuanshao-de-baiju-zhi-yin-buken-ting-jushou-de-hua-log-wy2zvpdjopbdyexb0ijqmph4|Hanji 1034]] qualifies Ju Shou's correctness by stressing that blunt moral language about Cao Cao's legitimacy and Yuan Shao's lack of just cause makes acceptance harder inside the hierarchy.
- Command weakening: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1033-geju-junfa-yuanshu-zuizhong-tuxue-er-wang-lt0ibs-en0oeoqkkcxl8sevjng4|Hanji 1033]] says Yuan Shao splits Ju Shou's troops among Ju Shou, Guo Tu, and Chunyu Qiong after factional warnings about excessive adviser power.
- Pre-departure fatalism and Yan Liang warning: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1043-yuanshao-tongma-caocao-jing-zhihaole-tade-piantouteng-ltibjj6xizsrynpgr9oyf-q3ydna|Hanji 1043]] has Ju Shou distribute family property, predict danger for Yuan's army, and warn that Yan Liang should not receive independent command at White Horse.
- Crossing-risk warning: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1044-guanyu-qianli-zou-danqi-xu-ganxie-caocao-busha-enqing-lpptlnc-e4zu4lcjr49bquxdw0pm|Hanji 1044]] has Ju Shou advise holding Yan Ford and testing Guandu rather than sending the whole army south, after which Yuan Shao strips his command and gives it to Guo Tu.
- Grain-convoy protection warning: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1048-xuyou-pan-yuan-xianji-guandu-zhanju-de-jingtian-bianshu-lt3sewxvtpytrufrex8b0s0p0hju|Hanji 1048]] has Ju Shou advise sending Jiang Qi to patrol around Chunyu Qiong's convoy before Yuan Shao refuses the added protection.
- Captured loyalist endpoint: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1049-guandu-zhizhan-jieju-jiemu-caocao-zhongyu-yi-ruo-sheng-qiang-lvtutwyhrix4rhiypa9dknz3da1m|Hanji 1049]] says Ju Shou is captured after Yuan Shao's flight, refuses to treat capture as surrender, receives Cao's respect, and is killed after repeated attempts to return north.

## Qualifications
The page is source-scoped and should not turn Ju Shou into a complete biography. The Guandu and White Horse sources do not say one rejected warning alone caused Yuan Shao's defeat; they treat advice framing as one mechanism alongside Yuan Shao's preferences, anger, factional infighting, command fragmentation, commander-fit mistakes, logistics protection failure, Wuchao misallocation, and Cao-side organization. Ju Shou's disaster prediction remains source-bounded forecast, not proof that no alternative Yuan-side execution was possible. Hanji 1049's captured-loyalist ending also shows that Cao Cao's respect for talent has limits when the captured adviser keeps trying to rejoin the enemy.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1048's grain-convoy protection warning through the proposed Jiang Qi patrol.
- Extended Ju Shou's Guandu role from strategy and crossing caution into specific logistics-defense advice before Wuchao.
- Clarified that Yuan Shao's rejection pattern now includes protection of the exact supply layer later exposed by Xu You.
