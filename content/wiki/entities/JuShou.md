# 沮授 / Ju Shou

沮授 / Ju Shou enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-993-shentingling-zhizhan-sunce-dazhan-taishici-lrivpk7col2l7k-s4dqzxxp2kqp1|Hanji 993]] as a comparator rather than the episode's main subject. The host invokes him while discussing how late-Han leaders often fail not because they lack access to talent, but because they do not properly use it.

In that comparison, Ju Shou belongs to the [[YuanShao|袁绍]] side of the talent-use failure branch. The page therefore links Hanji 993's [[LiuYaoLateHan|刘繇]] and [[TaishiCi|太史慈]] story to the broader wiki pattern in [[TalentAsStateTreasure|以贤臣为宝]]: available people do not become state capacity unless authority, trust, and decision processes let their judgment matter.
