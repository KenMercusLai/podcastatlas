# 长江商学院 / Cheung Kong Graduate School of Business

长江商学院 / Cheung Kong Graduate School of Business appears in [[ep255-chang-erdai-de-jicheng-zhi-zhan-nianqingren-jieban-hui-chongsu-qiye-ma-likvzmpgwxnetpaezxclsx4s-hkw]] as the institution that formalizes [[ChineseFamilyBusinessSuccession|Chinese family business succession]] learning. The episode says the school opened [[JieliChangjiang|接力长江]] in 2014 for family-business second-generation successors, responding to founder concerns about handoff.

The school's role in the source is not a full institutional profile. It represents the training environment where [[FactorySecondGeneration|厂二代]] compare experiences, learn management language, and try to convert family-specific operating problems into teachable patterns.

## Source Position
- The episode names 长江商学院 as a place where both founders and successors seek family-business education.
- [[FanXinyu|范鑫宇]]'s observations from its programs help structure the episode's claims about entry routes, tacit knowledge, and generational differences.
- Business-school learning is treated as useful but incomplete because successors still need direct exposure to company processes and people.
