# 梁启超

梁启超 appears in [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]] through his letters, family education, late scholarship, and posthumous political evaluation. The episode uses his correspondence with [[LiangSicheng|梁思成]] to show a demand for breadth: architecture should not become narrow specialization detached from literature, art, and life.

The source also discusses Liang's advice around 梁思勇's archaeology path, his shock after [[WangGuowei|王国维]]'s death, and his final excitement over new material for a Zhu Xi chronology. His funeral and later reassessment by Chiang Kai-shek are used to show how modern intellectual reputation could be caught between scholarship, politics, and national crisis.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-04-yurang-wei-zhiyao-fuchou-ln34tydha6iqwlrgmw7iugqfgfik]] adds Liang Qichao as a later interpretive voice on [[YuRang|豫让]]. The episode cites his 1904 《中国之武士道》 to argue that earlier heroic-retainer or knightly spirit weakened under unified autocratic rule and a habit of obedience to strong power.

## Key Claims
- Liang Qichao's education ideal is broad and synthetic: specialist training needs humanistic and historical range.
- His family letters connect [[CosmopolitanChineseScholarship]] to concrete travel, study, and professional formation.
- The source frames his late scholarly energy as part of a life that did not stop at political reform fame.
- The Zhouji 04 source uses Liang as a modern reader of pre-Qin honor culture, not as direct evidence for the events themselves.
