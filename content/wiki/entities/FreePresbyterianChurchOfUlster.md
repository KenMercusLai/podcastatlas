# Free Presbyterian Church of Ulster

The Free Presbyterian Church of Ulster appears in [[697-the-troubles-murder-in-belfast-part-1-glt4877520736]] as the church founded by [[IanPaisley]] in 1951. The episode presents it as part of Paisley's broader challenge to mainstream Ulster Protestant institutions, which he regarded as weak toward Rome.

For the source, the church matters because Paisley's religious authority gave his anti-Catholic rhetoric a public platform before and during his rise as a political agitator. It connects theology, spectacle, and unionist mobilization inside [[TheTroubles]]' pre-1968 background.
