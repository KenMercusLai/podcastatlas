# 赵俨 / Zhao Yan (late Han)

## Overview
赵俨 / Zhao Yan is a late-Han Yingchuan refugee turned Cao Cao official whose first wiki profile combines camp-choice judgment with strict enforcement against private kinship pressure.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1022 presents Zhao Yan as one of three displaced Yingchuan figures who initially use Liu Biao's Jingzhou as shelter. After Cao Cao receives Emperor Xian and builds the Xu court position, Zhao Yan treats the field as changed: he moves north with his family and enters Cao Cao's service. As Langling magistrate, he then arrests and executes the lawbreaking uncle of Li Tong's wife, making his official role a visible case of public law overriding private appeals.

## Key Characteristics
- Moves from refugee self-preservation to active service only after the political center around Cao Cao and Xudu becomes legible.
- Reads patron choice as a timing problem: hospitality from Liu Biao is different from durable restoration capacity.
- Exercises county-level legal authority against a socially dangerous target connected to a Cao-aligned military actor's household.
- Becomes credible because Li Tong accepts the public-duty frame rather than converting wife-side kinship into exemption.
- Links Cao Cao's camp-building to enforceable rules, not only talent recruitment or battlefield success.

## Qualifications
The transcript renders the name as "赵衍" and the office as "朗领长"; the wiki normalizes to 赵俨 / Zhao Yan and Langling magistrate from the historical sequence. The source's claim that Zhao Yan saw Cao Cao as able to unify the realm is treated as Zhao Yan's judgment in the episode, not as independent proof of inevitability.
