# Carthage / 迦太基

## Overview

Carthage / 迦太基 is the North African maritime polity that loses the First Punic War to the [[RomanRepublic]], rebuilds power through Iberia, and enters the Second Punic War after the crisis over [[Saguntum]] and Rome's demand for [[HannibalBarca]]'s surrender.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Carthage as an older Mediterranean power founded by settlers from Tyre and advantaged by harbors, fertile land, and a strategic position near Sicily. Its western network once includes influence in North Africa, Sicily, Sardinia, Corsica, and southern Iberia.

Defeat in the First Punic War costs Sicily, indemnity payments, and naval position; Rome then takes Sardinia and Corsica during Carthaginian weakness. Recovery shifts toward the [[BarcidSpanishPowerBase|Spanish power base]], whose mines, land, and manpower restore strategic capacity. The final war decision still involves Carthage: its authorities accept Hannibal's appointment and later reject the Roman surrender demand.

## Key Characteristics

- Maritime and commercial power with Phoenician roots.
- Loses islands, money, and naval freedom after the First Punic War.
- Rebuilds through an Iberian land empire and Barcid command network.
- Retains formal political authority despite the commanders' distance and power.
- Enters war after accumulated rivalry is focused through Saguntum.

## Qualifications

This is an origin-of-war profile rather than a complete Carthaginian history. The episode's maritime-power framing, independence of Barcid Spain, institutional politics, reported diplomatic scene, and claims about motive remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Created Carthage as the polity connecting the First and Second Punic War branches.
- Framed Spanish expansion as post-defeat recovery rather than only Hannibal's personal inheritance.

- [[BarcidSpanishPowerBase]] - Iberian system that restores wealth and military capacity.
