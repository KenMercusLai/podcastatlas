# Bomis

## Overview
Bomis was [[JimmyWales]]'s early internet search and community company whose advertising revenue funded experiments including [[Nupedia]] and the first phase of [[Wikipedia]].

## Current Profile
The episode presents Bomis as an experimentation platform rather than only a durable product success. It invited users to assemble topic-based link collections and explored blogs, social networking, and free online encyclopedias. Its advertising income paid programmers during the dot-com boom, while its collapse removed Wikipedia's original subsidy and forced the encyclopedia community to develop low-cost volunteer operations and a different institutional model.

## Key Characteristics
- Early search and topic-directory company built around community-organized links.
- Supplied programming capacity and funding for multiple internet experiments.
- Provided practical evidence that users could organize information at greater scale than a small editorial staff.
- Funded Nupedia and early Wikipedia through advertising revenue.
- Lost that funding capacity in the dot-com crash and eventually closed.

## Evidence
- Community experiments - [[vmp5702291377-vmp5702291377]] describes user-built topic collections and tests of blogs, social networking, and encyclopedia ideas.
- Financial role - [[vmp5702291377-vmp5702291377]] says advertising income funded programmers and the early encyclopedia projects.
- Transition pressure - [[vmp5702291377-vmp5702291377]] connects Bomis's decline to Wikipedia's reliance on volunteer labor and later nonprofit fundraising.
