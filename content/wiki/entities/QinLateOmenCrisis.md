# 秦始皇晚年异象危机 / Qin Late Omen Crisis

秦始皇晚年异象危机 / Qin Late Omen Crisis is the 211 BCE omen cluster presented in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-126-qinshihuang-siyin-zhimi-1-lgtlhau6ixqglk1ict1fs0jexjwe|Qinji 126]]. The episode groups [[ChineseAstrologicalPolitics|荧惑守心]], the [[DongCommandery|东郡]] meteor inscription "始皇死而地分," and the mysterious returned jade bi with "今年祖龙死" into one late-reign death-shadow narrative around [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]].

The crisis is political as well as ominous. The meteor inscription points to public resentment in conquered or heavily controlled territory; Qin Shi Huang's failed investigation and mass punishment show how fear of prophecy can amplify the message it tries to erase. The "仙真人诗" response then turns ominous atmosphere into a cultural-management problem: the court tries to broadcast sacred confidence when the signs imply cosmic withdrawal.

The jade bi branch links this crisis back to the earlier tour sequence. In the source's account, the returned object is the jade bi Qin Shi Huang had thrown into the river during the [[XiangshanShrine|Xiangshan]] storm episode, making a prior [[ImperialLocalGodConflict|local sacred conflict]] reappear as a personal death omen. That link turns [[ImmortalityQuestPolitics|求仙政治]], [[ImperialTourPoliticalTheater|帝国巡游政治剧场]], and [[ProphecyTriggeredPolicy|预言触发政策]] into one late-Qin anxiety field.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-126-qinshihuang-siyin-zhimi-2-ln15p5qb7lc20goni1ibcpmn3wca]] extends the crisis from signs into response. The host argues that Qin Shi Huang likely understood "祖龙" as himself, then treated the divination result "游徙吉" as a way to act against the prophecy. The crisis therefore produces [[OmenCountermeasureMobility|游徙避命]]: thirty thousand households are moved toward the northern frontier, and the emperor's fifth tour becomes source-scoped as an attempt to change fate rather than only a political inspection.

The event is also a source-critical case. The host notes that [[Shiji|《史记》]] and [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] do not handle the strange-sign material identically, and she questions whether "始皇" would have been the natural wording in a live anti-Qin inscription. The wiki therefore records the crisis as a historically meaningful narrative and political signal without treating every omen detail as settled fact.
