# 庄辛 / Zhuang Xin

庄辛 / Zhuang Xin enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-82-daqin-de-hewuqi-baiqi-laile-lo0w8mifkijhzmx7fdwcimmv7ade]] as the [[ChuState|楚国]] adviser whose warning to [[MiHeng|楚顷襄王]] becomes legible only after disaster. The episode cites [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] for his earlier criticism that Chu Qingxiang Wang was absorbed in pleasure and leaving the state exposed.

After [[BaiQi|白起]] breaks the Chu capital region, the king recalls Zhuang Xin and asks for a remedy. Zhuang Xin's answer is the branch behind "亡羊补牢": seeing the hare and calling the hound, or repairing the pen after losing sheep, is still not too late. The episode uses him to shift the Chu story from collapse to partial recovery, because his warning leads the king to face danger, appoint him as Yangling Jun, and recover some Huai River territory.

His wiki role is therefore remonstrance after delayed recognition. Zhuang Xin does not stop the first catastrophe, but he supplies a language for post-failure repair and connects [[StrategicDepthDefense|strategic depth]] with renewed political attention.
