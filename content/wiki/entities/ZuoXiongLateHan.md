## Overview
左雄 / Zuo Xiong is a Shun-era official in Hanji 819 whose reputation is shaped by both recommendation authority and correction. The episode presents him first as someone who recommends [[ZhouJuLateHan|周举]], then as the official Zhou Ju impeaches after Zuo recommends the previously corrupt Feng Zhi.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-819-2-ren-gong-bi-4-wanren-tade-quanmou-lingren-paianjuejue-lsslj3y-krnd8h-c8x5pgsqeinff|Hanji 819]] places Zuo Xiong in the court's talent-recommendation environment after the Jiaozhi case. He had earlier recommended Zhou Ju, which makes the later dispute sharper: Zhou Ju turns around and impeaches Zuo when Zuo recommends Feng Zhi despite Feng's corruption conviction.

Zuo's defense is that Shun's edict sought brave and capable men rather than morally pure men. Zhou Ju answers that the edict also did not ask for a corrupt man. Zuo then apologizes and acknowledges personal bias from old ties, and the source says his reputation rises because he accepts correction.

## Key Characteristics
- Recommendation-channel official whose authority depends on candidate judgment.
- Figure corrected by a prior beneficiary, making the case a test of public duty over patronal gratitude.
- Distinguishes talent from virtue in argument, but is forced to concede that corruption is not irrelevant to appointment.
- Gains reputation by admitting error rather than retaliating against Zhou Ju's impeachment.
