# Karl Drais / 卡尔·德莱斯

Karl Drais is the German baron and inventor who enters the wiki through [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]] as the source's starting point for modern bicycle history. The episode presents his 1817 two-wheeled running machine as a response to horse, oat, and transport stress after climate disruption made horse-powered movement more expensive.

His invention matters less as a finished bicycle than as the first product step in [[BicycleProductEvolution]]. It had no pedals, chain, or pneumatic tires, but it made a seated, steerable, two-wheeled personal vehicle imaginable before later makers such as [[PierreMichaux]], [[PierreLallement]], and [[JohnKempStarley]] stabilized the form.
