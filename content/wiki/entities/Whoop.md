# Whoop

## Overview
Whoop is a screenless continuous-wear health and fitness platform centered on sleep, recovery, strain, and behavior guidance, sold through a recurring membership rather than as a conventional general-purpose smartwatch.

## Current Profile
The newest source turns WHOOP from a category comparator into a bounded company case. [[WillAhmed]] describes a product designed for near-continuous wear, on-body charging, long battery life, personalized baselines, behavior journaling, AI coaching, and integration of wearable, laboratory, and medical-record data. He reports rapid membership growth after a slow first million, more than 50 billion hours of physiological data, and a business model intended to reward retention and continuing improvement.

Earlier sources remain useful category context: nursery products borrow adult wearable sleep-score language, Garmin faces pressure from specialist screenless trackers, and Apple's reported band experiments use WHOOP-like analysis as a comparator. The current profile therefore joins form factor, interpretation, longitudinal data, subscription economics, and athlete credibility while keeping health outcomes, privacy, scale, accuracy, and regulatory claims source-scoped.

## Key Characteristics
- Screenless, near-continuous wearable organized around sleep, recovery, strain, and health signals.
- Subscription platform that bundles ongoing software, coaching, features, and periodic meaningful hardware improvement.
- Uses personal baselines and composite scores to convert sensor streams into daily guidance.
- Combines journal, sensor, laboratory, and medical-record context in an emerging AI interpretation layer.
- Competes as a specialist health and performance product rather than a multifunction smartwatch.
- Builds credibility through long-term adoption by elite athletes and reported high member wear time.

## Qualifications
Two earlier sources use spellings that differ from WHOOP, so those connections remain cautious category matches. The newest source is a promotional founder interview and does not independently verify membership, valuation, wear time, dataset scale, measurement accuracy, model performance, health outcomes, privacy implementation, blood-pressure classification, or the medical anecdotes. Personalized deviations can justify attention or professional follow-up without diagnosing their cause.
