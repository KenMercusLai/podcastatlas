# 《FX战士久留美》 / FX Senshi Kurumi

## Overview

《FX战士久留美》 is the fictional finance work used by an [[YiLaoYongYi|一劳永逸]] episode to teach retail foreign-exchange mechanics and behavioral risk through Kurumi's family loss and later trading.

## Current Profile

The current wiki evidence covers the first two animated episodes as retold by the podcast, not the complete manga or anime. Kurumi's attempt to earn back her mother's loss turns [[ForeignExchangeMarginTrading]] into a story about leverage, trauma, overconfidence, and broken exit rules rather than a simple market-direction lesson.

The work is most useful here as a risk-education narrative. Early profit makes unsafe behavior look intelligent, while a larger trader's confidence and the availability of leverage pull Kurumi toward a position whose downside she has not independently bounded.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses family loss and compensation motives to make abstract FX risk emotionally legible.
- Explains currency pairs, long and short positions, margin, leverage, floating profit and loss, and forced liquidation through plot.
- Shows how a lucky recovery can reinforce prayer, delay, and rule-breaking.
- Connects authority imitation and selective evidence to escalating position risk.
- Treats loss chasing and borrowed-money leverage as threats to life outside the account.
