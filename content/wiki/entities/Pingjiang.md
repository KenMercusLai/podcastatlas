# 平江 / Pingjiang

## Overview

平江 / Pingjiang is the Hunan county presented in [[no-221-zhongguo-latiao-jianghu-1020016798]] as the widely recognized home of modern辣条 and as a case of county-level industrial adaptation.

## Current Profile

The county's辣条 industry grew from an existing sauce-dried-tofu craft base with equipment, apprenticeships, wholesalers, transport, and hometown networks. A 1998 flood and soybean-price shock made the old product difficult to sustain, encouraging workshops to substitute cheaper wheat flour and reuse their commercial system around麻辣面筋.

Pingjiang later became both production center and regulatory laboratory. Local firms helped fund standards, invested in clean workshops and testing, absorbed repeated media and enforcement shocks, and eventually supported museums, vocational education, and a formal "中国辣条之乡" identity.

## Key Characteristics

- Pingjiang contributed process knowledge and commercial networks even though it was not a wheat-producing center.
- Peer exchange and low technical barriers accelerated early workshop conversion and machinery demand.
- Hometown networks carried production knowledge beyond the county, including migration toward Henan.
- Food-safety scandals exposed the costs of low-price workshop competition.
- Local standard setting, factory upgrading, and later education institutionalized what began as dispersed craft knowledge.

## Evidence

- **Inherited industrial base:** [[no-221-zhongguo-latiao-jianghu-1020016798]] describes sauce-dried-tofu craft, machinery, apprenticeships, wholesale transport, and hometown sales networks before辣条.
- **Crisis adaptation:** [[no-221-zhongguo-latiao-jianghu-1020016798]] ties the wheat-flour substitution to the 1998 flood and sharply higher soybean prices.
- **Regulatory upgrading:** [[no-221-zhongguo-latiao-jianghu-1020016798]] traces local standards, clean-room investment, shutdowns, inspections, and national-rule transition.
- **Institution building:** [[no-221-zhongguo-latiao-jianghu-1020016798]] connects the category to a museum, vocational training, technical advisers, and county identity.

## Qualifications

- "辣条故乡" is a regional-industry designation, not proof of a single uncontested inventor or origin account.
- The source's early production, machinery, firm-count, and cost figures remain episode-scoped.
- Pingjiang's industry includes firms with different quality and strategy; the county label should not flatten those differences.
