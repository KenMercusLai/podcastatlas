# Ron Benaghi

Ron Benaghi appears in [[a-firm-andy-what-are-new-british-pms-plans-6a5deeca76f8ee25bb4bbf9c]] as a farmer who was present at the first [[Greenmarket]] with his father at age 16. The episode uses his memory of early crowds and fresh cabbage to show the market's original demand surprise.

His family farm becomes the concrete case for [[FarmersMarketUrbanism]]. Direct access to [[NewYorkCity|New York City]] buyers helped the farm expand what it grew in response to customers, turning a small market experiment into a practical alternative to selling the farm under suburban-sprawl and fuel-cost pressure.
