# Apollo 11

## Overview
Apollo 11 was the first crewed mission to land humans on the [[Moon]], carrying [[NeilArmstrong]], [[BuzzAldrin]], and [[MichaelCollins]].

## Current Profile
[[438-the-moonwalkers-with-tom-hanks-glt1422432857]] reframes the mission as a contingent chain rather than a foreordained triumph. The disaster speech, difficult landing, ascent-engine dependence, rendezvous, re-entry, and recovery show why the first step cannot be separated from preparation for failure or the work required to return.

## Key Characteristics
- First crewed lunar landing and globally watched public event.
- Carried a prepared contingency for Armstrong and Aldrin becoming stranded.
- Required Armstrong to respond to a hazardous landing area under difficult depth perception.
- Joined public symbolism to personal ritual, crew status, and spacecraft-layout constraints.
- Depended on Collins maintaining the command module and preparing for a possible solo return.
- Extended beyond touchdown through ascent, rendezvous, Earth return, re-entry, and recovery.
