# Oliver Hart

Oliver Hart appears in [[79-gewei-lingdao-danfan-zan-xuedian-boyilun-690004262]] as the contract-theory economist with whom [[NieHuihua|聂辉华]] studied during a postdoctoral period. The source also notes Hart's 2016 Nobel-recognized contribution to [[ContractTheory]].

The page is source-scoped: the episode uses Hart mainly to establish the intellectual lineage behind [[YiQieJieQiyue|《一切皆契约》]], not to summarize Hart's full research program.
