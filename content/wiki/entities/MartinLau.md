# Martin Lau / 刘炽平

Martin Lau, or 刘炽平, appears in [[yao-shunyu-laidao-tengxun-300tian-1-176-1]] as the strongest internal sponsor for [[YaoShunyu]]'s work on [[TencentHunyuan]]. The source says Yao reports directly to Lau and also to [[LuShanTencent]], giving the Hunyuan restart unusually high-level backing inside [[Tencent]].

The episode uses Lau to show that model-team reform depends on executive cover. He is described as personally meeting frontier AI researchers, becoming more involved in model-talent interviews after the [[DeepSeek]] shock, and giving Yao money, people, compute, and time to replace key leaders before the team had definitive benchmark wins.
