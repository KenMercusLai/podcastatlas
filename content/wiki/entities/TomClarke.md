# Tom Clarke

## Overview

Tom Clarke was a veteran Fenian and [[IrishRepublicanBrotherhood|IRB]] organizer whose Dublin tobacconist shop became a revolutionary hub before the [[EasterRising|Easter Rising]].

## Current Profile

Clarke supplied persistence and intergenerational continuity to an organization mocked for having attempted no serious revolution since 1867. After imprisonment for involvement in a dynamiting campaign in Britain, he returned to Dublin and helped turn the IRB toward renewed action. When the First World War began, he argued that republicans had to strike before Britain's external difficulty ended.

The episode treats Clarke as a bridge rather than a solitary mastermind: older Fenian experience gained force through younger organizers, volunteer structures, wartime conditions, and collaboration with other movements.

## Key Characteristics

- Veteran Fenian shaped by imprisonment after a dynamiting campaign.
- Organizer who used an ordinary commercial space as a clandestine political hub.
- Advocate of exploiting Britain's wartime vulnerability.
- Intergenerational link between nineteenth-century Fenianism and the 1916 rebellion.
- Persistent organizer whose effectiveness depended on a wider revolutionary coalition.
