## Overview

卫满 / Wiman is the Yan refugee whom the episode presents as the founder of [[WimanJoseon|卫氏朝鲜]], first consolidating a mixed refugee and local following and then receiving Western Han recognition as an outer vassal.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-364-2-hanwudi-da-chaoxian-de-lishi-wenti-lrid25t03ell_bs6siq6yvrj3lp9|Hanji 364-2]] says Wiman leaves Yan with more than one thousand followers, crosses the Pei River, settles beyond the former Qin frontier, and attracts local groups as well as Yan and Qi refugees. He then rules from [[WangxianCity|王险城]].

The episode's early Han agreement makes his authority relational rather than simply independent. The Liaodong administrator recognizes him as an outer vassal, supplies a legitimate frontier role, and expects him both to prevent raids and to let neighboring leaders reach the imperial court. Wiman then uses arms, wealth, recruitment, and conquest to expand over nearby polities. That combination of recognition and accumulation creates the position later inherited by his grandson [[UgeoWimanJoseon|卫右渠]].

## Key Characteristics

- Yan refugee who builds a following across the former Qin frontier.
- Founder and first ruler associated here with Wiman Joseon.
- Leader whose coalition includes refugees and locally described groups.
- Han-recognized outer vassal charged with frontier protection and court-access obligations.
- Expansionist ruler who uses military and material capacity against nearby polities.
- Dynastic founder whose accumulated position passes eventually to Ugeo.
