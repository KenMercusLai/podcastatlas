# 齐向东 / Qi Xiangdong

## Overview
齐向东 / Qi Xiangdong appears in [[no-216-shi-bu-sha-yi-ren-hongyi-dapao-zhouhongyi-de-sanshinian-quanmian-zhanzhengshi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-27-1008693437]] as the founder associated with early Qihoo and as the executive counterpart to [[ZhouHongyi|周鸿祎]] during the [[Qihoo360|Qihoo 360]] platform-war and later security-business period.

## Current Profile
The source uses Qi as an operating and organizational node rather than as the central biography. He matters because Qihoo's search-to-security turn, the 3Q crisis narrative, and the later Qianxin separation make the company less reducible to Zhou's public persona alone.

## Key Characteristics
- Qihoo-side founder/operator figure tied to the company's pre-360 and security-software formation.
- Crisis participant in the episode's 3Q account, including the report to Zhou that police had come to the company.
- Enterprise-security branch figure because the source says Qianxin separated from 360 and the two sides later became competitors.

## Evidence
- Early company role: [[no-216-shi-bu-sha-yi-ren-hongyi-dapao-zhouhongyi-de-sanshinian-quanmian-zhanzhengshi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-27-1008693437]] says Zhou joined Qi Xiangdong's Qihoo after leaving Yahoo and that Qihoo initially worked on search before turning toward rogue-plugin cleanup and 360 Security Guard.
- 3Q crisis role: [[no-216-shi-bu-sha-yi-ren-hongyi-dapao-zhouhongyi-de-sanshinian-quanmian-zhanzhengshi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-27-1008693437]] says Qi told Zhou that more than 30 police officers had come to the company during the Tencent conflict.
- Security-business separation: [[no-216-shi-bu-sha-yi-ren-hongyi-dapao-zhouhongyi-de-sanshinian-quanmian-zhanzhengshi-zhongguo-hulianwang-gushi-27-1008693437]] says Qianxin became independent and that by 2019 360 and Qi no longer had an equity relationship, turning the sides into competitors.

## Qualifications
Qi is not the episode's main subject, and this page should not be read as a full biography. It captures only the Qihoo, 3Q, and Qianxin relationships surfaced by this source.
