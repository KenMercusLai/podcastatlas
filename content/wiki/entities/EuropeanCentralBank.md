# European Central Bank

## Overview

The European Central Bank is the euro-area monetary institution presented in this source as having tools intended to prevent sovereign borrowing stress from becoming a self-reinforcing panic.

## Current Profile

In the episode's French debt scenario, the ECB is a circuit breaker rather than a complete fiscal solution. Its measures may interrupt a confidence spiral, while a separate European rescue fund could lend with conditions. Neither mechanism eliminates France's need to align borrowing, revenue, spending, growth, and political capacity over time.

## Key Characteristics

- Acts as a potential backstop against self-fulfilling euro-area sovereign panic.
- Can affect market confidence and borrowing conditions without setting national budgets.
- Sits beside, but is distinct from, conditional European rescue lending.
- Reduces acute spiral risk without removing debt-growth arithmetic.
