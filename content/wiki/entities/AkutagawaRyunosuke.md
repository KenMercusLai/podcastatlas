# 芥川龙之介 / Ryunosuke Akutagawa

芥川龙之介 / Ryunosuke Akutagawa first appeared in the wiki through [[76-xuanguailu-wantang-dangzheng-meidanwu-zaixiang-xie-damahou-de-gushi-683323220]], where his adaptation of [[DuZiChun|《杜子春》]] was used as a comparison point rather than a full author study.

The source says Akutagawa changes the ending so the test turns on seeing suffering parents rather than seeing one's child killed. In the episode's reading, the altered ending makes [[TranscendenceAgainstHumanFeeling]] more explicit: if becoming immortal means accepting the destruction of basic human bonds, then failing the test can be ethically preferable.

[[17-diyubian-zhongsheng-jie-ku-yi-nian-ru-mo-561780565]] turns Akutagawa into a central subject. [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] presents him as a short-story master whose work uses cruelty, religious pressure, social satire, and narrative compression to ask whether people can still be saved. The episode links his 1921 China trip, [[LuXun|鲁迅]]'s early translations, family background, and suicide to the dark pressure of the stories while keeping the literary reading source-scoped.

The episode's main case is [[HellScreen|《地狱变》]], read through [[UnreliablePraiseNarration]], [[HumanWorldAsHell]], and [[ArtAsRevenge]]. It then connects [[InAGrove|《竹林中》]] and [[RashomonFilm|黑泽明《罗生门》]] to [[RashomonEffect]], and uses [[TheSpiderThread|《蛛丝》]], [[KappaAkutagawa|《河童》]], [[TheNoseAkutagawa|《鼻子》]], and [[ShiroAkutagawa|《小白》]] to define an [[AkutagawaSalvationProblem]].
