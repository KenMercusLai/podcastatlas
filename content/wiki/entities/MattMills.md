# Matt Mills

## Overview
Matt Mills is identified in the current source as president of [[SailPoint]] and an advocate for extending identity governance from human accounts to non-human identities and AI agents.

## Current Profile
Mills presents enterprise security as a problem of governing access to data through people, devices, bots, and agents. His current operating position keeps every agent attached to an accountable human while moving authorization toward real-time, context-sensitive policy. He also distinguishes controlling access from guaranteeing the quality or predictability of an agent's output.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames identity as a frontline security control rather than a back-office compliance function.
- Supports accountable human ownership for discovered non-human identities.
- Favors contextual, fine-grained, real-time authorization over static permissions.
- Treats regulation, transparency, auditability, and demonstrated performance as foundations for trust.
- Acknowledges that complex probabilistic systems will still produce unintended consequences.
