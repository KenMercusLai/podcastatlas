## Overview

Zhang Mu is the Nanyang official who joins [[CenZhiLateHan|岑晊]] in urging [[ChengJinLateHan|成瑨]] to arrest and kill [[ZhangFanWanLateHan|张汎]]. His source role is narrower than Cen Zhi's but still important: he marks the enforcement action as an internal Nanyang administrative push rather than Cheng Jin acting alone.

## Current Profile

Hanji 871 introduces Zhang Mu as a Nanyang-side official, specifically a crime-office actor in the Zhang Fan case. He does not receive a full independent biography, but his co-presence with Cen Zhi matters because the action against Zhang Fan is presented as a local-administrative judgment against a protected private power.

The aftermath makes that judgment dangerous. Cheng Jin and Liu Zhi are jailed and later remembered as dying in prison, while Cen Zhi and Zhang Mu escape. Zhang Mu therefore belongs to the same liability chain as Cen Zhi, but the current evidence does not give a later shelter scene or independent speech for him.

## Key Characteristics

- Nanyang local official connected to criminal enforcement.
- Co-advocate with Cen Zhi for action against Zhang Fan.
- Participant in an enforcement case that rebounds through eunuch complaint.
- Fugitive survivor after Cheng Jin and Liu Zhi die in prison.
- Source-bounded supporting actor rather than a developed biographical profile.
