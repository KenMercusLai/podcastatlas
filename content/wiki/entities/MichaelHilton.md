# Michael Hilton

[[MichaelHilton]] is the lawyer in [[sand-heists-and-property-rights-in-the-caribbean-summer-school]] who investigated the 2008 stolen-beach case in northern [[Jamaica]]. He was called after an entire beach was allegedly removed by trucks and later reasoned that the missing sand was too large to hide and had likely been spread somewhere visible.

Hilton's role makes [[IllegalSandMining]] a proof and enforcement problem. The case involved holes and trenches, suspected resort use, helicopter inspection, sand forensics, police sampling, witness intimidation, dropped charges, and later settlement without admission of guilt.
