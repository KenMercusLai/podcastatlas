# Braden (The Residency founder)

Braden is one of the resident founders interviewed in [[tanfang-hacker-house-guigu-nianqingren-zhengzai-banjin-ai-chuangye-sushe-s10e10-f0870843-fd16-49f5-8f24-80281f9fff77]]. The source identifies him as a 20-year-old founder who began programming at age nine, did data-science and AI research at [[MIT]] Lincoln Laboratory at 13, and later helped [[OpenAI]] test GPT-3.

His source role is to explain why even a founder who already has an apartment may choose a hacker house. Braden wants age-peer density and "silent collaboration," making him a case for [[FounderLifestyleInfrastructure]] and [[StartupCommunityInfrastructure]] rather than only housing affordability.
