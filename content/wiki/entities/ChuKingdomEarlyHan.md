# 楚国 / Chu Kingdom (early Han)

楚国 / Chu Kingdom enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-174-liubang-weihe-dafeng-zijide-chouren-1-lnzzvpieqtgatmvgogz3msyhs60u|Hanji 174]] as the early Han kingdom [[LiuBang|刘邦]] grants to his younger brother [[LiuJiao|刘交]]. The source places Xue, Donghai, Pengcheng, and related counties in this new Chu kingdom.

This page is distinct from [[ChuState|楚国]], the pre-Qin and Chu-Han state tradition. The Hanji 174 use is a post-founding royal jurisdiction created after [[HanXin|韩信]] loses the older Chu kingship.
