# 贵小厨 / Gui Xiao Chu

贵小厨 / Gui Xiao Chu is a Guangxi-cuisine restaurant case in [[ep245-yinian-yidu-xunwei-zhilv-jinnian-qu-chi-chuangyi-cai-li9wtavemokcckialbbj7unpmqsz]]. The episode mentions its 老友鱼 as a more stable way to express the familiar 老友粉 flavor structure through fish.

The case is used for [[GuangxiCuisineLegibility|广西菜可识别化]]. Rather than relying only on 螺蛳粉 as the outside world's memory point, Gui Xiao Chu shows how a restaurant can move a regional flavor into a dish form that still feels recognizable, repeatable, and easier to explain.
