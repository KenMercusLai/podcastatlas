# 时雷 / Shi Lei

时雷 is the [[ShifenXiyin|十分吸引]] speaker in [[129-huobi-de-benzhi-yiji-huangjin-de-zhenzheng-jiazhi-chuantai-shifen-xiyin-lsjbfttqxf58uk-a4g8-srretkwb]], where he develops a monetary framework around [[MoneyAsFlow]], [[EndogenousMoneyCreation]], [[PaymentClearingNetwork]], [[CurrencyAnchorTransition]], and [[GoldAsCurrencySpareTire]]. His role is to move the discussion away from "money is printed by the central bank" toward money as credit, settlement, network consensus, and a changing unit of wealth measurement.

He was earlier referenced in [[vol-105-ruhe-panduan-yige-touzi-zuhe-shifou-shihe-ziji-lmaowq8820pa0jyjw6z93b6hstpe]] as the person behind 十分吸引 and as a source for the phrase "复杂适应性系统" in the episode's portfolio discussion. That source says Shi Lei has tried to localize [[BridgewaterAssociates|Bridgewater]]-style all-weather thinking for Chinese-market [[AssetAllocation]].

The wiki should keep the distinction between his framework and the host's caveat. In the money episode, [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] says the digital-currency section is worth understanding but remains partly outside his own conviction, so Shi Lei's Bitcoin and future-anchor claims should be treated as a source-scoped allocation-school view.
