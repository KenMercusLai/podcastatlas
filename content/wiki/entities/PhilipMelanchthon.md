# Philip Melanchthon

## Overview

Philip Melanchthon was a humanist scholar and Wittenberg colleague who joined the academic network supporting [[MartinLuther]] during the early Reformation controversy.

## Current Profile

The episode gives Melanchthon a supporting rather than independent narrative role. Alongside [[AndreasKarlstadt]], he helped make [[Wittenberg]] an intellectual base for Luther after the Ninety-five Theses moved from university dispute into a public church crisis.

His Greek-shaped scholarly name also illustrates the humanist academic culture surrounding the reformers: classical languages, self-presentation, and biblical scholarship belonged to the same university environment that sustained theological controversy.

## Key Characteristics

- Humanist scholar associated with Wittenberg University.
- Early academic ally of Luther.
- Participant in a faculty network larger than one reformer.
- Representative of the Greek and classical-language culture surrounding biblical reform.

## Evidence

- Academic alliance: [[435-luther-the-battle-against-satan-part-3-glt9360370091]] names Melanchthon among the Wittenberg faculty supporting Luther.
- Humanist setting: [[435-luther-the-battle-against-satan-part-3-glt9360370091]] discusses the Greek adaptation or translation of scholarly names around Melanchthon and Luther.

## Qualifications

This episode only establishes Melanchthon's early alliance and scholarly setting. It does not support a full profile of his theology, institutional work, relationship with Luther, or later role in Protestant confession-building.

## What Changed

- Established Melanchthon as part of Luther's early Wittenberg support network.
- Added the humanist naming and language culture surrounding that alliance.
