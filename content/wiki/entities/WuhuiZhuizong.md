# Wuhui Zhuizong / 无悔追踪

## Overview
Wuhui Zhuizong / 《无悔追踪》 is a Chinese television drama discussed here as a decades-spanning story about policeman Xiao Dali tracking suspected Nationalist spy Feng Jingbo through political movements, neighborhood life, secrecy, and historical time.

## Current Profile
The source treats 《无悔追踪》 as much more complex than a simple spy-hunting or main-melody story. Its force comes from refusing to settle the moral status of the two central men too neatly: Xiao Dali may be politically loyal and morally persistent, but he is also swallowed by suspicion; Feng Jingbo may carry a dangerous identity, but decades of near-nonaction turn espionage into an identity prison.

The drama's importance lies in [[HistoricalAmbiguityInMainstreamCinema|historical ambiguity]]. Hu-tong daily life, political campaigns, family silence, and social change make large history visible without turning every wound into one person's fault. Its ending leaves residue because Feng Jingbo's confession gives Xiao Dali's life meaning while also raising the question of whether that meaning redeems the lives consumed by the pursuit.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses spy suspicion as a long historical condition rather than only a suspense mechanism.
- Presents Xiao Dali and Feng Jingbo as mirror figures trapped by suspicion, secrecy, and time.
- Embeds political movements in hu-tong daily life, neighborhood relations, family damage, and ordinary humor.
- Preserves uncertainty around conversion, loyalty, paranoia, and moral responsibility.
- Contrasts sharply with [[ZhuaTewu|《抓特务》]], which the source says gives clearer answers and a neater reconciliation.
