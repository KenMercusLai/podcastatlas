# 杜素娟 / Du Sujuan

杜素娟 appears in [[ep254-you-xiaoying-x-wenqi-x-du-sujuan-nvxing-zhe-yisheng-dou-zai-fanfu-xuexi-xuke-ziji-lm-w7ryj4zay6vldsrkadohj997d]] as a 华东政法大学文学课老师 and viewer of [[WoXukeFilm|《我许可》]]. She reads the film through three generations of women and repeatedly maps 黄薇, 许可, and 胡春荣 onto her own girlhood, awakening, motherhood, and relation to her mother and daughter.

Her episode role gives the wiki several concepts at once. Her menarche story sharpens [[MenstrualStigma]], her refusal to let trainee doctors observe her gynecological exam grounds [[GynecologicalExamDignity]], her "second face" account reframes appearance anxiety, and her mother-daughter reflections deepen [[MotherDaughterIntergenerationalMirror]] and [[FemaleSelfPossession]].
