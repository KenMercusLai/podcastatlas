# Super Design

## Overview

Super Design is an open-source design agent that began as a weekend project for generating multiple frontend or visual-design variants inside an IDE.

## Current Profile

The product is used in the source as both a distribution success and an experimental operating system. Its reported organic GitHub growth showed open source could market an AI tool; later model and coding-agent progress reduced the perceived independence of its design capability. A [[Codex]] plugin then produced a reported growth inflection and motivated the team's ecosystem strategy.

## Key Characteristics

- Generates multiple design or frontend variants in an IDE workflow.
- Grew primarily through open-source and organic distribution in the source account.
- Became the test bed for a two-month AI-operations experiment.
- Received a reported growth and retention boost from a Codex plugin.
