## Overview

申耽 / Shen Dan enters the current wiki as the Shangyong administrator who submits when the Liu Bei-side force under Liu Feng and Meng Da arrives.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1165-zhangji-zhuan-didiao-caishi-dingji-shengcun-zhihui-lr7x5qltfi_ikktqabowt1garphl|Hanji 1165]] presents Shen Dan's response as a pragmatic submission under visible military pressure. Liu Bei reportedly leaves him in office, making territorial expansion depend on retained local administration rather than complete immediate replacement.

## Key Characteristics

- Local Shangyong officeholder facing an advancing external force.
- Chooses submission when the Liu Bei-side military position appears stronger.
- Retains administrative office after changing allegiance.

## Evidence

Submission under pressure:

Local continuity:
- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1165-zhangji-zhuan-didiao-caishi-dingji-shengcun-zhihui-lr7x5qltfi_ikktqabowt1garphl|Hanji 1165]] says Liu Bei retains Shen Dan as administrator.

## Qualifications

The current evidence does not establish Shen Dan's earlier allegiance, independent power base, negotiation terms, freedom of choice, reliability after submission, or later career. Retention in office may indicate pragmatic continuity, limited replacement capacity, or a negotiated settlement; the source does not distinguish among them.
