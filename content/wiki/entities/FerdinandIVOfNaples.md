# Ferdinand IV of Naples

## Overview

Ferdinand IV was king of Naples during the failed 1798 campaign against France, the royal flight to Palermo, and the 1799 counterrevolutionary restoration.

## Current Profile

The episode portrays Ferdinand as a popular but politically crude monarch overshadowed by [[MariaCarolina|Maria Carolina]]. He entered Rome after an initially unopposed advance, fled as French forces returned, and depended on [[HoratioNelson|Horatio Nelson]] to evacuate the royal family and treasure. From exile he sent [[CardinalRuffo|Cardinal Ruffo]] to raise a royalist army, then rejected Ruffo's lenient capitulation and rewarded Nelson after republican prisoners were detained and punished.

## Key Characteristics

- Neapolitan king associated with popular monarchy more than administrative seriousness.
- Figurehead of a premature triumph in Rome and rapid military collapse.
- Exiled ruler dependent on British naval evacuation and protection.
- Sponsor of Ruffo's counterrevolutionary mission.
- Restored monarch whose demand for punishment displaced a negotiated settlement.

The episode's comic portrait may flatten Ferdinand's government, popularity, and agency. Military decisions, formal authority over the capitulation, instructions to Nelson, punishment totals, and responsibility for individual cases remain source-scoped.

- [[FrancescoCaracciolo]] - former royal naval officer executed as a traitor after restoration.
