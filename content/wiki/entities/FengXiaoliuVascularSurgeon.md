## Current Profile
The source presents 冯小六 as medically trained but initially inexperienced in medical aesthetics. She describes moving from cleaning and acid treatments toward light-based procedures, water-light injections, and a prior hyaluronic-acid chin injection, while emphasizing that her perceived benefits, pain, and recovery are personal rather than universal.

Her role is complementary to [[LuluPlasticSurgeon|Lulu]]'s specialist guidance. 冯小六 makes the novice decision process concrete: appearance goals may relate to on-camera work and bare-faced presentation, but safety and individual tolerance still come before copying another person's result.
