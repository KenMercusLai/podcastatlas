# 渤海郡 / Bohai Commandery (late Han)

渤海郡 / Bohai Commandery (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-958-jiemi-shishang-zui-duanming-de-tongchoudikai-lve41q9gyijbop8wk-b7e6shogju|Hanji 958]] as the office [[YuanShao|袁绍]] gives to [[GongsunFanLateHan|公孙范]] after [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] blames Yuan Shao for [[GongsunYueLateHan|公孙越]]'s death. Yuan Shao appears to use the commandery as an appeasement or de-escalation signal.

The episode's point is that an office is also a military resource. Gongsun Fan brings Bohai's troops over to Gongsun Zan, so the concession strengthens Yuan Shao's rival instead of calming him. Bohai therefore becomes a compact source case for how [[InterestStructuredAllianceFragility|利益结构式联盟脆弱性]] can turn title grants and commandery control into defections.
