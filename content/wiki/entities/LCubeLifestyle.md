# L-Cube Lifestyle

## Overview
L-Cube Lifestyle is [[EricAlexen]]'s Florida company making UV-protective polyamide apparel for outdoor use.

## Current Profile
The company sells online and through Amazon while exploring wholesale and co-branded sports and outdoor opportunities. Its central strategic issue is category legibility: certification and fabric construction do not by themselves tell a shopper why to buy, so the advisers propose positioning the garment as mechanical sunscreen and distributing it where sun protection is already understood.

## Key Characteristics
- Apparel made with certified UPF 50 fabric in the source's account.
- Direct, marketplace, wholesale, and co-branded channel experiments.
- Margin pressure from Brazilian manufacturing tariffs.
- Requires education because shoppers may overestimate ordinary clothing's protection.
- Candidate for sunscreen-like packaging and sun-protection retail placement.

## Qualifications
The positioning is live advice, not validated customer research. The episode does not establish feasibility, retailer acceptance, packaging safety, or comparative protection across garment conditions.
