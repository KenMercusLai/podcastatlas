# The World of Yesterday / 《昨日的世界》

The World of Yesterday / 《昨日的世界》 is [[StefanZweig|Stefan Zweig / 茨威格]]'s memoir and the entry point for [[139-cong-zuo-ri-de-shi-jie-dao-ru-jin-de-ou-zhou-mei-hao-nian-dai-belle-epoque-ta-hai-neng-hui-lai-ma-ltvsgov8ht9-l7yp9qamdgjmyrf]]. The episode reads it as a witness text: a private memory of [[Vienna]], European cultural networks, travel, war, nationalism, Jewish exile, and the collapse of a liberal-cosmopolitan world.

The source's strongest interpretive move is to make the book contemporary. The hosts compare Zweig's memory of prewar Europe with a generation that grew up under globalization and then encountered deglobalization, visa friction, immigration politics, the [[Ukraine]] war, tariff conflict, and renewed national boundaries. That makes the book useful for thinking about [[BelleEpoque|Belle Epoque / 美好年代]] as both a real cultural memory and a class-filtered nostalgia.
