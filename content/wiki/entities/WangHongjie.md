# 王洪杰 / Wang Hongjie

Wang Hongjie / 王洪杰 is the historian interviewed in [[ep252-taipingnian-zhiwai-de-wudai-shiguo-shenme-yang-lnxm7tfs3zmve3erm9qlbehlefkh]]. The episode presents him as a scholar of [[FiveDynastiesTenKingdoms|五代十国]] whose work grew out of [[TangSongTransition|唐宋转型]] questions and research on [[FormerShu|前蜀]] political history.

In this source, Wang's main role is translation between specialist history and public understanding. He uses regime classification, periodization, personalities, institutions, social mobility, women, food, war, and memory to make the period legible without flattening it into a simple "乱世" label.
