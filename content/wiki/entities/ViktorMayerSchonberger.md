# Viktor Mayer-Schonberger / 维克托·迈尔-舍恩伯格

## Overview
Viktor Mayer-Schonberger / 维克托·迈尔-舍恩伯格 appears in [[episode-227-ershiyishiji-qingchun-wuyu-8211230332-278487]] through [[DeleteBook]], which Ren Ning uses to explain the social value of forgetting in a digital environment that remembers by default.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Mayer-Schonberger as the digital-memory theorist behind a key reversal: for most of human history, remembering required effort and forgetting happened easily, but digital records make memory cheap and deletion laborious. That reversal matters in the episode because young people grow up knowing that words, conflict, and mistakes can be screenshotted, searched, copied, and reported.

## Key Characteristics
- Supplies the source's digital-memory and forgetting frame.
- Is used to argue that forgetting can enable change, forgiveness, and fresh starts.
- Connects archive-like digital environments with self-discipline and risk avoidance.
- Helps explain why platform-native socialization can make mistakes feel less survivable.
