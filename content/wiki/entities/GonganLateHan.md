## Overview

公安 / Gongan is the riverside base where Hanji 1113 places [[LiuBei|刘备]] after the immediate post-Red-Cliffs allocation of southern Jingzhou territory.

## Current Profile

The episode presents Gongan as a limited foothold, not yet the broad and secure Jingzhou base associated with later “借荆州” shorthand. [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] controls [[JianglingLateHan|江陵]] and other Sun-side commanders hold nearby positions, while Liu Bei occupies a smaller south-bank area under the title of Jingzhou governor.

Gongan therefore marks the transition between mobile coalition dependence and territorial administration. It gives Liu Bei a headquarters and local platform, but the episode stresses that he still depends on [[SunQuan|孙权]] and remains constrained by the postwar distribution of force.

## Key Characteristics

- Riverside headquarters in the post-Red-Cliffs Jingzhou settlement.
- Limited Liu Bei foothold rather than uncontested control of the whole province.
- Territorial bridge between mobile political capital and later base expansion.
- Position whose value depends on the surrounding Sun-Liu distribution of counties, commands, and military pressure.

## Qualifications

The episode compresses county allocation, office appointment, naming, and jurisdiction. Gongan should not be used by itself to settle the full chronology of Liu Bei's Jingzhou expansion or later ownership claims.

## What Changed

- Created Gongan as the limited foothold preceding the larger Jingzhou base-transfer debate.
- Distinguished headquarters value from complete provincial ownership.

## Relationships

- [[LiuBei|刘备]] - ruler who establishes the postwar headquarters here.
- [[SunQuan|孙权]] - stronger coalition partner whose territorial decisions condition the foothold.
- [[JianglingLateHan|江陵]] - larger corridor strongpoint controlled separately in the episode's settlement.
- [[Jingzhou|荆州]] - province within which the limited base is contested and politically interpreted.
- [[StrategicBaseTransferAllianceConsolidation|战略基地转移式联盟巩固]] - later mechanism that expands a foothold into more durable partner capacity.
