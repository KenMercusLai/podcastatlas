# Hofburg Palace

## Overview

Hofburg Palace is the Vienna court setting where [[MaryVetsera]] is brought for clandestine meetings with [[CrownPrinceRudolf]].

## Current Profile

The episode uses the Hofburg as both imperial space and secret-access stage. Mary's first visits depend on [[CountessLarish]] and [[JosefBratfisch]], turning ordinary court geography into a hidden route between public dynastic authority and private scandal.

## Key Characteristics

- The Hofburg is the imperial setting that makes private access politically charged.
- Mary's route into the palace is mediated by cover stories and servants.
- After Mayerling, news returns toward the Hofburg and crowds gather outside before clear details are public.

## Evidence

- Secret access: [[690-a-murderous-affair-death-at-mayerling-part-2-glt7203783068]] says Mary is driven to Rudolf's Hofburg apartments through Larish and Bratfisch's arrangements.
- Gothic opening: [[690-a-murderous-affair-death-at-mayerling-part-2-glt7203783068]] uses Mary's letter about a dark entrance and ominous objects to frame the palace visit.
- Public shock: [[690-a-murderous-affair-death-at-mayerling-part-2-glt7203783068]] says crowds gather outside the Hofburg once news of Rudolf's death spreads.
