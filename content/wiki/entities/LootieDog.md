# Lootie

## Overview

Lootie was a Pekingese taken from Beijing's Old Summer Palace during the Second Opium War and presented to Queen Victoria, making the dog a living symbol of imperial plunder.

## Current Profile

The episode says John Hart Dunne found the dog after British and French forces sacked the Old Summer Palace in 1860 and brought her to Britain. Victoria's name for the dog made the circumstances of acquisition explicit rather than incidental.

Lootie was painted but then sent to the Windsor kennels and, in the episode's account, received little sustained royal attention before an unmarked burial. Her afterlife therefore sits uneasily between pet history, breed history, royal collecting, and the violence through which conquest objects entered British domestic life.

## Key Characteristics

- Pekingese removed from the Old Summer Palace after its sack.
- Transported to Britain by John Hart Dunne.
- Presented to [[QueenVictoria|Queen Victoria]] as a conquest trophy.
- Named in a way that openly registered looting.
- Remembered through a portrait and an allegedly neglected later life.

## Qualifications

The episode provides a short narrative rather than an independently verified animal biography. The precise discovery circumstances, Queen Victoria's intent in naming her, degree of neglect, date of death, and burial details remain source-scoped.
