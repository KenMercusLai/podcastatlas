## Overview

《申报》 / *Shen Bao* was a Shanghai newspaper founded in 1872 that the current wiki source presents as a sustained public forum for the [[YangNaiwuXiaoBaicaiCase|杨乃武与小白菜案]].

## Current Profile

[[29-dongxifang-de-yuanan-deleifusi-yangnaiwu-he-xiaobaicai-lmmhqfl-idiuetsvufgrmpso2l-8|episode 29]] says *Shen Bao* began following the case soon after it arose in 1873 and published more than seventy related pieces across over three years. Coverage allegedly moved from local curiosity toward scrutiny of factual findings, torture, procedure, the Beijing review, and the post-mortem examination.

The newspaper did not independently produce exoneration. Its role was to keep a local judgment visible while family petitioning, [[HuXueyan|Hu Xueyan]]'s resources, metropolitan official interest, and formal review channels supplied other kinds of leverage. The episode therefore treats it as an emerging public-opinion institution joining older patronage and petition practices rather than replacing them.

## Key Characteristics

- Early Shanghai newspaper founded shortly before the case it covered.
- Sustained attention to one local case across multiple years rather than a single sensational report.
- Shifted emphasis from curiosity toward evidence, torture, review procedure, and official accountability.
- Extended a local dispute into a wider public conversation about late-Qing justice.
- Worked alongside family advocacy, elite networks, and formal state review rather than outside them.

## Evidence

- Publication setting: [[29-dongxifang-de-yuanan-deleifusi-yangnaiwu-he-xiaobaicai-lmmhqfl-idiuetsvufgrmpso2l-8|episode 29]] dates the paper's founding to 1872 and the case to the following year.
- Coverage scale and development: [[29-dongxifang-de-yuanan-deleifusi-yangnaiwu-he-xiaobaicai-lmmhqfl-idiuetsvufgrmpso2l-8|episode 29]] reports more than seventy articles and a shift toward evidentiary and procedural criticism.
- Coalition role: [[29-dongxifang-de-yuanan-deleifusi-yangnaiwu-he-xiaobaicai-lmmhqfl-idiuetsvufgrmpso2l-8|episode 29]] places press coverage beside petitions, patronage, Beijing officials, and capital review.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one case and one podcast account. It does not establish *Shen Bao*'s full ownership, editorial politics, readership, distribution, finances, wider reporting record, or direct causal effect on the final ruling. The number, genres, wording, and chronology of its case articles require verification against the newspaper archive.
