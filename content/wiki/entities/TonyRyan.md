# Tony Ryan

## Overview

Tony Ryan is presented as an Irish aircraft-leasing pioneer who founded [[GuinnessPeatAviation|GPA]] after recognizing the value of placing idle airline capacity with overseas operators.

## Current Profile

Ryan's episode-bounded importance lies in turning one aircraft-placement problem into a repeatable leasing business. His early arrangement combined an idle Aer Lingus Boeing 747 with crew and maintenance support for a Thai airline, showing that aircraft access could be packaged as an operating service rather than only an asset sale.

## Key Characteristics

- Founder associated with Irish aircraft-leasing pioneer GPA.
- Opportunity spotter who connected an idle aircraft with overseas airline demand.
- Early practitioner of a bundled aircraft, crew, and maintenance arrangement.
- Entrepreneur whose GPA experience and restructuring aftermath fed into his later Ryanair path.
