# Luxeed / 智界

## Overview

Luxeed / 智界 is the [[Chery]] and [[Huawei]] branch in [[HongmengZhixing|鸿蒙智行]]. [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] uses it to show that each Hongmeng Zhixing brand adapts to a different automaker's capabilities. [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]] adds an R7 owner case where the car becomes a high-altitude mobile office and recording space.

## Current Profile

The current profile has two layers. The organization layer says Luxeed depends on Chery manufacturing, supply-chain, and export capacity, with early S7 delivery friction showing the execution stress of deep cooperation. The user-experience layer says R7 can be read as a smart-cabin proof point when a user turns its passenger space, laminated glass, shades, and interior materials into an emergency work setup.

## Key Characteristics

- Represents the Chery branch of the Huawei alliance.
- Demonstrates that Hongmeng Zhixing brands are adapted to partner capacity rather than copied from [[AITO|问界]].
- Carries a known execution caveat from early S7 delivery friction and later organization repair.
- EP279 adds R7 as a smart-vehicle living-space case through high-altitude voice recording and mobile work.
- The R7 story also supports a broader long-distance EV use claim, while remaining source-scoped.

## Evidence

- **Partner branch:** [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] identifies Luxeed as the Chery-Huawei branch and emphasizes Chery's mature R&D, manufacturing, supply-chain, and export background.
- **Execution stress:** [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] treats S7 chip shortages, factory relocation, platform ramp issues, and Chery's later independent Luxeed unit as evidence that partner organization design matters.
- **Mobile work scene:** [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]] describes a voice actor using a Luxeed R7 near Yamdrok Lake for urgent high-altitude recording, citing space, laminated glass, physical shades, and soft cabin materials.
- **Travel use:** [[ep279-dang-fangxiangpan-manman-songkai-women-ruhe-yu-che-xiangchu-lq2elbnjy6xrhixxqjs-y7erhlnv]] says the same owner drove a pure-electric R7 along the Chengdu-Lhasa route and found current infrastructure adequate for many lines outside some no-man's-land routes.

## Qualifications

- The R7 cabin-work story is an owner anecdote, not an acoustic or charging-network benchmark.
- The S7 delivery-friction evidence remains important; strong product definition still depends on partner-side execution.
- The episode's positive EV-travel claim does not remove route planning, charging, weather, altitude, and remote-area risk.
