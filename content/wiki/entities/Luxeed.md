# Luxeed / 智界

Luxeed / 智界 is the [[Chery]] and [[Huawei]] brand branch in [[HongmengZhixing]]. [[no-215-huawei-buzaoche-hongmeng-zhixing-daodi-shi-shenme-1007636843]] uses it to show that each 鸿蒙智行 brand adapts to a different automaker's capabilities rather than copying [[AITO|问界]] exactly.

The source emphasizes Chery's mature manufacturing, supply-chain, and export background, then contrasts that strength with early delivery friction around 智界 S7. Chip shortages, factory relocation, and platform ramp issues are presented as cooperation stress points; Chery's later independent 智界 business unit and legal entity are presented as organizational repair.

## Source Position
- Luxeed is the Chery branch of the Huawei alliance.
- The source treats delivery friction as a serious execution issue, not as proof that the cooperation model cannot work.
- Chery's independent business-unit response shows that partner organization design matters after Huawei supplies product and technology pressure.
