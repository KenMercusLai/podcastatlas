# Think Again

## Overview

*Think Again* is an [[AdamGrant]] book arguing that intellectual strength includes the capacity to question assumptions, test opinions, and experience revision as learning rather than defeat.

## Current Profile

In the bounded conversation, the book contrasts scientist mode with the defensive roles of preacher, prosecutor, and politician. It frames overconfidence as a cycle of conviction, confirmation, and arrogance, and rethinking as a cycle of humility, doubt, curiosity, and learning.

Grant does not ask readers to abandon causes or core values whenever evidence changes. The practical distinction is [[PrincipleStrategySeparation]]: principles may remain stable while plans are treated as hypotheses open to replacement.

## Key Characteristics

- Treats opinions as hypotheses rather than possessions.
- Contrasts inquiry with preaching, prosecuting, and politicking.
- Recasts doubt and correction as inputs to curiosity and learning.
- Preserves core principles while making strategies revisable.
- Connects achievement to humility, generosity, and character.

## Evidence

- Rethinking cycle - [[malcolm-gladwell-and-adam-grant-malcolm-gladwell-and-adam-grant]] contrasts overconfidence with humility, doubt, curiosity, and continued learning.
- Scientist mode - [[malcolm-gladwell-and-adam-grant-malcolm-gladwell-and-adam-grant]] recommends experiments, observation, and conversations that challenge one's reasoning.
- Stable values, flexible methods - [[malcolm-gladwell-and-adam-grant-malcolm-gladwell-and-adam-grant]] records Grant's distinction between commitment to principles and flexibility about plans.

## Qualifications

The source is an author conversation rather than the complete book or a systematic review of the research behind it. Some commitments are not safely experimental, and willingness to revise does not by itself determine which evidence or moral principle should govern.

## What Changed

- Established the book's rethinking model around scientist mode and stable principles with revisable plans.
