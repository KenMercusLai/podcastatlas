# Zhuhai / 珠海

Zhuhai appears in [[no-205-chaoshan-wangshi-qiaopi-jingji-tequ-he-meiyou-denglaide-shenzhen-qiji-989721046]] as one of the first four special economic zones. Its role in the episode is comparative: like [[Shenzhen]], it had direct border-adjacent logic through Macau, while [[Shantou]] was framed more as an overseas Chinese hometown zone.

The source uses Zhuhai to make [[ChinaSpecialEconomicZoneAsymmetry]] concrete. First-batch status did not mean equal area, equal strategy, or equal access to nearby capital and manufacturing spillovers.
