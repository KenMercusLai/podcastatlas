# 泾阳君 / Jingyang Jun (Qin)

泾阳君 / Jingyang Jun appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-89-qinwang-jing-bei-ta-dui-mabaonan-ljugedx8qxhtuzd1ja-xiwxgse19]] as one of the four Qin nobles named by [[FanJu|范雎]] in his warning to [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]].

The episode identifies the four nobles as close kin of the ruler and treats them collectively rather than biographically. Jingyang Jun therefore functions here as one part of [[QinFourNoblesCourtPower|秦国四贵权力结构]], the kin-and-office bloc that makes the Qin ruler appear isolated in his own court.
