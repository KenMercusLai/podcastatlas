# Mia / 自由职业营销从业者

Mia is the former internet-company ecommerce marketing worker and later freelancer in [[ep257-bu-shangban-yi-chang-taoli-yu-xunzhao-de-fuza-maoxian-li-wflyd48dmxvgersphxfp-gnng]]. She began not working in the ordinary organizational sense around 2019 after long-running misfit with institutional work, a serious conflict with a boss over ethically and commercially questionable tasks, workplace exclusion, depression, gastrointestinal symptoms, and recurring hives.

Her post-exit path is a useful but non-generalizable case. She quickly found an overseas brand client and moved into work she understood and could control more directly, which makes her story a concrete extension of [[SelfDirectedWork]]. The same story also grounds [[FreelanceMarketExposure]]: she had to handle customer sourcing, self-presentation, payment risk, advance payments, and the loss of a company's back-office shield.

## Key Claims
- Mia's case should not be read as proof that quitting automatically creates freedom; she repeatedly names luck, timing, existing skill, and client access.
- Her desired certainty is less about a fixed salary than about project control, professional ethics, and the right to decide how work is done.
- Her mother accepting and even defending the phrase "没工作，现在自己干" shows how family support can reduce stigma without removing long-term economic pressure.
- Her advice emphasizes [[WorkSelfWorthBoundary|separating work outcomes from self-worth]] and practicing small acts of off-work boundary setting.
