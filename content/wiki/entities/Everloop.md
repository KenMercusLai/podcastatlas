# Everloop

Everloop is the sustainable baby-gear company run by [[VinceGiudice]] and his wife in [[advice-line-with-curt-richardson-of-otterbox-e94bf774-de11-4434-843b-a551309a6773]]. The source says the company launched with a three-in-one high chair made from recycled HDPE and an organic bouncer chair, and it offers customers 20% cash back when they return products they no longer need.

The Advice Line discussion reframes Everloop around [[CircularProductTakeback]] and [[MeasuredChannelTesting]]. [[GuyRaz]] says the buyback promise may be more compelling to parents than the environmental material story alone, while [[CurtRichardson]] advises testing a handful of measurable channels and calling customers to understand why they bought and where they first heard about the company.
