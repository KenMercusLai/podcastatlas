## Overview
Zhou Jing was a late-Han senior official and Zhou-clan figure who appears in the current wiki as [[YangBingLateHan|杨秉]]'s partner in Huan-era personnel cleanup and anti-corruption enforcement. Hanji 858 expands him from companion anti-corruption actor to sikong who joins Yang Bing in an accepted cleanup request against eunuch-linked and unqualified officeholders.

## Current Profile
[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-858-weishenme-quan-ni-yiding-yao-huanwei-sikao-lobr-xznqo-ctjpc-ytpstubmahl|Hanji 858]] gives Zhou Jing's earliest current layer. It says he becomes sikong after Liu Chong is removed and identifies him as grandson of Zhou Rong, who opposed Dou Xian and is later connected in the source to the ancestry of [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]]. In office, Zhou Jing joins Taiwei Yang Bing in arguing that eunuch relatives, guests, and unqualified youths have spread through local offices, creating official resentment and popular hardship.

The request matters because it is accepted. Zhou Jing and Yang Bing ask Huan to have named civil, military, and guard offices report dismissible personnel to the Three Excellencies, turning anti-eunuch criticism into a review procedure. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-866-zhenzheng-huomingbai-de-ren-dongde-sanbuzhan-lhhmmyjfo6mkf-qs6bhhzu8hhln6|Hanji 866]] then supplies a later companion layer in which Zhou Jing cooperates with Yang Bing to punish corrupt officials. Across both notes, Zhou Jing matters less as a full biography and more as evidence that clean senior officials could coordinate institutional action, even while eunuch protection made enforcement risky.

## Key Characteristics
- Sikong in Huan's 163 court who joins Yang Bing in a structured cleanup request against eunuch-linked officeholding.
- Zhou-clan prestige figure whose source background links him to Zhou Rong and later Zhou Yu ancestry.
- Evidence of coordinated clean-ministerial action during eunuch ascendancy.
- Secondary but useful contrast case to officials punished for acting against protected eunuch interests.

## Qualifications
Zhou Jing is not yet a fully synthesized late-Han ministerial figure in the wiki. Hanji 858's Zhou Rong and Zhou Yu note is retained as source-scoped family background, not a reason to expand Zhou Jing into a clan-history hub. Until later sources expand his career, the page should remain a scoped companion profile to Yang Bing's integrity, personnel-review, and anti-corruption material.
