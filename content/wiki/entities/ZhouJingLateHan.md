## Overview
Zhou Jing was a late-Han senior official who appears in the current wiki as Yang Bing's partner in anti-corruption enforcement. His page is intentionally narrow: he matters here because he helps show that clean officials could still act, even while eunuch protection made enforcement risky.

## Current Profile
Zhou Jing's current evidence comes from the 165 CE episode in which he cooperates with Yang Bing to punish corrupt officials. The episode uses him less as an independent biography and more as part of a clean-ministerial counterweight to Emperor Huan's eunuch favorites.

That narrow role still matters. Zhou Jing's pairing with Yang Bing shows that integrity was not only a private trait; it could become coordinated office action when senior officials were willing to apply law against corrupt actors.

## Key Characteristics
- Senior late-Han official associated with Yang Bing in anti-corruption enforcement.
- Evidence of coordinated clean-ministerial action during eunuch ascendancy.
- Secondary but useful contrast case to officials punished for acting against protected eunuch interests.
