## Overview

阴兴 / Yin Xing (Guangwu maternal kin) is synthesized as a trusted Yin-family outer relative whose usefulness comes from restraint and public-minded judgment. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-721-ta-sha-jianu-ma-gongzhu-weihe-fanbei-shang-lryzcejdnrm4-92esnddfvyjkqli|Hanji 721]] presents him as a crown-prince guide and talent recommender; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-722-rang-liuxiu-xiansi-de-toutongbing-zenme-huishi-logwccdsca7vm2zzssu8ubm8rwra|Hanji 722]] then places him near Liu Xiu's illness-contingency risk and his refusal of the great marshalship.

## Current Profile

Hanji 721 gives Yin Xing an earlier public-role layer. [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] has him help guide Crown Prince Liu Zhuang, and the episode emphasizes his habit of recommending people based on usefulness to the state rather than personal closeness. He can praise someone with whom he has a poor relationship when that person is useful, and he does not promote showy but unfit friends at court.

Hanji 722 gives Yin Xing the emergency-trust layer. When Liu Xiu suffers severe headache and dizziness in 44 CE and fears he may die, he appoints Yin Xing concurrently as shizhong and summons him into the Yuntai Guangshi to entrust affairs. The episode presents this as evidence that Yin Xing is unusually trusted because he thinks from Liu Xiu's position rather than treating maternal-family access as private opportunity.

After Liu Xiu recovers, he wants Yin Xing to replace the dead [[WuHanEasternHan|Wu Han]] as great marshal. Yin Xing refuses by kneeling, weeping, and saying that he dares not accept because it would damage Liu Xiu's sage virtue. The current synthesis is that Yin Xing's value lies in self-limiting access: he helps with heir guidance, recommends talent beyond private feeling, and refuses the largest office when kinship makes it available.

## Key Characteristics

- Guangwu maternal relative identified as Yin Lihua's younger brother.
- Crown-prince guide in the 43 CE personnel-education layer.
- Public-minded recommender who separates state usefulness from friendship.
- Emergency trusted figure summoned when Liu Xiu fears death from severe illness.
- Temporary shizhong appointment in the illness-transfer scene.
- Refuses the great marshalship after Liu Xiu recovers.
- Outer-relative restraint figure whose political value lies in declining overlarge office.

## Qualifications

This page does not reconstruct Yin Xing's complete biography, offices, death date, or the full Yin-family network. The route remains deliberately suffixed because a later Hanji 740 source mentions a different Yin Xing context in the Chu-prison material, and this ingest does not prove those references are the same person. The transcript form 殷兴 is normalized to 阴兴 for continuity with the established Guangwu maternal-kin page.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 721 as the earlier crown-prince guidance and public-minded recommendation layer.
- Reframed Yin Xing as a restraint figure across both talent recommendation and refusal of excessive office.
- Preserved Hanji 722 as the emergency illness-trust and great-marshal refusal branch.
