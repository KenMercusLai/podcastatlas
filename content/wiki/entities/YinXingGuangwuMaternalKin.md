## Overview

阴兴 / Yin Xing (Guangwu maternal kin) is synthesized as a trusted Yin-family outer relative whose usefulness comes from restraint and public-minded judgment. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-709-mian-dui-bangjia-an-guren-weihe-buguan-renzhi-sihuo-luknor8yw839qwxogeyzwluo6qe2|Hanji 709]] now supplies the earliest direct layer: he refuses title or enfeoffment and advises [[YinLihua|阴丽华]] that outer relatives must not seek offices, marriage privilege, or luxury. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-721-ta-sha-jianu-ma-gongzhu-weihe-fanbei-shang-lryzcejdnrm4-92esnddfvyjkqli|Hanji 721]] presents him as a crown-prince guide and talent recommender; [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-722-rang-liuxiu-xiansi-de-toutongbing-zenme-huishi-logwccdsca7vm2zzssu8ubm8rwra|Hanji 722]] places him near Liu Xiu's illness-contingency risk and his refusal of the great marshalship.

## Current Profile

Hanji 709 establishes the restraint pattern at the family-reward level. Yin Xing is offered high honor because of Yin-family proximity to Liu Xiu and Yin Lihua, but he refuses and tells Yin Lihua that outer relatives should be satisfied, modest, and politically careful. The warning is not only personal humility; it is an argument that kinship privilege can create resentment, office distortion, and danger for the household.

Hanji 721 gives Yin Xing a public-role layer. [[LiuXiu|光武帝刘秀]] has him help guide Crown Prince Liu Zhuang, and the episode emphasizes his habit of recommending people based on usefulness to the state rather than personal closeness. He can praise someone with whom he has a poor relationship when that person is useful, and he does not promote showy but unfit friends at court.

Hanji 722 gives Yin Xing the emergency-trust layer. When Liu Xiu suffers severe headache and dizziness in 44 CE and fears he may die, he appoints Yin Xing concurrently as shizhong and summons him into the Yuntai Guangshi to entrust affairs. After Liu Xiu recovers, he wants Yin Xing to replace the dead [[WuHanEasternHan|吴汉]] as great marshal. Yin Xing refuses by kneeling, weeping, and saying that he dares not accept because it would damage Liu Xiu's sage virtue.

The current synthesis is that Yin Xing's value lies in self-limiting access. Across family title, heir guidance, talent recommendation, emergency trust, and refusal of the largest office, he repeatedly prevents maternal kinship from becoming private extraction or excessive public office.

## Key Characteristics

- Guangwu maternal relative identified as Yin Lihua's younger brother.
- Early outer-relative restraint figure who refuses title or enfeoffment and advises Yin Lihua against family overreach.
- Crown-prince guide in the 43 CE personnel-education layer.
- Public-minded recommender who separates state usefulness from friendship.
- Emergency trusted figure summoned when Liu Xiu fears death from severe illness.
- Temporary shizhong appointment in the illness-transfer scene.
- Refuses the great marshalship after Liu Xiu recovers.

## Qualifications

This page does not reconstruct Yin Xing's complete biography, offices, death date, or the full Yin-family network. The route remains deliberately suffixed because a later Hanji 740 source mentions a different Yin Xing context in the Chu-prison material, and this ingest does not prove those references are the same person. The transcript forms 殷心 and 殷兴 are normalized to 阴兴 for continuity with the established Guangwu maternal-kin page.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 709 as the earliest title-refusal and Yin-family restraint-advice layer.
- Reframed Yin Xing's later crown-prince guidance and great-marshal refusal as continuations of the same self-limiting access pattern.
- Preserved Hanji 721 and Hanji 722 as the public recommendation, emergency trust, and high-office refusal evidence.
