# Forge Global

Forge Global is the private-market platform in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-inside-the-private-stock-market-boom-spacex-anthropic-openai-the-rise-of-secondaries-41564170]], represented by CEO [[KellyRodriques|Kelly Rodriques]]. The episode frames Forge as part of the infrastructure that could make [[PrivateCompanySecondaries|private-company secondaries]] more standardized, company-permissioned, and productized.

The source presents the [[CharlesSchwab|Charles Schwab]] relationship as a signal that private shares are becoming an investable asset class rather than only a brokered shadow market. Forge's role is not simply matching buyers and sellers; it is building the rails for [[RegulatedSPVPrivateMarketAccess]], company-approved programs, and eventually lower-minimum products for broader investors.
