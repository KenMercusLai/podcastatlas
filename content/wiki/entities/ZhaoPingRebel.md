# 昭平 / Zhao Ping (Rebel)

昭平 / Zhao Ping enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-1-guailaotou-yiqiao-san-shua-zhangliang-chuanshou-shenshu-lqwjo13zzfgtndpajzpuvrb2h4l2|Qinji 130-1]] as a Guangling rebel actor originally sent by [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] to seize Guangling. He has not taken the city when he hears of Chen Sheng's death.

With [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]]'s Qin force approaching, Zhao Ping crosses the Yangtze to see [[XiangLiang|项梁]]. He falsely claims Chen Sheng has appointed Xiang Liang as Chu upper pillar of state and argues that Jiangdong is already pacified, so Xiang Liang should lead the [[KuaijiCommandery|会稽郡]] troops west against Qin.

His role is therefore not battlefield command but authorization manufacture. After [[ZhangChuRegime|张楚政权]] has lost its founder, Zhao Ping uses Chen Sheng's name as a remaining symbolic instrument to move the Xiang family from regional seizure into wider anti-Qin war.
