# Serge Laurent

## Overview
Serge Laurent is identified by the source as [[VanCleefArpels|Van Cleef & Arpels]]' director of dance and cultural programs and the curator leading [[DanceReflections|Dance Reflections]].

## Current Profile
Laurent approaches dance from archaeology, art history, museum work, and curation rather than from professional performance. His account centers on bodies as a shared but culturally interpreted medium, choreography as spatial architecture, and curation as the work of supporting artists while connecting them with different publics. He values dance that leaves interpretive room and argues that a work's most important effect may emerge after spectators return home.

## Key Characteristics
- Trained in Egyptian archaeology and art history, including study at the École du Louvre, according to the episode.
- Treats the human body as a shared expressive medium capable of communication beyond spoken language.
- Reads choreography through spatial occupation as well as visible movement.
- Frames curation as artist support plus deliberate connection between works, venues, and audiences.
- Values open interpretation, imperfection, ordinary movement, and delayed audience reflection.
