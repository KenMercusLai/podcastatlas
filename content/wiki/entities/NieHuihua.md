# 聂辉华

聂辉华 is the [[RenminUniversityOfChina|中国人民大学]] economics professor and author of [[YiQieJieQiyue|《一切皆契约》]] discussed in [[79-gewei-lingdao-danfan-zan-xuedian-boyilun-690004262]]. The episode presents him as a public-facing interpreter of [[ContractTheory]], [[GameTheory]], and real-world workplace and market decision problems.

His wiki role is to connect abstract economic theory to ordinary management situations: credentials, price wars, shirking, performance assessment, equity incentives, executive pay, and internal marketization all become cases where contract structure matters more than simple moral labeling.
