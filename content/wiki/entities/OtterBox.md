# OtterBox

OtterBox is the protective phone-case and mobile-accessories company founded by [[CurtRichardson]] and discussed in [[advice-line-with-curt-richardson-of-otterbox-e94bf774-de11-4434-843b-a551309a6773]]. The source says it began with waterproof boxes for outdoor enthusiasts, then grew rapidly after the iPhone created demand for protective cases.

In the episode, [[GuyRaz]] cites a source-scoped claim that OtterBox grew from $5 million to $575 million in five years after the iPhone became popular. Richardson later says the phone-case market became more commoditized, consumers replaced phones less often, and OtterBox revenue fell from a source-scoped $1.1 billion peak while profit improved.
