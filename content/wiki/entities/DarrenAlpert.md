# Darren Alpert

## Overview
Darren Alpert is the founder and CEO of [[Gheelish]], the ghee-based snack company that calls into [[advice-line-with-ben-goodwin-of-olipop-f9d3fe17-eb2c-440d-b223-990fed970727]].

## Current Profile
Alpert appears as a food founder who already has meaningful wholesale traction but is still working out how to explain an unfamiliar ingredient to mainstream shoppers. His case turns founder enthusiasm for ghee into a sharper packaging and customer-conversion problem.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder and CEO of [[Gheelish]] in Raleigh.
- Built early demand from homemade samples rather than from a DTC-first model.
- Won nationwide Sprouts placement after a cold email.
- Raised money from Gary Vaynerchuk and from friends and family.
- Asks how to educate shoppers about ghee without losing the simple reason to buy the snack.
