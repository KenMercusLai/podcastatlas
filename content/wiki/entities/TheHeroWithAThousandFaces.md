# 《千面英雄》 / The Hero with a Thousand Faces

[[TheHeroWithAThousandFaces|《千面英雄》 / The Hero with a Thousand Faces]] is the [[JosephCampbell|Joseph Campbell / 约瑟夫·坎贝尔]] book discussed in [[29-qianmian-yingxiong-edipusi-zhengzai-xiaban-ji-ditie-590112891]]. The episode presents it as a work with large literary and creative influence but real scholarly controversy: its broad comparison across world myths is powerful as pattern recognition, yet not fully aligned with more historical, source-specific, and transmission-aware mythology research.

The book matters in the wiki because it names [[Monomyth|单一神话]] and organizes [[HeroJourneyNarrative]] into departure, initiation, and return. The episode reads those stages through myths, fairy tales, religion, epics, and modern media while keeping [[InterpretationAndOverinterpretation]] visible: Campbell's scheme can clarify story structure, but it should not replace local evidence or turn every resemblance into proof of one origin.
