# Kuse

Kuse is the AI workspace company discussed in [[women-shi-ruhe-dingyi-openclaw-for-teams-xin-chanpin-xingtai-de-duitan-kuse-junior-lianchuang-jian-cto-yuhao-lkp1a0todflxoyycyo3zhrap3ebv]]. [[Yuhao]] describes the product as evolving from early email-marketing and design-agent experiments into a folder-based workspace where files, work context, and agents sit together.

The source says Kuse reached roughly $10 million ARR after a difficult product and pricing reset. Its lesson for the wiki is that [[AgentNativeSoftware]] is not only a capability demo: useful agents require [[AIInferenceCostStructure|cost-aware pricing]], retained user context, customer segmentation, [[AgentEvaluationBenchmarks]], and a willingness to abandon product forms that no longer fit the best retained users.

Kuse also serves as the internal testbed for [[Junior]]. Its own agents include [[KuseRing|Ring]], [[Azura]], and [[KuseTom|Tom]], and the company treats agent hiring as part of [[AIOrganizationDesign]]: new human headcount requests have to answer why the job cannot be done by an agent first.
