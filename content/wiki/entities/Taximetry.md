# Taximetry

## Overview
Taximetry is an AI-assisted advertising-creative company founded by [[RobinTauquedet]] and represented by one [[TomorrowToday]] interview.

## Current Profile
The source positions Taximetry between brand libraries and ad-distribution platforms. It generates or recombines creative assets, tests variations, returns campaign evidence to later production, and offers delegated, licensed, and white-label workflows. Its stated differentiation is orchestration across specialized models plus brand-specific controls, but the episode does not independently establish campaign uplift or defensibility against foundation-model providers.

## Key Characteristics
- Produces advertising variants intended to reduce [[CreativeFatigue]].
- Supports full generation as well as selection, cropping, and recombination of existing human-made assets.
- Uses different systems for images, video, voice, reasoning, and storytelling.
- Combines verifier agents with human review before public brand representation.
- Keeps performance feedback separate from the canonical brand hub through [[BrandIdentityFeedbackIsolation]].
- Leaves distribution to platforms such as [[Meta]] and [[Google]] while focusing initially on asset production.
