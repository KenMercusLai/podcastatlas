# D. C. Stephenson

## Overview

D. C. Stephenson was the Ku Klux Klan's powerful Indiana grand dragon, a mass recruiter and political broker whose assault of Madge Oberholtzer, murder conviction, and retaliation against former allies accelerated the collapse of the Indiana Klan.

## Current Profile

Stephenson fabricated a wealthy and heroic background while building an image of glamour, wealth, and inevitability. He expanded the Klan through aggressive recruitment, *The Fiery Cross*, theatrical arrival at the Kokomo gathering, and a data-driven “Military Machine” that linked ministers, endorsements, voting guidance, transport, and candidate support.

The same career joined corruption and predation. The episode describes a history of violence toward women, heavy drinking, political bribery, and the fatal assault of [[MadgeOberholtzer]]. After conviction and the refusal of a pardon, Stephenson released records of payments to officials, damaging Indiana Republicans and the [[KuKluxKlan]] network he had built.

## Key Characteristics

- He used false biography and theatrical self-presentation to manufacture authority.
- He was an unusually effective recruiter and newspaper-based propagandist.
- He built a coordinated electoral machine reaching candidates, ministers, school boards, and voters.
- Wealth, alcohol, sexual violence, and bribery contradicted the movement's public moralism.
- His leverage over officials survived conviction long enough to expose the corruption network.
