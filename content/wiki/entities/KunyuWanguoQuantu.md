# 《坤舆万国全图》 / Kunyu Wanguo Quantu

《坤舆万国全图》 enters the wiki through [[ep259-yu-miao-shifu-mantan-xu-guangqi-sibainian-qian-ruhe-rangren-biancongming-lkirczxnx8skbbfpmz5-m-mivs4e]] as the world-map object that first drew [[XuGuangqi|徐光启]] toward [[MatteoRicci|利玛窦]] and Western learning. The episode treats the map as an intellectual shock: it made the world visible through unfamiliar coordinates, scale, and spatial order.

The source's later discussion of a modern exhibition around the map also shows why maps are politically charged. Miao Wei recounts hearing claims that Ricci was a spy and Xu was a traitor, making the map a site where [[LateMingWesternLearning|西学东渐]], cultural insecurity, and present-day suspicion meet.

The page therefore connects cartography to [[HumanisticScienceCommunication|science communication]]: a map can explain, persuade, and unsettle because it changes what counts as the world.
