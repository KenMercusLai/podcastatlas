# Cordoba Caliphate / 科尔多瓦哈里发国

## Overview
The Cordoba Caliphate / 科尔多瓦哈里发国 appears in the Ceuta episode as the high-medieval Iberian Islamic power that extended influence into North Africa and took [[Ceuta]] and Melilla in 931.

## Current Profile
The source presents Cordoba as both political and cultural. Its Umayyad lineage after the Abbasid revolution, claim to caliphal status, and competition with Baghdad and the Fatimids are paired with its role as a center for literature, philosophy, art, astronomy, medicine, and translation.

## Key Characteristics
- Iberian Umayyad polity that claimed caliphal rank.
- Civilizational center in the source, not only a military power.
- Expanded southward enough to make Ceuta part of an Iberia-North Africa political field.
