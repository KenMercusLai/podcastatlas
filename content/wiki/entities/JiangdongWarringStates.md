# 江东 / Jiangdong (Warring States)

江东 / Jiangdong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-2-kongzi-zai-luguo-de-shenshi-zhimi-lphy12exg4u7f-30qkir96gv634r]] as the region [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] requests after arguing that his Huai north fief borders [[QiState|齐国]] and should be converted into commandery-county administration by [[ChuState|楚国]].

The source identifies Jiangdong with former [[WuState|吴国]] territory and treats it as safer than the exposed northern border. Its advantages are geographic: the Yangtze River provides a barrier, the region lies far from [[QinState|秦国]], and it gives Chunshenjun a more secure personal base while Chu's main strategic danger remains in the west.

That makes Jiangdong part of the wiki's fief-security branch. The move is not identical to [[FiefSecurityIllusion|封地筑城安全幻觉]] because Chunshenjun is not merely building walls around an exposed fief; he is trading an exposed frontier position for a deeper and safer regional base. The episode still treats the move skeptically because it may reveal personal safety preferences and a distorted threat map.
