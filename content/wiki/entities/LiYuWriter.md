# 李渔 / Li Yu

李渔 is cited in [[fanwai-15-banlatte-jiang-jinpingmei-la-erqie-haishi-luse-de-991508433]] as part of [[JinPingMei|《金瓶梅》]]'s reception history. The episode says he grouped the novel with [[WaterMargin|《水浒传》]], [[JourneyToTheWest|《西游记》]], and 《三国演义》 as "四大奇书", using that category to argue that Jin Ping Mei belonged inside major vernacular-fiction discussion rather than outside literature.

In this source, Li Yu's importance is classificatory. He helps establish [[GenreLiteraryLegitimacy]] for a work whose public reputation can be reduced to scandal, and he connects the episode's [[ClassicReadingComplexity]] frame to older Chinese critical ranking rather than only modern reinterpretation.
