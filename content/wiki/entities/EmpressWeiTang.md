## Overview

Empress Wei enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-536-3-jiemi-shangguan-waner-he-ta-beihou-de-nanrenmen-lqj7rpwwfllogrxrbav-xqwwnnnm|Hanji 536-3]] as a late Tang court power actor tied to [[ShangguanWaner|上官婉儿]] and [[WuSansi|武三思]]. The episode presents her less as an independent biography than as the factional pole that first protects Shangguan Wan'er and later becomes too dangerous to rely on.

## Current Profile

The episode says Shangguan Wan'er recommended Wu Sansi to Empress Wei, creating an alliance among the three. That alliance helps Wu Sansi move against Zhang Jianzhi and other anti-Wu restoration figures, while Shangguan Wan'er uses edicts to elevate the Wu family and marginalize Li-Tang royal members. After [[TangZhongzong|唐中宗]] dies in 710, Empress Wei's personnel moves appear to place her near a Wu Zetian-like path, but Shangguan Wan'er doubts the group's depth and begins preserving a Li-Tang fallback.

## Key Characteristics

- Factional partner of Shangguan Wan'er and Wu Sansi after the Shenlong Coup.
- Presented as a source of protection and influence within Tang Zhongzong's court.
- Associated with anti-Li and pro-Wu moves in the episode's account of edict politics.
- Becomes the dominant post-Zhongzong figure whose power alarms Shangguan Wan'er.
- Functions as the losing factional pole cleared by Li Longji during the Tanglong Coup.

## Qualifications

This page does not settle Empress Wei's full biography, her responsibility for Zhongzong's death, or the whole Tanglong Coup. The current evidence is limited to a compressed episode focused on Shangguan Wan'er.
