# 尹礼 / Yin Li (late Han)

尹礼 / Yin Li (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1026-jiemi-sanguo-di-yi-mengjiang-lvbu-de-luomu-lhjf-agujuliglqchumtff3tftxg1|Hanji 1026]] as one of the local armed actors whom [[ZhangBaLateHan|张霸]] brings toward [[CaoCao|曹操]] after [[LyuBu|吕布]] falls at [[Xiapi|下邳]]. The episode groups Yin Li with [[WuDunLateHan|吴敦]] and [[SunGuanLateHan|孙观]] rather than developing him separately.

For this source, Yin Li matters as part of the wider post-siege settlement. Cao Cao turns Zhang Ba's local prestige into submission and appointment channels, reducing the cost of controlling the Shandong/Taishan field before the northern confrontation.
