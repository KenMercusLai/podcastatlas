# Doubao Enterprise Edition / 豆包企业版

Doubao Enterprise Edition / 豆包企业版 is the enterprise packaging described in [[270-da-chang-yazhu-ai-bangong-feishu-he-dingding-que-xian-chengle-peijue-lmb4dgcgov3mr4cn7cikbghpfro4]]. [[EricFeishu|Eric]] says that in sales settings it can look like [[Feishu]] enterprise edition with a [[Doubao]] bot added: customers are buying AI, while Feishu becomes the carrying work surface.

The source uses this packaging to explain why [[Doubao]] may need Feishu more than Feishu needs Doubao in the short term. Doubao's consumer traffic can create heavy inference cost without clear direct monetization, while Feishu can connect Doubao to company documents, meetings, permissions, organizational relationships, and sales budgets.
