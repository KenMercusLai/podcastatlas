## Overview

Lou Hu / 楼护 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-616-you-xinji-de-ren-you-duo-kepa-lnhkk70905eqg4lqwh3mp2sxzamc|Hanji 616]] as the Guanghan governor who arrests [[LuKuanDogBloodCase|吕宽]] after the dog-blood case becomes a court matter.

## Current Profile

Lou Hu's role is a brief official-risk test. In the variant account, Lu Kuan reaches Guanghan and meets Lou Hu without disclosing the dog-blood act. Once the arrest order arrives, Lou Hu chooses state compliance over any private tie or hospitality obligation and sends Lu Kuan back to Chang'an.

## Key Characteristics

- Guanghan governor in the source's variant account.
- Temporary host or contact for the fugitive Lu Kuan.
- Official who chooses political self-protection and compliance with the arrest order.
- Contrast figure showing how Wang Mang's power reshapes private relation into state risk.
