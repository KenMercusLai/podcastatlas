# Gerard Way

Gerard Way appears in [[working-memory-the-surprising-decline-of-dementia-6a6b0eeca585d3be78cd9d1b]] as [[MyChemicalRomance]]'s frontman and the performer anchoring the band's revived stadium spectacle. The episode says he stayed in character during the set, wore a bandleader-style uniform, and framed the tour staging as political satire about authoritarianism.

His role in the source is to show that the emo comeback is not only catalog nostalgia. Performance, character, costume, and fan response make [[EmoRevivalIdentityCulture]] feel theatrical and participatory.
