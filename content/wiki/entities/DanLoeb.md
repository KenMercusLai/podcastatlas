# Dan Loeb

Dan Loeb is the [[ThirdPoint|Third Point]] founder, CEO, and CIO interviewed in [[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-dan-loeb-the-lost-art-of-short-selling-and-why-stock-picking-is-back-41552605]]. The episode presents him as an investor shaped by early online forums, short-selling campaigns, distressed-debt repetition, and the shift from opaque event-driven trades toward technology-aware [[StockPicking|stock picking]].

Loeb's investing account connects [[ShortSelling|short selling]] to forensic research and public pressure, but also warns that shorts cannot rest on valuation alone when momentum and retail enthusiasm can make expensive stocks more dangerous. His current framework puts [[ManagementQualityAsInvestmentEdge|management quality]], technology literacy, macro awareness, and cross-asset context alongside older [[EventDrivenInvesting|event-driven investing]] skills.

The same source also presents Loeb's philanthropy through education and criminal-justice reform. [[SuccessAcademies|Success Academies]] is the education-reform route into his concern with opportunity, while [[RossUlbricht|Ross Ulbricht]] and [[CriminalJusticeClemencyFramework]] represent his focus on individual cases where punishment, rehabilitation, or proportionality become central.
