## Overview

[[RayHuang|黄仁宇]]'s 《万历十五年》 is represented as a thesis-driven history that uses the outwardly uneventful year 1587 and six biographies to diagnose institutional limits in the [[MingDynasty|明朝]].

## Current Profile

The book's represented method joins narrative detail to [[MacrohistoricalInstitutionalAnalysis|大历史制度分析]]. [[WanliEmperor|万历帝]], [[ZhangJuzheng|张居正]], [[ShenShixing|申时行]], [[HaiRui|海瑞]], [[QiJiguang|戚继光]], and [[LiZhiMing|李贽]] pursue different strategies, but their achievements do not become durable, reproducible changes.

Its core diagnosis combines [[MoralSubstitutionForInstitutions|道德替代制度]] with weak [[NumericalManagement|数目字管理]]. Its principal qualification is equally important: six selected lives and a deliberately “insignificant” year are an interpretive construction, not a statistically representative account of the whole dynasty.

## Key Characteristics

- Begins from an apparently uneventful year rather than a conventional sequence of major events.
- Uses six contrasting biographies as windows into shared structural constraints.
- Connects political narrative to finance, land, military supply, information, and administrative technique.
- Blends literary storytelling with a strong historical thesis.
- Invites criticism for selecting cases around a prior diagnosis of systemic failure.

## Qualifications

This page records one podcast's account of the book. It does not verify quotations against an edition, reconstruct the publication record, or establish Huang's thesis as scholarly consensus. The six figures are interpretive windows, not a representative sample of Ming society.
