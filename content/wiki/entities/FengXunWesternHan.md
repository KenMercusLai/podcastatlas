## Overview

冯勋 / Feng Xun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-495-xihan-mingjiang-pk-yanren-chongchen-yingpengying-shui-neng-ying-lk9qa3yd8l17wyadfsi6cgubzfyo|Hanji 495]] as a Feng-family figure who privately warns [[HanYuandi|汉元帝]] about [[ShiXianWesternHan|石显]]'s monopoly of power.

## Current Profile

The episode gives Feng Xun a compact but consequential role. Shi Xian initially wants to approach the Feng family because [[FengZhaoyiWesternHan|冯昭仪]] is favored and the family is politically useful. Feng Xun instead tells Yuan that Shi Xian is monopolizing power and should be guarded against. Because Yuan already favors Shi Xian, the warning does not weaken Shi Xian; it makes Yuan displeased with Feng Xun and helps turn Shi Xian against the Feng family.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Feng-family figure in Hanji 495.
- Privately warns Han Yuandi against Shi Xian's power.
- Fails to overcome Yuan's trust in Shi Xian.
- His warning helps explain why Shi Xian later treats the Feng family as hostile.
