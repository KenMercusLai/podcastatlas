# Bucherer

Bucherer is the authorized watch retailer acquired by [[Rolex]] in 2023, as discussed in [[rolex-1]]. The source presents the acquisition as strategically significant because Rolex historically relied on authorized retailers rather than a broad owned-store network.

The hosts infer that Bucherer may give Rolex better visibility into customer data, waitlists, allocation behavior, and retail-channel power. That makes Bucherer the concrete source case for [[LuxuryRetailChannelControl]], especially when paired with Rolex's broader [[LuxuryScarcityDiscipline]].
