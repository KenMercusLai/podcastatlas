# 阏氏 / Ezhi (Warring States)

阏氏 / Ezhi appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-65-wo-chuanyue-qu-gei-langman-de-chuwang-nian-shou-shi-lvyd7pve-tfu4ixsqrbdvhm6g64j]] as the place reached by the five-state anti-Qin coalition in 296 BCE. The episode names Qi, Han, Wei, Zhao, and Song as the attacking states.

Ezhi's wiki role is to mark the point at which coalition pressure becomes strong enough for [[QinState|秦国]] to seek accommodation. Qin returns [[WusuiWarringStates|武遂]] to [[HanState|韩国]] and [[FenglingWarringStates|封陵]] to [[WeiState|魏国]], turning the battlefield notice into a case of [[StrategicConcessionDiplomacy|strategic concession diplomacy]] and [[HezongAlliance|hezong]] fragility.
