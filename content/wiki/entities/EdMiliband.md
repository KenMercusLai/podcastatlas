# Ed Miliband

Ed Miliband is one of the possible [[LabourPartyUK]] successors discussed in [[a-keir-death-experience-britains-pm-clings-on-698b0dc3337b2a3b2ef0aa07]]. The source presents him as another known Labour figure, but says his plans or route are unclear enough that he does not solve the immediate [[LabourLeadershipCrisis]].

[[a-firm-andy-what-are-new-british-pms-plans-6a5deeca76f8ee25bb4bbf9c]] uses Miliband as a cabinet-balance comparison after [[AndyBurnham]] becomes prime minister. The episode says [[ShabanaMahmood]] is rumoured as chancellor partly because she may be less divisive than Miliband or [[WesStreeting]].
