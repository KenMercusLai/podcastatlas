# 白公胜 / Bai Gong Sheng

白公胜 / Bai Gong Sheng enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-1-chaijie-jingke-ciqin-bi-bai-de-mimi-lnonhw9tlsu5dudu8750zvgeaz1p]] as [[SimaGuang|司马光]]'s negative comparison for [[YanTaiziDan|燕太子丹]] and [[JingKe|荆轲]]. The episode does not build a full biography; it uses him as the kind of person whose apparent 信 and 勇 fail because they are not governed by 仁义.

The source identifies Bai Gong Sheng as the son of [[TaiziJianChu|太子建]], a Chu crown prince caught in a violent succession and exile chain. That family background matters because it gives Bai Gong Sheng inherited grievance and a plausible revenge frame, but the episode's point is that grievance plus courage is still not enough to make an action righteous.

In the closing comparison, [[YeGong|叶公]]'s judgment becomes the key interpretive rule. Trustworthiness and bravery only become admirable when they are grounded in [[RenyiGroundedVirtue|仁义为本的信勇]]; otherwise, they can become destructive traits that resemble the assassin politics Sima Guang rejects in the Jing Ke branch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-119-2-zanmen-dou-yuanwang-le-beifu-2000-duonian-heiguo-de-yegong-haolong-lh-h-gezpqtvnyq40zlqac8s-5e]] gives the full narrative behind that comparison. [[ZiXiChu|子西]] recalls Bai Gong Sheng into [[ChuState|楚国]] despite Ye Gong's warning and grants him Bai county. Bai asks to attack [[ZhengState|郑国]] to avenge Taizi Jian, but Zi Xi first delays and then rescues Zheng when [[JinState|晋国]] attacks it, leaving Bai enraged.

The episode uses Bai's speech as evidence of character. While sharpening a sword, he tells Zi Xi's son that he is preparing to kill Zi Xi; the source treats this not as harmless bluntness but as [[SpeechAsMoralDiagnostic|言语作为德行信号]]. Bai later turns a victory over [[WuState|吴国]] into an armed entry to the Chu capital, kills Zi Xi, seizes [[ChuHuiwang|楚惠王]], and kills [[GuanXiuChu|管修]]. Ye Gong suppresses the [[BaiGongShengRebellion|白公胜之乱]], and Bai flees and hangs himself.
