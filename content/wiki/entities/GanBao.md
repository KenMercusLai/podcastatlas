# 干宝 / Gan Bao

干宝 / Gan Bao is the authorial figure attached to [[SouShenJi|《搜神记》]] in [[06-soushenji-ershisi-xiao-zhe-kou-guo-ganbao-bu-bei-536821044]]. The episode presents him as a historian-like collector who took ghosts, omens, and divine signs seriously, while also preserving material that later readers can approach as folklore, literature, religious evidence, and strange natural observation.

The page is source-scoped. Its point is not a full biography, but the episode's claim that "干宝不背" the whole burden of later [[TwentyFourFilialExemplars|《二十四孝》]] moralization. The source argues that he recorded filial wonders beside stories of resistance, revenge, nonhuman sympathy, wrongful punishment, and jealous authority, so his collection should not be reduced to the later didactic afterlife of selected tales.
