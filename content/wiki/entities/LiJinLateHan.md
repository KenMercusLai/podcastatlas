# 李进 / Li Jin (late Han)

李进 / Li Jin (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-980-lvbu-xiansha-caocao-kankan-yi-ju-hua-ruhe-niuzhuan-lishi-lgggw3vmonu-3wvzabvg943v5qcg|Hanji 980]] as the local figure who defeats [[LyuBu|吕布]] after the Puyang attrition phase. After locust damage and grain exhaustion force both sides to withdraw from the long fight around [[PuyangBattleLateHan|濮阳]], Lü Bu attacks [[ChengshiCountyLateHan|乘氏县]] and is beaten by Li Jin.

The page is intentionally source-scoped. The episode uses Li Jin mainly to show that Lü Bu's problem is not limited to Cao Cao: once supplies and momentum weaken, local resistance inside [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] can also push him eastward.
