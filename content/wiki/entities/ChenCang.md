# 陈仓 / Chen Cang

陈仓 / Chen Cang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-148-1-hanxin-yupan-le-zhanghan-de-yupan-lodhpuxpossjno87gorjkfrtitbe|Hanji 148-1]] as the central route and battle point in [[HanXin|韩信]]'s campaign against [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]]. The episode separates the later idiom "明修栈道，暗渡陈仓" from the full operation: Chen Cang is not a magic shortcut by itself, but the place where a broader design of army reform, flank feints, and central breakthrough becomes visible.

In the source's telling, Han Xin lets [[GuanYing|灌婴]] and [[FanKuai|樊哙]] create pressure on the side routes while [[CaoShen|曹参]] reinforces the false main-force signal. Zhang Han shifts attention toward those flanks, and the Han main force comes through Chen Cang, defeats him, and opens the route toward the collapse of [[ThreeQinContainment|三秦压制]].
