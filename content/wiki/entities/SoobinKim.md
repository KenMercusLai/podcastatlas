# Soobin Kim

## Overview

Soobin Kim is the reporter in the source who attends a Seoul Buddhist expo and explains young South Koreans' renewed interest in Buddhist events and practices.

## Current Profile

Kim's role is observational and interpretive. She reports on expo crowds, commercial stalls, a simulated Buddhist funeral experience, an AI monk music booth, temple stays, and the uncertainty over whether the trend is a fad or a lasting shift.
