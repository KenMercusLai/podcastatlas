# 吴懿 / Wu Yi (late Han)

## Overview
吴懿 / Wu Yi appears in Hanji 1146 as a Yizhou-connected elite whose sister's marriage to [[LiuBei|刘备]] helps link the conquering regime with the former Liu Zhang political network.

## Current Profile
The episode's current contribution is relational rather than biographical. It identifies Wu Yi as [[LadyWuLiuBei|吴氏]]'s brother and associates him with [[LiuZhang|刘璋]]'s regional power structure. Liu Bei's marriage to Lady Wu is therefore interpreted as a way to convert Wu Yi's local connection into a family tie during post-conquest consolidation.

## Key Characteristics
- Belongs to the elite network already embedded in Yizhou before Liu Bei's takeover.
- Serves as the brother linking Lady Wu's marriage to regional political integration.
- Illustrates how a conqueror may incorporate incumbent personnel through kinship rather than immediate exclusion.

## Qualifications
This page is bounded to one structured episode summary. Wu Yi's offices, military role, prior relationship with Liu Zhang, independent choices, later service, and the actual causal effect of the marriage require broader evidence. A family connection does not prove uniform elite loyalty.
