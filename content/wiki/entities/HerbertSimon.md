# Herbert Simon / 赫伯特·西蒙

## Overview
Herbert Simon appears in the source as an organizational and political-philosophy-adjacent thinker whose AI work represented the symbolic reasoning path around the Dartmouth workshop.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Simon to keep the Dartmouth story from becoming a narrow computing-specialist origin myth. Simon is described as coming from political philosophy and organizational research rather than from only mathematics or physics, and as having built AI-like machinery before attending Dartmouth.

Within the source, Simon's work with Allen Newell exemplifies symbolic AI: articulating human thought rules and using deductive reasoning to simulate human cognition.

## Key Characteristics
- Cross-disciplinary participant context rather than a narrow technical specialist in the episode's telling.
- Associated with [[AllenNewell|Allen Newell]] and symbolic reasoning.
- Said to have written little about Dartmouth in his autobiography.
- Used as evidence that early AI drew from organization, decision, and cognitive questions as well as computation.
