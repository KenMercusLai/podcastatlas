# Zheng Chao / 郑超

## Overview
郑超 is presented as director of endocrinology and of the weight-management center at [[ZhejiangUniversitySecondAffiliatedHospital|浙江大学医学院附属第二医院]]. In VOL.199, he explains weight management as metabolic-health work requiring cause assessment, sustainable pacing, muscle preservation, and coordinated care rather than starvation or rapid scale reduction.

## Current Profile
Within the current evidence, 郑超 is a clinician-educator who places endocrine evaluation at the entry to a broader [[MultidisciplinaryWeightManagement|multidisciplinary pathway]]. He distinguishes primary from secondary obesity, names thyroid, cortisol, pituitary, genetic, disease, and lifestyle factors as possible assessment contexts, and then connects nutrition, exercise rehabilitation, mental health, procedures, surgery, and monitoring to patient need. His public guidance repeatedly returns to gradual change, metabolic markers, body composition, and long-term maintenance.

## Key Characteristics
- Endocrinologist and weight-management-center director identified with Zhejiang University School of Medicine's Second Affiliated Hospital.
- Frames weight loss as metabolic-health and body-composition management rather than a scale-only or appearance project.
- Rejects unsupervised crash diets, complete carbohydrate avoidance, and celebrity timelines for ordinary listeners.
- Advocates cause-finding and individualized intervention before choosing diet, exercise, medication, endoscopy, or surgery.
- Presents mental health, muscle preservation, joint safety, and adherence as part of weight-management care.
- Describes connected monitoring and AI-assisted follow-up as promising but not yet mature.
