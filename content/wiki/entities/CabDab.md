# Cab Dab

Cab Dab is the cabbage-leaf-extract muscle and joint rub made by [[CabbageLabs]] and discussed in [[advice-line-with-bobbi-brown-of-jones-road-beauty-september-2025-427590c2-11bc-43fa-9c92-d1eccf05842a]]. [[MarkSokolowski]] says the product grew out of orthopedic patients using cabbage leaf wraps for pain and inflammation.

Cab Dab's source role is a trust-transfer problem. The product has early sales and profitability, but it needs to move from doctor-given full-size tubes into [[TrialSizeProduct]] samples, local media, social content, and trusted professional settings where the medical-origin story can travel without requiring Sokolowski to personally hand out every tube.
