# FireAid

[[all-in-with-chamath-jason-sacks-friedberg-spencer-pratt-on-fixing-la-wildfires-homelessness-corruption-the-fight-to-take-it-back-41236810]] adds [[FireAid]] as the fire-aid fundraising example [[SpencerPratt|Spencer Pratt]] uses while criticizing nonprofit and donation distribution in [[LosAngeles|Los Angeles]]. Pratt says victims told him they did not receive money and says he asked senators to investigate.

The episode does not adjudicate FireAid's finances or response. Its wiki role is to anchor [[MunicipalNonprofitAccountability]]: the campaign claim that emergency donations and public funds should be traceable to recipients.
