# Francesco Caracciolo

## Overview

Francesco Caracciolo was a Neapolitan aristocrat and naval officer whose defection to the [[ParthenopeanRepublic|Parthenopean Republic]] and rapid execution made him the central symbol of Nelson's Naples controversy.

## Current Profile

Caracciolo had served alongside the British and helped escort the royal family to Palermo before returning to Naples and joining the republic. Captured after the royalist reconquest, he was brought before [[HoratioNelson|Horatio Nelson]], tried aboard a British ship, and sentenced to hanging. Nelson refused requests to delay or change the method of execution; Caracciolo was hanged from a yardarm and cast into the sea. The episode treats his case as especially inflammatory because military service, aristocratic rank, personal defection, and royal vengeance converged in one prisoner.

## Key Characteristics

- Aristocratic officer in the Neapolitan navy.
- Former royal servant who assisted the flight to Palermo.
- Republican defector treated by the restored court as a personal and military traitor.
- Defendant tried rapidly under British naval supervision.
- Symbol of disputed honor, authority, and vengeance in Nelson's career.
