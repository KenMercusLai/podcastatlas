# Brothers to the Rescue

## Overview

Brothers to the Rescue is the Cuban émigré aviation organization whose refugee-search mission, later leaflet flights, and 1996 aircraft shootdown provide the crisis setting for [[the-queen-of-cuba-the-queen-of-cuba|The Queen of Cuba]].

## Current Profile

The organization was formed in Miami to search from the air for Cubans making dangerous sea crossings toward Florida. It later entered Cuban airspace and dropped anti-Castro leaflets over Havana, escalating conflict with the Cuban government. On February 24, 1996, Cuban MiG aircraft shot down two of its planes and killed four people; the United States condemned the attack, while the location of the aircraft became part of the political dispute.

For the episode, the organization matters less as a complete subject than as the setting for suspected foreknowledge. Cuba reportedly had a source inside the group, and an advance warning delivered to U.S. officials one day before the shootdown helped [[RegBrown]] form suspicions about [[AnaBelenMontes]].

## Key Characteristics

- Miami-based Cuban émigré organization initially focused on finding refugees at sea.
- Used private aircraft and later conducted anti-Castro leaflet flights over Havana.
- Lost two aircraft and four people to Cuban fighters on February 24, 1996.
- Became the subject of competing claims about airspace, warning, foreknowledge, and political responsibility.
- Serves as the precipitating event in the episode's Montes counterintelligence narrative.
