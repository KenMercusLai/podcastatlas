# 定陶之战 (195 CE) / Dingtao Battle (Late Han)

定陶之战 (195 CE) / Dingtao Battle (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-987-dingtao-zhizhan-caocao-he-lvbu-dianfeng-duijue-lofq0pq8n9rrfvfttunahisy0ave|Hanji 987]] as the battlefield payoff after [[XunYu|荀彧]] persuades [[CaoCao|曹操]] to secure [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] before pursuing [[Xuzhou|徐州]]. [[LyuBu|吕布]] and [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] advance from Dongming with more than ten thousand troops while most of Cao Cao's soldiers are out harvesting wheat, leaving fewer than one thousand in camp.

Cao Cao uses the local terrain to offset the force gap. The episode describes a western dike and southern forest near his camp; Cao Cao hides part of the force behind the dike, places a smaller visible group outside, and sends light troops forward to engage Lü Bu. When the fighting fixes Lü Bu's attention, the hidden soldiers attack and Cao's infantry and cavalry press together.

The result is a rout rather than a prolonged siege. Lü Bu withdraws at night, Cao Cao pursues, captures [[Dingtao|定陶]], and then splits forces to settle surrounding counties. The page therefore treats the battle as a compact case of [[BattlefieldInformationControl|战场信息控制]] and [[RootBasePriority|根本根据地优先]]: the earlier strategic choice to defend the base creates the conditions for an ambush victory that restores that base.
