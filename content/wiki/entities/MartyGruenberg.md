# Marty Gruenberg

Marty Gruenberg appears in [[tsr-s5-ronconway-v5-tsr-s5-ronconway-v5]] through [[RonConway]]'s account of the [[FederalDepositInsuranceCorporation|FDIC]] during the [[SiliconValleyBank]] weekend. Conway says the group heard Gruenberg was not inclined to guarantee deposits and was considering FDIC control or finding a buyer for the bank.

This page is intentionally narrow. The source uses Gruenberg as a decision-process figure inside the broader [[DepositGuaranteeCrisisResponse]] story, not as a standalone biography. His role is one side of the episode's tension between ordinary bank-resolution process and fast action to avoid contagion.
