# 《天真的人类学家》 / The Innocent Anthropologist

《天真的人类学家》 / *The Innocent Anthropologist* is the anthropology fieldwork book discussed in [[35-tianzhende-renleixuejia-zuo-tianye-hui-shangyin-de-603418624]]. The episode presents it as [[NigelBarley|Nigel Barley]]'s funny, self-mocking account of 1977-1979 fieldwork among the [[DowayoPeople|Dowayo people]] in [[Cameroon]].

The book matters to the wiki because it makes fieldwork method visible at the level usually excluded from formal ethnography: bureaucracy, transport, sickness, dentistry, debt, house building, eating, drinking, translation, missionary help, and local misunderstanding. That makes it a source for [[FieldworkRomanceDeflation]], [[EthnographicEverydayFriction]], and [[ParticipantObserverEntanglement]] rather than only a book about one African society.

## Key Claims
- The episode reads the book as a deflation of heroic anthropology, not as an attack on fieldwork itself.
- Barley's comic detail is methodological: daily logistics determine access, trust, dependence, and what can be known.
- The book's interest comes from showing the anthropologist being interpreted by the people he studies, not only from showing him interpreting them.
- Development projects, missionaries, colonial legacies, language, and state paperwork are part of the field site rather than outside context.
