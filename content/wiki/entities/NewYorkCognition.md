# New York Cognition

## Overview
New York Cognition is presented as an AI startup working on cognitive learning and continual-learning systems, with [[ApprenticeBench]] as its principal benchmark example in the supplied source.

## Current Profile
The interview positions the company around agents that improve after deployment by learning organizational rules, workflows, feedback, and task history. That places its stated problem beyond generic computer-use execution: the target is a system that develops from a novice into an experienced organizational worker across repeated tasks.

## Key Characteristics
- Research startup focused on cognitive and continual learning.
- Developer of a sequential production-learning benchmark.
- Interested in memory-note data and training methods for experience accumulation.
- Presented as a research company seeking both technical progress and market closure.
- Reported to be preparing further benchmark versions and a new financing round.
