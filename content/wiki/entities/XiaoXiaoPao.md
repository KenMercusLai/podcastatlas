# 小小跑 / XiaoXiaoPao

小小跑 appears in [[134-touzi-dashi-xilie-xiandao-pian-tamen-bu-zhi-zhuan-le-henduo-qian-geng-chuangzao-le-lijie-shijie-de-fangfa-lqkjpfqc0ymgvnboek5jmjycwtyg]] as the speaker from [[WenliLiangkaihua|文力两开花]] and [[QianglieTan|强烈谈]] who helped originate the [[InvestmentMastersSeries|投资大师系列]] with [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] and [[Yubai|雨白]]. His source role is to make investment masters a way to read the end of an era: when a cluster of masters ages or dies, the industry should ask what market structure, tools, and historical conditions made them possible.

The episode presents him as closer to trading, macro, virtual economy, and digital-asset perspectives than David Weng's stock and asset-allocation angle. That makes his contribution useful for [[InvestmentStyleMap]] because the same series needs to cover company research, macro trading, quant, and cycle thinking without pretending they are one method.
