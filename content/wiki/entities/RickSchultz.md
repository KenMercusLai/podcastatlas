## Overview
Rick Schultz is a retired [[PlanetMoney]] listener used as the episode's case for comfortable retirement during an otherwise stressful inflation period.

## Current Profile
The source presents Rick as financially comfortable because of pensions, retirement investments, homeownership, favorable timing, and earlier public supports. He notices higher gasoline and restaurant prices but does not experience the same day-to-day financial stress as many younger or working-age households.

## Key Characteristics
- He represents the asset-and-pension side of the divided economy.
- His retirement security combines state pensions, personal investing, homeownership, and timing rather than one simple savings choice.
- He expresses guilt and realism about younger households' weaker ability to save and invest.
- His story extends [[RetirementCashFlowSecurity]] by showing monthly income exceeding routine expenses.

## Evidence
- Comfortable cash flow: [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-8dbc38a9b9e4647dbd8e2ebddb601c4e-26201098-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-8dbc38a9b9e4647dbd8e2ebddb601c4e-26201098]] says Rick reports income above daily expenditures by nearly $2,000 a month.
- Pension and investment structure: [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-8dbc38a9b9e4647dbd8e2ebddb601c4e-26201098-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-8dbc38a9b9e4647dbd8e2ebddb601c4e-26201098]] says Rick has Kansas and Iowa public-service pensions and invested substantially in a personal retirement account from his mid-30s.
- Timing and policy support: [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-8dbc38a9b9e4647dbd8e2ebddb601c4e-26201098-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-8dbc38a9b9e4647dbd8e2ebddb601c4e-26201098]] names cheaper college, a now-expired first-time homebuyer tax credit, student loan forgiveness, and strong stock-market compounding as part of his security.

## Qualifications
Rick's case is not presented as a universal retiree condition. The episode treats it as an underreported but unevenly distributed retirement outcome, with luck, public benefits, market timing, and household discipline all contributing.
