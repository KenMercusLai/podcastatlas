## Overview

张奂 / Zhang Huan is a late-Han official, scholar, and frontier figure whose current wiki profile begins with 166 CE crisis redeployment. Hanji 870 shows the court moving him from frontier command to dasinong during Yuzhou famine, then sending him back to the northern frontier when raids intensify after his absence. Later sources add selective pacification, reward distortion, Qiang surrender-policy dispute, compromised military use in the Dou Wu crisis, post-coup guilt, direct remonstrance, and survival under pressure.

## Current Profile

Zhang Huan's profile is defined by useful capacity under bad political conditions. Hanji 870 makes state dependence visible: when Yuzhou famine is severe, Zhang is summoned to the agricultural-finance office; when Xianbei, Southern Xiongnu, Wuhuan, and Qiang pressure rises after he returns to Luoyang, the court sends him back as Protector of the Xiongnu with expanded oversight across frontier provinces and officials. Hanji 875 then shows the pacification side of that capacity: Southern Xiongnu and Wuhuan groups submit after Zhang returns, and he punishes only ringleaders while accepting the rest.

Hanji 878 gives a frontier-success and reward-gatekeeping layer. Zhang defeats [[XianlingQiang|先零羌]] forces attacking Sanfu, but the expected marquisate is reduced because he refuses to flatter eunuchs. He refuses the reduced reward and asks instead to move his household registration to Hongnong. Hanji 883 then shows his policy side: as Protector of the Xiongnu, he urges the court to accept surrender and peace from remaining [[DongqiangLateHan|东羌]] groups because elimination is difficult and [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]]'s hard line risks overreach.

Hanji 887 places Zhang in the military reversal that destroys Dou Wu. He returns to Luoyang without full information, the eunuch side uses forged imperial authority and Zhou Jing's temporary command, and Zhang moves with the remaining Five Camps troops into the confrontation. Hanji 889 and Hanji 890 show the repair attempt after that compromised use: Zhang refuses the title, asks for burial and relief around [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]], [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]], and [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦妙 / 窦皇太后]], and joins [[LiuMengLateHan|刘猛]] in recommending [[WangChangShumaoLateHan|王畅]] and [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]]. Hanji 902 shows a later and harsher risk environment, where Zhang survives by lowering his posture, withdrawing from office, teaching, and writing.

## Key Characteristics

- Scarce crisis official shifted between famine administration and frontier command.
- Frontier deterrence figure whose absence helps reveal northern-border dependence.
- Selective pacifier who accepts Southern Xiongnu and Wuhuan submitters while punishing ringleaders.
- Frontier commander whose military merit can be discounted by eunuch-side reward gatekeeping.
- Surrender-and-peace advocate when residual frontier groups are hard to eliminate cleanly.
- Anti-eunuch figure vulnerable to being used by the side he opposes during information confusion.
- Humbled survivor whose teaching and writing preserve agency after office withdrawal.

## Qualifications

- Hanji 870 gives a compact redeployment and frontier-dependence sequence; it does not reconstruct every operational detail of Zhang's office transfer, mandate, or frontier response.
- Hanji 875 gives a short summary of Zhang Huan's northern-frontier return and surrender handling; it does not reconstruct the full office appointment, operational route, or every submitter group.
- Hanji 887 and Hanji 889 make Zhang's knowledge and intention source-scoped: the stable claim is that he was used by the eunuch side, not that he deliberately supported the anti-Dou purge.
- Hanji 870, Hanji 875, Hanji 878, Hanji 889, and Hanji 890 render the name as "张宦"; this page normalizes to 张奂 because the frontier role, anti-eunuch reward pattern, and existing late-Han route match Zhang Huan.
- Hanji 883 renders the name as 张焕; this page normalizes to 张奂 because the Protector of the Xiongnu role and Duan Jiong dispute match the established route.
- The current page is still episode-bounded and should not be treated as a full biography of Zhang's frontier service.

## What Changed

- Hanji 870 adds a 166 CE redeployment layer before Hanji 875's selective-pacification layer.
- The page now treats Zhang as a scarce crisis official moved between famine administration and frontier command, not only as a frontier pacifier and later remonstrant.
- Hanji 878 and Hanji 889 remain distinct reward-refusal branches: one reduced after frontier merit, one morally compromised after the Dou / Chen crisis.
