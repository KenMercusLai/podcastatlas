## Overview

张奂 / Zhang Huan is a late-Han frontier and court official whose current wiki profile now begins with a 158 CE frontier crisis under [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]. Later layers make him a commander who combines visible composure, alliance fracture, pacification, selective force, court remonstrance, and survival inside unstable imperial politics.

## Current Profile

Zhang Huan's earliest current layer is the 158 CE Northern Corps command in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-844-ruhe-liyu-bubaizhidi-lsp49uw8atyiufrf-ryo-9ctos5z|Hanji 844]]. When the Southern Xiongnu and Wuhuan pressure the frontier and Zhang's troops panic, he publicly remains calm, lectures classics in camp, and lets his composure stabilize the soldiers before acting. His campaign then turns on secret diplomacy with [[Wuhuan|乌桓]], which fractures the Wuhuan-[[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] alignment and forces the Xiongnu side to submit.

The next major layer is a return from political disability into frontier usefulness. Hanji 859 says he had been barred from office after the Liang Ji case, that Huangfu Gui defended him repeatedly, and that he was first reused as Wuwei taishou. When frontier war intensified, Huangfu Gui then recommended Zhang as the better large-army commander and accepted a lower role, so the court made Zhang Duliao general.

The subsequent frontier record emphasizes calibrated command rather than one-note militarism. Across Hanji 870, 875, 878, 883, 887, 889, and 890, Zhang appears in famine redeployment, recall, Eastern Qiang pacification, selective pursuit, and court crisis. He can accept command, pursue enemies when needed, and also prefer settlement when the political objective is stabilization rather than destruction.

His court-politics profile is mixed but resilient. In Hanji 887-890, he becomes entangled in anti-eunuch maneuvers and countercoup dynamics, survives by reading danger quickly, and later remonstrates or refuses cooperation when a policy threatens state interest. Hanji 902 preserves him as an emotional-control and endurance example, so the full profile is not merely battlefield skill: it is the capacity to move between frontier coercion, negotiated order, and dangerous court judgment.

## Key Characteristics

- Crisis-composed frontier commander whose visible calm steadies troops before tactical action.
- Uses alliance fracture by turning Wuhuan away from the Southern Xiongnu and Tuge side before striking.
- Reads post-victory governing risk, even when Emperor Huan rejects his proposal to replace [[CheerChanyu|车儿]].
- Rehabilitated frontier talent whose later return to office depends on Huangfu Gui's repeated defense.
- Uses a mix of coercion and settlement in Qiang and border policy instead of default extermination.
- Operates in both field command and court crisis, where tactical success can become political vulnerability.
- Practices remonstrance and selective noncooperation when policy crosses his state-interest threshold.

## Qualifications

The page remains bounded to the listed podcast source notes and does not reconstruct Zhang Huan's full biography outside them. Hanji 844's "张焕" and Hanji 859's "张宦" transcript forms are normalized to 张奂 because the frontier command sequences and Huangfu Gui connection match the established route. Hanji 844's Che'er replacement debate is treated as Zhang's frontier judgment, not as proof that Emperor Huan's contrary legitimacy decision failed.
