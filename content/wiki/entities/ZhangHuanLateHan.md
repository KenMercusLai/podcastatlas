## Overview

张奂 / Zhang Huan is a late-Han official, scholar, and frontier figure whose current wiki profile joins two early [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]-era danger cases. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-890-xiang-dangguan-xian-xue-duifu-xiaoren-ltnvh6qu-mr8flsdlxf-e6rknpis|Hanji 890]] backfills him as a senior official who publicly reopens the [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] and [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] grievance. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-902-kongzhi-qingxu-shi-rensheng-bixiuke-ljynylvdlsdtjf4es9jmilav863b|Hanji 902]] later makes him a survival-first contrast with [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]].

## Current Profile

Zhang Huan's page now presents him as upright, visible, and politically vulnerable. In Hanji 890, he asks that Dou Wu and Chen Fan receive proper burial and that [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦皇太后]] receive due respect; he then joins [[LiuMengLateHan|刘猛]] in recommending [[WangChangLateHan|王畅]] and [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]] for high office. That direct pro-party and anti-eunuch memory pressure angers [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]]'s side and brings detention, rebuke, and a salary fine.

Hanji 902 shows a later and harsher risk environment. After [[WangYuLateHan|王寓]]'s refused recommendation becomes an accusation, Duan Jiong's pressure threatens Zhang's life. Zhang survives by lowering his posture in a letter, withdrawing from office, and turning his remaining agency toward teaching and writing. The synthesis is not that Zhang is timid; it is that his career shows why direct moral exposure and later self-preserving humility both belong to late-Han political survival.

## Key Characteristics

- Zhang Huan is presented as hostile to eunuch-side injustice and willing to reopen dangerous anti-eunuch grievances.
- His public recommendations connect him to the party-figure reputation field around Wang Chang and Li Ying.
- He is politically exposed because personal grievance, party-prohibition labeling, and palace-side interests can convert upright conduct into liability.
- His later humbled letter to Duan Jiong is treated as preserved agency rather than moral beauty.
- His post-office teaching and writing make survival productive, not merely bodily escape.

## Evidence

- Anti-eunuch grievance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-890-xiang-dangguan-xian-xue-duifu-xiaoren-ltnvh6qu-mr8flsdlxf-e6rknpis|Hanji 890]] says Zhang asks for proper treatment of Dou Wu, Chen Fan, and Empress Dowager Dou, directly touching the old grievance against Cao Jie's faction.
- Recommendation politics: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-890-xiang-dangguan-xian-xue-duifu-xiaoren-ltnvh6qu-mr8flsdlxf-e6rknpis|Hanji 890]] says Zhang and Liu Meng recommend Wang Chang and Li Ying for the Three Excellencies, making his speech part of the high-office and party-figure dispute.
- Personal-grudge exposure: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-902-kongzhi-qingxu-shi-rensheng-bixiuke-ljynylvdlsdtjf4es9jmilav863b|Hanji 902]] says Wang Yu retaliates after Zhang refuses to recommend him, pulling Zhang into party-prohibition danger.
- Survival and afterlife: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-902-kongzhi-qingxu-shi-rensheng-bixiuke-ljynylvdlsdtjf4es9jmilav863b|Hanji 902]] says Zhang's humble letter prevents fatal exile pressure and lets him return home, teach, write, and die old.

## Qualifications

- Hanji 890's transcript renders the name as "张宦"; this page normalizes to 张奂 because the role, office context, and existing late-Han pattern match Zhang Huan.
- The current page is still episode-bounded and should not be treated as a full biography of Zhang's frontier service.
- The survival reading should not erase Zhang's earlier directness; the page intentionally keeps both the exposed remonstrant and the humbled survivor in one profile.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 890 as Zhang Huan's earlier 169 CE remonstrance and recommendation branch.
- Reframed the page around a two-stage profile: direct pressure against eunuch-side memory control, then survival through humbled withdrawal.
- Preserved the transcript normalization from "张宦" to the existing Zhang Huan route.
