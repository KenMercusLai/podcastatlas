## Overview

张奂 / Zhang Huan is a late-Han official, scholar, and frontier figure whose current wiki profile now begins with 166 CE selective pacification. Hanji 875 says his return to the Protector of the Xiongnu role helps induce [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] and [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] submission, after which he executes only ringleaders and accepts the rest. Later sources add 167 CE frontier victory and reward distortion, Qiang surrender-policy dispute, compromised military use in the Dou Wu crisis, post-coup guilt, direct remonstrance, and survival under pressure.

## Current Profile

Zhang Huan's profile is no longer only a remonstrance and survival case. Hanji 875 gives an earlier frontier-pacification layer: Southern Xiongnu and Wuhuan groups submit after Zhang returns to the relevant northern-frontier office, and he punishes only the leaders who incited rebellion while pacifying the rest. This makes selective incorporation an early part of his profile before the later Eastern Qiang policy debate.

Hanji 878 gives a frontier-success and reward-gatekeeping layer: Zhang defeats [[XianlingQiang|先零羌]] forces attacking Sanfu, but the expected marquisate is reduced because he refuses to flatter eunuchs. He refuses the reduced reward and asks instead to move his household registration to Hongnong. Hanji 883 then shows his frontier-policy side before the anti-eunuch aftermath: as Protector of the Xiongnu, he urges the court to accept surrender and peace from remaining [[DongqiangLateHan|东羌]] groups because elimination is difficult and [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]]'s hard line risks overreach. Duan answers by accusing him of exaggerating the Qiang threat and resenting Duan's success, making Zhang a policy rival in the Qiang field before he becomes a court-danger figure.

Hanji 887 places Zhang in the military reversal that destroys Dou Wu: he returns to Luoyang without full information, the eunuch side uses forged imperial authority and Zhou Jing's temporary command, and Zhang Huan moves with the remaining Five Camps troops into the confrontation. This helps explain why Hanji 889 treats his later marquisate as morally compromised rather than merely honorable.

Hanji 889 and Hanji 890 show the repair attempt after that compromised use. Zhang refuses the title, uses omen and loyalty language to ask for burial and relief around [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]], [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]], and [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦妙 / 窦皇太后]], and then joins [[LiuMengLateHan|刘猛]] in recommending [[WangChangLateHan|王畅]] and [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]]. Hanji 902 shows a later and harsher risk environment: after [[WangYuLateHan|王寓]]'s accusation and Duan's pressure, Zhang survives by lowering his posture, withdrawing from office, teaching, and writing.

## Key Characteristics

- Selective frontier pacifier who accepts Southern Xiongnu and Wuhuan submitters while punishing ringleaders.
- Frontier commander whose military merit can be discounted by eunuch-side reward gatekeeping.
- Reward-refuser who turns distorted compensation into a moral stance.
- Surrender-and-peace advocate when residual frontier groups are hard to eliminate cleanly.
- Anti-eunuch figure vulnerable to being used by the side he opposes during information confusion.
- Remonstrant who later reopens dangerous Dou Wu / Chen Fan / Dou Miao grievances.
- Humbled survivor whose teaching and writing preserve agency after office withdrawal.

## Qualifications

- Hanji 875 gives a short summary of Zhang Huan's northern-frontier return and surrender handling; it does not reconstruct the full office appointment, operational route, or every submitter group.
- Hanji 887 and Hanji 889 make Zhang's knowledge and intention source-scoped: the stable claim is that he was used by the eunuch side, not that he deliberately supported the anti-Dou purge.
- Hanji 875, Hanji 878, Hanji 889, and Hanji 890 render the name as "张宦"; this page normalizes to 张奂 because the frontier role, anti-eunuch reward pattern, and existing late-Han route match Zhang Huan.
- Hanji 883 renders the name as 张焕; this page normalizes to 张奂 because the Protector of the Xiongnu role and Duan Jiong dispute match the established route.
- The current page is still episode-bounded and should not be treated as a full biography of Zhang's frontier service.
- The survival reading should not erase Zhang's earlier directness; the page intentionally keeps both the exposed remonstrant and the humbled survivor in one profile.

## What Changed

- Hanji 875 adds a 166 CE selective-pacification layer before Zhang's later Qiang-policy dispute and court-remonstrance arc.
- The page now treats Zhang's frontier profile as both surrender incorporation and battlefield success, not only reward refusal.
- Hanji 878 and Hanji 889 remain distinct reward-refusal branches: one reduced after frontier merit, one morally compromised after the Dou / Chen crisis.
