## Overview

张奂 / Zhang Huan is a late-Han official, scholar, and frontier figure whose current wiki profile joins four early [[EmperorLingOfHan|汉灵帝]]-era danger cases. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-887-dang-zhuduizhang-pengshang-zhuduiyou-jieguo-taican-lvyh9a-kvsdqaokmylgg139bvhfk|Hanji 887]] now supplies the original compromised action: Zhang Huan is summoned back without understanding the coup details and is used by the eunuch side's forged authority. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-889-weishenme-haoren-bu-changming-huohai-yi-qiannian-lt-g7qgkcf-fhy3xccecjagwqhku|Hanji 889]] then shows his moral injury, refused reward, and attempted repair for [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]] and [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]].

## Current Profile

Zhang Huan's profile is no longer only a remonstrance and survival case. Hanji 887 places him in the military reversal that destroys Dou Wu: he returns to Luoyang without full information, the eunuch side uses forged imperial authority and Zhou Jing's temporary command, and Zhang Huan moves with the remaining Five Camps troops into the confrontation. This helps explain why Hanji 889 treats his later marquisate as morally compromised rather than merely honorable.

Hanji 889 and Hanji 890 show the repair attempt after that compromised use. Zhang refuses the title, uses omen and loyalty language to ask for burial and relief around Dou Wu, Chen Fan, and [[EmpressDowagerDouMiao|窦妙 / 窦皇太后]], and then joins [[LiuMengLateHan|刘猛]] in recommending [[WangChangLateHan|王畅]] and [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]]. Hanji 902 shows a later and harsher risk environment: after [[WangYuLateHan|王寓]]'s accusation and [[DuanJiongLateHan|段熲]]'s pressure, Zhang survives by lowering his posture, withdrawing from office, teaching, and writing.

## Key Characteristics

- Zhang Huan is presented as hostile to eunuch-side injustice but vulnerable to being used by the side he opposes.
- His compromised military role in 168 CE precedes his refusal of a later reward, making guilt and reputation repair central to his profile.
- He uses omen and loyalty language to reopen dangerous anti-eunuch grievances around Dou Wu, Chen Fan, and Empress Dowager Dou.
- His public recommendations connect him to the party-figure reputation field around Wang Chang and Li Ying.
- His later humbled letter and post-office writing turn survival into preserved agency rather than mere escape.

- Compromised reward: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-889-weishenme-haoren-bu-changming-huohai-yi-qiannian-lt-g7qgkcf-fhy3xccecjagwqhku|Hanji 889]] says Zhang hates eunuch autocracy but is used in the coup, then refuses a marquisate after becoming dasinong.
- Omen remonstrance: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-889-weishenme-haoren-bu-changming-huohai-yi-qiannian-lt-g7qgkcf-fhy3xccecjagwqhku|Hanji 889]] says Zhang uses the snake and storm omen consultation to ask for burial and rehabilitation measures around Dou Wu and Chen Fan and for proper treatment of Empress Dowager Dou.
- Direct punishment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-890-xiang-dangguan-xian-xue-duifu-xiaoren-ltnvh6qu-mr8flsdlxf-e6rknpis|Hanji 890]] says Zhang's pressure around Dou Wu, Chen Fan, and Empress Dowager Dou brings detention, rebuke, and salary punishment after eunuch-side resistance.
- Recommendation politics: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-890-xiang-dangguan-xian-xue-duifu-xiaoren-ltnvh6qu-mr8flsdlxf-e6rknpis|Hanji 890]] says Zhang and Liu Meng recommend Wang Chang and Li Ying for the Three Excellencies, making his speech part of the high-office and party-figure dispute.
- Survival and afterlife: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-902-kongzhi-qingxu-shi-rensheng-bixiuke-ljynylvdlsdtjf4es9jmilav863b|Hanji 902]] says Zhang's humble letter prevents fatal exile pressure and lets him return home, teach, write, and die old.

## Qualifications

- Hanji 887 and Hanji 889 make Zhang's knowledge and intention source-scoped: the stable claim is that he was used by the eunuch side, not that he deliberately supported the anti-Dou purge.
- Hanji 889 and Hanji 890 render the name as "张宦"; this page normalizes to 张奂 because the role, office context, and existing late-Han pattern match Zhang Huan.
- The current page is still episode-bounded and should not be treated as a full biography of Zhang's frontier service.
- The survival reading should not erase Zhang's earlier directness; the page intentionally keeps both the exposed remonstrant and the humbled survivor in one profile.

## What Changed

- Hanji 887 adds the original military-use layer that explains why Zhang Huan's later reward is morally compromised.
- The page now links his reward refusal and Dou/Chen repair efforts to the coup reversal itself.
- Hanji 902's survival-first profile is preserved as a later adaptation rather than a contradiction.
