# Nick Cullinan

## Overview
Nick Cullinan appears in the wiki as the museum-side speaker in the [[BayeuxTapestry|Bayeux Tapestry]] segment who emphasizes how missing evidence invites interpretation.

## Current Profile
His source-bounded role is to frame uncertainty as part of the tapestry's force. The episode uses his comments on the missing ending, unknown makers, and unknown purpose to explain why the object remains open to new questions rather than exhausted by a single account.
