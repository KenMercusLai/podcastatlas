# Uttarakhand

Uttarakhand appears in [[settler-bet-a-west-bank-flashpoint-6a842bb539e73ea8f3c13a2d]] as the first Indian state in independent India to adopt a [[UniformCivilCodeIndia|uniform civil code]]. The source says that since January 2025, [[IndianMuslims]] there have been barred from following many customary family-law practices.

The state is treated as an easier test case for the [[BharatiyaJanataParty|BJP]] because it has mostly been BJP-run, is religiously significant, and was expected to offer muted resistance. It also supplies the source's [[LiveInRelationshipRegistration]] example, where couples must register cohabitation within 30 days or risk jail time.
