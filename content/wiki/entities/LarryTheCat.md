# Larry the Cat

Larry the Cat is the long-serving chief mouser to the Cabinet Office featured in [[for-bucks-sake-the-rise-of-self-made-billionaires-6a61e076562e3ad886d54c95]]. The source presents him as a fixture of [[Number10DowningStreet|10 Downing Street]] who has outlasted multiple British prime ministers and become a media object in his own right.

The segment is comic, but its wiki role is [[PoliticalImageMaintenance]]. Larry's Battersea-origin story, debated job performance, unofficial social-media following, photobombing of world leaders, rivalry with Palmerston, and separation from [[KeirStarmer]]'s cats are used as lessons in political backstory, perception, spotlight capture, and intolerance of rivals.
