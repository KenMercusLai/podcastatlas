# 阿飞（古龙人物） / A Fei (Gu Long character)

## Overview

阿飞 is an unranked young swordsman in [[SentimentalSwordsmanRuthlessSword|《多情剑客无情剑》]] whose stripped-down speed, attachment to [[LinXianer|林仙儿]], collapse, and recovery challenge the completeness of [[WeaponsRankingGuLong|兵器谱]].

## Current Profile

His sword is described as direct, rapid, and nearly inseparable from a successful strike. The episode nevertheless treats his highest potential as relational rather than purely mechanical: unlike [[JingWumingGuLong|荆无命]], he retains sympathy, understanding, and the capacity to grow through feeling.

That capacity does not protect him automatically. Lin Xianer's betrayal leaves him drinking and neglecting practice for a prolonged period. His release arrives quietly while drinking porridge, when he suddenly recognizes that continued fixation is unnecessary. The hosts see this as consistent with a personality that is both stubbornly committed and capable of decisive severance once perception changes.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses a minimal, exceptionally fast sword style oriented toward decisive action.
- Falls outside the published ranking despite elite ability and future potential.
- Retains feeling and empathy rather than cultivating emotional vacancy.
- Becomes functionally weaker when romantic fixation, betrayal, and alcohol displace practice.
- Can end a long attachment abruptly after a moment of clear recognition.
- Serves as the episode's candidate for a “多情剑客” whose feeling may deepen rather than merely obstruct mastery.

## Qualifications

The page is based on one conversational reading. A Fei's exact rank, the title's application to him, his comparative speed, the psychology of his awakening, and the proposition that feeling produces superior swordsmanship remain fictional or interpretive. Recovery from a damaging attachment is not generally instantaneous even when the novel stages it that way.
