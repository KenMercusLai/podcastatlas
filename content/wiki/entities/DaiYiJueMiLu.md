# 《大义觉迷录》

《大义觉迷录》 is the text [[YongzhengEmperor|雍正]] produced after the [[ZengJing|曾静]] case and discussed in [[11-yongzheng-wangchao-zhi-dayi-juemi-shijingqian-bi-xia-de-qiangu-qi-an-547265513]]. In the episode, it gathers imperial refutations, Zeng Jing's repentance, and the case narrative into a public teaching instrument meant to defeat anti-Qing rumor and justify Qing rule.

The book matters because it did not behave like a simple propaganda success. By printing rebuttals to accusations about succession, family violence, greed, Manchu rule, and the emperor's private conduct, it spread the scandal vocabulary it wanted to suppress. The episode treats this as [[PoliticalPropagandaBackfire]]: official explanation preserved the rumor, while [[QianlongEmperor|乾隆]]'s later suppression made readers suspect the text contained dangerous truth.
