# 盒马 / Hema

Hema is the store-warehouse fresh-retail route in [[no-200-dianshang-sanguo-zhi-qunxiong-zhulu-yaogua-gongzhang-chijian-juqian-yiji-108-zhong-sifa-974601500]]. The episode mentions it alongside fresh-grocery financing and warehouse models, using it as one of the attempts to combine offline retail, online ordering, and rapid local fulfillment.

[[vol-270-da-shi-dai-liu-zai-le-ta-de-shi-dai-1006063835]] adds Hema to the [[SupermarketCanteen|supermarket canteen]] branch. The source treats Hema's seafood steaming and low-priced meal offerings as a grocery-side answer to some demand once served by mall food courts: the supermarket can use supply-chain access, ingredient turnover, and midday traffic needs to make prepared meals useful beyond standalone dish margin.
