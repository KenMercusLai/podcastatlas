# 索耳 / Suo Er

## Overview

[[SuoEr|索耳]] is the author of [[LingDingShi|《零丁世》]], represented by one [[CultureLimited|文化有限]] episode as a writer of southern Chinese migration, dialect, marginal lives, and human–nonhuman entanglement.

## Current Profile

The source presents Suo Er as a writer born in 1992 and raised in Zhanjiang, with later study or work experience in Guangzhou, Wuhan, and Beijing. That movement, together with familiarity with Leizhou speech and Cantonese, is used to explain the novel's sensitivity to linguistic difference without reducing its many voices to autobiography.

His stated ambition is to write lonely, drifting, and marginal existence across large spans of time and space. The episode also reports his self-description as an “apprentice of the nonhuman world,” linking islands, forests, springs, stone, fungi, animals, weather, and material objects to the novel's historical imagination. The current profile remains source-bounded because the supplied episode summary is neither a primary interview nor a complete bibliography.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses regional speech as social memory and character structure rather than incidental local color.
- Joins family-scale inquiry to migration, war, labor, and transregional history.
- Accepts formal difficulty and loose connection in pursuit of a many-centered novel.
- Gives natural forces, nonhuman life, and objects interpretive weight alongside human actors.
- Mixes historical material, oral retelling, and fantasy while keeping certainty unresolved.
