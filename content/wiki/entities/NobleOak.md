# Noble Oak

## Overview
Noble Oak is the bourbon brand in the stolen interstate shipment described by [[default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356-default-mp3-ywr3ahjkcgo-b8f7b496cd7337e614f9c50ae34981a8-27045356]].

## Current Profile
Within this source, Noble Oak functions as a traceable product case showing that a finished beverage shipment bundles manufacturing, packaging, inventory, logistics, and brand risk. Its theft was reportedly enabled through a compromised dispatch identity rather than a roadside attack.

## Key Characteristics
- Bourbon brand associated with [[A21WineAndSpirits]].
- Subject of a stolen 10,800-bottle shipment.
- Shipment value reported at about $500,000.
- Replacement required coordinated packaging and production inputs.
