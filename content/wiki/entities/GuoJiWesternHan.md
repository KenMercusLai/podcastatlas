## Overview

郭吉 / Guo Ji is a Western Han envoy who appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-368-1-ewu-zhizhan-beihou-de-tanpan-boyi-lm1l-z62lcewag-x6kxwgyusukh|Hanji 368-1]] as the bearer of [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]'s demand that [[Xiongnu|匈奴]] either fight decisively or submit.

## Current Profile

Guo Ji's role is a compact contrast case. He communicates an unsoftened binary demand, the chanyu reacts angrily, and Xiongnu detains him. The episode then uses this failed encounter to explain why the next envoy must combine intelligence, cultural familiarity, and task-oriented judgment rather than repeat the same approach.

The current evidence does not establish that Guo Ji's firmness alone caused the detention or that a different protocol would necessarily have succeeded. His profile is therefore limited to the message, hostile reception, and narrative contrast supplied by this episode.

## Key Characteristics

- Envoy entrusted with Han Wudi's battle-or-submission demand.
- Delivers a binary political ultimatum without a recorded face-saving bridge.
- Is detained after the chanyu reacts angrily.
- Functions as the episode's contrast to Wang Wu's protocol-flexible mission.

- Hostile reception: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-368-1-ewu-zhizhan-beihou-de-tanpan-boyi-lm1l-z62lcewag-x6kxwgyusukh|Hanji 368-1]] says the chanyu becomes angry and detains him.
- Contrast function: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-368-1-ewu-zhizhan-beihou-de-tanpan-boyi-lm1l-z62lcewag-x6kxwgyusukh|Hanji 368-1]] presents Wang Wu's later selection and cultural fluency as the alternative approach.

## Qualifications

This profile is grounded in one short podcast summary. It does not provide Guo Ji's wider biography, full instructions, exact wording, conduct before the audience, detention conditions, release, or an independent account of Xiongnu motives. The contrast with Wang Wu should not be read as proof that directness always fails or accommodation always succeeds.
