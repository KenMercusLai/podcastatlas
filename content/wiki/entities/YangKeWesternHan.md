## Overview

杨可 / Yang Ke enters the wiki as the [[HanWudi|汉武帝]]-era official whom the source places in charge of intensified告缗 enforcement. His subordinates' actions provoke [[YiZongWesternHan|义纵]] to make arrests, turning fiscal extraction into a conflict over livelihood, implementation, and obedience to imperial orders.

## Current Profile

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-335-dangzhe-wudi-de-mian-huoqubing-weihe-gan-sha-liguang-zhi-zi-lupxhu64fi0d0xl223zq9pwenmy1|Hanji 335]] gives Yang Ke an enforcement role rather than a full biography. After voluntary contributions disappoint Wudi, Yang leads [[RewardedDenunciationFiscalEnforcement|告缗式奖励告发财政执法]] against concealed commercial wealth. The episode attributes huge confiscations and severe commercial damage to the campaign. When Yi Zong arrests several of Yang's subordinates in opposition to the way it is being carried out, Yang reports the action and Wudi has Yi executed for resisting the edict.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han fiscal-enforcement official associated with the告缗 campaign.
- Administrative link between Wudi's war-finance pressure and local denunciation or confiscation.
- Court reporter whose complaint about Yi Zong's arrests leads to imperial punishment.
- Source-scoped figure whose office, wider career, and precise legal authority remain undeveloped.

## Qualifications

The episode does not establish Yang Ke's office title, personal enforcement decisions, the identities or conduct of the detained subordinates, or the full legal procedure against Yi Zong. Its confiscation totals and claim of broad middle-class ruin are source-attributed and should not be treated as quantified institutional history without corroboration.
