# Liang Jianzhang

## Overview
Liang Jianzhang is represented in the wiki as a [[Ctrip]] co-founder, technical and analytic operator, returning CEO, economist, and population-policy advocate. The earlier Ctrip-platform source uses him mainly to explain founding-team complementarity and mobile-era recovery; the newer [[LuoyonghaosCrossroads|罗永浩的十字路口]] interview makes him a fuller profile whose business, demographic, and AI views all turn on the same question of how humans preserve future-oriented agency.

## Current Profile
[[kunzai-xitong-li-de-jiudian-ni-buzhidao-de-xiecheng-longduan-lianchengshi-keji-luandun]] presents Liang as the technical and analytic member of Ctrip's founding team, with a background spanning the Fudan juvenile class, Georgia Tech, Oracle, and later economic and demographic research. In that source, his return to the CEO role matters because [[Qunar]] and [[Elong]] increased pressure on Ctrip, and his push into mobile apps, direct supplier links, e-booking, and stronger hotel agreements became part of the platform recovery that later fed [[HotelPlatformPricingPower]] concerns.

[[liangjianzhang-luoyonghao-xiecheng-renkou-ai-ljpurcsyivjkwjyak-3kt3zly-fp]] turns the compressed founder reference into a life-and-ideas profile. Liang describes early computer competitions, a Fudan juvenile-class path, Oracle consulting, Ctrip's founding, his Stanford economics doctorate, remote-work experiments, pandemic live-streaming, internationalization, fertility policy, and applied travel AI as connected rather than separate chapters.

His current public profile in the wiki is therefore not just "Ctrip founder." He is a business operator who treats process management, experiments, and vertical data as sources of advantage; a demography advocate who links low fertility to future innovation capacity; and an AI-era thinker who wants travel companies to use AI deeply while keeping human judgment, ethics, and generational renewal central.

## Key Characteristics
- Technical founder and analytic operator in the [[Ctrip]] founding mix.
- Returning CEO associated with Ctrip's mobile recovery, supplier systems, and organizational transition.
- Experiment-minded manager whose evidence includes remote-work and hybrid-work trials inside Ctrip.
- Public demography advocate linking low fertility to long-run [[DemographicInnovationCapacity|demographic innovation capacity]].
- Policy advocate who emphasizes subsidies, childcare, education-cost reduction, female career support, and fertility-treatment support rather than simple moral exhortation.
- Travel-AI pragmatist who wants [[Ctrip]] to focus on domain applications rather than foundation-model competition.
- AI-era human-agency thinker who worries that automation can compress young people's career, family, and innovation opportunities.

## Evidence
- Technical founder and early capability mix: [[kunzai-xitong-li-de-jiudian-ni-buzhidao-de-xiecheng-longduan-lianchengshi-keji-luandun]] and [[liangjianzhang-luoyonghao-xiecheng-renkou-ai-ljpurcsyivjkwjyak-3kt3zly-fp]] both tie Liang to Ctrip's 1999 founding and complementary division of labor with [[JiQi]], [[ShenNanpeng]], and [[FanMin]].
- Mobile recovery: [[kunzai-xitong-li-de-jiudian-ni-buzhidao-de-xiecheng-longduan-lianchengshi-keji-luandun]] treats Liang's return as part of Ctrip's response to [[Qunar]] and [[Elong]]; [[liangjianzhang-luoyonghao-xiecheng-renkou-ai-ljpurcsyivjkwjyak-3kt3zly-fp]] adds the "all in mobile" account, app-screenshot product discipline, and business-unit shift.
- Process and experiment management: [[liangjianzhang-luoyonghao-xiecheng-renkou-ai-ljpurcsyivjkwjyak-3kt3zly-fp]] describes early call-center and service-process detail, remote-work research in Ctrip's call center, and later hybrid-work experiments for professional employees.
- Demography advocacy: [[liangjianzhang-luoyonghao-xiecheng-renkou-ai-ljpurcsyivjkwjyak-3kt3zly-fp]] has Liang argue that low fertility is a collective-action problem where individually rational decisions can damage future innovation, markets, and urban vitality.
- Policy package: [[liangjianzhang-luoyonghao-xiecheng-renkou-ai-ljpurcsyivjkwjyak-3kt3zly-fp]] grounds his prescriptions in cash support, childcare, lower education pressure, women's work-family support, assisted-reproduction support, and Ctrip's reported employee child subsidy.
- Travel-AI pragmatism: [[liangjianzhang-luoyonghao-xiecheng-renkou-ai-ljpurcsyivjkwjyak-3kt3zly-fp]] says Ctrip should use AI for customer service, marketing, itinerary generation, recommendation, and immersive cultural-tourism experiences, while staying focused on travel applications.
- Human-agency concern: [[liangjianzhang-luoyonghao-xiecheng-renkou-ai-ljpurcsyivjkwjyak-3kt3zly-fp]] links AI job displacement to young people's compressed pre-35 career, study, entrepreneurship, and family-formation window, and argues that humans should retain control over key resources, ethics, and innovation.

## Qualifications
The current evidence gives a strong founder-and-advocate profile but not a comprehensive biography. Many claims in the Luo Yonghao interview are Liang's own retrospective or policy argument, including experiment results, employee-benefit effects, fertility-cost estimates, and AI-risk judgments. The Ctrip-platform source is more critical of platform power, so Liang's operating reforms should be read as both company recovery and part of the later infrastructure that enabled platform leverage.
