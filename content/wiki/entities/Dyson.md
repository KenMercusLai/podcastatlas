# Dyson / 戴森

## Overview
Dyson / 戴森 enters the wiki through [[daisen-jinru-diandong-yashua-lingyu-chuantong-jindian-jiameng-chixu-shousuo-1010976825]] as a premium consumer-hardware company extending its personal-care branch into oral care through [[CameraJet]].

## Current Profile
The source frames Dyson less as a vacuum-only brand than as a company searching for adjacent hardware growth after more difficult or cancelled bets such as washing machines and cars. CameraJet puts Dyson's premium, engineering-led product story into oral care by combining brushing, water flossing, a camera, and app feedback, but mainstream adoption remains unresolved because the feature set is expensive and intrusive compared with ordinary toothbrushes.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses premium engineering-led consumer hardware positioning to enter adjacent household and personal-care categories.
- Treats oral care as an extension of personal care, not as a purely medical or commodity toothbrush category.
- Relies on sensors, app feedback, and cleaning mechanics to make a USD 499 toothbrush feel differentiated.
- Carries hardware-category-extension risk because prior Dyson expansion efforts have not all survived.
- Needs consumer trust around usefulness, privacy, habit formation, and price to move beyond curiosity.
