# 周昉 / Zhou Fang (Tang Painter)

## Overview
Zhou Fang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-464-gudai-de-chungongtu-daodi-you-duo-ye-lp-qvincrx1zfp5z2ggjygfnpbca|Hanji 464]] as the Tang painter to whom the episode attributes the lost `春宵密戏图`.

## Current Profile
The episode uses Zhou Fang chiefly to explain a proposed naming and setting transition in [[AncientChineseEroticImagery|中国古代春宫图的社会功能]]. It says `春宵密戏图` placed a sexually explicit scene inside the palace, represented Tang Xuanzong, Yang Guifei, two female attendants, and an observer, and caused later works of the kind to be called 春宫图. It then narrates the painting's loss and a late-Ming rediscovery and purchase by collector Zhang Chou.

## Key Characteristics
- Tang painter presented as an important figure in the episode's genealogy of Chinese erotic imagery.
- Attributed creator of a lost palace-set work called `春宵密戏图`.
- Used to connect the older cosmological meaning of “春宫” with a later erotic-art label.
- Known here only through a compact podcast account whose attribution chain is not independently verified.

## Evidence
- Work and setting - [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-464-gudai-de-chungongtu-daodi-you-duo-ye-lp-qvincrx1zfp5z2ggjygfnpbca|Hanji 464]] attributes `春宵密戏图` and its five-figure palace scene to Zhou Fang.
- Naming influence - [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-464-gudai-de-chungongtu-daodi-you-duo-ye-lp-qvincrx1zfp5z2ggjygfnpbca|Hanji 464]] says later images of the type took the 春宫图 name after this work.
- Provenance story - [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-464-gudai-de-chungongtu-daodi-you-duo-ye-lp-qvincrx1zfp5z2ggjygfnpbca|Hanji 464]] says Zhang Chou encountered and purchased the lost work in a late-Ming princely residence.

## Qualifications
The present evidence is one podcast transcript. The work's attribution, composition, naming influence, disappearance, rediscovery, purchaser, and provenance are retained as source-scoped claims and should not be treated as an independently verified catalogue record.
