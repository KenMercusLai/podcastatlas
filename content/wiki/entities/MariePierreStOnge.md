# Marie-Pierre St-Onge

## Overview
Marie-Pierre St-Onge is the nutrition and sleep researcher featured in [[eating-for-better-sleep-foods-that-improve-metabolic-health-dr-marie-pierre-st-onge-scim5220943767]], where she is introduced as a Columbia professor of nutritional medicine.

## Current Profile
The current wiki evidence uses St-Onge as a guest expert on reciprocal sleep–nutrition effects. Her profile centers on controlled sleep restriction, appetite hormones, food reward, free-living metabolic risk, dietary patterns, sleep architecture, meal timing, substrate oxidation, and the interpretation of functional-food and industry-funded studies.

Her practical position is pattern-first and qualified: prioritize food quality, plant foods, appropriate portions, and earlier daytime intake without turning one nutrient, oil, supplement, fermented food, or eating window into a standalone solution. The page remains bounded to one structured episode summary rather than a complete biography or publication review.

## Key Characteristics
- Studies how insufficient sleep affects appetite, food reward, energy intake, and cardiometabolic outcomes.
- Examines how dietary pattern, fiber, saturated fat, refined carbohydrate, and meal timing relate to sleep and metabolism.
- Distinguishes tightly controlled laboratory results from free-living behavior and longer-duration metabolic outcomes.
- Treats functional-food effects as modest and outcome-specific rather than substitutes for dietary patterns.
- Defends publication rights and statistical interpretation as safeguards in industry-funded research.
