# Clean Through

Clean Through is the [[Kao|花王]] cleaning product line highlighted in [[mai-xiangzao-qijia-de-bainian-huawang-weishenme-chengle-bandaoti-zhizao-de-guanjiang-qiye-1004213131]] as a Freon-alternative cleaner. The episode says Kao began selling it in 1991 after Freon solvents were being phased out because of ozone-layer damage.

The product matters because it is the bridge from consumer cleaning chemistry into electronics cleaning. Its early use case was removing flux after soldering electronic components, but the same underlying [[InterfaceScience|interface science]] and [[SurfactantTechnology|surfactant technology]] later became relevant to [[SemiconductorPrecisionCleaning|semiconductor precision cleaning]].

Clean Through therefore appears as a route into [[ChemicalControlAsManufacturingCapability|chemical control as manufacturing capability]]. The source treats the product not as a standalone brand story, but as evidence that environmental regulation, materials science, and customer manufacturing problems can open a new industrial market.
