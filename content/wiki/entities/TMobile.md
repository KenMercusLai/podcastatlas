# T-Mobile

## Overview

T-Mobile enters the wiki as the mobile-network provider sponsoring a [[RevisionistHistory]] episode about enterprise connectivity. Its representative [[MoKatibeh]] presents [[NetworkSlicing|5G network slicing]], T-Priority, and SuperMobile as infrastructure for maintaining selected service behavior under congestion and across remote work settings.

## Current Profile

The episode positions T-Mobile as both network operator and workflow-design partner. It says the company can tune capacity, reliability, performance, and latency for a slice, then combine adaptive connectivity with network-layer security and satellite communication in SuperMobile. Las Vegas Formula One, first-response operations, [[CNN]] field reporting, and [[SiemensEnergy]] maintenance provide the main cases.

The account is commercially informative but not independent. It reports customer testing and operational benefits without supplying comparative measurements, pricing, coverage boundaries, security evaluation, or procurement tradeoffs.

## Key Characteristics

- Operates the mobile network underlying the episode's enterprise cases.
- Presents configurable network behavior as a business-outcome tool rather than a generic speed upgrade.
- Markets T-Priority to first-response organizations and SuperMobile to broader mobile workforces.
- Combines terrestrial slicing, network-layer security, and satellite communication in its product narrative.
- Uses customer deployments and event operations to demonstrate prospective workflow change.

## Evidence

### Network and product design

- [[invisible-infrastructure-with-t-mobile-for-business]] attributes configurable capacity, reliability, performance, and latency, plus T-Priority and SuperMobile, to T-Mobile.

### Deployment cases

- [[invisible-infrastructure-with-t-mobile-for-business]] describes Formula One, Ryder Cup, MLB All-Star Week, CNN reporting, Siemens Energy maintenance, and first-response scenarios.

### Commercial evidence boundary

- [[invisible-infrastructure-with-t-mobile-for-business]] is explicitly a paid partnership and supplies no independent benchmark, price comparison, or coverage audit.

## Qualifications

This profile is bounded to one sponsored episode. Product capability, deployment chronology, security, customer benefit, and comparative performance are episode-reported claims rather than independently established findings. Satellite communication is described as part of a mixed-connectivity offering, not a complete replacement for terrestrial networks.

## What Changed

- Established T-Mobile's wiki profile around enterprise network slicing and mixed terrestrial-satellite connectivity.
- Added the distinction between configurable network behavior and workflow outcomes that still require customer-side implementation.
