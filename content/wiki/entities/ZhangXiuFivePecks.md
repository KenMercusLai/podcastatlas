# 张修 / Zhang Xiu (Hanzhong)

张修 / Zhang Xiu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-959-ruguo-ni-chuanyue-dao-sanguo-gai-ruhe-cunhuo-lroaihmpnrelmfcugym9zx2h2dhq|Hanji 959]] as the Hanzhong-side actor paired with [[ZhangLuFivePecks|张鲁]] in [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s Yizhou strategy. Liu Yan appoints Zhang Lu as督义司马 and Zhang Xiu as别部司马, then sends them against Hanzhong commandery administrator Su Gu.

The source does not build a full biography for Zhang Xiu. His role is functional: he helps make Hanzhong and the [[XieguRoadLateHan|斜谷道]] part of Liu Yan's separation from the Chang'an court, after which Liu Yan can claim that road blockage prevents normal communication.

This page is deliberately disambiguated from [[ZhangXiu|张绣]], the Wancheng commander. The two share a romanized name but are different late-Han subjects with different public routes.
